Chapter 10-23: "Seeking the Best"

――The distant Lugunica Castle, a white edifice against the evening sky, seemed to swallow itself whole.

"――Emilia..."

Subaru murmured softly, a pang of anguish in his heart. Emilia was imprisoned within that city's highest fortress. His hope was to one day place her on that throne, not chain her in a dungeon.

"...Emilia is a Royal Selection candidate. She should be treated with respect, at least outwardly. There's no way she should be locked away."

"I know, I know. But... it's more complicated than that..."

"――――"

"Ah, sorry... I know you were just trying to cheer me up."

"It's alright. It's a partner's duty to encourage their loved one, even when they're feeling down. Petra and Rem would be crying if they saw us like this."

"Well, at least Rem wouldn't be bitter," Subaru chuckled wryly.

He was surprised that he could laugh at all. Just moments ago, his heart had been breaking, overwhelmed by his helplessness and the knowledge that he couldn't stay with Emilia. Beatrice, however, was truly remarkable. He glanced over, only to be met with a playfully smug look from her, and another small sigh escaped him.

――Subaru and Beatrice, along with the rest of the Six Tongues, were preparing for their departure according to Russell's instructions.

The waiting area was surprisingly located in one of the largest warehouses in the commercial district. It gave Subaru a glimpse into the audacity of the Six Tongues – how they blended seamlessly into the city, hiding in plain sight.
Now, Subaru and the others were behind the warehouse, taking one last look at Lugunica Castle where Emilia was held captive, waiting for the perfect moment to escape.

"――――"

Crusch remained unconscious, while Rachins showed no resistance to leaving the capital city. The main members included "White Sheep" and "Black Dog," led by Russell, but there were also several others involved in deception and misdirection.
It was clear that as an intelligence agency directly under the kingdom, their operations were on a massive scale.

"But even with all that power, they've been completely outmaneuvered."

"Not outmaneuvered. Just... temporarily delayed. This is our escape route, our chance to regroup and strike back."

"A 'regrouping'... I guess it was a bit unexpected," Beatrice murmured.

Hearing this, Subaru tilted his head. "Unexpected?" Beatrice furrowed her brow slightly.

"It's just... I didn't expect you to decide so quickly to leave the capital. Leaving Emilia behind and not joining up with Otto and the others must have been a difficult choice."

"Yeah, it was... it definitely was. Just before I came here, I was talking to Miklotov about what we were going to have for dinner with Emilia. She said she was looking forward to making bento boxes with Rem and Petra today... and now this."

"Subaru..."

"It's painful, it hurts. But you didn't object, Beatrice. So, I guess you know that this is the best course of action?"

After a moment of silence, Beatrice nodded solemnly. She also knew that staying in the capital would only lead to more setbacks. However, she hadn't pushed him to make his decision; it was for him to decide for himself.
She wanted him to choose, not be forced into it.

"You're so mean, my Beatrice," Subaru said with a rueful smile.

"And that meanness is guaranteed for life."

"What a bargain. I made the right choice getting you under contract, Beatrice Insurance."

Subaru let her gently stroke his hair, feeling reassured by her strength and reliability. Normally, she would have told him to stop "before it gets too late," but even that was an act of consideration on Beatrice's part.
Just then...

"We're ready for departure. Are you both alright?"

The voice came from "White Sheep." She was wearing a white robe over her black suit – the same "Recognition Inhibitor" as Subaru and the others – and had let down her hair, subtly altering her appearance. This combination of light disguise and the inhibitor was designed to conceal their identities.

"Huh."

"W-What is it? Am I looking strange?"

"No, no, it's not that. It's just... you look completely different in a suit than you do in this outfit. Actually, your current attire suits you more; it gives off more of a 'White Sheep' vibe."

"I suppose so. When I was with 'Silver Fox' before... I wore clothes like this more often when I was still living with my mother."

"Ah, I'm sorry. I brought up some unpleasant memories."

To Subaru's apology for dredging up painful memories, 'Shiro' offered a faint smile. She then stroked her long, dark blue hair and said,

"...I am the one who should apologize."

"Eh? For what are you apologizing?"

"It's because of my convenience that I'm rushing you... Truly, Natsuki-kun Subaru probably would have wanted to make the decision with more time."

Subaru involuntarily chuckled at 'Shiro,' who averted her gaze and seemed even more troubled.
Although 'Shiro' was once a member of 'Beloved Children' under Capella and is now part of Russell's 'Six Tongues,' her inherent goodness makes her unsuitable for being either an underworld figure or a spy. She seems to be taking on the burden of Subaru's decision-making process.

"You don't have to worry about me. I don't feel rushed, and in fact, I appreciated you giving me the information I needed to make that decision."

"Natsuki-kun..."

"If you're still hesitant, then please stop. It makes me uncomfortable."

"Eh?"

"That 'Natsuki-kun' thing... use my full name. Call me Natsuki or Subaru, but not both. It puts me on edge."

Shiro shrugged and tried to sound as casual as possible. Seeing this, Beatrice, standing next to Subaru, sighed deeply.

"There's no need to overthink it. She's just speaking her mind directly."

"What other meaning could there be?"

"I don't know! I won't tell you!"

"That's how someone who already knows would phrase it!"

Subaru pouted and turned his back on Beatrice, covering his ears. Then, as if to break the tension of their exchange, Subaru's words seemed to have startled 'Shiro,' causing her to lose some of her stiffness.
And then—

"Are you going with Subaru-kun and Beatrice-san?"

"Yeah, that's right. You can even call me Betchan if you want."

"Betchan!? You haven't even been called that by Subaru!"

"Um... I don't think I can answer that..."

With a humble reaction, 'Shiro' relinquished her historical right to call Beatrice "Betchan." However, she looked at Subaru with an expression suggesting some other hidden anxiety.
As Subaru frowned at this sight, and as she opened her mouth—

"Um, Subaru-kun, Ti—"

"OI! You guys are still dawdling! Get a move on!"

Suddenly, Rachins burst in, his voice rough.
Rachins' sharp, intense gaze caused 'Shiro' to flinch and shrug her shoulders. Subaru shielded 'Shiro' from Rachins and glared at him.

"You know, that robe really doesn't suit you."

"Shut up, just go! Besides, why do I have to be babysat by Russell-sama? Let the slaves handle the work."

"N-No, it's not like we're slaves!"

"Shiro-chan, don't get so worked up about it... It sounds too serious,"

Subaru rebuked Rachins for his coarse language. Then, he looked out at the distant castle and took a deep breath—praying for her safety.

"I wonder if Emilia is praying for us in the same way."

"For us, not just you. That would make her happier, and that's what she would want."

In response to Beatrice, who seemed to understand his feelings with just a glance, Subaru turned his back on the castle and started walking.
He then walked through the waiting area and got into a Dragons carriage parked outside.
As he was quickly leaving the waiting area, he spotted someone with a hooded robe carrying what looked like a large box. He could see that they were struggling to lift it.

"Should I carry this to the Dragons carriage outside?"

"Ah..."

Subaru reached out and effortlessly lifted the box—well, "effortlessly" is an exaggeration; he used quite a bit of strength. The person who had been struggling with the box reacted in surprise.

"Are you alright?"

Subaru tilted his head at the startled person.
Although her face was hidden by the hood, she seemed to be a slender figure. However, since her true appearance was unknown, it wasn't as suspicious as 'Kuroinu.'
The suspicious thing, or rather, what Subaru suspected, was the other person's awkward reaction. However, before he could pursue it, the person bowed and quickly hurried away.

"Such a rude person wouldn't even say thank you," said Shiro.

"Well, some people are shy. Not everyone is as friendly and cute as Garfiel," Subaru replied.

"Why did you bring up Garfiel...? Well, I suppose I can see the connection," Beatrice muttered.

Appearance isn't everything; it also manifests in attitude and actions.
Subaru and Beatrice were nodding in agreement about this common understanding within the group when Shiro noticed Subaru struggling with the packages. "Excuse me," she said, "I made Subaru-san carry these... I'll tell 'Ao no Naga'-san later."

"Wait, is that person 'Ao no Naga'? Is that the one who healed my burns?" Subaru asked.

"Yes, that's right. She's a very skilled healer," Shiro confirmed.

Subaru reacted to the codename – the name "Ao no Naga." Shiro affirmed it. It was quite a clever choice, going from sheep and dog to fox, and then to snake for a healing mage.
According to Subaru's knowledge, snakes and medicine are inseparable. There's even a story in Greek mythology that Asclepius, the god of healing, carried a staff with a snake wrapped around it.
Of course, the connection between that and the name "Ao no Naga" was probably just a coincidence.

"In that case, I should be the one thanking you. Carrying these packages isn't much of a repayment," Subaru said.

"Ah, but it might be difficult. 'Ao no Naga'-san seems to not like talking to many people... I've been told by 'Ginpaku' to respect her wishes," Shiro explained.

"Hmm? A doctor who dislikes people? ...Sounds like a very skilled doctor," Subaru mused.

A skilled but eccentric doctor is a common trope in manga and anime.
Still, while Subaru understood Shiro's point, he decided to refrain from contacting "Ao no Naga." Instead, he would continue to help with carrying things whenever he saw her struggling, slowly repaying his debt.
Anyway...

"Let's go. We need to return," Subaru declared, turning to Beatrice, who was standing beside him, and stepped out of the waiting area.

"The principle of 'Recognition Inhibition' is to subtly alter the impression or atmosphere a person exudes, minimizing any potential distractions. In other words..."

"In other words?" Subaru prompted.

"It's just a formality within a tightly guarded perimeter. Don't rely on it too much," Russell said from inside the Dragons carriage, causing Subaru to nod slightly and readjust his hood deeper onto his head.

They were in a Dragons carriage disguised as part of a merchant caravan, about to leave the capital.
The Dragons carriages weren't luxurious; they were ordinary vehicles similar to those used by traveling merchants on the roads, with no ostentatious embellishments. To maintain the disguise, there were ten carriages in the caravan, which made Subaru slightly uneasy about attracting too much attention.

"Don't worry. The capital is currently in a state of heightened tension. Many people are choosing to temporarily leave the city because they dislike this situation. We're taking advantage of that," Russell explained.

"This 'heightened tension'...?" Subaru asked.

"Of course, it's due to concerns about dangerous individuals hiding within the city. Numerous kingdom soldiers are mobilized and conducting thorough investigations. The death of McMahon-sama hasn't been revealed to the public, but the atmosphere is tense," Russell replied.

"That makes sense... Everyone must be worried," Subaru said, acknowledging Russell's explanation. He felt sorry for the people of the capital who were caught up in the machinations of "Sekibun."
Russell then narrowed his eyes slightly and asked, "Does Natsuki-dono worry not only about Emilia-sama and the group but also about the citizens of the capital?"

"Eh? Well, I wouldn't say I'm *that* worried, but I do wonder if they're alright. Even someone as powerful as Capella probably wouldn't just arbitrarily replace citizens with Authority... That's what makes me uneasy," Subaru replied.

As a member of the group with decision-making power within the capital, it would be the optimal course of action for seizing control of the kingdom to replace members of the "Council of Wise Men" and other high-ranking nobles. Compared to that, exercising Authority over citizens is nothing more than harassment, but Capella might even do it gleefully.
Of course, there was a reason for revealing his presence and the effects of "Sekibun's" Authority in Priestella – to deliberately overwhelm "Six Tongues" – but...

"..."

"...Mr. Russell?"

Suddenly, as he thought this, Subaru noticed that Russell was silent.
In response to Subaru's call, Russell stroked his goatee with his finger, closed one eye, and briefly replied, "No."

"Lord Natsuki's concerns are well-founded. Whether or not it will be a mere deterrent is unknown, but 'Six Tongues' isn't withdrawing entirely from the capital. A significant number remain for intelligence gathering and disruptive activities. You can trust in their efforts."

"Yeah, I know. But that's something I have to leave up to you guys. If I had ten or twenty arms and five or six bodies, I could be more helpful..."

"That's quite an exaggeration. Subaru is already pretty amazing as he is."

"A fairly high praise. ...Anyway, the only people we can rely on on that side are you guys. Show us the true power of the intelligence agency 'Six Tongues'."

He showed his clenched fist, and Russell nodded as if to say he understood. While feeling reassured by this response, Subaru turned his attention from the Dragons carriage where he was sitting to the one behind them – another Dragons carriage.
As mentioned earlier, the Dragons carriages belonging to the disguised merchant caravan that 'Six Tongues' had formed numbered over a dozen, but the one Subaru and his group were in was at the front, with personnel scattered throughout. In particular, what concerned Subaru was Crusch, who hadn't woken up once during the wait.

Since falling asleep in the sewers, she hasn't regained consciousness.
Crusch had mentioned the 'Dragons Eye' before falling asleep, but that was a self-diagnosis, and she has no real understanding of what that 'Dragons Eye' actually is.
Beatrice had also said that there were no factors other than fatigue...

"Are you worried about Lady Crusch?"

"Well, yeah. But aren't you the same? If you want Crusch's strength to fight 'Beloved Children', aren't you feeling restless?"

"I am indeed concerned. However, a person of such caliber that they would be unmatched in diagnosis has said that there are no concerns about Lady Crusch's physical condition. Of course, there is concern regarding what Lord Natsuki mentioned as the 'Dragons Eye'..."

"For now, all we can do is treat her with respect and wait patiently for her to wake up."

"That is correct."

Subaru sighed at his own helplessness, having been given a polite response that addressed his concerns.
In reality, 'Six Tongues' is what considers Crusch's well-being to be the most important thing. They will undoubtedly do everything they can for her. It was then that he remembered...

"Speaking of which, wasn't it the person called 'Aosaji'? I think I briefly met them earlier. Are they the one tending to Lady Crusch?"

"..."Aosaji,' did you say? Did you meet them?"

"Yeah? Well, just a brief encounter before we left. But she wouldn't speak to me. Just like Miss 'Shiroheiji' said, she seems like a shy person."

"...That is correct. I believe that is accurate. 'Aosaji' is the one attending to Lady Crusch. Please rest assured."

It was so surprising that the shy 'Aosaji' had met Subaru that Russell's reaction took him a moment to respond for the first time.
However, while feeling relieved to hear that 'Aosaji' was attending to Crusch...

"Felis is probably really worried too."

Felis, who was relieved of her position as Crusch's knight and whose offer to mediate between them was dismissed by Subaru. She may still be in the capital, though we don't know her whereabouts since they parted ways at the royal cemetery. Naturally, she must have heard about this commotion.
He hoped that at least she had joined up with Wilhelm and wasn't doing anything reckless...

"Did we stop?"

Suddenly, Subaru felt the Dragons carriage come to a halt, and he instinctively stiffened his body. He quickly wrapped his arm around Beatrice in preparation for any unexpected situation.
However, Russell smiled reassuringly at Subaru's reaction and said, "Please don't worry."

"It seems we have arrived at the checkpoint waiting area in front of the capital's main gate. It's usually crowded, but it looks like we'll be waiting quite a while."

"Waiting for a while? Does that mean everyone is leaving the capital even at night?"

"That applies only to those who are quick to act and make decisions, and those who are astute in discerning opportunities. However, as tomorrow approaches, when this atmosphere expands further, even more people may move."

"It's like a replay of the Empire. I wonder if many people fled from the imperial capital at that time."

"I was thinking the same thing. We definitely don't want to relive that experience in the imperial capital."

Nodding at what he said, Subaru recalled Vollachia's capital, L.upugana. He remembered the mass migration of the Empire's people under Abel's orders after the "Great Disaster" transformed the capital into a city of corpses. However, that worked because of Vollachia's strong top-down control, making it difficult to achieve the same in Lugunica. Moreover, unlike the dead, there was no way to distinguish Capella's "Beloved Children."

"That's where Crusch's Divine Protection and our role come into play. Natsuki-dono, I believe this is something you already understand, but please always strive for the best course of action."

Subaru met Russell's gaze directly as he slightly lowered his voice. Russell spoke not as a merchant, but as the leader of the intelligence organization, "Six Tongues." It was a reminder of what truly mattered.

"The best... course of action..."

"Yes, that's right. For the sake of the kingdom, for the future, for those you care about, whatever it may be, always strive to reach the final destination. If necessary, choose options that may bring pain in order to achieve that."

"..."

His tone was calm, lacking any overt passion. However, that didn't mean it didn't resonate with Subaru. Indeed, words filled with fervor and emotion can have a profound impact. But Russell's lack of outward display stemmed from the fact that he had already been struck and forged to such an extent that further heating would be unnecessary. Heating iron before forging allows you to shape it into the desired form. However, there was no point in heating and hammering already shaped iron; only distortion.

"It seems we've reached the checkpoint. I will handle it, so please wait."

Russell stood up and descended from the Dragons wagon, called by the driver, and began interacting with the guards. Subaru watched him leave, then took a deep breath. Next to him, Beatrice said:

"Honestly. That man... Roswaal's friend, you understand now, right?"

"Indeed, Beatrice-san. I felt it too. The direction they face is different, but their trajectories are eerily similar. ...The best course of action..."

"Subaru always does that. Beatrice-chan will vouch for me."

It was as if Beatrice was saying that Subaru was slacking off. Of course, Russell had no such intention, and Beatrice knew that too. At the same time, she also understood that those who are capable often find themselves in a similar frustrating situation.

"The checkpoint... it's quite imposing."

He stood up, slightly opened the small window leading to the driver's area, and peered outside. He looked alongside Beatrice, who was peering in with a "Seriously?" look on her face, at the main gate of the capital city, which featured a normally operated service window, and a separate temporary observation post and waiting area. At the window, staff were requesting permits and manifests. Normally, those simply entering and exiting the capital wouldn't be subjected to such strict procedures. The level of vigilance at the entrance to the noble district was applied even here.

"Yes, I am deeply concerned. That is why it is customary for merchants to move quickly. We have prepared all necessary permits and manifests. While we have already affixed inspection seals to the cargo, would you like us to review them again?"

He saw Russell speaking with a guard at the checkpoint. Beside him was Shiro, and Russell was presenting her with numerous letters to give to the guard, each wrapped in what appeared to be a "Recognition Inhibition" robe. The guards seemed overwhelmed by Russell's calm but assertive demeanor.

"There are no apparent discrepancies in the documents, and the Commerce Guild seal is indeed present. The contents of the manifest... mainly pharmaceuticals?"

"It's essential for timely delivery, directly impacting transportation time and lives."

Russell leveraged his position as a representative of the Commerce Guild and appealed to their sense of ethics with the nature of the cargo. The guard seemed unable to find fault. And then, the ultimate move:

"Please... if we don't get this medicine, everyone in my village will..."

Shiro said this voice trembling, grabbing the guard's hand in what seemed like an impassioned plea. At that point, all doubt vanished from the guard's face. Still holding Shiro's hand, the guard cleared his throat and said:

"W-well, I don't see any problems. We can permit your passage..."

"Hold it! Who authorized skipping protocol?"

"!"

Just as the official was about to grant permission, a voice cut through the air. A man who looked older than everyone else reached out and intervened in the situation.

Probably, that person is the superior of the staff in charge. The superior, rushing out from the waiting area, snatched the documents from his subordinate's hands and, while checking their contents, said:

"This Commerce Guild certificate indicates items with proper inspection seals. However, there are rules, and they must be followed."

"That is a commendable opinion, but does it apply to situations where strict adherence to procedures is necessary?"

"You speak like a merchant, but just as merchants have their own ways, the Royal Guard has its own sense of honor."

"I understand. – I acknowledge that."

The subordinate nodded respectfully at the superior's words, and Russell concurred. It was clear from everyone's expressions that 'Shirogane' was feeling anxious, and Subaru felt the same way.
They had all the necessary documents prepared, and they knew there were dummy Dragons carriages.

"Well, this might be bad... Beako! Come to my pocket right now!"

"Are you panicking too much! Even if Betty is petite, she won't fit in your pocket! Besides, let's be more composed –"

Subaru and Beatrice were flustered about having the Dragons carriage inspected.
Just then, Russell, who seemed to have sensed Subaru's anxiety, waved his hand lightly as if to reassure everyone in the caravan.
Subaru felt relieved that Russell had a plan, but immediately after...

A loud jolt and crashing sound echoed from behind the waiting line, followed by multiple screams.

At first, Subaru misconstrued the jolt as the starting of a Dragons carriage, the noise as the creaking of its chassis, and the voices he heard as cheers – mistaking everything for something else entirely.
It was so sudden, with no warning whatsoever.

"What?!"

With a contorted face, holding Beatrice in his arms, Subaru jumped into the seat of the Dragons carriage and peered through the large window at the rear of the caravan – towards where the noise had come from.
He saw a chaotic scene: a disrupted caravan, plumes of smoke rising, multiple figures emerging from the smoke as if they were being ejected, and a building half-visible within the same smoke.

"Is that... an accident?"

"It looks like it. It seems like a Dragons carriage with a broken axle or something triggered a chain reaction."

"So, a building on the side of the road was hit. That's bad; there are probably many injuries."

As Russell said, the usual congestion due to the kingdom's turmoil was likely the cause. The accident occurred in the midst of the massive traffic jam, which led to pushing and shoving.
It was impossible to know how many people were injured, but they needed to get people out of the crash site quickly...

"Natsuki-dono, permission has been granted for the Dragons carriage to proceed. Please take your seats."

Russell said this to Subaru and the others, who were panicking. When he heard those words, Subaru was about to call out "Russell-san," but

"Eh...? What did you just say? Is the Dragons carriage leaving?"

"Yes. The staff members have gone to deal with the accident in the back. Since we have no irregularities in our documents, we are allowed to proceed. It is also necessary to clear the congested caravan."

"But... even so..."

"Are there any injuries? I heard that the cargo contained medicine."

"That's a deception. Not only are the listed items on the manifest present, but more goods are hidden in the cargo hold. That would be revealed if we opened the cargo. As I said – always strive for the best solution."

There was no harshness in his expression or voice, yet the sharpness of his words was overwhelming.
Subaru felt as if he had been cut by those words and stiffened at the premature opportunity to put that into practice. He understood Russell's point, even if he didn't want to.

"Shirogane-chan, please explain this to Natsuki-dono."

"–!"

Subaru was silent, clenching his teeth. Russell spoke from behind him, his voice soft. It was 'Shirogane', who had just been looking into the Dragons carriage.
She choked back a sob and turned her glistening round eyes towards Russell. However, Russell didn't look at her directly and repeated her name: "Shirogane."
Hearing that, 'Shirogane' climbed into the carriage as if trying to escape and placed her hand on Subaru's hand resting on the window frame –

"Subaru-san, please listen for a moment. It's alright. The Royal Guard will take care of it properly. – It's okay."

"...Shirogane-chan..."

Despite the ruthless decision that Russell was about to make, 'Shirogane' appealed to them in this way.
At that moment, a strong feeling of resistance rose within Subaru, but 'Shirogane''s voice and her trembling hand conveyed that she, too, would be deeply disappointed if he ignored it.
She was trying her best, working diligently, yet still striving for the best outcome. What should Subaru do in this situation?

"Subaru."

Beatrice called out to Subaru from within his arms.

Her eyes held a mixture of trust for Subaru and a resolute gleam. No matter what arguments were presented, her gaze was the same as when she promised to follow him, even to hell in the sewers.
That, even if it meant sharing guilt, Beatrice would not waver.

"Now, we should leave from here..."

"Yes, that's correct. A wise decision, Natsuki-dono. That is the best course of action for this situation."

The Dragons carriage’s window frame, where Subaru's hand had been resting, slowly lowered as 'Shiro's' hands released it. Seeing Subaru's movement, Russell said with a sigh of relief.
Yes, that was right. Russell's words were correct. He should follow them.

"..."

The accident might be considered a sort of blessing.
The Dragons carriage was about to be inspected, and Subaru and the others were on the verge of being discovered.
The guards were occupied with rescue efforts, allowing them to escape this situation.
All for saving the Capital, for saving the Kingdom.
That's why...

"Um?"

Just as he was trying to convince himself that leaving was the right thing to do, Subaru saw something outside the window, a sight he couldn't tear his eyes away from.
The hem of a white robe fluttering as 'Shiro' ran straight towards the accident site.

"..."

...Are you an idiot, Natsuki Subaru?

"…I thought we were supposed to value pursuing the best course of action, Russell-san."

"...Right. Your judgment is correct. Let's go now. We need to…"

"Then!"

"–!?"

"Then my 'best course' is not abandoning someone who can be saved here!!"

Shouting loudly, Subaru placed his hand back on the window frame.
He shook off the hands holding his, startling Beatrice and Russell, who were behind him, and said "Sorry!" before continuing:

"Let's go, Beatrice! Lend me your strength!"

"Of course, I shall assist you!"

Carrying Beatrice, Subaru leaped out of the Dragons carriage window. Behind him, he heard Russell calling him, but he couldn't stop. He wouldn't stop.
The accident? Ridiculous. Accidents are tragedies. They are definitely not salvation.

"You're right! I was wrong!"

He hadn't seen that 'Shiro' running away, and he wouldn't have realized it then.
One hundred percent, 'Shiro' is correct. A wave of shame washed over him for his actions.
How stupid are you, Natsuki Subaru?
Why don't you understand, Natsuki Subaru?
Even if there were people who wanted to throw away their lives, your standards wouldn't change.
It's that nothing changes.

"What!? Hey, this is dangerous! Those who aren't guards…"

"There are injured people! I'll help them! I'll pull everyone out!"

Amidst the swirling dust, in front of the accident site, a guard with his mouth covered by his hand – the older superior officer who had just been obstructing the passage of the Dragons carriage – stared wide-eyed at Subaru, who had suddenly appeared with a young girl cradled in his arms.
It was natural for him to be surprised.
But that surprise wasn't a reason to stop them.

"I can use Magic! It might be useful! Let me help!"

"...Alright, we need all the hands we can get. Come!"

After a moment of hesitation, that was a flimsy wall in front of the older guard's respect for human life. He directed Subaru and Beatrice to enter the accident site after being prompted by a guard.
The space filled with smoke, groans of pain, and the cries of children was, as seen from afar, a chain reaction caused by a single Dragons carriage whose axle had broken, along with buildings that had partially collapsed.

There were those who were bleeding and those who were motionless. Amidst the chaos of rescue efforts, shouts and screams filled the air.
Seeing the scene before him, Subaru's gaze wandered as he wondered where to start...

"–Over here! Help me over here!"

That voice was particularly loud, cutting through the noise and reaching Subaru's eardrums.
He looked towards the source of the voice, a figure in a white robe kneeling on the ground amidst the swirling dust, tending to an injured person trapped under debris – it was 'Shiro'.
Rushing to 'Shiro's' side, Subaru saw the state of the injured person: a large piece of broken beam had pierced through his torso, and he was bleeding profusely.
And then...

"Beatrice-chan, use your Magic to lighten the beam and pull it out! You can do it, right?!"

"Yes, I can. But why are you having me…?"

"Oh, stop being so slow!"

In a fit of frustration, he said abruptly, but in the next instant, 'Shiro' roughly pulled off her hood. From beneath it emerged auburn hair, and the Demi-Human features that were hidden by it stood out prominently.

"...cat ears..."

"Felis?"

Subaru's lips uttered her name in stunned disbelief.

He hadn't expected to see that familiar face there – the person who had rushed to aid those in critical condition faster than anyone else, Felis of "Aozora," was desperately working amidst the chaos.
