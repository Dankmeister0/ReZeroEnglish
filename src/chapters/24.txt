Arc 1 Interlude: "The Moon Is Watching"

A faint, glimmering blue light — the wavelength of water that governs healing — Reinhard watched it from a distance, and let slip a sigh so quiet no one nearby could have noticed.

Worry rose to his finely composed profile, and the faintest traces of battle still clung to his attire. Cut out that pose of his, standing before the ruins, and it alone could have completed a masterpiece painting.

And yet, however striking he might appear from the outside, from within there was a well of regret over his own shortcomings that no amount of words could exhaust.

Even now, Subaru was still being desperately treated — Emilia's doing. And his wound, in the first place, had come from an opening born of Reinhard's own complacency.

Subaru had taken a wound he should never have had to bear, and Emilia had been made to shoulder a burden she should never have had to shoulder.

On top of that, his own constitution meant that standing beside Emilia while she worked her treatment would only be a hindrance. Watching over things from a distance like this was the best he could do — and the fact that some coolly rational part of him could reach that judgment only deepened his self-reproach all the more.

"...There, that should do it."

Eyes closed, blaming himself, Reinhard's ears caught a voice like a silver bell.

With a motion as if wiping her brow, Emilia brushed the hair back lightly from the forehead of Subaru, propped against the wall, and confirmed the color returning to his face. Rising to her feet, she turned around with a satisfied nod.

"Treatment's done. He's — somehow made it over the worst of it."

"That is a great relief. And with that, Emilia-sama..."

Reinhard strode quickly to the contented Emilia, knelt at her feet, and bowed his head low. Every motion was without hesitation, a posture perfectly in accordance with formal etiquette.

"This time, my own shortcomings caused Emilia-sama a great deal of distress. I will accept whatever punishment you see fit for this failure."

Before his bent knee he set down the sword he had unbuckled from his waist, and Reinhard apologized for his failure. It was the greatest expression of contrition a knight could offer. Whatever judgment might follow, Reinhard was resolved to accept it without complaint.

But to this apology directed at her, Emilia said,

"That's the thing I really don't get about you people."

"Pardon?"

"You came to help at just the right moment, and somehow we all got through this safely. And yet you try to take on all the responsibility for every bit of hardship and pain along the way."

Wagging a raised finger, the silver-haired girl pouted her lips, looking somewhat put out. Then she turned that raised finger toward Subaru, who wore a peaceful sleeping face.

"That boy over there is way more honest about it. He even said, 'You saved me, so give me a reward' — though it wasn't a greedy reward at all."

Reinhard, too, had seen Subaru say — trying to act cool — that he wanted to know her name. At the memory, Emilia broke into a smile, and despite himself, Reinhard's own lips nearly curved as well.

"So," Emilia said, narrowing her violet eyes gently at him,

"Thank you for saving me. That's all I have to say to you. There's no crime to be found, so there's no punishment to give. If that doesn't satisfy you, just put it to use next time."

"...Understood. I am grateful for those words."

Bowing his head deeper still in a show of respect, Reinhard rose to his feet. Face to face, Reinhard stood a full head taller, looking down at Emilia. And yet — what was that sense of largeness he'd felt from her just now?

A difference in the size of one's Vessel, perhaps, Reinhard mused, gauging his own narrowness. And at the same time, he confirmed once more that Emilia truly was someone 'chosen' for a reason.

"By the way, you're dressed awfully lightly today..."

Watching the silent Reinhard, Emilia murmured as if just noticing. Her eyes were on Reinhard's clothing — today, being off duty, he'd dressed in an outfit based around black, chosen for wandering the royal capital at his leisure. To someone who had only ever seen him in knight's attire, it must indeed look strange, he realized.

"Today is my first day off in a while. I'm not wearing my usual Royal Guard uniform. My armor is back at the castle — as a knight, all I have on me today is this sword."

He shifted the sword he'd set aside back to his hip, exposing the ornate blade, its notches conspicuous, to Emilia's gaze. At that, she blinked in mild surprise.

"It's your day off — so why did you get caught up in all this?"

"That is a slightly complicated matter... It comes down to that boy there, Subaru, leading me here."

The boy whose consciousness was elsewhere now — his meeting with Reinhard was what had led him here. To explain it in order, the story would have to go back to the back alley where Reinhard first crossed paths with Subaru, and the exchange they'd had there.

At that time, Subaru had let slip to Reinhard that he was 'looking for a woman with silver hair and a white robe.' At that point, there was only one person in Reinhard's knowledge who fit that description.

Subaru sought contact with that person. In tracking his movements, Reinhard too found himself venturing into the slums.

"Along the way I encountered her, and here we are now."

"I see. Her, huh."

With her name brought up in conversation, Emilia's gaze turned to the corner of the plaza — to the girl still tending to the old man who had yet to regain consciousness. The blonde girl, feeling the gaze, turned around and lowered her eyes, looking uneasy.

"Emilia-sama, she is..."

"Reinhard. Thank you for all your help. I really am grateful. But with that said, I have a favor to ask. Don't interfere from here on."

Said with such a firm tone that Reinhard gave up any further mention of it. Taking his silence as assent, Emilia walked off toward the blonde girl. The girl, too, turned to face her approach as if bracing herself.

"Is that old man your family?"

Bending at the waist, Emilia crouched to meet the girl's eyes as she asked. At the question, the girl's face went blank with astonishment — no doubt because it was nothing like whatever words she'd been expecting.

Even Reinhard, who knew nothing of the circumstances between the two of them, could tell that whatever lay between them was not built on friendly familiarity.

Caught off guard, the girl scratched her cheek to collect herself, and, as if to hide her embarrassment, gave the head of the old man sleeping beside her a couple of light pats.

"Y-yeah, somethin' like that. Old man Rom's the only one I've got who's... yeah, like a grandpa, I guess."

"I see. I only have one family member too. He's asleep at all the important moments, and I could never say something like that to his face while he's awake."

"Same here, I sure as hell couldn't say this to Old Man Rom's face if he was awake."

If anything, the force of her blows against the old man's head had escalated from light pats to solid whacks — unconsciously, no doubt. Their speed picked up too, and his bald white head was starting to turn red.

Then she looked up at Emilia, a faint, weak light kindling in her red eyes.

"I figured you'd come down way harder on me."

"Well... if it were still like before, maybe I would have. But I guess I lost my nerve for it. So, just a little — I'll let it slide, for that boy's sake."

Can't be helped, Emilia said with a wry shrug of her shoulders.

At that gesture, and at being pointed toward the sleeping Subaru, the blonde girl lowered her face for a moment, then quietly muttered, "Sorry."

"You saved my life. I ain't gonna be an ingrate about it. I'll give back what I took."

With that, the girl rose to her feet and turned her gaze toward the alley. She called out to Emilia, "That okay?" and then,

"No tellin' if you two'll still be in one piece when I get back. ...I hid it somewhere else — that okay if I go get it?"

"How cautious of you. ...Not that I mind. I'll wait here."

"...You sure? I might just be talkin' my way outta this and makin' a run for it."

"You can run if you want, but that thing over there will chase you down, you know?"

Called out as "that thing" by Emilia, Reinhard straightened his posture. Whatever the blonde girl made of that sudden rigidity, she let out a heartfelt "ugh" of distaste and dashed off toward the alley, saying, "Back in a sec."

"Once she's back and everything's returned... we can finally head home."

"I won't ask for details, but please be careful. I'll have some of my men accompany you on the way back — please allow it."

"I don't need that much looking after... though I suppose after causing this much trouble, it comes to the same thing. Please do."

With her consent given, Reinhard replied, "Understood," then, following Emilia's gaze, arrived at Subaru's peacefully sleeping face. Well then, he prefaced,

"He — Subaru — what is your relationship with him?"

"A chance encounter."

Answered without a beat of hesitation, Reinhard couldn't help but be taken aback. Whatever look of surprise had crossed his face must have been quite something, because Emilia's lips curved into a smile.

"I mean, that's really the only way to put it. I don't remember ever meeting Subaru before. This, just now, should have been the first time we ever met..."

"And yet he was looking for you. Saying he had something he wanted to give you. In fact, here he is, in this very place, and moreover..."

*He put his body on the line to protect you* — Reinhard began to say, then caught himself.

Declaring it so loudly somehow felt like it would cheapen the very nobility of the act itself. Surely it wasn't something Subaru had done wanting to be seen that way.

Besides, even unsaid, Emilia surely understood that much already. Indeed, she picked up right where Reinhard had left off unspoken,

"That's what's strange about it. ...Right now I'm half-suspecting that pervert's mixed up in this somehow."

"Please don't speak so poorly of Margrave Roswaal. He is a fine man. Though it is true he's somewhat eccentric..."

"The fact that 'pervert' got through to you just fine tells me you already know exactly what I mean, Reinhard."

"...My apologies. Please keep this between us where Count Roswaal is concerned."

With a wink to convey his gratitude, Emilia replied with a vague "Sure, sure."

Then Reinhard steered the conversation back to Subaru.

"What would you like done about his custody? If you'll entrust him to me, my household would be glad to receive him as a guest."

"...No, I'll take him back with us. That'll settle things more clearly, and even if he turns out to have nothing to do with the pervert, it doesn't change the fact that he saved me."

"Thank you for your consideration," she added, and Reinhard responded with a bow of the head.

That settled the bulk of the discussion. After this, he would assign men to escort Emilia safely back to the mansion. In the meantime, Reinhard would need to see to the aftermath of this place.

Looking over the collapsed Loot House, Reinhard closed his eyes at the scale of the damage. Once again, he found himself resenting his own lack of restraint. Just the slightest miscalculation of force, and this was the result. Power that, handled poorly, could have wiped out the whole district — a stark lesson in self-restraint and self-reproach.

"What'll happen to this place?"

"The area will be off-limits for a while, and we'll issue a wanted notice for her — the 'Bowel Hunter.' Though given she's always had a shady reputation to begin with, it's more likely than not to come to nothing."

"And the girl, and the old man?"

"...I can't speak to the full circumstances, but what she and that elderly gentleman have been doing is, professionally speaking, not the sort of thing I can simply overlook. However—"

He paused for breath, then gave a small shrug of his shoulders.

"I am off duty today. And what's more, if the victims don't press charges, there won't be sufficient evidence for this either way. Ha. No — truly, I can't speak to the circumstances at all."

"Heh. What a wicked knight you are."

"I'm honored to say — this is the true nature of the man they call the Knight among Knights."

As they spoke, Reinhard sensed a presence approaching from the alley. From the stride and the rhythm of breathing, he knew without looking that it was the girl.

The gap-toothed girl came into view, looking at the two of them waiting with an awkward expression.

"Got it back, just like I said."

"Good, good, I'm glad you actually came back. I did feel really bad about siccing this gentleman on you."

"Knight among Knights, my ass — chasin' after a guy like that is straight-up insane. First time I ever seen someone faster than me. Scared the hell outta me."

Reinhard answered her upward glance with a smile, then stepped back to watch over the two of them. The girl approached Emilia, rummaging through her clothes.

"Here, givin' it back. If it's somethin' that important to you, hide it better next time so it don't get swiped again."

"It's a strange feeling, getting that advice from you. ...If it's not too much to ask, I'd rather you stopped doing this altogether — not just to me."

"Yeah, that ain't happenin'. Just so you know, this time I'm only givin' it back 'cause you saved my life. I don't think I did anything wrong, and I ain't plannin' on quittin'."

Brushing off Emilia's wish without hesitation, the girl's face settled into a hardened smile — an expression that felt almost painful to see on someone her age. Listening to her stated convictions, Reinhard nevertheless accepted them in silence.

He understood full well that, professionally speaking, this was not a situation he should simply let pass. And yet — what other way of life could these two possibly have? To brandish nothing but righteous principle without offering any alternative would be far too convenient.

Reinhard had seen enough of the royal capital to be able to think that much, at least. Emilia, too, seemed to sense it. Lowering her eyes with a touch of forlornness, she said nothing more on the matter and simply held out her hand.

"All right. ...You're stubborn."

"If nothin' comes outta doin' nothin', then maybe I won't. Anyway, here."

Taking out the item, the girl set it in her palm and handed back what appeared to be the stolen goods to Emilia.

For an instant, a flash of red glimmered across Reinhard's eyes. That dazzling light was one he recognized, and for the briefest moment, narrowing his eyes, Reinhard searched the sea of his memory.

Then, having found the memory he was reaching for—

"—Eh?"

"Reinhard...?"

He had already reached out and seized, from the side, the hand of the girl holding the insignia.

Surprise rose in the eyes of both parties involved. Together, they looked up at Reinhard, and fell silent before the earnest, serious look on his face.

"O-ow, that hurts... let go..."

The girl resisted with a weak, shaking-her-head sort of motion. But Reinhard's grip on her hand showed no sign of loosening. This was the strength of a man who, in earnest, could crush even steel. Restrained though it was, it was by no means a grip a slight girl like her could break free of.

"This can't be..."

A trembling murmur. It was Reinhard's voice that had spoken it.

It was Emilia who reacted to those words. Unease rose in her violet eyes.

"Reinhard, wait. I know it's true this isn't something that can just be let go without any consequence. But this girl didn't know the insignia's value. And I myself, the one it was stolen from, am not making an issue of it. — There was fault on my part too, for letting it be stolen. So—"

"No, Emilia-sama. What I am concerned with is not that."

Spoken to so sharply, from above, Emilia fell into a bewildered silence.

Forgetting even his own discourtesy toward her, Reinhard looked at the girl whose arm he held. Looking up at the young man whose hair was the same shade of red as her own crimson eyes, the girl's gaze wavered with unease.

"...What is your name?"

"F-Felt... it's Felt."

"Family name? How old are you?"

"I-I'm an orphan, y'know? I don't got some fancy family name. Age is... probably around fifteen, I think. Don't know my birthday. Just— let go already!"

Having regained some of her composure as she spoke, the girl thrashed about roughly. But with a skillfully measured grip, Reinhard restrained her, then turned to look at Emilia.

"Emilia-sama, I find myself unable to keep the promise I made just now. — I will be taking custody of her myself."

"...May I ask why? If this is punishment for the theft of the insignia—"

"That, too, is by no means a small offense... but compared to the gravity of overlooking the scene before my eyes right now, it is a trivial matter."

Emilia's brow furrowed in confusion and incomprehension.

Reinhard accepted her bewilderment as unavoidable. After all, to her, this was nothing but an ordinary, familiar sight. It would be cruel to expect her to notice.

"I need you to come with me. I'm sorry, but you have no right to refuse."

"The hell... just 'cause you saved me don't mean you get to—?!"

Trying to fire back crudely at Reinhard's words, the girl's posture suddenly gave way. Her words trailed off uncertainly, the strength draining from her shoulders, and with one last resentful glare at Reinhard, she said,

"Go to hell... damn it."

Having spent her last curse, her head dropped limply.

Reinhard caught the now-unconscious girl's body, and in one smooth, practiced motion, lifted her into his arms. At his easy, familiar handling of her, Emilia said,

"That's another un-knightly thing to do... if you're too rough with her, it'll leave lasting damage to her Gate."

"Fortunately, I've known this sort of thing since I was born, so I know how to handle it with care. — Emilia-sama, I imagine you'll be receiving a summons again before long. I ask for your understanding."

Gently taking the insignia from the unconscious girl's hand, he held it out to Emilia. The insignia shaped like a dragon was, without question, the very symbol of the Dragon-blessed Kingdom of Lugunica. In Reinhard's hand, the red jewel had glowed with a faint, dull light — but the instant it passed into Emilia's hand, it shone brilliantly, as if rejoicing at returning to its rightful owner.

"Please, take good care of Subaru."

Receiving the insignia, Reinhard bowed to Emilia, who gazed back at him in silence.

Feeling the girl's light weight in his arms, Reinhard idly brushed aside the blonde hair falling across her forehead with his hand. Her innocent, pale face — freed of the need to put up a front, unguarded — was, in that unconscious moment, as lovely as any girl her age ought to be.

Change her clothes, wash the grime of the alleys off her, and surely she would shine all the brighter.

A strong wind blew, sending Reinhard's red bangs dancing.

Through the gap it left, he looked up at the sky — above the royal capital, already sunk into the dusk — where the moon hung.

A full moon, glowing faintly pale blue, its beauty carrying some uncanny, bewitching charm.

"Tonight might be the last time in a while I get to look up at the moon in peace..."

Reinhard's whisper reached no one — no one but the moon looking down upon them.
