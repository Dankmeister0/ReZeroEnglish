Prologue: "The Lingering Heat of the Beginning"

*—This is seriously bad.*

Feeling the hard ground against his face, he realized he had fallen face-down.
There was no strength left in his body, and he had already lost all sensation in his fingertips.
Only a heat so intense it made him want to claw at his throat dominated the center of his body.


*—Hot, hot, hot, hot, hot, hot, hot.*


The moment he opened his mouth to scream, what spilled out was not a cry, but a clot of blood.
Coughing, he spewed out the lifeblood welling up from his throat with abandon. Gurgling, he vomited blood, foam bubbling at the corners of his mouth. In his blurred vision, he saw the ground stained crimson.


*—Ah, is all of this my blood?*


He was bleeding so much that his collapsed body was practically soaking in it. Human blood makes up about eight percent of body weight, and losing a third of it is said to be fatal—but at this rate, hadn't it already all run out?
Though he had stopped vomiting blood, the cause of the "heat" burning his body was still active. His barely moving hand reached for his abdomen; feeling an impossible sensation there, he finally understood.


*—What, my stomach is ripped open?*


No wonder it felt so hot. It seemed he was mistaking "pain" for "heat".
The sharp laceration had sliced his torso nearly in half, leaving him held together by a mere sliver of skin at his waist.
In short, it seemed he had run into a "checkmate" in life.

The moment he realized this, his consciousness began to rapidly slip away.
Even the "heat" that had just been forcing him to writhe in agony vanished somewhere, and the unpleasant sensation of blood along with the feeling of his hand touching his entrails were all carried off as companions to his departing consciousness.

The only thing left behind was his body, denied permission to accompany his "soul".
He moved that body just a fraction, a final command from his vanishing consciousness. He turned his head upward.

Before his eyes, black shoes stepped onto the floor covered in a carpet of fresh blood, sending out ripples.
Someone was there. And that someone, in all likelihood, had killed him.

Strangely, he didn't feel the urge to catch a glimpse of their face.
He didn't recall being so passive as to stand by and watch his own killer, yet his heart didn't hold even a shred of interest in the person's identity.

All he wished for was simply this—that she would be safe.

"—baru?"

He thought he heard a voice like a silver bell.
Since he was in a state where he couldn't even tell where his ears or nose were, it was far more likely to be a trick of his mind.
And yet, even if it was only a reconstruction born of memory, that voice was incredibly soothing, stirring his heart.

And so—


"—!"

A short shriek rang out, and the carpet of blood welcomed a new participant.
The body collapsed right beside him, right where his own arm lay limply outstretched.
That pale hand, falling powerlessly, intertwined with his own blood-stained hand.

It was likely all just a coincidence.

He felt as if those faintly moving fingertips squeezed his hand back.

"...wait."

Grabbing his fading consciousness by the scruff of its neck, he forced it to turn back, buying himself a few more seconds.
Both the "pain" and the "heat" were now far away—nothing more than the howl of a sore loser struggling in vain.


But, even so—


"I'll definitely—"


*—save you.*



In the next instant, he—Natsuki Subaru—lost his life.
