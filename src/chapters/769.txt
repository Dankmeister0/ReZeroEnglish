Arc 10 Chapter 11: "The Reason the Dragon Jewel Shines"

"Don't you think Sakura and Tiiga are being just awful? If I'd known it would come to this, I—I would have—!"

"Did it never once cross your mind, out of fondness for your own precious hide, not to go using a Sacred Rite to save someone?"

"I probably would have done it anyway, even knowing! But there's a world of difference between doing something knowing what it'll cost you and doing it blind! My whole state of mind is completely different!"

Filiore seemed well aware of her own rash, headlong nature—her self-analysis was, in fact, quite apt—as she wailed and glared with tearful eyes at Sakura, seated across from her.

Sakura, who styled herself Filiore's elder-sister figure, knit her brow in that troubled way of hers at Filiore's outburst.

"You can say that, but the two of us are just as troubled by all this, you know. Subaru-chan, Beatrice-chan, you understand, don't you?"

"No idea why you're dragging me and Beako into this, but yeah, I get the general sense that this was bound to happen one way or another."

"Setting aside whose fault it actually is, that Filiore was the one who set it all in motion is easy enough to guess just watching the two of you go back and forth, I suppose."

So saying, Subaru and Beatrice shook their heads in perfect, weary unison, offering their verdict to Sakura's question from where they sat right beside her.

For the record, the seating in the facing sofas of the drawing room currently had, on the host side, Emilia and Otto, with Filiore clinging to Emilia's arm, while on the guest side sat Sakura, and—having relocated—Subaru and Beatrice side by side.

And along the wall, in addition to Petra, who'd been there from the start, Rem, who had shown Filiore in, had also joined, making the drawing room livelier than ever.

"That said, pretty much the entire increase in headcount can be explained by Filiore alone. Anyway, why are we the ones sitting over here? This seating arrangement makes no sense."

"How dreadful! Emilia, did you hear that? Subaru is trying to tear apart me and Emilia, the truest of friends. Don't tell me—could he be an agent of the Church...?"

"The only agent of the Church here, by any reasonable measure, is you yourself, I suppose."

Beatrice sighed at Filiore, whose expression was one of dread, as if bracing for battle. Emilia, her arm still held captive by Filiore, gave a wry smile and stroked her friend's head.

"Now, now, don't look at Subaru like that, Filiore. Subaru is really, really kind, and he wouldn't come between us. Besides, what Subaru and I have and what you and I have are completely different things, aren't they?"

"T-that's true, I suppose that's true... By the way, which of us matters more to you?"

"Huh? That's..."

"Never mind, forget it! Don't answer that! The scripture says as much! 'The Trials the Dragon bestows are given out of love. As gold gains purity when tempered by flame, so too does the Soul, through the trials of love, come to shine with a radiance fit for the Dragon's heart.'"

Filiore hid her face and clung to the scripture—less the pious devotee of the Divine Dragon Church she claimed to be, and more someone using its teachings as a lid to clamp down on whatever was inconvenient at the moment.

Because she'd cut Emilia off mid-sentence, Subaru never did get to hear where his approval rating currently stood from her own mouth, leaving him with an unsatisfied itch of his own.

That aside—

"About what you said earlier... Filiore joining the Royal Selection—is it really because she's taking Priscilla's place?"

Ignoring Filiore, who was flushing red and pale by turns entirely of her own accord, Emilia turned her question toward Sakura across from her.

At the question, Sakura narrowed her sleepy, drooping eyes just slightly.

"...At the very least, Priscilla-chan's death is definitely one of the reasons. If it weren't, it would contradict what's written on the Dragon History Stone, you see."

"In other words, the Divine Dragon Church's policy is that it can't act against the fact that the Dragon History Stone names five Dragon Maidens as Royal Selection candidates—it must adhere to that."

"Of course, please don't think that just because that premise fell apart, we saw our chance and came marching into the castle with our heads held high. Even if a vacancy opened up among the Dragon Maidens because Priscilla-chan happened to be missing, the Church's policy was to keep its distance."

"But that isn't how things turned out. And the reason for that is..."

Otto deliberately left the conclusion unspoken, but everyone's gaze in the room gathered on Filiore, who had, at present, been the one to stir up the first ripple that created this whole situation.

Her throat seizing at the discomfort of that attention, she then clung to Emilia.

"I-it's not true, Emilia! I never had the slightest intention of becoming your enemy! It was just a bad coincidence! B-b-besides, in the first place—"

Her face pale, Filiore turned her wide, glaring eyes on Sakura.

"In the first place, I protest that the Church's habit of covering things up is highly questionable! And on top of that, it's all because everyone raised me like some sheltered child right up to today! Sure, I didn't dislike being excused from the studies the other girls had to do—it was nice and easy—but I never thought it would lead to something that tears me and Emilia apart! It's not my fault!"

"There, there, don't sniffle, Filiore. I'm not angry, and I've never once thought of the other Royal Selection candidates as enemies."

"E-Emilia...!"

At the gentle way Emilia stroked her head, Filiore's turbulent heart was soothed.

Watching this exchange, Sakura raised a hand to her mouth in surprise. "Oh my."

"Usually this part drags on and on, but Emilia-chan really is amazing at handling Filiore-chan... Is there some kind of trick to it?"

"Let's set aside the cheap joke about how Emilia-tan's fluffy tolerance for everything neutralizes any and all Filiores, and get back to—so what did you actually come here for, Filiore?"

Judging from everything up to this point, it was clear this wasn't a declaration of war for the Royal Selection. Surely she hadn't really run all the way through the royal capital just to whine and make excuses.

"Is that so? I'm not even remotely surprised that you think that, I suppose."

"Same here, but if we go with that we won't get anywhere..."

"Wait just a moment. Do you really think I'd act without thinking things through, even here? It's true I often move without thinking at all, but today is different. I actually have a plan."

"A plan?"

"Yes, a plan! I don't want to join the Royal Selection. But I do want to fulfill my duties as the Church's Saintess using my Sacred Rite. So, I'll have my dear friend Emilia hide me away, and I'll secretly go save the people of Priestella! How's that!?"

"Sounds like a Saturday-morning-cartoon villain's evil scheme."

It was a plan so full of holes even a child could see through it. To begin with, since the goal was helping people, it wasn't even a "scheme" in the criminal sense—but the biggest problem was that Sakura had already caught on to it.

In other words, the whole plot had stumbled right out of the starting gate.

"Also, if word got out that we'd kidnapped the Divine Dragon Church's Saintess, that'd be a huge mess."

"Emilia-neesama has a bit of that same quality, but not realizing you're a walking bomb is dangerous, you know."

"Wh-what, does that mean Emilia and I make a good match...?"

"That's not the part you should be blushing about, I suppose."

Wherever her confidence had come from, Filiore's plan was summarily shot down.

On top of that, Subaru thought Filiore was taking the situation a bit too lightly. She'd been steering the conversation, from a while back, toward declining her participation in the Royal Selection outright—

"You can't just throw away your qualification as a Royal Selection candidate on your own say-so. Isn't that right?"

"Emilia-tan..."

"I study hard every single day, too. I think I understand the Kingdom much better now than I did at first... and the binding force of what's written on the Dragon History Stone is the same. I had a reason I wanted to join the Royal Selection—but I think even without that, I wouldn't have been able to refuse."

Gazing at Filiore, who was clutching her own arm, Emilia let those words spill out with a serious tone.

That the Royal Selection candidates had no right of refusal was already an established fact. Just last night, Subaru had discussed it with Garfiel and Petra—the Covenant between the "Dragon-affiliated Kingdom" and the Dragon was iron-clad and deeply rooted.

In fact, Felt had once tried to refuse participation in the Royal Selection. But she'd been forced into accepting it, in exchange for the safety of Old Man Rom, who'd tried to take her away.

That meant the Kingdom would spare no measure to ensure that the Dragon Maidens named on the Dragon History Stone joined the Royal Selection—a kind of coercive force built into the whole arrangement.

Emilia was saying the same thing applied to Filiore.

"But... but this isn't what I wanted, is it?"

"Yes, I'm sure that's true. I think that if Filiore hadn't been able to make her emblem glow, none of this would have happened either. ...But the Dragon Jewel did glow, and Filiore was chosen anyway. I think Filiore, more than anyone, understands what that means."

"Uuu..."

At Emilia's words—words that carried something almost maternal—Filiore weakly bowed her head.

Filiore was a nun of the Divine Dragon Church. Without even needing to be told, she—who held faith in and reverence for the Dragon—was surely in the position to feel the binding force of the Dragon History Stone's writing most keenly of anyone.

Everything she'd said and done up to this point could even be called a childish flight from reality.

And yet—

"Then... yes, then how about this? Let's form a friendship alliance, you and I, Emilia. That way, I'll help you become Queen with everything I have. If it's for that, then the Royal Selection—"

"Thank you, Filiore. But—I really, really don't want that."

Quietly, but with an unshakable firmness, Emilia declared it plainly.

At the nobility in that silver-bell voice, Filiore's throat gave a thin, "Hiu—" Making sure Filiore could see, Emilia reached into her collar and opened her palm, revealing the emblem clutched inside.

At once, the overflowing red radiance of the Dragon Jewel lit the drawing room of the noble mansion with blinding light.

"Like I said before, I had a reason I wanted to join the Royal Selection from the very start."

Showing off the dazzling glow of the Dragon Jewel, Emilia spoke as if for Filiore's ears alone.

Emilia's original motivation for joining the Royal Selection had been to borrow the power of the 'Dragon's Blood' kept in the royal castle, in order to save her frozen homeland. But time had passed, she had met people, come to know things, and the feelings she staked on the Royal Selection had begun to shift within her.

Even so, that first wish would surely never fade or vanish entirely, no matter how it changed.

And then—

"It's the same for everyone else. Crusch-san has her reason, Anastasia-san has hers, Felt-chan has hers... Priscilla has hers, each of them had something they wanted to do, and that's why they all joined the Royal Selection. No, wait. That's not quite right."

"Not right? What do you mean, not right?"

"I'm sure that the people who can make a Dragon Jewel glow all have a reason they want to join the Royal Selection—something they can't achieve any other way. So if that's true, then you must have one too."

"I, too...?"

Emilia smiled bravely and sweetly at Filiore, who blinked in a daze.

Filiore had proposed that, if she became a Royal Selection candidate, she'd support her friend Emilia's wish—but making that Emilia's wish stand in for her own didn't quite sit right with Emilia. Something felt off about it.

For a reason like that, Emilia couldn't accept her as one of the people joining this Royal Selection of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica.

"Please, don't just say you hate it because you're overwhelmed by how it felt when you first heard the news. Think about it really, really carefully. I'm sure they won't let you refuse to join the Royal Selection. But I don't want someone who joins just because they have no other choice to sit in Priscilla's seat."

—Those were strong words, a declaration of will, and something like a prayer, all at once.

"——"

Filiore blinked at Emilia, who had thrown the full weight of her feelings at her, face to face. In the tension building between the two of them, close enough to touch, no one else spoke.

Into the silence that was born, Subaru, and everyone else besides, was unwilling to insert themselves. —So that no unnecessary impurity would mix into whatever choice came next.

"—Yes."

The one who broke the surface of that silent pool was Emilia, who had been watching Filiore closest of anyone. She narrowed her eyes, framed by their long lashes, and held out the emblem in her hand to Filiore.

Filiore reflexively took what was offered to her. —Whether it was out of ignorance or not, the Dragon Jewel in the emblem did not choose the moment it would shine.

"—If I, supposing I do join the Royal Selection..."

Even as Filiore murmured this, the Dragon Jewel in her hand shone just as it had in Emilia's. Blinking with a light the same color as her own eyes, Filiore made her declaration.

Faced with the inescapable fate of the Royal Selection thrust upon her, what creed would she raise?

"I want to renew the relationship between the Kingdom and the Church, the way it's been up to now. So that people at the castle won't hesitate to reach out to the Church, and people at the Church won't hesitate to reach out to the castle, over some silly hang-up."

"——"

"If I become Queen, I'll make a country where no one hesitates to help someone in need."

It was, admittedly, nothing more than an off-the-rack ideal, still lacking a great deal of detail.

But it was, in Filiore's own way, a wish to break through and improve upon the constraints and frustrations she'd grown up feeling as a member of the Divine Dragon Church, living and sensing them all her life.

And no one who saw Filiore hold up that wish would laugh and call her someone forced onto a palanquin by those around her, joining the Royal Selection reluctantly just to fill out the numbers.

"...I really should have driven her off for showing up without an appointment."

Subaru heard Otto mutter that under his breath at the conclusion Filiore had reached.

Dressed lightly, without his hat or cloak, Otto ran his fingers idly through his gray, unruly hair as he offered his assessment of what had just happened here.

Honestly, Subaru understood the urge to say the same thing himself.

"A super-powerful rival showing up at this point, of all times."

"I think this whole thing is a laughably reckless act of aiding the enemy, honestly... And the fact that it's our own leader, of all people, who encouraged it—that's the real headache."

"Relax. The stronger the opponent, the more Emilia-tan just... you know, gets into some kind of a good groove."

"If you're trying to reassure me, could you at least say something better than that!?"

"Honestly, this time I actually feel for Otto, I suppose. But even so..."

Beatrice trailed off there, narrowing her eyes and staring, as if dazzled, straight ahead—at Emilia and Filiore—and Subaru felt exactly the same way.

Certainly, if you looked only at what had just happened, you'd have to call it a foolish move. He could even picture Roswaal, hearing the report later, comically collapsing to his knees.

But it had been worth it. In exchange for the arrival of a super-powerful rival, without question—

"—I won't lose to Filiore either. Not ever."

—Emilia, aiming to become Queen, had, without a doubt, shed another layer of her old self.

"—And so, I humbly accept the request to join the Royal Selection."

With one hand pressed to her chest and the other making her emblem flash brightly, Filiore declared this with grand, fearless confidence, as if she had nothing in the world to be reticent about.

The venue was the royal castle—not the throne room they were summoned to every time something happened, but a somewhat more compact meeting hall apparently used for smaller discussions. In a space about the size of a school classroom sat a round table where roughly twenty people could gather face to face; apparently, meetings of the Council of Wise Men and the castle's civil officials were usually held in this very hall.

"See, remember the day the Royal Selection started? After Subaru got kicked out of the room, everyone moved and talked things over—it was this meeting room."

"Ah, the one behind where I got chewed to bits by Julius."

"I still can't quite remember it, but... Subaru, did you and Julius have a fight? You did make up properly, right? Because you two are really, really close now."

"Whether me and that guy are really, really close is up for debate, but it was definitely a necessary event for me and Emilia-tan to become really, really close."

To Emilia's whispered explanation, Subaru replied with his lips twisted into a frown.

In this world where Julius's 'Name' had vanished, he had no interest in digging deep, even as a lesson to himself, into how his own humiliation that day was now remembered by those involved.

Fortunately, empty seats stood out in today's meeting room, meaning hardly anyone connected to that day was present. After all, this gathering was what one might call a top-secret conference; the number of people involved from each camp had been kept to a bare minimum—so much so that from the Emilia Camp, only Emilia, Subaru, and his partner Beatrice had been admitted.

That said, one of that very small handful of attendees—the elderly man with the distinctive long beard who had greeted them—also happened to be one of the people involved that very day.

That old man—Miklotov McMahon of the Council of Wise Men—watched Filiore, who had made what could only be called a refreshingly clean change of heart, and said:

"Hmm. That answer is a most welcome one for those of us who made the request... but might I ask again? I did grant permission, but why exactly did it come about that Lady Emilia and Master Natsuki would be joining you here?"

"This morning, before I came up to the castle like this, they were kind enough to hear me out. Emilia is my irreplaceable friend, and Subaru is her knight... Honestly, I would have liked to bring everyone from Emilia's camp who helped push me toward my declaration."

"If you'd actually done that, well, neither I nor the Church would have much standing left, would we?"

At Miklotov's question, Filiore answered, and Sakura—the only one accompanying her from the Church's side—spoke up with a troubled look on her face.

Filiore looked dissatisfied with that answer, but given that having the entire Emilia Camp crowd around on her big occasion would be strange, Sakura's take was surely the more reasonable one.

But whatever impression Subaru had drawn from that exchange, it seemed the part of Filiore's words that had caught on something for Miklotov, smoothing down his long beard, was different.

The wise elder narrowed his sagacious eyes, inserting his customary "Hmm" of a sigh—

"Your friend, is it. Lady Filiore of the Divine Dragon Church, and Lady Emilia."

"...Is that, like, one of those things? Like Emilia-tan being a silver-haired Half-Elf and Filiore being a nun of the Divine Dragon Church, and how could they possibly, still going on about that kind of thing?"

"Subaru, wait a moment..."

At the murmur that reached her ears, Emilia cautioned Subaru, who couldn't help but lean forward.

But here, there was no one else from the reliable ranks of their camp besides Subaru and Beatrice. So Subaru would have to speak up on everyone's behalf and give it to Miklotov straight.

As the First Knight entrusted with Emilia, he had to stand firm and fight against unreasonableness, no matter what.

"Miklotov-san, you should know how hard Emilia's worked this past year and a half. Knowing all that, all wrapped up together, I wish the important people of this country would stop looking at this girl through tinted glasses forever. Half-Elf or whatever, can't we just be done with that already?"

"To say 'be done with it' rather overlooks the sentiments of the populace. That there is, from the start, no common ground between the so-called 'Witch' and Lady Emilia beyond a shared appearance and race is a settled matter, plain to see... but that the two share even that much is, in itself, a very large thing. I trust you understand."

"Ngh..."

Miklotov, with the seasoned skill of a veteran talker, made Subaru—who'd pressed the attack fairly aggressively—shrink back. The problem here, once again, was the bedrock common sense of this world's inhabitants, something Subaru simply couldn't feel in his gut.

And that wasn't a matter limited to Emilia, the silver-haired Half-Elf, alone.

"Inevitably, that you, a nun of the Divine Dragon Church, are to become a Royal Selection candidate will be met by the populace with a great deal of shock. It bends convention considerably on our end as well. It was, I must say, an exceedingly troubling decision."

"...Yes, I believe I understand that much. But..."

"Hmm. But, what would that be?"

"—. But then, why go so far as to bring me into the Royal Selection at all?"

The question Filiore raised was, itself, a perfectly natural one to ask.

In the way Miklotov had just phrased things, one could glimpse a certain circumstance: that the castle's side, too, was by no means simply welcoming a Royal Selection candidate from the Divine Dragon Church with open arms and enthusiasm.

"I too am a nun of the Divine Dragon Church... I understand full well that strictly upholding what's written on the Dragon History Stone is only proper, but the fact remains that even after Priscilla Barielle's death, five people bearing the Maiden's qualification were gathered to begin the Royal Selection. That fact cannot be undone. The writing shouldn't be considered bent, should it?"

"Or has the writing on the Dragon History Stone itself been updated, perhaps? Are you telling me it's been rewritten to say that when one of the five Maidens is missing, a substitute Maiden should be found and promoted in her place?"

"No, we have received no such report that the Dragon History Stone's inscription has changed. However, on this matter, I should say it is less a question of our own intentions, and more a matter of the sentiments of the populace."

"The populace?"
"Sentiments?"

Subaru and Emilia both tilted their heads, question marks rising at Miklotov's answer. At that, Miklotov lowered his voice just slightly.

"This will eventually become public knowledge regardless, but please, keep it in strictest confidence even within this room. The truth is, a rumor is said to be circulating among the common people. —That the Divine Dragon Church's Saintess saved Duke Karsten."

"—!"

"The castle has enforced a gag order regarding this matter. Naturally, information tends to seep out like rain through a shuttered window no matter how tightly one seals it off... but it's even said, with real specificity, that this Saintess shares the same features as the missing Princess of Lugunica."

"That's basically the entire secret leaking out, isn't it!"

Miklotov's face clouded, as if lamenting a grievous blunder—but if this were true, it meant the castle's control of information had suffered an embarrassingly shoddy failure.

"I don't want to assume the worst, but, Filiore... you didn't, in your excitement over having saved Crusch-san, go around happily bragging about it to everyone, did you?"

"Have I convinced you, I suppose?"

"Don't! I didn't do any such thing! I have some sense of discretion, you know! Well—perhaps I mentioned it to the people close to me, since I wanted material to persuade the Church... but as if I'd go around blabbing about it in the streets!"

"Hmm, Beako?"

"I would call it a gray so close to black it might as well be black, I suppose."

Subaru more or less agreed with Beatrice's harsh assessment.

He felt bad for Filiore, stamping her feet in frustration—but no matter how insistently she claimed she hadn't done it, the impression she'd left up to this point was one where "she probably did something along those lines" was hard to shake entirely.

Regardless, whoever's fault the spread of that rumor actually was—

"So then, could it be that Filiore making her emblem glow has also become a rumor? And that's why everyone's upset that it would be strange not to add Filiore to the Royal Selection candidates?"

"It has not yet come that far. However, Lady Priscilla's death is to be officially announced to the public in the near future. Once that happens, if Lady Filiore's existence is already known, voices calling for her participation in the Royal Selection will naturally flood the streets."

"So before that happens, you're getting the groundwork laid out in advance, huh..."

If Filiore's entry into the game was already a foregone conclusion no matter what, then settling matters early on would keep things smoother for everyone involved—was that the idea? He could understand that sort of grown-up scheduling, but even so, thinking that Filiore truly had no right of refusal in the matter nagged at him a little.

If she hadn't had the chance to talk things over with Emilia beforehand, Filiore would have ended up joining as a Royal Selection candidate with the worst possible mindset—utterly lacking the resolve a candidate needed.

"Now, now, you mustn't scowl so hard, Subaru-chan. One way or another, things worked out just fine in the end, so isn't the process not so important?"

"...And you're fine with that, Sakura-san? Originally, Filiore wasn't even supposed to leave the Church, right? Joining the Royal Selection wasn't part of the plan at all, was it?"

"—Whether we wish it or not, a leaf has no choice but to be tossed about by the wind and the water."

"——"

At the sudden drop in Sakura's tone of voice, Subaru couldn't help but feel taken aback.

In that single line was a bone-deep sense of emptiness—like an old, half-withered tree hollowed out at its core—a vacant kind of resignation packed into her words.

Somehow, it felt less like she'd been talking about Filiore at all, and more like she'd let slip a resignation belonging to herself, to Sakura alone.

"Well, it's a case of things turning out how they turn out, that's all. Besides, even if the girl herself gets motivated now, she might not stand a chance competing against the other girls after falling a year and a half behind, you know."

"Hold on, Sakura! Why would you go and throw cold water on my motivation like that! Once I'm in, I intend to go for the win... my tenacity is nothing less than that of the 'Dragon' itself!"

"I got curious, but how does the Divine Dragon Church actually feel about someone like Filiore tossing the word 'Dragon' around so casually?"

"Impious, I would imagine."

"Impious!!"

It was a harsher answer than Subaru had expected, and it confirmed his suspicion that this really wasn't the recommended attitude. Filiore really did carry on a stance about as far removed from the general image of a Saintess as one could get.

In any case, with most of his questions answered, Subaru had a fairly good grasp now of the purpose of this top-secret conference, as well as why the attendees had been kept to such a bare minimum. In other words, this was, so to speak, a private notification confirming the intent and policy of the parties concerned, ahead of a full, formal public announcement.

And with that much settled in his mind, Subaru found one more thing nagging at him.

That was—

"Hey, so if Filiore's becoming a Royal Selection candidate too, does that mean she's going to name her own knight, like me and Emilia-tan, or the other candidates?"

"Ah, Subaru, that might be a misunderstanding."

"Misunderstanding how?"

"Well, it just happens that all the Royal Selection candidates right now have knights, but that's not an absolute rule either. I mean, Anastasia-san and I didn't have knight contracts with Subaru and Julius from the very start, right?"

"That's true for me, but Julius was Anastasia-san's knight from the very start."

"Ah, is that so. I need to remember that properly, for Julius's sake too."

While Emilia made a small correction for the gap left by the missing 'Name,' Subaru found himself convinced by the explanation regardless. Indeed, at the very start, Emilia had had Roswaal, her backer, accompanying her, but a knight in the proper sense—Subaru—had been absent, or rather, he simply hadn't yet earned that trust.

So perhaps a knight wasn't strictly necessary for a Royal Selection candidate after all.

"Still, I figure it adds a certain shine to have one, if you're going to bother. So what'll you do? Is there anyone around Filiore who could meet the requirements... My Friend, maybe?"

"My Frien—... oh, you mean Tiiga. But that might actually be nice. Filiore and Tiiga seem to get along really, really well too..."

"Ehh, my knight, Tiiga? That's not the least bit special, is it."

At Subaru's nomination, Emilia clasped her hands together in front of her chest in wholehearted agreement—but from Filiore herself came a wave of booing with almost unbelievable force.

"Sure, Tiiga's capable, so I'm sure he'd handle being named a knight without a hitch, but he nags too much, and he's always the first to suspect me the moment anything happens—don't you think he lacks the reverence and loyalty a knight needs? Besides, even without being a knight, he still tags along after me everywhere I go now, so there's no point at all in bothering to name him one specially. Rejected! I reject it!"

"Well, he's your knight, so I figure you should just do what you like about it... huh? Actually, come to think of it, how does someone normally get named a knight in the first place?"

Take Subaru, for instance—he'd been knighted by Emilia's own hand, settling directly into the role of her knight to arrive at his current position, but how had it gone for Reinhard, or for Julius?

Taking on a knight surely wasn't a matter limited to Royal Selection candidates alone, but as for how the contracts involved actually worked, Subaru felt a fresh pang of embarrassment at his own ignorance, even at this late stage.

"Hmm, let's see. In most cases, it is the knight who seeks out a lord, swearing loyalty in order to receive their appointment—that is the general pattern. That said, when it comes to a knight of great renown, it is not uncommon for one to promise a generous stipend and favorable treatment, and to beg and request that such a person come to serve them."

"Oh, wow, didn't expect Miklotov-san to actually explain that for us. ...By the way, do you have your own knights on retainer too, Miklotov-san?"

"My household has, over the years, many who come and go frequently, in whom I place my trust. As a general matter, one who has an excellent knight offer them their sword does tend to command a certain measure of respect."

"That's... well, I can see that."

In fact, the status of having made the 'Sword Saint' or the 'Most Excellent Knight' one's own First Knight had surely served as a considerable boost to the reputations of the names Felt and Anastasia, respectively.

A First Knight's renown was also an important barometer in the Royal Selection. —Was Subaru, as a knight, actually managing to be a source of strength for Emilia? And also, would Crusch, who had dismissed Ferris from the post of First Knight, be thinking of taking on a new knight going forward?

"——"

He hoped she wouldn't. Wishing for that, in the clinging way he did, was, in the eyes of Ferris herself as the party concerned, nothing but Subaru's arrogance—his habit of wanting everything to go his own way.

The unavoidable rift that had formed between the two of them—if enough time passed and cooler heads prevailed, maybe a different answer would present itself. So until then, he wished the seat could just stay vacant.

Subaru had been sinking into sentimentality just like that, resigned to his own arrogance—

"...Just now, Miklotov-sama said that, right? That for an excellent knight, one should raise one's voice and beg, even go and plead, to have them come serve you."

"—? The heat in that, compared to what Betty heard, seems awfully different, I suppose."

"...I felt like even the nuance of it had changed by that point, honestly."

Filiore's murmur, her fist clenched tight with force as she pressed down her emotions. Drawn in by Beatrice's reaction to it, Subaru too returned from sentiment to reality.

Whether she simply possessed ears remarkably convenient to her own liking, Filiore had reinterpreted Miklotov's statement in her own personal arrangement, and on top of that, her cheeks had gone faintly flushed. Something about the way her cheeks were warming up suggested her feelings were running rather high—

"Could it be, Filiore, that you already have someone in mind you'd like to make your knight?"

"—! How did you—... is it because we're best friends?"

"Yes, maybe so. So, what kind of person is it?"

"I'll say this up front, Reinhard's already sold out."

"Obviously, but Subaru's also permanently sold and promised to Betty and Emilia, I suppose."

Drawing the full attention of Subaru and the other two, as well as Sakura and Miklotov sharing the room, Filiore gave a "Fufufu," a smile heavy with some kind of meaning, and then, matching everyone's gazes, threw open the thick scripture in front of her with a flourish.

"Listen closely. The scripture says as much. 'To wield the sword is the arm of man; to command life is the will of the Dragon. The footsteps of the chosen knight, even the ruts left by blood, shall become a road of silver.'"

"Um, and that means...?"

"In other words, the one fit to be my knight is someone recognized by the 'Dragon'!"

Though Subaru sensed a rather willfully self-serving interpretation at work, in the end, when it came down to a knight worthy enough to be recognized by the 'Dragon,' he genuinely couldn't think of anyone besides Reinhard who fit the bill.

Stretching the definition generously enough, one might argue Emilia—who had cleared the first-floor 'Trial' of the Pleiades Watchtower—also satisfied that condition, in a manner of speaking.

"A development where one Royal Selection candidate becomes the knight of another Royal Selection candidate has a nice twisty, interesting ring to it, but Emilia-tan already has me, so that development's a bust."

"Emilia, my knight... c-certainly, that is a proposal that does tug at the heart. But my hopes and my expectations are two different things. Now, stop trying to lead me astray."

Whether to tell her not to waver over something so small, or to say that of course she'd waver if the comparison was Emilia—unable to decide between the two, Subaru said nothing to Filiore either way.

Oblivious to Subaru's considerateness, Filiore gave a small cough and continued.

"Are you listening? Are you ready? As I said, if I, the Divine Dragon Church's Saintess, am to stand as a Royal Selection candidate, then my knight must be one recognized by the 'Dragon.' And, as far as I am aware, there is only one person who meets that condition—"

Once that title was set before them, the figure who inevitably rose to mind could be only one man.

Ironically, it was someone who, far from merely contending with Filiore for the throne, was already fated to fight over which of them was the true surviving royal blood of Lugunica.

The First Knight who served Felt, the 'Sword Saint' Reinhard van Astrea—could be no one else but—

"—Lord Heinkel Astrea."

"Hnn!?"

As if he'd been fed the wrong answer, an ugly sound caught in Subaru's throat despite himself. But Filiore, having declared it, wore a smug, triumphant look, while beside him, Emilia and Beatrice's eyes had gone just as wide, showing the same open shock as Subaru's.

An Astrea was an Astrea, sure, but it was the wrong Astrea from the one everyone had been thinking of.

"...Uh, Filiore?"

"Wh-what's the matter? I mean, everyone's, well, everyone's reacting kind of strangely, aren't they?"

"'Kind of strangely' doesn't begin to cover it. I'm begging you, say it one more time. Who's the one fit to be the knight of the Divine Dragon Church's Saintess, again?"

"Honestly, I wish you wouldn't make me repeat myself so many times, but... Lord Heinkel Astrea."

No matter how many times he heard it, the name refused to come out sounding any different, and Filiore tilted her head at that.

From her point of view, it was Subaru and the others' reaction that made no sense at all. And that made sense, in its own way—because in Filiore's mind, the man named Heinkel Astrea was—

"—One who overcame the 'Dragon's Blood' that coursed through his body and became an undying knight, one of the bloodline of the 'Sword Saint.' I want to name Lord Heinkel Astrea, of all people, as my knight."

Yes—Filiore, the sixth Royal Selection candidate, was declaring, in no uncertain terms, that the sixth candidate's very own First Knight ought to be none other than the 'Sword-Forsaker' himself, Heinkel Astrea.
