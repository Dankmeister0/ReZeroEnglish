She was born blessed with extraordinary looks and natural dexterity, but beyond that there was nothing else particularly noteworthy about her. Not something engineered by the Witch of Melancholy—rather, luck itself, the kind Aldebaran, by rights, should almost never have—luck he thought he'd used up entirely on his encounter with Priscilla—had come into play.

"—!"

Only for an instant does he stand stunned; then, battered by wind and water, Aldebaran strains his eyes through the fog-shrouded battlefield, searching.

Not the Witch of Melancholy. Not Ferris. Not the fallen Aldebusters.

The one and only, final move Aldebaran might still make on this battlefield.

With 'Death' sealed and his comrades gone, the last method by which Aldebaran—together with the 'Witch' who remains only within himself now—can fulfill the cherished wish they set out to achieve.

That was—

"—Roy!! If you're there, come here! Eat me... eat my 'Name'!!"

Recklessly altering the curse-mark's conditions, feeling the Od crack yet again under the massive strain, ready to make his very soul cough up blood, Aldebaran screams.

Reset this situation to zero—the one and only strategy left available even to Aldebaran, with his Authority sealed.

By having his 'Name' devoured, vanishing from everyone's memory, he'll overturn everything.

To that end, Aldebaran searches through the roiling cloud of dust for that hateful figure of Gluttony, and screams.

"Eat me! My—my 'Name' is—"

The being named Aldebaran, forced to bear the fate of the star that trails after.

The one whose destiny was to remove Natsuki Subaru from the world and fulfill the 'Witch's' cherished wish—and yet who, despite carrying that fate, yearned for the sun regardless, and as a result, a good-for-nothing who lost everything.

That 'Name' was—


"Rigel. —Natsuki Rigel!!"

That was the 'Name' of the foolish star who betrayed the 'Witch's' expectations, and let the sun set.
