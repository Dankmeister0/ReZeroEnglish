Arc 1 Part 1: "A Serrated Ten-Yen Coin is Useless"


—This has gotten seriously bad.


Penniless and at a loss, his mind was completely filled with that single thought.

To say he was penniless wasn't entirely accurate. His wallet was in his pocket, and except for having rather too many coins and too few bills, it was undoubtedly his entire fortune.

It was a pocketbook with enough leeway to go to the nearest shopping mall from his hometown, do some shopping at a bookstore, and grab some lunch. Nevertheless, there was no choice but to describe it as penniless.

After all,

"As I thought, the value of currency is totally different..."

Flicking the ten-yen coin in his hand—a rare "serrated ten-yen"—with his finger, the boy let out a long sigh.

He was a boy without any particularly defining features. Short black hair, and an average height that was neither tall nor short. Perhaps because he worked out, his build was somewhat muscular, which, combined with his cheap gray tracksuit, gave him a sportsman-like appearance.

Only his sharp, sanpaku eyes left an impression, but right now even the corners of his eyes drooped powerlessly, devoid of any ambition.

His appearance was so mediocre that if he blended into a crowd, you would lose sight of him in an instant.

However, the gazes of the people looking at him were thick with a baffling hue, as if they were looking at some "bizarre curiosity."

It was only natural, really—after all, among those staring at the boy, not a single one had "black hair" or wore a "tracksuit."

Their hair ranged from blonde, white, and brown to green and blue, and furthermore, their attire—armor, dancer-like outfits, jet-black robes—looked far too stereotypically fantasy.

Exposed to waves of blunt stares, the boy folded his arms, with no choice but to accept it.

"So, this is that, huh?"

Snapping his fingers, he pointed them at the people watching him and said,

"—It seems I've been summoned to another world."

In front of him, a carriage-like vehicle pulled by a giant lizard-like creature passed by.

His life had spanned seventeen years, and to tell the whole story would require exactly seventeen years.

Leaving all that aside, to explain his current situation simply, he was a "third-year high school shut-in."

To explain in detail, he was a "worthless piece of trash who, despite having college entrance exams right around the corner, threw away his parents' expectations and everything else to lock himself in his own shell."

There was no particular reason why he became a shut-in.

On an ordinary weekday, he had coincidentally thought, "Getting up today is too much trouble," and acted on the urge to play hooky, which was the initial trigger.

Dragging his feet, his self-imposed school absences accumulated, and before he knew it, he had fully become a shut-in who made his parents weep.

Day in and day out, he wallowed in laziness, remaining submerged in the internet with zero communication—

"And the result of that is an isekai summoning... Even saying it myself makes no damn sense."

Reconfirming his situation once more, Subaru let out another sigh, having lost count of how many times he had done so.

Moving away from the street where he had been showered with curious stares just moments ago, he was now sitting down in a slightly dim back alley.

The ground was paved, and though it was a sloppy job compared to modern Japan, it wasn't bad.

"Assuming the current situation is an isekai fantasy, is the civilization the standard medieval-style? From what I saw, there's no machinery, and the building materials are almost entirely stone or wood..."

Recalling the sights he had seen before sitting down in the alley, he organized the information in his mind.

Thanks to having plenty of time to indulge in delusions on a daily basis, his mental preparation for being "summoned to another world" was excellent.

First, he needed to calmly assess the civilization of this era, the differences in food, clothing, and shelter compared to modern Japan, differences in physical phenomena, and determine whether contact with the inhabiting "humanoid creatures" was possible.

"Alright, nice going, me. I didn't spend all that time daydreaming for nothing. Confirming the civilization level is good enough for now. First off, my money doesn't work here. But I managed to talk to a shopkeeper, so communication isn't an issue either."

Upon realizing he had been summoned, the first thing Subaru did was attempt to negotiate with a "grocer?" He had tried to buy some "apples?" lined up at the storefront, only to have his Japanese yen rejected.

From what he could see at the time, the currency in this world seemed to consist of gold, silver, and copper coins. The ease of understanding a world where the currency itself holds value was very typical of an isekai fantasy, to say the least.

"Well, it'll probably decline once counterfeits, debased coins, or stuff like 500-won coins start showing up, though."

*It'd be too heavy to carry around, too,* he murmured to himself as another carriage pulled by a lizard passed by the street. A massive cloud of dust kicked up, but the passersby seemed accustomed to it, remaining completely indifferent.

"Still, their numbers are few compared to cars. ...Come to think of it, I haven't seen any dogs or cats yet."

The giant lizard pulling the "carriage?" was probably a size larger than a horse. Though its overall mass didn't seem much different due to its slender build, a reptile that large felt incredibly out of place.

"It must be... commonplace. Both the lizards and the way the humans look."

And then there was the part he had left for last: the unusual appearance of humans in this world.

He could accept the colorful hair. If you dyed it, you could basically have any hair color anyway, and he had already made peace with that since this was an isekai fantasy.

What he viewed as problematic was a different aspect—for instance, "beast ears."

From a quick glance around, he had spotted "dog ears" and "cat ears." There were also "bunnies," and as for unusual specimens, he felt like he had caught a glimpse of something like a "lizardman."

But then there were also humans who looked no different from Subaru, so his conclusion regarding all this was—

"The genre is isekai fantasy. The civilization is typical medieval. Demi-humans are definitely a thing, and there's probably war and adventure too. There are minor differences in animals, but no change in their roles—I guess that about sums it up."

Having sorted out that much, Subaru let out a long breath that was different from a sigh.

Speaking his situation aloud, his brow furrowed at such a convenient development.

If things went according to his delusional scenarios, he would now make full use of his modern knowledge to go "I'm OP," but this world setting was slightly different from the ones where his prepared knowledge would be useful.

"My simulation for if I got sent back to the Warring States period was perfect, though... If I'd been sent there, I would've helped Nobunaga conquer the country."

In an isekai fantasy, the only useful knowledge he had was at most the recipe for black gunpowder.

Even that would be pointless depending on this world's civilization level. If "magic," a staple of isekai fantasies, existed here, gunpowder would probably be treated as nothing more than fireworks.

"Well, it's also a kind of cliché that magic isn't omnipotent. If there's a chance to contribute to scientific advancement, I'll do my best, but... the immediate problem is..."

The point that he had absolutely no idea why or for what purpose he was summoned to this other world.

He remembered the events before the summoning clearly. He had stepped out of his house for the first time in a while and was on his way back after buying instant ramen for a late-night snack at a convenience store. He hadn't felt like riding his bicycle, so he went on foot.

And on the way, he even remembered suddenly looking up at the night sky and thinking, *It's one step away from a full moon tonight.*

Then, he lowered his gaze, blinked, and before he knew it, it was daytime.

From night to day in an instant.

He immediately knew an abnormal situation had occurred. Though that was the only thing he knew.

Although he was calm now, his panic immediately afterward had been a display of cowardice that deserved to be passed down through generations.

"Well, if I'm the eldest son and I go missing, there won't be any generations to pass it down to anyway."

Muttering to himself, Subaru checked his belongings once again.

In his current situation in an isekai fantasy, starter equipment was more important than he had ever imagined. Right now, the fact that he was connected by even the thinnest of threads was what mattered.

First, a flip phone (battery almost dead), a wallet (filled with rental video store membership cards), the instant cup ramen he bought at the convenience store (tonkotsu soy sauce flavor), a bag of snack chips (corn potage flavor), his beloved gray tracksuit (unwashed), and well-worn sneakers (two years old). That was it.

"I'm screwed... Why don't I even have a pistol? What am I supposed to do?"

The only useful thing would be the snacks to fill his belly. Though they would only satisfy his hunger temporarily, and then it would be over.

"The situation is hopeless. And as expected, the cause is unknown. I don't remember stepping through a mirror or falling into a pond, and above all, if this is a summoning story, where's the beautiful girl who summoned me?"

So to speak, the main heroine was absent. From the perspective of the two-dimensional world, this was an unacceptable dereliction of duty.

To be summoned and then left aimlessly abandoned made him feel like he had been used and dumped.

Indeed, having finished checking his current situation, Subaru could not even escape reality and could only hang his head.

"Seriously, give me a break. What do you want me to do?"

Weak complaints and whines spilled out, and he was already on the verge of breaking. He wanted to go home, he thought, over and over.

Daydreams were fine as daydreams. Summoning to another world was only fun when you were unmatched and going "I'm OP" inside your head; if you were actually thrown into one, what could you do other than shrink back in fear?

"Anyway, my objective for the time being is to survive... but can I get by with my communication level at 1?"

Speaking properly with someone—excluding his family, his only contact had been with convenience store clerks. He had continued that kind of life for nearly a year. He had long forgotten how to gauge the distance between people.

"If it were online chat, I could type at the same speed as talking, though..."

Wiggling his fingers, Subaru felt anxious about the future.

Then, his expression changed. The reason was a sound.

Footsteps suddenly echoed in the back alley—looking up, three men stood at the entrance of the alley, blocking his path.
