First was this: keep playing Filore to hold off the "insurance"—the safeguard that might trigger whether or not Roy was watching. On top of that, she needed to search for a chance to catch Aldebaran and his whole group off guard.

Deep in the battlefield, the one who'd called her name so loudly was Old Man Rom—a figure she'd already come to miss after only two or three days apart. Not Old Man Rom's eyes. The pale-blue eyes of the girl perched on his shoulder.

He must have felt the presence of that terrifying Witchbeast pressing in close enough to sting, and yet the eyes Felt turned on him held no fear at all—only trust—and that set Old Man Rom's heart ablaze.

If this power had been at hand in the many battles Old Man Rom—Valga Cromwell—had fought over the years, the tactics, the outcomes, even history itself might have turned out completely different.

Simply witnessing the power of an Authority, simply feeling a Witch Factor so close at hand, was enough to summon such "what ifs" unbidden. —Even so, something was wrong.

Human beings couldn't act this optimized, not to this degree.

And yet, against every single attack, Felt and the others kept playing the single most effective card from their hand, at exactly the right moment.

Roy knew this feeling. It was Aldebaran, from the fight at the Prison Tower.

After breaking the seal on the imprisoned Roy, Aldebaran had beaten him black and blue just to demonstrate the gap in their power. That same baffling helplessness, being shut down completely without so much as a chance to fight back—it was like that. Close, but different. That had been the abnormality of Aldebaran alone. This, though, was the abnormality of the entire enemy force.

—Clearly, something here was off.

"—Ahh, I read that wrong."

The out-of-the-ordinary that defies common sense; the beyond-reason that logic can't explain; the beyond-argument not even worth discussing.

Faced with all these abnormal circumstances, his starving stomach on the verge of being filled up with nothing but questions, Roy arrived at a fact so obvious it made him curse himself.

Between that spirited battle cry and everything else, he'd been made to believe Felt was the enemy's core.

Even that manipulation of his thinking had been part of their plan. Realizing the true central figure wasn't Felt, but the mastermind hiding behind her, Roy turned his eyes that way.

The instant he did, the mastermind blinked, letting out a soft "Ah"—

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△

—Gods, buddhas, Od Lagna-sama. I vow that for the rest of my life, I will never wish to mend a broken relationship.
—Gods, buddhas, Od Lagna-sama. I vow that for the rest of my life, I will never get angry on anyone else's behalf.
—Gods, buddhas, Od Lagna-sama. I vow that for the rest of my life, I will never reveal a secret to anyone.
—Gods, buddhas, Od Lagna-sama. I vow that for the rest of my life, I will never show my weakness to anyone.
—Gods, buddhas, Od Lagna-sama. I vow that for the rest of my life, I will never watch a sunrise with anyone.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△

"—Could it be you've finally noticed?"

So responded the "Witch of Melancholy," who had "Compressed" both the thought processes of every ally present and the discussions needed to share them—and she closed one eye in a wink.
