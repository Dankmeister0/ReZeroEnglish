"—Hey, do you know my name?"

At that question, the young girl, Amyu Sears, drew a quiet breath.

It was an ordinary, mundane question.
It should have been a tedious inquiry that required neither time nor resolve to answer.
If she knew, she knew. If she didn't, she didn't. That was all it took.

"――――"

Yet, faced with a question that was so simple on paper, Amyu could not squeeze out a voice.

Life is a series of choices.
That was a truth Amyu had come to learn well enough through real experience in her short existence, even having just turned fourteen.

In life, one had to make decisions about everything.
They ranged from trivial matters of daily routine to monumental choices that could alter the course of a life. Regardless of scale, life was entirely constructed of decisions.

And right now, what had been put to Amyu Sears was a question carrying the greatest weight of her fourteen years of life—and perhaps the single greatest decision she would ever make.
It was that very question posed just moments ago, lacking any special air, entirely tedious.

"—Hey, do you know my name?"

The repeated question tightened around Amyu's throat.
However, the person posing the question did not seem to have any malicious intent. Repeating the question once more felt almost like an act of kindness—a consideration that felt strangely out of balance.

If the person tormenting Amyu was the questioner, the person most concerned for Amyu was also the questioner.
She could tell that all he truly sought was the answer to his question.
Precisely because she understood that, Amyu had no clues, no choice but to blindly search for the correct option, uncovering it through dialogue with her inner self.

—What, ultimately, was the right answer?

Knowing it, or not knowing it—which one aligned with the questioner's expectations?
Or, even if she didn't know, would it be better to answer that she did? Even if she knew, would it be better to answer that she didn't? —Confronted with an unfree binary choice, Amyu's heart screamed.

"—Hey, do... you... know... my... name?"

As if growing impatient, the segments of the repeated question were broken down even further.
Had he worried that his words weren't coming through? That anxiety scratched at the inside of Amyu's chest. She could be certain of one thing: failing to answer either "yes" or "no" would absolutely never meet the questioner's intent.

Without saying anything, without producing an answer, without making a decision, she would not be allowed to end this.
She would not be released. —Of that much, she was certain.

"――――"

Unable to utter a sound, Amyu stared back into the dark eyes directly in front of her and pondered.
Inside those hollow-looking dark eyes, she could see her own haggard, terrible face reflected.

Though she wouldn't say it out loud, her appearance—which she had considered reasonably presentable—had no trace of its former self left, cowering and frozen purely in terror of the questioner's overwhelming dominance before her.
As if she had aged decades in an instant, Amyu's face was utterly exhausted.
So weak that she felt as if she might die right here, purely from the pressure of the question—

"—Hey, do you know my name?"

Suddenly, that pressure evoking death unexpectedly filled Amyu with hope.
Experiencing a suffocating, chest-crushing sensation, a premonition took hold of her: by answering the question, she might be released from it.
The moment she thought that, she could feel the heavy, stifling sensation weighing on her entire body begin to thin.

The suffocating pressure brought on by the paranoia of wondering what she ought to answer. Desperate to be freed from it, she swallowed down her anxiety toward the initial question.
Perhaps it was a form of self-defense instinct, but to Amyu at this moment, it felt like divine revelation. Thus, her lips trembled, and she looked back into those dark eyes once more.

"—Hey, do you know my name?"

Answering the question would free her from the breathlessness of this moment.
With that single thought, Amyu finally moved her twitching tongue and spun her words.

"N-No..."

*I don't know*, Amyu answered, following her heart.
Detaching her thoughts from the choices that had been spinning endlessly in her head until now, she answered the question from a place of pure sincerity.

In truth, she knew neither the name nor the face of the questioner before her.
To her, living in a remote, backwater place—what one would call the frontier—even major events in the far-off Kingdom that she heard rumors about felt like occurrences in a distant world.
So no matter how grand a figure he might be, to her, he was an unfamiliar stranger—

"—I see."

It was a brief word.
She could not distinguish whether that single word held deep emotion or disappointment.
Regardless, Amyu's decision had been made. What remained was a matter for the questioner.

If life is a continuation of choices and decisions, that held true for everyone.
Just as Amyu had chosen, the questioner would also choose.

"――――"

Silently, the fourteen-year-old girl waited for the questioner's choice.


—All alone in the middle of a village where no one besides herself remained, she waited for the answer.

'Hey, Subaru. Isn't it about time for lunch?'

Stopping in his tracks at that call, Natsuki Subaru rubbed his stomach.
The time was already past noon, and now that it was brought up, he felt reasonably hungry. The reason he hadn't noticed until mentioned was that he had been focused on walking mindlessly.
The highway was so long, stretching on endlessly, that he had to concentrate just to keep walking. Thinking about what lay ahead, it was a dauntingly long journey that made his heart heavy.

"Still, breaks are important, aren't they? My bad, Emilia-tan. Did I tire you out?"

'No, I'm completely fine! But I was just worried about you, Subaru, walking non-stop. If you're okay, then that's fine...'

"No way, I was just getting famished myself. My stomach was practically sticking to my back. Whew, close call, close call."

'Is that so? That sounds super dangerous...'

Hearing Subaru's response as he exaggeratedly clutched his belly, the silver-haired girl—Emilia—placed a hand to her mouth and giggled.
With a voice like a silver bell, the most adorable girl in the world smiled. Watching that smile up close, Subaru scratched his head and let his own lips curl upward.
And then, from beside the two of them—

'Don't tease Emilia so much, I suppose. No matter how hungry you are, there's no way your stomach and back could stick together, I suppose.'

"Whoops."

Turning toward the voice, Beatrice stood there with her short arms crossed and a composed expression. At the colorful-dressed girl's point, Subaru narrowed his eyes with a gentle chuckle.
Seeing that, Beatrice raised her well-shaped eyebrows sharply, asking, 'What is it?'

'That face of yours, Subaru, it's bothering me, I suppose. What's wrong?'

"I just thought you saying 'hungry' was cute. Say it one more time."

'Gehh, I suppose! Don't get hung up on strange things, I suppose!'

Red-faced, Beatrice snapped at Subaru's useless banter. Watching Beatrice like that, Subaru and Emilia looked at each other and laughed.
Seeing them, Beatrice puffed her cheeks out even more adorably. —This feeling was undoubtedly genuine. And he knew that telling her directly would surely sour her mood.
After confirming that, Subaru said, "Alright," rolling up his sleeves.

"Then I'll gladly take Emilia-tan up on her concern and make it mealtime. I'll show off my cooking skills today. Any requests?"

'Ah, then I'd like you to use mayonnaise! See, Ram just remade a fresh batch of mayonnaise for us, right?'

"Yes, yes! Anything for Emilia-tan's order! Luckily, thanks to Ram, we have plenty in stock. She's finally realized the charm of mayonnaise..."

'—Stop talking nonsense.'

"Whoa!?"

Unloading the luggage he carried, Subaru began setting up camp off the edge of the highway. Seeing slender feet suddenly stepping beside him, a crimson pair of eyes looked down at the startled Subaru.
A valiant posture, arms folded. Though clad in a maid outfit, it belonged to a girl with a gaze completely devoid of any spirit of hospitality—

"What an abrupt greeting, Ram. At least let me finish my sentence."

'Hmph! Don't make me laugh. The only reason Ram lends a hand in making mayonnaise is because it is an official order. Me, opening my heart to a sour, white condiment like that? Horrifying.'

"Horrifying is going way too far! Besides, even you can't deny the destructive power of putting mayonnaise on steamed potatoes! I won't let you call that flavor a lie!"

The taste of piping-hot steamed potatoes infused with salt, slathered generously with mayonnaise.
That sweet, forbidden flavor that dominated the tongue was a paradise that anyone who appreciated mayonnaise ought to experience at least once—an ultimate compatibility between potatoes and mayonnaise that could never fade.
Recalling that exquisite harmony and blend of flavors, Subaru's throat gulped. Right beside him, perhaps imagining the same taste, he saw Emilia's and Beatrice's eyes sparkle.

"Look at the reaction of these two adorable foodies! Can you still slander mayonnaise as sacrilege after seeing that?!"

'...I see. It is just as Barusu says. The greatness of steamed potatoes manages to grant value even to an dubious condiment like mayonnaise.'

"Not that way, it's the opposite! Well, not that it's strictly opposite either, but you're stubborn, Sister!"

Ram would not yield, insisting that it was the contribution of the steamed potatoes, not the mayonnaise, that was great.
At her stubborn stance, Subaru shook his fists, but with a wry smile, Emilia stepped in between the two of them, saying, 'Now, now.'

'Subaru, calm down. I like steamed potatoes, mayonnaise, and dishes with mayonnaise on steamed potatoes, both of them. Besides, Ram is just being a little tsundere.'

'...Emilia-sama, please do not assume you can speak for Ram's thoughts on your own. The way you put it makes Ram's mental age sound lower.'

'Eh, sorry. But what do you mean by mental age...?'

Emilia looked bewildered at Ram's unreasonable and difficult phrasing.
Yet, what seeped through their exchange was not hostility, but an undeniable feeling of trust and affection.
Contrary to appearances, Emilia and Ram had built an ideal relationship. Calling it a master-servant dynamic was slightly problematic because Ram lacked any awareness of being a servant, but a bond greater than that existed between them.
Subaru, more than anyone, knew that very well.
At any rate—

"Sorry, Sister, but Emilia-tan's words take priority over everything. That's the style of this party. Which means today's lunch is officially all-mayonnaise!"

'Yay, hooray!'

As Emilia clapped her hands happily, Ram let out a exasperated sigh. Still, the fact that she didn't press the matter any further showed that Ram, too, was plenty soft on Emilia.
Of course, since Subaru was a purebred mayo-lover, this menu choice was pure favoritism.

"Naturally, Beako loves mayonnaise just like me, right? Happy, aren't you?"

'I won't deny it, I suppose. But speaking like you've seen right through me is irritating, I suppose. Betty's anger won't be cleared so easily, I suppose. I demand a meal that's delicious enough to make up for it, I suppose.'

"Heheh, as you wish~"

Puffing out her thin chest, Beatrice made her adorable request, and Subaru gave a bow. Seeing his theatrical attitude make Emilia and the others look satisfied, he set about preparing the meal.
That said, one couldn't do anything overly elaborate when cooking outdoors. Of course, using magic power allowed a certain degree of fire and water manipulation, but—

'Sorry, Subaru. I can't help you properly.'

"No need to apologize. This is where I get to show off my resourcefulness, right? On the contrary, feel free to fall in love with me all over again for my unexpected creativity."

'Gosh, you dummy.'

Smiling back at Emilia's subtle smile, Subaru used the available ingredients to add some flair to the meal.
Food was daily vitality. He didn't want to cut corners here. Checking the bag's contents, Subaru poured his heart into preparing a meal that could accommodate everyone's requests as much as possible.

Every single member traveling with him was someone who ate food with a delicious expression on their face.
He hadn't thought about it much before, but having someone enjoy the food he cooked was a joyful thing. It served as motivation for the cook as well.

If it was something this simple, he should have conveyed his joy to his mother much more properly.

'Hum-hum-hum...'

Keeping that sentiment in a corner of his mind, Subaru softened his cheeks at the sight of Emilia and the others looking happily over his hands as he cooked. He could even hear humming. She was tone-deaf.

'My, so big brother is on cooking duty today~? I'm a little worried~'

As he was preparing the meal, Meili popped her head out. Twirling her dark blue braid with a finger, she directed a coquettish gaze toward Subaru that belied her age. Facing that alluring glare, Subaru looked back with a suspicious eye.

"What's with the 'worried', huh, 'worried'? I won't feed kids who say cheeky things like that."

'No way~ That's abuse~ Besides, big brother tries to slather that mayonnaise stuff on everything, don't you~? I don't really like that stuff very much~'

"Wh-What did you say...?! You too... don't understand the charm of mayonnaise...?!"

'Don't make a face like it's the end of the world~'

At Subaru's flabbergasted expression, Meili sighed in exasperation. But the shock Subaru received from her response was immeasurable.
Even if it wasn't the end of the world, he felt a shock equivalent to finding out it was suddenly the end of the year.

'Even so, you are taking it somewhat too dramatically. Miss Meili is merely saying she cannot keep pace with your attitude of trying to match mayonnaise with every single ingredient.'

"Mgh..."

Meili, who showed a stance of rejecting mayonnaise, had her statement backed up by Julius, a handsome man whose very walking posture seemed elegant.
Approaching them, Julius offered a subtle smile to Subaru and added:

'To supplement, I am of the same mind as Miss Meili. Mayonnaise itself is not a bad thing, but if served alongside everything, the initial impression fades. Exercise some restraint.'

"You're a nitpicking, noisy guy, aren't you? This was between me and Meili, so I didn't need you inserting yourself into it."

'Maintaining a healthy environment for the group is a duty of its members. Should a seed of discord be overlooked, resulting in a bud that blossoms, one could not regret it enough. I consider it my obligation to offer counsel before that happens, but?'

"You always have a comeback for everything, don't you?!"

Julius poked at Subaru's rashness with roundabout phrasing every time. Nearly bested by his words and actions, Subaru raised his voice irritably.
Listening to their exchange, Emilia burst out laughing with a 'Fufu', and—

"Emilia-tan?"

'As usual, Subaru and Julius get along super well.'

At Emilia's gaze, which looked upon them as if watching something heartwarming, Subaru dropped his shoulders in exhaustion. Julius, in the same position, closed one eye while touching his bangs.
Only at this timing did Subaru and Julius share the same opinion, but there seemed to be a considerable gap between their opinion and how Emilia received it.
Because in her jewel-like, purple-blue eyes, the exchange between Subaru and Julius just now appeared to be a scene of close friends getting along fine.

"Listen, Emilia-tan. I always tell you, that's..."

'That's?'

That was, no matter how you looked at it, a massive misunderstanding.
Subaru tried to make an excuse to Emilia, who tilted her head, but—

"That's..."

'――――'

The words of excuse he tried to follow up with suddenly got stuck, and Subaru's tongue stiffened.
Instantly, Emilia's movement—smiling and tilting her head right before him—came to a complete stop. —No, it wasn't just Emilia who stopped.
All around Subaru as he cooked, the members who had been chatting harmoniously until a moment ago stopped dead in their tracks, exactly as they were, completely frozen.

—As if the world had stopped time, waiting for Subaru's next reaction.

However, the sound of the wind, the scent of the grass, and the boiling water told him that wasn't the case.
The world hadn't stopped. —It was only Emilia and the others who were frozen.

As if waiting for Subaru's mental image trying to reproduce them to catch up.
Only Emilia and the others were frozen—

"—Master~! I took a quick look up ahead, I did~!"

"――――"

Suddenly, an intruder broke into that still, silent world.
Sitting on a large rock tending the fire, a jumping figure leaped vigorously into the space right in front of Subaru.

"Shaula has returned with a landing, she has! Master, praise me, hug me, love me, you know~!"

Saying that, the person offering a full smile to Subaru was a tall woman with beautiful facial features.
Her long brown hair was tied into a single ponytail, boldly exposing healthy, white skin. Unsparingly showing off a body rich in feminine curves, she—Shaula—wore a carefree smile.

"――――"

However, faced with Shaula's full-throated expression of affection, Subaru's reaction was poor. And no wonder: the position she had leaped into was the very spot where Emilia had been smiling just a moment ago.
Her noisy, boisterous leap blew Emilia away, erasing even the other frozen figures. At that fact, Subaru knitted his brows.

"A-Ah, crazy? Wh-What's wrong, Master? Could it be I did something wrong again, I did?"

"...No, it's nothing. Don't worry about it. It's not your fault."

"Is that so, you know? Then I won't worry, I won't! Ah, more importantly, Master, you're preparing food? That makes me so happy! I was super famished, I was~"

Shaula's face had clouded over for a moment at Subaru's expression, but she immediately took Subaru's cover-up at face value, clinging to Subaru's arm with a face that had forgotten her previous anxiety.
Feeling the intense softness of Shaula's body pressed against his arm through his elbow and shoulder, Subaru nevertheless brushed her off coldly, saying, "Hey, get off."

"Nnn! Master, you're so mean, you know... But I won't give up! At this rate, I'm gonna charge right in and snipe Master's heart, desires, and manly soul, I will!"

"Sounds like all those targets are right on my centerline... Also, don't grab the arm of someone who's cooking. What if I cut my hand or burn myself?"

"When that happens, I'll gently lick the hurt spot for you! I'll lick and lick 24/7 and never let go!"

"That's just gonna turn my skin soggy..."

Shaula asserted herself, throwing her hands up in the air with pure momentum rather than substance. At Shaula's attitude, Subaru dropped his shoulders and gave a weak laugh.
Seeing that, Shaula raised her eyebrows with a "Oh-oh!"

"Did Master just smile, you know? Was there something funny?"

"The funny thing was you. You."

"Eh, is that... a marriage proposal, you know...?"

"It definitely isn't!?"

He had no idea how the logic had leaped, but Subaru landed a flick on Shaula's forehead—containing both protest and denial—as she blushed and fidgeted.
Receiving it, Shaula groaned an "Awoo~", and—

"Because, because! 'You're an interesting woman' is the classic, textbook interaction for a man and woman falling in love, isn't it?! What comes after that has to be 'Become my woman', right?!"

"Where on earth do you get this biased knowledge of yours? That's a pattern so worn-out you can barely even find it in my hometown anymore..."

In shojo manga or games aimed at women, male characters using such first-encounter events might not have been uncommon, but Subaru didn't know if that held true today.
At any rate, finding Shaula amusing wasn't for the sake of hitting on her.
It was just that Shaula's reactions, to Subaru—

"—What you say is unpredictable. It's fresh."

"――――"

"So talking with you is... not bad. That much is true."

He wouldn't say that talking to anyone other than Shaula was painful.
It was just that whenever he conversed with anyone besides Shaula, Subaru was naturally made aware of his own inadequacies time and again, and that was painful.
No one was at fault. —The one at fault was always himself.

"The bad one is always me. So..."

"In other words, this time it really is a proposal, you know?!"

"I told you it isn't!"

The mood to steer into a serious conversation didn't even last seconds, and Shaula exploded with dissatisfaction at the yelling Subaru. Flailing her arms and legs, she went, "Ehhh~", and—

"But, but! The plot where a fresh breeze blows into a boring, gray life is totally the classic of classics, isn't it~?"

"No, I mean, seriously, where is this spring of biased knowledge gushing out from for you..."

Intending to have a somewhat serious conversation, Subaru gave up after Shaula completely broke its stride.
Vaguely loosening his cheeks, Subaru focused on cooking.

By Subaru's hands cooking at the side of the highway, the meal prepared was for two people.
For Subaru and Shaula—there were no portions for Emilia, Beatrice, Ram, Meili, Julius, or any of the others he was supposed to have enjoyed conversing with until now.


—Recognizing that fact once more, Subaru's chest felt horribly empty, aching with pain.

　Being reminded again and again that one was an incomplete existence shaved away at one's nerves.
　Just imagining it made it obvious that it was not a healthy mentality, but being placed in that actual environment allowed one to vividly experience the feeling of the soul wearing away.

tTo put it simply, Natsuki Subaru's current state of mind was precisely that.


"—Master, Master, where are we going next, I wonder?"

"Let's see..."

In high spirits, Shaula walked beside Subaru while humming a tune.
Raising his gaze at her question, Subaru looked down the highway and thought.

'For now, heading west would be best. You're aiming for human settlements, right? If you want to get away from people, do an about-face and plunge into the Great Waterfall.'

"Sister really makes wild suggestions... I'll take the thought, though."

Mixing poison along with straightforward advice was a very Ram-like thing to say. Keeping that in mind, Subaru followed her advice and set his policy to aim west.

"I plan to keep heading west like this. ...But what about you?"

"Eh? Well, I'm sticking tight to Master, I am! We were separated for so long, so I don't wanna be apart from Master for even a second anymore. Please take good care of Shaula, who'll be watching over your life from good morning to good morning, you know!"

"From good morning to good morning... That's terrifying! That's all day!"

"Every single day and night, too, I am!"

Shuddering at the rather horrific stalker declaration, Subaru looked down with a troubled expression.
Honestly, walking along accompanied by Shaula felt like a strange mood.

Subaru, carrying a gloomy, dark guilt, and Shaula, behaving recklessly and openly. As a combination, the pluses and minuses of their energy might balance out, but putting aside Subaru—who was saved in no small part by Shaula's words and actions—he couldn't see any benefit for Shaula in staying with him.

"Master being Master—that's the most important thing for me, you know!"

"...Honestly, I don't really get it. Are you seriously going to follow me forever?"

"Of course, I am! 'Till death do us part', as they say! The love cultivated during our time apart won't wither from something like this~ No matter what happens, I won't leave Master's side. I'll even bear enough kids to make a baseball team, I will!"

"You throw one crazy fastball, don't you..."

Puffing out her ample chest, Shaula looked at him with sparkling eyes. Blocking her gaze with his palm, Subaru let out a sigh, his inner feelings complex and bizarre.

Shaula's direct words and actions—he wasn't sure if it was right to call them courtships, but as a man, Subaru didn't dislike them either.
Setting aside her eccentric behavior, Shaula was a beauty, and she was attractive.

"――――"

However, Natsuki Subaru's heart was not swayed by Shaula's temptation.
Because such human emotions were things allowed only to proper human beings. Ultimately, it was a qualification that the present him could never reach.

'I think Subaru should be a little nicer to Shaula. She's trying so hard, isn't it pitiful?'

Into Subaru's hesitation, that silver-bell voice suddenly interjected. Turning his gaze slightly to the side, he saw Emilia peeking into Subaru's face.
Looking at her cute, pouting expression, Subaru closed one eye.

"...Emilia-tan says that, but that's a pretty cruel thing to say, you know? Even though you know how I felt about you, Emilia-tan."

'Nnn... Is that so? Maybe. Sorry. Of course, even I feel lonely, but I think it's no good to mope forever. Because...'

"Because?"

'Because I am already—'

Narrowing her purple-blue eyes, Emilia tried to continue her next words. Subaru felt an impulse to interrupt her, not wanting to hear it, but that was difficult.
Not listening to Emilia's words here was harder than interrupting the words of God.
However—

"Whoops."

Even if voluntarily interrupting was impossible, being interrupted by an external factor was permitted.
Suddenly hearing noise approaching from behind, Subaru took his mind off Emilia and gave way to the side of the highway. The sound of approaching wheels announced the approach of a running dragon carriage.
Quickly pulling Shaula's arm, the two of them escaped from the dragon carriage's path.

"Ahn, Master, you're so forceful..."

"Get off."

Taking advantage of having her arm pulled, Shaula hugged him, but Subaru elegantly avoided her. Dodging her caused Shaula to crash full-body into a tree along the highway, letting out a scream of "Gyafun!"
Glancing at Shaula, who was reddening her bumped nose, Subaru waited for the dragon carriage to pass, intending to return to the highway—

"Hmm?"

Ahead of his gaze, the dragon carriage that had passed Subaru and Shaula came to a stop with a rattle.
It didn't look like mechanical trouble with the vehicle. In that case, no cause for stopping came to mind other than Subaru and Shaula.
Though it was still high day, it was a deserted road. Perhaps they had run into bandits—Subaru heightened his vigilance for a moment.

"—Could you perhaps be Mr. Natsuki Subaru?"

However, that vigilance quickly dissipated at the call of the person who dismounted from the dragon carriage's driver seat.
Called by name, Subaru lowered his guard and faced the tall young man coming toward him, who should have been sitting on the driver's seat of the dragon carriage.

A person of similar age to Subaru, with gray hair tied into a single tail.
His gentle features made him look slender, but unexpectedly, his build was quite solid. He didn't have the air of someone engaged in heavy physical labor, but neither did he look completely unrelated to it.
Setting aside such first impressions, what mattered to Subaru was that this young man recognized Subaru and called him by name.

"Ah, as I thought, it really is you, Mr. Natsuki. It's been a while."

"Ah—, well..."

While maintaining a subtle distance, the young man spoke with an atmosphere that left no doubt he was an acquaintance. Hesitating over how to respond to him, Subaru scanned his surroundings.
Receiving his gaze, Emilia, Beatrice, and the others shook their heads, indicating to Subaru that they had no idea who he was. If even Emilia and the others were like that, Subaru was completely at a loss, but he hesitated to start a knowledgeable conversation with a knowledgeable front.
That was Subaru's specialty, but due to various circumstances, he didn't want to do that right now.
Watching Subaru stumble in such a state of mind, the young man knitted his well-shaped eyebrows, and—

"Oh, could it be you've forgotten? It's me, Regin Suwen... My elder brother, Otto Suwen, is in your care. We met once before in the village."

"Regin... Ah, that Otto's brother!"

"Calling him 'that Otto' makes it sound like he's a stranger to you."

Laughing wryly at the unnatural phrasing, Subaru clapped his hands in front of the young man—Regin—as understanding dawned. Unfortunately, he had no memory of Regin's face, but he knew Otto.
Now that it was pointed out, he realized Regin and Otto shared many similar facial features. There was no doubt they were brothers, and that Subaru was an acquaintance.

"My bad, my bad. My mind's just really cluttered right now. Your name didn't pop out right away, or something like that."

"No, you're in a busy position, so it can't be helped. News of the Royal Selection has reached even my ears in the backcountry. ...Is my brother causing you any trouble?"

'Otto-kun causing trouble? That's ridiculous. Thanks to Otto-kun, all of us have been saved in so many ways.'

"That's right. The whole camp has been taken care of a lot by Otto... or something like that. The reality seems to be that if he weren't around, things would be rattling out of place everywhere."

"Is that so? Then that's fine..."

Regin, who had been somewhat apologetic, looked slightly relieved after hearing about his brother's current situation. Then, looking back and forth between Subaru and Shaula, who stood a little distance away, he said—

"Even so, Mr. Natsuki, what are you doing in a place like this? Walking along the highway without even riding a dragon carriage seems quite arduous..."

"Ah—, that opinion is completely spot-on, or rather, I'd like to use a dragon carriage if I could, but it's difficult due to various circumstances. Also, as for what I'm doing..."

Cutting his words off there, Subaru pondered for a moment.
Then, finding the appropriate words, he said—
