Arc 10 Part 24: "The Best Guy"


　Natsuki Subaru, who had jumped out of the window, ran toward the scene of the accident that had occurred at the rear of the convoy.
　There was no hesitation in his steps; one could even call them gallant. —It was the back of a person who considered it completely natural to help when someone's life was threatened.

"Foolish..."

　Russell Fellow could only watch his black-haired companion go with eyes that had witnessed something unbelievable—a companion who had shrugged off his call and made a decision without any regard for the consequences.
　Part of it was that he couldn't understand his companion's way of thinking. He also took pride in the fact that he had thoroughly explained the importance of their actions and what their priorities should be.
　But more than that—

"—'White Sheep', why didn't you stop him!"

　To advance the situation rationally, Russell questioned the true intentions of 'White Sheep', whom he had instructed to win him over.
　'White Sheep' had indeed placed her hand over their companion's. She had even spoken words to hold him back. Despite that, their companion did not comply—no, he was not swayed.

"You should understand as well. What is the absolute highest priority right now. Not a single second of our time should be spent on anything other than that. And yet—"

"—I tried."

"What?"

　Frowning at the raspy voice he couldn't hear clearly, Russell looked at 'White Sheep'.
　Facing the window from which their companion had jumped, 'White Sheep', drawing her shaken-off hand close to her chest, turned around. —Her round, golden eyes were welling up with large tears.

"I, I tried. I really did, to Subaru-san... But, Subaru-san just jumped out... That's just the kind of person he is!"

"The kind of...?"

"I'm sure I told 'Silver Fox' as well! All I can do is move feelings that are already there... I cannot make someone do something they would absolutely never do!"

"————"

"W-We must chase after Subaru-san and the others right away. We have to help the injured people too!"

　As 'White Sheep' pleaded with tearful eyes and a trembling voice, Russell placed a hand to his mouth.
　Deep within his narrowed eyes, his Divine Protection, which appraised the 'value' of all things in this world, reshuffled and rewove the various schemes he had constructed.
　He could not see the absolute correct answer. —Only the choice closest to it.

"What's this, what's this? Seems like a pretty cheerful situation, doesn't it? If you're gonna fight, use your fists, not your mouths. Better yet, let's make it a deathmatch, not an argument! Kuhahaha!"

"'Black Dog'..."

"Oh? What's with that face? Don't tell me you've learned to win people over with tears without even touching skin? That only works on men, you know. And I'm an exception."

　The one spitting those words as he boldly boarded the dragon carriage was 'Black Dog'—a man who had shed his black robe and mask, wearing a black suit over his true face, which was distorted into a tough, intrepid expression.
　'Black Dog' shrugged at Russell and 'White Sheep' facing him, sat down roughly in a nearby seat, and said,

"So, while I was wondering what kind of sluggish, pointless dawdling you were up to, you actually went and got your hands bitten by a brat like that! I went out of my way to make sure we could get out, so don't go wasting my efforts."

"Make sure we could get out? ...Don't tell me!"

"Whoa, stop, don't look at me like that. I only broke one axle. The terrified earth dragons are the ones who made the fuss so big! All of this, that, and everything was done for your sake. —Kuhahaha, just kidding."

"—! 'Silver Fox'!"

　Averting her eyes from 'Black Dog', who was crossing his legs in his seat and flashing a malicious grin, 'White Sheep' turned an eye burning with righteous indignation toward Russell.
　Faced with her sincere anger, Russell lowered the hand at his mouth and said,

"—Let us depart."

"A-Are you serious?!"

　Russell nodded to 'White Sheep', who stared in disbelief.

"Securing Crusch-sama was the highest priority from the start. I would have liked to secure the others as well if possible, but if our extended hand is severed, we lose everything. —Though it is a pity that by letting go of 'Blue Snake', we will not be able to test what I wanted to verify."

"Thaaat's why I told you, didn't I? If we had just burned them in the sewers, there wouldn't have been any loose ends."

"I have no intention of retracing steps to a topic that leaves no room for debate. Time is precious."

　Having decided on their course of action, Russell turned his back on 'White Sheep'. His mind had already discarded any thoughts of the companion who had headed toward the accident scene.
　'White Sheep', with a look of determination, reached out toward his back—

"Wait, don't do it."

　With those words, a long, extended leg blocked 'White Sheep's path.
　'Black Dog', slouching in his seat, glared at 'White Sheep', who bit her lip at the obstruction, and said,

"Didn't you say it yourself? You can't make him do what he wouldn't do."

"But this is...!"

"So don't cry, it's annoying. I'm not doing this to be considerate of some piece of shit's mood. It's just that there might be no need for you to do anything, is all."

"————"

　Shrugging at 'White Sheep', who blinked in confusion, 'Black Dog' kept his legs stretched out and raised a hand, pointing toward the rear of the dragon carriage—no, toward the accident scene.
　And then—

"—The nature of all this hullabaloo has changed. So, what's our plan now? Kuhahaha!"


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△


　At the accident scene, dust and debris swirled in the air, and the half-destroyed building was still in danger of collapsing.
　A severely injured person, whose body had been pierced by a wooden beam from the collapsed ceiling, was unconscious, and the wound was obviously fatal to anyone's eyes. —If the person who happened to be there, continuously casting healing magic, had not been him.

"—Ferris?"

　Subaru stared wide-eyed at the person who had pulled back his hood with one hand and revealed his face—Ferris.
　Just a moment ago, Subaru had been thinking about him. Having gone through such a terribly painful experience, yet rejecting the hand Subaru offered as arrogant, he had wondered how Ferris was doing now.
　He never would have imagined that he would be in the same group under the name 'Blue Snake'.

"What are you doing?! Do you even have ears?!"

"—!"

"Don't just stand there like a statue in front of a life! If someone dies here, it'll be our fault for not saving them!"

　The shock of the reunion was shattered by none other than Ferris's own words.
　Brought back to his senses by the desperate shout, Subaru recalled Ferris's immediate instruction—to pull out the wooden beam piercing the injured person's body. He set down Beatrice, whom he had been holding, onto the ground.

"Can you do it, Beako?!"

"Leave it to Betty, I suppose. —Murak!"

　An insufficient interval after successive battles at the McMahon estate and the sewers. Despite being in the worst possible condition, Beatrice didn't show it for a second as she activated her Yin magic, making the target lighter.

"I'm lifting it! Are you ready?!"

"Anyone who says they aren't is an idiot!"

"Watch your mouth! —Nng, ggggh!"

　While shouting back and forth with Ferris, Subaru carefully lifted the lightened wood, pulling out the piece that pierced the severely injured victim. A sickening sensation and sound followed, along with a massive spray of blood.
　But the moment it was pulled from the wound, Ferris showed his true capability.

"It's okay. I'll heal you right now."

　—At that instant, what overflowed was an incredibly gentle blue light.

"What a..."

　Beholding that light, an unspeakable murmur escaped Beatrice's lips.
　Even Beatrice, a Great Spirit who could cast healing magic herself, was left dumbfounded. It contained everything that explained why Ferris was called the 'Blue' and considered a peerless genius.
　It was the same as before—no, clearly, it was a miraculous healing art that had reached an even higher level of mastery.

"————"

　Everyone present who had rushed to the scene prepared for tragedy witnessed this sight.
　Subaru understood their feelings as they were uniformly captivated by the miracle. A fatal wound thought to be the end, a deep injury likely to leave permanent damage, a cruel ending—all were painted over by the light.
　It was a scene that looked as though it could be preserved as a religious painting for posterity—but,

"—Everyone! Carry out the injured who can be moved and gather them in one place! Prioritize the severely injured and those who hit their heads! Anyone who can walk on their own, get outside the building!"

　Shouting at the top of his lungs, Subaru dragged back to reality the people who had frozen before the miracle.
　Ferris was here. This was a miracle. It was insane luck. They had to make full use of it.

"We can save everyone! We have the absolute best healer right here!"

　Staring wide-eyed at Subaru's desperate shout, they exchanged glances and responded with a powerful "Oh!" Right away, they began rescue operations for those who had collapsed or whose legs were trapped under the rubble.
　Seeing this through, Subaru wiped his eyes with the back of his hand.

"Ferris, we can do this! I'll help! What should I do?!"

"Don't get in my way! Just do as I say! Hold this person down!"

"You bastard... Once all this is done, I'm going to talk back so hard, so you'd better remember this!"

　Uncaring about Subaru's persuasion of the others, Ferris's mind was entirely focused on saving lives—indeed, his foul mouth aside, Ferris's skill and decisiveness were top-notch. He prioritized the injured by urgency at a glance, using healing magic with appropriate output to pluck lives back one after another.

"A restaurant at dinner time... No wonder so many people got caught up in this."

　Now that he could finally see the full extent of the disaster area, Subaru spat out saliva mixed with dust.
　With the street congested by dragon carriages, many had likely remained inside the shop. Hence the high number of casualties, but fortunately, no one within Subaru's field of vision had died instantly.
　And if no one had died instantly—

"—You won't die. You can go home, safe and sound."

　The overflowing light of recovery healed and removed every single life-threatening wound.
　To save everyone. Not just as an aspiration, but as a tangible reality—

"—! Hey, look at that!"

　Suddenly, a voice contradicting that hope rang out, making Subaru snap his head up.
　One of the guards helping with the rescue was pointing at the half-collapsed restaurant—specifically, the half-destroyed upper floor, which was barely supported by a cracked pillar.

　The roof beam, which was in a precarious balance, gave a dying groan, and the second floor of the building tilted—below it lay Ferris, still treating the wounded, and other victims. If the upper half of the building fell, no one would survive.
　The moment that thought crossed his mind, Subaru—no, Subaru wasn't the only one who started running.

"—!"

　An older guard, his subordinates, and a civilian man who had been helping with the rescue had already bolted forward. Others were pushing themselves up, lifting their bodies, trying to move.
　Glancing at them, Subaru gritted his teeth as hard as he could and screamed frantically—

"—Beatrice!!"

"Betty is seriously out of gas after this, I suppose! —Murak!!"

　With Beatrice holding up both hands, the collapsing section of the building was enveloped in a faint purple light.
　But, lamentably, gravity would not eliminate the falling speed or the danger of the debris, even if its weight was altered. The outcome led by such mass would be the same. —Into that space, he slid.

"Nnngh...!!"

　Barely making it under the debris in time, Subaru assumed a posture to support the falling pieces, like someone caught in a collapsing ceiling trap. Subaru couldn't do it alone. The guards who had run with him joined him under the rubble a moment later, and everyone united to hold it up.

"Guh...! While we hold it, get the people underneath out...!"

　Feeling as if he were spitting blood, Subaru voiced the collective will of everyone supporting the rubble. Hearing this, those who had hesitated dragged the injured and Ferris out to safety.
　Until that was finished, they had to endure no matter what. —To Subaru, who was steeling himself,

"...Man, you're always shouting at the top of your lungs, kid!"

"Huh? Wait, you're...!"

　While holding his ground with all his might, Subaru noticed the person supporting the rubble right next to him and gasped in surprise.
　It was a familiar face—Kadomon, the fruit vendor he frequently crossed paths with in the royal capital.

"————"

　Staring wide-eyed at this unexpected reunion, Subaru simultaneously felt a wave of panic.
　He had immediately broken Russell's warning that the "cognition-impairing" robe's effectiveness was not absolute. By meddling here, his identity had been exposed to Kadomon, who knew him.
　But—

"Don't make that face. Saving people here—that's the truth of the kid I know."

　Hearing those words, Subaru almost lost the strength in his arms. Because of that, the burden on Kadomon and the others suddenly spiked, eliciting a "Nwoah?!" which made Subaru hurriedly put his strength back into it.

　He was surprised. He thought he was done for now that his identity was blown. —But he was glad he had come back.

"Everyone's out! On three, we push it up together and throw it back!"

　Gritting their teeth until they creaked, everyone shouted in unison, roaring, "One, two, three!!"
　What they threw off with all their might was practically the entire second floor and roof of the building. The instant it left everyone's hands, the magic's effect expired, and it regained its original, destructive weight.

"Get out, get out, get out, get out, get out, get out—!!"

　With the crushing sound of explosive weight echoing behind them, the group that had supported the rubble scrambled away with all their might. Escaping the near-lethal collapse by rolling away, they ended up tumbled on the ground.
　Sprawling flat on the ground after rolling around, they looked up at the dust-covered sky.

"Phew, haah... I'm, I'm not pinned anywhere... right?"

"You can rest easy, can't you? Didn't you say the best healer is right here?"

"Even if he can heal it, I don't want to come face-to-face with a severed limb!"

　Beside Subaru, who yelled in response, Kadomon, also lying on the ground, laughed, "Too true." Laughter arose from the others as well; it seemed that everyone in the rubble-holding group was safe.
　Somehow, they had managed to finish the job—or so they thought.

"Ferris is still struggling out there on his own. I need to go help..."

"—Well, I suppose that might be a bit difficult."

　Subaru, forcing his sluggish body up, turned around with a "Huh?" at the voice.
　The voice belonged to Beatrice, and it carried a sense of tension, the meaning of which Subaru understood immediately. In the street outside the damaged restaurant, the injured were being carried out in droves.
　In the midst of it all, Ferris was still diligently treating them, and yet—

"—Rebel, and first knight of Crusch Karsten, Felix Argyle of the 'Blue'. An order has been issued for your appearance at the royal castle. You will comply immediately."

　Because a squad of knights surrounding Ferris was grimly pointing their swords at him.


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△


"————"

　The squad of about thirty knights were all clad in the white uniforms of the Royal Guard. This utterly absurd sight caused Subaru's mind to momentarily go blank.
　In front of an accident scene, knights were pointing swords at a healer in the middle of rescue efforts? This was impossible.

"Wha... What are you talking about?! An order?! Look at the situation!"

"Yeah! That person is treating the injured! If it weren't for him..."

"—Objections are not permitted! This is also a grave matter of the capital. Let the city guards handle the accident. We, the Royal Guard, shall fulfill our duty!"

　In Subaru's stead, Kadomon and the older guard raised their voices in protest at this anomaly. However, they were drowned out by the piercing, angry roar of the long-haired commander leading the knights.
　In the brief pause that followed, the commander shot a sharp glare at Ferris—specifically, at his back as he continued to cast healing magic without even glancing at the knights.

"It is an order! Stop your hands, Knight Felix! Suspected of grave treason against the Kingdom alongside your master, how could we trust you with medical treatment?! Who knows what you might do to those you treat—"

"—Stop messing around!!"

　At that instant, Subaru exploded with rage at the unforgivable insults hurled at Ferris.
　Unthinkingly leaping forward, Subaru interposed himself between Ferris's back and the knights. He glared at the knights pointing their swords—no, at those unworthy of being called knights.

"With what kind of feelings do you think Ferris honed his healing magic?! Do you think it's just talent?! It's obviously because he wants to save people, you idiots!"

"————"

"You don't know what Ferris might do to the injured?! Yes, you do! He's healing them! He's saving lives! He's helping people who are on the verge of death! This is, beyond a shadow of a doubt, clearly the kind of thing the best guy in the world would do!"

　Subaru roared, his voice hoarse and his eyes bloodshot, wondering why they couldn't understand something so simple.
　As if he hadn't just exhausted his strength moments ago, he gestured wildly, exploding with natural, raw emotion, looking into the eyes of each knight, throwing his entire soul into his words.

"Kid..."

　Seeing Subaru's fury, Kadomon murmured weakly.
　To him, the guards, and those at the accident scene, this was completely obvious. Even among the knights, hesitation began to flicker in their eyes and expressions, with a few of their sword tips wavering.

　However, such righteous vacillation was—

"—I wondered who it was, but it is Natsuki Subaru, the rebel's accomplice."

　Crushed from above by a single sentence from the knight commander, whose eyes were clouded with hatred.
　The moment the commander uttered "Natsuki Subaru," the knights blinked repeatedly, correctly perceiving the presence of Subaru standing right before them. —The "cognition-impairment" had been bypassed.
　Once they realized the person speaking so plausibly was a wanted criminal, the knights' hesitation was swept away by their sense of duty, and a tension equal to—no, greater than before returned.

"Subaru..."

　As his identity was blown and swords were pointed at Subaru, Beatrice, who had pressed close to him, took his hand. However, having completely spent her mana on the building's collapse just now, they had no way to break through.

"At most, I can punch the leader in the face with Invisible Providence, huh... Sorry, Ferris. I just threw oil on the fire."

"—Shut up. I wasn't expecting anything anyway."

"Haha..."

"But you're good at talking, aren't you? Joke around and stall as long as you can. I'm going to save as many as possible."

　Without looking back, they exchanged those words with their backs to each other. Subaru smiled wryly at the caustic Ferris in his mind and steeled his resolve.
　The enemy wanted information on Crusch. They probably wouldn't kill him here. In that case, using his silver tongue and whatever tricks he had, Natsuki Subaru would fight the only way a magicless person could—

"We are well aware of your... no, *your* danger. To save the Kingdom from useless chaos, Natsuki Subaru, you will here—!"

"Whoa, wait, wait, wait! You're giving me way too much credit! In the first place, calling it a rebellion is..."

"Silence! All is for the sake of the Kingdom! That smooth tongue of yours—"





"—A wind of lies is blowing."

　—At that instant, a dignified gust of wind swept through.

"—Ferris."

"—Yes."

"Heal all of the injured. Ensure they can all return to their original lives without any lingering issues."

"I was planning to do so even if you didn't tell me."

"I see. Then it is well. —I shall buy you that time."



　Not many words were exchanged. But for the two of them, that was more than enough.
　For those two, who understood each other's abilities better than anyone else, that was all it took.

"————"

　The golden 'Dragon's Eye', its brilliance drawing lines of light in the air, bewildered the knights with agile movements. The knights spread out to avoid friendly fire, displaying coordinated teamwork, but—

"I can see it."

　The storm of swords should have left no gaps, but placing the tip of her slender sword into an opening as small as the eye of a needle, the 'Valkyrie' forcefully carved out a path—Crusch's sword flash danced, and the knights dropped their weapons.
　Groaning and retreating while clutching their arms, the knights fell out of the fight one by one, their wrists or the bases of their fingers bearing shallow wounds that stripped them of their ability to continue fighting.
　One might think this was a non-lethal sword technique to avoid taking lives needlessly—but,

"Are you the real thing? Or fakes?"

"—!"

"The two on both ends are good; the one who retreated to the back is evil. —There is no escape."

　Immediately following the ruthless declaration, the retreated knight pointed his unarmed hand at Crusch. At that moment, the presence of magic being activated warped the space.
　But—

"It's useless, I suppose."

　Beatrice's murmur beside her was proven true by Crusch's sword flash—a superb technique that slashed the magic before it could manifest. The wide-eyed knight was struck by a diagonal slash of light and collapsed.

　Subaru almost spoke up to stop her for a moment, but looking closely with knowledge of the circumstances, he could see that the mercy and ruthlessness Crusch dealt out had proper justification.
　Among the squad, those whose goal was to suppress Crusch and those whose obvious goal was to assassinate her were intermingled. —Every single one Crusch had cut down so far belonged to the latter group.

　From the earlier rampage of the knight commander as well, the involvement of someone—Capella—was clear.
　And Crusch's Divine Protection could accurately see through the involvement of that worst of Authorities.

"And unless I'm mistaken... is she even stronger than she was at the mansion?"

"You are not mistaken, I suppose. She is mastering those eyes far better than back then."

　Wielding a sword with no waste, no mercy, and no hesitation, Crusch's swordsmanship was far too outstanding as she fought and sorted through the knights based on the presence of Capella's influence, even choosing how to take each down.
　The 'Dragon's Eye' was growing accustomed to her. —Unsure of what that implied, Subaru couldn't bring himself to rejoice unconditionally.

"—Ah, that's everyone! I'm finished!"

　Crusch's unfolding display of peerless strength had successfully bought enough time for Ferris to complete his treatment.
　Ferris, with sweat on his brow, tried to stand up. As his body tottered and he nearly collapsed, Subaru quickly reached out to support him.
　Wrapping his arm around Ferris's slender body, Subaru held him securely, letting him lean on his shoulder.

"You really don't miss your moment, do you, Subaru-kun..."

"Don't say it like I was calculating the timing! You were insanely cool, man!"

"Your voice is too loud. Hold me up better. I'm gonna fall."

"You're so demanding. But... Crusch-san!"

　Ferris had done his job. It was time for Crusch to withdraw from the battle with the knights as well.
　To be honest, with her strength, it might have been possible to break through this encirclement head-on, but—

"If she falls asleep halfway again, we will truly be finished, I suppose."

"Honestly, that's what I'm scared of! Crusch-san, tell me you have some kind of trick up your sleeve!"

"Regrettably, I have none."

"Hieee!"

"Therefore, I have had one prepared. —'Black Dog'!"

　Just when hope seemed lost, Crusch called out a name that was far too dangerous to rely on.
　Immediately after Subaru, Beatrice, and Ferris let out a collective "Ugh" at the sound of it—

"Kuhahahahaha! Don't hate me or love me so much! The star of the show has arrived!"

　A laughter that made everyone feel unpleasant just hearing it echoed around, prompting the knights and onlookers who didn't recognize the voice to look around in surprise. However, the owner of the laughter was nowhere to be seen.
　Instead, what shrouded their vision was a massive amount of black smoke.

"It is a smoke screen. It is harmless."

　As voices of surprise and confusion arose within the black smoke, Crusch, running over, spoke in a quiet voice. However, while he didn't doubt Crusch, anything involving 'Black Dog' filled Subaru with anxiety.

"I think it'll be fine. If it weren't, you and I would be left behind here again, wouldn't we?"

"That's true! Alright. For now, let's get out of here—Invisible Providence!"

　The moment Subaru nodded at Ferris's point, an invisible black hand shot from his chest, piercing through the black smoke to punch straight into the face of the knight commander who was trying to step forward.
　Squirting nosebleed with a sharp "gah!", the commander fell flat on his back. Crusch glanced at him and said,

"That one is good."

"No way?! He's not one of the impostors?!"

"I felt a strong anger and hatred toward you. Do you know why?"

"That's Knight Zeol, right? He was at the castle during the Royal Selection. Isn't that why?"

"For goodness' sake, can we finally settle on how people view that incident?!"

　Sometimes it proved Subaru's innocence, other times it became the reason he was targeted—it was a total mess.
　Regardless of his laments, Subaru and the others slipped away from the situation under the cover of the black smoke screen. —A few of the knights tried to follow, but,

"We won't let you chase the kid and his friends. I really don't like the way the Royal Guard is doing things today."

　Blocking their path were Kadomon and the city guards.
　At this unexpected help, Subaru gasped, "Pops?!" in surprise, to which Kadomon laughed.

"Go! I'm rooting for you! For you, kid, and for Emilia-sama!"

"—! Yeah, I'll be back to get your vote!"

　Recognizing Natsuki Subaru as Emilia's knight, Kadomon's encouragement brought Subaru to the verge of tears as he responded, putting strength back into his legs to sprint through the black smoke once more.
　Behind his back, the bellows of the knights and the high spirits of the resisting city guards rose—

"—Inspection is complete! Let them through, Royal Guard! Let the city guards do our job!!"

"—Yo, you made it back. I totally thought you were dead."

"Chin... you're really the worst kind of companion..."

　Emerging from the thick black smoke that had aided their escape, they scrambled into the dragon carriage waiting outside the main gate. Rachins greeted the four of them with a casual wave of his hand.
　Subaru was amazed by Rachins's cold-heartedness, showing absolutely no intention of moving despite such a massive commotion.

"Do you not have a human heart? Are you a Witch Cultist?"

"Don't go throwing false accusations around! And saying I didn't do anything is a flat-out lie. I negotiated properly. Right, Duchess?"

　Rachins stuck out his tongue and looked to Crusch for confirmation. At his prompt, Crusch, who had adjusted her eye patch over her left eye, nodded, "Indeed."

"Rachins Hoffman is quite articulate. In fact, he is the one who settled the negotiation."

"Negotiation? What do you mean..."

"—With us, of course, Natsuki-dono."

　At the voice that came as a sudden surprise, Subaru and Beatrice's shoulders jumped in unison.
　The owner of the voice was none other than Russell, who had boarded the carriage a moment after Subaru and the others.
　Accompanied by 'White Sheep' and 'Black Dog', he had the carriage door shut and ordered the driver to depart.
　Immediately, the moving dragon carriage transformed into a reflection room with no escape.

"First, that everyone returned safely is the best outcome. Of course, luck was simply on our side, and I have many thoughts on the matter—'Blue Snake'."

"Mmh..."

"You were the one who made it a condition not to reveal your presence. Despite that, acting in such a manner made it impossible to keep it secret. Surely, you won't claim this is our oversight?"

"I know. I won't call it a breach of contract. This isn't the place to talk about personal feelings anyway."

　Ferris, who was addressed first by Russell, cast a sideways glance at Crusch. However, though Crusch was likely aware of his gaze, she did not react to Ferris.
　The atmosphere lingering between the two was incredibly complex—

"What should we do, Beako? Think I can break the ice? Should I try?"

"Wait, wait for a moment, I suppose. This is a time to watch and see. More than that..."

"Natsuki-dono, please refrain from such actions in the future."

"Guh... You really are angry, aren't you?"

　Subaru timidly tried to read Russell's expression, but as expected of the head of an intelligence agency, his face was unreadable.
　Russell's expression was a complete emotional calm, but there was no way he wasn't angry at Subaru, who had flatly disobeyed his instructions and leapt into the scene. —This face should definitely be counted as angry.

"Rather than angry, I am disappointed. I believed I had thoroughly explained what should take priority. —If not for Crusch-sama's words, I would not have risked staying in that place."

"Crusch-san's... words? What are..."

"It is simple. —At present, among those in this place, I have no intention of placing my trust in anyone other than Natsuki Subaru. I would refuse to cooperate with this 'Six-Tongues' without him."

"That's...! That's an incredible honor, but...!"

　Depending on how one heard it, it was a rather extreme opinion. Subaru became acutely aware of the eyes around him—particularly those of Russell, who had been burdened with unnecessary conditions, and Ferris, who shared a complicated relationship with her. However, Russell's expression remained unchanged, while Ferris sat in his seat and looked away, citing fatigue.
　At any rate, 'Six-Tongues' could not afford to let go of Crusch, and because of Crusch's stance, they couldn't just abandon Subaru and Ferris, who had returned to the accident scene.

"But, you know, Russell-san..."

"What is it? If your thoughts have changed after this incident..."

"Not really my thoughts, but... I think if something like this happens again, Ferris and I won't be able to just stand by and watch. So, I have a request."

"—I am listening."

"I want us to go with plans that keep casualties as low as possible from now on. —Is that a no?"

　Russell widened his eyes slightly at this plea, and 'Black Dog' in the back let out a small "kuha" laugh.
　He was aware he was asking for the unreasonable. But in fact, no one had died in this accident. Dealing with those touched by Capella's hand would be difficult, but they should be able to resist until the very end.
　Before Russell could respond to Subaru's thoughts—
