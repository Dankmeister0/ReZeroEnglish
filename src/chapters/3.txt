Arc 1 Part 2: "Don't Get Cocky, Said the Gods."

The men's contemptuous and mocking gazes pierced Subaru, yet he, in turn, was sizing them up.
They appeared to be in their mid-twenties, with unkempt appearances and expressions that seemed to reflect the darkness within. They weren't clearly non-human, but they certainly weren't good people.

"Damn, a mandatory event has started."

The men's sardonic smiles contrasted with Subaru's panicked attempts to wipe his face and stand up.
These were clearly thieves—and, given the world's setting, they might be capable of taking more than just possessions; they could take lives.
Mission 1: "Repel the Thieves" had begun. The win condition was the complete annihilation of the enemy. The loss condition was Subaru's death.

Subaru ignored the chill running down his spine, slapping his cheek.
He had to pull himself together. Hesitation would only lead to death. He had confidence in his decision-making.

"Besides, this is another world. If I'm supposed to be the overpowered protagonist, maybe I'm incredibly strong here. Maybe the gravity is only a tenth of what it is in my world... or maybe it's even less! If that's the case, I might feel lighter! Maybe I can actually do something!"

"That guy is rambling to himself," one of the men muttered.

"He doesn't understand the situation. Maybe we should explain it to him," another suggested.

Subaru's enthusiasm was met with a chilly reception from the men.
However, Subaru refused to be discouraged. He puffed out his chest and said,

"Hey, I can't get cocky. Let me tell you, I've had this fantasy countless times: being cornered by thugs in a back alley. I'm going to take you down and use your experience to fuel my own growth."

"What are you even talking about? You're mocking us. I'm going to kill you."

"That's what *you* said!"

Before the men could react, Subaru launched a preemptive attack.
He lunged forward, delivering a powerful right straight punch that connected perfectly with the lead man's nose. The impact caused the man's front teeth to shatter, and blood splattered from his fist.

"I actually hit someone! And it hurt! I hit them hard!"

He hadn't had a chance to simulate it, but this was his first real experience.
The man he hit fell to the ground, unmoving. Without hesitation, Subaru pounced on another man who was staring in shock.

"Eat this! A high kick worthy of a post-bath stretch!"

"Guh!"

The man's temple was struck by the arching kick, sending him crashing into the wall, writhing in pain.
Subaru was surprised by his success, and the feeling of being an "overpowered protagonist" began to solidify.

"I'm actually strong in this world! Adrenaline is pumping! This is going to be a victory!"

He turned to face the last man, intending to knock him down as well, and crouched.
But the moment he saw a glint of metal in the last man's hand, his crouching posture faltered.

"I'm sorry, I was completely wrong. Please forgive me. Just spare my life!"

He prostrated himself, offering the ultimate surrender—a gesture of humility.
The excitement he'd felt moments ago vanished, replaced by a chilling feeling as he heard the blood draining from his body. He desperately pleaded for mercy, pressing his forehead to the ground.

"I can't deal with knives. If I get stabbed, it's over. I don't have any skills to deal with someone wielding a knife."

He noticed that the two men he had knocked down had already gotten back on their feet. They were holding their bloody noses, shaking their heads, and seemed otherwise fine.

"What the hell? 'Damage small' from my overpowered attack? What's the deal with this summoning trope?!"

"What the hell are you babbling about! You're really going to regret this!"

He was trampled on as he tried to get up, his palms pressed against the ground. The man's face was contorted with rage as he spat and saw the knife being raised again.

"I'm going to strip you bare and leave you to the rats in this alley."

"Wait, if you're after valuables, there's nothing here. I'm completely broke!"

"Then you can be a rare kimono or footwear for the alley rats."

He thought, "Oh, there are rats in this world. They're probably weak monsters."

As he looked at the knife about to be swung, a desperate thought flashed through his mind.
He didn't see any flashforwards or feel the world slow down.
Then, as if a string had been cut, a voice cut through the air.

"Hey, stop it! You guys, really, that's enough!"

Someone had rushed into the alley.
The men turned to see who it was, and Subaru, still pinned to the ground, could only follow their gaze.
A petite girl with long, golden hair ran past them.
Her eyes were sharp and determined, and she had a mischievous glint in her eyes, along with a cute, buck-toothed smile. Her appearance was childish, but there was something endearing about her. She had just stumbled upon a scene of robbery and attempted murder.

At the perfect moment, a glimmer of hope ignited within Subaru.
This was the development he had been waiting for. He expected this girl, with her compassionate nature, to save him, the one whose life was about to be extinguished.

"Sorry, I'm in a hurry! Please live strong!"

"What? Really?!"

But his hope was quickly dashed.
The girl, after making eye contact with Subaru, raised a hand in apology and continued running without slowing down. She ran past the men and disappeared into a dead-end alley.
She kicked a loose board that was propped against the wall, effortlessly scaling the building and disappearing onto the roof.

The girl was gone, and a silence fell over the scene.
The girl had passed through like a whirlwind. Everyone was stunned, but the reality was that Subaru's situation hadn't changed.

"Did that just... make you change your mind?!"

"It just made me more annoyed. Don't think you can get away with this easily!"

Subaru knew he was doomed, as the knife-wielding man's eyes were filled with murderous intent.
He was still pinned to the ground, unable to get up.
He finally gave in to despair.

*Is this how it ends? Here, in a place like this?*
*I haven't exactly lived a righteous life, but this is a terrible way to go. What have I done to deserve this? It's a meaningless end.*
*It's not the pain, but something else that's making me cry.*
*It's the fear of ending with nothing, an empty void.*

"That's enough, you villain."

The voice resonated through the alley, silencing the sounds of the city, the men's insults, and even Subaru's ragged breathing.
