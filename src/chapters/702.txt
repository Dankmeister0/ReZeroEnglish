Arc 9 Chapter 7: "Reunion in the Sand Wind"

——For Subaru, this was his first time stopping by Mirula, the town nearest the Augria Sand Dunes, in almost three months.

Originally, he was supposed to have passed through it on the way back from the Pleiades Watchtower, but the trouble at the tower had sent him flying all the way to Vollachia, blowing that itinerary apart entirely.
So this visit to the run-down waystation town made only his second time here in three months.

"...Welcome, in from the sand wind."

So said the sullen-looking proprietor who greeted Subaru and the others as they passed through the shop's entrance, idly polishing a glass.
Subaru recalled being greeted in much the same fashion last time, and brushed as much of the sand clinging to his body off at the entrance as he could. Even so, there was no getting rid of it all, so he chalked the proprietor's foul mood up as a necessary expense and took a seat at the counter.

"What'll it be?"

"Milk, the cold kind."
"Milk, the warm kind, I suppose."
"Milk, I'd like a warm one too, please."

Ordered milk three times in a row at a tavern, the proprietor's rough face visibly soured. And yet, the fact that he set the milk on the fire without a single complaint or curse showed that, gruff as he was, he hadn't forgotten his trade.
While the proprietor worked, Subaru pulled the cloth wound around his mouth down with a *puha* and said,

"Phew, we made sure to dodge the 'Sand Hour' this time because of what happened last time, but the wind's still thick with sand no matter what. Guess I underestimated it a little."

Grumbling so, Subaru worked the feel of sand out of his mouth with a finger. Beside him, the girl who had likewise lowered her own sand-guard cloth nodded, "Yeah, huh."

"Even though Subaru warned us ahead of time, my hair's already completely full of sand... I'll have to comb it out properly later. I'll do yours too, Beatrice-chan."

"I've told you before, I suppose. Betty's grooming can be reset with a snap, easy as that, I suppose. Never mind Betty — focus on yourself, I suppose."

"Mm, I know, but Beatrice-chan's hair is so thick and full, it's worth the trouble to look after — I just want to do it, that's all."

"I know, I know. Fiddling with Bea-ko's hair is a bit of an adventure, after all."

Whenever the topic of Beatrice's hair came up, Subaru — who combed and tied it every morning to keep those drill twintails just so — had plenty to say. Granted, per Beatrice's own claim that she could reset her grooming with a snap, there wasn't really any need for upkeep at all; it was more a matter of feeling. As it was, Beatrice, having her own hair treated like some kind of attraction, puffed out her cheeks.

"Honestly, is Betty's stylish hair — or her cute cheeks, for that matter — some kind of plaything, I suppose?"

"Nobody was talking about your cheeks, but Bea-ko's cheeks are Mochi-Mochi Land too, you know."

"They're so nice to touch, aren't they!"

At the appearance of such an eager second, Subaru laughed a hearty "Right?!" and promptly scooped up Beatrice, who'd been sitting beside him, and set her on his lap.
Beatrice went stiff with a startled "myu!" but it was already too late.
Too slow to escape, her cheeks were now poked from both sides by two different fingers.

"H-hey, cut that out, I suppose! Not in public, I suppose! Show some restraint, I suppose!"

"Oh, would you look at that — Bea-ko's learned to feel shy in public. You've grown up. There was a time you had no shame at all... you used to try and follow me right into the bathroom."

"That was the very, very start of our contract, I suppose! That was a year and a half ago, I suppose!"

"You two were just as clingy with each other then as you are now, though. But — I think I might understand a little how Beatrice-chan feels about being close to Subaru right now."

So saying, the girl seated on his left leaned in and rested her head lightly on Subaru's shoulder. Startled by the light touch, Subaru met her eyes, and she gave a bashful "ehehe."
A girl at just the age to want spoiling, who nevertheless tried so hard to act grown-up that it was hard to indulge her properly. Charmed by this rare display from her, Subaru doted on Beatrice's cheek with one hand while reaching to pat the girl's head with the other—

"Here, milk's up. ...What is this scene."

"Oop, here it comes. C'mon, Bea-ko, back to your seat~"

"'Back to your seat,' nothing — you're the one who yanked me out of it in the first place, I suppose! Hmph, I suppose!"

With a wry smile at the proprietor's exasperated expression as he held out the steaming cup, Subaru set Beatrice back down in her seat and took the warm milk.
Then, as the proprietor finally set down the glass of cold milk Subaru had ordered—

"Ah, I knew it. You're the fella who went into the sand sea with that gorgeous young lady before, aren't you."

"Oh, you remembered? Yep, that's right — I'm the Milk Man from back then."

"...I heard. Word is somebody reached the Sage's tower. Might be a lot of folks from the royal capital heading out to that tower soon."

"——"

"Did you make it there?"

In the proprietor's low voice was a faint mix of hope and excitement.
As Subaru took a sip from his glass of milk, he glanced down at the proprietor's feet — one leg a prosthetic — and remembered that the man had likely been one of those who'd once tried to conquer the sand sea himself.
Back then, the proprietor had given Subaru and the others earnest advice as they'd set out for the tower. To this question from a fellow traveler of that road, Subaru grinned and stuck up a thumb.
And then—

"Yeah, I went to the tower and met the Sage. —A loud, overly familiar, adorable Sage. Got a little unfinished business now, so I'm about to make my second run at it."

That was how he answered.

——The journey to the Augria Sand Dunes: in other words, a return visit to the Pleiades Watchtower.

It was a journey that had begun with a request from Al, who had lost the mistress he'd loved with all his heart in the Vollachian Empire — Priscilla Barielle.
Al's aim was to read the Book of the Dead kept in the Pleiades Watchtower — to read the volume of the now-deceased Priscilla, and to touch what she had felt.
Faced with Al's plea that he had to do this no matter what, Subaru couldn't bring himself to say no.

"Priscilla would probably fly into a rage if she found out about this."

When he closed his eyes, he could recall nothing of Priscilla but her smile, for all that she'd been nothing but arrogant and haughty.
Subaru understood well enough that doing this ran against everything Priscilla stood for — understood it so clearly that he could easily picture the look of fury and contempt she'd give him for it.
Still, he couldn't help but think: what if their positions were reversed?

If Subaru had no Return by Death, or found himself in a situation like Priscilla's — one that even Return by Death couldn't undo — and lost someone precious to him.
In that moment, could he really stop himself from wishing to know that someone's final feelings? —In the face of that question, he felt that no amount of principle or idealism meant a thing.

Of course, Subaru had no intention of reading Priscilla's own Book of the Dead.
He had no right to do such a thing, nor did he think he could bear to read it even if he tried. So it wasn't as though he could say for certain that Al had that right either.
And yet, if anyone did have the right, it would be one of Abel, Yorna, or Al.
So—

"I want to respect Al's feelings, that he wants to read the Book of the Dead. I'm gonna take him to the Pleiades Watchtower. ...That's what I can do for Al — and for Priscilla."

Subaru could well imagine that Priscilla wouldn't want her own Book of the Dead read.
But at the same time, he felt that if Priscilla knew her own death had shattered Al's heart to pieces, plunging him into a despair he couldn't rise from, and that the means to overcome it was her own Book of the Dead, she might not refuse to let him read it after all.
Having been present at that final moment, having watched Priscilla dissolve into light within the morning sun, Subaru felt that such a meaning had been present in the exchange between Al and Priscilla.
Even so—

"I'll have you know, Ram isn't coming along. Ram wants Rem, who's finally made it back, to rest properly. Go work yourself to death all on your own."

So Ram declared the moment she heard Subaru's plan — but setting her aside, there were, naturally, plenty of objections to Subaru heading to the Pleiades Watchtower with Al.
Among them, the two who objected most strongly were Otto and, surprisingly, Emilia.

"I agree with Ram-san. It's not only about Rem-san, either — Natsuki-san, you must be more than worn out yourself from everything in the Empire. Your body stretching and shrinking like that, and even setting that aside, your nerves must have been strained the whole time. Why not take it easy for a while?"

"You know, I really, really understand how you feel about being worried for Al. I think I understand that Al's hurting too. ...But — it's not that the tower is bad, exactly, but that's where you got sent flying all the way to Vollachia, right? If I let you go there again, what if this time you end up somewhere like Gusteko...?"

Otto's opinion was practical; Emilia's came from anxiety.
Both were opinions born of concern for Subaru, and brushing them aside didn't feel good in the least. But the fact that Al needed a hand held out to him right now, in this very moment, tipped the scales, and Subaru made up his mind to head for the tower.
Frankly, on this one point, all he could do was keep bowing his head in genuine apology—

"—Garfiel, can I ask this of you?"
"Oh, leave it t'me, Otto-nii. Just like ya said — I ain't lettin' the boss outta my sight. Stickin' right with 'im."

"Beatrice, hold Subaru's hand tight, okay? You can never let go."
"You needn't tell Betty that, I suppose. Betty is absolutely done feeling that same regret again, like when you all got sent flying to the Empire, I suppose."

While Subaru had been racking his brain trying to scrape together words to persuade them, Otto and Emilia — who should have been the ones standing in his way — had already entrusted the matter to Garfiel and Beatrice respectively.
As Subaru blinked in bewilderment at this, the two of them shrugged at each other.

"Ever since we heard about Al-san, I figured it would come to this. ...It's just that, along with reporting on the matter of Priscilla-sama, Emilia-sama and I have to go to the royal capital. So."

"We talked about leaving it properly to Beatrice and Garfiel. Honestly, I really, really want to come along too, but..."

Weighing position against feeling on the scales of her heart, Emilia had properly decided to see her role through.
As a Royal Selection candidate, she had to report both the fact that she'd intervened, at Abel's request, in the war between the Vollachian Empire and the Great Disaster, and the matter of Priscilla losing her life in that battle.
In the wake of that news, the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica was bound to be shaken hard. The Royal Selection itself might warp badly out of shape, too, now that it was down one candidate.
Even so—

"I think what you want to do for Al is something only you can do — you're the one who spoke with Priscilla at the very end. —Please, take care of Al."

At the earnestness in Emilia's violet eyes, Subaru felt his mouth go dry.
Once again, so completely, he'd pushed his own way through and made kind Emilia and Otto shoulder the burden for it. By rights, as Emilia's knight — and, more than that, as someone who'd been party to everything that had happened in the Vollachian Empire — Subaru should have gone with them to the royal capital.
And yet, he let himself lean on everyone's kindness instead.

"—Yes! I'm coming along this time!"

As Subaru, moved nearly to tears by all this warmth, stood there, it was Petra who shot her hand up sharply and spoke.
Naturally, the trip to the Pleiades Watchtower would once again be a fairly long journey. Someone would be needed to look after everyone along the way and at the tower itself.
Volunteering herself for that role, Petra declared with fervor, cheeks flushed red—

"This time I'll make myself useful! Frederica-neesama, Ram-neesama, please look after the manor and Emilia-neesama and the others!"

"Y-yes... My, what spirit."

"Yes! I'm always the one left behind, so I decided this time I wouldn't hold back!"

Frederica, startled by Petra's valiant burst of energy, accepted it all the same.
As far as Subaru was concerned, having Petra along left nothing to be desired. With that, the members heading to the tower were settled, and Subaru finally turned to face Rem.
She'd finally made it back from Vollachia to Lugunica, and was about to go to Roswaal Manor — not the same manor holding so many of Rem's old memories, but there all the same, hoping to work through trial and error at her own pace to recover her former memories.

"It's incredibly frustrating that that's gonna move forward again without me, though..."

"It was the same when Rem was reunited with Sister, but please don't be frustrated over that. ...Are you really going?"

"Yeah, I'm going. I can't just leave Al hanging. You get it, right?"

"Rem understands. —That's exactly why you're unfair."

At those words, Subaru's eyes went wide with surprise.
At his reaction, Rem bowed her head and said, "That was too much,"

"Please come back safely. Until then, so you won't be disappointed, Rem will try her best not to remember anything."

"No, that's... that's just me being selfish. I don't want to get in the way of what Rem wants to do."

"Then that's impossible. —Because there's surely nothing you do that has no effect on Rem at all."

Saying that, she pressed a hand against his chest, and Subaru found he had nothing left to say.
Gazing at Subaru struck silent like that, Rem stuck her tongue out just a little — mischievously, as if to say she'd gotten one over on him — which only left Subaru with even less to say.

—Because even Subaru, who never seemed to grow up no matter how much time passed, understood perfectly well that this was Rem's way, and everyone's way, of cheering him on and pushing him forward.


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△


——And so, arriving once more at the town nearest the Augria Sand Dunes was today.

Thinking of everyone who'd sent him off, and of the tower's guardian from whom he'd been parted after his first attempt, Subaru felt a dull ache throb quietly in his chest.
In front of him, the proprietor, having heard the report that he'd reached the tower, lowered his face.

"Old man?"

"——"

Puzzled by the reaction, Subaru called out to him again, but the proprietor said nothing. Subaru shot a worried glance at Beatrice, checking whether he'd said something he shouldn't have.
But Beatrice only shook her head at his look.

"Nothing strange, I suppose. Only that you've got a milk mustache under your nose, I suppose."

"Ah! You could've told me sooner! I was trying to look so cool just now!"

"You did look cool, though? And also, I kind of thought it was cute."

"That's the kind of comment I only ever get from Emilia-tan, in my book..."

"Cute and cool" was an expression that suited Emilia well whenever she was at her most dashing. Most people could only ever manage one or the other, cute or cool, which made her a miraculously beautiful girl indeed.
Anyway—

"I see. I... see..."

While Subaru hastily wiped at his lip, he heard the proprietor mutter this with deep feeling. The proprietor then wiped at the corner of his eye with a finger and brought his palm down on the counter with a bang.

"Good. Well done. Drinks are on me today!"

"Seriously?! Then, since we're crossing the sand sea again and need to stock up on food, give me every last bit of food you've got in this place!"

"Don't push your luck!"

"Yeah, fair enough!"

The proprietor, in bright high spirits now, laughed along with Subaru at that. But still grinning, he glanced over at the girls accompanying Subaru, then tilted his head.
He was probably wondering why Emilia — who'd surely burned an even stronger impression into him than Subaru had, last time — wasn't at his side today. Subaru understood the feeling, but—

"Unfortunately, she's not with us today. But don't worry, she made it back safe and sound."

"I see. Well, so long as she's safe... but that makes for a shakier-looking lineup than last time, doesn't it."

"I'll have you know Betty was also one of the party that made it through last time, I suppose. Betty simply didn't show her face here before because she was busy treating a companion, I suppose."

"This is my true first time, but I won't lag behind Emilia-neesama either."

"Whoa, that's some confidence. Reassuring, mighty reassuring."

Subaru, who hadn't managed to pat her head earlier, reached to finally do so now as she puffed out her chest proudly — but the hand he extended was caught in an instant by the girl's own hand, shooting up from below.
Then, as Subaru blinked in surprise, the girl — Petra — peered up at him with a flat, narrow-eyed stare.

"Subaru, you just tried to pat my head, didn't you."

"Wha? Well, yeah... did you not like it?"

"...It's not that I didn't like it, but can I ask you something? Do you ever pat Emilia-neesama's or Rem-neesama's head?"

Asked in such a serious tone, Subaru blinked and fell to thinking.
Whether he patted Emilia's or Rem's head — well, now that she mentioned it, there had been a time he used to pat Rem's head, but if he tried patting Rem's head now, he might well get his arm twisted for it.
And as for patting Emilia's head, just imagining it made the difficulty level plain enough.

"No, for various reasons, I don't pat either of their heads. Don't do it."

"Then you can't pat mine either."

"Seriously?!"

"Seriously!"

Turning her face away with a huff, she flung his caught hand aside, and Subaru was left in shock. That Petra would ever go through a rebellious phase like this was, for Subaru, a bolt from the blue.
At the sight of Subaru so deflated, Beatrice shrugged and said, "Honestly, I suppose,"

"Then pat Betty's head instead. Right now, it's pat-all-you-like, I suppose."

"Uughh, please, Bea-ko, keep the all-you-can-pat service running forever, would you..."

"Hmph, that all depends on your attitude going forward, I suppose. See to it that you pat carefully enough that Betty keeps thinking it isn't so bad, having her head patted by you, I suppose."

"That's a high bar you're setting! But I'll clear it...!"

Toward the loftily aloof Beatrice, Subaru extended a hand with every nerve down to his fingertips attuned to the task. At the gentle touch of Subaru's hand, Beatrice let out a soft "hwaa."

"T-this is... rather... not bad at all, I suppose... in fact, is this good? Is it good, I suppose? Betty had no idea you were still hiding potential like this, I suppose...!"

"Naive, Bea-ko. Time apart is what nurtures love."

"I really think that too. Time apart really is like that, isn't it."

"Petra-san? There's kind of, a lot of pressure coming off you right now, isn't there...?"

As Subaru patted Beatrice's head with his god hand, Petra scooted her chair a half-step closer, her sharp gaze stabbing into him.
But Petra herself, at his words, feigned innocence with an "Is that so~?" As Subaru quailed under her attitude,

"Well, I dunno what to call it, but I get that it's a fun bunch. Still, you gonna be alright? Just 'cause it went well once doesn't mean it's a place you get to go into underestimating."

"Yeah, I know. The most dangerous one — the sniper who showed a lot of skin — is gone now, but the sand wind and the Witchbeasts are both still alive and well. Oh, right — that tip you gave us, old man, about the birds flying toward the tower! That saved us a ton."

"Glad to hear it — but you understand the problems too, right? What are you gonna do about 'em?"

"Well, that's exactly what this meeting's for."

In conquering the Augria Sand Dunes, three barriers stood in a challenger's path — one was the "Sand Hour," when the sand wind grew extraordinarily fierce; one was the ferocious Witchbeasts living in the sand sea; and the last was Shaula, though sadly, that particular worry was no longer necessary.
And so, having to deliberately brave the "Sand Hour," as for the Witchbeast most likely to obstruct that challenge—

"—Ugh, honestly, this area's just as covered in sand as ever~"

That petulant voice, along with the sound of sand being brushed off a body, came from the entrance, and Subaru and the others turned around at once, as if they'd been waiting for exactly this, and took in the newcomer.
Standing there was a girl in black attire, her hair worn in a blue braid.
When she noticed the eyes of Subaru and the others, seated in a row at the counter, she blinked in surprise for a moment before smiling mischievously.

"Yoo-hoo, mister, so you made it back safe and sound~. And Petra-chan and Beatrice-chan look well too, I'm glad~"

So saying, she — Meili Portroute — waved a hand, and a small scorpion popped its head out from her hair, wagging its tail in time with her movement.
In just that fashion, Meili and her little red scorpion together welcomed Subaru and the others back to the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica, and the long-awaited moment of reunion.

At Meili's appearance, Subaru turned and grinned back at the proprietor beyond the counter.

"That girl right there is our number one ally for conquering the Augria Sand Dunes."

"A grinning fool of a guy with three girls in tow... don't tell me the Sage's tower isn't as far off as I always figured?"

At the proprietor's words, spoken in blank astonishment, Subaru had to admit — now that he mentioned it — that this really was a lineup that invited exactly that reaction, and scratched his cheek with one hand while continuing to pat Beatrice's head with the other.
