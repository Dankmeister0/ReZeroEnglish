Arc 10 Chapter 6: "Instant Water Heater"

---

The Prison Tower was thrown into an uproar, people with panicked faces rushing busily back and forth.

Watching the scene from a distance, Subaru leaned his back against a wall a little ways off, hugging his knees.

"――――"

Resting his forehead on his drawn-up knees, Subaru let out a deep breath.

There had been too many shocking things, one after another. Coming face to face with a gruesome corpse without any mental preparation was bad enough — but that the body should turn out to be—

"...Are you all right?"

A voice called down to the head buried in his knees, and Subaru slowly raised his face. There stood Rem, her expression clouded with concern.

Crouching down right beside him, she peered into his face and gently wiped his dejected features with the handkerchief in her hand. The faintly dampened cloth was cool, and Subaru, feeling saved, let out a breath along with a "Sorry."

"There's no need to apologize. ...I hear you saw something terrible in there."

"Yeah. Part of it was that I didn't want you seeing that guy, but... just to be safe, I'm glad I had Rem wait outside. At least you didn't have to see something like that."

"Please don't be so overprotective. I've seen plenty of deaths in the Vollachia Empire. At this point, a little something like that wouldn't—"

"I get it. But if you don't have to be exposed to it, I want you to not be. If it's a situation where you tried to save someone and just didn't have the strength and they died — that can't be helped. But that... that was the kind of death that has nothing good to come from looking at it at all."

Even Subaru didn't deny that there could be meaningful deaths, deaths that came as the result of holding fast to one's convictions.

If he could deny even that much, he wouldn't have silently seen Priscilla off.

But the death he'd just witnessed at the Watchtower — the gruesome death of Roy Alphard of "Gluttony" — was something ugly and abominable, worlds apart from that sort of nobility.

There was no sane meaning in taking another's life and then desecrating the corpse to that degree.

What lay behind it was either some wicked scheme, or else a hatred so fierce it wasn't satisfied until it had tormented even a soulless corpse — and while an Archbishop of Sin might well deserve to be hated, no one, whoever they were, ought to die a death that brutal.

"Damn it...!"

Feeling something nauseating churn in his chest, Subaru clicked his tongue in frustration.

It was anger at whoever had torn Roy apart, and at the same time, frustration that the solution to the Authority of "Gluttony" — a thread he'd thought he could see — had slipped further out of reach.

Honestly, the thought had crossed his mind. — That if, after joining up with Reinhard, they had rushed straight to the royal capital without any detours, Roy might not have had to die.

And ultimately, Subaru had a means to confirm that. In fact, back when his body had shrunk, the Subaru with the mindset forged in the Empire would have carried it out without hesitation.

But—

"...Al."

Clenching the Al Orb hanging from his neck, Subaru bit the inside of his cheek.

Hypothetically, if Subaru were to lose his life right now and "Return by Death," the nearest restart point would be at the Pleiades Watchtower, before he'd sealed Al away with Ol Shamak. There was a chance the restart point had shifted since then, but as far as his current sense of it went, that was the last one.

— And Natsuki Subaru could not go back to that timeline again and repeat that moment, forestalling Al as he moved to act and sealing him away first.

"――――"

It wasn't that it was impossible as an option. Nor was it because of some restriction of Ol Shamak.

It was simply that Natsuki Subaru lacked the courage to go back to that scene and face Al again. If he pinned his hopes on the chance that he might change Al's mind through talking, he might fail to stop him — and Subaru himself might end up the one sealed away instead.

If that happened, the resolution of Subaru's own mess would once again fall to his companions. To avoid that, Subaru couldn't bring himself to entertain a rash "Return by Death."

But then what about Rem, what about the victims of "Gluttony" — it was right as that thought crossed his mind.

"...There you go again, trying to carry everything yourself."

Without warning, Rem, having said that, laid her hand over Subaru's, the one clenched around the Al Orb.

Subaru caught his breath despite himself, and Rem, looking him square in the face, narrowed her pale blue eyes.

"I'll tell you this much. — Please don't make it your duty to get my 'memories' back. My 'memories' are my problem... It troubles me, having you take it on from start to finish as if it were your own problem."

"—! No, that's not it. That's not what this is. Your 'memories' — I have to get them back."

"That's exactly what's wrong. Why is it you who agonizes over my problem more than anyone else? That's my worry. It doesn't belong to you alone."

"That's..."

Subaru's eyes wandered, and he found he had nothing to say under Rem's gaze.

What crossed his mind was the chill of that moment when he'd thought he might die, back in the walled city, when Rem had told him, "You are not a hero." Was he, like that time, about to have even the right to struggle for Rem's sake taken away from him—

"...Please don't make a face like a soaked puppy."

"Huh?"

"No, that was too generous a comparison. Even soaked, a puppy would still be cute, so your look is more like a soaked... soaked Flying Dragon, or something."

"...I'd welcome being called cool more than being called cute, though."

"It wasn't a compliment. And please don't misunderstand something else."

Subaru, bewildered at being told he was misunderstanding something, waited as Rem gathered her words in a deliberate pause. Then, prefacing it with "Listen,"

"I understand the general situation. The person held captive in this tower... someone connected to Spica-chan, was a lead to recovering my 'memories.' But that person died, and you're terribly frustrated about it. ...Even ahead of me, the one actually concerned."

"...When you put it that way, I sound like a real self-centered jerk."

"It seems you've finally seen where you actually stand."

Calmly, methodically, Rem paved the road for Subaru's understanding. Listening to her, Subaru — who had cornered himself without anyone's help — found room to breathe returning to him.

Perhaps she could see it in the change in his expression, because Rem, too, relaxed the corners of her eyes as if relieved.

"I'm upset too, you know. Standing on the brink of maybe getting my 'memories' back... But seeing you more frustrated about it than I am, I don't even have time to be upset."

"Well, uh, I'm glad it worked out that Rem didn't have to be upset."

"What? Instead, you're the one who's upset, and worrying the rest of us about it, you know?"

"I'm sooorryyy!"

Under Rem's stern gaze, Subaru clutched his head and declared his apology.

Of all things, to be treated as a thief of the right to be upset. But it was Subaru's honest feeling — he thought it would be fine if Rem and Emilia and the others never had to spend even a moment being upset, for as long as they lived.

"That's exactly it — it seems like you think it's fine as long as only you're the one upset, and I don't like that."

"Rem..."

"When something doesn't go well, I think it's your bad habit to believe it's entirely your own fault. And so you try to search for the solution all by yourself too... Won't you stop that already?"

"...So basically, you mean, don't agonize over it alone?"

"I've been saying that from the start."

"Were you saying that!? No, wait, maybe you were. Yeah, you were. You were saying it. I'm an idiot for not noticing you were saying it! Idiot, idiot!"

In front of Rem, who had abruptly turned her face away, Subaru thumped his own head with his fists. As he did, he felt yet another, separate strand of self-reproach rearing its head.

After all, Rem had spoken up to him now out of nothing but pure concern. And yet, because Subaru hadn't tried to properly face it, he'd failed to accept her thoughtfulness head-on, and made her say even things she hadn't needed to say.

"Sorry. I dragged you out here all fired up, and all I did was get your hopes up for nothing."

"I don't think that's something you're responsible for. It's just..."

"Just?"

"We'll end up putting an unnecessary burden on Spica-chan, won't we."

With the deaths of Lye Batenkaitos and Roy Alphard — the two who had called themselves the Archbishops of Sin of "Gluttony," the "Gourmet" and the "Foul Feeder" — Spica alone remained as the sole surviving holder of a claim to that Authority.

Which meant that the hope of ever recovering the "names" and "memories" still devoured by "Gluttony" now rested entirely on Spica's shoulders.

Rem looked uneasy at the thought of placing that pressure on Spica, but—

"Spica probably won't think of it as an unnecessary burden at all. ...But let's get in touch with Abel and have him make sure Spica's surroundings are properly looked after. Have Cecchi keep a proper watch too, or whatever — whether or not Cecchi's actually up to it is another matter."

"...Right."

This was a tragedy that had befallen one connected to "Gluttony." There was no guarantee the same threat wouldn't close in on Spica as well. Subaru shared the worry and unease Rem harbored, and resolved to reach out to Vollachia. Having settled on what he himself needed to do, he didn't want any interference to reach Spica, left behind in Vollachia.

Whatever measures he could take toward that end, he had to take them, even if it meant being called overprotective.

"—Sorry to keep you waiting."

Just as the conversation between Subaru and Rem had reached a pause, Reinhard came walking up, raising a hand lightly in greeting, having emerged from inside the Prison Tower. Reinhard had Beatrice at his side, and Subaru hurriedly got to his feet, brushing off his backside to greet the two of them.

"Reinhard, how was it inside? Sirius, is she..."

"Fortunately, I suppose I can say. I was nearly certain of it already, but she, held in the cell underground, was unharmed. Each cell in the Prison Tower is isolated from the others, apparently — she didn't even know 'Gluttony' had been imprisoned in the same tower. And it's not just her. Not a single one of the other prisoners was harmed either."

"Sirius and the other prisoners are safe... Well, whether that counts as good news outright or not, at least one thing's clear now."

At Reinhard's report, given after finishing his check of the tower's interior, Subaru nodded with a bitter expression.

Which was to say, the perpetrator who had committed this crime in the Prison Tower had exactly one target—

"—The Archbishop of Sin of 'Gluttony.'"

Subaru's and Reinhard's answers overlapped, and the two exchanged a glance and nodded to each other.

Given the state of the scene, there was no other way to put it. And yet, even that certainty wasn't free of nagging questions.

"...From what Beatrice-chan told me, that wouldn't have been a simple thing to pull off, would it?"

"Ended up finishing the verification, I suppose. That understanding isn't wrong, I suppose."

Dipping her chin toward Rem, who glanced her way, Beatrice came up to stand at Subaru's side. Quietly taking his hand as she did, she studied Subaru's condition.

"Feeling at least a little better, I suppose?"

"Not exactly better, but I'm doing better than before. Sorry for leaving the scene investigation all to you."

"Betty was the one who volunteered, I suppose. Besides, there's nothing even Betty can do for the dead, I suppose. What Betty can offer is only a Great Spirit's perspective on things, I suppose."

"I'd very much like to hear that opinion. I'm not well versed in matters of this sort."

Prompted by Reinhard, the three of them turned their eyes to Beatrice, who closed one eye. She gave a small "ahem" and swept her gaze over the three of them as if about to lecture.

"Originally, that 'Gluttony's' body was locked down solid with Shadow Magic, I suppose. Betty and Julius worked together on it, so it was airtight security that wasn't going to budge for just anything, I suppose. ...It's frustrating that Betty couldn't come up with that refined version on her own, I suppose."

"On that point, I've confirmed it with the Prison Tower's staff. The captured 'Gluttony' was completely... stopped, as if turned into a single stone tablet, they said."

"It's a thing that can't be tampered with from the outside either, let alone by the one imprisoned, I suppose. Undoing it would require a corresponding mastery of Shadow Magic, I suppose."

"That's not all. Even if you managed to get him out of the monolith, then you'd have to go up against Roy the moment he sprang out. And go up against him—"

"—and take his life. In other words, two stages would be needed."

At Rem's summing-up conclusion, Subaru and the others fell silent, their faces troubled.

As they'd established so far, killing Roy in his monolith state required both Shadow Magic skill enough to undo the monolith, and afterward, enough strength to actually kill Roy. Neither was something achievable overnight, nor were people with such abilities lying around out in the world by the handful.

"Not to mention, they also managed to slip past the Prison Tower's security net to get in. Shadow Magic, combat prowess, and stealth — assembling all three conditions at once is no easy feat."

"With all sorts of grudges thrown in, I'm half tempted to just cling to the conclusion that Olbart's the culprit."

Unfortunately, even the "Vicious Elder" couldn't satisfy the condition of being a wielder of Shadow Magic.

Then again, if it turned out one of the Shinobi Techniques could produce a similar effect and Olbart had been quietly doing this all along, Subaru wouldn't be all that surprised, ability-wise — it'd be right at the edge of believable.

"There's no doubt Vollachia's people are a warlike sort by nature, but surely, given the country's current situation, they wouldn't recklessly choose to jeopardize diplomatic relations. To begin with, I've heard that Lady Emilia and the others coming to the royal capital isn't unrelated to the matter of Vollachia either."

"...Yeah. Rather than suspect Vollachia of having a death wish, I'd rather believe that those wolves over there have gotten a bit more sensible. But, I'm sorry about all of this, start to finish, Reinhard."

"Sorry? Was there something you needed to apologize for?"

"You went out of your way to make a detour just to help bring Rem out here, and on top of that, you smoothed things over so we could get into the Prison Tower. And yet it ended up like this... ah."

"—Glaring."

Just as he was about to apologize for having made everyone waste their effort, Subaru suddenly noticed Rem's cold gaze fixed on him.

So this too was part of Subaru's bad habit of thinking everything was his own fault, was it.

Still, the fact remained that he had already thought it, so taking it back now felt awkward too.

"It's a shame the results didn't pan out, I suppose. But I'm grateful you went to all that trouble, I suppose."

"That! As expected of my Beako! So, what do you say, Rem?"

"Haaah..."

"A sigh!"

Rem's cold gaze gave way to a sigh, and Subaru covered his face with his hand. Watching the exchange, Reinhard let a small smile touch his lips.

Then, smile still in place, he said,

"It's nothing much. — You're welcome."

"...I see, so 'Gluttony'... Somehow, it's frustrating how things just won't go smoothly."

In a room of the Royal Manor, having received the report on what had happened at the Prison Tower, Emilia let those words slip, her shapely brows tilted at an angle, her complexion noticeably pale.

Such a show of fatigue was a rare thing for the always energetic Emilia. Even so, Subaru, aware that he himself bore some part of the blame for that fatigue, felt hopeless about it.

— Parting ways with Reinhard, who returned to Felt's side, Subaru, accompanied by Rem and Beatrice, headed to the Royal Manor where Emilia and the others were staying. There, they joined up with the group that had arrived earlier.

At the Royal Manor, nearly everyone from the Emilia Camp was gathered, save for Roswaal and Frederica, who had headed to the Barielle domain along with Schult.

Which was to say—

"So after driving our exhausted bodies all the way to the royal capital, it turns out it was all for nothing. Well, aren't you good at getting people's hopes up for nothing, Barusu."

"For the record, everyone besides you feels disappointed too, and among all of us, my regret's top-class. I nearly wept tears of blood over it."

"Hah! Tears of blood? That's just the blood vessels around your eyeballs bursting. Ram could make that happen for you right now, you know."

"Scary! Just because you're shocked doesn't mean you have to get this vicious!"

And so, Ram, more aggressive than usual, was likewise present.

Naturally, when it came to bringing Rem out to the royal capital, Ram — upon hearing the purpose — had strongly insisted on coming along without fail. Subaru understood the older-sister feeling that there was no way she could not be present for the moment Rem's "memories" might return, and since he'd wanted Ram there too, he'd agreed.

Still, from the challenge at the Pleiades Watchtower to the forced incursion into the Empire, the burden of these past few months of travel had likely been heavy. Even Ram, who had been holding herself up on sheer willpower, finally had the thread of her tension snap now that this attempt had ended in a bust, and though she still had sharp words to spare, she was thoroughly worn out.

In the end, after finishing off that bit of sarcasm at Subaru just now, she was led off by Rem and Petra straight to the bedroom, and it looked like she'd be under care there for a while.

"Though, being worn down isn't limited to Ram-san — it's true of all of us. There's the trouble with you and the others, Natsuki-san, the murder at the Prison Tower, and now the appearance of the 'Divine Dragon Church' and the chaos brought to the Royal Selection by the person at the center of it."

Watching Ram leave the room, Otto, his brows drooping, grumbled just so.

Being part of the group that had gone ahead to the royal capital with Emilia, he was supposed to have given the royal castle a thorough report on the "Great Disaster" of the Vollachia Empire, including the news of Priscilla's death. Among the topics to cover was the matter of the future agreement between Empire and Kingdom entrusted to him by Abel, and he must have taken pride in the fact that it was a major role, both for the camp's sake and the Kingdom's.

And then it had all been blindsided from a completely unexpected angle, thrown into a situation where there was no room for any kind of mental preparation. Even Subaru couldn't come up with words to tease Otto about it.

In the midst of all that, Subaru was agonizing over just what he should even bring up first—

"—Will you tell me about Al?"

As if she'd seen right through Subaru's hesitation, Emilia chose that topic as the first item to discuss.

Flam, Felt's attendant, could send information to her twin sister regardless of distance, through the power of her Divine Protection. Borrowing that power, they'd already conveyed a simple account of what had happened to Subaru and the others at the Pleiades Watchtower. But that had been the barest outline; the details were another matter.

Understanding how anxious Emilia and the others must have felt, Subaru explained it as thoroughly as he could.

That they'd gone to the Pleiades Watchtower to search for the "Book of the Dead." That, that night, Al had betrayed them and tried to do them harm, but they'd forestalled him and sealed him away first. That there had been no chance to talk through the root of the reason that had driven Al to such a violent act.

And also that he had no intention of ending things with Al while still not knowing that reason.

"—Mm, I feel the same way as Subaru. I think that's for the best."

After hearing everything, Emilia thought it over for a while before affirming Subaru's thinking that way, and it made him glad. Her violet eyes wavered with complicated feeling even as she quietly fixed her gaze on the Al Orb at Subaru's neck and gave a small nod.

Along the way, Subaru had already told Beatrice and Petra his thinking. So the ones for whom his current explanation brought fresh reactions were Emilia, who had just answered him so, Otto, wearing a face like he'd bitten into a bitter persimmon, and—

"—Al-san loved Priscilla-san. I can't say much more than that."

—Rem, who, having put Ram to bed, had returned to the sitting room along with Petra.

And so, the Emilia Camp's stance toward how to handle Al's actions was settled, for the time being. As for Otto's inner thoughts, which he'd kept to himself, perhaps so as not to disturb the mood — Subaru figured he'd have him vent them later together with Garfiel — and gave his own cheeks a sharp slap with both hands.

On the matter of Al, and the matter of the Prison Tower, there was nothing to be done about either right now. Which meant, if there was a topic that could still be raised at this point, the remaining agenda item was—

"—Let's talk about the 'Divine Dragon Church,' and this person named Filore."

About Filore, a presence that might well shake the very foundations of the Royal Selection.

"――――"

The moment that topic was raised, the air in the sitting room changed completely. But it wasn't so much charged with tension or urgency as it was thick with a much cruder sense of bewilderment and confusion.

In truth, Subaru included, everyone was, in fact, greatly at a loss over the whole matter.

"To begin with, what's the situation over at the castle right now? The premise that the 'Divine Dragon Church' doesn't get involved in the heart of politics, the story of a sixth candidate turning up who can make her crest glow, and the fact that she's going by the same name as the princess who vanished fifteen years ago — all of that came out of nowhere for everyone, right?"

"Um, seems like all of that is being discussed right this moment... But this time, the Church broke its 'ruuru' because it wanted to help the people who suffered in Priestella, right? Nobody could say that's a bad thing, and in fact..."

"—They healed Crusch-san's body. You saw it too, right, Emilia-tan, Otto?"

"Yes, we saw it firsthand. Right, Otto-kun?"

"I couldn't very well stare too intently at a lady, but at the very least, whatever black, twisted marks had been visible before seemed to have vanished."

Beside Emilia, who was nodding, Otto's gaze, as he answered, flicked briefly toward Subaru's leg.

Was that meant to suggest that, under the same conditions, Subaru's leg could be healed too? Honestly, it wasn't a huge inconvenience, but every time they bathed together, he might have been making Otto and Garfiel feel awkward about it, so if it could be fixed, he'd like to have it fixed.

"But owing the 'Divine Dragon Church' a favor just for that is kind of scary too... ah!"

"Aw, what's with you getting all jumpy, mister?"

"No, I was just thinking this might be another case of me putting myself last and getting scolded for it..."

"If you understand that much, then act with a bit more confidence about it."

Poked at by Meili and left exasperating Rem, that was how it ended. Still, saying out loud that he didn't want to make a deal disadvantageous to the camp over something with no real harm to himself seemed like it would get him scolded, so all Subaru could do was cover with a clumsy "ahem, ahem" throat-clear.

Then, though probably not meaning to come to Subaru's rescue, Garfiel, seated on the room's windowsill, spoke up with an "Oi."

Scratching roughly at his own head, he began, somewhat hesitantly,

"There's probably all sorts'a complicated circumstances an' whatnot. So what I'm 'bout to say is just how I feel about it, nothin' more."

"Garfiel... Mm, tell us. What is it?"

"...If they can heal the folks in Priestella, then I say heal 'em quick, and let 'em be healed. Black Dragons, flies, it's been nothin' but rough stuff, ain't it."

With one knee drawn up, half his body bathed in sunlight, Garfiel's plea carried real weight.

Garfiel had a family he'd grown close to in Priestella. During that battle, the father of that family had been transformed into a Black Dragon by the wicked hand of "Lust," and had even been used as bait at one point. Even now, his body, still transformed into a Black Dragon, lay sleeping frozen in a corner of the city, just like the other victims, by Emilia's hand.

If someone in that position could be healed, then who you relied on to do it was a secondary concern — that was what Garfiel was saying.

"And I feel the same way. If there are people suffering and going through something painful, and there's a way to help them, then it doesn't matter who does the helping. That's how I see it."

"Hmm, so that's the kind of thing you say, Subaru? Even though you went and dove headfirst into danger insisting you should be the one to wake Rem-neesama up."

"Come to think of it, I might've said something like that, but you sure remember well, Petra!"

"Of course I remember. Because I was pretty shocked about it, you know. I thought it was unfair to Rem-neesama, and I also thought about how, even hearing that, I'm not the type to get discouraged."

At Petra, pouting her lips mischievously, Subaru scratched his head, unable to argue back.

In truth, before Rem woke up, while saying that anyone could be the one to wake her from her endless sleep, he had, in fact, boasted that he wanted to be the one to do it. Though he was a little too scared to look and see what kind of face Rem, hearing all this, was making right now.

In any case—

"Setting aside what I said in the past, let's look to the future. ...No matter how the discussions at the castle end up settling, now that it's been proven the 'Divine Dragon Church' has a way to heal them, the people of Priestella will get treated... right?"

"I'd say so. No matter which way the heated debate leans, I don't think anyone will hesitate on that particular point. Whether it's treated as a simple debt owed to the 'Divine Dragon Church,' or as something more than that — that's probably where the focus will be."

"The sixth candidate for shrine maiden qualifications... huh."

Ultimately, how that person's standing would be handled wasn't something for Subaru and the others to weigh in on.

Depending on how the people of the royal castle, or rather, the "Council of Wise Men," decided such matters, this camp, centered on Emilia, would in turn decide its own stance.

Having thought that far, what nagged at Subaru was Filore herself.

"Emilia-tan, you met her in person directly, right? What kind of person was she?"

"Let's see... I thought she was a really, really hardworking, good person."

"I see. What do you think of Meili, then?"

"Huh? Meili? I think Meili's a really, really considerate friend and a hard worker, and a good girl."

"I get what you're trying to say, Subaru-oniisan, but stop using me as an example, will you?"

It wasn't that Subaru doubted Emilia's judgment of people, but under Emilia's assessment, just about anyone could carelessly end up judged as a sincere, earnest, hardworking good person. That said, since Subaru's own opinion of Meili was similar, it would be wrong to doubt Emilia's discernment on that basis alone.

Though hearing Abel's assessment instead might have been more on the mark.

"What about you, Otto? If you even got a glimpse, what did you think?"

"I didn't exchange many words with her, but I got the impression of a devout believer of the 'Divine Dragon Church.' Someone who sincerely believes that serving others is the best course, and also..."

"And also?"

"There seems to be a somewhat thoughtless side to her too. From what I heard, this whole matter was Filore-san's own unilateral decision, made in disregard of the Church's wishes."

"Unilateral? You mean helping Crusch-san, or making her crest glow?"

"The former, yes, though the latter is apparently just how things played out. Somehow, given the circumstances, I can't help but think the 'Divine Dragon Church' wanted to keep her existence hidden."

"Well, now, that's..."

No one present had an answer to Subaru's question of why.

In truth, was Filore's true identity the princess who had vanished fifteen years ago, or not? What of her appearance, said to show the characteristic traits of the Lugunica royal family, or what her name meant? Why had the "Divine Dragon Church" kept her existence secret while letting the Royal Selection begin and proceed on its own?

There were simply too many mysteries. Too many, and so—

"Say, Emilia-tan—"
"Listen, Subaru, actually—"

Their faces both lifting at once, Subaru and Emilia's voices overlapped as each tried to voice the same idea. Eyes widening in surprise, the two looked at each other, and feeling as if their thoughts had passed straight through their eyes, they laughed together.

It seemed Emilia had been thinking the exact same thing.

Namely—

"—Don't you want to go meet this person, Filore, directly, and talk with her?"

— Thinking back on it, Subaru had never really gotten a proper grasp of the royal capital of Lugunica as a whole.

There was the spot near the fountain in the commoners' quarter where he'd made his grand, dramatic entrance summoned from another world; the merchant district with the fruit shop whose owner he saw often; the various candidates' bases, where, after a thoroughly miserable falling-out and split with Emilia, he'd been kicked while already down; and the slum quarter with the Loot House besides — he had attachments to each of these places, but they amounted to only a small fraction of the sprawling capital.

Which was why the "Divine Dragon Church" chapel, standing quietly tucked away in a corner of the capital's commoners' quarter, was, for Subaru, something like a blind spot right under his nose — as if it had been built entirely outside his awareness.

"To think there was a church this easy to find, right here..."

"You're really, really surprised, huh. But a church... look, we saw one back in Priestella too, at the place where my wedding to Regulus was almost held, remember?"

"Oh, the place where I busted down the door! Come to think of it, that was a 'Divine Dragon Church' too, huh?"

With the surefire combination of church and wedding, back then, his sense of mission to save Emilia at all costs had run ahead of him, leaving no room to consider the setting's background. But now that he thought about it, that had certainly been a church, and given that the Kingdom had no custom of worshipping any god, a church located there was, inevitably, bound to be connected to the "Divine Dragon Church."

"If that's the case, then Wedding-Emilia-tan was a feast for the eyes, but the incident itself is a memory I'd rather forget, so my impression of the 'Divine Dragon Church' is starting off in the negatives."

"Betty wasn't there for that, but I suppose the Archbishops of Sin really don't do anything decent, do they. Still, letting that lower your impression of the 'Divine Dragon Church' is an unfair complaint, I suppose."

"Well, this one's flippant remarks are hardly something new, after all."

"Big boos from both Beako and Rem... I guess girls really do dream about wedding dresses? Is that why speaking badly of churches gives off a bad image or something?"

At Subaru's timid question, Beatrice and Rem exchanged a look, then both shrugged in unison. At that reaction, Subaru's shoulders slumped in dejection, and Emilia, hand at her mouth, giggling, smiled to smooth things over.

Catching that smile out of the corner of his eye, Subaru, seeing a good opportunity, pulled himself back together.

"I mean, sure, all sorts of circumstances led to this outing, but me and Emilia-tan, plus Beako and Rem... moving as this group, isn't this a dream party in a way?"

"Whether it's dreamy or not, I suppose it's certainly a fairly rare thing."

"Now that you mention it, that's true. Until Subaru brought her out of the Forbidden Archive, Beatrice hardly ever went out with us. And surely it was the same for Rem, before she fell asleep, right?"

"I have no memory of that, but I'm sure it must have been so. Getting to go out with Emilia-san and Beatrice-chan is fun for me too, you know."

"Hey now, you just left me out on purpose, didn't you. I'm lonely, I'm sad."

Even so, since this was a rare and welcome combination of company, Subaru didn't mind at all.

For the time being, this was the lineup that made up the "let's go visit Filore of the 'Divine Dragon Church'" team, the members entrusted with the mission of scouting out the enemy's situation.

Now, finally, the chapel where the "Divine Dragon Church" people in question would presumably be found was drawing near, and Subaru was wondering just what pretext to use to call Filore out — when it happened.

"—Ugh, quit calling me a liar already! Fine, I'll show you. This is the proof that I've been up to the castle!"

A spirited voice like that came from within the grounds of a large building surrounded by a stone wall — one whose roof and general structure suggested it was probably a church — the top of it just visible over the wall.

No sooner had they blinked in surprise at whatever this was than Subaru and the others saw it. — Beyond the stone wall, a bright red light swelled faintly, asserting its presence.

"Could that be..."

"Waah! Amazing, amazing!"
"It really glowed! What'd you do? What'd you do?"
"She finally stole it! Thief!"

"First a liar, now a thief!? What do you all think I am!? I am a nun! A bearer of the sacraments! Practically a Saintess!"

The clamoring voices of small children, and the voice of a woman who seemed to be dealing with them. At the content of what they'd heard, Subaru and the others exchanged glances, then hurried around the stone wall.

And then, where the stone wall broke off and they reached the front entrance to the church grounds—

"Now then, behold! This is the radiant light of our 'Divine Dragon,' which burns away those with wicked hearts in their souls! Even the scriptures say as much: 'Man is born of earth, and the Dragon descends from the heavens. Those who forget to look up to the sky, and who presume too much upon wings of their own back, shall know their sin through their fall!'"

"Kyaaa!"
"Waaah!"
"Ugyaaah!"

Declaring this at the top of her voice, they witnessed a nun making the crest's Dragon Jewel glow bright red in her raised hand, and using it to chase after the children as they fled, shrieking.

Not noticing the visitors, she kept chasing after the fleeing children, crest still glowing, letting out an "Oh-ho-ho-ho!"

"Now then, who shall repent before the dazzling radiance of the exalted, sublime 'Dragon'... hm, oh?"

"――――"

Coming to an abrupt halt, the nun turned around behind her as if creaking on rusty hinges. There, she spotted Subaru and the others watching the scene, and her mouth fell open in shock.

Seeing the reaction of the nun with golden hair and red eyes, Subaru found himself at a loss for what to say. Just then, beside him, Emilia rummaged around inside her clothes,

"Look, look, I can do the same thing too!"

and, pulling out her own crest, made it glow red just like the nun — Filore's — following up with no ill intent at all, as innocently as an angel, to say she was the same.

Right after, watching Filore's white skin — from her neck to her face to her ears — flush red all at once with embarrassment, Subaru thought, vaguely, that she was just like an instant water heater.
