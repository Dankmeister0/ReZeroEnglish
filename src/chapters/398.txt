"HUH!? CALLING ME BY MY SURNAME WITHOUT HONORIFICS MAKES US FEEL DISTANT, DOESN'T IT!?"

"Eh?"

tIt was a flow that was supposed to build a good mood, but that blew away completely, leaving me astounded.
tImmediately, Emilia made a surprised face as well.

tLooking at each other's faces, we both tilted our heads with a "Eh?".

"B-But, you said... changing how we address each other is proof of getting closer, right?"

"That's why I thought since we already had the stance of adding '-kun' to my name, dropping the honorific to just my name would be the next step!"

"That's why I dropped the honorific to your name...!"

tWhile trying to reconcile each other's intentions, Emilia suddenly choked on her words.
tHer eyes went wide, and pointing her finger at me—

"Could it be that in 'Natsuki Subaru', Subaru is the given name!?"

"Yes it is!? Didn't I tell you!?"

"B-But, there are people without surnames like me! And in most other people's names, the given name comes first, so I assumed...!"

tAt the panicked Emilia's statement, I finally understood the circumstances.
tI see! In other words, Emilia thought my given name was 'Natsuki'. And she tried to be bold by calling me by my given name without honorifics, only to blunder.

"So from Emilia's perspective, calling me 'Natsuki-kun' was what you were doing all along. That kinda changes the mood of today a bit."

"O-On my end, too..."

tThe strange sense of superiority that I was being called by my given name by a girl I just met turned out to be a misunderstanding, so I got a bit crestfallen.
tHowever, Emilia was furious at my crestfallen state. Flushing red, she looked at me with slightly teary eyes—

"T-To think I was calling a boy I just met by his given name out of nowhere... i-it's so embarrassing!"

"――――"

"N-No! Don't look over here! My face is bright red right now...!"

tPlacing both hands over her cheeks, Emilia tried to hide her face, which had apparently turned bright red.
tAt her attitude, I couldn't take my eyes off her.

tWhere did my crestfallen mood from earlier go? It was completely overwritten by a new surprise and emotion, becoming entirely invisible.
tBecause--

"You're so cute, Emilia-tan."

"That's... EH!? What!? Where did 'tan' come from!?"

"It's a change in address. See, when an affectionate nickname gets attached, it becomes cute, right?"

"Is that a cute nickname!? Liar, you're just teasing me anyway!"

tWith her face remaining bright red, Emilia began lightly patting my body over and over.
tIt was light—way too light. Compared to Ram, it didn't hurt at all. Of course, it was because she had no intention of causing pain, but it felt somehow funny.

"Uwahahaha--!"

"Geez, geez, Subaru, you idiot! I don't care anymore!"

tAlong the night road home from school, Emilia and I walked back while raising loud laughter and pouting voices.
tBefore we knew it, the way she addressed me had changed too, but I didn't point it out.

tI just felt that school life starting tomorrow was going to seem like something completely different.
tWithout knowing what the true nature of what I felt toward the girl walking beside me was, I simply surrendered to the impulse of laughter, satisfied with the conclusion of this day.
