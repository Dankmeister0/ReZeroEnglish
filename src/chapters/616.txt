Re:Zero EX: "Life in a World Mistaken from Zero II"
*An annual April Fools' joke taken a bit too far.*
*Please consider this a non-canonical side story and enjoy it with an open mind.*




"The pillow feels different..."

Subaru muttered as she checked the soft, pillowy sensation against the back of her head.
It wasn't just the pillow that felt different from her usual awakening. Her body—just a hair taller than the average Japanese high school girl—the bed supporting it, and the silky sheets were all entirely unlike what she was used to.

"So in short... alternate world life continues..."

Springing up with her usual morning-person reflexes, Subaru felt the annoying brush of her hair spreading across the back of her neck as she confirmed the fact.
Subaru had awakened in a bed of absurd proportions—a far cry from her middle-class upbringing—inside a massive room with space to spare even with such a bed.
Given that it was assigned to an uninvited stranger like Subaru, it was likely a guest room, and she deduced there was more than just one or two rooms of this scale.

"Stomach wound, completely gone. No stitches in sight either. Man, doctors in another world sure are top-notch. If I ended up damaged goods, I'd never be able to get married."

Cracking such jokes as she stepped off the bed, Subaru flipped up her top to inspect the fair skin of her stomach. Tilting her head and nodding while annoyed at the inconvenience of not having a hair tie, she realized...
This meant—

"—Looks like this time I managed to avoid 'Return by Death'."

With a sigh of relief, she accepted the fact that she had somehow survived the massive blood loss at the end of the clash in the Loot House.


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△


Natsuki Subaru, a high school girl in the bloom of youth, suddenly summoned to an alternate world.
Cast into a parallel realm without any particular special skills, she nonetheless possessed a special Authority bestowed upon her, a staple of portal fantasy since time immemorial.
Its name: "Return by Death"—a super-powered ability that sent her back in time upon dying, allowing her to trial-and-error her way out of tragedies and forge a path toward her desired future.


"Well, triggering it causes mind-numbing agony, so it's a cheat skill I'd rather never use again if I can help it, though..."

"Muttering to yourself in someone else's sanctuary—you've got nerve, girl."

"Whoops, the first villager speaks."

A voice called out from behind Subaru as she lamented her abysmal luck, wishing she could have chosen her power when being summoned.
When she turned around, a small figure sitting atop a step ladder in the middle of a vast archive was staring at her with half-lidded eyes.

—It was a shota boy around ten years old, clad in a reddish-purple tailcoat with short, tightly curled cream-colored hair.

Distinctive patterns floated in his round, bluish eyes, and a slightly cheeky expression sat on his adorable, neat features. A large book lay open across the knees of his short-pants-clad legs, making it clear he was deeply annoyed at having his reading interrupted.
Be that as it may—

"Glaring at me with such a cute face isn't gonna work on a high school girl who writes off pretty much every mammal smaller than herself as 'sooo cuuuteee!'"

"...Not only did you ruin Vitcha's masterfully crafted labyrinth, but you keep spewing incomprehensible nonsense. You severely lack respect, girl."

"Labyrinth? You mean that corridor that looked like it'd loop forever? Sorry, sorry for hitting the jackpot on my first try. See, it's not just my speech—my intuition can't read the room either. Forgive me~"

Winking with one hand raised, Subaru brought up the corridor she had passed through after exiting the room where she first woke up, where she'd encountered a rather bizarre phenomenon.
After repeatedly passing decorative paintings, vases, a continuous carpet, and doors hanging the exact same nameplate, Subaru had judged it to be an endlessly repeating hallway. Without hesitation, she had chosen the door to the very first bedroom she'd come out of.

Opening it had, as expected, brought her not to her initial room, but to the curly-haired shota who had presumably created the corridor in the first place.

"What do you wanna do? Should I step back out and do a grand, dramatic double-take? If that'll satisfy your ego, I'll put on the performance of a lifetime for ya."

"...Good grief. Vitcha's patience has reached its limit."

"Oh?"

Closing the book, the child climbed down the ladder and walked toward Subaru.
Intimidated by his oddly pressuring aura, Subaru's legs froze, leaving her standing stock-still as he closed the distance. Then, an outstretched hand gently touched Subaru's stomach.

"Eek."

"If those are your last words, you truly are a hopelessly foolish girl."

"Ghyah—?!"

Instantly, a shockwave shot through Subaru's whole body, originating from the child's palm pressed against her belly.
Before she could even think to brace herself, Subaru collapsed helplessly on the spot. Despite being the culprit, the boy didn't bother to catch her.
As a result, she bumped her head on the floor. The carpet was plush, though, so it didn't hurt.

"Not very gentlemanly... for a boy... in two ways, here..."

"I am surprised you can still speak. Nor is that assessment wrong. —Do not measure a noble being like Vitcha by your pathetic human standards, human."

With that cold, dry declaration as the last thing she heard, Subaru's consciousness faded.
Honestly, she was passing out far too often since being summoned, and she caught herself worrying whether sleeping on the bare floor so much would make a delicate maiden catch a cold—before blacking out completely.


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△


"Oh, he's awake, Brother."
"Indeed. She's awake, Reu."

Once again, the plush, strange sensation behind her head, ditto as above.
What dragged Subaru back to reality after her pathetic blackout were the voices of two young men reaching her through her closed eyelids from the morning that had arrived.

"Not only seeing me wake up, but watching me sleep... my Maiden Score is plummeting with a crash..."

It wasn't like her face was anything extraordinary, but even an ordinary face deserved minimum rights guaranteed by the constitution—that was the beauty of constitutional law.
A constitution might be the polar opposite of what's trendy for high school girls, but surely it stated somewhere that one must not carelessly stare at a maiden's sleeping face or her waking moment.
It had to. If that wasn't protected by the constitution, how on earth were other bashful maidens enduring such disgrace and humiliation?

"I will firmly fight alongside the Constitution...! Until I eradicate every man from this world who carelessly peeks at a maiden waking up, gets disillusioned on his own, and runs his mouth...!"

"My, I sense a rather useless amount of enthusiasm, Brother."
"Ah, yes. A woman who has wasted her life on useless chatter and useless thoughts, Reu."

"Hey! Talking crap just 'cause you think I can't move from my heart pounding! I'm mad now, both of you step outside! I'll re-educate you—!"

Stung by their harsh words, Subaru, her humiliation shattered by rage, finally leaped up.
Kicking off the sheets in a huff, Subaru looked toward the foot of her bed to find two slender figures watching her.
Though one had pink hair and the other blue, their facial features, eyes, and immaculate butler uniforms made the young men look practically identical.
At the sight of the two, Subaru's leaping momentum halted—no, it swung off in a completely different direction.

"A-A-A pair of hot twin putlers...!!"

The twins, both sporting bob-like hair with slightly long bangs covering one eye, were perfectly symmetrical aside from their hair color, making Subaru's heart race.
However, despite Subaru's glowing assessment, their reaction was less than favorable.
The blue-haired young man knitted his brows slightly, while the pink-haired one cast an overtly contemptuous glare at her.

"Guh... restrain yourself, Subaru...! Honestly, the moment I woke up in a mansion, I should've anticipated handsome putlers. If they were hot older gentlemen, I would've lost my mind entirely."

"Brother, she is a guest, but could something be wrong with her?"
"Reu, it goes without saying—what's wrong is her head, and it's far too late."

"If anything, Brother's wording is seriously brutal!"

Despite Subaru snapping back at his sharp tongue, the twin putlers maintained a cool composure.
If they were going to treat her as a guest, she'd prefer to be pampered like a delicate princess.

"In that regard, I bet Emilio-kyun would treat me way gentler..."

"—Is that 'Emilio-kyun' supposed to be me?"

"Oh."

As she pouted at the twin putlers' cold demeanor, a third voice ambushed her ears—a voice that gently caressed Subaru's eardrums and sent a sweet tremor through her chest.
Turning around, she saw a figure peeking from behind the half-open door—with dark violet eyes and silver hair, a drop-dead gorgeous young man who captivated Subaru's soul at a single glance was standing right there.

"I'm so glad, Subaru. You woke up safely."

"EMILIO-KYUN IS HERE—!!"

At the arrival of the handsome boy smiling softly and genuinely relieved for her safety—Emilio—Subaru opened wide those eyes that had been repeatedly insulted for looking menacing, and shouted.
Emilio was taken aback by Subaru's outburst, but then relaxed his lips with a chuckle.

"What's that '-kyun' thing? Where did it come from?"

He asked such an innocent yet oddly difficult-to-answer question.


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△


"Pleased to meet you, Natsuki Subaru-kun. I am the owner of this mansion, Roswaal L. Mathers, I a-a-am."

After one thing and another, Subaru was invited to the breakfast table and introduced to Roswaal L. Mathers, an indigo-haired presumed beauty claiming to be the master of the mansion.
As for why she was a "presumed beauty"—

"Why the mask..."

"Naturally, as part of my little hobby-y-y. Mystery tickles one's curiosity, does it no-o-ot?"

Roswaal shrugged without a hint of remorse, the left half of her face covered by a white mask. It resembled half a clown mask, featuring a painted jester's grin. The visible right half of her face, paired with her yellow eye, made her look like a genuine beauty, suggesting the mask might be covering a scar or something.

"My gut tells me there's no tragic back-story like that behind it."

"It irks me to agree, but your deduction isn't bad. Demanding a reason for every little thing Roswaal does is foolish."

"And that's the seal of approval from the cheeky shota."

Snapping her fingers and pointing toward the end of the long, white-cloth-covered table, the small child seated there scowled.
The child she had encountered when first waking up in the mansion wasn't just a figure from a dream after all, but a real, living shota.
Watching Subaru and the boy interact, Roswaal raised an eyebrow in amusement.

"So you've already hit it off with Victor. And here I thought he was giving me the silent treatment lately—has his shyness been cured, I wonder-r-r?"

"That is an unnecessary and completely mistaken interpretation, Roswaal."

"See? Cold as always, he-e-e is."

Roswaal held up her hands to demonstrate her cold relationship with the standoffish child—Victor.
Subaru still didn't quite grasp Victor's standing in the mansion. He didn't look like Roswaal's son, so perhaps there was a complex family situation at play?
Even in a parallel world, noble households were bound to have their own webs of drama and trouble.
After all—

"Who would've thought Emilio-kyun was a king candidate... so basically a prince!"

"I'm no prince. I don't have deep ties to the royal castle or the Capital. In accordance with the Covenant of the 'Divine Dragon', I was simply chosen as one of the candidates for the Royal Selection."

"I don't know much about Divine Dragons or Covenants, but the proof of being a candidate is..."

"The insignia Subaru was trying so hard to get back in the Capital~"

Stealing the final word of Subaru and Emilio's conversation, Tink—who had her own mini set of tableware prepared at the table—chimed in with a glance.
A spirit contracted to Emilio who claimed to be his mother figure, she held the insignia hugged in her short arms, showing it off.

That was the very insignia Subaru had fought tooth and nail to recover, the macguffin that had cost her her life three times in the Capital—

"But hey, I'm standing right here! And my stomach wound is totally gone! Oh right, thanks for healing me, Emilio-kyun!"

"Ah, it wasn't me who healed you—it was Victor. Victor is extremely knowledgeable in all kinds of magic."

"Ehh?! This cheeky short-pants?!"

"I am currently regretting having healed you. Had I known you were such an impudent, noisy girl, I would never have lent a hand."

"No, sorry, sorry, I was just hiding my embarrassment. Thanks for saving me, Victa. I'll definitely repay this favor in some way eventually."

"Before that, what did you just call Vitcha?!"

Subaru blew a kiss to Victor, who was shouting in an adorable voice with wide eyes.
She then looked back at Emilio and Tink, who was lounging next to the insignia.

"Anyway, going back to what we were saying—if I hadn't helped, your insignia would've been stolen and you'd have been in big trouble, right, Emilio-kyun? Like, disqualified from the Royal Selection!"

"...Yeah, that's right. So I owe Subaru a huge favor. Healing a wound on your stomach isn't nearly enough to repay it."

"For our house as well, if Master Emilio were disqualified before the Selection even officially began, it would have been a severe blow. We feel we must reward Subaru-kun in some fashion for preventing such a disaster-r-r."

"Yep, yep, exactly the direction I wanted this convo to go! In that case, my request is one-and-only! Just a single thing!"

Pointing her fingers sharply at Emilio and Roswaal—the two highest authorities in the room—Subaru slammed down her demand with gusto.
Subaru's wish was simple.

"Please hire me to work at this mansion!"

—It was a master plan aiming for that classic cohabitation setup, putting her under the same roof as her guy of choice to launch an all-out offensive.


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△


"You're too unselfish, Subaru. Asking for my name or a job at the mansion as your reward... it makes me worry about you."

That was Emilio's reaction when Subaru asked for employment.
The sight of the beautiful young man lowering his eyebrows and genuinely worrying for her filled Subaru's heart with arrows like a hedgehog, but that was beside the point.
Fortunately, her request to be hired went through smoothly, and with Roswaal's approval, Natsuki Subaru was successfully welcomed as a member of Roswaal Manor.

Establishing a stable foundation for living in a foreign world with nobody to rely on...
While she hadn't thought deeply about how huge an advantage that was, Subaru felt relieved to secure it.
On the other hand, if there was one thing making her reconsider...

"This maid outfit is way too revealing! Isn't this way too intimidating for someone like me to wear?!"

Sleeving into the official uniform of the Roswaal Manor maids, Subaru cried half-tears as she complained about the skirt's shortness and the breezy sensation on her back.
Naturally, as an employee, she intended to abide by the mansion's rules, but the otherworldly baptism—devoid of functional beauty—relentlessly shattered Subaru's self-esteem.

"Outfits like this only look good on cute girls! Having a girl with sharp eyes and a bony frame like me wear it just exposes me to my inner self shouting 'know your place'!"

"Brother, Subaru-san seems to be having some sort of internal conflict."
"Reu, it will only make her swell with pride. Pay no mind to the likes of Barusu."

"You twins could stand to have a bit more of a normal reaction toward a young maiden! I'm exposing ten times more skin than usual and enduring absolute embarrassment here, hello?! Counter my shame with some comments like 'it suits you' or 'fine clothes make the man' or 'it doesn't suit your station at all'!"

Despite Subaru shouting at them, the twins—named Rau and Reu—remained blunt.
They were twins who rarely showed emotion, but surely giving an appropriate compliment when a lady changed her outfit was universal manners!

"Thinking about it that way, isn't Ros-chi's management pretty lacking?"

"Do not criticize Master Roswaal so casually. Do you value your life so little, Barusu?"

"Stop glaring at me in a deadly serious tone! Also, my name's been turned into a flashbang spell for a while now! It's gonna trend!"

"Brother, Reu thinks taking her too seriously will only exhaust you."

"...Indeed. Hey, do not burden this Rau, Barusu."

"Sniff sniff, my coworkers are so cold, the world is a cruel place..."

Rau and Reu had an aura that kept outsiders at arm's length, yet their high loyalty to their master, Roswaal, was clear.
Though they seemed neck-and-neck in personality, it felt like Reu would be much easier to talk to than Rau, whether about work or private matters.

"Unapproachable Rau seems like the type to give the cold shoulder until he opens up."

"What is with that unpleasant evaluation? Were you not listening to Rau?"

"Brother, please leave this to Reu. You surely have a much more important duty than guiding a newcomer."

"...Indeed. I leave it to you."

Irritated by Subaru's personal take, Rau nodded at Reu's suggestion.
However, before leaving the spot, he cast a quick glance back at Subaru.

"Let me make one thing clear: do not mess with Rau's devoted younger brother. If you hinder Reu's work, benefactor to Master Emilio or not, I will throw you out of this mansion."

"Ooh, heavy intimidation. But I won't lose! I have my own ambition to achieve...!"

"Hmph."

Snorting loudly in contempt, Rau walked off, leaving Subaru pouting.
Watching his back retreat, Reu smoothly stepped up beside Subaru.

"Please do not take it to heart. Brother simply remains true to himself, always carrying a dignified honesty in his words."

"That's one way to put it! From the looks of it, your brother-worship is pretty intense."

"Brother possesses a greatness worthy of fascination."

"I see. By the way, what work did Rau go off to do?"

"Most likely, he went to take a brief nap in preparation for lunch..."

"WHAT IS YOUR DEFINITION OF AN IMPORTANT DUTY?!"

Instantly, Rau's previous grand posture was completely recontextualized as a front to slack off and take a nap, flipping Subaru's impression of him on its head.
If he was going to be that arrogant, he should at least be flawless at his job!

"Could it be his actual work is amazing? Like an expert in cleaning, laundry, and cooking?"

"Brother is good at cleaning. Though Reu is more skilled at it, and asking him to do laundry or cooking would be a bit problematic."

"Brother's reason for existing just vanished!"

Whether due to Reu's poor defense or Rau's pure lack of ability, his only merit was his good looks—his reason for acting high and mighty had completely evaporated.
Wait, thinking about it that way, why did Subaru have to be treated like that by him?!

"Gosh, what is with that guy...!"

She caught herself sounding like a heroine blushing over a man living freely outside her common sense, but Subaru's heart belonged solely to Emilio.
She wanted to be kind to those who were kind to her—that was Subaru's honest maidenly heart.

"Now then, I will teach you your duties. Please follow me."

"Ugh... Reu-ppe's kindness warms my heart. I like people who are nice to me."

"It is Master Roswaal's order, and it would be troubling if Brother's or Reu's workload increased needlessly. Reu holds no special feelings toward Subaru-san. Please do not misunderstand."

"Damn it! Cold shoulders from both brothers! Fine then, I won't lose!"

Whether straightforward from the older brother or polite from the younger, both treated her coldly. Undeterred, Subaru swore to the sky to brave it out.
If she worked hard and remained noble here, surely Emilio riding a white horse would come to rescue her.

"Wait, we're already under the same roof, so I cleared that stage. Huh? But how do I progress past that? My otome game knowledge pretty much ends once dating starts..."

"I will leave you behind."

"Ah, coming, coming... Woah, you walk fast! Where's the compassion?!"

"So as not to embarrass Master Roswaal, Subaru-san should also mind her language a bit more."

Receiving such grateful scoldings from her senior, Subaru hurried along, minding her skirt length as she boldly braved her work experience in an alternate world.


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△


For the next few days, her debut maid days were a series of hardships.

Truth be told, what surprised her was that Rau and Reu looked after Subaru quite attentively. Of course, any mistake Subaru made fell back on them, so one could call it natural.
Still, Rau with his sharp, icy tongue, and Reu with his heavy, blunt impact like a soft hammer blow, both played a part in helping Subaru improve.

"Though Rau literally just mouths off without lifting a finger."

"I see. Sorry, I haven't really looked into the details of Rau and Reu's work. I just thought they always worked hard."

"No, no, it's fine. Emilio-kyun is busy studying. But don't push yourself too hard. They say human concentration doesn't last long—whether there's an academic theory or not, or whether I'm just making it up myself, who knows."

"Oh, really... Wait, you're just making it up, Subaru?"

Who knew what the reality was? Theories flipped all the time anyway.
For instance, even the timing of seasoning meat with salt and pepper—whether to do it before cooking or during—was still debated.
Humans had likely been roasting meat since discovering fire, yet even such basic discussions yielded no final verdict. That's how theories worked.

"Or so rambles someone who doesn't know much about it anyway."

Wrapping up lazily, Subaru sat cross-legged on the grass and yawned.
No worries, she wasn't in her short maid skirt. Having finished her duties for the day with nothing left to do but sleep, Subaru was dressed in her loungewear tracksuit.

She had thought her tracksuit was a goner when slashed across the belly by the final blow of that terrifying assassin Aynz in the Capital, but luckily, they had saved her near-shredded tracksuit, allowing her to mend it and wear it once more.

"Glad I had my sewing skill—having it or not makes a world of difference for how long stuff lasts."

"Speaking of which, Reu praised you too. He said your mending skills are like a craftsman's."

"My skill tree growth is pretty lopsided, and I tend to pick abilities based on gut feeling. I'm the type who forgets to pick up passive stat boosts."

"Sorry, I don't really understand what you're saying."

Perhaps it was too much jargon; Emilio gave a strained smile and brushed off her character build ideas.
Still, it was Subaru's fault for not explaining properly, and she didn't mind if Emilio didn't get it. Just chatting with him like this was enough to make her happy.

Night at Roswaal Manor. Emilio was performing his daily routine of communicating with lesser spirits in the courtyard.
Ever since learning he spent his evenings this way, Subaru—worrying she might be a nuisance—had let her desire to be close to him win out, sitting at the edge of his vision.
Of course, if rejected, she'd suffer soul-crushing damage, but so far Emilio hadn't told her to leave, so she counted it as safe.

"――――"

Floating gently, emitting soft light as they fluttered through the night, were the lesser spirits.
Surrounded by them, brushing back his mystical silver hair, Emilio looked fleeting and ethereal, as though he might shatter into dust if touched.
Gazing up close at such otherworldly beauty, Subaru felt a warmth building inside her.

"The moon... is beautiful tonight."

"Because it's somewhere out of reach, right?"

"Guh-ack!"

"What's wrong?!"

Doused in cold water, Subaru's romantic spirit nearly suffered a cardiac arrest.
As Emilio innocently rushed over in worry, Subaru held up a hand, saying "I'm okay, I'm okay," bravely easing his concern.
That was when Emilio caught sight of her outstretched palm and let out a soft "Ah..."

Lifting her face in confusion, Subaru soon realized the meaning of Emilio's murmur.
Her outstretched fingers were covered in numerous bandages.

"Eek, how embarrassing. Hands covered in cuts aren't very girl-like, huh."

"That's not true. —Shall I cast healing magic? I'm not very good at it, but for injuries like those..."

"Nnn~ I appreciate the thought, Emilio-kyun, but I think I'll pass for now."

"Why?"

At her refusal, Emilio lowered his brows anxiously.
Sensing he felt hurt at being turned down, Subaru hurriedly shook her head, "No, no!"

"It's not about you, Emilio-kyun—it's my own thing. ...These cuts are proof that I'm working hard here. So I thought I'd keep them for now."

"Proof of working hard..."

"This one's from cooking, this one's from misusing unfamiliar cleaning gear, and this one's from looking away while wiping windows and jabbing my hand. Each one is proof of my growth!"

After all, for Subaru, who had never even held a part-time job, her maid days in this mansion were her very first work experience.
She had been confident in her dexterity, but such baseless confidence was crushed repeatedly during her initial duties, leaving her fingers looking like battered scraps.

Still, that wasn't a bad thing.

"When you have visible proof of your efforts, it helps you trust the path you've walked... Sorry, maybe I'm saying something weird."

"...No, not at all. I understand completely."

"Emilio-kyun..."

With a serious expression, Emilio didn't laugh at Subaru's silly obsession.
Touched by his words, Subaru felt her heart pound—not from frivolity, but from genuine warmth pouring into her.
Overwhelmed by these feelings, she decided to make a bold move.

"Um, Emilio-kyun... If you're free tomorrow, I'm planning to go buy groceries in the village... Would you wanna go on a date... I-I mean, go out together?"

"To the village..."

"W-Was that a no?! Did I misread our closeness?! Was I being a pushy sow?!"

"I didn't think anything terrible like that at all?!"

Having stepped in too deep, Subaru hallucinated herself being decapitated on the counterattack. Seeing her panic, Emilio frantically waved his hands, trying to explain "No, that's not it!"
Hesitating over how to phrase it, Emilio said,

"It's not about you, Subaru, it's a problem on my end. About the village... if you go with me, you might have an unpleasant experience..."

"That's not true! As long as I'm with Emilio-kyun, rose-colored sparkle effects will be scattered everywhere!"

"But... the people around might not look too kindly on us..."

"It's fine! No matter what faces everyone makes, I'll stick to your side with the greatest smile of the century, and the sheer annoyance will grow on you!"

"...Pfft."

Abandoning all outer boxing and going full in-fighter, Subaru's pitch finally broke Emilio's defense, making him laugh as if giving in.
Holding her breath, Subaru waited for his next words—

"Alright, okay. I'll go to the village with you, Subaru."

At Emilio's helpless, shy smile, Subaru was already making the grin of the century, cheering hip-hip-hooray in her heart.



"So anyway, tomorrow is my date with Emilio-kyun! Ehehehe, I'd say progress in our relationship is right around the corner! Isn't that awesome? Isn't it?!"

"Why did you go out of your way to come report this to Vitcha..."

"Well, Rau and Reu have early work tomorrow, so Victa—who doesn't do anything special and just wanders the mansion—was basically the only person left to talk to!"

"Vitcha is saying he doesn't understand the logic behind you breezing through 'Door Crossing' like it's nothing!"

Slapping his desk, a flushed Victor yelled.
It was late at night, and making a racket would bother others, but Subaru didn't scold him. Partly because she was in high spirits, but also due to the unique properties of this archive—no, the Forbidden Library.

Apparently, the child named Victor was an extraordinarily powerful being, skilled in magic that twisted and merged space.
The magic he used called "Door Crossing" was an incredible spell that connected the door of this Forbidden Library to any door in the mansion.
It was just that Subaru could bypass the intended randomness of "Door Crossing" purely on gut instinct.

"Hah, this is all a reward for my daily hard work... So, so, so, can I be a little greedier? What do you think?"

"Vitcha thinks nothing of it."

"Like holding hands! W-Would it seem shameless if I went to hold his hand?!"

"Listen! If you're going to talk, at least listen to Vitcha!"

"Bombardie-gh!"

While seeking romance advice from a child was questionable, answering an older girl's earnest dilemma with an invisible shockwave wasn't great either.
In the end, without getting any real answers, Subaru was blown away by a fierce wind, booted out of the Forbidden Library to end her rendezvous with Victor.

"A guy with such a cute face yet lacking cuteness. Someday I'm gonna smother him with affection."

Grumbling about being rolled down the corridor, Subaru dusted off her clothes, picked herself up, and skipped cheerfully toward her room.
Behind the closed door of the Forbidden Library after throwing Subaru out, Victor murmured—

"—It is none of Vitcha's concern."

Unaware that he had uttered those words.

"Behold! A new day, and a day of remarkable strides! Natsuki Subaru officially graduates today from being a girl who just waits around!"

The moment she paid off her sleepiness and bought full consciousness, Subaru sat straight up.
Her absurdly fast-waking brain immediately began formulating the optimal route toward fulfilling last night's promise—namely, her first date with Emilio.

First, cleverly manipulate Reu into giving her the easiest duties possible. Then brush off Rau's sarcasm with a mature smile, take on the village grocery errand, and eventually reach the finish line with Emilio.

"Alright, no time to waste! I'm transforming into my victory form right now—Whoa?!"

"――――"
"――――"

Finishing her strategy session while wiggling her hips on the bed, an un-cute scream tore from Subaru's throat as she turned around.
The reason was simple: figures other than Subaru were standing inside the room. Two of them.
And in Roswaal Manor, if two figures stood side-by-side as a pair, there was only one possible answer.

"Hey, hey! You guys were relatively considerate after the first day, but bursting into a maiden's room first thing in the morning is a taboo! You're looking at my bedhead face again! Don't look!"

Covering her face with both hands, Subaru tried to play off her rising embarrassment with jokes.
If they saw her actually blush, she'd want to die, and covering her face only made her realize there was drool at the corner of her mouth, making her want to die even more.
Even if it wasn't Emilio, she wasn't tough enough to suffer zero damage from boys seeing her terrible sleeping face. Not that she was a boy in the first place.
However, to Subaru's reaction, the two took an incomprehensible stance.

"Brother, our guest addressed us rather familiarly."
