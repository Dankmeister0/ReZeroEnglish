tThat mindset was quite far removed from common sense, and frankly, it was rather hard to comprehend.
tYet, at this very moment, it felt as though one could grasp a fraction of the logic that the "Blue Lightning" had so loudly proclaimed—that the world was watching the people as if enjoying a play.

tPerhaps that was precisely why one could clearly tell that the world had held its breath right now.

"――――"

"That would be the case. It is not my wish... no, my personal feelings are irrelevant."

"Indeed. What matters is not where one's heart lies, but the results of one's actions."
