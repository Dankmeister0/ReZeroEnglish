Arc 1 Chapter 7: "An Incomprehensible Reunion"

"—What's the matter, kid? Suddenly making a blank face like that."

"Huh—?"

Addressed like that by a rough-faced middle-aged man, he couldn't help but let out a dopey response.

At his reply, the man knit his already deeply wrinkled brow even further.

"So, I'm asking, what're you gonna do already. The Appa—you buying it or not?"

"Huh—?"

"The Appa! You wanna eat it, don't you? You're the one who came up and said so yourself, and then all of a sudden your eyes go somewhere else—scared the hell outta me. ...So, what's it gonna be?"

The scar-faced, powerfully built man had a small, cute-looking red fruit sitting neatly in his palm.

Subaru looked back and forth between the fruit—which closely resembled an apple—being held out to him, and the man's face.

"Ah, well, actually, I'm broke—mortal enemies with having any money at all."

"...The hell, then you were just window-shopping. Go on, get outta here! I'm running a business here. I don't have time to play along with tire-kickers."

Waved off perfunctorily, Subaru obediently scurried away from the spot.

He—Natsuki Subaru—looked around as he went,

"Huh? Huh? ...What is going on?"

All he could manage, amid his confusion and bewilderment, was to voice a question directed at no one in particular.

The main street was as crowded with people as ever, pedestrians spreading out to fill its entire width except for the occasional lizard-drawn carriage passing through.

The sun was still high in the sky. The temperature wasn't quite what you'd call hot, but seeing the fur on a wolf-shaped person passing by in front of him—a Werewolf, he supposed you'd call it—the vague thought "that must be pretty hot" drifted across his mind.

It drifted across his mind, but—

"This isn't the time to be having tourist thoughts like that. Um, what—what is going on!?"

He clutched his head and twisted at the waist. Subaru struck a pose of all-out anguish right there on the spot, and thanks to the sheer novelty of the sight, the eyes of everyone around him converged on him.

Those stares felt intensely familiar. They were the same kind—no, exactly the same—as the ones that had been thrown at him rudely from every direction, just a few hours ago.

"Right, a few hours ago... at least, that's what it should be."

As he spoke, Subaru looked around, and finally glanced up at the sky, tilting his head.

The sun was high. At least, as far as Subaru understood things, it should have been sunset just a moment ago.

This feeling, too, was not new to Subaru.

When he'd been dragged into another world—this world—he had likewise experienced night and day flipping on him. Because of that, the shock wasn't as great as the first time, but there was one condition that was clearly different from back then.

"My stomach wound... it's gone."

He muttered this after rolling up the hem of his tracksuit and checking the abdomen beneath it.

The wound that should have been there—sliced open by what looked like a large blade, spilling blood and guts everywhere—simply wasn't there. Naturally, there were no stitch marks either, nor any trace of blood on his clothes.

More than that, there wasn't even a speck of dust or mud staining his beloved tracksuit.

The convenience store bag in his hand was still intact, and his phone and wallet were still tucked into their respective pants pockets, just as before. In every sense, he was back to a flawless starting condition.

—He felt like he was going to lose his mind.

"Just being summoned to another world was already more than I could handle, and now what am I supposed to do with this...?"

As for the wound healing, there was already the precedent of Satella, so he could accept the absence of a wound.

But the wound Subaru had sustained in the Loot House just before was clearly a fatal one. It was the kind where, the instant his consciousness went out, he had felt certain he was dying.

If a wound like that could be healed, then he could only conclude that the Magic of this world was capable of even reviving the dead.

"That'd cheapen the value of life quite a bit... no, wait, before that—who did it?"

Aware that his memory was somewhat muddled, Subaru desperately tried to recall the moments just before he'd lost consciousness.

That's right, his stomach had been slashed open, and he'd nearly been killed. He thought there had been a woman's voice.

He'd found a corpse inside the Loot House, and then he himself had been attacked by whoever it was that had apparently killed the person who'd become that corpse. And then, on the verge of death—

"That's right, Satella!"

The silver-haired girl, worried about Subaru, had ended up coming into the Loot House.

Remembering his own actions before and after that, regret now belatedly flooded through Subaru's chest.

Satella had told him clearly.

He wasn't to try to handle things himself—if anything happened, he was to call for her.

Those words had carried a meaning beyond simple concern for Subaru. They had, quite precisely, been meant to double as a way of telling Satella that he was in a critical situation.

And yet, Subaru had neglected even that simple instruction to raise his voice.

As a result, Subaru himself hadn't been able to escape the attacker, and had ended up drawing Satella into that scene of carnage.

There in the darkness, drowning in a pool made of his own blood, Subaru had certainly seen the silver-haired girl cut down by the attacker.

He had seen her collapse into the same sea of blood, and go still before he did—

"'Take care of Satella'... isn't that what I was told?"

He remembered the sight of Puck, who had said exactly that while looking at Subaru, right before vanishing.

Those had been far from light words. It was only that Subaru himself hadn't taken them seriously enough—the very situation that little cat had feared had indeed been waiting for the two of them.

And despite being warned again and again, Subaru had let that chance slip by through nothing more than sheer oversight.

This present situation was the result.

Thrown out onto the street with no idea what was going on, not even knowing whether the people he knew were safe, having lost even any sense of direction, he stood there at a loss.

"Am I an idiot... no, I am an idiot. Where do I even find the time to hang my head like this. Anyway, I've got to find Satella and Puck..."

The two of them might both be dead—Subaru shook his head to drive away that cruel thought.

Even he himself—a man with no redeeming qualities, not the slightest bit useful, basically nothing more than a waste-production machine, if you wanted to put it that way—had survived.

Given that, he couldn't believe that the beautiful girl who could use that Magic, who was such a soft-hearted busybody, who wasn't exactly candid but had an intensely straightforward core to her—or that breezy, hard-to-pin-down oddball of a Spirit—could have died.

No—he simply didn't want them to be dead.

"Anyway, for now..."

—I'll head to the Loot House, Subaru decided.

If that place was where his consciousness had come to its final stop, then he had a feeling that whatever clues he needed for his next move would be found there.

No sooner thought than done. Here again, the speed of Subaru's decision-making shone through. Back in his original world, this decisiveness had mostly been put to use on decisions like giving up and deciding "I'll skip school today," but for Subaru now, it carried real significance in the sense of cutting through his hesitation.

But that spirited decision of Subaru's—

"Yo, kid. Why don't you come play with us for a bit."

—met the misfortune of being interrupted by three men standing there blocking the alley.

※※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※

"Hey now, what's with that blank face?"

"He probably doesn't get the situation. Might as well spell it out for him, huh?"

Seeing Subaru show no reaction to their intimidation, the men twisted the corners of their mouths in mocking sneers.

Subaru watched their attitude with the sense that he was being shown some sort of ridiculous stage play.

There were three men. Their appearance couldn't be called tidy by any stretch, and their faces were the textbook thug type, as if the crudeness of their upbringing and character had been etched straight onto their features.

Whether from being used to rough business or not, their faces and skin bore visible scars, and they gave off an easy-to-read air of condoning violence.

Subaru found all of it hopelessly familiar. After all, these men were—

"You guys... did you hit your heads or something, someplace I don't know about?"

—the very ones who, just a few hours earlier, had been the trigger for Subaru meeting Satella and Puck in the first place.

They were unmistakably mob-character types, but seeing as this other world was a three-dimensional world, it wasn't as if characters could just get recycled. So were they different people who merely looked alike? Well, when you put it that way—

"Three different look-alike guys all teaming up to pull off something like a mugging—does that even happen?"

One look-alike would be one thing, but three. And forming a party with the exact same faces on top of that—that was hard to imagine. Which meant these were the exact same lineup as in Subaru's memory, and—

"Either they hit their heads... or this is revenge, and fast. Guess that part doesn't change even in another world. Though not bringing more backup, I guess, is kind of decent of them?"

Back in the real world, if you took down one guy, that guy would come back for revenge with his buddies in tow. And plenty of times, no matter how many you beat, more enemies just kept popping up one after another, until you ended up facing some incomprehensibly strong boss and it was game over.

Compared to that pattern of repeating that negative chain, the sheer nerve of these guys coming back under their own power, rather than relying on outside help, probably deserved at least some credit.

Either way, though, given how things had actually gone down, targeting Subaru for revenge was a case of barking up the wrong tree, if he had to say so himself.

"Well, I get the urge to go after the weak-looking guy, so I won't call it wrong of you. But, this is kind of bad timing, I mean..."

"What's this guy even saying. Ain't he a little touched in the head?"

The men mocked Subaru as he sought a peaceful, talk-it-out way to resolve things here.

Even Subaru, for all his patience, felt something click at their attitude. The whole "let's settle this peacefully" thing was purely because he was in a hurry given the situation—at heart, Subaru was actually a fairly short-tempered person.

Even so, given how serious things were, he'd been bracing himself to just swallow this as a trivial insult—

"It's fine, kid. For starters, just leave all your stuff here. We'll let you off with that."

"Ah, yeah yeah. All my stuff, got it. I'm in a hurry, so sure, fine, whatever."

"Oh, and act like a dog! Get down on all fours, do a dog impression, and bark out 'please help me,' got it?"

"Don't get cocky, you bastards—!!"

The remark that came out was so obnoxiously smug that the thread of his patience snapped, already.

The men were rattled by Subaru's sudden explosion into motion. Amid their stunned confusion, Subaru zeroed in on the man standing second in line.

This was the man who had previously fended off Subaru's furious assault with a knife, quite successfully. Naturally, that same short-fused habit of immediately pulling out a knife was surely still intact. Which meant—

"You're first! I hate people who don't value their own lives! Drop dead!!"

A palm strike thrown with everything he had snapped the man's jaw upward. Following straight through, Subaru drove his left fist into the wide-open torso, slamming the man into the wall and sinking him for good.

Against the other men, whose reactions to the sudden turn of events were slow, Subaru first swept one man's leg out from under him, dropping him. Using that opening to lock onto the last man, he threw his whole body into a charge aimed at the man's lower half.

With the momentum of a body tackle, he hoisted the man up and rammed him into the wall with everything he had. He finished off the man, who groaned from the impact, with a kick, and when he turned around, the man who'd fallen was just getting back to his feet.

"Ngh... you bastard."

"Alright, one on one. Both the knife guy and the machete guy already went down—so what're you gonna do?"

Subaru, riding a flood of adrenaline and full of bravado, made the man's expression shift as though he'd been cowed somehow. In truth, this was the man who had actually been knocked unconscious by Satella's Magic during the earlier ruckus.

He was the only one whose weapon Subaru hadn't confirmed, but judging from his reaction just now, Subaru concluded he wasn't carrying anything lethal.

"If it's a straight-up brawl, I'm not the type to go down on my knees and beg that easy, either."

"Don't screw with me, you piece of shit. Ahh! Don't underestimate me, you brat!!"

Provoked by Subaru waving his outstretched hand, the man took the bait completely, spit flying as he shouted.

Subaru met the man's grabbing lunge with a straight punch. True to his aim, it struck the man's chest—but the man, carried by his own momentum, flung himself at Subaru anyway, trying to shove him back against the wall. But—

"Way. Too. Soft!"

Gripping both of the man's wrists as he clutched at his shoulders, Subaru tore the man's grip loose with arm strength that outmatched his own.

Seeing clear alarm run across the man's face, Subaru twisted his own mean-eyed face into something even more vicious, and said,

"Don't underestimate me just 'cause I'm a hikikomori. My grip strength, built up day after day pointlessly swinging a wooden sword, is over seventy kilos. And I can bench press up to eighty, too."

Crushing an apple with his bare hand at a family gathering and turning the mood at the table awkward was a dark chapter of his past best left unspoken.

It had been a hikikomori's life in which, day after day, he'd never once stopped training just his body. Against an opponent of similar build in a straightforward test of strength, Subaru had the sheer base physical conditioning not to lose, barring extraordinary circumstances.

The man screamed at the sensation of his wrist being crushed, and the instant his grip loosened, Subaru's knee slammed into him. The thug folded in half, struck in the gut. Subaru quickly circled around behind him and said,

"Don't hold it against me even if you die. This is something I've always wanted to try once—a reverse throw, ground-level edition!"

He wrapped his arms around the man's waist and, with the momentum of falling backward, flung his body through the air.

It was a throw close to a pro-wrestling backdrop, but letting go halfway through made it a nasty, dangerous move. The man crashed headfirst into the wall with no way to stop himself, fell to the ground, and stopped moving entirely.

After confirming the two men's silence, Subaru finally went over to the knife-wielding man he'd taken down first.

The knife man, having taken relatively less damage, writhed in pain but still tried, on instinct, to draw the knife from inside his coat as Subaru approached. Since he tried to draw it, Subaru kicked him mercilessly in the face. Down for good.

"—There, easy win! Evil has never once prospered in this world!"

Natsuki Subaru pumped his fist, celebrating his victory alone right there on the spot.

It was a relief to know that all that aimless daily training hadn't gone to waste. Anyway,

"Not that the situation's changed one bit, though. Anyway, I got interrupted, but I still need to get to the Loot House."

After confirming only that the three men weren't dead, Subaru hastily left the alley behind.

Seeing Subaru come out of the street unscathed, he could faintly hear passersby let out unexpectedly impressed "ohh"s, which bothered him a little—if you're going to witness something that looks like a mugging, he wanted to shout, then go report it already.

Of course, being the socially anxious type, there was no way Subaru could actually do something like that himself, and so he simply jogged away from the scene.
