Arc 1 Chapter 20: "The Cast Is Assembled"

I lunged at her waist, pulling the light body close as I rolled across the ground.

Just before hitting the floor, he felt his hair prickle, as if steel had grazed the back of his head and singed it. But he forced himself to write it off as imagination, using the weight in his arms as an excuse, and let the momentum of the roll carry him clear.

Rising to one knee and looking back, he saw Elsa standing frozen, a look of surprise on her face. He felt like he'd scored a point off her, and flashed a triumphant, strained grin of victory.

Elsa twitched as if to move at that grin of Subaru's, but a barrage of ice pillars from behind forced her to shift to defense. Grateful for the fake Satella's covering fire, Subaru looked down at what he held against his chest.

"You okay?! I was desperate, so cut me some slack if I grabbed somewhere weird!"

"I would've thanked you honestly if you hadn't said that. ...Anyway, why?"

"Hell if I know! My body just moved! Seriously a miracle! Make a miracle! If I had to say—well, you wouldn't know this, but this makes us even now! Even, got it? Got it!"

Releasing Felt, Subaru clenched his fist, giving himself a grudging GJ for his own actions.

In the second world, Felt had saved him from Elsa's murderous violence. Now he'd been able to repay that debt to her. It was a memory meaningless in this world—one where, in the end, both Subaru and Felt had fallen before her murderous blade anyway.

—Even so, a debt of gratitude received didn't just vanish. And if he could still move, then what came next was already decided.

"Alright, I can still move somehow. Guess coming this close to death once actually steeled my resolve. Fake energy's still energy, reckless courage's still courage. Do it, when to do it, gotta do it, should do it, just freakin' do it!"

"H-hey, mister..."

"Listen up, listen to me. Okay, Felt? I'm gonna buy time now, same as old man Rom did before he fell. I'll make an opening somehow, even just one, so while I do, you run. Sprint for the entrance without looking back. Escape, got it?"

"—! No, I don't got it! What's that supposed to mean, you telling me to turn tail and run?!"

Her red eyes glared up at him, and Subaru leaned in close to meet them head-on. He didn't miss the flicker of a moment where Felt's expression wavered at his intensity.

"That's right, turn tail and run with it between your legs. Honestly, I'd love to be the one doing that. I don't wanna stay a second longer in this violence-fest."

He roughly ruffled her blonde hair, then took a breath and said, "But—"

"You're fifteen, I'm eighteen. That probably makes you the youngest one here. So it's only natural to pick whatever gives you the best odds of living. It's just natural."

"Wh-what's that supposed to... don't screw with me, you were shaking like a leaf just a second ago."

"That was then! This is now! I'm not shaking now, so it's fine! Besides, I gotta do this before I remember and freeze up again. So get outta here already."

He pushed a hand against her still-protesting forehead to silence her, and rose to his feet with a stretch of his knees. At his feet lay the club that had slipped from old man Rom's grip. Hefting it up with effort, it was absurdly heavy, but not so heavy he couldn't swing it.

"I can land one good check-swing with this. ...Guess swinging that needlessly oversized practice sword every day was the right call after all. Who knew it was for a moment like this—nice going, me."

Praising himself, Subaru swung the club, then held his breath to gauge the timing.

Behind him, Felt, holding her breath, still looked like she wanted to say something, but there was no time to indulge her hesitation. The longer this dragged on, the lower everyone's odds of survival would fall.

Ahead, Elsa's movements as she danced amid the ice showed no hesitation. Not that Subaru, with zero real combat experience, could tell whether an opening even existed.

The best move would be to launch a silent surprise attack the instant he was certain her attention had slipped away from him.

So, the moment Elsa cut down a particularly large ice pillar and his position slipped entirely into her blind spot, Subaru forgot even to breathe as he brought the club down.

"—Hup!"

Maybe it was a burst of adrenaline-fueled strength, but the club's swing accelerated far beyond what he'd expected. A direct hit would have cracked a human skull open like a watermelon—except,

"Your aim's fine. Too bad your killing intent's so obvious it gives you away completely."

"Killing intent, huh! No clue how to control that!"

Against the strike from directly behind, Elsa knocked the club aside with the spine of her blade, deflecting its trajectory to dodge. Even as he groused sore-loser excuses about his failed ambush, Subaru bared his teeth,

"Now! Go, Felt——!!"

"———!!"

As if launched by Subaru's shout, Felt's small frame took off, riding the wind. Her speed was top speed from the very first step. Truly too fast for the eye to follow, she tore through the room, clearing the ice pillars and the frozen floor alike, her small body making straight for the exit.

"You think I'll let you go?"

Blocking her path from the side was a throwing knife Elsa had drawn from within her clothes. Whether in retaliation for Felt's earlier interference or not, the plainly ornamented blade flew straight, aimed at the fleeing girl's back. But—

"Letting her go is exactly what I'm wishing for!!"

Kicking up a table that stood right beside him, Subaru blocked the knife's path. The reflexive move paid off—the knife buried itself in the flung, ratty table and failed to fulfill its purpose. Internally, Subaru couldn't help but cheer his own miraculous form. And then—

"Awesome! Though my toes hurt more than I expec—bfugaru?!"

A long leg kicked him square in the side of the head, cutting his self-praise short and slamming him into the wall. Hitting shoulder-first spared his head, but the unexpected force stopped his thoughts cold. The pain arrived a beat late, along with the taste of blood as he cut the inside of his mouth open again—a rerun of the same old flavor.

"Rare for me, but that actually annoyed me a little."

"Bring on the annoyance! Ha-hah, se-e-erves-you-right! I got one of us away, clean as anything! Keep it up and let's enjoy that loser feeling!"

Standing up, he put on a brave face, provoking and riling her up to keep Elsa's attention fixed on him. Given her personality, it wasn't a strategy likely to have much effect, but as if reading Subaru's intent, she carved her smile deeper and put the fleeing Felt entirely out of her mind.

"Fine, I'll play along. In exchange, don't you dare bore me on this dance floor."

"Fair warning, if you're dancing with me, brace yourself. I've got no manners, so I'll be stomping on your feet nonstop."

He remembered the folk dance where he'd never gotten to partner with the girl he liked, taking a small hit of emotional damage.

Spitting out the blood pooling in his mouth, he re-gripped the club he'd never let go of. It wasn't a weapon that offered many chances. Rather than trying to use it the way old man Rom had, he poured every ounce of himself into simply meeting Elsa's approach head-on.

"Don't forget about your other opponent, either!"

They faced off head-on, while shards of ice struck in ambush from behind. Without so much as glancing back, Elsa swung her blade and knocked down every last one. Even Subaru couldn't keep up his wisecracks in the face of a sense that transcended human understanding.

"I'm about ready to grow tired of this little game... Can you still manage to entertain me?"

Her question came low, and her smile was the color of blood. Seeing a smile that sent a chill straight down his spine, Subaru shot a quick glance at the fake Satella. She met his eyes, and he gave a small nod of his chin.

"If you've got some hidden true power stashed away, now'd probably be a good time to bring it out."

"...I have a trump card. But if I use it, nothing will be left of anyone but me."

"I can still fight! I can still do this! It's way too early to play that card! Why are you giving up already? We're just getting started, still totally even here! Fight, me!"

He flailed his arms and legs to show he was still going strong, desperately trying to stop the fake Satella from giving up on everything. At the sight of Subaru's frantic expression, even in the midst of battle, the girl let her lips loosen just the faintest bit,

"I won't use it. Not while you're still trying this hard. Struggle, and struggle, and struggle through to the end. ...Living off your parents is a last resort, after all."

Seeing the girl's expression as she said that, as though she had no choice, something lit up inside Subaru.

That's not like me, this sentimentality—so he thought, looking back at the feeling welling up inside him now.

Not like him. Not like him at all, truly, none of this was like him. He'd always been indifferent to those around him, never leaving a mark on anything, never choosing to face any problem head-on no matter what stood in his way—just drifting along vaguely, like a cloud or something. Expecting anything from anyone, or having anything expected of him in turn—all of it, every last bit, should have meant nothing to him.

—And yet now, he found himself hoping. And because he'd hoped, he found himself disappointed.

He'd been shown her profile, over and over, tense and troubled, always searching for something—and because of that, he'd wanted to see her smile.

But this—a weak, fragile shift of expression, one on the verge of giving up, of accepting defeat—he couldn't forgive that being the first "smile" he ever got from her.

"No count..."

"—Huh?"

"Forget what I said earlier! All of it, forget it! I'm seriously fired up now, burning!! I'll do it, damn it all! I'm not letting you play that trump card—no way in hell!!"

He jabbed a finger out, declaring it to both the fake Satella and Elsa. Stomping his feet, spraying spit, he howled with raw emotion.

"Don't make a face like that, you're wasting a pretty face! And you—don't make her make a face like that, read the room, Sadistic-of-the-Year over here! Wasting a pretty face, again!"

"...You seem to have energy to spare."

"Haven't drawn blood even once this time, y'know! But tonight's club, 'Kotetsu,' is starving for your blood! Hmph! Hmph!"

He took a full swing with the club right there, like a bat. Dragged off-balance by the weight, Subaru stumbled unsteadily, and Elsa leveled her kukri knife at him,

"That's quite enough fooling around. Let's begin the dance. Do try to keep up."

"You're the one who shouldn't go down too soon. I throw right, bat left—don't underestimate me."

As Elsa leaned lightly forward, he pointed the club at her like calling a home run shot. That pointless posturing delayed his first response to Elsa's charge. He hurriedly reset his stance, moving to meet her slender form as it came flying in.

A fierce gust of a swing came at Elsa from the side, aiming to blow her head clean off. The mercy-free full swing was an all-out blow, braced for carnage—force enough that a direct hit could've turned part of her into ground meat. But—

"You think mercy's gonna win against an opponent like this?!"

The desperation of the situation had made Subaru forget any notion of holding back. Against the lethal blow, though, Elsa dropped even lower with practiced ease, dodging it with a movement that all but licked the ground.

"You spider woman——!"

"Though it's true you've been caught in my web."

He saw the blade rising up at him and instinctively threw his body backward. But he was nowhere near escaping the reach of the blade chasing him.

In that instant, so brief he nearly forgot to blink, what flashed through Subaru's mind was the memory of standing on his own two feet on the tatami for the first time, his parents clapping and saying, "This kid might be a genius."

"A life-flashing-before-my-eyes moment, and on top of that, given the current situation, a memory that leaves me nothing but sorry for myself——!!"

Fueled by that memory, humiliation snapped his knee upward. Launched without even aiming, it struck the torso of Elsa as she slid in right in front of him.

A husky, pained little gasp escaped her, and the blade's path wavered slightly. Into that gap—

"Shield of ice——nice cover!!"

"I'm not exactly skilled at conjuring them exactly where I aim. ...You nearly ended up an ice sculpture."

"You mean Elsa, not me, right?!"

A block of ice that appeared near his face blocked the blade, and as Subaru leapt back with a word of thanks, the fake Satella mused, wiping at her brow. At Subaru's follow-up remark, she gave a vague, noncommittal answer, and the barrage of ice resumed its harassment of Elsa.

"These insects are starting to get in my way. ...Time to wrap this up, perhaps."

"Whoa, whoa, don't underestimate this insect. Don't come crying if you get stung and break out in a rash!"

"Awfully high and mighty, coming from way over there in your safe zone."

Elsa focused on dodging the barrage, while Subaru provoked her, trying to break that focus. Ideally, he'd have liked to slip in amid the barrage and go for Elsa's back, but if he carelessly joined the attack, he risked friendly fire, so he couldn't quite bring himself to step in. That was probably also why the fake Satella's covering fire thinned out whenever Subaru shifted to attacking.

A classic case of the teamwork failures unique to an improvised team thrown together on the spot.

"But even so, the two of them, by overcoming trials together, would deepen the bond between them. Before long, trust would turn to love, and the blazing passion between them would be something no one could stop..."

"I can't make out what you're muttering under your breath, but I can tell it's incredibly stupid."

"Standing before one woman while thinking about another—there's nothing more boorish than that."

"Booed by both of them at once—unbelievable!!"

Even mid-combo, Elsa retained a certain composure. Following her movements as she deflected the ice chunks with his eyes, occasionally darting in for a hit-and-run strike, Subaru never stopped thinking.

The situation, steadily worsening, showed no sign of changing—if Elsa felt like it, it was plain as day that Subaru too would go down just as easily as old man Rom had. That it looked, in this way, like they were "exchanging blows" to some degree, was only because she, with her overwhelming skill, was underestimating him. Plus, Subaru's own fearful, hesitant mindset—which kept him from taking that last step into fatal range—was another factor. If Subaru had been some reckless, fearless amateur, this balance would've ended in a single stroke.

Even so, that timidity of his wasn't going to keep working in his favor for long. The ferocity of her strikes gradually increased, and Subaru's emergency dodges began failing to keep up, leaving him with more and more cuts. His upper arm, calf, underarm, and just barely his nape—shallow wounds began piling up, and bloodstains started standing out on his gray tracksuit from bleeding that wouldn't stop.

"Cuts hurt! Seriously hurt! I'm about to cry over wounds this pathetic!"

"—Maybe this really won't work after all."

"Ah, sorry! Kidding! Take that back! So quit trying to play your trump card like you're giving up! I can still do this! Totally, seriously, super fine, I promise!"

He waved a hand, playing it cool for the fake Satella, then immediately followed with a roundhouse kick. A total departure from his club-swinging pattern so far, a bit of unarmed combat meant purely to catch her off guard. But—

"There, got you."

"Ugh."

His kicking leg was dodged with plenty of room to spare, and on top of that, casually grabbed before it could even pass by. The kukri knife she raised was aimed at the base of Subaru's lifted leg, its momentum sharp and fast enough to easily lop the whole leg clean off.

Death by shock from blood loss and pain following amputation of the thigh——the words BAD END 4 flashed before his eyes.

"Nuoooo! Burn, whatever's inside me——!!"

He instinctively swung the club in his dominant hand around to block, but between its weight and the handicap of standing on one leg, he couldn't make it in time. A soundless scream tore from the fake Satella, and the blade reached Subaru's leg without mercy. Bracing for agonizing pain and a spray of blood, he let out a scream that quite literally coughed up blood——

"—That's far enough."

Piercing through the roof, a blazing flame descended into the center of the Loot House. The flame swept through the room with a terrifying, otherworldly aura, halting even Elsa's savage assault.

His leg freed, Subaru staggered backward, stumbling, and fell flat on his backside. And there before his eyes, amid billowing clouds of dust and smoke, he saw a brilliant crimson glow.

"Looks like it was a close call, but I'm glad I made it in time. Now then——"

"Y-you are..."

The flame flickered, and a foot stepped forward. Its destination was Elsa, who leapt back a great distance. She re-gripped her kukri knife, and for the first time, her composure vanished from her expression as she faced the presence before her.

Before that overwhelming sense of pressure, there was no longer any room left to enjoy the fight as mere entertainment.

A commanding presence that froze the expressions of Subaru, the fake Satella, and even Elsa. Drawing every gaze in the room to himself, his handsome, composed features didn't waver by so much as a fraction.

A young man whose sky-blue eyes reflected nothing but the purest sense of "justice" smiled faintly.

"Shall we bring down the curtain on this stage——!"

Sweeping back his crimson hair, the handsome man declared it in ringing tones.
