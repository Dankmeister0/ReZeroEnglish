For the Emilia Camp too, Petra had been in charge of preparing today's meal, while cleanup fell to Subaru and Garfiel, who'd lost at rock-paper-scissors.

"...But if it's true even by your own eyes, boss, then honestly, what I really want is for someone to deal with the whole Priestella problem already."

"But what if that ends up proving Filore-san really is the true royal? Then Emilia-neesama's Royal Selection might be over, you know?"

"That's a whole different matter, ain't it. That Filore 'Saintess' has already stepped out onto center stage. It's just that so far, only the higher-ups know about it."

Garfiel answered Petra like that, splashing up a lather of soap suds in the water tub as he washed. Narrowing his emerald eyes, he wrestled a stubborn patch of grime on a plate with the linen cloth he used in place of a sponge.

"I ain't laid eyes on her myself, but if just her looks are enough to kick up that much of a fuss, there ain't no hidin' somethin' like that, no matter how hard you try. Especially if she healed the duke's body and means to pull the same trick in Priestella."

"—Yeah, I'd give that understanding of yours a perfect score, Garf-san. You're really getting good at reading the state of the world. Good job, good job."

"Don't talk like you're grading me on a test! And let me tell you somethin' — I'm older than you, Petra!"

Petra, wearing the face of a teacher through and through, patted his head anyway, and with both hands stuck in the water tub, Garfiel couldn't resist — all he could do was grumble "gao, gao." Watching the two of them with a warm smile, Subaru couldn't help but sense an unpleasant current running through the state of the world.

"At this point, it already feels like a yellow light's flashing over how the Royal Selection is unfolding..."

If, from here, that light turned red, would it ever really flip back to green again, letting Emilia properly fulfill her role as a Royal Selection candidate?

"—"

Honestly, the moment the surviving princess narrowed down to a choice between Felt and Filore, Subaru feared the Royal Selection had already shifted into something seeking the answer to "which of the two is the genuine article with the rightful claim to succession."

Naturally, as Emilia's knight, that wasn't something he could accept — but setting that perspective aside, there was still something that didn't sit right with him.

It was something he'd brought up even on the way to the royal capital, riding Reinhard's superhuman sprint.

"In the first place, before Filore even showed up, once suspicion arose that Felt might be the princess, why didn't things just settle into 'Felt's the next monarch, case closed'?"

Of course, there was no mistaking that this world lacked convenient, reliable means of identifying individuals — things like DNA testing, fingerprint registries, or dental records.

But the grounds for suspecting Felt of being the princess were themselves quite shaky — an unusual physical trait, and an age that happened to match the missing princess's.

If that was enough to function as sufficient proof of lineage in this world, then the Royal Selection could easily have ended that very day, right before Subaru made a fool of himself at the royal castle.

"But that's not how it went. And the biggest reason for that is—"

"—the Dragon History Stone."

Subaru raised an eyebrow at having his conclusion preempted, and found Petra, having just finished drying the plate Garfiel handed her, holding it out toward him while looking his way.

"Yeah, that's it," Subaru nodded, taking both the plate and the conclusion from her.

"Some prophecy tablet that's supposedly helped out whenever the kingdom got into serious trouble throughout history... apparently that Dragon History Stone had instructions on it to hold a Royal Selection."

"Huh? Way you're sayin' that, sounds like there's somethin' about it that's botherin' you, boss."

"Nah, when I first heard about it, I didn't think much of it. I mean, it's a fantasy world, so I figured sure, mysterious items like that exist — if anything it got me kind of pumped..."

"But it's different now?"

"Yeah. Because these days, I've got plenty of firsthand experience with items that claim to tell you about the future... and a lot of it's been bad experience, too."

Subaru made a face like he'd bitten into a bitter bug, and Petra and Garfiel exchanged glances.

It had been nearly two years since Subaru started his life in another world, and honestly, this world had an awful lot of things that dressed themselves up as foreknowledge or the guidance of fate while trying to plant ideas in people's heads.

In Vollachia, Abel had treated the "Stargazers" as enemies, and the "Witch of Greed" he'd met at the tomb had handed Beatrice a blank book that dictated how to live. And above all, the "Gospels" said to reach the hands of Witch Cultists were even rumored to twist the very shape of who they were into something wicked.

How, exactly, was the Dragon History Stone in question any different from that kind of outside interference bending fate out of shape?

"I get the feeling that if something claims to show you the future, you end up leaning on it without meaning to. Especially if it's already got a track record behind it. But letting the mark win small at first, then wringing it dry for real later — that's a classic scam tactic."

You let them win where the stakes are small, all to drag them into a convenient choice later.

As long as you don't know what you'll be made to do at that crucial, high-stakes choice, it's dangerous to trust too much that fate's little tip-offs are good for you.

Thinking that, Subaru felt a sense of alarm toward the Dragon History Stone's prophecy itself—

"—"
"—"

"Huh? What's with that look on both your faces?"

Having voiced the misgivings he held, Subaru found Garfiel and Petra staring at him with wide eyes.

The two of them were the youngest of the group, but as a pair — one intuitive, one logical — they were reliable people to talk things over with. It was rare for both of them to react to a single question in exactly the same way. He'd been counting on the fact that if one of them tilted their head in confusion, the other would come through with a useful answer.

And to a bewildered Subaru, the two of them opened their mouths one right after the other:

"How do I put this—"
"—It just surprised me that the idea of doubting the Dragon History Stone's prophecy would even occur to someone."

"Huh, the idea wouldn't occur to you?"

"Yep." "Uh-huh."

When both of them nodded at the same time, Subaru thought it must be some kind of joke. But their eyes stayed dead serious, their expressions colored heavily with confusion and bewilderment, and Subaru blinked in surprise.

Their reaction felt like the kind you'd have on being shown a completely unexpected way to solve a quiz — the reaction of running into something genuinely unforeseen. It had been a while since he'd felt a culture gap like this — no, actually, he'd just tasted the same thing recently, over the matter of hereditary succession to the throne.

"Honestly, I figured that one was just the tragedy of me being way too unfamiliar with a monarchy where the king ranks above everyone... but is this something even deeper, like a matter of religious values?"

It stood to reason that different climates bred different customs and different common sense, but conversely, given how fundamental a difference in values this was, it was almost a wonder no major friction had erupted before now. He'd thought his life in another world had been fairly rough so far, but apparently it hadn't hit rock bottom yet.

It also made him suddenly understand, all at once, the weight behind this country being called the "Dragon-Devoted Kingdom."

"Petra's a lovely flower that bloomed in some rural village, and Garfiel's the golden rookie who grew up in a hidden village cut off from the outside world."

In other words, their standing as citizens of the kingdom — in terms of both education and privilege — was nothing compared to those born and raised in the great cities. And yet even these two had no notion of doubting the Dragon History Stone at all — proof of just how deeply trust in the "Dragon" had settled, sediment-like, at the bottom of their unconscious minds.

If that held true even for people with backgrounds like theirs, then those whose identities were even more strongly tied to the kingdom would be all the less likely to arrive at any notion of taking the Dragon History Stone lightly. That was precisely why this country had the groundwork in place to carry out something like the Royal Selection without hesitation, even in a situation with no royal family left.

If that was the case, then—

"—what Crusch-san was trying to do really was something incredible."

Setting aside whether her ideal was right or wrong, Subaru finally, belatedly came to understand just how enormous a decision Crusch's stance — breaking free of the Covenant with the "Dragon" — really was, in a world this saturated with trust in the "Dragon."

In fact, going purely by advance reputation, Crusch had been the overwhelming frontrunner for the Royal Selection.

The very fact that the Royal Selection hadn't wound up painted entirely in Crusch's colors made it plain just how hard the platform she'd championed was for the present-day kingdom to swallow. At the same time, Subaru found himself speculating about the true aim behind the Subjugation of the White Whale that had taken place right after the Royal Selection began.

No doubt, part of the reason behind that subjugation was Crusch's wish to let Wilhelm, whom she'd welcomed into her camp, finally settle his vendetta. But wasn't there more to it — an attempt to prove something, by felling single-handedly the White Whale, one of the Three Great Witchbeasts that had tormented people for centuries?

To prove her own resolve and her ideal: that she could lead the kingdom forward with a strong hand, without ever relying on the Covenant with the "Dragon."

—In the end, because of Subaru's intervention, the White Whale's subjugation became known to the world as the achievement of a joint operation among three camps, with the Emilia Camp and the Anastasia Camp cooperating as well.

Even so, Crusch surely hadn't minded. Wilhelm had settled his vendetta, and even while exposed to headwinds from all around her, Crusch had managed to take that first step toward carrying her ideal through.

And yet, that ideal of Crusch's ended up crushed in a way no one had foreseen—

"Am I an idiot. No — I am an idiot."

With his understanding finally reaching that far, Subaru keenly felt the shallowness of his own thinking.

He'd gone to visit her before he even understood the real meaning of what ideal of hers had nearly been snapped in two, and comforted her with promises to help, on the strength of nothing but shallow sympathy and sentiment. Put that together with the story of Crusch's tragic love with the fourth prince that Ferris had told him afterward, at the mausoleum, and it made him want to keel over and die of mortification just thinking about how thoughtlessly his words must have skated over the surface of it all.

But—

"Hey, boss. Not that I really wanna say this, but you runnin' your mouth off however momentum carries ya — ain't that just like the old tale of 'Garagokkuru's Lovesickness'—"

"It's just, there've been times we got saved by Subaru being reckless or going in without thinking things through, so... maybe we'd rather you not talk that down too much."

"Ahh!? That's exactly what I was about to say!"

"Sorry, I just wanted to score some points with Subaru."

Petra stuck her tongue out and apologized in the cutest way possible, and Garfiel wilted with a defeated "gao..."

At their words, Subaru bit down hard on the inside of his cheek without meaning to. He'd been startled at first, and then immediately lost track of what expression he was even supposed to make.

He did regret the words he'd chosen with Crusch, but he didn't want to scowl in the face of Petra and Garfiel's consideration either. The result was some halfway, mismatched expression, caught between conflicting feelings, as he said—

"...Don't go spoiling me like that too much. Once I start letting myself be spoiled, there's no end to it."

"Is that so? Then I want to spoil Subaru more and more. Right, Garf-san?"

"Dunno if 'spoilin'' is even the right word for it, but both you and Otto-nii look after everybody else and never take care of yourselves. Every once in a while, I don't mind tossin' you up in the air a bit myself."

"Last time you did that, Otto went straight through the ceiling..."

That, if he recalled right, had been the tragedy that unfolded when Otto, having plowed through a mountain of work, got drunk, and a fired-up Garfiel tried to reward him for it with everything he had. Otto hadn't been hurt, and the alcohol had wiped the memory from his mind, so it was a funny story by now.

Anyway—

"I figured there was already a lot I wanted to do and a lot I needed to do... but now there's one more thing I need to check on, too."

Full of gratitude, Subaru reached out both hands to Garfiel's and Petra's heads. Before the two of them, squirming ticklishly under his petting, he narrowed his eyes at the soap bubbles floating and popping in the wash tub.

There were plenty of people smarter and more knowledgeable than Subaru. Amid people like that, if he could give some meaning to his own presence through this kind of action, this kind of thinking—

"—then first, I want to really find out just what kind of thing the Dragon History Stone actually is."

What kind of power was at work in it, what intent was steering it toward whatever future — being able to doubt that was, he wanted to believe, precisely the strength of Natsuki Subaru, a man from another world.

First, he'd start by finding out. —If he really meant to stand beside Crusch and Ferris, both left to nothing but mounting frustration with no way to act, in the way he truly wanted to.

That said—

"...Knowing Ferris, he'd probably call this kind of thinking arrogant, huh."

With this coming the very next day, there wasn't a soul among them who could fail to connect the fact that the Divine Dragon Church, right at the center of the current storm, had gone out of its way to march straight into their midst, with the Royal Selection.

Naturally, Sakura, whom they'd shown into the parlor, was also presumed to have some reason of her own that called for real caution—

"—You're Subaru-chan, aren't you. Being stared at that intently makes me shy, you know."

"Oh, sorry. Was it rude of me, since Sakura-san's so pretty?"

"My, flirting while apologizing — what a naughty boy. I never thought you'd be such a dexterous one. I've heard all sorts of rumors about you, Subaru-chan."

"Rumors? About me?"

At the unexpected counterstrike, Subaru pointed at himself, and Sakura smiled and said, "Yes."

She went on stirring her own tea with a teaspoon, brought the cup to her lips once more, and let out a breath with a gesture that carried an indefinable air of allure.

Then, tilting her head at the vague silence that had settled in, she said—

"...Could it be that I was expected to say something more just now?"

"Well, given how the conversation was going, wouldn't you expect me to want to know just what rumor about me you'd heard, Sakura-san?"

"Eh? But surely Emilia-chan and the others in your own camp know you far better than that already, Subaru-chan. At this point, does it really matter what kind of rumors are floating around about you?"

"I see, that's rather a good point, I suppose. Indeed, Betty and the others understand Subaru best of all. There's nothing to worry about, I suppose."

"That's right. I'm so proud to have such a splendid knight of my own."

With Beatrice on his lap and Emilia at his right both stamping their seal of approval on him like that, Subaru felt glad on one hand, but the whole matter of the rumors about him ended up washed away in a way that left him less than satisfied.

In any case, including Petra standing by along the wall, it was Subaru, Emilia, Beatrice, and Otto handling the reception of Sakura, who'd come charging in without any appointment.

At present, the impression Sakura gave off was of a woman who was hard to get any real grip on, but—

"Sakura-san, are you close with Filore? We just became friends recently."

"Oh yes, I've heard about it from Filore-chan until I was sick of it. She goes around saying 'my Emilia, my Emilia' about every little thing... well, I suppose I'm a fellow member of Filore-chan's church, something like a stand-in older sister to her."

"Filore's big sister!"

"Wow, quite a gap in energy level between those two sisters, huh."

"A stand-in sister, mind you. That girl was raised as the church's treasured little secret, hidden away and cherished dearly, so you could also say she's more like the youngest daughter of the church as a whole. Hee hee."

With the corners of her eyes softened, Sakura spoke like that, the warmth of her smile staying exactly the same the whole time. Because of that, it was anyone's guess how much of her explanation about Filore could be taken at face value.

Still, the phrase describing Filore as the church's treasured secret, raised in hiding, was something worth taking note of.

"Then are we to understand that the reason Filore-san hasn't appeared in public before now is due to a policy of the Divine Dragon Church along those lines?"

"That's right, hmm. Well, that's all become meaningless now, seeing as Filore-chan went and leapt right out into the open all on her own."

"So Filore going rogue really was completely unforeseen by the church, huh..."

If he didn't know her personally, it would've been a hard story to swallow no matter how you sliced it, but for Subaru, who'd actually exchanged words with Filore, it was a turn of events that felt completely natural.

Just yesterday, she'd been ordered, against her will, to stay put at the church, but going by how she'd been acting, it had felt like she'd hit the limit of her patience any moment and come bursting outside on her own.

"Actually, that's exactly what happened — she really did burst out. And that's why I'm here today, intruding on you and Emilia-chan and everyone like this."

"...Eh!? You mean she already burst out — as in, today!?"

"Yes, it happened this very morning. I'd made sure to tell her all about today's plans since yesterday, and yet she really is a handful..."

"No, no, no, is 'she's such a handful' really an excuse to just sit here drinking tea!?"

Even without any grand public announcement, there was no mistaking that Filore had shot straight up the ranks to become a figure of importance within the kingdom. With someone like that off acting on her own, what exactly was her supposed minder doing lounging around here at leisure?

To Subaru and the others flustering over that, Sakura laughed "hee hee" as if it were none of her business at all, and said,

"Do rest easy on that front. As it happens, I already know exactly where Filore-chan is headed. Straight to her friend Emilia-chan's place, no less."

"Oh, is that all? Well, then... she hasn't come here!?"
"She hasn't, right!?"

Just when it looked like this might be a reassurance, the information failed to fully reassure them, and the panic carried right on.

From how certain Sakura sounded, there might have been a note or something from Filore announcing her destination, but no one in the camp had actually laid eyes on her. Naturally, neither Petra nor Otto knew where she'd gone either.

"Beako doesn't know either, obviously. She's been with me the whole time since I woke up today."

"That's right, I suppose. Betty went to bed early and rose early with Subaru, brushed her teeth with Subaru, did her 'radio taisou' with Subaru, and then had 'breakfast' with Subaru, I suppose. And through all of that, Betty never once saw that boisterous girl, I suppose."

"W-what should we do. If something's happened to Filore..."

Putting their heads together, Subaru and the others racked their brains hard over the whereabouts of the vanished Filore.

When she'd first appeared out of nowhere, her very existence had been a shock, and now, learning she'd suddenly vanished brought on yet another shock of a different kind. Filore was practically a human-shaped storm.

As Subaru and the others flapped about like that, Otto let out a small sigh beside them.

"Hey, what, Otto...! Don't tell me — you're thinking that since she's such a headache, it'd be cleaner all around if she just got blown away for good, or something..."

"Otto, even so, that's a bit too much Roswaal for you, I suppose..."

"Please stop the baseless rumor-mongering. Beatrice-chan, don't look at me like that either. ...Just to confirm, is Filore-san wearing the Divine Dragon Church's nun's habit?"

With a vein popping at the looks from Subaru and Beatrice clinging together, Otto then turned to Sakura to confirm Filore's attire.

When Sakura nodded to that question with "Of course she is," Otto said—

"Then there's nothing to worry about. Short of some extraordinary circumstance, a woman dressed as a Divine Dragon Church nun isn't going to get caught up in trouble out on the street."

"Is that true? Petra-chan, really?"

"Yes, I think Otto-san's right. If someone from the church were in trouble, I'm sure somebody nearby would definitely lend a hand."

At Otto and Petra's conviction, delivered as though it were only natural, Subaru and Emilia both felt their eyes opened, thinking, "Huh, so that's how it is."

"But that's assuming she's the one in trouble out on the street, right? What about something like some malicious somebody's wicked, grasping hand reaching out... wouldn't that be a whole different story?"

"What is that, some somebody made entirely of 'evil' and 'wicked' and 'malicious'... Given Sakura-san's whole attitude, I doubt that's a worry either. In the first place, the reason she's here is—"

"—My, Otto-chan really does think fast. That's exactly right — I got here ahead of her."

Just as Otto reached the conclusion he was about to voice, Sakura swept it right out from under him, stating the reason she herself had come to visit the royal guesthouse.

It was a subtly under-explained way of putting it, but combining the content with the real Filore's headlong recklessness, the answer clicked for Subaru too, a beat behind Otto.

"So basically, Filore left saying she was going to Emilia-tan's place, but since she doesn't know exactly where that is, she's making her way here asking around all over..."

"And you, her stand-in sister, got here ahead of Filore, who'll be arriving eventually, and are just sitting here drinking tea with Betty and the others, I suppose."

"Pitter-pat-pat, spot on. Emilia-chan's people really are quite excellent."

Sakura gave a little clap in praise, but Subaru and the others weren't naive enough to take that compliment entirely at face value. Besides, even combining people's kindness toward members of the Divine Dragon Church with Filore's sheer drive to act, could anyone really be certain she was absolutely safe?

Emilia seemed to share the same worry, and, furrowing her well-shaped brows, she said—

"Sakura-san sounds so confident, but I'm still worried about Filore. Maybe I should just go take a quick look around this area—"

"—Emilia-san, may I have a moment. A guest has arrived."

Rem's voice, knocking on the parlor door, cut Emilia's worry off right at that moment. Everyone's eyes turned to the door on reflex, and Emilia tilted her head, saying, "A guest?"

And right after that, the door was flung open from the other side with a bang—

"—It's terrible, Emilia! Help your friend, me!"

"Filore!?"

Right before Emilia's eyes, wide with surprise, the one who came tumbling breathlessly into the parlor was none other than Filore herself, the very center of the topic at hand.

With her red eyes brimming, she rushed straight up to Emilia, who was staring at her, and dove headfirst into her chest without letting up any of her momentum at all. The force of it was practically a headbutt, but Emilia caught Filore's charge without any trouble and held her close.

"Whoa, that startled me. Are you okay, Filore? You're not hurt anywhere?"

"Yes, yes, I'm fine... no, wait, I'm not! I'm in absolute tatters! Look — not my body, my heart is in tatters!"

"Oh no, that's terrible... where exactly should I look?"

"I don't know either, so just hold me tight!"

"Okay! Tiiight!"

The moment she walked in, Filore painted the whole room in her own colors with tremendous momentum. And in response to her momentum-driven words and actions, Emilia's gallant readiness to go along with it without a moment's hesitation shone through.

While Subaru watched the two of them embracing like that, Petra came up beside him and tugged at the sleeve of his tracksuit, saying—

"...Is that the rumored 'Saintess,' then?"

"Yep, that's the rumored 'Saintess' for you. So, how different was she from what you pictured?"

"Pretty different, maybe... oh, but I think it's fine. Every 'Saintess' is different, after all!"

"So it's an age of diversity even among 'Saintesses'... doesn't quite land for me, somehow."

Even Petra, skilled at both listening and talking, couldn't quite put into words just how overwhelmed she felt.

In any case, the worst-case possibility — that Filore, wandering the royal capital alone, might never manage to find her way to this guesthouse — had at least been averted for now.

"Now the question is just what Filore, who's going as far as begging for help, actually wants..."

"For now, I don't get a good feeling about it, I suppose."

Subaru was in complete agreement with Beatrice's guess. Off to the side from the two of them, Emilia soothed Filore, who was gradually calming down in her arms, in a gentle voice, saying—

"So, can you tell me what happened? What do you mean by help?"

"It's just, it's just that... something terrible has happened, Emilia."

"Terrible... *gulp*."

At Filore's portentous way of putting it, Emilia audibly swallowed. Watching Emilia up close like that, Filore went on.

It was this—

"—It seems I have to take part in the Royal Selection as a candidate!"

In a voice cracking through her tears, Filore lamented the tragic fate that had been thrust upon her.

Hearing that, Emilia's violet eyes went wide, Otto put a hand to his forehead, Petra bit her lip on her cute face, and Subaru and Beatrice exchanged glances, saying—

"Just as expected..."
"The bad feeling hit the mark, I suppose."

"Whatever am I supposed to do... eugh! Sakura!?"

Beside Subaru and the others, who'd just scored a successful but unwelcome foresight, Sakura — whose presence Filore hadn't noticed yet, since Filore had had her back to the parlor entrance — waved at her.

At this unexpected encounter with someone from her own church, Filore — who'd brought all this upheaval with her — had her mouth hanging open so wide it looked like her jaw might come unhinged. —It thoroughly ruined the effect of a pretty girl.
