Arc 10 Chapter 15: "Together to Hell"


—When the spreading hellfire swallowed Natsuki Subaru, Beatrice doubted her own eyes.

"Why...!"

The deployed E・M・T was a taboo technique that forcibly inserted instructions into the Mana-based interference generated within its field—primarily altering the structure of magic spells to dismantle and dissolve them—a cheat magic that, under normal circumstances, would undoubtedly be classified as forbidden arts.
Of course, if someone saw through the workings of E・M・T and wove a spell capable of functioning even after its magic formulation had been unraveled, there was a possibility it could be breached. However, such an anomaly—replacing a single part of a precisely constructed Magicreation Tool with something entirely different yet making it function identically—was impossible for anyone other than Roswaal or the Witch of Greed... her mother.
Barring such irregularities, no one should have been able to cast magic.
And yet—

"How on earth did you break through it, I suppose?"

"—It wasn't a grand breakthrough or anything. I just racked my brain and put my body on the line."

"――――"

Responding to Beatrice as she posed the question and narrowed her eyes, Tiga Rauleon was pushing himself upright, half his body leaning against the wall.
The young man, who should have been knocked out cold after being kicked into the wall by the raging girl with the 'Dragon's Eye'—Crusch—during the fierce clash, let out a deep, exhausted breath. Tiga held his beloved sword in one hand and pressed his other hand against his flank. —Seeing that the clothes around the flank he was touching were scorched and his skin charred, Beatrice shivered in horror.
That was not a wound inflicted by Crusch. And that very wound—

"...What on earth were you thinking, I suppose?"

"I only did what was necessary, Lady Beatrice. The scripture says: 'While the Dragon circles the sky, man shall till the earth. He who lets his allotted time rot in sleep shall fail to tread even the Dragon's shadow in the coming season of harvest'... or so it goes."

Tiga slowly shook his head and recited a passage from the scriptures of the Divine Dragon Church, reminiscent of Filore. However, if asked whether his ability to recite scripture by heart justified the decisiveness of his choice, Beatrice could not easily agree.
The scorched wound on Tiga's flank was caused by the same flames that had swallowed Subaru and Crusch—except those flames had burned him from the inside, not the outside.

The effect of E・M・T was to disassemble the magic formulation activated within its field.
Tiga had broken through this condition of being 'within the field' by sheer brute force.
His method—

"—Magic generated inside my own body isn't affected by this forbidden art. ...I wasn't entirely certain, but it seems I was right."

"――――"

"I realized it in the middle of the fight. Hey, magic seems unusable right now, but the effects of the Flow Method are still working properly. So—"

"Even so, neither cutting open your own stomach nor using magic while prepared to have your internal organs burned through that wound is an easy choice, I suppose. ...Only a human whose heart is trapped by something beyond a mere sense of duty could do such a thing, I suppose."

"It's a bit hurtful to sound like I'm being called crazy. ...Though I can hardly blame you."

Despite the heaviness in his voice from exhaustion, Tiga's answer itself was indifferent, causing Beatrice to feel her nonexistent guts chill to the bone.

The Tiga that Beatrice knew was limited to a very brief period—just a day or two since they met at the Divine Dragon Church's chapel. Yet, even in that short time, it was true that she had formed a favorable impression of him, seeing how well he got along with Subaru and enjoying his lighthearted tone and demeanor.
That impression did not align with the Tiga standing before her. —Indeed, his mechanical judgment to disregard his own well-being to accomplish a necessary task was the exact opposite of everything she had seen of him so far.

What on earth drove him to go so far?
An abnormal obsession beyond a sense of duty—Beatrice knew humans who acted out of such resolve. Tiga's obsession was of the same nature as Roswaal's.
A man who had spent four hundred years twisting countless fates, yet still sought to reach the realm of a Warlock to achieve his goal. —Was Tiga trying to reach the same place as Roswaal?

"I'm only doing what's necessary. —To take back my life."

In response to Beatrice's doubt and silence, Tiga gave a slight shrug. Then, looking toward the roaring flames deep inside the room—

"If you ask me, Lady Beatrice, your reaction is what's unexpected—or surprising, rather. Honestly, I was prepared for you to blast me with magic out of nowhere. After all, given what I've done."

"...Should I take that as a confirmation that this was no error in judgment made in the heat of the moment, I suppose?"

"Yeah, that's correct. —Duchess Crusch Karsten refused our repeated demands for surrender. We even gave her a final ultimatum, but she struck it down."

"――――"

"After killing Lord McMahon, she pointed her sword at us too—she wasn't in her right mind. So, making an on-the-spot decision, I thoroughly eliminated the danger. ...Is what I'm saying wrong?"

Masking his handsome face—the kind that gave off the impression of a stylishly charming man—with emotionlessness, Tiga questioned Beatrice on the rightness of his actions.
There was no face of a martyr repenting his deeds and begging for forgiveness, but rather that of an executioner calmly verifying where the responsibility for his blood-stained hands lay.
Within Beatrice, any initial impression of Tiga was irrecoverable.

"What you say isn't wrong, I suppose."

Facing Tiga directly, Beatrice narrowed her round eyes and nodded.
In truth, it was so. In the sequence of events since Beatrice and the others witnessed the tragedy at the McMahon mansion and encountered Crusch, the culprit, there was nothing wrong with Tiga's actions. He had done his utmost to follow Subaru's intent and cooperate in subduing Crusch, and it was undeniably Crusch herself who had rejected every single choice.
Tiga had every right to make a ruthless decision to protect himself from such a dangerous person. Even if that decision involved dragging Subaru—who had been his ally—into the collateral damage, it would be morally indefensible to blame him without trial for that reason alone.
Therefore—

"Betty is not so unreasonable as to blame you selfishly, I suppose."

"...I see. Then—"

"—However."

Saying that, Beatrice cut Tiga off.
For a moment, a look not of surprise, but of cold calculation flashed across Tiga's yellow eyes. Beatrice pushed any emotion regarding that to the back of her mind and continued.
Tiga's words and actions were justified, but even so—

"—Betty will take the side of the one who saved Subaru, I suppose."

—In that instant, the intense fire so hot it threatened to scorch the eyes turned toward it was cleaved in two from the far side.

"――――"

The flame was cut silently; as the swaying blaze split apart, standing calmly in the open space created at the center was the girl of the 'Dragon's Eye'—Crusch Karsten, who had delivered a hand-blade strike with her right hand to sever the flames while cradling the unconscious Subaru in her left arm.

"I cut the heat... no, the flow of force. So this is what a 'vital point' is."

Touching the eyelid of her 'Dragon's Eye' with the right hand that had delivered the hand-blade, Crusch muttered as if coming to an understanding.
While feeling amazed at the fact that a single sweep of Crusch's arm had dispelled those raging flames, Beatrice verified that Subaru, held in Crusch's arm, was safe, feeling an even greater sense of relief.

—Immediately after Subaru was swallowed by the flames, the reason Beatrice had managed not to lose her composure was that the path connecting her as a spirit to him as her contractor reported no grave danger.

That fact, alongside what she had witnessed in the final moment—Crusch pulling Subaru down and throwing herself in front of the approaching flames to protect him—settled Beatrice's mind.
Beatrice would side with Crusch Karsten.

"At the very least, I will hear her out, I suppose. Any detailed decisions can come after that, I suppose."

"—That's hardly a wise decision, Lady Beatrice. Just because she saved your contractor Subaru, you mustn't let your judgment be clouded. The Duchess is dangerous."

"Don't treat Betty like a reckless, thoughtless spirit, I suppose. Ah, but if Betty were a rash and indiscreet spirit, then throwing her lot in with the girl who saved Subaru might be entirely natural after all, I suppose."

"Is this really the time for wordplay?! People will notice this fire and rush here soon. It'll be too late to regret taking the wrong stance once that happens! What will happen to Subaru?!"

"Your words carry no weight when you almost burned Subaru alongside that girl, I suppose. Still, it is true, as you say, that we have little time, I suppose. Therefore—"

As she spoke, Tiga gasped at the movement of Beatrice slowly raising her right hand.
It was not the preparation for magic. Quite the opposite. Tiga sharply narrowed his yellow eyes at Beatrice's intention as she readied to snap her fingers, pointing his shamshir toward her while warning:

"Stop, don't do it! I won't be able to cover for you anymore!"

"Long ago, Betty regretted doing nothing more than crying cutely as she saw off a friend, I suppose. —I will not make the same mistake again, I suppose."

Even in a situation where turmoil surged like a raging wave, Beatrice would make her own decision and choose her own path for as long as she was permitted.
She would never again crouch powerlessly before what was precious to her, as she had done when she lost a friend by merely being swept along.
For that reason—

"—In this moment, I choose your side, Crusch Karsten, I suppose!"

Snapping her fingers, Beatrice used it as a signal to deactivate it—E・M・T.
Instantly, anyone with sharp senses would have felt the invisible field that enveloped the office vanish into thin air.
Neither Tiga nor the bearer of the 'Dragon's Eye' was an exception—

"—One-Cut of a Hundred Men."

The explosive gust released shook the entire estate, centered around the office of the McMahon mansion.

"—And then we shook off that man and escaped into the sewer, I suppose. I wanted to flee farther away, but I couldn't be reckless while Subaru was taking a nap, I suppose."

Listening to Beatrice, who snuggled close beside him to support his body, Subaru tried to organize the information in his confused mind and somehow managed to mutter, "I see..."
Honestly, it was pathetic that amid everything that had happened, he was the only one who had dropped out of consciousness so early. But he put those self-punishing thoughts aside for now.

"First off... Beako, keeping close to Crusch-san was a great call. If we had been separated in that situation, I wouldn't have been able to regret it enough."

"It is only natural to show the utmost consideration for my partner's feelings, I suppose. Betty was certain that Subaru would do this, I suppose."

"Though to an outsider it looks like a reckless choice, so I can't exactly yell 'Right on!' unconditionally."

Despite the dire situation, a faint, wry smile escaped Subaru at Beatrice's smug expression.
However, that part of the story was the only moment he could loosen his lips even a fraction. —Glancing at the throbbing burn on his arm, Subaru bit his lip.

"Tiga did this..."

"There is no doubt, I suppose. When he said during the fight that magic couldn't be used, it was either a bluff, or... No, it is nothing, I suppose."

"What is it? Saying there might be something concerning makes me concerned."

"I merely thought it was not something to dwell on when we already have so many troubles, I suppose. At any rate, that man used magic on the premise of involving us in collateral damage. Betty's opinion of him has hit rock bottom, I suppose."

"...That's because you love me so much."

Subaru felt extremely conflicted in the face of Beatrice's anger as she knitted her brows and puffed her cheeks.
Of course, he had no intention of glossing over the fact that he had been subjected to an attack that could have killed him. Still, it was an opening Tiga found at the brink of life and death while being overwhelmed by Crusch. He couldn't flatly condemn the decision Tiga made in the heat of battle.
At that moment, Beatrice's gaze toward Subaru grew stern.

"Subaru? You don't mean..."

"No, I don't intend to gloss over it. I definitely plan to demand an apology for the pain of this burn. I won't back down on that. That's only natural, right?"

"――――"

"Huh? Why are you looking at me with even more exasperated eyes? I just said I'd make him apologize?!"

Subaru's confusion deepened at Beatrice's deadpan stare upon hearing his answer.
Both sides had faults, but taking the other party's fault slightly more heavily—to Subaru, he thought he was taking quite a decisive stance with that alone.
Besides—

"At the very least, a large part of the friction between us and Tiga comes down to a misunderstanding. If we can talk properly somewhere, we can clear up those misunderstandings and—"

"—Regrettably, I have no intention of allowing you to do that, Sir."

Subaru tried to choose his words carefully to convince Beatrice. But the one who put a stop to Subaru's words was not Beatrice with her furrowed brow, but Crusch, who was walking ahead of them.
Leading the way through the dim sewer with the light emitted from a Lagmite Ore lamp, she clearly expressed her objection to Subaru and Beatrice's conversation—demonstrating it unmistakably by drawing her dagger and pointing its tip directly at Subaru.

"...C-Crusch-san?"

"I will not permit you to contact Tiga Rauleon. Leaving aside the fact that we crossed swords, he is one of the men currently commanding the pursuers on our tail. If you go to meet him carelessly, it will only lead to you being bound in chains, Sir."

"That's... but from Tiga's standpoint, it's unavoidable, or rather, given the circumstances..."

"――――"

"――――"

Facing Crusch, whose left eye was covered by an eyepatch, with only her single amber eye locked onto his, Subaru hesitated greatly in choosing his next words.
The atrocity at the McMahon mansion, as well as her pointing a dagger at him right here and now—talking to the current Crusch required the utmost care. Indeed, if he carelessly provoked her with an indiscreet remark, Subaru would end up making her stain her hands with blood this time.
With such thoughts, their standoff continued for a moment, until—

"...Remarkably, no wind of deception blows from you."

Saying so, Crusch was the one to lower her dagger first.
Subaru widened his eyes in surprise at her words, but as Crusch returned the dagger to the sheath on her belt, she continued:

"No wind of lies blows from you, Sir. Not from the time we encountered each other at Miklotov McMahon's estate until this very moment. That is why I permitted Beatrice to accompany us as well."

"—In the end... in the end, what do you want to do with us, Crusch-san?"

Glancing briefly to exchange a look with Beatrice beside him, Subaru asked Crusch directly.
The wind of lies—as the bearer of the 'Divine Protection of Wind Indication', Crusch could feel the thoughts and intentions of others as winds, serving as a sort of lie detector.
When Subaru first met her, she had seen right through the deception in his mind, making him acutely aware of his twisted nature. —And now, that served as the foundation for Crusch's words and actions.

Believing Subaru was the result of her Divine Protection of Wind Indication.
Then was cutting down Miklotov and the thirteen people at the mansion also the result of her Divine Protection of Wind Indication?

"Tell me, Crusch-san. What do you... no, most importantly, what about you yourself? What do you want to achieve by doing all this?"

"—What I wish to do... no, what I must accomplish, is to save this Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica from the hands of traitors. That is likely the final act I can perform for the kingdom."

"Traitors... and 'final act'?"

"Would you claim otherwise, Sir? I succumbed to the malice of the Witch Cult and spent a long time bedridden in futility. On top of that, resurrected by the hands of the 'Divine Dragon Church' in a manner directly opposing the abolition of the Dragon's rule that I pledged as my campaign promise—do you truly believe I still have a chance of winning?"

"That's...!"

"A wind of understanding is blowing, Sir. —You are an honest man."

Lowering her eyes slightly, Crusch etched a self-deprecating smile onto her lips.
Through her Divine Protection, Crusch must have seen it in the eyes of her feeble smile—Subaru's feeling of helplessness, unable to deny her words and unable to come up with a reliable breakthrough.
Hearing himself called honest or upright under such circumstances was hardly something he could be pleased with.

"That is enough, I suppose. Betty will no longer tolerate you acting like a tragic heroine and lashing out at Subaru, I suppose."

"Beatrice..."

"And Subaru is just as bad, I suppose. Why are you feeling down over something that isn't your fault, I suppose? At this rate, you'll start blaming yourself even if a child trips on the roadside, I suppose. If that happens, you'll be too busy to dote on Betty, I suppose. I strongly protest, I suppose!"

Placing her hands on her hips, Beatrice displayed an out-of-place anger. Knowing that this was her own way of showing consideration to downplay the tension between Subaru and Crusch, Subaru let out a breath.
Crusch, for her part, nodded with a solemn expression and said, "Indeed."

"As Beatrice says, this is not a moment to cling to minor friction. The more time we give the opposition, the more disadvantageous our position becomes. Therefore, Natsuki Subaru, Sir—no, Sir and Beatrice, I wish to request your cooperation."

"...Cooperation?"

"Indeed. Right now, we are in the midst of a crisis threatening the very survival of the nation due to the secret machinations of numerous traitors who have penetrated into the heart of the kingdom. In such a situation, I wish to borrow your strength—the strength of Natsuki Subaru, who cooperated with my house and made a tremendous contribution to the Subjugation of the White Whale."

"――!"

She sheathed her dagger and extended her open hand toward them. Facing Crusch as she spoke, Subaru involuntarily gasped and stared intently at her face.
What shocked Subaru was not just the request Crusch had voiced. Naturally, he was greatly surprised by that request, but what astonished him even more was that she spoke of the great battle against the White Whale fought in the past as something that had undeniably happened to herself.

Receiving that, Subaru finally voiced the question that had been lingering in his chest ever since he ran into Crusch at the McMahon mansion up to this very moment.
That was—

"Let me ask you one thing. —Did your 'memories' return, Crusch-san?"

"—Of course. Do you sense in the current me any remnant of the despicable, weak Crusch Karsten from the time when I lacked my memories? That question is downright offensive."

"――――"

At Crusch explicitly speaking of the restoration of her 'memories', Subaru's cheeks hardened.
The shock brought about was a complex mixture of joy and resentment. The fact that Crusch's memories had returned was joyous from the bottom of his heart. However, just because her memories were back, for her to disparage the Crusch from the time she lacked memories so harshly—

"A wind of dissatisfaction. Do you mean to say you supported the me who lacked her memories, Sir?"

"It's not that simple. Whether you have your memories now or when you didn't, both are Crusch-san to me—"

"—No, it is not! A Crusch Karsten without her memories cannot be Crusch Karsten. That is why!"

"――!"

"...That is why Ferris abandoned Crusch Karsten as well."

"Th—!"

Gritting her molars and trembling with emotion, Crusch spoke, and Subaru tried to clearly declare, "That at least is wrong!"
Not just in this moment, but even before they started moving, he had thought the same thing.
Crusch misunderstood the meaning of the decision Ferris had made, and the depth of his resolve.

Indeed, Ferris had reneged on Crusch's vow—the vow made for the deceased royal family.
Even Subaru, just hearing about it, could easily imagine how important that was to Crusch. But it was the same for Ferris.
Ferris, too, had loved the late Fourth Prince. Yet, he had still chosen.

Love for the living over love for the dead. —Subaru did not want that to be denied.
Much less the tragedy of two people who held mutual affection for each other being unable to share it simply due to a misunderstanding—

"—It seems our time to delay a decision has come to an end."

However, before Subaru could vent his intense emotion, the situation would not allow him to continue.
Crusch muttered as she looked behind them, into the depths of the sewer they had just passed through; the meaning of her murmur was made clear by the voices and footsteps echoing from far behind. —Pursuers.
A party searching for Subaru and Crusch had descended into the sewers in a group.

"If we talk to them properly..."

"I told you, Sir. You are already viewed as conspiring with me. The sword stripped away by your strange technique was also left behind at the scene. My involvement is obvious when cross-referenced with the wounds on the corpses. On top of that,"

"On top of that? What is it? Is there even worse news?"

"Despite the presence of Tiga Rauleon, who knew your intentions, you have also been designated as an accomplice in the murder of Miklotov McMahon. That man must have reported it as such."

"――――"

"The rest is your choice, Sir. If you deem me unworthy of your trust, you need only seek protection from the pursuers coming after us. —Farewell."

Saying everything she wanted to say and having no intention of engaging in further conversation, Crusch turned her back, threw her lamp into the sewage, and broke into a dashing run.
When even the faint light from the light source sinking into the drain water could no longer reach her back, only her receding footsteps pressed Subaru to make a choice like a countdown clock.

Should he reject Crusch's argument, merge with the soldiers pursuing them, spill everything, and seek protection? Or should he chase after Crusch's back as she disappeared into the darkness to fight a massive conspiracy all on her own? One of two choices.

"Damn it! I want to believe her! I want to believe her, but is there even really a conspiracy in the first place...?!"

She misunderstood Ferris's true intentions and was in a state of mind where she didn't hesitate to point her blade even at Subaru. Including her violent emotional outbursts, Crusch was in an unstable emotional state.
In the worst-case scenario, cutting down Miklotov and everything else might be nothing more than an unjustified rampage by a paranoid Crusch—a danger that could not be ruled out.
In such a situation, what was the right thing to do—?

"Subaru, calm down and listen, I suppose. No matter which option you choose and whose side you take, Betty will always be on Subaru's side, and stay by your side forever, I suppose."

"Beako..."

"I have had quite enough of being separated like in the Empire, I suppose. If Subaru isn't where Betty can see him, my heart races so much I can hardly breathe, I suppose."

Saying so, Beatrice told Subaru, who didn't know the right answer, that no matter what he chose, she would make it the right answer—no, that they would make it right together.
With that, his hesitation cleared. That's right. It was just as Beatrice said.
No matter what choice he made, struggling to make that choice the right one was Natsuki Subaru's best way of fighting, wasn't it?

"I can't leave Crusch-san alone. You're coming with me all the way to hell, Beatrice!"

"If I am with Subaru, I wouldn't mind being sealed for another four hundred years, I suppose!"

"I do mind, so could you not say terrifying things like that?!"

As he spoke, Subaru scooped up his reliable, beloved partner, and ran furiously through the dim darkness, chasing after the footsteps fading into the distance.

Whether she was right or wrong, he couldn't leave Crusch alone.
Whether it was to stop her or to help her, he must not leave Crusch's side.

"Ah, damn it, my arm hurts!"

The throbbing pain of his burned arm burned like an ache rebuking his foolishness.
Holding onto that pain, rather than lamenting his own situation—being dragged into all this and chased down before he knew it—Subaru worried more for how Emilia and the others would feel when word of this reached them.

Causing trouble was one thing, but the pain of causing them to worry was even worse.
To Emilia, Rem, Ram, Petra, Meili, Garfiel—to everyone. So, biting through the pain of body and mind, Subaru desperately strained his eyes in the dim light and,

"Do something to handle this somehow, Otto!"

...as he ran, tossing this sudden crisis onto a friend who wasn't even here.

—At the same time, in the royal mansion of the Royal Capital, Lugunica.

This day was supposed to be the starting point for an event of immense significance, both for the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica and for the camp of Emilia, one of the Royal Selection candidates.
A golden opportunity that made it possible to eradicate the lingering roots of the calamity that the Witch Cult had brought upon the Watergate City of Priestella. Emilia and her allies were meant to be present at that moment, realized with the cooperation of the Divine Dragon Church, to watch as months of powerlessness were wiped away—or so it was planned.

"—And yet, what on earth is this situation, I wonder?"

Having prepared for departure, they were merely waiting for Subaru and Beatrice to return from their errand elsewhere in the mansion. Among the camp enjoying tea time in the reception room until then, Otto Suwen strove to remain calm as he asked the sudden visitors.

Stomping noisily into the mansion were Royal Soldiers, all wearing equipment indicating their official allegiance to the kingdom—sensing a somewhat heavy tension radiating from them, Otto shielded the women, including Emilia, and stood in front.
Naturally, standing even further ahead of Otto was—

"Well, ain't this pretty damn rough? Just 'cause Lady Emilia is super sweet and soft-hearted don't mean you bastards get to act impolite to us, y'hear!"

"Garfiel, try not to intimidate them so much. They're guests. ...For now."

"Aight, got it, Otto-bro."

Soothe Garfiel as he stood at the very front of the camp gnashing his sharp fangs, if only for show, Otto created an opening to force the visitors to state their business.
It was playing both sides, but it framed the gesture as conceding a turn to put them in their debt.
If they possessed even a minimum level of understanding—

"—Everyone, sheath your swords. Do you realize who you stand before?"

"――――"

The moment that low voice sounded, the Royal Soldiers spread across the reception room instantly snapped to attention, straightening their backs in unison. Slipping through the ring of upright soldiers and walking over with heavy, booming footsteps was a large man so tall one had to look up to meet his eyes.
Covered head to toe in armor gleaming like steel, with a face cut like rock, he was the Commander of the Royal Guard of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica—the man standing at the pinnacle of the kingdom's knights: Marcos Gildark.

"Marcos-san..."

"I offer my deepest apologies for this unannounced visit, Lady Emilia."

Emilia blinked her amethyst eyes at the sight of Marcos stepping forward. In response to her reaction, Marcos offered a bow in flawless accordance with etiquette, in stark contrast to his rugged appearance.
Then, raising his head, his eyes focused on Emilia and everyone surrounding her camp—particularly observing Otto and Garfiel, who held stern expressions—as he spoke:

"My purpose in visiting today is to request Lady Emilia's attendance at the Royal Castle. There is a suspicion that we must verify as a matter of extreme urgency."

"That's quite out of the blue. A suspicion? What could that possibly..."

"—Regarding the background of Knight Natsuki Subaru."

"Subaru's?"

Following etiquette and paying respect, yet adopting a posture that brooked no denial, Marcos delivered his words. Hearing that the suspicion he spoke of was connected to Subaru, Emilia reacted aloud, while Otto and the rest of the camp showed their respective reactions in their expressions and demeanor.
Otto regretted that this hesitation created the very opening that allowed Marcos to deliver his next sentence.

Because the suspicion regarding Natsuki Subaru that Marcos declared was—

"Knight Natsuki Subaru is suspected of having deep ties to the Witch Cult—specifically, of potentially being something akin to a Sin Archbishop."

—...the watershed moment determining whether or not Natsuki Subaru would be branded an enemy of the world.
