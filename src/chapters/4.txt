Arc 1 Part 3: "First Magic"

Is this what they mean when they say time stands still?

At the entrance of the alleyway, just like the men from moments ago, a single girl stood.

She was a beautiful girl.
Her long, silver hair, reaching down to her waist, was tied back, and her intelligent eyes stared straight at us as if piercing right through. Her soft features blended beauty with youth, and a certain noble aura about her gave her an almost perilous charm.

She was about one hundred and sixty centimeters tall. Her dark-blue-themed outfit lacked any gaudy decorations; its simplicity only served to highlight her presence. The only conspicuous detail was the embroidery on the white coat she wore, shaped like the emblem of a "bird resembling a hawk." Even that solemn emblem was nothing more than an accessory to the girl's beauty.

"I cannot overlook any further violence. —That's far enough."

As words were spun from her lips once more, a shiver of profound emotion ran through his entire body.
Her voice, like silver bells, gently caressed his eardrums, and the words she spun had the power to make one's heart tremble.
Subaru forgot his own situation entirely, completely overwhelmed by her sheer presence.

And the men felt the same.
Faced head-on with her hostility, their hotheaded expressions from moments ago vanished completely.
Even the man with the knife turned pale, backing away toward the dead end.

"Wait, wait, wait! Hold on! I-I don't know what's going on, but we'll let this guy go! So please, give us a break..."

"I appreciate how quick you are to back down. It's not too late to fix this, so return what you stole from me."

"Like I said, we're sorry... Huh? Stole what?"

"Please. It is very precious to me. I could give up on anything else, but absolutely not that. —If you return it now, I won't take your lives."

Although her words carried a trace of pleading, the very end of them harbored a distinct, unmistakable anger.
The girl's gaze was sharp, and her outstretched palm held nothing.
Yet, everyone present could feel some indescribable force beginning to gather there.

"W-Wait, hold on! ...Um, I think there's a misunderstanding here!"

"...What?"

Pointing at Subaru, whom they had been kicking, the man asked, "Er, so you didn't come here to save this guy?"

"...He certainly is dressed strangely. Are you in the middle of an internal dispute? Three-on-one is hardly admirable... but if you're asking if he has anything to do with me, I have to say he is completely unrelated."

Perhaps thinking they were trying to dodge the question, a hint of irritation crept into the girl's tone.
Sensing her mood and growing frantic, the men flurried to explain themselves.

"W-Wait, hold on! If you're not after him, then we're a completely different matter! If something was stolen from you, it was probably that girl from earlier!"

"Y-Yeah, that's right! The one from just now! She kicked off the wall and escaped across the roofs!"

"At the back! Beyond there! At the speed she was going, she's probably already crossed three streets!"

Under the onslaught of the men's excuses, the girl's gaze locked with Subaru's.
Faced with a look that asked whether the men's words were true, Subaru, unable to bring himself to lie, found himself nodding.
Seeing this, the girl let out a reluctant, groaning "Ugh..." and nodded in acceptance.

"It doesn't seem... like a lie. So the thief went beyond the alley...? I have to hurry."

Turning her back to them, the girl began to walk out of the alley.
The men let out an obvious sigh of relief. Meanwhile, Subaru cursed his own stupidity for letting the atmosphere overwhelm him and throwing away a once-in-a-lifetime chance at rescue.
But...

"That being said, this isn't a situation I can just overlook."

Whirling back, the girl thrust her palm toward them—and from her hand, projectiles flew directly at the standing men.
The speed was major-league caliber, and the trajectory was a dead-on beanball.
Three sounds resembling solid baseballs striking flesh rang out, and the men were blown back with groans of agony.

What had struck the men and landed beside Subaru with a high-pitched clatter were chunks of ice.
The fist-sized blocks of ice—objects manifested in total defiance of the season and physical laws—dispersed into the air as if being consumed by the atmosphere the moment they fulfilled their purpose.

"—Magic."

The word slipped from his mouth instinctively, being the most fitting term to describe the phenomenon he had just witnessed.
He hadn't heard an incantation or anything, but that ice had definitely been generated from the girl's palm and fired.
Seeing this scene play out in reality right before his eyes, there was one thing he realized for the first time.
And that was—

"It's not as fantastical as I thought... It's got a disappointingly realistic feel to it."

He had imagined scattering light, bursting energy, or something like that.
In reality, crude ice just suddenly appeared and suddenly vanished. No elegance, no damn poetry to it at all.

"You've... gone and done it now."

Putting Subaru's impressions aside, the damage to those who had received that realistic blow was severe.
Two of the men wobbled to their feet. One had taken a bad hit and was unconscious, but the other two, though bleeding, were still functional. The man other than the one with the knife was now holding a rusty, machete-like weapon, ready for battle.

"Like hell we care if you're a mage or whatever now! The two of us will surround you and slaughter you... Did you really think you could win a two-on-one, huh?!"

Holding his bent nose with one hand, the knife-wielding man bellowed.
Unfazed by his shouting, the girl responded calmly:

"You're right. Two-on-one might be a bit tough."

"Then, would a two-on-two make it a fair fight?"

As if picking up where the girl left off, a new, high-pitched, androgynous voice vibrated through the alley air.
Startled, Subaru let his eyes wander. The men reacted the same way. There was no sign of anyone who could have made that voice, either at the alley entrance or inside it.
As Subaru and the others stood there bewildered and confused, the girl extended her left hand as if to show them.
Upon her upward-facing palm, resting on her white fingertips, was *that*.

"When you look at me with so much anticipation, well... it makes me blush."

The one who spoke, shyly grooming its face, was a palm-sized cat standing on its hind legs.
It had grey fur and floppy ears; by Subaru's common sense, it most resembled an American Shorthair. Except for its pink nose and strangely long tail, that is.
Seeing this bizarre cat, the man with the knife shouted, his face twisted in dread:

"—A spirit arts user?!"

"Correct. If you back down right now, I won't pursue you. Make up your mind quickly. I'm in a hurry."

Clicking their tongues in frustration at her terms, the men hoisted their unconscious companion and headed out of the alley.
Stepping over Subaru, the man glanced back at the girl as he passed beside her.

"Remember this, brat. You'd better watch your back the next time you wander around here."

"If you do anything to this girl, I'll curse your descendants to the last generation, you know? Though, in your case, you'd be the last of your line."

The threat was likely their desperate attempt at saving face, but the reply, though delivered in a light tone, was harsh.
While the palm-sized cat maintained a casual, grinning attitude, the men turned paler than they had all day, and this time they ran off silently toward the bustling crowd.
Once they vanished from sight, the only ones left in the alley were the girl, the cat, and Subaru.

"—Don't move."

Forgetting his physical pain, Subaru had been about to sit up to offer some words of thanks.
But the girl spoke to him in a cold voice completely devoid of warmth.

Her eyes were dark with wariness. Even though she understood Subaru was a separate entity from the thugs, she didn't believe for a second that he was a force of good. That was the kind of look she gave him.

All the same, her amethyst eyes as she stared at him were mesmerizingly beautiful.
Being thoroughly unaccustomed to beautiful girls, Subaru instinctively blushed and looked away.
Seeing his reaction, the girl, maintaining her wary gaze, smirked confidently.

"Averting your eyes because you have a guilty conscience. It seems my intuition was spot on."

"I don't know about that. That was just a boy's natural reaction. There was zero evil intent."

"Quiet, Puck. —You, do you have any idea who stole my insignia?"

Silencing the little cat, the girl threw the question at Subaru. She had a smug look on her face, a rare sight in this day and age. However—

"Sorry to disappoint you, but I haven't got a clue."

"No way!?"

As her smug expression crumbled, a glimpse of her genuine self peered through.
Her dignified demeanor from earlier was nowhere to be seen. In a fluster, she turned to the cat on her palm.

"W-What should I do? Was this really just a total waste of time...?"

"And that waste of time is ticking away as we speak. I think you'd better hurry. That thief was insanely fast on her feet, so she definitely has a Divine Protection of Wind."

"Why are you acting like this is someone else's problem, Puck?"

"Even though you were the one who told me not to interfere or say anything. Also, what should we do with that boy?"

Subaru let out a bitter chuckle as the spotlight returned to him as an afterthought.
The girl let out an "Ah," as if only just remembering his existence and current state. Subaru put up a brave front, pushing himself up to stand.

"You've done more than enough by saving me. You're in a hurry, right? You should get going."

—*I could help you out if you want, what do you say, miss?*
That was the line he had planned to deliver while running a hand through his hair with a sparkling grin, but...

"Uh-oh?"

"Ah, you probably shouldn't push yourself to stand up—and I'm too late."

His head felt heavy, and his body wobbled. The hand he reached out to steady himself missed the wall and grabbed thin air.
As a result, he ended up bestowing a second kiss upon the ground he had been lying on just moments ago.
Zero attempts at breaking his fall. Face-planting nose-first, a sharp pain threatened to steal Subaru's consciousness away.

"—So, what are we going to do?"

"He has nothing to do with us. It's not like he's going to die, so I'm leaving him."

Beyond the edge of his fading consciousness, he could faintly hear the conversation of those two (one person and one animal).
As expected of an alternate world fantasy, they sure hold some harsh views on human kindness.

A negative thought: *Am I really going to be abandoned in this alleyway like this?*
A positive thought: *Well, I was about to die, so I should be grateful just to be alive.*
Arriving at these two passive conclusions, Subaru's consciousness drifted further and further away—

"Really?"

"Really!"

At the exact moment his consciousness snapped, he saw the silver-haired girl looking back, her face flushed red.

"—I am absolutely, positively not going to help you!"

—*Even her angry face is incredibly cute. Alternate world fantasy is amazing.*

With that final thought, Subaru's consciousness plunged into darkness for real this time.
