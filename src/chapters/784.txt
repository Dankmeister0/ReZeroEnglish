Arc 10 Chapter 25: "I Want to Love the Smiling You"


—I used to think that the moment you pay back a mountain of accumulated debts would come far more dramatically.

"—Heinkel Astrea-dono, an order to appear in the Royal Capital has been issued. We ask that you accompany us."

It was in the middle of the day, as Heinkel sat in the corner of a tavern with cheap booze in hand, agonizing over how bleak his future looked, when he was surrounded by knights wearing the white uniforms of the Royal Guard who spoke those words.
Knowing full well who he was as they called for his surrender, Heinkel felt genuine sympathy for the knights, thinking they were being forced into a job they didn't want to do.

"The Royal Capital, huh... Just what crime are you dragging me in for?"

"Regrettably, we do not have the authority to inform you of that. However, if you intend to resist..."

Slowly, tension mounted among the knights.
Although his breath reeked of alcohol, his mind wasn't clouded by drunkenness yet. By Heinkel's intuition, aside from the three in front of him, there was a squad of knights waiting outside the tavern as well.
In short, they were prepared to use force if necessary. And Heinkel did not possess enough strength to overpower their sense of duty.
Above all else—

"Even if I run, it's a dead end, huh..."

It wasn't as if he was gulping down cheap alcohol out of sheer self-despair alone. Before taking a sip, he had racked his brains endlessly searching for a way out. The reason his feet had led him to the tavern after that was because, no matter how much time passed, he hadn't wanted to admit it. —That he was, at last, out of options.

"――――"

Looking down at the drink still half-full in the cup on the counter, Heinkel bit his lip.
For the two goals Heinkel had to achieve, both required a position he absolutely had to possess—a position that allowed him to intervene in the Royal Selection.
Originally, his contact with Priscilla had been accidental, and gaining that position had been sheer luck, but only after having it did he realize late that it was something he could not afford to lose. Thus, aside from the Crusch Camp involving his father and the Felt Camp involving his son, being taken in by one of the remaining two camps was the best Heinkel could do, but...

"The Emilia-sama Camp is out, and Kararagi's... Anastasia-sama's Camp as well."

Even Heinkel could understand that it would be difficult.
While he would have liked to place his hopes in Anastasia with her merchant's rationality, as he had seen in the Vollachian Empire, she was quite friendly with Emilia. She had likely heard all about the unsightly display Heinkel had put on in front of Emilia right from her lips.
Even without that, Heinkel was well aware that his reputation was the absolute worst.

Therefore, there was no chance of any Royal Selection candidate employing Heinkel.
And that was reason enough for the faint ray of light he had glimpsed to vanish, leaving him utterly crushed.

"Heinkel Astrea-dono. —What is your response?"

With the heightened tension intact, pressed for a definitive answer, Heinkel gulped down the remaining liquor in his cup and let his hand drop.
The spirit to reach for the beloved sword at his hip never arose.



En route during his transport, shoved into the dragon carriage without even having his hands bound, Heinkel felt taken aback.
He had been prepared for a far rougher journey, one where he wouldn't even be treated as human.

"...In the end, under what pretext is this happening?"

Sandwiched between the knights who still wouldn't answer no matter how much he asked, Heinkel pondered what crime he was being held accountable for. —There were plenty of possibilities that came to mind.

He had completely abandoned his duties as Vice Commander of the Guard Knights and his position as lord of the Astrea Domain, and there was more than enough suspicion of him interfering in the internal affairs of the Vollachian Empire. Most recently, there was also the fact that he had tried to cooperate with the plan hatched by Aldebaran.
Though, as for that, it had ended up collapsing before anything even started—to the point where Heinkel, who had been approached with the offer, felt like shouting, "This isn't what we agreed on!"

However, Heinkel felt no urge to blame Aldebaran for that.
Someone who set out to achieve something, only to end up failing to accomplish it. If he started placing blame for that, even drowning to death in torment a hundred times wouldn't be enough for the likes of Heinkel.

Once again, everything was back to square one.
Both dragging Reinhard down from the seat of "Sword Saint," and awakening his wife who had been sleeping for nearly twenty years.

Or perhaps the time had come.
To have even his reason to struggle stripped away, to be confronted with the command to just give up already. If that was the case, it was almost laughable.

"...Even so, this is strange."

After several days on the road to the Royal Capital, led by knights who stubbornly kept their mouths shut, Heinkel stepped off the dragon carriage and frowned upon realizing that where they had stopped was not the Royal Castle, nor the knights' guardhouse, let alone a prison.
What stood before him was an old church built in a corner of the Royal Capital—belonging to the "Divine Dragon Church," a building that, for Heinkel, held reasons that made it difficult to look at directly.

Speaking of the "Divine Dragon Church," it was the state religion of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica—a fortress of prayer where the majority of the populace, though varying in piety, offered their faith to the "Divine Dragon" whose blessings they received.
Once upon a time, Heinkel too had frequented the place, offering prayers time and time again in hopes of his sleeping wife's awakening. —However, after a certain night, he had never set foot there again. Nor did he have the right to, which was currently Heinkel's own self-perception regarding the church.
In such a house of prayer belonging to the "Divine Dragon Church," as Heinkel stepped through the entrance as if being pushed inside, waiting for him was a girl in nun attire with long golden hair and red eyes—

"Ah! I've been waiting for you, Heinkel-sama!"

The instant she saw Heinkel's face, the girl sent the chair she had been sitting on flying with a noisy clatter and came pattering over to him. Faced with the momentum of the restless girl beaming with a full smile, Heinkel instinctively took half a step back.
What he felt wasn't hostility. It was goodwill. He hadn't stepped back out of fear of a threat. It was doubt. The reason for that doubt quickly bore fruit as he looked her up and down once more.

Beautiful golden hair, and red eyes like burning jewels.
Those were characteristics that shouldn't exist.

"—I am Filore Lugunica. I am delighted to meet you like this, Heinkel-sama. I've been eagerly awaiting your arrival!"

"Fil...ore...!?"

"—? Yes, that's right, Filore. That's me... Ah, but of course! Anyone who hears that would be surprised. Things around me have been so hectic lately that it slipped my mind."

As she stuck out her tongue with a playful grin to cover her embarrassment, the shock that assailed Heinkel regarding the girl who called herself Filore was beyond words.
Because there shouldn't be two people in this world who bore that name.

"Thank you all so much for your assistance as well. Such wonderful work—surely the 'Dragon' will be pleased as well. As the scriptures say, *'The sword is swung by human hands, but life is guided by the Dragon's will. The footsteps of the chosen knight turn even ruts of blood into a silver path.'*"

Filore called out to the knights standing at the entrance of the church behind the shaken Heinkel. Hearing her clear, resonant voice, the knights seemed to exchange looks.
Naturally, from the perspective of those who had brought Heinkel all this way, leaving Filore and Heinkel alone together in this place was the last thing they wanted to do. However, paying no mind to the knights' concerns, Filore pumped a fist and declared, "No need to worry!"

"This person is of the 'Sword Saint' lineage and a wonderful individual chosen by the 'Divine Dragon'—I give him my personal guarantee! In terms of the kingdom as well, this is a truly grand encounter!"

As Filore thumped her chest and boasted, the knights' confusion did not fade.
On the other hand, Heinkel also had intense thoughts about her statement. So much so that it snapped him right back to reality from the initial shock of her introduction.

"If anything happens, please call for us immediately. We will be waiting outside."

Given their position, that was likely the absolute limit of the concession they could make.
Leaving behind those words, the knights, who kept their stern gazes fixed on Heinkel until the very end, closed the church entrance and waited outside. Once the knights disappeared behind the door, Filore crossed her arms, murmuring, "My, what a hassle."

"Given the circumstances, it's only natural everyone would be worried... but this takes priority right now, doesn't it? Once again, Heinkel Astrea-sama, I am delighted to meet..."

"—What on earth did you mean by what you said earlier?"

"Feh?"

Taking a step—closing the half-step distance he had previously backed away—he questioned her. Looked down upon at close range as a result, Filore opened her red eyes wide.
What an uncomfortable gaze. Her well-defined features bore far too much resemblance to the Lugunica royal family. —However, that couldn't possibly be.

"Filore Lugunica is the princess who vanished from the castle fifteen years ago. And that girl was found in the slums a long time ago and is currently in the Royal Selection..."

"—Ah, you mean Felt. Yes, I've heard that she is under such suspicion as well. But as for us, we've reached the conclusion that either way is fine."

"Huh...?"

"Whether it's me or her, one of us might be the princess who disappeared from the Royal Castle fifteen years ago. Coincidentally, both she and I are around fifteen years old, so I think a miraculous alignment has occurred there. But that isn't what's important."

"Th-There's no way a ridiculous story like that makes sense!"

Heinkel raised his voice at Filore, who so casually brushed off one of the kingdom's greatest incidents.
Indeed, it was ridiculous. Because of that incident, numerous things had strayed from their proper path. And that was by no means unrelated to Heinkel either.
The several events that occurred in the Royal Lugunica Castle on that night fifteen years ago—

"It ties into what you were saying earlier, too! Spouting nonsensical nonsense about me being of the 'Sword Saint' lineage or chosen by the 'Dragon'..."

"But fifteen years ago, the one who was bathed in the 'Dragon's Blood' stored in the Royal Castle and adapted to it without being crushed by its power was you, Heinkel-sama, wasn't it?"

"Wh—"

"Ah, this is supposed to be a secret, isn't it? Truth be told, I read it secretly in the church's secret archives, so I only realized later that I shouldn't spread it around."

Filore lowered her voice only now, but Heinkel was in no state to care about that.
It was shocking enough just to have that incident involving the "Dragon's Blood" fifteen years ago brought up, but the kicker was the claim that it was recorded in some secret archive of the "Divine Dragon Church."
He couldn't fathom the reason. True, there was a time when Heinkel frequented the church, but he was hardly a devout believer, nor had he ever carelessly poured out his innermost thoughts.
Above all else—

"――――"

The suspicions leveled at him, the events of that night fifteen years ago—they could never be revealed.
The responsibility for the matter was not something that stopped with Heinkel alone. It was a grave matter that could potentially extend to the entire House Astrea and the whole Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica.
It would cease to be a matter that could be resolved simply by gouging out Heinkel's own heart.

"Ha! I don't have the slightest clue what you're talking about, fake princess."

Therefore, Heinkel distorted his cheeks in a servile sneer and viciously mocked Filore.
Upon hearing this, Filore's eyes widened in surprise, gasping at Heinkel's denial. Had she not considered for a moment that he might deny it? If so, she was a foolish girl.

"Do you think if you collide head-on with utter honesty, everything will just bounce back nice and clean? If so, that's absurdly naive. The world isn't built that conveniently."

"Heinkel-sama..."

"Don't go slapping honorifics on me like some hand-me-down habit! I don't know what you were expecting from me, but too bad. Chosen by the 'Divine Dragon'? Don't make me laugh. Lineage of the 'Sword Saint'? Is that your angle? The Royal Castle and the 'Divine Dragon Church' have always kept their distance! To people like you who want everyone to revere the 'Divine Dragon' above all else, Reinhard must be a real eyesore!"

As he spewed curses in rapid succession, a murky blackness seeped into his chest.
He was merely spouting whatever popped into his head, yet unexpectedly, it felt as though it might not be wrong. The fame of the "Sword Saint" in the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica was immense, and with Reinhard—hailed as the greatest in history—as the current generation, it could be said to be reaching the realm of faith itself.
Perhaps to the "Divine Dragon Church," Reinhard truly was a thorn in their side.
The speculation that they intended to use Heinkel for the purpose of eliminating him was entirely plausible. —The very card Heinkel himself had used as leverage to negotiate with Royal Selection candidates until now took on a sickening moisture.
However, if he considered one of Heinkel's goals, perhaps even that—

"—The reason I searched for you, Heinkel-sama, is because I wanted you to become my knight."

—.
――――.
――――――――.

"...What?"

Due to his intense, teeth-grinding rumination to the point of tasting blood, he felt as though he had heard a strange hallucination.
Frowning at a mishearing that couldn't be explained otherwise, Heinkel stood bewildered, yet oblivious to his confusion, Filore raised her head and spoke.
Once more, looking straight into his blue eyes this time—

"Heinkel Astrea-sama, I would like you to become my knight. The lead knight to this Filore Lugunica, who is entering the Royal Selection late."

"Are...! A-Are you insane...!?"

"My, what a dreadful thing to say! Mind you, once you bind yourself to me in a vow of master and servant, I will expect you to mend your attitude as much as possible."

A point repeated as if to emphasize that he had not misheard. Yet, no matter how many points were piled on, a discussion whose foundation was wobbly could never be settled.
Filore had said it: that she would make Heinkel her lead knight as a Royal Selection candidate.

"Who fed you what ideas? Could it be Reinhard? Did he say something to you? Is that why..."

"Don't be ridiculous! As if I would decide something this important just by mindlessly following someone else's orders. I decided this firmly on my own!"

"Then you have even worse judgment! Didn't you hear anything from anyone else? What about the official knights out front? There must've been plenty of others you could've asked!"

"Yes, I did ask. Everyone put on stern faces and fervently insisted that I should reconsider."

"Then quietly do that! Reconsider!"

"I did reconsider! But no matter how many times I rethought it, Heinkel-sama was still the best choice."

He was struck speechless by Filore's response as she put her hands on her hips and puffed out her chest.
She was likely the type to rush headlong once she decided on something. He could tell that much from watching her behavior up to now. He understood, but what on earth were the people around her doing?

"Priscilla-jou had a stubborn side that wouldn't yield once decided as well, but in Priscilla-jou's case, there was an undeniable force behind her decisiveness and execution..."

In terms of refusing to listen to those around them, they might be evenly matched.
However, between Priscilla and Filore, there was a vast difference in the reliability—or rather, the strength as an individual—that they possessed.
Simply put, Priscilla was an adult, while Filore was a child. When a child was about to do something wrong, it was the duty of an adult to stop them.
Even if that child held a grand title like Royal Selection candidate, and even if the adult in a position to admonish her was an unqualified, foolish, miserable failure who should have died already.

"Dreamer or not, what you're looking at is a nightmare. And a bad dream at that, one you won't listen to others telling you is bad. As if I'd play along with something like that."

After declaring so, Heinkel sneered at his own foolish decision.
Having lost any candidate to cling to, drowning in alcohol because he couldn't fulfill his wish at this rate, when presented with a hand he couldn't have even prayed for, now he couldn't reach out and take it.
If it were truly that important, he should be able to reach out without choosing the means. —Then perhaps he, who had spent fifteen years and was still trying to choose which hand to take, was not truly serious.

Everything was nothing more than the remnants of an obsession, and Heinkel was empty inside—

*“—Aren't you just precious, Heinkel-san!”*

In an instant, that voice struck clearly in a corner of his mind, shaking Heinkel's very soul.

"—Louanna."

He hadn't heard that voice even once for nearly twenty years now.
Even so, if he closed his eyes and thought of her smile, far from feeling like yesterday, he could hear her voice as if she were right there in this very moment. Of course he could. Because he loved her.
Even if Heinkel were an empty, hollow shell, the heat called Louanna stuffed inside it, and everything that heat had given him, were entirely real.

Because not once, not for even a single second, had that ever faded.

"I have been appointed to the position of 'Saintess' of the 'Divine Dragon Church.' What I proclaim as a Royal Selection candidate is to eliminate every single reason that impedes salvation, starting with resolving the discord between the Royal Castle and the Church."

In front of the silent Heinkel, Filore spoke quietly, with a powerful voice.
Lifting his head, Heinkel looked at Filore. She was still gazing directly at him as before, yet the words she spun held none of the lighthearted aura from earlier.
He could tell that she was choosing her words, pouring her feelings and strength into them to ensure they reached him.

"Heinkel-sama, I've heard about your wife. You've been searching tirelessly for a way to awaken her for over ten years without giving up, haven't you? That is truly admirable."

"...Admirable, my ass. I've gotten no results. With Louanna still not awake, what's so admirable about it?"

"No, it is admirable! No one praises you, do they? But I think it's amazing! It's not something just anyone could do. Indomitable... no, surely there were times your heart broke. Even so, standing back up is what's amazing!"

"――――"

"That's why I want you to become my knight. Being chosen by the 'Dragon' is naturally a reason I, as the 'Saintess,' cannot overlook either. But you are someone who cannot give up straining all your strength for the sake of the person you hold dear. I'm sure we'll fit well together!"

Raising her voice, Filore extended her smooth, white hand. *Take this hand*, her glowing red eyes implored Heinkel.

*What a foolish girl*—no matter how much he listened to her, that impression wouldn't disappear.
The person a Royal Selection candidate chose as their lead knight became someone to whom they entrusted not only their life, but their political destiny as well. Even with a grand title like "Saintess" of the "Divine Dragon Church," Heinkel's infamy was capable of blowing even that away.
There wasn't a single shred of reason for Filore to choose Heinkel as her knight.

He should refuse. He ought to refuse. Not for anyone else's sake, but for the sake of the foolish girl right in front of him.
So that this foolish, kind girl wouldn't be mocked as an idiot or pointed at behind her back by everyone, Heinkel ought to reject this offer.

He should have rejected it, and yet—

"—Let me ask you one thing."

He was supposed to scoff and turn his back to leave, yet those words slipped from his lips.
Not rejection. Not curses. At his expression of intent to continue the conversation, Filore tilted her head.

"What might that be?"

"Word is that the 'Divine Dragon Church' has a secret healing arts forbidden from being made public. If... if that really exists..."

"—You mean the Sacrament?"

"—! It exists?!"

At Filore's words, which indicated she knew what he was talking about, Heinkel seized upon them. Filore widened her eyes at his intensity, then slightly lowered her gaze.
That shift in her expression affirmed the existence of the Sacrament, while simultaneously conveying that she had no conviction it would serve Heinkel's expectation—awakening his sleeping wife.

"—It exists. But I don't know if it will have an effect on your wife."

Sure enough, after a moment's silence, Filore shook her head.
However, he didn't sink into excessive despair. Heinkel was used to having his hopes betrayed and being pushed off a cliff. He had already proven time and again that it wouldn't kill him.

"I see. Is that so... Yeah, I figured."

He had exposed himself to enough disgrace already.
From a proper perspective, he shouldn't have even asked that question just now. Yet he did. And on top of doing so, his expectations had been cast aside.
With this—

"—So that means we have to try it out. I've been told until my ears hurt that I shouldn't use it too much, but it's to help someone, so it should be fine."

"—Huh?"

"Ah, but as I just said, I don't have conviction that it'll work. The Sacrament isn't omnipotent either, and it's affected by the state of that person's Soul, or rather... But the 'Divine Dragon Church' has various other things that haven't been made public either, so if we don't give up, a path will surely open. Yes, I'm certain of it."

Clenching the hand opposite the one she was extending into a tight fist, Filore enthusiastically made her case once again, just as she had at first.
At the substance of her passionate speech, Heinkel blinked, a raspy breath escaping him.

"――――"

Filore didn't say she could do it.
She didn't promise to leave it to her, nor that she would definitely save her. —She merely swore not to give up.

For twenty years, something that no one had understood about Heinkel, something he had been mocked as foolish for time after time, yet could never throw away—the girl before him affirmed it as if it were the most natural thing in the world.
He knew. —That no one anymore, not even the Heinkel reflected in the mirror, believed it was possible, carrying that doubt somewhere in their heart all along.

"Heinkel-sama, please guide me. Well, House Astrea is famous enough that even I have an idea where it is, but charging into a place where the master of the house isn't present feels a bit uncomfortable. Plus, my situation with Felt is a little complicated..."

As if unable to just stand around, Filore set off with brisk steps, only to pause. She turned back toward Heinkel, whom she had passed, and called out for him to follow.
When she turned around, her eyes widened in shock. —Because right before Filore's eyes, Heinkel was kneeling on the spot, bowing his head low.

"Heinkel-sama...?"

"I apologize for the repeated impoliteness I have displayed up to this point, Lady Filore."

To the astonished Filore, Heinkel consciously steadied his voice and spoke.

If he became the knight of a Royal Selection candidate, he could participate in the Royal Selection once again.
If she was a "Saintess" capable of manipulating even the hidden Sacraments of the "Divine Dragon Church," she might be able to wake Louanna.
If she was a potential princess carrying royal blood, her authority might grant access to the ancient knowledge sleeping within the Royal Castle, or perhaps even the "Dragon's Blood" itself.

If he lined up such calculations, he could invent any number of reasons to kneel.
However, the reason Heinkel knelt here was that none of those calculations came first.

*—Do I still want to act like a knight?*

"I would like to accept your offer. —Please make me your knight, Lady Filore."

"――――"

"You might come to regret choosing me, but..."

"No, I won't! Absolutely never!"

At Filore, who beamed like a flower in full bloom, Heinkel turned abashed and bit the inside of his cheek.
Remaining on his knees, he unbuckled the sword at his waist, placed it on the ground, and offered the highest knightly salute. —The posture of his spine, the angle of his face looking up at his lord, the knight's bow offering his sword—twenty years of disgrace had not erased them.

The remnant of a knight who once swore loyalty to the royal family was now, after all this time, affecting that air again.
It was ludicrous. Truly, endlessly ludicrous.

Smeared in that unfunny absurdity, Heinkel Astrea became the lead knight to Filore Lugunica, the new candidate for the Royal Selection.

—That was the day the master-servant relationship between the "Saintess" and the "Sword-Forsaker" was established.

- 「背剣者」 (*haikensha*): A title introduced at the end of the chapter in contrast to 「聖女」 (*seijo*, Saintess). It plays on 「背教者」 (*haikyousha*, apostate) and literally means "one who turns his back on the sword," rendered here as "Sword-Forsaker."
- 「めっこい」 (*mekkoi*): Regional dialect used in Louanna's recalled quote, meaning "cute/precious."
