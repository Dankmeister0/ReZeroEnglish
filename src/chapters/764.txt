Arc 10 Chapter 7: "My Friend"

"Are you okay? Think you can manage?"
"Sis, watch what your hands are doing! Careful, careful!"
"That's the rumored silver-haired half-elf, right? Just dump it right on her head!"

"Don't go making a fuss from the sidelines! And don't incite outright malice while you're at it! The scripture says as much: 'Let none touch the Dragon's scales with an impure heart. The defiled shall be struck by silver rain, and shall not pass through the golden gate until the sediment of their hearts is scoured away.'"

"……What's that supposed to mean?"

"It means that even just *thinking* about doing something bad is enough to make me angry, if you don't reflect on it! Now then, I have guests to attend to! Go play outside!"

With that, a clear, carrying voice cheerfully shooed the well-meaning children outside. Squealing amongst themselves, the children went dashing noisily out of the building.

Two boys and one girl—the girl being the foul-mouthed one among them—and, judging by how all three wore indigo robes dyed so dark they were nearly black, they were probably affiliated with the church.

Whatever the case, Subaru took it at face value: plenty of energy was a good thing.

"I'm sorry our children are so noisy. I do try to talk sense into them every day, but it doesn't seem to take very well—or maybe I'm just not taken seriously... *sigh*."

Watching the children's backs recede, the blonde, red-eyed nun—Filore—gave a slow shrug of her shoulders. Such was her lament, having been endlessly pestered by the children while preparing to welcome her guests, but in truth, the scene had looked to be about half affection and half being walked all over.

Still, rather than offer any words of comfort, Subaru and the others found their attention drawn to her precarious footing as she approached, carrying a tea set on a silver tray.

Clatter, clatter, clatter, clatter—everything on the tray was signaling imminent disaster.

Waiting at the table in the parlor they'd been shown into, Subaru and the others swallowed hard as they watched the transport unfold.

"...Um, should we help you with that?"

"Wait, please, don't touch it! Right now, if I do say so myself, there's an exquisite balance being maintained on this tray, and one wrong step will ruin everything. Easy... easy..."

"Y-you're doing sooo good...!"

Slipping between Rem, who had offered to help, and Emilia, who was cheering her on in earnest, Filore gingerly managed to crash-land the tray onto the table without tipping it over.

To Subaru and the others, who couldn't help but applaud with an "Oooh," she made a show of wiping her brow, and said,

"Ahem. Sorry to keep you waiting. And allow me to apologize for putting your hearts through such a sudden thrill. Normally I leave welcoming guests like this to someone else, but right now, I'm the only one here."

"No, it's fine. Thank you for welcoming us. If anything, it seems we're the ones who came at a time when you're short-handed."

"No, it's quite all right. The church's doors should always stand open. As the scripture says: 'To fear and revere the Dragon's power is the mark of love. Love without reverence is arrogance; reverence without love is servility; true love dwells in the nobility of standing tall before the Dragon.'"

"Um, and that means...?"

"In short, it means you should keep yourself sharp and presentable, so you're ready no matter when someone important might be watching."

Holding up an index finger, Filore answered Emilia's question thus.

She was presumably quoting a line from the Divine Dragon Church's scripture, but she had a real knack for breaking it down into something easy to understand. And for someone like Subaru, whose grasp of the faith ran shallow, having such stiff, formal language interpreted into something easy to swallow was a real help.

"Even so, was spraying spit at a bunch of kids really 'sharp and presentable'?"

"Ngh."

"And after that, the way your face went bright red the moment you spotted Betty and the rest of us was quite a sight too, I suppose."

"Ohh."

At Subaru and Beatrice's back-to-back jabs, Filore clutched her chest and groaned each time. Just as he was starting to enjoy how well she reacted, Rem suddenly appeared at Subaru's side and Emilia at Beatrice's, and each of them had an ear pinched—

"That last remark was unnecessary."
"Honestly, you shouldn't tease her like that."

"Ow, ow, ow, ow!" both of them cried.

—and so, made to pay the price for their little jab, Subaru and Beatrice were left with damage of their own. In the end, simply bringing out the tea had claimed three victims, by the count.

But enough of such nonsense—

"Allow me to properly thank you for coming all this way. I'm Filore... the nun of the Divine Dragon Church who's presently caused such an uproar at the royal castle."

"..."

At Filore's self-introduction, hand pressed to her own chest, Subaru and the others fell silent. Filore's expression clouded over before their eyes.

"S-say something, would you? Otherwise I'm going to start feeling anxious."

"No, I was just thinking—so you *are* aware you caused an uproar."

"Of course I am. ...I am. When everyone at the castle looks at you with that indescribable expression, anyone would think, 'Oh dear, did I mess up?'"

"Well, we weren't there to see it, but honestly, I know all too well that feeling of being so out of place you can't stand it."

"Really!? That hellish feeling!?"

"Oh, is that maybe about the time Subaru went and declared out of nowhere, in front of everyone, that he was my knight, and I got so embarrassed I said he wasn't?"

"Ungh, pretty much, yeah!"

With Filore leaping at the topic on one side and Emilia clasping her hands before her chest on the other, both hitting him with innocent exclamations, it was Subaru's old wound that now throbbed anew.

Or rather, it seemed that in Emilia's mind, her denial back then had simply been chalked up to embarrassment. He hadn't gone and formally interviewed her about her feelings at the time, but contrary to how cute the reason was, the damage Subaru had taken was brutal.

"...Just hearing about it makes it sound unbearable, but is that really true?"

"Unfortunately, Betty hadn't contracted with Subaru yet at that point, so I don't know the details. But I suppose it wouldn't be strange for the Subaru of that time to have gone and done something like that."

"Man, having Rem and Bea-ko both bring up stuff about that day now, out of nowhere, is really rough on me! For the record, the Rem from before she lost her memories knew the whole story, and she was actually pretty sympathetic toward me, you know?"

"Is that so? Or perhaps the me from before was simply too exasperated to comment at all?"

"Stop, now I'm starting to feel like that's exactly what happened!"

In truth, whatever Subaru's feelings had been back then, there was no denying that his approach, his methods, his phrasing, his presentation—every single choice—had been a mistake, one after another. But he'd already finished reflecting on all that, so, awkward as it was to bring up himself, he'd like to be forgiven for it by now.

But just as Subaru was getting pummeled by his own past deeds like this—

"—I understand. That feeling."

With a firm grip, a pair of hands reached across the table and wrapped tightly around Subaru's right hand. Looking up, he found Filore leaning forward from across the table, nodding at him.

To Subaru, who stared wide-eyed, she said, her red eyes gleaming with intensity,

"The cold stares of everyone around you, looking at someone who's suddenly appeared out of place."

"—!"

"Launching into speech with all that momentum, and then my own tongue, unable to turn back."

"Yes, yes. That's exactly it!"

"And after I finish saying my piece, that silence—long as eternity—waiting for someone, anyone, to say something!"

"Those few seconds stretch out endlessly...!"

"I thought it, loud and clear. ...Maybe I messed up."

"Filore...!"

Every ounce of the dread she'd tasted felt like his own—no, it truly was. Struck by that realization, Subaru found himself gripping Filore's hand right back.

There on the table, hands clasped together, Subaru and Filore nodded to one another.

Yes, Subaru and Filore were both comrades who'd let their momentum carry them into messing up at the castle—

"—except, isn't there a considerable difference between Filore-san, who actually produced results on the spot, and you, who merely postponed your chance to make up for it?"

"S-stop it... it was going so well just now..."

"That's right, that's right. As the scripture says: 'Beneath the same wing's shadow, sharpen not your claws against your neighbor. Brethren who quarrel beneath the same sky cast aside the Dragon's mercy of their own accord, and stray into wrongdoing.'"

"Um, and that means...?"

"It means that fellow sufferers of the same embarrassment should get along rather than bicker."

"I feel that! Maybe I should join the Divine Dragon Church!"

"That's a delicate matter, so don't go treating it so carelessly!"

Having been sympathized with so wholeheartedly that he nearly converted on the spot, Subaru was hauled back by a loving slap from Beatrice. If love for the object of one's devotion was the great driving force behind faith, then Subaru was indeed already a follower of the Beatrice faith, while also holding dual membership in the Emilia faith and the Rem faith.

"Wait, by that logic, I'm part of a lot of religions. I've been playing the nonreligious Japanese type this whole time, but from a god's-eye view, am I actually some kind of two-timer...?"

"...Well, I suppose that's about right, isn't it?"

"That is a shockingly, simply cold look!"

The instant he'd timidly muttered that, he was skewered dead by Rem's pale blue eyes. With Emilia and Beatrice both wearing wry smiles beside her besides, Subaru decided to drop this topic.

With that tactical retreat made, Subaru and the others turned their attention back to Filore. Honestly, she was already incredibly easy to warm up to at this point, but there was something they needed to tell her.

And that was—

"I heard you saved Crusch-san. It might seem strange for me of all people to be the one saying this, but... thank you. Really, thank you so much."

"..."

With that, Subaru bowed his head deeply, low enough that his forehead nearly struck the table. At his words of gratitude, Filore's eyes went wide, and she looked to Emilia and the others.

Even with his preface about it seeming strange, that hadn't been enough to explain away her surprise, it seemed. But at that, Emilia too bowed her head, and said,

"Let me thank you too. All of us wanted to do something, anything, for Crusch-san. And you're the one who actually managed it."

"In truth, I suppose we were still searching for something we could do. It's quite an achievement, I'd say."

"I've only heard about it secondhand, but I think it's a truly admirable thing you did."

With that, not only Emilia but Beatrice and Rem in turn offered their thanks as well, leaving Filore opening and closing her mouth wordlessly for a while. Eventually, having somehow absorbed the full shock of it, she slowly shook her head.

And then, before Subaru and the others, she wore a serene smile brimming with compassion, and said,

"—It's quite all right. I merely did what needed to be done. And if someone was saved because of it, then that alone is exactly what I hoped for."

That answer was every bit as fitting as the compassionate nun Subaru had in mind—so much so that, if only her nostrils hadn't been twitching with delight, it would have been flawless.

Of course, out of gratitude and a warrior's mercy, Subaru chose not to point that out.

"—I was taken in after being abandoned in front of the church as a baby. That was a little over fifteen years ago now."

Once the initial exchange, which had begun with introductions, settled down, Filore began recounting her own circumstances, the situation surrounding her, and the personal history tied to it.

At the opening statement about being an abandoned child, Subaru and the others couldn't help but exchange glances. —After all, this was precisely the unavoidable topic at the heart of the suspicion that "Filore = the vanished princess."

"Abandoned... so then, you don't remember your parents at all?"

"No, I don't know their faces or their names. But everyone at the church is my family. So as for feeling lonely over it... well, there were times I got all worked up when someone thoughtless teased me about it, but for the most part, no. There was always someone there for me."

"I see. But here's the thing—didn't you ever think it was strange, yourself? I mean, blonde hair, red eyes, and the name Filore—that's a pretty loaded hand, or, like, a critical hit, or..."

"This is the Divine Dragon Church, you know? It's only natural for the church to keep its distance from the royal family and the castle, so that sort of information never reached me. ...No—thinking about it now, I imagine it was deliberately kept from me. Grr..."

Trembling the fist she'd placed on the table, Filore fumed over her circumstances.

From their rather shocking first meeting and the brief exchange since, it was clear enough: Filore seemed to be quite the impulsive sort, or rather, someone at the mercy of her own emotions.

Even her direct appeal to the royal castle, the one that had forged such a strong sense of kinship with Subaru, appeared to have been the result of just such an emotional outburst.

"To begin with, I already thought the Witch Cult's recent rampages had gone beyond the pale. And then, on top of that, the incident in Priestella happened. ...I simply couldn't stand it any longer."

"And so you shook off everyone trying to stop you and stormed the castle alone, shouting."

"Could you be a little gentler in how you phrase that... let's see, how about 'stormed in, shouting with dignified grace'?"

"All right then, you stormed in, shouting with dignified grace."

"...Somehow I feel like that doesn't convey my earnest resolve quite as much as I'd hoped."

Even so, having followed her instructions to the letter, Subaru couldn't very well be held responsible for how effective the result was.

Whatever the case, it was clear that Filore had acted out of a sense of duty and strong indignation. What Subaru and the others were really interested in, though, was the root cause that had let her take such action in the first place.

And that was—

"—You said 'the Sacrament,' didn't you, Filore?"

At the word Emilia had brought up, the air in the parlor grew faintly taut.

The Sacrament—that was the "something" Filore was said to have used to heal Crusch's body, the very possibility she'd marched into the royal castle to prove: that it could overturn an Authority.

And, for whatever reason, it was also the means—one the Divine Dragon Church had kept hidden and refused to bring into the open—by which the Witch Cult's victims might be saved.

"..."

Wetting his lips with his tongue, Subaru tried to tame the faint tension rising in him.

Given that it was called a Sacrament, it was presumably a matter of considerable importance to the Divine Dragon Church—the kind of thing that, depending on circumstances, could mark you as an enemy of the church merely for knowing it existed.

If they tried to dig into it, surely even Filore would grow tight-lipped—

"—The Sacrament? Oh, yes. It's a secret art passed down only within the church since ancient times, and very, very few people can use it. The church calls those who can 'Saintesses,' and I happen to qualify, which is how I'm able to use the power."

"You said that shockingly easily—is that really okay!?"

"It's not a matter of okay or not—even if the church wanted to hide it, I've already gone and announced it in grand fashion at the castle. Trying to hide it now would be pointless... the scripture says as much: 'Defile not the earth the Dragon has purified with the poison of self-interest. To refill a spring once run dry requires a thousand years of prayer and the tears of the guilty.'"

"Um, and that means...?"

"In short, it means that if you try to hoard the benefit for yourself alone, it becomes a terrible ordeal to fix things when something goes wrong. —And I decided that now was precisely that 'something going wrong.'"

And so, defying the Divine Dragon Church's wish to keep the Sacrament's existence secret, Filore had followed the doctrine she herself believed in, and chosen to wield that power openly.

Naturally, the Divine Dragon Church, having had this sprung on them without consent, must have been thrown into a great panic, and the royal castle, now saddled with an enormous debt of gratitude, was surely just as thrown into confusion.

But whatever chaos had resulted, there was no doubt that the spark behind it had been pure goodwill.

"Right about now, they're probably discussing at the castle what to do with me... and taking part in that are the church's higher-ups, as well as the family I've had since I was small."

"But if it's about you, Filore, don't you get to join in the discussion?"

"Even though I wanted to, I got kicked out! It's about me, and yet they said things would get complicated if I were there... there's at least a little chance that wouldn't happen, isn't there? Don't you think that's awful!?"

"I suppose it's sad that they're rating the chance of that not happening so low."

"Don't rub it in, Bea-ko."

Filore, red in the face, protested that her treatment was unfair, but seeing how impulsive and hot-headed she was, one had to admit that the judgment of those involved was reasonable.

Filore claimed to be fifteen, and despite her mature appearance, on the inside she was every bit fifteen—or perhaps even more childish than that. No doubt that owed something to having been raised cut off from the secular world, sheltered like a girl kept in a box.

Honestly, what the Divine Dragon Church's intentions had been in raising her that way was beyond Subaru's understanding.

"So then, everyone involved is at the castle? That's why you were left here at the church all alone, Filore-san."

"That's about the size of it. Well, with those children from earlier around, it's not as though I feel lonely, but I've been told not to go wandering off on my own. And here I want to spread the power of the Sacrament as fast as possible—every single second counts—...!"

"That's a motivation for a Saintess I've never heard of before."

It presumably just meant she wanted to save people that badly, but even so, here was a Saintess with none of the usual Saintess feel to her.

That said, given what Garfiel had said back at the manor, and thinking of the victims waiting in Priestella for their turn, there was nothing to do but agree wholeheartedly with Filore's feelings.

If anything, Subaru found himself wanting to give Filore a push forward.

"I really get how you feel. I could ask Reinhard to fly you straight to Priestella in one go, or something like that..."

"Ngh...! What a tempting offer...! But I've been told to sit tight and wait."

"It's not like you made a promise about it, is it? And even if you had, if it means someone ends up saved, a little bit of..."

"Subaru? Are you maybe not just breaking your own promises anymore, but trying to get other people to break theirs too? That's a reeeally bad thing to do, you know...?"

"So the habitual promise-breaker has finally reached the end of the line, I suppose."

"Guh...!"

Scolded with a nuance of "I get how you feel, but," Subaru could only groan wordlessly. Alongside Filore, who let out a similar sound, the two of them ended up kneeling before the weight of a promise.

For now, all they could do was pray that whatever discussion was underway at the castle would reach a positive conclusion as soon as possible.

"—Come to think of it, you said you're a nun of the Divine Dragon Church, Filore-san."

"Rem?"

"Given that position, it's interesting that you let Emilia-san in so readily."

At Rem's sudden shift to a different topic, Subaru, who'd been offering up a prayer in the direction of the castle, knit his brow suspiciously.

But that the topic wasn't off the mark at all was clear from the reactions of Emilia, whose brows drooped, and Filore, who lowered the hands she'd been praying with alongside Subaru.

The true meaning behind what Rem had pointed out—the relationship between the Divine Dragon Church and Emilia—was this:

"The Divine Dragon Church holds sacred the power and grace of the Divine Dragon Volcanica, and the Covenant tied to it. And the Divine Dragon's greatest achievement within the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica would be the sealing of the Witch of Envy, would it not?"

"Hey, wait, does that mean..."

"The more devout a follower of the Divine Dragon Church is, the more likely they are to have complicated feelings toward Emilia-san... that's the gist of it."

Rem didn't turn to face Subaru, who had risen to his feet without thinking, and wouldn't even meet his eyes. Lips pressed tightly together, she was well aware that what she'd said was harsh, the kind of thing that could shatter the somewhat amicable atmosphere of the conversation so far.

Even so, Rem had spoken up. Rather than let an unnatural situation stay unnatural and be smoothed over unaddressed.

In truth, until Rem pointed it out, even Subaru hadn't noticed how strange it was.

Part of it was that the Divine Dragon Church had simply never been close to his life, but the bigger factor was that, while Emilia being an ultra-gorgeous girl was a fresh source of wonder to Subaru every single day, from the very first time they'd met right up until today, she had never once been an object of fear or dread.

But it mustn't be forgotten—that Emilia, by her very origins and appearance, was constantly confronted with her resemblance to the most feared Witch in this world.

"..."

And that resemblance to the Witch of Envy could hardly be something the Divine Dragon Church—which venerated the Divine Dragon, one of the Three Great Heroes who four hundred years ago had brought an end to that same Witch's tyranny—could simply ignore, nor something it could unreservedly accept.

Even more so given that Filore was the bearer of the church's secret Sacrament, a figure occupying the position of a Saintess. Whatever impression she, raised under the teachings of the Divine Dragon Church, might have formed of Emilia, there was nothing strange about it at all.

"Am I an idiot? No—I am an idiot."

This was something Subaru should have realized on his own, not something he needed Rem to point out to him.

Standing as he did in the position of Emilia's First Knight, he was woefully short on both the vigilance and the knowledge needed regarding places where she might be unfairly hurt. Was he really going to settle the account just by cursing himself as being at fault, even if it meant Emilia's expression had clouded over because of it?

He was supposed to have hurried to the royal capital partly out of regret over his own lack of knowledge, and yet—

"Rem, you went and studied up on this when I wasn't looking, huh? I'm really, really surprised."

But while Subaru was busy wallowing in regret, what Emilia herself said was neither a rebuke for her hapless First Knight, nor surprise at having been made to realize something by Rem's remark.

She simply smiled, and thanked Rem for choosing to bring up that topic.

"...It's nothing so grand as studying. It's only that, since I too am someone who was given a place to belong, I simply happened to hear about it from Onee-sama."

"But that makes me happy. Even though it means you were worried about me... isn't that strange?"

"...No, it's not so hard to understand. Being worried over can sometimes make you feel pathetic about yourself, but it can also make you feel cherished."

"Fufu, is that so? Then I suppose you were cherishing me too, Rem."

"...I believe you were the one who did that for me first, Emilia-san."

To Emilia, who looked at her straight on, Rem answered so with her eyes lowered. Even so, to Subaru's eyes, it was plain that Rem's manner was her own way of hiding embarrassment.

Emilia's honest, pure gaze had a way of peeling back all the convoluted schemes one might have been mulling over. Subaru understood Rem's feelings well enough, having had it driven home to him more than once himself.

"—Bea-ko, cheek!"
"Honestly, here we go, I suppose!"

Right after that exchange between the two of them, Subaru called out to Beatrice like that. Instantly, as though she'd been waiting for exactly this, Beatrice slapped Subaru's cheek with her small palm—with a smack, a numbing jolt shot out from that adorable little hand, and Subaru's shoulders shuddered.

"Eek!? What was that all of a sudden?"

Startled by the sudden action, Emilia gently touched Subaru's cheek. With the coolness of her pale fingertips soothing his stinging cheek, Subaru shrugged and said, "Nah,"

"Just having her fire me up a bit. Mentalism, restored via Bea-ko... let's call it Bea-ntalism, or maybe Bea-therapy. Which do you like better?"

"Sorry, I don't really get what you're saying."

To Subaru's spouting nonsense, Emilia responded in her usual way.

From that reaction alone, he could tell that Emilia neither feared the circumstances he'd been worrying about, nor felt uneasy over his own hapless failings.

"Of course, I can't keep coasting on that forever. Being worse at reading the room than an amnesiac Rem, when it comes to matters our camp needs to worry about, isn't exactly something to be proud of."

"Please do genuinely reflect on that."

"You're saying that because you believe I can do it, huh. Right, leave it to me."

"...Well, that's fine, I suppose."

With that cheer from Rem too, Subaru's flagging motivation bounced back. And with that, he turned crisply back to face Filore once more.

She was a nun of the Divine Dragon Church, and a Saintess. But precisely because of that—

"I think there's real meaning in someone like that being freed from their preconceptions. So, what do you say, Filore? How about the two of you forge a friendly relationship—you and Emilia-tan?"

"A friendly relationship? Hey, Subaru, does that mean...?"

"Yeah, in other words—you two becoming friends!"

Clenching his fist tight, Subaru declared it forcefully. At that, Emilia's violet eyes lit up, and she clasped her hands before her chest.

It went without saying, but Emilia was starved for friends. She was, after all, someone with the track record of throwing the proposal straight at Felt herself and successfully becoming her friend.

And it wasn't only Emilia, that friend-hungry monster—

"—Friends, with me? Are you serious!?"

With a loud clatter, Filore slammed her hands on the table and latched onto the idea.

Her large red eyes trembling, she looked back and forth between Subaru and Emilia.

"No, wait, I'm sorry, that must be some kind of mistake. I probably misheard. Not 'friends'—it was, say... yes, 'friend-less.' Meaning that I have no friends."

"This is bad. This Saintess is way too much of a loner."

"B-but, I might understand how you feel. When Subaru first tried to get close to me, I had no idea what he was thinking, and it made me sooo anxious."

"Sorry for making you anxious! My feelings haven't changed one bit since the start, they've only kept growing bigger with time... Ow! Rem, did you just step on my foot!?"

"No. I dropped an iron ball."

"You brought it with you, the Morningstar!?"

Hastily, Rem hid the Morningstar—the one she'd just dropped on the top of Subaru's foot—behind her back. He was left trembling in fear at having very nearly had the bones in his foot crushed, but there was something heartwarming about seeing Rem, dressed in her maid uniform, carrying around the spiked iron ball she'd diligently polished the whole time Subaru had been staying at the mansion.

"Hey, Filore."

Leaving Subaru and the others to their own devices, Emilia walked over to Filore, who was busy escaping into denial.

Then, when Emilia touched the hand Filore had set on the table, Filore's shoulders jumped with a start, and she stared at Emilia in surprise.

"It's a sooo lazy thing to do, riding along on something Subaru suggested in the first place, but—what do you say, Filore?"

"W-what, do you, mean by that? I don't quite understand... if you don't say it plainly, I might get the wrong idea. Or rather, am I not already getting the wrong idea, right this moment? Is this a dream...?"

"No, it's not a dream. And so there's no chance of you misunderstanding, I'll say it plainly. If you're willing, let's—no, let's you and me be friends!"

"—Yes, oh yes, absolutely, gladly!!"

In an instant, all her prior fragility and meekness flipped completely, and Filore's face lit up as she gripped Emilia's hand right back, the two of them nurturing this new friendship at zero distance.

Overwhelmed as he was by the sheer speed of this development, Subaru nonetheless felt, unmistakably, that the wind had shifted in a good direction, and nodded deeply.

"Glad things worked out without getting complicated. Still, that momentum... it reminds me of when Bea-ko finally lent an ear to my persuading."

"When it came to Betty, it was far more romantic and dramatic! I wasn't nearly that ravenously starved for it, I'll have you know!"

"Ow, ow, so cute, so cute."

Furious as though her indignation had nowhere else to go, Beatrice stomped repeatedly on the foot opposite the one Rem had nearly crushed, but both the weight of it and the way she sulked were altogether adorable.

Glancing over further, he saw that Rem, too, looked relieved, a hand pressed to her chest, at how the relationship between Emilia and Filore had come together.

Even though it had been a point that needed addressing, it was also a relationship that could have fallen apart entirely because of what she herself had brought up. One could well imagine what Rem must have been feeling.

"So, Rem, will you agree too? To being friends."

"I don't think it's something to take so lightly. Besides, I already have Katya-san."

"Isn't it fine to have any number of friends, though? Take me—I basically consider everyone I met on the Sword Slave Isle a friend."

"I already have Katya-san."

That was a fairly stubborn answer. Well, how one categorizes one's friends is largely up to the individual, so it would be crass to say much more about it.

At the very least, a friendly relationship had been forged between Emilia and Filore. Wasn't this a historic achievement, both for the Emilia Camp and for the Divine Dragon Church?

"I became friends with Felt-chan too, and I've promised to become friends with Anastasia-san as well—maybe I'm actually good at making friends."

Emilia, for her part, had grown confident from having made multiple friends in such a short span. At this rate, she seemed liable to start calling out to passersby in the street one after another, adding to her friend count.

That didn't seem like such a bad thing in itself, but somehow it also seemed liable to earn her a bad reputation in a different sense, so someone was going to have to put a stop to Emilia's hunger for friends, at least for now.

"Wait, hold on, Emilia—I'll go ahead and call you Emilia. We just became friends, didn't we? And yet, bringing up the names of friends other than me seems a bit much, don't you think? Not that it matters, really! It's fine, truly, but I would appreciate it if you paid a little more attention to me, seeing as I only just became your friend..."

Filore, for her part, had perhaps simply gone too long without any friends at all. Whether it was excessive expectations placed on friendship, her remarks were starting to carry a slight yandere edge.

It was probably because her hunger for friendship was tipping too heavily toward one person, so unless they increased her number of friends and spread that need around properly, it might get dangerous. If Rem couldn't be pressured into it, then it seemed Subaru or perhaps Beatrice would have to serve as the outlet instead.

"But actually, was this really all right on your end, Filore? Of course, the only thing my Emilia-tan has in common with the Witch of Envy is her outward appearance, but still."

"—? Do you have some complaint about my Emilia?"

"Yikes, no wonder this Saintess can't make friends!"

At the sight of Filore, having pulled Emilia's arm to draw her close, now nestled snugly against her chest, Subaru pressed a hand to his forehead, thinking this friendship might turn out more troublesome than expected.

At this exchange between Subaru and the others, Emilia, now essentially being hugged from behind by Filore, gave a wry smile and said,

"Come on, calm down. Sorry about that, my friend here."

"Wait, what? What am I even being one-upped on right now? Emilia-tan, what am I to you, exactly!?"

"Huh, isn't Subaru my knight?"

"Right!? Phew, that was close. My self-esteem's been restored."

The rift that might have opened between the silver-haired half-elf and the Divine Dragon Church never came to pass, but in its place, a crack had begun forming instead between Emilia's First Knight and her new friend—and it had been right on the verge of growing into something much bigger.

"—Filore, did you throw Lucian and the others out? Wasn't the deal that you'd be watched over, as proof you'd properly reflected on things?"

Just then, the parlor door was suddenly pushed open, and a young man appeared in the doorway.

A slender, handsome young man in a wide-brimmed black hat and deep purple attire—upon spotting Filore inside, along with Subaru and the others besides her, he widened his yellow eyes with an "Oh?" and said,

"So you had guests over. Sheesh, those kids, not even telling me about it—and abandoning their watch besides. I'll have to give them a proper scolding later."

"Oh, welcome back, Tiga. Look, look—this is Emilia, my friend. Isn't that wonderful? My friend... fufu, a friend, fufufu."

The young man kept the brim of his hat lowered, hiding his eyes. To him, Filore introduced Emilia, whom she was still hugging tight, joy trickling steadily out of her.

It was a face teetering on the edge of a friendship addiction—forgivable only by the narrowest margin, on account of her being a beautiful girl.

At Filore's words, the young man called Tiga raised an eyebrow and said, "Emilia?" comparing the Emilia in question, and then Subaru and the others, one against another.

"I see. I'm glad you've made a friend too. But giving me that triumphant look over it isn't very becoming of you. So, for instance... say, why don't we become friends?"

"Huh, me?"

"Yeah. I'm Tiga Rauleon. And you are...?"

"Natsuki Subaru. Nice to meet you, my friend."

"—Yeah, nice to meet you too, 'my friend.'"

Offered a hand by Tiga, who'd sauntered over at his leisure, Subaru shook it without hesitation. Tiga handled the foreign phrase tossed his way without missing a beat, even throwing in a wink, smooth as anything. And all the while, he murmured "my friend, my friend" under his breath a few times, clearly trying to make the phrase his own right away.

Meanwhile, the one unable to hide her shock at this lightning-fast forging of friendship between Subaru and Tiga was Filore, long a sufferer of friend deficiency.

Eyes wide with astonishment, she pointed back and forth between Subaru and Tiga, and said,

"A-aren't you scared? Like, what if he says no, or something?"

"Hey, what kind of upbringing did you give this girl? Fearless as anything about everything else, but a total coward when it comes to making friends—that balance is way off."

"Can't argue with you there. She's been kept locked up tight as a box, so she's starving for any point of contact with the outside world. This might turn into a long relationship, so ease her into it gradually, would you? She doesn't bite."

"That's an unnecessary aside! Of course I wouldn't bite! What do you take me for!? I'm Emilia's friend, you know!? And practically a Saintess besides!"

"Is it really all right that being a Saintess ranks lower on that list...?"

Rem's muttered comment was understandable enough—Filore's image had been through quite the rollercoaster in such a short span of time.

That said, the fact that Tiga, who seemed to be someone affiliated with the church, had returned—did that mean the discussion at the castle Filore had mentioned earlier had reached some kind of conclusion?

"That's about right. Though, that said, there's still a lot we need to discuss among ourselves. Sorry, but I'll have to ask you all to head out for today."

"Seriously, my friend? Keeping secrets between us seems awfully cold."

"Is baring absolutely everything really a requirement for friendship? I'd say a friend is more someone who can show proper consideration where it counts, and who makes the effort, mutually, to maintain the relationship. What do you think?"

"Damn, smoothly out-argued. Bea-ko, say something sore-loser-ish for me."

"Unlike you two, there are no petty secrets between Betty and Subaru, I suppose."

"That really was just sour grapes, wasn't it."

It wasn't as though anyone had seriously deliberated the definition of friendship, but Tiga's phrasing carried a fair degree of persuasive weight, making it a losing battle to keep pressing the point.

And so, riding along on Beatrice's sore-loser remark, offered up in consideration of Subaru's feelings, it seemed there was nothing to do but wrap things up and beat a quiet retreat.

"Well, we did get something out of this. I couldn't have known until we actually met her, but at least now I know Filore has no intention of throwing the Royal Selection into chaos."

"That's right, Filore's a good girl."

"Did you hear that, Tiga? That's my friend, right there."

"Yeah, yeah, I heard. Thanks for getting along so well with our Filore."

He'd worried for a moment what he'd do if she clung on forever, too attached to let go, but Filore obediently released Emilia there, and her rescue was successfully carried out.

Then, trading places with Emilia as she returned to Subaru and the others, Tiga, now standing beside Filore, removed his hat and gave an elegant bow, saying,

"Now, it'd be a shame to send off Filore's friend and my own my-friend with nothing but a thank-you and a shove out the door. Let me pass along one piece of good news to you all too."

"Good news, for us? What is it?"

"—Word is, Duke Karsten, who's been asleep, has woken up."

"—! Crusch-san has?"

To Subaru, who leaned forward instinctively as he asked back, Tiga gave a nod dripping with playful flair. Reacting exactly as the other man had intended, Subaru couldn't help but feel he'd been had.

But being had was fine by him. Crusch waking up was, without a doubt, good news.

"Emilia-tan, after this..."

"Yes, of course. Let's go pay her a visit. I want to talk with Crusch-san too."

"In that case, I'll go along with Emilia and the others too—"

"You're staying here, Filore. Wait with me for Sakura and the others to get back."

Filore, who'd been about to blithely fall in line with them, had her shoulder caught by Tiga's hand. She glared resentfully at the hand now resting on her shoulder, and said,

"...Isn't that strange, though? I mean, this is about Duke Karsten, isn't it? I have a duty to see the results of the Sacrament through to the end. Yes, a duty. What do you say?"

"If that's the case, then it wouldn't add up unless you fulfilled your other duties as the Sacrament's bearer too, would it? Of course, we'll go check on Duke Karsten's condition—later, as members of the church."

"E-Emilia..."

"Mm, don't worry, Filore. I'll be sure to visit on your behalf too."

"It's not getting through at all!? Even though we're friends!?"

She'd overestimated the power of friendship.

Either way, sorry as he felt for Filore, the urge to confirm Crusch's safety with his own eyes loomed large in Subaru too—large enough that he wanted to set off that very instant.

"Let's talk again some other time. I'm glad we got to talk today. Well, that's that, then."

"That's so halfhearted! I don't think that's good at all... mmph mmph!"

"Leave this to me, you all go on ahead!"

Restraining Filore, who was still overflowing with reluctance, Tiga—having volunteered himself for the villain's role—sent them off with words far too reliable for the occasion.

And so, with more than a little reluctance pulling at them, Subaru and the others made their escape from the church.

"Aww, you're leaving already?"
"How was Big Sis? How was she?"
"Bet she just spun her wheels, huh? Sheesh, she's hopeless without us."

And so, right as they stepped outside the church, they were also seen off by the children who'd been roughhousing with Filore earlier. It was a fairly harsh assessment, and also, quite an accurate one.

In any case—

"—Wait for me, Crusch-san. There's a mountain of things I want to talk to you about."

Unable to hold back his impatience, Subaru's pace only kept quickening.

△▼△▼△▼△

—And so, having seen off the retreating Emilia Camp, Tiga released Filore from his hold.

Freed, Filore staggered over and braced her hands on the table, her eyes lingering wistfully on the trace left behind by the now-departed Emilia and the others, before she turned to glare at Tiga.

"What's with that look? Got something to say to me?"

"Hatred..."

"Don't just go throwing out an ugly word like that! It can't be helped. Both the fact that we have things we need to discuss, and the fact that Duke Karsten woke up, are true."

"Hmph. Someone as wildly sociable as you, Tiga Rauleon, wouldn't understand, would he!"

Turning her face away with a pout, Filore then picked up her own teacup, left untouched on the table, and took a sip.

Given the whirlwind of events and the flow of conversation, she truly hadn't had a moment's chance to drink her tea until now.

Tiga watched Filore's profile, his yellow eyes narrowing, and then—

"Still, of all people, Lady Emilia, huh."

"...What is it? Don't tell me even you're going to start in on something about the silver-haired half-elf."

"That's part of it. But it's not just that. —Filore, the crest?"

Asked by Tiga, Filore looked a little put off for a moment before pulling the crest out from inside her robes. When she opened the hand that had been clutching it, the crimson Dragon Jewel let out a dazzling light.

Watching it—no, watching that light, and Filore, the one making it shine, he murmured:

And that was—

"—Things are about to get busy from here. After all, we're a year and a half behind, you know."
