Arc 3 Chapter 66: "――Fight"

※※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※

A swaying, swaying sensation, as though drifting underwater, filled his entire body.

A dark world, dark without end. There was no light source to be found, and he couldn't tell up from down, left from right. He felt nothing touching his body — and before that, he couldn't even sense the workings of his own hands, feet, eyes, ears, or any other sensory organ.

Even his consciousness was hazy, his thoughts refusing to settle. Where is this? What am I? Why has it come to this? — only such aimless, disconnected thoughts drifted up, formless.

'...loves you.'

Suddenly, someone's voice reached him from within the pitch black. To ears without eardrums to tremble, it reached him somehow not as sound but as consciousness itself. Hearing it shake his own thoughts directly, he cast his awareness about, searching for the voice's owner, nowhere to be found.

'...love you.'

It was a terribly fragile sound, one that wrung the listener's heart with a wrenching ache. And so his own heart, hearing it, was wrung until it hurt, until it ached. And yet that sensation carried with it a strangely nostalgic relief, and it stirred up, endlessly, an adoration so tender it threatened to spill over into tears.

If he had fingers, he wanted to reach for the one whose voice this was. If he had a mouth, he wanted to call the voice's owner by name. If he had arms, he wanted to hold the voice's owner close. If he had legs, he wanted to run to the voice's owner's side. If he had a body, he need never let the voice's owner go.

But here, now, none of it was permitted him, and that was unbearably sad, maddening, painful, galling — an anguish beyond enduring.

'I love you.'

The same feeling. Exactly the same. The words, taking clear shape, struck and shook his emotions, and a mouth that did not exist screamed the explosion of feeling aloud. The same feeling. No — more than that. However much of that given warmth he stacked up, it surely couldn't measure up to the feeling that scorched him for the one he loved. Even if she felt the very same thing for him, still, his was greater.

As if in competition, his adoration accelerated — and yet the one it should be delivered to simply wasn't there. The swelling emotion, the overflowing tenderness, brought unrestrained sins bubbling to the surface.

――Sloth, that could not wipe away her tears of sorrow.

――Lust, that wanted to melt together, to become one.

――Gluttony, that wanted to devour everything, to take everything.

――Greed, that wanted to love her, want her, crave her, to have everything of her.

――Wrath, at the unreasoning world that would not allow it.

――Pride, that scorned everything but her.

The space that had been dyed black was being painted over now with an overwhelming love. His heart trembled. His soul screamed. The very existence of his formless self was crying out in want. To offer up everything, to bare it all, to vanish utterly into this void — if only that were possible――.

'Really?'

Suddenly, the voice calling to him changed its color, shaking his heart anew. A question that asked for resolve. A question that asked whether he affirmed giving this present feeling shape, just as it was.

Of course, he thought. Naturally. What was there to hesitate over? And yet, even thinking it, something was wrong. Something snagged. Something stayed his heart's hand. There was confusion. There was hesitation. He was bewildered. Just what, exactly, could――.

'Can it end?'

――There's no way it can end.

For some reason, the answer came instantly to that question.

He didn't know the beginning or the end, what he'd been looking at, what he'd been seeking — none of it. Nothing at all. It was nothing but things he didn't understand, understanding was all he lacked — and yet the one thing his emotions refused to accept was staying that way, not understanding.

It can't end. There's no way it can end. It isn't over. I won't let it end.

'What do you want to do?'

I want to do something about it. I'll make sure I do something about it. Doing something about it is my――.

"...job. Because it's what I have to do."

A voice came out. Before he knew it, his feet were on the ground. He could feel his hands and feet; he could clearly understand himself again. He still didn't know what he'd left behind. But he understood that there was somewhere he had to run off to, somewhere he had to rush to.

'We still can't meet.'

"I know."

He understood nothing. But that much, he understood. Suppressing the urge to reach out his fingers, he turned his back on the voice and broke into a run.

If he stopped even once, he was certain the weight of lingering attachment would crush him too heavily to run again. So he didn't look back. He didn't even turn his mind to it. He left it behind, abandoned it, drew further and further away — but someday, without fail, he would take that hand.

'――I love you.'

The voice grew distant, and the world began to collapse, peeling away. Ahead, in the pitch-black world, a single point of white light appeared — the end of the darkness.

He ran, ran, ran, drawing nearer. And then, when he reached it――,

'Subaru-kun.'

At the very last, he felt that beloved voice call his name, and Natsuki Subaru――.

"――Bro?"

"Huh?"

Suddenly called by name, he felt his shoulder being shaken at the same time. Pushed by a rough, callused palm, Subaru's consciousness returned to reality.

――The instant he understood that, an immeasurable shudder tore through Subaru's entire body.

After only a few blinks' worth of time, Subaru understood it all. That the blank space that had just opened up in his continuous consciousness — was, once again, the extinction and rebirth of his existence that came from experiencing death, something he had already tasted more times than he could count. And then—

"No... way...!"

After all that hardship, after overcoming that much adversity, he had lost his life yet again. And as a result, Subaru had dropped every step of the process he'd gained on the way to reaching that point――,

"What's with you, bro? Your eyes just totally glazed over there, c'mon."

Turning back in a fluster, he found Ricardo's dog-like face right in front of him, eyeing him with a dubious look. A beast-smelling breath washed over his face, and Subaru, on reflex,

"It reeksh!?"

"Ow-ow-ow! What's the big idea!!"

He'd instinctively swatted away that snout with his arm, and Ricardo, suddenly on the receiving end of such rough treatment, blinked in bewilderment. At Ricardo's yelp, the surrounding gazes turned their way, wondering what was going on.

Among that storm of gazes――there was Julius. There was Ferris. Mimi and Tivey were there too, along with the rest of the subjugation force, and above all,

"Has your fatigue caught up with you, Master Subaru?"

— it was because Wilhelm stood there, saying so, showing concern for his condition.

"Ahh..."

A breath slipped out of him, as though all the tension had drained away, and Subaru let the strength ease out of his shoulders, only now noticing the fact that he'd been sitting slumped on the bare ground.

Feeling the cold of the earth against his backside, Subaru lightly clenched the fist he'd braced against the ground, confirming the gritty feel of grass and dirt within it, and—

"My save point... it updated, huh..."

A brush with certain death — he found relief in that stroke of luck amid misfortune, one too grim to laugh off as a joke.

――From what he could gather listening to those around him, it seemed Subaru had returned to a point after the battle with the White Whale had ended, in the middle of the Witch Cult countermeasures meeting he'd started after joining up with Julius.

Right around the point he'd been giving a light lecture on "Witch Cult hunting even a monkey could do," from their perspective, Subaru had apparently seemed to suddenly send his consciousness tripping off into some other world. In fact, since he really had regressed in time at that point, it wasn't exactly a wrong interpretation, but anyway.

"Pete-boy got me... that's about the size of it, right? No, wait, it's not fine at all."

Caught between the luck of being able to return to this scene, and the misfortune of having been forced into Return by Death in the first place, Subaru wore a complicated expression.

That he'd Returned by Death was nothing short of a blunder, but the fact that the point he'd returned to was his present location was, more than anything, a relief. At worst, there had been no small chance he'd have found himself waiting once again in front of that scar-faced, unpopular greengrocer.

"I mean, now that I think about it, the first time I remember returning to that old guy's place too... what the hell, what kind of connection is that? Does this world assign save-point functions to middle-aged men or something?"

There were public phones, mysterious crystals, or maybe an old typewriter, or confessing to a church priest as a side effect, and all sorts of other options, but—

"Come to think of it, having a middle-aged guy stand in for a save point is a fresh sensation... Ricardo's kind of the same type too, so is that the connection to right here, right now?"

"What's up with you, been starin' at my face somethin' fierce since a while ago. Somethin' on it or what?"

"It's nothing."

Turning his face away abruptly, Subaru let out a breath, careful not to let Ricardo see his expression just then.

Letting it out, he sent his thoughts running off in some random direction, taking a moment to settle himself, until the uncontrollable trembling of his fist was reined in to something he could just barely pass off as normal.

It wasn't as though the fear of death, or the relief of having picked his life back up after losing it, had no part in that trembling.

They had some part in it, but what truly shook Subaru to his core was a different feeling entirely — no, a raging passion.

Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti, "Sloth" — the raging fury he felt toward that hateful, worst of all adversaries, a fury that would never fade. That was the very root of what now set Subaru ablaze from within with a trembling he couldn't suppress.

"Possession — I guess that's what I have to figure it as. Ditching his original body and jumping into someone else, and then doing that to Wilhelm-san and Patrasche."

Before his very eyes, Subaru had watched the necks of the two of them — one man and one horse — be torn off, gruesomely. Right after that, with his thoughts in a haze, what exactly he'd been thinking was hazy to him too, but he knew the outcome of the action he'd taken with that same hazy head.

He'd pulled the treasured sword from Wilhelm's corpse and driven it through his own throat.

It was true he'd been shocked by the old swordsman's death, but Subaru didn't hold his own life so cheap, or treat it so carelessly, that he'd immediately follow him in death just to wish for a do-over.

It was possible that, after thinking it through, he'd chosen the same result out of a wish to start over — but even so, he'd have put more thought into it before acting, there in that moment. In other words,

"I don't want to believe it, but... did that bastard get inside me?"

Just on the face of it, it was the worst possible situation — the lowest of states, even to think about. Of all things, he'd shared a body with the single most contemptible existence in this world, and what's more, going by results alone, he'd lost the struggle for control and been made to take his own life.

For seventeen years, he'd thought he and his body had stood side by side, Vessel and Soul, but it seemed he was made to realize just how chilly a relationship that really was. Letting other souls hop aboard so readily — what a loose, fickle body he had.

"That damn bastard — I already thought he was the worst, but... now the gauge's completely pegged out. What am I even supposed to do?"

Hand to his chin, Subaru could only hold his breath at the sheer terror of Petelgeuse's Authority.

If, hypothetically, that thing could possess anyone it pleased regardless of who they were, and thereby prolong its life indefinitely, that would mean the impossibility of ever eradicating its existence.

Kill it and kill it again, and if it could just possess someone nearby and survive, then the only way to destroy it would be to wipe out every last human being.

He wanted to believe, at least, that its power wasn't so blatantly broken as that.

"In a pattern like this, how the hell do I beat it? The simplest scenario would be if there's some condition to the possession, but I can't tell what it is. A limit on the number of times... I don't know that either."

If there were conditions to the possession, even guessing at them was difficult. The first body had been a gaunt man; the next time he'd encountered it, a woman's body around twenty years old. And finally, Subaru's own body. No common thread could be found.

If it was a limit on the number of times, that was troublesome too — not only was there no sign of where the limit lay, but as things stood, the math worked out to needing to kill it at least three times before it would actually die.

"If I knew one kill wasn't enough, I could probably just bring it up with Wilhelm-san and go finish it off... but without seeing where the finish line is, killing it for good just turns into a war of attrition."

Having come this far, Subaru was made to fully grasp just how difficult it would be to finish Petelgeuse off for good.

Looking back, even that thing's fearlessness toward death had hinted at this very possibility. If, back in that former world when Puck had frozen it solid, it had survived precisely because it could hop from body to body like this, one after another, that would explain everything.

At worst — and this really would be the worst case —

"Is giving up on defeating Petelgeuse... an option too?"

If he prioritized his original objective, that was certainly one option worth choosing, without question. Galling as it was to say.

The reason Natsuki Subaru was, even now, hurrying back toward the Mathers domain with his companions in tow, was nothing else — it was, above all else, his single-minded wish to save the people of the mansion and the village, more than to annihilate the Witch Cult.

There had once been a time when that resolve had wavered, when hatred of the Witch Cult had made their annihilation his supreme goal, driving him to carry out revenge — but now, that raging passion had gone quiet.

The one who'd led Subaru to think that way was none other than the blue-haired girl. Without her devotion, Subaru would surely still be wandering, even now, through a deep darkness with no exit in sight, wearing his heart down to nothing.

And what she had said to Subaru was not, "Defeat them."

It was: Don't give up on the lives of the people who matter to you.

It was humiliating, to be sure. He couldn't suppress the urge to grind his teeth in frustration. But that, too, was one method within Subaru's power — no, one way of saving people that only Subaru could carry out.

Only Subaru, who knew the Witch Cult's attack was coming, could seize the initiative and rescue Emilia and the others. Prioritizing the annihilation of the Witch Cult was, in the end, only a matter of weighting toward eliminating the root cause — if survival was the priority, then the choice to flee was worth considering too.

More than anything, they had defeated the "White Whale," and the alliance was still ongoing. This time, they lacked the preparation to defeat "Sloth." If they solidified their preparations to defeat "Sloth" beforehand and worked out a strategy the same way they had for the White Whale, the chance to destroy it would surely come on its own. So――.

"Master Subaru."

Called by name in a quiet voice, Subaru — sunk deep in a sea of thought — lifted his head. Ahead of him, Wilhelm was watching him with a gaze as clear as a lake's still surface. His heart gave a high leap in his chest, as though he might be seen through, all the way to his very core.

"Is something troubling you?"

"Ah, no..."

"You would do well to put it into words. There's no need to carry it alone."

To Subaru, fumbling for words, Wilhelm pressed on as though to say he already understood. Noticing the exchange between the two of them, the people around them began to turn their gazes this way as well.

Feeling awkward and embarrassed under the swirl of eyes turned on him, Subaru gave a small shake of his head, and—

"It's not that. I was just kinda trying to get my thoughts in order. Doesn't do much good talking when I haven't got things straight yet, right? So..."

"And so you found yourself leaning toward the option of giving up, did you not?"

"Giving up, that's... not it."

Gently as it was put, Subaru felt as though one of his considerations had been rejected, and his lips twisted. "Giving up" was a poor way to put it. All Subaru wanted, in the end, was to avoid losing sight of his goal.

Because losing sight of it was exactly what had made him drop so many things, now that he'd been given the chance to try again, he wanted to make sure that, this time, he wouldn't let it slip through his fingers. But to that very train of thought in Subaru's mind—

"Master Subaru, it is the very height of tactlessness for me to put this into words, but allow me to say it all the same."

"O-oh. It's kinda unnerving when you get all formal like that. Yeah, alright, I'm listening."

"――Fight."

It was a low "word," one that shook the very air. And yet, receiving it, Subaru's body, his heart, his soul, all trembled without doubt.

Eyes open, Wilhelm — the Sword Devil — stared straight at Subaru. The fell aura leaking from his entire body enveloped Subaru completely, seizing him and refusing to let go.

"If you have resolved within yourself to fight, to resist, then fight with your entire body and soul. Do not give up, not for an instant, not for a second, not even for a fleeting moment — sink your teeth greedily into that single point you call victory, and do not let go. There must be no compromise. There can be none. If you can still stand, if your fingers can still move, if your fangs are not yet broken, then stand, stand, stand, stand, and fight. ――Fight."

"――――"

It closely resembled words Wilhelm had once given Subaru before. In the garden of Crusch's mansion, to a Subaru beaten down with a wooden sword, Wilhelm had, only in that single moment, let slip a glimpse of himself as the Sword Devil and spoken of the mindset needed for battle.

Back then, Wilhelm had called the Subaru who'd heard those words "a man with no intention of growing stronger," and in truth, Subaru hadn't faced him seriously either.

Now, it's different. So he thought. Because he'd been able to think: let it be different, let me change.

"You're telling me not to compromise."

"――Yes. However harsh a road that may be."

"By choosing to fight, I dragged all kinds of people into the battle against the White Whale. And now, by choosing to fight again, I'm dragging everyone here into it too. ――People are going to die."

Even as he said it, he could feel his own courage threatening to shrivel up. Other people's lives bore down upon Subaru's judgment, his thoughts, his actions. That it had always been this way, he now, for the first time, clearly recognized.

In the battle against the White Whale, Crusch's own intentions had still been riding on it too. The weight of the many lives lost in that battle had rested on both Subaru's and Crusch's shoulders.

But the battle ahead is different. This is a battle Subaru chose, Subaru wished for, Subaru dragged people into — and started all the same. The lives of every single person standing around him, taking up this fight, Subaru had to bear equally, all of them. This wasn't a matter of duty or responsibility. It was a matter of soul, and of pride.

"And even so, you're saying you'll go along with my selfishness? You're saying you'll entrust your lives to the greedy, shameless selfishness of a guy like me?"

"Whatever do you mean, 'even so,' at this point."

To Subaru's questioning of his own resolve, Wilhelm even offered a small smile. Looking around, Subaru saw the others wearing much the same expression as they watched him. That Ferris alone wore a sour face was entirely in character.

And to Subaru, unable to move, surrounded by such gazes, Wilhelm gave a nod, and—

"To move in order to protect what one wishes to protect — that, in itself, is the way of a knight. If so, then as one who shares that same aspiration, who among us could raise any objection to Master Subaru's path?"

"――――!"

At Wilhelm's words, the knights drew their swords all at once. Slower to react, the beastman mercenary corps watched this, and — with wry, sheepish smiles here and there — drew their own respective weapons in kind, raising them lightly in response.

"I understand the sorrow of having the will, yet lacking the strength. ――That is precisely why we are here. We are, now, Master Subaru's sword. The strength that will grant Master Subaru's wish."

"S-sword..."

"Let us do what must be done. For no one else's sake. For your own — not your selfishness, but your own true wish."

Subaru felt something that had been lodged in his chest drop away, all at once. It was, exactly as it seemed, the process of the dark-hued thoughts that had wrapped around him clearing away — and at the same time, what welled up in him was an almost itchy sort of embarrassment. ――And above all, an almost unbearable euphoria at how reliable they all were.

"I have to save Emilia and the others."

"Yes."

"But it's also true I have to take on the Witch Cult."

"Yes."

"Honestly, I was thinking it'd be better to run. There's a real chance this fight's gonna be way more trouble than I figured. At worst, just knowing they're coming is already a stroke of luck, so I figured running might not be such a bad option either."

The Subaru of just moments ago would surely have chosen that option. He'd have made peace with his feelings using some plausible-sounding logic, taken the second-best road, and kept fighting until he came away with results good enough. But the knights had affirmed him this far, this much. If he didn't answer that — wouldn't that make him no man at all?

"This is the only chance I've got to strike down 'Sloth' of the Witch Cult. I can't predict when he'll come next, and there's no guarantee what shape he'll even be wearing next time."

"――――"

"That thing has to be finished off here. ――Without fail."

Putting it off, getting away just for this one time — there was no way that would let him escape that thing's clutches. It was a tenacious creature. It was certain to reach out again with those hands of evil somewhere, someday, in some form Subaru wouldn't recognize. And there was no guarantee Subaru would be in a place to make it in time, then.

He had to consider that he'd never again be blessed with a chance as good as this one. And it would be arrogance to assume there'd be a next time, and false wisdom to think he should settle for second best.

"I'll save Emilia and the others. I'll beat down the Witch Cult. Having to do both at once — that's the rough part. Everyone ready? I've... only just now made up my mind."

"――Shall we go, then."

"Yeah, let's go. It's a race against time. I'll get my thoughts in order on the way."

Things he still had to think through, obstacles he still had to break past, hardships he still had to overcome, all still stood in Subaru's way. But even so — because there were people who would lend their strength to a powerless Subaru. Because there was someone who would support the heart of a weak-willed Subaru. ――He could keep his head up and look forward, without bowing it.

Let him resist "Sloth," the thing that stood athwart fate. Let him face his own sloth, that ever tried to flee toward the easy path, the weak path.

For that is what it means to fight. That is what it means to live.

To bring down Petelgeuse of "Sloth" — Natsuki Subaru's——

――The battle of Natsuki Subaru, and those with him, begins again.

  • 『――してる』/『――いしてる』/『――愛してる』: in Japanese these are progressively completing fragments of the single word "aishiteru" (愛してる, "I love you"), built up mora by mora. English can't replicate that sub-word buildup, so I adapted it as three degrees of clarity ("...loves you." → "...love you." → "I love you.") to preserve the escalating-recognition effect rather than the literal fragments.
  • 「ふらんだっす！？」: an odd, garbled exclamation Subaru blurts on reflex — plausibly a slurred/panicked version of "臭いっす" ("it stinks!"). Rendered as the tongue-slipped "It reeksh!?" to preserve the comedic garble; the exact intended sound is ambiguous.
  • 兄ちゃん (Ricardo → Subaru): rendered as "bro," a casual address rather than literal "big brother."
  • ペテ公: rendered as "Pete-boy," a mocking/familiar diminutive nickname for Petelgeuse.
