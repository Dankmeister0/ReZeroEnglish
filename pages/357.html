Arc 5 Chapter 41: "Heroic Fantasy"

"Y'all sure came back quick, huh?"

Anastasia greeted Subaru and the others as they returned to the city hall, a faintly strained smile on her face. Given that he'd left the building spitting a rousing battle-cry, Subaru — now reduced to slinking back — honestly wasn't thrilled about this particular reunion timing either. But circumstances had no consideration for either of their feelings.

"Yeah, we're back. But once we've taken care of something, we're heading right back out. There's a thing we've gotta do here at the hall."

"Come up with somethin' again, did ya? Can't say I'm gettin' a good feelin' about it."

"Well... anyway, the first shelter was a hell of a mess. That much I already told you through the dialogue mirror. I think 'Wrath's' influence is showing up in a lot of places."

"Shared emotions, right? Even Ah'm not immune to that, y'know. Once your feelin's start sinkin', they just keep sinkin' with no end in sight. ...Though seems there's some individual variation."

At Anastasia's inference, Subaru nodded in agreement. In fact, it was something he'd sensed himself along the way. The effect of Sirius's Authority seemed patchy, perhaps due to the physical distance from the man himself. Probably, whether or not one even knew of the Authority's existence had some bearing on how much its influence could be softened. Subaru's own experience — having managed to calm both Garfiel and Al — was proof enough of that.

"Dunno what set off what happened at the shelter, though. What worries me is whether this place, where our main fighting force is gathered, might end up going down the same road. I'd hate to come back and find we'd walked right into a bloodbath."

"That there's an unnecessary worry. Lucky for us, the folks gathered here are level-headed enough not to get swept up. If anythin', Ah'm more worried about someone sayin' somethin' that stirs up discord."

Al, who'd returned with Subaru, said this while glancing around the first floor of the city hall. Anastasia snorted at that, silencing the loose-lipped Al with a dose of sarcasm. Al shrugged and fell quiet after that, and Anastasia turned back to face Subaru.

"So, whatcha plannin' to do? Ya came back 'cause ya had an idea, didn't ya?"

"Yeah. ...By the way, where's Julius? Isn't he with you?"

"Ah'm not too fond of gettin' a question thrown back at me for a question. ...Julius has been actin' a bit odd too, actually. Might be 'cause Joshua still hasn't been found, but Ah get the feelin' it's not just that."

"Odd... come to think of it, was he a little off?"

It was true that after the failed attempt to retake the city hall, when Subaru had woken up and talked with Julius, there'd been an odd hesitancy about him — decisions and suggestions he wouldn't normally make, glimpses of a man unsure of himself. He was a man with a strong sense of responsibility. Subaru had assumed he was probably kicking himself over letting 'Gluttony' slip away, but maybe that wasn't all of it.

"Boss man. Takin' on everythin' yourself is a bad habit. Ah get that yer worried about Julius, but ain't there somethin' ya need ta be doin' right now?"

"Ah, yeah, you're right. Well, he'll manage on his own, no need to worry about him. More importantly, this is about something else. Anastasia. The broadcast Magic Vessel up on the top floor — is it still usable? Not broken, and there's an instruction manual or something?"

Prompted back to attention by Garfiel, Subaru asked his question. Anastasia blinked her round eyes at that.

"It ain't broken, and Ah've handled the same kind before, so that's fine, but... what business could ya have with that thing? What're ya plottin'?"

Under Anastasia's suspicious gaze, Subaru scratched his cheek. He figured he'd get pushback on this proposal, but it was the only thing he could think of — a plan to minimize the damage, one with the greatest possible effect.

"Like I said through the dialogue mirror, this town right now is showing strong effects from 'Wrath's' Authority. The shelter we visited turned... well, bloody, and the cause was irritation and stuff like that spreading unchecked. A little bit of negative feeling breeds the worst kind of disaster. That's the scary part."

"That's true, Ah think so too. The more people there are, the faster that anxiety over havin' no way to fight back accelerates. But given that the whole shelter system exists in the first place... no, even without shelters, people huddle together. Right?"

At Anastasia's question, Subaru silently nodded. The terror of Sirius's Authority was that its effect grew stronger the more people there were. And once the broadcast made the threat of the Witch Cult known, people, out of anxiety, couldn't help but huddle closer together. It was the worst possible method of toying with the very human instinct to band together against weakness. Whether the Witch Cult had aimed for that chain reaction on purpose was unclear, but the fact remained that the vicious cycle had arisen as a result. And even now, in this very moment, it was threatening people.

"And you've thought of a way to counter that?"

"It's more like 'might be possible' territory. But I think it's worth trying. Only..."

Under Anastasia's expectant gaze, Subaru's words trailed off hesitantly. Feeling Anastasia narrow her eyes, her stare seeming to pierce through him as if trying to read his thoughts, Subaru let out a deep breath.

"Once we actually start this, the same content reaches the Witch Cult's ears too. So in the sense that it provokes them, there's a chance it creates a different kind of danger."

"In exchange, it's likely to push away the potential danger that exists right now, that's what ya mean."

"Yeah, that's it. We've only just retaken the city hall, and right now, aside from Sirius's Authority, the shelters haven't been directly touched. Weighing that on the scale is a tough call, but—"

There was no telling how the Witch Cult would react to a major action on their part. There was always the danger, with the Witch Cult, of carrying a spark into a powder keg. The problem being that the powder keg in question was a place bound to explode eventually regardless of whether there was a spark or not — an utterly unmanageable situation.

"—Ah get the general idea of what Natsuki-kun's tryin' to do."

"Really?"

Anastasia, who'd been mulling it over, said this with a long sigh. Subaru raised his eyebrows in surprise at her reaction.

"Given the flow of the conversation just now, and the fact that ya checked on the broadcast Magic Vessel first thing — if Ah couldn't figure it out from that, Ah'd be a lot more worried about myself."

"W-well, yeah, fair enough. So, what do you think? You against it after all?"

He'd figured she'd oppose it, just as he'd expected beforehand. So Subaru would have to somehow talk his way past the formidable wall that was Anastasia—

"Well, can't be helped, Ah suppose."

"...Wait, you're okay with it?"

"Thinkin' it through logically, that's the best option. No matter how much Ah care about winnin', if we beat the whole Witch Cult and end up with a mountain of corpses left behind, that'd leave a real bad aftertaste."

Caught off guard by the unexpected answer, Subaru was momentarily stunned. Meanwhile, Anastasia bit her lip, seeming to struggle with how to deal with some hard-to-digest feeling inside her, but it was Garfiel who cut in.

"Yo, boss man, sis. So what exactly are we talkin' about doin' here?"

"Ya really are slow on the uptake. Even my Ricardo woulda caught on by now with all that talkin'."

Anastasia showed no mercy toward Garfiel, who clearly hadn't quite kept up. Garfiel bared his fangs irritably at her, but it was Al who clapped him on the shoulder from behind. Garfiel turned around, and Al grinned.

"What it comes down to, bro, is this: to counter this 'Wrath' Authority that's spread all through the town, the quickest way's to turn it right back on itself."

"Turn it back on itself, that's— what's that s'posed ta—"

"Sirius's ability is shared emotion. Right now, with everyone scared and anxious, tons of people's feelings are swelling up, and a small thing turns into irritation and explodes. So then—"

"If ya paint over that anxiety and fear with a different emotion... hope, then that gets shared through the same connection. That's the plan."

Al, Subaru, and finally Anastasia untangled Garfiel's confusion for him, one after another. Hearing that, Garfiel's eyes went wide and he let out an "ah" of understanding.

"So that's it! Yeah, that'd stop people from killin' each other! And if it goes well, even folks who've lost the will ta fight might get their fightin' spirit back!"

"If people get swallowed up by the mood around them, I bet even folks used to combat can't bring themselves to move. If we free them from that anxiety, I think we can counter the manpower shortage that's been a problem too."

"Ain't that somethin'! Let's do it, boss man! We got the Magic Vessel already. Start it up right now and—"

"Wait wait! This ain't as simple as all that. It's not like Ah hadn't thought about it either, y'know."

Anastasia clapped her hands, stopping Garfiel in his eagerness. At her gesture, Garfiel bared his fangs.

"Ah? Then why stop me? Ya were just agreein' it's the only way, and now yer chickenin' out at the last second?"

"Ah'm not sayin' Ah'm gettin' cold feet. Ah said Ah'd thought about it, didn't Ah. This plan's got problems beyond just simple pros and cons."

"The pros and cons are what we just talked about, right?"

"The pro is the goal of this whole plan itself. Clearin' away the anxiety of the people in this city, gettin' rid of that lingerin' worry. The con is that it means gettin' the message out to the whole city, which of course means it reaches the Witch Cult's ears too. There's no readin' at all how they'll react."

Anastasia, responding to both the snapping Garfiel and the composed Subaru, crossed her arms and continued with a "but—"

"As for this con, Ah don't think we need ta worry about it too much. To begin with, when they issued their demands, their broadcast never forbade resistance. Or rather, they probably don't care one way or another. Whether we get in their way or fight back."

"...Now that you mention it, there wasn't any penalty for us attacking the city hall either, in the sense of punishing the act itself. What happened to the people who were in the hall — that was just their own hobby."

"Hobby, huh. Good word for it. Really gets across just how twisted their tastes are."

Anastasia sighed, and Subaru, recalling the faces of the Sin Archbishops, felt his stomach turn. But they agreed on this: there was no danger in the broadcast itself. So then, what Anastasia saw as the problem was—

"Ah got no objection to doin' the broadcast itself. But the problem's somewhere else... namely, who's gonna say what on the broadcast."

"Who's gonna say what..."

Not understanding what Anastasia was getting at, Subaru furrowed his brow. Stir up hope in the people of the city through the broadcast, drive away the anxiety clinging to their hearts. To accomplish that goal, who says what, surely—

"Well, that'd be your turn, Anastasia. You're a Royal Selection candidate, you've got name recognition. If you show your fighting spirit with your own voice..."

"Hate to say it, but Ah don't think ya can expect that much effect from my words. Feels bad admittin' Ah'm not up to it, but—"

"——"

Anastasia shook her head, denying Subaru's rapid-fire suggestion. Subaru couldn't grasp the meaning of it. After all, Anastasia's standing was that of a Royal Selection candidate. Of course, the people living here in Priestella knew the publicly announced circumstances of the Royal Selection. Her name recognition ought to far surpass that of your average person.

"What do you mean, not up to it? I mean, Anastasia, you're—"

"If we're just talkin' name recognition, sure, that'd be me. If that alone could turn things around, Ah'd gladly do the broadcast. But that's not how it'll go. My name, right now, doesn't connect to any power capable of drivin' back the Witch Cult. 'Some famous person's gonna try fightin' the Witch Cult' — maybe, somehow, things'll work out, that's about the level of it."

"Even so—"

"That's not enough. What's needed is hope. Hope strong enough to move the hearts of people ruled by anxiety, all at once."

At Anastasia's declaration, Subaru couldn't find a response. Honestly, he wanted to condemn her weak-kneed reasoning and set her straight. But it was no one else — Anastasia herself who felt the frustration of how pathetic her current opinion was. Anastasia wasn't saying this thoughtlessly either. Quite the opposite. It was precisely because she'd thought it through, over and over, that she'd judged herself unsuited for this role.

"If it's just about smooth-talkin' and trickin' folks, foolin' and coverin' up, that Ah could do. Five outta ten, Ah could probably fool. But that's not hope worth clingin' to. It's just a lifeline that'll snap if the wind blows — barely enough ta shift feelin's a hair."

"Th-then... what about Crusch? She's got military renown too, and being Lugunican nobility—"

"...True enough. Crusch's words woulda had power, for sure. But that's the old Crusch we're talkin' about. The Crusch of now doesn't have that power. Especially since the Crusch of now is on the brink of death. That's a problem that comes before encouragin' anyone."

"On the brink of death? Is it really that bad!?"

Confronted with a worse situation than he'd been told, Subaru pressed in close to Anastasia. Anastasia, shorter than him, looked up at Subaru, lips pressed tight. On impulse, Subaru turned to Garfiel, but he shook his head weakly.

"Ah don't think the cat-eared sis is gonna die, no way. She's pourin' every bit of life force she's got into it... but makin' her stand in front of a Magic Vessel and talk, ol' me's against that too. She ain't in any state for it. That'd come through in her voice too."

"Damn it! Then, Julius. Julius should have the qualifications—"

"Sure, Julius is an excellent knight of the Royal Guard. One of the kingdom's finest, and someone Ah'm proud of too. But how far does Julius's own name recognition actually get us in this city? His odds of success are about the same as mine. And when it comes ta smooth talkin', Ah'm better."

The proposal to bring out Crusch was shot down, and having Julius stand up was rejected too. So then, among those remaining at the city hall who might be able to stir up hope, would it be Wilhelm or Ricardo? Ricardo didn't have that kind of pull or name recognition. And could you even bring up something like this to Wilhelm right now? How much of an advantage would the title of former Captain of the Royal Guard even carry?

"Then what should we do. Who else could—"

"Hey."

Just when he'd thought he'd finally come up with an effective countermeasure against 'Wrath,' he found himself blocked at the execution stage by the problem of finding no suitable candidate. As Subaru sank into thought, Al suddenly raised a hand in an offhand tone.

"This broadcast guy — couldn't it be you, bro?"

"—Huh?"

Said in such an utterly matter-of-fact tone, Subaru's reaction came a beat too late. He didn't even need to consider, mouth hanging open, what Al was suggesting. What kind of joke, what kind of prank, was this, and at a time like this that wasn't even remotely funny — what was he thinking?

"Look, Al. This is a serious conversation right now. And one where we don't even have time to spare. I can't deal with your goofing around too."

"Hey now, hold up. Sure, I'm the guy who got hired by the boss lady 'cause over half of what I say is unserious, but I ain't messin' around on this one."

"If you're not messing around, then how does the idea of having me do it even come up? If it's not a joke, and you tell me it's because you've lost your mind or gone off the deep end, I'm not buying that either."

"How, what — is it really that strange? Why don't ya take a look around?"

Al, still stubbornly unserious, suddenly dropped his tone and jerked his chin. Drawn by the motion, Subaru shifted his gaze to the other two — Anastasia and Garfiel. He figured the two of them, at least, must be watching this exchange with exasperation.

"...Hey, you two?"

"——"

But the two of them wore serious expressions, not exasperated or dismayed in the least. Both held earnest gazes fixed on Subaru. As if to say they, too, agreed with Al's suggestion.

"You're kidding, right? Why would it make sense for me to talk, and have that convince anyone? If Anastasia and Julius's voices don't reach, why would mine!"

"That's 'cause, like we were just talkin' about outside, bro. It's the result of everything you've built up to this point. Garf said it himself, didn't he? Boss man, boss man."

"What does that have to do with any of this!"

"It's got everything to do with it! What it took to get Garf callin' you boss man — that's what you've shown, bro. That's the trust behind it, ain't it? And here you are, apparently thinkin' your own achievements don't amount to much, so I'll say it plain. The title of the guy who took down the Witch Cult's 'Sloth' — right now, not just in this city, but in this whole world, you're the only one who's got it."

"——"

Al pressed in close to Subaru, raising his voice, bringing his face nearer. The cold forehead of his helmet knocked against Subaru's own, transmitting a hard sensation and chilled heat. From point-blank range, an unseen force of gaze pierced through, and Subaru caught his breath.

"In a city occupied by the Witch Cult, there's a man who killed a Sin Archbishop of the Witch Cult. Under the conditions we just laid out, is there anyone else who could stir up that much hope and expectation in people? If there is, it's only the Sword Saint Reinhard, and you, bro. And right now, here, it's only you."

"—!"

Struck hard on the forehead once, Subaru reeled back. Holding a hand to his forehead where they'd collided, Subaru stepped back, and Al shrugged his one-armed shoulders.

"Ah agree too. If someone's gotta do it, there's no one but Natsuki-kun."

"Anastasia..."

Still holding his forehead, Subaru turned to look back, and found Anastasia with her eyes lowered. It was an expression still dragging along the sense of her own inadequacy she'd spoken of a moment ago, and yet also one that felt the responsibility of having to entrust hope to someone. Only now, seeing that expression, did it finally strike Subaru — the existence of the great expectations riding on his back.

"Garfiel, you too? You think the same?"

"Ol' me don't know all the details about how boss man wasted that Sin whatchamacallit's 'Sloth.' But Ah'm thinkin' the same thing."

At Subaru's low-voiced question, Garfiel scratched his short-haired head.

"If someone's voice in this city's gonna be someone's hope... for me, that's the boss man's voice. If the boss man says he'll do it, says help me out — then Ah feel like Ah could do it too. That's how Ah feel about it."

"——"

It was a trust so heavy it was almost incomprehensible. Startled, breath caught in his chest, Subaru clearly felt the weight of the expectations being placed on him.

Turning his head, he looked at Anastasia. She nodded. Then he looked at Al. He too shrugged. Garfiel kept looking at Subaru, unchanged. When Subaru turned to face him, he nodded.

"——"

Receiving these responses from all three, Subaru tilted his head upward. Narrowing his eyes against the faint light of the Crystal Lamp in the first-floor lobby, he let out one long, deep breath.

—What an outrageous overestimation.

He'd felt it with Wilhelm too, with Julius, with Reinhard. They all had it so wrong about who Subaru really was. Misunderstood him.

They were all so much more admirable, worked so much harder, were so much nobler than him — and yet these same people, as though it were the most natural thing in the world, praised him, reached out a hand to him, spoke to him warmly and familiarly, and it had always tormented Subaru.

Being acknowledged like that by people he respected, people he never wanted to lose to, people he could never hope to reach — it didn't just call up joy. It was frightening. Someday, when his true self was exposed, he was bound to disappoint them. When they realized the real Subaru was pathetic, weak, hopeless — surely he'd bring sorrow to their eyes, make them regret everything they'd said and done up to now. He'd gone on thinking that, over and over. And yet—

Al, Garfiel, Anastasia — they all went on expecting it of him regardless. Feeling crushed by the weight, desperate as he always was — and yet as if being desperate alone weren't enough, they kept piling stone after stone onto Subaru's shoulders.

This was the road Natsuki Subaru walked. —The road he'd once sworn to a single girl, long ago, to be the hero belonging only to her.

Eventually, he could no longer remain a hero belonging only to her, and what Subaru had to shoulder instead was—

"—If you're gonna do it, bro. What you're gonna carry from here on out is a heroic fantasy."

Al spoke those words suddenly to Subaru, who'd fallen silent. As Subaru's gaze slowly dropped, Al spoke on, his voice somehow drained of force.

"Can't lose. Gotta win. Carry hope, shoulder expectations, show the way to the future, and fight. If ya make this choice here, that's what ya gotta become."

"...Not losing — that's always been true, hasn't it."

"The weight's different. If you lose, bro, it won't just be your loss anymore."

Subaru didn't quite understand the meaning behind Al's words. Subaru's fights had always been like that. When Subaru lost, it was never only Subaru who lost something. Everything Subaru wanted to protect was lost along with his defeat. It had always been that way. There had never been a time it wasn't. Because if he could lose without losing anything, he wouldn't even want to fight in the first place.

And yet Subaru fought, because there were things that couldn't be protected without fighting. And today, at this moment, those things were overwhelmingly large, and many.

"Huh, so it's no different from usual, then."

"——"

He let out a breath, and made up his mind. The chest that had been in such turmoil until a moment ago settled, and his field of vision grew strangely clear. Even without seeing his face, Subaru could tell Al, right in front of him, had gone still, holding his breath, dumbstruck.

"Anastasia. I'll do it. If it can be done with my voice, I'll do it."

"...Ya sure? Once ya choose to become hope..."

"What I do doesn't change. A hero, huh, not bad. Well, honestly, it's embarrassing beyond belief, and calling myself that is a bit much, but—"

At Anastasia's troubled expression, Subaru lightly rubbed the tip of his nose with a finger.

"Just doin' the hero thing — I decided that a year ago already. Otherwise I'd be too embarrassed in front of the girl watchin' me, and I'd never catch up to the back of the girl I'm watchin'."

"—That so. Then, fine. Boys sure do like to act cool."

Anastasia laughed, as if she couldn't help it, and gave Subaru's chest a light poke. He was a little surprised by that reaction.

It was because it seemed, perhaps, that this was the first time Anastasia had shown an unguarded, genuinely heartfelt expression. Tucking that feeling away too, into the chest she'd just poked, Subaru raised his head.

"Thanks, Garfiel. Al. Because of you two, I've made up my mind."

Saying that to the two behind him, he fell in behind Anastasia as she began to walk. Standing before the broadcast Magic Vessel, he turned over in his mind what he ought to say.

He still hadn't settled on what he should say, or what he ought to say. But somehow, it was strange that he felt no hesitation, no anxiety.

Was it because he could tell himself this was just like always?

—Or was it because he knew that, just like always, he had to look cool doing it?
