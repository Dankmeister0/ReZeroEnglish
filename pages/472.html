Arc 6 Chapter 67: "A Small King"

Subaru was struck speechless at the sight before him: a powerfully muscled man calling himself Rui Arneb.

It wasn't just the utterly unnatural way he'd appeared — the name he'd given was a name and a half in itself. If this were some kind of prank, it would be utterly unacceptable — and even if it wasn't, it was a phenomenon he couldn't easily come to terms with either—

"—I'd heard there was an Archbishop of Sin who could change shape, and it seems this is one of that sort. Though I do wonder about the appearance you've chosen. Did you think a look like that would rattle us?"

"Changes shape, huh... ah, you mean that yappy, chattery woman? Ouch, being lumped in with her actually kinda hurts our feelings. Besides, what she does and our 'Solar Eclipse' work on completely different principles. Hers is monkey-see-monkey-do. Ours is reproduction, see."

"Monkey-see-monkey-do, and reproduction...?"

The provisional Rui answered with a sinuous twist of that brawny frame. Subaru grimaced at the reply, and felt a fresh wave of distaste at Ram's mention of "other Archbishops of Sin." Once the being had named itself Sin and "Gluttony," the existence of other Archbishops had been a given — but just hearing about the ability of one of them was enough to stir up revulsion. Shape-changing — no doubt it was being put to some rotten use, whatever it was.

"Still, you're treating this body like some total stranger, but it actually happens to belong to a pretty famous person, y'know? Too bad the memories of the folks we ate don't stick around for our big sisters, though."

"Famous... meaning you borrow the shape of whoever you've eaten? I see. How very like you — the lowest possible method, taken to the height of bad taste. It makes Ram sick."

"Whoa, such foul language from big sister! At this rate, we'll be the ones embarrassed — and we still remember being your little sister..."

Just as the provisional Rui got that far— —it abruptly leaned forward, dodging a strike swept in from behind: a hammer of ice. Naturally, the one who'd thrown it was—

"Aw, you dodged it!"

"Fufufu, how merciless, Emilia-sama! Big brother took that one full-on, but a sneak attack like that won't land on us."

"If it won't land, I'll just keep attacking until it does! It's not that I want to ambush you — I just want to beat you!"

With that, Emilia reshaped the ice hammer in her hands, turning it into twin ice blades, and swung them down at the provisional Rui. But the provisional Rui evaded with a nimbleness unthinkable for that massive frame, and finally seized Emilia's slender wrist from above—

"Hyah!" "Kyaaaah!"

With a sharp turn of the body, Emilia was sent flying, weightless as anything. She twisted in midair to avoid landing flat on her back, planting an arm on the ground to keep from falling — but the provisional Rui's toes drove toward her face like a spear.

A direct hit would shatter the most adorable face in the world. —And then—

"Enough already — stop continuing this fight while ignoring Ram."

"Ram!"

Stamping down from above on the toes aimed at Emilia's face, sealing the enemy's motion, Ram thrust the staff in her hand at the provisional Rui's face. In an instant, a minuscule gust was born, loosed as a blade meant to slice the opponent's face to ribbons.

"—Palm of the Fist King!"

That strike of Ram's, seeming to embody maximum cruelty delivered with minimum force, was barely evaded by the provisional Rui, who snapped a palm-heel strike up from directly below. The rising palm caught the wind-blade from underneath, and an instant later the wind was swallowed by the shockwave and scattered into nothing. Having shattered the wind with the right palm, the provisional Rui immediately reached out with the now-free left hand to grab Ram in front of it. But by the time that hand arrived, Ram had already taken Emilia and withdrawn out of the enemy's reach, falling back to a position where the fight could be reset.

"Not bad!"

"Hmph!"

Clenching the left hand that had swung through empty air, the provisional Rui praised Ram's judgment and follow-through. Snorting at the praise, Ram pulled the nearby Emilia up to her feet by the hand.

"Emilia-sama, are you unhurt?"

"Yes, thank you. If Ram hadn't helped me, I would've been in for some pain. Still... did you see that just now?"

"—It seems this thing has clearly grown stronger."

To Emilia's confirmation, Ram nodded back that she'd already noticed. Listening to the exchange between the two of them from behind, Subaru shared the exact same opinion.

—It had been the briefest exchange of blows, but the provisional Rui's movements were swift and precise. It was only an amateur's judgment, but its strength probably surpassed Batenkaitos's by a wide margin with ease. That last motion, crushing Ram's strike, had been downright godlike.

"That just now was the Palm of the Fist King... the finishing move of this body and memory's original owner, the 'Fist King' Neiji Lockhart. The strongest palm around, capable of deflecting anything — the man was unrivaled on Sword Slave Island."

"...The way you go rattling on about your own strengths shows you're not used to this. It only works against you, cornering yourself. Ram won't hesitate to take advantage of that kind of thinking."

"That's totally fine by me, big sister! If you can corner us, I'd almost welcome it! I mean, come on — memories of defeat can be pretty sweet to look back on, right?"

"Who knows. Ram has only ever known victory, so she wouldn't know."

"Is that so? Sounds like you're missing out, not knowing life's bitter side."

The instant those words were spoken, the provisional Rui's form vanished from sight once more. It wasn't on the level of merely moving fast — this was a phenomenon that truly deserved to be called disappearing from the spot.

"—!?"

Ram's eyes went wide at the unexpected move. Even she couldn't pin down the enemy's position. Surely it hadn't fled—

"—Ram, look out!"

In that instant, Emilia pulled Ram close and leapt backward. A few strands of silver hair, lagging behind Emilia's evasion, were caught in the force of the blow and scattered across the passage. And the one who laughed "Hah!" at having its attack dodged was not the muscular warrior — but a narrow-faced figure baring a hairless, bald head.

With an appearance that gave off a faintly nervy impression, wrapped in something like a bright orange priest's robe, the figure licked its lips at the sight of Emilia and Ram, who had leapt clear.

"Nice, nice, I like it. Let's see if you can dodge the next one, hm?"

"Kyah—!"

In an instant, the bald figure vanished into empty air again, then reappeared from that same emptiness. It was a relentless string of surprise attacks, as if in retaliation for the blow taken from behind earlier. Leaping from blind spot to blind spot in succession, the dagger in its hand flashed toward vital points with terrifying precision. Emilia, still holding Ram, kept fending off every single one with a desperate counterattack.

"Hahaha! You can fight, you can fight, you really can! How are you seeing through the covert moves of this 'Leaper' Dorkell? It's downright mysterious!"

"Isn't it obvious! Pure guesswork!"

"Of course, it's not just that — Ram is helping too. —Emilia-sama!"

"Right!"

The provisional Rui leapt into a blind spot, launching a surprise strike. The enemy flitted about freely, wearing a vicious grin — but Ram read where it would appear next. At Ram's call, Emilia swept her long leg around in a bold arc. In that instant, the moment the bald head appeared in its new position, Emilia's heel drove into the side of its face.

"Guh...!" "Ha, yaaah——!!"

Still catching the enemy's face with her heel, Emilia spun halfway around and slammed the opponent into the ground. In that same instant, she raised her hand to grow an ice pillar up from the floor, then mercilessly drove the enemy down onto its back, straight toward the sharp, jagged tip. Had it landed unguarded, it would have been a terrifying finishing blow, piercing from back through chest. Unfortunately, though, it had no effect.

"Eh!?"

"'The skin of the "Carnivorous Beast" Beli Hainelga won't even let through a Holy Sword,' is the saying."

Shattering the ice pillar against its back, the one flashing a savage grin now was a rotund, bearded middle-aged man. He swung arms as thick as logs, easily as big around as Emilia's waist, launching an attack even while sprawled on his back — and Emilia, batting the blows aside, leapt back in a frantic scramble, again, and again.

"Ugh! Ah! Ugh! Hold on, at this rate...!"

"Emilia-sama, please let go of Ram."

"No way! I'm not going to abandon Ram like— mmph."

"That's not what I meant."

As Emilia fled the enemy while carrying her, Ram pressed a finger to Emilia's lips to silence her. To Emilia, her words cut off by force, Ram shook her head with a sigh.

"I mean it's about time you let Ram fight back too."

The instant she said that, Ram shook free of Emilia's arms and stood boldly before the giant. Seeing this, the provisional Rui trembled its rock-thick lips and swung its palm down at the girl.

"Bufuh! Get crushed!"

"Even granting every possible concession, it's 'please get crushed, Ram-sama.'"

Declaring this with haughty finality, Ram herself advanced straight into the falling palm. Without exaggeration, the giant's palm was big enough to crush Ram's whole body and then some. A head-on collision with that palm was as reckless as slamming face-first into a car — or so it looked to Subaru's eyes, at least.

But Ram—

"—————"

"Don't go thinking something this feeble could catch Ram."

Judging the exact instant the fingertips grazed past, dodging the attack with the barest minimum of motion, Ram's elbow — lips loosening into a sadistic curve — buried itself in the enemy's face. That slender, white elbow gouged into the bridge of the nose with a sharpness unimaginable from its delicate build, and the "Carnivorous Beast" reeled back mercilessly under the impact. The instant its exposed throat came into view, Ram's left hand chopped down onto it like a blade.

"—Guh."

"This nonsense has gone on long enough to make Ram properly angry. Hurry up and let Ram tear you to pieces, you contemptible cur."

Struck again and again at vital points no training could harden, the provisional Rui let out a groan of pain. Ram closed in without hesitation, this time driving her staff into the ample belly shielded by thick blubber. Then, the instant the sharp tip sank into that flab, she triggered wind magic, sending the massive body flying backward as though it weighed nothing.

"So... strong...!"

Subaru's eyes went wide at the sight of the provisional Rui scattering blood, bouncing and tumbling down the passage. Up to now he'd been so focused on watching the battle unfold that he'd nearly forgotten to breathe — but the moment Ram stepped onto the battlefield, the tide had turned in the blink of an eye. Of course, the deployment of their forces had assumed Ram could fight to some degree, but—

"Ram being able to pull this off is a happy miscalculation..."

"—it's not quite that simple. It seems we can't afford to rely on Ram alone."

"—? What do you mean by..."

...that, Subaru tilted his head to ask Beatrice in his arms. But before Beatrice could answer, the answer presented itself right in front of him first.

"—————"

With a lurch, Ram's stance broke as she tried to step forward. Subaru squinted, thinking she'd missed her footing, but that wasn't it. Sweat beaded on Ram's brow and the back of her neck, and she was visibly gasping for breath, clearly in distress. It had been the briefest moment — of course, it would be the height of foolishness to measure a life-or-death struggle by mere duration — but even so, there was a dramatic difference between how she'd been just moments before and how she was now.

"For a short span of time, Ram's sense for battle allows no rival. But..."

"A time limit...!? Damn it, Ram—"

Not knowing she had such a weakness, he'd sent Ram into a dangerous situation. Subaru's cry of alarm rang out in the same instant the provisional Rui's form shifted back into the muscular giant. Bearing down on Ram, whose feet had stopped moving, the enemy surged forward, thrusting out a palm meant to deflect anything and everything. If it landed directly, there was no telling how Ram herself would be sent flying.

"Ram——!"

"Going on and on — noisy, for a man."

To Subaru's desperate call, Ram only gave a curt, indifferent reply. Even as she did, the provisional Rui's strike closed in on her face—

"Ram knows her own weaknesses better than anyone. So—"

"That's why I'm fighting right alongside Ram!"

"—!?"

Leaping clean over the head of the kneeling Ram, silver hair streaming, Emilia landed square in front of the enemy. Against the foe with its palm braced forward, the tactic Emilia chose was disarmingly simple. She floated countless, uncountable ice weapons in the air, then unleashed the entire volume at the enemy all at once — a battle of sheer numbers.

"Ugh, aaaaaah——!!"

Against that staggering volume of ice, the provisional Rui bared bare palms and batted it away. Against the raging blizzard of ice bearing down on it, the provisional Rui defended, deflected, struck, and pushed through with an extraordinary bodily control. That skill, too, was plainly the work of a master beyond the ordinary.

In truth, the man called the "Fist King," who had fallen victim to Gluttony, must have been a genuine martial artist who had mastered skill to that degree. —And now, that power was being abused like this. Whatever strength he'd forged, whatever martial art he'd made his own — Gluttony's power stole it all sideways, wielded it as though by right, and sneered at the road he'd walked to earn it. It might even be, Subaru thought, that this power could reach further, to heights that even the original owner of those memories had never attained.

"Haaah!"

Watching Gluttony bat aside every one of Emilia's attacks and exhale sharply in what looked like satisfaction, such a thought couldn't help but cross his mind.

"Nice, the best! I mean, y'know, it's exactly when you fully reproduce the catalog specs of a memory — or go beyond them — that you really feel it was worth living in this body! Like, 'I used this borrowed Soul better than the owner ever did' — that feeling! Glad to be alive!"

"—That is the very pinnacle of poor taste in an idea."

"Maybe so! But hey, for us, who are different from ordinary people, to find joy in something different from ordinary people — isn't that just the natural flow of things? Don't you think? You do, right? You totally do, right?"

"Who knows? Is that so, Emilia-sama?"

"No, it's not like that at all!"

Shaking shards of ice off its palm, the provisional — no, Rui Arneb — grinned wickedly, and Ram, shrugging her shoulders, turned the question to Emilia. Emilia, receiving that question, hefted an ice axe and ice spear that had sprouted from the ground, one in each hand.

"Even if you're different from people in this way or that, you should still be able to think the same things, and feel joy or sadness the same way! For instance, I was alone for a long time, but I still enjoyed stacking stones by the riverbank."

"...That, in itself, feels like a rather special case, if you ask me."

"Ahh, I see. Sure enough, sure enough — as it happens, we've got a memory in here too of stacking stones by a riverbank."

"See!"

"But that wasn't because it was fun, see. It was just because there was nothing else to do. There just wasn't any other way to kill the time, that's all."

"Eh, is that... true?"

Stunned, her memory of stone-stacking denied, Emilia's expression clouded over. Seeing Emilia's reaction, Ram called her name from behind, a touch more sharply — "Emilia-sama" —

"Please don't fall into the enemy's trap. This is an Archbishop of Sin... it will twist any words at all to unravel our resolve. Don't let it deceive you."

"Right? You're right. Yes, I understand. I won't be fooled again."

"Well now, what an awful accusation. And here it's precisely because you, big sister, went and left us alone that we had no choice but to do that, see."

"—Silence!"

Against Rui, who kept piling on mocking words, Ram kicked off from the frozen passage once more. She wasn't in perfect physical condition. Knowing that, Ram still hurled herself at the enemy, because she understood that otherwise they would be trampled — that something which must never be defiled would be defiled. Ram's roar, valiant even through her fear, drove straight into Rui.

But—

"This time I'm right there with her, so if you're going to surrender, now's the time!"

"Thanks so much for the concern, but I think I'll pass!"

"Stubborn fool!!"

Even while her words stayed cute, the finishing blows Emilia drove home had nothing cute about them at all. Facing off against an enraged Ram and Emilia together, Rui switched fighting styles and forms with an almost distasteful fluidity, dragging the exchange into a rhythm of its own making.

"—————"

Watching this overwhelming battle unfold from the sidelines, there were two things that struck Subaru as astonishing. One was the coordination between Emilia and Ram — the sheer mastery and perfect timing of their teamwork.

There was the premise, from the previous loop, that Emilia had lost her Name and been forgotten, so that no one had known how to fight alongside her — but even setting that aside, the awkwardness that had existed between Emilia and Julius's teamwork was entirely absent between Emilia and Ram. Of course, part of that was that Emilia wasn't wasting effort trying to match herself to her partner — but far larger a factor was Ram's exceptional sense for battle.

"Hah——!"

"Tch! Seriously, you're a pain, big sister!"

The instant it tried to counterattack, a staff jabbed toward the bridge of its nose, and Rui clicked its tongue while wrenching back to dodge the resulting wind-blade. The movements weren't large, but Ram's support in those in-between moments kept Emilia alive, crushed Rui's advantage in the exchange, and drew the fight steadily in their favor. It lacked flash, which made it easy to overlook, but the work of constantly cornering the enemy so Emilia could land her full-powered blows cleanly, all while also managing her own positioning, was worthy of an award.

That teamwork between Emilia and Ram — outstanding coordination born chiefly of Ram's judgment — was one thing that astonished Subaru. And there was a second surprise to match it. It was—

"Ha-haa! Nice, that's nice, ain't that nice, might be nice, isn't it nice, don't you think it's nice, oh it's nice, it's just plain nice, it's gotta be nice, 'cause it's nice! Overdrink! Overeat! Guess our mood's finally starting to catch up with our feelings!"

"Ngh! It really is getting stronger and stronger...!"

"How infuriating...!"

Gritting her teeth, Emilia batted away an incoming fist with her twin blades, and Ram lashed out a wind-blade aimed at the enemy's feet. But Rui, now the "Fist King," knocked Emilia's blades aside with a palm, then became the "Leaper" to evade Ram's attack, then became the "Carnivorous Beast" to slam bodily into the icy passage. The shockwave tore through both Emilia and Ram, sending them flying backward, gasping for breath.

"—————"

Emilia and Ram's teamwork was, without question, highly refined. But astonishingly, Rui's rate of mastering the memories it had taken as its own was even faster. Likely, by switching bodies, Rui was able to render the acquired "memories" into flesh with perfect fidelity, and then wield them at will.

—"Solar Eclipse," Rui had called it, for certain.

Batenkaitos — Rui's older brother, before he'd lost his body — had also copied Emilia's cool-sounding technique, the Ice Brand Arts, back in the previous loop. But that had remained, at best, a reproduction within his own body. What Rui was doing now went beyond that in completeness.

A solar eclipse is a phenomenon in which the sun vanishes from sight, hidden in the moon's shadow — and presumably here, the true self, Rui, was the sun, while the moon covering it over was the reproduced "Name" and "memories." In other words, "Solar Eclipse" could be surmised to mean transforming one's own body into someone else drawn from "memories," and using that power completely as one's own. —The result of that was the Rui standing before him now.

And then—

"If there's a 'Solar Eclipse,' it's only natural to figure there's also a 'Lunar Eclipse' — but what kind of power would hide the moon...? No, before that — can this thing even be broken?"

The classic pattern for how copy-type abilities lose is that the user never fully masters how to handle the power, and falls short of the original as a result. But the copying ability of Gluttony, Rui included, fully compensated for that weakness by using "the opponent's own body and memories." This was a complete form of copy ability that ignored the usual playbook for beating it.

"This is bad... once Emilia and the others start getting pushed back, the whole situation changes."

Subaru clenched a fist in the front of his own clothes, gritting his teeth at his own helplessness — unable to lend a hand to the scene before him.

With Emilia and Ram's clash against Gluttony as the opening act, their comrades throughout the tower had already begun their own battles one after another. Meili, holding off the swarm of Witchbeasts surrounding the tower; Echidna, fleeing to "Taygeta" to evacuate Rem and Patrasche; and Julius, showing fierce movement on the second floor — the whole situation was unfolding with dramatic speed—

"—Julius is struggling out there. Is his opponent really Reid, then?"

"—Subaru? What in the world are you talking about? Where are you looking, and at what?"

"What do you mean, 'where am I looking,' Beatrice? Obviously..."

Held up in his arms, Beatrice blinked at catching Subaru's murmur. Frowning at her confused mutter, he started to answer — and then it hit him. Because, in truth, he himself couldn't say for certain just what he was looking at right now, or why he'd made a statement like that. Except—

"What is this? I can tell where everyone is and what they're doing. I can... see it?"

Still gripping the front of his shirt, Subaru became aware of the sensation — a mysterious feeling of perceiving something like faint, hazy points of light. It informed Subaru of the presence of his comrades scattered throughout the tower. By feel, it had already been present the moment he'd rejoined Ram and the others — and even then, he'd accepted it as something natural, as naturally as if it were a third arm he'd been born with.

But being born naturally with a third arm isn't exactly common. The instant he became conscious of that fact, Subaru understood just how unnatural a state he was in, and all at once a wave of horror surged up in him, something like an unacceptable nausea.

"—Gh."

But he ground his back teeth, endured it, and swallowed it down rather than let it out. Even if a third arm had abruptly sprouted on him right now, if he judged that it would prove useful, then he ought to sweep aside his revulsion and claim it boldly as his own. He could tell, roughly, where everyone was and what state they were in. —In a situation like this, with every last comrade scattered apart, was there any power better suited to Subaru than this? So—

"Hurry up and behave — become mine—"

This unnatural mass of power was something that had appeared in response to Natsuki Subaru's Soul. What rose naturally to mind was the Corridor of Memories, where he had faced Rui Arneb — the surge of power that had blossomed within him the instant before he was pushed out from there. He had given it a name. Yes, and its name was—

"—Cor Leonis."

Awareness and mastery — the instant he spoke the name, it spread through Natsuki Subaru's Soul. And the power crowned "Heart of the Lion" rightly bestowed its effect — no, its Authority — upon Natsuki Subaru. That effect was—

"Ngh, gh...!?"

"Subaru!"

With a lurch, Subaru's footing swayed wildly under a sudden weight in his body. The instant she noticed the change in him, Beatrice cried out something like a scream, but a headache kept him from answering her clearly. A pounding, deafening headache and ringing in his ears suddenly assailed Subaru's whole body. On top of that came a horrifying full-body fatigue and a chronic, creaking sensation in his bones — a parade of pain and suffering bore down on his entire frame, and Subaru dropped to his knees on reflex.

"Gah... ah..."

What is this, a voice of confusion echoed in his mind. This sudden, catastrophic malaise, a chorus of pain and agony too heavy to bear, his body grown leaden as if he'd swallowed poison, his breath ragged and gasping as though he'd just finished sprinting at full speed. At Subaru's dramatic change, Beatrice fussed over him in a panic, rubbing his back. Saved somewhat by the feel of that small palm, Subaru's head spun trying to grasp what had happened.

Just now, Subaru had tried to properly wield the Authority that had taken root within him, "Cor Leonis." —No, he still had the sense that it was continuing to operate even now. This very state, surely, had to be the state in which "Cor Leonis" was truly exerting its power. If so, then what did that ability actually mean? Subaru's body was heavy, his fatigue mounting — at this rate, he'd be able to do nothing—

Yes — Subaru himself would become unable to do anything. In exchange, though—

"—Ahh?"

A dry, startled voice rose out — Rui's eyes went wide as its attack cut through empty air. Still in the form of the "Fist King," Rui had just been showing off its ferocious fighting technique, cornering both Emilia and Ram and toying with them cruelly. Now that arm of Rui's was seized, its joint locked. And then—

"Haaah!!"

Driving her knee into the joint without mercy, Ram snapped Rui's right arm clean. A dull crack echoed out, and from inside the joint a broken white bone was exposed, pink muscle fiber trailing loose, agonizing splashes of blood scattering across the white, frozen passage.

"Igyaa... guheh!"

"Too slow!"

Spinning around, Ram's elbow buried itself in that face once again. In the same motion, she seized the enemy's hair from behind, slammed it into the wall, and pounded a series of right straight punches into the wide-open torso. There came the crunching sound of ribs caving in, and Rui's body folded into an L-shape — and as its face dropped down, Ram's front kick drove straight into it, sending the massive body tumbling in a violent somersault.

"...My body's light?"

Opening and closing her hand, Ram stared, as though astonished by the movement of her own body. Witnessing this astonishing sharpness and combat power firsthand, Emilia too widened her eyes at the skill, murmuring, "Amazing..." It was a display of movement so overwhelming it looked as if the ailment Beatrice had worried about in Ram had been temporarily lifted entirely. —Thinking that far, Subaru realized something.

"Could it be... that I'm taking on Ram's ailment?"

If Ram's plainly improved movement was connected to Subaru's own sudden malaise, then that was the only explanation that made sense. In fact, Subaru could see it — among the lights he sensed, the two directly ahead, presumably belonging to Emilia and Ram, something was flowing from one of them into himself. If he could sense his comrades' positions, and if he was taking on their ailments in exchange, then that meant—

"—'Cor Leonis' means 'Heart of the Lion.'"

The answer lay within Subaru's own knowledge of the Stars.

"—————"

The Authority of Greed, which Subaru had unconsciously named "Cor Leonis." Properly, this was the Latin name for Regulus, the brightest star in the constellation Leo, meaning "Heart of the Lion." But Regulus, too, before being rephrased as Cor Leonis, had a Latin meaning of its own — one that meant "a small king."

—"Heart of the Lion," and "a small king."

A lion, being itself the king of all beasts, could reasonably carry another name meaning king. But what did the accompanying word "small" signify? A small king of the lion — the king of the pride.

What sort of thing is called a king? It is—

"—The one who stands bearing everyone's feelings on their shoulders. That's what a king is. So—"

Natsuki Subaru's "Cor Leonis" lightened his comrades' burdens, as a king would. As the leader who ought to bear the weight of the pride, so that his comrades could fight at their fullest.

If that was so, then what Natsuki Subaru had to do was already decided.

"Coughing up blood the whole way — I'm good to go."

"Subaru?"

"Sorry, made you worry. My head hurts a little, I feel sick, my footing's shaky, my joints ache, and my arms and legs sting like they secretly took a hit — but I'm doing great."

"You were secretly attacked!? When, and where!?"

"Probably sometime during one of those exchanges too fast for my eyes to follow."

He couldn't properly explain this to the worried Beatrice. It was simply that the dull, throbbing pain in his limbs came from Emilia and Ram, fighting right in front of him — one of them, or maybe both, concealing wounds taken in the battle against Rui. And Subaru chose not to explain that he was quietly taking on those hidden wounds himself. Because that way was cooler.

"If this keeps up..."

If Ram, showing her full strength, could keep cornering Rui, then perhaps the drive to repel Gluttony — which had once already nearly ended in failure — might yet succeed. Defeat Rui, then rush at once to Julius, apparently locked in battle with Reid, or deal with the giant scorpion and the shadow—

"—?"

Having thought that far, Subaru once again turned his attention to the lights within his chest. Julius, apparently fighting on the second floor, somewhat above their own position; Meili, taking on the swarm of Witchbeasts out on the balcony. Echidna and the others seemed to have made it safely into "Taygeta," staying put in one place, from what he could tell. But—

"—Who is this?"

Apart from those, at a position on the same floor, Subaru noticed the presence of a light approaching from the direction opposite their own. It was the same direction from which, in the previous loop, Reid had come striding boldly. Praying it wasn't Reid, if at all possible, Subaru forced himself to look that way, straining not to acknowledge the sort of certainty already taking root within him. And then—

"Beatrice!"

"—! Minya!!"

At his shout, pointing down the depths of the passage, Beatrice instantly grasped that something was wrong and spread her palm wide. In response to her incantation, a shimmering, purple, crystal-like shape was born in that same instant. Its tip sharpened as it hovered, and in the next moment it was fired off like a bullet. It soared over the heads of Emilia, Ram, and Rui, still locked in combat in the passage, and beyond, further still. And from that position, it met head-on the attack of a presence that had been aiming for Emilia and the others' backs.

"—————"

White light shot through the passage, the sound of shattering glass rang out, and radiance scattered across the white corridor. Amid that whirl of glittering ice and shattered crystal, a red point of light could be seen flickering in the darkness. At the sight, Subaru felt his chest ache with pain — not for the first time. That threat again, being reproduced once more, he thought, gritting his teeth at the worsening of the situation.

But never, until this very moment, had facing that red point of light pained his chest so much. Before his eyes, that writhing red point of light, and the soft, faint glow Subaru felt within his own chest — the one that told him his comrades' locations — overlapped, one atop the other.

What that meant could only be one thing.

"What... is going on. You..."

"—————"

Swaying slowly, its long, sharp-stingered tail waving, a massive shadow clad in black carapace armor emerged from the darkness — the giant scorpion, making its entrance. But this appearance of the giant scorpion carried a different, weighty meaning for Subaru now. Because the presence that had appeared there was—

"—Is that you, Shaula!!"

Grasping a truth almost too hard to believe, Subaru cried out in a voice tangled with both fury and grief. Hearing that voice, the enormous scorpion, possessing no mouth with which to speak, raised its great pincers — and, as though affirming it, clacked those terrifyingly sharp claws together, high and loud enough to hate.

On and on, it clacked them.
