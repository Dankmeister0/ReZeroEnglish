Arc 6 Chapter 33: "■■■・■■■"

Left behind by the countless beating of wings, the abyss settled over the night balcony.

The artificial-looking birds spread their wings without fear, taking flight into the poor-visibility night sky. It was as if falling into that directionless sky was somehow far more comforting than remaining in this place. If that were true, Subaru would have agreed completely — that's how suffocating, how unforeseen, how nearly worst-case this situation was.

"――――"

The place was the Watchtower, a hidden balcony in the night. Julius stood blocking the entrance, his handsome features carved with bewilderment, his cheeks stiff. In his line of sight were Subaru and Echidna, who'd been exchanging a secret conversation in the night — to him, she was Anastasia, and that was how the two of them appeared.

"—What was that just now?"

In that tension-strung space, Julius repeated the words without changing a single one. The content was the same as what had spilled out of him a moment ago in stunned disbelief, but his voice had regained a little strength. That fact was a sad proof of Julius's inner fortitude.

—From where had he been listening?

"――――"

In response to Julius's question, Subaru forced his half-frozen thoughts into motion and arrived at that very problem. Where had he started listening — that was what mattered. The content of the conversation that had just been exchanged with Anastasia—Echidna—was absolutely not something that could be heard without preparation. To begin with, the secret shared between Subaru and Echidna in this attempt to conquer the Watchtower ran far too deep.

The matter touched on a whole range of factors — the 'Artificial Spirit,' the 'Witch of Greed,' the 'Authority of Gluttony.' Subaru had judged that hiding the details of the situation born from the convergence of those things from Julius — that telling him would only create confusion and cause him pain. Foremost among them was the fact that Anastasia's spirit had fallen asleep, and that what now inhabited her body was the artificial spirit Echidna.

And that—

"—Ahh, geez, Natsuki-kun, that won't do — wearing an expression that see-through."

"...Huh?"

Echidna gave the frozen Subaru's chest a light poke with her finger and smiled with soft, Anastasia-like charm. Her tone, her attitude, her gestures were a perfect trace of Anastasia, and for a moment Subaru's eyes went wide, dumbfounded, wondering what had just happened. Leaving Subaru behind, Echidna spun in place as if dancing, and—

"Sorry 'bout that, Julius. But it's not that we were tryin' to leave ya out, mind ya. I was just havin' a little important talk with Natsuki-kun about what to do after we get back from this Pleiades Watchtower, is all."

"――――"

"The reason I stepped out of the Green Room was 'cause Rem-san and the Earth Dragon pup were there, right? Not that I was worried about anyone overhearing, but somehow it just feels off havin' a secret talk with someone else around, don't it? So I changed places... and just happened to find this spot, perfect for it. That's all."

Echidna pressed her hands together in front of her chest and tilted her head. "Forgive me, yeah?" The gesture was charming, exactly the sort of thing Anastasia would do. But the way she was fudging the crucial part of the story was clumsy on a level Anastasia never would have been. It was as if the words were only a surface-level attempt to save appearances after being caught in an inconvenient moment — and in fact, maybe it wasn't even an "as if."

Echidna surely hadn't wanted this situation either, and had been caught off guard just the same. She had simply managed to act a fraction of a second sooner than Subaru. And that was—

"—You are not Anastasia-sama, are you."

"――――"

"Echidna, I would like you to tell me your circumstances. Things have come this far — you shouldn't be so poor a loser as to keep hiding things from me now."

After the barest hesitation, Julius pressed Echidna head-on. At his words, Echidna showed a flicker of a stance to object — "That's not—" — but

"Echidna."

Called again by Julius, she fell silent. As she was, she turned Anastasia's face toward Subaru, her pale blue-green eyes trembling with an emotion too hard to put into words. But Subaru couldn't think of any way to salvage things from here either.

"Julius, how much did you hear?"

"...From the part about Anastasia-sama's body."

Julius answered Subaru's question in a suppressed voice. That part alone was more than enough to justify getting emotional and falling apart. And yet Julius managed to keep at least the surface appearance of composure — that much had to be credited to him. Or perhaps he'd already leapt past that boundary, to a place beyond mere emotion.

"—Ever since the battle against the Witch Cult in Priestella, Ana's spirit has continued sleeping deep within her body. Because of that, it isn't Ana moving her body right now. I've been playing the part of Ana, all the way up to today."

Echidna too must have decided that, having come this far, there was no more fooling him. She began to explain flatly, without preamble, in a tone that simply laid out the facts.

In the midst of the struggle against the Witch Cult that had taken place in Priestella, Echidna had stood in for Anastasia in the confrontation with the Witch Cult — and afterward, Anastasia's spirit had not woken. That fact had been hidden from Julius, Ricardo, and the rest of the Iron Fang, and they had set their sights on the Pleiades Watchtower in search of a way to reverse it. —And that Subaru alone had shared those facts with Echidna.

"Why was Subaru alone given that information?"

"He was unaffected by any Sin Archbishop's Authority, and was the person most outside the confusion of the situation. Besides, he's a Spiritual Arts user under contract with Beatrice, who shares the same roots as me, an artificial spirit. Though, it's not as if I intended to confide in him from the very start. It's just..."

"...Just?"

"Just... he saw through the fact that I was playing the part of Ana, so I had no choice but to tell him."

At the explanation of how Subaru alone had come to know that Echidna dwelt in Anastasia's body, a strong tremor of agitation ran through Julius's eyes. The very fact that Echidna had faltered over her words was because she'd anticipated that agitation. Of course. That Subaru had been able to notice Echidna's act meant—

"That something even an outsider with little connection to her should have been able to notice, the man who calls himself the Knight of One failed to notice at all..."

"Wait, idiot! There's no need to put it like that!"

"――――"

"The situation... the situation was just bad, okay! After all that happened, you were pushed to your limit in your own way! And it's not just you — Ricardo, Mimi, all of them too, right? The reason I noticed was... I dunno, it was just a fluke, that's all!"

Subaru bit back against Julius's self-deprecating remark, trying somehow to stop him from wounding himself with his own words. But he couldn't find words fitting enough — words to comfort Julius, who was mocking himself for failing to fulfill his duty as the Knight of One.

But in truth, what could Julius have done? Was he really in a position to be blamed?

Betrayed—or rather, undermined—by the very liege he'd sworn loyalty to, by the companions who'd vowed to support that liege together with him, by the comrades-in-arms with whom he'd spent so long as a knight, by so much else that he had built up living as a knight all this time now crumbling like a sandcastle — how could anyone still tell him to stand? To be resolute. To be graceful. To be every bit the Knight of One — how could anyone say that? If being a knight was a way of life that didn't even permit him the humanity to be hurt, then being a knight was, for Julius Juukulius, nothing less than a curse.

"That... turning chance into certainty, always — that is the duty of the Knight of One."

"—! What do you mean, Knight of One... if that's what it takes, then a troublesome title like that—"

"Please don't tell me to throw it away. I... right now, I am even afraid of letting go of even one single thing that is mine."

Subaru's momentum-driven attempt at comfort was easily deflected before the chivalry Julius upheld. His throat closed with tangled emotion, and to a Subaru who could say nothing, Julius shook his head.

"Let's return to the matter at hand. —Echidna, what is your objective?"

"...To return this body to Ana. The reason I guided you all to this Pleiades Watchtower was because I prioritized that over the damage done by 'Gluttony' or 'Lust.'"

"In other words, the current state of affairs is not what you desired either. And no method to restore Anastasia-sama has yet been found. ...Suppose, hypothetically, I were to cut you down."

Julius set a hand to the knight's sword at his hip, narrowed his eyes, and posed the dangerous question. In response, Echidna lowered her eyes, gently touched her own chest, and—

"That I'm an evil spirit, making up excuse after excuse to try to hijack Ana's body... I have no evidence to disprove that suspicion. So even if you decided my words were a lie and tried to erase me, I couldn't stop you."

Though, she cut herself off there, and after a beat, continued.

"Probably, in that case, it would be a stroke of luck if what's left behind is just Ana's empty shell, unconscious... in the worst case, it could interfere with what keeps her alive, and she might lose her life."

Echidna offered her thoughts on Julius's hypothesis, the notion of cutting her down. Having said as much, she raised both hands lightly and

"Of course, there's also the chance this is all just desperate nonsense I'm spouting out of fear for my life. I myself can't declare with certainty that my death isn't a solution. If Ana could go on living because I die, there's a part of me that thinks that would be fine too. Though I don't want to die."

"Why can you go that far, for Anastasia-sama's sake?"

"Ana and I have an incomplete relationship. So it might not be right to measure it against how a typical spirit and Spiritual Arts user are supposed to be, but..."

She paused there once, and looked back and forth between Julius and Subaru. As if envying the two of them who, in their different ways, had each held a proper contractual relationship with a spirit, as Spiritual Arts users.

"I love Ana. I've been by her side ever since she was still a child. So I don't want to abandon her, and I want her to be happy. —That's my reason."

"――――"

"Julius, the reason I didn't reveal the truth to you was that I didn't want to invite needless confusion. If possible, Ana intended to keep my existence hidden entirely, and in fact, up until that incident in Priestella, she managed to keep me hidden. Thanks to how shrewd that girl is."

Even in Priestella, when Anastasia had revealed Echidna, whom she'd kept hidden all along, Julius must have been considerably shocked. How great a storm had raged within his chest at that moment, Subaru — who had prioritized rescuing Emilia — didn't know. Still, to have had his liege's secrets — and moreover, several secrets touching on the very core of who Anastasia was — revealed to him one after another over so short a span, at a timing they were never meant to be revealed, how deeply unwelcome must that have been for Julius?

"...I understand the relationship between you and Anastasia-sama. It's difficult to believe everything without reservation. But I have no choice but to believe. At the very least, doing anything to you right now would be rash."

"I see. I'm glad you're able to make such a rational judgment, Julius. Ana, too, would surely be glad you did."

"――――"

Julius released his hand from the hilt of his knight's sword, and without responding to Echidna's words, kept his silence. But that silence must have been far from acceptance, leaving behind a bitter, mortified feeling. Yet Julius chased that regret away with nothing more than a blink, and—

"I want to confirm something. If you continue manifesting at the cost of Anastasia-sama's Od... then naturally, the more strain you put on it, the greater the burden on Anastasia-sama's body. That much must be certain."

"That's right. Your understanding is correct. Eat well, sleep well, exercise moderately... it's a method that sounds like general health advice, but it happens to be just right for keeping Od consumption down."

"I see. Then... why did you do something so reckless on the second floor?"

Julius cut in, almost as an ambush, at Echidna, who had just barely composed herself and slipped something like a light joke into her tone. The content was what had happened on the second floor — and the recklessness he pointed to was, naturally—

"While I was fighting Reid Astrea, the second floor's examiner, Anastasia-sama... you, who was entrusted with Anastasia-sama's body, used magic and stepped in to support me."

This was during Julius and Reid's first clash. When Julius, faced with Reid's overwhelming swordsmanship, was beaten down with no way to fight back, Echidna had marshaled a vast amount of mana and unleashed magic at Reid. Far from being a decisive blow, the strain it placed on Anastasia's body instead reached its limit, resulting in her collapsing unconscious — but the problem wasn't the result. Why had she done it. That was the one point at issue.

"That incident must have placed no small burden on Anastasia-sama's body. Of everything we've discussed so far, that act alone contradicts your claims. Why is that?"

"That's..."

The fact Julius had pointed out was something that had bothered Subaru as well. In that moment, Echidna had acted with a desperate expression for the sake of the being-beaten Julius. It hadn't seemed like there was any lie in it, nor any calculation. What had been there was surely pure concern. That Echidna would direct such concern at Julius made sense for someone who had spent so long alongside Anastasia — but was that really all there was to it?

But at Subaru's doubt, and at Julius's words, Echidna said, "I'm sorry," bowing deeply from the waist right there, and—

"That, I feel, was a mistake on my part too. It's embarrassing to admit given how amateur it was, but... it was a judgment made from a strategic standpoint."

"A strategic judgment?"

"At that point, I couldn't tell whether the second floor's examiner intended to kill or not. If things went badly, we stood to lose a fighting force like Julius. I wanted to avoid that. Naturally, for Ana's sake too. And Reid Astrea turning his back toward us... that looked like an opportunity, to my eyes. Far from working out, though — I only ended up causing trouble."

Adding one more "I'm sorry" at the end, Echidna slowly straightened back up. There was no contradiction in the explanation. If she said it had been an amateur judgment, a careless action, Subaru had no grounds to deny it — setting aside anything emotional.

Was that the sort of story one could simply accept? But before Subaru could press further on the point, closing in on it—

"—Understood. I ask that you refrain from rash acts going forward. For no one else's sake but Anastasia-sama's."

"Understood."

"Wha—!?"

Julius, giving the appearance of having accepted it — and Echidna, nodding back in response. Subaru's eyes went wide at the exchange between the two of them, and he kicked at the floor — you've got to be kidding.

"How is it that story convinces you..."

"Subaru, I am convinced. Echidna has also promised to refrain from rashness from now on. What more is there to say? —This is a matter between Anastasia-sama and Echidna, and also a matter between Anastasia-sama and myself."

"――――"

"I'm sorry that some strange twist of fate seems to have dragged you into this. But this is, at the end of the day, a matter for us in Anastasia-sama's camp. It's nothing that should trouble your heart."

At Julius's words, trying to push him away from the problem, Subaru gritted his back teeth. Nothing that should trouble his heart — as if Julius could decide that on his own.

"Whatever I feel and however I take it, that's my business!"

"And so you take it upon yourself, and try to keep me from having to bear my own problem? ...Just as you kept silent about Anastasia-sama and Echidna."

"—!"

"I'm sorry. That was too much to say. ...But it's the truth."

In a voice that seemed suppressed, Julius, his gaze averted, said it flatly. At that voice, at that stubborn bearing, Subaru finally realized.

Julius wasn't keeping his composure at all. Far from the storm raging within him, he couldn't even manage to keep up appearances on the surface.

Made to lose sight of his own self, shown by the results that even his loyalty to his liege — the one thing he'd thought remained to him — had been a sham, and having the promise made out of consideration for him broken as well. And yet, still being unable to become emotional — that was the very shape of the man named Julius.

"I have no intention of arguing. For Anastasia-sama's sake as well, we need to urgently find a way to bring this situation under control. Echidna, I would like you to cooperate in earnest as well."

"...You're right. Now that I couldn't keep it hidden from you, there's no longer any reason for me to keep playing the part of Ana. Of course, that's assuming you can tolerate me speaking in Ana's form."

"That's fine. Seeing that form will only more strongly remind me that I must bring Anastasia-sama back."

Echidna's face turned sorrowful at that resolve — the intent to wound himself so fiercely. But Julius, looking up at the sky, didn't see that expression. It seemed he'd only just noticed, for the first time, that the night sky seen from the balcony was free of Miasma, and he softly narrowed his eyes at the glittering light of the Stars.

"There's no longer any reason to linger here. Let's go back inside. As for Anastasia-sama's body and Echidna, tomorrow... I must speak with Emilia-sama and the others about it properly."

"Yes, understood. I'll ready myself for it too."

So saying, Julius gently took the hand of Echidna as she started walking. It was surely a gesture identical, down to the last detail, to what he would do for Anastasia. No matter who resided within, his devotion to Anastasia didn't change. —Even if she, asleep deep within that body, had forgotten Julius entirely.

"Julius!"

Feeling something painfully poignant at that sight, Subaru had cried out on reflex. Left behind by memory, and yet with the other person still remaining inside himself alone, struggling desperately with only that feeling to rely on — that way of being, Subaru understood all too painfully well. Even if you're forgotten, you cannot forget. Sometimes that feeling alone is what drives you forward.

"――――"

Julius, having stopped in his tracks, didn't turn back, Echidna still in tow. His back was held unnaturally straight — straight, no matter how close to breaking his heart might be — and for some reason that infuriated Subaru, and

"Don't you have something you want to say to me?"

He'd kept silent about Echidna, about Anastasia's body. Even tonight, he'd promised to trade off the time spent in the Green Room, and yet, going back on that promise, Subaru had ended up here on the balcony exchanging a secret conversation with Echidna.

He could make excuses. There were reasons. He hadn't done it out of malice. Even so, whether or not there was malice, whether or not there was reason, whether or not there was excuse — none of that set his heart free.

So it would be better if Julius just spat out abuse at the top of his voice. Better if he hurled curses, threw his anger at him. Whether that was for the sake of Subaru's own guilt, or a thought genuinely meant for Julius's sake, he didn't know. And surely, Julius wouldn't do that. Raising his voice, spitting out grievances—

"—There is."

"――――"

"I understand. I understand what you were thinking, why you hid the truth from me. There's no way there was malice in it. What was there was only consideration, only thoughtfulness. I agree with your concerns, too. Even if our positions were reversed, I too would surely have kept silent toward you."

"――――"

"—But, even so."

He looked up at the sky. As if wrung out, his voice came.

"I didn't want either Anastasia-sama or you to think me unfit to be a knight."

Returning inside the tower required no special procedure. Just as when he'd gone out onto the balcony, he crawled through the hidden passage lower than his waist, and slowly made his way back inside the tower. Crawling along the floor on his knees, he found himself idly wondering whether Julius, who'd come out through here earlier, had crawled along on all fours the same way.

Unfortunately, Subaru didn't remember whether Julius, who'd gone back first, had been crawling or not. That was because he'd been knocked flat by Julius's words just before, left a little dumbstruck.

"――――"

Honestly, he'd thought nothing would be said to him. —No, that wasn't quite it. Subaru had probably just assumed, on his own, that Julius wouldn't hurl abuse at a Subaru wracked with guilt, wouldn't do him the favor of making things easy. That Julius's own integrity wouldn't allow it, and that neither anger nor grievance would be thrown at him — that the scene would simply be swallowed up in silence.

But it hadn't turned out that way. The words Julius had left behind at the end had become a thorn, driving straight into his heart.

He'd thought that if he were given grievances, it would make things easier. Not that he'd wanted to be made comfortable, but he'd thought it would be far better than being told nothing at all. But reality wasn't like that. The thorn Julius had left behind insisted on its pain, and from the wound it remained lodged in, blood kept flowing. Deep enough that he hesitated even to pull the thorn out.

"I can't... go back to the Green Room, huh."

Julius, taking Echidna with him, had surely escorted her all the way to the Green Room. And Subaru couldn't imagine him, afterward, coming all the way back down to the Dragon Carriage left in the lower level in search of a place to sleep. Just as Subaru couldn't face him, Julius surely had no face to show Subaru either. A deep-rooted problem — one could say it had been made deep-rooted by Subaru's misjudgment — but this was no longer a matter to be resolved overnight. At the very least, without taking time, without leaving enough space to talk it through calmly, it would never move toward resolution.

Thinking that, it felt shameful to slink meekly back to the Dragon Carriage. Even knowing Julius wouldn't be returning there, something about wrapping himself snugly in blankets pricked at his conscience.

That said, there was no one who would keep him company for an outpouring of Subaru's inner thoughts at this late hour. Rem, fast asleep, or Patrasche, who so strongly favored Subaru, were both in the Green Room, so out of fear of running into Echidna or Julius, he couldn't approach. Waking Emilia or Beatrice to have them listen would be far too selfish. So Subaru, with nowhere in particular to go—

"—Barusu?"

At that voice, Subaru made a faint sound in his throat and stopped in his tracks. Turning around in surprise, in the fourth floor's passage — footsteps ringing against the stone floor — the one who appeared was a pink-haired girl: Ram. Ram widened her pale pink eyes slightly, looked Subaru up and down, and—

"What a pathetic-looking face. How unsightly."

"...Straight to it the moment we meet, huh. Anyway, what are you doing here at this hour, Ram?"

"I'll return that question right back at you. ...Though I can imagine well enough what Barusu's been up to until this hour."

Ram came right up in front of him, arms crossed, cradling her own elbows, and at her words Subaru's cheeks stiffened. Told she could imagine what had happened, Subaru's expression clouded. At such a Subaru, Ram shrugged, exasperated, and—

"I'll bet you were off pouring out complaints to Rem again, for all the good it does. However cute and forgiving Ram's little sister is, stop foisting nothing but unreasonable demands on her."

"...Ah, that's what you mean. Well, yeah, I guess."

"—?"

Subaru's eyes widened slightly at the very Ram-like way of putting it, and then he gave a wry smile. It wasn't that she'd guessed his inner thoughts exactly right — it was a remark drawn from her prediction of Subaru's usual daily habits. Indeed, as Ram said, Subaru normally spent a great deal of his nights by Rem's side. In fact, that's what he'd done tonight too. It was only natural that Ram would assume he was on his way back from that. But today, that wasn't all there was to it—

"Stop making such a pitiful face."

"Ow."

"With a pathetic face, a pitiful face, a man whose stock is already low sinks lower still. Looking like that will bring Emilia-sama's dignity into question, seeing as she's made a knight out of you, Barusu. Fix it."

Ram flicked a finger against his lowered forehead. The force of it brought tears to Subaru's eyes, but at the sight of Ram sniffing, looking bored, his complaint died in his throat. More than that, he found himself feeling relieved.

"...I mean, seriously, Ane-sama really is Ane-sama, huh."

"Hah. Stop with the creepy remarks."

At Subaru's comment, rubbing his forehead, Ram scrunched up her face in utter distaste. That such an attitude was what saved him made him pathetic in his own eyes. It wasn't as if she was listening to his story, or trying with any real sympathy to understand what had happened.

"What were you doing until this hour, Ram?"

"How lecherous."

"Don't try to end the conversation with zero delay like that. It was just a little opener, wasn't it..."

Shrugging at her utterly unapproachable attitude, Subaru let out a light breath and turned his gaze past Ram, to the passage she'd walked from. The fourth floor was fairly spacious, but there wasn't any particularly notable facility to speak of. There was the Green Room, and the various cargo carried up from the Dragon Carriage. And—

"...The stairs to the second floor, huh?"

"――――"

"Don't tell me you'd gone up there. Alone."

"Rest easy. I'm not that reckless. Even if I haven't seen it with my own eyes, I'm not so conceited as to think I could handle Reid Astrea alone."

To Subaru, whose lips had twisted at the unpleasant thought, Ram denied the suspicion with something like a snort. In the process, a reference to Julius's unilateral decision peeked through, not quite fully concealed — but touching on that here and now would sting Subaru himself, too.

"I see, so something happened with the knight Julius. A fight?"

"Am I really that easy to read?"

"It's both that Barusu is easy to read, and that Ram is too clever. The latter carries more weight, so you needn't worry. ...No, actually, do worry about the former too. If you're ever tortured, the enemy will know your inner circumstances instantly."

"That scenario where I get tortured is way too terrifying to just casually bring up, you know."

Subaru answered while poking and prodding at his own cheek, and Ram only narrowed her eyes at him. Something about it felt like she wasn't entirely joking, and Subaru shivered. Given his position, it wasn't impossible that someone hostile to the Royal Selection, or to Emilia, might resort to such violence against him. He decided he'd keep it in mind.

"That aside, then why exactly are you here..."

"—I haven't gone up to the second floor. I only tried to."

"...Even though you said you weren't being reckless? Don't tell me — some kind of sneak-attack-while-he-sleeps plan?"

An attitude of going for the win by any means necessary wasn't something Subaru disliked. If Ram had snuck up there aiming for a time when Reid was asleep, in pursuit of that, he could understand it. The problem was whether Reid, who seemed to be some kind of reproduction from a past record, even slept at all, and whether, even if he was asleep, anyone could do anything about that thing regardless.

"Unfortunately, attacking him in his sleep is out of the question. I turned back partway up the stairs. That's how far outside the norm that monster is. It makes Garf look adorable by comparison."

"Garfiel's pretty much always got some cuteness to him once he's warmed up to you, though..."

"I'm talking about danger level, not behavior."

That didn't actually deny the part about him being cute in behavior, but since they were in the middle of an important conversation, Subaru let it go and knit his brow.

"I'm certain of it now. If we stop choosing our methods, he'll just stop choosing his too. Just as we discussed, clearing this really does require satisfying him just enough that he doesn't get serious."

"...You went all the way up to the second floor alone, just to confirm that?"

"Don't make me say it again. I haven't gone up to the second floor. It's too much for Ram as she is now."

Acknowledging her own plain lack of strength, Ram cautioned that preparation was essential before challenging the second floor. Hearing that time would be needed brought back the earlier exchange with Echidna and Julius, and Subaru couldn't help but frown.

"Barusu?"

"Hm, no, it's nothing. ...Well, it's not nothing, but for now, at least. Probably, once it's tomorrow, there'll be a proper talk about it."

"What a relentlessly suggestive thing to say."

"After all that build-up, I know, but it feels wrong for it to come from me. If I go and do something disloyal here too, that'd be a little past the point of no return."

Even now, it was a crack shaky enough that whether it could even be mended was in doubt. He didn't want to drive a further wedge into it, widening the crack. Ram, though probably not convinced, backed off from such a faint-hearted Subaru.

"Either way, clearing the second floor... clearing Reid, is going to take time and effort. If only Shaula knew a bit more that was actually useful."

"Well, it's true she wasn't much use, but don't be too hard on her. To begin with, if she hadn't helped us out, all of us would've ended up charred black under the sand."

To begin with, without Shaula, they never would have made it as far as the second floor's 'Trial.' Thinking of that, Subaru felt he could turn a blind eye to her disappointing showing as a Sage ever since they'd entered the tower. Getting past a 'Trial' with the examiner's own help was, if anything, far more exceptional a case anyway.

"Keep lining up nothing but pretty words, and you're bound to hit a wall somewhere."

"It's not like I think being spotlessly upright about absolutely everything is always right, either. It's case by case... this time just doesn't happen to be one of those cases, that's all."

"How carefree. ...Ram can't afford to take things so leisurely."

Ram muttered that, dissatisfied with Subaru's response, and shrugged, exasperated. Then, slowly turning her back, she said—

"I should get to sleep soon or it'll interfere with tomorrow. I'm heading back to the Dragon Carriage."

"Ah, right. Um, I..."

Explaining the reason it was hard for him to go back was itself hard to do. Ram, only turning her head back at Subaru who trailed off vaguely like that, let out a small sigh.

"Do as you like. But if lack of sleep ends up dragging us down, I'll wring it off you."

"Ah, sorry... wring what off me!?"

"I'll leave that to your imagination."

Waving a hand airily, Ram turned toward the stairs leading down to the lower level. Not touching what he didn't want touched, leaving his recovery up to his own efforts — perhaps that was her own particular kind of consideration. At that slender back growing distant, Subaru raised a hand, knowing full well she couldn't see it.

"'Night, Ane-sama. See you tomorrow."

"...Ram is not Barusu's Ane-sama. Stop calling me that."

Lately, even her refusals had lost their force, resistance amounting to little more than a refusal to formally admit it, even as she let it slide. Leaving those words behind, once Ram's figure had vanished from sight, Subaru cracked his neck and muttered, "Now then, what to do."

He couldn't go back to the Dragon Carriage. Going to the Green Room was awkward too. That being the case, he needed a place where he could either rest quietly until morning, or spend the time productively.

"If it's just sleeping, any old room would do, but..."

The likeliest candidate was the room where the cargo was stored. It was the room where the Dragon Carriage's cargo, foodstuffs and the like, had been carried in, but if he looked, there'd probably be something he could use as a pillow. The catch being that the hard floor might leave his body a little sore — barring which, one could argue he might as well just sleep in the Dragon Carriage instead.

Aside from that—

"Thinking about how to clear the second floor, how to get past Reid."

Honestly, this was the most constructive option. Most of the current problems would move toward resolution by conquering this Watchtower. It wasn't a sure thing either, but it was undoubtedly a major factor that could turn the situation around. Clearing Reid, as everyone had discussed over dinner, meant finding a way to satisfy him in earnest without drawing out his serious side — a fairly vague proposition. If there was even a chance to shave down that vagueness even a little—

"—That's it."

Having thought that far, Subaru snapped his fingers. At a thought that suddenly flashed like lightning through his mind, the direction his feet pointed snapped decisively into place.

"If this works out..."

Even if it wasn't a sure thing, it ought to be a move that could greatly advance the situation. His heart quickening at the idea, Subaru made his way toward that place at a hurried pace.

—In the nighttime Watchtower, only the sound of Subaru's eager footsteps rang out sharply. —Only the footsteps of one person, all alone.

—The sensation of waking is close to the moment your face breaks the surface of water.

Pulling the body up from where it had sunk into the unconsciousness called dreaming, circulating reality through the whole body in the form of breath. In doing so, consciousness slowly revives, breaks the surface, and is born anew. Sleep is death, and waking is birth — if one wanted to sound pretentious, one could put it that way.

In any case, setting aside such poetic musings, consciousness gradually moved toward wakefulness—

"—Subaru! Hey, Subaru, are you okay?"

"Wait, whoawaah!?"

The instant he opened his eyes, startled by the beautiful face right up close, Subaru rolled sideways. And the moment he rolled, the ground vanished from beneath him, and he fell a short distance, slamming his shoulder.

"Nngyaah!"

"Eek! Subaru, are you all right!? Why'd you suddenly roll like that!?"

"N-no, it's not like I made some conscious decision to suddenly go and roll over..."

Rubbing the shoulder he'd struck, shaking his head lightly, he slowly sat himself up. Then, blinking his eyes rapidly, Subaru was bewildered.

The place was a green room. Throughout the room, overgrown vines writhed and covered every surface, and the walls were entirely hidden. It was a room with an appearance so outlandish that he'd have believed it if told the room itself was made of vines.

And Subaru, it seemed, had apparently been lying in the middle of that room, on a bed woven of vines. He'd rolled off of it, ending up like this — he analyzed the current situation. Subaru was making such a coolly composed judgment, but there was a reason for it.

"Hmm, doesn't look like you hit anywhere too hard. That's really a relief. But I was suuuper worried, so don't startle me like that, okay?"

"Emilia, talking to him like that, Subaru might not reflect on it at all, I suppose. You have to say it more sternly, or Subaru won't understand just how worried Betty and the rest of us were, I suppose."

"That's true. See, Beatrice is saying so too, right? She was in such a panic when she couldn't find you, and when we found you collapsed she looked like she was about to cry..."

"You don't have to go and say things you don't need to say, I suppose!"

Right before his eyes, an exchange like something out of a comedy skit played out. Nodding along at the heartwarming exchange, Subaru turned around. Right behind him, as he sat on the ground, was the presence of some huge creature.

"――――"

It was a large lizard. A big lizard, black-scaled, as huge as a horse. And of all things, it was nuzzling up against Subaru, rubbing its snout against the back of his neck. Quite the friendly one, Subaru thought, gently stroking the lizard's head. And then, he let out a sigh.

"In other words, this is that, huh."

Calmly, composedly, slowly, he exhaled the words along with his breath. At Subaru's manner, the two girls in front of him tilted their heads.

"—Subaru?"

The two of them, breathing in unison like sisters, called out Subaru's name at the same time. A silver-haired girl beautiful enough to blind you, and a little girl in a dress, as lovely as a fairy.

A beautiful silver-haired girl, a little girl with ringlet curls, a giant lizard, a room made of vines—

Subaru opened his mouth wide, and shouted.

—Because, this, in other words, was that.

"The whole 'summoned to another world' thing——uuh!?"
