Arc 4 Chapter 68: "The Taste of Death"

—Through a world where everything looked warped and distorted, Subaru ran on with reckless abandon.

"――――"

This isn't sane. Not sane, not sane, not sane, not sane, not sane, not sane, not sane, not sane, not sane, not sane, not sane, not sane.

The words repeated over and over inside his head. As they repeated, what played behind his eyelids was the final moment Roswaal fell before his eyes to the Great Rabbit's fangs. Without resistance, accepting his own death so readily, without so much as a cry of pain as his flesh was torn to pieces, Roswaal had ended his own existence.

—It was abnormal. What else could you call this, if not abnormal, if not madness?

If it didn't matter that the current him died so long as his parallel-world self could achieve the goal—if this were a game, Subaru might have given the same weight to a player character's death. But this was reality. How could anyone entrust their real, actual life to another self who was, in truth, someone else entirely?

Roswaal, devoured by the rabbits before Subaru's eyes, was dead. And that Roswaal's consciousness would not return together with Subaru to the world he returned to through Return by Death. Perhaps staking hope on Return by Death was something he shared with Subaru, but the weight of what had to be paid to get there was too different between the two of them. Unlike Subaru, who could get it back, what Roswaal offered up as the price would never return.

"—ugh, gh"

As he ran, Subaru remembered Roswaal's horrific death and was assailed by nausea. Stomach acid surged up, searing his throat with heat. With no time to spare even to retch, he kept moving, searching, wandering through the Sanctuary in search of survivors.

—Hell had unfolded before Subaru once again.

The snow had stopped falling over the Sanctuary, but the wind still howled and lashed at it all the same. Wincing at the pain like cold files scraping his skin, he looked up and around—and from every direction, cries mixed with the wind reached his ears.

Chitchitchit, chitchitchit—that was the sound of saw-like rows of short, sharp teeth grinding against each other, a threatening noise as the rabbits closed their siege around the entire Sanctuary.

The Great Rabbits wandered the Sanctuary in search of prey. How immense must the hunger, the starvation gnawing at them be? When they found no prey, as if even the time it took to rest their jaws was too precious to spare, they sank their teeth into the rabbit beside them to stave off their hunger. Utterly broken, true monsters. The grating chitchitchit of teeth, and the death throes and gleeful cries of cannibalism ringing ceaselessly from every direction—it was eroding Subaru's sanity, bit by bit, little by little.

"—Whoa!"

Shaking his head to try to throw off the awful sound clinging to his ears, Subaru saw a rabbit pass overhead with its jaws open. There was a vicious clack of fang meeting fang, and the rabbit, having missed its prey, spun around on the snow, hissing a warning. An instant later, one of the Ryuzu duplicates running alongside Subaru dropped her heel straight down from above, crushing the rabbit's body underfoot. There came the sound of flesh gouged and bone snapping, and the rabbit spat out the contents of its small body from its mouth and died.

Letting out a breath, not sparing the corpse a glance, Subaru resumed his sprint. The Ryuzu duplicate moved her legs too, keeping pace with the running Subaru. Behind them, a short distance back, another rabbit arrived beneath the one just crushed. The sound of the corpse being devoured in a single breath reached his ears, and inside Subaru, the bell of ruin rang out louder still.

Only six of the Ryuzu duplicates remained around Subaru. Right after Roswaal had been taken down by the Great Rabbit, there had been eleven of them; now their number had been cut in half. Ordered to "protect Subaru's body," they had intercepted the Great Rabbits' assaults, thrown themselves bodily in front of Subaru to shield him, and one after another had been returned to Mana.

Subaru's mind had shut down regarding the fact that he was the one who had given the order for the duplicates to offer up their lives. For now, all he could think of was Rem's safety in the great cathedral, and Emilia's presence remaining at the tomb—everything else he had abandoned somewhere beyond thought. Because that was the only way he could still affirm his own actions right now, the only way he could protect his own heart.

"The— the cathedral is—!"

His feet catching in the snow, skirting the paths thick with Great Rabbits, Subaru made a wide detour around the Sanctuary and finally reached the great cathedral at the center of the village. In a village with no light source, the cathedral caught his eye at once. Of course it did.

—Because in that white world, the great cathedral alone was wrapped in blazing red flame.

"—Why…"

Kneeling in the snow, Subaru muttered in a hoarse, hollow voice. Amid the crackling sound of the spreading fire snapping at the building materials, he could even see Great Rabbits diving into the flames to devour the prey inside the cathedral, only to burn and fall like moths.

That they were so frantic to force their way into the cathedral meant there was something inside to satisfy their hunger. And that whoever it was continued to remain within those flames meant exactly what it seemed to mean.

"――――"

He could understand, to some degree, choosing to end one's own life rather than despair of surviving and be eaten alive by rabbits instead. He could understand it—not that he couldn't—but,

"Even so, I wanted them to keep fighting to the end…"

Was it too cruel a thing to wish—that they had fought, that they hadn't given up on living until the very last possible moment?

Roswaal, and the people of the Sanctuary—all of them treated their own lives far too carelessly. Subaru, having forgotten that he himself was, more than anyone, deserving of that very reproach, covered his face and wept.

Neither Roswaal nor Subaru had been able to be, for the people of the Sanctuary and the refugees of Arlam Village sunk in despair, something that would make them think to keep fighting until the very end and wait for rescue. If that much trust had been built, they would surely have refused to give up on life until their final moment. —This too, all of it, was Subaru's responsibility, Subaru's sin.

"At least let Rem…"

Hasn't she made it, I wonder—Subaru, ranking the value of lives in his head, was far too arrogant for that thought. In his mind, Subaru called out to the duplicate he'd ordered to bring Rem back to the cathedral—to Ryuzu, the representative persona. But there was no discernible response from anywhere that suggested the message had been received.

—Rem was inside that burning cathedral. Even if she had managed to escape, Subaru wasn't foolish enough to believe that Ryuzu alone could protect Rem and escape the Great Rabbits at the same time.

He clenched his back teeth. He tasted blood. Biting down on the taste of blood, biting down on the bitterness welling up inside him, Subaru bit down on his decision. —He had come this far, again and again failing to give up on something he should have already known full well was a lost cause. This time, truly, for real, it had to be the moment to give up.

"――――"

He could sense the chitchitchit of monsters gripped by starved obsession closing in. From the collapsing, burning cathedral, the rabbits that had given up devouring their prey there had noticed Subaru kneeling in the snow and the duplicates surrounding him.

Standing, brushing off the snow, Subaru let out a long breath. He didn't notice the feel of tears running down his cheeks. So he made no move to wipe them away either.

"Emilia…"

This was a world already ending. Even if it didn't end on its own, it was a world Subaru himself would end.

A world in which he hadn't been able to save a single one of the people he'd wanted to stay with, to spend his days alongside—when the end came, he wanted, at the very least, to be beside the girl he loved.

"Put your bodies on the line and protect me. —Once we reach the tomb, you're free to do as you like after that."

Telling the remaining six duplicates this without emotion, Subaru took one step, then another, fleeing the ring of Great Rabbits steadily closing in around him, and then broke into a run. Sensing their prey trying to flee, the Great Rabbits raised soundless cries and gave chase, drooling, hot on Subaru's trail.

"――――"

Two of the duplicates struck the first wave of charging rabbits as they lunged. The sound of flesh crushed and death throes lingered in the air, and immediately after, an even greater number of rabbits swarmed the duplicates. In an instant, their bodies were entirely blanketed in white fur, and the duplicates toppled sideways—the moment the fatal blow landed, their small bodies transformed into a stream of pale blue light.

In that final vanishing burst, an explosion of Mana caught the surrounding rabbits in its blast, and phosphorescence danced wildly across the night sky of the Sanctuary. Feeling the last radiance of the duplicates behind him on his skin, Subaru shook his head as if to shake off everything he was leaving behind, gritted his teeth, and kept running toward the tomb. —He kept running.

By the time he reached the tomb, Subaru's body no longer felt the cold.

Snow blurred his vision, and he had the sensation that even his eyelashes had frozen, yet Subaru, breathing out white breath through trembling lips, paid none of it any mind. His dull, heavy thoughts pictured only the figure of a single girl.

Making noise, treading the stone-paved passage, Subaru pressed on toward the depths. In the chamber of the Trial, there should be a girl waiting for him—the one he'd left there, laid down, alone.

"—Subaru?"

The instant he came upon the open space, a voice like a silver bell called his name. Drawn by that voice, he turned his steps and entered the hall—and the figure standing there, seeing him, cried out in a voice full of joy.

"It really is you, Subaru! Geez, where have you been? You had me worried!"

Saying this, Emilia trotted over and took Subaru's hand. With a pouting face, she pulled the hand she'd taken and clasped it to her own chest, conveying the warmth of her soft body as she looked up at him from below,

"…Are you tired?"

"Yeah… maybe I'm… a little tired…"

"Ehehe, I see. Well then, well then—"

Emilia laughed, her cheeks flushing at Subaru's honest admission. And then, still holding his hand, she suddenly sat down right where she was. Folding her legs to sit sideways, she pulled the hand she held to draw the half-crouching Subaru further in,

"Here, go ahead, Subaru."

"…A lap pillow, huh."

"Yep. Subaru likes my lap pillows, right? You said so yourself. I remember things like that properly, you know. Come on, go ahead."

Patting her own knee, Emilia gave a proud, bashful smile, and Subaru, following along, lowered himself down and, taking her up on the offer, laid his head on her soft thighs. For a moment, his short hair brushed against her soft skin, and Emilia let out an alluring little "nn—" from her throat, but she quickly settled into familiar ease and began stroking Subaru's head.

"How many times is this now, giving Subaru a lap pillow like this?"

"I dunno… third time, maybe? Feels like I'm always a mess when it happens."

"I like this too, you know—playing with Subaru's hair, his face, with my fingers. Here, boop, boop—"

Tugging at his bangs, pressing a finger into his cheek, Subaru let the cheerful Emilia do as she pleased. Because he could feel that this was her way of expressing love, not a shred of desire rose in him to bat her fingers away. In a world already ending—right now, he wanted to drown himself in Emilia's love.

—Because by now, nearly everything in his gut, all his blood, had already been spent.

Subaru's current state was gruesome enough that an ordinary person would want to look away. Fangs had raked his back, and if you peeled back his clothes, you would probably see bone. Combined with the massive bleeding from his torn-open thigh, the blood loss was tremendous, and when he'd swatted away a leaping rabbit, the fingers of his right hand had been taken with it, leaving only his thumb. That he had managed to make it here at all, even with his consciousness fading, might have been thanks to an obsessive, near-maddening tenacity—or the ironic result of the freezing cold slowing his body's metabolism. And even so—

"Subaru, did you get a little lighter than before?"

"Tried a bloodletting diet. You know… like tossing ballast, shedding weight, getting lighter and lighter, kind of…"

"I don't get what you mean, but you went and did something reckless for somebody again, didn't you? That's just the kind of person you are, Subaru. I know that but… I'm sooo worried."

"…………"

"Honestly, I want you to do that kind of thing only for me. But that's selfish of me, and I guess Subaru, the one who'd turn a blind eye to everyone else just for my sake—maybe I wouldn't want to see that side of you either. …This is selfish of me too, huh. Sorry."

Emilia's voice, piling up words quickly, grew distant. Inside the tomb, unlike the frigid air outside, the temperature was kept somewhat normal. Ironically, that returned Subaru's metabolism to its usual state, and the blood flow that had slowed began again. Bright red blood spread across the stone floor, staining it, and Subaru, coughing, brought up blood from his mouth as well. Flecks of spattered blood bloomed across Emilia's pale white cheek. But—

"Hey, Subaru, are you listening? There's so much I want to talk about, so much I want to ask, so, so, so much. Please. Stay by my side. Let me hear your voice. Let me hear it, okay?"

Emilia paid no mind to the feel of the blood spattered on her cheek. No—she hadn't even noticed. Her violet eyes were certainly looking down at Subaru, reflecting him there—but she wasn't accepting reality as it truly was.

By the time he'd returned from the mansion, Subaru, tortured and beaten nearly to the point of torment by Elsa, had been covered in wounds. Dragged all the way to the tomb by Garfiel, his appearance had likely grown even more gruesome. And yet Emilia hadn't noticed Subaru's injuries at all, nor did she seem to care. It was the same now, even with Subaru before her, chewed apart in various places by the Great Rabbits, missing pieces of himself here and there.

Emilia could not see reality right now. And perhaps, for Subaru too, it was the same.

"――――"

If things were as they should be, Subaru needed to warn Emilia of the danger and get her away from this place. The Great Rabbits had already filled the area outside the tomb, and before long, they would surely come pouring in here too. If that happened, even Emilia wouldn't stand a chance. Just as Roswaal had, just as the villagers had chosen to end their own lives amid the flames, Emilia too would be unable to escape a horrific death. Even knowing this, Subaru could not bring himself to tell her.

In just a little more time, his own life would be lost—and he could not run from the self-serving wish to greet those final moments at Emilia's side.

Roswaal's words and his horrific death, the regret of having let Garfiel and Ram die, the cruel transience of losing Petra and Frederica, the helplessness of failing to save even Rem or Beatrice—all of it was crushing Subaru. The pain, even the fear of death—none of it mattered anymore now.

—All he wanted, purely and simply, was to disappear from this world.

Subaru's wish—reckless, self-centered as it was—would be granted. The world began to turn hazy and white, his consciousness and soul drifting little by little further from this place.

Strength drained from his limbs, and the sensations of his body, already nearly lost, faded away entirely. What remained—what was left behind—was only Emilia, unaware that Subaru was slipping away.

"――――"

Am I going to leave Emilia behind? Like this—Emilia, who had no one left to rely on but Subaru, who had lost every other thing she could have leaned on—was he going to leave behind even Subaru, the one and only person she could turn to?

"Ah—"

It was far too late now for regret to change anything at all. Everything was already beyond saving. No voice came out, and the light drained from his eyes.

Unaware of this, Emilia simply tilted her head at Subaru's silence in a small, endearing way. Then she smiled softly, gently brought her face closer, and—

"Subaru—"

"――――"

—kissed the silent Subaru.

—Their first kiss tasted of cold, of death.
