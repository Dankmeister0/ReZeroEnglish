Arc 5 Chapter 68: "The Name-Devouring Gourmand"

The all-out war against the Witch Cult that had begun in the water gate city of Priestella was finally approaching its climax.

In the east of the city, a church had been frozen solid, towering icicles thrust up like spears. In the north of the city, the waterways had gone up in flame all at once, and a white-hot stone tower had collapsed with a thunderous roar. In the west of the city, part of the waterway had been destroyed, and a great volume of water had poured underground, causing flooding. In the center of the city, the government building that had served as the hub of the city's functions had lost its foundation and lost its very shape.

The battles that had broken out across the city had produced enormous damage, carving the threat that was the Witch Cult into the city itself. And yet, even so, those who fought back were driving away malice, striving to accomplish their purpose in a defensive battle fought with limited strength. And so, in the midst of this narrow struggle to overcome the water gate city's crisis, if there was one battlefield where the tide was running against them, it was probably this one.

Yes — the sudden, spontaneous skirmish against Lye Batenkaitos, the Witch Cult's Archbishop of Gluttony.

"Ha-ha! No, no, no, no, no, no, no good, no goo-oo-ood! What's the matter, what'll you do, what do you wanna do, what're you gonna do, what've you done!?"

The area known as the "Waterway District," surrounded by narrow, winding canals. In the great plaza at the center of that district, a small figure leapt about nimbly, laughing uproariously.

Using his short limbs to their fullest, tossing his long, unkempt, dark brown hair — the one making all this racket was still a young boy. At best in his mid-teens, he wore nothing but a single ragged cloth draped over his body, dressed in a way that could easily be mistaken for a street urchin's.

But however accurate that description of his appearance might be, it was difficult to keep holding onto that impression once one actually laid eyes on him in person.

That was how alien, how twisted, how detestable the presence of this laughing boy was. The abnormal sense of oppression radiating from his entire body was obvious at a single glance. It was a fatal madness that called out to the part of instinct that human beings, in the course of daily life, had worn away.

Anyone could sense the abnormality of his existence from that alone. He was a blasphemer who had stepped beyond the bounds of humanity, trampled on the wishes of humanity, and trampled the human world underfoot. A Witch Cult Archbishop of Sin — evil that kicked aside human hearts and human decency and laughed while doing it.

"The target's just one lousy brat! Surround him, don't let him escape! Take him down!"

"Yeah, yeah, just one lousy brat! Try and catch me, try and tear me apart!"

The gruff voice of command overlapped with the boy's cackling laughter. Lye Batenkaitos of Gluttony glared down the men closing in on him, and rather than retreating, casually stepped forward to close the distance instead.

"—!"

Without even raising a shout anymore, the men in white robes leapt at Batenkaitos. All of them clad in white, they were members of the mercenary band Kiritaka had left behind, the White Dragon's Scales. Gripping matching short blades, they struck at Gluttony from all four directions. Against this perfectly synchronized, simultaneous attack, Batenkaitos merely said—

"There's a snag in there. No good."

He slid his body into the gap in that "perfect synchrony," dodging all four blades with the barest minimum of movement. Twisting away from the blades, deflecting one strike with the dagger strapped to his right arm, kicking one man's torso to break through the encirclement. Directly ahead, a massive frame with crossed arms stood blocking his path.

"Ungh, aaargh! Screw it, here I go!"

It was Gaston, Felt's retainer. He charged Batenkaitos with that huge build of his, meaning to collide head-on with the secret technique he'd used to steal one of the Archbishop's daggers. Batenkaitos widened his eyes at the sight, then immediately smiled — a smile fitting for his age, though the slightly-too-sharp canines peeking from his mouth stirred up a trace of unease.

"You've got spirit, old man. I don't hate that!"

"I ain't old enough to be called an old man yet— boah!?"

Batenkaitos's right foot snapped up into the side of the charging Gaston's face. Gaston took the blade-foot strike full on the cheek, but he didn't flinch, glaring down at Batenkaitos. Just like the dagger strike before it, this blow hadn't gotten through to Gaston either. The principle was unclear, but Gaston's body completely nullified the force of both blunt and edged attacks. As it was, Gaston's arm stretched out, reaching to grab the skittering "Gluttony"—

"Palm of the Fist King—"

Still with his leg raised, Batenkaitos murmured something. His palm made contact with Gaston's torso. Compared to the kick, it carried no force, no speed at all — just a single motion of a touch. And yet, the instant that palm lightly pressed in, Gaston's body folded in half like the letter "く."

"O-gueh!?"

Gaston dropped to his knees, spilling forth a torrent of stomach acid, groaning in agony. Batenkaitos slipped past the side of the pitching body and, having broken through the encirclement, turned around and cocked his head.

Is that all you've got? his gesture seemed to say, and the White Dragon's Scales, their pride wounded, couldn't hide their dread and astonishment either.

"Monster..."

Whoever had let that word slip, it was concise proof of Batenkaitos's ability. Though perhaps it was only a mishearing that there had seemed to be a faint trace of envy mixed into that pained voice.

"...There's a surprising lack of a decisive blow, more than I expected."

Watching a battle that could not, even generously, be called a stalemate, Otto licked his dry lips and desperately turned his mind over. In this close-quarters skirmish against "Gluttony," their side's fighting strength was hopelessly thin — of course, everyone was fighting with all they had, but the reality was that it still wasn't enough.

Seven of Kiritaka's White Dragon's Scales, Felt with Gaston as two more, and Otto himself as a solo unit — that made ten in total. If, by some happy coincidence, someone who'd already wrapped up another fight elsewhere joined them here, and the tide turned all at once in a great reversal — that would be the ideal. But—

"I don't trust my own luck enough to expect that kind of good fortune to come my way."

Otto Suwen's life had always run cheek-by-jowl with misfortune and unreason. Otto always believed that luck, as such, didn't exist — that results could only be pulled in through one's own effort, preparation, and conduct. The lucky things in his own life were being born into an understanding family despite carrying a troublesome Divine Protection, and having his life saved by Subaru after nearly being killed by an Archbishop of Sin. He figured he'd used all that up already, so he didn't pessimistically dwell on his misfortune more than necessary.

"That water dragon your brother brought — it's outta commission now, ain't it?"

Beside the pondering Otto, Felt, likewise watching the situation unfold, called out to him. Her crimson eyes glared past the clashing blades of "Gluttony" and his opponents — at the water dragon thrown out onto the cobblestones, its long neck convulsing as it lay dying. The pitiful water dragons Otto had set loose, only to have them scattered by Batenkaitos, could unfortunately not be expected to rejoin the fight.

"Unfortunately, I can't ask any more of it than that. Those were all the water dragons I managed to speak with on the way here... If I could speak with another water dragon, I might be able to do the same thing again, but would you let me escape from here to do so?"

"Don't joke around. Just gettin' that songstress-crazy one to run off already cost us one of our trump cards. To play a second one, we'd need actual prep time."

As she spoke, Felt looked down at the long, slender bundle clutched to her small frame. Wrapped in white cloth, it was apparently the Magic Vessel "trump card" her retainer had been made to carry for her. It supposedly came with a guarantee that even Reinhard couldn't dodge it, but using it required a somewhat troublesome set of steps, and until those were met, it couldn't be used.

"Besides, songstress-crazy aside, you and me — even if we tried to run, that guy ain't gonna let us. Seems like we landed square in that bastard's sights."

"Honestly, that's the one thing I'd most like to be spared."

At Felt's snort, Otto's shoulders slumped in dismay. Still, he couldn't deny her words, because even amid the fierce fighting, he could clearly feel the force of a heated gaze being thrown their way.

Batenkaitos let out hot breaths, his eyes moist, his cheeks flushed, casting frequent glances toward Otto and the others. Cut out of context, that gaze might be mistaken for something amorous — but in truth, all that lurked behind it was the hungry appetite of a ravenous beast. They had simply passed muster as fine, blood-dripping cuts of meat in the eyes of that predator. Not remotely an honor, but three people here had met Batenkaitos's discerning palate.

Otto and Felt, and the unshaven man commanding the White Dragon's Scales. He too was issuing orders to his subordinates while pitting them against Batenkaitos and rushing this way. The man with the rugged features gave Otto a nod, speaking rapidly while keeping an eye on the battle.

"Dynas. Thanks for helping our young master get away earlier."

"That was thanks to Lady Felt and her people. And getting Kiritaka out of here mattered for keeping our path to victory open too. With that..."

If Kiritaka, having withdrawn from this place, moved according to Otto's instructions, another possibility might yet open up. Unfortunately it was something like an unproven wish, but in any case it shouldn't turn things for the worse. There could be any number of means to win, to profit. In the end, all that mattered was landing on one of them and letting victory come rolling in.

"—"

Watching Otto fall silent, his mind whirling furiously, the man — Dynas — looked surprised for a moment, then nodded at once. There was a note of understanding in it.

"I see, so it's just as I'd heard. As expected of the head of domestic affairs for Lady Emilia's camp, the foremost figure of the Witch Cult resistance. At this rate, I suppose I can look forward to your recipe for dealing with the Archbishop of Sin too."

"Thank you for the overestimation and the hyperbolic advertising. Um, sorry, but — by the way, does everyone here actually know what a head of domestic affairs does? I can't shake the feeling there's a bit of a mismatch with what I understand the job to be..."

"Don't get all timid or modest on us. Our own domestic affairs officer swings a big club around with that huge body of his too. Ain't that just what the job is?"

The reputational damage being done to the title "head of domestic affairs" was severe. Over the past year, he'd more or less resigned himself to being called that, but he hadn't accepted that its very meaning would mutate along the way. What was the cause of this? Was it that he kept running into situations like natural disasters, over and over? Subaru's fault. The moment he got back safely, Otto was absolutely going to smack him once. That much, he decided.

"Well then—"

He'd set aside worrying about the aftermath for later, and focus on the present situation.

Ahead of Otto's gaze, Batenkaitos clashed again and again with the White Dragon's Scales and Gaston, unreservedly displaying his abnormal combat prowess, mowing them down. And yet — the odd thing was that he wasn't trying to swing the dagger on his right arm.

"A bunch of grown-ups all ganging up and still can't catch one lousy brat!? No good, no good at all, that won't do, that just won't do, that's simply no good!"

Baring his fangs, Batenkaitos dodged the men using nothing but his footwork. Toyed with by his freely shifting rhythm and the bizarre swaying of his upper body, the short blades only ever cut through empty air. It was movement built on an entirely different technical system between his upper and lower body. Watching this strange spectacle, even to Otto's eyes, Dynas gave an involuntary groan deep in his throat.

"Mr. Dynas?"

"Ah, no... sorry. His movements are just a little bit... whoa!?"

"Big guy like you, don't go gettin' scared on us!"

Dynas, mumbling as he tried to deny the thought forming in his head, had his backside kicked by Felt, who'd circled behind him. Dynas turned around, and Felt, cheeks puffed and reddened, glared up with narrowed eyes.

"Once you've got your hand in someone's pocket, whether you make off with the goods or not, all you can do is keep going. If you're gonna get chased around either way, use whatever you've got to make sure you snag the goods. Get it?"

"Why is that a thief's perspective?"

Otto tilted his head at Felt's advice, but beside him, Dynas shook his head. He looked back at Felt, said "You're right" as a preface, and continued—

"This young lady's got a point. I need to say out loud whatever comes to mind, and find a way to break through. For now, about this sense of unease I've got..."

Dynas, calling Felt "young lady," clearly hadn't realized her true identity was a Royal Selection candidate. But judging this a minor matter for the moment, Otto didn't mention it. Instead, he turned his attention to Dynas's words.

"That Archbishop of Sin... he looks like a kid, but the way he fights, the way he moves — he's just too skilled, however you slice it. It's not just raw physical talent either — it's something different from that."

"—? So what, ain't there just people who're born freakishly good at fighting? Ain't Reinhard one of those idiots too?"

"The moment Mr. Reinhard becomes a valid point of comparison, I want to raise the white flag."

Felt's knight, and the strongest blade in the kingdom — Reinhard. He was surely at Subaru's side right now, taking on the battle against "Greed." But even to Otto, who'd only dabbled in self-defense, Reinhard's strength was something he could feel in his very skin. Just being near him stirred up both absolute reassurance and a sense of awe at the same time. The title of the kingdom's strongest wasn't just for show. Perhaps even the claim of "strongest on the continent" wasn't wrong either.

On the other hand, there was also a revulsion, a discomfort that welled up just from being near Batenkaitos. But it was a different quality from what he felt around Reinhard. Of course, there was no room to deny that Batenkaitos's combat technique was exceptional either.

"It ain't like that either. I've lived long enough, been through my share of hairy scrapes... but that kid's way of fighting isn't something talent can explain. That's the kind of movement you only get from years and years of built-up training."

At Dynas's remark, Otto and Felt looked at Batenkaitos again. Just then, Batenkaitos caught a blade strike on his raised arm. Grabbing the wrist of the man who'd struck at him, Batenkaitos spun his body, dove into the man's guard, and threw him to the ground as though carrying him on his back. Against the men pressing further attacks, "Gluttony's" feet traced a circle across the cobblestones, his limbs weaving with a swaying rhythm, throwing them off balance and tripping them.

Indeed, there was, in that fluid movement, a clean, refined power that stood apart from the violent atmosphere Batenkaitos wore. It was technique grounded in some established martial system, and he had mastered it. A boy who looked fourteen or fifteen, having perfected a technique that took years to learn—?

"Maybe there really are people out there called martial arts prodigies who could pull that off too... but I don't wanna believe a talent like that got handed to that brat."

That Batenkaitos had mastered something enough to make even Dynas say as much — and at the same time, Otto finally grasped the meaning behind the envy laced into the murmur one of the white-robed men fighting Batenkaitos had let slip. The ones actually fighting had realized the truth before Otto and the others had. That was why they—

"Skill with a dagger, unarmed combat, a perfected martial technique...?"

Having said that much, Otto felt a doubt rise against his own reasoning. The thought that surfaced was, simply: Is something like that even possible?

"—"

He'd heard talk that in the southern Vollachian Empire, children forced to become gladiator-slaves in the colosseum were sometimes trained and educated in fighting from a young age. If Batenkaitos had been in that kind of environment, it might not be impossible for him to have acquired this much skill even at his current age — but this wasn't that kind of practical, battlefield-honed technique either. The way to master something like this was to attach oneself to a proper teacher and learn the technique step by step. Or else—

"Stealing it... no — devouring it, and making it his own?"

Otto had already heard from Subaru's own mouth that "Gluttony" was a being that devoured a person's "name" and "memories." In fact, he knew firsthand the sight of a girl who had had both devoured, left in a state where she remained in no one's memory at all. Hard to believe as it was, he didn't doubt that this was a being that devoured something invisible to the eye. But until now, he'd never once considered what happened to the things it ate.

If, just possibly, "Gluttony" accumulated the memories of the people he devoured, and could make use of them— The technique of a martial artist who'd trained for ten years or more, or the fighting style of a warrior who'd walked across countless battlefields, or even the attacks of the girl who had once supported Subaru at his side, mimicked—

"Magic incoming!!" "Gaston!"

"—El Huma."

An electric shock of impact ran through, and Otto's shout and Batenkaitos's incantation came simultaneously. The air froze with a crackling sound, and the resulting mass of ice struck sideways into the members of the White Dragon's Scales, who hadn't anticipated the magic attack. But Gaston spread both arms and stood in the way of that hail of ice. The big man, still with his usual defensive resilience, took the pelting ice full across his body. Behind him, the White Dragon's Scales dove for cover, mitigating the damage. Even so, they couldn't avoid it entirely.

"Good call from the older brother there. Wasn't as much of a surprise attack as I'd hoped."

Having cast the spell, Batenkaitos curled up the corner of his mouth as he surveyed the wreckage. Gaston, having served as the wall, was breathing hard, and behind his back, of the six White Dragon's Scales, some had been wounded. One bled and crouched over, two had their legs taken out. Even the remaining three weren't unscathed. Their fighting strength had been halved, and on top of that—

"Since those words just came out of your mouth, I guess our little secret's been figured out, huh?"

"...Well, who's to say."

"You're not cut out for deception, big brother. Might wanna quit being a merchant before you get hurt worse."

Batenkaitos responded to Otto's strained bluff, and in the next instant his figure vanished. No — it was that he'd stepped forward so swiftly and naturally it seemed as though he'd vanished. That small frame slipped past Gaston's side, heading toward the White Dragon's Scales members who could no longer move. The three who could still move leapt back reflexively, but the three wounded ones couldn't be pulled away.

"Asuta. Ruckfelt. Hicks."

Murmuring the names as though whispering, Batenkaitos gently stroked the shoulders of those three. What in the world — everyone's eyes went wide as the three bodies touched by Batenkaitos jolted. And then Batenkaitos turned around, raised the left hand that had touched them, and—

"Yum."

He licked that empty left palm. In an instant, Otto was swallowed by a sense of wrongness. Something had happened, that much was certain. But what it was, he had no idea. And that wasn't the only thing he didn't understand.

"The ones collapsed at his feet — who are they?"

Otto pointed at the ground beneath Batenkaitos, who was showing off his long tongue. At the feet of that malice shaped like a boy lay three people in white clothing. They lay there, but their identities were unknown. From their build, they were presumably members of the White Dragon's Scales — but when had they appeared, and when had they been struck down?

"Sad, so sad, it's sad, isn't it sad, of course it's sad, precisely because it's sad. Even if it's a one-sided reunion for us, we still can't help but be satisfied."

"Mr. Dynas! Those three, who—!?"

"I don't know! I've never seen those faces before! But—!"

Being told these were strangers despite wearing the matching uniform carried no weight of persuasion. It didn't — but there was no intention or room for deception in Dynas's voice. He rushed forward like a shot, brandishing two blades in both hands, and cut at Batenkaitos.

"Don't, Dynas. We go way back. Your hometown's purification's just one step away, and here you are, cutting down a comrade like this — how stupid."

"—!? How the hell do you know about that!"

Recklessly trampling on Dynas's personal circumstances, Batenkaitos raised his right blade. Deflecting the two strikes of the short blades lightly with a single dagger, "Gluttony" drove his raised knee into Dynas's chest, then used the momentum to leap back all at once. Dynas, clutching his chest, coughed, but managed to secure the three unknown fallen. At the sight of him, Batenkaitos sighed pointedly.

"Don't get so hung up on the vessel. What matters is the heart, the contents, right? It's not the outside that makes a person who they are — it's the inside. We know how hard you've tried, Dynas. It wasn't your fault you couldn't protect Millian or Meili either. You just had bad luck."

"Shut up, shut up, shut up, shut up! What the hell do you know about me! What do you know! Don't just say whatever the hell you want! You rotten bastard!"

Roaring in fury, Dynas, forgetting the pain of the blow, charged again. His two blades whirled, aiming for Batenkaitos, but they were easily dodged by a Batenkaitos wearing the expression of someone who'd seen that very strike before. As he moved, Batenkaitos's hand reached for Dynas's back.

"Oop?"

"Quit ignorin'" "people, you jerk!"

Gaston tackled Batenkaitos's slender waist from directly behind. Batenkaitos's elbow slammed into his nose, but the force of the blow didn't push Gaston back. And with Gaston pinning down Batenkaitos, Felt came dashing in and leapt at him. She swung the long, slender bundle held in both hands down on his unguarded back of the head.

"Calling someone stupid, that's hurtful. We probably know a lot more than you do, y'know?" "Nguh!"

But Batenkaitos bent forward against Felt's blow, deflecting it with the dagger raised in his right arm. The force-laden strike glanced off with a grinding sound, its aim thrown wide off — but that left Felt's own posture badly exposed. With Dynas and Felt both showing openings at once, and Gaston within reach too — Batenkaitos's demonic hand reached toward all three—

"I won't let you!"

Just before it reached them, a thrown magic stone glowed red-hot at Batenkaitos's feet. The instant he registered it, Batenkaitos's movement stopped, and Felt and Dynas made an emergency retreat.

"Don't let go, Gaston!" "Remember this, you demon girl...!"

Still holding on, Gaston kept Batenkaitos from escaping the magic stone's effective range. The glow of the red-hot stone intensified, and immediately after, an explosion tore up the cobblestones with enough force to strip the pavement bare. Red and white light shot upward, and Felt came rolling toward Otto in the blast wind. He somehow caught her and looked over the extent of the explosion.

It was one of the "just in case" magic stones Otto always carried on him. Ever since the fight with Garfiel in the Sanctuary, he'd never skimped on keeping a certain amount on hand for his own safety. It was a painful expense for his wallet, but the effect was nothing to scoff at either.

"Is your companion alright!?"

"Heh, don't underestimate our big guy. He's my armor. A little attack like that ain't gonna get through him. Just..."

Right as she said that much, a huge silhouette burst out through the faint cloud of dust. It was Gaston. Covered in soot, he desperately slapped at his own face.

"Guaaah! Hot! Hot! Hot, hot! I'm gonna die!"

"He's fine with blasts and getting punched, but heat or cold, he can't block those."

Felt sighed at the sight of her retainer writhing from the intense heat. Having taken the fire magic stone's power up close, it seemed Gaston's life wasn't in danger, at least. Having confirmed that much, Otto peered into the smoke. The rising black smoke was of a decent scale, and there should be no way for Batenkaitos, who'd been at its center, to have avoided it. Beyond the black smoke, Dynas was there too, down on one knee but apparently unharmed.

And then—

"Bennett. Calcifus. Augusto."

"—!?"

A murmuring voice sounded, and everyone's gaze turned to a spot removed from the blast center. There lay three more people in white robes, and Batenkaitos, standing there unharmed. The boy licked his left palm again with a "yum."

Incomprehension followed immediately. Once again, three more unfamiliar people in white robes had appeared. The method of escaping the blast center, the sudden appearance of three more victims — all of it lay outside understanding.

"Damn it! What are those things! Where'd they... no, when did they even...?"

Beside Otto, Felt roughly clawed at her beautiful golden hair. She, too, no longer understood the meaning of what she was seeing. She didn't even know what exactly she needed to be scrambling to think through. But Otto understood. This strange phenomenon.

"This is what it means to have your 'name' eaten—!"

The girl called "Rem," whose form had vanished from everyone's memory, remaining only within Subaru. The same phenomenon was happening right now, right in front of his eyes. Presumably, the men lying on the ground who looked like they belonged to the White Dragon's Scales had had their names devoured by Batenkaitos. As a result, the memory of them "having been there" had vanished from the minds of Otto and the others. That was why they seemed to appear out of nowhere, and why no one knew who they were.

"—"

A chill ran through him. He understood anew the malice of the outlandish monster standing before him. At the hands of Lye Batenkaitos of Gluttony, even if Otto and the others were wiped out here, not only would the traces of the battle vanish, but their very existence would disappear without a trace. Not just the memory of their resistance, but the very fact that they had been here at all.

The possibility that he himself would cease to exist, and even the people who might remember him would disappear too — was that not perhaps a terror surpassing every other terror in this world?

Otto went pale. Though they hadn't reached the same conclusion in so many words, Felt's and Dynas's complexions had turned correspondingly bad too. Faced with odds worse than they'd imagined, he only now grasped the recklessness of it all. So much so that it made him think the real choice should have been to flee without a shred of dignity—

"Damn it, what an irritating bastard. One more time, same way as before...?"

"—"

Beside a weakening Otto, Felt muttered that, gripping her bundle tight. Unthinkingly, Otto turned a stunned look on her profile. Felt's expression showed confusion in the face of this abnormal situation. But her fine-featured profile showed no grief. As though she'd hardened her resolve not to break.

Feeling as though he'd been doused with water, Otto slapped his own cheek. Just what was he getting weak-kneed about? Until he went bankrupt and had to hang himself, he couldn't call it a loss. Right now, he was still only at the stage of maybe having a noose put around his neck. There were still chances left.

At the sight of those two, even Dynas and Gaston rose to their feet with resolve on their faces. And seeing that the four of them hadn't lost their fighting spirit, Batenkaitos nodded, satisfied.

"Good, that's good, good, isn't it, good indeed, maybe good, good, why not, it's gotta be good, precisely because it's good! Gluttonous! Voracious! You're all fit for the table! I'll rank you up among the finest of appetizers, Gaston. And Felt and Dynas — I'll properly savor you two."

Clapping his hands, Batenkaitos handed down a less-than-welcome evaluation to the three of them. Then "Gluttony" slowly turned his eyes toward Otto. The blasphemer tilted his head, and just when it seemed he'd move straight into appraising Otto too, his face turned dissatisfied instead.

"You look clever, stubborn, richly flavored, big brother... and yet."

"What are you..."

Having said that much, Otto caught on to the reason for Batenkaitos's dissatisfaction. Batenkaitos, calling out Felt and the others' names as though cherishing them. His ability to devour "names," and the content of what he'd just said, made it clear.

Batenkaitos couldn't devour the "name" of someone whose "name" he didn't know. That was why "Gluttony" couldn't devour Otto's "name" — because no one here had called him by name even once. That was the reason for his dissatisfaction.

"A request to the three of you. Please, from now on, absolutely do not say my name."

If his name wasn't spoken, Batenkaitos's goal, at least, couldn't be achieved. Perhaps even the whole string of what seemed like half-hearted, lukewarm attacks Batenkaitos had made up to this point had actually been a means of learning their names. Even allowing them time to confer might have been to wait for them to call each other by name, to finish setting the table for his meal—

"Sorry, big bro."

At the remark that leapt out of Otto's reasoning, Felt muttered apologetically. Her profile carried an awkwardness unlike anything he'd seen from her before, as she said—

"I don't even know your name to begin with..."

"Sorry about that, Mister Head of Domestic Affairs. I've forgotten your name too, position aside..."

"Yeah, sure, of course! I'm not even that close with any of you, and I'm not important either, right! Yay, damn it!"

Naturally, Gaston shrugged too, feigning ignorance. It was a complicated mix of joy and sorrow, but at least this had greatly reduced the risk of Batenkaitos learning his name. That much was certain.

"Dynas aside, I bet those two are covering for you, big brother. If not... well, then it just gets more annoying trying to pry it out of you."

"Wouldn't giving up and pulling back for today be one option? Come back another day... yes, when Mr. Reinhard, say, happens to be present, and we'll prepare a proper welcome for you."

"Turning your back on a feast like this? Don't be so unreasonable. A little something to fill the belly isn't enough for us — for me — to head home. Rui would scold me."

Naturally, persuading Batenkaitos was hopeless too. So then, combat was unavoidable.

"Gaston, don't you dare let go this time."

"Just 'cause you're not the one in pain, don't go doin' whatever you..."

"This time I'll properly join in myself."

Gaston, about to voice his objection to the order, widened his eyes at Felt's words. Then he laughed roughly, roughly patting Felt's head, mussing her hair.

"Don't joke around. If word got out I let my mistress take reckless risks, no telling what that old geezer would do to me."

"Don't touch my head. Only Grandpa Rom's allowed to do that."

"That's exactly why I did it."

With her hair mussed, Felt snorted and stood beside her. Beside them, Dynas readied his two blades, and Otto counted the number of magic stones tucked into his sleeves. Three in the left sleeve, two in the right. Five in total — he'd have to use them carefully.

"He touches you with his left hand, then eats. Consider it over the moment he touches you."

"Just hearing that, isn't that a stupidly high difficulty?"

"Getting your vitals cut with a sword ends it in one stroke too. Not much difference — get hit, you lose."

"Guess you're right, when you put it that way."

Felt muttered, seemingly convinced, and with that, their preparations were complete. Batenkaitos, with impeccable courtesy, waited for those preparations to finish. Otto narrowed his eyes at that well-mannered attitude, and the blasphemer, sensing his intent, laughed.

"Waiting for the table to be set is just manners, isn't it? Unlike that glutton Roy, we're gourmets. We have all sorts of standards when it comes to a meal."

Having said that much, Batenkaitos bowed with a formal, respectful gesture. It looked almost like an affectation, and yet strangely, it also looked like a gesture he was thoroughly accustomed to.

"So then, allow me to introduce myself again. Witch Cult Archbishop of Sin, in charge of Gluttony — Lye Batenkaitos."

"............"

"Isn't it manners to introduce yourself back, once someone's given their name?"

"After everything said so far, there ain't no idiot who'd give their name back to you."

By the customs shared among warriors, when one gave one's name, it was customary to return the courtesy. Perhaps Batenkaitos had made use of that very custom, drawing out the names of countless people up to now, laying them out on his plate and devouring them all. There was no reason for them to follow that custom. Spurning it as such, "Gluttony" laughed, and—

"Fair enough. Well then — itadakimasu!"

At the very signal for the meal, that small frame closed the distance in a single bound. His movement was unlike anything he'd shown until now — like a gust of wind sweeping through. Up to this point, Batenkaitos had been getting by on nothing but footwork and evasion, but now he unleashed his true strength for hunting down his prey.

Otto, a non-combatant, couldn't even track that movement, and couldn't react in time. But the other three were different.

"Don't you dare think you can beat me in a footrace."

Against Batenkaitos's wind-swift speed, only Felt kept pace with ease. The instant she kicked lightly off the cobblestones, her body moved in a single burst as though carried by the wind. Even Batenkaitos seemed surprised by that movement — his first strike swung through empty air.

"—!"

Into that gap, Gaston and Dynas struck simultaneously. Dynas's two blades, and a short blade Gaston had picked up in his own hand too. Against that slash, Batenkaitos, exposing himself defenseless, spread his legs and dropped his stance. Planting both hands on the ground, his body spun around with his legs extended. A sweeping kick along the ground took both men's legs out, throwing Dynas and the others off balance. Into that opening—

"Uwaaaah—!"

"Huh?"

Shouting, the fourth attacker — Otto — grabbed hold of him. Whether Otto engaging in close combat at this point was unexpected or not, Batenkaitos let out a stunned voice, and Otto succeeded in getting his arms around him. The instant he thought, now, restrain him— a stabbing pain assaulted Otto's stomach. Batenkaitos's left fist drove in, and Otto's body went rolling across the ground.

"Guek, gohh!"

"Right person, right place — like how there's rich and mild dishes on a menu, you've gotta know your place, big brother. I'll be sure to deal with you properly, later..."

In front of the muttering Batenkaitos, the two who'd lost their footing moved. Dynas caught the rolling Otto in his arms, and Gaston positioned himself to shield the two of them. At that formation, Batenkaitos cocked his head, and looking down at the ragged cloth he wore, he noticed something.

—At the spot where Otto had grabbed him, two glowing magic stones dangled.

"Uh-oh."

His attempt to remove them was too slow. The instant after Batenkaitos noticed the magic stones, both of them burst at once.

A duet of fire-attribute and water-attribute magic mineral stones. Red-yellow light and blue-white light swelled up simultaneously, and Batenkaitos's body was wrapped in that light as the shockwave tore through.

"—!!"

Caught by the direct blast up close, Otto and the others suffered damage from it too. Most of it fell on Gaston, taking the wall role, but even so, the shockwave and heat assaulted Otto as well. His skin was seared, or perhaps scorched by the cold. When the wave of impact receded, the second blast site was a terrible sight. The cobblestones had been gouged out, exposing bare earth, and scorched shreds of ragged cloth had scattered and fallen.

"Ah, what a mess. My good outfit's ruined."

And once again, Batenkaitos had moved to a spot away from the blast center. But this time, it seemed he hadn't gone unscathed. The boy, his long hair caught in the aftermath of the blast, muttered that in a somewhat displeased voice. Apparently, the tattered cloth that had fallen at the blast site was, without a doubt, what the boy had been wearing. Having lost the rags that had wrapped his whole body until now, the skin beneath was laid bare.

"—Ugh."

The one who let out an unthinking groan at the sight of Batenkaitos like that was Otto. Gaston and Dynas were both left speechless too, frowning at the same sight.

—Every visible inch of the boy's now-bared skin bore a mass of scars.

Marks of whips, marks of branding irons, marks carved by blades, marks roughly gouged away, marks torn out, marks of beast fangs, marks beaten black and blue — countless scars, without end.

Beneath the ragged cloth, wearing only a loincloth, the scarred boy turned around, and noticing the frowning Otto and the others, made a displeased face.

"Forcibly stripping a kid's clothes off and then reacting like that — that hurts, y'know. Isn't this the kind of thing grown-ups like?"

"...I don't know about the grown-ups around you, but ordinarily, people find that repugnant."

"Hmm. So then, here comes the pity again. Changing your attitude at the drop of a hat — that's exactly the kind of thing that makes you people untrustworthy!"

Spreading his arms as though showing off his scarred body, Batenkaitos shouted that. At his words, Otto grimaced, and Dynas too looked sick to his stomach. Only Felt and Gaston's expressions didn't waver. They glanced at the grimacing Otto and the others, and sighed.

"Hey now, don't go thinking anything weird. That kind of thing's everywhere, out there. Just happened not to be you or me — but it's everywhere, all the same."

Twirling the bundle in her hand, Felt said that coldly. There wasn't a shred of pity in her eyes. Of course, Otto too understood that this was not an opponent deserving of compassion — but even so, an Archbishop of Sin wasn't born mad from the start. At the very least, looking at Batenkaitos's body made one think that, too—

"—It's pointless to indulge in such dull speculation, I suppose. All it does is pile up needless regret, I'd think."

"—"

Suddenly, a voice that hadn't been in the great plaza until now rang out. Everyone, Batenkaitos included, snapped their faces up at once. The gaze of all five turned toward one of the canals ringing the plaza — from which a girl leapt lightly into the air and landed.

A frilled dress billowing softly, cream-colored hair curling neatly. Her haughty, composed face was a familiar one, and with those big round eyes, she surveyed the situation. And when her gaze settled on Otto, she sighed as though resigned.

"Sloppy nature and sloppy follow-through — Subaru alone is more than enough of that, I suppose. And Betty only lends a hand for Subaru's sake... this time, it's purely a special measure, I suppose."

"Yes, sorry, sorry to trouble you. But — thank you."

At the girl's harsh-sounding remark, Otto nevertheless felt a relief so profound it could have made his knees give out. That girl's very presence was one of the "paths to victory" Otto had laid.

"Now then, let's get this ruckus cleared up quick, so Subaru can hold Betty, I suppose."

Saying so with a weary look, the girl raised her hand and gave it a wave. No — not a girl. A Spirit. And among Spirits, a Great Spirit, no less.

Natsuki Subaru's contracted Spirit, Beatrice — finally joining the battle as reinforcements.
