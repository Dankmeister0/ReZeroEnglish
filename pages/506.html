Arc 7 Chapter 9: "The Empire's Way"

A threat that gnawed slowly at his whole body, an agony like blood turning to magma, was peeling Natsuki Subaru's existence away one layer at a time, trying to strip him bare of every ornament.
Like tearing off a half-healed scab and exposing the raw wound beneath to cold air—an extension of that same cruel act. His soul was touching a reality it could never be shielded from.

Would what came be pain? Grief? Sorrow?
Or something else entirely, something even Subaru couldn't guess.
If there was anything he could know, anything that offered salvation, it was only one thing.

That sense of despair broke off before it could reach an answer, and an unreal, liberating release—

"—Quit your wheezing, you're too damn loud."

"Ngmph—!"

Freed from the agony of a moment before, his mouth opened wide, ready to spray blood-foam.
His body, hollow as if a hole had been punched through his lungs, craved oxygen, and just as it tried to greedily savor that odorless, flavorless air, something was forced into his mouth.

Reeling from the unexpected shock, Subaru choked, and abuse rained down on him.
But he didn't know what was happening. More precisely, he couldn't see. The tightness he felt around his face was proof that something was wrapped around his eyes. His face was bound.
—No, it wasn't only his face that was bound. His hands and feet were restrained the same way.
And in that state, someone had just shoved something into his mouth.

"Cough! Ghk! Wh-why am I tied—guah!?"

"The hell you resisting for? You don't know your place, do you?"

"Ah, guh—!"

The instant he spat out whatever had been in his mouth, the rough man kicked him hard in the solar plexus. The impact knocked the breath out of him, and as Subaru toppled sideways, spit landed on him.
The humiliation of being spat on didn't even register beside the pain tearing through his chest. Just as his shut-off vision flashed red with agony, Subaru's mind was swallowed by a sea of confusion.

—Events from mere seconds ago were churning through his head.

"――――"

Roused by Todd and dragged out of the tent, he'd found the great forest outside engulfed in flame. The instant he realized it had happened because of his own careless words, an arrow had struck his back, and he'd fallen. The one who'd loosed it was a girl who looked young, and before his eyes the strength had drained from his body, his whole frame convulsing.
Coughing blood-foam, suffering, listening to Rem's voice as she rushed to his side, he'd tried to hold on to his fading consciousness—

"Here, I..."

"Ah? You still messing around..."

"—Whoa, whoa, calm down! We don't know anything yet! Can't be helped! More importantly, look—let's get that blindfold off him!"

"—Ah"

His thoughts, caught in a whirlpool of chaos, were pulled back to reality by the conversation happening above him.
Two men's voices. One was rough, the embodiment of coarseness and violence given voice; the other was soft, with the impression of a good-natured man smoothing things over.

—Both owners of those voices rose naturally to the surface of Subaru's mind, faces and all.

"Tch."

A click of the tongue, footsteps, and the man who'd kicked Subaru moved away. Right after, he heard a sigh mixing exasperation with wry amusement, an "ah well" kind of sound.

"Sorry about the sudden treatment. I bet you're feeling completely lost right now, but I'm gonna take the blindfold off, okay? Can't undo the ropes on your hands and feet though, so bear with that."

"――――"

Saying this, the man who approached removed the blindfold wound around Subaru's head.
A slight sting came with the sense of release, but before he could savor it, Subaru took a deep breath, once, twice, and finally held it briefly.
Then he waited for his vision to slowly return before opening his eyes.
And then—

"...Just as I thought."

As his blurred sight gradually sharpened, what spread before Subaru was canvas tents, campfires, and Imperial soldiers bustling busily about.
Not quite familiar, but a recognizable Imperial encampment—the borrowed ground where Subaru had run east and west doing chores.

"――――"

There was nothing strange about the sight itself.
After all, this was the camp where Subaru had collapsed from the arrow wound in the first place. He'd been treated while unconscious; there was nothing odd about waking here.
But to accept that explanation, the initial shock—the fact that a shoe had been shoved into his mouth—and the fact that his hands and feet were still bound now, needed accounting for.
However unfamiliar Subaru was with Imperial customs, if it was standard practice to bind a patient's hands and feet every time they were treated, he wished that nation ill.
Which meant, if that wasn't the case—

"I died, huh."

—That he'd undergone another Return by Death was the most natural conclusion to draw from this situation.
And, ironically, the way to confirm it came to him easily. All he had to do was turn his head and look at the green forest beyond the camp.

The jungle that Subaru's own careless words had caused to burn, permitting the Imperial soldiers' outrage.
The forest that should have been wreathed in flame and belching black smoke was, however, right there. Green jungle stretching left and right, filling the horizon, alive and well.

"――――"

Confirming that, the memory rose sharply in Subaru's mind.
Rem's voice, her presence, her plea—crawling desperately to his side as he lay collapsed from the arrow, spitting blood-foam, begging him not to die.
But Subaru had betrayed her. He had died, wretchedly, right in front of her.
In an unknown land, a man she knew—hated though she'd been—had died, and amnesiac Rem must have felt unimaginable anxiety and terror. Just imagining it made his chest feel like it would tear open.
And at the same time, he thought—never again did he want to make her feel that way.

"Found you right when we went to fetch water. Sorry, but you're our prisoner now."

So, with that fierce resolve held in his chest, a man crouched down in front of Subaru.
He knew the man with the soft smile and the drooping eyes. —Todd.

In this camp full of Imperial soldiers, the one person who'd treated Subaru and Rem with any friendliness.
The man who'd patiently put up with Subaru's utter ignorance of the Empire—Subaru had even thought it lucky that Todd was the first to take him in.
Until Todd pushed the decision to burn the forest, using Subaru's words as justification.

"――――"

By his own sense of it, not even ten minutes had passed, but Todd's carefree report sent a chill down his spine. The moment they'd judged it too difficult to enter the forest to subjugate the "People of Shudrak"—their unit's actual objective—the Imperial soldiers had burned the forest without mercy. And they'd lavishly praised Subaru's contribution as the deciding piece of information behind that choice.

In the Sacred Vollachia Empire, the strong were revered and the weak trampled.
A great nation whose symbol was a wolf pierced by a sword, teaching that only the "Sword-Wolves" had the right to live. A mentality like Todd's might not be so rare in the Empire.
Hearing there were Witchbeasts in the woods and immediately deciding to torch the forest fit that mindset well.
But of course, that wouldn't sit right with Subaru, nor with anyone from the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica. Maybe Roswaal, that lump of pure pragmatism, would be the exception.
Either way—this time, he mustn't let the forest be burned to scorched earth.

"――――"

Swallowing hard, Subaru counted the previous course of events as a failure.
Not just that he'd been killed, but that torching the entire forest, even as a safety measure, had been far too much.
If not for Subaru's careless words, Todd and the others might have arranged a peaceful parley with the "People of Shudrak" and settled things without bloodshed.

He'd stolen that possibility, put the people living in the forest in danger—no, enough making excuses. There was no way that great fire hadn't claimed victims.
Natsuki Subaru, through his own careless words, had taken the lives of people living in that forest.
Even if a Return by Death meant he could no longer intervene in the events of that timeline, he couldn't escape that fact. —Because Subaru would never forget it.

"――――"

So Subaru resolved not to repeat that same mistake twice.
Death was heavy—whether his own or someone else's—and it must never be allowed to repeat. Given that, being able to redo his first meeting with Todd and Jamal here was a small mercy amid misfortune.

The Return by Death restart point had shifted; after being knocked unconscious by Rem on the plains, he'd escaped the hunter in the forest and the Witchbeast's onslaught, then leapt right back to the moment right after being taken in at the Imperial camp.
From here, he'd build a friendly relationship with Todd, and if possible Jamal too, steering them toward negotiations with the forest's "People of Shudrak" without letting things break down.
To that end—

"You following what I'm saying? I get that suddenly being told you're a prisoner is confusing..."

"—. No, yeah. I'm confused. I'm confused, but, um..."

Crouched before the silent Subaru, Todd showed consideration for his state of mind. Taking in that attitude, Subaru mulled over how to choose his next words.
Last time, his relationship with Todd had been good. He should maintain that rapport while continuing to angle for favors for himself and Rem.
On top of that, he needed to carefully select what information to share, in order to restrain Todd's more extreme impulses.

"I was surprised, but I get that I'm a prisoner. I remember jumping into the river too. If you pulled me out, then you guys saved my life—"

"—Wait."

"Huh?"

Subaru, having roughly assessed the situation, tried to affect the demeanor of someone plausibly fished out of a river.
But Todd cut off his words, thrusting a palm toward his face.
His five spread fingers and palm blocked Subaru's vision, and he instinctively held his breath.
And then—

"—Why'd you just look at me like that?"

Right after Todd's voice came, cold and hard, something sharp drove into Subaru's right shoulder.
With his vision blocked, Subaru's reaction to the man's movement came too late; he only understood what had happened once he looked toward the strange sensation in his right shoulder.

—The sharp tip of a knife had been driven into Subaru's right shoulder.

"—!?"

The instant he registered what had happened, Subaru's throat let out a soundless scream.
A tremendous burning sensation exploded, centered on his right shoulder, and his whole body went rigid and numb with sharp pain. He had to stretch his body taut to disperse that pain throughout himself, or his brain—unable to absorb misfortune beyond its capacity—might simply burst.

That's how immense the shock of the unexpected stab wound was.

"Gyaaaaaaah—!!"

Following the soundless scream, seconds later, came a clear, pain-driven wail.
He wanted to writhe, to clutch or press at his gouged shoulder, to take some action—any action—to hold back the pain. But Subaru's hands and feet were restrained; he couldn't even pull out the knife lodged in him. It hurt, it hurt, it hurt, it hurt.

"Oi! What's with that scream?!"

As Subaru writhed in agony, screaming again and again, the one who came rushing in, alarmed by the extraordinary sound, was a rough-looking man with an eyepatch—Jamal.
Jamal, who up until moments ago had been the one kicking and beating Subaru, widened his eyes at the sight of Subaru with a knife sprouting from his right shoulder, screaming as though coughing blood.
And then—

"Todd, this ain't what you told me! You said not to touch these guys 'cause they had a noble's knife on 'em, wasn't that you?!"

"Yeah, that was the plan while we didn't know who they were. Fair enough for you to be mad that things changed. My mistake, my mistake."

"...So you're saying you figured out who this guy is?"

Jamal, who'd raised his voice, calmed down somewhat at Todd's answer. But at Jamal's question, Todd only tilted his head and said, "Who knows?"
Then Todd, standing, placed his foot on the body of the fallen, groaning Subaru.

"No idea who or where he's from since I haven't asked. But there's a good chance he's an enemy. So, preemptive strike."

"Guh, gyaaaaah—!"

"Ohh, that looks painful. Not much point making you suffer too much, but say, are you good with pain? What's your own self-assessment on that front?"

Todd asked this placidly, shifting his weight onto the foot resting on Subaru's body.
But Subaru, sprawled on the ground, had the knife in his shoulder pushed deeper into the wound by Todd's weight, and had no room to answer amid the unbearable reprise of agony.

His vision flashed, his throat burned, and a blood-choked scream tore out of him again and again.
No matter how many times he repeated it, the pain showed no sign of easing. It never faded, but struck him again and again as fresh despair, like waves that came in and washed back out.

"No answer. Defiant, huh. Definitely an enemy then."

"...Rolling him around like that, how's he supposed to answer anything?"

"Hm? That so? Damn, my bad. Since I don't feel pain much myself, I always screw up this kind of thing without meaning to. My mistake, my mistake."

Saying this, Todd finally lifted his foot off Subaru's body.
The added pain vanished, but the intermittent pain continued stabbing at him, and blood flowed from his mouth where he'd bitten down against the unbearable agony.
Tears streamed from the corners of his eyes too, and pitifully, he wasn't even allowed to wipe them away.

"You... and here you were, lecturing me about my conduct..."

"—? You beating up prisoners and subordinates for stress relief is one thing, isn't it? Don't play the gentleman with me now, that's a bad joke. I'm just doing what's necessary."

Meanwhile, ignoring Subaru's wretched state, the men continued their conversation above him.
Jamal placating Todd over the matter of stabbing Subaru—a scene that would have been unthinkable a little while ago.

What on earth was happening here?
Even setting aside that his resources were devoted to enduring the pain, his thoughts refused to come together.
Todd, who'd been so good to Subaru and Rem, why was he doing something like this—

"So, why'd you stab him?"

As if by coincidence, Jamal voiced the very question in Subaru's mind, directing it at Todd.
Asked this, Todd closed one eye and looked over at the writhing Subaru.

"When he took off the blindfold and saw me, his eyes tried to work me. Anxiety or tension, I get. Fear or crying, fine. —But trying to work someone, that's off."

"Work you, huh."

"Would someone who's out cold, kicked awake, blindfold ripped off, make eyes like they're gonna use whatever face they see first? Maybe some types would. But a guy like that's too scary to deal with. Better to just kill him quick."

Against the thoughtful Jamal, Todd laid out his reasoning with unwavering logic.
The reason he'd stabbed Subaru: he'd judged Subaru dangerous. And the best way to deal with a dangerous person was to neutralize them.
Because Todd had stabbed Subaru's right shoulder—knowing full well his left hand's fingers were broken and useless.

Stabbed in the right shoulder, fingers broken on the left hand—the function of both of Subaru's arms was severely compromised.
Todd had simply made that call and executed it instantly, to secure his own safety.
—Simply. What kind of word was that.

"You don't know he was thinking that the second he woke up though. Maybe he woke up while being carried and was listening in on us the whole time..."

"Nah, that's not it. I was watching the whole time to see if he was faking sleep. If he'd regained consciousness at some point, there'd have been some kind of physiological reaction. And if I somehow missed him faking it..."

"If you did?"

"Then that means he was deceiving us with a fake sleep act while plotting to work me. That's even scarier. Killing him would've been the right call after all."

Hearing this, Subaru drew a sharp, dread-filled breath even as he fought the pain.
Listening to Todd's explanation, Jamal's tone gradually softened. What had begun as shock and anger at the fact he'd stabbed Subaru was, bit by bit, being dismantled.
There was something bewitching in Todd's voice and manner of speaking, something that softened whoever he spoke to.

Observing from a third-party position, Subaru finally understood it.
Todd would draw close to the other person, show them understanding, and then offer up an appealing proposal that benefited them too. With their initial momentum killed and their minds softened, the other person would end up examining that offered proposal, unable to resist tasting it.

What was happening to Jamal right now, before his very eyes, was exactly that.
And it was also what had happened to Subaru himself, right before his Return by Death.

"Those young ladies traveling with him are hard to figure too, but the other two are simple, easy to deal with. Let's remove the disease before it becomes a hassle."

"Well, I don't mind that, but..."

"Or would you rather do it yourself? You held back when you were furious over your subordinate getting done in by the blue-haired girl—you could use someone to work your frustration out on."

"—That's true too."

At Todd's placid proposal, Jamal's face split into a vulgar grin.
Then, cracking his knuckles, Jamal walked past Todd and advanced on Subaru. That aura of violence Jamal wore made Subaru's stomach turn, but what truly sent a shiver through his core was Todd's words.

That Todd had offered Subaru up as an outlet for Jamal's frustration was one thing, but even more telling: Todd intended to keep probing at their intentions through other means, even after eliminating Subaru here.
For that, Rem, and Rui too, presumably, would be used.
But no matter what they were asked, neither of those two could answer. No matter how brutal the torture, there was nothing Rem could reveal.
Which meant—

"—"

Clenching his back teeth hard, Subaru used the taste of blood in his mouth as fuel, searching for some way to keep this from happening to Rem.
In a few more seconds, Jamal's violence would resume, and under an unrestrained, savage assault, Subaru would be killed or left half-dead. Even if Jamal somehow possessed a conscience, Todd wouldn't let Subaru live.
A man capable of setting the forest ablaze wouldn't hesitate to snuff out Subaru's life either.

One step, two steps, Jamal drew closer.
In that span, Subaru heated his thinking to its absolute limit, searching within himself for the answer he needed. Some way to break this situation open, some possibility, some miracle worth grasping—

"Scream nice and loud for my stress relief, brat. And for your woman's share too, you'll pay it off with your own bod—"

"—'People of Shudrak.'"

"――――"

Just as Jamal, spouting the standard threats of a rough thug, was about to unleash violence on Subaru—Subaru's lips wove that phrase.
Instantly, Jamal's movement stopped, and behind him, Todd's expression shifted too.
Jamal's face showed clear surprise; Todd raised an eyebrow, then—

"Heh."

He laughed.

"'People of Shudrak'... playing that card here, huh. Looks like you know how to gamble."

"Todd, this brat's talk ain't worth—"

"Wait, wait, Jamal. Punching and kicking can wait till later. But it's hard to make out what a guy's saying once his tongue or throat's wrecked. Best to listen first."

"Damn it!"

With nowhere to vent his anger, Jamal kicked a nearby wooden fence violently.
Watching that literal stress relief out of the corner of his eye, Todd turned back to Subaru. The smile plastered on his face was, terrifyingly, exactly the same as the one Subaru knew.
The same smile Todd had worn while treating Subaru's fingers, inviting him to a meal, praising his contribution to burning the forest—now Todd wore it while Subaru teetered on the brink of life and death.

"So, you brought up the 'People of Shudrak.' Can I hope you've got something to say that'll make us happy?"

"...Yeah. I know where the 'People of Shudrak' in the forest are."

"—! Oh, that's good!"

Delight seeped into his smile, and Todd clapped his hands together in front of his chest.
Seeing his reaction, Subaru became certain that the "miracle" he'd wrenched out of himself had succeeded in pushing back the imminent crisis.
But at the same time, this was a double-edged sword for Subaru too.

Because Subaru had no idea where the "People of Shudrak" actually were.
The masked man who'd given him the knife, and the hunter who'd shot arrows at him—he guessed one or both of them were connected to the "People of Shudrak," but he had no means of contacting them, nor any concrete idea of their location.

—In other words, this was a huge gamble, staked on Subaru's own life.

"So why do you know where the 'People of Shudrak' are?"

"...Because I'm one of them."

"I see, figured as much. Skin color aside, black hair, right? That's why I had a hunch. Shudrak having black hair is common knowledge."

"――――"

Handed a piece of information he'd known nothing about, Subaru, saved by sheer luck, felt deep relief.
Cold sweat beaded across his whole body, adding to the tension of this negotiation. The pain in his shoulder was only growing worse, and in sympathy, the fingers of his left hand were beginning to ache too.
On top of that, his body hadn't recovered from the toll of the Pleiades Watchtower incident, nor the clash with Rem in the forest afterward, nor the flight across the great river.

He had to hold his wavering consciousness and fraying spirit together, and keep facing this question-and-answer where a wrong step meant death. —All to survive, and rescue Rem.

To return to Emilia and Beatrice and the others, to reunite Ram and Rem.

"So then, your position would be scout, I take it. The clothes and that knife too—prepared to blend in with us?"

"—. Stole the knife from a traveler. Same with the clothes. And..."

"You were trying to probe our situation. Pretty bold plan. And you really were drowning, apparently—there was a chance the knife wouldn't even have been noticed..."

"But that made it more convincing, didn't it?"

When pointed out that the plan was slapdash, Subaru boldly grinned, claiming it was deliberate.
Twisting the corner of his mouth, baring his teeth, making his already bad look even worse. A stance meant to lend his words persuasive force.
Indeed, hearing this, Todd narrowed his eyes, weighing the true intent behind the statement.

"――――"

That silence weighed heavily, slowly strangling Subaru's life like silk thread.
Honestly, whether his words carried any persuasive force, whether his expression and manner of speaking backed that persuasion up—the pain and strain made it impossible to judge at all.

It seemed entirely possible that the next moment, he'd be dismissed as ridiculous, sneered at, and have his skull split open for offering such a laughable excuse.
The fact that he couldn't gauge Todd's stance only added to the tension.
And then, after a while—

"—So you'd sell out your tribe to beg for your life?"

Asked this by Todd, one eye closed, Subaru swallowed.
He'd drawn out the words he wanted drawn out. All that was left was not to get the answer wrong.

Sell out the tribe to beg for his life.
Subaru had claimed to be one of the "People of Shudrak," and now he'd sell out the "People of Shudrak."
He mustn't let this be seen as a bluff. On top of that, he had to make the right face, the right voice.

—The face and voice of a wretched, servile man selling out his own tribe to beg for his life.

"...Y-yeah, that's right. I'll sell out my own kind."

"――――"

"Please, I'll do anything. Hell, I'll even lure them out for you. Anything, anything at all!"

Stammering, cheeks twitching, drenched in cold sweat, Subaru begged for his life.
Clinging to his own life, treating others' lives as worthless—he needed to become a self-serving being that no one could ever look on with favor.

Nothing to it, really.
He'd seen plenty of models for that kind of humanity in his days spent in this world.
Though he'd never imagined the day would come when the Sin Archbishops—the worst of humanity—would become his models.

"Pathetic bastard. Don't like that look on your face one bit."

Hearing Subaru's plea for his life, Jamal spat out genuine contempt and anger.
At least, Subaru's behavior seemed to have steered things in a bad direction with Jamal. But too bad—there was no room left to deal with Jamal's feelings.

Right now, the one who held the right to decide Subaru's treatment here was Todd.
Actual rank or standing meant nothing here. This was the Imperial way—the strong trampling the weak—and that was exactly the custom being followed.

"—. That desperation, doesn't seem like a lie."

And at that, to Subaru who maintained his servile expression through sheer effort, Todd finally said this.
Hearing it, Subaru, fearing a false hope, felt a sense that his neck had been spared, if barely. At the same time, it was Jamal who reacted to this with displeasure.

"Oi, Todd! You gonna believe this bastard? A scum like this who'd sell out his own tribe to save his own skin, his word ain't worth—"

"Wait, wait, what are you saying, Jamal. This is the kind of thing a man desperate enough to sell out his own tribe for himself would say. Of course he'll desperately show us the benefit. Otherwise he can't grab hold of that precious life of his."

"~~!"

Flaring up, Jamal found the words that would have followed sealed off by Todd's.
That was precisely the low estimation of a servile man that Subaru had wanted Todd to hold. A man who'd sell out even his comrades' lives to save himself. To make them believe the information he offered had value, Subaru's own worth had to be dragged down to the bottom.
In that sense, being stabbed in the shoulder and screaming so pathetically had probably worked in his favor.
Though comforting himself with that felt like the pain was too great a price to pay.

"I don't give a damn what happens after this!"

In the end, just as Subaru had hoped, Jamal was talked around by Todd.
Chiding him even as he did it, Todd said "believe it, believe it," patting his shoulder.

"No doubt about it, he's clinging to life. —Whether it's his own, that's another question."

He said this, watching Subaru bow his head servilely.

△▼△▼△▼△

"—! Wait! What are you going to do with him?!"

So shouted Rem, gripping the iron bars of the cage, her eyes blazing with fury.
The place was the Imperial encampment. Having heard out Subaru's servile plea, Todd and Jamal were now leading him away, about to leave the camp.

In doing so, they had to pass by the cage where Rem, who'd fought back when captured, had been locked up—which was how her furious shout at the start had come about.

"――――"

Glared at by Rem, Subaru was in a truly wretched state.
Pulled from the river, his half-dried clothes hung heavy on him, and the stab wound in his right shoulder had received only the crudest first aid. His broken left-hand fingers had gone untreated entirely, and though his leg shackles had been removed, his handcuffs still robbed him of freedom.

Natsuki Subaru, looking every bit like a slave, was being led out of the camp.
The confusion and shock Rem must be feeling at the sight—it had to be chaos itself. Of course, Subaru had no way to explain the circumstances to her.
With the Return by Death, whatever slight closeness he and Rem might have managed to build had been reset; everything had to be redone from scratch.
Rem regarded Subaru with hostility, blaming him for the Witch's Lingering Scent, and Subaru had been given no time or opportunity to clear up that misunderstanding. On top of that—

"—! It's even worse than before, that smell... what is this?"

Catching the Miasma that grew heavier with each Return by Death, Rem's guard rose another notch.
From her perspective, Natsuki Subaru must have looked like the very embodiment of evil.
And since there was no point in correcting that impression beyond sparing Subaru's own feelings, there was no action he could take here to dispel the misunderstanding.

"...Quite the complicated relationship, huh? That young lady was supposed to be your companion, and yet she's like that."

"That girl... no, the two who were with me are—"

"The two?"

"—. I bought them both, as tools to make contact with you people."

Hesitating for a moment over how to answer, Subaru forced out the words with feigned callousness.
Right now, Subaru was a cold-blooded man who'd sell out his companions to save his own precious life. Naturally, he needed to show zero affection for either Rem or Rui—only pure, hundred-percent self-preservation.
He had to prevent the two of them—Rem, above all—from being taken hostage.

"So they're two people who know nothing. Asking them anything would be pointless... though you seemed to have already guessed as much."

"Well, it didn't look like an act, did it? The little one seems genuinely out of her mind, and the other one wasn't lying either. Though that's its own problem—even she doesn't seem sure why she's fighting back."

"Tch."

Todd wryly grinned, scratching his cheek, while Jamal clicked his tongue in irritation.
Leading them, along with Subaru, out of the camp were about twenty Imperial soldiers. From here, Subaru would guide them toward the settlement of the "People of Shudrak" in the forest.
He'd tell Todd and the others its location, contributing to the Empire's victory. And as a reward, they'd spare his life, putting into practice the old saying that the wicked flourish.

As a success story for a servile man, this was roughly a passing grade.
If one overlooked the fact that it was impossible, that is.

"Wouldn't it be smarter to just ditch them? You went to the trouble of buying them, though—if you don't need them, I could take them off your hands after the job's done..."

"Whoa, getting ahead of yourself there. Being forward-thinking's fine, but don't forget whether you survive depends on doing your job right. Worrying about those girls' treatment is way down the list compared to worrying about your own skin."

"Heh, right, right. Guess I let my greed get ahead of me, thinking about after it's all done."

If he pushed back here, his attachment to Rem would show, so Subaru switched tactics at once.
He couldn't rub his hands together thanks to the cuffs, but he responded to Todd with that kind of air in spirit. Fortunately, Todd didn't press further, waving a hand at Rem in the cage.

"Behave yourself, missy. For now, if nothing happens, nothing'll happen to you."

"Do you really think words like that carry any weight?"

"Dunno about weight, but whether you believe it or not is your problem."

Told this by Todd, Rem fell into a frustrated silence.
Regretting that he could offer no supporting fire, nor any further words, Subaru burned Rem's face and voice into his memory, fuel to stoke the fire in his own heart.

—He had to somehow get Rem out of the Imperial camp, and escape.

Subaru no longer entertained any thought of building a friendly relationship with Todd and the others to have them protect him and Rem. —They were dangerous, whether as enemies or allies.
Even as allies they couldn't be controlled, and the danger of them as enemies went without saying.
More fundamentally, he ought to avoid getting too cozy with people of the Vollachian Empire in the first place.

Subaru and the others' position was an extremely complicated one—one that, handled poorly, might not remain a matter of personal concern at all.

"Alright, let's move out. Everyone, stay sharp."

"I'm the one who gives the orders!"

Bringing along Subaru, who braced his resolve at that thought, Todd called out to his men.
Amid the Imperial soldiers' responses, Jamal bellowed, anger plain on his face.

△▼△▼△▼△

—After that, with Subaru in the lead, the group pushed into the Buddheim Jungle.

Their objective: the settlement of the "People of Shudrak" living in the forest, and pinpointing its location. —Except Subaru, their navigator, was bluffing; he had no idea where the settlement was.
In fact, this whole gamble rested on shaky ground even as to the true nature of the "People of Shudrak."

"――――"

Advancing through the dense forest, Subaru watched carefully for any chance to slip away.
His opponents outnumbered him, but even in light armor, they were equipped in ways that made them less nimble than Subaru. If he could seize an opening and flee, he might make it back to the camp before them and get Rem out. In truth, that was about the only plan he had right now.

Fortunately, the cage Rem was held in could be unlocked easily from the outside.
What mattered was Subaru getting hold of that key, and whether Rem would trust him enough to come along once he opened the cage. And then there was Rui to consider.

"If I say I'm leaving her behind, there's no way Rem comes with me..."

Subaru had already eroded Rem's trust enough just from the added Miasma of another Return by Death.
Even if he desperately scrambled back into her good graces from this state, Rem wouldn't welcome a decision to abandon Rui. If anything, there was a real possibility she'd beat Subaru down on the spot, take Rui back herself, and leave Subaru behind in the Imperial camp.

"...No, even Rem wouldn't do that. Not with her legs out of commission."

Only because her legs weren't working could she not act on it—if her body were in perfect condition, he suspected she just might. He suspected it, but having that happen to him would be a huge problem.
So if he was going to get them out, he'd have to bring Rui along too. There was no escaping this troublesome negative factor, this shackle he couldn't shed no matter what.
And then—

"So, how long to the settlement?"

"...About two, three hours, I'd guess."

"Two, three hours! That's pretty fast. If that's all it takes, that's a stroke of luck. From our end, this was supposed to be a mission that could take years."

Getting his answer, Todd's cheeks relaxed with good fortune.
Equipped in light armor, a hand-axe hanging at his hip, Todd's words reminded Subaru that he'd left a fiancée behind to take part in this mission.
Of course, that background didn't change no matter how Todd chose to injure Subaru here.

Todd, as an Imperial soldier, had been dispatched into the jungle under orders, forced to be apart from his fiancée because of it.
The same was true of the other soldiers, Jamal included. They all had circumstances of their own, their own reasons for carrying out this mission.

In that sense, there was no real reason for Subaru and Todd's group to be at odds.
Just plain bad luck. Misfortune piled on misfortune. The wind hadn't blown their way. Stringing together enough words, that's what it amounted to.
Still—

"...Not that it's my place to say, but isn't this reckless? Heading to the 'Shudrak settlement' with this few people?"

Walking at the front in his role as guide, Subaru spoke to Todd just behind him.
The Imperial party numbered about twenty; even with a guide, that was hardly enough manpower to search this vast forest properly.
At Subaru's question, Todd shrugged. "Can't be helped," he said.

"You're a fine source, but the higher-ups aren't expecting that much from your story. Only once we confirm it ourselves does the information actually gain value. Besides..."

"Besides?"

"If this is a trap of yours and we don't come back, well, us not coming back tells our friends back at camp that going into the forest is dangerous. Ha, long live Vollachia."

"'—Long live Vollachia!'"

Accepting sacrifice as unavoidable for victory, the Imperial soldiers echoed Todd's easygoing tone in chorus.
"Long live Vollachia," a cry that shook the forest air, seemed proof of just how devoted these Imperial soldiers were to a nation that revered such strength.
Either way—

"You in any position to worry about us, huh? No guarantee your head's still attached to your body once this is over. Best mind your manners with us."

"Mr. Jamal..."

"Don't you say my name, you low-down traitor. We'll chop you up and toss you, and the women—yeah, maybe offer 'em up to that skirt-chasing Second General Zikr. Bet he'd be thrilled."

"Jamal... enough with the nonsense already. It's exhausting."

Whatever it was about Subaru that rubbed him so wrong, Jamal's gaze and voice stayed openly hostile toward him.
Given that, and given his poor relationship with Rem too, letting Jamal hold the deciding vote on the situation could settle into the worst possible outcome for Subaru.
It was Todd, as ever, who soothed Jamal, telling him to calm down.

"More like, don't forget it's us who has to keep this guy happy. This guy gives us information that benefits us because he's scared for his life. In exchange, we spare his. Otherwise, the moment we reach the settlement, the Shudrak'll swarm us all at once."

"Fine by me. If they try that, we'll wipe them all out..."

"—You serious? I'm not signing up for a fight with no chance of winning. You wouldn't want to make your sister a widow before she's even married, would you? Right, brother-in-law-to-be Jamal?"

"Guh..."

Todd talked down the often thoughtless Jamal like that.
Subaru caught a glimpse of a relationship between the two he hadn't known about, but unfortunately he had no room to feel anything about it. He'd already made his 'resolve' regarding Todd and the others.

—The meeting, the wind, the luck—all of it had been bad.

"—But Rem matters more to me."

So Subaru had decided to bring harm to Todd and his men.

"—?"

Suddenly, near the rear of the search party's formation, one of the Imperial soldiers made a small sound in his throat. He turned his head as if noticing something, searching his field of view for the cause.
Chasing a faint but unignorable sense of wrongness, his gaze wandered restlessly through the jungle, and then—

"—Ah?"

In the darkness of the jungle, a faint red point of light bloomed, and his "eyes" met it.

"Witchbeast!!"

"—!?"

The instant their eyes met, the soldier who'd spotted it immediately warned his comrades.
His reaction up to that point was flawless; no fault lay with him. Nor with his comrades, who fell into a combat stance at once in response.

But the moment he drew his sword and charged straight at the Witchbeast—
—That, clearly, was overeager.

"Ohhhhh!!"

The soldier who'd drawn his blade tore through the forest with a roar and leapt at his target: a giant snake writhing its huge body—a serpentine Witchbeast some ten meters long, covered in green scales.
The same species Subaru had encountered in the forest before, its appearance was a genuine bolt from the blue for Imperial soldiers who hadn't known Witchbeasts lurked in the woods.
But trying to deal with the Witchbeast's danger immediately, though the correct instinct, was a mistake all the same. —Because the Witchbeast's target wasn't the Imperial soldiers.

Its target was none other than Natsuki Subaru, wrapped in a heavy cloak of Miasma.

"――――!!"

The blade struck scale, and roaring, the great snake sent the soldier flying with a sweep of its massive tail. Its red eyes glinted with ferocity, and a tremendous roar slammed into the group.

A year spent as Witchbeast bait had taught Subaru, a professional of the trade: in most cases, a Witchbeast would actively target him for his Miasma, but the moment it was harmed by someone, that changed. That held true whether it was the White Whale or a great serpent.

The White Whale had gone after Wilhelm, who cut it to pieces; the great serpent turned its fangs on the hunter who'd shot it.
That same principle applied here. The serpent's target shifted from Subaru to the uniformed Imperial soldiers who'd shown it hostility.

"—Wait, a Witchbeast!? Nobody told me about this!!"

Targeted by the roaring serpent's hostility, Jamal drew his twin swords, voice shaking with rage. And then, surprisingly, he charged at the Witchbeast with bold, agile movements that hinted at real skill.
But Subaru couldn't afford to cheer on Jamal's unexpected valiant fight.

Subaru had been counting on exactly this—it was why he'd walked the forest with Todd and the others for hours.

They knew nothing of the Witchbeast's existence, while the Witchbeast was drawn to Subaru's Miasma. Using his own body odor and Witchbeasts together was routine for him by now—Subaru was a proper "Witchbeast handler" in his own right.
When he reunited with Meili, he ought to challenge her for that title, given his track record.
But right now, there was no time to dwell on that.

"Right now—"

Taking advantage of this opening, he'd get back to the Imperial camp and free Rem.
Just as he moved to dash off toward that goal, a chill ran up the back of Subaru's neck. Following that instinct, he ducked his head without a shred of dignity.
An instant later, an axe swept through the exact spot where his head had just been and buried itself in a large tree right beside him.

"—"

If he hadn't ducked just then, he'd be dead.
Shuddering at that realization, Subaru glanced back just with his eyes to see the culprit.

"――――"

His gaze crossed with Todd's, who was glaring at him with sharp eyes.

"—"

Refusing to be caught by that gaze, Subaru broke into a full sprint through the forest.
If he stopped, Todd would catch him. And if caught, Todd would certainly take his life. There had been an unshakable resolve in those eyes. A pitch-black will.

He'd felt a different kind of fear than he had with the Sin Archbishops, or Witchbeasts, or even Reid Astrea.
Those eyes were colored by relentless, tenacious obsession. —Pitch-black murderous intent.

"Gah!?"

As Subaru ran, something hard and heavy pierced his back.
Unable to turn to look, what had struck his back near the shoulder blade was a thrown knife—ironically, one that had made its way back into Subaru's possession in the worst possible way.
Leaving it dangling from his shoulder, Subaru gasped for ragged breath and fled desperately.

Behind him, he could still sense the fight between the Witchbeast and the Imperial soldiers continuing, but Subaru was desperate too—desperate not to be caught by another Witchbeast, desperate not to be caught by Todd.

Desperately, desperately, desperately he ran, ran, kept running.
Breath ragged, coughing blood, stumbling again and again, actually falling more than once, covering himself in mud, he kept running, desperate to make it back to the Imperial camp.

"Rem... Rem... R-rem..."

Reduced to a pace slower than a child's walk, gasping through a parched throat, oxygen and stamina and willpower all nearly spent, Subaru clung to the one thing he was still attached to.

Get to Rem, get her out, save her, and go back to everyone.
Back to where Emilia and Beatrice, Ram and Petra and Frederica, Garfiel and Otto, and Roswaal too were waiting, and then give Rem back her gentle time.
The time that girl should have had, the precious time, and so—

"—Ah"

Reaching for that fragile dream, Subaru's foot clawed at empty air.
Suddenly the ground beneath him vanished, and with no free arm to catch his collapsing balance, he plunged headfirst.
Down, headfirst, somewhere. He couldn't even scream. His throat wouldn't open.

He just kept falling.
Falling, tumbling down, and just like that, faintly, like a bubble popping, his dream broke apart too.

"—Rem."

A hoarse voice leaked out, futile, and Subaru's consciousness cut off there.

"—How long do you intend to sleep, you fool?"

"Chokobi—!?"

From the pitch-black depths, a sudden impact yanked his consciousness up.
The blow landed on the side of his head—the sensation of someone stepping on his head as he lay sideways—no, not "sensation." That's exactly what had happened.
He'd apparently had the right side of his head against the floor, so the left temple that got stepped on and the right temple pressed into the ground were both assaulted at once. Thanks to that, the sharp pain jolted his consciousness fully awake—

"...Huh, wha...? I'm... guh—"

Feeling the taste of blood in the back of his throat, Subaru dazedly sat himself up.
Instantly, agony flared through his right shoulder, his back, his left hand and legs, everywhere across his body.

His vision flashed red with tremendous agony, and Subaru laid his raised body back down, shuddering like a fish pulled onto land.
Pain that adrenaline had let him partly ignore had come rushing back the moment he'd blacked out. But if this many places hurt this much—

"...I'm not dead."

"Obviously. Do the dead talk? The state you were in just now was frankly more entertaining than the antics of a half-baked clown. I shall commend you for it."

"Ah?"

Startled, still checking himself over, Subaru muttered without thinking—and an arrogant voice responded.
Once the meaning of those words caught up with him, Subaru, wary of the pain he expected to return, slowly raised his body again.
Looking around, he found he'd been laid on bare earth, with thick branches driven upright all around—no, this was a wooden cage.

Rem's face, replayed in his memory countless times, flashed before him, and Subaru realized he was locked inside a wooden cage. Realizing it, his mind spun with confusion.
Had he failed to escape and been caught by Todd and the others after all, he panicked for a moment—but—

"Don't rush so. Your pursuers aren't here. Though I confess it's an awkward situation in which to tell you to be at ease."

"You're..."

"I never imagined I'd see your face again. —Natsuki Subaru."

Calling Subaru's name as he stared, rigid, the figure twisted the corner of their mouth into a grin.
Since only the eyes were visible above the mask, it could only be inferred that they were smiling—but smile they did.

—Just like Subaru, locked inside a cage, the man laughed with arrogance.
