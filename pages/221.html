Arc 4 Chapter 55: "The Girl in the Crystal"

Seeing the girl trapped inside the crystal, Subaru found himself so entranced that he forgot, for a moment, to breathe.

That was how much the sight before him shook his heart — a terrible, beautiful thing.

Inside the translucent, faintly blue-tinged crystal, a girl sat trapped, her posture like someone kneeling with her knees folded beneath her.

"Frozen in ice" was the nearest way to put it, but unlike ice, which melts and releases what it holds, this crystal would last forever unless shattered.

And to shatter this crystal would, without question, mean shattering the body of the girl sealed within.

"Why... would anyone do this?"

The words slipped out in a murmur, carrying a faint edge of anger.

Subaru felt it quietly, but unmistakably — fury.

The girl trapped inside the crystal would never, under any circumstances, be freed from it unharmed. He had no idea what intent the person who'd done this had, nor what relationship they'd had with this girl. He didn't know — but it clawed viciously at his heart all the same.

"It's not... Ryuzu-san, is it?"

He stepped further into the room. Unlike his memory of before, the floor held no gaping hole this time — instead, the crystal stood enshrined at the very spot where the hole should have been.

The tall, vertical crystal was supported by an iron pedestal, and that fixture alone seemed well cared for, gleaming like something brand new amid a facility that was otherwise practically a ruin.

Facing the crystal stood a row of desks — the same ones, Subaru realized, that in the previous world had been smashed and left lying in the corner of the room.

The unlit room was, as before, thickly carpeted with moss that gave off a faint glow from the walls, providing just enough visibility to see by.

The tools and what looked like medical equipment scattered along the walls were still intact. Searching for discrepancies against his memory, Subaru arrived at one conclusion.

"This is the place that gets destroyed sometime in the next six days — whatever was being done here, wiped away so nobody can ever know."

Someone existed who found it inconvenient for this place to be discovered.

That someone, sometime in the days before the Great Rabbit's coming assault on the Sanctuary, intended to destroy this facility's crucial parts, dispose of the evidence, and bury it in darkness.

This time, Subaru had managed to reach here first.

"The prime suspect... is still Garfiel, I guess."

Subaru's whole search this time had started from his unease over Garfiel's suspicious behavior.

If Garfiel — with his fierce will to protect the Sanctuary and his barely-hidden affection for Ryuzu — had some fact Subaru wasn't privy to, it was entirely conceivable he'd be the one to do this.

There was also the simpler point: destroying that facility would be an extremely difficult task, one that required someone of Garfiel's caliber to pull off.

If there was another problem, it was this—

"I still don't have the faintest idea what this facility is even for."

Grateful it hadn't been destroyed, Subaru searched the room, but so far nothing stood out as a real find. Even the crystal holding the girl who was Ryuzu's spitting image — beyond being fixed and enshrined in the room — offered nothing else that particularly caught his attention.

Looking toward the wall at the far end of the room, past the row of desks, he could see the exhaust duct he'd once crawled through on his belly. Following it should, in theory, lead back out to the waiting room he'd entered from.

"...It's a little late to say this, but doesn't the floor plan seem off? Just this one huge room and a waiting room — that can't be all there is, no matter how you look at it."

The size of the facility as judged from the outside didn't match the layout of rooms within.

Tracing the mental map of the facility's exterior and interior he'd built up, Subaru sensed — vaguely, but unmistakably — that something was wrong. Somewhere, there was space enough to hide an entire room.

Crossing the room, Subaru stood before the wall with the duct's access hatch and patted it, testing the texture. The moss-covered wall felt less like plant matter and more like the fur of some animal.

Knocking on it produced no echo, either — it seemed the moss served some purpose beyond simply providing light. Given the layout, if there was some mystery space, it would have to be on the other side of this wall.

"Judging by how far I crawled, there should be a room about half the size of this one back there. Unless the wall rotates open or something, though, I don't think I can get in from this side..."

If that was the case, then the possibility lay with the waiting room instead.

Last time, Subaru hadn't had the mental room to spare to examine each room down to the last detail. In that cramped waiting room, he certainly hadn't had the wherewithal to search for traces of a hidden chamber.

Still troubled by the girl in the crystal, Subaru turned around, thinking he'd better start by checking the waiting room first—

"—"

The instant he turned, Subaru's eyes met those of a figure who had entered the room.

"...Ah?"

"—"

Their eyes locked — hers utterly devoid of emotion — and Subaru let out an involuntary sound.

A figure stood there, round eyes fixed unblinkingly on Subaru: a girl with long, pale-red hair, her body wrapped in nothing but a loose, oversized strip of white cloth.

She was the spitting image of Ryuzu, but the atmosphere she gave off was different from the woman Subaru knew. No — there was no atmosphere being conveyed at all.

She simply stood there, like empty air. Which meant—

"Ah, you're one of Ryuzu-san's..."

The word clone flickered through his mind, and Subaru hesitated over what to call the girl in front of him. Clone was, to begin with, nothing more than a label of his own imagining. With the girl's true nature still unconfirmed, his conscience balked at calling her that.

"Then again..."

He had no idea what else to call her, and the words died in his throat.

While Subaru fumbled for words, the girl stood quietly, watching him. Her expression didn't shift; she was a study in stillness so complete it was doubtful she was even breathing.

Subaru hesitated to speak to her; she, like a doll, seemed to be waiting to be spoken to.

The girl standing motionless there looked, to Subaru, exactly like that — and steeling himself, he opened his mouth.

"Hey... no, wait — you? Can you understand what I'm saying?"

"—"

"What's your name? Mind if I ask what you're doing here? And actually — what even is this place?"

"—"

"...'Sanctuary.' Garfiel. Ryuzu. Do any of those words ring a bell?"

"—"

Three questions, three silences.

He'd hoped that at least one of the questions might provoke some change of expression, but through every single one, the girl's face didn't so much as twitch. It was as if her facial muscles simply didn't function.

Pushing on a curtain — Subaru was at his wit's end.

Then, as he roughly scratched his head and let out a sigh, the girl suddenly moved.

"...?"

He instinctively tensed, but the girl's steps, as she began to move, were unhurried.

Coming through the same door from the waiting room that Subaru had entered by, the girl stepped into the room and walked, at an easy pace, toward the center — toward the crystal.

The girl approaching and the girl trapped inside the crystal were, indeed, identical.

Standing before the girl locked in a sleep from which she would never wake, the free girl bent at the waist and reached toward the lower part of the pedestal supporting the crystal.

"—"

With a click, part of the pedestal came loose, and Subaru's eyes went wide. It seemed the lower section of the pedestal was some kind of hinged hatch, storing something inside its hollow.

The girl crouched to work, her body blocking the interior from view, so Subaru craned his neck. Shifting position, he took a step forward to try to see what was inside the pedestal, and—

"—Ugh!"

A violent stench suddenly pierced his nostrils, and Subaru reeled backward, clapping a hand over his face.

The acrid smell assaulted the mucous membranes of his nose; what he felt wasn't so much a smell as outright pain. Eyes watering, gagging, Subaru shuddered under a pressure so intense it felt like his stomach's contents might come up.

The stench — he recognized it. It was without question the same rotten smell that had once hung over the interior of this facility. It was the sort of odor that made you suspect chemicals had been spilled, something hazardous to the human body.

He'd assumed, before, that it had been drifting up from underground, from where this room had collapsed, but—

"Actually, it's coming from inside that pedestal... which means it's from the environment around that crystal."

Pressing a hand to his nose, Subaru reached this conclusion and shifted position, eyes still watering. Inwardly, he shuddered at the sight of the girl continuing her work without so much as a change of expression, amid a stench so strong it felt like it was searing his eyes. Then, carefully, he peered over from the side at what her hands were doing, and his eyes went wide.

—What the girl's hands were manipulating inside the pedestal was an inner fitting engraved with a strange, intricate pattern, along with magic stones fitted at various points across it.

The magic stones glowed faintly, releasing the mana pooled within them, but some had lost their light — their contents apparently drained. The girl was carefully removing these and fitting in replacement stones from a stock inside the pedestal.

Looking at the complex pattern, Subaru recalled that he'd seen this too, somewhere in this facility, during a previous loop.

A magic circle — a staple, almost clichéd device in other-world fantasy stories, but one he'd hardly ever encountered in this world. That made it all the more striking, and he'd wondered, back then, what it was even for.

"Seeing it connected to magic stones like this... it gives off the vibe of a circuit, like in a machine. If the magic circle is the mechanical part and the magic stones are like the energy source — a battery, say..."

Once he pictured it that way, strangely, it was hard to see it as anything else.

Magical engineering, or perhaps mage-science — something like that.

While Subaru cocked his head, the girl seemed to have finished her work; she pulled out the spent magic stone and shut the hatch.

A few seconds later, Subaru noticed a prickling sensation across his skin.

—The air itself was trembling, faintly and finely.

"This is... like when magic gets used."

It was a sensation close to what he'd felt during large-scale spells — the battle with the White Whale, or when Julius's Knight's spell had activated during the fight with Petelgeuse.

Probably, some interference had occurred in the mana suffusing the air, and his body was sensitively picking up on the mana's responding movement.

This time, there was no need to wonder what the mana was responding to.

Before his eyes, Subaru watched the crystal's faint glow rapidly intensify.

Though it was a translucent blue, the crystal's glow — dim in the dark room until now — grew brighter, and the body of the girl within became correspondingly clearer.

The magic circle that had been inside the pedestal now appeared on the upper section supporting the crystal as well, its pale blue-white light dazzlingly illuminating the room.

"...A save point."

Who among the people of the modern world could blame Subaru for the word that slipped out unbidden?

A crystal set atop a magic circle. Glowing blue, brightly lighting the room — it was, unmistakably, exactly like the save points from the games Subaru had grown up with.

Despite how visually striking the display was, the fact that it had all happened in complete silence only heightened the sense of mystery, and Subaru, breath caught, couldn't manage to voice any reaction at all.

But while Subaru stood frozen before this breathtaking sight—

"—"

—the girl, having finished swapping the magic stones, simply ignored him and turned to head out of the room, without a second glance. The spent magic stone, now nothing more than a husk, had been tossed carelessly aside — true to form.

Noticing her back as she began to walk away, footsteps barely a whisper, Subaru hurriedly reached out and grabbed her shoulder.

"Hey, wait a second... o-oh, I actually touched you."

"—"

Given how faint her sense of life and presence was, Subaru had half-expected his hand to pass right through her — but thankfully, his fingers caught firmly on the girl's shoulder, and he managed to stop her.

For a moment, memory of the Witch of Envy's kin — the one that had exploded the instant it was touched, during his encounter with the Witch — surfaced, and wariness reared its head, but—

"—"

The girl only gazed back at Subaru with quiet eyes; at least there was no sign of her bursting apart.

Relieved at that much, Subaru looked straight back at the girl, who showed no sign of vanishing.

"Sorry for interrupting your work, but let's continue with the questions. Try not to ignore me this time, okay? I don't exactly have room to spare right now either."

"—"

"What were you just doing here? Why'd you make that crystal light up? Do you know the girl inside it?"

"—"

A retake of pushing on a curtain.

No matter how many times Subaru repeated the questions, all that came back from the girl was silence and a stare. It wasn't that she was refusing to answer — it seemed, rather, that she simply didn't have the option to.

The impression he'd formed of her — doll — was hard to deny, after all.

"If we could at least communicate enough for a yes or no, I'd have something to work with, but..."

Even that much was currently out of reach.

Still holding onto the girl, who looked like she'd simply walk off the moment he let go, Subaru turned his head to look back at the crystal.

The gem continued to blaze with dazzling light; the girl inside showed no change, but with the brightness, one thing had become unmistakably clear.

No breath. No heartbeat. No circulation of blood, of course.

—The body of the girl trapped inside the crystal had ceased all vital activity.

"...Calling it 'suspended animation' would be too optimistic, huh."

This wasn't as simple as warming a frozen body back up to revive it. Especially not when this girl's body had been crystallized.

Freeing her was, in every sense, a dream beyond dreaming.

"Powerless again, huh. Me."

Crushed under an unbearable sense of helplessness, Subaru touched a finger to the crystal's surface.

The cold that traveled up from his fingertip, the fact that it was constantly eating away at the girl, and her state — one in which she couldn't even feel it — left him unsure whether to feel relief or fury.

And it was right as that pang of sentiment crossed his chest that—

"—Eh?"

The cold traveling from his touching fingertip suddenly turned to heat, racing up his arm and coursing through his entire body.

"—Oh, ohh, ahh!?"

What had first felt like warmth instantly became a searing, flame-like heat scorching his whole body.

With the sensation of fiery fingers tracing along the inside of his body, Subaru let out a cry of pain and writhed where he stood.

The heat brought on breathlessness, his screams echoing through the room.

With no room to spare a thought for the filth of the floor, Subaru collapsed, limbs sprawled out across it. Convulsing, his vision flickering in and out—and then—

"—Hff, huh?"

Abruptly, the hellish stretch of time — one that had seemed like it would never end — came to a close.

The raging heat swiftly banked its flames, and Subaru's body was suddenly released from the storm of agony.

"...W-what... was that, just now."

Pushing himself up from where he'd lain, Subaru murmured while checking the condition of his limbs.

The agony just now, and its abrupt end — with no clue as to cause, process, or result, all that remained was a memory of confusion and pain.

"I'm... fine. But if I'm fine, then what was even the point of that."

The pain had come right after he touched the crystal.

The cause was probably the mage-science apparatus centered on the crystal. Maybe he'd touched some power source and been electrocuted, or something close to it.

As he got that far in his thinking, Subaru remembered that, wrapped up in the pain, he'd completely let the Ryuzu-lookalike girl slip from his mind.

"Oh, no—"

"—"

Subaru hastily tried to get up, but on lifting his head and seeing the girl still standing there unchanged, the tension in him eased with relief. And right in front of that eased tension—

"—"

—he watched the girl reverently kneel where she stood and bow her head.

Seeing the girl kneeling with her head bowed, Subaru was baffled.

Unless his eyes deceived him, that gesture, that posture, could only be read as an expression of respect toward Subaru standing before her — an attitude of submission.

"What's this all of a sudden? What are you playing at?"

Given how, up until now, she'd done nothing but ignore everything Subaru said, this sudden reversal in her attitude brought suspicion and wariness to the fore, even before surprise.

That was how deep the chasm between Subaru and the girl still was; enough to make him tense up like this. Neither of them had exchanged so much as their identities. Could he really not be suspicious, given that?

"—"

Before Subaru's wary gaze, the girl, who had been kneeling, rose to her feet.

Then she tilted her gaze slightly upward, looking somewhere past Subaru's head. Drawn along, he looked in the same direction, but there was nothing there to see.

It seemed that gesture had just been part of some idle motion of looking skyward while thinking something over. Subaru turned his head back around. And then—

"Whoa!?"

"—"

The girl was right in front of him again, close enough to feel her breath, and Subaru reeled back. But as he did, the girl's hand shot out and caught his arm—

"...? You want me to follow you?"

"—"

Wordlessly, the girl tugged at his sleeve, an action clearly meant to lead him away.

Met with silent assent to his question, Subaru stood stock-still for a moment, thinking it over.

The fact that she'd stayed so stubbornly silent this whole time suggested that maybe this girl simply couldn't speak. And yet she seemed to grasp the meaning behind Subaru's questions regardless. Which meant — shouldn't he take her attempt to lead him away as a sign that she was willing, in some form, to engage with him?

"Nothing ventured, nothing gained, huh."

"—"

"Never mind, just talking to myself. Alright — lead the way. I'll follow."

When Subaru nodded back, signaling his willingness to follow, the girl, still tugging at his sleeve, began to walk. Following in her footsteps, Subaru turned back one last time to glance at the crystal.

—Still, amid the pale blue-white glow, the girl remained fast asleep.

Led by the sleeve, he left the room, passed through the facility's corridor, and reached the waiting room. From there, the girl leading the way continued on, apparently intending to take Subaru outside the facility entirely.

"Not staying inside, huh. Going outside's fine and all, but..."

If this meant showing his face anywhere near the Sanctuary, it would give away that Subaru had snuck into the facility. Of course, he could always brazen it out by insisting he hadn't done anything worth being scolded for — but given his relationship with Garfiel, that wasn't something he wanted right now.

As Subaru fretted over what he'd do if the girl's feet started leading toward the Sanctuary—

"—Well now, that's quite the difficult face you're making, Su-bou."

"...Oh, come on. This timing?"

The voice called out to him just as he stepped outside the facility, exposed to the open air.

Turning toward the voice, Subaru's lips loosened at the sight of the person he found. Whether that was out of relief, or something else entirely, he couldn't say, but—

"I imagine there's plenty you'd like to talk about, but first, we ought to find somewhere else to do it."

"Yeah, let's do that. Honestly, there's way too much to catch up on."

Subaru shrugged in agreement.

And how, he wondered, did he appear to her right now — standing there beside a girl who was the spitting image of herself?

—Subaru, standing shoulder to shoulder with the girl who so resembled her, bathed in moonlight from behind — what did the original Ryuzu make of the sight?
