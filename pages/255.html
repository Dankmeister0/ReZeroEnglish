Arc 4 Chapter 87: "Demons Out, Two Clowns In"

Surprised glances turned toward Subaru as he burst into the room, breathing hard.

Both of them were people Subaru had thought of as strangers to anything resembling surprise. Feeling a certain satisfaction at their reaction, the wicked grin already on his face deepened further.

"...A bet?"

The one who murmured the word, narrowing his mismatched eyes, was Roswaal, lying in the bed.

The face that should normally have been done up in clownish makeup was bare now, stripped clean. It was Roswaal's naked face — one Subaru had seen only once before, across all his looping time.

Beneath the white paint, his skin was pale, and with the eyeliner gone, his face seemed to carry something more like innocence than sharpness — utterly unlike the impression Roswaal usually gave. The face that looked calculating and dubious under makeup transformed, the instant the makeup came off, into that of an earnest, wholesome young man.

Handsome is handsome, no effort required — Subaru privately summed up Roswaal's bare face with that verdict, then dipped his chin at Roswaal's murmur.

"Yeah, a bet. You and me, wagering our wishes... a serious, one-shot showdown."

"—"

Holding up a single finger, Subaru declared it to Roswaal without flinching. Roswaal narrowed his eyes, his expression that of someone weighing the proposal. But before Roswaal could say anything, a figure stepped between their locked gazes — the pink-haired maid, Ram.

With Roswaal at her back, Ram faced Subaru with a severe look in her eyes. Ordinarily, her gaze was sharp enough whenever she found an opening to needle Subaru, but this was on a different level entirely from those times.

"Wait, Barusu. Bursting into the room out of nowhere, and then to think what you'd say... Do you intend to burden Roswaal-sama while he's convalescing? That's far too disrespectful."

"Being sick or injured is a pretty weak excuse to go easy on him, given his current position. His glib tongue and black heart have nothing to do with his condition... so I'm gonna make him push himself a little, unreasonable or not."

"Barusu—"

"No matter who says what to me!"

Ram's eyes carried a blend of irritation and menace. But before she could move, Subaru beat her to it, stamping his foot loudly on the floor. Pointing at Ram, who froze warily in place, he said,

"I've got no reason to stop and no need to hesitate. That much ought to be true between you and me, right, Roswaal?"

"...Hm."

"Or are you gonna sulk and drop to zero motivation just because things strayed a little from your journal? Don't you have the guts to push yourself a bit, for the sake of the next you down the line?"

"...That's an interesting way to put it. The next me, is it."

Subaru chose his words to reach Roswaal without touching on Return by Death directly. Ram knit her brows, showing she didn't follow, but Roswaal seemed to have understood at once.

A faint trace of color returned to his previously lifeless expression, and Roswaal called out to Ram's back where she stood in front of him.

"Ram. Stand by... no, could you step outside for a li-i-ittle while?"

"...! But, Roswaal-sama."

"It's fine, it's fine. Subaru-kun isn't reckless enough to suddenly attack me just because we're left alone — no-o-ot at all. And turning the tables on him would be easy enough regardless. Isn't that ri-i-ight?"

"Yeah, pathetic as it is. If it came down to brute force, I'm not even confident I could drag you out of that bed."

At Subaru waving his empty hands in confirmation, Ram twisted her cheeks in frustration, as if grinding her teeth, then turned a worried gaze back to Roswaal once more.

"...Please, don't push yourself too hard."

Saying only that much, she bowed solemnly and headed for the door. Just before passing Subaru, she shot him a sidelong glance.

"...If anything happens to Roswaal-sama, I won't let it slide."

"I'd rather you worry about whether he's gonna do something reckless out of desperation."

Subaru watched Ram go, shrugging as she left, and once he saw the door close, he turned back to face Roswaal. Roswaal, still wearing a contemplative look, closed one eye, reflecting Subaru in his remaining yellow one.

"That's a qui-i-ite different look than the one you wore parting last night, isn't i-i-it. Some change of heart, in just a few hours?"

"A change of heart, sure, no denying that. Got lectured, then settled things with fists and friendship... no, it was too one-sided to call it a fistfight — more like a confirmation process."

He touched the cheek Otto had punched, recalling the faint redness still lingering there this morning.

Surprisingly, for how lanky he looked, Otto's stamina was nothing to scoff at. Probably the difference came down to the number of life-or-death scrapes each of them had been through. Subaru liked to think he'd been through his fair share since coming to this world, but apparently he still had a long way to go.

"What kind of brutal world did he come from, then..."

"Is that so, is that so. We-e-ell, we did confirm just yesterday that you hadn't been pushed far enough, tho-o-ough with a recovery this fast, it seems I wasn't mistaken about that."

"I was pretty much at my limit, though. ...Guess it just means I'm simple."

It felt like Otto's words and fists had beaten his twisted, warped resolve back into shape.

A brutally rough, simple method, he thought. Getting punched by a friend to be set back on the right path was about as naive as it got — if it had happened to someone else, he'd surely have laughed it off.

"But I don't think it's so bad. Finding out you might be able to break through a dead end, with a little help from a friend — that feels good too."

"How naive. Green and young. ...In the end, the suffering of this world can only be resolved by oneself. A feeble notion like relying on friends is unnecessary for you."

"Relying on people, relying on bonds, relying on feelings... and that's no good?"

"No go-o-od."

"That so. ...Then I guess we've got no choice but to settle this with a bet."

Roswaal's expression shifted. Subaru stepped forward, closing the distance to Roswaal on the bed, snapped his fingers with a crack, and thrust that finger at Roswaal.

"Like I said, let's make a bet. The chips are our wishes, and the bed's good for one hand only."

"I suppose I'll hear you out, at lea-a-ast."

Confirming that Roswaal wasn't rejecting the proposal outright, Subaru turned the finger he'd been pointing toward the ceiling and laid out the premise.

"This time, I can't go along with what you want. And it's not just this time — I don't plan to fall in line going forward, either... but if we both just keep insisting on that, you and me'll never meet. So let's set a deadline."

"A deadline?"

Roswaal echoed the question, and Subaru nodded, "Yeah."

Wetting his lips with his tongue, Subaru glared straight into both of Roswaal's eyes.

"This loop, I push through with my way of doing things. If I screw it up... then starting next time, I'll move the way you want. That's the deadline."

"...You, who holds the power to start over, would give up that right?"

"You said it yourself. That I haven't been pushed far enough. I think so too. ...Getting cocky about being able to start over — look what it got me."

Of course, he had no intention of rejecting that notion at its root.

In this cruel world, what could Natsuki Subaru do without Return by Death? He had no intention of denying the benefits he'd gained from Return by Death thus far, nor of playing shameless about it.

He'd simply changed his way of thinking. If it was a Return by Death that came after he'd done everything he could, he'd accept it. But if it wasn't that — if he died before he'd truly lived it out to the end,

"Then that's an insult to the people who'd cry for my sake. I'm not doing that anymore."

"A restriction on yourself, to that end... is tha-a-at it. As far as I'm concerned, it's a condition I could hardly ask for better, tho-o-ough — what guarantee do I have that you'll keep it?"

"A guarantee, huh."

"Yes, a guarantee. That's importa-a-ant, you know? After a-a-all, you — who can start over — could make this whole promise never have happened, since-e-e. Fail, redo, go back to last night even, and the-e-en calmly try some other method instead..."

"Roswaal."

Subaru said the name of the man voicing his concerns, quietly.

Roswaal, cut off mid-sentence by the address, widened his eyes slightly at the sight of Subaru's gaze fixed on him. Then, without changing the tone of his voice, Subaru pressed on.

"You think I'd do that?"

"—"

"If you think I'd do that... then this deal doesn't happen. That's all there is to it."

At Subaru's words, Roswaal narrowed his eyes, then raised both hands lightly and let out a sigh.

He continued, "No, no-o-o,"

"let's hear the rest of it, shall we-e-e. I'll save my judgment for the end."

"...Yeah, do that. Like I said before, I'm drawing a line. This time, I pour everything into this one shot. If it fails, I'll do it your way, like you said. Either way... if this method doesn't work, there's probably no chance left for me at all."

"It's not that you're confident, then. Just resolved, is that all it is. ...The-e-en, I suppose I too must answer in kind. Now — you've drawn a deadline and put forward this time as the last. So then, wha-a-at is it you'll ask of me?"

Roswaal's tone had begun, little by little, to return to its usual cadence.

Loading their respective wills into the negotiation, trading them back and forth, Subaru clapped his hands.

"My demand's simple. If my way of doing things happens to break the situation open this time, the future that unfolds will take a shape different from what you want. And when that happens, you'll probably lose the will to live in a world different from the Gospel... but none of that. Not this time."

"'None,' meaning you want me not to lose my drive? Ho-o-owever, no matter how you look at it, I'd have to say that's a difficult thing to ask of me. Of course, keeping up appearances is possible, tho-o-ough — but as for what's in my heart, even I can't..."

"It's nothing special, Roswaal. It's not like I want to be your enemy forever."

"...Hm?"

Roswaal tilted his head, unable to follow Subaru's reasoning.

At Roswaal's attitude, Subaru rubbed his nose with a finger and said,

"I get that you hate the idea of the future straying off the route fixed in the Gospel. But even in a future shaped differently from the Gospel, I'm gonna keep flailing around at Emilia's side to make her queen. And I'll probably end up relying on the power to start over too. — Whatever the process looks like, the final result definitely won't stray from your goal."

"—"

"Roswaal, my demand is simple. If I ever manage to open up a future that unfolds differently from the Gospel... then you throw that Gospel away and come along with me. We're making Emilia queen. And for that, your power is necessary."

No matter how deeply Roswaal had dirtied his hands with unforgivable deeds, his power was necessary for Emilia's goal. On a personal level, Subaru had felt revulsion at times, unable to understand him, unable to reach any common ground. Even on this timeline, if left alone, Roswaal's scheming would give rise to an irreversible catastrophe. — But that catastrophe, Subaru would nip in the bud with his own hands, and avoid the decisive rift.

At Subaru's proposal, Roswaal let out a long, deep breath.

Eyes closed, in a posture of deep thought, he touched his own chin and slowly parted his lips.

"So that's... the final landing place you're after."

"It's a convenient story, sure. But I love convenient stories. Emilia works her ass off, becomes queen, I stand beside her and give her my Blessing, and you get to be one of the people surrounding her too."

"For a long, lo-o-ong time, I've stuck to a single way of doing things — that makes this a difficult proposal for me. And since you mean to move someone like tha-a-at... the conditions must be harsh ones, I take it?"

"Yeah, that's right."

To Roswaal, who closed one eye, Subaru nodded and held up two fingers.

Feeling Roswaal's gaze fix on the tips of those fingers, Subaru lightly waved one of them.

"Two conditions. Two things you'd call impossible, unworkable. When I clear those conditions, I win the bet."

"And if the conditions go unmet, I win. You'll be giving up your humanity."

He said it in a low, heavy tone, looking at Subaru with both eyes now. Nodding, Subaru gritted his teeth once before Roswaal's gaze urging him on, then began.

"First, condition one. — I win Garfiel over to my side and bring him outside."

"—"

"You said it's impossible to bring him outside, obsessed as he is with staying inside the Sanctuary. I think so too. I think so, but... his power is going to be needed going forward. Even considering the feelings of the people of the Sanctuary, I can't just leave his tantrum as it is. I'm going to see through the persuading of Garfiel that you flatly called impossible."

"...And the second?"

The instant he heard the first condition, a dark emotion flickered in the depths of Roswaal's eyes.

But without remarking on it, Roswaal simply pressed for the next condition, and Subaru nodded.

"— I'll have Emilia break through the Trials of the Tomb. It's Emilia who overcomes the Trials of the Tomb and liberates the Sanctuary. Not me."

"Impossible!"

Raising his voice, Roswaal struck the bed hard with his palm.

A dry crack sounded, and Roswaal's face twisted into a mask of fury. Squaring his shoulders, he thrust a finger at Subaru.

"I told you as much last night. That thing can't break through the Trials of the Tomb. And there's no way Garfiel could ever cast aside his obsession with the Sanctuary, either!"

"That's exactly the kind of thing you don't know 'til you try."

"Just so, it's something you don't know 'til you try. And it's precisely because you've tried it over and over that you were so utterly broken in front of me, wasn't it!? That state of yours, and the resolve you're showing this time — those are exactly the proof that the two of them aren't worthy of that hope!"

Saying it as if shouting, Roswaal's shoulders heaved up and down. At the sight of him repeating ragged breaths, Subaru, however, kept an entirely composed expression.

"That's some seriously worked-up anger you've got going there."

"What did you say...?"

"The harsher my conditions are, the more it works in your favor, doesn't it? So getting angry that the conditions are harsh seems like it's missing the point, don't you think?"

"This is a matter of whether the bet holds up as a bet at all. Far from being disadvantageous, if its legitimacy grows shaky, the very outcome of the bet could be ruined. It's only natural that I'd raise the point."

Roswaal seemed to harbor suspicion at conditions so overwhelmingly disadvantageous to Subaru and so overwhelmingly favorable to himself. But Subaru's cheeks twisted at that reaction of his. Wickedly, wickedly, his grin deepened.

"Roswaal, seems like you're under a misunderstanding."

"..."

"I'm at too much of a disadvantage? Sure, at a glance, these are harsh conditions, no denying that. It's not like I don't think I'd need to do at least this much before I could claim any right to make you revise that long-cherished plan of yours. It's not that I don't think that... but that's a separate matter entirely."

To the silent Roswaal, Subaru, still grinning, said,

"You're the one who said it, Roswaal."

"—"

"When I'm pushed to the edge, I become the strongest card there is. — Your opponent, though not in the shape you wanted, is undeniably that strongest card. So, still not satisfied?"

At Subaru's declaration, delivered like a challenge, Roswaal said nothing.

He only stared fixedly at Subaru, steadying his breathing. Then, once his breath had calmed, Roswaal held up a single finger.

"...A Contract."

"—"

"Very well. Let's accept the conditions you've laid out just now, shall we-e-e. — Garfiel's release from his fixation, and Emilia-sama's liberation of the Sanctuary. When both of those are achieved, I will scrap my own plan and walk the path you carve. Let's exchange a Contract to that effect."

A faint light kindled at the tip of his raised finger.

A concentration of Mana, that rainbow-colored light closely resembled the one Julius had once produced when he destroyed Petelgeuse — the light that arises when multiple elemental attributes are bound together.

"Through the Gate, we'll carve our mutual Contract into our Souls. One may deceive anyone else, but not one's own heart. — A Contract carved into the Soul persists beyond distance, beyond time, even beyond worlds. It should hold its effect against your do-overs as well, I'd wager."

"Huh, so there is a way to handle it after all. ...Well, that works out better for me too, I guess. If we're both bound by the Contract, it saves me the trouble of having to beat you into line if you lose the bet and start whining about it."

"You don't seem to be taking it lightly, at least... very well."

Roswaal said little more about Subaru's readiness to accept the Contract.

The glow at his fingertip touched the center of Subaru's chest, and something seeped into his body from there. An instant later, he felt a pulse from within that seemed to open every pore on his body, and Subaru let out a breath.

"Ah, ha—"

"Just the same, let it be carved into my Soul as well. — When the Contract of Natsuki Subaru is fulfilled, so too shall the Contract of Roswaal L. Mathers be fulfilled."

In the same way, a rainbow-colored light burst above Roswaal's chest.

For a moment, the light spread across the whole of Roswaal's body, and by the time Subaru blinked, he had returned to his original appearance.

"That it, then?"

"That's it indeed. ...There's no turning back now."

Confirming they'd each drawn a line neither could escape, Subaru swallowed a small breath.

Then Roswaal, placing a hand over his own chest, said,

"Just as you'll do your utmost to fulfill the conditions, I too will act to bring about what's written in the Gospel. You won't fault me for that, will you?"

"...Five days from now — you gonna reenact the part where the snow falls, too?"

"...If Emilia-sama doesn't bring it down, then I suppose it'll fall to me to do it, I'd wa-a-ager."

In other words, a time limit had now been set.

The Great Rabbit's assault, five days from now. Before then, Emilia had to liberate the Sanctuary, and Subaru had to free Garfiel from his fixation.

"Well, if that's settled, there's no time to waste. I'll get moving."

"Subaru-kun."

Roswaal called out to Subaru, who was already stepping away from the bed to set things in motion.

Subaru turned back, and Roswaal averted his gaze slightly.

"The mansion, too — same day. ...Best of luck to you, for whatever it's worth."

"That's because if I go all out and screw up, I'll be more likely to act the way you're hoping for, next time around... is that it?"

"—"

Roswaal answered Subaru's response with silence.

Wryly amused at that attitude, Subaru pointed at Roswaal one last time.

"Roswaal, put your clown makeup back on — you're throwing off my rhythm like this."

"Hm, come to think of it... this might be the first time I've dealt with you bare-faced."

"In this world, yeah, guess it is."

Subaru could tell, from his meaningfully suggestive remark, that Roswaal's eyes had gone wide.

Feeling that reaction at his back, Subaru started walking.

"This is a match between you and me. Two clowns tossed about by fate — let's do this fair and square."

Leaving those words behind, he left the room.

The terms of the bet were set. — And so, from here, it begins.

Natsuki Subaru's final challenge, staked on the liberation of the Sanctuary.

  • Title: a play on the traditional Setsubun bean-throwing chant "鬼は外、福は内" ("Demons out, luck in!"). Nagatsuki swaps 福 (luck) for 道化二人 (the two clowns — Subaru and Roswaal), turning it into "Demons out, two clowns in!" I've rendered it literally as "Demons Out, Two Clowns In" to preserve the reference; the Setsubun allusion itself may be worth a footnote if this is published, since it won't be obvious to non-Japanese readers.
  • "バルス" (Ram's address for Subaru) rendered as "Barusu" per established fan/wiki convention (not in the provided glossary, flagging for verification).
  • Roswaal's elongated-vowel speech affectation is rendered throughout with hyphenated vowel stretches (e.g., "tho-o-ough," "wha-a-at," "shall we-e-e") per the style guide; placement approximates but doesn't always mirror the exact mid-word location of the Japanese elongation marks (ーぁ/ーぇ/ーぉ/ーぅ).
  • "試練" rendered consistently as "the Trials of the Tomb" per the provided glossary even in short repeated references (e.g. 「アレに『試練』は突破できない」), which reads slightly more formal/repetitive than the terse Japanese "the Trial" would.
