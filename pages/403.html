Arc 6 Chapter 1: "The Road Home by Dragon Carriage"

—Having left the city of Priestella, Natsuki Subaru and his party continued steadily onward, heading east.

Their destination lay at the eastern edge of the world map: the Augria Sand Dunes situated there—a notorious land that refused passage to any and all, and had remained a den of Witchbeasts for hundreds of years.

Miasma that scrambled one's sense of direction and ate away at the explorer's mind. Packs of Witchbeasts, evil's vanguard, that toyed with and devoured human lives. And the "Sage" Shaula, who wished for the world's peace and struck down without discrimination anyone who approached the watchtower.

"Even just what we know about, it sounds like a lineup for a suicide mission..."

Merely picturing the obstacles that awaited them was more than enough to make one's spirits sink. In fact, according to those in the know, the only people who ever headed there were either would-be suicides or Witch Cultists—in either case, people whose brains weren't functioning quite right. But they themselves were neither, and had not the slightest intention of dying. If anything, quite the opposite.

To give a path to live by, to people who had run out of roads. It was to obtain that seed of hope that they had set out on this journey.

"...Still, it's a strange thing."

Subaru murmured this, half-imagining the sway of a dragon carriage that didn't actually sway, addressing the faces seated across from him. Catching the remark, every gaze in the cabin turned his way. Four gazes in total—since there were five people present including Subaru, that meant everyone had turned to look.

"Strange? Did something happen?"

"Nah, it's nothing big, Emilia-tan. Just, y'know, thinking about how this particular lineup ended up sharing a dragon carriage together—it's a pretty unexpected combination, is all."

Subaru shrugged in answer to Emilia's question. Flanking him on either side were Emilia and Beatrice—that is, family. It was only natural that these two would be with him. The problem was the pair sitting across from him.

"Well, I suppose it is a bit odd to feel that way, honestly. To tell the truth, I'm a touch surprised myself. It's only natural Natsuki-kun would feel out of place too."

"...Yeah, guess so."

Laughing lightly, it was Anastasia who spoke, in Kararagi dialect rather than Kansai dialect. Subaru answered weakly in his imitation Kansai accent, and she touched her own hair, casting a meaningful glance to the side.

"Though if it's just a matter of feeling out of place, I imagine Julius-san has you beat?"

"That stings. But rest assured. —I too am a knight. A single night is more than enough to set one's heart in order. And this time it has already been several days... I promise you, I shall show no such unseemly display as before."

"How elegant. Well then, I'll hold you to those words."

At Anastasia's teasing remark, Julius bowed his head in reply, his manner exactly as it had always been. Which, put that way, was only natural. He had been the victim of the "Gluttony" Authority, but its effects had fallen not so much on him as on the people around him. If anyone's attitude were to change, then, it would be the people around him, not him. In response to their reactions he himself had been unable to fully hide his own unease—but true to his word, it seemed he had weathered it without incident.

"...Man, unbelievable."

Impressive, Subaru silently admired, though he didn't say it aloud. The strength of that man's spirit was, frankly, something to take one's hat off to. The sensation of being the only one left behind, of having only your own memories of someone erased from everyone around you—Subaru wasn't entirely unfamiliar with it himself.

Natsuki Subaru, who turned back time with Return by Death, carried memories of worlds that no one else in this world remembered. As for the experience of being forgotten by people with whom he'd once built a bond—if he had to point to one instance, it would be that very first stay at Roswaal Manor: those repeating days.

Summoned to another world, running himself ragged to save Emilia in the royal capital, and as a result invited to Roswaal Manor—after four repetitions, Subaru had finally made it through on his fifth attempt at that world. The memories of those four earlier worlds now existed nowhere but inside Subaru. In the midst of those days, the shock of being treated as a total stranger by the people of the mansion with whom he'd built a bond was something he still couldn't forget, even now.

That pain and sorrow would never fade.

"...What is it, I wonder? Subaru, why have you suddenly started petting Betty's head?"

"Don't mind me. Just getting sentimental thinking about how much you saved me back then. Thanks, thanks."

"That makes no sense at all, I suppose. And it's mussing my hair, so stop... well, you don't have to stop, but pet with a little more care, I suppose."

Scooting over by half a seat's worth, Beatrice let her head rest against him. As instructed, Subaru stroked her head, and in doing so was made to reconfirm, as a matter of fact, just how he'd felt back when he'd been saved.

Through all those repeated days at the mansion, Beatrice was the only one whose attitude toward Subaru never changed. He'd met her before the starting point of his Return by Death, and even afterward, she alone never changed how she treated him. That she had saved him through that was something he'd probably keep secret from her forever.

"After that, though, it was Emilia-tan who saved me."

"—?"

At Subaru's reminiscence, Emilia made a puzzled face. By her kindness, in her voice and manner, by the warmth of the lap she'd let him rest on, Subaru had been saved—and that was why he was here now.

"—————"

Which was exactly why he could understand, as lived experience, just how remarkable it was that Julius had pulled through all that alone. Subaru had borrowed so many hands, taken so much time redoing things four times over, just to overcome what Julius had accomplished on his own.

Or had someone lent him a hand too, Subaru wondered. Forgotten by Anastasia, unable to borrow help from anyone in the camp, cut off even from his connection with the quasi-spirit he'd Contracted with, unable to even remember his own blood-related younger brother—had someone been there for him, too? If so, Subaru thought, that would be good.

"You've been staring at my face for a while now... is something the matter, Subaru?"

"Nothin's the matter. Just thinking how much I don't like it when you get called 'elegant' and go all refined with your smile."

"Elegant, refined—quite the fine ring to those words. I never imagined I'd receive praise from you. Unexpectedly pleasing... no, that's overstating it. Well, shall we say, ordinary?"

"I wasn't tryin' to compliment you, but somehow that response still pisses me off!"

Answered back in his old manner—no, if anything, with his tongue running even more smoothly than before—Subaru found himself the one bested this time. The fragility that had shown on the side of his face back in Priestella, in his brother's hospital room, now seemed like a lie. Subaru recalled griping about that expression in the heat of the moment back then—a thoroughly black chapter of his history now.

"Subaru and Julius get along well, huh. Um, was it like this even before things got to be how they are now?"

"Emilia-tan, that's a total misunderstanding. There's no way me and this guy are close. Did you see my face just now? Not to toot my own horn, but that was the worst side-eye of my life."

"Really? I don't think that's true, though... ah, that wasn't about your eyes, I meant about how you two get along. Your eyes always look that bad, I think—reeeally bad."

"Always look that bad!?"

"But I like Subaru's eyes. Really, truly."

"Ah, damn, I wish I'd recorded that so I could cut out just the eye part later!"

Emilia gave a wry smile at Subaru's chagrined reaction, then turned a searching look toward Julius. Julius, catching that gaze, glanced briefly at Subaru, and said,

"I have no grounds from which to deny his words. In truth, to say that he and I are close... would be a bit of a stretch. For my part, I consider this something of a concession already."

"'Concession'—the very word already sounds condescending. Grovel a little more. ...Actually, that'd probably just piss me off more."

"A troublesome complaint. Whatever am I to do with him, I wonder."

Julius sought Emilia's agreement, but at this exchange between the two of them, she pushed back her silver hair and said,

"You really do seem reeeally close, though...?"

"I keep telling you, it's exactly as it looks."

"Eh? Then, you really are close, aren't you...?"

There was a mismatch in sensibility between her and them, but Subaru and Julius remained perfectly unruffled. Emilia grew ever more confused at their manner, and Anastasia pressed a hand to her mouth, laughing quietly. At Anastasia's reaction, Julius's lips relaxed as if in relief.

—To Subaru, who knew what lay beneath, that borrowed master-and-retainer relationship reeked of something fishy.

Because the Anastasia here was not, in the true sense, Anastasia Hoshin—only a fake wearing the same shell.

At present, the contents of Anastasia had been taken over by the artificial spirit Echidna—commonly known as Eridna. Eridna herself claimed to have no intention of taking over the body permanently, but just how far that could be trusted was another matter entirely. Subaru, who knew the truth of it, kept careful watch without letting Emilia or Julius, who knew nothing of it, catch on.

"...Subaru, that's about enough, I suppose."

Beatrice spoke up to Subaru, who had fallen into quiet thought. Even through his silence, the girl's hair had kept being petted, until it was mussed and her cute face had turned to a scowl.

"Whoa!? Sorry, Bea-ko! Your cute look is ruined, turned into this hideous..."

"Betty's cuteness doesn't get mussed by something as trivial as her hairstyle, I suppose! More importantly, you're making a scary face, I suppose."

Sliding half a seat's worth away from Subaru, Beatrice said this while frantically fixing her mussed hair with her hands. Subaru choked on a "Uh" at her words, and Beatrice, looking somewhat exasperated, added,

"There's no point making such a needlessly worried face, I suppose. ...Betty will keep an eye on it. If anything happens, Betty will notice far sooner than Subaru would, I suppose."

"...Right."

Beatrice layered these words atop Subaru's inner unease. Aside from Subaru, Beatrice was the only one who knew about Eridna's takeover of Anastasia. Originally, Subaru had promised to keep the truth secret, for two reasons: to avoid needless confusion, and because Eridna claimed she had no intention of taking Anastasia's body for her own. But he'd told Beatrice alone, reasoning that it would be too late if he waited until something actually happened. The two of them were both artificial spirits, both connected to the same "Witch" Echidna. If Eridna were plotting something, Beatrice would surely sense it.

Though, considering Beatrice's own feelings, letting her get too involved with Echidna hardly seemed like the wisest course—

"Rather than fretting needlessly, it would be better for Subaru to just be Subaru, and go on asking anyone and everyone for help like always. Though you should tell Betty first. I'll praise you for that much this time, I suppose."

—was the answer she gave him, so, well, that was that.

"—I can see it."

All at once, a silence fell over the dragon carriage, and then Emilia murmured this. Looking ahead through the carriage window, Emilia's eyes must have caught sight of their destination, far in the distance. Subaru too could tell that the scenery outside the window was beginning to look familiar.

"The old familiar road. Roswaal Manor's coming up soon, huh."

He let the words spill out, bracing himself as they approached this first checkpoint. As stated at the outset, this party's destination was the Augria Sand Dunes, to seek an audience with the "Sage" Shaula—but it had been decided that, both to prepare for that journey and as a waypoint on the long trip, they would stop at Roswaal Manor along the way.

Regarding the events in the city of Priestella, and their coming talk with the "Sage," there was a mountain of things Subaru wanted to discuss with the comrades of the camp who should be waiting at the manor. And besides that—besides that.

"It's not just a matter of a detour, either."

There was meaning to stopping by Roswaal Manor before setting off on the journey to meet the "Sage." Viewed broadly, it held a small significance within a small world. But viewed through the eyes of one small man named Natsuki Subaru, it held significance with vast possibility.

"—————"

Subaru narrowed his eyes as if trying to catch a phantom glimpse of Roswaal Manor, still far off and not yet in sight. At the sight of him, the other four in the carriage each turned their gaze toward him. The colors of those gazes, the emotions behind them, varied—but Subaru had no room to spare to notice any of them.

It was an hour later, just past noon, that the dragon carriage arrived at Roswaal Manor. The long journey of some ten days since departing the city of Priestella had, for the time being, come to an end.

"Subaru!"

"Oh, Petra— whoa!?"

The carriage pulled up to the manor on its Earth Dragons; Subaru thanked the driver and climbed down from the cabin. Just then, a small maid came pattering out from the direction of the manor and, without slowing, leapt straight at him.

Taking the full force and momentum of the girl against his chest, Subaru stumbled back but managed to catch her. Then, with her in his arms, he stroked the head of the girl who'd come flying in.

"You'll give a guy a heart attack jumping at him like that, Petra. Been gone almost a month—you doing okay?"

"I've been fine, just like you can see. But you—Subaru-sama, weren't you the one having a hard time? A letter got here the day before yesterday and I've been worried this whole time. You got hurt a bunch again, didn't you? You're not pushing through the pain without saying anything, are you?"

"Ticklish, ticklish."

Her reddish-brown hair swaying, Petra patted and prodded at Subaru's body to check him over. Subaru gave a wry smile at her hands, and the rest of the group climbed down from the carriage as well.

"C'mon now, Petra. Do your proper duty as a maid of this house. Or Ram or Frederica'll have your head."

"...Yes, sir. But I want to hear all about it later, okay?"

Having only just returned, it seemed Subaru had already thoroughly lost Petra's trust. Going off to negotiate over handing over a Crystal Ore, of all things, and getting swept up in an incident that engulfed the entire city—it was no wonder Petra was worried. Granted, the Witch Cult business hadn't been something Subaru had intentionally caused, but that excuse would probably fall on deaf ears. After all, it was a fact that most of the Witch Cult's aims had had something to do with the Emilia Camp.

"Honored guests, thank you for enduring the long journey. I am Petra Leyte, a maid in service to this household. Allow me to show you to the manor."

Setting aside his own musings, Petra recited her greeting with practiced poise. Julius, and Anastasia—who had descended from the carriage with his help—both looked impressed. Emilia and Beatrice, meanwhile, somehow looked a touch proud.

"Such a proper little thing, even so small. She's clearly been well brought up."

"Indeed, quite a different person from the one who leapt at Subaru just now."

"—Do forgive that unseemly display."

At Anastasia's smiling remark, seconded by Julius, Petra flushed at the comment. Looking down at the girl, Julius then turned to Subaru and said,

"I see... this only drives home all the more why you're so celebrated as the 'wrangler of little girls.'"

"For the record, Petra's not exactly little enough to count as a 'little girl,' and even giving you the benefit of the doubt, the only little girl I 'wrangle' is Bea-ko. Don't get the wrong idea."

"Nor does Betty intend to work so conveniently for Subaru's benefit that it counts as being 'wrangled,' I suppose. Don't you get the wrong idea either, Subaru."

Subaru shot back a sharp retort to Julius's teasing, only to be scolded even more sharply by Beatrice. Subaru pressed his lips into a flat line and fell silent, and Beatrice, sighing, went over to Petra. Standing beside the girl in question, she gave a small pinch of the hem of her maid uniform.

"Besides, look, there isn't really that much difference between Betty and Petra, I sup—wait, I suppose. Petra, I feel like you've gotten a little taller. And your hair's grown a bit too, I suppose...!"

"Well, it has been a month, and I'm in my growth spurt now. I'll get taller, and hair grows out if you don't cut it. Beatrice-chan's still the same size, though."

"Wh-what is the meaning of this...!"

Petra laughed and pulled the stunned Beatrice into a tight hug. After thoroughly embracing the pouting Beatrice, she said, "Well then, allow me to show you the way," and started walking.

"Well, finally, into the manor— ngh."

As Subaru made to follow her, someone's hand grabbed the back of his collar. He gave a strangled yelp and turned around to find, of all people, Emilia was the one who'd grabbed him. Still holding onto the back of his collar, Emilia jerked her chin toward the dragon carriage behind them.

"Subaru, Patrasche looks upset."

"Ugh... what a stubborn one."

Looking at the Earth Dragons at Emilia's remark, Subaru saw that of the two who had pulled the carriage, the beautiful jet-black Earth Dragon was glaring at him with unusually sharp eyes. It was Patrasche, Subaru's beloved dragon. When Subaru tried to meet her gaze, the Earth Dragon pointedly looked away and gave a small, displeased cry.

"Seriously, you had ten whole days and still couldn't make up?"

"I get it, but this time I really don't think it's my fault..."

Scratching his head, Subaru struggled with how to handle a sulking Patrasche. Thinking back, Patrasche's chilly treatment of Subaru dated back to before they'd even left the city of Priestella—no, further back than that, actually. After the battle against the Witch Cult had ended, when he'd gone to fetch her from the dragon stable, she'd already been in a bad mood.

With Otto out of commission from his injuries, Subaru hadn't been able to exchange words with Patrasche through him. Still, he'd known this beloved dragon a long time by now. He ought to be able to figure out roughly what she was mad at him about.

"I mean, I get that leaving you behind in the middle of all that chaos probably made you feel lonely and anxious, but—gofah!?"

"Subaru!?"

As if to say wrong answer, her tail swung around and caught him square on, sending Subaru sprawling into the dirt. As ever, Patrasche's aggressive way of responding held no gentleness whatsoever. If Subaru gave an answer other than the one she wanted, she'd land a blow on him without the slightest hesitation.

"C-can't believe it... even I, who dodged every one of Regulus's attacks, couldn't dodge that..."

"Are you okay? Patrasche, her tail started swinging around all of a sudden again—doesn't that mean if you don't get the right answer, she's going to do it again?"

Getting up on one knee, Subaru accepted Emilia's helping hand—only to be met with this terrifying remark of hers. Looking at Patrasche at her words, sure enough, the Earth Dragon was indeed swinging her long tail, slicing the air with its tip as if threatening him. It was as if to say the power would double with every wrong answer.

Subaru couldn't help a chill running through him; his throat seized up at the thought of giving another careless answer.

"You needn't fret so. Your Earth Dragon is overflowing with consideration for you."

But at that, Julius—his role as guide interrupted, and clearly out of patience—cut in. Subaru pointed back and forth between himself and Patrasche at his remark.

"Really? I'm covered in dirt here, though? This is consideration?"

"Surely you can see the proof of her affection in her eyes? She's a deeply devoted Earth Dragon. My guess is that she's angry that she couldn't be of help to you in the midst of that chaos, that you couldn't turn to her for help. If anything, that anger is likely directed more at herself than at you."

"What is that, how much are you trying to boost your heroine points, cut it out."

Subaru had no idea how much of Julius's read was actually correct, but at the very least, it was true that the swings of Patrasche's tail stopped at his words. Looking back at Patrasche again, the Earth Dragon still turned her face away, unchanged, but—

"Somehow, hearing that and looking at her again, she looks like she's blushing."

"You too, Emilia-tan? Same here. My heart's racing, so take it easy on me sometimes, Patrasche."

Calling out to her, Subaru reached out a hand, bracing himself nervously—and Patrasche finally lowered her head, letting him touch her body covered in hard scales. It seemed safe to say that their ten-day-long cold war had, one way or another, finally come to an end.

"Are we good now? Is it okay now?"

Unable to budge from the front garden of the manor no matter how much time passed, Petra was growing impatient. She'd been sent out to guide them, and yet this much time had already been eaten up.

"If I don't hurry back, Ram-neesama's gonna be mad at me again..."

"—If you're able to worry about that, then you're still a bit short on effort."

"Pya—!?"

Petra, who had been shifting her feet impatiently, let out a shriek at the voice and snapped her spine straight. Turning to look, coming from the direction of the manor was a pink-haired maid. Unmistakable, impossible to mistake for anyone else—it was Ram. After turning her glare on Petra and freezing her solid like a stone statue, she slowly turned her gaze toward the guests and Subaru's group.

"And here I thought Petra's work had been improving lately, and just when I thought I was about to finally approve of it, this display... Ram is truly disappointed."

"S-sorry, Ram-neesama... um, you were about to approve of my work?"

"Yes. Your cooking's better than Ram's, your cleaning's more attentive than Ram's, your laundry's gotten better than Ram's, and you go to bed and wake up earlier than Ram does—I was on the verge of approving."

"That's exactly the part where you should be questioning yourself!"

Just how badly could a veteran maid be losing to a girl with barely a year's experience? Even accounting for how quick Petra was to learn, the bar was set so low it wasn't even worth discussing. At Subaru's outburst, Ram gave her now-familiar "Hah" and scoffed.

"Why would I need to question myself? All Ram holds for herself is expectation and confidence."

"That part of you, at least, I genuinely respect."

At Ram's majestic declaration, Subaru answered, exasperated but sincere. In truth, there was something to be learned from Ram's forward-facing attitude—her sheer unshakability. Though he did wish the woman herself would reflect on herself just a little more.

"If I did that, I'd feel like I wasn't Ram anymore, I suppose."

"What a chilling thought. Besides, we can't just leave our guests standing around out here forever. Let's get inside, now."

Subaru agreed with Ram's shrugged remark. They really had eaten up too much time at the entrance. Stay out here any longer and Frederica or even Roswaal might come out to see what was going on. Quite the sight it'd be—Royal Selection candidates and a Margrave, all standing around out front, doing who-knows-what.

"Is Roswaal actually staying at the manor?"

Ram, walking ahead to lead the way, tapped Petra's shoulder to release her from her stupor. At Emilia's question to her back, Ram answered "Yes," without turning around.

"Fortunately, he was in residence at the manor when the letter arrived, so he has remained here as is. As for the outline of what happened in Priestella—that was covered in the letter. The details, I imagine, are a story that has piled up."

"Yeah, well, there was a lot going on..."

"The personnel have changed rather a lot since you set out, it seems. —Have Garfiel and Otto died?"

"Don't just kill 'em off! They're not dead! They're just delayed getting back 'cause they got hurt!"

Subaru's voice cracked as he objected to whatever pause that had been, said in far too pleasant a tone. Ram, unbothered, merely shrugged in response.

"A man with no composure shows his shallowness easily. Especially you, Barusu—there's a hole in the bottom of you and the contents are leaking right out, so do be careful. Shallow, and empty inside, to boot—how dreadful."

"Don't cut me that deep, come on! And, more importantly than that..."

If the letter sent from Priestella to the manor ahead of their departure had been shared around, then everyone should already understand that their return was no ordinary homecoming.

So, choosing his words to confirm the contents of the letter's latter half, Subaru was beaten to it as Ram raised a finger first. And then—

"I already told you, don't worry. Roswaal-sama is in residence... and both the 'Sleeping Beauty' and the 'Chamber of Confinement' are ready as well, either way. Though—"

At Subaru's silence, Ram paused there, gently running her tongue over her lips. Her red tongue-tip moistened her pink lips in a manner somehow coquettish, and she said,

"Neither of those preparations were Ram's doing—they were Frederica and Petra's work, though."

"Why are you the one looking smug about that?"

In any case, even after a reunion a month in the making—well, that was simply how she was.
