Arc 6 Chapter 34: "Stepping Out of the Convenience Store, and Into a World of Wonder"

Natsuki Subaru was born in Heisei-era Japan, a product of the "relaxed education" generation.

His life spanned seventeen years, and to recount the whole of it would take, well, seventeen years all over again. Cutting all that away and summing up his current standing in a single line: "high school third-year and shut-in." To go into a bit more detail: "a good-for-nothing who, right when exams were bearing down on him, tossed aside his parents' expectations and everything else to wall himself up inside his own shell."

There was no particular reason he'd become a shut-in. On an ordinary weekday, he'd simply thought, on a whim, "ugh, getting up today sounds like a pain," and acted on that whim by skipping. From there it had snowballed, one unofficial day off after another, until before he knew it he'd become a full-fledged, parent-reducing-to-tears shut-in. He spent his days gorging on idleness, sinking ever deeper into an internet with zero human contact—

"And the end result of that is getting summoned to another world... teh-beh."

"Subaru?"

Groaning to himself as he worked through the situation, Subaru suddenly had his cheeks seized by a pair of small white hands. Looking up, he found the culprit was a silver-haired beauty standing right in front of him.

—Frankly, she was an outrageous beauty.

Long silver hair that shimmered like moonlight, violet-tinged eyes set like jewels. Her long lashes trembled as she peered at him with obvious concern, a face so far beyond the ordinary that it felt like some kind of divine artwork—the sort that would make every artist on earth snap their brushes in despair.

And this astonishingly beautiful girl was, of all things, gripping Subaru's face at a distance close enough to feel her breath, leaning in with a "Hmm?" She smelled amazing.

"Subaru?"

"Y-yes, I'm Natsuki Subaru."

Called by name again in that bell-like voice, Subaru answered with a stiff, awkward smile. Not just his face—his voice might have been shaking too. And the smile was probably creepy. For some unknown reason, this beautiful girl was being weirdly familiar with him. Otherwise she wouldn't be staring at him and talking to him from this ridiculous close range.

"Mm, it is Subaru... right? Sorry. You just looked kind of strange for a second."

"S-strange how...? Like, my eyes or something?"

"No, that's not it. Your eyes are just as terrible as always, but I meant, did you hit your head?"

"My eyes are just as terrible as always!?"

He'd meant to lighten the mood with a little joke and instead got clobbered from an angle he hadn't seen coming. Subaru's voice cracked, and at his reaction the beauty stuck out her tongue a little and apologized, "Sorry."

Cute. What even was this girl? Not just familiar—outright, overwhelmingly friendly. He did notice the "just as always" phrasing niggling at him, but—

"—Emilia, it's too soon to conclude it's nothing just from that, I suppose. There's definitely something off, isn't there."

"—? But Subaru's eyes really do look the same as always."

"Nobody's talking about Subaru's bad eyes right now! The bad eyes can wait, I suppose!"

"Quit saying bad eyes, bad eyes! Picking on a guy's weak points isn't exactly classy! What's with you two... ah, no, I mean, you two, just because you're kind of cute doesn't mean—"

Subaru shot to his feet, raising his voice at the beauty before him and the one who'd called out to her—a girl decked in an opulent dress, with drill-curled hair every bit as flamboyant as her outfit, adorable as a fairy. But his tone trailed off partway through.

He'd meant to bull through on sheer nerve, but honestly—two gorgeous strangers, one a girl and one practically still a child, and this his first time meeting them? He simply didn't have the gauge filled for that kind of bite.

—And besides, the whole situation was strange.

If this was shaping up the way Subaru imagined, these two women were likely extremely important key figures to him—more than just "first NPC," probably something much bigger. So Subaru straightened himself up, unhurried, and decided to prioritize making a good first impression.

"Ahem."

With a single throat-clear to reset the mood, Subaru turned to face the two of them properly. Stepping back so both the two wary-faced girls and the huge black lizard curled up behind them were in view, he declared—

"—Allow me to properly introduce myself. My name is Natsuki Subaru!"

He jabbed a finger sharply at the ceiling, planted his other hand on his hip, and struck a pose. No spotlight, no disco music to back it up, unfortunately, but undeterred, Subaru flashed his teeth and gave it everything he had.

"An ignorant, unenlightened, indestructible wanderer against all heaven and hell! I may be a poor excuse for a man, but please be good to me!"

"————"

The declaration rang out proudly, and the two girls and the one lizard listening to it were left utterly dumbfounded. Facing that reaction, Subaru held his pose, waiting for a response. In his own private rules, whoever moved first here would lose.

Eventually, after a good ten-odd seconds of silence, the beauty and the little girl exchanged a glance, and—

"Um... we already know that, right?"

"That was an awfully belated self-introduction, I suppose."

"Whaaat!?"

So it was that his fired-up self-introduction fell utterly flat, greeted only with that deflating verdict.

—It wasn't until a little while later that the true gravity of the situation began to sink in.

Subaru, head tilted at how poorly his frankly excellent self-introduction had landed, listened as the two of them properly filled him in—and what he learned was startling.

There was a mismatch between his memories and theirs—these girls, whom Subaru was meeting for the very first time, apparently already knew him. Naturally, Subaru had no memory of any such thing on his end. And so—

"So basically, Subaru... doesn't remember anything? Not this tower, not what happened in Priestella... no, more than that—not Ram and the others, not Beatrice. ...Not even me?"

"Er... yeah, um, that seems to be the case, yeah."

"————"

Subaru answered meekly, shoulders hunched, and the beauty's eyes went wide. Seeing the intense turmoil surfacing in them, Subaru himself felt a stab of powerful guilt.

And the beauty wasn't the only one shaken by his answer.

"No memory...? There's no way that could... surely..."

The one murmuring that was the little girl, her face gone pale. If anything, she looked even more shaken than the beauty, unable to absorb the shock. Sitting beside Subaru on a bed woven from vines, the little girl gently pinched the edge of his sleeve. Her slender fingers trembled faintly, and it made Subaru's chest ache all the more.

"————"

He wanted, somehow, to say something to comfort the two of them in their shock and distress, but unfortunately Subaru didn't have that kind of room to spare himself. Honestly, there was so much coming at him out of nowhere that he felt like he might drown in it.

—At first, Subaru had assumed that what happened to him was simply "an isekai summoning."

The very fact that "simply" could be attached to something as absurd as being summoned to another world already told you how far off the beaten path this situation was—but that assumption turned out to be not far off the mark. In fact, mostly correct.

This was not the world Subaru knew, the world he'd lived in for seventeen years.

That much was obvious just from the two girls before him—their outlandish clothing, their inhumanly beautiful faces. And if that wasn't enough evidence, the defense would like to submit as Exhibit B one enormous black lizard, roughly horse-sized. Even the biggest Komodo dragon couldn't hope to match this black lizard's bulk. Add to that a living vine-creature capable of autonomously growing beds and chairs, and the verdict was clear.

—Therefore, this was a world where common sense didn't apply: another world. Unmistakably a world belonging to the so-called fantasy genre, one Subaru had never known.

The remaining questions were why Subaru had been summoned to this world, and who the summoner was. He'd assumed it would turn out to be one of the two girls in front of him—the heroine-type beauty, standard issue for this kind of story—but here the wind of the conversation had shifted direction.

Namely: the mismatch just mentioned, between the two of them, who claimed to already know Subaru, and Subaru, who had no memory of it.

"So you're saying we'd already met, and I'm the only one who's forgotten it... is that it."

At this new piece of information added to their exchange, Subaru's expression turned grim. Just having "isekai summoning" become his reality had already been maxing out his capacity. Now, on top of that, being told there was apparently a whole stretch of time after the summoning that he'd been active in—his mental storage space had reached a thoroughly cramped state.

Honestly, from Subaru's point of view, this was the kind of claim he'd normally laugh off with a "no way that's true."

And yet the beauty's and the little girl's demeanor were utterly serious; they didn't look remotely like they were lying. Of course, Subaru himself couldn't find even a fragment of the memories they described, so he could have stubbornly refused to accept it—but seeing the state the two of them were in, his conscience wouldn't let him. Besides, if it came down to a choice between his own opinion and the beauty's, Subaru didn't trust himself enough to bet on himself being right.

Of course, that wasn't his only basis for it. —The stronger evidence lay in the state of his own body.

"...Yeah, this definitely isn't the body I had right after leaving the convenience store."

Grumbling to himself, Subaru lifted his right arm and opened and closed his fist. The arm sitting there felt, if anything, a bit more solidly built than before. His palm had several calluses he didn't recognize. They weren't from swinging a bamboo sword—and for that matter, he hadn't so much as picked up a bamboo sword in over a year.

And the changes to his arm didn't stop there.

"Ugh, that's gross..."

He flipped the raised arm over, looked at the back of his hand, and grimaced. The arm was a little more toned than before, but far more strikingly, its appearance had turned pretty grotesque. From the elbow of his right arm down to the wrist ran a pattern of black splotches—something like veins standing out, covering the arm like a tastelessly done tattoo.

If it had been a bit less biological-looking, he might have been able to pass it off as a tattoo, but no matter how he squinted at it, it was clearly part of the arm itself, and there was no denying how grotesque it was.

"————"

He pressed a fingernail firmly into one of the black splotches. Tracing it with a finger, the texture felt no different from ordinary skin. No pain, no strange numbness, nothing like that. Digging his nail in only brought back an appropriately sharp sting in return.

If he really tore into the black skin with his nail, would blood come out?

—And if it did, would that blood actually be red?

"Subaru..."

"Ah! No, no, it's nothing, really. Just, uh, thinking my skin's a little overdone on the tan side. That's all, that's all."

It was a somewhat shocking change, but he brushed off the beauty's worried voice with a joke. Fortunately—if you could call it that—Subaru wasn't the type to fuss much over injuries to his own body. It wasn't that he felt no guilt at all about damaging the body his parents gave him, but the guilt he already owed his parents had piled up so high by now that this barely registered as an addition.

Still, this was a fairly extensive area. He vaguely thought that long sleeves, and gloves if possible, would spare other people's feelings.

"Always wearing long sleeves, and legally able to equip fingerless gloves. Then, right when things get dangerous, I whip the gloves off, and there's the black arm revealed... could this man be... 'Black Arm'...! Doesn't that kind of development get you fired up?"

"————"

Waving his right arm around in mock theatrics, Subaru licked his dry lips. Including the strangeness of this arm, the credibility of the beauties' explanation was extremely high.

To begin with, Subaru's most recent memory was "right after finishing shopping at the convenience store." He should have finished his shopping in a tracksuit, but his current outfit was nothing like the tracksuit he wore all the time—more like some grimy traveling gear. His feet weren't in sneakers anymore either, and his overly tanned arm wasn't holding a convenience store bag.

He had genuinely leapt not just across worlds, but across time as well.

Step out of the convenience store, blink, and the next instant is waking up—that was Subaru's understanding of it. If that were true, then at what moment had he lost consciousness? He'd noticed himself asleep, and then been called and woken up here. So what filled the blank stretch before that?

—Summoned to another world while at a convenience store, spent some time afterward, and lost the memory of it.

Isekai summoning—the exact dream scenario he'd fantasized about more times than he could count—and here it was, arriving in a form he couldn't purely rejoice over. What a rotten gift.

There was a stretch of time Subaru had spent in a world Subaru didn't know, and during that time, an unknown fact: he'd deepened bonds with girls he didn't know.

Could he trust that? Trust their goodwill, and lean on it?

"————"

"Ah, ahh, um, well, that! I get the urge to get down about it, but come on, let's shake it off and get our spirits back up!"

While Subaru had been mulling all this over, he suddenly heard himself putting on a burst of forced cheer. On either side of him, the two girls' downcast faces made his heart boil furiously.

It was certain that this situation was hurting them, troubling them. If the cause of that lay with Subaru, well, that left something less than satisfying too, but he was probably the only one who could do anything about it.

So Subaru held out both hands to the two startled girls, playing the fool all the more deliberately.

"From what I know, this kind of temporary amnesia thing just pops back on its own eventually, so no need for the huge worried faces, okay? Like in a movie, it all wraps up neat inside two hours, everything back exactly the way it was. Tragedy's just the spice that makes the happy ending taste better!"

"Sorry, I have no idea what you just said."

"Aw, really...?"

"But..."

To Subaru's rapid-fire pep talk, the beauty's reply gave no credit for the bravado. That deflated him a little, but she quickly shook her head. She lightly rubbed the corner of her eye with a finger, and then smiled softly.

"Subaru's still Subaru, after all... yeah, that's a relief."

"Huh. R-really? If you say that, I feel a little better too—"

"There!"

"Wha— what was that all of a sudden!?"

With a spirited shout, the beauty slapped her own cheek with a force that was honestly hard to overlook. The blow, delivered with both hands cupping in from either side, rang out with a sharp crack, and her cheek flushed bright red.

Subaru panicked at the sight, but the beauty just gave her head a light shake.

"Okay, spirit's back in. Can't be moping like that. Subaru must be way more shaken up than us, and here we are with the long faces the whole time."

"Doesn't match her looks at all, this girl's got some serious grit..."

"Come on, Beatrice, you too!"

Still red in the face, the beauty pressed on with impressively spirited words, and Subaru could only marvel. Riding that same momentum, she turned next to the little girl frozen beside Subaru. The girl in the dress flinched a little at the beauty's forcefulness, but—

"I get being shocked, and I get feeling sad too... but we need to think about who's having the hardest time right now. We're the ones who need to do something about it, right?"

"B-Betty's..."

"————"

The little girl faltered, unsure what to say. But to that childish hesitation, the beauty said nothing more, only watching quietly and waiting for her answer. She could have pressed further, forced the feeling out—but she didn't. Probably because this beauty trusted this little girl.

There was a bond between the two of them that Subaru, with no memories of them, couldn't begin to understand.

"Subaru is... probably having a hard time right now, isn't he, I suppose."

"...Well, I guess you could say that?"

A small stubbornness and a bit of glibness were what made Subaru answer that way. But the little girl wasn't satisfied with it. So Subaru scratched his head and said,

"Ahh, honestly, yeah. Yeah, I want someone to help me."

"————"

At the honest confession of his cornered state, the little girl's eyes went wide with surprise. Faint, pale blue eyes, with a strange pupil shape floating in them—for some reason, to Subaru it looked like the fluttering of a butterfly's wings.

"...Ahh, honestly, really! Subaru truly is a hopeless contractor, I suppose!"

Immediately after, as if that fluttering had whipped itself into a tornado, the little girl's whole demeanor shifted dramatically. She crossed her short arms and raised her voice as if utterly exasperated. Riding that momentum, she jabbed a finger sharply right at Subaru's nose, while the beauty smiled beside them and Subaru flinched.

"Always, always bringing nothing but trouble along—it's such a nuisance for Betty, I suppose! If this doesn't stop happening, Betty really will run out of patience with you eventually, I suppose!"

"O-oh... um, so that means...?"

"Since you managed to honestly say 'help me,' Betty will overlook it just this once, I suppose. —Since Subaru's the kind of weakling who can't bear to live without Betty around, being lonely and all, I suppose."

"You're really going there!?"

Subaru was floored by how dramatically the little girl's mood had turned around. Unable to live without her, lonely without her—what an exaggerated thing to say.

"Hmph, even if you say you've forgotten, Betty will just have to remind you right away, I suppose. —That Betty's contractor, no matter what happens, never fades to sepia in her memories; he's an annoying, noisy man through and through, I suppose."

"I don't get half of what you're saying, but if that's about me, that's really something!?"

Words like "contractor" kept popping up, too significant to just let slide—but the girl who'd been downcast had lifted her face again, and Subaru held back the tactless urge to interrupt over it.

Honestly, Subaru couldn't say he'd calmly accepted this situation yet. His confusion hadn't settled, he hadn't fully faced reality, and simply swallowing their explanation as fact wasn't easy either. But even so, for now, their earnestness had gotten through to him.

"My name's Natsuki Subaru. I don't know left from right here, but I'm probably your friend. I know it's a shameless thing to ask, but I've got one request for you two."

Standing up again, Subaru jabbed a finger at the ceiling and declared his name once more. Then, finally, he held out a hand to each of them and tilted his head.

"—Tell me your names."

"————"

At those words, for some reason the beauty's throat caught, and the little girl blinked. But it lasted only a moment. The two of them paused once, then slowly, each in her own way, broke into a smile.

"My name is Emilia. Just Emilia. Nice to meet you again, Subaru."

"Betty is the great Spirit Beatrice. Subaru is Betty's contractor, I suppose."

And so, that was how they gave him their names.

Now then, having somehow gotten through what might be called a "second first meeting" with Emilia and Beatrice—

"————"

Subaru found himself, at the breakfast table—or rather, the venue-for-explaining-the-situation formerly known as the breakfast table—feeling like he was sitting on a bed of needles.

Present with him, naturally, were Emilia and Beatrice. And besides them, five more men and women—the ratio a little lopsided, but a mix of both, at least. According to Emilia and the others' explanation, these were companions who traveled with Subaru, working together to conquer this tower—though from inside it, there was no telling what the place even was.

Running down the cast of stylish characters one by one: a glamorous beauty in an outrageously sexy outfit, a beautiful girl with an alluring air that belied her age, a charming pink-haired beauty with a sharp gaze that suited her pretty face, a young girl with a soft, gentle atmosphere about her, and finally a young man with a strikingly refined face and a noble bearing—that was the lot.

Every single one of them gorgeous, and at this point Subaru was fully grasping just how absurd this other world was.

"Well, personal preference aside, I'd say silver-haired beauty Emilia-chan's out in front by a nose."

"————"

He let that pointless little murmur slip out just loud enough to be heard, but the response was arctic. Naturally so—after all, they'd only just learned Subaru had lost his memory, that he'd forgotten them. The shock would be no less than what Emilia and Beatrice had felt.

"So, I know everyone's probably surprised too, but... Subaru's having a really hard time right now. Even in a situation like this—no, especially because it's a situation like this, please lend him a hand."

That was Emilia, who'd helped explain Subaru's circumstances. Though, as he'd learned in short order, she wasn't exactly the best at explaining things. It was Beatrice who'd reined in the story as it threatened to veer off in every direction and got the explanation across properly.

Thanks to that, everyone should have gotten Subaru's situation without excess or deficiency—but—

"—Lady Emilia, may I?"

The pink-haired girl raised a hand and checked with Emilia. Emilia, addressed with the honorific "-sama" as if it were only natural, accepted it as a matter of course and dipped her chin. "Yes."

"That girl is Ram. A maid at the mansion... do you know the word 'maid'?"

"That's not a problem... but 'at the mansion,' huh. Okay, got it."

The pink-haired girl—identified as Ram from Emilia's follow-up—did indeed seem to be a maid. The mere presence of a maid suggested Emilia was fairly high-ranking, but setting that thought aside, Ram fixed Subaru with a pale pink stare and said,

"Is this some kind of prank, Barusu?"

"I get why you'd suspect that, but this is for real. Also, my name's turned into some kind of eye-gouging incantation. ...You're the older sister of that girl sleeping in the bed?"

"————"

At the outlandish nickname, Subaru frowned in response, and Ram's eyes narrowed slightly.

The topic he'd brought up was the girl who existed in that green room—nicknamed, quite literally, the "Green Room"—the one where Subaru had woken up, besides Emilia and the others. Light blue hair, a face nearly identical to Ram's—a girl who'd stayed fast asleep through the entire commotion, indifferent to it all. She was the person Subaru had first asked about, once he'd made it through the "second" first meeting with Emilia and the others.

Apparently, one of the reasons they'd come to conquer this so-called tower was to search for a way to wake her up from that endless sleep.

"Sorry that I turned into a whole hassle right when you wanted to wake your sister up. But honestly, I'm pretty much at my limit myself. Complaints, please save for post-memory-recovery me, in bulk."

"...That way of talking—are you really forgetting? That flippant attitude, that tone, they don't seem any different from the usual Barusu at all."

"Hearing that actually makes me feel a little better, like you're saying there's something good and unchanging about me. Well, I guess a person's core nature doesn't change that easily—so treat the new me the same as the old one, yeah?"

Part of it was forced cheer, but Ram eyed Subaru's unburdened attitude with suspicion. That she could find it suspicious for being unchanged meant Subaru was managing to be, well, his usual self to that degree at least. Setting aside the memory loss, that felt like it wasn't entirely a bad thing. At the very least, in terms of how to treat him, there probably wasn't anything to worry about needing to be careful around him.

"So, with that settled—whoaaa!?"

"Master~?"

Just as he wrapped up his exchange with Ram and went to take a breath, Subaru jumped, startled by a warm breath against his ear. Leaping sideways in alarm, he found, standing close enough to have practically been licking his left ear, a glamorous black-haired beauty.

Black bikini, hot pants, and a cape—an outfit that seemed a little too pointedly aimed at a particular fetish—but what startled him wasn't the clothes, it was the movement. Just a moment ago she'd been sitting cross-legged on the floor; when had she gotten up and closed the distance?

"Ah, um..."

"Shaula-suu! Master's most beloved student, and the Star Watcher of this here Pleiades Watchtower-suu!"

"Star Watcher...? And, um, 'Master' means... me?"

"Yes-suu!"

With a smile as bright as the sun, the beauty—Shaula—nodded at Subaru as he pointed at himself. That utterly guileless smile completely flipped Subaru's impression of her. Going by looks alone, she seemed the most mature woman here, and with that outfit to boot, she had an air of very grown-up allure—yet her manner was practically childlike. Or maybe more like a puppy, thrilled beyond reason at getting attention.

Indeed, Shaula lived right up to that impression, swishing the long black hair bundled at the back of her head like a tail as she said,

"Still, Master, you really never learn and never get tired of this, huh-suu? How many times are you gonna forget me before you're satisfied-suu?"

"That's way past the level of learning your lesson or getting tired of it! And wait, so I've had my memory blown away this many times before!? Did jumping across dimensions make it easier for stuff to fall out!?"

At Shaula's breezy remark, Subaru's eyes bulged as he checked the room. He was reluctantly coming to accept the fact of his memory loss, but if this had been happening frequently, that was a whole different problem. It wasn't a constitutional thing at that point—it was an illness. If some local endemic disease of this world was causing amnesia, that was well beyond a matter of "hard to live with."

"Seriously, is that true? Am I really losing my memory that often?"

"There's no way that's true, so calm down, I suppose. Shaula, don't you go confusing Subaru any more than he already is. He'd only just settled down..."

Gripping Subaru's left hand, Beatrice, who'd stayed right by his side, let out a sigh at that. She shot Shaula a narrow, half-lidded glare, and Shaula stuck out her tongue with a "Beh."

"It's not like I was trying to trouble Master or anything-suu. Ah, but if it means Master's head gets all full of just me, well, maybe that's not so bad-suu. I'll become a femme fatale-suu. Master into femme fatales-suu?"

"Right now a femme fatale would just be one more thing adding to the confusion, so no thanks. What I need right now is more like an angel or a goddess or a fairy lending a hand, so the half-naked older sister slot's already filled."

"Aw, so mean-suu! I'm this bouncy and everything, and Master's so mean-suu~"

At Shaula's pushy, high-affection antics, Subaru laughed wryly and declined the offer. Honestly, it wasn't that he disliked the attention exactly, but the goodwill she was directing at him wasn't meant for this-moment Subaru—it was for the Subaru she adored and called "Master."

Not that he could say the same wasn't true of Emilia's and Beatrice's warmth toward him, too—

"Really, mister, you sure are a handful, aren't you~?"

Amid all of Subaru's inner musings, a young girl chimed in with that comment in a syrupy-sweet voice. She looked to be about the same age as Beatrice, but unlike Beatrice, who could be mistaken for a fairy, this girl had a cute face still within the bounds of human. Her dark blue hair was braided, and she cast a sultry, sidelong glance at him that didn't match her looks at all.

"Sounds like something I'd need to have a word with your parents about, raising you like this, baby."

"Mm. You'd better not do that. Mister and Mama definitely wouldn't get along. Anyway, more importantly, isn't it about mister's memory right now~?"

"Well, it's not like there's any prospect of it coming right back real soon... more important than that right now is your name, actually. If you tell me, that'll be step one toward getting friendly again."

"Pfft... 'friendly,' he says."

Something apparently struck her as funny, because the girl put a hand to her mouth and laughed at Subaru in a light, airy voice. It was a laugh somewhere between mockery and exasperation, yet not quite either.

Subaru had absolutely no idea why she'd find that funny, but—

"Meili, mister. If you've forgotten your knack for sewing along with your memories, I'd love it if you made me another stuffed animal~"

"Oh, one of my hidden talents is out in the open now, huh. Guess that means I must've been pretty fond of Meili. Same as Beatrice—little sister type?"

"That girl is the assassin who came to kill Subaru and Betty, I suppose."

"What kind of joke is that!?"

It was a fairly heavy joke coming from Beatrice, but for some reason nobody denied it. Surely not, Subaru thought, glancing at Meili, but she just gave a faint smile and waved her hand lightly. Whatever the actual truth of it was, a child assassin was exactly the sort of thing you'd expect from a fantasy world, so he decided to just file it away as something to keep in mind.

"So, that's Ram, Shaula, and Meili's self-introductions done..."

Shelving the various open questions about Meili for now, Subaru turned his gaze to the remaining two—a pale-purple-haired beauty with a scarf, and a young man with a refined face. In this room, these two were the only ones who hadn't said a single word regarding Subaru's situation yet. As the only other man in a place this heavily weighted toward women, Subaru had been hoping for quite a bit from him.

"————"

The young man's yellow eyes trembled, his silence carrying something close to grim intensity. It was an atmosphere that made it hard to casually speak to him, and even Subaru found himself unable to break in easily. Subaru had a certain reputation for deliberately shattering a mood no matter how heavy, but even so, this young man's state was more than enough to make him hesitate to push in. It was enough to make him wonder if this young man might be the one most shaken by all of this in the entire room.

"A little..."

"Hm?"

"I'd like to give him a little time to settle down. Is that all right?"

Speaking in the young man's place, raising a hand lightly, was the beauty with the scarf. Bundled up more heavily than the others, she spoke in a masculine tone that didn't match her cute appearance at all—the so-called "boku girl" type.

Either way, her words were a relief. Subaru and Emilia had both been racking their brains over what to do about it.

"Ah, yeah, right. Sudden as it was, it definitely gave him a shock..."

"From what I can tell, it's not only that, but..."

"Ah, so you really are a boku girl."

The girl who seemed exactly like a boku girl was, indeed, a boku girl.

Toying with her scarf, wearing a thoughtful expression, she gave a wry smile at Subaru's remark.

"For now, why don't you call me Anastasia. Honestly, if it weren't for your shocking confession, I'd been planning to make a shocking confession of my own instead—but the order got flipped."

"A shocking confession, huh."

Subaru was genuinely curious what kind of confession she'd meant to make, but Anastasia only offered a faint smile and no answer. What also caught his attention was how Emilia and Beatrice looked a little bewildered at that exchange too—an impression that suggested it wasn't quite that Anastasia's confession's content was unknown to them either, but something slightly different from that.

"Regardless, if Sir Julius needs time to settle down... let's use it to prepare breakfast. Lady Emilia, might I borrow Barusu for water-fetching?"

"Huh?"

Standing up and brushing off her knees, that was Ram. Her instinct to avoid letting the situation stagnate and instead put the time to good use was admirable—though on the other hand, there was a part of it that felt a little too coldly efficient, and Emilia looked startled at the unexpected proposal. More than anything, the idea of sending memory-lost Subaru off to fetch water—

"Subaru's memory is still shaky, so maybe it'd be better to let him rest..."

"Resting won't give us any prospect of his memory coming back. Barusu said so himself. Besides, memory or no memory, Barusu still has his duties as Lord Roswaal's servant. If people think he can skip work just because he's gotten a bit more forgetful, that would be a problem."

"That's pretty harsh, Ram-chan... Ram-san?"

"————"

Given a sharp glare, Subaru shrank his shoulders and cowed. Still, Ram's argument might have a point. Subaru was aware he was currently in a difficult position, but being coddled too much was uncomfortable in its own way.

Besides that—

"————"

Looking at the young man, slumped and being supported by Anastasia, Subaru felt like it might be better if he wasn't around while the man collected himself. Or maybe Ram had sensed that and this was her way of getting Subaru out of the room on purpose.

"If that's the case, not bad, Ram..."

"...In any case, let's go, Barusu. I don't expect much from your other abilities, so at least manage to fetch water properly."

"Ah, we should go too—"

"Coddling him too much won't do Barusu any good."

Calling him by his bare name had apparently been the right call, since Subaru's words drew no objection from Ram. Instead, she stopped Emilia, who'd moved to go along with Subaru as he was led away. Emilia looked ready to argue back, but Subaru cut in with a "Now, now,"

"Ram's got a point too. Whatever's going on in my head, there's nothing wrong with my body, and from what I've heard, I'm a servant? An errand boy? Something like that, right? So let me get back on duty, at least for water-fetching."

"...Subaru isn't a servant, he's my—"

"Your what? —! Wait, is it maybe, um, like a... boyfriend kind of thing...?"

"No, not that at all."

"Not that at all, huh! Well, I guess so..."

He'd asked, nostrils flaring with hope, only to get shot down flat by Emilia. His type, no question about it—but she was way too far out of his league to ever reach. Memory or no memory, was there any way Subaru could actually get close to a flower this exquisite?

"Anyway, forget about me, go take care of him over there. Hoping he'll have pulled himself together some by the time I get back..."

"...Yeah, that's right. Okay. I'll try talking to him somehow."

Setting the joking aside, Subaru lowered his voice and pointed toward the young man for Emilia. The young man seemed to be exchanging some words with Anastasia, but he hadn't fully recovered yet.

If Emilia and Beatrice took it to heart and helped him along, that would be a relief. Though, he had a vague sense it was probably best not to expect much from Shaula and Meili on that front.

"So, why don't you count on this one, Betty. I'm leaving it to you, contract-kid."

"—. Please stop calling me that, I suppose."

"—? Huh? Okay then, Beatrice?"

"...I suppose that will do, for now. I've been entrusted with Subaru's request, I suppose."

Beatrice, who'd been holding his hand, gave a small objection to Subaru's nickname for her. At that reaction, Subaru got the sense he'd chosen wrong somehow, but couldn't pin down exactly why. Letting go of Subaru's hand as he headed off to fetch water, Beatrice glanced over at Ram.

"Sister of the sisters, I'm entrusting Subaru to you, I suppose."

"Yes. Though it's not much of a distance, and there's no telling if there's any danger to speak of."

"It was in a place where there shouldn't have been any danger that Subaru lost his memory in the first place, I suppose."

"True enough."

"Ugh, can't argue with that..."

At the exchange between Beatrice and Ram, Subaru felt a pang of frustration. Just then, Emilia came back over from the corner of the room carrying a bucket, and held it out to Subaru.

"Take it easy with Ram, okay? I'll try my best to be ready to talk by the time you get back."

"Yeah, do your best. Good luck over there too, Emilia-chan."

"—. Okay."

There seemed to be a beat of hesitation before her reply, but he couldn't press her on it. Urged on by Ram's "Barusu," Subaru left the room, bucket in hand. Just before, he glanced back at the young man's group, but they still weren't in any state to be spoken to.

"...Is it okay if I ask what that's about?"

"You mean Sir Julius? That's a fairly cruel thing to ask, Barusu."

"Cruel, huh..."

Walking alongside Ram down the corridor, Subaru brought up the group they'd left behind—especially the young man who'd been shocked into total silence. Ram's answer to that was "cruel"; all he'd managed to learn was that the young man's name was Julius, and even his name gave off a sophisticated impression.

"I'll leave him to Emilia and Beatrice, but you went out of your way to bring me out here like this. Don't tell me the actual point was really just fetching water?"

A little ways from the room, Subaru went ahead and cut straight to the point with Ram.

"————"

"Silence, huh. Well, I wasn't exactly sure about it myself, and maybe I'm way off base and about to embarrass myself horribly, but..."

"————"

"...Or wait, is it seriously just water-fetching? If so, forget I said anything, seriously, because going 'there's something going on, isn't there?' with a smug look on my face is kind of embarrassing..."

"————"

"Um?"

With no response coming, Subaru's face stiffened, wondering if he really had been off base. But mid-sentence, as he tried to cover for himself, Ram abruptly stopped walking. Subaru, who'd gone a couple steps ahead, stopped too and turned back toward her.

Then she lightly swept back her pink hair and said,

"—Enough with the pointless act, Barusu."

A faint anger kindled in her pale pink eyes as she said it.

"Huh? Act, what act..."

"We went out of our way to come somewhere else, so you should understand. It's not nice to make a woman feel foolish like this, you sly thing."

"'Sly'..."

Subaru furrowed his brow, baffled by what she was even saying. But all the while, Ram wrapped her arms around herself and shook her head, exasperated.

"Knowing you, Barusu, you've gone and thought up something no-good again. Setting aside Lady Emilia, who can't hide her feelings to save her life, and Lady Anastasia, from a different camp, and Shaula, who can't be trusted... at least tell Ram what you're plotting."

—So that way, if push comes to shove, it'll be easier to respond.

With that implication laced into her words, Ram stated it flatly to Subaru. At that, Subaru's eyes wandered, and he scratched at his black hair listlessly with his black right arm.

"Um, look. Ram, I get what you're saying, I really do, but..."

"But?"

"Sorry, but this isn't an act, or a performance, or some scheme. My memory's genuinely, completely gone. So I can't live up to what you're expecting."

"Stubborn. It's nothing new for Barusu to try shouldering everything alone, but this time that won't do. Ram has a stake in this too—it concerns Rem. So make sure you let her in on it properly."

"No, that's what I'm saying..."

Subaru was at a loss over who exactly was being stubborn here, as Ram stubbornly refused to accept his words. There was no question that accepting the fact of his amnesia was hard to swallow, but with her being this obstinate about it, he was at his wit's end too. Besides, what would putting on an act of amnesia even accomplish in the middle of a situation like conquering a tower?

"Even Ram doesn't know what that would be... but Barusu must have some scheme of his own. So spit it all out. I'll keep it a proper secret, I promise."

"Sharing a secret just between the two of us is tempting and all, but..."

He didn't know the inner workings of it, but she was certain Subaru had some plan in mind. At Ram's insistence, Subaru's exasperation went past surprise into something close to disbelief. Being cornered with such a shaky premise left him with nothing to say in response. To begin with, just how much was she overestimating him with this "if it's Subaru" logic?

But to Subaru's thoughts on the matter, Ram—

"Barusu."

"Hm? ...Hey, wait!?"

Closing the distance between them by a step, Ram swung her arm and suddenly knocked the bucket out of Subaru's left hand. Metal struck the stone floor with a sharp clang that rang down the corridor.

Startled by that, Subaru was about to raise his voice in protest at what she thought she was doing. But—

"Oof."

Grabbed by the collar, by the time he registered it his balance had already been thrown off and he was pressed against the wall. A pained grunt escaped him at the impact against his back, and only belatedly did Subaru realize this had been done to him by the small girl standing right in front of him.

Belatedly he realized it—but he had no idea what she was aiming for.

"What're you trying to—"

"Talk. If you don't cut it out, Ram has her own ideas too."

"—! You just don't get it, do you! I already told you there's nothing! It's not a lie! I—"

Threatened in a low voice, and now that she'd resorted to physical force too, Subaru's patience finally hit its limit. If even after all this she still refused to believe him—

He reached to grab the slender wrist gripping his collar—

"—Just tell me everything!!"

"————"

The roaring voice, hurled at him from up close, drained the anger right out of Subaru's ears. It wasn't just surprise—though there was surprise, too. The sudden shout startled him, and that reflexively loosened the strength in his hands, sure. But more than that, what stopped Subaru's motion was something else entirely.

"Tell me everything..."

Her voice was shaking, badly.

Despite having no memory of her, Subaru felt a jolt of shock at that. Whether that shock came simply from having the impression he'd held of her betrayed, or from some other, different reason, he couldn't say for certain.

"...Please, just tell me everything."

"R-Ram?"

"...Please."

Weakly, pressing her forehead against Subaru's chest, the girl let those trembling words slip out. All the force from just moments before had completely drained away, leaving behind something purely, achingly pained.

It wasn't a tearful voice. She wasn't that fragile. She wasn't grieving. She wasn't that gentle with herself.

—It was only that the anguish lodged in her voice had nowhere to go. —Toward whom, toward what, was it even meant to be directed?

"If Barusu forgot, then Ram... Rem..."

"————"

"Rem, that girl..."

Rem—that was her younger sister's name. The "Sleeping Beauty," identical to her, laid to rest in the bed in the Green Room.

What history lay between that girl, and the older sister before him now, and Subaru—that was something the present Subaru couldn't even begin to imagine.

But he understood, at least, that Ram was genuinely clinging to something Subaru had forgotten.

"...Sorry."

Arms hanging limp at his sides, Subaru murmured a faint apology to Ram, still pressed against his chest, her expression hidden.

Whether he was apologizing for forgetting, or for having no answer to give her—it was surely both, and probably other feelings besides, neither one alone.

"————"

Ram said nothing more after that.

Subaru, unable to say anything either, closed his eyes, feeling the tight grip of her hands clutching at his jacket.

—And watching over the two of them, helpless to do anything, was only the bucket, rolling on the floor.
