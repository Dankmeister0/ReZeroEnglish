Arc 6 Chapter 76: "A Hell Called Oneself"

"――――"

――With the girl crying and clutching her head before his eyes after experiencing 'Return by Death', Natsuki Subaru stared intently at his own palm.

He wasn't hurt by being called a monster or being cursed at. She screamed that he was out of his mind, that there was no way anyone could endure it, and he could understand that.

Subaru himself had died this many times. He was well aware that such an experience was by no means ordinary. He had simply gritted his teeth and endured it. All with the single-minded resolve that it was better than anyone other than himself—his friends, his acquaintances, people whose faces and names he didn't even know, his precious companions, the people he loved—being subjected to something worse than death. And to that――

"――Yeah, I really am amazing, Natsuki Subaru."

Clenching the palm he had been staring at, Subaru offered himself heartfelt 'praise'. He had praised himself before with glib, empty logic that carried no substance. He had used such words like a consolation, or to psych himself up. However, this present 'self-praise' was different from that.

Terribly objectively, the 'memories' and 'actual feeling' of having observed the existence known as Natsuki Subaru praised the path he had walked up to this day.

"What do you know. Turns out I'm pretty incredible after all."

And that went for the 'Natsuki Subaru' who, having lost his 'memories', returned to the time of his initial summoning to another world and managed to reach this point by deciphering the 'Books of the Dead'. From a state of total ignorance about this other world, he had accumulated experiences of 'death' that rivaled that entire year of Subaru's life, and he had returned without letting his heart shatter to pieces. Thanks to him, the lost 'memories' and the 'memories' from afterward were able to achieve integration.

"Meili, Shaula—I left things to them, and they entrusted things to me."

Meili, whose habit of killing had begun to darkly close off her future. Shaula, who had been liberated from four hundred years of waiting in vain and wished for this blessed present to continue.

"Ram and Julius are pushing themselves too hard. If those two are doing that, it must be pretty damn serious."

Ram, who always knew the best course of action and displayed a thoughtfulness more dependable than anyone else's. Julius, who had lost his own anchor yet held firm to his conviction and continued to wield his sword.

"I ended up causing nothing but trouble for Beatrice and Echidna too, of all people. Good grief, what am I like..."

Beatrice, who had devotedly supported Subaru and noticed the limits of his heart before anyone else. Echidna, who had feared the most for the amnesiac Subaru and, in the end, granted him forgiveness.

"――――"

And even if he lost his 'memories', no matter how many times, no matter how often it repeated, he would still be drawn to you and return to this path. He wanted to. He couldn't help but do so. He couldn't imagine any other path. That was how much he loved you――

"――E.M.T."

With affection and certain peace resting on his lips, Subaru raised his face. Then, he looked toward Rui, who was still covering her face and trembling.

"Hey."

"Eep!"

"...You're way too terrified."

Having her take fright so exaggeratedly at a single call, Subaru scratched his cheek with a finger. She was a girl whose appearance could fairly be called that of a child. Seeing such a young girl trembling in terror and desperately rejecting the world tugged at one's heartstrings. It seemed she was naturally endowed with a sort of ephemeral fragility that made one unconsciously want to reach out a hand. Surely it was an innate aptitude for disarming another's vigilance and slipping past their defenses. ――Perhaps it was a crystal of infant-like innocence that had been forcibly polished. That Rui Arneb, knowing nothing of the world, was like an innocent newborn babe. However――

"――Rui Arneb, it's your loss."

――Natsuki Subaru would not forgive Rui Arneb, even as she sobbed like an infant.

"――――"

Confronted head-on with the proof of her defeat, Rui's eyes snapped wide in petrification. Watching terror spread through her eyes like ink dripping onto white paper, Subaru's heart remained as calm as a placid sea.

The world wasn't filled with problems that could be brushed off with "I didn't know." And the deeds that Rui and her brothers had committed were countless outrages and atrocities that could never be forgiven. It wasn't that she lacked the chance to know. Through the 'memories' of others, she had come into contact with joy, anger, sorrow, and pleasure, with likes and dislikes; she should have had plenty of opportunities to learn.

Yet what she absorbed from them was not compassion or care for others, but far darker, hideous desires, and behavior that trampled on and spat upon the earnest lives of others. Perhaps her teachers had been bad. Her older brothers were poor role models for her life. However, never once making use of an opportunity to turn back from the path she had strayed upon was her own decision.

"If you were part of me, you might already know this. You guys... the titles you Sin Archbishops take up are the same as the Seven Deadly Sins. And while the 'Seven Deadly Sins' is a romantic phrase cherished by chuunibyous, there's something close to it called the 'Seven Heavenly Virtues'."

The Seven Deadly Sins: 'Pride', 'Envy', 'Wrath', 'Gluttony', 'Sloth', 'Lust', and 'Greed'. And the Seven Heavenly Virtues: 'Prudence', 'Courage', 'Temperance', 'Justice', 'Faith', 'Hope', and 'Charity'.

If the Seven Deadly Sins were the inescapable karma that came with being human, then the virtues were the seven promises that humans bearing those sins must never forget in order to live alongside someone else. Because they respect those in one another, humans can live together with humans.

"But you guys... you violated them."

That was why the Sin Archbishops—why Rui Arneb—had become an unforgivable, great evil.

"H-H-Hee..."

Frightened, cowering, Rui trembled as if struck and pained by Subaru's words. Every single movement of another person, of the world itself, led directly to terror. Subaru understood that sensation as well. Having tasted the otherworldly sensation of 'Return by Death', in a suffocating situation where one didn't know how to overcome 'death', even the sound of dead leaves rustling in the wind felt fatal. Because he remembered that sensation of having his soul frozen, Subaru closed his eyes. And,

"Rui Arneb, it's your loss."

"――――"

"So admit it, and release everything."

Once more, Subaru repeated Rui's defeat so that it would reach her ears. In response to the girl who remained silent, he pressed his demand further.

The return of Subaru's own 'memories' was accomplished through the encounter between the Natsuki Subaru who had lost his memories and the Natsuki Subaru just before his memories were stolen—by reenacting Subaru's footsteps in the other world via the 'Books of the Dead', tracing his own memories to restore them. He had re-experienced his stolen 'memories' by utilizing the 'Books of the Dead'. In doing so, the integration of Natsuki Subaru from before and after losing his 'memories' was accomplished, successfully resulting in the return of Natsuki Subaru.

As a byproduct, Rui, who had entered Subaru as a Witch Factor, was expelled as a foreign object. But Subaru had seen with his own eyes how she freely extracted, stripped away, and manipulated stolen 'memories' as she pleased. If that was possible, then there must be a way to save the people whose 'memories' and 'names' had been stolen――

"You said that if you felt like it, you could do anything. In that case..."

Out of pride and self-esteem, she had pulled off the incredible feat of splitting a Witch Factor in two. If she could do that, undoing the effects of her own Authority shouldn't be impossible either. If that, if only that could be granted, then perhaps――

――He could take back the people whose 'memories' and 'names' had been stolen. He could take back Rem.

"In that case, release everyone you've eaten up until now. If you do that..."

"...If we do that, what?"

"――――"

Rui shortly cut off Subaru's impassioned voice. Slumped on the white floor, pulling her knees close to herself, Rui wrapped herself in her long, long golden hair that sprawled across the floor, staring at Subaru through the gaps in her hair. Dwelling within those eyes was unmistakable terror, dyed in that single color.

"If we do that, what?"

Rui repeated the exact same question. For a moment, Subaru was caught off guard by Rui's reaction, but seeing her posture open to dialogue, he quickly regained his composure. It was far better than her being too terrified to converse. He should confront her head-on and search for a compromise to change the situation.

"Release the many people you've eaten. If their 'names' return, the honor of those who've already died can be restored. If they're alive, they can be reunited with their families. If you do that for me, I'll..."

"――Let us off? If we do that, Onii-san will let us off? You'll let us off, you say?"

Cut off once again, Subaru's expression faltered. Watching Subaru's reaction, Rui opened her mouth, baring sharp fangs, and let out a "Ha!"

"Like hell you would! Not a chance!"

Jerking her head up with sudden force, Rui screamed, her eyes wide and burning with brilliant intensity. Her eyes looking at Subaru were moist with that unchanging terror, gleaming darkly. Harboring that gleam in her eyes, Rui rocked her body violently back and forth.

"No way! No way, not happening, no chance, of course not, because there's no way, because we know there's no way, that's why! Onii-san won't let us off! Absolutely, positively! Because!"

"――――"

"Onii-san crushes his enemies! Completely smashes them to the bitter end! Utterly and thoroughly! To smithereens! Closes out with a perfect game! Because you can! So there's no way you wouldn't! There's no point in not doing it!"

As if she had just heard a first-rate joke, Rui shattered the silence and let her emotions explode. The sight of Rui bursting into this fit looked strangely distant and small to Subaru. Trying to laugh, trying to get angry, trying to grieve, Rui's expression was an utter mess; bewildered, perplexed, and pleading before an expression of terror that was none of those things.

"――――"

Every outlet for Rui's surging emotions connected directly to 'terror'. Whether the premise was joy, anger, or sorrow didn't matter. Once she realized that every conclusion led straight to 'terror', over what was she supposed to feel joy, anger, or sorrow?

Even if she believed, that outcome too led to 'terror'. Subaru understood the depth of Rui's rejection more than enough. Cursing everything in the world, cast into a tempest of paranoid suspicion, one fell into a feeling of being rendered entirely immobile. Because that was the hell called oneself that Natsuki Subaru, too, had experienced.

The reason Subaru was able to escape from such a helpless hell was surely because there was someone who took his hand, and that warmth refused to let him go.

The silver-haired girl who had saved Subaru's life and heart in his first days in this other world. The girl in a dress who had tried to protect Subaru when he was betrayed by everything. The blue-haired girl who had gently hurt and healed alongside Subaru when he was ready to throw everything away.

――That was the reason Natsuki Subaru hadn't remained trapped in hell.

"――――"

Rui Arneb, drowning in terror before his eyes, had no such emotional anchor.

That was why she couldn't overcome 'terror'. ――To her, 'Return by Death' seemed like nothing more than a curse that dragged one into an inescapable, nightmarish hell. It was a cliché, but people couldn't live alone. Therefore, for her, imprisoned by 'terror', to be saved, someone needed to reach out a hand. Just as someone had once done for Subaru, someone needed to extend a hand. However――

"――I won't save you."

――Subaru would not reach out a hand to Rui Arneb.

"――Ah."

"I won't save you. I don't feel sorry for you, either."

Rui Arneb, the Sin Archbishop of 'Gluttony', the desecrator of 'souls' lingering in this white world. No matter how lovely her appearance, no matter how pitiful and fleeting her demeanor, no matter how much she gave off an attitude that provoked an adult's protective instincts, Subaru would not forgive her.

The Sin Archbishops had committed unforgivable sins. ――That was Natsuki Subaru's conclusion.

"Not that anything I say would be any salvation to you in the first place, though."

"Hee..."

Rui's wide, terrified eyes held nothing but fear toward Subaru. Here, Subaru was the only one who could understand the terror she harbored, yet Subaru himself was the cause of that terror. In other words, her labyrinth would never open. And to Subaru, who declared head-on that he wouldn't save her, that he hated her, Rui dug her nails into the formless white world, desperately scrambling backward to put distance between herself and Subaru――

"We know... We know Onii-san's tricks! Because we were Onii-san! That's why we know what Onii-san's going to do... but we won't let you!"

"You..."

"Give back what we ate? Never, no way! After all, this is our lifeline! If this is gone, Onii-san can kill us! As long as this isn't returned, Onii-san can't kill us! It's the opposite, the total opposite! So we don't get killed, we must never give this back to Onii-san! That's why!!"

Screaming at the top of her lungs, Rui thrust her palm toward Subaru. No blast or projectile shot from that raised white hand. But Rui's aim was realized in a different form. Slowly, Subaru's field of vision distorted into white, and a change occurred in the 'Corridor of Memories'. Detecting the presence of a foreign object, the world began to squeeze itself to expel it. As a result, Subaru's existence was extracted――

"We don't want to stay in the same place as Onii-san for even one more second! It's biologically impossible! Existentially impossible! Fatedly impossible! That's why, disappear! Eating Onii-san—Onii-chan or Ani-sama! Whichever of them wants to can do it! Count us out!"

Rui's decision—irritatingly enough, it was the best move available to her. Expelling Subaru from the 'Corridor of Memories' and sending him back to reality. And entrusting the confrontation not to Rui herself, but to her brothers, Lye and Roy. If the Sin Archbishops of 'Gluttony' could choose whether to share their meal or not, then Rui could bid farewell to that worst dish known as Natsuki Subaru. Whether her brothers would harbor the same agony as herself was no concern of hers right now.

"――Guh."

Holding his ground was difficult. With nothing to grab onto, and the very ground to brace himself against being ambiguous, Subaru was helplessly the weaker party in this 'Corridor of Memories'.

"If you... don't like it... why don't you settle things yourself..."

"We won't fall for that! It's useless trying to make us kill you! You're planning to beat us down with 'Return by Death'! We won't let you! Onii-chan and Ani-sama are going to devour Onii-san completely! That's our condition for victory!!"

Baring her fangs, Rui glared at Subaru as he tried to hold his ground. He could no longer resist that will. Realizing that, he let out a deep breath. Then, Subaru's black eyes pierced straight through Rui――

"――Hee."

"If your brothers are your last lifeline, then fine. ――I'll sever that line, and I'll make you pay for what you've done. Remember that, Rui Arneb."

Thrusting a finger at the frightened, cowering Rui, he continued.

"Carry the unpleasant things and the painful things yourself. ――Don't run away from your 'memories'."

Both shame and regret were 'memories' necessary to shape who he was now. All of it had become nourishment for the person named Natsuki Subaru, and the Natsuki Subaru built by all of it had piled up countless 'deaths' to reach this point. If Natsuki Subaru was amazing, it was shame and regret that had made him so.

"That being said, I'd really rather not embarrass myself like that ever again, though."

As his vision turned entirely white, in the midst of being thrown out of this space that was nowhere, this outside that was not the world, Subaru muttered those words. And as the 'Corridor of Memories' unraveled――

Her throat trembled, her lungs trembled, her heart trembled, her soul trembled. Even Rui, who had gorged upon all manner of 'memories', could not comprehend an existence so hideous. What was truly terrifying about that thing was its unbelievable mentality.

How could he remain sane? She couldn't understand. Rui had become part of that thing, and before that, she had uprooted nearly all of that thing's 'memories'. Slowly and thoroughly, she had gnawed away at and chewed those 'memories'. That act itself was roundabout, like peeling an onion layer by layer; a process of dividing and savoring over twenty different times a life that renewed itself with every 'death'. Before experiencing 'Return by Death', the act of eating those 'memories' split into multiple portions was something that made her heart dance with excitement as a brand-new experience, but now it was nothing but a terror beyond comprehension.

In the first place, why couldn't she take the 'memories' from before that so-called 'summoning to another world' took place? The power of the Authority of 'Gluttony' was originally supposed to be able to devour a target's 'memories' all the way back to the moment of their birth. Splitting the Witch Factor that was originally supposed to be one into two, dividing their 'soul' while remaining the same person—another self in the truest sense. The primary reason such a feat was possible was likely that Rui was an existence consisting solely of a 'soul' with no physical body, having no fear of altering her own self.

Our side ended up failing, but our side over there should've been able to fulfill our role. Amazing, amazing, you slipped in so cleanly, after all. Onii-san didn't notice in the slightest! That we were inside him!"

There, 'Rui' made contact with that thing that had forgotten everything once more, intending, if all went well, to devour that thing along with Rui who was a Witch Factor. Hearing the name of a Sin Archbishop caused a hazy, disgusting feeling to swell within her chest. That was because about a month ago, in the Watergate City of Priestella, they had clashed with multiple Sin Archbishops, resulting in various conclusions, various postponements of conclusions, and an absolute mountain of terrible hardships.

So many sad things and painful feelings had been born. Their alleviation and resolution had still not been achieved even now, which was why everyone was trying their absolute hardest.

"Why do you people have to do this――!"

"Why? Why would that be? Why could it be? Why is it? Why do ya think? Why might it be, huh? Why would that be, really? Why why why, 'cause that's just how it is!"

There was no serious ring to his mocking words; the opponent had no intention of offering a proper answer. Sensing that, she put strength firmly into her cheeks. If they could talk things through, that would be best. But if the opponent wouldn't respond to dialogue, she wouldn't hesitate beyond that. That decisive shift was clearly made in this very moment.

"――Icicle Line."

"Whoa."

Simultaneously with the chant, the sound of air freezing rang out behind her as she held her ice swords at the ready. It was proof that countless ice stakes with sharpened tips had been born in midair, prepared to be fired. Looking at that, however, Lye's composure still didn't crumble. Without any sentimentality toward that fact, she simply followed her frozen fighting spirit and initiated the attack.

"――――"

The number of ice stakes unleashed in a single breath didn't quite reach a hundred, but there were enough to draw close to it. Filling the passage that appeared to be made of stone, ice dust danced as the floor, walls, and ceiling served as points of impact, the white, freezing air dealing immense damage to any existence struck directly. However――

"Sorry, Onee-san. I won't say I'm sick of seeing that... but I've seen it before. See, we're geniuses, so we can't really let an attack we've seen once hit us!"

Swinging the daggers strapped to both hands, Lye sneered, having completely weathered the tremendous onslaught of ice. She gasped at his skill, and at the fact that he had endured it unharmed. However, if the first time was fended off, then a second time, a third time, and if that still didn't work, even more.

"Think you can win if you do it a hundred times? Even if you do it a thousand times, you won't reach me, y'know?"

"Then I'll just do it ten thousand times! I won't let you go where everyone else is!"

"Ahaha! Now that's on a grand scale! Boy oh boy, you're really something else!"

Putting a hand to his forehead, Lye let out a sigh split equally between exasperation and irritation. Watching him, she quietly held her breath, intending to follow through on her own words. They were words spoken in the heat of the moment, but all she had to do was act accordingly. A hundred times, a thousand times, ten thousand times. What everyone else couldn't do, she would do herself.

So, just as she was about to step forward to do that――

"――Lye Batenkaitos!!"

"――――"

From behind, a voice that belonged neither to herself nor to Lye echoed down the passage, causing her feet to stop involuntarily. Turning around immediately, drawn by the voice, a figure emerging from around the corner leaped into her field of vision. It was a boy with black hair and slightly scary-looking eyes. With a valiant expression, he came running onto the battlefield, looking at Lye and herself fighting in the passage.

"Ah..."

Instinctively, she thought to herself, Oh no. She had to say something, yet for a beat, a blank void formed in her words. That was caused by all sorts of emotions welling up inside her upon seeing the boy who had rushed to her side. However, before 'bewilderment' could surface in the boy's black eyes as he looked at her, she panicked, realizing she had to speak, and moved her lips.

"It's dangerous, so stay back! Um, you might, well, not know who I am, but that's the enemy! Leave this to me! Even though you might not know who I am!"

She wanted him to leave this place to her, and she didn't want him to think too much about who she was. Because at least right now, in this moment, if she thought about anything other than fighting, she felt like she would burst into tears. Because she didn't want to show such weakness and cause trouble for everyone. And yet――

"――It's all right, Emilia-tan."

The boy who had rushed over called her name with just a single phrase. With just that, the anxiety, the sorrowful thoughts, the emotions she couldn't afford to dwell on that she had been trying so hard to keep from spilling out of her chest—all of it burst away. Because――

"――My name is Natsuki Subaru. Emilia-tan's number-one knight!"

Right now, 'Emilia's' chest was beating so, so hotly.
