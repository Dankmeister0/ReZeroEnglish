Arc 1 Chapter 3: "First Magic"

Was this what people meant when they said time stood still? At the entrance of the alleyway, standing just like the men from moments before, was a single young girl.

She was a beautiful girl. Her long silver hair, reaching down to her waist, was bound together, and her intelligent eyes pierced straight through to lock onto him. Beauty and youth coexisted in her gentle features, while a subtle aura of nobility gave her a precarious, almost captivating charm. She was around one hundred sixty centimeters tall. Her attire, built around a base of navy blue, lacked any flashy decorations, its simplicity ironically making her presence all the more striking. The only eye-catching detail was the embroidery on the white coat she wore, shaped like an emblem of a bird resembling a hawk. Even that solemn grandeur served as nothing more than an accessory to her beauty.

"I can't overlook any further violence. —That's far enough."

As words spun from her lips once more, a shudder of deep emotion ran through his entire body. Her voice, like silver bells, pleasant to the eardrums, carried a power that stirred the hearts of those who heard it. Subaru forgot even the situation he was in, completely overwhelmed by her sheer presence.

And the same went for the men. Facing her hostility head-on, the bloodthirsty expressions they had worn until now were nowhere to be found. Even the man holding the knife turned pale, taking a step back into the dead end.

"Wait, wait, wait! Hold on! I-I don't know what's going on, but we'll let this guy go! So go easy on us..."

"I appreciate you being reasonable. There's still time to fix this, so return what you stole from me."

"Look, I said we're sorry... Huh? What we stole?"

"Please. It's very important to me. If it were anything else, I might give up, but that is absolutely out of the question. —Right now, I have no intention of taking your lives."

Only the very end of her words, which had carried a hint of entreaty, was explicitly steeped in anger. The girl's gaze was sharp, and the open palm she extended held nothing. Yet, everyone present could feel something indescribable beginning to gather there.

"W-Wait a minute! ...Um, I think there's been a misunderstanding here!"

"...What?"

Pointing at Subaru, whom they had been kicking around—

"Er, so you didn't come to save this guy?"

"...He's dressed rather strangely. Were you in the middle of an internal dispute? Three against one isn't very admirable... but if you ask whether it has anything to do with me, I have to say no."

Perhaps thinking they were trying to dodge the issue, a touch of irritation mixed into the girl's tone. Panicked by her attitude, the men flusteredly offered an explanation.

"W-W-Wait! If this guy isn't what you're after, then we're a completely different matter! If you're talking about something being stolen, it was probably that girl from earlier!"

"Y-Yeah, exactly! The one from before! She kicked off the wall and fled across the rooftops!!"

"Further in! Past that way! At the speed she was going, she's probably already three streets over!"

At the barrage of excuses from the men, the girl's eyes locked onto Subaru. Under her gaze, which silently questioned whether the men were telling the truth, Subaru found himself unable to lie and instinctively nodded. Seeing this, the girl gave a reluctant, groaning nod of conviction.

"It doesn't seem... to be a lie. In that case, the person who stole it is past the alley...? I need to hurry."

Turning her back to them, the girl's feet headed out of the alley. The men breathed an explicit sigh of relief. And Subaru cursed his own foolishness for being swept up in the mood and blowing a once-in-a-lifetime chance. However—

"Be that as it may, this isn't a situation I can just turn a blind eye to."

Turning back around, the girl aimed her open palm toward them—and from that palm, projectiles were unleashed at the standing men. The pitch speed was Major League level, and the trajectory was an outright beanball. Three sounds similar to hard baseballs striking flesh echoed out, and the men were blown back with groans of pain.

What hit the men and fell beside Subaru with a high-pitched clatter were chunks of ice. Fist-sized blocks of ice—objects produced in defiance of both the season and physical laws—dispersed as if swallowed by the atmosphere the moment they fulfilled their purpose.

"—Magic."

The word that spontaneously escaped his mouth was the one most suited to explain the phenomenon just now. He hadn't heard any incantation or anything, but that ice had definitely been generated from the girl's palm and fired off. Having actually witnessed the scene right in front of his eyes, there was one thing he realized for the first time. Namely—

"That's a lot less fantastical than I expected... It's got a disappointingly realistic feel."

He had imagined light scattering everywhere or energy bursting out, things like that. In reality, crude ice just suddenly formed and suddenly vanished. There wasn't a shred of atmosphere or romance to it.

"You... fucking did it now."

Subaru's commentary aside, the damage to those on the receiving end of that realistic blow was severe. Two men stood up on unsteady feet. While one had passed out—likely from taking the hit in a bad spot—the remaining two were still standing despite bleeding. The man other than the knife wielder had grabbed a rusty, hatchet-like weapon and taken up a combat stance.

"At this point, who gives a damn if you're a mage or whatever! We'll surround you and beat you to death together... You think you can win two against one, huh?!"

Holding his crooked nose with one hand, the knife man yelled in anger. Faced with his shouting, the girl didn't look faltered in the slightest,

"You're right. Two against one might be tough."

"Then how about two against two? Would that be even footing?"

Picking up right where the girl left off, an androgynous, high-pitched voice vibrated through the alley air. Startled, Subaru let his gaze wander. The men showed the exact same reaction. Neither at the entrance nor inside the alley was there anyone who looked like the speaker. Baffled and confused, Subaru and the men watched as the girl extended her left hand for the three of them to see. Upon her upturned palm, perched on her white fingertips, was that.

"When you look at me with so much expectation, well... it makes me bashful."

Saying so, washing its face as if embarrassed, was an upright cat small enough to fit in a palm. Its fur was gray with floppy ears, resembling an American Shorthair most closely by Subaru's standards—except for its pink nose and unusually long tail. Seeing the strange cat, the knife man shivered with terror and yelled:

"—A Spirit Arts User?!"

"Correct. If you back off right now, I won't chase you. Decide quickly. I'm in a hurry."

Clicking their tongues in frustration at her terms, the men picked up their unconscious companion and headed out of the alley. Stepping over Subaru and passing beside him, they glanced back at the girl—

"Remember this, you brat. Next time you wander around here, you'd better watch your back."

"If you do anything to this girl, I'll curse you to the last generation, you know? Though in your case, you'd be the last generation."

Their threat was probably their desperate attempt to save face, but the reply was harsh despite its breezy tone. The palm-sized cat maintained a frivolous demeanor, but the men turned paler than ever and, this time in total silence, sprinted off into the crowd. Once they were out of sight, all that remained in the alley were the girl, her companion, and Subaru.

"—Don't move."

Forgetful of his aching body, Subaru pushed himself up, intending to offer a word of thanks at least. To him, the girl spoke in a cold, unfeeling voice.

Her eyes were thick with caution. Even if she understood that Subaru wasn't with those men, her gaze showed not a shred of belief that he was benevolent.

That aside, her dark purple eyes looking at him were captivatingly beautiful. Unaccustomed to pretty girls, Subaru flushed red at that alone and instinctively looked away. At Subaru's reaction, her watchful gaze remained, but a fearless smirk played on her lips—

"You averted your eyes because you have a guilty conscience. It seems my judgment wasn't mistaken."

"I don't know about that. That was just a boyish reaction; there wasn't a trace of evil in it."

"Puck, stay quiet. —You know who stole my insignia, don't you?"

Silencing the little cat, the girl threw her question at Subaru, wearing a smug face unmatched in recent years. However—

"Sorry to disappoint when you're so confident, but I have no idea."

"No way?!"

As her smug expression shattered, her genuine reaction peeked out from beneath. Where her dignified attitude had gone, who knew; in a complete panic, she turned to the cat on her palm—

"W-What should I do? Was this really just a total waste of time...?"

"And that situation is progressing minute by minute, too. I think you'd better hurry. They were crazy fast on their feet, so the culprit definitely has a Divine Protection of Wind."

"Why are you acting like this is someone else's problem, Puck?"

"You're the one who told me not to meddle or butt in. Besides, what are you going to do about that boy?"

Subaru gave a wry smile as the spotlight returned to him as if remembered as an afterthought. Ah—the girl seemed to finally realize his presence and state. To her, Subaru put on a brave face and stood up—

"You helping me out is more than enough. You're in a hurry, right? You should get going."

—I could even help out if you want, what do you say, miss? —That was the plan, running a hand through his hair and flashing a brilliant grin as he said it, but—

"Huh?"

"Ah, you shouldn't try to stand up by force—and too late."

His head felt heavy, his body swayed, and the hand he stretched out to brace himself missed the wall and clawed empty air. As a result, he ended up bestowing a second kiss to the ground he'd been lying on moments ago. Zero breakfall. Falling face-first, the sharp pain dragged Subaru's consciousness away.

"—So, what are you going to do?"

"It's none of my business. It's not like he's dying, so I'm leaving him."

Beyond the edge of his fading consciousness, he could faintly hear the conversation between the two (one person and one animal). As expected of an isekai fantasy, they hold a pretty severe outlook on human kindness too.

A negative thought: Am I really just going to be abandoned in an alleyway? And a positive thought: Well, I was about to die, so coming out of it alive is a blessing in itself. Reaching these two passive conclusions, Subaru's consciousness drifted farther, and farther away—

"Really?"

"Really!"

At the exact moment his consciousness snapped, he saw the silver-haired girl looking back, her face flushed red.

"—I am absolutely, positively NOT going to help him!"

—Even when she's mad, she's ridiculously cute. God bless isekai fantasy.

With that final thought, Subaru's consciousness plummeted into darkness for real this time.
