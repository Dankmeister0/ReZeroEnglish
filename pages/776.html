Arc 10 Chapter 17: "Become Ashes"

—Not good.

Ram, who had accompanied Emilia to the royal castle, summarized the current situation in those two words.

First of all, Ram’s physical condition was not good. 　Within her own awareness, this was her absolute worst state in the past year. 　Even under normal circumstances, her body was rarely in peak shape, but usually, she kept her will tautly strung to forcibly lock down her baseline state and maintain performance. —That very thread had slackened in a moment of vulnerability, right now.

"Returning to the manor once seems to have been a mistake."

Having achieved their reunion as sisters in the Empire, she had wanted to bring the awakened Rem back to the manor as soon as possible. Believing that fulfilling this might also provide a clue to restoring her 'memories', she had rushed for results. 　Thanks to that, she was able to witness the long-awaited sight of Rem wearing the maid uniform of the Roswaal Manor, but Ram’s own tension snapped as a result. Consequently, she had ended up in this pathetic state.

tIn a situation where the camp needed to unite as one to dispel the absurd suspicions cast on Subaru, she was forced to expose herself in a state far from peak condition.

「――――」

tWalking quietly down the long corridor, Ram observed the castle guards passing along the way. tLike the soldiers who had come to fetch Emilia at the royal residence, their eyes and bodies bore a tension born of a sense of duty. After silently saluting, they let Ram and Emilia pass and watched their backs. —However, a short while after walking through, Ram felt through the air that the doors of the corridor were being closed. tThe meaning behind closing doors that were customarily kept wide open was obvious.

t—Not good.

tThe closing corridor doors, the tenser-than-usual atmosphere among the soldiers, and above all, being personally received by the Captain of the Royal Knights themselves—all pointed toward extreme vigilance. This meant nothing less than that the other party’s impression of them was already heavily tilted in a negative direction. tThis was the true nature of the second bad circumstance, entirely separate from Ram's physical condition.

"...I feel like we're being locked in veeeery politely."

"—. How surprising. To think even you possessed the sensitivity to feel that way, Emilia-sama."

tSlowing her pace slightly, Emilia murmured from beside her. At her impression, which sensitively picked up on the atmosphere of the royal castle, Ram lightly raised an eyebrow in response. tReceiving that, Emilia lowered the edges of her well-shaped eyebrows with a "That's true," and continued:

"I feel like the air is all tense, or something like that. Back when I lived in the forest with Puck, whenever I went near the local village, everyone acted like this... Maybe people are veeeery scared of us."

"Indeed. It seems Barusu has messed up veeeery badly."

"...I wonder if Subaru and Beatrice are alright. Crusch-san seems to be with them too, though."

tEmilia's dark purple eyes trembled slightly as she spoke, and Ram fell silent as well. tHonestly, the odds were not in their favor. From the information provided, Miklotov McMahon, a member of the Council of Wise Men, had been murdered, and Crusch Karsten was named as the killer, with Subaru and Beatrice being pursued as accomplices. tBecause Ram had been bedridden due to ill health, she only knew the bare outlines of the trouble surrounding Crusch and the Divine Dragon Church. However, Crusch, pushed into a corner as a Royal Selection candidate, driven to desperate violence, and Subaru, present at the scene, shielding her out of sentimental sympathy—it was a plausible scenario.

"Even with Beatrice-sama accompanying him... No, she spoils Barusu, so she is useless in that regard. Barusu lacks self-control. If only he would limit his thoughts to Rem and... Emilia-sama."

tAs an older sister, her feelings were complex, but she had to acknowledge that Subaru genuinely cherished Rem, and that had served as a great salvation in many ways. Even Rem, whose memories had not returned, said various things aloud, but her special feelings for Subaru were transparent inside her heart. tThat was precisely why Subaru needed to pull hard on his own reins and strictly limit to whom he extended his kindness. —Because trying to please everyone was what had brought about this curse right now.

"Ram, are you mad by any chance?"

"...Ram has long considered Barusu's fickle heart to be utterly irredeemable. However, that is a separate matter from the suspicion currently cast upon him."

"Yes, you're right. We have to talk properly and make them understand Subaru. So that we're ready no matter what they ask us after this."

"For the time being, please prepare about ten good points about Barusu, Emilia-sama."

"Huh, ten? I can only say ten? I wonder if I can narrow it down to just ten..."

tWatching Emilia grow anxious over a reason entirely different from her own, Ram felt impressed that she could list ten good points about Subaru. Aside from his timing, what else was there to praise? tAt any rate—

"—This is a critical moment."

tLooking back, it felt as though they were facing one critical moment after another. Setting aside such useless reflections, Ram turned her eyes forward alongside Emilia. —There, Marcos had halted, pushing open the double doors at the end of the hall to urge them inside. tFollowing his gesture and stepping through the doorway, Ram felt the silent tension heighten as numerous gazes inside the room and a taut, solemn atmosphere greeted them.

t—What Ram and Emilia were guided to was not the great hall, but a council chamber inside the castle used by key figures for deliberations.

tA room where Emilia had recently accompanied Philore, witnessing her declaration to participate in the Royal Selection and the appointment of her knight. Returning to this place mere days later, their treatment had completely transformed; the surrounding attitude was like watching criminals being escorted in.

「――――」

tLooking over the room they were shown into, Ram first checked the arrangement of the guards. tTwo on either side of the door, four along the walls, an ebony circular table surrounding the center of the room in a semicircle, and multiple figures standing on standby behind it. tNone of them had drawn their swords, nor had they even placed hands on their hilts. —Even so, it was clear that this place had been arranged from the start with worst-case scenarios in mind.

"A polite invitation, indeed."

tIt took the form of courtesy, but this was by no means a welcome. tCapping with a high-class box, elegantly cutting off the opponent's retreat—the high-class way of cornering someone. As if to prove it, the sound of Marcos closing the doors behind them overlapped twice. —The double set of doors separating the room from the corridor served to meticulously prevent any conversation inside from leaking out. tBut at this moment, Ram felt that it carried a far stronger sense of physical isolation beyond merely ensuring that the discussion in this council room remained unheard.

"—Emilia!"

tJust as Ram was assessing the room's atmosphere, a voice suddenly called out. The person sitting at the edge of the round table—a woman in a nun's habit, characterized by long golden hair and red eyes. tShe kicked back her chair with a clatter, jumped over the circular table without caring about the gazes of those around her, and plunged straight into the chest of Emilia standing at the entrance. tReceiving that momentum head-on, Emilia blinked her eyes in surprise:

"Philore, you came too."

"Of course I did! This is about Emilia and your knight, Subaru. If something happened, I thought I had to help, so I rushed here as fast as I could!"

"Philore..."

"It's alright! I'm here with you! I mean, I have no idea what's going on either, but there's no way Emilia is in the wrong! No matter what happens, I'm on your side!"

tClinging to Emilia as she spoke, the woman in the nun's dress—being called Philore, Ram recognized her as the new Royal Selection candidate who had played a role in the recent chaos in the royal capital. tRam had heard from Rem that she had become friends with Emilia, but she was attached to a surprising degree. Emotional with moist crimson eyes and on the verge of tears, she exuded both genuine concern for Emilia and an anxious air about how reliable she could actually be in this situation.

tIn fact, Philore's behavior was not welcomed by the members in the council room—the present members of the Council of Wise Men, several high nobles Ram recognized, and the Royal Guards. tAmidst a barrage of throat-clearing and harsh glares, the woman sitting next to the chair Philore had kicked aside called out, "Philore-chan?" tWith an easygoing tone and gentle expression, she looked at Philore clinging to Emilia:

"You're startling everyone, you kno~w. It's no good to act so rambunctious, you kno~w."

"Rambunctious!? I'm not acting rambunctious at all, I'm regretting from the bottom of my heart! If I had stayed close to Emilia, I could have shared her anxiety from the very start! The scriptures even say: 'The salvation of one is accomplished through the prayers of ten thousand. When the dragon's grace is shared with one's neighbor, the lonely soul finds healing and achieves true salvation!'"

"Interpretations are free to be made, but delaying the discussion won't leave a good impression, you kno~w? Won't Emilia-chan be the one to suffer the loss for as long as Philore-chan throws a fit?"

"Ugggh... Sakura, you idiot...!"

"My, oh my, looks like I've been disliked, you kno~w."

tThe woman called Sakura shrugged at the teardrop-eyed Philore's childish rebuttal. It was an exchange with a vast difference in skill between the players, but Emilia gently patted the head of the outmatched Philore.

"Mm, thank you, Philore. You're veeeery reliable. Please watch over me."

"Emilia... Yes. Yes! I will watch over you with my eyes wide open!"

"—Now then, Philore-sama, please return to your seat soon."

tAt Emilia's words, Philore wiped her teary eyes vigorously and nodded repeatedly. Seeing that he had finally gotten a chance to intervene, Marcos urged her to sit. tAt his direction, Philore reluctantly parted from Emilia, hurriedly set back the chair she had kicked over, sat down, and turned her face away from Sakura beside her with a hmph.

tAfter such an interlude—

"Emilia-sama, please proceed to the central seat. As for your attendant—"

"Ram will stand behind Emilia-sama."

「――――」

"Is there a problem?"

"No, there is no problem. Well then, please go ahead."

tAs a token resistance, Ram merely refused to be separated from Emilia, taking her place diagonally behind Emilia, who was seated at the witness chair in the center. Exchanging a brief glance with Emilia, Ram responded with a small chin-nod to the pair of eyes filled with anxiety and gratitude.

"Emilia-sama, we express our gratitude to you for taking the trouble to come."

tThe one who began with such heavy formality was a bald old man clad in a particularly striking presence among those seated at the semicircular table—Bordeaux Zelgef. tOne of the Council of Wise Men, provided with seven seats, and a hero of the kingdom who had personally swung a halberd in the Demi-Human War. Known for his uncompromising stance toward demi-humans due to that past, his compatibility with Emilia could be called the absolute worst.

tHis opening remarks, while showing verbal respect to Emilia, were perhaps a manifestation of an internal reality that was completely different. tHowever, setting aside such circumstances, Emilia first noticed not Bordeaux, but the fact that the seat to his right was vacant:

"That's where Miklotov-san usually sits, isn't it?"

「――――」

"Is Miklotov-san... really...?"

"Without error, it is a confirmed fact. For the Kingdom, it is an incomparably massive blow. It can be said that since the passing of the royal family, there has been no calamity of this magnitude."

tBordeaux shook his head slowly, replying in a raspy, low voice. tIndeed, considering the role Miklotov had played in the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica up until now, every single citizen of the kingdom would have no choice but to nod in agreement with Bordeaux's answer. tHowever, the impression Emilia held toward that answer seemed different. Narrowing her eyes framed by long eyelashes:

"...Is that all?"

"...What do you mean by that?"

"I'm sure it's a terrible thing for the Kingdom, of course. But Bordeaux-san, you were all close with Miklotov-san for a long time, weren't you? Then..."

"Do you think we should be overcome with grief and shed tears? However, the Miklotov-dono we knew would desire no such thing. He was not a man who would allow his own death to rot the Kingdom's brain and let its limbs slowly undergo necrosis."

「――――」

"The way we mourn our own differs from yours. That is all."

"...I see. I'm sorry. It must be veeeery hard on you too, Bordeaux-san."

tAt his firm reply, Emilia let out a quiet sigh. tDiffering ways of expressing grief—told that, Emilia backed down, but watching from a step behind, Ram formed a different impression from the soft-hearted Emilia.

tIndeed, it was a plausible argument. That if one were to carry on the wishes of a wise man who constantly fretted over the Kingdom's future, there was no time to stand still. tBut whether there was truly no room for mourning whatsoever remained doubtful to Ram. —At the very least, her suspicions regarding this developing situation only mounted.

"To reiterate, we wish to begin the inquiry into the reason for calling Emilia-sama to this place—the suspicions that have arisen regarding Knight Natsuki Subaru."

"The suspicion about Subaru, that's..."

"Yes. —It is the suspicion that Knight Natsuki Subaru is affiliated with the Witch Cult, and moreover, holds a significant role akin to that of a Sin Archbishop."

tRam frowned at the re-presentation of the suspicion conveyed by Bordeaux's low yet well-carrying voice. tRam personally thought it was a ridiculous suspicion, and as a camp, they had no choice but to deny it, but it was also a charge whose original source was unknown. tHonestly, Subaru had a history of shielding Spica, who was originally the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony, so probing into him would reveal plenty of vulnerabilities, but the Empire should have strictly enforced an information blackout on that matter. tAs long as Spica's Authority was required, the Empire would not want her true identity to spread either. —Hence, that shouldn't lead to suspicions against Subaru.

"I can answer that question clearly. Subaru is not Witch Cult. Subaru is my number-one knight and my irreplaceable ally."

"That's right, that's right! The bond between those two is strong! I can also clearly... mmmph mmmph!"

"Philore-chan? Let's stay quiet for just a little bit, okay?"

tEmilia answered the suspicion firmly, and Philore, who cheered her on, had her mouth stuffed by Sakura beside her, but the result brought by both was the same—the air remained utterly motionless. tNot to mention Philore's defense, which bordered on heckling, even toward Emilia, who clearly denied the suspicions against Subaru, the cold heat dwelling in their gazes did not change. tEmilia herself must have felt it as well. Before the other party could speak their next word, she interjected, "May I say something?"

"First of all, I'd like you to tell me why Subaru is being suspected of being Witch Cult. I spoke to Marcos-san at the royal residence, but Bordeaux-san and the others should know Subaru's achievements so far. Regulus too, thanks to Subaru..."

"The subjugation of multiple Sin Archbishops and two of the Three Great Witchbeasts, the White Whale and the Great Rabbit, is it? While the Great Rabbit remains unconfirmed, the rest are facts supported by third-party testimony."

"Right? And yet—"

"—However, those very achievements are the focus of suspicion."

「――――」

tThe focus of suspicion—Emilia gasped at the spark of suspicion set at the center of the discussion. tIt was an outrageous argument, to say the least. To have the very achievements Subaru accomplished against the Witch Cult called suspicious was intolerable.

"In other words, you are saying Barusu's achievements are far too convenient?"

"My apologies, but an attendant has no right to speak. Please refrain from speaking."

"I overstepped my bounds. I simply couldn't believe my ears."

tTheir eyes hardened strictly at the extra remark she added, but it was Ram's true feeling. tHowever, the other party also understood that such an argument was a false accusation—at least, as it stood, nothing more than mere malicious conjecture. But since they were forcing this inquiry on Emilia, they had to possess evidence beyond just this. tAnd that was—

"I'm sorry for Ram. But what do you mean, Subaru's hard work is suspicious?"

"It is the suspicion that everything was deliberate. That both the subjugations of multiple Sin Archbishops and the Great Witchbeasts were part of a scheme to infiltrate deep into the Kingdom."

"That's...!"

"An outrageous argument, do you say? However, against the Witch Cult, which has brought bitter suffering to every nation time and again, far too many results have been achieved in a mere year and a half. The same goes for the Great Witchbeasts. It is almost as though Knight Natsuki Subaru was intentionally provided opportunities to distinguish himself."

tAs Emilia choked on her words, Bordeaux pressed further. Riding Bordeaux's momentum, those filling the seats at the semicircular table nodded one after another:

"Achieving the subjugation of the White Whale, which even the Great Conquest failed to eliminate, and immediately following that by taking down the Sin Archbishop of Sloth... to slay one deemed a living disaster is far too convenient." "According to testimonies from those involved at the time, Knight Natsuki Subaru correctly predicted the date and time of the White Whale's appearance, and even intercepted and thwarted every single scheme of the Witch Cult in advance." "I also hear he shut down that Sin Archbishop with almost zero human casualties. Considering the casualties from past Witch Cult incidents, it is utterly unbelievable." "However, even for the Witch Cult, would they cut loose someone holding a vital position?" "There are rumors that members of the Witch Cult, particularly Sin Archbishops, despise one another. The possibility that internal conflict within the Witch Cult was utilized on this occasion also—"

"—Wait!"

「――――」

tEmilia's voice forcibly cut through the stormy wave of doubt that had burst forth like a broken dam. tLikely these were arguments the other party had been waiting for the right moment to make, but none of them served as a decisive blow for suspicion, lacking the power to make the seed of doubt sprout. tEmilia understood that too. That was why, even while battered by the stormy waves, the trust in her knight dwelling in her eyes remained utterly unshaken:

"To me, every single thing you just said only looks that way because you already decided beforehand that Subaru is suspicious. It's true that Subaru has veeeery good intuition and always finds the best way, and thanks to that, we've managed to deal with the Witch Cult, but..."

"That might be having the opposite effect..."

"But! Don't look at my knight only in terms of the Witch Cult or the Great Witchbeasts! At the Pleiades Watchtower, in Vollachia... no, that's not it. Even at the very beginning, when I was in danger here in the royal capital, Subaru saved me. Completely unrelated to the Witch Cult!"

"Emilia-sama..."

tRam's eyes widened slightly at Emilia, whose cheeks were flushed as she passionately appealed. tEmilia's counterargument, while unable to wipe away a certain childishness in phrasing, directly confronted the charges Bordeaux and the others leveled. tSubaru's impeccable timing—the one thing even Ram acknowledged—was not limited to situations related to the Witch Cult. So to speak, Subaru always had good timing. That suspiciousness was not equal to the suspicion of being a Witch Cultist. tTherefore—

"—We understand your point, Emilia-sama. We have no intention of defaming Knight Natsuki Subaru's honor through what amounts to mere false accusations."

"...Really? Then that's fine. In that case—"

"—However, your knight is heavily suspected of being the successor to the Sin Archbishop of Sloth. What do you have to say regarding this?"

tRam gritted her molars, realizing that everything up to this point had been nothing more than a preliminary skirmish.

△▼△▼△▼△

—The successor to the Sin Archbishop of Sloth. That was the primary suspicion leveled against Natsuki Subaru.

tAt that question coming from outside their expectations, Emilia's dark purple eyes trembled wildly. Meanwhile, hearing it behind her, Ram's light pink eyes deepened with confusion as well.

"I heard that the Sin Archbishop of Sloth was killed by Barusu and Otto, though."

tHearing Ram's perception, she felt like Otto would say, "I'd like to object to that!", but she had no ears to lend to the absent straight man right now. tHowever, while not directly involved, Ram had indirectly been involved in the battle against the Sin Archbishop of Sloth. There was no doubt that the death of that man—the most famous Sin Archbishop in the world—had marked the beginning of the Emilia Camp's feud with the Witch Cult. tBut for Subaru to be suspected of a connection with that Sin Archbishop of Sloth—

"Is that also because Barusu conveniently played an active role in Sloth's subjugation?"

"I said your right to speak—"

"No, it's fine. That is part of it. However, the primary focus lies in a certain testimony."

"Testimony?"

tMarcos tried to admonish Ram for prompting the discussion forward in place of the stunned Emilia, but Bordeaux gestured him down with a hand and revealed the rest. tThe central focus of why Subaru was suspected—that was:

"—The testimony of the imprisoned Sin Archbishop of Wrath."

"—! Sirius's..."

"That Sin Archbishop remains bound as a prisoner in the Prison Tower even now. She has not properly responded to our interrogations, but it seems she uttered several statements, including during her transport from the Water Gate City. —'The Sin Archbishop of Sloth resides within Natsuki Subaru.'"

「――――」

"During the award ceremony held in the Royal Capital... when praising the accomplishments of subjugating the White Whale and slaying the Sin Archbishop of Sloth, I recall a detailed report was provided. Among the details was that the Sin Archbishop of Sloth was an evil spirit capable of possessing others' bodies, hopping across multiple vessels. —What do you say to that?"

tEmilia's cheeks stiffened as Bordeaux narrowed his eyes under thick eyebrows and posed the question. tIt was not that suspicion toward Subaru had been born within her. Rather, it was that the misfortune of circumstances aligning against Subaru left her overflowing emotions unable to form words. tWhat the current story amounted to was this: they suspected that Natsuki Subaru's body had been hijacked by the Sin Archbishop of Sloth, and that from that day until now, a completely different person had been acting out his persona.

tTo Emilia, who had accepted Subaru as her knight, that was an intolerable insult. tAbove all, that suspicion was to Ram, as well—

"—Utterly out of the question."

tRam only realized her voice contained sharp hostility after hearing the words leave her mouth. tYet even being aware of it, she could not suppress the emotions poured into her next words. —Because that doubt, that insult to Subaru, was equivalent to declaring that Subaru's frequent nightly visits to the sleeping Rem were also done out of deliberate calculation.

"I cannot believe the renowned Council of Wise Men would convene a meeting like this based on such a delusion. Prior to the nature of the suspicions themselves, the source of the information is the issue."

"Then are you claiming this matter is completely groundless, nothing more than our worthless malicious conjecture?"

"Naturally. Believing the claims of a Sin Archbishop is absurd. The imprisoned Wrath was said to possess power that affected others' minds. Through that effect—"

"—Ram!"

tHaving cold water poured over her hot-headed consciousness, Ram choked back her breath. tLooking up, Emilia had stood from the witness seat and turned around, sharpening the gaze on her neat, beautiful face to feign anger at an impolite attendant. Having forced her into that clumsy acting, and having become emotional enough to be scolded by Emilia, Ram reached the height of self-reproach. tNot good. —Her body's lack of composure was negatively affecting her mindset. At this rate, what had been the point of volunteering to accompany Emilia?

"Ram is sorry again. If necessary, I will order her to leave this room."

tWith Ram reflecting behind her, Emilia turned back to the semicircular table and offered that proposal. tThe uncharacteristically strict tone was her own way of covering for Ram's mistake. Having her own responsibility taken over, Ram felt only mounting frustration. tEmilia's growth was delightful. If only it weren't under these circumstances. —If only it weren't in an inquiry where the conclusion had already been predetermined.

"...There is no need for her to leave. Hereafter, refrain from speaking. Should it happen again, Emilia-sama's own standing will be questioned."

"...I will keep that in mind. My apologies."

tExpressing her apology, Ram couldn't help but feel a bitter sense that the fact she was not asked to leave confirmed her hypothesis. tBecause she sensed an intention to keep Emilia and Ram grouped together in this place.

"Thank you for not throwing Ram out. ...In that case, let me ask too. Just because Sirius said so, do you really think Subaru might be the Sin Archbishop of Sloth?"

"Of course, initially it was dismissed as nonsense. Taking the words of a captive Sin Archbishop seriously would be foolish. But Miklotov-dono was different. He was a man of insight who would not mistake delirium to be disregarded for a sign of impending disaster."

"Miklotov-san... Ah, then could it be..."

tEmilia reflexively placed a hand over her mouth, and Ram realized the same thing. tToday, after making preparations to depart for Priestella, Subaru had taken Beatrice along, claiming under pretense that he had secret business, and went to his appointment with Miklotov. What happened after that was unknown in detail, but there was no doubt it had led to bloodshed and the current situation. tPerhaps the very reason Miklotov had summoned Subaru was—

"The detailed purpose of the meeting was not shared with us. However, given that Knight Natsuki Subaru was present, alongside Duchess Karsten who cooperated in the White Whale subjugation and the subsequent battle against Sloth, we are forced to suspect: Miklotov-dono was killed because something inconvenient for Knight Natsuki Subaru had occurred."

"That's—"

"Then how are we supposed to believe someone who voluntarily abandoned the opportunity to explain himself?!"

"—!"

tIn an instant, Bordeaux's booming voice exploded, and Emilia froze as if struck full in the face by it. tBehind Emilia's reaction, Ram also felt intense resentment at the terrible alignment of circumstances—or rather, the very situation engineered in such a manner. tThe other party had indeed called Emilia after solidifying arguments that could not be rebutted from the start.

"—The worst-case scenario hit the mark."

tFrom the sequence of events starting with the intrusion into the royal residence, the worst-case scenario that Ram and Otto had envisioned back then—an unavoidable summons to the castle and the accompanying division of their camp. tWhoever painted this picture had meticulously laid out their webs up to this point.

tBordeaux's interrogation continued unabated.

"Knight Natsuki Subaru fled the scene of Miklotov-dono's murder together with Duchess Karsten. According to the testimony of those present, Knight Natsuki Subaru used a baffling technique that prohibited others' magic and controlled an unseen 'something'. This too was reported during deliberations, but the Sin Archbishop of Sloth reportedly controlled invisible hands, did he not?"

"That's a spell Subaru and Beatrice created..."

"Furthermore, hearing more: those who fell victim to the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony lose their existence from others' memories, or conversely lose their own memories. Yet there are stories that Knight Natsuki Subaru remembered those who were forgotten. Why is that?"

"Rem and Julius didn't end up all alone because Subaru remembered them!"

"Through the atrocity of the Sin Archbishop of Lust, Duchess Karsten suffered unhealing wounds. Can you explain why Knight Natsuki Subaru remained unharmed despite being subjected to the exact same attack?!"

"Subaru was the one most frustrated about that!"

"Then who is Natsuki Subaru, and where did he come from?! That man has no record anywhere prior to declaring himself your knight at the royal castle! Why?!"

"Subaru is—!"

"—Stop it already!!"

tUnder the storm of hurled questions, Emilia, her answers cut off midway, was about to raise a voice full of grief as if on the verge of tears. —In that split second, surpassing her, what echoed through the council chamber was Philore, who had stood up from her seat at the edge of the room and slammed her hands on the table.

tA single tear trickled from her crimson eyes, her lips trembling uncontrollably:

"Stop ganging up and being so hard on Emilia...! She's the most confused one here, and she's the one who wants to talk to Subaru the most!"

"...Even we are not raising harsh voices without necessity. Above all, the ones who recovered Miklotov-dono's body and recounted the details of what occurred at the residence were members of the Divine Dragon Church—Philore-sama's colleagues."

"I know that! Even Taiga getting injured... but maybe Taiga bled too much and accidentally mis-saw something?!"

"That's really a bit too pitiful for Taiga-chan, you kno~w."

"—! Anyway! I object to this kind of method! The scriptures say so too! 'No soul under this sky is without salvation. Whenever one looks up to the heavens, the Dragon's shadow is always there, promising equal salvation to all children!'"

tPhilore pulled out her scripture book and slammed it onto the semicircular table. tHer emotional counterargument poured cold water on the discussion, but it did not change Subaru's disadvantageous position. And what Ram feared was an even further proposal of suspicion accompanying these charges against Subaru. tThat was—

"—Whether Emilia-sama, her sponsor Margrave Roswaal L. Mathers of the Western Borderlands, and her camp were aware of this issue or not."

「――――」

"If this was hidden through collusion within the camp, assuming the suspicion that Knight Natsuki Subaru is the successor to the Sin Archbishop of Sloth holds true, then the very reason for entering Emilia-sama in the Royal Selection and accumulating achievements up to this day..."

tEvery result of the various efforts accumulated by Emilia, Subaru, and the camp since the Royal Selection began—all of it would be declared a fabricated fake. tEverything that everyone had shed blood, sweat, and tears to build—all of it...

"—Emilia?"

tSuddenly, a bewildered voice struck the eardrums of Ram, who was clenching her fists tightly. tIt belonged to Philore, who remained pressed against the table over her scriptures, eyes wide open in surprise. She blinked her large eyes, staring blankly at the center of the room. tDrawn by her gaze, Ram looked over as well, toward the witness stand. —She looked at Emilia standing there.

tShe had been told things that could naturally cause the world before her eyes to go black. tDespair that crumbled the ground beneath her feet and caused her to lose her footing had been hurled at her. tIt would not have been strange at all if she had wept and screamed, swept away by a storm of unreasonableness and absurdity.

tBut—

"—My name is Emilia. Just Emilia. The 'Frozen Witch' of the Elior Forest."

tEmilia declared as she stood on the witness platform, pointing a raised finger toward the heavens. tIt was a pose Ram had seen many times—the posture used by an utterly reckless boy whenever he uttered and executed the single stupidest thing in the world.

"This isn't the first time I've been looked at with those eyes of yours. I've had those eyes turned on me so many times, felt down, and then lifted my head up with a heave-ho."

「――――」

"So even if we're back to square one, I don't care. I—we won't lose."

"Emilia-sama..."

"We won't lose. —Right, Ram!"

tEmilia called out without turning around, finger pointing straight up at the sky. tThe fact that she didn't turn around to check was, infuriatingly, nothing less than proof of her trust. Emilia completely believed in what Ram would say back, and in what kind of face Ram was making. tThat was why. That was why she didn't turn around. Ram had to answer that trust.

tShe herself had offered to accompany Emilia. tPushing aside everyone else who wanted to answer Emilia's trust here, Ram had done so herself.

"—Ha! Naturally."

tRam aligned her expression and her voice to meet that trust. tValiantly and grandly, spirited and dignified—in the way characteristic of the Ram Emilia believed in, so she could support Emilia from behind as she gritted her teeth against any adversity.
