Arc 3 Interlude: "A Scene in the Dragon Carriage"

The dragon carriage rolled quietly along the highway, wheels rattling in a gentle, steady rhythm.

Wrapped in the Divine Protection that guarded the carriage, one could barely feel the jostling or the wind at all. Subaru let himself sink into that stillness, and found himself thinking back over his experience — that this might actually be the first time he'd ever ridden a dragon carriage in anything like a relaxed state of mind.

If he had to pick, the very first time — heading from Roswaal Manor to the royal capital — should have been the most carefree, at least emotionally. But partway through, his own carelessness had twisted that trip into a brutal ordeal, leaving him no room to enjoy the ride. Though, given that the experience had ended up saving him in a few ways down the line, he couldn't call it purely a bad one.

And every time after that, he'd only ever been traveling from the royal capital toward Roswaal's domain — and the reason was always the Witch's Cult, without fail, so there was no way his heart could have been at ease. As a result, this was the very first time Subaru had ridden a dragon carriage without any burning urgency, without any reason to be on edge.

That was the case — was supposed to be the case, anyway.

"So, like... Petra, aren't you sitting kind of close?"

"Am not. Is that a problem, Subaru?"

The girl looking up at him with her round, sparkling eyes, her reddish-brown hair swaying, was Petra. She'd taken the seat to Subaru's left, and ever since they'd set off she'd been clinging tight, holding his hand without letting go for a moment.

At first he'd assumed it was because she'd been through something frightening. But with her smiling like this the whole time, hand in his, it was starting to feel like—

"I'm having paternal feelings. Fatherly instincts I shouldn't even have are bubbling up inside me right now...!"

"You had Big Sister all to yourself until just now. It's fine. She said there's still time before we reach the capital."

"Petra. Just now was, um, that was different. See, Subaru and I had something very, very important we needed to talk about. It's not like I was hogging him or anything—"

"Bleh. I'm not gonna lose to Big Sister. No way."

Petra's remark sent an already-flustered Emilia into even more of a fluster. Subaru couldn't quite grasp the full situation, but nothing in Petra's manner suggested any real hostility. It was probably just an extension of their usual teasing back-and-forth — though for Emilia, who wasn't used to children, the mental toll was no laughing matter.

"Emilia-tan, don't take it so seriously. She's just a kid, you know? Smile it off, big grin, just brush it aside with a nice broad smile."

"Just because she's a child doesn't mean I can pull off something so... underhanded."

"Nobody says 'underhanded' these days..."

"Mmu, there you go teasing me again."

Emilia pouted, looking unhappy, and Subaru laughed as he offered a quick "sorry, sorry." Watching this exchange, Petra tugged at his sleeve again, looking dissatisfied, demanding he turn his attention back to her.

Flanked on both sides by pretty girls, Subaru found himself in a situation he rarely got to enjoy. Though one relationship was currently in "pending" status on a confession, and the other was more of a "please look forward to it in three years" arrangement.

—Right now, Subaru and the others were riding in a dragon carriage making its way along the Lifaus Highway toward the royal capital.

It was a different carriage from the one that had been rigged with demon ore — this one driven by one of the young men from the village's volunteer corps, with Subaru, Emilia, and the children all riding together.

At first, the people around them had tactfully tried to arrange things so Subaru and Emilia could be alone together, but then Petra had barged in with startling energy, and that was that.

Having their time together interrupted might normally have earned even Subaru's complaints, but in practice, Petra's interruption had actually helped him out quite a bit.

After all, this was the situation right after he'd made all those embarrassingly bold declarations. He'd told Emilia — who'd left his confession pending — a cool line like "I'll wait," but the fire of romantic feeling burning inside him was blazing pretty fiercely, and combined with the sense of accomplishment from having brought everything to a safe resolution, there was no telling where it might have led.

Worst case, he could have disgraced himself by demanding compensation well beyond a mere lap pillow, in advance.

"Man, that was seriously dicey for a bit there. Talking that big and then blowing it right after — that'd be way too uncool."

"Blowing what?"

"Just saying I'm grateful you're here, Petra. Oh, and hey, you kept your promise not to leave Emilia-tan alone, didn't you? Good girl, good girl."

"Ehehe."

Subaru gently stroked the hair of the girl looking up at him, conveying his gratitude to her — doubled, really. If she and the other children had let go of Emilia's hand, Emilia might have pushed herself too hard again and gotten hurt. That it hadn't turned out that way, that Subaru's efforts up until now had borne fruit, was entirely thanks to Petra and everyone else around them.

Truly, he'd been blessed with the people around him. Blessed almost to excess.

"Once things settle down, there are way too many people I need to thank..."

The Crusch Camp — Crusch herself, Ferris, and of course Wilhelm. The Anastasia Camp too, however much it galled him — Julius, and the mercenary company. Russell was so unassuming Subaru almost forgot him, but he'd need to have a word with him too, and there was that promise to hand over the cellphone.

It felt bad that it would amount to deceiving him, but given the fortunate outcome they'd managed to salvage, he'd let that particular detail slide as a forgivable minor sin. Sorry about that.

"There's a mountain of things I need to think through too, geez."

First, he absolutely had to press Roswaal about this whole incident. And beyond that, there'd need to be talks with Crusch and Anastasia about how much of the credit for the Subjugation of the White Whale and the defeat of the Witch's Cult's "Sloth" should be shared, and to what degree. Especially regarding the White Whale — if Crusch's word was to be believed, Subaru himself would be credited with a share of that achievement.

He didn't have much hunger for honor as such, but if any renown could come to him — as a member of Emilia's camp — even a little, it would surely help her too. Even if it looked shameless, he wanted to actively pursue it.

And once all these various matters were settled, there'd be the village's compensation and the cleanup to deal with as well. The road ahead was full of hardship. —And before any of that, there was a mountain Subaru had to climb first.

"Ah, um, Emilia-tan... there's something really important I need to talk to you about."

"Mm, what is it?"

Emilia, who'd had her fingers threaded through her silver hair, slowly turned to face Subaru. Her violet eyes held deep trust in them, and every time he saw that light, Subaru could genuinely feel that his actions had borne fruit. He could feel that — and yet, thinking about what effect the words he was about to say would have on those eyes, he honestly felt afraid.

The matter Subaru couldn't avoid telling Emilia about — that was, of course, the matter of Rem.

If he were to speak of gratitude, as he just had, then in this loop, no one had helped Subaru so single-mindedly, so devotedly, as Rem had.

Her deep love and devotion had gently healed Subaru's once-broken heart, had lifted him back to his feet, and had called back the will to fight against fate itself.

Without that stretch of time, Subaru wouldn't be standing here now, and without her presence, he surely could never have escaped the hell of resignation.

And through the period when Subaru had suffered and struggled the most, unable to do anything at all, it was Rem who had stayed by his side, supporting him the whole way through.

Given all she had done for him, was it really so strange that Subaru had come to hold deep affection for her?

Until now, Subaru had always believed that a person's heart — his own heart — could only ever be devoted single-mindedly to one person alone. In fact, for Subaru, the one person he truly cared for in this world had been, without question, Emilia alone.

And yet Rem's presence had settled into Subaru's heart, growing into something just as large, without changing his love for Emilia in the slightest.

So Subaru had made up his mind, clearly. It might look unseemly, like some shameless act of defiance, but since he couldn't bring himself to give up on either feeling—

—he would have both Emilia and Rem. He'd make them both his.

And he'd already obtained Rem's approval for that outrageous idea. All that remained was persuading Emilia — and Rem had suggested they do that together, the two of them—

"If I'm gonna lean on Rem for even that, that's a new low even for me."

Even if he made an opportunity for Rem to join the conversation later, it was Subaru's responsibility to be the one to bring it up first. If he was going to scheme to win the love of two girls, that much was a hurdle he had to clear himself.

He drew a breath, choosing his words as he felt his heart pound. He kept his gaze, which wanted to drift, fixed ahead, on Emilia. She was staring right back at him. Ridiculously cute.

"This is something really hard to say, but I need you to hear me out. I mean, I know I'll have to tell my actual sister eventually too, but... I wanted you to be first, Emilia-tan."

"...Mm?"

Emilia's face clouded with confusion at Subaru's stammering, unnecessary preamble. Wrestling with his own weakness for trying to stall like this, Subaru desperately raced through his thoughts.

His mind was spinning faster than it had in days — faster than ever before. He pushed his synapses to the point of sparking, straining to land on the perfect answer—!

"Actually, it's about Rem. Rem, she, um... you kind of get it, right? And I know after that whole confession, this is pretty rich coming from me, but..."

Sweat beading slowly on his forehead, Subaru pressed desperately on. He'd somehow already drifted into sounding like he was making excuses, but he figured Emilia, who wasn't dense, would understand from just this much.

Then, in front of a sweating Subaru, Emilia raised a hand and said, "Wait a second."

"Subaru, calm down. I can't tell what you're trying to say anymore, and I can tell you're trying really, really hard. Come on, you're a good boy, so take it slow."

"'Good boy' — that's kind of a depressing thing to be called! No, this is on me for not being manly enough. Okay, I'll just say it straight. Um, so, the thing is, Rem's told me she likes me too, so once you fall for me, Emilia-tan, it'll be, like... the two of you together, both mine, or, I mean—!?"

Carried by momentum, he'd just declared he wanted them both.

Even he, looking back at what he'd just said, recoiled a little at himself. Of course, Emilia's reaction would probably be something just as horrified — or maybe she'd look at him with disappointment in her eyes, with contempt. Bracing himself for that pessimistic outcome, Subaru looked at her, fearfully—

"——"

Emilia's reaction was nothing like what Subaru had imagined.

She knit her brow at his words, pressed a finger thoughtfully to her lips, and fell silent. Maybe she was weighing what he'd just said, picturing some fitting punishment for Subaru — and yet the mood wasn't one he could joke his way out of.

"Subaru."

"Y-yes."

Called by name, Subaru looked straight back at Emilia.

She, too, met his resolved gaze head-on. But it was a look tinged with confusion — a reaction Subaru couldn't understand at all.

And then, her next words were something that truly, genuinely exceeded Subaru's understanding—

"—Who's Rem?"
