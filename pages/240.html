Arc 4 Chapter 73: "The Whereabouts of Weakness"

――Just how long had it been since he'd last heard that voice?

In real time, not so much had passed since she'd fallen asleep. A week at most — the kind of stretch that could easily pass between seeing an acquaintance, even a family member. And yet, to Subaru, it didn't feel that way at all.

For a man who had thrown his life away again and again, repeating time over and over, real time held no meaning. Measured by the time his soul had lived through, it had been an unfathomably, immeasurably longer span before his eardrums — before his heart — were made to tremble by that voice again.

"――Please wake up, Subaru-kun. I'd be happy if you'd show me your face."

The words fell from directly above, onto a body lying face-down on the bare ground.

The tenderness woven into that voice, the fervent affection in it, rushed to fill Subaru's heart with warmth. Into the empty, parched vessel of his heart, heat seeped in. From nothing more than a single gentle word.

――Just how much strength was she capable of giving him?

"...This is a lie."

"No. It isn't a lie."

"You can't be here."

"If Subaru-kun wishes it, I'll be by your side, always."

"There's no way — no way you'd actually be here, right when I need someone to fix everything for me most... not something that convenient..."

"Because I always want to be the most convenient woman there could possibly be, for Subaru-kun."

His voice cracked, spilling out unsightly, weak words one after another. And yet the voice that touched his never looked down on him, never thought less of him for it. She knew. She knew it all.

She knew Subaru was weak, hopeless, so fragile he couldn't live without clinging to something, that he never had confidence, that he was forever lost. And even so, she was the girl who told him she loved him — weak as he was, unable to be strong.

"――Rem."

"Yes. Rem, who belongs to Subaru-kun."

He lifted his face. Through his tear-blurred vision, a shade of blue rose into view.

Subaru scrubbed roughly at his eyes with a filthy sleeve, evaporating the tears, and looked. At the girl standing before him.

At beloved Rem.

"Remmm..."

"Yes, it's Rem. The helpful maid who's within arm's reach whenever Subaru-kun wants her close."

"Y-you..."

Tilting her head, teasing him with that light, playful air — Rem toyed with him. At her manner, before Subaru could say a word, he felt the air leave his lungs in a peaceful rush. Something heavy that had sat inside his chest dropped away with a soft thud. His breathing eased, and the small, screaming version of himself inside his skull vanished somewhere.

Saved — so easily, so absurdly easily — Subaru could only stand in a daze. The heart he'd thought utterly hopeless, deadlocked beyond repair, had been unraveled this simply, just by having one single girl in front of him.

"Rem, you're amazing..."

"Thank you. Subaru-kun is wonderful too."

Her smiling words fit together and yet, as always, didn't quite fit together at all. Even in an exchange like that, Subaru felt happiness, and what he'd been holding back nearly broke free as tears. Still slumped on the ground, Subaru lowered his eyes, and Rem knelt before him.

"Are you all right? Have you grown tired?"

"I don't... know. Am I tired...? I haven't accomplished anything yet."

In this do-over world, Subaru had done nothing but get knocked down, arriving at not a single right answer. He felt he had no right to say a word like "tired." Everyone else was suffering far more. Everyone else was in far greater pain. Why did everyone have to suffer so — the answer was obvious.

"Because I'm weak."

"――――"

"Because I don't have enough strength."

"――――"

"If I were stronger, smarter, if I were a man who could do so much more... everyone wouldn't have had to suffer like that, to grieve, to hurt like that..."

If Subaru had been strong enough to do it all himself, everything, alone — Emilia wouldn't have had to face her past and break her heart over it; he could have comforted Beatrice, worn thin by four hundred years of solitude; he could have saved Petra and Frederica from the murderer's blade; he could have kept the people of the Sanctuary safe from the threat of the Great Rabbit that endangered their lives; he could have shared the burden with Garfiel, who threw himself recklessly at keeping outside enemies away.

All of it, everything, every bit of it was Subaru's fault. So it was Subaru who had to pay for that weakness, by wearing his own soul down to nothing. ――Or so he'd thought.

"Have I... saved no one at all?"

"Subaru-kun."

"If the world kept going even after I died — then how many times, how many people... how many did I let die and just watch it happen?"

"Subaru-kun."

"How many times did I let you die? How many times do I have to... kill you?"

"――Subaru-kun."

His body trembled from some fear deep within, and Subaru confessed his sins in a rapid, frantic rush. He wanted to spit it all out, to be judged right then and there. Before his own heart could be shattered by his own hand, he wanted someone nearby to condemn him for it. He'd told himself he wouldn't make the same mistake again, and yet he'd taken the wrong path from the very first step — he wanted someone to call him the fool he was, a fool beyond saving, and knock him flat.

"――――"

――And yet, what was given to Subaru, who begged for punishment, was a gentle, enveloping embrace.

"R-Rem."

"It's all right. It's all right, Subaru-kun."

"What's... nothing is all right... none of it..."

Nothing — not one single thing — had Subaru accomplished. If Subaru didn't act, there were so many people he couldn't save. So many people who would meet terrible ends. Rem, too, was one of the people Subaru had to save. She, of all people, had every right to condemn Natsuki Subaru — too weak, never reaching far enough, never enough.

"You... me...!"

"――I love you."

Their foreheads were pressed together, and all she whispered was love.

"――――"

His words were sealed shut. He could say nothing at all.

Close beside him, pale blue eyes gazed straight into his. In the depth of the loving devotion held in those eyes, he felt himself about to drown.

"I love you, Subaru-kun. ――So everything, everything is all right."

"Th-that's not... an answer at all..."

"It is an answer. Why Rem is here. Why Rem forgives Subaru-kun. Why Rem holds Subaru-kun in her arms — that's the whole of it. That's all there is."

At a distance close enough to feel her breath, Rem's smile wrapped Subaru's heart in an invisible hand. He couldn't move. Couldn't so much as twitch. Small hands slipped around his back, gripping tight at the hem of his clothes, and held him — tight, so tight it felt as though they might become one.

"It must have been so hard, Subaru-kun."

"――――"

"All alone, hurt so badly... it must have been so painful, Subaru-kun."

"――――ッ"

"You don't have to feel this sadness anymore. It's all right now."

Desperate just to hold himself together, unable to answer, Subaru listened as Rem went on in that sweet voice. As if gently untangling Subaru's heart, as if melting away his hardened emotions.

"Everything painful, everything hard, everything weak about Subaru-kun — Rem will take it all in your place."

"............"

"Everything you thought — that you'd protect, that you'd fight, that you'd see it through — please, entrust it all to Rem."

"............"

"There's no need at all for Subaru-kun to bear the whole weight of everything. ――Leave it all to Rem, and just rest now. It's all right to fall asleep."

"...I... I..."

"Show me, once more, the Subaru-kun that Rem loves so much."

A hand was placed against his cheek, tilting Subaru's face up, and Rem looked at him straight on. Then, as if hesitating, her lips pursed, and slowly, Rem's face drew closer. Even in his sluggish, half-formed awareness, he understood what she meant to do, what was about to happen.

Close, so close he could feel her breath, the beloved girl's lips drew near. To meet, to entwine, to drown, and be dissolved, and sink down — would that be all right?

――Right or wrong — hadn't she already forgiven him for it?

How gently, how deeply Rem's words had seeped into Subaru's heart. His frayed, ragged emotions, his soul clamoring for someone to reach out a hand — she who understood everything about him was saving him again, right now.

To powerless Subaru, Rem lent her hand. Subaru's fragile back, Rem held up, firm and sure. Down foolish Subaru's path, Rem led him by the hand.

To lean on that, to cling to it, to depend on it entirely — if that would lead him to the right answer, then what meaning was there in struggling on alone?

Worn down to nothing, no longer even sure where he stood, no longer knowing which way to walk — so, then, give it all up, hand it all over, and—

'It's easy to give up.'

"――――"

'But—'

"――――"

'――it doesn't suit you, Subaru-kun.'

He heard a voice.

"――Subaru-kun?"

Rem's puzzled voice reached him from close in front. And no wonder — her face was now blocked from his view by the hand Subaru had wedged between their two mouths, mere moments before their lips would have touched.

Pulled away from the soft, entwining sensation their tongues had been about to share, Rem's eyes wavered, lit faintly with something like hurt. Watching that wavering light through the gap between his fingers, Subaru spoke.

"――Who are you?"

"Wha――?"

"I'm asking who you are."

At Subaru's low question, Rem's throat caught, and she faltered over her words. The faint hurt in her eyes deepened, and traces of anguish carved themselves into her expression. It was a feeling so raw it seemed to claw at Subaru's chest from both inside and out, just from watching it. As if to cover for that feeling, Subaru pressed a hand flat against his own chest, and bared his teeth.

"When I was stuck at the absolute bottom, with nowhere to go, desperately wishing someone — anyone — would fix things for me, on the verge of giving up and thinking it was already hopeless... I really did wish, right then, that you'd be there."

"――――"

"I thought that you, of all people, would come and sit beside me while I was stuck like that, hugging my knees, stewing endlessly over things already past, and be gentle with me. That's what I thought."

"――――"

"And then, you'd listen to me whine, let me spill every complaint, wring every last tear and everything else dry out of me..."

"――――"

"――and then say, 'Now, stand up.' That's what you'd say."

The slenderness of the fingers that had touched him, the warmth of the skin pressed close, the enormity of the love given to him — Natsuki Subaru remembered it all, with everything he had, body and soul. So, clearly, in front of the Rem before him — in front of the imitation — he said it.

"I won't say 'please rest now.'"

"――――"

"I won't say 'give up and leave it all to Rem.'"

"――――"

"Because the woman who likes me, who I like too, who's gentle with me, who loves me — and who's stricter with me than anyone else in the world, who never goes easy on me — that woman is Rem!"

Springing to his feet, Subaru roared the words and put distance between himself and the Rem before him. Still kneeling, looking up at Subaru from below, Rem said nothing. But her expression was still, even now, drowning in the sorrow of having been rejected by him.

"That's not right. Please listen, Subaru-kun! Rem — Rem isn't like that. It's just — Rem couldn't bear to watch Subaru-kun suffer... so I just wanted you to forget the painful things, just for now, and rest. That's all it was!"

"I'll show you my weak side too. I'll show you how fragile I am. I'll show you just how hopeless, how small a man I really am. ――But the one thing I won't show you is giving up."

Subaru was a hero — that was what Rem, the real one, had once told him. That he would be Rem's hero — that was what Natsuki Subaru had decided for himself.

From the moment that promise had passed between them, Subaru had already made up his mind. ――In this world, in this life, the only place Natsuki Subaru could willingly show his weakness was in front of Rem.

Only in front of Rem — who knew Subaru was weak, and who still believed he would try to be strong regardless — could Subaru go without hiding that weakness. Not in front of Emilia. Not in front of Beatrice. Not in front of anyone else in the world. Subaru had to be strong — and the only person he could show his weak side to was Rem, and Rem alone.

"So my weakness belongs to Rem. Because Rem wraps up every bit of my weakness and hides it away for me, in exchange, giving up is the one thing I'll cling to and never let out."

"――――"

"Get out of here, imitation. ――Don't you dare comfort me wearing my Rem's face, my Rem's voice!"

Having said his piece, Subaru thrust a fist toward Rem — the imitation. The one hearing Subaru's declaration had lost all words. Just like that, she lowered her face, and slowly, quietly, rose to her feet where she stood.

"I-I heard... it's not... what I was t-told..."

"Ah...?"

Tilting her head, blue hair swaying, the imitation strung words together haltingly, stumbling over each one. Hearing it, Subaru raised his voice in confusion——

"――――"

Right before his eyes, as if a statue were blurring out of focus, Rem's form grew indistinct. A vision like static on a late-night television screen filled his sight, and after a moment of the world being hijacked, a different figure stood there in her place.

――Someone he was meeting for the first time.

A girl with pale pink hair grown down past the middle of her back, with a mild-mannered — no, more like timid — bearing. Her features were each well-formed, but hers wasn't a face that stood out for exceptional beauty. Ordinary, cute in the way most people are, more or less.

She wore white clothes with long sleeves that left not even her wrists exposed, and with hands that stayed hidden inside them, she gently cupped her own cheeks, looking at Subaru with a timid, uncertain air.

"Who... are you?"

"Th-the 'Witch of Lust'... Carmilla. Th-that's me. N-nice, nice to meet you... mm."

At the answer from the girl who'd responded to his question — Carmilla — Subaru couldn't help but catch his breath. The girl had called herself the "Witch of Lust." Which meant——

"This nonsensical space is... inside Echidna's dream, is it?"

"That's, right, but also not... maybe. Echidna-chan is watching, the 'Trial,' so... the 'Trial' is always, kind of, like a dream, yeah... it is."

"That's not really getting to the point, or — no, before that even..."

Irritation crept into Subaru at Carmilla's manner of speaking. Naturally, his eyes turned sharp, and seeing that, Carmilla gave one great shudder and clutched her head in her hands.

"Don't, d-don't hit me..."

"I'm not gonna do that. I'm not gonna do that, but... what the hell was that just now, what did you think you were doing?"

"J-just now...?"

"Imitating Rem's appearance and standing in front of me! Was that your Authority!?"

If she bore the title of one of the Witches of Sin, then surely she, too, possessed some extraordinary Authority. If the "Witch of Lust" was no exception, then she should indeed have some Authority of her own. Supposing the transformation just now was it——

"A transformation — compared to the other witches, that's a pretty orthodox sort of power."

"I-it's not a transformation, I d-didn't... do that, okay? If, if I looked like someone else to you... th-that's... because, you, wanted to see it that way, okay?"

"What?"

"So, so... I, didn't want, to meet you... but Echidna-chan told me to go, so I did... and, and she lied to me too..."

Muttering on and on like that, Carmilla's words stoked the irritation building inside Subaru. Her way of speaking, the way her eyes darted, the weakness in how she lowered her gaze whenever she sensed him looking at her — every bit of it grated on his nerves. The mewling tone, the sulky way she talked — what was all that supposed to be? On top of not making clear what she was even trying to say, she didn't seem to understand at all how thoroughly she had trampled on something precious to Subaru.

Irritating. Infuriating. He wanted to shout her down, make her understand.

"You... do you even understand what you just did...?"

"Echidna-chan, said... th-that being kind to you would be fine... and that it would go well... but then, it turned out like this... I, I didn't want this, I said so..."

"Listen to me...!!"

"E-everyone... ganging up, on me, b-bullying me... that's, that's how it is. Echidna-chan too, s-she's like that too. It's all so cruel... so, cruel..."

"Can't you tell I'm telling you to listen――!!"

Trying to shout, Subaru realized his voice had come out as a hoarse rasp, air wrung dry from his lungs. He noticed it, but the anger scorching through him mattered more, and the thought vanished from his mind at once. Even the breathlessness was a trivial concern next to the irritation clawing at his chest. He wanted to shut that whining, sniveling mouth, wanted to slam every bit of his anger and anguish into her, make her understand what she'd done——

"――Any further, and it'll cost you your life."

"――――ッ！？"

In that instant, Subaru heard a voice murmured close by his ear, and came back to his senses.

At once, what struck him was the near-suffocation from having gone without breathing, and the pain of eyes left wide open too long, dry and aching.

"Ah — ah, aah?"

"A harsh remedy, but it's for the best that you made it back. ――Face Carmilla, the 'faceless goddess' of lust, and a person forgets to breathe. In the extreme, even the heartbeat itself."

"Ehak, guh... ha, haah..."

Spitting out saliva against the breathlessness, on hands and knees on the ground, Subaru's thoughts flickered in and out. But the voice he heard passed clearly through his ears, carrying its meaning to his brain. So Subaru wiped his mouth with his sleeve, looked up at the person who must have arranged this entire situation, and once again bared his teeth.

"What — what were you scheming — Echidna."

Met with a gaze from Subaru that carried even hatred in it, the white-haired witch smoothed back her own hair, and with an easy motion propped her chin on her hand at the table.

"Isn't it obvious? ――I'm a witch. It's scheming."

She said, and smiled.
