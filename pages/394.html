Arc 5 Chapter 77: "The Nameless Knight"

Struck by the force of the shove, his body veered off the dagger's trajectory. He was in no position to dodge the blade, and with his broken knight's sword, even blocking it would not spare him a grievous wound. It was an outcome anyone could see coming — and so Julius immediately realized he'd been shielded. But whether that realization brought relief and gratitude was another matter entirely—

"Ricardo—!"

"Ah, damn, I done messed up...!"

Ricardo, who had shoved Julius aside, let out a pained groan, his eyes snapping wide open. In the same instant his name was shouted, a mist of blood erupted, filling Julius's field of vision. The blood sprayed from Ricardo's thick, powerful right arm — from the elbow down, it was simply gone, the wound baring a smooth, severed cross-section. The fur-covered arm hit the flagstones with a thud, and the great cleaver it had been gripping went tumbling across the floor with a dull clatter as well.

"What have you—"

"Ya idiot! No time for that, Julius! Get yer head up, look ahead—"

Ricardo tried to fling out a shout of encouragement to Julius, who'd instinctively caught his breath. But the words never made it out — a slashing dagger caught him across the stomach, and a hard knee shattered the bridge of his nose. Reeling backward, Ricardo crashed to the ground, sprawled flat, and "Gluttony" laughed.

"Ha-ha! Like I'd let you finish that sentence!"

"—!"

Alphard's exultant figure, and Ricardo collapsed on the ground. Looking between the two, two choices surfaced simultaneously in Julius's mind. Which to prioritize — in that instant, a sliver of hesitation opened up. And "Gluttony's" appetite never once let such an opening slip by.

"Looking away during a meal — how rude, big brother!"

"You—!"

Like a spring-loaded puppet, Alphard leapt with tricksy unpredictability. Against that shape-shifting movement, Julius's reaction came a fraction too late. An outstretched palm and his broken knight's sword crossed paths — the sensation of a palm tracing across his chest — his own thrust evaded, and immediately after, a mysterious sense of loss washed over him.

"Ahh — much obliged for the meal."

With that voice as the last thing he heard, his consciousness, for some reason, grew distant, faded away, and then—

"It's a pitiful thing to admit, but it seems my 'name' was stolen from me in the midst of the battle with 'Gluttony.' That, I presume, is the situation I find myself in now."

A single remark from Emilia had brought the fact into stark relief. Julius shrugged, a wry smile touching his lips at having been forgotten by an acquaintance.

"Your 'name' got eaten... that's really what happened? But—"

The blasphemer who devoured people's "memories" and "names," the Sin Archbishop of "Gluttony." The threat of having one's "name" devoured, one's bonds severed — Subaru had believed he understood the horror of it well enough. But seeing Julius standing right in front of him, evidently alive and well, he couldn't help but keenly realize how shallow that understanding had been.

"I figured its victims all ended up like Rem or Crusch-san..."

Crusch, whose "memories" had been devoured, who had lost herself entirely, unable to recall who she used to be. Rem, whose "name" had been devoured, her existence erased from the memory of everyone save a few exceptions, now sleeping on and on in an unbroken slumber.

Those were the two symptoms of "Gluttony's" victims that Subaru knew well. But here now, Julius had fallen into a state altogether different from either of theirs. He hadn't lost his own memories, nor had his consciousness been taken from him.

—Except that from the memories of everyone around him, his very existence had been erased.

"Seriously, nobody remembers you at all? What if we just tried everyone, one by one—"

"I've already crossed paths with both Anastasia-sama and Ricardo. It was, I must say, quite the trying experience to be treated as a total stranger by the two of them. —Not being able to thank the person who shielded me is a rather frustrating thing."

"――――"

Julius answered evenly, his emotions suppressed, but the faint tension in his cheeks and in the edges of his words struck Subaru as somehow painful to witness. Of course it did. No matter how strong Julius's sense of duty as a knight, there was no way he could bear this kind of psychological burden so easily.

The terror and despair of watching relationships built up over time crumble, of the days spent close to others simply vanishing. That was a sense of loss Subaru himself had tasted painfully, back when he'd first arrived in this other world.

"Beatrice..."

"ベティー thinks she understands what Subaru is trying to say. But unfortunately, Beatrice has no memory of this man either, I suppose. Beatrice isn't in the Forbidden Archive anymore, after all."

With just that one word called out to her, Beatrice grasped his intent and shook her head, her expression troubled. What Beatrice meant, and the confirmation Subaru had wanted from her — it was whether Beatrice remembered Julius.

Since Emilia didn't remember him, of course Beatrice shouldn't be able to remember Julius either. That should have been true — but there was a possibility Beatrice was an exception. Because Beatrice was—

"You remembered Rem, though."

"I've said this many times already, but you should really think of that as the exception, I suppose. And now, with the man standing right in front of us, that's become a certainty, I suppose."

"So in the end, it's exactly as we theorized about your memory, huh."

That had been some time ago. Once, in the Forbidden Archive, Beatrice had mentioned Rem in passing, after her "name" had been devoured by "Gluttony." Subaru had recalled that remark and pressed Beatrice on it after their Contract, once the Forbidden Archive was lost — and from that discussion, they'd arrived at a single conclusion.

"While Beako was inside the Forbidden Archive, cut off from the outside, she wasn't affected by anything outside the room. So when Rem's name got eaten, you weren't affected by it in that instant either... that was the theory, right? So once you stepped outside the room, you weren't given any special treatment anymore... huh."

"That way of putting it makes it sound as though you have a complaint about Beatrice having left the Forbidden Archive, I suppose."

"N-no, that's not it at all! I get to walk around under the sun with you, I'm mammoth happy about it!"

"Hmph. Is that so, I suppose."

Well — that exchange, where Beatrice had gotten sulky, had indeed happened. And in fact, Beatrice's peculiarity showed no effect where Julius was concerned. Beatrice's theory had been correct — the Forbidden Archive itself was what had served as the barrier protecting her memory. Rather, in this case, the problem wasn't Beatrice's peculiarity at all—

"But then, why does Subaru remember Julius-san? Just like with Rem-san before..."

Emilia finally raised the question that, by all rights, anyone should eventually have arrived at.

Subaru, the only person in this entire world who had remembered Rem after her "name" was devoured. Even Ram, her twin elder sister, had forgotten — and yet Emilia and the others had never once brought up Subaru's retained memory. Perhaps that was because, watching how devotedly Subaru treated Rem, it hadn't seemed like he was spouting nonsense about memories, nor did it seem like he was simply deluding himself into believing some falsehood. But it wasn't only that — it was also, quite simply, that no one had possessed solid evidence with which to refute Subaru's memory.

This time, though, was different. This time, there was another party besides Subaru who remembered — namely, Julius himself, the very person forgotten by the world, was conscious and present. Naturally, that meant reconciling their two perceptions with each other, and the question of why Subaru alone was treated as an exception to "Gluttony's" Authority, came to the fore.

"Doesn't Subaru have any idea? No hiding things, okay?"

"It's not that I'm trying to hide anything... I mean, there's something, but also there kind of isn't."

"Isn't that the same as hiding something?"

"I think avoiding a definite statement because I can't be sure is different from hiding something."

Dodging Emilia, Subaru turned over the possibilities for his own exception in his mind. The very first thing that came to mind was the influence of the Witch Factor slumbering within him. If the Authority to devour "memories" and "names" was the power of "Gluttony's" Witch Factor, then it made a certain kind of sense that it, like the "Unseen Hand," simply didn't work on Subaru. It was likely the power of the "Witch of Envy" that let Subaru undergo "Return by Death." Perhaps that "Witch's" power was acting to cancel out the effects of "Gluttony's" Authority.

And there was another possibility, one that came to mind from the example of Beatrice's Forbidden Archive. That the reason might be that Subaru was a tripper from another world. Subaru's origins lay in another world; he was not, in a pure sense, a being born into this world. Because he wasn't a being of this world, perhaps he simply wasn't affected by any Authority that interfered with the concepts of this world — how did that hypothesis sound?

"Though, if it's the second one, there's an easy way to check. Just have Al and Julius meet."

The one other person in this world who shared Subaru's circumstances was Al. Regarding the exception to the Authority, if the latter hypothesis were correct, then Al ought to remember Rem as well as Julius. Though, as it happened, the Al and Rem of this world had never actually met, so there'd been no way to confirm it.

"I guess this time we really can't put it off, huh."

"Subaru?"

"About Emilia-tan's question — before I answer that, there's something I want to check first. Julius, you're coming along too."

At Subaru's first words after gathering his thoughts, Emilia's expression turned displeased. She probably thought something was being kept from her, but this was something that needed verifying. Though, if the hypothesis turned out to be correct, he'd have to gloss over the shared point between himself and Al — the "tripper from another world" part — and instead give some incomprehensible explanation about how people whose hometown lay beyond the Great Waterfall simply weren't affected by Authorities.

"You're not gonna say no, right? It's about you, after all."

"—I can hardly refuse. As things stand, it seems you are rather better informed about the strange predicament I find myself in than I am. I'll follow your lead."

"Why's your attitude so weirdly high and mighty right now?"

Perhaps having regained his composure over the course of this conversation, Julius's manner, chin drawn in, had returned to its familiar, elegant bearing. Very like him, in a way — but he was being far too mentally tough given the situation he was in. Lacking in charm, one might say, to put it another way.

"Better than having you come at me all humble out of nowhere and not knowing how to deal with it, I guess... Anyway, let's head back to the shelter. The others involved should be gathering there by now, too... oh right, what about Ricardo? He was at the control tower with you. He's okay, right?"

"...He was injured shielding me, but his life should not be in danger. Ferris has examined him thoroughly as well."

"...I see. Well, that's good, then."

At Julius's reply, Subaru's inner feelings were half relief, half self-reproach. Relief that Ricardo's safety had been confirmed; self-reproach at having so thoughtlessly asked the question directly of Julius, the man forgotten by someone he'd surely fought alongside. Julius, noting how Subaru's voice trailed off small, let out a sigh, and said,

"I don't expect delicate consideration from you. I'd honestly find it easier, and less confusing for everyone else, if you just behaved as you usually do. Now then, let's head back to the shelter."

So saying, Julius gave Subaru's shoulder a casual pat.

"It's not the most pleasant thing to say, but I have no choice but to leave my introduction to you. I'd appreciate it if you could refrain, on this occasion, from repeating that spectacular flop you performed in the Royal Selection hall."

"Don't go digging up a guy's black history! Damn it, so much for worrying about you."

Shaking off the hand on his shoulder, Subaru turned his back on Julius and set off toward the shelter. Naturally, he didn't mistake Julius's remark just now for his true feelings. It was simply that Julius had judged a smart, tactful response was needed to ease Subaru's guilt. Subaru understood that. And precisely because he understood it—

"God, I'm an idiot. No — I really am an idiot."

Why, of all times, right now, could he manage nothing but an attitude that made Julius go out of his way to be considerate toward him? In a situation where Julius himself, more than anyone, must surely be feeling an unbearable, helpless loneliness and anxiety, how could Subaru get his words and judgment so wrong?

His own thoughtlessness was maddening, and Julius infuriated him in equal measure. That he could put on his usual front in a situation where anxiety would be only natural — that, too, was a form of his strength.

And that strength was surely something Subaru could never obtain no matter how much he wanted it. He couldn't just leave Julius be — with that, Subaru felt a firm sense of purpose settle over him.

Watching Subaru's back, shoulders squared, stomping his way toward the shelter, Julius let a faint smile touch the corner of his lips. It was a fragile smile — one he would never let Subaru, with his back turned, see.

"That expression — you don't intend to let Subaru see it?"

Into that small crack in his heart, a bell-like voice suddenly slipped in. Turning, he found himself meeting a pair of violet eyes gazing at him. In that sorrow-tinged gaze, Julius suppressed his smile and shook his head.

"A small vanity, the hollow resistance of a defeated man. Please don't point it out."

"A defeated man, that's..."

"We were, I believe, the only ones spared while advancing on the control tower. Made keenly aware of my own lack of strength, left standing here even denied the shame of dying in battle. A complete defeat."

Stubbornly, Julius insisted on their defeat. At his manner, Emilia's eyes wavered with pain. Perhaps, right now, Emilia could see straight through to the weakness in his heart.

"I'm sorry."

But what Emilia said next was not what Julius had intended to draw out. As Julius raised his head, Emilia wrapped her arms around her own slender shoulders and said,

"Honestly, I don't know what I should say to you right now. I'm sure I must have known you, but I don't remember, and I can't be relied on the way Subaru can, either."

"...In Subaru's case, that's hardly much of a reference point. He's the exception here, it would seem."

"Even so, I know I'm hurting you. So all I can do is apologize... and — thank you, for Subaru's sake."

"――――"

Being thanked by her, on Subaru's behalf, didn't quite sit right with him. As Julius knit his brow, Emilia let out a breath and said,

"I think if Subaru had seen the face you're making right now, it would have hurt him even more. So, thank you for trying to hide it. I'm sorry."

"Please, stop, Emilia-sama. There's nothing here worth thanking me for, and besides... besides, you think far too highly of me. It's not out of any such consideration."

It was true. Julius felt almost uncomfortable under the weight of Emilia's well-meaning gaze. It wasn't out of any such admirable motive that he'd hidden his true feelings from Subaru. The reason was much simpler, and much more petty than that.

"I simply... don't want him — Subaru — to pity me. That's all it is."

"――――"

Facing forward, watching Subaru's back as he approached the corner ahead, Julius declared this flatly. Subaru, with Beatrice clinging close and tugging at his sleeve, wore a troubled expression. It was to that Subaru that Julius did not want to show his own weakness. As for why he felt that way, the reason was—

"I feel like I've just heard Julius-san's true feelings for the first time."

Emilia, starting to walk again, offered that assessment of Julius's remark. Julius blinked in surprise, and Emilia held up a single finger.

"I might not be able to help much, but I'll do my best to speak to everyone at the shelter, so they'll believe you too. So, please believe in us, the same way you believe in Subaru. Let's go."

"...Very well. And, Emilia-sama."

"What is it?"

Called to a stop, Emilia turned back, and Julius offered her an elegant bow. It might not exist in her memories anymore, but it remained firmly etched into his own — the courtesy that had become second nature to him, both as a knight and as a noble.

"Being addressed as 'Julius-san' by you, Emilia-sama, feels rather persistently out of place. Please, call me Julius, without honorifics."

"So that's how I used to call you. ...I understand, Julius."

Emilia nodded, a finger resting on her lips. Then she fell into thought, her gaze drifting to a point in the empty air, just slightly higher than Julius. And then—

"Can I ask you one more thing?"

"Anything at all."

"There are minor spirits right next to you... no, maybe lesser spirits. Little ones like that, flitting around anxiously... can you tell?"

"—Yes, I'm aware of them. They were, after all, buds that would one day bloom at my side."

At Emilia's remark, Julius closed his eyes. With his eyes shut, he could sense them clearly nearby — lesser spirits bearing the power of the six colors, flitting about him. Only, those buds seemed not to understand themselves why they were there at all.

And so, to them,

"My words right now cannot reach them. Just as my words can no longer reach my liege, or my comrades-in-arms."
