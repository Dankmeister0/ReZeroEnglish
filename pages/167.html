Arc 4 Chapter 1: "The Place We Returned To"

――The unfortunately overcast sky seemed to mirror Subaru's current state of mind.

Six dragon carriages stood lined up in front of the Karsten mansion. In some, the villagers of Arlam (apparently that was the name of the place) who had fled together from Roswaal's domain were already seated, while the last, still-empty carriage was the special one prepared for Subaru and Emilia.

The journey ahead was long. Unlike the trip out, they hadn't put the children in the same carriage this time — there was a mountain of things Subaru needed to discuss with Emilia along the way, and even he wasn't so thick-skinned as to keep the children in the same carriage as "her."

"It's going to feel lonely, isn't it."

The voice reached him quietly from behind, as Subaru stood gazing at the line of carriages.

Turning just his head, he found Crusch looking his way. Her long green hair stirred by the damp wind, she lowered her eyes, and Subaru scratched his head.

"Staying longer isn't going to move things forward, and there's no point dragging out our stay on your hospitality forever. Though really, I probably should be taking it easy and recuperating."

Opening and closing his fist, Subaru gave a wry smile as he considered his own condition.

Looking back, Subaru had originally come to this mansion to recover from his worsened condition. Roswaal's hidden intentions had been at play in that, and irritating as it was, that clown's wish had been splendidly fulfilled. Though the pretext behind it had collapsed, and what had been achieved had also been trampled underfoot.

"If it is Natsuki Subaru-sama's wish, our house would be glad to have you stay as long as you like... but I suppose that isn't something I can simply say, is it."

"I appreciate the kindness, and I think there's a lot I could learn here, but I've got a mountain of problems of my own to deal with. Both the White Whale and 'Sloth' — if we don't settle things while both our situations are still fresh, the merchants might scoop up the whole story for themselves."

Shaking his head at Crusch's well-meaning words, Subaru thought of the Anastasia Camp, Julius included.

As things stood, if he considered only the matters of the White Whale's subjugation and 'Sloth's' subjugation, this joint operation between three camps was, weighing results against losses, an overwhelming victory for Anastasia's side.

The Crusch Camp had accomplished the feat of felling the White Whale, a beast that had eluded slayers for four hundred years — but the damage its head, Crusch herself, had suffered could not be dismissed either.

The same was true for Subaru and the Emilia Camp, who had formed the core of the fight against 'Sloth' — matters proceeding without Roswaal, who was so thoroughly versed in the circumstances, was not a good sign. The damage, too, though not as fatal as what the Crusch Camp had suffered, had left a wound too large for Subaru to bear.

The Anastasia Camp, having lost part of its mercenary band but with its candidate, knight, and other forces still intact, could not be called the main force in either subjugation, yet it had played a large role while suffering few losses — a high payout indeed.

While the scale of the losses suffered by the other two camps made them hesitate over when to openly publicize this achievement, Anastasia's camp had no such hesitation — and that mattered a great deal.

Precisely because of that, in order to keep the Anastasia Camp in check, close ties with the Crusch Camp needed to be maintained.

At Subaru's decision, laden with such calculations, Crusch let out a wistful sigh. Subaru knitted his brows at the gesture, and she waved her hand with a faintly embarrassed look, saying, "No, it's nothing."

"That was an unbecoming display of weakness. It's only that I can offer so little help to those I owe such a great debt to, and I find myself ashamed of my own inadequacy..."

"Paying back debts right away might make life easy for a debt collector, but you don't need to worry that much about it when you're the one going through a hard time. Besides, I got a proper reward out of this anyway."

Speaking to the deferential Crusch, Subaru glanced at the head of the line of carriages.

Somewhat more ornately decorated than the other carriages, it was a high-quality vehicle marking VIP treatment, and the Earth Dragon pulling that honored carriage was —

"It's a rather modest request. To heal an injured Earth Dragon and simply ask to take her in."

"A dragon I owe my life to, rather than a person. We haven't known each other long, but she might be the partner I've crossed the most brushes with death alongside, in my entire life. Though from Patrasche's side, having to keep putting up with my troubles going forward might be more than she bargained for."

"—On that point, I daresay there's no need to worry."

It was Wilhelm who gently countered Subaru's words as he glanced sidelong at the Earth Dragon — Patrasche. The old swordsman, who had until then been checking the condition of the carriage Patrasche pulled, gave a small bow by way of apology for interrupting their conversation.

"For a Diana, said to be among the more difficult-tempered of Earth Dragons, to throw herself bodily into protecting her rider is no small thing. It seems the Earth Dragon has grown quite fond of you, Subaru-dono."

"I don't really remember doing anything special. Before the fight with the White Whale, they told me I could pick whichever Earth Dragon I liked, and she was just the one who caught my eye."

Their compatibility was, no doubt, a real thing. In that respect too, he'd been lucky.

Had he been paired with any Earth Dragon other than Patrasche, he doubted he would have survived either the battle with the White Whale or the fight against 'Sloth' that followed. Which was to say —

"I'm a body that can't be satisfied by any Earth Dragon but you anymore... eek, Patrasche, you man-killer!"

He touched a hand to the smooth-textured flank and struck a coy pose, looking up at Patrasche with upturned eyes. The Earth Dragon looked down at him with an expression of utter disgust at his overfamiliar attitude, and shook her body as if trying to jam his finger.

"Whoa, that's dangerous! Even as playing hard to get, that's overdoing it. A jammed finger — I haven't had that kind of heart-pounding scare since I overdid it wiping the floor with a rag in middle school! I'm gonna have a mild trauma over this!"

"Come now, it's merely a small bit of Earth Dragon playfulness. That the two of you can bicker so affectionately is only possible because of your unshakable bond of trust."

"Does that look like bickering to you!? Feels more like I'm just running my mouth one-sidedly while Patrasche rejects me in pure body language!"

The trust that needed no words to convey itself amid life-and-death struggles came to this the moment they stepped away from the field of tragedy. Dealing with a high-and-mighty young lady was, it turned out, unexpectedly difficult. Though even with her standoffish attitude, she did let him pet her in the end.

In any case —

"They're putting my name down as part of the White Whale's slayers, and I managed to protect Emilia-tan and pull off 'Sloth's' defeat. On top of that, I get to keep an Earth Dragon I really like... as far as rewards go, that's pretty great."

"That you have no real sense of how enormous a thing it is to have slain the White Whale — that, I would say, is one of your virtues, Subaru-dono. Someday, in a more fitting form, the world will surely repay you for that great feat. I look forward to that day."

"I don't think I did anything that grand, honestly. Wasn't the reality more like I just ran around in front of the whale's nose pretending to be bait?"

At Subaru's remark, which wasn't even meant as modesty, Wilhelm gave him a look somehow like one gazing on something heartwarming. Feeling an itch of embarrassment at the warmth of that gaze, Subaru shook his head as if to throw off the sentiment.

"Well, never mind about Patrasche for now... I guess this means it's goodbye for a while with you too, Wilhelm-san. Take care of that wound, all right?"

"My apologies for the worry I've caused. It seems that, with the distance now between us, it's settled for merely bleeding for the time being. In due course, the day will surely come when I stand shoulder to shoulder with you again, Subaru-dono. Until then."

Wilhelm's wound — an old, unhealing gash left by Theresia, the previous Sword Saint. Before the fact that this old wound had reopened, the light in Wilhelm's eyes was sharp. His mind was turned toward the two Archbishops of Sin who had struck at Crusch: 'Gluttony' and 'Greed.'

If something other than the White Whale had a hand in the death of the Sword Demon's wife, then those two, who had been closest at the time, were the foremost suspects.

Subaru too, like Wilhelm, bore a deep grudge against 'Gluttony.'

The Archbishops of Sin were people he would surely face someday. They were, if he had any say in it, the very last group he'd want to lay eyes on — but 'Gluttony' alone was a different story.

There was much he had to reclaim by defeating that Archbishop of Sin, of whom he knew only the title. Crusch's memories were part of it, and above all —

"Subaru-kyun. Rem-chan's all secured, so go check on her, would you."

So said the cat-eared figure leaning out from the carriage — Ferris. Following him as he poked his face out from inside the carriage Patrasche pulled, Subaru hurried over and peered inside. There, with one section of the spacious carriage's seating removed, he saw a girl laid out on a simple, built-in bed.

Not in her familiar maid uniform, but wrapped in a thin, pale blue garment — the blue-haired girl. A girl in a sleep from which she would not wake, whose very existence had been utterly forgotten by everyone around her.

The girl who loved Subaru, and whom Subaru too had meant to love in return.

"She's not gonna get thrown around or anything, right?"

"I've taken proper care of all that, you know. I am a healer, after all, for what it's worth? Though Rem-chan's actual injuries finished healing a while ago, so it's a bit awkward calling her sick or a patient at this point, nya."

As Subaru gazed at the peaceful-looking sleeping face, Ferris's tone stayed as light as ever. But his profile stood apart from his usual playful manner, and it seemed he too carried a keen sense of his own inadequacy.

Though the reason he felt that helplessness so keenly, Subaru suspected, had less to do with Rem than with his own peerless liege.

"Are you really taking her home?"

"I'm bringing her back. It's not like staying here and resting is going to heal her either way... no, wait, I wasn't taking a jab at you just now or anything."

"I know that. Subaru-kyun's not that nasty a person, nya."

Ferris gave a wry smile at Subaru's attempt to soften what might have sounded harsh. Then, almost immediately, his pupils narrowed, and he thrust a raised finger at Subaru's face, saying, "More importantly —"

"Rem-chan's one thing, but the bigger problem here is you, Subaru-kyun."

"Me?"

"Yes, you, don't play dumb. You went and pushed your Gate too hard again, didn't you? Forcing a huge flood of mana through while it was still mid-treatment — the entrance and exit of your Gate must have taken no small amount of damage from that, nya. Doesn't your body feel sluggish or anything?"

At Ferris's question, Subaru rolled his neck and shoulders.

Round and round — his body, its external injuries already treated, showed no sign of trouble. He even tried hopping in place, but nothing resembling the problem Ferris was worried about turned up.

"Nah, seems fine. It's not like I was really using that part much to begin with anyway. Gate aside, magic's not exactly something I use in everyday life."

"That's a way of thinking only a non-mage would have, nya. From where Feri-chan's standing, not being able to use magic is nothing short of an emergency... well, if that works for you, I guess that works."

Faced with Subaru's blithe lack of concern, Ferris seemed to give up pressing the matter further. And yet, feigning retreat, he swung his head all the way around, his big eyes darting this way and that.

"But you still can't go overdoing it, that part's ongoing, you know. I think I've pushed the toxins out of your body, but that doesn't fix the Gate that got all torn up from it. It'll take a good long while to repair itself... watch yourself for two months, okay?"

"Two months, huh. That's a low bar for a guy who didn't use magic for seventeen years."

He tossed off a joke at the diagnosis like that, but then remembered that it hadn't even been two months yet since he'd entered this world in the first place. In felt time, something close to four months had passed by now, but in actual elapsed time it was about a month and a half — he felt like he'd come a remarkably long way.

Thinking of all the things that had happened in that span, though, he was starting to lose track of just how high a hurdle two months of taking it easy really was.

"Well, surely I'm not going to keep getting dragged into one incident after another that often... wait, didn't what I just said sound like a total death flag!? I swear I just heard a little ping!"

"Sorry to say, Feri-chan doesn't specialize in treating what's up in your head, nya."

At Subaru's dismay as he reflected on his own words, Ferris's response finally turned properly cold.

Taking that as a cue, Subaru judged it was about time to wrap things up. Then, having a thought, he quietly held out his hand to Ferris.

"Nya-ni?"

"No, it just occurred to me I never properly said thanks for everything you've helped with. My treatment, sure, but honestly, with the White Whale and 'Sloth' too, there were plenty of moments where I couldn't have pulled off anything reckless without you around... And for Rem, too — I'm grateful."

"...I don't think that's meant as sarcasm or a jab, but that's sure how it's coming out, nya."

"My skill, 'Not Air-Reading,' just activated. Bear with it, please."

It had been a sincere expression of thanks, but it didn't seem to have gone over well with Ferris. Still, the feeling behind it must have gotten through — Ferris took the offered hand and shook it back, and a firm handshake was made. Feeling his palm like this now,

"Your fingers are so slender, your hand's so tiny. I figured this'd turn into some 'actually his fingers are rough and manly' kind of moment, but nope, guess not."

"You think Feri-chan, done up this perfectly and adorably, would let herself show off such a disappointing development? Not a stray hair, not a patch of rough skin — all natural, nya."

Proudly, Ferris lifted his free hand and let a white leg peek out from beneath his skirt for a moment. The transparent, beautiful line of the leg was boldly bared, and Subaru dropped his shoulders in dismay.

"But you're a guy."

"That's right, Feri-chan is a man in body and soul, nya."

"You take that much pride in it and still dress like this? How does that work, as a man?"

It wasn't that Subaru was so old-fashioned as to think a delicate style of dress was unforgivable for a man — but even he could tell that Ferris's whole bearing was charging headlong down the road diametrically opposed to manliness.

At Subaru's question, Ferris put a finger to his lips and swayed his hips fetchingly.

"Because Crusch-sama said this look suits Feri-chan best, that's why. Every being has the form that makes its Soul shine brightest, and that's the one that suits it. — Feri-chan's simply answering Crusch-sama's words with everything I've got, body and soul."

"But that's..."

He was about to add that the current Crusch didn't even remember saying that, but he stopped himself partway.

Ferris understood that much without Subaru needing to say it. Putting it into words on purpose would only wound him, and above all, doing to Ferris the very thing he himself would hate having done to him was out of the question.

If it irritated Subaru when people brought up Rem as though they understood, then surely Ferris wouldn't want to hear words like that from Subaru either.

"— No matter what happens to House Karsten."

"...Huh?"

Abruptly, the words struck sharp against the eardrums of a now-silent Subaru.

It was a quiet voice, cold, its emotion frozen through — so much so that even though he'd been listening to it right in front of him only a moment before, he was slow to realize whose it was.

With Ferris's head bowed, his lowered bangs hid his expression from view.

Still in that pose, Ferris tightened his grip on Subaru's hand.

"Crusch-sama, at the very least — I will protect her, no matter what."

"F-Ferris?"

"So — like — I said —"

Before Subaru, who had gone still with a catch in his voice, Ferris suddenly brightened and raised his head. What lingered there was exactly his usual mischievous gaze, as if that instant's transformation had been nothing but a lie.

"So you'd better keep your promise too, Subaru-kyun, okay? Or the mana inside your body will run wild and you'll go mad and die, nya."

"Don't say something that horrifying with a smile on your face! And don't go threatening your ally either!"

"Less a threat, more a death sentence?"

"That's worse! Honestly."

Shaking off the handshake, Subaru turned his back on Ferris. He glanced over, checking whether all the noise had brought about any change in Rem — but that had been too much to hope for.

Letting out a small breath, he put aside that betrayed hope and stepped outside the carriage. There, just then, Emilia, carrying luggage from the mansion, was exchanging words with Crusch and the others.

"Oh, Subaru. Is Rem-san's bed all properly set up?"

"Yeah, Ferris nailed it. So even if Patrasche and I pull off some flawless combination move worthy of the Kinoshita Grand Circus, she won't go tumbling out."

"I don't really get it, but I have a really bad feeling about it, so please don't do any Grand Circus stuff, okay?"

"Aw, too bad. Here I was hoping a racing heart would work the suspension bridge effect on you, Emilia-tan."

Dubbed the "Put-Her-Life-in-Danger-with-My-Own-Driving-and-Make-Her-Wonder-if-This-Racing-Heart-Is-Actually-Love!?" operation — a scheme that was pretty much a solid lump of arsonist-turned-firefighter logic.

In any case, Subaru couldn't stop a pain — slight enough not to be noticed — from stabbing at his chest each time the name "Rem-san" came out of Emilia's mouth.

For a moment, Emilia's eyes narrowed as she watched Subaru, having cracked his joke, fall silent. But before she could say anything about it, Ferris came out from behind Subaru and landed on the ground.

"Well then, looks like the carriages are all ready, and dragging this out any longer will just make it harder to say goodbye. — Crusch-sama, if there are any final words."

"Yes, of course."

As Ferris, taking charge, ushered his lady forward, Crusch took a step toward them. Thinking it rude to look down from above, Subaru, like Ferris, climbed down from the carriage and moved to Emilia's side. With the two of them in view, Crusch first drew a deep breath and placed a hand to her chest.

"First, though I have said it many times already, my deepest thanks to you both. That I, even having lost my memories, still have my life, and that what I wished for before losing them still carries on — I believe that is entirely thanks to your cooperation. Thank you."

"N-no... there's nothing I did that deserves your thanks, Crusch-sama. I was left out of most of what happened these past few days..."

"Well, technically Emilia-tan's credit is basically empty-handed, yeah. But don't worry, my achievements more than made up for it there. My credit is my Emilia-tan's credit."

Subaru backed up Emilia, who looked apologetically flustered, by thumping his chest with easy confidence. Hearing that, Emilia glanced sidelong at him and gave a small nod.

"Thank you. — Though I don't recall ever becoming yours, Subaru."

"Guh. My scheme to slip that past a third party so you couldn't deny it — seen right through!?"

"Honestly, there's no letting my guard down with you. ...Oh, sorry, I got us off track."

Emilia let out a breath at Subaru, who clutched his chest and staggered back dramatically, then apologized to Crusch, whom they'd left out of the conversation. But Crusch watched the exchange between the two of them with evident enjoyment.

"Not at all, I envy how close the two of you are. I too must hurry to grow as comfortable with Ferris and Wilhelm-sama again as I once was."

"Feri-chan is always wide open, heart and body, for Crusch-sama, you know."

Ferris put both hands to his cheeks, swaying his body sinuously. Leaving the octopus-like boy-girl writhing behind her, Crusch — whose capacity to simply accept it all remained unchanged — wore a womanly smile.

"Let us be sure to meet again soon. I hope to remain on close terms with you, Emilia-sama, and with you, Natsuki Subaru-sama, for a long time to come."

That, Subaru thought, was surely her true, unfeigned feeling.

Even having lost her memories, that nobility of hers had not been lost from her core. Lies and hollow flattery had no place adorning a way of life that made the two words "sincerity" shine so brightly.

Perhaps because that had come through so clearly, Emilia's eyes went wide with surprise, her lips trembling faintly.

"I'm... a rival candidate, to you, Crusch-sama. Even allied as we are now, surely someday we'll go back to standing against each other."

"Yes, that's true. If you are a rival candidate, Emilia-sama, then I too must make sure to work hard so as not to lose."

"And apart from that, I'm a Half-Elf. A silver-haired one, at that. ...Doesn't that frighten you?"

"Emilia-tan, that's..."

Subaru tried to stop her, thinking it a question that didn't need asking. But once he saw the seriousness and desperation in her profile, he couldn't bring himself to say another word.

Emilia was asking this in earnest. Knowing even a fragment of what she carried inside her, he couldn't bring himself to lightly interrupt that question.

And above all, Subaru knew that the person facing that question was Crusch Karsten. — And that meant there was no need to stop it at all.

"It is the state of one's Soul that determines the worth of one's existence. A person ought to live the life that shines most brightly, both for oneself and for others — a life unashamed before one's own Soul."

"—————"

"Or so I'm told my past self used to say, like some kind of catchphrase. It's strange... hearing it now that I can be objective about it, it sounds like something said from quite a height, doesn't it."

Crusch put a hand to her mouth, stifling laughter she couldn't quite hold back at her own past self. Emilia, hearing it, simply stood there stunned and silent.

"Emilia-sama, do you think your own way of living is something to be ashamed of?"

"...No, I don't. No matter what people around me think of me, I've lived trying to make sure that I, at least, could go on without hating myself."

"Then there is nothing for you to regret or fear. Polishing yourself, piling up effort upon effort, carrying your own way of being straight and true — you possess a wonderful Soul."

Smiling, Crusch extended the hand she had held to her chest out toward Emilia.

"I am glad to have come to know you. There is nothing here for you to fear, nowhere at all."

"—!"

Emilia bit her lip as if a pain had struck her chest, looking down at the offered hand. Crusch didn't hurry her, only waited quietly for her to move.

At last, hesitantly, Emilia's fingertips laced with her palm, and a gentle handshake was exchanged.

"Please stay well. I look forward to seeing you again before too long."

"Me too... no, by then, I'll have made sure I can stand tall and straight before you, Crusch-sama. Until then, please take care."

The two Royal Selection candidates pledged their mutual best efforts, and a promise was exchanged.

Watching the two of them beside him, a sense of accomplishment filled Subaru's chest. It was one of the shapes he had finally managed to grasp only after suffering, struggling, and being wounded.

Though he hadn't managed to gather up absolutely everything, whole and complete, on the way here.

"I don't want to blame you for the reason I forget what I actually managed to pull off and go around wearing a sad face."

Glancing toward the carriage, Subaru pictured the sleeping girl inside behind his closed eyelids.

He couldn't allow himself to use Rem as the reason for hanging his head in a moment that should be celebrated. She wouldn't want that either, surely — or was thinking that just his own ego?

"Natsuki Subaru-sama, please stay well too. I pray from the bottom of my heart for your continued achievements, and... for her condition to recover."

"I don't think it'd be a good thing if situations calling for me to really shine came up too often, honestly. ...To be blunt, I'm the kind of guy who's only useful as a last resort, the sort you'd reach for when you're desperate enough to want even a cat's help. As for Rem — this isn't someone else's problem for you either, Crusch-san. I'll do something about it. I will, no matter what."

The same hand was offered to Subaru as well. Feeling somehow embarrassed to receive it as a proper handshake, he covered for it by pressing his palm against hers instead.

A small, dry clap sounded, and with that, the contact between Subaru and her was over. Crusch blinked at her rebuffed hand,

"Let us meet again, without fail."

So she said, and with that, mistress and attendant alike bowed with dignified poise, seeing Subaru and the others off.

As they set out on the road home, the carriage was filled with a subtly oppressive air.

The carriage Patrasche pulled, given to them by Crusch as part of the reward — this too, no doubt, was a token of their gratitude — was, decoration aside, clearly of expensive make, and the softness of the seats combined with the lustrous interior made it almost too much to sit comfortably in.

The spacious interior was built to comfortably seat close to ten people, so with only three effectively using that space, it was only natural for it to feel like far too much room.

At present, the only ones keeping silent inside the carriage were Subaru, Emilia, and Rem, sunk in a deep, unwaking sleep. Subaru, stationed beside the sleeping princess, had no urge to leave her side, and Emilia, for her part, showed no sign of speaking either — perhaps out of consideration for the unconscious Rem.

As a result, all that flowed through the carriage was an awkward, uncomfortable air.

"—————Mm."

This won't do, Subaru thought, crossing his arms.

Making a racket was out of the question, but there was no shortage of topics that needed discussing. Their future stance toward the Royal Selection, for one, and to begin with, they still hadn't fully compared notes on the information from these past few days that had led to forming the alliance with Crusch.

As for Rem herself, given that no one but Subaru retained any memory of her, it pained him to think how she'd be treated back at the mansion. Just imagining what Ram would say upon seeing her sleeping sister was enough to send a chill down his spine. Not that it was something he could avoid, of course.

"I get that everyone's being considerate, but honestly, having the little rugrats along might've felt better than this..."

Among the group of carriages returning to Roswaal's domain was, naturally, the one carrying the village children who had ridden along on the way out. Right now the children were presumably riding with their parents, a result of the villagers taking the initiative to consider that there might be talk unsuited for young ears. That kind consideration, though, seemed to have backfired somehow.

Now then, what to do about this — as Subaru, rarely one to think ahead like this, raised his eyes,

"Are you two, by any chance, stuck for something to talk about? I honestly can't stand this — this oppressive silence or whatever it is anymore."

"What are you even saying, just butting in so smoothly. Wait, you were there this whole time?"

"That's awful!? Of course I was there! Have you forgotten the terms under which I agreed to help you in the first place, Natsuki-san!?"

It was Otto, spraying spittle with his overreaction, poking just his face in to shout. Serving as the carriage's driver up on the coachman's seat, he'd leaned his face through the hatch connecting the driver's box to the cabin and was commenting on the dead-quiet atmosphere inside.

At Otto's manner of speaking, Subaru tilted his head, nodding a few times with an "Ahh, ahh."

"Right, right, I remember now. Yeah, it was something about wanting to meet Roswaal, wasn't it. ...Still, how do I put this..."

"What?"

"No, I mean, running off after a man is one thing, but Roswaal of all people — I dunno, I gotta wonder about that. ...Oh, and I'm normal, plus I've got Emilia-tan, so being targeted would be a problem for me too."

"That's not what this is about at all! What exactly do you take me for, huh!?"

"A comic-relief merchant type?"

"You're treating me like a novelty act!!"

Otto's eyes bulged as if to say he was thoroughly wronged, while Subaru shook his head with a sigh. Watching the exchange between the two of them, Emilia's eyes went round.

"Somehow... you two seem really close. I'm surprised."

"Hey now, Emilia-tan, don't say that. Lumping me in with a money-starved wraith like this... I'm a wraith starved only for your love."

"You're the wraith! You're the wraith! Er, also, I'm not a wraith in the first place!"

"Otto, quiet."

Sighing at the peddler kicking up a fuss, Subaru stood and strode forward. Grabbing the hatch cover,

"Ah, hey, don't just go treating me like a nuisance again like that—"

"Okay, shut out!"

The hatch snapped shut with a bang, and the face of the noisy man, still shouting something to the very end, vanished from sight. Dusting off his hands with the satisfaction of a job well done, Subaru turned around to find Emilia gazing up at him blankly.

"Pfft." "Hyahaha."

Looking at each other's faces, they both suddenly burst out laughing.

Giving in to the impulse, their laughter burst out for a while, then quietly faded away. And once the impulse had settled,

"Reading an awkward mood and going quiet — that wasn't really like me."

"That's true, that's not like Subaru. The Subaru I know is always more energetic, more reckless — the kind of person who's noisy in this refreshing way that has nothing to do with how I'm feeling."

"I feel like that translates to 'a guy who fakes his energy and can't read the room.'"

In any case, it was true that Otto's presence had loosened up the mood in the carriage.

Galling as it was to admit, Subaru gave Otto silent thanks and sat down beside Emilia. Emilia gave a wry smile at Subaru settling beside her as if it were the most natural thing in the world.

"You just sit right down next to me without hesitating at all, don't you, Subaru."

"Well, of course, it's only natural to want to be close to the girl you like. I want to be as close as possible, breathing in the very air you just breathed out, Emilia-tan."

"That was embarrassing right up until it suddenly turned really creepy partway through."

Even as her face reddened at having his affection thrown at her so straightforwardly, Emilia grimaced at the highly perverted content of the latter half. Subaru tilted his head at her reaction.

"Well, see, whenever I try to just be my usual self, stuff like that just comes out."

"Right, that's just how Subaru is. Which is exactly why I can never quite take what you say at face value."

Emilia stared at him steadily, letting her words trail off.

Scratching his head, Subaru wavered over whether he should pick up where she'd hesitated to continue.

"It's just a guy thing — you can't seriously come on to a girl unless you play it off like a joke. But that I like you, Emilia-tan, that I look at you with lecherous eyes, that I want to be of help to you — all of that is completely for real. You can believe it, all right?"

"I believe you, but that's a different story from accepting it, you know?"

"That's fine. Believe it, and I'll work hard to earn your acceptance on top of that."

Looking back on it, he thought, almost like it was someone else's doing, that had been a pretty aggressive thing to say. In fact, Emilia's fluster at hearing it from Subaru was considerable.

Her expression strained to stay composed, but her cheeks and ears had gone red past any hope of covering it up. She'd surely never had the experience of being shown unconditional affection like this before. Of course, Subaru, on the wooing end, had no experience either, so one way or another, his own face was bright red too.

Even so,

"Better this than moping around with my head down — this is more like me. Right, Rem?"

"...Did you say something just now?"

"Just that I want to lift up your pretty hair, Emilia-tan, and ravish the back of your neck with my eyes."

"You always deflect like that right away. ...You're worried about Rem-san, aren't you."

Blocking off the escape route Subaru had tried to make with a joke, Emilia said it plainly. Taking that in, Subaru gave a wry smile and turned his gaze to Rem, asleep on the bed.

"I am. I'm worried about her, a lot. I keep thinking I have to do something, and I think I'll keep thinking about it for a long time. I want to keep you first in my mind, Emilia-tan... but this isn't something I can put in order like that. I'm sorry."

"If I got mad about that, I'd be a really horrible person, wouldn't I. I wouldn't get mad over something that important. ...I can tell, just from looking, that she's someone important to you, Subaru."

Like Subaru, Emilia narrowed her eyes at the sleeping Rem. Then, her lips trembling, after a moment's hesitation,

"You like her, don't you?"

"I like her. I like her a lot. About as much as I like you, Emilia-tan."

"I hate to say this, but... Subaru, are you the type to cheat?"

"I always figured I was pretty single-minded, honestly, but I think a guy who could have someone devote themselves to him that much and not have his heart moved wouldn't have any blood or tears left in him."

He recalled the loops of these past several days, recalled all the unconditional love he'd received from Rem throughout them. Having received all of that, how could his heart not be moved?

Before he knew it, her presence had come to occupy far too large a share of his heart.

"And here you told me you liked me."

"For the record, I do like Rem, but she likes me way more, you know? She's completely smitten — honestly, it's a real mystery to me."

Crossing his arms, he couldn't help but harbor doubts on this one point — being on the receiving end of such unconditional love. Did he really have the worth to be loved that much by a girl as accomplished as Rem?

Even now, he couldn't help but think so. Though he also thought he needed to become a man worthy of that value.

At Subaru's self-assessment, Emilia's lips softened into a small smile.

"I think I understand."

"Huh?"

"Why Rem-san fell so hard for you, Subaru. I bet she got to see all your good sides up close. You're kind of like — you have this disease where you do incredibly cool things sometimes."

"A disease. I mean... I guess I can't really argue."

Scratching his cheek, Subaru pouted to show his displeasure. Emilia paid his reaction no mind, closing her eyes with a composed look and saying, "That's right."

"I'm not going to fall for you that easily, though."

"That just makes it more worth the challenge. Someday I'll have you head over heels for me too, Emilia-tan, and then you and Rem, once she wakes up, can fight over me and settle it with a Judgment of Solomon. Ahh, just thinking about it makes me grin!"

Pulled by both hands, one by Emilia and one by Rem, fought over though he only had the one body — what a happy, cheek-loosening sight that would be.

So surely, someday, without fail—

"Guess I'll have to let you both pull until I get torn in two."

"I don't know what you're imagining, but I feel like I have to say this — I'm not going to tear you apart."

With that exchange as a starting point, the discussion inside the carriage went smoothly from there.

It was, after all, a journey that afforded them nearly half a day. However much there was to talk through, there was more than enough time to hash it all out.

They exchanged information from the past several days with each other, and in the end, with Otto joining in too, they worked out their future course of action.

As a result, once the discussion had come together,

"In the end, we can't work out any real strategy without sitting down and talking to Roz face to face."

Whatever else came up, the conclusion always circled back to where things had started.

In the end, Roswaal was the only one who had a full grasp of the Emilia Camp's capabilities and strength, and without bringing him into it, Emilia's side simply couldn't function.

"Well, if Ram, who headed for the Sanctuary, hooks up with Roswaal, he'll naturally end up making his way back to the mansion too. And when he does, first I'm slapping him across the face, and then we talk."

"That's a pretty aggressive stance to take toward the margrave who's supposed to be your employer, Natsuki-san."

"I figure I've earned the right to do at least that much, and he's committed the kind of sin that isn't forgiven unless he takes at least that much."

Looking back on everything Roswaal had pulled, one could see just how mild a judgment it was for Subaru to be willing to settle for a single slap. Emilia, too, seemed to have no intention of stopping the fired-up Subaru, taking the stance of "if it's just one" in tacit approval.

In any case, with the discussion settled in that form, and with the talk they meant to have back on the domain right before them, they passed through the forest and returned to the village — and immediately, Subaru and the others noticed something was wrong.

The familiar village scenery, empty of people, looked exactly as desolate as it had right after they'd evacuated the residents ahead of the assault on Petelgeuse — and with no sign now even of the subjugation force that had been staying there, the current state felt even more forlorn than before.

To put it more plainly and bluntly: there was no trace of the villagers having returned.

"Doesn't look like anyone's here, Natsuki-san. It's not that the place got ransacked or anything — it just feels like nobody's come back at all."

Having climbed down from the carriage and taken a quick look around the village along with the villagers, Otto voiced his impression. Subaru, who had looked around with a different group, regrettably shared the same opinion.

The silence was so profound that flashbacks of the massacre of villagers at Petelgeuse's hands assaulted Subaru's whole body, but at least he could confirm those fears were unfounded this time.

But that only gave rise to a different problem instead.

"The 'Sanctuary' Ram mentioned was supposed to be, what, seven or eight hours from here... so how is it they're getting back later than us, when we stayed holed up in the royal capital for three whole days?"

"Maybe they haven't gotten word that the Witch Cult's been defeated, so they're staying cautious?"

"You think Roswaal would abandon his own domain? By my reckoning, if 'Sloth' and Roswaal went at it head-on, Roswaal wins nine times out of ten. I figure not fighting head-on is exactly 'Sloth's' whole style... but even so, he'd at least send out a scout, wouldn't he?"

Roswaal, who could even fly, ought to be able to scout out his own attacked domain easily enough. And if he had any intention of scouting at all, he should have been able to confirm that the area around the mansion, cleared of the Witch Cult, was secure. That he hadn't done so meant —

"Either he's playing it safe..."

"...Or something's happened at the Sanctuary?"

Subaru and Emilia's thoughts aligned, and they looked at each other and nodded. Either way, without confirming the situation at the Sanctuary, they had no way of knowing what was going on.

The two of them's concerns were, at the same time, concerns for the village's own people.

After all, roughly sixty percent of the villagers had accompanied Ram toward the Sanctuary. Only the remaining forty percent — the children who had firmly insisted on going along with Emilia, along with their parents and a handful of the young men's association — had returned to the village, and at that rate, the village's ability to function was bound to suffer badly.

Nor would the villagers' state of mind be likely to lean toward anything cheerful.

"Either way, we've gotta do something about this. ...For now, let's head back to the mansion. I want to get Rem settled somewhere calm. Otto, you don't have anywhere to stay either, so you're coming to the mansion."

"Whaaat!? S-staying at the margrave's own mansion!? Honestly, I'd feel more at ease just sleeping in the carriage than getting caught up in a situation that outrageous!"

"Shut up and get caught up in it. We're in this together now. I'll work you to the point of nearly dying."

Ignoring Otto's muttered complaints, Subaru gave Patrasche her instructions, and they parted ways with the villagers to head for the mansion.

Fifteen minutes on foot, or a mere five by carriage — that was the distance to the familiar, nostalgic Roswaal Manor.

Last time, there hadn't been any leisure to take it in slowly, but looking up at it anew like this now stirred something deeply moving.

"Still, doesn't look like anything's changed. ...Doesn't seem like Ram and the others are back."

"But if nothing's changed inside, Beatrice should still be there, right? I hope she knows where the Sanctuary is."

"Oh, right. Crap, neither of us even knows where the Sanctuary is. We don't have any way to check if Roz and everyone are okay."

With their plans threatening to fall apart at such a fundamental level, Subaru knitted his brow at the darkness of what lay ahead.

A shadow of worry settled over Emilia's fine features as well, while Otto, not joining the conversation, seemed absorbed in looking around with obvious curiosity.

"Tch. Anyway, for now all we can do is pray Bea-ko knows something."

"Did you just click your tongue while looking at me?"

"Tch. You're way too self-conscious. Nobody's as interested in you as you seem to think."

"That's a horrible thing to say!"

Ignoring the deflated Otto, Subaru pulled the carriage up in the front yard and headed for the entrance.

First, call out Beatrice, then look around the inside of the mansion, secure a bed for Rem, and after that, work out their plans anew—

"I'm home, Roswaal Manor. Now then, my dear old home..."

As he said it, pushing open the front door and peering inside, Subaru's voice caught in his throat.

The reason was clear: what awaited him took a form entirely different from what he'd expected.

The entrance hall, carpeted wall to wall. Beside the great staircase leading up to the second floor stood what looked like an expensive vase, arranged with flowers to adorn it. From the ceiling hung lighting powered by Crystal Lamps, which one could fairly call an otherworldly-style chandelier.

That familiar sight of the entrance hall was, however, completely different from what he'd expected.

It was, compared to the state he'd expected—

"It's not that it's been ransacked... it's been put in order!?"

The carpet was stretched taut, not a single wrinkle in it; the flowers set in the vase by the stairs shone with a freshness practically glistening; and the chandelier had been meticulously polished, as if licked clean, enhancing the natural beauty of its Crystal Lamps.

At how utterly wrong the sight felt, Subaru stood frozen, at a loss for words.

Because he'd been so thoroughly stunned by it all, every one of Subaru's reactions afterward came a beat too late.

"—Who's there!?"

A small, faint sound, so slight it could easily have gone unnoticed, and Subaru, driven by a rising sense of urgency, moved his gaze. But by the time his awareness caught up to that shadow, it was already too late.

The shadow had already circled around behind Subaru—

A black shadow closed in, looming over Subaru from directly behind, and Subaru saw it.

Clearly floating within that human-shaped silhouette, a beastlike maw bristling with white fangs.

—And in the next instant, carelessly, Subaru's consciousness, the world itself, went dark.
