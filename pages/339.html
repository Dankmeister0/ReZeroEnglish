Arc 5 Chapter 23: "A Stirred-Up Situation"

"So? I think it's about time you explained what happened, I suppose."

Still hand in hand, they left the park, and once Emilia and the others had drifted out of sight, Beatrice slowed her pace and said as much. Beatrice seemed to want to stop and talk properly, but Subaru pulled at their still-joined arm, urging her on.

"Subaru?"

"Sorry. I'd love nothing more than to pick some place with no one around and hash this out nice and slow, but there's no time for that. —We've got less than fifteen minutes."

"...I understand, I suppose. Talk while we walk, that's fine, I suppose."

Seeing the urgency etched into Subaru's profile, Beatrice fell in line with his plan without argument. Grateful for his partner's understanding attitude, Subaru pressed forward at a brisk pace, carefully unburdening the still half-formed contents of his mind to Beatrice.

"A member of the Witch Cult is going to show up in the plaza we're headed to. We have to stop whatever nastiness it's got planned."

"The Witch Cult...!"

Beatrice caught her breath, and Subaru pressed on, choosing his words carefully. The tricky part was the rules and penalties governing information disclosure under Return by Death. Just because what he'd told Rachins had been fine didn't mean the same held for Beatrice—and that ambiguity was the nastiest thing about the shadowy hand of malice that bound Subaru.

That malevolent hand, which obstructed the disclosure of Return by Death information, seemed to judge not only the content of what Subaru said but also whom he said it to before deciding whether to trigger a penalty. Otherwise, how could it be explained that revealing the secret to Emilia had resulted in something crushing her heart as if in a vice? There was no other explanation.

So Subaru paid meticulous attention, every step of the way, to exactly what he told Beatrice. If it were only Subaru who got hurt, that would still be bearable. Scary, sure. Terrifying, even. But not unbearable. But if that malicious hand ever reached for Emilia or Beatrice, Subaru would be crushed under the weight of his own guilt. Because that hand might show mercy to Subaru, but it would never, ever show mercy to anyone else.

"As usual, you can't tell me where you learned this, I suppose?"

"...Sorry. I can't."

"That's fine, I suppose. Even without a reason, I'll believe you. The fact that Subaru told me at all—that's reason enough for Betty to believe, I suppose."

Beatrice squeezed his hand in response as Subaru swallowed down his own sense of inadequacy. Drawing strength from the warmth passed through her palm, Subaru searched for his next words.

Sirius, "Wrath," sensory sharing, soul cleansing—he weighed what information and possibilities he could share, and little by little brought Beatrice up to speed on the danger they faced.

"First off, the Witch Cult member who's going to show up is the Sin Archbishop of 'Wrath,' and, uh, he's a pervert."

"If that's the piece of information Subaru thinks needs to be shared before anything else, then Betty thinks Subaru is hopeless, I suppose."

"I'm feeling this out as I go, okay, trying to figure out what's safe to say. Anyway, pervert—no penalty there. Next is his ability, which is... some kind of sharing of emotions and sensations, I guess?"

"Sharing of... emotions and sensations?"

Beatrice tilted her head. She probably couldn't quite picture it concretely. That was understandable—Subaru himself didn't have a clear grasp of exactly what that Authority did.

"It's hard to explain, but... if the Sin Archbishop himself is overjoyed about something, then everyone around him—no matter how furious they were a second ago—ends up just as overjoyed as he is."

"...I don't really understand why that would be a threat, I suppose."

"The big thing is that you stop being able to recognize danger as danger. No matter how dangerous something is, you can't feel any sense of crisis about it. You just accept it, overjoyed. Put simply, it kills your ability to correctly assess the situation you're in. Does that make more sense?"

A crowd raising both arms in wholehearted welcome for a boy who was wailing, begging not to die. Being able to feel nothing but joy at everything happening right in front of you—that was terror itself. In that state, Subaru and the others probably would have joyfully awaited the very blade about to sever their own necks, right up until the instant it struck.

"So you understand the emotion-sharing part, I suppose. What does the sensation-sharing do?"

"Exactly what it sounds like. If the other guy feels pain, so do I. If you cut off the Sin Archbishop's head, everyone watching loses their head too. ...Talk about hopeless, right?"

Saying it out loud again, even Subaru himself grew queasy at how troublesome it was. Cut off his head, and your own head flies off with it—simply put, there weren't many abilities out there better designed to make you hesitate to kill. Thanks to Return by Death, at least he could plan ahead, but if he went to all the trouble of beating the guy only to wipe out everyone on his own side in the process, there'd be absolutely nothing to celebrate.

"And it's not like we can take him alive either—just making contact with him might be enough to drive someone insane. Whether you let him live or kill him, he's the worst kind of enemy, causing trouble for everyone around him either way."

During his second death, Subaru had been swallowed by terror and driven mad. Most likely, the cause was sharing that terror with Lusbel, whom he'd been trying to save. But if that were the case, it meant Lusbel had been feeling that same maddening terror the entire time, without end. It might even mean Subaru had less mental fortitude than that boy. He couldn't say with much confidence "I must be the stronger one" on that point—it was hard to imagine that Lusbel, who'd still been carrying on a conversation right up until the end, had actually been resisting that overwhelming terror the whole time.

His second death must have involved something more than simply being made to share terror. Something beyond mere sharing of emotion and sensation—and until he figured out what that was, building any kind of solid strategy to take down Sirius was utterly impossible.

"——"

Subaru said nothing, only gripping Beatrice's hand tighter. He'd brought Beatrice out here like this, and yet he had no idea how to break through. At this rate, all he'd done was drag her into a fight with no visible chance of winning. What should he do? Should he just close his eyes to the risk of madness and have Reinhard take Sirius alive for now? But summoning him the same way as last time—could Subaru actually give Reinhard a sufficient explanation to make him capture Sirius alive rather than kill him?

Or maybe, better yet, have Reinhard summoned before Sirius even appeared. If he suddenly attacked Rachins in the plaza, wouldn't that make him sense mortal danger and call Reinhard? And surely Reinhard, once summoned, wouldn't just cut Subaru down without even asking why. If Subaru explained there'd been an emergency need to call him, that would buy at least a few minutes before Sirius even showed up—

"What an idiot I am. No, seriously, I'm an idiot. If Rachins takes action to summon Reinhard, there's no way Sirius wouldn't react to that. All that does is move up the timeline—there's still no time to explain things to Reinhard."

Call for a capture right before the fight between Reinhard and Sirius begins. Could he manage that? Once he was actually face to face with Sirius, Subaru had zero confidence in his own subsequent thought process. Even if he told himself beforehand to call for a capture, wouldn't his mouth end up screaming "Kill him!" instead? He had a track record. There was nothing to suggest otherwise.

"Subaru. Actually, there's something I need to tell you—bad news, I suppose."

"...You're kidding me. I don't want to hear one more piece of bad news right now."

"I know, I suppose. But it has to be said. ...If you're counting on Reinhard as combat power, then on the same battlefield as him, Betty will end up useless, I suppose. Just a cute girl and nothing more, I suppose."

"Huh?"

At Beatrice's sudden statement, Subaru blinked in blank astonishment. Beatrice lowered her eyes, looking as though the words were hard to say.

"It's an issue with Reinhard's constitution, I suppose. That one is the pinnacle of anomaly in this world. Just by existing there, the mana around him blindly tries to obey him. It corrupts its normal function, and ironically, that becomes a shackle on him. Not only does it render his own magic useless, it renders every mage and spirit arte user around him useless too, I suppose."

"What, the hell, is that. There's no way something like that could actually happen..."

He trailed off there, realizing there was, in fact, something that came to mind. It was on the very first day Subaru had been summoned to this world—during the incident surrounding the emblem, Emilia's qualification to enter the Royal Selection—in the final loop, when Reinhard had clashed with Elsa. At the time, Emilia, who'd been treating old man Rom, had said something about how magic would become useless if Reinhard went all-out. He hadn't understood it then. So that was what she'd meant.

"If Reinhard alone can settle things, it doesn't matter if Betty is useless, I suppose. But if Reinhard alone isn't enough..."

"Then the option of having Bea-ko become useless isn't really an option to begin with, is it."

Talk about a rotten Illusion Breaker. Just by being there, it wiped out every magical function in the vicinity. It was very like Reinhard, in a sense—but right now, that was working against them.

—Bad. Bad. Bad bad bad bad bad bad.

Right now, not a single ray of light was in sight. Was calling Reinhard the right move, or the wrong one? He'd brought Beatrice along, but what was she even supposed to do? What was the correct move for Subaru to make? Should he ignore whatever Sirius did and just prioritize the safety of the people in the plaza, this once? But if he did that, Sirius would just move somewhere else and do the same thing. Pointless.

Subaru's mind burned with the effort of thinking, anxiety scorching part of his brain. Eyes bloodshot, he struggled desperately to think his way to an answer. But no answer showed itself before him. And still, time alone, merciless, left Subaru behind and ticked steadily onward—

"...Looks like we've arrived at the plaza, huh, Subaru?"

"—!"

At Beatrice's words, Subaru's head snapped up, and he looked at the plaza. The two of them had arrived at their destination—the plaza where the tragedy would occur. And he still hadn't found anything. All that remaining time had done was whittle itself down to bring them here.

The white Time Tower. The crowd filling the plaza. By Subaru's reckoning, there was less than ten minutes until the tragedy struck. What should he do? What was the right thing to do? What could he possibly do—

"Subaru, I might—I might have thought of something, I suppose."

Beatrice's voice reached Subaru, whose lips trembled and cheeks stiffened. His mind gone blank, Subaru blinked rapidly at the sweetness of that sound.

"You thought of something—a way through!?"

"It's just a possibility, I suppose. But if the Sin Archbishop's ability you described is a sharing with others, then... there's a high-level magic with a similar effect—'Nect'—that comes to mind, I suppose."

"Nect...!"

At Beatrice's words, finger raised, Subaru's throat clicked. Nect—a type of magic Subaru himself had once experienced firsthand. A magic that shared will and sensation between the caster and another person—it certainly resembled the effect of Sirius's Authority. Wondering why he hadn't thought of it himself, Subaru doubted his own head even as he asked,

"If it's the same as Nect, how do we deal with it?"

"...To begin with, Nect isn't really a magic that needs 'dealing with,' I suppose. The whole point of Nect's effect is unifying the will of allies, communication without words. The very idea of using Nect offensively against an opponent is a mistake from the start, I suppose."

Beatrice answered somewhat irritably at Subaru, who impatiently urged her to continue. Indeed, when Subaru had used Nect before, it had been—reluctantly—for the purpose of sharing sight with Julius. To counter Petelgeuse's "Unseen Hand," he'd needed to connect his own vision, which could see the hands, to Julius, who could actually fight.

Nect's true purpose was to be used for coordination between allies. It was absolutely not a magic meant to link enemies together, holding each other's bodies hostage.

"To begin with, there are conditions for connecting through Nect, I suppose. It shouldn't work without enough trust between the two parties for a mana pathway to connect them. The Sin Archbishop's Authority is clearly ignoring that domain entirely, I suppose."

"I figure forcing that through anyway is exactly what an Authority is for. More importantly—"

"How to deal with it, I suppose. —Simply put, this is a job for 'Shamak,' I suppose."

"Shamak's here! Seriously, is there nothing that spell can't do!"

At Beatrice's proposal, Subaru couldn't help but raise his voice. Shamak was that familiar to him. In pain, in hardship, in danger, in trouble—Shamak had always been there with Subaru. Before he'd made his Contract with Beatrice, it wouldn't be an exaggeration to say it was Rem, Patrasche, and Mr. Shamak who'd been the strength of powerless Subaru.

He'd thought that bond had been severed with the destruction of his own Gate, but even now, filtered through the Contract via Beatrice, Shamak still came to Subaru's aid.

"So that's it, Shamak... with Shamak, surely we can somehow manage everything...!"

"Betty gets it, Subaru has an extraordinary amount of trust in Shamak, I suppose. Honestly, Shamak's supposed to be the most basic of basic Shadow magic, with almost no practical use, I suppose."

"Even you, Bea-ko, I won't forgive you for badmouthing Shamak...!"

"What in the world makes Subaru say something like that so seriously, I wonder."

Looking unconvinced, Beatrice sighed at Subaru's heavy breathing. Then, keeping a wary eye on their surroundings, she kept one finger raised.

"Shamak is a magic that severs consciousness—it forcibly cuts off connection to one's surroundings, I suppose. When the caster is Subaru, the effect is a bit iffy, but with Betty, there's no problem, I suppose. No matter who the target is, Betty can sever their consciousness perfectly, I suppose."

"Which means...?"

"If we throw the crowd that's about to fall under his spell into darkness, all at once, with Shamak, that alone should let them escape the Sin Archbishop's Authority, I suppose. It should preserve the direction Subaru's worried about—not dragging the people around into it, I suppose."

Catching every single thing Subaru hoped for without missing a beat, Beatrice puffed out her chest with confidence. At that reliable answer, Subaru clenched his fist. Faint as it was, he felt like he could see a glimmer of light.

"Alright, good. If his ability can't reach anyone, that's our win. And then... and then?"

"The combat power to blast that Sin Archbishop away, without Reinhard, I suppose."

"............"

With Reinhard around, Beatrice's Shamak tactic wouldn't work. So it followed that Reinhard had to be excluded from the calculation of their fighting strength. But in that case, they needed to defeat Sirius without Reinhard.

"I should mention, Betty's going to be fully occupied with Shamak, and she'll also need to cast Shamak on whoever's fighting, timed right before the finishing blow. Can you fight, I wonder?"

"Yeah, figures. ...Damn, we're right back to square one after all."

Without Beatrice's backup, even closing his eyes to the risk of going mad, there was probably no way Subaru could beat Sirius. Even playing whatever trump card he hadn't shown yet, it was doubtful he could really go toe to toe. He was also, quietly, still a little hurt that his practiced whip technique hadn't worked.

"The people in the plaza back then who looked like they could fight were..."

He recalled the very first time in the plaza. The faces that had instinctively moved to act against Sirius when he appeared atop the Time Tower. If he remembered right, a beastman man, a woman with an eyepatch. Plus a grim-faced man and Rachins—four people. Even if Rachins didn't count as combat strength, what about the other three? If he added himself to make four, could he start to see some chance of winning, however slim?

"Don't talk nonsense. How much can I trust people whose skill level is a total unknown? As it stands, I haven't even talked to them yet..."

"—In that case, isn't this my turn? You know my skill level, and we can talk to each other just fine, can't we?"

"—!?"

At Subaru shaking his head at his own wishful thinking, a sudden voice came from behind. That voice, far too familiar, made Subaru and Beatrice both turn around in shock. Standing behind them, hand on her hip, was—

"E-Emilia-tan? Why are you here..."

"You were acting so strange that I followed you, and sure enough, you're carrying around something huge again. Trying to shut me out like that—I think that's a bad habit of yours, Subaru."

Like a mother scolding a mischievous child, her tone left Subaru's mouth opening and closing wordlessly. Stunned by the appearance of Emilia—who shouldn't have been there, who he hadn't wanted there—Subaru was left speechless. In his place, Beatrice looked up at Emilia.

"I told you to wait in the park, I'm sure of it, I suppose. Why did you follow us, I wonder?"

"...Honestly, I meant to wait too. It seemed like Subaru didn't want me tagging along. But then Priscilla said—"

"That red-haired girl?"

"She said that if I didn't chase after Subaru right now, I might regret it. If nothing had been wrong, I was going to just quietly turn back—but you two kept talking so seriously the whole time. I couldn't turn back after that."

The face of the one responsible for pushing Emilia's resolve, for driving her all the way here, rose in Subaru's mind. Gritting his back teeth, he mentally struck at the image of Priscilla's high, mocking laughter, the one who'd stirred up this whole situation. A girl who never failed to stir things up, no matter what. Her wickedness, combined with timing so fatally, perfectly bad it could only have been deliberate, had beautifully produced exactly the situation Subaru had wanted to avoid.

"Emilia-tan, I'm happy—happy that you feel that way, but, um, this is, right now—"

"The Witch Cult is going to show up, right? I heard you just fine. ...Even if Subaru tells me to go back, I'm not going back. This isn't someone else's problem for me either."

"Emilia!"

It wasn't for no reason at all that he was trying to keep Emilia away from danger. Raising his voice, Subaru tried desperately to send the stubborn Emilia away.

She and the Witch Cult must never meet. He didn't know why. There was no solid logic to it. But there were things that needed neither logic nor reason. Emilia and the Witch Cult must never, under any circumstances, be allowed to encounter each other. To Emilia, that group called the Witch Cult was the equivalent of some deadly poison she must never come into contact with. That held true for most people living in this world, but for Emilia, it was true to an extreme degree. Which was why—

"We'll handle it. Emilia doesn't need to get involved. Please, don't get involved."

"And you'll just close your eyes again while you and everyone else get hurt trying to protect me like that? Absolutely not. When Subaru fights, I fight too. When Subaru tries to protect someone, I want to help with that too. Just like Subaru protects me..."

"——"

"I want to protect Subaru too. Because Subaru, you look like you're about to cry right now."

The heart that must not break nearly broke at Emilia's appeal. To keep her away from danger, Subaru had to summon up his own courage. With a heart of steel, he had to stand against every hardship. And yet right now, Subaru was afraid. Terrified. Afraid of the fight ahead.

Three times. Subaru had already lost his life three times, defeated in battle against Sirius. And all within the span of a single hour—experiencing death that many times in such a short span was something not even Natsuki Subaru's considerable experience with death had prepared him for.

"Death" was always terrifying. There was no such thing as growing used to it, nor should there be. To have your life taken meant having your path cut off. It meant your way of living was denied, your existence trampled, your soul violated. That was what it meant to have your life taken.

Try as he might to paper over it with flippancy, something Subaru couldn't resist tormented him. Try as he might to hold firm to his resolve to protect her, the weak feeling of not wanting to die kept surfacing. No matter how much time passed, Natsuki Subaru could never overcome that weakness.

"...Subaru. I think it's time you gave up, I suppose."

"Beatrice..."

"Emilia's stubborn, I suppose. Now that she knows, there's probably nothing to be done about it anymore, I suppose. And Betty understands how Emilia feels too, I suppose. The wish to protect Subaru—Betty feels the same way, and... denying that isn't something Betty can do, I suppose."

Beatrice, the linchpin of their strategy and Subaru's decision-making organ in her own right. If even she raised the white flag, it would be difficult for Subaru to keep resisting alone. Emilia looked at him earnestly, Beatrice tenderly—the two of them watching Subaru together.

Under their combined gaze, at last, Subaru's resolve broke too.

"...The Witch Cult is bound to target you. If anything happens, put your own safety first, above everything else, and act accordingly, okay?"

"Mm, got it. Even if I get caught, Subaru will come save me, right? I'll believe that, and do my best."

"Don't say something so ominous..." "So—how much of the conversation did you hear?"

At Subaru's acceptance, Emilia's face relaxed into a relieved smile. Deflated by her attitude, as though she'd forgotten about the formidable enemy awaiting them, Subaru watched as Emilia touched a finger to her own lips.

"I think I heard most of it. A bad person from the Witch Cult is coming, and that person uses magic like Nect. Beatrice cancels out that effect with Shamak, and while that's happening, we have to work hard and beat the bad guy."

"That's a breakdown simple enough for a little kid, but yeah, that's about right. Emilia-tan, can I count on you?"

"Leave it to me. I've gotten properly stronger, you know."

Emilia struck a little fist-pump pose, both hands raised in front of her chest. The cute gesture lacked a certain sense of urgency, but she'd grasped the situation smoothly enough. Anxiety and inadequacy about relying on Emilia, plus the difficulty of timing Beatrice's magic just right—there was no shortage of failure factors, if not exactly anxiety factors. And yet—

"With Emilia-tan and Bea-ko here, there's no way we can afford to fail...!"

That was the kind of thing a boy turned into fuel for determination instead.

"Besides, it's almost time."

Having taken up Beatrice's proposal and joined forces with Emilia, most of the remaining time had already been used up. All that was left was figuring out how to respond once Sirius made his appearance. Ideally, while Lusbel was still left abandoned inside the tower, they could knock Sirius clean off the tower before anything else happened.

"Emilia-tan. Soon, some obviously suspicious guy is going to show up at the top of the tower. When he does, hit him with one big preemptive strike. Best case, you knock him right off the tower. After that, Bea-ko will handle things smoothly, and once she gives the signal, we start the fight."

"Mm, got it. Not sure if I can pull it off well, but I'll try."

At Subaru's instructions, Emilia's expression hardened with resolve, and Subaru and Beatrice exchanged a nod. It was right after their plan had solidified that it happened.

"—He's here!"

Leaning out from the window of the Time Tower, a lone monstrous figure appeared. A slender body wrapped in a coat, its face wound round and round in bandages—a deformed figure. From both arms hung chains, their ends dragging across the floor with a grating, ear-splitting sound, as the figure looked down over the crowd below.

Not one person in the crowd had yet noticed that grotesque presence. Standing atop the grand stage, Sirius trembled as if savoring the sight of the masses below, utterly defenseless against the threat looming so near, then spread both arms wide. When they came together, a sound like an explosion rang out—applause—and at the sound, the eyes of the crowd were drawn to the monstrous figure, and that speech began.

"——"

Holding his breath, Subaru watched that moment arrive with his own eyes. Gathering momentum, both arms came together before Sirius's chest with a violent clap—

"Ur Fuma!!"

An instant before, a massive pillar of ice had appeared directly above the tower, striking Sirius, who stood at its outer edge, dead on. A block of ice as thick as five Subarus bundled together gouged spectacularly through the white wall of the Time Tower with a tremendous sound and impact. Watching the by-no-means-fragile wall crumble, the tip of the ice pillar embedded in the tower's interior, Subaru nearly let his jaw drop.

"E-Emilia-tan?"

"You said preemptive strike, so I gave it a preemptive strike, but... was that bad?"

"No, GJ. I just wasn't expecting you to launch it before he even finished introducing himself, so I was surprised."

Part of the fault lay with Subaru for not specifying the timing, but the greater problem was probably Sirius's own behavior, which had gotten him marked as suspicious by Emilia at a single glance. That magical blow, delivered with zero consideration for something like "wait and see first," might well have crushed the utterly defenseless Sirius outright. Even so, no crushed corpse could be found amid the now-uproarious crowd, so perhaps—just perhaps—they'd managed to skip past the whole sensory-sharing business and beaten him outright.

If that were the case, it would be an almost absurdly lucky break, courtesy of Emilia going all out.

"Bea-ko, what do you think?"

"First, I think we'd better start by clearing up the misunderstanding with the people around us, I suppose."

To Subaru's real question—had that actually finished Sirius off?—Beatrice merely jerked her chin, her expression still exasperated. Looking around, Subaru saw the crowd steadily closing in around him, Emilia, and the rest, who had just destroyed the Time Tower. Among them were the beastman man and the woman with the eyepatch—people he'd once considered as potential allies, now eyeing him with wariness. A quiet grief struck Subaru at that.

"Not really the time to be saying that. Um, what do we do. First off, should I just explain we didn't do that out of malice or anything?"

"—No. More importantly, Subaru, you should step back."

Scratching his head, uncertain how to explain, Subaru felt Emilia pull at his shoulder. As he instinctively stepped back, Emilia moved out in front of him and swung her hand sharply from top to bottom. At that instant, the air rang out with a sharp chime, and a blue blade of ice formed in Emilia's hand. Holding the slender sword, Emilia pointed its tip at the crowd surrounding them.

"Emilia-tan? I don't think we need to go quite that far playing the villain..."

"That's not it. Look closely, Subaru. Everyone's eyes—they're not sane."

"—Huh?"

Startled by the firmness in Emilia's voice, Subaru looked at the faces of the people around them. And at the sight, his breath caught. Just as Emilia said, those were not the faces of sane human beings.

The crowd surrounding them, every single one, had turned bright red from the neck up, like tomatoes, every blood vessel in their faces bulging, their bloodshot eyes glaring at Subaru and the others. It wasn't a matter of doubting their sanity—it was a look unmistakably, undeniably filled with madness.

"Bea-ko! What about Shamak!?"

"...I think I messed up, I suppose."

"What?"

"This is a magic whose fundamental form is different from Nect, root and branch... no, it's not magic at all, I suppose. This is a curse... Curse Arts, I suppose!"

Beatrice's voice rose with displeasure and anger, and Subaru's brow furrowed at the sound of it. He didn't understand the difference in principle, but Beatrice had judged that the Shamak strategy simply wouldn't work. And having grasped that setback, there was a further problem on top of it. For the crowd, as a whole, to be swallowed by madness like this meant—

"—Reek. Reek. Reek. Reek. Reek. Reek. Reek. Reek. Reek. Reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek reek"

It was a hideously viscous sound, the resonance of a grudge cursing the entirety of this world.

"——"

With a crash, the outer wall of the Time Tower crumbled. Cracks ran through the ice pillar that had embedded its massive form in the tower, and in the space of a breath, the cracking mass shattered into countless fragments. Amid glittering, sun-catching ice crystals, footsteps sounded.

The monstrous figure. Not unscathed—half the bandages wound around its face had turned red with blood. Blood also trickled down its limply hanging left arm, and its gait, dragging the chains, was somehow unsteady. Emilia's preemptive strike had, without question, had an effect.

But it was also true that, in doing so, they'd pulled a trigger that should never have been pulled.

"I smell it, I feel it... the smell of a woman. A filthy, hateful smell, the smell of a half-devil who'd steal that person from me. Kill it and kill it and still it keeps coming, endless, like maggots, reeking filth. Don't make me laugh. Detestable. Even after burning that much, is it still not enough?"

"...What the hell, are you talking about?"

"I smell more, too—the smell of a woman. Not that person, but a smell that resembles that person. A filthy, base, contemptible smell, like a woman rotted and discolored and gone to maggots. Ahh, AHHHHH! AHHHHHHH! It infuriates me! It's detestable! It's hateful!"

Atop the barely-remaining outer edge, the monstrous figure clawed at its bleeding head and let out a shrieking scream. Spitting, stamping its feet, its bearing utterly unlike the composed manner Subaru knew, gone wild with a frenzy—the same madness as before, but its vector was clearly, entirely different now.

"Testing my! Love for my husband, are you, spirit! Not content with stealing my! Husband from me, half-devil harlot!!"

Baring its teeth, letting out a shriek of rage, Sirius leapt. As Sirius fell from the tower, bringing both arms together overhead, crimson flame rose from them. Streaming scarlet from both arms, Sirius landed in the plaza trailing fire in its wake.

Landing on all fours, the monster raised its head, flaming arms still ablaze. Emilia stood with her ice blade raised; Beatrice stepped forward as if to shield Subaru. Looking back and forth between the two of them, Sirius screamed in a voice steeped in fury.

"I am! The Witch Cult's Sin Archbishop! In charge of 'Wrath'!!"

Flames rose skyward, and the crowd, bathed in their heat, raised both arms to the heavens and let out unearthly cries. In a whirlpool of madness utterly unlike anything Subaru had known, the monstrous figure declared its name.

"—Sirius Romanée-Conti!! You damned half-devil, and you damned spirit—I'll burn the both of you together to cinders, and scatter your ashes before my husband's grave!!"
