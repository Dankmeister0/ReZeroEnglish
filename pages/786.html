Arc 10 Chapter 27: "The Sun That Doesn't Rise from the West"

"――How do you think we can successfully break out of the castle?"

If she had mumbled it in a voice like a buzzing mosquito, Heinkel probably would have taken it as a weak complaint from Filore, exhausted from being constantly shut down. He might even have felt relieved, thinking that even she, who always gave everything her all, had moments of weakness. But――

"Sorry. Could you say that again?"

"I said! How do you think we can successfully break out of the castle?"

"――. So I didn't mishear you..."

Her red eyes had lost none of their strength, and her voice remained firm. There was absolutely no trace of a broken person bowed by misfortune in Filore’s entire bearing. She did seem to realize they were discussing something that shouldn't be overheard, as she lowered her voice, but she couldn't hide the rebellious spirit brimming in her eyes and tone at all. Where had the meekness she’d shown toward Teega and the others just moments ago vanished to?

"That was to let those two let their guard down, of course. If I brought up something like this, there’s no way Teega or Sakura would even listen to me seriously."

"The same goes for me, then. Why did you think I would listen..."

"Because, Heinkel-sama, you are my knight, aren't you? You are on my side, not the Divine Dragon Church’s! ...Right?"

"This isn't the kind of secret you share with someone you're starting to doubt as you say it."

As Filore began to tilt her head in confusion mid-sentence, Heinkel vigorously scratched his head. Taking it seriously was absurd, but he understood that Filore had reached this conclusion after thinking it through in her own way. In that case, if he cut her off before letting her vent, there was no telling what kind of reckless stunt she might pull on her own. It was crucial to play along and defuse her impulse.

"First of all, about this breakout... in short, the people you want to help escape are Lady Felt and Lady Emilia, correct?"

"Eh? Yes, exactly! Oh, thank goodness. For a moment, my heart was pounding because I thought you were going to betray me, Heinkel-sama. But if you're also on board, we have nothing to fear!"

"――. So, what kind of plan do you have in mind?"

There was no way he was actually on board with such a foolish idea, but he prompted her to continue without interrupting. Though they hadn't been together long, what Heinkel had learned from his time with Filore was that if he stopped to address every single bizarre thing she said, there wouldn't be enough hours in the day. Completely oblivious to her knight's inner thoughts, Filore chuckled, "Hehehe, thank you for asking!" and secretly pulled a folded piece of paper from inside her clothes.

"Of course, I didn't just come up with this on a whim. Lately, I've been diligently looking into the castle layout in my own way."

As she spoke, Filore cleared away the pile of replies on the desk and spread the paper out. It was a floor plan of the Royal Castle, mapped out by Filore using her own eyes and feet during the areas she visited for prayers and petitions. It detailed areas accessible to visitors and healers, doors restricted only to castle staff, and, even to Heinkel—who was nominally the vice-commander of the Royal Guard—it was remarkably detailed. Furthermore, the hand-drawn map used different colored ink to note details of the security net, such as the number of knights she saw each time she visited the castle and the rotation times of the guards. ――It was the result of espionage work far more meticulous than Heinkel could have imagined, leaving no room for excuses.

"You... what in the..."

"How is it? Do you see how serious I am now? By the way, by eavesdropping on the civil officials who deliver the letters and other castle staff, I also found out that Emilia and Felt are being kept in the castle's east wing. Amazing, isn't it!"

"What the hell were you thinking?!"

Yelling at Filore, who failed to grasp the gravity of the situation, Heinkel snatched the hand-drawn map and crumpled it into a ball in his hand. Filore shrieked, "Ahhh!" but he didn't care. If something like this were found, it would be a major disaster. Evidence that Filore, a Royal Selection candidate and the Saintess of the Divine Dragon Church, harbored rebellious intent toward the Royal Castle—and what made it worse was that it was absolutely true.

"Listen, forget about this map. Forget the plan itself, too. First of all, there's no way it'll work. And even if it did, what then?"

It was a plan akin to a child's tantrum; its feasibility wasn't even worth discussing. True, Felt and Emilia were currently in a difficult position and lacked freedom. Filore's petitions were constantly blocked by the Royal Castle and the Church, which must have felt incredibly stifling as well. But that was all. This situation couldn't last forever. The Royal Selection had a clear deadline, and letting the chaos in the Royal Capital drag on would mean a loss of authority for both the Council of Wise Men and the Royal Castle.

"So, right now, we have to keep our heads down and endure. If we do that..."

"――And what happens if we do? If we just sit and wait for time to pass, will the clouds hanging over our heads disappear, and will the providence of tomorrow change?"

Her quiet voice pierced through Heinkel, who had been squeezing the map smaller and smaller in his hand, muttering as if squeezing out his words. Though she had literally had her hard work crushed, she didn't show a pained expression. Instead, she stared straight at Heinkel.

"Heinkel-sama, even if we behave ourselves, the sun will never rise from the west. We must take action. Prayer is a sacred and noble act, but if we choose it as our first and last resort, even the Dragon's wings will break under the weight."

"――"

"For the past month and a half, I believed I was doing everything I could. But if I give up here, I will feel as though I spent all that time just making excuses for myself. It would feel like I was only doing it to comfort myself and Emilia's group, just to be told, 'It didn't work out, but thank you for trying.' ――I didn't do it for that!"

With that final, louder exclamation, Filore slapped the wall of the room. On it was the magnificent design of the Divine Dragon revered by the Kingdom, identical to the carvings found in various parts of the Royal Castle. It was proof that the Divine Dragon Church also worshiped, praised, and believed in the same entity as the Dragon Kingdom.

Filore was a Royal Selection candidate and the Church's Saintess――meaning she could even be called an existence recognized by both the Kingdom and the Divine Dragon Church. Yet, her current will was undoubtedly in defiance of both.

"Do you remember, Heinkel-sama? I want to build a world where a hand can be reached out to anyone seeking salvation. If I become King, that is my goal. Anyone. Absolutely anyone. That is a very important point."

"――"

"If I, who insist on that, do not reach out my hand to Emilia and Felt who are in such a difficult position right now, who could possibly believe in me? Even I wouldn't be able to believe in myself. Because my actions wouldn't back up my words."

"...Do you understand what that actually means?"

It must feel good to proudly and shamelessly voice one's own desires. It might even be called heroic—provided she actually understood the meaning and value of what she would be giving up as a result.

"If you turn your back on both the castle and the Church, what will you even be? You are a Royal Selection candidate and the Saintess, which is why everyone listens to your words."

Just as no one could ignore him, no matter how miserable and despicable he was, because he belonged to the family of the Sword Saint, was the vice-commander of the Royal Guard, and was the biological father of the strongest Sword Saint in history. If you threw that away, who on earth would listen to the words of a nobody――

"――Heinkel-sama is here."

――Filore said it as if she had the backing of an army of ten thousand.

While touching the Dragon design on the wall, she declared a decision that might betray the very entity she had devoted her faith to, speaking to Heinkel, who wore the uniform of the Church that revered it. She didn't understand just how foolish of a decision it was. And yet, even though she didn't――

"...I knew I shouldn't have become your knight."

Regardless of status or utility, Heinkel regretted taking Filore's hand. Because of that, he had ended up being forced to listen to this utterly foolish consultation. And after hearing it, he was now stuck helping a girl who had no one else to rely on but him.

――Congratulations.

The words of congratulation from Reinhard, spoken in the hallway of the Royal Castle, echoed in his ears. Reinhard had congratulated Heinkel on being chosen as Filore's knight. He didn't think it was a lie or mere flattery. Reinhard wasn't capable of such duplicity.

But was that really true? Was this something to be celebrated? If being chosen as someone's knight and dedicating your sword was a joyful, wonderful thing, why did his stomach and chest ache so much, and why did his heart and soul have to groan in agony?

"Heinkel-sama? So, um, about what we were just discussing..."

Faced with Heinkel, who had covered his face with his palms and fallen silent, Filore spoke up. Peering at him timidly, she had likely said all she needed to say. Now, she was seeking an answer as to where this conversation would lead.

She was a girl who had written a hundred letters only to be met with endless delays. She was already sick of waiting. Her heart was probably rushing, wanting an answer immediately. It was harsh to Filore, but Heinkel's answer was――

"――Two weeks."

"Eh?"

"Sneaking into a castle is something you do with a lot more focus and seriousness."

As he spoke, he spread the hand-drawn map he had crumpled in his hand back out onto the desk. Smoothing out the wrinkles with his palm, he stared down at the plan full of holes, which she had undoubtedly created with utmost seriousness. As it was, it was completely useless.

"We need at least two weeks to prepare and refine the plan. The castle guards and patrol routes are bound to be reviewed periodically. The information Lady Filore gathered can't be used as-is. You don't want to be the idiot who gets caught because you blindly trusted outdated information, do you?"

"――! Does that mean...!"

"Wipe that look off your face. I still think Lady Filore is an idiot."

"Oh, what does it matter? Since you're going along with this little idiot, Heinkel-sama, you must be quite an idiot yourself! That makes us a master and servant pair of absolute fools!"

"An idiot can't become King. You're going to throw away both your status and your honor."

At Heinkel's response, Filore's expression brightened in an instant. To her, who looked ready to burst into a song of joy at any moment, Heinkel presented reality once more. They hadn't done anything yet. They could still turn back now.

"The most foolish part of this plan is that whether it succeeds or fails, everyone involved will end up in a worse position. Naturally, Lady Filore will lose her status as a Royal Selection candidate, and the Divine Dragon Church will be suspected of plotting treason against the Kingdom, prompting an investigation into the entire Church. Teega and Sakura will probably be interrogated and pressured to spit out everything they know, too."

Of course, the Divine Dragon Church had its own share of shady dealings. He didn't think a single action by Filore would expose all of its darkness, but what he needed right now was persuasiveness.

"If you act now, it will become harder to reach out to those you could have saved in the future. Permission to use your sacraments on the victims in Priestella will never be granted again. Indeed, the revocation of your 'Saintess' title will become a reality. That would be a complete rejection of everything Lady Filore has done so far. Even so――"

"――I will do it."

"――"

"I will do it regardless. The wish I harbor isn't something I came up with just because I'm a Royal Selection candidate or the Saintess. Besides, even if we wait..."

"The sun won't rise from the west, huh?"

"Exactly!"

As Filore pointed dramatically at his face, he pushed her finger down and warned, "You're too loud." She hurriedly covered her mouth with both hands, causing him to let out a sigh at the sight.

Why had things turned out this way? He didn't even understand himself. Yet, as he watched Filore's enthusiastic figure, Heinkel felt a reluctance to let go of the hand he had once taken, and thought:

――Do I still want to play at being a knight?

To this question, which he had asked himself before, Heinkel once again could not provide an answer.

――For the next two weeks, Heinkel dredged up his memories as the vice-commander of the Royal Guard—a position where he had been little more than a figurehead—like scraping the bottom of an old wine barrel.

He secretly took out an old castle map showing emergency evacuation routes kept by the Guard, overlaid it with Filore's hand-drawn floor plan, and devised a viable escape route. He accompanied Filore on her hundred-and-tenth and hundred-and-twentieth direct petitions, recording the placement of the knights and their shift rotation times aligned with the sound of the bells to grasp when the guard presence was thinnest. He then compared individual knights' strength and resolve with his own memories to choose the best day for the operation.

"Zeol is a nasty piece of work who does nothing but boast about his lineage, but lately, he's been assigned to the crucial east wing. ...Did he manage to suck up to someone?"

He was a man who specialized in using his status as a knight and his family lineage as weapons, rather than relying on swordsmanship or professionalism. In short, he could be called a budget version of Heinkel. Though when it came to neglecting duties and sheer laziness, there was no comparison between Heinkel and Zeol. At the very least, Zeol did his duties for the Guard properly, even if he wasn't competent. The only reason Heinkel hadn't been expelled from the Guard was entirely due to the power of his family name.

"Even so, they don't draw up guard schedules assuming someone is going to fall asleep."

The reason Zeol and his lackeys were guarding the east wing, where the highly important Felt and Emilia were detained, was because that was deemed sufficient. In other words, the captive Felt and her companion were likely behaving themselves exceptionally well. Or, they had been given a reason that forced them to behave. And that applied not only to Felt and the others, but also directly to Reinhard, who was doing the Royal Castle's bidding and had left his master's side.

"...In the worst-case scenario, even if Lady Filore wanders in, there's a chance they might brush her hand away."

If there was some hidden circumstance forcing Felt and the others to comply with their detention, they might not agree even if they were presented with a fully prepared escape plan. If that happened, far from having the ladder pulled out from under them, they might as well have it set on fire at the base. They would either sit there and be engulfed in flames, or jump off bracing for a fatal fall. Death or broken bones would be unavoidable. However, in a situation where direct face-to-face contact with them was forbidden, it was difficult to even uncover any hidden circumstances that might exist.

"I don't want to make a blind gamble while leaving such a crucial loophole open, but..."

The two weeks was a deadline Heinkel himself had set――of course, if the risks became too great, extending the period would be the sensible thing to do. Yet, even if they were granted indefinite time, a flawless plan would never be born.

――During the two weeks Heinkel spent preparing, Filore's efforts were truly remarkable.

Needless to say, Filore was a girl who was terrible at lying. If she was plotting something, it would show heavily in her attitude and words, making her inner thoughts immediately suspect. He had worried that she wouldn't possibly be able to hide it from Teega and Sakura, who had known her for a long time. And yet, Filore completely laid those anxieties to rest by boldly acting like her usual self, to the point where even Heinkel, who knew the truth, was fooled.

She went about her daily routine, not stopping her petitions for the inquiry of the two detained girls, her pleas for permission to use her sacraments on the people of Priestella, her letter-writing, or her direct appeals. Every time she was met with a delay, she genuinely cried and got angry, staying true to herself. It was obvious that her persistence in this behavior was a way of strictly disciplining herself, knowing that Heinkel had set a deadline and agreed with her ideas.

He couldn't betray that trust. True to Heinkel’s own assessment, after two weeks of preparation, the necessary arrangements were complete. Their reason for entering the castle, the hours when the guards were fewest, a diversion to draw attention elsewhere, the route to reach the captive Felt and her companion, the escape route with them――and the evacuation of those whose presence in the Royal Capital would become their weakness.

Over the course of two weeks, the reckless plan on paper had improved to the point where it required only courage as fare to trust their fates to it. ――Coincidentally, it was the very day that marked exactly two months since Heinkel and Filore had exchanged their vows of master and servant.

――In the sky above the Royal Capital, which had only just begun to turn white, bells were ringing to announce the morning prayers.

Having completed her daily prayers and fulfilled her duties as Saintess this morning as well, Filore stood before the main gate of the Royal Castle, holding her hundred-and-fiftieth petition in total.

"...Adding fifty more letters in two weeks is a bit much."

"Nonsense! A round number feels lucky, doesn't it? Since this is a plan you've meticulously crafted, Heinkel-sama, I won't spare any effort to make it succeed!"

"What kind of effort is that, exactly?"

As usual, her efforts were completely misdirected, but her energy seemed to be at its peak. In fact, contrary to Heinkel, whose heart grew heavier as the day approached, Filore's complexion only grew better and better, making him realize the difference in their caliber. Anyway――

"Are you nervous?"

"Yes! I feel like my heart is about to leap right out of my mouth!"

"If it does, swallow it back down. Don't go dropping it inside the castle."

"Of course. It would be troublesome if someone found it, and I'd feel bad for whoever had to pick it up."

The banter they exchanged was in their usual tone, and he couldn't help but admire her pluck. In that case, he couldn't afford to drag his feet. Heinkel dragged his heavy resolve along, raising his face to look at the castle. Whether in his duties as the vice-commander of the Royal Guard or as Filore's accompanying knight, he had visited the Royal Castle many times. ――But entering the castle with a conspiracy in mind was something Heinkel was doing for only the second time in his life, fifteen years since the first.

"I made sure to send Teega and Sakura off on errands. They've been entirely occupied with securing the route to the clinic until today, so they shouldn't suspect a thing."

"It was lucky that that part also fell into place right at the two-week mark. ――We can still turn back, you know?"

"There is no need to test my resolve so many times. The scriptures say: 'The wise solve the profound, the holy value the simple. Yet the Dragon's law is concise. To love, to protect, and to believe. No wisdom is deeper than this, and none can compare to it!'"

"That's quite a statement from someone about to turn her back on that very Church."

Heinkel shrugged at Filore, who brazenly said such things despite undeniably placing a part of her personal creed in the teachings of the Divine Dragon Church. There was no turning back now. As if to prove it――

"――Apologies for the wait. Please, go on through."

Upon their arrival, the gatekeeper bowed after confirming their reason for visiting and their identities, and the path beyond the heavy gate opened. Filore stepped forward, and Heinkel moved half a step ahead of her.

Using a petition doomed to be rejected as their cover, the master and knight boldly stepped through the front entrance, infiltrating the castle to break its walls.

――The plan went swimmingly, progressing with almost eerie smoothness.

"This is all thanks to the daily good deeds of myself, Emilia, and the others. It must be canceling out Heinkel-sama's bad karma from always looking down on himself."

Such was Filore's welcoming reaction to the situation unfolding exactly as discussed beforehand. It was a harsh way of putting it, but since things were actually going well, he hesitated to argue. In the first place, it would be strange to complain about things going according to plan.

On this day too, Filore paid a visit to the Council of Wise Men and high-ranking nobles under the pretext of petitioning for an inquiry, and was, naturally, dismissed with hollow, slipshod answers. While soothing Filore, who was genuinely frustrated rather than just acting, he slipped away from public view under the pretense of calming her indignation――and entered the planned route.

"――? Heinkel?"

At the back of the east wing, reached by avoiding the eyes of the patrolling guards, two sentries stood before their target room. One of them was the long-haired knight, Zeol Sandrick――a man Heinkel also disliked, who had long treated Heinkel as an eyesore. Zeol and his lackey, another knight, spotted Heinkel walking briskly down the passage with Filore behind him, their eyes widening in surprise.

"Why are you..."

"Because it's your shift."

Zeol stood frozen in confusion, and that split second was more than enough. Sinking his knees to drop out of Zeol's line of sight, Heinkel grabbed his wide-eyed opponent's wrist and pulled him in, slamming his hand into Zeol's open throat to steal his voice. As Zeol let out a choked groan, Heinkel swept his legs to bring him down. Before the remaining guard could draw his sword, Heinkel jabbed his pommel into the man's solar plexus, knocking him to the floor with a follow-up blow. Then, turning back to Zeol, who was clutching his throat on his knees, Heinkel faced him once more.

"Have you... lost... your mind..."

"I've been meaning to tell you this for a while, but if you're a knight, cut your hair or tie it back."

Leaving the gasping Zeol with those words, Heinkel struck the back of his head, knocking him unconscious and leaving both guards slumped on the floor. He then let out a long breath. ――With this, there was finally no going back.

"I suppose I'll count myself lucky that it was just Zeol..."

Naturally, it pained his conscience to knock out knights who were loyal to their duties. Finding a compromise in the fact that it was at least someone he had reason to dislike, Heinkel pushed the two collapsed men to the side of the passage. "Lady Filore," he called out.

"No one should be coming for a while, but this is the crucial part. It's a matter of how quickly we can persuade Lady Felt and Lady Emilia... What is it?"

"N-No, it's just, come to think of it, this is the first time I've ever seen you fight, Heinkel-sama, so I was trembling with surprise. My knight is so strong!"

"――. I only caught them off guard. A truly strong knight wouldn't let his guard down in the first place."

That, Heinkel believed, was the difference between a fraud with a title and the real thing. Just because a surprise attack had succeeded by chance, mistaking that for Heinkel's actual strength would only put Filore in greater danger. Having lectured Filore on this――

"――"

He slowly opened the door and peered inside. Even if they were effectively detained, the official stance was protective custody. Reasoning there was no need to worry about guards inside the room, Heinkel kept Filore behind him and remained on high alert. Seeing Heinkel's face, the person inside the room stared in wide-eyed astonishment.

"...Well, that's a face I didn't expect to see. Just so you know, I haven't seen your son's face in a while, either."

"――. You don't have to tell me. We've passed each other plenty of times."

With Reinhard brought up right off the bat, Heinkel replied with a bitter look. Still, it was to be expected. The only common topic between Heinkel and her――Felt――was naturally his son. And as they exchanged those words, Filore, unable to contain herself, popped her head out from beside Heinkel.

"――Heinkel-sama, is everything alright? Is it safe now? Is there anyone else?"

Whether she had any sense of danger or not, Filore literally poked her head into the room. Meeting her eyes, Felt made an overtly annoyed face.

"You're a pain in the ass even when I'm not locked up, and now you show up when I am..."

"Mind your manners. Lady Filore has gone out of her way to come see you."

To be honest, Felt's reaction was exactly what he'd expected and he even agreed with it, but Heinkel voiced his complaint to clearly establish his position and fulfill his role as a knight. However, Filore cut in, saying, "Now, now, now!"

"It's fine, Heinkel-sama! I know arguing with Felt about that is fruitless for both of us anyway. Rather than that, we should discuss something substantial!"

"You're too loud. Substantial talk?"

Reprimanding Filore for her volume, Felt compared the two of them with searching eyes. It seemed she had caught on to the intent behind Heinkel's way of addressing her. As usual, despite her unrefined attitude, she was quick-witted. Was it really the Royal Lugunican blood? ――No, if it were Lugunican blood, Filore's defenselessness felt far more fitting. Leaving Heinkel to get distracted by thoughts he didn't need to be having right now, Filore nodded vigorously at Felt's words.

"Of course it's substantial! It's a bumper crop! To tell you the truth, I figured you would be running out of patience about now, Felt. Because I was, too! So, am I right?"

"...Well, I won't deny it. But you running out of patience means..."

"――Let's break out. Me, you, Emilia, all of us together."

It was straight, without any detours, right to the point. At that incredibly bold proposal, Felt stared up at the ceiling in silence, remaining quiet for a moment.

"First big sis Emilia, and now you... what is wrong with you Royal Selection candidates?"

"Eh, what do you mean by that?!"

"Nothing, it's just that the big sis Emilia in my head was also eagerly getting ready to bust out. I was just wondering how your brains ended up wired the exact same way."

"Does that mean... it's because Emilia and I are best friends? Oh my... Felt, for you to think of us like that..."

"Hey, old man, tell me you didn't leave the entire plan to this airhead."

Exasperated by Filore, who was holding her blushing cheeks, Felt directed her question to Heinkel. Considering their relationship, she probably didn't even want to speak to Heinkel, but he was impressed by her willingness to hold a dirty tool when the situation demanded it.

"I won't tell you to completely relax, but I've put a decent amount of time into the preparations. I have the guard numbers and patrol routes mapped out. The escape path is an old royal escape route in the castle. I've also secured a safehouse for after we break out, and made arrangements to get out of the Royal Capital."

"Heh, you're better than I thought, old man."

"Right? Heinkel-sama is capable. But still, don't forget that I was the one who thought of escaping in the first place!"

"That's not something to boast about, that's something to reflect on."

"Whaaa?!"

She looked displeased that her expectations had been dashed, wondering why she had thought she'd be praised for that. Beside her, Felt crossed her slender arms and pondered for a moment, tapping her arm with her finger. After about ten seconds, she nodded. "Alright."

"Fine, I'm in. I was planning on making a move soon anyway. Though my plan was just to beat up the guy who brings the food, steal his keys and sword, and make a run for it. I figured if I found big sis Emilia along the way, great. It was pretty much a wing-it job."

"Winging it doesn't count as a plan..."

"I didn't have any tools or info, so what did you expect? But now, those tools and info have grown legs and walked right up to me. No way I'm letting this chance slide."

"See, see! I knew Felt would happily climb aboard! I told you, Heinkel-sama. I love this about her."

"I'm starting to not dislike that about you either."

Matching Filore's cheerful mood, Felt grinned, showing off her prominent canine teeth. Based on prior information alone, they should have had a complicated relationship that naturally led to bickering, but they had hit it off over their shared willingness to use any means necessary for a major cause.

"This is going to give me a massive headache later, but fine. Follow me. Lady Emilia is on a higher floor. They've made appropriate preparations to seal her magic――"

"――What about Reinhard?"

"――"

Just as he was about to move things forward now that they had her agreement, the question he knew was coming popped up, and Heinkel was left at a loss for words.

"Does that bastard know about this? Or is he waiting outside?"

She tried to play it off as a casual question, but Felt's voice was slightly lower. For over two months, Felt and Reinhard had not seen each other—something Heinkel and the others had heard during their previous encounter. Needless to say, this detention was not something Felt wanted. How did Felt view the knight who had left his master in such an undesirable position?

"He doesn't know. He knows absolutely nothing about this."

"――"

"I didn't involve him in this plan. Besides, if he had gotten wind of this, neither I nor Lady Filore would have been able to get this far."

That wasn't irony or sarcasm; it was Heinkel's genuine, unvarnished truth.

"...Is that so."

Felt undoubtedly understood what Heinkel's answer implied, but she gave only that short reply, showing neither letdown nor disappointment on her face. Instead, when her closed eyelids opened once more, what dwelt in her red eyes was a fire――a flame of anger, intensely dazzling. Stoking the fire with her passion, she nevertheless smiled boldly.

"In that case, there's even more reason not to stay in a dump like this. I want to wipe that smug look off his face, so let's get the hell out of here."

"――"

"What? Got a problem?"

"――. No."

Heinkel spat the word out so she wouldn't see through the emotion welling up in his chest at that moment. Honestly, he didn't know. Who on earth had bound Reinhard and this girl together? What was the intent behind it, or what kind of outcome was desired? He knew nothing. Yet, he was suddenly struck by the thought that if Reinhard were to ever choose something, it could only have been this girl. ――Or was it the other way around?

Was it Reinhard who had been chosen? If so――

"That's the spirit! At this rate, we'll slip Emilia out in a jiffy, and Heinkel-sama's brilliant strategy will handle everything after that!"

"――"

Filore's boundlessly cheerful voice ruthlessly cut off the sentimentality passing through his chest. After being taken aback by a statement that was the absolute height of relying on others, the tension left his shoulders. Worrying about things that couldn't be helped in situations where it didn't matter was Heinkel's innate bad habit. He felt as if he had been kicked in the back, told that such thoughts were useless.

"You sure have her trust. Are you really the same old man I know?"

"I'm the exact same. If push comes to shove, I'll hold my sword to your neck and use you as a hostage to force our way out."

"Ha! Yeah, that definitely sounds like the old man I know."

"Why are the two of you bonding over such a dangerous conversation?!"

Leaving Filore to tilt her head in puzzlement, Heinkel peered down the hallway, made sure nothing had been discovered yet, and jerked his chin to tell them to follow. Fortunately, contrary to his pessimistic expectations, persuading Felt had gone well. There was plenty of time before the guard shift rotation. ――We can do this, he felt a surge of confidence.

"――"

He glanced back to look at Filore and Felt following him. The two girls, both sharing the traits of blonde hair and red eyes, also held the same title of Royal Selection candidate. To be chosen and put forward for such a position required a reason. ――A reason like having fate on their side. Perhaps that was something they both possessed.

"Watch your steps and follow me."

Giving instructions that went without saying for Felt but left him anxious if not told to Filore, Heinkel and the girls headed for the upper floor where Emilia was detained. Though his explanation had been cut short, the room where Emilia was kept was supposed to have a spell formula to prohibit the use of magic, and a heavier guard presence than Felt's. This was due less to her combat strength being higher than Felt's, and more to the lingering, unshakeable fear of her lineage.

Even so, there was a ray of hope. ――The hope that Reinhard would not come.

"Their mistake was locking up the Royal Selection candidates in the same wing."

The east wing was now the place where the Royal Castle should place its heaviest security, but the Kingdom could not assign Reinhard, their strongest card, to this wing of all places. This was because they feared Felt's influence over Reinhard. With Felt in the picture, the Kingdom was forced to harbor a sliver of anxiety regarding their ability to move Reinhard as they pleased. ――That was what kept Reinhard away from the east wing.

With the Royal Capital's chaos not yet fully quelled, and under the pretext of maintaining public order, Reinhard had been deployed to guard the outer rim of the Capital today, rather than the Royal Castle. It was no exaggeration to say that Heinkel had spent more than half of the two weeks he used for preparation just to confirm this. ――Therefore, Reinhard would not come.

"Stop."

Arriving at the target floor, he peeked down the corridor from the edge of the stairs and stopped the two girls behind him. They had arrived earlier than scheduled, but the current time was right after a guard shift rotation――a period when their concentration should still be fully intact. Any careless move would lead to detection. And so, he held his breath, but――

"Heinkel-sama? It seems like there's no one here..."

"――"

Tapping Heinkel's shoulder from behind, Filore peeked down the corridor as well, knitting her brows. Just as she said, the hallway where guards should have been stationed was empty, with no sign of anyone in sight. If they ran down this hallway and turned the corner at the end, they could reach the room where Emilia was imprisoned without any obstacles.

"Could our daily good deeds have reached this far...?"

"This is strange."

"No way it's like this."

In front of a trembling Filore, Heinkel and Felt's opinions aligned. While Filore muttered behind them, "And you're my knight, too..." Heinkel held back a click of his tongue. It was a bizarre thing, but mentally, it would have been easier if guards had been exactly where they expected. An absence of what should be there. A lack of what was expected. ――A discrepancy in their assumptions, even if it worked in their favor as good luck, squeezed Heinkel's stomach like a vise.

When a streak of coincidences continues at a gambling parlor, only an amateur rejoices at their good luck. A professional suspects they are being allowed to win. ――That was the wisdom of a man well-accustomed to losing.

"――Stay here."

Instructing the two of them, Heinkel climbed the rest of the stairs. He stepped slowly down the hallway, closing the distance to where the guards should have been standing—one step, then two. A sharp silence rang in his ears, and the pounding of his heart felt hot and painful.

Seeking a chance to confirm whether this was just a phantom pain born of cowardice by taking another step――in that instant, a chill ran down his neck, and Heinkel's right hand reflexively shot to his sword hilt. An instantaneous draw rooted in survival instinct—but that split-second movement was stopped. ――No, it was blocked. Just as the blade cleared the scabbard by an inch.

"――!"

A palm coming down from above pinned the pommel. No matter how much force Heinkel put into his arm, the sword wouldn't budge a hair from its sheath. He could neither draw it nor put it back. It was a pressure so immense, it felt as if the castle itself were holding his sword down.

"――What are you doing here, Vice-Commander?"

When had he stood there? With that massive frame and heavy armor, how could he possibly have concealed his presence and footsteps so completely?

Faced with an incomprehensible reality and the runaway train of out-of-place thoughts rising once again, Heinkel's body trembled from the very depths of his soul.

Reinhard would not come. That deduction had been correct. But he must not make a mistake. Even if Reinhard was absent, there was a reason why the Royal Lugunica Castle was hailed as an impregnable fortress. And that reason was――

"Do you understand what you are doing? ――Heinkel."

――Indeed, it was the presence of the Commander of the Royal Guard, Marcos Gildark, who was shrouded in a heavy and solemn sword aura.
