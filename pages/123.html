Arc 3 Chapter 46: "The White Whale's Jaws"

Bathed in a spray-laden gust of wind, Subaru had the illusion of being struck head-on by a fist.

"――!"

The gale slammed into his whole body, lifting him from his seat, and he was nearly hurled clean over the driver's box and out into the open air. The fingers he threw out on reflex caught nothing, and his body was on the verge of tumbling into the darkness with no protection at all when—

"――ru-kun!"

A grip seized the back of his collar and forced his posture violently back down. He was slammed onto the seat rump-first as if flung down bodily, and at the edge of his sparking vision he saw Rem, one hand stretched toward Subaru's neck, the other hauling hard on the reins.

Her fine profile was etched with alarm, her sharp eyes fixed on the direction the wind was howling from.

"――!!"

She opened her mouth and let out a roar sharp enough to bare her pointed canines.

But even that fierce battle-cry of hers was swallowed by the storm and failed to reach Subaru, close as he was.

Yet the mana that filled the air caught her cry, though it never reached his eardrums.

Rem's spat-out incantation drew the mana together, and magic that reshapes the world was unleashed.

What was born were spears of ice, each nearly the size of Subaru's own body. In an instant three of them took shape in midair, their sharp points leveled at the darkness.

After a moment's pause, the ice spears shot forward with tremendous force, like arrows loosed.

The ice stakes, moving at a speed the eye could barely follow, streaked toward the dark with their aim set, and struck—their points driving into an end that had seemed limitless but proved surprisingly close.

An instant later—

"――fwa!?"

Again came the sensation of a hand gripping his neck, and this time he was hauled straight upward in one motion. The seat fell away as if he'd been yanked out of it, and Subaru's body shot toward the night sky without any way to resist.

Launched into the air, hurtling past the driver's box and leaving the carriage behind, he watched the carriage racing along below gradually recede.

Falling. Not thrown from a dragon's back, but thrown off a flying dragon carriage—such an absurd thought crossed his mind as a moment's escape from reality, and in that same instant, Subaru saw it.

A colossal mass, striking sideways, smashed that dragon carriage to splinters.

The sturdily built carriage frame was torn apart as if it were paper, and the large earth dragon pulling it was likewise blown apart by the impact—blood and viscera scattered across the highway, wood and flesh mingling into a single mass of mincemeat.

At the sheer, unreal horror of the sight, Subaru's thoughts went white.

It was a shock so total that he even forgot he was currently airborne. What snapped Subaru back from the blankness that the carnage before his eyes had driven him into was—

"Left――!!"

A girl's voice, right beside him, shouted like a scream.

In that instant, his body was flung down onto a hard floor with no chance to brace himself. A dull pain shot through his shoulder and hip, but before he could even voice a protest, he was thrown about by the centrifugal force of a sharp turn.

He rolled, and was on the verge of being flung out again when he managed to catch something rope-like against his fingertips and just barely stop himself. A beat later, Subaru lifted his head, looked around, and realized he was in the cargo bed of Otto's dragon carriage.

Some kind of fastening from the covered carriage's canopy. Winding the dangling rope around his wrist, Subaru tried to haul himself upright amid the violent shaking. Just then—

"Don't, Subaru-kun! Don't stand! The earth dragon's Divine Protection has been cut off. Rem and Subaru-kun can't move properly!"

Rem's sharp warning cut through the air toward Subaru, who was struggling just to get to his feet.

Looking over, he saw Rem, who had likewise landed on Otto's carriage, driving her right hand—blade-straight—into the floor to keep herself upright. Even her physical prowess couldn't manage to stand straight amid this shaking.

The familiar violent tremor was the effect of losing the bodily support granted by an earth dragon's Divine Protection. With the earth dragon's Divine Protection—which normally shielded riders from wind, noise, and jolting—cut off, they were plunged into a state where even standing was a struggle, and nausea set in.

Understanding this, and having caught sight of Rem, Subaru finally grasped the situation.

In other words, Rem had carried Subaru and leapt from the disintegrating carriage over to Otto's. Had her judgment come even a moment later, the two of them would have shared that carriage's fate—limbs blown apart, joining the ranks of unrecognizable minced meat.

Shaking off the chill that raced down his spine, Subaru pressed himself close to the edge of the cargo bed as if clinging to it, and raised his voice to be heard over the wind and the roar of their passage.

"W-what happened!? What in the hell is—"

An upheaval that had occurred in the span of mere tens of seconds. His mind couldn't keep pace with how dense it had been.

"You don't know!?"

But at Subaru's bewilderment, Otto turned back with a voice like a shriek.

His face bloodless, his teeth chattering, he shouted as if in agony,

"There's night fog out there! Something that big, swimming through the sky trailing fog behind it—there's only one thing it could be!"

His voice was gripped by the particular tremor of someone in despair.

As if confirming it to himself, as if refusing to accept it, shaking his head in denial, dragging in ragged breath after ragged breath with convulsing lungs, Otto screamed with all his strength.

"――It's the White Whale!!"

――The White Whale.

That name had come up only once before, in the very first loop of this world.

The name of the monster said to trail fog and block the highway. Because of that White Whale, said to have appeared on the Lifaus Highway, the first loop's return journey had been forced to detour around the road. It could be said that this delay was the very reason he hadn't made it in time to prevent the tragedy that struck the village and the mansion.

But he had never actually encountered it, and had never heard its name mentioned again after that—

"No way it's showing up now, of all times!?"

When they'd left the royal capital half a day ago, there hadn't been a single report of the White Whale's appearance anywhere.

Rem hadn't mentioned it, and more importantly, Otto and the others—whose very profession should make them avoid the White Whale at all costs—had still chosen to use this highway. Which meant the White Whale must have appeared sometime after Subaru's group had set out.

――No, worse than that—what if, at this very moment, it had only just now appeared on the highway?

The timeline lined up. In the first loop, Subaru's group had departed on the third day. By that point the highway had already been sealed off due to the White Whale's appearance. And now it was the middle of the night on the second day—if word of the White Whale's appearance spread by morning, the highway would likely be sealed by tomorrow.

Which meant that if they were going to run into it, tonight was the only night it could happen.

"No way, not now, not in Lugunica, of all times... running into the White Whale... ah, Dragon, O Dragon. Save us, save us..."

Eyes hollow, muttering under his breath like a prayer, Otto begged for salvation.

Seeing him utterly drained of not just fighting spirit but the will to live, Subaru felt the truth behind the merchants' dread of the White Whale settle into him as real.

In the previous world, Otto had once described the White Whale to Subaru as a symbol of ill omen for merchants. At the time Subaru had privately thought he was being dramatic—but seeing Otto's utter devastation now that he'd actually encountered the fog, he couldn't help but think that description had been almost childishly understated.

Lips trembling, mind clearly elsewhere, Otto nonetheless kept working the reins. His earth dragon, having sensed the White Whale's presence, had fallen into a panic, kicking off the ground at a speed that disregarded whatever stamina it had left, driving them ever faster.

Feeling that shaking directly through his whole body, Subaru turned and strained his eyes into the darkness. He searched, eyes roving, for the White Whale, but the pitch black—stripped of any light source—sealed the world away completely. It was a night already deep in darkness, one where even your own hands would be hard to make out without a light. And on top of that—

"I thought something felt sticky... it's the fog, isn't it."

Feeling droplets on his forehead that weren't cold sweat, he grimaced at the damp sensation on his palm when he touched it.

In a night already devoid of light, the addition of fog made securing any visibility hopeless. Indeed, even the glow of the Crystal Lamp lighting the ground beneath the running earth dragon's neck was dimmed by the night fog, its effectiveness cut by half.

"Rem! Can you see the White Whale!?"

"It's too dark, there's no way! ...But!"

Looking behind, beside, above, Subaru searched for any sign of that fish-like silhouette. Rem answered his call with anguish in her voice, then cut herself off mid-sentence, as if hesitating over something.

Concerned, he looked at her, but in the dense fog he could make out only her silhouette—not even her expression was visible to him now.

The fact that he couldn't see the very cause of this crisis—the White Whale itself—only accelerated his terror.

When Subaru had first spotted what he took to be the White Whale's eye, hadn't that eye been larger than the ring he could form with both arms outstretched?

If a single eyeball alone was that massive, then the White Whale, true to its name, truly possessed a body to rival that of an actual whale.

"But Rem's first strike should've landed. ...There's a chance it backed off."

Was that too optimistic?

The power of the ice spears Rem had loosed with her incantation ranked among the strongest magic Subaru had ever witnessed—strong enough that a single one would have killed him, and he'd have died three times over from the barrage.

However massive its body, even a creature like that should flinch from a grievous wound.

"What about the other carriages...?"

"They've scattered and fled. The moment fog appears, everyone splits up and runs immediately. With luck, they can outpace the White Whale and escape the fog."

Indeed—there wasn't another carriage running alongside this one. The other carriages that had surely been following behind until now had apparently scattered in every direction, following that very protocol.

――Inwardly, Subaru bit down on his bitterness at having lost the transportation he'd worked so hard to secure.

The timing couldn't have been worse. His plan to carry the villagers to safety had collapsed yet again. And if the fog hadn't rolled in, it might have served as a safeguard against some other threat entirely.

"No use crying over it now. Anyway, we just need to get through the fog, fast, and—"

Enduring the nausea of his organs being churned by the violent shaking, Subaru's thoughts turned to the problems waiting beyond their escape. With only one carriage left, he'd need to figure out how to get the villagers to safety after getting Emilia and the others away first. And that would cost them even more precious time.

And so, just as Subaru had lifted his gaze from the immediate crisis to the troubles further ahead—right in front of him, that is, directly in the carriage's path—

"――!!"

A gaping maw lined with rows of teeth like millstones was closing in from directly ahead, ready to swallow the carriage whole.

A roar thundered out, and the overwhelming violence of that sound and the accompanying blast of wind made the earth dragon recoil in terror. Its legs tangled, gouging the ground, and the wheels lifted as the carriage bed tilted sharply. A jar of oil burst through the canopy and was flung away, and Subaru, clinging to the edge, very nearly went flying with it.

Clinging desperately to the cargo bed, he could see straight ahead—in the darkness, unnaturally vivid, only the grimy teeth inside the White Whale's mouth stood out with perfect clarity.

It was only in this very instant that Subaru arrived, far too late, at an understanding of how naive his own grasp of the situation had been.

Encountering the White Whale, adrift in the depths of the night fog—that had already shifted from a problem of what happens after we escape the fog to how do we survive this very instant. And he hadn't had the composure to realize it.

"――ruaaaaaaa!!"

Just as jaws wide enough to swallow the carriage whole in a single easy motion closed in, a roar burst out, and with the impact, the wooden planking of the cargo bed floor shattered outward.

It was Rem who kicked off and leapt forward like a bullet. Beneath her white bonnet, streaming in the gale, her sharp horn was bared—the oni transformation. Rem, wielding an iron ball on a chain drawn from who knows where, swung it around.

"――Cut left and drive through!!"

"Left-left-left-left-left!!"

The iron ball crushed down on the White Whale's upper jaw from directly above, spraying a mist of pitch-black blood and forcing the gaping mouth to close slightly. Gouging into the earth, its momentum still undiminished, the massive maw bore down, and Otto's carriage—Otto spitting with the desperate focus of controlling his earth dragon—tore past right alongside that colossal face.

The right side of the racing carriage's cargo bed couldn't quite clear the enormous body and was caught in the graze, letting out a scraping shriek as if dragged across bare rock before it crumpled and was torn away. The wheelless bed groaned violently, its balance lost, and it began to flip over.

Naturally, Subaru, still on top of it, was helplessly about to be flung out into the darkness as well, and—

――Am I going to die?

Just before the price for his slow judgment was about to scatter his body without mercy, amid the roaring din came the light, chain-linked clatter of metal, and a merciless pain wrenched around his torso—he was yanked up off his feet as if plucked bodily, and hauled forward.

And then—

"Eat, thiiiiiiiis――!!"

With the iron ball that had hauled Subaru up now in her right hand, Rem used her free left hand to smash apart the joint between the driver's box and the cargo bed, seized the edge of the now-severed and drifting-away bed—and in that instant, a weight sufficient to make the carriage-pulling earth dragon whinny in strain built up, and with a single roaring cry, the massive freight carriage bed was hurled backward by Rem.

That gigantic mass, hurled like an entire building thrown bodily, struck the flank of the passing White Whale dead-on. A splintering crash of shattering wood rang out, and the writhing White Whale's tail exploded the ground, scattering clods of earth in all directions.

Whether it had actually done any damage, he couldn't say. But there was no doubt the White Whale was still very much alive. And, more importantly, that it had wheeled its airborne body around and fixed its glare on them.

"D-did, did we get it――!?"

Though he clearly didn't understand what had just happened, he must have grasped that most of his own carriage had just been lost. Given the price they'd paid for it, Otto's question carried a hope pasted thinly over pure desperation.

The thundering roar. The ceaseless tremor of the ground. The pressure closing in from behind, a pressure with a name: despair. Faced with all of this, even while knowing it was a wish with no meaning whatsoever—

"Why—why does it keep coming after us specifically... we're not the only prey out here!?"

Ranting, Otto cursed his own misfortune.

Buried in that was resentment at the unfairness of being singled out for attack when eight other groups of candidates existed as alternatives. Subaru shared the sentiment, but watching Otto continue to curse so bitterly, he found himself unable to voice it aloud.

A sacrifice. A wall of flesh. Had it come to facing the Witch Cult, he might well have used them the same way—and glimpsing that possibility in himself now left a bad taste in his mouth.

Besides, there wasn't a shred of composure left to spend on such thoughts.

The White Whale's threat still loomed behind them, and its speed swimming through the sky outpaced the earth dragon's. Even with the earth dragon freed of the carriage's dead weight, being caught was only a matter of time. And if they lost the earth dragon, there was no possibility whatsoever of outrunning it on foot.

He had to think. Something, there has to be something, something, something.

But no breakthrough came to mind. Even trying to grasp at any possible plan, with the spreading night fog rendering even the ground beneath his feet invisible, he couldn't find so much as a hint.

And then, while Subaru once again let time slip uselessly away without choosing anything, the oni girl made her own decision.

Amid the violent shaking, Rem quietly approached Subaru as he clung to the carriage frame. She, too, must have been feeling the same impacts, yet her footing betrayed none of it as she drew close to him.

"Subaru-kun. Please take this."

"What!? Is there something, some way to fix this—"

Had he glimpsed a ray of hope? Subaru lifted his face slightly, and into his hands was pressed a small pouch, heavy with weight. He grimaced at the weight of it, and immediately from the feel of the pouch's exterior recognized it as a travel purse holding coins—gold coins, by the feel.

What use could this money possibly be, here, now?

Unable to suppress the chill that ran through him at Rem thrusting the travel funds into his hands, Subaru forced a rising smile onto his face.

"R-Rem...? I know throwing coins can shift the power balance in a fight, but that's... that's a game thing, that's..."

"Rem will descend to the ground and intercept it. While she does, please escape the fog with this gentleman, Subaru-kun."

Clearly, in a voice steeped in resolve, Rem told him this.

Subaru, who had been trying to smooth over the moment with a dry joke, was struck silent by those words. Even as he stood there, Rem turned only her head back and said,

"Otto-sama, please look after Subaru-kun. The promised reward is in his hands. ――Once you're clear of the fog, please, report that the White Whale has appeared."

"M-money!? This isn't the time for that, life comes before money, you know!?"

Otto's response—he clearly hadn't heard the exchange behind him. Even so, it was plain he was doing everything he could to keep the carriage running, and Subaru saw Rem's lips ease into a relieved smile at that.

And Subaru hadn't missed it. The fact that she was telling Subaru and Otto, who would be the ones fleeing, to report that the White Whale had appeared meant—

"You... Rem, you don't mean—you're not planning on coming back alive, are you!?"

Rem's expression clouded with grim resolve.

Though the world around them remained sunk in unbroken darkness, for some reason her pale face alone now appeared to Subaru with startling clarity.

"I won't let you go, I won't let you go! If you die, I'll... I'll—!"

He let the pouch of gold coins fall to his feet and pulled Rem, standing before him, close by the waist.

He held her small frame in his arms, anchoring the presence that was trying to slip away. If he let go of this now, she would fling herself out there, past even the point of life itself.

That much, he had to stop. Otherwise—

"Ahh..."

Amid a surge of emotion that threatened to bring him to tears, Rem, held in his embrace, let out a warm breath.

At the sound of that rapture, so out of place for the moment, he lowered his gaze, and Rem, in Subaru's arms, looked up at him and smiled, entranced.

"Rem was born for this moment, wasn't she."

"What are you—"

He couldn't finish saying talking about.

An impact struck the back of his neck, and a sensation like the world flipping over assailed Subaru.

Rem, her arms stretched as if returning his embrace, had struck the back of his head with the blade of her hand. The strength drained from his body, and he sagged, collapsing against her.

"R-Rem... what are you doing..."

Not just his vision but his very consciousness swam and swam, and even holding his head up became difficult; still, Subaru clung desperately to the girl.

Rem gazed at Subaru's struggling form with eyes full of tender affection, and, as if chasing after his fading consciousness, whispered close to his ear. It was:

"It's all right. Rem will always be behind you, Subaru-kun."

――You don't need to do anything. Just always stay behind me.

It was Subaru himself who had said those words to her, this very morning, before they'd set out.

And so, true to those words, she would stand—stand to protect Subaru's back.

"No... that's not, I didn't mean it, like—"

His consciousness slipped away.

Receding. He couldn't hold on.

For a moment, he thought he felt himself held tightly, just once more.

Something soft and gentle touched his forehead, and then was gone at once.

And that was the last of it.
