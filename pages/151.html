Arc 3 Chapter 74: "Fulfillment of the Contract"

—Natsuki Subaru had an obsessive conviction that this situation must never be allowed to happen.

He didn't know why. There was no basis for it, no evidence—just a vague, formless unease. And yet the certainty that this must not happen sat firm and unshaken in his chest.

Atop the hill, Emilia stood with her silver hair streaming in the wind, palm outstretched toward him, while Petelgeuse spread both arms wide, his face alight with joy as if welcoming the encounter.

Subaru had, without even realizing it, been working all along to avoid the one thing that must never happen: letting these two cross paths.

And yet now, in this dead-end, hopeless, do-or-die situation—cornered with no cards left to play—she had walked right into it.

"Why... why now...!"

Before shock, before regret, anger surged up first.

It was anger at himself, for knowing this situation had to be avoided and yet failing to make the effort. And above all,

"You should have known better than anyone—Puck!!"

The furious roar tore across the grassland, reaching the spirit hovering beside the silver-haired girl. But the spirit, even as it caught the shout, gave no attention to Subaru at all—its gaze remained fixed, single-minded, on Emilia's profile.

It waved its paws, darting circles around Emilia, its face pasted with an urgency Subaru had never seen on it before.

"Lia! No, you have to leave right now! That's a Sin Archbishop of the Witch Cult! You should never have met him! You shouldn't be here at all!"

"It's too late to turn back now, and I have no intention of it anyway. Everyone's fighting so hard they've ended up like that—I wasn't raised to sit back somewhere safe and behave myself."

Emilia answered Puck's desperate plea, her cheeks tense with a certain strain. At her stubborn resolve, Puck pressed a paw to his face, shaking his head, "No, no, that's not it—"

"It's not about who's who or anything like that! It's the Witch Cult! It's the Witch Cult... ahh, I only just remembered now! The Trial! It's a Trial, Lia. The Sin Archbishops impose Trials! And by the Contract, I can't get involved in a Trial! This is as far as I go! So..."

Puck cut himself off, his expression going rigid as though barred from saying anything further. At the sight of the little cat—always so composed, never once letting his own pace be disturbed—even Emilia's eyes widened slightly in surprise.

"Puck... do you know something?"

"I don't know anything! But I know that I know something! That much I can tell. That's why this is bad, Lia. Go back right now. If you stay here, if you meet them, something more terrifying than death is bound to happen!"

Puck spoke an answer that was no answer at all. But for all his floundering, he made no move to physically stop Emilia, nor did he use his tremendous power to force the situation under control.

At this maddeningly frustrating attitude, Subaru felt an anger he couldn't contain rising in him. Without even understanding why his chest burned so hot,

"If Emilia ends up out here now... then what was the point of everything I—! What were you even for!"

"You're the one saying that to me!? Lia pushed past even my words—for whose sake do you think she came all the way out here...!"

His face twisted with mortification, Puck wrung the words out in answer to Subaru's shout.

At his answer, Subaru too grasped a truth he couldn't look away from, and self-reproach crushed down on him.

It was an obvious conclusion, a natural one.

There was only one reason she would have climbed down from the fleeing Dragon Carriage and come back to the battlefield.

Subaru and the others, who had stayed behind in the face of danger without a word, without telling her anything.

She was someone who always chose to lose out for the sake of others, who followed the shape of her own heart honestly no matter who it alienated—of course a girl like that would never accept being merely protected.

It was a misjudgment. Once again, Subaru had piled another one on.

And with the misunderstanding left to compound unresolved, it had come to light at the worst possible moment.

—There was no longer a single moment's grace to spare.

"Puck..."

Still atop the hill, Puck clung to Emilia, pleading with her to reconsider. But there was no way to bend her firm resolve.

Emilia, too, had half given up on asking Puck for help, and closed her eyes as if to rely on her own magical strength alone, beginning to chant.

And the eerie madman who had kept his silence, Petelgeuse—

"What... ahh, what a fine day, yes. Truly a reflection—surely there could be no body more fitting for a Vessel than this..."

Tears spilling, the madman took a step toward the hill, his voice trembling with passion he couldn't contain. His stepping foot wavered, but that gait was nothing less than a countdown to ruin, ticking down for certain.

"—"

Puck was no help to rely on. Driven purely by a sense of duty, Subaru rose to his feet.

The instant his touching hand pulled away, Patrasche's breathing quietly stopped. A single tear traced down Subaru's cheek. He wiped it away roughly with the back of his hand and broke into a run.

"Subaru-kyun!?"

Leaving Ferris's astonished voice behind him, Subaru ran, forgetting even the creaking pain that racked his entire body. Beside him, the battlefield where the Witch Cultists and the subjugation force clashed went unglanced-at; the only thing the running Subaru fixed his eyes on, single-mindedly, was the girl atop the hill.

Right now, he was the only one who could stop her encounter with the madman.

He didn't know how much good this body, this small and insignificant self, could do. He didn't know, but even so, he had to do it. Otherwise, how could he possibly—

"I would ask that you... not interfere—yes!"

Sensing Subaru closing in, Petelgeuse tilted only his head to look back, and his shadow swelled—jet-black magic hands burst forth, surging in a rush to entangle Subaru.

Against the crushing pressure of palms that seemed intent on tearing his limbs clean off, Subaru twisted his body, threading through the gaps without losing speed. Here, now, a change came over a body that should have been heavy with exhaustion and wounds—his concentration honed to a razor's edge.

In other words, the manifestation of hysterical strength born of an emergency.

Movement beyond his limits took hold of Subaru's body, and he kept dodging the encroaching palms by a hair's breadth.

At Subaru's abnormal movements, Petelgeuse clicked his tongue,

"How infuriating, yes, that you can see the Unseen Hand..."

"Don't you dare open that filthy mouth of yours again! I won't let you do a damn thing more!"

The distance between them dropped under twenty meters, and the number of magic hands pouring out to tear Subaru to pieces had swelled enough to blot out his field of vision.

But there was no fear of his body being torn apart. What drove Subaru was a flame of urgency, seething to burn through his whole body, shoving aside any heart's fear of death.

He drove forward, dodging a palm that grazed his cheek. From his left cheek to his ear split open in a gash, and the pain of gushing blood seared his nerves. But driving his consciousness—on the verge of being torn apart by agony—past its limits, Subaru's body pressed on, forward, forward—and his outstretched arm surged toward Petelgeuse's slender shoulder, reaching to seize it,

"If the Unseen Hand leaves me at a disadvantage, well, yes, then there's nothing for it."

"—Struggling in vain!"

"—Uldona."

Petelgeuse chanted the short incantation with a bored expression.

The instant the word reached Subaru's ears and his eyes went wide in shock, the ground beneath his feet shook violently, and his stance collapsed as if his planted leg had been swept out from under him. And then—

"No... way...!?"

"The Unseen Hand is proof of the affection bestowed upon me alone, yes... but did I ever once say that was the only thing I could wield?"

Petelgeuse, head tilted ninety degrees, spat out his vicious sarcasm as he receded from view.

No—the ground beneath Subaru's feet had heaved upward at a tremendous speed, shoving his body, directly above it, up into the sky.

The entire patch of ground beneath his feet sprang up like a spring-loaded platform, and Subaru's body, riding atop it, was flung effortlessly into the air. The height easily cleared ten meters—

"—Ngh, ah!?"

Still off-balance, he was slammed by the leaping ground, his chest taking a heavy blow that knocked the wind from him. His consciousness clouded white from the impact, and flung as he was in that state, Subaru had no effective means of countering the fall.

A fall from height. And one where, in all likelihood, he'd land headfirst—that much he'd already confirmed once before, courtesy of the "true Murphy's Law." Now again, he plummeted headlong, crown-first, on course to snap his neck and lose his life on the spot—an instant before that,

"Subaru—!!"

At the same moment the silvery bell of a voice rang out in urgent alarm, a translucent mass rose up from directly below to catch the falling Subaru.

It had been shaped with the utmost care to soften the shock of the fall, but given what it was made of, some damage was unavoidable. At the hard sensation and the cold sting against his skin, Subaru forced his eyes open in pain, and what met them was—

"A mountain... of ice? No, a tower...?"

An iceberg, built up to the same height as the risen ground in order to save Subaru after he'd been launched into the air—that was the true identity of the mass that had saved him from a fatal fall.

His blood-slicked skin stuck to the ice, and wincing at the pain of skin being peeled away, Subaru pushed himself upright, feeling relief at his narrow escape and the weight of an irreversible mistake all at once.

—There was no need to wonder whose hand had built this iceberg.

"—Emilia!!"

Turning around, Subaru raised himself on the slick ice and, in frustration, turned his face toward the hill. But the risen earth blocked his view like a sheer cliff face, and cut off as he now was on the far side of the hill from Emilia and Puck, he couldn't see what was happening with Petelgeuse either.

All he knew was that Emilia's chant had been interrupted. And she couldn't see Petelgeuse's Unseen Hand—!

"This is bad... this is the worst. To get around behind this wall...!"

He had to climb down the iceberg and get past the wall of raised earth. The ground that had risen to block off the hill had become like a sheer cliff, and the terrain made it hard to circle around to the other side. Alone, Subaru had no way of managing it.

The skin of his palms stuck to the ice, and the pain of it tearing away seared through his brain. Enduring the sharp pain again and again, tears welling at the corners of his eyes, he descended the iceberg, literally bleeding as he went.

It was fortunate that there happened to be several handholds to grab onto, and combined with his desperation, he made it down the mountain of ice at a startling speed, half-falling the whole way.

But with no time to catch his breath, Subaru looked up at the wall and was at a loss.

"This wall... damn it, that bastard was playing me the whole time...!"

Slamming his blood-soaked hand against the earthen wall, Subaru gritted his teeth, cursing his own shallow thinking yet again.

He had assumed—underestimated the man—that sealing off Petelgeuse's Unseen Hand alone would be enough to neutralize him. As a result, he'd been thoroughly outmaneuvered—

"—Sir Subaru!"

"...Wilhelm-san?"

From behind the downcast Subaru, the old swordsman came running up.

Bearing few wounds and his treasured sword in hand, he nodded to Subaru and glared at the wall.

"Only for an instant, but I caught sight of Lady Emilia. —Is the Sin Archbishop beyond here as well?"

"Yeah... yeah, that's right! I got played! Emilia... she can't meet that guy, Petelgeuse—it can't happen! Something awful will happen... I can't explain it well, but it can't happen. Just that one thing..."

Unable to put the reason for his desperation into words, Subaru clung to Wilhelm, choking down his frustration. At his incoherent words, Wilhelm nodded.

"Leave it to me. I shall carve this wall open with my own hand, here and now."

"You say cut through it, but..."

To Subaru's unspoken question of just how one was meant to deal with a wall so high it had to be looked up at, Wilhelm gave an answer that was blunt to the point of simplicity, and demonstrated it through action.

That is, he gouged into the wall with a flashing white blade, and in rapid succession, countless strikes began slicing through the earthen wall. The answer was simple, and quite literal: he would "cut it open."

"Wh—whoa..."

Witnessing the strikes of a true sword demon at such close range, Subaru could do nothing but stand transfixed by the overwhelming skill on display. Even in this moment of crisis, the beauty of the flashing blade and the heights of that swordsmanship made it clear—here, one could truly come to know that such a thing as the pinnacle of the sword existed.

Behind them, even as Subaru and Wilhelm withdrew from the front line to face the wall, the battle between the Witch Cultists and the subjugation force was reaching its climax.

The subjugation force—the beastfolk mercenary band—fought valiantly, Ricardo's great cleaver sending Witch Cultists flying. And behind them stood Julius, commanding six differently glowing Quasi-Spirits,

"Ia, Kua, Iku, Aro, In, Ness—lend me your strength, all of you."

The Quasi-Spirits called by name intensified their individual glows in answer to his call. In the next instant, six kinds of light kindled in the slender sword Julius gripped, becoming a blade of rainbow color, sending the Finest Knight forth into the battlefield.

"Finally ready, are ya!? Kept me waitin' a good long while, didn'tcha!"

"My apologies, Ricardo. I've placed a great burden on your valiant efforts. —Allow me now to show results worthy of that toil."

Answering Ricardo's cursing with dignified composure, Julius gave his rainbow-glowing blade a light swing. In the air, a rainbow traced the arc the blade had passed through, and glittering light danced wildly.

It was fantastical, certainly. But as for its practicality, that remained unclear—Julius, wielding the blade, closed in on a Witch Cultist. Noticing him, the black-robed figure braced a dagger low at the hip and lunged at the calmly advancing knight. To this—

"At this moment, my sword interferes with all creation. —Challenging it is futile."

The rainbow flashed, and the black-robed figure's dagger vanished, arm and all.

A strike that drew not even blood. Having lost both arms up to the elbow, the black-robed figure couldn't even comprehend what had happened to it before it was struck by the next blow and erased entirely.

"Now—let only those who wish to be captivated by the rainbow's radiance stand before me!"

Declaring this, Julius raised his sword as the Witch Cultists swarmed toward him. Alongside Ricardo standing at his side, Julius met them head-on—a sight altogether reassuring.

And meanwhile, here too—

"The wall... it's breaking through!"

"—Hyah!"

Beyond the digging strikes, faint light began filtering through the wall, and just as Subaru cried out in anticipation of the breakthrough, the tip of the treasured sword pierced clean through the wall.

Wilhelm, showered in dirt from head to toe, kicked the wall down with a front kick, securing a gap wide enough for a person to pass. Following the sword demon as he slipped through smoothly, Subaru too poked his head out on the far side of the wall.

A considerable amount of time had been lost. All the while, they'd been utterly unable to intervene. If, as a result, Emilia had been lost—

"Emi—"

But Subaru's worst imaginings had unfolded into reality in a form entirely different from what he'd pictured.

"—Ahh, it hurts, it hurts, it hurts, yes."

His voice trembling at the end of each word, Petelgeuse groaned in pain, spilling blood.

Already, one whole side of his body—especially the lower left of his abdomen—had a considerable number of icicles driven into it, and his left leg had been pinned to the ground entirely, a spike jutting up from the earth having pierced clean through the top of his foot.

And facing him, behind Emilia, several more blades of ice hovered, still awaiting their launch, making it plain at a glance that the battle had turned in her favor.

Precisely because it was so plain to see—it was the worst possible outcome.

"—Emilia!!"

Subaru shouted, straining his voice to try to keep it from becoming reality.

But before Subaru could put it into words, the dying Petelgeuse turned his palm this way—extending a jet-black magic hand—faster still.

The approaching palm moved so slowly—whether from Petelgeuse's own dwindling life force or otherwise—that it bore no resemblance to its former self. Even pulling back Wilhelm, who couldn't see it, to dodge left speed enough to spare, walking-pace slow. But that magic hand, which posed no conceivable threat to Subaru—

"Look out, Subaru—!!"

—to Emilia, who knew nothing of it, could only look like it would deal Subaru a fatal blow.

And so—

"Ahh, an abandonment of thought—you're 'Sloth,' yes?"

The face wearing that mad grin was severed by the falling blades of ice and sent spinning through the air.

Blood sprayed from the beheaded torso, and the body, drained of strength, collapsed. The head flew through the air, falling beyond the grassland, landing with a heavy sound somewhere out of sight.

Emilia had finished off the madman, Petelgeuse.

It was a "worst" of a different kind from the worst-case scenario Subaru had envisioned, and—

"Sir Subaru. It seems that thing has..."

Confirming the Sin Archbishop's death, Wilhelm's expression relaxed slightly. In the same way, Emilia, while chewing on the regret of what she'd done, showed relief settling into her shoulders as well.

Before the two of them, Subaru lifted his face. And he opened his mouth,

"Both of you, get away from me right now— but too late, yes!!"

Just as he stretched out both hands to shove Wilhelm, standing beside him, his mouth moved on its own, speaking words other than what he intended.

Immediately after, Subaru had the illusion that the ground beneath his own feet was swelling up. No—his shadow had burst open, and countless arms had been born from it.

They rose soundlessly like writhing serpents, targeting Wilhelm close at hand. But the old swordsman escaped it by a hair's breadth—unable to see the threat of the Unseen Hand even so, he caught the warning in Subaru's words just before and instantly spun about, leaping with wind-swift speed to Emilia's side.

Glaring after him in irritation was Subaru—Petelgeuse.

"Worst feeling... yes. Having someone else in your head—is it really this disgusting...!"

He dug his fingers into his own wound, keeping hold of his self even as the pain made his consciousness flicker. There was a terror that if he didn't stay tensed like this, he'd be taken over completely, right now.

The sensation of someone else—and a being to be hated, at that—sitting right beside his own brain with such shameless composure. Following the precedent of last time, he should have seen this coming.

That's exactly why this situation, of all things, needed to be avoided.

"What... Subaru, what's wrong with you?"

"You mustn't, Lady Emilia. Right now, that is Sir Subaru, but not only Sir Subaru. —Sir Subaru! Are you in your right mind!"

Wilhelm called out, holding back with his arm Emilia, who watched Subaru's strange state with concern. Grimacing at this, Subaru answered by stepping backward.

The matter of "possession" had been explained to the entire subjugation force as shared knowledge beforehand. From Subaru's behavior, Wilhelm too must have realized he was locked in a struggle against the madman's consciousness.

Naturally, severity settled over his expression, and the tip of the treasured sword he held turned toward Subaru. He wouldn't cut him down on the spot, but the wariness was real. And that was exactly what Subaru himself had asked of him before the fighting began.

"Glad you kept... your promise, yes, Wilhelm-san."

"I would ask that you not force my hand into cutting you down. ...Is there no way you can fight free of this without being dominated?"

"Who knows, yes... honestly, even now it's touch-and-go whether I'm holding onto my self at all. If I let my guard down for even a second, this thing's liable to... gouge out my own throat, or something."

Both his arms convulsed, and he could feel that the moment his attention slipped, his own fingertips would rise and creep toward his own throat. Left unchecked, they would gouge into his neck with force approaching the human body's limits, ending his life in agony. What a lowlife bastard, always so eager to make people kill themselves, every single time.

"I mean... with anyone else you just possess 'em yourself, don't you—but with me you go straight for making me off myself? What kind of twisted logic is that, yes...!?"

His voice trembled, his fingertips trembled, and his gaze began to lose its focus.

His spirit was being eaten into by Petelgeuse, and even control of his body had gradually begun to slip away. Already, he had no feeling in his toes. Slowly, creeping from one edge to the other, the existence known as Natsuki Subaru was being painted over by the existence known as Petelgeuse.

'It has been quite a while since possessing someone gave me this much trouble, yes.'

"Having a chat with your spiritual form... guess I'm really in the end stage now, huh. Damn it, yes."

A phantom Petelgeuse surfaced in his mind. The image was of the Petelgeuse he'd first seen—a vision that by now should have exploded into fine dust—but that ghostly form circled around and around Subaru, watching him struggle desperately with a gaze that seemed to lick at him.

'Good, good resistance, yes! Good expression, yes! Good effort, yes! A Trial! A trial trial trial trial trial! This is exactly how a Trial ought to be! It's the same for me, for the Witch, and for the half-devil who is nothing more than a Vessel! A Trial! Ahh, a Trial of love!'

"Quit yappin' in my ear, yes, damn it. What Trial... I'll keep you pinned down just like this... like hell anyone's gonna beat me in a battle of raw willpower..."

He put up a brave front, bluffed, whipped up his own resolve.

If he didn't, he felt he might give himself over to this self-destructive impulse right now. Or else reach out an arm from the shadow beneath his feet, on the verge of bursting, and want to trample everything around him into nothing, leaving not even a shape behind.

If this impulse was something Petelgeuse carried with him always, then Subaru could, in a way, understand the madman's every mad and bizarre act up to now.

Starved for destruction to this degree, biting off one's own fingertip would be nothing at all.

Bearing an Authority this difficult to control, it would be no wonder if one's mental balance gave way.

—Was this the world Petelgeuse saw?

'—I am not asking to be understood, yes.'

For the first time, the illusion that should have been nothing but a phantom Petelgeuse spoke coldly to Subaru.

Petelgeuse, who had expressed anger, madness, and manic joy as emotion up until now. For the first time, all color of emotion had drained from his expression.

If anything, this chilled Subaru to the bone more than any attitude he'd shown before, and—

'I do not ask that anyone understand me, yes. Above all, sympathy is nothing less than an insult—a humiliation—a disgrace to the love bestowed upon me, yes! To think of nothing but repaying that affection—to think it, to be conscious of it, to strive toward it! That alone is my salvation, yes!'

"You...!"

Bringing his face close, the illusion of Petelgeuse stuck out his tongue, warding Subaru off.

Revulsion, and some measure of pity, brushed across Subaru's mind, but he cast it aside from his awareness as nothing more than a moment's weakness.

Still holding their ground, madman and ordinary man wrestled for control—and into that,

"—Subaru!" "Subaru-kyun!" "Bro!"

Behind them, having annihilated even the last of the Witch Cultists, Julius came running up, Ferris and Ricardo close behind.

"Subaru!" "Sir Subaru...!" "—"

In front of him, coming down the hill, Emilia and Wilhelm approached, unable to erase their wariness even as it mixed with the worry seeping out from behind it.

His heart pounded, high and hard, its sound ringing out.

His heart raged at the sight of everyone gathered here for him, and above all, Emilia stood right beside him. Subaru's heart, trembling at her nearness, suddenly flared brighter—

"—"

Suddenly, the trembling in Subaru's hands and feet stopped.

The aftershock of his ongoing struggle against something trying to overflow from within him—the very thing that should have been showing itself as the shaking of his limbs—vanished, and he let out one long, long breath.

"Subaru...?"

At Subaru's newly composed manner, Emilia tilted her head.

Timidly, her fingers reached out toward him, moving to touch Subaru's cheek.

Just before those pale fingertips reached his cheek, Subaru's hand rose up and caught hers. Emilia let out a small "ah," and her deep violet eyes, looking at him, met his black ones.

And then Subaru's cheek drew a slow, easy smile,

"—Lia, don't!" "At last, I've caught you—yes."

Puck darted out, raising his voice in warning, but Petelgeuse's grin—splitting his cheeks wide—came faster.

Everyone moved at once to protect Emilia, her arm now seized—but that was stopped as black magic hands rose up and bound every one of them in place.

"Nyah!?"

"The hell is this!? Just like the kid said, ain't it!"

"So this is the Sin Archbishop's... 'Unseen Hand'!"

Bound, their movement sealed, Ferris, Ricardo, and Wilhelm let out groans of pain. Arms rising from the ground gripped their limbs in an iron hold, and all three were forced into a crawling posture, as if lead had been strapped to their hands and feet.

But—

"—Seems there's one here I'm poorly matched against, yes?"

"So it would seem. ...I'll ask that you release everyone, unharmed."

Having leapt back just in the nick of time to dodge the magic hand, Julius readied his sword.

By whatever means, Julius had escaped the Unseen Hand's binding, and Petelgeuse—now holding Subaru's body under his control—glared at him with irritation,

"—A Spiritual Arts User, yes?"

"And if I am, what of it?"

Petelgeuse clicked his tongue, looking thoroughly irritated to his core.

Against this, Julius maintained his unshaken composure, no doubt trying to buy time and work out a way to break the deadlock even by a little. However—

"Stalling for time is useless, yes. The Vessel is already in my hands—"

So saying, Petelgeuse raised Emilia's pale arm, which he still held fast. Held as she was, Emilia, unable to resist, was hoisted up into the air by countless unseen hands, even her mouth sealed shut, her chanting silenced.

Emilia writhed desperately, struggling to break free. But beside her, the great spirit who should have always watched over her forbade himself from lifting so much as a paw,

"Why... why am I even here...? Lia, ah, Lia, I'm sorry. I'm so sorry. Why... if I can't be of any use here, then why did Echidna even...!"

His muttering carried a note of bitter regret, resignation weighing down on his small body.

Catching this, Petelgeuse's movements suddenly stopped. He raised his head, and with a creaking motion slowly looked down at the little cat,

"Don't screw around with me, you bastard...!"

It was righteous fury, a rage he couldn't hold back.

That eruption of anger briefly called back Subaru's consciousness, which should have been driven off into the void.

Even though every will to resist should have already been broken, it was the sight of Puck's spinelessness alone that stirred his consciousness back.

If so, then—

"Trials, Contracts... it's all a load of crap, damn it. So then why are you even... here! Why did you... come all this way..."

"What do you know, Subaru? You don't know anything, do you. Even I couldn't remember it until this very moment. What could you possibly know about the Witch!"

Anger flickered in Puck's eyes as he looked up at Subaru, and the two of them glared at each other.

But after hurling that undying hostility at each other, Subaru's consciousness once again teetered on the verge of being swallowed by the flickering shadow of Petelgeuse.

His consciousness fading, he understood that this time, for real, he would be crushed for good—so,

"—Do it, Julius."

"What are you saying?"

"If this keeps up, I'll be taken over, and it's wipeout for everyone... If it's going to come to that, stop it."

He entrusted his wish to the one man here who could still translate his own will into physical action, sword in hand.

At the very least, to break this deadlock, he wanted Petelgeuse finished off. And if a Vessel was needed for that, he'd take on the role himself.

"No, Subaru. You must think this through. I am a knight, and a Spiritual Arts User. I have made a Contract to cooperate with your objective. To go back on it now would..."

"My objective is saving Emilia... The tally of my own life is a separate matter..."

At Subaru's answer, wrung out from somewhere deep, Julius's face twisted with anguish.

At the first sight of true hardship on the face of Julius—who had always carried himself with grace and never lost his composure—Subaru couldn't hold back a laugh.

His cheeks slackened, and a small breath escaped him.

'A foolish thing, a foolish thing, a foolish thing thing thing thing ing ing ing ing g g g g!'

The phantom Petelgeuse appeared once more, circling around as if to hurl abuse at Subaru for his resolve.

Even the ridiculousness of it stirred up more laughter, and at last a faint chuckle spilled from his throat.

"Subaru, I..."

"Don't you go making the wrong call now, Sir Knight—"

To Julius, who was trying to voice his hesitation, Subaru pushed him on with a trembling voice. But still Julius couldn't bring himself to act.

As if to push him from behind, the one who cut into the conversation everyone was watching with bated breath was—

"—Subaru-kyun's about to be taken over, and Julius can't bring himself to make the call to put him down, nya. So there's just nothing else for it, nya?"

Ferris, both hands bound by the magic hand and forced into a prone position, lifted his head and, from that pained posture, smiled faintly.

Sweat beaded on his forehead, and a single lock of hair fell loose in a way that was beautiful; even knowing he was male, one couldn't help but feel something sensual in the sight of him draped in nothing but a cloth over his shoulders.

And the sight of him smiling, his red lips parting, was precisely fitting of the word "enchantress."

"Wait, Ferris. What are you—"

"I'll make it so there's no going back."

Before Julius, who called out to stop him, Ferris gave a small click of the tongue.

A small sound like a kiss echoed out, and Subaru=Petelgeuse blinked, waiting to see what would happen. But there was no sign of anything happening anywhere—

—and then, in the next instant, Subaru's whole body was instantly seared by the sensation of his blood boiling.

"—Ah, ha!?"

Hot. Hot, hot. Hothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothothot.

His throat was hot, his eyes were hot, his body was hot, his tongue was hot, his nose was hot, his hands were hot, his ears were hot, his legs were hot, his blood was hot, his Soul was hot, his life was hot, hot hot hot.

His blood boiled, his organs simmered, his brain gave off a heat fierce enough to seem it might evaporate, and his vision clouded white.

At this sudden, fatal upheaval that had struck his body, Subaru couldn't even manage to voice words as words.

'Aaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaa—!?'

Just before his eardrums seemed about to burn away, he heard—saw—Petelgeuse tormented by the same agony as himself. Since they shared one body, the suffering Subaru endured was the same suffering that tormented him.

And that meant that he, too, was tasting this same helpless, hopeless ending.

—A feeling of serves-you-right welled up, and dissolved at once before the pain.

He writhed in agony, rolled, convulsed, and became unable to move at all.

When he came to, everyone who should have been bound was free. Of course they were. With the very person who should be wielding the Unseen Hand in this state, who could bind whom?

"Ferris! Why would you...!"

"It's not what he wanted himself, nya. He protected what he wanted to protect—so it's what he wished for... isn't it?"

Subaru no longer had the strength left even to move a single finger to express his agony. He understood only that something had happened, that it had been Ferris who caused it, and that Wilhelm was rounding on Ferris for it.

Emilia crouched down, shaking the shoulder of the dying Subaru.

"Wait... Subaru, this isn't right, this can't be. Please. Wait... no, no, this is wrong. I never wanted you to..."

Her voice was thick with tears. Who was she crying for, he wondered. She was probably crying for someone. She had always been that kind of hopelessly good person, one who did everything for someone else's sake.

He thought that if that someone were him, it might make him happy.

"Ah, hell, I can't watch this."

Ricardo turned his massive frame away.

And stepping forward from beside him was the Royal Guard knight with his purple hair faintly mussed—Julius, rainbow blade in hand, a trace of desolation lingering on his face.

"...It was my own failing that brought about Ferris's act. In time, I will surely answer for this."

Subaru couldn't bring himself to tell him not to worry over something so trivial.

Worry over it plenty, never forget it. If no one remembered how much he'd suffered like this, he'd never rest easy.

—The tip of the rainbow blade was set against the throat of the prone Subaru.

"No... no, Julius. Subaru's still..."

"His life-force has run out of control... This cannot go on any further."

Emilia said something more to Julius, but the knight shook his head and would not listen.

The sound of their exchange grew distant. Farther, and farther, and vanished somewhere.

'This wasn't supposed to happen... I—I was supposed to have received love, affection! The Blessing of the Gospel...!'

—Shut up. Come along with me to hell.

Far away, far away, he fell toward some unknown place, falling, falling.

Ah, was he going to die again? Falling to the bottom of the Abyss, dropping everything he carried, clutching his own failures close, dying wretchedly once more.

He looked back over the world.

So as never to forget the cause—what exactly had gone wrong.

"—Subaru, I'm sorry."

He heard that tear-choked voice, and with the voice of the girl he most wanted to hear as the very last thing, his consciousness sank away.

And then—ah—he understood. This time's failure, and the reason for it.

"I—"

—Subaru should never have made up with Emilia.
