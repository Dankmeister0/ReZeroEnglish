Arc 4 Chapter 85: "With Words, With Feelings, With Fists"

"――Huh?"

For a moment, Subaru couldn't understand what he'd been told, and let out a dumbstruck sound. Eyes wide, mouth hanging open, Subaru stared blankly. Emilia gazed back at him, and as if shaping the thoughts inside her into words, she said,

"I'm really, really happy that you think of me that way, Subaru, and that you'd act on it like that. It makes me feel so, so reassured, and I really do rely on you so much. ...But trying to find an escape route like that — that's not okay."

"N-Not okay, you say, but... to just unilaterally dump this problem on you like this—!"

"I'm the one who decided to face it. There's a place I have to reach, and there's a gate I have to pass through to get there, and right now I have to work hard to get past it. I don't want to make excuses about that."

Lips pressed firmly together, resolve kindling in her eyes, Emilia stood before Subaru, and he was struck with shock. Her resolute face was full of the shine of strong will. There was no trace there of the frail girl who couldn't even walk the road without Subaru reaching out a hand to pull her along. Why, how — as questions filled his chest, Subaru shook his head.

"I think that resolve of yours is admirable, Emilia. But you and that 'Trial' are a terrible match. Going into a fight with no chance of winning — a fight where the odds are... slim — with no plan at all, I don't call that being brave."

"...So the odds really do look slim to you."

"…………"

No words of excuse came to him, and Emilia gave a bitter smile at Subaru's silence. The faint droop at the corners of her eyes was the result of taking his assessment of her plainly, honestly. Unable to even choose the right words to follow up with in the moment, he felt himself a hopelessly lacking person.

"Can't you at least wait until we find some kind of lead? If you'll just give me time, I'll definitely... make things better somehow. Then you could feel safe for good, Emilia—"

"No, that won't do, Subaru. I sort of already know. That the 'Trial' in that tomb — there's no shortcut and no way around it."

"――――"

"It's strange, but I just know it. Even if we take time, if my resolve going in isn't firm, the result will end up the same. I know that."

"Ah..."

No words of denial came to him. He didn't know the details of the Trial's nature, but as far as the rule Emilia had sensed went, Subaru shared her opinion. No matter how many times one challenged it, the content of the Trial, its quality, would neither soften nor grow harsher. The same conditions, the same content, would greet the challenger each time. Leaving the nature of the Trial unchanged, while the outcome shifted according to the challenger's heart alone — that was exactly the sort of thing Echidna would favor.

That the comforting words he'd offered had been seen through, and that Emilia had grasped the essence of the Trial far more than he'd imagined — both were things that struck Subaru with astonishment. And yet, still, Subaru tried desperately to string words together, when—

"Hey, Subaru. ――Why is it that you try to help me?"

"――――"

The question, offered up ahead of him, was one that had been asked before, one that carried great meaning. How desperately Subaru had spent his time in order to be able to answer it. How much hardship he had overcome in order to convey that answer to Emilia. So, to the same question, Subaru could answer without hesitation, clearly.

"The reason I want to help you is because I love you. ――Because I love everything about you."

"――Mm. Yeah, I know. You love me a lot, don't you, Subaru."

"――――"

"That feeling of yours makes me so, so happy. So, so reassured. I rely on it so, so much. Just having you watch over me like that, Subaru — I know I can really give it my all."

Hand to her chest, cheeks faintly reddening, Emilia closed her eyes. With feeling welling up in her, she continued with "So—"

"Don't torment yourself thinking you have to do something. Just you watching over me is enough for me to keep trying hard. If you want to do something, if you'll indulge my selfishness, then I want you to stay by my side. I want you to support my back."

"Emilia..."

"When I feel like I might waver, if there's a hand there supporting my back, I'm sure I can stop myself from falling. In those moments when I sway like that, I want you there beside me, Subaru."

"――――"

"Thank you for always walking ahead of me, moving stones out of the way so I don't trip, smoothing the path, cutting through the brush, trying to take my hand and lead me. But if you keep doing that for me, I think I'll just keep leaning on you more and more. Because deep down, I'm lazy like that."

"L-Lazy... you say?"

He tried to fall into their usual back-and-forth, but the words wouldn't come. Inside Subaru's chest, an emotion swelled that he couldn't control. Something he couldn't put into words, something hard to understand. Just so it wouldn't sweep away all his feelings with the sheer force of its presence, Subaru gritted his back teeth and kept facing Emilia.

"I leaned on you, and leaned on you, and did nothing but lean on you... so this time, I want to try not doing that. The only thing that worries me is that every time I fail, I'll make you and everyone else worry... but I'll make sure that doesn't happen. I'll get through it as soon as I possibly can."

To Subaru, who couldn't find words to follow, Emilia offered a brave smile and said,

"Please just watch over me while I try my hardest like that, right beside me. ――That's the favor I want to ask of you, Subaru."

"――――!!"

He cut through the wind. His legs impatient, his heart driving him on. He ran as if flying across the uneven slope, scratches opening on his cheeks where branches raked across them, stumbling again and again but never stopping, running for as long as his breath held out.

"――――!!"

He raised a voice that wasn't a voice, opening his throat as if he meant to tear it apart, looking up through the gaps in the thick green darkness of night at the sky, staring at the moon and the flickering stars shining an eerie pale blue in the clear air, and screamed. ――He wanted to purge every last bit of foolishness from inside his body that way, wanted to empty himself of everything.

――The smile Emilia had shown him last, the one holding her fierce resolve, was burned into his eyes.

That smile, the resolve she had declared, and Subaru's own misunderstanding. Only when those combined did he finally understand the true nature of the impulse burning up from inside him, scorching his chest. And it was precisely because he understood the nature of that impulse that Subaru, unable to sit still, had flung himself into the forest the moment he parted from Emilia, tearing around it like a beast.

Not letting him stop, not even letting him flee into sleep — the raging emotion that felt like it would burst into flame every time he thought of Emilia — people call this feeling "shame." Shame had taken command of Subaru's entire body, refusing to let him stand still.

"I'm... I'm... !"

He was too stupid. Truly, truly, hopelessly foolish, beyond saving. When Roswaal had looked down on Emilia and called her "that thing," Subaru had flown into a rage and lashed out at him. He'd raised his voice, bared his fangs, refusing to allow words and attitudes that insulted and belittled her. Right after that exchange, he'd met with Emilia, poured out to her all the feelings of what he wanted to do for her, and been refused — and only then had he realized.

――It was Subaru, more than anyone, who had trusted least in Emilia's resolve, her determination, her strength.

Telling himself he had to protect her. Telling himself he didn't want her to suffer pain or sorrow. Subaru had held up words like those and racked his brain over how to keep Emilia away from hardship. He'd tried to take the Trial on in her place, and when that proved impossible, he'd searched for some side path that would let her avoid taking the Trial at all, and when even that seemed too uncertain — at worst, he'd thought, if he could just somehow deal with the Great Rabbit, the very reason for the time limit forcing them out of the Sanctuary — from every possible angle, all he'd been racking his brain over was how to keep Emilia from having to face the Trial at all.

And all the while Subaru had been scheming to protect Emilia out of nothing but his own self-satisfied, presumptuous urge to shelter her, Emilia had spent a night alone hardening her own resolve, her own determination, in her own way, and had decided to face the Trial head-on without running from it. And what she'd wanted was for Subaru to support her in that resolve.

None other than Natsuki Subaru himself had underestimated the girl named Emilia more than anyone.

"――――!"

The instant he realized that, shame he couldn't bear struck him like a blow to the head. To Emilia, who had asked for his answer, he'd given a response that couldn't be reconciled, and then, before her worried face, he'd raised a hand and fled the scene as if running away. And with that same stride he'd fled into the forest, and ended up like this.

Once before, in the royal capital, Subaru had hurt Emilia with the same kind of self-righteousness. Not knowing her thoughts or her resolve, growing arrogant with the Authority he'd just gained, never conveying to her the true meaning behind his selfish behavior — that had created a rift between Subaru and Emilia. Because of what happened then, he had established how he felt about her and how he wanted to show those feelings, and that was why he was able to stand here now, in this place.

――And yet, once again, Subaru had gotten it wrong.

Getting hurt in Emilia's place, taking her hardships onto himself, carving out a path for her. The way he went about it — not boasting of it to Emilia, not flaunting it to others — looked, compared to before, like some measure of progress. But in truth, nothing about its essential nature had changed at all.

He'd only gotten better at hiding the fact that he was getting hurt. His conceited habit of talking proudly about his wounds had merely gone quiet. The way he pushed his own self-centered notions onto Emilia while insisting he was right — that hadn't changed one bit.

"I'm... I—!"

His breath ran ragged, and the instant he lifted his gasping face, a thick branch struck him across the forehead. As he reeled back from the pain, the ground gave way beneath his feet and he clawed at empty air. He toppled sideways, rolling across ground caked with dirt and fallen leaves, and ended up sprawled spread-eagle on the earth.

His back against the cold ground that seemed to drain away all his heat, he lay there panting hard, staring straight up at the sky. Through a gap where the trees opened up, the night sky showed through. In a world with no streetlights, the stars burned fiercely in air so clear it seemed to prick the skin. Watched over by a sky full of stars, surrounded by unfamiliar constellations, Subaru felt himself dissolve into a whirlpool of tangled feelings — his own smallness, an anxiety with no visible end, a fear with all-too-tangible shape.

Exhaustion came crashing over him all at once, and he couldn't hold onto consciousness. It had been an overwhelming stretch of time. Not just the fatigue accumulated in his body, but layer upon layer of exhaustion piled up in his mind, dragging Subaru down into darkness.

Return by Death. The Witch's tea party. Roswaal's true intentions. And his own arrogance, and Emilia, resolved to stand alone. Amid all these thoughts, what was Subaru himself supposed to do to find the answer—

"Well ain't that just the-e-e life of luxury."

The chill in the air and a listless voice were the first things Subaru registered as he woke from sleep. His eyelids trembling against the cold sunlight, Subaru opened his eyes and grimaced. He'd looked straight at the sun peeking through the trees, and with tears welling in his eyes, tried to sit up—

"Guh, ah, ow...!"

A creaking sound like stiff joints grinding, and Subaru groaned at the pain. His body, having lain on the hard ground, had gone stiff as stone from the cold of the earth and air, and every time he moved to loosen a joint, a dull ache complained back at him.

"Dunno what the point was s'posed t'be, but sleepin' out somewhere with no roof ain't somethin' I'd recommend. 'Long as ya got a roof, a floor, and some gowlan, ya can get by,' or so they say, right?"

"Gowlan... no, wait, more importantly—"

Shaking his head, Subaru looked up at the source of the rough voice tossed his way — Garfiel, standing over him where he sat on the ground, bared canines clacking together. At the same moment, full wakefulness returned, and Subaru remembered just what situation he was in.

"That's right... I fell asleep like that yesterday and just..."

"Was doin' my mornin' rounds and caught yer stink comin' from the forest — had me wonderin' what the hell happened. Ya were sprawled out spread-eagle, so I figured maybe somebody got trigger-happy and jumped ya in the dark."

"If the prime suspect — you — didn't do it, nobody would. ...What time is it about now?"

Hand to his forehead, shaking his slightly heavy head, Subaru asked Garfiel. Garfiel snorted through his nose and said,

"No need t'panic — it's before breakfast, that much's for sure. Only folks up right now'd be the early-risin' old geezers and grannies, and the likes of us."

"Then it's not turned into some kind of uproar about me not coming back, has it. ...Before that happens, I need to get back to the cathedral or it'll be a hassle... no, right now, more than that—"

If Subaru hadn't returned during the night, there was a chance that Otto, who'd gone back ahead of him, would grow suspicious and raise a fuss. Even if that itself wasn't a problem, he wanted to avoid spreading anxiety among the evacuees from Arlam Village. With problems already piling up, bringing discord on top of it through his own carelessness was an absolute taboo he must not commit.

"...Yer face is lookin' pretty damn different from last night, I'll say that much."

"Huh?"

Clinging to a nearby tree to pull himself up, turning his neck, Subaru heard Garfiel speak up. He looked back to find Garfiel scratching roughly at his own short blond hair.

"Different from yesterday, when I couldn't tell if ya had it together or not — now ya got some kinda... I dunno, ya look refreshed or somethin'."

"――――"

"Tch, that ain't quite it either. Damn, can't put it right... oi, what're ya laughin' at?"

"Ku, hahaha..."

At Garfiel's observation, Subaru raised a hand to his own cheek. Feeling the corner of his mouth loosen slightly, he let out a trembling laugh from deep in his throat. Low and halting at first, it gradually grew louder.

"Ha, hah! Me, refreshed! That's rich, so that's how it looks, huh."

"The hell! What's so damn funny—"

"It's the opposite, Garfiel. Completely, totally the opposite."

"Ah?"

Choking down the urge to laugh, Subaru pointed a finger at Garfiel. And then—

"I'm not refreshed at all. Inside I'm packed so tight I'm about to burst, honestly. Everything I tried to do got shot down, everything I forced myself to push through backfired... I genuinely, honestly don't know what the hell I'm supposed to do."

"――――"

"When you really, truly hit a dead end, you end up laughing about it instead. If everything I thought I could do to fix things turns out to be no good... then I guess it's back to square one, huh."

He muttered it weakly, shoulders slumping. If he'd been wrong from the very starting point, then everything he'd built on top of that was wrong too. With the clock ticking down toward the deadline he needed to break through the problem before, it was the sinking feeling of realizing, far too late, that he'd gotten the equation wrong from the start. And worse still, the problem he was facing again now looked like the kind he hadn't even been taught how to solve yet.

Garfiel wrinkled his nose, seemingly at a loss for what to say to the deflated-looking Subaru. Since he knew asking Garfiel wouldn't get him a clear answer anyway, it was nothing more than idle grumbling on his part. An awkward silence fell between them — and it was then.

"――Want me to tell you what you should do?"

"――――!"

Startled by a voice suddenly falling from above, Subaru spun around in a hurry. That there was no surprise in Garfiel's expression as he looked up in the same direction was probably because he'd sensed the presence coming.

Following his gaze back, weaving between the gaps in the trees and walking toward them was—

"...Otto?"

"Yes, hello, good morning. That's right, it's me."

There came the dry crack of a branch snapping underfoot, and Otto, wearing a somewhat too-innocent smile, walked closer. Subaru blinked in confusion at his sudden appearance, but Garfiel clicked his tongue softly and said,

"Just so ya know, I only just found him myself. Ain't like I forgot about the guy and got caught up talkin'."

"I wasn't doubting that at all. And I'm glad to see you were found in one piece too, Natsuki-san. ――On top of that, mind if I ask you a favor?"

"...Go on."

"Would you mind leaving me alone with Natsuki-san for a bit? There's something I want to talk to him about."

Before Subaru, still unable to grasp the situation, Otto and Garfiel exchanged words with an air of mutual understanding. At Otto's final request, Garfiel ground his teeth, then shot a brief glance Subaru's way before saying,

"Don't go tryin' anything weird."

And with that, he left it at that and put distance between himself and the scene. Treading through the grass, passing out of the forest and back toward the Sanctuary, Garfiel's retreating back left Subaru behind, feeling somewhat abandoned. Wetting his dry lips with his tongue, Subaru said,

"When did you and Garfiel start getting along so well without me noticing?"

"While you've been running around in a panic, Natsuki-san, it's not like I've been doing nothing either. I've been building up a fair amount of rapport with the people here, and the evacuees too... well, that's beside the point right now."

Answering Subaru's question dutifully, Otto cut off the conversation with a gesture and fixed his eyes squarely on Subaru. No — "gazed" wasn't quite the word for the intensity behind those eyes. It was clearly a look that had crossed over into a glare.

"What..."

Uncomfortable under that stare, Subaru let his voice trail off weakly. Hearing that, Otto let out a small sigh and said,

"I overheard, vaguely, what was going on. Seems like you're pretty cornered, Natsuki-san — by a lot of things."

"――――"

"I'm not privy to the details, of course, so I don't really understand them. But you're stuck, aren't you? I could hear you practically whining about what you're supposed to do."

"So what if I am. ...You said something about knowing what I should do, didn't you."

Listening to Otto's somewhat cutting tone, Subaru brought up what he'd first said when he'd cut in. Otto had indeed said exactly that when he'd interrupted the silence between Subaru and Garfiel. Just what did those words really mean?

"You're saying you know what I should do..."

"Yes, I know. It's simple."

"Simple... you say?"

"Do you want to know?"

Something about the way he said it struck a nerve. Subaru had been agonizing and suffering this much, exposing himself at his most pathetic. And this was the kind of thing you said to a person in that state — nothing but grating on his nerves.

"O-Of course I want to know, obviously! Don't screw around! If there's something you know, then—"

"In that case, get ready."

"G-Get ready...?"

"Yes. First, slowly, take a deep breath..."

Reaching a hand out toward him, Otto added gestures as he instructed Subaru to breathe deeply. Not understanding the point of it but following the instruction anyway, Subaru steadied his breath, closed his eyes, and expanded his lungs—

"――――!?"

The next instant, a sharp impact struck him across the side of the face, sending Subaru sprawling to the ground. Unable to even brace himself, he went down hard, face-first into the dirt, his head spinning. Shaking his head, looking around to figure out what had happened, he caught sight of Otto with his fist still raised from the swing, and realized he'd been punched.

And then, before Subaru, who could only hold his breath, Otto — his reddening fist clenched — said:

"Quit trying to look cool in front of your friends, Natsuki Subaru."
