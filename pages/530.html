Arc 7 Chapter 32: "The Path Forward"

"How much time do you intend to spend fetching back one lone fool? Do you imagine the value of time passing for you and for me is the same?"

"H-hey, I already said I was sorry, didn't I……"

Met by his mistress's icy stare, Al lowered his head with a suitably chastened air. The moment he'd brought Subaru back to the meeting hall, this was the scolding that had been waiting for him. Since the whole point had been to bring Subaru back to the conference in the first place, it was no wonder he was being scolded for the unscheduled detour of talking things out. No wonder at all, but even so—

"Priscilla, don't be so hard on Al. It's not his fault. It's mine."

"Hmph. No doubt the two of you clowns were nursing some tedious anguish and consoling one another. That redness on your brow—did you strike it against a wall?"

"Do you have 'Clairvoyance' or something? That's scary accurate."

Priscilla struck down Subaru, who'd stepped in to defend Al, with a single line delivered as though she'd witnessed it firsthand. It wasn't as though the scene had been so commonplace that anyone could simply picture it, and yet Subaru couldn't help but shudder at Priscilla's powers of insight. In any case—

"With us gone, did the talks fail to move forward, or something……"

"A conclusion is not so easily reached. After all, you are Abel's strategist, are you not?"

"That's……"

Pierced by Priscilla's narrowed gaze, Subaru shot a glare over at Abel. In response to that look, Abel, seated with his arms crossed, silently ignored Subaru's accusatory stare. Treating Subaru as a strategist was surely not to Abel's liking either, but having told Priscilla so directly himself, he could hardly retract it now. The result being, the meeting couldn't be brought to a close without Subaru present.

"You know, don't you ever act without thinking and end up strangling yourself with it?"

"You would do well to mind the quality of your own utterances. Even the finest stratagem may be treated as either wise counsel or foolish scheme, depending on how the words are chosen."

"You sure keep bringing it up for someone who regrets calling me a strategist……"

Subaru grimaced at Abel's brazen retort and let out a deep sigh. Then, once more, he swept his gaze over the assembled faces in the meeting hall—Abel and Priscilla, and then Zikr, and Taritta, who'd been left to take over as chief—and closed one eye. It seemed the discussion hadn't been able to reach a conclusion without Subaru and Al present, but—

"The conclusion you couldn't reach—is that the matter of the cooperative arrangement with Priscilla? From what I heard earlier, Priscilla has a collaborator."

"So you have enough of a head on you to follow the conversation. Indeed, that is the matter. —You do recall that whoever claims the greater number of the Nine Divine Generals wins?"

"Yeah. Up to the part where the 'Ichi' is useless but still a pain in the ass."

It was a matter concerning an opponent who, if left unrecovered, would become an unmanageable assassin, but who, even if recovered, wouldn't contribute much in terms of turning the tide. If this were a simulation game, it'd be a troublesome unit. An ally with little to gain from, and a foe who, left alone, turned into a lethal poison.

"Still, that's talk about the final path to victory. It's not about whether Priscilla and her collaborator will lend support or whatever. Right?"

"—Hmph."

Priscilla gave a small hum in her throat at Subaru, who'd tidily summed up the discussion. For a moment, something crossed her crimson eyes that struck Subaru as unusually curious—a reaction as though she'd taken an interest in someone else, and, of all people, in him. Subaru hadn't thought he'd done anything special.

"What."

"Nothing. Only that, dressed as you are, I had thought you looked withered of face—and yet you've returned with a changed expression and a changed color in your eyes. What's more, if the circulation of your blood has improved even somewhat, that alters how you appear, if only a little."

"……Was that a compliment?"

"You may leap for joy if you wish."

"It's not that big a deal."

Since she hadn't denied it, it was undoubtedly praise, after Priscilla's own fashion. Snorting in response, Subaru nonetheless accepted the acknowledgment of the change in his own bearing with quiet grace.

Rem's own mouth had denied the words that had once been Natsuki Subaru's support. And yet, precisely by being scolded and spurred on—told this was no time to be brought to his knees by that—

"Well, if that's the case, then rushing back late to the meeting was worth it."

"I see. The fruit of a heart-to-heart between clowns, then. Remarkably devoted of you, quite unlike yourself, Al."

"Hey now, Princess, don't go accusing a nice, good-natured guy like me of acting out of character just for being kind to somebody. That's outrageous."

Teased by Priscilla, Al answered with a shrug of his shoulders. Which of the two—Priscilla's words or Al's—was correct, Subaru couldn't judge. To Subaru, Al was the only person from his own world, and also the one who'd slapped him on the back and driven him out of the dead-end alley of being surrounded on all sides. It was true that Al had an image of being somewhat coldhearted in other respects—saying harsh things, treating every situation as somebody else's problem.

"Even that, I can forgive if I chalk it up to the aftereffects of spending some brutal time in another world."

"Something about that feels like it's kind of a low blow, doesn't it? Or is that just me?"

Al tilted his head, looking unconvinced, but Subaru let that pass without comment. What mattered wasn't that Priscilla had reassessed Subaru, nor the fact that Al had contributed to that shift in her feelings.

"What's needed is a resolution to the debate……what Priscilla intends to do about her stance toward us going forward. Enough with the tangents already."

"Ah, my bad. My bad for being the kind of guy who's easily led off on tangents, even while playing strategist."

"――――"

Ignoring the sarcasm shot back at him, Subaru stuck out his tongue and let it slide as Abel's eyes narrowed in displeasure. Then he turned back to face Priscilla properly, prompting her to continue where the earlier discussion had left off.

"So, what's it going to be. Whether or not Priscilla and her collaborator lend support, Abel's going to storm the capital regardless……"

"Whether or not the Princess's backing is there sure changes just how much of a storming it'll be, though."

"How splendidly in step, you two clowns are. In any case, your assessment is correct. Therefore, going forward, in offering our support to you all……to Abel, we have set the condition that you demonstrate the worth of doing so."

"Condition?"

"A simple matter. —Bring one of the Nine Divine Generals over to your side, and show it done."

To Subaru, who furrowed his brow, Priscilla laid out the condition as though it were nothing at all. Hearing the condition thus pronounced, Subaru murmured "The Nine Divine Generals……" under his breath.

It wasn't an unreasonable condition, not by any means. To begin with, any challenge for the recovery of the throne presupposed acquiring a majority of the "Nine Divine Generals." And Priscilla was asking for just that first step. If anything, wasn't it a rather sweet deal, that merely fulfilling a prerequisite necessary for victory would also win Priscilla over as an ally——

"That's not exactly a condition to jump for joy over, is it, though."

"Naturally. With Goz's safety unknown, there is no Divine General who would follow me unconditionally. And there are not so many kabukimono eager to climb aboard a losing battle for the fun of it."

"……So unless someone can see even a sliver of a chance at victory, no one's coming aboard, huh."

At Abel's recitation of the grim state of affairs, Subaru too knit his brow into a difficult expression. Abel's own bleak self-assessment—if the emperor's authority carried no weight whatsoever with the Nine Divine Generals, then support from Priscilla and her collaborator was something to be desperately wanted. If nothing else, if that collaborator existed, it could have served as leverage for persuading a Divine General. At present, the only cards Abel held were the bold claim of "I am the true emperor" and the combined strength of the People of Shudrak and the fortress city of Guaral—that was about it.

"Saying you're fighting the Empire with a town and one tribe's worth of manpower—whether anyone would even listen is the question."

"One who cooperates with Priscilla……were they of the rank of upper count or higher, their name alone might have served as leverage."

"I'll have you know, do not expect mercy from me. I possess generosity, yes, but it is not the sort dispensed to beggars."

Resting her cheek on her hand, Priscilla trampled that faint hope underfoot without ceremony. It was something he'd known already, but there was no chance of her reversing her stance. In that case, all that remained was to tackle the condition she'd presented head-on.

"With respect, Your Excellency, might we not rely on General Cecilus after all?"

"Zikr, do you have some chance of success in mind?"

Into the heavy silence threatening to settle, Zikr's words drew everyone's hopeful attention. But to Subaru's expectant gaze, Zikr shook his head and said "No,"

"As His Excellency stated a moment ago, given the current balance of forces, anyone who would consider cooperating with us could hardly be called of sound mind. And so……"

"And so?"

"I fear we may have no one to rely on but someone of unsound mind, like General Cecilus……"

"Ah, so Zikr-san also thinks that guy's got a screw loose."

Cecilus's reputation consisted of nothing but scathing evaluations, and the fact that even Zikr had reached the conclusion that his very unsoundness of mind might be the key to winning him over spoke to how thoroughly ingrained that view was. Still, if there truly was no other option, then perhaps it was necessary to go after the troublesome unit they'd eventually have to draw to their side anyway, and do it first.

"The question is where he even is, right? Where does this guy normally live?"

"That one presently resides at Arakiya's house in the capital."

"I see, Arakiya's……wait, why?"

Said so nonchalantly, Subaru's understanding lagged behind Abel's words by several seconds. At the sheer absurdity of the content, even Priscilla looked displeased, her brow furrowing. From what he'd heard, "Ichi" was Cecilus and "Ni" was Arakiya, but——

"You mean, this Cecilus guy and Arakiya are, like, lovers or something?"

If so, the impression Subaru's group had left, having driven off Arakiya, would be worse than terrible. Subaru's group would have splendidly made enemies of both "Ichi" and "Ni." But to Subaru's question, Abel shook his head and said, "No."

"That is not it. Arakiya watches for the chance to kill Cecilus. But should she attempt his life and inflict damage on those around them each time, it would be intolerable. Therefore, I ordered Cecilus."

"Ordered him to do what?"

"If he must live somewhere, to live where Arakiya could easily strike."

"……I see?"

Even given a concrete explanation, the relationship was difficult for Subaru to grasp. He couldn't fathom the mentality that would let one's own generals try to kill each other unchecked, nor could he understand the mindset of obediently going to live in the house of the very person trying to kill you. To begin with——

"How do you even manage to live with someone who's trying to kill you……"

Having said that, a nagging sense that he'd said something odd sprouted somewhere inside him, but unable to find the source of that discomfort, Subaru quickly forgot the question. In any case, it was now established that Cecilus normally lived in the capital.

"So then, to win Cecilus over we'd have to go to the capital……wait, is it even okay for you to just waltz into the capital?"

"There is no chance of that. As things stand, my approaching the capital is tantamount to walking to my own execution by fire. And even going so far, whether Cecilus is even in the capital is unknown."

"Then we're stuck, aren't we……"

When it came to matters of the Vollachian Empire, especially military affairs, Subaru's knowledge was useless. Still, given that Zikr, an officer himself, had been the first to propose it, it could be assumed that it had been the best option available under current circumstances. With that proposal now rejected, the road ahead for Abel looked bleak.

"……And here we are, stuck marking time in a place like this, when there's no time for that."

Something dark and heavy began asserting itself deep in Subaru's chest. Bitter as sediment, it was a lump of helplessness that had refused to disappear no matter how hard he'd struck his head against the wall—something like an aftereffect of having been given up on by Rem.

Because of this thing, Subaru had lost Rem's trust. He had to remove this sediment as quickly as possible and win back her trust. To that end, every second spent marking time was precious.

"—There is a way."

But just as Subaru gritted his teeth in frustration, a single line from Abel stopped him short. Subaru, lifting his head as though struck, found Abel turning to face him, one eye still closed.

"Drop that tiresome, wretched face of yours. There is a way."

"Sorry, but with the character creation you get without paying real money, my face was the best I could manage. Never mind the rich-or-poor of my face. What's this 'way'?"

"It is Zikr's proposal. A portion of it, I shall adopt."

"A portion of my proposal? An honor, but that is……?"

Furrowing his thick brows, Zikr looked bewildered at hearing his own opinion had been adopted. Subaru felt the same bewilderment as Zikr. To begin with, Zikr's proposal had been to recruit "Ichi," Cecilus. Since going to the capital was impossible, that proposal ought to have been rejected outright—there seemed to be no usable part left over.

As Subaru pondered this, Abel gave a "Hmph" through his nose, and said,

"There is no chance to speak with that fellow Cecilus. But Zikr also said this—that if she is not of sound mind, there is a chance."

"—?! Surely not……Your Excellency, that is dangerous! Please reconsider!!"

"Huh? Huh? Huh?"

At Abel's words, stated so boldly, Zikr, his color drained, pressed back at him urgently. Blinking in bewilderment at Zikr's vehemence, Subaru was startled by what on earth was happening. Zikr seemed to have someone specific in mind, but Subaru couldn't place it at all. Judging from the flow of the conversation, it seemed they were about to approach someone of unsound mind, but——

"Is there someone crazier than that unpopular General Cecilus?"

"I do not say she is crazy! But that person is far too dangerous……!"

"……Well, I get the story about the Afro general, but from the flow of the conversation, isn't she also one of the Nine Divine Generals? If we cut her out too, aren't we down to zero candidates we could even rely on? I don't think this is a situation where we can afford to be picky."

"That is……true, but……"

Addressed from both sides by Subaru and Al, Zikr fell into a sour silence. Seeing the good-natured Zikr suffer so, Subaru felt as though he and Al were the ones doing something wrong. Or rather, the real problem lay with Abel's personnel management, appointing to key positions people who caused Zikr such anguish. It was a textbook example of the tragedy born from staffing based on ability alone.

"On that point, Lugunica really did run pretty smoothly……basically, I've never run into a nasty higher-up……oh wait, there was that old guy who badmouthed Emilia-tan."

Even that, now that he thought of it, was in part a remark born of excessive fear of the "Witch of Envy." At the time, Subaru, ignorant of such circumstances and armed only with recklessness, had been a foolish Valiant charging in. Now, encountering the same situation, he'd surely be able to face it with far better equipment. Of course, "not challenging someone for badmouthing Emilia" was never an option to begin with.

"So, who exactly is the person causing Zikr-san all this grief?"

"What is that idiotic manner of phrasing?"

To Subaru's probing, Abel responded with a look of displeasure,

"To answer in keeping with your manner of phrasing—that would be Yorna Mishigure, based out of the demon city of Chaosflame."

"Yorna Mishigure……"

Unfamiliar as the city's name was, the person's name rang a bell. Yorna Mishigure was one of the "Nine Divine Generals" listed off earlier, and her epithet was, if he recalled——

"'Prismatic,' or something like that, wasn't it?"

"Not bad, brother. Impressive that you remembered it in one go."

"I like memorizing the epithets of boss-tier characters in manga and stuff. Anyway, so this Yorna person is Zikr-san's……"

As he spoke, Subaru glanced over at Zikr and lost his words. Because Zikr, his face pale, was covering it with his own hands.

"The 'Shichi' of the Nine Divine Generals, General Yorna Mishigure……"

"Is she really that dangerous a person? From the name, I figured she'd be kind of feminine, but……"

If that impression was correct, then she would be a woman feared even by Zikr—known by the epithet "Ladies' Man," a man who'd behaved gentlemanly even toward Subaru while he was merely cross-dressing. What sort of shady character was about to come out of the woodwork, Subaru couldn't even imagine, but——

"She is a beautiful woman. On that point, not only I, but everyone would agree, I'm sure. But with General Yorna, there is somewhat of a……no, 'somewhat' does not begin to cover the problem."

"So, what exactly is this 'problem' you're talking about?"

"—Treason."

"「Huh?」"

Subaru and Al's voices overlapped at the word that slipped, quietly, from Zikr's mouth. Stunned, doubting his own ears as though he'd misheard, Subaru watched as Zikr, still covering his face with his hands, continued in a trembling voice.

"General Yorna Mishigure is a traitor who has raised rebellion time and again, threatening the reign of Emperor Vincent Vollachia."

"Don't put someone like that in a general's position!!"

And so, Subaru's second outburst of the day rang out through the meeting hall.

△▼△▼△▼△

"—It seems the discussion has been settled."

Some while after the outrageous information about the "Nine Divine Generals" and Subaru's furious shout had echoed through the room, Priscilla, judging that the talks had reached their conclusion, snapped her fan open with a click. For Subaru, honestly, it was a conclusion he had plenty to object to, but——

"There is no other way. As things stand, the only Divine General with any possibility of coming to our side is Yorna Mishigure alone."

"Wasn't it because everyone hates you in the first place that she keeps rebelling……?"

"No, in fact, one cannot say that so simply……reading that person's mind is, at least for someone like myself, impossible."

"Well, if Zikr-san says so, I guess that's how it is……"

Hearing it from the trustworthy Zikr, Subaru had no choice but to withdraw his doubts. Hearing this, Abel snorted in displeasure and said,

"You. What is the meaning of so readily accepting a thing merely because it is Zikr's opinion?"

"Even with the same opinion, who says it matters, doesn't it? You think my trust in you is somehow greater than my trust in Zikr-san?"

"I see. But what if Zikr were to die?"

"I'll kill you even as a thought experiment!"

Subaru hurled abuse at Abel for bringing up something so ominous. Given Zikr's loyalty, if ordered to by Abel, he might genuinely accept taking his own life. To prevent even the slightest chance of such a thing occurring, Subaru was putting everything into it. In any case——

"Setting aside the stupid talk, this Chaosflame place……was that it? Where is it? Is it far from Guaral?"

"Not so far, positionally speaking. That, too, is one reason it is a suitable next destination. Southeast from here——it lies south of the Buddheim Jungle."

"I see, that's true……"

Shown the location on the map on the table, Subaru found himself convinced by Abel's explanation. Chaosflame's location was south of the forest where the People of Shudrak had lived—farther in distance than the jungle and Guaral, but far more realistic than the capital, or lands further west still.

"So then, why's it called a demon city, anyway?"

"Rest easy, fool. It is no reason that would crush your faint heart. That land has, since ancient times, been a city where many races dwell in chaotic mixture. To begin with, Vollachia, even compared to Lugunica, is home to a diverse array of races, but Chaosflame is mingled together especially motley."

"Chaos……so that's the 'chaos' in Chaosflame."

Come to think of it, the fact that a city called "chaos" carried the word "chaos" in it was also suggestive. Being another world, it was probably just a coincidence, though.

"—At the demon city, we shall bring Yorna Mishigure, 'Shichi' of the Nine Divine Generals, under our banner. Should that be achieved, you will open your heart to us as well……correct, Priscilla?"

"That will suffice. What, I am generous. I do not tack on conditions after the fact."

"I feel like you said something about generosity being sold out a minute ago……no, never mind."

Glared at sharply by Priscilla, Subaru immediately withdrew his unnecessary remark. And so, with the course of action properly decided upon——

"—Abel, please, won't you take me along as well?"

"Taritta-san?"

Abruptly, Taritta rose to her feet and pleaded so to Abel. During the meeting, Taritta had sat in Kuna's place, but she had not once opened her mouth until now. There had likely been confusion at suddenly having to succeed as chief, but even now, the expression with which she gazed at Abel carried a thick air of desperation. Receiving her plea, Abel narrowed his black eyes and said,

"What do you intend? You are meant to have inherited the seat of chief of the Shudrak from your sister. Regardless of your own confidence in the matter, that shall not waver."

"I underztand that. The pozition my zizter passed to me, I cannot refuse it……But, with my current zelf, I lack the ztrength to lead my people, the Shudrak……"

Looking down, Taritta bit her lip hard and stared at her own hands. In her empty hands, what she clutched was, most likely, the same thing Subaru held clenched in his own chest—namely, a helplessness that tormented herself.

"I need……a chanze, zomething that lets me carry the pozition of chief with pride."

"—. And you seek that in the journey to Chaosflame?"

"Yez, that'z right……Iz that……not allowed?"

Having said it, she seemed to lose confidence, her voice draining of strength. But Subaru understood well what unease Taritta was carrying, and its true nature.

The sudden change of chieftainship had left Taritta saddled with a heavy role she wasn't confident she had the strength to match. In other words, what she was seeking was a success experience. A result achieved by her own strength, sufficient to convince herself—a success experience that would let her acknowledge herself.

——That was, at its root, the very same thing Subaru himself was seeking now.

"Abel, I'm for it. Taking over from Mizelda-san with that kind of mindset—I don't think Taritta-san would be able to bring out her full strength either."

"――――"

"Besides, either way, we're going to need an escort or allies along the way, right? We already know Taritta-san's ability firsthand. She's the comrade who cross-dressed with us……who charged into the city with us."

"Natsumi……"

He'd switched what he was going to say partway through, but the point stood. Taritta looked deeply moved at Subaru taking her side like that. Her hardship, he understood all too well. He wanted to back her up.

"If you leave, what becomes of the Shudrak?"

"For a while, I'll leave things in my zizter's hands az acting chief. Kuna and Holly will azzist her well. To protect Guaral, we can't move a large force anyway."

"You have thought that far ahead during your silence, then."

Hearing Taritta's unfaltering answer, Abel gave her thinking his approval. Then, tapping his own temple lightly with a finger, he said,

"Those heading to Chaosflame shall be few in number. Naturally, since we are to meet Yorna Mishigure, my presence is indispensable. But we are not marching in to attack."

"What we can bring along is, at most, an escort……"

"To that, add you, Taritta. As for the rest——"

"—I've been listening in on all this!!"

In the midst of Abel's speech, the door was flung open with force, and a figure strode in with bold confidence. Utterly unafraid of the discourtesy of interrupting an emperor's words was Flop O'Connell, the good-natured merchant who had, without the slightest hesitation, declared that very emperor his new friend.

Flop, nostrils flaring, monopolizing the attention of everyone in the room, declared,

"Miss Taritta, the resolve to volunteer yourself for such a difficult role—I was deeply moved! Finding your own way to approach a sudden, heavy responsibility……I think that's just wonderful!"

"Th-thank you, Flop……"

"To protect the safety of your journey, yours and the village chief's, I'd like to propose something. —I think it'd be best to bring my sister, Medium, along!"

Flop declared this forcefully, snapping up a finger with a sharp gesture. Carried along by his momentum, Subaru nearly found himself nodding along—but examined properly, it was a rather sudden proposal.

"A-and the reasoning behind that is?"

"Mm-hm, you'd wonder, wouldn't you. Well then, let me list the reasons I recommend Medium! First, she's skilled with a blade. Second, she's got charm. And on top of that, she's got great enunciation!"

"Enunciation……!"

"She speaks so crisply and cheerfully, the journey's chatter is sure to never grow lonely. And she's not shy around strangers either, so she can get along with anyone. So, what do you think? A gem of a candidate, right?"

Flop praised his sister, flashing his white teeth. However, of his confident selling points, two-thirds concerned Medium's likable personality, leaving only "she's skilled with a blade" as the sole practical selling point. In fact, Subaru had confirmed firsthand that this one point functioned properly, but——

"But, uh, did you actually run this by Medium-san?"

"Nope, haven't yet! But I'll tell her from here on, so it'll be fine!"

"……I-is that really going to be fine?"

This wasn't a matter of consulting about a trip or making plans to hang out. Being thrown into a life-or-death mission, only to be consulted about it afterward—would that really work out? If this caused a rift between the siblings, Subaru would feel awfully bad about it. Though, admittedly, he could also picture the siblings just laughing it off and accepting it—which was scary in its own way.

"Suppose Medium-san says it's fine—Abel, what about you?"

"—. I regard her skill as suitable. If she can fulfill the role, I have no objection."

"Then you can rest easy! Basically, my sister throws herself full force into whatever she's told to do! But, careful—she doesn't think much about it unless she's told, so watch out for that, hoh!"

Hands on his hips, Flop laughed heartily, "Ha ha ha!" He must have sensed it from his own words, and from Abel's demeanor—that this time, Flop himself had not been included among those accompanying them to Chaosflame.

"……You don't have to come along either, you know. Same goes for Medium-san."

"Don't be absurd, good sir. I have a goal. A vengeance I must carry out, must see through."

"……Yeah."

The ominous word that spilled from Flop's mouth was, Subaru knew, never an ominous notion at all, but was directed at the irrationality and unreasonableness that even this cheerful Flop could not forgive. In this world, the goodness of Flop that Subaru had never gotten to hear about—that alone was the reason Subaru trusted him, and had been able to spend his days with him up until now.

"My sister's the same way. Her purpose and mine, the path we walk—it's the same. If we abandoned you, your wife, and the village chief here, I could never hold my head up again."

"—. That's almost enough to make me cry. I might fall for you."

"Ha ha ha, coming from you dressed like that, good sir, that really does make my heart skip a beat! But that'd be no good, wouldn't want to upset your wife. Still, I'll take the sentiment!"

Even the way he deflected it was cool, and Subaru could only be moved by it. Most likely, Medium would gladly go along with Flop's words. In other words, one of the members accompanying them to Chaosflame was settled as Medium.

"Miss Taritta, please get along with my sister. Well now, if you managed to get along with even me, I'm sure you'll get along splendidly with her too."

"Y-yez……um, pleaze, you take care too……"

Fidgeting, eyes downcast, Taritta said this, and Flop thumped his own chest proudly in response. Watching this heartwarming exchange out of the corner of his eye, Subaru gave a small cough and said,

"Just out of curiosity, what about me……"

"You shall be left behind."

"—?!"

"Did you think that was what I would say?"

Abel slipped in a tastelessly-timed feint at Subaru, who'd been about to confirm his own place. Getting furious at that would only make it look as though he'd actually wanted to be taken along, so Subaru held himself back with all his might, gritting his back teeth and keeping silent. Snorting at Subaru, Abel prefaced with "Unfortunately," and said,

"You are already one piece of the bargaining material. Since bringing Yorna Mishigure to our side is essential, we cannot proceed with negotiations leaving that aside."

"When this is all over, I'm definitely punching you in the side of the face……wait, I'm bargaining material?"

"The fall of Guaral was your strategy. Before long, that fact shall become known. I shall see to it myself, in fact."

"What?"

Not understanding the meaning of Abel's words, Subaru blinked in bewilderment. That the fall of the fortress city had been Subaru's plan was beyond doubt, but he couldn't see the advantage in deliberately publicizing it. Being underestimated was, after all, Natsuki Subaru's usual starting line.

"—It is necessary to make them believe there is someone useful besides Abel."

To Subaru's question, it was Priscilla who answered succinctly. Hearing her words, understanding slowly seeped into Subaru's mind as well.

"Naturally, not the fool alone. The 'People of Shudrak' of the Buddheim Jungle, and Second General Zikr Osman, guarding the fortress city of Guaral, also serve as leverage in negotiations. But, the foremost among them……"

"The strategist who contributed to the city's fall……I told you already. In the Empire, the strong are revered. That holds true not only for martial power, but for cunning as well."

"――――"

And so, Abel and Priscilla assured him that Natsuki Subaru held value. Honestly, for Subaru, it was an evaluation that left him feeling thoroughly conflicted.

To be so highly esteemed was, in a way, almost embarrassing. And yet, at the same time, it also felt like an evaluation beyond his due. In fact, despite receiving such high regard, Subaru had lost Rem's trust and tasted the collapse of the ground beneath his own feet.

However highly he might be esteemed in the Empire, it was no match at all, weighed against Rem's trust. For that reason, Subaru's heart went unmoved even by Abel and Priscilla's assessment. However——

"If you're saying I'm useful, fine, good. Use me well. In exchange……"

"In exchange for?"
