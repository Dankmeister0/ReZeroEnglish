"Invisible Providence!!"

In that instant, faster than hand or foot or thought, working directly on the soul, a third phantom hand shot straight out. It scooped up "Black Dog" and Beatrice, casting her healing magic on him, both at once, yanking them clear at the last possible instant from the soundlessly collapsing ceiling, and flung them clear.

He'd made it in time — probably. But there was no room to confirm it. —Because overhead, above Subaru and "White Sheep" too, the same kind of silent crack had opened up, and the ceiling had begun to fall.

"—Wha—?!"

Springing up as if leaping backward, he grabbed the startled "White Sheep's" hand and broke into a full run at once. Dragging her along — she had no idea what was happening, having had her face covered while crying — he tore off, deep into the corridor. All the while, right on their heels, a rain of collapsing rubble kept chasing after them, soundlessly.

"Uwoaaaaahhh!!"

He had no room to look back, and with no sound to hear, he had no way to sense any sign of it ending. So as not to get caught up in that silent ruin, he just ran, and ran, and kept running—,

"—'Ash Star'-san! Please, stop!"

Caught off guard by the sudden forceful plea and the yank on his arm, Subaru stumbled forward. Just barely catching himself with a hand on the ground before he toppled over, he spun around in a panic. But the collapse hadn't reached this far. —No, it seemed the hand pulling him back had happened quite a while before this point. The area beyond them was buried in rubble for some distance, but Subaru and "White Sheep" were safe.

"Wait, what about Beako?! And that 'Black Dog' guy, too..."

"—. I lost sight of them beyond the rubble. But I could see the light of 'Librarian'-san's magic, so I'm sure—"

"Beako's... right, yeah. I can feel a faint sense of the connection."

Closing his eyes against the impulse welling up in him, he checked what "White Sheep" had pointed out. The pathway connecting a contractor and their Spirit, however far apart they were, guaranteed the bond with Beatrice still held. Which meant, in other words, proof that she was safe. Though "safe so far" was as much as that told him.

"Damn it, we knew there was an enemy out there, so why did we go and let ourselves get caught so easily..."

"Do you think it was strange too, 'Ash Star'-san?"

"Of course I do! That 'Black Dog' guy going off on some snide, sarcastic parade like that, sure, that's normal for him, but even Crusch-san — that wasn't like her. Even if something happened to Ferris, she's not the type to turn a sword on a companion..."

Even as he said it, one clearly unnatural thing after another kept surfacing in his own mind. Crusch drawing her sword, "Black Dog" resorting to force so quickly — both had been far too quick to act, no matter how you looked at it. And for that matter, Subaru, doing nothing but panicking and fuming, and "White Sheep," reduced to helpless bawling — everyone had been off, somehow. In fact, right now, in the "White Sheep" wiping her tears in front of him, that earlier fragility had vanished.

"Don't tell me that was an enemy attack too...?"

"I believe so. We were made to turn on each other. Split up from the group, right as planned—"

"Losing our hearing, and that other thing — what was it? Our emotions forced on us... no, it wasn't the same as that thing Sirius does. Getting angry, crying, it was all different, separately—"

Looking back over what had happened to them, they tried to form a hypothesis for what had been done. For an instant, the Authority of the Sin Archbishop of "Wrath" flitted through his mind, but that was an evil power that dominated other people's minds under the pretense of uniting everyone present's hearts as one. But this, now, was subtly different from that. Each of them had reacted differently—,

"—We'd lost the ability to hold ourselves back?"

"That's it! That's the closest to it! Losing the ability to hear sound, and having the lid come off whatever holds our emotions in check — is there a common thread there?"

"No. —'Ash Star'-san, listen. This might be really, seriously bad."

"I already think it's pretty damn bad as it stands, but — what is it? Tell me."

Clenching her hands tight in front of her chest, "White Sheep" fixed Subaru with a serious look. And then—,

"—There's a strong chance that, right here, right now, there are two or more Beloved Children among us."

  • The opening section is a dense wordplay showcase built entirely around 回る/回り ("mawaru," to spin/go around) used as verb, noun, and prefix in dozens of idioms (回りくどい "roundabout," 身の回り "surroundings," 回覧 "circulate," 回廊 "corridor," etc.). English cannot replicate this pun density; I adapted using a consistent "spin/round/turn/around" motif throughout rather than translating each idiom literally, which necessarily loses some individual jokes.

  • Several character names/codenames appear syllable-scrambled during the silence/spin sequence, then resolve to normal order once Subaru's senses return: シュクル "Shukur" ↔ クルシュ "Crusch," イヌクロ "Inukuro" ↔ 黒狗 "Black Dog" (Kuroinu), ヨウハク "Youhaku" ↔ 白羊 "White Sheep" (Hakuyou), トリスベア "Trisbea" ↔ ベアトリス "Beatrice."
