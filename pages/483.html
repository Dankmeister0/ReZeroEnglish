Arc 6 Chapter 77: "The Signal Fire of the Counterattack"

Following the heat of his feelings, running with all the strength he had, he arrived at that place.
Contrary to the heart driving hot blood through the depths of his chest, the air was cold enough to turn each exhaled breath white, chilled to a bone-deep clarity — and in that frozen, frigid world, he found the back he'd wanted to find.

"——"

A slender, supple back leapt into view, and for a moment his heart nearly burst.
Overflowing affection struck his chest, but he barely held the impulse in check. This was no time to die of a heart-explosion from sheer infatuation. Clenching his back teeth hard, past the fighting back before him, he caught sight of the face of the one she opposed.

Long dark brown hair, a shabby getup wrapped in what looked like rags. Above all, what stood out were the eyes — hideous eyes that had trampled other people's lives underfoot and made a literal meal of them.

"——Lye Batenkaitos!!"

He roared with all his voice, asserting his own presence, drawing the enemy's attention.
Even a single instant of diverted attention would be worth it. Of course, that might mean more of the attack came flying at him instead — but if it meant less harm to her, that was nothing at all.
Fueled by the love welling up inside him, he felt like he could fly right now if he had to.

"So long as it raises our odds of winning even a little."

He stopped short of the frost-covered floor, tensed his stomach, and glared at Lye. Watching her every move with full focus, ready for whatever came—

"Ah……"

That readiness of Subaru's was seized and shattered by a pair of violet-blue eyes that whirled around to meet his.

"——"

Black eyes and violet-blue eyes crossed each other's gaze.
In that instant, the wave of emotion that rose in her eyes was so immense it threatened to sweep him away. Always, her eyes ensnared his heart and tried to drown it.
Because they made him want to somehow scoop up every single one of the countless feelings held within them.
And so——

"It's dangerous, wait! Um, you might not know who I am, but, that's the enemy over there! Leave this to me! Even if you don't know who I am!"

He blinked at the sight of her, waving her hands frantically, calling out a warning to him.
A beat later, the meaning of her words sank in. — Somewhere inside himself, he was certain, there was a memory of being told this very same thing. At the same time, he understood exactly what must have happened.
And he truly thought — I'm glad it was me who got here first.

He knew what to say, knew how to call out to her.
More than that, what he'd wanted to call out to her from the very start welled up inside him, and—

"—It's okay, Emilia-tan."

Sorry for troubling her, worrying her, making her wait.
With that feeling behind it, he nodded, loosening his lips in a smile inappropriate to the moment. Then, watching the silver-haired girl whose eyes filled with understanding at that one word, he went on.

He pointed his right hand to the sky, put his other hand on his hip, and struck a pose.
There was no need to do it, but doing it anyway steeled his resolve. For that purpose——

"—My name is Natsuki Subaru. Emilia-tan's Knight of the One!"

"Subaru——!!"

"Wabuh!?"

The instant he'd said his piece with a face that said nailed it, the silver-haired girl — Emilia — came barreling toward Subaru at tremendous speed.
Carried by that momentum, she leapt at him, and Subaru, tipping far backward, somehow managed to bear her full weight. No need to worry — she was light as an angel's wings. No problem.
If there was a problem, it was——

"E-Emilia-tan!? That was so sudden, it startled me, and you're incredibly soft and you smell amazing! Did you change shampoos!?"

"Subaru, you idiot! Really, you huge idiot! I was so, so worried! And after all that... idiot! Idiot, idiot!"

"I'm being called a huge idiot! Well, I can't really argue……"

In his arms, with Emilia's big eyes bearing down on him in accusation, Subaru floundered. All the while, pressed close together with her, his heart was in complete chaos and his vocabulary had short-circuited.
Anyway, he thought, reluctant though he was, and gripped Emilia's slender shoulders to pull back and put some distance between them.

"Sorry for worrying you. But I'm back now. ...Recovered? Merged back together? I guess you could say I've become the complete version of Natsuki Subaru — anyway, point is, I'm okay."

"Ah……"

"Every past Natsuki Subaru is now a thing of the past. — Natsuki Subaru, reborn."

He rattled it off with sheer momentum, trying to force his way to earning Emilia's peace of mind. But at that, Emilia only blinked, and gently reached out to touch his chest with a fingertip.

A ticklish, gentle touch. Subaru couldn't help but shudder all over, and then——

"Did you really manage to become one with him?"

"——"

"Even Subaru without his memories was still Subaru. So even if you've remembered everything now, that Subaru who tried so hard, even in that short time……"

"—Ah, yeah. It's okay."

Touching his chest, in halting words, Emilia traced the footprints Subaru had left behind.
Through the merging of memories, there had been a Natsuki Subaru who could be understood either as having become one with him, or as having vanished entirely — and the time she'd spent connecting hearts with that Natsuki Subaru existed inside her too.

—Ah, then, it was simple.

"Inside me, and inside Emilia-tan too, there's definitely a part left over that says 'I' was there. So really, it's okay, Emilia-tan."

"……Okay."

"So from here on, please look forward to the perfect Natsuki Subaru. Using the pent-up frustration from this whole wild ride as fuel, let's go — full speed ahead into a shining future!"

"Sorry, I don't really understand what you're saying."

At Subaru's thumbs-up smile, Emilia slowly shook her head in response.
Even that cool reaction was endearing, something he'd longed for, and Subaru wanted to stay like this, flirting with Emilia, for however many minutes, hours, days it might take——

"—So, is that touching reunion about done, ya think?"

Speaking to Subaru and Emilia as they cautiously touched each other, a voice called out from a distance — the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony, wearing a sinister smile.
But Subaru raised an eyebrow at the fact that, in all this time, no attack had come.

"Yeah. I didn't expect you to wait around so politely. Had a change of heart?"

"Don't be ridiculous, I'd never devour my resolve! It's just, well, we're gourmets, y'see. Or as you'd put it, foodies? As someone who knows the joys of dining, I like to pay proper attention to the table setting too. It ruins the mood if the table's a mess. Right?"

Talking about table manners of all things, hard to apply here, Lye let out a high, cackling laugh. Listening to that grating cackle, Subaru nodded deeply.

And then—

"I see, that makes sense. I figured you were……"

"Figured what?"

"That your little sister got beaten so pathetically, you got scared."

"—!"

Immediately, the composed smirk on Lye's face changed completely.
The smile vanished in an instant, replaced by a burning, searing fury. Driven by that intense rage, Lye's body kicked off the frozen passage and came charging in a flash.

"What do you know about our Rui, huh——!!"

"I don't know a damn thing. I just had her force her way into my body one-sidedly. I don't know what she was thinking, and I don't want to know."

Perhaps he truly did have some feeling reserved only for his sister — enraged, Lye's dagger came rushing at Subaru. But Subaru wasn't the least bit afraid of the footsteps of death closing in.
If Lye, consumed by fury, could see nothing but Subaru — then that was exactly according to plan——

"—Don't forget I'm here too, okay!"

"Gah, buh"

With a valiant cry, Emilia's long, slender leg drew a beautiful arc. Building tremendous momentum, it spun around to intercept Lye's face as he charged straight in.
At the moment of impact, Emilia's ice combat art, "Ice Blade Arts," activated — her ice boots reinforcing the toe that drove into her opponent, as an added bonus.

"H-yaaaaaaah!"

Putting all her strength into her pale leg, Emilia swung through with the kick that shattered her enemy's front teeth. Lye's small frame couldn't withstand the force and went flying, sliding across the frozen floor and tumbling off into the depths of the passage.
There, Emilia turned her palm toward him further, and the ice stakes she conjured pursued Rui — no, Lye — mercilessly as he fell, ice dust filling the passage, dealing massive damage to Gluttony.

"There it is! That's the combo move of my provoking and Emilia-tan's adorable killing intent!"

"Is that supposed to be a compliment?"

"It totally is! Amazing! Adorable! Marry me!"

"Marr—... quit joking around."

Her cheeks flushing just slightly, Emilia's glare in response was unexpected. Having figured she'd brush it off much more coldly, Subaru found his own embarrassment bouncing right back at him.

"No, no, careful — I'm getting way too giddy just from seeing Emilia-tan again. Flirting's for after we've done everything we need to do and cleared up this mess."

"Subaru! We don't even know if we beat him yet! So don't let your guard down."

"I know! If we could actually take him down with that, I'd welcome it, honestly……"

Nearly simultaneously, both of them reined their emotions back in — Emilia turning her focus to the Lye Batenkaitos before her, Subaru turning his to the problems surrounding the Pleiades Watchtower.
Five obstacles — even with Natsuki Subaru's identity crisis resolved, they still continued to threaten the tower and Subaru's companions within it.
Still, some conditions had changed. For instance, the ferocious shadow that surged in like a symbol of time running out, swallowing the tower whole and ruining everything.
That——

"If that's the penalty I've always imagined it to be……"

The catastrophe of the shadow swallowing everything was a recurrence of the disaster he'd encountered before, in the Sanctuary.
What had caused that to happen, back then, was speaking with Echidna inside the Mausoleum and confessing everything about Return by Death to her. If what happened here in this tower was of a similar kind, then that shadow erasing everything would happen if Return by Death leaked out to the outside world on a large scale.
In other words, the cause should have been the fact that Rui Arneb existed inside Subaru.

Now that Rui Arneb had been expelled, her presence gone from within Subaru, whether that shadow would swallow the Pleiades Watchtower was about fifty-fifty.
So there was no option to bank on that and let things proceed slowly.

"Emilia-tan! I've got an idea!"

"—! Got it! Then let's do that!"

"I haven't even said anything yet!?"

The instant the options sorted themselves out in Subaru's head and he called out based on them, Emilia's enthusiastic agreement came flying back with no explanation at all.
Subaru, startled by this, got a firm "It's fine!" from Emilia, who continued,

"If it's Subaru's idea, then it's something that came out after you thought hard about it! I trust that so much more than if I tried to think of all sorts of things and come up with an answer myself right now!"

"—Ah, damn it! Saying something that makes me so happy!"

Scratching his head, Subaru stomped his foot hard on the floor as if responding to that itchy sort of trust. Then he pointed toward the depths of the passage, at the space where ice dust still swirled,

"For now, Emilia-tan, hit him with one more!"

"Eit!"

With a heavy thud, Emilia dropped a chunk of ice into the depths of the passage.
It would be nice if that unhesitating blow finished Lye off, but that was too much to hope for. Sure enough, Emilia, straining her eyes at the point of impact, let out an "Ah!"

"Subaru! That Sin Archbishop from before……"

"Tch, tenacious bastard. But he shouldn't have come out of that unscathed."

From the look on Emilia's face as she turned back, Subaru understood that Lye had vanished from the point of impact.
That cockroach-like tenacity was exasperating, but the fact that he sensed the bad situation and retreated at least once set him apart from someone like Petelgeuse, whose diligence tipped over into blind stupidity, or Regulus, who didn't have "humility" in his vocabulary at all.
Still, there was no way he'd withdraw from the tower entirely.

"Emilia-tan! For now, let's regroup with everyone else! We need to redistribute our personnel! Unless everyone gives it their best, we can't break through this situation!"

"Eh! But is it okay to let him go like that?"

"Honestly, if I could've finished him off just now, I would've wanted to……"

Having retreated once, Lye would surely be fighting with everything he had to buy time to catch his breath. In that case, the time and certainty needed to strike him down were hard to calculate.
So instead, better to prioritize their strategic maneuvering here, and leave his life on the table for now.

"Don't worry, he's not going to leave the tower. I really provoked him hard about Rui... about his little sister. A misguided siscon like him can't back down now."

"Siscon……?"

"Means someone who dotes on his little sister. In their case, it's a twisted kind of doting, though."

At least, on Rui's side, there didn't seem to be any affection toward Lye or Roy, her actual older brothers. If someone devoted themselves to Rui without knowing that, they were either a pathetic victim, or just a role-play fanatic intoxicated with playing the part.
But ironically, the one thing you could trust about a Sin Archbishop was the undistorted consistency of their distortion.

"A guy who can adapt on the fly, change his approach flexibly — that kind of guy wouldn't have become a Sin Archbishop in the first place."

That was Subaru's assessment of Sin Archbishops, built up through repeated, unwanted encounters with them.
Which meant cornering the shamelessly persistent Lye Batenkaitos wouldn't be easy. In the meantime, if Subaru didn't take action, the situation would only keep getting worse and worse, without end.
Before that happened——

"Emilia-tan! To everyone!"

"Mm, got it! But where everyone is, that's……"

"—Navigating that's my job."

The Natsuki Subaru Navigation System.
—Sub-Navi for short — he activated Cor Leonis.

"——"

Activating the Authority, with its familiar sensations mixed with unfamiliar ones, stirred an odd sort of feeling.
He became aware of the location of his own beating heart, and expanded his range of perception outward from himself, as if his heartbeat itself were spreading. It was close to the image of sonar, grasping one's surroundings through the echo of sound.
Except what he felt was the warmth of his companions, a faint light — a much more limited sense than that.
—But without any kind of versatility, specialized purely for sensing the presence of his companions, it was, for Subaru and the others right now, the most valuable Authority there was.

Zeroing in on the faint lights he sensed through the Authority, Subaru lifted his head.
First, just as he'd rushed to reunite with Emilia, next he prioritized reuniting with those she'd sent fleeing ahead of her. Namely——

"—First, I'll go collect my adorable partner!"

△▼△▼△▼△

—From the sensations conveyed through Cor Leonis, Subaru was coming to grasp most of what was happening throughout the Pleiades Watchtower.

Still, all that the Authority's effect let him know was the individual state of each companion, and a vague sense of distance centered on Subaru. It wasn't as if he could share his companions' field of vision, or know their condition in clear detail.
Even so — if they were wounded, he could shoulder the wound in their place; if they had a hangover, he could take that on too. That was the effect of Subaru's Authority of Greed, and there were all sorts of uses one could imagine for it.

"Subaru, are you okay? You look pale……"

"Mm, ah, I'm fine, fine. Well, I'm putting on a bit of a brave face, but I'm still okay. This much, I can totally push through."

Running side by side down the passage, Emilia worried over Subaru as he let out a heavy breath. Tickled in her heart by her silver-bell voice and violet-blue eyes, Subaru let his honest feelings slip out.
It hurt. But he could bear it. That was the truth.

"——"

The color in Subaru's face that Emilia had pointed out — of course, part of that was the effect of the whole ordeal with Rui that had unfolded in order to recover his memories. Mental exhaustion wasn't something that recovered easily; rebuilding a worn-down heart wasn't an easy thing.
But what was tormenting Subaru right now had a much simpler, more obvious cause. — Ram's burden, the suffering she carried with her day in and day out, which he'd taken upon himself ahead of time.

"Ram definitely wouldn't be happy about me taking on her wounds without permission……"

Ram, with her strong sense of responsibility, absolutely hated having others shoulder her burdens for her. If he proposed to her face-to-face, "I want to share the hardship with you," the best he could hope for was being scoffed at, and then having steamed potato stuffed up his nose for good measure.
So, he'd just shoulder Ram's burden without asking. He wouldn't let her refuse.
So——

"Subaru!"

Turning a corner in the passage, Emilia, with a clear line of sight ahead, called out to Subaru. The meaning of that call, Subaru — with the Lion Heart pounding within him — understood.
It was the companion who, while Emilia had taken on Lye, should have left the battlefield and put distance between them.
A jet-black Earth Dragon, and on its back, a blue-haired girl. Holding the reins was an older sister with a face identical to the girl's, and beside them stood a young girl in a dress——

"Bea-ko!!"
"Beatrice!"

"—!? S-Subaru, and some girl I don't know, are coming!?"

Beatrice, turning around, was stunned at the sight of Subaru and Emilia charging toward her at full tilt. But paying no mind to her shock, Subaru and Emilia both leapt onto her at nearly the same moment.
Scooping up the young girl, who let out a shriek of "Wakya!" as they went,

"Oh, Bea-ko! Bea-ko, you're so light! So cute! Such a smart-looking face!"

"W-w-w-w……"

"Look, Beatrice! Subaru remembered everything! Ah, but I guess he might not know who I am... but Subaru remembered! I'm so, so happy about that……"

"W-wait, would you two! Wait a moment! It's like a memory Betty knows and one she doesn't arrived at the same time — Betty's head's in a total uproar, I suppose!"

Getting tossed about, Beatrice was battered by Subaru's and Emilia's words in turns, her eyes spinning. But she quickly thrust out her palm, silencing Emilia first, and then,

"……Subaru."

"Yeah."

"You really remembered everything, I suppose? About Betty too, about all the time you spent with Betty, all of it?"

"Yeah, that's right. Don't make such a worried face. I remembered everything, and honestly I could fabricate as much as you want on top of it. A history of me and you, true and false alike."

"There's no need to fabricate the false parts, I suppose! Oh, honestly!"

Held up in his arms, Beatrice pressed both hands against Subaru's face, right in front of her. Watching him become a squashed, fish-lipped mess up close, Beatrice let out a deep sigh.
Then, her eyes with their distinctive markings trembling, she said,

"Subaru really does nothing but drag Betty around, I suppose."

"Wasn't the contract condition supposed to be that I'm the contractor who never bores you? Also, you told me before I'm not Superman, but……"

"—?"

"Objectively speaking, aren't I kind of a Superman?"

Closing one eye, winking, Subaru grinned at Beatrice. For a moment, at that answer, Beatrice's eyes went wide, and then her cheeks immediately puffed out.

"Don't get carried away, I suppose!"

"Let me get carried away. I work better when I'm riding high. And then……"

"——"

Glancing sideways, he found Ram standing there, an unamused look on her face. Narrowing her pale pink eyes, she was fixing Subaru with a piercing — no, skewering — gaze.

"Ah, Sister?"

"How quickly the ice thaws. In the end, it's just the sort of noisy fuss you'd expect from Barusu."

"……Well, in real terms it's only been about a day, hasn't it."

Called a noisy fuss, all Subaru could do was give a wry laugh.
In truth, thinking of the nearly twenty deaths Subaru had experienced, and the mental toll of accepting each as his own, it hardly felt like something that had happened in just one day.
As far as Subaru was concerned, it felt like he'd lost his memory for years.

"But I'm back now, right on cue. A man who can give a hundred and fifty percent for everyone's sake — please look forward to Natsuki Subaru."

"Hah! Fine. Say whatever you like. Only, even if merciful Ram were to overlook it……"

"Overlook it? ……bangladeshuh!?"
"Ukyaa, I suppose!?"

The instant he tilted his head at Ram's meaningfully clipped words, a tail strike from the Earth Dragon came slicing in from a blind spot, sending both him and Beatrice flying.
Spinning around and around, Subaru and Beatrice were caught by something soft. Looking, it was Emilia, who'd circled around to the spot they'd go flying toward. Supporting Subaru's body gently from behind, she smiled, "Fufu,"

"Patrasche-chan seems really, really happy that Subaru's back too."

"——"

Where Emilia's gaze pointed, the jet-black, majestic form stood, letting out a single snort. Having taken that powerful tail strike, Subaru readjusted his hold on the dizzy Beatrice in his arms, and,

"That's some old-fashioned tsundere move you're pulling... no, I was in the wrong. I gave you plenty to worry about too, huh. To begin with……"

"——"

Supported by Emilia, having steadied himself, Subaru faced Patrasche, whose sharp gaze bore into him. And, recalling the unwavering devotion of his beloved dragon during the time his memories were gone,

"Really, you're a lifesaver. I love you."

"——"

"……No scolding kick like usual, huh?"

Whenever he made a light declaration of love, Patrasche, ever the lady, would invariably scold him for it — but this time, that didn't come. Perhaps that meant,

"Even this Earth Dragon can tell the difference between a joke and the real thing, I suppose."

"……Guess so."

When had the two of them gotten so close? Beatrice spoke on Patrasche's behalf. With no denying tail-strike, there was no doubting Beatrice's translation.
If Beatrice picked up translation skills on top of everything else, Otto would finally be out of a job.

"—Rem."

On Patrasche's back, Rem was seated, her body resting against the saddle.
Of course, from her, still asleep, there was no way to hear a voice of joy at Subaru's memories returning. But seeing Rem's profile, the feeling that rose in Subaru's chest was something else entirely.

"——"

In that white world, tempted by Rui Arneb's honeyed words, at the very moment he was about to fall to the fangs of Gluttony — if not for the voice that kicked his helpless, cowardly self back into shape.
Truly, that person's existence — even when Natsuki Subaru forgot himself — wouldn't let him go.
So once again, having overcome a total of forty deaths, he'd come back.

"A-anyway, dwelling on all that can wait. Point is, my memories are back. I'm grateful everyone wants to throw a celebration, but let's clear up this situation all at once."

"Calling it a celebration is awfully high praise for yourself. Wonder what happened in this short span of time…… also——"

"—. Ah, me?"

Snorting at Subaru's lowered tone of voice, Ram's gaze then turned to Emilia. In her eyes rose the usual arrogance, along with an equal measure of unease.
As was clear from Beatrice's reaction too, indeed——

"This is Emilia. Looks like you don't remember her."

"……I know that she's the one who helped Ram and the others escape earlier. But that's all — I can't recall anything beyond that. Lady Beatrice……"

"Betty's the same, I suppose. ……The effect of Gluttony's Authority, I suppose."

At Ram's nod, Beatrice likewise lowered her face.
The same thing that had happened to Julius in Priestella had now befallen Emilia here inside this tower too. That fact would linger as a painful wound, and——

"Yes! I'm Emilia, just Emilia. There's plenty I want to say, but I'm family who looks in the same direction as both Beatrice and Ram!"

"——"

"As long as you understand that much, I'm fine right now. Ever since I knew Subaru remembered me, I've been feeling really, really good."

Hmph, Emilia made a fist with her pale arm as if flexing, and — no, it wasn't putting on a brave face, it was a genuine smile, one that truly feared nothing.
At that overwhelmingly confident display, not just Subaru but Beatrice too was struck speechless. Rare as it was, even Ram couldn't help but widen her eyes in surprise.

"Huh? Did I say something weird?"

"……Nah, not weird, more like — that was incredibly cool."

At Emilia's slightly flustered reaction, Subaru scratched his cheek and answered so. A beat behind Subaru's remark, Ram let out a deep breath and said, "I see."
She looked Emilia over carefully, and,

"Emilia, was it. Strangely enough, looking at you, the corner of Ram's mind feels oddly itchy. ……The same thing happened with Rem, too."

"That's……"

"It probably means you've vanished from my mind, and filling that hole is a struggle. Hard to imagine anyone besides Rem being someone like that to Ram, though."

Narrowing her eyes, Ram seemed to trace the presence of the vanished Emilia from within herself, as if with a fingertip. At the words she let slip, Subaru felt an oddly out-of-place sense of deep feeling.
Even as she said this, that Ram had cared for Emilia — Subaru, who'd watched the two of them from the side, had no doubt of that. Since the accumulated time between them had been lost, it was only natural Ram had nothing to go on.
And yet——

"This throbbing I feel in my head looking at you — that's the answer to the relationship between Ram and you. So relax. — Ram hates being the only one who takes a hit."

"—. Same goes for me. Absolutely, without a doubt, I'm paying it back."

"Yes, a billionfold return."

"That's a lot!?"

Emilia and Ram, exchanging fearless smiles, gave Subaru a feeling of reassurance.
Her name stolen, she should have been left isolated, her relationships bound to crumble. But Emilia hadn't lost to that isolation, and had kept the core of her heart strong. Thanks to that, the collapse that should have started from her couldn't undermine the foundation Subaru and Ram and the others stood on.
On top of that——

"Barusu, offer up your plan. Don't think that just remembering your own forgetfulness makes up for all the points you've lost so far."

"I know that without you saying it, but what a way to put it!"

Still, that she'd show a willingness to cooperate without needing to be persuaded was a relief.
After all, the dire straits this tower in the sand sea found itself in were far too pressing to allow for building trust, explaining the situation, and tying bonds to each problem one at a time.
Therefore——

"That gluttony festival those worst-of-the-worst three siblings started — let's smash it to pieces with our bonds."

"Yes!"
"Naturally, I suppose."
"Hah!"

Three different responses came back, and Subaru nodded deeply at each and every one.
And then,

"—We're all over the place, but it feels like coming home!"

With that, before the great trial ahead, he let out a small but genuine burst of feeling.
