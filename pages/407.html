Arc 6 Chapter 5: "Each Their Own Unease"

Now then, between the various discussions, the heated debate over who would accompany whom, and the farewells to those being left behind, no shortage of problems had arisen — but at last, the morning of departure came.

"So anyway, this ended up being a bigger group than I expected..."

Arms crossed, Subaru surveyed the members joining this "Sage's Tower Conquest Tour." Two camps were represented: the Emilia Camp and the Anastasia Camp. From the Emilia Camp, naturally, there was Subaru, Emilia, and Beatrice. Add to that the still-sleeping Rem and Ram, who was accompanying her — five in total. Then there was Anastasia, serving as guide through the Augria Sand Dunes, and her knight Julius. And to help them slip past the packs of witchbeasts roaming the dunes, Meili, the "witchbeast handler," joined as well — eight people altogether.

If this were simply a matter of a straightforward military operation, the force that had set out to retake Priestella had numbered more, and Subaru had experience with marches like the Subjugation of the White Whale or the assault on the Sloth Archbishop's lair. But purely in terms of traveling to a distant place — eight key people making that whole journey together — this was a scale of numbers he'd rarely dealt with before.

"Honestly, is this gonna be okay...?"

"Something worrying you? Subaru."

Tilting his head as he mulled over what lay ahead, Subaru found Emilia stepping into his field of view. Dressed in light traveling clothes, she watched him hum a noncommittal "hmm" before he answered.

"Thinking about our destination, there's no end to what could worry me. Setting that aside... it's kind of unnerving, taking such a big group somewhere dangerous. More people to protect means less coverage to go around."

"That might be true. This time we've got Rem and Ram along too, plus Meili and Anastasia-san — and we have to protect you too, Subaru."

"Wait, what!? Was I just casually filed under noncombatant!?"

"Oh, Subaru. That was obviously a joke. Honestly, taking it seriously like that."

Subaru's voice cracked at Emilia's raised fist, and she couldn't hold back a laugh. Subaru laughed too, a beat behind her, though his smile came out a little strained — understandably so. The fact was, Subaru's combat ability fell well short of Emilia's. In her eyes, he was someone to be protected — though of course, he had no intention of letting that impression stand forever.

"As for the other concrete worries..."

Taking a breath from his conversation with Emilia, Subaru glanced ahead. The location was the front courtyard of Roswaal Manor, where preparations for departure were quietly proceeding. And in the direction of Subaru's gaze sat a large passenger carriage, big enough to carry nearly ten people. Two earth dragons, both breeds suited for long-distance travel, were harnessed to pull it.

The problem was what stood beside that dragon carriage: a jet-black earth dragon standing unburdened, nose to nose with a small figure, glaring at each other.

Sighing in exasperation at the sight, Subaru stepped in between the two glaring parties.

"For someone with such a grand title as 'witchbeast handler,' you can't even get along with an earth dragon?"

"Aw, come on, mister. Don't get the wrong idea, 'kay? The only little animal friends I can train are the bad ones with horns. Any animal besides that just won't like me — not with the smell of those other kids clinging to me."

"Huh, is that how it works...? That sounds rough in its own way."

Shrugging at Meili's explanation, Subaru stroked the nose of Patrasche, who was locked in her staring match with the girl. The proud, high-spirited female earth dragon sniffed pointedly at his hand the moment he touched her — as if trying to overwrite the girl's scent standing in front of her with Subaru's own.

"For considerate, big-hearted Patrasche to be this blatant about it is rare... you two really must not get along."

"I can handle the other kids well enough, but that earth dragon's just too attached to you, mister. Like, if I let my guard down even a little, she looks ready to bite me. You better keep her properly trained, or I might end up as her snack — so watch out, okay?"

"Don't say something that scary like it's no big deal... Come on, easy Patrasche, easy."

Calming his slightly worked-up dragon, Subaru promptly loaded Meili into the carriage. Her luggage, as someone accompanying them as the "witchbeast handler," was minimal — she was a girl who owned almost nothing to begin with. Still, the fact that she'd brought along the panda plushie Subaru had made her was endearing enough to bring a smile.

"Seems like Natsuki-kun's having a rough time, keeping all the girls happy."

"...If that's what you think, I'd appreciate it if you'd stop giving me extra work."

As Subaru shoved Meili into the carriage, Anastasia approached with a teasing tone. Anastasia — though the person inside was the white fox Echidna. Over the twenty-odd days of travel from Priestella to the manor, most of the discrepancies between the two had been smoothed over. At this rate, if she kept impersonating Anastasia, before long even Subaru might not be able to tell the difference.

"I wish you wouldn't be so suspicious. I've said over and over it's not what I want either, haven't I? That's exactly why I've volunteered to guide us through such a dangerous road."

"My stance is that even accounting for that, your creator is more than capable of scheming something. I've said this plenty of times too — keep it in mind. It's not that I don't want to trust you, personally. If I could trust you, that'd be a lot easier on me."

"It's not particularly amusing, suffering this kind of disadvantage over someone I don't even remember. It seems my maker — whom I have no memory of — has thoroughly earned your displeasure."

"—"

"There, there, don't get so angry at me. Honestly, you're scary."

Glaring at Eridna, who'd slipped back to her true self midway through, Subaru watched her purse her lips and settle back into playing Anastasia. She then slipped past Subaru and likewise stepped into the carriage.

As for the rest of the party—

"Subaru-kun, could I trouble you to come over here and listen in as well, if you don't mi-i-ind?"

Turning at the call of his name, Subaru saw Roswaal had come out to the manor's entrance. Beside the clownishly dressed margrave stood Emilia and Julius. Thinking it an unusual pairing, Subaru walked over, and Roswaal glanced between the three of them, closing one eye.

"First, this journey is expected to take nearly two months. Given tha-a-at, we must assume some unforeseen circumstance will arise along the way. As for handling problems as they come, I don't think there'll be any major trouble with Emilia-sama or Anastasia-sama at the helm, but..."

"Mm, leave it to me. I haven't just been idling around, you know."

"Reassuring words. Emilia-sama's growth is a genuine joy to see. ...Now then, as for the day-to-day worries of the road, I'll leave those to each of you individually. But there's one important matter I need to raise. — It concerns Ram."

"—"

At Roswaal lowering his voice, Subaru and the others each shifted expression. Emilia's long lashes trembled, and Subaru's cheeks stiffened as though deep in thought. And the one person among them with no ties to the camp, Julius, furrowed his brow ever so slightly.

"Is it appropriate for me to be present for this discussion? Surely you understand, Mathers-kyou, that revealing the internal affairs of your camp to me — a knight in Anastasia-sama's service, however humble a retainer — would not be the wisest course."

"It's not as though I go around bringing this up with just anyo-o-one. Julius Juukulius-dono. Having observed your conduct here at the manor, and your dealings with Subaru-kun and the others, I judged you a trustworthy individual. I'd like you to take this request in that spirit."

"That's the most suspicious thing I've heard in ages, Roz-cchi."

"Sorry, I kind of thought that too..."

Roswaal answered Julius's concern in an earnest tone, and Subaru and Emilia raised their hands in near-unison to needle him. Setting aside his track record, the statement was simply too out of character for Roswaal. Perhaps he himself was aware of it too, as he gave Subaru and the others a wry smile and a "well, that's fair enough," before continuing.

"We-e-ell, I suppose it's only natural I lack credibility here. Still, it's a fact that I judged Julius-kun not the type to busy himself digging up dirt on another camp — that sort of petty character isn't him. That's why I want you to hear this too. This concerns a matter of life and death, after all."

"Life and death..."

Julius murmured it with a deadly serious face, and Roswaal nodded, "Yes, life and death."

"As Subaru-kun and Emilia-sama already know, Ram's constitution is somewhat unusual. Her body cannot fully contain the talent poured into it. Or perhaps it's better to say her flesh cannot provide fuel sufficient for the flame that is her talent."

"...I wouldn't say I know that much detail, 'already knows' or not."

"Is that so? The-e-en, what about the fact that she's of the Oni Tribe?"

"That much, well, yeah..."

Of course Subaru knew that the sisters Ram and Rem were of the Oni Tribe. He'd been saved more than once or twice by Rem wielding an oni's full strength with her horn grown in. And he'd heard from Ram herself that she was "Hornless" — an oni who had lost her horn. So Subaru hadn't thought there was anything especially shocking in this—

"—The Oni Tribe, you say?"

"Julius?"

It was Julius beside him, voice lowered, whose surprise was plain. On his fine-featured face, his eyebrows rose, and unease surfaced in his yellow eyes.

"I was told the Oni Tribe had already died out."

"The last settlement burned down nearly ten years ago, and Ram was the final survivor... oh, but considering Rem as well, I suppose the two sisters together are the final survivors."

"—"

There was something loaded in the way he said it, but Subaru didn't interject. Stronger than any irritation was his surprise that the sisters were a rarer breed than he'd imagined.

"Is there a reason the Oni Tribe died out?"

"...Rest assured, Emilia-sama. The extinction of the Oni Tribe was an inevitable result of natural selection among races. They refused to accommodate the changing times and clung to old customs to the bitter end. It was by no means that their numbers were reduced and they were driven to extinction through discrimination or prejudice."

"...I see."

At Roswaal's explanation, Emilia's expression settled into acceptance, though her gaze remained wistful. Emilia herself was of a rare lineage as well, being a half-elf. It was hardly unreasonable that she'd feel a mixture of unease and sympathy at the fate of a tribe that had died out, leaving only two behind.

"We all understand now that those two are of the Oni Tribe. So, what does that mean for us?"

Sensing the conversation stalling, Subaru deliberately raised his voice to push things along. At that, Emilia and Julius looked up, and Roswaal touched his chin.

"Setting aside Rem, whose physical activity is currently halted, Ram's body has the defect I just described. That is caused by the loss of the organ so vital to the Oni Tribe — her horn. The vast quantities of mana normally needed to move the body, which the horn ought to supply — without it, her body is being constantly eaten away by pain and lethargy."

"It's come to that...!?"

"It's no wonder you're surprised, Emilia-sama. That girl is far too strong-willed. I doubt she's ever once let her expression cloud over it."

Roswaal shook his head slowly at Emilia, whose throat caught. Subaru shared in Emilia's shock. He'd known Ram had no horn. He'd gathered in fragments, from things Rem had said, that Ram's raw ability before losing her horn had been exceptional. But he'd never considered that the lack of a horn carried a penalty beyond a simple drop in power — one that was, even now, continuing to eat away at her.

"—I see. I understand Mathers-kyou's thinking now."

Having heard only that much, Julius suddenly spoke up and nodded. At that reaction, Roswaal raised an eyebrow with a "hoh," and Subaru and Emilia exchanged glances. What they'd just heard was simply a confirmation of Ram's condition — what could that alone have told him about Roswaal's request? While Subaru and the others looked on in surprise, Julius smoothed back his forelocks.

"The racial traits of the Oni Tribe are their command of vast mana and the combat strength of their formidable bodies. The pillar supporting both is the horn — an organ no other race possesses. If that horn is failing to fulfill its role, causing her suffering, then something else must take its place."

"Enough that I regret not remembering your name — truly, how astute. To have it grasped this thoroughly from so little, it's almost satisfying."

"Oh, I see, that's what this is about..."

Roswaal marveled at Julius's explanation, and Emilia, having caught on as well, clapped her hands. Meanwhile, Subaru alone was left behind in confusion. Whenever things turned to matters of magic, the layman Subaru was always slow to follow.

"Don't just go understanding it between the two of you. So, what does that mean exactly?"

"It's simple. Lady Ram's body requires an intake of mana from an external source equivalent to what her horn once provided. Since she's lost her horn and can no longer manage that herself, she needs someone else to serve as that external source. ...And that is what you're asking of us."

"Normally, at night, I quietly handle the supplying myself."

"...Ah!?"

At Julius's exposition and Roswaal's words, the gears in Subaru's mind finally clicked into place. It was the image of Ram, hurrying off to Roswaal's chambers every single night. Honestly, Subaru had always averted his eyes from the rawness of it, assuming it to be some illicit relationship between master and maid. But now, having learned the true circumstances and thinking back on it — was that really what it had been?

"Did something occur to you, Subaru-kun?"

"N-no, it's nothing important. Go ahead, please continue."

"Is that so?"

Even discovering a year-long misunderstanding wasn't worth the trouble of correcting out loud. To begin with, Roswaal's family line, generation after generation, had been scheming toward a reunion with the Witch Echidna — a group practically exploding with suspicious behavior. And Roswaal himself had shown a remarkable degree of obsession toward Echidna, something Subaru wasn't likely to forget. If anything, learning Roswaal hadn't been cheating on Ram only made his obsessiveness make more sense.

"However, Mathers-kyou. Regarding your request — I believe I lack the ability to take it on."

Beside Subaru, lost in his pointless train of thought, Julius shook his head apologetically. Roswaal narrowed his eyes at that.

"Modesty, or is it that you won't lend support to a rival camp... it doesn't sound like either of those reasons. Providing mana to Ram requires all four attributes — fire, water, wind, and earth. Which is exactly why I thought you, more than Emilia-sama, would be the fitter candidate."

"What Mathers-kyou is hoping for is, I imagine, the ones around me."

So saying, Julius loosened his lips into something faintly weak. As he spoke, a pale light became visible, flitting about him — six distinct glimmers shimmering and swaying. Subaru recognized them at once: the six lesser spirits contracted to Julius — though now—

"My buds — but the bond that once existed between them and me has vanished. If I could still claim my former title, I would accept this request without a moment's hesitation."

"Having your name stolen severed your contract with the spirits too... is that it? And yet, the spirits don't seem inclined to leave your side even so?"

"Whether some residue of that severed bond still lingers within them, or whether the contract itself remains but has simply been bleached from view... in either case, that this unworthy self hasn't been abandoned is entirely thanks to my buds' compassion. What remains to me is little, and I doubt such meager strength could be of much help."

Touching on his bond with the lesser spirits, Julius let out a breath. Then, just for a moment, his eyes turned toward Subaru. Unable to grasp the intent behind that glance, Subaru could only return a puzzled expression.

"—For now, I am nothing more than a knight, devoted only to service. My apologies."

"I see. That's a shame. It's a setback, but..."

At the grim tone from Julius, Roswaal's voice dipped as well — but stepping in with her chest puffed out instead was Emilia. She stepped forward decisively, and minor spirits rose up floating around her.

"They're a little different from Julius's lesser spirit friends, but I can borrow strength from the minor spirits too. My own magic leans too heavily fire, but if I borrow the minor spirits' power, I can use mana of other attributes as well. How about that?"

"Naturally, I intended to ask this of you as well, not only him, Emilia-sama, so if you're willing, there's no problem at all. Though, I suspect at first you'll struggle a great deal with stabilizing the mana. To assist with that, please borrow Beatrice's strength."

"Bea-chan's?"

"When it comes to mana theory and application, Beatrice is exceptio-o-onally skilled. Right now, since her contractor is Subaru-kun, it's rather a waste of talent, wouldn't you say — managing to accomplish so much with so little mana to work with? I'd like her to lend that knowledge to you as well, Emilia-sama."

"Don't go saying 'so little mana.' But, huh, I see."

If Emilia and Beatrice working together could lighten Ram's burden, there was nothing to object to. Subaru was on the verge of accepting this, but then paused, tilting his head again.

"Wait, hold on, if that's the case, why are you telling me instead of talking to Bea-tan directly? I mean, sure, I want to be kept in the loop, but logically you should be talking to her."

"Well, because if I brought it to Beatrice directly, she'd refuse just for the sake of refusing, wouldn't she? Beatrice is far more caustic toward me than you might realize. Couldn't you perhaps put in a word, so that she'd be a bit ge-e-entler toward me?"

"Ah, that's a no can do. I'm — well, more than 'leaning that way,' I'm firmly Team Bea-tan on this one."

Subaru swiftly turned down Roswaal's pathetic-faced request. What was done was done. A year or so wasn't going to close that particular gap. Besides, the world wasn't kind enough that you could just easily patch things up with someone who — depending on how things had gone — might well have killed you.

"Come to think of it, being able to act alongside people who've killed me, or beaten me half to death... does that maybe make me kind of a big deal...?"

"Ah, Subaru, that's the face you make when you're getting cocky."

"I make a face like that!?"

In any case, Roswaal's request had been accepted. As for Ram, they'd rely on Emilia and Beatrice. Emilia was already motivated, so as long as Subaru squared things with Beatrice afterward, there shouldn't be a problem.

"I'm counting on you, Subaru-kun."

"What, you're being weirdly gracious. Not like you to talk that way."

"Gracious is fair, I think. — This is the first time Ram has ever said she wanted to leave my side."

"—"

That alone, Roswaal said without a trace of his usual teasing, in a genuinely serious voice. He pulled his gaze away from Subaru's face and looked up at the blue sky.

"She's a twin sister neither I nor the one herself should have any memory of. And yet, it seems even so, there's something the party involved feels all the same. Watching Ram defy my opinion so emotionally like that — it chills me. — Which is why I'm asking this of you both."

"...Yeah. I'll keep it in mind."

Even after everything that had happened a year ago at the Sanctuary, Ram still devoted herself to Roswaal. Even with a man who declared, flat out, that everything besides the one thing that truly mattered to him was secondary — even toward a man like that, Ram's devotion gave Subaru something to think about.

If this was a sign of change, he didn't think it was a bad one.

At the very least, far better a man who found himself troubled and overwhelmed by feelings he couldn't quite understand, than a monster who felt nothing comprehensible at all being kept as an ally.

"—Seems it's about time."

Before more words could pile up, Roswaal turned around. Behind him, the manor's door opened, and four girls emerged from within. All of them bore the title of maid of Roswaal Manor, and lined up together they made for quite the sight. Though, with one of them still asleep and her identical twin dressed for travel, the display fell just a bit short of full splendor.

"Master, the two of them are ready."

"Well done. — Ram, take care on the road."

Responding to Frederica's calm report, Roswaal called out to the travel-ready Ram. Ram, receiving it, pinched the hem of her skirt and bowed politely.

"Roswaal-sama, thank you for granting my selfish request. I will be sure to bring back results worthy of your consideration."

"I have high hopes. That said, don't push yourself too ha-a-ard. And don't let Emilia-sama or Subaru-kun push themselves too hard either. That, too, is your role."

"Understood, most thoroughly."

Subaru wanted to retort look who's talking, but Ram's glare silenced him. Defeated by that look, Subaru turned his gaze instead to the small figure standing beside Ram. It was Rem, dressed now in outdoor clothes, seated in the wheelchair Petra was pushing — the one Subaru had recreated from memories of his old world.

Wheelchair prototypes had originally existed in this world too, but this one had been carefully refined — caster-style front wheels that wouldn't obstruct turning, a modified footrest and seat, and of course a seatbelt and cushioning to protect the head, all already installed.

It was a one-of-a-kind creation, made by badgering Roswaal and pouring Subaru's own wages into it.

"Going this far out, I'm a little worried about maintenance..."

"Given Subaru-sama's dexterity and the quality of materials used, I believe it should hold up to use over the course of two months. Of course, please refrain from pushing it too hard."

"Please be careful with it, Subaru... -sama."

With Frederica's assurance secured, Subaru took the wheelchair's handle from Petra. Taking hold of it and moving around behind Rem, it was far easier than carrying her on his back. Of course, this assumed paved roads — it wouldn't be much use across sand, grassland, or gravel.

"Okay, I think I can handle this well enough. Petra, Frederica, take good care of yourselves too."

"Please leave taking care of the master to us."

"And looking after Otto-san and Garfiel-san too, right?"

Petra and Frederica nodded in response to Subaru maneuvering the wheelchair. With the journey expected to take two whole months, Otto and the others recuperating in Priestella would presumably finish their treatment and return to Roswaal Manor sometime during that span. Entrusting matters during his absence to the reliable maids, Subaru turned the wheelchair back toward the carriage.

"Well then, hate to say goodbye, but let's get this show on the road."

"Why should Ram take orders from Barusu. Don't get carried away."

"It's not orders or anything, but fine, you want to take charge?"

"Does Ram really strike you as someone that presumptuous? Please don't make Ram, a maid for whom grace and consideration are essential, act like that."

"What is with you? Are you just at that age where you want to complain about everything?"

"That's not it at all. Just—"

Cutting herself off there, Ram gave Subaru's hands a meaningful look. Subaru's hands were, at that moment, occupied pushing Rem's wheelchair. Catching the intent behind that gaze, Subaru let out a long sigh.

"If you want to switch, just say so..."

"Considering the meaning and purpose of Ram's presence on this journey, it's only natural you should yield that spot. ...The fact that you picked up on it without needing it spelled out — Ram supposes she can revise her opinion of you, if only slightly."

Subaru reluctantly gave up his place, and Ram took over pushing the wheelchair in his stead. As if tending gently to her sleeping sister, the wheelchair moved slowly forward toward the carriage.

Watching her back as she went, Subaru idly opened and closed his now-free hand for no particular reason. Just then, a small palm reached quietly from the side and took hold of it.

"Bea-tan?"

"No need for that pathetic look — it's not as though I'm losing to my sister when it comes to caring about that girl, so there's no need to worry, I suppose. Subaru can just do what Subaru can do, is all."

"It's not really like that... or, wait, is it exactly like that?"

It wasn't that he'd felt his role had been taken from him, but he supposed his expression must have looked that way to onlookers. With his free hand, Subaru touched his cheek, pulling and pinching at it experimentally. Just then, that hand too was seized — this time by a different pale hand.

"There, there, I'll just take that hand before it gets up to any more mischief."

"O-oof, Emilia-tan..."

"It'll be quite the spectacle to see what Subaru does once that girl wakes up and both his hands are already full, I suppose."

"Ah, now I'm curious too."

Sandwiched between Emilia and Beatrice, Subaru gave the two of them a helpless look. What came back at him, though, were only a sidelong glare and an amused glance. On top of that, he could feel Petra's gaze stabbing into his back, and even Ram, turning around in front of the carriage, shot him a look of cold contempt.

Surrounded on all sides — and watching all this unfold, Julius gave a nod, murmuring, "Hmm." At that dry reaction, Subaru's mouth twisted into a grand grimace.

"What, if you've got something to say, spit it out. Go on, say it!"

"Is that so? Then, just the one thing. — Surrounded by such lovely women, truly, what a man's dream come true. Though whether those two hands of yours can satisfy the full bouquet of blossoms — that, I find myself doubting very much."

"What is this, am I the one being blamed here!? Did I do something wrong!?"

At Julius shrugging with an exasperated look, Subaru's pathetic wail rose up into the sky. Unfortunately, there was neither an interior minister nor a chief officer present in this moment to come to his defense.

For the two months of the journey to come, it seemed Subaru would have to fight this lonely battle all on his own.

Grasping that fact, warmth and trust and affection flowed through the hands he held — and alongside it, an unease every bit its equal welled up in Subaru's chest, on that morning of departure.
