Arc 3 Chapter 72: "Incomprehension and Understanding"

Now that he thought of it, this was the first time he'd experienced a transposition without losing consciousness in the process.

The disorientation of a foot coming down someplace other than where it ought to be. The alienness of air that should feel familiar meeting his skin as something else entirely. The sense of foreign wrongness that came from gravity ignored, inertia ignored, and the visceral discord that followed.

All of it lumped together, mixed indiscriminately into one wave of nausea.

That was what Subaru felt the instant he came back from his blackout, shoved by Beatrice and sent flying out of the Forbidden Archive.

"—I like to think I already knew you were the type to do something outrageous, but having my common sense casually shattered like this... it's rather difficult to keep my composure."

"I'll show you the next trick at the New Year's talent show. Me and Drill-Loli Girl's combination move. This is inside the village, right?"

"It should be. Incidentally, if my guess is correct, the door you emerged from was, I believe, that of a livestock shed or something similar."

Julius pointed at Subaru's feet, his expression twisting into something like sympathy. Following his finger, Subaru realized he was standing in a heap of bird droppings.

"Transposed from the toilet into a chicken coop covered in crap... they really went out of their way to mess with me in that split second."

Grumbling on and on, Subaru slowly made his way out of the livestock shed, shaking the foot he'd lifted. The chicken-like birds still inside, seeing the sudden intruder depart, began pecking contentedly at their feed as if relieved.

Glancing at them sidelong, Subaru turned his head to survey his surroundings. Sure enough, this was unmistakably the village he'd passed through just a little while ago.

"Was that, perhaps, transposition magic? If so, you've had an extremely rare experience. Magic that connects one space to another is nearly a lost art, even among the shadow-attribute arts. There can't be more than five people alive who can use it."

"Lucky me, huh... nah, that's not the word for it. That idiot girl, what am I gonna do with her...!"

Trading light banter with the admiring Julius, Subaru turned back toward the manor, his eyes bloodshot with anger. What crossed his mind was a girl with cream-colored hair in vertical ringlets, the one who'd cut off their conversation and stormed off to do as she pleased.

The sorrowful expression she'd worn as they parted was burned into his eyelids, refusing to fade.

"Making a face like that and then telling me I 'can't.' Screw around all you like. Fine then, I'll just have to drag her out by force—"

"I'd think twice about that if I were you."

Rolling up his sleeves and rotating his shoulders, working himself up, Subaru was suddenly stopped short by a leisurely voice reaching him. His throat made a small "guh" sound, and his movement froze at the familiar voice.

Slowly, with a stiffness that seemed like it might make his neck crack, Subaru turned around—and found, floating through the air right in front of him, a gray-furred cat.

At the same moment, Julius noticed the presence as well. He straightened his back and bowed politely at the waist.

"It has been some time, Great Spirit."

"You really don't need to be so formal. My status as a spirit is basically just for show, and I don't plan on granting my blessing to anyone but Lia. Yeah, that's me—the freeloader of the spirit world..."

"There is no need for a reason to revere a being of great power. And if I may add—I myself am but a humble Spiritual Arts User. Along with the quasi-spirits within my contract, I have no cause not to hold you in high esteem."

"How elegant. Well said, well said."

Stroking his whiskers with one paw, Puck listened to Julius's absurdly courteous speech. Subaru, meanwhile, gazed up at the pair of them with cold sweat quietly beading on his back.

His heart pounded faster, and a numbness crept through his limbs. There was no sign of Emilia anywhere near Puck. He was moving alone. That fact alone was enough to set Subaru's nerves on edge.

"Being stared at with that much intensity makes even me a little uncomfortable, Subaru. Though I get it—it's been a while since you've seen me, and I'm unbearably cute."

"...As a self-professed connoisseur of soft, furry things, the scary part is I can't fully deny it, damn it."

Utterly unconcerned with Subaru's inner turmoil, Puck looked down at him and said something ridiculous. Riding along with the joke, Subaru shrugged, resisting the paralysis brought on by sheer psychological pressure. Even so, his pulse was still racing.

If he had to put a name to this feeling, Subaru thought, it would have to be fear. Toward this small cat before him—wearing a carefree expression, swishing its long tail, saying foolish things—Subaru now felt undeniable fear.

If this being ever unleashed its true power, Subaru would be swept aside as easily as blowing away dust. He had already experienced that fact, three times over.

Telling himself not to tense up, not to be afraid—that was asking too much.

"So, what did you mean by that just now?"

"By what, exactly?"

"Don't go forgetting your own words the second you say them. ...You stopped me from going to get Bea."

Irritated at Puck, who genuinely seemed to have forgotten what he'd just said, Subaru pushed down his conflicted feelings and asked again. Puck lightly clapped his paws together. "Ah," he said.

"Exactly what I said. If you're thinking of dragging Betty out, you'd better not. You met her, talked to her, and she pushed you away, didn't she? Then that's her answer."

"It's dangerous for her to stay at the manor. There's a high chance the Witch's Cult is gonna go on a rampage. This isn't the time to be leisurely about 'oh, she won't be found'—"

"I don't think anyone could break through Betty's door-crossing, and if it somehow did get broken through, well, that would just be how it goes. That's her answer, after all."

At the words thrown at him, Subaru's eyes went wide despite himself, his breath catching in his throat.

He thought about asking if this was some kind of joke, but the small cat in front of him showed no change in expression or demeanor. Its attitude seemed nothing but sincere.

"Isn't that awfully cold, even so? She adores you that much, and you're just gonna—"

"Of course I think of her like a little sister too. But that kind of family affection and how she chooses her own fate are two completely separate matters. Don't lump them together, please."

"You...!"

At the coldly detached answer, Subaru flared with sudden fury and took a step forward. Unable to believe Puck would take such an attitude, he reached out his arm, intending to grab him and force him to retract it. But—

"Subaru, that's far enough."

"Guh—you're stopping me too? Saying I'm... that I'm wrong...!"

"I don't have the full picture, but that's not it. Trying to change the Great Spirit's mind is a wasted effort. Our values and theirs are different. There's no meeting in the middle."

Julius held out an arm to bar Subaru's advance, shaking his head. As if to prove his words true, Puck, looking down at the enraged Subaru, remained utterly composed and unreadable—as if he felt nothing at all from the exchange.

"What's the matter?"

Blinking his dark eyes, Puck tilted his adorable little head and asked the question.

But it was precisely because his voice, expression, attitude, and mannerisms were no different from usual that it struck Subaru, right now, as something deeply abnormal.

Subaru's relationship with Puck went back a long way, counting from when he'd first been summoned to this world. He'd exchanged plenty of words with Puck, whom he'd encountered around the same time as Emilia, and had come to feel a certain fondness for this not-quite-human being. He'd thought they'd gotten along well up to now.

And yet the inner heart of this person he'd believed he understood so well was now, to Subaru, utterly incomprehensible.

Beneath that appearance, Puck was supposed to have more humanity than he let on—capable of laughing together, of trembling with anger, and even of grieving over someone else's regret.

"If Emilia heard this, she'd definitely be mad."

"—That's none of your business, Subaru."

For the first time, that was a clear word of rejection out of Puck's mouth.

In front of Subaru, who lifted his face, Puck spun around in midair, turning his back. And then—

"In any case, Betty won't be showing her face again. I doubt even the Witch's Cult would think to notice the Forbidden Archive. For her, staying out of sight is probably the safest thing. If you're family, you have to trust her."

"...If you're family, shouldn't you fix it when you think she's making a mistake?"

"Wanting to save and protect everyone, no matter who they are—Subaru, that's a line you get to say once you can properly take care of yourself."

To Subaru's strained, forced-out words, Puck responded with something cutting, then drifted further away.

Subaru could only watch that too-small back recede, gritting his teeth against frustration. Julius, standing beside him, patted his shoulder as if to console him, which prompted—

"I'm not doing this for sympathy. Don't, it just makes it worse."

"Your resolve is admirable, and that is the quality of a knight. But for now, as he said, there is still far too much you lack. —So, what will you do?"

"What will I do, meaning... what about?"

"Everything. The villagers have been persuaded and are already at the stage of boarding the dragon carriages. Lady Emilia and her attendant have been placed in a separate carriage from the villagers. All that remains is for us to finish preparing to depart. ...And what of the person you spoke of earlier?"

While Subaru had been talking with Emilia and Beatrice in the manor, it seemed Julius and the rest of the subjugation force had efficiently pulled things together here.

Evacuation preparations were already complete; all that remained was the lingering regret of the one who'd stubbornly stayed behind at the manor.

"If I take too much time and the evacuation gets delayed, it'll all be for nothing... damn it, I'll hold this against you, Bea. If only you'd just come along quietly."

"Regret can wait. The longer this takes, the more it undermines the calm we've worked to instill in the villagers. I believe now is the watershed moment."

At Julius's point, Subaru bit his lip, wracked with genuine indecision, shook his head, stamped his feet, groaned "Aaah!"—and then said:

"Guided by Ram, the manor's maid, we're evacuating to a place called the Sanctuary, where Roswaal's gone. There's fighting strength there too, so at least our safety should be guaranteed, they say."

"...Are you certain?"

"No, I'm not certain. I'm not certain at all, but... there's nothing else to be done."

Gritting his teeth against the frustration, Subaru turned back toward the manor.

Even now, somewhere behind a door no one would open, in a dark room, was the girl still perched on her stepladder, quietly absorbed in a book?

"You just keep worrying me, you delinquent girl. Please, for the love of god, do not get found... I'll deliver Emilia-tan safely and come straight back for you."

Pressing his hands together, Subaru offered a prayer to a god he didn't even believe in.

Having forced some kind of closure onto his heart through that empty gesture of prayer, he began walking, dragging his lingering, weak-hearted feelings along with him.

"Once we've guided everyone to the Sanctuary, we come straight back and make the manor our base for hunting down the remaining Witch's Cult. Petelgeuse... how's the Archbishop of Sin doing?"

"Wilhelm-sama and Ferris are keeping constant watch on him. If there's any sign of him waking, Wilhelm-sama's strike will follow immediately. And having Ferris probe his mana means faking sleep won't work on him at all—he's the ideal watchman for the job."

"Ahh, good, that's a relief. ...Alright, let's get moving. Patrasche!"

Heading toward the village's exit on the highway side, Subaru raised his hand and called the earth dragon's name. The black earth dragon, whose nickname had thoroughly stuck by now, walked over to Subaru and turned to face him leisurely, as if awaiting its master's mount, offering its back.

"The trust we've built together is honestly the only thing holding me up right now."

"——"

Giving the flank with its prominent hard scales a light pat, Subaru then leapt onto its back—not exactly gracefully, but with reasonably competent motion—and used the reins to get Patrasche walking.

The herd of earth dragons—the merchants' dragon carriages—were already waiting for the order to depart, and surrounding them stood battle-hardened warriors, weapons in hand, watching this way.

"Subaru, they're waiting for your order."

"Mine?"

Suddenly, Julius, who had ridden his earth dragon up alongside him, spoke to Subaru.

As if to confirm his words, the many gazes directed their way sent glances of assent. Naturally, his chest warmed.

Not sure whether the feeling came from embarrassment or something more like elation, Subaru raised his fist high overhead.

"—We're heading for the Sanctuary! Move out!!"

"—Ohh!!"

The overlapping voices of the knights rose high into the sky, and the great migration began.

"I met this wandering mercenary named Yuri, and I was, like, sooo surprised."

Lips pursed, chin resting between her raised knees, Emilia glared at Subaru with narrowed eyes. Her childish gesture was cute to an absurd degree, and Subaru couldn't help but stare, transfixed—prompting Emilia to pout even further and add, "Hey, listen."

"I was, sooo, surprised."

"I'm also currently in the middle of being surprised anew by how cute you are, Emilia-tan, but what about?"

"I'm saying, the wandering mercenary! I thought 'Yuri' was some total stranger, but it's Julius! You two were fighting so hard—when did you make up?"

Raising her voice slightly, she then pointed past the stopped dragon carriage. There, Julius could be seen talking with the beastfolk mercenary band. Spotting him, Subaru scowled with visible distaste.

"We didn't make up or anything. That's the wandering mercenary Yuri-san. Me and that guy have a thin little connection—employer and mercenary, tied together by money, nothing more. Of course we're gonna cooperate."

"You're being stubborn again... boys just don't get this kind of thing. Like how you made up with me—you should make up with Julius too."

"I wanted to make up with you so bad my heart was practically cracking apart, Emilia-tan, but with him, I have zero desire to make up. Him, me, hate, forever."

Chopping out the words one by one, swinging his arms rhythmically, Subaru declared this with a sulky expression. At his stubbornness, Emilia let out an exasperated sigh and shook her head weakly, giving up.

—About four hours had passed since departing from Roswaal Manor and the evacuation had begun.

Having now covered roughly half the distance to their destination, the Sanctuary, they had just reached the entrance to a forest road and had halted the march to take a break.

It was, after all, a mass migration that included no small number of children, the elderly, and women. Pushing them too hard would cause problems—that had been Julius's call, riding at the head of the column.

Given his impatience, Subaru had had his reservations about stopping. But once the break actually began, he was stunned to feel a wave of exhaustion he hadn't even noticed building crash down over his entire body.

Small wonder, really—he hadn't had a single moment of genuine rest since the negotiation with Crusch at the Crusch estate, which was already two days past now.

"Travel time for the whale hunt, plus the actual fight. Then right after that, forming the anti-Witch's Cult team and riding through the night, cutting down Pete-boy, and now here... yeah, no wonder I'm exhausted."

Reflecting on just how much unexpectedly hard labor had piled up, Subaru, utterly worn out, tipped back a container of water. It was handed to him by one of the merchants—a bamboo-tube-like vessel. Whatever magic was at work, the water inside was ice-cold, sliding down his throat and flooding him with vigor.

"Ahh—! This is what I live for, this one cup!"

"Barusu, that's such an old-man thing to say."

Passing by with another such container, mid-service, Ram cut him down with a single line as she walked past, right beside Subaru who was groaning over the refreshing feeling in his throat.

As he glared resentfully at her retreating back, Emilia, seated next to him, gave a small laugh.

"Subaru. Your face has changed sooo much, somehow."

"Really? Well, I guess it makes sense. Honestly the exhaustion's brutal, so the bags under my eyes are probably a disaster right now. Ugh, sleep deprivation is the enemy of good skin."

"That's not what I mean... I feel like, somehow, you've got the face of a man now."

Said with a smile, the words made Subaru's breath catch despite himself. Scratching his head so as not to let that momentary freeze show, turning his face away so she wouldn't see him blushing, he said:

"So you're saying you used to think I had no balls?"

"No balls...? What's that mean?"

"Nuwah, trying to hide my embarrassment and I go and drop an unexpected bombshell instead—those pure eyes hurt!"

Unable to keep up with the vulgar term, Emilia tilted her head, and the sight was as adorable as it was excruciating.

Clutching his chest, writhing dramatically, Subaru found white fingers stroking his silver hair, letting it fall down her back, as she asked:

"Did Beatrice listen to what you had to say?"

"............."

"You saw her, but it didn't go well... is that what that silence means?"

"Was it that obvious on my face?"

"Then I'm right. Fufu, I think I'm starting to understand how you feel, Subaru."

Even as Subaru's brows drooped miserably, Emilia's smile didn't waver. Feeling as though he didn't currently deserve to be smiled at like that—a smile he wanted to keep watching forever—Subaru deliberately looked away. Emilia reached her hand toward his profile, her slender fingers touching his cheek. Startled, he turned his gaze back to find:

"Don't make that face. I know how hard you tried for Beatrice's sake. Even after parting on such bad terms, you still came to me right after. There's no way you could abandon Beatrice."

"She's selfish and way too stubborn, that one. Ran off on her own and shut herself away. Soon as I get you all delivered to Emilia-tan, I'm heading straight back to drag her out and spank her."

"Wouldn't Beatrice get sooo mad if you did that?"

"Compared to the usual Tom-and-Jerry act between us, that's nothing. And Emilia-tan, if there's anything you want me to say to her, I'll say it straight for you."

As Subaru let the banter slide with the rhythm Emilia had helped restore, her expression suddenly stiffened. Noticing, Subaru's eyes widened, and Emilia lowered her gaze slightly.

"Beatrice and I haven't really had the chance to talk much. So I don't really know what I'd even say."

"Not really..."

Starting to speak, Subaru caught himself.

He realized, thinking back over their days at the manor, that he genuinely couldn't recall a single scene of Emilia and Beatrice actually talking.

Whenever Beatrice approached Emilia's side, it was invariably to speak with Puck, who was always with her. Once that was done, the girl would pass right by without a single word to Emilia—not even a glance. And Emilia, it seemed, had simply come to accept that as how things were.

"It's like Beatrice avoids me," Emilia said.

Sensing the reason for Subaru's silence, she offered that answer.

To Subaru, at a loss for words, Emilia let her violet eyes waver as if searching her memory.

"The first time we met at Roswaal's manor, Puck and Beatrice hit it off right away. Though even then, the two of them ended up alone together, so I don't actually know what they talked about."

"Those two have a weird relationship, huh. Acting all sibling-like."

To begin with, the pairing of a small cat as older brother and a little girl as younger sister made no sense at all. Where in the gap between two people with nothing in common could a sibling dynamic possibly fit?

As Subaru tilted his head, Emilia shook hers as well.

"I'm sorry, I don't know that either. Just, Puck told me, as part of our contract, not to pry into his relationship with Beatrice."

"Contract... like the one you make with spirits? Wait, what, you can put something that personal into a contract's terms?"

"It's the standard the spirit themselves uses to judge whether they can truly connect hearts with the practitioner. The content of the contract varies completely depending on the spirit. They say for a spirit of Puck's standing, there's actually supposed to be a really heavy price to pay, but..."

Saying that, Emilia trailed off, looking somewhat awkward. Cut off there without understanding why, Subaru prompted her with "But?" She closed her eyes.

"Puck is sooo, um, easy on me, so most of the conditions are simple ones. Though there are a bit more of them, to make up for it."

"Besides 'don't ask about the thing with Bea,' there's a bunch more?"

"A few. Like, in the morning my hairstyle has to be one Puck picked, doing simple exercises when I wake up and before bed, not being picky about food. Talking with the minor spirits morning and evening, and grooming Puck's fur..."

"That's spoiling you rotten! That's just making you live a healthy, wholesome lifestyle! Spoiling you absolutely rotten!"

So doting was Puck toward his girl that Subaru couldn't help repeating the objection twice. At his reaction, Emilia flushed and looked down.

"That's why I didn't want to say it. I never thought it was weird until I left the forest, but... since living at Roswaal's manor, I've started to think it's maybe a little strange..."

"What is that cat teaching innocent, pure Emilia-tan who doesn't know anything...! It's a problem that it just so happens to be steering her toward exactly my preferences! What is he, some kind of secret matchmaker working in my favor?!"

If Puck was essentially something like a foster parent, then Emilia's current air-headed goddess quality was, in a sense, the fruit of Puck's upbringing. Despite the fairly tense exchange they'd just had, it was disappointing to find himself so ready to forgive the cat, cheap as that was.

In any case—

"That's why I can't get it out of Puck... but for some reason, Beatrice avoids talking to me. I don't even know why. I just don't know."

Sorrowfully, returning to the original topic, Emilia closed her eyes. Her long lashes trembled faintly, and her set expression betrayed that she was holding back sadness.

Something in that face struck a chord in Subaru. Scratching his cheek with a finger, he said:

"Alright, got it. Next time, let's you and me both go pay Bea a visit in the Forbidden Archive together."

"Eh?"

"The Forbidden Archive. That moldy, unhealthy-looking room where book-loving Bea holes herself up. Living her whole life reading books in a place like that—soon her eyesight's gonna go and she'll end up some sickly kid with glasses like the bottom of a milk bottle. Before that happens, let's have her take a page from Emilia-tan, who works hard day and night to maintain her beauty and health!"

In front of her wide, blinking eyes, Subaru clapped his hands, thinking this a stroke of genius.

Puck and Beatrice got along; Puck and Emilia got along too. So if he threw Beatrice into the middle of Emilia carrying out one of Puck's suggestions, wouldn't there be some chance of improving their relationship? And even if it didn't work, getting the result that this particular approach failed would still be progress—progress in the form of "that didn't work."

"You gotta try everything at least once, Emilia-tan. If talking to her gets you run off, then next time try grabbing her instead. She might still run off, sure, but before she does, she's definitely gonna yell some complaint at you real loud. And if you yell your complaint right back at her just as loud... eventually, you'll end up laughing and talking like normal."

"——"

Subaru's characteristically reckless, optimistic communication seminar.

Even Emilia couldn't quite hide her astonishment at the sheer, incomprehensible intensity of it. And after a few seconds frozen in place, she suddenly—

"Fufu."

—put a hand to her mouth and burst out laughing.

"Oh, I got Emilia-tan's smile."

"Aha, honestly, what are you even saying. Subaru is... no, amazing. Subaru's amazing, really amazing. Yeah. Yeah, yeah... amazing."

Thinking it would've been nice to have recorded just the second half of that, in a rather indecent thought, Subaru found himself unexpectedly ticklish at being showered with unbroken praise.

Unaware of Subaru's state, Emilia let out a deep breath.

"Next time, maybe I'll try that. Maybe I'll try talking to her that way, next time."

"Yeah, let's do that. And to make that happen, right now it's full effort on the evacuation. After that, I'm coming back full effort, securing Bea full effort. Then, delivering her to you, Emilia-tan, full effort."

"You're always at full effort, huh."

"I've been learning recently that nothing good comes of half-assing life."

Sticking out his tongue in response, Emilia stuck hers out too in a small imitation. Though it was meant as a mimicry, the difference in cuteness was staggering by a factor of ten thousand, and Subaru was struck dumb by the shock. Leaving the frozen Subaru behind, Emilia said "It's about time," and brushed off the hem of her dress, dirtied from sitting.

"The planned hour of rest is almost up, so I need to get ready. Did you get to rest a little too, Subaru? You're not overdoing it?"

"Thanks to you, Emilia-tan, my relaxation effect is off the charts. E, M—"

"There you go again... it's been a while since I've heard that weird thing you say."

It's nice to have this exchange back after so long, Subaru thought, also brushing off his backside and standing up.

Then, with a twisting motion of his hips, he turned around and, without Emilia noticing, cast his gaze around at their surroundings.

Nearly thirty dragon carriages had been brought to a stop, forming a rest area of considerable scale for this break.

Here and there, merchants and villagers had gathered in their own groups, exchanging anxieties or information among themselves. And in all that time, aside from Ram, who was moving about serving drinks, not a single one of them had turned their steps toward Emilia and Subaru.

If it had been out of consideration for the good mood between the two of them, it would have been a heartwarming thing. But in reality, that wasn't it.

—No one wanted to have anything to do with Emilia, the half-elf.

Even the sturdy knights of the subjugation force, who had overcome hardship together with her, were no exception to that prejudice. For Subaru's sake, they made an effort not to let it show in their expressions or attitudes, but in moments like this, the part of them that naturally rejected her presence still surfaced.

Aside from Subaru and Ram's own circle, the only ones who interacted with her naturally were Julius, Ferris, and Wilhelm, plus Mimi, who didn't seem to think about much of anything at all.

In the middle of an evacuation—in what amounted, one could say, to a shared fate for everyone involved—even so, this was how things stood. The uncertainty of what lay ahead clouded Subaru's expression, and he wiped his clouded face with his palm so Emilia wouldn't see it.

Wiping roughly, flailing his arm, pointing a finger at the sky and spinning around dramatically, he said:

"Alright, guess it's about time to head out again! I know you wanna stay with me, Emilia-tan, but swallow those tears and get back in the carriage for now."

"By the way, Subaru, I feel like the earth dragon you're riding has been glaring at me sooo hard for a while now... it's really taken to you, huh."

His banter slid right past her, and looking where she indicated, he found Patrasche glaring at Emilia. The look on its face seemed almost like the pride of a mount unwilling to let its master be taken away.

"Hey now, partner, jealous of Emilia-tan? Come on, don't be like that—you and me, we're in this together for life. That part I won't budge on. But, well, my soul, I've entrusted to Emilia-t—buhfu!"

Attempting to soothe it in a smooth tone, he was sent flying midway by a swing of Patrasche's tail. Watching Subaru go tumbling vigorously across the ground, Emilia let her shoulders drop in exasperation.

"If you keep saying sweet things to every girl regardless of who it is like that, you're gonna get hurt. You have to save that for someone who really matters, or it's no good."

"M-my heart's number one star is... always... you...!"

"Yeah, yeah. Come on, we're leaving now."

Responding half-heartedly to Subaru as he rolled on the ground clutching his struck side, Emilia climbed into the dragon carriage. Looking around, everyone else was likewise wrapping up their own breaks and getting ready to resume moving.

"Barusu. No matter how tired you are, lying on the ground like that shows a serious lack of self-awareness as one of Roswaal-sama's servants."

Boarding the carriage after Emilia, Ram looked down with contempt at Subaru, sprawled there as if discarded.

After the break, the march toward the Sanctuary resumed.

Unlike the Lifaus Highway leading to the royal capital, this route, running through hilly terrain, was a heavy burden—not just for the earth dragons, but for the riders as well. The repeated slopes were bad enough, but they also had to make their way along forest paths and rough roads with exposed rock faces, and there were times they had to pass through stretches so narrow that barely a single carriage could fit through.

Naturally, the exhaustion weighing on villagers unaccustomed to this kind of march, and on the amateur Subaru as a matter of course, only kept building. Several times, Subaru had to consciously steel himself to keep from snapping under the strain.

"Not counting the break, that's about six and a half hours of travel now... you'd think it's about time we got some idea where this Sanctuary place actually is..."

Leaning his full weight onto Patrasche, aided by his skillful partner's consideration for its rider, Subaru surveyed the tilted world around him with his head lolling to one side.

But ahead, the road stretched out flanked by low hills on both sides once again, and with the far side of the hills out of view, their destination remained as unknown as ever.

"Subaru, I imagine it's about time we get a sense of the destination..."

"Say that ten seconds earlier next time. I wouldn't have had to be disappointed twice."

No doubt the advantage of his training—Julius, still maintaining his graceful bearing without a hint of fatigue, pulled up alongside him and gave a wry smile at Subaru's limp, listless remark.

He smoothed his wind-tossed bangs with a finger, then narrowed his eyes at the same road beneath the hill Subaru had been looking at.

"I see, it looks like another stretch where our marching pace is bound to slacken. —We can't afford to let our guard down."

"...I know. If I were the enemy, this is about when I'd make my move."

At Julius's sharpened tone, Subaru replied in kind, and Julius, glancing sideways at him, showed a faint trace of surprise in his eyes.

"It's surprising to find myself in agreement with you. So you think there will indeed be an attack?"

"Better to assume there will be. It's downright creepy that there's been no movement at all up to this point, and we've still got an Archbishop of Sin in our custody. I'm not banking on any sense of camaraderie or whatever from them, but... these aren't the kind of people who'll let it end here."

"You sound quite confident. I happen to think the same."

Nodding with satisfaction at Subaru's answer, Julius let the hilt of the knight's sword at his waist give a small clink, and turned his gaze toward the middle of the marching column of dragon carriages—toward the one carrying Wilhelm and Ferris, keeping watch over Petelgeuse.

"If killed, he possesses another body—if true, that's an Authority of the most troublesome sort. In the end, our best option is likely to seal him away with the Great Spirit's help."

"Yeah, that's probably it. I figured that was the best option too. Frustrating as it is."

If he were honest, Petelgeuse alone was someone Subaru wanted to see stop breathing for good.

Part of that was his own hatred telling him to do it, but more than that, it was a sense of righteous indignation. He didn't know the full extent of everything the man had done. He didn't know it all, but there was one thing he understood with certainty.

—This man was, without doubt, someone who deserved to die here.

"That's a hell of a way to think. Back in my old world, there's no way I could ever seriously think something like that."

Mouthing off with words like "die" or "kill" was easy enough to do. But since coming to this world, he'd been made to feel, over and over, the weight and value of a life. He didn't think he could ever say those words carelessly again.

And having been made to understand that, and still thinking what he thought now—that man truly was beyond saving.

No—it meant there were people who shouldn't be saved.

—That was when it happened.

"—Subaru!"

"—?!"

Julius suddenly let out a sharp cry, and at his raised hand's signal, Patrasche came to a stop. The swift instruction spread through the whole column, and the march ground to an immediate halt.

The cause was an order from the elderly knight leading the vanguard. He drew his sword, raised it to the sky, and, straining his voice to be heard by all, shouted:

"—Ahead! Numerous figures on the road between the hills! Black robes... it's the Witch's Cult!!"

The report rang out loud and clear, and Subaru and Julius exchanged a glance, nodded to each other, and dashed forward through the column, which was beginning to ripple with confusion, toward the front.

Running, Julius called out, and Subaru, not to be outdone, added his own:

"Act calmly! Knights of the Kingdom, protect the citizens! Iron Fang, follow me!"

"No need to panic, I'll handle whatever comes! We've got a bunch of strong, cool knights on our side, plus a tough, wild band of mercenaries! Now, keep your heads down and protect your mothers, Mild! Petra, you just wait and expect my victory!"

At Julius's command, the knights and mercenaries moved into action, and at Subaru's call, the frightened villagers pulled their faces back into the depths of their carriages.

Confirming this was done, they successfully split the combat personnel from the evacuees, front from back. Racing to the front, Subaru and Julius took in the sight of the deploying Witch's Cult.

Streaming out from beyond the hill, the Witch's Cult—roughly thirty of them in number.

There had been two undiscovered hideouts, and this was slightly more than the expected number of remaining stragglers.

"If I were Petelgeuse, I'd have prepared a separate strike force too, different from the ambush team. Not exactly surprising... how about it, Julius? Can we handle it?"

"More than expected, but we still outnumber them, and this is a frontal clash besides. The Witch's Cult, who lurk in the shadows and act with such cowardice—there's no reason we, as knights, should lose to them."

Drawing his knight's sword, Julius answered Subaru's question with dignified bearing. In step with his movement, the knights drew their weapons one after another, and the mercenaries astride their ligers began licking their lips as if hungry for the scent of blood.

Feeling the swelling tide of battle-readiness, Subaru turned to look behind him.

At the head of the retreating line of carriages was Emilia's. Through the open small window, her silver hair streamed in the wind, and he could see her violet eyes watching him with concern.

Raising a finger toward her as if to reassure her, he then turned his gaze further back still.

There, Petelgeuse remained captive, still silent, and Wilhelm, having dismounted from the carriage, stood ready with his treasured sword drawn, warily alert to threats both within and without.

"Crush that Witch's Cult up ahead, freeze Petelgeuse solid, and it's over. —Looking back on it, damn, what a long road it's been."

But at last, an end to that road seemed to be coming into view.

There was no basis for it. But somewhere, vaguely, his soul simply understood. Every being present here represented, for the other side, their full strength. His heart just knew it.

And so, there was no hesitation at all in throwing everything he had into this.

"We annihilate the Witch's Cult. Everyone—"

Drawing a breath, Julius let out a high, ringing command.

Battle-fervor surged explosively, and at the next shout everyone would charge forward as one—an instant before that,

"————!!"

what rang out was a deafening explosion, its shockwave threatening to burst eardrums.

A tremendous tremor traveled through the ground, and Subaru's upper body swayed, caught in the blast and shockwave. A ringing like struck metal echoed through his skull, and his vision flickered.

"What... the—"

Unable even to make out his own words amid it all, gripped only by the certainty that the source of the blast lay behind him, Subaru turned his head back.

—There, all that remained was the spot where the dragon carriage that should have been carrying Petelgeuse had been blown away without a trace.

"Damn—it!!"

The instant the possibility struck him, Subaru understood his own carelessness.

At the same time, the knights and mercenaries around him, seeing the same thing Subaru had seen, lost their words as well. With their battle-fervor doused like water thrown on a fire, the Witch's Cult approaching from the road beyond the hill—the woman standing at their head twisted her face into a grin.

"Now then, the beginning of the end—Death!!"

With a force and pressure vast enough to blot out the sky, a black hand of witchbeast reached upward.

Reaching upward.

Reaching—upward.
