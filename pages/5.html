Arc 1 Chapter 4: "Repaying the Lap Pillow's Kindness"

Subaru always thought that waking from sleep felt something like breaking the surface of water, face first.

When he opened his eyes, the slanting sunlight seared into them, and he grimaced at the glare as he rubbed them. Waking up came easily to him — once his eyes opened, his mind snapped fully awake almost immediately. That was just how Subaru was built.

"Oh, you're awake?"

The voice came from directly above, from somewhere over the head of Subaru as he lay there.

Turning his face toward the voice, Subaru realized he was lying sprawled on the bare ground, dozing with his head resting against something soft.

"Don't move yet. You hit your head too, so I can't be sure you're all right."

The voice, gentle with concern for him, matched an extraordinary, blissful sensation cushioning his head.

Subaru cast his mind back to the moment right before he'd blacked out, and immediately arrived at the conclusion that he now found himself in a situation extremely blessed from a certain boyish point of view.

—Could it be?! Was this the fabled lap pillow of a beautiful girl?!

Bowing to this heaven-sent blessing, Subaru feigned rolling over in his sleep so he could savor the feel of those thighs.

His cheek traced an arc through the air and arrived at the supreme sensation — and the fluffiness that pushed back against his whole face was even plusher than he'd imagined.

—Huh. Beautiful girls are hairier than I expected, he thought.

"—Wait, no way that's it—!"

"So glad you woke up, so glad indeed (falsetto)."

Objecting even as he did it, Subaru turned his face upward, and this time his fully awakened eyes showed him the world as it really was.

Right in front of him, upside down in his field of view, was the face of an enormous cat.

The cat's face, absurdly endearing, arranged itself into something like a smile.

"It was a stylish little gesture on my part, to let you spend your last moments before waking up happily (falsetto)."

"First things first — cut out that creepy falsetto."

Given the sheer abnormality of being given a lap pillow by a human-sized cat, that was the only request Subaru could manage. Beyond that, since he was already here, he decided to go ahead and enjoy the fluffiness against his cheek.

"Fluffy... so fluffy. What have you gone and made, oh God?"

"Ha, well, being appreciated this much makes it worth the trouble of growing all big, don't you think? Hm?"

The giant cat scratched its head in a bashful gesture, winking one eye as though seeking agreement. At the end of its gaze stood a silver-haired girl at the mouth of the alley, arms crossed in obvious displeasure.

There was no mistaking her — the girl whose image had burned itself into Subaru's memory and eyes in the instant before he'd lost consciousness.

"So that means you're that mini-sized cat from before?"

"Fufufu. Free-adjustable size, easy to carry around. And I brighten up your dull daily life with humor-packed conversation. One to a customer! Your companion for life. For more details, inquire with the Spirit Council."

The cat snapped its fingers with practiced flair as it delivered its sales pitch. The content didn't quite add up, but Subaru figured that was probably just its shtick, and let it go.

Then he shifted his attention to the girl approaching him.

"So, um... you ended up staying with me until I woke up..."

"Don't get the wrong idea. I only stayed because there was something I wanted to ask you — I had no choice. If not for that, I'd have left you behind. I mean it, I would have. So don't misunderstand."

Said so insistently, driven home again and again, even Subaru couldn't find room to push back.

A beautiful girl was bearing down on him with that forceful tone. For Subaru — communication level 1, and opposite-sex communication level 1 on top of that — the pressure was suffocating enough that all he could do was nod along, content ignored.

"So the healing Magic I used on your wounds, and letting you enjoy Puck's belly as a pillow until you woke up — all of it, every bit, was for my own convenience. Which means you're going to pay me back for it."

"Somehow that sounded like you were making a huge deal of doing me a favor, and yet by the end it just looped back around to a totally normal request."

It was reasoning straight out of the old proverb that kindness done for others always circles back around to yourself.

In response to Subaru's reply, the girl kept her stern expression and shook her head.

"That's not it at all. It's completely one-sided. —Now then, you must have some idea who stole my badge, don't you?"

To Subaru, who had hesitantly accepted her terms, the girl asked in a voice that had somehow dropped quieter.

At the content of the question, Subaru couldn't help but tilt his head in confusion.

Honestly, he couldn't shake the feeling that she'd asked him this exact same question right before he blacked out.

"Did I maybe hit my head hard again during the short time I was out?"

"You were only out for five minutes at most, and as far as I know, nothing like that happened."

"So déjà vu, then? Or maybe some hidden power of mine has awakened, letting me live through events just a little bit before they happen?"

Maybe he'd call the ability "Get Lost — as of the Day Before Yesterday."

In the sense of having the right answer ready for any question, it seemed like it might actually come in handy. Or he could go back before a test to prepare for tricky questions that had been bugging him. The possibilities were endless.

"Oh, right, I'm a shut-in!"

"Could you not go off on tangents ignoring what I'm actually asking? Now, the answer to my question."

"Um, well, in that case... I don't really have any idea, I guess..."

A badge, in this context, was probably something like what lawyers, prosecutors, or Self-Defense Force members wore to prove their identity.

Unfortunately, in the roughly hour or so he'd been here, Subaru had zero memory of seeing anything like that.

If he could get home, he probably had a mountain of toy badges he'd collected as a kid, but he had no idea how to get back — and even if he handed those over, he'd probably just end up frozen solid for his trouble.

So there was no way for Subaru to meet whatever expectations she was hoping for.

And yet, the girl showed no sign of disappointment at Subaru's answer, and simply nodded.

"I see. Well, that can't be helped, then. But I did get the information that you know nothing, so I've properly received payment for healing your wounds."

With logic that would make even a con artist blink, she thereby declared her own total loss to be, in fact, a fair trade.

Leaving Subaru dumbfounded behind her, the girl clapped her hands together loudly, as if shaking something off.

"Well then, I'll be going now. Sorry, but I'm in a hurry. Your wounds should all be healed by now, and I don't think those men will bother you again since I threatened them, but walking alone into a deserted alley at a time like this makes you no different from someone with a death wish. Oh, and that's not me worrying about you — that's a warning. If you run into a situation like this again, there's no benefit to me in helping you, so don't go expecting to be rescued."

Rattling all this off in a rapid-fire torrent, the girl seemed to take Subaru's stunned silence as agreement, murmured a satisfied "Right, then," and turned on her heel.

Her long silver hair swayed with the motion, shimmering with an almost fantastical light even in the dim alley.

The sensation he'd been leaning his weight against suddenly vanished, and Subaru scrambled to catch himself before he toppled over.

When he turned to look, the huge, plump cat that should have been there a moment ago was gone — it had shrunk back to the palm-sized form he'd first seen. The cat drifted, buoyant, like a balloon carried on the wind, floating toward the girl's back.

"Sorry about that. She's just not honest with herself, my girl. Try not to think too badly of her."

Following up with a laugh in its voice, the cat landed softly on the girl's shoulder. Her hand stroked its back once, as if confirming the feel of it, and then its form vanished, slipping away as though burrowing into her silver hair.

Watching her brisk, retreating back, Subaru turned the cat's words over and over in his mind.

—The intent behind that girl's words and actions, which apparently weren't honest at all.

She'd apparently been robbed, and had been chasing after whoever stole something precious to her.

In the middle of that, she'd found Subaru — a total stranger — being assaulted, and had cut into her own time chasing the thief just to help him.

On top of that, hugely inconvenienced, she'd treated Subaru's wounds after he collapsed, stood watch over him until he woke, and even repeated a question she'd surely already asked, using it as an excuse for payment — all so Subaru wouldn't feel indebted to her.

This wasn't even on the level of just "not being honest."

Subaru had never before met someone who went out of her way to carry out this much troublesome consideration, and who actually seemed to prefer doing it that way.

For the girl, whatever she'd gained from getting involved with Subaru amounted to precisely zero.

Considering she'd lost the fleeing culprit's trail and had time taken from her to treat Subaru on top of that, in terms of profit and loss, she'd come out overwhelmingly, decisively in the red.

The girl had every right to blame him for it, and Subaru had every obligation to take whatever abuse she threw at him.

And yet, in the end, she hadn't blamed him at all, nor had he heard so much as a word demanding an apology.

Because, to hear her tell it, helping Subaru had all gone exactly according to her own self-interested plan.

"Living like that has gotta cost you like crazy."

Muttering that, Subaru got to his feet and slapped the dust off his tracksuit.

His beloved tracksuit was conspicuously dirty, but showed almost no damage like fraying or tears. And more than that, the pain from all that kicking and beating had vanished completely.

He rolled his shoulders and worked his legs and hips to confirm he was in one piece, and once again felt just how absurd Magic really was.

And likewise, the sheer abnormality of a girl who'd do him this big a favor and settle for next to nothing in return.

"—Hey, wait up!"

He called out to the girl's back as she turned her head at the mouth of the alley, where it opened onto the main street.

She smoothed a hand over her long silver hair and turned around with obvious irritation.

"What? If this is about talking, we're already done. You and I are unrelated strangers now. Complete strangers whose lives just happened to cross for one brief instant."

"Don't say something that stings like that?! And even if you think it's over, I don't think it's over — not even close, not even a little."

Subaru dashed after her as though clinging to the girl's cold gaze.

Somewhere in the back of his mind he thought, this is exactly what a guy chasing after the girl who just dumped him looks like — but he spread both arms wide and blocked her path anyway.

"It's something important to you, right? Let me help too."

"But you don't have anything—"

"Sure, I don't know the name of the guy who stole it, or who they are, or their kinks, but at least I know what they look like! A pretty girl with blonde hair and a prominent fang! Shorter than you, smaller chest too, and I'd guess two or three years younger — something like that, really!?"

Getting flustered and talking a mile a minute, hype climbing without limit — that was one of Subaru's bad habits.

That habit was in full effect this time too, and frankly, he was creeped out by his own words.

—Kinks, chest size, way too much unnecessary information there. And on top of that, "pretty girl," "really" — what nationality did he think he was, anyway? He'd given up on English back in his first year of middle school.

The summer of his first year in middle school, during his very first summer vacation, he'd lost his English textbook, and ever since then Subaru had unilaterally cut ties with foreign loanwords. A tiny, personal version of national isolation.

And here he was, with what shameless nerve, tossing off stylish English in casual conversation—

Tension and regret were dragging Subaru toward a long, rambling trip down memory lane.

His back was drenched in cold sweat. His arms were a disaster zone of armpit and palm sweat. Heart pounding, breath short, dizzy and anemic, stuffed-up nose and a splitting headache — he was surrounded on all sides.

What rescued him from this self-inflicted, do-or-die crisis was—

"—You're a weird person."

It was the girl's voice, her hand at her mouth, tilting her head slightly as though looking at some rare, exotic creature.

She fixed Subaru with a look, as though appraising his worth.

"I'll tell you now, I can't offer any kind of reward. Believe it or not, I'm flat broke."

"Everything got taken, you see."

"Relax. I'm pretty much broke myself."

"There's nothing reassuring about that at all."

Deliberately ignoring the frequent interjections, Subaru thumped his own chest with a solid thud.

"Besides, I don't need any reward. In the first place, I want to help because I want to thank you."

"I haven't done anything worth being thanked for. As for the wound, I already got properly paid for that."

The girl remained stubbornly unyielding.

At her obstinate attitude, Subaru gave a wry smile, and prefacing it with "In that case," said—

"I'll help you for my own sake too. My goal is — yeah. That's it. It's to build up good deeds!"

"Good deeds?"

"That's right, if you rack up enough of them, you get to go to heaven after you die. And apparently a dream life of eating, sleeping, and being lazy is waiting for me there. So, for the sake of that, let me help you."

Even he wasn't sure what he was talking about anymore, but he'd said everything he wanted to say.

Subaru wore the look of a man who'd given it his all, while the girl's face turned thoughtful. But then the gray cat perched on her shoulder poked her cheek with its paw pad, and said—

"I don't sense any ill intent from him, and I think you'd be better off just accepting it honestly, don't you? Searching with absolutely no leads at all, given how big the royal capital is, is nothing short of reckless."

"But... I..."

"I think being stubborn is cute and all, but I think it'd be pretty foolish to let your stubbornness make you lose sight of your goal. I don't want to think my girl is a fool, you know."

At the little cat's provocative scolding, delivered with a shrug, the girl's brows rose.

Then, for several seconds, she agonized — "auu—," "unnn," "but—" — in a strangely alluring way, before finally—

"—I really can't give you any kind of reward, all right?"

So she said, and took the hand Subaru held out to her.

  • "一昨日きやがれ" is a pun on the common Japanese brush-off "一昨日来やがれ" ("get lost and never come back," lit. I adapted it as "Get Lost — as of the Day Before Yesterday" to preserve the time-paradox joke; the original insult's rudeness/register is only approximated.
  • "プリティーガール"/"リアリー" (Subaru inserting English loanwords into Japanese, then mocking himself for it despite claiming to have given up on English) loses its bilingual joke when the whole text is already in English. Rendered as "pretty girl"/"really" without typographic distinction; the following meta-commentary about his sudden "English" still makes the joke legible, but it's weaker than in the source.
  • 徽章 rendered as "badge" (not in the provided term list) per context (ID badge, like a lawyer's or officer's).
  • "氷塊の餌食にされるだけだろう" rendered as "he'd probably just end up frozen solid for his trouble" — likely a foreshadowing reference to being frozen by ice Magic; kept idiomatic rather than literal ("fed to a block of ice").
  • Title rendered as "Repaying the Lap Pillow's Kindness" — 膝枕 (lap pillow) is later revealed to actually be パックの腹枕 (Puck's belly as a pillow); I kept "lap pillow" in the title since that's the term used in the chapter title itself, but flag the lap/belly distinction as intentional wordplay in the text.
