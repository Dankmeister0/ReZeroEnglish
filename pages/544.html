Arc 7 Chapter 46: "Young Recklessness"

"The people of the city have the same power as Yorna...!?"

Racing down the alley with everything he had, Subaru's eyes went wide at the words Abel had just told him.
The fox-woman he'd faced atop the tenshukaku of the Crimson Lyuri Castle, the one who'd hinted at unfathomable depths — the very existence you might call the empress who ruled this magical city — Subaru had no way of knowing the full extent of her power.
But she'd been chosen as one of the "Nine Divine Generals," and had survived — been allowed to survive — however many times she'd rebelled against the emperor, so there was no mistaking that she was, in fact, very strong.
And that same, very strong Yorna, and everyone in the city possessed exactly the same power as her?

"There's no way something that terrifying could be true! A 'Soul Marriage Technique,' seriously...!"

"You think I'm spouting nonsense? Then explain how your limbs shrank. The Kingdom and the Empire differ in the foundations of their arts and the training cultivated upon them. Naturally, there will be phenomena beyond your imagining. Lost arts are too numerous to count."

"Even if you say that..."

Faced with such difficult words, Subaru bit the inside of his cheek in frustration and hung his head.
His legs tangled and he nearly tripped, but he fought through it so as not to be left behind by his companions. With their bodies shrunk, none of them — not Subaru, not Al, not Medium — had shoes that fit their feet properly anymore. So their feet were wrapped in cloth, makeshift tabi of a sort.
If he fell, or if the wrapping came undone, his feet would stop moving. That could not happen.

Even if he couldn't keep up with the conversation, he had to at least keep his body moving.

"That said, I too had considered it a possibility, but I hadn't truly believed it."

"...From the way you're talking, does that mean even you, Abel-chan, didn't know that older sister was a user of the 'Soul Marriage Technique'?"

"Even now I'm still doubting whether there's any other explanation. That is how far beyond sanity this act is."

Abel answered, his breath coming lightly ragged, the demon mask's gaze fixed straight ahead as he ran. At his words, Al growled low in his throat, and Medium turned her face toward Abel.
Even shrunken, Medium seemed to be the one with the most physical stamina left. She was still fine for now, but Subaru would be out of breath before long. Running away indefinitely wasn't going to be easy.

"—Stop. For the moment, it seems we've lost the ones who were near the inn."

It probably wasn't out of consideration for him, but Abel suddenly brought everyone to a halt.
With the mouth of the alley just ahead, the five of them hid themselves in the shadow of a jumble of scrap materials, catching their breath while keeping watch on the street.

"I wonder if Taritta-chan's okay. She could move way better than I thought."

"Eh? Ah, yeah, she was amazing. The way she was bouncing around, bam, bam, loosing arrows..."

"Bro, is that on purpose, or...?"

"On purpose? What do you mean?"

Subaru tilted his head, frowning at Al's question.
Had he said something strange? He'd just been praising Taritta's efforts in his own way, matching Medium's admiration.

At that answer, Al shook his head, muttering "Never mind" in a tone that suggested it was anything but nothing, then turned to face Abel instead. And then,

"That thing you said before, about it not being sane — what did you mean by that?"

"—I already told you. The 'Soul Marriage Technique' cedes a portion of one's own soul to another. The soul is, in essence, the Od at the root of a being."

"Od... ceding it, huh."

Al repeated the words, his voice trembling at the implication.
Od, as Abel had said, was the root of one's existence — not just the body, but the center of something far more spiritual, something like the wellspring from which Mana flowed.
In this world it was often treated as the same thing as the "soul." So the "Soul Marriage Technique" might just as well be called the "Od Marriage Technique." Though he wasn't entirely confident about that.

"But why would you get stronger from souls sticking together?"

"To give a close analogy — the relationship between a Spiritual Arts user and a spirit. Through forming a Contract, the practitioner and the spirit connect to each other's root. In doing so, it becomes a relationship in which power is drawn from one another. Physical distance has no bearing on it. You should have some idea of this yourself."

"A relationship where you draw power from each other — yeah, I get that much, but..."

Brought up in the context of Spiritual Arts users, Subaru's hand went to clutch tightly at his own chest.
It wasn't an ability he could boast much about, but Subaru was, in his own small way, a Spiritual Arts user too. He'd formed a Contract with a cute girl in a dress — Beatrice — and could do various things by borrowing her power. Not much, but still.
And, right, Emilia was a Spiritual Arts user too. He hadn't seen that little cat in a while, though.

"But what's wrong, Subaru-chin?"

"Ah, um, well... making a promise with a spirit is a really big deal, so doing the same thing — and with tons of people, too! That's, a little..."

"Mm-hmm."

"I th-think it'd be really hard, is what I mean."

To Medium, a skilled listener who drew things out gently and patiently rather than rushing him, Subaru honestly voiced what he'd been thinking.
The contractual bond with a spirit was something very precious, and doing the same thing with many different partners seemed difficult. At the very least, Subaru couldn't imagine doing it with anyone but Beatrice.
It wasn't just that Beatrice was originally the kind of child suited for that sort of bond — he found himself thinking that even setting that aside, it wouldn't have been possible.
And hearing Subaru's doubt, Abel gave a nod. "That's it."

"That is precisely why I called it an act beyond sanity."

"You mean the 'Soul Marriage Technique' itself is the dangerous part?"

"You may think of the 'Soul Marriage Technique' itself as roughly equivalent to the sort of contractual bond formed with a spirit, except it can be performed on any target without discrimination. But performing it on an unspecified multitude of people — that is the part that beggars belief."

"——"

"I said 'a portion,' but regardless, it is still a ceding of the soul. That is nothing less than tearing apart the very foundation of one's own being into countless pieces and handing it over indiscriminately. To maintain one's sense of self while doing so is not something that could be accomplished by ordinary means. Or perhaps... no, that line of thought is pointless."

Shaking his head slowly, Abel described the abnormality of Yorna's "Soul Marriage Technique."
Honestly, Subaru didn't grasp the situation as clearly as Abel seemed to, but he understood well enough that it was something truly terrifying.

For Subaru, it would be like giving away his own soul to countless people other than Beatrice. Of course, there were people even Subaru could imagine handing his soul to without fear of them doing anything wrong with it.
Medium and Al here certainly wouldn't misuse it. But if he were asked whether he could hand it to Abel, he wanted to whine and refuse.
And besides that—

"Uu..."

"—Ah."

A small voice growled, and Subaru's attention was seized by the one tugging gently at his hem.
Rui, her long golden hair tied up at the back of her head, had followed along obediently with their flight thus far. No — not just thus far.
Rui had been surprisingly well-behaved, not even raising her voice loudly the way he'd worried she might. She hadn't drawn the enemy's attention that way, and she'd done what was needed, when it was needed.
And—

"——"

Even now, the way she tugged at Subaru's hem wasn't to make Subaru deal with her own anxiety — it seemed more like she was searching for some way to ease Subaru's own unease.

"You..."

She was a hateful thing, a frightening thing, an unforgivable thing.
He'd already thought that through, more than enough, over and over, and he had no doubts about it.
The reason he hadn't abandoned her on the spot when the two of them were flung together into the Vollachia Empire was that the girl — Rem — had been trying to treat Rui with care.
Even now, the only reason he stayed with Rui was that he didn't want Rem to hate him for it.
And yet—

"—Well, this settles it, doesn't it?"

While Subaru wrestled with himself, unable to shake off Rui's hand, Al suddenly spoke up.
Al's gaze was directed at Abel, the demon mask and the covered face, the adult and the child, facing each other. Under Abel's gaze, Al pointed his thumb at his own chest.

"The people who attacked us are under the influence of big sis Yorna's 'Soul Marriage Technique.' Which means big sis Yorna's the one who set 'em on us. Q.E.D., right?"

"—That would be the most straightforward answer one could draw from the situation as it stands."

"You've got a funny way of putting it. If the ones coming after us are using big sis Yorna's power, there's no other option, is there?"

"Th-that might not be quite right..."

"Ah?"

Al reacted to Subaru's tentative interjection with a rough edge in his voice. Seeing Subaru flinch, shoulders jumping at the sound, Al touched the sheath of the qinglong blade on his back and said, "Sorry."

"Didn't mean to scare you. ...Bro, you're saying there's another possibility?"

"...Beatrice and I, we're in a Contract, but I'm not connected to her all the time. I mean, we are connected, but I don't know what she's doing right now, so..."

"So somebody could borrow the power without permission and go on a rampage on the sly, that it?"

At Al's blunt way of putting it, Subaru felt a pang of resistance but nodded anyway.
Even without knowing the exact scope and conditions of the "Soul Marriage Technique," that kind of mischief might well be possible. If so, then whether Yorna was truly the villain here became uncertain again.

"If, in the worst case, the enemy is Yorna Mishigure, then there's no recourse but to flee the magical city. Continuing the contest with Olbart would also become impossible."

"We'd end up hunted through the whole city. I agree with you there, but..."

"—Capture one of them, and the picture changes."

Touching his own cheek through the demon mask, Abel murmured this quietly.
Hearing it, Subaru opened his mouth in an "ah" of realization.

"Th-that's right! If we could just get one of them to talk, that'd settle it! Why didn't I think of that!"

"Ah, it's true~! Abel-chin, genius! That's it, that's it!"

"Right!"

At the answer that seemed to part the clouds, Subaru and Medium seized on it with delight. Clapping their raised hands together, the two turned back to Abel and the others in high spirits.
But their joy was cut short by the two who kept their faces hidden, Abel and Al.
Al let his shoulders slump at the excited pair.

"Yeah, well, if we could do that, we wouldn't be in this mess. That's the hard part, see. The other side, every single one of 'em's got big sis Yorna's power now."

"Oh, right..."

"Uuh, if only Taritta-chan were here, maybe we could've..."

Stamping her foot on the spot, Medium bemoaned their lack of ability.
Subaru shared her frustration. They were one step away from moving this situation forward, but they lacked the information, and they lacked any means of obtaining it.

"Something, isn't there something..."

Hand at his mouth, Subaru racked his brain trying to recall everything that had happened so far.
Since arriving in Chaosflame, all the various events and troubles that had occurred in this magical city. Meeting Yorna and the fake emperors at the Crimson Lyuri Castle. The next morning, the sudden "Infantilization," and the "hide-and-seek" proposed by the "Vicious Old Man," and the frightening people lying in ambush—

While Taritta was buying them time, he had to find some way to break the deadlock.
Otherwise, no matter how capable Taritta was, she'd be caught by that huge crowd and made to suffer—

"—Huh."

"Subaru-chin?"

In the middle of his turning thoughts, something odd snagged at him, and Subaru blinked. Noticing his expression, Medium peered into his face.
But he had no room to spare to answer her call. He felt a pressing urgency to reel in whatever it was that had just snagged his attention.

What had snagged was Taritta, who was working so hard for them. No — not Taritta herself, but the people facing her, the hundred frightening people targeting them.
He'd watched that hundred fighting Taritta from a distance. Something odd had caught there. It was, probably—

"Honestly! We don't even know if it's really the 'Soul Marriage Technique,' or if the enemy really is big sis Yorna or not, or if old man Olbart's even telling the truth!"

"—!"

Scratching roughly at the back of his neck, Al snarled this out, and Subaru flinched in surprise. But Al didn't seem to notice Subaru's surprise, kicking at the wall in impatience.
Somehow, the composure had drained out of Al entirely. The way he usually let everything roll off him like a willow branch swaying in the wind felt like an illusion now.
And still carrying that irritation, Al stepped right up into Abel's face.

"What are we gonna do, Abel-chan. I can't keep going along with this at this rate. Let me tell you, I've got things I'm prioritizing over your restoration to power. So..."

"I have no interest in your desires. But there is a way to confirm it."

"Confirm what, exactly?"

"Whether those people's toughness comes from the 'Soul Marriage Technique' or not."

Abel said this to Al, who was pressing in on him, and pointed out of the alley.
From beyond the darkness, from the street, came the scattered sense of people passing to and fro, footsteps, voices in conversation. And yet, no one seemed to have noticed them or be peering in.
Just the ordinary bustle of the crowd, nothing more.

"What about the street..."

"Choose one passerby at random and inflict harm on them without warning. Doing so will let us determine whether Yorna Mishigure's 'Soul Marriage Technique' encompasses every single person in the city, or not."

"——"

"If the wound heals, they are under the influence of the 'Soul Marriage Technique.' If they are injured, or die, then they are outside its influence — meaning the hypothesis that they are a wielder of the 'Soul Marriage Technique' can be ruled out as mistaken."

Flatly, still pointing out at the street, Abel continued.
At the sheer violence and outlandishness of the proposal, Subaru was struck speechless, unable to say anything. His brain went numb, having all but given up on thinking entirely.
Such an absurd idea, surely no one would take it seriously—

"I see... but there's also the chance they're not counted as residents, like us. What do we do if we draw that card?"

"One can distinguish residents from outsiders by their footwear. Of course, there is also the possibility that only those who surrounded the inn are under the influence of the 'Soul Marriage Technique.' In that case, it would serve as confirmation that Yorna Mishigure deliberately set this scheme in motion against us."

Either way, information would be gained — so Abel's nod seemed to say, and Al fell silent, clearly finding it worth considering.
It was a discussion wrapped in convoluted logic, hard for Subaru to follow. But even within the hard-to-follow parts, there was one thing he understood clearly.
It was that, at this rate, someone was going to get hurt.

"That's just—"

"—If you refuse it, then what? Offer an alternative."

"...!"

Abel looked down at Subaru, cutting off his reflexive urge to bite back before he could even speak.
Feeling as though his thoughts had been read and dismissed as shallow, Subaru's face burned hot. But he couldn't let himself be silenced. He didn't want to shut up and back down.

"W-without hurting anyone, there's got to be some other way..."

"If there is one, say it. I will listen to any proposal worth considering. —Though, I suppose expecting that of you as you are now would be cruel."

"That's—!"

"—You are one who fears, above all else, the emergence of sacrifice."

The cold voice pierced through Subaru's heated emotions like a blade.
Feeling a pain as if he'd truly been shot through, Subaru bit down on his back teeth and looked down.

Sacrifice — someone getting hurt, someone dying.
Of course he wanted as few deaths and injuries as possible, allies and enemies alike. To think that way, he was told, was because Subaru was naive, green, weak.

"But why is that supposed to be so wrong?"

"It is not a denial. Merely a fact. To achieve one's desires to the fullest extent requires either cunning or strength. Lacking either, one has no choice but to whittle down the desired outcome and compromise. That is the process and the result of the bloodless surrender you yourself spoke of."

"——"

"If you wish for neither death nor injury, you require the power to bring about that outcome. —If you truly desire it from the bottom of your heart, then to hold back is a contradiction."

Called out for holding back, Subaru's cheeks stiffened, and he blinked.
He couldn't grasp Abel's intent. Only the black eyes behind the demon mask fixed on Subaru, seeking out his true heart in the depths of a gaze that carried a light incomparably weaker than his own.

He didn't understand, didn't understand, didn't understand.
He truly didn't understand. What was this "holding back" of his? However hard he worked his sluggish mind, no way to fix things presented itself.
With short limbs and small lungs, he couldn't leap and bound the way Taritta could. The whip he'd brought was too thick in the grip, too heavy to wield properly.
And yet, if he didn't want anyone hurt, then for that—

"—Then it's fine if I get hurt, isn't it!"

His mind a complete jumble, Subaru found himself shouting this at Abel.
His lack of strength, his lack of foresight — he knew it all. Because it was a problem that had been with Subaru all along, long before his body had ever shrunk like this.

Something hot welled up from behind his tightly shut eyes.
Before it could clearly become tears, Subaru turned his back and took off running.

"Bro!?"
"Subaru-chin!"

At the sudden movement, Al and Medium cried out as if in alarm.
But without stopping at either of their calls, Subaru threw himself out of the alley, into the street.

He didn't want anyone hurt. Didn't want anyone to die.
The strength and cunning necessary for that — Subaru lacked both. If something had to be given in exchange to make up that lack, it would be Subaru himself.

He would confirm it. He'd bring back the information that could change this situation, bring it back in the moment it could still be reclaimed.
For that—

"Come at me! I'm right here!"

Leaping out into the street, Subaru spread both arms and shouted at the top of his voice.
A child's shrill voice cut through the street's clamor, drawing the startled attention of those around. Caught by so many wide, staring eyes, Subaru felt his heart shrink with pain.

"—Ah."

He regretted it the instant he'd done it, realizing he'd acted too hastily.
The eyes around him, the eyes of people watching a foolish child do something reckless, stirred a sudden, mounting shame and fear in him. He held his breath, thinking he might as well just turn and dash back into the alley—

"Wait."

As he panicked and shifted his footing to do just that, someone grabbed his arm.
Shoulders jumping, Subaru spun around — the one holding his arm was a short figure. A boy in plain, light clothing, with short curly hair — no, his hair wasn't cut short; it just looked short because the curls were so tight they coiled up close to his head.
A boy of about the same apparent age as Subaru currently was. Subaru's eyes widened at his sudden appearance, and he opened his mouth to ask why the boy had stopped him, when—

"Sorry."

—In the next instant, the boy, his right eye ablaze, sent him flying with an effortless throw.

"—Eh."

Subaru had no idea what had happened, swallowed up by a spinning, whirling field of vision.
Suddenly, the boy had thrown him. With a motion like swinging up the arm he'd grabbed, he'd tossed him into the sky, easily, simply.

"W-waaaaaaah—!?"

He'd charged out into the street, determined that it was better him than anyone else getting hurt.
And yet, no sooner had he regretted it and tried to retreat, than even that retreat had failed — he'd been thrown, and now he was screaming.

Why, how — the questions bounced around in his skull, a sensation like his insides being wrung out.

The people fighting Taritta had had the same burning eyes as this boy. If that was the effect of the "Soul Marriage Technique" Abel had spoken of, then they'd been granted a portion of Yorna's power. To confirm that, he'd charged out, bravely offering himself as the sacrifice.

None of it made sense to Subaru, spinning and falling through the blue sky.

Spinning around and around with unnatural force, it seemed a miracle his body didn't strike any of the surrounding buildings or decorations. Whether the boy had aimed for that or not, he had no idea.
Only, the obstacles rushing past the edge of his vision at terrifying speed were frightening, and he fell toward the blue sky desperately praying not to hit anything — and then, that too came to an abrupt stop.

Slowly, the thrown body of Subaru reached the peak of its arc, and now it began to fall, headfirst, straight down toward the ground.

"Aaaaaah—!"

The voice that had risen away from the ground now echoed as it drew closer to it once more.
He didn't know how high he'd been thrown, but he had to break his fall somehow. Yet he couldn't recall on the spot how to take a proper fall. To begin with, would knowing how to fall even matter, dropping from such a terrible height? Falling straight to the ground like this, his body might be torn apart, his head crushed, his neck broken, and he'd die—

—And it felt as if "Death" were about to come very close again.

"Al-chin!"

"I know, damn it!"

Right after, mixed in with Subaru's own scream, he heard familiar voices straining with effort.
Immediately after, a sound like something taut being cut, and the sound of several long, slender things falling in a chain, their nature unclear — and before Subaru's body hit the ground, it was caught.

"W-waaah, waaah... oof!"

Caught by something soft, but unable to fully kill his momentum, Subaru's body rolled across that softness at considerable speed. Reaching the edge of it, his body was flung out and rolled onto the hard ground, a groan and the taste of sand filling his mouth.
Everywhere, his body ached. But—

"Am... I alive...?"

At the realization that he hadn't died after all, Subaru let out a stunned voice.
Managing to sit himself up, he finally understood what had happened. —Al and Medium, the two of them, had made a split-second decision to save him.

To catch Subaru falling from the sky, the two had leapt at a stall's tent set up in the street, cut it free from its moorings, and stretched it taut to serve as a makeshift cushion.
Thanks to that, Subaru had been spared being slammed into the ground, his life saved.
However—

"Ah, ow ow ow..."
"Ugh...!"

The two of them, having worked so hard to catch him, hadn't come through unscathed.

"A-Al! Medium-san!"

In Subaru's field of view as he sat up, the two of them lay collapsed in the street.
Of course, Al and Medium, still under the effects of "Infantilization," couldn't perform as they normally would. Stretching out the tent to catch the falling Subaru, they'd been sent flying by the impact.
And the bad news wasn't finished yet—

"I wanted it to end with that..."

Speaking these words, his face twisted in anguish, was the boy who had thrown Subaru.
He looked over Subaru, and then Al and Medium, both likewise fallen — and covered his anguished face with his palm. —Though the flame in his right eye remained, uncovered.

"Y-you..."

"It's necessary. This city... we can't afford to lose that person."

"—?"

Somehow pulling himself up, Subaru tried to draw the boy's attention to himself.
It was born purely of the desperate wish to keep Al and Medium from being harmed, but the boy's answer lay far outside Subaru's imagining, far from understanding. Still, along with the boy's evident desperation, one more thing stirred Subaru's heart.

"You too..."

It was the same thing that had snagged at him back in the alley, when he'd been racking his brain.
Taritta, fighting to buy them time to escape, and the people with the burning single eye who'd faced her — their expressions—

—they'd all, it seemed to him now, worn the same pained look as this boy.

"I'm sorry. I won't ask you to forgive me."

Shaking his head, the boy stepped slowly forward, doubt lingering in his eyes even as his expression itself lost all trace of hesitation.
Even if he tried to crawl backward and flee, he couldn't retreat. His legs wouldn't move.
So at the very least, so as not to look away, Subaru stared fixedly at the boy's face, and noticed.

Amid the boy's curly hair, peeking out just slightly from the side of his head — a small horn.
Seeing the white curls and the horn, Subaru thought, absurdly out of place, that he looked just like a sheep.

That was the last thing he registered before the boy's hand, eyes ablaze, swung down in a great arc—

"——"

Grimacing, eyes open, determined not to miss what the boy did, Subaru watched.

"Uuh!!"

He watched the golden-haired girl who threw herself between him and the boy get sent spinning through the air like a falling leaf, scattering a dull thud and red blood in her wake.
