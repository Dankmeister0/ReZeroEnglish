Arc 10 Chapter 30: "Ballpoint Pen"

By the time Subaru had shed not just the wig and makeup but the dress as well, erasing every trace of the operation, and returned to the great room of the hunting lodge, the others too were just beginning to trickle in, one after another.

The hunting lodge was your typical rustic lodge, but it was far more spacious than the name suggested — plenty roomy enough for a number of people to bed down in. Even so, once the main players from the operation at the Moreau estate all gathered under one roof, it did get a bit cramped.

So when Subaru spotted an unusual face among them, he raised an eyebrow.

"Didn't expect to see 'Black Dog' here too. You never show up to these meetings."

"Kuhahaha, it's not like I'm eager or leaning forward on this one either. It's ju-ust that I've been nagged and nagged to show up for the future-strategy meetings. Such is the misery of serving under someone."

The one cackling as he said this was "Black Dog," seated at the edge of the great room with his back against the wall and his legs sprawled out on the floor. Like Subaru, who'd changed out of his dress, he too had shed his servant's uniform and was back in his usual mask and robe.

Personally, Subaru didn't think the guy needed to hide a face that handsome, but everyone's got their own way of feeling about these things, he supposed.

"Anyway, hearing a guy who's usually such a slacker complain about the misery of service doesn't exactly land. If you don't shape up, it's 'White Sheep' who ends up carrying the load, you know."

"Oi oi, quit it with the fickle, no-shame act already. Half-devil, a duchess, and now a tiny tot in tow — how many more do you need to corral before you're satisfied, pretty boy?"

"It may be true Subaru's fickle, but your attitude is beyond the pale, I suppose. To Betty's eyes, 'White Sheep' looks positively pitiable. Perhaps I should give her a shoulder rub to console her sometime, I suppose."

"I think that's a great idea. Bea-ko's shoulder rubs don't do a lick of good, but they're cute and healing all the same."

"Black Dog" punctuated his snide remark with a thumb drawn across his throat, and Subaru and Beatrice answered in unison with a thumbs-down of booing disapproval.

The gestures themselves looked ominous enough, but this was, believe it or not, the amicable rapport the two months had cultivated. Subaru was the one who'd taught it to him, but "Black Dog" seemed to have taken an unexpected liking to it.

"I'm sorry about 'Black Dog,' both 'Ashen Star' and 'Librarian.' He doesn't mean any... well, I think he does mean it a little, but there's no real malice behind it. Probably. Really."

It was "White Sheep," playing the guardian's role, who apologized in "Black Dog's" stead.

As she held out the teacups in both hands, Subaru gave a wry laugh and said, "No, no, it's fine," gratefully accepting both his and Beatrice's cups.

"It's not something you need to apologize for, White Sheep. He's a grown man — let him clean up after himself. ...Oh, this is good. You've really got a feel for my taste."

"Thank you for saying so! Though, the flavor isn't really my doing — 'Blue Snake' helped me get it just right."

"Hm, Feri—'Blue Snake,' huh."

Catching himself just barely before he blurted out the real name, Subaru looked toward the center of the room.

There stood a simple little table, at which Crusch — who had played such a major role in the operation just now — sat resting with her arms crossed and eyes shut. When a cup was set down in front of her, the eye not covered by her eyepatch opened, and her gaze met Ferris's, who had placed the cup there.

Ferris, who had probably hoped to set it down unnoticed, let out a breath as though resigning himself to it.

"...Your tea, Lady 'Emerald Beauty.'"

"Ah. My thanks."

"How's your body doing?"

"I wouldn't call it good condition, but compared to before, the power's stopped tossing me around quite so much. I suppose I'll just have to keep coaxing it along."

"I see. I made the tea lukewarm, by the way."

"Is that so. I'm no good with anything too hot. That helps."

"I know."

"I see."

With a nod of the head, Crusch brought the tea to her lips, and Ferris took the seat farthest from her across the table, propping his chin on his hand. It was such an unremarkable little exchange, and yet the tension had been at an absolute maximum.

"Ah, I nearly forgot to breathe there..."

"More like Subaru actually stopped breathing, I suppose. Betty got dragged along and felt like she was choking too. Isn't there anything to be done about those two, I wonder?"

"Believe me, I've been wanting to do something about it for two months straight..."

Worries of every stripe were in endless supply these days, but the most immediate one at hand was, as just demonstrated, the friction between Crusch and Ferris.

Subaru only knew the barest surface of the circumstances behind it, but he'd been fumbling along uneasily, wondering just how much help a third party could really be in mending a crack that had formed in a lord-and-vassal bond. What was needed, he figured, was something like the role Otto had played reconciling him and Emilia back at the Sanctuary.

"Since he's not around, I'll say it honestly — that guy's amazing sometimes. How does he even come up with a vision for untangling a mess like this and setting it right?"

"I doubt it's as simple as shutting those two in a box together the way you did with Subaru and Emilia, I suppose."

"If we shoved those two in a box right now, I feel like it'd just be an awkward, unbroken silence..."

Not that Subaru and Emilia had made up simply by being locked in a box, either, to be fair.

Of course, Subaru had made his own efforts to nudge Crusch and Ferris toward reconciliation — trying to address the two of them at the same time whenever possible, or engineering chances for them to be alone together.

But it kept fizzling out — the two of them would just end up communicating through Subaru as a go-between, or their teamwork was so seamless that no conversation was needed at all.

In fact, the operation this time around had originally been proposed with Ferris playing the wife's role instead of Subaru—

"He refused, flat out, absolutely would not wear the dress, I suppose."

"Yeah. Man, if Natsumi hadn't been around, who knows how the operation would've gone. Rachins aside, there's no way in hell 'Black Dog' could pull off a dress."

"...There's no rule saying it absolutely had to be someone in drag, I suppose."

"But it caught the fake viscount off guard, and it let me hide Bea-ko inside the panniers. Not just two birds with one stone but three, with proven results, and we've even got a track record from the Empire. No reason not to do it. — Q.E.D."

"Somehow I feel like I'm being strong-armed by that logic, I suppose...?"

Beatrice pursed her lips, wearing the face of someone at that age full of worries, and Subaru stroked her head. Just then, Russell and the others arrived late, apparently having been talking in another room.

"Pardon the interruption to your pleasant chat. Let's discuss our future course of action, shall we."

So saying, Russell accepted a teacup from "White Sheep." Behind him, Rachins trailed along wearing a thoroughly worn-out expression, and Subaru elbowed him in the side. "Hey."

"What were you two talking about? Cooking up more mischief together, all buddy-buddy?"

"Buddy-buddy? Are your eyes just for show? He was just dumping a pile of petty chores on me. That bastard's got me confused for some kind of slave or something."

"Well, if you mean he holds the power of life and death over us, then I guess you, me, and Bea-ko are all Russell-san's slaves too, in a way."

"If that's true, then the load's way too lopsided onto me! You lot could stand to... ah, forget it. Leaving it to you two would worry me a lot more."

"That's probably exactly the reason why, isn't it."

"Ahh?" Rachins shot back, seemingly with no self-awareness at all about being the type who suffers for everyone, but there was no doubt that very disposition was exactly why Russell kept pinning him with one thing after another.

His manner of speech was rough, but underneath it he was probably the earnest, dutiful-bureaucrat type. Subaru hadn't heard the details, but maybe it had something to do with the surprising detail that he actually came from a distinguished family.

Anyway—

"—We've received word from 'Peach Rabbit.' It looks like we can expect some progress against the 'Beloved Children.'"

At Russell's report, delivered with one hand braced on the little table, the air in the room suddenly tightened.

Naturally, Subaru too sat down with Beatrice settled on his lap, and Russell watched with satisfaction as everyone except "Black Dog," still seated on the floor, took their places at the table.

"Traveling together these past days, 'Emerald Beauty' and the rest of you should understand well enough by now just how dangerous the 'Beloved Children' are. Unless we root them out entirely, the kingdom's collapse is unavoidable."

"Yeah, and it's not just tonight's business. With 'Replacement Dolls' popping up all over the place like this, you can't even hug someone without worrying anymore."

"For Betty, it's a matter of life and death, I suppose."

Subaru exchanged a nod with Beatrice in her usual spot on his lap, biting the inside of his cheek at the thought of just how deeply the "Beloved Children's" evil hand had spread through the kingdom — far beyond anything he'd imagined.

If this problem went unsolved, then even if Emilia managed to claim the crown, there was no way she could build the kind of nation she wanted. This was a dark cloud that Subaru, as someone living in this kingdom — and above all, as a Royal Selection candidate's knight — had to clear away himself.

"So, 'Peach Rabbit,' was it? What's she saying, exactly? That she got hold of a roster of the 'Beloved Children,' or found their headquarters?"

"I understand the hope, but no such roster likely exists. Only their mother holds the whole picture of information... keeping them from sharing information, keeping them isolated, that's one of her methods of discipline, wasn't it?"

"Um, yes. She... was that kind of person."

Touching on a memory that was probably painful even to recall, "White Sheep's" brow dipped slightly.

It was a turn of the conversation that only piled more hatred onto the "Beloved Children's" top figure — Capella of "Lust." But Ferris's hopes weren't so different from Subaru's own. If the fight against the "Beloved Children" was making progress, they wanted to leap at any chance to sweep them all up in one net.

"But if it's not that, then what information did we actually get?"

"—Information suggesting that it might be possible to restore those affected by 'Lust's' Authority to their original state, including the 'Replacement Dolls.'"

—!

In that instant, it was Ferris who sent his chair scraping loudly as he shot to his feet.

With one hand braced on the little table, his eyes went wide and his lips trembled.

"Restore, as in... you can heal them? All of them?"

"It's only a possibility — merely a thread to pull on. Calm yourself, 'Blue Snake.'"

"How could I possibly calm down?! Because if that's true, then..."

Ferris trailed off, head bowing partway through, but Subaru could guess the rest.

Probably something like this: if such a method existed, he never would have needed to rely on the "Divine Dragon Church." And if he hadn't relied on the "Divine Dragon Church," then Crusch would still, even now—

But—

"Regretting the past only ever gets you a sense of helplessness. Reflection is fine, but I can't imagine dwelling on it ever produces a good result."

Ferris's self-recrimination was denied by none other than Crusch herself.

Subaru glanced at Crusch as Ferris's cheeks tensed painfully, but with even her uncovered eye now shut, it didn't look like Crusch was about to give him the answer he wanted.

"As I said, the information we obtained is a thread toward a solution... at the very least, it should help toward uncovering the principles behind 'Lust's' Authority. And once we understand that, a countermeasure should follow."

"As things stand, my Wind Reading Divine Protection can tell truth from falsehood, but it can't save the victims of an Authority. Looking back on my own case, it isn't as though there's no method at all, but—"

"—Honestly, I'd rather avoid relying on the 'Divine Dragon Church's' Sacred Rite as much as possible."

Quiet but brooking no argument, Russell's tone left Subaru involuntarily taken aback.

"I appreciate the honesty, but why's that? I mean, the Sacred Rite's effectiveness is proven, what with... you know, that."

"My own body is proof of its effectiveness. — Filore's Sacred Rite healed me. Though, of course, whether it's fair to equate 'Lust's' Authority with whatever it was eating away at my body remains unknown."

In place of Subaru, who had faltered over his words, Crusch — the one most concerned — spoke for herself.

In response to that question, Russell touched his chin whiskers and cast a brief glance toward "White Sheep." Catching it, "White Sheep" gave a small nod, and said,

"I know about Filore too. Whether that girl is suited to being a Royal Selection candidate aside, there's no question the 'Divine Dragon Church' is an organization that can't be fully trusted. ...When I was one of the 'Beloved Children,' I was also a member of the Church. I'm sure there are others—"

"Even the Church has 'Beloved Children' worked into it... well, I guess that's not exactly shocking anymore, given the higher-ups in the castle already got taken. And besides..."

"—Tiga Rauleon. That one also seemed to be siding with the enemy."

Catching the brief glance Subaru shot her, Crusch nodded and brought up Tiga's name.

Tiga had fought Crusch at Miklotov's mansion, and afterward apparently reported even Subaru — who'd fled together with her — as a co-conspirator in Miklotov's assassination. Given the circumstances, the chances he was cooperating with the "Beloved Children" side were fairly high. — There was even a chance he was one of the "Beloved Children" himself.

"I really thought that guy and I could've been friends... well, no use regretting it now. Anyway, so the point is, the 'Divine Dragon Church' isn't a united front, and the 'Beloved Children' have definitely infiltrated it — so we can't carelessly rely on the Sacred Rite. Is that it?"

"—"

"Huh? 'White Sheep'?"

"Ah, sorry, I spaced out for a moment!"

"White Sheep" answered in a fluster at Subaru's repeated call.

Only, it hadn't looked so much like spacing out to Subaru — more like she was bearing some kind of pain, or thinking of something far away, and that bothered him.

But before Subaru could press the matter, Russell cut in with a cough.

"Setting that aside, I believe it's healthy to keep a certain distance between the royal castle and the 'Divine Dragon Church' regardless. Regardless of the new Royal Selection candidate's wishes, I have no desire to take steps that let the Church's influence grow too large."

"Hah, like we're in any position to be so picky about our methods. If the kingdom falls apart while you're busy playing favorites, that'll be a fine bit of extra shame heaped on the 'Six Tongues,' won't it."

"Naturally, I have no intention of misjudging our priorities. If there's no other move to make, we'll have no choice but to rely on the 'Divine Dragon Church's' Sacred Rite even so. Though, that couldn't be called a realistic option unless we can first root out the enemy lurking within the Church."

"—So then, what about this and that? Let's hear this so-called realistic and feasible plan of yours, then."

It was "Black Dog" who tossed out that jab at Russell, having taken over the explanation from "White Sheep." Still stretched out on the floor with his long legs sprawled, "Black Dog" laughed off the gathering stares with a "what, what is it,"

"I'm ju-ust sick of a conversation that goes nowhere and never moves forward, is a-all. C'mon, talk, talk, open up already! Or else... I'm about at my limit for sleepiness and patience both!"

"—. As I said, if we can just understand the principle behind 'Lust's' Authority, we might be able to chart a path toward a countermeasure even without the Sacred Rite. However, that requires a certain body of knowledge."

"A certain body of knowledge?"

"Yes. Which brings me to you, 'Ashen Star' — I have something to consult you about."

Still not entirely satisfied at having been prompted by "Black Dog," Russell nonetheless pushed the conversation forward, and at his words Subaru pointed at himself and tilted his head.

What in the world could this lead-up be building toward, that ended with a consultation aimed at him?

"If it's something I can help with, sure, but what's the consultation?"

"—I want to get in contact with Roswaal. I'd like to draw on his knowledge. So, would you happen to know of any way to reach him, wherever he's hiding?"

"With Roswaal, I suppose?"

At the sound of that name, Beatrice knit her brow suspiciously.

Just as Emilia and the others were being held in the castle, and Rem and the others had fled the royal capital, Roswaal too had likewise vanished, together with Frederica; Ryuzu and her replica sisters, the "Meyers," from Roswaal Manor; and Sylphy and the rest.

From what Subaru had heard, Roswaal had refused a summons to the royal castle and simply skipped out, so his standing was now firmly on the wanted-fugitive track. — In other words, the whole camp were now marked criminals, every one of them.

"But other than Emilia and Ram, who I hear are still in the castle, we don't know where anyone else is either. Roswaal's no exception. I'd think Russe— 'Silver Fox' would know that too?"

"Yes, that's what I was told. At least, according to the reports that reached me."

"—. So in other words, you suspect that Betty and the rest of us are hiding some means of keeping in contact with Roswaal and the others behind your backs, I suppose."

"If you did, it would only be the most natural sort of precaution to take, so I wouldn't blame you for it."

"It'd be no surprise if you had a method or two of your own for contacting your 'friends,' I suppose."

"Unfortunately, three or four of those seem to have already been shut down. To begin with, I was never able to place any of the 'Six Tongues' around Roswaal, so this really is a genuine case of whereabouts unknown."

Russell shrugged, and Beatrice's gaze remained sharp on him.

But to Subaru, this was a fruitless little standoff. After all, having no way to contact Roswaal was just as true for Subaru's side, and Russell's suspicious streak was simply one of his bad habits. Maybe an occupational hazard.

"By the way, what exactly is this about wanting to borrow Roswaal's knowledge?"

Depending on the answer, they might find a solution without relying on Roswaal at all.

Of course, Russell had probably already thought it through thoroughly and landed on "Roswaal" as the answer, but there was no harm in asking.

Subaru asked with that thought in mind, and Russell narrowed his eyes slightly before answering,

"—Regarding a Witch's research."

"Eh..."

At the word that had just leapt out, Subaru's eyes went wide and he sucked in a breath.

Ignoring Subaru's reaction, Russell nodded and continued—

"It seems one of the Witches was researching the transformation of souls, and I want to look into whatever knowledge exists on the subject. — Though, admittedly, it needn't be Roswaal specifically, so long as it's someone with the relevant expertise."

△▼△▼△▼△

—A Witch's research, becoming the countermeasure to "Lust's" Authority.

"That's a blind spot I never saw coming. I mean, thinking about it, it's weird that the word 'Witch' even feels surprising, given this is all about the Witch Cult in the first place. It's just that those guys never talk about the 'Witches' at all."

By some twist of fate, Subaru had run into every one of the six Sin Archbishop seats except "Pride," but the only one who'd gone on and on about the "Witch" had really been Petelgeuse — he couldn't recall the others ever mentioning the "Witches" at all.

"No, wait, I feel like Sirius said something too. But that felt more like a grudge over losing Petelgeuse than anything else..."

"Whatever the case, it's nothing good, I suppose. The thought of those people talking about the 'Witch' — about Mother — is chilling enough on its own, I suppose."

Beatrice answered Subaru's mutter, but the weakness in her tone and her distracted air made Subaru sit down beside her on the bed, draw her head close, and hold her against his chest.

They'd cut the discussion in the great room short and retreated to a room of their own in the hunting lodge. It was, extravagantly, a room for just the two of them, so here they could open their hearts to each other without worry.

"Not that I'm actually hiding anything even when everyone else is around, though."

Unfortunately, that stance didn't seem to be understood by Russell, at least.

Russell apparently had him misjudged as the Emilia Camp's knight, some heavy-hitting schemer holding a whole hand of hidden cards. Part of what gave that impression was probably his negotiations with Crusch and Anastasia for the Subjugation of the White Whale, so it might be a bit convenient to claim Subaru bore no responsibility for it at all.

In any case, Subaru had gotten Russell to give him time under the pretext of "looking into a way to contact Roswaal," and used it to set up a private talk with Beatrice.

Naturally, the topic went without saying—

"—A 'Witch' who researched the transformation of souls, huh."

"...Does Subaru also think that's Mother, I suppose?"

"When you say Mother, you mean, well, the Echidna with the deep ties to you and Roswaal."

"That's the one, I suppose."

Beatrice nodded in his arms with a small bob of her head, and Subaru let out a small, "Hmm."

The "Witch of Greed" had a habit of scattering confusion through Subaru's life in this other world time and time again, and true to form, she seemed poised to get tangled up in this matter in yet another complicated way.

"There's the Echidna I know, and Bea-ko's mother Echidna. Then there's Anastasia-san's Eridna, and the Sphinx I ran into in the Empire... it's like a bargain sale on Echidnas."

"If you ask Betty, the 'Witch of Greed' that Subaru and Emilia know is the shoddy knockoff, I suppose. Mother isn't some mean-spirited villain like that, I suppose."

"Well, from what I've heard, the one who sounds like the bad guy is definitely the Echidna I know..."

The Echidna Beatrice spoke of was the one credited with creating the "Sanctuary," and above all, the one who had brought Beatrice into the world — a being Subaru could only feel gratitude toward. Her existence was made plain by the coffin hidden deep within the Sanctuary's mausoleum, and the remains laid to rest inside it.

Subaru himself had seen that Echidna sleeping in the coffin, so there was no mistake about it—

"But even with the same features, she's a different person. ...Somehow, your Echidna feels more mature, more like an older-sister type, I guess — more like a Mom-dona, a Mother-dona kind of feel."

"W-what kind of name is that, I suppose...! Even if it's Subaru, Betty won't allow Mother to be treated so carelessly, I suppose...!"

"No, no, sorry! I get how you feel! I get it, but how else do I put it.."

Subaru did have a hypothesis, at least, for solving this puzzle. — Namely, that the Echidna he'd encountered in the mausoleum was her younger self, and that after growing up and acquiring the bearing of an adult, the result was the Mother-dona with the temperament befitting Beatrice's mother.

If that were the case, then the Echidna in the mausoleum — having grown younger again in her state as a soul — might have dropped every bit of good sense she'd worked so hard to acquire along the way.

"That would explain her being such a devil of a witch... I guess. Either way, it's probably not a possibility you want to consider, huh, Bea-ko."

"I don't think Subaru's lying, I suppose. But it's hard to believe all the same, I suppose. And besides, Betty's skeptical about that Russell fellow's idea too, I suppose."

"Skeptical meaning, not that you doubt whether what Russell-san brought will actually work as a countermeasure against 'Lust'?"

"That's right, I suppose. — Even if Mother's research holds the key, Betty's never once seen a book related to it in the 'Forbidden Archive,' I suppose. Not a single one, I suppose."

At Beatrice's words, spoken with her gaze lowered, Subaru felt a complicated feeling he couldn't quite put into words.

The four hundred years Beatrice had spent in the "Forbidden Archive" had been, for her, both a mission entrusted by her mother and a stretch of time that had forced far too long a solitude upon her. In those four hundred years, Beatrice claimed she had read through every single book that had ever been in the "Forbidden Archive."

And among them, nothing touching on the "soul" that Russell was after had ever turned up.

Only—

"There's no way Echidna wasn't researching the 'soul,' that much's for certain."

In the mausoleum, Echidna had told Subaru she had conducted research aimed at achieving immortality, and in fact, Sphinx — a failed result of a policy along the lines of transferring the "soul" into a different vessel — really did exist.

So it was certain that the "Witch" who had researched the "soul" referred to Echidna. The problem was that not a single piece of that research material existed in the "Forbidden Archive."

By Subaru's hypothesis, the future form of the Echidna he knew was the Mother-dona whom Beatrice adored as her mother. If the ruthless research had been conducted, it should have been done by the twisted-natured one who ought to be called Past-dona or Young-dona — meaning it would be, from Mother-dona's perspective, her own past self's research.

If so, it would only be natural for a book related to it to exist in the "Forbidden Archive" entrusted to Beatrice.

"...It's making my head spin, I suppose. But I can't believe at all that there's some 'Witch' other than Mother who delved deeper into that field than Mother did, I suppose."

"Well, none of the 'Witches' I've known ever gave off that kind of vibe either."

Calling to mind the Sin Witches besides Echidna — Minerva, Sekhmet, Daphne, Carmilla, Typhon — none of them seemed to have anything of that researcher's temperament about them at all. — Nor, of course, did it seem like Satella would fit that mold either.

"...So basically, even though Echidna was the one who did the research, there's a reason she didn't put those materials in the 'Forbidden Archive'? Like, maybe it wasn't forbidden enough?"

"Even if it's called the 'Forbidden Archive,' it's not as if everything in it was actually forbidden, I suppose. There was even a meat pie recipe book that Ryuzu taught Mother, sitting right there, I suppose."

"Well, a secret recipe's forbidden enough in its own way—"

With a wry laugh, Subaru started to say as much, but partway through he suddenly caught his breath. At his reaction, Beatrice looked over at him with round eyes. "Subaru?"

For a moment, a certain possibility flashed through his mind. It was one that could never fit the impression Subaru held of Young-dona in his memory, but one that could fit, given the impression of Mother-dona he'd heard about from Beatrice.

The reason forbidden-grade content had been kept out of Beatrice's "Forbidden Archive" —

"—Maybe it was because of what happened with Ryuzu-san."

"...Ryuzu's?"

"Ah, when I say Ryuzu-san, I don't mean the Granny Ryuzu who's still our go-to source of wisdom now — I mean yours... Bea-ko's, first friend."

"—"

Beatrice's eyes went wide, the distinctive pattern in both her irises trembling with violent agitation.

There was the existence Beatrice had once confided in him — a friend from her days at the "Sanctuary," a girl named Ryuzu Meyer, the original of the replica Ryuzus.

That girl, said to have been sacrificed to the Sanctuary's system, had been Beatrice's very first friend, and her parting from that friend had greatly influenced Beatrice's choice to embrace solitude.

And the Sanctuary's system, Subaru had also heard, was an application of the "Witch of Greed's" immortality research — that is, research making use of the "soul." — Which was why the thought had struck him.

"Maybe your mother just didn't want to leave any materials that would remind you of that where you could get your hands on them, or something like that."

"Mo-ther... would..."

"—!"

It was barely even worth calling a passing thought — layering assumption on assumption until it was practically a fantasy. Hearing such an unsubstantiated theory, Beatrice's eyes welled up, and Subaru panicked.

Her feelings and memories of her mother were precious things, tucked away in the treasure box of Beatrice's heart. Worried he'd gone and trampled through it without any consideration, he scrambled for some words to smooth things over.

But Beatrice, in his arms, only pressed her head against him more firmly, and said,

"If that's really how it was, I'd be happy, I suppose."

"Beatrice..."

"Betty doesn't know anymore who 'that person' is that Mother told her to wait for, I suppose. Or what Mother was thinking when she entrusted it to her... but if she felt that way, before leaving that place — the 'Forbidden Archive' — in Betty's hands, then I'd be happy, I suppose."

"...I see. Yeah, that's right. I'm sure that's exactly how it was."

Haltingly, uncertainly, Beatrice pleaded to be allowed to take joy in the possibility of her mother's love.

Stroking her back, Subaru cast aside the shitty little notion Young-dona had voiced — something about wanting to know who Beatrice would choose as "that person" — deciding it was probably just one more piece of harassment aimed at him. No, not just cast aside — he made sure to stomp on it for good measure too.

That way, Beatrice's feelings were saved, and Subaru's own chest felt a little lighter for it too.

"But even if that heartwarming theory settles the question of why the book in question wasn't in the 'Forbidden Archive,' it still doesn't actually solve our problem."

Even if the information that would counter "Lust's" Authority really was the fruit of Echidna's research, it was all back to square one if there was no way to actually obtain it — or so he thought.

"On that matter, Betty does have an idea, I suppose. ...No, not that I had one already — it's more like it just occurred to me now, I suppose."

"W-what do you mean? All I understand right now is that you're cute."

"Understanding something that goes without saying doesn't earn you any praise, I suppose. — It's a simple matter, I suppose. If the book compiling Mother's research isn't in the 'Forbidden Archive,' then it's simply somewhere else, I suppose."

"Which means... you actually have an idea where that 'somewhere else' is?!"

Subaru gaped in astonishment, and Beatrice, pulling her face back from his chest, wiped roughly at her eyes with the back of her hand before giving a cute, confident little nod, wearing a triumphant look.

"It's the place where Betty and Mother used to live, I suppose. Whether going to the 'Sanctuary' or anywhere else, it was basically the place you passed through first, I suppose."

"That means... your childhood home, Bea-ko?"

"My home... I suppose that could indeed be one way to put it, I suppose."

With this unexpected piece of information springing up, Subaru naturally blinked in bewilderment.

Somehow, without meaning to, he'd assumed Beatrice was more or less from the "Sanctuary" herself, but thinking calmly about it, the place where her friend had been was somewhere she'd merely visited, not her home. That Beatrice had a proper home apart from that came as a fresh surprise.

"So that would be, like, the Witch's house, I guess? Where is it?"

"There's no reaching it by any normal means, I suppose. That place is set up so you can only get there through a 'Door Crossing' from a fixed location, I suppose."

"A-a 'Door Crossing,' huh, been a while...! Well, that's some understandable security, at least!"

It was, one could say, exactly the kind of security you'd expect from a "Witch." In fact, it seemed like the sort of system that could sit untouched for hundreds of years if the conditions were never met.

And that security, Beatrice alone could unlock—

"And this fixed location — do you know it too, Bea-ko?"

"Of course, I suppose. But the world's changed quite a lot since the old days, so I need to pick a port that will still let me make a 'Door Crossing' for certain, I suppose. Which means..."

"Which means?"

"—Flugel's Tree."

"...Wha?"

With a hand to her lips, Beatrice, having weighed it over seriously, named the place that had come to mind.

Hearing it, Subaru blinked rapidly, held up one finger, and said, "One more time."

"What was that?"

"I said, Flugel's Tree, I suppose. Since it stands out so much, it makes for the perfect landmark to hide a port at, I suppose. — In order to reach Kugelschreiber Castle."

"—"

With a crisp snap, Beatrice held up a finger to match Subaru's own, delivering her bold declaration, and Subaru, hit with a double shock, fell silent for a while.

Flugel's Tree, as everyone well knew, had already been snapped in two. This could arguably be laid at Subaru's own feet, so whether the port was even still functional would need checking.

And the other shock was—

"'Kugelschreiber' means 'ballpoint pen' in German, you know."

It was a bewildered jab at the name of Beatrice's childhood home — just who, and with what in mind, had named the place that, he had absolutely no idea.
