Arc 8 Chapter 19: "Valga Cromwell's Scheme"

Roswaal's blow, born of a temper too short to hold back, kicked off the battle whether anyone liked it or not.

"—Die, would you."

Against that cold, curt line, a scorching hellfire burst forth as if to scorch the sky itself to nothing.
Fierce flames spread across the night sky all at once, and surely every warrior locked in their life-or-death struggle against the undead had their eyes drawn to that burning heaven.
The goal was just to burn away a single enemy, and yet this was overkill.

"Burning down the mansion to kill a fly in the room, is it, I suppose."

Setting a mansion ablaze for the sake of an objective — that's an outrage only Petra and Otto would commit.
Beatrice, who secretly felt grateful that without that kind of resolve she might never have found the courage to leave the Forbidden Library, still thought this was too much.
An overkill to rival even that pair, and Roswaal had delivered it as his opening punch.
And yet—

"Threat assessment: revision required."

Twisting in midair, the enemy who had escaped the burning night sky — Sphinx — was very much alive.
No matter how thoroughly one burned down a mansion, if the fly one meant to kill got away, it was meaningless. All Roswaal had done was scorch the sky and startle everyone around him.
If Roswaal alone had been here, that might have been the end of it.

"El Mīña."

Her pink hair streaming as she flew freely through the sky, Sphinx found a violet-indigo flicker blooming along her flight path — the pinnacle of dark magic, freezing its target's time in place and shattering it.
A killing blow already proven to be maximally effective against the undead.

"It's a real shame nobody but Subaru and Betty has mastered dark magic, I suppose."

Even against an army of the undead, there'd be nothing to fear if a whole row of dark magic users stood ready. Though even by the standards of Beatrice's long life, she'd never once seen ten or more dark magic users gathered in one place, so she knew full well she was wishing for the impossible.
With that useless little thought, Beatrice shot down her own hesitation before it could take root.

—So that she could put a bolt through the abominable "enemy" wearing Ryuzu Meyer's face.

"Ryuzu……"

Ryuzu Meyer's fleeting smile flickered across the back of Beatrice's mind.
Unlike the Ryuzu sisters whom Garfiel and Frederica cherished as a grandmother, Ryuzu Meyer, their progenitor, had been Beatrice's very first friend in this long life.
Back then, she hadn't been able to acknowledge it. She had been far, far too slow to admit as much.
But now was different. Now, Beatrice could properly hold the weight of that woman's existence in her heart. Which was exactly why this "enemy," wearing that shape, that face, was to Beatrice—

"Downright infuriating, I suppose!"

As Beatrice roared, in her field of view Sphinx charged headlong straight into the violet arrows being loosed at her.
Narrowing her eyes just slightly, the "enemy" raised both hands before her face, and from the five fingers of each hand loosed white light, slicing apart every waiting Mīña in an instant.
The violet-indigo crystals shattered without so much as a sound and scattered in pieces — but Beatrice's frustration wouldn't be dispelled by something so trivial.

"—!"

Sphinx, who should have swept the threat aside, had her expression twist ever so slightly.
The reason: the violet-indigo crystal shards she'd just sliced apart, still in their shattered state, spread out as flying shrapnel and clung to the high-speed flying Sphinx in pursuit.

"Magic is about the image. The moment you think it's been blocked, that's when it falls apart, I suppose."

But it wasn't the arrow shape itself that mattered about the crystal arrows Mīña formed. Their true effect was freezing the target's time at the point of impact and then shattering it.
Even without the shape of an arrow, so long as it pierced the target, neither shape nor size mattered.
And so the shrapnel of Beatrice's stubborn, fighting spirit wasn't about to let Sphinx get away.
And then—

"Threat assessment: revision required, again—"

"—Hundred years too soon to be reconsiderin' anythin'."

Dodging the crystal shrapnel, Sphinx, desperately trying to flee, found Garfiel's ferocious visage suddenly beside her, having kicked off the ground and leapt into the air.
Eyes sharp, fangs bared, Garfiel snarled like a savage beast, swinging his supple arm in a great arc, driving a blow with all his strength toward Sphinx.

In that instant, Garfiel's and Sphinx's eyes met.
Any hesitation in Garfiel's heart at driving a fist into someone wearing his grandmother's face—

"The smell of your heart ain't the same as Granny's—!!"

Such outside concern was entirely unnecessary for the Emilia Camp's dependable man-at-arms.

The unleashed fist caught Sphinx square across the face, knocking the girl's body from midair down to the ground, and drove it, fist and all, brutally into the earth.
A roar and a cloud of dust rose as if a boulder had fallen, and even Beatrice's eyes caught sight of Sphinx's body flying like a scattered leaf beyond the arc of his swung arm.

A single blow from Garfiel, merciless, without hesitation, filled with pure, unadulterated rage.
Struck directly, even Beatrice might have been reduced to mana in a single hit by something of that magnitude — and Sphinx, sent flying by it, tumbled and rolled beyond the rising dust.

"Surrounded three-on-one. Not bad teamwork, wa-a-as it?"

"Says the one who threw the first tantrum, I suppose."

"Teamwork with you makes my skin crawl."

At Roswaal's shrugging, thoroughly-composed-looking act, both Beatrice and Garfiel each fired off an insult. Rebuffed like that, Roswaal shrugged once more.
And then—

"……Threat assessment revision required, again."

Slowly moving her body amid the settling dust, Sphinx murmured that.
Dropped to the ground and caked in dirt, Sphinx showed no pain on her face, but her voice carried clear evidence of accumulated damage.

"――――"

Even so, she let her golden eyes gleam and fixed her gaze on the three of them.
There was no emotion like hostility or anger in it — only the gaze of an intellectual monster, one that regarded them, purely and simply, as an object of its own interest.

"Tch, I don't like this."

Feeling the same chill from that gaze that Beatrice did, Garfiel clicked his tongue. Roswaal too narrowed his eyes silently, clear displeasure written on him as he began gathering mana within himself.
Watching out of the corner of her eye lest Roswaal get ahead of himself again, Beatrice glared at Sphinx as she tried to rise, and stepped forward.

Representing the two unreliable men beside her, she meant to drag the truth out of her—

"I'll go ahead and call you by name, Sphinx. Just what is it you're thinking—"

—was what Beatrice was about to say.

"――――"

Sphinx, who had risen there, had her body tremble, her gaze dropping to her own chest.
From Sphinx's thin chest, the tip of a blade protruded. It was a fatal blade, stabbed through from her back and bursting out through her chest.
And the one gripping that blade was—

"An unpleasant-smelling girl. No reason exists to let her live."

Having declared as much with brutal finality, Mizelda pulled the dagger free and stabbed again.
After gouging into her back and chest three times over in a concentrated assault, she grabbed Sphinx's hair to force her face up, and with the dagger held reverse-grip, mercilessly slit her throat.
It was an undead's body — no blood flowed — but there was no doubt every single one of those wounds was fatal.
Sphinx's body, too, dropped hard to its knees there, and toppled forward.

"You, you, you……!"

Beside the collapsed Sphinx, Mizelda was checking that no blood had gotten on her knife, and Beatrice, eyes wide with shock, found herself at a loss for words.
Roswaal and Garfiel, too, had nothing to say to what Mizelda had done.
At the three of them looking like that, Mizelda tilted her strong-eyed, beautiful face and said,

"I understand she was someone with a grudge between you. But save your words for her grave. It is the custom of the battlefield."

"Well, I mean, that's… fair, I guess……"

It was a blunt, unsentimental opinion, but Garfiel gave up on arguing, finding no room for rebuttal.
Beatrice had been repeatedly startled by the values of the people of Vollachia before now, but she revised her understanding of their severity once again — her prior sense of it had still been far too naive.

"Sphinx."

Off to the side of Beatrice's and Garfiel's shaken state, Roswaal stepped forward. He looked down at the fallen Sphinx and, his profile giving away little emotion, called out her name.

Mizelda's blow — that it was fatal was clear not only from where the wounds had landed, but from the way Sphinx's body was crumbling apart, bit by bit.
Already, most of her lower body had turned to dust, and the girl's face, which had been cracked from the start, had begun flaking apart part by part; it was only a matter of time before the whole of her shattered to pieces.

And yet Sphinx's life force, the undead's life force, was not yet spent.
She had no strength left to counterattack or struggle uselessly, but she had just enough left to move her golden eyes and look back up at Roswaal looking down at her.

"――――"

Even knowing the person inside was someone else, it still hurt to watch Ryuzu's form crumble away.
What Roswaal felt was surely different from Beatrice's sentimentality, but with Mizelda's thoroughgoing realism getting in the way, perhaps he too had been robbed of any means to soothe that feeling.
Either way, the root cause that had tormented the three of them was—

"……I should have thought it through more carefully."

Amid the ongoing collapse of her entire body, bit by bit, Sphinx — showing not a trace of pain on her face — let that murmur slip out, and Beatrice heard it.
At first, Beatrice took it as words of regret over her own lack of strength.

"—You people are—"

But the words that followed made clear that wasn't it.
At that added remark, Beatrice caught her breath, and Garfiel gave a low "Hah?" But even Beatrice couldn't say she grasped its true meaning clearly. Still, she sensed, without a doubt, that this wasn't the kind of parting shot one could afford to ignore.
And then—

"—Oh no!"

Roswaal's head snapped up as if struck.
His expression plastered with alarm and self-reproach, he spun around — back the way they'd come, toward where the Coupled Dragon Carriages had flown on ahead, with Beatrice.

Drawn along by Roswaal's alarm, Beatrice turned to look that way as well.
And, driven by the clamor rising within her own small chest, she called out,

"—Subaru," his name.

△▼△▼△▼△

An instant — yes, it had all happened in an instant.

The moment his eyes caught something wrong and he gave voice to the question, everything was swallowed in white light.
Emilia beside him, Rem, even Julius — none of them were given a chance to react, and of course Subaru could do nothing either, as the white light swallowed everything whole, and vanished.

What that meant, Natsuki Subaru grasped instantly.

—That his own life had been snuffed out, and Return by Death had begun.

Proof of it—

"—I love you."

—came in the whispered voice of the one who, having found him once, would never let him go again.

"Um, could I trouble you to deal with my confusion first?"

The instant that voice, split evenly between exasperation, exhaustion, and a hint of irritation, reached his ears, the "Ready, go!" switch flipped inside Subaru.

"――――"

He confirmed his footing, his surroundings, and grasped the moment from the faces present.
The corridor of the Coupled Dragon Carriages, the scene where Rem had joined him mid-conversation with Emilia and Julius. Given the flow of the conversation, Subaru's last statement had been something considerate of Rem's state: "Maybe there's a chance Priscilla put some ideas in your head back in Guaral or something……" At the front of the Coupled Dragon Carriages, Abel and Otto and the others were hard at work assessing their forces in detail; far behind, having sped ahead, Beatrice, sent off with Roswaal, was approaching things from a magical angle to find a means of countering the undead. At the place Beatrice and the others had headed, Garfiel and the People of Shudrak were fighting fiercely, and the Pleiades Battalion should be there too — his heart was noisy with both a prayer that no one get seriously hurt and a trust that they'd be fine.

—And with the switch flipped, Subaru took all of that in in a single instant.

"Subaru?"

Emilia called his name, her voice startled. In her eyes, she must have caught, for a moment, Subaru's eyes darting rapidly around.
Once before, Tanza had pointed out to him that she'd seen him in this same state.

'Sir Schult's eyes were rolling about so — it was, um, unsettling.'

That was Tanza's comment, chosen carefully but not quite carefully enough, on Subaru's abnormal appearance immediately after a Return by Death as he worked to grasp the situation.
One can imagine how much more reserved Emilia's reaction was, having witnessed the same thing.
In any case—

"Emilia, hold on a sec."

Raising a hand to fend off further questioning, Subaru's mind sharpened to a fine point.
The sense that had been indispensable back on the Isle of Swordsmen's Bondage, an environment premised on dying and retrying, on aiming for everyone's survival, now enveloped Subaru, and he brought his utmost concentration to bear on determining the cause that had led to his own death.

This wasn't the moment to be making noise over some slow-witted feeling of "did I just die." Only once his sense of the situation caught up to the reality of "if I died, then—" could he begin working toward a solution.

If he couldn't make that switch quickly, he had to understand that both he and everyone around him would lose their lives twice over.

"—? Um……"

"Rem, please, hold on."

At Subaru's profile, focused as it was, Rem, brow furrowing faintly, turned a questioning eye on him. But Emilia raised a hand to still Rem's question and held her back.
In that span, Subaru finished linking together the events immediately preceding his "death" in the previous loop with the death itself—

"――――"

Outside the window of the running Coupled Dragon Carriages, he once again caught sight of the foreign object mixed into the scenery.
In that instant, Subaru threw himself at the carriage window.

"—Julius! Outside!"

"Understood!"

The change in Subaru that Emilia and Rem had sensed was, naturally, also picked up by Julius.
Proof of it was that he moved without a moment's hesitation the instant Subaru called out. Drawing his knight's sword behind Subaru as he threw himself at the window, Julius cut the corridor wall away with a diagonal slash, then kicked the wall down with a long leg extended from behind.
Just like that, holding Subaru under one arm, he leapt out all at once, beyond the range of the multiple Earth Dragons' Divine Protection of Wind Evasion.

"Landing's on you!"

"Consider it entrusted."

The instant they left the protection's range, a fierce wind and inertia slammed into them. But Subaru had handed off every preparation for survival entirely to Julius, and he took it up.
Softly, yellow and green quasi-spirits rose up around Julius; one turned the wind into a carpet, the other turned the earth into a cushion, supporting the two of their landing.

But Subaru's attention wasn't spared even a fragment for the masterful skill woven by the bond, restored once more, between Julius and his quasi-spirits.
Fixedly, whether at the moment the window he'd leapt through was sliced away wall and all, or during the graceful landing amid the raging wind afterward, his gaze was locked on a single point — a girl falling from the sky.

"So it really is, the same as Ms. Ryuzu……"

Pink hair, a black pullover robe — the appearance was identical to the adorable, granny-ish Ryuzu. But no matter how worried Emilia and the others were for Subaru and Rem, surely they wouldn't have gone so far as to drag even Ryuzu into the Vollachian Empire.
Which meant that couldn't possibly be the real Ryuzu. Nor was there any chance it was one of Piko and the others, the duplicate bodies sharing Ryuzu's exact shape and form.
To keep them distinguishable, all of them had different hairstyles, or wore individual ribbons or hair ornaments. That none of that was present meant this was none of them.

"Julius! That girl! Pin her down!"

Pointing at the fake Ryuzu still airborne, not yet landed, Subaru shouted.
Hearing Subaru's cry, Julius too spotted the girl's figure blending into the night sky. Setting aside every question of who she was and what she intended, all for later, the "Sword Saint" flew.
Dropping Subaru onto the soft earth underfoot, Julius's body, trailing the remnants of the wind carpet, bounded through the air off that as a foothold, closing in on the fake Ryuzu in a straight line.

At Julius's ferocious, life-or-death momentum, the fake Ryuzu recognized him as a threat too.
At once, her eyes flared with a gold light vivid even in the darkness of night, and understanding clicked into place — the pale, bluish skin he hadn't been able to make out from a distance, marking her as one of the undead.
Just like that, the fake Ryuzu turned a hand toward Julius, that abominable white light about to be loosed at him, when—

"—Innes, lend me your strength."

The light kindling at the fake Ryuzu's fingertips was answered by a different white light that faintly illuminated Julius's whole body, while, in turn, a black light thinly wrapped around the fake Ryuzu's entire form.
The instant it became clear this was the simultaneous exercise of light magic and dark magic by two quasi-spirits, Julius's knight's sword flashed, and the strength drained from the light-wreathed fake Ryuzu's arm.

"Explanation required."

"I've cut the tendon in your shoulder. It will no doubt reconnect swiftly, of course, but……"

To the fake Ryuzu's question about why her arm had lost its strength, Julius answered dutifully. At the end of that answer, twisting his body in midair, Julius struck the girl suspended in the air with a long leg that whistled through the air, driving her body down into the ground.
Where the fake Ryuzu fell, the earth's very nature shifted along with a yellow light. The ground, gaining viscosity, caught the girl's body softly yet deeply, then hardened all at once.
As a result, the fake Ryuzu found herself unable to move, as if wrapped in a quilt of stone.

"I don't recommend resistance. If you cannot heed this warning, then living or dead, I will treat you as an 'enemy' all the same."

"――――"

To the fake Ryuzu lying flat on her back on the ground, Julius, sword leveled at her, declared as much.
Having thus neutralized his opponent, Julius nodded to Subaru, who had watched the whole exchange play out. Subaru, who could only stand there watching the entire sequence, scratched his cheek with a finger.

"Nah, that's exactly what I wanted you to do…… For real, though. That guy's clearly gotten way stronger than before."

His fighting style, combining support from the quasi-spirits, was smarter and more refined than before.
It might be called the true measure of the "Spirit Knight," contracted with all six types of quasi-spirit — a hybrid style of combat blending magic and swordsmanship.

"Wow, amazing, you took her down in no time at all."

From behind Subaru, treading through the grass, came Emilia, running up.
Having apparently jumped down from the carriage in pursuit of Subaru and Julius, she stared wide-eyed, seeing no opening for herself to have jumped in given Julius's brilliant handiwork.

"Emilia-tan, where's Rem?"

"She went to tell Otto and the others that you and Julius jumped off. I hurried to catch up right away too, thinking I had to hurry, but……"

"Nah, it's not that you're slow, Emilia-tan — it's that he's way too fast. Also, not bringing Rem along too was a total home run."

He'd half-suspected that Rem, as she was now, might well have jumped down right along with them, but thanks to Emilia's good judgment, it looked like they'd manage not to drag her into danger.
Just as he thought that far, though, he reconsidered — even the Rem who still had her memories would surely have jumped down without hesitation either — so telling her to stay behind, however he'd phrased it, had been an absolute home run on Emilia's part.
In any case—

"It's not Ms. Ryuzu, right?"

"Looks just like her, but I'm pretty sure it's a zombie. Come to think of it, could Bea or Ms. Ryuzu even turn into zombies……?"

He didn't even want to think about Beatrice or Ryuzu dying, but given how their bodies were built differently from ordinary living creatures, whether zombification was even possible for them was an open question.
In fact, given that this undead in Ryuzu's form existed at all, unless it was some miraculous lookalike, its origin had to be the same as Ryuzu's.
Mulling over a line of thought that probably didn't need mulling at all, Subaru, together with Emilia, approached the captured fake Ryuzu without letting their guard down, just to be safe.

"A zombie capable of both speech and thought. Subaru, exercise caution."

"Yeah. Actually, there's a decent chance she'll pull something worse than she looks capable of. Emilia-tan, can you keep a close eye on her too?"

"Yes, leave it to me. And you — if you just behave yourself quietly, um……"

Entrusted with keeping watch, Emilia, fired up, faltered over what words to use toward the fake Ryuzu.
Whether a warning of "I won't hurt you if you don't resist" meant anything to an undead was dubious at best. Still, they absolutely could not let their guard down.
This small being had, just moments ago, obliterated the Coupled Dragon Carriages, Subaru and everyone else along with it, in a single breath.
Even with all four limbs sealed and immobile, there was no telling if that was truly enough.

"Shame your preemptive strike failed, huh."

"――. You are―"

"My name is… I feel the conditioned reflex to introduce myself, but we're not exactly trying to be friends here. Let's just leave it at: you're dangerous, and we stopped whatever you were planning."

"I see. A curious specimen. At a glance, you don't appear to possess any outstanding ability."

Still lying flat, the fake Ryuzu offered that observation while looking up at Subaru.
He was used to being underestimated, so the assessment didn't even sting. Beside him, Emilia looked like she wanted to say something, but Julius shook his head to stop her.
Either way—

"No other zombies have shown up. Since you can fly, did you come on ahead alone? If you went and rushed in thinking that'd be enough to take us down, tough luck."

In truth, that very preemptive strike had gotten the better of him once already, but he didn't let so much as a hint of that show.
If this provocation got the enemy to spill something, all the better. Though, apparently the blood hadn't drained from her skin for nothing — he got no sense that she was about to become emotional.
Still, this type of opponent, when kept repeating wildly off-base statements, tended to feel the urge to show off their own intellect — that was just how it always went.
If necessary, Subaru was prepared to play the fool of a lifetime, giving the performance of his life as a hopeless idiot.
But—

"—Valga Cromwell."

"Hm?"

Abruptly, the lips of the girl lying prone spoke an unfamiliar word.
—No, it was a sound he might have heard somewhere before, but Subaru couldn't immediately recall when or in what context.
Beside Subaru, whose brow furrowed in confusion, Emilia's and Julius's reactions were different.
Both of them showed a reaction suggesting they recognized the name.
But before Emilia and the others could voice their follow-up questions to the fake Ryuzu's remark—

"This was a scheme of his, not carried out at the time."

That — the fake Ryuzu continued on before they could.
And what followed continued on seamlessly too, ignoring the question that had come a beat too late.

"Subaru!!"

In that instant came Emilia's voice, her expression changing color, and she was pulled bodily close by her arm. Just like that, Emilia clenched her back teeth, and a wall of ice was conjured around Subaru and the others.
Inside that ice wall, Julius, too, reset the knight's sword he'd had leveled at the fake Ryuzu underfoot, wrapping himself in a rainbow light gathered from the power of all six quasi-spirits.
Against whatever crisis was coming, Emilia and Julius completed their instant of preparation.
That—

"—Wha―"

—a white light pouring down from beyond the sky blew away in an instant, as if mocking them.

△▼△▼△▼△

"Um, could I trouble you to deal with my confusion first?"

"—!"

Rem's voice, exasperation, exhaustion, and irritation held in equal measure, reached him, and Subaru let out a short breath.
Unlike last time, self-reproach for failing to avert a "death" he'd known was coming, and the soul-deep tremor of receiving a shock he'd known was coming, pummeled Subaru's heart from within, over and over.

—He had completely misjudged the cause of that "death."

He'd latched onto an obvious, easy-to-see change and, presuming he'd fully blocked it, let it go to his head — that was the result.
The first time, the interval between perceiving the fake Ryuzu's presence and his "death" had been too short, and he'd let slip what had truly been happening.

The outrageously powerful white light that had obliterated him and the others hadn't been fired by that fake Ryuzu at all — it had come from somewhere else entirely.

"Subaru?"

"――. Emilia, hold on a sec."

Noticing Subaru's introspection, Emilia tilted her head, and he answered with a raised hand.
He cut his reflection short and cast his regret aside. Reflection still had room to grow into something useful; regret was just self-indulgence masquerading as having reflected.
That would save no one — not the self he was indulging, nor any of the people around him he was neglecting by doing so.
He wanted none of that. He absolutely, positively wanted none of that.

In that moment, the fake Ryuzu had brought up the name "Valga Cromwell." It still didn't ring any bells for Subaru, but Emilia and Julius had seemed to recognize it. Should he ask them for the details? —No, that could wait. Even if he learned the truth of who this Valga was, it wouldn't make the power of that white light that struck next disappear. That attack had stood before him and the others as a real, and moreover outrageous, threat. He had to block it — but was it even possible to keep it from being fired in the first place? Given the situation, there was no way the fake Ryuzu was unrelated to the attack, but was she even an undead who would respond to negotiation? If she was an undead capable of exchanging words, wasn't it dangerous to shut the door on negotiation? No — if she were the kind of opponent reason could reach, she wouldn't be coming at them to kill without a word first. Whether or not she could be talked to, before he could sit her down at any negotiating table, he first needed to have a table to sit her at—

"—? Um……"

"Rem, please."

Beside Subaru, whose thoughts were sparking rapidly, Emilia held back Rem's question.
The same flow as before, but from here on, he had to steer things into a different course than last time. In order, he sorted through in his head what he'd done, and to break free of the track that led to the outcome of "death," he changed the necessary branch point.
To that end—

"—Julius! Outside! Emilia, you too!"

"Understood!"
"Yes! Got it!"

The instant the girl who heralded the coming "death" flickered past the window outside, Subaru threw himself at the window and called out to Emilia and Julius behind him.
Without hesitation, Julius's sword strike sliced through the wall of the Coupled Dragon Carriages, and as the kicked-down wall gave way, Subaru and the others went outside. Before they could escape the range of the Divine Protection of Wind Evasion, Subaru shouted—

"—Rem! I need a favor!" — as much.

△▼△▼△▼△

A long leg flashed out in a single kick, striking the fake Ryuzu down to the ground, and the earth, its nature altered, caught the undead's body — a quilt of soil once again binding her entire form.

"I don't recommend resistance. If you cannot heed this warning, then living or dead, I will treat you as an 'enemy' all the same."

"――――"

Told this while lying flat on the ground right beside him, the fake Ryuzu gazed up at Julius, sword leveled at her. Perhaps she was marveling at Julius's graceful handiwork, but in his manner of fighting, none of Subaru's Return by Death advantage had been put to any use at all.
At Julius, who had pinned down the opponent through sheer, unadulterated skill, Subaru clenched his fist as well.

"It's not Ms. Ryuzu, right?"

Julius, having neutralized his opponent in a single instant's clash, and the fake Ryuzu at his feet — at the sight of her, Emilia, who this time had jumped down from the Coupled Dragon Carriages together with him, furrowed her well-shaped brows.
Subaru, too, still didn't know the fake Ryuzu's true identity. All he knew was that she was undead, and that she bore both hostile intent and the means to act on it against him and the others — nothing more.

"Looks the same, but she's not Ms. Ryuzu or one of Piko and the others. More importantly, put me down and stay on guard! Get ready, Emilia!"

"On guard? But Julius already beat her, didn't he?"

"Something bigger's coming. Something that'll get us if we're not ready for it."

Saying that, Subaru jumped down from the arms of Emilia, who'd been carrying him.
He'd left the support for this landing to Emilia, having had Julius pin down the fake Ryuzu instantly instead. Just like that, Subaru nodded to the startled Emilia, then dashed over toward Julius, who held the fake Ryuzu restrained.

"Nice work! But there's more to come! Something huge's about to get fired in from beyond the sky! We have to stop it or everyone's in serious trouble!"

"From the sky?"

"I've got Emilia taking deep breaths already!"

Skipping over the finer details, Subaru pointed back at Emilia behind him.
There, Emilia was spreading her arms wide, breathing slowly, deeply, and at length. She was raising her concentration, preparing for what was to come.
Seeing that, Julius nodded too, and six colors of quasi-spirit began swirling around him.

"――. You are―"

At Subaru briskly hurrying Emilia and Julius through their preparations, the gaze of the fake Ryuzu, restrained on the ground, took on a note of suspicion.
From the exchange just now, she'd surely already grasped, quickly enough, that it was Subaru who was behind whatever had pinned down her movements. But Subaru wasn't about to let the fake Ryuzu's surprise end there.

"Valga Cromwell."

"――――"

"That's the name of your battle strategist, isn't it?"

Closing one eye, Subaru let the fake Ryuzu know he could see right through her scheme.
In truth, he didn't have the faintest idea what the fake Ryuzu was scheming, let alone who Valga Cromwell even was — but not letting so much as a hint of that show was the whole technique of a good bluff.
He'd take information he'd gotten straight from the fake Ryuzu herself, and use it to shock her right back.
Sure enough, the fake Ryuzu, whose expression had remained unchanged until now, stiffened her cheeks, turning a gaze on Subaru even more intently focused than before.

"I judged that mage and the spirits warranted caution, but you too are now marked as required attention."

"……Your scheme's been seen through. Might as well give up gracefully—"

—was what Subaru tried to keep bluffing. But the fake Ryuzu wasn't the type to have her resolve broken by a bluff, and besides, her whole intention had been something else from the start.
The fake Ryuzu's scheme wasn't the kind of thing that failed just because it had been seen through.

"Valga's scheme is one that cannot be stopped, even if seen through."

In that instant, that same white light came pouring down from beyond the sky, aimed at Subaru and the others — no, that wasn't right.
The white light hadn't been aimed at Subaru and the others at all. It had been aimed at the fake Ryuzu lying there on the ground, loosed to sweep away everything on the ground around her.

"—Subaru!!"

Before that oncoming light of "death" could obliterate Subaru's very existence, Emilia thrust out her hand, and the ice barrier she'd conjured collided head-on with it.
At the same moment, yanked by the collar, Subaru's body toppled down at the feet of the braced Emilia. Right before Subaru's eyes, the fake Ryuzu, positioned closer to the light than Subaru was, was swallowed by the white light and vanished.

It had probably been like this all along, every time.
That white light had been aimed at the fake Ryuzu, loosed at her. Her very presence served as the marker to target that white light, and the fallout from the impact had swallowed up Subaru and the others, the Coupled Dragon Carriages, along with it.
Charging into the heart of the enemy to become a living target for an overwhelmingly powerful bombardment. —No, since she was already dead, a dead target, then. Either way, whoever had come up with this was completely out of their mind.

"That bastard Valga Cromwell——!!"

"Al Clauselia!!"

Subaru's heartfelt shout was answered by Julius's incantation, sword tip turned toward the light.
The rainbow-colored aurora conjured burned like a wall, clashing against the white light, and stopped short, just barely, the multiple sheets of ice barrier Emilia had raised from being shattered by it.

The white light of destruction, and the protective ice wall wrapped in that aurora.
Unlike last time, when they'd had no choice but to take the hit unprepared, this time they'd had time to gather their strength against the white light. That was the reason Emilia and Julius were managing to hold their ground against it now, compared to before.
But even so—

"Ugh, yaaaaaaah—!"
"Gh, ngh……!"

Their voices strained with desperation, Emilia and Julius fought against the white light.
But for the two of them, together, to be unable to push it back — just what kind of power was this? Unable to do anything in this moment, all Subaru could do was brace Emilia's back as she held her ground.

"Hang in there! Both of you, hang in there!"

Clenching his back teeth, Subaru, unable to offer any physical support, shouted encouragement instead.
It would be nice if that fired the two of them up, summoned up enough power to blow the white light away entirely — but things didn't work out so conveniently.
No matter the situation, a helping hand never suddenly reached out from thin air to save the day.
No matter how much you prayed, how much you wished, a card you hadn't been dealt couldn't be played in this hand.
And so—

"—Well now, good job calling for me. Good, good boy."

At that, a voice so carefree it stood in stark contrast to the desperate situation spoke up, and Subaru held his breath.
Someone walked past, right beside him. As he did, the man patted Subaru's head with a big hand, then, with the easy air of someone out for a stroll, stepped out in front of Emilia and Julius.
The two of them, startled by this sudden intruder's actions, but—

"Here's the death spot."

No sooner had he said it than the figure who'd stepped forward in front of the three of them swung an arm drawn from his sleeve, and threw something into the midst of the white light.
It was a light harboring the power to swallow up everything and turn it to dust. If it reached beyond where Emilia and Julius were desperately holding it back with their barrier, that too would be swallowed and snuffed out.
Or so it seemed it would go.

"No way……"

With a breath like a sigh, Emilia's violet-indigo eyes went wide.
What was reflected in those beautiful eyes of hers wasn't the white "death" that had been closing in right before them, but that same light, vanished without a trace, and the night sky beyond, thick clouds gouged into the very shape of that light.

Witnessing the same sight, both Subaru and Julius lost their voices.
The aurora-wrapped ice wall that had clashed against the light had vanished too, and now that the fake Ryuzu, who had made herself the target, had vanished into the light, there was nothing left there at all—

"Good thing the little blue devil's kid called out for me. But all three of you did well too. Want a candy?"

Saying so, the Wolf Human who had wiped away that overwhelming "death" with his own hand — the strongest in the city-state — turned around, grinning at the dumbfounded Subaru and the others.
Just like that, he rummaged around in his robes, and with the pipe still in his mouth, tilted his head.

"Ah, guess I don't have any candy on me."

And, waving his empty hand about, he left Subaru, all the strength drained out of him, to plop down on his backside.
