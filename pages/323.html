Arc 5 Chapter 7: "A Boat Journey to a Man of Deep Karma"

"A man who had his heart stolen by a songstress, huh. That's... how do I put it, a nickname that sounds cool on the surface but really isn't cool at all, is what that sounds like."

Hearing Anastasia's overly dramatic answer, Subaru let that comment slip. The one who ended up laughing at that was Anastasia herself, the one who'd said it. She slipped a hand into her soft hair, twirling the ends between her fingers as she said,

"Yeah, that's about right. Folks use it more like a pet name than a proper title. But it's the man himself who goes around sayin' it—'My heart's been stolen by a songstress,' and all that."

"Um, so what does that actually mean?"

"A man who has lost his heart to a single woman, and who declares his love for her without reservation. —Doesn't that strike you as rather close to your own conduct at the Royal Selection?"

"It's not exactly a black mark on my history, but quit dragging it up just to needle me."

At Julius's mocking remark, Subaru twisted his lips and clicked his tongue. Looking back, his disgraceful display at the Royal Selection a year ago was the kind of thing that made him want to clutch his head and roll around on the ground. Not that his feelings back then had been wrong, exactly—it was mostly a matter of how he'd expressed them. Anyway,

"I get from personal experience that his head's turned out pretty pitiful. So, what's he like as a person?"

"The nickname's a bit much, sure, but the man himself is pretty serious and level-headed. Bein' the heir to the Muse Company isn't just about bein' the eldest son, y'know. He's got the business sense to back it up as a given. No need to doubt him on that score."

"I can vouch for that too, Natsuki-san. The young master of the Muse Company—Kiritaka Muse's name is fairly well known. The Muse Company was originally an old, established house dealing mainly in magic ore, but Kiritaka's skill is well regarded even against that storied name."

It was Otto who added this to Anastasia's account, evidently having heard of the man's reputation through merchant connections. But no sooner had he spoken up in Kiritaka's defense than he turned a sharp look on Anastasia instead.

"Naturally, when it came to the high-purity magic ore Emilia-sama requires, I made inquiries first and foremost with the merchant houses that specialize in it. Even though we couldn't move too openly, I'm sure we approached the Muse Company as well. ...That they kept their cards close to their chest, I can chalk up to their own agenda. What concerns me more is how Anastasia-sama came to know of it."

"Ain't that just a matter of trust? Could be sincerity too, I suppose. Only natural that things go smoother when the person who actually wants somethin' shows up in person, no?"

"...I suppose we'll leave it at that."

Faced with Anastasia's feigned innocence, Otto gave up pressing further. He then shifted the main thread of the conversation to the magic ore in question.

"First of all, you're certain the magic ore we're after actually exists?"

"I get why you'd doubt it, but I'm not fool enough to tell a lie that'd get found out the second someone checked. It's somewhere conspicuous, and the other side shouldn't be able to play dumb about it either."

"I see. Are we free to handle the negotiations ourselves, then? Were any conditions attached to introducing us?"

"Such a worrier. Don't fret over that either. I just happened to have a good idea where the ore Emilia-san and the others are lookin' for might be, and was kind enough to tell you. We already agreed on the reason for that, didn't we, Otto-kun?"

A favor doesn't come with a price tag—that was Anastasia's stated position. Otto fell silent, apparently satisfied, and an agreement was reached on how the ore would be handled. As the conversation reached a lull, Emilia timidly raised her hand.

"Um, is it okay if I ask something?"

"Ask away. Business and relationships both, when you boil 'em down, come to a matter of trust. If clearin' up a doubt is the best way to build that, it's a cheap price to pay."

"You'd probably say that's also because it doesn't cost you any money..."

"Well, if there were an hourly rate involved, that'd be a different story. But mostly, your guess isn't wrong. Natsuki-kun, you might turn into somethin' with a little trainin'."

At Anastasia's smile, Subaru shrugged, silently declining the offer. Beside him, having been given leave to ask her question, Emilia tilted her head slightly.

"I understand about Kiritaka-san now, but I'm curious what kind of person this 'songstress' is. Is she famous?"

It was a simple question, but Subaru found himself nodding along—yeah, good point. A songstress that had captured Kiritaka Muse's heart. Now that he thought of it, Subaru recalled that Muse, Kiritaka's family name, was also the name of some kind of goddess of song. A strange, curious coincidence—but if it meant he'd been fated to be drawn to a songstress, well, there was something romantic in that.

"That's assuming this 'songstress' isn't some historical great, like Hoshin of the Wilderness. If we're talking history-buff territory, there's no cure for that."

"You can set your mind at ease on that point. The present-day 'songstress,' Lady Liliana, is a living woman currently residing in this city of Priestella. It wouldn't be an exaggeration to say she is presently... kept, by the aforementioned Kiritaka."

"'Presently,' meaning it wasn't always that way?"

"I'm told she was originally a traveling bard, singing as she journeyed. And that in the course of wandering from place to place, she caught Kiritaka's eye, and so it stands now."

Listening to Julius's answer, Subaru pictured a small bird shut inside a cage. A bird that had once lived free, caught in a man of power's eye and made into a caged bird. Wasn't that just the sort of turn one might expect? From what he'd heard, Kiritaka showed the kind of affection that bordered on obsession. Was this Liliana person, too, spending her days shut away, unable to go outside, in some unhappy confinement?

"That's not a good story. Song isn't something anyone should be able to restrict—it's meant to be free."

"I agree with you on that point, but I suspect some misunderstanding has arisen. Though, given Kiritaka's attachment to Lady Liliana, it's hard to fault anyone for thinking it."

"My opinion of this Kiritaka guy is steadily dropping. Is the negotiation actually gonna be fine? In my head he's some stubborn crank you can't reason with."

He pictured a typical unpleasant rich man, fat and bloated, oozing with greasy desire. As Otto had mentioned earlier, this was the sort who'd played dumb and ignored their request for the magic ore. Asking Subaru to picture him favorably was asking too much.

"The thought of bringin' Emilia-tan in front of some lecherous-lookin' guy like that gives me the creeps."

"Emilia-sama should be fine, most likely. Kiritaka is a somewhat difficult man to handle, but he isn't so indiscriminate as that. However..."

There Julius cut himself off, his eyes wavering over how to put the rest into words. At this rare display of hesitation from him, Subaru knit his brow. Julius let out a small breath, then shifted his gaze from Emilia to Subaru in turn.

"Considering the worst case, it might be best not to bring Lady Beatrice along."

"What's that supposed to mean!?"

"By the way, Kiritaka-san's always happy to sit down and talk things over with me. I figure that oughta get the gist of the situation across."

Julius's words, and Anastasia's. Standing up, Subaru pieced the two of their remarks together and arrived at a single answer. Which was,

"...A lolicon?"

"Let's say instead that he holds standards that outsiders find difficult to fathom, ones he won't compromise on. Naturally, that does nothing to diminish Anastasia-sama's charms."

"Even your damage control can lack elegance sometimes, huh."

It was a strained bit of covering from Julius, but Anastasia didn't seem to mind in the least. Since no one denied it, Subaru took that as confirmation of the whole matter. Still, honestly, he couldn't quite shake the feeling of "not this again."

"One troublesome lolicon was already plenty, between Clind-san and now this..."

The image of the all-purpose butler's thin face came to mind, and Subaru wanted to clutch his head. Still, there was one decisive difference between Clind and this Kiritaka. Namely, that Clind, presumably, showed no interest whatsoever in Anastasia. Clind valued inner loli-ness above all; even if someone's appearance was that of a loli, if their insides had grown up accordingly, he'd show no interest. This was proven by his attitude toward Ryuzu, and by the respect he showed Emilia. Just how the world looked through his eyes remained unclear, but those eyes had seen through to Emilia's inner immaturity, and revered her as a loli of the spirit. Kiritaka, on the other hand, was a man who valued outer loli-ness. Anastasia was, by all appearances, roughly the same age as Subaru, but her build was immature for it. Given that there was scarcely any sign of further growth to come, hers was a figure one might call a legal loli. Kiritaka's predilection for someone like her went without saying.

"Our Bea-ko's an all-purpose loli, compatible with both the Clind type and the Kiritaka type..."

"I don't quite follow, but I have a feeling I'm being spoken of rather rudely, I suppose."

"You idiot, that's not it! I'm worried about you! You... just how much danger does your mere existence pose as charm? Get a little self-aware and put my mind at ease, would you!"

"Eh, ah, um... W-well, if you're worried about me, I suppose it can't be helped. Fufufu."

Even though he'd been pointing out her lack of a sense of danger, Beatrice happily latched onto Subaru's hem. For the time being, he held her hand in place from above so she couldn't slip away. For a while yet, in this city, it might be wisest not to let Beatrice out of his sight.

"Um, so basically, does that mean he likes short girls?"

"What an innocent answer. That's not quite it—more simply put, he gets his pleasure from plucking unripe fruit..."

"STAAAAAAHP! Don't go putting any more weird ideas into my sheltered angel's head! I understand about the guy already! No more of this! Okay, we're done, done!"

Subaru put on a grand show of shielding Beatrice with his left hand and Emilia with his right. Anastasia laughed at his display of overprotectiveness, and even Julius gave a wry smile.

"Setting aside Natsuki-san's posture on all this, I've got a grasp on the terms and everything else. If possible, I'd like to make contact with Kiritaka sooner rather than later—should we head over to the company offices?"

"Let's see. Kiritaka-san's actually a pretty busy man, believe it or not. Since he's also involved in runnin' the city, he's probably either at the city hall or the company offices, one or the other."

Anastasia answered Otto, who was trying to get things moving again. Taking this in, Otto drew his chin back and turned to face Subaru and the others.

"Showing up out of the blue for a meeting is asking too much, surely. First I'd like to get word to him ahead of time and set up a proper, calm place to talk... So, which one should I run over to first?"

"Right... honestly, it's less a question of which and more that I don't have this town's map in my head at all yet, so it's kind of an impossible choice to make."

As far as he could see from the main gate, Priestella's size was nothing to scoff at. Even if it wasn't quite on the scale of the royal capital, an unfamiliar townscape was a threat all on its own. Subaru prided himself on his sense of direction, but in a city like Priestella, where the waterways took priority and the walkways twisted around them, he wondered how much good that pride would actually do him.

"There are probably small-boat guides who use the waterways. Since a lot of people visit as tourists, they'll likely fawn over us thinkin' we're a cash cow."

"Just between us, I get seriously seasick. Back in elementary school, on a field trip, we crossed a lake by boat and I got sick in a way that wasn't normal at all, and everyone shunned me for it."

"I don't quite get it, but it sounds like a memory that hurts to hear, I suppose."

At Subaru recalling this faintly scarred memory, Beatrice wore a look of pity. In any case, it would be pathetic to let regret over the past keep him from taking a step forward now. Subaru weighed Otto's proposal, figuring that as long as they wouldn't get lost, either destination was fine. Just then—

"Forgive the interruption, but let me put your mind at ease—that worry is unfounded."

"Julius, what do you mean?"

At Julius's interjection, Emilia's eyes went round as she asked back. Julius gave Emilia a faint smile.

"It's simple. Regarding a meeting with Kiritaka, we've already sent someone from our side to make the request. Once our messenger returns shortly, I should be able to give you the details."

"Messenger?"

"I've sent my younger brother Yoshua, along with a man named Ricardo, as messengers."

Hearing Julius's answer, Subaru understood at last where the two absent men had gone. At the same time, he found the thoughtfulness of it a bit maddeningly likable. It could be called, in a sense, only natural consideration for a foreseeable problem, but—

"Between this and the welcome party in front of the inn, you sure do move efficiently."

"As the one who extended the invitation, making such preparations is only natural courtesy. It's hardly worth remarking on, Subaru. Though I'll keep the details to myself."

"Suit yourself."

His pompous, exaggerated way of talking hadn't changed one bit. Even as his hostility toward Julius deepened further, Subaru also found himself puzzling over the mystery of just how his camp managed to move so fast. Anyway,

"In that case, galling as it is, I guess I'll wait for the two of them to get back. Not that we could head out anyway—our bodyguard hasn't come back yet."

"That's the thing."

Loosening his posture into an easy slouch, Subaru brought up Garfiel, who hadn't been seen since they were brought into the inn. At once, Anastasia latched onto the subject.

"Mimi and the others haven't come back on my end either—what's goin' on? That kid, that Garfiel kid, what's he like? Tell me all about it in detail."

"Name's Garfiel Tinzel. A fifteen-year-old who's a bit of a dreamer, fond of flights of fancy. His flaws are a habit of chewing on hard things and snoring loud enough to be a nuisance. But he's also a straightforward, single-minded, pure-hearted young man who won't bend his feelings for the girl he likes even if she beats the tar out of him."

"What's that, sounds like a real catch."

Anastasia seemed to be trying hard not to lose her cute little sister figure to anyone else, but she was underestimating him. Subaru, for his part, thought of Garfiel as his own cute little brother figure. Whether or not he actually wanted to pair him off with Mimi was a separate matter—he had no qualms whatsoever about talking Garfiel up.

"I wonder what kind of pretty voice the songstress sings with. I'm really, really curious. If I got to meet her somewhere, do you think I could ask her to sing for me?"

"Lady Liliana is a very sociable woman. I'm sure she wouldn't turn down a request from you, Emilia-sama. If you meet with Kiritaka, you'll likely cross paths with her naturally as well. You could make your request then."

"Wow, really? I can't wait."

Emilia, for her part, had already seemed quite curious about the songstress in question, and Julius's words only raised her anticipation further. Watching the two pairs of lord and vassal converse like this, Otto let out a sigh.

"I always figured relations between candidates for the throne would be a lot more cutthroat... Is it just me being too on edge about this?"

"There's no need to feel down about it, I suppose. It's less that you're overthinking it, and more that Subaru and Emilia are underthinking it, I wonder."

That Beatrice, rare as it was, responded to Otto with something like sympathy was itself enough to foretell just how exhausted he was bound to become, in both body and mind, over the course of their stay in this city.

Garfiel finally returning to rejoin Subaru and the others, and Yoshua and the rest—who had gone as Anastasia's messengers to arrange an appointment with Kiritaka Muse—coming back, happened at almost exactly the same time.

"Kiritaka is apparently quite busy—today he's holed up in the company offices rather than city hall. In any case, if we head there, he says he can make time for us."

"Ah, I see, I see. Good work, Yoshua."

Having returned to his liege and his brother, Yoshua gave the assembled Subaru and the others a glance before making his report. Anastasia nodded, satisfied, and then his gaze turned toward the group.

"...Thank you for making the journey from so far away. I believe Anastasia-sama has already explained, but word has been passed along to Kiritaka."

"Yeah, thanks for the trouble. Thanks to you, we won't waste any extra time."

"Think nothing of it. I merely followed Anastasia-sama's orders, nothing more. Truth be told, I'd rather have kept my brother and Subaru-sama apart entirely."

"You're as honest as ever, aren't you."

At Yoshua's frank hostility toward Subaru, it was Julius, of all people, whose eyes went wide. He seemed not to have known about the animosity his younger brother harbored toward Subaru.

"Yoshua. He, along with everyone else here, is a guest Anastasia-sama has invited. Any discourtesy toward a guest reflects directly on the name of our liege, Anastasia-sama. Mind yourself."

"...My apologies, brother."

At Yoshua's sulky look, Julius let out a small breath. Then, with eyes carrying an apology, he gave Subaru a slight bow of the head.

"I'm sorry. Let me apologize on my own behalf as well. My brother would never normally behave this way... perhaps it's the unfamiliar surroundings—he seems on edge."

"Not that I mind, but if he's not used to it, then his big brother oughta keep a proper grip on the reins. I'd rather not get bitten by the both of you brothers at once."

"Heh. I'll keep that in mind."

At Subaru's remark, somewhere between sarcasm and a joke, Julius dipped his chin. Yoshua watched the exchange with obvious discontent, but the moment Subaru noticed his gaze, he hastily looked away. Deep-rooted jealousy, one might call it.

"Well then, taking you up on your kind offer, we'll go ahead and head over to Kiritaka-san's. Is it all right if we still think of this as our inn?"

"You go right on thinkin' that. The Water Hagoromo Inn ain't just unusual—it's got a real track record for guest satisfaction and all that too. Look forward to dinner, yeah?"

"Is that so. Then I'll look forward to that too."

At Anastasia's confident air, Emilia clapped her hands together with a smile. Then, smoothing her long silver hair with her hand,

"To enjoy a good meal without any worries weighing on me, I've got to make sure we have a proper talk with Kiritaka-san."

With Emilia, who said this as she rose to her feet, in the lead, Subaru and the others left the inn together. As for getting to the Muse Company offices where Kiritaka was, Yoshua had apparently arranged for a canal boat to be waiting on the waterway in front of the inn.

"Oi, boss! Don't you go leavin' me behind! You'll be in trouble without me!"

What stopped the four of them as they turned toward the waterway was Garfiel, come dashing out of the inn in a hurry. His shining golden hair and clothes slightly dirtied, he rejoined the group looking unusually worn out.

"Ahh, I went through hell. If that big dog-faced old man hadn't shown up, I'da been dragged around even more."

"Dog-faced means Ricardo, huh. Weren't you just off havin' a grand time on a date with Mimi till the loud-voiced old man stopped you?"

"Date?! You gotta be kiddin' me. The second that shorty dragged me off, another shorty who looked just like her showed up, and I nearly got murdered by a half-cryin' shorty. Couldn't exactly fight back and make her cry more neither, so I got run ragged all over the place..."

According to Garfiel, it was Ricardo who'd put a stop to that whole disaster. Mimi, of course, along with the two younger brothers who'd egged the ruckus on—the level-headed Tivey had probably just gotten caught in the crossfire. Hetaro, who'd shown off his full brother-complex trying not to let anyone take his sister away, seemed to have gotten scolded the worst of the three, but in any case, that was apparently how Garfiel managed to break free.

"So when I finally got shown to the sittin' room, you guys weren't even there no more. Panicked and came chasin' after you, that's the whole story."

"Ah, my bad. We're just about to head out to negotiate with the guy who's supposedly got the magic ore we're after. Thinkin' about it calmly, if we'd gone without our bodyguard, the whole point of bringin' you along woulda been lost."

"No kiddin', geez."

With a visibly relieved Garfiel added to the party, Subaru and the others made their way to the waterside where the canal boat waited. A pleasant-seeming boatman was waiting on the water, and one by one, they boarded the arranged boat. The boat was small enough to be called a skiff, evidently built with the practical purpose of ferrying tourists and city residents around, with room for only about eight people including the boatman.

"Make the boats too big and you run afoul of city law, and besides, us guides gotta think about gettin' past each other too, y'know. It's all about give and take."

The dark-skinned boatman answered Subaru's question with those words. He'd never given it much thought traveling by dragon carriage along the highways, but here, where canal boats served as an established means of getting around the city, a certain set of traffic rules had apparently been put in place.

"If we crash and go under, people'll just say the boatman's no good at his job. This boat's been passed down from my parents' generation too, so I couldn't show my face again if I sank it."

"Fair enough. Do you ever run into trouble with the water dragons... any hassle like that?"

"Told you it's give and take, didn't I? In the water, there's no way we can match a water dragon, so the custom is: spot one, and you let it go first. Some guide boats are even pulled by small water dragons—might be worth ridin' one at least once, I'd say."

The boatman recommended it with a hearty laugh, and it made Subaru want to try it out for himself at least once. He remembered the thrill of seeing an earth dragon for the first time; he wondered if a water dragon might give him something close to that same feeling.

"This is my first time crossing over water by boat. I'm getting really, really nervous and excited."

"Really? Oh right, there's no sea or anything over here, huh."

"What's a sea?"

"It's like a puddle with no end to it. Where I'm from, it stretched out all around."

"Hmm... but if that's the case, it'd be a relief if you got thirsty, wouldn't it."

Subaru laughed at Emilia's childlike answer. Unfortunately, drinking seawater when thirsty was a surefire dead-end flag. He decided against explaining that seawater was, in fact, saltwater, since there wasn't much point going into it here.

"Most every river's got a bridge over it, after all. Though when it comes to smuggling without passing over a bridge, using a boat is a fairly common method."

"You talk about that like it's personal experience."

"I—I've never actually done it, okay!? I—It's, y'know, just secondhand knowledge! Geez, don't go casting weird suspicions on me, honestly!"

"Otto bro, you're sweatin' somethin' fierce."

Ignoring Otto's confession-like outburst with all his might, Subaru watched as, at the boatman's signal, the small boat slowly slid out into the main current of the waterway. The little boat drifted out along the left lane of the water's flow, heading toward the city center. Strangely enough, looking across from the boat's side to the opposite lane of the canal, that current appeared to be flowing uphill, against the slope's incline.

"How's the water even flowing like this?"

"Fufufu. Actually, I know the answer. Here, look over there, at that edge of the town."

Emilia patted the shoulder of a puzzled Subaru and pointed off into the distance. Turning his eyes that way, he saw a tall stone tower standing beside the outer wall that encircled the city. The massive towers, it seemed, stood one each at the north, south, east, and west of the round city, each asserting its presence.

"Those towers are control towers that regulate the flow of water throughout town. Inside, there's a magic vessel with a really, really complicated mechanism built into it, and it runs on the power of water-attuned magic ore, apparently. I hear the big floodgates in the city are operated from there too."

"Huh, that's a strange thing. Personally, even on the water, the fact that traffic rules still keep everyone in a left lane feels like a fresh kind of surprise to me."

Nodding along at Emilia's explanation, Subaru came to understand the strange mechanism behind the waterways flowing through the city. Priestella, the floodgate city, was indeed—now that he thought about it—quite different from other cities in many respects. Starting with the city's laws, there still seemed to be a great many things he needed to learn.

"City law and all that, but wasn't he also said to be involved in runnin' the city somehow?"

"You mean Kiritaka-san? That's what they said. I wonder what kind of person he is... If we could just get him to hear us out and have him let us have the magic ore, that would really help."

Touching the pendant hanging from her chest, Emilia murmured this without much confidence. Listening to her murmur, Subaru put a hand to his chin and lowered his eyes. Then, his head swaying with the slow drift of the boat, he murmured something small under his breath.

"...gh."

"...What did you just say, I wonder."

It seemed only Beatrice had caught the murmur, faint enough to nearly vanish. At her puzzled voice, everyone but the boatman turned to look at Subaru. Sweeping his gaze over his companions' faces one by one, Subaru smiled and said,

"Uh-oh. I think I'm gonna throw up."

—In an instant, chaos erupted aboard the boat.
