Arc 7 Chapter 18: "The Siege of the Fortress City of Guaral"

——It was a scene that rose up again in Subaru's mind the moment he shut his eyes.

Inside a forest thick with overgrown trees, mud underfoot and the tense, heavy air of armed men. The roar of a Witchbeast that split through it all, and the fighting spirit that surged with the start of battle. And then, the ferocious bloodlust directed at Natsuki Subaru, the one who had summoned it all——.

That was something Subaru had done of his own will—consciously, knowingly, deciding to do it, and then doing it. He had set a balance within himself, weighed what mattered against what didn't, and acted. He had resolved to inflict harm, and acted on that resolve.

What became of men unaccustomed to fighting Witchbeasts, once pitted against one, he did not know. Maybe they'd subdued it in an instant. Maybe someone had been badly wounded. It wasn't something he wanted to think about, but it might not have stopped there—someone might have died.

——No, he ought to assume that someone had.

Having decided to do it and done it, he owed it to himself to justify what he'd done, to admit it plainly. Natsuki Subaru had tipped the scales and carried out a plan that might well have killed the men on the other side.

Not Witch Cult fanatics, not Sin Archbishops. Not evildoers who harmed others out of malice. People who followed orders from above and had chosen to carry weapons as a way of earning a living—people he could have spoken with, people with whom, depending on circumstance, he might have built something like friendship. He had attacked them.

If that was so, then this, surely, was only natural. Inevitable. The way of things.

The bill for Natsuki Subaru's actions was one Natsuki Subaru had to pay.

△▼△▼△▼△

"—Mister, you shouldn't scowl like that."

"——"

"Good luck never visits those without a smile and some room to spare—wh-what, mister!?"

The instant the axe's edge sank into his skull, his vision cleared with a hard, cracking sound. And in that suddenly clear vision, what leapt into view was Flop, a finger pressed to the bridge of his own nose. The handsome young man with the long bangs and slender face—the memory of his head being split open, mere seconds ago, played back, and Subaru instinctively clapped a hand over his mouth and dropped to his knees. Nausea surged up, and the chain of violent, murderous events that had just befallen him tormented him—his heart pounding fit to burst, his innards clenched tight, a shrill ringing filling his ears. Flop's voice, worried for him, sounded distant; none of the words reached his head at all.

"—Todd."

Through the fierce ringing in his ears and the screaming of his own organs, Subaru's lips shaped a short sound. It was the name of someone he'd met in the Vollachian Empire—the first person Natsuki Subaru had ever directed pure hostility at—and, beyond that——

And beyond that, the murderous assailant who had just carried out that ghastly massacre.

"——"

The tavern going up in flames. The billowing black smoke. Drinkers falling one after another to the axe—caused by an irritant substance mixed into the smoke. Probably some crude tear-gas contraption stuffed with strong spices, but the effect had been devastating. He'd used a magic stone to start the fire, sealed the exit, and funneled the survivors toward the back door. He'd blocked that door too, and then, riding the relief of the moment it was broken through, had ambushed and finished off the most capable fighter first. And then, shielding himself from the smoke with a wet cloth, he had gone straight for Subaru and killed him, deliberately, without fail.

His last words, his voice, and above all the eyes visible over the cloth had told Subaru exactly who the man was. That was Todd. ——A man who was supposed to have died in the Buddheim Jungle.

"No…"

Even that had only ever been unconfirmed information. The Imperial Army's camp had been devastated by the attack led by Abel and the People of Shudrak. He'd heard that more than a hundred had died, and, dreading the thought of being wounded by that knowledge, Subaru had shut out anything more. And then he'd quickly taken Rem and decided to part ways with Abel's group.

So he'd never even considered that there might be survivors from the attacked camp. Much less that the same held true for Todd and his men—men who had nothing to do with the Shudrak attack, whom Subaru himself had chosen to attack.

"If this is the nearest city to the jungle, of course survivors would flee here."

It was inevitable that Imperial soldiers who'd barely escaped an attack on their camp with their lives would make for Guaral. And yet Subaru hadn't even considered that, and had strolled, oblivious, right into a city where the enemy lay waiting. With Rem in tow, no less.

"…Enemy."

The word had risen in his mind, and now he set it on his tongue again and tasted its bitterness. Back in his old world, "enemy" was a word that existed only in manga and games. Even after being summoned to this other world, the only things that word applied to were Witch Cultists and Witchbeasts—harmful, hostile things. So this was the first time he'd called someone who wasn't like that an "enemy." And this "enemy," of all things, was one that Natsuki Subaru himself had made, through his own actions.

"—!"

With a hard clack, Subaru bit down on his back teeth. Flesh tore, sharp pain and the taste of blood seizing his consciousness by the collar and dragging it back to reality by force. If that wasn't enough, he was ready to chew clean through every bit of flesh in his mouth. He had no use for his wrung-out organs, the shrill ringing, his trembling limbs, his numbed brain.

He couldn't afford to be crushed by terror and bloodlust right now. Even if it was only bluster, he couldn't let it be stripped away until the situation changed.

"Mister, get a hold of yourself, mister! Do you need some water?"

"—I'm fine. Sorry, Mr. Flop, I worried you…!"

His color and his voice were surely nowhere near "fine," but Subaru told himself so anyway as he slowly got to his feet. His knees still trembled, his gut still churned with discomfort. But he couldn't keep crouching there forever. Even while Subaru indulged in his own fragility, time kept moving. And even now, Todd's plan of attack was surely proceeding, patient and watchful.

"Hey, I need a favor, Mr. Flop. Could you go back to the inn and get my medicine from Rem?"

"Medicine? Mister, is something wrong with you?"

"Yeah, my chronic shiny-ankle disease is acting up pretty bad."

"My word! An unknown ailment!"

Flop's eyes went wide with astonishment at the nonsense diagnosis. Taking advantage of his good nature didn't sit well with Subaru, but he closed his eyes to it—there was no other way right now. Getting Flop away from him, as fast as possible, was the priority.

If Todd's goal was revenge, then splitting up would force him to come after Subaru instead. Once Flop's safety was secured, Subaru could focus entirely on protecting himself. He still hadn't settled on what the right move was, but——

"I'll meet up with Rowan at the tavern, and get out before the ambush. After that…"

It was bound to be a fairly haphazard plan from here on. But with no time to stop and think things through slowly, all he could do was act on whatever seemed best and hope for a stroke of luck.

"If it's Rem…"

If Flop went back to the inn for medicine with some unheard-of name, she'd surely sense that something had happened to Subaru. He wanted to believe she would. And if she did, he wanted to believe she'd keep Flop from coming back to the scene. It was mostly wishful thinking, but it was the best Subaru could manage right now.

"Please, Mr. Flop!"

"A-alright! Wait here! Keep your spirits up, mister!"

Moved by the desperation in Subaru's voice—no act at all—Flop turned back the way they'd come toward the inn. Subaru watched him go, then hurried toward the tavern in the opposite direction. He'd shake the dead-drunk Rowan back to his senses as fast as possible, slap down gold coins, and hire him all over again. Rowan had been taken down by a surprise attack, but surely, when it came to a straight fight, he had the skill—Subaru wanted to believe that too. He was doing nothing but cling to fate at this point, but please, Rowan, be the strongest man in Vollachia——

"—Ah?"

Just as Subaru, in something like a prayer, was about to break into a run, he heard a rasping voice behind him. The alley was deserted; there was only one person that voice could have come from. The question was what feeling had shaped that voice, and what it connected to. But he understood at once that it could only lead somewhere far from good.

"A-aaaaaaah——!!"

Following the rasping voice came Flop's scream, cracking with panic. Subaru spun around instantly and found Flop sprawled on the ground down the road. Flop lay on his back, his raised right leg pierced through the thigh by a small knife. Buried to the hilt, it had stolen the strength from his legs.

"Mr. Flop!"

Subaru, already turned to run, spun back around and bit his lip as his feet slid on the ground. He kicked off anyway, sprinting toward Flop, whose leg had been struck.

Once again, something had blindsided his expectations. If they split into two, he'd assumed the enemy would naturally prioritize Subaru. Instead, Flop being targeted first was completely outside his calculations. For that matter, the attacker striking immediately, right here, had been unexpected too.

"Mr. Flop! I'll get you somewhere we can treat this right away!"

Carrying a heap of regret and self-recrimination, Subaru rushed to Flop's side. Flop writhed in pain from his leg, but there was no time to treat it properly now. Lend him a shoulder, get him somewhere with people—no, that could invite the same disaster as being nearly run down on the main street again. Maybe the best move, even if it strained things, was to drag him toward the tavern. The one mercy in this misfortune was that the knife had caught his leg. As long as no major blood vessel had been hit, his life shouldn't be in——

"——"

Right there, a question suddenly surfaced in Subaru's mind. That Flop's life wasn't in danger was good news. But why was that? Judging by the massacre at the tavern, the enemy was thoroughly skilled at killing. So why had the attack on Flop stopped at his leg?

This was only manga knowledge, but it was about a sniper's tactics in combat. A sniper, hidden and picking off targets from a distance, wouldn't kill an entire group outright when facing several people at once—he'd deliberately wound rather than kill, and then lure out whoever tried to rescue their fallen comrade, cutting them down one after another.

A nightmare tactic exploiting the bond that kept people from abandoning the wounded. Why had that memory surfaced in this exact moment? It was because——

"Oh, n—"

The instant a bad premonition flashed through him, a dark glint flickered in the alley right beside the fallen Flop. An instant later, Subaru, who'd reflexively raised an arm to shield his head, was blown backward by a sharp blow struck head-on.

"Gah!?"

Taking the hard impact full-on, Subaru was sent flying and slammed the back of his head into the ground. His vision whited out, the ringing in his ears screaming again, and Subaru let the momentum of his fall carry him into another roll backward. Putting distance between himself and whatever had struck him, he reached a hand toward his forehead, which throbbed with heat from a blow that hadn't quite finished him off. He figured the hard strike had probably split his forehead open——

"—Ah?"

The right hand reaching for his forehead had been blown clean off at the forearm, between wrist and elbow.

"G-gyaaaaaaah——!!"

The right arm, its filthy stump exposed, showed white bone and pink muscle fiber, blood gushing out in time with his pulse. He scrambled to clamp down on the wound to stop the bleeding, but his left palm too had split lengthwise, its crushed fingers pointing in every direction. Ugly proof that he'd taken a hit from the enemy. For that matter, running to Flop's side unguarded in the first place had been the mistake. Mistake, mistake, mistake mistake mistake mistake mistake, screw up screw up screw up screw up screw up——

"—Hm."

Pressing down on the blood pouring out of him, Subaru was plunging into panic from pain and loss. And it was in front of that Subaru that a small sound clicked in someone's throat—a man with an axe hanging in one hand. Stepping out from the alley was a young man with orange hair bound back in a bandana. Todd.

He wasn't hiding his face this time. The murderous eyes Subaru had glimpsed at the tavern—their identity had been no mistake, no trick of the light. Todd really had come to kill Subaru.

"Ugh, giiiii……!"

His clenched back teeth cracked, and with bloodshot eyes Subaru glared up at Todd. Whether the emotion welling in him was anger, hatred, or something more pathetic—a plea for his life—even he couldn't say, but Todd's reaction was ice-cold. Wearing an expression that betrayed almost no feeling at all, he traced a finger along the axe blade that had destroyed both of Subaru's arms.

"Gotta sharpen this better. Sloppy, sloppy."

He said it flatly, already thinking about what to fix next time.

"Ka, ah……"

"Well then."

Subaru's eyes went wide, his trembling tongue trying to form words. But Todd paid them no mind at all, and with an offhand motion swung the axe up again at once. As if Subaru's words, his very existence, held no interest for him whatsoever——

"A-aaaaaah——!!" "Whoa!"

But before Todd could bring the raised axe down, someone leapt onto his body. Not Subaru, groaning in pain with his arms destroyed. The one who lunged at Todd, to protect Subaru—incapacitated by a single blow—was Flop. The good-hearted merchant, who should have been fighting through agony from the knife in his right leg, clung to Todd's back like he was trying to bite it, and looked at Subaru over his shoulder.

"Mister! Run! Run right now——"

His slender face full of desperate earnestness, Flop's urgent plea was cut off as his chin was jerked upward. Grabbed from behind, Todd struck Flop with an elbow, and the young man, a stranger to violence, was peeled off with ease. Then, turning to face the fallen Flop, Todd raised his axe.

"Don't……" "Ready, set!"

There was no time to stop it—after a light call, a hard, sharp wet sound rang out. Struck by the elbow, Flop's upturned face took the axe blade, his head split along with his face, and the young man became a corpse in an instant. Blood flowed, brain matter spilled, his limbs convulsed. Watching the alley slowly soak in blood, Subaru gaped, mouth hanging open.

Only pain, confusion, and resurging terror robbed Subaru of any coherent thought. This man before him—just what was he?

"Me……"

"Hm?"

"You resent me, resenting me, I get that……"

Dodging the blood of the dead Flop, Subaru said this to the turning Todd. Tears and snot streamed down freely. Blood ran from his mouth where his back teeth had cracked, and doused in the blood spurting from his own severed right arm, Subaru was a horrific sight from head to toe. He hadn't wet himself, at least, though that was no comfort at all.

Once again, he'd let Flop die. By the method he'd used to try to save him, because Subaru hadn't been enough, he'd let him die again. With a death-face he couldn't even show Flop's own sister, Medium—he'd let him die like that.

"But, the people around—don't drag in the people around him, damn it……!"

There was reason enough for resentment. Even if it wasn't exactly justified, there was a history between Subaru and Todd that made it feel like a natural conclusion. So it was inevitable that Todd would come after Subaru. But dragging others into it was against the rules. It was cowardly. Nothing like a fair fight. That was wrong. Wasn't it wrong?

Subaru's accusation was already losing all logical shape. Hearing his outcry, Todd gave a small, low "aah," and,

"What are you even talking about, resent you?"

He said this, tilting his head in apparent puzzlement as he wiped blood off his cheek in the blood-soaked alley. At his blank, unbothered response, Subaru caught his breath for an instant, but unbearable rage surged up almost immediately, and he spat, "Don't screw with me!" spraying saliva,

"You lay in wait, set a trap……relentlessly hunted me……!"

Relentlessly, relentlessly, he'd been driven into a corner. No matter what Subaru did, Todd had gotten ahead of him beforehand, prepared thoroughly, laid traps to guarantee the kill. To target Subaru that thoroughly and then play dumb here—it was a pointless, shameless refusal to own up. It was a desecration of Flop's death. Did he think he was humiliating Subaru, venting some grudge?

"You……!"

"I don't know what you're misunderstanding, but me killing you has nothing to do with resentment. I found something dangerous in the city. Of course I'm gonna kill it, no questions asked."

"——"

"Killing a venomous snake isn't about resentment, it's about fear. And for that, you use whatever tools you can."

Nothing more, nothing less than that, Todd said, carefully peeling hair and skin off his axe blade—fragments, presumably, of Flop's shattered skull—while Subaru could only stare, stunned.

There wasn't a trace of a lie in Todd's calm attitude, even the faint smile on his face. More than that, Todd's every attack on Subaru up to now, proved his words were sincere.

Todd had judged Subaru dangerous, and had no intention but to kill him. That was why he wouldn't listen to anything, wouldn't let him do anything, wouldn't let him say anything. And he didn't even hate Subaru for what happened in the jungle. The only impression Todd had taken from that was that Subaru was a dangerous individual.

Which was why Todd wasn't acting on emotion at all—just flatly, calmly trying to kill him.

"You're the same kind as me. —I'm not giving you time."

As he said it, Todd planted the sole of his boot on Subaru's chest and kicked him flat onto his back. Unable to resist, Subaru fell backward, and Todd straddled him and raised the axe.

His eyes wide, Subaru searched for the right words. Using Return by Death, probing every scene for a path leading somewhere better, was how Subaru had always seized his victories. But there were situations where even that, which had worked even against a Sin Archbishop, didn't apply.

That was——

"—Wai—" "No waiting."

——That was when the person on the other side was a cold-blooded killer.

"—Mister, you shouldn't scowl like that."

"—!"

"Good luck never visits those without a smile and some—wh-what's wrong? You've gone dead pale all of a sudden."

Watching an axe fall straight for one's own face would drain the color from just about anyone's. Reflexively pressing a hand to his own face, Subaru confirmed the coldness of his bloodless skin, and the fact that the hands feeling that coldness were still intact—relief and terror churned through him at once.

"Already, already……"

Subaru had no idea, none at all, what to do.

By the clock, what had happened to Subaru had spanned barely twenty minutes. In those twenty minutes, he'd already died five times. At the climax at the Pleiades Watchtower, he'd repeated "death" more than fifteen times to find a way to win, but there, he'd had the certainty of moving forward one step at a time.

This time, he had none of that. Natsuki Subaru's stacked-up corpses gave no sense of contributing to any victory. If there was only one thing he could say for certain, it was——

"—Right now, too, I'm being watched."

Todd was already watching Subaru while he was with Flop. That was why, the instant they split up, Todd had used Flop as bait without mercy. If Subaru had it in him to sacrifice Flop as a decoy, things would be different, but since he didn't, if Flop was attacked, Subaru would go all-out to save him.

This wasn't like weighing Rem's safety against Todd's men's lives. Flop had been kind to strangers like them. There was no way Subaru could ever choose to let him die. So splitting up from Flop wasn't a workable plan.

At the same time, Subaru had also lost the option of going back to the inn. He didn't know exactly when Todd had spotted him, but if it was somewhere along the way to the tavern, then the inn's location—Rem's location—hadn't been discovered.

Yes, Rem's location hadn't been found out. That much he could trust, fairly firmly. If Todd had known where Rem was, he would surely have used her, more methodically, as a tool to kill Subaru. It was ironic that his trust in Todd's cunning was itself the proof that Rem hadn't fallen into his hands.

"Anyway……"

Biting his lip, covering his face with his palm, Subaru forced his mind to turn. Time—above all, he simply didn't have enough time.

If he split from Flop, Todd would attack immediately. Conversely, waiting to counter an attack—no, this wasn't an opponent he could beat just by dodging the first strike. With no weapon of his own, he'd need to strip the enemy's combat ability in one blow. Impossible.

If he fled to the main street, Todd would send a runaway dragon carriage to run him down. Even if he dodged the runaway carriage, there was a high chance Todd would exploit the chaos on the street for his next move. On top of that, uninvolved bystanders would get caught up in a runaway carriage. Impossible.

If he tried some other route, every alley scattered through the district was Todd's hunting ground. Watching every direction at once was impossible, and even if he managed to parry the first strike, he'd end up in the same spot as the counterattack plan—lacking the combat power. Impossible.

So the best option really was still this: hurry with Flop to the tavern, and before Todd could finish preparing his assault, get their own forces in order to meet him. Just how seriously he could get the blackout-drunk Rowan to take this was the sticking point, but of the options he could think of right now, this had the best odds. Nothing else came to mind.

"Damn it, damn it……"

What a troublesome enemy he'd made. If this were a Sin Archbishop, there'd be something almost endearing about it—men who relied on their Authorities had a weakness built into their strength. Unraveling the trick of the Authority was how their weak points got exposed. But Todd had none of that. He'd use anything and everything, exactly as he'd said himself.

He relied on nothing in particular, and gave no thought to the damage around him. What excuse he planned to give afterward, once Subaru was dead—that too was a complete unknown. He clearly wasn't considering anything past the kill itself. There was a terrifying purity to it: no wasted thought, only killing what needed killing.

"Mister? Are you alright? If something's wrong, maybe we should head back to the inn……"

"N-no! That's no good! That can't happen."

He answered too forcefully, and Flop's eyes went wide at the intensity of his voice. The moment he did, Subaru cursed his own fragile nerves in earnest. If he acted strange, Todd would grow suspicious. And if that happened, the hard-won advantage of Return by Death would vanish.

Since he'd already been spotted, the only leverage Subaru had left against Todd was the assumption—still, presumably, held—that his own presence hadn't been noticed. Whether the plan was to lie in wait, set a trap, flee, or fight back, Todd could never be allowed to realize that Subaru was already aware of him. That was——

"—Wait."

Just then, an idea flickered through Subaru's mind as he desperately tried to plan a countermeasure against Todd. Todd wasn't fixated on any single method, and gave no thought to damage to "those around him." —But what he disregarded was damage to "those around him." Not damage to "himself."

If anything, it was precisely because he wanted to minimize damage to "himself" that he resorted to ambush. Hadn't he said as much, in his own words?

That "killing a venomous snake isn't about resentment, it's about fear." If that was true, then——

"—Todd! I know you're there!"

"Wawawah!?"

Following the idea that had struck him like lightning, Subaru raised his voice in a shout. Flop, startled by the sudden outburst, jumped where he stood. But surely Flop wasn't the only one caught off guard. Todd too, trailing and watching them from hiding, must have been startled.

Trusting in that surprise, Subaru sharpened his eyes, twisted his cheeks into as vicious and villainous an expression as he could manage, and swept his gaze menacingly across the surroundings,

"Well if it isn't the stubborn bastard! I figured you'd finally kicked it after all that, and here you are, still alive—lucky son of a bitch! But not this time, I'm not letting you slip away again! I'm gonna kill you!"

He put every bit of menace he could muster into his voice, smearing the words with malice and hostility, hurling abuse into the air. Wherever in the alley Todd was hiding, he made sure his voice would carry there for certain—so it would be unmistakably clear that Natsuki Subaru knew he was there.

"You think you can beat me? What a joke! I'll laugh, I swear, hahahahaha! I wanna see it again, you running away like a pathetic coward!"

Flaunting both taunt and mockery in equal measure, Subaru laughed loudly in the middle of the alley. He genuinely, truly thanked his own shameless nerve for having stage presence, just this once. If he hadn't, his voice would have trembled, fear would have shown on his face, terror in his eyes. That none of it showed was thanks entirely to the rottenness at the core of Natsuki Subaru's character.

"M-mister……?"

"Shh. Mr. Flop, keep quiet."

Silencing Flop, who stood dumbstruck at Subaru's sudden transformation, Subaru pulled him along by the arm and strode forward with all the confidence he could fake. He wouldn't go back the way they'd come. For now, it was safe to assume Todd was lurking somewhere back there. So partway through walking, he stopped, turned his head to look back down the alley behind him, and,

"Yeah, come at me whenever you like. I'll tear you to pieces just like you want."

Unsure if it would even register, he added a raised middle finger as one final taunt. And then, hiding the thundering of his heart, Subaru put on a fearless smile and walked on down the alley.

"——"

Honestly, it was pure gamble. There was a real possibility that Todd, enraged by the taunt, would burst out of the alley swinging his axe. —But Subaru chose to believe that wouldn't happen. Todd wasn't the type to fly into a rage. That man was the type who calmly, methodically, kept searching for the optimal move.

Precisely because of that, Subaru bet that his current bluff would work on Todd. Tracing back Todd's thinking—spotting Subaru in the city and deciding to kill him—the root of it was eliminating Subaru as a dangerous element. If that goal connected to ambush as the path to victory, fine, but if not, he'd surely pick a different move next.

Todd wasn't attached to any particular method. That was where he differed from a Sin Archbishop. And it was that very flexibility Subaru intended to exploit.

"Now, what next……"

He'd acted on the plan that had struck him in the moment, but what came after was still unknown. If Todd had grown more wary of Subaru, that should have bought some time before his next attack. In this window, Subaru had to choose: fight, or flee.

To fight, he'd need to bring Rowan in from the tavern. With no other options coming to mind, borrowing his strength was the best choice available. To flee, he'd need to head for the inn, grab Rem's hand, and get out of the city. He'd have to ask Flop and the others to come along too—sorry as it was, they'd be in danger otherwise. And if he chose to flee the city, the place Subaru could go was——

"—So that's it."

"Mister?"

Watching the alley behind them warily, Subaru's eyes went bloodshot in that instant. Just this once, he forgot his fear of Todd, his guilt toward Flop, his worry for Rem, his love for Emilia, his longing for Beatrice—forgot all of it. Forgetting it all, he seized the idea that had surfaced and shut his eyes tight. And then——

"—We're leaving Guaral. Right now. Before my bluff gets seen through."

△▼△▼△▼△

Once he'd settled on the next course of action, Subaru moved fast. Even after leaving the alley and setting things in motion, Todd made no move to attack—proof enough that the earlier taunt had worked to sharpen his caution. But a bluff like that would only buy a temporary reprieve.

"He's gonna see through it any second. Right now, before he can, we need to get somewhere out of his reach……"

They had to run. Resolved on that, Subaru brought Flop along with only the barest of explanations, back to the inn they'd just left—the one he'd departed from before all five deaths—and dashed up the stairs. Then he pounded on the door to the room where Rem and the other women were, and rushed inside.

"Rem! Are you—whoa!?"

"Whoa! Oh, it's just you guys! Almost killed ya there!"

The instant the door flew open, a cold cutlass blade was pressed to Subaru's throat. Medium, gripping the blade, withdrew her weapon with an apologetic "sowwy, sowwy." Behind Medium, deeper in the room, Rem's eyes went wide at the near-miss, and,

"W-what's this all of a sudden? I thought you'd left, and now you're back so soon……"

"Rem!"

"—!"

Still startled, Rem started to snipe back at Subaru's abrupt return with his ashen face. But Subaru didn't let her finish—he strode quickly to her side and pulled her into his arms. Held tight from the front, her slender body, Rem shrank her shoulders and held her breath. And then,

"……Let go of me, please."

"…Ah, sorry. I just—got a little carried away……"

"That much I understand. From your state right now, it's clear something serious has happened."

Having confirmed she was safe, Rem calmly pried loose the overwhelmed Subaru. He'd braced himself to be scolded for acting purely on impulse, but Rem only let out a long sigh and, without touching on his rudeness just now, said, "So?"

"What happened?"

"……Ran into someone dangerous. I bought us some time for now, but we can't stay here long. I know we just got here, sorry."

"Leaving the city, then. Understood. Luiche, please carry the luggage."

As if she'd read Subaru's mind—that explaining in detail was a luxury they couldn't afford—Rem accepted the situation smoothly. Of all things, Rui, taking Rem's instruction, gave an "Uー!" and hoisted the luggage onto her back at once. —No, wait, something was off.

"Why haven't you unpacked? We just got to the inn……"

"——"

"Don't tell me, Rem, you……"

Rem fell silent, saying nothing in response to Subaru's suspicious look. But that very silence, held firmly, was as good as confirmation of his suspicion.

"So that's how it was……No wonder you came along so easily……"

"—Mister, you seem to have thoughts on the matter, but now isn't the time for that, is it?"

"Mr. Flop."

Wrestling with a feeling he couldn't quite settle over Rem's attitude, Subaru had his shoulder tapped by Flop, hand on his forehead. At Flop's serious expression, Subaru felt himself pushed back into motion. He couldn't afford to waste the time bought by that once-in-a-lifetime performance.

"Sister, we're leaving the city too, with these three! Apparently there's some dangerous rogue lover after our friend here! We have to get his wife and niece out of harm's way!"

"Whaaa, really, big bro!? But I already took my boots off!"

"Put them back on, sister! Boots can be worn any number of times once you put them back on! That's their strength!"

"Ohhh! Amazing, big bro! You're a boot genius!"

Moved by Flop's fierce persuasion, Medium nodded along and hurried to pull her boots back on. It wasn't Subaru's place to comment on the siblings' exchange, though he did feel that whatever logic had convinced her was deeply suspect—still, he scooped Rem up into his arms.

"H-hey! At least on my back……"

"It's an emergency! The pack frame's already loaded on Mr. Flop's ox cart, and—Mr. Flop! Where's the ox cart!?"

"The inn's stable! I should mention, Falo's Botekurif is practically our third sibling! An adorable little brother we simply can't leave behind!"

"Big bro! Botechin's a girl!"

"An adorable little sister, then!" "Uー! Uー!"

Despite the dire circumstances, trailing an absurdly noisy crowd behind him, Subaru rushed down to the first floor with Rem still in his arms.

"Sorry for the racket! Keep the room fee, we're not staying!"

Passing by the front desk without asking for a refund on the unused night, they burst outside. Heading straight for the stable, they found Flop's cart among those tied up there.

"How fast can Falo run flat out!?"

"Ha ha, I've never actually let him go full tilt. What do you think, sister, how fast?"

"Dunno, but probably faster than big bro!"

Taking in that far-from-reassuring answer, Subaru pushed Rem up onto the cart bed. Right after her, he tossed in Rui, who'd come running along, and threw open the stable entrance. With Flop and Medium climbing onto the driver's seat, preparations for escape were complete. All that was left——

"Um, what's a 'rogue lover'? What did you tell Mr. Flop?"

"Not the time for that right now! Mr. Flop, get Botekurif running full speed!"

"Right! Understood! Run, Botekurif!!"

Rem, giving him a flat look, tugged at Subaru's sleeve where he'd climbed onto the cart bed, but Subaru, ignoring her question, called out to Flop's back instead. At that, Flop snapped the reins and struck the Brave Ox—Botekurif—hard on the back. And the cart began to move. Slowly.

"Way too slow! We're basically walking!"

"Botekurif! Run for us! Listen to your brother, Botekurif!"

"I don't think he considers you his brother……"

Rem's remark felt uncomfortably close to the truth, but Botekurif's running speed—no, walking speed—didn't change. At this rate, Subaru carrying Rem on his back and everyone running would be far faster. And as Subaru's sense of urgency mounted——

"Botechin! Run! Or I'm making you dinner!"

"—!!"

Raising her drawn cutlass overhead and scraping it to make a ringing sound, Medium shouted. Whether it was the wide grin on her face as she said it, or something in her tone that Botekurif took seriously, the next instant the ox let out a great, thick bellow and took off running at a furious pace down the road.

"WHOAAAAAAH!!"

Thrown around by the sudden, tremendous acceleration and jolting, Subaru let out a scream from the cart bed. Bursting out of the stable and charging onto the main street, Botekurif turned sharply, nearly drifting, and Subaru, almost thrown clean off the cart bed, had his hand caught just in time by Rem.

"Th-that was close! Thanks, Rem, your hand's so soft—"

"Huh?"

"Don't let go so suddenly!"

An unnecessary comment slipped out in the rush of relief, and Subaru, released, banged his head on the cart bed. But fortunately, he wasn't thrown off, and everyone remained aboard the racing Falo cart. The Falo cart tore through the street at full speed, drawing every eye as it swerved left and right, dodging other dragon carriages, ox carts, and dog carts, heading for the grand front gate.

"If we make it through the main street, there's the checkpoint at the front gate……"

"No, it doesn't look like it'll be that simple."

"What? Oh, come on, come on, come on, come on!"

Following where Rem pointed, ahead of the racing cart, Subaru's eyes went wide. Blocking the road ahead, deployed straight across the main street, were men clad in armor stamped with the national crest of the sword wolf. Imperial soldiers—trying to bar Subaru's group's path.

"Todd, huh……! Changed strategy and called in his buddies!"

Though Todd himself wasn't visible among the waiting line of soldiers, there was surely no doubt he had a hand in them trying to block Subaru's group's way. Faced with the taunt in the alley, Todd, judging he was at a disadvantage alone, had gathered allies. A thoroughly rational, inevitable decision. Infuriatingly precise——

"—You bastards! Don't think you're getting away from us!!"

And in Todd's absence, standing at the head of the deployed soldiers was a familiar face. A man who looked like the very picture of coarse, brutal roughness, an eyepatch over his right eye—Jamal. He'd been with Todd, and was likewise a man Subaru had once trapped in the jungle. Since Todd had survived, it wasn't strange that he had too, but——

"You had the nerve to sic a Witchbeast on us! I'll kill you!"

"……So he's just simply resenting me. That's honestly kind of a relief."

Jamal's bloodshot eyes and furious shouting felt far more human, and oddly reassuring. Still, that didn't make Jamal's presence any less of a threat. Jamal included, the formation of Imperial soldiers was a genuine danger. The question was how, exactly, to break through it.

"Big bro, keep a good grip on those reins, yeah? Counting on ya."

"Right, go get 'em, sister!"

But before Subaru could settle on his next move in the limited time available, up on the driver's seat, the O'Connell siblings, Flop and Medium, had already arrived at their answer. Before anyone could stop her, Medium planted a foot on the front edge of the driver's seat and pitched forward——

"—Boom!"

——kicking off the seat with the sole of her boot, launching her body forward at almost arrow-like speed. Cutlass drawn, Medium plunged straight into the formation of enemies ahead.

"Heyaaaah!!"

Gouging the ground with her heel to stop herself forcibly in the enemy ranks, Medium swung both arms. Her cutlass carved through the air in a raging storm, and a tremendous shockwave erupted, sending armored Imperial soldiers flying high, scattering blood as they went.

"S-strong as heeeeell——!!"

"Is that supposed to be a compliment, toward a woman?"

"What else am I supposed to say!? That's totally a compliment! Ms. Medium's incredible!"

Taking Rem's cold correction with his honest reaction, Subaru raised his voice at this unexpected show of force. Hearing Subaru's shout, Flop, up in the driver's seat, gave his nose a proud rub with a finger.

"How about that! That's the strength of my little sister for you! I'm hopeless in a fight myself, so my sister and I make up for each other's……"

"You're covering each other's weak points! Now I get what that meant!"

"So you understand!"

Pleased by Subaru's answer, Flop's eyes lit up and he flashed a bright, white-toothed grin. It had all been Flop's own boast until now, but Subaru had just seen the truth of it with his own eyes. Rampaging through the enemy, Medium was cutting down Imperial soldier after Imperial soldier blocking the road, carving a path open for the Falo cart to pass.

"With this……"

"—Don't get cocky, you damn bitch."

"Uwakyan!?"

Just as light seemed to appear, Subaru clenched his fists in hope. In that instant, a flash of white steel lunged at Medium, and though she caught it with her cutlass, her body was sent flying with astonishing ease. Medium, startled by the impact—the one who'd knocked her back was a man gripping a sword in each hand, just as she did: Jamal.

"We're already short on numbers as it is—like hell I'm letting this chaos whittle us down more! Get down here and grovel at my feet, all of you!!"

"Uwakya! Whoa! Big bro, this guy's strong!"

"You've gotta be kidding me!"

Hurling abuse in a fit of rage, Jamal swung his twin swords and closed on Medium. She managed to block, but even from the outside, the difference in raw combat power was plain. This was the first time Subaru had properly seen Jamal fight, and the man was far more skilled than the impression Subaru had formed of him—enough to make him wonder whether it hadn't actually been Jamal, and not someone else, who'd turned the tables on Elgina, the beast Subaru had unleashed in the forest.

"This is bad! No room to maneuver!"

Thanks to Medium's fierce fighting, the number of Imperial soldiers blocking the road had thinned. The Falo cart's momentum looked like enough to break through by force—but only if Jamal, standing planted like a Nio statue in the middle of the road, weren't there. As long as Jamal remained, there was no getting through cleanly.

"—Sister!"

At that instant, Flop shouted. Cornered, Medium spared a glance toward her brother's ringing voice. Perhaps, one might have hoped, some brilliant last-ditch advice was about to fly out——

"—You can do it!!"

What flew out instead was pure, undiluted spirit and nothing else. At that, Subaru's thoughts went blank, and even Jamal was caught off guard. But Medium O'Connell, bound to her brother by blood, was different.

"I can do it——!!"

Roused by her brother's cheer, Medium roared, her cutlass humming with force. Pivoting completely from her defensive stance, she surged into an all-out offensive, and a storm of swung cutlass strokes descended on Jamal's entire body.

"You think a desperate, reckless charge is gonna work on an Imperial soldier……!"

"Ugigh!"

But deflecting that furious assault with his twin blades, Jamal's counter cut into Medium. She took gashes to arm and leg, blood flowing, her face twisting in pain, but even so she diverted her defensive moves into attacks, pressing to pin Jamal down.

Was she seriously planning to stay behind and hold Jamal off like this? Subaru tried to shout, "Don't do that!" desperately——

"Take this."

"Whoa!? Rem, what are you—a rope?"

Before Subaru could shout, Rem shoved something into his chest—a rope that had been on the cart bed. Looking at it, it was quite long, clearly meant for securing cargo, thick and sturdy-looking. But what was he supposed to do with it—while Subaru puzzled over that, in front of him,

"—Eyepatch man!"

Shouting that, Rem stood, hefted a wooden crate loaded on the cart bed, and hurled it boldly straight at Jamal. Called out, Jamal turned with an "Ah?", and seeing the crate closing in on him, swung an arm irritably, splitting it clean in two with ease. And then he took the full brunt of the spices packed inside, showering over his entire body.

"Guoah!? Wh-what is this……"

His vision blotted out by billowing powder, Jamal thrashed his arm in agony. In the opening this created, Medium moved to seize her chance, aiming her cutlass for his back——

"Ms. Medium!"

But faster than that, the Falo cart reached the battlefield where Medium and Jamal fought. Subaru swung the rope Rem had handed him and threw it toward Medium. Seeing this, Medium chose grabbing the rope over striking at Jamal.

"Got it!"

"Got it, roger!"

The moment Medium's voice sounded, Subaru braced with his whole body against the weight suddenly dragging on the rope. Bracing his feet against the cart bed, he took Medium's weight, helping to bring her back. Relying on the rope, Medium leapt, driving herself toward returning to the Falo cart's bed. With that, they'd recover Medium, break through the front gate, and get outside——

"I told you, don't think you're getting away!"

At that instant, tearing through the cloud of spice, an enraged Jamal burst forward. Taking aim at Medium's back as she was mid-leap, he swung his twin swords down without mercy. It looked like Medium was about to be sent into a sea of blood by Jamal's strike——

"Bu—" "That's payback from the riverbank!"

Jamal's face took a direct hit from the pack frame Rem hurled from the cart bed. The pack frame—the one they'd meant to give to Flop's group—shattered to pieces against Jamal's face, its purpose fully served. Jamal was sent tumbling backward, and Medium made it back to the Falo cart. She flung the cutlass she'd been holding onto the cart bed and sprawled out flat on her back right there.

"Ah-bwah-ah-bwah, that was close! So close! Big bro, that was so close!"

"Ohh, indeed it was, sister! Well done, mister, and you too, ma'am! You really saved us!"

"Truly, truly, thank you! You saved us!"

"N-no way. It's completely the other way around, you saved us."

Medium, back on the cart bed, and Flop, cheering her on. But the siblings' gratitude was entirely misplaced. If anything, in this city, the O'Connell siblings had been the ones saving Subaru's group, from start to finish. And on top of that, dragging them into Subaru's own mess—how could he ever apologize enough.

"Stop——! Stop! Sto—whoa!?"

A gate guard, trying to plant himself in the path of the racing Falo cart, dove aside at the last second. Racing past him without slowing, the cart carrying Subaru's group barreled toward Guaral's front gate. Scattering the checkpoint line in chaos, they burst all at once out of the city.

Their time in Guaral had lasted barely three hours—an absurd state of affairs. But somehow they'd broken through the checkpoint and passed the gate. Botekurif would have to keep giving his all, running a while longer to shake off any pursuers. And then, he still needed to talk with Flop's group about what came next——

"——"

Amid the violent jolting, they burst through the gate and out of the city. The view opened up—a vast, sprawling plain and the horizon stretching out—and it was, at last, the moment of escape.

——That was when a figure dropped from directly above the gate, axe raised high, straight at Subaru.

"OHHHHHHH!"

The blow came down, splitting straight for the crown of Subaru's head. It was a strike none of them—not Rem, not Medium, certainly not Flop or Rui—could react to. A nightmare of a blow that could only be blocked if you'd anticipated it. But——

"—Figured you'd try that."

Subaru caught the descending axe with the cutlass Medium had dropped on the cart bed. He'd guessed that right at the moment their escape succeeded, at the one instant everyone's guard would drop, Todd would drive a strike straight into the head of his target: Subaru.

——Five deaths' worth of terror had convinced Subaru that Todd would do exactly that.

"You really should've just let me kill you!"

"Ngh……!"

Bloodlust filling his eyes, Todd forced the axe down with all his strength, trying to cleave Subaru in two. Subaru had blocked with the cutlass, but the impact numbed both his hands; dropping it was only a matter of time.

Neither Rem nor Medium would make it in time for this life-or-death standoff. After finally dodging the fatal blow, to be killed by Todd right here, after all that——

"Aー Uー!!" "Whoa!"

The force behind Todd's pressing axe suddenly weakened. Grimacing, Subaru looked and saw it was Rui, clinging to Todd's body. Her blonde hair flying wild, Rui was desperately struggling to stop Todd's violence.

"Don't get in my way, brat!"

"Ahh!"

Shaking off Rui's interference, Todd struck the girl's face mercilessly with his elbow. Taking the elbow strike, Rui screamed and toppled over.

"—! Damn you, you bastard!"

Seeing that, Subaru gritted his teeth and shoved Todd back with all his strength. Caught off guard, Todd stumbled, and the standoff broke apart, distance opening between them. But that was well within Todd's reach with his weapon—he swung the long haft up for his next strike. Before that could land——

"—You're watching, aren't you! Do it, Kuna! Holly!!"

Before Todd's next blow could come, Subaru screamed as if coughing up blood. He had no idea how far his voice had carried across the plain. He didn't know, but——

"Well then, Subaru. Don't forget—we're watching you." "Right!"

That voice echoed in Subaru's skull like an answer, and in that instant, a whistling sound tore through the sky. And the whistling sound flew straight, sucked into Todd's flank——

"Kah"

With a faint groan of pain, Todd's body was flung violently sideways, sent flying. Unable to stay on the cart bed, carried by the sheer momentum of his sideways spin, Todd was thrown clear off the Falo cart, tumbling across the hard ground without being able to break his fall. He rolled once, twice, three times, growing smaller, farther and farther away.

"Haah, haah……"

"W-what was that just now……?"

On the cart bed, now empty of the blown-away Todd, Subaru dropped the cutlass and sank to his knees, while Rem lifted up Rui, who'd taken the elbow strike. Rem too wore an expression that clearly said she had no idea what had just happened, but Subaru had a certain kind of certainty—that if they could just make it outside the gate, help would be there. The basis for that certainty was——

"That rotten-tempered bastard of an Emperor……when I get back, I'm punching him, no question……"

Slumped weakly against the cart bed, Subaru spat the words out bitterly, picturing the face of the wicked man who, surely, had known all along.
