Arc 4 Chapter 77: "All Alone..."

At the moment he heard that declaration of love, what could Subaru even call the shock that ran through his entire body?

An illusion struck him, as though a bolt of lightning had shot straight through him from the crown of his head to the tips of his toes. A sensation of gooseflesh, as if every pore on his body had opened at once, and beneath his skin, a feeling like the blood in every vessel of his body had begun to boil over. Scarlet crept up from his neck to color his face with each pounding beat of his heart, and Subaru staggered backward, a ragged breath escaping him.

He couldn't keep standing here, not like this. If he stayed here, his breath would reach her. His fingers would reach her. Before instinct could shove reason aside, he had to put distance between them, or there would be no holding back. And if that happened — if it ever came to that — Subaru would be swept away, drowned in love—.

"Stop it..."

"I love you."

"Please, stop..."

"You. Only you. I have loved you, and I will go on loving you, forever and ever."

"I said stop it, damn it—!"

Shaking his head, batting the air with his arm, Subaru tore his awareness away from that hot, clinging gaze. Even now, he still couldn't see Satella's expression, standing there before him. So he had no way of knowing what heat, if any, lay behind the eyes fixed on him. That should have been true. And yet the hot pounding in his chest showed no sign whatsoever of quieting down.

By consciously forcing all of it down, by desperately raising his voice, by hurling out his rejection as though coughing up blood, Subaru kept managing to hold onto the core of himself. Literally — unless he kept fighting, kept struggling to hold onto his own awareness like this, he was certain something fundamental about his very existence would be twisted out of shape. It was too horrifying a thing to imagine.

Even as Subaru bared this much rejection, performed this much revulsion, hammered the fact of it home again and again, Satella simply stood there, unmoving, facing him. Her expression was invisible, wrapped in that veil of darkness — he shouldn't have been able to read it. And yet, impossibly, Subaru could tell that his words just now had wounded her, that she had lowered her eyes. Some part of him wanted to gently stroke her hair, to murmur words of comfort to that pained profile, to whisper love until she smiled again.

Even as he denied her this thoroughly, some part of his heart kept urging him to love Satella.

"Wh— what the hell are you?! What did you do to my body?! Did you plant something in me to control my heart, the same way you planted the seed of 'Return by Death'?!"

Subaru hurled his distrust of his own heart — a heart that refused to obey his own will — at Satella. Here, now, his own heart had begun reacting in ways that suddenly defied all understanding. If a Witch with power beyond the natural order was interfering even with the strongest of his own emotions, that was simply too monstrous to bear.

To warp a person's heart however you pleased — that was an act lower than human, the worst thing a person could do.

For Natsuki Subaru, the very first light he'd found in this world was his feelings for Emilia. Groping blindly with nothing to hold onto, Subaru had first been saved — pulled from a desperate situation — by Emilia's outstretched hand, and the memory of how she had rescued even his nearly-worn-through heart still made her shine, undimmed, even now. Swallowed into the days of a loop that repeated from the point of "death," fighting alone through hardship after hardship, the things he wanted to protect, the people who mattered to him, had only multiplied — and through his exchanges with them, piling up words, bonds, feelings, Subaru had only accumulated more to carry. Now, he couldn't honestly say his feelings for Emilia alone were what drove him. But even so — for Natsuki Subaru, Emilia had been the first light. And Satella was trying to force on him a "feeling of love" equal to what he held for her.

No words exchanged, no warmth of touch shared, no time spent together, no bonds built up — despite there being nothing at all between them, she was trying to steal "love" alone, by force.

What else could you call this, if not horrifying?

"You, and Echidna too... you're both out of your minds! Is everyone here completely incapable of understanding anything?! I'm sick of it!"

Toward the faceless Witch standing before him, and toward the white-haired Witch standing behind him, Subaru raised his voice without hiding his revulsion, aiming it at each in turn. Satella, forcing hollow, substance-less love on him — and Echidna, trying to ensnare others with a curiosity no ordinary person could ever sympathize with — both were monsters beyond Subaru's comprehension.

"I take offense at being lumped in with her. Even filed under 'Witch,' from where I stand, that's a being lower than a Witch, base and vulgar. Though I won't argue with the verdict that she's incomprehensible."

"Shut up. I haven't forgotten your own viciousness either, pretending to care about me. ...Enough. There's no point being here. Let me out. I don't want anything more to do with either of you!"

Spitting his abuse at Echidna's words, clutching his head, Subaru begged for release from the dream castle. He didn't want to spend one more second in front of Satella and Echidna. There were more things than he could count that he needed to do — this was no time to be adding to the pile, here, now. He wasn't clever, he wasn't all-powerful; there was a limit to what he could process. Obstacles already stood in his way that had exceeded his capacity to handle — so why did problem after problem keep piling on?

"I'm not borrowing your hands. Whatever's out there, I'll deal with it myself, all of it. That's fine, isn't it?! That's how it should've been from the start, I should've—"

"And then? You'll die and loop again, make a bunch of people cry, and excuse it all with 'it couldn't be helped, it was for gathering information.' Wow. So impressive."

To Subaru lining up his words of parting, Minerva — arms crossed — snorted derisively. When Subaru shot her a glare with just his eyes, Minerva's composed face flushed red as she said,

"What? You got something to say back?"

"What business is it of yours? The pain, the suffering, the wounds, the wearing away — all of that from 'Return by Death' is my problem. You've got no right to lecture me about it."

"It's easy to talk about being ready for pain and suffering and agony when you're the one saying it, isn't it? Coughing up blood, flesh torn open, bones shattered — no matter what the people watching feel seeing that, you get to keep using the excuse that you're the one shouldering the worst of it."

"What did you just—?!"

"If you're the one who takes the most obvious, most flashy wound of anyone, then the people who get hurt in smaller ways from the fallout of your actions don't get to say anything, do they. Because you're suffering the most. Because you're in the most pain. Because you're hurting the worst... it's only natural that you get to silence everyone else's complaints, right?"

Whether it was the act of speaking that built up her fury, Minerva's tone grew sharper, and Subaru bared his own teeth back at her. At words so full of malice, even Subaru couldn't help but object.

"Are you saying I— that I'm silencing everyone by drowning myself in some exaggerated tragedy?! That the dead end I'm in right now is just some performance for that purpose?!"

"Not exactly that. It's just — the conclusion that 'it's fine as long as I'm the one who gets hurt most' is cowardly. I think Echidna's black-heartedness is questionable, and I don't understand Satella's roundabout way of doing things either, but... I think the way you're twisted is a lot more disgusting than either Witch."

"————"

"And more than anything — from where I stand, someone who beats down and heals every injury there is — your way of living isn't just the opposite of mine, it's my natural enemy. This girl doesn't deserve to go unrewarded like that."

Thrusting a small fist at Subaru, Minerva declared this with her breath coming hard through her nose. Then, at the tail end of her words, she added a small mutter, and her blue eyes turned toward Satella, standing beside her. Satella, still standing there unmoving, had kept silent ever since Subaru's abuse had been hurled at her. Neither affirming nor denying, she showed no sign of any intent regarding the exchange just now. As if lamenting that fact, Minerva's eyes narrowed slightly.

But right now, Subaru couldn't care less about the wavering of their emotions.

"Disgusting... unrewarded...?"

Catching on Minerva's final words, Subaru's shoulders — bowed downward — began to tremble faintly. The trembling grew, and when he finally lifted his face, he was laughing. It was all so absurd that he couldn't help himself.

"What the hell is that. Disgusting, my ass — why do you think I ended up choosing to do things this way in the first place? Why do you think I ended up thinking the way you're calling twisted? Given everything I have to work with, isn't this the obvious conclusion — both the method and the mindset? Isn't it?!"

"————"

"You! You're the one who made me like this!"

Shouting, Subaru hurled his fury at Satella, who seemed to be trying to escape responsibility by clinging to silence. Accepting "Return by Death," using its properties to overcome obstacle after obstacle, crashing into hardship after hardship — Subaru had run this far on that strength. Every time he carved the despair of "death," tasted again and again, into his soul, he had turned it into the strength to take another step forward. That was how he had run this far.

—And it was Natsuki Subaru's own experience, scarred over and over, that had led him to this conclusion.

"Getting hurt, suffering — all of it, every bit, is mine alone! If it's just me, that's a happy ending, isn't it?! I grit my teeth, I choke down my own rage and grief and everything else, no matter how much pain I go through before I die — I won't let the fingertips of that despair reach anyone else! From beginning to end, I'm the only one who gets hurt, and that's fine! What's wrong with that?!"

By repeating "Return by Death," he could, through trial and error, eventually find the path to the best possible outcome. Just as Echidna herself had said. He couldn't accept Echidna's invitation to exploit that resolve to satisfy her own curiosity — but he could take on the same kind of challenge, alone, by himself. Unlike Echidna, who wanted to take needless detours, Subaru — pouring everything he had into finding the best path — would surely need far fewer retries than he would going along with Echidna. Of course, even so, he could already imagine it would still amount to an outrageous number of tries. But even so, it was worth the attempt.

If a future where no one had to get hurt lay somewhere beyond his own scarred, outstretched hand—

"I said I don't understand any of this, that I'm sick of it, just now. Yeah — my bad, my bad. There's not a shred of a lie in how I feel about that, but... there was something I was grateful to you for, too. I forgot that. Forgetting even that — I really was an ungrateful bastard, huh."

"————"

"There's exactly one thing I'm grateful to you for. Thanks for giving me 'Return by Death.' I'll give you credit for that much. Without it, I wouldn't have been able to protect a single thing that mattered to me. I'll go on relying on this power from here on out too. So for that, and only that, I'm grateful."

He'd already made his peace with facing endless trial and error. The option of running from the fate blocking his path had been dead and gone for a long, long time now.

Ever since that moment — when he'd taken her hand and asked her to run away with him together, and she had refused him. There was no option to run. All he could do was keep fighting. That was the vow he'd made. And she was counting on him for that too. Believing in him. Believing that Subaru wouldn't run, that he would keep fighting. A man who kept getting back up — that was who Subaru had to be. If he wasn't, then he couldn't go on being Rem's hero.

"So the one thing I'll thank you for is this power you gave me. Thanks to it, even someone like me with no redeeming qualities at all can push past a dead end and—"

"—...Don't."

"A dead end, and—"

Suddenly breaking her silence, Satella murmured something, cutting through the words Subaru had been piling on, one after another, as if to expel the heat building up inside his chest. Catching a fragment of it, the momentum of Subaru's words faltered. His face stiffened, and he asked, once more, for her to repeat what she'd just murmured. What had she just said? As if it were something he didn't want to hear.

Subaru held his breath, and after a moment of silence, Satella said it again.

"—Don't cry. Don't get hurt. Don't suffer. Don't wear that sad face."

Satella whispered this to Subaru as if pleading with him. At those words, Subaru's heart shook with violent emotion. It was anger, and it was shock, and it was every incomprehensible feeling all tangled together into one.

"Y-you... saying that..."

The whirlpool of emotion was too vast; he didn't know what to say. His throat closed up with the intensity of it, and Subaru opened his mouth, then closed it again, staring at Satella in stunned disbelief.

Satella, still shaking Subaru's unsettled heart, pressed on further.

"So — love yourself."

"S-so that's it after all... you keep doing this, twisting my emotions, and in the end all you want is for me to love you. Words like that won't—"

"—No."

Shaking her head, Satella cut off Subaru's trembling words. Her expression was still invisible. And yet Subaru could feel, on his very skin, how the expression behind that curtain of darkness was looking at him now. —Satella, she was, right now, looking at Subaru—

"—Love yourself more."

Surely, she was gazing at him with a face full of tenderness—.

It took a considerable amount of time for the meaning of those words to sink in, to seep into his brain. And the moment it finally did soak in, what took hold of Subaru's heart was a shapeless wave of trembling emotion.

"What the hell are you... saying now..."

"...Don't get hurt. Take better care of yourself."

"You're the one who gave me the power of 'Return by Death.' You're the one who gave me this power that let me find a way to keep moving forward."

"—I love you. So love yourself too. And protect yourself."

"If I threw this method away out of some selfish love for myself, what the hell would I have left?!"

As if rejecting Satella whispering her endless love, Subaru shouted, pressing a hand to his own chest.

"You know it too, don't you?! I don't have any power at all! No wisdom, no skill, nothing special about me! The only thing someone like me — with nothing — has, is the 'Return by Death' you gave me! So the only thing I have left to pay with is my own life!"

"Don't be sad."

"I've already accounted for the pain, for coming close to death. I'm fine with that — that's fine! As long as I'm the only one who suffers, I'm fine with it!"

"Don't suffer."

"If I get hurt more than anyone, if I go through more than anyone, if I position myself so I can protect everyone — then nobody but me has to go through anything painful! I don't ask for anything more than that!"

"Don't cry."

"It doesn't matter what happens to me, does it?! Nobody's going to care what happens to someone like me, right?! No matter how battered and broken I get, as long as everyone else reaches the future safely, that's—"

Because, if Subaru didn't keep getting hurt on the front line like that—

"As long as the future comes with no one missing from it, that's—"

—Because he might, once again, lose someone somewhere he could never take it back.

"...Rem isn't here."

"————"

"If I'd been smarter, if I'd had more strength, if I hadn't spared myself at all, if I'd been out front risking my own body — I could have avoided it. I should have been able to."

That sense of loss, that despair from that time, had bound Natsuki Subaru ever since. That was why Subaru had chosen, now, without even considering relying on anyone, to fight on alone, getting hurt alone. He believed that was the one correct way to do it.

"That's why I have to believe... I have to believe there's a way to make it work out somehow..."

That "Return by Death" was the means that would solve everything. That if only he could master it well enough, Subaru wouldn't have to lose anything. Believing that, telling himself that, convincing himself that getting hurt was simply necessary — how else could he ever bring himself to face that despair again?

"I... I don't want to lose anyone else, ever again, like I lost Rem—!!"

Clutching his head, Subaru screamed, rejecting every sound he could hear. Before he knew it, he'd sunk down onto the ground. Forgetting even to put distance between himself and Satella, he curled into himself, retreating into his own shell, making himself small, denying that sweet whisper. Poison. It was deadly poison. To Subaru, everything about Satella's existence was poison trying to dissolve his will.

The heart that had sworn it wouldn't break began to crack. And through the cold gap in that crack, despair seeped in, reviving the loss of that day, beating Subaru's heart down again and again without mercy.

"That's not... how a child acts, is it."

A voice murmured, quietly.

Watching Subaru weep and scream, stubbornly clinging to the conclusion he'd reached all on his own, shaking his head in refusal — the Witch who had kept her silence until now murmured, quietly.

"Crying, wailing, whining that you don't want it, taking everything on all by yourself... this is just—"

"————"

"—This is just a lonely, all-alone child, isn't it."

In a voice full of pity, Sekhmet passed that verdict on Subaru now. The other Witches, silent at Sekhmet's murmur, made no move to deny it.

The sight of him was, all too precisely, exactly what the words of the "Witch of Sloth" had just described. Subaru's current state was, in truth, so pitiful to look at that he seemed nothing more than a weak, small child.
