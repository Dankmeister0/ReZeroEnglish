Declaring this with solemn finality, Priscilla-sama leaps, Sunsword still in hand. It was a motion I can only call flight. It looked like nothing more than a light little hop, yet its momentum was as light and swift as if riding the wind. Reaching the canal's edge ahead of the surging crowd, Priscilla-sama points the Sunsword's tip toward the water's surface. And then, right after that.

"—?!"

Before my eyes, a wall of crimson bursts upward — no, that's wrong, it's flame, a wall of fire. The towering blaze goes past red, almost looking white. The light stands stark and fierce, as if refusing even the word "flicker," proving the sheer ferocity of its heat.

What's burning is the canal — the water's surface itself. Water is what puts out fire, water is fire's opposite, its natural enemy — a sight that overturns that very premise. Priscilla-sama's Sunsword flame burns even water. And what's more — all four canals surrounding the plaza, all of them, at once!!

"This is..."

Before that otherworldly sight, the bandage ghoul — no, let's stop that. "Wrath," yes, even "Wrath" is left speechless. Flames that "blazing" doesn't begin to describe, simply there, existing. It halted the crowd trying to cross the canal, pinning them in place.

Of course it would. Moths diving into a bonfire is nature's law, but it's humans who possess the wisdom and instinct to refuse that same ending.

"Claiming it wasn't domination even in appearance, all this talk of love — that came back to bite you. If it's a power that subjugates so thoroughly it overrides even instinct, something this trivial shouldn't be enough to stop them."

Resetting her Sunsword stance, Priscilla-sama mocks the silenced "Wrath" with those words. Scorn, derision, contempt, disdain — there's no other way to describe it, that wicked, beautiful face.

Ah, what a thing to say — I had misjudged her. I'd only ever seen Priscilla-sama's stern, harsh face, and even so had presumptuously thought her beautiful, but that was nothing at all.

There could be no beauty more trembling than this wicked face of hers right now.

"Even binding free will, if the instincts remain unbound, this is the result. It seems none of them intend to be burned alive just to prove the love you speak of."

"————"

"This is the result of the love you've cheaply repeated over and over. This is what they call a laughingstock, Sin Archbishop. Such a grand title, and yet — pathetic."

Wearing that mocking smile, Priscilla-sama goes on and on berating the endlessly silent "Wrath." I found myself gripping tight with sweaty palms, watching how it would unfold. That feeling from a moment ago — "is Priscilla-sama really amazing?" — blew clean away.

Priscilla-sama's amazing! Priscilla-sama's terrifying!

"To begin with, the notion that love means becoming one is shallow to start. I am singular and supreme — on that basis alone, no matter how you struggle, you mediocrities could never become one with me."

Whoa, Priscilla-sama's amazing! Amazing, but, wait, this is heading somewhere weird, isn't it?

"You may approach me, but catching up to me is simply impossible. The love you speak of has already collapsed. Wishing to become one, then, is nothing but empty delusion."

"U-um, Priscilla-sama, maybe it's about time to wrap up that particular—"

"Mistaking it for becoming the same is delusion in the extreme. Wishing for two different beings to become one is a denial of the self. Why should there be value in drawing close to something with no self to begin with. Difference is the fundamental premise; what one does having accepted that difference is the way of the world. —You, vulgar wretch."

"Ukyaaa! Why go so far as to deny her completely!?"

This! This is definitely going to make her snap! Definitely going to! It's definitely going to! Well, Priscilla-sama probably has the confidence to handle her snapping no matter what, but for a small-time commoner like me, I'd really rather things get wrapped up without provoking that, you know!

Just as I feared, at Priscilla-sama's words, the "Wrath" person just hangs her head, saying nothing, dead silent. Well, of course — say whatever you please, however you please, that much, and anyone would snap. If I talked back I'd probably get it back a hundredfold, so even if I snapped, I'd keep my mouth shut.

Completely, hopelessly derailed now, I guess — honestly, if Priscilla-sama could just go ahead and slice "Wrath"-san clean in two right now, that'd wrap things up quicker, maybe...

"Fool. Do that, and everyone but myself will be cut in two along with her. I wouldn't mind seeing this city dyed in blood, once in a while, but there's no need to do it in one's own country. So that opportunity is postponed — you, meanwhile, will fulfill your role."

Ah, right. That was my role to begin with, wasn't it. Falling under "Wrath's" influence apparently means sharing in that person's pain and wounds too. And my song was supposed to be for breaking that condition, or something.

"Now that I'm seeing the real thing, I'm getting super anxious about whether my song can actually pull this off..."

"If you fail, both her head and your heads will drop together, that's all. If you don't want that outcome, best you sing your heart out."

Still clutching Lyulyre, I finally shrink back now, of all times, at the weight of the responsibility and the height of the wall before me. The canal's ablaze, so the crowd can't reach us, but everyone out there is still effectively a hostage, and the only way to free them is my song! And Priscilla-sama's job is—?

"So, so basically, Priscilla-sama's job is to keep me alive until I can win the crowd over with my singing!?"

"Should you die, well, no help for it — I'll simply take her head early. Consider the lives of everyone around here to rest on your throat."

"Ukyaaah!"

"Slow on the uptake. Now then... hm."

I shriek, covering my face, and Priscilla-sama's expression changes. Priscilla-sama looks toward "Wrath," who's remained silent all this while, her well-shaped brows furrowing. With a bad feeling, I peek through my fingers to look that way too, and—

"—How interesting."

"Huh?"

"Interesting, very pleasant indeed. I should call it delightful. No — perhaps putting a name to this welling excitement in my heart would be crass."

Saying this in a low voice, "Wrath" slowly lifted her head. Within that bandage-hidden face, only the eyes assert themselves, rolling wildly around. They looked at Priscilla-sama, looked at the wall of flame, looked at me, and — didn't look at me!

"Sorry, I got a bit carried away. But, thank you. I've woken up. That's right, isn't it — sparing no effort to understand one another, and yet being so selfish about it—"

Was that a smile, maybe? Mouth splitting sideways, an unpleasantly white-toothed grin — the monster smiled. Warmly, as if welcoming a friend or family — even after having just been denied so thoroughly!

"Allow me to properly introduce myself once more. I am a Sin Archbishop of the Witch Cult, in charge of 'Wrath,' Sirius Romanée-Conti. Please, do get to know me."

A small, polite bow. Then the bandage ghoul — monster Sirius — set the chains on her arms jangling. Whirling her hands round and round, unraveling the restraint, extending the chains' reach, spinning them round and round, cutting the wind, the overlapping clang of metal turning downright vicious, slicing through the air itself. And still, the monster smiles.

"This is a Trial! Yes, without a doubt, a Trial! In this city where I've been reunited with my husband, in order to meet that person again and exchange words of love, this must be a Trial befalling me! The rightness of my love is proven by the very fate that reunited me with that person! And you — you are the Trial standing in my way!!"

In a bright voice, blooming like a maiden in love, the monster swings her chains and closes the distance in a single rush. Bad, bad, even a graze and I'm dead.

"So the crowd's mine to hold, and dealing with that thing too. Well then, at least perform a service befitting your insolence."

"Uhieh!? Priscilla-sama!?"

"You focus on preparing to sing. —Should the Sunsword's daylight ever dim, I'll take that vulgar wretch's head regardless of the cost. Before that happens, mind you."

Setting such arbitrary restrictions on me like that——! Not that my protest would ever be heard, of course — Priscilla-sama and monster Sirius's opening clash crossed, and the battle began!

Listening to the fierce clash of sword against chain, I think, somehow, I have to do something. Not so much out of a sense of duty as driven by some indescribable emotion, I found myself running toward the wall of flame burning the canal. In sheer desperation.

Here goes! Damn it, I haven't got a single idea, though!

"For starters, please listen. —Hoshin of the Wilderness, hooowt?!"

Getting as close to the canal as I possibly could, trying to sweep at least everyone within earshot into a whirlpool of music, the moment I got close, my whole face got a good lick from the flames below!! No way! No way! I made a show of trying to soldier through the ballad, but Priscilla-sama's wall of flame is super hot! Super hot! Hotter than normal! But I can't sing without getting this close, since honestly, nobody would even see me otherwise!

"Uwooooh, hot hot hot hot! My poor little hands are getting scorched! If my throat, my lungs burn away, this fight is our defeat... wait, huh?"

Having gotten a good tongue-lashing of fire and regretting my recklessness, thinking my face was now beyond a second look — both my hands, which should've taken the same amount of flame, are completely unharmed... and, wait, the wooden Lyulyre took the fire too and isn't burning at all, what's going on?

"Not... normal fire...?"

Well, considering the source, it was obviously never going to be normal fire to begin with, but this seems like — it doesn't burn? Hot, but doesn't burn?

Then that means! Maybe this can eventually be overcome! What is this thing!? A flame that's all bark and no bite!?

Guess bringing me along was a mistake after all——!!
