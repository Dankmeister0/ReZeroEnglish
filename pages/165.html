Arc 3 Interlude: "Each Their Own Vow"

—Lying there on the bed, her expression was peaceful, so much so that to Subaru she seemed as if she were simply asleep.

Looking at the lashes framing her closed eyelids, he thought vaguely that they were quite long. Even her face, which normally kept a deliberate blankness, was now filled only with the softness proper to her age, now that it no longer had to guard itself in sleep. Now that he thought of it, he'd never once had the chance to see her sleeping face.

She had always woken earlier than Subaru, and there had never been an occasion for her to go to bed later than him. The Rem he knew was always resolute, disciplining herself, hiding how young she really was. And yet that stubborn front of hers had so often come down in front of him.

Her surprised face, her flustered face, her sulking face, her face on the verge of tears, and the smile she'd show him afterward once they'd made up — there should have been so many more chances to see them, from here on.

"—Rem."

He called her name, and touched her pale cheek, but there was no response from her.

She lay on the bed not in the familiar maid's apron dress he knew so well, nor was the white headdress that adorned her sky-blue hair anywhere to be found.

The clothes she wore to work, to fight — that was, after all, something she no longer needed now.

"So this is where you were."

A voice called out to Subaru as he passed stagnant time in the still, silent room.

Slowly, with reluctant movements, he looked behind him to find a woman standing there, her long hair swaying gently. She wore a deep navy dress, unostentatious yet brimming with refinement, and even the demure way she walked toward him overflowed with elegance.

And yet, faint though it was, hesitation and uncertainty came before all her actions, and combined with the dignity she naturally possessed, it left a strangely mismatched impression. Subaru, too, couldn't help but feel that same sense of wrongness in dealing with her.

"Is she..."

"Nothing's changed. Not like there's anything I could do anyway — I was just here. Pathetic and pretty pitiful of me, I know."

"That's not... I think she'd be glad of it too, wouldn't she?"

The woman offered comfort, though timidly, to Subaru as he hung his head. But his reaction to it was fierce. He raised his head, sharpened by her words, and glared at her face. He hadn't meant to make his gaze so sharp, but he couldn't help it. In response to Subaru's reaction, the woman put a hand to her mouth and apologized, "I'm sorry."

"I spoke out of turn. I've upset you, haven't I."

"No, I'm the one who should apologize. That was just me lashing out... misdirected resentment, really. At this rate Rem would really scold me for it. Something like, 'Taking it out on people and hurting them like that isn't good, is it, Subaru-kun.'"

He shrugged, lowered his voice, and imitated Rem's manner of speech.

In his mind he could trace the words in her voice exactly. It was sad that this, too, was something no one but Subaru could understand anymore.

Even an impression that resembled her not at all had no one left to point that out.

At Subaru's hollow gestures and empty voice, the woman before him lowered her eyes in pain, holding her left arm with her right hand as if bracing it, and fell silent.

Silence settled over the room, casting a shadow over both their minds. Steeping himself in that familiar sensation, Subaru shook his head inwardly — this wouldn't do.

To keep soaking in this sea of despair would surely be easy, would surely spare him the sharp pain. But that wasn't something Natsuki Subaru should do.

Not something the man Rem believed in, the man she loved, should ever do.

"Was there something you needed from me?"

"Yes. Everyone would like to gather once and talk together. They're all in the sitting room, so if you could, um..."

When he broached the subject to prompt her along, the woman nodded with a relieved expression and continued speaking. But her words caught partway, and seeing her uneasy face, he furrowed his brow — then realized, belatedly.

"It's Natsuki Subaru."

"...I'm sorry. Natsuki Subaru-sama, is that right? I'll remember properly. I've been told you're someone to whom I owe a great debt, and yet I've been rude. I apologize."

"It's fine. There's too much for you to remember right now, so I'm not going to worry about it."

The woman bowed her head, truly looking apologetic.

And every time she behaved so gracefully, so — if it must be said — womanly, that sense of wrongness stabbed at his chest. Though even Subaru wasn't crass enough to say so aloud.

Shaking his head, Subaru cast aside both her gratitude and his own unease, and stood up. Finally, he turned back to look at the girl on the bed, gently brushed the hair from her forehead with his fingers, and said,

"See you later then, Rem."

Her faint breathing, and the body that undeniably still existed here.

—For her, forgotten from everyone's memory, that body was the only self she had left.

Leaving her behind him, Subaru turned to face the woman, gestured toward the door, and said,

"The sitting room, was it? No point keeping them waiting too long — shall we go?"

"Yes. Let's do that, Natsuki Subaru-sama."

She tilted her head slightly, smiling faintly, a fragile-looking woman. Her long green hair swayed with the motion, making her femininity stand out all the more sharply.

Hating to acknowledge it, Subaru turned his face away, hiding his true feelings behind a polite smile, and said,

"Sorry to make you come get me — Crusch-san."

And so he called out the name of someone who now seemed almost like an entirely different person.

—By the time Subaru reached the royal capital, everything was already over.

He barely remembered the conversation with Emilia along the way.

He should have been at her side, filled with the satisfaction of having finally rescued her, but as the dragon carriage raced onward, Subaru's mind was consumed by the existence of one other girl alone.

"—Who's Rem?"

Puzzled, confused, she had spoken those words with her head tilted.

If there had been even a fragment of her usual joking manner in that gesture and those words, Subaru might have been able to play along, trading light banter with her over it.

But Subaru couldn't find a single shred of hope in her demeanor, and Emilia never once, before his stunned face, chirped "Just kidding~" to lighten the mood.

Petra didn't remember her either, nor did any of the other children.

Having confirmed that much with everyone in the carriage, Subaru urged the driver on with a face like death, racing straight for the royal capital.

He wanted to believe it was some kind of mistake. He believed it couldn't be true.

Because everything should have gone well. Subaru should have grasped the best outcome. He'd saved everyone, accomplished his goal, overcome pain and suffering and sorrow and all manner of things — he'd taken wounds that would never heal, but he should have managed it somehow.

And yet—.

"Ah, it's Subaru-kyun. As expected of Crusch-sama — finding a wandering cat is right up her alley, isn't it nyaa?"

While making his way to the sitting room, one figure spotted the two of them walking down the corridor and called out. It was Ferris, his short skirt swaying, his cat ears freed from his knight's attire and twitching. He came bounding over with a springing gait, and gently took the hand of Crusch, who had stopped walking.

"Ferris-sa—"

"It's just Ferris, nyaa. After such a long, loooong friendship between Feri-chan and Crusch-sama, adding a -san now is so cold, so lonely, isn't it nyaa? Really, how mean."

With his other hand, Ferris poked affectionately at Crusch's shoulder. Crusch showed a faint bewilderment at his overly familiar attitude, but seemed, on the whole, willing to accept it, bowing her head and saying "I'm sorry."

"It won't be as easy as before, but I'll do my best. Ferris... yes, Ferris."

"No need to rush, nyaa. Feri-chan is always on Crusch-sama's side, and I'll stay by your side no matter how long it takes. Besides, getting to be with such a demure Crusch-sama like this feels like discovering a whole new charm — kind of makes me happy, nyaa?"

Ferris swung her captured hand up and down, chattering giddily. Watching sidelong, Subaru couldn't help but feel a small flare of irritation rise within him.

He couldn't understand how Ferris could keep treating Crusch, changed so utterly, in exactly the same way as before. Subaru couldn't guess what conflict lay behind that smile, but even so, its lack of humanity stood out all the more starkly to him.

"Subaru-kyun, come on to the sitting room. Emilia-sama and Vil-jii are already there, nyaa."

"...Yeah."

Discontent crept unmistakably into his voice, but Ferris paid it no mind. "C'mon, c'mon, Crusch-sama," he said, leading her by the hand toward the sitting room.

Crusch, sensing the strange atmosphere flowing between Subaru and Ferris, knit her brow in confusion and looked between the two of them, but in the end followed after Ferris without managing to put anything into words.

Exhaling, Subaru bit his lip lightly and closed his eyes.

He was on edge. Right now, he could face anyone only with badly frayed emotions. He didn't want to comfort himself by calling that unavoidable.

He didn't want to blame this ragged state of his emotions on her, of all things.

He fell into step behind the two of them, entering the sitting room last. Feeling everyone's gaze gather on him, Subaru looked around the room. Aside from himself, there were four people present — Emilia and Wilhelm, along with Crusch and Ferris, who'd entered ahead of him.

He was likely the last of those gathering here. Closing the door behind him, he sat down beside Emilia — not because anyone invited him to, but as a natural matter of course.

"Subaru..."

"I'm fine. I've calmed down now, Emilia-tan. —I'm okay."

At Emilia's concerned call, Subaru loosened his mouth slightly in response. Still, he didn't turn his gaze toward her, nor could he look at her directly. Right now, being close to Emilia felt like it would lay bare every ugly side of himself, and that terrified him unbearably.

"Well, since the main faces are all here, let's talk."

Clapping his hands to draw everyone's attention, Ferris took charge of the proceedings. Since Crusch, as things stood, couldn't serve as facilitator, the role fell to him by default. He swept a glance over those seated, stepped alone to the front of the room, and raised a hand.

"Doesn't seem like anyone's against it, so let's get right to it... shall we confirm the situation, nyaa?"

Smiling, he began to move forward with the proceedings everyone had been waiting for.

※※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※

—The situation Rem, Crusch, and the others had fallen victim to was simple enough at its core: a simultaneous attack by Archbishops of Sin of the Witch Cult — two of them appearing at once, no less.

After the battle with the White Whale, while cutting away only the head of the beast and heading back to the royal capital, Rem and the others — wounded and all, in the midst of their triumphant return — had been attacked by Archbishops of Sin.

In that attack, half the subjugation force returning home had been killed. The beastman mercenary corps accompanying them had withdrawn immediately, each on their own judgment, and thereby escaped disaster, or so it was said.

"From what I hear, by the time the vice-captain and the others brought the royal capital's knights back to the highway, neither of the Archbishops of Sin was still there, nyaa. What was left was the corpses of our knights, and..."

"...People in circumstances like my own, I take it."

Crusch picked up the end of Ferris's account, biting her lip and knitting her brow. The anguish that surfaced in her expression was likely rooted in her own sense of inadequacy.

After all, she could probably only think of this story as if it had happened to someone else.

Because—

"My own memories, erased... huh. You think this is the Archbishops of Sin's doing too?"

"Ten to one, I'd say, nyaa. Feri-chan's seen a few patients before with symptoms just like Crusch-sama's. Their own memories suddenly wiped clean, and even Feri-chan's magic couldn't restore them. Up till now it's always been chalked up to unknown causes, but..."

"The Archbishop of Sin of 'Gluttony' — its Authority, without a doubt, I should think."

Wilhelm nodded gravely. The old man's expression was stern, his sharp gaze fixed on Crusch. At that sharpness, Crusch shrank back slightly, and seeing that reaction, Wilhelm apologized, "I'm sorry."

"I hold nothing against you, Crusch-sama. I'm sorry to have frightened you. I still have much to learn, it seems."

"No... I'm the one who should apologize, for being such an inadequate master. I am trying to remember you as well, Wilhelm-sama, but..."

At being called "Wilhelm-sama" by Crusch, a faint pain crossed the old swordsman's face. It was likely the responsibility of a retainer who felt shame both at his beloved lord's pitiful state and at his own failure to protect her. Since Subaru held similar feelings himself, he could now understand the old man's heart as if it were his own.

What he couldn't understand, conversely, was Ferris, who showed so little reaction to the transformation of his beloved master.

Ferris ignored Subaru's inner thoughts completely and continued,

"Just when we thought we'd cleaned up the Archbishop of Sin of 'Sloth,' now suddenly it's 'Gluttony' and 'Greed,' nyaa? Talk about overachievers. Well, I guess it makes sense that all the Witch Cultists moving at once like this is unusual — it's probably tied to the rare event of Emilia-sama rising to prominence, nyaa."

"M-me...?"

Suddenly named, Emilia looked at Ferris with a startled expression. Nodding to her, Ferris swayed his body coquettishly and said "Well—"

"There's no way the Witch Cult would overlook the existence of Emilia-sama, a Half-Elf, is there nyaa? Normally they hide so quietly it's downright creepy, but whenever they do kick up a fuss, it's always tied to something like that."

Listening to Ferris's reasoning, Subaru crossed his arms and recalled a previous conversation. The night before the battle against the White Whale, Subaru had discussed with Ferris and Crusch the possibility of the Witch Cult making a move, and they'd responded affirmatively.

The conclusion they'd reached then must have been based on just such precedent.

But,

"That... I don't really know much about this 'Witch Cult' thing... the Witch is the Witch of 'Envy,' right?"

Emilia's timidly raised hand produced a startling remark.

Subaru doubted his ears at the words, and could see Wilhelm's and even Ferris's expressions stiffen. The only one not surprised was Crusch, left just as behind on the situation as Emilia.

At the reaction of the three of them, Emilia grew even more hesitant.

"I'm sorry. I can tell from everyone's reaction that this is something I'm supposed to know, but I was never told. Really."

"But Emilia-tan, you knew about the Witch of 'Envy' at least, didn't you? Because I remember when we—"

At their very first meeting, Emilia had given a false name, calling herself Satella. Subaru, who hadn't yet known about the loop, had called her by that same name after dying once, and earned her anger for it. In other words, she knew that the Witch of "Envy" was a name not to be spoken lightly.

But Emilia shook her head at Subaru's words.

"In the settlement near the forest where I lived, I was hated for, um... resembling the Witch of 'Envy.' So I think I understand how the Witch of 'Envy' is treated, as an existence. But people called the Witch Cult, I..."

"Let's set aside what kind of life Emilia-sama's been living for now, shall we. Even so... the very person involved not knowing this much is really something else, nyaa."

Shrugging and raising both hands, Ferris said this with a sarcastic edge and let out a sigh. Feeling irritation at his attitude, Subaru clicked his tongue and glared at him.

"That's a shitty thing to say. Have you ever thought about how much courage it takes to admit you don't know something you don't know? What's wrong with asking about something you need to know?"

"Coming from Subaru-kyun, that really carries weight, nyaa. Truly, master and knight both."

Ferris's words didn't even bother hiding sarcasm at Subaru's irritation. This time Subaru's indignation flared clearly and he began to rise from his seat. But—

"Ferris. That remark just now cannot go unremarked. Apologize."

Before Subaru could put strength into his knees, a voice rebuking Ferris came first. And of all people, it was Crusch, who had been watching the situation unfold in silence, who had spoken.

The woman in the dress, her weak expression from moments before suddenly transformed into something as dignified and sharp as her old self, fixed her own knight with her gaze.

"As Natsuki Subaru-sama said, there is nothing laughable about the attitude of asking about what one doesn't know. You have no right to mock it. Do you understand?"

"...Yes, Crusch-sama."

After that severe rebuke, softness returned to her tone at the very end — that was Crusch as she was now. Glimpses of her former self flickered at the edges of her words and manner, and Subaru found himself surprised. Ferris, too, with eyes that couldn't quite hide his own astonishment, said,

"Emilia-sama, I apologize for the rudeness. Subaru-kyun too, my bad."

"You're... nah, forget it. More importantly, let's talk about the Witch Cult. Emilia-tan wants to hear it too, and honestly, I don't know all the details myself."

At Subaru's somewhat resigned words, Ferris responded lightly, "Roger that." Then he put a finger to his lips, swaying the hem of his skirt.

"First off, the Witch Cult is, as Emilia-sama said, a group that worships the Witch of 'Envy.' Hardcore fanatics who've been active since four hundred years ago, back when the Witch was at the height of her power. Even to the Knights, they're such thorough villains there's a standing order for immediate extermination."

"Immediate extermination... are they people bad enough that such a harsh order would even be allowed?"

"They're the type who'll turn a village or a capital into an enemy without a second thought, if it serves their purpose, nyaa. Actually, this time too, a village near the Margrave's mansion was in danger, and down south in the Vollachian Empire, an entire city got wiped out by the Archbishop of Sin who showed up this time, you know?"

Emilia blinked in disbelief. While agreeing with her reaction, Subaru already knew the terror of these people down to his bones. Petelgeuse's example alone was enough to gauge the abnormality of the others. Though from what he'd heard, "Greed" seemed to be an opponent whose combat strength was on a whole different level from Petelgeuse's.

"Bit of a jump in the conversation there, but the Witch Cult has six bishops bearing the names of the Sins — basically, the organization's top brass, nyaa. Each apparently carries the name of the Sin belonging to one of six Witches who existed separately from the Witch of 'Envy.'"

"Six Witches... 'Sloth,' 'Greed,' 'Gluttony,' 'Lust,' 'Wrath,' and 'Pride' — those six, right?"

"Yep, that's right, nyaa. Among them, the especially famous ones are 'Sloth' and 'Greed.' Greed's famous for the rampage that destroyed a city, like I just said. Sloth, on the other hand, was usually the reason behind most Witch Cult incidents. But as for Sloth, thanks to this subjugation force's efforts, that one's been successfully defeated... is what it comes down to. Right, Subaru-kyun?"

"Yeah... 'Sloth's' Petelgeuse is definitely dead. I watched with my own eyes until he dissipated completely, so there's no mistake."

Nodding to Ferris, Subaru thought back to the final moments of the hateful Petelgeuse. That scream, calling Subaru's name with hatred, lingering, refusing to leave his ears. A shriek like a curse of resentment — was that what was now forcing this brutal fate upon him?

"That leaves five remaining Archbishops of Sin. Two of them are the culprits who attacked Crusch-sama and the others this time, nyaa. Witch Cultists appear and vanish without warning, and how they go into hiding when they're not active is a complete mystery. Even after four hundred years, wiping them out entirely hasn't made any progress. Their goal is... said to be the revival of the Witch of 'Envy,' nyaa."

"Revival of the Witch...!?"

At that unbearable remark, Subaru kicked back his chair and stood. While the women present flinched in surprise at his sudden movement, Subaru waved his hands wildly.

"Revival — is that even possible? The Witch of 'Envy' died four hundred years ago, didn't she? Bringing her back to life, that's..."

"Subaru-dono, the Witch of 'Envy' is not dead. Her life is still bound, even now, at the edge of the world. Loathsome though it is."

Wilhelm told Subaru this in a quiet voice as Subaru's voice rose.

Eyes wide with shock, Subaru turned toward him, and Wilhelm narrowed his eyes, sternness settling over his expression.

"Near the Great Waterfall stands the Evil Sealing Stone Shrine. The Witch remains there even now, sealed away, unable to be fully destroyed. Even with the power of the Sage, the Dragon, and the Sword Saint combined, her existence could not be utterly annihilated."

"Sealed... no, wait, I think I've heard something like that before... but then, all the more reason — couldn't they just destroy the shrine if they wanted a revival? Why don't they do that?"

He recalled hearing something like that from someone before. But that only brought up the question now in his mind. If she was sealed, all they'd need to do was break the seal and let her out. Why they didn't, and why the Witch Cult instead scattered destruction and slaughter every time a Half-Elf appeared, made no sense to him. But Subaru's point was again cut off by Wilhelm shaking his head in denial.

"First, there is the matter that approaching the shrine is nearly impossible. Near the Great Waterfall, the working of mana drops drastically. In that place, there's scarcely any being who could withstand the Witch's Miasma within the shrine. Moreover, physically slipping past the Sage's watch is not possible."

"The Sage...?"

"The Sage, Shaula. A hero who, together with the first Sword Saint and the Divine Dragon Volcanica, worked to seal the Witch. She's said to still be living in seclusion near the Great Waterfall, at the Pleiades Watchtower even now. Though 'seclusion' is really only a name for it — the truth, I imagine, is that she keeps watch for anyone plotting the Witch's revival."

"That's... quite the long life she's had..."

If she'd been there since four hundred years ago, then that made for quite a long-lived Sage indeed.

Something about it nagged at him slightly, but Subaru tilted his head and set the question aside for now. Turning back to Ferris, he said,

"Okay, I get why they can't just drag the Witch out. But even so, I still feel like that's a weak connection to this whole 'revival' business..."

"Don't ask Feri-chan that, nyaa — Feri-chan's not a Witch Cultist, so I don't know the real reason. We'll just have to hope some captured prisoner spills something useful under interrogation or torture, won't we?"

Ferris tossed off an answer to Subaru's question by simply giving up on thinking about it. Subaru felt dissatisfied at that, but since Ferris's point was reasonable enough, he let it drop.

In any case, having heard the story that far, Emilia nodded.

"So that's how it is. That's why I was like that just now... but Puck never said a single word about any of it."

"What's Puck saying right now? I've got a mountain of things I want to talk to him about too."

"That's the thing, he won't answer when I call him. He seems to be materialized, but all I can tell is that he's somewhere nearby..."

Subaru couldn't bring himself to say "it's fine, it's fine" to a shrinking Emilia. In truth, a talk with Puck was essential for him. After all, it was a fact that Puck's remarks had had an influence on why Subaru had failed to anticipate the appearance of the Archbishop of Sin of "Gluttony."

"Anyway, that's about it for the Witch Cult, nyaa. So, let's move on to talking about what comes next, taking all that into account."

"What comes next...?"

As Ferris clapped his hands to shift the topic, Subaru looked up to find him nodding at Subaru with an extraordinarily bright expression, and, still smiling that way, said,

"Honestly, this alliance thing... why don't we just call it off, nyaa?"

※※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※

—The air in the sitting room froze, while inside Subaru, the heat rose instead.

Taking in the words he'd been told, weighing them, Subaru wet his lips with his tongue and asked, in a quiet voice,

"What did you just say? Cancel the alliance? What's your angle?"

"Exactly what it sounds like, nyaa. Because as things stand, I feel like there's hardly any merit left for either of us in continuing this alliance, nyaa."

Ferris's expression seemed almost impressed that Subaru hadn't flown into a rage — and even in that, Subaru thought he sensed a hidden intent to provoke him, to gain the advantage in negotiations, which only made him more strictly command himself to stay calm. Though he couldn't quite hold back the boiling in his heart.

"Never mind the mining rights business — we agreed on cooperating to subject the White Whale, and we actually pulled it off. Grabbing only the good parts and saying 'sayonara' — isn't that a bit too shameless, no matter how you slice it, huh?"

"It's that the downsides got too big, that's all, Subaru-kyun."

"Ah?"

Against Subaru, who couldn't help but adopt a combative stance, Ferris never once lost his composed attitude. He waved a small raised finger and said,

"See? Now that there's precedent for the Archbishops of Sin of 'Gluttony' and 'Greed' showing their faces at the same time, there's a high chance the Witch Cult will come poking around Emilia-sama's camp even more than before. This time, Crusch-sama ended up a victim of this... do you really think anyone wants to get involved any further than that?"

"That's..."

At Ferris's argument, Subaru grimaced and glanced sideways at Crusch. Seeing how utterly she'd changed, Subaru hesitated to flatly deny what Ferris was saying. He himself bore just as deep a wound.

For that reason, it wasn't Subaru who raised his voice in objection to Ferris's remark, but—

"I object to that opinion, Ferris."

Wilhelm, still seated, leaned his body forward and said this plainly, his eyes fixed on Ferris. At those words, Ferris narrowed his eyes and gave a thin smile, saying "Oh?"

"And what would your reasoning be, nyaa? Crusch-sama has suffered this way from 'Gluttony,' and yet you'd still keep the alliance with Emilia-sama and the others going, still get involved with the Witch Cult. What's the merit in that?"

"There will come a chance to take revenge upon 'Gluttony' — upon those who wronged our master."

"Is that more important than Crusch-sama's life, nyaa?"

Against Wilhelm's resolute declaration, Ferris pressed back without yielding a step either. In both their words lay the weight of thoughts for their respective masters, making each remark heavy.

"If we keep on with the Witch Cult, something like this time will surely happen again. And when it does, Crusch-sama as she is now can't even protect herself. Feri-chan will heal her wounds, her heart, whatever it takes... but if she dies, that's it, nyaa. You get that?"

"But we cannot simply let the very perpetrator who caused this situation slip away scot-free. Moreover, Crusch-sama's memories might well be restored if we defeat that Archbishop of Sin. For us to withdraw now would be far too rash."

"Defeat the one who did it and get the memories back? What are you even saying, Vil-jii? Beating the very person who ate her memories to get them back — sounds like you've been reading too many fairy tales or watching too many plays, nyaa—"

"—Felix!!"

A sharp, furious cry and killing intent swept through the room, and everyone present felt the illusion that a wind had actually blown through it.

While everyone flinched in surprise, Wilhelm, his sharp gaze unbroken, said,

"Felix. Never say those words again, not in front of Subaru-dono."

"...Sorry."

Called twice by his real name rather than his nickname, Ferris lowered his eyes with a pained expression.

Both their gazes turned toward Subaru, who had sunk back into his chair and was looking down. His hand was clenched hard and tight, shaking faintly as it drew blood.

Gently, a pale set of fingers came from above to cover it.

"...Emilia-tan."

"I can't say something like 'it's okay' as if I understand everything. I want to understand how you feel, Subaru, but... I don't know anything about that girl you've forgotten, so anything I said would just be cowardly, I think."

Glancing up, he saw Emilia's violet eyes wavering with grief. His own weak-looking face was reflected in her eyes, and realizing that this was the Natsuki Subaru she saw right now made him furious at himself.

Feeling saved by her concern for him even now, Subaru shook his head.

"I can't say 'it's okay,' not even under pain of death, but right now, I'm fine. Don't worry about it, Ferris either. I... haven't given up a shred of hope."

"Really, Subaru-kyun just doesn't know how to give up, does he, nyaa."

Feigning nonchalance to match Subaru's own pretense, Ferris likewise softened his expression. Still, his position remained unchanged from before.

"Feri-chan doesn't agree with continuing the alliance. Feri-chan will definitely get Crusch-sama's memories back. So revenge against 'Gluttony' can just be left alone, nyaa."

"What should be done, what you will do... that too is entirely Crusch-sama's judgment to make. It is not something we should decide carelessly, on our own."

In the end, there was no landing place for the discussion but there. As both their gazes gathered on her, Crusch, with the air of one who'd known this was coming, nodded firmly.

"Right now, there is still so much I don't understand. I can't remember a single thing about who I used to be. I imagine that for all of you, dealing with me now is nothing but bewildering... even so, my first thanks go to all of you, for respecting me as I am. And if I can, I would like to live up to your expectations. I intend to keep striving toward that."

Crusch Karsten, resolute in bearing even after losing her memory.

How far does a person's will, their essential self, remain rooted in their core? Watching her remain as she was, even after undergoing the phenomenon of losing her memories, Subaru couldn't help but wonder.

In any case, the discussion of the alliance could only be set aside for now.

"Either way, the practical talks need someone who actually understands the inner workings of Emilia-sama's camp — Margrave Roswaal or someone like that, nyaa. Let's set up a place where we can talk with the Margrave present, and go from there."

"Yeah, that's fine. And, about what happened this time..."

"Not a word to anyone — that much, alliance or no alliance, you'll keep to."

Fixing Subaru with a sharp gaze, Ferris said this in an uncharacteristically low voice.

Subaru unconsciously caught his breath, but had no reason to object, and nodded. It was only natural, as a decision on their part. If Crusch's current state leaked outside, her position as the "frontrunner of the Royal Selection" would undoubtedly be lost.

Weighed against the honor of "the Subjugation of the White Whale," it was a calamity of a scale impossible to predict the consequences of. That was precisely why the Anastasia Camp hadn't been included in this discussion.

"Never mind Julius, but Anastasia-sama would absolutely take advantage of a situation like this. It's a good thing that Crusch-sama's condition wasn't seen by those people, nyaa."

"...But they need to be part of the discussion about dividing up credit, don't they? What are you gonna do about that?"

"We'll just say Crusch-sama's health isn't well, and Feri-chan will handle it one way or another. Subaru-kyun and the others just need to keep quiet, that's all. Understand, nyaa?"

At Ferris's attitude — not so much refusing further involvement as forbidding it outright — Subaru felt the stubbornness of it, but could do nothing but nod.

In the end, the discussion could only conclude without any further progress. A confirmation of just how hopeless the situation was, and a tentative alignment between the two camps, uneasy as it was about the future — that was the only way it could be brought to a close.

※※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※

"Wilhelm-san, thank you for earlier."

Once the meeting in the sitting room had ended and they'd stepped outside, Subaru called out to stop Wilhelm. Wilhelm halted and turned back, saying "No" and continuing,

"I could offer little real help. I can only be ashamed of my own inadequacy. And above all, this time as well, I was unable to be of assistance."

"That's not true at all. Without you, Wilhelm-san, we couldn't have even beaten the White Whale, and after that I couldn't have trusted Emilia-tan and the others to your care with any peace of mind. I'm grateful."

It was the unvarnished truth, without any hidden reservation. And yet, even at Subaru's gratitude, Wilhelm's expression didn't brighten.

He was a man of such scrupulous loyalty that he shouldered even others' pain — a costly sort of character. This man, too, was of the type who was far too kind. Subaru smiled at him and said,

"Things aren't settled yet, but you'll go visit your wife's grave and all that, right? I still can't say everything's fine, but at least you got her killer, so."

"—!"

The moment Subaru brought this up, trying to change the subject, Wilhelm's expression crumpled dramatically.

Seeing the complicated wave of emotion that surfaced there, Subaru could only be confused. Sensing Subaru's bewilderment, Wilhelm bent deeply at the waist.

"Subaru-dono, I must apologize to you."

"H-hey, stop that. If this is about earlier, I've got nothing but gratitude for you, Wilhelm-san, seriously—"

"No, that isn't it. Earlier, I did not take your side out of consideration for you, Subaru-dono. I shamefully, selfishly tried to continue the alliance with you all out of my own self-interest. And my own shamelessness in concealing that fact is, only now, something I'm ashamed of."

Not understanding the meaning of Wilhelm's words, Subaru could only knit his brow.

As Subaru stood there, Wilhelm straightened his body, removed his coat, and rolled up his sleeve. Beneath the rolled sleeve, bandages were wrapped around his left arm near the shoulder. A wound still remained there, it seemed, staining red as blood seeped from within.

"That looks painful. But if there's a wound, shouldn't Ferris heal it quick—"

"This wound will not heal. It is the result of a strike bearing the Divine Protection of the 'Reaper,' which deals wounds to the opponent that never heal."

"Won't heal, that... then, Wilhelm-san!"

Subaru understood well enough what it meant to bear a wound that would not close. If the bleeding never stopped, that was the same as having a fixed limit set on one's life. But unlike Subaru, who was filled with alarm, Wilhelm shook his head calmly.

"My life is not the issue here, in this matter."

"There's no way that's true. What can be done... about that wound?"

"This is not a wound taken yesterday or today. It is one taken long ago, merely reopened once again. And that, to me now, is far too significant a thing."

Listening to Wilhelm's quiet voice, Subaru noticed his own body trembling slightly. Not understanding the meaning of his own body's reaction, Subaru realized his teeth had begun to chatter, unable to align properly. And he realized, too, that the cause was the overwhelming, bone-chilling killing intent pouring out from the swordsman-demon before him.

Wilhelm continued in a quiet voice.

"A wound from the 'Reaper's' Divine Protection grows more potent the closer the one who bears the Protection draws to the one they wounded. Draw near the one you dealt the wound to, and even a closed wound will reopen. That is the nature of this wound."

"Then, the person who gave you that wound long ago, Wilhelm-san — they're nearby—"

"The one who gave me this wound on my left shoulder was the previous Sword Saint."

At those words, Subaru held his breath and looked at Wilhelm.

Wilhelm, his eyes fixed on Subaru, lit with an emotion frozen to the point of ice, said,

"Theresia van Astrea. My wife's sword wound has reopened. —I must continue to involve myself with the Witch Cult, in order to confirm that."

※※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※

In a situation full of things he didn't understand, Subaru had once again found his feet carrying him to the room where Rem slept.

Ever since returning to Crusch's mansion, he found himself coming to her side like this whenever he had a spare moment.

Even knowing it wouldn't happen, there was a weakness in him that couldn't help but hope she might wake up. In his current state of mind, Subaru had neither the courage nor the strength to keep facing Emilia. Emilia, too, likely understood that hopeless emotion of Subaru's. Knowing that simply being near him now would only cause him more pain, she gave him time alone except when he sought her out — even though she herself must have been just as anxious, if not more.

Once again, relying on someone else, trying to comfort his own weakness.

Even while hating that weakness in himself, he still couldn't quite bring himself to let it go.

"You told me I was strong... but it seems like I can't find that guy who acts tough, except in front of you, Rem."

Rem lying there looked no different than she had before he'd gone to the sitting room.

There was breath. Her heart still beat. But beyond that, not a single sign of life stirred in her. She was here, and yet she wasn't. Now, she existed only within Subaru.

And yet—

"—What kind of wind blew you in here, of all people?"

"Is it really that strange for me to be here? I had a connection with this girl too, you know. Isn't it fine for me to drift by and check on her?"

"With what face do you get to say something like that..."

Touching Rem's sleeping forehead, Subaru glared straight ahead — at the gray kitten floating there, its long tail swaying.

This spirit, who had made himself scarce in the sitting room, being here of all places was nothing if not wrong. At Subaru's thorny glare, Puck looked put out.

"Why glare at me like that? Did I do something?"

"...You haven't done anything right now, have you. Emilia-tan was looking for you — is it okay for you to be prowling around here like this?"

"Whether it's 'okay' is a bit of a delicate question. I'm not exactly restricted by Lia, but that doesn't mean I want to trouble her either."

Flicking his whiskers, Puck murmured this in his usual carefree manner. But then he added "But," and drifted up in front of Subaru's face.

"I thought it'd be better to talk with you now."

"...That all-knowing attitude of yours really pisses me off."

Clicking his tongue and looking away, Subaru found Puck still waiting silently for him. Exhaling, and though it galled him to play along with whatever Puck had in mind,

"You haven't told Emilia about the Witch Cult, have you. What's your deal?"

"It's not that I have any deal — if it's something she can go on living without knowing, isn't it fine not to know? I'll answer if she asks, but Lia never did ask, and... if it's possible not to get involved with people like that, isn't it best not to?"

"Yeah, well. If there's no reason to get acquainted with something in a place where it's better not to know it, then not getting involved is probably for the best. But Emilia's different now, isn't she. She left the forest to compete in the country to become the queen. That means contact with those people is unavoidable. —You must have known that."

Lowering his voice, Subaru pressed Puck for his real intent. But the little cat took Subaru's fierce manner in stride, swaying easily.

"I did predict the Witch Cult would show up. But whether to tell that to Lia is a separate matter, I think."

"Even if it means putting Emilia and everyone around her in danger! I don't know what you're thinking, but if we'd just left it alone this time, Emilia would've—!"

"I see. So you worked hard to protect Lia, then. That means this girl was a sacrifice made to save Lia. In that case I should be grateful to her..."

"————"

In that instant, Subaru's will condensed, ignoring everything else, into the single motion of driving his fist forward.

Toward the spirit before him, Subaru drove his fist forward with all his strength, without a shred of hesitation. But the spirit dodged the blow easily, and, looking startled, washed his face with his paw.

"That's a bit of a surprise, isn't it — what's this about?"

"Never touch Rem again. Not with your hands, not with your mouth."

Even Subaru was surprised at how quiet his own voice came out.

His emotions had boiled so hot that perhaps something in him had simply gone strange.

Puck watched Subaru's demeanor with his round eyes, then said, "Understood," stretching his small body.

"I'm sorry for the careless remark, my bad. That wasn't something I should have said. In its place, let's just say... let's talk a little about 'Gluttony.'"

"...What good would that do?"

"If you know the being that ate that girl's 'name' and 'memory,' it might raise the odds of Subaru's hopes a bit, I thought."

Subaru's head snapped up as if struck. At his reaction, Puck nodded, twitching his pink nose, and looked upward as if searching his memory.

"'Gluttony's' Authority, simply put, is the power to eat. It eats the opponent's 'name,' stealing it from the memories of everyone around them, and eats the opponent's 'memory,' stealing their own memories from them. Have both taken, and what's left is just an empty husk, no longer anyone at all. An empty husk can't do anything, and nothing can be done to it either. That girl's condition is exactly that."

"Name, and memory..."

Crusch, whose memory had been eaten, and Rem, who'd had both taken.

That was the result of the Authority the victims of this incident had suffered. Then that meant—

"If we defeat the Archbishop of Sin of 'Gluttony,' can we get it back...?"

"Well, who knows. I hate to put it this way, but... regurgitating what you've eaten — can something like that even be done? That's something only the one who did it could answer."

"But there's a possibility. There is, isn't there. A possibility of getting Rem back...!"

He turned around. Rem still lay there, deep in her slumber.

She was breathing. Her heart was still beating. Her body was alive. Only her heart and her name had been devoured by a demon. If that was so, then there was hope.

"'Gluttony's' Archbishop of Sin — I'm absolutely going to beat them senseless."

"I don't think it'll be that simple, though."

Puck's last remark didn't even reach Subaru's ears.

For Subaru now, that hope alone was the last fortress he had left to cling to.

—When he'd returned to the royal capital, heard of the disaster that had befallen Rem and the others, seen her condition, and understood that there was no way to get her back, Subaru had, without hesitation, driven a dagger into his own throat.

He couldn't recall the emotion of that moment. It was true that he'd felt no lingering attachment to losing that stretch of time in which he'd achieved everything through the best possible outcome, and answered everyone's expectations with all his might.

Rather than lose Rem, rather than move forward into a future without her — he would gladly taste that agony again, as many times as it took. That much, at least, he remembered.

The sensation of driving the blade into his throat, of himself dissolving into blood, pain, heat, and a sense of loss.

When it cleared, what Subaru saw was Rem lying on the bed—.

The updated revival point. The changed scene he would restart from. That was what thrust Subaru into hell.

Once more he tried to choose death, thinking it must be some kind of mistake, but Subaru hesitated. It wasn't that the pain, the death, frightened him. He'd simply realized something.

Even if the scene he restarted from returned to the previous one, Subaru couldn't save Rem.

If he returned to the scene after the battle with the White Whale, before the fight with Petelgeuse, then Subaru would already be several hours removed from having parted with Rem and the others.

Even if he tried to catch up to them as they were attacked on the way home, he wouldn't make it in time. And more than that, turning back to save Rem would mean abandoning Emilia. Even if he managed to explain the situation well and go back, was there really a way to win against two Archbishops of Sin?

Subaru and Julius were both essential to overcoming Petelgeuse. And the scene of getting Emilia and the others to safety couldn't be pulled off without Wilhelm's strength either.

To save Rem, he'd need Emilia; to save Emilia, he'd need Rem — unless he sacrificed one, he couldn't save the other.

Having realized that cruel choice, the hand moving toward his own death had stopped the motion of driving the blade into his throat.

Unlike the White Whale's "Mist," her body was here, and yet Rem remained in no one's memory. And Subaru himself, standing dumbly beside her, unable to do a single thing.

That pathetic, helpless stretch of time — it ends here, now. He would end it.

Taking the sleeping Rem's hand, Subaru resolved himself. This time for sure, he resolved.

—I will surely bring you back.

"—I'll bring you back. Rem, I'll definitely bring you back, I swear it."

Subaru had told her this once. That in front of her, the man she'd fallen for would become the greatest hero of all, and show her.

Then wasn't he still only partway along that road?

"I will, without fail... your hero will, without fail, come to meet you. —Wait for me."

He raised his head. Bared his fangs. A declaration of war, to the enemy.

To those who'd been bold enough to cross him, who'd laid hands on what must never be touched, who'd trespassed into territory that must never be violated — he would make them regret it.

No one else. Natsuki Subaru, and no one else, would do it.

"Without fail. —Without fail!!"

He couldn't bear the thought of days starting over from zero, with no memories of her in them.

So, without fail, he would bring her back.

The days that had been lost, the time he'd walked with her, the time he would walk with her yet to come.

He would draw them back into his own two hands once more, no matter what it took.
