Arc 5 Chapter 81: "The One Who Fills the Vessel of Greed"

—The series of "Witch Cult incidents" that began in the water gate city of Priestella.

The tragedies that struck the citizens, the scars of battle left scattered across the city. The need to make up for lost manpower, the many civic functions still far from fully restored. Plenty of problems still remained, but even so, the situation was moving toward resolution, and things were beginning to advance toward the next story.

For Natsuki Subaru too, the city's many problems weighed on his heart. And yet, looking out over the townscape now that the Witch Cult had been driven off, he couldn't help but feel a small, conceited swell of pride—that maybe he'd contributed something to that outcome.

"Even though plenty of unsolved problems are still left..."

Of the scars left behind by the Archbishops of Sin, those left by "Lust" and "Gluttony" in particular were immense. The citizens whose bodies had been mutated by the Authority of "Lust"—their bodies had been placed into a state of "suspended animation" by Emilia's hand, and now awaited the moment of their awakening deep within a shelter in the city. And as for the people who had fallen prey to "Gluttony's" appetite, most of them remained even now in an endless, unbroken sleep, robbed even of the bonds that would let anyone truly long for their waking.

Emilia's expression, when she'd proposed postponing the resolution of the mutated citizens' problem, had been painful to see. Julius's heartache, too—having lost his place, agonizing over what he was to be—was beyond imagining. And the wounds in the hearts of the city's people, who had become parties to these many problems, went without saying.

Everyone had been wounded. Doing everything he could to heal those wounds—that was what Subaru had to do. And—

"Because there's still a problem left that I have to clean up."

To resolve that last problem—the one not yet brought to light—Subaru nodded. This much, at least, was something only Subaru could do.

"Julius says he's coming with us."

"Is that so, is that so. Well then, that's a load off my mind."

When Subaru returned to the conference hall after finishing up various discussions, Anastasia was there to greet him.

In the conference hall with its round table, Anastasia was the only figure left. The meeting of the principal members had already wrapped up hours ago. Some had long since retired to their inns, and others were busy preparing to leave Priestella. Even setting that aside, it had been an exhausting day. There was no need to keep leaning against a hard round table, spending time in the gloom of an empty shelter with no one around.

"...Something wrong?"

And yet she'd stayed here, waiting for someone to come. Not that Subaru had been certain of it. But he'd noticed, vaguely, that some part of him had been braced for the thought that she'd be here. After all, right now, there probably wasn't anywhere she could feel at ease.

"So that makes it Emilia, me, and Beako heading for the Pleiades Watchtower. Add Julius and you to that, and it's five people total."

"That's a decent-sized party, ain't it just about right? Oh, and it's not five—it's six. You can't go forgettin' about my cute little Echidna, now."

She unwound the stole from around her neck and spread it out on the round table, making it dance. The white fox stole submitted to its mistress's unreasonable little game as obediently as a puppet. Or perhaps "as if" wasn't quite right.

"I haven't forgotten. —That's exactly why it's five."

"—"

Leaning his back against the conference hall door, Subaru said this to Anastasia as she held up the stole. At his words, Anastasia's smile froze. Her soft, gentle smile melted away and vanished, and she slowly tilted her head. Drawing the stole up to her mouth, she said, looking puzzled,

"Oh my, how curious. Whatever gave it away that I'm not Ana, I wonder?"

Clearly enough to notice at once, Anastasia's manner of speech had completely changed from before. It was terribly familiar, overly familiar even—but at its core, it rang utterly hollow. Even with the same voice as hers, it was obviously, unmistakably different.

"If you meant to hide it, you should've acted a little better. Sure, as far as I know, Anastasia's the realist and rationalist among the candidates... but she never talked or carried herself with as little humanity as you."

"I'd kept watching Ana in my own way, and thought I had the impression down, but it seems it doesn't go quite as well as I'd hoped. You're the second one to see through it."

"Second?"

"Al-kun saw through it too. Calling me a 'Witch,' of all things—how cruel of him."

"Well, that's..."

Aptly put, Subaru thought, impressed with Al. Anastasia's Echidna and the "Witch" Echidna were fundamentally different, but they couldn't be unrelated, so it wasn't exactly wrong.

Whether it should be credited to Al's insight, or to something only he could notice. He, too, was a summonee from another world, like Subaru. If the power of the "Witch of Envy" had something to do with otherworldly summoning, then Al might have some connection to the Witch as well. Really, he ought to talk with him about that in more depth at some point—

"Anyway, never mind that for now. The real problem is you, trying to take over Anastasia's body and make it your own."

"'Taking over'—that's not a very gentle way to put it. It's true the current situation has the nasty quality of looking like that at first glance, but being misunderstood that way is nothing short of deeply regrettable. Really, it pains me."

"The way you say that makes it sound like it isn't true at all."

"Because in fact, it isn't, even if it looks that way. I've merely borrowed Ana's body out of unavoidable necessity. If I hadn't, neither she nor I would have survived—that's my claim on the matter. And continuing to sit here in Ana's vessel afterward isn't exactly what I intended, either."

"Too long. So, in short?"

"I borrowed the body, fine, but then I couldn't get out."

So, this Echidna-of-the-stole—let's just call her Eridna for now—Subaru had never once thought Eridna was unrelated to Echidna, but even so, he'd never felt Echidna's shadow in Eridna quite as clearly as he did in this exchange. The way she went the long way round with words about everything was just like the original.

"Well, let's hear you out, then."

Pushing off from the door, Subaru settled into a posture for talking with Eridna. It seemed safe to assume, for now, that the danger of her lashing out because her identity had been exposed—can't let him live now that he knows—was gone. Subaru sat down across the round table, in the seat opposite Eridna, and said,

"To start with, what exactly does 'borrowing the body' even mean?"

"Put simply, it's a state where I overwrite my existence onto Ana's Od and borrow her freedom of movement. In this state, control over Ana's body is mine to command, and it's even possible to use magic by exercising Ana's Gate, which is normally flawed."

"What do you mean, 'normally flawed'?"

"Digging right down to the roots, aren't you. I understand the urge to know, so I won't blame you, but wanting to know about other girls—won't your Emilia-sama get jealous? And wanting to know about other spirits—won't your Beatrice?"

"None of your business, and if they do get jealous, that's fine too, since they're both cute either way. Quit stalling and tell me."

He rapped his fingers irritably on the round table, and Eridna shrugged. Then she carefully folded up the stole she'd removed, and prefacing it with "Ana, you see...," she said,

"She's a girl born with a flawed Gate. You know the Gate is an organ that takes in mana from the atmosphere and expels mana from within the body, I take it—well, Anastasia's intake function doesn't work properly. Chronic mana deficiency, you could call it. As for people with the opposite flaw, who can't expel it from their bodies—I imagine you have someone in mind there too."

"I don't really get what's so bad about that being bad, and I have no idea who you mean by that 'someone.'"

"Is that so? How unexpected. Incidentally, the one with the flaw of not being able to expel it is the descendant of the 'Sword Saint.' Though in his case, the amount he takes in is abnormal to begin with, and it all seems to go toward his physical abilities, so there's no real harm done."

"Reinhard?"

At Eridna's remark, Subaru raised an eyebrow, struck by something unexpected. But now that he thought of it, he had a feeling Reinhard himself had mentioned something like this somewhere before. That Reinhard couldn't use magic—that was the one respect in which he was a poor student. But in exchange, the intake power of his Gate was strong—ah, I see, so that's why, same as with Beatrice, things get out of hand when a spirit gets close to him.

"Well, it's not like he has zero long-range attack options even without magic, and honestly, with that guy, I wouldn't be surprised if he could just send a blade-pressure slash flying and cut something down anyway, so that hardly counts as a handicap. Anyway, back to Anastasia."

"I don't mean to dodge the question, it's just my habit to want to talk about whatever I know. Now then... right, we were on Ana's constitution. Ana's Gate has an underdeveloped intake function, so it doesn't work properly. Which means magic can only draw on whatever mana already exists in her body. And once that runs out, next comes the Od, the very source of life. You can't let someone do something that reckless, can you? That's why Ana can't use magic."

"But it doesn't add up that you borrowing the body means magic can be used. The part where there's not much mana to begin with doesn't change. Or are you saying you're using up what little she's got?"

"—"

"Don't go quiet, answer me."

"If shaving down her life is the only way her life gets saved in the first place, then there's no other choice. That said, Ana and I have already settled this matter between ourselves. There's no reason for an outsider like you to have a say in it. You wouldn't want me putting my nose into your Contract with Beatrice, either, would you?"

That struck home. Subaru's relationship with Beatrice, and their Contract, belonged to Subaru and Beatrice alone. He didn't want outside will intruding on that, and he'd refuse it even if someone tried.

If the same terms were claimed by Anastasia and Eridna, Subaru had nothing left to say. Because that was the bond between a Contractor and a Spirit—an absolute connection that no one else was allowed to violate.

"This time, I borrowed Ana's body partly by her own will, and partly because it was an emergency. You heard about the Archbishop of Sin who came to the city hall, didn't you? Driving them off required Al-kun and me to give it everything we had. It was a choice forced on us by necessity."

"So what's this about not being able to go back?"

"Yes, that's exactly today's problem."

At Subaru's words, born of desperation, Eridna clapped her hands together and smiled. Even with Anastasia's appearance, it was a smile whose contents were clearly different. How strange, Subaru thought, but he quickly set that sentiment aside. At last, they were cutting into the real matter at hand. Right in front of Subaru, Eridna touched a hand to Anastasia's slender chest, and said,

"This isn't the first time I've borrowed Ana's body like this. But it hasn't happened all that often, either. Ana and I don't have a formal Contract, you see. That's precisely because, given her Gate's condition, I didn't want to place a constant burden on her. I pride myself on being rather fuel-efficient, even among spirits. If all I need to do is simply exist, I hardly require any mana supply from a Contractor at all."

"I see. My Beako wants to hold hands three times a day, though."

"I'd guess two of those three times, she just wants to hold hands, plain and simple. How lovely for you both. —Now, back to us: given all that, my experience borrowing Ana's body hasn't been extensive. This makes, what, the fourth or fifth time at most. Our acquaintance is coming up on eleven years now, so wouldn't you say that's surprisingly few?"

"Hmm, I don't know about that. If you think of it as a rate of once every two years, that's about the same as catching the flu—not exactly what I'd call low."

"That's harsh."

Eridna chuckled low in her throat, laughing just like the Witch Subaru knew. Before long, Anastasia's figure began to blur and overlap with Echidna's in his mind, hazily, and it frightened him. Echidna's existence had left a knot inside Subaru that wouldn't go away. Given what had happened with Beatrice too, he'd rather never meet her again if he could help it.

Besides, if things went on like this and something happened to the real Anastasia, it would be a devastating wound for Julius as well. He wanted to avoid that too.

"So, what happened with this Miss Echidna, troublesome as the flu?"

"I don't know what this 'flu' of yours is, but in any case, my experience with this is limited. So I don't know myself why this happened—there's no precedent for me to go on... My consciousness has become unable to separate from Ana's body. As a result, Ana is sleeping deep within her own Od."

Eridna spoke as if the Od truly lay just beneath the hand she'd rested on Anastasia's chest, then turned to look at the limp stole lying on the round table. With Eridna's consciousness residing inside Anastasia, this stole ought to be nothing more than an empty fox husk now, but—

"I thought I'd managed it well enough back in the meeting hall, working it like a puppet."

"Most people probably just got thrown off by the visual impact. Besides me, I bet there were a few others who thought something was off, too."

Or so he thought. Though it was possible that the sense of wrongness was something only Subaru, who knew about artificial spirits, had picked up on.

"Even if they didn't notice at the time, anyone close to her will realize soon enough that you're not Anastasia."

"And yet, so far only people with shallow relationships to her, like you and Al-kun, have seen through it. Doesn't that suggest my impersonation of Ana is going rather well?"

"Ricardo and the kittens are all a little too caught up in their own troubles right now. So is Julius."

"—"

At those words, Eridna narrowed her eyes. Subaru made a puzzled face at her reaction, but Eridna quickly let out a breath, and said,

"So, Julius really is Ana's knight after all. From the flow of conversation in the meeting hall, I'd thought it was almost certain, but... the Authority of 'Gluttony' truly is a fearsome thing. That it can steal away even from the memory of one who ought to be outside the ordinary, like me."

"What do you... want to do with Anastasia?"

"—?"

"Whether or not you ever intended to take over Anastasia's body—honestly, there's no point pursuing that, so I won't. Frankly, even if you say it isn't true, I have no basis inside me for believing you. But—"

Anastasia's body not being returned—that could not be allowed to happen. For that "Most Excellent Knight," it would amount to the very loss of one hope. The spiritual death of a candidate—he wanted nothing to do with the Royal Selection being settled with a stain like that on it.

"I won't budge on her getting back to normal, Echidna."

"Set your mind at ease. Even I'm not so arrogant as to think of taking over Ana's body and living the rest of my life in her place."

Eridna said this with a world-weary air, to a Subaru who'd pressed forward braced for a furious reaction. With a sorrowful expression, she wrapped her arms around Anastasia's thin, small body, and said,

"You see, I'm fond of Ana. Spending over ten years together, even without a Contract between us, wasn't merely out of some urge to observe her. I can't say for certain whether this feeling is accurate, but I'm aware of feeling something close to a guardian, or family. If it's at all possible, I want her to be healthy, and above all, happy."

"—"

Even and flowing as ever, Eridna spoke on—but in the way she touched Anastasia's slight body, now her own, and gave voice to those words, there did seem to be real affection. The way Puck felt for Emilia, the way Beatrice felt for Subaru—perhaps Eridna felt that same thing for Anastasia. If that were so, then—

"Then your real reason for wanting to meet the Sage is..."

"You've guessed correctly. —In my heart of hearts, I don't feel a thing, one way or the other, about people who've had their names devoured by 'Gluttony.' All I want is to learn how to give Ana her body back. And to that end, I intend to make use of all of you as well."

"Is there any guarantee the Sage even knows how?"

"No guarantee. But if it's the Sage, said to see through everything, to know all things, then there's a possibility. I'm simply betting on the possibility that offers the highest chance of success. That's all."

At Eridna's words, carrying such firm resolve, Subaru couldn't immediately think of a rebuttal. It was without question a terribly self-centered, selfish conclusion. But Eridna had her own purpose, and her own will to act in pursuit of it. If so, then what Subaru needed to confirm was—

"It's really true that you know a way to reach the Sage—to the Pleiades Watchtower?"

"But of course."

"You're supposed to be the type with no memories of the past. So how does someone like you know the way to a Watchtower nobody knows how to reach? That doesn't add up."

"What I know, I know. It's a bother to be asked for grounds for that, but, well. If I had to dress it up in words, I suppose it's because reaching that place is my destiny."

"Destiny? Whose destiny, decided by who?"

"The Creator, I suppose you could call it."

It was an affected answer from Eridna, but as far as Subaru was concerned, it was the worst possible answer. If the Creator she spoke of was Echidna, then the culprit who'd burned only the way to the Watchtower into an artificial spirit's memory could be none other than that same Echidna. Which meant something connected to Echidna existed at the Pleiades Watchtower.

It was, without question, something that stoked both an unpleasant premonition and a certain measure of hope for the Sage's knowledge.

"Have you managed to convince yourself, I wonder?"

Eridna asked this of Subaru, who'd fallen silent after arriving at a single conclusion. Subaru hesitated to nod right away, and let out a long, deep sigh instead.

"It's not fine enough a thing to call 'convinced,' but I understand it for now, at least. You've got your own things to do and your own goals, and none of it gets in the way of ours."

"Just so. Each of us has something we want to ask the Sage. So we'll cooperate to reach the Sage's side. Nothing strange about that at all."

"Stop. The moment you say that, it starts sounding shady."

"How cruel."

Keep talking with Eridna in Anastasia's shape any longer, and Subaru felt he might lose his mind. Either way, it would be a long acquaintance on the road to the Pleiades Watchtower. The Augria Sand Dunes where the Watchtower stood lay at the eastern edge of the map of the world—a long journey indeed.

"I'll get used to it gradually, so just give me some space, will you."

"You don't gotta hate on it that much, do ya? Honestly, Natsuki-kun's so cold to such a cute girl. It hurts my feelin's, it really does. Mou, mou."

Winding the stole back around her neck, Eridna traced Anastasia's mannerisms of speech and behavior. A well-made performance, sure enough, but—

"Your intonation on 'uchi' is off. And your Kansai accent's too smooth. Compared to the Kararagi folks I know, it doesn't sound put-on enough."

"Not put-on enough?"

It was a difference within an extremely narrow range. Eridna dutifully worked her throat as if to check what Subaru had told her, then finally let out a breath as though giving up. There was nothing more Subaru needed to confirm with Eridna, either. Whether Anastasia's body would be returned depended, in the end, on how the Sage of the Pleiades Watchtower responded. Except—

"Don't tell Julius—or anyone—that you're borrowing Anastasia's body."

"...I don't mind that, but it's a surprise that it's Natsuki-kun who doesn't wanna tell."

"It's already a rough enough time as it is; I don't want to stir up any extra trouble. And if it comes out that the real one who came up with this plan wasn't Anastasia but you, Ricardo and the others might turn against it right away. It'd be a problem for me too if we couldn't get to the Watchtower. Selfish of me, I know."

There was a chance Ricardo, Mimi, and the others would worry for Anastasia's safety and try to stop it. Thinking that through, even if it was an excellent means of resolution, they might end up having no choice but to give up the journey to the Watchtower. And that would be a problem for Subaru and the others too, who wanted to save the victims of the Authorities.

"It's better if the Sage can solve everything at once—the victims of 'Gluttony,' the victims of 'Lust,' and of course, your problem with Anastasia too. Once everything's worked out, I won't let Ricardo and the others complain. Well—even if they do, I won't listen."

"Sounds like a line from the Book of Hoshin—'so long as the books balance out in the end.'"

"There, I'm in full agreement with Mr. Hoshin."

As expected of Hoshin, suspected fellow countryman of his—the man had a point.

"Well then."

That was about where the discussion wrapped up, for now. Worst case, if Eridna had harbored thoughts of misusing Anastasia's body, this could have turned into Priestella's final battle right here—and knowing that, Subaru felt a measure of relief.

Precisely because of that, the question that followed caught Subaru right as his guard had come down.

"By the way, Natsuki-kun."

"Hm?"

Subaru turned back just as he'd put his hand on the door, meaning to head out of the conference hall. Still leaning her weight against the round table's chair as ever, Eridna tilted her head fetchingly toward the turning Subaru, exactly the way Anastasia would, and said,

"—There's someone else you'd like to ask the Sage how to bring back, isn't there?"

"—"

"Either way, there's people right here in Priestella with the same condition, ain't there? If you're gonna ask how to bring 'em back, it'd make things clearer to bring along at least one case to show."

With his hand still on the doorknob, Subaru's throat, his very breath, froze solid. To a Subaru whose face had gone rigid, eyes wide open, Eridna went on evenly, and finally said,

"So, what'll you do? It's all up to you, Natsuki-kun."

"I... "

"Either way, you're gonna stop by Margrave Mathers's mansion, aren't ya? You'll need to prepare to cross the Augria Sand Dunes, and you gotta give notice before heading for the Watchtower. So then, your Sleeping Beauty oughta be right there."

"—"

"I don't think that's a bad thing, myself. It's just her bein' the first of everyone you'll save... that much luxury oughta be allowed for you, Natsuki-kun, don't you think?"

Eridna's even, level voice struck Subaru, for some reason, as a terribly demonic temptation. He understood what she was getting at. And there was no mistaking that some part of him wanted to give in and follow it, and yet he couldn't answer right away. That was surely because—

"Subaru!"

"—!"

Called by name, Subaru looked up in surprise. Standing right in front of a Subaru whose breath had caught were Emilia and Beatrice, the two of them. Both of them widened their eyes at his reaction, tilting their heads and asking, "What's wrong?"

"You said you were going to see Julius-san... Julius, but when I went to the sickroom you weren't there, so I got worried. Where were you?"

"Ah, it's nothing... c'mon, that guy's got a face so gloomy it's hard to look at for long, right? So, not so much airing out a room, but airing out my eyes, kind of thing."

"Really? I think Julius has quite a handsome face, though..."

"Emilia-tan thinks so too?"

"Oh, but Subaru's face is fine too, I think it's nice. See, the more you look at it the more character it has, or something like that, I think."

"That backup is painful to hear!"

Emilia flusteredly tried to correct herself, but it was a matter of phrasing regardless. As Subaru wryly smiled and let his shoulders slump, this time it was Beatrice, silent beside Emilia, who caught his attention. Beatrice kept glancing repeatedly past Subaru's shoulder, toward the shelter they'd left behind. Her expression looked as if she had some idea about the conversation held there.

"Subaru, if you're going to do anything dangerous, you should call for Betty, I suppose. Leaving you alone is far too precarious—Betty can't feel at ease at all, I suppose."

"That's exactly how I feel about you all the time, you know. You're so cute that I'm never at ease, always worrying some kidnapper's gonna lure you off with a piece of candy."

"Betty is not such a cheap spirit, I suppose! Don't you make fun of her!"

Beatrice fumed and came at him with soft little thumps, and Subaru hoisted her up. Carrying a startled Beatrice, who let out a "Fwah!", he started walking alongside Emilia.

"Let, let me go, put me down, I suppose! Ah, but—put me down in a way where you don't let go, I suppose."

"That's a tall order, so you're staying like this for a while."

Beatrice's body was light, but oddly warm. The common wisdom was that children ran hot, and it seemed that held true even for a young-girl spirit like Beatrice—strange, given she was a spirit. As Subaru wryly smiled to himself over that thought, Emilia beside him peered intently at his profile. Met with her upward gaze, Subaru asked, puzzled, "What's up?"

"Is it unusual to see me and Beako fooling around?"

"No. That's not unusual at all, this past year... it's just, right now, I think Subaru's making a face as lost as I've rarely seen this whole past year."

"—Really? I wouldn't say everything's fine, exactly, but most of the problems have been solved, and I figured my face muscles were pretty relaxed right about now."

"If Subaru says so, then I'll believe it, but..."

As Subaru worked his cheeks around demonstratively, Emilia lowered her long-lashed eyes. Then, slowly, as if driving the point home word by word, she said,

"Once you decide what you're going to do, promise you'll tell me. And if you can't come up with an answer no matter what, promise you'll properly talk it over with me. That's all I want you to promise."

"A promise, huh."

"Right, the kind of promise Subaru's bad at keeping. You're good at making them, though, aren't you?"

"Whoa, that's an unusually poisonous line for Emilia-tan."

Given his track record with promises so far, Subaru found himself on the receiving end of a harsh assessment from Emilia. Watching Emilia, smiling faintly, hold out her little finger, Subaru hoisted Beatrice up onto his shoulder in one motion, and, still holding the fussing little girl crying "What are you doing, I suppose!", hooked his little finger around Emilia's.

"Finger-cut promise, swear it true, break it and swallow a thousand needles!"

"Fingers, cut."

Their fingers parted. Emilia, still holding up her cut finger, smiled at Subaru, and said,

"Subaru, how many needles is that in total now?"

"Hmm, I don't think it's up to ten thousand yet."

"Well then, make sure it really doesn't reach ten thousand, okay?"

To Emilia's words, spoken almost like a prayer, Subaru answered briefly, "Yeah." It probably wasn't possible for Emilia to feel absolute reassurance from that answer. To begin with, she probably hadn't meant to extract that promise for that purpose, either.

So this promise, then, was a caution Subaru set for himself.

'—That much luxury oughta be allowed for you, Natsuki-kun, don't you think?'

Eridna's final temptation resurfaced in his mind. That much luxury—was it allowed for Subaru? Would it be allowed? And who would forgive Subaru for indulging in it?

"I'll come up with an answer. —By the time we get back to the mansion, for certain."

Even so, perhaps it was only to be expected of a kin who shared a name with that "Witch." She really was good at exploiting a person's weakest point.

"Honestly, what a hateful woman..."

"Did you say something just now, I suppose?"

"Nah, just thinking, carrying you like this, I've got free rein to smack and grope Beako's butt all I want."

"Giyaaah, I suppose! N-no, let go after all, I suppose! Put me down! Slowly, gently, like handling a flower with care, I suppose!"

"Ha ha ha ha."

"Stop smacking my butt while you're laughing, I suppose——!!"

Still carrying a clamoring Beatrice on his shoulder, Subaru followed after the slender back walking ahead of him. Emilia kept glancing back, her face plainly wanting to be included. Blessed as he was with so much, saved as he was by so much—

—and yet he found himself wishing that one more girl were here too, appalled at his own "Greed" for thinking it. Natsuki Subaru's battle in the water gate city was drawing its curtain to a close.

—Toward the next story, the one leading to the tower of sand, quietly, for now.
