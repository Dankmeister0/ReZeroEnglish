Arc 7 Chapter 65: "School Food Fighter"

"Chaosflame was destroyed by a great, terrible calamity. The evacuation of the residents should have already been carried out by Yorna-sama, but beyond that, I..."

The round-browed girl's voice was filled with sorrow, her brows drooping and her eyes downcast.
Subaru had thought she wasn't a girl who showed her emotions much, and in fact, even now her expression wasn't one you'd call sad on its face. But he thought anyone who didn't recognize this as sadness was a fool.
Subaru's head wasn't the sharpest, but he didn't want to be a fool. So he made sure to properly take in what she — Tanza — had said, and tried to stay close to her pain.
Except —

"Ha-ha, a calamity great enough to destroy the demon capital — how grandiose. Even while I've been cooped up on this island, the world keeps marching along in a flurry of developments. I'll have to be careful not to fall behind and find myself unneeded!"

"Ceci, could you maybe just be quiet for a bit?"

"Huh? Did I do something again?"

The person who'd tried to push the conversation forward while completely ignoring both Subaru's inner turmoil and the pain in Tanza's heart — the fake Cecilus — got glared at, and tilted his head with a face that said he had no idea why.
Whether before or after the 'Sparka,' his attitude never changed one bit, which left Subaru exasperated, then astonished, then exasperated all over again.
Especially considering how many dozens of times this heartless boy had tried to listen to Subaru's last words.

"...Still, if Ceci hadn't woken Tanza up there at the end, it wouldn't have worked out."

"Ah, you needn't feel indebted to me for that. The moment you'd pushed it that far, the victory was already Bussu's — the final push was just icing on the cake. Besides, whether this girl would wake up at all was a gamble in itself!"

"I'm sorry for the trouble I caused."

To the sour-faced Subaru, the fake Cecilus offered modesty that didn't feel very modest at all. Hearing it, Tanza bowed her head slightly, which left Subaru — on the receiving end of the help — unable to say anything.

— Subaru had made it through the Sword Slaves' Isolated Island's violent welcome ceremony, the 'Sparka.'

Through repeated, mind-numbing trials, Subaru and the others had somehow managed to cut off that lion's head and win, but it had to be said that without the cooperation of Tanza and the fake Cecilus, that would have been difficult.
If the fake Cecilus hadn't woken Tanza at the very end, and if Tanza hadn't taken the sword lodged in the lion's neck and severed its head —

"Who knows how many more times we'd have failed."

Of course, it was a fight that couldn't be abandoned until it succeeded, so giving up was never an option. Still, whether or not you had the will to keep going had a huge effect on the outcome.
And depending on circumstances, it was entirely possible none of them would have survived.
If it had ended that way, no amount of regret would have been enough.

"Weitz, and Hiain, and Idra — they all fought hard together too."

Cooperating had been incredibly difficult, but if even one of them had been missing, none of them would have made it out alive.
That all of them had survived was, for Subaru, the best possible outcome. He'd managed to act like a proper leader, and that fed into his own confidence.
This — this was the kind of thing that let him proudly call himself the son of Natsuki Kenichi.

"Schult-sama?"

"Ah, sorry, sorry, just thinking about something. So, um, about Chaosflame."

"—. Yes. If we could return to help with the reconstruction as soon as possible..."

"...Yeah. I want to get back and reunite with everyone too."

Returning to the original topic, Tanza's downturned face looked terribly pitiful.
Worrying about family and home was only natural, and Subaru felt the same way. All the more so upon hearing that Chaosflame had fallen into such dire straits while he wasn't even aware of it.
He was anxious about everyone scattered apart — especially whether Rui had been found by that spiteful Abel.
If Yorna was with her, surely she'd protect Rui.

"————"

The anxious-looking Tanza had changed back into the kimono she'd worn in the demon capital.
From what he'd heard, Subaru had slept a full day after the 'Sparka' ended. He'd woken up in the healing room, and a toothless old man claiming to be a healer had told him there was nothing particularly wrong with his health, but mentally, his body still felt heavy and sluggish.
Still, he couldn't afford to dawdle around in a daze.

"The other three besides me... Weitz and the others? Are they awake too?"

"Those three gentlemen have already woken and returned to their own room. All three of them are apparently sharing a room. You and I share a room too, Schult-sama, and, well..."

"Yep, yep, that's me! You see, the room where Bussu first woke up — that's our sleeping quarters. I'm glad to finally have a roommate. Well, 'have' isn't quite right, since I pulled her in myself!"

"Pulled in...?"

"Don't worry about it too much. The story is that Tanza and I fell into the lake."

Now that he mentioned it, Subaru had a hazy memory of swimming desperately so as not to drown.
Whether he'd swum across in one go, or nearly drowned a few times along the way, was also fuzzy, but that wasn't really important, so thinking about it further was pointless.
In any case, hearing that the other three were safe was a relief. Unavoidable as it had been, he'd ended up learning quite a lot about their circumstances during the 'Sparka.'
A coward, a craven, and a fraud — not exactly praiseworthy characters, but each of them had their reasons for becoming a coward, a craven, and a fraud.

"Right, same as how I'm Superman, huh."

"Oh my, what a confident-looking face. But but I think that's for the best — it raises your manliness quotient considerably. Getting tossed around by circumstances, agonizing over this and that — that's the behavior of a bit player or the common masses! Bussu ought to carry himself with dignity and majesty!"

"I've got no intention of indulging Ceci's story-brain, but I agree with that."

To begin with, moping too much wasn't good.
Somehow, Subaru felt he'd been too passive about all sorts of things up until now.
Whatever it was, a passive response just let the other side get carried away. That went the same whether the "other side" was an opponent you hated or fate itself. Lately, fate had been getting way too carried away with itself.

"From now on, I'm gonna go after my goals more actively, more greedily."

For the time being, Subaru's current goal was to escape the island.
Escape the Sword Slaves' Isolated Island and make his way back to Chaosflame together with Tanza. Of course, the big goal was leaving the Empire and returning to everyone in the Kingdom, but —

"Get too fixated on the big goal and you'll trip. Summer homework, too — you tackle it one thing at a time. Can't write the whole diary in one go."

Believe it or not, Subaru was the type who actually did his summer and winter break homework properly.
Occasionally there'd be a classmate who came back to school after break without having done any of it, and he honestly had no idea what kind of nerve or reasoning let them pull that off.
Somehow it felt like losing to them in imagination and courage, which was a little frustrating.

"But, the me who does his homework is still better, so..."

"—By the way, Schult-sama, might I have a moment?"

"Hm?"

With his hand on his chin, watering his own self-affirmation, Subaru was hesitantly addressed by Tanza.
When she caught his gaze on her, she gestured with her hand toward the fake Cecilus beside them. Then, watching him sit on the bed, dangling his legs with a carefree look on his face, she said,

"This gentleman here... um, is he not Cecilus Segmunt-sama...?"

"Mm, ah, yeah, he's the wolf-boy who keeps insisting he is."

"Ahaha, called a wolf-boy, how rude! And also, what does that even mean? If it's supposed to mean werewolf, please stop with the insults. Sure, I may be a bit hard to communicate with at times, but wouldn't you say I have more of a dangerous charm about me?"

"Sorry, I have no idea what you just said."

Pressed with all that at once, Subaru found the words slipping out naturally, and he pressed a hand to his own chest.
When he closed his eyes, he could still recall the face of a beloved girl. And along with it, the faces of everyone else important to him. Even so, he was aware that the drawer for those memories was undeniably stiffening.
He couldn't let this situation drag on much longer.

"Anyway, forget Ceci's flippant remarks — what's bothering you, Tanza? He's a famous general of the Empire; it wouldn't be strange for some kid who doesn't know his place to impersonate him, would it?"

"That may be true, but... um, I once caught a glimpse of Cecilus-sama before, and he resembled him so closely that..."

"You've seen him before? Ceci?"

"Cecilus Segmunt-sama, I mean. Previously, as an assassin sent against Yorna-sama."

Tanza explained that what she'd seen before was, without question, the real Cecilus.
Hearing that, Subaru remembered something he'd heard before too. If he recalled correctly, since Yorna kept rebelling so often, subjugation forces had apparently been sent after her many times.
Though now that he knew Yorna's true character, Subaru's impression of that was the exact opposite of what it used to be.

"Trying to kill Yorna-san — what a rotten guy..."

"Exactly. Cecilus-sama is a thoroughly wicked man... no, wait, that's not the important part. What matters is that this Cecilus-sama and that Cecilus-sama, despite the difference in age, are the spitting image of one another, and that's what concerns me."

"The spitting image, but with an age gap?"

"Yes. — I wonder if he might be Cecilus-sama's brother."

Having seen both the "other" Cecilus and this fake Cecilus with her own eyes, Tanza's words made Subaru stare hard at the fake Cecilus beside him.
He hadn't considered that possibility. Well, of course he hadn't — Subaru had never seen the real Cecilus, so he had no way to judge resemblance one way or the other.
But it was just his luck that someone who did know the real one happened to be around. Resigned, the lying fake Cecilus would surely now meekly admit to the lie he'd told —

"No, no, that's not it. I'm an only child, and I have no identical siblings! Of course, there's a chance Father made a younger brother after parting ways with me, but going by the flow of this conversation, you mean an older brother, right?"

"Yeah, that's what she means, but you're really bad at letting go, huh..."

"How very hurtful! But but I have grounds for this! Grounds that make it strange whether I have an older brother or a younger brother — either way!"

Watched by the narrow-eyed Subaru and Tanza, the fake Cecilus crossed his arms and pouted. Just how convincing could this pouting fake Cecilus's so-called "grounds" possibly be?
For now, Subaru and Tanza's eyes silently urged him to explain, and—

"The grounds are simple: my own existence."

"How so?"

"If I had an older brother, then the fact that I exist means Father must have killed him! And if I have a younger brother, then it's strange that I haven't been killed by Father! In other words, the very fact that I'm alive proves neither an older nor a younger brother could possibly be alive and exist! What do you think?"

"...How so?"

Delivered with all the force of a completed proof, it was a piece of logic that convinced absolutely no one.
Going by that explanation, the fake Cecilus's father sounded like he was under some kind of curse that only let him have one child. Whether that was true or a lie, the curse version somehow sounded like the better option.
Personally, Subaru wanted to believe the lying fake Cecilus was just piling lie on top of lie.
Except—

"The more brazenly confident you act about this, the more it starts to feel true, weirdly enough. Tanza, what do you think?"

"...I'd like to go along with whatever Schult-sama thinks."

"Ugh, that's a lot of responsibility."

Relied upon by the reserved Tanza, Subaru pressed a slender finger to his small chin and fell into thought.
The fake Cecilus, tilting his head as if to say "why doesn't anyone understand," probably wouldn't give a satisfying answer even if asked directly about his family situation.
What mattered here was simply whether or not to believe the fake Cecilus.

If he didn't believe him, then things stayed as they were — the fake Cecilus was just a wolf-boy, plain and simple.
But if he did believe him, then the fake Cecilus had no siblings, leaving only Tanza's testimony that the fake Cecilus and the real Cecilus closely resembled one another.
And if he took both that testimony and the fake Cecilus's own self-image entirely at face value—

"I'm scared to ask this, but Ceci — you haven't shrunk, have you?"

It was a possibility he'd rather have denied if he could, but it wasn't a question he could avoid asking.
If the one standing here had been the grown-up, pre-shrinking Subaru, he might have hesitated to ask after weighing all sorts of overwrought considerations. But the one standing here was the shrunken, pre-growth Subaru, so he wasn't suited to holding onto a question for long.
With the mindset of "I'll apologize if asking this makes him mad," he tossed out the question carelessly.
At this question born of such circumstances, the fake Cecilus blinked lightly in surprise, and—

"Shrunk, me? Ahaha, that's a funny thing to say, Bussu! If a human body shrinking were actually a thing, that'd be amazing, wouldn't it? So much fun!"

Laughing heartily, the fake Cecilus slapped Subaru's shoulder in high spirits as he said this.
At his words, Subaru furrowed his brow, thoroughly thrown into confusion.

"Okay, which is it...?"

Was he playing dumb or not? And if he was playing dumb, was it consciously or unconsciously?
Subaru himself had a real sense that, due to the effects of the 'Infantilization,' things were steadily slipping out of his memory. So there was a chance the fake Cecilus was forgetting all sorts of things for the same reason.
But even so — compared to Subaru, who still remembered more than he'd forgotten — that would mean the fake Cecilus had forgotten even the fact that he himself had shrunk.

Could something like that even be possible?
If the fake Cecilus had undergone 'Infantilization' just like Subaru, then this fake Cecilus's current mental state might be exactly where Subaru himself was headed.

"Th-that damn old geezer...! I mean, wait — did Ceci get shrunk by Olbart-san too...!?"

If that were true, it would be a huge deal — a huge deal within the Nine Divine Generals themselves.
That this, too, was Olbart's doing was something he'd rather not consider. But it was equally unthinkable that there were multiple people running around with such an absurd technique besides Olbart.
Naturally, he found himself wanting to conclude that Olbart was the one who'd shrunk the fake Cecilus too.

"Wait, wait, so — Ceci isn't a fake, he's the real deal, and Olbart-san is the one who shrunk the real Ceci, and Ceci is what's left of me... is that it?"

"S-Schult-sama, are you all right? You seem quite confused..."

"...I'm fine. I'm super confused, but it's also somehow coming together super well."

Listening only to the thoughts spilling haphazardly out of his mouth, it probably sounded like a mess, but in Subaru's mind, the situation had more or less narrowed down to two possibilities.
One: this fake Cecilus was actually the real Cecilus Segmunt in an 'Infantilized' state — the "that damn geezer Olbart" theory.
And two: taking advantage of his resemblance to the real Cecilus, the fake Cecilus had elevated his own wolf-boy act into a lie convincing enough to pass for truth — the "that damn brat fake Cecilus" theory.

"I'll keep both the damn brat and the damn geezer in the back of my mind for now."

"Hoho, that sounds like quite the intriguing tune. Care to elaborate?"

"Nope. Telling you probably wouldn't accomplish anything anyway."

"Ahaha! What an unfriendly attitude! It's a rather self-centered way of thinking, but I don't hate that direction — in fact, I kind of like it."

Rather than getting angry at being brushed off, the fake Cecilus looked thoroughly delighted — provisionally still "fake Cecilus" for now — and his reaction was, in many ways, a relief.
With so many worries already on his plate, having someone he didn't need to be careful around was precious.

"Anyway, Tanza, let's set the Ceci matter aside for now too. Real or fake, he's equally unreliable either way."

"...That's true, I suppose. He's someone who tried to harm Yorna-sama, so personally, I couldn't possibly feel favorably toward him."

"Nice and honest, that helps. Now then..."

Having reached a sort of conclusion, Subaru shifted his mindset.
Now that he was awake, occupying a bed in the healing room was no longer necessary. He'd probably head to that room the three of them had mentioned earlier, the one given to the three of them. But before that—

"I want to feed my pretty empty stomach. Listen, my belly's been growling so sadly this whole time."

Rubbing his stomach as he said it, Subaru made a rather shameless demand there on the island of his captivity.

△▼△▼△▼△

— There's a saying: "A hungry army cannot fight."

Whatever obstacles Subaru and the others were going to face from here on out, they were all essentially battles. To fight them, body and mind both had to be in top shape, or he'd be in trouble if he couldn't move when it mattered most.
Recklessly pushing forward while skimping on sleep — that wasn't a judgment call he could afford to make.

"Feels like before I shrank, I had more stamina and used to pull stunts like that all the time. If I get that mixed up, it'll probably come back to bite me."

So, he wanted to make sure to get meals and sleep whenever the timing allowed.
It seemed he'd carelessly slept a full day from the exhaustion of the 'Sparka,' but he decided to take that as having gotten proper rest, and chalk it up as a chance for his stamina to recover.
With that, it was time for a meal, and guided by the fake Cecilus, he headed toward the dining area —

"...Eat, Schult. This is your share."

A large plate was set down before him with a thud, and Subaru's eyes went wide at the bone-in meat piled on it.
Looking up in confusion, he found the tattoo-covered face narrowing its eyes as if to say "What?" As if he had any right to ask that — if anyone should be asking "what," it was Subaru.
Having this done to him with no explanation whatsoever — just what was he supposed to do?

"Weitz, that's a bit much, don't you think. Look, Schult's frozen stiff."

"Hah, ain't that just sayin' he don't need no consideration? Besides, ain't no reason to pile a mountain of meat on a runt like this. We should split it up amongst— ow!"

"This is the portion we discussed and agreed on... If you want to go back on it, convince me..."

Saying this, the fork in his hand jabbed the Lizard Human in the side. Unable to bear it, the Lizard Human yelped and fled to the other side of the table.
Sighing at the commotion, the rust-colored-haired one shrugged and looked at Subaru.

"Sorry for the noise, Schult. How's your body?"

"...Feeling okay, I think. How about you?"

"None of us are seriously hurt either. Thanks to you... to your — no, to you."

Correcting himself, the rust-colored-haired one — Idra — bowed lightly to Subaru.
Not just Idra, either. The bickering tattooed man and the lizard man, Weitz and Hiain both, raised a hand toward Subaru or dipped their chins, agreeing with Idra.

Right now, Subaru was in one corner of the area on the island reserved for the sword slaves — a great hall where tables and chairs had been set up and used as a dining space.
The moment he'd stepped into that hall, the three of them appeared and caught him, and before he knew it, he'd been whisked over to one of the tables and had the large plate set before him.
Honestly, it was a dizzying, bewildering situation, but—

"Wait, did you guys save this for me?"

"Huh? What else would it be? A brat too dense to even appreciate gratitude when he sees it... ow!"

"Don't talk like it's your own doing, I'll pound you..."

"I didn't say that! I'm just showin' gratitude, ain't I!"

Pointing at the heaping pile of bone-in meat, Subaru's question sparked yet another spat.
It wasn't exactly a smooth or elegant way to express it, but apparently this was gratitude nonetheless. That a feast like this could be served made him wonder just what the treatment of sword slaves here was really like.

"They make you fight to the death, but then feed you all you can eat...?"

"Strictly speaking, it's not killing each other, it's a death match. Also, as a matter of policy, Gustav-san apparently believes that if a sword slave is going to die, it should be in the arena — dying anywhere else would just prove the management's incompetence. So you can actually live pretty comfortably here."

Beside the head-tilting Subaru, the fake Cecilus pulled up a chair and sat down without a trace of shame. Subaru had figured that with his brazenness he'd reach straight for the plate too, but surprisingly he didn't — instead he poured water from a pitcher into a chipped cup and sipped at it little by little.

"Everyone agrees that Schult-sama contributed the most. So they decided to leave the feast for Schult-sama."

"Tanza... is that true, everyone?"

Opposite the fake Cecilus, Tanza sat in the chair flanking Subaru on the other side, and at her words, Subaru looked over at Idra and the other two in surprise.
At that look, Idra gave a deep nod and said "Yeah,"

"As I said before, the fact that we're all safe like this is thanks to you. On the night you survive your first 'Sparka,' a feast is served for that 'group.' It didn't seem right that you couldn't have your share."

"For the record, it was my idea that we save it..."

"Heh, well I sure as hell wasn't plannin' on leavin' any for the brat!"

Taking turns, they explained why the feast on the large plate had been left for him. But at Hiain's last remark, Tanza beside him tilted her head,

"Was that so? As I recall, it was you, Hiain-sama, who first said it would feel wrong to eat without him."

"Hah!? I never said that!? I don't remember that!? Hey, you little brat, don't go makin' stuff up or I won't let it slide!"

"I had the honor of doing so," Idra put out a hand to stop him, and as Tanza gave a small bow, Hiain — unable to argue back — choked on a "guh" and turned his reptilian face away, giving up the fight.

Even the not-so-honest lizard man included, this was, in the end, a token of gratitude from all of them.

"Well then, gratefully, without holding back at all!"

"Oh-ho, quite the appetite! Showing you've got the capacity to accept it graciously — very nice indeed."

"Don't push yourself and upset your stomach..."

"No worries needed. Believe it or not, I was famous back at my school as the food fighter who'd polish off even the lunches of kids who were out sick."

Getting milk from a kid who couldn't drink it — child's play. Brushing his teeth — also child's play. That was the battle style of school food fighter Natsuki Subaru.
The meat was cold and tough, but the heavy seasoning wasn't bad, and he had no complaints. Above all, the fact that everyone had saved this feast for him was the best spice of all.
But as Subaru tore into the bone-in meat like that, Weitz shook his pattern-covered face and said "No...,"

"Don't get the wrong, creepy idea... I'm not worried about you..."

"—? Then what, you're regretting giving me the meat now?"

"That's not it either... it's the death match..."

"————"

Weitz's words were low and muffled, but they landed on Subaru with tremendous weight.
He nearly dropped the bone-in meat in his hand on reflex, but the fake Cecilus's hand shot out from the side, snatching it up and stuffing it right into Subaru's mouth.

"Mmf mmf...!"

"Easy, easy, you'll choke. Also, what our tattooed friend there said was a little short on details. No need to worry, it's not like there's a death match coming up right away."

"Mmf... r-really?"

"Yes. So far, no such notice has come through. Though apparently there's no telling when word of a death match might arrive."

Dealing with everything but the bone on the piece of meat, then dropping the remains onto his plate with a plop, he looked at Weitz. Hearing what the fake Cecilus and Tanza had added, Weitz nodded.

"That's what I said..."

"You didn't say that! I thought you meant they were gonna make us kill each other again right away!"

"It's not that harsh a place, apparently. Until the island's master changed and policy shifted, though, it seems the treatment was such that Schult's anxiety would have been warranted."

"Before the change... you mean, since Gustav-san took over?"

At the question, Idra nodded, and Subaru reached for the next piece of bone-in meat.
In his mind, he pictured Gustav's fierce, unreadable face, and puzzled over just what kind of person he was.

The cold-bloodedness of throwing Subaru straight into a 'Sparka' the moment he woke up.
He'd assumed the man was practically the embodiment of the Vollachian Empire's cruel, brutal rules, but from what he'd just heard, it didn't sound like he was forcing the sword slaves into fights that would kill them right away.

"Or maybe he just prefers doing things slowly instead of all at once?"

"You can tell he's not the type for a quick, merciful finish, that's for sure. Actually, if Bussu hadn't woken up, it's possible the three of them here would've been treated as a 'group' and had their own 'Sparka.' If it had gone that way, this little meal might've been a much gloomier affair!"

"D-don't joke about that! Anyway, why the hell are you even here!?"

Hiain pointed at the fake Cecilus, who'd laughed off Subaru's remark, and barked at him.
Come to think of it, he had a point. The fake Cecilus was, technically, the one who'd put in a good word to get Subaru and Tanza onto the island, but that was about the extent of their connection.

"From what I hear, every sword slave here is made to join a 'group' for their first 'Sparka.' Afterward, depending on performance, the 'group' may be dissolved, or so I'm told, but your 'group'..."

"Ah, no need to worry about my 'group.' Everyone besides me ended up dead, you see, and luckily I'm now the type of sword slave assigned death matches solo."

"What...?"

"I mean, I'm the strongest, after all! Nobody can beat me, so really, there's nothing whatsoever to worry about, ahaha!"

At the fake Cecilus's proclamation, delivered with a slap of his knee, Subaru and everyone else fell into an awkward silence.
Though the reason Subaru and Tanza fell silent and the reason the other three fell silent were probably different — and even between Subaru and Tanza, he suspected the reasons diverged a little at the root.

Idra and the others probably thought the fake Cecilus's words were pure bluster; Tanza suspected the fake Cecilus might be a blood relative of the real Cecilus Segmunt.
And Subaru thought there was a chance he was a shrunken Cecilus Segmunt — and if that were true, then even in this state, the fake Cecilus might genuinely be the strongest.
If so, having the fake Cecilus as an ally could be a huge advantage—

"—Ah-ah, mustn't do that, Bussu. Looking at someone with such covetous eyes only invites them to look down on you."

"Wha?"

"A person with the qualities to stand above and ahead of others mustn't go around giving such needy, grubby looks. That's not stylish in the slightest — an absolute non-starter! If you want to woo me, you'll need not words but action and resolve, and a killer line to seal the deal."

As he spoke, the fake Cecilus picked up two picked-clean bones from his plate and crossed them in an X in front of his own face, offering this advice to Subaru.
Struck by words sharp enough to seem like they were reading his very thoughts, Subaru swallowed hard.
And then—

"...You said not words, but if you need a killer line, that's words too, isn't it?"

"Oh, wow, you're right! That really doesn't quite land! What was I thinking!"

Eyes wide as saucers, the fake Cecilus leaned back hard against the back of his chair, then, as if sprung by a coil, lurched forward. Subaru froze as the face loomed suddenly close, and watching that, the fake Cecilus broke into a beaming grin.

"Well now, well now, I've overstayed my welcome. I've got my own business, people to talk to, ways to kill time and all that, so I'll take my leave here for now. No worries, we're roommates, so anything left unsaid can wait till then!"

"Ah, ah, roger."

"Roja? Not sure what that means, but it's got a nice, slick ring to it! Well then, roja!"

Getting the interpretation cheerfully wrong, the fake Cecilus raised a hand and dashed off before anyone could stop him.
There'd been no real reason to stop him, but Subaru's eyes still went wide at the sheer speed of his departure.
In truth, he hadn't been that much of a nuisance. It had been nice to have at least one person nearby who knew the ins and outs of the Sword Slaves' Isolated Island.

"Well, having him talk five times more than the information I actually wanted is a bit exhausting, though."

"Schult-sama, please continue your meal."

"Ah, right, yeah. Gotta recover my stamina even more."

Whatever Gustav's thinking, Subaru had no intention of sitting quietly until the next death match came around.
To begin with, he had no intention of staying on as a sword slave for long. His goal was to take Tanza and escape this island — today or tomorrow, if possible.
Even in the worst case, if it meant going against Gustav.
Except, if he did that—

"What's wrong, Schult?"

"Um, I was just thinking about everyone, worried. Do you think you guys can manage without me?"

"Ah? What're you talkin' about. You ain't plannin' on runnin' off, are you? If you are, give back the meat, the meat!"

At Subaru's worried expression, Hiain opened his mouth wide and bellowed.
Getting yelled at was only natural, he supposed, but Subaru had people more important to him than these three. Perhaps he should think about a way to get not just Tanza, but all three of them, out together too.

"...Or better yet, what if everyone on the island escaped together?"

"Schult-sama?"

"Ah, sorry, sorry, talking to myself. That was a bit too far-fetched, even for me."

Even as a mutter under his breath, it was a dangerous notion he couldn't carelessly let others overhear.
But Weitz, Idra, Hiain, and of course Subaru and Tanza too, had all been dragged onto the Sword Slaves' Isolated Island by force, and none of them wanted to be made to fight death matches here.
If everyone else felt the same way, maybe Subaru's idea wasn't so outlandish after all.

And just as Subaru was thinking along those lines—

"So you finally woke up, kid?"
"That last 'Sparka' — that was really something!"
"Damn shame I missed it. Had to hear all the rumors secondhand."

As Subaru reached for the meat on his plate, people began gathering around him in a crowd.
The ones calling out to him so familiarly were, every last one of them, men with the unmistakable look of "checkered pasts" — no need to explain further; surely every one of them was a sword slave.
Newcomers to the hall and those who'd been watching Subaru's group from a distance both drew closer, forming a small crowd centered on Subaru.

"Um, and you all are...?"

"Ain't it obvious just lookin' at us? The island's veterans, that's what. You lot gave us a 'Sparka' that sure wasn't boring, flailin' around like that!"

Surrounding the wide-eyed Subaru, the men burst into a great roar of laughter.
It wasn't mocking, though — the tone was one of genuine praise. Meanwhile, surrounded like this, Idra wore a wry smile, Hiain looked uncomfortable, and Weitz stayed silent, looking sullen.

"That little missy's intervention at the end was a sight to see. Way stronger than she looks."

"Thank you very much. It is all thanks to the grace of the one who loves me."

"Oho? That so. Not sure I follow, but hell of a death match!"

Not pressing too deeply on Tanza's bowed remark, the men — big-hearted enough to strike a good balance — went on gesturing animatedly as they recounted Subaru's group's fight in the 'Sparka,'

"Still, damn impressive. That arena beast — that shadow lion — took out a fair number of 'groups.'"

"If you lot had gone down too, the count of 'groups' it took out would've hit a clean ten. Lately the 'Sparka' matches have been tough back-to-back."

"Probably Governor Gustav's doin'. Once he takes you in as a sword slave, he treats you courteous-like, but the entryway's the harshest part... next show's gonna be a scary one."

The men chattered on without running out of things to say, telling him all sorts of things about Ginunhive's circumstances. Subaru nodded along to each one, gradually picking up bits and pieces of information about the island.
Sure enough, it seemed the policy had changed quite a bit since Gustav became the island's administrator. Also, that lion had apparently been fairly strong, which meant Subaru's group, having won, was held in high regard.

"I see, so it was worth all our effort. ...What's a 'show'?"

"Ah, here, nobles from all over the Empire gather regular-like, and there's a show where our death matches get put on as a spectacle. Both the 'Sparka' and the daily death matches are just practice for the real thing."

"Practice..."

"Dyin' in practice don't accomplish nothin'. The Governor knows that much, I reckon. Long as you do things properly you won't hear complaints outta him... ah, well, there's an exception."

"An exception?"

The heavily-muscled, fierce-looking man rubbed his nose, looking reluctant to say more, and Subaru tilted his head at that.
He stayed silent for a while, then let out a deep sigh and said,

"You've been gettin' friendly with him, so it's a hard thing to say, but — quit hangin' around that one."

"That one, meaning...?"

Going by the man's tone, and thinking of who wasn't present, only one person fit the bill.
Subaru picked up with "You mean—" and,

"You don't mean Ceci? If anything, I'm the one he keeps tagging along after..."

"Yeah, seems like he's taken a shine to you, but the question's how far that can be trusted. — He showed up here about twenty days back."

"Uh-huh."

"His first 'Sparka,' he just stood there and watched while every last one of his own 'group' got wiped out, didn't lift a finger to help. Once all his allies were gone, he killed the arena beast with his bare hands, easy as anything."

"————"

"Ever since, every time he's handed a death match, he doesn't stop till the other side's dead. And on top of it all, for whatever reason, the name he goes by is that of one of the Nine Divine Generals — Cecilus Segmunt."

As he spoke, the fierce-looking man let genuine, deep-seated distrust bleed into both his face and voice, and glanced toward the entrance of the great hall — the very entrance the fake Cecilus, Cecilus Segmunt, had just walked out of moments before.
And he wasn't the only one wearing that look. Everyone present who wasn't part of Subaru's own 'group' — everyone who didn't know him — wore the same expression.

A person who acted friendly toward Subaru, who'd subjected him to a tediously endless display of that incomprehensible story-brain of his, who'd calmly tried to take a dying Subaru's last words without a second thought — and who now drew fierce, visceral loathing from sword slaves living lives constantly bordered by death.
That person —

"— Governor Gustav and that grim reaper. Those two are the ones at the center of just how twisted this Sword Slaves' Isolated Island has become."
