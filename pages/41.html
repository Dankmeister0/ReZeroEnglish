Arc 2 Chapter 17: "The Sound of Chains"

"So, Ram and Rem said they wouldn't be able to drop by your room tonight... which is why I took over supervising your studies instead."

"Is that so?! Whoa, I'm thrilled, seriously what a surprise! Ah, sit down, sit down. You'll want some tea, right? Oh yeah, I pinched some top-tier tea snacks just for an occasion like this."

Inviting Emilia into his room, Subaru frantically began making preparations to entertain his guest. He sprinted down the hallway, ducked into the tea-preparation room designated for the servants' floor, whipped up tea and treats, and dashed right back. The whole feat took him a mere two minutes.

Demonstrating the skills he had honed during his servant life in the manor to their fullest extent, Subaru pulled off a level of performance he wouldn't even display for the master of the house, Roswaal.

"Thanks for waiting! I'm back! I brought out the prized tea leaves and emergency snacks, so let's eat together—say 'ah'!"

"I feel like you've completely forgotten the main topic... Do you mind if I ask something?"

At the question from Emilia, who was standing awkwardly in the middle of the room, Subaru—still clutching the tray—tilted his head in confusion. Before his eyes, she made a deliberate gesture, pointing toward the bed.

"Doesn't that look... awfully meticulously made?"

"Ah! No! It's not like I was plotting anything! I was just trying to lay Ram-chi down! Nothing suspicious at all!"

"What were you planning to do after laying Ram in your bed?"

"Whoa, that misunderstanding took a sudden hairpin turn, didn't it?!"

Placing the tray on the desk, Subaru dived onto the bed under her suspicious glare. He thoroughly ruined the bedding he had spent so much time carefully prepping.

"See? Now it's all completely ruined. Right! A wicked girl like Ram-chi gets a messy, rumpled bed and likes it!"

"So you still intend to put her to sleep here."

"My excuses can't hold the line! Puck, come baaack!"

"It's already his bedtime, so I'm not going to wake him up... Not that it really matters, though."

As Subaru clung to the thought of the gray cat, Emilia gently hugged her arms and averted her gaze.

"It's fine for you to be friendly with Ram and Rem, Subaru, but you have to maintain proper boundaries, okay? Especially since those two are still young..."

"Having a strong moral sense gives you extra points in my book, too. But wait, isn't there someone in this manor far more deserving of that lecture than me? Hint: long hair, total pervert."

"Ah, that one is hopeless... I've given up on correcting him."

"The light literally vanished from Emilia-tan's eyes! That actually gives me chills!"

Her demeanor, imbued with resignation, was like that of an abandoned puppy, bludgeoning his protective instincts as if with a club. Long story short, no matter what face Emilia made, Subaru found her adorable. Ultimately—

"E・M・T (Emilia-tan is Seriously an Angel)."

"You still have that face you make when you're saying something suuuper ridiculous. I think I'm starting to get a handle on you, Subaru."

"Well aren't you saying something nice! But don't go thinking you can see right through me so easily! Ah, here's your tea."

"Ah, thank you, thank you."

Emilia accepted the offered tea and quietly quenched her thirst. Giving up the chair in front of the desk to her, Subaru sat on the bed and sipped his tea. Sharing the prized snacks between them, a relaxing, gentle time flowed between the two. Perhaps a moment like this was what people called happiness.

"And that... is what I think... Subaru."

"You're saying something totally ordinary, yet for some reason it feels like it hits deep."

"It's a verbal trick. The key is to pronounce it like it's written in hiragana. All you're doing is stating the obvious as if it's obvious, though."

Perhaps having grown accustomed to Subaru's nonsense, Emilia's demeanor remained natural. Knowing that she too was enjoying this peaceful moment made him feel somehow happy. Thus, time quietly drifted by—

"Wait, I didn't come here to play! We can't just be relaxing!"

"Coming to a guy's room alone at night—what else could that be called besides a late-night hangout?! You're playing with fire here!"

"I think you already know this, but I can use magic even without Puck, you know?"

The dry air crackled and popped, and Subaru could feel the room's temperature plunge instantly. Seeing the steam rising from the tea grow thicker and more intense, Subaru decided pushing his luck any further was impossible. With a wry smile, he turned toward the desk.

"Okay, okay! Got it! Let me study, let me study! A heart-pounding study session with Emilia-tan in a private room at night... Just saying it is getting me excited!"

"Yes, yes, I get it. Anyway, what do you usually do?"

Swapping places so Subaru sat in the chair, Emilia placed a hand on the backrest and asked from right beside him. Feeling her breath close by, Subaru nervously opened his notebook and reference book.

"Right now, I'm practicing writing and memorizing the basic I-script. I heard this fairy tale anthology is mostly a collection of stories written in I-script, so being able to read this is my current goal."

"Hmm, a fairy tale anthology..."

Picking up the book with the reddish-brown spine, Emilia flipped through its pages to check the contents. Suddenly, the finger turning the pages paused, making Subaru look up at her with a "Hmm?"

"Did you find a story that caught your eye?"

"Nnn, not quite, but a little bit. Well, once you're able to read this, Subaru, I'm sure you'll understand."

Closing the book with a snap, Emilia moved over and settled herself on the bed. Seeing Emilia sitting on the bed he had just messied up sent a faint wave of excitement through Subaru, making him fidget. However, seemingly oblivious to Subaru's beastly instincts, she stretched her body out—

"Honestly, I was planning to talk with the spirits I can only meet in the dark, but since Ram and Rem asked me to, I'm prioritizing you today, Subaru. You'd better be grateful."

"I'm beyond grateful! Seriously, it's divine guidance!"

"Wh-When you thank me that much, I feel a little embarrassed."

"Thank you! Ram-chi! Rem-rin! I will never let your thoughtfulness go to waste! Natsuki Subaru will become a real man!"

"Wait, them?! What about me?!"

After shouting his gratitude toward the twins who created this situation, Subaru turned back to the disgruntled Emilia and thrust out both hands.

"Of course, for Emilia-tan, I have an overwhelming downpour of maximum gratitude and appreciation! How about a massage once we're done studying? As a token of my daily thanks, I'll take your hands, your feet, your waist, and melt away all your built-up fatigue! Ehehe."

"Your hands look perverted, so no."

"Damn it, my own honesty betrays me! But I don't hate that about myself either!"

Emilia sighed at Subaru, who leaned over the desk pretending to cry. Putting an end to the conversation with that familiar gesture, she clapped her hands together.

"Come on, stop fooling around. You have proper work tomorrow too, so let me finish before it affects your sleep. Go on, get back to it."

"Ayy, roger that. Oh, Emilia-tan, feel free to eat the rest of the snacks, okay?"

"Yes, yes, thank you... Though, eating between meals, huh."

It seemed that even in another world, refraining from snacking for maidenly reasons was a universal trait. Gaining that bit of useless knowledge, Subaru began his daily character-writing practice with Emilia behind him. Once he actually got down to business, Subaru was capable of focusing on his studies. While Emilia's presence was certainly distracting, given that he was on track to fulfill the major premise of "making a promise," he had no real reason to rush.

Thanks to that mental composure, Subaru calmly copied down the characters. After a brief period of silence, Emilia called out from behind him.

"Hmm, you're not getting off track as much as I expected."

"Because I'm focused. Once I get into something, I'm the type who loses sight of everything else around me. So I'm straight as an arrow when it comes to the person I like, too!"

"I hope so. But as long as you're making eyes at me, I wonder~"

Subaru flinched at Emilia's teasing attitude. In all likelihood, his approaches so far hadn't been taken seriously by her at all. He was being so direct, so he wished she'd believe him at least a little.

"But with my skill level, flirting without mixing in a joke is impossible! I'd seriously die of embarrassment halfway through! I'm like a Heian nobleman, in love with love and shortening my own lifespan!"

"Yes, yes, stop fooling around. ...Honestly, if you'd just take things that seriously all the time. You'll end up being abandoned by Ram and Rem, you know?"

Emilia muttered toward Subaru, who held his head in his hands, as she hugged one knee to her chest on the bed. Because her skirt was short, it made for a rather risky view, but being utterly defenseless, she was oblivious to such subtleties. Tilting his body slightly, Subaru focused all his visual acuity into the corner of his eyes. If he turned his gaze normally he'd get caught, so he used the classic technique of looking while pretending not to look.

"This is the stealth technique of one who inherits the lineage of 'Yin'! Gate, fully open!"

Subaru burned with a strange sense of mission. Ignorant of his scheme, Emilia lowered her eyes while sitting in that provocative pose, her expression uncheerful. She quietly murmured,

"Hey, Subaru. I don't really want to ask this, but... why don't you take things seriously?"

"Being seriously non-serious is my policy... doesn't sound like the kind of answer you're looking for. Uh..."

"Yes, a serious conversation. —Ram was grumbling a bit, too. She said it feels like Subaru is... how should I put it, slacking off here and there during work."

Perhaps because it felt like tattling, Emilia's tone as she questioned him was hesitant. But hearing this, Subaru had no choice but to wince at the pain of having hit the bullseye.

If his work performance was evaluated as cutting corners in between, he had to admit it was an entirely accurate assessment. In truth, Subaru hadn't been putting his heart into his work. Rather, it would be more accurate to say he was intentionally calibrating his efforts to produce the same results as last time.

Compared to last time when he hardly understood the work, Subaru now actually knew a thing or two. If his servant skills last time were rated ALL 'C', they were now ALL 'C+' so to speak. In reality, he was still only at the start of climbing the long, long hill of servanthood, but it seemed even that tiny difference couldn't fool the eyes of his seniors.

Naturally, guilt made Subaru's lips heavy. Seeing Subaru unable to utter a word, let alone offer a rebuttal, Emilia muttered "I thought so" under her breath, before continuing:

"It's not like you don't feel guilty, right? You seem oddly conscientious in weird places, Subaru. You don't slack off on studying, either."

"Well, I have my reasons, I guess... and studying is for my own benefit too... Realizing it as I say it, I'm doing everything entirely for my own reasons, aren't I?"

Looking back, it was the absolute worst. Troubling the twins for his own convenience, absorbing only the parts that benefited him, and cutting corners on everything else. A triple crown of absolute garbage.

"F-From tomorrow on, I will change my attitude and give it my all, so please forgive me, my Queen."

"Mhm, you may rise... Is that not quite right?"

Tilting her head, Emilia wondered if her phrasing was off. Subaru felt relieved by her softening attitude, while firmly resolving to make good on his words starting tomorrow. At the very least, tonight marked a clean breaking point. From tomorrow on, he wouldn't need to trace his previous actions, nor would he need to intentionally cut corners at work.

He would be able to start repaying the debt of gratitude he owed to Ram and Rem over these four days. Though, quitting slacking off didn't mean his stats would instantly shoot up, of course.

"In things like this, the spirit is what counts. My earnest effort has a value money can't buy. That's right, priceless."

"Joking around again just when things were getting good... Did you finish studying?"

"Today's portion, yep! Oh yeah, I have a favor to ask Emilia-tan, will you hear me out?"

Fidgeting with his hands, he launched an upturned-eyes begging attack. It was a gesture targeted directly at her maternal instinct, but Emilia pulled back slightly with a look of distaste.

"No way, I have a suuuper bad feeling about this."

"A negative impression so strong it gives you goosebumps?! It's nothing like that, at all! It's just, to give my all starting tomorrow, I was thinking I'd like a reward, that's all!"

"A reward? Just so you know, I don't have any money I can spend freely."

"I'm penniless and alone in the world until I get paid, too! Bu-bu-but just hear me out. Yeah, since I'll be working seriously starting tomorrow... let's go on a date!"

With a thumbs-up and a flash of his teeth, he struck what he considered his ultimate signature pose. At Subaru's familiar posing, Emilia blinked in bewilderment.

"A 'da-te'... what do you do on one?"

"Hmph. When a man and a woman go out alone together, that is already a date. What happens during it is known only to the Goddess of Love."

"In that case, you went on a da-te with Rem today, Subaru."

"My date virginity was stolen?! I've been defiled!"

As Subaru wrapped his arms around his shoulders and wept bitterly, Emilia waved her hand with a wry smile, saying "Yes, yes..."

"I understand that you want to go out together, but where are we going?"

"Ehee, actually, in the village right near the manor, there's a super lovely doggy-woggy. Also, there's a field of flowers—seriously gorgeous. The joint appearance of Emilia-tan and a profusion of blooming flowers... I definitely want to preserve that forever with my magic vessel."

Quietly resting in a corner of the room were his starting items, which had survived the battle at the Loot House and been retrieved along with him. The contents of the plastic bag were largely untouched; his cell phone, of course, as well as the snacks and instant cup ramen were all still intact.

"If I only had a charger and an outlet, I'd fill the memory capacity to the brim with pictures of Emilia-tan... and change my wallpaper every single day. Ugh, I seriously want to, so super bad. Why weren't you summoned to another world, Thomas!"

He could only call out the name of that famous King of Inventors and lament the gap between civilization levels. The cell phone's battery bar had already dropped by one; it was only a matter of time. Eventually, he would just have to gamble on the possibility of charging it with lightning magic or something.

That's a solution straight out of a kids' manga...

"Hmm, the village, huh..."

In front of Subaru as he muttered to himself, Emilia placed a hand against her cheek with a thoughtful expression. Come to think of it, Subaru remembered she had pondered over it like that last time as well. Relying on the memory that it wasn't negative thinking—like not wanting to go out with Subaru—he recreated the memory of how he had pushed the issue back then. That's right—

"The doggy-woggy is super cute, let's go!"

"But I might be a burden to you, Subaru, and the villagers might..."

"The kids are innocent and literally an army of angels, let's go!"

"...Sigh, alright. I get it. I'll go with you."

"The flower field is super colorful and wonderful, let's go... Wait, huh, for real?"

Feeling caught off guard, as if there had been less resistance than last time, Subaru involuntarily dropped his act and questioned her. Emilia pouted her lips.

"If that will get you motivated starting tomorrow, then I'll accompany you. Honestly, you really shouldn't be slacking off so much anymore, okay?"

"I won't, I won't! I'll be super fast. In fact, I'm ready to leave the dog, the kids, and the flower field behind to be alone with you!"

"You've completely thrown away the whole point of the da-te!"

Emilia landed a perfect straight-man retort to Subaru's statement, which had practically begged for a comeback. For a while after that, the two chattered merrily back and forth until they realized the night was growing late, whereupon Emilia stood up from the bed with a "Well then."

"If you want, you can even sleep in that bed, you know?"

"For some reason you're breathing heavily and the sheets are messy, so I'll pass."

"Damn it! Why is it so messy... Ram-chi, obstructing me even when she's not here!"

He felt as if Ram's face—which until moments ago had been at the pinnacle of his gratitude—was floating in the night sky, looking down at him with contemptuous eyes from on high. Beside Subaru, who was imagining a scene like she had passed away under the starry sky, Emilia stepped over to the window and likewise looked up at the sky.

"Mhm, the stars are beautiful tonight, too. ...I'm sure tomorrow will be nice weather."

"—Yeah, and it's going to be an unforgettable day."

"There you go again..."

Leaning her back against the window frame and turning around, Emilia was about to admonish Subaru for making light remarks again. But her lips stopped moving as she looked at him. It was likely because she saw that Subaru's expression was unusually serious.

Noticing Emilia falling silent, Subaru blinked.

"Huh, what's wrong? If you take too long, sleepy old me is gonna indiscriminately turn Emilia-tan into my hugging pillow, you know~?"

"Subaru, you really are a strange boy."

"Suddenly an unjust assessment?! Did I do something?!"

Flustered by the attack from outside his expectations, Subaru panicked. Emilia pulled away from the window, crossed the room, and passed by Subaru's side, sweetly saying, "It's nothiiiing." With her hand on the doorknob, she turned back to look at him.

"Well then, Butler Subaru-kun. Work properly starting tomorrow. Rewards are called rewards because they are given only to those who work hard."

With a light gesture resembling a salute, her silver hair fluttered as she left behind a smile. Without waiting for his response, the girl's shadow disappeared outside the door. Even if he reached out his hand, he could no longer reach it. The door shut with a click, leaving Subaru alone in the room. However—

"Hey, hey, hey, hey, for real? Man, now I'm seriously getting pumped up. For real."

The promise he had wanted to make had been made once again. And now, Subaru could challenge this night once more.

Whether he could pass through the fourth night and reach the promised morning of the fifth day. The time for the showdown was roughly six hours.

"Alright, let me at you, Lady Fate—"

Rather than sitting on the bed, Subaru sat on the floor with the bed as his backrest, eagerly awaiting the passage of time as night brought forth the morning.

The chilly sensation of the floor, after sitting for nearly two hours, had long since been warmed by his body temperature and offered no help in keeping his consciousness alert. Yet, Subaru's body was at the peak of wakefulness, completely untouched by the clutches of drowsiness. The reason was simple.

"Who could sleep with their heart pounding like this?"

His heartbeat was fast and elevated, the sound loud and sharp as if ringing directly in his ears. He could acutely feel the sensation of blood circulating through his entire body, and he could tell the tips of his fingers were aching with a faint numbness.

"All this just because I can't wait for my date with Emilia-tan? Jeez, am I an elementary schooler who can't sleep the night before a field trip? Reminds me of oversleeping on the school trip."

Literally the whole family had overslept. Just shows how much they had all been looking forward to it. The sorrow of that day, when he alone had been handed a convenience store bento, still lay throbbing quietly in the depths of his chest.

Reflecting on those painful memories to distract himself, Subaru kept gazing up at the night sky. The glitter of the stars remained unchanged, with only the moon's position moving little by little toward the west. There were probably about four hours left until the sun eventually rose from the eastern sky.

It really was a long time, he thought deeply. Even without a trace of sleepiness, he had to remain on guard because he didn't know what might happen. Considering the possibility of an attack, killing time was out of the question; surrendering his eardrums to the explosive sounds of his own body while continuing to think was the only course of action available to him right now.

He reflected once more on these four days—that is, his second set of four days. The initial blunders, and several differences from the first week. Their impact on the journey leading up to tonight was likely quite significant. However, on the other hand, he judged that he had passed through most of the key events lingering in his memory. The second week was essentially a form where minor sub-events were added to that foundation. If he presumed the cause of his previous Return was failing to set necessary flags, was there a possibility that events like tonight's study session corresponded to those flags?

"I can't rule out the possibility, but..."

Assuming there was an entity guiding Subaru through the loop, whether it would deem that study session important was hard to say. Then again, he couldn't sense any other major changes besides that. His relationship with Emilia remained unchanged, and his relationship with Ram and Rem felt somewhat better than before.

"If I have any lingering regrets left..."

...it was the fact that he couldn't meet with Beatrice tonight.

In the previous world, immediately after making the promise with Emilia, Subaru had broken into Beatrice's room via "Door Crossing." He didn't recall having any major conversation, and thus had to admit the probability of it being a flag was extremely low, but this time his relationship with her was even worse than before. Hounded by strict time management, Subaru had hardly gained any opportunity to see her face-to-face during these four days.

"Though even last time, all we did was trade insults whenever we saw each other."

Even as he muttered that, Subaru couldn't ignore the lingering discomfort in his chest. While it was true he didn't remember having any deep conversation with Beatrice, in this second iteration, what had initially saved Subaru's heart was unmistakably her attitude. It was precisely her attitude—making no attempt to hide her suspicion of Subaru—that had brought him relief and allowed him to pick himself back up.

"Maybe I should've at least thanked her."

In this world, she wouldn't have the slightest clue what he was talking about, and he could pretty much guess how she would reply if he did say it.

Even though their relationship was one where they traded insults whenever they met, thinking of Beatrice softened Subaru's lips into a slight smile. Even those unchanging exchanges were memories that brought a chuckle when recalled like this. If he could reach tomorrow, if he could reach the morning, there were so many more things he wanted to do.

Not just Beatrice—he had plenty of things he wanted to say to Ram, Rem, and even Roswaal. Of course, he hoped they'd forgive him for doing so only after lavishing ten thousand words upon Emilia first.

"Oops, ten thousand might not be enough to express all my feelings. And whatever feelings are left unexpressed, I'll demonstrate through my actions..."

He embraced the blushing Emilia of his fantasy, pantomiming the act of locking lips with her flush-cheeked, upturned face, eagerly grinding away at an imaginary CG gallery.

Looking back, those eight days combined across the previous and current loops brought a smile to his face. Perhaps because his internal tension was relaxing, he felt his eyelids growing slightly heavy, even though there were still more than three hours until morning.

"After coming this far, falling asleep on the job is seriously no joke. This isn't like playing an online game..."

Muttering to himself, he rubbed his eyelids to shake off the sudden drowsiness. However, the sleepiness brought a subtle chill along with it, eliciting an involuntary shiver and a wry laugh. Wrapping his arms around his shoulders, he rubbed his arms to raise his body temperature. Yet no matter how much he did, the chill showed no sign of receding; on the contrary, there was no sign of the sleepiness fading either.

—Something was wrong.

The optimistic view of his situation shifted as Subaru noticed the change.

—It wasn't just cold. It was agonizingly cold.

Looking down beneath the sleeves of his tracksuit, he could visually see goosebumps breaking out on his skin, trembling from a deep, internal chill. It was abnormal. The climate of this otherworldly realm was warm, usually maintaining a temperature comfortable enough with his tracksuit sleeves rolled up.

Why, then, was he now shivering so hard that his teeth chattered violently?

"Bad... Don't tell me... this is...!"

Sensing terror rather than just cold in his trembling, Subaru hurriedly planted his hands on the floor. However, the shaking had already spread throughout his entire body, to the point where the hands trying to push him up couldn't support his weight. Taking nearly thirty seconds, he somehow managed to stand up. Yet his legs supporting his body wobbled at the knees, threatening to collapse at any moment if he let his focus slip.

The sensation of blood draining from his face. A sluggish exhaustion draining strength from his limbs. The heartbeat that had been so loud now weakened, and he could feel his very thoughts growing dull and heavy.

With no strength anywhere in his body, he felt as though he would forget to breathe if he didn't do so consciously. Trembling his lips as he gasped, Subaru dragged his shaking legs toward the outside of the room—crawling out into the hallway to seek help.

"...S-Someone..."

He tried to call out loudly for someone, but his voice came out raspy, as if his throat were blocked. His bone-dry throat throbbed with pain merely from air passing through, and the lethargy seemed to possess the contraction of his lungs itself, slowing his breathing. This is bad—that single thought dominated Subaru's mind.

Not a single concrete solution came to mind, nor did Subaru understand what was being done to him. The only thing he understood was the fact that his current situation was threatening his life.

Groaning, he made his way toward the staircase with faltering steps. It was a familiar path, and not a great distance by any means. Even so, his destination felt overwhelmingly distant. Just reaching the stairs left his breath ragged and broken, and taking steps toward the upper floor from there was a grueling ordeal that seemed to scrape away a piece of his soul with every single step.

"Haa... haa...!"

Climbing step by step using all his limbs, he somehow reached the upper floor. His destination was further down the dimly lit hallway—where the person Subaru sought resided. There was a sickening sensation as if his innards were being melted down and stirred together; unable to suppress the surging nausea and tears, he wept as his crawling feet moved forward, forward.

He had to reach Emilia, to where Emilia was.

A sense of mission, a sense of duty, an indescribable emotion drove Subaru onward. There was no longer any miserable clinginess to his own life or a desire to be saved; there was only a compulsive, absolute imperative that, pathetic as he was, he had to get there.

Emilia's room was located at the innermost end of the upper western wing of Roswaal Manor. By the time he reached the same floor, Subaru was already on the verge of his final breath. Had anyone seen him objectively, they might have averted their eyes from his gruesome state.

Leaning his weight against the wall, soiling himself with excrement and urine, his face utterly stained with tears and vomit, his appearance had lost even the bare minimum dignity of a human being. Lethargy enveloped his whole body, and muscles everywhere refused to obey. A destination just a fraction further away felt to Subaru as distant as the clouds in the sky.

"――――"

His breathing was rough, and a sharp, high-pitched ringing echoed in his ears. Therefore, Subaru noticing that strange sound was purely, utterly by chance.

It felt like he heard the sound of chains clanking.

A sense of wrongness—that stopped Subaru in his tracks. Instantly, unable to support his weight, his body leaning against the wall slithered far down. He tried to brace himself, but could not hold on. Collapsing onto the floor—

"—Ngh?"

The next moment, an impact struck Subaru.

His whole body lurched wildly, and the body that was supposed to fall to the floor was blown away. Only after bouncing across the ground multiple times and cleaning the floor with his face did Subaru realize he had suffered some tremendous impact.

There was no pain. There was only a sluggish, numbing discomfort stretching from the tips of his limbs to the contents of his stomach. A lethargy akin to the numbness of stopped blood flow dominated his body.

"What... happened..."

He muttered as he tried to push himself up, attempting to raise his face-down body. But his trembling arms had no strength even as they clawed at the ground. No, something was wrong. Even setting aside the lack of strength, he couldn't keep his balance. In the first place, while his right arm was trying this hard, why wasn't his left arm even showing itself?

The already dim hallway grew distant, as if refusing to offer even its faint light to aid him. Through his blurred, distorted vision, Subaru looked at his fruitlessly struggling right arm, and then turned his eyes toward the left arm that had abandoned its duty—

—And realized the left half of his body had been torn off from the shoulder down.

"—Ah?"

Toppling his lying body onto its side, Subaru turned his left side upward and froze in blank bewilderment. Of the two arms needed to stand up, the left half didn't exist. His left arm had been blown off from the shoulder, and splintered flesh and bone peered out from the gouged wound. Even in the night air, the pink interior was vivid, continuously spilling a ceaseless stream of vivid red.

The wound wasn't limited to his left shoulder; it extended to his left flank, where part of his internal organs bulged out from another deeply gouged opening. How many times had it been now that he was witnessing his own intestines?

Thinking that in a state of half-stupor, a moment later the pain of seeing his wounds pierced through his entire body. A sensation that could no longer be described as pain or heat choked Subaru's throat as he thrash-flopped like a fish hauled onto land, robbing him of even the leeway to scream as he writhed in agony.

"AAAAAAAAGGGGGHHH—"

His vision flickered, red and yellow lights racing alternately before his eyes. It painted over the familiar mansion scenery from just moments ago, dragging Subaru's consciousness away to somewhere not of this world.
