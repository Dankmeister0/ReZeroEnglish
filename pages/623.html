Arc 8 Chapter 7: "Todd Fang"

——Todd Fang was a soldier of the Vollachian Empire, holding the rank of lance corporal.

He was the fiancé of Katya Aurélie, younger sister of his colleague Jamal Aurélie, and now, following a deployment to the Buddheim Jungle in the empire's eastern reaches that led into combat against the rebel army in the walled city of Guaral, he had taken up the post of attendant to General Arakiya, the "Spirit Eater."

Then came the siege of the imperial capital, Lupugana, touched off by rebels rising all at once.

He fought in that siege too, as a common soldier, battling fiercely to protect the people of the capital — above all, his fiancée Katya — wielding his own weapon and fighting the rebels with courage.

His true nature, though, was that of a "Werewolf," a being treated with contempt and loathing in the Vollachian Empire, exposed to age-old discrimination and persecution, one who had shaped his own way of living precisely because he could never be reconciled with others.

That was Todd Fang — a werewolf of the empire.

——I don't intend to tell you any more than that.
