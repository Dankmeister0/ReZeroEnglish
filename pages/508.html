Arc 7 Chapter 11: "The Lifeblood Ritual"

"—So what the hell is this 'Lifeblood Ritual'?"

"For the People of Shudrak, who place great value on pride and the worth of a sworn pact, it's one custom they can never simply disregard. The details are best left for them to explain. More importantly—"

Answering only the surface of Subaru's question, Abel shot him a sidelong glare. The young Shudrak girl had gone off to relay Abel's earlier message to the chieftain, Mizelda, leaving only Subaru and Abel in this place. Which meant the time available for a private conversation was limited as well.

"Let's hear it. You said you were taken prisoner at the camp outside the forest. How were you treated?"

"...The wounds on my shoulder and back are proof of my accomplishments there. And they had me doing chores, too."

To be precise, the chores and the abuse belonged to separate loops, but Subaru, pressed by Abel's overbearing presence, answered as he did. Hearing this, Abel narrowed his eyes with a "hmph," glanced at Subaru's left hand, and said,

"Seeing as you make no mention of your fingers, that must be a separate matter. I take it you were done in by the woman you were chasing after."

"Ugh... what's that got to do with anything?"

"It serves as proof that you're a fool who's fallen for a woman who'd break your fingers."

That characterization was hardly an appropriate way to describe the relationship between Subaru and Rem. But there was neither the time nor any obligation to dwell on it and explain the details at length. Abel too, promptly disengaging his attention from the state of Subaru's left hand, went on,

"Chores, you say—then you saw the inside of the camp too. Roughly how was it laid out? Wring out that empty head of yours and drag it up from memory."

"A few tents, and roughly how many people were in the camp, maybe... hey, what's this even about?"

"Don't you understand? It should be obvious. What you saw—"

Peppered with rapid-fire questions, Subaru scowled, and Abel clicked his tongue. But Abel couldn't answer Subaru's question. Before he could, the sound of multiple footsteps came toward the cage again. It was Mizelda, dragged along by that same girl, and her group behind her—

"Heard it from Utakata. You two said you'd take the 'Lifeblood Ritual.'"

Resting a hand on the head of the girl clinging to her leg—the one called Utakata—Mizelda, her hair dyed red, fixed a serious gaze on Subaru and Abel. It was a look laced with the same sharpness, the same overpowering intensity, as when Subaru had trampled on her people's pride as warriors earlier.

"Just where did you learn of the 'Lifeblood Ritual'? That's a rite passed down only among us, the Shudrak."

"Don't make me laugh, young chieftain of the Shudrak. Do you truly believe that in this day and age, your people's traditions are unknown to anyone else? Put two humans together and secrets leak out. Stop wishing for the fairy tale that your unity is some unbroken monolith."

"——"

"Your precious 'Lifeblood Ritual' is no exception. In fact, I know what kind of rite it is, and what has happened with it in the past."

Mizelda's gaze sharpened, and Abel's rhetoric only grew more heated in response. At his overbearing manner, not only Mizelda but the expressions of the Shudrak around her stiffened as well, and inwardly Subaru swallowed hard.

As it stood, Subaru alone had no idea what this "Lifeblood Ritual" Abel spoke of actually entailed, left behind by the conversation. Still, one thing was certain: it was an important rite to Mizelda and her people, and Abel, treating their feelings with contempt, was not welcome. So, to prevent any further confusion, Subaru raised his voice with an "Um!"

"Sorry to interrupt while things are heating up, but could someone tell me about this 'Lifeblood Ritual'? It's probably not unrelated to me either, right?"

"...Why do you think tha-t?"

"Well, this masked bastard here threatened me earlier. Something about whether I could sacrifice everything. My answer was 'no way in hell I can.'"

"Then—"

"The only thing I can put on the line is myself. You're overestimating your own influence a bit, aren't you?"

Talk of sacrificing everything was a privilege afforded only to people of considerable power. Unfortunately, caught here by the Shudrak with neither hand nor foot free to act, Subaru and Abel probably didn't even have the qualifications to stand before such a grandiose choice. So the only stakes he could wager were what was already in his own hand. As things stood, that meant Natsuki Subaru, and nothing else.

"But like Abel said, I need you all to hear me out too, Mizelda-san. I'll say it again even though it's just repeating what I said before—I'll say it as many times as it takes. At the very worst, to protect what matters to me, I need you to at least let me out of here."

"...I see. Then it seems you do have the qualifications to take on the 'Lifeblood Ritual.'"

At Subaru's appeal, desperate to somehow make the conversation work, Mizelda murmured that quietly. At the answer, Subaru's eyes went wide, and a faint sound escaped Abel's throat. But hearing Mizelda's murmur, there was one person who reacted excessively. It was one of the group surrounding Mizelda, standing beside her—a woman with hair dyed blue.

"Sister! Are you serious? Taking these men's words at face valu-e..."

"I'm not taking them at face value, Taritta. I just thought it a shame to simply cast aside."

"Sister..."

The woman called Taritta lowered her face at the words of the one she'd called "sister," Mizelda. It seemed the two were sisters; now that it was pointed out, indeed, their features—striking for the intensity of their eyes—did resemble each other closely. And so, having brushed off her younger sister's words, Mizelda turned her gaze back to Subaru and said,

"You've heard of the 'Lifeblood Ritual.' It's a rite passed down among us Shudrak since ancient times, to prove oneself to the tribe. You could call it a coming-of-age rite."

"Coming-of-age... ah, that kind of thing. But we're not exactly—"

"Not of the Shudrak. Everyone here understands that without being told. Don't waste time playing the fool. What matters is the essence of the rite."

Learning it was a rite meant to grant recognition as a Shudrak adult, Subaru faltered, and Abel looked exasperated at him. His cheek twitched at the remark, but even so, Subaru understood what he meant. The essence of a coming-of-age rite lay in having the group recognize the challenger as a full-fledged member. In other words, the essence of the "Lifeblood Ritual" was—

"A rite of passage so the Shudrak will hear us out as equals..."

"Precisely."

Affirming Subaru's line of thought, Abel folded his arms and looked at Mizelda. Receiving that gaze, Mizelda too drew her chin in and said,

"If you mean to challenge the 'Lifeblood Ritual,' then prepare yourselves."

"And what happens if we withdraw? Are you going to tell us, at this point, that you'd release us? Regrettably, my head isn't so out of touch with the world that I'd hold out hope for such a convenient tale. Neither is this Natsuki Subaru's."

"Whuh!?"

Amid the two of them getting worked up on their own, Subaru, folded into the mix as if fully game, was startled—but Abel didn't so much as glance his way to care. Swept along at that pace, Subaru said to Mizelda, who was asking "How will you do it-?"

"...I'll do it. If there's no other way, I'll take the rite and get you to hear us out. Though I'd rather it not be some ritual that takes days and days."

"Fair enough. We don't want that either. In that case..."

"Sister, if that's so, wouldn't Elgina be fitting-?"

To Mizelda, mulling over the proposal after Subaru had steeled himself to undergo the rite, Taritta offered a helping hand with that suggestion. At her younger sister's proposal, Mizelda nodded deeply and said,

"That would do. The 'Lifeblood Ritual'—whenever it's performed, the greatest hardship available is chosen."

"The greatest hardship... which is—"

"—Elgina."

To Subaru, who swallowed hard, Mizelda repeated that word once more. At that, Utakata's shoulders jumped and she shrank in on herself, and even the other Shudrak women were wrapped in a certain tension. The reaction of these women, proud as warriors, was more than enough to stoke Subaru's unease. And yet—

"Neither you nor I can turn back now. Are you prepared?"

"You went ahead and decided everything yourself, and now you're acting all high and mighty about it. Just because you're owed a favor from me doesn't mean you get to do whatever the hell you want..."

He owed Abel a debt for that one knife, but any such delicate feelings had been utterly blown away by their exchange here. Of course, he was also grateful that Abel had recovered from Subaru's earlier misstep and created an opening for the women to hear them out.

"As far as I know, nobody who hides their face is ever any good!"

"There are reasons for it. Impertinent though it is, I won't deny it."

In stories, when a person with a hidden face appears, that person is often connected to the protagonist somehow. In this case, Subaru was in the protagonist's position, but—

"Abel's got a slimmer build than my dad, and his voice is different too. Besides, there's no way I'd mistake him for my dad."

"...You seem to be talking about something pointless."

"It's not pointless, it's about my dad. The most annoying and coolest guy in the world."

"——"

Perhaps it wasn't a topic of much interest, but the temperature of Abel's gaze visibly dropped. In truth, Subaru had only brought up such a pointless thing because a masked figure had appeared here in the Empire, likening it to an old space-war movie from years past. Probably, it was fine to just think of it as "someone with a hidden face I don't know." And, setting aside such pointless musings, Mizelda gave instructions to her fellow tribespeople, saying,

"Abel and Natsuki Subaru, we'll take you two to Elgina. Prove it—accomplish the 'Lifeblood Ritual' magnificently!"

With that, the cage was opened, and Subaru and Abel were led outside.

Once brought out of the cage, Subaru and Abel, neither blindfolded nor bound, were marched outside the settlement, surrounded by the People of Shudrak. The dense, deep forest was like walking blind, groping through darkness, and Subaru nearly lost his footing more than once, each time saved by the Shudrak around him.

"Oop, sorry. Caught me again..."

"It's fine-. I'm strong, so it's really no trouble at all-."

So said the woman with hair dyed yellow, catching Subaru as he nearly stumbled. Her manner of speech and features were soft, and she had a plump build. It was a rare type among the Shudrak, who tended to be lean and muscular, but she had a very approachable air about her.

"Is your wound doing okay-? I was the one who treated it-."

"Ah, so it was you who did that. Yeah, I'm fine. Still a little, well, actually quite a bit, I mean pretty painful, but it's better."

"Ahahaha, an honest one, aren't you-."

Saved by her easygoing, laughing manner as well. In truth, since she'd also been the one to treat his wound, he supposed he'd been saved in a double sense. Her generous, gentle atmosphere naturally put Subaru at ease. Though he was quite bothered by the fact that she kept a bone-in piece of meat in one hand the whole time.

"Hm-? Hungry-? Want some meat-?"

"Ah, no, I'm fine. Not that I'm not hungry, but if I eat I won't be able to move."

"Ahaha, that's true-. Besides, if your stomach's full, dying's more painful too-."

"Haha..."

Munching away at her bone-in meat, she was, for all her gentle appearance, still Shudrak. In any case, the Shudrak guiding Subaru and the others somewhere in this manner showed no sign of hostility toward them. Mizelda too had been the same way—it seemed that, from the moment Subaru and Abel resolved to undergo the "Lifeblood Ritual," the fallout from their earlier failed negotiation was already behind them.

In other words, regardless of the ritual's outcome, they'd already succeeded in restoring the women's opinion of them. If so, then perhaps, even should the results of the ritual prove unimpressive, they might still get another seat at the negotiating table.

"—Making that face while entertaining such convenient thoughts, I see."

"...Don't go reading my thoughts off my expression or the look in my eyes. You too—are all Vollachians like that?"

"I neither know the cause of your exasperation nor care whom you're comparing me to. But people of the Empire learn to watch others closely in order to survive. There's that difference between you and Kingdom folk."

"Watch others closely, huh..."

Walking alongside Abel under escort, Subaru found something in his words that struck a chord. It was a lesson that anyone without overwhelming military strength ought to keep in mind, not just people of the Empire. Subaru himself, without watching and observing his opponents closely, couldn't even seize what few chances he already had. It was a lesson to keep firmly, firmly in mind going forward as well.

"By the way. Don't you think of trying to escape while you still can?"

As Subaru mulled that over, Abel whispered to him as if murmuring in his ear. At the sheer coldness of the suggestion, Subaru, startled, narrowed his eyes.

"Would you cut it out with these weird temptations? Not that I haven't thought about it, but I'm not doing it."

"Oh? Why not? There's more room to run now than there was in that cage. If you create an opening well, you might slip past the Shudrak's eyes."

"Well, when my head went hot earlier I nearly did something that reckless, but..."

At Abel's mischievous remark, Subaru looked around once more. The darkness of the forest was deep; Subaru couldn't see more than a few meters ahead. What was more, even the direction and distance back to the camp he had to return to were vague—there was no future in running. On top of that, every one of the Shudrak around them was far better with their hands than Subaru, whose own were both out of commission.

"—? Something wrong-?"

"He's smitten with Ho. Ho, prettiest in the village."

"Whyaa-, you're embarrassing me-."

The woman beside him and Utakata were talking about Subaru's darting glances that way. Shaking her head reluctantly, the woman's cheeks flushed—very cute, but with no openings whatsoever. Surely, if Subaru tried to run, he'd be pinned down in an instant and that would be that. More than anything—

"If I ran off, what would happen to you?"

"—. I see. So that's the sort you are. A despicable, pointless hero complex."

"The hell'd you say?"

Cutting his gaze away, Abel spat that out. Stung by the remark, Subaru moved to snap at the profile hidden behind that mask, but before he could press him for what he actually meant, Mizelda, leading the way, came to a stop.

"Here it i-s."

"Here—there's nothing to see..."

Even with the raised torch lighting the surroundings, all that was visible was, at most, a few meters' range. To Subaru, it looked like nothing but the same unchanging forest scenery. Just what could possibly be here—

"Go and you'll fin-d out."

"Wa—ahh!?"

Straining his eyes into the darkness, Subaru leaned forward. His back was shoved by Taritta, who had circled around behind him, and after one step, two steps, his foot cut through empty air. Proof that his footing had vanished, the ground to brace against gone.

"This feeling... again!?"

Crying out despite himself, Subaru found the blank patch of ground was, to be precise, a steep slope. Planting his feet against it, he slid down, trying not to tumble head over heels. He raced down the steep slope like that and, somehow or other, caught his breath at the bottom.

"Ah, that was close... didn't even get a chance to brace myself, and suddenly—!?" "Move."

Just as he'd barely managed to stop, a powerful impact struck his back, sending him sprawling forward after all. Looking back resentfully, he found it was Abel who'd crashed into his back. It seemed Abel had been pushed down the same steep slope as Subaru.

"Doesn't look like the bottom of some pit, but... is this the site of the ritual?"

"So it would seem. Now, the question is what comes next. Elgina, was it."

"You got any idea what that is?"

"El means 'great' or 'large,' but..." Abel trailed off, "—hm."

At the bottom of the slope, mid-conversation between Subaru and Abel, who'd been driven down, something was thrown in. It landed at Abel's feet—a cloth-wrapped bundle. And peeking out from within that bundle was—

"My belongings, and your junk."

"Mine's belongings too!"

What had been tossed in were the confiscated equipment of Subaru and Abel. Inside, along with the Guiltywhip, was the knife that had been lodged in Subaru's back—the one Abel had given him—having made its way back around to this place. Abel too picked up his own sword and cloak, quickly putting them on.

"But why would they—"

"We were watching-! Do your best-!"

Following Abel's lead in recovering his gear, Subaru's question was answered by a high, piping voice. Looking up, he saw a girl on the edge of the slope waving both hands—Utakata, apparently the one who'd thrown their equipment down. Neither Mizelda nor Taritta had raised their voices at Utakata's act. Perhaps this much assistance didn't interfere with the progress of the rite. And, at the same moment—

"Well the-n, good luck-."

"Seriously..."

He saw the yellow-haired woman from earlier, in her leisurely voice, at her leisurely pace, smiling placidly, seal the entrance to the slope with a boulder. That it wasn't a trick of the eye was beyond doubt from the tremor of the earth that reached him. Unbelievable strength—it explained whose hands had made that sturdy makeshift cage.

With the entrance sealed shut like that, the place Subaru and the others had been dropped into was a ravine space roughly twenty meters wide, left to right. Opposite the sealed entrance, darkness spread before Subaru and the others, but he should probably abandon any naive notion that a straight dash through it would let them escape.

"Natsuki Subaru, how much can your hands move?"

"Huh? My hands... it's exactly as you see. My right won't lift, and my left can't grip hard either. Of course, anything delicate is out of the question too... whoa!?"

"Put it on whichever finger's less damaged! There's no time!"

So saying, Abel tossed a ring from among his own belongings toward Subaru. Catching it on reflex, Subaru, unable to refuse Abel's peremptory tone, slid the ring onto the middle finger of his left hand. It was set with a black gemstone, a ring carrying an air of luxury alongside a strange sense of pressure.

"What's this thing?"

"A ring with magic sealed inside. Kiss it before use. There's a limit, but it spits fire."

"Huh? Magic? A kiss? Just what are you—"

"—Here it comes."

Leaving Subaru behind, unable to keep pace with the rapid turn of events, Abel drew his own sword. Drawn along by his narrowed eyes, Subaru hastily took up his whip as well. And with their equipment more or less readied—

"...Come on, you've gotta be joking."

Backed against the slope with its exit sealed, Subaru, standing beside Abel, was left stunned at the sight before him. Slithering slowly along the ground, emerging from the darkness, was a mass of green scales gleaming with a wet, glistening sheen—a giant serpent. A witchbeast, the giant serpent, that they'd already encountered twice before in this Buddheim Jungle.

"Don't tell me—Elgina...?"

"——!!"

Holding his breath, Subaru's muttered words came out tentative, testing. The giant serpent opened its great maw and let out a tremendous roar, as if affirming exactly that. Bathed in the ferocious gust of wind from head to toe, Subaru's body stiffened.

Elgina equals giant serpent, and the "Lifeblood Ritual" pits you against the greatest hardship there is. If that was so, then the wall Subaru and Abel had to break through this time was—

"Now then, fight! Prove your worth as warriors! The hunting eyes of the Shudrak are watching-!"

"Damn it all! Knew it!!"

From atop the cliff, Mizelda's spirited voice rang out over Subaru and the others facing the witchbeast below, along with the high, high cheering cries of "Waah—!" from the other Shudrak. Watched over by voices that could be either cheers or jeers, the giant serpent coiled to strike—

"—Here it comes, Natsuki Subaru!"

"I can see it! Damn it, I've been getting tested nonstop lately!"

With that, drowning out Subaru's lament, the giant serpent surged forward, and the "Lifeblood Ritual" began.

△▼△▼△▼△

—The witchbeast inhabiting the Buddheim Jungle, "Elgina."

By Abel's account, "El" apparently meant "great," so the word for serpent must be riding on the "gina" portion. Or perhaps that too was simply the Shudrak's own peculiar name for it. Either way, verifying that and contributing to the annals of cultural anthropology would have to wait.

"Right now, dealing with the enemy in front of me takes priority—!"

The giant serpent lunged, jaws thrown wide, bare fangs flashing, its body more than ten meters long, as if a great tree possessed of will were rampaging through the forest. Its trunk, too, was thick as if several logs had been bundled together, and the force of its swinging tail was enough to inflict serious injury with a mere graze. As always, witchbeasts were built, physically, with the intent to kill humans.

"Bea—"

Calling out the name of the partner not present here—Bea, the beginning of a name—Subaru clenched his back teeth. In sudden emergencies, Subaru had always found it best to entrust judgment and reaction, faster than his own thinking, to his partner Beatrice's discernment and adaptability. As things stood now, that showed up instead as a shortfall in Subaru's own ability to react—

"First..." "Fool! Is this a time to stand gawking!"

The instant his face stiffened with the realization he'd botched it, a furious shout accompanied a hand seizing the back of Subaru's head, hair and all. With a shriek of "Gyep," Subaru was yanked backward and dragged down. Over the head of the fallen Subaru, the giant serpent's fangs snapped shut without mercy, a tremendous sound erupting as the air itself was bitten to death. At the same moment, a burst of smoke and wind sent the ground flying in a spectacular blast.

"Whoaaaa—!"

Pushed by that violent shockwave, Subaru was sent tumbling helplessly across the ground. But he couldn't keep tumbling forever. To pick up a narrow escape from death only to fumble it away was a fool's work. Riding the momentum, he sprang back up, trying to put distance between himself and the serpent, when—

"Don't make me say it again, fool. Keep your presence down."

"Ungh"

His head was slammed down, and Subaru was pinned to the earth. Looking over, he saw Abel, likewise coated in dust, wrapping both himself and Subaru inside his cloak. Even so, it wasn't a very large cloak. There was no denying it was too small for two, and Subaru ended up straddled by Abel.

"Wh-what's this... ah, right, 'Concealment'!"

"Correct. If we hold our breath, it can't spot us immediately, even at close range. ...Even so, what rotten luck. Had the 'Lifeblood Ritual' been anything but a test of strength, we might have had a chance."

Watching for the giant serpent's presence at such close quarters, anger and frustration mingled in Abel's eyes. From the way he said it, Subaru understood his thinking all too painfully well.

The content of the "Lifeblood Ritual" apparently differed each time it was performed. Among the possibilities, surely, were methods other than fighting a witchbeast. But what Subaru and Abel had been thrown into was the path of proving themselves as warriors by fighting the giant serpent.

"Both my hands are out of commission, and Abel's swordsmanship is second-rate... what a shit situation."

"Second-rate, you say. As things stand, you can't even lend an arm to keep from being a burden to me."

"I've still got a mouth left to talk back with... right, about that thing from before."

Amid the billowing smoke, Subaru raised his left hand, showing the ring on his middle finger. The ring Abel had tossed him, with its inadequate explanation. Something about kissing it, or fire coming out, all sorts of incomprehensible things—

"How do I use it?"

"I told you already. Kiss the jewel and let it recognize you as its owner. After that, it's a matter of the feel of using magic."

"What is this, some light-novel weapon ring...!"

Eyeing the ring half in disbelief, Subaru grimaced at the explanation. Meanwhile, ignoring Subaru's commentary, Abel kept watch on the giant serpent's movements beyond the smoke. Even he, who'd had room to spare back in the cage, couldn't hide the tension in the face of a real threat. Taking deep breath after deep breath, Abel gripped the hilt of his sword tightly and said,

"Even if we can close the distance, whether we can pierce those scales is doubtful. Unless we aim for a part without scales—the eyes or mouth, or perhaps a spot where the scales are thin—the attack won't get through."

"We'd need to create an opening for that, or it's hopeless. Somehow..."

"You create that opening. What else are the two of us here together for?"

"I was gonna say it myself, but it still pisses me off when someone tells me to be the decoy...!"

Even so, given their equipment and physical condition, there was no other way to split the roles. Subaru on support, Abel on offense—a fairly familiar arrangement by now. As ever, Natsuki Subaru's lot in life was to only ever manage the role of assist.

"At present, the witchbeast has lost sight of us. Draw its attention with the ring's flame. We'll strike in that opening."

"Yeah, got i—"

Pouring resources in first, aiming for maximum effect, was basic strategy in a fight. By chance, they could use the smokescreen the witchbeast itself had created, using the environment to bring down the enemy. Just as Subaru was thinking along those lines, he suddenly felt something off.

The source of that unease was the witchbeast "Elgina" itself. A witchbeast in the shape of a giant serpent, its sheer size comparable to an anaconda, but its threat level exceeding an anaconda's given how actively it targeted humans. And if the opponent was a serpent-type witchbeast, if its ecology resembled that of an actual snake, then—

"—!"

The instant the chill of realization struck him, Subaru unconsciously pressed his lips to the ring. Then he raised the ring overhead, aiming it toward the smoke Abel was glaring into. Before Abel could even question the act, Subaru parted his lips and—

"—Go-a!"

At that instant, flame burst against the muzzle of the giant serpent as it tore through the smoke to strike at them.

—What's called a pit organ is a snake's heat-sensing organ. Snakes, which often inhabit forests and rocky shadows and tend to be nocturnal, are equipped with pit organs to grasp the position of prey even in darkness. Through this, they can detect a prey's temperature and, even in the dark, swiftly locate and capture it. The device known as thermography applies this same principle, but a snake possesses it naturally—truly, an assassin lurking in the darkness. And, infuriatingly, this giant serpent apparently possessed a pit organ as well.

"——!!"

The giant serpent, closing in stealthily, met with an ambush the instant it struck. Shrieking at the heat that scorched its muzzle, it snapped its head upward, and Abel struck instantly. Not letting the opening slip by, Abel's sword—a thrust, his most piercing strike—drove toward the giant serpent's throat, the sharp blade gouging deep into the witchbeast's scales—or so it seemed.

"Ngh...!"

A groan escaped Abel as the impact knocked his right arm back. Having only shallowly pierced the scales at the tip, the blade was blocked from going any deeper. His stance hadn't been ideal, but there was no doubting it had been a strike with everything behind it. And still, it hadn't gone through.

"Take this!!"

Glaring at the retreating Abel, the witchbeast reared to press its follow-up attack, but a fireball collided with the side of its face. Red light burst amid a wave of heat, scorching the humid air of the jungle, but the damage to the giant serpent was negligible. The witchbeast stuck out its long tongue to lick its singed cheek, then turned its yellow eyes toward Subaru, opening its great maw to roar.

"Damn it—"

A single clash—barely thirty seconds since the fight began. But in just that short span of time, it was already clear Subaru and Abel had no chance of winning. Abel's sword couldn't break through the scales, and the crippled Subaru's tricks were a poor match for their opponent besides. Fundamentally, standing no chance against overwhelming violence was the limit of what Subaru could do.

"Go-a! Go-a! Again, go-a!!"

Toward the lunging giant serpent, Subaru swung his left hand, firing off magic wildly, again and again. Each shot released a flash of light, the ring's flames missing the witchbeast, instead crumbling the cliffs of the ravine battlefield, temporarily separating Subaru and the others from the beast.

"Hey, Mizelda-san! This thing's—"

Subaru, about to protest that it was pretty brutal, caught his breath. Up above, the Shudrak watching over their desperate struggle—every single one of them had nocked an arrow, aimed straight at Subaru and Abel.

"——"

Expressionless, with cold eyes fixed on their prey, the Shudrak stood ready. Mizelda, Taritta, that gentle yellow-haired woman, even Utakata—every last one of them, without exception, glared down at Subaru and the others with cold eyes.

"—Ah"

"This is a rite once begun. There's no escape from the 'Lifeblood Ritual.' If we fail to defeat that thing, not only will your wish go unfulfilled—you won't even manage to keep your life."

At the Shudrak's cold gaze, Subaru froze, and Abel told him so. It was the same difference in worldview about life and death that he'd felt again and again since being flung into Vollachia, from the imperial camp to this very place. —These women could laugh together with someone one moment and kill each other the next. Looking at Utakata, he understood: this was a view of life and death ingrained since childhood.

There was no point at all in questioning the rightness or wrongness of that worldview here. This was not a place to clash ideologies and argue an opponent down. What was needed was to follow their rules and win the "Lifeblood Ritual."

"The scales on its body won't give. If piercing the heart is impossible, do we aim for the brain through the eye or mouth?"

"The brain being the weak point is common across species, but... that's probably tough too. Then the win condition we're aiming for is a bit above that."

"—Above?"

The idea of inflicting a fatal wound to wrest out victory was proving difficult. So there was no choice but to seize victory some other way besides a fatal blow. If this had been the witchbeast's nest, some underhanded tactic like taking the giant serpent's eggs hostage to force surrender might have worked, but that wasn't the case here. Then there was nothing for it but to target a weak point common to witchbeasts.

"Break its horn, and a witchbeast submits to whoever broke it. —That's the only way."

"And the plan?"

"Same as before. I'm the decoy, the attacker's the shady masked man."

"Shady? The only one here is a noble masked man."

Exchanging words with the ever-glib Abel, Subaru drew a deep breath and let it out. The win condition shared, what needed doing was settled.

With the frozen gazes of the Shudrak watching from behind and above, Subaru and the others, to prove their worth as warriors, challenged the giant serpent that claimed the forest as its own. Proof as a warrior—it didn't suit him, and he had no desire for it, but—

"If I can't get to your side without it, then I'll get it."

—Thinking of Rem, left behind at the imperial soldiers' camp, Subaru stepped forward with force.

"——!!"

Tearing through the smoke, the giant serpent, jaws wide, came charging in. Straight ahead, Subaru thrust out his left hand toward that serpent. Seeing it, the giant serpent's yellow eyes took on wariness, closing its mouth and shifting its head aside.

The lowest-grade flame hadn't done much damage, but it seemed to have drawn out enough of the giant serpent's aversion to make it reluctant to be struck again. That wariness proved its undoing. Right now, there was no ring on Subaru's left hand.

"My left hand isn't aiming for your face—it's aiming above it!"

So saying, what was thrown from Subaru's left hand was the tip of his whip. His master Clind had drilled the technique into him so he could handle it nearly the same with either hand. He worked his left hand—which could still move passably with two fingers—hard, more than his right, which couldn't lift above shoulder height. The tip of the whip wasn't aiming for the serpent's scales, of course, but for a thick branch of the dense jungle canopy above the serpent's head. Wrapping the whip around it, Subaru's body shot upward with force.

"—!"

Chasing after Subaru as he leapt vigorously upward, the serpent stretched its body, its jaws closing in. Had he not folded his knees on reflex, Subaru's body would surely have been caught in those closing fangs, both legs torn off from the thighs down.

"—! Sister! That man's running away!!"

"No—"

At the sight of Subaru rising sharply and circling above the ravine that had become their battlefield, Taritta, nocking her bow, let out a cry. But Mizelda pushed down the bow aimed at Subaru with her hand. And Mizelda, her green eyes shining, said,

"He's not running—he means to fight!!"

In Mizelda's field of vision, cheering as if in acclaim, Subaru spun round and round through the air on his whip. Tracing a circling trajectory much like some amusement park ride spinning through the sky, Subaru took aim at the edge of the cliff with the ring on his right hand.

"Goooaaaaahhh—!!"

It was less an incantation than a battle cry. And as Subaru raised his voice like that, flames poured from his right hand, catching on the vines and branches stretching toward the ravine at the cliff's edge, becoming a tremendous inferno that scorched the ravine.

"Eeeeek—!?" "Wahhh! Utakata, that's dangerous-!" "Aaah, Sister! Sister! Is this really all right!?"

Seeing the ravine burst into flame, the Shudrak watching the battle began to stir. Utakata and the yellow-haired girl clung to each other, and Taritta begged her sister for permission to shoot Subaru down. But Mizelda, eyes shining, hadn't even noticed the plea. She simply gripped her fist tight, her eyes riveted to the scene.

"Good, good, this is thrilling!"

"Aaaaaahhhhh—!!"

Mizelda's cheer overlapped with Subaru's last cry before his breath gave out. As the light of the ring, scattering flame, faded, in response to that final, dying roar of a howl, a fireball launched and crumbled part of the cliff, and the giant serpent drew back to avoid the falling rocks. But—

"——"

Retreating step by step, the giant serpent understood there was no escape route left around it. Already the fallen trees in the ravine had caught fire too, the blazing radiance no longer even needing the light of the torches. More than anything, flames scattered this lavishly meant—

"Likely some method of sensing heat. —And now, that too is useless."

Hidden within the cloak of "Concealment," Abel, lunging in, vanished from the giant serpent's sight.

"——!!"

Sensing danger, the giant serpent's eyes flared fiercely. But above, there was Subaru's presence trailing miasma, his heat signature dead amid the flames, and Abel's form rendered invisible. All the giant serpent could do was dive from where it stood into the path with the least fire. And that was the false escape route Subaru had crafted by scattering flame—

"Haaaaaahhhhh—!!"

At that instant, as the giant serpent dove into the escape route, Abel, leaping down from the cliff, struck at its head. The upraised sword traced an arc, a flash of silver streaking toward the twisted horn on the serpent's head. It bit in diagonally toward the horn's center, aiming to cleave clean through in one stroke—

"——!!"

It was the very brink of the horn being severed, the witchbeast losing its will, the decisive blow. Howling, the giant serpent gave one last desperate twist of its head in a final struggle to escape the strike. But a desperate struggle was just that. Such a futile effort shouldn't have worked. —Not against the blow of a true warrior.

"Gah—"

The head's twist knocked the blade's arc off course, and Abel's strike halted partway through the horn. Before he could pour more strength into his arm and resume the stroke, the sweeping tail caught him. Battered by the impact, Abel's slender body was sent flying sideways. Unable even to break his fall, his body tumbled through the fire-wreathed ravine, blood spilling from a coughing throat.

"Kohh... damn it, I slipped up... Can't manage it the way that fool did..."

Sprawled on the ground, coughing blood, Abel turned to face the giant serpent as it turned toward him. A once-in-a-lifetime, decisive chance for reversal gleaming murderously in its eyes, the giant serpent slid toward Abel. Struck by the blow, Abel couldn't stand, nor did he have the leeway to wrap himself in "Concealment."

Its great maw opened, a serpent's characteristic swallowing motion bearing down on Abel. Witnessing it firsthand, there was no longer time to think.

"I'm—Returned by Death—"

That he'd blurted this out was something that hadn't happened in quite a long while. But to Subaru, who had relived his own footsteps within the "Book of the Dead" inside the Pleiades Watchtower, the experience of trying to draw a witchbeast in that way was as vivid as if it had happened just the other day. Precisely because of that, it could be said that this hand had occurred to him in that instant, reflexively.

"Gh, gah—"

As color drained from the world, sound vanished, and even the flow of air ceased to be felt, in its place, coming toward Subaru, was a black shadow spilling out into the frozen world. It was the same substance as that vast quantity of black shadow that had surged toward him after he'd dealt with the "Trial," lost Shaula, and stood there crushed—

『—I love you.』

"...Ah, I've heard that till my ears are calloused."

To that short line, Subaru responded in kind. Immediately after, a hand slipping in seized his heart, and searing agony ground through his entire body from the fingertips inward—his vision wasn't so much dyed red as it felt as though his eyeballs had burst, an impact that shattered Subaru. Pain he could never grow used to, and an obsession, a despair, that gave no hint of ever ending. But eventually, as it grew distant—

"Look at me—!!"

"——!!"

The instant color, sound, smell, and sensation returned to the world, Subaru shouted that out with full force. Sensing the returning presence, swelling with miasma, the giant serpent turned—not toward the frail masked man in front of it, whom it could kill anytime, but toward the vigorous, reeking Subaru above.

"I'm counting on you—!"

The instant his eyes met the turning giant serpent's, Subaru had already pressed his lips to the ring on his right hand. From there, he let go of the whip all at once, his body arcing through the air toward the giant serpent. —To make it reach that head, it had to turn to face him. The confession of "Returned by Death" was for exactly that purpose. Partly, too, so as not to let Abel die. And then—

"Aaaaaaahhhhh—!!"

Planting a foot on the giant serpent's upper jaw, Subaru flew forward in an ungainly, pitching lunge. Before him was the white horn, blade buried halfway through, one more strike from being severed—the sword's pommel. Spinning his body toward it, Subaru drove his right fist into it with everything he had.

Of course, he didn't think an ordinary punch from him alone could sever this thick witchbeast's horn. But it wasn't an ordinary punch. —It was one blow, ring, magic stone and all, laced with mana.

"——"

The ring's jewel, colliding with the pommel, cracked, red light leaking out. In an instant, that leaking red light swelled, and centered on Subaru's right arm and the horn on the giant serpent's head, it detonated—and all sight and sound were blown away.

"Hah"

Spinning round and round, Subaru fell to the ground, tumbling over once, twice, three times. He'd struck his whole body violently, but he couldn't even tell the extent of the damage it had caused. Only that his right side burned as if aflame, and he couldn't even see what state it was in.

"——"

His body, on its back, convulsed, yellow bile spilling from the corner of his mouth, and the collapsed Subaru felt a tremor through the earth. That it was the shock of the giant serpent collapsing, its head dropping right beside him, was something the dying Subaru, in the midst of his own crisis, couldn't register. But—

"—Natsuki Subaru! Hey, Natsuki Subaru! Get up! Get up now!"

To Subaru, whose consciousness now hung by a single, nearly severed thread, someone rushed roughly over and called out, shaking his body. He couldn't tell where he was being grabbed and shaken, but shaken he was, regardless. The searing heat surged back, and, spilling voiceless cries, Subaru let fluid drip from every opening in his face.

He couldn't think anymore. He wanted to let go of consciousness, right now. Pain, heat, thirst, and suffering, and every bad word that existed in this world seemed to be swirling in his head—

"Stand, and say what needs saying! The woman—what do you want done about the woman named Rem!"

"—Ah"

"Speak it with your own mouth! I cannot speak your wish for you!"

The forceful, heated demand was jammed into his ears, and on top of that, his body was hauled upright. He couldn't even tell which way was up, head or feet, in this state, yet he was hauled up regardless. His body wouldn't lift. Probably, only his upper half had somehow been raised.

"Listen, People of Shudrak! You've seen it with your own eyes! We've accomplished the 'Lifeblood Ritual,' and proven our worth as warriors! Then there is something you, as kin, must do!"

"—Ah, Mizelda, chieftain of the Shudrak, has witnessed i-t! O warrior, our kin! What do you wish? Cry out what must be done!"

Voices, right beside him and from above, rang and reverberated inside his head. As if his brain's defenses had vanished, the voices passed through him unblocked. Though he barely understood their meaning, he was shaken by the shoulder, shaken by the head, his very soul shaken.

"Answer, Natsuki Subaru. Speak your wish. Wring out everything you have."

"—Ah"

"Behind those closed eyelids, paint what you desire. Nothing is given to one who cannot voice their own wish. —There's no feed to give a lazy pig!"

Behind his closed eyelids, paint what you desire.

He could see a silver-haired girl. A young girl with cream-colored hair, a girl with pink hair, a gray-haired young man, a blond boy, and many, many other faces, people's faces, all visible.

—And he was glad, so glad, that the blue-haired girl was among them.

"Rem... please..."

"What!!"

"S-save her..."

Tasting a sensation as though he himself were peeling away, flaking off bit by bit, Subaru's lips wove those words. The moment he did, the hand gripping his shoulder—probably his shoulder—tightened its hold. And the voice's owner nodded quietly with an "Ah," and said,

"Did you hear, People of Shudrak. This is our new kinsman's wish. This, he should have proven with his own life. His wish, what he has seen—then!"

"Say no more. —We have both pride and courage."

"——"

Limp, the strength drained from his body, consciousness drifting away. The voice that had forcibly tried to anchor him made no further attempt to do so this time. Slowly, slowly, slowly it grew distant—

"—You have fulfilled your duty. Leave the woman to us."

At the very last, the very end, he thought he heard only a voice, incomprehensible yet somehow reassuring. Or so it felt. He thought he did.
