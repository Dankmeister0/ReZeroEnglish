Arc 3 Chapter 6: "A Precarious Pair"

Relying on memory to guide him, Subaru arrived at the Loot House—a decrepit ruin situated deep in the innermost part of the royal capital's slums.

Whether some miscalculation had occurred during the capital's construction or not, the building looked like the sort of thing you'd plop down just to hide an oddly leftover chunk of space—a massive structure with a perspective all wrong. It ran on a system where the light-fingered folk living in the slums gathered goods obtained by every means, legal and illegal alike, from all over the capital, and a ringleader would fence it all off. This place, a hotbed of corruption exactly as its name suggested—

"Wiped clean. Nothing left at all, huh."

Subaru offered up that observation as he surveyed the completely leveled remains of what had once been the Loot House.

The building of the Loot House had collapsed without a trace, and the debris and any hazardous materials had already been cleared away, leaving nothing behind. Traces of the foundation from when the storehouse was built still remained in the ground, but if you consciously ignored that, the erasure was thorough enough that it wouldn't be strange to claim the Loot House had never existed at all. Remembering all over again that this state of affairs had been brought about by nothing more than a single sword stroke—

"Yeah, Reinhard's completely stopped being human…"

Picturing that refreshing, handsome redhead, Subaru praised his inhuman prowess without reservation. Though whether the man in question would be pleased by praise with a heavier tint of negativity than positivity was another matter entirely.

"It's obvious, but there's no sense of anyone around either. Subaru, what should we do?"

Subaru had simply been overwhelmed by the scene before him—or rather, unable to shake the impression of how tidy it all was. Unlike him, Emilia had been scanning the area with an eye more suited to their purpose, and now she posed that question.

"I still think contacting Reinhard is the best bet, but…"

"Yeah, that's about the size of it. I figured if we came here we might learn what happened to Felt and old man Rom afterward, but I guess it's not that easy."

It wasn't quite "the culprit returns to the scene of the crime," but it was only human to hope that some kind of epilogue might play out on the stage after the event had ended. Reality, though, didn't work like fiction, and everything had been thoroughly carted off from this place, opportunistic looters included. Literally the only thing left here was residue—and such faint residue at that, it seemed liable to scatter on the wind just from Subaru and Emilia's visit.

"In other words, there's nothing left for us here. Goodbye, Loot House. You were a pretty effective event-trigger point, this place."

Giving a thumbs-up in farewell to the memories, Subaru scooped up the bag of Appa at his feet, took Emilia's hand, and moved away from the spot. Briskly—he wasn't the type to live his life forever tugged back by nostalgia. Goodbye yesterday, hello tomorrow. Welcome, newlyweds.

"Anyway, the slums are dangerous too, so let's get out of here quick. As for what comes next… guess finding Reinhard really is the surest bet. Felt went off with Reinhard, right?"

"Yes, that's right. He said he wouldn't do anything bad to her, but… his expression suddenly changed."

From what Emilia said, it sounded like he'd overturned a matter that had been settled by looking the other way. Given that he was a young man who seemed the type to guard even a verbal promise with his life, the impression that he'd instantly retracted his own words felt strangely out of character. Incidentally, injured old man Rom's fate had also apparently been left in Reinhard's hands.

"I wish we could've gotten a bit more out of him, but we had our own reason to hurry back to the mansion."

"Meaning?"

"…You remember, there was that child whose stomach had been torn open? I couldn't think of anyone but Beatrice who could properly heal that, so we were in a rush."

Ah. Nothing more than his own story, really. Having flung out his apology at the top of his lungs, Subaru switched gears with a "well then—"

"Anyway, seems like the conversation's looped right back around to hunting down the handsome guy. From what little we talked about before, asking at the guard post or wherever seemed like the way to go, but…"

Digging up the conversation from their first meeting as best he could, Subaru offered up what might pass for a lead. Though even as he said it, he didn't find it especially convincing. After all, if Reinhard were stationed at a guard post, that would put him solidly in the position of the town's neighborhood cop—

"No way a beat cop like that just exists lying around. Nine times out of ten, that bastard's some proper knight whose hobby just happens to be wandering the town on his days off."

A sword with obviously fine craftsmanship, and the grandiose epithet "Sword Saint." Add to that combat ability like his and call him an ordinary soldier, and you'd never be able to walk the capital's streets again without fear. Emilia gave a small nod, seeming to agree with Subaru's assessment.

"I think it's unlikely Reinhard's at the guard post too. But even if he's not there himself, there should be someone who could put us in touch with him, don't you think?"

"Well, categorization aside, they're both soldiers, that much's a fact, and the guard post's probably got the castle's phone number on file or something… wait, there's no such thing as phones here, is there?"

"Phones?"

Emilia tilted her head, finger pressed to her cheek. Rating that gesture "adorable!" internally, Subaru racked his brain over how to explain.

"Simply put, it's… like, a way to talk with someone in a faraway place. Ah, not some primitive method like shouting really loud."

"If you mean a dialogue mirror, I think they have those…"

At Emilia's somewhat uncertain words, it was Subaru's turn to tilt his head. Seeing his display of ignorance, Emilia gave a small "hmm" before continuing.

"It's a Magic Vessel for long-distance conversation—a pair of mirrors where you can talk with whoever's reflected in the paired mirror. Compared to most Magic Vessels there are quite a lot of them, and you can find them in all sorts of places."

"Huh, so even though there's just the one connecting line, they've got a proper way to do it. Mirrors, huh, mirrors… sounds pretty magical. Fantastic!"

In that case, would what showed in the mirror not be yourself, but the person you were talking to? Come to think of it, though Subaru had heard the term Magic Vessel before, the only one he actually knew of was the fake one he'd used to trick old man Rom—a mock cell phone. Hearing that such a thing genuinely existed, he couldn't help the boyish excitement bubbling up.

"So even without any talent for magic, maybe there's a talent for fighting using Magic Vessels waiting for me. This time for real, my winning streak begins—honestly, though, I don't really want to run anymore."

He was well aware of his own lack of ability, and if he was honest, what he really wanted was to avoid situations that demanded ability in the first place. Before being summoned to another world, he'd idly fantasized about cutting a dashing, heroic figure of himself, but now that he actually faced reality, he understood all too well how reckless that fantasy had been. As a practical matter, faced with the harsh reality, what had Subaru actually been capable of besides clawing to survive, unsightly and past caring about appearances?

Drifting along that current toward negativity, Subaru shook his head to cast the thought away. Why had his mind gone running off in that direction—it was because, without him noticing, silence had fallen between the two of them.

"Emilia-tan?"

"…Eh? What's wrong?"

She reacted a few seconds late to his call, blinking her violet eyes at him. At her ambiguous response, Subaru furrowed his brow.

"That's my line for you, Emilia-tan. You suddenly went quiet, it startled me. Was something bothering you?"

"—No, it's nothing. So, shall we head to the guard post?"

Her attitude hadn't seemed like "nothing" at all, but the way she said it refused any further probing, and pressing the matter felt out of reach. So all Subaru could manage, cowed by her follow-up question, was an ambiguous "Ah, uh, yeah," that could've meant either yes or no.

"The guard post is over in the noble district, so it's a bit of a walk from here. If we're gone too long Rem will get sulky, so let's hurry a little."

As an aside, Rem had originally offered to accompany Subaru on his tour of the capital, but her duties as caretaker had ended up spanning so many tasks that she'd tearfully ceded the job of minding Subaru this time to Emilia. Right about now she was probably showing tremendous, heroic effort somewhere—securing tonight's lodging and handling everything else.

Led by the hand, entering the road out of the slums, Subaru silently watched Emilia's back. Draped in a white robe embroidered with a hawk crest, her solemn attire rejected words, giving Subaru the illusion that his mouth had literally been sealed shut.

Being at Emilia's side, feeling his heart lift and flutter—that had become an indispensable part of Subaru's daily life these past weeks. So there was nothing more frightening to him than being rejected by her attitude like this. What—which one—which of his remarks had soured her mood; his heart filled with impatience, Subaru desperately searched for the answer.

If Emilia rejected him, Subaru would be left completely isolated in this world with nowhere to belong. Emilia alone was, in this world, the very meaning of Subaru's existence.

That "self-definition" began working in a bad direction, and static started running through Subaru's thoughts. His brain seemed to scorch, his vision flickered. A murky black emotion began to spread through his chest, and some unconscious part of him understood it bore the exact same shape as a black palm he'd seen once before—and just as that surging negative emotion carried him along, Subaru's lips began to tremble—

"—That's about enough, don't you think, Subaru?"

A voice suddenly rang in his head, snapping him back to his senses, and Subaru's eyes widened in shock. There, before him, resting his weight against the collar of Emilia's hood while stroking his own belly and gazing back at him, was Puck—the one who'd spoken words that seemed to whisper directly into his brain.

I saw a sign that wasn't looking too good, so I figured I'd throw in a little cold water. I'd rather you didn't do anything untoward to my daughter. —I don't want to have to dislike you.

Puck spoke in his usual mild tone, twirling his whiskers. At the content of his words Subaru's face stiffened in alarm, and he glanced fearfully at Emilia's back walking ahead of him. But she seemed not to notice Subaru's fright at all, continuing to walk on.

No need to worry about Lia. This is a message I'm sending just to you, directly.

…You mean even my inner voice gets through with this?

Telepathy only really works when there's a considerable difference in power. Well, just think of it as the gap between you and me.

Puck could be seen laughing soundlessly, a rippling sort of chuckle. Subaru was no longer surprised by the little cat's ability to see the color of his emotions, but he doubted he'd ever get used to the unpleasantness of this thing called telepathy, resonating straight in his brain. Still—

Ah, well—thanks, I guess. I was acting a little off just now.

He'd had the sensation of being on the verge of being left utterly alone in the world, desperately struggling against just that one thing. Reaching a hand toward the one back he could cling to, and what he'd meant to do with it—shaking his head, Subaru, having lost track of the pressure he'd felt moments ago, no longer knew. Only, he had the sense that he'd been running on some strange train of thought, as if he weren't quite himself.

To put it plainly, Lia's bad mood isn't your fault, Subaru. She just got a little caught up remembering something and fell into self-loathing.

Puck's words pulled Subaru back from the distrust he'd been feeling toward his own state of mind. At his words, Subaru tilted his head.

Self-loathing?

Just something that triggered an unpleasant memory. That's all that's got her sulking. Missing that part is one of her cute points, though.

Leaving the crucial part unspoken, Puck wore a smile brimming with fatherly affection. Feeling a sense of exclusion at that attitude, as if left out of something, Subaru suddenly realized—

—Thinking back on it, he knew nothing at all about Emilia.

The Emilia Subaru knew was a beautiful girl striking for her silver hair and violet eyes. A Half-Elf, and by standing, a candidate for the position of Queen of this Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica. Currently living at Roswaal Manor under her patron, accompanied by the Spirit Puck, whom she was close enough to call family. Honest with herself, prone to putting on a brave front, kindhearted to a fault, willing to take a loss for others' sake, prone to acting like a big sister despite having quite a few blind spots herself, and seemingly easy to deceive on top of it all.

Over the two weeks since meeting her, that was the sum total of the Emilia Subaru had seen. Only now did Subaru realize that this was entirely surface-level information about her, and that he had never once stepped into her inner self, her circumstances.

Come to think of it, Subaru didn't even know the circumstances behind Emilia's participation in the Royal Selection. Nor why she'd come to Roswaal Manor, nor even the reason she'd been in the royal capital the day he met her.

As though it were only natural, he'd been absorbed only in receiving what she gave him, never even seeking to know anything beyond that. And this was the result. Faced with an Emilia who was rejecting him over circumstances only Puck knew, Subaru, knowing nothing, could do nothing but fall silent.

Bottling up your true feelings like that, one way or another—seems like you're a difficult case yourself, in your own way.

Even having realized his own shallowness and fallen silent, that didn't mean his inner heart could be silently occupied all the way through. At Puck skimming the surface layer of his heart, Subaru shrugged.

Coming from the guy who's been told he's second to none in bluntness, that's a bit off the mark, don't you think? Seems the great Spirit-sama still needs a bit more training, meow?

Adding an exaggerated gesture to make light of it, Subaru showed through his attitude that he wanted to cut the conversation short there. But—

Hey, Subaru.

Not picking up on Subaru's intent at all, Puck's talk wasn't over. Words whispered not to the eardrum but to the heart admitted no rejection, and Subaru declared his silence as his own intent and waited to see what came next. His attitude was clearly unwelcoming. But Puck, not the least bit fazed by his reaction, went on to declare, one-sidedly—

—Please don't get my hopes up too much. And don't get Lia's hopes up either.

…Huh?

Hope is a gentle poison. Even knowing it'll eat away at the body eventually, if you mistake it for something within reach, you can't help but reach for it. You, Subaru—are exactly that. Poison.

Narrowing his black eyes slightly, Puck looked at Subaru, his expression blank. Pinned by those emotionless eyes, Subaru couldn't help but stiffen. Under that gaze, as if being appraised, he gritted his teeth against the sensation—

What's that supposed to—

But before an answer to Puck's incomprehensible words could reach him—

"We're here."

Emilia's feet, the hand pulling his along, came to a stop first.

"Whoa—"

Stumbling forward, he barely caught himself before colliding with Emilia's back and looked up. Ahead of him, having been so absorbed in his conversation with Puck that he'd lost track of it, the transformed cityscape finally came into view. Buildings that had been a chaotic jumble, a scene that deserved to be called a slum, had flipped entirely into a street lined with neat, orderly buildings.

The noble district—if the name meant what it implied, this was the land where the upper classes would reside. As a matter of course, the scenery here was more refined than the district where commoners lived; put simply, the money spent was on an entirely different scale. The buildings, of course, but also the roads, the walls, even the trees planted to maintain the aesthetic.

Right at the point where one street over became an entirely different world, as if to seal off the entrance to that other world, an enormous building stood. Even amid the transformed scenery, it was a building of particular height. Where the world he came from, like this one, was dominated mostly by buildings of two to three stories, this facility alone before him maintained a height of roughly six stories. Its rear adjoined part of the outer wall, and the terrace set atop it would surely command a view of the entire city. But the blunt, unadorned atmosphere of the building made that terrace feel less like a place to enjoy the scenery and more like a place built for looking down on the people below.

Even without knowing the building's purpose, it carried an intimidating presence that made you want to straighten your posture unconsciously. Subaru recalled that small-citizen instinct of shrinking before a policeman even when you hadn't done anything wrong, and made a sour face.

"This is the guard post that patrols the royal capital. It's also where they check the identities of people coming in and out of the noble district, that sort of thing."

"So that's why it's built way out here. …Still, I guess no matter the era, no matter the world, there's no resisting the strange power that police organizations carry."

Even without knowing that, this oppressive weight. It was certainly not the kind of atmosphere where you could casually knock and ask about a lost item or a missing child. A town police box would be far more approachable than this.

"Anyway, let's get inside and talk about Reinhard… why are you shrinking down like that?"

"There're some sleazy cops out there in the world who'd get off on singling out a guy like me for questioning, you know. So it's a conditioned reflex."

"I don't know what you're saying, but does that mean you can't get away with doing bad things?"

"As expected, you see right to the heart of it."

Emilia, having correctly grasped the essence of Subaru's remark, let out a sigh. Then she turned forward and walked toward the door, intending to knock first at the guard post's gate. Following her hooded back with his eyes, Subaru suddenly noticed the little cat's form was nowhere to be seen. Vanished at some point without him noticing—was he burrowed back into her silver hair again now?

Puck, having said his piece and promptly vanished. Frowning at his suggestive manner, Subaru tilted his head, wondering what exactly he'd meant.

Still, it wasn't the sort of problem he'd find an answer to just by worrying over it. Abandoning his folded-arm contemplation early, Subaru took a step forward to fall in beside Emilia. It was almost the same moment Emilia moved to knock on the door, but—

"—Oh my, what a rare place to run into you."

The guard post's door swung open from the inside, and a young man's face appeared before either of them could act. The young man bowed courteously to Emilia.

"It's been a while, Emilia-sama. Have you been well since then?"

—and, addressing her while she still wore her hood, called her definitively "Emilia."

That alone sent a jolt of wariness through Subaru's chest, but Emilia, receiving his bow, shifted slightly yet remained composed.

"…Yes, thank you. Nothing in particular has changed. Um, Julius."

"I'm honored you remembered me. And I'm glad to see Emilia-sama's beauty remains just as unchanged."

The young man called Julius flashed his teeth in a smile and delivered that cheesy line without a trace of self-consciousness.

He was a tall young man. He looked about ten centimeters taller than Subaru, so somewhere around 180-odd centimeters. His hair was a bluish purple, worn on the longer side and styled with an almost dandyish care. His build was slender but carried no impression of frailty—one might better call it lithe. He was the sort of handsome man for whom such glib remarks suited perfectly, and Subaru couldn't help feeling a stab of jealousy at those amber eyes so captivating to the opposite sex. And as for his attire—evident from the fact that he'd come out of the guard post—

"Isn't it unusual for one of the Royal Guard to be at the guard post?"

A uniform adorned with dazzling ornamentation. Sleeves through a uniform bearing the motif of a Dragon, a rapier-style sword at his hip. —More than anything, his very bearing spoke eloquently of exactly what this man, Julius, did for a living.

"A bit of both—offering the soldiers here my regards, and taking the chance to inspect the town… A friend asked me to come by, but perhaps it's not so bad to prioritize friendship every once in a while."

Raising a hand, Julius answered Emilia as though singing. He spun his words as if intoxicated by his own manner of speaking, gazing meaningfully at Emilia before him, and continued with an "after all"—

"To have stretched my legs out into the town like this and been rewarded, ahead of schedule, with the sight of so lovely a flower—to wish for more than this would surely invite divine punishment."

As he spoke, Julius took Emilia's hand with a practiced gesture and knelt right there. Then, without pausing for breath, he gently pressed his lips to the back of her pale white hand.

Subaru watched the whole sequence unfold, stunned, powerless to do anything about it. A beat late, his emotions came to a boil, and he took a step forward, ready to condemn this act—both smug and thoroughly grating on his nerves. But—

"Thank you, Julius. And I'm sorry to spring this on you out of nowhere, but…"

Emilia's hand, thrust out behind her, stopped Subaru just as he was about to charge at Julius, nostrils flaring. Subaru, unable to move, failing to grasp her intent, watched as the situation unfolded slowly before him.

"I have a bit of business, and I was hoping you could put in a word to have me connected to the castle…"

"Ah, I see. So it's the guard post's dialogue mirror you need. Understood, allow me to show you the way."

Rising and opening the door, Julius glanced into the facility.

"I do feel some hesitation about bringing Emilia-sama to such an unrefined place, but…"

"You don't need to worry about that. Please."

"Then, this way…"

At Emilia's words, Julius nodded and turned to lead the way back into the guard post. Seeing Emilia follow at his back, Subaru hurried to chase after them as well, but—

"Subaru, wait here."

"Huh?"

Turning back before the door had even fully closed, Emilia cut him off with those words. Subaru, momentarily unable to find his voice, protested his displeasure at Emilia's current attitude through gestures alone. But Emilia slowly shook her head at this.

"I really do want you to come with me, but I don't think Julius would be happy about it if you were there, so please wait."

"What's that supposed to mean? So keeping him happy matters more than me?"

For a moment, taking it as though she were prioritizing Julius over him, Subaru pouted. At that, Emilia's face fell into a troubled expression as she said, "That's not it."

"It's not about upsetting Julius—it's that I know you'd end up feeling bad, and I don't want that. Please, understand."

"I already felt plenty bad just now, if you want to know."

The kiss to the back of the hand—even as an act of respect and admiration, it was unforgivable to Subaru. He and Emilia had even been on something like a date, walked hand in hand through the capital together, but he still had no real sense that she thought of him as a member of the opposite sex. Which meant that a kiss was one of the steep hurdles Subaru himself would eventually have to clear—and it was simply impossible not to feel fierce hostility toward a man who, in a different context perhaps, had cleared it so effortlessly.

If at all possible, he didn't want this man having any more contact with Emilia. More than that, sending her off to that man's side without Subaru there was absolutely out of the question. —That was Subaru's manly resolve, but—

"I won't let it drag on, I'll settle things and come right back, so be a good boy and wait. You can't disappear from in front of the guard post, okay?"

Her tone was gentle, but it carried, once again, a heavy tinge of rejection. Faced with her attitude, thoroughly determined to keep Subaru away from her affairs, Subaru—once again afraid to press further—could say nothing at all.

Then the door closed, cutting off the space between them, and Subaru pressed a palm against the heavy gate, hung his head, and let out one long, long sigh.

"God, I'm pathetic… What am I even doing. Well, I'm not doing anything, is the problem."

Nothing but self-loathing came out of his mouth. Charging in with jealousy written all over his face, only to fail to even fully vent that jealousy—what was that about. Being able to recognize his own spinelessness for himself was enough to make him depressed if he dwelt on it.

"That said, moping in front of the guard post forever and getting questioned for it wouldn't feel great either."

Recognizing himself, from an outsider's view, as looking thoroughly suspicious, Subaru moved briskly away from the front of the guard post. Still, per Emilia's instructions, he could only go as far as the opposite side of the street where the guard post remained visible. Putting a reasonable distance between them, he leaned his back against a wall and sighed again.

"That smug bastard… said he was Royal Guard or something, didn't he."

If Subaru's understanding was correct, that meant he was part of the so-called Royal Guard's inner circle. If there existed something called a knights' order, the Royal Guard would presumably be something special within it—originally meaning direct attendants to the royal family—but what was their standing now, in a kingdom currently without a monarch?

"Had that whole elite vibe going on, that smug bastard. Casually slobbering all over Emilia-tan's cute hand like that…"

With the correction of bad memory bias, the act Julius had committed was steadily being elevated, in Subaru's mind, into something monstrously villainous and perverse. Bottling up dark emotions like this, all he could do was wait, and it was maddening. Undeterred by Julius's perverted behavior, all he could do was pray that Emilia would accomplish her original goal and come back as soon as possible.

"And then I'll gently welcome Emilia-tan back after she's been hurt by that man's hands. 'Subaru, I… I've been defiled…' 'That's not true at all, Emilia-tan… no, Emilia. No matter what's done to you, in my eyes you'll always be, more than anyone, more than anything, beautiful and noble. Those eyes of yours are the most precious jewel in this world…'"

His delusional story exploded outward, and imaginary-Emilia, eyes brimming with tears, gently closed them. Just as he was about to gently seal those lips from above, Subaru suddenly noticed something crossing the street.

"N-no way…"

His lips trembling, the mental theater that had been playing out until just now came crashing down. As dream-Emilia, blushing adorably, dissolved away in his fantasy, Subaru found himself unable to look away from the overwhelmingly real sight unfolding before him.

—Before Subaru's astonished eyes, crossing that spot, was one adorable Beastfolk.

Its small body, standing no taller than Subaru's waist, swayed valiantly as it toddled along, its entire form covered in an unbelievably fluffy white coat. A perfect Beastfolk form, incomparable to any mere Demi-Human with animal ears. A dog, probably—yes, a dog, he thought. Its fur, pure white as freshly fallen snow, grew long, lending its shape a charming, rounded cuteness.

This, in an instant, was what it meant to have one's heart stolen. Subaru, unable to hide his heartbreak at Emilia's attitude, fell utterly before the strongest fluff-child crossing his path. In other words: total loss of will to fight before the battle had even begun.

His soul as a Fluff-ist, the accumulated frustrations of his time in another world, the self-loathing that had just erupted mere minutes ago, the current of unrequited love toward Emilia—all of it combined as factors, and Subaru's free will was completely sealed.

He wanted to bury his fingers in it. He wanted to tangle his hands in that white fur. He wanted to hug it as much as he pleased, nuzzle his cheek against it, lift it up and spin around, and sing love, every kind of love, to the whole world. To fluff is love, fluffing is the cornerstone of peace, fluff alone will save the world. You don't need to fluff to be able to fluff. But if you're going to fluff, you want to be fluffed back. Fluffing, and being fluffed in turn—that is the true purpose of a Fluff-ist, and to be able to fluff yet not fluff is, for a Fluff-ist, tantamount to choosing death. In other words, to fluff equals life for a Fluff-ist, and without fluffing, the light of a Fluff-ist's life would—

"—and while I was doing all that, where the hell am I…"

Having completely lost sight of the Beastfolk, Subaru snapped back to himself, standing frozen with his head in his hands.

Around him spread an unfamiliar, narrow alley. The street in front of the guard post where he'd been just moments ago—at the very least, this was clearly far removed from where that gorgeous noble district had been. This was the result of neglecting everything else in his single-minded pursuit of fluff.

"Not even able to just wait around—am I lower than a dog!? Though I guess getting so worked up over a doglike fluff-child that I lose track of everything pretty much confirms that already!"

Distracted by the fluff-child's appearance, dazed, he'd chased after its form, and here he was. How much time had passed was unclear, but if he didn't hurry back, all that awaited him was Emilia's scolding. By now, surely, Emilia was spending her time at the guard post anxiously, wanting nothing more than to get back to Subaru's side as soon as possible. He hoped, at least, that that was what she was thinking. And if she, having accomplished her purpose, came back to find that he'd vanished without even managing the simple task of waiting quietly— Subaru shuddered, picturing the future in which she looked at him with cold, disdainful eyes.

"She'd give up on me for good after that, wouldn't she. No time for playing cute and sticking my tongue out, this is serious!"

Scratching his head, he recognized he was in the middle of an emergency among emergencies. Subaru dashed through the alley, thinking he simply had to get out to the main street. But without any sense of the area, and with the alleys all winding on in similar fashion, his body refused, contrary to his urgent wishes, to let him escape so easily.

"Dammit, of all times. Wait, I got it! There was that trivia I picked up from beans—if you keep touching the wall the whole way through a maze, you're guaranteed to reach the exit! This is a gift! Time to touch the wall, and—GYAAAH!!"

Triumphantly pressing his palm to the wall, Subaru's hand met the grimy surface, which let out a sticky, squelching sound and instantly maxed out his discomfort index.

"What is this, it's sticky AND slimy! It better not be some kind of puke-like liquid?! Someone launched it straight at a wall, the power of booze is truly terrifying!!"

Wiping the liquid—thankfully, it seemed neither vomit nor a toxic substance—onto a different wall, Subaru, unable to rely even on Grandma's book of household wisdom, was at a loss. And of all times for this, no sign of another person could be sensed on the street either, leaving Subaru, who couldn't survive without relying on others, with no one to turn to.

"Trapped like this in a maze he could never escape, Subaru eventually stopped thinking altogether… BAD END number, uh, 8, 'Died Alone'—that's genuinely not funny."

Finally out of room for jokes, Subaru's situation grew seriously dire. If nothing else, toward some direction with signs of people—just as he thought that—

"—You bastard, you damn bitch! Don't screw around with me!!"

—that was when his eardrums clearly caught the presence of someone it was hard to decide whether to approach or not.
