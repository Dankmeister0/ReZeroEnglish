Arc 7 Chapter 1: "Baptism"

――When the billowing smoke cleared, Ram was made to taste a helplessness she rarely felt.

"――――"

Her pale pink eyes wide, she strained to see into the space still thick with smoke.
But no matter how she searched that emptiness, she couldn't find the one thing she was looking for.
Something that moved, or a black shape and a cute little blue one—she searched the gaping hole in space for them, but there was no sign of either—

"—Ram!"

As she tried to take a step forward, a hand seized her shoulder from behind, yanking her mind back to the present. Looking down, she saw she'd been a hair's breadth from putting her foot through the cracked flooring—if she hadn't been stopped, she might have fallen straight through.
That she hadn't even been watching where she stepped, quite literally, was an embarrassing lapse indeed.

"Ram, thank goodness you're safe... Is Patrasche all right too?"

"Emilia-...sama..."

Ram bit her lip. The one gripping her shoulder was Emilia, who should have been up on a higher floor.
Slightly out of breath, Emilia checked that Ram was unharmed, then turned to check on Patrasche, the black earth dragon crouched behind her.
Watching her, Ram finally recovered enough composure to take in the state of her surroundings.

Around them, the fourth floor of the Pleiades Watchtower was in a terrible state.
The corridor of the supposedly sturdy stone tower had collapsed, its ceiling and floor smashed to pieces. Through the gaping holes in the walls, the sky and sands of the sand sea were visible, and a dry wind blew in through them.
But the room that had taken the worst of the devastation was the very one Ram and Patrasche had been in only moments before—the room known as the "Green Room."

"――――"

The room, whose walls, floor, and ceiling had once been thickly overgrown with grass and vines, had been stripped of that accumulated growth root and stem, leaving nothing of its original shape.
The bed woven of grass and vine, the faint, gentle glow given off by the presence of the spirit—everything, gone.
And the biggest problem of all—

"Ram... where did Subaru and Rem go? They're safe, aren't they?"

Beside Ram, Emilia peered into what had been the Green Room. Ram couldn't answer her question right away.
Even though she knew that her own painful silence was answer enough.

◇◆◇◆◇◆◇

"—First, let's get straight on what happened. Otherwise we won't be able to talk this through properly."

So saying, Anastasia looked around at the assembled group and took charge of the meeting.
With the white fox stole—Echidna—still wrapped around her neck, Anastasia brushed a finger through the fur, faintly dirtied with dust, and said,

"About that black shadow that hit the tower earlier..."

"For the time being, it has vanished. I believe we have the Divine Dragon Volcanica's power to thank."

"Both the shadow and the dragon's actions were so sudden, it made me wonder what on earth was happening."

As the survivors gathered in the tower began sharing what had happened, Julius and Echidna both followed Anastasia's lead.
Like Anastasia and Echidna, Julius's white knight's uniform was badly soiled, though that wasn't only due to the fierce battle in the tower—the earlier incident had played a large part as well.
Still, none of the three had sustained any further injuries, which was at least some relief.
That much they owed to the Divine Dragon Volcanica, who had been on the topmost floor of the tower.

"Volcanica swept away that surging shadow with its breath, I suppose. If it hadn't, Betty and the others might not have gotten off so easily, I suppose."

Beatrice folded her short arms, and Anastasia nodded at her words.
Honestly, when Volcanica had suddenly unleashed its breath, Anastasia—uncharacteristically for her—had braced herself for death. That was how abrupt the whole thing had been; there'd been no way to respond.
Of course, that one breath hadn't been enough to make her fully let her guard down.

"Its response to the approaching crisis was superb, but the Divine Dragon's condition remains poor as ever... For now it seems to be behaving itself under Emilia-sama's instructions, at least."

"Out of consideration for Julius, I'll refrain from sayin' it's gone senile... but the fact that Mr. Divine Dragon is scary is the same either way. Still, what happened is just like Beatrice-chan says."

"I suppose."

Volcanica alone had been able to respond to that sudden crisis. If the dragon hadn't blown away the encroaching shadow, who knew what would have become of them by now.
That said, what kept them from celebrating outright was—

"There sure was a crazy amount of it, huh. But then it all went and got Big Brother and the others at once..."

"Meili-chan."

"—Ah, sorr-y."

"――――"

Perhaps rattled herself, Meili hadn't chosen her words carefully, and Anastasia scolded her for it. Meili obediently apologized, her gaze drifting toward the silent Emilia and Ram.
As a matter of pure result, the ones hit by that shadow were two members of the Emilia Camp—Subaru and Rem—so the Anastasia Camp had suffered next to nothing.

"Though I can't quite say that's the whole of it—that's what travelin' together means, after all."

Muttering that only to herself, Anastasia glanced over to check on Julius and Echidna.
While she herself had switched places with Echidna and lain sleeping in the depths of her own consciousness, the two of them had been traveling together with Emilia's group.
Of course Echidna, with a decade-long acquaintance behind her, was visibly shaken—but even Julius, whose memories of it all were still nearly brand new, was plainly unsettled too.
In that light, it was actually more surprising to Anastasia that Beatrice—who should have been just as close to the missing pair—was keeping up with the conversation so composedly.

"Are you doin' okay, Beatrice-chan? No, wait, 'okay' ain't quite the right way to put it. But you're not panickin', are you."

"...Betty panicking wouldn't bring Subaru and the others back, I suppose. Right now, the real problem would be flying into a panic and botching the first move, I suppose. That's the one thing Betty wants to avoid, I suppose."

"The first move? You mean..."

"—Bringing Subaru and Rem home. The actions needed for that, right?"

Anastasia, brows furrowed in puzzlement, had been about to press Beatrice further. It was Emilia, supporting Ram's shoulder, who cut in ahead of her.
Emilia's violet eyes bored straight into her. Startled by the intensity of that gaze, Anastasia's eyes widened slightly, and she wet her lips with her tongue.

"Well now, here I thought you'd be all broken up, and you're givin' me a look like that. What's goin' on?"

"It's not complicated. Subaru and Rem were swallowed up by that shadow and carried off... Volcanica drove it away, but just a little too late. But..."

"They are not dead because of it. That much is certain."

Still supported by Emilia, Ram opened her closed eyes and answered.
Letting out a weary breath, she gently touched her own forehead—the spot where, as an oni, her horn should have been. That was the basis for her certainty.

"Not somethin' from bein' an oni, but... the sisters' shared sense? That's how you're connected to Rem-san?"

"Yes, that's right. Rem is alive. ...As for Barusu, who can say."

"Subaru's fine too, I suppose! Betty guarantees it, I suppose!"

Beatrice's face reddened as she shouted this in response to Ram's all-too-typical remark. But Beatrice was the spirit under Contract with Subaru, so her claim carried its own weight of credibility.
In other words, the survival of the two missing people had now been confirmed from two different angles.

"Subaru through the bond of Beatrice-sama's Contract, and Rem-jou through Lady Ram's shared sense, respectively... they're worth believing. And for my part, I'd like to hope as well."

"How honest of you. ...Well, I'd rather Natsuki-kun and the others turned out safe too, mind."

Getting too friendly was its own kind of problem, Anastasia thought, stroking her stole at Julius's reaction.
Their alliance with Emilia's group was, after all, only temporary—they remained rivals contesting the Royal Selection. She had no wish to mistake that and invite needless trouble.
Still, precisely because they were rivals, debts owed ought to be settled properly.

"So then? I think it's real-ly good that Big Brother and the others are alive, but where'd the two of them go, I wonder-r?"

Meili, blissfully unconcerned with reading the room, asked this while playing with the little crimson scorpion perched on her head.
At her question, Julius opened his mouth to say, "That would be—"

"We couldn't determine the details, but that shadow was yin magic... or at the very least, something of the yin attribute. Likely the same nature as what sealed away Roy Alphard in the dragon carriage, or what seals the Witch of Envy in the shrine. If so, the one with expertise in it would be..."

"—Betty, I suppose. Betty's assessment isn't much different from Julius's, I suppose. That was a mass of the yin attribute, I suppose. And more specifically, close to a directional Shamak, I suppose."

"Shamak? Isn't that just the basic yin-attribute spell? That had that much power behind it?"

"...There aren't many who can use it, so Betty figured plenty of humans were mistaken about it, I suppose. But it's a problem when even a fellow spirit like you has the wrong idea, I suppose."

Under Beatrice's flat, withering stare, Echidna, in her white fox form, shrank her shoulders. Sighing at the reaction, Beatrice raised a finger. "May I explain, I suppose?"

"Shamak's defining trait lies in 'separation,' I suppose. A low-level Shamak severs body from consciousness; a high-level one divides space from space, I suppose. Betty's Door Crossing is an application of that—connecting one room to another to achieve something close to teleportation, I suppose."

"That's the trick Beatrice-sama used to play back at the mansion. Calling you for mealtimes was such an ordeal that, frankly, I grew rather sick of it."

"Being told that so frankly, right here and now, has Betty quite shaken, I suppose. Betty does feel bad about it, but the apology can wait... Anyway, if it comes to it, Shamak can divide even the sturdiest of objects using the same principle it uses to divide space from space. It's the Witch of Envy's specialty, I suppose."

"By that explanation, wouldn't Natsuki-kun and the others have been torn to pieces?"

If the shadow really was that dangerous a weapon, it sounded like nothing less than a statement that Subaru and the others, once swallowed, stood no chance of survival.
But Beatrice shook her head at Anastasia's doubt.

"That was only a hypothetical, I suppose. At the very least, that shadow wasn't meant to cut Subaru and the others to pieces, I suppose. But it was trying to swallow them up and carry them off somewhere, I suppose."

"Across space, to somewhere else... in this case, I'd guess to wherever it itself is from?"

"—There's the matter of Barusu's miasma, after all."

Echidna broke down Beatrice's explanation into simpler terms, and Ram delivered the conclusion.
The miasma that clung to Subaru—the same that had stirred up witchbeasts in great numbers on the way to the Watchtower—was by now common knowledge among the Royal Selection candidates.
Though the origin and precise nature of this miasma remained entirely unclear.

"What we do know is that miasma draws witchbeasts, and that things connected to the Witch Cult tend to give it off strongly... that's about the extent of it."

"Um, Subaru does get chased by witchbeasts a lot, but he's not connected to the Witch Cult. I know it looks re-eally suspicious, but..."

"It's fine, it's fine, nobody's doubtin' that at this point, Emilia-san."

"Really? Oh, good. Subaru's a good boy, so..."

Emilia let out a relieved breath, but the underlying problem ran deep regardless.
Just as she'd said, Anastasia no longer entertained the idea that Subaru had ties to the Witch Cult. And yet the mystery of his origins still bred doubt within her.
She didn't think of Subaru as someone with ill intent. But unconscious malice existed too.

—There was even the possibility that Subaru's very existence was a bomb ticking away behind a facade of good will.

"Whoa, well, that's a worry for later... anyway, I get the preamble. The shadow that swallowed Natsuki-kun and Rem-san was meant to carry the two of them off somewhere. The question is, where?"

"And what became of them as a result of Volcanica's breath interfering."

Beatrice and Ram, each connected to Subaru and Rem respectively, probably had a rough idea of their destination.
If all they knew was that the two were alive, with no clue at all as to where they'd been sent, the search would be a nightmare. —After all, not searching for them was not an option anyone here would consider.

"Please, both of you. Where are Subaru and Rem? Tell me."

At Emilia's earnest plea, Beatrice and Ram exchanged a glance.
Then, after several seconds of silence, they said:

"—South, I suppose."

"Ram senses the same direction. I can't tell the exact location, but it's quite far."

"South? South from here would be..."

When Beatrice and Ram both named south together, Emilia's well-shaped brows drew together. She was likely picturing the world map as hard as she could, but it was Anastasia who raised a hand instead.
Their current location, the Augria Sand Dunes, sat at the easternmost point of the world map. South from here would put Picoutatte, one of the five major cities, or Flanders in the running—

"If it were merely that, I can't account for the looks on their faces."

Having apparently arrived at the same thought, Julius voiced what Anastasia was thinking.
Even as she noted, with approval, that the way their thoughts kept pace with each other made him a knight beyond reproach, Anastasia closed one eye and said, "That's right."

"Even allowing for some distance, if they'd wound up caught somewhere around there, you could call the damage minimal. And yet their faces aren't lookin' good... which means it's further south than that, doesn't it?"

"Further south, that couldn't mean..."

"—You mean they went all the way to the Vollachia Empire in the south?"

Compared to Emilia's shock, Meili's surprise was a much smaller thing.
But that could be put down to her simply not grasping the gravity of it. If it were true, it would explain Beatrice and Ram's reactions well enough.
If Subaru and the others had crossed a national border—and into the southern Empire, no less—that was a serious problem.

"The southern Vollachia Empire and the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica have a non-aggression pact durin' the Royal Selection... that's how it's supposed to be, right, Julius?"

"Yes. You may not know this, but I accompanied the delegation that proposed the pact. Along with Reinhard and Ferris, we met directly with the Emperor of the Empire."

"I heard the Empire and the Kingdom don't get along at a-all, but does that mean it's fine now? If we go look for Subaru and the others..."

"—No, unfortunately, it isn't quite that simple."

At Emilia's tentative question, Julius lowered the corners of his brows and shook his head.
Amid his handsome features, he quietly lowered his gemstone-bright yellow eyes and said,

"The Empire has, for the past few months, temporarily forbidden all travel to and from the Kingdom. Something similar happened with the Holy Kingdom of Gusteko to the north in connection with the Royal Selection, though that restriction has already been lifted..."

"But the Empire's has not been lifted, is what you're saying. Given our journey to the Watchtower, this isn't the most current information—but assuming the situation hasn't changed—"

"So it's going to be really hard to go rescue Subaru and the others?"

"Unfortunately, yes."

He had turned the matter over from every angle, yet Julius's conclusion came down to that one regretful word.
Anastasia, too, could only arrive at the same conclusion as Julius. If they meant to enter the Vollachia Empire, a frontal approach was out of the question.
They would have no choice but to seek out a back route, and that would require both time and connections.
There was nothing that could be done immediately. Thinking of the two whose safety remained unknown, Anastasia expected Emilia to be frantic with worry—

"—Then we have to move fast. We need to get blood from Volcanica and bring it back to Priestella... and take the captured Gluttony to the royal capital, right?"

"Emilia...?"

"I know, Beatrice. My heart's racing so-o much, so-o much. But flailing around in a panic here won't help Subaru and the others. ...I have to stay calm."

But it was Anastasia who ended up surprised by Emilia's composed judgment, when she'd expected her to fall to pieces. To Beatrice, who wore the same look of surprise, Emilia gave a firm, resolute nod.
Her eyes wavered, and her voice trembled faintly, but she was making every effort to hold herself in check.
As if to say that, in order to bring Subaru and the others home, she had no intention of standing idle for even a single second.

"Both of you—Subaru and Rem are surely together, aren't they?"

"...Comparing notes with Beatrice-sama, that does seem likely, yes."

"I see. ...Then Subaru will surely do whatever reckless thing it takes to protect Rem. So there's no need to worry about Rem. Subaru's a little scary, though."

Her trust in Subaru ran deep, but Anastasia shared just as much of her worry.
Left to his own devices, Natsuki Subaru had already lost his memory once in this very tower. That self-punishing boy had a bad habit of neglecting himself in order to protect the people he cared about.
That could inspire others just as easily as it could be a source of concern.

"Even so, all we can do is put our faith in Natsuki-kun's quick thinkin', I suppose. Like Emilia-san says, sittin' around groanin' in this tower ain't gonna help us none. What we can do is move."

"Understood. ...Beatrice-sama, if there is one point of concern—"

"...Subaru's body, I suppose. Subaru's Gate is broken, so he can't expel Mana on his own, I suppose. If it's left too long, he could burst from a Mana blockage, I suppose."

"I see. We're in a similar boat, so I'm feelin' a real kinship with Natsuki-kun now."

Anastasia's own Gate had a defect she'd been born with.
Being unable to properly draw in Mana was, for instance, a fatal flaw for a mage. Though since it had nothing to do with the business sense and luck a merchant needed, it had never caused her much real trouble.

"Ana..."

"I know, I know. We can talk about my and Echidna's business separately, later on. —More than that, looks like there's no time to be restin'."

Anastasia clapped her hands, and taking that as their cue, Emilia and the others nodded back.
Their goal was to search for the missing Subaru and Rem, and in order to prepare a search party for that, they would need to make their way back from the Pleiades Watchtower as soon as possible.
On top of that, they still had to accomplish at least part of the original purpose that had brought them to the tower in the first place.

"My head's spinnin' just thinkin' about how busy we're gonna be..."

"Yes, really. But Subaru and the others must be having a much harder time of it."

Emilia clenched her fists tight, thinking of Subaru sent off to some far distant place. Meili, the little crimson scorpion still perched on her head, narrowed her eyes at that profile and shrugged. "Yeah, yeah."
With that, the group moved to begin preparations—or at least, they were about to.

"Oh, by the way, has Big Sister Ram told everyone that thing yet?"

"—. Not yet."

"—? That thing?"

At Meili's sudden question, Ram's face soured. At her reaction, Anastasia and the others, who'd been told nothing, turned their eyes toward Ram.
In response, after a moment's hesitation, Ram said,

"Emilia-sama, there's no confirmation of this yet, but... the ones swallowed by the shadow and sent to the Empire may not be only Barusu and Rem."

"Not only the two of them... but Ram and Patrasche and Meili are all here, so shouldn't that account for everyone who was in the Green Room?"

"Well, right before that shadow came floodin' in, a girl suddenly showed up. And Big Brother was callin' her the third Gluttony or somethin'."

"A third Gluttony!?"

Hearing news too alarming to let pass, Anastasia and the others were stunned speechless.
The demand to hear the details Ram had kept hidden, and the heated debate over whether their plans needed revising, quickly boiled over.
As the Pleiades Watchtower fell into a fresh uproar, Emilia looked out through the great gaping hole where the Green Room had once been, and clasped her hands together before her chest as if in prayer.

"Please, Subaru. —Please, be safe, together with Rem."

◇◆◇◆◇◆◇

—Far away, at the very moment Emilia's prayer was swallowed up by the dry sky over the sand sea.

"――――"

On a grassy plain caressed by the wind, a boy was lifting a girl up in his arms.
The boy had black hair and a sharp gaze. His sanpaku eyes, with their wide margins of white, gave him a perpetually sullen look—the sort of face people sometimes described as looking like it could kill someone, even at rest.
But right now, the corners of his eyes had softened, and his lips curved into a small arc. A faint hint of tears showed at the edges of those sanpaku eyes, and the boy was desperately fighting to keep them from blurring his vision.

Well, of course.
How long had he waited for this moment? Thinking back on all those heart-wrenching days, how could he possibly bring himself to look away from the girl before him, even for an instant?

Cradled in the boy's arms, gazing back at him from up close, was a girl with bright blue hair.
Her big round eyes blinked, and her lovely face carried the faint traces of a will not yet fully gathered. It looked, fittingly, like the face of someone still half-asleep—and that description wasn't wrong.
In fact, she had only just woken from a long, long sleep. It was only natural that her mind wasn't working properly yet, and that grasping reality would take her a little longer.

"...He... ro..."

Her trembling lips repeated the word, as if echoing what the boy had just said.
In response, the boy—Natsuki Subaru—nodded back again and again, saying, "Yeah, yeah."

"That's right. That's right, Rem. I'm your hero. This whole time, I've been..."

"――――"

"Rem?"

Trying to steady the tremor in his own voice, Subaru strained to catch the voice of the girl—Rem.
Perhaps her mouth was too dry, but Rem's tongue and lips couldn't quite form the sounds. Still, he leaned his ear close to her lips as she tried to convey something, determined not to miss a single word.
Just the fact that Rem was moving her lips, trying to tell him something, was enough to make him tremble with joy.

"—Uh."

"What is it? No need to rush. Rem, what are you trying to..."

—tell me, he'd meant to finish, but the words cut off.
The instant he leaned in close to Rem's mouth, focusing every bit of attention on catching her words, a hand shot out and seized his head and jaw.
The next moment, Subaru's voice cracked into an "Ueh!?"—and not just his voice, his whole body was flipped over on the spot.
And—

"...R-Rem?"

—by Rem, who had reversed their positions and now straddled him.
Subaru's eyes spun at the suddenness of it, unable to react in time. Looking down at him from directly above, Rem's blue eyes swept over his entire body, taking it in.
Then, letting out a quiet breath, she said,

"Just what is it you're after? What are you planning to do to me!"

"――――"

"Calling yourself my hero, out of nowhere... and who is this 'Rem' you keep saying? Answer me!"

With her knees pinning down Subaru's shoulders, immobilizing him, Rem's hands closed around his throat. Pressed down under her weight, Subaru gasped in agony, legs kicking wildly.
But Rem's skill at restraint was masterful. He couldn't win back his freedom in the slightest.

"Guh—ah, gbah..."

"If you won't talk, I'll simply keep going until you want to. There's no point in dragging this out. Now, answer me. What is your objective—"

"—Kgh."

Whether she was doing it on purpose or simply hadn't noticed, the pressure Rem's arms exerted on Subaru's throat was too strong to give him any room to answer her question.
As his consciousness began to fade from lack of oxygen, and it dawned on him that he was going to be strangled to death by Rem at this rate, Subaru kicked his legs all the more desperately.
This long-awaited reunion, one-sided though it had turned out to be, ending in a finale like this—he couldn't accept it, and he wouldn't allow it.
Even if, hypothetically, she didn't remember a single thing about the two of them.

"Ogh, ah..."

"What is it? If you're willing to talk, show a bit of sincerity first—"

Rem had just begun to frown at Subaru's writhing when—

"Ah-uh!"

"—Kyaa!?"

Out of nowhere, a figure lunged in from the side and sent Rem's body—still straddling Subaru—flying off him. —No, rather than "flying off," it would be more accurate to say the figure tangled itself around Rem and went tumbling across the grass with her.
With the weight lifted, Subaru rolled onto his side, coughing violently—"Cough! Cough!"—and, his eyes now watering for a reason quite unrelated to the emotion of the reunion, looked toward where Rem had gone tumbling.
There, he found Rem tangled together with a small girl.

"Uh! Uuuh!"

"Wh-what is this, who are you... stop it! Now isn't the time for—"

A blonde-haired girl lay sprawled atop Rem on the grass, baring her teeth and growling, face flushed red, and the sight left Subaru's mind blank.
Sucking in a breath, Subaru shot to his feet and dashed over to Rem and the girl.
And then—

"You! Get off Rem, you little—!"

"Ah, uh!!"

Subaru swatted away the hand reaching for Rem's hair, seized the girl—the Sin Archbishop, Rui Arneb—in a full-nelson hold, and tore her off of Rem.
The Gluttony's light body thrashed in his arms, but the most resistance the girl could manage was kicking her legs. Beyond that, she only growled and squirmed, making no further attempt to fight back against Subaru.

"Auh! Uh, uuuh!"

"Damn it, what's with her...! Squirming all over the place... hey, hold still! Rem, are you okay!? Not hurt anywhere!?"

"N-nothing's wrong with me. If anything, it's you who's been coughing over and over since just now..."

As Subaru held down the flailing Rui, Rem answered his call with her well-shaped brows knitted. Keeping a wary eye on him all the while, she slowly tried to rise to her feet—

"—Eh?"

Her knees suddenly gave out from under her, and Rem dropped straight down onto her backside.
The unnatural way she'd crumpled made Subaru's eyes widen too. But she, not noticing his gaze, pressed her hands to her knees in confusion and tried once more to stand.
However—

"...My legs won't do as I say."

"Don't tell me you can't stand?"

"N-no, that can't be... this, this is...!"

At Subaru's stiffened voice, Rem answered quickly, trying to force strength into her legs. But the more desperately she tried, the more it only threw the seriousness of the situation into relief.
Her legs weren't even failing to plant themselves—her will simply wasn't reaching them at all.

"Legs weak from lying down so long? No, but the strength she used to drag me down a second ago wasn't the strength of someone who's been bedridden."

It was common knowledge that a long hospital stay could sap a body's muscle strength from lack of movement.
It was equally well known that this could weaken the legs and hips so much that rehabilitation was needed just to stand and walk again—but could such an effect really be confined to the legs and hips alone?
This wasn't a case like someone who'd kept moving their upper body during a hospital stay. In Rem's case, she'd been asleep, unmoving, for over a year. If her body had weakened, it should have weakened evenly, all over.
And yet the way this trouble had surfaced in Rem's body, in such a strange, localized form—

"—Could it be feedback from whatever happened when Nee-sama fought?"

Trying to pin down the cause of the change in Rem's body, that was the thought that flashed through Subaru's mind.
The story he'd been told in the Green Room, of Ram's desperate battle against the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony. Cornered because of Subaru's own inadequacy, Ram had chosen, as a last resort, to split the burden with Rem.
As a result, the burden that had belonged to Ram—said to have been exceptionally strong even among the Oni Tribe—had flowed into Rem, and he remembered Ram admitting she had no idea what effects it might bring.
If that were true—

"...It's my fault."

A remark that, if Ram had heard it, would surely have earned him another scolding for conceit.
But if Ram had gone to reckless extremes because Subaru had failed to see through the task he'd taken on, and the effects of that had landed on Rem, then it truly was Subaru's responsibility.
With no reliable companion anywhere nearby, and only Subaru, Rem, and—for whatever twisted reason—the strangely behaving Rui here with them, that responsibility weighed all the heavier.

"Your fault... did you do something to me!?"

"No, that's just a figure of speech, but..."

"To begin with! Who are you, and who am I!?"

"――――"

Rem struck her uncooperative legs hard and glared at Subaru with eyes swirling with intense emotion.
Hearing Rem's anguished outburst, like a fit of temper breaking loose, Subaru tasted the bitterness of a terrible imagining becoming reality.

—Who am I, Rem had said.

Who are you was still bearable. But the question of who she herself was cut through Subaru, who had so longed for this reunion with Rem, with terrible force.
He'd already had a premonition of it, from the way she'd been calling herself not "Rem" but "I."

"...The same condition as Crusch-san, huh."

Robbed of both her "name" and her "memories," Rem had fallen into an unending sleep.
There were two other patterns among Gluttony's victims: Julius, robbed of his "name" and forgotten by everyone around him, and Crusch, robbed of her "memories" and left without a sense of self.
Rem had woken with her "memories" gone—in a state of amnesia.
Waking into a situation that made no sense at all—a boy with a nasty glare, a girl who did nothing but growl, and legs that refused to move—it was only natural she'd be confused.

"Uau!"

Perhaps tired out from thrashing, Rui had gone quiet at some point without anyone noticing, and now, dropped from Subaru's suddenly slack arms, she landed hard on her backside with a yelp.
Rui rubbed her bottom and rolled away. Without sparing her any attention, Subaru slowly walked toward Rem.
Rem watched him approach, her eyes full of sharp wariness.

That gaze reminded him of the first time Rem had ever turned open hostility on him.
It was easy to forget, given how close they'd grown since then, but Rem had originally been rather shy around strangers, a girl who was hard to get close to before you'd earned her trust.
In that respect, Ram, whose attitude never changed whether you'd earned her trust or not, was far easier to deal with.
Though, terrifyingly enough, there was even a theory that he and Ram had never actually grown close at all.

"Anyway, never mind about Nee-sama for now... hey."

"W-what. Let me warn you, if you're planning to do something to me..."

"—Rem."

"Eh?"

Rem's expression, tense until now, went slack with surprise.
Halting his approach, Subaru kept himself at a distance just beyond arm's reach and said,

"Rem. That's your name."

And once more, he spoke her name aloud.

"――――"

Said so gently, as if laid over her, Rem fell silent, unable to hide her confusion. Still, deep behind her lips, her red tongue moved faintly as she repeated, "Rem," as if testing the word.
As though trying, once more, to let it settle into herself as her own name.

"Honestly, I don't fully understand what happened either. But we got separated from the friends we were with, and now we're somewhere neither of us recognizes. Even you can tell that's a pretty bad situation, right?"

"That's..."

Still carrying her confusion, Rem's eyes turned not to Subaru but to the surrounding grassland.
The grass rustled in the breeze; the sun hung high, and her skin picked up on the sticky, humid climate. It was a damp sensation, quite different from the dry air of the Augria Sand Dunes.
The very taste of the air had changed—that was how different a place this was.
In other words—

"We can't count on rescue coming any time soon. We have to somehow act to save ourselves out of this, one way or another. So..."

"So what? What am I supposed to do? Me, whose legs won't even move properly?"

"...I figure saying this is just going to make you suspicious of me all over again, but just being here is enough. As long as you're breathing, talking, and looking around with your own eyes, that's all I need."

"—? You mean, watch for danger and warn you if you see any?"

"Not quite. But that's fine too."

It really was enough, just that Rem had woken up, was breathing, was speaking to him.
It was an oddly unambitious wish, but since all Subaru wanted from a woken Rem was simply for her to be well, this was the plain, unvarnished truth.
Of course, the problem of her lost "memories" still had to be solved, and Emilia, Beatrice, and Ram, left behind at the tower after they'd been separated, must be worried sick.
He wanted to rejoin them as soon as possible. —He wanted to let Rem and Ram see each other again.
To let Ram, who cherished her sister so dearly even though that sister no longer remembered her, see her again.

"Please, just for now, won't you trust me? I'll protect you with my life... no wait, if I traded my life for it there'd be no point, so I'll protect you at the risk of my life. I swear it. So please."

"—. Suppose I accept that offer. What would you do?"

"Right, well. We can't just move without any plan, so as for a strategy..."

In response to Rem's cautious question, Subaru gathered the information he'd need to decide on a course of action.
As already noted, he and Rem were in the middle of a wide-open grassland, and around it, in a ring, he could see large trees growing thick.
In every direction, before the horizon could be reached, the view was cut off by the tops of trees, which meant they seemed to be in an open clearing within a forest.
Honestly, entering the forest of some entirely unknown land was nothing but dangerous—

"First off, the golden rule if you get lost in a forest is to use GPS to let your friends know where you are, but..."

"Gee-pee-ess...?"

"I know. There's no such thing here. ...But Beako has a bond with me, so there's a chance she could pin down my location through something similar. In a sense, you could say my very existence works as a GPS."

Depending on the circumstances, there was also a chance Ram might pinpoint Rem's location through their shared sense. In that sense, both Subaru and Rem served as a kind of GPS link to their friends.

"Next is securing a water source—no matter what else, getting water is critical. It's probably best to set up a base camp and expand our search radius from there. As for edible plants or fruit... ah, good thing I learned this stuff from Clind-san. Bless the old master..."

Clind, who while teaching him parkour and whip-handling had also drilled all manner of other skills and knowledge into him. There had been plenty of times he'd nearly cracked under the training of that all-around capable butler, but thanks to it all sinking in, he could still hold onto some direction even in a situation like this.
In any case—

"There's more besides that, but the point is I'm not moving around with no plan at all. Get it?"

"...To some extent. Even if I wanted to resist, I'm in this state, after all."

"...That's a remark that lets a little too much honesty slip through."

Subaru tried smiling to put her at ease, but Rem's reaction wasn't encouraging.
Having lost her "memories," she had no foundation at all on which to trust Subaru. If her legs had been free, she might well have already made her escape.
Not that he wanted to call the misfortune that had befallen her a stroke of luck.

"Hope your legs start working again soon."

"—! Even if you say that, I don't know how. What do you intend to do?"

"I told you. For now, the plan is to find water. Given the state of your legs and all, I'd really appreciate it if you didn't fight me on this, but..."

So saying, Subaru closed the last step of distance between them, crouched down, and turned his back to her.
Seeing him in that posture, Rem surely understood what he intended.

"You intend to carry me on your back?"

"There's also the option of carrying you princess-style, technically, but I won't be able to keep that up for very long. If you'd let me piggyback you instead, that'd help me out personally, I think."

"――――"

Rem fell silent for a moment, staring hard at Subaru's pathetic face. Then, letting out a slow sigh, she hesitantly reached her hands toward his back.
Rem's slender arms slipped over his shoulders and clasped together in front of his chest. Feeling the soft weight settle against his back, Subaru rose slowly, careful not to jostle her as she clung to him.

He felt Rem's weight. But what struck him, carrying her, was how light she was.
Over the past year, he'd had occasion more than once to carry Rem while she slept, and every time, he'd felt firsthand just how difficult it was to carry someone with no consciousness of their own.
But he felt none of that now, with Rem clinging to him of her own will.

"—? Is something wrong?"

"No, I just felt a strange sort of emotion, that's all. Now, about finding water—"

"Before that... what about that girl?"

"...Ah."

Rem tilted her chin over his shoulder, and following where she indicated, Subaru remembered the problem.
There on the grass, rubbing her bruised backside and rolling about, tangled in her own long blonde hair, was Rui. —What was to be done about this strangely behaving Sin Archbishop?

"――――"

Even Subaru could tell that Rui's current state was unnatural.
She had never had anything resembling a sound mind to begin with, but that was in the sense of being vicious—not because she'd shown any hint of this kind of infantile regression.
If anything, she'd possessed an intelligence beyond her years, with room enough in her thinking for a demonic streak that took pleasure in licking at the raw, splintered edges of another person's heart.
But look at her now.

"Ah, ah-uh."

The moment she'd woken, she'd licked Subaru's face; now she growled like a toddler with no knowledge of language, throwing tantrums like a baby.
Something fathomless had undeniably happened to Rui's mind.
That said—

"Does that give me any reason to feel sorry for her?"

That she was an unforgivable evil—that fact would never waver.
In that final confrontation in the Corridor of Memories, Rui, having experienced Return by Death firsthand, had suffered such psychological shock that she'd come to fear not just Subaru, but the entire world.
By that point, she had already become a pitiable girl in full.
But Subaru hadn't saved her then. He hadn't even wanted to.
Despite having countless choices available, she had consistently chosen the path opposed to humanity, and in the end had lost sight of any chance to correct course, cutting off her own retreat—that was what a Sin Archbishop was.
Neither her two older brothers, nor she herself, were any exception.
Having committed an unforgivable evil, Rui Arneb had turned into a beast fit only for hell.
—Why should Subaru be the one to save such a creature?

"You're not going to help her?"

"...It's complicated. We were in the same place, but she's not one of my friends. If anything, she's the polar opposite of a friend. Leaving her behind won't weigh on my conscience."

"――――"

He could feel Rem, on his back, hold her breath—an understandable reaction.
To Rem, who knew nothing of the circumstances, Rui could only look exactly as she appeared—a small, helpless girl. Even if, in truth, she was one of the blasphemers who had toyed with countless lives.
So Subaru's choice was singular.

"We're leaving her behind. ...She's not just dead weight—she's a danger I can't afford to bring along."

Scooping Rui up from the surging shadow back in the tower had been a misjudgment made in the heat of an emergency. If he'd abandoned her there instead, he and Rem might well have avoided ending up like this at all.
It was, quite literally, all harm and not a shred of benefit.

"I... see."

"Yeah, that's right. Not that I feel great about it myself, but..."

Rem and Subaru himself were the top priority. He wouldn't let himself lose sight of that.
With that resolve, Subaru ignored the sprawled Rui and turned toward the forest on the opposite side—

"—So even hollowed out like this, it seems I was right to trust my own instincts after all."

It was a voice, terribly cold and dry.
Whispered right beside him, right in his ear, the sound of it made Subaru breathe out an "Eh." But before he could react any further than that, a slender arm wound around his neck.

—Rem, riding on his back, was strangling his neck from behind.

"—Gah."

"You say all these things that sound pleasant to lure me in, and then in the end, you'd abandon a little girl. How am I supposed to trust someone like that, just because they tell me to? Don't be ridiculous."

Unlike her uncooperative legs, the strength of the arm around his neck was pure, unmistakable oni strength.
Unable to pry it loose, Subaru found his breathing completely cut off. Arching back, without meaning to, he toppled backward onto the grass.
But even pinned beneath Subaru, Rem didn't loosen her arm. And because she was wrapped around his back, he had no arm free to shake her off.

Why, the question filled his mind completely.
There was no why about it. It was exactly as Rem had said. In front of a girl who knew nothing, who understood nothing, Subaru had rushed things too fast out of his own impatience.
And now he was paying the price for it—

"Reeking of something so vile, and claiming you're not scheming anything—how transparent can you get!"

"――――"

Hearing "something vile," Subaru remembered.
Back when he'd first met Rem, the greatest reason she'd distrusted and regarded him as dangerous had never been his poor first impression or the sharpness of his eyes.

—The Witch's Lingering Scent.

Even Rem, who had lost her "memories" and had nothing left but herself, could still sense it, unchanged.
That was the greatest cause of Rem's suspicion of him.
Remembering it, realizing it, had come far, far too late—

"—Ah."

He twisted his body desperately, trying to offer some excuse, but it was hopeless.
And so, slowly, slowly, Subaru's consciousness sank down into the depths of darkness.

That he didn't want this—didn't want to be killed by Rem, of all endings—he screamed with everything he had.
But the scream never became a voice.

◇◆◇◆◇◆◇

"—! Rem!?"

A sudden jolt back into consciousness sent Subaru's upper body snapping upright.
The pain that instantly assaulted his throat sent him into a coughing fit, and hacking up tangled phlegm, he somehow managed to sit up and look around.

He was in the grassland, sprawled where he'd been left.
It was a familiar sort of awakening, but he immediately knew this wasn't the déjà vu brought on by Return by Death.

—Because nowhere around him was there any sign of either Rem or Rui.

"This is... definitely the same place we got sent to. I'm..."

Recalling what had just happened, the moment he touched his own throat, the pain brought the unpleasant memory rushing back.
Strangled by Rem while carrying her on his back, and then—his life taken. —No, that wasn't right.

"My throat hurts... which means Rem didn't kill me."

She'd choked him unconscious, but hadn't gone as far as killing him.
Even as he marveled at the judgment of that Rem with her icy voice, Subaru let out a breath of relief—then immediately scolded himself that this was no time to feel relieved.
He hadn't died. Which meant the world was continuing on from that worst-case turn of events.
Rem, doubting his very humanity because of the Witch's Lingering Scent, had left him with the worst possible impression. —The fact that neither of them was here now was surely because she'd tried to flee from Subaru.
In the end, Rem had despised Subaru's decision to leave Rui behind.
To her, he must have looked like a truly heartless man, abandoning a small child in an unfamiliar grassland.

"That's a misunderstanding, I'd say, even if she might not believe me even if I told her...!"

Regretting his own string of misjudgments, Subaru slapped his own cheek and got to his feet.
Looking up, the angle of the sun told him not much time had passed. He didn't want to call this lucky either, but Rem's legs were still out of her control.
With legs like that, she couldn't have gotten far. As proof—

"There are drag marks on the grass...! I can follow this!"

Chasing after two people who'd fled into a forest stretching out in every direction, with no clue to go on, would have been the start of an impossibly difficult game of pursuit.
But by following the traces left on the grass, he could at least tell where the two of them had entered the forest. Whether he could track them further from there was a gamble, but—

"I've made plenty of bad bets in my time!"

Shouting something nothing to be proud of, Subaru charged furiously after the traces in the grass. With his own two feet, he was quickly able to pinpoint where the two of them had entered the forest.
Thick with densely growing trees, it struck Subaru as having something of the atmosphere of a tropical rainforest.
For a moment, some bit of TV trivia surfaced in his mind, about how the great Amazon jungle was practically a death trap for humans—

"If Rem's gone into a place like that, there's even less way I can just let it go."

Neither he nor Rem had made any preparations whatsoever for entering a forest before ending up here.
Thinking of Rem, fleeing desperately through the forest on legs that wouldn't obey her, he couldn't help but curse himself for every single rash decision he'd made, one after another—what an idiot he'd been.

"—Rem! Come out, please! I'm begging you! I was wrong!"

Trudging through the forest over soft ground and thick undergrowth, Subaru called out at the top of his voice. Of course, there was every chance this would only feed Rem's fear and drive her further away.
Even so, it was better than wandering the forest endlessly with no clue and nothing to rely on.
More than anything, if he didn't keep taking actions that let him feel he was doing something to find Rem, the guilt and self-loathing threatened to tear his chest apart.
So many people had worked so hard for Rem's sake—if something happened to her now, how on earth could Subaru ever make amends?
Not that he could even atone by dying, given his circumstances.

"Rem—! Where are you! Answer me! Please, just don't go far from my side!!"

As if he didn't care if his voice gave out, he shouted on through the dense forest, blocked at every turn by trees.
His limbs, pushing on through the forest, grew heavy, his exhaustion immense. Thinking back, his body had only just come through the fierce battle at the Pleiades Watchtower, and he'd slept for only a few hours since.
Even with the boosted recovery from the Green Room spirit's healing, it was a drop in the bucket.
If he wasn't careful, the moment he found Rem, the relief might drain the strength right out of him and he'd collapse on the spot.
Wary of such an absurd possibility, Subaru kept wandering through the forest—

"Rem—! Please, answer me! I'm begging you, I was wrong!"

Cupping his hands around his mouth, Subaru kept shouting.
It was a plea from the very bottom of his heart, but there was no answer from her, and Subaru's spirit only wore thinner and thinner.
He kept on chasing after the vanished shape of Rem, straining his eyes desperately at the trees of the forest—

"――――"

Just as he opened his mouth wide again to call Rem's name, something caught the edge of his vision.
It was a faint change glimpsed through a gap in the trees, beyond the thickly grown leaves. He saw that it moved differently than the grass swaying in the wind—

"Re—"

The instant he turned his face that way, filled with hope.

—That was when an impact, closing in at a terrifying speed, caught Subaru square in the chest.

"—!?"

Unable even to cry out, his feet lifted clean off the ground from the force of it, and Subaru's body was flung backward. He slammed into a tree trunk back-first with tremendous force, the impact knocking the breath from his lungs.

"Guh... wh-what... was that...!?"

The blow, coming straight out of that gap in the trees he'd been looking at, threw his thoughts into confusion, his head spinning.
But given the force of the blow, he understood at once that it had been an attack. So, faster than his thoughts could gather themselves, Subaru instinctively tried to leap away from the spot.
But he couldn't leap away. Because—

"—Ah?"

Because the thick arrow that had pierced clean through Subaru's chest had gone on through his back and pinned him to the great tree behind him.

"G-guh...!"

The instant he became aware of it, blood came surging up his throat and burst out all at once.
In great gurgling gushes, blood poured out unstoppably from his pierced, torn organs and everything else besides. Gurgling and choking, spitting up blood in place of breath, Subaru struggled to breathe once more.
In agony, he grabbed at the arrow in his chest, trying to pull it free.
It wouldn't budge an inch. The arrow held firm, driven straight through Subaru and into the tree, sealing his every movement.

What was this arrow.
It was a hopelessly, abnormally thick and powerful shot. A mighty bow indeed—his pierced body was pinned like an insect specimen, left to thrash uselessly and ugly—

"Agh, ub, egh, mmh...!"

Through the gaps in the flooding blood, he still tried to call the name of the girl who had to be somewhere in this forest. Into that wordless cry he poured a warning of the threat lurking in the woods.
Someone hidden in the forest had used a bow and arrow to take aim at him.
And Subaru, pathetically, unable to reach Rem, left here now, unable to do anything at all—

"—Mmh."

Just what was happening. Where was this place, and what was he supposed to do.
A rising heat, and a pain he'd registered far too late, spread through his entire body, and blood began to flow from Subaru's eyes, his nose, even his ears.
Tasting the sensation of himself being lost, emptied out, and feeling the cold approach of "death," Subaru strained his eyes desperately, throat trembling, and called her name to the very last.

—To the very end, he called her name.

Gurgling, gurgling, drenched in blood.
To the very, very last, he called her name, called it, and kept on calling it.

Kept calling—
