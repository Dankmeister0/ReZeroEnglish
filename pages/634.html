Arc 8 Chapter 18: "A Reunion, Brief as It Was"

Long pink hair streaming behind her, a girl in black robes hovered in the air.
Her sharp, narrowed eyes, and her faintly long ears, proved from appearance alone that her origin was that of the Elf race, reviled in this world.
And yet, strictly speaking, that outward feature was a mistaken impression.
Because if one traced this girl's history back, the most distinctive fact about her was not the matter of her being an Elf, but rather the manner of her birth.

Using Ryuzu Meyer, who had been an Elf, as the base material, she was a duplicate created by a certain formula placed in the "Sanctuary" — a new life brought into being by the same mechanism used to create artificial Spirits.
Among the many duplicates produced that way, there was one that had been judged the greatest failure of them all.

That one betrayed the intentions of the "Witch" who had built the formula and exploited the mechanism, and as a result brought forth countless victims, carved into the Kingdom's history as a great calamity.
—That was Sphinx: the artificial monster born of the "Witch of Greed" Echidna's failed attempt to duplicate her own existence.

"――――"

Overhead, at the sight of the "enemy" floating in the night sky, Roswaal caught his breath.
An encounter with an enemy that should never have been possible — it dealt no small shock to his spirit. No — let him admit it without false bravado.
It was a blow struck with full force, one that threatened to crack Roswaal's heart itself.

This was a reunion that should never have been able to happen.
Of all people, Roswaal was the one who knew best of the monster's existence overhead. He had, after all, faced Sphinx directly during the "Demi-Human War," when she had run rampant.
And at that time, Roswaal's own fist should have crushed the life out of that monster.
And yet—

"No loss of limbs. Memory also in good condition up until just before. My appearance… fixed in an undesired state. Self-recognition is a troublesome thing. Countermeasure required."

Sphinx murmured this in a quiet voice, stretching her own arm in midair.
Those golden eyes and that pale, bloodless skin were unmistakably those of an Undead, yet she possessed a far more solid sense of self than any Undead Roswaal and the others had encountered thus far.
Whether or not memories from life and a sense of self remained, and what accounted for the differences from one Undead to another — determining that had been part of why Roswaal and the others had come here. But this was a blunder.

And a blunder roughly forty years in the making, at that.
Sphinx's survival was a devastating blow to Roswaal, but what he regretted even more was that Beatrice and Garfiel happened to be present.

They should never have been allowed to meet her.
Quite apart from Sphinx's true nature — that particular "enemy," wearing that particular form, should never, under any circumstances, have been allowed to meet the two of them, Beatrice and Garfiel, who held such deep feelings for that very form.

Hiding that regret behind a blink, Roswaal armored his heart and, without taking his eyes off Sphinx—

"—Garfiel, don't get ahead of yourself."
"—Beatrice, get back."
"—Roswaal, calm down, I suppose."

All three spoke up at once, each toward a different target, and a momentary silence followed.
If each of them had called out to whichever of the others they thought most likely to lose their head, then perhaps it was fair to say, in a positive light, that all three had already dealt with their first shock. Digging into what Beatrice and Garfiel actually understood could wait.

"There is a difference in the way she speaks, and in the air she wears. That one's a special Undead, isn't she?"

Unlike Roswaal and the others, Mizelda, who had no particular reason to freeze up, narrowed her eyes.
At her words, Roswaal set his jaw, and before Beatrice or the others could hurl words at Sphinx, he opened his own mouth first.

"Surely not — don't tell me you've survived as an Undead ever since right after the 'Demi-Human War'?"

"There is some discomfort in how that question is framed. If 'Undead' is the appropriate term, wouldn't 'survived' be an inappropriate one?"

"I see. So you don't intend to answer the question. —As irritating as ever."

"I merely pointed out that the form of the question was inappropriate."

It was a response delivered as though she'd been wronged, yet Sphinx's expression never changed as she said it.
Originally a monster with nothing resembling real emotion, she had lost even more of that warmth of heart by taking on the form of an Undead. One could truly call it the coldness of a corpse.
Still, the mere fact that she now possessed the body of an Undead was enough that Roswaal could not suppress his sense of wrongness.

The formula behind the duplicates created by the "Witch of Greed" Echidna adopted, at its core, the same mechanism as the artificial Spirits whose bodies were composed of Mana.
And there was also the improvement to the "Sacrament of the Immortal King" that he and Beatrice had rushed to unravel just moments ago.
This monster had clearly become a more dangerous existence than before.

"The hell are you, huh? Answer me, oi."

Watching the exchange between Roswaal and Sphinx and apparently deciding it was going nowhere, Garfiel cut in.
The boy clacked his sharp canines together, rage pooling in his emerald eyes, as he said,

"I know damn well nobody but Granny talks with that face and that voice. But every other face like it, I've got their names and where they live down pat."

"――――"

"So what the hell are you?"

What Garfiel spoke of were Ryuzu, whom he cherished as his grandmother, and the duplicates born the same way as her but who, having been given no role, had only a faint spark of self-awareness. Those duplicates had also been given names, and Ryuzu — now living apart from them — had been serving as their teacher, with hopes that they might eventually be entrusted with work for the camp.
In any case, it seemed Garfiel had grasped by instinct alone that Sphinx was neither one of those, and yet was one born by the same means.

To Garfiel, she was a blasphemous existence, as good as a defilement of his family. In response to the boy's earnest question, Sphinx quietly lowered her eyes and said,

"It seems it was you all who have been disturbing the Mana in the atmosphere just now."

"—!"

She ignored Garfiel's existence entirely.
As if his question had never been asked, Sphinx's gaze turned to Roswaal and Beatrice instead. At that, Garfiel's throat caught, and the fury in his eyes intensified.
Understanding Garfiel's anger, Roswaal closed one eye. Glaring at Sphinx with only that yellow eye, he hesitated between two options — abuse or provocation.

"I have a rough guess as to what you are, I suppose."

"Beatrice."

"Be quiet, Roswaal. Since that thing came out while Betty was shut up in the 'Forbidden Archive,' I suppose Betty'll overlook your having kept quiet about it, for now. But—"

Beaten to the punch while choosing her words, Roswaal was told this and sighed.
She had already seen through the reason Roswaal had never revealed Sphinx's existence. His negligence on that front would no doubt be pressed further later.
But for now—

"I won't let you get away this time, I suppose."

Raising her hand, Beatrice fixed Sphinx squarely with her round eyes. Meeting Beatrice's fiercely determined gaze head-on, Sphinx nodded.
That monster, too, turned her own hand toward Beatrice.

"I too have recognized a threat. Elimination required."

The instant she said those words — words that should never have been said — the color in both Beatrice's and Sphinx's eyes flared faintly stronger—

"—Die."

Faster than anyone, the blazing explosion of Roswaal's magic announced the start of the battle.

△▼△▼△▼△

Stopping in the corridor of the Coupled Dragon Carriages, Subaru, struck by a sudden pang of unease, turned his eyes to the window.
Over the faintly dim night sky spreading above the Empire's plains, he placed a hand on his chest, where his heartbeat stirred faintly out of rhythm, and prayed for the safety of the girl he'd sent off into that distance.

"Subaru, are you okay?"

Noticing Subaru's state, it was Emilia at his side who spoke up.
Narrowing her beautiful violet eyes, she peered into his face; Subaru raised a hand and nodded, "Yeah, I'm fine."

The discussion between the leadership of the Emilia Camp and the Vollachia Empire, joined by the Karagi envoy delegation, had reached a natural pause, and the specialists were now exchanging views on the numerical matters — calculating military strength, securing supply lines, and so on.
If called upon for a tactical breakthrough, Subaru could be of use, but when it came to this kind of realistic, large-scale logistical discussion, he couldn't help but feel his own inadequacy.

"Even with the Pleiades Battalion, I left all that stuff to Gustav-san and Idra…"

"Mm, I think I understand how you feel, Subaru. Honestly, I wish I could actually be useful in discussions like this too, but… I'd still just be holding Otto-kun back."

"If anything, it's Ram and Otto staying behind in there, diving headfirst into the discussion, that's the weird ones. Ram I get, but I feel like that guy's nerve for fear just died at some point."

"Now, that's too much, even for a joke. Otto-kun's working really, really hard for our sake."

Emilia puffed out her cheeks at that assessment of their camp's all-too-reliable steward.
And so, facing Emilia in this calm moment, Subaru's heart couldn't help but flutter — both with genuine joy at being reunited with her, and at her being, as ever, dead center of his type.
Even seeing her every morning made his heart race; after being apart for a while, the impact was devastating.

"I'd love to fill my mind entirely with praise for how cute Emilia-tan is today, but—"

"You're worried about Beatrice and Roswaal, aren't you."

"Bea-ko might get a little too fired up, thinking it's for my sake."

Called out on the reason he'd been looking out the window, off toward the distant sky, Subaru gave a wry smile.
He'd said it half-jokingly, but he also thought it wasn't as much of an overreaction as it sounded. In fact, it was true that Beatrice had gone charging off, nostrils flaring, insisting it was for Subaru's sake.
His cooperating with Roswaal — someone he had plenty of complicated feelings about — was, in part, an extension of that same sentiment.

Beatrice and Roswaal, the two who'd come up in their conversation, had left the Coupled Dragon Carriages, joined up with the group carrying out delaying tactics against the enemy forces, and were now engaged in a certain investigation.
That investigation was, in short, to determine the true nature of the Undead's characteristics.

"So basically, we're checking with eyes that can actually use Magic whether the zombies are really being resurrected by this 'Sacrament of the Immortal King' thing everyone keeps talking about… huh."

"It seems like Vollachia has fewer people who can use Magic than Lugunica does, so there are probably things that only well-read Beatrice and otaku-natured Roswaal would notice. If we can pass that along to Abel and the others, I think that would be a way of fulfilling what we said when we asked to help."

Having said that, Emilia tilted her head slightly.

"Honestly, I wish I could be useful too… but I don't really know that much about the details of Magic. I always just kind of use it with a 'here we go.'"

"Emilia-tan's the intuitive type, after all. It's like — your needs are different from bookworm Bea-ko or otaku Roswaal, but you've got your own moments where you properly shine, so it's fine. Honestly, in my eyes you're always shining like the brightest star up there."

"Sorry, I have no idea what you just said."

He'd meant to cheer up Emilia, who was regretting not being able to help Beatrice and the others, but pouring on the words had only backfired.
Still, being able to share his anxiety with Emilia like this, rather than holding it in alone, was a small blessing.
To begin with, Beatrice and Roswaal weren't the only ones Subaru was worried about. Garfiel, fighting hard in the same place, and everyone in the Pleiades Battalion, worried him too.
Of the Battalion, only Tanza had come aboard the Coupled Dragon Carriages; everyone else was, according to plan, disembarking to join the delaying-tactics force.

They were comrades who'd made it through fierce battles together, united as one, all the way to this point.
Even Cecilus, who for whatever reason still hadn't rejoined them, included — Subaru trusted them.

"Even so, worry is worry, you know~. Please, everyone, just — don't push yourselves too hard, don't get too carried away…"

Just as Subaru pressed his hands together in a prayer-like gesture—

"—Subaru, Emilia-sama, so this is where you were."

"Ah, Julius."

The door partitioning the corridor opened, and Emilia raised her eyebrows at the handsome figure who appeared from beyond it. As she'd named him, it was Julius who emerged, a knight's sword worn at his waist over a wasou. Giving a small bow, he came right up beside Subaru and Emilia standing in the corridor, and the lips of what had been, until a moment ago, a somewhat stiff expression relaxed.

"We didn't get to exchange many words earlier."

"Mm… yeah. Sorry for worrying you and Anastasia-san, and Echidna too. Thanks for coming all this way to find us."

"—. Now that's a surprise."

At Subaru's honest expression of gratitude for the efforts of Julius and Anastasia, Julius genuinely raised an eyebrow slightly, and said,

"To think you'd voice your thanks so candidly. Could it be that, having grown younger, your stubbornness has softened to match your appearance?"

"Shut up! I mean, there might be some side effects like that, but I'd say thanks even if I hadn't shrunk! Where do you think we are?! It's hell, you know!"

"It's not hell, it's Vollachia, isn't it? Talking like that isn't fair to Abel and the others."

"You think? Most of the reason I feel like this country's hell is because of that guy's methods, so doesn't that mean he's got no right to get annoyed at me?"

Of course, Subaru figured Abel had his own reasoning, and it wasn't hard to imagine he'd been trying his best, in his own way, to run the Empire well. But that aside, the result had been the suffering Subaru had personally endured, so Subaru felt he had every right to say at least this much.

At Subaru's protest, Emilia looked troubled over what to say, apparently unable to entirely deny Subaru's point either.
Meanwhile, at Subaru's biting remark, Julius traced the scar near his eye with a finger and said,

"…I thought so back at the meeting too, but Subaru, what exactly is your relationship with His Imperial Majesty Vincent? Given how you're speaking now, it seems awfully—"

"If you're about to call that disrespectful, you'd faint if you knew what I've actually done to that guy. Just so you know, I'm not running wild on some 'kid privilege' pass. The reason things ended up like this in the first place is that old man's fault too, for not keeping a proper grip on him."

"I'm almost afraid to imagine. Emilia-sama, are you familiar with the details?"

"Yes. Basically, Subaru and Abel get along much, much better than I expected? At first I was worried whether the two of them could get along at all, but… honestly, Subaru really does make friends fast."

"Friends, huh…"

Sorry as he felt toward Emilia, who looked proud of it as though it were her own achievement, Subaru was still, in some vague way, in the middle of deciding just where exactly to place Abel among the people in his life.
At the very least, even though the two of them had thrown punches while baring their hearts to each other, he wasn't sure you could call that the kind of relationship where they'd hit it off right away, like a couple of delinquents duking it out on a riverbank.

"—. Subaru, may I take it that you consider me a friend?"

"Eh? Well, that's, um, yeah, I guess so? Up until now I thought it was iffy, but for the time being — we crossed the sand sea together and everything, so, that's fine, right? I think it's fine."

"I see. That's a relief. Good news, right after the news of your and Emilia-sama's safety."

Amid his exaggerated joking, Julius's question had, at least, been resolved.
Though honestly, that much was obvious at a glance given Subaru's current state — it was just the tip of the iceberg. At present, Subaru's biggest problem, plain to anyone's eyes, remained unresolved.

"Subaru, about your body—"

"The old man Shinobi did it. You should get him to do it to you sometime too. I'd like to see that."

"I'll pass. I'd rather not confront my own younger self, if it's all the same. Though Anastasia-sama or Emilia-sama, now, must have been quite adorable in their younger years."

"Emilia-tan's! Right! That angle! Why didn't I think of that!"

"Me? Hmm, I wonder. I did see myself when I was small, in the 'Sanctuary,' but I think Subaru and Beatrice now are cuter."

Emilia, whose self-evaluation was never particularly high, said this, but surely that couldn't be right.
Given how beautiful Emilia had grown up to be, she must have been an adorably cute little girl too. It might well have been a close contest with Beatrice, even—

"Still, hearing you talk like that puts my mind at ease. It seems the path back to your original self is already being arranged."

At that remark from Julius, Emilia nodded, "That's right."
Reaching from behind up to Subaru's shoulder — given their difference in height — she said,

"The old man who made Subaru little also seems to be on Abel and the others' side. He's out fighting the 'zombies' right now, but when he comes back, we have to ask him. To turn Subaru back to normal. And then…"

"Ah — actually, there's something I need to tell you about that."

"—?"

Feeling a little awkward, Subaru looked up at Emilia directly behind him. Emilia, her hand still on his shoulder, widened her eyes at his words.
With Julius in front of him also furrowing his brow, Subaru scratched his cheek with a finger.

"About my body — even once Olbart-san gets back, I'm planning not to turn it back yet. I think it's better if I stay this small for a while longer."

"Eh!? Why? Oh, could it be — because a kid's body needs less food to eat? If that's it, I can share my portion with you…"

"That's an incredibly cute kind of thoughtful, but that's not it."

Shaking his head, Subaru gently rejected Emilia's guess. At his answer, Julius narrowed his yellow eyes with a serious expression and said,

"Knowing you, you must have some reason you believe this is necessary…"

"Yeah. I haven't introduced you or Anastasia-san to them yet, but I've got comrades in Vollachia — the Pleiades Battalion."

"Pleiades…"

Given the existence of the Pleiades Watchtower, Julius caught the resonance of that word sharply. But sensing that pursuing it would only derail the conversation, he set aside the finer questions and urged Subaru on.
Nodding at that consideration, Subaru continued.

"These are my comrades in that Battalion. I don't really get the details of the mechanism myself, but when all of us, myself included, become united in feeling, it makes us incredibly strong. It's not just a figure of speech."

"—. Emilia-sama?"

"Mm, that part seems to really be true. Tanza-chan and Subaru's other friends are all incredibly strong, and they've been fighting so hard."

"And — I've been lying to all those friends. The lie that I'm the Emperor's son."

Even with Emilia's confirmation, Julius's eyes widened at what came next. Emilia too pressed a hand to her mouth as a small "ah" escaped her.

"Now that you mention it, I did hear you were going by that name… um, but Subaru, you weren't born in Vollachia, were you?"

"No, that's right. My hometown's nothing like this hellhole. It's way more peaceful and love-pi."

"…Even in the fortress city, there was talk of a 'black-haired crown prince.' Hearing 'black hair,' I couldn't help but think of you."

"Just so you know, this was Abel's idea, so direct your complaints his way. Anyway, the important thing is that everyone in the Battalion believes it. So…"

When he'd set out from the Isle of Sword-Slaves, Subaru had won over the comrades who would come with him by making them believe he was the son of the Vollachia Emperor.
That lie now lay, heavy, at the very foundation of the Pleiades Battalion's unity today. Subaru had no intention of continuing to deceive them forever, of course.
Once this unprecedented great calamity shaking the Vollachia Empire was resolved, Subaru would return to his original size, and, taking Emilia and the others, and Rem, would make his triumphant return to the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica.
And for Subaru and the others to bring this battle to a decisive victory, the Pleiades Battalion's cooperation was absolutely essential.

"That's why I still can't go back yet. I have to be the 'Natsuki Schult' that everyone in the Battalion believes in. At least until this fight to reclaim the Empire is over."

"Subaru…"

Clenching his small fist — one he'd already grown used to seeing — Subaru stated his resolve clearly like that.
Even up until declaring this to Emilia and the others, Subaru had still been hesitating.
Naturally, the larger body was more truly "his" than the small one. The desire to return to his original body was strong — he wanted to lift Beatrice up easily, wanted to talk to Emilia at eye level.
But that yearning of his couldn't be weighed against what was genuinely necessary.

"—. Honestly, no matter where you are or what form you take, you never change, do you."

Hearing Subaru's resolve, Julius let out a small breath and said that. Running a hand back through his bangs, he looked at Subaru with a gaze split evenly between exasperation and understanding.

"You force the most difficult, most unexpected decision — on anyone, whether it's someone else or yourself, without distinction."

"…It's not that noble a resolve. It's more like, 'I still kinda wanna stay a kid a little longer' — sounds like the kind of line from some old Showa-era pop song."

Told this straight to his face by Julius, Subaru answered that way out of a vague embarrassment. Just then, a hand was gently placed on Subaru's head, and turning to look, his eyes met violet ones.
Emilia's eyebrows drooped, and she looked at Subaru with eyes full of tenderness.

"See, that's exactly the kind of thing that troubles me about you, Subaru."

"Ugh, on that count I really can't make any excuses. I mean, I do want to go back to my proper body and get all lovey-dovey with Emilia-tan too, you know? But…"

"I understand. Subaru thinks things through a lot, and then picks whatever he decides is best, right? I think that's just how Subaru is too — same as Julius does."

Emilia smiled at Subaru, who was flustered, hands waving up and down. She then poked Subaru's nose with the finger of the hand still resting on his head, and added, "But you know—"

"No matter how small you are, I know that Subaru is still Subaru. So even if you never get to go back and stay little forever, I can wait — forever, until you grow big again."

"Even I'd rather avoid it coming to that, not being able to go back… wait, was that just now…?"

Said in such a gentle tone and manner, Subaru blinked, feeling like he'd just been told something incredible. But the one who'd said it didn't seem to fully grasp the meaning of Subaru's reaction, tilting her head in innocent confusion, without a trace of ill intent on her face.
No, being looked at with an innocently tilted head didn't help matters at all. What a little devil she was, in her own way.

"――――"

Feeling, despite himself, the color rising to his cheeks, Subaru looked to Julius as if seeking help. But Julius merely shrugged at Subaru's gaze, mercilessly deflecting his plea.
Cursing that unreliable Julius inwardly, and regretting his earlier verdict — maybe they weren't really friends after all — Subaru let his gaze wander—

"Huh?"

—and it collided head-on with the staring gaze of a girl who had just stepped out of a room facing the corridor.

△▼△▼△▼△

"Huh?"

The one who let out that sound at the sight of Subaru and the others — a girl with blue hair and pale blue eyes as her distinguishing feature — the moment Subaru laid eyes on her, his face lit up at once.

"Rem, thank god. I wanted to talk to you too."

At the sight of Rem appearing before him, everything that had just happened moments before leapt clean out of Subaru's head.
Along with Emilia and Beatrice and the others, Subaru had lost consciousness after the turmoil in the imperial capital, and hadn't had a chance to talk with Rem at any length either.

After his clash with Abel, once he'd told Katya what needed to be told, Subaru had left her in Rem's care and gone straight into the discussions.
Now that those discussions had reached a pause, this was finally their reunion.

"So, um, how's Katya-san? Has she settled down?"

"—. Given the circumstances, not easily. But she's cried herself to exhaustion and fallen asleep for now, so her brother is watching over her."

"I see. Yeah, that's… right. Sorry for leaving you with such a hard job."

"…It's something I wanted to do, so it's fine. Um, by the way, there's something…"

Answering Subaru, who had come up in front of her, Rem lowered the tone of her voice slightly. At that preamble from Rem, Subaru tilted his head, wondering what it could be.
But before Rem could get her words out—

"—Lady Rem, you've regained consciousness!"

That was Julius's voice, raised in surprise at Rem's presence, and it came first.
His eyes widened, his voice bright with honest surprise and joy; Subaru nodded, "That's right!"

"Sorry. I should've told you, and Anastasia-san and Echidna too. After we got separated from everyone, Rem woke up. Except…"

"My memories of Lady Rem have not been restored within me. Is the situation the same as mine?"

"Not just that — she's in the same hybrid state as Crusch-san, too."

"…Then, her own memories—"

At Subaru's explanation, Julius furrowed his finely shaped brow.
He seemed to immediately sense this wasn't a situation to celebrate unreservedly, and looked at a loss for what to say. But after closing his eyes once, when he opened them again, his expression had regained its composed sharpness.

"Lady Rem, forgive my abrupt address. I am Julius Juukulius, a knight in service to a certain person, and a friend of Subaru's."

"This person's… friend…"

"Being emphasized like that makes me want to push back, but yeah, that's right. This guy's also one of the people who helped out a lot while we were waiting for Rem to wake up."

"That is—"

Hearing of his relationship to Subaru as Julius bowed elegantly, Rem raised her eyebrows in surprise. After a beat of that surprise, Rem too bowed her head where she stood.

"I'm sorry to have caused you concern. I still can't say I'm at full strength, but for now, I'm able to stand and walk on my own two feet like this."

"That is wonderful news. Both for your own sake, and for all those around you who cared for you."

"—Yes."

Subaru wanted to grumble, as ever, about how flawlessly considerate his phrasing was, but in front of Rem, who nodded softly with a hand to her chest, he couldn't bring himself to voice such petty jealousy.
In any case, he was glad he'd been able to tell Julius that Rem had woken up like this. He'd have to properly tell Anastasia and the others later too.

"But I'm really glad that, thanks to Anastasia-san and everyone coming all the way to the Empire, I got to see Rem again like this. You all were worried about her too, weren't you."

"Yes. Lady Anastasia's heart ached over it as well. Lady Rem herself won't remember, but as someone who traveled together with her, I'm truly overjoyed from the bottom of my heart."

"Mm, I think so too. And that's all thanks to Subaru working so hard."

Saying that, Emilia smiled and once again patted Subaru's head.
He understood that the height difference made it easy to do, but being patted over and over like this made him feel, rather uncomfortably, like he was being treated as a child.
It felt wonderful, sure, but there was also a man's pride at stake that didn't want to accept being treated like a kid by Emilia.

"E-Emilia-tan, don't dote on me so much."

"I do think you're cute, but I don't think I'm doting on you? I just think Subaru's amazing, that's all. As expected of my knight."

"Huh?"

"Eh?"

Just as Emilia was patting Subaru's head in that manner, a sharp voice suddenly cut in out of nowhere, and Emilia's hand stopped.
Wide-eyed, Emilia looked to see Rem, standing across from her with Subaru between them.
Rem was staring fixedly, with her pale blue eyes, at Emilia's hand resting on Subaru's head.

"Rem? Is something wrong?"

"No, um, I'm just a little confused. Emilia-san, may I confirm something?"

"Confirm? Sure, bring it on, whatever it is…"

"'Bring it on' — nobody says that these days…"

With hands still resting on shoulder and head, shoulder and head still bearing those resting hands, Subaru and Emilia both tilted their heads in unison at Rem's words.
Rem, watching them with a suspicious gaze, looked back and forth between Subaru and Emilia and said,

"…What exactly is the relationship between the two of you?"

"Me and Subaru?"
"Me and Emilia-tan?"

At Rem's question, Subaru and Emilia looked at each other.
Indeed, as things stood, Rem had only just been reunited with everyone in the Emilia Camp, and Subaru had heard that she'd at least grasped her relationship with Ram — but the finer details beyond that hadn't been covered yet.
Really, this might be something that ought to be handled with everyone gathered together, but—

"Right. To put it simply, Emilia-tan is the head of our camp, and the future queen of a country called the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica."

"I'm still working hard to actually become that, so I'm just a candidate right now. And Subaru is my knight — my number one ally."

"Number one ally…"

"Yeah, I'm giving it everything I've got to make my Emilia-tan's dream come true."

"That's right, he's working so hard. Not for me — well, I mean, Subaru is mine, but…"

At Subaru's proud declaration, Emilia answered, a little bashfully. Hearing their two answers, Rem then put a hand to her own forehead.
After a while of apparently thinking hard in silence, she said,

"I'm sorry. I'm confused, but — is this because of me?"

"—. No, I don't believe this is Lady Rem's fault. Speaking as a third party."

"Thank you very much…"

For some reason, it was Julius who answered Rem, who seemed lost in thought, in that manner.
It wasn't just Subaru — even Emilia hadn't quite caught on to what was confusing Rem, so it was a bit hard to accept Julius, an outsider to the camp, acting like he understood.

"Did I say too much all at once about Lugunica and everything to Rem just now, I wonder…"

"I did mention bits and pieces beforehand, at least… oh, wait, could it be that Priscilla put some unnecessary ideas in her head back in Guaral or somewhere…"

"Wait, please. Why is Lady Priscilla's name coming up? Don't tell me — has Lady Priscilla come to the Vollachia Empire?"

"Um, could you please deal with my confusion first?"

While trying to resolve the question that had come up, a new question arose instead, and things threatened to go in circles.
For now, feeling that there were far too many topics that needed to be properly shared with Julius and Anastasia and the others, Subaru found himself troubled over how to prioritize which problem to handle.
Above all, resolving Rem's confusion should come first, but—

"――――"

Just as he and Emilia were trying to find a way to resolve Rem's confusion, Subaru's attention was suddenly drawn to something that flickered faintly at the edge of his vision.
It was something that, outside the window, had fallen from the night sky down onto the plains.

"Subaru?"

Emilia's voice called out in question to Subaru, who had widened his eyes without meaning to. Putting off answering that call, Subaru rushed to the window and peered outside.
Normally, Subaru would never, under any circumstances, neglect Emilia's call. But right now, he prioritized confirming that glimpsed something over answering her.
Straining his eyes against the flowing scenery, he caught, once more, in his sight, that thing which had fallen from the night sky.
It was—

"—Why here?"

Pulling out from the edge of his memory a person who shouldn't have been there, Subaru blinked his eyes over and over, and spoke the name.
There, was someone who should have been spending quiet days in the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica—

"—Ryuzu-san?"

"――――"

It surely wasn't that she'd heard Subaru's whisper.
At a distance and volume where it shouldn't have been possible to hear, and yet — Subaru's eyes met those of that small figure who had turned to face him, and by instinct alone, he sensed that they had recognized each other.

That small figure, still gazing back at Subaru, moved her lips as if to say something.
Trying to catch what it was, Subaru leaned forward—

"—Ah"

—and in the next instant, a white light poured down onto the Coupled Dragon Carriages, swallowing Natsuki Subaru and most of those who had boarded that dragon carriage, and blew them away.
