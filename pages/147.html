Arc 3 Chapter 70: "The Reason I Came Back"

"――Why did you come back?"

With a terribly serious expression, Emilia looked at him and asked that question. Subaru saw his own face reflected in her quiet, violet-shining eyes. Like a light reflected perfectly on a lake's surface, calm and still, it bore into him as if seeing straight through to what lay inside.

Naturally, a shiver-inducing wave of emotion swept through Subaru's entire body. Whether that arose from positive or negative feeling, drawn out by her penetrating gaze, Subaru himself couldn't say for certain right now. But there was one thing, just one, that he had as an answer to that question.

"――Why did you come back?"

The question, repeated. Thrown again at Subaru, who had not yet answered — but not to hurry him along. It came at him softly, like a gentle wave, and Subaru held his breath, lightly wetting his lips with his tongue. Then, closing his eyes for a moment――

"Hey."

"――――"

Silence answered him. But it wasn't a silence withholding a reply — it was one urging him to go on. Knowing that, Subaru didn't falter, and shaped his next words. Facing each other, each carrying complicated feelings, and yet — having been given the chance to exchange words at all――

"After all that ridiculous slapstick just now, don't you think it's kind of rough trying to keep this mood going at this point?"

"...That's really, really unconvincing coming from you, Subaru."

At Subaru's words, his expression softening, Emilia let out a deep sigh, and — looking terribly, terribly drained, as if she simply had no choice — she loosened her lips into a faint smile.

※※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※

――Subaru's lucky perverted moment, engineered by Ram's guiding hand, met a spectacular end at the hands of Puck's fatherly educational guidance.

Afterward, with an additional assist from Ram, Subaru was sent flying out into the garden, and by the time Ram finally helped him up, he'd been grinning the whole while in a way that earned him nothing but her disquiet — and as fate would have it, the whole sorry spectacle eventually made its way to Emilia herself――

"Did you hit your head hard? You were upside down — did you hurt your neck? Ah, there's even dirt in your ear... and why is this outfit covered in blood and mud like this? Are you okay? Are you hurt anywhere? Look, if you're hurt you need to take it off right now..."

"Wh— wait... no, hold on, it's still daytime and people can see, you can't just strip me down here...!"

"You really did hit your head a lot, didn't you..."

"I'm just like this normally! Thanks for worrying, but that look in your eyes is starting to hurt!"

Fending off Emilia, who kept reaching for him in obvious concern, Subaru shook his head, twisted his neck to dig the dirt out of his ear, brushed the grime lightly off his clothes, and — as if to prove he was still in one piece — did a round of high-knees on the spot before finally re-entering the drawing room. Inside, across a small round table, sat the two heroes of the earlier commotion, Puck and Ram, having tea together, face to face.

"Quite the performance."

"Oh, not at all. Far too kind, coming from a Great Spirit such as yourself."

"What the hell are you two doing having a nice cozy tea party?!"

Cutting in between the two of them as they exchanged textbook flattery, Subaru planted his hands on the table and glared at them both. But neither so much as flinched at his outrage, sipping their tea with perfect composure. Puck's tiny, personal-sized cup made for an oddly surreal sight. They swallowed their tea with an audible sound. Then, after a small breath, the two of them looked up at Subaru in unison.

"Thanks to Ram's little slip, we got to see something quite nice, didn't we. Let's call that a fair settlement."

"Why are you acting so high and mighty? Are you the king or something?"

"You should be grateful I'm merciful enough to leave your head attached to your shoulders. Thinking of the shame I put Lia through that words can't capture, I can only lament my own lack of restraint."

"You almost blew my head clean off, that's what's shocking here! There aren't many people in history whose cause of death was listed as 'lucky perverted moment,' you know!?"

The world is a big place, so somewhere out there, if you looked hard enough, maybe someone really had died that way. Though even Subaru, with his unmatched record of dying over and over, wanted no part of that particular method. In any case, after all that――

"As long as you're not hurt, it's fine. There's a lot we need to talk about, though..."

Subaru had been scowling at the two unrepentant offenders' responses. Walking past him, Emilia murmured that in a quiet tone. Her silver hair flowed with her steps, tracing an arc of glimmering light as she turned. Subaru, momentarily captivated by the sight, watched as she settled onto the edge of the bed and looked steadily up at him.

"――Why did you come back?"

And so, they returned to the first question.

Having finished recounting all that up to now, it really was hard to keep the mood serious. It wasn't as if he'd meant to turn it into a joke, but that was the result all the same, and Subaru scratched his head. Ignoring him, Emilia pouted, her lips sulky.

"Ugh, it's all — all — Ram and Puck's fault. It wasn't supposed to be like this... it shouldn't be like this. Honestly."

"Our deepest apologies, Emilia-sama. If your anger has not yet subsided, I am prepared to accept any censure or punishment you see fit. ――On Barusu's behalf."

"Don't you have even a shred of self-awareness that you're the perpetrator here?!"

"Ram always believes she is above, and Barusu is below."

"Y-you bully! You're a bully! I've never seen a maid this far from obedient carrying around this much giant-ism before!"

Given how demure her bearing and appearance were, seeing Ram's sheer nerve up close again after so long hit like a shock all its own. As Subaru found himself in a conversation with her that had made exactly zero progress from the days he'd left behind, Ram suddenly narrowed her eyes and studied him closely.

"Wh-what..."

"You look a little less anxious now. About a hair's width better than before, I'd say."

"――――Ngh."

At that merciless bit of consideration, Subaru couldn't help but catch his breath. In truth, until just a moment ago, his hands and feet had felt as heavy as if stuffed with lead, and before this reunion with Emilia his heart had been wound tight with anxiety rather than any kind of excitement. But now, that restlessness and unease had eased somewhat, and even his tongue, which should have been utterly parched, no longer ached.

Realizing that this was Ram's doing — that she'd deliberately arranged things to loosen the knot in his chest — a deeply complicated wave of gratitude washed over Subaru. And so,

"You're way too hard to read. I've got issues with that, you know."

"A woman must forever toy with a man using riddles that have no answer. If she's solved too easily, he'll grow bored and wander off elsewhere before long, won't he?"

"That's just you saying something that sounds clever to dodge the question. I'm not impressed. ――Though I am grateful, for the record."

"Emilia-sama. Just now, Barusu was thanking Ram for letting him peek at Emilia-sama changing. The absolute worst kind of lecher, this man."

"I would never commit sexual harassment that sophisticated! You're seriously back to your usual self already, aren't you!?"

Any gratitude long forgotten, Ram covered her ears and pretended not to hear Subaru's loud protest. Faced with such shameless business-as-usual, Subaru finally ran out of words. But watching the exchange between the two of them――

"――Fufu."

"Oh."

Subaru saw Emilia press a hand to her mouth, unable to hold back a burst of laughter. She'd been trying her best to suppress it, but once she noticed Subaru had caught her and raised an eyebrow, she seemed to give up trying altogether.

"I mean, you two, fooling around like that... honestly, I feel silly for even trying to hold it together."

"That's fine, isn't it. Better to laugh and talk than sit there fuming. There's been a lot going on, but even so, laughing while we talk beats anything else. I'll play the fool as much as it takes for that. Right, Ram?"

"――Hmph."

"Wait, you're not backing me up here!?"

Pleased that Emilia had responded that way, Subaru's mood lifted and his mouth ran on without thinking, earning him a look of contempt from Ram. When Subaru reacted to that, it only made Emilia's mouth loosen further, and the loop repeated itself over and over.

"You two really haven't changed at all. Somehow, it feels like the first time I've laughed in — really, really a long time."

"Indeed. Ram, too, hasn't seen Emilia-sama like this in quite a while."

To Emilia, who was catching the tears welling at the corners of her eyes with a finger, Ram bowed at the waist and said this quietly. Hearing it, Emilia's expression stiffened just slightly, and having witnessed that, Ram, as though she'd accomplished what she set out to do, said,

"Barusu. I'll leave the rest of this to you — handle it well."

"Wait, what about you? What are you gonna do about this weird mood you're leaving behind?"

"I already set the stage to make talking easier for you, didn't I. The rest is your job. Ram has a lot of preparations to attend to, and is busy — thanks to the reasons Barusu brought upon us."

At that quiet, penetrating look aimed up at him, Subaru had no choice but to fall silent. Confirming there was no rebuttal from Subaru, Ram bowed deeply to Emilia.

"Emilia-sama, Ram has preparations to see to, so please excuse her for a while. As for the situation, please hear it from Barusu's own mouth... Ram will follow whatever judgment and instructions Emilia-sama gives."

"Ram... but, I..."

"Ram will follow Emilia-sama's judgment, whatever it may be. Those are Roswaal-sama's orders."

Cutting off Emilia as she fumbled for words, Ram said only that, flatly, and left the room. Just before the door closed, the glance she shot toward Subaru was sharp enough to straighten his spine on the spot. Right down to the very last moment, she'd made him a handful.

Looking at the table, Subaru realized Puck was nowhere to be seen anymore. Whether he'd slipped away unnoticed into Emilia's hair, or dissolved back into mana and withdrawn from the scene entirely — at least for the moment, formally speaking, the room now held just the two of them. Subaru, and Emilia, alone together.

"It's been — really, really a long time since we talked like this, hasn't it, Subaru."

Unable to find his own opening, Subaru had been enduring a stretch of silence, and it was Emilia who broke it first with those words. When Subaru looked up, Emilia, still seated on the bed, had calm eyes, with no trace left of the intense emotion from that time before.

At least, he thought, they'd managed to get past that first awkwardness in the face of that fact. Though "gotten past" felt like the wrong way to put it — more like she'd let him get past it.

"Ah, being apart from Emilia-tan felt like an eternity. Every day my Emilia-ase levels kept dropping, and I thought I might suffocate from the deficiency."

Emilia-ase is Subaru's own personal term for the "Emilia component" that gets replenished inside him through actions like talking with Emilia, gazing at her, or catching a whiff of her lingering scent. When the deficiency reaches its terminal stage, side effects like self-destructive despair and losing all hope in the world start pouring out, making it a troublesome condition requiring urgent resupply.

"Is that the reason you came back?"

"――Nah, that's part of it, but it's not the whole thing."

To his light joke, Emilia's reply came flat, without inflection. Subaru took that as a kind of turning point, and with a strangely settled heart, began to think. That he needed to check his answer against hers — for that moment back then, that place where they'd talked past each other.

"Wanting to see you was the biggest reason, but of course there's a bunch of other reasons too. And the runner-up among them is this — I came to help you."

"――――Ngh!"

He saw Emilia catch her breath, her hand on the bed gripping the sheets tightly. Her expression stiffened, deep unease spreading through her violet eyes. But she quickly hid the shock that had run through her beneath a composure she put on, pasting on a smile so shallow even Subaru could see through it.

"What are you — what are you even talking about. Help, you say, but there's nothing, really. Nothing for Subaru to worry about, nothing at all..."

"You're really bad at hiding things, Emilia-tan. I came all the way up here through the village too, you know? I've got a rough idea of what happened."

"...I see. So that's how it is."

At Subaru's pointing it out, Emilia's smile went weak. And yet, she managed to keep her face, which had started to droop, turned upward, never once looking away from Subaru — that alone was admirable. A person crushed under their own helplessness quickly indulges their own will and lets their head hang. That much, at least, she refused to allow herself.

"Listen, Subaru. I... well, because I'm a Half-Elf—"

"Yeah, I know. I was there at the Royal Selection venue too, and I've had the chance to hear about it from all kinds of people. Probably more than you think, I know about it."

Walking closer, Subaru moved to Emilia's side where she sat on the bed. Then he knelt so as to meet her eye level, reflecting himself directly in her violet eyes.

"I don't intend to resent being a Half-Elf. I think it's pointless to compare myself to others and argue over what's different, and I don't want to belittle myself just because being different feels like a bad thing. That's how I feel about my own blood."

"Yeah. I think that's really, seriously admirable."

"But it seems like it's not that simple, at least from how other people see it. Just by existing, a Half-Elf makes all kinds of people feel uneasy, or afraid."

Quietly, holding her emotions in check as much as she could, Emilia spoke of it flatly. Listening, Subaru fought desperately to keep from showing on his face the aching tenderness welling up in his chest. The sight of her — fighting so hard against the environment surrounding her, against the lament of the weak heart that arose within her — was beautiful, noble, and fragile enough that it seemed it might vanish at any moment.

"Ram told me. That there's a strange atmosphere in the forest. Even the minor spirits have noticed something horrible, and they're frightened every morning. Something terrible is going to happen, for sure. Something so awful you'd want to look away."

"――――"

"That's the state things are in right now. It's dangerous to come back here — even for you, Subaru. So if you can, you should leave the mansion as soon as possible... your treatment's probably still not finished, right? So—"

"So if I leave because of that, what happens to you, Emilia-tan? It's dangerous for you too, and for the villagers, isn't it, same as me? You think I'm the kind of guy who'd hear that and just walk off somewhere?"

To her plea, Subaru shook his head, flatly refusing. At that, Emilia bit her lip faintly, and then, with a slight hesitation resting on her tongue, said "um—"

"Of course, I'm going to leave the mansion myself the moment we're ready too. I need to rejoin Roswaal, there's a lot we have to think through. The villagers will come along too. That's why I already talked with the village about it—"

"They wouldn't listen, right? That's exactly what you were dancing around a second ago. Getting all tongue-tied like that isn't like you at all, Emilia-tan."

"Ah..."

Realizing how incoherent her own words had become, Emilia fell silent, looking embarrassed. That reaction of hers struck Subaru as strangely novel, and yet not something he could feel even a shred of pleasure about. He tilted his head toward the door that led to the village.

"――I already arranged for the villagers, and for you and the others, to be evacuated."

"...Eh?"

"I bowed my head to all kinds of people in the royal capital and gathered a bunch of merchant Dragon Carriages, sent them to the village. Enough to fit every single villager and make a run for it. There's a seat reserved for you and Ram too, of course. Not exactly built for comfort though, couldn't manage first class."

At the rapid-fire barrage of Subaru's words, Emilia blinked in confusion, unable to keep up. Undeterred, Subaru pressed on, showing her both the urgency of the situation and, as if to prove he could be relied on to handle every bit of it,

"Most of those shady black things lurking in the forest, we already took care of, thanks to some reliable allies I dragged back from the capital. The rest of them, we'll deal with properly and clean up once everyone's safely evacuated. I'll make it all work out."

"Wait, hold on... Subaru, how do you even know all that..."

"Guess it's because a lot of people gave me warnings, and lectures, made me reflect on myself, taught me things, let me lean on them, supported me, believed in me, let me believe in them, made me want to believe, made me try to believe — stuff like that. That's probably why they helped me get here."

To Subaru's answer that wasn't really an answer at all, Emilia couldn't find words to follow up. And yet, every bit of it was Subaru's honest feeling. Everything he'd been given in the days after he parted from Emilia and stayed behind in the royal capital — it couldn't be put into words. There were things taken from him too, and he'd come here to take them back. But what he'd been granted in those days spent working toward that was, to Subaru now, by far the greater thing.

"I... I said something awful to you, Subaru."

"It's fine. I mean, saying I don't care would be a total lie, and honestly there were times I cared so much I thought I might've gone into depression from the nightmares, my thoughts spiraling into nothing but negativity."

"I'm really, really sorry, I..."

"If you don't just let that go, I've got nowhere left to stand, you know. Ahh, but well, look, we're here now like this, so don't worry about it. Though I'll keep worrying about it myself, just so I don't forget how stupid I was."

Emilia's eyes widened at Subaru's words. Nodding back at her, Subaru's own heart carried vivid memory of that scene from before, those hours at the royal castle. There was no way he could forget. He mustn't forget. That humiliation, that rejection he'd deserved to receive. His own foolishness, so hardened by self-righteousness that he'd driven Emilia to say things she surely never wanted to say. And so,

"Right now, I want to help you. I've prepared for that. Can't call it perfect, but it's everything I could manage with what little I've got. So, I want you to take this hand."

"But, I... I can't be that weak, I..."

"It's fine to be weak, Emilia-tan."

To her hesitation over grasping the hand he'd offered, Subaru smiled, irresponsibly. Her position, her role, her future — all demanded that she be strong. And that was surely correct. Crusch's nobility, Anastasia's resilience, Priscilla's arrogant sense of self — having seen them all in the royal capital, Subaru thought that, too, was probably a quality fit for a king. And yet, he also thought this:

"I'm super weak too, honestly, hopeless — but I've managed to make it this far because all kinds of people reached out and lent me a hand. Going forward too, I'm counting on other people's help to somehow keep muddling through — total reliance on outside strength, that's me. Course, I don't plan on just taking and never giving back. What I've borrowed, I'll pay back — and with the hand that pulled me along, I want to pull someone else along too."

"Ah..."

"So isn't it fine if, at first, it's just you being pulled along? Being weak now doesn't mean you can't be strong someday. For now, I think it's just about right for us to sit back to back, leaning on each other."

There's no need to be complete from the very start. The idea that a king should be strong, noble, more righteous than anyone else — that's a childish notion. But he also understood that it was a childish fantasy everyone held toward that existence, regardless. And so she, aiming to become that king, wasn't allowed by anyone to be weak.

――But is being weak really such a bad thing? Subaru found himself thinking.

Even while weak, you can still walk forward, facing upward, working to become strong. So if that's the case, isn't it fine to walk alongside someone, believing that, trusting in that?

"I'm here now because all kinds of people pulled me along by the hand. If I can, I want to be someone who can pull your hand too."

"Suba... ru..."

"So take my hand, Emilia-tan. If you can believe there's room for a guy who watches over the path to you becoming king — not from ahead, not from behind, but from beside you."

Watching her hand reach out, hesitant, Subaru let the corner of his mouth curl up faintly. Then, too impatient to wait any longer, the instant her fingertips touched his, he gripped it tight, and pulled her — letting out a small cry — up by the arm as he rose to his feet himself.

Still holding hands, the two of them, now standing, gazed at each other at close distance. His heart pounded. His feelings felt ready to burst. Hiding that surge of emotion, Subaru twisted his mouth into a mischievous grin.

"Feel a little easier standing up now?"

"...Subaru, you idiot."

At the question, Emilia lowered her eyes and murmured in a small voice. At her tone, Subaru's smile vanished, and he panicked, wondering if he'd said something wrong, but—

"Subaru's an idiot. Really, honestly, an idiot. Idiot, idiot, idiot."

"That's a lot! I mean — okay, yeah, I'm well aware I'm an idiot, yes, sorry."

"I really was thinking about a lot of things, you know. That I'm going to become king, and that means there's so much I have to learn, so much I have to be able to do. That I mustn't rely on others, that leaning on people isn't good — I was thinking about it so, so much!"

Hand on her hip, her free hand, Emilia raised her voice at Subaru, who shrank smaller and smaller. Listening, Subaru could only clutch his head with his own free hand and endure the sudden storm of Emilia-ness that had erupted upon him.

"The villagers wouldn't listen to me, and Ram will obey what I say but won't give her own opinion. Puck's... no point asking him anyway, and Roswaal keeps going off somewhere... I had no choice but to keep thinking about it all alone, the whole time."

At such a crucial moment, neither Subaru nor Rem had been at her side. The psychological burden she'd carried — if Subaru had only been beside her, he surely could have done something to help.

"I even started thinking, why not just pretend to be an evil sorceress and rain ice down on the village, force everyone to evacuate whether they liked it or not..."

"I— I think it was probably a good call not to go through with that. Let's stay cool about it, yeah?"

"You came back before I could actually do it! And that alone was already so much for my head to handle, and now you go and say all this stuff on top of it... it's really, honestly a lot to deal with."

A small, deflating sigh escaped her, and a brief silence settled over the room. Uncomfortable, Subaru was at a loss, unable to read Emilia's feelings. He'd gotten carried away with a lot of what he'd said, and now he wanted to punch his earlier self, but it was too late for that. If he could, he'd love to turn back time, pretend none of this had happened, and come back with a more polished set of pickup lines to try again — but that wasn't an option. A man who couldn't even make proper use of a revenge chance: Natsuki Subaru. Even he had to admit, that particular brand of foolishness wasn't the kind that would ever be cured, even by death.

"Subaru, you're making a hundred faces."

"Nobody says 'making a hundred faces' these days... though I guess it's been a while since we did this back-and-forth too."

Pressing a hand to his face to settle his expression, Subaru looked at Emilia. Just then, she happened to be looking up at him too. Their eyes met, and time seemed to stop. To Subaru, unable to move, Emilia wore a calm expression. Then, still like that, she parted her lips slightly.

"Me — I think I said something awful. But I don't think I said something wrong."

"――Yeah, I think that's fine. I don't think what I felt back then was wrong either. Though I do think the method and how I expressed it and the process and the result were all wrong."

"Isn't that basically everything, though?"

"I'm deeply reflecting on it! But even now, I still think the feeling itself wasn't wrong."

On that one point alone, he wouldn't lie. Seeing Subaru stubbornly refuse to budge, Emilia laughed softly. Then,

"Our hands are still held. Subaru's hand is really, really hot, isn't it."

"I bet my face and ears aren't losing to it right now either... y-you're not gonna tell me to let go?"

"You held on so tightly and pulled me up like that. It's your fault."

Lifting their still-joined hands, Emilia gave them a small sway beside her face. Her smile overlapped with that gesture, and a terribly warm heartbeat seeped into Subaru's chest.

"Will you help me again? Subaru."

"As long as you'll still let me."

At Subaru's answer, Emilia closed her eyes. As if letting the answer she'd received settle inside her, searching for the reply that lay within herself. Then she opened her eyes, looking straight at Subaru with her violet eyes.

"Tell me. About all the people and things you've prepared."

"Yeah, listen up. And if you think it's amazing, feel free to shower me with praise."

"Don't get carried away."

With her free hand, she gave him a light knock on the head. It didn't hurt, but he was so happy she'd reached out and touched him on her own that Subaru turned his face away to hide the tears that welled, unbidden, at the corners of his eyes.

"Well then, let's go find Ram and talk it all through. Seriously, there's more I want to tell you than I could ever finish saying, but that's for after we get past what's in front of us now."

"Okay, got it. I'm really, really curious, but let's do that for now."

Leading Emilia by the hand, pleased that she didn't resist, Subaru started walking toward the door. They needed to catch up with Ram, who'd gone on ahead, and help with preparing what to carry out of the mansion. The villagers' evacuation was probably being handled well enough by Julius and Wilhelm.

The matter of Subaru's own heart, and how he'd face Emilia — the thing that had weighed on him — had reached some kind of resolution, even if the full answer was still deferred. Amid a feeling like a weight lifted from his shoulders, his hand was suddenly tugged.

"Subaru, there's just this one thing I need to say right now."

"Hm?"

Pulled by Emilia, who'd stopped walking, Subaru stopped too and turned back. And to Subaru's questioning face, Emilia held her breath once, and then broke into a smile like a blooming flower.

"Welcome home."

――It was for that smile, he thought fiercely, that he had come back here.
