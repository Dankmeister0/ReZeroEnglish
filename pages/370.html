Arc 5 Chapter 54: "The Combat Power of Non-Combatants"

"Gluttony...!"

Faced with the nightmare standing before him, Otto couldn't stop the cold sweat trickling down his back. Otto had heard the name of the enemy claiming to be an Archbishop of Sin — "Gluttony." But that name was...

"From what I heard, the Archbishop of Sin of Gluttony was supposed to be named Roy Alphard."

"Aww, did you two meet us before we met you? If so, mister, it's pretty impressive you weren't finished off — normally that wouldn't happen. Roy's such a picky eater, everything's a feast to him."

The boy calling himself Lye Batenkaitos cackled at Otto's question. Listening to that laughter, Otto understood that his own frightening suspicion had been confirmed. —There were two Archbishops of Sin of Gluttony.

"No, to be precise, at least two, is it..."

Lye and Roy — or rather, Batenkaitos and Alphard. In the worst case, he might need to assume a nightmare scenario like the Great Rabbit, with countless numbers of them. Julius and Ricardo were supposed to be the two who'd gone to assault Gluttony's control tower, but if the ones waiting there weren't alone, then not just a hard fight but outright defeat was all but certain.

"Hey! Is now really the time to be chatting it up with this bastard! We've got bigger problems than that right now!!"

Otto sharpened his senses, feeling a searing sensation crawl across his forehead. Interrupting his train of thought was the voice of an extremely brash girl. Of course, Otto had already noticed the presence of the speaker. And because he'd noticed, he was also confused.

"Felt-sama, why on earth are you here? Weren't you told by Reinhard-san to stay put and behave in the shelter!"

"Like hell I'm gonna curl up small in some shelter while the city's in a state like this!"

The girl who declared this so boldly, blonde hair and red eyes — seeing her face to face again like this, there was no mistaking that distinctive figure. Felt.

"So you couldn't sit still, dashed out here, and ran into an Archbishop of Sin. If that's the flow of events, then your luck is every bit as bad as mine..."

"You don't have to be so pessimistic about it, mister. For us, any encounter is just spice for the feast. They call us 'Gluttony,' but we understand the importance of proper preparation, you know."

Centered on Batenkaitos, the positions formed a triangle with Otto and Felt. There was a figure at the remaining vertex too, and Otto recognized that person as well.

"Good to see you again, Kiritaka-san..."

"Hard to take that sincerely when it's said in such a gloomy voice with such dead eyes! Though I understand the feeling, somewhat!"

Kiritaka's slender frame, wrapped painfully in bandages, stood before Otto, who couldn't quite bring himself to be glad of the reunion. This was the man who had clashed with Gluttony while Otto was escaping Muse Company headquarters to join up at the city hall, and whose fate had been unknown ever since. Glad as he was that the man had survived, Otto had serious doubts about him as a player on this particular stage.

"..."

And that, no doubt, was a sentiment Otto held about both Felt and Kiritaka simultaneously. —He'd have liked, at the very least, someone who could actually fight.

"Three groups of non-combatants running smack into the enemy's main force. What kind of bad joke is this? Please, stop."

"Me and the diva addict here at least brought allies along, so we're better off. You're the one who's way too unreliable, showing up alone, mister."

Felt clicked her tongue at Otto's grumbling. On that point, Otto had no comeback. Felt had a rough-looking, hulking retainer at her side. Kiritaka too had brought along several members of the White Dragon's Scales. Otto alone was unaccompanied and unencumbered.

"Doesn't matter how many of you gather, it's all the same. None of you can quench this thirst of ours! Aaah, where is he, I'm looking for him, I want to meet him, want to meet him, let me meet him—"

Surrounded by people pessimistic about their combat strength, Batenkaitos's demeanor remained utterly unshaken. The boy swung both arms loosely at his sides, muttering incomprehensible words with an ecstatic look on his face.

"Want to meet who? What on earth are you talking about?"

Otto seized on those trailing words to try to keep the conversation going. Batenkaitos's composure was grounded in something real. If he felt like it, he could crush them all in an instant. Otto needed to buy time, even a little — and if possible, create an opening.

"Man, having to explain this over and over is getting really annoying. Nobody else will spill it, no matter what. I hate it, hate it, I really hate it, don't you hate it too?"

"..."

Felt and Kiritaka shook their heads grimly at Batenkaitos's words. So it seemed the two of them were the ones who'd been asked and refused to answer. And from the fact that a conversation was, surprisingly, taking shape at all, Otto guessed that talking with Gluttony wasn't entirely impossible.

He'd managed, with the Divine Protection of the Soul of Language, to hold conversations with beings that ordinary humanity could never hope to exchange words with. He could try negotiating here too. Whatever the difficulty, it had to be better than the problems that swirled around Subaru.

—Lend me your strength here, would you, Natsuki-san.

"No need to say that. I might just be able to help you. Go ahead, tell me. Is it about the artificial spirit you were after?"

It was a fairly dangerous gambit. Voicing it so smoothly might well light Batenkaitos's fuse. But Batenkaitos shook his head. Taking Otto's willingness to engage as sincere, the boy smiled happily.

"What we want to know is just one thing... that broadcast a while ago, the one that could be heard all over the city. We're looking for the hero who did that."

"..."

Retract that last thought. Subaru really didn't need to lend his strength after all — and if possible, Otto would rather not have lent his name either.

"That beloved, beloved hero is apparently coming to pass judgment on us. This small chest of ours could just burst with longing for it."

"...Is there nothing to be done about that man's talent for attracting the most persistently troublesome people, I wonder?"

If the man himself had been there, he'd surely have shot back, "I never asked for this!" But there was no point grumbling about someone who wasn't present. At Otto's reaction, Felt scowled.

"That's why I told you talking's a waste of time! Like hell anyone here's gonna sell out one of their own. Only someone like Reinhard would do something that merciless."

"I do question that assessment somewhat, but since I saw the part where Felt-sama was held captive, I can't really say anything!"

Felt, breathing hard through her nose, apparently hadn't given up anything in answer to Batenkaitos's same question. Kiritaka seemed the same. Both of them surely understood that Batenkaitos's target was Subaru, and yet they'd thrown that fact away without hesitation.

"..."

As a matter of personal judgment, that was admirable. But in this situation, it had to be called far too hasty. Put simply — Subaru wouldn't think of it as being "sold out."

"You asked the wrong people, it seems. These two barely have any real connection to the hero you're looking for. They're just a couple of hasty types who got fired up by his speech and rushed out of the shelter."

"Hah!?"

"Wai—"

Veins bulged at Felt's temple at Otto's remark. But it was Kiritaka who silenced her instead. As befitting the head of the Muse Company, he grasped Otto's intent immediately. At the same time, he glanced this way. At that questioning look, Otto gave a small nod.

"If you're not simply going to force it out of us regardless, I'll guide you to the hero. I do value my own life. Though I'll need some guarantee for it."

"Ohhh! You know? You really know? Where our hero is! What our beloved hero looks like! That weak, fragile person we can't help worrying over, who needs someone to support them!"

"—? Yes, I suppose."

Otto nodded at Batenkaitos's fervent outburst, though something felt off. It sounded almost as though he knew Subaru to some degree. For someone supposedly describing an idealized image of a hero, the way he spoke was far too intimate with the actual person of Natsuki Subaru.

"Yes — allow me to guide you."

But Otto forced that unease down. This was Subaru, after all. It wouldn't be surprising if he were acquainted with two or three Archbishops of Sin. Otto didn't quite think he had history with all of them, but — Greed, Gluttony, Lust, Wrath — that was all of them so far. He'd had history with all of them from the start.

"You're making an awfully sour face there, mister."

"None of your concern. More importantly, what will it be? Kill us all here and get no leads, or guarantee everyone's lives and be brought to the hero. Which will it be?"

"That's a nasty way of putting it. This is what you call a 'deal,' huh? That kind of thinking stuff, neither of us is any good at."

"Then it's not a bad idea to just go along with whatever's recommended to you, is it? Speaking as a merchant, that is."

"...Hmm."

He held the initiative in the conversation. For an Archbishop of Sin, Batenkaitos was surprisingly compliant. That, at least, made him seem as childish as he looked, and it was that very warped imbalance that made him a monster. Perhaps — perhaps he too was, in his own way, a pitiable boy—.

"—You just pitied us, didn't you?"

Just as that sentimental thought brushed through Otto's mind, Batenkaitos suddenly spoke in a low voice.

"What?"

"That look. I know that look. A look of contempt. A look of scorn. A look that sees us as merchandise... aah, I see. That's why I felt something was off this whole time."

Naked disgust and hostility surfaced in the eyes Batenkaitos turned on Otto.

"You're a merchant, aren't you? One of those types who slap a price on things, sell people off to others, and fatten their own pockets. Human worth, human intentions, all of it, all of it! You put it all on a scale and calculate it, don't you, you damned wraith?"

"That's... I think there's a bit of a difference of opinion there."

The situation was rapidly taking a turn for the worse, and Otto, already walking a tightrope, felt a blindfold being added on top of it. Whether he could make it across — the answer was already visible in the faces of Felt and Kiritaka, watching the exchange.

"Like hell I'm gonna listen to something like that from you! In the end, this world is nothing but gluttony! Gluttony! Eat, chew, suck, slurp, until you swallow it down — nothing's trustworthy until then!"

Stamping his feet, baring his fangs, Batenkaitos let his overflowing bloodlust run wild. There was no holding it back now. Facing a shell about to detonate, neither tricks nor words could reach him any longer.

"Guess it was always gonna come to this."

Felt said, dissatisfied, spinning the knife in her hand. Coincidentally, her weapon of choice matched the short blades strapped to both of Batenkaitos's arms. Though the gap in skill was surely immense.

"In that case, we'll have to rely on the good people of White Dragon's Scales..."

"Hey now! We're technically here too, y'know!"

The retainer beside Felt raised his voice, but Felt just shook her head. In other words, he was on comic-relief duty. Like Subaru in a life-or-death situation, more or less.

"Just thinking that makes that man's worth drop dramatically..."

"Are we done talking now?"

Batenkaitos slowly surveyed Otto and the others' faces. Fighting spirit burned in every one of them. Seeing this, Batenkaitos nodded, satisfied.

"In fine dining, preparation and ingredients matter most. Only once good materials are gathered does the true virtue of food come alive!"

"I sort of understand that, and sort of don't..."

"Not understanding is perfectly fine! Our aesthetic isn't something we ever expected anyone but us to understand! Now then, shall we — Itadakimasu!"

He must have already fixed his target during the conversation. Batenkaitos opened his mouth wide and closed in on Otto with terrifying leaping power. Standing at the water's edge, Otto pointed a finger at the blasphemer flying at him.

"It would've been nice if the insurance had just stayed insurance!"

"—Hah?"

"This is what I mean—!"

In front of a suspicious, furrowed-browed Batenkaitos, Otto struck his heel against the ground twice, loudly. Hearing that sound, as if drawn by it—.

"—————!!"

A swarm of Water Dragons burst from the canal behind Otto and surged all at once toward Batenkaitos.

—That Otto had succeeded in luring the swarm of Water Dragons owed almost entirely to the effect of Wrath's Authority.

Wrath's Authority, having spread across if not the entire city then an extraordinarily wide area of it, had shaken the citizens' emotions violently, causing the faintly sprouted seeds of unease to bloom all at once, and bringing about tremendous confusion and paranoia as a result. That said, Natsuki Subaru's speech just now had turned that very effect around to raise morale instead — and it was also the basis for Otto's covert maneuvering, which had led him to borrow the Water Dragons' power.

"Guh, oooohh—!?"

The sheer volume of the surging swarm of Water Dragons was more than Batenkaitos, airborne as he was, could withstand. Writhing their limbless, serpentine bodies, multiple Water Dragons — each easily over a hundred kilograms — crushed down on Batenkaitos. Their scales gleamed a slick, wet blue as fang after fang turned toward the crushed Batenkaitos.

"Water Dragon hunts are brutal."

Fangs sunk into prey, then twisted to tear the flesh apart. Against that small body, set upon by countless Water Dragons, not even a scrap of flesh would likely remain.

"..."

The aftertaste was far from pleasant. Under the influence of Wrath's Authority, the Water Dragons had been in a state of intense agitation, and Otto had cunningly deceived them using the Divine Protection of the Soul of Language. He'd lured them into cooperating with the sweet pretext of striking down the one behind the city's chaos. Had it not been for this clash with an Archbishop of Sin, it would have been a promise he'd have had no choice but to break — but well, here they were.

"That's amazing! Did you do that, mister!?"

Felt cheered and ran over. Not so much as an eyebrow twitch at the fairly gruesome sight before her — she clearly had nerve.

"I merely gave the enraged Water Dragons somewhere to point their fangs. Archbishop of Sin or not, nothing beats the ways of nature."

"That may be so, but... you're a scarier man than I imagined, Otto-san."

Kiritaka, approaching as well, let slip a comment that leaned more toward the reaction of a decent, ordinary person.

"In any case, I'm glad you're unharmed. Kiritaka-san, I never imagined you'd actually be alive..."

"My back was slashed rather badly, but fortunately, White Dragon's Scales are a renowned mercenary company. We keep healers on hand as well."

Even so, there was no hiding how painful his condition looked. Still, what exactly had driven Kiritaka to keep moving around despite an injury like that? As if sensing the meaning behind Otto's gaze, Kiritaka placed a hand over his chest with an expression of newfound gravity.

"Isn't it obvious? Pride. I hold the position of an executive in Priestella's administration. I heard the broadcast, but I cannot simply retreat and leave everything to others."

"I think that resolve is admirable, but..."

"Of course, I understand perfectly well how little use I'd be in a real fight. But even someone like me should be able to at least assert his pride."

Kiritaka's fervent declaration was clearly buoyed by the combined effect of Wrath's Authority and Subaru's speech. Indeed, that speech of Subaru's — which had no doubt served as an emotional anchor for the citizens — was too potent a medicine for someone with as strong a sense of duty as Kiritaka. Potent enough to permit reckless action that fear and reason would normally have stopped.

"Don't go thinking of it as just recklessness."

As if reading what was in Otto's mind, Felt pouted and said it.

"Everyone's got the right to fight for something that matters to them. No need for some grand reason, no need for solid proof — nobody's got the right to stop someone who just wants to do something."

"That's... a fine sentiment for a single individual, but not a judgment someone in a position of responsibility is allowed to make."

"It's just a figure of speech! Besides, I never said that's what's happening here. Both me and these guys came out here 'cause we've actually got a chance of winning."

"A chance of winning, you say?"

Backing up Kiritaka, and still pressing her point, Felt rubbed under her nose with a finger.

"I heard that broadcast of yours too, mister. That idiot Reinhard's supposed to have joined you at the city hall too. Everyone connected to this besides me was already gathered there, right?"

If Felt felt she'd fallen short somehow, that would be a mistake. As the saying goes, there's a right person for every job — there are things only certain people can do. Following that logic too far would leave Otto unsure of his own reason for being here, so he decided not to press further.

"Is Heinkel properly restrained, at least?"

"Camberley's got him under watch at the shelter. Me and Gaston just got back from picking something up."

Saying "something," Felt jerked her chin toward Gaston. In the big man's hands was a white-wrapped bundle that looked like some kind of long, slender spear.

"What's that?"

"Secret weapon Old Man Rom — our brain trust of an advisor — had him carrying. Some kind of Magic Vessel, apparently."

"A Magic Vessel!? At a time like this, how convenient!"

The true terror of a Magic Vessel's power lay in its ability to bring about results that should otherwise be impossible. Hearing "secret weapon," expectations rose accordingly.

"The conditions for it to actually work are kinda a pain, but if you clear those, the power's guaranteed. Though, the one it was meant for, you already finished off, mister, so it's gotta go to someone else..."

"—!"

It probably wasn't triggered by what Felt had just said, but that was the moment a scream reached Otto's ears.

Otto spun around as if struck, and Felt and Kiritaka's eyes went wide at his reaction. They hadn't heard the scream just now. Of course they hadn't. It wasn't a scream raised in a voice audible to ordinary people.

"You're more fun than I expected, but... plain water lizards don't have much to chew on. For us gourmands, even as an appetizer, it's pretty poor quality."

The voice that reached him, one that seemed to mock everything in this world. At the same time, the swarm of Water Dragons — which should have been devouring their prey in a frenzy — began thrashing wildly. Their tails, bodies, and heads flailed in a manner similar to their earlier agitated state, but the convulsive groans and the blood gushing out made it clear something abnormal was happening.

"Felt-sama, that Magic Vessel — it does pack a punch, right?"

"According to Old Man Rom, not even Reinhard could block it, apparently."

"I see. That's reassuring... Kiritaka-san!"

"Y-yes, what is it?"

Felt answered firmly, while Kiritaka's face showed some hesitation at the state of the Water Dragons. His White Dragon's Scales bodyguards were one thing, but for Kiritaka himself, a non-combatant, to remain here was nothing but dangerous.

"We'll buy time here — me, Felt-sama, and the good people of White Dragon's Scales. In the meantime, Kiritaka-san, please head to the city hall — no, to Shelter Eight in the First District!"

"What's there, if I go!?"

"Go, and you'll understand everything. The chance for victory you brought with you will come into play there."

His expression changed, strengthened by the resolve in Otto's declaration, and Kiritaka gave a firm nod. He turned to the guards behind him.

"You heard him. I'm heading to the shelter now, following Otto-dono's instructions. I want the rest of you to stay here and fight alongside them. To protect this city."

"Our job was supposed to be guarding the young master... when exactly did it turn into this much of a headache?"

"Wrong. The job was never to guard me. 'Help me achieve my goal' — that was the original contract."

Kiritaka answered the wryly smiling members of White Dragon's Scales with dead seriousness. That he'd switched to referring to himself as "I" [boku] rather than the more formal register — perhaps that meant Kiritaka's personal feelings had won out over his position as a city official.

"Help me achieve my goal, White Dragon's Scales. Let's fight to protect Priestella, this precious place of work, and to save our beloved diva, Liliana."

"Even though she won't even look your way."

"Whether or not she smiles back at me has nothing to do with whether I keep loving her. I love Liliana. There's no better reason than that to risk my life."

Having declared this, Kiritaka looked at Otto and Felt. Then he lifted the bag he was carrying in his hand.

"I will make it there without fail. When it comes to this city's map, and to Liliana — no one knows either better than I do."

"For a second there I almost thought that was cool, but nah, still creepy."

Otto agreed with Felt's assessment, but kept it to himself, and instead gave a silent nod toward Kiritaka's resolve.

"—Shall we get ready, then?"

The writhing Water Dragons had stopped moving; eyes rolled back white, they were on the brink of death. Slipping through the gaps in their bodies, Batenkaitos came walking forward slowly. The blasphemer in the shape of a boy confirmed his glaring adversaries and hugged his own shoulders happily.

"Nice, that's more like it. Recklessness and courage aren't the same thing, and desperation and resolve are worlds apart! That's the face of people who understand that. Makes me happy. You've finally earned the right to be on our table."

"So up 'til now we were headed straight for the trash can, huh — man, you really know how to piss a person off."

"Whether being recognized as an enemy is a good thing or a bad thing is a separate question. Personally, I'd think being underestimated would leave a lot more options open."

Would Subaru understand this kind of thinking, perhaps? Or was even this thought of Otto's, right now, something shaped a little too obviously by Subaru's influence? An unwelcome thought. In any case—

"Kiritaka-san!"

"—Wish me luck!"

Following Otto's call, Kiritaka broke into a run to escape the scene. At the sight of Kiritaka trying to flee the battlefield alone, Batenkaitos tilted his head.

"Come on, don't do that. We just got things nicely lined up between our enthusiasm and our appetite, y'know!"

Chasing after the fleeing Kiritaka's back, Batenkaitos's body leapt forward with tremendous force. Using the whole of his small frame, he shot diagonally through the air at unbelievable speed. Batenkaitos's fangs came within a hair's breadth of passing clean through Kiritaka. —A hair's breadth.

"Gaston!"

"If I die from this, I'll come back and haunt you crying!"

Felt's voice tore through the air, and almost simultaneously the huge man, already in motion, cut across Batenkaitos's path. Crossing both arms in front of his face and dropping his hips into a stance was Felt's retainer, Gaston.

"Don't get in my way—"

Batenkaitos swung the short blade on his arm, meaning to cut the obstacle down. The steel blade gleamed dully, and the strike drove into Gaston's bare arm. A sharp, clean sound rang out, and Batenkaitos's blade snapped.

"Hah?"

"..."

The question mark hanging in Batenkaitos's voice was the same question on the minds of Otto and the others watching. Gaston's stance hadn't budged. With that arm, he'd snapped Batenkaitos's short blade in two.

"Our big guy here's plenty tough — he's my armor, after all."

Felt, looking pleased at having rattled him, hurled the knife in her hand at Batenkaitos. Batenkaitos dodged it by kicking off Gaston with force. Somersaulting backward to put distance between them, White Dragon's Scales shifted position to block the path. Kiritaka's escape — that much, at least, had succeeded.

"Hmm. Heh. I see."

Having been thoroughly outmaneuvered, Batenkaitos's face nonetheless kept its pleasure-soaked grin intact. He went on, surveying Otto and the others facing him one by one in turn.

"The ones Rui would be happy about — three of you, maybe."

Whispering that with a trembling breath, he detached the broken short blade from his arm. That left his left hand bare and only his right hand armed — but even so.

"Somehow, I don't feel like the gap's closed at all."

As ever, Otto's inner alarm bells kept ringing, screaming of the greatest danger. Driving that deafening sound from his mind, Otto looked at Felt's profile. Seeing the same fierce fighting spirit still burning undimmed on that young face, he made up his mind.

There was no path of retreat here. They would fight.

"Fighting more often this past year than most seasoned warriors — I have to wonder what that says about me as a merchant."

The words he muttered, audible to no one but himself. And so no one noticed that, for all the pessimism in those words, the tone of his voice carried none at all.
