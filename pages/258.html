Arc 4 Chapter 90: "—I'm sorry"

—He'd meant to cut straight to the heart of it, resolved and ready. But the instant he saw the wave of emotion rising in her glistening eyes, Subaru realized he'd been seized by a terrible regret.

There was no doubt that the single thing he'd just said had torn the scab off a wound in Emilia's heart. Under the noble banner of concern, he'd roughly clawed open a cut that hadn't yet finished healing. Subaru couldn't help but feel, as a kind of phantom pain, the same searing ache she must have felt.

"I already know that the Trials of the Tomb show you your past... I mean, everyone's told me that much."

"——!"

Even so, chasing after whatever lay beyond that pain, Subaru pressed deeper still. A violent tremor ran through Emilia's expression as she bit her lip, her trembling eyes still fixed on him, unable to look away.

He held off on telling her that he himself had undergone the Trial directly. He couldn't carelessly say something like "I managed it, so you can too" — and besides, right now Subaru had had his own qualification stripped from him. She'd have every reason to think he was lying just to comfort her. Rather than risk that, it was better to simply convey how earnestly he felt for her.

"So that's why I think that's the reason you ended up turning back too. You've been agonizing over it, suffering, carrying it all alone, I'm sure... and I know you're planning to face the Trial again tonight, just like that."

"——"

Across the four repeated worlds, Emilia had never once turned to Subaru about the contents of the Trial. Partly because Subaru himself had half dismissed her right to take up the challenge, insisting there was no need for her to undergo the Trial at all; and partly because the opportunity for Emilia herself to confide in him had simply never come. The former problem had now resolved itself, since Emilia was the only one left who could attempt the Trial. The latter was, at this very moment, about to be settled.

Listening to Subaru's voice, Emilia's cheeks stiffened as she lowered her face. Before her eyes, framed by their long lashes, could turn away completely, Subaru pressed on.

"Even so."

"——"

"Can't you share even a little of the burden that's about to crush you with me? If there's a past you're too afraid to look back on, will you let me stand beside you while you face it?"

The tilt of her bowing head stopped, and, as if half-afraid, she looked back up at him. He couldn't let those eyes of hers reflect an anxious, feeble version of himself. Groundlessly brimming with confidence, Subaru squared his shoulders and met her gaze head-on. Baseless confidence and bluster — that much, he was very good at.

"Thinking back on it, I still don't really know anything about you, Emilia. I like you. Part of it's that you're totally my type to look at, sure, but it's also because of what I've seen of the person you are inside, through all the time we've spent together — that's what really gets me."

"——"

"I can say with my chest out that I love the you that you've become. But what experiences you went through to get here, what you felt, what you thought along the way... none of that, I know. I figured I never had the chance to know, or the need to. Because what matters more than the past is now, and what comes after. ...But."

"...But?"

"If a moment's come now where you have to look back on your own past, and you're saying you're too terrified to stand there alone... then will you give me the right to stand beside you, to face head-on whatever it was that made you who you are, whatever you have to confront?"

The right to take her place, to shoulder the hardships that fell upon her instead of her — that right had already been stripped from him. So instead, Subaru wanted the right to stand at her side and hold her up, to let her lean on him, when she grew too exhausted and was about to collapse. It might be nothing more than a small comfort. But there had to exist, without fail, a moment when even a small comfort could save a heart.

"——"

Subaru braced himself and waited quietly for Emilia's answer, though she said nothing. The wavering in her eyes conveyed the fierce conflict raging within her. Hesitation and confusion, guilt and self-loathing. A whirl of emotions raged inside her slender frame, thrashing as if trying to tear their way out. At last, Emilia spoke in a small voice.

"S-Subaru, just... just you being here already helps me so much... so I can't ask for more than that, I can't burden you any further..."

"I don't think of anything you could burden me with as a burden. Being able to do something for you is a happiness to me. When you're in trouble, and you wish someone would reach out a hand to you — I want to be the first one to do it."

"——!"

He told her again, to Emilia who, in a feeble voice, had tried to wave off his offer. Unless Emilia truly, seriously refused, Subaru had no intention of backing down here. He was well aware he was pushing into territory she didn't want to speak of. Even now, her half-hearted attempt to demur wasn't enough to shake his resolve. It wasn't with that flimsy a resolve that he'd thrown his Contract in Roswaal's face.

Still torn, Emilia squeezed her eyes shut tight and looked down.

"Subaru is..."

"——"

"Subaru, you believe in me, so..."

The words that should have followed never left Emilia's mouth. Right at the edge of finishing that sentence, her own integrity refused to let such a cowardly thing be spoken. There is no act more shameful, for a person, than doubting the trust of someone pleading with you in earnest. That very thing was the self-righteous accusation Subaru himself had once, unable to hold back, hurled at Emilia.

The spirit that made Emilia hold her tongue remained noble even now, cornered as she was. So Subaru neither asked her to repeat what she'd started to say nor dug it back up, and Emilia, as if regretting she'd said even that much, let her shoulders drop.

"...Just ask what you want to know, Subaru."

"............"

"If it comes from my own mouth, it'll surely come out all in pieces, nothing that makes sense... So, pull it out of me instead, Subaru."

"...Are you sure?"

"—Yes. Because I think that, for me, this is probably another kind of Trial."

At the sight of Emilia smiling so faintly, her voice tinged with resignation, Subaru momentarily lost his words. Then, shaking his head as if to collect himself, he pointed to the bed to reset the mood.

"Anyway, this might turn into a long story, so let's sit down, yeah?"

"...Yeah... you're right."

Emilia straightened herself and sat down on the bed. Subaru dragged over a chair and sat facing her directly. Smoothing out the wrinkles in her somewhat rumpled clothes, Emilia waited for him to speak.

With her waiting on him like that, now that Subaru had finally reached the crucial moment, he hesitated for a few seconds over what to ask first, then found his words.

"In the Trials of the Tomb, what past did you see, Emilia? From what I've heard from people who've been through it, it's supposed to be, um... something like a memory of your own greatest regret."

Subaru chose his words carefully, so she wouldn't realize he'd experienced it firsthand. The first Trial was about confronting one's own past. But the past Subaru had seen hadn't been a "past that had actually happened." It had used his family from his original world — the proof of his own regret — and his past guilt itself as its stage, with what amounted to an entirely newly-formed drama performed on it. So then, what shape had the Trial taken for Emilia?

At Subaru's question, Emilia licked her dry lips once, as if to moisten them.

"What I... what I saw, of the past, was probably... a memory from before I fell asleep, I think."

"—? Before you fell asleep...?"

"Right, before I fell asleep. It's a hazy memory, not very clear... but the me I saw there was still small, so it must be that."

Emilia closed her eyes as if searching through memory, speaking in the manner of a confession, and Subaru was confused by her words. He understood what she meant by "a smaller version of myself." If the Trial reflected the past, then depending on how far back it reached, one might well end up confronting one's own childhood. But the phrase "before I fell asleep" — that, Subaru didn't understand.

"Wait a second. What do you mean by 'before you fell asleep'? Is that different from just, you know, a normal night's sleep?"

"Yeah, it's different. Before I fell asleep means... before I slept, for so long, inside the ice, within the great tree in the forest. So it's a story from a long, loooong time ago."

"Inside the ice... Emilia, what do you mean?"

The context refused to connect, badly enough that he almost suspected she was deliberately making it hard to follow. And yet his imagination alone was already dragging cold claws down his spine. Feeling impatience hammer at his chest, Subaru forced himself to keep calm.

"Answer me, Emilia. Inside the great tree, inside the ice — what does that mean?"

"...Just what it sounds like."

"——"

After a beat, Emilia looked up at Subaru and told him.

"I was frozen in ice, together with the great tree in the forest, for a long, long time. Until Puck found me and got me out of there — for a really, reeeally... long time."

'—Finally, I found you.'

—Who...?

'I'm sorry, I'm so sorry. Leaving you all alone — I'm truly sorry. I've been searching for you the whole time. On and on, searching and searching, I never stopped looking for you.'

—Where is this? It's so cold.

'I'll get you out right away, okay? In such a lonely place, all alone... why did this child have to go through this... why did it take me so long to...'

—Hey, who are you? Why are you crying?

'—Because you're more precious to me than anything else. That's why I'm so happy to see you again.'

—Happy... that we met?

'That's right. I... I was reborn again just to meet you.'

—Who are you?

'I'm... I'm your greatest ally. Your number one, number one, strongest ally.'

—Then, are you my...

'—Yes, that's right. That's exactly it. So, from this day on, I'm your family. From this very moment, you'll never be alone again. —I swear it.'

—Really? Then, that must be...

"—I was so, so happy."

Emilia placed a hand over her heart, recalling that moment of happiness. Listening to her words, Subaru felt his mouth rapidly going dry.

Emilia, who had slept inside ice. The great tree of prayer that had stood in her homeland — frozen together with its trunk, she had spent all that time there until Puck's hands pulled her free. Just how much time had that been—?

"Emilia. The place you lived — that would be the Elior Forest, right? The forest that's been frozen since ancient times, the one that's supposedly still slowly spreading..."

"Mm, that's right. By the time I woke up, that forest had come to be called the Frozen Forest. —Before I fell asleep, back when I lived there with everyone, there wasn't any snow at all — it was a place full of bright sunlight and green, surrounded by it."

"Green... no, wait, more importantly, who's 'everyone'?"

It was a place he only knew secondhand. Subaru had no idea about the before-and-after of Elior Forest. So instead he pressed her on the other part that had caught his attention.

"Everyone's just everyone. The Elves I lived together with in the forest village."

"Elves... which means your family was there with you too, right, Emilia? Your father and mother and... maybe siblings, too?"

"——"

Seeing Emilia's eyes fill with sorrow at his words, Subaru realized once again that he'd let something thoughtless slip out in the moment. Emilia had said it herself once before, hadn't she — that Puck, at her side, was the only one who stood in for a parent to her, her one and only family.

Thinking back on it, it should have been obvious that her family had been lost in some way.

"S-sorry... I didn't mean..."

"It's fine. You're just worried about me, Subaru... But my family wasn't in the forest. Everyone in the village was kind to me, and they'd smile at me... but there was no one in that forest I could call blood family."

"...What do you mean, no one. Your parents...?"

At the question, Emilia quietly shook her head. Then, as if to distract herself by touching something with her fingers, she began fidgeting with the end of her braided hair.

"By the time I was old enough to remember things, neither of them was there. I didn't really think it was strange, back then... There was someone like a mother, though. Someone really, reeeally kind, and strong, and cool... someone like that, there was."

"——"

"But that person too, and everyone else... they fell asleep the same way, at the same time I did. Even now, inside Elior Forest, there are so many people who fell asleep and never woke up."

"—Wha—!?"

Emilia's voice was flat, as if she'd resolved only to state the facts. The content made Subaru's throat catch, but paying his reaction no mind, she went on.

"Ever since I woke up, I've been watching over everyone still asleep, together with Puck, the whole time. So that, someday, if someone else woke up from sleep the way I did, they wouldn't be left not understanding anything... that's what I thought, and that's why I stayed there, the whole time."

"...Hold on a second."

The sheer density of what was being said made it hard for Subaru's mind to keep up. In Elior Forest, on the day Emilia first saw snow, just what had happened?

"As far as I know, Elior Forest started freezing over... yeah, it should've been a little over a hundred years ago. I heard that somewhere before, at the Royal Selection or something."

"Right. I heard that too, after I started studying at the mansion, and I was really, reeeally surprised."

"So that means you were there, Emilia, right at the moment Elior Forest started freezing over? Does that mean you know the cause?"

"—No, I don't know."

Shaking her head, Emilia denied his words. At Subaru's furrowed brow, she lowered her eyes with a pained expression.

"I really don't know. What happened back then... my memory isn't clear. All I remember is that I was small, and that it was terrifying. But since I just kept sleeping after that, even that memory is hazy..."

"You keep saying 'small,' but about how old were you at the time?"

"...I think it was probably around when I was seven."

"Seven... Elves count age the same way humans do, right?"

Emilia nodded at his question. The ordinary way — a year added for every year that passes. Elves were famous as a long-lived race, and Emilia, being a half-elf, was no exception. That said, even long-lived elves start out as newborns once, so there was no blaming the little seven-year-old Emilia of back then. By simple arithmetic, that made Emilia's current age seven plus a hundred-odd years.

"An age gap that size isn't something to worry about at this point. Once the other person's from another world entirely, worrying about that kind of thing doesn't even mean anything anymore."

"...Subaru, is something wrong? Did I say something strange...?"

"No, no, nothing like that. I just found myself thinking there's a bit of an age gap between you and me, that's all."

Partly to sort out his own thoughts and partly to clear the air, Subaru adjusted the mood with a bit of banter. It probably wasn't that Emilia had guessed his intent, but at his words she loosened her tightly tensed cheeks somewhat and let out a small breath, saying, "Yeah, I guess so."

"But since I spent so long asleep without any awareness, I don't really have much confidence that I've grown up as much as my actual age..."

"Is that so? I can't really say, since I don't know much about how fast elves grow, but going by human standards, I'd say you've grown up plenty."

Casually taking in Emilia as she sat on the bed, he brushed off her worried unease with a laugh. Her limbs had grown long, and the curves of her body carried a womanly fullness. Her melancholy violet eyes and delicate features only heightened a mysterious beauty that seemed to drift back and forth between girl and woman. Emilia had grown up plenty, as a woman.

But it seemed Subaru's assessment missed the point of Emilia's actual worry, by a fair margin. "That's not it," Emilia said, shaking her head.

"The ice that put me to sleep didn't stop time — it only put my consciousness to sleep. So even inside the ice, my body kept growing properly the whole time. For a while after I woke up, the way my body moved felt so different from before I fell asleep that I kept messing things up, over and over."

"Ice like that... I see, so that's the kind of drawback it causes."

If a body that had been a young seven-year-old's before sleep had transformed into a fully grown adult body by the time she woke, that was bound to cause confusion. In manga and anime, it's common enough for a child's body alone to suddenly grow into an adult's, but there's no way adapting could really be that easy. With the brain's sense of self out of sync with the body, struggling the way Emilia had was only natural.

"Roswaal took me out of the forest, and I studied out in the world... and when I learned I'd been asleep for nearly a hundred years, I was really, reeeally shocked. That I'd been asleep that long."

"If you age normally inside it, then if anyone besides an elf or some other long-lived race got trapped in that same ice, that'd be the end of the—"

—"story," he was about to say, when Subaru felt he'd just heard something outrageous. Closing his eyes, Subaru quietly began piecing numbers together in his head. He calculated, added, subtracted, then recalculated several times over to double-check, turning a vague question into a firm suspicion.

"Emilia, just now... you said you slept for almost a hundred years, right?"

"Yeah, that's right, but...?"

"And before you fell asleep, you were about seven, right?"

"Mm, that's right. Subaru, what are you—"

"Emilia. How many years has it been since Puck woke you up?"

At the very least, her being taken out of the forest by Roswaal was, from what he'd heard, something that had happened within roughly the last half year. Before that, Emilia had apparently lived alone with Puck in Elior Forest. The question was the span of time between when she fell asleep, when she woke, and when she met Roswaal.

At Subaru's question, Emilia, still frowning in thought, touched a finger to her own lips.

"...Maybe, around six or seven years, I think...?"

"——"

Hearing Emilia's answer, the question that had sprouted in Subaru's mind turned to certainty. And that fact tore through his entire body like a violent shockwave.

Born, then seven years old, then asleep for about a hundred years, then awake seven years ago. Which meant, in other words, this:

—Emilia's actual age was roughly one hundred and seven. Her apparent age, eighteen. And her mental age, fourteen.

"Actual age, apparent age, mental age... none of them line up..."

A threefold discrepancy in age that should have been impossible, made real only because she was an elf. Inside Subaru's head, a great many of the puzzling things about Emilia's past behavior suddenly clicked into place.

He'd always thought it strange that, for an elf who'd lived over a hundred years, she was oblivious to flattery, and that for how she looked, she seemed to lack experience in dealing with people, and that every so often, an endearing childishness would show through in her gestures and manner. All of it had been a side effect of her having spent the greater part of her life inside ice.

"Fourteen... that's basically the same as Felt..."

Why did a girl like that have to be saddled with such an enormous burden? His irritation at the whole system of the Royal Selection, and at Roswaal, only kept mounting. And then, reflecting on how he'd unexpectedly gone off on a tangent with a topic meant only to smooth over the mood, Subaru cut back into a subject that was, after all, far from unrelated.

"You said you don't know why the forest got frozen. Then what is it you see, inside the Trial? Isn't it that hazy... memory from before you got frozen?"

"...Yes, I think so. That scene I see is surely, I think, a memory from before I fell asleep... something that really happened."

"If that memory scares you that much, then it must be that you ran into whatever outrageous thing froze you and the other elves, and you're unconsciously refusing to face it, aren't—"

"—No."

"But that's about the only thing that would scare you that much, isn't it? The Trial's supposed to show a person their greatest regret. So then what you see, Emilia, would have to be..."

"I said that's not it—!?"

His reasoning taking shape, Subaru had let heat slip unwittingly into his words — and Emilia's cry blew it all away. She blinked at once, as if regretting that she'd shouted, but then, shaking off her hesitation, she closed her eyes and turned a face on the verge of tears toward Subaru.

"What I... what I saw, in the Trial, wasn't anything like that. I never saw anything like that. ...What I, what I saw was..."

"E-Emili—"

"—The demon's child."

A chill ran down Subaru's spine, sharp and cold as if an icicle had been driven straight through his back. Emilia covered her face with both hands, hiding her expression. From behind that unseen face, a quiet, emotionless voice went on.

"The seed of calamity. The silver-haired child of ill omen. A life that should never have been born. The wellspring of hatred. An unforgivable soul. A demon. —The Witch's daughter."

"——"

"By everyone who'd been kind to me, everyone who used to smile at me — in the cold snow, that's what they said to me, and then..."

Emilia's arms, her legs, her whole body trembled in small, rattling shudders. The past she'd faced in the Trial — the past she should have left behind, beyond her long sleep. Having remembered that raw, naked malice, an unstoppable grief was now assailing her body.

"What happened after that, I don't remember anymore, inside the ice. But everyone else, even now, surely hasn't forgotten cursing me while inside that ice. Because it's stayed just like that, the curse carried on unbroken, the whole time."

"——"

"That's why I wanted to get everyone out of the ice... and then, apologize to them."

With a face on the verge of tears, Emilia lifted her head as if looking at people who weren't there, and bowed it quietly. And then—

"I'm sorry, for all the trouble I caused. —I love all of you, so much."
