Arc 1 Chapter 17: "Negotiations at the Loot House"

Having come this far, Subaru judged that any further detours would only sour the impression he'd made. Given that Elsa might be prowling somewhere nearby, he wanted to wrap up this deal as fast as possible. But contrary to that intention, Felt's guard went up another notch. She pressed a hand to her chest, where she'd apparently tucked the badge away, and said,

"Why do you know I lifted the badge? I shouldn't've leaked it to anyone but the client, and I only stole it just now. Your ears gotta be way too big to catch wind of that so fast."

"Yeah, now that you mention it, you're right, I was way too hasty, seriously careless of me!"

"...You gotta hide what's in your gut better than that if you wanna negotiate, y'know? Bro, you're spilling your guts way too easy for just a little poke."

Felt's expression, drained of its venom, watched Subaru clutch his head over his careless slip. She crouched down, bending her knees to bring her eyes level with his, and prefacing with "So?" continued,

"What's this about buying the badge? You and the lady who hired you for this are on different sides, right? Business rivals or something?"

"If you call it business rivals, well, I guess you could call it that, or maybe not, who knows, there's a whole mountain of maybes here?"

"Don't get it. Well, whatever, that part doesn't matter much."

While he racked his brain over how to talk his way through it, she laughed breezily. From within her clothes she produced the badge shaped like a dragon. In Felt's hand, the red gem set into it caught the light. She dangled it in front of Subaru as if showing it off, and said,

"As far as I'm concerned, I'll just sell to whoever offers the higher buyout price. Though there's a decent chance the lady who hired me gets pretty mad if I go back on the job."

"There's also a decent chance her anger's on a whole other level, though — well, that's my problem, not yours."

Setting aside the fact that he'd needlessly made negotiations harder for himself, Subaru cleared his throat and composed a serious expression.

"So, that means you'll hear out my offer?"

"Any deal with a chance of profit, I'll listen to. Obviously, right?"

"Tough as nails. ...I've got something on hand worth over twenty gold royals. On those terms, I want to buy your badge."

Felt's white ears twitched, and the pupils of her red eyes narrowed like a cat's. Her expression didn't move, but her tail was plainly wagging away — which was honestly kind of endearing. Keeping up her quasi-poker face, she crossed her arms.

"Heh, I see. That's a pretty decent price you're offering. Makes all my hard work worth it. ...But you know your business rival's offering about that much too, right?"

"Liar, it's a deal for ten gold royals. Get greedy and you'll die, no seriously."

In fact, that was likely how he'd died the first time around. Cause of death: greed. Felt must have figured that if he'd guessed the exact number, there was no bluffing her way past it. Her eyes widened slightly at his words, and she scratched her head as if resigned.

"Damn, you know that much, huh. ...Yeah, that's right, ten gold royals. Though if word got out there was another buyer in the mix, she might offer more, y'know?"

"That part's no lie, though," she added, the corner of her lips curling up — a girl of maybe fourteen or fifteen. Sharp little thing, Subaru thought, giving her brazenness a certain amount of respect, while resolving to keep pushing the negotiation from a position of advantage.

"You're not gonna just quietly settle for my offer, are you."

"Obviously not. Besides, that story of yours from before smells fishy too. My ears didn't mishear. You didn't say twenty gold royals — you said something worth that much. Seems kinda unfair that only my hand's been shown, far as negotiating goes."

"I'd think how many cards you can prepare before a negotiation is a matter of skill, but... it's also true you can't judge without me laying my cards on the table."

He wanted to avoid this dragging on with her stalling for time. Concluding as much, Subaru drew out his cell phone — the key item for this negotiation — from inside his clothes. At the appearance of the small device, Felt only furrowed her brow the barest amount. As ever, she gave only a thin reaction to anything not directly tied to money.

"That's the twenty gold royals? Looks like nothing more than a hand mirror to me."

"This is what's called a 'Magic Vessel,' all the rage in town these days. It cuts out time and freezes it. Now then, brace yourself and behold — NATSUKI Flash, eight-round burst!!"

He switched on continuous shutter mode, and the mechanical shutter sound and flash fired off in rapid succession. White light sliced through the back alley, and Felt, catching the full brunt of it, let out an unladylike shriek of "Whoa-ah!" in response to the sound and glare. Pleased that this reaction was more satisfying than last time, Subaru thrust the screen at the girl, who wore a look ready to complain. Her red eyes went wide with surprise.

"This is the power of this 'Magic Vessel.' It lets you preserve an image with this much precision. And as a bonus PR point — it's the only one of its kind in the whole world. So, whaddya think?"

Subaru looked down at her with his nose stretched smug and long, and Felt gave a "hmm, hmm" through her nose. She peered closely at the phone in her hand, looked it over thoroughly, then nodded in what seemed like acceptance.

"...Not a lie, doesn't seem like. But is this really me? If it's supposed to cut out the world so precisely, I think I'm way prettier than that."

"The raw material seems decent, I feel, but with decoration this rough, hard to say for sure. ...Get yourself cleaned up, put on some proper clothes, and I've got a feeeeling you could really transform."

"The way you're talking down to me and that weird sing-song ending is pissing me off... somehow I really don't wanna show you."

He'd earned himself some needless resentment there, but Felt's overall reaction seemed favorable. Still, the fact that she wouldn't easily go along with his little scheme was exactly what made the toughness of this slum's residents what it was. Felt smirked wickedly at Subaru's smug face.

"That said, I'll grant it's a novelty, but how much money it's actually worth is iffy. Just so you know, I'm not empty-headed enough to swallow whatever your negotiating partner tells me whole and believe some sweet nothing about this being worth twenty gold royals."

"...Well, that's fair enough, I guess. Personally I wouldn't mind if my brain were a sponge, but yeah, a third-party opinion's genuinely necessary here."

He'd have been glad if he could carry this through on pace and momentum alone, but he'd expected it wouldn't go that smoothly. The problem was who to designate as that "good-faith third party."

"Deep in this slum, there's a place called the Loot House. It's exactly what the name says. Fastest way's to ask the cranky old geezer who runs it. He's got a fair eye for goods. Seen plenty of stuff in his time too, so he oughta be able to judge even a 'Magic Vessel.'"

"Yeah, figured that's where this was headed..."

And Subaru had also expected that Felt would make exactly that suggestion. From her perspective, the Loot House was both the meeting spot with Elsa and a base with a bodyguard geezer she could rely on if things turned violent. Factoring in the need for an appraising eye on the "Magic Vessel" card, there was no option but that one. Still, what Subaru genuinely, sincerely wanted was to settle this before ever reaching the Loot House.

"I've got zero objection to having that old man take a look..."

"Calling a guy you've never even met 'old man' right off the bat — you might regret it once you actually meet him, y'know? He's surprisingly scary to folks who don't know their manners."

"For someone like that, though, he sure was quick to fetch milk for a foul-mouthed little brat like you..."

This was obviously a tangent, but Subaru guessed there was probably some doting-grandpa feeling behind it after all. Anyway, the problem wasn't the person — it was the place.

"Couldn't you call him out here or something? Outside, I mean. Here, you can even use my phone if you want."

"You're shoving that at me and I got no clue what you mean."

"Fair enough. Well, my contacts list only has 'family' and 'other' in it anyway."

Beyond that, to pad out his pitifully small contact count, there was a lineup of the police station, fire department, ambulance, the time-announcement service, and the weather forecast. It was the kind of loner behavior that almost made him strike a smug pose without thinking.

"I don't get what you're even hung up on, but if you're in a hurry, let's just head to the Loot House already. Honestly I had some other stuff I wanted to do too."

"Stuff like what?"

"Nah, turns out the owner's surprisingly stubborn, so it's a real pain shaking him off — sabotage tactics. Slip a little cash around and the folks nearby will happily do it for you."

"Right, let's go, let's go right now, quick, let's just go already, chop chop."

Pushing Felt's shoulder from behind as she started walking, Subaru forcibly hurried her along the path to the Loot House. While rushing her along as she puffed her cheeks and complained "what's your deal," Subaru privately gave himself a GJ for having averted a bunch of pointless damage. Interfering with the fake-Satella's pursuit of the badge for the sake of a little pocket change was drawing the short straw and then some. Better to go hungry and sleep at home than get pelted with ice chunks and writhe in agony.

"But we're gonna get him to take a look real quick, wrap it up snappy, and then bam, we're out."

"Bro, why are you in such a rush? You're sweating buckets. Live a little braver."

"Everyone in this slum keeps saying that — is it some kind of code phrase!?"

It's not "live braver," it's "live shrewder" that you should be saying, Subaru thought, but he left even that thought behind. And so, leaving such musings by the wayside, he headed for the Loot House for the third time in this fourth iteration of the world.

—Get in and out fast. Dash out the moment it's done. Leave her behind if he had to.

Burning with that resolve deep in his chest, Subaru gave a firm shove to the back in front of him.

"Ow, that hurt."

"Ow!"

He got kicked.

"To the giant rats,"

"Where do you even buy boric acid balls? Poison,"

"To the skeletons,"

"Digging holes takes way more effort than you'd think. Pitfalls,"

"To our noble and exalted dragon,"

"It's a fantasy world so I bet they really exist, but if I actually came face to face with one I guarantee I couldn't do a thing. Still, the romance of it means I do genuinely want to meet one. Can't even lie to my own wishy-washy heart about that, and yet somehow I still don't hate that about myself. What a crappy feeling."

"Can't even say the password without tacking on some unnecessary preamble!? That just pisses me off more!"

The door was flung open as if kicked from the inside, but Subaru, who'd anticipated it, leapt back swiftly and took no damage. The one growling in frustrated frustration was a giant whose height didn't remotely match the doorway — the same bald-headed Old Man Rom, familiar by now. His face was flushed red, looking dangerously high blood pressure.

"Get worked up like that and you're gonna burst a blood vessel. Even modern medicine calls that pretty risky."

"If you know it's bad for my health, don't go making me angry, then! What are you doing here! Today I gotta clear the place out, so you can't come in! You can't come in! Serves you right!"

Old Man Rom stomped the ground in a decidedly un-adult-like manner. But what overturned his look of triumph was—

"Ah, my bad. This guy's my customer too. Let him in, Old Man Rom."

It was Felt, who'd been hiding behind Subaru. She looked at the crestfallen Old Man Rom with sympathetic eyes, then glanced sidelong at Subaru, who was innocently whistling.

"You've got a pretty rotten personality, you know. Worst, to put it mildly."

"Something about me just makes people wanna tease me, I guess. The type that shines when picked on, or something... Wait, I thought I'd already confirmed I'm an M, but turns out I've got some S in me too? Versatile on both fronts!?"

"Don't know, don't wanna know, so I won't ask further. Coming in, Old Man Rom."

Slipping past the dejected Old Man Rom, Felt strolled into the Loot House as if it were the most natural thing. Ignored by her look demanding an explanation, Old Man Rom turned a bewildered face toward Subaru instead. Subaru nodded along sagely at that wrinkled face.

"That's the trouble with people who march to their own beat. We regular folks just get left behind, am I right?"

"Feels like I oughta start by redefining the word 'regular'... just get in already."

As if giving up on everything, Old Man Rom listlessly shrank his massive body and headed back inside. Subaru followed behind that back, welcomed into the dusty air of the Loot House. He cast a wary glance around the interior, but thankfully there was no sign of Elsa or the fake-Satella lying in wait. Perched carelessly on the counter, Felt was tipping back a bottle of milk as though she owned the place. Noticing his gaze, she said,

"What. This bottle's the only cold one. Not sharing."

"Brazen to the point your nerves never even flinch, you. Fine, I'll take booze, old man."

"You're pretty shameless yourself! Not sharing! Not sharing, I tell ya!"

His massive body creaking the floor as he lumbered over, Old Man Rom moved to shield a wooden crate behind the counter that looked like it held the liquor stash. His flustered reaction was almost pitiable, and Subaru laughed lightly, saying "just kidding."

"I see, so it's over there. Now that I know where, it's as good as mine..."

"Your true feelings are leaking out! Trying to steal booze from the owner of the Loot House himself, that's about as low as it gets!?"

"Aw, come on... you're making me blush. Besides, when I steal, I don't do it myself. I hire Felt for that."

"Sorry, Old Man Rom. Once I'm hired for a job, I can't turn it down."

"Not a single ally in this place — wasn't this supposed to be my house!?"

Given that his blood pressure was starting to genuinely worry Subaru, he decided to call it quits on teasing Old Man Rom for now. Felt, who seemed to have been just getting into the swing of it, looked dissatisfied, but Subaru just gave her an appeasing smile and headed for the counter. Taking a seat on the fixed stool, and mindful of the time, he wasted no time getting to the point.

"So, old man. Got sidetracked there, but I want to get to business."

"Feels like you ran way off the road there on your own, but fine — what is it?"

"What I'd like is an appraisal, I guess you could call it. Put a price on this 'Magic Vessel' I brought, and back that up for Felt."

The moment talk turned to business, a seriousness settled into Old Man Rom's grayish eyes. He glanced at Felt as if to confirm, and seeing her nod in affirmation, turned his gaze back. His eyes conveyed that he wanted to see the item in question, so Subaru pulled the phone from his clothes. Its metallic appearance first caught his interest, and within that oversized hand, looking every bit like a toy, his delicate fingertips traced over it as if to confirm its texture.

"So this is the Magic Vessel. Even I've never seen the likes of it before..."

"Probably the only one in the whole world. Also, it's a pretty delicate machine, so handle it with care. If it gets broken, I'm on a level of seriously-gotta-die."

In the sense of having to start over, that is. As Old Man Rom examined the exterior closely, he slowly folded open the flip phone. The startup sound elicited his first jolt of surprise, and then the wallpaper screen triggered a second.

"This picture is..."

"Figured the timing was perfect. To show off what it can do, I set it to a shot from Felt-chan's day-in-the-life — as the wallpaper."

The wallpaper image was one of the shots he'd taken of Felt earlier. He'd picked the one he thought came out cutest, and between that and the decent image quality, he figured it made for a fairly presentable picture. Old Man Rom compared the image with Felt, who was slurping her milk right beside him.

"Well, this is a surprise. There can't be anyone who could paint with this much precision."

"It's a Magic Vessel that cuts out a slice of time and seals it right in. No human hand could achieve this kind of clarity, right? If you want, old man, I can take your picture too."

"I'm curious, but it also seems kinda scary. Doesn't it take away your life or somethin'?"

"Guess no matter what era or what world, everyone's first thought seeing a photo is that same old superstition..."

Old Man Rom's reaction was straight out of some pre-modern era. Beside him, Felt pricked up her ears at the mention of anything lifespan-related. To reassure them both, Subaru answered, "Get your picture taken and you'll still live a normal eighty years," then used the camera function to snap Old Man Rom's photo. Looking at the finished image, Old Man Rom gave a low "Hmm." Being shown this right in front of his eyes was more than enough as a test of the phone's capabilities. To begin with, Old Man Rom's favorable impression of the phone was something guaranteed by firsthand experience.

"This truly is something to be reckoned with. If I were the one handling it, I'd move it for fifteen — no, twenty gold royals, no less. It's worth that much."

Whether it was his craftsman's pride as a dealer being stirred, Old Man Rom's eyes gleamed unusually bright. A craftsman who fences stolen goods was a rather ambiguous profession to feel proud of, but his stamp of approval was honestly reassuring. Subaru turned to face Felt, an involuntary smug look rising as he snorted through his nose.

"So, there you have it — that's my hand of cards. Just as I declared, an item worth twenty gold royals or more. I'd like to propose trading this for your badge."

"That face of yours — you keep flashing it and it's really starting to piss me off."

Perhaps it wasn't sitting well with her that things were proceeding exactly as Subaru intended, and Felt wore a dissatisfied expression. Still, that couldn't outweigh the prospect of her own pockets getting fatter. Her gaze fixed on the cell phone in Old Man Rom's palm.

"Well, I'm honestly glad to have it confirmed that thing's actually worth money. Guess I don't need to doubt the twenty-gold-royal figure either. Your hand's noted."

"Right!? So, deal's settled then. Selling it well is on you from here. Good luck! Now then, I'm in a hurry, so if you'll excuse me..."

He hurried over to Felt and held out his hand with a "gimme," asking for the badge. But his hand was gently pushed back down from above. Felt brought her face right up close to Subaru's furrowed brow.

"Hold up a sec. Why're you in such a hurry?"

"Me, in a hurry? Not particularly, no? Ah, and — don't bring your face so close."

"What, you the type to get all flustered with a girl's face up close?"

"No, it's more that you clearly haven't bathed in days. There's a stinging stench that hits right in the eyes."

His jaw got clocked from straight below. Reeling back, Subaru's eyes welled up from the searing pain of biting his tongue.

"No mercy for a lady's opponent!?"

"You show no mercy with your smart mouth either! Already drawing blood here!"

His mouth cut rather badly, the taste of iron stirring an unpleasant sensation in Subaru. Felt's face reddened, and she brought the blond hair draped over her shoulder up in front of her face.

"Do I really smell that bad..."

"I'd love to say that's just embarrassment talking, but I can't lie to myself, dammit."

Still teary-eyed, he turned his face away and crumpled to his knees on the spot, striking an overwrought, weepy pose. At the sheer detail of his little performance, Felt's anger gave way to exasperation, and she took a deep breath while lightly propping her forehead with a finger.

"Fine, hold on. Let's back up. Calm down, take it easy."

"Back up, huh. Right. For starters, I think we oughta start by fixing up your sleeping arrangements. That spot's got a lot of rotting garbage lying around, so just sleeping there, the smell probably soaks right in..."

"Get off the topic of my smell and back up one more step, would ya!?"

"...You too... quick with... the kicks..."

The front kick that came out of her embarrassment landed a clean hit on Subaru's jaw, positioned right where he'd been crouched. He didn't bite his tongue this time, but the blood that hadn't even stopped from the first hit started flowing again from the second impact.

"Swallow too much blood and it gets seriously nasty, y'know..."

"Learn your lesson from that and take our conversation more seriously. So, having backed things up properly, I'm asking again — why are you in such a hurry?"

Her anger wiped from her expression, Felt asked with a deliberately composed look on her face. Subaru read the room and sought help from the silent Old Man Rom, but got none. Left with no choice, he shrugged.

"Life's a finite thing, you know. Cherishing every single second, cutting out all the waste as much as possible..."

"Ah, yeah yeah. Don't need that. Anyway..."

Felt brushed off Subaru's attempt to dodge the question with a trailing, evasive answer. Narrowing her red eyes, she cut straight to the heart of it with a flat, matter-of-fact tone.

"In the first place — why do you even want this badge, bro?"

  • "ヨ・カ・ン" (spaced-out katakana for 予感, "premonition/feeling") is a stylistic emphasis device in the original; rendered as "I've got a feeeeling" with elongation to approximate the visual/verbal effect, since English lacks an equivalent spacing convention.
  • "強く生きろ、じゃなくて強かに生きろ" is a pun on "強く" (tsuyoku, "strongly") vs. "強か/したたか" (shitataka, "shrewdly/toughly") — both read differently but share the kanji 強; adapted as "live braver" vs. "live shrewder" to preserve the near-homophone joke as best as possible in English.
