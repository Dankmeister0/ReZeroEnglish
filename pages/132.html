Arc 3 Chapter 55: "Outpost"

Slay the White Whale—

Once the preliminary negotiations concluded and those two words—the subjugation—took on real shape, the parties involved moved swiftly. True to their word, the two merchants, Russell and Anastasia, tore through the city gathering every weapon and tool they could get their hands on, while Crusch threw herself into assembling the subjugation force she'd long been preparing and securing the Dragon Carriages that would serve as their transportation.

Watching it all, Subaru finally understood—from the world he'd lived through before—why arranging a Dragon Carriage became so difficult after the third day. Word of the White Whale's appearance would reach the royal capital, and that was roughly when Crusch would start moving to deploy her force along the highway.

Watching people dash about one after another in that dizzying whirl, Subaru found he couldn't just sit still, even though it was already nearing the dead of night.

"I should—"

"There's nothing for you to do-nya, is there?"

Before Subaru could finish offering to help with something, someone cut his resolve off at the knees. It was Ferris, hand pressed to a mouth caught mid-yawn, tears welling faintly at the corners of his eyes. Subaru shot a flat, narrow-eyed glare at that girlish face, and Ferris twitched the chestnut-brown cat ears atop his head.

"Arranging supplies, organizing the subjugation force—none of that's really Subaru-kyun's department, is it-nya? It's plain as day you'd just muck it up and make things complicated, so best behave yourself, hm?"

"Like hell I can just sit here. Everyone's running themselves ragged because I'm the one who said we should do this. And here I am—"

"There, right there—that's where you're wrong."

Ferris jabbed a finger at Subaru's mouth, cutting him off sharply. Subaru narrowed his eyes at having his words forcibly clipped, and Ferris flicked that same finger—still resting against his own lips—against Subaru's nose.

"That whole 'it's my fault' way of thinking—Ferri-chan doesn't much care for it, or, well, doesn't care for it at all-nya."

"Why not? I mean, really—"

Wasn't it Subaru's own words that set everyone working straight through the night in the first place? For the very person who'd started it all to just sit around waiting so shamelessly—

"The reason Lady Crusch decided to go after the White Whale is for Old Vil's sake, y'know."

Ferris murmured it abruptly, in a small, quiet voice. Caught off guard by the content, Subaru let out a blank "Eh?"

"Slaying the White Whale is Old Vil's cherished, long-held wish. The previous Sword Saint—Old Vil's wife—was killed by the White Whale, and apparently Old Vil wasn't by her side when it happened."

"The previous one…"

"He gathered every scrap of information on the White Whale like his life depended on it. Where it had appeared before, where it appeared after that. The season, the time, the weather, every condition he could line up—and at last he grasped something like a pattern, faint as it was. But nobody would listen to him…"

Alone, in solitude, Wilhelm bent bloodshot eyes over books and old records, hunting for his chance at vengeance against the monster that had killed his wife. How many nights had that old swordsman weathered through? —And that obsession bore fruit at last: Wilhelm gained a faint glimmer of insight into the White Whale's movements. And that was—

"The kingdom didn't have the stomach left to challenge something that had wiped out a subjugation force with a Sword Saint in it. And when he tried to rally other backers, he got nothing but silence. I think Old Vil must've felt pretty hopeless, hm."

Trying to avenge someone, only to be unable to even reach the feet of the hated enemy. Subaru knew that particular despair, born of helplessness, all too well—the sin of weakness never lets you off the hook.

"Seems like he even thought about throwing everything away and challenging the White Whale alone. I think he found it more shameful not to be able to fight than to lose. —Men are idiots, aren't they. I'm sure his wife never wanted anything like that."

"That's true."

The one who voiced agreement with Ferris was Rem, standing beside Subaru. Having listened silently to the conversation until now, she swayed her blue hair, gazed quietly at Subaru's profile, and pressed a hand to her chest.

"Rem wants the person she loves to stay healthy and well, always. Even if Rem were gone, she'd want to be remembered with a smile."

"…It's way too early to be talking about becoming a memory."

Unable to bear Rem's sentimental words, Subaru gave her blue hair a light bop with his fist. Then he opened that fist into a palm and slowly slid his fingers through her soft hair, stroking it. Rem accepted his rough gesture, narrowing her eyes fondly.

"And the one who reached out to Wilhelm-sama was—"

"Lady Crusch-nya."

Ferris let out an admiring sigh, as though recalling that moment.

"Lady Crusch really is such a kind person. Even someone who's despairing, grieving, someone nobody else would spare a glance—Lady Crusch just can't help reaching out a hand. Just like that time—oop, oop—"

Ferris hastily clapped a hand over his own mouth, sealing away whatever came next, and stuck out his tongue with an embarrassed laugh.

"Okay. No more of that. So, what I'm trying to say-nya is, this is all thanks to Subaru-kyun."

"Mine…?"

"We wandered off topic, but that's where we started, right?"

The conversation had leapt to Wilhelm's past, and Subaru remembered the topic he'd nearly forgotten. It had originally been about the source of all this frantic bustle preparing for the White Whale hunt.

"'Thanks to,' not 'because of.' Those two might look alike but they're completely different, you know? Now Old Vil can finally repay his wife. Even properly declaring his family name was—"

"Ferris. Your tongue is running a bit too loose, don't you think."

"Nya!?"

Ferris gave a small jump at the voice from behind, and turned around with a sheepish look to find an old gentleman standing there, hands clasped behind his back. He fixed narrowed eyes on the shrinking Ferris and said,

"Broadcasting a man's private shame like that hardly strikes me as good taste, don't you think?"

"It's less an exposé and more, well, Ferri-chan's own Anatomical Chart of Old Vil, or something like that…"

Ferris pressed his fingertips together, pouted, and drew out the ends of his words in a wheedling tone. With the cute looks and the cat ears, it was easy to be fooled—but he was a man. Naturally, such coquetry stood no chance of working on Wilhelm.

"In any case, one oughtn't chatter on so freely without the person's own permission."

"Yes, sir."

Cut off flatly, Ferris drooped his shoulders and slunk away in defeat. Watching him go, silence abruptly settled over the room. Having overheard Wilhelm's past without meaning to, Subaru felt awkward almost beyond bearing. He considered evening the score by spilling some embarrassing chapter of his own dark history, but that reeked of a cornered social invalid grasping at straws, and he abandoned the idea at the last second. As a result, the silence dragged on, and Subaru wracked his less-than-brilliant brain for some other topic—

"I apologize for letting you hear such an unpleasant tale. It's nothing but an old man's idle, worthless history. Please, forget it."

But Wilhelm spoke first. Seeing a bitter, listless smile touch his lips, Subaru resolved to respect his wishes. Don't ask anything more—that was the old man's wish. So Subaru wouldn't.

"—You truly loved your wife, didn't you."

—Rem-san!? He was rattled enough to mentally tack an honorific onto her name. That's how hard Rem had just gone and planted her foot straight into a minefield nobody saw coming.

Oblivious to Subaru's inward panic, Wilhelm raised his eyebrows, paused a beat, and said,

"Yes. I love my wife. More than anything, more than anyone, no matter how much time passes."

Wilhelm's confession carried all the weight of the years poured into it. Subaru had heard, from Wilhelm's own mouth, a few remarks in the past suggesting he doted on his wife. Each time, it had come through just how much Wilhelm treasured his family—but looking back now, knowing she was deceased, it struck him with an altogether different weight.

"I still have preparations to see to for tomorrow, so I'll take my leave. The two of you should get a good rest tonight as well."

Turning his back on the two of them, who'd fallen silent, Wilhelm said this and his figure receded.

"Tomorrow—"

Subaru found himself calling out to that retreating back before he knew it. The footsteps stopped, and to that back that didn't turn around, Subaru said,

"Tomorrow, both me and Rem are joining the fight."

"That's…"

"You think I'd just stand by quietly while an ally goes up against a powerful enemy? Don't worry, Rem can fight… and even I've got things I can do."

Firing off the words in rapid succession, Subaru cut off any denial before Wilhelm could voice it. And then—

"Let's team up and beat that whale bastard senseless! I'll help out with everything I've got!"

Thumb up, teeth gleaming, Subaru vowed to fight alongside Wilhelm. At that declaration, Wilhelm stayed silent for a moment, then said,

"My wife was a woman who loved to admire flowers."

It came quietly, an answer of an entirely different flavor than Subaru's vow had called for.

"She never cared for swinging a blade, and yet no one was more beloved by the sword than she. Permitted to live for nothing but the sword, my wife too accepted that fate."

Knowing the strength of Reinhard, the current Sword Saint, made it plain enough that the Divine Protection in question was more than any human body was ever meant to bear. It was so outlandishly immense that it endlessly narrowed down the very future, the very possibilities, of whoever was granted it.

"It was I who broke my wife's sword and made her cast aside the name of Sword Saint."

'A man without talent'—that was how Wilhelm had once described himself to Subaru. And it was precisely for that reason, he'd said, that he'd devoted half his life to the sword just to reach the level he stood at now. How many times had this old man tasted failure, how many times had his spirit been broken, before he achieved that long-cherished goal? And then—

"She cast aside the sword, became just one woman, and I took her as my wife. I believed that meant everything was forgiven—that she could go on living as Theresia, and not as the Sword Saint. —But the sword had never let her go."

Why had that woman—who was supposed to have cast the sword aside—joined the subjugation force against the White Whale? But Wilhelm's recollection never touched on that point.

"Subaru-dono, my thanks."

he said in a single breath, then continued,

"In tomorrow's battle, I will find my answer with my own sword. At last, I'll be able to stand before my wife's grave. At last, I can go and see her."

With those parting words, Wilhelm left the room, for good this time. Left behind, Subaru couldn't help but feel a kind of respect, man to man, for Wilhelm's resolve. A person really could carry a love that earnestly, that far.

"Subaru-kun…"

Of the two left in the quiet room, it was Rem's voice that suddenly dropped into the silence. He turned his gaze toward her without a word, and it caught hers, just as she looked up at him.

"If Rem were gone, would you remember her for just as long?"

"…That's such a bad omen I don't even want to answer."

He said it in a sulky voice and gave Rem's forehead a light poke with his finger. She raised a hand to the spot his finger had touched, and smiled, happy, as though she'd gotten exactly the answer she'd been hoping for.

The next morning. Time remaining until the White Whale subjugation: seventeen and a half hours.

"Count on me, big bro!!"

In the garden of the Karsten estate, still holding the cold air of early morning, that boisterous voice rang out at full volume—loud enough, it seemed, to reach every corner of the sprawling mansion. Getting hit with it point-blank, right in front of him, was more than Subaru could take. He clapped a hand to his ear and screwed his face into a magnificent scowl, glaring in protest, but—

"I heard all about it from the boss-lady! We're gonna get in on this whale hunt too today, so let's do this right, eh!!"

"Your voice is too damn loud!! Are those beady little eyes of yours not even registering my reaction!?"

Addressed in a voice like a gale-force wind, Subaru's own voice rose to match it despite himself. Receiving Subaru's utmost effort at volume with evident pleasure, the dog-faced beastman threw his sharp-fanged mouth wide open and laughed.

His whole body was covered densely in short, reddish-brown fur, with a deeper, scorched-brown patch running like a mohawk down his long, narrow head. His eyes were sharp, and rows of blade-like canines gleamed in his mouth, but there was a certain charm in the way the corners of his eyes crinkled when he burst into idiotic laughter. That said, he easily stood a good two meters tall, and the sight of his brawny, muscle-bound frame wrapped in what looked like black leather clothing gave off the distinct impression that wilderness and civilization had duked it out and finally reconciled.

By his own claim he called himself a kobold, but there was no way that build belonged to any kobold. Subaru's image of a kobold was a small person with a dog's face who walked on two legs. Two out of three traits matched, but changing just that last one apparently changed the whole impression this much.

"No good, Natsuki-kun. Ricardo's got ears that just don't pick up anything inconvenient. The trick to dealing with him well is to keep your distance while you talk."

"So that's why you're standing all the way over there even though you're the one who introduced us!! My carefully styled hair's getting messed up something awful! Not that it's long enough to really style, but still!"

Raking his short hair back and forth as he shrieked, Subaru voiced his complaint at Anastasia, who stood a safe distance away, not bothering to hide her stifled laughter. At Subaru's reaction, the beastman—Ricardo—gave his shoulder a nudge with a huge palm.

"Watch how you talk to the boss-lady, kid! She's my employer, so go a little easier on her!! Thing is, she can't hardly talk to anybody without money getting in the way, so she's starvin' for a regular friend—she's easy pickings right now!"

"Ricardo. You're no good at keeping secrets, so maybe don't go badmouthing me, hm?"

"That ain't badmouthing!! I'm just worried about you, boss-lady! Coming all the way out here from Kararagi with hardly anybody you know, you must be lonesome, right!? So here! Look! Ta-daaa, it's Friend Number One!!"

"Don't chatter away using me as the go-between!! And quit shaking a guy's head so hard! My neck's gonna come off!"

Shaken about by strength that was far beyond normal—in this case, "inhuman" in the literal sense—Subaru's neck joints started making genuinely worrying noises, so he made his escape. Practically rolling away out of Ricardo's reach, he worked his forcibly-twisted neck around, checking for strained muscles, and said,

"Close call, close call. Getting sidelined by an injury from small talk right before the big battle? Not funny. Even I can't accept that punchline, not with my spirits this pumped up…!"

"Man, what a weird kid. Here I am just tryin' to be friendly."

"That comes with a physical assault attached! I'm no good with guys who've got a stronger personality than me!"

Shaking his head peekaboo-style, Subaru kept his distance from the approaching Ricardo by circling around him. The two of them kept up their standoff, inching in a circle without the gap ever closing, while Anastasia looked on from outside the circle—an utterly baffling scene to any onlooker. And then—

"From the looks of things, introductions are already finished, it seems."

The one who spoke, stepping down into the garden, was the green-haired beauty—Crusch Karsten. Instead of her usual formal dress, she wore a lightweight suit of armor stripped of nearly all ornamentation. With the joints left open and mobility clearly prioritized, it seemed like something that might leave her defense a bit uncertain, but—

"Battle attire is better off easy to move in. Don't worry—this armor is inscribed with a Divine Protection of Earth. As long as my mana holds out, it's far sturdier than it looks."

"Man, magic and Divine Protections are still broken as hell in this world… Makes me wonder if maybe I've got some Protection lying dormant in me too, just waiting to wake up."

"However long one sleeps, one doesn't forget how to breathe, does one? For those who carry a Divine Protection, the Protection is just the same. If you have no awareness of one, you'd best give up the idea."

Someone had shot him down with much the same argument once before, a good while back, so Subaru pouted, clicked his tongue, and tossed the wish aside. Glancing sidelong at Subaru's childish reaction, Crusch looked up at the beastman standing across from him, scratching his head.

"I see. I'd heard talk of you, but you're an even finer soldier than the rumors suggested. So you are Anastasia Hoshin's—"

"I'm just hired help, ma'am. As for you, Miss Crusch—seein' you in person, you're really something else…"

Ricardo snorted as he looked back down at Crusch, arms crossed, taking in his huge frame. Wrinkling his muzzle, he let out a wringing, throaty laugh.

"Quite the specimen, ain't she. This Royal Selection thing's shapin' up to be a real headache, eh, boss-lady!!"

"That's exactly why I'm out here doing her favors like this. How big a price tag I get to write depends on how well you do your job, Ricardo."

Anastasia shrugged, and Ricardo exploded into booming laughter. Subaru grew anxious that Ricardo's blunt attitude might earn Crusch's displeasure, but Crusch, facing him, showed no sign of minding it at all. She glanced over at Subaru and said,

"Did you manage to rest last night?"

"Thanks for asking, yeah. Though it didn't feel great sleeping all carefree while you all were running yourselves ragged."

"The right person for the right task. As far as your job goes, it was already fulfilled the moment you gathered Russell Fellow, Anastasia Hoshin, and myself last night and settled us on subjugating the White Whale. Though it seems you have no intention of stopping there."

Crusch turned and looked straight at Subaru. Uncomfortable under that direct gaze, Subaru made a show of shrinking back, but—

"You intend to take part in the battle, I hear… Can you actually fight?"

"Can't fight, no. If you're counting me as a fighting force, that's cutting it a little too close even for 'wanting to borrow the paws of a cat,' I'd say. Make it 'the paws of a dog'—a big one."

"Wait, weren't you just talkin' about me!?"

"I wasn't!! Man, your ears really do only hear what's convenient!"

Subaru shouted back at Ricardo, who'd cut into the conversation, then scratched his forehead as if to reset himself, prefacing with a "That said—"

"Against the White Whale specifically, I think I could actually be pretty useful."

"Let's hear it. What's your basis for that?"

"I don't much want to believe it myself, but… apparently my body odor has some kind of property that draws Witchbeasts in."

Softening the nuance just slightly, Subaru laid out his plan for when he joined the fight. The Witch's Lingering Scent that clung to Subaru's body—it was unclear exactly how it had come to soak into his flesh, but just as with the Juggernaut, he could reasonably expect it to serve the role of drawing the White Whale's attention. The problem was that the White Whale's threat level was several dozen times that of the Juggernaut, and there was no way Subaru could evade it approaching on his own, let alone intercept it.

"So I figure if I get put in some fast Dragon Carriage or whatever and just keep tearing right past the White Whale's nose to keep drawing its attention… that'd be a way to use me effectively without anyone getting hurt."

Honestly, even Subaru had his doubts about the plan as he said it out loud. He was essentially offering himself up as live bait to be dragged around the battlefield, useless as a fighter but useful as bait. It was a role assignment that would make even someone with a death wish go pale, but—

"Astonishingly enough, there's not a hint of a lie in that."

Crusch, who'd had one hand at her chin and a half-doubting look in her eyes, let the tension go out of her shoulders. Her Divine Protection of Wind Reading must have laid bare the truth of Subaru's words, leading her to weigh the plan's actual usefulness. She gave a single nod.

"Very well. I'll have you use an Earth Dragon known for both speed and stamina. Ride double with Rem and there should be no trouble getting around. That said, you'll follow my instructions as a rule."

"Oh, so you're heading into the battlefield yourself too, then."

"Do you think I'm the sort who could sit deep in a chair back at the estate, waiting for good news?"

Crusch flicked a fingernail against a fitting on her armor, a fierce smile crossing her handsome, dignified features. At that dashing figure, Subaru shook his head and offered a small apology for asking something so obvious.

"—It's about time, I'd say."

Accepting Subaru's apology, Crusch murmured that with one eye closed. As if her words were the cue, the people involved began gathering into the garden one after another.

First to appear, out in front, was Wilhelm, changed into battle dress. The lightly-armored old swordsman wore only the bare minimum of protection, guarding just his vital points, with a total of six slender swords strapped at his waist, three to each side. Following behind him, Ferris was dressed in what looked like a curved, women's-cut suit of knight's armor, armed with nothing but a single dagger fixed at his hip—a stance that made sense given how, weighing his own abilities, he'd resolved to stick strictly to rear support.

Arriving a bit later was the man with dull gold hair, Russell. His face showed the fatigue of a night without sleep, but his eyes alone burned bright, betraying just how fired up he was. Out of the corner of his eye, Subaru watched Anastasia, already in the garden, join up with Russell and start some conversation, while Ricardo, rocking his huge frame, twisted his mouth into a ferocious grin.

Once the main figures had gathered, veteran soldiers whose names Subaru didn't know began filing in one after another—members, presumably, of the subjugation force Crusch had assembled. Perhaps only the principal figures had been called here, because the number of them was quite small, around ten. And more than that—

"This bunch looks awfully short on youth, somehow."

Subaru muttered the thought aloud just as it occurred to him. Right in front of him, the subjugation force members stood arrayed in a line behind Wilhelm, and their average age seemed to run considerably high. Their leader, Wilhelm, was past sixty, but the knights following him hardly looked to be under fifty either. Knowing Wilhelm's strength firsthand, Subaru had no doubts about the old swordsman's own skill—but what about the rest of them?

"It seems every one of them has some connection to the White Whale."

"Rem."

"Yes. Rem, Subaru-kun's Rem."

Subaru turned his gaze to Rem, who had suddenly reappeared at his side as if she'd sprung up out of nowhere. Same expressionless face as always, and despite heading off to battle, still dressed the same as ever—in her maid uniform. When he'd pointed that out earlier, at their morning reunion, she'd said,

"This serving outfit is handmade by Roswaal-sama—a combat maid uniform with a Divine Protection of warding woven into it. So there's nothing to worry about at all."

Or so she'd said. Going-out wear, cooking wear, chore wear, leisure wear, combat wear—quite the range. And yet the only option was ever a maid uniform, which said everything one needed to know about Roswaal's particular tastes. Anyway,

"Connection to the White Whale means… what, people tied to past subjugation forces, something like that?"

"It seems most of them have retired from the front lines, but they gathered in answer to Wilhelm-sama's call. Their skill and their morale both feel more than sufficient."

"Old soldiers burning for revenge, huh… that's the kind of thing that fires a guy up."

Even as he said it, Subaru wondered—was the fact that these people, each with their own history against the White Whale, had joined this subjugation force also a piece of that "kindness" of Crusch's that Ferris had spoken of the night before? If that put the operation itself at risk, it would defeat the whole purpose, but Crusch didn't seem the type to compromise on that front. And Wilhelm's own obsession surely wasn't soft enough to let him carelessly drag dead weight out onto the battlefield either. Depending on the situation, he might well weed out any excess baggage before they even reached the battlefield.

"Now that I think about it, that position this time is basically me…!"

"That's how Lady Crusch's subjugation force is arranged, yes… but what about those people over there?"

Setting aside Subaru, lips trembling in dread, what Rem was concerned with was the opposite side from the veteran soldiers of the subjugation force—a group spread out at the edge of the garden that had been giving off an atmosphere like a whole separate space for a while now. Following Rem's gaze to take in that group, Subaru let out an "Ah" of understanding.

"Anastasia's private soldiers… or rather, some mercenary company she's got hired, from what I hear. Apparently they're from Kararagi, which explains the distinctive way they talk, and, well—distinctive looks too."

"Distinctive all around, it seems. So that's them."

He'd probably get used to that Kansai-ish way of speaking eventually, but for Subaru, the look of them was a harder thing to get used to. The mercenary company led by Anastasia—starting with their leader, Ricardo—was made up entirely of animals that walked on two legs, in other words, a company built entirely of beastmen.

There was Ricardo, whose combat strength was obvious at a glance, and others with builds that looked like they could rival his; there was even one no taller than Subaru's waist, a bear-like beastman with fluffy white fur. Judging by the staff it carried, it was probably specialized more toward magic than direct combat. Around thirty of them stood lined up like that, doing a nice job of stoking Subaru's sense of being in an other-world fantasy.

"Since Anastasia herself can't fight, seems like she's lending the power of money instead. And apparently Ricardo's going to be hanging around me somehow, so try not to pick a fight, okay."

"As long as he doesn't do anything to harm Subaru-kun, Rem has no intention of stirring up trouble herself. Oh—Subaru-kun, the hair Rem worked so hard to fix up is all messed up."

"Ah, yeah, that jerk Ricardo messed it all up earlier."

"Let's beat him senseless."

"That's a fast jump to a conclusion!?"

A hairstyle that wasn't even mussed up all that badly nearly became a spark for conflict, and Subaru hurriedly held Rem back. Then he checked the time on a whim—it was nearly time to set out.

"But before that—"

A tense, crackling air began to settle over the garden, and one after another, the people gathered followed the already-lined-up subjugation force in straightening their posture. Crusch stepped forward, and the atmosphere shifted toward the sense that she was about to give a speech before departure. Slipping through the gap before that happened, Subaru, with Rem in tow, made his way over to the two conversing merchants.

"Figuring the two of you, as merchants, are the type who'll at least hear a guy out so long as the pay's right—I've got a little something I want to talk about."

He'd decided to lay one piece of groundwork before the White Whale hunt began.

"—Four hundred years."

The appointed hour arrived, and before the assembled warriors, those words announced the beginning.

A weighty resonance, a taut atmosphere. Amid a sharp sensation like pain shooting through a straightened spine, Crusch, drawing the attention of everyone gathered there, squared her shoulders with dignity. Planting into the ground a treasured sword engraved with the crest of House Karsten, and resting a hand on its pommel, Crusch swept her gaze over every face in a single breath.

"The worst calamity ever to strike this world. Since the rampage of the Witch of Envy, since the White Whale born of that Witch's hand made this world its hunting ground and trampled the weak as though it owned every inch of it—that much time has passed."

The Witch of Envy, who once destroyed half the world and whose name still lingers as a byword for terror. That Witch's servant, the mist-born Witchbeast that, even now that its mistress has been struck down, still revels in its freedom. A true monster, one that has claimed too many victims and swallowed down the fighting spirit of countless souls.

"The number of lives the White Whale has taken defies counting. Combined with the sheer maliciousness of that mist's nature, it should be said that no one truly knows the exact number of victims. Across four hundred years, the gravestones bearing names, and the gravestones that could not even keep a name, only continue to grow in number."

At Crusch's words, there were old soldiers who bowed their heads and gritted their teeth against sobs. There were old soldiers who dug their nails into clenched fists until blood welled up. There was an old swordsman who had stored up an inexhaustible passion inside his chest, and who had gone on quietly waiting for the chance to let that fury finally explode. Their regret, their grudges piled as high as the count of the dead, began to wreathe the air of the garden in a dark, murky shadow. —But,

"But those days of regret end today."

"—————"

"We shall end them. We will slay the White Whale, and bring an end to countless sorrows. To the sorrow that could not even reach the point of grieving, we shall grant a proper chance for tears."

"—————!"

"Let us put an end to this pitiful Witchbeast, already stripped of its mistress, yet still obeying an order that never ends."

Subaru's chest grew hot. Without a word, he could feel that everyone there was holding the same emotion in their hearts as he was. The old soldier who had bowed his head, the old soldier who had clenched his fist, the old swordsman who had shut his eyes—all of them now opened their eyes and gazed at Crusch, standing before them. Receiving the heat of all those gazes, Crusch thrust a hand forward and raised her voice.

"We march to war! —Our destination: the Lifaus Highway, Flugel's Tree!"

"—Yeahhh!!"

Answering voices overlapped, and the roar of feet stamping the ground created the illusion that the very garden shook. Swept up in the surging heat of fighting spirit, Subaru too shouted along at the top of his voice. Amid it all, stronger and higher than the rest, Crusch drew her sword and thrust it skyward, declaring—

"Tonight, by our own hands—we slay the White Whale!!"

The battle to subjugate the White Whale—the greatest operation since being summoned to another world—begins now.
