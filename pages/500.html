Arc 7 Chapter 3: "VS Rem"

"—Whoa, just like she said. What the hell, this is completely neglected."

Peering behind the tilted shelf, Subaru grimaced at his own handiwork. Having taken off his butler jacket and rolled up his shirt sleeves, Subaru struck the pose of a cleaner — though it was the kind of work that deserved "apprentice" or "amateur" stuck in front of it.

He'd been getting better at using his natural knack for handiwork to handle the manor's chores efficiently. Just as he'd been thinking that, it felt like his conceit had been pointed out directly. Watching Subaru's reflection from behind, a shadow gave a small snort through its nose. "Hah!"

"Good grief, Barusu is Barusu after all. Ram thought you'd grown and figured she'd let you spread your wings a little, and this is the sorry result... Be ashamed."

"Ah, I've got nothing to say back to that... wait, yes I do! Why are you sitting there so smug!? You haven't done enough work yourself to lecture me!"

"Ram, unlike Barusu, is genuinely good at working efficiently. That's why, back when the morning duties were being divvied up, she steered things so her own share would be the bare minimum."

"That's not the ability to work briskly, that's the ability to dodge work!?"

Winning without fighting, rather than winning by fighting, is the truest strategy of war. Put nicely, that was the vibe; put unkindly, it was superb slacking performance — but since Subaru hadn't managed to call it out back in the morning, there was nothing he could say now, after the fact. Still—

"Don't you think neither me nor sis wants the strain of that falling on Rem?"

"Hmph... cheeky, for Barusu. But you have a point."

Scratching his head pathetically as he said this, Subaru drew a narrowed glance from Ram. As their faces both turned toward the far end of the room, a figure was there, bustling busily about. The hem of her maid uniform's skirt swaying, a blue-haired girl was wiping down the room's small details with a damp cloth—

"—Rem."

"Ah, yes, sorry. I got a little absorbed in it. How was the back of the shelf, Subaru-kun?"

The girl, Rem, turned at his call and smiled softly, asking Subaru the question. Being on the receiving end of that smile made him feel bad about having to give such a pathetic answer, but there was no hiding it, so Subaru resigned himself at once.

"Yeah, it was just like you said. Even the places you can't see, properly... Guess the attitude of 'looks clean on the surface, so it's fine' isn't great."

"No, that's not true at all. When it comes to dressing things up on the surface, Subaru-kun has no equal. The best in the whole Kingdom!"

"That's the kind of statement that gets you sued for defamation!"

He knew Rem meant no harm, but the assessment stung all the same. Being good at glossing over the surface was indeed something Subaru was aware of about himself, and—

"As expected, you're good at seeing through to the essence of things, Rem."

"Shut up, sis! I'm a little self-aware about it already, don't push me down any further!"

Subaru shot a sharp look at Ram, who sat on the bed hugging her own arms. But she returned an even sharper look, and he retreated, tail between his legs. Rank and power both — here, Natsuki Subaru was at the very bottom.

"Damn, having a senior maid pick on you is rough... In that regard, Rem's so kind!"

"Yes, of course. Rem would never say anything harsh to Subaru-kun. Even if Rem doesn't say anything extra, Rem believes Subaru-kun will understand on his own."

"Being eaten alive by kindness, from the inside."

Sliced up on the outside by his sister's venomous tongue, and torn to shreds on the inside by Rem's sweet poison. He was starting to lose track of whether his situation was actually blessed, or he was just telling himself it was — but that, too, was probably just a matter of how he chose to look at it.

"Argh, don't put yourself on a pedestal, Natsuki Subaru. Sis aside, all you have to do is live up to Rem's expectations. I'll do it! Right, Rem!"

"As expected of you, Subaru-kun, that's the spirit! Rem is impressed!"

"Heh, this positivity is one of my strong points too. So, what should I start with?"

"Let's see. First, the window sash hasn't been wiped at all, so let's redo that. For the top of the shelf, don't just stretch up on your toes — use a step stool and wipe it thoroughly. As you saw, the back of the shelf doesn't need daily attention, but if you skip it too long, it becomes a real problem. Then there's the bed legs, and the underside of the mattress..."

"A barrage so intense I might topple forward!"

The pointers poured out of Rem like water off a tilted board, and Subaru's mental note-taking hands couldn't keep up. And yet — to think that a room which, by the standards of Subaru, reputed to be the Kingdom's best at surface polish, looked reasonably clean, still had this much room for improvement.

"Seriously, Rem notices all kinds of things. Is that part of being a proper maid too?"

"Of course. Ah, but if you think about it from the other person's standpoint, some things naturally come into view. Rem is always thinking about Subaru-kun, after all."

"I'm happy about that, but it means my dust-covered flaws keep getting spotted one after another..."

Rem's answer, delivered with a firmly clenched fist, was adorable, but Subaru felt awkward all the same. Still, having an eye sharp enough to catch the fine details, and the meticulous work to match, was proof — however much Rem might modestly deny it — that the fundamentals of maidhood had been thoroughly ingrained in her. Truly, the reputation of the all-around maid was no lie.

"Naturally. After all, Rem is Ram's cute little sister."

"Wait, why are you acting all triumphant, sis!?"

"Shameless confidence suits sis, and that's part of her charm too."

Ram, lounging haughtily atop the bed, drew such exchanges from Subaru and Rem back and forth. That, too, was just another everyday scene among the maids and servants working at Roswaal Manor—

△▼△▼△▼△

"—Guohh! Owww!"

A moment of weightlessness, then the pain of being caught by hard ground. He'd known he was falling and had braced for the landing, but the impact was substantial all the same. Especially unexpected was the follow-up blow from a thick branch lying where he rolled. Getting caught right around the shoulder blade brought unbidden tears to his eyes from the sudden pain.

"Ow, ow... ah, good thing there was no follow-up attack besides Mother Nature. Bless this knife..."

Rising to his feet with tears in his eyes, Subaru sheathed the knife in his hand and let out a breath. Turning around and looking up, he saw the trunk of the great tree that had, until a moment ago, been dangling him in midair. From one of its thick branches, a long vine stretched down toward the ground. The vine had been cut partway through by the knife, and at the end of the severed vine—

"A loop... I've seen this kind of thing in manga and stuff, but traps like this really do work."

Saying so, Subaru removed the vine loop tied around his right ankle. It was a trap set on the ground: step over the loop just right, and it would cinch tight around the foot and hoist it into the air — frankly, even having been caught in it himself, he had no idea how exactly the mechanism achieved that. And he had no time to spare investigating the answer.

"No memories, but the knowledge and dexterity Rem had are still intact, huh... Chasing after her like this, I'm reminded — Rem really is sis's little sister."

It was an obvious enough fact, but being made to feel it anew was another matter. Naturally, this wasn't the way he wanted to be made to feel it, so the sensation was bitter indeed.

—More than an hour has passed since Subaru entered the forest chasing after Rem.

Following the advice of the masked man he'd encountered in the forest on the other side, he'd managed to see through the disguised route Rem had taken while fleeing with Rui. Thanks to that, he could say with certainty that the distance between him and the fleeing pair had shrunk. However, the biggest factor keeping that distance from reaching zero was Rem's vigilance, which stood as an obstacle.

Wary of the Witch's Lingering Scent — the Miasma — clinging to Subaru, Rem hadn't stopped at disguising her footprints; she'd also set up traps all over the forest meant to hold him back. Since that first brutal hit, the number of traps Subaru had discovered (or, one might say, been hurt by) numbered no fewer than ten. To pull off setting that many while fleeing on legs that weren't free to move, and while dragging along a burden besides, was something Subaru could only tip his hat to.

"If it were just tying grass together, or a shallow pitfall, it'd almost be cute..."

Small traps meant to trip Subaru up or twist his ankle. That sort of trap did little damage and posed no great obstacle to continuing the chase. The trouble was that there were an inordinate number of them, requiring constant vigilance whenever walking through grassy patches or areas thick with fallen leaves.

"With Rem's arm strength, digging a pitfall deep enough to catch just one leg is a snap for her — that's the sticking point. The physical difference we were born with is showing hard."

Mixed in among these small traps were medium ones, more serious attempts to immobilize him using vines or fallen trees, like the one that had just strung Subaru up. If, by some miraculous stroke of luck, Subaru hadn't happened to have gotten hold of a knife, who knows how long it would have taken to escape that vine trap. Or his temper might have flared, delaying his resumption of the chase considerably. And what made Subaru hesitate most, more than his impatience—

"Shh!"

He drew the Guiltywhip from behind his waist and swung it, striking the suspicious patch of ground hard with its tip. The next instant, aimed at that very spot, came a branch strike with tremendous recoil — one blow, two, three in rapid succession. A direct hit would have broken an arm or two without question. A large trap meant to completely rob Subaru of his ability to act — not many of these had been set, but the mere fact that they existed at all was slowing his advance.

The single thick branch trap set at the forest's entrance — that, too, could be counted as part of these large traps. The deeper he pressed into the forest, the more the danger and power of the large traps he found kept increasing. That wasn't because Rem, being chased, was losing her mercy — it was because she was growing.

"The more she gets chased and keeps building traps, the more she's learning and getting better at it... Damn, as expected of you, diligent as ever, Rem. Just wish you weren't showing that off right now."

He'd known Rem was a hard worker, good at putting in effort, and it was heartening that even without her memories back, that quality of hers hadn't been lost — but that was a separate matter from this. Put simply, Rem was growing in the midst of battle. Given that Subaru's own growth had more or less plateaued, the already harsh gap in ability was only becoming more pronounced. He needed to get his hands on Rem before this gap became decisive, but—

"—————"

He disarmed the large trap in front of him and held his breath for a moment. Looking all around, searching, for the next escape route Rem had left behind after setting this. To find it, he needed to trace the few traces she hadn't managed to erase. Of course, it was possible she'd left no trace at all — but as things stood, Rem didn't have that much capability or leeway to spare. And so—

"—There it is."

In the gap between the trap-laden trees, he found a small mark where bark had been peeled away. Unrelated to the large trap, it was a modest scratch, like a cat clawing at a house pillar. And yet, such childish-looking scars had guided Subaru this far. In short, its true identity was the trace of the "burden."

"—————"

It was ironic, but the factor dragging on Rem's feet and allowing Subaru to keep up the chase was the presence of the Great Sin Archbishop she was fleeing with — Rui Arneb. He had no idea how well Rem and Rui got along, but these traces made clear that Rui wasn't being clever or cooperative with Rem's actions. No matter how desperately Rem erased her footprints, Rui was ruining it.

"Damn it..."

Even as he took note of this good news and used it to help his pursuit, Subaru's heart didn't lighten. Of course not. That Rui's presence was, as a result, helping Subaru along — given his relationship with that vile Great Sin Archbishop — was nothing he could be unreservedly glad about. Even if it wasn't Rui who had directly stolen Rem's "name" and "memories," the sin of the three siblings who were the Great Sin Archbishops of Gluttony was equal among them. None of them got to have a lighter sin just because.

Whether her own body didn't exist, or she'd erred in how she'd chosen to live — none of that mattered. That was Subaru's conclusion regarding Rui Arneb, screeching away in that white world. So even if he managed to catch up with Rem successfully like this, even once it came time to persuade her, Subaru had no intention of compromising on how Rui would be treated. In the first place—

"Why do I have to be playing tag with Rem like this...!"

Thinking about catching up to Rem and discussing how to handle Rui, Subaru bit his lip against the resurging anger at the unfairness of it all. He'd considered the possibility that Rem, upon waking, might have lost her "memories" and "name." Of course, it would have been best if the original, fully-intact Rem had come back — but given the precedents of Crusch and Julius, he couldn't hold much hope that Rem would wake up restored to her old self. That anxiety had proven correct, and as a result, Rem had forgotten both herself and Subaru. Even so, Subaru had believed he could hold his ground and support Rem.

Helping each other with Emilia, Ram, Beatrice, and the rest — his camp's companions — and supporting Rem together, all of them as one: that had been the basis for Subaru's resolve to stand firm. And yet now, Subaru was in a forest with no one to rely on, chasing after a fleeing Rem.

"Why does it turn out like this... why is it always..."

Why couldn't things ever be settled cleanly, all at once, without complication? Rem waking up having remembered everything, surprised at the time that had slipped away but weaving the story ahead together with him from there. That would have been fine. Even if, hypothetically, Rem's state stayed just as it was now, if he'd had companions around willing to work hard alongside him, he wouldn't have had to suffer through a calamity like this. That would have been fine too.

Fate always prepared the harshest road for Natsuki Subaru. And it aimed that road not only at Subaru, but at the precious people around him.

"—Haven't you whined enough already, Natsuki Subaru?"

Gritting his back teeth, Subaru slapped both his own cheeks hard with his hands. The sharp pain and shock rattled his consciousness, temporarily shoving aside the weak-hearted thoughts of a moment ago.

Yes, fate had always shown him a harsh road. And that was exactly why Natsuki Subaru had been struck again and again by the whip of hardship, and each time had spat blood and risen back to his feet, facing forward.

"A man who eventually turned the hardship standing in his way into his own whip — that's me."

Strictly speaking, the Guiltyraw that had become the raw material for that whip hadn't been as much of a wall standing in his way as that made it sound, and it ranked on the lower end of difficulty among the hardships he'd faced — but he talked big anyway. Talking big to psych himself up, stoking his emotions, pouring heat into his crafty little head to find a way to fight — that had always been Subaru's method. He'd stuck to it doggedly. There was no other way. So today, he'd do the same.

"Think, think, think, me. If I just keep chasing like this, eventually even Rem is going to notice what Rui's been doing. Once that happens, the trail goes cold. Before that happens..."

—He needed to either catch up with Rem and the others, or find a way to get ahead of them.

"—————"

He analyzed the difference in strength between them, thinking hard about his opponent's strengths and his own. Rem's strength in the current situation: the dexterity and thoughtfulness that hadn't been lost even without her memories. A growth curve that kept improving with practice, and the fact that her face and voice were adorable, and that he'd like to keep watching her move for even longer — but that part could wait. Against that, Subaru's strengths were his handling of the whip and knife, and the infuriatingly helpful clues Rui kept leaving behind, his bad eyes being a charming aside — and above all, that he knew what kind of girl Rem was, more than memory-less Rem herself did.

"...Rem must have noticed I'm chasing her."

The sheer number of traps proved it — Rem sensed Subaru's pursuit. Otherwise, there'd be no reason to prepare such an excessive number of traps. If she'd only wanted to be safe, she'd have placed a few at the start just in case and then prioritized fleeing. That she kept setting traps instead meant she was certain Subaru was chasing her. And the reason Rem could be certain of that was, again, likely the Witch's Lingering Scent.

"Just how much do I reek right now, exactly..."

He tried sniffing his own arm, and while he sensed some dustiness and sweat, it wasn't as if he were giving off some foul stench. Of course, there was also the fact that a person has trouble noticing their own smell — but in this case, the problem was that the Witch's Lingering Scent could only be sensed by those capable of sensing it at all.

He'd heard before, from Rem and Beatrice, that it faded day by day — but that it also increased dramatically right after, or with the frequency of, Return by Death. And Subaru had, over this past half-day, repeated "Natsuki Subaru ×2" inside the watchtower, and been flung straight to this place afterward. In other words—

"—In the history of my life in another world, I'm currently the most Witch-reeking man there's ever been."

Whether the Witch's Lingering Scent had a ceiling or not was unclear, but it stood to reason that Subaru right now was giving off quite the stench. If so, his pursuit was surely already found out. No matter how much distance she put between them, if it never actually widened, Rem was bound to grow impatient too. Normally, provoking impatience to draw out the opponent's mistakes was one of Subaru's go-to tactics — but since he wanted to build a new, friendly relationship with Rem, he wanted to avoid pushing that too far.

"Rob Peter to pay Paul..."

Living that saying out to the letter summed up just how bad the current situation was. Thinking that, Subaru meanwhile disarmed two, three more small and medium traps, tracing the clues Rui had left behind as he chased after Rem's trail. It really did feel like Hansel and Gretel, picking up breadcrumbs. Except Subaru was alone, and the difference was that here, the pair of two was the one running away.

"Oh, and this time it's somewhere easy to spot. Next..."

Finding a strip of peeled bark, Subaru fixed his next direction. Since Rui had no intention of signaling his location to Subaru, the clues he left behind had no consistency and were, more often than not, very hard to spot. The truth of the matter was probably that, while Rem was busy working on her traps, Rui — left off to the side — was doing this on his own. There'd been a stretch of hard-to-find clues, but it was a relief to get an easy one now.

"Glad it's still there. If Rem finds it and gets rid of it, the trail would..."

He started to say "go cold," but stopped himself mid-sentence. Then he went back to the tree he'd just passed and looked at the one with its bark peeled away. It was a large, sturdy tree, and the peeled spot was in quite a conspicuous place. Could Rem really have overlooked this?

"—————"

Being thrown off by the footprints in the meadow, Rem's gradually improving skill at trap-making, and now this bark, peeled off in an almost showy, look-at-me way. These combined in Subaru's mind, completing a puzzle of unease. Eventually, a single answer emerged within Subaru.

"If it's the Rem I know..."

Sharp-eyed and thoughtful as she was, Rem would have erased any blatant trace like this. Given that it had been left behind, unless Rem had fallen into some serious tunnel vision—

"—There!"

Ahead on the path he was about to take, Subaru pulled up a thick clump of grass from underfoot and flung it. Its roots tangled with dirt, it traced an arc through the air and dove into the tall grass ahead—

—the very next instant, with a tremendous roar, the grass patch caved in, and a great pit swallowed the ground whole.

"Whoa...!"

A hole so large it made all the small traps up to now look like nothing appeared right in front of Subaru. And the trap didn't stop there. Aimed at the newly appeared pit, the surrounding trees shrieked and snapped, toppling in. The fallen trees crashed one after another into the great pit, and the freshly opened hole was buried over almost instantly. Had Subaru fallen into that pit, he'd be buried right now, unable to move, buried alive.

Here, at last, a grand technique had been unleashed, one that used all the previous traps as a psychological hook. The very thing that had served as a hint for his pursuit had been, all along, lying in wait with its jaws wide open, ready to bury Subaru alive. It was tempting to praise it as a very Rem-like method — but—

"—The Rem I know doesn't stop there."

Setting a psychological trap, and if the pursuer falls for it and is stopped in his tracks, that's the ideal outcome. But the Rem Subaru knew was thoughtful and hardworking, with an adorable face and voice, the kind whose earnest efforts alone were enough to warm his heart, and—

"If you run out of patience, you come at them yourself. —Isn't that right, Rem!"

Saying so, he turned and looked up at the tree with the peeled bark. It was exactly at that moment.

"—!!"

That Rem, teeth gritted, leaped down at Subaru from that tree's branch.

△▼△▼△▼△

Unable to shake his pursuer, and with her traps failing to hold him back. In such a situation, what would the Rem Subaru knew do?

Once she knew his position by smell, and the identity of the breadcrumbs he'd been relying on had been discovered, she would turn that around, set a trap, and try to cut off the cause directly. And Subaru's read on this proved correct. The problem was—

"—Haaaaaah!"

The gap in strength between him and the leaping Rem meant he couldn't stop her.

With a pained cry of "Guoah!" Subaru was sent flying by Rem's arm as she came down on him. Frankly, that he'd managed to move this far on legs that weren't fully free, and that his own body had reflexively tried to catch Rem, were both things Subaru hadn't anticipated.

"You just won't quit, no matter what!"

"W-wait, Rem, listen to me..."

"Enough talk!"

Her swinging arm connected, and Subaru's nose began to bleed. But his bloodied plea went unheard by the furious Rem. She lay sprawled on the forest floor, glaring up at Subaru with those blue eyes.

"If you'd given up on us back there, I wasn't going to do anything more. And yet you kept chasing us... Stop it!"

"Put that simply, it really hurts, you know..."

"My nose feels like it's about to twist off! I can tell the moment you get close. And it's even worse now than back at the meadow..."

Pressing a hand to his bleeding nose, Subaru staggered to his feet. Subaru standing, Rem sprawled on the ground — the situation looked to favor Subaru, but that was an advantage that could be overturned by arm strength alone. Worse, if Rem started moving nimbly on just her arms, like some kind of scuttling thing, Subaru really would be knocked into the great pit this time, and that would be the end of it.

Carefully gauging the distance between them, there was nothing to do here but clear up the misunderstanding.

"Rem, listen to me. I apparently reek something awful to you, but..."

"...Yes, you reek."

"That takes me back...! You reek, I get that, and I get that it feels like something bad to you, but I don't have any hostility toward you!"

Raising both hands, Subaru showed that he had no intention of opposing her. But even with that claim, Rem's wariness didn't fade. That was how much the Witch's Lingering Scent was driving her, empty as she now was, into a corner. Truly, no matter how you looked at it, anything to do with the Witch never brought anything good.

"There's the smell, sure, and I know my first impression is bad. Even so, I've been living with myself for eighteen years. So let me start over."

"...Start over?"

"I was wrong. To you, who's forgotten everything and is scared, I couldn't explain a single thing. It was all because of my own circumstances, and I couldn't take your feelings into account..."

His impatience and anxious haste — those were the reasons he hadn't been able to be considerate of Rem. It would be easy enough to defend himself by blaming it on those. But what was the point of such a hollow, self-serving excuse here?

What was needed wasn't words to protect himself. What was needed were words to plead his case, to loosen Rem's hardened heart.

"You matter to me. I just want to protect you. So please, listen to me. Don't refuse me. —Give me one more chance."

"—————"

Hands still raised, Subaru bent deeply at the waist right where he stood, pleading. He didn't know if he'd managed to pour his whole heart and sincerity into it, but he'd put everything he felt into it. If that could shake Rem's heart even a little, if it could buy him time to explain—

"—Is that all?"

"...Huh?"

"Is that all the explanation you have for me?"

At that response from Rem, Subaru raised his head hesitantly. The emotion in her voice was different from what he'd hoped for. But it was also different from what he'd feared, and that brought only confusion.

Rem's voice was colored with a quiet anger, an unbearable fury.

"R-Rem...?"

"That you chased us down, and that your body is giving off an unbelievably wicked smell — of course that's suspicious, of course that's strange. But."

She cut herself off there, pressing her lips tightly together. Even so, toward Subaru, who still didn't understand what would come next, her blue eyes flashed with hostility, as if she regarded him as a truly unforgivable villain.

"More than any reason you could give, I can't get past the fact that you tried to abandon a little girl like that. How am I supposed to trust someone so heartless and vile?"

"—Ah"

At that gaze accusing him of wickedness, Subaru lost his words. The words struck home, sinking into his brain, and Subaru understood that from the very first move, in a way entirely unrelated to the Witch's Lingering Scent, he had chosen wrong in trying to earn Rem's trust.

The bad eyes he'd inherited from his parents, the lingering scent thrust upon him against his will — neither of those was the true reason he'd lost Rem's trust. It wasn't something he'd been born with; Subaru had damaged her trust through his own actions. Even if that "little girl" was in truth the embodiment of wickedness, a Great Sin Archbishop — from the eyes of someone who knew nothing, she was a young, helpless girl. That was what he had overlooked.

"—————"

Subaru couldn't come up with an answer in the moment for what to say. Having overcome hardship again and again — sometimes failing to overcome it and losing his life instead, then searching for a solution from a different angle — even so, in this moment, Subaru had no answer within himself.

Apology, excuse, or truth — which should take priority?

Apologize for the fact that he'd tried to abandon a child? Make excuses, insisting there'd been a reason for it? Confess the truth outright, that she was a young monster?

Whichever he chose, he didn't feel it could change the distrust in Rem's eyes before him. And that was already the fixed result of Subaru's actions, sealed by Return by Death.

"—You've gone quiet."

His gaze wandering, his cheeks stiff, unable to speak — Rem ran out of patience with him. She hoisted herself up, trying to put distance between herself and Subaru. She didn't seem to intend to strike Subaru down here to eliminate any future threat. Perhaps she thought that a Subaru this crushed wouldn't come chasing after them again.

Of course, that wasn't true at all. Even if he were shaken off here, Subaru would keep holding out his hand until Rem chose to take it. There was no such thing as a goodbye here. There was no such thing — and yet—

"—Re—"

He tried to call out to Rem as she turned to leave. He would say her name first, without thinking at all about what words would follow, just to call out to her. And then—

"—————"

As Subaru reached his hand toward the back of the twisting Rem, something changed in his field of vision. Beyond the dense trees, a shadow flickered faintly — one he recognized.

"—Rem!!"

Faster than the thought "it's coming," Subaru instinctively threw himself at Rem's back. Startled by his sudden movement, Rem's small body went rigid. It was the moment he wrapped his arms around that small body to shield her.

—An arrow, loosed from a mighty bow, tore through the air overhead, and a great tree, shot clean through the center, was blown apart.
