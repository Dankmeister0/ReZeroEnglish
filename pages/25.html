Arc 2 Chapter 1: "Unfamiliar Ceiling, Endless Hallway"

For Subaru, waking up groggy was something of a foreign concept. The moment his eyelids opened, what leapt into view was a white glow with an artificial feel to it. An unfamiliar ceiling, fitted with some kind of crystal that — by what principle, he had no idea — gave off a faint light illuminating the room. He had a well-earned reputation for waking up well — once his eyes opened, his mind snapped fully awake almost instantly. That was just how Subaru was built.

"...This pillow feels different."

Rolling over, he noticed the sensation under his head wasn't what he was used to. And this fluffy, cozy feel was nothing like the futon he was used to sleeping on — the one he never bothered folding up, to the point the floor beneath it had actually discolored.

"On top of that, it smells amazing. Sniff, sniff... smells like sunshine! Well, technically that's actually the smell of mites getting roasted to death by the sun, but still."

That fact ranked somewhere in the top three things he'd have preferred never to learn in his life. Having, in a sense, thoroughly savored the scent of death, Subaru finally hauled himself upright. Propping himself up on one arm, he looked around — and found himself in a room that screamed upper class at a glance. The bed he'd been sleeping in looked like it could comfortably fit five Subarus, and with the bed as its center, the room itself looked to be about twenty tatami mats in size. If this were a rented one-room apartment with twenty mats of floor space, it'd be an outrageous waste of square footage. But—

"—going by the standard tropes of a mansion, that's just how it's supposed to be, I guess."

Fully awake now, Subaru climbed down from the bed and did a quick once-over to check his physical condition. First, his shoulders and the various joints of his arms and legs, then high knees and squats to check his cardio and stamina — already gasping for breath, he pulled up his clothes to check—

"My stomach wound... nope, nothing there. No bruising, and of course no gutted-open scar either. No stitches either — the surgery in this world must be top-notch. If I ended up with a scar, I'd never be able to get married."

Cooking, laundry, sewing — every kind of housekeeping skill, he had ALL of them unlearned. His main selling points were sturdiness and a healthy appetite for anything. So then, was there some fine gentleman out there willing to take a bride like Subaru?

"If I were him, I'd pass for sure. ...Anyway."

The room, just as he'd first thought, was simple aside from the bed — a potted plant by the window, and some furnishings and paintings decorating the walls. He couldn't be sure such things even existed in this world, but he saw nothing resembling a clock, and no calendar either.

"The question is how long I was out cold... yeah, about five hours, maybe?"

He touched his own chin, gauging roughly how much time had passed by the feel of his stubble. It was a nifty trick for telling time fairly accurately without a clock. Going by the feel of his stubble-clock, roughly five or six hours had passed since he'd last checked. In other words, it was around the time the first day since his summoning to another world was drawing to a close. Having thought that far, Subaru touched his stomach once more, and—

"Anyway, one way or another... does that mean I managed to avoid Return by Death this time?"

Voicing the conclusion he'd been unable to bring himself to state since waking, he finally steeled himself to face the reality he could no longer look away from.

Just before he'd lost consciousness, he'd counted off on his fingers, replaying everything that had led up to that point.

"Died pathetically the first time, died bravely the second time, pulled off a pretty flawless dog's death on the third, and on the fourth I bit it from a stray shot after watching a death match to the bitter end — that's roughly how it went, but looks like I got to keep my life this time, and that's a relief. If I'd died after all that, I'd have been a total extra."

That every single cause of death was something like that was, well, part of his charm. In any case, on the bed in that room too spacious to feel at ease in — its neatly made sheets and comforter now thoroughly rumpled — Subaru sat cross-legged, lost in thought. Now that he'd avoided Return by Death, this place was, without question, different from his usual restart point. There was maybe a one-in-ten-thousand chance this was the greengrocer's home, where he always restarted, but the possibilities he could actually consider right now were—

"Either Reinhard's place, or Emilia-tan's place."

Those two were the only people well-disposed toward Subaru who also seemed like they might own a mansion like this. Probably the latter, Subaru figured, his own wishful thinking mixed in. The treatment of his stomach wound — that was probably Emilia's magic. If treatment hadn't been administered right then and there, Subaru's DEAD END would have been unavoidable, he figured, and more than anything, a man's heart wants to believe he was healed by a girl's own hands.

"I mean, it'd be fine if it turns out that pretty boy can also heal people... but a guy's allowed to dream a little, right? It's another world, after all~"

He poked at the sheets with his finger, offering that brief, girlish assessment. Then Subaru bounced himself up, using the bed's springs to land on the floor. For now, he wanted to figure out exactly where he was.

"In that case, this is the start of a brand-new adventure. ...No, no, come on, me! Feeling more annoyed than excited — are you seriously thinking about going back to your shut-in days!? Those days where all you did was eat, play games, watch anime, sleep whenever you felt like it, and lurk on forums posting comments... man, I wanna go back."

It wasn't like he'd had some deep-seated wish to be summoned to another world. For that matter, he hadn't had any complaints about his shut-in lifestyle to begin with. He'd never once thought his dull, uneventful days were wasted, and being able to sleep as much as he wanted was basically the NEET life's crowning glory.

"I mean, sure, I felt a little bad for my parents... but in a way it was more like they'd just given up trying to stop me, or were tacitly letting it slide."

He worked out normally, ate meals at the table, went out whenever he pleased, and mooched allowance money like it was nothing. Aside from not going to school, nothing about his life was hindered, so strictly speaking, Subaru was less a shut-in and more a truant.

"What a shocking revelation. If I hadn't had this chance to reflect on myself in another world, I might have gone my whole life never realizing I'm not actually a shut-in... could it be that this is why I'm here...?"

He resolved firmly to be more careful about how he described himself from now on. He didn't want to carelessly self-identify as a shut-in and get chewed out by the World Shut-In Certification Association or whatever.

"I bet every single member of that association is a shut-in too, though... wait, if that's the case, how do they even coordinate with each other...?"

One mystery calling forth another in an endless loop of thought. Tilting his head, Subaru tossed aside his long chain of reasoning with a "who cares," and crept toward the door. Ideally, while he was killing time, he'd have encountered someone coming to check on him — gotten an explanation of the situation and a grasp on where things stood. That would have been the ideal.

"Or, you know, the classic where you wake up and there's a girl by your bedside who was nursing you, going 'Oh, you're awake?' I mean, that's usually how it goes, right? Summoning me and then not even having the beautiful girl who summoned me at my side — this whole summoning gig is looking pretty sloppy..."

If there was one point in its favor, it was that, as per the standard conventions of an isekai fantasy, it had at least gotten the part right where every single person he met was good-looking. Anyway, among the people he actually knew by name, there were only five. Tonchinkan was clearly an extra if he'd ever seen one, and one of them was, at best, a wrinkled old geezer.

"Felt seemed like she'd shine if you polished her up, and Emilia-tan and Reinhard go without saying. Elsa is... whoa, my fingers are trembling, no count!"

His trauma gauge filled right up, so she got ruled off the scale entirely. He'd admit that, looks alone, she was gorgeous, but with a killing addiction thrown in, he couldn't quite bring himself to a cheerful "that gap is what makes her charming!" Not even he could manage that.

"Well then, now that I've reached a conclusion, guess it's time to bid farewell to stalling and set off on my journey... starting something new is super exciting!"

Muttering that casually under his breath, he carefully turned the doorknob and opened the door. Cool air crept into the room from outside, and his bare feet let out an overblown "fwuh" at the sudden chill of the floor.

Stepping out of the room, he found a long hallway painted uniformly in warm tones spreading before him. Both left and right, the path stretched on and on, with similar doors dotted along it at intervals. Every one of those doors probably led to a room built just like the one Subaru had been sleeping in — and if there were this many rooms of twenty-mat size, this mansion was bigger than he'd imagined. Unable to help himself, Subaru swallowed hard, imagining just who owned this place.

"Wow, that's all I can really say. Super spacious, more like vast. And yet, as huge as this place feels... there's not a hint of anyone around."

Walking barefoot down the hallway — its smooth, polished flooring (a bit like a school hallway) — with soft patting footsteps, Subaru couldn't help but frown at the silence. "A hint of anyone" was a figure of speech, but in this case, maybe it'd be more accurate to call it the sounds of daily life — either way, nothing of the sort reached Subaru here in this hallway.

"Same as that room earlier — it's way too quiet... quiet enough that making noise almost feels like it'd be a crime."

Normally, Subaru's personality was the type to shout "Anybody here?!" at the top of his lungs, but given his current situation, even that felt like a risky move. After all, at this point, Subaru hadn't the faintest grasp of his own situation. A moment ago he'd simply assumed this was the mansion of someone friendly toward him, but it wasn't impossible that, after he'd lost consciousness, Elsa had assembled some hundred-thousand-strong army and come back for a counterattack. If that were the case, then it was Elsa's side that had captured Subaru, and the mansion would instantly class-change from the home of some kindhearted tycoon into a hideout for a band of assassins.

"Guess I really should've waited for them to make the first move. Should I go back to the room now, hug my knees, shake like a leaf, and pray to whatever gods are listening?"

Gazing at the furnishings decorating the hallway too, Subaru began humming to himself as he plotted his retreat. The paintings inside the room had looked like landscapes, but the ones decorating the hallway were mostly odd, abstract works. Looking at the geometric patterns, he put on airs of knowing something about art, muttering "Hmm, there's something about this brushwork," and then—

"No, no, missing out on a flag with that kind of weak-kneed thinking just won't do, me. I decided to take things more positively, didn't I? I swore to it, didn't I? Never mind that I don't actually remember deciding that, or who I supposedly swore to — I'll just fill that in mentally."

Erasing the option of turning back, Subaru set off, aiming for now to find someone. Right or left — he chose right without much hesitation. He recalled some manga once having a line along the lines of "Left's got more traps, heh heh." On calmer reflection, which direction counted as left or right would flip entirely depending on which room you came out of, but he only realized this after walking a while, so he quietly buried the thought.

"Still, it's just door after similar door... and still no sign of anyone."

He hadn't reached a dead end either, and after passing about five doors, Subaru grew confused. There had to be a limit to how big a place could be. In terms of time, it had only been a minute or two, so someone might tell him not to whine so soon — but in that time, he'd easily covered three hundred meters. Normally speaking, could a hallway really stretch three hundred meters without a dead end in sight? This was just too strange, no matter how you looked at it — scratching his head, Subaru casually glanced back. At worst, he figured, going back to the original room might be unavoidable, and—

"Huh?"

Letting out a startled voice, Subaru tilted his head even further in bewilderment. Behind him — the path he'd just walked — the fact that its far end was also out of sight was the same as before, but the problem was—

"Wasn't this painting... the same one I saw in front of the room I first set out from...?"

He clearly remembered joking about "this brushwork," so there was no mistake. There was a chance it was simply the exact same piece displayed twice, but lining up similar rooms along a single hallway and decorating them with the same painting would be in seriously poor taste.

"Which means, to put it more plainly... this is a loop."

Probably, once you moved a certain distance down the hallway, you got quietly teleported to the opposite side of the map. Or else — flipping the usual isekai fantasy conventions on their head — there was some moving-floor trick operating subtly enough that Subaru hadn't even noticed.

"If it's the moving-floor pattern, that throws the whole historical accuracy right out the window. ...Still, between this and Return by Death, seems like I've got quite the connection to time-loop stories, huh."

Muttering that retort to no one in particular, Subaru opened the door of the nearest room. Inside was a plain twenty-mat room with nothing but a bed. In other words, the very room Subaru had originally been laid to sleep in.

"So basically, the message is that moving around is pointless, so just sit tight?"

So to keep Subaru from doing whatever he pleased once he woke up, someone had gone and restricted where he could go, like this? It felt like a wild waste of effort and magic power, but maybe the aim was also to flaunt an overwhelming difference in power and sap away any will to resist.

"Well, sure, thinking about it normally, that probably would sap someone's will to resist. Get hit with a bizarre space like this, and of course you're going to be freaked out. Thinking about it normally, anyway."

With an "mmm," he stretched his body, did some knee bends and leg stretches. Bracing a hand against the wall, he thoroughly loosened up his Achilles tendons, warming his muscles and getting himself into condition.

"Normally I'd be all 'no way, this is too much, yahoo!' and dive straight for the bed, going full speed into lazy-sleeping-until-someone-shows-up mode, but..."

He crouched down in the middle of the hallway, settling into a crouching starting position. On your mark, get set—

"When you've slept half a day straight, you're not the least bit sleepy anymore!!"

With that thoroughly uncool declaration, Subaru took off running with everything he had.

His speed was below Felt's — honestly, below old man Rom's too. His endurance was poor, the kind of sad stamina that gave out at the seventy-meter mark of what should've been a hundred-meter dash. But it was more than enough to sprint through this one moment.

He ran, and ran, and ran. Onward, again and again, without end. The end of the hallway still wouldn't come into sight; no matter how far he ran, the same scenery kept looping back. It meant his effort was futile, mocking Subaru's desperation. But Subaru didn't stop running. As if to show, through sheer attitude alone, that plodding on, straight ahead without wavering, was the one and only thing an ordinary man like him could do.

By now, whether the hallway had no one in it, or whether this was someone else's mansion, or the fact that his stomach had been slashed open half a day ago, or that he was starting to need to use the bathroom — none of it mattered anymore.

"No wait, that last one is NOT fine! Not fine at all! Crap, before I knew it the urge came surging up something fierce! Both the big one and the small one, at the same time!"

With a tight squeeze, Subaru's boy parts began to cry out in agony, and whatever crisp, dashing form he'd had a moment ago vanished without a trace. His running stance evolved, ever so slightly pigeon-toed, upper body swaying with an affected wiggle, into something like a girl's mincing run.

But the world had no intention of letting up on mocking his efforts, treating a difference like his running style as a trivial matter. Against that repetition, almost malicious in its persistence, Subaru's legs finally began to slow. Shoulders heaving with ragged breath, sweat even beading on his forehead — in the midst of this lonely battle, having gained not a single thing, Subaru sank to his knees on the spot. And then—

"There's... no choice... but to do it here."

Casting everything aside, he put his hands to the clothing guarding his lower half. His dignity as a human being might be lost. Not even able to relieve himself where he ought to — if he wasn't careful, he'd end up worse-trained than a dog or cat. But he'd accept even that abuse, gladly. It wouldn't break Subaru's spirit.

"Besides, for a man who's just discovered a new fetish, that kind of abuse might just turn out to be pleasurable——"

Declaring that with the first refreshing smile he'd worn since being summoned to this other world, Subaru lowered himself to sit right there. That he at least moved to the edge of the hallway was the one faint sign of reason he had left. —And then.

"Natsuki Subaru, here it comes—— huh?"

Right as Subaru braced himself to push, a change occurred before his eyes. The scenery that had kept repeating itself no matter how far he ran — that same unchanging hallway — suddenly warped and buckled within his field of vision. It was as if the world had been crumpled up in a fist like a sheet of paper, then immediately smoothed back out. As if to confirm this newly un-wrinkled scenery, Subaru blinked, and stood back up while holding back the urge that had reflexively retreated.

"Not really sure what just happened, but... all's well that ends well?"

Looking around, he found himself in a world where the end of the hallway was now visible. Directly ahead of Subaru was the abstract painting he'd seen so many times before, and in front of that painting was the room where he'd been laid to sleep. There was no mistaking he'd been sent back to his starting position, but it seemed he'd broken free of the loop after that. The clear condition had been hard to figure out, but—

"In short, the key to victory was my need to pee, huh. Now that's a convoluted flag. There's no way you'd notice a condition like that going in normally... whoever came up with this is straight-up demonic."

"Demonic" here was a coined variant of "divinely inspired," more or less. Not so much something Subaru had invented, more like he'd just blurted out whatever popped into his head.

"Well, now that I've finally escaped the loop, guess I'll head back to the room and sleep..."

Scratching his head, he glanced back at the first door. And then, whether he was imagining it or not, a distinctly chilly sensation crept into the air of the hallway. Nothing was said in words, but the feeling behind it came through pretty straightforwardly all the same. Apparently, he'd earned someone's disapproval.

"Someone's out there willing me to go back, go back, and then when I finally exceed their expectations, I just toss the whole thing aside. ——Yeah, I can see how that'd tick someone off. ...Fine, I get it! I'm going!"

Hurling an irritated shout at whatever unseen cause was behind the loop, Subaru started walking. Fundamentally crushing whatever scheme was at play here had been an option too, but that wouldn't move the event forward, and besides, the urge to relieve himself was still very much ongoing.

"In other words, to protect my dignity, I've got no choice but to press on. ——Whoever's behind this really gets it, don't they. That I'm the type who just can't back down!"

He declared this with a triumphant look. Yes — Subaru would fight. For the sake of his bladder.

The hallway's loop had ended, but as if in exchange, it was now wrapped in a strange atmosphere. That distinctive feeling struck Subaru as somehow similar to the sensation he'd felt when Emilia was about to cast magic. It was nothing but a total amateur's hunch, unreliable as that was, but in short, this hallway was saturated with mana. Unlike whoever had set up the teleportation loop, this presence wasn't even trying to hide itself. Whoever the caster was, they had to be quite confident in themselves — confident enough to set up a trap and openly declare, "Here's a trap, come get it," without a hint of concern.

"Charging in blindly is dangerous. But then again, too much caution is basically the same as cowardice. And more than anything, I'm on a time limit here..."

The wave had receded for now, but it was common knowledge that this same wave would eventually return as an even higher one. Holding his breath and wiping the sweat from his brow, Subaru steeled his resolve and took the first step. He turned the doorknob of the door before him, and—

"A hallway packed with traps after a whole bunch of looping. In a classic dungeon event like this, surprisingly enough, the very first door often turns out to be the goal."

Opening the door on a hunch that amounted to little more than a passing thought——

"...Honestly, what an infuriating man, right down to his very core."

Inside an unfamiliar library, he was met with the resentful mutterings of a curly-haired girl staring straight at him.
