Arc 4 Chapter 71: "Ending List"

Shallow but sharp, the blade tore through skin and drove inward, and from the wound, life came pouring out.

The gushing crimson blood spattered the green grassland in mottled patches, and before the purple-haired young man looking down, Subaru could see his own body convulsing reflexively. Eyes rolled back to white, foam bubbling at the corners of his mouth, his throat kept disgorging a tremendous amount of blood. Gradually the flow began to slacken, and just as a sound like breath escaping was heard—

'————'

Subaru understood clearly, now, that his past self had just lost his life. It wasn't as though there was some shared sensation between his consciousness-only self and his past self. And yet, even so, the raw, visceral feeling of his throat being torn open kept echoing back at him — at the Subaru who was consciousness alone, soul alone — as a reverberation that would not fade.

"Emilia-sama, please... wipe his face. Subaru's face."

"————"

"Not by me — he would want it done by you. If nothing else, let it be your hand."

Wiping clean his blood-soaked knight's sword and sheathing it, Julius called out to the stupefied, dazed Emilia. At the feet of Subaru, who lay collapsed on his back, the silver-haired girl sat crumpled, her knees given out beneath her. Her violet eyes, stripped of expression, could not accept reality, and the tear-tracks wetting her cheeks went unwiped, glimmering as they caught the light.

At the sight of her, Subaru's chest — nonexistent as it was — was gouged by a sharp pain, for who knew how many times now. Emilia's grief-stricken face raised up the punishment Subaru had turned his eyes from, bared its fangs, and scraped away at the callous way he had carried himself until now.

"Su... baru..."

Slowly, with a crawling motion, she reached her hand toward Subaru's face, and Emilia's palm gently wiped at the face fouled with blood and vomit. Bare-handed, heedless of the filth, she struggled to make the anguished, twisted expression on his face something bearable to look at, and once she had wiped away the blood, Emilia said,

"Why...? Why would Subaru do... something like this...?"

A question mark. To a listener who would never answer again, Emilia flung out a question like empty words into the air. No ear to hear it, no mouth to answer — none of it functioned anymore. To the corpse that Subaru had become, not a single one of Emilia's words reached him anymore.

'————'

Looking down on the scene, Subaru recalled, searching his memory, when exactly this scene — different from the one before — had taken place.

—It was the aftermath of the second battle with Petelgeuse, when he'd failed to break the man's possession and had destroyed the madman along with Subaru's own body.

With the circulation of mana inside his body thrown into disarray by Ferris's magic, straining the capillaries and organs all throughout, the corpse could hardly be called a pretty sight, even generously. Blister-like rashes had broken out here and there across the visible skin, and the half-open eyes were stained bright red where the blood vessels had burst. Before being wiped clean, the lower half of his face had been stained with a nosebleed severe enough that, had it not been for Julius's coup de grâce, he would surely have been left in an even more wretched state of death.

Still, even a peaceful death mask wouldn't save the hearts of those left behind. Especially not for those who had come through the battle with the White Whale and finished the decisive fight with Sloth, vowing to return in triumph to the royal capital — their despair, their faces full of regret, were each of them enough to wring the heart.

"Subaru-dono... I am... sorry, truly..."

It was Wilhelm who knelt and bowed his head to the dead Subaru. Wilhelm, who had cut down every last Witch Cultist under Petelgeuse's banner, hung his head, his face set in an expression of bitter, gall-swallowed grief at the outcome of the battle. Among the elderly knights of the subjugation force too, some lamented the tragic turn of fate just as Wilhelm did, others drove their fists into the ground in raw impulse; there were even some who, overcome by passion, spilled tears.

Subaru was struck speechless by the sight of his own death being mourned to such a degree. If anything, it brought a crushing weight to Subaru's heart even greater than simply being shown the scene of his own death — a scene he had never let himself consciously consider until now.

"Why... even after Subaru became like this, he still... me... hey, why was that?"

Resting her hand against the cheek of the silent Subaru, Emilia kept calling out to him, though her words would never reach. At that heartrending sight, Subaru realized something else, belatedly.

In this world, Subaru had never given Emilia an answer to her question. To the question she had put to him in the royal capital — 'Why do you help me?' — he had never conveyed his true, heartfelt answer. And so Emilia had no way of knowing the reason behind Subaru's devotion.

—That created a decisive rift from the scene shown before, and yet both were, alike, the results of irrevocable sins Subaru had committed.

"The Witch Cult, which has tormented the world for so long — its vanguard, Sloth, has been repelled. That is an achievement of tremendous significance for the world. —But."

Looking down at Subaru's remains, Julius tapped his fingertips against the hilt of his sheathed sword. Again and again, the rhythm repeated, its intervals gradually narrowing,

"Not every sacrifice made toward that end can be accepted. —I wished I could have exchanged more words with you, Natsuki Subaru."

Murmuring the words with pain in his voice, Julius turned his face away from Subaru's dead face. Looking up at the sky, the knight let sorrow settle into his eyes,

"I wanted to call you a friend."

Julius's whisper-faint, strengthless voice brought an end to the world of the grasslands.

Fade to black once more, and then, with the regression, Subaru's body jerked as he awoke with a shudder.

"—Guh, ha! ...Ah, ahh, ha, ahh!?"

Writhing in place, he found himself lying on a hard, cold floor. With the smell of moss invading his nostrils, Subaru rolled about, throwing himself wholly into the hollow act, trying to escape the swelling emotion threatening to whip up a storm inside him.

He must not think about what was happening. He rolled, and rolled, straining his inner ear, tormenting his lungs until he gasped for breath, wanting only to shave away, even by a fragment, the share of consciousness given over to thought, to sink down into unconsciousness.

"—Ugh, gh!"

But even trying to deceive himself through such a manner that stripped away his humanity, the moment he struck the wall and bounced back, his purpose was derailed. Pain traveled up from his battered back, and he felt blood seep from where his forehead had scraped the floor. Pressing his face against the floor, he repeated ragged breaths, and before he knew it, tears had begun spilling from the corners of his eyes.

—Pathetic. Worthless. Beyond saving.

Just how many times, how many more, would Natsuki Subaru keep being beaten down by his own weakness? No matter what circumstance assailed him, no matter what suffering struck him down, what would it take to obtain a heart of steel — one that would never waver, never snap? So weak, so brittle — and that was why Subaru had, until now—

"Is this... the bill coming due, for all the times I pretended not to see, for looking away...?"

It wasn't as if he had never thought of it. At the edge of his awareness, Subaru had entertained that possibility not just once, but many times. And yet, the reason that possibility had never risen higher than the edge of his awareness was nothing other than the fact that he had, unconsciously, refused to examine it, to give it any real thought.

For a Subaru who 'Returns by Death' — the existence of a world after he dies... if he ever let himself think such a thing existed, his way of fighting would crumble out from under his feet. Everything he had meant to save would be left behind by Natsuki Subaru. No — it was Natsuki Subaru who had been doing the leaving-behind. By meeting 'death' in ways unsightly and self-serving, Subaru had left the world behind and fled, alone, into a new one.

If the world that Natsuki Subaru's rash decisions had abandoned still went on existing, then the very scenes he had just been shown were exactly that. They were the scenery lying beyond even the hell from which Subaru's death set him free.

"—No... way."

His consciousness, all at once, began to grow faint and distant again. Not drowsiness — a forced severance from reality, as if his awareness were rapidly whiting out.

'Behold the now that should not be.'

By his ear, once again, a voice whose owner he couldn't place whispered. Chasing after whose voice it might be, with his awareness growing hazy — he realized.

—That it was, unmistakably, his own voice.

Before a corpse with its skull crushed in, there knelt the figure of a girl.

From the fall from a great height, the human body of flesh had shattered under an impact it could not withstand, the black-haired head spilling its contents onto the ground, blooming a flower of death in crimson.

'————'

At the sensation of his consciousness switching over once again, Subaru no longer even felt surprise. He'd already guessed that, after the forced shift of consciousness, a phenomenon like this would arrive immediately after. If there was one thing he couldn't have predicted, it was the single point of what scene would unfold before the consciousness thus led into wakefulness—

"Right to the very end, saying things that make no sense... it's... there's nothing left to..."

Standing beside Subaru, who had died in the fall and now lay sprawled spread-eagled on the ground, spitting the words out, was a girl with pink hair — Ram. Her usually neat appearance was in disarray, her uniform conspicuously snagged and torn here and there. On her profile, deliberately shaped into a blank expression, welled up an unbearable tangle of feeling, and anger.

It was not so much a face grieving Subaru's death, as one seized with fierce anger at the outcome. Ram roughly scratched at her own head, then turned around.

"Is this all going exactly as you planned too, Beatrice-sama? For it to come to this, to block Ram's way like this, is your...!"

"————"

Ram's face, about to launch into a quick-tongued accusation, stiffened, and her words cut off. Ram's pale pink eyes were looking at Beatrice, who knelt there in front of Subaru's corpse. Heedless of the hem of her dress getting soiled, the girl sat down onto the ground — and at the sight of Beatrice like that, Ram's eyes wavered with unease.

"Beatrice-sama..."

"—Why."

A voice, murmured softly. Paying no mind even to Ram calling her name, Beatrice stared fixedly at the dead Subaru. From the corner of her blue eyes, Subaru could see the trail a tear had drawn.

—Beatrice was crying. In the face of Subaru's 'death.'

That fact drove the blade of guilt viciously into Subaru's heart. With the pain of his heart being gouged, he felt heat well up behind eyes he no longer had; he wanted, right now, to run to that young, small body and speak some word to her.

But the legs, the arms, the mouth to do so with — none of it remained to him anymore.

"I know... at least, that you aren't him... but... still..."

Beatrice, her expression gone, kept letting fall drops of tears as she murmured as if in delirium. At the pitiful sight, Ram seemed to give up on saying anything further to Beatrice. She let out a breath, and turned a scornful eye on Subaru's corpse, its neck bent at an impossible angle,

"'I love you,' huh — what a truly hopeless story."

'Behold the now that should not be.'

A cold, hazed with white mist, cold enough to freeze even the air itself, ruled over the world.

The frozen forest cracked and shattered with every gust of wind, and the scenery, forcibly wrung dry of mana, could not sustain its own existence and crumbled back into dust. Trees, townscapes, living creatures, the world itself — all turned into white crystals in the driving wind and were scattered to fragments, as a whitening end slowly encroached upon the world.

'————'

This time, what lay before Subaru's eyes was the end of the world. The world was about to meet a cold, merciful end, just as consciousness did when it whited out. But,

"—So, it's you after all."

A low voice, one that made the very air tremble, rang out carrying a note of resignation. An instant later, a ground-shaking rumble ran through the earth, and the scenery changed all at once with the impact of an enormous body collapsing. Trees were mowed down, and as they fell they crumbled away like frost, the forest leveled flat in a chain reaction across its whole expanse.

The cause of the destruction that had flattened the frozen woodland was a four-legged beast of towering, look-up-at size — a creature that seemed feline, its fur grown out gray.

The beast, its fangs — too large to fit within its own mouth — snapped halfway off, breathed white breath out through the gaps between its sword-like teeth, and, lying on its side, turned its blazing golden eyes forward. And, its whole body trembling as if convulsing,

"How galling... even knowing it would come to this, I couldn't change it."

"—I understand, more or less, what happened. Which is precisely why it's regrettable."

To the beast's rational voice, one carrying no sour grapes, simply an attitude of accepting the fact before it, a strikingly clear, beautiful voice answered. A voice that, even amid a world's ending, hadn't lost a fragment of its healthy vitality. Standing tall and straight, his red hair stirring in the white wind, was a blue-eyed young man.

"Are Emilia-sama and Subaru both gone now, nowhere to be found?"

"Lia has gone to sleep, forever. A world without that child in it has no value in existing at all. This body of mine, unable to protect her, and that man too — we share the same guilt—"

"And for that reason, you mean to destroy the world?"

"I knew I would be stopped. But doing so is this body's vow."

Drawn from a sheath engraved with a dragon's claw, a gleam of steel was leveled at the nose of the beast before him — Puck, having revealed his true form — and Reinhard, the 'Sword Saint' gripping the blade, quietly shook his head. In his blue eyes he held deep sorrow and tenderness both,

"I understand your regret. I feel the same. But that doesn't mean it's alright to lash out at random with that regret. What you're doing, the result of that vow, will bring chaos upon the world. —That, I can never allow."

"Because it isn't right?"

"Yes, because it isn't right. —I am the standard of what is right. A sword that corrects wrongs. And so, here, I will cut you down, Great Spirit-sama."

Despite the overwhelming difference in mass, which way the balance of power tipped was obvious to anyone's eyes. Even Puck, having shown his true form, could not so much as make Reinhard's cool composure waver, and was already at his last gasp before him. If the sword tip leveled at him traced but a single silver flash, that alone would be enough for Reinhard's blade to cleave even a being such as a spirit clean in two. The sheer ferocity of the sword-intent surging off him made that plain to everyone around.

"—Kh."

So at the sound that abruptly rang out, Reinhard's brow furrowed. Even the consciousness-only Subaru, amid his sparse emotion, felt a question mark surface.

He couldn't quite judge what that short, broken sound that had reached him was. Precisely because he couldn't believe it was really what it had sounded like.

"Kh, khhh... haha, fuhaha!"

"—What is it that's so funny?"

Throat trembling, even in his near-death state, Puck let laughter spill out, his face twisted. Unable to grasp the true meaning behind Puck laughing like that, even with his very life held in another's grip and his own actions thwarted, Reinhard put the question to him. But Puck, as though Reinhard's very response only fed the laughter all the more,

"What's funny? It's funny, of course it is, it's bound to be funny. Reinhard, you... no, you understand nothing at all."

"............"

"I remembered. What I ought to be. An understanding far too late in coming. And knowing that — and knowing that you still don't understand it — that's what makes it so unbearably funny."

That was, including the fact that the pitch of his voice was different from how it was in his usual form, a remark far too unlike Puck. Even for Subaru, who had no small memory of dealing with Puck, the spirit in his cat form, this was the first time seeing him address another with a voice so full of malice.

It was different even from when Emilia had been killed and he had turned his hatred on Subaru and Petelgeuse. Even then, surely, Puck had still been Puck. But now, the figure mocking Reinhard seemed to have become something else entirely, unlike any form Puck had ever shown before—

"...I will prevent any further harm. If you must resent someone, resent me."

"I won't resent you, Reinhard. You're a hero. A hero has a role, a deed, that only a hero can carry out. I won't resent or blame you for living up to that."

"————"

"You're a hero, Reinhard. —You can be nothing but a hero."

That very last moment was, out of everything, the voice most plainly overflowing with malice.

Having heard it out fully, Reinhard raised the sword he held overhead and, pouring sword-intent into it, loosed a single flash — and following the blade's gleam, a tremendous quantity of heat was generated. It split the sky, bored through the air, shattered the earth, whirled the mana into a vortex, and cleaved every object that lay along the blade's straight line — the light converged, and before Subaru's watching consciousness, the world shifted out of place.

'————'

The world, white and shrouded in cold, was reborn after the torrent of the slash had subsided. The world's dislocation was mended, the whirling mana formed itself into a ring and was returned to the world, flowers budded from the shattered earth, the bored-through air came to hold warmth, and sunlight poured down through the split-open sky.

A slash by the Sword Saint, in which the world's end and its rebirth occurred all at once. And the enormous beast that had been cleaved by that slash had been wiped from the world root and all, without leaving so much as a trace.

The huge body that had, just moments before, unmistakably been right there, was gone — and not even the aftershocks of the destruction that body had caused remained anywhere.

'————'

With a high, ringing sound, Reinhard sheathed his own knight's sword. The wind blowing through stirred his red bangs, and Reinhard, narrowing his eyes against the sunlight, looked up at the sky and let out a breath so faint no one could have heard it,

"—Felt-sama will grieve, I imagine."

he murmured, closing his eyes.
