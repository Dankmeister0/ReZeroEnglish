Arc 9 Chapter 34: "Gravity"

Experiencing Clind's "Teleportation"—or as he himself described it, "Compression"—was something Petra hadn't felt in about a month, while for Meili it had only been a few days.

"It might startle you the first time, but there's no need to be scared," Petra advised Flam, who hadn't experienced it before, speaking from her own experience.

It was a considerate warning, meant to keep Flam from panicking if she wasn't told what was about to happen. But Flam shook her head and said, "No need."

"There's no need to worry on my account. Hardly anything could surprise me more than the young master already has."

'Even his own household treats him like a walking shock machine? Just how much of a handful is he?'

At that almost-too-resilient answer, 'Subaru' offered an exasperated comment of his own.

Still, from the memories shared through the Book of the Dead, Petra knew about the time Reinhard had blown away a building with a single sword stroke and scattered Meili's sister with it—so she understood all too well why Flam had grown so thoroughly used to the extraordinary.

"Even if Flam-chan's fine with it, I hope our sleeping Sensei doesn't get dizzy from it~ The first time Steward-san teleported her, Little Red Scorpion-chan panicked something awful~"

"Now that you mention it, I wonder if there's such a thing as teleport-sickness. Even in Vollachia, it seemed like some people were fine with Spica-chan's 'Teleportation' and some weren't."

Meili shrugged, her hair stirring as Little Red Scorpion poked its head out from within, pincers lifting in a little shrug to match its mistress's.

That remark brought to mind Spica's "Teleportation," left behind now in the Empire—whether it came down to constitution or something else, some people just couldn't handle it, strong or weak notwithstanding. Petra had been fine with it, but Garfiel, who hadn't been, used to curl up in a sulky ball afterward.

At Petra's question, Clind raised a finger. "Indeed. Consideration."

"While I cannot say the number of trials or subjects has been especially large, no one has yet fallen ill from it. Reassurance. The Authority of 'Melancholy,' strictly speaking, is not 'Teleportation'—it is the result of 'compressing' the time and distance that would otherwise have been required. Abbreviation."

"I still don't get it no matter how many times I hear it~"

'So basically it's like King Crimson, right? No, wait—I'm not sure it can skip over the in-between results too, so maybe it's not quite the same after all...'

"No need to worry about getting sick, and it's a power that buys us the one thing we want most right now—time. Knowing that much is enough for now."

Petra offered that conclusion to Meili, who tilted her head in confusion, and to 'Subaru,' who was busy chewing the explanation over in his own way.

'Subaru's' way of putting things seemed like something she could understand, but spending time and thought on it right now felt like too much of a luxury. If time allowed, she'd like to carefully sort through every memory and scrap of knowledge poured into her, to become someone who understood everything there was to know about Subaru—but.

"Clind-niisama, could I ask you to go ahead?"

Far from indulging that wish, Petra couldn't even spare the time to hold onto it, and so she urged Clind on.

The lens of Clind's monocle caught the light as he nodded. "Yes. Timely." Considering the phenomenon he was about to set in motion, his easy manner was almost too casual—and all the more reassuring for it.

As Petra marveled at that, Meili tugged at her sleeve. "Hey."

"Petra-chan, about that price thing from before~"

"—It's fine. Clind-niisama gave it his seal of approval."

"That's not it~ ...I don't even know why myself, but if Petra-chan has to end up on the losing end of it, I really don't like that~"

"On the losing end..."

Meili looked like she didn't quite know what to do with her own feelings, and her words made Petra blink.

Her friend's blunt concern, and the reckoning she'd already made with Clind over the price—the feelings that passed through Petra's chest in that moment were as rich and complicated in color as they'd ever been.

Even so, Petra smiled at Meili.

"Thank you, Meili-chan. But really, I'm okay."

"...If that's a lie, I'll never forgive you~"

It was a threat squeezed out only after some thought, and its clumsiness made Petra nod, still smiling.

She gently drew the sleeve Meili had let go of close against her chest, then went looking for 'Subaru.' There was little chance of leaving his phantom form behind, but it was a matter of feeling, not fact.

When she found his translucent back, 'Subaru' was gazing off at the sea of sand in the distance.

'If I'd just had my act together a little more, he wouldn't have to go through all this.'

In the eastern sky—where the air itself screamed, the color of the heavens had turned to something ghastly, and the roar of earth being scoured away thundered on—the battle between the Sword Saint and the Witch of Envy still raged.

It was a clash between beings of overwhelming power, the kind that could decide the fate of the world. Faced with it, 'Subaru's' profile, full of regret over his own lack of strength, made Petra catch her breath with a pang of sorrow.

'Subaru,' worried for his friend, said nothing about the exchange Petra had just had with Clind, respecting her wishes.

In the extreme, there should be no keeping secrets from a being that existed inside Petra's own head—if he wanted to, he could strip bare any information Petra had tried to shut her ears to.

But Imaginary Subaru never did that. Whether it came from 'Subaru's' own good sense and consideration, or from her own will bending him to her wishes, Petra—her sense of self, her affection, and her memories all tangled together—couldn't tell which.

Still, whichever it was, Petra thought—

Even if it was consideration, even if it was simply the result Petra herself had wished for, the way 'Subaru' existed was still drawn from how Petra Leyte herself saw the real Natsuki Subaru.

And so—

"Clind-niisama, please."

"—Understood. Commendation."

Still holding her fondness for 'Subaru' close, Petra gave Clind the signal.

Petra, Meili, and Flam. Add Patrasche pulling the dragon carriage and the unconscious Garfiel and Ezzo, and that made six people and one dragon in all—plus 'Subaru' and Little Red Scorpion, tallied as one more phantom and one more creature—and with that, the group set out to move with Clind's help.

For that, the "price" Petra had proposed was fed into the Od Lagna—

"——"

In an instant, quite literally in the blink of an eye, the world switched over, and the "Compression" was complete.

She closed her eyes, and when she opened them, the scenery had changed entirely. The taste of the air on her tongue and skin, the smell of the world reaching her nose—both shifted completely, and Petra felt in her bones that the Authority had been used.

"——"

For a moment, Petra shut her eyes and let her thoughts dwell on the fact that they'd set things in motion.

But with that one second of prayer, she set her sentiment aside on a shelf and turned her attention to the matter at hand. From here on, she and the others didn't have the luxury to waste even a single misstep.

Yes, with that thought Petra steeled herself once more—

"Whoaaaa?! Wh-what, what?! A dragon carriage just came flying out of nowhere?!" "R-Reinhard? No stupid stunt like that could be anyone but him!" "Am I still dreaming or something? Toto! Totooo! Lend me your soft knees!"

"Argh, quiet down! What's all this racket about— wh-where did that come from?!"

Enough to douse Petra's resolve, that was the noisy, restless welcome awaiting them on the other side of the "Compression."

'Huh?! Never mind Old Man Rom, but—Blockhead?! That's Blockhead! Why are those guys together?! Nande why?!'

It's worth adding, as one final note, that 'Subaru,' the first to react to that grand welcome, had also raised his voice loudest of all at the unexpected shock of it.

"Petra-chan! Meili! Are you both alright?!"

"—!" 'Emilia-tan...!'

The moment Petra laid eyes on Emilia, her face pale with worry as she rushed over to them, Petra felt a sweet, overwhelming emotion explode outward inside her chest.

A warm, fuzzy, numbingly saccharine feeling filled her chest, and her mind overflowed with flowery praise for Emilia's beauty and loveliness. That rapture wasn't limited to admiring her looks, either—it extended to her gestures, her way of speaking, and even, in the end, the sound of her footsteps.

"That's... incredibly blatant...!"

Swept up in that unmistakably intense wave of emotion, Petra's head spun.

Up until now, the biggest effect she'd felt from the Book of the Dead had unquestionably been the presence of Imaginary Subaru—but this Emilia shock rivaled even that.

Frankly speaking, an overdose of Emiliase might just be fatal—

"—No, stop it, don't say something so stupid, me...!"

Having very nearly had half her consciousness swept away, Petra forcibly grabbed herself by the scruff of the neck before she could lose herself entirely, and just barely managed to stay rooted in reality.

She'd known it was coming, but her love for Emilia had come flooding out far too boldly.

That in itself left her with a hazy sort of jealousy—but more than that, Petra herself was overjoyed to be reunited with Emilia, and having her own feelings of cherishing Emilia get shoved aside was something Petra, personally, objected to.

So Petra held back the urge to think of Emilia—who had spotted them and was rushing over, tears welling in a face so beautiful it made you catch your breath—as E-M-T, and spread both arms wide.

And then—

"Emilia-nee-tama...!"

"Nee-tama?!"

Her tongue had slipped magnificently, startling Emilia right at the height of their touching reunion.

She'd very nearly called her "Emilia-tan" and tried to correct course at the last second, only to fail to pull it off completely. Honestly, it was a blunder quite unlike Petra.

In any case—

"But I'm so glad... You both really came to see me."

"Yes. And I'm so glad you're safe too, Emilia-neesama."

Overcome with emotion, Emilia pulled her into a hug, and Petra hugged her back, answering so.

On Petra's other side, Meili, being hugged just the same, gave the unaffectionate response of "I can't breathe~..." while gingerly, nervously wrapping her arms around Emilia's back.

'Whoa! Seeing Emilia-tan safe and sound is such a huge relief! Huh? Did she get even cuter while we were apart for a bit? Her growth potential is insane, right?'

Floating in midair, 'Subaru' was moved to tears, gazing at Emilia hugging Petra and Meili from every possible angle. He was getting way too excited. But that couldn't be helped, either.

After all, given the timing of the Book of the Dead, the last Emilia 'Subaru' knew was the one who'd been utterly worn down back at the Sanctuary.

Back then, Petra hadn't yet been as close to Emilia as she was now, and hadn't been able to do anything to help her. If even Petra felt frustrated about that, then the exasperation 'Subaru' had felt experiencing it up close firsthand must have been on an entirely different scale.

'Ahh! This is huge, Petra! Look! Emilia-tan is walking around, full of energy!'

"That's right. Walking on those long legs of hers. I'm glad."

'I'm so glad...! I'm really, truly glad...!'

So Petra hesitated to dampen 'Subaru's' trembling emotion. While offering the occasional agreeable murmur to his outpouring of feeling, she savored a quiet relief of her own.

The reason for that relief was singular: seeing Emilia, she hadn't ended up flinching in fear.

"The last thing I saw, in the Book of the Dead..."

The Sanctuary sinking into shadow, and Natsuki Subaru—caught in an inescapable obsession, clinging to the spiteful-seeming means the Witch had laid out for him, laying hands on his own life.

In that final moment, Subaru-as-Petra had seen it—that the terrifying Witch of Envy wore the very same face as the person she loved most.

"——"

Whenever she tried to think deeply about the reason for that, it was like reaching for something shrouded in fog—no proper thought would ever quite take shape. And still, the emotion that had been burned so vividly into her refused to fade.

That was why she'd been afraid of seeing Emilia again. No—to be precise, what frightened her was the possibility that she might not be able to look at Emilia the same way as before.

"But I'm glad it didn't turn out that way. ...Though my heart's racing now for a different reason than before."

"Huh? Petra-chan, is something wrong?"

"It's nothing. I'm just happy to see you again, Emilia-neesama."

Keeping the inner conflict she'd felt hidden away, Petra rejoiced, with genuine feeling, at being reunited with Emilia. At that, Emilia blinked in surprise before smiling, while atop the head of the still-unaffectionate Meili, Little Red Scorpion clacked its pincers cheerfully on its mistress's behalf.

And then—

"—Just when things started getting noisy, here you are after all, little missy."

A low, rasping voice cut in, and Emilia let out a startled "Ah."

At the doorway of the room, left wide open when Emilia came rushing in, a large silhouette stood knocking on the door, peering inside.

That silhouette was—

"Felt-chan's grandpa!"

Emilia blinked at the sight of the old man ducking through the doorway with some effort—Old Man Rom. At her words, he rubbed his bald head with a wry chuckle.

"The way you put it, you sound like you honestly think we're kin... Folks around here call me Old Man Rom. This is the first time we've had a proper talk, though, isn't it."

"Um, that's right. We didn't get to talk at the castle on the day the Royal Selection started, and when Felt-chan stole my emblem, it wasn't exactly a calm moment for conversation either..."

"...You don't have to keep glancing over so worriedly. I know the whole story, so it's fine~"

As Emilia responded to Old Man Rom's greeting while also worrying over Meili in her arms, Meili herself answered with an attitude about half annoyance and half appreciation.

That exchange between the two of them touched on the one connection between Emilia and Old Man Rom—the incident, some time ago, when Felt stole the emblem that served as proof of eligibility for the Royal Selection from Emilia, an incident in which Meili's older sister, Elsa, had been involved.

Thinking about it that way, the three of them shared a rather curious bond. Counting 'Subaru,' that made four. And if you included Reinhard too, well—there'd be no end to it.

"Calling you 'little missy' and the like doesn't quite suit your standing these days, does it. May I call you Emilia?"

"Yes, that's fine. Nice to properly meet you, Old Man Rom."

'Whoa... these two properly introducing themselves feels like some kind of historic moment.'

"That's a bit much."

Watching Emilia and Old Man Rom shake hands and exchange greetings, Petra found it funny that 'Subaru' would call something as big as "historic."

There was admittedly a bit of poignancy to it, sure, but the way he put it made it sound like she'd just witnessed the reunion of two people who hadn't spoken in over ten years.

Setting 'Subaru's' rapture aside for the moment—

"I've heard about what happened with Felt-chan too. It must have been rough for you and the others, Old Man Rom?"

"Mm, indeed it was. I'd like to get everyone's accounts sorted out on that front. Petra."

"Right. I think it'll be a little while yet before Clind-niisama gets back, so I think we should go ahead and start here first. ...Since this is a race against time."

At Petra's final addition, both Emilia and Old Man Rom nodded deeply at the same time.

Yes, a race against time. It was exactly why Petra and the others had resorted to using such an outrageous trick to get here in the first place.

—At present, the place where Petra and the others had all gathered was the Royal Lodge of the royal capital, Lugunica.

Among the members of the Emilia Camp who'd scattered to act on their own, this was partly because her whereabouts were clearly known, but if they were going to gather around any one person, there was no one it could be but Emilia.

With that in mind, after linking up with Old Man Rom and the others via Clind's "Compression," Petra had hurried straight to the capital—

"Even I was taken aback at how torn up this capital's gotten."

"It's been a while since I've been to the capital too, but it wasn't this beat-up all over the place last time I saw it, and there definitely wasn't anything like that sculpture, either~"

Meili, gazing out the window, chimed in on the heels of Old Man Rom's remark as he crossed his thick arms.

Unlike the two of them, who'd been here before, this was Petra's first time coming to the royal capital. Fortunately, the Loot House she'd just recalled from the shared memories had been located in the capital's slums, so relying on that memory, Petra too could compare the capital as it once stood with the capital as it was now.

Sure enough, the capital in that memory hadn't had all these gaudy towers of ice standing here and there, and the Noble Quarter, lined with its stately homes, surely hadn't been in such disarray.

"I never cared much for the place—infuriating as it was—but to see the Noble Quarter leveled flat like that. From what I've heard secondhand, the evacuation was already finished, but even so, it's an outrageous business."

"Meili-chan mentioned it too, but—those ice towers, was that you, Emilia-neesama?"

"Ah, um, yes, that's right, but there was a reason I ended up doing that! Everyone was in terrible danger, and there wasn't the slightest room to hold back..."

Pointed at the sweeping change that had come over the capital's streets, visible in full from the Royal Lodge that had barely survived intact, Emilia flustered over how best to explain it.

Finding her flustered fluttering endearing, Petra worked together with Old Man Rom to untangle everything that had happened, one thread at a time—

"—So, it really was Al-san, then."

"...Yes. It's because I couldn't stop Al that the city ended up in such a mess."

"It's not your fault at all, Emilia-neesama. If you hadn't made those ice towers, the people of the capital would have been in real trouble... So, E-M-G—Emilia-tan, seriously, Guardian angel!"

"Mm, thank you, Petra-chan... eh? What did you just say?"

Petra shook her head vigorously, denying the blame Emilia was heaping on herself.

Hearing that Al and Volcanica were involved in the massive damage that had struck the royal capital of Lugunica too, if anything it was Petra who felt responsible. If only Al's scheming had been stopped back at its starting point, the Pleiades Watchtower, after all.

"If you start saying that, there's no end to it~ We all got outmaneuvered by that helmet guy, bit by bit. The ones in the wrong are Helmet-san and his buddies, right~?"

"Aye, that's so. Reflecting on your shortcomings to fill in the gaps is one thing, and a fine thing at that, but lamenting your lack of strength does no good. Call it an old man's rule of thumb."

"—. Yes, I think you're both right. Al's the one who did wrong, and Al is the one who has to answer for it. And time spent moping is time we can't afford to waste, isn't it!"

At Meili's and Old Man Rom's words, Emilia set her cheeks and steadied her resolve. Letting out a small breath at Emilia's reaction, Petra too tightened her resolve, bracing herself.

Just as everyone said, weakness disguised as self-reflection was a bad habit. The road only ever led forward.

'Al probably thinks the same way, too. ...Otherwise he wouldn't have pulled off something as line-crossing as breaking a Sin Archbishop out of prison. What the hell is that guy actually thinking?'

Picking up on Petra's sentiment, 'Subaru' scowled at the outrage Al had committed in the royal capital.

In truth, setting Volcanica loose to deal massive damage to the capital was an outrageous act of terror in itself—but it was a story that could get overshadowed the moment you learned his true objective: aiding the escape of Gluttony.

The Sin Archbishop of Gluttony—a figure with a deep history tied to Petra and the others—being let loose, of all things.

"Simply listing off everything he's gotten up to is enough to make one's head spin. He attacked those connected to Royal Selection candidates and abducted one of the knights. He turned the Divine Dragon Volcanica, guardian of the kingdom, against us, and used the Witch of Envy to seal away the Sword Saint's movements. On top of that, he marched into the capital holding one of the Royal Selection candidates hostage, half-destroyed the Noble Quarter, and broke a captive Sin Archbishop out of confinement, all told."

'Isn't that insane? How many years in prison does that even add up to?'

"Sounds like enough for about a hundred death sentences..."

All over again, the sheer number and weight of the charges against Al—no, against Al's whole gang—were nothing short of extraordinary.

It looked almost like he was going for "every bad thing in the world, all at once," but Petra didn't think it was reckless abandon—she thought it was all based on a plan.

Yes, Al had a proper plan. He was working through the steps needed for it, and it just so happened that nearly every one of those steps looked like the crime of the century.

"Isn't a plan that needs a Sin Archbishop in it something you should reconsider from square one~?"

"Well, there's the Witch of Envy in the mix too, so there's no excusing it at all, but..."

To begin with, there was no reason Petra should have to make excuses on his behalf, either.

From the impression she'd gotten through the Book of the Dead, Subaru too seemed to have thought of Al as a fellow countryman—someone he didn't quite dislike—but this was easily, thoroughly over the line of what could be forgiven.

On that point, it was probably the shared opinion of everyone gathered here—no, of everyone involved, including those not present.

"Right now, Clind-niisama is going around to everyone else, bringing Frederica-neesama and the others here. Emilia-neesama, what about Otto-san?"

"—Ah! Um, well, about Otto-kun..."

"I can tell from that reaction. He's overdoing it again, isn't he."

"Ugh... yes, that's right. I'm sorry I couldn't stop him."

Emilia wilted, drooping as though she herself were to blame, and lowered her eyes. But there was no fault in Emilia. If there was fault anywhere, it lay with Otto—and in this particular case, even he wasn't in the wrong.

Otto already pushed himself too hard on a daily basis even within the camp, so Petra and Frederica and the others were always telling him to rest—but this time was outside the scope of that warning.

In fact, Otto, who wielded the Divine Protection of Spiritual Speech, was far too useful to spare in a situation like this.

"Reconnaissance and espionage using insects and birds and whatnot, is it. A Divine Protection that lends itself to all manner of mischief... though it seems like it'd shoulder him with plenty of trouble in return, too."

"Ohh, spot on. Hat-san looks like a born worrier at a glance, after all~"

"I've lived a fair long while myself, but I've never once heard of a 'Divine Protection of Spiritual Speech.' If a Divine Protection with this much apparent use has gone so utterly unknown, it likely means none of the people ever granted it have lived very long."

Meili giggled, but Petra agreed with the darkly grim speculation in Old Man Rom's words.

It was true that overusing the Divine Protection of Spiritual Speech seemed hard on the body—Petra herself had seen, back in the Empire, the sight of Otto getting a nosebleed after relying on it too heavily.

Though that had also come out of necessity, in the middle of a war where there was no room to hold back.

"It's not just about Subaru and the others—he's especially worked up over Garfiel... He seems really, really fired up about it, like 'I'm absolutely going to do something about this.'"

"Probably, if Garf-san were awake, he'd be all down on himself going 'Otto-anii asked me to do somethin', so what the hell'm I even doin'...' They're two of a kind, after all."

"I was surprised how good Petra-chan's impression was~"

Setting aside Emilia's and Meili's surprised reactions, it was easy enough to picture Otto and Garfiel both wanting to push themselves too hard for exactly that kind of reason.

Garfiel, still comatose, had been left in the care of a treatment house in the capital, but once he woke up, they'd need to be careful he didn't turn around and needlessly blame or hurt himself.

"I hope Frufoo-chan can manage to talk Otto-san down."

Letting that slip, Petra worried over Otto, who wasn't present here.

No matter what insects or animals he negotiated with, Otto himself had to be there to hear out the information they gathered. Because of that, Otto was tracking Al's group on his own—or rather, in a party of two, together with his beloved dragon and longtime partner, Frufoo.

'Otto, huh... I'm worried he might screw up at the crucial moment, and I don't know how far it's okay to rely on him.'

"If he heard you say that, I bet you'd see the angriest face Otto-san's ever made."

Taking too much onto himself was just how Subaru was. It wasn't only Otto—anyone in the camp would probably get angry at being told that—but Petra figured Otto would be the scariest of all.

Even so, it was true that this wasn't a burden that should rest on Otto alone. If Clind brought Ram back with him, they should be able to borrow the power of her Clairvoyance and share the load.

Having thought that far, Petra suddenly remembered something.

"Oh, right, Emilia-neesama, there was something I wanted to ask you."

"Hm? What is it, Petra-chan?"

"You might not have any idea what I'm talking about, Emilia-neesama, but actually, on the way here, Meili-chan and I suddenly felt sick out of nowhere..."

The question Petra brought up concerned something that had happened right around when they'd linked up with Old Man Rom and the others—their coordinates pinpointed by Flam's Divine Protection of Telepathy with her sister Grassis—and just after they'd agreed to teleport to the capital next: Petra's whole body had suddenly broken out in gooseflesh, struck by a shock that felt like it was churning up her very mind.

The dread had been so intense that Petra had wondered whether it was some side effect of the Book of the Dead, or perhaps an omen that Reinhard had lost and the Witch of Envy was about to come bearing down on her—but since Meili had felt the exact same sensation, she'd concluded it was probably neither.

"But given the situation, right? Flam-chan and Old Man Rom and the others were fine, but Meili-chan and I, and Clind-niisama too, all felt it. So I thought it might not be unrelated to Al-san... Emilia-neesama?"

It was an anomaly that hadn't reached any conclusion even after talking it over with Meili and Clind.

Petra cut short sharing that potentially dangerous piece of information the moment she saw Emilia's reaction. Emilia's beautiful violet eyes had gone wide, and her cheeks had gone stiff.

It was unmistakably the reaction of someone who had some idea what she meant.

"...That really, really awful feeling—I think it's probably the doing of the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony."

'...Gluttony, you say?'

"Ugh, that's an unpleasant thought~"

At the mention of the Sin Archbishop they currently hated most of all, 'Subaru' frowned, and Meili stuck out her tongue. Petra too pressed a hand tightly to her chest, and urged Emilia to continue with her eyes.

"I don't know exactly what was done to me either. But Al had Gluttony—Roy—do something, and then I suddenly felt sick too, and while my head was spinning..."

"Al-san and Gluttony both got away."

"Yes, that's right."

Emilia's shoulders drooped despondently as she nodded, without much strength behind it.

At Emilia's reply, Petra glanced over at Old Man Rom, and the shrewd elder in charge of the Felt Camp stroked his own head with a large hand.

"When it was just Petra and Meili and the others talking it over, I thought it a confused sort of tale, but with even Emilia caught up in it too, and Gluttony mixed in on top of that, I've a bad feeling about this."

"The common thread is... that everyone affected is on Emilia-neesama's side?"

"It'd be a lot clearer if Fang-san were awake, or Hat-san would just quietly wait here for once, or if Subaru-oniisan hadn't been captured, wouldn't it~"

'Including me on the present timeline, that's three fools with terrible timing...'

That it was tied to Al was just as Petra had suspected, but with Gluttony, of all things, turning out to be involved, the seriousness of what made this unidentified attack so unsettling had jumped by an entirely different order of magnitude. It made leaving Spica behind in the Empire feel, just a little, like it might have been a mistake.

Spica too, whatever twists and turns had led there, was one of the Sin Archbishops of Gluttony—

"No, no, thinking like that is no good. I'm not Master Roswaal, after all."

What someone could or couldn't do, whether they were useful or not—if she started judging people's worth from that angle, she'd end up turning into Roswaal in no time flat. Sharpening one's insight was all well and good, no matter how far, but the moment her humanity started running dry, that was a warning sign to heed.

"As for this business with Gluttony, for now there's nothing to do but keep it in mind. Now then, according to what your camp's civil officer has to say, their destination is..."

"—The Grand Mogolade Geyser, in Kararagi."

Old Man Rom steered the conversation away from the inconclusive topic, and Emilia picked up where he left off.

That was Al's group's final destination, and the way they'd come to learn it was—

"Felt, dragged along with them, left it behind, and one of your own people passed it on, is that it. I trust Felt a hundred percent, myself, but what about the rest of you."

"You mean Otto-kun? Forget a hundred percent—I trust him about a million percent."

"Emilia-neesama, that was a percentage, not a plain number. But it sounded really cool."

Emilia's answer, unbound by the concept of percentages, warmed Petra's heart, while Old Man Rom and Meili gave exasperated, wry smiles.

But that Emilia had still properly answered the thing Old Man Rom actually wanted to know—that was just like her.

'...She's not just cute—she's gotten seriously strong, hasn't she, Emilia-tan.'

'Subaru' gazed at her with the eyes of someone looking at something dazzlingly bright.

Having been pulled up from partway through resolving the crisis at the Sanctuary, 'Subaru' couldn't fully grasp just how Emilia had cultivated this greatness of hers, and it left him frustrated.

Petra wanted to tell him. —That the Emilia standing here now had been forged together with Subaru, the two of them running a three-legged race side by side.

"No, wait, with Beatrice-chan too it'd be a three-person, six-legged race... if that logic works, I want to be counted in too, and I bet Garf-san would get sulky if we didn't include him either. What about you, Meili-chan?"

"What about me, what even is this conversation~? It's a little scary when you suddenly start talking like Subaru-oniisan~"

'Yeah, okay, that is something I'd probably say, but isn't Meili's read on this weirdly sharp?'

Still carrying a certain lingering wistfulness, 'Subaru' gave a wry laugh at Petra and Meili's exchange.

In any case, Old Man Rom seemed satisfied with Emilia's earlier answer, and the fact that Al's group's final destination was the Grand Mogolade Geyser had been shared with everyone.

Honestly, no one knew what was actually waiting at that location—

"As long as we know the location, we should be able to get ahead of them with Clind-san's power, shouldn't we."

"—. Right. Old Man Rom, what about Blockhead-san and the others?"

"For now, aside from the badly wounded, they should've entered the nearby town as instructed. That power of the young pup Clind's... it's not something you can go using freely, is it? I'll fit myself to whatever constraints you've got."

Shrugging his broad shoulders, Old Man Rom showed his understanding even toward Clind's Authority, despite receiving no detailed explanation of it.

Through the "price" Petra had proposed, the limits on using Clind's "Compression" Authority had been lifted, but even so, that price would eventually fail to cover things depending on how many times and how many people it moved. First they'd get the situation set up with the fewest people possible, and only commit the maximum number once the assessment was complete.

Even that preparation had a glimmer of hope thanks to Otto, right in the middle of his own reckless doings.

Beyond that, the more information they could gather on what formation Al's group intended to take before reaching their final destination, the better.

And then—

"—The means for that are already in our hands. Opportunity."

At a voice that arrived right on cue, as if waiting for exactly this moment, Petra and the others turned around all at once. Bowing under the full weight of all their gazes was Clind—the very person they'd just been wanting to talk to.

At timing so perfect it seemed like he'd been watching for the right moment, Emilia's face lit up at once. "Clind-san!" she called out.

"Thank you for sending Petra-chan and the others here. Thanks to you, we've properly been able to meet like this."

"No thanks are necessary. Returning to Mirula was my own judgment call, and that I've been able to meet your expectations this way is the result of Petra giving me a push. Encouragement. More to the point, I apologize for taking so long to return. Apology."

"I don't think it was really that long, but did something happen, Clind-niisama?"

Petra tilted her head at Clind, who shook his head slowly in apology.

Certainly, given the performance of "Compression," one might say Clind's return to the Royal Lodge had taken somewhat longer than expected. But he'd surely also needed to explain the situation to whoever he went to fetch, and it didn't seem like something worth apologizing for as being "late."

Was this simply perfectionism showing through, precisely because he was so capable? As Petra puzzled over that, Clind looked her way and spoke. "No. Denial."

"As for the reason for the delay, I believe having you experience it directly, rather than explaining it in words, would be the quickest and surest way to help your understanding. Firsthand impression."

'Directly?' "Experience?"

At the unnatural choice of words for what was supposed to be an excuse for tardiness, 'Subaru' and Petra both tilted their heads.

Emilia and Meili seemed to share the same puzzlement, while Old Man Rom, in a sense the one able to stay most objective, crossed his arms, watching with keen interest for whatever was about to happen.

And then, as everyone watched, Clind stepped aside from the doorway of the room, and—

"Hah. Kept you all waiting, did I."

Striding in with commanding presence, shoulders cutting through the air as she stepped into the room, was the pink-haired maid whose high-handed, take-no-prisoners manner suited her so well—Ram, as you well know.

Having reunited with them after Clind went to fetch her, she was dressed in a maid uniform Petra hadn't seen her wear in quite some time, and Petra—a fellow maid herself—felt a wave of nostalgia at how long it had been since she'd worn one herself.

But—

"Ram, thank you for—com..."

Emilia's words trailed off as she went to welcome Ram in her maid uniform, smiling, ready to rush over. Her feet stopped, and the hand that had been reaching to take Ram's hand instead came up to press against her own forehead.

"Um, wait."

Emilia let out a bewildered sort of noise.

At this sudden turn, Petra should have been worried about her—but she couldn't be. Because Petra, and Meili too, were both being tormented by the same inexplicable sensation as Emilia.

"This feeling, is it—"

The moment she looked at Ram, an inexplicable, wordless, enormous agitation had come welling up.

Petra felt that this baffling, unmanageable sensation was close to the sickening feeling they'd just been talking about, the one that came from Gluttony. Except that where she'd sensed strong malice in that earlier sickness, she felt none of it in this sensation now.

This wasn't that sort of thing at all—it was something else entirely, a strong, strong pull, a gravity—the sensation of something being violently drawn out of her at the sight of Ram in her maid uniform.

And the answer to that pull appeared right away, from behind Ram.

It was—

"—I'm terribly sorry for arriving so late to join you all, Emilia-sama."

So she said, stepping up alongside Ram—a blue-haired maid, dressed in the same maid uniform, executing a smooth, graceful, and thoroughly composed curtsy.

Of course, it was a face she knew. And yet, the version of her that Petra knew shouldn't have been in any state to wear that maid uniform so well, to carry out such flawless manners. —No, that wasn't right either.

Over and over and over again, her understanding was revised.

No. That's right, it was wrong. The idea that she lacked the knowledge and manners of a proper maid was absurd. Because in this world, she was the very first person of all to teach Natsuki Subaru—and Petra, back when she was still just a village girl—what a proper maid ought to look like.

That fact, Petra Leyte remembered now, through that very pull.

In other words, standing there was—

"That man... In order to bring Subaru-kun back, please allow Rem to do everything in her humble power."

Yes—the original all-purpose, endlessly useful maid of Roswaal Manor, mistress of courteous insolence and sharp-tongued remarks, Rem, gazing straight ahead with her pale blue eyes, made her declaration.
