Arc 7 Chapter 25: "Reunion Like Burning Blood"

A "crimson" girl, vivid as searing flame, richly fragrant as blood.

At the top floor of the city hall, in a space that had abruptly become a battlefield, monopolizing the gaze amid the devastated ruin was a violent beauty holding a brilliant red jeweled sword in hand.
Dazzling orange hair, a lavish dress the color of blood, a figure rich with feminine allure poised with the sword — even from behind, this was a person whose identity struck with overwhelming force.

"――――"

Speechless, staring at that back, Subaru's mind—arms still spread wide—was thrown into confusion.
For that instant alone he forgot the pain wracking his body and the breathlessness choking him, and in his empty head, bouncing about in chaos, was the word-spirit of a single question: why.

Why was she here.
Why had she saved Subaru.
Why could she stand so calmly against Arakiya.

Why, why, why, why, why, why, why, why, why—the questions had no end.
Ruled by that endless string of questions, Subaru forgot the passage of time. Then, as if mocking his frozen state, the crimson woman gave a snort.

"What a vacant face. Has my nobility burned your eyes?"

"—! You got eyes on the back of your head or something?"

"Fool. Do you take me for such a deformity? I can tell the face of a base fool from his breathing alone."

Without turning around, the woman answered with such lofty assurance—Priscilla Barielle.
Subaru's eyes widened at the answer of a woman who shouldn't have been able to be here at all, and no words of denial for her absurd claim of reading expressions from breathing came to him.
All he could do was stand there dumbstruck, while before him the situation slowly began to move.

"Now then."

With a short breath, Priscilla, holding her red jeweled sword at the ready, swept her gaze across her surroundings.
The city hall's ruined state, thoroughly transformed by the sudden intruder's rampage. Among the wounded People of Shudrak and the unconscious Zikr, barely able to move at all were Subaru, Rem behind him, and Abel out on the balcony.
And the one who had accomplished all this—

"—Arakiya."

"――――"

Narrowing her crimson eyes, Priscilla quietly fixed her gaze on her opponent.
The one who held silence under that gaze was the dark-skinned, silver-haired half-beast—the Empire's Number Two of the Nine Divine Generals, Arakiya.
Reputed to be the second-greatest power in the Vollachia Empire, she had shown a glimpse of strength that lived up to that name. With her red right eye, not covered by an eyepatch, she met head-on the piercing gaze charged with Priscilla's iron will—no, that wasn't quite it.

"Prin...cess..."

Until a moment before, Arakiya had maintained a transcendent, unshakable world of her own. But under Priscilla's gaze, after a single beat, that world collapsed.
What rose up in its place was violent agitation, and joy just as immense.

"Princess, Princess, Princess...!"

Dispelling the dangerous air around her, Arakiya repeated the word again and again, as if to confirm it.
In it could be glimpsed the clinging of the weak to the strong—like a lost child reunited with a parent, like a small younger sibling trying to reel in the bond with an older one.

—The relationship between Priscilla and Arakiya, the two of them, was beyond any outsider's understanding.

As ever, even the reason Priscilla had come to this place remained a mystery to Subaru. All he could sense was that some extraordinary past lay between the two of them.
And that this past relationship had broken the fighting spirit of Arakiya, the most dangerous presence in this place.

"—!"

Lowering the slender wooden branch clutched in her hand, Arakiya, overcome with emotion, moved to step forward. Perhaps she meant to throw herself straight into Priscilla's chest.
To embrace her, giving in to the surging passion in her heart.
But—

"Prin—cess—"

"Silence."

That was not to be granted.

The short, fierce shout came together with a horizontal flash of light.
Just as Arakiya took a step forward, a crimson gleam swept past mere centimeters from her bare toes, and red flame flared up as if to bar any further advance.

"――――"

It was, at most, a fire with the strength of a bonfire, reaching only to about the height of a shin.
A flame Arakiya, with her long legs, could easily have stepped over if she'd cared to—and yet, she froze, as though an insurmountable wall had sprung up before her.
Eyes wide at this unexpected act, Arakiya stood still, and Priscilla continued on, coldly.

"Arakiya. Why did you just now try to come to me?"

"Eh...?"

"Surely you didn't imagine that I, my heart racing at our reunion, would take you into these arms? If so, I can only be utterly appalled at your simple-mindedness."

Layered into Priscilla's words was a contempt for Arakiya. Laced with genuine exasperation, it completely severed any approach Arakiya might make.
Arakiya must have felt that chasm too. Her eyes wavered in shock, her mind working frantically to find the right answer to Priscilla's words.
But—

"P-Priscilla-sama..."

"Priska is dead. —Has time passed, have you gained rank, and still you have changed nothing at all?"

"—!"

"How disappointing. Even the soil of my homeland, after so long away, stirs no deep feeling in me, worn thin as it's become."

Priscilla's assessment, making no effort to hide her utter disillusionment.
Honestly, Subaru lacked both the understanding of her and the stability of the situation needed to gauge what was truly in Priscilla's heart. Even so, he could tell that her heartless words had dealt Arakiya's heart a grievous wound, drawing blood.

A deep wound, being spurned by someone precious, someone with whom a bond had been forged.
Told to her face that she was disappointing, Arakiya's eyes wavered—it would have been no surprise if she'd crumpled beneath that wound. But instead of crumpling, she let a fierce passion kindle in that single red eye.
Not anger, but something that should be called resolve, or determination—a flame.

"...I don't care, how Princess thinks of me. It hurts. But, I've decided."

"—. Oh? You've decided, have you. Can it recall my interest? Speak, and tell me what you've decided."

To Arakiya's quiet plea, Priscilla tossed back a provocative challenge, whether she cared or not. In response, Arakiya raised her face and roared, "I—!"
Roaring, Arakiya bent the knees of her slender body,

"To the Empire! I'll take back Princess's rightful place! For that—"

The target of Arakiya's outburst, the point her passion aimed at, was not the Priscilla in front of her, who had no intention of listening.
Her single eye passed over Priscilla and swung toward a mistaken direction. The tip of that surge of passion was aimed at Abel, who still stood on the balcony watching how things unfolded.

"That lying Excellency——!!"

Eyes ablaze with passion, Arakiya's slender frame leapt high into the air. Ignoring the wall of flame between herself and Priscilla, she set her aim on Abel at the balcony.
Her leaping power was extraordinary. But for the Empire's second-ranked power, that should be no surprise.
The problem was that there was no one but Priscilla with any means to stop this act of violence.

"This is bad, Abel's going to—! Priscilla!"

"Do not make such a racket. And besides, how presumptuous, base fool. From whom did you gain permission to address me so familiarly, without title?"

"This isn't the time for that..."

"—I said, do not make a racket."

Priscilla, sword lowered at her side, paid no heed to Subaru's pleas.
After that threat, which made her next move unreadable, Subaru held his breath as, in his field of view, Arakiya's leaping form closed on Abel like an arrow.

"――――"

Clinging to the half-collapsed balcony railing, blood streaming from his forehead, was Abel.
The man had shown no sign of dismay even at Priscilla's sudden intrusion, and now, his black eyes still brimming with dominance, he stood facing Arakiya head-on, unflinching.
Still unarmed as ever, and it was common knowledge that his arms held no strength capable of meeting one of the Empire's strongest in battle—why he didn't despair, Subaru could not understand.
Without understanding—

"Abel—!!"

Knowing it was meaningless, Subaru tried to rush over, only for the strength to drain from his legs on the very first step. Stumbling, falling, all he could do was reach out a hand as, in his field of vision, Arakiya closed in on Abel—

—And in the next instant, the chain of events that followed was beyond Subaru's comprehension.

"――――"

Eyes fixed on the closing Arakiya, Abel stamped hard on the balcony floor. Immediately, cracks spread through the half-collapsed balcony, and the footing collapsed all at once.
Naturally, Abel, standing atop it, should have fallen with no way to stop it—but that isn't what happened.
Abel's body, which should have been falling, hung suspended in midair. His right arm, stretched taut, gripped the curtain from the top floor that had been dragged out onto the balcony.

Hiding a lifeline beneath the hand he'd rested on the railing, Abel had collapsed the footing of his own will.
Had the opponent been some rank-and-file soldier, it might have been a plan to drag them down along with the collapse and finish them off below in one stroke.
But the opponent was one of the Nine Divine Generals, and moreover, the Number Two.

"Playing around...!"

Baring her fangs at Abel, who clung to his lifeline, swinging like a pendulum, Arakiya struck.
Having lost the footing she'd meant to land on after her leap, she was nonetheless someone whose strength lay not only in superhuman physical ability but superhuman uncanny power besides.
Her legs shimmered like heat haze, and from the knee down transformed into flame, controlling her posture in midair like some war machine venting fire from thrusters in its legs.

The flame that had burned Mizelda, the ferocious tornado that scattered rank-and-file soldiers, the taffy-like substance that had propped up a falling pillar, the great wind that had stripped Rem of her armaments, and now, finally, turning part of her own body into flame.
Arakiya's uncanny power—its versatility was a threat with no bottom in sight at all.

"Excellency, die——!"

Aimed at Abel, unable to hold his own posture and spinning helplessly, came a branch whose hidden power no one could gauge.
Whether it would be used to stab, or magic unleashed from it, or some other, stranger supernatural effect entirely was unknown. —Unknown, but Abel would be shattered to pieces.
That certainty alone was enough to narrow Subaru's black-eyed pupils with panic and urgency.
But—

"—Whoa there, bro. Didn't even recognize you as bro till I heard your voice just now."

In that instant, a human shape cut in between the fiercely bearing-down Arakiya and the dangling Abel.
The shape dashed across the pillar Rem had felled and Arakiya had fixed in place, and hurled a strike from the side into the branch as it was leveled. A broad-bladed dao's slash—Arakiya twisted her wrist and parried it with the branch, but was knocked skyward, her momentum sapped.
Meanwhile, the one who'd landed after having his all-out surprise attack blocked without effort stamped his feet on the ground with a "Gaaah!"

"Damn, my arm hurts! Don't go knocking back someone's all-out swing like it's nothing, it's depressing!"

Stepping back, shaking his struck arm, a string of complaints capped off the sequence of events. And the one who'd done all this was, once again, someone entirely outside Subaru's expectations.
A man of bizarre appearance, his head covered by a jet-black full helm, the rest of him decked out like a mountain bandit. The man, looking like some highwayman or brigand, was notable for his thick right arm gripping the dao and, opposite it, his missing left arm, lost from the shoulder.
A man Subaru knew. Someone he should have realized would naturally be here, if Priscilla was here.
It was—

"—Al?"

"Yo, bro! Never figured I'd run into you clear on the other side of the border. Kinda wanna trade notes on that getup of yours and celebrate this wild coincidence, but..."

Tilting his head, the one who let out that absurdly carefree voice, out of place in the moment, was Priscilla's knight—no, her retainer, Al.
Responding to Subaru in his usual breezy manner, he kept his guard up against Arakiya, who hovered in midair with her legs turned to flame.
In this situation, should Subaru be more amazed that Al had blocked even a single blow from Arakiya, or that the wooden branch, taking a direct hit from the dao, showed not a scratch?

"Feel free to be impressed. Wasn't like it worked on the first try, after all."

"Move! Can't kill, Excellency!"

"I'm here on purpose to get in the way, so no, can't move for you. If I let this slide, my head's gonna part ways with my neck. But still..."

Arakiya roared, her face a mask of fury, and Al looked her over from the front with unabashed frankness.
Arakiya's outfit left little to the imagination, but there was nothing lecherous in Al's gaze. There was only something like deep, wistful nostalgia.
In fact, Al let out an admiring "Haaah~" of a sigh, and said,

"Well would you look at that, all grown up. Figured you'd turn into a looker, but damn."

"...Who?"

"That kinda hurts, y'know, even said casual like that. And here I thought we were the type who'd entrusted our lives to each other!"

At Al's familiar-sounding call, Arakiya's brow furrowed in a question mark. Al kicked a piece of floor debris at her slender frame to keep her at bay, putting distance between them.
Watching Al's handiwork, Priscilla gave him a short call—"Al"—and said,

"You understand my reason for bringing you rather than Schult. Get to work."

"I am working! Look like I'm having a grand old time playing with a cutie to you? Ten seconds of slacking off and I'm dead meat, y'know!?"

"Going by looks, you're hardly different from dead meat as it is."

"Come on, even I'm better off than dead meat—! Whoaaa!?"

Receiving that heartless cheer from his mistress, Al's concentration broke and he nearly died to one of Arakiya's blows.
Arakiya, wheeling through the air more freely than a bird, spun and circled, unleashing branch strike after branch strike, which Al, looking utterly desperate, batted away one, two times.

"...In the way..."

"Hearing that from a girl your age really does hurt an old man's heart for real."

Abel dangling, and Al continuing to shield him.
Glaring down at the two of them, battle-aura surged through Arakiya's entire body. And yet, the reason she hadn't unleashed the kind of terrifying area attack she'd shown at first was—

"Priscilla..."

Arakiya calling her "Princess."
Priscilla's attitude was cold, nothing but a complete severance—but not so for Arakiya. That was the reason that great devastation had not been unleashed.
So if anything could move this situation, it should be Priscilla, the still-uncommitted force.

"――――"

"Do not turn such a clinging gaze on me, base fool. I may praise that manufactured beauty of yours, but to think it would move me is nothing short of insolence."

"Ngh...!"

To Subaru, still on his knees, turning to look at her, Priscilla was haughty, giving him no purchase at all.
The one who moved instead was Rem, who had until then been sheltered behind Subaru's back. Pressing her lips together, she stood before Priscilla on unsteady legs.
And then—

"Please. I beg you, lend us your strength."

"—. Hmph, how commendable, that speech. Compared to the base fool, at least you seem to know your manners."

"...Then—"

"Do not be hasty. And watch. The preparations for the situation to turn have already been made."

Rem's plea—or the possibility of provoking Priscilla's displeasure—had made Subaru's back run cold, but Priscilla did nothing rash, merely jerking her chin to indicate the battlefield.
At that gesture, Rem turned with a surprised "Eh," and Subaru, drawn along, looked the same way.
The battlefield of the balcony—left alone, it should have been only a matter of seconds before Abel and Al were both slaughtered by Arakiya. And indeed, that situation was still ongoing.

"Gah! Gah! Aogh! Guah! Uboah!"

Arakiya, wheeling through the sky on burning legs, launched hit-and-run attack after attack, and Al, in a terribly clumsy, graceless way, was somehow managing to fend them off.
But a string of miracles cannot last forever. The impact of each blow accumulated in Al's body, and just as his reactions visibly began to lag, one massive hit sent his upper body reeling.
Al, losing his balance, was left defenseless against the next attack—and it was at that very moment.

"—!?"

Arakiya, about to kick off the air, had her body sway unnaturally, her control thrown into disarray.
That this was not the kind of attack motion an ordinary person could recognize was made plain by her eyes, wide with shock, and the stifled groan of pain she suppressed.

"――――"

Everyone was struck with surprise at the sudden change that had come over Arakiya—what had happened?
Only two were not caught up in that surprise: Priscilla, calmly surveying the situation, and the one who had set the trap, who should have been caught in the midst of the fierce exchange—

"—Now, spring it!!"

—came the command, hurled into air thick with the smell of scorching and dust.
The voice that rang out, more stern than angry, more grandiose than arrogant, was Abel's, still hanging in midair. The man, still dangling from the curtain, raised his voice at the opening that had arisen in the battle above.
As though he'd been certain this moment would come.

"Save the trick reveal for later, will ya!!"

Answering Abel's forceful tone, Al leapt.
An ungainly jump, twisting his body as he unleashed a strike with his dao that traced an arc through the air, aimed without mercy at Arakiya's out-of-control frame—no, there was mercy.
The wrist gripping the dao turned, so that it was the blunt back edge, not the blade, that met Arakiya.

"Fool."

Seeing through that trick meant to avoid killing, Priscilla spat the word out shortly.
And the eyes of this arrogant, deeply calculating woman had rightly foreseen how things would unfold.

"Get back...!"

At the moment of collision, a dull sound rang out, making Subaru want to look away.
Al's all-out blow flew true toward Arakiya, and she took it head-on with her raised left arm. Her elbow bent the wrong way, broken bone tearing through dark skin, blood spraying.
A gruesome sight—but not a fatal blow.

From deep within the jet-black helm came a low growl from Al, and the sense that his cheeks had stiffened in shock.
An instant later, Arakiya's slender leg struck Al's neck sideways, a tremendous impact slamming his body to the floor. His body bounced, flew off, and out through the collapsed balcony.

"Guh, aaaahhh——!?"

An unbecoming scream trailed off, and Al vanished from sight.
His safety was, of course, a concern—but with this, the obstacle between Abel and Arakiya was gone, once and for all.
Returning her malfunctioning legs from flame back to their original state, Arakiya set her soles on the floor. And now, at last, she meant to freely take Abel's life.

—And it was at that moment that a black shadow lunged furiously at Arakiya's back.

"O, ohhhhhhh——!!"

Sending debris flying, a shadow assaulted Arakiya with a fierce battle cry. It was a figure gripping a large knife in a reverse grip, unleashing attacks purely on hunting instinct.
For an instant, the sheer intensity of the ambush nearly outpaced comprehension of who the attacking shadow was.
Wounded, her trained, tall frame charred black, her black hair dyed red, and the white markings on her skin marking her as one of the People of Shudrak—Subaru, with a start, cried out.

"Mizelda-san!!"

"Aaaaaah!!"

Not answering to the name called, Mizelda continued her blood-vomiting roar.
Mizelda, who had been the first victim of the intruding Arakiya's opening attack. Her whole body scorched and pitiful from flame, she continued her assault as though burning up her very life.

Every merciless swing of her blade aimed at Arakiya's vital points.
Unlike Al, there was no mercy or compassion held back for the prey at the crucial moment. Watching her fight, Subaru belatedly understood that the one Abel had ordered to "spring it" was her.

Abel, foreseeing Arakiya's malfunction, had rallied the near-dead Mizelda's desperate fight.
Under that sudden, seemingly irrecoverable situation, Subaru couldn't even imagine how many brain cells Abel had burned through to conceive this divine calculation.
And yet, even so, it fell one step short.

"――――"

For all Mizelda's valiant fight, malfunctioning or not, Arakiya's ability to respond was that of a master.
Grimacing with annoyance at the barrage of attacks from Mizelda, burning her dying life away, Arakiya easily blocked them all with the roughly wielded wooden branch.
There was no longer any point remarking on that branch's durability.
The shockwave of the branch's sweep knocked the knife from Mizelda's hand, and in that instant, with Mizelda now unarmed, the branch thrust straight into her torso.

Her trained, steel-hard abdominal muscles were pierced by the branch without resistance, an ugly sound ringing out.
It churned mercilessly through Mizelda's insides, wholesale destroying vital organs, driving the sturdy Amazoness body toward death—

"With this, finally..."

"—Where are you looking?"

The instant Arakiya's attention slipped, believing she'd eliminated the obstacle.
A bloody froth at the corner of her mouth, Mizelda's eyes blazed with fierce light. Baring white teeth stained red in a savage grin, she seized Arakiya's branch-wielding right hand with both of her own.
Gripping it tight, tighter still, sealing it in place.

"――――"

The single instant of stillness born there might have been the last opportunity.
But of those Abel's cunning and Mizelda's devotion had created that instant for, not one among Subaru's allies could seize it.

The People of Shudrak, centered on their valiantly fighting chieftain, hadn't recovered from the great storm just before, and Subaru, barely managing to hold onto consciousness, was no different.
Even Rem, who had brought down the pillar, couldn't reach that moment—not with her leg's constant impairment.
No one could unite as one, and the once-in-a-lifetime chance was slipping away.
Except—

"—Puff out your chest all you like, oni girl. For it was your plea that called down my single strike."

Except that it was Subaru's allies who couldn't reach it. A third force was another matter.

"――――"

Surveying the battle from above, Priscilla closed the distance in a single step, aiming for Arakiya's undefended back.
For an instant, Arakiya sensed the presence closing from behind and moved to intercept. She meant to fling away Mizelda's tall frame, still gripping her arm, and slam her into the enemy behind.
But she couldn't. —Sensing the true nature of that presence, she found she couldn't.

"Prin—"

The crimson flash of a sword struck Arakiya, who to the very end had never been able to sever her attachment to Priscilla.
Blood burst forth, seared by flame, and Arakiya's body swayed violently.

"I told you, didn't I, Arakiya. —That by the next time we met, you were to have made your resolve."

The true meaning of that promise exchanged in the past was beyond any outsider's right to intrude upon.
All that could be understood was that the promise was the answer behind Priscilla's merciless strike, and the answer to Arakiya's unresolved lingering attachment as well.

"――――"

Taking the blow of the crimson jeweled sword to her back, Arakiya's body collapsed to the floor.
Mizelda, who had gripped her arm and sealed her movement, was caught up in the fall as well. The two of them collapsed together in a tangle, limbs sprawled out like puppets with their strings cut.

"――――"

The relentlessly shifting situation, a state like the air being endlessly churned by sword and flame—swallowed now by a sudden hush, Subaru felt the illusion that time itself had frozen.
The obsessive thought that a single breath, or even the smallest step, might ruin everything, that all of it might shatter apart like some illusion—

"—Mizelda-san!"

It was Rem, raising an urgent voice, who shattered that stillness of time.
Crawling forward across the floor on unsteady legs, she hurried to Mizelda's side, where she lay collapsed in a tangle with Arakiya.

Mizelda—burned all over, blown about by the great wind, and now pierced through the stomach besides.
Kneeling at her side, Rem caught her breath, and with a face of desperate resolve, laid both hands on Mizelda's body, seeking to tether her to life with the faint light of healing magic.

"Is this a time to be standing dumbstruck? Lend a hand."

"—Ah"

Startled by Rem's actions, Subaru, unable to move, had Abel's voice reach his ears.
Abel, still unable to climb up from his dangling position. Subaru shook his head and hurried over, looking down into the hollow gap of what had been the balcony.

Abel, hanging by the curtain wrapped around his right arm, turned his sharp eyes up at Subaru.

"You survived. What terrible luck you have."

"...Sounds like you've still got plenty of edge left in that smart mouth of yours too."

Twisting his cheek at Abel's endless mouth, Subaru grabbed the curtain and hauled him up.
Honestly, if Subaru was battered, so was Abel being hauled up. He wanted nothing more than to throw down his limbs and let go of consciousness right now, but he couldn't afford to.

"It's not just Mizelda-san..."

Caught up in Arakiya's violence, many wounded lay collapsed.
In an environment where those who could use healing magic were precious, first aid that didn't rely on magic was also needed. There was no room to let go of consciousness.
To fall here would mean accepting the loss of something.
That could not be allowed to happen.
So—

"Hurry up and get up here...!"

Gritting his teeth, dragging the curtain, Subaru grabbed Abel's hand once it was within reach. Trusting the feel of the grip returned to him, he somehow hauled up the man taller than himself.
Before Subaru, who fell back on his rear, Abel, restored from his dangling position, caught his breath slightly and said,

"Well done. I shall commend you."

"Shut up..."

Cutting off that praise with not a shred of sincerity in it, Subaru clicked his tongue softly.
Just as he was.

"—Do not move as you please, base fool. Whom do you take to be the ruler of this place?"

"――――"

Subaru, still on the floor, and Abel, on one knee, both fell silent.
The one who quietly overpowered them both was the crimson beauty, arms crossed as if to flaunt her ample figure—Priscilla's glare and her single word.

They knew each other, and it wasn't as though they were strangers.
But asked whether their relationship was a good one, one would hesitate to nod. That wasn't only because they stood in opposing camps in the Royal Selection—Priscilla's temperament itself was the cause.
Priscilla, haughty toward everyone and never one to yield, was a powerful ally when she could pursue the same goal as you, and an unpredictable bomb when she couldn't.

At the water-gate city of Priestella, she had been a reliable ally in the fight. Turning to this time—how would it play out?
Could they consider her, who had lent a hand in repelling Arakiya, an ally?
In any case—

"Loath as I am to admit it, the ruler of this situation would be you, wouldn't it, Priska Benedict."

In place of Subaru, unable to move or speak, it was Abel who spoke up.
Still on one knee, Abel had addressed Priscilla by a different name. Yet that name was one that not only he, but Arakiya too, before she'd fallen, had spoken.
Called by that name, Priscilla gave a small snort and said,

"Unfortunately, Priska Benedict lost her battle and died a wretched death. Could one already beneath the grave speak so boldly as this?"

"...I see. Then who are you, and what name do you go by?"

"—Priscilla Barielle. That is my name. Do well to remember it, Vincent Abelux."

Answering the question with bold assurance, Priscilla's and Abel's gazes crossed.
Between them passed a storm of gazes bearing pressure like flame or thunder. Subaru, caught between them, could do nothing but bow his head and try not to be blown away.

Priscilla, who had known Abel's name, and Abel, who had called Priscilla by a different name.
Sensing the extraordinary relationship between the two of them, Subaru quietly held his breath.
And then, into that grim, bloodstained air—

"—Hey, anybody, I don't care who, wanna help me out here? I'm about at my limit, y'know."

—came the pitiful cry for help from Al, knocked off the balcony and caught on an exterior crystal lamp, shaking the air in vain.
