One Day II Part 1: "A Certain Sisterly Relationship"

――Watching it was maddening. That was the one thing everyone who witnessed the scene agreed on.

"Y-yo, sis. That load looks heavy. Want me to carry it for ya?"

"Garf... I'm fine. If I go on being treated as a guest forever, it'll start to feel awkward, so I'm just helping out a little. You should go enjoy yourself, Garf."

"O-oh yeah? A-a'right. Lemme know if somethin' comes up."

Garfiel answered Frederica, who was pushing a handcart, with few words, standing rooted in place and scratching his cheek with a finger. Frederica, for her part, gazed at Garfiel for just a moment before seeming to catch herself and turning her attention back to her work.

The small wheels bit into the floor, making a faint sound as they carried on down the manor's corridor. Garfiel watched his sister's retreating back with a vacant expression.

"...Maddening."

Clawing at his short blond hair, Garfiel let out a groan and walked off in the opposite direction from his sister. Watching that scene from around the corner of the hallway, Subaru pressed a hand to his forehead and lamented the exchange that had just made him itch all over with secondhand awkwardness.

"It's been a week since they properly settled down and reunited, and yet... those two look sooo agonizingly slow about it."

The one who let that slip was Emilia, hiding in the same spot and in the same way as Subaru. Subaru had crouched down, and Emilia had peered over him to watch the exchange just now. Feeling Emilia's breath drawing away, Subaru stood up and rolled his hips.

"A reunion after ten years... and one after ten years of complete silence, at that. I heard their parting back then wasn't exactly a cheerful 'good luck out there' kind of thing, so I get why it'd be awkward, but..."

He crossed his arms and tilted his head. He understood it, but it was still maddening. Still agonizing. Watching it made his back itch. Garfiel and Frederica, siblings, had been like that ever since their reunion. And the annoying part was that they put on a reasonably convincing act of getting along whenever other people were watching.

Garfiel looked hot-headed and simple, but he was surprisingly sharp and could act the part when he needed to. As for Frederica's skill at putting on appearances, that went without saying. Without any apparent coordination between them, the two siblings had managed to fool plenty of onlookers over these past few days. Though, for whatever reason, Subaru kept happening to run into the two of them alone together, so it had been obvious to him the whole time — and this time, Emilia had witnessed it too.

"It looked like Garfiel was reaching out, but somehow Frederica doesn't seem to be looking straight at him. They finally got to see each other again, so why?"

"When there's a lot of baggage from how you parted, it's hard to just simply be happy about a reunion. Well, that's just manga knowledge talking, but... I guess the problem's on Frederica's end."

Just as Emilia said, Garfiel seemed ready to open his heart. But Frederica's attitude toward him left something to be desired. Probably what Frederica felt was something like guilt toward her little brother. Garfiel, whom she had left behind in the Sanctuary. During that time, over no small stretch of years, Garfiel had quietly honed his fangs and nurtured a stubbornly closed-off child's heart. Of the troubles surrounding the Sanctuary this time, about a third of them could be said to stem from that. And a portion of the responsibility for bringing that situation about did, indeed, fall on her.

That said, it wasn't as if Frederica had betrayed Garfiel, and it wasn't really anyone's fault. Or rather, if you had to point fingers, the one at fault was Roswaal. So neither Subaru nor anyone else in the group had any intention of blaming Frederica or Garfiel.

――But apparently what the people around them thought and what the two people involved thought were different things.

"Tough situation, huh..."

In the end, it came down to Frederica's sense of responsibility being too strong. Originally, when the Sanctuary was on the verge of being lost, it was Frederica who stepped out into the outside world to build a place so the people inside wouldn't be left stranded. For a girl of around ten years old, it was a decision noble and heavy beyond her years to shoulder. Perhaps even that sense of duty had its roots in having left Garfiel behind. As a result, the Sanctuary was indeed lost, and the very thing Frederica had feared came to pass. Thanks to her efforts, a system to take in the Sanctuary's residents — however rough around the edges — had already taken shape in various places. That much was an achievement she ought to be proud of. And yet, if her guilt outweighed her sense of accomplishment, it was because her self-reproach ran too deep.

"I mean, that's no good, right? If you did something that impressive, you oughta stand tall about it."

"Mm, I think so too. And if she thinks she did something bad, she should properly apologize. And then get forgiven... I want those two to get along."

The hallway where both Garfiel and Frederica had already vanished from sight. Emilia glanced toward the spot where the two of them had faced each other moments before, narrowing her violet eyes wistfully. Subaru studied her profile and nodded. "Yeah, you're right."

"Alright. Let's give them a push, then — see if we can nudge this along ourselves."

"A push, meaning... you want to help them make up?"

"Exactly. A tangled-up sibling relationship, brother and sister. If it's too sticky to move on its own, why not have some outsiders stir things up and let some fresh air in?"

Subaru clapped his hands and raised a single finger, striking a proposing pose. Emilia fell silent for a moment, thinking it over, then nodded with a determined look.

"That's... right. Yeah, I think family should get along. Okay. Let's do it. We'll work really hard and make those two make up!"

"Nobody says 'work really hard' like that these days..."

Subaru murmured vaguely at Emilia, who was pumping a small fist, fired up and ready to go. He thought, come to think of it, it had been a while since they'd had this kind of exchange, and this kind of retort.

The various problems surrounding the Sanctuary had barely reached a temporary resolution when the Emilia Camp was struck by a fierce round of aftermath cleanup, along with the question of where to base themselves going forward.

The manor, burned to the ground by Roswaal's scheme (though the man himself partially denied involvement). Even if they intended to rebuild, restoring something reduced to cleanly charred rubble would take time. Apparently, even in this world, there was no convenient magic to restore something that had lost its shape, nor any Fullmetal Alchemist-style technique to throw a building together in an instant. The only option, it seemed, was the ordinary route: calling in builders from a nearby village or town, or craftsmen who specialized in constructing noble manors and villas.

"We-e-ell, that manor was originally a secondary residence, separate from the main house... a place to keep Lady Emilia hidden away before the Royal Selection began, so she wouldn't draw attention. Once the Royal Selection got properly underway, I'd already been planning to move our base to the main house before too long. So-o-o, there isn't really much of a problem to speak of, you see."

That was Roswaal's answer when they went looking for a base other than the burned-down manor. Apparently House Mathers owned several residences besides the secondary one that had burned down, and among them, the one referred to as the main house was the very place already prepared as their future base.

The main house, it seemed, had no residents apart from the staff entrusted with its upkeep. For the time being, once that manor finished preparing to receive its masters, they would move their base there. The problem was where to stay in the meantime, in place of the burned manor.

"No need to worry about that either, I've got it covered. Not too far from the Sanctuary, there's a manor belonging to relatives of mi-i-ine. A branch line of the Mathers family, you understand. We should be able to impose on them there. Though descending on them in too great a number might cause some trouble, I imagine."

Roswaal's relatives being the source of the proposal was itself a cause for unease, but no one present had any alternative but to go along with it. And so, after discussion, it was decided: the key figures of the Emilia Camp would rely on that distant relative's manor, while the residents of the Sanctuary and Arlam Village would head to Arlam Village for the time being. The Sanctuary's residents would either be welcomed in as villagers of Arlam, or else introduced afresh to some other town or village within the domain. Frederica was the one laying the groundwork for all of that, and it was, quite fairly, something that ought to be credited to her.

To begin with, the Sanctuary's residents, though a mix of Demi-Humans, were all half-bloods. None of them differed drastically in appearance from humans, so blending in wasn't especially difficult. As for the differences in custom that came from not knowing the outside world, the kind-hearted people of Arlam Village would surely be happy to show them the ropes without complaint. Even with appearances squared away, various problems would surely pile up down the road. Even so, that no glaring trouble had occurred so far was a testament to everyone doing their best. If only, before anything happened, the problems already in view could be resolved.

――The matter of repairing the relationship between Garfiel and Frederica was judged to be the sort of problem that ought to be settled during just such a stretch of happy, uneventful time.

"So anyway, we're about to launch Operation Make Garfiel and Frederica Get Along... any good ideas?"

"You walk into my room and the first thing out of your mouth is that nonsense? Disturbing Ram's peaceful hour is a heavy crime, Barusu."

Ram said this in a voice frozen with emotion, glaring up at Subaru from her seat in the chair. Her expression was, as ever, unreadable, but the faint swirl of emotion visible in her eyes was surely the fruit of their acquaintance by now. Judging from Subaru's eyes — certified, so to speak, in reading Ram's emotions — what Ram was currently feeling was something like "irritation."

"You look at me with those eyes a lot, but does that mean you're basically irritated every time we interact? Doesn't being annoyed all day long get tiring?"

"Rest assured. It's only when I'm faced with someone I find tiresome to talk to, or someone I don't think there's much benefit in dealing with."

"I see, that's a relie— wait a second."

Subaru frowned at Ram, who had just implied, without saying so outright, that he was one or the other right now. At his reaction, Ram gave a small snort through her nose and closed the book she'd been holding. Then she rose from her seat and tried to offer the chair she'd been sitting in to Emilia, standing beside Subaru.

"Lady Emilia, please."

"Thanks. But it's fine. I'll tire you out if I stay long, so I'll be leaving soon anyway."

"I see. Then I won't hold back."

"You didn't even offer it to me. And you really don't hold back at all, do you, sis."

Subaru shrugged as Ram neatly reclaimed the seat she'd half-offered away. But he caught the way Ram's brow twitched faintly at part of what he'd said. What Ram had reacted to was, most likely, the word "sis."

"Rem's condition hasn't changed, it seems."

"...No. Today too, she's sleeping quietly, so quietly it's hard to tell if she's alive."

At Emilia's concerned voice, Ram answered with her tone dropped slightly. Where their eyes were directed, right beside where Ram sat, was a bed, and a single sleeping girl. Short blue hair, and features identical to Ram's, who kept watch over her sleeping face. She was dressed in a pale blue sleeping gown, though the size of the chest pushing up the coverlet was perhaps the one difference from Ram.

Needless to say, the name of the girl who kept sleeping on was Rem. Even after the Roswaal Manor burned down and their base moved, the girl remained asleep. Unless the cause behind it was removed, she would likely go on sleeping just like this. And that cause was——

"Still not ready to admit it?"

"I told you, didn't I? Ram isn't so thoughtless as to swallow everything whole before even exchanging a word with her. ...Though it's also true that what's in front of my eyes is too convincing to simply deny out of hand."

Ram's answer carried complicated emotion in her pale red eyes, and Subaru's mouth twisted. Ram's expression as she watched the sleeping Rem was, to Subaru, who knew what their relationship had once been, terribly sorrowful. An older sister who doted on her younger sister; a younger sister who adored her older sister in turn. Rem and Ram's relationship as sisters had once been exactly that kind of beautiful family bond.

Rem kept sleeping, and in Ram's memory there existed neither the name nor the memories of that beloved little sister. It was something Subaru had known, something he had expected — and yet it still stirred up an unbearably wretched feeling in him. And even so——

"For someone with such complicated feelings about it, you sure come here on your feet every day."

"...I wonder why that is. Honestly, even Ram isn't entirely sure what Ram wants. It's just that, somehow, being near this girl who calls herself Ram's sister settles my mind. ...No, there's also a part of it that doesn't settle at all."

"A part that doesn't settle?"

"It's not quite the same as looking at a face like my own... When I gaze at this girl's sleeping face, something stirs deep in my chest. It's like chasing after a formless mist — just this sensation of something I can never quite reach."

Subaru caught his breath quietly as Ram gently laid a hand over her own chest. Rem's memories were lost, and in a world where everyone but Subaru had forgotten her — even in such circumstances, her existence remained lodged in Ram, her one blood relative, like a thorn. Ram herself didn't seem to know what to call that thorn, but if it was something the girl named Rem had left behind in the sister she adored, that alone was enough of a foothold.

"I can tell you stories, what she was like, memories — whatever I can, if you want?"

"――You'd best not."

To Subaru's offer to help her recover her memories, Ram answered with a shake of her head. As Subaru's brow furrowed, Ram pressed a hand to her lips as if in thought.

"This unreachable emptiness. It's like there's a hole in Ram, right where this girl's presence should be. If that's the case, then whatever gets poured into it will surely just spill right back out. Even now, being told this girl is Ram's sister... presented with something as plain as our faces as proof, and still it doesn't feel real. I have this feeling that the moment I stop coming to see her like this every day... even these feelings I'm holding now might disappear."

"...Is that the Witch Cult's 'Gluttony' curse?"

Emilia, who had kept silent up to this point, listening, cut in with a look that said she couldn't let that pass. In front of Ram, who lifted her face, Emilia raised her brows with a rare glint of anger.

"A gluttony that devours 'names' and 'memories'... I never had a good image of the Witch Cult to begin with, but this might just be the worst of it."

"...The Witch Cult."

At Ram's account, Emilia too lowered her eyes and murmured the word softly. Subaru, meanwhile, was both startled at Ram's deduction and sickened at its sheer cruelty. Subaru, who clearly remembered Rem, had no real sense of it — but Rem's existence hadn't simply been "lost" from Ram and Emilia's memories. It was "being lost," continuously. Like sand trickling endlessly through an hourglass that never stops, even now, on and on.

"So in the end, unless we cut off the cause at the root, there's nothing to be done..."

The more Subaru talked about their memories, the faster the sand seemed to fall. Perhaps even from Subaru, the memories would vanish the moment they were spoken aloud. Ram's fear was that Rem would vanish from this world entirely — that, too, was likely part of it.

"Lady Emilia, it seems you have some feelings of your own about the Witch Cult."

Beside Subaru, who was biting his lip, Ram looked up at Emilia. Under the gaze of those pale red eyes, Emilia's pale profile stiffened, and then she slowly lowered her chin.

"Up until now, I've thought about all sorts of things regarding 'the Witch.' Because our features are similar, I've had plenty of nasty things said about me too... but the Witch Cult, though—"

"――――"

"I think I'd just quietly filed it away among the things I wanted to forget. But 'that' as I remember it, and 'that' as it is now — I can't help but think of them as two completely different things. I want to know what happened after that, and why it turned out this way."

"Emilia-tan, I don't want to say this, but... those people aren't the type you can have a normal conversation with, y'know? You're probably gonna get hurt."

Subaru had no intention of breaking Emilia's resolve, but staying silent wouldn't be fair either. The Witch Cult Subaru knew was, from head to tail, a lump of malice built entirely from fanatics. He had no idea what "that" Emilia spoke of had been like in the past. But the Witch Cult as it existed now was "that."

"Thanks. For worrying."

At Subaru's concern, Emilia gave a faint smile and shook her head.

"It's okay, I understand that much too. The things in my memory, the people who were with me... it's already a hundred years ago. There's no way they could still be alive now. A hundred years is a reeeally long time for a human lifespan. So I don't expect to see them again."

"But you still want to know what happened to them... right?"

"Sorry, for being selfish. But I feel like I'm the only one who has to know. What happened in that place, what feelings existed there... how Juice and Mother felt — I'm the only one who saw it."

Whether picturing the two people in her mind, Emilia's eyes looked lonely, though her lips still carried a gentle smile. Her mother's name, and the other one, Juice. So those were, for Emilia, precious memories — and also memories connected to a Witch Cult utterly unlike the one that existed now.

"Complicated feelings, Mr. Juice, whoever you are..."

Muttering only inside his mouth, Subaru let out a breath tinged with something like a grudge toward a person whose face he'd never even seen. It was a complicated feeling about the fondness and sorrow with which Emilia spoke that name. If the Witch Cult's policies hadn't changed since the time this person called Juice had held a place among them, Emilia might never have been driven into such dire straits. If he was going to stand on Emilia's side regardless, Subaru found himself thinking with self-serving resentment, it would've helped if he'd stood by her from beginning to end.

"――But Ram doesn't have the confidence to behave as gently as Lady Emilia."

It was a voice, still quiet as ever, but laced with a bone-chilling hostility. Subaru unconsciously held his breath and looked at Ram's profile as she sat, gazing at Rem. Amid her expressionless face, the light in her eyes — strangely red now — danced, and she said,

"Whatever the Witch Cult is, it has nothing to do with Ram. Ram won't say a word against Lady Emilia's wish to hear them out. I won't say anything — but please remember that's a separate matter from Ram's revenge."

"Ram..."

"Witch Cult, Gluttony, whatever they're called, Ram doesn't care — but Ram always repays what she's given, whether it's a debt of gratitude or a grudge. A vile wretch who bores a hole in someone's heart — being torn to pieces wouldn't even be enough."

Rage and a killing intent poured out from her entire body, and Ram's small frame seemed to blur before Subaru's eyes. As though, right there, stood some towering presence of overwhelming force — an Oni, precisely.

"Gluttony, Ram will tear apart and kill so thoroughly it won't even be worthy of memory."

More than resolve, it was a death sentence. There was no one present to whom the sentence was pronounced, and even her tone was carefully composed. And yet it was, unmistakably, a death sentence — the sensation of an icicle driven into his back made Subaru hesitate even to speak.

"――――"

After that, silence fell completely over the room. Even the sound of shifting felt like it might crack the taut air, so no one moved. What finally broke that tension was, of all people, the very one who had drawn it taut.

"That wasn't like me, was it."

The moment she let that slip with a breath, the tension of a moment ago vanished entirely. Subaru let his shoulders drop in relief.

"Nah, that was plenty like you. The Ram I know, when it comes to her little sister, is exactly the type to get all violent while still being a proper big sister about it."

"...I see."

It had been a dangerous thing to say, but there was no doubt it came from genuine feeling for Rem. Subaru chose to focus only on that part, and felt glad for Ram's sentiment. Besides, Subaru couldn't forgive Gluttony either. If he could, he'd even want to be the one to take Gluttony's head himself, without ceding it to Ram.

The sensation of killing someone — his desperate, all-out fight against Petelgeuse hadn't left Subaru with any particularly clear sense of it. Perhaps, at the crucial moment, his hesitation to take a life would hold him back. Even so, "Gluttony" was unforgivable, and he was resolved to do whatever it took if it meant saving Rem.

"...Guess we got off track."

Scratching his head, Subaru kept the dark thoughts he'd just been having off his face. Ram had been looking at him meaningfully, Emilia with concern, but he was fairly sure he'd managed to put on a smile for both of them.

"That's right. Since you're interrupting Ram's leisurely enjoyment of this sudden windfall of a vacation, you must have brought a matter worth the trouble."

"What's with that tyrannical way of putting pressure on me. Frederica, who's in the same position as you, feels bad about being treated as a guest and is helping out with the manor's chores, you know."

"Ram is currently injured. Besides, Frederica working despite being treated as a guest is, if anything, the one failing to read the room. ...No doubt she can't just sit still with Garf nearby, or else Clind's been stirring her up."

"Clind-san?"

The name Ram spoke with such distaste belonged to the young steward serving the branch family of House Mathers whose hospitality they were currently relying on. A slender, handsome young man radiating an overwhelming aura of "the capable, gentle type." He gave off something reminiscent of Julius, though unlike that man, his manner was courteous and remarkably considerate. Which was precisely why it struck Subaru as odd that Ram didn't seem to view him very favorably. Then again, perhaps in Ram's eyes, everyone besides Roswaal looked that way.

"Ask the two of them directly about why Frederica and Clind don't get along. More importantly, I want to get back to my reading, so hurry up and state your business."

"Sorry, for dragging this out so long. I think Subaru started to say it earlier, but it's about Frederica and Garfiel..."

Emilia, at last, tried to steer the conversation back on course with the imperious Ram. The plan to somehow fix the awkward relationship between Garfiel and Frederica had been drawn up well enough, but the crucial matter of actual content had immediately run aground. The reason being that neither Subaru nor Emilia had much experience to draw on when it came to tackling the task of mending a sibling relationship.

Subaru was an only child through and through, and so was Emilia. Neither of them had been blessed with siblings, so an answer regarding blood ties different from a parent-child bond didn't come easily to either of them. Though, to be fair, in both their cases even their parent-child relationships were somewhat removed from the ordinary — but that's a matter best set aside here.

And so, in search of an advisor, they'd wandered the manor and, having a rough idea of where to find her, ended up visiting Ram. After all, as far as Subaru was concerned, she was the closest thing to a pair of siblings he had at hand. As things stood, the relationship between the two of them existed nowhere but in Subaru's memory now — but even so, given how well they'd once built a relationship as older and younger sister, there was hope that Ram might have something useful to say. And above all, Ram was an old acquaintance of both Garfiel and Frederica, the siblings actually involved. She might come up with some approach to closing that ten-year gap between them, drawn from some episode neither Subaru nor Emilia knew about.

With eyes full of all that hope, Emilia turned toward Ram — and her lips froze mid-motion. Subaru tilted his head at Emilia's sudden stiffening, followed her gaze to see what had happened, and froze the same way.

"...What?"

Under the weight of both their stares, Ram narrowed her eyes, looking distinctly uncomfortable. In her hands was a single book, drawing both their gazes.

She had been holding a book titled How to Grow Closer to Your Younger Brothers and Sisters — a title uncomfortably, critically relevant to the matter at hand.

――It seemed that theirs wasn't the only pair struggling to figure out how to handle a sibling relationship.
