Arc 3 Chapter 3: "Royal Capital Travel Plans"

"You're going to the royal capital, right? I'm coming too!"

The guest had gone home, and the drawing room had just breathed a sigh of relief—that atmosphere Subaru shattered magnificently with his opening line.

He threw open the door without warning, set a tray with tea down on the table, and wordlessly handed cups to the two parties to the discussion with a "here, here."

"Snacks are capped at three hundred yen, and no staying up late just because you're excited about the departure."

Subaru talked with all the momentum of someone for whom his own participation was simply a given. In response, the two who'd received their cups—Emilia and Roswaal—exchanged glances.

"See?"

"...Right."

Roswaal, one eyebrow raised with a smirk, was met by Emilia's weary agreement. Not liking the atmosphere of the two of them understanding each other without him, Subaru writhed lightly in protest.

"Don't go understanding each other with your eyes, that's jealousy-inducing! Mix me into the conversation too, entangle me, sing me a lullaby!"

"To begin with, who told you we're going to the royal capital... did I let it slip...?"

As if ashamed of her own slip of the tongue, Emilia put a hand to her forehead, then slowly shook her head. Turning to face Subaru, she prefaced with a "listen" as if explaining something to a child.

"We're not going on a pleasure trip. I've been summoned for something important... yes, it's important."

"I get that. It's Royal Selection stuff, right? A matter that could shake the whole kingdom, I know that much. But even so, I'm asking. Take me with you."

Subaru knelt on the carpet and pressed his palms together as if begging.

Not knowing how to respond to the plea, Emilia looked around the room's faces as if seeking help. But—

"What's the matter? Ah, don't mind me, please choose as your heart desi-i-ires."

One remained a bystander, smirking.

"This tea leaf's aroma... could it be Ram's secret stash... no, that's too much even to consider... but if it's Barusu, he might just do it...!"

One was utterly preoccupied with the leaves used in the tea that had been brought in.

And as for the last one—

"Isn't it fine? Since he wants to go so badly, why not just take him along. It seems Subaru-kun has acquaintances in the royal capital too, and there's the fact that he originally came to be carried to the manor because of an injury. You should let them see his face and set their minds at ease."

—this was the one who should have offered the most sensible reaction, and yet found herself in the position of backing Subaru up.

"Yosh, nice assist. Rem, Rem, Reeem, come here."

"Yes!"

Responding to Subaru's beckoning from the carpet, Rem smiled brightly and slid over to sit beside him. As if it were already routine, Rem offered her head, and Subaru, smiling, deployed advanced technique to stroke and praise her without mussing her blue hair.

Watching Rem, utterly compliant, purring happily under his hand, Emilia felt a curiosity rear its head—ah, maybe I want to try that too—and suppressed it.

"To begin with, what does Subaru intend to do by coming along? It's true this is related to the Royal Selection, but that has nothing to do with Subaru—"

"If you say something lonely like 'nothing to do with me,' I'll cry. Whether Emilia-tan can become king—or queen, I guess—hangs in the balance, and I want to be involved, even if it's just on the sidelines. And to do that, I at least need to be within arm's reach."

"That's exactly why I'm saying I can't take you."

Emilia met Subaru's eyes, brimming with determination, and knit her brow.

His feelings made her happy, but she'd already been saved by them more than enough. Her life had been saved, and just days ago he'd nearly lost his own life saving many others. The debt she owed Subaru was immeasurable—pitiably so, given how little he himself seemed to grasp it.

On top of that, to be given even more by him—that kind of dependence wasn't something she could allow herself.

"If I take you along, you'll definitely push yourself too hard again. I don't want to let that happen. Understand?"

"Emilia-tan's the one who doesn't understand. I hate overdoing it, hate recklessness, hate rashness—I won't do anything beyond my own capacity."

Against Emilia's pleading, Subaru crossed his arms, still kneeling formally, and puffed out his chest.

To his brazen, defiant declaration, he added a "but," and continued,

"If it's within arm's reach, that's a different story. Especially if it's something to do with Emilia-tan. Right? Please, understand."

"That kind of thing... I don't understand it at all..."

Emilia couldn't fathom why Subaru was pressing this so hard.

Given all the suffering, all the pain he'd already endured, Emilia's feeling was that she wanted to let Subaru rest now.

Why, of his own free will, would he push a body that wasn't even fully healed to force through something so reckless? It was baffling to her.

Surely his usual glib talk wasn't something he actually meant seriously.

As Emilia's thoughts sank into a sea of confusion, Subaru looked up at her with the eyes of an abandoned dog. And as silence fell over the drawing room—

"Ye-e-es, that's e-e-enough,"

—it was Roswaal who broke the mood, literally clapping his hands.

He had been watching the exchange between the two before him with something like deep feeling, but now—

"Since the discussion isn't going a-a-anywhere, I'll just wra-a-ap it up quickly. —Subaru-kun, you shall accompany us to the royal capital. This is an order from your master."

"Roswaal!?" "Yattaa! Now you're talking, Rozu-cchi!"

Roswaal brushed aside Emilia's reluctant objection. At his words, Emilia looked caught off guard, while Subaru responded with a delighted thumbs-up.

But to those reactions, Roswaal wagged a finger and went "tsk tsk tsk."

"Ho-o-owever, Subaru-kun's coming to the royal capital is stri-i-ictly for treatment purposes. Entirely separate from anything to do with the Royal Selection. Understood?"

"Treatment...?"

Subaru tilted his head at Roswaal's words, a question mark practically visible.

Not understanding the meaning, he tried to stand up while still puzzling it over—and, perhaps because his legs had gone numb from sitting formally, he toppled forward spectacularly. Rem, beside him, instantly caught him by the scruff of the neck.

"Gueh! ...Nice assist, Rem. But next time, do it a little more gracefully."

"Mnn, so no petting for me, then...?"

Rubbing his throat, Subaru offered his tart critique, and Rem, dejected, stepped back. Then Subaru turned back to Roswaal and leaned forward with a "so?" as if demanding to know the true meaning of his earlier statement.

"What do you mean, treatment purposes? Not to brag, but aside from the scars I've got left, I'm basically back to full health, you know? I'll admit there's a little room left for rehab."

Subaru spun both outstretched arms in circles, showing off just how well he was doing. But everyone in the manor, Emilia included, knew that his body was far from perfect.

There were signs of several inconveniences in his daily life, evident both in his manor work and in the morning "radio calisthenics" he insisted on doing. That he was trying, and failing utterly, to hide it from those around him only made it all the more pitiable.

On the subject of Subaru's bodily troubles—something that had become an unspoken understanding among them—Roswaal shook his head at Subaru's continued attempt to play dumb.

"No use hi-i-iding it. Everyone knows you have lingering aftereffects. Using the Bokko Fruit while depleted. Taking Ulgarm's fangs and having far too many curse marks etched into your body—those are the main factors, but because of them, the flow of mana circulating through your body right now is extremely poor. Sluggish."

"...I mean, it's hard to take seriously being told I'm 'off' by something I can't even see."

"Subaru. The mana that fills a body is quite literally a lifeline. For that flow to stagnate means the circulation of life itself is being obstructed. ...Please, I'm begging you, listen properly."

Perhaps embarrassed at having his poor condition seen through, Subaru wasn't taking it terribly seriously, and Emilia said this to him. Whether her feelings got through or not, Subaru offered a small "sorry."

"Got it, my body's in crisis. So how does that connect to getting treated in the royal capital?"

"Treating your body requires the power of an expert in water mana. Did you happen to meet the envoy who was here earlier, Subaru-kun?"

"The tall cat-ears girl playing cute? Honestly, not really my type."

"That girl is one of the most exceptional wielders of water mana in the entire royal capital. Borrowing her power should make it possible to heal your body's ailments."

At Roswaal's words, Subaru's face twisted openly in distaste.

A person he's not good with—Emilia silently agreed with his assessment. That person's way of seeing right through you, and then talking circles around you regardless, wasn't something she was comfortable with either.

Under the pretext of delivering the summons to the royal capital, she'd been made to discuss all sorts of things she hadn't wanted to.

Still—

"She went so far as to let you owe her a favor, just to arrange this opportunity for your treatment. Don't you think you ought to indulge Emilia-sama's thoughtfulness a little?"

"Huh?"

"Wait, Roswaal! That's—"

At being exposed as, if not careless, then certainly deliberate, Emilia flushed with indignation and rounded on Roswaal. But Roswaal, cool as ever, let her angry voice wash right past him.

"Emilia-sama isn't fond of that girl either, you know. But there's the matter of Subaru-kun's body, isn't there? So she gamely sat through the conversation and, one way or ano-o-other, managed to wring out a concession, and got your treatment arranged. Rather touching, don't you think?"

"RO-SU-WAA-RU!"

Her secret dealings fully exposed, Emilia called his name syllable by syllable.

Her silver hair swayed with anger, and she could clearly feel blood rushing to her face from both fury and shame. She began weaving mana, wild with rage, thinking to hurl it at his long indigo hair—but,

"Emilia-tan, seriously?"

—Subaru, at the center of it all, asked, and that dissolved her fury entirely.

Trying to confirm the truth of the matter, Subaru looked at her, and Emilia turned away, embarrassed.

"Because—it's partly my fault that Subaru's body isn't healed. The reason you even came to this manor in the first place was because you protected me... and that business with the witchbeasts in the forest, too—that was something I should have handled, but Subaru did it instead. So this is just, well, repaying a debt, or making fair compensation for a loss, or something like that..."

"Emilia-tan, that phrasing's too stiff. It sounds so businesslike it's killing the mood."

Subaru gestured, poking fun at Emilia's rapid-fire self-justification. To that, Emilia said "anyway!" forcefully,

"Subaru worked so hard, so you have an obligation to accept the gratitude owed for it. Otherwise I won't be able to face you as an equal going forward. Think of it this way—this way I won't have to feel a strange sense of guilt every morning when I see you, so really, it's for my own sake."

"That's a pattern I haven't heard in a while, but wait, you think that every morning!?"

"I don't! I mean, I was grateful, but—!"

It had all become a complete mess.

No matter how she tried, she was truly bad at expressing her own thoughts to someone else. Subaru's probably exasperated too, she thought, glancing timidly at his face—and sure enough, there was a wry smile there.

Still wearing that wry smile, though, he crossed his arms and said,

"So in the end, Emilia-tan was actually pro me-going-to-the-royal-capital too, huh. Then why the whole opposition pose at first?"

"Because if I let your demands through that easily, knowing you, you'd get carried away and push yourself too hard. I know you're a reckless little troublemaker."

"Nobody says 'reckless little troublemaker' these days..."

Subaru muttered under his breath, tilting his head, and Emilia stuck her tongue out at him in frustration.

At that, Subaru's expression went oddly rapturous, and if it counted as some kind of small revenge, Emilia was more than satisfied. Quietly, she let that settle her grudge for now.

And then,

"We-e-ell then, it's settled. Subaru-kun will be accompanying us to the royal capital too. We'll spend all of tomorrow on preparations, and depart the morning after—that work for everyone?"

"Understood." "No objections!" "Understood, Roswaal-sama," they said together.

At Roswaal's closing words, everyone in the room gave their respective response.

And so, the direction of Roswaal Manor's trip to the royal capital was decided.

The trip to the royal capital, with Subaru now included, was planned, and the departure was set for the day after tomorrow.

Even with such a hectic schedule arranged, the reality remained that the servants had to carry on with the manor's day-to-day work as usual.

Subaru, too, as part of his rehabilitation as a servant-in-training, had been assigned the simple task of tidying up the guest rooms, and was diligently at it.

"So then, does that mean Ram's staying at the manor?"

Carefully smoothing the sheets in the guest room, Subaru asked Ram, who stood behind him.

Ram, doing nothing at all under the pretext of supervising Subaru's work, nodded languidly at the question.

"Yes. We can't very well leave the manor completely empty. Normally Rem would be the one staying behind, but this time she was selfish enough to say she 'wants to go.' So Ram allowed it."

The elder sister, having let her younger sister's wish stand, curved her lips ever so slightly.

Ram, for all her airs, was clearly soft on her sister. Despite being kept away from her beloved Roswaal, she didn't seem the least bit put out by it.

"Somehow, you look kind of happy."

"Happy... maybe so. That girl, who used to think of nothing but doing her best at whatever was given to her—the fact that she now has a will of her own might make Ram happy."

Of course, Subaru wasn't the only one who'd found Rem's disposition regrettable. Ram, after all, was her twin, together with her since birth. She must have wished, far more times than Subaru ever had, that something could be done about it.

In that sense, Subaru and Ram could be called comrades who shared the same opinion about Rem's nature.

But Ram turned a sharp gaze on her fellow comrade-in-arms.

"The one thing Ram dislikes is that the catalyst was a man like this."

"I've heard that one too many times already, and it doesn't erase the fact that I had a little influence on Rem's heart! Go on, be jealous!!"

"Fura."

"Whoaaa!?"

With a single incantation, a gust of wind kicked up, and the blast sent Subaru's body flying onto the bed. He landed right in the middle of the sheets he'd just finished making, rumpling them completely, and, buried in white cloth, surfaced as if swimming through the soft fabric.

"Hey! Magic's not fair, magic! Using magic to retaliate because you lost the verbal argument—that's seriously nasty!"

"It's not retaliation. Ram simply released the dark feelings pooled in her stomach by unleashing them on Barusu."

"That's what people call retaliation, or taking it out on someone, or venting your frustrations!"

Speaking to Ram, who had turned her face away, Subaru then set about briskly remaking the bed.

His body was still far from its best, but his dexterity remained unimpaired, and the beauty of the neatly made white sheets was, even by his own judgment, something to be proud of.

"Anyway, coachman, plus personal attendant, plus bodyguard, plus guide—Rem's range of talents leaves me speechless."

"You're forgetting the 'Subaru-kun's attendant' title Rem's been calling herself lately."

"That one's her own unofficial club, though."

Still, given how much he owed her one way or another, he couldn't exactly deny it outright.

In any case, the main party for this trip consisted of four from Roswaal Manor. Emilia, the one directly concerned, and her patron Roswaal. Subaru, who'd wrangled a ticket out of sheer stubbornness. And Rem, accompanying them as the one in charge of looking after the three of them.

Which left Ram and Beatrice as the ones staying behind—

"Thinking about it again, that's a seriously worrying group to leave in charge. Are you guys really going to be okay?"

"Rest assured. —Oni don't die even after three days without food or drink."

"You're not planning to cook for yourself at all, are you!?"

Subaru couldn't help but call out such a brazenly shameless declaration of neglecting one's duties.

"If it were just you, fine, but what about Bea-ko? I can already picture her coming downstairs, finding no meal prepared, and having a fit—'What is the meaning of this, ukiii!'"

"An imagining that, if she heard it herself, would definitely get you sent flying. Beatrice-sama should have just gone along too, honestly."

"Don't casually admit that taking care of her is too much of a hassle, caretaker! Just make her steamed potatoes, that's fine! Alternate between salt and mayonnaise servings each meal, and that should last about three days, probably. Worst case, she can just suck mayonnaise straight from the bottle."

While making a suggestion meant to sound like an improvement, Subaru inadvertently revealed the depths of his own mayo-obsessed abnormality. Ram, seemingly with no particular opinion on the matter, nodded in agreement.

And so, unbeknownst to Beatrice herself and without her present to object, her miserable diet for the coming days was decided.

Having settled that much, Subaru waved a hand and said "no, wait,"

"If even the meals alone inspire this much anxiety, what about everything else? Can you actually properly handle cleaning the whole manor and everything?"

"A foolish question, Barusu. Ram is better at cleaning than cooking."

"Even your best subject is worse than Rem's! You really are just the older sister, through and through!"

"That's right. And that is everything there is to it."

Even though nothing in particular had been decided, Ram looked at Subaru with an utterly triumphant expression.

Faced with an attitude that left him at a loss for how to react, Subaru scratched his cheek.

"So, in the end, what's happening with the manor's work?"

"In exchange for indulging Rem's selfish wish to go to the royal capital, Ram set a condition."

"Let's hear it."

"Simple. —Once we're back, Rem will work off however much time was lost, all in a single day. That's the deal."

Turning Ram's words over in his mind, this time Subaru truly lost the ability to speak.

He looked at Ram with suspicious eyes. Meeting his gaze, Ram gave a bold, confident smirk and raised the corner of her mouth.

"You'll come to understand exactly what it means to leave the manor in Ram's hands."

—There, beyond even Subaru's expectations, was a shamelessly refreshing declaration of dereliction of duty.

It was such a wonderfully brazen bit of freeloading that it was enough to make even the homebody Subaru resolve to properly get his treatment and go help Rem out.
