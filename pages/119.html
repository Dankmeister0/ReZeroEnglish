Arc 3 Chapter 42: "The Night of the Breakdown"

Cut through by a cold gaze, Subaru trembled his shoulders without a word. Whether it came from anger or from sorrow, Subaru—caught up in the surging torrent of mingled emotion—could no longer tell. Only,

"That's wrong... I'm... properly, for everyone's..."

Crusch's declaration was wide of the mark. That he was acting purely out of hatred for the Witch Cult was nothing but a warped, misguided read on things.

Subaru's feelings, their very wellspring, had always—always—been for someone else's sake. They had to be. And yet the words that followed refused to come out clearly, and hearing this, Crusch shook her head.

"One cannot deceive others with a lie that fails to deceive even oneself. What else should I call the light dwelling in your eyes right now, if not madness, if not bloodlust? Are you aware of it, Natsuki Subaru?"

"——"

"You have worn those eyes ever since you returned to this mansion, have you not?"

Whether pity or something like sympathy was folded into that dry observation, Subaru's reaction to hearing it was dramatic all the same—he found himself reaching for his own eyes, trying with his fingertips to confirm what he himself could not see. The very act of doing so only proved that some part of him could not fully deny Crusch's words.

"I do not know why you cling so to the Witch Cult. Many have had their lives twisted out of shape by that cult. You, too, are likely one of them. Your anger, your hatred, may well be justified. But that has no bearing whatsoever on these negotiations."

"Suppose—"

"Hm?"

Refusing to let the concluded negotiation be declared over, Subaru pressed on even as Crusch pronounced its end. She closed one eye, waiting for him to continue, and he drew a breath.

"Suppose I do hate the Witch Cult. So what? Yeah, they're vermin infesting the world. Better to wipe out every last one of them. I do think that. I do—but like you said, that's got nothing to do with this."

An undying hatred, an unrelenting despair, the craving for Petelgeuse's death that had made its nest in Subaru's chest—all of it filled his whole body with a dark, seething vitality. He was aware that this was not the sort of thing that left a good impression on others. But even something like this was, right now, an emotion Subaru needed in order to hold onto his sanity. And he prided himself on being able to insist on what needed to be done without being swept away by that emotion. So—

"That's not a reason to break off negotiations, is it—!"

"You're straying from the subject again, Natsuki Subaru. Whether the wellspring of your actions is hatred has no bearing on the merits of these negotiations. Though it does bear a great deal on the fact that you are unsuited as a negotiating partner. Perhaps it is not entirely unrelated to the negotiations after all."

"Unsuited... what do you mean by that..."

Literally grinding his teeth, Subaru forced the question out through them. Crusch folded her arms and, with the patience of someone dealing with an unreasonable child, broke his question down for him.

"If we suppose the wellspring of your actions is hatred for the Witch Cult, then perhaps even your approach to Emilia herself has been nothing more than a stepping stone toward that end."

Listen, Crusch said, raising one finger of her right hand atop her folded arms.

"If Emilia declares her candidacy for the Royal Selection and her origins come to light on the public stage, it is obvious the Witch Cult will stir as a matter of doctrine. As bait for drawing out and pinning down those who normally leave no thread to grasp at, there is scarcely anything more effective."

"Are you saying I'm—using Emilia as bait to get my revenge on them!?"

"Do you truly believe your current conduct lends any credibility to a cry of denial? Hatred dwells in your eyes, and bloodlust seeps into every word you speak. Neither of those is the sort of thing that can be papered over, worn away, or—still less—forgotten, once it has set so deep."

Wrong, wrong, wrong, wrong, wrong.

Crusch's words absolutely did not capture the essence of who Subaru was. The root of Subaru's feelings began with a desire to save someone. His bloodlust toward Petelgeuse was nothing more than a necessary component for achieving that. To mistake the means for the end, to say his eyes were fixed on nothing else—that was reading far, far too much into it.

Since things could not be set right without killing him, what problem could there possibly be in seeking the means, the method, to do the killing? He deserved to die. He must not be allowed to live. I am in the right. Since I am trying to do the right thing, everyone ought to cooperate with me.

"My hatred and their evil have nothing to do with each other! They're—they can't be allowed to live! That's why they should all be killed! That's how everyone gets saved! That's how everyone gets to live! So that no one has to die, they're the ones who should die—"

"I did tell you, Natsuki Subaru. A lie that cannot deceive even yourself cannot deceive others."

Her voice was cold toward Subaru, who argued back with ragged breath and bloodshot eyes. Crusch narrowed her eyes and, still seated, looked up at Subaru heaving for breath.

"To claim you are not acting out of hatred, out of bloodlust, out of loathing for the Witch Cult—that carries no conviction."

"Why... would..."

"Do you not understand?"

At Subaru's hoarse question, Crusch's eyes held a pity plain for anyone to see. But Subaru, unable to grasp what she meant, only knit his brow, and seeing this, she lowered her eyes, unable to conceal her disappointment and dismay.

"—Not once have you said you wish to save Emilia."

"—Huh?"

"Your words dress themselves up on the surface as wanting to save someone, wanting to protect others, while underneath, black emotions seethe and boil. At the very least, it does not match the figure I saw in the throne room."

Unable to swallow the meaning of what had been said, Subaru's gaze wandered.

—He didn't think he wanted to save Emilia?

That couldn't be. Subaru had always, ever since falling into this world, ever since she first saved his life, gone on living for Emilia's sake. The throne room, the incident at the training grounds, even now—all of it was the same. If he left this situation as it was, she and the village would be lost. That was surely why he was acting. Never, never, never had his heart been stolen away by hatred—

"You are not permitted to go any further than that."

Abruptly, that voice broke the silence and was hurled at Subaru.

The moment his consciousness was jolted back to reality, standing before Subaru with his posture straightened was an old man—Wilhelm. He stood beside Crusch across the table, gazing at Subaru with a faint pity seeping through the wrinkles that spoke of his years. That condescending gaze, that bearing, that pitying expression—it grated strangely on his nerves.

"—Subaru-kun."

Amid his raw, frayed emotions bristling with irritation, he felt a tug at his sleeve and turned to find Rem, still seated, pinching the edge of his clothing between her fingers. On her finely composed face was a look of urgency, and no small measure of sorrow.

"Please calm down. Making a scene here won't solve anything. And even if you tried, Rem wouldn't be a match for Wilhelm-sama."

"...Make a scene? What are you talking about? Making a scene, I wouldn't..."

"Now, now, isn't that just a wee bit much, nya?"

—do such a thing, he'd been about to continue, when Ferris shrugged at him and let out a great sigh. He raised his eyes to Subaru and pointed at the arm Rem wasn't holding.

"Gripping a teaspoon in a clenched fist like that, what were you planning to do with it, nya? Maybe your parents didn't raise you right, but that grip is just not okay, nya~."

Only at Ferris's pointing it out did Subaru notice. His right hand was clutching the spoon from the teacup that had been served. And gripping it, moreover, upside down by the handle, in a rough hold as though meaning to bring it down on something.

—When on earth had he done that?

"Just because someone hit the mark doesn't mean you get to throw a fit about it, nya. Go making a big scene here, and while Rem-chan holds you back, old man Wil will cut you clean in two, you know?"

"And I would rather not give such an order. We have spent several days together, and there would be political complications to deal with. More than anything, it would soil the room."

Despite being the one nearly subjected to an act of discourtesy, Crusch's manner as she looked up at him retained composure. It seemed to show the largeness of her vessel, and at the same time seemed to mock the pettiness of Subaru, who had nothing but this tiny piece of metal to pour his anger into. All of it grated on him terribly.

"So no matter what, you won't lend me your strength?"

So it was that, rather than an apology, defiant words came first. Ferris looked displeased at this, and Wilhelm closed one eye to glance at his mistress. But Crusch herself answered the question with an "Indeed," and said,

"Your statements carry little credibility, and even were we to cooperate, what we stand to gain is unappealing. —Therefore, I will be watching from the sidelines."

"The Witch Cult's coming. When that happens, it'll be you who kills the people of that village. Your 'Sloth,' knowing and doing nothing, will kill that village."

"That is a remarkably arrogant thing to say. Well then, allow me one thing."

Having spoken the epithet of that despicable man, Subaru glared at Crusch. Receiving that clouded gaze, Crusch rose to her feet and looked straight into Subaru's eyes. That penetrating stare gave Subaru the discomforting sense that it was peering from behind his eyes clear into the depths of his heart. And then,

"I am, more or less, able to discern whether the person before me is lying. I have long prided myself on never once being deceived by another."

Abruptly, Crusch began speaking of such a thing. Still peering into the depths of Subaru's eyes, which had shifted color in suspicion, she went on.

"Speaking from that experience, I will say this: you are not telling a 'lie.'"

"Then..."

"You do not believe it to be a lie yourself. You cling stubbornly to your own delusions as truth. —That is nothing less than madness. You are a madman, Natsuki Subaru."

Right there, Subaru understood clearly that these negotiations had broken down. He could not imagine accomplishing something so important alongside someone who would treat him as one and the same in nature as that madman, that hateful man he so despised. More than anything, his heart could no longer bear such humiliation.

Gritting his teeth, Subaru bit clean through the edge of his lip, and blood trickled down his chin. Seeing this pitiful sight, Crusch spoke.

"Ferris, do heal him."

"I refuse!"

Refusing far faster than Ferris could even move, Subaru turned on his heel and left his chair. His destination was the exit of the drawing room.

"Dinner is about to be served—will you not join us?"

"Sitting down to a meal with a madman? What a chilling thought. I don't know if you fancy yourself some man of refined taste or a kabuki actor, but that's overplaying the part a bit much."

Answering irony with irony, Subaru put his hand to the drawing room door. Glancing back with just his head, he saw Rem, having straightened herself, bow politely to the others as well.

"Though it was a short time, we've caused you a great deal of trouble. On behalf of my master, allow me to offer my thanks."

"So that is your answer... no, Lord Mathers' answer?"

"Yes. I've been instructed to respect Subaru-kun's wishes in all things."

It was an exchange whose meaning eluded him, and from where Subaru stood he could not see Crusch's face. But in the voice she directed at Rem in parting, there was no small measure of regret. It was a different emotion from the clear rupture she had directed at Subaru, and even that felt somehow irritating.

Watching Rem hurry over to join him, Subaru opened the door. It was a house he knew well enough, though it belonged to another. From here to the entrance hall, then out past the gate into the noble district of the royal capital—the route was perfectly clear to him.

"Do you have any prospects?"

"Try to make a decent king, why don't you. The dictator type."

Spitting out those last words thrown at his back, Subaru slammed the door shut behind him. Thus, the negotiations with Crusch came to a close in thoroughly wretched fashion.

It was past evening when, negotiations broken down, Subaru stepped out into the noble district. The sun had already sunk into the western distance, and night's curtain was slowly beginning to fall over the world. Amid the Crystal Lamps lighting the streets at even intervals, Subaru stood with his arms folded, leaning his back against the iron fence, waiting for Rem to come out.

"Damn it, every single one of them..."

Drumming his fingers against his folded arms, Subaru let his irritation out in a low growl. What came back to him was the exchange with Crusch just now, the repeated chewing-over of the humiliation of having been thoroughly outmaneuvered. Swirling in his chest was regret at having failed to secure her cooperation, and—

"A bunch of stubborn fools... why do they get in the way of what's right..."

—a feeling not far from an inexhaustible hatred toward the women who stood in the way of his path.

Subaru's intent to annihilate the Witch Cult surely wasn't wrong. Which meant it was their reaction, their refusal to cooperate, that was wrong. They didn't understand, because they hadn't seen that carnage, that heartlessness, that vile man's cackling laughter with their own eyes, hadn't heard it with their own ears, hadn't felt it in their own skin. That those were vermin not worth keeping alive.

"Enough, that's enough. Forget the ones who screwed up, the heartless ones. Right now, focus on what's in front of you..."

Better to value pressing forward, even if only a little, than to stand rooted in shame and regret. To begin with, requesting aid from Crusch had only ever been Subaru's second-best option. When it came to annihilating the Witch Cult, there were other candidates with far better odds. Namely—

"Sorry to keep you waiting, Subaru-kun."

Just then, as Subaru tapped his foot in irritation, Rem appeared, passing through the gate. In her arms as she approached were the various belongings they had used during their several days staying at Crusch's estate—since they couldn't simply be left behind, Subaru had been waiting for her to come out after retrieving them. Having made such a grand exit with such fine parting words, it would hardly do to go back in saying he'd forgotten something. In Subaru's stead, since he couldn't go back, he went to meet Rem, who held their belongings in both hands.

"Sorry about that. Give the bags here, I'll carry some too."

"It's fine. They're not heavy, and Subaru-kun, you're still recovering."

But Rem firmly refused his offer. Ordinarily Subaru would have pressed the matter further, but with his thoughts occupied elsewhere for the moment, it wasn't worth insisting on. He lowered the hand he'd immediately held out.

"Come to think of it, you didn't object to leaving Crusch's place."

"No. Not if that's what Subaru-kun chose."

"Well, it's a bit late for that anyway. After making that big a scene, there's no way I could stick around dragging out the treatment. Whatever the deal was with the debt, I did something rotten to Emilia-tan."

Emilia, who had offered up something in exchange to entrust Subaru's treatment to them—there was some guilt in repeatedly, and now abruptly, undoing that kindness of hers. That was something he would need to properly talk through with her once the Witch Cult business was settled and he'd made things right with her. It'd be fine. Surely she'd talk to him, once he'd pulled her out of this crisis. And for that, he needed Petelgeuse dead—no question about it.

"Subaru-kun. About the negotiations with Lady Crusch..."

"On and on about credibility this, benefit that—nothing but a bunch of high-minded nonsense. She's got no heart. Who the hell would follow someone like that?"

Cutting off Rem, who looked as though she had something to say, Subaru spat out the words and shut the topic down there. Whether out of consideration for Subaru's state of mind, Rem didn't try to pursue the subject any further. Instead—

"What will we do now? If what you told me is true, Subaru-kun, we don't have a moment to spare."

"If it's true?"

"—! We don't have a moment to spare. Lord Roswaal's mansion—will we be heading back?"

Subaru cut in at the snag in her words, but Rem, not engaging with it, pressed her question, and Subaru put a hand to his chin and shook his head, saying, "No."

"If it's just the two of us going back now, we won't be able to do much. We need to bring back proper forces to counter them. Failing that, we need some kind of alternative measure."

If only Subaru and Rem went back, it would just mean retracing the same course as before. Or, if they departed earlier than last time, there was a chance they might reach the mansion without encountering the cultists at all—but,

"Either way, we're still short on the numbers to hold them off with the mansion's forces alone."

The ones who could actually fight were, in practice, only Ram and Rem. Emilia, in cooperation with Puck, might perhaps be counted among those able to fight as well, but beyond that there were only the villagers and Subaru, an amateur. The Witch Cult followers under Petelgeuse's command were all armed, and their fighting strength, while perhaps falling short of Rem's, was on a level Subaru stood no chance against. The odds that the villagers and Subaru could meaningfully be counted into the balance of forces were vanishingly slim.

"We're short on numbers, on strength. What the hell is Roswaal even doing..."

If he were there alone, used well, there was a real chance he alone could wipe out the Witch Cult's forces. And yet, in both previous loops, his presence had gone unconfirmed. The same went for Emilia—where in that mansion had the two of them even been...

"Subaru-kun, about Lord Roswaal... if his schedule after departing the royal capital held to plan, there's a very high chance he isn't at the mansion right now."

"—! You knew? That Roswaal not being at the mansion was according to plan?"

"Lord Roswaal was to visit one of Garfiel's... um, one of the influential figures within his domain, and might be staying there for several days."

"Damn it, the worst possible timing! Is that why they couldn't deal with the attack!?"

At Rem's answer, exactly as he'd feared, Subaru clawed at his head and spat out a curse. So that pervert of a lord really was absent from the mansion at precisely the critical moment. If that turned out to be the fatal cause behind the domain being trampled underfoot, then—

"No excuse Crusch could make would change the fact there's something seriously wrong with this lord's competence..."

As his image of Roswaal—pervert, but capable—was rewritten into something far more hopeless, pervert and incompetent, Subaru wracked his brain desperately. In other words, with Roswaal, their greatest fighting asset, now off the table, the situation remained the same overwhelming shortfall in strength as before. That held true even if Subaru and Rem managed to make it back safely.

"We really don't have any option but to bring reinforcements back with us."

Raising his head to declare this, Subaru nodded toward Rem, who watched him with bated breath. The course was set. And the two of them had no time to spare. If they meant to surpass the outcome of the previous two loops, they needed to leave the royal capital by tomorrow at the very latest. Given that it was now just turning into evening, that left roughly half a day.

"Anyway, all we can do is go find whoever else might help us. Rem, do you know the royal capital well?"

"I've been here a few times, and I've been going around with you these past few days, so well enough. ...But who?"

"Isn't it obvious? —The most promising candidate there is. The handsome face of justice himself."

To call Reinhard van Astrea the ideal person for the job, from Subaru's point of view, would be no exaggeration.

A martial prowess that could only be described as god-given, a beauty of form bestowed as though blessed by a goddess. A gentle temperament that treated everyone without discrimination, and above all, an integrity beyond question. He was the very symbol of absolute justice, the sort of man who would never overlook even the smallest evil—and thinking of his character, Subaru was certain: his cooperation was all but guaranteed.

The vileness of those Witch Cult followers, of Petelgeuse, was an affront to human dignity itself. It was something that could never be forgiven—and Reinhard, surely, would never forgive what Subaru himself could not forgive either.

There was also the calculation that, unlike Crusch or Ferris, Reinhard would not bring up some clever talk of mutual benefit and the like. Before someone so sincere, someone in whose chest righteousness itself seemed to shine, it would be absurd to try haggling by dangling vested interests. If this cause was just, he would never allow such an oversight, one that would sacrifice the kingdom's people, to stand. And so, for Subaru, the breakdown of negotiations with Crusch wasn't even much of a blow.

Reinhard would surely, without fail, become Subaru's ally. With that tremendous swordsmanship of his, he would surely, without fail, strike Petelgeuse's head clean off.

Feeling his lips twist with anticipation, and with a sadistic satisfaction beyond even that, Subaru made his way toward him at an unhurried pace—

only to arrive at the deserted annex and be informed that his hero had "departed the royal capital two days prior."

Night is coming. Roughly twelve hours remain until the time limit to leave the royal capital. Roughly four days remain until the mansion is attacked and that carnage begins anew.

—Over this attempt to annihilate the Witch Cult, dark clouds had begun to gather.
