Arc 6 Chapter 16: "Remnants of Mastication"

——Something was breaking, and consciousness alone kept listening to the sound of it, on and on.

Bone ground to splinters, brain matter crushed flat, eyeballs cracking and spilling the fluid within. The skull—it was the bones of the head being crushed. He could feel the precious things packed inside giving way, root and all, to the force of sharp fangs, turning to mush.

Gray brain and cerebral fluid spilled out in thick clumps, mixing with the nameless yellow fluid that had pooled at the front of the skull, while the fine mesh of blood vessels tore and bright red blood came pouring out. All of it together, one indistinguishable mass, and consciousness and memory transformed into vomit the color of flesh.

Pain—there was pain. And a suffocating tightness, too. Like a headache splitting his skull apart—so he thought, and consciousness gave a bitter laugh at itself.

It wasn't a matter of pain like something splitting apart. It had already gone past splitting—everything precious had already been smashed to pieces. On top of that, the part that felt pain had long since been crushed too, so what was the use of saying anything now?

The brain that stored memory had drained away, the organ meant for thought was crushed, and the parts fundamentally necessary to sustain life had been stripped away entirely—did he know what happened once that occurred?

Once it reached that point, a human being met 'death.' So, naturally, he too—

"——rus. Barusu. Pull yourself together."

Something seized the scruff of his hazy consciousness and hauled it forcibly out into a bright place. The first thing he felt upon returning was someone's urgent voice close to his ear. The voice carried not just a tremor of sound, but also—faintly—the sensation of a slap against his cheek.

"Barusu, if you don't wake up already, I'll burn your eyelids off."

"——!"

Hearing such a terrifying thing said to his half-awake mind, consciousness surged rapidly upward. Called by the voice, he passed out of the world of darkness, consciousness breaking free of that suffocating environment as if breaking the surface of water. And just before his eyelids could be burned off, he opened them, and—

"——Barusu, are you awake?"

"————"

Right before his eyes was Ram's face, her pale pink eyes narrowed. Her breath reached him; if he lifted his chin even slightly, their lips would almost touch, that close. Not, of course, that there was anything romantic behind it. The only reason Ram had brought her face this near was that the surroundings were so dark neither of them could make out the other's features otherwise.

As he became aware of Ram in front of him, his perception belatedly began picking up other things—the sensation beneath him, for instance. His body was sprawled on fine grains of sand; Subaru lay spread-eagled in the sand sea. Subaru let out a breath, and Ram, after peering for a moment into the depths of his eyes as if checking something, slowly drew back with the lantern in one hand. The orange light sliced through the darkness in her grip, and Subaru understood that he was still—no, that he had once again been caught in the sand labyrinth. That he had 'Returned by Death.'

"I, was..."

Looking around at the darkness, Subaru took his time recalling what had happened. The shock of the moment of 'death' was always vivid, and the sensation of memory being shaken never grew any less jarring, no matter how many times he experienced it. Tossed about in the earthquake within his memory as he strove to recall the certainty of 'death,' Subaru caught hold of the very end with his fingertips, and remembered.

"————"

What surged back into his mind with terrible force was Ram's face, lit by the lantern, twisted in hatred. And then, sparked by their exchange of curses, a killing spree devoid of any meaning or purpose whatsoever, and its gruesome conclusion.

"Ugh...!"

"Barusu?"

Before Ram's puzzled face, Subaru unconsciously clapped a hand to his mouth. It was not, in fact, a cry of horror at recalling that mere seconds ago he and Ram had tried to kill each other—but a wave of nausea from recalling the horrific end Ram had met.

"Uh, gh... gag—"

For Subaru, his own 'death' was already something he'd experienced more times than he had fingers on both hands. And in this world where he 'Returned by Death,' the fact was he had tasted the loss of people close to him—Emilia, Beatrice—almost as many times as his own death.

But for Subaru, 'death' was never something one grew used to. That was true of his own death, and equally true of anyone else's. There was fear in the thought of dying himself, and the thought of someone close to him dying tore his heart apart just to imagine.

——And what's more, the horrific way Ram had died last time was a shock unlike anything he'd tasted before.

He had watched a stomach ripped open, a neck severed, a whole body mercilessly wounded, watched people slaughtered without mercy. Every one of those deaths had been steeped in agony and grief, and Subaru could never bring himself to be so tasteless as to line up deaths and compare them. But having her head crushed like that, its contents chewed, her body mangled—that was a first.

He had watched Ram's finely made face caught mercilessly between fangs and crushed. And it had been done by the hands of one of the beings Subaru trusted most in the world—Patrasche.

"...Ah, gah. Gehh, hahh—"

Trying desperately not to remember was no different from remembering. The harder Subaru tried to forget Ram's horrible end, the more vividly the pink hair and the ruined remains of her head, left in the earth dragon's mouth, rose up in his mind.

As a result, unable to digest the nausea welling up within him, Subaru retched toward the sand. But his throat and stomach hadn't fully recovered from the shock of 'Returning by Death,' and did nothing but spasm, refusing to obey his will.

"Geh, ehh...!"

"...I thought you'd finally woken up, and this is what you do? Pathetic."

He put his hands to the ground, desperately trying to force up the contents of his stomach, but nothing came. His mouth was bone dry—not even a drop of saliva came out, which was almost impressive in itself. He couldn't make out Ram's expression as she watched him, lantern in hand, but her tone made it more than clear enough that she was exasperated at him gasping so desperately.

Naturally, that cold attitude of hers brought back the events just before his 'death.' That inexplicable surge of murderous rage, and the exchange of insults that had sparked it—and the impulse that had driven it all screamed inside his chest, and it frightened him. But then—

"——Don't you dare bite."

"——!"

With that single warning, Ram crouched down beside Subaru and lifted his chin. Subaru, kneeling, flinched in surprise at the sudden move, but Ram paid no mind to his reaction, forced his mouth open, and—her face utterly, thoroughly unimpressed—thrust her pale white fingers straight down his throat.

"...!? Guh, ehh—"

"I knew Barusu's clumsiness was bone-deep, but this is basically a baby."

His throat violated by another person's fingers, his esophagus roughly assaulted— And then the stomach and throat that had been refusing to cooperate with vomiting suddenly accepted this new intrusion, and, as if uniting in common cause, began working frantically to force up stomach acid and saliva. Eyes welling with tears, Subaru let the nausea take over and emptied it all onto the sand. It was only stomach acid and fluid he brought up, but even so, he felt far better than before.

"Ehh, gehh... haah, huh... sorry, I'm... fine now..."

"Really? Good for you, glad you're all satisfied, are you not."

"You...!"

Ram shrugged as Subaru wiped his mouth with his sleeve, answering him in baby talk. He had plenty to say about that attitude, but the fact remained he'd just made a mess at baby level. There was no room to argue, and even now, Ram's palm was gently rubbing his back. It was a hard-to-read, biting sort of kindness.

"Your hand—you can stop now. More importantly, where are we—"

"You remember the barrier breaking and the sand sea splitting open right in front of us? We got swallowed up along with the dragon carriage, and this is where we ended up after being thrown clear."

Shaking off Ram's consideration, Subaru looked around as he spoke. Hearing her answer, Subaru wasn't sure whether to feel relief or unease that his 'Return by Death' point had stayed the same. Either way, though, he had to act quickly. Following that judgment, he tried to keep speaking—

'When you woke up underground, did you worry about Rem, Barusu? About Emilia-sama? About Beatrice-sama? Did you properly worry about whoever it was you couldn't find?'

"——uh"

What resurfaced in his mind was the edge of Ram's words, cutting viciously into his heart. The discord that had led to last time's terrible end—that situation where allies had turned and killed each other—was clearly abnormal. No doubt it had been him, but he could hardly believe that the self he'd just recalled had been in any normal state of mind. To begin with, the trigger itself had been abnormal. That a mere accumulation of petty irritations could pile up from an exchange of insults into a killing spree was simply too absurd.

"Barusu?"

Ram tilted her head at Subaru's inability to speak. Last time was discord born of an abnormal situation. So naturally, there was no value in being troubled by words spoken in such a state. ——Only, it also felt unnatural to dismiss it all as a lie. Even if it had been amplified far beyond what was normally possible, the dissatisfaction and anger Subaru had felt toward Ram had not been fake. It wasn't as though everything had been a lie.

If so, then it was surely true that Subaru's attitude—not worrying about Emilia and the others—had stirred some hard-to-put-into-words displeasure in Ram. If he was to be considerate of that feeling, Subaru ought to at least go through the motions of worrying about them here. In truth, he genuinely was worried, so it wouldn't be a lie to act that way either. Only—he understood that worrying wouldn't gain him anything.

"————"

Only now, truly only now, did Subaru realize the deceit in that, and the words caught in his throat. He knew it was necessary to avoid discord, and still his heart hesitated to voice that deceit and mislead Ram. Worrying about his companions out of calculation, deceiving his companions—it disgusted him. Of course, it was a hesitation he had to swallow down. To invite ill will over something like that would be utterly pointless.

"That... everyone... um..."

"——There's no point in blaming yourself that much. There's no meaning in pursuing whose fault it was. There ought to be something more worth prioritizing than wasting time on that."

But before Subaru could get the words of concern out, Ram had already drawn her own convenient conclusion about his feelings. Or rather, she'd misread the grave look on his face. It was a rare thing, given Ram's usual insight. The fact that she—someone who at times spoke as though she could see straight through a person's heart—had made this mistake was, in its own way, proof of just how anxious she was.

"We've already wasted enough time with Barusu puking his guts out."

Of course, Ram would never let herself show such disorder of heart. Faced with Ram's stance—not quite stubbornness, not quite bravado—Subaru couldn't decide what to say. In the end, he said nothing, and it was Ram who moved first. She wiped off the finger she'd shoved into Subaru's mouth, then turned the lantern toward the depths of the labyrinth.

"We can't afford to dawdle. We need to link up with Rem, and... with Emilia-sama and the others."

"I—I know... um, who's here?"

"Aside from Ram and Barusu... it looks like someone's just returned, right on cue."

Trying to smooth things over, Subaru confirmed with Ram's back, following her words and her gaze to look deeper into the passage. Coming from the far end of the labyrinth, their light swaying gently, were Anastasia and Patrasche. Seeing the pitch-black earth dragon melting into the darkness from a distance, Subaru's eyes trembled faintly.

That dignified, fierce, and beautiful face, flickering in and out of the orange light. The row of fangs like blades lined up inside that closed mouth, crushing the girl's head, and then, one after another, even Subaru himself—

Something like that couldn't possibly happen normally.

"Am I an idiot... no, I am an idiot."

Clenching his jaw against its faint trembling, Subaru tried to force down his own weakness. In the midst of that abnormal discord, the killing intent turned on him couldn't possibly have been real. Being killed by Patrasche had carved itself into his heart as a wound that wouldn't heal, even for Subaru. And still, Subaru was supposed to have repeatedly faced down the very beings who had killed him.

"That's right. Rem, and Ram too, at first, they were..."

Even Ram, who just now had let a bit of warmth show through. Even Rem, who had supported him so devotedly and forgiven him so much. Their relationships had started at their worst; there had been times his life was targeted, times it had actually been taken.

Compared to that, this last unintended incident was nothing much at all.

"That's right. That's right, isn't it. ...It should be. It has to be."

Wrapping his arms around himself, Subaru rubbed at his own skin as if bearing up against the cold. Underground in the sand sea, this place ruled by sand and darkness, was indeed cold. But the reason he felt this chill had nothing to do with the outside air.

"——Natsuki-kun, looks like you're awake."

"——Thanks to you. So, how did the scouting go?"

Ahead of him, Ram and Anastasia were exchanging words. They were probably comparing notes on the strategy for clearing this sand labyrinth against the information they'd just gathered. Normally, Subaru would join in—there was much he needed to report. And yet, right now, his knees wouldn't move until he'd settled his heart first.

He was supposed to have left behind the events and effects of the moment before 'death' when he 'Returned by Death.' And yet, however much he tried to banish it, the fear kept surfacing, and the fierce emotion of that moment kept reviving.

Subaru went on, endlessly, struggling to push that feeling back down.

"————"

The jet-black earth dragon simply watched over Subaru in his struggle, with what looked like sorrow.

——Subaru's heart, though it had supposedly 'Returned by Death,' had not fully returned to normal.

Setting aside that one circumstance, the four of them explored the underground labyrinth in much the same way as before. Searching for the sight of their companions, the group, with Subaru at the lead, advanced slowly through the cold darkness. Lighting the passage with a lantern, straining their eyes for any shadow lying fallen at the roadside, their pace was sluggish, maddeningly so.

"————"

Honestly, Subaru wanted to skip this whole process of confirming the way ahead. For Subaru, this stretch was already a path he'd walked twice. There was no way Emilia and the others would be lying anywhere within sight. And yet there was no point appealing to that with the two behind him, and if they said there might be something he'd overlooked, he'd have no way to argue.

As a result, the march—already unsettled and frustrating enough—crawled along without picking up pace, and time ticked by, building up nothing but Subaru's own impatience.

"I hope everyone else is safe too... in our case, having Patrasche-chan with us made us just barely lucky, I guess."

"————"

"If you think about what it's like without legs, the sand sea's a nightmare. What worries me is, well, we're just plain short on firepower, aren't we..."

"————"

"Worst case, I won't hesitate to play my trump card either, but I gotta be careful not to overdo it, since there's no backup after that, y'know? So I'm counting on you two, okay?"

"————"

"...Hey, are Natsuki-kun and Ram-san even listening? Here I've been talking all by myself this whole time."

Anastasia, who had been talking the entire way through the search, sounded like she was starting to pout. Objecting to the lack of any response, Subaru stopped and turned back at her complaint. He turned the light on Anastasia, up on the dragon, let out an exasperated breath through his nose, and said,

"Man, you really never run out of things to say, do you. ...Now's supposed to be when we're desperately looking for everyone else. No time for idle chatter."

"That's not idle chatter at all, thank you very much. I'm properly talking about things relevant to our plans going forward. Besides, keeping the chatter going is usually your job, Natsuki-kun. If you're quiet of all times right now, two girls—no, four—are gonna get anxious."

"Four, huh."

Anastasia countered Subaru's twisted mouth with that comeback, her mouth never running dry. Counted as two, that would be Ram and Anastasia, but "four" must mean she was adding Eridna and Patrasche. Strictly speaking, since Anastasia and Eridna were currently one and the same, it should really be "three"—but that wasn't really something worth correcting anyway.

"————"

At Anastasia puffing out her cheeks, Subaru narrowed his dark eyes, lost in thought. The real Anastasia was dormant right now, and it was just Eridna putting on the act. Even so, that act had settled in enough by now that the two seemed almost fused together. Regardless of who was holding the reins, the fact that he couldn't see through her expressions or words to what lay beneath remained, unfortunately, unchanged.

——Last time, in the midst of the discord that spiraled into that killing spree, the sheer horror of how it ended had kept his attention fixed almost entirely on Ram and Patrasche, but there had been something off about Anastasia's demeanor too.

Ram's hostility and Patrasche's malice—the two of them clearly going wrong—had been easy enough to read. But Anastasia's had been hard to judge at a glance as madness, and to the very last moment, he'd never been able to tell what color her heart had turned.

Of course, given the difference in raw strength, Anastasia had ended up killed by Ram, but hiding a knife from the emergency bag, pretending to mediate while watching for an opening—her cunning was dangerous no matter how you looked at it. If he didn't stay conscious of that at all times, he could have his throat slit while he slept and think nothing of—

"...Why do I keep..."

——thinking only of hostility, of enmity, over and over?

"——!"

Facing forward down the passage, Subaru struck his own cheek where Ram and the others couldn't see. The blow, thrown without any restraint, split the inside of his mouth, and he felt a sharp pain and the taste of blood on his tongue. By hurting himself like that, he tried to correct his clouded mind.

Ram, Anastasia—they weren't the ones who were abnormal. The one who, even now, hadn't been able to let go of that abnormality was none other than Subaru himself.

"Amazing. Realizing for yourself that you're the crazy one—that's not chilling at all, nope."

Was it him who was mad, or the other person? There had been a time Subaru had arrogantly believed everything but himself was wrong. Ultimately, of course, that had all been mistaken. Subaru, lost and floundering, had simply been arrogant enough to decide he alone was right and go around denying everyone else—a foolish experience, nothing more. In the end, he'd been shown, pointed out, something he should have known from the very start—that he was the one in the wrong—and he'd managed to accept it. So that memory, at least, was a correct one, a vivid one.

But in his current state, Subaru was recognizing his own abnormality from within a state of normalcy. And on top of that, he hadn't managed to tame that abnormality—which made it purely, thoroughly terrifying.

Subaru had felt this before, had some sense of familiarity with this state. This bleak, barren feeling in his heart, and this endlessly solitary, ravenous descent into madness.

This was the very ground the madman, Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti, had once stood upon. The landscape that madman had seen—being aware of his own madness, perceiving his abnormality as normalcy.

"Barusu, is something wrong?"

Tasting an unpleasant sense of kinship with that, Subaru felt Ram's voice reach his trembling back. Subaru swallowed down the blood mixed with his saliva, then turned back to the two of them, acting as if nothing were wrong.

"Nah, it's nothing. Just got sand in my shoes, feels gross."

"...It's not as though it would affect Ram's heart one bit even if Barusu alone went silent, but Anastasia-sama does have a point."

"Meaning?"

"I don't just mean going quiet—but it's not right to bottle something up and make a girl anxious. If you don't want to do that, learn to hide it better."

"————"

Struck by that flat statement, Subaru twisted his mouth into a scowl again. The meaning of Ram's words was plain, impossible to misread. And with that, he realized Ram's remark had essentially been a translation of Anastasia's hard-to-read consideration. It felt as though he was being seen right through, and it set his heart on edge.

"It's vague, this feeling. I don't think talking about it would even make sense."

"It's a bit late to say I can't make sense of anything you say, Barusu. If that's what worries you, you ought to be more considerate on a daily basis."

"Ram-san really doesn't hold back when it comes to Natsuki-kun, huh."

At Subaru clicking his tongue, Ram fired back her usual sharp tongue in return. Its venom was so thick and so pointed that even Anastasia gave a wry laugh. To Anastasia's mild attempt at calming things down, Ram merely shrugged and said,

"Don't worry. Barusu is delighted. He's a pervert who delights in hardship."

"What are you even talking about? Hey, what exactly did you see to make that call? Don't just turn me into your punching bag however you please, okay? You know the rule—only people who're ready to get hit back should throw punches?"

"Hah."

Ram's snort of mockery was very nearly her usual self. Unlike Subaru's own state of mind, she had already regained her composure, and he found himself both envious and, at the same time, resentful and irritated by that—and disgusted with himself for even thinking it.

Still, even so, trading their usual banter thinned out the shadow of unease. That, without a doubt, was the very light Subaru had built up through his everyday life with them.

"To try to explain the hard-to-explain... I'm just being a little too paranoid right now."

"————"

Putting it into words like that made it sound even flimsier than he'd imagined. Given that, he could hardly blame Ram and Anastasia for the twist of their mouths.

"————"

Glancing over, even Patrasche seemed to look faintly exasperated with him. For Patrasche, clever and well aware of how things ought to work, Subaru's current pathetic state must be hard to even watch. She'd honestly be better off giving up on him and switching to some more suitable master instead. In fact, it seemed like Ram or Anastasia would probably handle Patrasche far better. If she thought he was in the way for that reason, it would only be natural for her to bare her fangs at him too—

"Stop, stop it..."

"For someone whose way of hearing things sounds so dumb, I get that it's serious."

Anastasia offered that assessment as Subaru, locked in a staring match with Patrasche, grew steadily more grim. Being told objectively that something was off brought both relief and unease at the same time. In short, it meant the option of removing Subaru had now taken shape in their minds. Before it came to that, if anything, he ought to—

"I'm being, like, super annoying."

"You know, I wonder if it'd honestly be easier to just tie him up and carry him at this point."

Turning red, then pale, running through an entire one-man theater of expressions, Anastasia finally threw up her hands. At Anastasia's verdict, Ram furrowed her brow with a thoughtful "but—"

"Even if you tore off Barusu's limbs like that, it wouldn't be a fundamental solution."

"Don't use such strong language. You'll make an enemy out of me."

"You're not much of a threat, but Ram would rather not make an enemy of Emilia-sama or Beatrice-sama either. ...And I wouldn't be able to face Rem afterward."

While Ram fell into thought, Subaru struggled not to let hostility take root in him. Even as he fought his own emotions like that, there was, in fact, something he found strange.

It was hardly the moment to be thinking about it, truly not the time. And yet, having set out on this journey to conquer the Pleiades Watchtower and now having gone through two 'Returns by Death' in this sand labyrinth, the question had surfaced.

——What bothered him was how unusually sensitive Ram seemed whenever it came to Rem.

"————"

Of course, for Subaru, it was a good sign that Ram cared about Rem. For Rem, who existed in no one's memory but Subaru's, he was the only one bound to her in a two-way connection. That gave Subaru a certain sense of being special, in a way—but in any practical sense, it accomplished nothing.

And yet Ram was trying, with boundless earnestness, to draw close to Rem's isolated circumstances. Of course, the fact that Rem's memory didn't remain within her was something he'd been made painfully aware of over this past year. But at the same time, it was equally certain that Ram took the existence of this girl, Rem, who was the very image of herself, more seriously than she would a mere 'truth told to her by Subaru.'

And somehow, that feeling seemed to bring Ram a reality that exceeded even what Subaru had 'imagined might exceed his imagination.' That much could be gleaned from the earlier discord in the labyrinth—the outburst sparked by Subaru's own thoughtless words and behavior.

It wasn't that she remembered. And yet Ram's feeling was equal to remembering. Her affection ran deep—probably deeper than Subaru gave her credit for. ——But was that really all there was to it?

"...Lewd."

"No, it's not lewd. That's not the look I'm giving. That's just more paranoia!"

"Saying that while conveniently ignoring yourself? This is hopeless."

"If I'm the one up on the shelf, then you've climbed right up onto the household altar and you're looking down on me from there."

And so, trading banter like this, he could reassure himself that nothing had changed in their relationship. Even if his mood could flip in an instant, warm feeling turning to cold malice. As long as that lasted, he could keep himself from hating anyone.

"I'll keep an eye on Natsuki-kun's behavior, but... the cause is probably that, huh."

"You noticed too, Anastasia-sama?"

"Well, when Natsuki-kun's this obviously affected, it kinda makes sense."

At Subaru's condition—plagued by paranoid suspicion—Ram and Anastasia exchanged a look, as though they had some idea of the cause. Or rather, Subaru himself already knew the cause well enough. Given the two of them's insight and wariness, all the more so. Even while chatting like that, it was infuriatingly clear they were paying careful attention to his every move.

"Infuriating, huh... what an idiot."

"Did you say something?"

"Just about my own stupidity. So, what's this 'idea' of yours?"

While Subaru puzzled over his own sensibilities, Ram and Anastasia tilted their heads right back at him. Then, in answer to his question, the two of them pointed straight ahead in unison. Following that gesture to look forward, he understood, indeed, what they meant.

——Right ahead of them, that fork in the road had appeared once again.

"I've been feeling this nasty wind blowing for a while now... but now I know the cause."

"This foul wind is what's calling the discord. According to Meili, Barusu's body odor draws Witchbeasts, and to begin with, the compatibility is bad... or, well, maybe I should say good."

"So you know what this awful feeling is?"

"Cross-reference Barusu's current state with Barusu's body odor, and there's only one answer."

"Would you stop saying 'body odor' over and over."

The previous two times, the unanimous verdict had simply been a 'nasty feeling.' Now, with Subaru's constitution and his current state tangled into the mix, a different conclusion emerged.

Ram and Anastasia exchanged a glance, and at the very same moment, nodding to each other, said—

"Miasma."

"That Witch thing. In that case, what's up ahead is..."

"I can't be certain, but it's common knowledge that there's originally a 'Witch's Shrine' deep within the dunes. Given that the sand sea's split open like this, if it's somehow connected by some mistake..."

"That'd make for one heck of a shortcut, wouldn't it. Anyway, that said—"

They concluded that the cause of the discord that had stirred up their minds and driven them to bloodshed was the miasma. Having voiced the possibility of some dreadful presence lying ahead, Anastasia gave a wry smile and pointed down the path.

At the fork splitting in two directions, miasma drifted from the right, while a centaur's den lay to the left. Of course, Subaru fully intended to properly explain the threat lurking down the left path sometime after this—but the priority right now was which way to go.

"So, which way do you think is the right call?"

At Anastasia's question, Subaru and Ram exchanged a glance. And then, without so much as a fraction of a second's difference, their voices rang out together, saying—

"——Left."

And so it was decided.
