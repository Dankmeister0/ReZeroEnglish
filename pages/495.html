Arc 6 Chapter 88: "We Ask of Your Will"

——The light that erupted below the clouds and above the clouds occurred at almost exactly the same moment.

Thick cloud, and a divide greater still, split the battlefield into two — if anything existed that could observe both simultaneously, it could only be some observer from a world outside. The battle that had begun with the assault on the Pleiades Watchtower was, at long last, approaching its climax.

That situation was——

"Guh, oooooohh——!!"

The sand where the blast landed was blown skyward, and amid the raging sandstorm and shockwaves, Subaru screamed. The destructive force assailing his whole body was more than enough to pulverize him in an instant. And yet he wasn't reduced to dust — because——

"——E, M, M!!"

One of the three original spells devised by Subaru and Beatrice together. Put simply, it was an absolute-defense spell that stopped the flow of time within Subaru and Beatrice's bodies, rendering them immune to outside influence.

"Right after we came up with it I was so damn hyped, but ever since the worst bastard alive turned out to use a similar power, using this makes me feel like I've never been less picky about how I stay alive!"

"We've already talked plenty about that, I suppose! Betty decided ages ago she'd just close her eyes to it, I suppose!"

Hearing Subaru's lament, Beatrice, riding on his shoulders, bellowed back at him. The Sin Archbishop of the Witch of Greed, Regulus Corneas's "Invincibility," was a power so strong it could be called an upgraded version of this very E.M.M. Worst possible wielder, but unmatched practicality — the level Subaru should aim for, yet the last level he ever wanted to reach. Honestly.

"Even a guy who makes my stomach turn just remembering him is worth using as a reference……!"

Living by devouring experience — put that way, it might sound better than it was. But that, too, sounded uncomfortably close to the thinking of the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony, and Subaru was thoroughly sick of it — look left, look right, and there was always some bastard getting in his way. But without it, he couldn't protect himself, Beatrice, or Meili in the first place.

"——Ah."

In Subaru's arms, Meili, eyes shut, let out a delirious breath. Her expression was pained, but as long as it was pained, that was proof she was still breathing. The damage flowing into him from her seemed to be gradually easing, thanks to Beatrice's healing magic. Probably within the margin of error, but it had to be improving. Surely. Probably.

"Using healing magic and E.M.M both at once is hard, I suppose! If it weren't Betty, all three of you would already, already be pushing up daisies, I suppose!"

"I know! It's just……"

Grateful for Beatrice's scolding, Subaru questioned himself inwardly. As for whether there was damage — at this point there was nothing to do but grit his molars and bear it. The problem was how much mana Subaru had left — to begin with, despite being useful only as a mana tank, Subaru's tank capacity was about average at best, maybe a notch below. And by using E.M.M., that mana was being consumed at a rate like water pouring out of a bucket with a hole in it — literally. At this rate, before long, either E.M.M. or the healing magic would run dry. And——

"Stopping Meili's healing magic isn't an option……!"

"Then there's no choice but to cut E.M.M. The timing, and what comes after, will be up to you, I suppose!"

"Right, leave it to me. Bea-ko, you handle Meili's treatment, and be ready as my convenient spirit who reacts to my orders the instant I give them!"

"That way of putting it is — awful! I suppose!"

Bickering, exchanging a glance, Subaru and Beatrice released E.M.M. with not a second's discord in their coordination — and bolted from the spot as if launched.

Behind them, the crimson scorpion and the pack of witchbeasts, fighting even amongst themselves, still had eyes for Subaru and his group. As always, saddled with the handicap of being unable to leave the watchtower, the troublesome part was that the crimson scorpion's focus was aggressively fixed on Subaru. If, worst case, that didn't change——

"——The 'Witch.'"

Revealing his own Return by Death generated miasma, drawing the surrounding witchbeasts toward himself. Unimaginative, but it was one of the methods Subaru relied on often. Already more than dangerous enough as things stood, but if the crimson scorpion's obsession lay with him, there was value — maybe — in using it to set the other witchbeasts against each other. But the stakes for gambling on that were too large.

If Beatrice fell, if Meili fell, if Subaru himself fell, then Ram too — who was shouldering no small burden by lending them her strength — would collapse along with them, and in the end the whole thing would come crashing down like potatoes on a vine. Thanks to the power of "Col Leonis," the sense that they all sank or swam together had grown far too strong, as a result. How Regulus, wielding this very Authority, had managed to become so utterly alone — Subaru truly couldn't fathom the way that man lived.

"————"

"——! E, M, T!!"

At the end of a chain of useless thoughts, Subaru sensed a future where white light vaporized his head, and with his throat gone dry at the premonition of "death," he cut loose the second of his original spells. "E.M.T." eliminated the weakness of E.M.M — being unable to move — and succeeded in canceling out any magic head-on: an anti-magic spell. In principle, if a thing was released bearing mana, there was nothing this spell couldn't negate. However——

"Cut E.M.M. and drew the trump card five seconds later, damn it!!"

It wasn't just a case of all bark and no bite — worse, he'd lost any means of following up, leaving him in a genuine, total dead end. Still bearing down on Subaru and the others, the crimson scorpion's tail stinger, the flames of the starving horse king, and the attacks of the other assorted witchbeasts converged on them, aiming to reduce them to cinders. Holding his breath, Subaru braced his body. Without using the third and final original spell, there was no breaking this deadlock. But that one was unfinished. If it failed, there was a chance the three of them would end up adrift in imaginary space——

"I don't trust myself enough to bet on some last-minute awakening——!"

He could admit Natsuki Subaru was a hell of a guy, but that didn't mean he blindly believed himself to be some super-guy who could break through any situation he was thrown into. He just didn't know how to give up. Knocked down, he got back up more times than most people — which, by the same token, meant he'd been knocked down more times than most people, too.

"This isn't the time to be showing off my losing streak. It's a bad bet, but……"

"——You mean to muscle through on stubbornness and pride alone? How very like you — that decision."

Just as Subaru was about to resolve to try it anyway. A sudden voice fell from above, and a silhouette cut in between the incoming "death" attacks and Subaru's group. It was so dazzling that Subaru instinctively shut his eyes. No exaggeration — it really was dazzling. Because that silhouette shone in rainbow colors.

"——Al Clausellia."

An instant later, against the raging onslaught, a light was released to beat back the light. Destructive shockwaves, all-consuming flame, a suicidal charge willing to trade life for the kill — all of it. A black light dimmed them, overflowing water swallowed them, and a rising sandstorm deflected their force. Truly, a feat like commanding nature itself — and the one who had wrought it landed on the sand and swung a slender, long knight's sword with an elegant back turned to them——

"——I had my reasons for rushing over. It looks like I made it just in time."

Saying so, glancing back at Subaru, was none other than the "Most Superior Knight," Julius Juukulius. Even with his white attire stained here and there with blood and grime, he stood there with unhurried composure. Just like the rainbow radiance he wore, his bearing was more elegant and graceful than ever before. At his magnificent arrival, Subaru's voice trembled as he said, "Julius……"

"You……when you finished, I told you to go help somewhere else that was dangerous, didn't I!"

"Ah, I heard you. That's why I came here. My apologies, but comparing it against the other fronts, I judged this to be the most dangerous."

"Shut up! What happened to your face! Where's Reid!?"

"A complete defeat. He made a very satisfying escape after winning."

"Lame as hell! If you're gonna lose, at least come back having won! I'd have died if you hadn't shown up — I'm only saying this once, so thanks!"

Hearing the gratitude mixed in with the insults, Julius, sporting a fresh white scar under his left eye, gave a soft "heh" of a laugh. A pretentious sort of attitude, but it seemed that he too, having finished his battle with Reid, had come away with something. As proof——

"You made up with the quasi-spirits?"

"To be precise, they bloomed from bud into spirits proper. And 'made up' isn't quite the right expression, either — we were never on bad terms to begin with."

Surrounding Julius as he answered were six quasi-spirits — no, spirits now — their light faint but growing brighter. Robbed of their "names" by the Authority of Gluttony, and having lost that bond as well, the spirits had followed Julius without being able to lend him their strength, their confusion lingering for a long time. But the rift between the two sides had closed, and a bond deeper than before had taken root. Smoothly re-forming contracts with six spirits at once — what a shameless charmer.

"I'm barely managing to court Bea-ko, and here you are, an insatiable bastard."

"Unfortunately, I was the one who ended up devoured, in fact."

"That's not funny! You've gotten way too unhinged about this!!"

Subaru's eyes went wide at Julius, who could turn even having been a victim of an Authority into a joke. Having called it a "satisfying escape after winning," there must have been some kind of resolution with Reid. Since Reid's body should belong to Roy Alphard, it was only natural to think that this meant a resolution with one half of "Gluttony" as well. And the fact that Julius made no mention of the danger meant it was safe to assume that neutralizing Alphard had succeeded.

"Julius! Perfect timing, I suppose! Lend us Kua, I suppose!"

"——Understood."

Above Subaru's head, Julius nodded instantly to Beatrice's call. He too had sensed that Meili's condition, cradled in Subaru's arms, was a matter of urgency. From among the six spirits, the blue water spirit Kua darted out, and its gentle power, together with Beatrice's healing magic, poured healing mana into Meili's body. On top of which——

"You need me to focus purely on buying time, then."

"Yeah, like you can see. She's gone red and Shaula's pissed as hell, but can you do it? Weren't you just beaten by a red guy?"

"All the more reason for a rematch — though to phrase it that way in front of a lady would be quite rude."

Judging it best to face her head-on, Julius readied his knight's sword and squared off against the crimson scorpion — Shaula. Yet at the sight of the knight who'd cut in, no will could be sensed from the crimson scorpion's compound eyes. Its killing intent remained fixed on Subaru without wavering; anything in between was merely an obstacle. Even Julius, having elevated himself a step further as a Spirit Knight — that was the reception he got.

"Subaru, I'll take on Lady Shaula. As for the rest……"

"I handle it myself, got it."

"No — accomplish it in cooperation with Lady Beatrice."

"My 'myself' in a situation like this is like seventy percent Bea-ko factored in, though."

Honestly, even seventy percent was putting on quite a lot of airs. Eighty, ninety percent — the only things Subaru could really take pride in as Beatrice's contractor were his sly little head and his bag of petty tricks, so even ninety-five percent wouldn't be strange. In any case——

"Glad you came back……"

"You as well — it seems you've reassessed your own worth, and that's for the best."

Exchanging that brief remark, Subaru and Julius each focused on their own roles. Julius advanced himself to keep the witchbeasts' attacks from reaching Subaru's group, exposing himself to a rain of blades and bolts to reduce the damage to those behind him.

Meanwhile, Subaru, keeping the dying Meili away from harm from the witchbeasts, thought to focus on buying time until the victory conditions were met.

"——!"

A sense of shock ran through him, and Subaru's head snapped up as if struck. The reason was a shift that had occurred at the Pleiades Watchtower. It was——

"——Ram's presence — it's gone?"

——The breath Volcanica unleashed became blue light and struck at the topmost floor.

"————"

Emilia blocked it with an ice shield forged with all her might, and tried to break through. On the journey across the sand sea to reach the Pleiades Watchtower, the ice shields she'd used to catch the white light pouring down from the tower hadn't lasted an instant against a Dragon's breath. Multiple layers of ice shield melted instantly, and an attack on a scale where even the slightest reduction in power would count as a blessing rained down upon Emilia.

"————"

For an instant, the black monolith she'd sheltered behind flashed through Emilia's mind. Sturdy — perhaps not even a Dragon's breath could budge it. But she had a premonition that if it were ever lost, by some chance, the "Trial" would be ruined. And besides — this was a feeling unrelated to clearing the "Trial,"

"If that got destroyed, I'd be sooo lonely……"

That handprint, giving her a strange sense of déjà vu, and the monolith that bore it. She had no idea what connection it had to her, or whether it was all in her head. She just wanted to find out what that feeling really was. So she couldn't lose it. In order not to lose it.

"————"

Deploying as an Icicle Line, she concentrated in one point the mana that had been in a state ready to respond. Emilia possessed an amount of mana that even surprised herself, but that didn't mean she could handle all that vast mana at once. No matter how much mana she'd stored up, the amount she could put out at once was limited to what her Gate allowed. Even so, boasting an output more than ten times that of an ordinary mage, the strength Emilia had built up over her many years as a Spiritual Arts User expanded that potential even further.

Mages used their Gate to draw on mana stored within themselves and interfere with the world. Spiritual Arts Users borrowed the power of spirits, using mana in the atmosphere to interfere with the world.

Then, someone who possessed the aptitude for both — Emilia — could do it.

The amount of water from a tap is fixed, but if you pool that water in a bucket, you can use much more of it at once. Emilia carried that out using her own body and the world as the bucket. Holding the mana overflowing from within herself out in the external world, an application of an ultimate spell that ignored the Gate entirely——

"——Absolute Zero."

Something Subaru had named, a purely theoretical exercise he'd said might be too difficult to actually pull off. At almost the very same moment Subaru cried out that he didn't trust himself enough to bet everything on a single do-or-die attempt, Emilia was staking everything on a gambit she had never once succeeded at. And she made it succeed.

If the strength of Emilia's magic under ordinary circumstances was one, then the power of this spell, manifested by applying the overflow, was perhaps ten — or approaching a hundred.

In that instant, the white blank that swept over the world carried a force beyond mere description as "the air froze" — a force that seemed to halt even the unstoppable passage of time itself. Even the Dragon's breath, which should have been an inescapable "death," was no exception.

The blue light and the absolute zero collided head-on, and that clash carved a blank space into the world.

"————"

In an instant, those two forces of the utmost extreme, without even a moment's stalemate, annihilated each other. Truly astonishingly, the annihilation came with neither sound nor shock, and by the time the halted time began moving again, Emilia had already leapt forward, ice spear at the ready.

"Teee, yaaaaaaah——!!"

Her slender throat straining, Emilia charged straight at Volcanica. Her whole body had had its strength thoroughly wrung out of it; her body was terribly heavy. Even though the mana itself was something she'd sent outside her body to use, the burden on Emilia, the one handling it, was immense.

Even tipping over a bucket that's holding water naturally takes strength for that. And since she'd used the strength to tip over a spring rather than a bucket, it was only natural that Emilia was utterly exhausted. And yet, she raised her voice.

"I can't afford — to give up!!"

By shouting like that, Emilia spurred herself on. Energy and mana were different things, but even so, by telling herself with all her might, she felt some sleeping strength welling up within her. It might be a delusion, but if she was the one being deceived, then telling herself a lie once in a while wasn't such a bad thing.

『——Sate~llaaaa!!』

Its breath blocked, Volcanica roared and swung its forelegs and tail. An impact coming from outside her perception — Emilia defended against it relying on the sensation of the ice particles she'd deployed around herself — seven ice soldiers born, shattered as they helped her advance.

Dodging a downward blow sideways, an ice soldier's body stopped the following foreleg, and borrowing its shoulders, Emilia leapt into a great jump; the tail strike swung at her in midair was stopped, at the cost of themselves, by two ice soldiers stacked shoulder to shoulder to gain height.

"Taa! Teya! Soyaa!!"

Borrowing strength from their noble sacrifice, Emilia spun her body and drove her ice spear home. It wasn't enough to gouge the outer hide of the "Divine Dragon," but it was more than enough to draw its attention. Dodging the irritably swung foreleg with a graze of her silver hair, Emilia carried out a life-risking charge into Volcanica's bosom. From that position——

"That white scale, right there——"

Praying she could touch it, that a strike might land, Emilia looked up. The "Divine Dragon," now close enough to touch again, its white scales so hard to look directly at — witnessing them, Emilia's eyes went wide. The reason was shock. It wasn't scales that had turned white. That wasn't it. What was there was a white scar so large it could be mistaken for a single scale.

"An old…… wound……"

The wound had already closed; touching it should cause no pain now. And yet the "Divine Dragon" hated simply having that wound touched, thrashing about that violently over it. Realizing that it connected to some indelible old memory of Volcanica's, Emilia caught her breath. Seizing on that one instant of hesitation, Volcanica beat its wings.

"Ah——!"

Volcanica's massive body, flapping its wings, shot upward at once, leaving behind Emilia with her hand still outstretched. I messed up, Emilia deeply regretted her own mistake.

This was the single worst situation to find yourself in when crossing blades with a winged being. Attacked one-sidedly from a position she couldn't reach, the debt would pile up in an instant.

"No, you don't——!!"

Emilia reflexively planted her hand on the floor, and ice surged up at once from beneath her feet. Extending the improvised ice footholds skyward, Emilia desperately reached out, trying to catch up to the soaring Volcanica. But even that ice foothold, extended ten meters, twenty meters, reached its limit, and if it gained any more distance than that, it would be out of reach——

"——Everyone! Please!!"

In response to Emilia's cry, the ice soldiers came climbing up the ice foothold. The ice soldiers climbed to the very tip of the foothold at its limit, and from there leapt all at once, then leapt again off the back of the one who'd jumped before, repeating this six times, and on the back of the last one, Emilia stepped —

"Sorry!"

At the moment of her hard final push-off, the ice soldier used as a foothold snapped in the back. And yet all seven falling ice soldiers, without exception, gave a thumbs-up and smiled as they plummeted. Carried by their support, Emilia's hand, in her final leap, reached for Volcanica's tail——

『——Foolish.』

With those words, the tail was pulled back, and Emilia's fingers grazed empty air. Then, toward the wide-eyed Emilia, the withdrawn tail came flying back with tremendous force.

In midair, there was nowhere to flee. Even if she instantly conjured an ice shield, the force that would reach her after it was shattered alone was more than enough to be lethal to Emilia.

"——Ah."

Failed, this is bad, what do I do — all sorts of thoughts raced through Emilia's mind at once. In a sensation where the passage of time seemed to slow, she desperately searched for some way out, mobilizing every corner of her mind and body for anything she could do. But giving up was simply not an option. Because not a single one of the people Emilia loved most had ever chosen to give up. So——

"I won't give up either!"

But brave words alone would save nothing. As if to teach her that harsh truth, the tail strike of the ancient, long-lived "Divine Dragon" bore down on Emilia——

"——Lady Emilia!!"

At that instant, a ferocious gust blasting up from directly below gave Emilia's ascent just the slightest boost.

△▼△▼△▼△

Pushed by the surging wind, Emilia's situation shifted, if only slightly.

From a situation where she would surely not have escaped "death" at all, to one where the chance of "death" was ninety percent. And that ten percent chance of survival, Emilia — who knew no such thing as giving up — seized magnificently.

"——!"

Aimed at Emilia's head, the "Divine Dragon's" tail swung through. Because the force of her ascent had increased, the aim of that tail strike shifted from her head down to her torso. Understanding this not through perception but through instinct, Emilia folded her knees with all her might. Making herself small, trying to escape the striking range — the swinging tail grazed the very tips of Emilia's toes, and with the tremendous impact, her body spun at high speed.

"————"

Still clutching her knees, Emilia's body was sent flying straight upward. Swallowed by an impact that felt like it would send her innards flying out through her head, Emilia gritted her back teeth hard, deployed an ice foothold in the sky above, and forcibly braked her own body.

With a loud thud, Emilia, having taken the full impact with her whole body, looked down with tears in her eyes. Through the ice ceiling made in the sky as a foothold, Emilia's view — with heaven and earth flipped — showed Volcanica's head, and, far off, human figures showing themselves from the topmost stairway.

To be precise, not human figures. A figure of a person, and the shadow of an Earth Dragon.

"Ram, and……!"

Reaching a hand toward her, helping boost Emilia's ascent, was Ram, who had crashed in. Seen from a distance, Ram was covered in blood, battered from head to toe, and Emilia couldn't hide her shock that she'd come rushing here in such a state. But thanks to Ram's help, Emilia avoided having her head struck and dying.

Borrowing that assistance, Emilia once again put strength into her knees. Using this ice ceiling as a foothold, she meant to launch a sudden assault on Volcanica all at once. In front of Emilia, though, Volcanica was showing something strange.

Still in the posture of having swung its tail, it wasn't looking at Emilia — it was looking down below. She thought perhaps it had decided Ram, who'd helped her, was its next prey. But that wasn't it. The ancient "Divine Dragon's" golden eyes weren't looking at Ram. It was looking at——

『——Patrasche?』

"N-Noooooo——!!"

Drowning out Volcanica's murmur, Emilia's body was launched downward. A half-instant late, Volcanica's tail, its head turned up, shattered the ice ceiling. Too slow. Emilia's figure was already gone from there, and moreover, she wasn't even aiming straight for Volcanica's throat.

The launched Emilia had created another ice foothold — no, an ice "slope." The very thing she'd made together with Subaru back in Priestella, while fleeing from Regulus, for the purpose of accelerating without killing her momentum — she conjured it in midair now, gliding on it with her ice shoes.

Emilia's speed accelerated in midair, keeping the pursuing tail attack from reaching her. The ice runway born in the sky, chased by the tail right after Emilia's silver hair streamed past it, accelerated faster and faster and faster and faster, and then——

"Cheyaaaaa——!!"

Launched off the runway, Emilia's kick closed in at high speed on Volcanica's throat. Dodging the forelegs closing in to grab her, it flew straight, like a loosed arrow, and reached the white scar at Volcanica's throat.

『————Guh!!』

Emilia's white shoe sole struck the throat, and Volcanica screamed again. At the sky-splitting sound, Emilia covered her ears with a "Kyaaaa!" and, from the recoil of the kick, plummeted all at once. Falling, falling, falling——

"Kya! ……Wah, thank you!"

The falling Emilia was caught by the ice soldiers who had themselves fallen, having been used as footholds. Helped by the soft impact, Emilia got to her feet on the spot. Then she confirmed that she'd returned to the topmost floor, and that Volcanica above was thrashing in agony. After that, once more, she ran for the central pillar's monolith.

Running, running, running toward that monolith's handprint, the one that gave her such déjà vu——

"I knew it!!"

Reaching the monolith, this time with nothing in her way, she pressed her hand against it with full force. The monolith shook from the force and impact, but Emilia's hand fit precisely, exactly, over the handprint in question. She had no idea how many people in the world might have a hand that looked just like hers, but at the very least, this monolith's handprint belonged to someone whose hand looked just like Emilia's. And then——

『——Thou who hast reached the tower's summit. Treading the first floor, petitioner of omnipotence.』

"Ah……"

Down toward Emilia, hand still on the monolith, the flapping "Divine Dragon" descended. Still keeping its massive body afloat in the sky, Volcanica — which had once returned to a sort of dazed, senile state — again posed the same question as at the very first. But this questioning felt different from the one that had come from the depths of total oblivion the first time. — She felt, correctly, that the questioning was truly beginning now.

『——I am Volcanica. By the ancient Covenant, I ask of the will of the one who would reach the summit.』

Words she'd been asked, again and again. I ask of the will of the one who would reach the summit. — In other words, she was being asked how the person who reaches the top feels. What do you want to do, what do you wish for, what have you come here to do.

"————"

To that question, Emilia had many answers. What she wanted to do, what she wished for, what she'd come here to do — there were so many answers. But right now, in this very instant, what Emilia wished for most urgently was——

『——I ask thee. Thy will!』

Hearing the question repeated, Emilia's eyes went wide, and she opened her mouth. And in a loud voice, she answered.

"——Everyone, get along!!"

△▼△▼△▼△

In that instant, a tremendous wind howled, and the sand sea was swallowed by its force all at once.

"Whoa!?" Subaru, dodging the witchbeasts' onslaught, cried out, and Beatrice, fighting hard to treat Meili, gasped, "What was that, I suppose!?" Naturally, the same went for Julius, who was locked in a superhuman battle against Shaula.

——No, his shock might have been even greater than theirs. Because even though the tremendous whirlwind of dust had covered his vision, and he'd fallen back sensing danger to his life, there had been no follow-up attack. And witnessing the reason for that, Julius's astonishment only continued.

"——This is. Subaru!!"

"Ah!? What! The sand's bad, I can't see around……"

"Never mind that! Over here!"

Hearing Julius's urgent call, Subaru, spitting sand from his mouth, turned his face that way. Then he understood the reason for Julius's urgency, and his eyes went wide. It was——

"——Shaula!?"

"~~!!"

At the sound of Subaru's astonished voice, what filled his view was the sight of the crimson scorpion, flipped onto its back by the dust whirlwind, its multiple legs flailing on the sand. Until then, having shown no reaction whatsoever to any attack, mechanically closing in to corner them — the tower's administrator, self-proclaimed Star Watcher — an error had occurred in exactly the activity it had claimed for itself.

"Did you do something!?"

"No, nothing special. I was focused entirely on withstanding the assault. The moment that sandstorm just now blew……"

"Sandstorm…… that's right, that wind……"

The ferocious wind that had swallowed Subaru's group — being called a "sandstorm" made him notice something odd. The Augria Sand Dunes' sandstorms mercilessly swallowed all who crossed them, but ever since breaking through the barrier in question, not once had a strong wind blown around the watchtower. Not once had there been anything worthy of the name sandstorm. And that it blew just now was——

"——Subaru! Look, I suppose! The sky."

"————"

To Subaru, his thoughts scattered in a thousand directions, Beatrice shouted in her cute voice. Hearing it, Subaru was drawn to look up, and saw. A change that had occurred at the Pleiades Watchtower, an unmistakably drastic shift.

"——The clouds, they've cleared."

Piercing through the clouds, the Pleiades Watchtower stretched toward the heavens. Its very top had, literally, been shrouded in cloud, unable to be glimpsed from below. That strange cloud that had enveloped the watchtower had vanished entirely. That was when he finally realized — that wind just now was the aftershock of dispersing those clouds. The clouds had cleared, and the top of the tower could be seen from below. What that meant, by Subaru's hopeful reckoning, was only one thing.

"——Did you pull it off, Emilia?"

In Subaru's perception, sensed through "Col Leonis" among his comrades within the tower, Emilia — who should have vanished from his senses — along with Ram and Patrasche, had returned. Most likely, Ram and Patrasche had gone to support Emilia, and had achieved results there. In other words, if Subaru's thinking was correct——

"The tower's rules have been rewritten…… Shaula! Hey, Shaula! Listen!"

"~~!"

"You don't have to fight us anymore! You're free now, you're——"

The crimson scorpion, flipped over and writhing — was the cause of her suffering the ancient Covenant carved into her being, now severed? He didn't know the finer details. But it didn't matter anymore. She didn't need to suffer any longer——

"Hey, Shau——"

"——! Subaru!!"

Just as he moved to approach, calling out to her, his collar was grabbed. In that instant, through the space he was dragged out of, thrown off balance, a sharp tail slashed through with ferocious speed. Grazed by that wind, feeling the scorched smell of the air on his nose, Subaru was struck speechless.

If Julius hadn't held him back just now, he would have taken a direct hit. But what tormented Subaru wasn't the sensation of "death" — it was——

"Hey, Shaula! Shaula! Why, come on, snap out of it!!"

"~~!!"

Listening to Subaru's desperate appeal, the crimson scorpion slowly righted itself on the sand. Its posture restored from being flipped over, its compound eyes wavered, but then slowly fixed on Subaru again, drool spilling from its vicious fangs.

That was not the bearing of a being with any reason left——

"——Subaru, I'm afraid,"

Saying so, Julius gripped Subaru's shoulder and moved to step forward. But understanding what Julius intended, Subaru grabbed his arm and held him back.

He understood that Julius was trying to take on the role of doing the dirty work. But he couldn't let him do that.

"I decided I'd save her. — I'm the one who's going to bring her back."

"An unconscious sense of duty, as her so-called Master?"

"No."

Shaking his head, Subaru answered Julius's words like that. That wasn't it. The reason Subaru wanted to save Shaula wasn't because he was her Master, but——

"Not because I'm her Master. Because she got under my skin — that's why I'm doing this."

"————"

"Same as with Bea-ko. Someone who's been alone in this sand tower forever, who broke down crying because the few days she spent with us were fun — how the hell am I supposed to leave her be?"

Gritting his back teeth, Subaru declared this, still gripping Julius's arm. Julius, looking back at him, let out a breath.

"……Stubborn. But that's how it should be."

"——Julius?"

"No, I was simply impressed all over again. Once you've put on airs, you have to see them through to the very end, after all."

Smiling faintly, Julius traced the scar on his left cheek with a finger as he answered. Narrowing his eyes at that answer, Subaru noticed his free left hand had been taken hold of, something soft. Looking, he saw it was Beatrice who'd done it. She gazed up at Subaru with those round eyes, the most adorable eyes on the face of the earth,

"Meili's, past the worst of it, I suppose. As for the rest——"

"You'll help me get her out of here?"

"If Betty said no to that, what kind of devil would she be, I suppose. ……Honestly, Subaru really is a hopeless partner, I suppose."

At Beatrice's answer, Subaru gave a wry laugh and scratched his head. Then, firmly clasping hands once more with his precious contracted spirit, he turned to face the crimson scorpion — Shaula.

Spirit Knight and contractor, the two of them standing side by side, faced the weeping girl they had to save. And then——

"I'm already worn out, body and soul. — So hurry up and let yourself be saved, Shaula!"

——The final extended battle of the Pleiades Watchtower assault begins.
