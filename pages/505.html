Arc 7 Chapter 8: "Name"

"Really, Subaru, you're such a hard worker, aren't you."

"Huh, you think so?"

Praised in that bell-like voice, Subaru's nose puffed with pride. He'd just wrung out the rag he'd pulled from the water bucket and had been diligently wiping down the floor. He was in the middle of cleaning a guest room when the owner of that voice, Emilia, poked her face in. She smoothed down her long silver hair with one hand and nodded, "Mm," casually.

"There was just that whole commotion with the Witchbeast, and you're already back at work right after recovering. It surprises me."

"Heh-heh, oh, it's nothing much— wait, huh!? Is this actually a lecture about not overdoing it while I'm still recovering!?"

"Eh, that wasn't what I meant, but... now that you mention it, maybe it is. You shouldn't, Subaru. You should be resting a little more."

"Ugh, backfired."

Subaru regretted his own overthinking—he should've just accepted the compliment gracefully. But while he was busy reflecting on that, a third voice went "hmhmhm" near Emilia's shoulder. It was a small gray cat, wriggling its way out from within Emilia's silver hair.

"Gotcha, Subaru. Just as I figured—if I got Lia to praise you honestly, you'd try to read between the lines and dig yourself into a hole."

"Puck, you... what a roundabout scheme! What was the point!?"

"Heh, isn't it obvious? I'm a fickle cat spirit. Just like I bewitch people's hearts with my cuteness and my fluffy fur, this too was just a bit of mischief... nya nya nya."

"Oh, Puck, saying weird things again. You shouldn't tease Subaru like that."

The wicked-faced little cat spirit, mid-confession of his crime, was defeated by having his ear pinched. Emilia, dangling Puck from one hand, apologized to Subaru with a "sorry about that."

"I'd just finished studying and was walking around the mansion a bit. Then Puck said he'd found you, so..."

"No, no, it's totally fine. Honestly, I want to spend even one more second a day with Emilia-tan. If anything, I should be thanking Puck for scheming this."

"Really? I don't quite get it, but I'm glad you're not mad. That's a relief."

"You didn't get it!? I said it embarrassingly straightforwardly!?"

Smiling, Emilia furrowed her well-shaped brows at Subaru's protest and tilted her head. It had been a fairly direct expression of affection, but sadly, it hadn't seemed to land properly with Emilia. Beyond this, there was really nothing left but the most direct words possible.

"That's the kind of thing a guy wants to save for the right situation, the right mood, an auspicious day..."

"I'd recommend nighttime, by the way. If it's while I'm awake, I'll definitely interrupt."

"You're being almost admirably shameless with that fatherly instinct."

Fathers the world over never approve of their daughters dating, and watching Puck made it clear that held true even in another world, even for a furry creature. Subaru gave Puck a flat look, and the cat spirit puffed out his chest proudly on Emilia's shoulder.

"Naturally. Considering how cute Lia is, there's an absurd number of bugs swarming around her. If I don't work hard to chase them off, there'd be no end to it."

"No, no, wouldn't it be better to just pick one bug early and settle on it?"

"I refuse any bug with shifty eyes. Besides, letting a bug anywhere near Lia is weird to begin with. I'll properly find her a suitable match, even if it takes a thousand years or so."

"That's a mythological scale!"

Doting parent behavior at its most extreme, but the problem was that with a combination of a half-elf and a spirit, waiting a thousand years was actually a real possibility. Unfortunately, Subaru was human, so he couldn't compete on that kind of scale.

"Even if I really push myself, a hundred twenty or so is probably my limit..."

"—? What are you two talking about?"

"Ah, that was just me talking about my lifespan."

"Lifespan... come on, Subaru, that's silly. You're still practically a child, it's way too early to be worrying about that."

"Hmm, there's a gap in perception here..."

Emilia laughed behind her hand, chuckling as if she'd heard a fantastic joke. Subaru gave a wry smile at that laugh, though there was also a man's complicated joy in seeing the smile of the girl he was interested in. Truly a complicated feeling, but first things first—he needed her to actually notice him.

"At the very least, as long as I'm being treated like a kid, I can't even get in the ring."

"That's the right attitude, Subaru. Yeah, first you've got to become a proper adult. Otherwise, I won't let you cross our threshold."

"Wait, isn't this Roswaal's mansion? Whose threshold?"

Hearing Subaru's quiet resolve, Emilia, arms crossed with her short limbs, tilted her head in confusion at Puck. Still, hearing that didn't discourage Subaru. Once he'd decided what needed doing, all that was left was to charge straight at the goal.

"Heh, just watch, Puck. I'm the type who doesn't mind grinding away at the same thing every single day. Like leveling up in a game!"

"Fuhahaha, foolish human. Then prove you're not just a big talker. I'll be waiting with the princess, deep in the castle's innermost chamber. Go forth, princess!"

"Princess, meaning me? Honestly, what I'm aiming for isn't princess, it's king."

While Subaru and Puck got worked up on their own, Emilia—responding with a noticeably different temperature from the two of them—smiled at Subaru, who was wringing his rag until it was bone dry.

"But do your best at work, okay? If you work hard enough, I'm sure someone will be watching."

"Not just someone—I want Emilia-tan to be watching..."

"Yes, yes, understood. I can't watch all the time, but I'll come check on you now and then. And besides..."

Her brows drooping faintly, Emilia paused there for a moment. Then, toward Subaru who waited for what came next, she formed a smile that could make one lose oneself in it, and said:

"The person watching you work hardest is yourself, isn't it? So you have to try hard so you don't disappoint yourself."

And with that, striking at the very essence of effort, she made Subaru fall for her all over again.

"And so, having remembered that episode of falling for Emilia-tan all over again... still, tough is tough, no matter what."

Grumbling as much, Subaru scratched his head, surveying the thoroughly wrecked inside of the tent. He'd been picked up at an encampment where soldiers of the Vollachian Empire were bivouacked, and until the supply convoy to town arrived, he was being put to work as a general handyman. For Subaru, practicing "he who does not work shall not eat" in place of the immobile Rem and the freeloading Rui, helping out Todd and the other imperials—who had given him a place to sleep, food, and even treated his injuries—wasn't something he minded doing. However—

"When they do stuff like this, it actually gets to me pretty bad."

Grumbling as much, Subaru repacked the various supplies scattered across the ground into their sacks. Naturally, no matter how slovenly and rough the imperials might be, there was no way they'd store the supplies supporting their frontline life this haphazardly and messily. To begin with, the supplies in this tent had only just been tidied up by Subaru yesterday, huffing and puffing the whole time. There was only one reason it had ended up trashed like this again.

"Harassment from some rotten-natured jerk..."

"Ahh."

"Ah, or there's also the possibility that you secretly trashed it in the middle of the night. Finally decided to show your true evil nature, Archbishop of Sin?"

"Uu?"

While tidying up the scattered materials, Subaru glared sideways at Rui, who was peering over at what his hands were doing. But Rui only responded to Subaru's question vaguely, gnawing on her own finger. It seemed she had no intention of showing her true colors yet. If she'd just keep her true colors hidden forever, that would honestly be a relief for Subaru too.

"...What am I even supposed to make of what Rem told me yesterday."

Even this morning, Rui had been toddling after Subaru ever since he woke up. He recalled the sharp look Rem had given him earlier, off doing separate rehab exercises for her leg, but it was unreasonable that Subaru was the one being inconvenienced by Rui tagging along like this. In any case, compared to yesterday, there was now one more reason Subaru didn't know how to handle her—namely, the information Rem had told him: her testimony that she couldn't sense the Witch's Lingering Scent on Rui.

"——"

He'd never given it much thought before, but surely the Witch's Lingering Scent wasn't Subaru's exclusive property. In fact, he recalled Rem using those words before against Witch Cultists. She'd reacted to Petelgeuse and the Witch Cultists he led as well. On that point, Subaru—whose Miasma concentration increased every time he underwent Return by Death—had apparently seemed abnormal even to Rem back when she was still favorably disposed toward him.

"There was Ulgarm, and the fight with the White Whale... the power of Miasma has come in handy on a regular basis, but still, fundamentally this thing is basically a magnet for unnecessary disaster, isn't it."

Even though there was no denying it had been useful in the past, right at this moment it was only functioning as a reason for Rem to dislike him, so as far as Subaru was concerned, his impression of Miasma was the worst of the worst. And no matter how much he thought it over—

"There's no way someone connected to the Witch Cult—especially an Archbishop of Sin—has nothing to do with Miasma."

That was the conclusion Subaru reached. Since he didn't know the cause of Miasma's occurrence or its specific details, this was, admittedly, nothing more than armchair theorizing if someone wanted to call it that. Still, there was something he wasn't willing to concede. The connection between an Archbishop of Sin and the "Witch" was obvious—that was what made them the embodiment of evil as Witch Cultists. However—

"Conversely, could you argue that anyone not giving off Miasma isn't connected to the Witch Cult? Seriously, with this one right here in front of me?"

"Aoh?"

Beside the pondering Subaru, Rui just groaned with a vacant expression and voice. Sighing at that, Subaru pushed himself up and resumed tidying the messed-up inside of the tent. The tools that had been leaned up were knocked over, the repacked bundles undone and their contents scattered. It was a situation that looked exactly like something a child would do.

"Well, it's probably just plain harassment..."

The freshly tidied tent being trashed again wasn't likely Rui's covert scheming—more likely it was harassment or interference from someone in the encampment. Being in an imperial encampment, that was only natural, but he had points of contact with people other than Todd. And their attitudes were, for the most part, hard to call friendly. On that point, it was just as Todd himself had said—that he was the odd one out.

"Still, this is a lot better than suddenly getting punched or having a boot shoved down my throat."

If someone said his standards for happiness and normalcy had dropped, he couldn't deny it, but in fact, not receiving that kind of painful welcome made the situation that much easier to bear.

Not to brag, but Subaru was used to being kept at a distance or treated as a nuisance. It wasn't for nothing that he'd stumbled at the debut of his high school life and remained an outsider for nearly two years afterward. There hadn't been active bullying, but when it came to being treated as an awkward presence, he was a seasoned veteran. Thinking about it that way, his high school classmates had probably all been decent, good-natured people. Even though they treated Subaru like he was invisible, none of them had ever bothered to actually harass thin air, or take pleasure in hurting it.

"I hope all those kind-hearted people are making it big over there. Like Inabata-kun, who went out of his way to bring me the class handouts—hope he's become somebody big."

Recalling classmates whose faces had already grown hazy, Subaru finished tidying the inside of the tent in less time than yesterday, restoring it to about the same state as before. Still, it was basically the same as filling in a hole someone dug back up again—so as far as today's work balance sheet went, it was firmly in the negative. Emilia had once told him that someone—or at the very least, he himself—was watching his efforts, and Subaru wanted that to be something he could lean on.

"As things stand, if it's just you watching, that's no motivation at all. If Rem, at least, were watching, my mood would be a lot different..."

"Ohh, uwahh."

Whether she'd learned a bit from being scolded, Rui at least hadn't gotten in the way of Subaru's work today. Grateful for even that much, Subaru stepped outside to move to the next tent, and—

"—Whoa!?"

Right as he stepped out of the tent, something caught his foot and he went sprawling spectacularly. He instinctively caught himself on the ground with his hands, but while his right hand was fine, his left hand throbbed slightly. Though it was on the mend, it wasn't fully healed—the claw marks Rem had left. So, groaning "guh..." Subaru looked behind him, and—

"Yo, coward. What's the matter, groveling on the ground like that?"

"You're..."

Before Subaru's widened eyes, standing right beside the tent's entrance was a man with a brutish look about him. An eyepatch over his right eye, patchy stubble—a figure who looked like the very embodiment of the word "thug," and the very man who'd once forced Subaru to eat his boot at this encampment. If he recalled, Todd had called him—

"Jamal, right, that's what they called—gah!?"

"Jamal-san, it's called. You and that woman you're with both got no manners, huh."

The instant the man—Jamal—judged that Subaru had dropped the honorific, he moved straight to direct action. Jamal stomped down on Subaru's left hand as he lay sprawled on the ground, on the splinted, bandaged fingers, and ground his heel into them steadily. The pain in the crushed fingers flared up again, grinding. Subaru bit back a scream in the back of his throat and endured it, when—

"Ah-uh!" "Ah?"

Rui, screeching, latched onto the leg Jamal was using to stomp on Subaru's hand. The girl's weight was light, and Jamal's trained frame didn't budge an inch. As she clung on, he grabbed Rui's long hair and forcibly tore her off.

"Hey, she's a kid!"

Seeing that, Subaru blurted out in a flash of anger. Hearing that protest, Jamal twisted his mouth in displeasure, yanking hard on the hand gripping Rui's hair, drawing an "ah!" scream from the girl.

"So what if she's a kid? Word is, you've been treating this brat pretty coldly. What, sudden change of heart?"

"That's not... push her too far and it'll be you who regrets it."

"Ha, what a lame excuse. Come up with something better."

Snorting, Jamal swung his arm and flung Rui to the ground. Tumbling, Rui pulled her captured hair close, holding her head and glaring at Jamal with teary eyes, an "uu—" escaping her. Honestly, there was no telling if Jamal's outrage might trigger Rui reverting back. Subaru's desperate bluff hadn't been entirely empty words. Fortunately, or so it seemed, there was no sign of Rui's original personality awakening from the pain and anger. Though in this case, it was hard to judge who exactly it was fortunate for.

"An unruly little brat. No, it's not just the brat. You, and that other woman too—not a single one of you sits right with me!"

"Guohh!"

Perhaps his anger built up as he spoke, Jamal kicked Subaru across the face. Caught off guard with his mouth open, Subaru's teeth sliced up the inside of his mouth badly. Blood came gushing out. As the taste of iron rust spread instantly across his tongue, Subaru looked up at Jamal.

"Mbeh... for what it's worth, we're vouched for by Todd-san—or, like, he said it's fine for us to stay here..."

"Heh, Todd-san, huh. Real attached to him, aren't you. That your lifeline?"

"——"

"Unfortunately, my rank's higher than his. Not that I won't hear requests, but there's no reason I have to do what he says."

Speaking roughly, Jamal stepped in on Subaru again. He instinctively curled his body to protect his head, but this time the kick was driven into Subaru's stomach. His stomach churned under the toe of the boot, and as Subaru groaned from the impact, the kicks kept coming relentlessly.

"First off, back at the riverside, two of my men got taken out by your woman. Those two are useless now, gotta be sent back. Real black mark on my record. Someone's gotta pay for that... and then that Todd bastard goes and finds something extra."

"—Ngh"

"If you weren't carrying that knife around, I'd have just torn you to pieces and been done with it. Being a soldier's rough, huh, right?"

Relentless kicks, and words designed to grate on his nerves. He didn't even need to look up at Jamal's one glaring eye to know what the man was after.

Jamal's aim wasn't to hurt Subaru. It was something beyond that. Most likely, his goal was to provoke Subaru into striking back, then cut him down as justified self-defense. Jamal had said he wouldn't listen to Todd's words, but from the exchange back when Subaru was first forced to eat his boot, it was clear their relationship wasn't one he could completely disregard. So Jamal wanted a reason. A reason he could kill Subaru for.

And by extension, that was also a reason to retaliate against Rem. If that was the case, Subaru wouldn't take the bait. As long as it kept Jamal's vile malice from turning toward Rem, even if Subaru's left-hand fingers got broken again, even if the rest of his fingers got broken too, Subaru would count that as a win. For that, he had to endure, endure, endure, endure—

"—Hey, what are you doing there?"

"Tch."

Just as Subaru continued to endure, a voice other than Jamal's came flying in. Instantly, Jamal clicked his tongue, drew back his foot, and slowly stepped away. Then, approaching with footsteps, came the orange-haired young man.

"Heard you headed this way with no business here, and figured it'd be you."

"Todd. Awful overprotective, aren't you. That imperial-capital dagger really your favorite that much? Sucking up to a coward like this?"

"...Jamal."

Narrowing his eyes, Todd glared at Jamal. A somewhat hostile air flowed between the two, but it was cut off when Jamal said, "Forget it." Having unilaterally broken the tension, Jamal looked down at Subaru, whom he'd just been kicking a moment ago.

"Learn from this, and watch your step where I can see you. Won't be my fault if you go sprawling like this again, will it? Hahahaha."

Making sure to drive home the point that no violence had ever occurred against Subaru, he passed by Todd's side. Subaru, too, had no words to stop him. To claim damages here would be the same as taking Jamal's bait. Only once Jamal's back had completely disappeared from view did Subaru pull himself upright.

"Ahh, damn it... hurts... That bastard, got a rotten personality like some spoiled woman. Everything he does is so underhanded..."

It sounded like nothing but resentment toward Rem, but really, the guy just had a bad personality. To begin with, the reason Rem had gone after him in the first place was probably because Jamal had provoked her in that same high-handed manner. Of course, it was also possible Rem, lacking her memories, had jumped to conclusions and struck first.

"Well, that can't be helped, since Rem doesn't understand anything."

Rendering that hopelessly lenient verdict on Rem, Subaru spat out the bloody saliva in his mouth and slowly stood up where he was.

"You doing okay? Rough luck getting tangled up with Jamal."

Todd called out, frowning, to the unsteady Subaru. If he hadn't come, Jamal's assault would likely have continued. Subaru was grateful he'd cut it short. But more than that—

"I'm fine. More importantly, how's Rem—"

"That girl's helping with the cooking. Sit her in a chair and she's real efficient. Jamal won't do anything where people can see... probably."

Such an uncertain answer couldn't put Subaru's mind at ease. Wiping the blood from his mouth, Subaru looked at his left hand. The splint had come off, the bandage undone. The finger that had seemed to be on the mend had started discoloring again in that unpleasant way.

"Aw crap... looks like I'll need to get that treated again. Damn it, Jamal."

"...What kind of guy is he, anyway?"

"Jamal? He's a peer of mine from when I became a soldier, a real high-flyer. Comes from minor nobility, promotion to Third General isn't even a dream for him... ah, you know what a Third General is?"

Subaru shook his head, and Todd raised a finger, nodding, "Right." According to his explanation, the soldiers of the Vollachian Empire had a rank system called "Generals." Private, corporal, Third General, Second General, rising up—

"When you get to First General, there's only nine of those in the whole empire—a class apart. Warriors directly under the emperor, called the 'Nine Divine Generals.' Well, once you're at that level, it's not about lineage or achievements anymore."

"You mean talent?"

"Exactly. So us regular folks aim for around Third General."

Listening to Todd's account, free of any tragedy, Subaru recalled Jamal's earlier attitude. A "General" was, in other words, meant to fill the role of an officer in the military—but Subaru couldn't imagine Jamal having that kind of caliber at all. Self-centered, low in empathy. A textbook incompetent-superior type.

"On the battlefield, apparently there's plenty of soldiers who die from a stray arrow from behind—pass that along to him from Todd-san's mouth for me."

"Scary thought. So, what about treating that hand?"

"Could you? ...After I check on Rem's face first."

"Geez, head over heels, huh. ...Feel kinda bad for that other girl."

Even pushing through the pain in his finger, confirming Rem's safety was the top priority. At that attitude from Subaru, Todd shrugged and gestured toward Rui, curled up in front of the tent. She had the hair Jamal had pulled wrapped around her finger, growling low like a beast.

"Face of a kid who doesn't like taking it lying down. I like a kid with some fighting spirit."

"Ah! Uu, ah!"

Turned toward with Todd's smile, Rui howled as if answering back. Subaru almost said, if you like her that much, why not just take her off my hands—but figuring that if that got back to Rem he'd just get scolded again, he zipped his mouth shut.

"—That smell, did you pick up another injury?"

"Uh... you can tell?"

"I can tell. There's a child watching, so please act with some decency."

That was Rem's admonishment, shared over the same table at lunchtime. There were parts of that he couldn't quite agree with, but since there was no benefit to arguing, he just nodded along. Beside him, Rui, mimicking him, nodded too.

This was after receiving Jamal's underhanded bullying, and afterward getting treatment through Todd's kindness. After first confirming Rem's safety, Subaru had gotten treated in the medical tent—the one in charge of treatment had been exasperated, wondering if he was back again—and after finishing that, he'd finished tidying another tent and joined up with them at lunch.

Lunch at the encampment was closer to a rationing system, where people who'd received their share from the distribution point formed loose groups and cleared out in sequence. Since Rem was helping with preparing and serving the food, and out of outsider's reserve, Subaru's group ended up near the end, essentially receiving the leftovers.

"Well, not in a position to be picky. Just having something to eat is a blessing in itself."

Saying so, Subaru brought his and Rem's portions over, lining them up on the small table at the edge. Rui, cheekily, had secured her own portion just like Subaru. And so, the three of them, each in their own way reluctant, gathered around the table for a late lunch.

"How'd it go until noon? Anything rough?"

"Nothing in particular. They were considerate about my leg too... All I helped with was some light cooking. And even that, while being taught."

"While being taught... like, did your body remember anything?"

"——"

At Subaru's rapid-fire question, Rem narrowed her pale blue eyes and pressed her lips together. Subaru panicked, thinking he'd pushed too hard, but Rem soon let out a breath and said,

"...If I said I hadn't hoped for that, I'd be lying."

"Rem..."

"I thought that if I tried various things, something might come naturally to my hands. But that was probably too convenient a hope."

Looking quietly down at her own hands, Rem murmured as if ashamed of her own naivety. But who in the world could call the hope Rem held naive? Who could call Rem's stance—clinging to an answer when she couldn't even recall the elements that made up herself—naive?

"...Why is it you're the one making the pained face?"

"Why, well, that's..."

"—. You know the me from before. That much I understand. No need to doubt it."

Subaru, having lowered his eyes, was startled by Rem's words. Up until now, Rem had done nothing but voice negative opinions about Subaru over and over—this was practically the first gesture of reaching out she'd shown. Hope sprouted in Subaru's chest, wondering if she'd come to understand. However—

"But no matter how many times you call me 'Rem,' I can't accept that as my name. No matter what's said to me, that won't change."

"Ah..."

"Perhaps, if this child could speak, it might be different."

As she said it, Rem, her eyes softening, stroked the head of Rui beside her. Rui, letting herself be petted, was absorbed in finishing off her own share of food in front of her. That callous attitude aside, unfortunately, even if Rui had a proper sense of self, there was no way she'd speak of Rem from her own mouth. Even if she had a mouth willing to speak of it, Subaru wouldn't let her. No matter what, the undying hostility would see to that.

"What's this, what's this, such serious faces. Bit of a gloomy table, isn't it."

Into the meal of those three came Todd, butting in with his easygoing manner. It was an intrusion right as an awkward silence threatened to settle. Relieved at the air being changed, Subaru glanced at the man taking a seat beside him. "Todd-san, huh." Between this and the Jamal incident earlier, this good-natured man kept saving him.

"Appreciate you looking out for us, but shouldn't you eat with your own guys?"

"Hm? Well, they're people I've known a long while, won't change our relationship much just because I skip a meal or two with them for a day. More importantly, I'll spend my time making a good impression with you three."

"Being made an impression on doesn't do much for me without any cash on hand."

"That's more of a pay-on-results, or pay-when-you-succeed kind of deal. Think of it as an upfront investment."

Todd's light-hearted manner of speaking, whether intentional or not, changed the mood. Then, saying "But more importantly," Todd slung an arm firmly around Subaru's shoulder. Pulling the startled Subaru close, he said near his ear,

"Compared to yesterday, you two seem to be talking properly. Made up, did you?"

"Made up... I dunno. I'd like to think my sincerity got through to her at least a little."

"—. I can hear you, you know. If you think we've grown close, you're mistaken." "Uu!"

With a huffy air, Rem voiced her displeasure at the two men's conversation. Rui, perhaps meaning to take Rem's side, seemed to be cooperating with her as she wiped her mouth. The combined effect of Rem's curt answer and Rui being accepted by her weighed heavily on Subaru's chest. That seemed to get through to Todd, arm still around him, and he gave Subaru's shoulder a light pat, saying "hey now,"

"No need to get so down. You've got her here, close enough to talk to. That alone puts you way ahead of me, compared to my situation."

"Ah, right, you mentioned being apart from your fiancée."

"Yeah. My fiancée's living in the imperial capital. Not just this mission I've got to wrap up—the time we're apart is just too long to begin with. Ahh, it's rough being lonely like this. Right?"

"So that loneliness is the reason you keep looking out for us?"

"That's about the size of it. So go ahead and let me use you all to my heart's content. Worth having picked you up."

Sensing that Todd was trying not to let him feel obligated, Subaru silently thanked him. Saying thanks directly would probably be tasteless. Todd seemed to have accounted for that too. In that mood, the meal for the four of them proceeded, but—

"Actually, we're supposed to be getting a ride with the supply convoy, but how much longer do you think this mission of yours will take, Todd-san?"

"Told you, didn't I? Until we find the 'People of Shudrak' hiding in the forest... if we can't find them, we might be stuck out here for years. Serving the crown ain't easy."

"'People of Shudrak'..."

Holding a wooden spoon in his mouth, Subaru pondered at Todd's sour-faced answer. The "People of Shudrak"—when Todd had first asked him about this at the encampment, Subaru had guessed that the person referred to by that name might be the masked man he'd met in the forest. He owed that man a debt for giving him the knife. That's why he hadn't told Todd about his existence either—but now, it had started to feel like a breach of good faith.

In terms of debts owed, he was indebted to Todd no less than to the masked man. So then, what reason was there to honor his obligation to the masked man, but not to Todd?

"What are you guys planning to do once you find these 'People of Shudrak'? Setting up camp like this, are you gonna... fight them?"

"——"

He tried to phrase the question as casually as possible. Still, something like tension he couldn't fully hide crept into his voice. In fact, though she wasn't part of the conversation, Rem's expression reacted to hearing that topic too. Even trying not to show it, a reaction of avoidance at the word "fight." And Todd, asked the question, closed one eye at Subaru's words,

"Nah, seems like the 'Generals' would rather avoid fighting if they can help it. I don't know the details myself, but the 'People of Shudrak' are apparently a pretty formidable tribe. If it comes to a fight, a tough battle's a given—this time too, negotiation's supposedly the goal."

"Negotiation? What kind of negotiation with a forest tribe?"

"Don't go asking a grunt like me too much!? ...Not that I know for sure, but probably something like swearing fealty to the imperial capital, or by extension, His Imperial Majesty, right?"

"So the 'People of Shudrak' don't follow the emperor of the Vollachian Empire?"

"Some don't. And that's Vollachian style too, isn't it?"

Wearing a warrior's smile as he said that, Todd made Subaru raise his eyebrows. He then quietly responded with just "Vollachia, hooray," accepting the exchange as it stood. If he believed Todd's words, it seemed the imperial army also wanted to avoid attacking the "People of Shudrak." If so, wouldn't it actually be better for Subaru to offer up his information—potentially avoiding needless bloodshed and conflict? Still, the information Subaru held wasn't much to speak of, and to explain why he'd been hiding it, he'd have to reveal that he'd falsified his identity too.

"Ugh, this is tricky. Solve one problem, create another..."

"...Somehow, no matter when I look at you, you've got a crease between your brows. Your looks aren't exactly great to begin with, so you might as well smile at least."

"Ouch, that stings! ...If I talked to you with a big smile all the time, would you respond a little more gently and cheerfully?"

"Huh?"

Rem's response, genuinely bewildered, broke Subaru's spirit early. Abandoning his twitching attempted smile, Subaru slumped his shoulders, and Todd laughed at him.

"Well, even a long road's gotta start with a first step. I'm watching your heroic efforts, so don't worry."

"Thanks, Todd-san. ...Now, the honest truth about not being able to see your fiancée?"

"Hahaha, nice one, keep it coming, keep it coming."

"You jerk!"

Was he comforting him or teasing him? Either way, it was a fact that Todd's manner like this was a lifesaver. That he could avoid getting too weighed down in this state of emergency was undeniably thanks to him. Which was exactly why he wanted, at the very least, to see their objective accomplished quickly.

"When's the actual push into the forest gonna start?"

"Once the other camps finish deploying, we all move in at once, that's the plan. The forest's big and deep. Even working a full day, the ground we can cover is limited, but..."

"I see. Well, can't exactly rush through it. No telling what's in there, and it's a place where big Witchbeasts wander around too."

The masked man Subaru suspected might be one of the "People of Shudrak." Or, the hunter, who might also be one of the "People of Shudrak." And the giant serpent Witchbeast they'd encountered during the skirmish with the hunter—Rem's traps were still set somewhere around that area, too. Thinking about it that way, the hardships awaiting Todd and the others as they pushed into the forest were easy enough to imagine. Even if he couldn't explain the full circumstances, he felt like he ought to at least mention Rem's traps, or unnecessary confusion might arise—

"—A Witchbeast?"

But right as Subaru was thinking that, Todd, who'd been drinking water beside him, widened his eyes. He wiped the water trailing down his mouth with his sleeve, staring at Subaru with startled eyes. Not understanding the meaning of that surprise, Subaru widened his own eyes in turn, "Eh?"

"Did you just say Witchbeast? That there's a Witchbeast in that forest?"

"No, uh, I did say that, but... did I say something weird?"

"Of course you did. Witchbeasts aren't something you just run into so easily. If it were Lugunica, the Witchbeast superpower, maybe, but this isn't even along the border with the kingdom."

"——"

"You're not joking, are you. Hey, girlie."

At Todd's suddenly serious tone, Subaru, confused, couldn't say anything. Then Todd redirected his question toward Rem. Rem, put on the spot, answered Todd's question with "Yes,"

"Answer me. Did you see a Witchbeast too, girlie? In this Buddheim Jungle."

"I'm not sure what exactly you mean by 'Witchbeast,' but... if you mean a large green creature, then yes, I saw one."

"Horns on its head?"

"Horns? ...I believe so—white, misshapen ones."

The instant he heard that answer, Todd, his cheeks stiffening, stood up on the spot. He looked at Subaru once more, and,

"What kind of Witchbeast was it? What did it look like?"

"Uh, a snake. A huge snake. Nearly ten meters, one giant one."

"One, just one, huh... damn, in a forest this huge, there's no telling if it's really just one. But you don't seem to be lying either. Circumstances have changed!"

Roughly scratching at his own head, Todd, his complexion changed, turned his back on Subaru and the others. Just as he started to move away, he suddenly said "Ah," as if noticing something, and turned back around, coming back toward Subaru and the others.

"That was valuable information. Without it, things could've gotten real bad. You saved us."

"—Oh"

Todd roughly ruffled the heads of both seated Subaru and Rem. Caught off guard, they both widened their eyes with delayed reactions, but he paid it no mind and turned his back again. And heading toward the others gathered around their own lunches, he said,

"Squad leaders, assemble! Heading to see the 'General'! Important matter!"

Clapping his hands to gather his comrades, he stirred up a commotion throughout the encampment as he headed toward the tent in the back—likely the important one where war councils and the like were held. Watching that furious energy off half-dazed,

"...Quite the reaction. Is that creature—the Witchbeast—that important? Of course, I understand it's a dangerous creature."

"—. No, actually, I might not have properly understood that myself either."

"Hah..."

Rem's reaction, best described as dubious, but Subaru himself still hadn't managed to fully organize the contents of his own head. That was how much of a bolt from the blue Todd's reaction had been for him.

"In the Empire, Witchbeasts are rare..."

That was completely unexpected, or rather, a circumstance he'd never even imagined. To begin with, for Subaru, life in another world and the existence of Witchbeasts were inseparably linked. Setting aside the first day of his summoning in the royal capital, the existence of Witchbeasts had been a major part of most events since.

There was Ulgarm in the Witchbeast incident Meili caused, the fight against the White Whale that was necessary for Wilhelm to fulfill his long-cherished wish, and the Great Rabbit that had tried to devour the Sanctuary whole. In Priestella, there hadn't been a Witchbeast per se, but the Augria Sand Dunes and the Pleiades Watchtower he'd headed to in order to resolve that problem were practically the very stronghold of Witchbeasts. Of course, the Witchbeast most memorable to Subaru would have to be the "Crimson Scorpion."

"...Got a little sentimental there for a second, but anyway. With that kind of track record, the idea that Witchbeasts might be rare never even had room to occur to me."

He'd simply assumed, like monsters in a TV RPG, that Witchbeasts showed up anywhere in the world regardless of location. But apparently that wasn't the case. Thinking about it, even back in his original world, lions and giraffes weren't just anywhere across the globe, so in a sense it was only natural, but still.

"First time I'm hearing Lugunica gets called the Witchbeast superpower..."

Even though they were formidable beings, Todd had changed his complexion over just a single Witchbeast appearing. Measured against that common sense, it was no wonder Lugunica was called the Witchbeast superpower. Someone like Meili was practically the stuff of fairy tales by comparison at this point.

"So then, that girl—I wonder, if she went somewhere with hardly any Witchbeasts, some country or region, could she live like a normal kid..."

Thinking about the future of Meili, who'd gotten separated from them, Subaru's pondering profile was interrupted by Rem's voice. Wondering what was the matter, he looked over, and she gestured with her hand toward the table. There, he found Rui, having emptied her own meal, fast asleep on the table, breathing softly.

"It seems she's had her fill and fallen asleep. It's aggravating, but could I ask you to carry her?"

"You'd go so far as to call it 'aggravating'..."

Wry-smiling at Rem's phrasing, Subaru tilted his head with a sigh. Cleaning up all four portions of food, including Todd's forgotten one, he reluctantly scooped up Rui's body. He could tell from the times she clung to him—she was light. On the outside, she was just an ordinary girl. Truly, only on the outside was she just an ordinary girl.

"Is Rem gonna be okay? My back's free, if..."

"What kind of ungainly request is that? I have no need to borrow your hand. I can manage myself just fine, thank you..."

Saying that, what Rem picked up was a wooden staff propped against the table—though "staff" was a generous term for what was really a thick branch, apparently picked up somewhere. Cloth was wrapped around the grip to prevent splinters from digging in. A makeshift, improvised cane, more or less. Using it, Rem stood up. Her gait was still somewhat unsteady, but—

"—I'm fine."

"...Really, though? No need to be stubborn, you can rely on me if you're struggling."

"I won't rely on you. I'm fine to this extent. You just make sure not to drop that child."

"Alright, fine. But remember this much—the reason I'm carrying this one like this isn't because I want to, it's for your sake."

"What in the world makes you say something like that so seriously..."

Rem, exasperated at Subaru's stubborn refusal to be seen as voluntarily kind to Rui, planted her cane and, with halting steps, followed after Subaru. For now, they'd return Rem and Rui to the tent lent to them, and Subaru would continue tidying the tents. —Though what Todd and the others would do concerned him.

"—. Are you worried about those people?"

"...Eh? Ah, yeah, something like that. No, I know myself that I'm being disloyal to someone I owe a debt to. The Witchbeast talk was probably careless of me too."

"—A person you owe a debt to, is it."

Feeling like he'd become a villain who did nothing but lie, Subaru's mood sank somewhat. But listening to Subaru's talk, Rem murmured something with a hint of meaning. "A person you owe a debt to"—the phrase, pulled out and repeated on its own, carried a feeling that was hard to read. But it certainly didn't seem to be a positive one.

"Rem?"

"—. No, it's nothing. Don't worry about it."

"No, given how that just went, 'don't worry about it' is kind of impossible..."

"Is that so. Then, please don't speak to me."

"Now you've put even more distance between us! If you were going to say something, just say it! I'm curious!"

Since he was walking to match Rem's pace, the two of them made slow progress. Perhaps out of irritation at having her pace matched, Rem let out a small sigh. Then, sensing Subaru's continued insistence, she slowly shook her head and said,

"That Todd-san, or whatever his name was. ...I don't hold a particularly good impression of him."

"Huh? Why? He's letting us stay at the encampment with nothing to offer in return, and he's protected us from that nasty monster 'Boot-Feeder.' Not feeling grateful for that would be a bit much."

"I didn't say I don't feel grateful. Of course I'm thankful. It's just..."

"Just?"

Rem, cutting herself off there, showed a slight hesitation at Subaru urging her on. But the silence that formed lasted only about two seconds, and along with a deep exhale, she said,

"—I think it's difficult to trust someone who hasn't tried to ask for your name."

"——"

Told that by Rem, Subaru unconsciously held his breath. And as he tried to retort, wondering what she meant, he suddenly found himself looking back over everything up to now.

—Someone who hasn't tried to ask for your name, Rem had said.

Keeping that in mind and looking back, indeed, it was true. Todd had never once, not a single time, called Subaru by name. He'd always kept calling him "you there." If he didn't know the name, that was only natural. But—

"I-isn't that just a coincidence? Even Rem, you don't call me by name either—"

"—Natsuki Subaru, isn't it. I think there's a difference in meaning between knowing but not saying it, and not even trying to know in the first place. That's all I'm saying."

"——"

"That's the extent of my opinion. Either way, we have no choice but to rely on those people."

Saying that, Rem passed by the stopped Subaru and walked ahead. Watching Rem's back gradually moving further ahead, Subaru found himself unable to say anything.

Unfortunately, Subaru had no way to loosen Rem's stubborn heart. As the Witchbeast matter had made clear, Subaru was far too ignorant of this world's common sense. He wasn't even fully versed in the kingdom, let alone the empire—an unknown land in every respect. Perhaps in the empire, there was some special significance to whether or not you asked someone's name. Maybe asking before introducing yourself was considered rude. But even if there were such a rule, Subaru couldn't explain it to Rem. His own ignorance and lack of education disgusted him more and more.

"...How long are you going to keep standing there?"

"Ah..."

Startled by the voice, he looked up to find Rem, a little ahead, turned back to look at him. She wore a slightly impatient expression, both hands resting on her cane, glaring at Subaru. The moment he saw her waiting for him like that, something tightened in his chest. He nearly ended up kneeling right there on the spot.

"Guh...!"

"Wh— wh-what's wrong!? Don't tell me, your finger—"

"No, I just thought, Rem's waiting for me, and it just kind of got to me..."

"...I won't say what exactly, but I've lost out here."

Saying that with a flat expression and tone, Rem finally turned her back on Subaru again. Hurriedly chasing after that back, Subaru narrowed his eyes, replaying both his apology to Rem and the words she'd just told him.

Telling himself it was probably just Rem overthinking things.

△▼△▼△▼△

—And so, the resolution of the question that had lodged itself in the depths of Subaru's chest came the next day.

Todd, having parted ways after lunch, went straight into discussions with the "General" of the encampment, and the war council apparently dragged on late into the night. In the end, after delivering Rem and Rui back to the tent, Subaru had no choice but to carry on with his assigned work while holding onto a murky unease, and on top of that, that night Rem treated him even more coldly than the day before, leaving him with a distinct feeling of having nowhere to belong. However—

"—Hey, wake up, wake up. How long are you gonna sleep, you."

"Nnh?"

Shaken by the shoulder, the sleeping Subaru was roused by someone. Waking up easily was one of Subaru's few virtues, but there was still a difference between waking on his own and being woken by someone else. Moving his somewhat sluggish thoughts, he opened his eyes to find, reflected in the view of Subaru lying on the ground, Todd's face.

"...Todd-san?"

"Ah, you look worn out. Can't be helped, doing unfamiliar work like that. Anyway, thanks to you..."

"—Natsuki Subaru."

"Hm?"

Todd spoke quickly to Subaru, who was slowly sitting up. He blinked at Subaru, who had suddenly stated his own name. For a moment, he seemed unsure what that meant, but—

"Natsuki Subaru, that's my name."

"Hm... ah, were you actually bothered about being called 'you there'?"

"Nah, it's not like I was that bothered, but... since I never introduced myself, I thought maybe I'd been super rude without realizing it."

"Haha, that's overthinking it. But, Natsuki Subaru, huh, got it, got it."

Laughing lightly, Todd patted the shoulder of Subaru, who lowered his eyes in embarrassment. Seeing that his manner hadn't changed, Subaru felt some relief. It seemed yesterday's worry from Rem had just been overthinking, and Subaru's own unease had been needless too. Todd genuinely seemed to have just forgotten that detail, muttering "my mistake, my mistake" before saying,

"Anyway, that matter's important too, but there's something even more important. Thanks to what you told us yesterday, the 'Generals' changed their strategy."

"Changed the strategy... you mean, for taking the forest?"

"Right, right. I mean, on top of clearing an unknown forest, if there's a Witchbeast living in there too, that changes things quite a bit. Our own casualties wouldn't be something to laugh off. So—"

Cutting himself off there, Todd broke into a broad, beaming grin. Then, cupping the still not-fully-awake Subaru's face in both hands, he said,

"We're wrapping up the operation, quick and easy."

"Quick and easy? So then, does that mean, Todd-san, you can go back to your fiancée?"

"Haha, that's right!"

At Todd's enthusiastic nod, Subaru shared in the joy too, "Ohh!" With the year-long deployment plan changed and a return home in sight, Todd's joy was naturally redoubled. Clapping hands with the overjoyed man, the two of them danced together inside the tent. Then, naturally—

"...Um, could you two please be a little quieter."

"Ah, sorry, Rem."

Sitting up from her own bed, Rem glared irritably at the two men. It probably wasn't just because she'd been woken up that she was in a bad mood. The reason for her irritation was surely that her dream had been interrupted by the two men's dance steps. After that, she gave her head a light shake, muttered "honestly...," and then,

"—? Does it smell strange to you, somehow?"

"Smell?"

"Yes. Aside from your own body odor."

Sniffing, Rem waved a hand at Subaru as if brushing away something foul-smelling. Subaru, somewhat wounded by that gesture, was quickly apologized to by Todd with an "my bad, my bad."

"Thought we had enough distance that it'd be fine, but I guess a good nose can tell. But, gotta act quick once it's decided, or things'll feel unsettled, right?"

"Todd-san?"

Saying that, Todd opened the entrance flap of Subaru and the others' tent. Then he beckoned for them to come out. So, Subaru exchanged a glance with Rem, handed her the cane, and headed for the entrance. Then, standing beside Todd, he looked.

"—Huh?"

It was thick black smoke, billowing up with tremendous force, and a powerful scorching smell. And across his entire field of view, the great jungle that seemed to fill the horizon from left to right in the distance—the "Buddheim Jungle"—was wrapped in crimson flames, blazing furiously.

"This is..."

Beside the frozen Subaru, Rem, witnessing the same sight, fell speechless. Both Subaru and Rem stood rooted to the spot, staring at the forest burning crimson like a nightmare, the jungle being consumed, a world coming to its end.

"With a Witchbeast lurking in there, there's no telling how much damage we might've taken on our side. Once I argued that, Second General Zikr, who's commanding this, understood."

"——"

"Thanks to the information you gave us, we avoided taking any casualties on our side. Huge help."

Saying that with a laugh, Todd clapped Subaru's back with his palm. Struck by that casual impact, Subaru's lips trembled. His lungs trembled, his throat trembled, his voice trembled too. At Todd's unchanged, friendly attitude, what Subaru's trembling voice managed to weave was—

"W-why...?"

"Why, what?"

"You said... you said you didn't want to fight the 'People of Shudrak' in the forest, right?"

If it came to a fight, a hard battle was certain, and the hope was for negotiation, to make them swear fealty to the emperor. That's what Todd had told Subaru yesterday, over that meal. Subaru had inwardly taken comfort in that, believing that meant no fighting would break out.

"This is worse than fighting, even...!"

"Yeah, I didn't want to fight. No telling how many casualties we'd take on our side. I might've died too. But thanks to you giving the 'General' something to work with, the problem got resolved. The 'People of Shudrak' can't oppose His Imperial Majesty anymore either."

"—Ngh"

"I get to head back to my fiancée sooner too. Man, you were a real find. I've properly told the 'General' about you, so you should get a reward for sure."

Joking that he might even get a second dagger, Todd patted Subaru's back once more. Then, muttering "oh right" as if remembering something, he said,

"Was told to come get you once I'd shown you the results of the battle. Sorry, but breakfast might be a bit delayed. But, you don't need to tidy up the tents anymore. In a while, we'll be pulling out of the encampment."

"—Ah, eh?"

"Sheesh, get it together. —Don't go scaring the girlie."

That last part said in a whisper, Todd left the spot, leaving behind a genuinely good-natured smile. In the end, Subaru could say nothing to the retreating back, could only stay silent.

"——"

But whether Subaru stayed silent or was tormented by inner turmoil, the sight of the forest burning far off before his eyes wouldn't change. The rising flames would swallow everything, burning away all life living on that land. That would spare neither the giant serpent Witchbeast, nor perhaps the masked man spending his days in the forest, nor the hunter who'd pursued Subaru and the others. —Everything, reduced to ash.

"—Ngh"

Suddenly, beside Subaru, who gritted his teeth at the shock, Rem's body wavered. Reflexively catching her slender frame with his hand, the instant he touched her, Rem's body stiffened. And the expression on Rem's face, looking up at Subaru, overflowed with fear and rejection.

"Ah..."

"It's not your fault... I understand that. But—"

"——"

"Please don't touch me."

For a moment biting back the fear that had nearly swallowed her whole, Rem slowly pushed Subaru's hand away. Not shaking it off, not wrenching it away—just pushed it aside. Her words were surely her true feelings. That this wasn't a situation Subaru had deliberately caused—Rem understood that too. But the comfort that brought was minimal. Faced with what had actually, truly happened, that understanding was far too little—

"...Sounds like that child's woken up."

Saying that, Rem averted her eyes from Subaru, averted her eyes from the burning forest, and, as if averting her eyes from something she didn't want to see, turned toward Rui inside the tent.

Subaru couldn't bring himself to call out to Rem's retreating back. Even within himself, he couldn't make sense of what had just happened. Right now, nothing but wrong answers came to mind, no matter what he might say. So he couldn't stop Rem, moving away at a halting, infant-crawling pace. Unable to stop her—

"—Oh?"

Biting his lip, watching Rem's small, rejecting back, Subaru let out a sound at suddenly noticing a small sensation against his own back. Wondering what had happened, he turned around, but there was no object or person there that had touched Subaru's back. Only, at the moment he turned, something flickered at the edge of his vision. As if it had circled around behind him, matching his turn—

"Uu—!!"

Right after, as if a child's tantrum had exploded, Rui inside the tent raised her voice. An Archbishop of Sin loud the moment she woke, but right now, more than the toddler-like Archbishop of Sin, there was a far more pressing matter right in front of him. Though there wasn't much he could actually do about it either way.

"Uwa, ah-ah!!"

"—Ngh, shut up! I've got something serious going on right now! No time to deal with—"

He started to yell at Rui, who was thrashing and wailing—no time for that—but Subaru's brow furrowed. Rem, sitting on the ground, holding the writhing, flailing Rui from behind, had her expression completely change once again. Different from the fear and rejection of a moment ago—pure, astonished bewilderment. Her wide blue eyes were staring at Subaru—no, more precisely, not at Subaru, but at,

"...My back?"

From the subtle angle of her gaze, Subaru worked out what exactly Rem's eyes were fixed on. Following suit, he twisted his neck to peer at his own back. And, belatedly, he realized. —The true identity of the thing that had just circled around to his back a moment ago.

"—A fletching."

That was the true identity of what had flickered across Subaru's vision. And naturally, a fletching came attached to an arrow shaft—and the fact that it was swaying against Subaru's back meant—

"—Ah"

—Meant nothing other than that a fired arrow had struck Subaru's back.

"——"

His head swayed dizzily, and Subaru, unable to remain standing, collapsed backward on the spot. Reflexively his hand grabbed the tent's entrance flap, and the tent tilted with the force of his fall. But with no room to spare for concern over that, Subaru's body crashed sideways to the ground.

"Kyaaaaaaa—!"

Seeing that, Rem let out a shrill scream. Thoughts spinning round and round, the pointless, out-of-place thought—that this was the first time he'd heard Rem's normal scream—flooded his mind and spilled out of his ears.

"Ah, uwah!"

Scrambling on all fours, Rui approached the fallen Subaru. She shook his body roughly, but he had no voice left to scold her, no strength left to resist. What a state to be in—from a single arrow.

"Somebody! Somebody come here! ...It's, it's fine, right!? Because, a wound this shallow, from an arrow like that...!"

Discarding her cane, half-collapsing as she rushed over, Rem desperately called out, staring at the wound on Subaru's back. Ah, Rem really is kind, he thought. Even unable to trust Subaru because of the Miasma, even after an offhand word of his had produced that burning world—still, seeing Subaru collapsed before her eyes, she raised her voice like this to try to help.

In front of this Rem, he didn't want to show weakness. What's a mere arrow wound, why not stand up with room to spare, Natsuki Subaru. Watching period dramas and historical films, he'd always thought—dying or being unable to move from a skinny arrow like that stuck in you meant you lacked guts. Well, if it were a huge arrow punching clean through the chest that'd be a different story, but as Rem said, the arrow lodged in his back hadn't carried much force. In fact, it had struck so softly he'd almost thought it was a pat on the back. So why was it turning out like this—

"—Gehh, bu, uegh"

"—Ngh, don't tell me, poison?"

Right as he vomited up the rising burning sensation, Rem arrived at the same conclusion as Subaru. It wasn't the arrow's force that would kill him. It was the poison coated on the arrow, eating away at him.

His limbs stopped moving, his head wouldn't function, as if he were delirious with a high fever. Something gushed profusely from his eyes, nose, ears, and Subaru's entire body began shaking violently. His ears started ringing painfully loud, and he couldn't hear Rem's voice worrying over him. Couldn't hear Rui's grating wails either. Couldn't hear anything anymore.

Poison, poison, who, why, an arrow, a hunter, the forest, burning, burning and burning, Subaru carelessly, the Witchbeast, Todd, burning, Rem, Rem, Rem—.

His consciousness spiraling into confusion, Subaru let out a groan, blowing bloody foam. And, his bloodshot eyes wide open, trying somehow to see Rem's face, he noticed.

—It was a small, small figure glaring at him from about thirty meters from the tent, a distance that would take less than ten seconds at a run.

"——"

A child. A small child, not so different from Rui. A child with sharp, unpleasant eyes. —No, that wasn't right. It wasn't that her eyes were naturally sharp. She was glaring at Subaru. With eyes clouded by hatred, glaring at Subaru with murderous intent.

Her hair and face smudged with soot, a girl gripping what looked like a shortbow, her eyes clouded with hatred. It had been those fingers, that hand, that will, that had fired the poisoned arrow at Subaru.

"——"

Being hated was only natural. Being wished dead was only natural too.

The outcome Subaru had brought about had driven that girl down a path toward hatred, toward this fate. If so, then this—was this the retribution that had come for Subaru—

"—No! Wait! Please wait. Wait..."

Desperately, a voice sounded near his ear. He wanted to wait for her. Wanted to stop where he stood. Wanted to take her hand, smile at her.

He could do none of it. He could do none of it, and still—

Blowing bloody foam, bub, bub, convulsing, eyes rolling back white, losing control of his bladder, vomiting, spewing out organs melted into a mushy mess, he fell into the darkness.

"Waaait...!"

Unsightly, filthy, a thoughtless fool, falling into the darkness. Falling—
