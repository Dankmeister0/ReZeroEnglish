Arc 4 Chapter 65: "Passion in the Snow"

He was dragged roughly to the front of the tomb and thrown down near the entrance, tumbling to the ground. Spitting out the unpleasant sensation of something sherbet-like—snow, or was it frost?—that had gotten into his mouth, Subaru, his exposed skin mistaking pain for numbness, twisted only his neck to look back.

"That's... pretty rough handling."

"I ain't got the brains to be careful with ya. Be glad it's you gettin' the rough treatment. Or would ya rather it was this girl instead?"

Looking down at Subaru sprawled on his side, Garfiel let out a white breath and ostentatiously showed off Rem in his arms. Whether Garfiel thought of it in terms of a hostage or not was unclear, but it was, without question, leverage to threaten Subaru with.

"Just... don't try anything weird with Rem."

"Long as you're plannin' on meetin' our demands, I'll keep it that way."

Speaking quietly, Subaru pressed his hands against the thinly snow-covered ground and, working his numbed fingers, managed to stand. Right beside him, the Ryuzu duplicate who had carried him here stood gazing vacantly in his direction. As always, she was clad in nothing but shabby rags—a wretched sight. In this cold, it felt far too meager to count as clothing.

"Can't something be done about how these kids are dressed? ...It's hard to watch, they look so cold."

"You know what these girls are, don'tcha? They ain't got the ability to feel cold to begin with. If you're stallin' for time, I got no reason to play along."

"Don't read into it like that. Even I don't think stalling's gonna make things any better."

In the near-blizzard, Subaru heard Garfiel's warning at his back as he turned to face the tomb. A stone ruin floated alone in the white, hazy world. Even before the raging fury of nature, Echidna's tomb remained composed, its eerie maw spread wide, waiting for its challenger. Emilia had to be inside.

"How long's it been since Emilia went in?"

"Since the night before last, so it's about goin' on two days now. Honestly, long as she ain't dead, I don't much care what state she's in."

"I guess that's how it'd look from where you're standing. ...Wasn't there ever an option where you went in and dragged her out yourself?"

"I can't go into the tomb. That's how the Contract works."

There was something suggestive in the way he said it—it summed up Garfiel's entire position. Subaru had no idea how many of the Sanctuary's residents knew, but Garfiel had indeed set foot in the tomb before. He had met Echidna, gained the right of an Apostle of Greed, and thereby qualified to hold command over the Ryuzu duplicates. Why he hid that fact, and why on top of it he was trying to keep the Sanctuary from being freed, Subaru didn't know.

"If I go in and ask Echidna... would I understand, I wonder?"

"Quit mutterin' to yourself. I already told ya—get in there, now. Drag that half-devil out and put a stop to this snowy mess, quick. Otherwise I'm gonna have to do somethin' I don't wanna do."

Garfiel lightly hoisted Rem up in his arms, hitching his cheek into a sharp, angled grin. It was a smile that didn't suit him, but Subaru knew he was someone who could actually carry out that kind of intimidation. Whatever his true feelings, if it meant protecting the Sanctuary, he'd easily put his claws to one or two girls who shared the face of the person he loved.

"Don't do anything to Rem. —That's my condition."

"...Get goin'."

Leaving those words behind in as cold and detached a voice as he could manage amid the freezing wind, Subaru turned his feet toward the inside of the tomb. Behind him, Garfiel watched his back intently. He didn't understand Garfiel's true intentions. And then he remembered something he'd forgotten to tell him.

He'd forgotten to tell Garfiel, her younger brother, about Frederica's death. He could only assume the cold, the anger, had scrambled his head.

Was he, right now, in his right mind? And if he was, how could he possibly be? Petra was dead. Frederica, whom he'd suspected, was innocent. The Sanctuary he'd returned to was in this state. His relationship with Garfiel was at its worst. And the safety of everyone else left behind in the Sanctuary was anyone's guess.

With conditions this bad continuing on and on, how could he possibly stay sane? He mustn't stop thinking. He mustn't give up. He had to look ahead, look up, and pile up whatever he could pile up, in order to grasp the future he needed to grasp. Otherwise, why would Subaru even—

"――――"

Only the intermittent sound of Subaru's footsteps struck the dry floor of the tomb. Unlike outside the tomb, inside there was almost no trace of the raging cold's influence. It wasn't some illusion of having outrun the chill—it was, in fact, simply less affected. With Subaru's entry into the tomb, its function of welcoming the qualified activated, and a faint light lit the once-dark interior of the ruin.

Following the dim glow that beckoned him deeper along the walls, Subaru pressed on through the tomb, his limbs stiff and awkward as though the blood in his body had begun to freeze. And then, past a corridor that felt endless, an open space appeared.

It was the chamber of the First Trial, the Trial that forced one to face the past. And there, in the place he'd finally reached—

"—Subaru?"

The silvery, bell-like voice he'd longed to hear welcomed him softly.

In the darkness, Subaru, hearing his name called, strained his eyes at the figure in front of him. As his eyes slowly adjusted to the dimness of the ruin, and long silver hair and eyes of deep, inviting violet-blue leapt into view, Subaru couldn't hold himself back and called out her name.

"Emilia."

"That's right. That's right, Subaru. ...It's me."

At the sound of those four short syllables he'd voiced, and the fact that he'd gotten an answer to them, Subaru felt as if he might collapse. He knew it was overblown, but it was an emotion he couldn't suppress.

Fatigue, lethargy, a sense of loss. There had been no shortage of feelings battering Subaru, but before Emilia now, his knees buckled under the weight of everything he hadn't let himself acknowledge, having kept himself so tightly wound until this moment.

As Subaru pitched forward, about to fall, an arm shot out and caught him. It was a soft, warm sensation. Looking up, right before him, a pale, beautiful face gazed down at him, and Subaru, forgetting the situation entirely, choked on a breath of surprise. Right now, he was being gently held by Emilia.

"Ah, s-sorry... my legs just... gave out..."

"It's okay. I'm not thinking you did it on purpose, like you were aiming for this or something. And even if you had been, I'd have caught you anyway."

Talking over Subaru's excuse, Emilia cut off his escape route. Since it was, far from an accusation, a gentle bit of support, Subaru let out a deep breath of relief—and immediately noticed something was off about Emilia.

Emilia's manner was no different from usual. Kind, gentle, a little airheaded, brimming with consideration, cute, with a somewhere-childish charm to her—too much the same as always.

This "usual" Emilia was the usual of quiet, peaceful days spent at Roswaal Manor. It was not the usual of an Emilia strung taut with a sense of duty, unable to break through the Trial.

"E-Emilia... while I was gone, did... something..."

—Had something happened that brought about a change in her feelings?

Trying to ask as much, Subaru searched for the right words. But before he could get them out—

"—I was lonely."

"...Huh?"

Unable to catch Emilia's murmur clearly, Subaru knit his brow and asked again. Her silver beauty was right there the moment he lifted his face. Gazing into her eyes, close enough to feel her breath, Subaru braced himself this time not to miss a single word. Meeting Subaru's gaze head-on, Emilia spoke.

"I was lonely, Subaru. —Because you went and left me behind."

"Ah... no, that's... not it. I didn't mean to leave you behind or anything..."

"――――"

"I think I wrote it in the letter too, but... there was something I had to do. That's why I couldn't be with you for a little while. I really do feel bad about making you lonely. And even after putting you through that, I couldn't even properly finish what I had to do, so..."

"Fufu."

Speaking quickly, Subaru piled excuse upon excuse under Emilia's steady gaze. But before he could finish, Emilia broke into laughter, as if she couldn't hold it in any longer. At that reaction, Subaru could only doubt his eyes.

Emilia, laughing—in the middle of a conversation, and one this tense, at that? Where was there anything funny in what he'd said? And before that—Emilia wasn't the kind of girl who'd choose to react that way.

"You don't have to make excuses so desperately, I'm not mad, y'know. Honestly, Subaru, going all pale like that... fufu."

"E-Emilia...?"

"I told you, it's fine, Subaru. You left me a proper letter, and you wrote so much, so much, so-o much of it just for me. I was lonely, and there were times I wanted to cry, but... I read that letter over and over again."

Saying something so touching, Emilia's smile deepened. Her lovely smile was enough to bewitch, her sweet whisper enough to seize hold of Subaru's chest. Just hearing that she had treasured the letter he'd left behind, that it had been like a support for her heart, was enough to make Subaru's own heart start to swim in feverish passion.

But damming up his own consciousness as it threatened to be swept away by that fervor, Subaru couldn't help but feel a slow, simmering sense of foreboding at Emilia's altered state. Something was wrong somewhere. Something was off. The unease he'd been feeling since earlier hadn't been resolved even once, and it had carried him all the way here.

What was wrong? Where was the wrongness? Emilia was right here, so dear to him— and, dear as she was, she was responding to him just as he'd wished.

"Emilia... what happened with the Trial?"

"The Trial..."

"That's right, the Trial. That's why you went in here, isn't it? I'm sorry for making you go through something so hard alone. I want to apologize for that too, and I want to know what happened. Even if it didn't go well, I won't think anything less of you for it, but the fact that you're here now like this means..."

"It didn't. It didn't go well, you know? I still haven't overcome the First Trial—the past. I'm sorry, when you were hoping for me and worrying about me and everything."

"Ah..."

At Emilia's answer, a hoarse sound slipped from Subaru's throat, and he regretted it at once. That sound might have sounded like disappointment to Emilia. If so, Subaru had betrayed his own words—having just told her he wouldn't mind—only a moment later. Subaru was seized with that anxious urgency, but then, suddenly, a smooth sensation touched his head. Emilia had slipped her fingers into Subaru's short black hair and was stroking his head with her palm.

Not understanding the true meaning behind the action, Subaru blinked in bewilderment. At his surprised expression, Emilia smiled brightly, her cheeks coloring red, and said,

"You know, sometimes you want to touch my hair, don't you, Subaru? So I thought that sometimes I'd like to try doing this to you too. Fufu, you're just full of openings, Subaru."

"Emi... lia...?"

"I kept thinking, what would I do if you just disappeared and left me behind like that... I thought about it so much, so-o much, so much, over and over. It felt so, so scary. That's why, when you came just now, I was so happy."

Even though she had just finished telling him she'd failed the Trial, there was nothing reflected in Emilia's eyes but Subaru. Eyes burning with heat, eyes glistening with tears, gazed at him. How long had Subaru longed for the day he would be reflected there like this? How much had Subaru yearned to have his name called with such warmth, to be gazed at by eyes glistening with passion? Everything, until now, this moment—all of it had been for the sake of tasting this fervor. And so—

"Subaru. Let's stay together forever, okay? Stay with me? As long as you're here, I won't need anything else, not anything at all—"

Subaru had never imagined that the day Emilia would profess such blind love for him would come to feel this terrifying.

※※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※

Still holding Subaru in her arms, Emilia went on stringing together words heavy with fervor.

"At first, when I heard you were gone, it hurt so much. I got scared. Because I hadn't been doing anything right at all... and I thought, maybe you'd gotten fed up with me. Once I thought that, I got so scared, so scared, and I couldn't stop my body from shaking..."

"――――"

"But then I noticed there was a letter, and I could tell it was your handwriting, and right away the fear went away. You're amazing, Subaru. Even though I was that scared, you blew all those feelings away right away... yeah, I always feel like you're the one saving me."

"――――"

"The letter itself made me happy too. You wrote so much, so much, so I wouldn't worry, didn't you? It must have taken so-o long. The fact that you spent that time for my sake, that you must have kept thinking about me the whole time you were writing it—that made me happy too."

"――――"

"In the letter too, you told me 'I love you' so many times, didn't you? When you said it in the dragon carriage, I was so happy I cried, but... even reading the letter, I still almost cried again. I realized just how big a thing I'd been given... that's what I thought. That's what I noticed."

"――――"

"So when I saw you come back to me, Subaru, I just couldn't hold it in anymore. Deep in my chest, in the deepest place, a small version of me is calling your name. And then, I just couldn't help wanting to reach out like this, and touch you, so badly..."

"――――"

"Hey, Subaru. I'm sorry, for everything up until now. I've been so awful to you this whole time, haven't I. I made you keep holding back, even though you felt this way about me. I understand now, a little, just how cruel that was."

"――――"

"Carrying feelings like that, and still holding back—that must be so hard. I was so selfish, in front of a Subaru who was holding back like that. I was supposed to want to think about you, to understand you... but I didn't understand you at all."

"――――"

"But it's different now. I think about you all the time, Subaru. I feel it all the time. The way you... said you loved me, the way you felt about me... now I think I want to feel that way about you too, maybe."

"――――"

"No, wait, I'm sorry. That was cowardly of me, wasn't it. Even when it was scary, even when you didn't know what I was thinking, you told me properly, Subaru."

"――――"

"So I'll tell you properly too. —I will tell you."

"――――"

"Hey, Subaru. I... love you. I love you so much. I think about you, only about you, and I think I want to be with you forever."

"――――"

"If you feel the same way about me, Subaru... I'd be happy... or something."

"――――"

"Ehehe. Yeah, yeah... I love you. Subaru... I love you so much."
