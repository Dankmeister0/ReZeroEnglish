Arc 9 Chapter 32: "A Man Named Clind"

—While Aldebaran's group was relentlessly pursued by formidable foes without rest, there were two other places in the Kingdom undergoing fierce battles every bit as intense.

One was the battle of the "Sword Saint," striving to prevent the destruction of the world from the "Witch of Envy," who manifested a portion of herself from her sealed shrine and threatened to swallow the Augria Sand Dunes. Both the "Witch of Envy" and the "Sword Saint" possessed power capable of ending any fight in an instant against anyone else, but precisely because they faced each other, the battle remained a stalemate, its conclusion continually postponed. Literally, the clash would likely continue for seven days and seven nights.—At the cost of the world's collapse.

And the other was a struggle that, unlike those of Aldebaran's party or the "Sword Saint," involved no physical violence, yet possessed ample potential to deeply influence the outcome of the other two battles. Namely, the desperate struggle of those pursuing the "Trailing Star," with Petra Leyte as the key.

—Petra did not know nearly enough about the man named Clind.

He was their ally. Of that, there was no doubt. Annerose Miload, whom he served as master, was an intelligent young girl who paid no mind to her youth or the handicap of being related to Roswaal; she was one of the Emilia Camp's powerful supporters, and even from Petra's perspective, undeniably a person captivated by Emilia. Clind, who devotedly supported Annerose and enjoyed total trust as an all-powerful steward, had also looked after Petra when she first started working as a maid, teaching her etiquette and manners.

And it wasn't just Petra; almost everyone connected to House Mathers, including Frederica and Ram, had received Clind's guidance and learned the ABCs of domestic service from him. As such, Clind could be considered the progenitor of all servants tied to House Mathers, and by rights, Petra ought to treat him as a teacher or a mentor. However, the one who corrected that perception of Petra's was none other than the man himself.

"I have no intention of taking on such a grand role as guiding or teaching. Misunderstanding. I am merely passing down what I have heard from my predecessors. It is simply that I happen to have remained in this position for a long time, giving me more opportunities. Refusal."

One could describe Clind's way of thinking as a sign of humility and grace. Yet Petra somehow sensed that this was the genuine, unvarnished truth for Clind, who rarely showed any emotional fluctuation on his face.

"When all is said and done, that man simply undervalues himself. Because his self-assessment is so low, he claims his own work isn't anything worthy of praise either... He's mocking us!"

Such was the review from Frederica, who was uniquely harsh toward Clind alone. But with regard to this opinion from Frederica—whom Petra believed to be absolutely correct and more wonderful than anyone in the world—Petra only partially agreed, holding a slightly different view.

To be sure, at first glance, Clind's attitude seemed like that of a person with low self-esteem. On the other hand, Clind almost never displayed hesitation or doubt when conveying something to his juniors or younger people through his words and actions. If he were truly a person with low self-esteem, he would hesitate and waver much more. In Clind's way of being, there was no such mundane hesitation.—Just like Petra.

"If I set my mind to it, I can do anything."

If most people heard that, they might snort and scoff at it as arrogance, but Petra didn't care about "most people"; she cared about the people close to her. And she believed that those close people believed in her confidence and potential.

Petra was aware that she was a bundle of potential, with the talent to make it bloom. She was aware that she wasn't just cute, but possessed the talent required to walk any path she desired, along with the grit to keep striving while trusting in that talent—a being blessed by heaven with two, even three gifts.

Speaking as someone possessing such blessed talent, Clind's way of being was not that of a person with low self-esteem, but rather on the high side. Clind knew he possessed exceptional abilities. On top of that, he simply did not consider it something to be proud of. That was why, on the surface, he appeared humble and modest.

"But I really, really hate that."

—Petra did not know nearly enough about the man named Clind.

However, despite not knowing much about him, Petra honestly didn't like Clind. This wasn't unrelated to how Frederica—who was polite and motherly-gentle toward everyone—showed a special attitude and expression only around Clind, or the fact that Petra had a hunch about what kind of feeling that stemmed from. Naturally, it was also partly because Clind had a long acquaintance with Roswaal and seemed quite close to him. Yet the biggest reason stemmed from Clind himself, apart from his relationships with Frederica or Roswaal.

—Above all else, his posture as someone who had already "retired from the game" directly contradicted Petra's core principles.

"—We're back, so lend us your strength! I know you can hear me, Clind-niisama!"

Passing through the entrance gate of Mirula, the town closest to the sand dunes, in their dragon carriage, Petra raised her high-pitched voice, calling out for the familiar man. Thanks to Patrasche, who had dashed fiercely across the sand sea for a day and a night from the Pleiades Watchtower, there was no doubt that Petra and the others had taken the shortest route in the shortest possible time. Even so, they were far behind Al, who had borrowed the wings of the Divine Dragon. To catch up to his distant back, Clind's power was indispensable.

"Clind-niisama!"

Pulling the reins to bring the carriage to a stop, Petra raised her voice again in the center square of the town. In response to Petra's earnest plea, the voice of the man she sought did not answer. Yet, believing beyond doubt that her voice must have reached him, Petra looked around.

"What's going on? There's no one in town?"

Taking in the same view as Petra, Imaginary Subaru spoke up. Just as Imaginary Subaru said, despite the sun still being high in the sky, not a soul could be seen in the square.—No, not just the square. There was no sign of human presence in the entire town. Although Mirula had the strong impression of a deserted post town, there should still have been at least a hundred or two hundred residents living there. For not a single person to be seen was strange.

"...Could it beee that the helmet guy wiped everyone out or something?"

"That's terrifyingly dark! He's not like you or Elsa, you know!"

"Meili-chan, don't say scary things. —And Subaru, Meili-chan doesn't do that kind of thing anymore. It's the fruit of her education."

The first half was directed at Meili, and the second half at Imaginary Subaru. To be sure, it would be a lie to say the worst-case scenario Meili mentioned hadn't crossed her mind for a second, but that possibility could be ruled out by the fact that the deserted town showed no signs of being ransacked. If Al had gone on a rampage together with Volcanica, the town would have been far more destroyed, transformed into an irrecoverable disaster area. The state of this town felt less like such a violent disaster and more like—

"It looks like they fled in the night." "Feels like a night runner situation."

Petra and Imaginary Subaru's thoughts unexpectedly aligned. Since Imaginary Subaru was an inhabitant of Petra's head, it was a natural result, but as a maiden in love, having her thoughts match her beloved's made her a little happy. Besides, she had another reason to feel positive.

"—While it is slightly misleading, your assessment is largely correct. Keen insight."

With those words, a man stepped forward into the town square devoid of residents. In the middle of the desolate, dreary town landscape, he was a figure maintaining an uncommonly refined air. His mysterious presence had an unassertive strength, like a single drop of deep blue paint fallen onto a sheet of pure white drawing paper. Even if this weren't a desolate post town, but the bustling crowds of the Royal Capital or a packed venue blasting off summer fireworks with loud booms, his natural aura gave the impression that one would never overlook his existence.

"Hold on. I'm currently now-loading Petra's knowledge at top speed."

"—Clind-niisama."

"Ah, you beat me to it!"

While Imaginary Subaru—who was meant to represent Subaru before he had met Clind—frustratingly lamented being intercepted, Petra lightly hopped down from the driver's seat and looked straight at the opponent. The all-around steward, and the person holding the key to turning this situation around—Clind.

"—. Judging by the state of Mirula and the turbulence of that sand sea, it seems cancelling my return was the right decision. Good fortune."

Adjusting the monocle over his left eye with his hand, Clind narrowed his eyes at the empty town, and then at what lay behind Petra and her companions—the murky sky and its cause. And then—

"Let us talk, Petra. It appears we require it. Urgency."

△▼△▼△▼

"—I see. Deep deliberation."

Having finished listening to a rough explanation, Clind placed his fingers against his slender chin and closed one eye. The contemplation of someone possessed of sharp, striking beauty carried a majesty like a painting in itself, but unfortunately, Petra had no leisure to be entranced by it, nor was her taste oriented in that direction. If it was simply a matter of good looks, looking in a mirror or looking at Emilia was the quickest way.

"What is the condition of Garfiel and the one named Ezzo? Confirmation."

"I don't think either Big Brother Fang or Teacher-san are in danger of dying. Though it is a bit concerning that Big Brother Fang, who's supposed to be super sturdy, isn't waking up easily..."

"Yeah. I think Garf-san probably shielded Ezzo-san."

Taking over the second half of Meili's response, Petra thought of the two resting inside the dragon carriage. That Garfiel, whose selling point was his durability, had suffered heavy damage just like Ezzo—a dwarf and a mage—was likely the result of Garfiel taking the front line to shield Ezzo when they were hit by the Divine Dragon's attack. Without that self-sacrifice, Ezzo might not have survived.

"So Garfiel took a direct hit from a Dragon, huh? ...I still haven't fully wrapped my head around the fact that he's a guy who'd go that far, or that he's a fourteen-year-old eighth-grader."

"Just so you know, Garf-san is fifteen now. By the way, I'm almost fourteen too."

"Time flies, man. No wonder I thought Petra had gone from a pretty girl to a beautiful woman."

"――――"

Once again, she needed to keep in mind that this was merely the Imaginary Subaru inside her head, not a statement from the real Subaru. Though, even if it were the real Subaru, he was totally the type to say this much, and she had heard him say similar things before. Even knowing it was his genuine feeling and not flattery, the fact that he could say such things so casually to someone other than Emilia was, well, troubling in many ways. At any rate—

"I'm worried about Garf-san and Teacher-san, but my biggest worry is about Al-san. Clind-niisama, you felt the same way, which is why you waited for us, right?"

"To say we feel the same way would be somewhat inaccurate. The reason I remained in this land was due to a slight sense of discomfort when I saw Petra and the others off. Suspicion."

"A slight... sense of discomfort?"

"Yes. In truth, I mentioned it to Subaru-sama and Beatrice-sama, the two of them. Retrospection."

At this story Petra and Meili hadn't known, Clind cast his eyes down languidly. Indeed, since the two he had informed had both fallen into Al's hands, Clind's self-reproach must be considerable. At that awkwardness, Imaginary Subaru, who didn't know Clind, seemed somewhat sympathetic—

"No, the more I hear, the more it sounds like Beatrice and I are at fault. I mean, Clind-san did proper reporting, contacting, and consulting, right? And we didn't pass it along, right? So this outcome is only natural..."

"There's no way Subaru and Beatrice-chan are at fault. Subaru, keep quiet."

"My own willpower isn't allowed to interfere with my own reflection statement!"

Subaru lamented as if struck by sheer unreasonableness, but Petra would not forgive anyone who carelessly badmouthed Subaru—even if it was Subaru himself. Furthermore, the way he just said it dragged Beatrice into it too. Beatrice was cute and valiant, so she was blameless. She would not hesitate for a second to engage in a level of blatant favoritism that would make anyone shudder if they heard it. Such was the rule in the court of Petra Leyte, and such was the ruling of Presiding Judge Petra.

"Sooo? What was the thing that bothered you, Steward-san?"

"Even at that time, I could not offer a specific point. Incompetence. However, thinking back now that the situation has moved... there was room for doubt in the setup of the situation. Concern."

Prompted by Meili, Clind kept his eyes lowered as he put his thoughts into words. The "setup of the situation" that had troubled Clind likely referred to the time when he had seen Petra and the others off at Mirula.

"It was not unnatural enough to cause alarm. Listening to the explanation, the roster was plausible. --However, it also felt like a combination that could only be established by painstakingly forcing through two or three logical steps. Artifice."

"Artifice..."

At Clind's explanation, Petra chewed on the word in her mouth. To be honest, the fact that Clind was couching his words to avoid stating outright—the misstep in selecting the members to accompany them to the Pleiades Watchtower—had constantly existed in Petra's mind as a self-punishing logic tormenting her. If it had been Emilia, Ram, or Otto who went with Subaru and the others instead of Petra, wouldn't things have turned out differently? For that matter, if Frederica had gone in Petra's place as originally planned, they might have prevented Al's outrageous deed before it happened.

Meili was indispensable for crossing the Augria Sand Dunes, and Garfiel was the most reliable combat strength in the camp for protection; those two were essential members of the party. It was only Petra. Only Petra was a replaceable member.

"――――"

Should she have spoken up more? To delay going to the tower, or to act with everyone together? Of course, it was a fact that their return from Vollachia and the events that transpired in that country needed to be reported urgently, and providing emotional care for Schult, who had lost his pillar, could not be omitted. It was precisely because such sound judgments were deemed necessary that the current lineup had come to be.

Yet Petra lacked confidence that her own subjective, selfish wish hadn't seeped in. The desire not to be separated for even a moment from Subaru, whom she had been apart from for so long, and her selfish ambition to be of use to him. In a place where neither Emilia nor Rem was present, hadn't Petra wished that her own strength and presence could support Subaru? That was why she hadn't even possessed the leeway to notice the discomfort Clind had caught on to—

"--Petra, no matter how you look at it, you're overthinking things."

Onto Petra's hand, which was tightly clutching the hem of her skirt, Imaginary Subaru's intangible hand overlapped, and a gentle voice whispered in her ear. Yet Petra couldn't be saved by Subaru's words so easily.

"I mean, it might sound weird coming from me, but sure, it's totally possible you had a tiny bit of ulterior motive, like wanting to be with me. But that's not a bad thing. And even if you had a teeny bit of that feeling, it was only one percent. The remaining ninety-nine percent was your kindness—I know that for a fact."

"I hate this... Even that is just me making the Subaru in my head say it..."

"If you bring that up, I won't be able to say anything at all! That's a forbidden card!"

Seeing Imaginary Subaru protest with a slightly red face—even though saying out loud that she wanted to be with him must have been embarrassing for him to say—Petra let out a long breath. She truly hated how easily she was moved by his thoughtfulness. It probably wasn't that Petra was an easy girl, but rather that Subaru's words possessed far too much power.

"Besides, I'm betting there's a real possibility that the very fact you're suffering isn't your fault at all."

"Does that... have something to do with Al-san being too strong?"

"Well, I've never seen Al fight with a sword in person, so I might be completely off base... but I do know about Reinhard."

Even if Al's true strength was an unknown quantity, she knew the strength of this world's peak—Reinhard. Both Garfiel and Ezzo were formidable experts far beyond Petra's reach. Having defeated those two, it would be normal to assume Al was even stronger. However, no matter how strong Al might be, he could not match Reinhard.

"When I first saw him back at the Loot House, I didn't really get the common sense of this world, but... even by this world's standards, that guy is a total monster."

"Yeah. But Al-san managed to do something about Reinhard-san too. Flam-chan definitely used her Divine Protection to call Reinhard-san..."

"At the very least, he wasn't instantly killed by Reinhard. We can agree that even with a helper character like the Divine Dragon, that's not something you can easily pull off, right?"

"Does that have something to do with our party combination?"

"Could be. I feel like there are too many things that can't be explained just by him being strong."

At the point of doubt raised by Imaginary Subaru, Petra carefully pondered as well. Indeed, the moment one faced Reinhard, strength of any degree lost its meaning. Through sheer superiority of force alone, one could not evade Reinhard to accomplish their objective. In other words, the terror of Al lay in a reason other than raw strength.

"Honestly, things have been going way too conveniently well for Al up to this point."

"But when someone is always lucky, there's usually a reason beyond luck."

"Bingo."

Though she couldn't grasp the core of it, Petra considered that point worth keeping in mind. By searching for other possibilities like this, her aimless guilt eased somewhat as well. She thought that perhaps this had been Subaru's true intention all along.

"Petra-chan? Are you okay? You were spacing out..."

"—. Sorry, Meili-chan, I'm fine. And, Clind-niisama."

"What is it? Confirmation."

"I don't think the lingering doubt Clind-niisama felt was wrong. We still don't know the reason why, but without knowing what that catch is, no one can stop Al-san."

"――――"

"It's just us. Only we can stop Al-san. --We have to stop him, and then bring Subaru and Beatrice-chan back."

Placing her hand on her own chest, Petra strongly appealed to Clind. Holding it in until the very end, uttering that final breath required a great deal of courage from Petra as well.

"――――"

Imaginary Subaru, arms crossed, stared at Petra's profile with a complex expression. Without turning her gaze toward him, Petra steadfastly believed in the conclusion inside her. Believed in it to the end.

--Petra had read a Book of the Dead. In the worst-case scenario, Al might have killed Subaru and Beatrice, erasing them from this world. Having a Book of the Dead for Natsuki Subaru normally meant concluding as much.

"But my Subaru isn't normal."

She had impulsively said "my," but it was a fact. The part about him not being normal was a fact. Natsuki Subaru possessed a power called Return by Death—an outrageously painful, agonizing force that only someone truly kind could handle properly. Petra surmised that Subaru's past experience of dying might have been that single book she had seen. Because--

"After the book I saw, did Subaru really manage to come all this way without dying even once?"

"That's like negative trust right there. But based on what Petra knows... with all the Sin Archbishops taking the stage at once, the adventure at the Pleiades Watchtower, the literal hell of the Vollachian Empire... even to myself, it's pretty hard to believe I could've cleared all that with a flawless run."

"...Yeah."

The brilliant achievements Subaru had built up until now—things she had thought were results achieved on top of already terrible ordeals—when she realized they were an assortment of hells far beyond what she imagined, far from being disappointed in Subaru, her heart was broken by his sheer devotion. And at the same time, she realized.—The depth of Subaru's emotional wound from letting Priscilla die.

Subaru was deeply, deeply hurt—deeper than anyone could imagine. That was precisely why he blamed himself, taking the burden onto his own shoulders, and resolved to do anything for Al's sake. And Al had betrayed all of Subaru's feelings, as well as his incomparable resolve to "save everyone even if I die." If he had killed Subaru and Beatrice on top of that, even if Petra tore Al's body, heart, and soul to shreds, she wouldn't be able to achieve true vengeance.

"Petra-chan, are Big Bro and Beatrice-chan..."

"They're alive. Absolutely."

"...I suppose so. Both of them, especially Big Bro, are so stubborn, aren't they?"

Muttering in a quiet voice, Meili gently rested her head on Petra's shoulder. Even Meili, who wasn't straightforward, genuinely worried for Subaru and Beatrice. Making those around him feel that way was the result of the footprints Subaru had left behind.

"Between Beatrice and Meili, I really wonder what the 'me' I don't know got up to."

Imaginary Subaru muttered in amazement as if it were someone else's business, despite referring to himself. Loosening her lips at his demeanor, Petra turned back to face Clind once again. She had already conveyed her will and purpose. All that remained was to await his reply. Meeting Petra's gaze dead-on, Clind quietly let a breath slip through his thin lips, and--

"'Only you can do it,' is it? High-spirited words. However, that spirit is something worthy of note. Admiration."

"――――"

"To be frank, the moment the 'Witch' began moving at the edge of the world, bringing about a situation only the 'Sword Saint' could stop, I had half-resigned myself to it being irrecoverable. Reflection. However, if you are to kindle such spirit--"

"Wait, Clind-niisama."

Petra cut off Clind mid-speech as he began to speak. It wasn't because she thought his speech would drag on, nor out of fear of going off on a tangent.—If phrased in Clind's manner, it was a sort of indescribable point of discomfort. But precisely toward him, who regretted having overlooked that catch before, Petra could not overlook this point of discomfort now. What caught Petra's attention was Clind's phrasing.

"I said we. And that we includes you, Clind-niisama. Please don't say 'you' like it's someone else's business."

"That is... Hesitation."

"Even at a time like this, I'm going to say it. Clind-niisama, for a long time now, you've been with us with a look on your face like you're outside the circle, right? I hated that."

If it were standing back a step as an adult, interacting with the Emilia Camp as someone required in that position, she could still understand. But Clind was different. It was the same toward everyone. Towards Annerose, whom Clind adored as master; towards Roswaal, with whom Clind interacted like a bad friend; towards Frederica, who couldn't lock Clind out of her life—it was the same.

"Living like that isn't going to be allowed in Emilia-neesama's ideal kingdom."

"――――"

"There's no such thing as someone else's business. If you're alive, meeting people, talking with them, looking at their faces and spending time together, the good and bad things born there apply to you whether you like it or not. You can't cheat by taking just a little bit or picking only the good parts—you're not allowed to!"

"Petra-chan..."

"If you act like that, one day everything important to you will turn its back on you!"

Sensing Meili touching her sleeve, Petra declared fiercely, almost losing her breath. Partway through, she became unsure if she was expressing what she wanted to say, or whether her message was conveyed properly. It was surely the influence of the Subaru from the Book of the Dead.

"Hey now, isn't that also part of the 'cheating' you just talked about?"

Imaginary Subaru said with lowered brows, but she consciously ignored him for now. What was far more important was Clind's reaction--

"'Everything important... turning its back on you,' is it...? Acute pain."

As Clind muttered that, Petra held her breath slightly. Partly because the painfully poignant emotion embedded in Clind's whispering voice struck her heart sharply. But even more than that, it was Clind's facial expression that compelled her.

"――――"

Clind rarely showed his emotions on his face. Joy, anger, sadness—Petra couldn't recall ever seeing him grieve, but in any case, expressing everything with a expressionless face was Clind's way. Yet in Clind's expression, there was a change.—It was sorrow.

A poignant voice and a sorrowful expression. When those two overlapped, people would describe it as fleeting. At this moment, Clind bore a fleeting, poignant face reminiscent of nostalgia for the past.

"It has already happened, you see. Nostalgia."

"Clind... niisama."

"I have already had everything turn its back on me once, Petra. Self-derision."

Hearing those words, Petra remembered her own statement from a moment ago and felt embarrassed. She had spoken grandly as if she knew everything, talking about emotions she had only just grazed. Yet Clind already knew that feeling, and had been carrying himself with that awareness as he lived in the present. Even feeling that shame, however, Petra still wanted to say it.

"Even so...!"

"—Yes, even so. Admiration."

"Huh?"

"I once let go of everything. Even if becoming empty as a result is a fact, along the path I walked afterward, there were things I gained and accumulated as well. Reflection. --Petra, my relationship with you and the others was one such thing. Revelation."

Saying so, Clind closed his eyes and looked up at the sky. Clouds were torn apart in patches across a sky that seemed to be barely holding back the end of the world on the other side—what did he see up there?

"Why did I remain in this place based solely on a faint sense of discomfort, without being ordered to do so? It was likely because I, too, found it intolerable to let things pass by like this. Resolution."

"...Um, Clind-niisama, does that mean..."

"Does that mean 'You were right, Petra-chan, I'm sorryyy'?"

"Meili-chan!" "Whoa, Meili-paisen, laying it right out there!"

Beside the flustered Petra, she was surprised by Meili stating it so bluntly. Beside her wide-eyed face, Imaginary Subaru's impressed reaction calling Meili "Paisen" struck her as classic Subaru, even without memories of him saying it. Leaving Petra's confusion aside, Clind let out a small laugh, "Heh," and--

"I apologize for lacking a sense of ownership and causing you anxiety. Deep apology. On top of that, Petra, I express my gratitude to you. Great favor. --I, too, wish to cooperate proactively."

"Clind-niisama...!"

As Clind stated this and bowed respectfully, Petra's eyes sparkled. However, as if pouring cold water on Petra's excitement, Meili continued with an "I mean,"

"It's not like Steward-san was planning to get in our way or say he wouldn't cooperate, righttt? Isn't Petra-chan just overthinking it?"

"Th-That might be true, but...!"

"No, that is incorrect, Meili. Correction. Possessing a sense of ownership means that I will also take the initiative to offer solutions to resolve the situation. It is vastly different from being a bystander. A different person entirely."

"'A different person entirely' is way too exaggerated!"

"But that means we can expect that much from you, right? In that case! With Clind-niisama's mysterious power, take us to everyone..."

Leaning forward eagerly, Petra was fired up to make full use of Clind's power. In truth, what Petra relied on most in this situation was Clind's power of "teleportation." His teleportation, which reduced the distance to a destination to zero in an instant, would be an indispensable asset to chase after Al, who had headed off somewhere accompanied by the Divine Dragon.

Although Al's destination was unknown, he was accompanied by the incredibly conspicuous Divine Dragon. With the cooperation of Emilia, Ram, and—honestly, she disliked him, but—Roswaal, identifying their destination and catching up should be entirely possible. With her breath huffing with enthusiasm, Petra geared herself up, but--

"Regrettably, my movement technique is not something that can be used without limit. Rather, in terms of restrictions, it is quite an inflexible power. Regret."

"Clind-niisama, you liar! Traitor!" "To build up our hopes and pull that! You total premature-joy-giver Clind-niisama!"

"Y-You're saying too much, Petra-chan. I don't think Steward-san meant any harm..."

The beam of light she had glimpsed suddenly vanished behind clouds, driving both Petra and Imaginary Subaru to anger. Only half of that fury reached Clind and Meili, but being brought up only to be let down was a fact. In the first place, it was a power whose mechanics were unknown, but if the "teleportation" she had counted on was unavailable, it was reduced to a story where a handsome guy just positively tagged along.

"To be precise, that is not 'teleportation,' but the 'compression' of space and time. Explanation. It 'compresses' the distance meant to be traveled and the time required. However, because it could significantly affect the state of the world, it requires the oversight-blindness of Od Lagna. Expediency."

"Od Lagna? What's that supposed to be?"

"The origin of mana and such. In Subaru's knowledge, it's something like an unconscious god... probably."

Explaining to Imaginary Subaru, who tilted his head at the unexpected term Clind uttered, Petra herself wasn't entirely certain of her own understanding. Having studied under Roswaal to learn magic, Petra had received a standard lecture regarding Od Lagna, but whether she truly understood it was questionable. Even Roswaal had said it was hard to claim one completely understood Od Lagna.

However, Od Lagna was an extraordinarily massive lump of power—in a sense, something in a class of its own, distinct even from the peak of powerful beings like the "Sword Saint," the "Divine Dragon," or the "Witch of Envy."

"What happens if you can't trick this Od Lagna thing?"

"It is unknown. The very fact that it is unknown is terrifying. Trembling."

Responding to Meili's question, Clind slowly shook his head. In his answer, she felt no intent to deceive or evade. In other words, Clind, who seemed to know everything, shared the exact same view as Roswaal. But if so--

"Clind-niisama, you've done that several times before, right? Based on what you just said, that means you were able to trick Od Lagna... What do we need to do?"

"Petra..."

"Because that method exists, you were able to use your power, Clind-niisama. What do we need? For example... using magic requires mana. Subjugating spirits requires a contract. Then, to get Od Lagna to do something, does it require a price?"

"—. You truly never cease to amaze me, child. Hats off."

Clind's reaction corroborated that Petra's speculation was correct. Namely, by offering a price to Od Lagna, one receives permission to use Clind's extraordinary power. Or according to Clind's wording, one earns its blind eye.

"Bribes or kickbacks, huh? ...Super worldly for a god."

As Imaginary Subaru grumbled with a grimace, Petra was in complete agreement. On the other hand, she also felt that a god demanding a price was more trustworthy than a god granting charity for nothing. Above all, Petra knew to death that the world was not built on easy difficulty where one could get what they desired without making sacrifices. Therefore--

"Tell me what is needed, Clind-niisama. No matter what it is, I will definitely prepare it. Because it's for Subaru, Beatrice-chan, and everyone."

"My, my, Petra-chan, aren't you strange? Just a moment ago you said it yourself—not 'I', but 'we'."

"—. Sorry, sorry. But thank you."

Petra turned a smile toward Meili, who had hugged her arm as she hardened her resolve. Thinking back, she had never imagined she could form such a relationship with Meili. When Meili had been imprisoned in the mansion's dungeon, Petra's frequent visits were driven by calculated intentions: hoping Meili would be swayed by affection and cease to be a dangerous existence. She had never imagined Meili nestling close to her like this when she resolved something alone.

And all of this was something Petra had received from him.

"—Possibility. Answer."

Before Petra and Meili, Clind paused for a beat and said those words. What was there was not hesitation or vacillation, nor the doubt or anguish of revealing this to young girls—nothing negative like that. Clind accepted the situation as his own concern, acknowledging Petra and the others facing the same problem as true companions. With that, he continued.

"The impact and possibilities that would be brought upon the world and Od Lagna—that is the price. That is the condition for the use of the Authority of the Witch Factor of Melancholy that I possess. --Confession."
