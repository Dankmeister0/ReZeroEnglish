Arc 4 Chapter 98: "The Bed Where the Warmth Faded"

Close your eyes, and even now that sight comes back vivid and clear.

White. Everywhere, an unbroken world of silver-white. The young Emilia, just waking, breathed out a white breath and realized she was standing in the middle of a snowscape. Why? her heart asked. But there was no answer. She had no memory of before or after; her awareness was thin, scattered.

She pushed herself up from where she'd fallen and ran to the window to look at the scene outside. The familiar green forest settlement was now buried entirely beneath a white ending, slowly, steadily freezing over even as she watched.

To Emilia, seeing snow for the first time, it wasn't even clear yet that this was the phenomenon called "snow." Only instinct was searing its cold, its cruelty, vividly into the small girl.

Not understanding anything, Emilia's throat tightened, and with her face twisted she broke into a run. The moment she burst out of the building — an old dwelling carved from a great tree — from the old house, into the outside world, cold wind that cut like a blade and cold that stabbed the soles of her feet like knives assaulted the girl all at once.

Her feet were caught by the snow almost immediately, and she fell face-first to the ground. A cold sensation. Crystals of snow like white powder. Seeing up close for the first time the white, piling grains of ice, fear was born inside Emilia. Something so beautiful it made her tremble, yet so cold it terrified her.

Her simple clothing — little more than a thin cloth wrapped around her body — could not shut out the chill. The shaking that came from deep in her core was born of both cold and fear. With the snow stealing her body heat and threatening to steal her heart along with those white crystals, Emilia, sobbing, brushed the snow clinging to her whole body away and ran again.

Silently, ceaselessly, the powder snow kept falling from the sky. Gasping, snow crept in past her raised chin and stuck to her throat; tears traced down the cheek of her coughing, running face.

She didn't know why she was crying now. She was just afraid. Terrified. Why was she alone? Where had everyone gone? Where had the people who were always kind to her, who always smiled at her, who always reached out a hand to her — where had they all gone?

As she tried to bring their faces to mind, Emilia felt a stagnation rise in her thoughts. The faces of everyone in the settlement, rising in her mind — those faces, those smiles — were being painted over by a black shadow, trying to steal their very existence out of Emilia's head.

"—————"

Shaking her head wildly, Emilia kept running, tears streaming. She mustn't think. If she thought of everyone, she would be swallowed by the black shadow. Everyone precious to her would vanish from inside her own head. But if she didn't think of them, she would be alone. Cold, in a white world where she could see nothing, alone — for the young Emilia, that was an unbearable terror.

Young Emilia was powerless, ignorant, capable of nothing but struggling within the white ending. And still the falling snow, as if mocking her struggle, drew her in, dropping her small body further into the pure white ending.

No one is here. I can't meet anyone. And I can't even 『————』 to everyone anymore.

"—Aaah!"

Losing all sense of where to go in the white world, her numbed hands and feet stopped moving, and Emilia, wailing like an infant, sank down where she stood. From the knees down she sank into soft snow, and though it should have been cold, she could no longer feel the cold. Her skin, once said to be as white as snow, had been burned bright red and cold by that very "snow."

Just like that, as if fleeing from everything, Emilia buried her head in her arms and sank into the snow. The snow kept falling without mercy, piling onto the small girl's body, and Emilia's tiny form disappeared into the white—

"—Emilia!!"

A high voice, sharp enough to tear through the silence, called Emilia back from the sleep she was falling into, the sleep from which she would not wake. She forced open eyelids framed by long lashes and jerked upright. In the same instant, her light body was pulled forcibly up out of the snow.

"—————ah"

"It's all right, Emilia. It's all right, it's all right. Thank goodness... I found you...!"

She'd meant to cry out, but her throat, frozen to its limit, only let out a thin, ragged breath. But the other understood Emilia's state even so, and held the small girl close, her voice trembling with genuine relief at her safety. Pressing her face into that short silver hair, sniffling with a red nose, Emilia expressed her feelings with her whole body — to show, through action, that she felt the same overwhelming love now being turned on her.

The happiness of being lifted up again like this, of being able to exchange words in someone's arms. In the midst of the white snow that had given the still-small Emilia despair, the fear that everything might be over — a certain warmth now existed right in front of her.

Holding Emilia, who trembled with both joy and cold, the short-haired silver-haired woman let her relieved expression harden again, and began to run, looking all around them. Then, breathing out white breath, she brought her lips close to Emilia's ear in her arms.

"Emilia, listen. Right now you don't understand anything, and I'm sure you have so many worries... but it's going to be all right. All of it. I'll make it all right somehow. Even if, say, I end up separated from you here — I will never, ever leave you alone..."

Even as she was spoken to so earnestly, Emilia had no idea what was being said to her. She only knew that the thought of being separated from the person in front of her was terrifying, and with numb fingers she grasped at the woman's chest, shaking her head desperately, eyes brimming with tears. For a young Emilia who knew nothing, appealing to the other's affection was the only way she knew to get by. By continuing to do that, Emilia could remain the Emilia that everyone doted on. It had always been that way. And so it would be here too, and from now on—

"—————!"

"No. No, Emilia. That was fine, until now — but from now on, it can't be like that anymore. You have to become strong, and wise, and brave. If you don't, you won't be able to bear the weight of who you are, and I'm afraid you'll meet a sad end. And that would be sad for me too... for everyone, for my brother too."

"—————u"

She shook her head desperately, over and over. And still, the woman cut down Emilia's plea without mercy. Right here, right now, she condemned the young Emilia's dependence, telling her it must not be allowed to continue.

She couldn't believe it. It hurt more than she could bear. Until now, every time she'd been told no, things had somehow worked out by doing this. So why not this time? At the moment of her greatest suffering, greatest pain, greatest fear, why didn't the old way work anymore? If there was a better way, why had no one taught it to her until now, of all times? — She began to hate them all, unbearably.

"—————!"

"I'm sorry, Emilia. I'm so sorry. For never teaching you anything important, for hiding everything from you... for letting you go on being nothing but a sweet little princess — forgive me... forgive us..."

—I won't forgive you. I won't forgive you. I won't forgive you. I won't forgive you. I won't forgive you. I won't forgive you. I won't forgive you.

"Please — don't come to hate the people around you who cherished your smile, or their kind lies..."

—I hate them. I hate them so much. I hate them so much. I hate them so much. I hate them so much. I hate them so much. I hate them so much. I hate them so much. I hate them so much.

"—————"

She hated lies. She hated lies. Lies brought nothing but sadness. Lies were the cause of everything not going the way it should. Lies left Emilia alone. That was why she hated lies. Because she hated lies. Because she hated liars. Everyone was 『————』.

"Our precious Emilia... someday, surely, I'll tell you..."

"—————"

She shook a voice that would not become sound. She didn't want to hear anything more. Whatever she heard now, nothing would change. The howling wind, Emilia's own shrieking cry, drowned out the end of the woman's words before the world could hear them.

At Emilia's rejection, at the sight of her thrashing and struggling in her arms, the silver-haired woman's face turned sorrowful — but then, as if shaking off every emotion, she turned her face forward again.

"—Ah"

—And then, the true ending came.

The figure running with Emilia in her arms caught sight of something ahead and stopped. At the faint jolt, Emilia lifted her face, and in her upturned field of view — she noticed that the face of the one holding her had gone rigid in a way she had never seen before. The emotions rising in that expression were complicated — surprise and sorrow, anger and grief, revulsion and longing, and, somewhere within it all, a measure of relief.

The white, cold wind striking her cheeks grew fiercer. A blizzard rose, so fierce that merely keeping her eyes open became difficult, and Emilia, out of sheer terror, pressed her hands to her frozen-feeling ears and cried out. And then—

And then— ———————. ——————————————————.

The morning of the fifth day of the Sanctuary — for Natsuki Subaru, the day he had staked everything on to break this loop for good opened with the worst possible report.

"Barusu. — Where has Emilia-sama gone?"

"—Huh?"

Subaru, washing his face at the water's edge and wiping down his body with a damp cloth, gave a dazed reply upon hearing Ram, who had strode into the midst of a row of bare-chested men without the slightest hesitation.

It was currently just past dawn — around eight o'clock by the reckoning of his old world. The evacuees and settlement residents who had risen one after another, along with the men of the group, were all washing off the night's sweat together at the water's edge. Into that washing area of exposed male bodies, Ram had come striding with total composure, and the first thing out of her mouth was a question about Emilia's whereabouts.

At a woman being here as though it were the most natural thing in the world, a few of the men shrank away and beat a hasty, embarrassed retreat from the spot — but Subaru had no room to spare for teasing them about it. Because he simply could not make sense of what Ram had just said.

"Emilia's... what?"

"Exactly what I said. When Ram went to attend to Emilia-sama, the building was empty — not a soul inside. Ram assumed at first that Barusu had taken her out somewhere..."

"Empty, as in... how did that even happen. I mean, I talked things through properly with Emilia after she woke up, before I left the building... and then I came here."

Unable to hide his shock at the exchange with Ram, Subaru replayed that morning's events in his mind. The night after she'd asked him to hold her hand, Emilia had woken the next morning and given a quiet smile to Subaru, who had been watching over her sleeping face at her bedside. Even then, he'd thought it unlike her — she had low blood pressure, after all — how well she'd woken. Emilia had glanced down at her hand, still held in his, and then, to Subaru who wanted to continue last night's conversation, she'd said in a quiet voice,

"I want to get my thoughts in order first, before we talk properly. Once Ram's helped me get ready, I'm sure I'll be able to talk by then."

And with that, she'd sent Subaru out of the room. Subaru himself had felt impatient, but pushing Emilia when he considered her state of mind would have been cruel. More than anything, here she was, so composed. Being overly solicitous about it might well have backfired. That had been his judgment, at least.

"So we said our goodbyes on that, and I figured I'd leave the rest up to you..."

"And so she left the building before Ram arrived to attend her... is that it. I see. — A failure on your part, Barusu."

"—————"

There was nothing Subaru could say against Ram's icy voice. In truth, it was his own oversight — he'd let his guard down seeing Emilia's state and hadn't done a proper verbal handoff with Ram. But of all people, that this would lead to such a result with Emilia of all people...

"Maybe she just stepped out to relieve herself, or..."

"Are you saying Ram is so careless she wouldn't have checked her surroundings on the way here?"

"That's not what I— but then why would..."

"Do you truly not understand why?"

Ram's pursuit showed no mercy toward Subaru, whose voice caught in his throat. Ram's gaze, cold as always, had grown colder still in this moment. It was the same look she'd given him the night before, when she'd glanced at him with that bored, distant look during her questioning. Seeing disappointment and dejection plainly visible in those eyes, Subaru lowered his own.

"—Are you saying Emilia ran away?"

"What other interpretation is there? Or would you rather believe that the opposition to opening the Sanctuary has been watching, eagle-eyed, for a chance when both Barusu and Ram were away from Emilia-sama's side — and that they acted on it this very morning?"

"...It's not like that's impossible."

"If you cling to that, it's pathetic beyond words. Either way, the fact remains that Emilia-sama is not here. Even setting aside that there's no way for her to leave the Sanctuary, this is bad."

Ram's stance was severe, but she still had the consideration to keep her voice low enough that it wouldn't carry to those around them. Even she understood that if word got out that Emilia had thrown away her challenge to the Trial and vanished, it could damage Roswaal's reputation. No doubt that was precisely why she judged this a matter to be handled with such care.

"Emilia..."

Lifting his face, Subaru murmured her name in a faint, uncertain voice. Just as Ram had said, even if Emilia had given up on everything and tried to run, the barrier of the Sanctuary would never let a half-elf like her out into the world beyond. Emilia was bound to the Sanctuary — no matter how far she ran, she could never truly escape. She wasn't foolish enough not to realize that herself. Which meant that even knowing all that, if she still wanted to run — just how far had she been driven?

"That's on me and Puck..."

Puck's misjudgment — a failure of imagination about just how great a shock it would be for Emilia to have her bond with him severed. And Subaru's own mistake — judging the wounded Emilia only by her surface composure, mistaking it for calm, and missing the signals she was sending.

If Emilia truly had run, it was the result of those two failures compounding one another.

"Supposing Emilia-sama is hiding somewhere... Barusu, any idea where?"

"Wait. Don't just decide she ran off like that's settled. To begin with, there's nowhere in this Sanctuary she could go and stay out of sight for long. There's barely anywhere Emilia could even wander around unnoticed."

Waving a hand to hold back Ram, who seemed ready to reach a conclusion, Subaru made his case. Ram, who'd been standing half-turned toward him, let out a breath, tipped her chin down slightly, and said,

"I'll admit it's premature. But if so, then what do you propose? It's clear enough that none of the men have seen her, given that Barusu was here the whole time. Are you hoping, then, that she's been spotted by the women still at the cathedral?"

"If it's the people of Arlam Village, if they saw Emilia wandering around by herself, they'd call out to her. Worst case, maybe one of the Sanctuary residents saw her... it's not too late to draw a conclusion once we've checked that."

Rattling off countermeasures at speed, Subaru half understood himself that this was simply putting off the inevitable conclusion. Ram, listening to his proposal with an attitude of hearing him out for now, was likely thinking the very same thing. What Subaru and Ram shared in common was this: that Emilia had gone missing of her own will, and that it would be inconvenient if that fact became too widely known. Whether that was out of concern for Emilia, or for Roswaal, was another matter.

"If she's found quickly by either route, we'll have gotten away by the skin of our teeth. Still, we should prepare for the worst-case possibility. You understand that, don't you, Barusu."

"...I don't want to think that way, but..."

Ram's insistence was also a warning not to look away from the worst-case scenario. If Emilia, having left the building, was fleeing the Trial, and remained unfound, and that fact became known throughout the whole of the Sanctuary — it would create a wound with no recovery possible. To deal with it before that happened, there was no other way than for Subaru and the others who knew the situation to run themselves ragged searching.

"If we ask around and still can't find her..."

"Then we'll have no choice but to comb the forest with every hand we can use."

"...That's not a job that finishes in a day or two."

Lifting his face, Subaru looked out at the trees of the forest surrounding the Sanctuary, brow furrowing. Cut off from the outside world by the barrier, the Sanctuary was still more than large enough to hide a single person within it. And if Emilia truly was hiding somewhere in the forest, she didn't even need to stay in one place.

Today, and tomorrow. There were barely forty-eight hours left before the deadline of the wager and the Contract expired. Within that time, he had to find Emilia, get her to face the Trial, and see her through it.

—Could he do it? No one else could. Someone who had failed to notice her heart fraying to this point, who had driven Emilia to this — could that same person give the cornered Emilia the strength to face forward again?

"Am I overestimating myself here, Puck..."

"...I wonder if this is somehow connected to her Contract with the Great Spirit being severed, too."

"In the sense that the biggest support holding her heart together is gone, it's not unrelated. But for it to snap her like that, just from that alone... neither I nor he thought it'd go that far."

There had to be something else — one more thing, layered on top, pushing her further. Thinking back on Emilia's composed voice last night and this morning, that was the only conclusion he could reach.

"—————"

Surely not, he thought. He'd been careful — or so he'd meant to be — confirming things before acting. But if, just supposing, Emilia had noticed—

"—Natsuki-san!"

Subaru, his face tense, jumped at the sharp call of his name. Looking over, he saw a gray-haired young man waving his arm wide from the direction of the plaza at the center of the settlement, running toward him desperately. It was Otto — who was supposed to have stayed at the cathedral helping the villagers prepare breakfast, skipping the morning bathing.

Coming at a dead run with his face drained of color, he stopped right in front of Subaru, hands braced on his knees, breathing hard.

"There's — there's several things I need to tell you. Quite a few, if I break it all down... but for now, broadly, two things. Good news and bad news."

"That's got me worried too, but there's something I want to ask first. Has Emilia shown up over at the cathedral? Has anyone mentioned her?"

"—? No, I haven't seen Emilia-sama yet this morning. And I haven't heard anything like that from the cathedral either."

Otto frowned slightly at having his own news preempted by a question whose purpose he couldn't guess. Still, he answered dutifully — and at his answer, both Subaru's and Ram's expressions visibly clouded. Otto seemed to sense something was wrong from their reaction, but apparently judged that his own news took priority over pressing the matter, and said,

"So — good news and bad news. Which first?"

"I hate that they come as a set, but... fine, give me the good news first."

If it was bad news, he'd need plenty of time afterward to rack his brain over how to handle it. Even without that, the present situation — not knowing where Emilia was — was already the worst it could be. He wanted to avoid piling bad news on top of that and ending up with his head in his hands. At Subaru's decision to put a cushion in between, Otto folded his arms, nodded, and said,

"The good news is this: just as we discussed, Natsuki-san, I've settled things with the evacuees. Everyone's made their own small preparations, and they can move out any time today, whenever you're ready. I asked them to be ready to respond to last-minute instructions too, so even a sudden call will work."

"—I see. Thanks. Honestly, that should've been on me — going and talking to everyone myself, getting their cooperation."

"Luckily, I've gotten close with everyone these past few days anyway. Thinking about how we'll get along going forward, having a chance like this to do it was honestly a good match. Anyway — the preparations are all in order."

A clear voice answered his words of thanks, but Otto's expression, for having accomplished work he ought to be proud of, was far from bright. Something serious enough to overshadow good news just reported — what could it be? Losing to the silent scrutiny, Subaru pressed for the rest of what Otto had brought — the bad news.

"So, what's the bad news?"

"Just a little while ago, a furious Garfiel came storming into the cathedral, looking for you, Natsuki-san, and absolutely fuming."

"Looking for me...? What? I don't think I've done anything to set him off yet, this time around..."

With Garfiel's persuasion waiting as the final showdown this time, Subaru had made a point of exercising the utmost caution around any contact with him — avoiding conversations with Garfiel as much as possible, both in his planning with Otto and after the wager with Roswaal had been struck. Thanks to that effort, up until this morning Subaru had barely had to face Garfiel properly at all, arriving at the day of reckoning largely unscathed —

"Ideally, I wanted to deal with him only after I'd settled things properly with Ryuzu-θ first..."

"That's exactly it."

At Subaru's words hoping for things to be tackled in proper order, Otto suddenly held up a finger. Subaru's eyes went wide at the gesture. He had no idea what Otto meant by "that."

Still hovering a question mark over his head, Subaru watched as Otto, his expression grim, said,

"It seems that Ryuzu-san has been missing since this morning too. Garfiel's supposedly been tearing through the whole village with a desperate look on his face, searching for her. From what I could tell, that's why he wants to talk to you too, Natsuki-san."
