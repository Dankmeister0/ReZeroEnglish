Arc 3 Chapter 9: "Divine Protection, Reunion, and a Promise"

About five minutes after they'd taken off down the rough road — dragged along by the arm she held, Subaru pressed doggedly onward, his breath ragged and his footing unsteady.

"Hurry it up, or they'll catch us. Is this really the time to be dawdling?"

"You... you're one to talk... Time-out, seriously, just— hang on a sec...!"

When they'd first taken off, Subaru had been the one in the lead, but stamina issues had already flipped their positions. The girl running ahead wasn't even winded, moving with total ease. Subaru, on the other hand, couldn't withstand the prolonged all-out sprint and was already falling apart.

In fairness to Subaru's honor, one excuse for his exhaustion was that he hadn't yet fully recovered his condition. His body, still mid-rehabilitation, had a low stamina stat even under normal circumstances — and right now it was sitting even lower than that. Small wonder, really.

"What a pathetic creature. Falling behind a comely maiden such as myself — have you no shame?"

"I'm gonna try using 'I just got out of a sickbed' as my get-out-of-jail-free card here... Though, honestly, things are looking pretty grim."

Behind them, the threat of the thugs chasing Subaru and the girl was still very much alive. Fortunately, having won the initial dash, the two of them had put a fair distance between themselves and the group. But since the alley was basically a single straight path, it was obvious that slowing down even a little would let the pursuers catch up eventually. Ideally, they'd reach the main street before that happened, but—

"Why do we keep heading further and further into the dark, empty parts with no people around...? You got some kind of plan here?"

He called out to the orange hair bobbing along ahead of him with unwavering steps. The girl answered his question with a bright, breezy "Ha ha ha, no idea!"

"Whatever I do turns out favorably for me in the end. Thus there has never been any need to think too deeply about it, nor will there ever be. Trust in my doings."

"I'm humbled... Seriously, is this actually gonna work out...?"

So far, the fact that they hadn't run into a dead end was undeniably to her credit. And considering the possibility of becoming trapped rats rather than reaching the main street, it might be premature to call their current path deeper into the maze a step for the worse. Still—

"Without some kind of bright outlook here... this is getting rough on the stamina front too."

"—Hmph. This has become troublesome indeed."

Subaru, gasping, fumbled the second half of his sentence. Right in front of him, the girl abruptly stopped. Still being pulled by the hand, Subaru was dragged to a halt along with her, but—

"Whoa, hold on, we don't have time to stop — if we don't put some distance between us, they'll catch up."

He jogged in place, calling out in an attempt to get the stopped girl moving again. But the girl tilted her head at his words, then turned a bored gaze on him.

"—I'm bored of this."

"Bored...? Huh!?"

"I said I'm bored of this. To begin with, why must I be the one running? My actions are mine to decide. They shall never be dictated by the conduct of those crude ruffians behind us."

"T-that's easy to say!? But this situation doesn't really allow for—"

Subaru panicked at the young lady's outburst of selfishness. Honestly, he couldn't afford to waste time on this back-and-forth. If he only had to worry about his own skin, the best option would be to ditch her and make a break for it. But that would mean losing sight of his original goal entirely.

Subaru clutched his head, caught between ideals, reality, and the urge to flee. Watching him run through his repertoire of expressions, the girl let her lips curl into a mischievous smile.

"Hmph. I've decided. — I shall grant you the honor of carrying me."

"No thank you!!"

He crossed both arms in an X to declare his refusal of the worthless proposal. The girl scowled, looking noticeably offended by his instant rejection.

"Not just anyone is granted the honor of carrying me. To throw it away of your own accord — you truly know no fear."

"Do I look like some musclebound stud who can carry a whole person while running? Even at full strength, carrying a girl with a lower physique stat than you was already my limit after ten minutes! And right now my stamina's already bone dry and both my legs are a newborn fawn's, dammit!"

He pointed at his trembling legs, barking out his pathetic complaint with defiant gusto. Looking down at Subaru's unsightly display, the girl let out an exasperated sigh — "What a hopeless fellow" —

"—Acra."

she murmured quietly.

"Huh?"

The instant her murmur reached his ears, a faint light suddenly sprang up around Subaru. It enveloped his startled body entirely, a golden glow forming a veil over his skin. Wrapped completely in the golden light, Subaru raised both hands in front of his face.

"Wh-what is this!? M-my aura's turning visible and physical...!? Man, I'm getting kinda pumped...!?"

Subaru was thrown into confusion by the abnormality happening to his own body. Hidden behind his flustered state, the girl moved leisurely around to his back.

"If you're so against carrying me, then this will do."

"Whoa— oof!"

The moment she finished speaking, the girl lightly leapt onto Subaru's back. Her arms wrapped around his neck, and Subaru braced his legs, bracing for the full weight of the girl — small as she was — to bear down on him. But that worry proved needless. Because—

"What— you're light as a feather... no, wait, my body's feeling abnormally great!?"

Despite carrying the girl on his back, he couldn't feel her weight at all. More than that, even the bag of Appa in his hand had lost its heft, and the legs he'd been jogging on felt as light and fresh as if they'd been swapped out for new ones.

"Once you've received my Blessing, anyone can work like a carthorse. You shall do the same."

"Whoa, I don't quite get it, but I can work with this! Natsuki Subaru, here I go—!"

He put one hand on the girl's waist to keep her from being thrown off, and took off running. Light, so light — his legs felt light. His whole body felt as if it had shed the shell of the flesh, so airy that a single stride felt like it covered as much ground as five had, just minutes before.

The distance that should have closed during their standstill opened up again, and this time showed no sign of shrinking. Even the presence of the men falling further and further behind was something his sharpened senses picked up keenly. Subaru was filled with an omnipotent sense that every function of his body had been raised to its absolute limit.

"What is this, amazing! My body's so light! Nothing scares me anymore!"

Lowering his body to cut wind resistance, Subaru rode the momentum until his body itself became the wind. His field of vision narrowed as he tore through the narrow, rough back alley at breakneck speed, and in the midst of it he turned just his head back toward the girl.

"Since we've got a bit of breathing room now that we're this far ahead, lemme ask... why'd they even pick a fight with you in the first place?"

Given that those guys had seemed the type to fold in the face of authority, he'd have thought a girl this flashy-looking would be someone they'd hesitate to touch. No matter how he looked at it, she didn't look like some ordinary commoner off the street. Then again, it was also possible those blockheads had simply been too blockheaded to think that far.

At Subaru's question, the girl gave a small huff through her shapely nose and prefaced her answer with "It's nothing of consequence."

"I was out sightseeing to kill time and got separated from my companion. While looking for them, I decided to take in some of the grubby back alleys one doesn't normally see, and that's precisely when I ran into those men."

"But just for that, they'd pick a fight...?"

"And when I laughed at their absurd appearance, that's what came of it."

"That's your fault!?"

"This is exactly why I can't stand dealing with men who have no patience."

"Maybe you should try holding your own tongue a little!?"

A typhoon of a girl — Subaru found himself feeling sorry for the blockheads swept up in her disaster. Though in this particular case, the one who'd caught the worst of the fallout was, quite squarely, Subaru himself.

While they kept bickering back and forth, Subaru came upon a path ahead that turned sharply to the right at a right angle. He pictured the flow of it — killing his speed by kicking off the wall, using the rebound to swing right — and just then, a brilliant idea flashed through his mind. It was this:

"Just following the road like this, we're never gonna shake them for good. So instead of letting the road dictate where I run — I'll make my own road—!"

"Has the effect gone so strong it's addled your head?"

"Don't say something so mean! And once you see this, you're gonna regret ever doubting me!"

Declaring this, Subaru shifted up yet another gear and piled on more speed. The girl on his back shared the same field of view. Which meant she must have understood that Subaru's momentum was headed for nothing but a head-on collision with the wall in front of them. She tightened the arms wrapped around his neck—

"Hey, you. What are you thinking? At this rate we're going to crash—"

"Quit your yappin' and just watch, fire!! Behold — the wall-climbing technique passed down straight from the light-fingered brat of the slums, Felt!!"

Still carried by his momentum, he planted a foot on the ashen-gray wall and, defying gravity, took a second step. With a resounding thud, Subaru's body rose upward — a wall-walk that would've had Newton on the verge of tears —

"King crab~!"

— didn't happen.

On the fourth step, his footing slipped clean out, and he flipped backward once before slamming face-down onto the ground. Unable to break his fall, he smashed his face and rolled around in agony, groaning,

"Ihh hurhh, ihh hurhh, ihh hurhh, ihh hur...!"

"Just what are you even doing?"

The girl, having leapt clear at the very last moment, looked down at the scraped-up, whimpering Subaru with the eyes one might give a curious animal. In her hands was the bag of Appa, spared from the disaster of the fall. Relieved to see it had come through unscathed, Subaru pushed his sprawled body upright.

"Dammit, of all things to fail at... Felt, you jerk, giving me weird false hope like that..."

He cursed the blonde girl who wasn't even present, though from her perspective this was a bum rap if ever there was one. It was true she'd scaled that wall once, but she surely had no memory of ever teaching Subaru how to do it. Of course, Subaru had no memory of being taught, either. Having put on a full display of self-indulgent failure, Subaru scratched his head and tried to pull himself back together and stand up. But—

"Huh, huh, wait, I twisted my— hold on, ow!? Whoa, hey, this really hurts, this really super hurts!? I just twisted it but it hurts like it's broken!"

"I've put every function of your body into an overdriven state. Of course your limbs move exactly as you will them to — and naturally, you'll feel pain more acutely than usual too."

"Don't 'naturally' me!?"

The girl explained the magnitude of her buff effect on Subaru as though it were the most natural thing in the world. Apparently feeling no sympathy for Subaru groaning in pain, she mercilessly urged him on with "Now, up, up." But Subaru refused, whining like a child, "It hurts, it hurts, I'm gonna cry." The time and distance they'd worked so hard to gain was being squandered all over again, and he could sense the men's rough, approaching presence closing in once more.

And it was right then—

"Here I finally catch sight of you again after all this time, and what do I find you doing."

Lumbering into view, the owner of that voice showed his massive frame from the right-hand path they'd been meant to turn down. Subaru looked up. Where an ordinary person's head would be was still no higher than this man's chest. Raising his gaze further still, he found a bearded, bald head—

"Y-you're..."

The muscular old man he recognized was looking down at Subaru and the girl.

"The main geezer's here! Now we win—!!"

"It's been ages, and already you're annoying me, boy. I'll leave you behind."

"Help me, seriously, help! My chastity's in danger, and so are my internal organs!"

The former was the girl's crisis; the latter, Subaru's. This assumption stemmed from a prejudice that bad men out there must surely be selling organs on the black market, but hearing it, the old man — Rom the elder — looked over the girl's attire, then Subaru.

"What, poking your nose into trouble again, are you? Kicking up a fuss with a girl in tow — you're not one to be underestimated, boy."

"I don't need lecherous-old-man comments like that! Just help me!"

Bearing through the pain, Subaru stood and hopped over on one foot to plead desperately at Rom. Scratching his hairless head in bewilderment, the old man cast an appraising look toward the silent girl. Exposed to that gaze, the girl's face plainly showed her displeasure.

"Do not cast such rude eyes upon me, you grubby old man."

"I'm one to talk, but you've got a pretty foul mouth yourself!? What are you saying to the miracle geezer I found in the depths of hell!? Don't take it the wrong way, Rom-jii. Both of us are just a little too honest at heart, is all!"

"You're as good as ever at sapping a man's motivation! Now hurry up and hide!"

Clamping a hand over the mouth of the girl, who looked like she still had plenty more venom to spit, Subaru hopped off in the direction Rom indicated with a turn of his head. There, stacked scrap lumber leaned against a wall, and with a good crouch, there looked to be just enough space to hide both their bodies. He shoved the girl into the spot first, then wedged himself in after her like a cork sealing a bottle. The girl looked ready to complain about the dusty spot, but Subaru desperately clamped a hand over her mouth again and somehow managed to smother her discontent.

"Rom-jii, okay."

"What d'you mean 'okay'... I'll block you two with my body, so don't move. If we're found, it's just as much trouble for me as for you."

Grumbling all the while, Rom used his massive frame to shield the two of them further from view. Leaning his back against the wall and folding his arms, he stood watch while the pair held their breath behind him. Only a few dozen seconds after they'd hidden, a rush of multiple footsteps flooded into the alley nearby—

"The hell! I thought it'd be some kid, but it's just a geezer!"

As though he'd meant to pounce the moment he sensed someone, the man running in front snarled the moment he saw Rom's massive frame. Rom shot back a silent, withering glare, and that alone was enough to cow the man's bravado — he stumbled back a step with an "O-oh."

"What's all this racket. Startling an old man like that isn't something I care for."

It probably wasn't even meant to carry particular menace, but when a giant of a man like Rom spoke in a low, ill-tempered voice, that alone was an intimidation all its own. It hit its mark precisely, sending a ripple of unease through the man and the others who'd caught up behind him. But—

"Well if it isn't old man Cromwell."

One man from the group pointed at Rom and called out the name. Subaru raised an eyebrow at the unfamiliar name, but the more striking reaction came from the man being called by it — Rom himself. Deep, bitter creases carved themselves into his already wrinkled face.

"I don't much care to be called by that name."

"You really in a position to be making demands like that? Word is you tanked the Loot House and the slum folk aren't exactly warming up to you these days."

"It was a place that had built up years of grime. Having it swept clean is more refreshing than anything, if you ask me. ...These days I do as I please."

Rom answered the leering man with barely a flicker of expression. Perhaps judging he'd get no further rise out of him, the man shrugged.

"Heh, well, suit yourself. But if you're not gonna bow your head to Russell-san, you'd best come pay your respects sooner rather than later. The longer you drag your feet, the worse it's just gonna get for you."

Having spat that out, the man moved off down the alley again, the others in tow. But before he vanished from sight, he turned back—

"Oh, right. Old man Cromwell... a couple of brats didn't come through here, did they?"

"Haven't seen any. What about you lot — you wouldn't happen to know a blonde girl I'm acquainted with, would you?"

"Don't know her. Heh, some brat you just picked up matters that much to you? Let's neither of us go senile, eh."

Waving a careless hand, the men finally left for good this time. Rom watched their retreating backs intently, biting his lip as though holding back anger.

Peering at his profile through the gap in the scrap lumber, Subaru couldn't help but feel a certain unease. That Rom, whom he was reuniting with after so long, was friendly toward him was a welcome fact. But the way he looked right now was slightly out of step with the image Subaru held of him, and—

"Mmtil hmn..."

"Hm?"

A whisper-like voice reached him, and Subaru broke off his train of thought to glance beside him. Right next to him, close enough that he could feel her breath, the beautiful girl was crouched down, her mouth still sealed by the palm he'd pressed over it to silence her. Working her muffled lips against his hand, she managed,

"Ihf lohn duh yoo inhen'... kip mah mouf covuhed!"

— A sharp, merciless pain as her canine teeth sank straight into his palm.

"—Ahhn!!"

A puppy-like, high-pitched scream from Subaru echoed quietly through the corner of the back alley.

"Thanks for hiding us, Rom-jii. I was half-worried you'd gone senile after that hard knock to the head, but it's great to see you're doing fine."

"You... those men are probably still prowling around here somewhere, so you're welcome to have a change of heart, you know."

"Don't talk small when your body's so big! One small-timer's plenty around here, and that's me, yeah!"

Rom sighed, a weary look on his face, at Subaru giving him a thumbs-up with a gleaming grin.

The scene had shifted from the narrow alley to a somewhat more open street. After Subaru and the girl crawled out from the gap in the scrap lumber, Rom had led them here, saying it was a spot out of sight of prying eyes. With the threat of the pursuing men removed, Subaru and the girl were finally able to catch their breath. It was that sense of relief that had prompted Subaru's opening remark.

"Hey, you. You're talking to him awfully familiarly — who is this old man? Explain yourself to me."

The girl, who had kept silent up until now, finally tugged at Subaru's sleeve in irritation. Gesturing at Rom with her chin in a haughty manner, she demanded to know. Subaru gave an "Ah" and nodded.

"This old man's the hidden face of the royal capital's slums — a fence who used to run the light-fingered crowd, a Giant they call Rom-jii, a stingy old bastard. His specialties are blind spots, spoiling his granddaughter, and being cannon fodder."

"I get that you're happy to reunite with the old man you'd been wanting to see, but aren't you getting a little too carried away, boy?"

Rom clamped a hand over Subaru's finger, which had been pointing at him, chiding him for it. Subaru responded with a barely-repentant "My bad, my bad,"

"Still, even if it's mostly tomfoolery, it's not like it's all lies either."

"So some of it's true, is it. In other words, a light-fingered, stingy, blind, cannon-fodder old man living in the slums. What a pitiful reputation to have earned after living this long."

"Your partner here's plenty aggravating too!"

Before Rom, who stamped his feet in frustration, Subaru and the girl exchanged shrugs with each other. Then Subaru turned back to the dejected Rom.

"Either way, I really am glad we got to meet again, Rom-jii. Honestly, back then I was on death's door myself, so I had no way to check on how things turned out."

"...Quick to switch gears, or something like that — you're a boy that throws off my rhythm. I heard your belly got sliced clean open, but you look plenty lively."

Rom gave a wry smile, then gave Subaru's whole body a quick once-over. He looked pained at the scars remaining on Subaru's exposed skin.

"I'm one to talk myself, but it looks like that knife-wielder really did a number on you."

"Nah, the spot Guts-chan got me is basically just my stomach — all these other ones are from stuff that happened after that whole incident."

"It's barely been a week since then — what on earth happened to you!?"

"It's a long story and kind of a slog to get into, so let's skip it. I'm alive, so I can stand here and talk like this — that's enough for me. Anyway, back to the topic at hand—"

Hiding his scars from Rom's startled gaze, Subaru pointed in the direction of (what he assumed to be) the slums and brought up the matter properly.

"What've you been up to since the whole Loot House fuss? I mean, honestly, your workplace is gone now — are you eating properly? You're probably just living off deli food again, aren't you?"

"I've no idea what you're on about in that second half, but I'm getting by well enough. It's not as though drinking myself silly at the Loot House was my only line of work. Living small and scrappy in the underside of the royal capital this long, a man picks up connections here and there."

Rom answered with a hearty laugh at Subaru's fretting, worried like some country mother. In truth, without a certain measure of trust within the slums, one could never have taken on the role of overseeing stolen goods in the first place. His talk of connections probably wasn't a bluff, either. Either way, if there were no lingering injuries from that incident or worries about his livelihood since, that was one relief for Subaru. Of course, compared to Emilia and the others at Roswaal Manor, it was a matter he spent proportionally less of his heart on.

"So there, it's not like I was worried about you or anything, Rom-jii!"

"Sorry to interrupt while you're saying something creepy, but there was something I wanted to ask you too."

Rom said this to Subaru, who was displaying a pointless bout of tsundere-ation, his voice suddenly dropping in tone. Seeing seriousness mix into Rom's expression, Subaru straightened out of his loose posture as well. Just then, his sleeve was suddenly tugged from behind. He turned around. The girl was standing at a distance closer than necessary, and Subaru startled at her beauty up close. She said to him,

"I am bored."

"Don't go being selfish in the middle of this."

"This is not selfishness. This is the world's own choosing."

"Quiet, you. Alright, let's do a quiz. In this world, are there more uphill slopes or downhill slopes? Let's think about it."

He tossed the girl carelessly into a sea of contemplation and turned back to Rom. To the old man, looking put out at having his momentum interrupted, Subaru gestured with meaning, "Go ahead." Rom sighed at Subaru's attitude, then shook his head as if to reset himself.

"Boy. You wouldn't happen to know where Felt went, would you?"

"...You haven't heard? Word is Reinhard's the one who took her."

"Reinhard... you mean the 'Sword Saint'? Why in the world would the 'Knight among Knights' end up taking Felt anywhere?"

Rom tilted his head, looking utterly baffled. Subaru pitied the old man's misfortune, thinking "So it really was the effect of that hard knock to the head after all" — but on reflecting back on the Loot House incident, he understood the discrepancy. Now that he thought of it, during that incident, Rom had lost consciousness before Reinhard even barged in. And in the time between then and Subaru himself losing consciousness, he had no memory of Rom being conscious enough to have contact with Reinhard. Which meant—

"And so poor Rom-jii, left behind at the ruins of the Loot House, woke up with no one to fill him in on what had happened, and could only stand there stunned before the wreckage of his own home."

"I wasn't reduced to anything so pitiful. I woke up in a guard post. Getting treated was a relief, but I made my exit soon enough after that."

"Ah, right, of course. Yeah, that would've been a little uncomfortable."

Waking up in a police infirmary when you've got skeletons in your closet isn't exactly a comforting feeling. It was no wonder he hadn't had the composure to hear the full story and had made an early escape. In other words, because Rom had left the guard post without hearing the full circumstances, he'd never managed to make contact with Reinhard, who was said to have taken Felt into his protection, and had been forced to remain in the dark right up until now.

"So that's where the stories crossed wires. Okay, well then, let me fill in the part before I lost consciousness, and then patch in what happened after, bit by bit."

With that preface, Subaru put on a one-man reenactment, complete with gestures, of the great battle at the Loot House — "Sword Saint VS Bowel Hunter." Rom marveled at Subaru's pointlessly intense acting, and even the girl, who'd been looking bored, had her eyes light up as she begged him to go on.

"And then I said this to her as she stood there startled — 'I want you to tell me your name.'"

"Gahh! He said it, he actually said it!" "Ho-ho, quite an elegant turn of phrase, isn't it. I don't dislike it myself."

Somewhat sidetracked but still on course, he finished telling his tale of valor. The retelling had gotten all three of them a bit fired up, still buzzing with excitement, but it was Rom who first came back to his senses.

"This ain't the time for that. Ahh, so in other words, boy, you don't know anything beyond the fact that Felt was taken away by the 'Sword Saint,' is that it?"

"Yeah, pretty much... hm? What's up?"

"Uphill slopes and downhill slopes are obviously the same in number!"

"Bwoomph—!"

Struck by a body blow from the girl who'd abruptly arrived at the correct answer, Subaru crumpled to the ground in agony. Shaking out the fist she'd used to punch him, the girl seemed satisfied and declared, "The rest of this you may handle yourselves." Rom stood dumbfounded for a moment, then looked down at the groaning Subaru with pity.

"It's not really my place to say, but I do think you ought to choose your partner more carefully, boy."

"I already chose mine ages ago! Now I'm just waiting for her to choose me back! I'm in the middle of doing my best not to vanish from her sight, dazz!"

Springing back up, Subaru pointed out Rom's mistaken assumption with a flourish of a pose. Then he cleared his throat and steered the conversation back on track.

"With those long-time connections of yours, is there any way you could get a direct audience at Reinhard's house?"

"If my connections were that all-powerful, you think I'd be living off cheap booze in a shack? Still, if that's how it is, this becomes a troublesome matter. — Of all houses, House Astrea, is it."

The latter half of his murmur formed only inside Rom's mouth and never reached Subaru's ears. Looking up at Rom's grave expression, Subaru gave a resigned shrug.

"Well, I might have a way to talk to Reinhard myself, so if I find anything out, I'll let you know too, Rom-jii. I was already planning to check on how Felt was doing anyway."

"That'd be a load off my mind... but you've got some strange connections of your own, don't you. Is it through that young lady over there?"

Perhaps remembering the sort of person Subaru had shown himself to be during their previous meeting, Rom seemed to have concluded that the source of Subaru's confidence lay not in Subaru himself, but in those around him. That judgment wasn't wrong in itself, but assuming it had to do with that girl was a mistake.

"Nah, not even close. For starters, I don't even know that girl's name."

"Just what kind of hell have you been through with someone whose name you don't even know!?"

"Now that I think back on it, I was going all out for Emilia-tan back when I didn't even know her name either, so I figure this isn't really all that strange behavior for me."

Perhaps feeling some weariness at Subaru's breezy answer, Rom pressed a finger to the bridge of his nose, closed his eyes, and gave up the thought entirely with "There's no point even thinking about it." Then Rom looked straight at Subaru.

"Even if it's not through that young lady, there's still a chance you could arrange to speak with the 'Sword Saint,' is that right?"

"I figure it's higher odds than what you've got, Rom-jii, seeing as you've got nothing. Trust in me — with all the reassurance of crossing a rickety old rope bridge!"

"I'm the sort who tests a bridge before crossing it, myself... how did it come to this, I wonder..."

Rom rubbed his bald head and nodded as though resigning himself to it, in the face of Subaru proudly thumping his own chest.

"Aye, understood. I'll leave it to you, then. If you find out anything about Felt, I'd like you to tell me too. I'll repay you if I'm able."

"Don't go splurging on my account. So there really is something like granddaughter-doting going on, huh?"

"—Yeah. That girl's like a granddaughter to me. So I'm asking you."

"Well then, consider it done."

Said so plainly and without a shred of embarrassment, there was nothing for Subaru to argue with. The wholehearted affirmation felt good enough that his mouth loosened into a smile without meaning to. He wondered how that blonde girl would feel if she knew she was loved this much. Knowing her, he figured she'd probably turn red in the face and put on a tough front about it.

"If I find something out, how do I get in touch with you, Rom-jii?"

"Let's see... there's a shop called 'Kadomon' on Merchant Street. If you give my name to the tough-looking fellow there, he'll be able to put you in touch with me. I'll leave it at that."

"Roger, roger, Kadomon... Kadomon?"

It felt like a word that had been etched into his memory only recently, and Subaru tilted his head. For now, as long as he knew it was on Merchant Street, there shouldn't be a problem. If he checked place by place, he'd surely be able to find the shop before long.

Nodding to show he understood, Subaru and Rom exchanged a firm promise. For Subaru, it was simply a bonus along the way, and there was no reason to refuse. Just doing what he'd already meant to do, and being able to ease the limited remaining life of an old man whose days were numbered — if he thought of it that way, it was a bargain.

"...is probably the kind of excuse Emilia-tan would make. 'This is for my own sake too,' or something like that."

"Did you say something?"

"Nah, nothing. Oh, right, Rom-jii. Sudden as it is, I'm gonna go ahead and put you to work already."

Holding up a finger, Subaru turned to Rom, swaying his hips to draw his attention. Rom made no secret of his displeasure at the sudden request, but there was the promise they'd just made not long ago. Bitter expression still in place, he took the stance of at least asking, "What is it." Subaru prefaced his response with "It's nothing much, but—"

"Truth is, both me and this girl are completely lost. Before we even get to keeping any promises, at this rate my whole adventure's gonna end right here! So I need you to guide us to the main street."

"...What, that's all? Fine, leave it to me. Which street?"

"For starters, just get us to the front of the guard post."

"You— were you even listening when I said I fled the guard post!?"

Rom's voice cracked into a shriek that was swallowed up into the sky above the alley. It was a sky under which nearly a full hour had passed since he'd gotten separated from Emilia.
