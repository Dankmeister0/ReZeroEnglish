Arc 8 Chapter 66: "Magic Crystal Cannon"

——The story now rewinds to before Cecilus Segmunt accomplished his star-cleaving feat.

"That's why the one who's way more fired up than me went flying off. Should be fine, long as whoever she's up against ain't stronger than me, I figure."

"If we're talking about someone stronger than you, Halibel-san..."

"The only one who's definitely stronger than me is the Kingdom's 'Sword Saint.' Everyone else, it comes down to matchup and circumstance."

At Halibel's easygoing answer, Subaru felt both reassured and a chill of dread.

Same as with Cecilus — when someone strong openly boasted of their own strength, it was nothing but reassuring to have them as an ally. All the more so when they were in the world's strongest class.

Which was exactly why Subaru couldn't help but swallow hard at just how far beyond the norm Reinhard must be, when someone of that world-strongest caliber guaranteed his strength with an "absolutely."

"You two seem awfully at ease, chatting away like this."

Abel's irritated voice cut into the exchange between Subaru and Halibel.

Sword in hand — the Sunlight Sword — he ran at the head of the group, tracing lines of fire with the crimson blade, cutting down the Undead that charged in along the way and setting each of them ablaze, serving as their vanguard.

The Sunlight Sword's power was tremendous, letting Abel — who, left to his own devices, could barely manage a catfight with a child like Subaru — move with skill rivaling a first-rate martial artist.

Honestly, the sight of Abel kicking off the ground with such agility and dancing through his swordplay was so jarring it nearly broke Subaru's brain.

"You insolent lot — don't leave all the fighting to your Emperor."

"Quiet, that's your punishment for hiding your trump card. Besides, it's not like we're leaving all the work to you—"

Before Subaru could finish saying "not," a tremor in the ground cut him off.

"――――"

The cause of the tremor was two Undead that had leapt down from above.

A hulking giant and a tall, lanky figure landed blocking the path, weapons raised behind the sullen-faced Abel and the glaring Subaru respectively, ready to make them pay the price for the folly of bickering on a battlefield—

"'Monstrous Arm' Rondando."

Running the Sunlight Sword through the Undead that had appeared right behind him, setting its existence ablaze, Abel called out the name while his quiet black eyes reflected the Undead standing behind Subaru.

Taking the offered name, Subaru turned to look at the man standing behind him — one whose right arm alone had developed to a bizarre degree — and saw Halibel stopping the man's surprise punch with a single upraised finger.

It wasn't that Halibel had overpowered the giant in a straight contest of strength.

"No need to feel bad about it — you're plenty strong. I just let the ground take the hit for me, is all."

Halibel stood with his heels lifted off the ground, and beneath his feet the street rippled like water's surface, dispersing the impact. It threw into sharp relief both the Monstrous Arm's raw power and, at the same time, the gulf in class between him and Halibel.

Whether or not that was any comfort, in either case, it was time for the mercy stroke.

"Rondando!"

"Ah!"

Spica, her other hand joined with Subaru's, pressed her palm against the torso of the now-still Monstrous Arm. In the next instant, the Undead's role was stripped away, and the malice clouding the man — Rondando — blinked in surprise.

Turning to dust from the fingertips, Rondando was freed from the mission that had been forced upon him. Whether the faces of those who vanished this way truly looked at peace, or whether Subaru simply wanted to believe that, he couldn't say.

"It has to be a far more peaceful end than having one's Soul burned away, I suppose."

Beatrice, holding Subaru's other hand opposite Spica, understood the sentiment he felt.

At his partner's gentle consideration, Subaru said, "Yeah," squeezing her small hand back, then looked up at the Wolf Human beside them.

"Halibel-san, that helped a ton! Thanks!"

"No need, no need, His Majesty's got a point. Can't be lookin' the other way all the time myself — gotta at least pull the weight of the one who's sleepin' through this."

Halibel laughed at the thanks, bobbing the pipe clenched between his teeth up and down.

Cradled in the Wolf Human's arms was the unconscious Jamal, but for all his open boasting of his own strength, he was being far too modest about how much he'd actually done.

Sorry as it was to Jamal, Halibel was reinforcement more than sufficient to fill the hole left by his absence.

"It'd be a problem if you were satisfied with just filling in for Jamal, I suppose. An outlier like you needs to work all the harder and act the part, I suppose."

"The very thing I held my tongue about...!"

"Rough on the wolf, ain't ya. Though bein' expected of that much does get me fired up. 'Course, if I go and overdo it and end up dead, the Wolf Humans might just go extinct by accident."

"Not to put too fine a point on it, but if things ever got bad enough that you died, Halibel-san, I've got a strong suspicion it wouldn't just be the Wolf Humans going extinct — the whole world would be right there with them..."

It wasn't an exaggeration — saying it aloud only reminded him this was a fight on that kind of scale.

As always, Subaru was just desperately resisting the problem standing in his way, doing his best to deal with it, and trying with all his might to get through it together with his allies as one.

"Don't go making an ordinary Joe like me fight an enemy of the world... What, Spica, what's with that look?"

"Uu-au...?"

"Spica seems to want to say it's a bit late for that, seeing as you're Betty's partner and became Emilia's knight, I suppose."

"There's no way Spica knows the finer points of world affairs like that!"

He protested Beatrice's arbitrary interpreting. Still, in broad strokes, Spica's reaction didn't seem entirely off the mark. He tried to stare intently at his own hand, but since both of his hands were linked with little girls, he couldn't even do that. How regrettable.

Halibel watched the scene with his thread-thin eyes, clearly in high spirits—

"—That's enough playing around."

Once again, Abel's voice cut through the air, cold and flat.

But this time, not a single person, Subaru included, had any complaint about the summons — because they had finally arrived right at the doorstep of their destination.

"—The Crystal Palace."

The towering, majestic castle had been overwhelming even seen from a distance, but now, looking up at it from close by, the impression only grew stronger rather than fading.

Being praised as the most beautiful castle in the world was no idle boast.

"...What an outrageous castle, I suppose."

Beatrice murmured this, squeezing Subaru's hand tight. What was packed into that murmur wasn't admiration for the castle's beauty, but a quiet fear.

When Subaru glanced sideways to ask what she meant, her eyes — marked with their distinctive pattern — wavered, and she said,

"Gathering this much high-purity Magic Crystal, rare as it is, in such abundance — that's no ordinary feat, I suppose. Even if you turned the whole Empire upside down, you couldn't produce something like this, I'd wager."

"Be that as it may, it's clearly right here in front of us, isn't it?"

"...Which is why the shortfall in Magic Crystal was made, I suppose. No wonder there are so few Spirits in the Empire willing to lend humans a hand, I suppose."

"That means..."

Beatrice's face was tight with displeasure, and her words sent a chill through Subaru.

In short, a magic stone was a lump of condensed Mana, and the highest-purity ones were called Magic Crystals. And if Beatrice, upon seeing the Crystal Palace built so lavishly from them, spoke the way she just had while clearly furious, that could only mean—

"Prepare a great quantity of colorless Mana and condense it, and a Magic Crystal can be produced artificially. I won't deny that such a process was involved in completing this Crystal Palace."

"Betty will say it too, not just Subaru. —I hate the Empire, I suppose."

From the way Abel spoke and Beatrice reacted, Subaru sensed the fact that a great many Spirits had been sacrificed to build this castle. Even Subaru, a Spiritual Arts User in the most minor sense, couldn't help but have thoughts about that—

"Bea-ko, I get how you feel. But that and this fight are—"

"Now, now, no need to lay it on so thick — this kid understands just fine. Sure, Vollachia's history reeks of blood something fierce, but if you look back at Shinobi's, it's much of a muchness."

"Don't go comparing one low thing to another low thing just to see which one's less bad, I suppose."

Puffing her cheeks out, Beatrice answered Subaru and Halibel like that.

Then, noticing Spica peering at her past Subaru, she said,

"It's all right, Spica. Betty's conclusion is the same as Subaru's, I suppose. Betty hates the Empire, but among the Humans living there, there are some who've helped us, some Betty doesn't hate. So Betty will fight properly for their sake, I suppose."

"Eaou..."

"...Betty could've sworn Spica just called her Bea-ko. Subaru is the only one Betty allows to call her that, so no, you may not, I suppose."

Beatrice said this looking even more displeased than when she'd first seen the Crystal Palace, then smiled. At that smile, Spica let out a relieved "Uu," and Subaru, too, let out a long breath.

Meanwhile, Abel, who lived in the castle that was the root of all this evil, was utterly indifferent to this small reconciliation of hearts, simply gazing intently at the castle.

"Even if it wasn't you personally, we're talking about what your ancestors did. Show a little remorse."

"An Emperor does not bow his head easily. If you're truly determined to pursue responsibility for what happened then, go walk the castle grounds yourself. There may yet be someone who had a hand in its construction."

"Seriously, you..."

Subaru gave Abel a flat, unimpressed look for his endless mouth, but the bickering with the castle right before them ended there. —In front of the Crystal Palace, figures rose up in droves.

"Looks like the elite guard meant to keep us from gettin' into the castle. Seems like a real handful of skilled hands gathered there, but..."

"If the defenses are this tight, that's proof there's something all the more important hidden inside. Abel! Where in the castle should we head for?"

"Given the 'Stone Mass' is being used for this so-called Forbidden Art, it'll be either the treasury holding the true body of Moguro Hagane, the core of the Crystal Palace, or the underground sanctuary where sacrifices are offered to bind the Stone Mass. Near the sanctuary there's also a dungeon... no, never mind that."

"――――"

Faced with obstacles appearing one after another, the group steeled themselves for the assault. Amid that, Subaru narrowed one eye at Abel, who had swallowed back words he'd started to say.

What Abel had briefly touched on was the subject of the Crystal Palace's dungeon — and picturing who might be chained up there, Subaru let out a sigh.

Priscilla — the one who had stayed behind in the imperial capital and whose whereabouts had since become unknown.

Her survival had been confirmed thanks to Schult, who could sense their bond, but even through Subaru's repeated attempts, she had never once been sighted in the capital.

And it was in order to reunite her with Abel that Sphinx, defeated just a short while ago, had tried to drag him off to the castle.

"I already know about your relationship with Priscilla. Even if you're worried about your little sister, I'm not gonna give you grief over it."

"Do you intend to hold this over the Empire as a weakness?"

"Shut up, you sister-obsessed jerk! Your sister's personality is the absolute worst, but she's on my list of people I'm not letting die, so I'm saying let's just go save her while we're at it!"

Ever since they'd first met, Priscilla had done nothing but jerk him around, and he had no fond memories of her. But her attendant Al was from the same world as Subaru, and Priscilla had helped him out more than once.

Besides, he also wanted, just once, to save Priscilla when she was in a genuine, total pinch, and see what kind of face she made saying words of thanks.

"Knowing that girl, she'll probably just say you took too long, or that it was a bother, I suppose."

"I can picture it exactly, but that's exactly why I need to see it for myself. Abel, that's my policy."

"—. Do as you please."

Somehow, it struck Subaru as a kind of dry run for Priscilla's own thankless response, but he exchanged glances and nods with everyone besides Abel.

For a moment, he felt a pull to wait for Emilia and the others to catch up — the ones who, he'd heard, had been joined by the past Vollachia Emperor whom Halibel had once seen off. But—

"Can't let a moment like this slip by just 'cause I'm feeling homesick for them! Let's go!"

"I suppose." "Uh!" "Let's do that, then." "Don't fall behind, now."

"Scatter!!"

—For Viva the Dissector, this life after resurrection was like a dream.

Those revived as Undead followed the will of their sorcerer for a variety of reasons.

Many of them felt an aversion to fighting to destroy their homeland, and so had most of their sense of self stripped away, placed under a suggestion that directed an instinctive violent impulse toward the living.

On the other hand, a certain number had received almost none of that mental manipulation.

For instance, those whose abilities might be drastically impaired by such mental tampering were left with only the lightest suggestion — just enough, at minimum, that they wouldn't defy their sorcerer's wishes.

Viva, too, was one of those for whom the effect on the mind had been kept to a bare minimum.

But that wasn't because the sorcerer wanted to avoid weakening Viva's abilities. —It was because Viva had not one shred of reason to defy the sorcerer's will.

Did not opposing the bearer of the Great Disaster, who wished to destroy the Vollachia Empire, mean Viva harbored some hatred for the Empire?

Unfortunately, the answer to that question had to be no.

Where Viva's aims aligned with the Great Disaster's bearer had nothing to do with the Vollachia Empire's survival. —It was the pursuit of the Soul, and the nature of its being.

Viva came to be called "the Dissector" because, as a warrior of the Vollachia Empire, he fought countless enemies and took apart their bodies by the thousands, by the tens of thousands.

But dissecting body after body, without regard for whether the person was alive or dead, wasn't because Viva harbored some perverse taste for it. It was for the sake of inquiry.

At the far end of all that dissection, what Viva truly wanted to confirm wasn't flesh and blood, but the Soul.

The individual differences between lives, what distinguished the strong from the weak, the distinction between man and woman, what made an individual an individual — these were things Viva wanted to know, and so he dissected many lives.

Even after all that, in his living days Viva never managed even the faintest, Fingers'-worth of result in sensing or touching the Soul. He died with regret unfulfilled. That was the whole of Viva's life.

But in dying and being revived, Viva saw beyond the hope that should have been cut short.

By being revived through the power of the Witch, who twisted the workings of the Soul to her own ends, he himself came to understand one fragment of the Soul he'd never so much as glimpsed while alive. And the Witch had granted him the chance to put that fragment of understanding into practice, to grow it into a larger fragment still.

That was why this era was, for Viva, an age of miracles.

"More, more, more and more and more, more more more more more, I want to try more, dazo."

The Witch who had revived Viva affirmed his insatiable spirit of inquiry.

Given the right, within the castle, to toy with lives to his heart's content in order to probe the Soul, Viva had been furnished with an endless supply of test subjects he could never have hoped for in life, and an environment where the more he did, the more he was praised for it — he was, quite literally, savoring an age of glory.

For the sake of this inquiry, it didn't matter how many lives were lost.

If anything, the more that died, the more the variety of material he could use over and over again increased — so the more he killed, the more Viva's wish was fulfilled.

And to that end, there was still a great many more lives that needed taking—

"—Viva the Dissector."

"――――"

"Your deeds, both in life and in death, are unfit to look upon. Therefore, even knowing your name, I grant you no option but to be burned."

A red flash of the blade swept out, and before he knew it, his field of vision was spinning end over end through the air.

Viva realized this was the result of his own head being severed only when, amid that spinning view, he spotted his own headless body and watched it burst into flame.

It wasn't only the body that burned — the severed head, too.

It was an overwhelming force that reminded him of the young boy who had once killed him, but the decisive difference was that the flame brought by this crimson blade reached even beyond death.

Tasting the sensation of his Soul itself burning, Viva felt the greatest joy he had known since his revival.

"Ah, I see it now, dazo — where the Soul lies—"

As his flesh, his life, his Soul burned away, Viva was satisfied with the result.

Even after killing so many, dissecting their bodies, and endlessly toying with lives over and over, laughing at what a roundabout path it had been, when trying it on himself would have been the quickest way all along.

—For the group storming the Crystal Palace, the fighting had reached a fever pitch of ferocity.

A horde of the dead stood blocking their way as if refusing the living entry, and the bizarre forms of those dead, welling up in seemingly inexhaustible numbers, were horrific enough that most anyone would have hesitated to press on.

An Undead they'd already encountered, one called "Giant Eye" Izmail, had wandered the capital in a form far removed from its original shape, and the flavor here was close to that.

If there was a difference, it was that, unlike Izmail, whose form had been remade chaotically, driven purely by destructive impulse, the grotesque shapes of these dead had a concept behind them.

More aggressive, more cutting-edge, more avant-garde — molding the clay however curiosity led.

Except what this being molded wasn't clay, but life. And on top of that, it possessed the knowledge and sense to consider just how best to express that curiosity.

Hence—

"—Viva the Dissector."

Whenever Abel called out a name, it was proof that Spica's Stardevour was ready.

So any foe that could be defeated with Stardevour would be defeated with Stardevour. That was the unspoken understanding, or so Subaru had thought.

"――――"

And yet, Abel had cleanly severed the head of the very foe he'd just named, with the Sunlight Sword.

Naturally, the Sunlight Sword's power, unleashed without mercy, set the foe's Soul ablaze. —How much suffering that entailed, Subaru, who had never had his own Soul burned, couldn't say.

But the very fact that Abel had cut down with the Sunlight Sword a foe he could have felled without causing suffering was, in itself, a clear expression of his anger toward that foe.

"Scary, scary. But I get where His Majesty's comin' from, too."

Halibel, commenting on what could fairly be called Abel's execution swing, opened his thread-thin eyes just a fraction, his golden gaze gliding through the air as he cut through the thick of the enemy.

What stood in the way of Halibel's smooth movements were grotesque Undead that looked modeled after long-armed, long-legged giants, but they were no match at all for the strongest man in Kararagi.

Their long arms, their long legs, snapped without mercy, over a dozen of them together sank waist-deep into ground that had gone soft as if melted, rendering them immobile.

It was probably some kind of earth-melting technique, though it was hard to tell how it differed from Magic.

And what's more—

"Now don't push yourself too hard, y'hear."

"Changed this much from their original shape, I don't reckon you'd even manage to tell their names."

"Wasn't fond of makin' a kid fight to begin with, anyhow."

That Halibel could do the exact same thing, three of him at once, was simply beyond the norm.

Subaru had known Halibel could split into clones, but seeing it up close all over again, it felt like flat-out cheating. Clone techniques were already strong on their own — the horror was having someone of near-top-tier strength wield it.

"Betty seriously thinks it's a good thing you're on our side, I suppose."

Beatrice loosed purple arrows from her outstretched hand, halting the Undead closing in with them, sharing the same thought about Halibel as Subaru.

Just as Halibel had said, against Undead whose shapes had been so drastically warped, so far removed from their originals, Spica was unable to land an effective blow.

"Uu!"

"I know, it's frustrating, right. But don't rush, don't get impatient, don't panic. I'd rather thin their numbers out here than get surrounded after we're crammed inside the castle."

Soothing the growling Spica, Subaru fixed his eyes on the misshapen Undead.

It was a battle where Beatrice and Halibel held them off, and Abel whittled down their numbers — using Stardevour on any he could name, and the Sunlight Sword on any he couldn't.

Fortunately, it seemed the ones with warped shapes weren't being churned out one after another, systematically, without end.

Conversely, that meant they'd been revived as Undead first, then reshaped by hand, through some means, into those forms afterward — though that didn't make it any less sickening.

"Still, the fact that beating them once means we can free them from that state..."

Subaru was just about to say it was a mercy, when—

"—Subaru!!"

"—!"

At Beatrice's voice, her face drained of color, Subaru's whole body stiffened.

Beatrice's round eyes were wide open, and Subaru understood at once what she was reacting to. That was how unmistakable the disturbance was.

"Uu-uau!"

Spica, witnessing the same disturbance, pointed and cried out at its cause — the Crystal Palace, slowly beginning to glow across its entire form with a pale white light.

The castle was wrapping itself in radiance, but there was no time to admire its beauty.

For the Crystal Palace to be glowing like this could only mean—

"—They've activated the Magic Crystal Cannon!"

Spinning around swiftly, cutting down two Undead front and back in the same motion, Abel called this out the moment he saw the Crystal Palace's glow.

The Magic Crystal Cannon — the very thing mentioned just moments before, a weapon of army-annihilating power fired using the Magic Crystals scattered across the entire Crystal Palace and the power stored within them.

At the light heralding its discharge, Subaru caught his breath.

"Are we the target!?"

"No! Mowing down the ground right at the castle's own feet with the Magic Crystal Cannon would be far too reckless, even for foes who can revive as Undead without limit! The target is something else!"

"――――"

Hearing Abel's shout, Subaru's thoughts flared hot and began racing.

Abel's guess that the target wasn't them was, far from a relief, actually worse. If the target had simply been Subaru and the others, dealing with even being blown away would have been simple by comparison.

What was truly irrecoverable for Subaru was for an ally to die somewhere beyond his reach.

He had never once experienced the Magic Crystal Cannon actually being fired.

Having come this far, past defeating Sphinx, he could not let it be fired before they rejoined their allies.

Stop the Magic Crystal Cannon. But there wasn't time.

There was no doubt this light was the charge before firing, but not a single precondition for stopping it had been met. No good. No good, no good, no good, no good, no good.

He couldn't let that thing fire—

"—Stopping the launch itself is impossible, I suppose."

"――――"

Beatrice said this to Subaru as his thoughts spun furiously.

Blinking, Subaru looked at Beatrice. And though she'd just said it was impossible, she looked back at him with eyes that hadn't lost a shred of their strength.

To that partner of his, the most adorable and dependable in the whole world, Subaru forced his cheeks into something like a grin, and said,

"Bea-ko, will you go reckless with me?"

"Honestly, Subaru can't do a single thing without Betty, I suppose."

"Yeah, that's actually the truth of it."

So saying, he re-gripped her hand — a hand hardly different in size from his own now — as if to make sure of it.

△▼△▼△▼△

The crimson sword swept out, tracing along the ground, and the plaza before the Crystal Palace burst into flame.

The raging red flames roared like a beast, blocking the path of those who had leapt across the divide between life and death, letting none of them advance further, permitting no interference.

"Leaving only the Emperor at the front line, is it. —Insolent lot."

Leaving that curse behind him, a shadow tore furiously away from the Crystal Palace, which continued to grow brighter.

Seen from a distance, it looked like nothing more than a black shadow streaking past — but it was a jet-black-furred Wolf Human, one renowned as among the strongest in the world, running with everything he had.

Once the Wolf Human running like that had built up enough speed from his sprint—

"Alright then, go on and give it everything you've got——!"

As Halibel, sprinting straight ahead full-tilt, kicked off the ground, another Halibel waiting ahead used his own clasped hands as a foothold and hurled his body into the sky with tremendous force.

Halibel shot upward like an arrow, like a bullet — but it still wasn't over.

Next, two more Halibels leapt from the buildings to either side, closing in to catch up, and each pressed one foot against the sole of the first Halibel's foot as he arced through the air, saying—

"Not done yet."

"Gotta give it my all, or Ana-bou'll have my hide."

The kicks of the second and third Halibels sent the first Halibel flying even higher into the sky.

Halibel went hurtling diagonally upward, faster and faster, and once that momentum reached its peak height, he braked his posture in midair and said,

"This is as far as I can buy you. Think you can manage from here?"

"—Yeah, that helped a ton!"

At that answer, the Halibel holding the three of them let out a satisfied chuckle. Then, arching his back with all his might, he hurled Subaru and the others even further.

Using his clone technique, the four Halibels working in concert bought them distance and altitude all at once, and Subaru, holding Beatrice and Spica tight in each arm, went flying.

With altitude thus sufficiently gained, buffeted by fierce winds over his entire body, Subaru still forced his eyes open and looked at the Crystal Palace, now grown small with distance.

The Crystal Palace was pushing its white light to the absolute limit, and Subaru fixed his eyes firmly on which direction the Magic Crystal Cannon's aim was pointed—

"—Spica!"

"Uau!!"

He tilted his head toward the direction the Cannon faced, and in the next instant, Spica's Teleport activated.

Spica, carrying Subaru and Beatrice along with her, Teleported — stacking the jumps again and again, closing the distance to the Cannon's line of fire all at once,

"Eao!"

"Honestly! I told you no! —Muraku!"

In answer to Spica's desperate cry, the Shade Magic Beatrice cast made Subaru and the others weightless.

Now that Subaru and Beatrice had been made utterly weightless, Spica twisted her body in midair and gave them one last, forceful kick, as if delivering a final push.

Catching Spica spinning away, out of the corner of his eye, Subaru gripped Beatrice's hand tight in his own, and said—

"Abel, Halibel-san, Spica...!"

Calling out the names of the comrades who had worked together to bring them this far, Subaru clenched his back teeth hard and opened his eyes wide.

In the next instant, the Crystal Palace's light flared with one especially intense flash—

"Bea-ko, I love you!"

"No need to say it, I suppose."

Holding each other tight, Subaru and Beatrice faced the light head-on.

And then—

"—Al Shamak."

The light of the Magic Crystal Cannon was swallowed into a vast, vast rift that opened in the sky.
