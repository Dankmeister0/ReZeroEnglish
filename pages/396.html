Arc 5 Chapter 79: "The Sage's Watchtower"

Sorry for the delay. I have a feeling — not entirely unfounded — that the deadly busy stretch might finally be winding down, so I'd like to get the update pace back on track.

"The Sage... Shaula..."

The name that fell from Anastasia's lips sent a murmur rippling through the assembly hall.

As everyone exchanged glances, faces disbelieving what they'd heard, Subaru alone was left behind, utterly lost.

Wearing a troubled expression, Subaru gave Emilia, seated beside him, a couple of pokes on the shoulder.

"Hey, um, is this Sage Shaula person famous or something?"

"...Subaru, didn't you read stories about the 'Witch' when you were learning your letters? I'm pretty sure that fairy tale was in one of the many books you went through."

"The 'Witch' from a fairy tale... oh, right, the picture books. Now that you mention it, I think there was a story about a 'Witch' in there."

Prompted by Emilia, Subaru dredged up the old memory from a year ago.

It had nearly been buried under the whirlwind of days since, but he'd studied his letters from children's picture books a year ago — right around when he'd first been taken in at Roswaal Manor.

It had been a collection of the world's most popular fairy tales, and among them had indeed been one about the "Witch of Envy." That said—

"Honestly, it wasn't detailed enough for me to remember much of anything. It was more like, 'once upon a time, in a certain place, a witch did some bad stuff,' that level."

"Was it really that vague...? Um, let's see."

"—Long ago, a symbol of terror who brought chaos and ruin to this world: the 'Witch of Envy.'"

Emilia was still puzzling over how best to explain things to the openly ignorant Subaru when Julius, seated across from her, spoke up in her place.

"What remains on record is only that she was a half-elf who wielded overwhelming magical power to command shadows, and possessed a personality of the utmost cruelty and cold-bloodedness. Beyond that, all that's known is her name — Satella. Even now, the scars she left upon the world remain vivid."

"...Huh."

Subaru gave a small nod at Julius's halting, deliberately unemotional explanation.

The very effort he put into keeping his delivery flat only made his true feelings all the more painfully apparent, but no one in the room remarked on it, and his explanation continued regardless.

"At the Great Waterfall in the far east of Lugunica, there is a shrine built from Evil Sealing Stones. That witch remains sealed within it to this day, still exhaling a vast amount of miasma."

"...I'd heard she couldn't be destroyed outright. But still, managing to seal away a witch that powerful is impressive."

"That's where the Sage's name from earlier comes into play."

Julius nodded at Subaru's question.

The knight touched the sword at his hip and turned his gaze toward the edge of the round table — toward the red-haired young man.

"Four hundred years ago, three heroes contributed to the sealing of the 'Witch of Envy.' One of them, the 'Sword Saint,' was named Reid Astrea — the child of the sword who first bore the title and the 'Divine Protection of Sword Saint' that Reinhard now carries on."

"There are also records claiming that Reid Astrea, the first 'Sword Saint,' never actually received the Divine Protection. Not everything in the well-known legends is necessarily fact. Of course, it's historical truth that Lord Reid built up what House Astrea and the name 'Sword Saint' are today."

Reinhard, the descendant of the man in question, added this in response to Julius's glance.

And yet, for someone discussing an ancestor's history-making feat, Reinhard's expression as he spoke seemed strangely troubled. It looked partly like concern for Julius, and partly like reluctance to dwell on the topic of the Divine Protection he himself carried.

In any case—

"So, the Sword Saint had a Sage among his companions, that's the gist of it?"

"To be precise, it was Sage Shaula and the Divine Dragon Volcanica... you know, the dragon protecting the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica. Joining forces in that battle to seal the 'Witch of Envy' is apparently what led to the promise that has Volcanica still watching over the kingdom today."

Subaru had heard the name of the "Divine Dragon" Volcanica many times before, at the declaration ceremony for the Royal Selection.

As he recalled, the Dragon's Blood was said to bring fertility to barren land, cure any illness, and grant either tremendous power or tremendous vigor — some outrageous claim along those lines.

"It's customary to refer to the 'Sword Saint,' the 'Sage,' and the 'Divine Dragon' together as the Three Great Heroes. You'd do well to remember that."

"Ohh, that's got a nice ring to it... Got it, thanks for the rundown."

Subaru raised a hand to Julius and Emilia, then glanced over at Beatrice. The girl noticed his look and slowly shook her head.

Unfortunately, it seemed the legend from four hundred years ago was beyond even Beatrice's knowledge.

Beatrice, an artificial spirit created by Echidna, was hopelessly uninformed about anything beyond what was needed to maintain the Forbidden Archive. Having spent far too long shut away, she was also quite out of touch with worldly affairs, so it made sense she wouldn't know the finer details.

"Sorry for stalling the discussion. Let's keep going. So, about this Sage Shaula, the real matter at hand..."

Apologizing for derailing the conversation, Subaru took the initiative to steer things back on track. But partway through, he noticed something off and couldn't help but tilt his head.

He now understood the background of Shaula, the hero — the "Sage" — from four hundred years ago, but—

"Wait, hold on, she's still alive? It's been four hundred years."

"Is that really so strange...? I mean, I'm actually about a hundred myself..."

"—!?"

At Subaru's mutter, Emilia pressed a finger to her lips with a puzzled look. Her remark set off a small stir in the assembly hall, but for Subaru, it landed as a "well, fair point."

Thinking it over calmly, Emilia the half-elf was, in actual years, over a hundred, and Beatrice was a loli of around four hundred. Puck had said something about being four hundred too, if he remembered right — which meant the average age within the Emilia Camp might well hover somewhere around a hundred.

"That's a pretty shocking fact, but... anyway, moving on to whether the Sage is alive or not—"

"She's alive. That much seems certain."

"Seems...?"

Subaru knit his brow at Julius's less-than-crisp answer.

But Julius wasn't the only one being evasive — everyone around them shared the same air. Ferris, Reinhard, and the rest of the Royal Guard in particular wore especially troubled expressions.

"Uh, what's going on here?"

"Her whereabouts are known, and her survival has, in all likelihood, been confirmed as well. But no one has managed to actually exchange a word with the Sage herself... I suppose that's the situation."

"That makes even less sense — what's going on?"

Her location was known, and — vaguely — her survival had been confirmed. And yet, contact was impossible.

That was about how Subaru would have summed it up, but—

"Currently, Sage Shaula has built a tower near the 'Witch's Shrine' and has remained holed up inside it ever since, keeping watch to deter anyone plotting the 'Witch of Envy's' resurrection. Ever since that time, without pause."

"...Four hundred years?"

"Four hundred years."

It was a staggering thing to hear.

Beatrice, who'd spent four hundred years shut up in the Forbidden Archive, was already remarkable enough, but this Sage Shaula was quite the stubborn one too.

"The tower where Sage Shaula lives is called the Pleiades Watchtower. And there, day and night, the good Sage has been fighting nonstop to stop the Witch's revival."

"...Right, I've got some thoughts about that tower's name, but never mind, go on."

"'Go on,' you say, but that's basically the end of it. Sage Shaula works herself to the bone, keeping watch over the shrine forever for the sake of world peace — the end."

Ferris clapped his hands together with a "the end," his face the picture of ill humor.

Even calling it "the end" clearly hadn't wrapped anything up, as his attitude made obvious, and it certainly didn't explain the funeral-like mood hanging over the hall.

Naturally, there had to be some reason for it—

"So, um, is she some kind of problem person?"

"Sage Shaula, keeping watch over the Witch for the sake of world peace... that name's known far and wide, all over the world. I knew it myself even back when I was in Kararagi. But at the same time, Sage Shaula's known for somethin' else, too."

"—?"

Anastasia gave a soft, elegant smile and let a beat pass before continuing.

Then, to Subaru, who was already sensing something unpleasant coming, she said:

"Sage Shaula trusts not a single soul — an extreme case of misanthropy. Anyone who approaches the shrine or the watchtower, no matter their purpose, gets wiped out to the last man, or so they say."

—According to Anastasia's explanation, this was the situation.

Sage Shaula had built the Pleiades Watchtower and kept unbroken watch over the "Witch's Shrine."

Many attempts had been made over the years, by many different challengers, to make contact and establish some kind of exchange with the Sage — but every single one had failed and come to nothing.

And the reason for that was none other than Sage Shaula's own interference.

"The Great Waterfall at the eastern edge of Lugunica... the 'Witch's Shrine' and the watchtower are apparently in the same general area, connected by land. But from the Sage's point of view, there's no way to tell what anyone approaching is actually after."

So the Sage, without distinction, wiped out anyone — whether a depraved Witch Cultist trying to break into the "Witch's Shrine," or someone hoping for a friendly approach to the Sage at the watchtower.

Good or evil, friend or foe, righteous intent or malice — none of it mattered, because it was simply the safest course.

As a result, not a single case of successful contact with Sage Shaula had ever been reported, nor recorded, in these four hundred years.

"Buuut, since anyone who gets close still gets attacked, over and over without end, that's proof enough Sage Shaula's still up in that tower, nya."

"That's one hell of an inconvenient Sage..."

"Not really, nya. Truth is, there are way more Witch Cultists trying to get near the shrine than Subaru-kyun would ever imagine. And the good Sage, knocking 'em out one after another, is doing a solid job protecting her one real goal — stopping the 'Witch of Envy's' revival."

Even so, despite offering a favorable opinion, Ferris's expression never brightened. That same unresolved look was shared by every member of the Royal Guard present, which suggested the whole order might once have been given a painful lesson by the Sage on some large scale.

Meanwhile, Subaru found himself nodding along to Ferris's diplomatic take.

Viewed one way — as someone who let the ends justify any means — she was an absolute nuisance. But given how vile the Witch Cult was, such caution was only natural.

If anything, it was precisely because someone had kept up that level of vigilance against the "Witch of Envy" all this time that the world could be said to have some measure of a safeguard against her.

"The true nature of some hermit who despairs of the world and cowers in fear for as long as time allows matters not to me. What matters is the fact that you are borrowing from my finite time. If this idle chatter continues, I shall return to the inn forthwith. Schult must knead my feet."

"Y-yes, ma'am! Priscilla-sama has worked ever so hard and is terribly tired! I shall tend to her with all my heart, that I will!"

Schult answered, red-faced, as he was pulled close and half-buried in her ample bosom. Priscilla, stroking the boy's head with a wicked smile, had never seen much value in this discussion to begin with.

That she'd sat through this much of it without throwing a fit was practically a miracle in itself.

"Don't like lumpin' myself in with Her Highness over there, but yeah, I feel the same — let's get to the point already. Spit it out about the Sage."

Felt demanded this, her face impatient, agreeing it was time to cut to the heart of things.

Sharing that sentiment, Subaru turned back to Anastasia.

"Sorry we keep derailing this, but — what about this hermit-ish Sage, exactly?"

"Aaand that finally brings us back to where we started."

Anastasia clapped her hands. Then, gently touching the fox fur around her neck, she swept her gaze across every face at the round table.

"—Everyone already knows Sage Shaula contributed to sealing the 'Witch of Envy' with her unmatched wisdom and magical power. And she's said to have insight broad enough to see clear across the world, and knowledge deep enough to know just about everything there is. If both of those are true, no exaggeration... don't ya think she'd probably know a way to deal with the Witch Cult's mischief too?"

"...But that's still just wishful thinking on our part, right?"

"So, are you sayin' you're against it, Natsuki-kun?"

It was a question answered with another question, and Anastasia's counter wasn't an easy one to respond to.

Purely going off the title of "Sage," turning to her honestly seemed like a reasonable option. Subaru didn't think it was a bad idea on that front. Someone whose achievements had endured in legend might genuinely know some way to resist the Witch Factors of the Deadly Sins.

But a separate unease had its grip on Subaru and wouldn't let go.

The name of the "Sage" Shaula, and the Pleiades Watchtower where she dwelled — those two keywords were what kept him from accepting the proposal at face value.

Because both "Shaula" and "Pleiades" were words all too familiar to Subaru.

"—"

In the modern knowledge Subaru remembered, Shaula was the name of a second-magnitude star in the constellation Scorpius.

The Pleiades in "Pleiades Watchtower" was even more blatant — it was the name of a star cluster, the Pleiades, whose Japanese name was "Subaru."

Of course, he didn't think it was literally pointing at Natsuki Subaru himself, but in this world, the mere appearance of a star name he recognized was enough to max out his guard.

The Witch Cult's Sin Archbishops — Petelgeuse, Regulus, Sirius, Capella, Alphard, Batenkaitos.

Given that every Sin Archbishop whose name was known so far bore the name of a star from Subaru's own world, it was simply impossible not to approach Sage Shaula and the Pleiades Watchtower with some preconceptions.

"Setting aside Subaru-kyun's objections for now..."

Unable to come up with an effective counterargument, Subaru fell silent, and Ferris cut in on his behalf. He held a finger to his cheek, keeping up his usual manner apart from the sharpness in his eyes.

"The idea of visiting Sage Shaula might be fine and all, but isn't the how of it the real problem? Is there even a way to do it? Nobody's ever managed to reach the watchtower."

"When you say nobody can reach it, you mean 'cause the Sage is too strong?"

Felt asked, sitting cross-legged in her chair, picking up on the implication in Ferris's words. She shot a sidelong glance at Reinhard.

If it really did just come down to the Sage's strength being out of reach, then—

"Ain't this something Reinhard could handle no problem? Strength's about all he's good for, but if it's just about bein' strong, he's amazing."

"It's rare for you to compliment me, Felt-sama. Thank you."

"See, this is what I mean."

Felt clicked her tongue in irritation at Reinhard's reply. But right after, Reinhard's brows drooped in a troubled way.

He went on, prefacing apologetically with, "However..."

"Unfortunately, I was unable to reach the watchtower myself. I lacked the strength for it."

"...If this guy calls himself lacking, ain't that a little terrifying?"

"I don't mean lacking in combat strength, Felt-sama. There's no domain in this world beyond Reinhard's skill. But the Pleiades Watchtower is a different kind of obstacle entirely."

"You sure know a lot about it... well, guess that's just natural if you were a Royal Guard."

Ferris looked uncomfortable as Julius came to Reinhard's defense. Watching the awkward tension among the three former Royal Guards out of the corner of his eye, Subaru turned to Reinhard.

"What was the goal, trying to meet the Sage?"

"It was an order from the kingdom. To find a cure for an illness. — This was two years ago."

"Two years ago..."

The mention of an illness and the phrase "two years ago" together let Subaru piece together the background.

Two years back was right around the time when, one after another, members of the royal family had reportedly fallen to an illness of unknown cause at the royal castle. An epidemic with no known cure — most likely, what Reinhard had been ordered to do was make contact with the Sage in hopes of learning how to treat it.

But it had never come to pass, and that was precisely why all three former Royal Guards wore such grim faces.

"The watchtower and the shrine are located at the Great Waterfall on Lugunica's eastern edge — within the Augria Sand Dunes that spread out around it. You can spot the watchtower from a distance, even from the entrance to the dunes. So you'd think you couldn't possibly lose sight of the place, but..."

"But?"

"Strange phenomena occur constantly throughout the dunes, and they prevent anyone from approaching the watchtower. One theory holds that the miasma leaking from the shrine is the cause."

"On top of that, drawn in by the miasma, the Augria Sand Dunes have become a den of witchbeasts. In lands saturated with miasma, witchbeasts grow ferocious and resilient on an entirely different scale. In that sense too, heading out there is considered suicide to begin with."

"So it's a maze of a desert, complete with a witchbeast den. Yeah, that's hell, all right..."

On top of the Sage's own troublesome nature, there was a desert that even Reinhard couldn't cross, teeming with countless witchbeasts prowling it — there were simply too many reasons to give up.

It made perfect sense that contact with the Sage had never once been achieved in four hundred years.

"—But what if there were a way to cross through that hell?"

"—"

Just as a gloomy mood began settling over the room, the sudden remark made everyone look up.

Anastasia wore a triumphant smile, as if to say she'd cut in at precisely the perfect moment. Then she nodded, making sure everyone could see it.

"That's exactly why I bothered bringin' up the Sage's name in the first place."

"A way to reach a Sage who's been in hiding for four hundred years, and Anastasia-sama from Kararagi just happens to know it, right at this moment? ...I wonder what method that could possibly be."

"You're wastin' that cute face of yours. No need to look so prickly, I'll explain it properly."

Anastasia deflected Ferris's needling with ease and lifted the fur off from around her neck.

Then, spreading the fur out on the round table, she lifted its head.

"I mentioned it before the defensive battle, remember — my artificial spirit, Echidna. This little one knows the way to the Pleiades Watchtower. — Which means we can go meet the Sage up there."

"—"

Subaru caught his breath at Anastasia's declaration.

The artificial spirit Echidna, this being disguised as a fox fur stole, was the key to reaching the Sage.

"—Goodness, all this attention is a bit much. You're making me blush."

So said the white fox spirit, raising its head.

Just like the Witch whose name she borrowed, exactly how far Subaru could trust her remained an unknown.
