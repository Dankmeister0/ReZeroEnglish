Arc 5 Chapter 32: "The City Hall Capture Meeting"

"Oh, come on! The healing magic isn't working! To think we've got no choice but to treat her with such a primitive method, nya...!"

Grinding his teeth in irritation, blood spattered across his cheek, Ferris swung his arms.

Before him, Mimi lay limp on the makeshift bed, her breath thin and ragged, blood still seeping from her chest.

He pressed a magically-purified cloth to the wound and wound bandage after bandage around it, doing everything he could to stanch the bleeding. If the wound had been on a limb, elevating it above the heart might have slowed the blood loss — but Mimi's wound was in her chest, directly over the heart itself.

How much time she had left could only be said to rest on her own strength to survive.

Watching the desperate treatment unfold, Subaru's attention kept drifting to Garfiel, who had slumped against the wall. Unable to look at Mimi, Garfiel hung his head, blood-caked fingers buried in his short blond hair — and his own condition was in no state to be optimistic about, either.

It wasn't only Mimi's blood on him — Garfiel himself bore no small number of wounds. The blood wiped from around his mouth where he'd coughed some up, and the bleeding at his left shoulder and right knee, were especially gruesome. His trousers were torn clean open at the knee, the flesh beneath gouged away badly enough that the white of bone showed through.

"Garfiel. For now, we've got no choice but to leave Mimi to Ferris. You need to treat your own wounds too. Can you cast healing magic on yourself?"

"...Yeah."

At Subaru's words Garfiel dipped his chin, then slowly laid his palm over his wounds and began sending healing mana into his own body. Once Subaru had confirmed the wounds were gradually closing, he lowered his eyes slowly to the dialogue mirror in his hand.

The old swordsman reflected there wore a troubled expression on his wrinkled face, saying nothing.

Subaru couldn't claim to know the full depth of the conflict stirring within him, only its surface. Surely Wilhelm had arrived at the same thought Subaru had.

"A wound that won't close, huh..."

"Nine times out of ten, it's safe to assume it's a wound from the Divine Protection of the 'Reaper.'"

Picking up where Subaru left off, Wilhelm spoke the conclusion he'd already reached.

The Divine Protection of the "Reaper" was a fearsome power that laid an unhealing curse upon any wound its bearer inflicted.

It seemed safe to conclude that the reason magic couldn't close the wound in Mimi's chest was the influence of that very Divine Protection.

And as far as Subaru knew, there was only one person who came to mind as a bearer of that Protection.

Of course, he couldn't say for certain that the same Protection couldn't reside in someone else, but—

"Wilhelm-san. I'm scared to even ask this, but... how's the wound on your arm?"

"—"

At Subaru's question, Wilhelm closed his eyes. Then he removed his coat and quietly showed Subaru the area of his left shoulder.

Bandaged tightly, there was no conspicuous trace of blood to be seen. Could he take that to mean there was no bleeding at all?

"Even supposing whoever gave me this wound bears the same Protection as my wife, the fact that my wound hasn't reopened means it cannot be her. Though that much, of course, should go without saying."

"Wilhelm-san..."

Subaru couldn't tell, from the way Wilhelm's voice had lowered, whether he was disappointed or relieved.

As far as Wilhelm was concerned, his wife was someone he'd believed lost fifteen years ago. Even if the events of a year past had cast doubt on her death, that wasn't the same as being able to hold on to hope.

Yet even if unconditional hope was difficult to hold, believing in some small hope was simply human weakness. Even if Wilhelm carried that kind of weakness, Subaru didn't think it anything to be ashamed of.

So in this moment, Subaru couldn't find words to offer Wilhelm. And Wilhelm, for his part, wasn't looking for easy comfort or encouragement either.

So while Subaru fell still, unsure what to do, a change stirred behind him.

It was—

"Hey, kid. Sorry to bug ya while you're still tendin' that wound, but got a minute?"

It was Ricardo who spoke, dropping himself heavily onto the floor.

The beastman, who'd been standing near Ferris during his desperate treatment, sat down in front of Garfiel with his fur soaked in fresh blood in various places, glaring at him with sharp eyes.

Under that gaze, Garfiel slowly raised his head.

"Dunno exactly what happened. But if you hadn't carried her back here, Mimi'd be dead for sure. So, here."

"—"

"Thank you. Really. I owe ya one."

Ricardo planted both fists on the floor and bowed his head deeply.

Watching Ricardo press his forehead nearly to the floor, thanking him for bringing back someone who was as good as family, Garfiel's face went blank with astonishment.

Mimi's condition still allowed for no optimism. Subaru understood the guilt Garfiel must be carrying for failing to protect her fully. But that guilt was misplaced — none of this was Garfiel's fault. What point was there in blaming him?

If Subaru was honest, Ricardo too must have prayed for Mimi to come home safe. There was no way his heart could be at peace seeing her return in that state.

And yet, in the way he still bowed his head, Subaru saw the sincerity at the core of Ricardo's heart.

At the same time, he felt an unbearable anger toward whoever had done that to Mimi.

So—

"Garfiel. It might be hard to talk about, but tell me what happened. It's hard to imagine anything beating you this badly. And besides..."

Even as he pressed Garfiel for an explanation, a thought crossed Subaru's mind.

It was the same thought that had flickered through his head earlier, when he'd been sorting through information with Julius and Wilhelm and weighing the do-or-die situation Priestella now found itself in.

The Witch Cult had seized five key points across the city, holding the lives of everyone within it hostage.

If their assumption was correct — that five powerful figures each controlled one of those key points — then alongside Wrath, Greed, and Lust, there was a strong likelihood that Gluttony was among them too.

—And it was Gluttony, above all, who was the target Subaru could never afford to let slip away.

Greed, who had taken Emilia. Wrath, who hunted Subaru because of his ties to Petelgeuse. Lust, whose glimpses of a hideous humanity had already shown through. And Gluttony, the one with whom Subaru shared a bitter history.

The situation was without question the worst it could be — but at the same time, it was also the greatest opportunity there could be.

Now, caught as they were in the web the spider had spun, was the perfect chance to hunt the spider down.

"Somehow, we've gotta take down every single one of them. Otherwise, there's no way all of us get out of here safe."

"—"

At Subaru's declaration, Garfiel stared at him, wide-eyed with surprise.

His expression looked as if he'd been struck from a direction he never saw coming, and Subaru nodded back firmly.

There wasn't a shred of light visible yet. The situation was the worst it could be, and things were completely deadlocked.

But that was no reason to give up — and for Subaru, this was simply how things always went.

As always, he'd thread the needle through conditions as narrow as a needle's eye, and everyone would come through safely together.

Because from here on, it was up to him to set the actions in motion that would make that happen.

"...After I heard that broadcast, yours truly headed for city hall smack in the middle of town, with the squirt in tow. Figured we'd go beat the snot outta whoever owned that damn unlikeable voice."

"We were just talkin' about how that was the only move we had left too. Guess you two beat us to it."

"Whole way to city hall, there weren't no watchmen, nothin' gettin' in our way. So yours truly figured we'd just bust straight in... and that's when—"

Garfiel cut himself off, his fists and fangs trembling.

It wasn't fear — it was anger. Though Subaru suspected the edge of it wasn't aimed at his enemies, but probably at himself.

Garfiel clacked his fangs together hard once, biting down, and let out a hot breath before continuing.

"Two enemies showed up. One's a huge guy carryin' two huge swords on his back. Other's some skinny woman, gripping a long sword. Both of 'em, if it came down to a straight fight, probably about even with yours truly... no. Probably stronger than me."

"Stronger than you? Seriously? Is one of them the one behind that broadcast?"

"...Don't think so."

It was a story that made him want to doubt his own ears.

Garfiel was the strongest fighting force in the Emilia Camp, and among all the members mustered to stand against the Witch Cult, he ranked at the very top in raw ability.

And here he was, declaring that not one but two opponents were stronger than himself. If his words could be trusted, that meant they weren't even Sin Archbishops — just ordinary Witch Cultists.

"Didn't feel none of that rotten malice off those two, like what was in that broadcast voice. Even when yours truly was wide open, they didn't cut me down. Maybe that was... I dunno, some kinda swordsman's code of honor or somethin'."

Having faced them directly, Garfiel seemed to hold something close to reverence for their opponents' strength.

His uncharacteristically shrunken bearing made it plain that what had happened to Mimi still weighed heavily on him.

Having listened to Garfiel's account, Ricardo slapped his own knee with a dry crack and stood up. Then he grabbed Garfiel by the shoulder.

"Whether they're strong or not, I got that plenty clear already. What I wanna know is, if those two are the ones who did this to you, which one did that to Mimi. Which one do I gotta cut down to settle the score. Just tell me that."

"...The one who cut the squirt was the woman. But that's exactly why that woman—"

"—Might I ask that you leave that woman to me?"

Ricardo, burning for vengeance over Mimi, and Garfiel, equally sworn to avenge her. It was Wilhelm, who had been silently listening through the mirror, who cut in between them.

There was no question the information had been more than he could simply let pass unremarked. But asking the other two, who knew nothing of Wilhelm's circumstances, to understand that was asking too much.

"Why? Ain't got nothin' to do with you, Wilhelm-han. No matter who you are, you got no right to get in the way of me avengin' my precious family."

"The circumstances... I cannot speak of until I have certainty. But if it is as I suspect, that woman and I share a deep history. That being so, this is the one thing I absolutely cannot yield."

"Listen here... even you, if you push me to anger, I won't hold back either."

Ricardo's fur bristled with irritation, but Wilhelm, too, would not budge from his resolve.

Understanding both their circumstances, Subaru honestly couldn't say which of them was in the right. So it wasn't Subaru who put a stop to their exchange, but—

"—Wilhelm. And Ricardo-sama as well. This is no time for allies to be quarreling among themselves. Every life in this city, every one of our people, is in danger."

"Crusch-sama..."

In a dignified voice, her cheeks set firm with resolve, Crusch scolded the two of them.

At the rebuke from his liege, Wilhelm's face showed shame, and Ricardo scratched at his head — blood still up — before crossing his arms roughly.

With the powder-keg quarrel between allies averted for the moment,

"Alright, alright, let's wrap this up then."

Anastasia clapped her hands lightly, took control of the mirror's discussion, and pointed at Subaru.

She smiled at Subaru, who looked a bit taken aback, and toyed with her fox fur stole as she spoke.

"First off, about our opening counterattack — Natsuki-kun's proposal to strike city hall first. Crusch-san and I both back that. There oughta be people captured at city hall who know the city's systems well, so there might be a way to deal with the waterway even without takin' back the control tower. Bit of wishful thinkin', maybe, but still."

"No, I feel the same way. Besides, if they start moving on us hard and our forces start gettin' whittled down, our options get fewer too. If we're gonna move, sooner's better."

"...Wow, you really have gotten dependable this past year. Anyway, it's like Natsuki-kun says. Right now, thanks to these dialogue mirrors, we can coordinate across all three locations. Luckily, seventy percent of our total forces can move right away. We should be able to pull off the strike on city hall."

At Anastasia's opinion, Subaru glanced briefly at Garfiel and Ricardo.

Seizing city hall all at once would require committing a commensurate amount of force.

As things stood, the forces available for the assault on city hall were Garfiel and Ricardo from this shelter, and presumably Julius and Wilhelm from another.

Members of the Iron Fang, along with Ton and Kan. Gathering other adventurers who happened to be staying in the city too, they should still be able to bolster their numbers further.

"If I'm honest, having Reinhard would make this perfect. ...Can't we ask Ton and Kan to call Reinhard out?"

"About that — it's a strange situation."

Julius answered Subaru's thought of relying on their greatest and strongest asset. He glanced toward what seemed to be the two thugs at the same shelter before continuing.

"Before entering the shelter, it seems those two fired a spell into the sky once, just as they'd been instructed beforehand. But Reinhard, who should have come running, hasn't shown himself. And there's something else, not a very pleasant story..."

"'But' what. Honestly, I'd rather not hear any more of this."

"Then take it without reservation, and share the same feeling I did. —Felt-sama's two attendants parted ways with Felt-sama and Reinhard shortly before this all began. And it seems that, at the time they parted, the two of them were speaking with a red-haired man."

"A red-haired man... don't tell me that's that bastard."

"I'm afraid I can neither confirm nor deny that from my own mouth."

Listening to Julius's genteel answer, Subaru ground his teeth in indignation.

If the person Felt and the others had met with was, as Subaru imagined, Reinhard's father — Heinkel — then what could the two of them possibly have gone to talk to that man about?

And why, even now, in the midst of all this, was there no sign of him moving?

"But that guy showed up at the spot for Sirius's speech, in the previous loop. ...What's different? Does that mean he could move before he heard the broadcast?"

Subaru couldn't clearly pin down what condition had changed between then and now.

Even so, learning that Reinhard couldn't be called on right now was more than enough to leave him thoroughly discouraged.

Just as Subaru's shoulders slumped, Ferris came back from deeper within the shelter. His face drenched in sweat, his women's clothing stained with dark, blackish blood, he said,

"...Phew. I've done everything I can do."

"So, how's Mimi? Is she gonna make it? She's gonna make it, right?"

Ricardo pressed in on Ferris, who was wiping the sweat from his brow, breathing hard through his nose. Behind him, still unable to stand, Garfiel wore a face full of desperate hope as well.

But to those two pleading gazes, Ferris shook his head, mercilessly.

"I'm not sayin' she won't make it, nya. But the fact her wound won't close hasn't changed, nya. Right now I've forced the link inside her — the one running through her Divine Protection with her little brothers — to strengthen, and that's barely holding things together, but..."

"You mean the Divine Protection of 'Threefold Split'? So what does that mean?"

"Originally, the triplets have a Protection that lets them share fatigue and injuries between them. I forced that link stronger from my end, so more of her critical wound gets shouldered onto her brothers than normal. That's bought her more time before her life runs out, but..."

"—That means when Onee-chan's life runs out, we die too, right?"

A high, breathless voice reached them from the mirror.

Ricardo scowled and peered into the dialogue mirror, where Hetaro and Tivey sat side by side, reflected. Both of them clutched their chests in obvious pain.

"You idiots. Seriously, you're a couple of hopeless idiots."

"...But, thinking that this is the same suffering Onee-chan's feeling, it kind of makes me happy, being able to share it with her a little."

"I'm not as hardcore about it as Onii-chan. So, Captain, I'm trusting you to fix this soon. Because if I die, I'm coming back to haunt you."

The two younger brothers, sharing the damage of their sister's wound, suffering the same grievous injury in kind.

Having watched Hetaro and Tivey lie down at the other shelter, Ricardo let out a deep breath and shouldered his cleaver.

And then—

"...If we're doin' this, it's gotta be fast. Otherwise there's no point. Right?"

Growling in a low voice, Ricardo was, by now, probably beyond anyone's power to stop.

And there wasn't a single heartless soul in this place who couldn't understand the surge of feeling driving him.

"I'll send out the Iron Fang from my end to secure the road to city hall. After that, ideally we send our elites charging straight into the keep and take it all in one go. For now, the enemy's that huge man and the thin woman — plus, most likely, 'Lust.'"

"Our elites here would be Garfiel and Ricardo. Plus Wilhelm-san and Julius."

"—I will be joining as well."

It was Crusch who spoke up, rising to her feet with her hair tied back neatly.

She held a longsword in hand, having removed the skirt portion of her dress to reveal knee-length trousers beneath, showing her readiness to fight.

"Joining, you say — can you actually fight, Crusch-san?"

"Not as much as before, perhaps, but I trained under Wilhelm. I also have the power of the Divine Protection of 'Wind Reading.' I don't intend to become a burden."

Crusch's skill before losing her memory had been enough to let her fight admirably even against the White Whale. But her current ability, now that her memories were gone, was an unknown quantity to Subaru.

Honestly, given that her personality now carried a more conspicuously feminine gentleness compared to before, Subaru had assumed her aptitude for combat had been lost along with it, but—

"Crusch-sama's natural gift for the sword has not diminished in the slightest. On that point, I can vouch personally."

As if to dispel Subaru's doubts, Wilhelm gave his firm assurance. The old swordsman nodded, his eyes turning through the mirror to gaze at his liege, Crusch.

"That said, please do not push yourself beyond your limits. I ask that you place your own safety first, above all else."

"To bear wounds and shed blood before the people is the duty of nobility. If innocent people are weeping, who could stand back merely to spare themselves? I will fight, Wilhelm."

"...Honestly. It's precisely because you are the way you are that I offered you my sword in the first place."

To Wilhelm's earnest counsel, Crusch answered with unwavering resolve. Seeing Wilhelm dip his chin in satisfaction at his liege's reply, Ferris shot his hand up.

"Me! Me! Feri-chan! If Crusch-sama is going, Feri-chan wants to come too! Please let me accompany you!"

"Ferris, you're to go around the various shelters and cast healing magic on those who need treatment. I'm grateful for the sentiment. But you mustn't lose sight of your purpose. Don't mistake where the battlefield you should be fighting on truly is."

"Nn, ngh..."

Falling silent in frustration, Ferris racked his brain desperately for a rebuttal. But there was no talking his way around Crusch, whose argument was the very soul of reason, and he raised a white flag with a face on the verge of tears.

"Wil-jii. You'd better protect Crusch-sama, absolutely. I mean it, absolutely absolutely."

"Yes, I understand. Even at the cost of my life — even should I burn this life of mine to ash right here — I will, without fail."

In the answer Wilhelm gave, entrusted with that charge, ran a thread of grim, tragic resolve.

On this side, Ricardo gave the cleaver on his shoulder a light swing, while Garfiel, treatment finished, had risen to his feet even as he still leaned his back against the wall.

Through the mirror, the scene showed Wilhelm standing sharp with his sword at his hip, and Julius taking his place calmly beside Crusch, now changed into her fighting attire.

The force setting out to strike city hall numbered five in total.

On Anastasia's orders, the Iron Fang would hold the Witch Cultists in check and clear the way.

The hour of decisive battle had come — and so, Natsuki Subaru too.

"Ngh, gooohh...!"

"H-hold on, Subaru-kyun, what are you doing!?"

Gritting his back teeth against the pain in his right leg, Subaru somehow forced himself up onto his feet.

Seeing Subaru straining a leg whose flesh hadn't even finished knitting back together, Ferris rushed over in a panic and smacked him hard on the head.

"Ow, hey."

"Of course it hurts! I told you absolute bed rest, so why d'you keep pullin' reckless stunts like this, nya!? Are you cursed to always defy Feri-chan's medical orders or somethin', Subaru-kyun? Your leg could just tear right off and it wouldn't even be strange, nya!"

"Even if it tears off and falls right off, there's still something I've gotta do. Ferris, even you should understand how I feel. Are you telling me to just lie here quietly and wait for good news to fall in my lap?"

"...Mm."

At Subaru pressing him, Ferris pouted and fell into a stammering silence.

Sending his comrades off to a place that might become their grave, and waiting for the outcome — Subaru couldn't bear treating himself that way. If working his clever little head might give him the power to help save someone, how could he possibly just lie here?

"You get to fight your fight by healing people. So I should get to fight mine too. Beatrice protected me, and Emilia's still in danger from 'Greed' right now. Are you really telling me to just stay quietly out of the way in a state like this?"

"...Can you really say you won't regret it, even if your leg comes off, nya?"

"I'll regret it, sure. But I'd regret it a whole lot more if I didn't go."

"Haaah. ...Well, if you're gonna do it, at least keep lookin' cool right to the end, nya."

Ferris pressed a hand to his forehead with an exhausted expression, letting out an exasperated sigh.

Then, breathing hard through his nose, he flicked Subaru's forehead — Subaru still bearing down on his pain — knocking his head back before gently laying a hand on the wound on his right leg.

"What I'm about to do is really, truly just a stopgap measure, nya, got it?"

"A stopgap... ah, wait, hold on, Ferris-san. The wound really hurts, so if you dig into it like that just wait, ow ow ow ow... wait, it doesn't hurt?"

Ferris dug his claws roughly into the wound, tormenting Subaru — but then a faint light seeped from the wound through his right leg, and the pain that had felt like a knife gouging into him rapidly receded.

Startled by the unexpected effect of the magic, Subaru looked up at Ferris's face.

"Trump card."

"S-seriously...!? What, if you had such a convenient spell, don't hold out on me, use it sooner! Alright, alright, I can move!"

With Ferris sticking out his tongue and playing coy, Subaru sprang lightly up onto his right leg. Delighting in a leg that felt no pain, he stepped in place. Pain, movement, no problem.

He slapped a palm against the wound, welcoming this astonishing change. Then he felt something wet and sticky against his palm and looked down at his hand. It was bright red. The wound on his right leg had torn open again.

"H-hey hey hey hey!? Wasn't this supposed to be healed!?"

"I never said I healed it, nya. I asked if you'd regret it even if your leg came off, didn't I, nya. All Feri-chan did was erase the sense of touch, pain included, from Subaru-kyun's right leg. As long as you're careful not to actually let your leg come off, I think you can manage running around and all."

As Subaru stared unsettled at his bleeding leg, Ferris rewrapped it with fresh bandages and cast his spell again. Even with the bleeding stopped, unease mounted in Subaru at a leg that transmitted no sensation at all.

It was close to being anesthetized, but unlike anesthesia, his movement wouldn't grow sluggish. Purely the sense of touch in his right leg had vanished; ordinary movement remained possible.

But pain was also a limiter, meant to keep the body from being pushed past its limits. Removing that for one's own convenience meant—

"Obviously, at this point you're definitely overdoing it. Once this whole mess is settled, you're absolutely gonna have some kind of aftereffect. If you wanna keep it light, you'd better be careful, nya!"

"...Got it. Thanks. I owe you."

"...Subaru-kyun definitely, absolutely doesn't listen to a word Feri-chan says, does he, nya."

At Subaru nodding as he tested the condition of his right leg, Ferris puffed out his cheeks and turned his face away.

Subaru wanted to tell him that wasn't true, but whether he could actually follow Ferris's words was something he wouldn't know until the moment came.

Better not to make promises he couldn't keep. Subaru thanked Ferris once more, then hurried over to Garfiel and Ricardo.

"So, that's the deal — I'm coming too. No use trying to stop me. Sure, I might not be much of a fighting force, but there's stuff I can do too..."

"Stop you? Why would I stop you. Havin' you along's worth a hundred men. I'm countin' on ya."

"Stuff I can do... huh?"

Having braced himself to be turned down, Subaru instead met with an unexpected welcome, left feeling like the wind had been knocked out of his argument before it even started.

Subaru looked back at Ricardo, wondering just what was going on, and the beastman opened his mouth wide.

"Back at the White Whale, and with 'Sloth' too — I saw you bustin' your ass, kid. If you think Wilhelm-han's the only one who rates you, you're dead wrong. I look where it counts too. Well, whether I actually like ya depends on the coin, though."

"O-oh yeah? Feels kinda weird, somehow."

Encouraged by Ricardo's words, Subaru's participation was accepted without any trouble at all.

Before leaving the shelter, Subaru stopped by Beatrice's bedside and gently stroked the forehead of the quietly sleeping girl.

"Beatrice, I'm heading out. For all I made you push yourself, it's my turn to give it everything now. I'll make sure to beat them, every one, and get Emilia back. Just rest easy here for me."

"—"

There was no answer. Drawing strength from nothing but her peaceful breathing, Subaru rose to his feet.

Beside him, Garfiel and Ricardo were speaking to Mimi as she lay in evident pain. Unconscious, Mimi had no strength left to answer, but on the two men's faces, in colors that contrasted with one another, a fierce resolve could clearly be seen welling up.

"We leave the shelter and rendezvous at the great waterway that leads to city hall. —Everyone, stay sharp."

Before setting out, they exchanged glances with one another, and everyone rose, each swearing to fight their hardest.

The battle to reclaim Priestella — the assault on the central city hall.

Two swordsmen lay in wait, along with the Sin Archbishop of Lust.

Each carrying their own private resolve, the warriors stepped forward, out onto the battlefield.
