Arc 3 Extra: "The King"

—A story of a miracle that began one day, on a whim of Subaru's.

"So, Emilia-tan, you're studying to become queen, right?"

Amid the after-dinner chatter, in the center of the dining hall where the manor's principal members had gathered, Subaru's question cut oddly clean through a lull in the noise.

That it wasn't brushed aside, that its opening line reached every ear in the room—that was the first mischief of fate.

At Subaru's question, the one who tilted her head was Emilia, seated beside him. She combed her fingers through her own silver hair, while with her other hand she poked at Puck sitting on the table.

"That's right, but why bring it up all of a sudden?"

"It's not really sudden. It's been sitting vaguely in the back of my head this whole time. Like, hey, the girl sitting next to me making a face over green peppers is actually in this ridiculous position where she might end up becoming queen someday."

"Don't call it the green demon, Peppir."

In retaliation for his little joke, she poked his forehead with her finger.

At the small, sharp sensation, Subaru put a hand to his forehead, and behind the hardness of her nail he thought he could feel her softness, which left him inwardly quite pleased. Ram, passing by and catching sight of him out of the corner of her eye, let steam curl up from the teacup on the small tray in her hand.

"What a slack-jawed face."

She spat the barb out and kept walking.

Kneading his own cheek at the remark, Subaru bared his teeth like a child. "Guh—!"

"Don't go passing by at weird times and catching people mid-smirk. Also, only bringing after-dinner tea to Roz-cchi and nobody else is way too much favoritism!"

"Favoritism? This is simply, clearly, a matter of priority within Ram's own ranking. Roswaal-sama first, then Emilia-sama and the Great Spirit-sama, and then, past an insurmountable wall, through a hole dug deep and clean through the other side... well, I suppose Barusu might be allowed in there too."

"I've never once experienced being that far behind the top tier of anything!"

Chest puffed out, Ram calmly ignored Subaru's outcry and glided smoothly over to Roswaal. Roswaal, who had been glancing at a book in the seat of honor, noticed her and took the offered tea with practiced ease, raising it to his lips. He let it rest on his tongue, savoring it.

"Hmm, yes, after a meal, nothing settles things quite like Ram's te-e-ea. One might say I li-i-ive for this single cup, don't you think?"

"You do me too much honor."

Ram's demeanor was quiet, thoroughly obedient—but only where Roswaal was concerned.

Shown such a blatant difference in treatment, it was strange how it drained the will right out of picking a fight over it. Not that swallowing his complaints did anything to dissolve Subaru's simmering discontent, of course.

"Please don't be too upset. It was Rem who imposed on Sister, Subaru-kun."

Speaking up, the one gently scolding Subaru's sulky face was a girl in a maid's uniform stepping out from the kitchen—Rem, carrying a large tray with tea sets for everyone.

She smiled at Subaru as he turned around, lightly lifting the tray.

"I wanted very much for Subaru-kun to have tea made by Rem. I had Sister give me a lesson again, and prepared it myself."

"Ho-ho, now that's some admirable service spirit, Rem. I'll gladly taste your resolve!"

"Yes, please do. Under Sister's supervision, and with the resolve to give it my all even to the point of coughing blood—prepared with the mindset that even if I never brewed tea again, it wouldn't matter, that I was staking everything on this one cup. Please, drink."

"That's a lot of soul-grinding resolve for such a casual tea break!?"

Nodding at Subaru's astonishment, Rem set her all-or-nothing tea down on the table. Gently steaming, it looked at first glance no different from ordinary tea, but it was tea brewed at the cost of what might as well have been blood, sweat, and tears. Surely the iron content alone was incredible.

"Well, no use overthinking it. Here goes one sip— mm, tasty! There's definitely something, some kind of flavor, that feels like it might not be entirely impossible to say is somehow subtly, faintly different from usual, in a good way, mountains of it, practically!"

"So does that mean it's good? Which is it?"

Emilia tilted her head at Subaru's momentum-driven commentary, but honestly, as far as impressions went, it was tasty. Tasty, but the difference from the usual tea still wasn't exactly clear. To begin with, drinking something as fancy as black tea or coffee was rare for Subaru in the first place. He had a child's palate, so back in his original world his experience was mostly limited to soft drinks, or coffee-flavored milk at best.

"Come to think of it, it's been ages since I've had cola or anything. Carbonation's made of carbon dioxide, right? Wonder if there's some way to make that here too."

"Subaru-kun, Subaru-kun, how was the tea?"

"Oh? Tasty! Guess it really does change the flavor when there's feeling put into it!"

"Really!? ...And, um, are you perhaps starting to feel feverish at all, Subaru-kun?"

"Feverish...? No, I don't think anything's changed."

Tilting his head, Subaru frowned at the oddness of Rem's remark.

The slightly strong flavor of the tea was quite good, but it wasn't as though it had caused any dramatic change in Subaru's body. Just a pleasant warmth added to a full stomach after a meal. At his answer, Rem stared at Subaru intently, then hid her mouth behind the tray with a slightly dissatisfied look.

"As long as Subaru-kun is pleased, that's all that matters."

"I feel like your face, your words, your eye color, and that question just now don't quite line up, though?"

"That's not true at all. More importantly, if you were satisfied with the taste of the tea, wouldn't it be fine to praise Rem for showing such an eager spirit of self-improvement?"

"Suddenly remembering to be a lovable little thing, aren't you! Well, fine. Come 'ere."

Beckoning Rem, who kept glancing over hoping for praise, Subaru stroked her blue hair with his palm as she leaned in close. Wagging an invisible tail, she closed her eyes happily, giving a small sniff and nuzzling closer, which made his heart race.

And then, having forgotten his original purpose, Subaru felt a gaze pierce the back of his head.

"Staaare."

"Even glaring at me with a silent, sulky look like that doesn't dim a pretty girl's prettiness one bit, you know. ...What's up?"

"Don't 'what's up' me. Having a conversation cut off in the middle bothers me a whooole lot."

"Conversation?"

Subaru put a finger to his lips, making a genuinely puzzled face. Even Emilia's eyes narrowed a little at that, and Subaru hastily threw up both hands.

"Sorry, sorry, my bad, forgive me!"

"I'll forgive you and listen, so go ahead and say it. Come on, on the count of three."

Prompted by her outstretched palm, Subaru gave a wry smile and scratched his head. Then, reluctant to let it go, he muttered "ahh" before continuing.

"Emilia-tan's aiming to become queen, right?"

"I have a feeling this is going to go the same way as before..."

"It branches off from here. And if we're talking about being queen, sure, ability matters, but shouldn't there be just as much emphasis on mindset?"

"Mindset..."

Blinking at Subaru's remark, Emilia murmured softly, looking surprised.

She had probably expected him to come at this from some completely unrelated angle. Part of him did want to live up to that expectation, but for now he'd settle for having caught her off guard enough to see that surprised face.

Fortunately, after the surprise, what surfaced in Emilia's eyes was something like agreement—an "that's a fair point" kind of feeling.

Seeing that, Subaru snapped his fingers lightly. "Right?"

"The resolve to stand above others. The strength of spirit to endure the pressure of carrying people's hopes. And above all, the strong will to see one's own convictions through to the end—! If you're going to call yourself queen, I insist these elements are absolutely essential!"

Fist clenched tight, Subaru rose from his seat and made his declaration in a loud voice.

Naturally, the eyes of everyone in the dining hall turned to Subaru, and he swept his gaze around the room, bathed in a complicated mixture of amusement, exasperation, surprise, and affection.

Hesitantly, Emilia raised a hand at him.

"I get what you're saying, Subaru, but... what do you want to do with that claim?"

"Simple. Emilia-tan, who's aiming to become queen, can't do without these elements right now. So, why don't we go ahead and train up that royal mindset of yours a bit?"

"Umm...?"

Leaning in close and snapping his fingers, Subaru left Emilia blinking, unable to keep up. Grinning inwardly at her being left behind, Subaru twisted his cheek into a smirk—this was riding a wave he could carry through on momentum.

Wearing a wicked grin, Subaru rudely planted his foot on his chair, rising one level higher. From that height he surveyed the room, thrust a finger to the heavens in a pose, and shouted.

"I hereby declare the opening of the first ever—the tick-tock, ticking clock of destiny edition of the 'Roswaal Manor King Game'!!"

"The rules are simple! Prepare one lot for each person, with one marked as the king and the rest just numbered blanks. Everyone draws a lot, and whoever draws the king picks a number and gives them an order. The order is absolute—whatever the king says goes. That's the rule!"

Subaru explained the rules of the "King Game" in the middle of the dining hall.

Listening to the simplified explanation, Roswaal put a hand to his chin and nodded, looking impressed. He smiled, satisfied with Subaru's brief summary.

"I sa-a-ay, how interesting. A ni-i-ice, easy-to-understand rule, isn't it."

"Simple, but with just the right amount of tension—that's the heart of this game."

Giving Roswaal a thumbs-up in response, Subaru glanced over at Rem, who was busily preparing lots for everyone beside him. Not that he doubted her, but in a game like this, fairness was the thing that mattered most of all.

Winning purely on luck alone—if you wanted to be cynical and say that royal status is mostly decided by bloodline anyway, there could hardly be a more fitting game than this.

In any case—

"The lots are ready."

"Right, let's see... yeah, no problem. Let's go with this!"

He checked the lots Rem handed over.

Numbers were written on wooden chopstick-like sticks, and only the "king" lot bore a deformed hand-drawn picture of Puck, made by Subaru himself. Looking closely, there was no telling trick or distinguishing mark on the lots—it should proceed without a hitch.

"So whoever draws Puck is the king?"

"Yep. And the king announces themselves and names a number. At that point, everyone besides the king has to be careful not to let their number be seen. Not knowing is what makes it fun."

"Oh my. Then I can't single out Barusu to boss around."

"That's exactly the kind of person this rule exists because of! No naming names allowed either!"

Snapping the words at Ram, Subaru let out a breath and swept his gaze around the table. Naturally, everyone had gathered together for the after-dinner chat.

Emilia, Rem, Ram, Roswaal, and Puck, plus Subaru—

"Wait, hold on, where'd Beako go? She was definitely there in the corner just now, desperately picking green-pea-like vegetables out of her omelette rice."

"If you mean Beatrice-sama, she slipped out of the dining hall just around when Subaru-kun tried standing on his chair."

"That drill-loli... spoiling the harmony, is she. Right, hold on a sec."

Announcing this, Subaru dashed out of the dining hall.

And exactly two minutes later—

"Yo, I'm back."

"I honestly can't believe what a terrifyingly accurate hunch you have! Betty didn't want to get dragged into this, I suppose!"

"Yeah yeah, that's exactly how a needy little thing talks trash like that. All right, that makes everyone."

He set Beatrice, carried on his shoulder, down into her seat, pinning both her shoulders from above to cut off any escape, and even she finally let out a sigh of resignation. Perhaps for the sake of some small peace of mind, she moved over to where Puck was and took up a spot beside the little cat, shooting Subaru a sharp look.

Letting that keen gaze roll off him from right to left, this time everything really was ready.

"Here."

"Yes."

When Subaru held out his hand, Rem set a lot in it as though she'd known exactly what he needed. Feeling the lots—now exactly one more, for Beatrice's share—Subaru's mouth curved into a smile, though his narrow, sharp eyes only grew more wickedly keen.

The lots were placed in the center of the table; Puck flailed about chaotically shuffling them, and once the numbered ends were hidden, everyone reached out their hands.

And then—

"Right, everybody's got one? Then, the customary chant— who's the king?"

Subaru gave the call with a full grin, and everyone pulled their lots at once.

Looking down at the one drawn into his own hand, Subaru found, to his great disappointment, the character for "2" written on it.

Once he knew his own kingship was impossible, the only remaining question was who among the others had become king—and then, hesitantly, a hand rose.

"Ah, mine has Puck's picture on it."

—and there was Emilia, showing off some excellent luck at just the right moment.

Holding the deformed drawing of Puck, Emilia looked at Subaru with an expression mixing surprise and mild confusion.

"Um, so what do I do with this?"

"You're the king now, Emilia-tan, so you can go ahead and boldly play the tyrant. Like, number two pats the king's head, or number two holds the king's hand, or number two naps with their head on the king's lap, or number two feeds the king 'say ah,' or number two promises the king a super passionate date..."

"Then, number two takes the Peppir off the king's dinner plate and eats it all."

"Nooooo! Steering failed! I'm number two—!"

Head in his hands, Subaru let out an anguished cry, and in front of him Emilia said, "Ah, so Subaru's number two," looking honestly surprised, as if she hadn't realized it before.

Still, an order was an order. Only by upholding it was the dignity of the King Game preserved.

"Though, if peppers don't show up at dinner, there's no burden on me at all, mind you!"

"Rem. I'd like the dinner menu to make ge-e-enerous use of Peppir. A ta-a-able dyed all green would be a most fi-i-itting sort of feast, wouldn't you say?"

"Roswaal, you bastard... how quick you are to grasp the whole point of the King Game!"

Glaring at Roswaal for sealing off his escape route, Subaru then looked to Rem. Caught between her master and Subaru, one might have expected her to be forced into an agonizing decision, but instead—

"I do have feelings that want to prioritize Subaru-kun's wishes. I do, but Roswaal-sama is Rem's master. So I can't very well disregard his words... I'm sorry."

"What's the real reason?"

"When Emilia-sama eats Peppir, she's more noticeable so people don't tend to notice, but Subaru-kun also makes a face he dislikes it, so I thought I'd use this opportunity to correct that."

"You're paying way too much attention to me!"

Looking and tasting almost exactly like green peppers, Peppir was one of the vegetables Subaru, with his childish palate, disliked. In front of Emilia he pretended to eat it fine, but inwardly it was one of the ingredients he wanted to avoid. There were, in fact, quite a few vegetables he secretly disliked—tomatoes, eggplant, turnips, and so on. —Though Rem probably already knew that too.

"Anyway, round two, let's go. Come on, who's the king!"

"Ah, that's me."

Subaru shouted somewhat out of desperation, but once again his lot came up blank.

Following Emilia's lead in strong luck was the little gray cat—Puck—and holding the lot, nearly the same size as himself, in both paws, he tilted his head.

"Naming names directly is against the rules, right? In that case... how about number five flicking number one's forehead?"

"Whoa, pulling off the two-number designation trick right out of the gate... You're no amateur, are you?"

"Is there really an amateur or a pro at a game like this? ...And, let's see, number five and number one are..."

Puck's black eyes darted around, and two hands rose, two lots. Ram held the one marked "5" at the tip, and Emilia held the one marked "1."

In this case—

"So it works out to Ram flicking Emilia-sama's forehead. Ah, what an irreverent situation. A flick to Emilia-sama's forehead... the King Game is terrifying...!"

"Even as you say that, Ram, you look super pumped, practice-swinging... practice-swinging? Is a practice swing even a thing for this? ...But please, d-don't make it hurt, okay?"

"If it doesn't hurt, it isn't a flick. There we go."

"Yeowww! Ouch! That reeeally hurt!"

A red mark was left on Emilia's adorable forehead, and Ram watched with satisfaction before relaxing her shoulders. Half in tears, Emilia looked put out, but this brisk lack of ceremony felt somehow like the very essence of the King Game, and by the game's rules, Ram's attitude was the more admirable one.

"Now you can either seek vengeance on Ram out of a lingering grudge, or move on with a clean slate. —All right, round three! Who's the king!"

If the turn didn't come around to everyone, some people would fade into the background.

Given the boisterous nature of the King Game, luck could skew unevenly, and that was a situation the host wanted to avoid—but such small consideration was, for the moment,

"—Destiny... draaaaaaaaaw!"

nothing but a trivial matter to Subaru, who had just drawn "King."

Clenching his fist in a pose of triumph, Subaru repeated "YES, YES, YES!" in gratitude for his own luck.

"I-Is it really that exciting? You've only been king once so far."

"You can't really talk about the King Game until you've been king, Emilia-tan! All right, let's do this, let's do this... though, coming out the gate at full throttle and killing the mood isn't great either, right, yeah, not great."

His mouth twitched with barely contained glee, but as they say, the hasty beggar gets less alms.

Better to hold off, stay calm, and first lay a foundation for the rest of the game. First, create a gap in everyone's guard—that is, a lapse in vigilance—and once they'd relaxed, strike hard—

"—Number three, take off just your underwear."

"—Eh?"

He'd been staring at Emilia during the "strike hard" part, and his patience had simply run out.

At Subaru's declaration, the room froze, and everyone stared at him, dumbfounded. But like spit that can't be swallowed back, an order once given couldn't be retracted.

Nodding at the surrounding looks of disbelief, doubting they'd heard right, Subaru slowly opened his mouth.

"Number three. Takes off. Just her underwear. Hurry!"

"Wh-what, wait, what, is an order like that even allowed!?"

"It's allowed! A king's order is absolute, right!? If the king says die, it's a vassal's duty to go die even at the risk of their own life, right!? Right now, all of you are, if only for this moment, vassals to me, the king, right!? So if I say strip, then strip! Strip!"

Pounding the table, Subaru pleaded with a tongue that was gaining more and more momentum by the second.

Emilia's mouth opened and closed like a fish, while Roswaal, watching from the side, burst into laughter. Ram looked at Subaru with utter contempt, and nobody noticed the gleam in Rem's downcast eyes. Emilia panicked, Puck flicked his whiskers. And there was Beatrice, sneaking toward the door trying to slip out of the room—

"Betty. What number were you?"

"I wanted to be left alone, nii-cha!?"

Puck stopped Beatrice as she tried to leave the room, and she spun around with a flustered look. Puck picked up the lot left behind in the seat she'd been sitting in.

"Ah, it's number three. So Betty's the one taking off her underwear. But hey, what would I have done if it'd been me? I'm basically always naked."

"In that case I'll sew you your own custom miniature underwear, and you can put that on and strip. Kind of a pointless exercise, but... anyway, so it's Beako, huh."

Subaru glanced toward Beatrice, standing near the entrance to the dining hall. Her elegant face had gone red, and her small clenched fists trembled.

"Hah... read the room, would you."

"That's the one thing I refuse to accept from you! What an outrageous order to give! There's no reason at all I should have to obey a command like that..."

"Hmm, huh, is that so, is that so. So you're just going to trample all over the King Game's most sacred custom like that, huh. I see, I see, hmm."

Turning his face away, not bothering to hide his utterly disillusioned tone, Subaru's words rattled Beatrice. Watching her out of the corner of his eye, Subaru let out a breath and said, "Never mind."

"Something like this—I think once you've joined in, a trust forms between everyone involved. No matter how harsh the order that comes down, you rise to meet it, as much as effort allows. That kind of... I don't know, mindset, or maybe dignity as a person, something like that."

"Dignity, is it."

Roswaal, not bothering to hide his amusement, let his lips relax into a smile. At his interjection, Subaru nodded fiercely—"Yeah, dignity"—then pointed at Beatrice.

Watching the drill-haired girl flinch slightly at the force of his gesture, he went on.

"It's not about the result. What matters is the will to keep the order... no, to keep the promise! You joined the King Game, and the king gave you an order. That order, in a sense, is the king's trust in you. Being able to do your utmost to answer that trust... that's the bond that's formed between you and me through the King Game."

"————"

"Maybe it's one-sided. This is just my opinion, after all, and maybe you have no reason to go along with it. But still... this is what I think. By confirming our bond through the form of the King Game like this, something invisible—but real—forms between us."

Hand over his heart, Subaru poured his feelings out to her.

Under the weight of his words, the torrent of emotion, Beatrice's lips trembled as she looked down. Watching her with a gaze full of tender affection, Subaru extended a hand.

"So... strip already."

"————"

"Ugh, this is getting tedious, just read the room and hurry up and take off your underwear and sit back down. I'm not expecting some huge reaction or anything, so, come on, isn't this fine already?"

"—Perhaps you should just die."

An instant later, an invisible shockwave sent Subaru flying with tremendous force.

"I confirmed in a separate room that Beatrice-sama took off her underwear and put it back on again. No problem with the rules. That settles it, doesn't it, Barusu."

"...Yeah, no problem."

Ram, back from the next room escorting a red-faced, indignant Beatrice, spoke with plenty of contempt in her voice, and Subaru answered with a sulky face.

He'd been sent flying by Beatrice's embarrassed retaliation (though its power seemed excessive for that), tossed around so hard he'd lost all sense of up and down. Still dizzy, Subaru had been diligently tended to by Rem, who'd rushed over.

"Are you all right, Subaru-kun? You're still barely recovered, not at full strength... Shall we continue this on Rem's lap?"

"Just thinking about a situation during the King Game that's somehow more king-like than the actual king is a new one. That offer's super tempting, but the King Game takes priority for now. I'll take the sentiment, though."

Saying this to the devoted Rem, Subaru sat himself back up, and with the two returnees included, everyone took their seats again. It had been a fairly rough situation, but it seemed no one had dropped out.

Honestly, thinking it over,

"Quite the odd bunch... everyone's surprisingly into playing along, one way or another."

"It would be truly lonely to end things there, wouldn't it. I myself haven't been king or a victim even once yet, you know?"

"'King and victim'—now that's a way of putting it that shows you really get it. ...Beako's back because she's a tsundere, fine, but Emilia-tan, aren't you going to stop this?"

Beatrice shot Subaru a sharp look at his remark, but he coolly ignored it. Emilia, on the other hand, having had the question turned her way, looked flustered. "Eh, ah..."

"That, um, how do I put it... um."

"What, what's up? If there's something you want to say, I'll totally listen."

"You, um, won't laugh... will you?"

"Me, at Emilia-tan? No way. Even if I have to play the fool just to see Emilia-tan's smile, I would absolutely never do something like sneer at Emilia-tan, I suppose."

Beatrice, mocked by the imitation of her speech pattern, shot him a stern look, but he blithely ignored that too. Emilia, half in doubt at Subaru's words—or rather, seeming to come to terms with something inside herself—nodded small.

"I've... never really joined in on something like this with everyone before, so... it's, um, kind of fun... and it'd be a waste to stop."

"What is this, she's adorable."

Subaru couldn't help but drop his act at Emilia's answer, given with lowered eyes and reddened cheeks.

Flustered by a response better than he'd expected, Subaru faced Emilia, who—perhaps thinking she was being teased—puffed out her cheeks.

"See, that's exactly it, teasing me right away like that. It's really, really embarrassing, you know."

"I wasn't teasing you... damn it, what is with this girl. Is she trying to kill me with itchy, frustrated feelings...? I'm impressed...!"

Subaru trembled at just how overwhelming Emilia's combat power was. It seemed the others around them felt something similar—some sending Emilia gentle looks, some resigning themselves and renewing their resolve to play on, and some just glaring straight at Subaru. Various reactions, all around.

In any case, with Emilia's will renewed, they got back into the swing of things.

"Who's the king!"

Everyone pulled their lots at once, glancing down quickly at their own.

In Subaru's hand was the "4" lot. A flicker of an urge to click his tongue at not being king rose and passed, but it also killed the mood if the king kept coming up the same person over and over. Natsuki Subaru was the type to read that kind of air and trample right over it anyway, but doing so right after Emilia's earlier comment took some nerve.

Setting his own inner thoughts aside, among those raising their heads, the one who raised a hand—holding up the lot marked with Puck's picture—was Rem, seated beside Subaru.

Her expression, usually kept as carefully blank as possible, had her eyes shining with an odd brightness.

"It's Rem. I'm the king. I did it, Subaru-kun. I'm the king."

"I mean, I get that you're happy, but you're way too hyped! We gotta keep the orders coming fast or the pace'll be terrible. ...So, Your Majesty, what's your order?"

"Ah, right. How careless of Rem."

Wagging her invisible tail furiously, Rem drooped for a moment, then quickly regained her momentum and looked up again. Staring straight at Subaru, she said,

"Then, how about... number one gives the king a great big hug?"

"It was super obvious who you were aiming for, but I'm not number one!"

When Subaru showed his lot, Rem crumpled where she sat with an expression like it was the end of the world. It was rare to see someone so devastated by the outcome of the King Game, but knowing she was aiming for him made it awkwardly touching too.

Anyway, whoever had drawn number one under Rem's designation now had to hug her, but—

"Rem, come here."

"Sister...!"

Holding up her lot marked "1," Ram beckoned, and Rem looked up, running into the arms Ram spread open for her.

A beautiful scene of sisterly love—one could call it that, at least—but as the elder sister embraced her younger sister, she murmured into her ear,

"Listen, Rem. Barusu's like that, no matter what. A small-minded man who can't even grant one of Rem's wishes. ...Seems this game, meant for deciding a king, has inadvertently exposed that fact."

"Don't go poisoning the mind of one of my few allies! And besides, this isn't a game to decide a king, it's a game a king decides! Don't twist the whole point!"

"Sister... Sister... Rem won't be discouraged."

Being comforted, Rem nuzzled her cheek against her sister's. Watching the affectionate scene between the two beautiful sisters was the kind of thing that could run wild with one's imagination, but restraining that particular feeling within himself, Subaru cleared his throat. On to the next round.

"Right, resetting... Who's the king!"

"Oh my, I've drawn it at last. Now that I'm actually king... it's so-o-omehow surprisingly hard to decide what to order."

Holding the "king" lot, waving it back and forth with a smile, was Roswaal.

At his announcement, a cold sweat trickled down Subaru's forehead. —Because among this group, Roswaal was the one whose behavior as king was hardest to predict.

Since ancient times, those who enjoy the King Game have fallen into a few types.

Like Subaru, the "omnivore type," who simply voices whatever desire comes to mind.

Like Rem, the "carnivore type," who zeroes in on one individual and tries to make the most of it.

Like Puck, the "herbivore type," who tosses out noncommittal, wishy-washy orders and lets things drift.

Like Emilia, the "commensal type," who simply enjoys the game aspect of the King Game itself.

And like Roswaal, the "gourmand type," whose sole aim, regardless of romance or even gently coasting through the game, seems to be reveling in the sight of a victim's suffering.

Not romance, not even a soft-pedaled game, not even any consideration of the possibility of becoming a victim himself—just an existence issuing orders purely for his own amusement. That was the temporary king now holding the "king" lot.

Holding his breath, Subaru prayed that the number on his own "3" lot wouldn't be called. Roswaal glanced briefly at the tense Subaru, then said,

"Well then, number three can down a glass of milk with Tabasca mixed in, in one go, shall we say?"

"Damn you, Roswaal, that's obviously aimed at me!!"

At Roswaal's merciless proclamation, Ram appeared with a mottled, discolored glass of milk at a speed that seemed almost prearranged.

In the cup, generously laced with Tabasca (an otherworldly Tabasco), it gave off a chaotic hue and an unsettling odor.

"...Are you drinking it?"

"The King Game is a bond forged by testing one's dignity as a human being..."

"Once you put it that way... damn it! Natsuki Subaru's going for it—guh, bleh, my throat... burning... aaargh!"

Clutching his burning throat, Subaru collapsed, half in tears, and slammed the drained cup down on the table.

"How's that! I did it, dammit! Got a problem, dammit!?"

"No, no, that's more than enough. Palate cleansers are forbidden, of course—now then, shall we move on to the next game?"

"You bastard, next time I'll make you eat your— King's, who's— Destinyyyyy, draaaaaaaaaw!!"

Anger toward Roswaal shifted into hope for the next game, and the joy of drawing king overrode all of it.

Holding aloft the lot with Puck's drawing, eyes gleaming brightly, Subaru declared,

"Right! Number two! Number two hugs the king's head into their chest! At minimum until the next round! How's that!"

Subaru shouted, glaring straight at Emilia.

There were seven players; with Subaru out as king, that left six, four of whom were women. Emilia was number one, but even if it was Rem, that would still be a perk for the king. Ram would be fine too. Holding up the "2" lot Subaru had named was—

"You got some kind of grudge against me or something—!?"

"That's completely Betty's line, I suppose!? What are you even thinking!?"

Subaru screamed as Beatrice once again drew the number, and a scream was screamed right back at him.

But the King Game was absolute. Having already gotten past even the underwear order, this level of command probably didn't seem all that difficult to Beatrice anymore.

"Well, hurry up and come here, I suppose. Get it over with, and Betty will ask something of nii-cha next time too, I suppose."

"Coming in with my hopes crushed anyway... whoa, you really are tiny! What is this, this is a straight-up crime scene... what's with you? Do you have some status ailment called 'flat chest' or so— ow ow ow ow ow!!"

"Say something stupid like that again and I'll accelerate the time flow on your scalp and make you go completely bald, I suppose. Now then, who's the king, I suppose!"

Somehow, once the initially reluctant Beatrice got into the swing of things, the King Game afterward proceeded at a brisk pace, if not exactly calmly.

Fast-forwarding through some of what followed:

"This time for sure...! Subaru-kun... no, number five, gives the king a kiss on the forehead...!" "Not me, but wow, you're really asking for a lot of physical contact!?" "Ah, that's me. Then, Rem, I'll give you a little peck on the forehead..." "A forehead peck, who even says that these days!"

"Number six goes to the manor's courtyard—which is too small. Why don't you go check the barrier out at the mountain behind us instead? A light... ten round trips, say." "I'll die! Making someone barely recovered do a stunt like that... you, are you targeting me!?"

"Then, number four cleans Ram-sama's room. If even a speck of dust is left, I'll thrash you." "'Ram-sama' is so refreshingly queen-bee of you! Also, you and Roswaal both, targeting me specifically... you're not using clairvoyance, are you!?" "...Hmph, and if I were, what of it, Barusu?" "Don't act all defiant like it's my fault I noticed! I knew something was off!!"

"Destiny again! Now, number four treats the king to a home-cooked meal made with love... you again!!" "Choke down this grilled, stewed, and dried thing, I suppose!" "You two get along so well. Makes me want to have some warm, fuzzy time with Ria too."

"Oh, it's me. Well then, let's have number two feed me some mayonnaise I've been on a real kick about lately, 'say ahh' style." "Well, we-e-ell, for someone who's supposedly a Great Spirit-sama, aren't you quite the spoiled one. Being handed such a great honor is flattering, however-r-r." "Who wants to watch this footage!? You two go do that in a corner somewhere!!"

"Yay, Betty's turn finally came, I suppose. Nii-cha... um, so, number four gives Betty a belly rub. Until Betty's satisfied!" "I've actually got abs, believe it or not!" "Do you have some kind of grudge against Betty, I suppose!?" "I'm the one who wants to lose it! What is it with you, are you my best friend or something!?"

And so, fast-forwarded or not, a fairly bittersweet mix of results kept repeating.

The game itself surpassed twenty rounds, and just as fatigue and a sense of accomplishment were nudging the group toward calling it a night,

"King, me. Um, let's see, hmmm... what should I do?"

Emilia, having drawn king, thought it over with a finger pressed to her lips, looking terribly delighted.

Her cheeks had gone slightly flushed, betraying no small amount of excitement. Combined with her unusually alluring manner, it put Subaru in a strange mood.

In front of him, Emilia gave a single nod. "Okay."

"Then, number six... turns the person of the opposite sex into an animal that matches their image?"

"I'm number six, but... what's the point of that?"

"Mm. No point needed. Come on, Subaru! Quick, quick!"

Emilia tapped the table lightly, urging Subaru along as he tilted his head. Confused by her uncharacteristic manner, Subaru put a hand to his chin and thought it over briefly.

"Well, Ram's a cat, Rem's a dog. Beako's a bear... and Emilia-tan's a rabbit, I guess."

"What kind of logic is that?"

"Just what animal ears would suit everyone, is the conclusion I came to. Fickle Ram gets cat ears, obedient Rem gets dog ears. Lonely, adorable Emilia-tan gets rabbit ears... and Beako's got 'bear' right there in her name, so bear ears seemed fitting."

"I just got the distinct impression Betty alone got assigned completely at random, I suppose!"

"No point agonizing over something like this seriously... huh, Emilia-tan, what are you—"

Waving off Beatrice's indignation without much thought, Subaru noticed Emilia, wearing a loose, unguarded smile, raising both hands. Lifting them up, she gave a laugh—"Ehe"—and waved them.

"I'm not super great at fine details, but how's this?"

"How's this, meaning—oh!"

Light scattered and gathered into Emilia's hands. And in Subaru's astonished field of vision, there suddenly appeared, nestled in Emilia's silver hair—a translucent hairband shaped like a pair of rabbit ears.

Looking closely, one could tell it was made of ice, and it adorned her with just the kind of adorable charm Subaru had imagined, exactly as pictured.

"It's cold." "Is this... the image you had for me, Subaru-kun?" "Honestly, I'm not satisfied, I suppose..."

Just like Emilia with her rabbit ears, ice hairbands shaped into each animal ear Subaru had imagined appeared atop each of the women's heads.

Sharply pointed cat ears reflected Ram's untamed freedom; drooping dog ears reflected Rem's touchingly earnest devotion; round bear ears reflected Beatrice's, all told, rather sweet temperament.

It was such a feast for the eyes that Subaru wanted to drop to his knees and worship the sight, but—

"It's such a pain that my phone battery's too low to take a picture. I'm too crushed about this. At least let me burn it into the shutter of my heart... and, well, that aside—"

While burning the women's gorgeous (from Subaru's point of view) appearance into his retinas, Subaru couldn't hide his astonishment at this transformation in Emilia, who had just pulled this off.

It was a fantastic order, and one could say without exaggeration that it had achieved the very reason he'd started this King Game in the first place—but it was, to an extreme degree, unlike Emilia.

She was usually the type to actively try to stop this sort of thing, and yet—

"Subaru-kun, Subaru-kun."

"What's up, Rem?"

Rem beckoned him over with a little wave, and he leaned in close. She tilted her head, wearing her dog ears.

"Am I cute? If you like, you could pet my head, and dote on me a little..."

"You're cute, and normally that'd be heartwarming, but right now I like the Rem who might solve my confusion even more!"

"I see, that's a shame. In that case, I'd like to resolve that confusion and be praised for it. Emilia-sama's odd behavior is likely the effect of the 'Fannel Fruit.'"

"Fannel Fruit?"

At Subaru's questioning tone, Rem nodded, "Yes," and pulled a small nut out of the pocket of her apron dress.

"This is it. If you grind up this Fannel Fruit into powder and ingest it... it has the effect of making you become brutally honest with yourself. Surprising, isn't it."

"Yeah, surprising. ...So, why would Emilia-tan end up ingesting that?"

"Probably because Rem tried to have Subaru-kun drink tea with Fannel Fruit mixed in, and mistakenly served it to Emilia-sama instead... ah, Subaru-kun, that grinding hurts, it hurts!"

"Punishment for a bad girl! Also, so that's what that reaction was about when you first served the tea!"

Subaru finally understood the real meaning behind Rem's earlier question, asking if he felt "feverish," before the King Game began.

The medicine meant for Subaru had accidentally been drunk by Emilia instead, and the result was her present, delighted state. But if that was the case,

"So becoming honest turns into that, meaning Emilia-tan is actually secretly kind of childish...?"

"What's this, you two getting all cozy together again... nooo faaair."

Seeing Subaru press his knuckles against Rem's head, grinding away at her in punishment, Emilia rocked her body, pouting. She turned her face away with a sulky expression.

"Just when I think you're being nice to me, it's that. Getting my hopes up for nothing... mean. Mean. Meeeaaan."

"I have no idea what's going on but you seem really mad, what do I do!?"

Emilia threw a childish tantrum, collapsing forward onto the table. Faced with a side of her he'd never seen before, Subaru looked around for help, and—

"Well, Barusu... in any case, since you're the one who caused this situation, handle it well."

"That would be best, I suppose. Nii-cha, come here and have some tea with Betty instead."

"Ria's cute like this too, but it's probably safer to keep some distance for now. Well, I'll leave the rest to you, Subaru."

"We-e-ell then, I suppose I'll go back to playing around with paperwork too."

"I'm honestly stunned at how little camaraderie you all have! I'm never relying on any of you again!"

Watching the group abandon their duties and scatter, Subaru saw that the only ones left in the dining hall besides himself were Emilia and Rem.

With only three people total, there was no way to continue the King Game.

"Guess there's no helping it, we'll call it a night for this round. ...The bigger problem is what to do about this Emilia-tan situation."

"No. Nooo! I wanna play more! Play more!"

"More like emotional regression than honesty at this point. ...Kind of makes me sad, thinking the result of being honest is this clumsy way of being spoiled."

Emilia shook her head, refusing to accept the game ending. Subaru reached out to try to calm her somehow, but in her childlike state, she showed no sign of listening.

Finally, leaving a bewildered Subaru behind, Emilia grabbed the lots off the table.

"Here, me, 'king'! Um, number one! Number one has to listen to my order!"

"Emilia-tan, the King Game doesn't really work with just a king and no one else..."

"Since Rem drew number two, that means number one is necessarily Subaru-kun."

"Rem-san!?"

Turning around at this unexpected betrayal, Subaru found Rem waving the "2" lot she held. She held the lot to her lips and murmured, just quietly enough for only Subaru to hear, "Only for now."

"Emilia-sama, please, give your order. Obeying the king's command is a vassal's duty, after all."

"Then, um, number one, um, Subaru..."

"...Go easy on me, okay? My legs are already shaking from running around so much, my throat's still kind of burning since I never got that palate cleanser, and that home-cooked meal after that was rough too."

Confused but bracing himself to accept, Subaru readied himself.

It was rare to see Emilia like this, and given the circumstances, he was ready for pretty much any order. Something that wouldn't leave lasting physical damage or aftereffects would be ideal, but—

"Pet my head."

"Huh?"

Hearing something he hadn't expected, Subaru let out a bewildered sound.

Eyes wide, Emilia looked straight at him.

"Pet my head. Gently, like you always do with Rem."

"Eh, wait, that's it? That's fine?"

"It's fiiine, hurry up. Huuurry."

Emilia began kicking her feet impatiently. Pushed along by her fidgeting, Subaru drew close beside her and slowly reached out toward her long silver hair.

"Is this okay?"

"A little gentler... yeah, like that."

Feeling the silver hair slip smoothly through his fingers, Subaru petted Emilia's head just as she'd asked. It was a relief that the awareness of it being an "order" came first, sparing him from having his nervous trembling betray itself to his fingertips at touching her.

To be allowed this close to her, allowed to touch her hair—he'd never imagined such a thing could come his way without any mental preparation at all.

—Quite the faint-hearted man, considering he'd tried sneaking an indecent order into the King Game in the first place.

And then, just like that,

"...Huh, Emilia-tan?"

"————"

Sensing something off in her silence, he looked at her, only to find that, still slumped forward on the table, she had fallen asleep.

Watching her peaceful sleeping face, seeing something like contentment settle over her profile,

"She's fallen asleep, hasn't she. The Fannel Fruit also causes strong drowsiness."

"I'm not going to press you on what exactly you were planning to do with me after making me honest and sleepy, for now... but is that why you went along with Emilia-tan's King Game?"

"I think Emilia-sama needs, once in a while, some time to truly rest at heart. As it is, the weight she carries on her shoulders is no ordinary burden."

The playful, feigned ignorance from before had vanished, and Rem had returned to her usual, all-capable maid demeanor. She gently stroked the sleeping Emilia's hair, then looked at Subaru.

"I'll show you to her room... will you carry her, Subaru-kun?"

"Given it's a legal chance to touch Emilia-tan, plus the sheer awfulness of the visual of you carrying Emilia-tan, there's no real choice to make here."

With a wry smile, Subaru gently scooped Emilia up into his arms.

Her weight was lighter than her appearance suggested, and even as he marveled at that lightness, Subaru pictured the upstairs floor—her room.

"Might as well have moments like this once in a while, to let your guard down. It was fun, at least."

"Yes, I'd like to do it again sometime. Though there's the cleanup to deal with."

Opening the dining hall door, Rem led the way for Subaru, carrying Emilia.

At her words, he tilted his head and looked back, and sure enough, the dining hall bore a fair amount of visible wreckage as evidence of what had happened.

Afterward, he and Rem would have to join forces to clean up the traces. Ram surely wouldn't help, of course.

"That ice dog ears thing, doesn't it melt? How long are you gonna keep wearing it?"

"It's not made on a level that would melt. ...If Subaru-kun wishes it, I'll keep wearing it forever."

"Cute, but not something to overuse. Show me again at the next King Game."

"—Yes. Then, let's do that. It's a promise."

Rem smiled, happily, and Subaru found himself smiling back despite himself.

Even the expression of the stirring Emilia in his arms looked somehow peaceful, smiling as though she'd been watching the two of them smile all along, and somehow, his heart felt warm.

※※ ※ ※ ※ ※ ※ ※ ※ ※ ※ ※

—This was an event that took place just five days before Subaru and Emilia would come to a falling-out in the royal capital.

Neither Subaru, nor Emilia, nor even Rem had any inkling of the turbulent fate awaiting them beyond this point.

Only, if there is one thing worth setting down here.

—The promise was never kept.

The seven of Roswaal Manor, gathered together like this, playing the King Game—never happened again.
