Arc 6 Chapter 66: A Second Chance Toward the End

—That he had undergone a Return by Death — that was the first fact he had to deeply accept.

"——"

Stroking the head of Beatrice, who gazed up at him worriedly from within his arms, Subaru replayed in his mind the "crisis" that was both the event that had just befallen him and, at the same time, an event yet to come.

That he had Returned by Death meant he had failed to avoid a situation that put lives in mortal danger.

He had left a disaster he'd known was coming unaddressed, and put his companions' lives at risk — no, that assessment was too soft. That wasn't it.

He hadn't merely put their lives at risk. Subaru had gotten Emilia and the others killed.

He'd known it would come to that, and still he had let it slip by.

Five separate obstacles, each impossible to ignore, bearing down on the tower — he'd known about every one of them, and yet his own shallow thinking and complacency had cost him the chance to avoid the crisis.

In the final moment, what had almost certainly dealt the finishing blow to Subaru's life was the black shadow that tore mercilessly through even this massive, formidable tower — that was the greatest, and the last, of the obstacles.

Including that, he ran back through the five obstacles once more.

First was the horde of Witchbeasts converging on the tower all at once.

Second was the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony, coming after Subaru's "memories" and "name."

Third was the giant scorpion that had somehow slipped inside the tower and lurked in blind spots to attack.

Fourth was Reid Astrea, roaming freely through the tower and rampaging without regard for friend or foe.

And fifth, last of all, was the black shadow that swallowed everything and ruined it all.

These crises all proceeded simultaneously, and overlooking even one of them was enough to easily collapse the whole plan.

Perhaps the terrifying flood of jet-black shadow could be called a kindness from this absurd, unreasonable world, letting Subaru know his time limit. If so, it was the very picture of an unwanted favor.

"The first one — the horde of Witchbeasts — I'll leave to Meili and Shaula..."

He could say with certainty that this choice hadn't been wrong.

Last time — no, it was already the time before that now — but in the loops up through the one before last, the five obstacles must have occurred in parallel as well. Among them, Subaru had also witnessed the scenario where Witchbeasts stormed into the tower.

If that happened, either Emilia or Julius — some precious fighting force, in any case — would have to be diverted to holding back the Witchbeasts. Avoiding that was thanks to this loop, the one where Meili had survived.

Because of Meili's presence alone, he could say without doubt that he'd taken one step forward.

But it still wasn't nearly enough.

Even if they managed to hold back the horde of Witchbeasts, Subaru and the others had been powerless against the obstacles that followed.

The Sin Archbishop of Gluttony had appeared and stolen Emilia's "name"; Subaru had somehow managed to bring Julius into the fight against Batenkaitos, but then Reid had joined the fray.

As a result, Emilia and Julius had been forced into a two-front battle against both Gluttony and Reid, bleeding precious time, and on top of that had been hit head-on by the giant scorpion's intrusion and the shadow's declaration that time was up.

In other words, that balance of forces could not be allowed to happen again. There hadn't been enough hands to go around.

On top of that, what worried Subaru was the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony — if what Rui Arneb had said was true, then there should be two of "Gluttony" here.

He'd encountered Lye Batenkaitos. But the other one was nowhere to be seen.

—Bad. Bad, bad, bad. This was bad, all around.

The situation kept shifting moment by moment, and Subaru was the only one who grasped the whole picture, yet even after stacking up this many deaths, he still hadn't uncovered the full shape of it.

He'd spent far too long standing still. Why had he wasted so much time doing nothing—

"—Would you calm down, I wonder!"

"Iyhow!!"

Just as Subaru's overheating thoughts seemed about to send smoke pouring from his ears, Beatrice seized his cheeks firmly between her small hands, and the pain yanked his mind back to reality.

Still crushing his cheeks, Beatrice continued, close enough that her breath brushed his face.

"Subaru, tell me what happened inside the book, I suppose. There's no use keeping it all bottled up alone. Talk properly, and we'll think it through together. ...That's our strength, I suppose."

"What happened inside the book..."

Hearing Beatrice's earnest appeal, Subaru took stock of the situation he found himself in.

He'd accepted the fact that he'd Returned by Death. Now he had to acknowledge, and etch into his mind just the same, the place he'd come back to.

This was the library of the third floor, "Taygeta."

In a room ringed by shelves holding countless Books of the Dead, Subaru had returned to the very moment right after his consciousness came back from challenging Reid's Book of the Dead.

And as for what had happened to Subaru inside the book—

"—'Get up,' is what."

"Huh?"

"No, I mean I got kicked in the back and booted out of giving up. Damn it, pathetic. How little progress have I even made."

Before Beatrice's wide-eyed stare, Subaru scratched roughly at his own head.

Then, still holding Beatrice, he stood up and slowly looked around — at Echidna and Meili, the two who had waited quietly for him to wake.

He knew what the missing members — Emilia, Ram, Julius, and Shaula — were doing.

The crisis was already underway; there was no time to stand still.

So—

"I'll keep this short. I failed to see Reid's memories in the Book of the Dead. Something interrupted me. The Book of the Dead connects down to the feet of something called Od Lagna, and I ran into a real pain of a guy down there."

At Subaru's rapid-fire explanation, the three of them blinked, taken aback.

Silently apologizing for not being able to give them time for their hearts to catch up, Subaru pressed on to the decisive line.

Which was—

"—The Sin Archbishop of Gluttony, Rui Arneb, went and declared war on us."

"Even so, to run into a Sin Archbishop all the way out here at the eastern edge of the world. It really does seem there's some unbreakable bond of fate between you and them, Natsuki-kun."

Having finished listening to the explanation, Echidna teased Subaru's circumstances with that remark.

Even as he listened, Subaru and the others were already dashing down the library stairs, moving to rejoin the companions who had left "Taygeta."

"Unfortunately, I've got amnesia over here. I don't know a thing about whatever long history I supposedly have with those Sin Archbishop types. ...Am I really that tangled up with them?"

"At the very least, they're the reason we ended up coming to this tower, I suppose. We made a long journey through the sand searching for a way to undo what they did."

"I think I heard the gist of why we came to the tower, but so they were the root cause of it all, huh. Those guys really never do anything decent."

Grimacing in disgust, Subaru groaned inwardly at the fate that bound him to such unpleasant people.

He didn't press further, but neither Beatrice nor Echidna had specified that the one they were bound to by fate was "Gluttony." If Subaru's guess was right, there were probably seven Sin Archbishops in total, so it seemed likely the one involved wasn't Gluttony alone.

"That's depressing enough that I don't want to get into it right now, but—oh."

"Echidna! And Subaru's with you too!"

Just as Subaru and the others, having returned to the fourth floor, burst out into the corridor, the figure who happened to be running toward them — Julius — let out a startled exclamation.

Last time too, after confirming the abnormal situation surrounding the tower, Julius had come back to "Taygeta" to inform Subaru, Echidna, and the others of the circumstances. This time, they'd simply caught him a little earlier.

Seeing that Subaru had safely returned from the Book of the Dead, Julius said,

"Are you all right, Subaru? Did you obtain the crucial information from the Book of the Dead?"

"Same answer I gave Beatrice and the others — I failed at that. I'll fill in the details later, alongside my questions, in parallel with dealing with this crisis."

"Meaning what, exactly?"

"I mean I've got a rough idea what's going on already. —Right now, there are Witchbeasts around the tower, aren't there?"

The instant Subaru posed that question, an especially violent tremor shook the tower.

That faint, ceaseless rumbling like the earth itself groaning had reached everyone inside the tower. Julius's eyes widened slightly at having the truth — the very reason everyone had sprung into motion — spelled out for him.

But he quickly set his jaw and nodded.

"That's correct. At present, the Pleiades Watchtower is surrounded by a great number of Witchbeasts. On the tower's outer perimeter, Lady Shaula is fighting valiantly to hold back their advance, but..."

"If the cause of this rumbling is a whole horde of naughty little animal-chans, then no matter how strong the naked lady is, she's gonna stop being able to hold them back sooner or later, huh."

"Which is why I'd like to borrow your strength. What do you say, Meili?"

Julius turned to Meili, who had cut into the conversation, and earnestly asked for her help. In response, Meili shot Subaru a meaningful glance.

It felt like a look asking Subaru whether or not she should go along with this, so he let out a small breath and nodded.

"I'm asking too. Meili, it's got to be you — no one else can do it. Sorry to throw this at you right away, but help us all take one more step forward."

"...Hmm? Honestly, Onii-san, after all those big words you said before, you're being so pathetic about it. But since you asked so honestly, I'll let you leave this to me. You'd better be grateful."

"Yeah, deeply grateful! I love you!"

"So cheeeap..."

Meili pouted her lips at Subaru's thanks, but readily agreed to handle the Witchbeasts. With that, one of the five obstacles was cleared. So far, it matched the previous loop.

Which was why—

"Then, let's get her to Lady Shaula's side—"

"No, we're splitting off from that. Meili, do you know where the balcony is? Work with Shaula there and hold the Witchbeasts back somehow."

"At the end of the corridor, right? Honestly, you really work people hard."

With just that, Meili let out a sigh and took off running. She seemed listless and unenthusiastic, but that was just her way of posturing — you could tell from the pace of her feet as she obeyed Subaru's instructions. That was her running full-out.

And then,

"Subaru, both with the Witchbeasts and other matters, you seem to have some kind of certainty about things. What do you know?"

"Right. I'll explain on the way. For now, just come with me. —If we don't hurry, Emilia-chan and the others are in danger."

"—!"

Julius's eyes went wide with shock. Subaru clapped him on the shoulder and took off running full tilt, Beatrice cradled in his arms, dragging Echidna along in a mad rush.

Julius, dazed for a moment, quickly came back to himself and caught up to Subaru's back with ease.

"I already told you my dive into the Book of the Dead failed. I couldn't see Reid's memories. Instead, I ran into someone else down there... the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony."

"—Impossible. Why would Gluttony be inside the Book of the Dead?"

At the name of his fated enemy, Julius's expression froze. It was Echidna who called out "Julius" to his profile.

She alone wore an expression as if frustrated by her own slow feet, and raised a finger.

"From what Natsuki-kun says, the Book of the Dead is a cradle of Od Lagna... part of a mechanism for storing the memories of the dead, the trajectories of their lives. And the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony devours others' 'memories' and 'names.' In other words..."

"You mean to say their Authority makes use of a fragment of Od Lagna's power? ...No, wait — you said you met Gluttony inside the book. If so, Subaru, the cause of your amnesia is—"

"Yeah, it's not some trap or mechanism built into the tower. It's a pure FOE... an accident brought on by a random encounter. I'm in the same victim bracket as you, matching set and all."

Never in his wildest imagination would he have guessed that challenging the Book of the Dead would lead to an encounter with Gluttony.

And if Subaru and the others' guess from last time was correct, the only Book of the Dead where an encounter with Gluttony was even possible was that single volume of Reid's, reproduced within this tower.

Just how unlucky did you have to be for that to happen? He wanted to lodge a formal complaint against his own conduct up until yesterday. It was enough to make him seriously want to believe that ridiculous nonsense about a fated bond with the Sin Archbishops.

"A matching set, and in such poor taste too. ...But if you and Gluttony already had contact as of last night, then this Witchbeast assault as well?"

"That would mean we gave the enemy over half a day to prepare, I suppose. If only Subaru had remembered it, things would have— ah! I mean, I'm not blaming Subaru for it, I suppose!"

"I know. But I deserve to be blamed. I wish yesterday's me hadn't screwed up, and I wish today's me were smarter."

If he had been, they could have met the enemy in a far more solid state of readiness.

But there was no use dwelling on that now. What mattered was fighting with the hand he had.

What he needed was resolve and decisiveness, and — never mind himself — the will to trust his companions.

"As you guessed, the Witchbeast horde is Gluttony's doing. On top of that, Gluttony itself is going to come storming in... and it's going to go after Emilia-chan and the others."

And last time, before Subaru and the others — slow to catch on — could arrive, Emilia, forced to fight alone, had sent Ram and the others fleeing, and ended up having her "name" devoured as a result.

This time, he would prevent that before it happened. —That was why he'd cut corners through so many stages along the way.

"If Emilia-chan's name doesn't get stolen this time..."

—then he could probably prevent Ram from being forced out of the fight.

"——"

Last time, the reason Ram had become unable to act was that, as a result of Batenkaitos stealing Emilia's "name," Ram had been unable to bear the resulting gap of Emilia's now-nonexistent memories.

If that danger were removed, he could count on Ram as a fighting force. And if they took charge of Rem, whom she was carrying, on their end, that problem should be resolved too.

And if the Emilia-Ram pair could hold Batenkaitos in check—

"Julius! You've got a job too! We'll handle Gluttony on our end. I know full well he's your fated enemy too, and how badly you want to knock his lights out, but—"

"But what? In this situation, is there anyone who should take priority over Gluttony—"

"—Reid's coming down. If he butts in, everything falls apart. Someone needs to stop him."

"——"

At that explanation, yet another wave of shock etched a furrow into Julius's brow.

But this time, confusion and doubt outweighed simple surprise.

Naturally so.

As for the horde of Witchbeasts and the assault by the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony — both of these could be explained away as things Subaru had heard directly from Gluttony inside the Book of the Dead.

But if Reid's presence got tangled into it, that was a different matter. There was no connection between Reid and Gluttony. At the very least, the tower's functions had nothing to do with the Sin Archbishops.

Which meant there was no logical basis to be found for that explanation—

"...Suppose, hypothetically, that it's true, and Reid Astrea comes down to the lower floors. In this situation, would he really act in disregard of all this?"

"You don't think he would? Really? Is that guy really the type to be swayed by the situation around him?"

"...Sorry, but Betty doesn't think so either, I suppose. That one's the type to prioritize whatever he most wants to do at the moment, I suppose. No matter the situation."

Held in Subaru's arms, Beatrice nodded with an unpleasant certainty. At her words, Julius faltered, and turned to Echidna as if seeking a final opinion.

In answer to Julius's look, Echidna shrugged her slender shoulders.

"I share their opinion. That said, it's hard to believe he's roaming freely through the tower. Or rather, I don't want to believe it, to be precise... Natsuki-kun, is that information as well?"

"—. Yeah. I heard it from Gluttony."

After a moment's hesitation, Subaru nodded, brazenly lying in answer to Echidna's question.

In truth, it was unlikely that Gluttony had any hand in how Reid had come to walk free. At the very least, Batenkaitos had been startled by Reid's presence, and given how easily he'd been brushed aside, there was no chance he'd summoned him as an ally.

So this was, in the end, purely an extralegal measure Subaru resorted to because he couldn't otherwise convey the credibility of the information he actually had.

"——"

At Subaru's somewhat strained answer, Julius fell into thought with a difficult expression.

But even as he agonized over it, time kept slipping by moment by moment. Especially now, with the Witchbeast horde and the Sin Archbishop both in play, the tower's situation was actively worsening.

Unable to bear watching that, Echidna spoke up: "Julius,"

"I feel the same reluctance you do, about pulling you away from a visible threat and sending you toward a potential one. But Natsuki-kun's words..."

"...deserve trust. Yes, I know, Echidna. Honestly, I find it galling to be pulled away from the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony, my own sworn enemy as well. But—"

"—Reid Astrea can only be entrusted to you. Just like I said before."

"...If I don't win, the whole calculation falls apart, was it. Honestly, what an impossible line to argue with."

If the calculation went awry, if the gears fell out of alignment, an unthinkable tragedy would almost certainly follow.

Perhaps sensing that in the seriousness of Subaru's black eyes, Julius let out a deep sigh.

"I'll promise you this. I'll take on Reid. —But if it turns out he doesn't move beyond the second floor, I'll come join you there instead. Any objections?"

"None. If Reid doesn't wander off and you end up free, nothing could be better. I'll leave the timing of the call to you, but beating Reid is your job too."

"Consider it understood, then. Echidna, Lady Beatrice, I leave the rest to you."

"Leave it to me, why don't you..."

At being left out of that request in favor of the other two, Subaru's cheek twitched, but Julius, with a theatrically dashing gesture, smoothed back his forelocks and turned away with a flourish.

The hem of his white garment fluttering, Julius kicked off the floor with his long legs and headed for the stairs to the second floor.

"—I won't press you on how you came by your certainty."

"——"

"What matters is that we trust you. I'd be glad if you didn't betray my feelings, or his."

"I don't know what you're talking about, but I owe you one."

Once she'd seen Julius's back off, Echidna spoke to Subaru in a lowered voice. The true meaning behind her words was a quiet pointing-out of how thin the connection between Reid and Gluttony really was.

Swallowing that sense of wrongness, Echidna and Julius had chosen to believe Subaru's account. He had to live up to that trust, that expectation.

"—Ngh."

Just as that thought crossed his mind, Subaru felt a prickling sensation deep in his chest.

For a moment, Beatrice's brow furrowed at Subaru's reaction, but paying her response no mind, he turned and narrowed his eyes.

—He recognized this heat.

It was the strange heat he'd felt at the very end of the last loop, right before being swallowed by the shadow. His heartbeat quickened, and Subaru gritted his teeth against the prickling warmth, closing his eyes.

Behind his closed eyelids rose faint, fragile motes of light, swaying softly.

They appeared right there in his arms, at his side, and also in the direction Subaru now turned his face—

"Let's hurry! We'll do our part and go join up with Emilia-chan and the others!"

"That's fine by me, but will the three of us actually be of any use? Frankly, you, Beatrice, and I right now are about as non-combatant as it gets."

"Betty could manage some fighting strength if she tried, I suppose. Besides, Subaru's aim probably isn't to be reinforcements in the first place, I suppose."

Leaving the running Subaru to his own devices, Echidna and Beatrice traded opinions.

Echidna's concern was well-founded, and Beatrice's guess was correct.

Sadly, Subaru and the others wouldn't amount to combat reinforcements for Emilia. Instead—

"—Barusu! You're awake!"

"Ram!"

Rounding a corner in the corridor, the same sight as before appeared — Ram, riding on Patrasche's back, cradling Rem in her arms.

She leapt down from Patrasche's back with a flourish and tossed the reins to Subaru.

"You took too long waking up! I'm leaving Rem to you! If you hurt her, or touch her in some weird way, I won't forgive you, so guard her like your life depends on it. Ram is—"

"Wait wait wait, that's all way too fast! I get it, but calm down! You're—"

"The Sin Archbishop of Gluttony is here! Emilia-sama is fighting back, but she's at a disadvantage. If Ram doesn't get back there right away, it'll be too late!"

"——"

In an instant, a tangle of emotions passed through Subaru's chest.

Worry for Ram, who was rushing frantically back to the battlefield; relief at hearing Emilia's name come from her mouth; and anger blooming at the confirmed presence of the hated Batenkaitos.

Wrestling all of it down, Subaru handed the reins he'd just taken over to Echidna.

"Echidna! Get Rem and Patrasche somewhere safe! Not the balcony or the second floor! We can't go near the green room right now either. Probably 'Taygeta' is the safest bet!"

"Natsuki-kun, what about you!?"

"Beatrice and I are going after the Sin Archbishop, with Ram!"

With the reins pressed firmly into her hands, Subaru looked into Echidna's startled pale blue-green eyes. To her, breath catching faintly, Subaru stroked Patrasche's neck and jerked his chin toward Rem on her back.

"You said it yourself just now — don't betray our trust. I'm asking you the same thing. I'm counting on you with Rem. This girl is someone 'Natsuki Subaru' can't do without."

"—What a strange way to put it. Aren't you Natsuki Subaru yourself?"

"...I've got a little bit of faith that you, of all people, might understand how I feel."

He'd heard that Echidna's existence was, at its root, sustained by borrowing the body of a girl named Anastasia. In a way, that put her in a position similar to the current Natsuki Subaru, who was overwriting himself onto the existence of "Natsuki Subaru."

At those words from Subaru, Echidna's face showed a flash of realization.

"Natsuki-kun, don't tell me you—"

"—I'm counting on you."

Without letting Echidna finish, Subaru left them behind and took off running.

Ram was already heading down the corridor ahead. As he left, he stole just one glance at Rem's sleeping face on Patrasche's back, steeling his courage.

"Why did you come, Barusu. Rem is..."

"Rem told me to worry less about her and get on with what I need to do! Lectured me about it inside the book!"

"—! Rem said that? What does that mean?"

At the words of Subaru now running alongside her, Ram's pale pink eyes flickered with agitation. But there was no time to painstakingly explain everything that had happened in the Corridor of Memories.

So Subaru conveyed only the most important part, briefly.

"Rem told me to fight, and to go get her back. So I'm coming along too!"

"Of course, Betty's here too, so don't you forget it, I suppose."

"—. Fine, that'll do for now. I'll grill you a hundred times over about it later."

"A hundred times over sounds terrifying!?"

Coming from Ram, that didn't sound like a joke at all, and Subaru shuddered. Still, letting him off with just that, when he hadn't explained a single important thing, was her act of mercy.

And the situation that demanded exactly that was already bearing down right in front of them—

"—Ice Brand Arts!"

The next moment, in the middle of the freezing corridor, Emilia's back came into view, dancing with an icy weapon in hand.

Facing her was the grimy-looking Sin Archbishop, Lye Batenkaitos.

Seeing it, Ram cried out.

"Emilia-sama!"

The fact that it was Emilia's name confirmed for Subaru that they'd made it in time.

At the same moment, hearing her name called, Emilia noticed them behind her — "Eh, Ram!?" —

"Why did you come back!? And Subaru and Beatrice too — I'm glad you're safe... but! It's really, really dangerous right now! Get back! Stay away!"

"Rem has been evacuated somewhere safe. Ram will lend a hand now as well."

"But Ram, you're—"

"You think I'd just retreat because I was told to?"

Answering Emilia, Ram reached down to her leg and drew a slender wand strapped there.

It was the sort of short wand one pictures a mage carrying. It looked like an entirely unremarkable item, yet somehow it seemed to give off a strange, oppressive presence.

And seeing Ram raise it, Gluttony, keeping his distance from Emilia, laughed.

"Ha-ha! What, what, you actually came back, Nee-sama! Oh my, so cool! Why, why is Nee-sama always this good-looking? Seriously, Nee-sama's wonderful!"

"—Tiresome. I'll kill you, Sin Archbishop."

Ram's quiet killing intent stabbed into the Sin Archbishop, who clapped his hands and cackled.

Beside her, catching sight of it out of the corner of his eye even though it wasn't aimed at him, Subaru felt a chill run down his spine at the sheer intensity of her fury. But even under that direct glare, Batenkaitos's composure didn't crack.

"Good, good, that's fine, sure, why not, it's fine, it's fine, I think it's fine! Gluttonous drink! Gluttonous feast! I'm sorry it's staying such a one-sided, overwhelming grudge. Really, this should've been a much more touching reunion."

"What are you—"

"Stealing the 'name' and 'memories' of someone precious to you — think of it in terms of a revenge story, and that makes us the ultimate mortal enemy, right? And yet, most of the people we devour don't even remember it happened. No matter how much we love Nee-sama, how much we understand her, Nee-sama doesn't have the faintest clue how we feel! It's like pouring Solt all over some mouth-wateringly premium ingredient — such a waste of a good thing."

On and on, the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony rambled through an explanation that was of absolutely no use to anyone.

In truth, Gluttony's power to steal others' memories amounted to trampling on a person's whole life. If your family, your lover, someone you owed a debt of gratitude to were attacked by them, the worst of it would vanish into oblivion, and the hatred that should have followed would be lost as well.

In a way, that functioned as a kind of safety mechanism, preventing anyone from ever taking revenge on them.

But—

"—Doesn't that sound incredibly boring, though?"

But Batenkaitos, of all things, declared flatly that it was boring.

"——"

"All that rich, mellow hatred, that thick, muddied rage we should've gotten — all of it, that fresh, pungent emotion, gets wiped out entirely. Which is a huge loss, from our point of view. ...Until you came along, Onii-san over there."

"Me...?"

"Onii-san, for some reason, even when we eat someone's 'name,' you still remember them, right? I've figured out a bunch of the reasons why, sort of. It's probably something about the place Onii-san was born being a little different from here, but what matters is the result. Onii-san, you're our savior."

As he spoke, Batenkaitos spread both arms wide and gazed at Subaru with an entranced expression.

It was a look of genuine, burning passion, as though truly lovesick for Subaru.

"No, let me go ahead and put it plainly! Onii-san is our hero! So endearing, so earnest, so anxious without someone by your side, so mean sometimes, and the more we think of you the more our chest aches and aches — and a hero who understands that feeling, no less...!"

"Cut the creepy talk! What are you even trying to—"

"Of course it's a heartfelt feeling. How cruel, how pitiful. And here we are, thinking of Onii-san so devotedly, so single-mindedly."

Subaru had no way of knowing how much of that tangled mess of emotion was genuine. He couldn't imagine it, and didn't want to.

But, considering everything Batenkaitos had said up to now, the answer became clear on its own.

The presence this thing had absorbed harbored feelings for Subaru — no, there was no need to hedge around it.

Those were Rem's feelings, which this thing had swallowed up—

"So how about it! Want to relive that touching moment all over again, if you'd like! Let's start right here, Onii-san! From the beginning, or rather... nge—"

"—Hyaaah!!"

"Bugah!?"

At that instant, Emilia, who had crept up behind Batenkaitos, brought down the massive ice hammer cradled in both arms onto the back of the Sin Archbishop's head without a moment's hesitation.

At the merciless blow, straight out of some comic strip, Batenkaitos's words cut off, and for an instant his eyes rolled completely white before he collapsed face-first.

"——"

"...Eh, did I win?"

"I did it!"

Batenkaitos lay silent, while Emilia pumped a fist in triumph. Looking back and forth between the two, Subaru's eyes went wide at the terrifyingly anticlimactic ending.

"...What an anticlimactic way for this to end, I suppose."

"...Indeed. Where exactly is Ram supposed to put all the feeling she'd worked up to tear that thing to pieces? Emilia-sama!"

"Eh, what? W-was that bad?"

"—. No, that was splendid. Yes, truly splendid."

Beatrice, held up in Subaru's arms, and Ram both voiced their disappointment at Gluttony's collapse, one after another.

For Ram in particular, whose long-standing grudge against her sworn enemy had just been trampled by a sneak attack, Emilia's award-worthy feat left her with distinctly mixed feelings.

But,

"Well, sure, I've got a mountain of things I want to say, but the very first words out of my mouth are — Emilia-chan, good job! Now let's just tie up Gluttony and—"

"—Sorry, but that's not gonna happen, Onii-san."

"——"

Just as Subaru was thinking that the second of the five obstacles had been dealt with in an unexpected way, Batenkaitos — who should have been lying limp on the ground — rose unsteadily to his feet.

At that unnatural movement, Subaru's eyes went wide, and Beatrice stiffened.

Naturally, wariness rose in Emilia's and Ram's eyes as well—

"Did getting hit knock something loose in your head? If you can't even grasp the situation, what with your love-struck ramblings at Barusu and all—"

"Ah, ah, never mind all that. We've already told you our feelings, and we're done holding back too..."

Cutting off Ram's words, the now-standing Batenkaitos hung his head, hiding his face behind his long, dark brown hair, muttering something under his breath.

Overlapping with that muttering, a warped, ghastly sound echoed through the corridor.

"No way..."

At the bizarre spectacle accompanying that horrific sound, Emilia's violet eyes blinked in disbelief.

Reflected in her beautiful eyes was Batenkaitos's body, its very skeleton warping with grotesque sounds — the small, boyish frame transforming into that of a hulking, muscle-bound man.

It was a nightmarish sight, but the true nightmare, for Subaru, was what came after.

"There we go, Onii-san, surprised? This is 'Solar Eclipse'... well, think of it as one of our trump cards, I guess? Unlike before, this time I'll make you mine by force."

"That's some seriously creepy talk you're spouting, you drag-queen bastard. You're—"

Feeling a nightmarish chill of dread, Subaru broke into a cold sweat as he asked.

In answer, the huge man smiled a bewitching smile, making no attempt to hide his uncanny air, and bowed.

"—Sin Archbishop of Gluttony of the Witch Cult, Rui Arneb."

"——"

"I can't stick around too long, you know. This is just a little nibble while onii-chan's asleep — I've got to go around and wrap up every last bit of it, after all."
