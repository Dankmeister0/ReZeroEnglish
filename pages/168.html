Arc 4 Chapter 2: "Insults and Gratitude"

—Oh, Subaru, how pathetic, to have gone and died.

Shaking his head, still hazy from lying there in a daze, Subaru pushed himself upright and looked around. He was normally quick to wake, so maybe the fog clouding his mind meant this hadn't been ordinary sleep. As he racked his brain trying to recall what had happened before he lost consciousness, what came to him first was that the place he was in looked familiar.

"The mansion's living room... right?"

"Oh, Subaru, you're awake?"

The moment he murmured that half to confirm it, the door opened and Emilia peeked her face in. Her silver hair bound in a braid, her expression brightened slightly as she walked over to where Subaru lay on the sofa, bending at the waist to meet his eyes. Stared at intently by those round eyes, Subaru shrank his shoulders a little and said,

"Um, what happened again, Emilia-tan?"

"Right after we came into the mansion, we heard you scream. Otto-kun and I were both so surprised. So we rushed inside, and..."

"Was I... asleep or something?"

"That's a little misleading, but... the general gist isn't wrong, maybe?"

Emilia tilted her head, a finger to her lips. Nothing in her manner conveyed any sense of urgency about the situation Subaru had described. The moment he'd woken up, finding her so relaxed had already told Subaru that whatever had happened, it wasn't a dire emergency—but even so, the strangeness of it all refused to fade. He was fairly sure that right before he'd blacked out, some creature with sharp fangs had—

"Lady Emilia, might I have a moment?"

A knock came from outside the door, and a woman's voice called out to Emilia. Turning her gaze that way, Emilia answered, "Yes," and the door slowly swung open. Watching the door open without much thought, Subaru suddenly felt a flicker of doubt. —Hold on, that voice... he was pretty sure he'd never heard it before.

And that suspicion of his was soon cleared up by whoever stood on the other side of the door.

"I've brought a drink and a change of towels—oh, it seems you've woken up."

Saying so, the figure smiled, and Subaru's eyes locked onto her.

She was a woman with long, translucent golden hair, her posture ramrod straight. Refinement showed in every detail of her bearing and movements, her fluid motions betraying not a hint of wasted effort. Her outfit was the serving uniform familiar around Roswaal Manor—in other words, a maid's dress—cute yet functional, and worn with the same impeccable neatness. The tray in her hands held, just as she'd said, a pitcher and some towels, which she set down soundlessly on the table in the center of the living room. —If one were scoring it, it would be a perfect, flawless score.

That is, if you overlooked how menacing her build and smile were.

Neatly worn as the serving uniform was, the wearer stood slightly taller than Subaru, her physique barely distinguishable from that of someone heavily muscled. On a man, it would simply read as sturdy—but on a woman, it instantly became something beyond mere "sturdy." And then there was the smile that crowned her flawless bearing—ruined by a row of fangs sharp enough to be plainly visible even from a distance. Looking more closely, the sharpness in her narrowed eyes was likewise far from ordinary; the pupils of her green eyes held the same glint as a feline predator sizing up its prey.

"It's a pleasure to meet you. I am Frederica Baumann, and I have the honor of serving as a member of the household staff at the residence of Margrave Roswaal L. Mathers—"

"Your face is terrifying—!?"

Cutting off her polite self-introduction, Subaru's mouth blurted out an all-too-honest impression. At that, the woman's expression—the very party who'd just heard it—stiffened faintly, and after several blinks of those fearsome eyes... tears welled up, slowly.

"Fu, eh..."

"Huh?"

"Subaru, you idiot!!"

Subaru gaped at the woman as she stammered and turned her face away. An instant later, a furious shout came along with a sharp pain as his ear was yanked. As he yelped, "Ow, ow, ow!" and glanced over, he found Emilia there, her normally gentle brows drawn tight in anger.

"How could you say that to a girl! Do you have any idea how devotedly Frederica—"

"P-please, Lady Emilia, that's enough. It's all right. I—I was the one at fault. I was simply so happy to be called back to the manor that I got carried away. ...I forgot that my appearance isn't the sort people warm to."

Tugging at the sleeve of the furious Emilia, the woman—the one called Frederica—shook her head. With the hand not holding Emilia back, she covered her own mouth.

"I'm terribly sorry for startling you. And earlier, I committed a grave discourtesy—mistaking the returning Natsuki Subaru-sama for a suspicious intruder."

"A suspicious intruder... oh, wait. I think I'm starting to see how this all connects."

Freed for the moment from Emilia's discipline, Subaru rubbed his ear and mulled over Frederica's words. He soon grasped the rough outline of the situation up to this point. In other words, the mysterious figure Subaru had encountered right after entering the mansion was—

"Frederica-san, who'd already come back to the manor, and she took me down thinking I was an intruder. Then Emilia-tan came in after and cleared up the misunderstanding, and here we are... something like that?"

"Exactly right. ...My, you do think quickly."

"Well, it wasn't hard to follow the flow of things... but wait, before that."

Having confirmed with Frederica's nod that his guess had been correct, Subaru looked past her to Emilia, who stood with her arms crossed, jerking her chin toward Frederica behind her back. He understood all too well both what that gesture meant and what was going through Emilia's mind. So Subaru got off the sofa and turned to face the maid.

"I'm sorry for saying something so awful the very first time we met. Whether I was just waking up or just being stupid, I did something a woman shouldn't have to forgive. Boil me, roast me, whatever you like... though I'd appreciate it if it didn't hurt too much."

With an attitude a bit too meek to be called dignified, Subaru bowed his head. It was true that their first contact hadn't exactly started on good terms for either of them, but unlike her, who'd acted with the intent of repelling a suspicious intruder, what Subaru had said was an outright blunder, plain and simple. Just as he'd said, if it would clear away her anger, he had to accept whatever treatment she saw fit. Though if possible, he'd rather skip the physical pain and just have his heart gouged out with insults.

And yet, Subaru's resolve for this manfully unmanly apology was—

"—Fufu, what an amusing man you are."

—swept away by Frederica's smile, hidden though it was behind the hand still covering her mouth. With a question mark hanging over Subaru's head, Frederica lowered her head, her translucent golden hair falling forward, taking the same posture as the apologizing Subaru.

"It's I who must apologize. I was, at Lady Emilia's suggestion, testing you, Subaru-sama."

"Testing?"

At Frederica's claim, Subaru and Emilia tilted their heads at the same moment. Neither understood what she meant. Subaru, fine, but if Frederica's words were to be believed, it made no sense for even Emilia to be confused. Yet Frederica only deepened her smile at the sight of the two of them mirroring each other's puzzlement.

"Even with a sense of duty to protect the manor, I still committed a discourtesy against you, Subaru-sama, a guest. I had resolved that the only way to take responsibility was to offer up my own head."

"No, that's way too quick to resolve on. I'm a guy you can talk things out with, you know?"

"Yes, Lady Emilia argued just as forcefully. She went on so earnestly, listing such flowery praises of you, Subaru-sama, that even I, listening, nearly blushed..."

"Fwah!?"

Rattled by Frederica's unexpected words, Subaru let out a strange yelp and looked at Emilia. There, he expected to find her blushing and trying to cover it up—

"Freeee-de-riii-ca."

—but she wasn't. Instead, Emilia stood with her hands on her hips, glaring at the maid with an uncharacteristically dangerous look. In response, Frederica said, unfazed, "Oh my, how frightening,"

"As unamusing as ever, Lady Emilia. Normally, in a scene like this, the proper form is to blush and flounder, regardless of whether what I said was true or not."

"Eh, is that so... wait, no, I'm not falling for it today. Even I learn something if I get tricked over and over. That's right—Frederica's eyes go crossed a little whenever she lies!"

"I wasn't aware of that. By the way, Lady Emilia, are you aware that your own ears grow longer whenever you tell a lie?"

"A lie!?"

Emilia, who'd been pointing at Frederica with a triumphant look, hastily grabbed at both her own ears at those words. But the moment that reaction was drawn out of her, the match was already Frederica's. The flustered Emilia still didn't seem to realize her defeat, but Subaru, who'd watched the whole exchange unfold, let out a breath and shrugged.

"Sure looks like a total loss to me. ...So, do you need me to introduce myself as Natsuki Subaru, or what?"

"Yes, of course I'll accept it. Let's get properly acquainted with each other, then, shall we?"

Saying so, Frederica took the hand away from her mouth and flashed a grin lined with bared fangs. Unlike her reaction from before, witnessing her formidable resolve—one that even turned her own smile into a weapon—Subaru finally, truly, went limp.

"Now that I think about it, I did hear a little something about this. That there was a maid who quit shortly before I came to the manor. I've been here a month, so... she quit about three months ago?"

"That would be about right. I resigned for personal reasons. I remember how terribly lonely it was, leaving this place. ...Though I ended up coming back far sooner than I expected."

Frederica laughed, hiding her mouth behind her sleeve. As long as she kept her mouth covered like that, combined with her beautiful golden hair and a gaze that could just barely pass for dignified, she seemed the very picture of a lovely woman. Though her apparently mischievous personality and that fanged mouth of hers inevitably undid the effect.

The setting was still the living room of Roswaal Manor. Subaru and Frederica had just finished a brief exchange of information beyond their names. And in hearing her introduce herself properly, Subaru recalled that her name had rung a bell.

"If it was three months ago, you must know Emilia-tan already, huh?"

"Hmph. That's right, so there."

"Don't hear 'hmph, so there' much these days. And that's a pretty old-fashioned way to sulk—cute, though, hey."

Emilia, not even glancing at Subaru as he spoke, sat on the sofa, apparently listening in without joining the conversation. Ever since she'd belatedly realized she'd been had by Frederica, this had been her state. Anyway,

"So you came back sometime in the last two or three days, right? It's been three days since we left the village... no, four including travel time. Sounds like we just barely missed each other."

"I was surprised myself, coming back to the manor only to find it empty. Fortunately, there was a note left in the master's study, so I wasn't too bewildered."

"A note?"

"Yes, from Ram. To think she called me back to the manor herself and then handled the actual notice so carelessly... though thinking that's just so like her is probably me spoiling her too much."

There was something seasoned about Frederica's wry smile, and Subaru guessed that her acquaintance with Ram must span a fair number of years. Which meant, at the same time, that she'd surely spent just as many years with Rem—who by now had surely vanished from her memory too.

"So why did Ram call you back?"

"I don't know the reason clearly myself. Though I imagine Lady Emilia, being party to it, would know."

Put on the spot, Emilia found both their gazes turning to her cheek as well. She herself kept her face averted, still holding fast to an attitude of "I'm absolutely furious right now, so there." Even so, it was plain as day that she was sneaking half-lidded glances their way, well aware of their attention.

"Emilia-tan, cheer up already... I mean, it wasn't even me who made you mad this time, so Frederica better apologize properly."

"Please forgive me, Lady Emilia. What happened earlier was my fault. I was simply so glad to see you again after so long that I let myself get carried away."

"...You really won't tease me like that anymore?"

"No, I won't. I swear I'll never use that particular trick on you again, Lady Emilia."

Frederica's excuse didn't quite clear Subaru's suspicion that it was mere sophistry. But Emilia, our goddess that she was, seemed to accept her words at face value all too easily, and the sullen look she'd worn until now eased into a "well, I suppose it can't be helped" sort of expression.

"All right. I'm not angry anymore. Is that good?"

"Yes, I'm terribly sorry, Lady Emilia. —Too easy."

For some reason, only Subaru caught that latter murmur, and he shot Frederica a startled look, but she wore an utterly innocent face. Meanwhile, Emilia, not even noticing she'd just been called "too easy," raised a finger to her cheek and said, "So, then,"

"Um, the reason Ram called Frederica back to the manor... right?"

"Right, right. I figured, calling back someone she'd let go in such a hurry, it must be some urgent matter... oh, wait, I guess I already know what that might be."

Urgent matter or not, it wasn't so long ago that this manor and Arlam Village had been under threat from the Witch Cult. Judging by the skill with which she'd taken Subaru's consciousness in an instant, Frederica too, true to the tradition of Roswaal Manor's suspicious maids, surely had some manner of combat ability. In other words, Ram must have called her in as emergency manpower for the manor's defense—

"It's because Ram's housekeeping skills are a total disaster and the manor was falling apart... right? Somehow, in just a few days, there were fewer and fewer rooms you could even live in."

"That's a way more desperate reason than I expected! And she really doesn't live up to her own talk... no, wait, she'd already analyzed herself as hopeless! Fine, she was right, but at least try to prove yourself wrong a little!"

His overthinking had missed the mark entirely—the real reason was one desperate enough to break your heart.

Emilia gave a wry smile at Subaru's outburst, then swept her gaze around the living room—no, through it, as if surveying the entire manor beyond—

"But thanks to Frederica coming back, the manor's all clean now, isn't it? I think Ram made the right call, leaving it to someone capable instead of stubbornly making things worse."

"I don't think you mean it this way, Emilia-tan, but that line stings! And also, I still don't think that's a reason for her to give up so instantly!"

"Whatever one thinks of Ram, for my part, it's been a while since I had such a rewarding job. Fortunately, since everyone was away, I was able to put all the time I'd otherwise spend attending to you all into cleaning and tidying the manor instead."

There was something formidable about Frederica, a glimpse of her diligence showing through. Even as he found himself holding his breath at the housekeeping prowess she radiated, Subaru couldn't help but feel keenly, on the other hand— the compensating force the world exerted to paper over the gap left by Rem's erasure, a result of the Authority of Gluttony.

"Ram alone can't keep the manor running, so relying on someone else was the natural conclusion... huh."

And so Ram had contacted the resigned Frederica and called her back to the manor—summoning Frederica as a substitute for Rem, in order to keep Roswaal Manor functioning even though it could no longer sustain itself without Rem's presence. But Subaru was the only one who knew that sad truth. Frederica had simply answered the call when needed, while Ram remained unaware of why there had suddenly opened up such a gap between her own capacity and the manor's needs that she required Frederica's help at all. That was the whole of it. Still,

"Sorry for getting all somber on my own there, but... is there maybe some rule that you can't be a maid at Roswaal's manor unless you're some kind of oddball?"

"...? Subaru-sama, what are you even saying, when the master himself is the one doing the hiring?"

"That's some seriously unpleasant persuasive power!"

That alone was answer enough to settle every question he'd ever had, reliable as ever. At that, Frederica nodded, satisfied, then straightened up and looked at Subaru. As he braced himself, wondering what now, Frederica lowered her voice and said, "By the way,"

"The coachman has been left waiting in the dragon carriage parked out front for nearly an hour now... is that all right?"

"Hm? Oh, you mean Otto. Right, left there a whole hour... well, yeah, I guess that's fine, really. I do want to get Patrasche properly into the stable to rest, but Otto doesn't need that kind of consideration."

"We went through a life-or-death crisis together and this is how heartless you turn out to be, Natsuki-san! I never imagined I'd rank lower in priority than an Earth Dragon!"

The one who threw open the living room door with a bang as he said this was none other than Otto, the very subject of their conversation. Shoulders squared in indignation, he glared at Subaru, snorting, his displeasure plain. At his sudden appearance, Subaru slowly stood, shook his head, and let out a breath.

"No, that's wrong, Otto."

"Wrong how? It's too late to walk back what you just said—"

"It's not that you rank lower than the earth dragon. You rank way lower than the earth dragon."

"That's a second bottom! That's even worse!"

Satisfied with Otto's foot-stomping reaction, Subaru turned his gaze toward the window. That side faced the main gate—which meant it was where the dragon carriage led by Patrasche should be parked. Following the direction of his gaze and catching his meaning, Otto made a sour face.

"Patrasche-chan's already in the stable. She's a proud one, hard to handle, but she seems to not want to be any trouble to you, Natsuki-san, so she's obedient enough."

"Hearing that from your mouth makes me doubt the power of your Word Spirit Divine Protection. If she were a person, she'd be straight-up kuudere material—Patrasche, when did that flag even get raised?"

"How should I know something like that. More importantly..."

Puzzling over where Patrasche's earnest devotion had come from, Subaru tilted his head as Otto brought up another topic. It was, again, about the handling of the carriage—which meant—

"What are you going to do about the girl sleeping inside? It's a shame to leave her cooped up in the carriage the whole time, and if you're busy I could carry her to a room myself—"

"—Don't touch Rem."

Otto's offer had come with no ill intent. But the coldness in Subaru's own voice as he answered carried an edge sharp enough to startle even himself, and he could tell Otto had flinched, taken aback. Murmured under his breath, the words had come out low and dark, weighted with something heavy and sticky. Fortunately, it seemed not to have reached the women's ears, but even as Subaru felt faintly unsettled by the abnormality of the voice that had come from his own throat, he went on—

"...I'll carry her in myself, so you don't need to bother. Wouldn't want you hauling a girl around and screwing up your back, anyway."

"For the record, peddlers carry around way heavier goods as part of the job, so I don't think I'm as frail as you seem to think, Natsuki-san."

Otto played along after a brief moment's hesitation, going along with Subaru's glib joke meant to cover for what he'd said before. Grateful for the consideration, Subaru let out a breath. That reaction just now had been excessive by any measure. Unintentional as it was—no, precisely because it had been unconscious, that was the problem. His nerves had grown so raw that he was starting to feel hostility toward anyone who tried to interact with Rem, whether out of goodwill or ill.

"Not a good sign... damn it, how pathetic. Why am I always like this..."

Why, having supposedly gotten past one thing, did he immediately trip over the next pebble in his path? Why could he never seem to get hold of something strong enough to keep him standing tall? If only Rem were here, if only he could still be gazing at Emilia—if he could have stayed alongside the two of them, surely he'd have had that unshakeable something in his grasp by now.

"My own fault... no, I'm the one making Rem pay the bill for it. What a rotten kept man I am."

There surely had to have been some better way to handle things. Up until just before the loop ended a few days back, he'd been convinced he'd done his very best. But surely, somewhere, there had existed an even more perfect result—flawless, the best of all possible outcomes. Subaru had let slip the path that would have led him to find it, and could only pass through with a mediocre, compromised route, arriving at an imperfect future. Rem's sacrifice was the price of that failure.

If Subaru had only been smarter, he should have been able to notice. Before evacuating Emilia and the others from the manor, the personal letter Subaru had entrusted to Crusch's messenger had turned out blank inside. He'd concluded that a Witch Cultist accompanying the messenger had swapped it out to sow confusion—but that didn't add up. At that point, the Witch Cult couldn't have grasped what a threat Subaru's group posed, nor did it seem likely they'd resort to such an indirect method as swapping a letter merely to sow distrust within the Emilia Camp. More than anything, if they'd meant to do that, wouldn't falsifying the contents have been far more effective than simply leaving it blank? So then, why had the letter been blank? If it hadn't been the work of a Witch Cultist, there was only one answer.

"Rem was the one who wrote the letter's contents. I was the one who asked for it to be delivered, and it was Crusch-san who carried it—so only the fact of the handoff remained, while the contents themselves vanished."

That was the fate awaiting any existence whose memory and name had been devoured by the Authority of Gluttony. Erased from the world, all that remained was a world stitched together in ways that made no sense. The wrongness, which could only be noticed if one consciously looked for it, became impossible to consciously notice at all once the existence itself had been erased. And if that were so, then what had that existence been for, and for whose sake—

If he'd taken the fact of the blank letter more seriously, if he'd thought it through properly and seen through to the truth, maybe—just maybe—he could have done something about it. Thinking back on what Emilia had said, the letter had arrived the night before the final day. If the contents had already gone blank by then, that meant Rem had been struck by Gluttony sometime around that hour. At that point, not much time would have passed since Subaru had parted from Rem. The chance to reunite with her, however slim, should still have remained. But in reality, Subaru had missed that chance. Why he'd missed it, he couldn't say anymore, not now. Had there been no sense of wrongness at all?

Ram, the sister-complex that she was, and Emilia, who should have known Rem had been left behind together in the royal capital—neither of the two had brought up Rem even once, so why—

"—Ah."

And then, all at once, it struck him. The instant it hit him, Subaru let out a foolish sound and pressed a hand to his forehead. Then, in one fluid motion, he strode to the wall and slammed his head against it with all his strength. Impact and pain. But once wasn't enough—he repeated it a second time, a third.

"Wh-Subaru!?"

All three had been dumbstruck by Subaru's bizarre behavior, but Emilia was the first to snap out of it, calling out to him in alarm. She grabbed his shoulders from behind and turned him around as he kept slamming his forehead,

"What's gotten into you all of a sudden? It's not exactly news that you do weird things, but that was a bit much even for you... ah, look, your forehead's gone all red."

"I was just genuinely, thoroughly disgusted with my own stupidity, seriously."

Feeling the coolness of Emilia's fingertips against his forehead, Subaru shook his head hard, an expression of self-mockery. Just as he'd said, his own foolishness was too much to even look at directly. Subaru looked Emilia, so close now, straight in the eye, and said,

"Emilia-tan, I have a favor to ask."

"Wh-what is it? Wait, Subaru, your face is too close, and your eyes are scary..."

"Could you go ahead and hurl some abuse at me, hopeless idiot that I am?"

"Eh?"

Emilia's eyes went wide with surprise. Reading refusal in that reaction, Subaru reached for her shoulders with both hands, holding her firmly in place so she couldn't get away, and leaned his face in even closer.

"Please. Don't forgive me—just insult me."

"I—I can't do something like that. I don't think you did anything wrong, Subaru..."

"Come on, work with me here!"

"Even if you say that..."

"Please! If you do it, I'll dedicate my very soul to you, Emilia-tan...!"

"Don't say something that heavy, it just puts me in a bind! Ugh, fine, I guess there's no helping it."

After no small amount of hesitation over Subaru's desperate, perverted request, Emilia reluctantly nodded. Then she gave a small cough, glanced up at Subaru from beneath her lashes, and said,

"Subaru, you idiot."

"Ugh."

"A rowdy brat, stubborn, selfish, a bad kid, rotten to the core, always getting carried away with himself."

"Ngh, ngh."

"You worry about other people all the time when nobody asked you to, way beyond your station. A soft touch, taking the side of a hated Half-Elf like that. Stepping forward in place of me when I'm down from being reviled, acting recklessly and getting yourself dragged through the mud—reckless to a fault."

"Ngh... hm?"

"A coward, who won't give me a straight answer even when I'm listening, always trying to slip away with vague words. A huge fool, who comes running to help even after we had a huge fight, the moment I'm in trouble. A sly one, who gives me exactly the answer I want to hear right when things get impossible and I need someone to fix it. And a slacker, the only one who fell asleep while everyone else was running around cleaning up after it was all over. Subaru, you numbskull."

"Don't hear 'numbskull' much these days either... anyway, Emilia-tan."

He'd been expecting a string of harsh reproaches, but the abuse she'd heaped on him turned out different from what he'd anticipated. Rather than clawing at his heart with ugly wounds, it had come out deep and gentle, hurting not just Subaru's heart but Emilia's own, the two wounds given and received together. At Subaru's address, Emilia, still looking up at him, pouted her lips and said,

"What."

"I mean, is that really how you saw me, all this time?"

"Honestly, it kind of just came out—words I didn't expect. I got so caught up in the momentum I barely even knew what I was saying myself. ...Do you think it was my real feelings, Subaru?"

"Who knows. Whether something that comes out on momentum counts as your real feelings or not... that's a tough call, if you ask me."

At the very least, Subaru had experience regretting words that had spilled out of him in the heat of the moment. Was it truly a real feeling, something he'd always thought deep down, or was it nothing more than a fleeting outburst of emotion, let loose by momentum alone? He supposed that was probably something no one could ever really know for certain.

"Thank you, Emilia-tan."

"I just complained at you, that's all. And you're thanking me for it... Subaru, are you some kind of pervert?"

"I'm an Emilia-tan specialist pervert. As long as it's you sending it my way, whether it's abuse, slander, or a haiku about traffic safety, it stimulates my pleasure center just the same."

"I don't get that last part, but it seems like something I really don't need to understand, so I'll let it slide. —So, are you satisfied now?"

She answered with a knowing little laugh, but right at the very end, a shadow of worry crossed her eyes. It was unfair how she'd show something like that so naturally, making it impossible to ever pull away from her. At that gesture of hers, Subaru grinned, showing his teeth,

"Yeah, I'm fine. Actually, no, maybe I'm not. If Emilia-tan gave me some kind of courage-inducing kiss charm, I might just manage—"

"I'm afraid the request window for today has already closed."

"Goddamn it! I blew it! Why am I always... too slow...!"

Emilia gave a wry smile at the sight of Subaru genuinely crumpling to the floor in frustration. In any case, after venting his frustration for a good while, Subaru popped back up, entirely unfazed, and looked around the room.

"Anyway, setting that aside, I've got a bit of business to take care of. Sorry to Emilia-tan and everyone, but I need a little time. Shouldn't take too long, though... what, Otto, what's with that face?"

"I feel like I ought to file for damages after having to watch that whole cringe-inducing exchange up close, but I'll set the price negotiations aside for now... what exactly are you going to do?"

Having been left out of the loop, Otto muttered this with obvious dissatisfaction, and Subaru crossed his arms, tilting his head. Now that he thought about it, Otto had no idea that there was still one more person, unknown to him, presumably lurking somewhere in this manor. If he were to explain to Otto where he was about to head, what words would even be fitting? He puzzled over it for only the briefest moment before unfolding his arms,

"I'm just gonna go see the drill-haired loli holed up in the moldy-smelling room."

—and with those words, utterly abandoning any duty to actually explain, left Otto thoroughly confused.
