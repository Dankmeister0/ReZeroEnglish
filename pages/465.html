Arc 6 Chapter 60: "A Single Patch of Sunlight"

—The "Corridor of Memories" and "Rui Arneb."

"——"

Subaru fell silent before Rui, the blonde girl who had answered his question.
It was an unexpected space of white, and an unexpected encounter with a girl he hadn't foreseen at all. And to make matters worse, this girl seemed to be in the mood to explain all sorts of things with a knowing look on her face—

"...To begin with, what the hell is a Witch Cult, or 'Gluttony,' or a Great Sin Bishop, whatever that is?"

"Ahaha!"

Arms crossed, head tilted, Subaru's words made Rui clap a hand over her mouth and burst into laughter.
If you froze just that single moment, it might have made a fine painting—a girl standing in a fantastical, chalk-white landscape. But Subaru's instincts had been blaring nonstop since a while ago.

Having grown up in the peaceful, present-day Japan, Subaru's survival instincts were probably not much use most of the time. That they were screaming this loudly said everything about how alien this girl's presence was.
And—well, this was half unavoidable, given the unexpected situation he'd been thrown into—but against this knowing, all-too-informed presence, Subaru's own store of knowledge was woefully inadequate.

Witch Cult, Great Sin Bishop, and for that matter this "Od" whatever-it-was and the Corridor of Memories—maybe all of this was common knowledge in this world, but unfortunately Subaru didn't have that kind of common sense to draw on.
Which made it hard to even ask a follow-up question—

"—The Witch Cult, to put it simply, is a gathering of this world's most hated people."

"Oh?"

"Even you must've at least heard the name 'Witch of Envy,' right, mister? The Witch Cult has deep ties to that Witch... well, think of them as her believers, more or less."

"...So then, what just happened was a Great Sin Bishop?"

Great Sin and Bishop—quite the contradictory pairing in feel, but naming-sense-wise it clicked well enough for Subaru. After all, hear that combination and you instantly know—

"That it's the name of a villain."

"Rude, rude, rude, so rude, ru-u-ude, how rude of you—"

Shaking her head reluctantly, Rui wrapped her own slender body against Subaru's gaze. And yet the smile never left her lips; her refusal was only for show.
A girl whose true feelings couldn't be read. No—vague and slippery would be more accurate.

"Don't bully a poor defenseless girl with words like that, mister. We get hurt too, you know? We happen to have an unusually delicate, easily wounded heart."

"Not convincing at all. And that first-person pronoun of yours—is that some kind of character quirk? Or are you going for a cool 'one-man army' vibe? You keep switching between 'we' and 'us,' it's not consistent."

"Ahh... don't worry about it. I've just got a few too many egos going, so it's a bit up in the air which one's in charge at any given moment. Honestly I'm getting kind of sick of it myself."

As she spoke, Rui glanced down briefly, then added, "But," and continued,

"Can't be helped, though. It's a gift from big brother and big bro, after all, and if I don't properly accept it I'd be a failure as a little sister. Siblings have to help each other out, don't they?"

"...What a devoted, adorable little sister. I'm an only child, so I'm almost jealous."

"Are you now? Who knows, maybe by now you've got yourself a little brother or sister too, mister?"

"Don't say scary things like that!? I don't want to imagine it!?"

Still, his parents were a close, loving couple, so it wasn't entirely a joke.
If those two found out Subaru had vanished, would they really try for another child—no. Impossible.

"——"

If Subaru disappeared, his parents were the type to keep searching until they found him.
What had his mother thought of the son who left without even saying goodbye? What had his father felt, hearing about it?

Subaru found himself hoping—please, let his summoning to this other world have been a reincarnation instead.
Rather than make his parents suffer the agony of endlessly searching for a son who'd vanished, he wanted them to be told that he had died and been reborn into another world. That would be so much easier. So much more of a mercy.
So—

"—I get it, mister."

"—! Don't screw with me!"

Cradling the hair that carpeted the floor in both hands, Rui peered up at Subaru from below, and he flared with fury.
He hadn't said his current anxiety out loud. She'd simply looked at his dark expression and spoken as if she understood his heart.
That made him furious beyond reason, and Subaru shouted at Rui and turned his back on her.

"Like hell you understand me! Spouting whatever you—"

"—You feel sorry for your dad and mom, don't you? You regret being such an unfilial son, not even saying a proper goodbye. No, you've regretted it this whole time. Then and now, right?"

"——"

Still spouting words like some know-it-all who thought she understood him, Rui gently wrapped her arms around Subaru's back from behind.
A small, light body. Subaru held his breath and stiffened.
Not because of the girl pressing against him. But because of the content of what she'd said.
That know-it-all remark had, without question, struck at a piece of his heart with unerring accuracy.

"Wondering how I know? Of course I know. I mean, there's no one in the world who understands you better than we do, mister."

"—Don't touch me!"

"Aww."

Shaking off her arms, breathing hard, Subaru put distance between them, and Rui pouted.
What was with this girl, seriously? Did the women of this world have no hesitation whatsoever about physical contact with men? Way too familiar.
And what scared him was how close he'd come to letting his weakened heart lean into that warmth.

"What ARE you? What are you trying to say!?"

"We just want you to feel at ease, mister, that's all. It's fine, it's fine. We've properly made our peace with our feelings for dad and mom. Even if it was one-sided, we feel like we faced it. Our heart's all cleared up. On the surface, anyway."

"——"

Laughing, Rui dug the nails of her right hand into her own left arm. With a force painful just to watch, she began scratching and gouging at her thin, pale arm.
As Subaru frowned at the act, Rui showed him her unnaturally red, long tongue and said,

"On the surface, totally healthy. Looks like there's nothing at all weighing on the heart. You're good at it, mister. It's sad how good you are at it, mister."

Something about that statement clawed viciously at his chest, and Subaru's mouth twisted.
Following the warning bell of his instincts, now ringing in a different key, he decided he shouldn't indulge this any further.

"I don't really get what you're trying to say. I don't get it, but stop, you'll hurt yourself. Let's play catch with this conversation instead. No fastballs. Let's aim for nice, easy arcs."

"Both of us?"

"Both of us. So, for instance... let's go back to what you were saying about the Great Sin Bishops."

Not wanting to be swept along by Rui's pace any further, Subaru steered the topic back a step.
If "Taizai Shikyou" meant Great Sin Bishop, then he had some idea of the connection between "Great Sin" and the word "Gluttony."

"If you're 'Gluttony,' shouldn't there be about six more like you?"

"If you count big brother and big bro, that makes exactly six, maybe? Ah, but two of them died recently, so it's probably four now. Those two could've just died sooner, honestly."

"...Judging by that tone, not much team spirit going on there."

"Obviously not. We call ourselves Great Sin Bishops and all, but at the end of the day we're just a bunch of people the world hates. Just a different name—same thing as the 'Witch.'"

Saying that, Rui plopped down onto the floor, sitting with her knees folded. In doing so, she seemed to sink into her own long golden hair, creating an oddly comical scene.
Scratching his head, Subaru sat down in a spot where he wouldn't step on her hair, and tilted his head. "Witch?"

"'Same as the Witch'—you mean the one everyone apparently finds so terrifying?"

"I mean, admittedly the 'Witch of Envy' is worse-natured than us, so I don't want to be lumped in with her, but the rest, sure. The 'Witch' and the Great Sin Bishops are the same thing, just called different names. Just a bunch of good-for-nothings who matched with a Witch Factor, called different things depending on the era and their position."

"——"

"Well, right now you don't remember the 'Witch' or the Great Sin Bishops or us, so maybe it doesn't matter either way to you. I get it, I get it, I do get it, because I get it, and precisely because I get it, I want to keep on getting it..."

"Shut up."

"Aww."

Subaru held up a hand against the wave of rambling words crashing over him and rested his chin on it.
Somehow, he felt like he was being told something important. None of it clicked for Subaru personally, but it seemed like he wouldn't have to go back to Emilia and the others completely empty-handed.

But that wasn't why Subaru found himself lost in thought here.
What made Subaru fall silent and think was the by-now-unavoidable sense of discomfort—no, familiarity—in the way Rui had been responding to him this whole time.
If there was one thing he could point to as the reason—

"You're, what, some kind of god-type thing?"

"God-type... ah, is that it? Like some isekai reincarnation thing? I don't really get it, but that's got nothing to do with us. Though I guess this might be the kind of place that gives you weird feelings."

Giggling, still sitting on her own golden hair, Rui spun herself around in place, her hair fluttering to gesture at the empty white world.

"This place is exactly what it looks like. A place where everything disappears, and so, a place with nothing. Being here all alone in a place like that, I guess it does look like I'm some kind of guardian deity of this place, huh?"

"The Cradle of Od Lagna, was it? Along with the name 'Corridor of Memories,' none of it makes a lick of sense to me, start to finish."

"Hmm, hmm, hmm, hmmm, well, yeah. ...To put it simply, this is a place where souls get filtered."

"Filtered? Souls?"

Unfamiliar with the expression, a question mark floated up in Subaru's head.
Filtered—meaning something like purified—but he'd rarely heard that term applied to souls.
Still, Rui said "That's right, that's right" happily, pulling her knees in close, and continued,

"When you use a rag once, you wash it and dry it and use it again, right? Souls are the same. You wash off the grime that's built up, and reuse it again in a clean state."

"That grime that's built up—you mean, like, memories? Experience?"

"If that's easier for you to understand, sure, go with that. Whatever works for you, mister."

Making a face at Rui, who stuck her tongue out at him, Subaru twisted his cheek and turned his head around to scan his surroundings.
As ever, the white space—the Corridor of Memories—held nothing new to look at. In this endless white world, there was no sign of the kind of clear-cut physical evidence Rui had been describing.

If this place really was as Rui had said, shouldn't there be souls floating around, or filtered memories and experiences visualized in some form?

"It's not something that convenient."

"That Od Lagna god sounds pretty spiteful, then."

"It's not some grand thing like a god, that. There's no such fine philosophy behind it. It's just a mechanism. A mechanism so the world doesn't get broken."

"A mechanism..."

"Witch Factors, Divine Protections, the 'Sword Saint,' the 'Witch'—none of it registers with Od Lagna at all. If there's anything good about Od Lagna, it's that it's equal, fair, and indifferent without favoritism."

Narrowing her eyes as if bored, Rui pressed her face between her drawn-up knees. Watching sidelong as she distorted her cheek against her pale kneecap, Subaru let out a small breath.
She'd been remarkably candid this whole time. Probably, he thought, she hadn't told any real lies. That was exactly why Subaru exhaled.

He breathed out, breathed in, breathed out once more, and then looked at her.
And asked.

"—Was it you who took my memories, up through yesterday?"

"Yep."

The culprit answered his question without the slightest hesitation.

"——"

His question affirmed so easily, Subaru closed his eyes.

Somehow he'd had a feeling she wouldn't deny it. In their brief exchange, he'd sensed she wasn't the type to do that.

Rui knew too much. She knew Subaru's inner state far, far too well.
Rui Arneb was intimately familiar with "Natsuki Subaru," down to details even the present Natsuki Subaru himself couldn't have known.
That went beyond anything explainable by mere "remarkable powers of observation," and that was exactly why Subaru had asked her plainly, and gotten his confirmation.

Strangely, what welled up in his chest wasn't anger, but acceptance.
In other words—

"—So yesterday's me came here too, huh."

"Strictly speaking, the way you got here was a little different. But well, the goal was the same. Just the result came out a bit differently. But wow. Amazing. How many times did it take you to get this far?"

"——"

"Hey, answer me, mister. We answered you, didn't we? —How many mister are you, since we ate you?"

A chill shot up Subaru's spine at Rui's question.
Her eyes, her question—clearly the manner of someone who was certain about Return by Death.

Well, of course. Naturally.
If she had taken "Natsuki Subaru's" memories, and could freely browse through them, there was nothing strange about her knowing of Return by Death.
Return by Death wasn't a power that belonged only to the memory-less Subaru of now—it was undoubtedly a power "Natsuki Subaru" had possessed even before losing his memory.

Surely "Natsuki Subaru" had used this power to survive countless crises. The result was Emilia, Beatrice, the trust of his companions—proof of what you might call a cheat, a bit of unfairness.
Subaru had no intention of condemning that. Cheat or foul play, when someone's life was on the line, there was no reason to hesitate using it. "Natsuki Subaru's" choice was the right one. So now, with "Natsuki Subaru" gone, Subaru would do the same.

And so, acknowledging its usefulness, he had swallowed the power of Return by Death.
And yet, contrary to that resolve, a strange unease, a misgiving, sat in Subaru's chest.
That inescapable dread, that taboo—Rui Arneb was violating it.
And that was—

"—You mean, that it's not supposed to be known? Too late for that, mister. I mean, we met you yesterday too, didn't we?"

"——"

"If there's a 'rule' about not letting it be known, we broke that ages ago. But what happens in the Corridor of Memories doesn't leak out easily. That's why the scary, scary 'Witch' doesn't move."

Still with her hands and feet on the ground, Rui leaned her face in close to Subaru, who sat cross-legged. She wore a seductive smile unbefitting her age, her red tongue flickering as she said,

"So, mister. How many times?"

At that voice, like a tongue directly prodding his brainstem, Subaru felt a numbing throb of pain.
Then, his tongue and throat trembling faintly, he said,

"...Five times."

"—! Amazing, so amazing, it's amazing, how amazing, isn't it amazing, it's just so amazing, precisely because it's amazing, because I admire it so much... Gluttony! Gorge!"

"Guh!"

"I want to savor you so much my stomach could burst, mister! From our experience, appetite and lust are pretty similar. And lust, well, that's love, right? Which means we—"

Shoving Subaru down and straddling him, Rui breathed hot, excited breaths into his face. Her cheeks flushed, her eyes entranced, she ran her tongue along Subaru's neck without hesitation.
And as she continued, Subaru could guess exactly what words were coming next—

"—I love you."

The memory surfaced of the words of love hurled at him again and again in the previous loop, when Subaru, in a hopeless situation, had wished for "death"—and his heart exploded.

"—Don't touch me, you little pervert!"

"—ow."

Eyes wide, Subaru grabbed the collar of the girl pinning him down and forcibly slammed her onto the white floor. Reversing their positions, this time it was Subaru straddling her.
A slender, light body. With her long golden hair spread across the floor, as though he'd pushed her down onto a bed of gold, Subaru pressed a hand to her throat and bared his teeth.

"Let your guard down? Too bad for you! In this position, I've got the overwhelming advantage! Unless you want me choking you right now, give me back my memor—"

"You mean, give them back? If we don't, you'll strangle us? Our poor, fragile little throat?"

Her throat gripped, her life and death in his hands, Rui gazed at the heavily breathing Subaru, her eyes still carrying the same unfaded excitement from before, and loosened her lips.
Then, from those loosened lips, she asked in a voice like a curse,

"You think you can actually do that, mister?"

"—You think I can't?"

"Not think—know. I mean, look, right now we know you better than you know yourself, mister."

As she spoke, Rui poked her own cheeks with her index fingers, tilting her head provocatively. Holding his breath at her attitude, Subaru looked down at his right hand gripping Rui's throat.

All he had to do was put a little strength into his arm, to show her a bit of his resolve.
If he proved he was serious, surely Rui would reconsider.

Could he kill her? That, admittedly, was hard. Difficult.
Even with the noble excuse of a mercy killing, Subaru hadn't been able to bring a rock down on a companion's head.
So even knowing she was an enemy, could he really tighten his grip on the throat of the girl in front of him—

No—choking her, at least, he could manage. It was necessary.
He pictured Emilia, Beatrice, Ram, Meili, Julius, Echidna, Shaula, Patrasche—everyone. And then.
And then—

"—The strength's drained right out of your arm, mister."

"——"

"We really weren't planning to resist, you know? Because here, we're exactly what we look like—a fragile little girl. Unlike big brother and big bro, we can't draw out our power unless we take the form of whoever we ate."

Eiya, Rui poked at Subaru's right hand, still gripping her throat, with the same finger she'd used to poke her own cheek. With that feeble amount of force, Subaru's palm slipped off her throat all too easily.

"Damn it...!"

"Don't get so down, mister. You did great, great. Because honestly... we didn't even think you'd be able to make it back here."

"You think that's supposed to comfort me?"

Still in the position of having pinned her down, Rui, lying on the ground, already wore the expression of someone past the worst of it. Subaru searched for words to break through that expression and press his demand, but nothing quite right came to mind.
So in the end, watching Subaru fall into what amounted to sour grapes, Rui let out an "ahaha," a sound like someone observing fish swimming in a tank, and said,

"But hey, isn't it a good thing, really? If you got 'Natsuki Subaru's' memories back, you'd die, mister—this way, at least you don't have to do something as stupid as commit suicide."

"...Ah?"

"Huh, what's with that weird reaction? Don't tell me you hadn't noticed? When the memories come back, they overwrite your current self, and this existence disappears. ...That's basically the same as dying, isn't it?"

—At Rui's attitude, as though answering an obvious riddle, Subaru froze.

He would die. Vanish. Told, plainly, just like that.
Told that once "Natsuki Subaru's" memories returned, the consciousness that was here now—Subaru's memories—would be overwritten and disappear.
If that wasn't "death," then what was it—

"—Right now, 'Natsuki Subaru' is dead, isn't he? He's nowhere at all. But if 'Natsuki Subaru' comes back, this time you'll die, mister. You won't have anywhere to go."

"——"

"So, like, is it really worth it, going that far to get 'Natsuki Subaru' back? You can do the same thing, can't you, mister? You must've come to like the people around you too, the same way. And the people around you must've come to like you back, the same way. —What's so wrong with that?"

"What's wrong..."

If asked what was wrong with it, surely nothing was.
Neither for "Natsuki Subaru," nor for Natsuki Subaru, was there anything wrong at all.
Subaru was a person full of flaws. So full of flaws he disgusted himself. Ask him who was the person he hated most in this world, and without hesitation he could answer: himself.
That was how hopelessly, thoroughly inadequate Natsuki Subaru was.
But on this particular matter, there was no fault on Subaru's part.

—There was only the one, inescapable fact: that there was only room for one patch of sunlight.

"I wanted... for Emilia and the others..."

He'd wanted to give "Natsuki Subaru" back to them.
That was why, he'd told himself, if the chance to reclaim his memories came, he wouldn't hesitate to seize it—that was the resolve he thought he'd steeled himself with.

But he had been averting his eyes from the fact that his own existence would completely disappear.
If he allowed himself the convenient thought, maybe the two sets of memories would blend, or some piece of the current Subaru's existence would linger somewhere inside "Natsuki Subaru"—he'd been hoping some kind of miracle would resolve things into a good shape.
Such an uncertain hope of Subaru's—

"Who knows? We've never seen anyone get their memories back, so we wouldn't know."

Rui bared her teeth as though mocking Subaru's inner conflict, her eyes like a cat toying with a mouse.
This culprit, who had stolen these memories, irresponsibly declared she had no idea what happened afterward. It wasn't a lie—surely, it was the truth.

Rui Arneb didn't return what she'd taken from others to their original owners.
So she genuinely had no idea what would become of Natsuki Subaru as a result of the memories returning.

"Mister, you've got this life you were given, so you've gotta live it up."

This irresponsible usurper, so close to Subaru's face, continued.

"Because we ate 'Natsuki Subaru's' memories, you're here, mister. Which basically makes us like your birth parent, doesn't it? Choosing to die right in front of your own parent—isn't that being an unfilial child, mister?"

"That's such a ridiculous thing to—"

"—Memories are what shape a person, mister."

Low, cold, her expression gone blank, Rui spoke that one line in an unusually serious tone.
Involuntarily, at the sharpness of that resonance, Subaru held his breath and fell silent.
At the same time, he felt like it wasn't the first time he'd heard that phrase. That resonance, those words—hadn't he heard something like it, perhaps, right before facing his second "death"—

"The current you has relationships that the current you built, mister. Why not try living freshly, positively, all over again? We think that's one option too."

"——"

"Besides, this might be a rude thing to say, but... 'Natsuki Subaru' isn't exactly the ideal picture of a man, you know?"

Closing one eye, Rui, with an expression like she was reluctant to say it, struck at Subaru's heart.
Still straddled atop Subaru, she clasped her hands in front of her chest and, with eyes like a dreaming maiden, stared into his dark eyes—

"Poor, poor Emilia! Just because her birth resembles a Witch from the past, she's shunned and pitied by everyone! Ahh, and yet how kind I am, to stay by her side even so!"

"Wha—"

"Weak, fragile Beatrice! With no one to rely on, a lonely girl who's spent all her time in solitude! I have to be the one to guide her down that dark, precarious path myself!"

Before the stunned Subaru, Rui went on reciting, resonant and clear, the names of the two girls who had prayed for his safety and sent him off, and grotesquely twisted impressions of them.
Whose impressions these actually were, and what Rui was trying to say by all this—Subaru understood.
He understood, but even so—

"Devoted, selflessly loving Rem! Foolish and beautiful, oh so pure. Surely she's an incomplete being who can only feel truly alive by desperately dedicating herself to someone other than herself. This is exactly the sort of person I have to guide and lead!"

"What... what are you trying to do!?"

"I'm just voicing what 'Natsuki Subaru' actually thought. What he wanted was a sense of superiority. He never once really thought it was for someone else's sake. He just surrounded himself with convenient people and got drunk on the pleasure of extending a helping hand. Any stray dog that won't warm up to him, he doesn't feed. He pushes it away."

"——"

"Do you really intend to hand your current self over to a 'Natsuki Subaru' like that?"

The question, laid over him once more.
It was a demand from Rui, from "Gluttony," for Natsuki Subaru's confession.

Speak your true feelings, Rui was demanding of Subaru.
Whether he didn't want to die, whether he wanted to die, and if he were to die, whether he would die for a man like that.

—No, Subaru wasn't going to die for the sake of "Natsuki Subaru."
—No, to begin with, Subaru hadn't intended to die at all. He'd wanted to give "Natsuki Subaru" back for Emilia and the others' sake, but that and his own death were—
—No—but could he really trust "Natsuki Subaru"? At the very least, "Natsuki Subaru" had killed Meili, and carved incomprehensible letters even onto the wall...

"—So, what do you want to do, mister?"

"—!"

As she spoke, Rui took hold of Subaru's hand with her slender arm and pressed it against her own throat.
Once again, this time guiding it herself, Subaru's arm was placed against Rui's thin neck. If he put strength into both arms, surely it would break with ease.

He'd just concluded that he couldn't do it.
But not doing it might amount to the same thing as choosing to kill "Natsuki Subaru." —At the very least, that was what Rui was saying.

Suppose, just suppose, he broke this thin neck, and as a result "Natsuki Subaru" came back and the current Subaru vanished—would the outcome of that action, too, become invisible to him?
If so, then what would be the meaning of Subaru scraping together what little courage he had left to act here?

"Well?"

"——"

"What'll it be? What are you gonna do? What is it? What're you doing? What're you gonna do about it? What do you want to do? How do you want to do it? You can do it however you like. It's fine whatever happens. It's fine to just let it happen, so. We'll forgive you no matter what you do—"

Taunting, mocking, cursing, Rui's words struck against Subaru's eardrums.
Rui Arneb, "Gluttony," a Great Sin Bishop, a slender girl, a hated existence, a birth parent.

She was forcing Natsuki Subaru to choose what to do with "Natsuki Subaru."

"Well?"

Well.

"—What do you want to do, mister?"
