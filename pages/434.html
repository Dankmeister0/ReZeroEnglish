Re:Zero EX: "Life in an Erroneous World from Zero"

This is the annual April Fools' Day special project. As this is a parallel universe story to the main line, the character relationships have changed in various ways, though some characters remain unchanged. Please only read if you are fine with that.

――What dominated his consciousness was an intense 'heat' emanating from the center of his body.

"Ghh, uuuugh! It's so... hot!"

Feeling the hard ground against his face, Subaru realized he was lying facedown. And yet, his limbs would not move to let him stand up. As if he were no longer himself, the freedom of his body had been stolen away.

And yet, it was no joke that the scorching heat burned Subaru without end.

――Hot, hot, hot, hot, hot, hot, hot.

When he opened his mouth, what spilled out instead of a scream was a large quantity of blood. At the end of his pain and suffering, on the verge of drowning in his own overflowing blood, Subaru was forced to thoroughly taste the absolute height of torment in this world.

――Just what in the world did I do?

A desire to escape the suffering caused this tearful complaint to spin repeatedly in his mind.

Just what in the world did I do? He was aware that his way of living was hardly one to be praised. But that was not unique to Subaru. No one lived a life so pristine that they could proudly boast about every single day to all of humanity. Everyone must have felt guilty, felt regret, turned a blind eye, or compromised at some point.

And yet, why did he alone have to suffer a fate like this? When every other human being was granted a free pass by fate, why was he the only one forced to shoulder this?

"Ah, damn it..."

Gurglingly, a murmur leaked through the gaps of overflowing blood. It was regret, hatred for his own helplessness, resentment toward fate, and――

――It was also disgust with himself.

"――――"

Even if he was hurt this much, even if he suffered this much, even if he met with a death-like ordeal this much. Even if his body was burned by scorching heat, even if his flesh was literally carved up by sharp pain, even if his very life was threatened.

Because the image of the girl burned into his mind repeatedly returned only with her smile and her tragic face in death.

"I will"

Now, once again, he gave voice to his resolve. He gave voice to his determination. He gave voice to his lingering regrets, his remorse. Seeking a future that remained unreachable no matter how many times he challenged it, no matter how many times he crawled, no matter how many times he wished for it.

With both the 'pain' and the 'heat' drifting far away, the loser bared his teeth pitifully, barking like the loser he was.

"――definitely"

The swung murder blade mercilessly closed in on the dying ember of his life just moments before it was snuffed out. However, even that did not enter his sight. His resolve was already absolute.

――I will save you.

The instant he harbored that wish once more, Natsuki Subaru lost his life.

――Upon beholding the familiar sight once more, Subaru felt a strange sense of relief and weakness.

"Safely made it back, much to my relief... or something like that."

Looking out at the traffic of the Royal Capital, Subaru drew tally marks on the ground with a picked-up wooden branch, and after continuing until he was satisfied, he stomped them out with his foot and sighed.

Natsuki Subaru was an ordinary, school-refusing third-year high school student from Earth, the third planet of the Solar System. Looking at his rough appearance—a tracksuit, sneakers, and a plastic convenience store bag in hand—no one would doubt that fact. However, that logic only held true in a cultural sphere where foreign terms like tracksuit, sneakers, and convenience store made sense and shared common values. Which was to say, what he meant was――

"――Isekai summoning stories are a lot tougher than I thought, huh."

A lizard as massive as a horse pulled a carriage, kicking up a cloud of dust as it crossed before his eyes. Naturally, such a sight could not be seen in the Earth realm Subaru knew. If asked whether that was a horse in special effects makeup—a theme park cast member of sorts—he couldn't easily nod at that either.

Because that lizard wasn't just one beast; they overflowed throughout the streets and the city, and among the people commanding those lizards, far too many looked like they were wearing special effects makeup. Humanoid beasts—people dressed as races like Beast-humans or Demi-Humans—numbered at least twenty or thirty within Subaru's field of vision alone. Of course, there were plenty of general visitors sharing Subaru's human appearance, but they were buried under the violent individuality of outward appearances and barely registered in his consciousness.

In the first place, he couldn't afford to waste his thoughts on such escapism forever. Neither the Beast-humans nor the lizards were cast members or a movie shoot. This was a cultural realm where such things were commonplace, and here, the alien element was not them, but Subaru himself.

――Subaru had already come to realize this painfully well after spending over ten days feeling it firsthand.

"...Time to go."

Cutting off his useless thoughts, he patted his rear and stood up. Holding the picked-up wooden branch in his right hand, Subaru walked with faint, aimless steps toward the street――not blending into the crowd on the main thoroughfare, but stepping into a back alley.

Unlike the main street crammed with people, the back alley sandwiched between tall buildings had few signs of life. That place, fully dressed as befitted an alleyway, possessed a silence as though it were isolated from the outside world by a single street. In short, no matter what happened here, screams would hardly ever reach the main thoroughfare.

Therefore, when Subaru wandered into such a place with a face practically screaming 'country bumpkin', villains looking for an easy target naturally made their appearance.

"Hey, pal. Why don't you come hang out with us for a bit?"

When he turned at the voice calling out to him, three shadows stood blocking the path. With a lineup neatly gathered to suit every need—large, medium, and small—Subaru felt their earnest commitment to research. Though he didn't know research into what.

"――――"

Tilting his head, he looked at the path opposite the trio. Behind him, this alley was a dead end, and if he wanted to get out to the main street, he had no choice but to have the trio let him through. And as for whether the trio had any intention of letting Subaru out to the main street――

"Hey, hey, what's with that dazed look?"

"He doesn't get the situation. Wouldn't it be better to teach him?"

At Subaru's sluggish behavior, the trio wore vulgar sneers and began plotting how to torment him. From their perspective, Subaru was a complete amateur. A convenient mark—he couldn't blame them for licking their lips before him.

However, that was entirely a flaw in their calculations. To be sure, Subaru had never been in a real fight, nor did he have a hidden past of learning martial arts. Their assessment of him as an amateur was not wrong.

However, if it came to experience against this specific trio, Subaru was already a battle-hardened veteran.

"――Ah?"

Aiming at the careless giant—naming the trio Ton, Chin, and Kan, he thrust his arm out toward Ton. Grasped in that arm was the wooden branch he had picked up in the street earlier, and its tip, while not particularly sharp, bit into the soft part of the throat, piercing through after a moment's resistance.

"Hey?"

At that instantaneous occurrence, Ton's eyes widened, and Chin and Kan standing to his left and right froze in shock. With this, Ton was out of commission. And toward the frozen pair, reaching his free hand to the one of average build――Chin, Subaru grabbed his hair and ear and swung him around. He gave him no room to resist. Using momentum, he slammed Chin's head into the adjacent wall all at once. The sound of something hard crushing rang out, and Chin collapsed while smearing a trail of blood on the wall.

At the same time, Ton, with the wooden branch still stuck in his throat, dropped to his knees and fell over, rolling back his eyes. A bad way to fall. Because he fell forward, the branch thrust even deeper.

With this, two were rendered incapable of fighting. Remaining was just one last person――

"Hih..."

The small man――Kan's face turned pale as his two companions were brought down in an instant. If he had abandoned the two and sprinted out to the street just like that, it would have been a contest of running speed against Subaru, and he might have had a chance to survive. And yet, Kan looked at the two fallen men and hesitated to flee. Not knowing it was already too late, he wasted that limited, single second of grace in vain.

Fool. Utter fool. Suffer the retribution for making such a foolish choice.

"Guh, ga, ahhh..."

Wrapping his hand around the thin neck, Subaru applied force and slammed Kan against the wall behind him. He lifted the small, struggling body while keeping it pressed against the wall. Applying strength to both hands, he choked him tighter and tighter. Lifted until his eyes were at the same height as Subaru's, Kan's eyes widened as he was strangled, his loosely hanging mouth gasping for oxygen. However, with his airway forcefully shut and his freedom taken away, no salvation would come to him.

"Do you know how many times in total I've met you guys now?"

"Gu, kho..."

"Eighty-eight times. 'Eighty-eight' is a lucky number for prosperity, they say. How about a laugh?"

Gazing fiercely into the face that was gradually turning reddish-black and dripping tears and drool, Subaru spoke. However, naturally, Kan possessed no such leeway. Soon, losing not just leeway but the power to resist, Subaru threw the limp body onto the spot.

Looking down at the fallen three, just in case, he forcefully stomped on each of their necks once. Feeling the sensation of the sneaker's heel crushing something hard gave him peace of mind for the time being. Only Ton's neck was thick, requiring him to carefully stomp it five times. He just couldn't get the hang of it no matter how many times he did it. Though on lucky occasions, it was dealt with in a single stomp each.

"Strangling isn't very smart. ...The feeling is gross too, so let's stop doing that."

Based on those reflections, Subaru borrowed two knives from Chin's body. Then, dragging the three corpses appropriately to the side of the path, he left the back alley as if nothing had happened.

Entering the alley and running into Ton, Chin, and Kan had been set in motion quite early. He had spent a little time handling Ton, Chin, and Kan, but it was a matter of mere seconds. Subaru hurriedly cut through the main street and stepped forward toward his destination.

"――――"

Gazing at the state of the street, he stroked his chest in relief, having made it in time. The main thoroughfare called the commercial district—there, too, was filled with a tide of people shuffling left and right no different from anywhere else, fulfilling its duty of proving the bustles of the Royal Capital of Lugunica. Even just standing there, the clamor leaped into his eardrums more than enough. However, the atmosphere of that lively hustle suddenly shifted into a different form.

"――Wait! Geez! Wait up!!"

Rending the clamor of the main thoroughfare above all else was a beautiful, silver-bell voice. Pregnant with a sense of urgency, yet somehow unable to hide the gentle nature of its owner, that voice was directed toward a small shadow slipping through the crowd on the street.

"Heheh!"

Spilling a cat-like grin, slipping through the gaps between people was a blonde girl. Held in her hand was something gleaming, vividly revealing the sense of having accomplished a job. And then, aimed at that girl, a pale blue radiance――an ice spear flew across the street.

"――gh!!"

Surprised by the unexpected attack, the girl evaded the ice spear using leaps and bounds to the fullest. That magical attack had been fired on a street filled with people, and as confusion arose from the sudden turn of events, the citizens of the Royal Capital cleared the path all at once, raising both hands to declare they wanted no involvement. Extremely coordinated movements. Saying that getting caught in trouble was everyday life in the Royal Capital might be an overstatement, but it might be close to that.

At any rate, running through the crowd that cleared the path, making her appearance was the target person.

"――――"

The moment he laid eyes on 'her', Subaru tasted a sensation as though the entire world had frozen over. Neither the wind, nor people's voices, nor even time could be felt, and every shred of his consciousness was stolen away by that body.

Fluttering her long silver hair, making her deep purple-blue eyes shine with intense awareness. White, slender limbs, and a mystical white robe that seemed tailored for a fairy――

In a world where time had stopped, she alone escaped the standstill of time, passing by in front of Subaru.

Her goal was the blonde girl who had slipped past just moments before――Felt. Having had something stolen by Felt, she would run all over the Royal Capital to retrieve it. And for her, that was the path leading toward an unavoidable fate of misfortune.

However, he would not let that happen. He would absolutely not allow her to be caught by the fate of 'death'.

"I will definitely save you."

At her receding back, Subaru swore his eighty-eighth vow. Just how much persuasiveness such a repeatedly broken vow possessed, he did not know. He did not know, but if he didn't give up, if he kept resisting, if he kept wishing to save her――

"Wait for me. ――Satella."

The power to rewind time upon death: 'Return by Death'. With this power of 'Return by Death', this was the eighty-eighth time Subaru was redoing the world.

It was a challenge already approaching the triple-digit milestone, but every bit of it had been spent for the sake of saving the silver-haired girl――Satella――from fate's dead end.

"I've tried all sorts of trial and error, but... it doesn't go like Ton, Chin, and Kan."

And that was Subaru's conclusion regarding the greatest adversary in this fate――Elsa Granhiert.

The item stolen from Satella, the insignia—the one who commissioned Felt to steal it was that pitch-black woman named Elsa. This woman was the dangerous individual who drove Satella to her death time and time again. So far, over the course of eighty-seven attempts, Subaru had scrambled repeatedly to eliminate Elsa and secure Satella's safety, but faced with Elsa's combat prowess that was already in the realm of the non-human, he suffered defeat and lost his life with his belly sliced open more than fifty times.

A sadist who loved disemboweling, Elsa was not an opponent he could fight and beat. If it were still scrubs like Ton, Chin, and Kan, he could pattern their actions to some degree and devise a strategy for certain victory. However, at a level of strength like Elsa's, the instant Subaru tried to take action, it wouldn't be strange if his head were severed in the blink of an eye. In fact, instead of his head, his belly had been slashed open in the blink of an eye dozens of times.

Subaru could not fight and win. Felt, Old Man Rom, and Satella were the same. Taking that conclusion into account, the tactic Subaru chose was――

"Lovely! Simply lovely! I would be delighted if you could entertain me even more!"

"Guaaaah――!!"

Black blades swung one after another, and every time the wind whipped up, blood spurted out. The number of people falling onto the street had already exceeded what could be counted on both hands, and Elsa's expression, drenched in blood, went past ecstasy, appearing as though she were experiencing intermittent climaxes.

Despite being in such a gruesome situation, Elsa's allure only increased the more blood she was bathed in. In the past, there was a tale of a woman who attempted to maintain her youth by bathing in the fresh blood of virgins, referred to as a vampire, and right now Elsa looked precisely like that very 'vampire'.

"...This one's a failure too, huh."

Sighing, Subaru, lurking atop a roof, narrowed his eyes as he looked down at the tragedy.

The location was a corner of the slums, a particularly deserted section surrounded by ruins, and using a sort of plaza space as the stage, the gory tragedy was steadily expanding. Exchanging swords with Elsa and falling to her murderous blades one after another were the guards of the Royal Capital. Having received a report from a well-meaning citizen, discovering the dangerous individual Elsa Granhiert, and challenging her to capture her only to lose their lives――as a well-meaning citizen, it was a sight that made his chest ache.

"To think the difference in skill between regular guards and Elsa would be this huge..."

At this rate, it was no different from offering sacrifices to make Elsa happy. Subaru's expectation had been that if they were people in a position like guards protecting the Royal Capital, they might enhance their bodies through some kind of magical power and fight Elsa on equal terms, but that had apparently been nothing more than wishful thinking.

As expected, Elsa's strength was in an extraordinary realm even in this world. His resentment toward Elsa grew stronger and stronger. But no matter how strong his anger was, Subaru's power could not stop that murderous woman. He couldn't see Elsa being brought down by a lucky punch from the guards, either.

"Looking any further is just a waste, huh."

He felt sorry for the guards who became sacrifices, but he would end this attempt here. He had no intention of slacking off yet, but even those who died here would breathe again in the reset world if Subaru ultimately failed and lost his life. As sacrifices for that purpose, he would swallow his tears and let them stay dead for now. If there were a next time, he wouldn't expect anything from them, so they could peacefully protect the peace of the Royal Capital.

Just as Subaru reached that conclusion and tried to leave the area――

"――My."

Stopping in her tracks, Elsa, with her murderous blade raised, traced her blood-stained lips with her tongue. The brimming madness and bloodlust were proof above all else that Elsa, having grown bored of the game of massacring guards, had found a new prey. And chosen as Elsa's pitiful sacrifice was――

"――That's enough."

Subaru hallucinated whether a red flame was standing there.

"――――"

Gasping, rubbing his eyes, and looking properly once more, he was finally able to recognize that it was not fire, but a person.

It was a young man with red, flame-like hair and blue eyes that trapped the clear sky within them. Looking slender, but with a tall, flexibly trained body wrapped in white attire, he carried a single sword with an awfully grand mounting at his waist. His facial features were a divine sculpture so well-formed that if a hundred people walked by, all hundred would turn around and be mesmerized. Violence of beauty transcending gender—the reason it didn't feel overbearing was perhaps due to the young man's gentle demeanor.

A presence that made one's soul realize at a glance that he was a being different from the common crowd――

"Knight Reinhard...!"

"You men fall back. She is... the 'Bowel Hunter'. There are already too many sacrifices. I don't want to cause any more."

At the trembling voice of a surviving guard, the young man――Reinhard――said while faintly casting his eyes down. It was grief for the lives slaughtered like dust, and righteous indignation toward the slaughterer named Elsa who had done it.

Even to Subaru watching from afar, the heart of the young man named Reinhard was visible. What a man, getting angry for such proper reasons, under proper thinking, with a proper mindset.

Grieving death, hating slaughter, regretting his shortcomings, overwriting regret with conviction. That was the way of being for that young man called Reinhard――a knight.

"Reinhard van Astrea. Of the 'Sword Saint' lineage. Lovely, simply lovely!"

"Every day I feel like I'm about to be crushed under the weight of such overestimation. You are the 'Bowel Hunter', I assume?"

"Yes, that's right. Oh dear, what should I do? Even though I have my primary work to attend to, to think I would bump into you in a place like this."

Exhaling hot gasps in ecstasy, Elsa turned her heat-moistened eyes toward Reinhard. On the other hand, Reinhard's expression was dead serious, his blue pair of eyes possessing only a sense of duty with no playfulness whatsoever.

Facing each other with polar-opposite philosophies, the conclusion their hearts derived was identical.

That there was no option other than slaying each other to achieve their goal.

"Normally, I would recommend that you surrender, but..."

"Facing this tragedy, would you say that to me? How kind of you. Cruel to the point of cruelty, both to me and to them."

"――No, I share the same opinion. Showing you leniency here would not be faithful to their deaths. Therefore, I won't ask you to surrender."

At the words of Elsa, who joyfully kicked at the corpses of the fallen guards, Reinhard slowly shook his head. And extending his hand to a guard waiting nearby, he gave a short command: "Your sword."

"Please, go ahead."

When the instructed guard proffered his sword, Reinhard gripped it and tested the feel. At that sight, Elsa knit her brows in suspicion,

"You won't use the sword at your waist? I wanted to feel the sharpness of the legendary Dragon Sword against my skin."

"Regrettably, this sword possesses a trait of discerning opponents worth drawing against. It's a slightly troublesome trait, but it seems the sword does not favor you. Instead, I shall face you with this one."

"Hmm."

As Reinhard took a stance with the received sword rather than the one at his waist, Elsa sighed. However, her somewhat dissatisfied attitude lasted only a moment. Instantly switching gears, the woman licked her lips at the anticipation of blood and battle with the young man standing before her. And then――

"'Bowel Hunter', Elsa Granhiert."

"Of the 'Sword Saint' lineage, Reinhard van Astrea."

Exchanging names with each other, the two collided as they became the wind in the center of the plaza. The match was decided in a single clash, in just that one instant.

An unextraordinary slash—it generated a vortex of light, and the shockwave collapsed a section of the slums. The sword strike of the man called the 'Sword Saint' was truly 'power' itself.

Natsuki Subaru watched that scene with wide eyes. Without even understanding the cause of the tears running down his cheeks and his trembling knees――

Though it was a development different from what Subaru had set up and expected, it could be said that the ultimate objective of overthrowing Elsa had been fulfilled.

Reinhard's entry into the battle was unexpected, but if his wish came true, all's well that ends well. Thanks to that, the affair surrounding Satella's insignia would be resolved safely. As long as Elsa, their deal partner, didn't join up with them, Felt and Old Man Rom wouldn't do anything reckless to retain Satella's insignia either. It was hard to imagine Satella killing the two of them in anger. The story centered around the Loot House would come to a close with the minimum necessary characters. That was a desirable result for Subaru as well. Therefore――

"――Why are you helping me, I wonder?"

"You don't have to trust me. But if you're saying you're fine being surrounded by guards and losing your chance for revenge, slash me down and run off as you please."

"――――"

As if spitting it out, Subaru said to Elsa, who was huddled up leaning against the wall behind him. Her entire body was bloodstained, her black outfit torn in various places, exposing her fair skin. However, since painful scars were prominent on her skin, there wasn't a shred of room to feel embarrassment over that. To begin with, Subaru had zero interest in Elsa as a woman. He was merely utilizing an opportunity because he thought she could be of use.

"The guards are frantically searching for you. I guided them in the wrong direction, so it'll take them a while longer to notice this way. How are your wounds?"

"It hurts terribly. It hurts so much I feel like I'm going to die. Fufu, how lovely."

"That sensibility is beyond me. But I'd be in trouble if you died, so I want to manage somehow."

He had gone out of his way to take risks to guide Elsa toward survival. If she collapsed here, or worse, if Subaru got dragged into the guards' search, the accidentally generated Satella-survival route would vanish like foam on water. In that case, he would simply keep rolling the dice again until this outcome showed up, but still.

"Could you make a path to the southeast of the slums? If you do, I can meet up with my sister. She should prepare wound care and an escape route for me."

"Sister! Ha, shrewd of you to operate as sisters, and an irredeemable story."

It wasn't that she trusted him, nor would it make his stomach turn any less if she did, but at Elsa's words, Subaru formulated a plan to overcome this hurdle. In the process of fleeing, they had already drifted quite far south. From here, moving to the location Elsa specified wouldn't take long. With the so-called holy gold coins Elsa possessed, the slum dwellers Subaru bribed were interfering with the guards' search, guiding them the wrong way. There were no problems.

"What I don't understand is your objective."

"I want to make you owe me a favor. Someday, a time might come where it proves useful."

"A favor, is it? That's mysterious. ――When you want to kill me so very badly."

Lending Elsa a shoulder and helping her flee, he was told that. Right up close, Elsa's black eyes looked up at Subaru, trying to peer into the emotions deep within. However, there was no need to pry and look inside. The answer in Subaru's black eyes was just as Elsa had picked up on. Subaru wanted to kill Elsa right now if possible. However, if he challenged her without thinking of the consequences, Subaru couldn't beat even a dying Elsa, and in the first place, that was short-sighted.

Subaru currently assumed his situation to be a terribly skewed game board. A battle premised on considering every move, trial and error, and playing the optimal hand. If one thought of this like shogi, it wasn't so naive that victory could be gained by blindly chasing away every piece on the board. If possible, turn enemies into allies. Given the chance, do not hesitate to change positions.

For that sake, even an opponent he felt deep-seated hatred toward and wanted to kill had to be utilized.

"I want to kill you. I definitely will kill you someday. But not today."

"I see."

There was no need to hide it, so Subaru laid out his inner thoughts to Elsa. If Elsa were a cautious person, she would have killed Subaru on the spot to eliminate future troubles. However, Subaru had conviction that it wouldn't go that way.

It was a conviction of intent to kill that could only be harbored by Subaru, who had been killed by Elsa over eighty times out of those eighty-eight times.

"How lovely. Binding you and me together with hatred. Eventually, without fail. Surely your words will be proven to me. That is very, very lovely indeed."

"――――"

Loosening her blood-soaked red-dyed lips, Elsa smiled like a maiden in love. With that smile right before him, Subaru thought from the bottom of his heart that this woman was hideous.

"Shall we meet again without fail?"

"See ya, Big Brother. I'll thank you for Elsa's sake~"

Handing Elsa over to the young girl with deep blue hair who had joined them, Subaru breathed a sigh of relief. Waiting in the abandoned house Elsa had specified was a girl still young, in her early teens. At first, Subaru was surprised by her youth, but the girl treated Elsa's wounds with a practiced attitude, and once she finished preparations to quickly flee the Royal Capital, she left the abandoned house post-haste.

Barely securing a means of communication with Elsa was the reward Subaru gained. Whether it was a reward commensurate with the risks taken, he was not certain.

"Whether it is or not depends on my actions from here on out, huh."

Taking off his tracksuit jacket stained with dried blood, Subaru tied it around his waist and began walking. His feet pointed toward the opposite side of the slums, in the direction of the Loot House. At that place where Elsa had been unable to go, whether Satella had successfully retrieved her insignia—that alone was on his mind.

"――――"

Taking his time to reach the Loot House, Subaru widened his eyes at the sight.

"This was unexpected."

Spilling those words, what spread out in front of Subaru was the sight of the Loot House frozen solid. Rather than frozen solid, describing it as the Loot House being encased in ice was more accurate. Wondering what on earth had happened, he did a light inquiry with a nearby resident:

"The old man who was the boss of the Loot House and his granddaughter were dragged off by the guards. Word is they crossed some terrifying user of magic... I don't wanna get involved."

"Are those two, the granddaughter and the old man, safe? What about the magic user?"

"Heard they weren't hurt, but I didn't get a good look at either of 'em. That's enough, ain't it?"

Perhaps finding Subaru's desperate momentum eerie, the man shook off Subaru's arm and hastily melted into the darkness of the back alley, moving away. Watching his back go and ruminating on the man's words, Subaru stroked his chest in relief.

How much of the man's words were fact was unknown, but at least he wouldn't mistake news of deaths for what he just heard. Felt and Old Man Rom being caught by the guards couldn't be helped given the line of work they were in. They should eat prison food for a while and reflect on their actions.

And knowing that Satella was safely rescued just as Subaru wished――

"――Well then. All that's left is what I do from here."

Scratching his head, Subaru realized his objective had become empty. Summoned to an alternate world, granted a power like 'Return by Death', making full use of it to save that sweet, adorable silver-haired Half-Elf. To accomplish that, dying eighty-seven times to reach this point, but...

"Shoot. Would it be better if I died and redid it to pry out why Elsa was trying to steal the insignia from Satella...?"

He should have pried during the time he carried the injured Elsa, but he had completely forgotten. Even so, if he asked deeply into Elsa's circumstances back there and revealed that Subaru's sympathies lay with Satella, there was no telling how Elsa would have acted. As a result right now, both Subaru and Satella were alive. If that was the correct answer:

"If possible, I'd like to know more details about Satella, but..."

Where Satella came from, where she went, whether they could meet again—he didn't know. Until he found that out, there was the option of repeating, but... If there were another way besides that――

"――I wonder if you guys can teach me?"

In front of the ice-encased Loot House, Subaru turned around while putting both hands in his pockets. There were no signs of people. And yet, within Subaru's view, he could see countless human silhouettes standing.

Unremarkable faces, a moderate mix of men and women, young and old—an ununified group. If there was just one single thing unified within that group, it was their pairs of eyes.

Every single one had dead eyes. Dyed in madness, seeking ecstasy, for that purpose. Surely, if he looked in a mirror, Subaru would have the exact same eyes.

"――――"

Loosening his cheeks at that sentiment, Subaru looked up at the sky above. A moon, chillingly clear, silently looked down upon the frozen Loot House and the madmen.

It was nearly two months after that when Satella's identity was revealed in detail.

"The Royal Selection! A silver-haired Half-Elf! Recommended by Margrave Roswaal L. Mathers! Not Satella, but Emilia――!"

Pouncing on the detailed information that rushed in when further news had ceased without any progress, Subaru clapped his hands with an expression of delight.

The information that unexpectedly flowed in was from the notice of the 'Royal Selection' posted not only in the Royal Capital but throughout the kingdom following a decree from the Royal Castle. Collapsing on the spot from his knees, Subaru fell without support, rolling. Failing to reach Emilia, awkwardly rolling on the cobblestones past her side without breaking his fall.

Before long, lying flat on his back looking up at the sky, there was a blue firmament looking down upon the crimson Royal Capital. Sandwiched between red and blue, Natsuki Subaru met his end.

"Why?"

Closing his eyes, attempting to accept the end, Subaru noticed something and opened his eyelids. Standing right near the fallen Subaru, looking down, was Emilia. Tears falling from her eyes hit Subaru's cheek, making him open his eyes.

"Why?"

A question repeated over and over. What that 'why' meant, Subaru thought.

'Why' did you do such a thing? 'Why' did it have to end up like this? 'Why' did you come here to be killed by me?

Surely, various 'why's were there. And he wanted to give answers to all those 'why's, but the time left for Subaru was only a tiny bit.

So Subaru, as if exhaling a breath, put the answer onto his final breath.

"――I love you."

'Death' approached. The familiar 'death'.

Every time he met 'death', Subaru was taken to a dark, lonely place he didn't know. It was a place so truly, truly lonely that one couldn't endure it alone.

Every time he met 'death', Subaru was sent out from here. To that world where blood, pain, tears, wailing, and a tiny bit of beloved things existed.

However, it was fine now. He was satisfied now.

"――――"

He felt someone welcoming Subaru in this dim world was whispering something.

It was comfort, it was encouragement, it was confirmation, it was a confession.

Why was that? Even though this was not the beloved person Subaru sought. The beloved person, in a place that was not here, in a place where he was not, would have her wish fulfilled and be rewarded.

Because paying much in sacrifices for that, in the end he offered himself. So it was fine. Even if she didn't save him. Salvation was received at the very beginning.

"――――"

It was surely a call. Gently, affectionately, lovingly, his name was called.

So Subaru, while accepting his existence departing from the spiral of 'death' and fading away. While accepting it, responded to the words of '――――'.

"――――"

――Even if you reject me, I will never forget you.
