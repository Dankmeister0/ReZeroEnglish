Arc 6 Chapter 52: "God, Please Forgive Me"

――A horrifying sword dance was unfolding on the fifth floor of the open atrium.

"――――"

The eyepatched man let his long red hair whip about as he wielded his powerful body with total freedom—vertically, horizontally, every which way, utterly unrestrained—conjuring an unbelievable spectacle with nothing but a short, brittle stick clutched in his hand.

"――Ah!!"

Charging at the redhead was a Witchbeast, a centaur fused into one body of man and horse, letting out a wail like a newborn infant's cry. The Witchbeast's headless human torso swung a flaming sword in each of its two arms, driving down absurd firepower that would char a human body with the barest graze. But the man deflected it with, of all things, an utterly unremarkable wooden stick, weaving through the scorching heat as though dancing.

The wood that accomplished this never caught fire from the flames. It never ignited. Why?

――Probably, simply, he was swinging the stick faster than it could burn.

The wooden stick wasn't burned by the fire because he swung it faster than the fire could burn it. He could deflect the flaming blade strikes because he applied only the minimum force necessary through the wind of his swing to knock the strikes off course. And the reason he looked like he was having fun in a space wrapped in bloodlust turned to scorching heat was simply that the man's head was broken.

Reid savored the insane situation like it was the best of all possible worlds, and resumed the "Trial."

"There, there, there, there, there, there, there, there! What's wrong, you! Playing around, you! This ain't like last time, this is the moment to strike, you! Use your little friends around you, pay me back a little, why don't ya! Come on, come on, come on, come on, come on, come on, come on, come on!"

Roaring, spittle flying, Reid held one stick in each hand amid the flames. His blue eyes glared at Julius, who was likewise surrounded by the blazing scene. Julius, dealing with the surrounding Witchbeasts with a knight's sword in one hand, wore an expression layered with tension and bewilderment, unable to accept Reid's overwrought conduct. Still wearing that expression, Julius shouted.

"What exactly are you thinking!? This many Witchbeasts have surged up from underground! The entire tower is in crisis right now—this is a situation where we should be joining forces!"

"Hah! A guy with such well-mannered swordsmanship even thinks in a well-mannered way. You, can you even enjoy life like that? In my experience, a guy who does what he wants to do is stronger and having more fun than a guy who holds back what he wants to do, you."

"What are you—"

"Besides, what's the problem with a few creepy burning horses running around? It ain't no different from a bit of rain falling. Rain's more of a pain, honestly. I got curly hair."

From a common-sense standpoint, Julius offered common-sense, sound reasoning—and Reid laughed it off with insane, unreasonable nonsense from an insane standpoint. The fact that he compared a swarm of Witchbeasts attacking with unmistakable bloodlust to the same dimension as raindrops was proof that Reid's sensibilities were dead. It could never hold up under any sane logic, and served no purpose except to fill the listener's heart with incomprehension and confusion.

"――!"

And indeed, hearing it, Julius's thoughts were thrown into disarray by logic he'd never considered, and as a result, hesitation crept into his swordplay, delaying his response to the centaur's attack.

"Guh!"

Julius didn't meet the arcing flaming blade with his own sword—he instinctively dodged backward in something like a back handspring. An instant later, he evaded another individual trying to crush his head under its hooves by using the recoil of his landing to leap forward, then drove a thrust into the torso of the Witchbeast that had just swung its flaming blade, drawing an ear-splitting scream from it, before turning toward the Witchbeast behind him――

"――Ah!!"

The moment he tried to turn, a hoof-strike caught Julius's left shoulder. A dull sound rang out, and pain shot across his fine-featured profile. But he used the force of the hoof to spin his body, cleaved the fully-extended leg of the Witchbeast in two with his knight's sword, sliced through the tilting torso, and then struck his own bleeding left shoulder hard with the hilt of his sword. A raw, visceral sound of flesh and bone. Rougher first aid than what Reid had done to Subaru—the bone snapped back into place.

"Hah! That's it, that's the stuff. Not bad, that face of yours."

As the burning, massive bodies and rampaging hooves turned the fifth floor into a scorching hell, Reid watched Julius—the lone human resisting despair—with a cruel smile of satisfaction, as if this was exactly what he'd wanted. The Witchbeasts, having no basis for judgment, regarded Julius and Reid as equally hostile enemies. But how troublesome each of them actually was would be obvious to anyone with intelligence, clear as day.

Bleeding, his usual elegance marred, Julius's sword held off the Witchbeasts' onslaught, steadily whittling down the enemy one after another. Meanwhile, Reid, his cool expression never wavering, was reaping the lives of Witchbeasts like the Grim Reaper, with physical skill equal to or greater than Julius's. And in between――

"――Guh."

"But your feet are wide open. Well, from where I'm standing, everything about you is wide open."

Reid, looking bored as he picked at his ear, lashed out with a long leg that struck directly into the torso of the turned-away Julius. Whatever the force of that kick was, Julius's body was sent flying as easily as a cloth-wrapped stone, slamming into the tower's wall. The impact ran through the entire tower, and the Witchbeasts' shrill roars welcomed their prey's dire straits. The pack of Witchbeasts rushed toward Julius, who lay collapsed on the floor coughing blood, each vying to be first.

"Guh, ah!"

Against that lethal onslaught, Julius slid his upper body along the floor upside-down, then sprang up, swinging his long legs to kick away the hooves. Using that same momentum, he used the Witchbeasts' torsos and shoulders as footholds to break through the encirclement, then thrust his left hand toward a cluster of Witchbeasts that had left an opening.

"――――"

Nothing happened. Julius clenched his outstretched left hand, an expression of bitter regret crossing his face. The Witchbeasts turned, closing in on Julius, who had missed his chance――

"――Even now, you still ain't goin' all out, you."

Reid cut in between Julius and the Witchbeasts, sighing as he swung his sticks. Two sticks, one in each hand—the wind of the blow they whipped up lifted the massive bodies of a dozen-plus centaurs into the air, and the sensation of their hooves swinging through empty air created a gap in the Witchbeasts' awareness.

"Die already, you nag horses."

Into that gap Reid leapt, kicking lightly off the floor, and methodically, one by one, without fail, he dealt with the airborne Witchbeasts—slashing torsos, piercing hearts, snapping human upper bodies in half, crushing whole bodies with kicks—killing each by a different method of slaughter, wiping them all out. It was a matter of mere two or three seconds from when they soared into the air to when they scattered as corpses on the ground. Witchbeasts boasting a terrifying degree of danger were killed without any means of resistance, annihilated to the last.

And so, the centaurs on the fifth floor were wiped out, and it seemed a moment's respite might finally arrive amid the turbulent situation, but――

"Tch, no end to these things. More and more of 'em keep pouring out of the nest."

Reid clicked his tongue, and as if to confirm his glance and his words, the cry of infants and the sound of hooves surged up once more toward the fifth floor from the stairs leading down to the sixth. At this rate, it was only a matter of time before Witchbeasts came climbing up at a pace that would double the pile of corpses already stacked. If the vast desert outside was, hypothetically, the Witchbeasts' home, then Reid couldn't imagine their stock ever running dry. Reid seemed to have a similar thought, for he casually crossed his arms and said,

"People come down here in the mood for an outing, and just when we're about to finish clearing our plates, you keep gettin' in the way. Real rotten bunch, ain't you, hey."

"Wait, going out, did you say? Are you saying that in this situation, you intend to leave this tower!?"

"Don't make me repeat a conclusion I already gave. I told ya. Who holds back from goin' out to play just 'cause it rained a little? Besides, there ain't nobody interesting inside this tower. The one guy who could be my playmate is a total knockout... you lot couldn't even handle bein' my playmate right now."

"――"

Grinding his back teeth, Julius felt fierce emotion rise at Reid's attitude, yet hesitated to give it expression. If he exploded here, negotiations with Reid would fall apart. Then the situation would plunge back into a two-front battle against both the Witchbeasts and Reid. That, surely, was what Julius's reason concluded. Watching Julius's momentary hesitation—an anguish plain to see—

"Guess there's no helpin' that nature of yours."

Reid's mutter, tinged with genuine disappointment, made Julius's cheek go stiff. However much heartache was born in that moment, the swirl of complex emotion that passed through Julius's eyes, his expression, made him look like a child whose view of the mountain peak he aimed for had been swallowed by clouds. The pain his inner self bore there was something no one but he could ever know.

――But the very next instant, the blazing inferno erupting from below, aimed straight at the frozen, standing Julius, was clearly visible to the "ordinary man" surveying the battlefield from directly above.

"――Ah"

Come to this point, Natsuki Subaru, who had been watching the battle on the fifth floor from directly overhead, realized that he'd forgotten to breathe, so consumed had his attention been by what unfolded below.

"――――"

Below him, the melee of Reid and Julius, joined by the Witchbeasts, existed on a dimension where it would be presumptuous even to imagine Subaru—a lump of mediocrity—inserting himself into it.

Amazement and awe at Reid's beyond-the-pale skill were nothing new by now. Let's set that sentiment aside. But Julius, whose fighting style Subaru was witnessing firsthand for the first time—his swordsmanship and body control existed at a level where Subaru had not the faintest sliver of an opening to exploit. If he were to challenge Julius to a fight, Subaru would have his limbs taken off in the blink of an eye, exposed to a humiliating defeat. Even if it were with wooden swords instead of real blades, surely he'd be given a merciless beating without ever managing to crack that cool expression.

Emilia, too. Julius, too. Even Ram. Or perhaps Beatrice, Echidna, Shaula—needless to say—everyone in this tower was someone whose strength little Natsuki Subaru's power could never hope to match. He had underestimated the inhabitants of this fantasy world, these beings from another world. His only achievement was killing the young and frail Meili. And even that had been the doing of "Natsuki Subaru"—Subaru himself had had virtually no part in it.

You cannot kill what you cannot kill. Natsuki Subaru realized he lacked the power to carry out the "Book of the Dead" plan.

"――――"

That realization gave rise to hesitation within Natsuki Subaru.

Standing frozen in shock, his expression drained away, Julius was slow to notice the Witchbeast behind him. Reid, despite noticing the Witchbeast's presence, showed not the slightest inclination to warn him. If things continued as they were, Julius would be burned by the Witchbeast's flame and suffer a fatal wound. ――The possibility that had crossed Subaru's mind earlier, coming to fruition. Julius's "Book of the Dead" would be added to the archive.

Subaru's own hands could never bring about such a situation. Only by making use of an accidental, fluid circumstance could he drive someone toward "death"――

"――Behind you, Julius!!"

"――!"

Hearing the shout, Julius's body reflexively broke free of its stiffness. Without hesitation, his slender frame twisted into a sideways aerial spin to the right, narrowly grazed by the blazing inferno unleashed from behind, which scorched the fifth floor's stone with tremendous force. The explosive intensity that dyed the space red came from the fact that the Witchbeast that had unleashed that flame attack was more than a size larger than any individual they'd faced so far. A physical difference like that between adult and child, mature and juvenile—whether Witchbeasts made such distinctions, Subaru didn't know, but that was the level of difference he sensed.

"――――!!"

Wreathed in flame, letting out a piercing cry, an adult centaur, trailing several juvenile centaurs in its wake, once more occupied the fifth floor with its swarm. The situation reverted back to a deadlock, and Julius, caught in the aftershock of the blazing inferno, stripped off his cloak—its hem already catching fire—and, now unburdened, turned back toward the Witchbeasts――no, he didn't.

His gaze was fixed overhead, on Subaru, still frozen in the posture that had signaled the danger.

"――――" "Uh..."

For an instant, across a long, high distance, Subaru's and Julius's eyes met. Far away, at a distance where they couldn't clearly make out each other's faces, Subaru nonetheless caught the complex emotion held in those yellow eyes, and a groan slipped from the back of his throat. Doubt, confusion, hesitation, concern—various shades of negative emotion swirled in the depths of his eyes. It pierced Subaru with an indescribable guilt, and then―― And then, toward Subaru, who at that very moment wanted nothing more than to flee from it all, Julius——

"――I'm counting on you to look after Echidna, and Anastasia-sama!!"

"――――"

Julius pointed his knight's sword at Subaru—no, at what lay behind Subaru, presumably indicating the fourth floor as a whole—and shouted those words. What was contained in them was trust—cracked, wounded all over—and a plea clinging to that trust.

Whether shouting that at this moment was even the right thing to do, Julius himself had not fully cleared his own doubts. It was words shouted in the midst of that uncertainty. Doubt, confusion, hesitation, concern—all of it was there, and yet he did not stop, he decided. And so――

"――!"

As if struck, Subaru moved his feet, heavy as though glued to the floor, and ran. Stumbling, nearly tripping, running in an ungainly shape. He turned his back on the open spiral staircase and took off running.

He didn't know where he was headed. Whether he was fleeing or not—even that he didn't know. He didn't understand his own emotions, and yet his feet wouldn't stop.

Leaving Julius below, in the place with the swarm of Witchbeasts and Reid, Subaru ran like a hare fleeing for its life.

"――Hah. Really, no helpin' that nature, huh."

Far away, Reid's voice muttered, having watched the exchange as a bystander. It was almost the same words as before, almost unchanged in content.

Whether they held, ever so slightly, a different emotion this time—that, too, remained unknown.

※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※

――Why did I shout. Why didn't I just let him die as he was.

"Haah, haah...!"

Running, gasping for breath, Subaru's mind repeated that self-interrogation over and over. Why did I shout. Why didn't I just let him die as he was.

"If you let someone die, unlike killing them yourself, the feel of it shouldn't stay on your hands, should it?"

Nestled close behind the running Subaru, the girl's phantom spilled out a sulky, saccharine complaint. It was a phantom that hadn't appeared even once since Reid had shown up. That girl's voice pressed at Subaru, accusing him as if to curse, "And here you couldn't even stand having your own throat strangled."

It really was true. Subaru's actions contradicted themselves. Thought and action, wish and purpose—none of it lined up.

The "Book of the Dead" plan was always there at the edge of his mind, and his craving watched for its chance with predatory patience. The phantom of the girl he'd killed saw right through Subaru's inner conflict, calling out to him as if pushing his hesitant back forward—so why, then, had Subaru let the chance slip away?

"――――"

He'd given Julius a chance to escape the blazing inferno. But in the end, he'd left him behind, down below. The threat of Reid and the Witchbeasts still remained in that place—wasn't this just a passive way of letting him die, of not wanting to be involved in the decisive moment himself?

Was it because the impulsive terror of having strangled Meili to death with his own hands had seeped into the depths of his heart, that he couldn't bear to watch someone else die before his eyes? Was that why he'd fled here so desperately?

Regardless of the words Julius had entrusted to him, laid upon him――

"――Subaru!"

Subaru's feet, running with no thought for anything, were forcibly stopped by a sudden call. Stumbling to a halt, the voice had come from a side passage he'd crossed without noticing. From beyond that bent corner, a figure came running toward Subaru. It was――

"I've been looking for you! Is it bad if I come over there right now, I wonder?"

"B-Beatrice...!? And..."

Lifting the hem of her dress, running up with a face full of desperate resolve, was Beatrice. Following Julius, confirming her safety brought Subaru both relief and surprise at once. But the surprise didn't stop there. Behind her, accompanying her, was—

"Echidna!? Why are you two together..."

"...Of all places, to run into you here, Natsuki-kun."

Slightly out of breath, the girl following behind Beatrice—Echidna—murmured. There was surprise at the unexpected pairing, but what gripped Subaru's heart more harshly was the emotion Echidna's pale blue-green eyes directed at him.

"――!"

That undisguisable suspicion forced Subaru, whether he liked it or not, to become aware of the position he stood in. Reid, of course, knew nothing. Even from Julius's and Beatrice's attitudes, he hadn't been able to tell for certain. But Echidna's attitude made it unmistakably clear.

――Echidna knew.

She knew what had happened, in the room where Meili's corpse had been hidden, when Subaru, trapped in a cage of ice, had exchanged words with Emilia and Ram. And presumably, this wasn't limited to her alone.

He remembered the complex emotion Julius had shown at the very end. Everyone must be sharing what had happened to Subaru. If so, Echidna's reaction was natural—rather——

"Beatrice, you're..."

"――! Now's not the time to talk about that, I mean it! Are you coming this way, I wonder!"

But contrary to Subaru's hesitation, Beatrice unilaterally grabbed his hand. His trembling voice smothered, Subaru, caught by her small hand, was caught off guard by the action. If, in fact, she'd been informed of Subaru's situation by Ram and the others, there was no telling how much resolve such an action required. Wasn't it a reckless act, careless enough that Subaru himself wanted to ask if she was in her right mind?

"Beatrice, are you serious!? He's outside the cage! In this situation!"

But at Beatrice's action of trying to pull Subaru's hand along like that, it was Echidna who shouted, her color changing. She questioned the rightness of Beatrice's action, and then turned her own hand toward Subaru. In the gesture of pointing a finger, there was more pressure than its appearance suggested. That posture, seemingly meant as a check on him, Subaru sensed was no mere bluff—it was equivalent to having a gun barrel pointed at him.

But that mortal danger, Subaru accepted as only natural. From Echidna's standpoint, being wary of Subaru was only to be expected. If anything, it was Beatrice who was being strange. ――Even now, in front of Subaru, going so far as to confront Echidna.

"Get out of the way, Beatrice! With him acting this suspicious, I can't let you make the dangerous choice of going with him! He's not Natsuki Subaru, just as Ram claims!"

"That's not true, I mean it! If we join hands... surely Betty, touching him like this, can tell, I wonder! The Contract between Subaru and Betty is still connected. That much, someone in the same position as Betty should understand, I wonder!"

"...Even so, I can't trust him! He's outside the cage under these circumstances! Just how am I supposed to believe him? There's no explanation that could possibly satisfy me!"

In Echidna's expression as she shouted that, a strong emotion Subaru had never seen from her before overflowed. Could she really become this emotional—that thought alone spoke to how strong her impatience was, cornered as she was, desperate to eliminate the threat before her eyes. Desperate, truly.

"――――"

A great swarm of Witchbeasts had surged up from below, and the whole tower's situation hung in danger. Amid that circumstance, watching her unreasonable outburst of emotion, Subaru suddenly thought.

"That's enough."

"Subaru?"

Beatrice, still holding his hand, widened her eyes at Subaru's whisper-like mutter. Subaru untangled her small, soft hand. Suddenly, with an almost audible snap, whatever had been strung taut inside him until then broke. It just broke.

"...What do you mean by that?"

Echidna frowned as Subaru pushed Beatrice's shoulder back and stepped forward. Confusion, and beyond that, wariness. Neither moved Subaru's heart.

"Just as it looks. I surrender. ...You can all do whatever you want with me now."

It felt like his emotions could no longer do anything but let things happen as they would. He'd committed a crime he had no memory of, become desperate to hide it, drawing the suspicion of those around him, and even when he only tried to live peacefully, his life was targeted, forever haunted by the shadow of "Natsuki Subaru."

"Do you think showing me some admirable attitude will soften my stance? If so, that's a huge misunderstanding. I won't let my guard down around you."

"Fine. Include that too, do whatever you want. ――This tower is done for already."

Beyond what Subaru could handle. Too many situations beyond his capacity, too many things gone out of control, had piled up. That was meant as a weak admission of the stifling deadlock of the situation. But apparently, it sounded to Echidna's ears as something else entirely.

"This tower is done for, already...? You mean, you've already accomplished your goal?"

"...Goal?"

"Don't play dumb! Your goal is the 'Witch' in the shrine, isn't it!? That's been achieved, and so you've shown your true colors like this. ...I should have followed Ana's instincts. I never should have brought anyone else to this place."

Echidna spilled the words painfully, her voice overflowing with self-blame and remorse. Subaru had no idea what lay behind it. She was seeing something Subaru couldn't see. And she suspected Subaru's involvement with whatever that was. ――Did it even matter anymore? In this world already ending.

"Subaru, Subaru! What's happened to you! That way of talking, it doesn't suit you at all, I mean it! You wouldn't stop here of all places, I mean it!"

"Suits me? Me?"

Subaru's expression drained away; watching from the side, Beatrice tugged at his sleeve, pleading. His ears trembling at her near-crying voice, Subaru looked at Beatrice.

"What suits me, what does that even mean. What you all see, this 'me'—where is it?"

"...You lost your memory. Are you still going to keep up that terrible performance? Knowing that Ram lost her memories of her sister, that Julius was forgotten by himself, that so many people had memories of someone precious stolen from them, are you still going to keep up such a cruel act!"

"An act!? You think it's an act!? Don't screw with me! If it were an act, don't you think I'd pick a position I could play a lot better!? Who—who would ever want to become 'Natsuki Subaru' by choice!? As if anyone could become something this disgusting!!"

With his mind on the verge of turning to mush, Subaru overwrote Echidna's anger with a fury that exceeded it. If he had the freedom to choose, if there had been any margin of choice, who would ever think of wanting to become "Natsuki Subaru"? Who would want to become something this distorted, this unbearable?

――Who would ever wish, ever want, to become "Natsuki Subaru"?

"All of you, ganging up on me, and you don't even know! Who am I! I lost everything! I was just heading home from the convenience store! Today, the only person I remember talking to is the store clerk! And suddenly it's another world? A tower of sand? Corpses! A 'Trial'! An imposter! 'Natsuki Subaru'! Don't screw around! Don't screw around with me!"

"――――"

"That's right! It's all my fault anyway! I wanted to go somewhere that wasn't here! I didn't want to go home! I was scared of putting on a fake face and causing trouble for Dad and Mom! So yeah, I was excited at first, only at first!"

Beatrice, Echidna, stood frozen, eyes wide at Subaru's exploding emotions. They wouldn't understand the meaning of it. They couldn't understand the meaning of Subaru's anguish.

He'd confessed to them, once, that this was another world. But they'd never truly understood, in the real sense, the "isekai" Subaru had tried to convey to them. ――There was a divide there that could never be filled.

Subaru was not the "Natsuki Subaru" they were looking for. And they, too, could never be the salvation Subaru was looking for.

"You're wondering why I'm suddenly losing it now, aren't you? I don't even know myself! But right now, all of a sudden, I hit my limit! Something just snapped, like I'm just this kind of person after all! I can't do anything, no matter what's wanted of me! I can't! So!"

"――――"

"So... please, just forgive me now. Please forgive me. Please, let me go home... If God wants to punish me, fine, I understand... It was my fault. I was the one who was wrong."

At some point his throat had gone hoarse, a bitter pain stinging the back of his nose, and Subaru found himself crouched down. Pressing his forehead against the floor of the passage, he begged for forgiveness. Not knowing who else to ask, he prayed to God. He called to mind the names of every god he knew, and prayed.

If this was the punishment brought upon him for his own laziness, please, he wanted it forgiven. He'd surely reflected, surely regretted, enough to change a lifetime.

So please, he begged, please forgive him. Please don't drag anyone else into the divine punishment meant for foolish Natsuki Subaru anymore. He didn't want to be hurt, and he didn't want to hurt anyone, either.

"――――"

At Subaru, crouched down, pleading in a voice choked with tears, Echidna and Beatrice fell silent. Staying close beside Subaru, who had crumpled to the ground, Beatrice's palm gently stroked his back. That persistent, unwavering touch of her palm—why did it remain by his side, even now? Hearing the answer to that was too terrifying for Subaru to bear.

"...I don't trust you."

"Echidna..."

At Echidna's conclusion, wrung out as if forced, Beatrice called her name in a hoarse voice. At that call, Echidna slowly shook her head.

"Even if you cry and cling to me, as long as I can't wipe away even a fragment of doubt, my answer remains the same. I... I have a responsibility, having borrowed Ana's body. To get her back safely, I don't care who hates me, who resents me for it."

"――――"

"...But I also don't want to do anything that would make me unable to face Ana when she returns."

With that, Echidna slowly lowered the finger she'd pointed at Subaru's forehead. At once, the pressure that had filled the passage vanished, and Subaru could tell that the finger that had been leveled at his life had been withdrawn.

But that fact had nothing whatsoever to do with the salvation Subaru sought.

"Beatrice, you go with him. I'm going to Julius. If I meet up with him and nothing's wrong, let's meet upstairs."

"...Understood, I mean it. Come on, Subaru, stand up now. If you say you can't stand, Betty will carry you there herself, I mean it."

Echidna turned her back, and Subaru could tell Beatrice respected her decision. As Beatrice tried to lend her small shoulder to Subaru, glancing at her struggle out of the corner of his eye, Subaru watched Echidna's retreating back grow distant.

――I'm counting on you to look after Echidna, and Anastasia-sama!!

"――What exactly do you think you're doing?"

Turning back just her head, Echidna fixed a harsh gaze on Subaru, who had grabbed the sleeve of her left hand, trying to hold her back. At Echidna's question, Subaru, his face streaked with tears and mucus, felt his cheeks stiffen.

Asked what he thought he was doing, no answer came to him immediately. Even Subaru couldn't bear the accusing look in Echidna's eyes. And yet, it had been Echidna's judgment, hearing Subaru's pathetic plea, that she shouldn't lay a hand on him. That concession-like judgment—Subaru's own fingers were now ruining it.

"I thought I'd conceded. Why is it, then, that it's come to this?"

"...Julius asked me to."

"Him? Ridiculous. That he'd make such a judgment... is plausible enough, I suppose."

For a moment, Subaru's tear-choked voice gave Echidna pause, and she continued, "But before that—"

"Did you meet him on your way here? He should have gone to check on the fifth floor. If you met him despite that... no, more importantly, asked you what? He's currently—"

Bombarded with rapid-fire questions, Subaru was overwhelmed by her intensity. To begin with, the cracks in his heart hadn't even settled. Why, then, did this body move for the sake of a world he'd wanted to let go of entirely, wanted to abandon as beyond hope?

Even recalling Julius's shout, something that couldn't even be called a promise, why――

"Ah, this is getting nowhere. In any case, the last place you saw Julius—"

"――Ah"

Echidna, pushing aside her own impatience, tried somehow to choose her words and continue the conversation. Watching her effort, Subaru held his breath.

Not because his hesitation over what to do had cleared. Rather, an urgent matter had arisen—more pressing than Echidna before him, more pressing than this impossible situation.

"――――"

Glaring at Subaru, behind Echidna—beyond the passage—a red point of light floated in the darkness. Subaru's attention was seized by what it was.

The red point of light, floating in the gloom, moved slowly, and caught Subaru's black eyes. Subaru understood intuitively that it had "met his gaze."

At the same moment, the being whose eyes had met his—its full shape—came dimly into view in the faint darkness. A black body blending into the darkness, a glowing red point of light, a pair of unnaturally developed, sharp pincers, and most distinctive of all, a fiercely trembling stinger――

"Ha"

――A scorpion of unimaginable size had its stinger aimed this way.

"Subaru!!"

The instant that tail lashed out, he heard Beatrice scream beside him.

――Light swept through the passage, and the shockwave violently shattered the stone-built fourth floor.
