Arc 7 Chapter 5: "It's Tough Being a Man"

"Wagh—!"

"Eek! Subaru-sama, I'm so sorry!"

Water came splashing down from above, drenching him completely, and as Subaru let out a startled yelp, a high-pitched apology came flying back at him.

Shaking his head and looking up, he found his eyes meeting those of the girl looking down at him from an open window with curtains fluttering in the breeze—Petra.

It seemed Petra had just tossed some water out the window from the second floor.

Of course, she wasn't the type to do something as ill-mannered as dumping water carelessly into the garden. She must have aimed for the edge of the flower bed, where there shouldn't have been anyone to get splashed.

"And yet there I was, unluckily digging around in the dirt right there…"

"I-I'm sorry, Subaru-sama! I'll bring you a change of clothes right away…"

"Ah, it's fine, it's fine, don't worry about it— it just got me soaked, that's all… gah, it stinks! This isn't just water, is it!?"

"Well, it's because it's the water I was using to clean the room… Anyway! I'll be right there, so wait by the well!"

Petra gave the order to Subaru, who was reeling from the smell of the dirty water he'd just been doused with. With that, her cute face vanished back through the window, and he heard the patter of her footsteps as she dashed off to fetch a change of clothes.

Subaru gave a wry smile at Petra's flustered haste, and with an even wrier smile at his own bad luck, headed for the well in the manor garden as she'd told him to.

"Well, I was planning to change clothes once I finished with the dirt anyway…"

He consoled himself with the thought that this had merely moved up the timetable a little.

If Petra was bringing him a change of clothes, he figured he should rinse off the grime first. Before drawing water from the well, Subaru hastily stripped off his shirt—

"—Hmm, stripping off so suddenly. Quite bold of you, Su-bo."

"Wait, gyaaaah!?"

"Oh my, what a girlish scream. …Don't you think the roles are usually reversed?"

The one watching Subaru scream, seated on a garden stone beside the well, was a peach-haired girl—the old woman of the wind, Ryuzu.

She looked at Subaru, who was hiding his bare upper body by clutching his shirt to his chest, her intelligent eyes—so at odds with her childlike appearance—glimmering with amusement.

"No need to hide like that. I'm quite used to the sight of a boy's bare body. I lived with Gar-bo, after all."

"I mean, maybe so, but embarrassing is embarrassing, isn't it?"

"Nothing to be embarrassed about. Besides, you've got quite the decent build yourself, Su-bo. A feast for the eyes, a feast for the eyes."

"See, it IS embarrassing!? Ryuzu-san, isn't this totally out of character for you!?"

Ryuzu was known for her leisurely, composed way of speaking—behavior that sat strangely at odds with her appearance—but aside from her looks, she generally gave the impression of being faithful to whatever role she'd been assigned.

So it came as a surprise that she was now commenting on Subaru's bare body and teasing him a little.

"I guess being freed from the heavy burden of leading the Sanctuary made a big difference?"

"There's no small amount of that in it, I'd say. Though even after leaving the Sanctuary, I've settled into the various places Roz-bo prepared, and I've been entrusted with similar roles there too. So if you're asking whether a weight's come off my shoulders…"

As she spoke, Ryuzu narrowed her eyes and turned them toward what lay just behind her—the manor.

Strictly speaking, it wasn't the manor itself she was looking at, but the people living inside it. More precisely, the brother and sister she doted on like grandchildren.

Garfiel and Frederica had made up, and Garfiel had finally been freed from the cross he'd carried on his back for so many years. Though it had been something of a surprise just how much more of a rowdy brat he'd turned out to be once freed of it, beyond anything Subaru had imagined.

"Ryuzu-san, doesn't it get lonely, living apart from Garfiel and Frederica?"

"Hm… what's brought this on all of a sudden, Su-bo?"

"Was it sudden? Well, it just occurred to me. I mean, for Emilia-tan's sake with the Royal Selection, it's a huge help having the two of them here at the manor. But still…"

"It can't be helped. I myself, of course, and the other copies—my sisters—there'd be too many problems with us living at this manor. And I don't imagine we'd be much use here anyway."

"—"

At Ryuzu's answer, given with a small sound in her throat, Subaru pressed his lips together.

The emotional part of him wanted to shout that this wasn't a conversation about being useful or not.

But this wasn't the kind of matter that could be settled with the logic of a child's tantrum.

As things stood, Roswaal Manor only permitted those with a role to fill and the ability to fulfill their responsibilities to stay there. This was a time that called for exactly that kind of severe discipline.

"…Even so, Garfiel's only fourteen, right? Still just a kid."

"—. You're a kind boy, Su-bo. Worried about Gar-bo, are you?"

"Well, yeah. …When I was fourteen, I was a total snot-nosed brat. I'm still a brat now, sure, but back then I was even more of one. If I'd been torn away from my parents at that age, I probably would've hated myself for how useless I was."

Of course, measured against the standards of this other world, the days Subaru had lived through were far more than merely comfortable—more like being gently rocked in a cradle.

Even if Garfiel's age of fourteen corresponded to something like middle school back on Earth, that comparison didn't apply to this world, and Garfiel was far more mature than Subaru had been at that age.

And yet, even so—

"I just think that whatever I got to have, the people around me should get to have too…"

"Heh, let me correct myself. You're not just kind, Su-bo—you're soft and naive too."

"There's this famous line I know that goes, 'a man's got to be not just strong but kind, too'… wait, by that logic, I'm not strong, so I'm not a man? Then what am I?"

At Subaru, unable to fully defend himself, Ryuzu chuckled, a low sound rumbling in her throat. Subaru found the reaction rather unfair, and Ryuzu, wiping the corner of her eye with a finger, said, "Sorry, sorry,"

"I understand what you're getting at, Su-bo. I'll drop by often so Gar-bo and Frederica don't feel lonely. That should do, shouldn't it?"

"Feels kind of bad, like some outsider pestering you about it."

"Nothing bad about it at all. …Though, if we're talking about a child too young to be away from their parents, isn't there someone younger than Gar-bo in this manor?"

"That would be…"

Just as Subaru had a rough idea of who she meant, footsteps approached. When he and Ryuzu turned to look, it was Petra running toward them.

Petra, arms full of Subaru's change of clothes, came dashing over with a cheerful "Subaru-sama~!" But her feet came to a stop just short of reaching the well.

"Wah! Eek! Subaru! Why are you naked!?"

"Ah, oops, I was going to wash up, but I got caught up talking."

Petra, her face flushed red, covered both eyes with her hands as she remarked on Subaru's nakedness. Though, true to form, she was watching him quite closely through the gaps between her fingers.

Petra, blushing all the way to her ears, was flustered, going "Wah, wah!"

"See, Ryuzu-san? This is what you call the cute reaction of a girl her age."

"Asking me to behave like that too—you're quite the sinful one, Su-bo… Besides, there'd be no point in me doing it anyway. I'm an old woman, you know."

"Aside from that old-fashioned way of talking, you really don't give off an 'old woman' vibe at all, Ryuzu-san…"

Answering Ryuzu's leisurely remark, Subaru hauled a bucket of water up from the well. Then, dousing himself with the cold water he'd drawn, he shouted "Ch-cold!" as he did,

"Still, that reaction of yours is pretty grown-up for your age, Petra."

"Ah, Subaru-sama, you're treating me like a kid again, aren't you. Just so you know, I turned thirteen on my birthday not long ago. Only one year apart from Garf-san!"

"Even accounting for the timing of birthdays, the fact that there's a stretch where Petra and Garfiel are only a year apart is something I find pretty amusing, personally…"

Setting personality aside, Garfiel's combat ability and appearance didn't quite scream "fourteen years old." Meanwhile, Petra, still young and adorable in looks, was slowly beginning to bloom from bud to flower.

Time couldn't be stopped, of course, but growth was still a precious thing.

"Well, I'm eighteen myself, and Emilia-tan and Beako never seem to change in appearance. In that regard, I sense great potential in Petra…"

"Now, now, Su-bo, I do wonder whether a teenage boy should be looking at things that way."

"Well, when you put it that way… oh, right, Petra, thanks for the clothes. Though you did drop them from your hands while you were staring at me."

Shaking his head vigorously to fling off the water, Subaru picked up the shirt lying at Petra's feet, brushed off the dirt, and put it on. Only now realizing she'd dropped the clothes, Petra went "Wah!" and blushed at her own blunder.

"Uu, I'm sorry. About the clothes, and the water earlier…"

"Nah, it was a cute mistake. Setting the clothes aside, getting doused with water was just my bad luck. Thanks to which, I ended up flashing my indecent state to two maidens."

"Am I a maiden too… well, well, you're quite the smooth talker with old women, Su-bo."

Ryuzu shook her head slowly, and Subaru gave a wry smile at her ever-consistent reaction. Then he gave the still-contrite Petra a gentle pat on the head and smiled, saying, "It's fine."

"Everyone makes mistakes, and nobody got hurt. No problem, no problem."

"…Okay, understood. I'll be sure to write it in today's diary."

"You keep a diary? That's impressive. I'm actually pretty good at sticking with daily habits, but a diary is the one thing I just can't keep up."

He could turn daily muscle training or jogging into a habit just fine, but strangely, a diary was the one thing that never stuck. Probably because what there was to write about wasn't the same every day.

That, and back when he was a bit of a truant, his days were so unchanging there was nothing to write about anyway.

"Though my Beako Lovely Diary is still going strong…"

"Are you going to show that to Beatrice-chan later?"

"Yeah, I want to show off just how much I cherish Beako on a daily basis."

Besides, Beatrice's reactions were exaggerated and adorable. For the sake of doting on Beatrice, Subaru was willing to overcome even his own weakness of never sticking with a diary.

Anyway—

"So, what were you and Ryuzu-sama talking about, Subaru-sama?"

"Ah, just chatting? Like whether it's lonely being apart from Garfiel. …But hey, you're apart from your mom and dad too, aren't you, Petra. Don't you get homesick or anything?"

"Homesick… you mean missing home?"

Petra pressed a finger to her lips as she puzzled out Subaru's words, brow furrowing. She thought it over for a moment, murmuring "Hmm, well," before saying,

"I do feel lonely sometimes. But this is something I decided I wanted to do, and I'm right in the middle of doing it. I can't go around complaining."

"Whoa, so grown-up… Ryuzu-san, did you hear that?"

"I heard, I heard. Honestly, making a face like she's bragging about it."

Subaru turned around, proud of Petra's chest-puffed-out answer, and Ryuzu nodded in agreement.

Petra was completely right, through and through.

It was something she'd decided she wanted to do, and she was right in the middle of trying to accomplish it.

If that was the case, then without complaining, even in the midst of hardship, one had to smile and hold one's head up.

"Subaru-sama, was my answer good enough?"

"—. Ah, of course. As expected of you, Petra! That's exactly the answer I wanted!"

"Kyaa!"

In response to Petra's uncertain question, Subaru scooped her up into his arms.

It felt just like when he did this with Beatrice normally, but Petra was heavier than Beatrice—that was because Petra had grown that much more.

"Guess you're getting a bit old to be carried like this, huh…"

"Of course I am! …But if it means you won't look so lonely, I'll play along a bit longer."

"Oh, thanks."

Petra turned her face away with a huff, but forgave Subaru's selfishness all the same. Taking advantage of her kindness, Subaru savored, just a little longer, this lingering trace of Petra's childhood.

Watching the two of them,

"Well, well. I can't tell anymore which one of you is the child."

Ryuzu muttered, in her old-woman way, with her childlike face.

—Splash, splash. Doused from the head down in pitch-black blood, Subaru's flashing memories broke off.

The common thread was having liquid dumped over his head, but if he ever told Petra he'd remembered this particular scene at a moment like this, she'd probably chew him out something fierce later.

Still, in order to even get the chance to receive that scolding, Subaru couldn't afford to stop moving here.

"Why did the hunter's arrow hit the great serpent…!?"

His reluctant flight, Rui in his arms, had been interrupted by the presence of a great serpent lurking hidden in the forest.

Just as its huge maw was about to swallow him whole, a fierce interference had slammed into it, and Subaru had escaped with his life by the narrowest of margins.

"—"

What had bored into the serpent's thick body—thick enough to be roughly three Subarus around—was an arrow loosed by the very hunter who had been tormenting them mercilessly until just moments before.

He couldn't help wondering if it had been a stray shot, but given that it had struck dead center in the serpent's body, the odds of that being an accident felt awfully slim.

Which meant—

"He saved me…!?"

It made no sense to him, and yet no other explanation came to mind.

It seemed unlikely that the hunter had simply had some sudden change of heart, or that they'd stumbled into some shonen-manga development where the hunter declared, "I'm the one who's going to kill you."

But going purely by what had happened, Subaru had been saved. And the effects of that single shot didn't end with merely stopping the serpent's fangs in that instant.

"——!!"

Pierced through the body by the arrow and bleeding, the great serpent let out a cry that seemed to grind against the very sky.

Rather than chasing after Subaru's fleeing back, the serpent instead began slithering through the forest with furious speed toward the direction the arrow had come from—toward the archer who had pierced it.

The sight of a ten-meter-long serpent slithering across the ground, closing in on its prey, was overwhelming.

It showed none of the sluggishness one might expect of so massive a body, gliding across the earth as though the forest floor itself were moving.

Against the serpent's approach, the hunter fired another arrow in response, but it missed.

"——!!"

The serpent bared its drooling fangs and lunged at the hunter.

The hunter leapt aside to dodge, then loosed an arrow at point-blank range in an attempt to finish off the witchbeast.

Beyond the trees, a ferocious life-or-death struggle unfolded between man and witchbeast.

As the two sides, neither giving an inch, scattered violent crashes of sound through the air, Subaru, still carrying Rui, dashed back to the great pit where Rem was.

And then—

"Rem, give me your hand! We're getting out of here while we can!"

"—! Is the child all right?"

"Yeah, sound asleep, annoyingly enough! Come on, hurry!"

Subaru reached his hand out to Rem, who had her back against the pit wall. But Rem looked at his outstretched hand, shook her head and said "No," and hauled herself up over the edge on her own.

It was a stubborn refusal to accept his help. Fine then, Subaru thought, withdrawing his hand, and instead pulled out his whip to bind Rui's body to his own back.

If he abandoned the girl, it would only spark another quarrel with Rem. He wanted to avoid that.

"And on top of that—!"
"H-hey—"

Once he'd firmly secured Rui's body to his back and confirmed she wouldn't fall, Subaru forcibly scooped up Rem, who had just climbed out of the pit.

Carried in his arms in a princess-carry, Rem's expression went rigid at the suddenness of it.

But—

"Right now, think about which is the least bad option—me, the hunter, or the snake!"

"…If it could understand words, I'd pick the snake."

"It can't, so settle for second place—me! Here we go!"

He figured it was an obvious order of priorities, but he still averted his eyes from Rem's face, which couldn't quite hide her conflicted feelings, and with the hunter and serpent still locked in battle behind them, Subaru fled the battlefield at full speed.

Whichever of the hunter or the serpent won, it would probably come after Subaru and the others next. There was no telling whether the fight would end quickly or drag on, but he wanted to put as much distance between them as possible.

"—What are we going to do?"

"Both of them know this forest way better than we do…! We need a way to shake their pursuit, but as far as losing the snake goes, my body odor is a lost cause. So at the very least…"

He wanted to pray that the hunter would be the one to win.

If their pursuer had nothing to do with the Witch's Lingering Scent clinging to Subaru, there would be ways to shake them off.

"Pick a route that leaves as few footprints as possible, erase our tracks, and then…"

"Leave traces in a different direction… right!"

As she spoke, Rem snapped off a branch that had brushed against her, flung it off into the distance, and scratched marks into trees in a direction different from the course they were fleeing along.

With the broken branch and the marks scattered in two different spots, perhaps that would throw off whoever was tracking them.

"—"

As he ran on like that, carrying Rem, an oddly out-of-place feeling of nostalgia crossed Subaru's mind. He'd run through a forest like this before, chased by a witchbeast.

Except back then, the one in his arms hadn't been Rem, but Ram.

"Even her big sister's forgotten it, so I'm—hah—probably the only one left who remembers, but…"

"You're out of breath. At this rate, they'll catch up to us."

"I know that! Geez, the two of you sisters are both… hah… merciless…!"

Even though he was carrying a different sister this time, the sharp-tongued treatment he got was pretty much identical.

Spurred on by that thought, Subaru scolded his own winded body, left the job of watching their rear to Rem, and ran desperately through the forest.

In any case, he'd been on the move nonstop the entire day.

Both physically and mentally, he was in tatters, and if he could, he'd love nothing more than to sprawl out on the ground. In fact, the moment he shook their pursuers, he was absolutely doing that. Eight hours of sleep.

"So hold out until then, body of mine——!!"

"—! Wait!"

"Owowowow!? What!?"

Rem, in his arms, gave a sharp tug on Subaru's ear as he cried out in exertion. As Subaru grimaced in pain, Rem pointed off to a direction away from their course,

"I hear the sound of water. Flowing… a river? Couldn't we erase our tracks there? Also, you mentioned needing water, didn't you."

"You were listening to that survival lecture… actually, save the being-touched-by-that for later, if there's a river, that'd really help! If we can just get across it, they shouldn't be able to follow so easily…!"

Unfortunately, the pounding of his own heart and breath were too loud for Subaru to make out the sound of water, but there was no reason to doubt Rem's hearing right now.

Following Rem, who pointed and said "That way," Subaru changed course, running on in search of the river.

And then, weaving past clusters of trees, leaping over patches of grass, at the point where the path opened up—

"—A river!… b-but?"

The instant the forest opened up and his view widened, Subaru too could finally hear the thunderous roar of water—yes, a thunderous roar of water.

No wonder—the sound of water he was hearing belonged to a great river. And it was flowing far below them, at the foot of a cliff nearly ten meters down.

As though mocking his earlier thought of just crossing it quickly to erase their tracks.

"This is, no matter how you look at it…"

In his arms, Rem caught her breath at the sight of the great river below.

Given the ferocity and height of the raging current, her speechlessness was only natural. There was probably also some self-reproach in it, for having relied on her own instincts to lead them here.

But there was no time to dwell on that regret, nor time to console her over it either.

"—Damn it, has it been decided!?"

Somewhere far off in the forest behind them, a tremendous roar echoed up into the sky.

It sounded like the serpent's cry, laden with some kind of emotion—whether it had won or lost, whichever of them had survived was now coming this way.

"Before that, from here we need to—"

"—Please leave me behind."

Right as Subaru was thinking they needed to move on, Rem, cradled against his chest, said those words.

At the tension in Rem's voice, Subaru let out a breath, "Huh,"

"W-what?"

"Please leave me behind. It's my fault we've been forced into this needless detour. There's not a moment to spare. I'll try to hold whatever's coming off somehow…"

"D-don't say something stupid! There's no way I'm leaving you behind…"

"Then what are you going to do!? You're already out of breath, your knees are shaking, and you're dragging along a woman who can't walk and a child—what more can you possibly do!"

"—"

Right up close, Rem, her face flushed, pleaded with him like that. It wasn't that he was overwhelmed by her intensity, but Subaru couldn't come up with an immediate retort either.

Asked what he was going to do, Subaru wasn't clever enough to produce a ready alternative.

But precisely because he wasn't clever, an answer that required no cleverness came to him at once.

"—No. Not happening. I'm not leaving you behind."

"—! Such stubbornness…"

"Who's the stubborn one here!? I get that you're the one feeling responsible! But you're aiming that sense of responsibility in completely the wrong direction! As if I could ever leave you behind!"

"Wha…"

"There's no point without you! If it comes down to you dying, I'd rather die myself. What do I have to do to make you understand!"

Throwing out his real feelings without a shred of joking, Subaru tried to make Rem retract her stance.

It was the truth. Of course Subaru didn't want to die. Even if Return by Death would give him another chance, he didn't want to die. So this was really just a matter of which was less bad—the worst option or the worse option.

And yet, even so—

"Both you and I are going to choose the option where neither of us dies."

"…And what about the child on your back?"

"If it were fine to use her as a decoy, I would, but I don't want to deal with the fallout of doing that either. So for now, I'll just carry her along with us too."

In any case, it was infuriating how Rui always ended up wedged into whatever problem existed between Subaru and Rem.

But if he abandoned Rui here and now, his relationship with Rem would likely become irreparable. That wasn't an option. So no matter how much it galled him, he wouldn't throw her away.

"—"

Hearing Subaru's firm resolve, Rem's eyes widened and she fell silent.

Within her, a conflict could still be glimpsed—one she couldn't quite swallow—over how to judge Subaru, wrapped as he was in that unacceptable, wicked fragrance.

Catching sight of that conflict out of the corner of his eye, Subaru looked around, searching for an escape route.

But a path to survival wasn't going to present itself so conveniently. It was hardly unreasonable that Rem would suggest using herself as a decoy, given the situation.

So then, all that was left was—

"—We'll have to jump."

"Wh— w-wait! That's reckless, of all things! Look at the state we're in!?"

"A deadweight tied to my back, a Rem who can't move her legs, and me—with three broken fingers, ribs that might be cracked too, and running on empty…"

"About your fingers— anyway! It's reckless in that state! From a height like this… the moment you jump, you'll black out and just drown!"

Pointing at the great river below, Rem laid out her realistic objection.

Ten meters up, with an injured man having to somehow support two people who couldn't move properly, needing to withstand the raging current and reach the far bank—it was only natural that this looked exactly like suicide.

"But it's not suicide. And even if it were, we'd die together."

"Absolutely not!"

"Owaah!"

Subaru's toothy grin was met by a resounding slap from Rem's open palm. His head twisted from the sheer force, and Subaru winced, "Ow, ow," his struck cheek turning red,

"Fine. Since you say so, we won't die."

"—"

"I'll grant your wish. —Because I'm your hero."

Hearing that line, Rem's eyes went wide.

Not because her memory stirred, but likely out of shock that this same suspicious line, spoken to her right after she woke, was being repeated again without a hint of learning his lesson.

But that was fine.

What he'd just said wasn't really meant for Rem to hear. —He'd only wanted to cast a spell on the man reflected in her pale blue eyes, the pathetic man hiding behind a false bravado.

"Hold on tight."

Sensing danger closing in, Subaru told her that with an exhale.

Rem still tried to resist, but before she could choose the words to stop him, Subaru's feet had already kicked off the ground. Sensing the fall about to come, Rem's hand clutched tight at Subaru's clothes.

And then—

"—If you die, I will never forgive you!"

Ah, guess I can't die then, Subaru thought with a wry smile, and kicked hard off the cliff.

A shock and a pillar of water shot up, and his whole body was swallowed by the furious current, spinning round and round.

Thanks to having barely managed to enter the water feet-first, the damage he took was kept to a minimum.

Even so, "minimum" still packed more than enough force, and for Subaru, whose stamina gauge was already deep in the red, he couldn't shake the impression that he'd gritted through it on sheer scraped-together nerve.

Not that there was any need to shake that impression off, anyway.

"Blub—"

Without even needing to worry about impressions, Subaru's whole body was soaked through with water. Like a hand towel being churned around inside a washing machine, he was simply tossed about by the force of the current sweeping him along.

He wanted to somehow surface and breathe. But he had no freedom to. Even trying to thrash his arms and legs to float up, his arms were full with something precious, and he couldn't manage even that.

"—"

Even as the water tossed him about, he could still feel it—affection cradled against his chest, and an object of animosity strapped to his back. Neither the whip binding her nor the arm holding her tightly had come loose.

"Blub, blub—"

The impact of the plunge had knocked out most of the breath he'd meant to hold.

He wasn't swimming exactly, but simply staying conscious required both stamina and oxygen. With both nearly depleted, there was no time to be leisurely about it.

"Blub, blub—"

Water poured in through his nose and mouth, and it felt like it was seeping in through his eyes and ears too.

His flailing hands and feet clawed uselessly at the water, and he felt like he was being carried helplessly down the esophagus of some massive creature called "the great river," straight toward its stomach. If he reached "there," there would be no taking it back.

He had to do something before reaching that point.

"Blub, blub—"

As he clawed desperately at the water, all sorts of unnecessary thoughts began writhing through his mind.

Just as he'd thought of Petra and Ryuzu when doused in blood, and just as he'd remembered carrying Ram while running through the forest, all kinds of things flitted through his head.

Were Emilia, Beatrice, Ram, Meili all right? Julius, Anastasia, Echidna were probably managing somehow. If Patrasche were here, everyone would be at ease. If only Patrasche were here right now, she could have helped them. Helping and being helped, over and over, that was how it had always gone, and the one who should have been his greatest partner in that was Rem—yet she'd broken his fingers. It only occurred to him now, but that had hurt like hell, and yet he hadn't cried out. Good job. He didn't want to look uncool in front of Rem. Same in front of Emilia, in front of Beatrice, in front of Petra and Garfiel too. It was fine with Otto, Clind, and Frederica, since they already knew how pathetic he was. But if Roswaal found out, things would get scary, so he had to hide it somehow, no matter what. He needed to get back to Priestella soon, help the people who were in trouble, and the Royal Selection, and tomorrow, and everyone—

"Blub, blub—"

Everyone, was—

△▼△▼△▼△

"—Kohh."

He flung his hand out with all he had, hauling his body toward a branch he'd managed to grab. It was his left hand that caught the branch, and the three ruined fingers screamed in protest, but he paid it no mind.

His whole body had gone numb with cold, sensation dulled and distant. It was possible that not just three but all the fingers on his left hand were broken, but even that felt like a distant matter.

"Ough, bluehh—"

He coughed, retching up a great gush of the water filling his stomach.

Even as he did, he somehow pulled the weight in his right arm closer, bringing its face above the water's surface. Staring at the limp, unconscious profile, he desperately hauled himself, branch by branch, up toward the bank.

"Kohh, ohh—"

On the bank he'd somehow scrambled up onto, he gave in to the nausea and vomited up a huge amount of water. Then, fighting against the lingering sensation of water still inside him, he laid the girl he'd pulled from the river down on the ground.

"—"

He brought his ear close to her mouth to check her breathing. No response. Biting his lip, he pressed firmly down on the girl's chest, performing CPR.

Still, she didn't breathe back to life. Wondering if he should give rescue breaths, he bent down and brought his face close—right as she coughed up water with a "gohh." He tilted her head to the side, letting the water drain out.

"Haah, haah…"

Enduring the exhaustion wracking his entire body, he undid the whip binding, setting down the weight on his back. Perhaps it was a blessing that she'd been unconscious from the start—the deadweight he set down was breathing faintly.

Which meant that everyone, safely—

"Safely…"

His head swam violently, and he collapsed to the ground right where he was.

He thought he needed to somehow get away from the bank, to at least hide himself in the bushes, but his body wouldn't obey him at all. His stamina had run out completely.

Unable to move so much as a finger, his consciousness sank down into a deep, dark unconsciousness.

Like being caught in an antlion's pit, he kept falling, endlessly, endlessly, into a dark world.

The last wish before his consciousness cut out entirely wasn't about the hunter, or the snake, but about someone else entirely—

"R-Rem…"

There was only the wish that, at the very least, she alone would be saved safely.

△▼△▼△▼△

—.
—.
—.

"—Ah—"

Slowly, his consciousness was pulled up from the depths of a cold darkness.

Quietly, the forgotten act of breathing came back to him, and Subaru drew air into his empty body. More, more, craving oxygen as though he were drowning, he opened his mouth wide—

"—Wheezin' and pantin', shut the hell up, you."

"Mmgh!"

Something was forced into his wide-open mouth, along with a mouthful of abuse.

He opened his eyes wide in shock, but couldn't see anything. It seemed something was wrapped around his face, covering his eyes. Still, he could tell that whoever had shoved something into his mouth was being violent about it.

The taste of dirt and grass, and something big and hard between his teeth—he quickly realized it was a shoe.

Someone was jamming the toe of their own shoe into Subaru's mouth.

"Ogh! Bleh! Wh-what's… goeh!"

"The hell you resistin' for. You don't get what position you're in?"

"Kohh, gahh—"

The instant he spat out the shoe, its toe kicked him hard in the solar plexus. Winded by the impact, Subaru coughed, and the violent man looking down at him spat on him.

Even as this was happening to him, Subaru's mind was in complete turmoil.

Unable to see, unable to understand what was going on, suddenly subjected to violence.

On top of that, he couldn't even rub his aching chest, since his arms were bound behind his back. His legs seemed to be tied too, making it impossible to stand and flee.

"W-what… is…"

"Ah? You, how much longer you gonna keep messin'—"

"—Now, now, calm down! He doesn't understand what's going on! It can't be helped! More importantly, look, let's take his blindfold off!"

"Tch."

In front of Subaru, curled up and drooling, two men were arguing about something. The one who'd arrived later was persuading the violent one, and Subaru could tell, from the click of a tongue, that the rough presence was withdrawing.

Then came a mild-mannered man's voice, saying "Well, well,"

"Sorry about all that, coming out of nowhere. I bet you've got no idea what's going on, but I'll take the blindfold off for now, all right? Can't undo the ropes on your hands and feet, though, so bear with that."

"—"

As Subaru remained silent, the man slowly reached a hand to his head and undid the tightly-bound blindfold. There was a slight pain along with a sense of release, and Subaru took a deep breath, bearing the ache in his chest, and quietly waited for his sight to return.

And then—

"—What is this place?"

With his vision restored, what spread out before him were numerous tents and campfires. And beyond that, a rough atmosphere of men bustling busily about, clad in swords and armor.

In the mind of Subaru, momentarily struck speechless, the image that best captured the scene was—

"…I've seen something like this in a Taiga drama."

Not that it had anything to do with having just seen a great river moments earlier, but what crossed Subaru's mind was a scene from a Taiga drama—a camp bustling with preparations before a great battle.

It was as if this were a reenactment of exactly that—no, that wasn't quite it.

"Happened to spot you right when we went out for water. Sorry to say, but you're our prisoner now."

The one who came around to stand in front of him was presumably the man who'd removed his blindfold.

He put a hand on his hip and, with a somewhat good-natured, troubled expression, said that to Subaru.

—Natsuki Subaru had become a prisoner.
