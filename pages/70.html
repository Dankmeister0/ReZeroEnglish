Arc 2 Chapter 46: "A Demon-Touched Flash"

The face-off lasted only a few seconds, but to Subaru, the silence stretched on as if it were hours.

Rem's eyes, which had seemed to lose all reason, blinked once — and in that instant, emotion returned to them.

"...Sister, and Subaru-kun?"

At the sound of her startled voice, Subaru let out a breath of relief, and Ram, stirring in his arms, blinked her eyes open.

"Ah, Rem, I was worried about you. Running off on your own like that — that won't do."

"I'm sorry, Sister. Rem was desperate to do something about it..."

"Rem didn't need to push herself that hard — a way to handle it turned up. If we use Barusu here as bait, everything's settled."

"Oh! I see! So that's the trick! Rem is moved to tears!"

The twins rush together, clasping hands. Communicating with a synchronization rate never before seen, they turn their expectant eyes on Subaru, who thumps his chest in response.

"Well, leave it to me. I'll dazzle you with the greatest bait performance of the century!"

"Kyaa, wonderful! Such manly conduct, worthy of Emilia-sama!!"

Cheered on by the two of them in raucous applause, Subaru basks in it, nose held high. The long, agonizing loop ends here at last; having splendidly seized the light he glimpsed, Natsuki Subaru sets off down the road to glory—.

"...is how it was supposed to go, ideally."

Muttering that in a pathetic voice, Subaru cuts off the delusional scenario even as he edges backward, step by step. In front of him, Rem's eyes, turned toward him, show no trace of the emotion that had returned in his fantasy — only pitch-black bloodlust, and nothing else. There is no way, by any stretch, that her mental state looks fit for conversation.

Each individual bead of sweat feels as heavy as lead. The weight of sweat pooling on his eyelids threatens to seal his vision shut, and Subaru tries to hold it off by blinking one eye at a time. Blinking both eyes at once, creating an opening and paying for it in pain — he's had more than enough of that from his bout with the Witchbeasts.

—The moment such a thought occurs to him, Subaru realizes with a bitter laugh that he's already treating the Rem in front of him as an enemy. Just what did he come charging into a place like this to do, anyway?

"Reeem-rin. It's meee, your friend Suuubaru-kun."

Without meaning to, in order to shift out of his rising wariness — and, incidentally, to break the oppressive silence — Subaru deliberately puts on a cheerful air and calls out to her. In response to Subaru's friendly greeting, Rem's reaction is—

"Don't give me that burning stare, I'll get burned."

—sharp enough to make a sound, as she snaps her head around and locks onto him. Up until then, her gaze had been sweeping the surroundings as if surveying everything at once; now he can tell it's fixed entirely on him. He may have made a mistake.

That's how tremendous the demonic aura radiating from Rem's current form is. Her familiar maid uniform is drenched in backsplashed blood over her entire body, and because fresh, not-yet-dried blood has been layered over blood that had already dried once, the two-tone contrast of murky blackish red and vivid crimson makes the scene all the more gruesome. Perhaps her transformation into an oni brought some physical change with it — bluish-green veins writhe beneath the pale skin visible through her torn sleeve, and the claws on both hands have grown as sharp as any Witchbeast's. The iron ball she carries at her right arm, her "self-defense" tool, remains as ever-present as her own skin, and it's clearly had no shortage of opportunities to be swung, matching her exploits blow for blow. Caked in clotted blood and various other matter, it seems to have grown only more vicious for it.

He can say this with certainty: It's only because he had some degree of mental preparation for Rem's state that he's managing to keep anything like composure now — if he'd stumbled onto the current Rem unawares on a dark road at night, he would wet himself, no question, a hundred percent.

Yet even exposed to that much madness, the white horn jutting from her forehead — that alone, as if disavowing all knowledge of the carnage, retains its pure white beauty. Even though it's the most symbolic part making Rem into a savage "oni," that horn alone strikes Subaru as strangely, jarringly out of place.

But the situation has no consideration for Subaru's feelings. Looking around, he sees that the Witchbeasts, scattered after falling to the valley floor, are keeping their distance behind rocks and deep in the forest trees, close enough to observe from. No doubt they're gauging the movements of Subaru's group, and of Rem. If they judge the odds favorable, it's easy to imagine them rejoining the fray.

For Subaru, sitting at the bottom of the power balance, their reentry into the fight would mean, quite literally, certain doom. That alone makes it no exaggeration to say that Rem's next move is his lifeline.

He holds his breath, closes his eyes once, and looks straight back into Rem's gaze. Whatever flicker of emotion might rise in those eyes glaring at him — he pours everything into his own gaze so as not to miss even a trace of it. And then—

"...Sister..."

It was a hoarse, utterly exhausted voice. But it struck Subaru's eardrums as a real, undeniable sound.

Lips trembling, as if bewildered, Rem's eyes are fixed single-mindedly on Ram's profile. There, unmistakably, is emotion running in a positive direction, and Subaru marvels at it. Even in the midst of losing her sanity, even while surrendering herself to pure fighting instinct, her awareness has firmly perceived the other half of herself — the sight of the sister she adores beyond all else.

"You really are the model of a sister-complex. Good thing we linked up. Now, let's—"

"Let Go."

The interruption of his words and the iron ball's arrival, wrapped in a howling gale, came at the same instant.

That he managed to tilt his body to the right in that instant was close to a miracle. It was a stroke of luck that the laceration on his right calf had thrown off his stance just as he tried to step forward. The spike of the passing iron ball grazes his left shoulder, and his brain boils with the searing pain of flesh being torn away. His throat shudders with the pain, but there's no time to let his awareness be dragged into it. Stepping diagonally forward,

"That hurt, damn it!!"

Spinning his body from the side of the gouged, bleeding shoulder, Subaru sidesteps as if ducking beneath the chain of the overshooting iron ball. Immediately after, the whipped-back chain lashes the spot where Subaru had been standing, carving a sharp, distinct pattern of an iron serpent into the earth. It's not hard to imagine that, had his evasion been even slightly late, the flesh of Subaru's back would have burst open in that same pattern.

Chilled by the imagined pain of torn skin, Subaru looks to Rem as if clinging to her. But there's no change in her posture as she turns her eyes his way. The eyes drenched in hostility remain as vacant as ever — it's safe to conclude, from appearances, that she's still lost to sanity.

"Went full oni, but can't control it, huh, is that the setup..."

From her current behavior, Subaru makes that guess. If so, the question is what's different from last night. Last night, Rem also went through an oni transformation, but by that point she seemed to have regained her senses immediately. The issue might be elapsed time — or maybe an eye-opening, shocking sight was the trigger. If the fact that Subaru shielded her and took a serious wound right in front of her eyes affected her heart that much—

"Want to take a shot from the iron ball? Instant mincemeat, but..."

If the same conditions as last night hold, he figures she'll surely come back to herself. In exchange, Subaru becomes ground meat.

"Second-best plan is... getting super mauled by the dogs."

If he throws himself flat on the ground, prostrating himself, the ones hiding among the rocks would probably devour him with a hearty "what kind of wild thing turns down a meal set right in front of it!" It seems like it could work, but there's no guarantee Rem will let him get away with throwing himself down like that, and it seems likely to become a curse-overwriting sale — putting the cart entirely before the horse. At worst, Rem abandons him completely to the end and it becomes a predation ending — he can practically see the words "died a dog's death."

Following this pessimistic line of thought, there's really nothing left to do. Ram's consciousness, which might understand the situation, is still far away; he's been shaking her, trying to see if she'll somehow come back, but it looks like it'll take time before that has any effect. And neither the Rem before him nor the Ulgarms around them look inclined to wait for that.

Subaru licks the sweat running down his cheek, wets his lips, and lets his mouth run. If he can't think of any move to make, he'll run through the whole gamut of patterns typical for dealing with someone who's lost their mind. To begin with, a total sweep of options is the only way through. So, doing that is the Subaru way—.

"Hey, Rem. To you, so heartless you can't quite place who I am, listen up. It's happy-embarrassing treasure time about who I am. Right, come to think of it, that was three days ago. Riding some weird high, I went to do the laundry, and standing in front of the laundry basket I was assigned, I couldn't help getting worked up... whoaaa!!"

He tries to stage a comeback with heart-and-memory-appealing bonding talk, but it's cut short instantly by the short-tempered Rem's interruption. The iron ball spins and comes hurtling in, snapping and smashing the trees in its path, scattering wood chips as it closes in — he barely dodges it with a forward roll. The chain's follow-up attack after that, he evades gracefully with a hop-step-jump, taking pains not to drop Ram. Turning around,

"Interrupting a guy mid-sentence to beat him to death, that's got no manners!? I'd like to meet your family... oh, she's right here!"

"Give Sister... Back...!"

To Subaru, who shouts while looking down at Ram in his arms, Rem mutters, leaning her body forward. Or so it seems — but then she suddenly pulls back the iron ball she'd thrown with just a swing of her arm, and using the recoil of that swing, spins her body sharply—

"—!"

Rem's spinning back-kick lands directly on the torso of a Witchbeast that had been about to leap at her from behind — an explosion-like roar bursts out, and even from a distance, it's plain to see the innards of the blasted-away Ulgarm are a mangled mess.

That's what becomes of trying to profit off someone else's fight. The Witchbeasts, who had been about to launch a wave attack timed with the vanguard's approach, involuntarily stop in their tracks at the sight of that gruesome death.

Which is to say, it's a folly equivalent to rolling onto their backs and baring their bellies right in front of the hunter.

A blow launched in a horizontal sweep smashes together the bellies and heads of two Witchbeasts standing side by side. Paying no mind to the scattering scraps of flesh, Rem steps forward, riding the momentum of that blow. She crushes underfoot the foreleg of a Witchbeast that flinched at her advance, and as it stalls, her other foot kicks up into its snout — a light, crisp sound rings out, and the Witchbeast's cervical bones twist and shatter in an impossible direction. Rem then leaps straight to the face of the last Witchbeast of the wave attack, and, grabbing the cowering creature by the scruff of the neck, does nothing more than hurl it straight up into the sky with all her might.

Drawing an arc, the Witchbeast, turned into a fastball, trails behind it a thin, drawn-out howl. It grows distant, distant, then close, close, and finally cuts off abruptly with a sound like fruit being smashed against hard ground.

Slaughter upon slaughter. Butchery for butchery's sake. Carnage within carnage. No other impression comes to mind for what's unfolding before his eyes.

An overwhelming gap in ability, like something out of children's play. It's a difference in class as living beings. Between an "oni" and a "Witchbeast," they might rival each other in ominousness, but never in absolute power.

And still, the sight of the Witchbeasts challenging an "oni" they cannot beat is utterly beyond Subaru's comprehension. Their limbs shake with fear, they let out anguished howls in the face of death, they even seem to feel grief and outrage over the discarded corpses of their comrades — and yet, they absolutely refuse to flee from the fight.

Perhaps that, precisely, is the root of what makes them "Witchbeasts" — creatures born of the Witch's hand, called even an external enemy that brings harm to mankind.

Witnessing that reckless charge — in a sense, a scene of active suicide — Subaru furrows his brow at a doubt that suddenly rises in him.

Rem's figure, flattening the pack of Witchbeasts with overwhelming violence. The doubt is this: might her tremendous combat strength, viewed from the side, not yet have been fully unleashed even in the two attacks directed at him so far?

Not to brag, but Subaru has a fair amount of confidence in his own physical ability. In gymnastics and the like, there's almost no move he can't do — back handsprings, somersaults — and in agility, too, he's the holder of records that greatly exceeded the national average. But as for whether that physical ability of his is actually superior to that of Witchbeasts living wild, he can't for the life of him believe he beats them in agility, explosive speed, or stamina. At best it's a toss-up whether he'd win a contest of raw strength — and even that he can't say for sure, recalling how he was once thrown down by sheer brute force.

To Subaru, arriving at that in some ways deflating self-assessment, the rampage Rem has unleashed before his eyes is by no stretch something within the realm of the dodgeable. A blow that a Witchbeast can't evade and gets turned to mincemeat by — Subaru should, naturally, be taking the same. And yet, the contradiction that he's already succeeded in surviving it twice — pressing the thought further, Subaru finds there, once again, a thread too thin to cling to, and yet he clings to it.

"Even having lost her sanity, that she's aware of Ram's presence means—"

Looking down at the girl breathing pained, sleeping breaths in both arms, he gently brushes aside with a finger the bangs falling over her forehead. Every incoming iron ball has been on an instant-kill course aimed above Subaru's neck. But looked at another way, that's a sign of intent not to finish off anyone but Subaru.

Because Subaru is holding Ram, Rem's blows are being pulled, held back. That's why there's no matching severity between her mercilessness toward the Witchbeasts and the attacks directed at Subaru.

Building that inference, Subaru unconsciously catches his breath. If that reasoning is correct, he might be able to get through this. —This situation, which looks utterly hopeless at a glance, might be crossable, in the same precarious way as crossing a rickety bridge on the verge of collapse — but crossable all the same.

"And for that—"

He looks around. The current layout at the bottom of the valley: Subaru in the center. Rem standing to the north with her back to the cliff. The Ulgarms scattered widely to the east and west, forming an encirclement. From the Witchbeasts' perspective, both Subaru and Rem should be equally prey, but as things stand, they appear to be prioritizing Rem due to the danger she poses. Rem, for her part, is paying Subaru a certain amount of attention too, but she's too occupied dealing with the attacking Witchbeasts to go on the offensive against him. However, it's not hard to imagine that if the number of Witchbeasts interfering with her actions decreases, Rem will turn her prey toward Subaru in order to reclaim her sister.

Taking all that into account, there's only one course of action left for Subaru to take. Steeling himself, he plants his feet apart, and, drawing a breath, fixes his gaze on Rem.

—Jump in, me. Men need guts, women need charm.

"So don't make that scary face, smile, Rem. —I am 'Death...

The second stoppage of the world today, and from it, the festival of screams brought on by the swirling black mist. Even bracing for the agony to come, it's not something he can just bear. All the more so because this time it's not just one hand — the left hand, too, is emerging.

Perhaps having gotten a taste of success with the right arm, before Subaru's eyes as he forces open his unmoving eyelids, the sensation slipping through his breastbone and stroking his innards has transformed into the equivalent of two palms. One rests on his heart, and the other, as if lovingly, gently touches Subaru's cheek. —A wave of horror rises, and immediately after, pain overtakes every nerve completely.

Agony, agony, agony—! The world turns upside down, and at the same time there's a ferocious discomfort as if the entire contents of his stomach are being kneaded together. He stops being himself. From head to toe, every part of him feels replaced, wrong. A numbness so total that nerves can't even recognize themselves as nerves sends his brain into a boil, and his consciousness gradually clouds. And yet, only the sensation of the soft palm touching his cheek is warm, and there's a sense of relief there, as if his heart and body might melt away centered on that spot — but Subaru knew of a sensation that surpassed even that, without needing to rely on it—.

"I'm back...."

His vision blurs. His soul wears thin. Neither pain nor suffering — he's brought nothing back with him into reality. No time has passed, either. Before his eyes, the standoff between the Witchbeasts and Rem continues in the same seesaw battle it had been — but the moment Subaru returns to himself, a major shift occurs in that confrontation.

As if something too enormous to overlook had suddenly appeared on the scene, the attention of both Rem and the pack of Ulgarms turns his way. Just as planned, it's likely because the Witch's presence enveloping Subaru has surged, more intense than before.

—Rem roars. Trying to snatch back her beloved, adored sister from a hostile presence radiating a tremendous Witch's Lingering Scent.

—The Ulgarms all begin howling at once. As if trying to gather every pack in the forest, their instincts drawn along by that nostalgic scent.

—Subaru, too, roars. Dodging, with razor-thin timing, the claws of the leaping Witchbeasts and the incoming iron ball, as if demanding that fire be lit to his own life, his own soul.

The melee begins. Oni, Witchbeasts, and an ordinary human — occupying, without even needing to stop and think about it, a wildly out-of-place corner of the fray, Subaru keeps dancing at the center of the chaos with the utmost care.

Subaru's actions remain consistently simple. Keep a not-too-close, not-too-far distance from where the fighting intensifies, and dodge with everything he has, purely regarding whatever sparks fly his way — that's all.

Right before his eyes, meanwhile, one Witchbeast is struck by the iron ball and instantly turns into a stain on the rock wall. But undeterred by its comrade's death, the Witchbeasts form up three at a time in coordination, pressing in to inflict serious wounds on Rem. Their shallow scheme proves futile — their leap is intercepted by a brushing sweep of her hand, and one by one, as their movement stops, they fall prey to the weapon swung down upon them.

Watching that out of the corner of his eye, Subaru readjusts his hold on the slipping Ram and leaps backward. Narrowly escaping a lunging Witchbeast, he throws himself into Rem's attack range. At that, Rem, sharp-eyed enough to notice his approach immediately, counters. He dodges the thrust of the iron shaft with a sudden stop, ducks under the delayed-arriving chain, and the iron ball, passing overhead, blooms a bright red flower on the head of a Witchbeast that had been closing in from behind. Having confirmed that much, he throws away all shame and decorum and disengages at once in a cockroach-style crawl across the ground. A Witchbeast blocks Rem as she tries to give chase, and he succeeds in getting away. Keeping a certain distance, praising his own judgment for saving his life with a sigh of relief,

"—Haah! I'm surprisingly not bad at this, huh."

He jumped around desperately to avoid getting bitten by dogs, shoved the dog that chased him onto a maid girl, and crawled away like a cockroach from the tantrum of the girl he'd angered.

Put into plain words like that, it makes him want to lightly die, but he resolves to see it as something done in order not to die, and rather glorifies the unsightly spectacle in his head. As it stands, the plan Subaru envisioned is working. Both in terms of buying time and reducing the total number of Ulgarms, things are shaping up not badly at all.

Glancing up, from multiple directions leading down into the valley, he senses the presence of Witchbeast packs converging on his position. Subaru's body odor, having spread across a considerable range of the forest, won't stop stirring the Ulgarms' instincts. A sinful business, indeed.

So, he thinks, a chance to live has appeared. In that way, even though the situation hasn't actually changed for the better, he lets himself feel a premature surge of joy.

"—Uh?"

It arrives suddenly. A new pack of Ulgarms enters the valley floor, throwing themselves into the death match with Rem. Watching that scene, just as he lightly shifts his body to prepare for an evasive move, a dizziness-like sensation throws Subaru's balance badly off. His knees give out, and he nearly topples forward. Instinctively, he twists to shield Ram in his arms, and falls to the ground on his injured left shoulder. A groan, as if the agony had sealed his throat — but what dominates Subaru's awareness even more than that is—

"This chill—"

He recognizes it, that abnormal cold chilling him from the core of his body. Even as he's put his body on the line this much, burning his soul, throwing himself into the thick of the fire — an interruption that douses that passion in cold water.

At that, Subaru once again regrets his own blunder. The more numbers gather like this, the greater the danger grows. The presence before him is so overwhelmingly powerful that, should it judge it wants even a little more strength, resuming the curse it had put on hold should have been the very first option to come to mind as a means to take.

He casts his gaze around. Some one of the Ulgarms that has joined the scene here is activating the curse cast on Subaru and siphoning off his Mana. But scanning around, he sees no Witchbeast that looks the part. Before that, even, he's in no position to even imagine what state a Witchbeast undergoing curse activation would be in.

And now, in a body where even moving a single finger has become a chore, even if he could somehow identify the source of the curse, there's no way he could stop the Curse Arts, even at the cost of trading his life for it.

"...Is this it, then."

Coming this far, does everything vanish into futile effort? The chill, growing ever stronger, begins to draw Subaru's consciousness away into the distance. He tries to reach out to hold onto it somehow, but no matter how far he reaches, it only claws emptily at the air—

Suddenly, the chill vanishes, and blood returns to his palm.

—A roaring boom rang out at the very same instant Subaru's condition returned to normal.

Forcing open his hazy vision, Subaru lets his gaze wander in search of the source of the sound. And deep in the forest, he sees Rem, stomping the ground hard and slamming her iron ball down onto the bedrock. Beneath that iron ball, a single Witchbeast lies exposed, its corpse burst apart.

Sensing the causal link between his own remarkable recovery and that scene, Subaru gets to his feet. The Witchbeast Rem had just crushed had been the one carrying out the curse on Subaru. With its life snuffed out, the execution of the curse must have been interrupted. What a fearsome coincidence — he finds himself half in love with his own extraordinary luck—.

"—Nah, no way."

The spot where Rem had been standing just before is now a considerable distance from where she is now. There's only one reason to ignore all the scattered Witchbeasts in between and pick off that one alone as a target.

Casting everything else aside, sustaining claw lacerations even as she tore through the fray, Rem singled out the Witchbeast that had activated the curse and struck it down. What that means is not lost on Subaru.

Even having lost her sanity, even in a mental state where she doesn't know who Subaru is — Rem never mistakes her beloved sister, and she hasn't forgotten, either, the very first purpose behind why she plunged into the forest.

If so, then, he thinks. Subaru rubs his eyelids, consciously reminding himself of what he must do. Rather than using her in some underhanded, deceptive way like this, he thinks he ought to follow through on his original goal. Which is to say, in short—

"The original ideal picture. The maid sisters Ram and Rem, and a heart-pounding joint curse-removal operation."

Only by achieving that, Subaru reckons, does he have a chance of making it back alive out of this curse-drenched situation. Therefore, he has to bring Rem back to her senses. What Subaru has business with isn't the "oni" — it's her, the officiously polite, jump-to-conclusions, snap-decision-prone, endlessly troublesome her.

"—The horn."

Suddenly, that voice shook Subaru's eardrums from right beside him. Looking down, he sees Ram, still cradled in his arms just as she is, with her eyes half-open, moving her lips even amid her hazy consciousness.

"You're awake?"

"I figured now was the tastiest timing, is all..."

"Ah, good instincts, Sis. —The horn, you said?"

At Ram, giving a small laugh, Subaru too puts on a smile that's half bravado, half resignation, and asks back. She dips her chin, seeming weary,

"That horn is what makes Rem into an oni... land one solid, powerful strike on it, and... she'll come back..."

"You sure?"

"Should be. Probably. I'd like to think so."

"That's awfully vague!? But, I'll believe you."

Saying that, Subaru looks at Rem's figure. The white horn extending from her forehead is roughly ten centimeters long. If you picture Puck standing straight up on someone's forehead, that's about the size of it.

—One good hit, there.

"Isn't that kind of impossible?"

"Scrape together your wits and your courage and figure something out."

"Actually, I have thought of a method that might reach it, if I scrape together my wits and courage."

At Subaru's reply, Ram raises an eyebrow, looking surprised. Subaru gives her a wry smile, twisting his lips as if reluctant to say it,

"But you're definitely gonna get mad."

"If it means my sister comes back to her senses, Ram won't get mad."

"Really?"

"Really, truly."

"Swear it on Rozzy?"

"...Choosing that as your assurance shows a real disregard for your own life. Yes. I swear it on Roswaal-sama."

Since Ram says it with such thoroughly manly conviction, Subaru decides he'll respect her word.

Ahead of him, Rem turns unhurriedly to face him. The core of her awareness remains fixed on him, while the net of her vigilance spreads out to the Ulgarms around them. Against Rem in that all-directions state of alert, Subaru moves to strike the horn. Which is—

"Ohhh, hyaah!"

"—Huh?"

Twisting his body, cocking at the hip, and swinging his arm with his whole body's rotation as the follow-through — he hurls Ram, cradled in both arms, flying hard toward Rem.

She surely never imagined she'd be thrown. Ram's dumbfounded face recedes as she sails through the air, drawn in toward Rem. Even Rem, for all her oni-state composure, seems stunned by this — but the instant she recognizes that what's flying at her is her defenseless sister, she instantly drops the iron ball and reaches out her arms to secure her body.

Deftly moving her claw-lengthened fingers, Rem catches her sister in her blood-soaked arms. In that instant, a gentle color flashes across her expression, which until then had been drenched in nothing but bloodlust.

—Not to be late for witnessing that instant, Subaru's own body had already launched itself.

At the same moment he threw Ram's body, Subaru's own body surged forward in the same direction. While Rem's gaze follows Ram rising overhead, he crouches low and dashes through her blind spot, closing the distance. Stepping in, his right hand draws the sword from his hip, and, riding that momentum, aims straight for the horn above her head. Cutting through the wind, with the timing catching her completely off guard.

Even Rem, as sharp as she is, can't even react to this surprise move — but,

"—Ahh."

By the width the blade's edge falls short, by the one step his powerful lunge fell short from being scared — under the razor-thin margin of the situation, he narrowly, barely fails to catch the horn.

At the chance-of-a-lifetime opportunity slipping clean out of his hands, Subaru is stunned,

"I choked!! One more step, I was one step short on courage!!"

The swing goes wide, and the momentum spins his body around. Right before he turns his back fully, at the edge of his vision, he sees Rem's left hand cocked into a spear-hand strike. If that were thrust straight out, it would open a clean hole clean through Subaru's torso. By Rem's hand, again — that alone, no, he thought, and bit through his own lip the instant after—

The ground beneath his feet exploded, and the resulting surge of earth and rubble sent Subaru's body flying hard.

"Oohhhhhh—!?"

Struck by a shrapnel-blast of stone, his skin torn open and bleeding, Subaru, flung through the air, sees below him the cause of the current calamity.

The eruption of ground, south of Subaru's position — the cause is a puppy-sized Witchbeast, lying flat there. The one he'd thought he'd lost track of ever since falling to the valley floor had, it seems, been waiting for the moment Subaru and Rem lined up along a single line of fire. The instant it judged it could take out both at once, it unleashed its magic like a surprise attack, delivering a flashy interruption via a surge of earth and rubble.

But that scheme—

"————!!"

Rem roars, and delivers one stomping step against the erupting ground. The surge of earth that had been about to form is canceled out by the force of that blow, its only effect reduced to violently ruffling her blue bangs. Slamming raw force against magic to cancel it out — it's surely not that simple a situation, but it's close to that kind of magic-crushing method.

Rem, holding the sister she caught close and precious, steps sideways to escape the backlash of the magic. —She's forgotten entirely about Subaru, falling from above.

Spinning, spinning, the world turning end over end is, by any stretch, not something he can perceive normally. But his right hand is still firmly gripping the one-handed sword, and, fortunately, his point of impact is clear of the rock wall — just hard-packed dirt. And there, right beneath him, unaware of him, is Rem, her head exposed.

There's nothing more than this, nothing beyond this, nothing further to hope for from here on. He winds up, grips the one-handed sword with both hands, and readies the full force of his being.

By pure chance, upon chance, stacked upon chance — and even then, it's a toss-up whether it'll reach. Hooray for convenient plotting. Long live miracles. Even the whims of the gods do their job, once in a while.

—If only they'd worked a little while ago, during that first flash of the blade, at least it would've looked cool.

A bitter laugh. No time left. Right up close, in a world slowing down, growing sluggish, there is Rem. He can see the horn. He flips the blade in his upraised swing to strike with the flat, unleashing every ounce of stored power.

"Smile, Rem. —Today, I'm more oni than any oni."

The blade is loosed, flying straight — the flash of the sword streaks toward the white horn—.

A high, ringing clang of steel striking steel rang out sharply through the Witchbeast forest. Immediately after, accompanied by Subaru's undignified scream at his botched landing.
