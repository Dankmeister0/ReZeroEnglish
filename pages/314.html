One Day II Finale: "Under the Moon, a Haphazard Step"

—The great hall, which he surely had set foot in before, had transformed into something entirely different from what Subaru remembered.

A red carpet blanketed the floor, and rows of candelabras stood in ordered lines. The flickering of their red flames lent the room an added solemnity, and it straightened the backs of everyone gathered there without their even meaning to.

Lined up neatly along the walls were nearly all those connected to the mansion. Of course the principal figures were present, but even House Miload's attendants had been made to attend—padding the numbers, perhaps.

If only the people connected to Subaru had been gathered, it would have inevitably ended up a purely private affair. Even Subaru understood the need for an occasion like this to be witnessed by many eyes.

Even so, was it really all right to have gone so far as to line up even Ryuzu's duplicates for this?

The Ryuzu beside him gave a small nod, as if to say not to worry about it, but what worried him worried him regardless. Even knowing they wouldn't do anything without specific instructions, he couldn't help feeling anxious that these girls—little more than small children—might cause some kind of trouble.

Though he supposed those around him probably felt an equal amount of anxiety about him.

Looking over the attendees one by one, there was plenty to raise an eyebrow at.

It was particularly amusing that all the people connected to him, every last one, had put on such properly formal dress.

Roswaal and Annerose looked perfectly at home in their finery, but the way Otto and Garfiel couldn't quite pull theirs off left them no better than Subaru himself. Garfiel, at least, wore a sour expression over the tightness at his collar, but Otto—seemingly oblivious to how badly his outfit sat on him—stood out for sheer comic effect. Otto, man, that's rough. Was he trying to get a laugh?

As for the servants, Frederica and Clind included, their everyday attire was already formal to begin with. It seemed a bit odd that Ram was lined up right alongside them in her usual maid uniform as though that were only natural, but then Subaru's eyes fell on the spot right beside Ram, and he stopped breathing.

There, seated in a chair, was a blue-haired girl.

Of course, she wasn't awake. She remained fast asleep. And yet, someone had thought to bring her out to this place and seat her among the attendees with Ram at her side—a consideration so thoughtful it was infuriating. Whoever's idea it had been, Ram, looking his way with a smug, self-satisfied face, struck him as thoroughly infuriating.

Moving his gaze further along, there was Petra, proudly showing off how dressed up she was.

Petra was still young, but the dress lent her added splendor, and the girl shone with a brilliance that lost nothing even next to Beatrice or Annerose. She was supposed to be just a village girl—where had she gotten such stage presence?

Beside her, Beatrice wore her usual attire, but the expression she turned toward Subaru seemed somewhat softer than usual. At the faint blush on her cheeks, Subaru found himself recalling what had happened in the waiting room, and he too felt a small stab of embarrassment.

And then, straight ahead—

"――――"

Waiting for him there, a silver-haired girl was looking his way.

Emilia, dressed in formal attire, captivated Subaru with a radiance utterly unlike her usual self.

Her silver hair shimmered like moonlight, and her amethyst eyes were set like jewels. Her features, so perfectly composed they seemed to tremble, held her lips pressed together, and he could see she was tense before this important ceremony.

Her attire immeasurably heightened the purity Subaru always felt from Emilia in everyday life; the sacredness of its design, reminiscent of a shrine maiden's robes, and the nobility of its gold trim burned the loftiness and solemnity of this ceremony into the mind of anyone who beheld her, almost painfully so.

The instant her figure entered his sight, whatever restless flutter had lingered in his heart settled at once.

The emotion that had seemed to boil up from deep within him lost every trace of itself, and every presence besides Emilia's vanished from the edges of his awareness.

It was by no means that he held the attendees watching over the ceremony in low regard.

It was simply that, in this ceremonial place, he understood exactly what he had to do, who he had to look at, and where his heart belonged.

Even without it being put into words, he simply understood it clearly, in his heart.

"――――"

With no one instructing him in anything, Subaru stepped forward as though led by something.

Silently across the deep-piled carpet, forgetting for now even the weight of the knight's sword at his hip, he moved toward Emilia—single-mindedly, as if in the grip of a fever, and yet with a heart as calm as a windless sea.

The storm of gazes cast upon him did not sway his heart in the least, not now.

Nothing stirred Subaru's heart now except every single movement Emilia made before him.

He walked up before Emilia, close enough to reach out and touch her.

Standing on a dais raised just slightly above him, Emilia held her tremblingly beautiful cheeks taut with tension. Before that sight, Subaru knelt where he stood.

He dropped to one knee and bowed his head.

The ceremonial forms Garfiel had explained to him in words guided his body with an almost frightening smoothness. Eyes closed, he felt the heat of the gazes pouring down on him from above.

In a sensation like forgetting to breathe, Subaru raised his face, his skin prickling with a pleasant tension, and unfastened the knight's sword from his hip.

He lifted its considerable weight reverently and drew it sideways before his chest.

Bathed in the brilliant red light of the candelabra flames reflecting off the blade, Subaru's and Emilia's eyes—different in color—lit with the same shining hue.

"――――"

Burning the beauty of the blade freed from its sheath into his eyes, Subaru gently held the sword out to Emilia.

At the sight of the knight's sword raised before her, Emilia's lips trembled with some welling emotion.

But she held back, through sheer willpower, the words of power that had almost spilled out, and restrained herself from being swept away by the wave of emotion.

Then her pale fingers reached out and touched the knight's sword, lifting it—heavier than she'd expected—until its tip slowly pointed toward the ceiling.

The beauty of Emilia holding the sword—Subaru suppressed the selfish urge to keep watching it, and bowed his head once more, closing both eyes.

What was offered before Emilia was the sword, the pride of a knight, and—in the sense of representing the knight himself—his body and his neck.

"――――"

The life and body of a knight, offered up to his liege.

At the sight of Subaru showing her this, Emilia's lips and eyes wavered once more. But this time, the hesitation lasted only an instant. Neither her tightly pressed lips nor her eyes gazing straight ahead held any trace of it now.

The descending tip of the blade touched Subaru's left shoulder.

The flat of the blade rested on his shoulder, and Subaru nearly let out a groan at its weight. The pressure bearing down on him was not physical—it was nothing other than a weight upon the spirit.

And yet, the sensation that assailed Subaru may well have been nothing less than the very "pride" that every knight ought to carry.

And now, in this very instant, Natsuki Subaru understood it too, for the first time.

The blade's tip, having touched his left shoulder, now moved to his right.

He tasted the same weight as before, but this time, the cold of the sword he felt even through his formal wear did not fade.

Of course it wouldn't. The most crucial moment of the ceremony was only now, in this form, coming to pass for the first time.

"――――"

"――――"

Silence fell over the great hall.

No—the hall had already been filled with quiet before this. But that earlier quiet had held within it a faint excitement, a hush concealing a passion almost noisy in its intensity.

But the silence of this moment carried a different heat than what came before.

It was true silence, arriving after leaving behind every trace of excitement, of fervor.

It was a stillness that descended equally over the hearts of Emilia, of Subaru, and of every attendee watching over them.

The right to break it belonged to only a single person in this place.

"—To the sun that looks down upon the dazzling world. To the stars that watch over the world as it falls into sleep. To the wind, to the water, to the earth, to the light, to the spirits that fill all things."

The silence was broken.

Emilia's lips wove the ceremonial invocation as though singing it.

"—To the great world that receives you, that raises you, that sends you forth."

He trembled. His heart trembled.

His teeth had begun to chatter—what was it in Subaru's heart that he could no longer bear?

Even chasing after his own bewildered heart felt maddeningly slow.

For now, he only wanted to drown in the bell-like ring of her voice.

"—To the pride that has supported you, that has made you, that has built you."

He felt the heat of the gaze pouring straight down on him from in front growing stronger.

A passion that seemed as if it would burn him from the inside out.

As his heart played out a frenzy that made him want to cry out, Subaru simply waited for the moment of the question.

"—May you live in a way that brings no shame before all that watches over you, before the world that raises you, before the pride that supports you. May you be unafraid, unshaken, unwavering, and true to your own heart."

The invocation ended.

The question was coming.

The ceremonial forms ended here. Even Subaru did not know the answer to the final question.

But—

"—Just as your resolve stands, and just as everything that surrounds you does, will you swear to protect this body of mine from this moment on?"

—His heart knew exactly how to answer Emilia's question.

"By the sun, by the stars, by the world, by pride—and—"

To everything the invocation had named, he would speak his resolve and gratitude for being here, now.

And before speaking his vow, Subaru pictured in his mind the ones to whom he owed unqualified gratitude.

And so, naturally, his lips wove that word.

"—By my father, by my mother, I swear it upon them both."

"――――"

"I will protect you. I will see your wish granted. —My name is Natsuki Subaru."

He raised his face.

The sword still rested just beside his right cheek. But its gleam didn't register in his sight at all.

Now he could see nothing but the violet-indigo brilliance gazing back at him.

"Emilia. —I'm your knight, and yours alone."

"—Mm."

To the words he'd spoken, an answer came in reply.

Emilia's eyes grew moist with a wave of emotion she could no longer contain.

But she barely held back what threatened to spill over, and drew the blade's tip away from Subaru's shoulder.

The sword was then turned about, and Emilia held the knight's sword out to Subaru.

He received it reverently with both raised hands, and the knight's sword was returned once more to its sheath.

He fastened the sword back at his hip, and, still kneeling, Subaru looked up at Emilia.

Seeing Emilia give a faint nod, Subaru rose to his feet.

And then—

"Bit late to say it, but Emilia-tan, that outfit is super sexy-cute."

"Idiot."

—The solemn air of the ceremony shattered, and Emilia, red-faced, stuck out her tongue.

On the tables brought into the great hall now sat dishes of every color.

At the standing buffet, regardless of status or position, all those who had attended the earlier knighting ceremony gathered together, giving the whole affair the air of a small social mixer.

"Here I just went through the single most nerve-wracking stretch of my entire life, and everyone else is living it up. Must be nice."

Watching the meal from a distance, Subaru had stepped out onto the terrace and stood there in the night breeze.

He'd set a plate of food he'd brought from the table, along with a drink, on the nearby railing, but the amount on it hadn't changed since he first brought it out.

Neither the food nor the drink would go down his throat easily.

The sensation of heat still hadn't faded from his head and neck.

His stomach kept insistently protesting its hunger, but somehow his chest was too full for any more food to go down.

"――――"

At the edge of his vision, Petra had come out in front of the hall and, still in her dress, was performing a little dance. It was the kind of dance shown off at festivals in Arlam Village, but whether Petra had added her own arrangements or not, her poised and confident bearing made it lose nothing even in a noble household.

Pulled along by the hand, red-faced and stumbling through clumsy steps, was Beatrice. She was desperately trying to keep an expressionless, unmoved front, but Subaru didn't miss the way her ears and the bridge of her nose trembled, unable to hold it together.

As ever, it seemed Beatrice, unable to stand firm against Petra, had been swept along into it.

His cheeks loosening at the novel sight, Subaru picked up the glass beside him. He'd finally found enough composure to at least wet his tongue with it, though his hand still couldn't quite reach for the meat pie that was Garfiel and Frederica's joint creation.

"—Subaru, so this is where you were."

A voice called out to Subaru, who had his weight against the railing and was leaning back to gaze up at the sky. When he lowered his eyes, standing before him was a fairy of the moon, made only more beautiful by the moonlight bathing her.

"Oh wait, it's just Emilia-tan. I thought you were an angel."

"There you go saying weird things again. Are you drunk, by any chance?"

"I'm a minor, so I don't drink. If I'm drunk on anything, it's the mood—and myself."

"See, you are drunk after all."

Emilia laughed, a light, rolling laugh, and Subaru knit his brow at how different she seemed from usual.

Then, seeing the fair skin peeking out from her modest dress—the nape of her neck and her cheeks tinted a faint red—he understood the reason for her behavior.

"What, Emilia-tan? Forget whether I'm drunk or not—looks like you're the one who's had some alcohol."

"I did not drink. They were serving these sweet fruit drinks. I wouldn't drink alcohol and get all weird like that, so there!"

"'So there'... cute."

Pouting, lips pursed, Emilia had completely lost the solemnity from the ceremony earlier.

In other words, standing there now was simply a girl who was, through and through, nothing but adorable.

"Hey, Subaru. Why are you out here all by yourself?"

"Mm, like I said before. Drunk on the mood, and on myself."

It was a glib answer, but not entirely off the mark, either.

In truth, unable to release what welled up in his chest, he'd been out here alone in the night air—what else could you call that but drowning in his own feelings?

Of course, there was no way he could plainly confess something like that to anyone.

"Could it be... you regret it?"

"That's the one thing that's absolutely not true. I'll get mad, Emilia."

"Right, that was my fault just now. I'm sorry. But I'm glad."

Still flushed from her light buzz, Emilia closed the distance between them by a step.

Emilia lined up beside Subaru, who leaned his weight against the railing, in the same pose. Their shoulders nearly touched, and even through their clothes, Subaru's body flushed hot.

"Subaru. Sorry for springing the knighting ceremony on you so suddenly. I'd been preparing for it the whole time, so I just assumed you knew."

"Nah, maybe it was me being an idiot for not noticing. Now that I think about it, every time you'd ask, 'Subaru, are you practicing properly?' I just brushed it off with whatever excuse fit the moment."

Emilia had assumed Subaru knew, and had been diligently checking on his progress.

Only Subaru had failed to notice, muddling through each moment as it came, never quite understanding what Emilia meant, while diligently mastering entirely different things instead.

And as for the content of the ceremony—

"That's Roswaal's fault. Actually, pretty much everything lately is his fault. Is his whole goal to embarrass me? He's been getting carried away way too much recently."

"I think Roswaal's always been like that... but yes, it does seem like he's been messing with you more than before. Maybe he wants your attention."

"That's a terrifying thought, Emilia-tan."

That was, admittedly, a situation he'd rather avoid—it would make a man like Roswaal even more of a lost cause.

As Subaru made a sour face at an answer that, unexpectedly, might not have been mere joking, Emilia laughed and waved a hand lightly, saying, "I was just kidding," and—

"I'm sure Roswaal still doesn't quite know how to act, since his scheming only just got found out. I bet he'll settle back into how he was before, given a little time."

"Settling back into how he was before doesn't exactly scream 'remorse' to me, either... but I guess it beats him changing so drastically I don't know how to deal with him anymore."

Better over best. It felt like a passive sort of choice, but for now, he'd settle for that.

With the conversation reaching a lull, Emilia tipped back the glass beside Subaru. She'd had it in hand from the start, and if his guess was right, it was fruit wine.

He had the feeling her tipsiness was only going to deepen further, and it left him with the strange, conflicted urge to both dread it and want to watch.

"Hey, Subaru."

"Huh, what? The alcohol's got you feeling hot and you want to take something off? Not a good spot for that. Okay, let's change locations. Yeah, let's do that."

"Sorry. I don't really get what you're saying. That's not it."

Emilia leveled a slightly sharp look at Subaru, whose imagination had gotten ahead of itself. Shrinking under that gaze, he bowed his head, and she tilted her chin toward the great hall visible from the terrace.

"Everyone looks like they're having fun."

"Um, yeah, they do. For a noble household, having even the servants mixed into a standing buffet like this feels pretty homey. As a small-town, common-folk kind of guy myself, it's a pretty ideal sight."

"Yeah, I think so too. This is—reeeally—such a good sight, I think."

Subaru noticed, out of the corner of his eye, how her violet-indigo eyes took on a look of tenderness and longing.

What Emilia saw through those eyes and what Subaru saw were, perhaps, a little different. Surely, in this sight, Emilia saw a peaceful time free of distinctions in status or species.

That was an entirely different perspective from Subaru's own way of seeing things, which only skimmed the surface.

They were looking at the same thing, yet thinking of different things.

And Subaru didn't think that difference was a bad thing.

"What's wrong, Subaru? You've got this reeeally gentle look on your face."

"I dunno. Maybe I'm just happy that I get to look at the same thing you are and think, 'ah, that's nice.'"

"Really? Well, if you're feeling the same way I am, then I'm happy too."

"I dunno about that. That part might be different. And I think it's fine if it is."

Emilia's gaze flicked briefly toward Subaru. Feeling that look on him, Subaru kept his eyes fixed straight ahead, only letting his lips relax into the faintest of smiles.

Seeing that smile, he could tell Emilia nodded, as if satisfied by it.

It was a moment in which something hard to put into words had gotten across, and both of them could accept that it was enough as it was.

"Ah, that Otto. He's getting way too carried away—he's not even good with alcohol."

In the middle of the great hall, Otto—goaded on by Garfiel—filled a glass to the brim with what looked like expensive liquor and downed it in one go. The glass slammed down onto the table, and applause rose from those around him at Otto's impressively executed chug.

But the very next moment, everything from Otto's neck up turned bright red, and with the same momentum, it then went dead pale. A traffic-light change of color, and right after, Garfiel hoisted Otto up and dashed out of the hall in a panic.

"Headed for the bathroom, probably."

"I wonder if Otto-kun's all right. Just now, he looked like a wild dog that ate a poisonous mushroom in the forest..."

"It's only after drinking yourself under the table once, all the way to your limit, that a person truly discovers they've become an adult, Emilia-tan."

"Is that so?"

"Nah, I don't actually know either."

He was a minor, after all.

And besides, whatever liquor Otto had just brought back up must have been some fine vintage indeed. It seemed unlikely that cheap, mass-produced stuff would be served at this banquet.

When Subaru sent a brief, searching glance that way, Roswaal, sharp-eyed as ever, noticed and raised his glass. The margrave, in his usual clown makeup even today, poured down the very same liquor that had knocked Otto for a loop without the slightest trouble. Whether that was a matter of sheer presence or simple habit, who could say.

"...Hey, Subaru. There's something I want you to hear."

"Funny, that. I've got something I want to ask you too, Emilia-tan."

Breaking the sudden hush that had settled over them, Emilia whispered so only Subaru could hear. He gave a small nod in response and, still leaning against the railing, turned to face her.

Emilia turned to face him at the same moment, and they found themselves gazing at each other from a distance close enough to feel each other's breath. Subaru instinctively drew back—but,

"Don't run away."

Emilia's hand caught Subaru as he began to pull away.

The step back that should have put a full pace between them ended up only half a step, and instead brought them even closer together. Stumbling forward, Emilia's forehead met Subaru's chest with the momentum.

He tried to hastily pull back, but Emilia, gripping the hem of his formal coat, wouldn't allow it.

"Uh, Emilia-tan? I mean, I'm happy about this position, but it's kind of nerve-wracking to talk like this..."

"I'm nervous too—this is the first time I've told something this important to anyone. I'm sure we're even, you and me."

"I—I think I'm coming out ahead here, though..."

He tried to cover with a wry smile forced onto his face, but he couldn't push away Emilia looking up at him.

Feeling Emilia's warm body heat, Subaru, if nothing else trying to loosen up his own rock-hard tension, moved stiffly to shield her from view of the great hall.

Facing sideways like this, it would be obvious they were embracing. If he shifted position just a little, though, anyone in the great hall would only see Subaru with his hands on the railing, gazing up at the night sky as if composing poetry.

"Alright, no more worries about being seen—tell me anything you want."

"...All right, then. An important story for my knight. Why I'm taking part in the Royal Selection after Roswaal invited me to. That... story."

"――――"

It was a part Emilia had never told Subaru before now.

And surely it would also connect to the Trials of the Tomb that had broken her time and time again as she tried to overcome them—the scenes from her past.

Subaru caught his breath and looked down at Emilia against his chest.

Their gazes collided. Seeing himself reflected in Emilia's upturned eyes, Subaru steeled himself and gave a small nod.

"Originally, in the Elior Forest, before it froze over, my mother and I... and other elves like us, all lived there together."

Told haltingly, it was a story colored by gentle memories and sorrowful feelings.

Stumbling here and there, Emilia related her clumsy tale of the past to Subaru just as her heart felt it.

Emilia, who never knew her parents. Fortuna, who loved Emilia in her parents' stead. The villagers who kindly took in Emilia and her mother, who had nowhere else to go. And the group calling themselves the Witch Cult, along with a person named Juice, who had secretly supported them all without anyone knowing.

Even within a constrained world, Emilia had spent her days surrounded by affection and kindness.

Then came the event that ruined all of it—the day the Elior Forest froze.

The Witch Cult's rampage; the witch calling herself Pandora and the Sin Archbishop who appeared alongside her; the emergence of the Witchbeast called the Black Serpent; the tragedy that befell Fortuna and Juice. Emilia, who lost both her mother and her forest for continuing to keep a promise. And then the long span of time spent frozen in ice, and Puck, whom she met the moment she awoke.

"Puck told me he'd been waiting for me the whole time, that he'd been looking for me. Ever since then, true to his word that he'd protect me, he's stayed with me. Even now, he's waiting inside this magic stone for the moment he's woken up. ...That much, I know for certain."

"But you can't actually talk with Puck, can you?"

"He's still asleep. But that's not because Puck refuses the Contract with me. I think it's because this magic stone doesn't work as a vessel for Puck once he's awake. It needs to be a finer, colorless magic stone, or it can't hold him. If I can find that vessel, then all it'll take after that is a trigger... and I'm sure he'll show his face again."

At Emilia's throat hung a blue magic stone, fashioned into the shape of a pendant.

It was a fragment of the massive magic stone that had once sealed away Ryuzu Meyer, but insufficient to hold Puck, it never conveyed the sleeping spirit's will to her.

As though his help during the battle with Garfiel had truly been his last bit of meddling.

"Okay, I get it about Puck. But the Royal Selection—"

"Puck and I spent all our time together in the frozen forest. Sometimes I'd show my face in a village near the forest, but... I don't think I was very welcome there."

Just how much distance was packed into Emilia's "not very" was something Subaru could only guess at. And surely Emilia had no wish to speak further about that divide, either.

"Roswaal came... maybe it hasn't even been a year yet. But it was so sudden, both Puck and I were reeeally surprised."

"Well, if a guy in makeup like that suddenly showed up, I'd be surprised too."

"That too, but it's that he was suddenly inside a forest no one should have been able to enter. He greeted me in the village square when I came back, like it was the most natural thing in the world. In that vague, spacey way of his—'welcome ba-a-ack,' he said..."

"That'd..."

—surely have been quite the shock.

He wouldn't bother commenting on Roswaal's rotten personality at this point, but thinking of how Emilia and Puck must have felt back then, he could well imagine their shock in that moment.

"Puck got so, so angry... From morning until evening, he was almost constantly fighting with Roswaal. Looking back on it now, I'm glad Roswaal didn't end up frozen solid."

"Your smile's cute, but I don't think that's really a laughing matter."

"You think? Anyway, while Puck and Roswaal were fighting, we ended up telling each other what we wanted, and that's finally how it turned into a real conversation..."

"So Roswaal made melting the ice in the forest the condition for inviting you, Emilia-tan..."

Emilia's eyes went wide at Subaru jumping ahead to the conclusion.

At her reaction, Subaru gave a wry smile.

"I can tell from the way the story's been going so far. And I caught wind of something along those lines in passing before, too. But, well..."

There had to be a part where Emilia's understanding then and now differed.

Before, she had said the freezing of the Elior Forest was beyond her own power—that even working together with Puck, she hadn't been able to melt the ice.

But—

"If it was you, Emilia-tan, back then, who froze the forest, shouldn't you be able to melt the ice with your own hands?"

"...Yeah, I thought about that too. But I don't think I can, not really."

"Why not?"

"Because no matter what I try, it seems I can't bring out the same amount of power as the me in my memories."

At Emilia's words—anxious, yet touched with certainty—Subaru knit his brow.

The Emilia in her memories—if the story was to be believed, her power had utterly overwhelmed human comprehension, so much so that even the witch calling herself Pandora couldn't find an opening to strike back against it.

What did it mean that this power was absent in the Emilia who had awoken, the Emilia who had regained her memories?

"But you fought the Great Rabbit without giving an inch."

"It's true I've gotten to where I can use magic without relying on Puck or the little spirits. But that's all it is, still. I can't pull out that power."

"――――"

As though grieving her own powerlessness, Emilia clenched both fists, then shook her head weakly.

At the sight of her expression, ashamed of her lack of strength, Subaru instantly felt ashamed of the disappointment he'd felt welling up in himself.

Emilia herself, of all people, was regretting who she was now. Knowing that, there was no way he could allow himself even a thought that blamed her for it.

Besides, it wasn't as though Subaru particularly wanted Emilia to be strong.

"Okay, that's enough beating yourself up. Let's get back to it. I'll accept for now that you can't melt the ice, Emilia-tan... so then, how come Roswaal can?"

"............"

"If it's impossible for you or Puck, then, to put it bluntly, it should be impossible for Roswaal too. However amazing he is as a mage, he doesn't have ten or twenty times your power, Emilia-tan. So why?"

"It's not that Roswaal himself has the power to melt the ice. It's just that he knew of a possibility that could melt it... and he simply told me about it."

"A possibility that could melt the ice?"

A possibility for melting a forest that not even the power of a great spirit, nor the strength of the finest mage, nor even the very witch of ice who had frozen it in the first place, could melt—what on earth could that be?

"Dragon's Blood."

"――――"

"The Dragon's flowing blood, which grants the earth its bounty and heals lands whose proper state has been damaged. That power, they say, could surely melt the ice in the forest."

"Emilia, but that's..."

Did that mean killing the Dragon?

Was she saying the Dragon, that being who had watched over the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica all this time, would be offered up as a sacrifice, just to save her own home, the forest?

For an instant, the gravest of concerns crossed Subaru's mind. But—

"No, Subaru, that's not it. Just a single drop of the Dragon's blood is enough. Apparently, back when a famine struck Lugunica once before, the Dragon's blood was used to revive the land. I looked it up in the history books myself, so it's certain."

"Oh, phew... no, seriously, that had me worried for a second. I mean, if you went and killed the Dragon..."

Wouldn't that mean releasing the "Witch" sealed away by the Dragon's power?

"――――"

As though his chest had been ensnared by anxiety, Subaru forgot to breathe.

The witches he'd met at Echidna's tea party. And the "Witch of Envy" who had sent him off in that final moment. Subaru hadn't forgotten her existence.

Nor had he forgotten his resolve in the instant of that parting.

But that was a being that should never be set free.

A being whose release into this world must never be allowed to happen.

That his instincts screamed this at him was, without question, a plain fact.

"The royal family of Lugunica has an opportunity to speak with the Dragon when they exchange the Covenant with it. It seems a few drops of blood, bestowed by the divine Dragon Volcanica at that time, are kept preserved in the royal castle. I want to become queen and rely on that power."

"So that's why you're taking part in the Royal Selection..."

"...I told you before, didn't I. That I'm taking part in the Royal Selection for a reeeally selfish reason. What I just told you is that selfish reason of mine."

Her words carried a smile, but it was a smile that felt like the flip side of anxiety.

In truth, Emilia's eyes, looking up at Subaru from within his embrace, trembled faintly with unease.

It was the face of someone afraid of what Subaru might say, of what he might think of her resolve.

That she felt this anxious seemed to mean Subaru's presence in her had become something immovable enough to matter that much—or so, it seemed, he was allowed to think.

"It's okay, Emilia-tan. There's no way something like that would make me think less of you."

"...Subaru."

"Even calling it self-serving, it's not like you're thinking of lining your own pockets or anything. If there's a way to save the people you want to save, and you're going after a position you can use through legitimate means instead of turning to theft or some other wrongdoing, then there's no reason anyone should blame you for it."

He smiled at her, trying to reassure her. But Emilia's expression still didn't clear.

Subaru understood that too. The words Emilia wanted weren't the ones he'd just given.

If he were to offer up what her true heart, her truly yearning heart, was searching for—

"Are you shrinking back because you think your own reason for entering pales next to the other candidates, Emilia-tan?"

"—!"

"That's exactly the 'grass is greener' thing. Sure, someone like Crusch has both fine character and a fine purpose, but think back on Anastasia or Priscilla. Their reasons weren't anything to be praised, in fact."

Greed and self-interest—the reasons those two candidates had thrown themselves into the Royal Selection.

Subaru hadn't been present for it, but what noble reason had Felt even claimed for entering the Royal Selection, anyway?

Emilia's feeling of wanting to save someone was nothing to be looked down on next to those.

"Besides, whatever you felt at the very start, it's not like you're exactly the same now, is it?"

"...How do you know that?"

"You were just looking at the great hall with such a gentle look in your eyes. Seeing that, I could tell."

What had been unfolding in House Miload's great hall was a scene of humans and demi-humans, nobles and commoners and servants, mingling together without distinction of status or race.

Subaru had called it ideal, and Emilia had taken it in with a gaze full of longing.

Subaru had no doubt at all about what color of flame was now lit within Emilia's heart.

"If your goal is to see a sight like that, I'll help too. I think that's a good thing myself. If that becomes one more reason for you to keep working hard, Emilia-tan, I won't let anyone get in the way."

"Really... you'll help me?"

"What do you think I just swore to you a little while ago? You don't have to look so anxious about it—I want you to rely on me before anyone else. I'll help with anything I can, and if there's something neither of us knows, we'll rack our brains over it together."

"――――"

Emilia caught her breath, her eyes wavering more than they ever had before.

Unsure what to say, her trembling lips couldn't quite put her heart into clear words.

"—Mm."

So Emilia only murmured that single word.

And then she smiled.

—Subaru thought that alone was enough.

"Alright, doubts cleared up."

Declaring it, Subaru threw back the glass he'd set on the railing in one go. Then he grabbed the meat pie, gone completely cold by now, and popped it into his mouth, chewing.

The flavor hadn't dulled a bit even cold, and the pastry crumbled apart pleasantly in his mouth. No wonder Garfiel had boasted about it as his masterpiece—it was exquisite.

"Subaru, if you eat that fast you'll choke."

"If you'll feed it to me, Emilia-tan, I'll savor every bite."

"I feel like I already did that for you once before, when you were exhausted..."

At Emilia's answer, Subaru gave a small wry smile and drew her by the hand toward the great hall.

Led by the arm, Emilia glanced up at the sky just once, and then followed Subaru's escort, walking side by side with him back into the great hall.

The banquet was still going strong, and with the guests of honor's return, its energy returned along with them.

Garfiel, back after bringing the thoroughly drunk Otto along, tried to drink some more himself and ended up writhing in agony after taking hits from both front and back, courtesy of Frederica and Ram.

Petra and Beatrice's mismatched dance had reached its climax, with sweat beading on Petra's brow while Beatrice, too, showed off her own competitive streak.

Annerose wore a discontented look over Subaru having been off with Emilia, and Clind poked a finger at her puffed-out cheek, only making his mistress angrier.

Ryuzu and Roswaal sat side by side, clinking their glasses together as though renewing an old acquaintance, and tipping them back.

"Nice sight, isn't it, Emilia-tan."

"Yes. I'm sure this is my ideal sight. I won't ever forget it."

Just as she said—let's make it an unforgettable night, then.

For now, that meant crashing into the most conspicuous spot in the great hall, right in on the two girls dancing there.

Neither of them knew the first thing about the steps, but surely the joy of it was something they shared.

With careless, haphazard steps, the knight and the witch—this new lord and vassal—stepped forward together, caught between bewilderment and smiles.
