Arc 2 Chapter 9: "The Merit of Three Deaths"

"So in the end, why did you do something like that, Subaru?"

"I told you everything, exactly as it happened. If there's some other reason... hah. Don't tell me — you, evil loli over there, you tampered with my mind...? An evil little girl — 'evil-girl' for short!"

"After pulling something like that, now you're deflecting the blame onto me, I suppose?! The worst! This guy's the worst, I suppose!"

"Shut up! Like anything a loli who's been guzzling booze since broad daylight says has any legitimacy! Aren't you just drunk and seeing and hearing things wrong? And anyway, what's the legal drinking age in this country to begin with!"

Spraying spit as he shouted back at the ranting Beatrice, Subaru reflexively turned to fire the question at Rem, who happened to be standing right beside him.

Unfazed by the abrupt change of subject, Rem bent politely at the waist and said,

"What might 'okay' mean, honored guest?"

Still bent at the waist, she tilted her head with impressive dexterity, and Subaru scratched his head as he said,

"That's where we're starting?! It means 'is that all right'!"

"I see," Rem nodded. Then she exchanged glances with Ram, and—

"From fourteen years old." "From fourteen years old."

"Seriously?"

"What might 'seriously' mean?" "'Seriously'?"

"It means things like 'is that true' or 'are you certain' or 'like, isn't that super wild?'"

"Huh." "Huh."

The two of them nodded in perfect sync, as if impressed. Which, applying that logic, meant—

"Seriously, drinking's okay from fourteen?!"

"It means: is it truly all right for one to partake of alcohol from the age of fourteen?"

Mutual understanding achieved, a perfect translation had been accomplished here.

In their shared delight, the three of them couldn't help but exchange glances, and raised their hands—

"Yeaaah!"

—and high-fived one after another. Finally, with their hands still raised together toward the heavens, the three of them spun round and round in a circle on the spot.

"...Is your little skit finished, I suppose?"

"What, you've got no rhythm at all, drill-loli."

"That ri-ri chirping is annoying, I suppose! What is even wrong with you, I suppose!"

Looking positively furious, Beatrice crossed her arms and slumped back in her chair. Seeing her fall silent like that, she'd probably decided she was done arguing with Subaru. Subaru spotted Puck, up on the table, moving in to console her chestnut hair, and decided to leave that matter to the little cat.

Then, once more, he turned to face the silver-haired girl, with whom nothing at all had been resolved.

Maybe it was just his imagination, but she seemed to be looking at him with even colder, more disapproving eyes than before.

"Even that look is cute — this is basically a crime at this point..."

"...Sigh. I think I'm starting to get the hang of dealing with you, Subaru. Just let those quips you seem to say to anyone slide right past, and get to the point."

"'To anyone' is uncalled for. I only say stuff like this to you, Emilia-tan, seriously! For real-sies! Seriously for real-sies!"

"Yes, yes, I'll forget you said that, so let's get to the point."

Clapping her hands together to reset the conversation, Emilia obediently returned to her seat. Subaru had no choice but to follow suit, and once he'd settled back down, Roswaal spoke up.

"No-o-ow then, we've strayed quite fa-ar off topic, but shall we get down to business? Subaru-kun, is that all right?"

"Well, given that I haven't had my head lopped off after all that, I'll go ahead and hope it's not bad news."

At Subaru's words, Roswaal gave a whistle. Emilia, too, looked somewhat surprised — probably because she'd overestimated him, assuming he'd committed all that disrespect on purpose, to probe their intentions.

Of course, in truth it had been nothing but idle chatter and glib improvisation.

Before that got found out and he was exposed, Subaru pressed forward and pushed on. If he kept hold of the conversation's initiative, there'd be less chance of being steered somewhere bad.

"So, anyway, here's my guess, seeing as we're on the topic. You went out of your way to bring up this whole talk about Emilia-tan being a queen candidate. There's some connection there, right?"

"Are you really a pervert, Subaru? Or are you not?"

"Isn't that a pretty extreme pair of options to choose between?!"

Emilia, eyes wide, offered a light "sorry, sorry." Subaru was fairly seriously offended, but Roswaal picked up where the apology left off.

"But well, your guess is spot-o-on. The earlier talk about the queen candidates and the matter of how you'll be treated are connected. ——Emilia-sama."

"Yes, I understand."

Emilia gave a small response to being addressed and reached a hand into the folds of her clothing.

All at once, Subaru noticed that the softness had drained from the atmosphere of the dining hall. The faces around the table hadn't changed, but the air they carried had been solemnly transformed.

Naturally, even Subaru found his back straightening without meaning to. Before him, Emilia set down on the table the thing she'd drawn from within her clothes. It was—

"I-Isn't that the crest?"

It was the dragon-shaped crest that had fallen victim to Felt's thievery — the very one that had finally made its way back to its owner's hands only after Subaru had died no fewer than three times.

The red jewel shone brilliantly in its owner's hand, its radiance so strangely deep and clear that it stirred a sense of awe even in Subaru.

"The dragon represents the crest of Lugunica. It's grandly called the 'Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica,' after a-all. It's a symbol used everywhere — on castle walls, on weapons and armor, you name it. That crest in particular is exceptionally important. After all—"

Roswaal paused for a breath. At Subaru's gaze urging him onward, he nodded, then prompted Emilia with a glance to continue. She closed her eyes, and then—

"It's the qualification of a Royal Selection candidate. ——A touchstone to confirm whether someone is fit to sit upon the throne of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica."

At this fact, stated with no strength behind it, Subaru's eyes went wide, and he looked again at the red gleam on the table. The dragon crest with its wings spread wide — the sparkle of the jewel at its center backed up her words, and putting all this information together—

"D-Don't tell me... you'd lost the crest that qualifies you for the Royal Selection?!"

"'Lost' makes it sound so bad! It was stolen by a light-fingered girl!"

"Same thing——!!"

Shouting loudly and slamming the table, Subaru shot to his feet.

The dishes very nearly went tumbling off the table, but Rem, standing by, caught them beautifully. But without even glancing at that save, Subaru looked down at Emilia, whose face had gone red, eyes brimming with humiliation.

"Making a face like that, stirring up my inner sadist — that won't do at all! I mean, seriously, what's going on here?! If you lose it, can't you just get it reissued at some government office or something?!"

"I'm not sure what a 'government office' i-is, but strolling up somewhere to casually get a replacement made would be difficult, I'd imagine."

Watching the flustered Subaru, Roswaal, composed, fiddled with the oversized collar of his outfit as he spoke.

"A king is, in other words, one who bears the kingdom on their shoulders. If someone hoping to take up so weighty a duty can't even manage to protect a single small crest, that's simply unspeakable. How could anyone possibly think to entrust a country to such a person, I wonder?"

"Yeah, that's fair. Only someone as careless as Lord Mito himself would keep trusting a job to Careless Yahei over and over."

"Careless?" "Yahei?"

As the twins split the mysterious names between them, Subaru began to piece together the situation.

The stolen crest, the encounter in the back alley, the struggle at the Loot House, and now this hospitality — it all converged on a single answer.

"If the guards found out you'd lost the crest, that'd be seriously bad news. So Emilia-tan had no choice but to go searching for it all on her lonesome, like a lone wolf."

"...Yes, that's right."

"The one who stole it was Felt, but the one who put her up to it was Elsa. She said she'd been hired by someone. Does that mean there's somebody out there trying to stop Emilia-tan from becoming queen?"

"Probably so, yes. Stealing the crest to knock someone out of the Royal Selection is an easy enough scheme to come up with, after all."

Once it was put into words and confirmed, the various events of yesterday's dealings began to fit together.

Emilia's stubbornness, the person who'd hired Felt and Elsa. And the value of the crest — the direct cause of Subaru's repeated murders.

"Now that I think it through and put it all together — I did seriously good work! What, that's crazy, right?! Emilia-tan was this close to dropping out of the Royal Selection! It's not like you can just go 'oopsie, lost the crest, teehee' and have that be the end of it, right?!"

"I seriously didn't understand about sixty percent of what you just said."

"That doesn't matter right now. What's important here is this: looking back on it, I basically did a pretty darn great job for you, Emilia-tan!"

Realizing unexpectedly how great his own achievement had been, Subaru was over the moon. He rose from his seat yet again, breathing hard through his nose as he leaned his face in toward Emilia, who remained seated.

"Heh-heh, so, whaddya think, whaaadya think, Emilia-tan? Didn't I pull off a pretty nice and happy bit of heroics there? At this point, I can't help but expect some kinda reward, can I? Can I, can I, can I?"

Trailing off with a sound like a cicada's cry, Subaru gently placed a hand under the chin of the downcast Emilia and tilted her face upward.

Narrowing his eyes and playing the lecher, his whole bearing was by now that of a thoroughly seasoned corrupt magistrate. His mood, too, had climbed to quite a good place, so he braced his stomach muscles, waiting for the comeback he figured was due any moment now. But—

"...That's right. Subaru, to me, you're already a tremendous benefactor. More than just having my life saved could ever repay. So, tell me anything."

"Huh...?"

"If it's something I can do, I'll do anything. No — let me do anything. What you connected me back to means that much."

With a hand pressed to her chest, she gazed back at him with such a serious expression that Subaru lost his words.

He let go of the hand that had been touching her chin, his own emotional gauge failing to match up with how much more serious a turn the conversation had taken than he'd expected.

——Oh crap, I seriously can't read the room here.

Having ended up a loner precisely because he couldn't read the room, Subaru had a keen nose for "the air of having failed to read the room." That said, he couldn't read "the air that precedes the air of having failed to read the room" — the sense ordinary people are equipped with — so in the end, he still wound up saying things that failed to read the room all the same.

And true to form this time as well, Subaru found himself at a complete loss, his own room-blind excitement failing to mesh with the seriousness in Emilia's eyes.

"Y-You shouldn't just go around saying 'anything' so easily, you know? Like, say some ba-a-ad guy like me might put you through some heh-heh-heh, ooh-hoo-hoo kind of stuff."

"...That's fine too. If that's what you want, Subaru, I'll go along with it. Heh-heh-heh or ooh-hoo-hoo, or even 'ahn, ahn' or 'chome chome' — I'll..."

"A girl shouldn't say such indecent things! And besides, you looked like you were about to cry by the end there!"

Subaru felt relieved seeing Emilia hugging her own shoulders, looking a little like she might cry.

If she'd truly steeled herself with that much tragic resolve, even Subaru would have been at a loss for how to handle it. Of course, he chose to close his eyes to the somewhat disappointing fact that she'd apparently thought him the type to actually do such a thing.

Subaru placed a hand atop Emilia's silver hair and gave it a light pat.

For a moment, Emilia seemed startled by the feel of his palm, but seeing Subaru do it without a word, she simply fell silent and let herself be petted.

Finding her small-animal-like reaction endearing, Subaru turned back to face Roswaal.

Roswaal, who had been watching the exchange between the two with what looked like genuine enjoyment, raised his eyebrows as if to say "oh?" when he noticed Subaru's gaze.

"What is it? If we're in the wa-ay, well, I suppose we could make ourselves scarce?"

"Doing it for the first time on the dining table is way too abnormal, that's shocking. What exactly are you planning to use this silverware for, you perverted, corrupt noble...!"

"I don't believe I sa-aid anything close to that, though."

At the wry, sighing smile of the tall man, Subaru's mouth curled up at one corner, and then, with the hand not petting Emilia, he touched his own chin — the sort of thumb-and-forefinger-pinching-the-chin gesture a boy detective might make while deducing something.

"N-Now, now, now, listen up, Roz. Sure, there's a deal dangling in front of me right now that's way too good for the likes of me. Seriously, it's straight-up carrot-in-front-of-the-horse's-face territory. But my brain's not so empty that I'll just keep running after it, lured along. Before I go jumping on board with all this, I've still got plenty I want to ask, you know?"

"Ahh, let's hear it, then."

"Well then, here's my question. Answer it honestly and with sincerity, all right? If Emilia-tan's a queen candidate, then what exactly are you?"

Jabbing the finger that had been resting on his chin toward him, Subaru threw the question at Roswaal. At this, the mage twisted the corner of his mouth, forming an even more delighted smile.

  • "でジマ！マジでジマ！": the original is a wordplay where マジで ("seriously"/maji de) reversed mora-by-mora spells でジマ (de-ji-ma), evoking "Dejima," the historic island in Nagasaki. This reversal pun has no English equivalent, so I substituted a reduplicated "for real-sies" gag to preserve the comedic rhythm; flagging the loss of the original pun.
  • "かなかなかな": mimics the cry of a higurashi (evening cicada), which sounds like "kana-kana-kana" — punning on the Japanese sentence-ending particle かな ("I wonder"). Rendered as "Can I, can I, can I?" which keeps the repetition but loses the cicada association (noted via "trailing off with a sound like a cicada's cry" in the narration).
  • "うっかり八部衛"/"黄門様": likely a parody reference to うっかり八兵衛 (Ukkari Yahei, "Careless Yahei"), a bumbling retainer character from the Mito Kōmon jidaigeki franchise, with 黄門様 referring to "Lord Mito" (Mito Kōmon) himself. The source text renders the name with 部 instead of the standard 兵, which may be an intentional in-universe corruption/joke or a variant; I've translated using the standard "Yahei" reading — worth checking against wiki/fan convention if this pairing has an established localized name.
  • "りーりーうるさいのよ": リーリー is conventionally the onomatopoeia for a cricket's chirp in Japanese; possibly echoing the repeated "ri" sounds in "ドリルロリ" (drill-loli). Translated as "that ri-ri chirping," but the connection is somewhat speculative.
