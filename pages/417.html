Arc 6 Chapter 15: "Scattered in the Sand"

Subaru's fingers closed around the slender, white throat.

He squeezed with all his strength, and even as he felt the cervical bones creak and grind beneath his palms, an oddly misplaced thought crossed his mind — how slender this girl's neck was.

"...ah, ugh."

Pinned beneath him, straddled, her throat crushed under the full force of his grip, a groan escaped Ram's lips.

Her finely composed face twisted in agony, and drool trickled from the corner of her pink lips. Inside her mouth, her red tongue flickered and darted, seeking freedom, while her limbs thrashed in desperate resistance.

"—————"

If Ram fought back in earnest, Subaru wouldn't stand a chance against her.

At the Sanctuary, however briefly, she'd one-sidedly beaten Garfiel senseless, and even setting that aside, she surely had at least some training in self-defense. Naturally, she should have any number of ways to deal with a man pinning her from above like this.

But all of those options required at least one limb to be free.

"—Invisible Providence."

"...!"

As he cinched tighter and tighter around her throat, Subaru was unleashing the black emotion churning in his chest.

The invisible pool of darkness took on substance, and now it worked to pin down Ram's limbs. With her arms and legs restrained, she couldn't struggle effectively. If he kept pouring strength into the arms strangling her, her breath would give out before long.

And once her breath stopped, Ram would surely regret the vicious words she'd spat earlier.

That Subaru had neglected Rem, that he thought nothing of her — such selfish, baseless delusions, such thoughtless, misdirected words that had wounded his heart — for all of it, he could make her pay with her life.

"It's your fault. Yours, yours, yours!"

He hated Ram. He hated Ram. He hated Ram.

Small things had piled up, and now, through one great trigger, it all came spilling out.

His hatred poured into the fingers around her throat, his nails digging into her white skin until blood dripped.

"Hnn...!"

Feeling the warm slickness of blood at his fingertips, Subaru panted, and Ram turned a hate-filled gaze on him.

Even on the very brink of the flame of her life guttering out, what was this fierce resolve in the pale pink eyes glaring up at him? Eyes that seemed to say having her life taken wouldn't be enough to kill her.

Once he'd strangled her to death, he'd gouge those eyes out. He decided as much.

"I'll tear you to pieces, you damn woman. ...The thought that you — someone like you — has been looking at me with a face just like Rem's makes me sick."

"............."

"Huh? What, I can't hear you. If you've got something to say, spit it out—"

"...ra."

Overlapping with the tail end of Subaru's snarled words, Ram murmured something, faint and small.

The instant he narrowed his eyes at the sound of it—

Beneath Subaru, who had Ram pinned to the ground, the sand itself exploded, and both their bodies were blown apart.

"Wha—?!"

"Ngh...!"

Caught in the sudden blast, sprayed with sand that fouled his eyes and mouth, Subaru toppled backward, tasting sand and pain. Ram was caught in the explosion too, her small body taking the brunt of it even worse than Subaru, sent tumbling across the ground.

Lacerated by the blast, Ram left a trail of dripping blood scattered across the sand.

But she had broken free of Subaru's grip. That was the fact of it.

"Ram! You bitch, you used magic—!"

"A sliver... of breath. That's all... I needed... to cast..."

Ram said this to Subaru, propping herself up on her knees, gasping for breath.

Even while being strangled, Ram had barely managed to chant the wind spell "Fura." Unable to aim it precisely or afford to miss, she'd set it off directly beneath herself.

As a result, she'd caught Subaru in the blast along with herself, and escaped the worst of it.

"Tch—!"

"—————"

Before he could even fully register the fact, Subaru's hand was already reaching behind his hip for his whip. At the same moment, Ram drew a small wand from within her clothes, her will to fight blazing.

In sheer destructive power, the mage Ram had the advantage, but in speed of attack, Subaru wouldn't lose. The whip's rapid strike could reach even the superhuman-level fighters of this world. He'd crack it across that composed face of hers and make it something no one could bear to look at twice.

"Crying and apologizing now won't do you any good!"

"That's my line. A heartless womanizer like you, Barusu, isn't even worth letting near Rem. Get sliced into rounds and wither away on this sand."

"Keep talking!"

Facing Ram's quietly blazing killing intent, Subaru's resolve to answer it hardened as well.

By the light of the fallen lantern between them, the two circled each other, inching for distance, glaring without pause.

In mere seconds, the killing would begin in earnest—and it was in that instant just before—

"—Okay, that's enough outta you two!"

"—?!"

A loud clap rang out, and at the dry sound of it, both Subaru's and Ram's heads snapped up. The interloper who'd done it, Anastasia, was looking down at the two of them from atop her dragon.

Still seated calmly on Patrasche's back, alone, she glanced back and forth between the two who'd been about to fight to the death, and said:

"That's enough, now. You two're supposed to be on the same side, and here you are glarin' at each other with those scary faces — I can't just let that slide. There's a limit, even for you two."

"A limit? Yeah, there's a limit all right. And she's the one who blew right past it and said something she never should have! So I'm making her pay for it!"

"Oh, come on, there's just no talkin' to you. What about you, Ram-san?"

Anastasia looked exasperated at Subaru, who jabbed a finger at Ram, spittle flying as he shouted. She turned the question to Ram, but the pink-haired girl's answer was little different from Subaru's.

Wand still trained warily on Subaru, maintaining her combat stance, she said:

"A foolish question. Barusu's attitude is beyond intolerable... I'm well past the limit of my patience. If he cares so much about only himself, then I'll grant his wish and drop him into a darkness all his own."

"I'm so done with you I can't even respond. Drop dead."

"No good on this side either, huh. Honestly, what is this. This is way more than I signed up for..."

With Ram showing no sign of easing her hatred, Subaru's fury showed no sign of fading either.

Anastasia shrugged, throwing up her hands at the two of them trading barbed murderous intent. Watching her out of the corner of his eye, Subaru began to feel irritation toward Anastasia as well.

Why, in the first place, did Anastasia get to act like some outsider who had nothing to do with any of this?

Whose idea, exactly, did she think it had been to come all the way out to the Augria Sand Dunes and aim for the Sage's watchtower in the first place? She was unmistakably a party to this, one of the people with the strongest motive of all for reaching the watchtower — so who did she think she was, leaving everything to everyone else?

And on top of it all, here she still sat, alone, up on Subaru's own Patrasche, looking down on them—

"Hey, you. What's with the lecture from up on high? Get down."

"...Oh geez, that's the direction we're goin'?"

"Before you talk down to anybody, get down to the same level first. I'll hear you out after that. If you can't manage that much, don't you dare talk to me like you're above me!"

He cracked the whip in his hand downward, and along with the whistle of it cutting the air, a spray of struck sand kicked up.

It was a weapon that would burst skin and leave a hideous wound if it landed. Even if Anastasia's true self inside was Eridna, surely she had no wish to damage her host's body.

Sure enough, following Subaru's demand, Anastasia slowly climbed down off Patrasche. Landing gingerly on the sand, she tilted her head and said, "So?"

"I came on down like ya asked... so, Natsuki-kun, what is it you wanna do?"

"Isn't it obvious? I'm making that idiot take back what she just said. If she won't take it back, then there's only one thing left to do."

If Ram apologized in earnest, from the heart, and retracted every word she'd said and truly changed her attitude, Subaru wasn't entirely unwilling to consider leniency.

But Ram's reaction to hearing this was plain and singular.

"So that's what Natsuki-kun's sayin' — what d'you think about that, Ram-san?"

"Even I have to say — my conclusion was so flawless there isn't a single point in it worth retracting. It's beyond me what he thinks I ought to be rephrasing, or how."

Ram declared this with a scornful huff, her attitude one of utter, thoroughgoing contempt.

That was her answer. And with it, any chance of forgiveness was lost forever.

"You heard her. Stay out of this. I'm making sure that face of hers — the one just like Rem's — only exists once in this world."

"—————"

"Huh? Oi. Got nothing to say now?"

"Just talking to myself here, but — talk to a fool and foolishness rubs off on you. So I won't."

A vein bulged on Subaru's forehead as his patience finally reached its breaking point.

He surged forward, charging straight at Ram, ready to tear her limb from limb — and Anastasia leapt out in front of him, spreading both arms wide.

"Now now now! C'mon, calm down! It's way too early for conclusions!"

"Get out of the way! You want me to kill you first instead?!"

"Just leave this to me, okay? C'mon now, Ram-san, hold on a sec..."

Holding back Subaru as he tried to step forward, Anastasia forced her way over toward Ram instead. Ram, silent, still glaring at Subaru, reluctantly came to stand beside Anastasia.

Still, it seemed unlikely this would make her change her stance. Indeed, judging by her expression, Ram had not the slightest intention of doing so — she'd only come over to Anastasia out of some sense of obligation.

"Listen, Ram-san. I get that the mood's bad and everybody's feelings are all frayed up right now, but..."

"That hardly matters right now. More importantly, that fool—"

"—Yeah, you're right. Doesn't matter anymore, does it."

Irritated, Ram moved to cut the conversation with Anastasia short as well. For the first time, her gaze left Subaru, turning to look Anastasia dead in the face.

The trigger for it was an impact. An impact that drove into the left side of Ram's chest — from a thick-bladed knife gripped in Anastasia's hand.

"Ah, guh...?"

"Just weighed up which one of you's more useful, is all. Forgive me for it, will ya?"

With a practiced twist, she drew the knife back out of the wound.

Ram looked down at the fatal wound now in her left breast, blood spilling from the corner of her mouth. Then she looked up at Anastasia, saw the girl raise a hand lightly and smile — and collapsed.

Ram crumpled limply onto the sand, and the blood pouring from her was swallowed up by the dry ground.

"Wha—huh?"

Subaru watched it happen, powerless to do anything at all.

His palm, still gripping the whip, trembled violently, and his back teeth ground together in fury.

"You—why would you—!"

"If Natsuki-kun kept fussin' around like that, Ram-san's guard woulda never dropped and it woulda turned into a real fight to the death, right? And what if you'd both ended up hurt, or worse, both dead? I can't get outta a place like this on my own, y'know."

Anastasia tilted her head, her expression utterly guileless, in the face of Subaru's furious glare.

Her words were logical enough, but what was he supposed to do with this raging emotion now crushed under the weight of that logic? The impulse he'd meant to unleash on Ram remained unspent, his fury unreleased.

"Or is it — you plannin' to work off that irritation on me instead?"

"—————"

"Don'tcha think it'd be plain stupid to get so caught up in some petty anger that you lose sight of the goal? Losin' a comrade over somethin' this trivial — once is already more than enough, don't you think?"

Anastasia's words doused his raging emotions, slowly, like water poured over flame.

She had a point — it would be foolish, after coming this far, to keep whittling down the number of minds available to think. And Anastasia — or rather, Eridna — was their guide to the Pleiades Watchtower. Whether her knowledge would still prove useful at this point was questionable, but she was too valuable to simply throw away.

Unlike Ram, who only said infuriating things and served no practical use whatsoever, Anastasia was a piece he couldn't afford to lose.

"...Fine. I'll play along with your little pitch for now."

"Mm, that's good. Ahh, what a relief. I was worried there."

Anastasia patted her chest in exaggerated relief, breaking into a broad smile. Then, stepping carefully across the sand, she came over to Subaru and held out her hand.

A handshake, presumably.

"A handshake for makin' up, and for lookin' after each other from here on out."

"—————"

Subaru looked down at the hand offered with such an innocent expression and fell into thought for a moment.

What Anastasia was really thinking — or rather, what Eridna was really thinking, as things stood — was genuinely beyond him. He wasn't naive or optimistic enough to believe every word out of her mouth was the truth.

After all, Anastasia had just killed Ram with that very same smile on her face, using a knife she'd kept hidden. Yes — a knife she had kept hidden.

The thick-bladed knife in her hand looked capable, handled right, of cutting through bone.

It was something that had been packed alongside the lanterns in the emergency supply bag, a survival tool chosen for its practicality. But Anastasia had kept it hidden all this while, only using it for self-defense at just the right moment. —No, perhaps it hadn't even been self-defense.

"Natsuki-kun?"

"—————"

"Can't bring yourself to take my hand?"

The outstretched right hand was empty, but the left still held the knife. Handshake distance was, by the same token, knife-strike distance.

If he took her hand, at least one of his own would be tied up. At this range, whether the knife or the whip had the advantage wasn't even worth considering. Wasn't the very act of taking her hand a trap in itself?

—Shouldn't he kill her before she killed him?

"Natsuki-kun?"

"No, it's nothing."

Subaru gave a thin smile, then reached out for Anastasia's right hand.

The moment their hands clasped, the instant her guard dropped completely — that would be his opening.

—Invisible Providence.

The "Unseen Hand" activated once more, silently, its black phantom fingers slowly taking aim at Anastasia's slender throat.

He wouldn't make the same mistake he had with Ram. This time, he'd have the black palm crush her throat without mercy.

He'd show this woman a thing or two — the one so smugly confident she held the advantage with her knife.

Though, of course, however much he wanted to show her, this palm wasn't visible—

"—————"

And so, with the black phantom hand's fingers already hooked around Anastasia's throat, Subaru slowly closed his hand around the one she offered. The feel of her slender fingers — and Anastasia's smile deepened.

At the same moment, a grim, murderous smile rose to Subaru's own lips.

"Well then..."

Now, he was certain of it.

Before Anastasia could say whatever she was about to say, before she could raise the knife, he unleashed the power of the phantom hand. Her slender neck was seized by black fingers, her cervical spine snapped in an instant—

—But an instant before that, a released blade of wind cleaved Anastasia's torso in two from behind.

It happened right after the illusion of a gust of wind passing.

Red spray burst out before his eyes, and with a dazed, "Eh?", Anastasia's body was severed clean through the middle, top from bottom.

A great gush of blood spilled across the white sand, steam rising from hot innards and waste mixing with the cold night air, a horrific stench filling the hollow.

Pink coils of small and large intestine, viscera still faintly twitching with life, rolled out onto the sand — the scene had become, in effect, an exhibition of one girl's insides laid bare.

"—Ah?"

Witnessing the sight, Subaru looked down at his own hand.

There, still clasped tightly in his right hand, hung the upper half of Anastasia's body. Her eyes were wide open, staring up at Subaru in blank shock.

Behind her, the lower half of her body — severed at the waist — had already toppled over, doll-like legs twitching in spasms as relaxed muscles let loose a stream of urine.

"Ah—ahhhhhhh—?!"

At the horrific sight of Anastasia, a scream tore from Subaru's throat.

He tried to shake the grip off his arm reflexively, but the strength of the hand clamped around his was inhuman. Unable to break free, all Subaru could do was swing Anastasia's now half-weight body around wildly. The violent motion only worsened the bleeding, jostling the intestines still connecting upper and lower halves, spattering bodily fluids everywhere and thickening the stench.

"L-let go! Let go of me!"

"No, no! I don't wanna die...!"

"You're already dead! There's no way you're gonna make it!"

Her lament was far more clear, far more alive than he could have imagined, and Subaru shouted back with a scream of his own.

Her upper and lower halves had been split apart; this much blood and viscera had spilled out. That she hadn't died instantly was wrong. That she was still gripping his hand was wrong. Everything about this was wrong.

"Let go let go let go let go let go let go LET GO—!"

"No, no! Don't — don't leave me here...!"

"It's too late to say that now—guah?!"

Anastasia, upper body alone, clung to his arm, and searing pain shot through Subaru's shoulder as he tried to fling her off. Looking down, he saw Anastasia driving the knife still clutched in her left hand into his body, trying to keep him from escaping.

The sharp blade pierced clean through Subaru's left shoulder, even cracking the collarbone beneath, and the searing pain and rush of blood tore another scream from him.

"Gaaaahhh—?! You damn, freaking—just hurry up and DIE already!!"

"N-nooo...!"

Grabbing Anastasia's face roughly, Subaru forcibly tore her body away from him. The girl screamed something through her tears, and at last Subaru managed to wrench her off and hurl her aside.

Already small to begin with, Anastasia's body — now halved and drained of so much blood — felt almost weightless, hollowed out. It bounced across the sand and rolled to a stop in the pool of blood it had made.

"Hah, hah, haah...!"

Paying it no more mind, Subaru clapped a hand to the stab wound on his right shoulder. His vision flickered with the pain, his heart pounding fit to burst at the sight of the blood, but the wound wasn't life-threatening.

Gripped by a nausea-inducing tension, Subaru finally arrived at a thought that came far too late.

That blow just now, the one that had killed Anastasia—

"—Barusu."

"Guh—ah."

A shadow rose swaying before Subaru, who sat sprawled on the ground, gasping in pain.

In the faint light of the sand-buried lantern, what emerged was a slender girl, half her body drenched in blood — Ram.

Gouged through the chest by Anastasia's knife, she'd been thought dead, but she still drew breath. And in that same relentless will, she had used magic to kill Anastasia.

"...You damned fool. If you're gonna die, die alone."

Looking up at Ram as she swayed, Subaru spat this out bitterly.

Ram, standing there, was well past merely battered — Anastasia's blow had unmistakably been a fatal one. The bleeding from her chest wound hadn't stopped; even now, blood streamed endlessly from the hem of her skirt. She would bleed out before long.

But before that happened, she could still take Subaru down with her.

"D-damn it..."

Subaru cast his gaze around, searching for his whip. But even if he found it, his right arm — the one he used to wield it — was wounded through the shoulder. His left hand couldn't handle it the same way as his dominant hand.

Then there was nothing left but this.

"Invisible Providen—?!"

The trump card he'd already relied on so many times in this short span.

The instant he tried once more to unleash the power of that invisible black palm, his brain erupted in a searing pain, an agony like an awl boring through his skull.

"Gah!? Aahh!? Ghoahh!?"

As waves of agony struck him again and again, vomit spilled from the corner of Subaru's mouth.

Whether it was the price for pushing his trump card past its limit, a hellish feast had erupted inside his skull, starting from his nerves, and it tore his consciousness to shreds. The pain in his right shoulder was nothing in comparison.

A sense of loss like his skull and brain were being scraped away, his very soul gnawed at, sealed Subaru's awareness shut.

Far from putting up even a clumsy fight with the whip, Subaru — writhing helplessly where he lay — was easy prey.

Ram, dying, moved her body on nothing but the fuel of her hatred, slowly raising her blood-soaked wand toward Subaru.

"Fuu—"

"Aaahh! Gahh! Ohhhh aaahhh!?"

Clutching his head, Subaru writhed in the pool of Anastasia's blood.

Looking down at him, Ram's lips moved haltingly, and the chant began.

And it was—

"Ra—"

"—————"

The incantation completed, and a blade of wind sprang forth, ready to shred Subaru to pieces.

An instant before that happened, a sound rang out through the cold, hollow sand — something hard being crushed between teeth.

The grating sound continued, again and again, mixing with wet sounds and the noise of flesh being chewed.

"Ah... ah."

He'd been certain of it — that he would die, be sliced apart.

But the "death" that should have come never arrived. Eventually, from the head of Subaru, still panting and thrashing on the ground, the headache and the sense of loss gradually began to fade.

"Ah, ha, ah, huh?"

He covered his face with his left hand and shook his cold-sweat-drenched head roughly. A dull ache remained, but the knife-sharp pain had receded. Gasping for breath again and again, he hauled himself upright with everything he had.

Taking an absurdly long time just to sit up, Subaru wiped at his own wet cheek.

His palm came away stained crimson, and he realized the source was his own eyes — apparently, he'd wept tears of blood.

Blood streamed heavily from his nose too; the lower half of his face was probably a horrific sight. That pain, like an awl driven straight into his brain — it had to be the price of the "Unseen Hand."

It was a common enough story. The feedback from using a special power beyond his limits — that's what had hit him.

"What... happened..."

Wiping his face roughly with his sleeve, Subaru looked around.

He had no idea how long he'd been writhing in agony. And yet it seemed impossible that he'd had the luxury of that much time.

Something had happened, and because of it, he had—

"Patrasche?"

"—————"

Beside Subaru, sitting there in a daze, the jet-black Earth Dragon had drawn close.

Crouched in the sand, the dragon noticed his call and swished her long tail, showing she was alive and well.

"You're okay, then. Um..."

At once, the events of just before came flooding back.

Anastasia cleaved in two, and Ram, who had done it, looming over Subaru, taking aim.

Yes — as things had stood, Subaru should have been torn apart at Ram's hands.

"But then why... where's Ram?"

Had she collapsed, her strength finally spent?

It seemed a suspiciously convenient turn of events, but Ram too had suffered a fatal wound. It wouldn't be strange if that were what had happened.

"Am I lucky, or unlucky..."

Having nearly died, he didn't want to call himself lucky. But then, if his luck had truly been bad, he'd be dead — so he couldn't quite call it unlucky either.

Either way, now wasn't the time to dwell on it. If the obstacles were gone, he needed to get away from here quickly and head for the watchtower.

"Patrasche... sorry, but let me ride."

"—————"

"Patrasche?"

He called out and reached for her back, but the dragon didn't obey his command.

If anything, Patrasche, crouched there, still hadn't so much as turned her face toward Subaru even once. She simply sat there, her massive frame settled calmly on the sand, breathing faintly ragged breaths beside him.

"Hey, Patrasche. You listening? Hey."

Irritation began, gradually, to build.

It was close to what he'd felt toward Ram and Anastasia — a spiral of negative emotion piling up at an unnaturally rapid pace.

Faced with a dragon that wouldn't answer his call, he felt the urge to teach her her place.

"Hey, Patrasche, look at me! Hey!"

"—————"

"You damn — listen to me for once—"

As he slapped her back roughly and raised his voice, Patrasche lifted her head. Snorting roughly at the gesture, Subaru moved to bring their faces together and hurl his words at her — and then he noticed.

Patrasche's mouth, now turned toward him, was stained an abnormal, deep red.

"—————"

That crimson was a color he had, in this short span of time, already become thoroughly, sickeningly familiar with.

It was smeared across his own body in places too, and it was still flowing from his right shoulder even now. The stench hanging in the air was thick with waste and that same color — there was no mistaking it.

But he hadn't wanted to notice, and he shouldn't have had to.

"—————"

Around Patrasche's mouth, turned toward him now, countless strands of pink hair were tangled in her fangs.

"Hi—"

Beyond Patrasche's crouched, massive body, Ram — whom he'd lost sight of — lay collapsed.

He couldn't tell whether her fallen body lay face-down or face-up. Looking at her head would settle it easily enough — and that was exactly why he couldn't tell.

Everything above the neck had been crushed and chewed beyond recognition, which was precisely why there was no telling.

Her skull crushed by savage fangs, brain matter spilling out, Ram was dead. Just as Anastasia had died with her intestines spilled out, Ram had died with the contents of her head spilled out instead.

And Patrasche, who had done this, now gazed at Subaru.

Those yellow, reptilian eyes were terribly sharp, steeped in madness.

"—Sto—"

Those jaws opening wide before his eyes were the last thing he saw.

Right up until his consciousness finally cut out, Natsuki Subaru kept hearing the sound of his own body being crushed between teeth. On and on, even after his head was crushed, even after his ears had died.

It was strange enough — where was he even hearing the sound from? — that he almost wanted to laugh, but he had no mouth left to laugh with, no life left to laugh with, and nothing left worth laughing about anyway.

Crushed between the jaws of his partner, he died.

  • 「ふー」 (Ram's fragment mid-chant, rendered "Fuu—") is ambiguous — could be an exhale or the start of a spell-word; kept as a breathy sound rather than assuming it's a syllable of "Fura."
  • Anastasia's Kansai-dialect speech is rendered with informal contractions/phonetic spelling ("gonna," "y'know," dropped g's) to mark her distinct register per the instructions' principle of preserving recognizable speech patterns, though no specific convention was given for her in the terminology list.
