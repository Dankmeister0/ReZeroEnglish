Side Story: "Rem Meets Subaru"

1

—Feeling a faint throb in her chest, the girl turned her gaze to the window outside.

Looking past the mansion window toward the gate, and the single road connecting it to the highway beyond, the girl turned back around.

"Roswaal-sama, it seems sister is returning. Though, she seems to be in a bit of a hurry."

"O-o-oh my, back earlier than scheduled, is she? If she's arriving soon, that would mean she left the other side at night, wouldn't it?"

To the girl who reported this on the strength of a throb in her chest, the man responded in his characteristically vague, breezy tone.

He was a figure seated in a leather-upholstered chair, a steaming cup in hand. His long indigo hair and his mismatched blue-and-yellow eyes were captivating, and at first glance his bearing could be called that of a fragile, elegant gentleman.

However, that noble impression was utterly ruined by the clownish makeup applied to his face.

Roswaal—the man so addressed split his rouged lips sideways and turned his gaze to the girl.

"In a hurry, is she. Something must have happened, then. Do you know the details?"

"My apologies, I don't know the particulars. However, it seems she has business with Beatrice-sama."

"With Beatrice? I see... but that's going to be quite the troublesome affair, isn'‑t it."

Roswaal looked up at the ceiling as he spoke, then set his cup down on the desk with a theatrical gesture. He crossed his long legs the other way, turned his chair to face the girl directly, and said,

"Well, whatever the case, there's no way Ram would do something pointless. I'm sorry to ask, but could you go find Beatrice for me? I imagine it'll take a bit of doing."

"Yes, understood."

Receiving her master's order, the girl lightly pinched the hem of her maid uniform and responded with a perfect curtsy. Her bowed head was adorned with a white headdress over hair cut short and neatly trimmed, colored blue.

Receiving his attendant's bow, Roswaal nodded his chin in satisfaction.

"Well then, I leave it in your capable hands. —Rem."

The girl—Rem—bowed once more, solemnly, and then quietly withdrew from the study.

2

Her blue hair swaying, Rem strode briskly down the corridor of the sprawling mansion.

This was the residence of the Margrave of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica, Roswaal L. Mathers. Rem held the position of head servant of this household, an all-purpose maid who shouldered the bulk of the mansion's operations.

Roswaal Manor was quite sizable as far as mansions went, but at present, due to various circumstances, only two servants resided there full-time—Rem, and her twin elder sister, Ram. As a result, in truth, most of the household's work, which one might think would suffer from a lack of hands, was handled by Rem alone.

And on this particular day, following word from her sister, who had been away from the mansion on business, came the exchange just described.

"Given the distance for our synesthesia, I'd say it'll be about an hour before sister returns."

There existed magic capable of communicating across distance without any time lag, but Rem and her sister had no need of it.

The twins possessed a synesthetic bond—between Rem and her elder sister Ram lay a special, extraordinary ability that conveyed strong emotions and small intentions to the other. It was precisely this synesthesia that had conveyed her sister's return, along with a strange sense of urgency.

However, whether Rem could actually fulfill her sister's instructions was another matter entirely, not something she could be certain of.

"If only the Great Spirit-sama were here, this would go so much faster... Beatrice-sama?"

There was no use wishing for what she didn't have, so Rem bravely took on the difficult task before her.

The task itself was extremely simple: try door after door, opening every one that caught her eye. However, the mansion had nearly a hundred doors. Naturally, it was no easy work.

"Beatrice-sama, where might you be?"

She opened a door bearing a plate that read "Archive." She was greeted by the sight of countless books and stacks of innumerable documents. Ordinary enough. It was the archive, after all.

Ordinarily, no one made a habit of staying in the archive. Nor was that Rem's objective.

"Beatrice-sama? Beatrice-sama?"

Calling out as she went, Rem calmly, yet at an unusually brisk pace, opened door after door in succession. After about five minutes of this—

"Enough! You're noisy, and bothersome, and altogether much too clamorous, I suppose!"

With such a violent, yet adorable, voice, a girl came bursting out of the room right next door.

It was a young girl with hair done up in ringlets, clad in a lavish dress. A girl with features as flawlessly arranged as a doll's—at her appearance, Rem straightened her back and bowed deeply.

"You've been noisy as a chattering flock of birds this whole time, I suppose! Just what business could you possibly have with Betty at this hour of the night!"

"My apologies, Beatrice-sama. But I'm glad—you were still awake."

"Hmph, even asleep, I'd have been woken by that racket, I suppose. You've got some nerve, little sister."

To Rem's bow, the girl—Beatrice—haughtily sniffed, book still clutched to her chest. It was a rather high-handed attitude, but Rem paid it no mind whatsoever.

Beatrice was a being of noble standing. That she should treat a servant like Rem this way was only natural.

"By the way, Beatrice-sama, Roswaal-sama is summoning you. He's asked me to convey that it's a matter for which he absolutely requires your assistance, Beatrice-sama."

"Summoning Betty through a servant—my, how important he's become, I suppose. Seems he's forgotten there's no hierarchy between Betty and Roswaal, I suppose."

"────"

More than the late-night summons itself, it was the one-sided manner of it that seemed to displease Beatrice, who grew visibly cross. Rem, faced with this, could say nothing and could only press her lips shut.

"...Haah. Before Betty's mood changes, you'd best hurry up and lead the way, I suppose."

Breaking the brief silence, Beatrice showed her magnanimity, as if she'd simply grown tired of arguing. Saved by that consideration, Rem began walking down the corridor, leading Beatrice onward.

"Hmm... I can hear big brother's instructions. You're to prepare hot water, a clean set of towels, and a guest room, I suppose."

"A guest room?"

"That's right, I suppose. —Seems that half-baked girl is bringing someone home with her."

Her eyes freezing with emotion, Beatrice muttered this with a somewhat displeased air. Without remarking on the comment, Rem silently turned Beatrice's instructions over in her mind.

The guest rooms were always kept in perfect readiness. All that was needed was to straighten the sheets and air out the room. As for the rest—boiling water, preparing clean cloths—she understood at once: an injured person was being brought in.

That also explained why her sister needed Beatrice.

—Beatrice was a wielder of healing magic on a level Rem could never hope to match.

In the distance, she heard the whinny of an Earth Dragon, and sensed that a Dragon Carriage was drawing near the mansion.

3

—The black-haired boy lying in the bed breathed deep, steady breaths, as if he were dead.

Outside the window, treating the boy who had been brought in at dawn had taken a considerable amount of time, and the sun had already taken on an orange hue as it sank toward the west. Before too long, night would fall.

"This time, I think we can finally relax..."

So murmured the silver-haired girl—Emilia—seated in a chair beside the bed, her lips loosening in relief.

It was Emilia who had brought this boy back and requested treatment for his grave injuries. Now that the commotion had finally settled somewhat, Rem posed a question to her.

"Emilia-sama, now that things have calmed down, perhaps you could tell us the circumstances..."

"Mm, right. Um, this boy's name is Subaru. In the royal capital, I was going through kind of a lot... and he saved me there. My great benefactor, for my life and so much else."

"I... see."

At an explanation that was, to say the least, vague, Rem glanced briefly at the person standing beside her—her twin elder sister, with pink hair, whose face was the very image of her own.

Receiving her younger sister's glance, Ram—her expression unchanging—shook her head.

"Ram doesn't know the details either. It happened while she was separated from Emilia-sama."

"Separated...? Sister, weren't you accompanying Emilia-sama as her escort...?"

"That was so as to encourage Emilia-sama's independence, and foster a stronger sense of responsibility in her, on purpose."

"As expected of you, sister... Rem is deeply impressed."

It was a rather presumptuous thing to say, but Rem accepted her sister's statement unconditionally. At this exchange between the sisters, Emilia burst out laughing.

"You two really are so close, aren't you. But don't make me laugh too much. I want to let him rest quietly for now."

"So this person is someone Emilia-sama met while separated from sister, is that correct?"

"No, not really. Subaru is my... what is he to me, exactly?"

Emilia touched a finger to her chin and began pondering in earnest. There was no sign she was trying to dodge Rem's question—she genuinely seemed at a loss for an answer.

If it was an encounter difficult to explain, and she wasn't trying to hide anything, then it simply meant there was too little information to go on about this person.

—Wasn't this exactly the sort of individual who should be kept furthest from Emilia right now?

"—Rem. This person is Emilia-sama's benefactor, and Roswaal-sama has acknowledged as much. However foolish his face may look, he is a guest of this household. You understand what that means, don't you."

"But, sister—"

"Rem."

A single, short address. That alone made Rem feel as though her sister had peered deep into the recesses of her heart, and she found she could say nothing further.

Watching the two sisters fall silent with some curiosity, Emilia slowly rose from her seat.

"I really do want to stay by his side until he wakes up, but... they did say he won't wake until morning."

"It seems the treatment used up nearly all of his own mana. Ram was rather surprised that Beatrice-sama agreed to help treat someone of unknown origin."

"In exchange, tomorrow Puck's going to belong to Beatrice all day. And, um, Roswaal is... leaving the mansion again tonight, wasn't he?"

"He departed before evening on urgent business. He's expected to return tomorrow morning."

"I see. Well then, let's step out of the room for now, shall we."

Seemingly satisfied with the sisters' answers, Emilia gave a light stretch and gestured for the two of them to leave as well. Then, at the last moment, Emilia combed her fingers through the bangs of the boy lying in the bed—Subaru.

"When you wake up, I've got a lot I want to talk to you about, Subaru."

In that whispering voice, Rem thought she detected something like a sweet anticipation.

With that, the three of them left the room, scattering into the corridor to their respective duties. Emilia in particular had an obligation to report the commotion in the royal capital to Roswaal.

"Ugh, I'm definitely going to get scolded, aren't I. What am I even supposed to say..."

Shoulders slumped, Emilia trudged off toward her private chambers in the mansion. Watching her go, Rem parted ways with Ram, who still had work remaining, and headed for the kitchen to prepare dinner—

"—Before that."

Having confirmed that the presence of the other two had faded away, Rem slipped once more into the room where the boy slept.

She closed the door behind her without a sound and, undeterred by the dim, unlit room, walked straight to the bed.

Holding her breath, only the boy's peaceful sleeping breath reached Rem's ears. Even in the darkness, Rem's pale blue eyes clearly captured the boy's innocent, sleeping face.

—A boy of unknown origin, unknown purpose, and altogether far too suspicious.

Emilia had called him a benefactor, but Rem found it hard to believe he had no ulterior motives. Emilia was beautiful, and yet, watching her was nerve-wracking—she was so full of openings, and there was no shortage of people who targeted her with ill intent.

—Shouldn't something be done before a problem arose—before the peace was disturbed?

A dangerous thought reared its head, and without a word, Rem reached her hand toward the boy's neck. And then—

"Uhihi, even angry, she's so cu-cute... other world, fantasy..."

The boy, wearing an utterly slovenly expression, drooling, muttered some loose, nonsensical sleep-talk.

The moment she heard it, Rem found herself at a loss for what to do with her outstretched hand.

"—Gyan!"

She flicked his forehead with her finger, and at the sight of the boy yelping in his sleep, still fast asleep, Rem felt a measure of satisfaction.

"A guest I absolutely cannot see myself getting along with... Please leave as soon as possible."

Before leaving the room, only that stiffly formal, impertinent murmur of Rem's fell into the empty guest room.
