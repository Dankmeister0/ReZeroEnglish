Arc 4 Chapter 80: "A Rough Tongue"

Waking came from the sensation of something rough stroking his cheek.

The moment he became aware of consciousness surfacing, what Subaru felt was a lethargy invading his entire body. Every vein felt so heavy it was as though lead had been poured in place of blood. When he tried to open his mouth to breathe, his dried, cracked lips split apart, a sharp pain spreading the taste of blood through his mouth. In a mouth where even saliva had run dry, he could feel his stinging tongue crawling about in search of the moisture that was blood.

His limbs moved sluggishly, and his head worked poorly, as if he had a fever. He didn't even have the strength to push his eyelids open, and had to roll his heavy eyeballs just to manage it.

And then—

"...You, huh."

The instant his vision took on color, Subaru saw before him a jet black different in shade from the inside of his eyelids. The thing moving there breathed out a beast's musky breath, all the while continuing to lick his cheek as if tending to sleeping Subaru.

A glossy black body, a slender, refined form. Sharp yet somehow endearing reptilian eyes, and fangs like knives that looked like they'd finish you off in one bite—from that mouth where those fangs peeked out, a red tongue extended to lick Subaru's cheek. It was his beloved dragon, Patrasche.

Once Patrasche noticed that the now-awake Subaru had perceived her, she stopped licking and settled down to sit, as if waiting for words. The surprisingly dexterous Earth Dragon was capable of folding her knees to sit in place.

Seeing Patrasche in front of him, Subaru realized he'd been leaning against something hard, his legs sprawled out on the ground. Tilting his head to look behind him, he found a moss-covered stone wall at his back—one he recognized as being near the entrance to the tomb.

"I was definitely inside... why am I out here...?"

Up until now, waking from the dream castle had always meant waking from inside the tomb. Of course, it would be a different story if someone had dragged unconscious Subaru outside, but the only two people who could enter the tomb in the current 'Sanctuary' were Emilia and Garfiel.

The idea that either of them had carried Subaru out felt utterly unrealistic.

"Then again, I can't imagine I crawled out here on my own either, so just what..."

He tried to continue with who, but the word was suddenly cut off by another voice ringing out.

Far off, beyond Patrasche, a figure came running with a raised voice, and as it drew closer, out of breath and nearly tripping over its own feet—

"Hey! P-Patrasche-chan, hold on a sec...! Hah, hah... If you'd gotten away, I'd have been in serious trouble... huh?"

The one who caught sight of Patrasche and stopped in his tracks with a look of profound relief was a gray-haired young man—Otto. He let out a sigh of relief at Patrasche's condition, then noticed Subaru right beside her and tilted his head.

"Natsuki-san? What are you doing out here of all places?"

"Isn't it obvious? Moon bathing. What about you, what were you doing at this time of night? Depending on the answer, I might just hand you over to Garfiel."

"I don't know why you're assuming I did something wrong, but the reason I'm breaking a sweat at this hour isn't entirely unrelated to you, Natsuki-san."

Subaru bantered, trying to salvage some semblance of his usual composure at the sight of Otto. Otto shrugged at Subaru's words and shook his head as if to say good grief.

"Not unrelated to me?"

"It was making such a racket, so I went to check on the Earth Dragon stable, and Patrasche-chan was kicking up a fuss. I thought, maybe something's wrong, or maybe she'd built up stress from being cooped up for days, and I figured I'd unfasten her tether and take her for a light walk—and then, bam."

Otto punctuated this with a clap of his hands and glared at Patrasche's noble profile. But Patrasche paid him no mind whatsoever, only staring fixedly at Subaru.

"Completely off her radar, apparently—well, fine. Anyway, she shoved me aside and bolted out of the stable. I was seeing stars for a while myself, but then I genuinely panicked thinking how bad it'd look for me if she got loose outside, and that's how I ended up here."

"So finding her here with me is what put you at ease."

"Yes, exactly. Natsuki-san, did you give Patrasche-chan some kind of instruction?"

"No time for that. Other than going to feed her, I hadn't even shown my face..."

"Then she must've been worried about you. She tore out of there in that much of a hurry, after all."

"——"

At Otto's murmured word worried, Subaru started to object, but his throat closed up. A thought surged up—surely not—and seeking proof, Subaru checked the state of his own body. He found the traces at once.

On the right shoulder of his jacket, there was a faint dented mark of teeth and a trace of saliva. And the back of his body was thoroughly caked in dust and dirt, as if he'd been dragged.

"Patrasche..."

"——"

Her round eyes turned toward Subaru. As the Earth Dragon waited in silence for her master's words, Subaru caught his breath before speaking.

"Did you pull me out of the tomb?"

Of course, there was no way Patrasche could answer Subaru's question in words. But once he became conscious of the dirt on his own body, Subaru looked at the dragon and noticed several things like lacerations on her jet-black hide. Wounding an Earth Dragon's hide, covered as it was in hard scales, wasn't easy even with tools. And more than that, to Subaru's eyes, those wounds looked as if they'd arisen from inside her body.

He suddenly remembered. —That the tomb had a power that repelled those who lacked the qualification to undergo the Trials.

Roswaal's injuries, now recuperating in his sickbed, had originated from that very thing. If someone without the qualification set foot inside, the tomb bared its fangs at the intruder—in other words—

"You got hurt like that just to drag me out of there..."

"——"

"Why would you do something so reckless... I was fine, I'd have just woken up and walked out normally, and that would've been that. You didn't need to go and get yourself hurt rushing to pull me out like that..."

The lacerations carved into Patrasche's hide were sharp enough that the red flesh beneath the black skin was faintly visible, and the way they oozed blood was painful enough to look at that it made him want to wince. And that Patrasche had gone and dragged Subaru outside even at the cost of wounds like that—frankly speaking, it had been a pointless thing to do.

Unable to grasp the true meaning behind what Patrasche had done, Subaru lowered his eyes, and the dragon pressed her muzzle close to him. Against the neck of Subaru, who still sat there limply with his legs sprawled out, that hard, rough texture rubbed against him again and again. They couldn't exchange words, and even the sense of mutual understanding he'd occasionally thought they shared had, in truth, been one-sided—theirs was a relationship where he was constantly the one being looked after, in scene after scene.

"Otto."

"Wh-what is it? Given the mood just now, if I'm intruding I can make myself scarce somewhere else..."

"Ask Patrasche why she tried to save me... please."

Otto possessed the Divine Protection of the Soul of Language, which should let him converse with beings of different natures, like animals and insects. Naturally, he ought to be able to communicate with Patrasche too. What Patrasche had been thinking, to try and drag Subaru out even at the cost of injury—that fundamental question was one he couldn't stop worrying at now.

But at Subaru's request, Otto twisted his lips into an unwilling expression.

"Honestly, I'd rather not, Natsuki-san."

"Don't say that, please."

"Judging from what you just muttered to Patrasche-chan, you were inside that tomb, doing whatever this 'Trial' thing is, weren't you? I've had a vague suspicion since this afternoon and just now that you're qualified to undergo the 'Trial,' but given the state you're in, you must have failed, right?"

"...Yeah, that's right."

The impact of his exchange with the witches was strong enough that his memory of it was starting to blur, but in this loop Subaru hadn't told anyone that he'd already cleared the first Trial. He'd made an exception only for Garfiel, and had gone straight from there into the tomb for the second Trial—the witches' tea party. His current battered state wasn't only because of the Trial, but he couldn't think of a reason to correct Otto's misunderstanding, so Subaru just nodded along as he was questioned.

Otto's shoulders slumped at that, and he let out an exaggerated sigh as if utterly exasperated.

"I can more or less guess your reasons for doing something like that, but still, that was a foolish thing to do, Natsuki-san. And on top of getting a rough time of it in there, you end up worrying your beloved dragon like this. Patrasche-chan's instincts are sharp, so she must've sensed something was wrong with you. That's why she sent me flying and rushed over here... and I'd guess the wounds aren't unrelated either."

"——"

Otto had traced the same line of reasoning as Subaru and arrived at the same conclusion. Subaru had gotten that far too. The problem was the one point of why Patrasche had gone that far for him. That was what he wanted Otto to draw out of her, but—

"What's with that look? Don't tell me you were serious about what you just said?"

"Then the other way around—do I look like I'm in any state to be joking right now?"

"I feel like even beaten to a pulp, you'd have the nerve to crack a lame joke, and honestly, in this situation I'd almost rather be told it was a joke. ...Do you really not get it?"

Asked in a low voice, Subaru found himself overwhelmed by Otto's gaze before he could even object. Otto was looking down at him with the eyes of someone seeing something unbelievable—to put it plainly, the eyes you'd give a fool. Was there some grand oversight on Subaru's part?

But shifting where he sat, Subaru had no idea what it could be, and only furrowed his brow in confusion. His impatience made sweat bead on his forehead, but no thoughts came to him. Seeing this, Otto let out a second sigh.

"My Divine Protection isn't as all-powerful as you seem to think, Natsuki-san. Being able to communicate doesn't mean it gets translated. It comes through clearly to me, but trying to convey it to someone else through me is difficult—it's a matter of nuance."

"——"

"And that's the look that says do it anyway, huh. Fine, I will... though I wonder if there's even any point to this."

Grumbling his displeasure the whole while, Otto reluctantly did as Subaru asked. Walking up to Patrasche, who still had her muzzle pressed against Subaru, Otto gently stroked her black back and—

『——』

From Otto's open throat came a high, rasping sound. Something impossible as human speech—a call transformed by the working of the Divine Protection of the Soul of Language into the form best suited for communicating with an Earth Dragon.

Raising her head, Patrasche answered in kind with a whinny of her own. Hearing it, Otto opened his mouth again, and after several exchanges of whinnying back and forth—

"That's it, but... hmm, it really is hard to find the right way to put it into words. Her way of expressing emotion is different from a human's to begin with, so how do I explain something only I understand..."

"Don't drag it out. Please, just tell me."

"I'm not dragging it out... ahh, this is really troublesome! I mean, conveying this requires a weirdly delicate touch, you know."

Scratching his head, Otto looked up several times as if pondering, then lowered his head again to think it over, repeating the cycle until Subaru, still seated, started bouncing his leg impatiently—and finally Otto let out a breath.

"Well. All right. I've picked out the words that are probably the closest match."

"Yeah... what did Patrasche say?"

"Um, well, I think it's something like, 'Don't make me say something like that.'"

"—Huh?"

Scratching his cheek in embarrassment, Otto's words made Subaru's eyes go wide. He waited to see if more words would follow, but nothing more seemed forthcoming. To dumbfounded Subaru, Otto added, "I mean—"

"Patrasche-chan is saying, 'Don't make me say something like that.' Well, that's about what I figured, honestly."

"Don't make her say that... what does that even..."

"What does it mean, indeed—it's exactly what it sounds like. If I can add my own take: 'Do you really need to be told a thing like this?' is roughly the gist."

To Subaru, whose confusion only deepened, Otto held up a finger and said, "Look, all right?" before continuing.

"The moment she realized you were in danger, she couldn't just sit still—she rushed out, didn't hesitate to hurt herself lending you a hand, stayed by your side the whole time until you woke up, and the moment she saw you open your eyes, she was so relieved she practically smiled. How you'd feel about someone who does something like that for you—I don't think that's any different whether you're human or an Earth Dragon."

"Ah—"

"Of course even Patrasche-chan would think 'don't make me say it out loud.' Shown that plainly through her actions and still not noticing—isn't that past dense and into something else entirely? Lucky you."

Hearing Otto's exasperated answer, Subaru became fully aware of his own thickheadedness. Then, looking at Patrasche right beside him, he found the dragon gazing back at him with the same gentle eyes as always—and as if she'd noticed the change in his heart, she gave her long tail a shake and rose to her feet.

"——"

As Patrasche pressed her muzzle close again, Subaru's hand moved naturally to stroke her. Running his palm over her hard, rock-like hide, Subaru spoke in a trembling voice.

"So... that's how it is, huh. You... you like me?"

"——"

"You care about me, huh. I see."

There was a sensation like something lodged in his chest finally settling and dropping away. At Subaru's words, Patrasche whinnied and, as if hiding her own embarrassment, rubbed her muzzle fiercely against his palm like sandpaper. Wincing at the sensation of his skin being scraped, Subaru tried to speak—

"Oh, ah..."

"Natsuki-san?"

A hot droplet suddenly traced down Subaru's cheek. Tears. Something he hadn't even been aware of had welled up all at once and spilled over. He hurriedly raised a hand to wipe it away, but trying to hide it now was too late. Otto had already seen.

"Crying because you've realized an Earth Dragon is fond of you, Natsuki-san..."

"That's not... that's not it, it's just... the timing just happened to line up in the worst way... damn it, right when I was already kind of short on any sense of that, an answer just came flying in out of nowhere, so I wasn't prepared for it..."

The turn of events was almost unfairly, cruelly well-timed, and Subaru desperately fought down the feelings still welling up inside him.

At the witches' tea party, Subaru had come to acknowledge his own true feeling of I don't want to die. Just as strong as his desire to protect the people precious to him was his desire to remain together with them. And he had only just vowed to go on confirming, step by step, whether he himself was worth being missed by those precious people—a feeling difficult to be sure of.

And into that came Patrasche's unconditional loyalty and devotion. Confronted with something like this before he'd even taken action himself, what was he supposed to do?

The very question Subaru had been agonizing over, holding his head in his hands over what he should do—Patrasche had brought him the answer, right along with his waking.

At least in Patrasche's eyes, Subaru was someone significant enough an influence that, upon realizing he was suffering through a nightmare, she'd thought she wanted to drag him out of it even at the cost of hurting herself.

"Never thought you'd be the first one to teach me that. —Thanks, Patrasche."

As if answering the loyalty and devotion shown to him, Subaru too stroked Patrasche with feeling. Savoring the touch of his palm, Patrasche stood resolutely with her back straight. Though the swaying of her tail subtly betrayed just how pleased she was.

"Now that you and Patrasche-chan have reaffirmed your bond, are you all right, Natsuki-san?"

"Yeah, you saved me, thanks. ...All right in what sense?"

"Your body, your head, obviously. That 'Trial' inside there is pretty rough on you, isn't it? You're crying from missing human contact and all—Emilia-sama was in a similar state too, you know."

Subaru wanted to object to the teasing remark, but since Otto undeniably had leverage on him now, he chose not to touch on that part. Instead, thinking of the state Otto had described Emilia in, lowering his voice—

"It's not exactly easy, no. But it's probably better that it's me going through it than Emilia. Still, even you're worried about me... could it be you like me too?"

"Could you please not say something so unsettling!? Honestly, there's a limit to how starved for human contact you can be. Don't tell me you're planning to go around asking everyone you meet that, not satisfied with just Patrasche-chan?"

"Would that be so bad? Honestly, right now I'm on the fence about whether I can even affirm myself, so I could use as many words of encouragement as I can get."

"Right, right, glad to see your usual mouth is back in working order. ...Just don't get the wrong idea—my concern for you, Natsuki-san, is purely out of consideration for our future working relationship."

Making a sour face at Subaru's threat of further oddball behavior, Otto held both hands up toward him as he said this. "Consideration for our relationship" was a needlessly pompous way of putting it, but for someone wanting to make his position as a merchant clear, it was probably a necessary thing to say.

"The reason I keep you company, Natsuki-san, is because I want to maintain good relations with Margrave Mathers. If that becomes a problem, or if it puts me in any personal danger, I'll be hightailing it out of there without a second thought. Just keep that in mind."

It was a coldhearted thing to say, coldhearted as such things went, but it was less severity than something that ought to have been an unspoken understanding between them, put bluntly into words. That he'd go and say it outright regardless was, in its own way, just further proof of Otto's good nature—though that was nothing new at this point.

"Yeah, that's fair. You're... no, wait."

Hearing Otto's realist declaration, Subaru started to nod along, then stopped. Something about the preface to his words just now had brushed against a nagging discomfort in his chest. The answer came to him at once, and Subaru let out a breath—"Ha."

"...What is it?"

"Nah, I just remembered something. Ah, right, right, that's how it was."

Nodding repeatedly at Otto's dubious expression, Subaru put a hand to his head and looked up at the sky. In the loop that had begun here in the 'Sanctuary,' Subaru had acted alongside Otto again and again. And each time, Subaru had seen it for himself. So, looking at Otto, he said—

"If your own life's on the line, you'll go hightailing it out of there, huh."

"Yes, of course. Naturally. I've got no obligation to be that loyal to you or anyone else, Natsuki-san. Better to live and run than to—"

"You're not going to run."

"—Huh?"

Subaru murmured this to Otto, who was trying to play the carefree realist with an all-too-light tone. Then, to the wide-eyed Otto, Subaru turned to face him head-on and said—

"—You won't leave me behind and run, Otto."

He remembered the time Otto had snuck into Garfiel's hideout to help him, even though Garfiel had shown he wouldn't hesitate to use violence. And the time Otto had joined the villagers in fighting to hold the line, to protect Subaru from the threat of the beastified Garfiel.

Even playing the villain, lining up cold, callous words—Subaru knew the real Otto wasn't like that. So—

"Otto. —Because you're my friend."
