Arc 3 Chapter 47: "At the End of Disgrace"

――. ――――. ――――――――――――――Ah.

There was a shaking, an impact, the sensation of his face hitting something. It shook his consciousness awake from the blank void, rousing Subaru back to reality.

He tried to lift his head and raise his upper body, but the violent jolting thwarted him. His hand slipped, and just before his head could slam into the floor again, the mass supporting his midsection caught him. A heavy, hard feeling pressed against his abdomen. Reaching out, he confirmed it was a bag stuffed with gold coins—and his memory rushed back, recalling the moments just before he lost consciousness.

"—Where's Rem!?"

"Natsuki-san!? Are you awake!?"

Tossing aside the bag of gold coins he had been clutching, Subaru scrambled onto all fours and looked around frantically. The darkness was as thick as ever, and the violent swaying left no doubt that they were still atop the Dragon Carriage. In front of him, responding to Subaru's sudden cry, Otto turned his head back. Seeing Subaru shake his head and try to crawl forward, Otto spoke:

"Don't move! You hit your head, and the Divine Protection has worn off! The Earth Dragon is running at full sprint—there's no room to worry about your comfort!"

"I don't give a damn about that! What about Rem!? What happened to Rem!?"

Bellowing back at Otto, Subaru swept his eyes over every corner of the driver's bench. The carriage had lost the cargo bed it was pulling, leaving only the driver's bench in a bizarrely unbalanced state. Space for passengers was so limited that he didn't even need to look frantically to realize she wasn't here.

Even so, until he confirmed the truth with his own eyes, he couldn't bring himself to accept it.

"Answer me, Otto. What happened to Rem...!"

"That young lady..."

Seeing Subaru so agitated that he looked ready to leap at him, Otto must have realized the danger of keeping silent. Gasping, he made an agonizing decision to utter the next words:

"To let our carriage escape... she got off to intercept the White Whale."

He stated the reality: the exchange from right before Subaru lost consciousness had been carried out.

"――――"

Subaru gasped once at those words, and then:

"Turn back."

"Huh?"

"I said turn back! We have to save Rem... save Rem! Turn back right now!"

In the cramped driver's bench, Subaru closed the distance even further and grabbed Otto. Otto, who had his hands full trying to control the completely panicked Earth Dragon, couldn't react to Subaru's violent movement. Clutched by the collar, he flusteredly cried, "Wait a—!"

"A-Are you insane!? Turn back... what do you think will happen if we turn back!? Didn't you see how terrifying the White Whale—that monster—is!? It's suicide!"

"I'm telling you to turn back to save Rem because I saw that monster up close, damn it!"

Veins bulging on his forehead, Subaru lashed out at Otto, who was shaking his head in refusal. The threat of the White Whale was burned into Subaru's eyes. Swimming faster than a Dragon Carriage despite its colossal size, blowing away a heavy vehicle with a single sweep of its tail. On top of that, easily tracking their position through the fog, taking almost no damage from Rem's magic.

Without a doubt, it was the greatest enemy he had faced since falling into this parallel world. Compared to that thing, dealing with Elsa—who was human-shaped—or taking on a horde of countless Juggernauts would yield far clearer strategies. But against that monster, he couldn't picture a vision of victory at all.

"That's exactly why I can't just leave Rem here...!"

Subaru knew full well how strong Rem was in her Oni form. And that understanding told him: even with all her strength, before the White Whale, she was nothing more than a helpless, weak prey.

If left behind, she would undoubtedly be lost. There was no meaning in that. There was no point in Subaru surviving if that happened. Subaru had returned to save her. To rescue her. Because in the future Subaru desired, her presence was indispensable.

Without Rem, Subaru would lose sight of even himself. He wouldn't be able to validate himself anymore. Subaru needed her—needed someone who would affirm his existence.

"Like I'd let her sacrifice herself to buy time! If we run, we all run! Otto, turn back! Or else..."

"Are you an idiot!?"

However, Subaru's voice was blown away by an even louder roar of anger. The hand clutching Otto's collar was grabbed in a reverse grip as Otto's hand slid up; a moment later, Subaru's feet left the floor and his back slammed against the driver's bench.

"Gah...!"

"If you think you can force a traveling merchant—who sometimes has to navigate the highway alone—to listen through brute force when your Divine Protection has run out, you are sorely mistaken!"

Twisting the wrist he still held, Otto rolled Subaru onto his stomach and locked his arm. It was done by Otto's right arm, even as he kept holding the reins with his other hand. As Subaru groaned from the unexpected counterattack, Otto panted heavily:

"Understand this! That is your current state! Returning like that, what do you think you can accomplish!? Are you going to waste that girl's feelings—the feelings of the girl who stayed behind for us!?"

"Don't you dare talk about Rem! Someone who's abandoning Rem... letting her die... what right do you have to speak about her!? Turn back! Save Rem right now!!"

"Argh! You're impossible to talk to!"

Otto clicked his tongue at Subaru, who was squirming frantically in an attempt to break the joint lock. Keeping his eyes fixed ahead, he kept the Earth Dragon sprinting straight while continuing:

"Even after seeing how terrifying the White Whale is, do you still not understand!? It has reigned over the world for decades, and there have been countless campaigns to kill it! The fact that it still hasn't been killed is proof of its strength!"

Lashing out at the stubborn Subaru from above, Otto shouted "Do you understand!?", his voice laced with intense fury and helpless frustration.

"Hundreds of armed people have challenged it and failed to kill it! What good will it do if we, who have no weapons and no strength to fight, go there!? Rescue the girl standing in front of it!? We can't even do that! There's no way we could!"

"Before even trying...!"

"Some things are obvious from the start! Against a monster that killed the subjugation force dispatched by the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica... that even killed the previous Sword Saint... there's no way anyone could win...!"

The squeezed-out confession carried a tremor, as if he were holding back tears. Otto himself harbored an indelible rage toward that creature. But even so, the opponent was simply too grand for that anger to be directed against. In order to teach the stubborn Subaru this truth, Otto was forced to utter words that forced him to realize the sheer scale of their enemy, repeatedly crushing his own spirit in the process.

"The Sword... Saint...?"

Hearing that, Subaru, who had refused to listen to a single word until now, suddenly deflated. From his dazed lips slipped a single word from Otto's outburst.

"Sword Saint"—the title granted to the strongest person Subaru had encountered since falling into this parallel world, the very symbol of strength to him.

Even if the previous Sword Saint was not the same person as Reinhard, if they possessed enough power to bear that same title...

"The White Whale that killed them is..."

...Does that mean it's even stronger than Reinhard?

Subaru felt terror rapidly take hold of his heart. Reinhard's existence, his strength, was the very embodiment of "strength" that Subaru couldn't help but wish he possessed. If even Reinhard would have no choice but to kneel and lose his life before that colossal, monstrous form—then...

"How is that... even possible... Why... why like this..."

He wanted to save Rem, wanted to rescue her. If he didn't return right now, that wouldn't come true. Though his mind understood this, his crucial fighting spirit failed to reach his limbs, his soul.

Looking at the collapsing Subaru with pitying eyes, Otto spoke in a quiet voice:

"I am weak, and you are weak. That's why we can't save that girl. —We can't catch up to her strength."

—But Rem isn't strong either.

Subaru should have known that. He did know that, yet he couldn't utter a word in argument. With his head hanging low, he yielded to the swaying of the carriage while the Earth Dragon sprinted straight through the darkness.

Rem, whom he had left behind, grew further away even now. Without doing a thing to stop it, Subaru moved further away from her. Kept moving further away.

Five minutes, ten minutes—it was around the time that much had passed since Subaru had hung his head.

"Natsuki-san, what is that...?"

Otto, who had been driving the Earth Dragon in silence until now, called out to Subaru while squinting at something ahead. Slowly raising his head, Subaru crawled up beside Otto and turned his gaze ahead as well. —There, dimly blending into the darkness, a light was visible.

"It's muffled by the fog... but that's light from people!"

"Are we... getting out of the fog...?"

"Even if we did, it should be night outside, so a light would be unnatural... They're probably like us, someone caught in the fog..."

As if corroborating Otto's guess, the other party, having likely noticed them as well, came charging straight toward them. After a short moment, the vehicle pulling alongside them was indeed another Dragon Carriage, and sitting on the driver's bench yelling out to them was—

"Finally... finally, other people...! Hey, this is fog, right!? Which means... the White Whale... the W-White Whale showed up...!"

A middle-aged man screamed, frothing at the mouth, in a state of literal panic. Caught in the night fog, he pulled his carriage alongside theirs as if finding the faintest glimmer of hope, desperately seeking an answer.

Even knowing that the man's voice carried a plea for salvation—hoping he was mistaken—Otto still shook his head and said:

"Yes, it's the White Whale. We've already encountered it. Fortunately, we seem to have escaped for now, but until we get out of the fog, there's no telling when or where we might run into it again."

"I-Is that true...!? Ah, damn it, this is the worst! Why... why is this happening...?"

Watching the man hold his head in despair, Subaru glared at Otto's profile beside him. Because Otto saying "fortunately" made it feel as though he had completely forgotten the fact that they had abandoned Rem.

Unaware of whether his anger was justified or merely a means to distract himself from his guilt, Subaru called out to Otto, "Hey..."

"What is it, Natsuki-san?"

"Don't talk like that, Otto. Saying 'fortunately'... What do you think Rem was feeling when she..."

Every time he spoke of Rem, whom they had left behind, a pain like thorns tightening around his heart shot through him. The hopeful thought that she had stayed behind and was using her wits to survive against the White Whale—he couldn't even summon such optimism after seeing that terror.

At this point, even Subaru had to admit it. Rem had remained in that place, and once again, to save them—to save Subaru—she had died—

"Who's Rem?"

Her tragic resolve and devotion—

"—Huh?"

"No, I mean, who is Rem? None of the other merchants who got scattered had a name like that... Who are you talking about?"

Otto tilted his head, unable to grasp the intent behind Subaru's words. That gesture clearly seemed like an act of trampling upon Rem's memory—

—and Subaru slammed his raised fist full-force right into the side of Otto's face.

Instantly, a violent jolt rocked the Dragon Carriage, and Subaru, losing his balance, fell backward onto the driver's bench. Otto, knocked sideways onto the bench as well, held his face, quickly scrambling up to glare at the fallen Subaru.

"W-What are you doing!?"

Otto stared wide-eyed at Subaru's violent outburst in utter disbelief. But Subaru found Otto's words and actions just as unbelievable. Pushing his fallen body back up, Subaru bit his lip so hard it bled, snarling, "Don't play with me..."

"What the hell are you talking about...!? Rem stayed behind so we could escape, and you ask 'who's that'!? Don't play dumb! You bastard... do you want to die...!?"

"I'm telling you I have no idea what you're talking about! What's wrong with you, suddenly blurting out weird stuff... Did seeing the White Whale make you lose your mind!?"

Otto continued to plead complete ignorance in the face of Subaru's relentless accusations. Subaru's vision dyed crimson with uncontrollable fury. Time felt agonizingly slow, and a surging bloodlust commanded Subaru to snap the thin neck of the man before him. Reaching out his hands, intent on forgetting everything and crushing the ungrateful bastard's life—

"W-What are you two doing!? Now's not the time to be fighting! If you've got time to bicker, just focus on getting out of the fog—"

Seeing the two shouting at each other in an atmosphere so dangerous it threatened to escalate into murder, the man in the parallel carriage let out a flustered shout. His desperate calls to stop them had no chance of reaching the glaring pair. Tuning out the man's voice completely, Subaru was about to turn his hostility toward Otto into physical violence.

What stopped him at the last second was, once again, action from the neighboring carriage. Not that the man's earnest persuasion had prompted a reconciliation between them, however.

"Get out of the fog and escape the White Whale—"

The man, who was voicing a realistic escape plan, had his body thrown directly into the maw of the White Whale, which swallowed the carriage along with the ground from behind. Tilting its mouth skyward after swallowing the carriage and its pulling Earth Dragon whole, a grand sound of destruction and a death agony echoed from the chewing monster's jaws. Wood and iron were crunched to pieces, and the Earth Dragon screamed as its flesh was ground down by millstone-like teeth. Muffled beneath the noise of destruction, the man's crushed voice failed to carry, vanishing fleetingly before reality.

"Wha... ah—"

Subaru and Otto were rendered speechless by the silent approach and crushing destruction of that colossal body. Otto's eyes trembled before the majestic, terrifying sight of the approaching White Whale once again, while Subaru—

"Why... are you... here..."

Muttering in a trembling voice, Subaru dropped to his knees on the floor as he stared at the White Whale's flank. The White Whale, unscathed, smacked its lips in delight at the feast spreading in its mouth, showing not a shred of interest in the despair of the two tiny beings beside it.

"If you're here... then..."

What had become of the girl who had stayed behind in that place to draw away this colossal body? Faced with that overwhelming existence, he couldn't help but ask a question whose answer was already plain.

Of course, the aberration before him would never answer Subaru's question. Slowly swallowing the prey in its mouth down to its stomach, the White Whale looked down at the running carriage—at Subaru—with its massive eyes, as if assessing its next victim.

"U-Aaaaaaaaahhhhh—!!"

Unable to withstand the pressure, Otto was the first to lose his mental balance and shriek. The Earth Dragon also understood the presence of the White Whale, accelerating even further in accordance with its master's command on the reins. Pulling away for just a moment, terrified of the presence of overwhelming mass pursuing from behind—

"Why why why why why... why is it chasing us so relentlessly... We should have... pulled away...!"

Clinging to the accelerating carriage, the limp Subaru let Otto's words wash over him. As if listening to Otto's screams and lamentations allowed him to turn his eyes away from his own despair.

"Why only us...!? In the dark like this... why...! Is there... is there some kind of target on us or something...!?"

Otto's weeping cries, rejecting the death drawing near, pierced Subaru's chest. But in those cries, Subaru felt an image form in his mind that he couldn't ignore. The White Whale relentlessly chasing after them. The reality that the monster approached as if they had a target on them. That meant—

"It can't be..."

Tumbling to the back of the driver's bench, Subaru squinted toward the White Whale swimming behind them. The darkness was deep; the pitch-black falling over the world hazily obscured even its colossal form. Yet even in that darkness, he could dimly peer at the monster's front—its huge mouth agape as it closed in on them.

Along with the existence of the twisted, misshapen horn protruding from the White Whale's head.

—The Juggernauts that had swarmed in the Witchbeasts' forest had looked like large wild dogs with horns growing from them. And Witchbeasts, created by the power of the Witch, had the habit of being drawn to the scent of the Witch emitted by Subaru, attacking him as a result. In other words—

"Is that monster... is the White Whale a Witchbeast too...?"

Voicing that unbelievable possibility, Subaru shook his head at the reality he couldn't accept. Yet thinking of it that way made everything fit together. The reason it had singled out their Dragon Carriage first among the many scattered carriages. The reason it had relentlessly targeted this carriage even after he jumped onto Otto's carriage. And the reason it had caught up to this carriage despite Rem dropping to the ground, prepared for death, to buy them time.

The existence of the White Whale chasing them through the dark. Even Rem's hesitant attitude as she chased after them, looking as though she had realized something.

"Is it... because of me...?"

Because Subaru was here. Following the scent of the Witch clinging to his body, was the White Whale relentlessly targeting this carriage? Had Rem realized that truth before anyone else, dropping off the carriage of her own accord to protect Subaru? To protect Subaru. For Subaru's sake.

"No way... Rem... I... I didn't... because of me...!"

Under the crushing weight of grief and pressure surging over him, Subaru covered his face and crouched down. The fact that he had lost Rem—the fact that he had caused her loss—assailed Subaru simultaneously from inside and outside his body. As Subaru lay crushed under that despair—

"Natsuki-san..."

Approaching from behind, Otto called out in a quiet voice and touched his shoulder. His fingertips trembled, frail as if he had no idea what to say. His hand weakly gripped Subaru's shoulder, and—

"Otto..."

"Please die."

The next instant, Subaru's body was shoved forward with force, losing support and being thrown headfirst off the carriage.

—Huh?

His vision flipped upside down, his thrown body spinning wildly as he lost all sense of up and down. In that world flipped completely upside down, he saw Otto stretching a hand out toward him, laughing hysterically. He opened his mouth so wide his white teeth showed, drool dripping—

"Y-You, you, it's your fault! If it's chasing us because of y-you, take r-responsibility! Ahaha! Die, please die! Die and save meeee!"

Seeing Otto frantic and laughing, Subaru realized his mind had snapped. Cornered so far that upon hearing Subaru's weak murmur, he clung to it as an excuse to push him off.

The moment he reached that realization, Subaru's body struck the ground. Slamming back-first into the earth without mercy, all the air in his lungs was squeezed out without exaggeration. Broken bones crunched beneath his groans of pain, and as his body kept rolling, the repeated impacts compounded his injuries, forcing him to vomit blood onto the grass.

Even the fact that he landed on soft, grassy ground brought no comfort before such an impact. Blood streamed from his forehead where it had scraped against the dirt, the agony of his dislocated left shoulder choked back his cries, and splintered ribs pressed into his abdomen from within. As he opened his mouth to give in to the surging nausea, shattered molars were spewed onto the grass along with overflowing blood.

An impact so severe it robbed him of even the ability to register pain. Retching heavily again and again, Subaru slowly raised his head, vaguely watching the light of the Dragon Carriage that had thrown him off flee into the far distance.

Strangely, no resentment welled up inside him. The reality was that the pain and suffering left him no room for it, but besides that, there was a strange feeling that prevented him from blaming Otto. Or perhaps somewhere in his heart, he had already forgiven Otto, recognizing that Otto was merely a bystander caught up in this, doing what he had to do to survive.

"—Gah... cough, wheeze..."

Such sentimentality was wiped away by the taste of blood filling his mouth, the excruciating pain rending his entire body as if suddenly remembering to hurt, and—

"――――!"

—was blown away before the impossibly colossal presence that appeared right in front of where Subaru had fallen.

—It was overwhelmingly massive, a monstrous form that forcefully impressed upon him the folly of resistance. Approaching right before the fallen Subaru, close enough that he could reach out and touch it, the White Whale exhaled a foul-smelling breath from its oversized mouth, inspecting Subaru's existence.

To a diminutive human, a mere breath from the White Whale was equivalent to a gale. Let alone supporting himself with his injured body, a single exhalation tumbled him across the ground, causing Subaru's throat to burst with screams as broken bones twisted painfully inside him.

Looking down at Subaru writhing in agony, the White Whale stood still as if toying with him. Placidly holding an unshakable, overwhelming superiority, no one could call its composure negligence. Their caliber as living beings was entirely different. It was truly like an ant challenging an elephant. Like a human challenging a whale—and in the middle of the ocean, no less. When that human was already battered and bloodied, the very notion of a fight was absurd.

—I'm going to die. I'm going to be killed.

Amid sensations dominated by pain and nausea, Subaru perceived what drew near. The feeling of despair he had tasted countless times before. The disappointment of losing himself. Above all, the helplessness toward his own inability to accomplish anything once again midway through. Those feelings cosied up to Subaru familiarly, throwing an arm around his shoulders intimately, sneering at his pathetic display this time.

What had gone wrong? He couldn't understand anything anymore. Only that now, having lost Rem, nothing remained in Subaru's hands. Even his pathetic attempt to survive felt like a foolish resistance.

A trivial, boring, worthless way of living that accomplished nothing.

He felt the tip of the White Whale's snout drawing near before his eyes. Inside its gaping mouth, powerful teeth that easily crushed even the hard-scaled Earth Dragon prepared to grind, chew, and devour Subaru's flesh, bones, and soul.

He tried to spit out sour grapes—telling it to just kill him already, to hurry up and sink its fangs into him—

"I don't... want to die..."

Despairing at his own weakness that couldn't even manage that, Subaru lost hope once and for all. An unprecedented sense of powerlessness sent something ice-cold rushing inside his chest. Amid a disappointment so intense his blood froze solid and his vision turned pitch black—

"No... I don't want to die... Help... I don't want to die! I don't want to die, don't want to die, don't want to die... No no no nooo... Help me, Rem, save me..."

Whimpers, weak complaints, an unstoppable, filthy attachment to life spilled from his mouth. Clinging to the life already lost, the life he had caused to be lost, the weakling who could do nothing, the loser who accomplished nothing, the scum who could protect nothing screamed out that he still held his own life dear.

It was pitiful. Tragic. This exact appearance was what it meant to be a disgraceful display. A sight everyone would turn away from in absurdity, hurl insults at, and slander as painful to look at. Humans could never consider clinging to life to such an extent to be beautiful.

Miserable. Even a bug's way of life had more charm to it. So desperate to save himself that he desecrated the dignity of noble, proud existences—it was truly the very definition of "a pig's desire."

"No... I don't... want to die... help..."

Still he crawled, still he fled frantically, slyly weeping in pursuit of any remaining chance at survival. Before he knew it, his powerless body stopped moving forward; his fingertips merely brushed against the grass, lacking even the strength to scrape the soil. The energy to wail had long been exhausted; rolling onto his side took the last of his strength.

"I don't... want to die..."

And so, a plea for his life leaked from the mouth of Subaru, who had rolled onto his back. That was his final resistance. He couldn't do anything anymore. He couldn't think anything. He couldn't cause anything to happen, left with no choice but to let whatever would happen happen.

And yet, no matter how long he waited, the impact never came. The familiar presence of death, the terrifying future of being crushed between teeth, failed to arrive.

The fear of waiting for an end with no known conclusion easily shatters a human mind. Writhing in a horror too great to bear, Subaru struggled with his trembling body, casting his gaze about in search of that despair, and—

"...Eh?"

—He realized that the White Whale, which should have been right in front of him, was nowhere to be found.

※※※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※

From then on, Subaru ran solely on his sheer attachment to life.

"I don't want to die... I don't want to die, don't want to die..."

Out of breath, his legs unsteady, dripping blood entered his eyes and blurred his vision. But Subaru ran without care even for that. In the first place, his field of vision was lost in the depth of the dark and fog; he couldn't see anything. He didn't even register that blood had gotten into his eyes.

Cradled in a night where neither stars nor moon could be seen, Subaru couldn't even see his own limbs. Though he ran stepping heavily on the earth, he couldn't tell if he was truly running on the ground. Or perhaps, without realizing it, he had long been swallowed whole by the White Whale, and was now merely advancing inside its stomach toward his own demise.

"Hic..."

Chilled to the bone by the dangerous imagination crossing his mind, Subaru ran weeping. Whether the flowing tears were due to pain or despair, he didn't even know anymore.

In the darkness, Subaru was utterly alone.

Losing Rem, abandoned by Otto, and even the White Whale leaving him behind. To someone who had lost all value of existence, no one would bother with such a being.

"I don't want to die."

He didn't even know why he felt that way anymore. What meaning was there in living? What came from not dying?

He couldn't save anyone, couldn't protect anyone. He could do nothing, couldn't possibly do anything. What meaning could there possibly be in such a man living?

No answer came. He understood nothing. It was a proposition so vague he didn't even know if an answer existed. Would a day ever come when that answer appeared? And if it did, would it be him who grasped it?

Was this rambling line of thought a work of self-defense to distract from pain and terror? Or was it horrifying that even in a moment like this, his mind continued to exhibit self-preservation?

Still he ran. Kept escaping. That existence that had left him behind, that formidable Witchbeast that swam so long as fog remained—there was no telling when it might catch Subaru's scent again and reappear.

Though perhaps even a terror like that was too good for the current Subaru to hold.

"—Ah?"

Around the time he had exhausted every self-deprecating thought and could no longer think of words to curse himself with, it happened abruptly.

—Passing through the edge of the fog, moonlight suddenly broke into his view.

At the sudden end of a darkness he had thought endless, Subaru's body kept running, unable to fully believe it. As if doubting that if he didn't keep running, this soft moonlight would disappear.

After running for a while, Subaru finally recognized with clarity that this wasn't a convenient delusion of his own making. Upon recognizing it, the sensation of blood circulating returned to his limbs, and the reality that he had survived soaked into his brain.

Yet what welled up was not joy at having grasped life, but—

"Once again... I..."

...only disappointment in himself for having grasped life at the end of struggling pathetically once again. Though the life he had craved so desperately was in his hands, it brought him no emotion now. On the contrary, an unbearable guilt surged inside his chest, and feelings of shame he had forgotten even existed closed in as if to burn his entire body to ashes.

"Rem... Rem...!"

Covering his face, he spilled unbearable hot tears endlessly. Even knowing he possessed neither the right nor the worth, Subaru had no choice but to call her name, attempting to comfort his own soul.

How much time had passed while he crouched there, crying endlessly? Making a slow creaking sound, it came toward where Subaru squatted.

"Y-You're..."

It was a Dragon Carriage and Earth Dragon approaching unsteadily, pulling a vehicle covered in blood that had lost its original shape. It was unmistakably the Earth Dragon Otto had owned. However, atop the driver's bench pulled by that Earth Dragon, the young man who had pushed Subaru off was nowhere to be seen.

"Why are you... Where is he... Where's Otto?"

The question left his mouth, but naturally, there were no words to answer it. Staggering, Subaru stood up and walked toward the approaching carriage, looking up at the tragically damaged vehicle—and noticed.

—The multiple daggers shaped like crucifixes plunged around the driver's bench, and the traces of blood.

After emerging from the fog, he had been attacked. Subaru couldn't even imagine how Otto, who had been so frantic and barely managed to escape with his life, had been in the face of the despair that assailed him afterward. Yet what had happened was clear just by looking at this Earth Dragon moving on its own.

"...Let's go."

Muttering quietly, Subaru lifted his aching body and climbed onto the driver's bench. Kicking off the embedded blades with his foot, he grabbed the torn reins with his working right hand, shaking them in imitation to give instructions to the Earth Dragon.

Unfamiliar with the feel of the reins being shaken compared to its master's, the Earth Dragon looked up at Subaru with round, puzzled eyes. Yet, whatever it felt seeing him shake the reins over and over, the Earth Dragon slowly began stepping forward along the highway.

—Beneath the silvery moonlit glow, the Dragon Carriage slowly began to run.

Companions who had both lost precious existences, paired together as if licking each other's wounds, slowly, slowly moving forward under the mockery of the stars.

Slowly, slowly, the carriage kept running.

Kept running.
