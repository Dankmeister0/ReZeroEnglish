Arc 10 Chapter 24: "The Best Person"

Subaru Natsuki, who had leaped out of the window, ran toward the scene of the accident that had occurred at the rear of the carriage line. 　There was no hesitation in his stride—it could even be called dashing. —It was the back of a man who believed it to be only natural to save someone whenever their life was threatened.

"Folly."

Russell Fellow could only watch with disbelief in his eyes as his black-haired companion shook off his warnings and made a decision with utter disregard for the consequences. 　Partly, it was because he could not fathom his companion's way of thinking. He also felt a sense of pride in having thoroughly lectured him on the importance of their actions and taught him what priority meant. 　But even more than that—

"—'White Sheep,' why didn't you stop him!"

Russell demanded to know the true intent of 'White Sheep,' whom he had instructed to win him over in order to advance the situation rationally. 　'White Sheep' had indeed placed her hand over his companion's. She had spoken words to hold him back. Nevertheless, his companion did not comply—no, he had not been swayed.

"You must understand as well. What takes top priority right now. Not a single second of our time should be spent on anything other than that. And yet—"

"—ried to."

"What?"

Frowning at the choked, barely audible voice, Russell looked at 'White Sheep.' 　Facing the window through which his companion had leaped out, 'White Sheep' pulled the hand that had been shaken off against her chest and turned back. —Large teardrops pooled in her round, golden eyes.

"I... I did it. I tried with Subaru-san, properly... But, but he ran out anyway... That's just the kind of person he is!"

"The kind of...?"

"I must have told 'Silver Fox' as well! All I can do is stir up feelings that are already there... I can't make someone do something they would absolutely never do!"

"――――"

"L-Let's go after Subaru-san and the others right away! We should help the injured too!"

As 'White Sheep' appealed to him with tearful eyes and a choked voice, Russell placed a hand over his mouth. 　Behind his narrowed eyes, his Divine Protection—which assessed the 'value' of all things in this world—dismantled the various schemes he had constructed, rearranging and reweaving them. 　He could not see the correct answer itself. —He could only make the choice closest to it.

"Well, well, looks like things are in high spirits here, huh? If you're gonna squabble anyway, use your fists instead of your mouths. Hell, make it a fight to the death, not an argument! Kuhahaha!"

"'Black Dog'..."

"Oh? What's with that look on your mug? Don't tell me you figured out how to sway people with tears instead of skin-to-skin contact? That only works on guys, you know. And I'm an exception."

Spitting those words out as he boldly boarded the dragon carriage was 'Black Dog'—a man who had shed his black robes and mask, his rugged, intrepid face now set above a black suit, twisted into a smirk. 　'Black Dog' shrugged at Russell and 'White Sheep' standing opposite him, sat down messily on a nearby seat, and said:

"So I was wondering what you two were dawdling and whining about, and it turns out you got your hands bitten by a kid like that! After I went to the trouble of making a way out for you, don't throw away a guy's hard work."

"Making a way out? ...You couldn't mean..."

"Hey, stop that, what's with that look? All I did was break one axle! Making a huge scene out of it was the panicked earth dragon's doing! Every single bit of that was done for your sake. —Kuhahaha, just kidding."

"—! 'Silver Fox'!"

'White Sheep' averted her gaze from 'Black Dog,' who was crossing his legs on the seat with a villainous grin, and turned her eyes, burning with righteous indignation, toward Russell. 　Receiving that earnest anger, Russell lowered the hand resting against his mouth and said,

"—Let us go out."

"A-Are you serious?!"

As 'White Sheep' widened her eyes in disbelief, Russell gave a nod.

"Securing Lady Crusch was our highest priority from the start. If we could have secured the others, I would have liked to do so, but if the hand we reach out is severed, we lose everything. —Though it is a pity that letting go of 'Blue Snake' means we cannot test what I wished to confirm."

"Thaaat's why I told ya, didn't I? If you'd just burned them in the sewers, there wouldn't have been any lingering trouble."

"I have no intention of backtracking to topics beyond debate. Time is precious."

Having set his course, Russell turned his back on 'White Sheep.' From his posture, any consideration for the companion who had rushed off to the accident scene had already been discarded. 　Toward his back, 'White Sheep' reached out her hand with a resolute expression—

"Hold it, don't bother."

So saying, a long, extended leg blocked 'White Sheep's path. 　'Black Dog,' sitting slouched in his seat, glared at 'White Sheep,' who bit her lip at the interference.

"You said it yourself, didn't you? You can't make him do what he won't do."

"But doing this is...!"

"I said don't cry, it's annoying. I didn't say that out of consideration for that bastard's mood. It's just a matter of 'maybe you don't actually need to do anything' kind of deal."

"――――"

'Black Dog,' his legs stretched out, shrugged at 'White Sheep,' who blinked in confusion. He raised a hand and pointed toward the rear of the dragon carriage—or rather, toward the accident scene. 　And then—

"—The nature of all this fuss and commotion just changed. So, what happens to your plan now? Kuhahaha!"

Dust and powder blew up into the air at the accident scene, where a half-collapsed building was still in danger of complete destruction. 　The severely injured person whose body was impaled by timber from the fallen ceiling was unconscious, and to anyone's eyes, the wound was fatal. —If the person standing there and continuously casting healing magic had been anyone other than him.

"—Ferris?"

Subaru's eyes widened in shock at the presence of the person who removed his hood with one hand and revealed his face—Ferris. 　He had been thinking about him just a short while ago. Wondering how he was doing now—after going through such terrible hardship, yet rejecting Subaru's outstretched hand as arrogant. 　He had never imagined that he would be using the name 'Blue Snake' and traveling in the exact same group.

"What are you doing?! Do you even have ears on your head?!"

"—っ"

"Don't just stand there frozen in front of a life! If someone dies here, it's our fault for failing to save them!"

The shock of the reunion was shattered by words coming from none other than Ferris himself. 　Brought back to his senses by the desperate shout, Subaru complied with Ferris's instruction—to extract the timber piercing the severely injured person's body. He set Beatrice, whom he was holding, down onto the ground—

"Can you do it, Beako!"

"Leave it to me, I suppose. —Murak!"

Back-to-back battles at the McMahon manor and in the sewers, with an inadequate rest interval. Despite being in the worst possible condition, Beatrice didn't show a trace of it as she activated her Yin magic, lightening the target.

"I'm lifting it! Are you ready?!"

"Anyone who isn't ready is an idiot!"

"Your mouth is as foul as ever! —Ngh, gugugu!"

Shouting back and forth with Ferris, Subaru carefully raised the lightened timber, pulling out the wood that had impaled the casualty. A sickening sensation and sound followed, accompanied by a heavy gush of blood. 　However, the moment it was removed from the wound, Ferris demonstrated his true caliber.

"It's okay. I'm going to heal you right now."

—In an instant, what overflowed was an overwhelmingly gentle blue light.

"How..."

Witnessing that light, an inarticulate murmur leaked from Beatrice's lips. 　Even as a Great Spirit who could cast healing magic herself, Beatrice was left stunned. It contained every reason why Ferris was hailed as 'Blue' and considered a peerless genius without equal. 　It was the same as before—no, it was a miraculous healing technique that had clearly reached an even higher level of mastery.

"――――"

Everyone who had rushed into the scene prepared for tragedy witnessed this sight. 　Subaru could understand how they were all captivated by the miracle. A fatal wound thought to be beyond hope, deep injuries that would surely leave lingering aftereffects, a cruel ending—all were painted over by the light. 　It was a scene reminiscent of a religious painting meant to be passed down through generations—however,

"—Listen, everyone! Carry out all the injured who can be moved and gather them in one place! Prioritize the severely wounded and those who hit their heads! Anyone who can walk on their own, get outside the building!"

Subaru raised his voice at full volume, pulling back to reality the people paralyzed by the miracle. 　Ferris was here. This was a miracle. It was insanely good luck. Milk it for all it's worth.

"We can save everyone! The greatest healer is right here!"

Their eyes wide at Subaru's desperate shout, they exchanged glances and responded with a powerful "Yeah!" Rescue operations immediately began for those who had collapsed or were trapped under rubble. 　Seeing that through, Subaru wiped his eyes with the back of his hand,

"Ferris, we can save everyone! I'll help! What should I do?!"

"Don't get in the way! Just move as I tell you! Hold this person down!"

"You jerk... I'm gonna talk so much back to you once this is all over, so just you wait!"

Disregarding Subaru's speech to the crowd, Ferris's mind was completely focused on saving lives—and indeed, foul tongue aside, Ferris's skill and accuracy of judgment were second to none. At a glance, he prioritized the injured by urgency, casting healing magic with output matched to each case, salvaging lives one after another.

"A restaurant at dinnertime, huh... No wonder so many people got caught in it."

With the scene of disaster finally becoming clear, Subaru spat out saliva mixed with dust. 　With the road jammed with dragon carriages, many people had likely remained inside the establishment. That accounted for the high number of injured, but fortunately, as far as Subaru's eyes could reach, no one had died instantly. 　And if no one had died instantly—

"—You won't die. You'll go home properly."

The overflowing light of complete recovery healed and removed every single wound threatening those lives. 　Saving everyone—not just an ambition, but a tangible reality within reach—

"—! Hey, look at that!"

Suddenly, a voice contradicting that hope rang out, making Subaru snap his head up. 　One of the guards helping with the rescue was pointing at the half-demolished restaurant—specifically at the partially destroyed upper floor, barely supported by cracked pillars.

The roof beam resting on a precarious balance let out a death rattle, and the second floor of the building tilted—underneath were Ferris, who was still administering treatment, and the injured. If the upper half of the building fell, no one would survive. 　The moment that thought struck him, Subaru—no, Subaru wasn't the only one who started running.

"――っ！"

An older guard, his subordinate, and a townsperson assisting with the rescue had all sprinted forward. Others were pushing themselves up, lifting their bodies, trying to move. 　Seeing them out of the corner of his eye, Subaru gritted his back teeth with all his might and screamed recklessly—

"—Beatrice!!"

"This is truly my last drop of energy, I suppose! —Murak!!"

With Beatrice extending both hands, a faint purple light enveloped the falling section of the building. 　Sad to say, however, universal gravitation would not reduce the falling speed or danger of the rubble just because its weight changed. The outcome brought by such immense mass remained the same. —And into that, Subaru slid.

"Ngh, gah...!!"

Barely making it under the debris in the nick of time, Subaru assumed a posture as if caught in a drop-ceiling trap, holding up the falling pieces. Subaru alone couldn't do it. The guards who had sprinted alongside him rushed underneath a moment later, supporting the weight together as one.

"Gugi...! While... we have time, get the people underneath out...!"

Feeling like he was spitting blood, Subaru shouted the unanimous plea of everyone holding up the rubble. Hearing this, those who had lagged behind dragged out the injured and Ferris. 　They had to endure until that was complete, no matter what. —To Subaru, who was steeling himself,

"...Man, big bro, you're always shouting at the top of your lungs!"

"Ah? Wait, you...!"

Holding his ground with everything he had, Subaru noticed the person supporting the debris right beside him and gasped. 　It was a familiar face—Kadomon, the appa shopkeeper with whom he frequently crossed paths in the Royal Capital.

"――――"

His eyes widened at the unexpected reunion, and simultaneously, Subaru felt a surge of panic. 　He had immediately broken Russell's warning that the power of the perception-blocking cloak was not omnipotent. By stepping forward like this, his identity was exposed to Kadomon, who knew Subaru. 　However—

"Don't make a face like that. You helping people here—that's the truth of big bro as far as I can see."

Hearing those words, the strength almost drained from Subaru's arms in shock. As a result, the increased burden made Kadomon and the others yell, "Nuooh?!", forcing Subaru to hurriedly apply strength again.

He was stunned. He had thought he was exposed and done for. —But he was glad he came back.

"Everyone's out! On three, everyone do a one-shot banzai push! Dump it into the back!"

Gritting his teeth until they creaked, Subaru held on, and everyone joined voices to roar, "On three!!" 　What they threw down with a full-power thrust was virtually the entire second floor and above. The moment it left everyone's hands, the effect of the magic ran out, and it regained its original, destructive weight.

"Get out, get out, get out, get out, get out, get out—!!"

With the sound of crushing destruction of explosive weight behind them, everyone holding the rubble evacuated at top speed. Rolling away from the rubble that felt almost murderous, they literally tumbled to the ground. 　Tumbling and sprawling out flat on the ground, they looked up at the dust-covered sky.

"Haa, haa... N-No limbs trapped anywhere... right?"

"I think you can rest easy, right? After all, we've got the greatest healer here, don't we?"

"Even if he could heal it, I don't wanna be sitting face to face with a severed limb!"

Beside Subaru, who raised his voice, Kadomon, also lying on his back, chuckled, "No doubt about that." Laughter echoed from the others as well; apparently, the rubble-holding squad was entirely unharmed. 　Somehow, one job was taken care of—or so it should have been.

"Ferris is still struggling out there all alone. I gotta go help..."

"—That might be a bit difficult, I suppose."

Subaru, forcing his lazy body back up, turned around at that voice with a "Huh?" 　The voice belonged to Beatrice, its tone laced with tension—and Subaru quickly understood why. On the street outside the accident-ridden restaurant, the injured were being brought out all at once. 　And right in the center of it all, even as Ferris continued working tirelessly to treat them—

"—Felix Argyle the 'Blue,' knight number one to the traitor Crusch Karsten. You are ordered to present yourself at the Royal Castle. You will comply immediately."

A squad of knights had surrounded Ferris, threateningly pointing their swords at him.

"――――"

The squad consisted of roughly thirty knights, all clad in the white uniforms signifying membership in the Knights. The sheer absurdity of the scene caused Subaru's mind to go blank. 　In front of an accident scene, pointing swords at a healer in the middle of emergency care? Impossible.

"Wha... What are you saying?! Orders?! Look at the situation!"

"Yeah! That person is treating the injured! If not for him..."

"—Objections are not permitted! This is also a matter of utmost importance to the Royal Capital. The city guard will handle the accident response. We, the Knights, will fulfill our duty!"

In Subaru's place, Kadomon and the older guard raised voices of protest against the madness. But their voices were drowned out by the piercing roar of the long-haired commander leading the squad of knights. 　During the beat of silence that followed, the commander shot a sharp glare at Ferris—at his back, as he continued casting healing magic without paying the slightest attention to the knights.

"It is an order! Stop what you are doing, Knight Felix! Suspected along with your master of grave treason against the Kingdom, how could we possibly entrust treatment to you?! Who knows what you might do to those under your care—"

"—! Don't you dare give me that bullshit!!"

In an instant, pushed past his limit by the unforgivable verbal abuse hurled at Ferris, Subaru erupted. 　Jumping forward instinctively, Subaru thrust himself between Ferris's back and the knights. Glaring at the sword-wielding knights—no, creatures unfit to be called knights—

"How do you think Ferris worked so hard to refine his healing magic?! You think it was just talent?! It was because he wanted to save people, damn it!"

"――――"

"You say you don't know what Ferris might do to injured people?! Of course you know! He's healing them! He's saving lives! He's rescuing people who are on the brink of death! That's what the best damn person in the world would do, clear as day to anyone with eyes!"

Voice raspy, eyes bloodshot, Subaru roared at them, furious that they couldn't grasp something so obvious. 　With gestures so energetic you wouldn't believe he had just drained his strength moments ago, he exploded with pure, common-sense emotion, locking eyes with each knight and hurling his entire soul at them.

"Big bro..."

Watching Subaru's rage, Kadomon murmured weakly. 　To him, the city guards, and everyone at the accident scene, it was crystal clear. Even among the knights, hesitations began to flicker in their eyes and expressions, with several wavering sword tips visible here and there.

However, such genuine, decent wavering of emotion—

"—Look who it is. Subaru Natsuki, collaborator to the traitors."

—was crushed from above by a single sentence from the commander, whose eyes were clouded with hatred. 　The moment the commander uttered "Subaru Natsuki," the knights blinked rapidly, correctly perceiving Subaru standing right before them. —The perception-blocking effect was shattered. 　And once they realized the one spouting reasonable arguments was a wanted fugitive, the knights' hesitation was pushed aside by their sense of duty, rekindling the same tension as before—no, an even greater tension.

"Subaru..."

As Subaru's identity was exposed and swords were pointed at him, Beatrice, stepping close, took his hand. But having exhausted her mana during the recent building collapse, there was no means left to overcome this situation.

"At best, I can punch the leader in the side of the face with Invisible Providence, I guess... Sorry, Ferris. I just poured oil on the fire."

"—. Shut up. I wasn't expecting anything anyway."

"Tahaha..."

"But you're good at talking, aren't you? Joke around and stall as long as you can. Save as many people as possible."

Without turning around, exchanging words with their backs to each other, Subaru gave an internal wry smile at Ferris's sharp mouth and steeled his resolve. 　The enemy wanted information on Crusch. They shouldn't kill him on the spot. If so, then with glib tongue and clever tricks, it was time for Subaru Natsuki's way of fighting without magic—

"You... No, your dangerousness is well understood. To save the Kingdom from unnecessary chaos, Subaru Natsuki, you will right here—!"

"Whoa, wait wait wait! You're overestimating me way too much! Besides, about that treason stuff—"

"Silence! Everything is for the Kingdom! That fast-talking mouth of yours—"

"—A wind of falsehoods is blowing."

—In that instant, a dignified gust of wind blew through.

"Heal every last one of the injured. Make sure all of them can return to their daily lives without lasting harm."

"I was planning to do that even if you didn't tell me."

"I see. Good then. —I shall buy you the time."

Not many words were exchanged. But for those two, that alone was enough. 　For the two of them, who understood each other's capabilities better than anyone else, that was all it took.

"――――"

The golden 'Dragon's Eye'—its gleam drew lines of light through the air, baffling the knight squad with agile movements. The knights spread out to avoid friendly fire, demonstrating well-coordinated teamwork, but—

"I see it."

It was a storm of blades supposed to be airtight, yet inserting the tip of her slender sword into an opening no larger than the eye of a needle, the 'Valkyrie' forcibly carved out a path—Crusch's flashes of steel danced, causing the knights to drop their swords. 　Groaning and falling back while clutching their arms, the knights had all received shallow wounds to their wrists or the bases of their fingers, systematically stripped of their ability to fight on. 　It seemed like a non-lethal sword that refrained from needlessly taking life—or so one might think, until,

"Are you genuine? Or imposters?"

"――っ"

"The two on either flank are good. Sir, you who retreated into the rear—you are bad. —There is no escape."

Right after that ruthless declaration, the retreating knight aimed his empty hand toward Crusch, and in an instant, the sign of magic being cast distorted the space. 　However—

"It's useless, I suppose."

A murmur from Beatrice at Subaru's side—proven true by Crusch's sword flash, a master technique that slashed through magic before it could even manifest. The wide-eyed knight received a diagonal slash of flashing steel and collapsed.

For a moment, Subaru almost spoke up to stop her, but looking closely with full knowledge of the situation, he realized there was a sound basis for Crusch's mercy and ruthlessness alike. 　Among the squad of knights, those whose goal was merely to subdue Crusch and those whose obvious aim was her assassination were mingled together. —Every person Crusch had slashed down so far belonged to the latter group.

From the earlier outburst by the knight commander as well, the involvement of someone—Capella—was obvious. 　And Crusch's Divine Protection was capable of accurately discerning the involvement of that worst kind of Authority.

"Plus, unless I'm mistaken... she's gotten even stronger than she was at the mansion?"

"It is no mistake, I suppose. She is mastering the use of her eyes far better than back then."

Swinging her blade cleanly, relentlessly, and without a trace of hesitation, Crusch sorted through the knights during battle based on Capella's influence, selectively choosing how to deal with each. Her swordsmanship was simply sublime. 　Her 'Dragon's Eye' was settling in. —Unable to celebrate that fact unconditionally, given what it implied.

"—! That's everyone! It's done!"

Crusch's overwhelming battle display had bought every second needed until Ferris completed his treatment. 　With sweat glinting on his forehead, Ferris tried to stand up on the spot. As his body wobbled and almost collapsed, Subaru reached out instinctively and supported him. 　Subaru wrapped an arm around Ferris's slender frame, holding him firmly by lending a shoulder.

"You never miss a clutch moment, do you, Subaru-kun..."

"Don't say it like I was waiting for an opening! Man, you were cool as hell!"

"You're too loud. Hold me up properly. I'm gonna fall."

"You're so demanding. But... Crusch-san!"

Ferris had fulfilled his task. It was time for Crusch to pull back from the fight with the knights as well. 　To be honest, with her power, breaking through the encirclement head-on might be possible, but—

"If she passes out midway again, it really will be over this time, I suppose."

"Seriously, that's what I'm scared of! Crusch-san, tell me you have a plan!"

"Regrettably, I have none."

"Hieeee!"

"Therefore, I had one prepared. —'Black Dog'!"

Just when hope seemed lost, Crusch shouted a name far too dangerous to pin one's hopes on. 　At the sound of it, Subaru, Beatrice, and Ferris all groaned "Ugh"—and immediately after,

"Kuhahahahaha! Don't hate me or love me so much! The star of the show has arrived!"

A laugh that made everyone feel unpleasant just hearing it echoed around. The knights and bystanders who didn't recognize the voice looked around in shock. However, the owner of the laugh was nowhere to be seen. 　Instead, what covered their vision was a massive plume of black smoke.

"It is a smoke screen. It is harmless."

As cries of shock and confusion rose amidst the black smoke, Crusch dashed over and spoke in a quiet voice. However, not to doubt Crusch, but when it came to 'Black Dog's work, anxiety won out.

"I think it's fine. If it weren't, you and I would be left behind here again, wouldn't we, Subaru-kun?"

"That's true! Got it. For now, out of here—Invisible Providence!"

In that instant, nodding at Ferris's point, an invisible black magic hand shot out from Subaru's chest, piercing through the smoke and punching right through the face of the knight commander who was trying to step forward. 　Spurting blood from his nose with a sharp gasp, the commander fell flat on his back. Crusch glanced at him briefly,

"That one was good."

"No way?! He wasn't one of the fakes?!"

"I sensed powerful anger and hatred directed at you. Do you have any idea why?"

"Isn't that Knight Zeol? He was at the castle during the Royal Selection. Maybe that's why?"

"Give me a break, settle on an evaluation for that incident already!"

Whether it served as proof of Subaru's innocence or as a reason to be targeted, it was a total mess. 　Grievance aside, blending into the smoke screen, Subaru and the others slipped out of the situation. —Behind them, several knights attempted to follow, but,

"I won't let you chase big bro and the others. I really don't like the way the Knights are doing things today."

Standing in their way to block them were Kadomon and the city guards. 　At that unexpected aid, Subaru gasped, "Pops?!" and Kadomon grinned back,

"Go! I'm rooting for you! For you, big bro, and for Lady Emilia!"

"—! Yeah, I'll be back to get your votes!"

In response to Kadomon's encouragement—given with full recognition of Subaru Natsuki as Emilia's knight—Subaru answered, nearly brought to tears, and poured strength into his legs to dash through the smoke once more.

Behind his back, the roars of the knight squad and the rising spirits of the city guard resisting them rang out loud and clear—

"—The inspection is complete! Let them through, Knights! Let the guards do a guard's job!!"

"—Yo, you made it back? I thought for sure you were dead."

"Chin, you bastard, what a terrible comrade you are..."

Passing through the dense smoke that helped Subaru and his companions escape, they hurried into the dragon carriage waiting outside the main gate. Rachins lazily waved his hand to greet the four. 　In the face of such a huge commotion, Rachins's coldheartedness in showing zero intention to move was downright astounding.

"Do you even have a human heart? Are you a Witch Cultist or something?"

"Don't pin false suspicions on me! And saying I didn't do anything is a total lie. I did proper negotiations. Right, Lady Duchess?"

Sticking out his tongue, Rachins directed the conversation to Crusch. At his call, Crusch, who had refastened her eyepatch over her left eye, nodded with a "Indeed."

"Rachins Hoffman is an articulate speaker. In truth, he was the one who brought the negotiations to fruition."

"Negotiations? You mean..."

"—With us, of course, Natsuki-dono."

At the voice that sounded out as a surprise attack, both Subaru and Beatrice jumped. 　The speaker was none other than Russell, who had boarded the dragon carriage a moment after Subaru and the others. 　Accompanied by 'White Sheep' and 'Black Dog,' he instructed the coachman to set off and shut the carriage door. 　Immediately, the dragon carriage sprang into motion, transforming into a reflection room with no way out.

"First, everyone returning safely is the best possible outcome. Of course, luck was merely on our side, and I have many thoughts on the matter—'Blue Snake.'"

"Hmm..."

"You were the one who made it a condition that your presence not be revealed. Nevertheless, acting as you did made concealment impossible. Surely you won't claim this was our fault?"

"I know. I'm not saying you breached the contract. This isn't the place to talk about personal feelings anyway."

Ferris, who was addressed first by Russell, shot a glance out of the corner of his eye at Crusch. But though Crusch must have noticed his gaze, she didn't react toward Ferris. 　The atmosphere drifting between the two was undeniably complicated—

"What should I do, Beako? Think I can break the awkward mood? Can I try?"

"Hold on, hold on a moment, I suppose. This is a situation to watch and wait. More importantly..."

"Natsuki-dono, please refrain from such actions in the future."

"Gugh... You really are mad, aren't you?"

Subaru timidly gauged Russell's expression, but as expected of the head of an intelligence agency, his face was unreadable. 　Russell's expression was a complete calm of emotion, but there was no way he wasn't angry at Subaru, who had defied his instructions head-on and rushed out to the scene. —This face definitely counted as angry.

"Rather than angry, I am disappointed. I believed I had spared no words in explaining what should take priority. —Had it not been for Lady Crusch's words, we would not have risked remaining at that scene."

"Crusch-san's words? What do you mean...?"

"It is nothing complex. —At present, among those gathered here, I have no intention of placing my trust in anyone other than Subaru Natsuki. Cooperation with this 'Six Tongues'—I refuse it without you."

"That's...! That's an insanely huge honor, but...!"

Depending on how one took it, it was quite an extreme declaration. Subaru grew acutely conscious of the eyes around them—especially Russell, who had an extra condition thrust upon him, and Ferris, whose relationship with her was complex. However, Russell's expression remained unchanged, while Ferris, citing exhaustion, sat in his seat and turned his face away. 　At any rate, the 'Six Tongues' couldn't let go of Crusch, and because of Crusch's stance, they couldn't abandon Subaru's group and Ferris when they rushed back to the accident scene.

"Still, about this, Russell-san..."

"What is it? If your thinking has changed after this incident..."

"It's not so much my thinking, but... if something like this happens again, I think Ferris and I won't be able to just ignore it. So, I have a request."

"—. I am listening."

"I want us to go with plans that keep casualties as low as possible from now on. —Is that no good?"

At that request, Russell widened his eyes slightly, while 'Black Dog' in the back let out a "Kuha" chuckle. 　Subaru was well aware he was asking for the impossible. But in reality, no one died in this accident. Dealing with those touched by Capella was difficult, but they could resist until the very last moment—or so they ought to be able to. 　Before Russell could say anything to Subaru's line of thought—

"I-I do too! I think Subaru-san's way is better as well...!"
