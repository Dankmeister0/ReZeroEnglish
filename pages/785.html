Arc 10 Chapter 26: "Words of Congratulation"

—From the moment he was appointed as Filore's knight, Heinkel had felt a sense of danger regarding her reckless, headlong personality, but that perception had not been entirely correct.

Filore's recklessness was far beyond anything Heinkel had imagined.

"—So, the medicine is in the warehouse, but it can't be delivered?"

"Yes. The congestion of dragon carriages entering and leaving the royal capital is terrible, and the church's carts are completely stranded. At this rate..."

"The medicine will spoil, and there are many elderly people at the clinic who will be in danger if they don't take their medicine every day... In that case, how about we do our best to carry it out ourselves?"

"Eh?! B-By yourselves?!"

"Yes! If we can't move the dragon carriages, we can just carry the cargo by hand... Don't you think that could work?!"

At the entrance of the 'Divine Dragon Church', the young man holding the inventory of medicines leaned far back, startled by the sheer momentum of Filore, who clenched her fists and leaned forward. Taking the young man's reaction as agreement, Filore looked ready to dash out to the commercial district at any second, but Heinkel hurriedly stopped her, saying, "Wait, wait."

"Filore-jou, doing it that way won't solve the problem even if the sun sets and rises again tomorrow. You can't seriously be thinking of carrying the cargo all by yourself."

"What are you saying? Of course not! Heinkel-sama is my knight, so we are in the same boat, in the same boat! We won't go alone; we will go together!"

"As if I'd let you! Even if we resort to brute force, there's no way I'd let someone of your standing, Filore-jou, go. If anyone is going, I'm going alone."

"Don't be so distant! I am surprisingly strong, you know. Back at the monastery, I often rearranged the rooms instead of the old maids with bad backs!"

"That's! Not! What! I'm! Talking! About!"

Filore, who was operating at full throttle, widened her red eyes at Heinkel's angry shout. Looking at the girl blinking her sparkling, jewel-like eyes, Heinkel clawed at his red hair and turned a grumpy "Hey" toward the young transport worker.

"What you need is a means to transport the medicine to the clinic in the East District, right? There should be clinics in the West and South too; how are things over there?"

"The West and South? Those roads are still passable, so there's no fear of the medicine being delayed there..."

"Then it's simple. Instead of moving the medicine, just move the patients."

Saying so, Heinkel snatched the medicine inventory from the youth's hand. The items listed on it were all familiar to him, and he could roughly guess the condition of the patients who needed them. Ever since his wife fell asleep, he had researched treatments for every illness under the sun. Living in the capital and coming from a privileged noble family, Heinkel had used those advantages to gain more pharmaceutical knowledge than average healers.

"However, I haven't seen the patients directly. Ultimately, it's up to the healers, but it's more realistic than trying to figure out a way to get an entire dragon carriage through. If we secure the road to the East District while doing this symptomatic treatment, things should go smoothly next time. —So, for now, what do you think, Filore-jou?"

"Eh? Yes! You're right! Ah, but what about those who can't be moved from the clinic...?"

"The clinic shouldn't be entirely filled with critically ill patients. If so, a single wooden box packed only with high-urgency medicines should suffice for the time being."

"I see! Then I will take that wooden box—"

"Hey."

"—No, I'll leave it to you! Carrying cargo is your job, after all!"

Glared at by Heinkel, her enthusiasm dampened, Filore turned a smile toward the young man. Having the inventory handed back by Heinkel, the young man nodded at Filore's words.

"Understood, I will do so. As for the clinic..."

"Don't worry. The clinic and the 'Divine Dragon Church' are incredibly close! I'll send a messenger immediately to handle things so there are no misunderstandings. You just focus on your duty."

"—Yes! Thank you both very much!"

With a look of relief, the young man bowed with a sense of duty and rushed out of the church. Watching him leave, Heinkel's shoulders slumped from fatigue. It was fine for Filore, who listened to consultations from start to finish without losing wind, to receive thanks. But for Heinkel, who had merely stood beside her, being thanked felt incredibly awkward. After all, he was a man who, in truth, had no right to be standing here.

"Good work, Heinkel-sama! That was a consultation where our perfectly synchronized cooperation truly shone. Since I've sent the messenger, he should be able to face his duty with peace of mind."

Utterly unaware of Heinkel's inner thoughts, Filore, who had entrusted the task of sending a messenger to a church follower, returned and lightly raised her hand toward him. She didn't lower it. When he stared at her for a moment, she still kept it raised, moving her hand left and right in front of Heinkel.

"……What is it?"

"Not 'what is it,' it's a ritual to celebrate each other's efforts! When I raise my hand like this, you match your hand to it, Heinkel-sama. By doing so, we share the feeling of having guided each other well. It's a standard rule of the 'Divine Dragon Church'!"

"I never saw anything like that in the church back when I used to go."

"It might be a recent rule! Also, it might have a bit of my own touch added to it!"

Considering the nature of the Church, which had worshipped the 'Divine Dragon' without change for four hundred years, Filore's own influence was a far more convincing explanation than the idea of rituals coming and going out of style. Unfortunately, since Filore didn't look like she would drop it until the ritual was completed, Heinkel let out another sigh and slapped his palm against hers with a smack.

"Filore-jou, about earlier..."

"Ah, it was a wonderful flash of inspiration! Don't worry. I made sure to tell the messenger to inform the people at the clinic that it was Heinkel-sama's idea."

"—. Restrain yourself. In two ways."

"Self-restraint?! My least favorite word...!"

Her smiling face turning into one of betrayal, a pale Filore staggered and leaned against a chapel pew. Watching her, he let out a deep breath through his nose. He wished she wouldn't double the things he wanted to complain about in such a short span of time.

"In the first place, stop sticking your head in and poking around in the fireplace ashes yourself. There's no telling what's in those ashes, and most of the time you'll just get burned. If you have to sift through the ashes, use someone else. Starting with me."

"Is that... because Heinkel-sama is an immortal knight who won't lose to fire?"

"No. It's because if someone is going to get hurt, it should be someone who doesn't matter if they get hurt."

He chose to use harsh, clear words to correct Filore's mindset. Filore's methods were precarious. However, her natural, eye-catching appearance was one of the indispensable qualities of a ruler. Having entered the Royal Selection late, she had to avoid situations where she might get burned—quite literally—and play her hand effectively. And so—

"Stop trying to bring up my name at every turn. To put it bluntly, it will do nothing but hinder Filore-jou's Royal Selection campaign, and I'm not joking."

"That's not..."

"But because they hear it was my idea, it might even make the clinic proposal harder to pass. That's the sort of standing I have now, and it wouldn't surprise me if that happened. Sorry, but I am well aware of it."

"—!"

Filore looked frustrated at that answer, but since the one making her wear that face was none other than himself, Heinkel had no words of comfort or encouragement. To be honest, he still regretted his choice. After all, Heinkel should never have accepted Filore's request, which he could only view as a momentary lapse of judgment. Watching the attitude of the young girl who had become his master, earnestly facing the Royal Selection despite her clumsiness, he cursed himself all the more for being nothing but an obstacle to her ambitions. Especially since that mountain of accumulated bad reputation was something he had earned of his own accord.

"In the end, nothing I do ever works out..."

Even though he intended to act in pursuit of the best outcome at any given moment, his past actions would later come back to slowly strangle him. It was like an unavoidable trap he had set for himself, frequently catching up to Heinkel's life a lap late. He had tripped over the holes dug by his past self more times than he could count. Which was precisely why, whenever some sort of achievement could be made, not mixing in an impurity like Heinkel would yield the best result for Filore—

"—Argh, this isn't like me!"

Just as Heinkel was narrowing his eyes in deep thought, Filore suddenly cried out in front of him, pinching the back of her fair hand hard with her fingers. As she let out a shriek of "Ouch, ouch, ouch!", Heinkel widened his eyes.

"W-What are you doing?"

"It's a punishment for my own helplessness! Normally I'd slap myself on the cheek, but if I did that, I'd have to meet people with a bright red face, wouldn't I? So, whenever I want to discipline myself, I've always done this. If my hand is just red, I can make the excuse that I slammed the table in anger!"

"Is that even an excuse? The part about reacting in anger is completely bare..."

"It's fine! Reacting in anger is the truth, anyway! And no matter what anyone says, I will not budge on this. From now on, I will not hide Heinkel-sama's achievements!"

A finger was pointed sharply at his face, causing Heinkel to instinctively lean back. Closing the distance of his retreat, Filore advanced. Heinkel stepped back. She advanced. He stepped back. She advanced. He was cornered against the wall, her finger pointed right between his brows. And then, keeping Heinkel pinned against the wall, Filore said,

"I will accept that Heinkel-sama being bad-mouthed left and right is due to your past behavior. I still grind my teeth at it, but I will swallow it. But! I will not stand for past actions dulling the value of your deeds today or tomorrow!"

"Filore-jou..."

"If there is a life saved by your words, then that might be a life that couldn't have been saved without them. If I can become king, and you are my first knight, we will have many opportunities to witness such moments. That is why, in preparation for that day, we must restore Heinkel-sama's honor starting now!"

"――――"

"So, while I will listen to Heinkel-sama's advice as much as possible, I will never obey opinions like 'disparage Heinkel-sama,' 'keep him hidden in a box,' or 'let's just throw away that drunkard of a knight~'. I declare it here and now!"

Slowly lowering her finger, Filore touched Heinkel's uniform—the official Church Knight uniform he had been made to wear as the knight of the 'Divine Dragon Church's' 'Saintess'. The blue-themed uniform was sacred and solemn. It was roughly equivalent to, or perhaps even more awe-inspiring than, the uniform of the Imperial Guard, making him feel terribly unworthy to wear it, fully aware of how ill-fitting it looked on him. But—

"But eventually, everyone who looks at this uniform will think of Heinkel-sama. When that time comes, you can even use the Church's authority to act as high and mighty as you please, you know."

Filore winked, showing a clumsy attempt at charm. At her words, Heinkel let out a sigh. Her full-tilt, headlong attitude spared no one. And that, of course, applied to Heinkel as well.

"……'When Filore-jou becomes king,' huh. In that case, is helping people at the church just self-interest?"

"How can you say something so terrible?! You see, this is an incredibly good deal. When we listen to the consultations of those who come to the church, the consulters are saved, I get a reputation for doing good deeds, and we feel great because we did something good... right?"

"Don't include me in that as if it's natural. I..."

"But isn't being thanked by others a wonderful thing? It is to me. That's why, even though the title of 'Saintess' is heavy, I love the church."

As Filore nodded and puffed out her chest, Heinkel could find no answer. Perhaps because she had said her piece, or perhaps out of consideration for Heinkel, Filore did not press him for the answer he couldn't give. And the chance to voice his answer was postponed by the sound of the church doors opening once more.

"—Excuse me. There is a conflict between residents right near the church..."

"Oh dear, how terrible! We must rush over immediately! Heinkel-sama, let us go too!"

"—. Don't run. We can't afford to increase the number of people who fall over and need our scarce medical supplies."

"In that case, at a fast walk!"

Saying so, she took three large strides toward the messenger, and then, as if forgetting her promise to walk fast, broke into a run. Heinkel scratched his head again. Then, clicking his tongue, he hurriedly chased after the back of his reckless master.

"—You do your job more properly than I expected."

During the hours when the orange sunset poured in through the large high windows, a voice spoke to Heinkel's back as he stared down at documents in a church room. Turning around, the one looking at him from the doorway was a slender, elegant man—Tiga Rauleon, a priest of the 'Divine Dragon Church' who served as Filore's guard. Tiga, who had apparently accompanied Filore as her attendant even before her entry into the Royal Selection, had been introduced to Heinkel immediately after his appointment as her knight, and they had crossed paths frequently since then. Naturally, Tiga's first impression of Heinkel had been the worst—no, that wasn't unique to Tiga, but could be said of everyone around Filore. In fact, Filore was the only one who had welcomed Heinkel's appointment as her knight.

"Well, that's obvious. Anyone would, and I would have done the same."

Objectively speaking, Heinkel's current position was far beyond what he deserved. Filore was already precarious enough, a constant source of anxiety for those around her. The idea of Heinkel, whose bad reputation was endless, standing right beside her was something anyone with a shred of sanity would find completely unacceptable. Even though it had been done by Filore's own 'Dragon's Decree', finding someone who agreed with it would be far harder than finding someone who objected.

"—? Why are you suddenly looking off into the distance? Did you not hear my sarcasm?"

"No, I heard it clear as day, and I sympathize with you guys. Taking care of Filore-jou must be hard enough, let alone having someone like me dragged into the mix."

"If you knew that, I would have preferred you to quietly decline the offer in the first place."

"I have no comeback for that. I suppose that would have been the proper, adult response..."

Cutting himself off, Heinkel hesitated over whether to continue. But prompted by Tiga's gaze, he reluctantly opened his mouth. The reason he couldn't refuse the hand Filore had extended to him was clear.

"Simply put, it's a lack of grace—a kind of obsession that is as far from mature judgment as you can get."

If an adult was defined as someone who could cleanly draw lines, cut losses, and give up, then Heinkel was a terribly unsightly creature who had completely failed to grow up. Yet, on the other hand, the adult Heinkel had admired most in the world was someone who never let go of his sword until the very end, cutting down even the impossible, and never giving up on the things he loved.

That he had yearned for something he could never reach, something he couldn't even lay a finger on. That might have been the beginning of Heinkel's curse. —Even if he spoke of such things, Tiga would likely have zero interest in them.

"—. I had your circumstances looked into. Don't take it personally. I don't want to leave a dangerous element by Filore's side. Besides, it was already a famous story in certain circles anyway."

However, at the tone carrying Tiga's subsequent words, Heinkel raised his brows. Having spent a long time living while constantly reading others' expressions, Heinkel was highly sensitive to the nuances of negative emotions, whether they were contempt, mockery, sympathy, or pity. That was precisely why he wanted to tilt his head. For there was, without a doubt, a trace of concern for Heinkel in Tiga's voice.

"You've been glaring with a sour face at Filore's schedule for a while now. Isn't it incredibly hard to get that girl to stick to a schedule?"

Leaving Heinkel's doubts aside, Tiga peered over his shoulder from behind, offering a keen observation that showed a deep understanding of Filore. Indeed, tomorrow's schedule—making the rounds to pay her respects as a Royal Selection candidate, and the duties she had to fulfill as the Church's 'Saintess'—made Filore's days dizzyingly busy. Even beyond what was written on the schedule, she attended services at sunrise, and after finishing her prayers, voluntarily listened to the consultations of those visiting the church.

"To top it off, she's of a temperament that absolutely cannot overlook any problem she spots. If she runs into a lost child or an elderly person struggling to carry baggage on the street, the rest of the schedule is finished."

"Yeah, I get it. Once before, Filore completely vanished, and it turned out she had accompanied an elderly person to carry their luggage, going all the way to a large town three stops away, and only came back after being treated to tea at their house."

"...As long as her title is just 'Saintess,' that might be fine. Well, actually, even with the title of 'Saintess,' doing that is risky enough."

Now that she was a Royal Selection candidate, she couldn't keep acting as she always had. Irreplaceable roles attract irreplaceable duties. This was expected of Filore as well, and it went without saying which took priority. And yet—

"Filore-jou doesn't want to neglect her duties as a member of the church either, and on top of that, she plans to fully follow through as a Royal Selection candidate... It's just asking too much."

Dragged along by her sheer enthusiasm, Heinkel was constantly on the run. In place of Filore, who stuck her nose into everything, he had to figure out who to hand off arising problems to for resolution, reorganize delayed schedules, apologize to people they had appointments with, and anticipate and prepare necessary documents and proposals ahead of time. He was completely overwhelmed by tasks that had not a shred of resemblance to those of a first knight. In fact, ever since he had become Filore's knight, there had been zero opportunities for him to draw his sword.

"Even so, it seems you haven't skipped your training during Filore's morning services. I had assumed on my own that you weren't a morning person."

"...How could someone who's bad at waking up early serve as a knight?"

"I see, it really seems unfit for me then. I'm glad such a major role as a knight never came my way. In any case, there's no denying I would have lacked the skill for it."

Heinkel glanced sideways at Tiga, who aloofly analyzed himself while adjusting the position of his hat.

"Lacking skill, huh."

To be honest, Heinkel found it hard to agree with Tiga's claim. As someone who was originally entrusted with guarding Filore, Tiga, who carried a curved sword, possessed skills that even in Heinkel's eyes were considerable. He had a supple, well-trained physique, a demeanor that suggested plenty of actual combat experience, and above all, excellent, unshakeable courage. He isn't a swordsman or a knight, but he is a warrior. —That was Heinkel's assessment. Still—

"How is your wound doing?"

"Hmm, I still can't say I'm in perfect shape. It's gotten a lot easier, but it was a fairly deep wound to begin with... Truly, the alias of 'Valiant Maiden' is not just for show."

"Duchess Karsten, who killed Lord McMahon and fled..."

At Tiga's reply as he stroked his flank through his clothes, Heinkel knitted his brows. The name he spoke belonged to the very person who had dealt that deep wound to Tiga's flank, and who was suspected of lighting the fuse of the chaos currently swirling through the royal capital.

Crusch Karsten, initially considered the absolute favorite in the Royal Selection and known by her alias "Valiant Maiden." Currently, she was wanted as the culprit who assassinated Miklotov McMahon, the central figure of the Council of Wise Men, and her whereabouts were being pursued across the kingdom.

The incident itself was shocking enough, but what forced him to tilt his head in confusion was that Natsuki Subaru, who was supposed to be Emilia's knight, was also wanted as Crusch's accomplice. Furthermore, not only was he suspected of being an accomplice, but he was also under the absurd suspicion of being the Sin Archbishop of Sloth himself. In connection with this, Emilia, who was deemed suspected of treason against the royal castle, and Felt, who could not clear the suspicion of identity fraud, were detained in the castle, and the affiliates of both camps fled the royal capital. At that time, a large witchbeast was released into the city, and the 'Sword Saint' was deployed to resolve the situation.

That was what had already occurred by the time Heinkel was brought back to the capital, and it was also the factor that continued to spread a nightmare-like chaos not just through the capital, but across the entire kingdom.

"By now, the bleakness in the capital must be at a level that will go down in the kingdom's history. On the streets, knights and guards have an ominous atmosphere about them, and the townspeople have no cheerful topics to talk about, just hanging their heads."

"People with options can choose to leave the capital. But the number of people who don't have that choice is overwhelmingly greater. Honestly, the church is busier now than it has been in years."

"And in exchange, Filore-jou's support solidifies, huh?"

"I don't like that way of thinking. Not me, and certainly not Filore."

At Heinkel's slight irony, Tiga's gaze grew sharp. Realizing that Tiga was genuinely displeased rather than joking, Heinkel raised both hands in a gesture of surrender. But regardless of Tiga's feelings, that was indeed the reality of the situation. The more the kingdom's chaos spread, the more the people lost trust in the royal castle, turning instead to the church that worshipped the 'Divine Dragon' which had continuously protected the country with a proven track record. This effect was undoubtedly serving as a tailwind for Filore, who had entered the race late. And for Heinkel, as Filore's knight, this should have been something to celebrate, but—

"Father-dono..."

As he muttered, the image of his missing biological father, Wilhelm, crossed Heinkel's mind. Crusch was wanted for Miklotov's assassination, and Wilhelm, who served her, was also one of the affiliates who had vanished from the capital. He was likely assisting her flight after reuniting with his master, but there was no question that they were at an overwhelming disadvantage. Of course, it wasn't as if he didn't harbor a daydream-like expectation that the famous 'Sword Demon' could carve a path through any adversity with his blade.

"You're in a tough position as well, Heinkel-san."

Sensing his inner thoughts from Heinkel's mutterings and expression, Tiga shrugged. A tough position—that observation was probably correct. But if we're talking about that, ever since Heinkel was in Priscilla's camp, the Astrea family has been completely fractured.

"...My hardships are nothing to write home about. The only issue is that this might cause trouble for Filore-jou. Father-dono and my son... they are both grown adults. It's only natural for them to clean up their own messes."

"—. At least, your father wasn't there at the place where I clashed swords with Duchess Karsten. I hope it's just a false accusation for him."

Tiga patted Heinkel's shoulder as if to encourage him, and Heinkel, hesitating on how to respond, ultimately gave a flat, half-hearted "Yeah." The detail that Wilhelm was not present at the scene was something Heinkel didn't need to be told to know. If Wilhelm had been there and a situation had arisen where Tiga clashed swords with him, Tiga wouldn't be in this world right now. Moreover, there was another reason why his response had been so vague. And that was something he could absolutely never reveal to Tiga, who was standing right here.

—Because that reason was the indelible distrust toward the 'Divine Dragon Church' that had begun to sprout within Heinkel.

—In the current Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica, the role played by the 'Divine Dragon Church' was immense. Originally, under the protection of the 'Divine Dragon' Volcanica, Lugunica was promised peace and the defense of the nation, continuing to prosper to this day. Because of this, most citizens naturally harbored a deep sense of awe toward the 'Divine Dragon', which inevitably translated into unconditional trust in the 'Divine Dragon Church'. Heinkel had been no exception. That was precisely why, when his wife fell into a slumber from which she would not wake, he had believed in a place of prayer and miracles, and had frequented the church constantly.

Unfortunately, Heinkel's prayers had borne no fruit, and furthermore, he had committed a massive error for which he could not show his face to the 'Divine Dragon' or its followers. Ever since, he had distanced himself from the church, and he could not help but mock himself once more for his current, unbefitting position. Once his time spent lamenting his own foolishness was over, Heinkel had to face it. The distrust toward the 'Divine Dragon Church' that had sprouted within him—specifically, concerning Filore's existence. Or to be more precise, the Church's intentions behind having Filore participate in the Royal Selection.

Having been appointed as her knight and watching Filore up close, he was repeatedly made to realize her terrifyingly genuine, straightforward goodness. Filore, who continued to give her all in front of everyone under any circumstance, possessed a precarious judgment when she appointed Heinkel as her knight. But when forced to make choices, she did not fluster; she made decisions on her own responsibility and had the execution power to realize them. To that end, she talked a lot, laughed a lot, cried a lot, and moved a lot. The talent to move others by voicing her own desires, a virtue that could be called natural charisma—that was truly the lineage of the Lugunican Royal Family. Although Filore herself said she did not have the answer to whether she was the princess who had vanished fifteen years ago, nor was she seeking it—

"—There cannot be two princesses."

This was certain, as Heinkel was well-acquainted with Ford Lugunica, the king's younger brother—the father of the vanished princess and the biological younger brother of the previous king, Randohal Lugunica. Ford's daughter was not a twin or a triplet; there was unquestionably only one. And that sole daughter had vanished from the royal castle after that night fifteen years ago. The vanished princess—it could only be either Filore or Felt, who had both stepped onto the stage as Royal Selection candidates.

Hence, someone was plotting. —To elevate one of the two girls, Filore or Felt, as the surviving royal member, in order to use them at will. Thinking about it that way, he couldn't help but harbor strong distrust toward the 'Divine Dragon Church', including the circumstances of Filore's entry into the Royal Selection. However—

"...What is it that I want to do?"

Thinking this far, Heinkel's thoughts ground to a halt, as if paralyzed by fear. It was an extremely foolish act, akin to knowing that one is surrounded by a dense, blinding fog with sunset fast approaching, yet doing absolutely nothing about it. But within that dense fog lay the light that Heinkel had yearned for over many years. And if he took a step in the wrong direction, that light would be lost forever. Therefore, he could not move. He was unable to act, terrified of making a fatal mistake.

—In the end, without finding an answer, only time continued to pass.

From the time Heinkel and Filore's master-servant relationship was forged, days turned to weeks, and soon a full month was about to pass. During this time, the Filore Camp smoothed out the flow of goods that had stalled due to the capital's turmoil, responded to consultations sent from churches and clinics across the land, and steadily increased her support as a Royal Selection candidate. As for Heinkel, still unable to shake his distrust toward the Church but unable to bring himself to hinder Filore either, he spent his days fleeing from the looming anxiety by desperately throwing himself into resolving the problems right in front of him. Yet, despite these Royal Selection activities, the two matters that Filore herself desired most strongly had shown absolutely no progress.

"...Has it come back again?"

In a room in the church, looking down at the mountain of documents built upon the desk, Heinkel knitted his brows in exasperation as he spotted a familiar envelope among them. On the other side of the desk across that mountain of papers, a woman working while stifling a yawn—Sakura Element—contrary to her unmotivated attitude, was efficiently sorting the documents by sender and distributing them so they would be easy to organize and review. With her sleepy eyes and lazy demeanor, she cast a brief glance at Heinkel.

"Ye-e-es, it seems so-o-o. It helps that their replies are as quick as always, but as for the contents... ah, it seems it's been put on hold again this time too-o-o."

"On hold, huh. How many times does this make?"

"Let's se-e-e, forty-seven letters to the royal castle, big and small. Thirty-two to the Church, and with additional reference materials and attached written statements, since Filore-chan-n-n is such a diligent writer, it seems we've already easily surpassed a hundred le-e-etters."

Sakura deftly checked the register at the edge of the desk with one hand while sorting letters with the other. Dispirited by her answer, Heinkel let out a sigh.

A hundred-plus letters of sheer obsession. The contents of the documents Filore sent almost daily to the royal castle and the Church were, in short, requests for a hearing and visitation with Emilia and Felt, who were detained in the castle, and requests for permission to treat the Witch Cult's victims left behind in the Watergate City of Priestella using Filore's Miracles. Filore was tenaciously struggling to go through the proper formal procedures for both, but—

"Why won't those guys nod their heads? Setting aside a meeting with Emilia-sama and the other, the Priestella matter is surely worth a try. Or is there some flaw in the paperwork?"

"Oh-o-oh, are you doubting my work? If so, I've made Filore-chan-n-n go through all that wasted effort for a hundred letters. I must be quite a nuisance then-n-n."

"I was just asking. I'm not doubting you. If you were that incompetent, you wouldn't have been given such an important title as the 'Saintess's' administrative officer."

"Is tha-a-at so? I think there's a good chance it's just riding on my family's coat-tails. Don't you think so-o-o, Royal Guard Vice-Commander Heinkel-chan-n-n?"

"—. Don't call a middle-aged guy '-chan,' Sakura-chan."

"I'll put your request on ho-o-old, Heinkel-chan-n-n."

Giving an answer that showed absolutely no intention of listening, Sakura handed Heinkel the document returned from the royal castle. Flipping it open, the contents were something he could have written with his eyes closed.

The requests are currently undergoing careful deliberation, centered around the Council of Wise Men, and we ask that you wait a little longer for a conclusion. —Whether it was the treatment of the two detained girls, or the use of the Miracle in Priestella, the answer remained the same, with only the wording slightly rearranged.

"They don't reject it, but they don't permit it either. How long do they plan to keep this up?"

"Who kno-o-ows? It's for the higher-ups in the castle to decide. Given the situation, I'm sure everyone is being very ca-a-areful."

"They're piling caution upon caution. Filore-jou is at the limit of her patience, and lately she keeps insisting that her heart is being 'scraped raw'."

"That's a very Filore-chan-n-n way to put it, isn't i-i-it?"

Sakura giggled, putting her hand over her mouth, but unfortunately, Heinkel had no desire to join her in laughing. Rather, watching Filore's forced cheerfulness increase day by day, becoming clearly painful to look at, was enough to make him feel depressed too.

"If the castle is a no-go, what about the Church? Filore-jou is a 'Saintess,' isn't she? Can't she even decide for herself whom to use her Miracles on?"

"She can't decide that-t-t. It's not something so chee-e-eap, you see."

Faced with such a flat assertion, Heinkel looked slightly taken aback. Sakura tapped the three piles of letters on the desk in order with her finger, saying, "Listen closely-y-y."

"To begin with, 'Saintess' is not an official rank within the Church-h-h. It's a special title granted to believers who have received an exceptionally strong blessing from the 'Divine Dragon'. Though people appreciate them and they get to sit in front of the high-ranking officials at ceremonies, it doesn't mean they have a seat in the Holy Council where the Church's elites gather, or that they hold strong speaking power over the-e-ere."

"Despite that, she's treated outwardly as the face of the Church."

"Being a symbol that supports the people's faith in a visible form is also an important part of the 'Saintess's' jo-o-ob. Though, even if we tried to quietly assign Filore-chan-n-n only the role of a symbol, it would probably be harder than teaching the 'Dragon's' tail to 'sit-t-t'."

He had nothing but agreement for that, but he disliked the explanation leading up to the conclusion. It seemed to imply that Filore, currently bearing the role of a Royal Selection candidate, was nevertheless meant to completely fulfill her position as the symbol of the 'Divine Dragon Church' in the form of a 'Saintess'—to put it bluntly, a mere figurehead.

"Actually, I think Filore-chan-n-n is quite suited to be king-g-g. Your attitude, Heinkel-chan-n-n, is probably the result of being charmed by Filore-chan-n-n's royal caliber, isn't i-i-it?"

Heinkel was bad at dealing with Sakura, who laughed as she saw right through his silent thoughts. Her competence, which didn't match her appearance and demeanor, could be felt in every word and action. Tiga was a top-tier church guard, but in Sakura's case, rather than just being top-tier among the civil servants dispatched by the Church, she made him wonder if she was one of the truly select elite. In fact, while it was Filore's diligence in writing that produced the hundred letters, it was Sakura who had gathered the necessary documents and handled the procedures for all of them. She had her hands in nearly every department related to the 'Divine Dragon Church'.

"If they just had one of you, the Church would probably run smoothly on its own."

"Oh, no-o-o, you overpraise me. I could never put on the face of being everyone's hope like Filore-chan-n-n can. Besides, an organization where the whole thing fails just because a single person makes a mistake is too precarious to run-n-n."

"...I see, how modest of you."

The answer from Sakura, who waved her hand dismissively, was in a way a provocative swipe at monarchy itself. In the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica, the Council of Wise Men existed to compensate for that, but in the current situation where even the Council's movements seemed sluggish, Heinkel, as a mere fragment of a kingdom knight, had no words to offer in response.

"I suppose there's talk of them being in disarray since Lord McMahon passed away..."

He hadn't observed the discussions of the Council of Wise Men from up close, but Miklotov was indeed their central figure and coordinator. However, that didn't mean the other members of the Council lacked a voice or decision-making power; active opinions should have been flying around, spearheaded by figures like Lord Bordeaux Zergev, who was known for launching heated arguments. In reality, the problems faced by the capital and the castle were not just one or two, and responses to them were proceeding as swiftly as before, despite Miklotov's absence.

In short, the only two matters being kept in limbo were the ones Filore was actively trying to get involved in—though Heinkel had no idea what that implied.

"Is the Church not permitting Filore-jou to use her Miracles... because it's related to Duchess Karsten being wanted?"

"I can't deny tha-a-at. In fact, Filore-chan-n-n using her Miracles without going through the Holy Council was seen as a major problem, and there was even talk of revoking her 'Saintess' certification, so I hea-a-ar."

"They can actually revoke a 'Saintess' status?!"

"In the rules, ye-e-es. I don't think it's ever actually been applied. She escaped it this time too-o-o, but once it's been discussed, things won't remain safe forever, will they-y-y?"

Sakura said it casually, but Heinkel could only feel shocked once again. The independent action of Filore that had greatly shaken the royal castle was apparently a massive event for the 'Divine Dragon Church' as well, and had even jeopardized Filore's own position. And the situation where Crusch, who had received that Miracle treatment at such costs, was now wanted as the perpetrator of Miklotov's assassination was nothing but a headwind for Filore.

"Having everything you do blow up in your face... that takes a toll..."

"Aren't you quite passionate about this-s-s? Ah, but of course. For Heinkel-chan-n-n as well, permission for Filore-chan-n-n to use her Miracles is a matter of life and death, after a-a-all."

"――――"

"Or is it because you've really settled into the role of Filore-chan-n-n's knight?"

At her teasing gaze and voice, Heinkel snorted loudly. Unfortunately, he was under no obligation to answer Sakura's sadistic sarcasm. Of course, it was true that he wanted to verify as quickly as possible, even by a single second, whether Filore's Miracle could save his wife, Louanna. But—

"For the people waiting in Priestella, it's not a matter of whether it might work or not; the effect is highly anticipated. If whether they can be saved or not depends solely on whether permission is granted, it's only natural to want it to go through immediately."

"Prioritizing others over yourself? Was Heinkel-chan-n-n really such a nice person-n-n?"

"I'm not talking about that. I'm saying that while waiting for salvation is one thing, being kept waiting is completely different."

"—Indeed."

To his tit-for-tat, spat-out honest words, Sakura's response had lost its teasing tone. Though he looked at her with suspicion, he couldn't detect any change in her expression or the color of her eyes that would have justified that feeling. It seemed it had just been his imagination. At any rate—

"—Let's just quietly fulfill our roles, shall we-e-e? If we do, I'm sure at least one good thing will happen for us-s-s."

Such words, for a member of the 'Divine Dragon Church', felt to Heinkel to be far too modest, yet at the same time, a terribly earnest murmur.

△▼△▼△▼△

"—I've decided! If writing a hundred letters didn't work, then I'll deliver the hundred and first letter in person! If I look them straight in the face and beg, they'll surely find it hard to refuse!"

And so, prompted by the words of Filore, who wore a face as if she had received a revelation from the 'Dragon' itself, her knight, guard, and administrative officer were all forced to accompany her to the royal castle. Filore's attitude as she blustered that "this time for sure" was something to be respected, but—

"—I cannot accept this!!"

The moment they stepped out of the reception room and began walking down the chalk-white corridor, Filore raised her voice as if she had reached the absolute limit of her patience. At her angry voice echoing down the castle corridor, passing civil servants and servants looked over in surprise. Mindful of them, Tiga placed his index finger to his lips.

"Filore, if you raise your voice in a place like this, you'll stand out too much. If that reaches the ears of the higher-ups, next time they won't even let you into the reception room; they'll turn you away at the entrance hall."

"But how can they treat us like this?! I told them that if they let us know what is lacking, we will prepare it ourselves, but all we get is 'under consideration,' 'on hold,' or 'completely in the dark'...! What on earth are we supposed to do?!"

"There's probably nothing to add or fix to begin with-h-h."

"Then they should just let it through!"

"—They had no intention of listening from the very start."

Appealing in her anger, Filore turned at Heinkel's words. Her red eyes were welling with tears, but Heinkel had no handkerchief with which to wipe them away. As a substitute for a handkerchief, he gave her the raw answer she needed without mincing words.

"The reason you're being stopped isn't because of flaws in the paperwork or lacking conditions. What they want is for you, Filore-jou, to give up and stop bringing this matter to them."

"That's... that's just wrong! Who could possibly be thinking of such a thing?!"

"Yeah, I think that's the real problem too."

Filore looked as if she were throwing a tantrum, but she had already done everything possible before resorting to one, and she wasn't allowed to do anything beyond it. In truth, the doubts Filore currently harbored were things Heinkel couldn't answer either, and the incomprehensibility of the past month and a half had only continued to build.

...Just when I thought everything had been set up far too conveniently for her.

Honestly, except for her late entry into the Royal Selection, it had felt as if everything else about Filore's appearance had been thoroughly laid out for her by the 'Divine Dragon Church'. However, once the lid was opened, while her support in the chaotic capital was steadily growing due to her own actions and goodness, the visit to the Watergate City that was supposed to solidify her foundation—permission for this was not being granted by none other than the Holy Council, the top tier of the Church. If they wanted to make Filore king, this was undoubtedly the move that had to be made. And yet, far from supporting this path, the 'Divine Dragon Church' consistently maintained an attitude of blocking it, and the Council of Wise Men was not acting in her favor either. Under these circumstances, the distrust Heinkel held toward the Church began to accumulate in a different form than before. However, even if the nature of his distrust toward the Church changed, there was one thing that did not. —The fact that Filore was still an immature child whom adults had to support.

"Filore, I understand how you feel, but let's go back for now."

Urged by Tiga's gentle voice, the frustrated Filore was led by the hand as they began to walk. On the way to the castle entrance, the castle affiliates passing by stopped and bowed politely upon seeing Filore. As a Royal Selection candidate, and as a 'Saintess,' the respect directed toward her was genuine. Yet, it was ironic that not a single one of her wishes could be granted.

And so, dragging a heavy silence behind them, they turned a corner in the corridor—

"――――"

Catching sight of the person walking toward them from the front, Heinkel gasped involuntarily.

"—Reinhard."

Before he could even think, the breath he had swallowed was spat out as the sound of that name. The red-haired youth clad in a white knight's uniform, with the 'Dragon Sword' hanging at his hip—Reinhard van Astrea—also stared straight at the person who had called his name. Naturally, their respective blue eyes, proof of their blood connection, crossed paths.

"It has been a while, Father."

"……Yeah."

He cursed himself for such a pathetic response. It was a complete surprise. He had been distracted by his thoughts, and because it was an unexpected encounter, the curses and abuse he normally would have prepared could not be summoned in an instant. In the end, finding no words other than that foolish reply, silence fell between father and son.

"――――"

Behind Reinhard, two individuals wearing the white uniforms of the knights accompanied him. At a glance, they could be seen as subordinates accompanying the 'Sword Saint' on a mission. However, their gazes were focused not on their surroundings, but constantly on Reinhard's movements. Above all, Heinkel did not recognize the faces of those knights. —At least, they were knights of unknown origin whom Heinkel did not know. Why were these knights accompanying Reinhard—?

"—! Reinhard! Perfect timing! I wanted to talk to you too!"

Trampling right through the muddy air between father and son, Filore rushed toward Reinhard. The knights behind him instinctively braced themselves, but Reinhard raised a hand to restrain them.

"Filore-sama. You were at the royal castle."

"Yes. We were talking about Emilia and Felt, and also about Priestella. But right now, never mind about me, it's you."

"Me?"

"Yes, that's right. How is Felt? You've been meeting her frequently, haven't you?"

"――――"

Faced with her closing the distance without holding back, Reinhard quietly caught his breath. Eventually, Reinhard slowly shook his head, "No."

"Pardon? Does that... mean you haven't met her yet today?"

"That is not it. It has been since Felt-sama entered the royal castle."

"Felt is in the castle... and not once?! Even though over a month has passed?!"

Filore's voice spiked, causing the knights behind him to tense their shoulders in unison. However, Reinhard quietly nodded at her reaction. But it was no wonder Filore was shocked. This was on a completely different level from Filore not being permitted to visit Emilia and the other.

"You are Felt's knight, aren't you? For you not to be able to meet her, that is incredibly strange. Don't you think so?"

"Visitations are forbidden, so it cannot be helped."

"B-But, you want to see her, don't you? You must want to talk to her."

"I am the sword of the kingdom. Since an order has been handed down from the castle, I have a duty to obey it."

"You are not just the sword of the kingdom, you are supposed to be Felt's sword as well!"

Flabbergasted by what she was hearing, Filore's wide-eyed words were far too direct, and it was visible how they pierced Reinhard's chest. But as to how Reinhard would answer her unconscious indictment, Heinkel knew it all too well. That was—

"—I am the sword of the kingdom, Filore-sama."

The conversation had come full circle, returning to his initial answer. It wasn't that Filore's words were insufficient. Nor was it that Reinhard failed to understand what she was saying. No matter what he was told, no matter what he felt, Reinhard would not change his answer. Perhaps it was more accurate to say that he could not change it.

"Do you believe that Felt deceived the kingdom?"

"No."

That, too, was an immediate, unhesitating response. Before Filore, her eyes wavering, could ask why that was, Reinhard continued.

"My believing in Felt-sama and being able to prove her innocence are two separate matters. If I disobey orders, it will only deepen the suspicion toward Felt-sama and worsen her position further."

"So, you have no choice but to do as you are told?"

"――――"

To that question, Reinhard chose neither affirmation nor denial. However, choosing neither was in itself a statement of his stance.

"――――"

Heinkel looked at the two knights standing behind Reinhard. It was obvious that they were not on Reinhard's side. In that case, their role would be to monitor Reinhard, but what a meaningless measure. If Reinhard felt like it, he could strike them down in an instant, or vanish before they could even notice, reaching Felt's side without being stopped by anyone in the castle. But Reinhard would not do that. He could not. As long as he was the 'Sword Saint', that was—

"Then, at least convey this to Felt."

Clenching her fists, Filore spoke to the silent Reinhard.

"I believe in Felt. I believe in Emilia too. Please tell her that there is someone outside the castle who doesn't believe for a second that the two of them betrayed the kingdom."

"……Thank you."

"A letter is fine too. I'll write it."

"But I cannot make that promise. I am also forbidden from delivering words or items to Felt-sama."

"How could...!"

"I am sorry. I have my next duty to attend to."

At his relentlessly painful response, Filore's well-featured face distorted. However, Reinhard did not yield to Filore's reaction. Leaving them with a bow, he went to pass by their side. Just before his shoulder brushed Heinkel's, his steps suddenly stopped.

"Father, I heard that you have become Filore-sama's knight."

"……Yeah, apparently. Got a complaint?"

"—Congratulations."

Hearing that before he could even utter an insult, Heinkel was lost for words. At a distance close enough for their shoulders to touch, Reinhard's gaze turned straight toward Heinkel, looking at him. Faced with his son, whose height had completely caught up to his before he knew it, Heinkel could not bear it and averted his eyes.

"……As if I'd know whether it's something to congratulate me on."

"Even so, I am glad that you were chosen, Father."

"――――"

Heinkel could say nothing in response to those words. It was neither sarcasm nor irony. Reinhard was sincerely blessing his fallen father for having been chosen as a knight by someone.

Did he not remember what kind of abuse he had hurled at him in Priestella? Had he forgotten that in the same city, Heinkel had pointed his sword at Reinhard's master? Or were even those actions mere trifles that left not a single scratch on Reinhard's heart?

With a small bow, Reinhard walked down the chalk-white corridor, accompanied by the two knights. Filore continued to watch his retreating back with a look of frustration. Heinkel could not even bring himself to glare at his departing son's back as Filore did.

△▼△▼△▼△

"—Filore, those remarks to Reinhard were too careless, as expected."

As soon as they returned to the 'Divine Dragon Church', Tiga closed the door to the reception room and turned to Filore with a stern expression. He had remained silent in the dragon carriage on the way back from the castle, likely thinking deeply about how best to admonish her.

"You might have thought you chose your words carefully, but there were castle knights and other affiliates present. If word of this reaches the Council of Wise Men, you will be seen as rebellious toward the royal castle as well, Filore."

"Let them think that. I really am rebellious, after all."

"That's not what I'm talking about. I'm saying that if even you are banned from visitations or entering the castle, you truly won't be able to do anything."

"I know that."

"You don't, which is why you did such a—"

"I do know!"

Filore sharply cut off Tiga's words. At her intensity, Tiga gasped, and Sakura lowered her eyes. Reacting to the two of them, Filore murmured in a hoarse voice, "I'm sorry..."

"I know... I'm sorry. But because I know, it makes me all the more furious. If we don't obey what the castle decides, they won't treat me as a Royal Selection candidate anymore. And if they don't treat me as a candidate, I won't be able to save Emilia and the other. That is why we have no choice but to wait as we're told."

"Filore..."

"I'm sorry. I understand perfectly that you two are saying this for my sake."

"—Yeah."

"Let me speak with Heinkel-sama alone for just a bit."

At her request, Tiga nodded, albeit hesitantly. Sakura left the room first, and Tiga followed after. The door closed, leaving only Filore and Heinkel in the room.

"――――"

On the desk lay the month's worth of return letters from the royal castle. Filore looked at each and every one of them, and eventually let out a quiet breath.

"Heinkel-sama."

"What is it?"

Even Filore, as resilient as she was, must have been taking quite a toll from this. Her desperate pleas had not gotten through, and she couldn't say her voice had reached Reinhard either. Even for her, who threw herself headlong into everything, it was only natural to grow exhausted from such lack of progress. Heinkel, too, had suffered a massive drain on his mental energy from the unexpected encounter with Reinhard and the unpredicted words of congratulation. Nevertheless, bracing himself with what little spirit he had left, he prepared to receive Filore's words.

And then, leaning her back against the closed door, Filore voiced to Heinkel a matter she could not—and would not—allow Tiga and Sakura, who had departed, to hear. It was—

"—How do you think we could pull off an escape from the castle?"
