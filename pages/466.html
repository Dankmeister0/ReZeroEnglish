Arc 6 Chapter 61: "—Stand Up"

—A choice, and a cruel one at that, was eating away at Natsuki Subaru.

Deep within his chest came the slow, agonizing sizzle of something burning to ash.
　Whether that was his humanity, his faith in himself, or his feelings toward "Natsuki Subaru"—all manner of such things were scorching away.

With his hands wrapped around a young girl's neck, pinned beneath him as she sneered in mockery, Natsuki Subaru stood at the precipice of a scene that would decide his own fate—the fate of "Natsuki Subaru."

"――――"

He couldn't hear the beating of his heart. Though his breathing was ragged, his lungs were likely doing nothing at all. Despite the sheer desperation of his predicament, not a single drop of cold sweat dotted his brow.
　Surely, that was because the body of Natsuki Subaru present here was not real.

He had not been bodily transported upon reading the book; his mind alone had been dragged into this place—engaging in such detached analysis amidst a crisis like this was likely nothing more than a form of escapism.
　And so, by casting his thoughts far afield, Subaru's heart sought a fleeting moment of peace.
　Yet neither time, nor space, nor the opponent before him would grant him such an escape.

"Come on, what're you gonna do, Onii-san?"

The girl pinned beneath him looked up at the paralyzed, wavering Subaru, a sadistic sneer plastered across her face.
　Peering into Subaru's dark irises, she flicked her tongue as if licking his very eyeballs.

"Pinning down a helpless little girl, wrapping your hands around her slender throat. Doesn't it give you shivers? Or with a constitution like yours, Onii-san, is this sort of thing old hat?"

"—Tch!"

"Look at you, shaking all over. So-o-o cute. Can you really make that super-duper important choice like that?"

Tilting her head, Rui, lying on her back, planted a kiss upon Subaru's wrist. That chilling gesture, paired with the feverish glint in her upturned eyes and her callous words, dragged a certain memory to the surface of his mind.
　A heartless scene Subaru had witnessed once before. —Except the perspective had been reversed. Not what Subaru had seen, but the view of the girl facing him.

The sight of Subaru pinning her down, an evil expression upon his face, throttling her neck.
　Just like right now—the sight of "Natsuki Subaru" strangling Meili to death—.

"Ngh."

—The moment he realized how close this scene was to that one, Subaru's entire body, even his cheeks, went rigid.

"—I knew it, it rings a bell, doesn't it?"

"Quit... screwing with me...!"

"We're not screwing around at all. If anything, aren't you the one who isn't taking this seriously, Onii-san? You oughta love yourself more seriously, more earnestly, with all your heart."

"――――"

"Ah, that's it, that's right. Love yourself. Go on, love yourself. —Just like the people you hold dear wish for you to, you've gotta love yourself too, y'know?"

Her flippant tone let a wave of seemingly plausible words skate across the surface.
　Did she genuinely care to be heard, or was her capacity for empathy dead from the start? Was it calculated, or natural? Was she mocking him, or comforting him?
　It was all vague. Everything about Rui Arneb's existence was utterly nebulous.

Vague, so terribly vague.
　Rui's words—everything she said rested on shaky ground, like leaves drifting unstably atop the water's surface. If only he could decisively dismiss it all as arbitrary and warped, would even this hesitation clear away?
　No, to begin with, just how dangerous was it to accept her premises as the foundation of his thoughts?

"What's the proof... that if I do what you say, it'll turn out like that?"

"Proof?"

"What proof is there... that if I take back 'Natsuki Subaru,' the me who's here right now will disappear...?!"

"There isn't any. Nope. None at all. 'Cause there isn't. I'm telling you, there isn't. Not a bit. Even though there isn't. Though that's how the story goes. Though that's how it works, y'know? ...Does that make you feel any better?"

"――――"

"You're going in circles, Onii-san. We can't talk about things we don't know, either. If we, or Big Brother, or Elder Brother die, do the things we ate come back, I wonder? —Honestly, we've never given back what we ate, so we wouldn't know-o-o. I mean, we already ate it up."

Rui opened her mouth with a wide "Aah," flaunting her strikingly sharp canines and red tongue, displaying the depths of her throat to Subaru to show there was nothing inside.
　If stripping away others' memories, or the memories of someone within another, was what she termed "eating," then of course no physical trace would remain.
　Yet to Subaru, the words "we already ate it" felt dreadfully heavy.

"What're you gonna do, Onii-san?"

Once more, Rui pressed the question.
　Throughout their exchange, Subaru's hands remained fixed around her neck. Unable to exert force, yet equally unable to pull away entirely, Subaru continued to interrogate his very existence.

"Kh, ngh...!"

Dying was scary. It was terrifying.
　Yet this was a terror distinct from the four "Returns by Death" he had endured up to this point.
　The proposition bearing down upon Subaru's soul right now was a death weighed on the scales against "loss of self."

That was what "death" was supposed to be in the first place.
　Once you died, the consciousness of that existence was lost, with no second chance granted.
　Perhaps that meant Subaru, who had been coddled by second chances every time he messed up, had no right to complain.
　Perhaps having the choice to disappear or not, and having the time to agonize over it, was an extravagant luxury in itself.

Even so, it was his life.
　Placed in a situation where he had to choose whether or not to snuff out that flame with his own hands, Subaru's heart cracked further with every passing second.

"――――"

In this other world, Subaru had already died four times. Every single one had been over a short span of time.
　Cast into an unknown world, meeting people he had never seen before, only to be driven to death immediately afterward by unavoidable calamities.
　The total time he had spent conscious here would amount to less than two days.

A brief, painfully brief time. —Yet Natsuki Subaru possessed, beyond those two days in this other world, seventeen years spent in the world he came from.

He had a father. He had a mother. Friends, too—few as they were, and questionable as it was to call them friends, there were people who would at least return a greeting. Looking back on the past, there had been folks he got along with in elementary and middle school, and plenty of familiar faces among his neighbors.
　He hadn't done a great job of it. Subaru was awful at living.
　Even so, in his own clumsy way, he had spent time stumbling through trial and error, and while none of it had been life-or-death, he had still struggled across what felt like his own grand stage.

Was he to abandon all of that—those accumulated times and memories?

If "Natsuki Subaru" returned, the fact that those things existed wouldn't vanish. But the current him, who had held those times so dearly in his thoughts, would disappear without a trace.

Trading a promise with Ram, swearing to protect Meili, etching Echidna's forgiveness into his heart, rallying Julius to fight, believing Beatrice to be precious, and Emilia—.

—Was the him who had fallen in love with Emilia going to vanish?

"No..."

That realization caused the body of Subaru present here to crack, without any metaphor.
　Fissures ran across his cheeks, and a spiderweb of cracks crept along both arms pressed against Rui's throat. There was no pain. Nor did blood spill from the fractures. Strangely, within those cracks lay darkness. Not flesh or bone, but a bottomless, unnatural darkness.

Just as he had deduced, he had not brought his real body here.
　The body Subaru had here wasn't the real thing. And because it wasn't real, it directly mirrored his current state of mind, fracturing and crumbling away.

The fissures spread, and the surface began to peel and fall away.
　Surely, that was the shell of deception called "Natsuki Subaru" that Natsuki Subaru had cloaked himself in.
　As piece after piece flaked away, his false bravado stripped away right along with it.

"No, no, no... No...!"

"That's right. Of course."

Shaking his head in denial, he rejected the fear of the death awaiting him—no, the fear of loss.
　Why did he have to be lost? When he had only just admitted to himself that he loved the person he loved.

"I don't want to..."

"Yeah, yeah. We get it. We totally get it. 'Cause we get it. We really do, y'know?"

"I don't want this...!"

"It's your life, Onii-san. Why should you have to hand it over to someone else?"

"I want to... be with everyone more..."

I want to be with everyone more.
　He had come to love them. He truly had. In less than two days' time, even though he had doubted them more than once, engulfed in paranoia, wanting to kill, wanting to flee.
　Subaru had fallen in love with them. Right now, they were so very dear to him.

If he was with them, if they thought of Subaru as someone precious, then maybe he could come to love even his detestable self.
　He had managed to think that. He had managed to look forward and believe that.

When a patch of warm sunlight had finally shone down upon a life that had only ever faced backward.
　Why did he have to let go of that with his own hands?
　There was no way—

"—I don't want to."

"Right. That's it. —Then, what d'you think you should do?"

"...I'll stay me."

"Yeah, Onii-san stays Onii-san. That's what's right. You snatched it away once already. It's musical chairs. The guy sitting in the empty seat is the king."

"――――"

"Gotta ask the one you shoved aside to see themselves out. Acknowledge it. Your own existence. You should shout it at the top of your lungs—that you're the real deal! Right?! Isn't that right?!"

Directly beneath him, close enough for their breaths to mingle, Rui roared, her wide eyes gleaming brilliantly.
　With the ferocity of a snapping beast—no, in fact, she sank her teeth into Subaru's wrist that still pinned her throat, carving an admonishment into him along with vivid pain.

Fixing her gaze upon his wavering dark eyes, Rui Arneb screamed:

"Acknowledge it! —'Natsuki Subaru' is the stranger closest to you, Onii-san!"

Her roar implored him to establish himself.
　To stop doing something so foolish as trying to die for someone else's sake.
　Why did anyone ever have to sacrifice themselves for another?

—All the more so just to let another existence, someone who wasn't him but bore his name, meet the people he loved, whom he would never be able to see again.

Just to hand over the time spent living alongside them—those precious, irreplaceable days.
　How could such an absurdity ever be accepted?

"Come on, kill him! Let's kill him! Kill him! You gotta kill him! Kill him, and! If you just kill him! Let's just kill him! Go ahead and kill him! Finish him off! If you just kill him! If you slaughter him down to nothing!"

"'Natsuki Subaru'..."

"—Then you'll be the one and only Natsuki Subaru in this whole wide world, a substitute for nobody!"

"—!"

The one and only Natsuki Subaru in this world, a replacement for no one.

Holding hands with Beatrice, trading banter with Ram, making Meili pout, being exasperated by Shaula's blunt openness, exchanging smiles over harmless chatter with Echidna, standing back-to-back with Julius, receiving Patrasche's unconditional love, earning the right to live alongside Emilia.
　If the one who possessed all of that was "Natsuki Subaru," then Subaru would—that man, he would—.

"――――"

A sudden swelling emotion blurred his vision.
　His mind was directly influencing his flesh. The thumping of a heart, the ache of drawing breath into his lungs—right now, he would surely be able to feel them with striking clarity.
　Yet what he felt most powerfully of all were tears he could no longer hold back.

Whether that was anger or sorrow, jealousy or envy, guilt or fear.
　What kind of passion had spawned it, Subaru had no idea at all. It was all things he couldn't understand. Yet through his tear-blurred vision, Subaru saw it.

"――――"

Someone was looking down at Subaru, and at Rui.
　Staring at the pathetic, tearful Subaru, who had Rui pinned beneath him with his hands around her neck.
　As far as Subaru could think, there was only one person it could possibly be.

"...Did you get scared and show up, 'Natsuki Subaru'?"

"――――"

The blurred silhouette said nothing.
　Standing upon the white floor, set against the white world, in a hazy white form, it watched Subaru.
　Toward that presence that had hastily rushed out, Subaru, his face a crumpled mess, spoke:

"I... I don't want to disappear. I don't want to die. That's why, I..."

"――――"

"I want to be with everyone. I love everyone. That's why, I..."

"――――"

"That's why, I..."

Like excuses, he piled on his whimpers.
　Just like when he had wrapped his hands around Rui's neck. —Subaru had reached the conclusion that he did not want to be lost. That was why he declared it to the silhouette that had appeared before him.
　Even if that meant killing the person before his eyes, who surely had the very same face as him.

Because he, "Natsuki Subaru," was the stranger closest to him.
　Therefore, Subaru ought to have that right.

Natsuki Subaru would kill "Natsuki Subaru," and seize that single patch of sunlight—.

"That's why, I'm not you! You and I are...!"

Different, he tried to state clearly, attempting to sever the possibility.
　Just as he tried to do so—it was at that very moment.

"...Who're you talking to, Onii-san?"

Stunned, her eyes wide, Rui asked with a face as if the wind had been taken right out of her sails.
　Tilting her head, she stared in the same direction as Subaru, trying to see the silhouette. Yet her brow furrowed suspiciously, and her sharp canines trembled as she spoke:

"—There's no one, no one there, so who're you talking to, Onii-san?"

"――――"

With her fangs clattering, Rui muttered in disbelief.
　Shaking her head in denial, the expression she had worn up until now vanished, replaced by a look as if terrified of something.

"This is our place... Nobody should be able to get in. In this place, who're you talking to besides us... Stop it. Onii-san belongs to us, you're ours...!"

Despite Rui's clinging words, Subaru's attention didn't shift in the slightest.
　His awareness remained entirely focused on the silhouette that lingered in his sight, refusing to fade. In his tear-blurred vision, the swaying silhouette's outline grew just a little clearer.

A silhouette of someone he didn't know at all.
　As its outline gradually sharpened, to Subaru, it looked as though it were smiling.
　Shaking his head, blinking hard, he tried to see that smile more clearly—.

"—Why are you trying to choose only one?"

A question was posed.
　In a voice he had never heard before, the voice of someone who shouldn't be here.

A blue-haired girl—whom he had never once seen smile—stood there, smiling.
　And that smiling girl, toward the silenced Subaru, kept that smile as she—,

"—Stand up!!"

—Her very first words.
　—In the sternest voice in all the world, she bellowed at Natsuki Subaru.

"—Stand up!!"

The voice struck the cracked Natsuki Subaru, battering him, beating him down, thrashing him.
　Without mercy, without hesitation, the furious shout shattered Natsuki Subaru, accelerating the spread of those fissures. —As though casually digging claws right into his bared heart.

"Stand up!"

The blue-haired girl raised her voice toward Subaru.
　Glaring at Subaru, the girl roared at the top of her lungs. Roared, and roared, and roared.
　At Natsuki Subaru, who remained on his knees, pinning a girl down, cracks spreading across his dazed face.

"Stand up!!"

The repeated, furious command.
　Over and over, it mercilessly, unreservedly beat down Subaru's heart.

Why did he have to be subjected to such words?

It hurt. It was agonizing. It was painful. It was sad. His heart felt ready to burst.
　Rarely in life was someone bombarded with decisions this agonizing, one after another, without any time to prepare their heart. —He had stopped lamenting why he was in such a plight.
　That was why, at the very least, he had reached a conclusion. So wasn't that enough already?

"Stand up!"

His whining, his stubborn conclusion, his heart trembling before loss—as all of it cowed Subaru's spirit, the girl before him refused to permit it in the slightest. Imbued with resolute rejection, she forcefully drove her words into him again.
　He had made his decision. She could at least validate it. At the very least, she could show a hint of understanding for his struggle. Wasn't it enough? He had agonized over it plenty. And yet, why did she push Subaru so hard?

"Stand up—!"

She would not forgive Subaru for choosing as his fracturing heart dictated.

"Stand up—!"

Was she still going to say it?
　Why—what was with this voice, with this girl?
　Even though it hurt this much, even though it was this agonizing.

"Stand...! Stand! Stand! Stand up!"

Who was she, this girl?
　Where in his memories was this girl?

He had never exchanged a word with her. There were no memories of her inside the current Subaru.
　Who she was, what kind of person she was—he only knew her surface.
　A relationship that couldn't possibly serve as a reason to hold back.

And yet, why—why was his chest burning this hot?
　Why was there a heat welling up from deep within his chest?

"Stand up, Natsuki Subaru! Stand up! —Rem's hero!!"

The tearful voice of a girl not in his memories—at her shout commanding him to be a hero, his heart trembled.
　His heart trembled so opportunely he could almost laugh at how ridiculous it was.

The cracks, the fractures accelerated.
　It was quite literally the sight of Natsuki Subaru breaking free of the shell of "Natsuki Subaru."
　Yet what slumbered inside that shell was subtly different from moments before.

—No, if it was truly going to change, it would start right here.
　As commanded to stand, crushing his frightened heart beneath his teeth, he would stand.

"Once you've stood, go. Go, and save everything."

What is "everything"? What does that even mean?
That's way too vague. Just what on earth is "everything"?

"Everything is everything. Every single thing. All of it, all of them, yourself, and even the stranger closest to you!"

What kind of talk is that?
Can I even do something like that? Does this girl seriously believe I can?
Lacking so many things, unable to even save myself—someone like me?

For the sake of the people Subaru had come to love, for the people who treated Subaru as precious, for the people Subaru had come to want to cherish, for the memories of the people he didn't want to lose.
　Did she honestly believe that even Subaru, who had been about to throw away just one single thing, could really do it?

"You can. After all—"

After all.
After all, what?

Give me strength, give me an answer. If you're going to give it, give it with those words.
If I may wish for it, let it be your words, blue girl, to me—.

"—Subaru-kun is Rem's hero."

"――――"

With a soft thud, something settled deep into his chest.
　That which had been stagnant and black was purified, as though by the sound of a young girl's confession of love. —No, it was not as though it were a confession of love. That was a confession of love.

Though it gave him yet another reason why he didn't want to hand his place back over to "Natsuki Subaru."

"—Hah."

At the same time, that wasn't the only thing that grew.
　Purified by the girl's words, that which had been dark and stagnant flared with brilliant light, altering its form.
　And then, it began to pulsate at the place where Natsuki Subaru longed for his strongest core.

"――――"

It pulsated. Having lost everything, having been left behind by all.
　Yet even so, still longing, still desiring, wanting to tether everything together, refusing to let a single thing slip through these fingers—unwilling to let go of even himself with his own hands.
　Answering that cowardly "Greed" that wished for so much, it blossomed into the power to grant that desire.

—The drifting Witch Factor bonded with his existence.

"Come to me—Cor Leonis."

Within Subaru, the seed of "Greed" that had lost its place sprouted.
　And as he stood firm, a solidified existence—

"――――"

At that moment, the smile of the blue-haired girl alone offered its blessing.

※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※

"—Onii-san?"

"――――"

Looking up at Subaru as he slowly rose to his feet, Rui called out to him.
　Her pinned hands now released, Rui, still bearing an expression of bewilderment, pushed herself up upon the golden bed woven from her own hair, blinking in confusion.

"What's... wrong? Come on, let's pick up where we left off... Where we left off, yeah?"

"――――"

"Where we..."

Told to pick up where they left off, Subaru moistened his lips with his tongue.
　Considering this was a mental existence, it was nothing more than a meaningless habit, a hollow comfort.
　Yet through that single gesture, there was something he realized.

—His head was clear right now, to a degree that astonished even himself.

The baffling confusion and frenzy from just moments before had both receded like the tide, leaving him as tranquil as if he had rowed out into the center of a calm, windless sea.
　That was why—

"You don't need to say another word. I'm sick to death of your twisted, crooked explanations."

He could calmly accept that the mass of malice in the shape of a young girl before him had been trying to warp Subaru's will and manipulate him to her own convenience.

At Subaru's accusation, Rui shook her head frantically with a "No, no, no."

"Twisted and crooked... That's so mean. In our own way, we were just trying to give Onii-san all kinds of advice out of the goodness of our hearts..."

"Stop deliberately throwing around words you know I recognize, too. It's useless trying to shake me like that. —The me right now won't waver anymore."

"――――"

At those words, Rui narrowed her eyes. She likely didn't understand the finer details of the change that had occurred within Subaru. Subaru didn't understand the specifics, either.
　Only that an unshakeable "Greed" had firmly established Natsuki Subaru.

Subaru, too, had defined himself.
　Into the shape desired by none other than the girl who had bellowed at him in that moment.

"――――"

Glancing away, Subaru looked past Rui rather than at her.
　The girl who had hurled those merciless words at him until just moments ago was nowhere to be seen. The instant Subaru had stood up and faced forward, she had vanished.
　Yet perhaps that was for the best.

The one she truly ought to reunite with was not here, nor was it this Subaru.
　No, that wasn't quite accurate either. Rather, the one who ought to reunite with her should be the Natsuki Subaru who had regained his memories of her and his feelings for her.

And there was no need to draw a line between that "Natsuki Subaru" and Natsuki Subaru.

"Even though... I was told over and over again."

—He had been told that even if he lost his memories, Subaru was still Subaru.

When he had stubbornly insisted that there was a clear, insurmountable divide within himself, that he had to separate the two, those words had been Subaru's burden, a chain of curses.

Yet look at it now.

To the Subaru whose purpose had now solidified, it was a compass, a thread of hope.
　Reeling it in, hand over hand, it would surely guide Subaru straight and true, without hesitation, back to the precious people holding the other end of that thread.
　That was why—

"—Put away your knife and fork, you dine-and-dasher. Ain't no noodles for the likes of you."

"――――"

Her eyes went wide.
　Rui Arneb's eyes widened as she stared at Subaru pointing his finger at her. And seeing not a shred of sentimentality in Subaru's expression, she hung her head.

"Ah..."

Hanging her head, a raspy breath leaked from her lips.
　It was a breath steeped in an emotion utterly impossible to describe.
　Pushing herself up, Rui's shoulders trembled as she pulled her knees to her chest, curling into a ball atop the carpet of her golden hair.
　Then, slowly raising her bowed face—

"—Ah, damn it, damn it, damn it! We were one step away, just one step away!"

Glaring at Subaru with eyes full of hatred, Rui squeezed those words out in a cursed hiss.

"――――"

"We were just one step away, y'know! So close, y'know! Why, why'd it go wrong, I wonder? —Who on earth bewitched you, Onii-san?!"

It was a tone of voice steeped in a dark, simmering hatred for that hopeless divide—like a dead soul at the bottom of hell envying the living who reveled in paradise on earth.
　With a voice brimming with that sheer rancor, Rui continued:

"We were just one step away from tearing 'Natsuki Subaru' and Natsuki Subaru completely apart...!"

"...What's that supposed to mean? Why would you pull a stunt like that?"

"—Isn't it obvious?! Because you can't eat the same person twice, can you?!"

"—!"

Drowning out Subaru's bewildered voice, Rui screamed in a cracked voice that sounded as though she were spitting blood.
　She pushed herself up on all fours and glared at Subaru with a look that had completely inverted from before—a beast-like countenance stripped of all humanity.

"They had to be separate! The 'Natsuki Subaru' we ate once and the leftover Natsuki Subaru had to be separate! We put so much thought and flair into making sure of that... and it's all down the drain! It's enough to make you laugh!"

"...Nobody's laughing. There's not a single funny thing about it."

"Really? You think so?! But Onii-san, you hate us, don't you? Doesn't it feel good to see the people you hate crying? Feels great, doesn't it?! When you... when you were the only one who could satisfy us, who're sick and tired of eating... When only you could satisfy us, 'Satiation'!"

Looking at the bloodshot-eyed Rui, Subaru muttered "Satiation" under his breath.
　If he hadn't misheard, the title she had claimed for herself was supposed to be "Gluttony." How had that turned into "Satiation"?
　Before the bewildered Subaru, Rui shouted up at the white sky with an "In the first place!":

"Neither Lye the 'Gourmet' nor Roy of 'Bizarre Eating' understand a damn thing! Gorging themselves indiscriminately one after another like idiots... For the sake of us, who're locked up in here with no freedom of choice? Don't make us laugh, you useless brothers!"

Clutching her golden hair, Rui thrashed her body about, spitting as she ranted.
　Subaru couldn't comprehend the full meaning of her screeching tirade. Lye and Roy—were those names?
　However, from what he knew of the existence of "Gluttony" and memories, a few things became clear—

"You and your buddies are going around stealing other people's memories, and their names... is that how to put it? Anyway, you've been devouring that stuff nonstop. Right?"

Having his memories eaten, losing sight of who he was—that was Subaru.
　Having his name eaten, weeping in agony at being forgotten by everyone around him—that was Julius.
　And having both eaten, forgotten by the world and plunged into an slumber from which she could not wake—that was Rem.
　All of that was the work of "Gluttony"—of Rui and the comrades whose names she had just spat out—.

"Why the hell are you doing that? What's your goal?"

"—To become happy, of course."

"――――"

Hearing that instantaneous reply delivered in a single breath, Subaru choked on air.
　Paying no heed to his reaction, Rui clicked her teeth, her eyes gleaming with mental instability.

"To be happy. What other purpose could there be? Being happy is the point of living, isn't it? Or did you think that because we're hated, even that's twisted up? No. Not at all. It isn't. 'Cause it isn't. It's not. It's totally not. I'm telling you it's not! Because I said it's not! Ah, our soul aches...!"

"What does your goal of being happy have to do with stealing other people's memories...?"

"—Onii-san, haven't you ever thought that life is unfair?"

"I have."

"Aha!"

Sinking her teeth into the back of her own pale hand, Rui questioned Subaru. When Subaru nodded without hesitation, Rui gave a bitter sneer. "Thought so."
　Sneering, she ran that hand, marked with teeth indentations, across her slender chest.

"We have, too. Or rather, life is unfairness itself. You can't choose how you're born, you can't choose your parents, you can't choose your environment, you can't choose your future—you can't choose a single damn thing. The system's already built that way. You're already stuck on the conveyor belt."

"――――"

"—But what if it weren't like that?"

Before the silent Subaru, Rui tilted her head.

"What if you could choose how you're born? What if you could choose your parents? What if you could choose your environment? What if you could choose your future? What if every single option was just the way you wanted? ...Anyone would pick a better life, wouldn't they? Am I wrong?"

"That... might be true, but..."

Subaru wondered where the connection lay between that and Rui's atrocities.
　Yet to Subaru's doubt, Rui clicked her teeth.

"—That's it."

"...Huh?"

"If you could choose how you're born, if you could choose your parents, if you could choose your environment, if you could choose your future, if every option was yours to command, anyone would pick a better life. —That's why we're taking our time, searching with all our might for the greatest life for us."

"――――"

"It's gotta be out there somewhere! A rose-tinted future where we can hold our heads high, be ourselves, and truly feel glad that we lived this life! Until the moment we cross paths with that fateful life, we'll eat, bite, chew, lick, suck, gorge, binge! Feast!"

With eyes burning bright, Rui Arneb loudly proclaimed her magnificent ambition.
　From the bottom of her heart, she truly believed that this was the pursuit of happiness—the one and only way to seize the finest future for herself.

In her own life, Rui found neither hope nor expectation.
　Because to her, the life of the girl called Rui Arneb had had a terrible starting hand. The starting line was all wrong. —That was why she wanted to erase it.

She wanted to win a self blessed in every way: birth, parents, environment, future, talent.
　She had defined that as the indispensable condition for enjoying life to the fullest.
　Therefore—

"And for that, you steal other people's memories and devour them...?"

"Once we find the life we want, we'll slap on the 'Memories' and 'Name,' and live that life with our heads held high. Too bad everyone so far hasn't made the cut... There were some lives that came close, but at this point, we just can't be satisfied by ordinary, run-of-the-mill experiences anymore."

Speaking with fervent, trembling breath, Rui gently traced her slender fingers beneath her patchwork clothing, making a coquettish gesture unbecoming of her age—no, that wasn't it.
　If her words as "Gluttony" were true, then by devouring countless lives of others up until now, she had accumulated a volume of experience that an ordinary person could never fathom.

Men, women, children, the elderly, even leaping across the boundaries of species and living beings—she was a satiated consumer of life who had relished and exhausted every conceivable existence.

Just as Rui had called herself.
　She was satiated. Sick of eating the lives of others.
　To her, who had grazed continuously on the "tastiest parts" of tens of thousands of lives, every possible event was a mundane occurrence—stale, boring, lacking any novelty.

Yet once he understood Rui's mindset, something else instantly made no sense.
　Namely—

"If you're such a picky eater, why the hell did you go through such convoluted lengths just to take a bite out of me? Is it competitive eater pride, where you can't stand leaving scraps on your plate?"

"It's not for a boring reason like that. —Because you're our destiny, Onii-san."

"――――"

Taking her words at face value would make a fool of him, Subaru thought, glaring at Rui with anger and vigilance.
　Yet the passion in Rui's eyes as she looked at Subaru held no deceit. She was genuinely infatuated with Subaru—or more precisely, with Subaru's "life."
　Why? Because—

"Young and old, men and women, leaping across all kinds of humans, races, stations, and everything else, we've eaten so much—but there's only one thing we don't know. D'you know what it is?"

"Hell if I know. How to mourn your own worthless self, maybe?"

"—The experience of 'Death.'"

Subaru froze, one eye half-shut.
　Gazing at Subaru, Rui lifted her slender arms, turning both palms toward him.

"No matter how many memories of others we devour, it's impossible. The memories of 'Death' are the one thing we can never get our hands on. I mean, think about it! Memories are records of being alive. So memories of when you die don't exist. —Only you're an exception, Onii-san."

As if yearning for the power of "Return by Death" from the bottom of her heart, envying it, pining for it.
　To a girl who had grown weary of this world, she yearned for the only man who could grant her a fresh moment.

"Hey, hey, what does dying feel like? It's gotta be awful, right? Agonizing, right? Painful, right? It hurts, doesn't it? But there were times it didn't hurt, weren't there? Some people say it feels good—is that true? When you die, are you actually glad every time? Or have you stopped caring altogether? Is it a breeze? Hey, hey, hey, hey, hey, hey!"

"...If you have my memories up until yesterday, shouldn't you already know that?"

"As memories, sure! But those are old, and they don't feel real! We want a raw sensation. Stale, recycled ingredients aren't enough to satisfy us. What'll satisfy us is a fresh, juicy realm that nobody else knows!"

That's why, she continued:

"A special memory, the only one in this world, that no one else can ever experience! And not just that—the sheer convenience of being able to die and redo it right away whenever you mess up! Even after finding your best life, there's always the chance you'll ruin it with some screw-up, right? But with your life, Onii-san, that won't happen! Don't worry, we'll play the part so well nobody'll ever notice!"

"――――"

"Emilia, Beatrice, Ram, Meili, Julius, Echidna, Shaula, Patrasche, Petra, Otto, Garfiel, Frederica, Ryuzu, Roswaal, Clind, Annerose, Felt, Reinhard, Old Man Rom, the Ton-Chin-Kan trio, Crusch, Ferris, Wilhelm, Ricardo, Mimi, Hetaro, Tivey, Priscilla, Al, Schult, Heinkel, Kiritaka, Liliana—every single one of them, every last one, every last one! We'll fool them all, and live out a happy life to the very end!"

Holding out her thrust palms toward him, Rui adorably tilted her head.

"So please. —Let us eat your life until we're stuffed full, won't you?"

Begging like a child, she had surely chosen the most fitting plea, the most fitting way to act spoiled for this moment, from among the countless memories she possessed.

Proof that no matter what ingredients you gathered, it was meaningless if the chef was incompetent.
There are no bad ingredients, only bad cooks, was a favorite quote of Subaru's.
　Never had he felt the truth of it so keenly as right now.

Countless, boundless heaps of experience that ordinary humans could never hope to possess.
　Subaru had never laid eyes on a creature who squandered it so utterly.

"—There ain't gonna be a third time. My suffering, my deaths, my life, every damn bit of it is mine. There's not a single scrap I'm giving to you!"

"――――"

"Starve to death, you idiot. If you can only pick one way to die in your life, that's the one I'd recommend to you. —Suffer the most out of anyone in the whole damn world."

Drawing his thumb across his throat in a slicing gesture, Subaru declared it flatly.
　At his words, Rui's eyes went wide, and then she looked at her own hands. Covering her face with both hands, she looked up at the white sky and wailed, "Aaaaaah."

"We failed. We did. We messed up. We failed. It's ruined. We totally blew it. Because we blew it. Because we failed... Ah, aaaaaah!"

Her knees knocking together violently, Rui collapsed into a heap on the spot.
　Her genuine shock was proof of just how earnestly she had tried to persuade Subaru. Given that the result of that earnestness had been that pitch, her deranged mentality went without saying.
　As far as Subaru was concerned, he didn't have to drop his serve-you-right attitude for a second.

"Things aren't gonna go the way you want. My name is Natsuki Subaru. The name Kenichi Natsuki and Naoko Natsuki gave me. —Nothing more, nothing less. I am me."

"Even though you might get overwritten and disappear?"

"I'll teach you a magic spell. —That's that, and this is this."

The magic spell he had hurled at Julius, he would now hurl at himself.
　If he took back "Natsuki Subaru," the Subaru of this moment might disappear. But he might not. There might be a way not to disappear.
　They might find a way to somehow share that single patch of sunlight.

"A guy like me, who barges into other people's hearts with muddy boots on, has no business sitting politely in a patch of sunlight anyway. That's my answer. Go get a haircut, moron."

Spitting out those parting words, Subaru turned his back on Rui.
　He paid no heed to the girl slumped on the floor, clutching her head. Right now, finding a way out of this incomprehensible space was far more important.
　He had to return to Emilia and the others as soon as possible, and challenge Reid's "Book of the Dead" once more.
　In the first place, why had Reid's "Book of the Dead" been connected to this place—

"—Ah, geez. Guess there's nothing left but to leave it to Big Brother and Elder Brother."

"――――"

Behind the pondering Subaru, Rui muttered with a sigh.
　Hearing that, Subaru, who had been pressing his ear to the floor and tapping the ground, turned around. Rui was lying atop her golden hair, hands covering her face, kicking her legs in frustration.

Big Brother, and Elder Brother.
　Titles Rui had uttered several times now. If they were who Subaru suspected—

"—Lye and Roy? The 'Gourmet' and 'Bizarre Eating'?"

"We can't get out of here. So if Big Brother and Elder Brother don't eat for us, we can't even choose what to eat. ...That's why we asked them."

"Asked them..."

Chewing over words that gave him a terrible premonition, Subaru urged her to continue.
　The wait for Rui's next words felt excruciatingly long. Eventually, as if tracing Subaru's anxiety, Rui's red lips trembled:

"This is the second time you've come here since last night, Onii-san. —So Big Brother and Elder Brother both noticed. Where you are, Onii-san."

"――――"

—She revealed that those two threats were closing in on the Pleiades Watchtower.

"The two of them are super curious about you, Onii-san. I mean, of course they would be. —After all, you've got a whole mountain of experiences they've never tasted before, Onii-san."

"Dammit...! You...!"

"—Ah, that's not fair."

Leaving aside Subaru, who trembled upon learning that fact, Rui suddenly murmured.
　The meaning of her disconnected mutter became clear to Subaru an instant later.

"Is this the exit?!"

Behind Subaru as he turned around, a fissure formed in the white space, and the other side could be seen wavering.
　An unmistakable anomaly, a spatial leap that should not exist, was occurring right there.
　Resonating with Subaru's will—his need to return to the archives.

"――――"

"You can't do it. There's no way you can do it, Onii-san. Even though we wanted you to."

Faced with the exit, before stepping through, Subaru hesitated over what to do with Rui.
　Given how she had been throttled, and her attitude after laying bare her true motives, it was likely true that she possessed no more combat ability than she appeared to have.
　Therefore, it was physically possible for Subaru to finish Rui off right here.
　The moment he directed a wavering gaze toward her with that thought in mind, she saw right through his intentions.

"Whether driven by suspicion or despair, you can't kill yourself, you can't kill others, and you can't even kill someone you hate. Spineless coward. —Even though we licked you so gently."

"—. No thanks, your teeth get in the way. Go get braces, idiot."

Subaru flipped off Rui's bitter face as he delivered his parting shot.
　Without waiting to see her reaction, Subaru moved to throw himself into the spatial fissure. Right before he did, a momentary hesitation arose.

It wasn't that he was reluctant to leave Rui behind. He was glad to be rid of the sight of that face.
　What Subaru was reluctant to part with was not Rui, but the voice that had commanded him to stand.

The girl who had appeared for just that single instant to rouse Natsuki Subaru.
　The girl who had been stripped of her memories and name, and who, for that very reason, was the only one who could call out to Subaru at the edge of the world.

"It's fine. —I remember the promise."

Natsuki Subaru would surely never forget it.
　That was why they would surely meet again.

—Hoping that next time, he would get to hear not just her stern voice, but her gentle one as well.

"――――"

Bolstered by that resolve, Subaru leaped into the fissure to return to his friends.
