Arc 3 Chapter 84: "Just That Story"

—Before he knew it, Subaru's consciousness had once again been led into a world of darkness.

A hazy, wandering consciousness. Every organ—hands, feet, eyes, ears—was gone, and the being called Natsuki Subaru drifted through the void as a consciousness that was nothing but a name. In this world there was never any ground, nor any visible end. Only darkness spread on endlessly, everything dyed pitch black, shadow blanketing all.

But though Subaru had been drawn here unconsciously just as before, there was one thing clearly different from every time before. In this everlasting darkness, where until now even his memories had been hazy and holding onto his own will had been difficult, Subaru could now clearly recognize his own existence.

He had no hands, no feet, no body—he could not translate will into action. And yet he was allowed to guide his will into thought, to let his mind range over reason.

A world of fleeting, bubble-like dreams, forgotten the moment he woke. A garden of shadow that always brought Subaru a nostalgia that squeezed at his chest. A beloved "someone" he met every time he came here, whose face and voice he never knew.

The darkness before him wavered, and he sensed the shadow slowly taking on a human shape. A slender body was born out of pitch black, its right and left hands each clearly joined to it. What had once been a presence with only a single hand had regained both arms, and the palms that took shape reached toward him as if seeking out his existence.

I want to touch her, I want to reach for her—Subaru's heart was captivated, reflexively. But he had no hand to respond, no mouth to speak the words even if he wished to—so he could do nothing.

Subaru could not touch her, and she could not touch Subaru. Just to reach for Subaru, how much time had she spent building that body? On how much time, and how much sacrifice, did those outstretched arms stand? Even as he unconsciously grasped some fragment of that truth, Subaru was crushed by his own helplessness.

"'…I love you.'"

The shadow, its unseen face surely twisted in anguish, whispered its love to Subaru. He wanted to answer that word, felt he had to answer it. He wanted to repay it with voice, with action, with love. Love offered could only be met with love in return. And yet—

"...Subaru."

Someone's voice was calling Subaru's name.

His formless consciousness realized the voice hadn't come from the her before him, but from behind. Eyes that shouldn't have existed looked back. Beyond the darkness, a pinprick of light floated. The voice was, without doubt, coming from there.

'I love you.' "Subaru."

The words were cast at him at the same time. He wanted to answer the love. He had to answer the call. The voice of the her standing before him, the voice of someone reaching from the light behind him—led on, drawn in, Subaru's consciousness slowly, slowly drifted away from the garden of shadow.

Pulled, as if by a thread, toward the light. Sensing that turn in Subaru's heart, the voice before him trembled with anguish.

'I love you, I love you, I love you, I love you, I love you, I love you, I love you—' "...Please, Subaru."

The whispered love, repeated over and over. A name called with nothing but pure feeling behind it. He remembered his own name. And he remembered the person who called it that way. He remembered what he had to do. He remembered where he had to go. —So he could not stay here.

'I lo—'

"This time, I swear, I'll be the one to come see you."

A mouth that didn't exist, a will that shouldn't have been able to reach anywhere, cut through the words and announced his decision. The voice fell silent. The distance grew. His consciousness was swallowed from the world of shadow into the world of light, wrapped in pure white brilliance, and vanished.

'…I'll be waiting.'

Leaving behind only that murmur at the last, Subaru's consciousness melted into the light—

Consciousness rose up out of the sea of sleep, breaking through the surface of waking, and his eyelids opened. The sunlight stung his newly-woken eyes so badly they welled up, and through his blurred vision Subaru saw a pair of faintly glowing violet eyes. They were right there, close enough that he could feel breath on his face, beautiful enough to steal his gaze, and an actual breath reached him from a pair of pink lips—he panicked to death.

"Your face is close!?"

"Wah, ah, Subaru, you're awake! Oh, thank goodness. Really."

Realizing that what had been so close was Emilia's eyes, and that her face was near enough to feel her breath, Subaru shot instantly from hazy awareness to full wakefulness. Emilia looked at him flailing in a panic and, with no idea how hard his heart was pounding, let out a relieved sigh and pressed a hand to her chest.

Dammit, she's cute. Even as his eyes followed that gesture of hers, Subaru noticed that the angle of their positions relative to each other was oddly off, and—

"I'm lying down, and Emilia-tan's kneeling properly. And factoring in that at this distance, there's this heavenly sensation under my head..."

"You don't need to work it out so strangely, it's a lap pillow. Was it comfortable?"

"How peacefully do you think I was sleeping? There's no way it was bad. As a reward for hard work, it doesn't get better than this."

Without any reserve, still leaving the weight of his head on her knee, Subaru gave a light laugh. Seeing that smile of his, Emilia gently firmed up her mouth, which had gone soft with relief. The air shifted. Now that they'd confirmed each other's safety, there was more that needed saying.

"Um, can I ask what happened with everything?"

"I'm the one who wants to ask what happened with everything. ...You mean after the magic stone Subaru grabbed from the dragon carriage exploded, right? It really was awful."

Still in the same position, Emilia lowered her eyes and began recounting the events, bit by bit. How Subaru had leapt from the dragon carriage without any explanation, and mounted the earth dragon and ridden off far faster than she could try to follow. How, trying to chase after him, she'd been held back by Puck and Otto, and had nearly fainted at the sight of the pillar of flame rising in the distance. How, frantically searching for Subaru, they had gone to the fallen Flugel's Tree and found Subaru and Patrasche collapsed there. How Patrasche seemed to have thrown herself over Subaru as if to shield him.

"What happened to Patrasche? I sort of remember her licking my face all over right before I lost consciousness."

"She kept licking you even after you passed out. When we tried to separate you from her to treat you, she growled something fierce. If that peddler—Otto-kun—hadn't talked to her, we might not have even been able to start treatment."

"You brute, Patrasche. How rude, to Emilia-tan of all people... No, I guess it means she was trying to protect me no matter who came near. What a loyal dragon. I'm falling for her."

He'd only known Patrasche for a day and a half or so, but the number of brushes with death they'd shared had already climbed to the top of the list since he'd entered this world. Granted, that was partly because this loop in particular had been one crisis after another. In truth, he never would have made it this far without Patrasche's help. She was, strictly speaking, just an earth dragon borrowed from Crusch—but if Crusch was going to give him a reward—

"I'll take Patrasche, that's what I'll do. Let's stick together for a long time, partner... Anyway, so, where's Patrasche now?"

"Her burns were bad, but it doesn't seem life-threatening. I did the first aid, but right now Ferris is looking after her, along with Wilhelm-san."

"Ah... so Ferris has joined up too."

Ferris's name coming up in the flow of talk about treatment let Subaru relax, the tension easing from his shoulders. Setting aside all the mean-spirited things Ferris said and did to him, there was no doubting his skill as a healer. Patrasche's treatment would surely go smoothly. At the same time, the fact that Ferris—who should have been badly delayed leaving the village—had already caught up meant...

"Don't tell me I was out for a really long time?"

"I think about four or five hours. If it weren't for the conversation mirror, we'd probably still be rushing to the royal capital trying to find a healer right about now."

As she spoke, what Emilia held in her hand was one of the conversation mirrors Petelgeuse and his people had used. One had been Julius's, one Ferris's, and the last had been Subaru's own—apparently she'd borrowed it from inside his coat after he'd lost consciousness. Probably because she'd noticed a call coming in from Ferris. Thanks to being able to make contact, Subaru's treatment had gone ahead too.

"So, I guess that means everyone's together now?"

"Ferris is, yes... and Julius too. I was so surprised. I never could have imagined Subaru and Julius together."

"There's a reason bluer than the sea and greener than the mountains behind that, Emilia-tan. Explaining it from scratch would honestly just be depressing, or something like that..."

The relationship between Julius and Subaru that had surprised Emilia was hard to put into words. Or rather, even now Subaru himself wasn't entirely sure what kind of relationship it was. An infuriating rival he couldn't stand; a stylish, unlikable-but-genuinely-capable ally; a comrade-in-arms who'd watched his back in a situation where either of their mistakes could have cost the other his life. In other words, if he had to sum the man up in one word—

"I. Hate. Him."

"Why are you suddenly talking in broken sentences?"

"I was trying to express my feelings about a guy who defies description, in as few words as I could manage. You can guess how well that went. ...Anyway, where's everyone now?"

Continuing that topic any further felt too awkward, so he changed the subject. Emilia gave him a helpless little smile at that, and, saying "Let's see," went on,

"We're resting until Ferris finishes up all the treatment. But I think that'll be done soon, so then we'll set off for the royal capital again, I think. It seems we need to talk with Crusch-san, thanks to how hard Subaru worked."

"Oh man, I worked crazy hard. I was seriously playing an away game the whole time, and I busted through it with a ton of bluffing and cheating. Just thinking back on it still makes my stomach hurt!"

"Yeah, really... thank you."

Being thanked so plainly by Emilia, Subaru instantly felt embarrassed about the cocky thing he'd just said, and managed only an "Ah, well." Still, it was a fact that the results of his actions had turned out to be a plus for her. There was no need to hide it anymore, anyway.

Coming to that realization anew, a plain, honest joy welled up inside Subaru. Over all these days, it felt like he'd piled up a great deal of memory since that moment that had brought about their parting. For every repetition, Subaru had stacked up time that Emilia knew nothing about. Using everything he'd gained across that time to the fullest, Subaru had earned this moment. Its results were rightly something to be praised, and he had that much confidence in them.

He'd done this much. He'd built up this much. He'd finally reached here. So this time, Subaru felt, he had finally earned the right to make that moment—the stupid words he'd blurted out back then—mean what they were supposed to mean.

Looking around, it seemed they were in the bed of a dragon carriage with its canopy open. There was no sign of anyone else nearby—just Subaru and Emilia, the two of them alone. He had no idea what the rest of the group was doing, but in the silence all he could hear was the rustle of the wind, and the world felt cut away, belonging to just the two of them.

Strangely enough, the situation was the same as back then. Covered in wounds, waking from unconsciousness, in a place with just the two of them.

"I feel like I've been dreaming for a long, long time..."

In truth, everything that had happened between the moment of that parting and this loop—this final loop—felt as unreal as if it had all been some kind of dream. That was how many times he'd been pushed to the extreme, how many times he'd been flung down into the Abyss of despair. Truly, there was no way to describe it except as one nightmare after another—

"A bad dream... no, that's not right."

"Was it... a good dream?"

Tilting her head, Emilia picked up where Subaru's words left off and turned it into a question. At her words, Subaru closed his eyes and looked back on the days he'd been about to call a "nightmare." They had, indeed, been one hopeless situation after another—never anything he could look back on and laugh about, and if he could, a string of scenes he'd rather erase from memory entirely.

He'd rushed into folly, behaved selfishly, forced his own willfulness on others, had his expectations cruelly betrayed, been struck down by disappointment and despair, fallen into a collapse of the spirit, given himself over to the edge of madness, been ruled by resignation until he nearly threw everything away—and from that, he had been saved.

None of it could be undone. Without it, the Subaru of today wouldn't be here. So even though those had been dreamlike days, a stretch of time where nothing but suffering caught his eye—

"It was good. It was real."

That long, long, nightmarish stretch of time remained only within Subaru. He could make it his past. But he wasn't allowed to make it a dream. He would carry with him every result of every tragedy his own actions had produced, every catastrophe he'd brought about. That was the cross Subaru had to bear—Subaru, who had been seized by the power beyond common sense called Return by Death, and who had used that power to carve open a future.

"How much have you heard about it?"

"Almost nothing at all. Julius said I should hear it from Subaru."

"That bastard, so damn nosy."

Did he think he was being considerate or something? That nice-guy assist was so on-point it made him want to gag. Subaru cursed at Julius's thoughtfulness with a mischievous grin, then let out a breath. Holding his breath lightly, he looked at Emilia, and,

"That day, you asked me 'why,' didn't you. Why I'd help you. Why I'd try so hard at so many things. Why, you asked."

"Yeah, I asked. And you said it was because I'd saved Subaru. ...But I haven't done anything like that. I haven't been able to do anything at all. I'm the one who's always being saved by Subaru, and I can't do anything for you. And even so, Subaru got hurt, saying it was for my sake..."

"No, back then I was out of my mind, or..."

I was out of my mind, he tried to say outright—and found he couldn't finish the thought. He hadn't been out of his mind. At that point, the man called Natsuki Subaru—weak, foolish, thinking of nothing but himself—had believed that was the right thing. He'd simply assumed that his overbearing, self-satisfied feelings would be received, just like that.

Subaru knew the end that had come to a man who'd loudly insisted on the existence of that kind of self-serving love. He'd experienced it in his own flesh, and had shattered that love with his own two hands. And he'd also burned into his eyes the figure of someone who had proven, rightly, the existence of a love that was truly given.

"Back then, I was only thinking about myself. I'll admit it. I said it was for your sake, but really I was just drunk on the idea of 'a me who works hard for your sake.' I assumed, all on my own, that if I kept acting drunk like that, you'd just accept it."

"Subaru..."

"I'm sorry. I used you, and reveled in it. Every word you said back then was right. I was the one who was wrong. ...But there's one thing that wasn't wrong."

He had used Emilia for his own sake. Subaru affirmed her tearful cry from that time. But looking back at the self who'd been rejected in that scene, there was still one thing he couldn't concede.

"I want to save you. I want to be your strength. That's serious, that's real, that's no lie."

"...Yeah, I know."

Emilia nodded at Subaru's words. Then her violet eyes wavered greatly, and she blinked once and looked at Subaru. And then,

"Why do you help me?"

The words she'd said back then. The words asked again just hours ago. And now, once more, the same words held out in search of an answer. Subaru's answer was just one thing.

"—Because I love Emilia. That's why I want to be your strength."

Looking straight back into her eyes, Subaru told her that, clearly.

—In the end, the origin of everything Subaru did came down to that single, utterly simple thing. Wanting to be her strength, wanting to be at her side, wanting to save her, wanting to see her smile, wanting to stand beside her, wanting to live a future with her.

All of it, every bit, from the top of his head to the tips of his toes, down to his very Soul, with everything he had—he loved Emilia.

That was why, even facing death, even actually dying over and over, even getting hurt and hated and suffering, Subaru clawed his way back up, sank his teeth in, and came back like this. Just to arrive at that single answer, how many roundabout paths had he taken? He was appalled at his own stupidity.

"――――"

Hearing Subaru's answer, Emilia closed her lips and chose silence. Looking up at her with a strangely calm feeling, Subaru—who a moment ago had been full of a sense of accomplishment—suddenly realized something serious.

—Wait, isn't this me having just seriously confessed and now waiting for an answer?

For Subaru, who in his whole life had never confessed to anyone or been confessed to, this was the first ridiculous situation of its kind he'd ever faced. Okay, lie—he had been confessed to. In this world, too, by the sweet, sweet Rem. But that and this were different matters. The whole business of his feelings for Rem and the whole business of his feelings for Emilia might point in the same direction, but they were separate issues.

As Subaru, alone, kept his expression unchanged while agonizing inside, Emilia's own expression suddenly crumbled. She bit her closed lips, and her wide-open violet eyes narrowed just slightly, looking as if she might burst into tears at any moment.

"I-I'm... a Half-Elf."

"I know."

In a trembling voice, Emilia reconfirmed something far too obvious at this point. She caught her breath at Subaru's instant answer, then shook her head, and,

"Silver hair, a Half-Elf... just because I look like the Witch, all sorts of people avoid me and hate me. Really, terribly, they hate me."

"I saw it. I know. They're a bunch of people with no eyes."

Getting swept up by nothing but appearance, and on top of that because she resembled some great sinner of the past—how absurd. Without seeing a single thing about who she truly was, who had any right to call themselves someone who hated her?

"I don't have much experience with people, so I don't have friends or anything. I don't know much about the world, so I sometimes say strange things... and, because of the Contract, my hairstyle's different almost every day, and even my reason for having to become king is... really, really selfish..."

In the way she tried to list her flaws and ended up saying even things that didn't need saying, he caught a glimpse of just how flustered she was. And even that fragility—trying to put her own lack of self-confidence into words and affirm it—felt precious to him now. So,

"No matter what anyone says to Emilia, no matter what you think of yourself, I love you. I love you a lot. I love you so much. I want to stay by your side forever. I want to hold your hand forever."

"Ah..."

"If you can name ten things you hate about yourself, I'll name two thousand things I love about you."

He raised himself up off Emilia's lap, matched the height of his gaze to hers, and said it looking her in the eye. Her mouth fell slightly open, and as she looked at Subaru, her eyes welled up with tears before his very eyes. Large drops spilled out the instant she blinked, tracing clear paths down her white cheeks.

"That's how I want to treat you—as my own kind of 'special treatment.'"

"...Special treatment that makes me happy to receive... this is the first time in my life."

He reached out a hand and gently caught the tears spilling down. Emilia's palm came to rest over Subaru's hand where it touched her cheek, and they exchanged each other's fiercely warm body heat. She closed her eyes, and, still feeling Subaru's fingertips on her cheek,

"Why two thousand?"

"Because a hundred times isn't enough to express how I feel."

Subaru grinned, showing his teeth, and Emilia let a tearful smile show through as well. That smile was so dazzling, even the spilling tears looked like jewels, and he found it funny how cheap a man he must be, to feel this satisfied over a single smile. Laughing like that, Emilia rubbed her cheek against Subaru's fingertips, and,

"I'm happy. I'm really, really happy. I never even imagined a day would come when someone would tell me they loved me."

In all of Emilia's days up until now, "special" had meant nothing more than discrimination. That was why she'd been terribly afraid of anyone giving her "special treatment." And knowing that about her, knowing how she felt, Subaru gave her "special treatment" anyway. Even if no one else in the world would, Subaru alone would.

"What should I do? Subaru's telling me how you feel like that, but... I don't know what I should do."

"You don't have to rush. I'm not exactly asking for an answer right away. Eventually, in a proper form, and getting a yes out of you—that's already a settled matter, as far as I'm concerned."

With so little experience of being loved by anyone, she was, in that respect, like a small child—and it was endearing. He wouldn't rush her, wouldn't push her for an answer. He wanted to be the one she turned that feeling toward, once she understood more fully what it meant to be loved by someone, once she came to know that she, too, could love someone. He resolved, fiercely, to be the kind of man worthy of having that feeling turned toward him.

"Is it... okay? For me... for someone like me to get given nothing but things that make me this happy. To feel this happy, this... extravagant..."

"It's fine, isn't it. Let's be extravagant. It's not like too much happiness ever hurt anyone—if it overflows and there's some left over, you can just hand it out to other people."

—So.

"Take your time, Emilia. Slowly, thoroughly, at your own pace—it's fine if you fall for me that way. I'll do my best to make you head over heels for me, walking right there beside you."

A small "hiu" caught in Emilia's throat. Just like that, she put a hand to her cheek and began to let tears she could no longer hold back spill down, one after another.

Reaching out a hand, Subaru stroked her long, beautiful silver hair as if combing through it. Softly, softly, gently.

All the while, Emilia kept on crying and crying. And Subaru kept on and on, stroking that beloved girl of his, endlessly.

—Beneath a sky drawing toward evening, an outlander and a silver-haired half-demon leaned close together, exchanging their feelings.

A long, long repetition of hardship and despair. A calm, quiet time finally won by overcoming all of it.

This is nothing but a story whose sole purpose was to obtain this time. A story of nothing but taking the long way around, continuing to miss each other, continuing to lose their way.

One unconfident boy conveys his feelings to one unconfident girl. He worked hard for just that alone—a story of nothing more.
