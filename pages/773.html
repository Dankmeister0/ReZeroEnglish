Arc 10 Chapter 14: "Traitor"

—The Eyes of the Dragon. That was the phrase that intuitively sprang to Subaru's mind.

Not Earth Dragons, Water Dragons, or Flying Dragons, but true "Dragons"—opportunities to set eyes upon beings worthy of being called as such were hardly common. Even so, having witnessed the "Divine Dragon" Volcanica at the pinnacle of the Pleiades Watchtower and the "Cloud Dragon" Mezoreia in the Vollachian Empire, Subaru likely possessed a relatively high amount of "Dragon" experience.

However, it was not that abundant witness experience that gave Subaru this intuition. This was a sensation rooted far deeper, in the primal instincts of a living creature.

When standing face-to-face with a Dragon, one felt it—the difference in tier as a living organism. A sense of awe toward an existence residing on a fundamentally different plane of life—that was what humans felt toward Dragons, without exception. That exact feeling was the confirmation.

The sensation Subaru felt upon seeing Crusch's left eye after she removed her eyepatch was precisely that.

"――――"

A non-human eye, shining gold and imbued with an unearthly light—Crusch, harboring what could only be called a "Dragon Eye" within her socket, made Subaru's very soul quiver in fear.

What had happened to her? What did that left eye signify? Why would she not drop her sword even with her wind sealed? How had things come to this? The swirling vortex of helpless questions churning in his mind was perhaps an instinctive defense mechanism, born from the fear of facing the reality before his eyes. Swallowing down those convoluted rationalizations, Subaru somehow managed to open his mouth—

"—Ah."

—He felt as though his heart had been pierced through by her golden-lit gaze.

"—Shamak!!"

In that instant, replacing Subaru whose movements had frozen, Beatrice thrust out her hand and chanted. Grasping Subaru's reluctance to fight Crusch, the spell she chose was Shamak, the Yin magic that detached an opponent's consciousness from reality—an exceptionally effective spell for neutralizing a target, cast with a level of proficiency incomparably superior to Subaru's own.

It should have enveloped Crusch's head, severing her fighting spirit from this place—or so it was supposed to. However—

"—I can see it."

Crusch nonchalantly dodged the swirling black haze that had materialized without warning in midair, simply by tilting her head.

"—kh, Shamak! Shamak, Shamak, Shamak!"

Swallowing her shock at her initial strike being dodged, Beatrice unleashed black hazes into the air in rapid succession. Crusch dodged every single one of the relentless, chaining hazes closing in to engulf her with agile movements, and ultimately struck her sword against the magic at its inception, shattering the spell formula itself.

Dispelling magic before it could activate—since all magic only takes effect once gathered mana is given direction, the logic that destroying its structure prevents it from forming was understandable, but...

"Actually executing that is a completely different story, isn't it?!"

Displaying the divine feat of cutting down magic before it could even become magic, Tiga, his voice laced with marvel, lunged at Crusch from the side. Demonstrating the dexterity to attack simultaneously with scimitar and kick, Tiga pressed forward, and the battle between him and Crusch, whose sword tip danced in response, resumed. However, that was—

"I believe I told you. I can see it."

As Crusch declared so quietly, even a layman could tell her movements had clearly changed. The change was obvious not just by sight, but by sound as well—because unlike their fight a moment ago, something had disappeared from their battle. Sound. The clashes of steel against steel, the spark-flying swordplay—no longer were they exchanged.

Tiga's slashes, his kicks—Crusch dodged them not with her sword, but purely with her body movements. Even an amateur could tell which maneuver carried greater composure: blocking or dodging. And what made that possible was—

"—The Dragon Eye."

Subaru swallowed hard, gulping down an unsuppressible marvel. Most likely, the actual speed of Crusch's movements hadn't changed all that much. What had changed drastically was not the speed of her movement, but the speed of her initiation. In a battle where lives were exchanged in split-second offensives and defensives, if one person obtained "something" that shaved off a tenth of a second from every single moment, that person's advantage would be immeasurable. What Crusch was doing right now was erasing that tenth of a second.

"Guh... kh!"

As if supporting Subaru's hypothesis, the tides of the duel inside the office tilted abruptly. Tiga, who had been unleashing a variety of dynamic techniques with his whole body, and who had managed to clash head-on with Crusch after a fashion, had his sword dance interrupted and was beginning to be forced entirely onto the defensive.

His attacks smothered at their inception, blades thrust into the gaps between his combinations, Tiga—his freedom of movement sealed off—had impatience showing on his previously composed profile as he barely resisted. However, even devoting himself entirely to defense did not leave him unscathed. Blood seeped from his shoulder and thigh, until finally blood trickled from a slash across his cheek, the dripping trails of red telling the tale of his desperate struggle.

At this rate, it wouldn't be long before Tiga was added to the victims of the McMahon manor. Now that Miklotov was already beyond saving, that was a worst-case scenario that could not be allowed to happen. Therefore—

"Oooooooh!!"

While Tiga tried to suppress her with his sword and Beatrice with her magic, Subaru roared, snapping the whip he drew from behind his waist and sending the tip of Guiltywhip howling through the air.

From the moment he decided to use E・M・T, Subaru's objective was to subdue Crusch. Leaving the battle up to Beatrice and the others until now wasn't solely because he was bewildered and stunned by Crusch's change. It was for this single surprise attack. Faster than steel, faster than magic, the crack of Guiltywhip closed in on Crusch. No matter what the trick behind the Dragon Eye was, dealing with an unfamiliar attack—

"—I have seen that before."

"—O-oh."

In an instant, struck by two shocks, a pathetic breath leaked from Subaru's lungs. The first shock was that his heavily prepared surprise attack had been thwarted with ease. Before the whip released from Subaru's hand could fully extend, Crusch brought her blade to meet it, knocking its tip down to the floor. An attack that should have been the fastest in this room—having it chopped down so effortlessly was worthy of marvel. But compared to what the second shock brought, it was nothing.

The second shock—that was witnessing the poignant emotion harbored within Crusch's eye as she struck away Guiltywhip—not her Dragon Eye, but her natural amber right eye.

"—I don't get it."

To speak bluntly, that gaze of hers looked as though she was about to burst into tears, leaving him with nothing but confusion. Crusch was the one acting weirdly, yet it felt as if Subaru, trying to restrain her, was being treated like the bad guy. Even though he had literally been killed once, this wasn't on the level of "a beautiful woman's tears are unfair."

Of course, if called a baseless accusation, that would be the end of it. In reality, Crusch remained expressionless with her lips pressed tightly together, merely casting a glance at Subaru holding the handle of the swatted whip. —Still, for some reason, he thought: Isn't she on the verge of tears?

—I'll stop those tears.

"—kh!" "Careless—!"

Setting aside Subaru, who gritted his teeth at the emotion flickering in Crusch's eyes, Tiga—who interpreted things differently but spotted an opening—flashed his shamshir with a ferocious movement. The white blade of the scimitar danced, howling like a beast's fangs as it closed in on Crusch's slender frame—

"I told you, I can see it!"

In a split second, twisting her body in line with the arc of the flashing curved blade and dodging the strike so closely it grazed her skin, Crusch raised her voice and slammed her rising knee into Tiga's chin. With a muffled groan of "Gah!", Tiga reeled backward, and a succession of flashing slashes rained down upon him, spraying his blood across the office as he instinctively guarded his vital spots. As his feet staggered back, a powerful kick pierced his torso. Blown straight back, Tiga collided spine-first into the wall and collapsed heavily. However—

"I'll gather your bones, my friend!"

Using the brief moment Tiga had bought him, Subaru closed in on Crusch. He swung his whip, targeting the hand holding Crusch's sword—

"I can see that too."

Throwing out those words, Crusch dodged the whip stroke with a mere flick of her wrist, her turning sword tip transitioning into a thrust aimed straight at the leg of Subaru, who had carelessly closed the distance. That silver glint was about to pierce the top of his foot, forcing Subaru to pay the toll for his reckless advance—

"Beatrice!"

"—kh, you're a partner who asks for nothing but the impossible, I suppose!"

With a single shout, Beatrice grasped Subaru's intent and cursed under her breath, throwing her whole heart into using her tiny body to grant her partner's wish. The moment the blade reached him, it activated—the ultimate defensive magic, E・M・M.

"――――"

An original magic created by Subaru and Beatrice, shifting their existence half a step out of reality to reject all physical and magical interference—the ultimate defense activated. Even so, pulled off as a feat within the active field of E・M・T, using E・M・M inside it was a first, and its success was nothing short of a miracle—

"I'll make love calls morning, noon, and night mandatory, I suppose!"

"I love you, Beako!"

Paying the cute price demanded for this miracle on the spot, Subaru immediately shifted gears from the fact that her thrust had failed, locking his sights on Crusch as she readied her next move. In exchange for an ironclad defense, E・M・M suffered from the flaw of immobilizing the user. The instant he took another step, the invincibility period would end. The next tip of her blade would mercilessly pierce him. Therefore—

"Invisible Providence!"

Before her next strike could fly, he played his cards in rapid succession. Gushing blood from his nose, an invisible black arm extended from Subaru's chest, grabbing the arm of Crusch holding her sword and forcefully halting the follow-up attack she was about to unleash. The invisible arm squeezed Crusch's wrist tightly, making her drop her sword and disarming her. Her actions thwarted in rapid succession by cards Subaru held that she didn't know about, Subaru lunged at her remaining arm with his whole body, pinning her against the wall with three arms combined with his unseen hand.

"Crusch-san! Listen to me!"

Grabbing both her wrists and forcibly pressing Crusch against the wall, Subaru appealed to her. Even then, Crusch twisted her body, attempting to drive a knee into Subaru's flank, but he forcefully leaned his body into hers, pressing so close their breath mingled to seal her movements. Right before his eyes, Crusch's heterochromatic pair of eyes—amber and gold—were staring at Subaru. Staring straight back into them,

"This... no matter how you look at this, it's wrong! It's all misaligned! I won't let things keep going like this!"

"Natsuki Subaru..."

"I... I won't accept any of this! I won't accept it!!"

Crusch's cheeks stiffened at Subaru roaring at the top of his lungs. In the end, his main point wasn't much different from when he had whined before Return by Death. Even so, compared to earlier when he had merely stood frozen and screamed, he wanted to believe that now—having given Crusch room to ponder—they could reach a different outcome.

Miklotov's life, and the lives of those at the McMahon manor, would not come back. Even so, there had to be a way other than sitting idly by and awaiting the worst possible end. To achieve that, it was indispensable for Crusch to lower her sword here. He would persuade her—

"—Eh?"

Expecting that and waiting for Crusch's response, a raspy breath slipped from Subaru's throat. The reason was what happened right in front of him—Crusch's mismatched eyes suddenly sharpened, and using the recoil from bouncing her back off the wall, she forcibly dragged Subaru down with her. Taking the surprise attack head-on, Subaru pathetically collapsed onto the spot. —However, that was not a counterattack aimed at Subaru. Rather, it was the exact opposite.

—Because in the very next instant, a raging hellfire of tremendous heat swallowed Subaru's vision in brilliant red.

△▼△▼△▼

—Feeling as though he heard a distant sound like a steam whistle, his consciousness slowly floated upward.

Breaching the surface of awakening, the first thing he felt as he emerged was a stingingly sharp pain. Feeling it intensely prickling his whole body, especially his right arm, a pained sound escaped his throat without thinking.

"—Ugh."

"—! Subaru, you're awake, I suppose? Are you alright, I suppose?"

A voice immediately noticing Subaru's awakening upon hearing that leaked groan. That familiar voice belonged to Beatrice, his partner who greeted the morning with him every day. However, an awakening accompanied by pain like this was vastly different from their usual daily routine.

"Bea... ko...? What happened to me..."

"You shouldn't force yourself to sit up, I suppose. Your burns are still severe, I suppose. ...If Betty had enough mana, she would heal you right away, so I'm sorry, I suppose."

"You don't need to apologize... Burns?"

Hesitantly opening his eyelids, he saw Beatrice's downcast face peering down at him. Seeing the Great Spirit with sadness welling in her large round eyes, he started to instinctively comfort her grief, but stopped. Hearing the word "burns," his dormant brain began to awaken, lagging behind his body. That's right. Just before losing consciousness, he saw flames. And before that—

"—Crusch-san!"

Recalling the tragedy at the McMahon manor, Subaru sat up with momentum. Once remembered, a vivid flood of memories began to restore itself all at once. Miklotov's death, Crusch confessing that she did it with her own hands, the scene where he tried to subdue her, and the Dragon Eye dwelling in the left eye of the resisting Crusch—things had moved with far too overwhelming momentum. However, Crusch came first. At the very end, using E・M・M and Invisible Providence, Subaru had succeeded in subduing her. After that, he saw sudden flames—

"Right, an incredible blaze flared up... Is my arm hurting because of those burns? Damn it, it stings...! But this is nothing—"

"C-calm down, I suppose! I haven't been able to cast healing magic on you due to a lack of mana, I suppose! If you don't rest quietly, you might catch a terrible disease, I suppose!"

"Now's not the time for that! Right now, Crusch-san comes before me. Me collapsing with a fever is completely whatever compared to—"

—that, he declared, just as he was about to brush off Beatrice's concern.

"—It seems you have awakened, Natsuki Subaru."

"Eh?"

Suddenly hearing an unexpected voice, Subaru's eyes widened. Stepping forward from the gloom in the back of Subaru's field of vision was Crusch, the very person he was about to go looking for. Having re-covered her left eye with her eyepatch and illuminating the surroundings with a Lagmite ore lamp in her hand, Crusch stepped over to Subaru and Beatrice while they were arguing, taking his right arm.

"As I thought, this is not a wound that can be left alone. It is merely first aid, but take this."

As she spoke, Crusch placed the lamp at her feet and pulled an embroidered white handkerchief from her breast, gently wrapping it around Subaru's right arm as a substitute bandage. Watching the handkerchief become soiled as it touched his throbbing burn wound, contrasted with the gentleness of Crusch's hands as she wrapped it, Subaru blinked in bewilderment, unable to catch up with the situation.

"—? What is wrong? A wind of confusion is blowing."

"...Well yeah, it's blowing at gale force! In my memory, up until just a moment ago, we were, like, going at each other with frantic expressions, right?"

"I believe you were the only one whose expression was frantic, Sir Natsuki."

"I'm talking overall! Between the two of us, if I had a frantic face, that means fifty percent of us were frantic, right?!"

"I see. That does sound like it makes sense, in a way."

Nodding with a deadpan face, Crusch left Subaru—who had countered banter with banter—with no choice but to stand dumfounded. Only after that did Subaru finally realize they were conversing in a strange location—a damp corridor that could hardly be called hygienic. He had been laid on a cold, hard floor; the air was clammy with a fishy odor, and once he actively noticed it, the space reeked of sewage to a degree that was hard to ignore.

"Or rather, is this literally a sewer?"

"That's part of the reason why I don't want Subaru pushing himself right now, I suppose. If you carelessly catch tetanus or something, curing it right now would be a real ordeal, I suppose."

"So you're serving as both pretty doctor and tomboy nurse for me... That's super appreciated, but more importantly—what's going on?"

"――――"

While showing understanding for Beatrice's concern for his well-being, what remained equally incomprehensible was Crusch, who was just as eager to treat Subaru's burns as his devoted partner was. Narrowing her eyes at Subaru, whose voice had dropped slightly, she let out a quiet breath and said,

"This is a sewer a short distance from Miklotov McMahon's manor. Judging by the distance traveled, we are likely right around the midpoint between the Noble District and the Commercial District. Even using the sewers, we cannot simply pass through the checkpoints controlling movement to the upper levels. For now, that will be our obstacle."

"Hold on, hold on, please wait a second. I can't follow this at all. In the first place, everything before and after I passed out is super vague. Though..."

"Though?"

"Based on what you just said... by any chance, are Crusch-san and I currently in the same party?"

"Party member..."

Crusch knitted her brows at the unfamiliar phrase, but in response to Subaru's query, Beatrice—who had legitimately passed Level 2 of the Modern Speech Certification—nodded, "I suppose."

"There are all sorts of troublesome matters tied up in this, but that girl harbors no hostility toward Betty and the others, I suppose. It was that girl who carried the unconscious Subaru all the way here, I suppose."

"Had I left you there, you too would have been unable to escape death by fire, Sir Natsuki. At the very least, I felt no falsehood in your words. Therefore, your death is not my desire."

"I see. Well, thanks for that... but,"

The final sight before passing out and the trace of the large burn on his right arm partially proved that the reality of being saved from a tight spot by Crusch was true. There was no reason Beatrice would conspire with Crusch to trick Subaru of all people. However, the reason Subaru couldn't offer an honest thanks was Crusch's phrasing—Subaru's death was not her desire. But Miklotov and the others' death was.

"...Crusch-san, did you really cut down Miklotov-san?"

"—. I affirmed it at the scene as well, but it is the truth. Miklotov McMahon and the manor personnel employed by him—I cut every last one of them down with these hands."

"—kh."

"Do you require an explanation for the reason as well? This too is a repetition of what was said at the scene, but Miklotov McMahon and his associates were traitors, existences that brought harm to the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica. Thus, as one of the high nobility, I delivered punishment with my sword."

"That's, that's what I don't understand! Traitors? Bringing harm to the Kingdom? What does that even mean? Was he plotting something so stupid that you couldn't tolerate it?"

"—At the very least, there is a high probability that he was conveniently exploiting the prophecy of the Dragon History Stone."

Subaru, who had been pushing the conversation forward to burst the bubbles of doubt surging one after another, held his breath. That piece of information Crusch added at the end was related to the "Dragon History Stone"—the very thing Subaru had wanted to confirm directly with Miklotov today. In this kingdom, the prophecy stone engraved with contents pertaining to the covenant with the almighty "Divine Dragon"—that was the "Dragon History Stone," a key item that served as the grounds for the start of the Royal Selection in which Emilia was participating. However, as a relic he had only heard stories about without ever laying eyes on the real thing, Subaru had wanted to verify its existence with his own eyes if possible.

"That Dragon History Stone is being exploited? By Miklotov-san?"

"It is difficult to imagine that the Dragon History Stone, which foretells major crises of the realm, said nothing of the royal family passing away one after another. If so, what is plausible is a case where the party entrusted with guarding the Dragon History Stone intentionally concealed the prophecy that was given, failing to make it public."

"But... but isn't that way too late to bring up now?! In the first place, that kind of doubt must have existed since before the Royal Selection started. Right? After all, Crusch-san and Ferris—"

Regarding the incident in which the royal family passed away, they must have had the motivation to investigate its details. They had been particularly close with the Fourth Prince, one of the deceased royals. However, Subaru wasn't insensitive enough to pry by bringing that up aloud.

"—Failing to hold the option of doubt is my bitterest regret from back then."

Deducing what lay beyond his unsaid words, Crusch admitted her own mistake. Of course, calling it a mistake was far too merciless toward someone who had lost someone close. Subaru had heard from Ferris as well that the relationship between the Fourth Prince and Crusch went well beyond a simple close friendship. So much so that it was likely Crusch's primary motivation for seeking the throne. However—

"Then why start suspecting that all of a sudden..."

"You should understand without me saying it. Sending the 'Divine Dragon Church' to my domain, skillfully deceiving Ferris with clever words in an attempt to make me withdraw from the Royal Selection."

"—kh!? Crusch-san, that's—"

"Ferris has a strong sense of responsibility. My situation being what it was, mental exhaustion took its toll on him. When sweet words were whispered into his ear by the person called the Brain of the Kingdom, it is no wonder Ferris misjudged. Otherwise—"

"――――"

"—Otherwise, there is no way he would do something that trampled on the oath between me and His Highness."

Crusch cast her eyes down, lowering her voice, striving to suppress her emotion. Yet, the more that effort came through, the less Subaru knew which way to tip the balance inside his heart.

Emotionally, he naturally wanted to believe Crusch. Apologies to Miklotov, but if Miklotov truly was a traitor who exploited the kingdom for personal gain, then Crusch's actions possessed legitimacy. Perhaps in a proper trial or similar venue, mitigating circumstances might even be acknowledged. However, Subaru remembered that Miklotov was the man who, despite Subaru exposing himself in an unwatchable, pathetic display during the Royal Selection assembly, had still spoken words acknowledging him as Emilia's knight.

Crusch was Subaru's benefactor, and Miklotov was equally a benefactor as well. Wishing for a convenient outcome where one side was unilaterally in the right seemed far too difficult. Besides—

"Ferris..."

Even knowing he might be hated by Crusch—even prepared to be dismissed as her knight—Ferris had taken the hand extended by the "Divine Dragon Church," making a choice with his master's withdrawal from the Royal Selection in mind. Even if that meant reneging on the oath concerning the Fourth Prince, he had made that choice. Subaru didn't want to simply brush off that decision as the result of being manipulated by someone.

"...I get Crusch-san's stance for now. I want to talk this through thoroughly. There are a few other things too—"

"Wait, Sir Natsuki. Your doubts are reasonable, but since you are awake, I would like to prioritize moving first. Remaining here indefinitely is not wise."

"Ah, because of the sewer location? Sure, I wanna change locations, but we still haven't finished talking..."

Subaru pressed further against Crusch, who slowly shook her head to cut the conversation short. However, Beatrice tugged on the sleeve of Subaru's unburned left arm. Glancing down, he saw a faint tension resting on Beatrice's face as she spoke,

"On this matter, Betty agrees with that girl, I suppose. At any rate, we should flee first, I suppose. If we dawdle, the pursuers will catch us, I suppose."

"Even you're saying that... Wait, wait wait, I can't just let that slide. Fleeing? Pursuers? I don't get what you mean. That makes it sound like us being in a sewer is almost like..."

"――――"

"Almost like... we're on the run..."

As he spoke the words, a slowly dawning bad premonition left Subaru's tongue feeling numb. Meanwhile, Crusch picked up the lamp placed on the passage floor once more, while Beatrice, brows furrowed in trouble, repeatedly opened and closed her thin lips, wondering what to say. From their prompt attitudes and considerate demeanors, it was plain to see. —Jokes aside, Subaru and the others had been forced into the position of fugitives.

"I don't understand this at all! There are way too many missing explanations! And besides..."

Looking around as he spoke, Subaru confirmed there was no one present besides Beatrice and Crusch, pointing out the blatant anomaly—the absence of Tiga.

"Tiga is... He was in the same manor too. So why isn't he anywhere to be found here? What happened, why, and how did things end up like this? Explain that—"

"—Tiga Rauleon, was it? Regrettably, that man is our pursuer."

"...What?"

Crusch's voice answered his question, but it only birthed a new mystery in Subaru. Placing incomprehensible thing upon incomprehensible thing, the tower of building blocks that became an accumulation of incomprehensible facts naturally made it impossible to grasp the full picture. With that exact feeling, Subaru blinked in utter bewilderment and looked at Crusch.

In response to Subaru's inquiring gaze, she raised her lamp, letting its white light illuminate her beautiful face with its left eye covered by the eyepatch, as she continued. Which was—

"Currently, you and I are being pursued. By Tiga Rauleon, and by the knights of the Kingdom. —Under the suspicion of being traitors who plotted the murder of Miklotov McMahon and the overthrow of the Kingdom."
