Arc 4 Chapter 51: "Love Love Love Love Love Love Love Love Love Love Love Love Love Love Meee"

Subaru watched in silence as the golden-furred great tiger — as Garfiel — burst apart.

The savage beast's raised claws struck at the witch's head with enough force to have blown away her upper body, slender torso and all, in a single blow. Such a direct hit would surely have torn even the witch to pieces — no doubt of it. But it never landed.

Garfiel had thrown one of Ryuzu's duplicates as a setup, casting aside all restraint in his bid to seize victory. But just before his arm reached the witch, Subaru saw the mottled wounds gouged into him by the shadow begin to writhe.

The cause of that writhing was a black shadow that had crept up from beneath the witch's feet and wound itself around Garfiel's limbs. The shadow slid its tip into the wounds already carved in the great tiger's flesh, and though it should have had no substance, it spread destruction all the same — shredding his body and sending blood spraying.

And then, riddled with gashes from head to toe, Garfiel's massive frame could no longer withstand the pressure of the shadow swelling within it, and it burst.

There could have been no resisting it, no enduring it — it was a way of destroying a life so relentless it bordered on obsession.

What had been Garfiel became, in an instant, a heap of dark red flesh scattered across the plaza. The only proof that he had ever existed in this world was the golden fur clinging to the scattered fragments — a small, meager testament.

"――――"

No words would come.

Just moments ago, the plaza had held more than twenty lives, counting the doll-like duplicates of Ryuzu. In the span of a few dozen seconds, that number had fallen to just two.

No — if it came to that, this Sanctuary should have held a hundred lives, easily.

To think that every last one of them had now been dragged into the shadow — the crime committed by the shadow before him was too heavy. Too unforgivable.

Blood began to flow again into the part of him that had been numbed by the tangle of changing circumstances and mounting desperation, and Subaru's body stirred with an utterly natural response.

Namely, the raw, primal emotion welling up toward the witch standing before him — anger.

"—I love you."

"Shut up."

"I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you."

"I said shut up...!"

A black shadow whose outline blurred, whose very height was impossible to make out.

Its voice remained as indistinct as ever, as though filtered through some voice changer — impossible to even tell if it belonged to a man or a woman.

And yet despite that voice, the sticky, cloying passion poured into it came through with infuriating clarity.

Her love never wavered. Even after drinking down the people of the Sanctuary, even after butchering Garfiel without mercy, her interest, her attention, her love, remained fixed on Subaru and Subaru alone.

It was so warped, so eerie, so nauseating that Subaru felt sick to his stomach.

Just standing opposite the witch felt like it was chipping away at his sanity. Something close to frenzy welled up inside him, and his chest was now a chaotic mess of hatred and disgust.

"I love you I love you I love you, I love you I love you I love you."

Unmoving, rooted in place, the witch went on murmuring her love for Subaru, endlessly, like a curse.

With a passion so intense it seemed ready to melt. Her utter failure to read the room, how out of place it all was, put even Subaru to shame.

Even with the object of her love making his displeasure plain on his face, she stubbornly kept thrusting forward her overbearing, self-satisfied love.

Every bit of that love was appalling.

But above all, what enraged Subaru most right now was—

"I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you."

"........."

"I love you I love you I love you I love you — Subaru-kun."

"—Don't you call me that!!"

Subaru answered that saccharine address by raising his voice in fury.

That voice besotted with love, those gestures, that way of addressing him — every bit of it struck Subaru's rawest nerve.

"Who gave you permission to call me that. Don't screw around. Don't you dare screw around! Don't you dare!!"

The closeness of standing right beside him.

The affection poured into a form of address.

The dearness of standing within arm's reach.

There was only one person in this entire world Subaru allowed to address him that way.

—And it most certainly was not the witch standing before him now.

"Don't make me laugh, you filthy excuse for a witch. That belongs to one person, and one person only. I won't hand it over to anyone else. No — there's nothing I'll allow you, not one strand of hair, not one scrap of a cell, not even the dirt under my fingernails. I wouldn't waste it on you—!"

"――――"

Subaru, enraged, let fury carry the emotions swirling inside him spill out.

Breathing hard, shoulders heaving, he kept glaring straight at the witch before him.

There was no way he could win against her.

A monster that had swallowed down half the world. The witch of witches, who had just now, before his very eyes, dispatched a powerhouse like Garfiel with a strength that didn't even deign to consider him a threat.

A calamity of the worst order, one who sank every life into shadow without a shred of interest, while whispering an obsessive love toward only one person.

That he could stand here glaring straight back at such a monster struck him as strange.

He could only assume he'd become reckless, that whatever restraint he'd had had simply snapped.

If the witch felt like it, she could sink Subaru into the shadow faster than a man could be pulled under a swamp — quicker than the blink of an eye. Or she could skewer his whole body on the tip of that shadow and turn him, like Garfiel, into an ugly burst of fireworks and fertilizer for the forest.

Knowing all that, why could he still face her without his spirit breaking? It could only be because, somewhere below conscious thought, Subaru held a certain conviction about the witch.

And that was—

"――――"

"...She's not... moving?"

Having screamed out his emotions and grown somewhat calmer, Subaru grew suspicious at the absence of an action the witch should have taken by now.

When he noticed it, the words of love that had kept spilling out like an incantation — the outpouring of affection she hadn't paused even while bursting Garfiel apart — had stopped. Cut off.

Looking closer, the shadow's encroachment, which had been spreading to swallow the whole plaza floor, had slowed — no, stopped entirely. Keeping the unpleasant sensation of the shadow underfoot at bay, Subaru shifted his footing, picking out ground not yet steeped in black.

All the while he kept his eyes fixed on the witch, but there was no reaction.

The witch stood stock-still, arms hanging limp at her sides, still wrapped in a density of shadow thick enough to warp the bending of light, her expression hidden.

With her this wide open, he might well be able to strike her down for good if he charged in now.

"Why so sudden... don't tell me my words actually got through to her that much?"

Even as he thought it was impossible, Subaru grimaced at the fact that he couldn't fully deny it either.

It seemed unlikely his words carried that kind of weight, but given the timing — right when her words and movement had stopped — the fact remained that no other explanation fit.

But that possibility also carried with it a feeling Subaru could hardly bear.

"If simply being rejected by me could throw her this off balance..."

...then if he'd only raised his voice sooner, Garfiel and everyone else might never have had to die.

With Emilia, Ram, Ryuzu, Otto, and Garfiel — who had thrown himself in Subaru's place — all having had their lives scattered before the shadow, Subaru no longer had the will to keep living in this world.

It had only just been guaranteed to him by Echidna that there was no limit on the number of times he could Return by Death. He hadn't been aware of it himself, but perhaps that had bred some complacency toward his own life.

Wasn't that why he'd pulled back the moment Garfiel refused his proposal — even the offer to act as a decoy, something within his own power to do?

Given that he could see the witch's obsession with him, he should have been able to anticipate that taking action himself might provoke exactly this kind of reaction.

"I can't read what she'll do next, but... does that mean my very existence is a weak point for the Witch of Envy...?"

Given that her obsession hadn't loosened its grip on Subaru even once, and this whole situation had arisen from it, the possibility seemed high. The problem was whether, even having worked that out, a chance to make use of it would ever come around.

To begin with, the loops starting from the Sanctuary had changed shape practically every single time. Because of that, Subaru had been kept scrambling just to cope, still nowhere near finding even a thread leading to a solution — but even so, this time's change dwarfed anything that had come before.

Elsa, Garfiel, and the Great Rabbit alone had already been more than he could handle — throw the Witch of Envy into the mix, and it was completely beyond him. Even the thought that he'd need to work out her pattern of appearance, the same as with the other three, was enough to sap what will he had left.

That was how overwhelming the witch's threat was — how utterly hideous her very existence was.

Even trying to work out a strategy to resist her started to feel pointless. In the sense of breaking a person's will to fight before the fight even began, the witch's slight frame was more terrifying than the White Whale's vast bulk.

"――――"

Little by little, even facing a witch who wasn't moving, Subaru could feel his heart being worn down.

The witch showed no movement either. She paid no mind to Subaru burning through his thoughts, working them over. She was simply, singularly, absorbed within herself.

Time passed without him knowing what he should do, what outcome he should even hope for.

That time was ticking steadily by was made plain enough by his own breathing, the pounding of his heart loud enough to be an annoyance, and the tepid trickle of sweat running down his forehead.

Continuing this standoff that wasn't even really a standoff would get him nowhere. Just as Subaru drew a small breath, bracing to act—

—the flash of insight struck, sudden, in that very instant. It was—

"—Wait... could it be Echidna's tea party?"

"――――"

"While I was invited to her castle, I ran my mouth about all sorts of things I was forbidden to say. I figured the reason no penalty came was because that place was somehow exempt, but..."

—what if that wasn't it at all?

What if the witch had never once given Subaru permission to confess Return by Death to anyone else? What if, for speaking of it so carelessly, she had been trying, as always, to mete out punishment in that world where time stood still?

What if the witch, unable to manifest at Subaru's side while he was invited to the Witch's tea party, unable to interfere there, had nonetheless tried to punish him regardless?

—what if that was the truth behind the tragedy unleashed upon this Sanctuary?

"Just how... selfish are you...?"

Was this massacre nothing but a tantrum, thrown because she couldn't carry out her punishment against Subaru?

Did she really think she had the right to do such a thing? Flaunting the power to do it — just who was she trying to show off to, and what exactly was she trying to prove?

"—I love you."

Perhaps it was because Subaru's thoughts had arrived there, brushing against a fragment of the truth.

The witch, who until then had been as still as a shadow puppet, resumed her ghastly activity once more. She turned what could be called her head toward Subaru, and from her mouth the incantation of love spilled out again.

With the whisper of love, the shadow began advancing once more, and the plaza floor started to sink beneath it again. Feeling the sensation of his feet being swallowed by a bottomless swamp, Subaru kicked off the ground and put distance between them,

"What's the deal, huh...? The second I bring up another woman's name, you suddenly perk right up, don't you!"

"I love you I love you I love you I love you."

"No matter how much love you whisper at me, I'm never gonna love you! The first and second place in my heart got filled up a long time ago, and I'm not handing them over. There's no room in there for a witch."

Tit for tat — though all the witch offered in return were the same strings of love-words, delivered without so much as a shift in intonation.

And yet Subaru found in it a genuine flare of emotion, and with his provoking spirit in full swing, he twisted his cheeks into a vicious grin. Rubbing people the wrong way was his specialty — let's see if it worked on a witch too.

"Don't go rattling off 'I love you' like it means nothing — it's cheap."

"I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you."

"The first 'I love you' I ever got for real, in this world... it had enough power in it to make a hopeless piece of garbage like me aim to become a hero."

Enough power that even a wretch who'd been snapped and twisted out of shape, who'd tried to run away, could believe he'd challenge, again and however many times it took, a future he'd nearly given up on facing head-on.

That is how strong, how noble, how vast real love is.

"Your whispered love isn't ever gonna reach my ears. And there's not one single thing about you — jealous, taking your tantrums out on everyone, causing full-blown massacres — that I could possibly feel any fondness for."

"I love you I love you I love you."

"If it came down to falling for a witch like you..."

"I love you I love you I love you I love you I lo—"

What he should say, what he ought to say, what would strike this witch's rawest nerve hardest of all — when it came to getting under someone's skin, there was nobody better than Subaru, and he knew exactly what to do.

So Subaru smiled, cold and cruel, and turned a contemptuous look on the witch,

"Even among witches, I could still love Echidna and the others a whole lot more than—"

"――――"

The instant he spat those words out, the witch's incantation stopped.

And then—

"――――oh."

Subaru's field of vision — the world itself — was swallowed by shadow in a single instant.

Subaru watched, powerless to do anything, as the shadow bore down on him with tremendous speed and mass.

The black shadow extending from beneath the witch's feet looked strikingly similar to the Unseen Hand that Petelgeuse had once wielded. The differences were that, unlike the Unseen Hand — which no one but Subaru could see, and which could be dodged once seen — this one attacked at a speed that made evasion all but impossible.

And so, in this moment too, Subaru's eyes managed to take in every bit of it — being wrapped in shadow, being wrapped and lifted far above the treetops of the forest, being plunged back down at a speed faster than natural fall, being hauled down right before the witch's eyes — all of it.

All of it assaulted him faster than he could process it as reality, and even as vomit spilled from the corner of his mouth from the shock of his overtaxed organs being churned around—

"Ghh— gburk...!"

His vision spun and flipped, and Subaru couldn't hold his consciousness steady.

His feet weren't touching the ground. Something soft, cloth-like, was gently binding his entire body. The restraint was nowhere near rigid, and yet his movement was completely sealed off — he couldn't even find a spot to brace against in order to break free.

He thrashed and struggled, but only his fingertips, the tips of his toes, and everything from the neck up responded to his will — everything else was already swallowed beneath the shadow's garment.

When clarity finally returned to his blurred vision, Subaru noticed a hazy mass of shadow filling his line of sight, and his voice froze in his throat.

—Right in front of him. Right in front of his very face, the witch stood close enough that he could feel her breath.

She studied Subaru, caught within her garment of shadow, from point-blank range, her eyes — impossible for him to read — boring into his own as if trying to drill a hole through them.

His neck was fixed in place, and there was no escaping her gaze. Closing his eyes might have let him escape that staring contest, but somehow he found even that forbidden to him, unable to act — and so Subaru suffered the misfortune of facing the Witch of Envy up close.

"I love you. I love you. I love you. I love you. I love you."

Enunciating each syllable, pausing between them, the witch went on spilling out her unchanging love even now, in this situation.

Subaru, who had lost his voice, swallowed by the situation, felt an unbearable spirit of defiance flare back up the instant he heard those words.

He put strength into his immobilized body, glaring wide-eyed at the witch. And then, meaning to hurl scathing abuse at the witch parroting her one-track refrain, he opened his mouth,

"It's not about distance. I told you it doesn't reach my heart—"

"I love you. I love you. —Love me."

This time, it was Subaru's words that were cut off by the witch's.

Subaru knitted his brow, blinking, wondering whether what he'd just heard had been a mishearing. At his reaction, the witch swayed her head upward,

"Love me. Love me. Love me. Love me. Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me Love me — Love me"

A chill ran through him — and this time, it was true, unmistakable fear that Subaru felt toward the witch.

Up until now, he'd managed to paper over it all with defiance, stubbornness, welling rage — but his own inner truth was laid bare before the witch's call, now that the wind had shifted.

No matter what was said to him, no matter what was done to him, he had kept his chin up, refusing to let only his heart give in.

Subaru had believed there was reason enough, meaning enough, in clinging to that much stubbornness — had believed he'd found it, standing against the Witch of Envy.

That last scrap of courage, that rash conclusion, was smashed to splinters.

A grotesque visage. An aberration. A madwoman. A mad love. A monstrous violence. A monstrous being. A witch.

She had gone on whispering that she loved him, and still understood she could not obtain love in return. And so this time, she sought love in a form bordering on force. Not so much greedy as simply, wretchedly, shameless.

And then he understood.

The witch wanted Natsuki Subaru — but she was not looking at Natsuki Subaru at all.

What the witch saw was not Subaru, but the Vessel called Natsuki Subaru. She wanted only the surface of Subaru, and wished to be loved only by the surface of Subaru. Whether that was genuine or not meant nothing to her — she had lost the capacity for sound judgment.

To love Natsuki Subaru, and to be loved by Natsuki Subaru.

That alone was the entirety of the Witch of Envy — the very meaning behind the ruin of the world.

—It made no sense.

Understanding that, the question rising in Subaru's chest circled right back to where it had started.

Namely: why, to this degree, was the witch so obsessed with him?

He had never met her. Never spoken with her. This was even the first time they had ever laid eyes on one another.

So why would someone like that love him this madly?

None of it made sense. Not one part of it was logical. It would be easy enough to say that love can't be measured by common sense — but the witch's love had gone far, far beyond that dimension entirely.

"—Love me love me love me love me love me love me love me love me."

The witch, wanting only a surface love, wanting to be deceived into mistaking it for true love.

No doubt, if Subaru offered just one word in answer to that love, the witch would release him from this bondage on the spot.

If this were the ordinary Subaru — calculating, cunning, playing the angles — he might have answered her courtship with some honeyed line.

But—

"—I hate you."

"――――"

"I will never, ever love you."

As his very soul refused, Subaru turned down the Witch of Envy's courtship.

"――――"

At Subaru's answer, the witch fell silent — however many times that made now.

Surely there weren't many humans who had crushed the Witch of Envy's spirit two, three times over. Subaru felt a petty pride in that. Her body slowly began to lower in altitude.

Still wrapped in her garment of shadow, Subaru's body, which had been raised aloft, was brought back down to the ground. But that didn't mean he was being released.

Bound as he was, his body began sinking, feet-first, into the ground beneath the witch — into the shadow that had begun swirling there.

Having found she couldn't obtain Subaru's heart, the witch seemed to have decided to absorb him instead. It was a decision that could only be called shortsighted, disturbingly literal-minded.

But as his legs sank beneath the shadow and the fear of losing all sensation crept over him, Subaru suddenly felt a flicker of doubt.

If he was swallowed into the shadow like this, there was no question he would lose his life.

That, in a way, was something he could resign himself to. He had come to this defiance against the witch half-prepared to meet "death." So that much, he could accept.

But—

—If he had been undoing his past "deaths" all this time through the witch's own power, would it even be possible to undo a situation where the witch herself took his life with her own hands?

"――――!"

The instant it struck him, Subaru mounted a resistance far too late to matter. With his lower body already swallowed by the shadow, whatever resistance he could offer was hardly more than a child's flailing. And yet he couldn't help but try.

Thinking it through further, whether or not he could undo it was beside the point — he didn't even know if being swallowed by the shadow would simply, cleanly take his life. If things went badly, he might be absorbed into the shadow and fused with the witch, forced to spend an endless span of time in which even death was denied him.

And across that unbearably long stretch of time, would his resolve, his conviction as it stood now, wear away, fray, and eventually give in to the witch? Subaru didn't trust himself enough to say with certainty that it wouldn't. Which meant he couldn't allow himself to be swallowed like this.

At worst, even if it meant biting through his own tongue, before he fell into the witch's hands— that decision was—

"—Wh, gah?"

interrupted, before it could go too far, by a sudden heat that welled up in his chest.

A heat hot enough to scald bloomed in his left breast, and Subaru glanced down, startled. The heat began emitting light from within, bright enough to spill out even through the shadow wrapped around him.

And what surprised him further was that, from the spot where the light had appeared, the shadow binding Subaru began melting away, vanishing bit by bit.

"This — this could work...!"

Before he could even think through the source of the sudden light, Subaru twisted his body and used its power to sever the shadow's garment. Widening his range of motion, he cut himself free enough to work an arm loose, and the moment it was free, he reached his fingers straight for the source of the light.

And what his fingers, drawn from inside his coat, closed around was a handkerchief fluttering in the wind — Petra's handkerchief, embroidered with a gray cat.

"Petra's... handkerchief...? Why is this... — no, never mind!"

Setting the question aside for later, Subaru swung the handkerchief in his palm in a single slash. What should have been soft fabric rapidly hardened at his will, sharp as a knife, and sliced clean through the shadow connecting the witch to the ground.

"――――"

"Amazing! This — this could actually work!"

Next, Subaru drove the handkerchief's blade into the shadow that had swallowed his lower body. The light-spilling tip sank into the shadow, which for a single instant seemed to gather toward that light — and then, an instant later, burst apart without a sound.

Once the shadow blew away, all that remained was Subaru, both legs sprawled out on the ground.

He rolled backward at once, confirming both his legs were intact, then dropped into a low stance with the handkerchief braced at his hip, eyes darting over the thing still leaking light.

Petra's embroidered handkerchief. It seemed hard to believe her feelings for Subaru alone could have wrought a miracle like this. And a moment later, a name came to mind — someone who might well have rigged this handkerchief to do exactly this.

"That Echidna... don't tell me she knew this would happen."

The image of the white-haired witch's face flickered through his mind, wearing an expression that all but said insurance, this is insurance. When they had parted at the tea party, Echidna had plucked the handkerchief from him within the illusory world, calling it her price. He hadn't had a moment to consider how something handed over in a dream might come to bear on reality — but apparently, interference on the scale of a little rigged trick could cross from dream into waking world after all.

Either way—

"If this gives me a way to fight the witch, then fine — you've got my thanks."

"――――"

Faced with the fact that the shadow she had spawned had been erased, the witch stood stock-still, dazed. Subaru drew a short breath, then circled to the side to strike at her opening,

"Carelessness — that's your downfall!"

From beside the still-standing witch, Subaru drove the handkerchief-blade upward in a thrust. The witch didn't move. But as though by some automatic defense, shadow burst from beneath her feet — the same shadow-dress-like guard Garfiel had once described.

"—Rraaaaah!"

But even that couldn't stop the light of the handkerchief Subaru wielded.

The knife-sharp handkerchief tore through the dress of shadow as easily as breaking through a spiderweb, and the tip drove straight on toward the side of the witch's face — a direct hit.

"Got you—!"

With a surge of triumph at the solid feel of impact, Subaru spun with quick footwork to reverse the blade's momentum, turning on the spot to drive it into the witch's body once more, when—

"—Eh?"

The sight before him made him freeze mid-motion.

Right in front of him, the still-standing witch was looking at Subaru. The strike of light that had caught the side of her face had indeed landed, and it had peeled away the mask of shadow from her face — hazy and indistinct until now — exposing it to the open air.

A familiar, well-known face — a silver-haired girl — stared back at Subaru with eyes frozen of all emotion.

"Emilia...?"

There was no response to his call. But everything else about her was moving, and violently.

The garment of shadow, which should have been destroyed, surged back with renewed force. It coiled around Subaru's body from the feet up once more, and this time, with no mercy in its grip, wrenched a scream out of him.

Starting from his right flank, his left side was seized fast in the shadow's garment. Only his right arm, holding the handkerchief, could still move — even that with its range badly restricted, unable to move properly.

With Subaru's movement sealed once more, the sea of shadow dragged him down toward its depths without hesitation. Whether it had lost any intent to hold back or not, the speed now far outstripped what had come before.

His lower body, and then his left side up to the shoulder, sank beneath it, leaving only his right side from the chest up, and the barest sliver above his neck, exposed.

Desperately raising his head, Subaru resisted with his half-submerged body,

"Emilia! Emilia!? Why, how, why!?"

When he'd seen her presence vanish from the graveyard, seen the Sanctuary swallowed by shadow, Subaru had assumed she too had been sunk into the sea of shadow by the witch's hand.

His entire fight against the Witch of Envy up to this point had carried, in no small part, the meaning of a battle waged to avenge her.

—Why had she been wearing the shadow, attacking the Sanctuary?

His thoughts wouldn't come together. She gave no answer. She wasn't looking at Subaru. Seeing a cold, violet-indigo gleam in her eyes he had never seen before, Subaru began to doubt whether her consciousness was even present at all.

And he had no time left to confirm that doubt.

"Gh, ah..."

Deeper and deeper, his body was dragged further into the shadow.

The parts already swallowed had no sensation left. Worse than a mere lack of sensation, even the feeling that he still existed at all was slipping away — and faced with that fact, Subaru became keenly aware once more of the risk of being swallowed by the shadow like this.

He checked the handkerchief in his right hand, and the remaining parts of himself that could still barely move, and made his resolve.

Even as he steeled himself, the white-haired witch flickered once more through his mind.

He wanted to correct what he'd said before, and offer the witch one more word.

"That Echidna... don't tell me she seriously thought it might come to this...?"

If so, her thoroughness, her foresight, was almost enough to bring him to tears.

Tears, though — tears of blood.

—Closing his eyes, then snapping them open, Subaru drove the handkerchief in his right hand straight into his own throat.

He felt the sharp tip tear through flesh, punching a hole through the fatal part of his throat. Blood surged backward, flooding his throat, and as it poured into his lungs his consciousness began to drown.

Suicide. The Witch of Greed had prepared this chance for him.

Not a means of fighting the Witch of Envy. She had likely sensed, from the start, that the content of their conversation in her castle would strike the Witch of Envy's rawest nerve. This was the price being paid for it.

"――――!"

At Subaru's suicide, the Witch of Envy, for the first time, exploded with an emotion other than love.

But drowning in his own blood, his consciousness already slipping away, Subaru could no longer perceive it.

All he could see, before him, was the familiar girl's face twisting in anguish — and no matter what lay beneath that surface, seeing a girl wear such a sorrowful expression still, even now, made what remained of his heart ache all the same.

Blood welled up, flooding his throat, and he could no longer properly form words — but even so, Subaru spoke to the girl before him. Not to the imposter filling that vessel, but to the girl herself.

"I will — without fail—"

—save you.

In the next instant, Natsuki Subaru's life came to an end.
