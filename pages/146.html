Arc 3 Chapter 69: "Fragments of Days"

—The village was wrapped in an uncanny atmosphere.

Kneeling with his head bowed, Julius led the way — the members of the Witch Cult subjugation squad could be seen at the center of the village. None of them appeared to be badly wounded, so it seemed safe to assume the surprise attack on the Witch Cult itself had succeeded. After that, the plan had been for each team to enter the village separately and guide the evacuation.

"What in the world is going on here?"

Unable to grasp the situation, Subaru knit his brow, demanding an answer from the scene before him. At the center of the village stood Julius and the rest of the subjugation squad, and behind them, as if hiding, stood a group of merchants who looked as though they'd only just arrived. As before, it was the same familiar faces minus Otto lined up there, but their profiles were thick with unease and confusion.

Facing that camp stood the villagers, and here too the faces were all ones Subaru knew well. The children, it seemed, had been hidden indoors, and the ones standing face to face with the knights were the adults, starting with the young men's association. And standing at the head of those villagers, meeting Julius's gaze head-on, was—

"—Ram!"

A lovely girl with pink hair cut short and neat, the picture of charm in a dainty apron dress. And yet her expression was sullen, her arms crossed with an arrogance that set her sharply apart from her twin. Sharing a face with the girl who made up such a large part of his emotional support, that dissonance struck Subaru, on reflection, as a jarring discrepancy.

"Subaru, you've come—"

"...Barusu, so you're back."

At Subaru's raised voice, the two leaders of the opposing groups reacted at once. Julius's expression lit up as though he'd seen a ray of hope, while Ram's emotionless face creased with a displeased wrinkle. Feeling conflicted at Julius's welcoming mood, and something like a wry smile at Ram's unfriendly attitude, Subaru dismounted from Patrasche's back and dashed over.

"Wait wait wait wait, I can't see what's going on here, but let's all just calm down. Why don't you leave this one to me, huh?"

"To Barusu? That's about the funniest thing I've heard all day... More to the point, what face do you think you're wearing, coming back here?"

"Glad to see your sharp tongue's still in fine form. Don't think something that small's gonna get me down."

Ram gave him a cold, dismissive look, but at the sight of Subaru brushing off her venom so easily, a flicker of surprise showed in her eyes. Finding that a little satisfying, Subaru said, "Anyway," and turned back to Julius.

"I've got about a million questions about what happened... but first, let me confirm something. The surprise attack went well, right?"

"Without a hitch, thanks to the signal you sent up. Though, since a few of them had come out of the burrow, we couldn't avoid crossing swords entirely."

"As long as we didn't take losses, that's good. Our side pulled it off too. Wilhelm-san has custody of the Sin Archbishop."

When he pointed, Wilhelm, stationed a little apart from the commotion, raised a hand. At his feet lay Petelgeuse, trussed up like a rolled mat, and one of Wilhelm's hands stayed permanently on the hilt of his sword, ready to strike the instant anything happened. Glancing over at that, Julius let out a relieved breath and said, "As expected of him."

"By and large, I'd say the preliminary stage of the operation was a success. But a problem has arisen."

"Yeah, seems like it. Not that I get what it is, exactly..."

A gaze bored into the back of his head as the two of them murmured in hushed voices. Turning toward the source, he found himself face to face with Ram, arms crossed, glaring at him as if trying to scorch him with her eyes. Letting out a breath, Subaru shrugged as if to say, well, well.

"It's been what, days since we last saw each other, and you're being all prickly. I'm not saying give me a hug, but can't I at least hope for a warm word or two?"

"What you want is an insult sharp enough to draw warm blood to the surface? Ram doesn't have time to indulge Barusu's masochistic tastes. What is the meaning of this crowd?"

"What do you mean, what's the meaning — didn't I write that letter real hard, real carefully? If the merchants are here, the messenger from the capital should've arrived too."

"Subaru, that's exactly the problem."

Subaru tilted his head at Ram's interrogation. From behind, Julius reached out and touched his shoulder, presenting the problem. But Subaru couldn't quite grasp what he meant.

"The letter... the personal missive, you mean. Yes, the messenger certainly did bring a letter. Calling it a 'personal missive,' no less."

In a low, quiet voice, Ram reached a hand inside her maid uniform. Subaru recognized that flat, toneless quality in her voice, and he blinked, wondering what it meant. It was the telltale sign that Ram was feeling anger she could barely contain.

"A personal missive handed over with such a grand escort — and it turns out to be a blank one. What an amusing little touch. Mind telling me what this means, Barusu?"

Thrust toward him, the letter was spread open before Subaru's eyes. For a moment his vision blurred out of focus, and Subaru stepped back in shock as he took it in: the fact that the letter in Ram's hands — the missive itself — was entirely, completely blank.

"Blank!? Why!? That can't be right... I mean!"

"I was told it was urgent news, and this is what I find when I open it. On top of that, a bunch of unidentifiable people come marching straight into the village, and to top it off, an armed group besides. Anyone would be wary of that."

"That's not it! These people are from Crusch-san's household, and that's Anastasia-san's mercenary company... no wait, more importantly, what was written in that letter—"

"Su~ba~ru~-kyun♪"

He snatched the letter from Ram's hand as if tearing it away, staring hard at the blank page. He was about to spit out some excuse-laden words in his confusion, when he was suddenly stopped by a pair of fingers reaching out from behind him. Turning at the sharp tug on his ear, he found Ferris up close, wearing a cat-like grin. Ferris flicked Subaru's nose with the fingers of his other hand, the one not pinching his ear.

"Ow!"

"Calm down, take a deep breath, and let's sort out what we nya-eed to talk about, 'kay? No need to rush — things are mostly going fine, so just sit back and relax, nya. Right?"

"Easy for you to say, calm down, but this isn't exactly something I can just calm down about..."

"Want me to slip you some tongue and kiss you calm by force?"

"I'm calm! All the blood just drained right out of me at incredible speed! Cool down achieved!"

Before those mischievous eyes could close in any further, Subaru snapped his spine straight and beat a retreat from the spot, putting distance between them in what might as well have been a triple axel, before dropping his gaze back to the letter in his hand. It looked like the very same letter he and Rem had groaned and struggled over writing together, back before leaving the capital, to be entrusted to the messenger through Anastasia at Crusch's mansion. Except that the contents had been entirely emptied out — or rather, it looked more like they'd been swapped out.

"So, this whole uproar is the result of the advance information never getting through. Then that loud name Julius was announcing earlier was..."

"Tempers were running high, and things were on the verge of coming to blows. I had no choice but to invoke my name and try to calm the situation. Though with your arrival, that worry proved needless."

Brushing back his purple hair, Julius let out a wistful sigh and said, "However—"

"—now everyone here knows that I'm Julius, Knight of the Royal Guard."

"Just so you know, anyone who already knew your face and name, already knew. —That aside, well, I do appreciate you stopping things right before they turned into a riot."

"Think nothing of it. It's simply a knight's duty."

At Subaru's thanks, offered while looking away, Julius laughed without any sign of taking offense. Feeling a vague irritation at that attitude, Subaru cleared his throat and turned back to look at Ram. Having had the conversation interrupted so many times already, the sharpness of Ram's glare had picked up an edge or two beyond where it had started. Sensing keenly that he was about to get physically cut somewhere any moment now, Subaru pressed on.

"The missive being blank — well, that's some kind of mix-up on our end. There were two main points. One, that the Witch Cult is lying in wait, targeting the mansion. And two, that for the evacuation, we got the merchants to cooperate and prepared dragon carriages. We want everyone to get on those and escape somewhere safe, together with the villagers."

"And are you going to claim these gentlemen with their dangerous-looking gear are merchants too?"

"Ah... no, these people are—"

Subaru stammered out a reply to Ram, whose hostile posture hadn't relaxed one bit. But the answer to that last question came not from Subaru, but—

"We are, on this occasion, a force of reinforcements sent ahead of the alliance formed between His Excellency Duke Crusch Karsten and Lady Emilia."

Stepping forward, the chestnut-haired figure knelt. The deference in the manner of speaking was beyond reproach, the fluid grace of every gesture proof of a thorough education. Delivering this formal address with a refinement quite unimaginable from the usual playful, mischief-prone demeanor was the cross-dressing knight with cat ears atop his head. Ferris looked up at Ram, who stood straight-backed, and said in a quiet voice,

"My name is Felix Argyle. I am First Knight to Duke Crusch Karsten, and I serve as her representative for this alliance. For the failure in communication, no words of apology could ever suffice... but for now, I would ask that you give priority to Subaru-dono's account."

"...An alliance."

At Ferris's unfailingly courteous manner, Ram murmured that quietly and turned her eyes to Subaru. She touched her thin lips and furrowed her brow in a difficult expression.

"It seems you've managed to fulfill the duty you stayed behind in the capital for, at least."

"If someone had just spelled out a little more clearly what exactly was expected of me, I feel like I wouldn't have had to struggle this much."

Not boasting of the results he'd brought back, Subaru voiced that complaint simply as his honest feelings. Roswaal's secret instructions were far too cryptic. He'd died three times over, gotten a mountain of hints from Rem besides, and only then had he finally grasped the thread. It was a first-attempt killer if there ever was one.

"Anyway, that's how it is — I've mostly fulfilled the duty I stayed in the capital for. Now all that's left is to accomplish the purpose I came back for, and greet tomorrow with everything settled happily. So, on that note, I want to hurry the evacuation along..."

"...Ram noticed the strange presence lurking in the forest too. It never showed up directly to my clairvoyance, which made it unsettling beyond words. Yes — the Witch Cult."

Ram's clairvoyance was the sort of ability that synced with the sight of beings whose wavelength matched her own, hopping from one set of eyes to the next in succession. If her words could be trusted, not a single one of the Witch Cultists had a wavelength that matched hers. Whether that was cause for relief or lament was a delicate question, weighing the merits and demerits.

"We took out the majority — about eighty percent of them. We've got the enemy commander in custody too."

Subaru reported their current results to the still-sullen Ram. Hearing it, she blinked in what was, for the first time in this whole exchange, genuine surprise.

"Wouldn't you call that practically annihilated?"

"With these guys, I think the right rule is, if you see one, assume there's thirty more. Can't relax till every last one's been hunted down. So to play it as safe as possible, evacuation is a must."

"Would holing up in the mansion be a bad move?"

"Those guys would set the place on fire without a second thought. If we don't want the mansion — the one packed full of all our memories — burned down, running is the smarter, simpler call."

Even Subaru found the comparison he'd offered a chilling one, and Ram's face made her displeasure plain. It seemed she'd reacted to the mention of "burning" in particular — maybe she had some unpleasant memory tied to fire. Something Rem had once said flickered at the edge of his mind, but he couldn't quite bring it back.

In any case,

"We've secured enough manpower to carry the villagers and the three of you from the mansion. Roswaal's not here, is he?"

"No. Roswaal-sama is currently at the Sanctuary... visiting Garfiel's place. Ram's been ordered to wait here for Lady Emilia's decision."

"Emilia-tan, huh..."

At Ram's answer, Subaru let his eyes wander in search of the silver-haired girl. But it seemed she hadn't come down to the village yet, and there was no sign of her nearby. At that gesture of his, Ram let out a small breath.

"Lady Emilia is resting at the mansion right now. —There's been rather too much for her to endure, lately."

"Endure?"

"Barusu will understand soon enough."

He tilted his head at Ram's vague words, but she showed no sign of elaborating further. Judging that this attitude of hers would get him nowhere, Subaru decided that, for now, he'd at least get everyone present loaded onto the dragon carriages, and clapped his hands to draw attention.

"All right, listen up! Morning, everybody, long time no see! My name's Natsuki Subaru! It's only been... what, a week? I'd like to think I wasn't forgotten in that short a time, but anyway — I'm back!"

Calling out at the top of his voice, the villagers who'd been eyeing him warily from a distance began, one by one, to poke their faces forward. Their suspicious expressions locked onto Subaru, and upon recognizing a familiar figure, their faces began to loosen with a measure of relief. As the sharpness of their stares began to ease, Subaru raised both hands so everyone could see and spun around in place.

"So, sorry to jump right into it, but I need you all to hear a little request. It's simple. Think of it like a little picnic. We rented some dragon carriages and reserved everyone a seat. The chairs are hard, but they don't jostle much — special model, comes with its own Divine Protection. Get into little friend groups and hop on board. If you'd just go off and have a nice outing for half a day, or a whole day, that'd be a huge help."

Saying something along the lines of, "Okay, everyone, form groups with people you like~," Subaru went and reopened his own old wound. Whenever that kind of thing started up, Subaru's brand of social standing was such that he'd get shuffled fairly quickly into the leftover group, and stand out awkwardly even there. Even as he recalled that gloomy chapter of his past, the villagers, hearing his call, looked at one another.

"Why do we have to run away and do something like that?"

The one who timidly threw out that question was a man with a close-cropped haircut. The man, representative of the young men's association, kept touching the hilt of the sword he held as he looked at Subaru.

"You're saying it in a bright way so it doesn't sound so grim, but what you mean is running, isn't it? Abandoning the village... why do we have to do something like that?"

"Look, this is just how I naturally talk, so don't go digging for some hidden meaning in it, but there's no need to think of it as dramatically as 'abandoning the village.' Really, if you'd just give us half a day or so..."

"—It's the Witch Cult, isn't it?"

When the crew-cut young man let that slip quietly, a ripple of unease swept through the whole village. It wasn't just the villagers — the same was true of the band of merchants who'd been watching to see which way the discussion would tip. All of them alike carved tension into their expressions, lips trembling.

"I knew it, it's the Witch Cult." "How horrifying... why would such dangerous people—" "Isn't it obvious? Of course it is. Damn it, what has the lord gone and done..." "Why would he... a half-demon... a half-elf, of all things..."

"Hey, wait a minute."

Subaru held out a palm toward the villagers' words, murmured quietly yet clearly enough to shake the air. Feeling the back of his throat go dry and tight, Subaru stepped toward where the voice had come from. Turning to face one of the slender young men, he said,

"What did you just say?"

"I— I'm not wrong, am I? I mean, the fact that those people are coming is because..."

"I'm asking you! What. Did you just. Say!"

With a ferocity that looked ready to lunge and grab him, Subaru bellowed, eyes sharp. At once, hit by that roar, the young man's eyes darted about pitifully, and he turned his head around as if seeking help, but everyone else had looked down and none of them answered his silent plea. Abandoned by his heartless surroundings, he looked back at Subaru as if resigned, and said,

"I— If you get involved with a half-elf, the Witch Cult starts making trouble. That's just common sense. Even a kid knows that. And yet the lord, of all people, went and recommended a silver-haired half-elf as a candidate for king, didn't he..."

"And what's that got to do with what was just said?"

"That's exactly it! You get it, don't you!? If the lord goes around openly announcing his support for a half-elf, that's bound to provoke the Witch Cult! In fact, that's exactly why you're here right now, isn't it... so then—!"

"—Don't you dare say something stupid like, 'if only that girl hadn't been here from the start.'"

Swept up in his own momentum, the young man was about to say something he shouldn't. But Subaru, reading where he was headed, got there first. Pushing the gasping young man's chest back with his palm, Subaru looked all around. Looking, he saw the same fear reflected in everyone's eyes, and he let out an exaggeratedly heavy sigh.

"Who exactly is it that's trying to put this village in danger?"

"————"

"Who's the one who went and did something so vile that people are scared to death of the very word 'Witch'?"

"————"

"All of you — aren't you looking at the wrong person here? Sure, I get it, it's a lot easier to throw your frustration at someone you know is right there than at some unknown enemy. I'm not exactly in a position to talk big about it either, since I'm the same way."

Everyone's discontent flows downward, from the strong to the weak. Naturally, anyone would rather aim their complaints and whispered grudges at whoever is weakest. If anyone understood that, it was Subaru, the very representative of the weak. And still, even knowing it was self-righteous of him, this was how Subaru wanted to think.

"I know full well that I'm the worst. So I want to believe that everyone besides me has got something better about them than I do, somewhere. I know it's just a kid's selfishness, but please. —Don't make me think, 'I shouldn't have bothered going through all this trouble.'"

Subaru wanted, fiercely, to save Emilia in the mansion, and Ram, and Beatrice. And just as much, he wanted to save the people of this village where he'd spent his days. Even if the time they'd shared was short, the villagers had treated him warmly, and he'd begun to grow fond of them for it. So please — don't betray that impression of himself. To that selfish wish of his, Subaru bowed his head.

"Whatever's going on with Roswaal, that doesn't matter right now. And whatever you've got to say about the half-elf girl at the mansion — Emi... put that on hold too. Being here in this village right now is dangerous. So please evacuate. That's what all this preparation has been for."

Even from the earlier young man's attitude, a deep-rooted prejudice against half-elves had been plain to feel. Subaru had known of that on a surface level, but now, seeing it directed by people he knew, familiar people, he was forced to realize just how superficial that knowledge had truly been. Had Emilia been enduring this kind of contempt all along?

As Subaru kept his head bowed, someone suddenly came to stand beside him. It was Julius, resplendent in his Royal Guard uniform. He stood proudly beside Subaru, bending his tall frame smoothly and firmly at the waist.

"I ask that you please heed him. I swear upon the Dragon and upon the pride of my knighthood that I will guarantee the safety of every one of you. For now, please, lend an ear to his words."

"————"

Any number of thoughts flickered through his mind — that this was none of his business, that his lines were as pretentiously smooth as ever — but what filled Subaru's chest, purely and simply, was gratitude toward Julius. Finding it embarrassing to have such a thought about the handsome man's profile, Subaru, still bowing, averted his eyes from Julius standing beside him and muttered,

"I'm not gonna thank you for this, all right."

"Heh. —That's exactly the answer I wanted to hear."

Yeah, Subaru thought — deep down, this guy really was an irritating one.

"Walking through the mansion like this with Barusu again — it feels like it's been quite a while, somehow. Even though it's only been about a week, really."

"I'm in complete agreement. Though the substance of what's making us feel that way is probably pretty different."

"Is it, though?"

"It certainly is."

Walking side by side down the hallway of Roswaal Manor — his beloved, nostalgic home — Subaru traded that kind of idle chatter with Ram. Looking back, across his repeated days, there had only been two times he'd made it as far as Roswaal Manor and gotten to walk around inside. The first time, there hadn't been a trace of any survivor left in the house—

"—Ram."

"Did you call me?"

"No, I just got hit with a wave of Ram-iniscing. Current-tense Ram's fine just the way she is."

"That nonsensical way of talking you have, Barusu — after not hearing it for a while, it's somehow even more irritating."

Ram, nose in the air, primly ignored Subaru's flippant remark. And that maid-clad figure walking a little ahead of him — in the first world, Subaru had found her lifeless body atop a bed. The only mercy, it seemed to him then, had been that the manner of her death hadn't been gruesome.

In the second world, the mansion had collapsed from Puck's sudden growth, and as for the third world — merely recalling it made his spine threaten to freeze over with the memory of the sight.

"Barusu, you're about to walk right past."

"Oh, my bad, my bad... wait, this is—"

Lost in thought, he hadn't noticed Ram stopping, and backtracked the distance he'd overshot to look at the door. The place Ram had led him to was one of the guest rooms.

"Isn't this Emilia-tan's room?"

"Right now the only ones in the mansion are Ram, Lady Emilia, and Lady Beatrice, so everyone's gathered together in the central wing. Especially since last night was particularly exhausting."

Recalling what Ram had told him, Subaru bowed his head slightly, thinking of the strain Emilia must have been under. This came after Ram had given him a rough account of what Emilia had been doing since returning to the mansion. After coming back from the capital with Roswaal, and after that man had gone off to make his rounds among various influential figures, Emilia, left behind, had apparently thrown herself into her studies, begrudging even the time to sleep.

"She was absorbed in it as if possessed. Almost as though something unpleasant happened in the capital, and she was trying to forget it."

"Ugh...!"

"Almost as though some man put her through something unpleasant in the capital, and she's trying to forget all about that man."

"Don't slip in weird extra words halfway through! Not that I can... totally deny it, but..."

Ram sighed at Subaru's voice trailing off. Then, shaking her head as if exasperated, she gestured at the door with her hand and said,

"After Ram noticed the strangeness in the forest through her clairvoyance and reported it, she must have grown even more exhausted. You saw how the villagers were acting earlier — you understand, don't you?"

"I've got a vague idea, but I don't really want to put it into words. —Rejection, huh."

"Rejection? Barusu really does imagine things kindly, doesn't he."

Ram gave a scornful little laugh through her nose at Subaru's words, then immediately let her expression go blank. After a beat, with Subaru falling silent, she said in a weary tone,

"—Denial, that's what it was. If it were rejection, there'd still be some way to reach out a hand. Being brushed off means you touched them, at least. But denial — well, is that the same?"

"............"

"If even the touch itself is loathed, I wonder how you'd ever close the distance."

At Ram's testing tone, Subaru held his breath, unable to give an answer. Ram, too, didn't seem to have expected Subaru to actually answer. Without any sign of disappointment, she signaled the end of the moment with a breath and went on, "That was a mean thing to say."

"Noticing the ominous presence in the forest, Lady Emilia went down to the village to try to get everyone evacuated to the mansion. And she was refused. Though as Barusu well knows, she's not the type to back down easily just because she was turned away once."

"But I also know she's not the kind of girl who wouldn't get hurt from being treated that coldly, with no way to even get a foothold."

Unable to bear his irritation, letting it spill out on his tongue, Subaru glared at the hallway window and narrowed his eyes at the distant view of the village beyond it. It wasn't that the villagers were particularly narrow-minded or heartless, exactly. This was simply the ordinary reaction, the treatment Emilia had received all along as a matter of course. Was it really so arrogant to wish, at the very least, that it could change?

"Then are you saying this feeling of mine, this fury I can't help feeling at the unfairness of the world, is 'arrogance' too?"

Having spat that out, Subaru closed his eyes once, then turned toward the door as if to shake it all off. The face he raised had wiped away its negative emotion, at least making the effort to forget it for this moment. Perhaps out of respect for that intent of his, Ram made no further comment on it, and instead said,

"Lady Emilia, are you awake?"

A small, firm knock sounded twice, echoing through the hallway where the morning air hung cold. A few seconds after Ram's call and knock, with a pause between—

"—Ram? Yes, I'm awake."

came the reply, a voice like a silver bell, beautiful enough to lose oneself in, striking lightly against Subaru's ears. At the purity of that voice — gentle, noble, and resolutely composed — Subaru momentarily forgot the anxiety that had been sitting in his chest until then.

All the various anguishes tangled together — what he'd say when he saw her, what words he should line up — had all been blown away, if only for that one moment.

Because it was to hear this voice, to see her, that he had come back.

"Excuse the intrusion, Lady Emilia. —Barusu has returned."

Pushing the door open, Ram bowed reverently and reported the servant's return to her temporary mistress. Following that small body as it led the way inside, Subaru, forgetting even to breathe, stepped halfway through the door with faltering steps.

"Eh, Subaru—?"

"Long ti—"

He hadn't rehearsed anything, but he'd at least wanted to greet their reunion with a smile, so Subaru put on as gentle a smile as he could manage, lifted a hand lightly, and brought his focus onto Emilia as she turned around toward him—

—and the sight of Emilia's back, her white skin dazzling mid-change of clothes, burned itself into his eyes.

"—Oh my, how rude of me."

Casually, in front of the two of them frozen stiff, Ram stuck her tongue out innocently and gave herself a light knock on the head. But neither of the other two had any composure left to spare for calling out that little "oops, my bad" routine of hers.

The two of them stared at each other wordlessly, and Subaru's gaze traveled from Emilia's face, down her exposed back, along the slender, long line of her supple legs, all the way to her bare toes, taking it in as though drinking it in. The instant his prepared smile stretched into something lecherous—

"To do such a thing to an unmarried girl—"

—a small gray cat came fluttering down out of nowhere and unleashed a blast of magical power straight into Subaru's frozen, leering face. Helpless to resist, Subaru's body was sent flying into the hallway. Ram, hurrying after him, dove into the corridor, and right as Subaru was about to smash into the window—

"It'd be a real problem if the window broke."

"Fweh!?"

By a hair's breadth, the window opened just before impact, and Subaru's body sailed straight on out with all its momentum intact. Fortunately, since it was the first floor, he was spared a fatal fall, but he skidded face-first across the grass still wet with morning dew, tumbled, and plowed into a flower bed before finally coming to a stop.

The taste of dirt filled his mouth, and before his eyes stood a wall of fertilizer-rich mud. His one good outfit, already bloodstained, now had a fresh coat of dust layered over it — as for his nasty glare, well, that one was just how he was born.

"Puck, you went too far... Oh, geez, Subaru, are you okay!?"

From far, far away, from beyond the window of the mansion he'd been sent flying from, came the flustered voice of the girl he loved, and Subaru, for some reason, burst out in a small laugh. Nothing had been settled yet, nothing had ended — and yet it felt as though a fragment of the days he'd wished he could live through once more had glittered, just then, with a sparkling light.

Until Ram, come to help him up, reached out a hand as though looking at something unsettling, Subaru kept right on chuckling quietly, softly, to himself.

  • Title 「日々の欠片」rendered as "Fragments of Days," matching the final scene's echo of the phrase; "Pieces of Everyday Life" would also work.
  • Ferris's speech quirk (inserting にゃ/"nya" into words) rendered via English wordplay ("nya-eed," "'kay... nya") — an adaptation rather than literal match, since the Japanese phonetic substitution has no direct English equivalent.
  • 半魔 ("hanma," a slur for half-elves referencing taint by the Witch) rendered as "half-demon" — a reasonable but uncertain rendering; other translations use "half-devil."
  • 「思い出しラム」(a pun on 思い出し笑い, "reminiscence laugh," swapping in Ram's name) adapted as "Ram-iniscing" to preserve the wordplay rather than translating literally.
  • テヘペロ (a cutesy "oops, tongue-out" gesture/interjection) rendered descriptively as "stuck her tongue out innocently... that little 'oops, my bad' routine" rather than with a single English equivalent.
  • 「初見殺し」rendered as "a first-attempt killer" (gaming slang for an unavoidable first-try death/failure) — adapted somewhat loosely into English idiom.
