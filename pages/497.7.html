Scorpion Tale Epilogue: "The Days I Waited for You"

1

Sparkling, sparkling, twinkling.
Sparkling, sparkling, shining.
Sparkling, sparkling, dazzling.

Sparkling, sparkling, sparkling — everything vivid, everything aglow.

"——Mmn~, looks like Master's good-morning smooch is on hold again today too~"

Tugging her freshly-woken body this way and that in a stretch, Shaula once again succeeded in greeting a good, crisp awakening entirely solo.

She cracked her neck with a series of pops and went "Hnnngh~" as she arched her back where she stood.

"Aaah, my head's been all foggy and I've been way over-stressed this whole time, but I've finally cleared it out. Yeah, bottling stuff up is seriously bad for your health."

Reflecting on her recent unhealthiness of mind and body, Shaula felt anew the importance of the basics of beauty care. Brooding over this and that had never suited Shaula in the first place.

That was how Kaka-sama had designed her, and afterward, through her association with Redhead and Blondie and Beardy and the rest, Shaula had grown up freely in exactly that mold.

And of course, it goes without saying that the single most important factor in making the great blossom called Shaula flower was "his" love.

"Still, that was a weird feeling. Mixing up Kaka-sama with somebody else — if she ever found out, she'd sure as anything hit me with a truckload of snide remarks. Whew, scary."

And it wasn't only about Kaka-sama that she'd been confused, either — it had been about Redhead and the others too.

Thinking it might have been a hallucination born of accumulated fatigue, she'd slept plenty yesterday. The result: her hair and her skin were both at peak luster, though honestly, the crispness situation up in her head remained unclear.

In any case, she'd thrown the uninvited guest out, so there probably wouldn't be any more mix-ups like that.

"Even so, trying to take over my number one spot... in other words, Master's seat — that's, like, impudence beyond a thousand measures. That's a straight red card and off you go~, is what that is."

From Shaula's perspective, the woman had labored under a delusion that made absolutely no sense.

She couldn't even remember the name clearly anymore, but no matter how out-of-bounds by Shaula's standards the things she'd said were, there was no mistaking that she'd been pregnant. So, keeping "his" instructions as a given, Shaula's straight red card had been a compromise — ejection from the tower, not from this world.

"I chucked her outside the 'Sand Time,' and I did send her off with water and grub and all, so from there it's a gamble on whether somebody happens to pass by~"

Honestly, Shaula was confident it had been a pretty cunning answer that slipped through a loophole in "his" instructions, but whether she could actually let "him" hear about it felt dicey, so she considered kept-or-broken to be a choice sitting right on the borderline.

Fortunately, the woman's slip of the tongue had come in the morning, so the dumping had taken place during daylight, when the possibility of someone passing by wasn't exactly nil. If she survived, she ought to be grateful for her own luck.

At any rate, the days with the uninvited guest had reminded Shaula of the existence of other people for the first time in several hundred years, but there wasn't much to be gained from it. Cramped, was how it felt. Not for me.

"Ah, but there was one thing I wanna say thanks for."

Perhaps that was why — on top of the pretense of half-keeping "his" instructions — she'd decided to spare that outsider's life.

And that thing was——

"——I got to reconfirm that a Master-shaped hole can only be filled by Master, so thanks for that part and that part only."

——That was the harvest of the few days Shaula spent with the uninvited outsider.

2

Sparkling, sparkling, twinkling.
Sparkling, sparkling, shining.
Sparkling, sparkling, dazzling.

Sparkling, sparkling, sparkling — everything vivid, everything aglow.

"——Mmn~, Master's good-morning back-hug vanished like dew again today~"

In the days that followed, for Shaula it was the arrival and departure of one unchanging day after another, repeated over and over and over, piling up in layer after layer.

The sun rose and set, rose and set again, and through it all Shaula worked through her fixed routine while gazing out at an unchanging sea of sand.

"——And-a one, two, three, fou-r!"

"He" had devised it; Kaka-sama gave out before even a tenth of it was done, Beardy clumsily played along, Blondie laughed merrily and saw it through, and Redhead let out a huge yawn and wouldn't even look.

Working through that memory-laden Ra-a-adio Calisthenics, Shaula bathed her whole body in sunlight.

Because "he" had taught her it was necessary for maintaining a glamorous, nice body.

"——The way this is feeling, just keeping it shut away might not be good enough."

Spilling that excuse-like remark, Shaula looked around the stone chamber carved with countless tally marks, snapped her fingers, and conjured the gun-ports of Hell's Snipe — the ones she'd once been told made you a full-fledged warrior if you could produce ten — over a hundred of them at once, and trained them on the wall.

Then she renovated by brute force the stone chamber that would definitely have creeped "him" out on his return — sticking her tongue out at the rule forbidding destruction of the tower with an "I'm not a challenger."

"——Come to think of it, if I'd saved some appa seeds, could I have grown 'em in the Green Room?"

Voicing that stray idea, Shaula found herself regretting something from very long ago.

The spirit of the Green Room was the only being besides Shaula to have spent long years in the tower — she felt no camaraderie with a thing that never even showed itself, but if there was anywhere inside the tower where nature could barely manage to grow, that was the only place.

Still, even within the tower, that one spot was the Green Room spirit's territory — that tacit understanding had held all this time, so Shaula meddling there would likely be unwelcome. Well, if it seriously got in her way it'd mean war, but for all that they had virtually no dealings, the acquaintance was a long one. So she let it slide.

"——Did the pack change generations or somethin'? Coming at me — seriously, what idiots."

While down on the ground gathering food, blasting witchbeasts dead left and right, a pack of misshapen half-human, half-horse witchbeasts — gabaou — came charging at her, and she annihilated them.

The previous pack had stopped laying a hand on Shaula after she'd made an example of their leader, but as the generations turned over, the effect of that example wore thin.

With no help for it, she slammed a Hell's Snipe into them to make the gap in power clear. If she didn't, the witchbeasts would start trying to get into the tower too — the Star Watcher's job kept her busy.

"——I'm just too devoted and cute for words. Master really oughta be aware of the extraordinary opportunity loss he's racking up, and the love-dividends he ought to be collecting that he's letting slip."

Day after day, morning after morning, the heart and body she had polished for "him," the obedience with which she went on keeping "his" instructions, the boundless love that welled up as she thought of "him" — she wanted "him" to properly understand that none of it was something to be taken for granted.

"——Nah, it's fine if he doesn't get it. Even if he doesn't get it, I'll just report it to Master straight from my own mouth and make him get it."

So — one day sooner, one second sooner, even just a single glimpse would do——

"——I wanna see you, Master."

3

Sparkling, sparkling, twinkling.
Sparkling, sparkling, shining.
Sparkling, sparkling, dazzling.

Sparkling, sparkling, sparkling — everything vivid, everything aglow.

——The arrival of that day came without any warning at all, all of a sudden.

That dragon carriage was the first challenger in a long while to slip through the "Sand Time" and break onto this side.

However, even if you managed to break through the "Sand Time," beyond it waited a witchbeast zone — a horizon-spanning field of flowers where countless oiran bears grew in clusters. In most cases — or rather, near enough to a certainty — those who cleared the first exam that was the "Sand Time" were destined to fall to the fierce assault of the witchbeasts in the second exam.

But these challengers didn't commit the all-too-common blunder of waking the sleeping colony of oiran bears; they calmly skirted the flower field, making the difference between them and the rest vividly plain.

"...Whoa."

At that deft handling, she felt heat go rippling across the great ocean of Shaula's heart.

If they'd answered the baptism of the Augria Sand Dunes this precisely, then perhaps — perhaps this was the return of "him" that Shaula had been waiting for.

But that hope, too, deflated at tremendous speed when the challengers, having left the witchbeast zone, leisurely began some sort of discussion.

"Okay, boom."

At the group that had obligingly settled itself into her crosshairs, Shaula let a Hell's Snipe fly.

In proportion to the heat of the hope that had been stoked just beforehand, Shaula's attitude in firing it was thoroughly offhand. But in her own defense, the challengers who betrayed her expectations were the ones at fault. That concludes the defense.

"If it were Master——"

If the one returning had been "him," he would surely have waved a hand, or raised a smoke signal, or given some kind of sign to the Shaula he'd left behind in the tower as the "Star Watcher."

As long as there was a signal, no matter how little Shaula might recognize it, she had all the mental preparation she needed to improvise along with it.

But there was no signal. ——Then it wasn't "him."

There was zero hesitation in Shaula as she moved to blow those lives away with a Hell's Snipe.

But——

"——Oh? Seriously? They blocked it."

Noticing Shaula's Hell's Snipe, someone had instantly thrown up walls of ice, layer upon layer.

Of course, Hell's Snipe was not a supreme technique that could be stopped by anything so slight, but a single blade among the group cut down the shot after the ice walls had marginally blunted its force and speed.

Back when she was still unpolished it might have been another matter, but a Hell's Snipe honed over several hundred years being stopped was a first in the history of Shaula. At that not-at-all-common experience, the hope that had once deflated caught its breath a little, and Shaula opened the next gun-port.

She didn't think it was "him," but they might be challengers who had cleared the conditions "he" had set, qualified to enter the tower. To make certain, she immediately fired off a second shot.

And that one was blocked by a strange method different from the first——

"——Huh?"

In that instant, Shaula, who had narrowed her sights further in anticipation, doubted her own eyes.

"No way... No way, no way, no way, no way no way no way no way no way no way no way no way no way no way no way no way..."

Inside a strange magic field, dead in the center of the scene where her Hell's Snipe had been attenuated, Shaula caught sight of someone impossible —— no, not impossible. Not. Impossible.

There was no way it could be impossible. Because "he" had promised Shaula.

That he would come back without fail, that she should wait for him — yes, he had promised——

"——Found you."

"——Found you."

——By the narrowest margin, by a hair's breadth, Shaula made it in time.

The mana pool that was the cause of the "Sand Time" had been unraveled by "his" magic, and as a result "he" fell, together with those who were with him, into the depths beneath the sea of sand. Shaula rushed frantically to retrieve them, but she guessed wrong between the two groups "his" party had split into, and came up empty the first time. Having shoved the confusing bunch into the tower, she headed for the other option —— and there, "he" was on the brink of death.

"...Or, well, maybe he didn't need my help at all?"

Facing off against a single gabaou that had strayed from its pack, "he" had been locked in a mortal struggle.

Of course, since "he" was immortal and invincible, Shaula's help would have been unnecessary. But it couldn't be helped. She'd wanted to show off something cool. She'd wanted him to see how strong she had become while "he" wasn't watching, that "his" pupil had kept his instructions.

To have him see, and then, and then, and then — she'd wanted to be praised.

"Master, I worked super hard. I super wanted you to praise me."

The moment of reunion was, frustratingly, put off a while longer.

"He" had collapsed unconscious, sleeping with some woman she didn't know clasped in his arms, and at his unchanged lack of restraint she went "gununu," but a capable man being popular is a law of nature.

Besides——

"Right now I'm seriously walking on air with joy and happiness, so I can let most things slide in the spirit of a goddess of mercy. Really, really, this isn't a dream, right?"

Pinching her own cheek between her fingers, Shaula confirmed the dream that wouldn't end, and felt relief.

The instant she relaxed, crimson rushed up her cheeks and her eyes grew moist. Her heartbeat turned noisy, and she couldn't stay still where she stood.

"Master, Master, Master, Master, Master..."

Each time she said the word, she felt her chest grow hot, her cheeks slacken into a grin, her heart melt sweetly.

She had longed for this day for so, so long, and yet now that the day had actually come, she couldn't even get the order straight of all the things she'd wanted to do, wanted to say, wanted to tell him.

"Aah, but but, this much is definitely for sure, so this is the very first of the first."

Even with her emotions in gridlock, her feelings crossing wires, her thoughts in incoherent pieces, this one thing was absolute.

Through all those days of waiting, this feeling of the Scorpio woman alone was the genuine article, never once wavering——

Sparkling, sparkling, twinkling, shining, dazzling — you, glittering so vividly.

"——No matter how many hundreds of years go by, always and always and alwaaays, I've loved you, Master."

《The End》

Afterword

Hello there! Huge thanks for keeping me company through another disc-release bonus novel! It's Nezumiiro Neko & Tappei Nagatsuki!

Just when it felt like I'd written the bonus novel for the anime's third season only a little while ago, I've been graciously allowed to write the bonus novel for the fourth season too. Yes — when the interval between anime seasons is short, the deadline for the next disc bonus novel comes around fast as well. That was a blind spot. Well, I had fun writing it!

At any rate, this time's story is centered on Shaula, a character who appears in and gets the spotlight during season four!

It takes the form of her memoirs — of the long, long years she spent as the Star Watcher at the Pleiades Watchtower until Subaru's party arrived — being forcibly dragged out of her by an unscheduled visitor. As I'm sure all you wise readers have already worked out, the Leilani Alnair who appeared this time is the previous Sin Archbishop of Gluttony, and is essentially the papa of the current crop of "Gluttony" siblings. You'll have to fill in a lot of the fine details with your imagination, but the point of the story was that there was, in fact, a history here.

Incidentally, since this bonus novel comes with the first disc volume of season four, I imagine those of you following Re:Zero through the anime alone went, "Wait, what's all this?" But don't worry. Everyone following the original novels is going, "Wait, what's all this?" too. Please think of it as that rare a volume — stuffed with information that has never been told before!

Four hundred years ago: the ones who got through Shaula's days alongside her, a glimpse of the world situation surrounding them, the madcap antics of a band who traveled a Re:Zero world unlike the present one, and the transformations of the world those brought about that connect to the present day —— all of it is a sneak preview of a story that will be told in due time.

It will become a story depicted from a different perspective in the main series eventually as well, but for now, with Shaula having proven the power of a love that doesn't fade even after several hundred years, this story called "SCORPION TALE" comes to a close. As for the very end — the arrival of the one she had awaited with all her heart, and just how overwhelmed Shaula was by it — I'll leave depicting that to the main series.

As of the moment I'm writing this afterword, the anime broadcast hasn't yet reached the point of portraying Shaula's inner feelings, but I have no doubt it will be something wonderful.

Everyone, please look forward to the ending that awaits Shaula's four hundred years of solitude!

Well then, once again, thank you for sticking with a bonus novel all the way to the end!

From here on out, in order to enjoy Re:Zero to the absolute fullest, I'll keep writing with my soul poured into every stroke of the pen, so please continue to support me going forward!

Now then, season four has one more bonus novel to go, so I'm off to bust my hump writing that one too!

The anime turned out fantastic, so savor it a few hundred times over! Well then, see you again!

  • お師様: rendered as "Master" throughout (Shaula's term for Flugel/Subaru). Not in the supplied glossary; flagging for wiki check.
  • かか様 ("Kaka-sama," i.e. "Mama"): left romanized, consistent with the honorific-preservation rule. 赤毛/金髪/ヒゲ are unnamed nicknames in the source; rendered as "Redhead"/"Blondie"/"Beardy."
  • 星番: rendered "Star Watcher." Not in the supplied glossary; check against wiki (may be "Star Keeper"/"Stargazer").
  • 餓馬王 (がばおう) rendered as "gabaou" and 花魁熊 as "oiran bear" — best-guess transliteration/semi-translation for beast names not in the glossary.
  • 『砂時間』 rendered "Sand Time" (literal; puns on 砂時計, hourglass). Flagging in case the wiki uses another rendering.
  • ラジーオタイソー: stylized katakana for radio calisthenics; I kept a stretched "Ra-a-adio Calisthenics" to preserve the odd spelling.
  • 「一団にいた一人の剣《き》」: the furigana き on 剣 is unusual and the referent is unnamed; I rendered it as "a single blade among the group" to keep the metonymy and avoid naming a character.
  • 「……ッス」: an utterance consisting of nothing but Shaula's verbal tic. Rendered as a wordless "...Whoa." since the tic has no English equivalent to strand.
  • Afterword, 「現役の『暴食』たちのパパやという感じ」: the Kansai-copula や makes this read as "is the papa of the current Gluttonys." Given Leilani is described as pregnant, this may be a typo for ママ or a truncated パパやママ; I translated it literally as "papa."
