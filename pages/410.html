Arc 6 Chapter 8: "Baptism of the Dunes"

―The morning of departure came.

It was the day after the day after, with one rest day inserted following the preliminary groundwork and information-gathering. At the entrance to the town of Mirula, Subaru and the others had gathered, having finished their preparations for crossing the dunes and each donning their equipment. Amid all this, Subaru let out a breath of admiration at the sight of the dragon carriage.

"Whoa, so this is..."

"The Earth Dragon for crossing the dunes—strong on sand, of the Gairasu breed. A race adapted to sandy winds and dryness, uniformly large-built but gentle in temperament. Known locally as a native breed prized for how easy it is to handle."

Julius supplemented, glancing at the Earth Dragon harnessed to the carriage, for Subaru who was staring wide-eyed at it. The Augria Sand Dunes had to be crossed on the way to the Pleiades Watchtower. Traveling the vast road ruled by sand, ordinary Earth Dragons' legs couldn't overcome such difficult terrain. Hence the Earth Dragons that had pulled them along the highway thus far were being switched out, and from here on the road would be opened up by this native breed, the so-called Gairasu.

"A native breed, huh. I see. Like the Water Dragon in Priestella—the world really is wide."

Before him, Subaru crossed his arms as he gazed at the yellow-skinned Earth Dragon being coupled to the carriage. Subaru had already spent over a year in this world. He'd thought his sense of wonder at Earth Dragons had faded considerably, that he could hardly recapture that first flush of astonishment—but his knowledge had been too narrow.

"――――"

A yellow Earth Dragon with a flat head, its whole body bristling with spine-like scales. Its build was close to that of Otto's beloved dragon, Frufoo. Frufoo was a breed noted even among Earth Dragons for its stamina, its owner boasting it could run three days and three nights straight. In Subaru's frame of reference, Frufoo's appearance was closest to a Triceratops.

The Earth Dragon before him now, by similar reckoning, resembled an Ankylosaurus. Except its tail was short, and unlike the original, didn't look like it would serve as a weapon. And more distinctive than its skin or facial features was the construction of its legs. The thick, short tips of all four legs curled round, sharp claws serving like spikes, anchoring its massive body to the earth.

"Those look like feet that would do some real damage if it stepped on you by accident."

"The sand of the Augria Dunes is fine-grained. Without feet like these, they'd risk sliding down the slopes. The change in the Gairasu breed's legs is said to be an adaptation for exactly that."

"So it's an adaptation to desert terrain. Horse substitute, camel substitute—what would the Water Dragon be, I wonder. Having animals pull boats isn't something you really see back in my original world, now that I think about it."

A swan boat floated up in Subaru's musing mind, but that was just a boat shaped to resemble a swan—in truth it moved by human pedal power. Nor had he ever heard of a custom of having crocodiles or hippos pull boats, so nothing came to mind as a Water Dragon equivalent. More importantly, though—

"Is it really okay to take my Patrasche somewhere these sand-adapted guys are built for? I can't push our camp's finest lady too hard."

"Your beloved dragon is of the Diana breed, said to be the progenitor of all Earth Dragons. A lineage descending from the very first ancestral Dragon that once claimed dominion over land, sea, and sky alike... she has more than sufficient adaptability, whatever the environment. Regrettably, my Shaknar will have to wait behind in town."

Answering Subaru's question, Julius looked wistfully toward the inn. Shaknar was the name of Julius's beloved dragon, a beautiful blue-skinned Earth Dragon. Beloved though she was, his Earth Dragon, too, had forgotten Julius, her name devoured by "Gluttony." But a well-disciplined Earth Dragon never fails in courtesy no matter who its rider is. Ever since their one-sided reunion in Priestella, she had accepted Julius from the start, and over the month and a half of travel to this point, it seemed a bond close to their former one had been forged once more. All the more reason he must feel a pang of guilt at leaving his beloved dragon behind in town.

"Still, to call your own beloved dragon the finest lady in your circle... I find myself torn between praising you for coming to understand how to treat an Earth Dragon, or offering a word of complaint regarding your assessment of the women around you."

"...Honestly, Patrasche's feminine charm is nothing to scoff at."

His wistful gaze lasted only a moment before Julius immediately concealed his true feelings behind a teasing remark. Sensing his intent, Subaru played along without much thought. Having seen him through it, Julius went to greet the Earth Dragon whose reins he would be holding. Subaru, in turn, looked to the carriage, stepped up, and knocked on the door.

"Carriage just got the wheels swapped, rest stayed the same, huh."

Before there was any response to his knock, Subaru glanced over the modified carriage. Even so, the only conspicuous changes were around the lower body of the vehicle—the wheels—and the sand-guards fitted to the window frames as a countermeasure against the sandy wind.

This carriage, ingeniously outfitted for long-distance travel, was—to put it plainly—equipped with multipurpose functions like a camper van. Toward the front of the cabin was a space lined with seats, while from the middle to the rear was a simple living space—quite the impressive piece of engineering. While ensuring livability, it could seat up to ten passengers. Honestly, the first time Subaru had laid eyes on such a carriage, he'd been struck by how easily it had exceeded his imagination.

"No electricity, no electronics, but there are tools that use magic-stone crafting as a substitute. Humans really are creatures that chase convenience no matter where they end up, huh."

"—I don't know what you're musing so deeply about, but are your preparations finished, Barusu?"

While he tilted his head over the exterior, the carriage door opened and a cold voice rained down at the same time. Turning, he found Ram standing up from the seat nearest the door, looking down at him.

"Preliminary prep's done as of yesterday. Supplies are plenty. All that's left is mental and physical readiness, and I'm plenty good on that front too. What about you?"

"Indeed. Thinking about spending days on end in this carriage, no amount of care could really put one's mind at ease. Weak as we are, if we're attacked in the middle of the dunes, we can't even call for help."

"—It's fine, don't worry."

Ram narrowed her eyes meaningfully, and hearing this, Emilia poked her face in. She seemed to have been tidying the rear of the carriage, and now walked over to Ram's side and patted her shoulder.

"No matter what attacks, I'll properly protect everyone inside. Subaru and Julius are outside too, so it's perfectly fine."

"As expected, reassuring. See, Ram? Emilia-tan says so too, so don't worry."

"Indeed, Emilia-sama. Ram, too, finds that very reassuring. However, it's not only malicious things that attack. Among the ranks are those driven by wicked, lustful impulses..."

As she spoke, Ram's meaningful gaze turned, meaningfully, toward Subaru. The moment it landed, the implication behind her look and words got through, and Subaru's mouth twisted.

"You've spent nearly a month of peaceful nights with me and you're STILL suspicious of that!? I've never once gotten worked up in front of you like that! I don't have that kind of courage!"

"It's pathetic to declare that so loudly, but given the beauty ratio inside this carriage, it wouldn't be strange for anyone to lose their senses somehow. Ram is constantly on edge. Try to grasp a maiden's feelings."

"On edge? Don't you mean threatening?"

At Ram's remark—something along the lines of "boys and girls shall not sit together past the age of seven"—Subaru's voice trembled. Emilia, incidentally, merely tilted her head in puzzlement at Ram's manner of speaking. Same old E-M-T-ness as ever, and Ram's usual insolence. Still, something was bothering him, and Subaru scratched his head listlessly with an "ahh."

"Sis, are you doing okay health-wise?"

"...Unlike you, to ask that. What's brought this on?"

"Your worry might be needless, but is my worry needless? That's all I want to confirm."

"For a Barusu, being so perceptive is awfully cheeky."

Her complexion wasn't poor, nor was her breathing labored. If anything, she looked no different from her usual self—but she didn't deny what Subaru said. She didn't put up a brave front, nor did she try to hide it. In that respect, Ram was unexpectedly sincere.

"Your poison tongue's lost its usual edge. If you're seriously unwell..."

"After spending twenty days to get here, with the goal right in front of us, you want me to stall? It's the usual thing, but today especially, it's not funny."

"Ram, don't talk that way."

Cutting Subaru off, Ram's sharp eyes turned even sharper. At her attitude, Emilia raised her eyebrows and, looking angry, put her hands on her hips. Still standing at Ram's side, she turned her hand toward Subaru.

"Subaru's worried about you too, Ram. And I'm worried about you as well. I've been doing the treatment Roswaal told me to, properly, every day, but..."

"Not sure if calling it 'treatment' is even the right word, but yeah, it's not exactly your specialty. Even with Emilia-tan and Beako doing it, it's not going as well as it would with Roswaal, huh."

"...I have no intention of using that as an excuse. Nor do I intend to be a burden."

"You are worrying us, you know?"

"Enough that the usual Barusu could pretend not to notice."

"I'm not the usual Barusu right now, so I can't pretend not to notice."

It wasn't tit for tat. It wasn't talking past each other either. In any case, there was a quiet exchange of words, and at Subaru's remark, Ram bit her lip in displeasure. Yet her eyes glaring at Subaru still lacked their usual vigor. She must have been aware of it herself. Ram let out a breath and turned back to look inside the carriage. Right beside her seat, she looked at the wheelchair placed there, and the single girl seated upon it. It was a space modified to have a carriage seat removed so a wheelchair could be set there. The sleeping girl, unaware even of this exchange between the people concerned, remained lost in slumber.

"I won't let you leave her behind. To begin with, even if she were left behind, it wouldn't improve Ram's suffering. If anything, being separated from Emilia-sama would be a matter of life and death for Ram right now. Are you trying to kill me?"

"Don't jump to conclusions. I never said one word about leaving her behind from the start. And if it came down to leaving you behind, then obviously we'd be leaving Emilia-tan behind too."

"Mm! Subaru, I absolutely refuse that."

"That's—why I'm saying it's fine! I just wanted to check something with Ram!"

"Something to check?"

"I said that at the very start..."

Having his opening line forgotten amid repeated jumping-to-conclusions, Subaru gave a wry smile. Seeing Emilia and Ram exchange glances, both wearing puzzled expressions, Subaru said:

"Ram. I can tell when you're not your usual self, so if something's wrong, tell me right away. Don't try to hide it or brush it off. Even if you do, I'll drag the truth out and help you anyway."

"――――" "Fufu."

Pointing a finger and declaring it outright, Ram, for once, looked taken aback. Beside her, Emilia put a hand to her mouth, unable to hold back a smile that spilled out despite herself. Eventually letting the initial burst of laughter pass, Emilia kept her lips curved in a somehow happy way, and said,

"I think that part of Subaru is really, really nice."

"...Eh!? Does that mean you're falling for me all over again!?"

"Don't talk about that in front of sleeping Rem. Menace to women."

"Not enemy of women, but menace!?"

Ram gave a "hah" through her nose at Subaru, whose voice cracked. Then she stretched out her slender leg and roughly tried to kick Subaru, still standing on the carriage step, right off.

"Wh-hey, hold on! What are you doing, I'll fall!"

"Falling aside, Barusu's proper place isn't inside but outside. Mount your prized Earth Dragon and secure the safety of our carriage with bloodshot eyes. Now, hurry up."

"Not 'hurry up'—you need to properly deal with what we just talked about..."

"—I listened, and that's that. So go."

Ram said this in a hard voice, her kicks growing sharper still. Kicked around the thigh, Subaru retreated dejectedly, and just before leaving the carriage, exchanged a glance with Emilia. At that look, Emilia gave a nod as if to say "I understand."

"Guess that'll do for now. But anyway, you—mmph."

"Go."

An unexpected kick landed square in his solar plexus, and Subaru, doubling over, fell out of the carriage. Ram promptly shut the cabin door, and letting out a breath, glanced at Emilia. Then—

"...Emilia-sama, what is that expression?"

"No, it's nothing in particular. But somehow, I thought Ram was cute."

"That's an unwelcome assessment. Rather cheeky of you, Emilia-sama."

"Hmm~"

Having let her tongue slip from being teased, Ram, uncharacteristically, stiffened her cheeks at her own blunder. At this change in Ram's expression, Emilia narrowed her eyes, all the more delighted.

"The face you always show Subaru—have you finally decided to show it to me too?"

"...I let my guard down. Please forgive my rudeness."

"I'm not angry at all. If anything, I'm a little happy. Somehow it feels like when you act like that, Ram is opening up her heart to me. I've always been a little envious of Subaru."

At Emilia's guileless response, Ram fell silent for a moment. Then, smoothing down her own hair, Ram spoke with a face drained of venom,

"Emilia-sama has changed as well. When we first met, you looked to me like someone with only a hard, brittle resolve—like glasswork."

"Do I look a little stronger now?"

"If anything, sweeter... it's as though the glasswork became spun sugar."

"Wow, Ram, how harsh."

At Ram's spiteful remark, Emilia finally couldn't hold it in and burst out laughing. And so, for a while longer, the girls' words continued to be exchanged inside the carriage.

—The conquest of the Augria Sand Dunes began several hours later.

Further east from the town of Mirula, some ten-odd kilometers, roughly a little under an hour by carriage, the sand began. As the dry sand and the wind laden with miasma grew stronger, and the grassland underfoot gave way to sandy plain, it was finally time to plunge into the Augria Sand Dunes.

"――――"

This time's formation consisted of one dragon carriage, pulled by a single Gairasu-breed Earth Dragon. Alongside it ran Patrasche, carrying Subaru, the two making their way together across the sand. The flat-faced Earth Dragon wasn't fast, but its heavy-footed gait had a solid sense of stability. Patrasche, with her partner changed, had been wary at first, but after running together for several hours, she seemed to have found something to appreciate in her new companion—her proud expression remained unchanged, but she no longer seemed dissatisfied.

"If I had to say, this Earth Dragon's dissatisfaction right now is probably directed at Betty, I suspect."

So said Beatrice, tucked snugly in Subaru's arms. Handling the reins while holding the girl curled up against his chest, Subaru shook his head, saying, "No, no."

"That's reading too much into it. Patrasche's not that petty a dragon."

"...Perhaps Subaru would be wise to cultivate a keener eye for his surroundings."

Sitting sidesaddle on the jet-black Earth Dragon, Beatrice grasped the hem of her skirt as she said this. Compared to before, Subaru's dragon-riding skill had improved considerably. Until now he'd simply relied on Patrasche's consideration, but now he was at the level of a fledgling apprentice rider. Seated in the saddle, gripping the reins. Seating Beatrice in front of him and holding her from behind while riding double—the posture, too, was beginning to look practiced.

Of course, the only Earth Dragon Subaru rode was Patrasche, and this wasn't remotely the first time he'd ridden double with Beatrice like this. So Beatrice's imagining was, however you looked at it, far too much a persecution complex. Or so he wanted to say, but—

"A proud Earth Dragon doesn't like to carry anyone but its master. And among them, this Earth Dragon of Subaru's is especially so, I suspect. If it were just Betty alone, she would surely never let herself be ridden."

"Isn't that simply because you're bad at riding Earth Dragons?"

"If that were the reason, then there'd be no explanation for why Subaru gets along so well with this Earth Dragon, I suspect."

She had a point. If Patrasche's standard for liking her partner were riding skill, Subaru would have failed the very first test. Since that wasn't the case, there had to be some reason this lady's eye had settled on Subaru. And in fact, Patrasche's attitude toward anyone other than Subaru was fairly harsh.

"Well, it's true that not knowing why I'm liked is unsettling."

"That's right. Subaru isn't the type to be liked without reason, I suspect."

"...In other words, you're saying you have a solid reason for loving me so much?"

"That's of course, Betty is... what are you trying to make me say!?"

With their bodies pressed together, there was no way for Beatrice, her face red, to dodge her own attack. As she was slapped weakly by feeble palms, Subaru soothed her with a "there, there." Just then, as if displeased with this exchange happening on her back, Patrasche raised her upper body, shaking the two of them.

"Oh, whoa!"

At the posture control reminiscent of a motorcycle wheelie, Subaru instinctively pulled Beatrice into a tighter hold. Normally, thanks to the power of the "Divine Protection of Wind Evasion," he felt neither the sway of riding nor the wind, but even so, such exaggerated movements or the change in angle during a sharp turn still had an effect on him.

It was harassment, done with full understanding of that quirk. As Subaru glared reproachfully at the back of Patrasche's head, the Earth Dragon glanced back at them for just a moment, narrowing her eyes as if to say, "You'll bite your tongue."

"Somehow, looking just at the haughtiness, she reminds me of Ram. Might get along surprisingly well."

"Or else, they might clash and never understand each other at all, I suspect."

Subaru and Beatrice exchanged these words with feeling, nodding to each other in agreement. And then—

"—Subaru, Lady Beatrice. It's heartwarming to see the two of you so close, but it's about time to properly brace yourselves."

A voice was thrown at the two of them, so lacking in tension, from off to the side. It was Julius, holding the reins on the driver's seat of the carriage running alongside them. Rather than riding an Earth Dragon alone, he was devoting himself to the role of driver—a role that seemed slightly ill-suited to him, though Julius himself didn't seem to mind. Or perhaps he simply didn't have the leisure to mind it. After all, beside him sat—

"It's nice that Onii-san and Beatrice-chan are getting along and all, but if I get left out too much, I'll get sulky too, you know~"

So said Meili, seated there, casting him a sidelong glance ill-suited to her appearance.

For the conquest of the Augria Sand Dunes, Meili, their countermeasure against Witchbeasts, needed to be constantly at work. As such, on guard against the Witchbeasts inhabiting the dunes, Meili was stationed at the spot best suited for her to respond—that is, the driver's seat, from which she could keep watch on the front of the carriage. And so Julius had ended up perpetually taking on the job of relieving her boredom.

"Your escort duty is being handled by the knight beside you, isn't it? He ought to be way smarter, more stylish, and more elegant than me."

"More than half of that, I don't get at all. And it's not that I have a problem with the knight-san. It's Onii-san I have a problem with."

"Me?"

Twirling her braided hair, Meili glared at Subaru, puffing out her cheeks.

"Onii-san's the one who dragged me along here, so I figure Onii-san has a duty to keep me company, y'know."

"Don't start acting like some unreasonable little kid. Look, that slot's already full up with Beako, okay?"

Beatrice, puffed up, shrugged her shoulders, and Subaru looked at Meili once more. Her sulking manner was age-appropriate, but her mood would have a significant effect on the road ahead. Considering that, neglecting Meili took some courage. That said, if he started worrying about that, in practice, he'd never be able to keep up with everything.

"What you're saying is fair enough. But before you start talking about rights and obligations, how about you first properly fulfill your own role."

"My role, huh."

"We're about to enter the real heart of the dunes soon. I don't think monsters are gonna go 'roar' the moment we step in, but if that happens, calming them down is Meili's job. You get that, right?"

"...Yes, sir. Beatrice-chan gets spoiled, but Onii-san's mean."

He hadn't meant to be mean, but it was a line that could hardly be taken any other way, results considered. Glancing sideways, Subaru noted Beatrice looking somehow satisfied, and raised a hand to Julius. In response, Julius quietly dipped his chin. Placating the small lady's mood would, regrettably, fall to him to take over. More importantly, though—

"Subaru."

"Yeah, I see it."

Beatrice's quiet call, and Patrasche's faint whinny, glaring straight ahead. Drawn back to attention by these, Subaru narrowed his eyes too.

Before them, stretching on and on from Mirula, unmistakable in its silhouette, stood the towering figure of the long, immense tower—and around that tower, the sandy earth that surrounded it, now visible.

Their grand arrival at the sand labyrinth encircling the Pleiades Watchtower—the Augria Sand Dunes.

The famed "sand wind" of the Augria Sand Dunes has particular "sand hours" when the wind blows especially fierce.

Three times a day—morning, afternoon, and dead of night—the sand wind sweeps through, carrying sand and miasma from east to west, a quietly encroaching disaster. The wind is strongest particularly during the dead-of-night period, when the sand wind continues blowing for hours on end. For nearly the whole night, movement is effectively impossible while the sand wind blows through the small hours.

For this reason, the conquest of the Augria Sand Dunes proceeds gradually during the day, with several hours of "sand hour" breaks worked in during the morning and afternoon. The grains of sand blanketing the ground are fine, and just as rumored, their footing was repeatedly thrown off by the poor terrain. The march crawled along at a slow pace, and frustration often mounted. Yet, in the midst of it, there were times Subaru felt somewhat let down. That was—

"The annoyance of the sand wind and the bad footing aside... it's not as bad as I thought it'd be."

Turning his face from the sand wind blowing from upwind, he pulled up the cloth over his mouth to secure his breathing. There was a faint gritty feel of sand between his teeth, but this level of baptism was hardly different from his time in Mirula. The sand-swirling field of vision was tinted yellow, and there was an itchy sensation of sand getting into his clothes, but concretely, that was about the extent of the effect. The dunes' ferocity was minor.

"When I heard 'sand dunes,' I was fully braced for it to be deathly hot."

"It's not that this area lacks greenery due to natural environmental conditions, I suspect. It's that the hanging miasma kills everything, which is why a landscape like this spreads out here. That's why rain even falls on these dunes, and the temperature doesn't swing to any extreme, I suspect."

Subaru's mental image of a desert had, after all, been strongly one of a scorching hell. He'd never once set foot in an actual desert in his original world, but deserts depicted in games and manga always seemed to carry that image of blazing sand. All the more so, the harshness of the Augria Sand Dunes was, compared to his imagination, almost comfortable.

"Given that the nearest town wasn't hot or cold, I guess it'd be strange for the dunes to turn into some extreme scorching hell. Which means the main things I really need to watch out for are wind and Witchbeasts, huh."

"And also, not losing our way... I suspect."

With one worry gone, Beatrice reined in Subaru's somewhat optimistic mood. At her remark from within his arms, Subaru fixed his gaze ahead again, mindful of the wind. Still, at the far end of Subaru's line of sight, the ever-present Watchtower loomed, its imposing form towering up.

"You talk about getting lost or not, but I feel like it'd be unnatural to overlook that thing."

"Betty thinks so too, I suspect. But nothing would be strange no matter what happened, I suspect. Whatever kind of trickster the 'Sage' is, I don't know, but the fact that no one's ever reached it is proof enough."

"――――"

Of course, Subaru had no intention whatsoever of taking this lightly. Realistically speaking, with a structure that colossal as a landmark, losing one's way aside, it's hard to imagine losing sight of the goal itself. But surely everyone who had ever challenged this place had thought the same. And if, thinking that, they still hadn't arrived, there had to be a reason for it. Nothing would be strange no matter what happened. Just as Beatrice pointed out, this was that kind of demonic realm.

"In that case, it's about time to set up the next fallback measure."

Holding his breath, Subaru looked back over the path they'd come. Scattered here and there in the sand were the footprints of Patrasche, carrying them. However, only the most recent ones remained, and even those were overwritten and hidden by shifting sand within seconds. A natural killing trap, prepared by nature itself.

Never mind the Watchtower ahead—when it came to making one lose the path already traveled, this place was exceedingly effective. However, that was only true against those with nothing but ordinary means at their disposal.

"The one behind us is about to disappear from sight. Alright, good timing."

Handling the reins, Subaru instructed Patrasche and drew his body close to the carriage running alongside. The carriage, too, left behind spike-like Earth Dragon footprints and wheel tracks, but those were subject to the same conditions as Patrasche's. So the markers they used to trace their path needed some ingenuity.

"Emilia-tan, if you would!"

"—Okay, got it."

Knocking on the carriage from outside, Emilia appeared beyond the door along with her reply. To Emilia, her whole body wrapped in a white robe as a sand countermeasure, Subaru squinted at something behind them, off in the distance, and pointed at the barely visible, blurred "something" in his field of view.

"Sorry to keep putting you to work, but, sensei, if you please!"

"Subaru's the one doing the lookout duty. It's nothing at all. ...What's 'sensei'?"

"Just a formality when making a request. So, so, please."

"Mm, request received. —There!"

After a bit of light banter, Emilia looked in the direction Subaru pointed. With an expression too adorable to really call a glare, Emilia let out a listless kiai and unleashed her magic power. An instant later, an immense amount of mana swirled in Emilia's palm, and the magical power, gaining strength at an accelerating rate, shot straight up through the sky all at once, the air bursting with a resounding crack.

—And several seconds later, standing erect in the middle of the sandy plain was a giant tower of ice.

"Yeah, well done."

Confirming the finished result, Emilia narrowed her eyes, looking fairly satisfied. It was nowhere near the scale of the Pleiades Watchtower, of course, but amid the dunes, its silhouette stood out remarkably. And these ice towers had, up to this point, been erected one after another along the path Subaru and the others had crossed through the Augria Sand Dunes, spaced so as not to lose the way.

"If footprints won't work as markers, then mark the way with something more conspicuous. An application of magic."

"Still, I really am impressed by Subaru's cunning. You come up with such things."

"Once I realized the dunes weren't hot, I figured this method could work."

Even the ice made by Emilia's magic, it seemed, couldn't defy the laws of nature—vulnerable to heat. All the more so, had this been a real desert, this forbidden trick might not have worked. But here in these dunes, it was an effective strategy. Emilia's standing ice towers were functioning fine as markers. Worst case, if things truly went wrong and they were forced to retreat, they should be able to trace them back home.

"This tower too... yeah, the footing looks fine. Somehow the base is so loose and sandy that I can't shake the fear it might just topple over."

"It's plunged in there good and deep, so I think it's fine, but are you worried?"

"Nobody says 'fine' these days. ...Well, alright."

Sweeping away his unease, teasing Emilia, Subaru lifted his face. At his response, Emilia looked a bit dissatisfied, but for now he answered her discontent with a wry smile. Then, over Emilia's shoulder, he cast his gaze into the carriage behind her. The faces inside should be unchanged, presumably—but there was one person he needed to make sure remained "unchanged" in a different sense.

"Where's Anastasia?"

"What's up, Natsuki-kun? Lookin' for lil' ol' me?"

Right after his eyes wandered, Subaru's shoulders jumped at being addressed by the very person in question. As if cutting into his field of view, Anastasia peeked her face out from beside Emilia. She looked down at the startled Subaru, her pale blue-green eyes narrowing faintly.

"No need to be so surprised, ya know. You knew I was inside too, didn't ya?"

"I knew you were inside, I just wasn't expecting you to sprout out from under Emilia-tan's arm, so that startled me."

"...Hey, Subaru. Something about the way you just said that felt really unpleasant."

"Even I, rare as it is, got genuinely irritated just now. What a coincidence."

Disliked by both Emilia and Anastasia at once, Subaru regrouped. In his arms, Beatrice shrugged her shoulders, but for now he turned back to face Anastasia.

"This dune conquest, we're relying on your knowledge. Guide us properly, alright?"

"Natsuki-kun, you worry too much. I'm comin' along, so the success or failure of this trip is tied to my fate too... there's no way I'd slack off. Relax, would ya."

"...To trust a fox—easy for her to say, I suspect."

At Anastasia's words, Beatrice spoke in a voice only Subaru could hear. That was entirely Subaru's sentiment too, but it wasn't grounds to press the accusation. If it came down to that, then the very act of setting out to conquer this Watchtower in the first place was a mistake of choice. For now, without complaint, both sides should simply do their utmost in their respective roles.

"Right, Anastasia?"

"That's right, Natsuki-kun. If somethin' happens, I'll let Julius know, and once we get close to the spot Echidna mentioned, I'll pass that along too. No need to worry 'bout a thing."

At Subaru's pointedly ironic address, Anastasia—Eridna—showed no sign of unease. For now, it seemed there was no problem proceeding as they were.

"The wind seems to be picking up. Subaru, I'll close it if nothing else, but..."

"...Ah, we're fine. Emilia-tan, take care of everyone inside."

"Mm, leave it to me. Subaru, get along with Beatrice too, okay?"

"No need to worry about that, I suspect. He's basking in it, I suspect."

Putting a hand to the door, he exchanged parting words with Emilia as she returned inside. At Emilia's last joking remark, Beatrice fired back a quip, and her smile vanished behind the closing door. Subaru then made his way around to the front of the carriage, showing his face at the driver's seat.

"Emilia-sama's markers seem to be functioning well. As expected, you're quite the crafty one."

"You could just call it cunning too, y'know."

"I wouldn't put it that way. I envy the flexibility to conceive of ideas that are hard for either of us to come up with. Such an unorthodox trick—I could never have thought of it myself."

"'Unorthodox' is a way more flattering evaluation than 'crafty'!"

Seated at the driver's seat, Julius wore a faint smile, barely noticeable as such. His fine features were now wrapped in white cloth as well, making his expression unreadable. Still, over this past month, they'd exchanged enough words that Subaru could gauge the degree of his banter just from his tone of voice.

"Meili, how's it going over there? You working?"

"You're sayin' that to me? Onii-san and this carriage haven't run into a single bad animal-chan yet, have you~? That's proof I'm workin', isn't it~?"

Peering into the back of the driver's seat, sure enough, there sat Meili, cheeks puffed out. She too had a mantle pulled over her head as a countermeasure against the sand wind, answering Subaru's question with nothing but a displeased voice. At Meili's answer, Subaru brushed the sand from his head while surveying the route ahead.

"Well, sure, but the results of your hard work are kinda hard to see, you know? I can't tell if the Witchbeasts aren't showing up 'cause of you, or if they're just not around this area..."

"...Hmm~, sayin' something like that, huh."

Having gone this far without encountering any obstacles besides sand and wind, Subaru, let down by the gap between the dunes' fearsome reputation and reality, said something careless. At once, Meili said that in a low voice, and, as if pondering something, pinched her own cheeks with both hands and fell silent. At this, Subaru had an honest bad premonition.

"Subaru, apologize to her. That was clearly a slip of the tongue on your part."

"Y-you don't have to tell me, I already know that. Ah, ahh, Meili? That was kind of my bad just now. Not kind of—totally my bad. Please don't get upset. I didn't mean anything by it."

"...Nuh-uh, it's fine~. I'm not mad or anything~. I was just thinkin' there's a point to what Onii-san's sayin', that's all~."

"O-oh yeah? Good. Glad you're way more mature than you—"

"—So, I'll show you clearly what it means to have me around~."

Cutting off Subaru's excuse, apology, and relief, Meili spoke words that carried an oddly ominous ring. At the unsettling resonance of it, Subaru and Julius exchanged glances, hastily trying to weave together words of appeasement. —The very next instant.

"――――"

Patrasche, and the carriage's Earth Dragon too, both stamped the ground hard in unison and came to a halt. The Earth Dragons' instincts sensed the approach of something before anyone else, urging the masters holding their reins to be on guard. And Subaru and Julius, both wide-eyed at the same moment, saw it.

"――――"

Before their eyes, the sandy earth suddenly began writhing as if possessed of will, and immediately something came slithering out from beneath the sand. It was a horrifyingly huge creature, sand clinging all over its body, letting out an unimaginable, ear-splitting shriek from its gigantic mouth. It had no limbs, and the sight of its long, thick trunk-like body twisting about brought a serpent to mind. But at the edges where the skin shared the color of the sand, it had a slick, gleaming texture; a drifting stench of decay clung to it; and horizontal stripe patterns were carved at even intervals along its body. Watching its head slowly lower toward them, Subaru realized its true identity.

It wasn't a snake. —This was an earthworm.

The thickness of its body was more than Subaru could wrap his arms around, and its head and mouth were large enough to swallow four or five grown humans whole. The stench surrounding it resembled a rotting smell, and from its mouth cavity lined with fine teeth, dripping saliva sizzled, evaporating the sand with a hissing sound.

"――――"

The eyeless head turned toward them, making a motion as if sniffing the air. In this instant, Subaru completely forgot to breathe. Through the reins, he could even feel the blankness of Patrasche's thoughts, and inside his arms, Beatrice clutched hard at Subaru's clothes. Sense of smell—the moment that thought struck him, Subaru remembered the Witch's stench he himself carried. If a Witchbeast reacted to that, and if it reacted to it here, this whole group would—

"Ugh, geez, stinky. Hurry up and go home, get outta here already."

"――――"

Before the terror-stricken Subaru and the others, Meili called out with such an offhand voice. At her words, the giant earthworm turned toward Meili, but promptly obeyed the small girl's instruction, burrowing its massive body back down into the hole it had dug.

A few seconds later, the hole from which the earthworm had emerged was completely buried in sand once more, and silence alone dominated the surroundings.

"...That, just now, was probably a Sand Worm. A Witchbeast that likes to lurk beneath sandy ground, but a bit bigger than the ones I'm familiar with."

"A bit bigger, how much bigger?"

"By my knowledge, they're normally about the size of an adult man's arm."

Even at that size, a worm emerging would be a plenty grotesque threat. But the Sand Worm just now was hundreds to thousands of times bigger than what Julius knew. It's said that even native Witchbeasts in the Augria Sand Dunes grow ferocious—perhaps that difference in scale should be considered a variation of the same phenomenon. In any case—

"Th-that huge noise just now, what was that...?"

The carriage's small window opened, and Emilia's face appeared through the window connecting the driver's seat and the cabin. Even without seeing the Sand Worm itself, she must have heard the roar that Witchbeast let out. Emilia looked anxious, but Subaru, in the moment, couldn't find his voice. In place of the frozen Subaru, Meili turned to face Emilia and said,

"No need to worry about it~. I just showed Onii-san and the others how lucky they are to not have to worry about that, that's all~."

"Nah seriously, I really can't hold my head up around Meili-san!"

At Meili's words, Subaru's paralysis broke, and he offered his heartfelt, unstinting praise from the bottom of his soul. At this change, Emilia looked surprised, but Meili, after her own moment of surprise, quickly looked satisfied.

"Really~? You mean it~? Onii-san's got a newfound respect for me~?"

"Yeah, major respect. Without you, I'd have felt firsthand just how dangerous this place really is. Augria Sand Dunes, scary! So scary!!"

He understood, in part, why so many reckless adventurers had challenged this place and never returned. Indeed, that was reckless in the extreme, no question about it.

Calling the dunes' baptism "mild" or "underwhelming" was utterly ridiculous. Long live peace, hail tranquility.

"How very glib, I suspect. Appalling."

"Shut up! You were freaking out too, weren't you! Ahh, scary!"

"B-Betty was just keeping Subaru company because he was tense, I suspect. Don't misunderstand."

"Don't act so tough. You wet yourself a little too, just like me, didn't you? I know it."

"Wet myself, I suspect!?"

Their squabbling flared up, but for the moment, no one scolded them for it. Making a commotion might risk drawing Witchbeasts closer, but the power of the one who could counter that had been more than sufficiently proven. So even Julius made no move to stop the two of them bickering.

Still, he tightened his resolve, aware they were treading on thinner ice than he'd imagined. It was a first day of the dune conquest that would, in hindsight, prove to have carried more than enough meaning in that regard.

Suddenly, a light streaked across the sky, piercing the wind.

"――――"

With a violent roar, the ice tower, struck head-on by the impact, was shattered at its midpoint and came crashing down. The pulverized ice tower was instantly reduced back to mana, and what fell to the ground was swallowed up by the sand piece by piece, vanishing from the world without leaving behind either wreckage or trace.

The light streaked across the sky several more times in succession.

The number of times, by coincidence, matched the number of ice towers the silver-haired girl had erected. What this meant, no one could tell at this point.

Still, the conquest of the sand labyrinth was by no means an easy thing. That much, at least, was an undeniable fact.
