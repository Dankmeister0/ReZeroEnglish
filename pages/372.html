Why couldn't his face be seen? Because Al-san hid his face with a mask.
Rumors speculated he suffered burns or had scars, but I preferred the theory that Priscilla made him wear it on a whim.

Because the mask changed daily, and he seemed to have a personality unrelated to such tragic sentiments.
By the way, Al-san had quite a heavy backstory involving a prosthetic left arm, but thanks to Priscilla's family power or something, it was quite high-tech. Once before, he opened a can of peaches with a can-opener function. It's like a Swiss Army knife prosthetic.

At this rate, a catfight could break out, but if one did, the one who'd suffer would be Anastasia.

Students who were suppressed and dragged into the infirmary were confirmed to emerge quiet as different people, no matter how rowdy they had been. Because they adamantly refused to answer questions about what happened, rumors of "Bowel Practicum of Terror and Despair" circulated behind the scenes.

Did a statement like that become a flag?

Changing shoes at the shoe locker, it was time to head straight to the classroom.

"—Hey, Subaru! Receive!"

Then, a small foot landed on top of my prepared hands,

"Blue Mountain Receive!"

"Fungah!"

A light body flew up—not quite as high as a volleyball, but onto the shoe locker right behind me. Turning around to see what was going on,

"Ssh!"

What in the world was happening? While Rem and I blinked our eyes in confusion,

Likewise from down the hallway, a handsome guy with a refreshing smile came running up.
With burning red hair and sky-blue eyes—or rather, a perfect existence constructed entirely from striking features: Reinhard.

"'Sup, what's wrong?"

"Felt-chan? Um..."

Surely a handsome guy like Reinhard wouldn't be short of options, even if saying he had his pick of the lot was an exaggeration.

"Alrighty, saved my skin~"

A sound of landing lightly on the floor came from behind, accompanied by a tomboyish female voice.
Blonde hair, short stature, red eyes, and a snaggletooth as her charm point—a girl named Felt. Reinhard's sought person and the luckiest girl in the whole school... though in her mind, "unwilling" was attached.

"Reinhard-san is so passionate, yet Felt-chan dislikes it?"

"Of course I dislike it! Besides, gettin' tossed around by a misunderstanding ain't fun either. He just spotted someone a bit novel and got into a weird mood. It's the same feeling as discovering a rare beast in an unexplored region! Give me a break!"

It was a cold, brutal opinion against Reinhard, the idol of all girls in school.
Felt was speeding down a shojo-manga-like Cinderella story, but unlike those around her, the perspective of the party involved was cool to the extreme.

As Reinhard's friend, I sympathized with how harshly he was described, but—

Having only received advances until now, Reinhard's approach when making advances himself was clumsy in its own right. Because of that, Felt had parts where she looked at him with suspicion, making this misaligned perception truly frustrating to watch.

A pale-faced Felt fled in the opposite direction from where Reinhard departed.
She'd be found two minutes later, and a fierce battle of speed would unfold again inside the school, but well, that's another story.

Rem's agreement blended into my wish, submitting our hope to God.

Regrettably, Beako didn't have a cute enough personality to cry over this... Wait, she might cry. She unexpectedly has weak spots.
