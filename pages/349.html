Arc 5 Chapter 33: "The Battle for City Hall"

—The city of Priestella had fallen into a silence that made this morning's chaos feel like a lie.

Walking over the cobblestones, he glanced briefly at the flowing canal. The water ran clear and unclouded, still obediently tracing its set course. The strange mechanism by which a single canal carried currents flowing in opposite directions side by side remained intact—if only the crowds would return, one might almost believe the city's present crisis had been nothing but a dream.

"Boss, we ain't got time to dawdle."

"Yeah, I know. Gotta figure every second we're late retaking City Hall bumps the danger up ten percent or so."

"At that rate we'd hit the limit in eleven seconds flat. Well, guess I ain't one to talk!"

At the head of the group, Garfiel narrowed his eyes in protest at Ricardo's booming voice. But the demi-human's profile showed no sign that the familiar scolding meant anything to him at all. Cleaver slung over his shoulder, stamping the ground with each powerful stride, Ricardo paid no mind whatsoever to the tension and guilt weighing on Garfiel.

Ricardo looked like his usual self. Garfiel, clearly, did not. Even so, three of Ricardo's subordinates—people he thought of as family—had been hurt. That his heart was anything but calm had already been made plain enough back at the shelter. Garfiel, meanwhile, had set aside his usual baseless confidence and recklessness, casting a wary eye over his surroundings with a vigilance that bordered less on caution than on outright fear.

"...Not that I'm one to talk, either."

It wasn't only the two of them who couldn't hold onto their usual composure. Subaru himself, with his injured right leg and Beatrice's departure—and above all, not knowing whether Emilia was safe—couldn't hide the anxiety gnawing at him. Pushing too hastily for a change in the situation was itself a risk, but experience had taught him that letting the enemy keep the initiative was the surest way to invite the worst outcome.

Each in their own way, the members of the City Hall assault team carried their burdens. Without a single encounter with Witch Cultists or rioters, they reached the meeting point agreed upon with the members from the other shelters. And there, already waiting—

"Subaru-dono."

"Thank goodness. You're unharmed."

Wilhelm and Crusch brightened at the sight of Subaru and the others arriving safely. Julius, too, was there of course, and he smoothed his hair back with a light touch of his hand before speaking.

"I imagine you're anxious about Emilia-sama, but are you sure it's all right for you to join us here?"

"I'm not so much of an idiot that I'd lose sight of what matters most for everyone. And—it pisses me off to say it, but given what the bastard who took her is after, I don't think we need to worry about him hurting Emilia. Not for now, anyway."

"I can imagine how you feel. If Anastasia-sama were in the same position, I doubt I could stay calm either."

Nodding at the considerate words, Subaru turned to face Wilhelm. The old swordsman stood with his arms crossed and eyes shut, a quiet killing intent radiating from his entire body. What he was picturing, what thoughts churned behind his closed eyes, Subaru couldn't guess. But Wilhelm, noticing Subaru's gaze, opened his eyes, reached into his coat, and held out a communication mirror to him.

"Subaru-dono, as we discussed, this is a communication mirror. There may not always be room to spare attention in the midst of battle, so I ask for your care with it."

"Got it. That should be all of them distributed as planned, I think."

Subaru tucked the mirror he'd been handed into his pocket and nodded back at Wilhelm. It had been agreed that the communication mirrors would be carried by whoever was in charge of liaising among the members of the three shelters.

Subaru, the least combat-capable member of the assault team, carried one. Ferris, who made rounds between the shelters, carried another. And the last was held by Anastasia, who was gathering information. Ideally, this would let the three locations coordinate closely with one another.

"Now then, let's go over this once more. At the point when Garfiel-sama scouted it, City Hall was being guarded by two Witch Cultists—a man wielding a greatsword and a woman wielding a longsword. Is that correct?"

"Yeah, that's right. Both of 'em are freaks with a blade. Anybody less than me, they'd get sliced clean in half before they knew what hit 'em."

To make sure everyone gathered was on the same page, Crusch took the lead in organizing what they knew. She gave a small nod at Garfiel's answer, then looked between Wilhelm and Julius in turn.

"In addition to those two, we should expect the Sin Archbishop of 'Lust,' believed to be occupying City Hall, along with further Witch Cultists. Does either of you know anything about the Sin Archbishop of 'Lust' that's been passed down?"

"I'm afraid not. At least, among the Royal Guard, as far as I know, no one has ever spoken of 'Lust.' The most notorious ones were 'Sloth' and 'Greed,' but as for 'Greed,' this time it's Subaru who—"

Mid-sentence, Julius turned the conversation over to Subaru. Subaru nodded.

"Yeah, 'Greed' showed up too, and I don't think what I heard before was a lie. Except... the Empire, wasn't it? The claim that he's strong enough to beat their strongest knight—I've got my doubts about that. Honestly, the way he moved was amateurish enough that even I could've held my own. But... none of my attacks worked on him."

"That ain't just you bein' weak, is it?"

"Not on that level, no. The Sin Archbishops turned on each other, and 'Wrath' dumped fire all over 'Greed'—but 'Greed' didn't even dodge, and he came out without so much as a burn, not even a scorch mark on his clothes. I don't get the trick behind it."

For lack of a better word, let's just call Regulus's Authority "invincibility." If it really were nothing more than a convenient label, that would be the worst kind of power—one offering no real foothold for a counter at all. Subaru wanted to believe it couldn't possibly be so straightforwardly unreasonable.

"If our 'strongest' were here, we probably wouldn't have to worry about this..."

"It's unthinkable that Reinhard wouldn't show himself in a situation where innocent people are at risk of being hurt. We should assume something has happened to him too—something keeping him from moving. Including, quite possibly, that he's clashing with other Witch Cultists elsewhere."

Julius alone shot Subaru a meaningful glance, one only Subaru would understand. Before the incident began, both Felt and Reinhard had apparently had some kind of contact with Heinkel. Subaru could only hope the ugly possibility forming in his mind about why they couldn't move wasn't the truth.

"There's something else I want to nail down. That name 'Lust' gave himself—Capella Emerada Lugunica. What's the point of claiming to be Lugunica royalty?"

"Ain't he just messin' with us? Everybody knows the royal family's wiped out."

"Or perhaps it's some kind of message? It feels a bit premature to dismiss it as mere mischief."

In response to Subaru's question, Ricardo and Crusch offered differing views. Both opinions were worth considering, in the sense that either was plausible for the Witch Cult. "Lust" was someone whose rotten personality came through in his voice alone. As Ricardo said, there was plenty of chance it was simple mockery, and it was equally conceivable it was some vicious riddle. But it was Wilhelm, raising his hand, who offered a view different from either.

"There is one thing that comes to mind."

"Comes to mind how?"

"I cannot say regarding Capella, but the name Emerada Lugunica does ring a faint bell. Though I have no direct acquaintance with the matter... there was, in fact, a person by the name of Emerada-sama once in the Lugunica royal family."

"—!"

Everyone's eyes went wide at the unexpected information, and Wilhelm touched a hand to his chin.

"This goes back to before the Demi-Human War—before my own time in service, so more than fifty years ago now, I believe. In the Lugunica royal family of that era, there was said to be a person named Emerada-sama, one both remarkably beautiful and wise."

"And 'Lust' is claiming that same name, Emerada? What for?"

"The intent behind it, I cannot say. But I understand that Emerada-sama fell ill young and passed away from it. However... no state funeral was ever held for her."

That no funeral had been held upon the death of a member of the royal family was a strange thing to hear. Subaru tilted his head, wondering why, and Wilhelm knit his brow as if considering how best to put it.

"The official reason given was that the political situation of the time made it difficult. But the truth of it, I gather, was that public sentiment simply did not wish for it."

"The public didn't wish for it?"

"Emerada-sama was beautiful and deeply wise, but... her nature was said to be cruel in the extreme, harboring a darkness beyond what anyone else could fathom. For that, she was regarded as the black sheep even within the Lugunica royal family, and even the fact of her death was kept hidden for some time."

It was an unconfirmed story, one touching on the dignity of a kingdom to which he had once pledged his sword, and it clearly pained him to tell it. Wilhelm's words grew halting in the latter half. In its place, though, the sheer rottenness of "Lust" claiming the name Emerada came into sharp relief.

"It couldn't possibly be the real person, so... 'Lust' calling himself Emerada must be—"

"A jab at the Lugunica royal family, and tasteless harassment aimed at anyone who knows Emerada-sama's name. A roundabout method meant to sow suspicion and paranoia, I would think."

At Wilhelm's conclusion, everyone present let out a sigh, their faces twisted with something between disgust and despair. Unlike Subaru, Garfiel, and Ricardo, whose loyalty to the kingdom ran thin, the inner feelings of Crusch, Wilhelm, and Julius were impossible to guess. It was a vicious act—kicking dirt at the royal family on the way out the door, mocking them all the while.

"Still... Capella. Capella, huh."

"Does that name mean something to you?"

Julius, sharp-eyed as ever, noticed Subaru pressing a hand to his forehead and muttering "Lust's" name under his breath. Subaru made a sour face at the attention, prefacing his answer with a "nah," before continuing.

"It's not just Capella. Regulus and Sirius too. And Petelgeuse, now that I think about it... Nah, it's hard to believe there's any real meaning to it."

"Whaddya mean, boss? There's some meaning behind their names or somethin'?"

"It's nothing big. Just... I've got a feeling their names might all match star names from back home. A little, anyway."

"Star names?"

It was Crusch who blinked with interest at Subaru's answer. Seeing that the others were interested too, Subaru scratched his head.

"Don't get your hopes up or anything. It's just, back where I'm from, stars get names, and a lot of them happen to overlap with the Sin Archbishops' names. I'm kind of into star names and the stories behind them, so I know a fair bit."

"That's a rather unexpected hobby for you. Stars, of all things."

"For the record, my own name, Subaru, comes from a star too. Anyway, that doesn't matter right now. That's all there is to it. Sorry for the boring tangent."

Brushing off everyone's questioning stares, Subaru felt a twinge of embarrassment. But as Subaru tried to close the subject, Crusch held up a hand to stop him.

"Wait, please, Subaru-sama. Are you certain that's really all there is to these star names? That it's purely coincidence their names match? Nothing more?"

"What do you mean, nothing more? What else could there be?"

"For instance, is it possible the star names from your homeland, Subaru-sama, are the very root of their names? The Witch Cult is a group shrouded in mystery from its founding to its activities. You mustn't shut off a possible connection on your own before even considering it."

"—"

Taken aback by Crusch's unexpected insistence, Subaru found himself thinking it over despite his surprise. Honestly, Subaru had assumed the correspondence with star names was mere coincidence. After all, this was another world—there was no reason at all for the star names he knew to have made their way here. But could he really say that with such certainty?

Here in Priestella, Subaru had already seen with his own eyes that Japanese-style architecture had been carried over in the form of so-called wafu construction. In Kararagi, okonomiyaki—and perhaps even the Kansai dialect itself—had taken root as culture, thanks to Hoshin. Perhaps the founding of the Witch Cult, too, was tangled up with modern knowledge Subaru recognized. Some tasteless attempt at naming Sin Archbishops after stars, actually carried out.

"Petelgeuse. Regulus. Sirius. Capella..."

"Exactly. You said those star names come with stories attached, Subaru-sama. What kind of stories are they? There might be a connection."

"Stories, huh, stories..."

As his ties to his original world grew ever more distant, he dug up memories of the stars that had already begun to fade. He'd loved it, that star atlas—loved it dearly. Upon learning that his own name came from a star, Subaru had devoured that atlas hungrily, engraving countless stars into his memory. Among them were stars bearing the very same names as the hateful Sin Archbishops—

"The Giant's armpit, and the Hand of al-Jauza."

"Huh?"

Hearing the word "armpit," one she hadn't expected at all, Crusch tilted her head. But Subaru, not noticing her reaction, grabbed her slender shoulders and shook them, pressing in close as he spoke.

"The Hand of al-Jauza. That's it. It was the Hand of al-Jauza!"

"S-Subaru-sama? What are you... the Hand of al-Jauza?"

"It's the etymology of Petelgeuse—no, wait, Betelgeuse! His power is the 'Unseen Hand,' and the origin of Betelgeuse's name is the Hand of al-Jauza!"

Call it a stretch if you want to laugh at it—go ahead and laugh. But was this really just coincidence? Was it really the kind of match you could laugh off as a stretch? As someone who actually knew his stars, Subaru wanted very badly to point out that it wasn't Petelgeuse but Betelgeuse. That, in fact, was exactly why he'd been slow to notice all this.

"Sirius means 'the scorcher,' 'the glowing one'—hmm, that one's iffy? He did breathe fire, but it doesn't line up as neatly as I'd like. ...Regulus means 'little king.' That's a perfect fit for that self-centered bastard's whole worldview, isn't it! And then there's Capella...!"

"Capella?"

"It's the she-goat! It was the she-goat! Capella means she-goat!"

His memory whirring into overdrive, the connections between the star lore and the Sin Archbishops took on real meaning in Subaru's mind. Beaming with a triumphant smile, Subaru puffed out his chest as if to say, well, how about that. Hearing Subaru's answer, Crusch—still gripped by the shoulders—furrowed her brow in dismay. When she turned her gaze to the remaining four, they too wore troubled expressions.

"An armpit, was it." "And 'the glowing one,' apparently." "A puny little king, huh." "A she-goat, was it."

"—Huh?"

At the puzzled reactions from the four of them, Subaru finally realized, belatedly, that the information he'd unearthed had landed with far less impact than he'd hoped.

"Subaru-sama, I'm sorry. This is all because my thinking fell short."

And so, he'd managed to make even Crusch feel apologetic about it.

The attempt to close in on the Sin Archbishops' roots through the correspondence between star names and their names had failed spectacularly. But they couldn't afford to dwell on that failure forever. Before launching into a lightning operation where speed was everything, they had to avoid wasting any more time. And so, having shared everyone's abilities and combat styles, Subaru and the others set out.

Along the way, members of the Iron Fang who'd accompanied Julius and Crusch scurried about, securing the path while maintaining a defensive posture, and the six of them safely reached the straightaway leading to City Hall.

"Ain't changed one damn bit from last time."

Garfiel snorted, clicking his tongue as he confirmed there was no sign of the enemy. By his account, it was right as they'd charged down this straightaway to storm City Hall that they'd been ambushed. For now, though, neither Garfiel's nose nor Subaru's eyes could detect any sign of those figures. If they weren't there, all that was left was to take City Hall outright. In terms of raw numbers, fewer enemies should have been welcome, but—

"—"

Garfiel, a shield equipped on each hand; Ricardo, resettling the cleaver on his shoulder; and Wilhelm, his gaze as calm and still as a windless sea, fixed quietly on the plaza. For these three, what did it mean that no sign of the enemy could be found? For Wilhelm especially, there should have been far too much he wanted to confirm.

"It's an open area. I had my quasi-spirits take a quick look around, but there's no route that would let us sneak in unseen. It seems there's nothing for it but to go straight in through the front, out in the open."

Julius, who had sent several of the six quasi-spirits under his command to check the surroundings, reported this to Subaru and the others. It was an unpleasant location—hard to attack, easy to defend.

"Can't you just have your spirits check inside directly? Even just knowing how many enemies there are and how they're positioned would make this a lot easier."

"Unfortunately, my buds aren't capable of carrying out or reporting back on orders that complex. And we can't rule out the enemy having countermeasures against spirits, so I have to say it's difficult."

"Well, guess I can't ask the impossible. Damn it, so it really is straight through the front, huh."

If the commotion spread further, there was no telling what kind of example might be made of someone inside City Hall. But letting time pass would only invite the situation to worsen further. The Witch Cult claimed they'd make their demands known in their next broadcast, but negotiating with them was never going to be possible.

"Let's go with the plan. Depending on how strong the enemy turns out to be, but the basic idea is to gang up on them one at a time. Finish them off fast, then round up everyone occupying the place inside."

"Optimistic, but let's hope it goes that smoothly."

Julius answered Subaru's words with a wry note, and everyone rose to their feet at once. Without any signal, they all broke into a run down the straightaway together, surging into the plaza before City Hall.

There was no sign of the enemy appearing from anywhere. Garfiel led the charge, with Wilhelm and Julius following behind him. Ricardo came next, and Crusch and Subaru brought up the rear side by side. Subaru felt no wrongness at all in his right leg. It was a strange state—no pain, no sensation—but it didn't hinder his running one bit. And then—

"—Here it comes!"

Two figures dropped down from directly overhead, aimed straight at Garfiel in the lead. Seeing a broad blade and a slender longsword flash through the air, Crusch, witnessing the ambush from the rear, gave a valiant shout and drew her sword.

—A Hundred Men, One Stroke was unleashed.

What cleaved the sky at an angle was Crusch's finishing blow, an application of her Divine Protection of Wind Reading. It was an ultra-long-range slash of wind, capable of extending its reach dozens of meters, so long as the target was within sight.

The same strike that had once dealt the White Whale a grievous wound tore forward and struck the two figures in midair dead on. The clang of steel meeting steel rang out, and the hulking figure and the slender one went spinning through the air.

"Did that do it!?"

"No, it was blocked! It didn't connect!"

The ambush and the counter-ambush against it had ended in a wash. Both figures in black spun through the air and landed on the cobblestones, each having completely blocked the blade of wind with their own weapon. A pair of greatswords, and a single-edged longsword. Wrapped head to toe in black garb, their appearance was precisely the worst fashion sense that Witch Cultist fanaticism could produce.

Showing no effect from the slash at all, the two figures leaned forward slightly and kicked off the ground to intercept. But just before that—

"You blocked Crusch-sama's strike, but how about this?"

Three differently colored lights circled above the figures' heads, and the released radiance poured down upon the Witch Cultists. The six quasi-spirits under Julius's command, split into two groups of three, unleashed magic on the hulking man and the woman. The unfamiliar magical light bore down with tremendous pressure, forcing both enemies to their knees.

As the enemies struggled beneath the pressure, Garfiel and Wilhelm went for the woman, while Ricardo, cleaver raised high, leaped at the hulking man.

"Take this!"

"—Hah!"

"This ends it!!"

A blow whose impact would crush flesh on a direct hit, and a silver flash born of the sword demon's full effort, both raced in. The great cleaver's strike, born of a strength unreasonable even among demi-humans, likewise came down in a straight line, meant to split an unguarded skull. A killing distance, a killing timing—and yet—

"—"

The kneeling woman spun the longsword in her hand and swept it low at Garfiel's and Wilhelm's feet. As the two of them instinctively hopped back to dodge the strike, she spun her body along the same arc as the sword, her long leg extending to coil around Garfiel's neck and drag him back into the magic's effective range.

"Wha—!?"

By closing in on Garfiel, the woman placed herself outside the magic's effective range. Her knee smashed into the face of Garfiel, whose movement was pinned by the pressure, and her free arm—the one not holding the sword—seized his left arm, using the shield at the end of it to deflect Wilhelm's slash with ease. Garfiel let out a cry of pain at the feat, and Wilhelm a low groan.

In return for his hesitation came a spinning kick driven into the old swordsman's torso. The kick punched clean through his trained abdominal muscles, folding Wilhelm's body into a bent shape. He somehow managed to keep his footing, but the woman added another half-turn to her spin, and her other leg came around in a spinning back kick that slammed him into the ground.

"—"

Meanwhile, Ricardo's strike, too, was stopped before it could crush the hulking man's skull. Against the cleaver strike aimed at crushing his crouched skull, the hulking man simply dropped both his greatswords. Then he swung up his now-empty arm and slid it in between the cleaver and his own head.

"You outta your damn mind!?"

The consequence of that misjudgment played out exactly as one would expect: both arms severed. Even with the cleaver's dull edge, the force and power behind the swing were on another level entirely. The hulking man's thick arm was severed near the elbow, and both arms went flying, spraying dark, murky blood.

"—"

With the hulking man now missing both arms, Ricardo pressed in further, swinging the cleaver in a horizontal sweep. A strike powerful enough to fell even a great tree came in, aimed at sending the man's head flying. But the hulking man scooped up one of the greatswords he'd tossed onto the cobblestones with yet another arm, and swung it up to knock the strike aside.

"What the hell!?"

Unfazed by Julius's magic, the hulking man unleashed slash after slash at Ricardo with an agility that belied his massive frame. He'd nullified Julius's magic with a curtain of blood spraying from the stumps of his severed arms. A resolve that would sacrifice an arm in an instant's judgment, yet still see through the magic's effect and weakness to act on it.

Backed by the hulking man's evident combat experience, his skill with the greatswords began to overwhelm Ricardo's defense. As Ricardo managed to knock both blades aside but reeled back off balance, yet another of the man's arms drove into his torso. Ricardo groaned at the force of the blow, and another fist still slammed into his face, sending the demi-human's huge frame flying backward.

"—"

"—"

With the three of them beaten back and defenseless, the woman and the hulking man raised their weapons to finish them off. It was then that the remaining three, having barely caught up, threw themselves into the fray—

"Composite magic, Fail Gohr!"

"Connect!"

"Please!"

At Julius's incantation, wind whipped up, and vermillion flames swirled into the churning air. The resulting fire tornado bore straight down on the woman's figure, and she, who had been about to press her advantage against Garfiel and the others, leaped back a great distance.

Then, overlapping voices like prayers accompanied a slash and a whip-strike, both unleashed at once. Crusch's blade of wind, and, a beat behind it, Subaru's whip, both struck the hulking man's body. The diagonal slash rocked his massive frame, and a sound like silk tearing carved a painful wound across his chest. But neither attack did the hulking man any real damage. What actually stopped his follow-up was Ricardo, falling backward, landing a kick straight up into the man's jaw.

"Serves ya right, ya bastard!"

"This isn't the time for that—get back, Ricardo, now!"

Ricardo rolled backward with the momentum of his kick, scooped up his cleaver, and retreated while wiping the blood from his nose. Subaru and Crusch joined him there, squaring off against the hulking man. Off to the side, Subaru's eyes went wide as he saw the fire tornado slam directly into the woman.

"What the hell is that!? You can pull off flashy magic like that!?"

"It's nothing but a bluff. Not refined enough as a killing spell. In fact..."

Julius answered Subaru's praise with a bitter note, and the proof of his words appeared right where his gaze led. Against the fire tornado engulfing her entire body, the woman gave a single flash of the longsword in her hand—and that alone severed the wind's core, causing the tornado to lose its balance, collapse, and vanish.

The woman's terrifying sharpness with a blade. And, astonishingly, the hulking man too—

"Come on, you're kidding me..."

Opening the front of his black garb revealed the hulking man had multiple arms besides the ones severed at the elbow. He picked up his own severed arms and pressed the wounds together, a wet, sticky sound of flesh and blood entangling. In the next instant, the severed arms had been rejoined, scars remaining but reattached. As if testing the sensation, the reconnected arm gripped a greatsword and swung it around with a showy flourish. Both enemies looked very much still in the fight.

"And meanwhile, our first strike's already been shot to hell."

A glance to the side showed Garfiel and Wilhelm, spared further harm thanks to Julius's cover, giving each other's wounds emergency treatment with Garfiel's healing magic. But in reality, it was undeniable that Garfiel and Wilhelm, together, had both been handled at once. The sense of despair that left behind wasn't something a little effort was going to wipe away. Still, to say they had no way to fight back at all would be wrong.

"We've learned they're insanely strong up close... but ranged attacks connect."

Julius's magic, Crusch's blade of wind, even Subaru's whip—all of them landed. It was possible the last of those was simply written off as having no real effect even if it connected, but the other two were undeniably attacks capable of deciding the fight on their own.

"—"

Subaru looked at the two of them, meaning conveyed in the glance, and Julius and Crusch both gave a small nod. Garfiel and Wilhelm, too, must have grasped the enemies' skill at close range by now. And Ricardo had never held to any principle about fighting one-on-one in the first place. The close-combat members would halt the enemies' movement, and in that opening, they'd drive in powerful magic. That, most likely, was the best path to victory while keeping their losses to a minimum.

With everyone's intent aligned, they synchronized their breathing for the next assault. And it was right as they moved to close in—

"Reckless, hopeless, and utterly witless—a real triple threat! Seriously, hoooow do you filthy hunks of scum-meat manage to live with yourselves, being this stupid and this ugly and this shallow all at once? If it were me, I could nevaaaer stand it! Kyahahahahaha!"

A shrill, idiotic cackle, wholly out of place on a battlefield, suddenly cut into the scene. Everyone understood, though, that this voice's owner appearing meant things were about to get even worse. So Subaru and the others tensed in dread, their eyes darting about in search of the source. Where—where was it? As if mocking Subaru and the others for searching—

"Where the hell are you lookin', huh? This is exactly why you dim-witted little dumbasses are hopeless—it's sad, really. Now pop those sesame-seed eyes of yours nice and wide, rack that empty little skull of yours for all it's worth, and carve deep into your filthy little souls exactly who I am!"

"—No way."

Beside Subaru, who was scanning their surroundings, Crusch let out a hoarse, breathless gasp. Her amber eyes wide, she craned her neck upward, staring at the sky above. Sensing instinctively that "Lust" was somewhere in the direction of her gaze, Subaru looked the same way.

Where their eyes landed was the rooftop of City Hall—close, and yet far. The mockery rained down on Subaru and the others from up there, its owner looking down on the ants below. And, in fact, that description could not be denied. Because—

"Kyahahahaha! What is that, that face! That dumbstruck idiot face! Did you put that on special, just for me? 'Cause if you did, that's such a magnificent impression of a monkey I could just about give you a prize for it! Want my spit? Bet you'd be thrilled to bits with my spit! For you filthy hunks of scum-meat, that'd practically be a drooled-over treasure, wouldn't it!"

The idiotic cackling echoed on, and Subaru and the others stared up at it, stunned. The two swordfighters, the hulking man and the woman, showed no reaction whatsoever to the presumed ally above them.

Looking down on that battlefield scene, "Lust"—

"So, allow me to properly introduce myself! I'm the Witch Cult's Sin Archbishop in charge of 'Lust'—"

—A single black dragon, claiming the name of "Lust," looked down and sneered.

"—Capella Emerada Lugunica-sama, that's who! Now die! All you rotten hunks of scum-meat!"
