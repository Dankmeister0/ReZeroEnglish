Arc 4 Chapter 66: "A Crimson Snowscape"

Seeing Subaru emerge from the tomb alone, Garfiel's hostility had risen to the point of piercing the skin.

The chill inside and outside the tomb was worlds apart, after all. Compared to the tomb, which had retained some measure of warmth, the frigid 'Sanctuary' stripped away the body heat and stamina of anyone standing still, second by second, in great swaths. The blizzard that never let up, the white curtain that kept blocking his view. His breath felt like it would freeze solid the moment it left him, and Subaru couldn't hold back the shivering from the very core of his body.

Garfiel glared at Subaru, who hugged his own shoulders and shuddered. Baring his fangs and clacking them together, Garfiel turned his attention to what lay behind Subaru, but—

"Doesn't look like she's gonna stick her face out from behind ya, huh, oi."

"Yeah, she's not coming out. Emilia's sleeping inside right now."

"Sleepin', is she?"

"She's exhausted. Seems like she's been waking up, taking on the 'Trial,' waking up, taking on the 'Trial,' over and over for two days straight. Her heart and body are both worn thin. She hasn't even eaten. A girl's been pushing herself way too hard."

Thinking of Emilia's state of mind—having stubbornly kept challenging the 'Trial' and still failing to break through it—Subaru could imagine her frustration, her sense of her own inadequacy. That was, precisely, the same helplessness Subaru himself had chewed on again and again.

"..."

Deep within the tomb, in the chamber of the 'Trial,' Emilia slept with a happy face. Recalling the warmth of Emilia's body as she whispered her blind love for Subaru and kept holding him tight, Subaru was struck down by affection hot enough to boil his blood, and regret bitter enough to make him want to die.

He could still remember Emilia, her cheeks flushed, her voice trembling with passion, trying to drown Subaru in every word he'd ever wanted to hear, every emotion she had. How many times had Subaru thought of just sinking into that soft corruption, going under together with Emilia? No one could possibly know.

Cutting himself free of a temptation from Emilia that could have led even a god astray, Subaru had come back out here. He had no intention of telling the Emilia sleeping inside about what was happening outside. And Subaru had no intention of letting Garfiel's malice reach Emilia either.

Contrary to Subaru's quiet resolve, the flames of Garfiel's fury showed no sign of weakening. Kicking up the snow at his feet, Garfiel kept baring his sharp white fangs, clacking them together.

"Ya didn't drag the half-demon out. Snow ain't showin' any sign of lettin' up either. Ya come back here draggin' a face like a wet rag and no gift to show for it—so how the hell d'ya plan on makin' up for that, ah?"

"—Emilia said she loves me."

"...... ...... ...........Ha?"

Perhaps it was because Subaru's remark, cutting into the momentum, was so utterly out of place. For a moment Garfiel wore the look of someone who had no idea what he'd just been told. But quickly deciding he was being mocked, his expression hardened even further.

"Ya ain't seein' the situation clear, huh—looks like it ain't just the half-demon inside who's lost it, it's you too! The nerve, spoutin' some damn love-struck nonsense in a situation like this, oi! Oi! Oi! Ahh!?"

His anger taking on heat, the snow that touched Garfiel's body turned to white mist and evaporated. It wasn't an optical illusion that his body looked a size larger—he was starting to shift his physical state from human toward the great tiger.

Even watching that happen, Subaru's expression didn't waver. With the same face he'd worn when he said the words Garfiel had just called love-struck nonsense, he looked at Garfiel with dry eyes. In front of the enraged Garfiel, Subaru repeated himself.

"Emilia said she loves me. Said all she needs is for me to be there."

"—You—"

"With that cute face, in that spoiled voice, with gestures that made me tremble, so close I felt like I'd melt, touching so near I could feel her breath... she said it. She said it to me."

"So what the hell's that got to do with anything!? That half-demon bein' stuck on you—I knew that the second I walked in here. If you're sayin' you two got your feelings sorted out and want a damn blessing, I'll break it down for ya real simple—"

His snarling began mixing in with his curses, Garfiel's hostility pushing his body toward transformation. And then, with Garfiel looking ready to pounce at any moment, his words drove into Subaru. —It was already past the limit.

"...There's no way. No way in hell."

"Ahh? Can't hear ya. Speak up—"

"—There's no way in hell Emilia would say she loves me!!"

"———!"

Lifting his face, Subaru screamed. Even Garfiel fell silent before that surge of emotion. Glaring at Garfiel as he flinched back, Subaru let his heart explode with an anguished expression.

The words they'd exchanged inside the tomb, the warmth of their touch, the love he'd confirmed—he cast it all aside. It was precious. Of course it was precious. And yet, that thing he could hardly bear to let go of would not shine, not in the true sense, inside Subaru. If only he could have been foolish enough to keep being deceived by that false glow. That he couldn't become quite that much of a fool—that was the unfortunate part of being Natsuki Subaru.

"There's no way she'd say it. Emilia, saying she loves me... leaning on me, entrusting everything to me, saying she needs nothing else as long as she has me... absolutely not."

"Wh-what the hell are you talkin' about, oi."

"There's no way she'd ever say, flat out, that depending on me like that, that her feelings for me are her whole world. —If Puck were here, there's no way she'd ever be that completely absorbed in me..."

He couldn't say how much he'd wished to be Emilia's number one. But Subaru neither thought so highly of himself, nor so little of Emilia, as to believe he could be smug about having become her number one at this stage. The one Emilia entrusted with her deepest trust, the one she'd cling to in the very last extremity—that was Puck.

It was only because Puck wasn't appearing before her right now that she'd chosen Subaru as the next thing to lean on. He didn't want to believe that confession of love, those hot fingertips, that trembling breath, were all lies. He didn't want to believe it—but it wasn't the real thing.

Lifting his face, Subaru glared at Garfiel. Facing Garfiel, whose expression had somewhat receded from the earlier wave of fury, it was Subaru's turn now to bare his teeth.

"Who drove that girl to the point of having to cling to someone as useless as me? Who made her think, over and over, her heart broken again and again, that even so she couldn't stop—who made her agonize like that?"

"That's a necessary thing, ain't it!? Somethin' she chose for herself, ain't it!? You sayin' that's my fault, or the rest of 'em in the 'Sanctuary,' ah!?"

Against Subaru's force, Garfiel threw his own force right back. At Garfiel's roared answer, Subaru slowly shook his head.

Who had driven Emilia to the edge? He knew the answer to that without even needing to ask.

"It's not a question of whose fault—it's my fault."

"—Hah!?"

"It's my fault. There's no mistaking it—Emilia being driven that far is my fault. Because of me. Because of you. Because of all of you."

"...Don't screw around. If she can't handle the pressure and just breaks, then that's all the vessel she's got, ain't it!? Holdin' up some high-and-mighty goal with that weak a resolve—she deserves to be mocked, don't she!?"

"Yeah. You're right. Emilia's too kind to take on that pressure head-on. So she carries it all alone, can't confide in anyone, and breaks under it. —The truth is, that was supposed to be my job."

Facing Garfiel's rage, Subaru could feel his own heart cooling, as if it had merged with the white landscape around them. It felt like the thing he had to do had just been spelled out in black and white.

"That's right. It was something I was supposed to do. That's what I'm here for... I was the one who said that to you, and look what I've actually been doing..."

"What the hell are ya agreein' with all on your own, oi. ...Nah, forget it. Forget it. Ain't no point goin' along with your nonsense anymore. 'Ya can't quench a Moldova thirst.' If you can't do it..."

"You'll go into the tomb and bring Emilia out yourself? Can you even do that?"

"...The hell's that supposed to mean?"

A low, threatening growl. Garfiel's words were meant to intimidate, but that only prompted Subaru to voice a baseless-sounding guess of his own.

"Garfiel, I already know you're the 'Apostle of Greed.' I know that's the only thing that carries the right to command Ryuzu-san's copies."

"..."

"What follows necessarily is that you, being the 'Apostle of Greed,' must have gone into the tomb before. ...No, it'd be more accurate to say you've undergone the 'Trial.'"

"—Y-you..."

"You took it on, didn't you? The 'Trial.' I don't know why you're so stubborn about hiding it. Maybe it's because there's a rule that residents of the 'Sanctuary' aren't allowed to enter the tomb. Or if not that, maybe it's for Ryuzu-san's sake—she went into the tomb to help you after you'd gone in."

"———!"

Garfiel's expression changed color. So the matter of his family really was a wound for him. Watching that color shift into anguish, Subaru kept forming his guesses even as he spoke them aloud, in real time.

"Frederica told me you'd gone into the tomb. I also know that Ryuzu-san went in after you."

"Th-that... blabbermouth...! Wasn't enough for her to just walk out of here, now she's gotta go suckin' up to the outsiders too...!"

"Is there someone it'd be bad for that to get out to? To begin with, who exactly is it that the residents of the 'Sanctuary' made their contract with? The Witch Echidna is the one who created the 'Sanctuary.' So does that mean the residents of the 'Sanctuary' are still keeping a contract with the dead?"

"That's—enough of that—!"

Refusing to let him say more, Garfiel kicked off the ground, becoming wind as he lunged at Subaru. Claws sharp enough to pierce steel plating aimed straight for Subaru's face by the shortest possible path—

"—It's Roswaal who's making this snow fall."

"———"

Right before Subaru's eyes, having touched the heart of the matter, Garfiel's claws stopped just short of reaching him. To Garfiel's dumbstruck expression, Subaru gave a small nod.

"It's not Emilia. Puck isn't with her either, so it's beyond her power. And even if by some chance Emilia had caused this, there's no way she could talk to me without letting on about it at all."

"Th-that's... just your own convenient... guessin'...!"

"Yeah, it's just something I believe. Even if that girl fell into despair, I believe she's not the type to throw a tantrum that would ruin everyone and everything around her. That's all—it's just what I believe."

By process of elimination, so to speak—not something the person being accused would exactly appreciate having pinned on them. But it was by no means a baseless claim.

"It's Roswaal keeping you all bound to the 'Sanctuary' too, isn't it?"

"You get that from Frederica too?"

"No way... I put together the circumstantial evidence and information, added a good dose of prejudice and bad impressions, and just went for it, figuring a false accusation wouldn't be the worst outcome. —Turns out I was right, apparently."

"..."

To the silent Garfiel, Subaru let out a white sigh. —It was that indescribable feeling of deflation when the person you'd suspected of being the mastermind turns out, quite plainly, to actually be the mastermind. It had been obvious Roswaal was scheming something, but why he'd maintain a contract confining the residents of the 'Sanctuary' to this place, and why he'd torment those very residents with snow—no matter how much he thought about it, no proper answer came. Then there was only one thing left—

"Guess there's nothing for it but to go land a punch straight on that smug mug of his."

At Subaru's resolve-laden mutter, Garfiel lowered his arm. Subaru could tell that the same fierce emotion now churned in Garfiel's expression as well.

"—My, my, seems you're both terribly angry, don't you think?"

Reclining on the bed in his assigned room, Roswaal welcomed Subaru and Garfiel's visit with those words, smiling with amusement, his usual clownish makeup unchanged.

"Yeah. Right about now, pretty damn angry. Me, maybe not so much, but you can tell this one's about ready to jump you, can't you? Watch what you say."

Standing so as to block the doorway, arms spread wide, Subaru gestured with his chin toward the one beside him as he said this. There, where Subaru indicated, was Garfiel, letting out a low growl.

The beastly sound of his breathing was proof, perhaps, that he was holding onto his human form by the barest thread of reason. Even indoors, the cold seeped through the stone walls. Both Subaru and Roswaal were breathing out white puffs of air, but Garfiel's breath alone carried so much heat it looked as if it might come out tinged red.

"An interesting pairing, isn't it? If I recall, Garfiel had been declaring rather loudly that he'd tear Subaru-kun in half the moment he got back, hadn't he?"

"The circumstances mighta shifted a little. Whether they've really shifted or not, gotta make sure before I know who I'm turnin' into mincemeat."

"Don't just have a casually terrifying conversation like that. And Roswaal, don't take that kind of outrageous remark like it's nothing."

On the way from the 'Sanctuary' to the manor, the exchange with Garfiel had left even Subaru himself with lingering self-loathing over how ugly it had been. It wasn't hard to imagine what kind of abuse Garfiel—who surely hadn't forgotten that humiliation—must have hurled at Roswaal and Emilia.

To Subaru, brow furrowed, Roswaal shook his head, saying "now now-ow," and closing one eye, gazed at Subaru and Garfiel with his single yellow eye.

"If that's how it goes, then that's how it goes, Subaru-kun."

"Wow, you really do hate me, huh. I'm hurt, Roz-cchi. So you wouldn't feel a thing if Garfiel tore me to shreds?"

"Oh-oh dear, isn't that rather faint-hearted of you? It's not as if it's a foregone conclusion that Garfiel would win, should he and Subaru-kun come to blows, is it?"

"You think I could win? Hear my track record and even you'd be impressed—in a bad way."

After all, ever since being summoned to this other world, Subaru had picked up plenty of fresh wounds but next to no experience actually winning a "fight" on his own. Beating down that idiot in the back alley, barely bringing down the Juggernaut, and finishing off Petelgeuse when he was already half-dead—that was about the extent of it.

"Startin' to sound like a better record than I expected, but if I went at it with Garfiel at full health, I'd be a pile of meat in two seconds flat. That much self-analysis I can do."

"I wonder. With the right conditions in place, I suspect it could turn out to be a surprisingly good match, actually."

Narrowing his eyes, Roswaal looked Subaru up and down as he said this. Weighing those words, there was, unfortunately, nothing in them Subaru could nod along to. Subaru dismissed Roswaal's remark with a shrug, and at almost the same moment, Garfiel's heel cracked the floor beside him.

"Who the hell cares about that shit right now!? There's somethin' way more important we gotta be talkin' about, ah!? You two sleepy or somethin'?"

Carving a small crater into the center of the room with his heel, Garfiel bared his teeth and snarled at both Subaru and Roswaal. The light feinting before getting to the point didn't sit well with someone as straightforward and hot-blooded as him. Then again, for Subaru too, this kind of subtle political maneuvering was neither familiar nor a good fit. Following Garfiel's lead, Subaru gave a single nod, then—

"The snow outside—you're the one making it fall, Roswaal."

He cut straight to the heart of the matter.

"..."

At Subaru's question, Roswaal kept his mouth shut. Subaru, too, fell silent, waiting for Roswaal's answer. Silence dropped over the room, and the only sounds were the wind freezing against the window from outside the house, and the fastidious, clock-like rhythm of Garfiel clacking his fangs together.

"Subaru-kun."

"Yeah."

"—Did you hear that from me?"

"..."

It was a question whose meaning made no sense. Subaru had run several simulations in his head of what kind of answer Roswaal might give back. There was the pattern where he'd laugh fearlessly and say "well spotted"; there was the pattern where he'd show open agitation—"Wh-what nonsense are you talking... Evidence, do you have evidence!?" The most likely, he'd figured, was the pattern where he'd deflect with "I'm not quite sure what you mean, hmm-mm."

But the answer Roswaal actually gave back was none of those he'd imagined.

"Me or whoever else—I'm talking to you right now, so how the hell would that conversation even happen? Did you mix something up, or what?"

"H-hmm... I see. I see. I s-e-e. ...How disappointing."

Unable to make sense of what the words meant, Subaru showed his incomprehension to Roswaal. At that, Roswaal lowered his eyes and let out that remark on a weak breath. His normally pale profile looked, somehow, even more drained of strength than usual. To Subaru, it looked like something unrelated to his body not being in perfect condition—something coming from the state of Roswaal's heart.

"—Y-e-es, that's right. A slip of the tongue, a mis-speaking. What a strange thing to say."

When he lifted his face, Roswaal immediately retracted what he'd just said and gave a thin smile. The smile that formed on those rouge-painted lips looked different, to Subaru, from his usual one. But paying no mind at all to that minor shift in Roswaal, it was Garfiel who took a step forward.

"Ain't gonna deny it, huh, oi."

"When one is already under suspicion, wouldn't stringing together words in an unseemly attempt at denial only make one look all the more like a liar? Especially since I'm fairly confident that, given my usual words and conduct, I haven't exactly earned your trust to begin with, hmm-mm."

"Ya know exactly what you're doin', don't ya. Then I figure you've already got a good idea what I'm about to do next... yeah!"

Letting out a sharp breath, Garfiel closed the distance of several steps to zero in an instant. Striding up to the bed, Garfiel reached out an arm to seize Roswaal by the throat. It happened too fast for Subaru's reaction to even call out a warning in time. But—

"—You—"

"I won't allow any discourtesy toward Roswaal-sama, Garf."

Ram, leaping out from the next room, caught Garfiel's outstretched arm with her whole body. His extended right arm seized in front of her chest, Garfiel glared at Ram before him, his throat trembling with a growl. Subaru, who hadn't noticed Ram was even in the house, was startled by her appearance, but let out a breath of relief—at least thanks to her, immediate bloodshed had been averted. And so—

"Ram. You really are a fine attendant."

"Yes, Roswaal-sama—"

When their exchange brushed past his ears, Subaru didn't think anything odd of it at all. It was just a remark of appreciation from Roswaal toward Ram, who had put her body on the line to protect her master. There was nothing strange in that. Ram had, indeed, done her job. So what could possibly be the problem? Lifting his face, Subaru furrowed his brow and thought about it.

Subaru stood before the entrance of the bedroom. In front of him was Garfiel's back, and small Ram stood on the far side of Garfiel's compact frame. Behind the two of them was the bed, where Roswaal had been lying down, ostensibly recuperating—that was the layout of the room. —When had Roswaal gotten up, exactly?

"..."

It felt like it had happened in a single instant. In the span of a single blink, Roswaal had risen from the bed and walked over to where Ram and Garfiel were locked in their spark of confrontation, right nearby. And then—

"..."

What was that? It looked as if something like a human arm was jutting out from Garfiel's back. Coming out from the center of his chest, punching through the middle of his back, that thing, equipped with five writhing fingers, looked to Subaru like a human right arm.

"Guh... gh...!"

Right before his eyes, Garfiel's body shook violently. Slowly, the back of the jacket he wore began staining crimson, and his knees started to buckle. Unable to support himself, as Garfiel dropped to his knees, the arm vanished from his back. Instantly, a great gush of blood spilled from the hole now left unplugged.

"—Eh?"

Garfiel, collapsing. Ram and Roswaal, looking down at him. And from the chest of Ram, looking down at him too—

"Roz..."

"You really were a fine attendant."

Cutting off Ram, who tried to call his name in a weak voice, Roswaal said this gently. His left hand softly stroked Ram's pink hair, and Ram, her cheeks flushing red, accepted it with an entranced expression. —From the corner of that smiling mouth, fresh blood spilled out, a beat late.

Of course it did. Because her chest had been pierced clean through, from behind.

The arm was pulled free. Ram's small body, unable to bear even that light impact, toppled forward. The one to catch her was Garfiel, who was himself bleeding profusely from his own body. He caught up the girl who'd collapsed into his arms, holding her upright.

"Ga... Roz...! R-Ram...! Ram, Ram, Ram, RamRamRamRamRam!"

For a moment, his heart—on the verge of being consumed by hatred—was wiped clean by the sight of the girl he loved before him. Garfiel screamed the name of the girl in his arms over and over, and amid a howl mixed with blood, released pale phosphorescent light from both arms. Subaru knew that vivid glow to be the effect of healing magic. And that Garfiel, having prefaced it by saying he wasn't especially good at it, was nonetheless a being capable of wielding it.

Even now, having just taken a fatal wound through his own chest, Garfiel was pouring everything he had into treating Ram in his arms. His body, undulating in time with his heartbeat, was transforming. Body hair covered his exposed skin, his fangs began to lengthen, his pupils narrowed sharply all at once. His muscle mass swelled overwhelmingly, and as his frame grew a size larger, his clothes, unable to bear it, began to tear.

It was Beastification into the great tiger, the loss of reason. The beast's instinct to protect its own wounded body and the human reason wishing to save the life of his beloved before him were clashing violently, throwing off sparks. But—

"..."

"Beastification is troublesome, you see."

Tilting his head lightly, Roswaal said this and let his leg flash out toward Garfiel in a single arc. His long leg, swung sideways, became wind and struck Garfiel's temple directly—there came a light sound, like an egg cracking, and, absurdly, Garfiel's head burst open in a spray of red.

Garfiel's body, having lost everything above the neck. Blood gushed like a fountain from the severed cross-section of his neck, filling the room with the stench of blood as the corpse collapsed on top of Ram. Pinned beneath it, Ram too lay utterly still, her expression still faintly smiling. Garfiel's healing magic never took effect either. By the time it left Roswaal's hand, Ram's heart, already destroyed, had already stopped beating. Garfiel had simply been pouring out everything he had without ever realizing it.

"Even I find it difficult to use other magic while I'm using magic on the scale of interfering with the weather, you see. —As a court mage, this is rather an unbecoming state to be caught in."

Wiping his blood-stained leg roughly on a nearby sheet, Roswaal—having killed both Ram and Garfiel with his bare... foot—turned to look at Subaru, who stood frozen, unable to move a muscle. And then Roswaal, with an attitude and tone that seemed no different at all from usual, said:

"Now then—shall we talk? Natsuki Subaru-kun."
