Arc 7 Chapter 67: "Behind the Back Molar"

At first, when he heard Subaru's request, old man Null contorted his gap-toothed mouth into a spectacular grimace.

From what Subaru had heard, old man Null was the island's healer — playing at being a doctor, more or less — but before coming here, he'd never once tended to anyone. The island's administrator, back before Gustav took over as warden, was already here, and in a harsher era when people died more easily, he'd happened to fall into the role of healer.

It started with nothing more than pressing a dirty rag against the wound of a sword slave who'd lost an arm. That became the thing people called him out for whenever there was an injury, and eventually he was told he no longer had to stand in the arena as a sword slave — instead, he'd work the treatment room as its healer.

Since then, he'd spent a long stretch of time as a healer, weathering more than a few crises befitting the position. As a result, he'd become capable of stitching wounds and mixing simple medicines, and he'd flash his yellow teeth in a grin and say he'd be set for life as a healer, right up until he died. Seeing that face of his twisted into such a grimace, Subaru genuinely felt bad about it.

But if he wanted to accomplish what he needed — what he had to pull off — on this island, it was an absolutely indispensable thing. So Subaru made his request to old man Null, a man doing honorable work saving lives.

"—Could you put together a poison that kills a person instantly, just from biting into it or licking it?"

He'd done old man Null wrong. But it was necessary. Passing out mid-battle and surviving in some half-dead state was, for Subaru, a far bigger problem — there ought to be a means of resetting the board. And once he had that means secured, Subaru was flawless.

"Well, a bit more leg length would help," he muttered — a gripe born from moments in the fight where he came up one big stride short.

Of course, that missing stride would get forcibly resolved next time by launching two strides faster. Trial and error, over and over, hunting for the best move, grasping the future he wanted with his own two hands. And so—

"—That's it! Well done surviving the 'Sparka'!! By the authority vested in me, I hereby welcome you as members of the Sword Slave Isle!!"

The low command rang out, praising the end of the fierce battle that had unfolded in the arena. At the center of the arena, sprawled on its back, lay the corpse of the gladiator beast, two swords — one large, one small — driven into its torso. Its wing-arms and long tail had been cut away, leaving it a pitiful sight.

But that wing-borne mobility, and that vicious tail strike, had truly, truly been a menace. Taking those two things down had been absolutely necessary to land damage on the main body, so Subaru wanted it on record that it hadn't been beaten down needlessly.

And besides that—

"Gustav-san, you're kidding me."

Breathing hard, shoulders heaving, Subaru looked up at Gustav standing in the seat of honor. Arms crossed, all four of his sturdy limbs, the island's ruler had watched the "Sparka" through to its conclusion. Subaru had pegged him as an inflexible stickler, but he never expected the victory declaration to come out word-for-word identical.

It seemed unlikely this time and Subaru's own time were coincidentally the same by chance, so presumably Gustav's call was simply the same every time. Anyway, setting that aside—

"We d-did it... we did it, we did it! We survived! We actually survived!!"

"We're alive...! Ah, waaaahhh!"

"Guhic, hnn...!"

"...I can't believe it. I thought it was the end of the world..."

"Could it be we're dead, and this is somewhere beyond Od Lagna...?"

Around the gladiator beast's corpse stood five figures, each celebrating their victory, dazed, in their own way. Orson, Hitz, Nadorei, Kuonson, and Codoley. All Lizard Humans, but no two of the same subspecies — grasping each one's individual traits, and coordinating around them, had been the equation for victory.

Same as it had been with Weitz and the others — if even one of them had been missing, victory would have been out of reach. Though, of course, without Subaru there, it would've been utterly impossible regardless.

"But we won...!"

Snap, pointing decisively, Subaru jabbed a finger toward Gustav in the seat of honor. At that provocative posture from Subaru, Gustav didn't so much as move his stern face — only narrowed his eyes slightly in response. Without a word, he turned his back, stepped down from the seat of honor, and returned into the island's interior. Cleanup of the corpse and handling the surviving sword slaves he left to his subordinates, the guards.

"Schubarz!"

"Oh?"

Someone came running up and called out to Subaru as he watched Gustav walk off. It was Hiain, who'd leapt down from the spectator seats and come sprinting over. Every scale on his body looked bristled, and he grabbed Subaru's shoulder with his broad, webbed fingers.

"What the hell were you thinking!? No, in the first place, why the 'Sparka'...!"

"Punishment. It's a punishment."

"Punishment, huh?"

"Yeah. I asked the other sword slaves about it. If you defy Gustav-san, the guards — the island's management — first you get a warning, then punishment. Bad enough offenders become curse-fodder."

He'd asked around among several sword slaves and properly corroborated this. Everyone seemed to know that Gustav wanted to avoid needlessly shrinking the sword slave population, and their understanding of the rules laid down here was consistent. Punishment — meaning, forced participation in a "Sparka" conducted by an unrelated "Squad."

"The 'Sparka' is basically a rite of passage for people newly becoming sword slaves. Once you've already become a sword slave, you don't take part again. You've got your own 'Squad,' and no reason to bother."

On top of that, the "Squad" assembled for a "Sparka" was decided at random from among the members who'd entered the Sword Slave Isle at that time. In other words, there was no picking based on compatibility, no choosing who you liked. You got paired with people whose abilities, specialties, even backgrounds you barely knew, and unless you coordinated with them to bring down a gladiator beast, you couldn't clear it — a brutal ordeal.

There was no upside to doing it, and the odds of dying were dangerously high. Every sword slave agreed unanimously: the "Sparka" was the harshest death-match on the entire Sword Slave Isle.

"Well, right now it seems like everyone thinks the worst death-match is the one where you get stuck fighting alongside Cecilus, thanks to him."

"—. I get why you mixed into the 'Sparka.' But."

"But?"

"In the first place! Why the hell did you get punished!? Right after that talk up there, getting thrown straight into a 'Sparka' — that's not normal! It's like..."

There, Hiain cut off his raised voice and his eyes darted away. Sensing the hesitation in the air, Subaru shrugged his small shoulders slightly. He had a rough idea what Hiain wanted to say.

"Like I was trying to save Orson and the others?"

"—!"

"You're not wrong. If I hadn't been there, they'd all have died."

In truth, even with Subaru there, the battle had been a mess. To unite with people even worse suited to fighting than Hiain, he'd had to start by becoming part of their tight-knit Lizard-Human group. That's where the memory of having seen Chaosflame turned out to actually be useful.

"You were all aiming for Chaosflame together, huh?"

"Wha—"

"You should really have that conversation properly. You finally got to see each other again, after all."

As he spoke, Subaru laid his hand over Hiain's hand still resting on his shoulder. Hiain's slit-pupiled eyes went wide, staring intently into Subaru's black ones. Meeting that stare, Subaru tilted his chin toward the five of them.

The five, still basking in the joy of survival, were gradually regaining enough composure to look around, and one of them, Codoley, noticed Hiain.

And—

"Hiain! It's Hiain! He's alive... he's alive!"

Codoley's voice rang out, his face brightening with delight, and the other four turned to look at Hiain too. They all spoke up at once, delighted at Hiain's presence, reacting with nothing but warmth.

Seeing that, watching Hiain's face crumple—

"I made this chance happen at the risk of my life, you know. Savor it properly, brother."

Slapping the hunched, stooped back, Subaru sent Hiain off toward his companions. Hiain hesitated several times but advanced with halting steps—

"S-Schubarz!"

"Hm?"

"...Thanks."

With just that, Hiain walked off toward the five waiting for him. Orson and the others held no hostility, no ill will toward Hiain. After all, Subaru already knew why the five of them had been captured and brought to the Sword Slave Isle in the first place. And that if they properly talked it out, there was no reason for them to be at odds.

"...Schubarz-sama?"

"Whoa!"

Watching Hiain's reunion with his companions, Subaru was startled by a voice from behind. He jumped and, out of sheer surprise, choked.

"Ah, that was close... I nearly died...! Don't scare me like that!"

"That's my line! What's the sudden idea, doing something so...do you want to die!?"

"I don't want to die!?"

Turning around with a hand over his mouth, Subaru found Tanza scolding him with tremendous fury. Under the young girl's furious protest, Subaru was cowed but shook his head anyway. But his excuse did nothing at all to satisfy Tanza's expression.

"Your sudden participation in the 'Sparka' shocked me greatly. And the fact that it was the result of you picking a fight with Governor Gustav yourself makes it seem like you don't value your own life at all."

"That's a total misunderstanding, that I don't value my life. I don't think there are many people who know the worth of life better than me. That's exactly why I saved Hiain's friends too, isn't it?"

"I'm talking about your own life. ...It's true it worked out in the end, so we saved them, but even from where I stood there were several dangerous moments. This is nothing but a miracle."

"A miracle, huh."

Her voice dropping, Tanza scolded Subaru's recklessness from a grounded, realistic point of view. She wasn't wrong — there really had been several moments that were nerve-wracking to watch. He understood why Tanza wanted to call it a miracle. But that's not what it was.

"A miracle is something you're given by the gods. This is something I seized myself."

So this wasn't a miracle at all. It was proof that Natsuki Subaru had kicked goddamn fate square in the shins.

"...Was this recklessness purely to save Hiain-sama's companions? Or is it retaliation for that verbal spat with Segmunt-sama?"

"Nah, I really was pissed at Ceci, but if I was risking my life over that I'd be way too far gone... There was a real purpose to saving Hiain's friends too, you know."

"——"

"That's a seriously doubtful look!"

The persuasiveness of Subaru's explanation was collapsing fast. Under Tanza's utterly unconvinced gaze — the kind you'd give a criminal caught red-handed — Subaru's shoulders slumped, and he muttered weakly, "It's true, I swear..."

At his pathetic state, Tanza let out a small sigh.

"Then what was your purpose, Schubarz-sama?"

"That's..."

Recovering herself to ask the question, Tanza watched as Subaru turned to look back once more. There, Hiain was talking with his five companions. Hiain's voice, faintly cracking, reached them even from here.

So—

"Let's wait a little longer before I tell you."

"...Schubarz-sama?"

"Seriously doubtful look!"

Exasperated at his own lack of credibility, Subaru surreptitiously slipped a finger into his mouth. He double-checked the position of the little wrapped packet lodged against his back molar.

"——"

What sat there was the last resort Subaru had asked old man Null to compound. Having clawed his way to this loop through a desperate gamble, he could not afford to throw it away over some careless slip-up. He had to be extremely careful not to die from a stupid mistake.

—Thinking that, Subaru pressed the wrapped "medicine" firmly behind his back molar.

△▼△▼△▼△

"From what Orson and the others said, Chaosflame's been wiped out."

"——"

At Hiain's report, his face bitter, Tanza's round eyebrows drooped and she looked down. Even having half-expected the worst, being told it was true didn't soften the blow. Like knowing you're about to trip — it still hurts when you fall.

With the "Sparka" over and Orson and the others safely surviving to be welcomed as sword slaves, they were now being led around by the guards, shown the island's facilities and the shared quarters. Companions or not, Hiain — belonging to a different "Squad" from them — couldn't join in on that, and so he'd shown up here, quietly, at the room shared by Subaru's "Squad."

And with that, he'd brought the news Subaru had wanted to hear.

"This is the kind of outside news you wanted, right? Stuck on this island, no outside information reaches us."

"Half and half. No, let's call it seventy-thirty. Seventy percent for Hiain and Orson's sake."

"Keep telling yourself that, you creepy brat!"

It was mostly genuine, honestly, but Hiain, arms crossed, turned his face away with that attitude anyway. That Hiain had gotten his feelings sorted out enough to think about why Subaru had mixed into the "Sparka" made Subaru genuinely glad. Somehow, Hiain's expression looked brighter too — probably because he'd managed to hear the crucial story from Orson and the others.

And that story was—

"...Those idiots got themselves caught trying to buy me back from the slave trader. Hopeless, through and through."

"Gods and fate are both cold-hearted. That's why I stepped in. ...Should we just say it was 'us' who saved them?"

"Ha, how do you figure that? You're the only one who actually took part in the 'Sparka.'"

"We could say Hiain got down on his knees, sobbing snot everywhere, begging 'please, Schubarz-sama, please help them'..."

"Talk in your sleep when you're actually asleep!"

A hand shot out, grabbed Subaru's head, and shook it back and forth. Letting go of the dizzied Subaru, Hiain sniffed and noticed Tanza's state. Hearing the news about Chaosflame, she was looking down, and Hiain called out to her, "Squirt—"

"You down about Chaosflame? What, is it—"

"...Chaosflame is my hometown. Even right up to the moment it was on the verge of collapse, I was in that city."

"Oh, r-really... that's, uh, that's—"

Thrown off by the unexpected answer, Hiain's eyes darted about. It showed how poorly he handled sudden trouble, but he quickly raised his voice, "But listen!"

"It's true the city itself apparently got wrecked, but that doesn't mean the people living there were wiped out entirely. The whole lot of them moved together to somewhere else... ah, though it comes with a catch."

"A catch, you say...?"

"Well, I don't know how much of it to trust, honestly."

With that preface, Hiain hesitated over what came next. But by now, both Tanza's and Subaru's interest were already fixed on hearing the rest. Sensing that, Hiain let out a big sigh, then—

"Apparently there's some kind of big rebellion going on out there. The word is, the people from Chaosflame joined that rebellion. Well, the top of the Mad City was always staging some coup or another, so I figure the city getting blown to bits is probably the result of those idiots pulling something stupid again... eep."

"—Yorna-sama is not foolish."

"S-sorry! I don't know what I said wrong, but sorry!"

Having explained it in a rapid patter, Hiain flinched at Tanza's sharp glare and his voice cracked. Once again picking a needless fight and drawing the other party's anger, that was Hiain for you — but behind him, Subaru found several points of relief in what he'd just heard.

Of course, that was on the premise of believing everything in Orson and the others' account, relayed through Hiain.

"Even if Chaosflame's a lost cause, Yorna-san and the people of the city are fine. If the rebellion's making news, sounds like Abel and the others are safe too."

The fight to retake the imperial capital couldn't proceed without Abel, after all. Which meant Abel was alive. He wanted to hope that Medium, Taritta, and Al were safe too. —And that, at Yorna's side, Rui was safe as well.

"...I really can't afford to waste time after all."

Even fragments of news from outside only sharpened his craving to get out there. Of course he worried about everyone in Chaosflame, but he was also worried about the people of Guaral — where Yorna and Abel's group would presumably try to rendezvous — Rem, specifically.

Though if she found out he'd shrunk down like this, he suspected he'd get scolded all over again.

"—. Honestly, I wanted to show Rem that she could rely on me."

Personally, he felt his head was clearer and running better like this now, but Subaru couldn't argue that it wasn't persuasive from the outside looking in. The fact that Tanza kept eyeing him with suspicion probably wasn't unrelated to his small body, either.

In any case—

"Right, got it. Thanks, Hiain, that helped a lot."

"What helped was us... er, whatever, just glad you get it."

Hiain answered Subaru's thanks with a mumble. At that answer, Subaru gave a small laugh, and was just about to try smoothing things over with Tanza when—

"—Schubarz, the Governor's summoning you."

Outside the shared room, the one calling out was a guard of the Sword Slave Isle in a black uniform. At that call, Hiain's expression went stiff, and Tanza's eyes went round. She looked toward the guard.

"Governor Gustav wants Schubarz-sama? Why—"

"Not told. No need to know. Obey quietly. Or else..."

"It's fine! I'll come along quiet as a lamb, no need for the tough talk. ...By the way, if I don't obey, is that punishment?"

"——"

"Kidding, kidding."

At Subaru's glib remark, one eye closed, the middle-aged guard's lips twisted. His attitude made it obvious he wasn't fond of Subaru — understandably so. This guard had been present when Subaru was talking with Gustav before the "Sparka." He was the one who'd witnessed the very moment that triggered Subaru's participation in the "Sparka" — the moment that made him a target for punishment. To him, Subaru must have seemed utterly incomprehensible — a child crazy enough to volunteer for a "Sparka" of his own accord.

"Well, I'll be back in a bit. Tanza, be a good girl and wait for me."

"...It is you, Schubarz-sama, who ought to behave yourself, if anything."

"Ugh, can't argue with that."

At Tanza's stifled tone, Subaru's wry smile deepened further. Following the guard, who silently urged him to hurry, Subaru headed out of the room. Then, at his back, Hiain called out, "Schubarz."

"Um, that is..."

"Hm? Something up?"

"Ah, no... j-just, watch yourself out there. Even someone like you — if our 'Squad's' numbers drop, that's a problem for us too."

At that awkward, roundabout show of concern, Subaru's face went blank for a moment. Then he burst out with a "pfft" and nodded back at Hiain.

"Yeah, got it. See you in a bit, then."

Waving lightly, Subaru parted ways with Tanza and Hiain for the time being. Then, following behind the guard walking ahead of him—

"Gustav-san, was he mad?"

"...The Governor is not the sort to get emotional. Unlike myself."

Left with the unspoken implication not to go making him angry with some needless remark, Subaru shrank his shoulders meekly.

△▼△▼△▼△

—And so, the two who'd seen Subaru off, taken away by the guard.

"Oi, what the hell did Schubarz go and do..."

"He was just now taken off by a guard — something beyond the 'Sparka,' perhaps?"

Into the room where Tanza and Hiain remained, Weitz and Idra now poked their heads in, saying as much. The two of them had also watched Subaru's "Sparka" from the spectator seats. Naturally, they had a mountain of questions for the boy who'd pulled off such recklessness.

That said, not being as desperately on edge as Hiain, they hadn't been driven to anything so rash as leaping down from the stands—

"Tanza, do you know the circumstances?"

"...Whether I know or don't know, it's complicated. It seems Schubarz-sama is the sort to keep secrets even from me."

"An unfathomable one... though he's got no small amount of grit to him, I'll grant that..."

At Tanza's downcast words, Weitz and Idra exchanged glances and sighed at that. Listening to the three of them talk, Hiain stayed silent, tight-lipped. Hard as it was to read a Lizard Human's expression, his darting eyes were clearly turning something over.

In the end, addressed by all three as "Hiain," "Hiain-sama,"—

"What's with that face..."

"No, um... y-you two have also noticed, haven't you, that Schubarz mixing into the 'Sparka' was deliberate...right?"

"That... anyone would think so, watching him back there."

Idra answered Hiain's halting words, and Tanza and Weitz nodded in agreement. It was more or less the shared understanding of every sword slave who'd watched that battle in the arena. And in everyone's eyes, without Subaru's performance, today's "Sparka" would never have been cleared.

"I've no idea what he meant by doing that..."

"—. I don't know either. But, but listen."

"—? But what?"

"I don't know for sure why he did that, but why he was able to do that — well, maybe... I might..."

The other three could tell what would follow: I might understand. Not why Subaru had taken part in the "Sparka," but why Subaru had been able to take part in the "Sparka" and survive. For Tanza, and no less for Weitz and Idra, that was a question that hit close to home.

Sensing their sharp, intent gazes on him, Hiain swallowed hard.

And then—

"This is, well, something I heard from some folks who just came in from outside... apparently out there, there's word going around that the Emperor's illegitimate child is somewhere out there. And, well, that's the thing—"

"——"

"—the story is, it's a kid with black hair and black eyes."
