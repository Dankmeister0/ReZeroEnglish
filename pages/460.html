Arc 6 Chapter 55: "You, Waiting for the Snow to Melt"

―There is a darkness—black, dim, distant, deep, long, heavy, bitter.

"――――"

There was a sensation of something oppressive and suffocating clinging to every inch of the body, as if all the bad things in this world had been mixed together into one.
Face, body, limbs—every stretch of skin was tainted by that darkness, oozing, weeping blood, an unpleasant sensation like an unending thirst clinging on and on.

Can it be imagined? A feeling as if the entire body, without exception, were covered in scabs.
The skin pulled taut, and touching it returned a sensation that didn't feel human at all—and even the fingers reaching out to touch the scabs were themselves covered in scabs, so that the true shape of "oneself" could not be known.

―No. What truly could not be known was not only the outward shape.

"――――"

Something deeper—perhaps it should be called the essence instead.
Or perhaps it should be spoken of as the "Soul."

The shape, the state of one's own "Soul" had been lost, and after repeating an endless, hopeless meandering, at last, it felt as if a finger had caught hold of one fragment of it.
And since the response returned to that catching finger was the aforementioned scab, if it could not quite be called futile, then the unease and revulsion were all the greater for it. Was it truly right to keep drawing this thread in?

"――――"

Beyond this, was the "self" one truly sought actually there?
When the end of this thread was reached, might an entirely different self begin instead? It was a strange thing to imagine, but not entirely impossible. In fact, what had happened to oneself carried a suddenness and unreality close to that.
To accept it as one's own affair, to accept it as a trial that had visited oneself, and to seek the sight that lay beyond overcoming it—how much time had been spent on just that much?

"――――"

And so, there was a strong unease.
Was it truly right to go on ahead? Was the place one had accepted, embraced, and sought really there?

The "self" one had entrusted things to, believed in, forgiven, wished for, tried to become—was it there?

"―I love you."

―That unease, beyond all comparison, melted and faded, dissolved by a voice that seemed to guide.

"――――"

―Toward a light—white, bright, high, noble, beautiful, sweet.

That Soul, Natsuki Subaru, was――.

※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※

In an instant, consciousness was drawn up from the edge of a loose, deep sleep, and Natsuki Subaru awoke.

"――ah"

A weak, faint first breath escaped his lips.
It was terribly hoarse, lacking in vitality, but unmistakably his own voice. That fact told him he hadn't been reincarnated as some single-celled organism incapable of speech.
That was one step forward. All that remained was to quickly confirm he wasn't some being with an entirely new set of values――,

"―Subaru, are you awake?"

"――――"

The voice that shook Subaru's eardrum from close by was one of silver bells.
Cool and gentle, calm and strong-willed, radiant, and beloved—that voice.

The voice he'd surely been hearing until just a moment ago, in such a terrible state.
Hearing it now made his heart leap. His chest ached, and Subaru, enduring the pounding, slowly turned his gaze to the side.

"――――"

―There, waiting, was a violet-indigo brilliance, eyes filled with gentle concern.

"...emi, ria?"

"Yes, that's right. Subaru, are you okay? Can you get up? Can you talk properly?"

"Um..."

At being called by name in halting fashion, the owner of those violet-indigo eyes—Emilia—loosened her lips and tilted her head. Her long, beautiful silver hair flowed down over her smooth white shoulders. It looked almost like moonlight elegantly swimming through light, and its fierce beauty seared Subaru's heart.

―To put it plainly, an unearthly beautiful girl was there.

"Uh, ah..."

The instant he became aware of it, blood rushed into Subaru's cheeks at a tremendous speed.
His face grew hot, his face turned red, his eyes wandered and his voice failed him. His ears flushed to the point of aching, and a sound of "awawa, awawa..." leaked out.

"Awawa...?"

At the sight of him so violently flustered, Emilia's shapely eyebrows knit together. Even that small, modest gesture carried a different sort of impression, like two masterpieces painted by some once-in-a-generation artist.
Witnessing it at such close range, close enough to feel her breath, made the pounding in his chest—already inseparable from pain—climb even higher and stronger, tormenting Subaru.

"――――"

What was this. What on earth was this.
Was this real? Was it some phantom with mass, or something? In the desert, what you see is a mirage of an oasis—that is, the convention that you see what you most desire at that moment.
Following that rule, wasn't this a mirage? What an extravagant illusion――,

"A-are you okay, Subaru? Something really must be wrong with you. You collapsed, after all."

"Hyaw!"

"See, just now, that 'hyaw'!"

Subaru, in the depths of confusion, still had his shoulder tremble at the smooth palm placed against his forehead.
Seeing that, Emilia grew convinced of Subaru's poor condition and blinked, while Subaru himself, having his "Emilia Mirage Theory" refuted, felt like a scholar whose geocentric model had just been overturned.

But he had certainly felt the touch. Her existence was affirmed by reality.
And so long as the real, existing Emilia was calling out to him, that he himself was Natsuki Subaru was likewise affirmed.
And above all――,

"―You've been ignoring Betty this whole time while talking, haven't you? Honestly, Emilia wasn't the only one who was worried."

"――!"

From the other side of Emilia came a young voice complaining discontentedly, and he turned. What leapt into his field of view as he turned was a young girl puffing her cheeks adorably――,

"Beatrice..."

"What's with that pathetic, sulky voice, I suppose... You look like you can't even believe adorable Betty is really here."

At the weak greeting, Beatrice's brows drooped as if troubled. Her words sounded scolding, but the tone carried a heavy trace of worry and relief.
Relief that Subaru had woken, and worry that he'd lost consciousness in the first place. Beatrice's attitude, suggesting both of those—no, her very presence—moved Subaru's heart.
Which meant――,

"―Nya!?"

Subaru grabbed the primly composed Beatrice and pulled her light body straight to his chest. Light. Truly a light body.
Caught utterly off guard by the sudden action, Beatrice could offer no resistance, her eyes blinking rapidly as she was pulled snugly into Subaru's arms. Atop the green bed woven of vines, Subaru confirmed her existence with all his strength.

"Beatrice, Beatrice, Beatrice!"

"W-w-w-what is it, I suppose!? What's wrong with you!? Isn't this too sudden, I suppose!"

"You, you... you really do have a face that puts me at ease! It's like coming home to see it. I could just melt."

"I would like to believe that's not meant as a compliment, I suppose!?"

Holding Beatrice close and gazing at her face, Subaru spoke with heartfelt sincerity. Her face reddening at his actions and words, Beatrice pressed both her small palms hard against Subaru's cheeks.
The girl's small fingers caught at his cheeks and ears, and amid that adorable pain, Subaru felt, right here in this place, the certain reality of the girl named Beatrice.

"Honestly! Subaru, don't go clowning around the moment you wake up! We still don't even know why you collapsed..."

At the sight of Subaru hugging Beatrice and carrying on so gleefully, Emilia, feeling a slight sense of being left out, interjected.
Concerned for Subaru, Emilia reached to gently take hold of his shoulder—and stopped.

"―Subaru?"

Emilia's voice, which had carried more worry than anger, had its mingled emotion shift entirely into worry. Her eyes, widened in surprise, stared at Subaru's shoulder, which she had been about to touch.
At the faintly trembling, sobbing shoulder of Subaru.

"...uh, ngh"

Noticing Subaru choking back sobs in his throat, Beatrice, her expression clearing of confusion, gently stroked his tear-wet cheek.
His hand, trembling in small tremors, would not let go of Beatrice's young, small body. Beatrice understood that this stemmed from unease and fear.

And so Beatrice spoke gently, as if calling out to Subaru's heart.
You don't need to cry. I am here. Subaru, it's all right.

"Don't cry, Subaru. There's no need to rush. Take slow, deep breaths, and calm down. Beatrice and I are both properly here with you."

Just as Beatrice did, Emilia too comforted Subaru upon the bed.
With the hand she had hesitated to use before, she now touched Subaru's shoulder without hesitation, and Emilia's silver-bell voice honored Natsuki Subaru's actions, his decision.

"――――"

The two of them, their existence, their way of being—unchanged.
In a world where everything had crumbled, been lost, become irrecoverable, the two of them, who had still, even in the jaws of death, prioritized others over themselves, prioritized Subaru, and remained noble—unchanged.

To confirm that, to seek that, and this time, to see it through to the end.
For Natsuki Subaru to reclaim everything, as "Natsuki Subaru"――,

"I'm back..."

With a voice mixed with sobs, in an unbecoming manner—as pathetic and unseemly a situation as could be.

―Natsuki Subaru began a new loop to reclaim everything.

※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※

"So that's how it is—it seems I actually dropped my memories somewhere in the 'Taygeta' archive. I might cause everyone trouble with conversations not lining up and so on, but I'd appreciate your understanding on that front."

"――――"

At the breakfast table, seated in a circle right in front of his companions, Subaru said this and bowed his head politely.
Everyone's reactions to Subaru's bombshell—or rather, carpet-bombing—of a statement varied. Still, the most prominent reaction was confusion, with bewilderment and grief seeming to come later.

"Everyone's really, really worried about you, Subaru. But if we don't keep ourselves together, you're the one who'll be most anxious, Subaru..."

"...Lady Emilia, you say that, but―"

Beside Subaru, who had bowed his head, Emilia offered support with a troubled expression.
But it was Ram who cast a skeptical eye at Emilia's words. Hugging her own arm, she narrowed her pale pink eyes and glared at Subaru.

"To Ram, it doesn't look like the crucial Barusu here is the one most anxious about this. Or rather, what is this farce, Barusu?"

"It's not a farce. I'm being real straight about my anxiety and the facts. I can practically picture the tragedy that'd come from me stubbornly clamming up and not saying anything—it makes my chest want to burst."

"――――"

At Subaru's sour-faced reply, Ram's gaze grew ever more doubtful.
Emilia, who had heard the circumstances beforehand in the green room, reacted flusteredly to the tense exchange between the two of them. Having been told about the "amnesia," Emilia and Beatrice, while shaken, accepted it out of concern for Subaru—that pattern held true as before, and this time too followed the same shape, with Subaru having asked her beforehand to back him up at this confession.

―Having retired midway through that harrowing loop, Natsuki Subaru had plunged into a new one.

To put it in a cool way, he'd resolved to start his otherworldly life again from zero—but of course, the reality was nothing so fine as that.
Resolved as he was, there had been the possibility that everything would have simply ended in the moment he faced "death."

That this hadn't happened, and that he'd instead been given the chance to restart via "Return by Death," honestly filled him with relief and gratitude he couldn't suppress. ―But he had no intention whatsoever of relying on that entirely.

The power of "Return by Death" dwelling within Subaru was an immense force that twisted fate itself.
That its trigger was "death" was hard on Subaru, its user, but if that was considered one of the costs of twisting fate, then perhaps that much was only to be expected.

One cost among several—yes, Subaru thought of "death" as merely one of the costs.
It was only natural to assume that a power this strong came with a price to match. Naturally, Subaru assumed his own "Return by Death" was no exception to that rule.

There was likely a limit on the number of uses, or else something was probably being sacrificed with each repetition.
Subaru wasn't conceited enough to think himself so doted upon by the goddess of fate as to be granted infinite attempts. The only people he could proudly say had ever loved him were his parents.

Given that, trial and error to learn the limits of "Return by Death"—that is, deliberately courting "death" himself—naturally fell out of consideration as an option. It would be no surprise at all if this were his last chance.
Supposing it were the pattern where something was being sacrificed, the common trope would be trading away a precious existence or an important memory, wouldn't it?
Unfortunately, for a Subaru without memories, the only things that could count as precious existences—aside from his family in the original world—were Emilia and the others he had contact with here in the tower.

And having thought that far, what occurred to him was――,

"Don't tell me my memory loss is the price for 'Return by Death'..."

It was a terrifying thing even to imagine, but it was a sufficiently plausible possibility. Offering up memories as the price of "Return by Death." A vicious sort of design, but "Return by Death" itself was tasteless to begin with.
What was most frightening of all was that, even supposing that were true, there was no way to confirm it.

In fact, the connection between Subaru's memory loss and "Return by Death" was unknown. For now, since becoming aware of his "amnesia," Subaru had already died four times, and at least within the range he could himself track, he hadn't confirmed any further loss of memory.
His memory from the point he woke in the tower after returning from the convenience store onward was vivid.
―He could only hope there weren't countless loops missing from his memory somewhere in between.

"―Hey, are you listening, Barusu?"

Ram's sharp voice pulled Subaru, lost in thought, back to reality. At that, Subaru let out a "Hm..." from the back of his throat, and,

"Yeah, I'm listening. I get that it's shocking. It's sudden, and I get that it's hard to just believe right away, but..."

"You get it, but?"

"I've got no..."

"There's no reason for Subaru to tell such a tasteless lie, I suppose. Ram, too, should have at least that much trust in Subaru's judgment."

In place of Subaru, who was choosing his words carefully, it was Beatrice who answered Ram. Sitting properly beside Subaru, she had been fully on Subaru's side ever since accepting the circumstances in the green room.

"Lady Beatrice..."

"Emilia's words aren't exactly a lie either, I suppose. Losing his memory, the one most anxious about it is Subaru, I suppose. That's why he even cried like a child, all sniffly, I suppose."

"That episode—hearing it told is embarrassing."

At this unexpected revelation, Subaru scratched his cheek and chalked up the reason for his tears to "that sort of thing."
In truth, it had been a tangle of many things—being able to Return by Death, the reunion that had been granted, the chance to start over given to him. But tears were tears.
There was no real point in digging deeply into the reasons behind a man's tears.

Regardless, he felt nothing but gratitude toward Beatrice for taking his side like this.
Like Emilia, Beatrice had been confused at first upon hearing the story in the green room, but having taken longer than Emilia to swallow the circumstances, she'd promised her support to Subaru with a thoughtfulness and capacity for understanding that belied her appearance.

―And precisely because of that, the fleeting relief Beatrice had shown at the end of the previous loop, and her words "I brought you out"—those came back to Subaru's mind, and he felt his chest tighten as if bound in chains.

Just what had "Natsuki Subaru" done for Beatrice?
Not knowing that, yet relying on her trust anyway—the guilt of it. He warned himself not to accept that as something to be taken sweetly for granted.

"Honestly, kicking down a delicate topic like losing my memory with logic like 'there's no reason to lie about something like this here' is pretty rough, but I'd appreciate it if you could swallow that."

"Swallow it, he says..."

"On top of that, let's talk constructively. Luckily, I'm in a forward-looking mood right now. I'd welcome any talk aimed at moving forward, and... if you've got something to say, I'll listen properly to that too."

Building on Beatrice's argument, Subaru said this and bowed his head once more. To back Subaru up, Emilia also bowed her head, saying, "Please, believe him."

"――――"

Seeing the solemn attitude of Emilia, Beatrice, and Subaru, even Ram seemed unable to find words of rebuttal. And once the reflexive pushback against the initial shock had run its course, what arrived belatedly was the natural bewilderment.

Of course, Ram wasn't the only one shocked by Subaru's "amnesia" announcement. She simply showed the most conspicuous reaction—the reactions of the others besides Ram, Echidna, Julius, and Shaula, matched the pattern Subaru had already experienced twice before.

"――――"

Honestly, his reunion with Emilia and Beatrice had already hit him hard, but the moment he was able to reunite all at once with the other members here, Subaru's heart was shaken to its core.
Julius, left behind in the lower level with Reid. Echidna, who'd had both legs blown off and died apologizing for having doubted Subaru. Shaula, whose whereabouts had been unknown within the tower amid all that chaos. And Ram, who had turned her strongest suspicion on Subaru and with whom he'd never gotten the chance to reunite afterward.
Everyone. Every single one of them was here. He'd been given the chance to exchange words with all of them again.

And so, above all, the one Subaru was most keenly aware of in this moment was――,

"―Still, geez, Onii-san really is a troublesome person, isn't he?"

"――!"

"What's with that reaction? Making a face like you'd met someone who died—isn't that suuuper rude?"

Yes, even hearing Subaru's story, the girl spoke lightly, showing no great surprise at all.
Her dark blue hair braided, her black attire never without a single fashionable accent—the young assassin, Meili. Meili Portroute, moving, speaking, undeniably there.

"Meili..."

"Ohh? So you remember my name, at least. ...I mean, I don't even know what's different about you from usual, Onii-san. What exactly did you forget?"

"――. Yeah, it's a little complicated. Right now it might look like there's no trouble with conversation, but if you dig a little deeper, it's pretty shaky. It's what you'd call episodic memory loss—I remember the names of things fairly well, but memories involving people are pretty precarious."

"...Does that mean, like, even things from yesterday?"

"―That's right."

Narrowing her eyes, her voice dropping slightly in tone, Meili asked. At her question, Subaru hesitated for only a moment, then gave in to the feeling and, rather than lining up excuses to soften it, answered honestly.
He probably could have talked his way around it with some painful excuse, but he didn't. He'd decided not to. ―As much as possible, Subaru would deal with them all honestly.

"―Even yesterday, huh. That's, well, that's..."

"――――"

Hearing Subaru's answer, in some sense more shocked even than by the "amnesia" announcement itself were Julius, who murmured something under his breath, and Echidna, who was said to have had some suspicious secret meeting.
But setting aside their reactions,

"Master, did you lose your memory again, ossu? How many times do I gotta be forgotten before you're satisfied, ossu? I'm gettin' a real bummer outta this, ossu~"

Shaula said, pressing down on her ample chest and pouting discontentedly.
Up until now, Shaula's nonsense could be brushed off lightly, but coming at this point, her remark carried a subtly hard-to-ignore weight.

"It's a weird feeling digging into your nonsense, but is your Master really the type whose memory just up and vanishes like that all the time?"

"―? It flew off pretty often, ossu. When I got up in the morning and said hi, he'd often go, 'Who're you again? Don't remember ya. Never heard of ya,' treating me like some ex-girlfriend, ossu."

"Hmm, at that level, I can't really tell if that's just messing around or not..."

Hypothetically speaking, if this were an ordinary day after Subaru had gotten closer with Shaula, he could well imagine himself trading that kind of light banter just to tease her.
But Subaru was also aware that, having lost his memory, there was a version of himself choosing not to tell Emilia and the others about it, trying to hide it. It wasn't out of the question that pretending to joke around was itself a way of masking the fact that he'd genuinely lost his memory.
Even he found himself unbearably troublesome. In fact, having died four times already, it wasn't even funny as a joke anymore.

"I understand about the memory loss. Honestly, I have to say it's still something that'll take time to accept... but it seems wise to act on the assumption that this tower has the possibility, some kind of trap, of causing such a phenomenon."

"The most likely scene for it happening is the 'Taygeta' archive, where Emilia-chan and the others found me collapsed. It was already a place with a questionable reputation to begin with."

"-chan..."

"―?"

At Echidna, who had begun considering things with a serious expression, Subaru nodded and laid out his own opinion. But partway through, it was striking that Emilia murmured something with a lonely note, just once.
In the previous loop too, she had shown this kind of expression and reaction several times during conversation with Subaru. In the end, the true cause of it still hadn't come to light.
Was there some fatal oversight somewhere? ―That thought scared him.

"―Everyone, sorry for the shock, anyway. I don't think it's realistic to just take this all in at once and keep the conversation going as if nothing happened. Let's take a break for a bit. I'll go fetch water with Ram in the meantime."

Proposing this, Subaru sprang up from the center of the circle. At that, Ram's brow twitched slightly, and Emilia and Beatrice watched Subaru with concern.
But Subaru nodded to the two of them and turned his dark eyes to Ram,

"Let's go, Ram. ―You looked like you wanted to lure me to the water spot anyway, didn't you?"

"―How lewd."

At Subaru's invitation, Ram averted her gaze and murmured that.

※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※

"So, what was the meaning of that farce earlier? Since you've gone to the trouble of drawing Ram out like this, I take it you intend to actually explain?"

Buckets in hand, Subaru and Ram left the meeting area and headed for the water spot. Along the way, judging that they'd put sufficient distance between themselves and Emilia and the others, Ram brought this up to Subaru.

That she never once truly took Subaru's "amnesia" claim at face value—that was the same every time. This wasn't a matter of evidence or of stubbornness; there was a more important reason behind it.

―Rem's existence. Ram's beloved, still-sleeping little sister.

It was precisely out of concern for her that Ram could not accept Subaru's "amnesia."
That was exactly why she stubbornly denied it. The detailed circumstances she didn't know. But surely, Subaru had had some connection to Rem while she was awake.
And that had greatly supported Ram, in her existence as Rem's older sister.
And so――,

"Don't go entrusting Lady Emilia and Lady Beatrice with such a heavy role too often. Lady Beatrice is one thing, but it's still too much for Lady Emilia to bear. So, I'll credit you for thinking to drag Ram into this instead. Now, the full story..."

"―Ram, it's true that I've lost my memory. It's not a lie, not a trick, not some plan."

Subaru had to deny the very thing Ram was clinging to, the thin thread she was trying to rely on.

"――――"

Hearing Subaru's straightforward words, Ram cut herself off and narrowed her eyes. What dwelled in the depths of her pale pink eyes was bewilderment and unease—and the spark of a fierce anger.
That spark would soon grow into a blaze threatening to burn Subaru's very Soul to ash. And what had lit it was none other than Natsuki Subaru's own suspicious, dishonest conduct, time and again.
Truly, it was an example of one's own deeds coming back to ruin oneself.

"I've lost my memory. I can just about grasp everyone's names and relationships inside the tower, but beyond that, things are shaky all over the place. This too is true."

"Stop."

"I've already told Emilia and Beatrice, and told them exactly the same thing. There's nothing more I can tell you than that. Right now, my hands are empty."

"Stop, Barusu. Any more of this..."

"I know I came to this tower to reclaim a lot of things that were taken. I know we're in the middle of the 'Trials.' But that's it. My own motive is..."

"Barusu, don't say any more."

"―About Rem too, I..."

"Barusu――!!"

I don't remember, Subaru told Ram, with a heart-wrenching resolve.
Ram, who had been showing through her attitude that she didn't want to hear Subaru's words at all, grabbed at him with a face contorted in fury upon hearing his apology.

"Guh!"

Grabbed by the collar, he was slammed back against the wall. With unbelievable strength for such slender arms, Ram pinned Subaru down and glared at him from point-blank range.
In the depths of her pale pink eyes, he could see the spark had flared into a raging fire, threatening to consume both Subaru and Ram herself.

When this flame burned Subaru away—no, burned Ram away—tragedy would repeat itself.

"What do you think you're doing? Such a... such a worthless lie!"

"It's not a lie... You think I'd tell you a lie like that..."

"Are you saying you're not lying? Then what am I supposed to do? Am I supposed to believe it? That Barusu forgot Rem... such a, such a ridiculous story!"

"Ram..."

Her eyes sharpening further, Ram glared at Subaru from a distance close enough for their lips to touch. In the blazing fire, Subaru finally realized it came not from anger but from grief.

The conflict she carried ran deep, far more complex than Subaru could imagine.
Having repeated this four times, Subaru had only just begun to touch a fraction of it. To truly understand, in the fullest sense, the feelings and wounds someone else carried in their heart—just how much depth of thought would that require?

That Emilia and the others could pierce straight through to it in a single attempt, when Subaru, wielding his repeated cheat power, only barely grasped it after four repetitions—that was dazzling to him.
And because he couldn't just keep being burned by that dazzling light, Subaru――,

"―I will definitely bring Rem back."

"―!"

Looking straight back into those pale pink eyes so close before him, Subaru gathered strength into his throat and said it clearly.
Hearing that, Ram's eyes widened in surprise again, but anger quickly moved in to hide it.

"With what mouth do you say that... bring her back, what does that even mean, you forgot her, Barusu did, forgot Rem!"

"Even so, I'll bring her back. Rem, and my memories, and the purpose of coming to this tower—I'll accomplish every single bit of it, and we'll all go home together. ―That much of a reward is only right."

"―Barusu?"

"It's only right... considering everything that's happened in this tower."

There was breathlessness too. But it was for a different reason that Subaru's face twisted bitterly. Ram frowned at Subaru's reaction, and the strength in the hand gripping his collar loosened slightly.
That hand, Subaru now grabbed with both of his own and pulled away. In the same motion, he switched their positions.

"―How lewd. Let go of me."

Pressed against the wall in his place, Ram, staring at Subaru from close range, spat this out.
But her words, weak and lacking force, didn't make Subaru flinch.

"Ram. I'll bring back my memory, and Rem too. So lend me your strength for that."

"――――"

"I need everyone's strength. The 'Natsuki Subaru' you all knew until yesterday might never have said something this pathetic. But right now, I..."

Julius had entrusted, Beatrice had believed, Echidna had forgiven, Emilia had wished.
That "Natsuki Subaru," expected of by everyone like that—perhaps he could have changed this deadlocked situation even alone.
But the Natsuki Subaru of right now couldn't do that. And to sulk over it, give up, and whine that he could do nothing—the people in this tower were far too dear to him for that.

"I know you can't trust me, can't forgive me, for having forgotten Rem. But please, put that anger aside for now. In exchange, I'll make you a promise."

"A promise...?"

"I will definitely see it through. I'll keep biting down on it, no matter how many times it takes. If I break this promise, if I give up in front of you, then do whatever you want with me—boil me, roast me, whatever."

"――――"

Ram's eyes widened, and the heat of her anger drained away from them. Naturally, what surfaced as the heat receded was another emotion that had been hidden behind that anger.
Watching that emotion, Subaru drew his head back and continued, facing her straight on—at just about the same distance as when they'd met eyes across the ice cage.

"That's my resolve."

"...Why go that far. If Barusu really did forget Rem, you shouldn't be able to want to bring her back that badly."

"――――"

"When you forget, it's empty. There's just a hollow blank left there, and the feelings for what was there are lost. They vanish. Love, hatred, warmth, loneliness—all of it."

Ram's quiet voice put on a cold hardness.
Those words, so heavily weighted with lived experience, were perhaps the very blankness she herself had once endured. That was why Subaru's resolve was hard for her to believe.
She was asking whether it was even possible to hold such a strong wish toward emptiness.

"Honestly, that's right. My memory is empty, and however I felt about Rem up until yesterday, that's already slipped through the cracks of my fingers, gone..."

"Then why?"

"But I know that you treasure Rem, that you're desperately wishing, struggling, to bring her back."

He had seen Ram, wishing and struggling to bring back Rem, her beloved little sister.
Witnessing that wish, that love, held so fiercely, Subaru had been overwhelmed by it. And because Ram, desperate like that, was one of the people Subaru had wished could be saved――,

"Right now, the reason I want to bring Rem back is for 'Natsuki Subaru,' and for you."

"――――"

"So when I give up, what happens next is up to you. That'll be my atonement for selfishly dropping the contents of my own head and making you cry."

"I wasn't crying. Don't be ridiculous."

"Ow!?"

Slapped hard across the face with tremendous force, Subaru crumpled right there on the spot.
Holding a hand to his reddened cheek, Subaru looked at Ram as if he'd seen something unbelievable.

"Y-you... I just said something that took a lot of courage, and..."

"Getting all worked up on your own—what's courageous about that? Anyway, a promise from Barusu, don't make me laugh. Of all things to volunteer, you had to pick the least trustworthy condition in the world."

"Emilia-chan said that to me too—just how many promises did I break before today!?"

"Was there ever a promise you kept?"

"That bad!?"

Abused with a voice gone utterly cold, Subaru once again revised his assessment of "Natsuki Subaru." For better or worse, his stock fluctuated wildly, but broken promises were quite the sharp drop.
Generally, if you make a promise, running around to keep it is the bare minimum obligation.
Precisely because people believe a promise made will be kept, whether or not anyone is watching, they're willing to take on a loss for someone else. This was proof that spirit was lacking.

"'Natsuki Subaru' really isn't much of a guy after all, is he..."

"Yes, that's right. It seems you were mistaken, but Barusu up until yesterday wasn't so competent a man that he could handle everything alone either. If anything, he was a slowpoke who specialized in trying to handle things alone and ending up spreading the damage further. Ram, too, was constantly troubled by him."

"Seriously? Why'd you even bring a guy like that to the tower..."

"He was pushy. And he was a man whose mouth ran on and on with nothing but talk. He was reasonably dexterous for odd jobs, though, suited for handling chores. He was also good at soothing Lady Emilia and Lady Beatrice. And then..."

Being thoroughly trashed like this, Subaru, sitting cross-legged on the floor, felt rather uncomfortable.
Even though it wasn't about himself, he was being scolded as if it were. Being spoken well of about "Natsuki Subaru" by Emilia and the others carried its own complicated pain, but having Ram go on at such length badmouthing "Natsuki Subaru" was, well, complicated too.
Figuring he might as well hear it all out at this point, Subaru gave in entirely, and,

"And then what? Short legs, bad memory, picky eater, stubborn to a fault?"

"Short legs, bad memory, picky eater, stubborn to a fault."

"Yeah, figured."

"―And he treasured Rem."

"――――"

Suddenly, the tone of her voice changed, and color crept into Ram's frozen, emotionless voice.
Warm—if a voice's tone could be described as a color, it would be a soft pale pink, an enveloping, faint hue.
A tenderness for Rem, for her little sister, showed in her voice, and, imagining the time when "Natsuki Subaru" had been by her sister's side, a soft affection that still had not faded peeked through.

Pale pink was the color of gentleness—enough so that Subaru nearly mistook it for a truth of the world.

"Barusu. ―You really have forgotten Rem, haven't you?"

"...Yeah."

Ram's eyes reflected Subaru and would not look away. Incredible. He genuinely admired her for it.
In a scene like this, hearing words he didn't want to hear, Subaru knew he himself would look away. And yet she—Ram—never once tried to avert her eyes.

"Barusu. ―You really will remember Rem again, won't you?"

"Yeah, I'll remember. Not just Rem—everything else too."

"I honestly don't care in the least if everything else stays gone. Just remember Rem, and only that."

"Don't ask for the impossible. Let me get all of it back..."

"I'll say it again. Remember Rem, and only that, even if it kills you."

"Yeah, that I can swear to. ―Even if it kills me, I'll remember all of it."

Literally, even if he died, he would remember all of it.
What "Natsuki Subaru" had seen, heard, felt, and built up in this other world, everything that had brought him this far—he, Natsuki Subaru, would reclaim all of it.

"...Fine. I'll let this slide, this time."

Hearing that answer, the pressure Ram had been radiating suddenly vanished.
Sensing it, Subaru, still sitting cross-legged on the floor, asked, "You sure?"

"It's what I asked for, but really, is that okay?"

"Aren't you a man? Take it with grace. I've heard Barusu's resolve. On top of that, you even said that if you gave up, I could boil you, roast you, shave you, gouge you, chomp you, or beat you, whatever I like. If I didn't lend an ear to that, my motherly kindness would be called into question."

"I don't recall those steps after boil and roast..."

"Did you say something?"

"Nothing."

Shaking his head slowly, Subaru responded to Ram in polite speech.
"Motherly kindness" was quite the grand claim, but considering the saying about even a Buddha's patience running out after three strikes, Subaru had already plunged into a fifth attempt that even a Buddha couldn't forgive.
If he couldn't cling to god or Buddha, then entrusting judgment to a motherly figure was perhaps not a bad idea either.

"Stand up, Barusu. Ram won't allow giving up, and Ram won't allow bending the knee."

"Don't lump sitting on the ground in with those... oop."

Springing up, Subaru brushed off his backside and turned to face Ram properly.
Leaning her back against the wall, straightening her disheveled clothes and hugging her own arm, Ram now looked just as usual—her standing posture making him think this was simply Ram's "usual"—and she looked back at Subaru.

"...Did you say the same thing to Lady Emilia and Lady Beatrice too?"

"Those two... didn't seem like they'd even considered the idea of me giving up in the first place."

"That's true too. ―Bad Barusu must have rubbed off on you."

"That's why I can't ask that of those two. Or of Julius and Echidna either, emotionally speaking."

And besides, probably—Emilia and Beatrice, Julius and Echidna.
The answers of those four, he thought, he'd already heard in his contact with them during the previous loop.
So the answers that remained, he would confirm from here on.

"Still, huh... From what you're saying, the guy I was until yesterday wasn't all that great a person either, was he."

"With or without memory of Rem, your worth to Ram changes drastically. Watch your mouth."

Coldly discarding that remark, Ram turned her back on Subaru and started walking.
The two of them had spent a long time stopped along the way to fetch water, but going back empty-handed would only give Emilia and the others strange cause for worry.
Bucket in hand, Subaru caught up to Ram and fell into step beside her.
And then,

"I... 'Natsuki Subaru' was really here, wasn't I?"

Subaru asked quietly, toward Ram's profile.
It was less a confirmation than a weak, uneasy murmur, close to it. Something like that was probably ill-suited to say right after having sworn not to give up.
It would have been no surprise at all for Ram to punish him for it, saying the words hadn't even had time to dry on his tongue.

"―Idiot."

But Ram didn't do that. She didn't even stop walking, and, scolding Subaru as if cherishing him, said,

"Right now, it's simply hidden from view for a little while. It's only that so many things have piled up on top of it, burying it deep, that it feels lost. It's like a flower buried beneath cold snow—when the season of snowmelt comes, it will show itself again. ―That's surely all there is to it."

To Ram, who showed no expression as she said this, Subaru couldn't show his own expression right now.
Right after putting on such an air of composure, he couldn't let her see a face this pathetic.

And so, it was precisely because Ram said nothing more and didn't even try to look at him that, in this moment, her way of being felt, to Subaru, truly like that of a mother.

※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※

The situation was, he wanted to believe, changing significantly—but in truth, it wasn't quite that much.

This wasn't the first time Subaru had confessed his memory loss, and the sight of everyone, while left with some confusion, outwardly absorbing the shock together—that too was a scene he'd already witnessed.
Still, when the placement of one's heart, the way one braces oneself, changes, so too does the way one sees each person.

Last time, Subaru had doubted Emilia and the others that much, suspecting every corner of their actions, attitudes, remarks, from every angle. Convinced they had to be scheming something.
But strip away that filter of suspicion, and what came into view, from every angle of their actions, attitudes, remarks, was concern for Subaru, and, fundamentally, self-directed reproach.

In the end, they had consciously restrained themselves, trying not to let Subaru feel anxious.
That he'd found their behavior suspicious, unnatural—that had simply been a problem on Subaru's own side.

"Do it properly. Do it right, Natsuki Subaru..."

Telling himself this, Subaru stared intently at his own palm.
The cause of Subaru's memory loss—there was a high chance it lay in "Taygeta." Breaking through the "Trials" mattered, but getting to the bottom of that memory loss was also an urgent task.
Though it hadn't happened yet, if amnesia were to strike people other than Subaru one after another, they could well end up in the absurd situation of everyone saying "nice to meet you" to each other all at once.
And besides, he might not actually have the luxury of taking his time about this.

"Last time, and the time before that, the inside of the tower was absolute chaos."

Two loops back, inside the tower, Subaru had discovered the "corpses" of Emilia and the others—no, of his companions in the tower besides Emilia and Beatrice, one after another.
Last time was different from that; this time, he'd instead had to watch his companions' "deaths" one after another, and Subaru's heart had grown ragged and frayed.
But this abnormal state of affairs was, in either case, a calamity bound to occur within the tower before too long.

As one who knew the worst tragedy, Subaru had to prevent it from happening.
He would do everything in his power toward that end. ―And so, first of all, Subaru――,

"――――"

Behind Subaru, who stood conspicuously at the edge of a high place, came a faint breath.
A presence killed just subtly enough that, if one were paying attention, one could just catch the edge of its existence. Sensing it in what felt almost like cheating, thanks to foreknowledge, Subaru spun around at the last instant.

"―!"

"Whoa, careful there. ―Don't go falling in my place."

The outstretched hands swung through empty air, and Subaru, catching the body of the person pitching forward from the momentum, reached out and steadied them, pulling them back before they could fall.
That body was light. Not the ominous kind of lightness he'd encountered in various situations before, but the appropriate lightness for that girl's appearance—yes, appropriate for her.

"Now then, let's talk. ―You're going to take responsibility for killing me, you know."

Saying that, Subaru smiled at the girl whose arm he'd caught—Meili—and delivered his climactic deduction to the culprit who had, twice in the past, pushed him to his death.
