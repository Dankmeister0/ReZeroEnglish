Arc 4 Chapter 110: "Reason to Believe"

Finding Emilia curled up small, hugging her knees, gave Subaru a sense of relief that felt oddly out of place.

That he'd been able to find her was one thing. That she'd been here, waiting to be found, was another. The conviction that this had to be the place, and the wish that it would be — both had come true, and he could feel something heavy that had been lodged deep in his stomach finally drop away.

"Still, gotta hand it to you, Emilia-tan. Good thinking."

"............"

"This place — nobody would ever find you here. Not many people can even get in, and the ones who can are all people who don't want to."

At the very least, aside from Emilia, only three people could enter this place — Echidna's tomb. One refused the Trials of the Tomb and despised the place. One had seen a past that wasn't his own and, because of it, entrusted the future of the Sanctuary to someone else. The last had earned the Witch's displeasure mid-Trial and had his qualification stripped from him. Everyone else with the qualification kept strictly to the rules laid upon them and stayed out of the tomb. Which made it, quite literally, a hiding place tucked behind the consciousness of everyone but Emilia.

Subaru's words, meant sincerely as praise, drew no answer from Emilia. She stayed small, silently looking up at him.

"—Mind if I sit? Honestly, standing's kind of rough right now."

"............"

"Silence counts as a yes where I'm from. Well, pardon me, then."

Under her watchful gaze, Subaru declared this with more confidence than he felt and lowered himself down beside her. Two fists' worth of space opened up naturally between them — the outer limit of his nerve and his heart as a man. Closing that distance would have to wait until words and gestures had closed the distance between their hearts first.

Right now, the gap between their feelings was clearly wider than that fist's width.

"――――"

"――――"

Sitting side by side, a stretch of silence fell between them. Subaru waited patiently for Emilia to speak, while Emilia stared at his profile as he sat like that, her lips trembling hesitantly again and again before she finally managed,

"Subaru is..."

"――――"

"Why... are you here?"

"Why, huh. That's a tough one. I guess it's because I've been thinking about you the whole time, Emilia-tan, and I managed to figure out exactly where you'd gone."

That was actually something of an honor. At the very least, within this Sanctuary, it meant he'd been thinking about Emilia harder than anyone, and had guessed her feelings more accurately than anyone. Though, if he'd truly understood her feelings in the way that mattered, they probably wouldn't be having this conversation inside a tomb right now.

Emilia's eyes widened at his answer. Then she shook her head, as if the answer he'd offered wasn't the one she'd been asking for.

"No. That's not it, Subaru. Not why you came here, but... how? This place is supposed to be closed to anyone without the qualification."

"Are you forgetting, Emilia-tan? I stepped inside here on the very first day, to carry you out after you collapsed in there. If someone's as hated by the Witch as Roswaal, apparently just entering feels like your body's about to burst. I don't get that bad. It's just this endless floating feeling, like right before an elevator stops. Not unbearable."

"...I see."

Disappointment, that her reasoning — that no one could possibly follow her in — had been wrong. And beneath it, a faint, worried glance at Subaru, who was even now fighting down nausea. She had to be desperately trying to put her own feelings in order, and yet even in a moment like this she couldn't help worrying about someone else — it was almost painfully admirable, and it made his chest ache with fondness.

"How did you... know I'd be here?"

Talking felt better than silence — it distracted her, eased something in her. Perhaps sensing that from the flow of conversation, Emilia watched Subaru take a deep breath and, composing herself, asked a different question. Her words carried doubt and resignation tangled together, awkward things to handle. The resignation was listless; the doubt had a paranoid edge. Both were remarkably rare things for Emilia to show — maybe even things Subaru was seeing from her for the first time.

"...Subaru?"

"Ah, sorry, sorry. Why I thought you'd be here — well, it's that. There's nothing about you I don't understand, Emilia-tan. That's basically it."

"Liar."

He hadn't meant it as a joke, but his glib words were cut down without mercy. Emilia, chin resting between her raised knees, tilted her head to look at him. The emotions adrift in those violet eyes were, right now, so tangled they seemed on the verge of swallowing her whole.

As if unwilling to let those emotions be scooped up and seen, Emilia looked away, pursing her lips where Subaru couldn't see,

"Talking like that won't fool me. —Even I don't understand myself very well. There's no way Subaru could understand me."

"People are surprisingly bad at seeing themselves, actually. Sometimes other people see all the way down to your feet better than you ever could."

Not quite from personal experience, but something like that happened often enough. How reckless, how rash, how thoughtless you were being — you often couldn't see it yourself when your blood was up. Sometimes you only realized it when someone else pointed it out. Just as it had once been for Subaru — only realizing it when someone else said it aloud.

"Whether Emilia-tan was here or not — I half believed it, and half hoped for it."

"Half, half..."

"I ran all over the village and couldn't find you anywhere. So instead of thinking about where you'd gone, I thought hard about why you'd go somewhere. And then I figured — it'd be here. Finding you, I was seriously relieved."

"...Was that all? Relief?"

"Hm?"

Emilia posed the question to Subaru, whose lips had relaxed with relief. Her voice was quiet, almost fading away, and when Subaru raised an eyebrow at it, she looked deep into his eyes, as if trying to peer straight through them,

"Finding me here — was that all? Just relief? ...Aren't you angry, or anything?"

"What's this, Emilia-tan? Don't tell me you're scared I'll be mad at you."

At Emilia's timid manner, Subaru nearly burst out laughing. Disappearing without a word, and then, once her whereabouts had been found out, being afraid of getting scolded for it — that was exactly the kind of thing a child would do. Knowing her true age now, and her true mental age, made it a little more understandable, but it was still unlike how she'd behaved up until now. Still, Subaru didn't let himself dwell on that strangeness, and simply shook his head.

"I'm not angry. I was worried, and honestly, I was in a total panic — but I'm not angry. Including the fact that I found you here — I'm glad about all of it."

"...I see."

The feeling Subaru held toward the vanished Emilia was different from anger. If he felt anger at all, it was directed at himself, for having overlooked the signs that she might do something this drastic out of sheer desperation. Even the anxiety that all their careful preparations might come crashing down had, now that he'd actually found her, been swept away by relief. So Emilia's worry was needless. That was what he'd meant to convey.

"You're not angry."

That had been his intention — and yet what was folded into Emilia's murmur was not relief.

"—Emilia?"

"Subaru's not angry with me. —You won't even get angry with me."

Her voice, small and hoarse, was trembling. By the time Subaru, puzzled, furrowed his brow, it was already too late to notice. Looking down, biting her lip, Emilia's eyes were wide open.

Tears welled up, filling them to the brim, as if she were straining to keep them from spilling over.

"Why won't you get angry with me?"

"Emili—"

"I did something selfish, didn't I? I caused trouble, didn't I. I disappeared without saying anything and made you worry, didn't I? Made you anxious, wondering if I'd run away or something... I did things like that, didn't I. If someone did that, you'd want to be angry, wouldn't you? Isn't that how you feel too, Subaru?"

Cutting off Subaru's attempt to call out to her, Emilia rattled off her feelings in a rush. She pressed at him, insisting on the selfishness of what she'd done, demanding to be condemned for it.

Overwhelmed by the desperate intensity of her manner, Subaru finally realized he'd made a decisive mistake in choosing his words. Emilia hadn't been afraid of Subaru getting angry at her. Emilia had been afraid of her actions going uncondemned. Because that meant—

"Why won't you get angry with me...? Is it because you don't... expect anything from me? Being kind to me even when I fail, over and over — isn't it because you're not disappointed in me? Because you already think... it won't work out anyway... isn't that it?"

"――――"

Perhaps this was the thing Emilia had been anxious about all along, the thing lurking in the depths of her heart that she'd never been able to put into words.

Herself, challenging the Trials again and again, and coming back each time with her spirit broken. There were people who looked on her failures and let their disappointment show, thinking little of her for it — and among them were people like Subaru and Puck, who never blamed her.

Emilia had been saved, in part, by the presence of people like Subaru and Puck. But at the same time, she had surely been fighting, all this while, against an anxiety she could never quite wipe away.

Disappointment came because you were expected to succeed. Thinking little of yourself came from disgust at your own inability to resist. And yet, no matter how many times she failed, being comforted so gently, again and again, brought only temporary relief to her heart — while a still-greater anxiety threatened to scorch it.

The kindness Subaru and Puck showed her — that was the thing Emilia had been afraid of all along.

"That's not it, Emilia. That's not what I think at all."

Grasping, far too late, the height of the wave breaking over Emilia's heart, Subaru raised his voice. If he didn't catch hold of Emilia here, right now, something terrible would happen. If she rejected him now, he'd never be able to reach out and catch her again. So without even the composure to think through what he should say, he cried out.

"The reason I can't be angry with you isn't because I think that way, it's—!"

"Then...! Why! Why didn't you... keep your promise...?"

"――――ッ!"

His hasty words were cut off, and Subaru's expression stiffened at the topic she'd raised. The 'promise' Emilia spoke of — that could only mean the one exchanged between her and Subaru the previous night, and whether it had truly been kept was—

"You said you'd hold my hand until morning! And Subaru promised, said you understood... so why did you let go? Why didn't you keep the promise...?"

"――――"

"S-Subaru... and Puck too... break their promises and go off somewhere. Leaving me behind, going off somewhere... liars. Subaru's a liar. Puck's a liar... liars, liars... liarsss...!"

Her voice, tearful and fierce, condemned him for having broken his promise. Head bowed, tears spilling, Emilia knocked her head against Subaru's shoulder, and her strengthless hands struck at his chest. There was almost no force in the blows at all — and yet it hurt like being shot through. All of this was pain Emilia had been carrying that Subaru had failed to notice and overlooked. She'd had it inflicted on her, one-sidedly, by the two of them — by Subaru and by Puck.

"P-promises are important, I... I told you before, I told you before! For a Spiritual Arts User, for me, promises matter... so I asked you to keep it... and you apologized for not keeping it before... and now, again, you broke a promise..."

"...Emilia."

"You can't break a promise. You can't lie... you have to keep your promises... because, if you don't... if you don't, I... to Mother, and to Juice..."

Her face pressed against Subaru's shoulder, Emilia's feelings wandered with nowhere to go. Rising emotion and the anguish of betrayal threw her thoughts into a thousand scattered pieces, twisting them into chaos. Her halting words dissolved into incoherence, until at last Emilia's voice broke into the sobbing wail of a child,

"Lying is bad... it's bad...!"

The spilling words shook with anguish, and Subaru felt a pain like his own heart being clawed apart. 'Promise' — between Subaru and Emilia, that word had rung out with different meanings, again and again. There had been a time he treated it lightly, hurt Emilia by it, and dug a rift between them. After their reunion, they had confirmed the meaning of that promise, and surely bound themselves together in the shape of a bond.

And yet again, 'promise' rang out not as a gentle sound, but as a heavy, punishing weight binding the two of them. More than anything, the way Emilia spoke the word 'promise' was far too unlike her usual self. Something — some crucial part at the very foundation of who Emilia was — was what bound her to promises like this.

"――――"

Burying her face between her raised knees, Emilia was still sobbing. With every second he faced her like this, Subaru's heart was carved apart by guilt.

What should he say — he thought desperately, listening to the sound of her sobbing. Should he apologize? Should he act as though he understood? Should he comfort her with all his might? Should he try to convey just how much he cared?

The thoughts spun round and round in Subaru's head, and he couldn't grasp even a hint of the right answer. What, why, how, what should he do, what was the best thing to do—

"――――"

He thought, and thought, and thought, and in the midst of his whirling thoughts Subaru closed his eyes, and then. He felt like he'd finally arrived at an answer for what to do.

"Emilia. —I love you."

"――――"

It was, terribly, words out of place for this moment.

"...Eh?"

Hearing Subaru's words, Emilia let out a stunned voice and lifted her face. Her violet eyes, still brimming with tears, opened wide, and her gaze caught Subaru. His reflection, distorted on the surface of her tears — so he made himself keep his own heart, facing her, unshaken. Because there was no longer any hesitation in the words he had to convey.

"Night after night, throwing yourself against the same Trial over and over. So what if it's the Trials of the Tomb. It's just the past, isn't it. Don't go on sulking forever about something that's already happened."

"...Ah, uh."

"When I offer to do it for you instead, you go stubborn on me, saying it's your job to do, or whatever. If that actually cleared it, fine, but if the result's the same, that's all just talk, isn't it. Think about how it feels on the other end, having to watch you lose over and over and over."

"S-Subaru..."

"And then on top of it, the moment your pet-slash-guardian disappears, you can't even stand up and walk properly on your own? Crying your eyes out, worrying everyone, throwing away your duties to sulk in bed? Give me a break, seriously, I can't keep up with you anymore."

At Subaru's spat-out words, Emilia's eyes went wide as though she'd seen something unbelievable. Her welling eyes lost even the tears themselves in the shock, and her lips trembled weakly, unable to form meaningful words. Without question, more than ever before, Emilia's heart had been wounded. For the first time, she was being torn apart before a malice and contempt that Natsuki Subaru had never once directed at her.

Emilia's expression twisted. It wasn't the anguish of tears, nor the fury of a tantrum, nor the emptiness of being crushed by resignation. Struck by abuse she had never been subjected to before, Emilia's expression changed into something else entirely — not any expression that would ever have suited this.

—The corners of Emilia's mouth curved into a terribly parched smile.

"Th-that's... right. O-of course Subaru would... th-think that about me, too, of course..."

"――――"

"I deserve to be told awful things like that... I do. Ever since coming to the Sanctuary... no, ever since long before that... I've just been causing trouble... so I..."

"Yeah. Honestly, since coming to the Sanctuary, there hasn't been one good thing. Not that I'm one to talk, but even so, this is way too much. There's no way to spin it."

Subaru affirmed Emilia's trembling self-denial. At that, Emilia's throat made a strangled, choking sound. Swallowing down something like a sob, she went on, still wearing that agonized smile,

"So I... deserve to be given up on... by Puck, by Subaru too..."

"Sure. After pulling this much and still showing no sign of improving. It makes total sense that the feeling of 'let's do something about her' would give way to 'let her sink or swim, whatever happens.'"

Emilia tried to reach a conclusion from the denial Subaru had handed her. Subaru snatched the ending right out of her mouth and delivered his own scathing verdict of her instead.

"—But."

Right before that ending arrived, Subaru cut his words of denial short. Emilia lifted her face to look at him. In her eyes floated an emotion only Subaru could recognize. —It was the same emotion Subaru himself had once worn. And so.

"I love you. —Emilia."

With the same words that had once refused to let Subaru escape, Subaru now refused to let Emilia escape.

The long lashes framing her eyes trembling, Emilia's mind had been entirely swept away by Subaru's words.

At the sight of Emilia frozen, thought itself stopped, Subaru's cheeks eased faintly. It wasn't relief, and certainly wasn't mockery — if he had to name it, it was nostalgia.

Everything, including what Emilia had been longing for, was so achingly familiar he wanted to shout.

"I love you. I love you, love you, love you, so much it's ridiculous."

"W-what... are you suddenly saying..."

"I love that super beautiful silver hair, I love those violet eyes that shine like jewels when they well up, I love your voice, just hearing it puts me in a dream, and your long slender arms and legs, your pale skin, even the height difference — it's all too perfect, and just being near you makes my heart pound so hard I'm going crazy about it."

"――――"

"I love that you're a little scatterbrained sometimes, I think it's cute how hard you try at everything, I respect how desperately you can push yourself for someone else, I can't stand seeing you neglect yourself like you do, and I keep thinking that if I could just keep feeling every one of your expressions, every one of the things that move you, right there beside you forever, that'd be the best thing in the world..."

"Don't... don't joke around at a time like this!"

The feelings for Emilia spilled out of Subaru's mouth smoothly, effortlessly. Emilia shook off what he was saying and screamed, her voice raised in fury.

Shoulders trembling, eyebrows raised, Emilia bared her anger at Subaru's attitude — an attempt, it seemed to her, to change the subject, to dodge her condemnation, to make a mockery of everything that had come before.

"Why are you suddenly saying something like that! That's not what we were talking about! S-Subaru said I was completely hopeless, that I wasn't nearly enough, said that! Said you couldn't keep up with me anymore, couldn't stand to watch anymore... th-that someone like me, that..."

"Yeah, that's right. Shown that much uselessness, results that make you sigh over how it's all just talk — I'm short-tempered as it is, so normally I'd have run out of patience with you a long time ago. If it weren't for the fact that it's you, Emilia."

"Why?!"

Even while affirming how pathetic the results had been, Subaru denied the most important part of it. As if that didn't sit right with her, as if it were unforgivable, Emilia raised her voice.

"Being useless, being beyond saving — that's all me, isn't it!? So why are you trying to overlook it. Why are you letting it go. Why..."

"If you just want the answer to that, I've already said it more than once. It's because I love you!"

"――――ッ"

To Emilia, pressing close with her tearful voice, Subaru raised his voice right back, face to face with her. Emilia recoiled as if pushed back, but Subaru closed the same distance she'd retreated. Either way, they stayed close enough to feel each other's breath.

"I love you. So no matter how pathetic a side of you I see, I just think, 'oh, a new side of Emilia,' and even when you're falling short everywhere, I still find myself cheering, 'this is where you push through!' No matter how disgusted you get with yourself, I won't hate you for it."

"――――"

"Even if you get so sick of your own uselessness and pathetic-ness that you think you deserve to be judged just as harshly by everyone around you... I'll keep expecting things from you, and I won't use your weakness as an excuse to give up on you, to write you off."

Emilia's eyes wavered. That was the same look she'd turned on Subaru moments ago — a weakness that wanted to accept denial, to receive resignation, to sink down into sorrow and grief. Emilia had wanted to be denied. There are times when being told, by yourself and by others alike, that you're hopeless, that you're beyond saving, feels like the only way to finally be saved.

Subaru knew that. And he also knew what it meant to be pulled back from that brink. Because Natsuki Subaru, too, had once tried to give up on himself in the same way — and still, in the end, hadn't been allowed to.

"I'm completely gone on you. Everything good about Emilia looks dazzling to me. Of course, I know it's not all good things. You're... you're not an angel, not a goddess, just an ordinary girl. When things are hard or painful you want to cry, when things are unpleasant you want to avoid them, and if you could choose only the easy, happy things, some part of you wants that too."

"――――"

"But even the weak parts, even the parts you'd call ugly — I love the whole of what makes you Emilia, all of it. So... even now, I'm not disappointed in you. Not at all."

"—! That's... that's so selfish!"

The shape of the feelings Subaru wove from his own mouth. Hearing them poured out one after another, Emilia, unable to control her confusion, argued back.

"You said all that, denied me like that, said I was hopeless so many times, and now you say you still love me... th-there's no way I can believe that! Why Subaru could believe in me like that, I... I don't understand it at all!"

"That's wrong! You've got it backwards from the start! It's not, 'I believe this or that about you, so I love you.' —That's not it. I love you. That's why I can believe in you. That's how it goes!"

"Loving me alone isn't a reason to believe in me!"

"—! If loving you isn't enough to believe in you, then who the hell would go through all this pain just because he happens to like a troublesome woman like you, and come here to save you anyway!"

Voices rose, feelings crashing into each other. Subaru pressed a hand to the wall and stood, and Emilia rose to face him in turn.

Close enough their foreheads might collide, eyebrows raised at each other, Subaru and Emilia roared their feelings. Spit flying, faces flushed red, "You're the one who's wrong" — two people who had never once, in all this time, raised their voices at each other like this.

"I love you! So much I could go crazy, so much I'd be fine dying for it! That's why I put up with the pain, put up with how sick I feel, and stand here in front of you right now even though I want to throw up!"

"I didn't! I never asked for that! You just say whatever you want... you're the one, Subaru, who never thinks about my feelings at all! Standing there in the line of fire because of me, always getting hurt for it... you have no idea, none at all, how it feels to watch that happen!"

"Like I'd know, I haven't even thought about it! All I think about is how to look cool in front of you! How to make sure I look my best in your eyes, how to get the result that'll make you happiest... after all the trouble I go through, the least you could do is make a cute face and go along with it!"

"Don't talk about me like I'm a doll! If you really wanted to make me happy... th-then why do you break your promises! All I want is for you to keep the thing I asked you to! Why won't you even do that! You actually hate me, don't you!"

"I love you!!"

"Liar!!"

Emilia's raised voice overlapped with Subaru's, who was pouring out his feelings almost in desperation. Did she have any idea how long a road it had once taken him to be able to say these feelings aloud. How many obstacles Subaru had crossed just to be able to say the words he needed to say. This confession of love, scattered so thickly it might almost seem cheap — and yet every part of it was, for Subaru, the honest truth, poured out with his whole body and soul, seeped into him down to the last shred of his Soul.

"It's not a lie! I love you! What about you — what do you even think of me?! Acting all coy like that! Every time you make that cute face and act like you're interested, do you have any idea how much it shakes me up! Cut it out!"

"I-I'm not acting like anything! I'm just being normal, don't say weird things! I have so much to think about right now, it's too much, how I feel about Subaru is... I can't think about that right now! Stop it! Don't put me on the spot!"

"Who's putting who on the spot?! Me? Or you?!"

"Subaru is! It's you, isn't it?!"

It was an argument with not a shred of logic to it — pure emotion, hurled back and forth. Like two children in a tantrum, shouting abuse in each other's faces, Subaru and Emilia declared their feelings at the top of their lungs.

Their voices echoed through the dim, cramped tomb, and the place that had been filled with a long silence rang now with more noise than it had known since it was built. Their outburst seemed enough to shake awake whatever slept within the tomb, and the two of them, breath ragged, went on with their fruitless quarrel.

"I can't believe anything you say anymore! You're a liar, Subaru! You break a promise and still show your face in front of me without a care... y-you thought I hadn't noticed, didn't you! But I was watching properly! I was watching properly to see if Subaru would keep his promise to me!"

"Don't do something that nasty! Pretending to be weak just to test somebody — aren't you ashamed of yourself?!"

"I don't have to take that from a liar who broke his promise!"

"Me breaking a promise and this are two different things!"

At Subaru's shameless attempt to shift the subject again, Emilia's cheeks flushed with anger. Her emotions overflowed past the breaking point, and the words wouldn't come. Emilia drew several ragged breaths, and forced the words out.

"Why... why did you break the promise...?"

"...I know breaking the promise was wrong of me. That I wanted to hold your hand and stay with you until morning — that part was true."

"That's not what I'm asking. —Why did you break the promise."

"...I can't say."

Teeth clenched, Subaru answered Emilia's question with a pained groan. At his attitude, even now, still trying to dodge the question, Emilia let out a long breath.

"You won't keep your promise. You won't even tell me why you broke it. ...So what am I supposed to do. If you're going to say you love me... then really do it! Or else I... I can't believe you...!"

"Emilia."

"If you'd kept the promise, stayed with me until morning! I'm sure I could have believed in you! Believed in you and entrusted you with everything! But instead, Subaru broke the promise... so I thought I really was hopeless... that Subaru and Puck were both going to leave me behind..."

Face crumpling, Emilia dug her fingers into her silver hair and hung her head. The fierce emotion of moments before shifted in temperature, changed direction, and Emilia wrapped her own arms around herself,

"With Puck gone, scenes keep rising up in my head. ...Inside my head, unfamiliar scenery, conversations I don't remember, keep spilling out more and more..."

"――――"

"Even though I thought I remembered everything properly, until now, the memories that come out are all things I don't know... but they're definitely my memories, and every time I remember, every time something comes out that must have been there but I'd forgotten, it makes me anxious...!"

The memories Emilia spoke of — those were the true memories she had wanted to turn away from, the ones Puck had dragged out of her in exchange for their bond. To seal away a past she didn't want to remember, Emilia had used Puck as a lid, locking those memories inside. Now that her Contract with Puck had been severed, with its restraining bond gone, the memories overflowed, and her true past was filling up her inside.

But to her, that was dramatic enough to feel like her own transformation.

"I finally realized I've been leaning on Puck, running away from all kinds of things... Puck probably disappeared to teach me that. But I'm scared. I'm scared. With Puck gone, and the real memories spilling out... it feels like I'm not going to be me anymore."

"――――"

"When all these memories have completely overflowed... I'm sure I'll become a different me. The me now believes memories different from the true ones are where I began... but if I remember my real beginning... the me who's walked this far will surely disappear..."

Starting from a false memory, Emilia's resolve and determination had been shaped. Then, once the true memories were restored, once the shape of that beginning changed — what would become of the resolve and determination Emilia held now, the path she had walked to get here?

『—What matters isn't the beginning, or the middle. It's the end.』

"――――"

A voice suddenly echoed in his mind. A distant voice he should know well — close to Subaru's heart, and yet belonging to someone he could no longer meet. Words handed to him as homework, at the very last moment of their parting.

Ah, that's right — he thought.

However you began, whatever road you walked, right up to the very, very end — who has the right to decide whether it was a mistake or not?

"No matter what memories Emilia remembers, nothing changes. I love you. I'll go on loving you, just the same as always."

"—! I can't believe it. That you'd tell me you love me... even if I disappear, even then, you'd still say that...?"

"I can say it. No matter what happens, you won't disappear. I love you."

"...Even though you're a liar. Y-you wouldn't let me believe you... even though..."

"—Then I'll make you believe it."

In a trembling voice, with trembling eyes, Emilia tried to refuse Subaru. Words couldn't get through. Gestures couldn't convince her either. If so, there was no way left to convey the depth of his feeling except through action. And so,

"Suba..."

"If you don't want it, dodge."

Close enough to feel each other's breath — no, closer than that, close enough that not even breath could pass between them. Subaru reached for Emilia's shoulder and brought his face close to hers. Seeing him approach, Emilia's eyes filled with confusion, and her body went rigid.

He waited, one second. If she pushed him away, that would be the end of it.

"――――"

But Emilia closed her eyes. Whether that was resignation, or the result of hesitation, Subaru couldn't tell.

"—nn" "—dz"

Their breaths tangled together, Emilia held hers, and Subaru's brow furrowed with a flash of pain. The small sound came from their teeth clicking together in the momentum. He tasted a faint, throbbing pain first, but it was quickly swept from the corner of his mind by an intense heat.

Soft lips. A kiss that was only a touch.

For Emilia, it was her first. For Subaru, it was the second kiss he'd shared with her. Unlike the first, which had tasted of cold, of 'death' — this second kiss tasted of hot, burning 'life.'

"—Ah"

Neither of them pulled away first; their lips simply parted.

Their faces, which had been touching, drew apart, and, forgetting even to breathe, they gazed at each other. Flushed cheeks. Welling eyes. Even his own reflection in Emilia's eyes wore an utterly melted expression. Subaru, the first to come back to his senses at the sight of his own pathetic face, breathed as though remembering how, and said,

"I love you."

"――――"

"No matter how bad a side of you I see, even after an argument like this, I still love Emilia, unchanged. That's not going to change no matter what — so I believe in you, always. And as for why—"

"Because you love me..."

Picking up the end of Subaru's sentence, a dazed Emilia touched her own lips with a finger. As though tracing the softness still lingering there to confirm it was real, tears spilled over. The tears trailing down her white cheeks glimmered as they fell, like drops of moonlight.

"It's only natural to feel anxious when unfamiliar memories come flooding in. I get feeling scared, like some stranger of a self might come out of it. But that doesn't mean the road Emilia has walked disappears, and it definitely doesn't mean your feelings change."

"How can you say that... so certainly...?"

"Because what matters isn't the beginning. It's the end. —The woman I respect more than anyone else in the world told me that."

To the mother who was usually the worst at reading the room in the world, and yet who taught him the most important things in the world. Not that he truly understood it yet — but because he wanted to come to understand it. Because there was someone standing in front of him he wanted to think he could come to understand it together with.

In front of Emilia, standing frozen with anxiety, Subaru shrugged his shoulders in an easy, carefree manner. As if to kick that anxiety away, as if to say it was nothing.

"It's fine, Emilia. No matter what you remember, I'm on your side. Remember whatever you've forgotten, all of it. And if you're still scared even then, let's find it."

"Find... what?"

"Just like I can charge forward on the strength of loving you, Emilia — I mean the important feeling that'll let you charge forward too, without worrying about everyone around you."

For someone else, Emilia never hesitated to offer up herself. That way she had, of putting others first — it was noble, and beautiful, and Subaru loved it dearly.

But the phrase "for someone" was terribly gentle, and terribly sad. Because feelings for a faceless someone would surely never reach as far as feelings for someone whose face you could see.

"Though I'd love it if that important feeling turned out to be pointed at me."

"My... important feeling..."

Whether or not Subaru's words reached her ears, Emilia pressed a hand to her own chest and lowered her eyes. Where she reached her fingers was the spot where, until now, the crystal stone that served as Puck's Anchor used to sit. Now that their bond had shattered, reaching there to check found nothing to touch. Just fingertips scraping at empty air. And yet Emilia closed that hand firmly, and,

"Inside all these memories, once I get them all back... will it be there — my important feeling?"

"Yeah. I bet it will. The reason to keep walking."

"—Mm."

Not quite half-doubting, but not an expression of full, complete conviction either. Watching Emilia give a faint nod, Subaru closed his eyes and looked up at the ceiling.

The words spoken to him, back when he himself had been lifted up the same way, had felt like they carried more strength. He felt like he'd been saved by gentler words, and harsher words, and stronger words.

—Am I actually managing to be some kind of strength for Emilia?

"――――"

Putting that question into words out loud would be far too uncool. He exhaled, and the moment the tension left him, the nausea he'd forgotten about came rushing back to shake him at his core. He caught himself against the wall, only just managing to hold back the urge to retch.

"Subaru?"

"Nothing... is what I'd like to act tough and say, but it's actually plenty. Kind of bad right now, honestly. Anyway, if we're going to keep arguing or keep talking, let's do it outside, if that's fine."

"...As if you don't mean that anymore."

At Subaru, pale-faced and putting on a brave front, a faint smile crossed Emilia's lips. Though weak, it carried the strong flavor of someone desperately trying to act normal. A clear answer had not yet formed within her. The anxiety, too, had not been entirely wiped away.

Hand on the wall, Subaru started walking toward the tomb's exit. Behind his unsteady, stumbling back, Emilia's hand reached out hesitantly, as if to touch him, then drew back, again and again. It seemed even she had been greatly affected by the touch of their lips a moment ago. Looking back on it now, his face grew hot at the thought of just how bold a thing he'd done.

Still, all such sentimental musings were, for now, left behind.

"――――"

For Emilia's sake, the best way to show that Subaru was on her side. That was to fulfill the role he'd set for himself, and to protect her, and her feelings, to the very end. So—

"I've got to see through what I need to do, all the way to the end."

They stepped outside the tomb. The bright sunlight greeted the two of them as they walked out of the darkness. And then,

"—Yo. Sorry to keep you waiting."

"Tch."

At those words, at Subaru raising a hand, someone clicked their tongue in irritation.

"—I wasn't waitin', not one bit."

—Standing there, his entire body stained with blood, was Garfiel, radiating hostility.
