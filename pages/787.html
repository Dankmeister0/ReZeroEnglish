Arc 10 Chapter 28: "Too Late Now"

—The "Sword Saint." —The "Blue Lightning." —The "Adorer." —The "Mad Prince."

Each of these was a title bestowed upon the strongest in this world. The Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica, the Vollachian Empire, the Kararagi City-States, and the Holy Kingdom of Gusteko—beings hailed as the mightiest in each of the Four Great Nations, transcendent figures who had attained a level of strength far beyond human intellect.

Yet, as was common knowledge, even among those ranks, one stood a cut above the rest in sheer power: the current "Sword Saint," Reinhard van Astrea, the strongest being on the face of the earth.

Reinhard’s existence—so colossal that it was said no greater living being would ever be born, past, present, or future—could not be contained even within the framework of the Four Great Nations’ mightiest. Yet for that very reason, a certain fact went quietly overlooked. Namely, that under the shadow of the anomaly named Reinhard, the legendary fame of a warrior who possessed enough power to naturally stand alongside those other three had been severely underrated.

The person who had no choice but to remain submerged in the shadow of the strongest "Sword Saint" in history—that man was none other than—

"—Marcos."

In a hoarse, weak voice, Heinkel’s lips uttered the man’s name. Heinkel himself was tall, but this giant of a man stood a head taller, effortlessly pinning down Heinkel’s attempt to draw his sword with a palm the size of a child’s head. What was terrifying was that this was not mere physical strength. If Heinkel tried to draw it, it was pressed back; if he tried to lower it, it was pushed in—hilt and scabbard remained perfectly joined, without a fraction of a millimeter of the blade coming into view.

As if demonstrating the difference in stature between Marcos, the Captain of the Royal Guard, and Heinkel, who had been relegated to the seat of a figurehead Vice-Captain.

"—Take your hand off the sword, Heinkel."

A low voice that vibrated deep in the core resonated down the hallway. A frame nearly two meters tall, with thick muscles fit to support such a massive physique, wrapped in full silver plate armor—even in stillness, his unhideable sword aura and overwhelming presence made him feel even larger, to the point where he was praised as being more giant than a Giant.

Marcos Gildark—Captain of the Royal Guard of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica, a tough warrior known by the moniker "The Monolith." Ever since his younger days as a mere rank-and-file knight in the order, he was a man about whom even the "Sword Demon" Wilhelm van Astrea had declared that the position of future Captain was beyond doubt.

"—!"

In an instant, cold sweat covered Heinkel from head to toe. His back became drenched, and greasy sweat burst forth on his face and neck. Cold sweat from fear, greasy sweat as an alarm bell of survival instinct—he found himself strangely impressed by his body's versatile ways of showing terror.

"Felt-sama, Filore-sama, please step away from this man."

Leaving aside Heinkel, who was once again committing the folly of letting a bad habit of out-of-place thoughts master him, Marcos cast a sharp, fleeting glance toward the back of the corridor—at the two figures peeking over from the stairs. At his command, there was a sense of Filore and Felt holding their breath, and—

"W-Wait, Lord Captain! Heinkel-sama is..."

"—Shut up!!"

"Huh!?"

Heinkel's bellow instantly silenced Filore, who had spoken up to Marcos. However, the voice that tried to feign wild, furious rage cracked, its tail end raspy and pathetic. He had no leeway to turn around. But he could vividly picture Filore, her red eyes wide with surprise, staring fixedly at his back.

His chest ached. But it was necessary—silencing her mouth. Right now, there was still—

"Successfully slipping into the position of Filore-sama's knight was one thing, but invading the royal castle aiming for the persons of Felt-sama and Emilia-sama... Who set you up to this, 'Sword-Forsaker'?"

Feeling the unhideable disgust and contempt beneath the emotionless voice, Heinkel distorted his cheeks in self-derision. —Yes, right now, it could still be framed like that.

That it was all Heinkel's scheme, that Filore had merely been used by him, and that Felt too had simply been forcibly dragged out, with no intention of treason against the royal castle or the Divine Dragon Church. To make that excuse hold water, he had kept Filore uninvolved in the preparation of the plan for the past half-month.

Of course, it was an insurance policy he would have preferred not to use if possible. However, the activation of that insurance was now guaranteed. —Because he had run straight into Marcos.

Because he had been caught by an opponent he could never, ever beat, even if he were given a hundred, a thousand, or ten thousand chances to challenge him.

"Old man..."

Beside Filore, who had been silenced by the shout, Felt muttered softly. The tone of her voice was proof that she had caught on to the meaning of Heinkel's insurance policy. What a sharp girl she was. She probably wasn't Lugunican royalty. Filore, whose ragged breathing showed a lack of resignation and poor understanding of the situation, was far more persuasive as a member of that soft-hearted clan. And on top of that—

"Abandoning your duties as Vice-Captain, drowning yourself in alcohol, making your son continuously clean up after your own blunders... what are you plotting now, dragging Royal Selection candidates into it? —Answer me."

"――――"

"Answer, Heinkel. For once in your life, show some knightliness."

At those words, Heinkel's vision flared crimson red. For once in your life, show some knightliness—to be told that by a man blessed with the power to act knightly, blessed with the opportunities, blessed with courage... a thick, dark impulse welled up from the bottom of his heart.

Marcos had to know. What Heinkel had lost, what he had thrown away, and for what purpose he had done it. Knowing all that, what right did Marcos have to berate Heinkel as he struggled and squirmed?

"Spare me your whining, Heinkel."

"—Ah..."

"There are plenty of people who face hardship just as you have, yet hold their heads high without losing heart. Despite being born into a position far more blessed than theirs, you ruined even that through your own actions. —Who could possibly sympathize with you?"

He couldn't say anything. Nothing at all. A flat voice, neither shouting nor spitting, gouged deeply into Heinkel's guts. With his insides thus gouged out, Heinkel bit the inside of his cheek, raising his face from the pain. Meeting head-on the cold eyes of Marcos right before him, he opened his mouth. To spit out a villainous line fitting to activate the insurance he had set up—

"—Hih."

But from his constricted throat, not even a single word—the "That's right" he ought to say—came out.

"—. You really never grow, do you."

It was a statement like a sigh, belonging neither to disappointment nor to dismay. However, the colossal fist that followed those words ignored all previous quiet dialogue entirely, striking the side of Heinkel's head and slamming him directly into the stone wall.

"Gah!"

The stone wall caved in from that single strike, radial cracks shooting across the floor and ceiling like lightning. The muffled groan he leaked was swallowed by the sound of destruction, and stone fragments rained down upon the collapsed Heinkel.

He had been punched. That was all. Without even taking a proper stance, a single strike like brushing away a nuisance—with just that, Heinkel's body was sent flying like a thin twig. In the Vollachian Empire, Heinkel had been forced to face a Dragon against his will, but the sense of intimidation he felt from Marcos was precisely the same as what he had felt from that Dragon—the overwhelming sensation of facing an organism built on an entirely different scale.

"Heinkel-sama!!"

A scream-like voice—Filore's cry sounded distant. Amidst the crumbled stone fragments, he placed a hand down to push himself up. But the hand that touched the floor slipped, and Heinkel pathetically slammed his face into the rubble. A fierce pain shot through the tip of his nose, and blood dripped. Just as that blood trickled into his throat, causing him to choke, a merciless boot toe kicked straight up into Heinkel's jaw.

"Gah—!?"

Kicked in the face, Heinkel's body flew straight upward and slammed into the ceiling. As he was about to get embedded in the ceiling, his leg was grabbed, and he was forcibly slammed back down onto the floor. —No, he was plucked from the floor again, hurled upward to the ceiling with a lift, and down to the floor with a drop. Ceiling, floor, ceiling, floor, up and down, brandished like a child playing with a doll.

"S-Stop..."

"Begging? You always do that."

The plea, mixed with blood and gastric juice, was swallowed by the impact of wind buffetting him sideways, and granted. Having every inch of his body battered against floor and ceiling without exception, it ended by him being hurled against a cracked stone wall. The accumulated shock far exceeded Heinkel's comprehension; he had no idea what part of his body was intact and what was broken. Even though he didn't know—

"You really are a fellow who can't be reasoned with."

"Ah, gh—!"

Marcos's descending heel crushed Heinkel's right hand with sheer force. The back of his hand and wrist were smashed by the hard heel; even as he instinctively tried to pull his arm back, it wouldn't budge, as if pinned between giant boulders. The foot was twisted right there, and four fingers, excluding the thumb, made a sickening sound.

"Ghi, aaaaagh—!!"

"Stop howling. It's insufferable to hear."

While cursed for his nature of letting out ugly screams, Heinkel bared his teeth. He tried to bite the ankle of Marcos's foot that was crushing his right hand, attempting to tear it off. It was futile. The opposite foot kicked his face away, leaving a deep boot print stamped onto his forehead.

"Give up already."

Demonstrating an overwhelming difference in power, Marcos spat those words out dismissively. Give up—what a stupid thing to say. His will to fight had scattered to the winds long ago. In the first place, he hadn't possessed even a shred of something as grand as a "will to fight" ever since he reluctantly agreed to cooperate with this plan.

That was right. Yes, indeed. Of course. Heinkel had known from the very start that a plan like this had no chance of succeeding.

"Why are you like this?"

His vision burst into white. Grabbed by the back of his head, his face was slammed into the floor with all Marcos's force.

"You don't learn a single lesson."

Grabbed by the lapels and lifted up, a fist punched straight through his abdomen while his feet dangled. With nowhere for the shock to escape, all his internal organs began a chorus of agony as if exploding inside his body.

"What do you want to do?"

A second blow, a third, a fourth, a fifth—the impacts continued. The grand chorus interrupted with each blow and resumed even uglier than before; despite being struck in the stomach, he felt as if the contents of his brain were spilling out, leaving him unable to think anything at all.

"What do you want to become?"

There was something. There had to be something. Something... had been there. Things he wanted to say, things he ought to do, desires, things he couldn't throw away—some kind of... something, had to have been there. It had to be.

"Hey, Heinkel Astrea. —How many more times, how many more people, how much more must you disappoint others before you're satisfied?"

The hand of Marcos gripping his lapels let go. Unable to support his body on his toes touching the ground, Heinkel collapsed forward, slamming his knees and forehead against the floor, blood spreading outward centered around him as he tumbled face-down. A massive nosebleed and vomit poured from his mercilessly crushed face, and his internal organs, beaten and churned again and again, flowed out as bloody stool.

"――――"

Can't stand. Can't stand. Can't... stand.

"Please! Stop this already! Don't hurt Heinkel-sama any more!"

Blood flowing from above and below, drowning in the blood he himself spilled, a girl's high-pitched scream tried to shield Heinkel as he hovered on the brink of drowning. It was terribly absurd. There was nothing here worthy of that scream, or of that appeal.

"Filore-sama, you are being used by this man."

"No! That's not it... That's not how it is! I was the one who asked Heinkel-sama myself—"

"Fool, stop! Don't go ruining the old man's stubborn pride!"

"—!, Felt, but still!"

Over the head of Heinkel, who lay fallen and unable to move, a desperate exchange took place. Felt was right. Filore was wrong. Against "The Monolith" standing in their way, the girls would never be able to escape. People only ever talked about Reinhard, but Marcos was that kind of existence too. Now, the goal of this plan was no longer to fulfill victory conditions, but rather to minimize damage and beg for mercy—it had shifted to that perspective. That was why Felt was right, and Filore was wrong.

"Cut it out already, old man! At this rate, you're gonna die!"

"Heinkel-sama...!"

Felt, who knew what had to be done, and Filore, who couldn't throw away her dreamy ideals even in this situation—both of their voices were calling out to Heinkel.

Unfortunately, he lacked the strength to answer either voice. With his breath literally fluttering like a dying insect, just barely managing not to drown in his own blood and stomach fluids took everything he had. Besides, whether it was "stop already" or "don't kill him," appealing to Heinkel was meaningless; it had to be directed at Marcos, who was beating Heinkel into a rag.

Even Marcos must have found this completely intolerable. Or perhaps he had finally reached the limit of his patience. Even before now, Marcos had looked at him with eyes of contempt, but it had been a long time since Heinkel was physically beaten down like this. Today, at this moment, the sight of Heinkel dragging around a Royal Selection candidate to use for his own scheme was conveniently villainous in Marcos's eyes. At long last, he could legitimately unleash his everyday—no, his long-accumulated, built-up rage.

That was why he was so relentlessly pursuing Heinkel, who was already defenseless and helpless—

"—What good is it now, you damn fool?"

With a click of his tongue, Marcos stepped forward. The fist is coming. The moment his mind registered it, the side of his face was struck by the wind of the approaching punch, his cheekbone shattering as his body spun half a circle, slamming into the wall of the corridor once again—this time the opposite one. His embedded body had its collar grabbed, and as he was pulled back, a knee was thrust up into his back. Bones from his spine down to his hipbones and ribs made creaking, shattering sounds, and he vomited a massive amount of blood.

"O-Aagh—!!"

Spilling blood, the arm he swung out had its wrist caught, an impact was applied from the outside to his fully extended elbow, and white bone tore through skin and flesh, peeking out from the snapped arm.

"Ugh, aah... gh..."

Marcos's hand thrust into the mouth Heinkel had opened in preparation for a rising groan. His fist was forcibly opened inside the mouth, and with a hard crack, Heinkel's jaw was dislocated. Several molars were broken as well, while iron hammers of fists and elbows battered the side and back of his head again and again and again.

"—!"

Half of his hazy vision went completely dark. Perhaps his eye had been crushed. His nose was squashed, the inside of his mouth was a mess, his arm was broken, his organs had burst, and there wasn't a single spot on him that wasn't soaked in blood.

It was relentless. It wasn't something that could be described with words like "merciless" or "ruthless." An act far more cruel, far more agonizing to look at than mere killing or mere torture—had Marcos lost himself to rage? He shouldn't have been a man with the temperament to go so far as to continuously beat the corpse of an opponent he had already unilaterally battered.

And yet, why did he continue to punch, kick, and trample? Against Heinkel, who had long since—no, had never possessed from the start—even a speck of intention to resist—

"—Even after being beaten this much, do you still intend to draw your sword?"

— —— ——— What?

"――――"

He had no idea what Marcos was talking about. Forget intending to draw a sword—not a single shred of will for that was surging within him. In fact, his right arm was broken from the elbow down, everything past his wrist was crushed, and all his fingers were pointing in bizarre directions.

His face had been struck and crushed repeatedly; the right side of his vision was pitch black, showing nothing. Inside his mouth overflowing with blood, the sound of breathing barely escaping through the gaps in his teeth was that of a dying man; there should have been no need to question the fighting spirit of a man who was barely alive.

In that halved field of vision, he could see Marcos splattered with the blood that had sprayed from Heinkel, and beyond him, the two girls being forced to watch this "fight"—this one-sided violence. One had her cheeks stiffened, while the other had her large eyes filled with tears, clasping her hands before her chest as if praying. From the sight of them, too, it came across vividly just how unbearably gruesome this scene was.

And—

"—Ah?"

At a small sound that faintly vibrated his eardrums, a voice of confusion leaked from his throat. That sound came from his beloved sword, hanging from the sword belt firmly wrapped around Heinkel's waist. The quiet, clear, crisp sound of a blade sliding out of its scabbard as it was drawn—

"――――"

With his right arm broken and crushed, his face battered and pierced, his internal organs burst, spewing blood from both ends—even so, the left hand he had fiercely protected was drawing the sword.

"Ah... I see..."

It was simple. Marcos was indeed not the despicable man who would violate the code of chivalry by continuing to beat an opponent who lay fallen and helpless.

It was just that Heinkel was still standing. It was just that he was still trying to draw his sword.

"Still..."

—Do I still want to play the knight?

"――――"

A foolish whistling through a blood-soiled nose echoed foolishly in the hallway taut with serious tension. He was silent, but not soundless. He was playing the knight, but he was in no position to call himself one.

Even so, he drew his sword. For what purpose? There was no plausible answer. Only, beyond his broken body and dimming consciousness, he could hear something.

It was not a sound from this very moment. It was—

'—Heinkel-sama is here.'

Reviving in his head as thoughts drained out alongside blood, an irresponsible voice. Even when threatened that she would lose everything, the foolish woman—the girl with no eye for people—who declared that something would still remain by her side, her voice lingered in his head.

'—Congratulations.'

He could hear a voice of congratulation. Not sarcasm, not irony, but a voice blessing that position, that state of being. A voice forced into the constraint of being unable to carry out that way of life even while knowing full well what purpose a sword was meant to be drawn for—it remained in his heart.

'—You're so adorable, Heinkel-san!'

He could hear a beloved voice. Just distant, just far away, a voice that felt purely, dearly beloved remained in his soul.

In his head, his heart, his soul—what else needed to align?

"O-Ooooh...!"

With an inarticulate roar, the accumulation of training ingrained into the dead-on-arrival wreckage of his ideals moved Heinkel's left arm.

There was no stance, nor any footwork. Swung around by the weight of his collapsing body, it was a flash of steel that could never be called a proper sword strike even if one's mouth were ripped open to say so. Yet that flash of steel undeniably reached Marcos's thick neck.

"――――"

Marcos's stern face, truly deserving of being dubbed "The Monolith," narrowed its eyes. The slash reached him. Marcos didn't try to draw his sword, nor did he deflect it with his gauntlet. The slash was simply blocked by the gray bedrock covering the skin of Marcos's thick neck. A neck thick as a log, forged through training. What covered it was multiple rising layers of rock shell; the thickly deployed armor of stone effortlessly accepted the steel sword strike.

Sparks scattered. The blade bounced off, the shock traveling through Heinkel's left arm. —On the rock shell covering Marcos's neck, only a single white line had been etched.

"It's over."

Having unleashed his strike, a fist plunged into Heinkel's now completely defenseless torso.

"――――"

He didn't even feel pain. The instant the fist touched him, his field of vision flipped completely, the world alternating between dark and bright. Realizing that was the result of him flying through the air in vertical cartwheels came only after his entire body took the impact, slumped against the wall he smashed into, and sank down with legs sprawling outward for real this time.

Half-buried in the cracked wall, at the side of the limply fallen Heinkel, a high-pitched sound echoed—the sword held in his hanging left hand hit the floor without power. This time for sure, neither fingers, nor eyelids, nor tongue—nothing moved.

"――――"

One step, two steps, three steps—approaching footsteps could be heard. Firmly treading on the hallway as he drew near, feeling Marcos's approach through his skin, Heinkel was about to face the approaching end moment by moment, head-on.

It ends. Is it ending? Will it be brought to an end? To think it would be Marcos who closed the curtain on this terrifyingly foolish life of his. The thought that he wouldn't feel relieved at all by this was so profoundly ridiculous—

"...S'top."

He said that to the slender back standing before him, having run past Marcos to shield him.

"No, I won't stop. I couldn't possibly!"

Spreading her arms wide, the girl stood in front of Marcos as if shielding Heinkel. Her face pale with all blood drained, cheeks wet with tears she couldn't hold back, her teeth chattering from Marcos's pressure that carried almost overwhelming savagery—yet still she stood. Filore was standing there, shielding the battered Heinkel.

"Please step aside, Filore-sama."

"N-No. I refuse. I will not. I am not moving from here."

"That man is using you."

"That's wrong! That's not it at all! All of this was something I asked for!"

At the words of Marcos, who quietly crept closer, Filore raised her voice to steel her own heart and shouted back. Wait, stop, don't let her say any more—his heart panicked. But it was useless.

"I was the one who asked Heinkel-sama to lend his aid to save Emilia and Felt, and to figure out a way to get us out of the castle!"

"――――"

"Heinkel-sama stopped me again and again! He tried to convince me to give up, saying I would lose my position as a Royal Selection candidate and my title as 'Saintess'! Even so!"

"――――"

"Even so, I was the one who pleaded with him! With Heinkel-sama—with my knight!"

The more she pressed her point, the more the trembling vanished from Filore's voice. What a simple girl. Raising her voice to stir her fearful heart—as if that could truly dispel one's fear. And yet, as a matter of fact, Filore straightened her back, wiped her tears, and stared down her opponent.

"That man is no knight. He is the 'Sword-Forsaker', who abandoned his qualification as a knight of his own accord."

Marcos was right.

"No, you are wrong. This man is my knight. A true knight who swore an oath."

What Filore was saying was wrong.

"――――"

Pitting their arguments straight against each other, Filore and Marcos glared at one another. In a standoff where height, age, ability, and everything else differed by lightyears, nevertheless, the one who cast his eyes down first was not Filore. It was Marcos.

"I see."

Short and heavy, it resonated like an old rock being split by raindrops. Saying so, Marcos slowly opened the fist he had held tightly clenched. Then, just as his lips moved to say something—

"—That's enough."

A clear sound like silver bells echoed down the devastated corridor.

"—Emilia."

The one who muttered that in daze was Filore, who had turned toward the sound from deep within the corridor. Unfortunately, the dying Heinkel could not even move his neck. However, footsteps of high heels and a faint wave of cold air provided the confirmation for the intruder's identity. Before long, into the edge of Heinkel's vision reflected the girl with long silver hair swaying—Emilia, who had opened the closed door and walked all the way here. And that was—

"Geez, give me a break. Just slipping past to reach the door felt like putting my life on the line, I'm tellin' ya."

It was the achievement of Felt, who stood beside Emilia while spitting complaints. At the same time Filore had rushed in to shield Heinkel, Felt had seized a lapse in Marcos's attention and bolted to the door of the room where Emilia was. And she had brought her out, forcing her to witness this battle scene. —Though what Emilia thought of it, he couldn't tell.

"That's enough, Marcos-san. Heinkel-san has a suuuper durable body, but if you do any more than that, he's going to die. I definitely can't overlook that."

"Even if this individual is a fool plotting to make use of your person, Emilia-sama?"

"Even if that were true, it's still no good. Besides, Filore was just explaining very earnestly that it's not like that. Right?"

Saying so, Emilia winked at Filore. Receiving that, the stunned Filore returned to her senses and hurriedly nodded her head vigorously up and down.

"Yes, yes, that's right! That's right! Heinkel-sama wasn't trying to use Emilia or Felt! If that were the case, I would be the mastermind!"

"Don't say it like that, it's gonna make things confusing! Anyway, point is, the old man wasn't trying to bring harm to us or Sister Emilia!"

"Wasn't trying to at all!"

When Emilia and Felt stood flanking Filore on either side, Filore put on a face as if she had gained an army of reinforcements, pointing a finger sharply at Marcos. In truth, three Royal Selection candidates were now standing side by side. What's more, facing off against the Captain of the Royal Guard, Marcos, as if shielding the battered Heinkel. That was, to say the least—

"—For a man who is the Captain of the Royal Guard to be glared at by three Royal Selection candidates all at once, what a scene that makes one look forward to the future."

That voice belonged to another new arrival, different from the three candidates, and of course from Heinkel and Marcos. The sound of approaching footsteps came from the same room deep in the hall as Emilia's. The one locked in that room together with Emilia was—

"—Ram-dono."

"You certainly went wild, Marcos-sama. Even though the spell formula was rewritten for today, doing this much could easily get noticed by someone else. Are you in your right mind?"

"I intended to cut whatever corners I could, but in this state, I have no excuse."

To Marcos slowly shaking his head, the pink-haired maid who had spoken so haughtily—Ram, the twin sister of the girl Priscilla had taken a liking to—snorted, "Hmph!" At that exchange, it was Emilia who furrowed her brow, asking, "What does that mean?"

"Umm, judging by what you just said, it sounds like Ram and Marcos-san are suuuper good friends."

"No, that is a misconception, Emilia-sama. The one who is suuuper good friends with Marcos-sama is not Ram, but Roswaal-sama. They are said to be great best friends of fifteen years."

"That sounds scandalous, so allow me to correct you. It is true that we have known each other for fifteen years, but calling us 'great best friends' affects my honor."

"Ah, that answer sounds suuuper like a friend of Roswaal's..."

"W-Wait, wait, wait! Why are you guys suddenly getting all friendly, hey!"

Following Ram and Marcos's responses, Felt interjected with common sense from beside Emilia, who had put a finger to her lips, calling out the sheer absurdity of the situation. She pointed straight at the messily battered Heinkel and shouted:

"This Stone-Face was thrashing around like he was gonna kill him just a second ago! Thanks to that, the old man ended up in this state, didn't he? What the hell is going on?!"

"Indeed. There is something Ram would like to confirm... Filore-sama, was what you said earlier true? That you snuck into the castle to save Emilia-sama and Felt-sama?"

"Huh? Yes, that's right! We came to rescue them. We properly thought through a plan for it, managed to get Felt out smoothly, and came all the way here intending to get Emilia too with that momentum..."

"I see. —Did you come up with that yourself?"

"The plan was made together with Heinkel-sama. The idea was my own."

Placing a hand over her chest, Filore confessed as if she had nothing left to hide. Far from feeling ashamed, Filore answered with an air of feeling refreshed to have spoken out at last, causing both Ram and Marcos to furrow their brows. Facing the meaningful reactions of those two, Filore spoke up with an "Um—"

"I haven't quite grasped the situation yet... but would it be alright if I treated Heinkel-sama? Nobody's going to stop me, right? Not that I'd stop even if you tried."

"Yes, of course, let's do that. Heal him quickly... Ah, but healing magic might not work as well here. Since magic has been made unusable..."

"Fufu, it's fine, don't worry. My Sacrament is not magic."

As she spoke, Filore squatted down right in front of Heinkel. Feeling her gaze up close, Heinkel, unable to move, could say nothing.

"You don't need to say anything. Heinkel-sama's actions were the best proof of all."

—I don't understand. What did Heinkel prove?

"――――"

While Heinkel remained in the dark, Filore's hand gently touched his face. From there, a soft, warm sensation gradually spread outward. It was certainly a sensation different from that of healing magic. —If this was a Sacrament of the Divine Dragon Church, perhaps this, bringing results distinct from magic, could reach what was unreachable. Even in the middle of having his own body healed, Heinkel found himself thinking such thoughts. Leaving Heinkel and Filore aside—

"So, Sister Emilia's Margrave and Stone-Face are friends, and what were you guys plotting with the maid?"

"The truth is, though it may be hard to believe... I also visited today with the intention of taking Emilia-sama and Felt-sama out of the royal castle."

"...Eh!? Marcos-san, taking us?"

"Yes," Marcos nodded heavily, and Ram, crossing her arms, supplemented his words.

"Ram was originally instructed by Roswaal-sama. If anything were to happen in the Royal Capital, Ram was to rely on Marcos-sama at that time. That he would surely lend his strength... That is why Ram accepted being detained alongside Emilia-sama."

"I did not explicitly state 'surely.' It depends on the circumstances of the time. Given my position, it is undesirable for me to favor a single Royal Selection candidate. However, in this case, circumstances are different."

"Which means you thought it was a situation where you had no choice but to side with us too, huh."

"Precisely. To that end, I reduced the security of this wing to a minimal staff, in order to delay discovery of the situation as long as possible..."

"And Ram cooperated to rewrite the magic-restricting spell formula placed on this floor. Redirecting part of its power into a barrier to prevent the commotion and voices here from leaking outside."

"Ah, I get it now. That's why nobody came runnin' to reinforce even though Stone-Face beat the crap out of the old man like that."

The explained circumstances aligned with the baffling facts, and Felt nodded in understanding. Then, while concentrating on her Sacrament, Filore, who had been eavesdropping, asked, "U-Um, excuse me?" without stopping her hands—

"Um, if that's the case, then Heinkel-sama getting beaten up so badly by the Captain..."

"Means it was a futile struggle where allies were beating each other up, wouldn't it."

"I do not think so. Neither that it was a futile struggle, nor that this man is an ally."

"—!"

At Marcos flatly refusing to recognize Heinkel as sharing the same cause, pain shot across Filore's face. However, though he felt bad for Filore, it was natural for Marcos to think that way. Heinkel's past actions were not so light that a slight shift in behavior could overturn them.

"Marcos-san, if you prepared to take us out, does that mean you're coming with us too?"

"That is my intention."

"Is that okay for the Captain of the Royal Guard? Things in the Royal Capital are already shaky as it is, right? If the Captain vanishes on top of that..."

"If pus cannot be drained without shedding blood, one must not shy from bloodshed. Besides, the Royal Guard is not so fragile as to crumble over the loss of a single man. I trust that each knight keeps in his heart how his sword ought to be raised."

Marcos declared firmly that he had already made up his mind and would not change it. Pulling her chin in at that answer, Emilia looked at Filore and said,

"Filore, we are going with Marcos-san. What about you two...?"

"Of course I'm going with Emilia and the others! I intended to from the start, and we can't comfortably stay in the current Royal Capital anyway. Besides..."

"Besides?"

"I want the Captain to realize that Heinkel-sama is a proper knight."

"――――"

At Filore declaring this so passionately, Marcos narrowed his eyes in silence. No matter how many words were strung together, there was a mountain of past actions that rendered him unworthy of belief. Leaving Marcos's reaction aside, Felt scratched her head, and Emilia smiled.

"Yes, then let's go together! Thank you, Filore. Even though doing something like this is going to be suuuper trouble, you did your best for our sake."

"Not at all! This is only natural. After all, I am Emilia's best friend, and with Felt, we have, well, what? A complex relationship, but aren't we like sisters! ...Like sisters! Aren't we!"

"Did you like how that sounded? Why'd you say it twice..."

At Felt's exasperated sigh, Filore lowered the hands she had been holding out, exhaling a long, long, deep breath. Sweat glistening on her forehead and her breath slightly ragged, she nodded, "Alright," and—

"We've managed to buy some time for now. Heinkel-sama, do you think you can stand?"

"...Nah, sorry, but..."

Since he didn't have a mirror, he couldn't tell what he looked like, but at least he felt that the pain in his struck head, broken molars, and snapped right arm had subsided considerably. Though Heinkel's physical toughness was guaranteed for a certain reason, he couldn't tell how much of this was the effect of the Sacrament and how much was the result of his body's abnormality. At any rate, standing up would take a bit more time—

"—We have no time for banter, so pardon me."

Saying so, Marcos stepped over without hesitation, lightly lifting Heinkel's body with his arms and roughly slinging him over his shoulder like luggage. In the process, the sword Heinkel had been gripping was snatched away and thrust back into its scabbard.

"Lord Captain... Did you choose to believe in Heinkel-sama!?"

"No. It simply means that if I am to strike him down, it does not have to be here."

"T-That would be useless! Even if you cut his torso clean in two, I'll just stick him back together with my Sacrament!"

"Geez! Save the bickering for later! Let's go before we're spotted!"

Chiding Marcos who carried Heinkel, and Filore who snapped back at him, Emilia looked over the faces of everyone present and took the lead. Each of them was prepared to step into an irreversible situation by making this decision. Seeing everyone's expressions with that in mind, Emilia gave a deep nod in return. And then—

"—We have to regroup with Subaru and everyone else. Everyone is probably doing suuuper reckless things right about now, so I'm worried!"

"Bro probably wouldn't wanna hear that from us when we're breaking out of prison ourselves, though."

Exchanging such words, the group left the castle with more people than either side's original plan had accounted for. And so, while making their escape from the castle—

"Heinkel-sama is truly amazing. I feel so proud!"

"...My nose is broken, though."

Even though she had watched him get turned into a tattered rag, Filore was somehow looking proud, and that response was the absolute best the immobilized Heinkel could muster.
