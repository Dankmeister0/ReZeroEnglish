Re:Zero EX: "The Butterfly Dream (Original)"

Waking up with his eyes seared by sunlight had lately become a standard part of Subaru's mornings.

"Heeeey! It's morning! Wake up, wake up, Subaru-sama!"

He prided himself on being a good riser, but even so, being handed the first foothold toward consciousness didn't mean he snapped fully awake on the spot. His brain had to register that the sunlight was inviting him back to reality, and it took a little while to make the trip home from the world of sleep.

Lately his sleep had been especially deep. Of course, that only meant he was working himself to the bone, body and mind, while he was awake — proof that his days were full, at least.

"Come on, you have to get up! Get up! Look — geeeet uuuup!"

"Noisy."

"Mgwuh!"

The voice went off right beside his ear as he lay curled in bed, showing not the slightest consideration for his circumstances. Subaru retaliated against the voice being poured over him from close enough that he could feel the breath of it — by hauling it into his arms.

With a squashed-frog sort of noise, the owner of the voice ended up in bed with him.

It was still the season when mornings were chilly, so taking in a bit of human warmth brought the temperature to exactly the right balance.

"Ahh, I'm coming back to life here. Sinking back into a nice, mellow, peaceful second round of sleep like this — there's something to be said for that... don't you think?"

"Mm! ...Honestly, I... um, I guess I don't mind..."

Whatever momentum she'd had a moment ago had evaporated; the voice was already halfway won over by Subaru's sweet talk.

They'd known each other a good while now, and although she was normally the responsible type, the instant Subaru started spoiling her, a childish expression would peek out.

That part of her, he found unbearably dear. Fickle of him, admittedly.

"All right, good girl. So let's just stay like this and spend the morning cuddling—"

"But the duke-sama is in charge of breakfast today... so she might get mad at you?"

"Urk... was she?"

Reminded of what he'd let slip his mind, Subaru's enthusiasm deflated. If he chose to sink into the dizzying pleasures of a second sleep, the aftermath would be terrifying.

No help for it, he thought, giving the girl in his arms one last good, hard squeeze in bed before flinging the towel blanket up and away.

"Nothing for it, guess I'll get up. Ahh, damn it, another beautiful day out there, huh!"

Bouncing off the bed springs, Subaru did a few light knee bends where he stood. Then he looked down at the girl who was still sprawled where he'd dragged her under the covers.

"Hey, hey... sprawled out all disheveled like that, your sex appeal is off the charts. I'm always telling you a girl shouldn't go around being so indecent — Petra."

"It's fine. I only let my guard down in front of Subaru-sama... in front of Subaru."

Sticking out her tongue and smiling adorably, Petra Leyte — now grown into the age where a maid uniform suited her well — said something so endearing it ached.

He held out a hand to her and pulled her up off the bed.

"Well then, shall we head to the dining hall? Everyone's probably gathered by now, right?"

"Yes. Let's go, Subaru-sama."

Petra smiled when he nodded at her. Still holding her hand, he stepped down from the bed.

Hand in hand, the two of them set off at a slow walk down the corridor — the red-carpeted halls of Royal Lugunica Castle.

"You're late."

—The first thing that greeted Subaru as he entered the dining hall was a dignified voice that made her extreme displeasure unmistakable at a single stroke.

Bathed in the intimidation packed into that voice, Subaru broke into a faint cold sweat down his back. Then, so his inner cowering wouldn't show, he manufactured a smile and raised a hand lightly.

"Sorry, sorry. Nah, I guess the fatigue's been piling up a bit, y'know. Just couldn't get myself out of bed."

"Do not string together clumsy lies. Do not forget that I can see through what is your true intent and what is mere surface. ...I expect you were fondling the maid who came to wake you again this morning."

Pinned by a sharp sidelong glare, Subaru whistled and turned his face away.

In point of fact, he had been flirting with Petra. Though whoever had morning wake-up duty, there would have been a certain amount of flirting regardless.

"In any case, it is a fact that you made light of your promise to me. ...I suppose it cannot be helped, being treated as worth no more than that."

"No, that's not what I meant—"

"I have no need of consolation. I understand it well enough myself. I am... not an endearing woman. Unlike the other women who surround you, I have never been eager about anything but swinging a sword. I know almost nothing of dressing up, or of cosmetics. It is only natural that I cannot satisfy—"

The uncharacteristic self-deprecation kept coming, surely because everything she'd bottled up day after day was finally overflowing. It didn't take some grand trigger for a dam like that to break.

It was simply that dissatisfaction, piled up and up, had chosen this moment to show its face. And the fact that he hadn't noticed until it reached this point was, without question, Subaru's own failing.

"—What is the meaning of this?"

"If you can read my heart, shouldn't you be able to read my intentions too?"

"Do not tease me."

Held in his arms, she turned her face away. She wouldn't look at Subaru, and shook her shoulders as if ashamed to be embraced. But that was resistance in form only.

"What I can see is no more than the direction of the wind. I may glimpse the surface of another's feelings, but I cannot see through to what lies beneath. ...And so the true intent behind your actions, I cannot know unless I hear it from your own mouth."

"That's a pretty embarrassing thing to go asking about."

"Is your true intent something embarrassing? ...That much, even I could not see."

He felt her give a wry chuckle in his arms. Glancing down, his eyes met hers as she looked up at him. Their faces were close as they held each other; the distance to breath on skin could be closed in an instant.

So Subaru decided to answer her question by a method more eloquent than words.

"—Mn."

Lips overlapping, and the feel of a tongue reaching out hesitantly. The heat of the sighs escaping her was unbearably sensual, and he could feel his ears burning at the way she pressed in, begging for the kiss.

After their lips had lingered together for a good while, they parted, neither quite the one to pull away first. Her breath was faintly ragged. Seeing her eyes gone moist — knowing that he was the one making a woman of such habitually resolute bearing wear so lascivious an expression — scorched something deep inside Subaru.

The thought that he alone got to see this face sent heat rushing to the core of his body. He reached out an arm, meaning to seek more of her—

"—This morning, we stop here."

But she blinked the passion out of her eyes, and his outstretched arm was briskly blocked.

With nowhere left to send his sensual excitement, Subaru opened and closed his fingers with lingering regret. Then, when he shot her a resentful look, she wore that familiar, dignified smile.

"If the brakes come off, it will interfere with much of today's schedule. Moreover, your position is no longer something that may be kept to myself alone. So — we stop here."

"...Once in a while it'd be cute if you let yourself drown in it hard enough to forget yourself, y'know?"

"I told you. I am not an endearing woman. —Though forgetting everything and spending a day in idleness with you does have its appeal."

"—That's the dirty trick, y'know. Showing off the feminine side at exactly the right moments."

The hand he stretched out as one last bit of mischief was lightly batted down from above. Shaking out the slapped hand, Subaru watched her turn on her heel with a brisk sweep.

"Come, let us eat. This morning I went all out— not that I have ever cut corners on any meal I serve you, but this morning's is a particular point of pride."

"It's very Crusch-san that the real killer lines come out when you're not aiming for them."

His shoulders sagged, and then Subaru headed for the table, where a smell that stirred his empty stomach was waiting.

She spread her hands over it proudly: behold my handiwork.

"I know you are a busy man, but at least for this one span of time, sharing a table, I would ask you to let me keep you to myself. —Forgive my selfishness, husband."

"If that counts as selfish, then I'd say you're way too cute, my bride."

When he took a seat at the large table, Crusch, as always, sat down immediately beside him. And then she watched out of the corner of her eye as Subaru made the first move toward the food.

And until he'd taken that first bite and said, as ritual demanded, "Delicious!", a faint anxiety would show in the depths of those dignified eyes.

That's really playing dirty, he thought — and then Subaru tossed the first bite speared on his fork into his mouth, and, as ever, found himself saying that it was delicious.

—Under the banner of official duties, Subaru was locked in a losing battle against the documents piled mountain-high on his desk.

"No matter how much I do, there's no sign of an end. You see scenes like this in manga and anime all the time, but turns out they can actually happen in real life..."

"While you were muttering, here's more, Barusu. Stop taking breaks forever and get back to work. Slowpoke."

"Do you say stuff like that fully aware of my position?!"

One of the document mountains was finally nearing completion, leaving only four more sacred peaks — or so he'd thought, until more white ranges were mercilessly added. Once the mountain count had risen by another four or so, Subaru leveled the quill pen in his hand at her.

"You think you can keep looking down on me forever? Do you have any idea how much power I hold in this country now? The days when you get to treat me like your junior are just about over, understand?"

"That's rich coming from Barusu, who seems to have forgotten he can't even stand up properly on his own without borrowing someone's hand. If Ram abandoned Barusu here and left the room, just who would help ghostwrite this mountain of paperwork? Know your place."

"I'm so sorry, nee-sama! Please! Don't abandon me! Help me!"

Retracting his words instantly, Subaru threw his body across the desk and begged, pride discarded.

Watching this, the pink-haired maid snorted, "Hmph."

"Whatever your position, the things that don't change don't change. It's about time this exchange got stale. You should trade up for a better line of patter."

"I bow before nee-sama's magnanimity, seriously. If anybody else saw a scene like this it'd be a disaster, you know? 'I'm Being Blackmailed By A Maid!' — that scandalous headline would be splashed across the front page."

"Ram on top, Barusu underneath. There's no falsehood in that fact. So there's no problem."

"Nee-sama, you're seriously unreal!"

Subaru's voice trembled at her unchanging high-handedness, and Ram exhaled in apparent exasperation. Then she pulled a spare chair up to Subaru's work desk and drew a spare quill pen from the penholder.

"Ram will ghostwrite what can be ghostwritten. She'll only take confirmations, so answer verbally."

"All right, you're always a lifesaver. Without nee-sama this country seriously wouldn't run."

"Show your gratitude through your conduct."

"Specifically?"

"Isn't it common courtesy to attach '-sama' to my name?"

"That's another exchange that'd be a disaster if anyone saw it!"

In any case, unlike Subaru, whose hands stopped whenever he ran his mouth, Ram's speed at clerical work was abnormal. From what he'd heard in passing once, back at Roswaal Manor she'd also worked behind the scenes, clearing away paperwork in Roswaal's stead.

He had no memory of ever witnessing such a scene, so at first it had come as a fair surprise.

"So you weren't just lounging in a chair with your nose in the air all that time, huh."

"I feel I'm being assessed rather dishonorably."

"As if. I'm praising you. As in, you really can't judge a person by their looks, attitude, or behavior."

"Out of spite, Ram will approve this petition from a radical political faction."

"S-T-O-P!"

For someone who'd said she would take confirmations, Ram was processing a good deal at her own discretion. Not that Subaru went out of his way to point it out.

However much he griped out loud, it was also proof of a trust that couldn't be greater — the conviction that there was nothing about her work he would ever need to correct.

He stole a glance at Ram's profile as she faced the documents.

That taut, expressionless face was there as always. The soft features, the looks that retained a certain childishness, were unchanged since the day they'd met. And so was the fact that when she smiled she was as lovely as a flower in bloom — a bloom you were lucky to witness maybe once a year.

"...Your hand has stopped again, Barusu."

"Ah, sorry. I was looking at Ram."

"——Tch."

"You clicked your tongue?! If it were Rem she'd go bright red and make the cutest face!"

"I've told you to stop lining Rem and Ram up for comparison, haven't I. I'll wring it off."

"Wring what?!"

"Your what, I'd say."

Fixed by a freezing sidelong glance, Subaru experienced a distinct whoosh somewhere deep in his body.

He hadn't brought up the name with any malice, but if it had gotten under her skin, that was a failure on his part. Normally she was the one harping on him to prioritize Rem over Ram.

"It's not that I was comparing, but the part about being captivated by you was my honest feeling. Seriously, if you didn't talk like this and didn't make unnecessary movements and nobody knew your personality, you'd be super cute."

"Meaning that once one sees how she speaks, sees how she moves, and comes to know her personality more deeply, she transcends that and becomes downright adorable. —I'll report this to Emilia-sama and the others."

"I wasn't flirting and I didn't say that much!"

Her high opinion of herself and her low opinion of Subaru were as constant as ever. That said, he liked to think that by now he understood that deep down she didn't rate herself very highly at all, and that carrying on so haughtily was a pose.

Which meant that even her unendearing remarks felt forgivable, if you took them as a cover for embarrassment.

"You've got that unpleasant, lukewarm look in your eyes again, Barusu."

"Have I? ...Well, sorry about that."

He pulled his gaze from Ram's profile and returned to his paperwork so as not to incur any more of her anger. Detours were genuinely fun, but if he didn't finish what needed finishing, nothing on the schedule would go right.

Work as work — do it through to the end, and then play.

"Ram and Rem, compared as though they stood in the same place... I'd rather you didn't. Because it makes it sound as though Barusu could look at Ram as the same kind of... object as Rem."

Absorbed in his work, Subaru never noticed at all that Ram, gazing at his profile, had murmured that under her breath.

—Only the sound of quills gliding across white paper went on flowing through the office, as if dancing.

"—I'm bored."

The door was flung open roughly, and at the first words out of the person who entered, Subaru's face went sour.

He dropped his gaze as though to pretend he hadn't seen anything and started to return to inspecting the items lined up on the table in front of him. But—

"Can you not hear my voice? How impertinent."

"Gah?! You — there's no call to go flipping things over!"

In revenge for being ignored, she kicked the table over with all her strength, and everything that had been on it went clattering spectacularly across the floor.

He hurriedly righted the table and gathered up what had fallen. It was fortunate that nothing there was fragile enough to break from the impact, but honestly, it had been terrible for his heart.

Having confirmed the retrieved items were unharmed, he let out a relieved breath, and then Subaru glared at the person who had kicked the table over — a girl in a red dress as loudly self-assertive today as ever. Priscilla.

"Listen, you, I'm swamped with work today too. I keep telling you, send word before you come. Then I could turn you down properly before you ever got here."

"Do not be absurd. When I find myself burdened with idleness depends entirely on my mood, and is known to no one. And yet why should I have to arrange myself around your convenience? Know your place."

"You know your place! Why is it that every self-centered woman around me treats me exactly the same no matter how much my position changes? Am I, like, an honorary title or something?"

"How trivial. How tedious. Your speech and conduct lack elegance even more than before. Behaving like that, you will never walk a kingly road capable of satisfying everyone. —To think you brought us down and usurped the throne, and are in such a sorry state... It is so pathetic it makes me want to quarter you."

"Splatter!"

With a sadistic light kindled in her crimson eyes, Priscilla turned a bored face on the yelling Subaru. A girl as self-serving and severe as ever. Facing her was exhausting beyond measure — and the worst of it was that there was genuine logic at the root of her assertions.

Because there was no denying that the position Subaru now occupied was one he'd obtained by shoving aside the wishes of women like her.

"Still, what shabby taste. Just what were you gazing at to soothe your idleness?"

"I wasn't killing time. This stuff here is a collection of new technologies people have been making lately using magic crystals and whatnot — they're catching on in the towns and countryside. It's pretty interesting, looking at this stuff. Makes you appreciate human ingenuity."

"Oh? New technologies, you say. What sort of effects do they have, for instance?"

"For instance... let's see."

At Priscilla's question, Subaru picked up, from among the items he'd rearranged on the table, something like a palm-sized metal box.

With her eyes narrowed as she demanded an explanation of its effects, Subaru gave the bottom of the metal box a light tap. With a small click, a blue flame lit at the top of the box.

"Hoh. A fire-starter small enough to carry about."

"Back home this thing's called a lighter — or a Chakkaman. If these spread, you'd be able to have a barbecue without using magic, and you could use it as a bit of a light too... maybe? Plus, having one on you makes starting a fire outdoors way easier."

"It also seems likely to produce a dramatic increase in unpleasant sorts who burn buildings down."

"Ngh...!"

Priscilla thrust a hard-nosed opinion at him. Judging by her usual words and behavior it was difficult to tell, but this girl's mind worked considerably better than considerably well.

She stared hard at the Chakkaman in Subaru's hand.

"There is a fire magic crystal inside it, shrunken down. The mechanism at the base agitates it to produce flame... but if there is any flaw in the mechanism, what was meant as a bit of heating could very well burst instead. A magic crystal used repeatedly degrades in both the strength of its effect and its duration. If the replacement frequency proves too high, popular adoption is a long way off yet."

"Gnnngh...!"

The flaws she'd reeled off matched exactly with the list of necessary improvements that had come up from the developers. The discernment to spot all that at a glance — though you could just as well rephrase it as a talent for finding other people's weak points.

"In any case, since you made a show of this one first, I presume it is the piece you are proudest of? If this is your proudest work... one can guess at the level of the rest."

"Sh-shut up, shut up. Listen. The willingness to take on unknown technology — people call that romance. In every age, it's romance that's driven humanity forward. I believe it... I believe that one day this Chakkaman will open a great road for mankind."

Against Priscilla's failure to appreciate romance, Subaru clenched a fist and argued back. Then he tapped the bottom of the Chakkaman again, meaning to show off the gallant figure of that blue flame—

"Huh? Huh?? It's not catching."

"Hm, a flaw already. Romance or whatever it may be, in that state placing your hopes in it is truly comical — indeed!"

Priscilla's bored expression abruptly hardened, her final syllable rising. In the next instant, she drew the fan from her bosom and struck a single flashing blow — the tip of the Chakkaman in Subaru's hand was blasted away by the force, and burst with a bang where it landed.

At the red flash that flared large for just an instant in the corner of the room, Subaru caught his breath.

"The worst of the flaws manifesting at just this moment... the developer ought to be severely punished. He came near to bringing grave harm upon your person, which is more important than anything to this kingdom."

"...I'm not gonna go that far. But — thanks."

If Priscilla hadn't made that split-second judgment and knocked the Chakkaman away, he might have taken a serious burn. Or the metal bursting outward might have done heavy damage to his face.

Subaru patted at the face he'd escaped harm to.

"It'd be a problem if my mug got any worse looking."

"Come now, it is not so bad. Once one grows used to it, there is something to be doted on."

As he slumped down into a chair, Priscilla walked over, saying that. "That so," Subaru answered weakly to what was, unusually, something like consolation from her.

And then, without a shred of hesitation, Priscilla planted herself grandly on Subaru's lap.

"Hey?"

"I told you I was bored, did I not? Besides, thanks to me you avoided a mortal wound this time. In which case, a little unscheduled contact can hardly be helped, hm?"

Priscilla shifted on his lap, pressing herself flush against him, extraordinarily soft. They'd touched each other like this any number of times by now, and still he never got used to her leaning against him. No matter how long it went on, an abnormal heat, as though it were the first time, underpinned her allure.

At the touch of her fingers, an illusion of his throat rapidly drying out. He needed something to wet his lips, right now.
