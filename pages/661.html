Arc 8 Chapter 44: "How to Answer Trust"

Every muscle in his body surged with life, and the fighting spirit welling up sharpened his canines to fine points.
The vitality flowing up from the soles of his feet, planted firmly on the street cracked and peeled back by the aftershocks of battle, filled his soul, and he glared head-on at the imposing sight spreading its wings, straight ahead, high in the sky.
The sound of coursing blood was quick, its pulse loud and high, like a drum rousing his very existence.
In other words—

"—I'm feeling FAN-TAS-TIC!!"

Gnashing his fangs, Garfiel roared and drove forward, shattering the street beneath his charging stride.
Reflecting his own image in the eyes of the imposing sky-borne presence that met him — the white "Cloud Dragon" — Garfiel's blood, flesh, and soul alike could not hold back their cheers.

As one arrow of the rebel army, he had crossed fists with one strong opponent after another in the decisive battle for the imperial capital.
His clashes with a man closing in on the "Nine Divine Generals" and with one of the "Nine Divine Generals" themselves, unmistakably formidable fighters both, had driven home the role Garfiel had set for himself as a member of the camp.
Clashing with the "Generals" of the Vollachian Empire was, for a warrior, a true honor.
But even acknowledging that as precious experience, this — this was what he'd been waiting for.

"A chance ta go toe-ta-toe with a dragon, of all things!"

Any citizen of the dragon kingdom of Lugunica, any life living in this world, knew well enough just how immense and beyond human comprehension a "Dragon" was.
Garfiel was, of course, no exception. He knew a "Dragon" was not something to be measured by the logic of the human world.
And yet Emilia had faced off against a "Dragon" twice now — once at the Pleiades Watchtower, once in the decisive battle for the imperial capital — and had come back alive gloriously both times.

"Dragons? Well, both Volcanica and Mezoreia were suuuper strong. They're huge, and their breath was so dangerous... I was surprised the whole time!"

That was what Emilia had said when she'd told him about the threat of "Dragons."
She'd used gestures and everything to convey just how outrageous a presence a "Dragon" was, but setting aside any issue with her powers of description, it simply hadn't fully come across.
That gap — the part of it Emilia's words couldn't quite convey — he would experience firsthand, right here.

"—!"

The ground erupted as Garfiel closed the distance with ferocious speed, and the "Cloud Dragon" opened its mouth toward him.
Bracing himself for the same breath that had scorched the imperial capital just moments before, Garfiel prepared himself — and Mezoreia unleashed her breath exactly as the buildup foretold.
Except—

"Goh—?!"

Struck by a strange heat and impact unlike either scorching flame or bitter cold, Garfiel let out a pained groan.
It happened the instant after he leapt sideways to dodge Mezoreia's unleashed breath, escaping the line of fire. A second blast of breath came screaming after him, straight at the spot he'd just dodged into.

There was no need to be dazzled wondering what had happened — what his opponent had done was plain.
Rather than exhaling in one continuous blast capable of sweeping away an entire district of the capital in a single breath, she had simply chopped it up and released it in short bursts. Not one long exhale, but two short ones.
No — not two. If she could split one breath in two, she could just as easily do three, four times the same.

"Tch!"

His left arm scorched from the breath, Garfiel used the momentum of his sideways leap to crash straight through the wall of a house lining the street, ducking out of the "Cloud Dragon's" line of sight.
But no stone building could withstand the "Cloud Dragon's" breath. This was nothing like the house of straw — or brick, for that matter — that Subaru had once told him about, the one the wolf's huffing and puffing couldn't blow down.
Struck by the impact of successive fine bursts of breath, the house was stripped of its walls in an instant, its furnishings smashed to pieces, threatening to bring harm down on Garfiel, who had leapt inside—

"OOOOOOOOHHHHH!!"

—should have. But before the breath-struck house could be torn apart, Garfiel's roaring arms ripped the entire building up out of the ground and hurled it, magnificently, into the sky.
Ferociously spinning and scattering debris across the street as it flew — a house-cannonball. Curiously, it was the very same tactic Natsuki Subaru had employed during the retreat from the imperial capital, and now Garfiel wielded it himself. Faced with the mass of projectile launched from his hands bearing down on her, Mezoreia let out an especially large breath.

"In the way, is it!"

Her roar, cute in tone despite her towering, imposing form, blew the house apart before it ever reached the "Cloud Dragon."
But if Mezoreia thought that result bought her a moment's rest, she was sorely mistaken.

"C'mon c'mon c'mon c'mon c'mon c'mon c'mon c'mon!"

Having had his first house-cannonball blocked, Garfiel proceeded to seize the neatly arranged buildings of the imperial capital's streets one after another, hurling them boldly, one after the next, up at the "Cloud Dragon" in the sky.
A combination of Garfiel's raw strength and the ground of the capital having been loosened by repeated shocks — but even so, Mezoreia's golden eyes went wide at this outrageous barrage of mass projectiles.

"Meh-meh-meh-mezoreia is-is-is ANNOYED, is it!!"

Mezoreia beat her wings and met the incoming house-cannonballs head-on.
Her sharp-clawed arms thrashed wildly through the air, blasting apart one house, then two, and her tail, swung from below to above, smashed three buildings at once with a single devastating blow.
And then—

"—Just die already, is it."

"—!"

Using the wreckage of the hurled and shattered houses as footholds, Garfiel had leapt skyward — and catching him dead in her sights, Mezoreia, having seen through his surprise attack, brought both dragon-claws down from left and right.
Caught in a pincer like hands clapping before her chest, Garfiel, airborne, fired his shield-equipped arms forward as if to strike back — catching the dragon's right arm with his left, and the dragon's left arm with his right, respectively.

A tremendous impact ran clean through Garfiel's entire body, his clenched teeth cracked, and blood burst from his nose.
But any human other than Garfiel would have been reduced to a lump of flesh in an instant, without lasting even a single second. Even Mezoreia's whiskers trembled at the result, her surprise plain at the existence of a human who wasn't crushed.
At Mezoreia's astonishment, Garfiel twisted his cheek into a grin and turned it on her,

"How's tha—"

—before he could show his full spirit, the tail dropping straight down from above struck the body pinned between the two arms directly — Garfiel's body was blown backward with force that left sound behind, tumbling again and again and again, reshaping the cityscape with each impact, before finally slamming to a stop against the imperial capital's defensive wall from the inside.

"O-oi!? Oi, kid!?"

Amid the thick, billowing smoke, the man who witnessed the marks left in the street — where Garfiel had bounced, gouged, and shattered the ground — was Heinkel, his voice cracking.
The man, who could only stand there dumbfounded watching the clash between Garfiel and the "Cloud Dragon," dashed over to where Garfiel lay after taking a direct hit with force that would turn a hundred ordinary men into mincemeat.

"Dead... is he dead? He's gotta be dead! Something like that..."

"...Don't go... killin' me off, on yer own..."

Before the pile of rubble, the voice reached Heinkel as he pictured a life snuffed out in vain. Startled, Heinkel's eyes caught the rubble crumbling, revealing Garfiel sprawled there, legs splayed out.
Garfiel leaned his back against the wall, head hanging limp, face hidden. Then, mumbling with his mouth, he spat out a broken fang along with a mouthful of blood.
And then—

"Ahh, damn... dragons really are somethin' else. Now I finally get what Lady Emilia meant, sayin' it all soft like that."

Shaking his head, he slowly rose to his feet.
Feeling with his tongue inside his mouth, he found a tooth broken halfway, a new one trying to grow in around it in some half-formed way — he shoved a finger in and forcibly yanked it out by the root. Right away he could feel the tip of a new tooth poking out from beneath the one he'd pulled, and he gave his twisted neck a loud crack.

"You... you're not hurt at all...?"

"The hell I ain't hurt. Took one hit from a dragon... nah, two, three hits shoved into me. Call it the 'Living Miracle Manfroy,' or somethin' like that."

"――――"

"But I can still go. —Damn glad I'm the kinda me that can."

His whole body ached and groaned, dull and sharp pain both, but Garfiel clenched both fists and, breathing shaky breaths, made that boast anyway.
Here, having taken a direct hit from an attack beyond human comprehension, from a dragon no less, he could still stand.
Being the kind of self who could do that — he was truly glad for it.

"...Something's wrong, something's wrong with you!"

It was Heinkel who cried out, unable to help himself, at Garfiel's muttering.
The man, his wide blue eyes reflecting Garfiel, scratched violently and messily at his own head with his free hand,

"Why'd you come back? It doesn't make any damn sense! It's a dragon... a DRAGON! There's no way to win! Coming here was a fool's errand! And yet!"

"That goes both ways, don't it, old man."

"Ah...?"

"If bein' here, squarin' up against a dragon, makes somebody a fool, then that makes you the same, don't it."

Struck speechless by those words, Heinkel's hand stopped clawing at his hair, and his eyes went wide.
His attitude seemed to say don't lump me in with you, but if that was what he wanted, it was fatal — because in Heinkel's other hand, the one not scratching his head,

"You're still holdin' that sword, ain'tcha."

"――――"

That it hadn't been thrown away, Garfiel believed, was proof of some last shred of hope in Heinkel.
Maybe his fingers had just locked up from fear, unable to let go. —No. He crushed that thought.
Maybe he'd forgotten himself and was just holding it without meaning to. —No. He crushed that thought too.
Maybe, in this very moment, he was about to sheathe it and run. —No. He crushed that thought as well.

He crushed every weak thought, every faltering will, every reason to lose his nerve.
Because he believed that crushing every last one of them, all the way through, was what forged the face of a man who gritted his teeth and stood up to face his hopes head-on — that was why he crushed them.

"The boss, and Otto-nii, they're both like that. I wanna be like that too."

"I... I'm..."

Trembling, Heinkel looked down at his own hand shaking around the sword's hilt, visibly rattled. Garfiel, glancing sideways at him, straightened his posture with a thud of his back against the wall.
And then, to Heinkel who still hadn't recovered, he spoke.

"Ya asked me why I came back, right."

"—Ah?"

At Garfiel's quiet tone, Heinkel's eyes widened.
To Heinkel, waiting for the rest of the words, what Garfiel offered wasn't a continuation of the sentence — it was a hand shoving him square in the chest.

Heinkel was sent flying, toppling backward dramatically into the street.
The next instant, breath unleashed from the "Cloud Dragon" high above the center of the city came bearing down on Garfiel, who stood with his back to the wall — and Garfiel dodged it by leaping straight up.

"O-ohhh...!"

Garfiel, having leapt straight up with his back to the wall, was pursued by the breath, which looked like a white beam of heat, tilting its line of fire upward to follow. The streets were scorched, the wall reduced to dust, as the breath chased Garfiel — who had fled skyward — up, and up, and up.
Just as it was about to catch up to the tips of his toes, before it could scorch his entire body—

"The boss told me."

In the midst of the dragon's breath, which threatened to burn the whole world to ash, grazing his very toes, there was not a trace of impatience in his voice. What was there instead was trust, and the pride of a man wanting to answer it.
Curling himself into a ball mid-air, Garfiel's soles touched a wall that still retained its original shape. —The next instant, he kicked off it with leg strength that sent cracks radiating outward across it, and flew forward.
Forward, forward, forward, toward the "Cloud Dragon" soaring through the imperial capital's sky, his newly regrown fangs sharp, mouth open wide, drinking in the wind as he closed the distance—

"—He told ME! To go bring down the dragon flyin' around up there struttin' its stuff!!"

"—?!"

Flying straight above the breath, closing in on the "Cloud Dragon," Garfiel drew back both arms and fired them forward simultaneously, and destructive force that put cannon fire to shame caught Mezoreia square on the snout.
A boom rang out across the sky like a warehouse packed with magic stones exploding, and the "Cloud Dragon," struck head-on by the blow, reeled backward — and in the next instant, sprayed crimson blood.
From her shattered snout, the dragon spilled blood from her nose, and Garfiel, slammed back down to the ground by the recoil of his own punch, was drenched in it in spectacular fashion. As he was drenched, Garfiel laughed.

"Gahahahaha! Made a dragon bleed from the nose, I did!"

"Y-youuuu!!"

Mezoreia flew into a rage at the unthinkable humiliation, and at the sight of it, Garfiel laughed all the harder.
Watching from a distance, Heinkel — pushed away and saved from the breath — sat sprawled on his rear on the ground, staring on in blank, dumbfounded amazement,

"...He's out of his mind."

Heinkel murmured that weakly, and at almost the very same moment, a shift in the air equal to the battlefield Garfiel had created facing off against the dragon arose all across the imperial capital.
Before that sight — the dragon and tiger clashing, utterly indifferent to anything else — Heinkel could not move.
Unable to flee, unable to stand, he simply could not move.

—And yet, his right hand kept holding onto the sword all the while.

—Time now rewinds slightly, to just before the battlefield of dragon and tiger clashing came to be.

"――――"

The sensation of cold water striking his cheeks, his forehead, snapped Subaru's consciousness fully awake.
It wasn't that he'd been drowsy, nor that he'd felt any heaviness in his body. And yet there was a sense of something clicking into place inside him, and he let out a deep breath.
Before him, in the basin of water rippling, he saw his own face, warped by the waves. A face that retained some childishness — or rather, could only be called childish, with a nasty look in the eyes.
He'd long since gotten used to it, but this was not a face he should think was fine as it was. As he poked and prodded at it with his fingers—

"Subaru, are you okay? Is something wrong, maybe?"

Someone spoke to him suddenly from beside him, and Subaru lifted his face from the basin. In front of his raised face was Emilia, peering at him with those violet eyes as beautiful as jewels.
Her face closer than he'd expected, Subaru's heart raced even as he answered,

"I-I'm fine and dandy, Emilia-tan. Just loosening up my cheek muscles a little. See, I always wanna show Emilia-tan my very best smile."

"I mean, as long as it's not a smile you're forcing, I'd be happy no matter what kind of smiling face you show me, Subaru..."

"Ohh, that's a lotta heart-fluttering... whoa, whoa."

Finger to her lips, Subaru was thrown off by Emilia's utterly guileless words. Trying to wipe his wet face with his sleeve to hide his warming cheeks, he found a hand towel offered to him from the side — no, two hand towels, from both sides. Beatrice and Tanza.
The two of them, originally wide-eyed, went even wider in surprise at having done the same thing, but quickly recognized each other and narrowed their eyes,

"Deer girl, Subaru desires Betty's towel, I suppose. Withdraw that one."

"Schwartz-sama hasn't said any such thing. Isn't it rather presumptuous to speak your own thoughts as though they were Schwartz-sama's opinion?"

"Wha-wha-wha-what an infuriating girl, I suppose!"

"Wait wait wait wait, why's this turning into a fight! Look, I'll use both! I'll wipe the right side of my face with one towel and the left with the other!"

Stepping into the spat that had erupted out of nowhere, Subaru tried to use both towels to smooth things over. But the two of them just sighed at his response. It hadn't smoothed anything over.
At Subaru's shoulders slumping, Emilia said "Honestly," hands on her hips,

"Beatrice, Tanza-chan, don't go giving Subaru a hard time. He's already worn out from everything that's been happening, one thing after another..."

"Ohh, Emilia-tan's kindness really sinks in... though I'm not saying you two aren't being kind to me either! Really, you two always help me out so much... the more I say it the more it sounds like an excuse!?"

"Well, well, I suppose," said Beatrice.
"It's simply how Schwartz-sama is," said Tanza.

At Subaru clutching his head, Beatrice and Tanza reluctantly softened their attitudes.
Subaru couldn't help but tilt his head at having become the villain of the piece without meaning to, but since Emilia patted his head gently, pat pat, he decided to let it go.
In any case—

"But really, I'm not just putting on a brave face, I'm genuinely fine, Emilia-tan. I properly rested at the fort until departure and everything... after all, if I carelessly keeled over from lack of sleep, the whole Pleiades Battalion would fall apart."

The Pleiades Battalion, bound together by strong ties — its strengths and weaknesses were both plain to see.
United as one, the battalion was an unstoppable, gung-ho group — but what held it together was Subaru's presence — not to brag, but his "Cor Leonis."
Its effect, too, would cut off abruptly the moment Subaru fell asleep or passed out, so from here on, even a careless nap wasn't permitted.

"I know that, so it's fine. Trust me, Emilia-tan."

"Really? Well, if you say so, but can you really promise me you won't overdo it?"

"You really don't trust me at all..."

"Think hard about why that might be, with your own conscience."

Trust was built up from a stack of past actions. With Emilia implying as much without saying it outright, Subaru had nothing to say back.
Still, it was true that Subaru's mind and body were, to his own surprise, in good shape. Likely because excessive tension was keying up his spirit, so once this whole battle was over and done with, the debt would probably come due all at once and knock him flat.
But if borrowing against future energy could get him through this, he was prepared to accept that debt afterward without complaint.

—At present, the "Corps to Save the Vollachian Empire from Ruin," having set out from the fortress city of Garkla and bound for the imperial capital of Lupugana, was in the midst of swapping out dragon carriages with their destination in sight.
Along the route from Garkla to Lupugana, they would stop at one of the scattered camps, where Abel and the other strategists confirmed the state of the war while the earth dragons were exchanged for fully fit ones, in preparation for the decisive battle.

"Of course, Patrasche's staying with us the whole way."

Reaching out, Subaru stroked the neck of his beloved jet-black dragon, tethered to the carriage — Patrasche.
Having safely rejoined them in the empire, Patrasche had not changed her treatment of Subaru one bit despite his shrunken state, and had delivered a mighty blow of her tail as retribution for the worry he'd caused.
The blow that sent him crashing spectacularly into a wall had been reminiscent of the damage he'd taken from his clash with Guiltilaw on the Sparka, the Isle of Sword-Slaves, but if one blow was all it took, that was a bargain.
Since then, she had accepted Subaru's abject apologies and was now, in this way, going along with him at full effort.

"Heeh, so this little earth dragon here, she's your kid, huh."

And it was Halibel who came over just as Subaru and Patrasche were roughhousing.
The tall Wolf Human Shinobi rocked the long pipe held in her big mouth up and down with her lips,

"When I was tanglin' with that dragon zombie, she was doin' real fine work, real proud of her, I was."

"Oh! Halibel-san, you get it too, huh, how great my Patrasche is!"

"Ya know it. You're a lucky one. —Ana-bo and this here kid and everybody, all worryin' over you."

Halibel, renowned as the strongest in the city-state, and Subaru — about to burst into unreserved delight at her seal of approval — widened his eyes at the words that followed, then nodded seriously.
He hadn't needed to be told, but hearing it said made him think it anew. —He was blessed.
Not only had Emilia and the others, Julius and Anastasia, broken through all manner of adverse conditions to enter the empire with him, they were sticking with him through all his selfish demands, right to the end.
Just how reliable and reassuring that was, and how guilty it made him feel.

"That's exactly why I've gotta do everything I possibly can, too."

"Subaru? Do you remember the promise you just made with me?"

"I remember, I remember, no reckless stuff! But if it's absolutely necessary...?"

"See, there you go, breaking it again already."

Pouting her lips, sulking at him like that, Emilia made Subaru scratch his cheek.
At the sight of it, Halibel chuckled low in her throat, thoroughly amused. With what awaited them possibly a battle for the fate of the empire — or maybe even the world — she was remarkably composed.

"Halibel-dono, that's soo-omethin' you probably don't want Subaru-kun sayin' to you either, isn't it now."

"There he is, I suppose."

"Well, seein' as we're together, I suppose I would show up now and then, wouldn't I. I'd like it if, sooner or later, people in the empire would recognize me as fully on their side, if you'd beee so kind."

Shrugging with a "well, well," Roswaal wryly smiled at Beatrice's attitude.
As he showed his face at the edge of the camp where the dragon carriage had stopped, Emilia tilted her head at him and asked, "Is that all right?"
Presumably, Roswaal, as the current strategic advisor of the Emilia camp, was supposed to be accompanying Abel, who was speaking with the officials in charge of the camp.

"I do think of Roswaal as properly on our side, but I suppose that means Abel and the others don't feel the same way...?"

"If so, then I'm troubled to find I have no place in either the kingdom or the empire, wooould I say. —But worry not, over there, His Excellency Vincent has entered the phase of addressing the troops himself and raising their morale. If anything, I'm merely in the waaay."

"I see. Well, that's good then. And Roswaal, your place is properly right here with us, so don't worry. As long as you don't go scheming anything bad."

Her lips curling into a smile, Emilia gave an angelic answer to Roswaal's self-deprecation.
Though Roswaal only gave a wry smile in response, saying nothing whatsoever about whether or not he was scheming anything — which was rather sacrilegious of him.

"Still, it ticks me off that Abel's grand return seems to be having exactly the effect he wanted."

As he said this, Subaru glanced toward the center of the camp, from where the cheers of a great many imperial soldiers could be heard.
It wasn't a battle cry to raise morale before heading into combat, but a cheer of overwhelmed emotion — one of joy at being addressed directly by the emperor they served.
Obvious as it was, Abel's sullen face — which Subaru had seen so much of he was sick of it — was something most citizens of the empire were never permitted to see up close.

"When I think about how I'd feel if I were just an ordinary citizen who only knew Emilia-tan's face and name... I kind of get it... ah, no, thinking about it like that, I might cry."

"What's wrong, Subaru, aren't you my knight? Pull yourself together!"

"Right! It's not a dream! Whew, that was close, thanks to Abel."

Slipping in a bit of idle chatter that would probably have made Abel scowl if he'd heard it, Subaru nonetheless welcomed the fact that the emperor's calculated gambit was having its intended effect.
Still, he couldn't wholeheartedly praise it when he thought about how Medium must feel, being put on display as the bride-to-be after the war's end, purely to boost the soldiers' morale.

"I just hope Spica, who's been glued to her side, is managing to distract Medium-san even a little bit... that bastard, what does he even think marriage is."

"Yeah, really. I think Abel's suuuper smart, but I think that particular thing about him is not so great."

"That's exactly why he keeps gettin' kicked off the throne, doin' stuff like this."

Nodding along together, Subaru and Emilia found themselves in perfect agreement on Abel's shortcomings. At the sight of the two of them, Halibel chuckled once again,

"Ain't scared of nothin', are ya, goin' up against the Vollachian emperor like that."

"No matter who it is, as long as Betty's with him, Subaru has no reason to be afraid, I suppose."

"Given that he refuses Yorna-sama, it's common knowledge that His Excellency the Emperor is heartless."

"That's a conversation I'd raaather not have the people of the empire overhear."

In a camp swept up in excitement over the emperor's grand return, badmouthing the emperor was bound to end in bloodshed — Roswaal gave a wry smile amid the scathing opinions being lobbed at Abel. Wry smile still on his face, Roswaal then said "So," and closed one eye,

"For Garfiel to stay out of sight this whole time we've been talking, he must be concentrating awfully hard. —Seems he's fired up over Subaru-kun's big appointment for him, dooo he?"

"—Yeah."

Roswaal turned his still-open yellow eye toward him, and Subaru nodded at his words.
Garfiel, who hadn't joined in on the group's conversation here, was currently inside the dragon carriage tethered to Patrasche, quietly meditating to sharpen his focus.
It was proof that he was giving everything he had to answer Subaru's expectations. —The expectation Subaru had placed on him, asking him to go toe-to-toe with the "Cloud Dragon" Mezoreia.

"With her mobility and destructive power off the charts, I want Mezoreia's movement locked down somehow. Right now, Garfiel's the one I can trust most with that job."

"Mind if I ask why? Ana-bo did tell me to follow you folks' instructions as much as I can, but... I'm stronger than that tiger cub, aren't I?"

At Subaru's decision, Halibel tilted her head, puffing on her pipe.
If she felt this was undervaluing her own strength and was taking offense, some explanation would be needed — but Halibel's tone was entirely flat.
To her, voicing a purely tactical question, Subaru naturally lowered his chin as well,

"I'm not doubting you're the strongest of the people we've got right now, Halibel-san. It's just, the right tool for the right job, so to speak... there's someone else I want you to hold back instead."

"Now that's a scary thing to hear. Puttin' the dragon off to the side and sayin' there's somethin' else... you tellin' me there's somethin' out there more trouble than a dragon?"

"In a way, yeah."

There was no point beating around the bush, so Subaru affirmed it plainly.
At Subaru's flat declaration, Halibel fell silent for a moment. But then she quickly twisted her wide Wolf Human mouth into a grin,

"That so, that so. Guess I'll have to get fired up too, then."

And with that, she showed her willingness to accept and follow Subaru's instructions.

"Garfiel and Halibel-san... and there's a place you want us to go, and something you want us to do, too, right?"

Once the exchange between Subaru and Halibel settled, Emilia, her expression serious, looked at Subaru. She touched the magic crystal at her chest with a finger, her violet eyes piercing through him.
Not just Emilia. Beatrice and Tanza too, and Roswaal as well, were all looking his way.

"That's right. Everyone here... including Abel and the others over there — it's an all-out effort."

"—. Fortunately, Subaru knows roughly where the enemy is, I suppose."

"Right. That special power, the one you used at the Pleiades Watchtower too — the Authority, wasn't it."

"Yeah. Thanks to that."

At Emilia and Beatrice's words, Subaru struck his own chest with a fist.
The achievement of "Cor Leonis" demonstrated at the Pleiades Watchtower — grasping the whereabouts of someone far away, and using it to handle the problems within the tower — was what made Emilia and the others trust Subaru now.
It was the "lie" that let Subaru know, in order to counter the powerful enemies waiting at the imperial capital of Lupugana, exactly who should be sent where for the best possible outcome.

"—I'll do what I can do."

Emilia, Beatrice, Spica, Tanza, Garfiel, Roswaal, Halibel, Abel, Medium, Jamal, Olbart — he assigned them all.
Using every card in his hand, to win the fight for the imperial capital—

"Of course, I'm counting on you too, Patrasche."

Reaching out, Subaru stroked her neck once more, and Patrasche whinnied in response.
Amid everyone's spirits rising at that one call from Patrasche—

"—Subaru-kun, this is really the best plan, isn't it?"

Roswaal, his gaze fixed unwaveringly on Subaru's face.
At Roswaal's question, Subaru nodded deeply and answered.

"Yeah, this has gotta be the best way. —I made sure of it properly."

This was simply Natsuki Subaru doing what Natsuki Subaru could do.
So it shouldn't count as breaking his promise to Emilia — the promise not to do anything reckless.
