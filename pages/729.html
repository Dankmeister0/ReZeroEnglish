Arc 9 Chapter 33: "A Girl Named Petra Leyte"

"—Possibility, as the price."

Petra murmured the words to herself, testing them on her tongue, and knit her finely shaped brows.

Possibility — that was the thing that had to be exchanged in order to use "Teleport," the mysterious "power" Clind possessed. She'd imagined some more straightforward price: an enormous quantity of Mana, say, or some rare magic crystal — but—

"The price is possibility? What in the world's that supposed to mean?"

Imaginary Subaru cocked his head in exaggerated puzzlement, echoing the very question in Petra's own mind. Taking full advantage of being an insubstantial phantom, he spun himself sideways through the air over and over, putting his whole self into broadcasting just how unconvinced he was by Clind's explanation.

Of course, that adorable display was visible to no one but Petra.

"Wait, hold on — did this guy just say the 'Melancholy' Witch Factor? He said that, right? A Witch Factor — that's one of those keywords Echidna mentioned that sounded like nothing good could come of it!"

"...I don't think very highly of that 'Witch of Greed,' so I'm not sure how much I should trust what she says."

"Yeah, well, she's the type who's easy to misunderstand, that's for sure. It's not like I trust her either, but if you think of it as her just being bad at dealing with people, it kind of changes how you look at her a little. Though she's definitely got a nasty streak. A nasty, nasty streak."

"Right. With my handkerchief, she... never mind."

Petra lowered her eyes at Imaginary Subaru, whose voice trembled as he fixated on something other than the price at hand.

The scene of despair she'd witnessed in the Book of the Dead, and the method the Witch had prepared to end it — the one Subaru had chosen — were not things Petra ever wanted to speak of. Nor did she want to look at them straight on. But they weren't something she could look away from, either. They were, in short, troublesome.

In any case—

"So a Witch Factooor means the majordomo guy was an Archbishop of Sin toooo?"

It was Meili — guileless, and in her own way oblivious to the mood in the room — who cut straight into that awkward subject.

It was a term Petra wouldn't have recognized before reading the Book of the Dead, but Meili apparently had had some occasion, somewhere, to learn about Witch Factors. The relationship between Witch Factors and the Witches — and by extension, the Archbishops of Sin — was something Petra had thought she needed to press for answers on herself.

Honestly—

"If this Clind guy turns out to be an Archbishop of Sin just like Petelgeuse, I gotta wonder how much of that nice, friendly chat we just had we can even trust..."

"Says the Subaru who forgave Spica-chan, who was the Archbishop of Sin of 'Gluttony'?"

"What are you talking about!? There's no way I'd forgive 'Gluttony' after what it did to Rem!?"

"...Maybe you should spend a while catching up on my more recent memories."

The same was true of his relationship with Beatrice — the Subaru from the timeline of the Book of the Dead that Petra had witnessed had a huge gap in knowledge and relationships compared to the present Subaru. Thinking back, she recalled Subaru occasionally speaking ill of Echidna, so she guessed that, somewhere beyond her own knowledge, all sorts of things must have happened between Subaru and Echidna.

Without a doubt, that woman was bad news, so Petra's instincts had been right all along. —And while that mental conference went on in the background, Petra kept a wary eye on Clind alongside Meili.

She believed Clind wasn't a bad person, and she knew of the precedent set by Spica, who'd once been an Archbishop of Sin — but trusting him unconditionally, the way Emilia would, was another matter entirely. Emilia's guilelessness was adorable and charming, so Petra hoped she'd keep growing up just the way she was, but still.

"Meili's suspicion is only natural, but I am not an Archbishop of Sin. Negation. To begin with, the 'Melancholy' Witch Factor is an exceptional thing. Unlisted. Even one compatible with this factor would not be admitted among the Witches or the Archbishops of Sin. Shunned."

"—————"

"The previous holder... the one compatible with the 'Melancholy' Witch Factor, called himself the 'Warlock of Melancholy.' Self-proclaimed... no, it was a name given by another. That person paid no mind to what he was called — it was simply that his wife thought the name suited him, and so that was what she called him. Term of endearment."

"Warlock..."

"A Warlock instead of a Witch, huh! Makes sense. I was worried he'd come up with something like 'Sorcerer-man' next."

It apparently struck a chord with him, and Imaginary Subaru snapped his fingers in admiration. Petra, meanwhile, murmured the word to herself and looked back at Clind.

If that same explanation applied to Clind as well, then—

"So then, does that make Clind-niisama the 'Warlock of Melancholy' too?"

"No. Negation. I am not compatible with the 'Melancholy' Witch Factor. That is precisely why I require a price to draw out the Authority of 'Melancholy.' Incompatible. Ordinarily, one compatible with a Witch Factor requires no price to exercise its Authority. It is a right to rewrite the world, sanctioned by Od Lagna. Official."

"A right to rewrite the worrrld. I keep hearing that, but I do wish this Od Lagna person would be a liiittle more choosy about who they hand it to."

"Seconded."

As Meili pouted, lips pursed in dissatisfaction, Imaginary Subaru reached out to pat her head with a hand that couldn't actually touch her, while Petra pressed a finger to her lips and sorted through the information.

She wanted to believe Clind's denial that he was an Archbishop of Sin, or a "Warlock." And she'd taken in the rule that the special power a Witch Factor apparently granted — something a compatible person could use freely — required a price from an incompatible one trying to use it.

If that was the payment required to use Clind's power — not truly "Teleport," but rather "Compression" —

"For example, if someone else — if I could use the Witch Factor Clind-niisama has, would that mean I could use 'Teleport' as much as I wanted without paying any price?"

"Petra! That is absolutely a terrible idea, don't even think about it!"

"Why not? Subaru, you get it too, don't you? ...Subaru's 'Return by Death' is definitely the same kind of thing as the Witch Factor Clind-niisama's talking about."

"That's..."

Something about it rang true, and Imaginary Subaru fell silent, his face bitter.

At the tea party with Echidna, Subaru had learned the fact that he'd absorbed a Witch Factor — at the time, the story was that it belonged to the Archbishop of Sin of "Sloth," whom he'd just defeated, but factoring in things like the reason the "Witch of Envy" was so fixated on Subaru, that probably wasn't the whole story.

"Return by Death" was clearly no Magic, curse art, or technique — it was an unmistakably alien power. In other words, there was a strong chance it was an Authority. And if that were the case, then Natsuki Subaru was also a "Warlock."

"Then it's fine if I become a 'Witch.' It's not scary as long as I'm with Subaru."

"...You might be jumping to conclusions, y'know. My 'Return by Death' could have nothing to do with any Authority — it could just be, like, one of those 'gift from a god who hasn't even shown their face yet' deals that come standard with these other-world stories."

"Subaru, you keep a lot of secrets, but you're really bad at lying."

That was probably why he kept things hidden instead — to avoid a real conversation about it, so he wouldn't slip up.

Cherishing the gentleness of the boy she loved even in this, Petra closed her eyes, then turned back to look at Clind once more. Clind's eyes widened faintly, his expression seeming struck by the strength of her gaze and her resolve, but—

"I regret to say that method cannot be adopted. Rejected."

"—! Why not!"

"First, it is impossible for Petra to be compatible with the 'Melancholy' Witch Factor. And second — the 'Vanity' factor is unlisted... no, I should say it is outside the numbering. It is an exceptional factor, tuned for one compatible person each — the Valiant and the Saintess. Custom-made. It will not be compatible with anyone else. And moreover—"

"...Moreover?"

"Even those who were compatible could not escape the corruption of the spirit. Remorse."

Clind lowered his eyes, lips pressed into a tight line. From that reaction, Petra sensed that he had known those two compatible individuals — the Valiant and the Saintess. More than that: the sole person compatible with the "Melancholy" Witch Factor, the one who had fallen into spiritual corruption — the "Warlock of Melancholy" — was in fact that very Valiant.

And the fact that Clind now carried that "Warlock's" Witch Factor meant that the Valiant was no longer anywhere in this world—

"Okaaay, okaaay, I get that you're having a reeeally important talk, but now that Petra-chan's dicey little resolution's been shot down, can we get back to the maiin topic?"

"Hmph, what do you mean, 'dicey'..."

"It waaas dicey. Even if a Witch Factor of all things took a liking to Petra-chan, I'd be dead-set against it. You shouldn't rely on a power like thaaat."

"Meili-chan..."

There was no malice in it; Petra could tell the words came from genuine worry for her.

Meili was someone who knew about Witch Factors without ever having attended a Witches' tea party. Judging by the way she'd just spoken, she might well have had some encounter with a being who carried a Witch Factor — an Archbishop of Sin. And yet, her advice was, in its way, ironic.

"—————"

Imaginary Subaru gazed at Meili with his brows lowered, his expression too gentle to be called merely complicated.

Given the incident surrounding the Sanctuary and Roswaal Manor, and the fact that Meili had been captured amid that turmoil, she had had a hand in that matter — the deaths of Petra and Frederica that Petra had witnessed in the Book of the Dead.

The only reason Petra and Meili's relationship hadn't ended simply as victim and perpetrator of death was, of all things, thanks to Subaru's "Return by Death."

So Petra couldn't help but think that it all came down to how a power was used — but—

"...Okay, got it."

"Good giiirl."

Meili's relief at Petra's answer was plain to see. Petra took her hand and nodded.

Either way, if the "Melancholy" Witch Factor couldn't be compatible with Petra, then the plan of becoming its holder in Clind's place, to bypass the need for a price, was off the table.

"If Subaru's a 'Warlock,' then I'll be a 'Witch' too" was simply an appealing scenario on its own terms — but if there was no real benefit to it, it was an option that should be scrapped.

"As Meili says, let us return to the main topic. Resumption. In any case, as I have explained, payment of a price will be necessary. And the price is influence exerted upon the world. Possibility."

"—. Could you explain that a little more, please."

"For example, there exists a Magic that produces fire with a single incantation. Its usefulness needs no explanation; through this Magic, many futures are opened up. Promising. Cooking, medicine, metalworking, smithing, war... the list is endless. Immense. This is influence, and this is possibility. And—"

"...If someone offered up that Magic as a price, would everything that was supposed to happen because of it just disappear?"

"That much possibility, which the world was originally meant to use, remains as unused room. And by exactly that much room, that margin, one who is not compatible can exercise the Authority of 'Melancholy.' Complete."

From the way he brought it to a close, Petra understood that Clind had finished explaining what was necessary.

She understood now. —In short, it was a matter of resources. The resources Od Lagna managed, for running the world, had a limit.

Ordinarily, that resource was used right up to its limit — but by offering something as a price, a surplus could be created, and using that surplus in secret was the condition for using Clind's Authority.

Having understood that much, clever, lovely Petra realized one more thing.

"...When we went to the Empire, we had Clind-niisama use his Authority a lot, didn't we. What was the price for that?"

"—. The Master paid it. Secret."

"...I really, really hate that."

Irritation and resentment made Petra's stomach twist and boil.

In that situation, everyone in the camp had wrung out every last ounce of effort to go save Subaru and the others. Beatrice had kept sleeping, believing in her reunion with Subaru; Otto had even bent his own principles to rely on his family. Emilia had desperately charted that course for them all, and of course Petra believed she herself had done everything she possibly could, too.

And yet, that the one who had paid the greatest cost of all was Roswaal — who wouldn't even try to speak of it — was an outrageous humiliation to Petra.

"I dunno, maybe it's some kind of atonement thing? Like a 'sorry about the Sanctuary and the manor' sort of deal?"

"I don't think it's a feeling that simple. That's why I don't like the Master...!"

"Hmm, Petra's anger is only fair..."

Whether it was because he hadn't yet gotten through the Empire portion of her memories or not, it was frustrating that Imaginary Subaru couldn't quite match her on the emotional level — though she didn't particularly want him to understand this ugly, murky jealousy of hers, either, so Petra threw that murk down into the well of her heart, sealed the lid, and hammered it shut so she'd never be able to peer inside again.

Her accumulated grievances against Roswaal were nothing new at this point. And it made sense, too, that Roswaal would be a fitting candidate for meeting the conditions to use Clind's Authority.

"If it's the Master, he has plenty of things he could offer as a price — Magic, Magicreation Tools, and so on."

Of course, that didn't mean that having a lot of something meant none of it was too precious to part with.

Whether Od Lagna factored the depth of one's attachment into its evaluation, Petra didn't know, but it was a "possibility" that, if not given up, would have gone on affecting the world. Whatever it was, Petra didn't believe the world could be moved by something one held no attachment to.

—"Possibility" required passion, and attachment.

To offer up something without those things, in some cheap, easy scheme for salvation, and think you could win back something truly precious that way — such a shabby, unworthy logic could never hold.

So Petra—

"—I'd be fiiine offering up something myself."

"Whoa, hold on."

Meili murmured that quietly — perhaps she'd arrived at the same idea as Petra. As Imaginary Subaru blinked in surprise, Meili went on, "I mean, weeell—"

"The fact that I'm even aliiive, when you trace it back, is thanks to you guys letting me off the hook. I'm not saying it'd be paying back a debt or anything, but isn't it a pretty straightforward wayyy to look at it?"

"Petra, stop this girl — stop Meili! I don't know what happened between her and the me of right now, but I sure as hell didn't help her out with anything like that in mind!"

"Yeah, I know."

Imaginary Subaru's argument, made with his face gone pale, was nonetheless one Petra accepted without any trouble.

Subaru must have saved Meili not out of some calculated notion of making her push herself or pay him back someday, but simply because he'd wanted to save her.

Subaru surely wouldn't want Meili to do anything that would shut that possibility of hers away.

"Meili-chan, no. The most valuable thing you could offer up would be your Divine Protection for controlling Witchbeasts, but if that were gone, you'd be worthless, Meili-chan."

"My, myyy! Petra-chan, that's so meean of you."

"Sorry. 'Worthless' was too much. You'd just end up being nothing but cute, Meili-chan."

Honestly, Subaru and Emilia and the others would probably say that would be fine either way. But Meili herself, of all people, couldn't accept becoming just a cute girl.

Petra understood that all too painfully well. —Cute girls couldn't simply be cute girls, not to the extent that everyone around them assumed.

"—Petra-sama, Meili-sama."

A voice reached them, catching them somewhat off guard, and Petra and the others spun around toward the Dragon Carriage. There stood a girl who had leapt down from the carriage bed and was running toward them — Flam.

She had woken from the state of self-induced unconsciousness she'd fallen into while crossing the Augria Sand Dunes. The circumstances behind Flam's loss of consciousness were tied to her Divine Protection—

"Flam-chan! I'm so glad you woke u—"

"The conditions for using my 'Telepathy Divine Protection' have been met. I can reach my sister... Grassis. I believe I should also be able to exchange word with Felt-sama and Rachins-sama and the others."

"—————"

Flam, prioritizing this over confirming everyone's safety, quickly relayed her own situation.

The moment she'd woken, she must have wanted desperately to contact her family about the clash between Reinhard and the "Witch of Envy," and yet she'd held that back — Petra could feel nothing but gratitude for it.

Without a doubt, once the Felt Camp received word from Flam, they too would be in a position to act toward resolving things, now armed with information about the outrage Al had committed.

"With Reinhard unable to move, I feel like it's anyone's guess how much we can actually rely on Felt or old man Rom, though..."

"—. No. We can't afford to be stubborn about doing this all ourselves."

"Petra..."

"This isn't just a problem for us anymore. It belongs to everyone in the Kingdom."

Al had run amok, and Natsuki Subaru and Beatrice had been taken — this was no longer some small, localized matter. It had to be seen as a situation that had spiraled into something on the scale of the nation, the scale of the world, with the Sword Saint, the Divine Dragon, and the Witch of Envy all tangled up in it.

Accordingly, Petra and the others — who understood the circumstances best of all — must not hold back on sharing information.

"Flam-chan, find out where Felt-sama and the others are right away. As long as we know the location, we can join up with them with Clind-niisama here."

"—! Understood."

At Petra's crisp instructions, Flam glanced at Clind for only a moment, holding back her urge to ask for the full details, and agreed at once.

It was Meili who tugged at her sleeve instead, saying, "Petra-chan?"

"We still haven't settled the matter of the price for the majordomo guyyy."

"—No, that's already decided."

"Huh?"

Meili's eyes went wide at that answer. Petra gently loosened the fingers gripping her sleeve, stepped briskly over to Clind, and glanced sidelong at Imaginary Subaru.

Even knowing full well that he was only a convenient phantom of her own making, staring intently at what she was doing—

"Cover your ears for just a second."

At Petra's request, Imaginary Subaru paused a beat, then obediently complied. Once Imaginary Subaru had covered his ears, Petra rose up on her toes, saying, "Clind-niisama," and brought her mouth close to his ear, whispering something to him softly.

It was—

"—————"

"—would that work as a price?"

Petra lowered her heels back to the ground; at her hushed question, Clind's eyes went wide, frozen in place. Petra tilted her head — even by her own standards, an ultimately adorable gesture — and at the sight of it, Clind let out a breath.

With a long, deep sigh, he removed his monocle and looked back at Petra with his bare face.

"It should be possible. Estimate. But Petra, that—"

"I'm so glad."

"—————"

"I already knew, though. —That I have a 'possibility' in me that can move the world!"

Puffing out her chest proudly, Petra didn't let Clind say another word.

The question she'd let only Clind hear had gotten its answer. And whatever that answer meant, Petra was thoroughly pleased with herself over it — Clind was Clind, after all. There was no guarantee his judgment would match Od Lagna's, but at the very least, Clind had acknowledged the value of that "possibility."

"I'll offer up the price, Clind-niisama. So we can save the world... no, honestly, the world doesn't really matter to me. I just want to save the people who matter to me."

"—Understood, Petra. Of all the girls I have ever known, you are the most courageous. Admiration."

Bowing deeply, he said it the way he might to Annerose or Roswaal — or the way he would to anyone he truly, wholeheartedly believed to be splendid.

Smiling back at him, Petra noticed Meili's gaze fixed on her. Seeing something uneasy in her round eyes, Petra gently pulled her into an embrace.

"I dunno, but I bet Petra-chan is a huuge idiot."

"Fufu, how mean. I'm smart, and I'm cute and cool, you know."

Petra shot back at Meili's unflattering remark like that, and over the shoulder of the girl she held, she gazed at Imaginary Subaru, standing there looking rather lost.

At last, Imaginary Subaru lowered his hands from his ears, and gazing at that face of his — one whose dearness never ran dry no matter how long she looked at it — Petra, updating her own personal best for cuteness, declared:

"Subaru, I love you."
