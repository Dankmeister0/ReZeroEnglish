Arc 5 Chapter 39: "Knightliness and the Man Who Came Too Late"

Having renewed their resolve and finished their discussion, Subaru headed with Anastasia down to the lower floors of the city hall. The large room where Subaru had been laid to rest was on the third floor of the city hall — the middle of a five-story building — and had apparently originally served some role like a conference room.

"The top floor got burned up by the dragon's breath. So now the fourth floor's the top one. That's where we've got the victims of 'Lust' gathered, waiting."

"...If the top floor burned, what happened to the Magic Vessel used for the broadcast?"

"That's safe. We took it down and it's in storage. The Magic Vessel itself is shaped kinda like a metal box, small enough to carry on a stand. Apparently the sound the box picks up gets relayed by other Magic Vessels set up all around the city that are meant to pick up sound."

An antenna, or perhaps a speaker, would be the closer analogy. A metal box and a speaker — the two together made up one Magic Vessel. Recovering both the hostages and the Magic Vessel safely was one of the few pieces of good news, one could say. Setting aside the propriety of how the Magic Vessel had been used, and the discomfort of calling the hostages "safe" at all.

"You said the people who were hurt are gathered on the fourth floor."

"Whether they got turned into flies or into dragons, their minds are still properly there, intact. So they'll listen to what we say, and they'll follow along. ...Whether that's a good thing or not, even Ah can't rightly say."

"............"

Whether it was a blessing that people's minds remained was something Subaru couldn't speak to either. If their very minds had been turned into insects, becoming creatures of an entirely different nature, they wouldn't have to grieve over having their forms changed. But that, too, would be a loss of self. Yet could you say that losing your own body, and going on living in that state, was not a loss? That answer could surely only be given by someone who had experienced the same thing.

"They can barely move their bodies as it is. Thanks to that, nobody's tried to kill themselves. Prob'ly it's all too sudden, they haven't even wrapped their heads 'round the situation yet. ...Just glad we secured 'em before they settled down enough to."

"Kill themselves — you mean suicide? That kind of thing..."

"You think there's nothin' to worry about?"

"――――"

That answer, too, wasn't one Subaru could give easily. All that came through was that, faced with this utterly deranged situation, Anastasia was handling countermeasures far more calmly than Subaru was.

"While you're alive, there's hope. But if your heart dies, even if your body doesn't, hope's snuffed out just the same. Gotta live. No matter what state you're in, gotta live."

Anastasia murmured this while facing forward, not showing Subaru her face. But if that clinging sentiment was her view of life and death, then Subaru shared it.

They had to live. As long as they lived, surely the chance to resist would never run out. For that reason, too—

"Anastasia-sama."

On the ground floor, Julius was the first to notice Subaru and Anastasia coming down, and he called out his liege's name. The reception lobby on the first floor was, for Subaru, a place he'd only passed through in an instant earlier. But even to his eyes, the traces of a fierce battle were etched deeply enough into the lobby that he could tell a ferocious fight had unfolded there.

Sword strikes and scorch marks from magic scarred everything from the desks and chairs to the very walls, and wiped bloody footprints asserted themselves here and there, still glistening. Julius had been regretting letting "Gluttony" slip away, but it came through clearly enough that he hadn't fallen behind by choice.

"How did the talk with Subaru go?"

"Ran on a liiiitle longer than expected, but it ended peacefully. Same as us, Natsuki-kun's fired up, says he won't lose either. So..."

Nodding at Julius's question and lightly teasing him, Anastasia turned her gaze further inside. Across from the stairs Subaru and the others had come down, near the lobby entrance, stood Ricardo, his massive frame swaying. According to what Anastasia had said earlier, he was supposed to be out patrolling and trying to make contact with survivors, but—

"Welcome back, Ricardo. How'd it look outside?"

"This look like a face full of good news to you, missy? It's pretty bad. Prob'ly gonna get worse as time goes on. Those bastards are damn good at makin' folks miserable."

Scowling with irritation, Ricardo let out a sigh as if grumbling over a poor haul. Roughly scratching at his dog-faced head, Ricardo gestured instructions to the Iron Fang members scurrying around nearby — a few headed back outside again, while several others sat down in a corner of the lobby to rest.

"Making people miserable, from the Witch's Cult? No, I get that the current situation itself is already about as miserable as it gets, but you mean something else on top of that?"

"The broadcast, boss. That damn broadcast."

At Subaru's puzzled tilt of the head, Garfiel poked his face out from beside the stairs. Gnashing his fangs irritably, Garfiel spoke, and Subaru asked back, "Broadcast?"

"The broadcast — you mean the one where they made their demands? Was there something else besides the demands?"

"Nothing quite so dramatic. They simply went to great lengths to publicize how every force that attempted to retake the city hall was thoroughly handled. It was a truly nasty piece of propaganda, very much in keeping with that Sin Archbishop of 'Lust.'"

"Thanks to that, every shelter we showed our faces at had its fighting spirit knocked clean out. Hard to get folks fired up to take their city back when they've just heard that."

Julius closed his eyes; Ricardo wrinkled his muzzle. Hearing their answers, Subaru understood what they meant by the Witch's Cult's "harassment."

What Capella had hammered home over the broadcast, alongside the demands, was news of the defeat suffered by Subaru and the others, who had struck early in their attempt to retake the city hall. Naturally, the city's residents who had no strength to fight would be disheartened by the defeat of those who did. And those who did have strength to fight would, before long, come to understand the meaning behind the early assault on the city hall. They'd understand what reasoning had driven Subaru and the others to strike so soon. And just as surely, they'd realize that such an assault required a certain amount of force to attempt — and that an assault mounted with that much force had failed.

In other words, Capella's broadcast had been carried out for the express purpose of snapping the fangs of resistance in everyone still holed up in the shelters, combatant and non-combatant alike, without distinction.

"From the city hall's records, we found where the shelters in the city are. So we've been makin' the rounds startin' from the nearest ones, but... you can guess the results."

Ricardo confirmed Subaru's present misgivings bang on, with a gesture indicating a swing and a miss. This much was a matter of the human heart. Subaru couldn't bring himself to blame Ricardo and the others for failing to persuade people. The sheer nastiness of the Witch's Cult had simply been one step ahead.

"Once the will to fight's gone, so's the will to resist. And once that happens, next comes somethin' even more dangerous."

Beside Subaru, whose face showed a look of bitter regret, Anastasia murmured, touching her chin. When Subaru looked up at her voice, Anastasia tilted her head and said, "Right?"

"Once people convince themselves they can't win by fighting, the option to fight disappears from their heads entirely. So what do you think comes next, for people?"

"You're not expecting me to say 'they despair and give up,' are you?"

"If it was just huggin' their knees, curled up, sniveling and cryin', that'd almost be cute. But it won't stop there, I'd wager. Even if the option to fight disappears, the will to live doesn't. So once that happens, what options are left?"

"...Don't tell me."

Subaru grasped the essence of what Anastasia was getting at, and it chilled him to the bone. Rising alongside it was an unbearable revulsion toward the Witch's Cult for having engineered this very situation.

As if confirming the answer for a shuddering Subaru, Anastasia clapped her hands. And then:

"The conditions for bein' saved are already laid out. In the form of demands. So it stands to reason that folks desperate enough will try to meet those demands by any means necessary. Wouldn't be strange at all if people started gettin' picked out — owners of 'Artificial Spirits,' owners of the 'Book of Wisdom.' And then, 'lovers or married couples to serve as sacrifices.'"

"Not everyone's going to go to that kind of extreme!"

"True 'nough. Some folks might say they'd rather not live on top of a sacrifice, and just try desperately to flee the city instead... Either way, chaos is comin', no avoidin' it."

If panic spread, this precarious balance would collapse in an instant. Subaru didn't want to imagine how the Witch's Cult would treat this city once that happened. Each of them held absolute authority to bring everything crashing down. A dictator's switch, capable of washing everything away.

"Before that happens, all we can do is go around and talk people down... isn't it?"

"Not realistic."

"But as a practical matter, that's way too pessimistic a conclusion!"

If everyone's anxiety went unchecked, there was a real possibility their hearts would tip into panic. And if so, the only effective medicine against that was to offer hope.

"We're pushing forward with preparations for a counterattack. If we say that and stir up some hope, wouldn't that keep panic at bay?"

"At the very least, Ah think we gotta brace ourselves for some losses along the way. If they don't think gettin' involved is worth it, those bastards'll just get cocky all over again, sure as anythin'."

"Wait, Anastasia-san. I feel like we're talking past each other here."

Sensing something off in the way Anastasia was steering the conversation, Subaru's mouth twisted. At his expression, Anastasia let out a weary sigh.

"Natsuki-kun's idea of not allowin' any sacrifices is pretty, but it ain't realistic. From the moment we got cut down so bad on the first move, there's no way we come out of this without pain and wounds. That much stands to reason, right?"

"The first move, the second move — yeah, that's true. But that's different from resigning ourselves to more sacrifices going forward. It's different from preventing a danger before it even happens."

"If doin' that led to victory in the big picture, Ah'd be all for it. But it won't. We only put people on the boat who've still got the grit to swim even if the boat sinks. ...Puttin' on people who'll just drown when it sinks, who're nothin' but deadweight — that don't lead to a winning path."

"—! Winning, losing—"

"If we lose, everybody's at the bottom of the water! If you don't like talk of winnin' and losin', then it's live or die! Wantin' to scoop up everybody — that's way too soft, Natsuki-kun."

Anastasia's raised voice piled onto Subaru, who was flaring up himself. Julius stepped forward, reaching out an arm to hold back Subaru as he seemed about to close in on Anastasia. But Julius's gaze wasn't on Subaru — it was fixed on Anastasia. Standing shoulder to shoulder with Subaru, Julius narrowed his eyes.

"Anastasia-sama. I understand your feelings. But I share Subaru's opinion on this. If the danger you fear could truly come to pass, I would rather we act to prevent it beforehand. Needless harm to citizens must weigh on your conscience as well... This is exactly what the Witch's Cult wants."

"Julius."

Standing squarely against his liege, Julius backed Subaru with righteous indignation still burning in him. Subaru felt both surprise and reassurance at this. If Julius — a man who held fast to the code of a knight — agreed with him, then surely he wasn't saying anything wrong. But Anastasia, smoothing her fur collar, replied to him,

"Y'know? It ain't like Ah'm sayin' 'abandon so-and-so' 'cause Ah want to. Ah don't think everyone in every shelter is gonna panic and run wild, either. It's just that there's a possibility of that. And we can't address every single possibility one by one, can we?"

"But..."

"Ah'm not a child, listen up. We use what forces we've got, do what we can. We'll absolutely make the Witch's Cult pay. Ah'll take back everythin' that falls under my responsibility. But the wider Ah stretch that 'everythin',' the lower the chance of actually gettin' hold of any of it."

To Julius, biting his lip in frustration, Anastasia's words were cold and unsparing. And what she said to Julius doubled as words aimed at Subaru's own recklessness. Of course, it wasn't as though Subaru didn't understand Anastasia's point.

For someone to save someone else — that was a weighty thing. Even one person saving one other person couldn't always be done satisfactorily. Much less across a wide scope, with many people — the more it grew, the further realistic success receded, and the higher the chance of a bad outcome. It was simple arithmetic, something even a child could work out.

The more Appa you tried to carry, the higher the chance some would slip from your grasp, the higher the chance you'd stumble not seeing your footing, the higher the chance your arms would fail to hold them all and they'd come undone.

"We're havin' this discussion to figure out how to win. If all you're gonna do is throw a tantrum, we don't need that right now. Ah'd think the knight's title ain't so cheap that you can't tell the difference?"

"――――"

At Anastasia's probing words, Julius shut his eyes tightly. But seeing his clenched arm drop and his head bow from behind, Subaru understood that Julius was about to withdraw his objection. And yet—

"Backing down here would tarnish the knight's title a whole lot more."

"...Natsuki-kun, were you even listenin'? That'd make this no different from what happened at the royal capital the first time. You did properly receive your knighthood, for whatever it's worth, didn't you?"

"That's right. I'm a knight too, for what it's worth. And because I'm a knight, this isn't something I can give up. Something I shouldn't give up."

The more Appa you tried to carry, the more you were bound to drop — that much was certain. But what Subaru, as a knight, and Julius, as a knight, were carrying wasn't Appa. It was something far more precious. Not silent Appa that made no complaint even if dropped. Human lives — lives that could cry, that could rage.

"Giving up from the start and moving with that resignation already baked in — no thanks. I haven't been steeped in this world's common sense quite that much yet."

"There you go again with the nonsense... What about the White Whale, and the Witch's Cult after that. There were casualties then too, fightin'. Did Natsuki-kun talk this same sore-loser talk back then too?"

"Don't mock me, Anastasia. The people who fought and became casualties back then had resolve. It's sad that people died, and the ones who died didn't want to die, but they had resolve. Having resolve or not — that's a completely different thing."

He knew it was a convenient argument, and it might not hold together logically. But as a fact, it existed — the moment where lives were staked, and the resolve that came with it.

"The people of this city have no obligation to be asked for that kind of resolve. Turning this place into a battlefield was the choice of the people who made it one. It's wrong for them to get dragged around by that choice."

"Even if they hate it, that choice comes for people without resolve regardless. If that happens, don't those people have to resolve themselves too, whether they like it or not?"

"No. It's only right that those who've completed their resolve are the ones who meet, in kind, others who've completed theirs. Bracing for that every single day, and working hard to protect the people who haven't finished bracing themselves — that's what a knight is, isn't it? That's what I expect of knights, and that's the kind of big talk I gave the kids back in the village."

Having received his knighthood, gotten fussed over here and there a bit, and talked grandly about what a "knight" was, Subaru had naturally come to think this way. Thinking that way, and talking with his nose growing long while the village children's eyes sparkled at him — because of that, Subaru meant to try his best to live up to it. Because Emilia, listening beside him back then, had had sparkling eyes too.

"I'm Emilia's knight. I want to fight for Emilia. But that doesn't mean it's fine for everything else to go to hell as long as Emilia alone is protected. Julius is your knight, Anastasia-san. More than anyone, he wants to fight for you. But that alone won't satisfy him. Because knights are creatures who like to show off, and they're greedy."

"――――"

"Julius'll go on showing off right up to the brink of death. After all, this guy's the finest knight there is. Which means he shows off more than anyone."

In front of a silenced Anastasia, Subaru pointed his thumb at Julius. At once, Julius — who had been listening quietly — showed a faint sign of flustered surprise. Even Anastasia's dumbfounded expression, and Julius's reaction, were rare enough sights that Subaru couldn't help twisting the corner of his mouth into an amused grin, even though this was hardly the moment for it.

"There's a simple, clear-cut goal here — beat the bad guys. It's ridiculous to fight while carrying the guilty feeling that you might have to abandon someone. Save everyone, beat everyone. Let's decide on that before we lose the fight in spirit."

Choosing from the start to abandon something, and failing to catch it despite trying, were two entirely different things. Writing it all off as mere self-satisfaction was easy enough to say, but—

"—Living for the sake of your own self-satisfaction is, if anything, about the most human way a human can live. I'm with you on that, brother."

"—!"

To Subaru, who was overwriting sweet idealism with more idealism, agreement came from a third party who hadn't been part of the conversation until now. Everyone startled at the sudden voice and turned as one toward the entrance of the hall. The figure standing there shifted uncomfortably under the barrage of stares.

"Whoa there, don't go hittin' me with all that heat, it's a lot to handle. I know full well I ain't got the kinda looks worth starin' at. Got no confidence I can live up to expectations either."

"—Al."

Announcing his harmlessness with clownish words and gestures was the man in the jet-black helm — one of Subaru's acquaintances who had gone missing since they'd parted ways at the inn that morning: Al. Unchanged in appearance and unchanged in attitude, he looked around the lobby restlessly, then said:

"This everybody important who's holed up in the city hall? Nobody else around?"

"...Crusch-san and the others are all up top. What were you doing?"

"Me? Well, soon as the ruckus kicked off I hightailed it and hid, obviously. Then, figured things settled down some, so I've been scopin' things out. There was that broadcast too, so I figured if I came to the city hall, I'd prob'ly find brother or somebody who's got a handle on what's goin' on."

Displaying an exasperating carefree attitude, Al answered while fiddling with the joints of his helmet, making little metallic clinks. At his answer, everyone besides Subaru couldn't help but feel put out. It was irritation at someone who had at least some ability to fight prioritizing his own hide in a situation like this.

"Don't make that face at me. Was it my fault I showed up late to the fight? I feel bad I wasn't there, sure, but that's just how the stars lined up, ain't it. And I don't figure one more of me joinin' in woulda changed the outcome anyway."

"Oi, boss. What's with this guy? He tryin' to pick a fight with us?"

At Al's shrugging remark, Garfiel was the first to hit his limit of patience. Come to think of it, Al and Garfiel had never met. Garfiel hadn't been present when Priscilla showed up at the inn and shattered their little gathering. In his eyes, Al was just some strange, unfamiliar man laying out results-oriented commentary after the fact.

"Wait, Garfiel. That's Al. He's the knight of Priscilla, one of the Royal Selection candidates. Might not have told you, but right now all five candidates are gathered in the city, so—"

"Gotta correct you there, brother. I'm the princess's subordinate, not her knight. I don't think enough of myself to claim that kinda righteous title. Oh, and that ain't a jab at you, brother, just so's you know."

Al corrected Subaru while calling him off, and further needled him. At that, Garfiel's veins finally bulged for real, and he bared his fangs as if about to snap at him.

"Alright, that's enough! That's enough outta both of ya! It's gettin' complicated, so hush!"

Before the spat could grow into something bigger, Anastasia clapped loudly to change the mood. She glared at Al with her round eyes.

"Showin' up outta nowhere and soundin' the mood sour right off — that somethin' you picked up from your master? Copyin' her bad personality's only gonna wreck your own reputation. Quit it."

"Much obliged for the advice, but sadly this is just how I naturally am. I got a reputation for gettin' on people's nerves as a matter of course. Throwin' the other guy off his pace is a survival skill too, y'know."

Slipping a finger through the gap at the neck of his helmet to scratch at his nape, Al drawled this out unashamedly. Answering that rebuttal with a sigh, Anastasia turned to look at Subaru.

"Things are gettin' complicated, but Ah's stance ain't changin'. We plan to win while keepin' in mind that there'll be losses. If Natsuki-kun really wants to try hard to make sure there ain't any casualties, go ahead and think it through as much as you like. It's not like Ah want casualties either."

"So for now, you're not going to stop me from going around the shelters?"

"...Ah'll set a time limit. Either way, we still need fightin' strength. If there's somebody who can fight holed up in some shelter, we gotta call on 'em too, same as you."

Reluctantly, but Anastasia withdrew her opposition to Subaru's plan. Full-fledged agreement would've been too much to hope for. He wouldn't press his luck by complaining now.

"Take a communication mirror with you. For now, Ah'll send word for you to be back six hours before the demand deadline. After that point, there's no room for a single slip-up, keep that in mind."

"The demand deadline — come to think of it, I never heard that. What time is it?"

"Tonight, the moment the date changes. —From now, that's roughly nine hours."

In Anastasia's place, Julius announced the deadline. If they were to hold a war council six hours before that, the time they had left to actually move freely was, in effect, three hours from now. Within that span, Subaru would somehow have to come up with an argument to convince Anastasia. On top of that, they'd need to find the strength and the method to defeat the Witch's Cult before the deadline, and carry that out. Only then would the city be saved. No — even that alone wouldn't be enough.

Retrieving Emilia. Restoring Rem's memories from "Gluttony." And returning the people transformed into "Lust" back to their original forms. Only once all of that was accomplished would it be complete victory.

"No time to waste. Is there a map, for going around the shelters?"

"There is. Take as many copies as you need. Just make sure not to overlap with the spots Ricardo and the Iron Fang kids already covered, or are covering now."

Heeding Anastasia's warning, Subaru was handed a map with checkmarks on the spots Ricardo had already covered. Copying those onto a fresh map, it seemed the Iron Fang had been prioritizing the farther shelters first. Given that Subaru would be covering ground on foot, it was a relief that the nearby ones were still left. It was almost as if things had been left that way on purpose, knowing this was how it would go.

"Subaru, let me come along too."

"Julius, huh. No, that'd be a problem. More hands would definitely help, no doubt about it, but it's also a problem if there's nobody left at the city hall who can fight."

Though Julius offered to accompany him, Subaru turned him down, citing the shortage of manpower. Ricardo was set to keep patrolling outside as well, and Subaru planned to run around with Garfiel in tow. Wilhelm was upstairs, but leaving the entirety of this building to him alone would be far too much of a burden, no matter how you looked at it. Even without that, the dark clouds hanging over Crusch's camp right now were thick enough as it was.

Hearing Subaru's answer, Julius nodded, though with obvious frustration. It was a rare sight, seeing him somewhat lacking his usual composure, worked up like this. Subaru lightly patted his shoulder, then jerked his chin toward Garfiel.

"Garfiel, come with me. I want to check the other shelters, look for people who can fight. If anxiety's spreading, holding that back is part of the goal too."

"...Oh, got it. Leave it to me."

At Subaru's request, Garfiel nodded, a beat late but firm. Seeing that through, Subaru spread out the map in his hands and considered which direction of Priestella, spreading out from the city hall at its center, he should tackle first. What he wanted to prioritize was fighting strength — and, of course, whether Reinhard was safe.

"Could you check the shelters 'round that inn area first? I figure the princess prob'ly hasn't gone too far from there."

"That'd mean cuttin' through here's the shortest way... hold on a second."

Subaru stopped Al, who was pointing at the map and brazenly steering things toward his own agenda. At Al's puzzled tilt of the head, Subaru pointed a finger at him.

"You coming along?"

"'Course I'm comin', bein' left alone for real would suck. And if I can't find the princess, that's a problem too. If I'd known it was gonna turn out like this, even if she told me to get lost somewhere, I wouldn't have left her side. Now that it has turned out like this, though, if I don't go lookin', I'll be in for it later."

"...That's some real devoted loyalty right there, huh."

However backward the reasoning, more manpower was purely welcome. Garfiel made no secret of his displeasure at hearing Al would be tagging along, but for now, there was nothing to do but overlook personal likes and dislikes. And, grudging as it was, Priscilla was still one of the people Subaru wanted saved too.

"If it's Priscilla, I met her in the park in the First District fifteen minutes before the commotion started. If she's still around, she should be in one of the shelters near there."

"No kiddin'. That's some seriously good intel, brother. Alright, that's where we start then."

Delighted at Subaru's information, Al slapped him roughly on the back, again and again. Leading the delighted Al and the scowling Garfiel along, Subaru left the city hall to make his rounds of the shelters.

Watching his back as he left—

"You've gone and made yourself the villain of the piece, haven't you."

"Quiet, you furry-collared fox. It ain't like Ah's not thinkin' anything through either. ...If Natsuki-kun comes back and still says the same thing, Ah might not be so opposed to considerin' it."

Anastasia murmured, her expression now thoroughly drained of its venom, and her collar gave a small shiver. No one else noticed that this exchange had even taken place.
