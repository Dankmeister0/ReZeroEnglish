Arc 6 Chapter 47: "I Will Not Forgive"

A dark, dark, dark, dark, dark, dark, dark place. Deep in the back, back, back, back, back, back, back, back of the mind.

Self, I, me, who, you, thou, Natsuki Subaru, Meili Portroute. Natsuki Kenichi, Elsa Granhiert, Natsuki Naoko, Petra Leyte, Emilia, Shaula, Beatrice, Frederica Baumann, Anastasia Hoshin, Garfiel Tinzel, Julius Juukulius, Otto Suwen, Ram, the blue-haired one, who is it, is it me, is it you, is it I, is it myself, is it someone else, me, you, thou, I——

——Self, I, Natsuki Subaru. Self, I, Natsuki Subaru. ——Who, I, Meili Portroute. Who, I, Meili Portroute.

"――――"

Thoughts spun round and round and round, and in that hazy sensation blurring reality and unreality, things melted together, mixed together, entangled together, cherished each other, hated each other, suffered together, loved each other, longed for each other, killed each other, wished for each other, destroyed each other, threatened each other, understood each other, cried together, laughed together, could never understand each other.

The self is the self, nothing more than the self; the other is the other, nothing more than the other. There, no room for compromise; there, no mercy of give-and-take; there, no common ground for understanding; there, no bond by which to think of one another — only an empty, hollow completeness.

"Subaru..."

"――――"

Shaking his head, he drowned himself in the desperate work of pulling out the thorn called "other" from within himself. Supporting the boy gasping for breath from either side, gently touching his shoulders, were Emilia and Beatrice. Feeling the worried gazes of the two against his cheeks, the boy — the entity that should be called Natsuki Subaru — struggled to fix the boundary between self and other, to reclaim himself.

Circling thoughts, swirling emotions whose correct answer had been lost in the melting-together. His lungs convulsed, and when he touched his throat at the sensation of struggling to breathe, what came rushing back at once was the strong pressure that had been placed around his neck, and after it, the creeping terror of suffocation that had assailed him. The vivid sensation of death closing in revived itself, and the surge of nausea brought hot tears welling up.

"――――"

Facing that terrifying sensation, Subaru worked slowly, carefully, to exclude from within himself the elements that could not possibly be his own, straining to establish himself as a single, unified being. First person, second person, memories, recollections, impressions, emotions, all the rest — he sorted them, dissected them.

If he could sort out the impurities like that, remove what had mixed together, he could return. If he didn't, he would stay like this, melted together, mixed together, unable to be peeled apart. Himself, and the girl killed by these hands of his, mixed together as one——

"—Natsuki-kun, what exactly did you see? Can you tell us?"

"Uh, ah?"

To Subaru, in that state of personality-agitation, a voice spoke from directly in front of him. Looking up, he saw someone crouched down to bring their eyes level with his — a person with pale blue-green eyes, Anastasia — no, was it Echidna now? In any case, it was her. To Subaru's recognition of her, Echidna, with a serious light still in her eyes, pressed on with her words.

"I can guess you're in a difficult position. But the present situation is a grave one for us as well. If possible, we want information as soon as we can get it. So that we don't misjudge our response."

"Wait, Echidna. Subaru just went through something awful, so—"

"Of course, I'm well aware of that. I also understand that his recklessness was for the sake of everyone here. Precisely because of that, we have an obligation to respond swiftly to his judgment."

Subaru could only let out ragged breaths; Echidna's assertion pierced through Emilia's attempt to shield him. As she spoke, Echidna's gaze flicked for an instant to the book lying on the floor behind her. Her eyes lingered on its thick black binding, its spine, and she continued, "Because—"

"—the young girl who accompanied us is missing. And on top of that, in the archive said to hold the records of the dead, a book was found bearing what seems to be that girl's name. ...The situation is serious. We don't have the luxury of standing still."

"That's... true, but..."

Against the low-voiced force of Echidna's argument, Emilia couldn't muster an immediate rebuttal. In fact, Echidna's appeal was reasonable. In this abnormal situation, Subaru was the only one holding what could be called information. No one present had the luxury of considering his feelings and waiting for him to calm down. Knowing this, Echidna, prepared to be blamed for it, hardened her heart and threw the question at Subaru anyway.

Faced with that resolve, Subaru too clenched his back teeth hard and touched a hand to his own throat. The sensation of being throttled with full force revived itself. He closed his eyes. And then——

"Let me ask you again, Natsuki-kun. What you saw, the contents of the book, were—"

"—Meili's... memories. It was."

"――――"

To Echidna's question, Subaru answered honestly, without deflecting. At once, beside him Emilia caught her breath, and across from him Echidna closed her eyes. On the faces of Beatrice, Julius, and Ram present, too, flickers of dismay and grief passed.

The 'Book of the Dead' had shown Subaru Meili's record. There was not a single person present who failed to understand what that meant.

If only this were some mistake — if only the book of Meili's record had been prepared in error. How much easier it would be to cling to such an irresponsible hope. But there was not one person here willing to entrust themselves to that self-deception, to flee toward the easier path. Especially not the girls beside Subaru, his face hardened with anguish, who were trying to support his heart.

"Calm down, Subaru. Calm down, and focus on finding yourself again, I suppose."

"...S-sorry."

"It's fine, I suppose. At a time like this, you should lean on Betty with everything you have. ...This isn't only Subaru's fault, I suppose. You mustn't torment yourself over it, I suppose."

"――――"

Beatrice, having stood up, cradled Subaru's head against her, his breathing ragged, and told him so. And yet the irony was that the very hand of the girl stroking his black hair, while holding back Subaru's thousand-fold shattered heart at the water's edge, was at the same time stirring it up with such cruelty.

Beatrice had, with such compassion, told him it wasn't his fault. But this was no one's fault but his own — it was a sin committed by 'Natsuki Subaru.' Not knowing that, Beatrice's gentle words felt so absurd, so sad.

"—Lady Ram, what do you think?"

As Subaru and Beatrice exchanged those words, Julius, his expression serious, turned the question toward Ram from the side. Relatively speaking, the shock showed less strongly on the faces of these two. Tracing back through Meili's memories, one could learn why: of everyone in the party, these two had always kept their guard up around Meili. At least, as far as Meili herself had sensed, Ram and Julius were the two who had never let their wariness of Meili, the assassin, lapse. By contrast, she had strongly felt the carelessness of Emilia and Echidna.

"If we're to believe Subaru's words as they are, then Miss Meili already—"

"Barusu isn't clever or coldhearted enough to lie in that pathetic state. ...There's no mistaking that the book there is Meili's. The question is whether someone should go double-check it, isn't it."

Hugging her own thin arms, Ram said this while glaring at the book still lying on the ground. Taking in her words, Julius furrowed his well-shaped brows in thought.

"—If it were me—"

"Confidence because it'd be your second time? If it's a matter of a second time, Barusu is under the same conditions and look at the state he's in. Of course, one could also think it hit particularly hard on Barusu because he's immature emotionally, but..."

"――――"

"Unfortunately, Ram doesn't rate Julius's current mental state as more stable, or better, than Barusu's."

At Ram's words, her pale pink eyes sharpened straight ahead, Julius caught his breath. But he soon shook his head, saying "That's fair."

"...Harsh, but I have no grounds to argue against Lady Ram's words. Given the state I was in yesterday, acting on my own selfish judgment, I have no right to claim otherwise."

"I'm afraid I share that opinion as well."

Echidna cut in on Julius's self-deprecating remark, raising a hand. She toyed with the fur of the white scarf at her neck with her fingers as she spoke.

"Though the grounds for my argument aren't about the rightness of Julius's mental state. I'm simply saying, having seen Natsuki-kun's condition, that this is a dangerous undertaking, and I'd like to oppose it on those grounds alone."

"Dangerous — that much is a fact, isn't it. He's clearly not himself."

"Indeed. It seems the effects of having read the book are showing more strongly than last time. Whether that's a matter of the number of times, or a matter of which book was read, is hard to judge."

"The number of times, and which book was read — you mean?"

Ram and Julius tilted their heads, and Echidna nodded, "Ah."

"Simply put, either the burden increases the more books one reads... or seeing the record of someone close to oneself distorts the heart. I think the possibilities split into these two."

"Someone close, is it."

At Echidna's raised finger and her conjecture, Ram turned her eyes toward Subaru. Bathed in that gaze, Subaru, held against Beatrice's slight chest, was desperately trying not to cry out.

Probably, Echidna's guess was correct. The reason Subaru's heart had taken such heavy damage was the latter of her two possibilities — the burden brought on by seeing the record of someone close, or at least familiar, to him.

The record of a 'life,' vivid and raw, belonging to someone in a closer relationship — what it brings is immense.

Naturally, people take more interest in those close to them than those distant. Subaru too, in fact, had wished for that. In fact, the reason Subaru had come to the point of his spirit half-merging with Meili's was precisely because he had stepped in that deep, wanting to know her.

As a result, Subaru found himself face to face, deep within 'me,' with an immeasurable void. The inertial emptiness of life itself, that that young girl had gone on carrying, endlessly——

"—Anyway, we can't just sit still. Let's go find Meili."

Just then, a sharp crack of air rang out. It was Emilia who had done it, clasping her hands together before her chest and raising her head firmly. Having drawn the archive's attention to herself, her words made Subaru's eyes go wide.

"Find her...?"

——Find, find her, what does that even mean. And what meaning could it have. ——Meili, she, 'me' — is already dead. ——Before she died, no one even bothered to care.

"Even if we find her, it might already be too late. We should have been there with her, and maybe we couldn't be. But we still have to go find her."

"――――"

"No one knows where that girl was, do they? First, let's find her... and then look for what we can do for her. Let's go find her."

Emilia's words lacked any concreteness, and were far from solid or wise. What meaning could there be in that — so insisted the unchanging voice of 'me' inside Subaru. A realist would surely have argued that there was a more meaningful way to spend the time.

And yet, not a single person present objected to Emilia's proposal. Not one person flatly denied it as wrong.

So what followed Emilia's proposal was, in practical terms, discussion of how to carry it out, and declarations of how to divide up the search.

"We'll have no choice but to change our plans for the day. Let's split up and search for her."

"Ram will... go first to make sure Rem is safe. Before coming to call Lady Emilia and the rest of you, Ram was with the spirit the whole time, but... now, once more—"

"That's best, Lady Ram. ...Subaru, I know this is cruel, but I need to confirm something. What you saw in the 'Book of the Dead' — did it include Miss Meili's final moment as well?"

Choosing his words carefully, Julius asked; Subaru hesitated over the answer. Did he see Meili's end — the answer to that question was yes. Subaru had tasted, more intimately than anything, the moment Meili's 'life' was lost. Strangled, his consciousness fading with the struggle to breathe, his thoughts filled to bursting with despair and hatred, and then that abruptly cut off. ——That moment must have been the moment the switch of her life was flipped off.

Having experienced that firsthand, Natsuki Subaru had, of all things, taken part in concealing her corpse. Natsuki Subaru was cooperating with the man who killed 'me.' None other than 'Natsuki Subaru,' the most hateful being in this world, and Natsuki Subaru who knew 'me' —

"—Natsuki-kun, I need you to answer. Did you see her final moment? Where she is now. What exactly was the cause of death — did you witness it?"

"――――"

To Subaru, who couldn't return an answer, Echidna pressed further, suspicion settling on her lovely features. Her words made his emotions fray at the edges. His heartbeat quickened, sweat soaking his back clammily. Whether Beatrice beside him would notice — that terrified him.

——Doesn't everyone actually already know that Subaru is involved in Meili's death?

Knowing it, and playing dumb, trying to corner him with this roundabout method in order to draw a confession out of him — such a foolish thought even crossed his mind. But every time such a thought arose, the color of the gazes directed at him denied it.

The eyes Emilia, Beatrice, Echidna, and Julius turned toward Subaru. It made him feel that he alone — struggling desperately to protect himself with blood-stained hands and a filthy, cowardly mind — was the only one whose heart was hideous and rotten. If that were so, then why not simply give ear to 'me's' wish instead——

"—I didn't see all the way to her dying. But it's got to be inside the tower, no question."

That temptation offered by 'me,' Subaru bit down on and denied. The dangerous emotion sprouting within him — it was a sweet curse left behind by Meili Portroute, one meant to force Natsuki Subaru into a confession of his sin.

A thought that had certainly taken root within Subaru. He wanted Emilia and the others to find Meili's corpse. To find it, to grieve, to regret, to see their faces twist in anguish, and thereby to let the pent-up feeling in his heart find some relief.

Whose feeling this even was anymore — Natsuki Subaru's, 'me's,' or 'Natsuki Subaru's' — he himself was starting to no longer know.

"...Do you still have the strength left to read the book again?"

"Echidna!"

To Subaru, unable to give a decisive answer, Echidna posed what could be called a merciless proposal. For an instant, Subaru moved to respond with "If you tell me to, I will," but before he could, Beatrice snapped out a fierce retort.

Still cradling Subaru's head, Beatrice's round, large eyes sharpened.

"What name do you want Betty to scream, I wonder...! In any case, Betty won't let you do such a thing, I suppose. Beyond this point, Betty objects for reasons other than emotion, I suppose."

"Given the risk of mixing further, I don't recommend it either. I only wanted to confirm whether he had the resolve to do it. Even if he insisted he would, I had no intention of letting him."

"...I'll pray that's your true intention, I suppose."

Withdrawing her opinion, Echidna received from Beatrice words spoken with fierce anger. Just then, into the uneasy air drifting between the two of them, Emilia and Julius stepped in simultaneously.

"That's enough. I'm against making Subaru push himself any further too. And I'm against us standing around talking here forever. ...I want us to get moving, soon."

"I agree with Lady Emilia. —Given the circumstances, if something has happened to Miss Meili, an accident seems most likely. Slipping on the stairs or the balcony, or perhaps stepping outside the tower—"

"—That's not it, yo."

Suddenly, Shaula, who had not taken part in the discussion until then, cut in to deny the concern Julius had voiced. She picked up Meili's 'Book of the Dead' lying at her feet, and pinching its spine with her fingers, swung it side to side.

"If Shorty No. 2 had gone outside the tower, yours truly would've turned into a killing machine already, yo. Since that didn't happen, that means nobody's stepped outside, yo."

"You handle that book so readily, considering how it's the sort no one wants to touch."

"Leaving it spread out on the floor was bugging yours truly too, somehow. If this counted as disrespect toward the archive and ended up with yours truly presenting a Kill 'Em All special, that wouldn't sit right either, yo."

Saying this, Shaula tossed the 'Book of the Dead' from hand to hand in her palms like she was juggling. For someone claiming to fear disrespecting the archive, her demeanor carried far more of an irreverent air than that. In fact, at that attitude, Julius furrowed his well-shaped brows.

"Lady Shaula, I'd ask you to stop playing with that book. Don't you have..."

"A heart to grieve, or somethin' like that? Don't ask yours truly that, dunno. Sure, Shorty No. 2 was clingy with yours truly. Yours truly didn't dislike No. 2 either, but... in the end, yours truly doesn't care about anything except Master."

Brightly, openly, Shaula laughed and brushed aside Julius's words. There was no trace of bravado or feigned wickedness in it. That this was Shaula's unquestionable true feeling — at least Subaru could believe that much.

In the end, she too was one of those beings who moved according to feelings ordinary people found hard to understand. ——No. Perhaps, to begin with, Subaru — having spent only this brief, limited stretch of time with these people — simply didn't possess the ability to understand any of their true hearts at all.

Behind the mask of a smile, how many complicated emotions, how many faces, can a person hide? That very thing he had just now, chewing on the life of 'me,' been made to feel with painful clarity.

"—Let's split up and go look for Meili. Subaru, stay here with Beatrice."

Subaru's self-recrimination, and the twisted mood Shaula had left behind — Cutting through both with a single line, Emilia raised her voice to spur everyone into action. At her instruction, Subaru felt a shamefully unworthy emotion well up in him. He wanted them to find 'me'——

"Beatrice, please look after Subaru. I'll go check on Reid's place myself."

"The right choice, I suppose. —Take care, Emilia. All of you, take care, I suppose."

Exchanging words, entrusting their wishes to one another, Emilia and Beatrice divided their roles and wished each other well. Then Emilia, taking Ram, Echidna, and the others with her, scattered off into the tower to search for Meili. Watching that retreating back with no words to offer, Subaru saw them off——

"—So it seems you don't plan to go along, yo."

Having seen off the search party, beside Subaru who remained in the archive, Beatrice glared at Shaula, who stood lingering there, and spoke. At Beatrice's harsh gaze, Shaula drew out her words sweetly with a "Well~"

"Yours truly's job is strictly Watchtower duty, yo? Sure, once that crown's off, body and soul yours truly belongs to Master, so if it's Master's wish, yours truly'll do it with everything yours truly's got."

"Then shouldn't you go and search for Meili as well, I wonder? Instead of loitering around here."

"—Is that really so, yo?"

Suddenly, Shaula tilted her head, turning a gaze toward Subaru that carried a strangely alluring quality. Leaping over Beatrice's head, she threw her question directly at Subaru. There was in it a peculiar allure — a first-ever expression from Shaula, who, in contrast to her appearance heavy with sensual overtones, had always seemed open and childlike, and thereby somehow far removed from that very allure.

"――――"

At that sudden, unexpected charm, Subaru's heart leapt hard once more. Unthinkingly he raised his face and met Shaula's black eyes, unreadable in their intent. Shaula, seeing Subaru's face, loosened her lips without any malice, and gently drew the 'Book of the Dead' close against her ample chest.

"If it's what Master wants, yours truly'll even go shoot down the moon, yo. So, not the half-demon, or Shorty No. 1, or Pretty Boy — it's Master yours truly wants to ask."

"Me..."

"Master, what should yours truly do, yo? Go search for Shorty No. 2? Or..."

There, she cut her words short, and did not speak what came after. She simply awaited Subaru's judgment, holding the 'Book of the Dead' to her chest, standing by. At her unwavering attitude, Subaru turned inward to examine himself, at a loss.

Where, exactly, lay the correct answer among his own feelings.

"――――"

The proposal Emilia and the others had made, and the conflicted feelings it stirred in him — these very things vividly proved the contradictory state of the being now called Natsuki Subaru.

——Meili Portroute's life, one that could hardly be called long.

By reading through the 'Book of the Dead,' he had, in digest form, sampling only the parts he liked, chewed it in a blasphemous manner, swallowed it, and digested it in an invisible stomach. 'Me's' life — 'Natsuki Subaru' had savored it to the full, in a double sense.

As a result, the mingled consciousness of Natsuki Subaru and 'Natsuki Subaru,' by taking in a girlhood utterly different from its own at the root, split into a mentality bordering on split personality.

——Subaru wants to hide his involvement in Meili's 'death.' ——Subaru wants Meili's corpse, which he hid, to be found. ——Subaru hates 'Natsuki Subaru,' who killed 'me.'

Hence, contradictory wishes were born within Subaru, driving him to act in order to fulfill them.

"—Shaula, go find Meili. Help Emilia and the others."

Before he knew it, Subaru had given that order to Shaula, who stood awaiting his instruction. Receiving the order, Shaula raised her eyebrows, straightened her posture, and saluted.

"Roger that, yo. If it's what Master wants, yours truly'll hup-two-three it, no matter what, yo."

Saluting cutely with a wink, Shaula held the book out to Subaru. Reflexively catching the black weight of the book, Subaru frowned, and Shaula stuck out her tongue at him. The moment it looked mischievous, like some kind of payback, she turned her back on Subaru in a flash and leapt down the archive's stairs in a single bound. Watching her black hair, dancing on her back, disappear from view, Subaru — released from that strange pressure — let out a breath.

"What a strange thing..."

——Natsuki Subaru, wrongfully accused, who doesn't want the corpse found, doesn't want his involvement in the crime exposed. ——'Me,' the victim, who wants her remains found, who wants her resentment and anger set free. ——'Natsuki Subaru,' the perpetrator, who foists murder and injustice onto others, piling wickedness upon wickedness.

These coexisted within a single body, pressing decisions and choices upon Natsuki Subaru. If he truly wished only for his own peace, Subaru should have kept silent about Meili's life and death, should have used his head more, and disguised the contents of the 'Book of the Dead.'

That he couldn't do so was, in fact, partly because Subaru simply wasn't clever enough to think that far ahead — but more than that, it was because of his empathy for 'me's' unresolved grief, and his hostility toward 'Natsuki Subaru' — his anger at himself, at the thought that that wicked being must not be allowed to run free.

Last time, and the time before that, and the time before that — it was a fact that someone had driven Subaru to his death. But even that, he now thought, might have been a trap set by the 'Natsuki Subaru' lurking within him, meant to make him hate others and send Subaru himself into a rampage.

After all, wasn't what he'd thought before reading the 'Book of the Dead' correct? That Emilia, and the others, and Meili too — not a single one of them was truly wicked, and that the only wicked one was Natsuki Subaru alone?

"Subaru, you mustn't torment yourself too much, I suppose. You should let go of that book too, I suppose."

"――――"

Glancing over, he saw Beatrice kneeling right beside him, gazing at him with worry. That gaze looking straight at him, worried about him — struck by the same light Emilia had shown — an uncomfortable feeling took hold in Subaru's chest.

This feeling Beatrice directed at him — it was, by rights, not meant for himself as Natsuki Subaru, but for 'Natsuki Subaru.' That he, hiding the loss of his memory, should receive it, and moreover, knowing 'Natsuki Subaru's' true nature, keep silent about it — that felt unbearably dishonest.

Shouldn't he tell her? The current Natsuki Subaru was one who could, without even knowing it, commit an atrocity like taking Meili's life. If so, before that danger turned once more toward Emilia, Beatrice, and the others, shouldn't he tell them everything——

"...Why are you so kind, to someone like me?"

"—. That's an awfully sudden question again, I suppose. Whatever's the matter, I wonder."

"No..."

If a single decisive word was going to crack open this borrowed relationship, then perhaps he wanted to ask beforehand — the truth of it. The meaning behind the trust that Emilia, Beatrice, and his companions placed in 'Natsuki Subaru.'

"――――"

Trust — the instant he thought that word through, a murkiness rose in Subaru's chest. What 'me' had directed toward Elsa. What Emilia and the others — the women in this world who treated Natsuki Subaru with such warmth — directed toward 'Natsuki Subaru.'

A jewel that only Natsuki Subaru could never possess, could only look at from afar. Its traces, its lingering scent, its afterimage, the warmth that must once have been there — why must Natsuki Subaru alone be forced to witness it, this close, like this?

"――――"

Why would such a man. Why would such a cruel person. Why would a man who could sneer so hideously. Why would a man involved in Elsa's death. Why would a man who could laugh and kill Meili. Why would a man who tried to hide 'me's' death. Why would such a man — such a man, such a man — be loved. Be loved by them.

"――――"

He wanted to know. He didn't know. He wanted to know. He didn't know. Only he, only Subaru, only 'me,' didn't know the reason. Couldn't know. Could never know. One-sidedly, everything, one-sidedly.

"――――"

Would asking make it clear? Could asking even pose the question? Would they tell him? Could he believe that was the truth? The smile 'me' had shown, the smile she had put on — was it fake, was there emptiness in her heart, is a mask something everyone wears — after all, even Natsuki Subaru had never wanted to show his true self outside the house, not even to his own father and mother.

If so, where was there a way to know the truth? Their true feelings, their true hearts — why they trusted 'Natsuki Subaru' —

The way to know that — shall 'me' tell you?

"――――"

The voice echoing in his mind cruelly tempted Natsuki Subaru. And he realized that the answer to the difficult question dominating his skull lay close at hand.

Even now, in his own arms, wasn't there a means to know the answer?

"Subaru?"

Watching Subaru fall silent, Beatrice narrowed her eyes uneasily. Without turning to face that gaze of hers, Subaru only looked down at what he held in his arms.

"――――"

——The thick black book seemed to welcome, with cruel readiness, his curious desire to know.
