Arc 6 Chapter 63: "Five Obstacles"

—Witchbeasts descending on the watchtower.

Every witchbeast in the Augria Sand Dunes surging forward at once — at Julius's report, Subaru and the others' expressions stiffened, a grave color passing through each of their eyes. What they felt through the soles of their feet was a faint tremor and rumbling shaking the tower's floor — and being told, without exaggeration, that this was the sound of witchbeast footsteps and roars echoing through the entire tower, it was no wonder they were shaken.

"I only have a vague idea how big this desert even is..."

In the loop where suspicion had driven Subaru to attempt an escape from the tower before, his flight plan had collapsed on the very first step, so the harshness of the dunes lay outside Subaru's actual experience. Still, the sight of a landscape that was nothing but sand as far as the eye could see was hard to forget, and he could imagine a corresponding severity. That said, it was hard to picture a desert as teeming with life—

"Originally, the Augria Sand Dunes were also a breeding ground for witchbeasts. The damage from witchbeasts targeting humans who ventured into the sand was worse than the harshness of nature itself."

"There was apparently a time when the army was dispatched, with plans drawn up to wipe out the dunes' witchbeasts. Though, judging from this rumbling, you can guess how that turned out."

Julius and Echidna each offered an answer to Subaru's question. In other words, this rumbling was no paper tiger, and Subaru's hope that the enemy numbered, at most, a zoo's worth was way off the mark. More precisely, it seemed there were witchbeasts on the scale of a savanna. No wonder the situation invited despair. But then—

"—So, is that why you came to fetch me?"

The one who spoke up was Meili, fiddling with her own braid. She was the only one who, upon hearing about the witchbeasts' arrival, showed no unease-driven agitation. That wasn't a reaction born of predictability, though. It was the kind of reaction that came from not feeling threatened by witchbeasts at all.

"It's a pathetic thing to admit, but yes, that's right. I need to borrow your strength."

At Meili's question, Julius, his cheeks tense, nodded. To handle the mass of witchbeasts descending on them, put Meili — who had the ability to command witchbeasts — to work against them. Julius's reasoning made sense. It was the same idea that occurred to Subaru first, too. And yet, a strange feeling was sounding an alarm deep in Subaru's chest.

"Subaru? What's wrong? You've got a weird face, I suppose."

"...Having a weird face has been my destiny since the moment I was born."

"Isn't that just your eyes, I suppose?"

Right beside him, Beatrice, noticing Subaru's bitter expression, looked up at him with worried eyes. Under her gaze, he let out a deep breath. With the force of exhaling a great deal of carbon dioxide, he also breathed out something like a shapeless unease along with it. Still, it remained vague and indistinct, refusing to take concrete form.

"Subaru, is something bothering you?"

"Don't grill me so hard. Though I guess it's my fault for making a face like I've got something to say. I'll apologize for that, and add one more thing. An exclusive scoop I picked up from the 'Book of the Dead.'"

"——"

Around the breath-holding Julius, Beatrice and the others, who had already heard that word, widened their eyes. Honestly, their reaction was predictable, but he couldn't explain the situation without saying it. The cause of this sudden shift — the reason the witchbeasts were surging toward the tower en masse—

"—It's 'Gluttony.' The Sin Archbishop of 'Gluttony' is sending witchbeasts at the tower."

"—! Why?"

"That part, I'm sorry to say. Inside Reid's 'Book of the Dead,' I met one of the 'Gluttony' sinners. More precisely, I was reunited with one of them. Apparently we'd met last night, too. And that..."

"That's the culprit behind your lost memories, I take it?"

Cutting off the conclusion, Julius spoke it himself, and Subaru dipped his chin. Calling it "as expected" was a strange way to put it, but Julius's comprehension was sharp. He'd connected the name and ability of 'Gluttony' and arrived at the conclusion Subaru had hoped he'd reach right away.

"I won't pretend I'm not shaken. Once again — this time, was nothing taken from you?"

"Luckily, aside from being declared the target of a lifelong stalking campaign, my body's still clean. Though I just finished explaining myself to Beatrice and the others, too."

"I see. ...Lady Beatrice?"

"Your concern is Betty's concern too, I suppose. For now, no abnormalities are visible, I suppose."

"No trust at all..."

His face convinced, Julius immediately shot a question at Beatrice for confirmation. Subaru understood there was no helping it, but it left him with complicated feelings all the same. In any case—

"So it sounds like, as of last night, the enemy already knew we were here. And from there, about half a day... they came to make trouble, pet in tow. Is that about the size of it?"

"At normal travel speed, it's hard to imagine reaching this tower in half a day. Especially factoring in witchbeast interference along the way. However..."

"However?"

"Unlike our own difficult journey, if 'Gluttony' possesses the power to command witchbeasts... then it becomes purely a matter of travel speed."

At Echidna's shrugging comment, Subaru crossed his arms with a difficult expression. The assumption that 'Gluttony' had the power to command witchbeasts was, at minimum, one they ought to make. At the very least, there was no doubting that the enemy's guidance was what had brought the witchbeasts surging toward the tower. They needed to keep in mind that the enemy had such means at its disposal.

"What's this about a matter of travel speed?"

"...It's a land of sand. Even without witchbeast interference, if you get your feet caught in sand, your speed drops. Even going straight, without ever losing the way to the tower, I'd assume it would take several days."

"But there's another possibility?"

"—If overland travel eats up time, then going by air is a different story."

"By air...!"

Presented with an unexpected option, Subaru's black eyes widened in surprise. By air — indeed, with that means available, travel speed would be drastically revised. It made sense that a bird soaring through the sky could move faster than a person walking on the ground.

"I had this arbitrary preconception that flying was something special. But with magic around, is flying through the sky just a common thing?"

"That's not true at all, I suppose. Flying through the sky is a type of compound magic, and the method is complicated, I suppose. Considering the risk of falling, it's normally too dangerous to attempt, I suppose. The only ones who'd do it are fools, geniuses, or fools who are geniuses, I suppose."

"It's a well-known story that Margrave Mathers ascends to the castle from the sky..."

"That would be the fool who's a genius, I suppose."

It seemed Beatrice had taken a dislike to this as-yet-unseen margrave. Glancing at Beatrice, who looked adorably sulky, Subaru tilted his head at being told that flying by magic wasn't common either.

"If it's not magic, then a giant bird... ah, a dragon! Riding on the back of a flying dragon, right!"

"In fact, the technique of dragon-riding is an established secret art in the southern Vollachia Empire. The Empire monopolizes that technique, but with 'Gluttony's methods, seizing it would be simple."

"Just hear it from someone who knows the technique. Lick up their memory and take it, huh."

Thinking about it that way, the enemy possessed a means that was terrifyingly powerful in an information war. Devour a 'memory,' and you could make your own everything from a secret someone wanted kept hidden to anything else; devour a 'name,' and you could erase even the fact that you'd done so, along with the very existence of the person you took it from.

—Memories shape a person, as the saying goes.

"——"

A person's worth, their journey — Subaru believed these were things carved into memory and history. Now, in particular, with that sense of values, Subaru felt utter disgust at the vile power of 'Gluttony.'

It was ruinous. 'Gluttony's power to steal others' memories was malice that defiled and ruined everything. That someone would use it for their own happiness, in pursuit of their own contentment, was absurd. Twisting fate through wrong means, through methods that defied all reason.

—And that's coming from the guy who does "Return by Death," huh?

"—!" "Subaru!"

Unconsciously, Subaru had bitten down hard on his lip, and Beatrice called out to him. Feeling his arm being pulled, he looked over and met her uneasy blue eyes. A sharp pain lingered at the corner of his mouth — it was pathetic that it was a wound he'd inflicted on himself by biting. Licking the blood from the wound with his tongue, Subaru bowed his head and said, "Sorry."

"For a second, a nasty smug face flashed through my head. Making that face eat its words is one of my big goals... but the problem that comes first is her brothers."

"We already knew there were multiple 'Gluttony' Sin Archbishops, but did you learn something?"

"It's confusing because of a seriously hard-to-parse first-person pronoun, but I think so. One who's holed up inside the 'Book of the Dead,' and two older brothers of hers, I think."

In the 'Corridor of Memories,' Rui had repeatedly used the terms 'Onii-chan' and 'Nii-sama.' Taken at face value, that meant Rui had two older brothers. Whether that turned out to be proof of the three 'Gluttony' siblings exactly as stated was another matter, but—

"At the very least, she didn't seem like the type capable of that kind of maneuvering. Well, coming from me, who nearly lost to that little bit of scheming, that's not much of a thing to say."

If he'd been thoroughly played by the enemy's design and had gone through with strangling that thin neck, what would have happened? If not for the voice of that blue-haired girl who'd appeared on the scene, by now, surely—

"—So, what is it you're trying to say? Going off on tangents like this — talking in circles is your bad habit, Subaru."

At that, as if her patience had run out, Meili shook her body and voiced her displeasure. The girl who, from the very start, should have been given her role, was plainly unhappy that things had been put on hold. Understanding her frustration, Subaru raised a hand to placate her, saying "I know, I know."

"I get how you feel, but it was a minimum necessary conversation. Sure, going in circles is my bad habit, but think of it as part of my charm and love me for it—"

"Do you want to get bitten?"

"My bad! Anyway, witchbeasts came rushing in, a whole ton of them! So, let's ask the witchbeast specialist, Professor Meili, to handle it! That's the natural flow, right? I did say it, didn't I. The other side are the ones who stole my memories."

"That means..."

Seeing Subaru grow careful in his tone, Meili too lowered the pitch of her voice. She understood the real meaning behind what Subaru was trying to convey. And the same held true for the other companions around them, their expressions changing. Subaru swept his gaze over all their faces and continued, scratching his head.

"I'll preface this by saying it's my fault, okay? But they have my memories. — They set the trap already knowing the strengths of this lineup here. We have to beat that."

—Whoever controls the information war controls the battle.

That felt like it had been some kind of victory formula in modern warfare, or so Natsuki Subaru, poisoned by various gaming cultures of modern-day Japan, understood it. Even setting that aside, playing poker with your hand already visible to your opponent gave you next to no chance of winning — that much was a formula even a child could grasp. Hence—

"Given that the enemy has seen our forces, I can understand your concern."

Leading the way ahead, Julius narrowed his eyes, tossing only his voice back over his shoulder. Having finished a simplified explanation of the situation, they now shared the recognition that the enemy's approach might be dangerous. Still, piling an anxiety factor onto an already unclear situation might, if anything, only throw them further off balance — that thought did rear its head a little, but—

"It's far scarier to have important information kept secret and find out about it later, I suppose. Subaru's thinking now is the right call, I suppose."

"Really? You sure? Nobody's starting to hate me, right?"

"Why are you so anxious about that? It's fine, I suppose. Nobody's going to start hating Subaru, I suppose. If anything, l-l-l..."

"Okay, okay. I get it. It's fine, I'm reassured. Love you."

Holding hands as they ran side by side, Subaru nodded at Beatrice, who was doing her best to send an encouraging message. Honestly, having the part he'd left unspoken put into words for him was more embarrassing. It was far easier to say words of love than to have them said to you. There was no need to doubt his own feelings.

"So, where's Shaula!?"

"Ah, just ahead... there."

At the front, running, Julius pointed toward a stone-built passage. It was a corridor that appeared to dead-end, but a camouflaged side path was hidden just to the right. Bending low, Subaru followed Julius as he ducked through the wall, and the moment his body emerged outside the tower, he was greeted by a fierce, sand-laden wind and a chain of unfamiliar sounds.

—To Subaru's ears, it sounded exactly like countless panes of glass being shattered all at once.

"Uu— ryaryaryaryaryaryaryaryaryaryaryaryarya—i!!"

"——"

The place was a space like a balcony set into the tower's outer wall. As could be told even from the great spiral staircase leading to the lower levels, the fourth floor existed at a height that was far more than a few dozen meters off the ground. On that balcony, high up where dry winds battered, leaping side to side in what looked like a dance, was a stunningly beautiful woman with glossy black braids trailing behind her—

"—Shaula!"

"Ah! Master, you came, ossu!? I'm so happy, ossu! Consider this my big moment, or maybe a workplace tour, ossu! Like a parent visitation day, so go ahead and take a good look, ossu!"

Shielding his eyes from the sandy wind with his arm, Subaru shouted, and Shaula answered back in a bright, cheerful voice. What she'd actually been doing, though, was a fantasy almost too incredible to believe.

Spread out across the full width of the balcony, arranged in a single row, were countless gun-ports formed in midair. "Gun-ports" was a bit grandiose, but no other word seemed fitting. Gun-ports, muzzles, launch-holes — whatever you called them, they were undeniably hollow openings for firing something. White magic-circle-like formations floated in the air, forming a great array of gun-ports, their aim tilted sharply down and to the side, toward the ground below. And then—

"—Infinite Hell's Snipe!!"

"What is that, so cool!!"

The instant Shaula shouted the technique's name, the white gun-ports flashed brilliantly. Drowning out Subaru's nonsense that followed immediately after, a sound like glass shattering rang out again and again across the sky above the dunes. At the same time, the gun-ports lost their shape, unraveling and dissolving into the air. This was the true identity of the light, ringing sound that had greeted Subaru and the others the moment they'd stepped out onto the balcony. And the purpose behind Shaula playing that sound had one single aim.

—A single volley of shots fired, white light closing in on the ground at literally the speed of light.

When the white light struck the sand, it burst with fierce brilliance, blasting the earth apart. The same held true when it struck the back of a witchbeast charging furiously across the sand. The witchbeasts kicking up sand-dust as they made for the tower took the white light on their backs one after another, scattering blood and flesh across the sand. The parched sand drank up the blood and flesh, and the scattered corpses were trampled by other witchbeasts — the white light's carpet bombing, never missing its mark, savagely whittled down the total number of witchbeasts closing in.

The number of white lights approached a hundred, and the power of a single shot was enough to blast through two or three witchbeasts at once. And Shaula had unleashed volley after volley of them in a mere short span of time. Considering she'd been fighting alone the whole time Julius went to fetch Subaru and the others, her kill count was probably already nearing a thousand. And yet, even Shaula's carpet bombing amounted to nothing more than a drop in the bucket against the swarm of witchbeasts blanketing the tower's surroundings like an army of ants. That was how immense the total number of witchbeasts gathering from the dunes was.

"Hey, hey, hey... I really don't want to ask this, but don't tell me—"

"From this vantage point, we can only see one direction around the tower. But the same sight is spreading on the opposite side of the tower too. You should assume that without doubt."

"There's also the possibility that only this side of the tower has, like, sugar water smeared on the wall or something, right?"

"If there really were someone foolish enough to pull a stunt like that, Betty would slap them across the face for real, I suppose."

The unceasing rumble underfoot, and the black mass writhing below. Told that it was closing in from every direction of the tower, Subaru felt like he might collapse under the sheer weight of reality. As an aside, he also had a thing or two to say about the visual design of the witchbeasts visible in the distance. Truly grotesque, eerie, incomprehensible — God's design skills earned a C-minus.

"How was it, Master! Did you see my performance!? Also, the angle from behind was subtly amazing, that was my appeal to you, Master. How about that too!?"

"Your spirit not breaking even in this situation is amazing, I'm genuinely impressed! I figured you're the type who grows more from praise for effort than results, so, good, that's really good! Nice bulk! Also, I can't spare the attention to judge your angles, so let's focus!"

"Okay Google, ossu! Defense! Offense!"

Honestly, Shaula's unbreakable spirit was something to admire. Even as he tossed out fairly slapdash words of encouragement, watching Shaula respond so gamely from her back stirred up a bit of guilt in Subaru. At the very least, if this fight ended safely, he wanted to repay her somehow, but—

"First, getting through this is the top priority...! Meili!"

"You don't have to shout so loud, I already get it. But..."

"But!? But what!? 'But, it's fine if I just kill all the witchbeasts, right?' Is that it? Oh, sure, go ahead, please!"

"Don't expect me to be that convenient, geez. Even I can't handle this many bad animal-chans."

Hands pressed to both ears, Meili made a sour face as she surveyed the ground below from the balcony. Then, letting Subaru's outrageous wish pass right by, she set her cute, young profile firmly. And, licking her pink lips with a hint of allure—

"—So, I'll move the kids I had prepped, and throw them at it, 'kay?" "—!!!"

Meili said that and pointed her hand toward the ground, and at the same moment, a massive mass burst up from the ground, blasting the dunes apart. From this distance — where even a large witchbeast looked no bigger than a bean — the being that had blown away the ground was clearly visible. That meant it was that enormous. — Likely thirty or so meters long overall, a giant worm had appeared, and with its huge body, it began crushing the surrounding witchbeasts flat.

"That thing is..."

"Just in case something like this happened, I had it prepped and tamed. I was actually planning to sneak it away quietly, but I messed up letting it out here."

Face to face with that giant worm, Subaru caught his breath, and Meili stuck her tongue out at him. Unfortunately, Subaru's surprise wasn't so much about the worm itself as the fact that he recognized it. That was the witchbeast he'd encountered once before, when he'd tried to flee the tower. Thinking back, Subaru had suffered damage himself from that worm bursting up all at once from underground. And afterward — that the worm had been blown away by white light was, indeed, a fact tucked away somewhere in the corner of his memory.

So the worm was Meili's, and the light had been Shaula's, all along. Tasting a belated sense of understanding, along with surprise and unexpectedness, Subaru reached a hand toward the head of Meili, who was putting on a tough act, and pulled her — unable to dodge in time — into a forceful pat.

"Wah, ah, h-hey!"

"I dunno if it's just how you are or a habit, but you don't have to act tough. I don't believe for a second that you were trying to run off and leave us behind or anything like that."

"Hmph, why can you say that so confidently?"

"I mean, I'm you and you're me, and everyone's different and everyone's fine, I guess?"

"Hah?"

At Subaru's words, Meili voiced her incomprehension, and Subaru had no intention of making her understand either. Ultimately, at this stage, Subaru could read through the deception in Meili's words right away. After all, he had once viewed the memories of her, who had died, as if they were his own. As for who she was while inside this tower, without a major shift in her values — that much, he could speak of as though he understood, with confidence.

And so, with his hand still resting on the head of Meili, who groaned "uu—" in discontent, Subaru turned his attention to the situation currently unfolding — and its correspondence with what had happened in the previous loop.

The witchbeasts surging en masse was probably something in common with the situation from before, when the burning centaur had been allowed to breach the tower's interior. In other words, allowing witchbeasts to breach the tower's interior had itself happened in the previous loop, too. And it had been Julius, fighting hard on the lower floors, who'd been busy dealing with that. If so, had Shaula, just like this time, been dealing with the witchbeasts outside from the balcony? And, with her hands full, had ended up allowing a breach.

But this time, it wouldn't turn out that way. Against this massive horde of witchbeasts, they had a major advantage this time that had been sorely lacking before. And that was Meili's presence — the girl who, in the previous loop, had lost her life at Subaru's own hands, without his intending it, was the very key to breaking open this situation.

"If Meili fights the witchbeasts together with Shaula, the situation changes. That frees up Julius's hands? If that's the case..."

—They could reposition Julius as a combat force to deal with one of the other problems.

"——"

The moment he reached that thought, Subaru understood it was essential to move the personnel needed to handle the multiple problems occurring simultaneously in this tower, to the positions where they were needed.

—The horde of witchbeasts blanketing the dunes. —The Sin Archbishop of 'Gluttony,' launching an attack on the tower. —The vicious giant scorpion, roaming the tower's interior as though it owned the place. —The vast black shadow, threatening to swallow not just the tower but the dunes themselves. —And, Reid Astrea, who had at some point begun wandering freely through the tower.

"Our forces are me and Beatrice, Emilia-chan and Ram. Meili and Shaula, plus Echidna and Julius..."

"Shall we also count, as extras, the two Earth Dragons and the healing Spirit in the green room? It doesn't hurt to keep them as options."

Echidna shrugged as Subaru began counting off enemies and allies on his fingers. Nodding at her words, Subaru added Patrasche and the large Earth Dragon on the lower floor to his hand of cards as well. Just as Echidna said, this wasn't a situation where they could afford to be stingy with their hand. Whatever it took, adding it to their hand, he had to spin his cunning head at full speed and satisfy their victory conditions. In that sense, too, he wanted to keep track of every ally within reach—

"—Hey, wait a second. Isn't this taking too long, no matter how you look at it?"

"——"

"Emilia-chan and Ram just went to pick up Rem and the others from the green room, right?"

The two allies not present here — their delay in rejoining left Subaru's throat feeling parched with unease. The green room was on the fourth floor, meaning the same level Subaru and the others were currently on. There was a possibility they simply didn't know this location and had gotten separated somewhere inside the tower.

"Given the situation, if it were simply a matter of not knowing where we are, Lady Ram would surely find some way regardless. Or else, Lady Emilia would show herself by, say, breaking through a wall."

"Ram, sure, but what's your assessment of Emilia-chan turned into? There's no way those cute skinny arms could break a wall. And even if she could, that's not her personality, right?"

"The fact that your confidence is wavering is proof enough, I suppose. But Betty has a bad feeling too, I suppose."

"—! Shaula! Meili! Can I leave this to you two!?"

With agreement from Julius and Beatrice as well, Subaru called out to Shaula and Meili. In response, Shaula, still setting up and firing her light gun-ports, gave a thumbs up, and Meili flicked her braid aside and puffed out her thin chest.

"Leave this to me and go on ahead, ossu!" "I can handle this much on my own, no problem. You'd better come back safe with those sisters, or I won't forgive you."

With Shaula blurting out what was probably the top line on her list of things she wanted to say before she died, and Meili showing them her dependable back, Subaru nodded to Beatrice and the others and took off running. Ducking through the wall and bursting out into the corridor, he asked—

"With Meili and Shaula holding the line, what about the chance witchbeasts get inside the tower?"

"It's not zero, but there was a possibility they could enter the tower through the underground sand palace we fell into — Natsuki-kun, do you not remember it? Though that too, thanks to Meili-kun—"

"The worm went wild and the underground collapsed?"

"That underground passage had that many branching paths. It's unlikely to hold up structurally."

At Echidna's affirmation, Subaru clenched his fist. In other words, in two senses, Meili's presence had stopped the witchbeast stampede in its tracks. If witchbeasts couldn't get in from above ground or below, they could defend well enough.

With the witchbeast horde dealt with, of the five major problems, four remained. Even so, it was a path dizzying enough to contemplate — but crushing even one mattered greatly. Beyond these five obstacles—

"We're going to clear this tower..."

Just before Subaru could finish the words —

"—Barusu!!"

"—! Ram!?"

On the way running toward the green room, he looked up at a voice reaching him from down the corridor. Looking ahead, charging furiously toward Subaru's group of four was a jet-black shadow — Patrasche. On the back of the sharp-featured Earth Dragon, Ram clung tight, and in her slender arms she held firmly onto the still-sleeping body of Rem.

"Ram! Are Patrasche and Rem okay too!?"

"Yes, somehow. We went through something awful while Barusu was napping. How could you sleep through something like that. Get up already."

"My bad! Don't gang up on me with your sister! Look, see, I'm standing! I'm running!"

Sliding lightly down from the Earth Dragon's back, leaving Rem entrusted to the saddle, Subaru got hit with Ram's sharp tongue. It happened to resemble the words Rem had used to spur him on in his dream, and it stirred up a strange sense of realization in him — that they weren't just alike in looks, they really were sisters.

"—? Barusu's strange behavior is a little concerning, but that's not what matters right now."

At Subaru's words, Ram's brow furrowed slightly in suspicion, but she quickly shook her head and turned her attention behind her. That was the direction of the corridor she'd just come from — where the green room was. Subaru, too, felt a sense of wrongness that Ram had brought only Patrasche and Rem, without Emilia, whom she should have gone with.

"Yeah, I've got things I want to ask too, and things I want to say. You, the one who was supposed to go with—"

"—On the other side of the corridor, we ran into someone who calls herself the Sin Archbishop of 'Gluttony.'"

"——"

Cutting off his question, Ram stated it flatly and clearly. At the force of her words, Subaru — and Beatrice and Julius too — fell silent. So it was Echidna, the least shaken among them, who responded instead.

"You said the Sin Archbishop of 'Gluttony'? On the other side of the corridor?"

"Yes, that's right. — And that Sin Archbishop of 'Gluttony' is currently fighting someone."

"...Someone?"

That explanation gave an increasingly strange impression. Ram's words remained, as ever, full of spirit and confidence, but precisely because of that, the vague part within them gave rise to a deeply far-reaching sense of wrongness. When Subaru pressed her on that vague part, Ram nodded, "Yes."

She nodded, and then she said:

"—Someone with silver hair, someone I don't know, is clashing with the Sin Archbishop of 'Gluttony.' She told us to run, Ram and the others — and she's still saying it, even now."
