Arc 2 Chapter 15: "The Second Miscalculation and the Betrayal of Expectations"

Subaru spat defiance at the rising sun, and the second first day at Roswaal Manor opened its curtain. All he had to do was watch the sun rise and set, five times over. How to spend that time was, naturally, a matter of policy — "follow the previous flow as closely as possible" was the course Subaru had charted.

As he'd resolved in the garden, Subaru intended to keep his promise to Emilia from before the loop. To do that, he had to cross that moonlit night again and exchange that promise with her. And as a matter of loop-story convention, there was one conclusion that had, more or less, been established.

Namely: if you walk the same road, the story arrives at the same destination. Since he was tracing the same flow as before, that only made sense as a story. The feelings and actions of the people involved would overlap, and the outcome would head toward the same ending. What mattered to Subaru was changing only that ending, into a future convenient to him. And, in the process, collecting every single memory that ought to occur along the way. In other words, using the loop to cherry-pick the best of everything — that was the supreme objective.

Quick-save and quick-load his way to a conclusion more to his own taste. That was Subaru's lofty, if slightly underhanded, scheme —

"—Somehow, I already screwed it up."

Right from day one, things had gone wildly off from his predictions, and Subaru sank his body into the bathtub, blowing bubbles, muttering that out listlessly.

Floating spread-eagled in the steam-filled bathhouse, Subaru looked back over day one. The morning of resolve, and from there, the runaway catastrophe that had followed.

Right after the morning routine with Emilia, Subaru had quietly resolved to retrace the previous flow. Honestly, he doubted he'd been able to trace, word for word, the parts he'd said on impulse — but the broad shape of the conversation should have followed the same course as before.

Puck's petting privileges, Emilia's nickname, the first meeting with Roswaal. Then, at breakfast, the explanation of the emblem's worth and Emilia's standing. Whether he'd managed natural reactions in between was debatable, but he wanted to praise himself for doing a decent job of it, in his own way. He felt like stamping it with a gold star and a "Very Well Done."

"If I actually scored it, it'd probably be a disaster, so let's pass on that."

Setting aside the harsh review of his own performance, things had, more or less, proceeded within the palm of Subaru's hand. After that, the conversation had naturally shifted to the topic of Subaru's "reward," and just as before, his position had class-changed from "otherworldly shut-in" to "trainee servant of a pervert noble."

After that, Ram had been assigned as his instructor, and things moved into the first day's labor, starting with a tour of the manor — and it was from here that things went strange.

"Why the hell is it going totally different from last time? What am I supposed to do with all this preview knowledge I've got... I was all 'oh, I did this exact problem in cram school!' pumped and ready to coast through it, y'know."

Subaru, only his face poking out of the bathtub, muttered with his chin resting on the rim. Warmed thoroughly all over, he could feel his joints ache dully, sluggishly, as the memories came flooding back.

He'd followed the policy, retraced the previous flow, and judged he could ride the momentum straight through — but what had crushed that confidence was the content of the work Ram had assigned him. Kitchen duties, simple room cleaning, laundry, tidying — the work itself hadn't changed from the previous loop.

"It's just that the... difficulty level went up quite a bit, or something."

Or maybe it was simpler to say the sheer volume of work entrusted to him had increased, in both quantity and quality. Put less charitably, the workload dumped on him had increased.

"Last time I was already dead on my feet, and this time it was hard in a whole different way... damn it, I figured if the content was the same I'd at least get to coast a little."

Complaints spilled out at the harshness he hadn't anticipated, but at the same time, Subaru privately judged that the current situation wasn't a good one. This was the result even after he'd made the effort to trace the previous content. If things diverged this much from the content on day one alone, there was no way he'd be able to line things up with the previous loop from day two onward. He told himself not to worry about small discrepancies, but on the other hand, he was terrified of the possibility that those discrepancies would accumulate and eventually throw off even the big problem that was surely coming.

"Especially this time, since I don't even know why I came back..."

This time's pattern was: sleep normally, wake up, and find he'd gone back. Unlike the clarity of dying and returning last time, just thinking about how to cope with this time — when he had no way to predict when it would come — was exhausting.

"If it's diverging this much, can I even trust my memories anymore...?"

The weak-hearted thought slipped out unbidden, and he sank his body back into the water as if to shake himself back to attention.

He recalled the first day in the royal capital, when he'd met Emilia — that dense, eventful day. In the small details, Subaru had walked a different path each time, but the broad strokes of what happened had been common across every loop. He couldn't know for sure without avoiding the major events. And thinking over the events of these days that had stuck with him, aside from day one, there was only the promise with Emilia. If he could just reach that point, the situation should fall back into the same flow as before. After that, all he had to do was ride that flow and search for whatever had dammed up its momentum.

The thoughts he hadn't been able to organize amid the hectic labor finally came together, somehow, alone in the bathhouse, and Subaru settled on a firm policy. Naturally, he noticed himself relaxing in the hot water, and surfaced at the limit of his breath. Breaking the surface, breathing in the cool air, he inhaled deeply —

"—Hey there, mind if I join you?"

A fully nude noble stood before him with his hands on his hips, and Subaru regretted breathing at all. A cool breeze blew in from beside him, passing between two legs before reaching Subaru. Of course, since he was in the bath, the tall figure was completely naked. Whether it was noble etiquette, or simply the natural bearing of a man arriving at the bathhouse, there was no sign at all of hiding the holy sword at his waist — utterly, fully naked. The nude figure stood close enough that Subaru could reach out and touch him, looking down along with the holy sword swaying in the breeze. The sensation of being looked down upon by a "thing" — it was deeply humiliating, and—

"It's reserved. I decline."

"It's a facility in my manor, and my property, you know? I ought to be allowed to use it as I please."

"Then don't ask. Just get in the bath on your own."

"Oh my, how harsh. And you don't understand — certainly, this bathhouse is without question my property, but..."

Roswaal knelt on one knee, leaning his body toward Subaru, who glared up at him with narrowed eyes from within the tub. Then, reaching out a hand, he gently pinched the chin of the unresisting Subaru, and—

"You, in your position as a servant, could also be said to be my property, couldn't you?"

"Chomp."

"No hesitation at all!"

Subaru bit the unpleasant fingers pinching his chin, fairly seriously, drawing out a yelp, then swam backward, putting distance between himself and Roswaal. The bathtub was roughly half the size of a twenty-five-meter pool. The pointlessly enormous space screamed of a noble's self-indulgent hobby and had no taste to it whatsoever, but relaxing alone in it leisurely let one bask in a meaningless sense of dominion. Which was why bath time after work had been, throughout the last loop, one of Subaru's pleasures.

"Another development I didn't expect..."

He grumbled that, then did a backward somersault in the tub. Letting his back drift along the surface, he curled his submerged body small into a cannonball crouch — executing the ultimate technique, "Hot Water Coconut." Pretending to be an inanimate object, he let his body ride the waves, staring into the water as he thought.

Over the four days of the previous loop, there hadn't been a single occasion when he and Roswaal had bathed together. Even before that — in the previous loop, Roswaal had been extremely busy, so much so that while he was in the manor, there'd been almost no chance to even see his face. Ram and Rem, at least, seemed to meet with him regularly, but as for Subaru, aside from mealtimes, the contact he'd had with Roswaal since day one could be counted on one hand.

The rhythm of soothing his fatigue with a long soak in the tub hadn't changed, so it seemed the one who'd changed was the other party.

"...Is it interesting, that?"

"It's an impression of the tropical fruit turned deadly weapon, aka the seaside slapstick assassin known as 'Mr. Coconut.' Despite that pastoral appearance, he comes in a straight nosedive from above unsuspecting tourists' heads, aiming right for the base of the skull."

"You really do have quite the mouth on you... ah, pardon me, I'll take the spot beside you."

Settling himself beside the floating Subaru, Roswaal sank into the tub and let out a long sigh. The pleasure of a hot soak was universal across worlds — proof of wordless mutual understanding. Subaru, subtly putting distance between himself and the lean frame that had naturally settled beside him, stretched out his body as if letting his guard down, and—

"So then, sir. You're bathing pretty late, huh?"

"Work piled up a bit. By the time I finished clearing it, it had gotten to this hour. Still, I'm glad we got this chance to talk like this. How was your first day?"

"I spent it meaningfully. Wanna hear the pillow talk from every muscle in my body? I bet they'll chat with you sniffling till dawn."

"A tempting offer, but tonight's pillow already has a prior engagement. Some other time."

Casually dropped like that, all Subaru could do was mutter "Damn it" in frustration. Even Subaru could tell that the relationship between this noble in front of him and the twin maids had gone past merely suspicious and into certainty. Subaru, who proclaimed himself devoted to Emilia alone and, true to that, paid no mind to any woman besides her, still didn't harbor the twisted sensibility to think, watching two lovely girls belong to someone else, "Hehe, there's something oddly thrilling about this cuckold feeling!" But neither could he muster the mature opinion of "You'd better make those two happy, you hear!" All he could manage was plain, petty jealousy.

"Right, right, are Ram and Rem doing well? The two of them have worked at the manor a long time, so they should know how to handle a junior."

Whether or not he knew what Subaru was thinking, Roswaal brought up the very names of the girls Subaru had just been picturing. Feeling as though he were being conveniently led along, Subaru nonetheless nodded "Yeah" in response to the question, and—

"With Rem-rin it's a bit rough, but I'm getting along with Ram-chi just fine. If anything, isn't Ram-chi a little too familiar? It's not about the senior-junior thing — she's been like that since I was in the position of a guest, that girl."

"From what I hear, Rem-rin handles ALL the support, and Ram-chi's the inferior version of her little sister."

Both sisters, apparently, would flatly declare the disparity in every domestic skill without hesitation. An older sister who fell one, two steps behind her younger sister in every single skill. Under normal circumstances, that seemed like a setup that would breed a crushing inferiority complex.

"When I asked about it, she shot back instantly, 'Ram is superior because Ram is the older sister.' The sheer nerve of that answer floored me."

"Speaking of nerve, I'd say you're quite something yourself... but I see. So that's how she answered. Is that so..."

Roswaal shook his head thoughtfully, glancing sidelong at Subaru. Peered into by those unreadable mismatched eyes, Subaru felt the illusion that his very heart was being seen through, and twisted his body slightly. Then—

"Pushing in so bluntly, without much reserve at all. That's a go-o-od thing."

The bath was located in a corner of the manor's basement, and the bathhouse was shared by men and women. However, the water was replaced for each bather, so there was no sense of accomplishment to be gained by, say, entering after Emilia and drinking the water.

Ram and Rem had briskly handled all that while Subaru shrank into the background, stuck peeling carrot-like vegetables. Though, not understanding the mechanism behind this "acting on mana" that had been mentioned so casually, he doubted he was in any position to help with heating the bath or cooking rice anyway.

Back home, I lived next door to a guy named Kawasaki who got praised nonstop with four-character phrase after four-character phrase — 'unmatched under heaven in a fight, a life of solid simplicity, knowledge and looks of truly striking handsomeness' — and I'm the guy who lived next to him."

In truth, the first time, she'd explain things politely and thoroughly, but from then on, if he asked the same question again, she'd look at him with the same mercilessness one might direct at a pig on a farm.

Judging that sticking around any longer would only stir up trouble, Subaru decided to make an early retreat, saying, "Well then, pardon me, senpai," and started to leave. But—

"Barusu, after this, is there anything?"

"'Anything' my ass, I'm just gonna sleep. Tomorrow's early too, obviously, damn it, senpai, mornings are seriously rough."

At Subaru's words, a hybrid of rebellious spirit and weak complaint, Ram gave a small "I see" and fell silent. As he stared at her, watching her not continue with anything further, she seemed to call out to herself with a "Right," as if reaching some kind of decision, and—

"Well then, I'll come by later, so wait in your room."

"—Huh?"

That was all she said, before walking off.
