Arc 8 Chapter 74: "Love"

—What's needed is to grasp hold of a thing without shape.

A thing that's unmistakably there, and yet the instant you try to touch it, it loses its original form, slipping past your outstretched fingertips and receding into the distance.

Like water, like wind, like light.
Like heat, like a shadow, like a dream.

The more eagerly you reach for it, the more you fear breaking it, the farther away it drifts.

"No matter how you try to touch Me, you cannot grasp Me."

The other party is right in front of him, and he is, without question, touching them—and yet he cannot reach them.
His feet planted firmly on the ground, his skin bathed in the vermillion of the setting sun dyeing the world, his eyes fixed on the one before him, the heat of the flame burning in those eyes—he can feel all of it clearly.

"—Sphinx."

He calls that name, and devours the role of the "Witch" who schemed everything, who wished to become a calamity.
The vile Authority that stole countless human lives, that bred misfortune, that concealed disaster—this time he means to use it fair and square, to seize happiness, to win back the lives of so many people.

He desperately chases after the soul of the "Witch" who resists that, a soul that, quite literally, shifts through a thousand changing forms.

"——"

By continuing to revive the Undead, the "Witch" Sphinx came to understand the nature of the soul.
Through that, having succeeded in recreating the "Witch of Greed," she gained the means to escape both the flame of the "Sun Sword" and the light of "Star Meal" by altering the nature of her own soul.
It felt like an impossibly aimless journey—like searching for a single needle dropped in a vast desert, like hunting for a spirit that dissolved into the air along with words no one ever knew, or like chasing after someone who'd flung themselves into a sea that doesn't exist in this world.

"You don't row out onto the 'Great Ocean' without a map or a compass."

A man from the same world as him calls out those words to Subaru's back, as he prepares to face the final, decisive battle of the empire's upheaval.
A face hidden behind cold iron, never shown to anyone. No doubt his eyes too—Al's eyes—were kindled with flame just like Subaru's, burning the same way.
At Al's words, Subaru becomes aware of just how precarious his own footing is.
Al's right. If he knows he's standing in a desert, gazing out over an unknown world, adrift on a great ocean, then he can't afford to grope blindly.

"—Uau."

The small hand gripping his right hand asserts its presence through warmth and touch.
The reason he can tell Spica is there isn't simply because their hands are joined. It's because, long before this contact, he collided with her again and again trying to understand her.
Over and over, trying to know the girl he couldn't understand, Natsuki Subaru has—

"The map is my heart. The compass is you two. —Spica, Rem."

The more he looks back on it, the worse those early days after being flung into the Vollachia Empire had been.
With no one to rely on, pushed away by the Rem he wanted to stay close to, clung to by the Spica he'd tried to push away, telling her not to come near—he'd kept walking, having lost his way.
But Rem was Rem even without her memories, and Spica was Spica despite her past, and no matter where he went or what he did or who he met, Subaru was nothing but Subaru.

"—Sphinx."

He calls that name, and with his hand still clasped in Spica's, he reaches to touch the soul of the "Witch."

—The power of "Star Meal" is a wish.

A power that had only ever stolen and debased, now bent to the convenient purpose of saving someone—a self-serving, dependent wish, no different from pinning one's hopes on a shooting star.
But precisely because of that, precisely because he's entrusting a wish to something never meant to carry one, he has to be sincere.
It isn't enough to simply call out a name. To know someone's name and call out to them is the same as declaring that you're joining their life, stepping up onto their stage.

"Then I'd better cut a fine figure while I do it! Life is a grand stage every single second of every single day! Since everyone is an actor and everyone has a role, everyone is obligated to speak memorable lines! Is there such a thing as a line that doesn't need to stay with you? A performance that doesn't matter? There's no such thing!"

What words could he say to a stranger that would make them turn around?
How many times would he have to reach out to a stranger before they remembered him?

If he wants the other party to look at him, if he means to touch their soul, then before anything else, two people who don't know each other have to become two people who do—there's no starting otherwise.
He knows the best way to make someone know who he is.

"My name is Emilia. Just Emilia."

That's right. Ah, that's exactly right. Not "you," not "thee." Not "I," not "Me."
—This is a dialogue between Natsuki Subaru and Sphinx.

"—Sphinx."

Not a blind call meant to grope for a soul, but a call meant to make her turn around.
At the sound, different from anything before, he can tell that the "Witch"—no, the woman—who had been walking, crossing, drifting through the vast desert, the unknown world, the great sea, as though fleeing from him, turns to face him.

"——"

Close enough to feel her breath, and yet only now, for the first time, does he meet her eyes.
No matter what expression the other party wears, ignoring their heart, you can never touch the deepest place. Such an obvious act of communication—he's going to carry it out now, in the empire's final battle.

"My name's Natsuki Subaru. It comes from the name of a star. —And you?"

Three Sun Swords aligned beyond all reason, and the giant magic crystal warrior standing in the way of the empire's tomorrow.
Both sides harbor power beyond the ordinary, and as they clash, the earthly sun that refuses to let the sinking sun claim the empire pushes back the encroaching dusk, its brilliance lighting up the capital.

"——"

The magic crystal flickers balefully, unleashing a light of ruin that stains the capital's streets black, but the sword-wolves scatter in three directions without a word exchanged, dodging with nimble grace.
As the dark light of the giant magic crystal warrior falls upon the grass and trees, upon the flowers, freezing the flow of life itself, the "King of Thorns" races up the side of a building, aiming for the leg of the inorganic, humanoid giant.
The dazzling blade swings, sweeping toward the giant's unguarded leg—and is deflected by a hard impact.

"Oh? So it had at least enough modesty to cover its bare skin."

Her ancestor the "King of Thorns'" sword is blocked, and the "Sun Princess" laughs. Her crimson eyes watch as a white garment, woven of mana, unfurls to guard the giant's massive body.
Unlike the "Steelfolk" Moguro Hagane, for the "Witch" to don clothing—perhaps she is a woman after all.

"Brace yourselves!"

In contrast to the laughing "Sun Princess," the "Wise Emperor," his expression hardened, shouts.
An instant later, a colossal arc of light sweeps out, the ripple effect of trying to hunt down the enemies darting about at its feet reshaping over a kilometer of the capital's streets.
What accomplished this was the pillar of magic crystal the giant clutched in its arm.
Once built to encircle the Crystal Palace, it now stands as tall as the giant itself, a greatsword fifty meters in blade length—in sheer scale, a weapon that yields nothing even to the "Sun Sword."
And yet—

"Well done. Flashy is exactly to my taste."

Riding the blade of the swung magic-crystal greatsword, "Sun Sword" driven in, is the "Sun Princess."
From the tip of the follow-through, over a hundred meters above the ground, she glares at the giant's head—once the mount for the magic crystal cannon, now the throne of the "Witch."
The throne has become like a closed flower bud of magic crystal; she cannot meet the "Witch's" eyes directly.
Then—until she makes the "Witch" look at her—

"I too shall show you a dazzling 'entertainment'!"

In that instant, in response to the flicker of the "Sun Sword," the magic-crystal greatsword in the giant's hand bursts into flame all at once.

Swelling purple fire, fifty meters of magic-crystal pillar turning to light, blinding the eyes of the world.
Naturally, burning the greatsword means losing her footing, and the "Sun Princess" who did it plummets straight toward the ground—until a white arm stretches up to the heavens, and a heavenly maiden with fluttering wings of flame sweeps her away.

"Well done."

The "Sun Princess's" praise brings a smile to the intervening heavenly maiden's cheek, and strength to her heart. As the two dance through the sky trailing a band of light, the giant magic-crystal warrior draws a second greatsword and moves to cut them down—

"I dedicate this single stroke to my star and to my people—"
"Do not underestimate the pinnacle of the sword-wolves!"

Aiming for the giant's slender torso, two arcs of blade-light streak upward at diagonals from the lower left and right.
The combined technique of the "King of Thorns" and the "Wise Emperor" smashes head-on through the garment meant to protect the giant's body, driving a flashing white blow mercilessly into its massive form.
The bright clang of treasured swords striking magic crystal rang out across the capital's sky like the giant's scream.

"——"

With a creaking groan the greatsword shatters, and cracks spread across the giant's entire body. It's proof that this being beyond the ordinary is being cornered—and at the same time, a cue for the giant to change its way of fighting.
Countless fissures across its whole body glow from within, and in the next instant, quite literally in every direction with no blind spot, a rain of light—no, an inescapable mist of light—is unleashed.

Were it raindrops, the "Sword Saint," the "Blue Lightning," the "Praiser" could dodge them.
But turned into droplets of mist, it becomes difficult even for those transcendent figures to evade completely.
And so—

"'Sun Sword' Vollachia!"

At one point in the sky and two points on the ground, three points in all, the crimson treasured swords blaze brighter and conjure a wall of flame. It paints over the misting rain of light that gouges away whatever it touches with a crimson flame that burns to nothing whatever it touches.
The "Witch's" destruction is blocked, but the cracks across the giant's whole body that did it widen further, and like a snake shedding old skin, the purple glow peels away, the giant's brilliance deepening a stage further.

To keep that giant magic-crystal warrior from making its next move, a wave of power different from the "Sun Sword's" spreads out—and slowly, as though drawn up by an unseen giant palm, the buildings forming the capital's streetscape lift up from the earth, ten, twenty, nearing a hundred of them, encircling the giant.
The one who did this is the heavenly maiden, turning the hand opposite the one suspending the "Sun Princess" toward the giant—

"I am the princess's dog."

"That's the right spirit."

An instant later, the storm of buildings whirls into a vortex, surging to devour the giant magic-crystal warrior's entire body.
Against that storm, the giant grips the remaining magic-crystal pillars, one in each outstretched arm, wielding them as a pair of greatswords, and spins the upper half of its massive body at high speed—a sword-dance of smashing, destroying, annihilating unfolds.
As an overwhelmingly powerful sword-dance, it doesn't just cut down the approaching mass of buildings—it becomes a tornado swallowing up the mist of light as well, and tries to sweep in triumphant procession over the capital of Lupugana.

"How many times must I witness the end of this world in a single day."

Before the unimaginable sword-storm of light, the "Wise Emperor" sighs—yet without ever weakening the gaze of his black eyes, he advances, crossing blades with the wind that tramples the capital, the empire, the world.
At the same time, the sword-flashes of the "Sun Princess" and the "King of Thorns" converge with the "Wise Emperor's" aim, halting the sword-storm's advance.

"—Guh."

Blood trickling from his bitten lip, the "Wise Emperor" holds his ground, radiance rising at his back. It appears likewise at the backs of the two others resisting the sword-storm, and the resonance of light and power begins to swallow the sword-storm in flame.
The tornado wrapped in purple light, burned by the "Sun Sword's" fire, gradually changes color. The swirling mist-rain of sword-wind turns to sparks, and eventually reshapes itself into a pillar of fire tall enough to pierce the heavens.
Excellent. That an incomplete "Sun Sword," unable to show its true worth, could accomplish this much—

"——"

Gripping the "Sun Sword" tightly, the "Wise Emperor" narrows his eyes at the rising pillar of fire, before the blaze burning brilliantly, its intensity growing stronger still.
In the midst of battle, he knows full well it's foolish to stop and spare thought for anything else.
And in that very instant—

"—I do so love you."

Breaking through the burnt-out sword-storm of light, without a moment's respite, the giant magic-crystal warrior lunges out. The blow of the greatsword it swings is kicked back by a leaping pair of high geta sandals.
A beat late, caught in the piercing gust of wind, robes whirling, hovering in the air is the "Kaleidoscope," flames kindled in her eyes, switching her beauty and her dance in a dazzling instant.

"——"

Still targeting the "Kaleidoscope" as she hovers helplessly in midair, the giant swings the other greatsword, the one not knocked back, in a sweeping arc. But that too fails to connect.

"I won't let you touch my star so easily."

The "King of Thorns," cutting in, sweeps the "Kaleidoscope" up in a sideways embrace, and receiving the blow head-on with the "Sun Sword" held vertically, burns clean through the greatsword's blade.
The epilogue of "Iris and the King of Thorns," reborn in the present age—the severed tip of the greatsword bounces off the ground and flies on, scarring the capital from north to south.

The instant attention turned to the south of the capital—a white light surged north through the city.

"——!!"

In the southern sky, a dragon wreathed in cloud, its white wings beating, roars.
The "Cloud Dragon," who fought only for the sake of its beloved, disregarding all notions of life and death, has changed its allegiance, breathing forth breath in defiance of ruin.

Struck by the direct hit, the giant magic-crystal warrior staggers back. Staggering, it drives its sharp feet into the streets to hold its ground, resonating with its remaining magic-crystal pillar—the pillar shatters with a shrill ring into countless small swords, which, like the white garment before it, begin forming a defensive array around the giant.

Still, the fighting spirit of the "Witch," the "Great Disaster," does not break. Then neither will the others.

"——"

Truly at the center of the Vollachia Empire's all-out war, the "Wise Emperor" turns his awareness, if only faintly, outward.
A man who cannot help but join a scene where someone is staking their life, however small his strength, however aware he is of his own frailty—has not shown himself.

"No doubt you too have flung yourself into some reckless battle."

Carrying a certain expectation and trust in those words, the "Wise Emperor," his treasured sword's radiance intensifying, glares at the giant magic-crystal warrior growing ever more ominous, and steps forward.

"Onward, my chosen 'Generals.' —We are but the specks of dust in the eye of the 'Great Disaster.'"

—Natsuki Subaru and Spica, the two of them, face off against the "Witch" Sphinx.

Al's mission was to keep the enemy from drawing near, so that nothing could interrupt the desperate struggle of Subaru and the others to catch that tenacious, stubborn soul of the "Witch."

"Yeah, come on! Nobody can beat me and the princess!"

Roaring to keep himself charged up, Al keeps swinging his improvised dadao.
Behind Al, Sphinx sits slumped against the barrier wall, and in front of her, Subaru sits cross-legged, planted squarely, continuing to face her head-on.

What Subaru was trying to do together with Spica was an idea beyond even Al's imagination.
Relying on the Authority of "Gluttony"—an outrageous scheme; when he first heard it, he questioned Subaru's sanity. But in the end, Subaru judged he could do it, and Priscilla ordered him to.
If it leads to a future, Al will support it with everything he has. He'll see it through.

"Ul Goa."

Above Al's head, Roswaal, flying freely through the air, rains down fire.
Even if it isn't the highest tier of magic, unleashing that much magic in rapid succession is far from ordinary. And yet even that abnormal magical prowess can't overwhelm the opponent, not this time.

"Ul Huma."

Countering the pouring rain of fire is a barrage of water shells launched from the ground into the sky.
Both in sheer volume and range of effect, this is a wielder of world-class caliber, a magical battle at a level you simply couldn't witness in the present age—a mage and a "Witch," each turning the world to their own side, each making the world betray itself, each using the world as fickle as they please.

Though Priscilla loudly proclaimed her presence, not every "Witch" is drawn to her like moths to a lamp. Some of the "Witches" saw through the fact that the "Sun Sword" wasn't the only thing they ought to fear, and had come to this battlefield.
But even so—

"Then so be it, I suppose!"

Appearing to be a young girl, moving like an athlete, Beatrice, her right eye ablaze, activates shadow magic.
In an instant, both raised hands generate a pale purple haze across the surrounding area, slowing the movements not only of the invading "Witches" but also of the Undead charging in mindlessly, driven purely by instinct, obeying orders.
Al seizes on Beatrice's fine assist with gusto.

"Blame your unlucky stars!"

The exquisitely controlled magic weakens the enemies, granting Al a window of invincibility all his own. Cutting down the wretched Undead one after another, Al winks at Beatrice through his helmet in thanks for the support.
Instantly, Beatrice's color drains, even though she shouldn't have been able to see the wink at all.

"That's bad, I suppose!"

"Huh?" Al turns at her voice, and sees something hurtling toward them with tremendous force—a purple-glowing, ten-meter-scale chunk of debris, bouncing along the ground.

Al has no way of knowing that it's the severed tip of the greatsword the giant magic-crystal warrior—the one Priscilla and the others are fighting—had wielded. What he does know is that a direct hit from that debris would grind him and Subaru's group behind him into mincemeat—

"Carry them—"

He flees, taking Subaru's group with him, out of the line of the incoming debris.
No telling how many attempts it would take, but there's no time to calculate that. In any case, trial and error on the front line of death beats armchair theorizing every time.

"Updating the matrix, thought experiment—"
"—Don't you dare give up on your own accord!!"

In that instant, a mighty arm crosses past Al and slams into the debris head-on, meeting it in combat.
A roaring boom and a fierce gust blast past behind him, but the incoming debris is stopped dead in its tracks. The one who pulled it off is Garfiel, sharp canines bared and gnashing.

"Sorry I'm late, Beatrice! Boss man…!"

"You made it just in time, I suppose! Protecting Subaru and Spica is the win condition, I suppose!"

"Ha! Easy enough to understand! Like they say, 'if you wanna whisper sweet nothings, take a page from the King of Thorns!'"

Kicking away the massive chunk of debris he'd stopped, Garfiel knocks his fists together in front of his chest. His eyes too are burning, not as a figure of speech, and his whole body brims with fighting spirit in resonance with his battle-lust.

"—Requires: exit."

But a quiet voice announces the "Witch's" entry into the fight, and as if struck, Al and the others look up to see a sky swirling with countless lights. Some manner of gravity seems to pull at the light-vortex; surrounding buildings and rubble lift up, get swallowed into the vortex, ground down, and turned to dust of light.
That light-dust itself carries a sense of destructive force, and with a single word from the "Witch" it's about to level the ground below—

"—Halibel-sama, if you would, please."

"Sure thing. I like cheerin' on a kid who's tryin' her best."

Two different voices are suddenly heard, and in the next instant, a cannonball in a kimono—no, a Deer Human girl—goes flying.
The girl spins in midair and drives a kick into the "Witch's" back, right as she's about to release the shower of light-dust. With the force and power of that kick, the poor "Witch" is smashed clean in two.

"Yeah, yeah, not bad at all. With a kid like you around, the empire's future is safe."

With that same momentum, the girl, nearly swept into the light vortex herself, is saved in the nick of time by a jet-black Wolf Human.
The wolf-man neutralizes the "Witch's" prepared light-vortex with a swift piercing hand-thrust, and with that same hand catches the girl gently, landing on the ground.
It's confusing—the same wolf-man both threw the girl and caught her.

"A new monster's been added! Is this a Cecilus-type thing?!"

"Can't deny it, but I don't love that category."

"It's been a while. If I may be so bold, comparing Cecilus-sama and Halibel-sama would be discourteous—their characters are entirely different. —Shall we protect Schwarz-sama?"

"O-oh. Quick on the uptake! And any strong ones joining are more than welcome. Let's protect my brother together!"

"Sure thing. Seems like we're gettin' close to the finale, an' we've been given quite the gift, after all."

The kimono-clad girl he'd seen in the Divine Capital—Tanza—introduces the wolf-man as Halibel. In both their eyes, Priscilla's Blessing burns like flame; there's nothing more reassuring.
On top of that—

"Whoever it belongs to, I don't know, but that's a nicely-timed gift, I suppose."

So saying, Beatrice presses her small palm flat against the giant chunk of debris. —What Garfiel caught wasn't just rubble; it's a mass of magic crystal.
Beatrice breaks it down into mana in a single breath, and roughly a hundred swords of purple-blue crystal spring up all around at once, and in time with the motion of her lifting hand, a sword-dance begins.

"Such an abundance of mana, going to waste—Betty might as well put it to use, I suppose. If any of you so much as scratch Subaru, it's an unforgiving punishment, I suppose."

"In that case, I'll be the one to administer Beatrice-sama's punishment myself."

Watching sparks fly between Beatrice's and Tanza's gazes, Al shrugs.
—Seems like keeping Subaru unscathed had better be added to the matrix's update conditions too, he thinks.

And so, protected by Al and the others, Subaru remains in a state of extreme concentration.

It's not that he's unconscious, nor that he's dreaming. And yet the battle unfolding around him lies outside his awareness, and his own sense of self isn't located within the capital at all.
Right now, the whereabouts of the being called Natsuki Subaru isn't the battle-torn capital, but a white world containing nothing but himself, Spica whose hand he holds, and Sphinx facing him from the front.

—It was a space that could be mistaken for that same "Corridor of Memories."

"For me, it's mostly a place of bad memories."

A place he arrived at unintentionally, where his "memories" were forcibly devoured.
He put Emilia and Beatrice through terrible pain here too, and it's a fresh memory how thoroughly he wrecked Ram and Julius.
Above all, this is the place where "Natsuki Subaru" once had to disappear.

"But that's exactly why all of me is here."

"Uh?"

"You don't need to know this place. For you it's just collateral damage. That's fine as is."

He smiles at Spica's puzzled face, then lets out a long, deep breath.
In this white, hushed space, the reason Subaru faces both Spica and Sphinx here isn't simply because all their names happen to start with the same syllable.
It's because all of them are deeply bound to a "self" other than his own, and he wanted to talk with them, just the three of them, undisturbed.

"Sphinx is the name of a monster that existed in the Architect's knowledge."

"Hm? Oh, so that's where the name comes from."

"Yes. Had the Architect's original wish been granted from the start, I would have been named Echidna. Since that didn't happen, another name became necessary."

Facing him, Sphinx explains the origin of her name with a calm that surprises Subaru.
Sphinx—the one Subaru knows is a monster with a lion's body and a human head. She guards a pyramid, and has the reputation of loving riddles.

"In the morning, four legs; at noon, two legs; at night, three legs… you know what that is?"

"—? Isn't it a monster? Or, a shapeshifting monster."

"That's what I'd think too. By the way, the answer's a human."

He offers the classic riddle, and gets sour looks from both Sphinx and Spica. As if to say they can't accept it—but complaints should be directed at whichever other Sphinx came up with the question.
In any case—

"Thanks for telling me. In return, there's something I want to show you too."

"Something you want to show Me?"

"Yeah. —Though whether you'll be happy about it is another matter."

With that preface, Subaru gives a small laugh, then glances briefly at Spica. As if to ease his unease, she lifts their still-joined hands and says, "U!"
It's Spica's own way of cheering him on—she who came to terms with her "self" a step ahead of Subaru.

—Inside the "Corridor of Memories," closing his eyes, he can smell his own scent strongly.

He's heard that memory and smell are closely linked. Many memories are tied to particular scents, and that might be why the "Corridor of Memories" carries that same impression.
Either way, tracing his own scent, walking through the unreal "Corridor of Memories," Subaru slowly tries to reel in something uncertain, to make it certain.

Across countless "Returns by Death," Olbart—who had lost both arms—apologized lightly, saying it would be difficult to undo the "Infantilization." —So instead of an apology, Subaru asked him to teach him instead.
The vessel and the soul want to return. All that's left is to guide them back, using the scent as a trail.
If he does that, then—

"—My name is Natsuki Subaru, the one and only penniless nobody under heaven."

The voice cracking that same dumb joke grows a little deeper, a little lower. The height of his gaze shifts, and the feel of Spica's hand in his suddenly seems smaller.
But it's the opposite. Spica hasn't shrunk. —Subaru has changed.

"——"

A sensory change. The soul had been ceaselessly trying to return to its original shape.
The emotion of that being fulfilled spreads through every cell of Subaru's body, quite literally, as growing pains of heart and soul completing the passage from child to adult, traveling through blood vessels and nerves.

And so, having reclaimed himself in a true and certain sense, Subaru once again—no, for the very first time—faces Sphinx directly, head-on.

"—Nice to meet you, Natsuki Subaru."

Reading Subaru's intent, having witnessed the change, Sphinx offers a first-meeting greeting. Hearing it, Subaru thinks: this one might actually be someone he could talk to.
With his head and body restored, with heart and soul made whole, Natsuki Subaru thought exactly that.

"But we have to settle this."

"Yes, that's right. —Requires: parting of ways."

"…Why?"

"Why, what?"

"Why did you try to do it all by yourself?"

That was Subaru's honest question.
The "Witch" Sphinx had caused the "Great Disaster" that would destroy the Vollachia Empire, toyed with countless lives, done a mountain of things that surely couldn't be forgiven. But did it have to be that way?
If Sphinx's purpose had been nothing but the recreation of the Architect—the "Witch of Greed."

"You must have had other ways to go about it. You got the method wrong. If you hadn't gotten the method wrong, we—"

"Could have avoided fighting altogether? That's impossible. Requires: reconsideration."

"Why!"

"Because there's a reason within Me, distinct from recreating the Architect—one that's certainly there."

Looking down at herself, Sphinx tells him the decisive difference with a faint smile.
That smile—Subaru can see it as proof that Sphinx is blessing Sphinx herself, and he finds he can't press her any further.

But at the same time, Natsuki Subaru finds something, clearly and certainly.

"Uau."

"Yeah, I know. —Thanks, Spica."

Spica calls out to Subaru, who has bitten his lip. She must want to react to the fact that he's returned to his original size, but she holds it back, considerate of him instead.

He wants to answer that feeling. —For that, even if it means falling into hell.

"—Sphinx."

"What is it? Whatever you may be, I—"

"Happy birthday."

So saying, he reaches out and touches Sphinx, whose eyes widen at a blessing she's never heard before.
And then—

Basking in a pleasant, dusk-scented breeze, Priscilla applauds her own existence.

Using the mechanism of the Crystal Palace that had once been a "Meteor," her heart raced at the rampage of the giant magic-crystal warrior Sphinx raised up, and the banquet against Vincent's assembled "Generals."
A dream that never wakes, a performance that never ends, a story without an "ending"—she wants to keep watching, forever.
But such a childish "ego" isn't fitting for the closing act of this battle—

"I shall lower the curtain that befits me."

So saying, Priscilla releases her grip on Arakiya's hand as they fly above the clouds, and falls toward the sky.
Falling straight down, Priscilla sheathes the "Sun Sword" in its sky-sheath, and with both arms spread wide, bathing her whole body in the setting sun, she closes her eyes.

Unseen, the full might of those who share the flame of her soul comes through like sweet nothings whispered from the world itself.

Arakiya, wreathed in a band of light, drives back the "Witch's" magic; the dragon armored in white cloud holds the damage back from spreading beyond the city. Yorna, reunited with her beloved and standing beside him in loving harmony, is busy shattering the earth with extravagant displays of affection, heedless of her daughter's watching eyes.
And then, the unworthy elder brother, Vollachia's emperor, Vincent Vollachia—

"You're late."

He speaks that short line, and lifts his black eyes, unafraid, to the giant magic-crystal warrior before him. The giant raises both magic-crystal greatswords in its two arms, poised to cleave through the capital and the empire's summit together.
Into that moment, lightning bursts through from behind Vincent—

"—Let the heavenly Observers bear witness. Which the world shall choose… no! That it shall choose me!"

Two strokes—white sword-light and black slashing-light, a blade-flash that could sever even the light of the stars and the logic of curses—streak across the sky.
It severs both arms of the giant magic-crystal warrior, the harbinger of the capital's annihilation, clean from the shoulders. Amid a roaring boom and billowing smoke, the glow of magic crystal turns to dust, and the flicker of the "Dream Sword" and the "Wicked Sword" cut off its existence.

Even so, the armless giant swings the countless swords hovering around it, trying to cut down the empire's will to resist.
Its aim naturally includes Priscilla, still in freefall.

"—Priscilla Barielle."

Light of ruin so forceful it seems to pierce through closed eyelids, repainting the very color of the sky. Sensing its approach bearing down on her, Priscilla opens her eyes and stretches both arms out to either side.

In the next instant, the "Sun Sword" thrown by the "Wise Emperor" lands in Priscilla's right hand, and the one thrown by the "King of Thorns" in her left, simultaneously, and the swung twin blades burn away the magic-crystal swords the "Witch" unleashed in a single stroke. The chain-reaction of burning swords swirls around the giant magic-crystal warrior, a curtain of crimson flame covering the sky.

"——"

For a moment, Priscilla's form vanishes from everyone's sight—and in an instant, the form of the "Sun Princess," breaking through the curtain of flame, closes in straight on the giant's head, on the closed magic-crystal bud.
What accomplished this was the third "Sun Sword"—launched from its own sky-sheath, Priscilla flies riding her own "Sun Sword," her brother's and her ancestor's "Sun Swords" held one in each hand.

—The "Sun Sword" carrying Priscilla, its tip, drives into the magic-crystal bud, breaking through the closed petals and thrusting inside.

"—Priscilla Barielle."

In that instant, she cuts down with the "King of Thorns'" "Sun Sword" a barrage of light-orbs surging to meet her.
Flame fills her vision entirely, and Priscilla, landing on the floor, advances.

"—Priscilla Barielle."

At the same moment, leaping over the flame that filled her sight, Sphinx bursts out, a sword of light in hand.
Their gazes cross, and Priscilla burns through her opponent's sword with the "Wise Emperor's" "Sun Sword."

"—Priscilla Barielle."

Priscilla smiles at Sphinx, whose eyes burn with passion as she calls out her name again and again. Catching that smile in her sight, Sphinx leaps back, readying both hands.
Ten fingers, left and right, birth ten blades of light that rampage wildly through the bud, refracting unpredictably off the inside of the magic crystal, a beautiful kaleidoscope of "death" flashing.

At that chaotic, emotional glimmer, Priscilla stretches both hands toward the heavens.
And gripping the hilt of the treasured sword thrust down from above—

"—Priscilla Barielle!"

"That is my name."

At that ringing cry, hostility burns with unmistakable heat within it. Straight at that, Priscilla brings the blazing, brilliantly shining "Sun Sword" down.

—A single crimson flash, whether dawn or daybreak, a light suggesting a new sunrise, casts its heat over life. The magic-crystal bud is lit from within, and blooms.

In other words, it is the budding of a soul—

"Take pride, Sphinx."

"——"

"You fulfilled the role of my antagonist as none other than yourself. —Well done."

Rising from the follow-through of her swung "Sun Sword," Priscilla addresses Sphinx standing before her. At that, Sphinx narrows her black eyes, and,

"…Even though I fell short?"

"Naturally. Who do you take me for?"

Scoffing at the question, Priscilla answers so.
At that answer, Sphinx's brows droop, and with the sharpness gone from her face, she lets out a breath, and,

"Priscilla Barielle. —This is I, the mortal enemy of the 'Great Disaster' Sphinx."

So she smiles, as one who has found meaning in existence apart from the reason for which she was originally created.

—That was the resolution between the "Sun Princess" Priscilla Barielle and the "Great Disaster" Sphinx.

△▼△▼△▼△

Once before, she had come close to being destroyed.

Even then, there had been a purpose left unfulfilled, but she had calmly accepted ending without reaching it, and the inside of her chest had been quiet as a night after sunset, and worthless.
Turn that around—what about now?

"Just like back then, I'm going to vanish without reaching my wish, and yet—"

Her heart, whether dawn or daybreak, waits eagerly for the rising sun rather than the setting one, like a small child; a certain "heat" is kindled in her chest, she can feel it, making a body that shouldn't have a pulse, pulse.
And that, she has been shown, was the true identity of that dreadful "heat" that had shone so blindingly in the cold, parched eyes of the monster in that place she had been tethered to for decades upon decades.

"Thank you. For teaching me what I am. —Requires: gratitude."

Formless, uncertain, ambiguous—her self has now been established as "herself," and though it's far from what she'd originally thought she had to become, it doesn't feel unpleasant.
She can say it again, clearly. This being born as a monster is not a substitute for the "Witch of Greed."
So, vanishing now without fulfilling her own singular wish, she says this instead:

"—Ah, how frustrating."

△▼△▼△▼△

"—Sphinx."

Subaru calls that name, and Spica, having touched the soul, devours its essence.

The moment "Star Meal" activates, a quiet sense of accomplishment and satisfaction wells up in Subaru, along with, at the same time, a sense of loneliness. Perhaps it's something close to the loneliness of a meal ending amid unceasing laughter.
Truly full, and yet just the slightest bit wanting—a moment like that.

"——"

He celebrated the birth of a single life, and with that same mouth helped devour her.
But it wasn't out of hatred. He didn't want to torment her, didn't want to disappoint her. There was simply no other way for this to end.
For Subaru and the others, and for Sphinx, this was the only ending that could ever have been.

"U!"

Sphinx crumbles softly into dust and fades away. Spica, who saw it through to the end together with him, calls out to Subaru, who witnessed her final moment.
He has to say something to Spica too—thanks, words of comfort.

"And I should tell Abel and the others that the enemy's leader is gone—"

He's saying that, about to stand, when—

"—Subaru."

That voice, heard out of nowhere, ignores his eardrums, ignores his skull, and resonates directly in his brain.
It's always like this. That beautiful voice, like a silver bell, always strips Natsuki Subaru bare. It turns the exposed, naked Subaru around, and brings with it an urge that makes him want to cry.

"——"

Unable even to speak, Subaru stands and turns, and the sunset light strikes his eyes. That sunset, which should be bringing the afterglow of victory, is an annoyance right now. —In that light, there's a face he wants to see.
Long silver hair that seems to have captured droplets of the night sky's moon, purple-blue eyes—the most beautiful jewels in this world—from the top of her head to the tips of her toes, there isn't a single part he doesn't find dear.

"Uau."

Spica, releasing his hand, pats the back of Subaru, who's on the verge of tearing up at the surge of emotion.
Given a cue and courage by the touch of that palm, Subaru breaks into a run.

He passes by Al giving him a thumbs-up, is seen off by Halibel exhaling purple smoke, pats the shoulder of Garfiel, who's bawling his eyes out on Subaru's behalf, pokes the cheek of Beatrice, who's holding back the urge to leap at him, wryly smiles at how very Tanza it is that she bows deeply, and has his jacket draped over him by Roswaal, descending from the sky.

"Subaru-kun, are you not stark naked?"

Because of his size returning, Subaru's clothes, sized for his child self, are mismatched and ill-fitting. In his original world, this would be the situation of having no clothes to wear to go buy clothes.
But this isn't his original world. This is the other world where Subaru is living now.

"Suba—"

She, running toward him from the other direction, tries to open her mouth to speak, but Subaru, without slowing his momentum, silences her by embracing her from the front.
Her slender body, held tight in arms that are properly his own size once more—

"—E, M, T."

"…Idiot."

Embraced head-on by Subaru, Emilia, a little startled, smiles wryly, as if she can't help it.
Their reunion in the Vollachia Empire had of course made his heart soar too. Being able to touch her again after their separation, Subaru had felt his feelings for her, from the heart.

But still, it's different. —With this heart and this body and this soul all together, that's Natsuki Subaru.

"Welcome back, Subaru."

"Yeah. —I'm home, Emilia-tan."

Emilia says this, having sensed that same fullness in Subaru's soul.
And beyond the two of them, sharing the joy of their reunion, the Crystal Palace blazes, brilliant and burning.

—That was the closing ceremony, long, long in coming, of the Vollachia Empire's "Great Disaster."
