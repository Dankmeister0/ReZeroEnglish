Arc 8 Chapter 23: "Rui"

Truly, Natsuki Subaru thought, theirs had been a strange relationship.
Not so much strange as vicious—a chance encounter that had led Subaru and the others to this place.

An empire whose every principle, whose every way of being, he detested utterly: the Vollachian Empire.
A Rem without her "memories," whom he'd wanted so badly to ask why, wanted to weep.
A Sin Archbishop of "Gluttony," Rui Arneb, whom he'd hated as your fault, all of it your fault.

Set up in a situation where there was nothing to do but curse it, grieving and raging along the rails that had been laid down for him, colliding again and again—Subaru had died and died and survived through these days.

People he had touched, people who had helped each other—the Vollachian Empire, too.
A Rem without her "memories," whose kindness and depth of care were, painfully, still just the same.
A Sin Archbishop of "Gluttony," Rui Arneb, who had devotedly, desperately served him, risking her life again and again.

Savoring the curse that had been set up for him, refusing to go along the rails that had been laid down, hurting each other gently even as they helped each other—Subaru had died and died and survived through these days.

Should he curse, grieve, hate? Or refuse to curse, refuse to grieve, and forgive?
The time had come when that relationship, left ambiguous for so long, had to be settled.

"——"

At Subaru's declaration on entering the guest room, a sense of the air drawing taut spread through it.
In the room were Rem and Rui, along with Ram and Tanza—four people in all. The former two as the parties concerned, the latter two as witnesses—Subaru had wanted to speak with all of them present.
Emilia, Beatrice, and the rest of the group were waiting for this to be settled. Waiting for whatever answer Natsuki Subaru arrived at, whatever shape it took.

"...Did you finish your important talk with Abel-san?"

While Subaru was still considering how to begin, it was Rem who spoke first.
She had Rui seated beside her, holding her hand—whether consciously or not, it seemed almost like a kind of warning shot. To that question, Subaru shook his head.

"We're on hold for now. This has to get settled before that conversation can move forward. ...That talk with Abel, it's got to do with Rui too."

"Aー u?"

"Why does it involve Rui-chan?"

"Because of a prophecy from someone called a 'Stargazer'—it came down to Rui's existence being important for winning this fight."

"—!"

Rem's expression tightened bitterly as Subaru laid out the premise without holding anything back. Still holding Rui's hand, Rem fixed her pale blue eyes on Subaru and said, "Then—"

"Are you saying you want to make Rui-chan fight? She's this small, a child who still doesn't understand anything, and you'd do something so cruel to her..."

"Whether fighting is necessary or not, that's a question for later. But let me say one thing first, about what came before it. —That Rui is a child who doesn't understand anything, that's wrong."

"What are you..."

"Even if it's hard for her to communicate in words, Rui properly understands the situation she's in. She can choose who she wants to side with, and who she doesn't. And knowing all that, she's here."

"That's..."

At Subaru's quiet words, Rem looked down, at a loss for words.
Rem, too, knew that Subaru's counter was the truth. If it weren't, Rui simply wouldn't be here in the first place.
From the moment she'd been flung into the Vollachian Empire, in a situation she couldn't have understood, clinging to Subaru and Rem—the first people she'd met—like some kind of imprinting. To explain it away with something so simple would be absurd, given the outrageous, life-or-death situations they'd all lived through together.

If the imprinting wasn't affection but simply a defense instinct seeking a protector for her own survival, then it went without saying that staying with Subaru and Rem was a net negative for her survival.
If all she wanted was to survive, Rui would have had an easier time of it away from them.

"Ram agrees with Barusu on that point. Rem's only told me bits and pieces of what happened, but the fact that that child is here, apart from both Rem and Barusu—that's clearly a choice she made herself."

"Sister..."

"Don't misunderstand, Rem. Ram is entirely on Rem's side, and has already made up her mind to help tear Barusu limb from limb afterward, but Ram won't twist the facts to say it."

"There was one part of that declaration I can't let slide, but—thank you."

To Ram's interjection, Subaru offered thanks somewhere between gratitude and a grimace.
He sensed that this, too, was a kind of warning shot from Ram's side. At the same time, it was also a statement that Ram intended to engage with this matter fairly, not simply take Rem's side emotionally.
It was exactly the kind of behavior that matched the evaluation of Ram that Rem herself so often voiced.

"Sister, you're... too kind, aren't you..."

Whether or not she heard the mutter that barely escaped Subaru's mouth, Ram said nothing, only gave a small snort through her nose, crossed her slender legs, and put her lips to her teacup.
Beside her, Tanza, who had seemed somewhat out of place, looked at Subaru with her dark eyes and said,

"Like Ram-sama, I will not take Schult—Subaru-sama's side either. I can't gauge the full circumstances, so if you placed me here thinking I'd be an ally, please don't be mistaken."

"I know. Tanza's always properly hard on me. I wasn't expecting you to take my side."

"——"

"Huh? Did that make you a little annoyed? Why?"

Following Ram's lead, Tanza had declared herself a neutral witness—and now her expression turned faintly displeased.
Tanza showed little change in expression, but this displeased look was one of the more common ones. It usually came out in response to Cecilus's reckless words and deeds, or when Hiain said one word too many.
And, at a fairly high frequency, it was also directed at Subaru.

In any case, thanks to Ram and Tanza staking out their positions, the groundwork was set.
With that done, Subaru turned once more to face the two who truly mattered here: Rem and Rui.
And then—

"Rui, I'm about to tell you something important now. About you—everything in my gut, held back for nothing. Don't run away. Will you listen?"

"...Aー u!"

"Yeah. Good girl. Thanks."

After a moment's hesitation, Rui nodded, her voice brightening.
That reaction, too, was proof that she properly understood what was being said around her. Rem glanced sidelong at Rui's profile, her eyes wavering.
Rem, hoping to find there the emotion she wished for, would have used even the slightest sign of Rui's reluctance as a reason to stop this conversation.
But in Rui's profile, she could find no reason to do so.

"I think both Rem and Rui understand already. That from the very start, I've been wary of Rui, kept her at a distance... hated her, the whole time."

"——"

"That's the biggest reason Rem doubted me so relentlessly at first, right? Trying so hard to keep the two of you apart that she even broke my finger."

"That time... I went too far too."

"It's fine. Looking back now, that's a good memory between me and Rem."

"Huh?"

Rem gave Subaru a look questioning his sanity as he answered while bending the fingers of his left hand. Not just Rem—Ram and Tanza wore the same expression, so before his heart could be broken over a story about a broken finger, Subaru changed the subject with an "anyway."

"I was seriously, thoroughly wary of Rui. And Rem was seriously, thoroughly suspicious of me because of it. I don't know how Rui felt through all that, but you could feel the tension in the air, right?"

"Uー?"

"After we got separated once and reunited with everyone in Shudrak, and after we fled back from Guaral once, and around the time of the battle to retake Guaral after that—it was like that the whole time."

So the gulf between Subaru and Rem had only widened, harder and harder to bridge.
There was no doubt that a large part of why Subaru had come to resent Rui was that her presence had caused his relationship with Rem to sour.

But that ill feeling toward Rui changed after he and Rem went their separate ways, when he headed to the demon city of Chaosflame, and things happened there.
Spending time with Rui without Rem as an intermediary, watching her try to protect Subaru with all her desperate might, little by little, bit by bit, Subaru had been forced to acknowledge her.
That was why, after getting separated in the demon city and reuniting with Beatrice in the capital, he'd been able to feel honest joy that Rui was there too. By then, that initial ill will was nowhere to be found.

"If we could've just gone on like that, not thinking too hard about it, I think it would've been easier. But that's just not possible. It's like a wound. Some wounds heal if you leave them alone, but some just keep getting worse if you leave them. This is a wound you can't leave alone."

To heal a wound, you have to treat it.
And treatment doesn't always mean relying on magic or medicine—sometimes it calls for bold methods.
This, too, was the kind of wound that demanded that.
So—

"I never once said it, until now. Why I hated you, Rui—why I hated you."

"Uau..."

Staring steadily into those blue eyes, Subaru laid his feelings bare.
As if answering Subaru's earnest gaze and voice, Rui didn't look away either. Whatever she was told, she was resolved to accept it—that resolve was in the girl.
Instead—

"...Please stop."

It was Rem who said that, biting her lip, her voice trembling.
She said it, her voice shaking as if pleading, still holding Rui's hand. No—that wasn't quite right. It wasn't Rui whose hand was being held.
It was Rem. It was Rem whose hand Rui was holding—that much was clear.

"I don't want to hear it. I don't care what your reason for hating Rui-chan was. Isn't it enough that you're just heartless?"

Shaking her head weakly, reluctantly, Rem rejected Subaru's words.
Avoiding tightening the hand she'd laid over Rui's, she instead gripped her other hand so hard her bones creaked, trying to shut out what was to come.
He wanted to respect Rem's feelings here. He wanted to grant whatever Rem wished for.

"I'm sorry. But I can't let you close your ears to this either."

But it was no good.
He couldn't grant that wish of Rem's, couldn't respect it.
All Rem had was the choice to flee this room and not listen. If she truly chose to do that, Subaru had no right to stop her.

But Rem understood too.
To shut her ears to Subaru's words here meant turning her back on her own missing "memories"—

"——"

—and it meant betraying the feelings of Ram, who was silently watching over her.

"Even so, I don't want to... I don't want to hear it...!"

Squeezing her eyes shut, clenching her teeth, Rem did not choose to flee from here.
But she voiced her rejection of what lay beyond Subaru's words, again and again, with all her strength.
Struck in the chest by those words, Subaru still did not take his eyes off Rui.

"Uau."

Rui's lips moved, giving voice to a sound that wasn't quite words.
Subaru understood perfectly well that the sound was calling out his name—"Subaru."
Because he understood it—

"Rui, the reason I hated... resented you was—"

"Stop it...!"

"—because you're the one who took Rem's 'memories.' You're the Sin Archbishop of 'Gluttony.'"

—because he understood, Subaru had to answer it.

△▼△▼△▼△

He'd said it. The words he'd left unsaid, at last.
The moment he spoke them, there was none of the satisfaction of confessing a secret, none of the cathartic relief of denouncing a hated enemy.
What there was, was anguish—the weight and bitterness of it only deepening even as he unburdened what he'd been carrying in his chest.
And what followed accelerated that even further.

"Waaaaah——!!"

Letting out a high cry, her face crumpling, Rem's hand shot out.
Her hand seized Subaru by the collar, and with tremendous force he was shoved down, mounted with no chance to resist.
Even without his body being small, he wouldn't have stood a chance—and being small now made it even worse.
If Rem put her full strength into those arms, Subaru's mouth would be sealed shut in an instant.

But Rem hadn't done it—not until that decisive sentence had come out.
And even now, in this moment of straddling Subaru—

"Why... why are you...!"

Wringing out her voice, her face crumpling with violent emotion, glaring down at Subaru's face from a distance close enough to feel his breath, she still didn't try to use that arm's force.
Even with her emotions erupting, Rem stayed rational, right up to that final line.

"——"

Feeling Rem's hot breath on his face, Subaru raised a hand to hold back the others.
The instant he'd been pushed down, Tanza had reflexively moved to protect him—he'd seen, at the edge of his vision, Ram holding her arm back.
From his side too, Subaru signaled that it was fine, and looked up at Rem before him.

Drip by drip, from Rem's pale blue eyes, tears fell onto Subaru's cheek.
Not anger, not sorrow, but an eye that held a deep, wrenching helplessness—and the reason she'd held onto that final line of reason. That was what those tears carried.
It was—

"Even without you telling me, I already knew... I knew Rui-chan had something to do with the 'memories' I can't remember...!"

"Rem..."

"Because there's no other explanation, is there? The reason you kept pushing Rui-chan away, again and again trying to keep her from me—it can only be that being near Rui-chan is dangerous for me. There's no other reason, is there...!"

Her breath, her voice, her eyes—all of it trembling weakly, Rem poured out her heart.
Hearing that, Subaru closed his eyes. It was only natural.
Rem herself, of all people, was the one who had suffered the loss of her "memories."
There was no way she could care less about her own "memories" than Subaru or anyone else did.
Of course she, more than anyone, had been yearning, agonizing, over where her "memories" had gone.

"Of course I'd notice... Do you think I'm stupid? Maybe I am. A stupid woman who doesn't know anything. I don't know anything about you either! I don't even want to know! And yet you go barging in without asking...! I hate you. I hate you so much!"

"——"

"You're nothing compared to Rui-chan. Rui-chan has always been with me, always cared for me... and Rui-chan is my—"

The tears that kept spilling without end struck not only Subaru's cheek but ran down Rem's own cheeks and chin, adorning and disguising both her body and her heart with their droplets.
Knowing the truth, and still wishing to remain not knowing it.

"It's all... it's all some kind of mistake... it's all a lie you're telling..."

"—Rem."

"—Ah."

As she weakly let those words spill out, Rem's eyes shimmered with tears, and when a large droplet fell, her blurred vision must have momentarily cleared, and there, she must have seen Subaru's face.
Pinned beneath her, Subaru gently cupped Rem's face in both hands, fixing her gaze on himself.
Black eyes and blue eyes met, and Subaru spoke to that beloved face, soaked through with tears.

"It's not a lie. Everything you thought was true. Rui was our enemy."

"—! An enemy, what do you mean? Is it because she did something to my... my 'memories'? Then... then!"

Shaking her head hard, Rem pulled away from Subaru's hands and sat up. Still like that, she turned to look at Rui, who remained standing where she had been, and said,

"Then I'll... I'll forgive Rui-chan. I forgive her, so isn't that enough? See, that settles everything, doesn't it...?"

"No, it doesn't. That doesn't solve anything."

"Why not?!"

"—Because I will never forgive Rui, who made you suffer."

Her voice cracking, having declared she forgave Rui, Rem heard Subaru's flat declaration in response. Her eyes went wide, a hoarse breath escaping her. All at once, the strength drained from Rem's body, and Subaru slowly sat up.
Still astride Subaru's body, Rem faced him at close range as he rose.

"Not just me. Ram, everyone else—everyone who thinks Rem matters—none of us will forgive Rui. No matter how much you say you forgive her."

"That's... not..."

"And besides, Rem... what Rui did isn't just about you. There are far, far more people—a great many people—suffering because of what Rui did, because of the sin of 'Gluttony.'"

Even if Rem truly forgave Rui, even if it was mercy from the bottom of her heart and not just a burst of emotion, there were far more "Rems" in this world who could not forgive Rui.
Unless those people were saved too, Rem's desperate plea would never bear fruit.

"...Many others too?"

"Yeah. An overwhelming number of people are suffering."

"Then... then there's nothing to be done, is there?"

"——"

"From the very start, this was a problem with no solution, wasn't it? Telling me there's nothing that can be done... is that— is that what you call settling this?"

Her lips trembling, large tears traced down Rem's eyes once more.
Perhaps more intensely than she had ever grieved over her own "memories," Rem wept now over Rui.
As if to say her own "memories" didn't matter, that she wanted to save Rui, wanted someone to save her—swept along by that emotion, Rem's lips trembled. She wept.
Those tears, that trembling voice—Subaru felt them tear at him like a blade.

"Sorry. I know I'm being ridiculous."

"...Don't apologize."

"But I'm sorry. All I can say to you is painful, hard things."

"So don't apologize...! I don't want to hear it...!"

"I'm sorry, but please listen."

"So...!"

"I don't want to give up. —I don't want to just stay unable to forgive Rui."

"—Eh."

A sharp breath escaped Rem's throat, her eyes going wide.
Facing her, Subaru took a deep breath and licked his lips. He wanted every single word, without fail, to reach the ones he needed to reach, exactly as he meant them.

"I'm the same as you, Rem. I want to forgive Rui. I don't want to stay unable to forgive her. But I can't. Because Rem matters to me."

"—!"

"So I can't forgive Rui, who did something so terrible to you, who took your 'memories,' who's still making you suffer like this even now."

Reaching out gently, Subaru wiped away the tears running down Rem's cheek with his finger.
Even as he wiped away that one drop, countless more had already wet her cheeks. Even so, Rem didn't break his finger.
Taking that as not being rejected, Subaru continued.

"Everyone told me off about this, plenty. Told me not to say such stupid things, not to even think it—I got scolded hard. Otto probably still thinks things would be simpler if Rui just died."

But that, surely, was the natural way to think.
Not exactly a self-cleansing instinct, but everyone understood in their hearts that it had to end that way. Subaru understood it in his head too—which was exactly why he'd been opposed so strongly.
But even so—

"Julius told me something. That guy—he's amazing. Or maybe he's an idiot, the other way around. Even he, a victim every bit as much as Rem, said something like that—normally you wouldn't say that. He's an idiot."

"——"

"Smart people and idiots, all sorts of people told me all sorts of things... cool people, kind people, amazing people, so many of them told me things, and I thought about it. Thought about it a lot, and decided. Where I'd land on this."

"Where you'd... land..."

"I want to trust Rui. I want to forgive her. ...But I can't forgive her right now."

Speaking his thoughts aloud like that, watching Rem's eyes waver before him, words spoken by a certain man crossed Subaru's mind.

"As if I could ever get along with someone who decides other people's lives and deaths on a whim, based on nothing but their own preference."

Words spoken by a man he could never see eye to eye with dug their claws painfully into Natsuki Subaru's soul.
He'd been told that an attitude of deciding forgiveness or condemnation on a whim, based on personal preference, wasn't worth trusting.
Surely those words were right. The least trustworthy thing of all was Subaru's own heart.

And yet, with no one else to hand this heart off to, he had to come to terms with it and carry on.
Coming to terms with a soul so easily swayed, so quick to flip its own position, so fragile and pathetic.

"—Ah."

Startled by Subaru's words, Rem had gone still, and he pulled her into a tight embrace.
Given the difference in their heights, Subaru drew himself out from beneath the body pinning him down, and gathered the collapsed Rem's head against his stomach in an embrace.
He wanted her to feel, truly, from the bottom of his heart, how much Rem mattered to him.
And then—

"—Uau."

Hearing that voice, Subaru, called to, looked toward Rui.
Released by Rem, Rui had been standing there, and understanding that her turn had come, she called out to Subaru.

"Uー..."

Seeing Rui standing there looking uncertain, Rem, still held by Subaru, let out an "Ah" and hurriedly got to her feet.
Then Rem embraced Rui from the front, saying,

"I'm— I'm sorry, Rui-chan... we, on our own..."

"Au, uau, aー au."

"I'm sorry...!"

Apologizing weakly, Rem wiped her own face with her sleeve and stood beside Rui.
Once again she gripped Rui's hand tightly, but now with an expression that wasn't leaning on her. Subaru felt a small pang of jealousy toward Rui, who had moved Rem's heart like that.
And with that—

"—Rui, do you still want to become me?"

"...U?"

"...What are you talking about?"

Not understanding the meaning of Subaru's question, the two of them tilted their heads in unison.
Yes—the two of them tilted their heads together. Not just Rem, but Rui too. —"Rui" too.

"Just like I told Rem a moment ago. Even if Rem forgives you, I won't forgive you. All the other many people who suffered from 'Gluttony'—they won't forgive you either, most likely. But—"

"...But?"

"But, Rui, I want to forgive you. If it's possible to forgive you, I want to. So, let me hear it."

Making sure his voice didn't waver, Subaru looked straight at Rui.
He saw Rem gripping Rui's hand tightly, and Rui gripping it back in return. He watched, hoping that was the answer to the relationship between the two of them.

"Are you a Sin Archbishop? Or are you a possibility?"

"——"

"I've been told, over and over. And I think so too. This world doesn't forgive Sin Archbishops, and it shouldn't forgive them. The Sin Archbishop of 'Gluttony,' Rui Arneb, is a being that shouldn't be forgiven."

"——"

"But you saved me. Protected me, again and again. You didn't even know you wanted to become me. You're nothing like the Rui Arneb I know. And yet, you can still use the Authority of 'Gluttony.' You still carry the Witch Factor."

That was the entirety of Subaru's impression of this "Rui," built up over all the time they'd spent together.
The same appearance as the Sin Archbishop Rui Arneb he'd encountered in the "Corridor of Memories," possessing the same Authority as her, and yet her demeanor didn't seem like that of the same person at all.
It was like a certain "Natsuki Subaru" from some other time, like this "Rem" here now.

The same, and yet different.
Something that could choose, of its own will, both to be the same and to be different.

To that "Rui," he wanted to ask—

"Are you a Sin Archbishop that no one in this world will ever forgive? Or are you a possibility—a chance to somehow make up for what the Sin Archbishop did?"

"A, uu..."

"Can you... can you live your life anew?"

If—if it were so.
If "Rui's" situation were the same as "Natsuki Subaru's" or "Rem's," then what Subaru was asking was a terribly cruel, unreasonable question.
Subaru knew well the agony of having a debt not of your own making, not something you remembered or recognized, pressing down on you—a debt belonging to a self that wasn't you. Rem understood it too.
And now he was trying to place that same burden onto Rui as well.
And yet—

"If you can do it, if that's what you want, then take my hand."

As he spoke, Subaru slowly held out his hand toward Rui.
Rui's eyes moved back and forth between Subaru's face and the offered hand. In the same way, Rem, holding her breath beside the girl, traced Subaru's eyes and hand with her gaze.

"Rui, a lot of people curse you, just like I do. I can't speak for all of their feelings. But just one thing."

"——"

"I don't know how you can make everyone forgive you. But... but I can at least tell you what I need, in order to forgive you."

Before coming here, before being granted this time, Emilia had told him something.
Emilia had said Subaru was the one who'd taught her—but that was absurd. Subaru was always the one being taught, by everyone, all the time.
Even how to resolve his own feelings—he hadn't understood it without being taught.

Why he wanted to forgive Rui.
How he could go about forgiving her.
All of it. The way to reach that resolution was—

"—Rui, save a great many people."

"——"

"Right now, you're caught in something unfair. I know it might feel like you're about to be saddled with an outrageous cross to bear, for something you have no memory or knowledge of doing. Even so—"

Taking a deep breath, looking at Rui with unwavering eyes, Subaru told her.

"Save a great many people."

"——"

"Keep saving them, and saving them, and keep on saving them—if you go on saving more than you took... then at the very least, I—just I, myself—can be on your side."

—Whatever you've done, it never, ever simply vanishes.

That had been Anastasia's remark before, and again just recently—words that had frozen Subaru solid.
Words that had taught him that even his "Return by Death" held something that could never be undone—words he'd once taken as something close to trauma, though now he could see them differently.
But when Anastasia had said that, she'd also said this, alongside it:

"If you want people to believe in your own righteousness, you have to show them something worthy of it. To change an evaluation, you can only overturn it with a different evaluation."

That was the single most important part of the advice that had become Subaru's trauma.
The reason Subaru was now holding out his hand to Rui like this was that her actions had overturned the ill will Subaru had once held toward her.

He wanted to bring about that same change of heart that had happened in him, in everyone who had suffered from "Gluttony."
That was—

"—That's the 'starting from zero' that I can offer you."

Far from zero—on a global scale, she'd have to start from a deep negative.
An impossibly, absurdly vast and outlandish task, the kind of grandiose delusion no one could believe could ever be accomplished.

But that was the very best Subaru could offer her.
And if it was this impossibly, absurdly vast and outlandish task, this grandiose delusion no one could believe could ever be accomplished, then Subaru could help her with it.
Just as Natsuki Subaru himself had once been helped, in exactly the same way.

"——"

Hand still outstretched, Subaru waited quietly for an answer.
He wouldn't rush her. He wouldn't wait forever, either. He would take exactly as much time as was needed, and wait, quietly, for the answer that was needed.

"I... don't know."

In that silence, beside Subaru and Rui, who had both fallen quiet, Rem murmured.
For a moment, it seemed like it might mean she wouldn't accept Subaru's proposal. But Rem's gaze said otherwise.
She continued.

"I don't know anything about what a Sin Archbishop is, or a name like Rui Arneb. Any of it."

"——"

"But if this world won't allow either that Sin Archbishop, or the person named Rui Arneb, to exist in it... then what will this child become?"

Her voice trembled. But there were no tears welling in those pale blue eyes.
As if to insist that with a vision blurred and clouded by tears, neither the answer she wanted nor the answer offered to her could be properly seen, Rem's eyes stayed steady and clear.

If she chose to be neither the Sin Archbishop nor Rui Arneb, then what would she become?
Walking the path of "Rui," carrying an unreasonable burden, seeking a forgiveness that might not even exist on the other side of it—what would such a girl become?
Asked that, the words Subaru could offer in return were—

"—Spica."

"Eh...?"

"To the one who will live a new way, live her life anew as a new self—I give her this name."

Rem's eyes went wide, and Rui's eyes widened too.
If she chose a way of living different from before, if she would aim for a future utterly unlike anything before now, then Subaru intended to do everything in his power to help her.

In a world where no one forgives Sin Archbishops, where no one forgives Rui Arneb, still, Subaru wished from the bottom of his heart to forgive the girl before him.
Because he wanted to be able to forgive her, he wished for her to become someone—

"Spica..."

Dazed, stunned, Rem spoke the name aloud, her gaze drifting sideways.
The girl was staring steadily at Subaru. Subaru, too, looked back at the girl without looking away.

There was a wish he had, for how he hoped this would go.
But if he spoke it aloud, the girl would surely make it so. She would honor Subaru's wish rather than her own—so he wouldn't say it.

Because he wanted her to choose it for herself, not for anyone else.
Because he wanted her to be able to look back, after walking and walking and walking on, at the path behind her, and think: that's the path I chose, and walked, myself.

"—Uau."

"——"

The girl's thin lips moved. Subaru's name was called, softly.
Silent, Subaru waited. He didn't want to rush her. He wouldn't wait forever either. He wanted to hear the answer that came after taking the time needed, choosing what was needed.
And then, as Subaru held himself back with all his might, the girl's expression changed.

"Auu au."

Words spun loose and soft, and a smile rose along with them.
And from her blue eyes, from the corners of them, tears traced downward, and the girl's hand gently took hold of Subaru's outstretched hand.
Welcoming that thin, soft touch, Subaru squeezed his eyes shut tight.

A new way of living, neither Sin Archbishop nor Rui Arneb.
An unerasable past, in which she had been both the Sin Archbishop and Rui Arneb.

Carrying that with her, walking a thorny road ahead—Subaru gripped the hand of the girl who would walk it, "Spica," tightly, so as never to let go.

"Someday, I want to forgive you. —So let's do our best together."

"...Au!"

Grinning, showing her white teeth, Spica smiled through her tears.
Watching that smile of Spica's from the side, Rem's welled-up emotions swelled and finally broke.

"—!"

Weeping hotter and harder than ever before, Rem threw her arms around Spica.
