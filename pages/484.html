Arc 6 Chapter 78: "Four Corners"

"—Listen up. Right now, this tower's in serious trouble. How serious? Picture an Othello board where all four corners have already been taken."

"That's... pretty bad, isn't it."

Ram's expression darkened as she watched Subaru hold up a single finger and launch into his explanation with a grave face. She wasn't the only one turning serious — Emilia and Beatrice, listening to the same explanation, did too.

"Othello" — strictly speaking, "Reversi" would be the more diplomatically correct name to use — is a board game in which players use black and white pieces to fight over control of an 8×8, sixty-four-square board.

Naturally, it was a fragment of knowledge from the original world Subaru knew, but since it was relatively easy to reproduce here, it had been brought to Roswaal Manor fairly early on, sparking a minor sensation — and earning particularly rave reviews from Emilia.

Which was why, even with this quick explanation, the gravity of it came through loud and clear to Emilia and the others. In Othello, having all four corners taken meant the game was as good as over.

For what it's worth, Ram hadn't played much, but on the rare occasions she did, she was frighteningly good — quite literally demonic, in her case, and small wonder.

Anyway—

"All four corners gone is an outrageous situation, I suppose... Even as a handicap, that's simply too much, I suppose."

"If I got that many corners, even klutzy me, all in a flap, might actually win..."

"'In a flap'? Nobody says that anymore..."

"—! Subaru, say that again, just now!"

"Later. Later."

Emilia had latched onto Subaru's obligatory quip, and now looked disappointed at having her request turned down. A pang of guilt sprouted in him at the sight — but as he kept telling himself, flirting could wait.

Breaking the current deadlock came first. And to that end—

"The four corners... are the two Gluttony Archbishops inside the tower, and the swarm of witchbeasts they've called in. And then — this one's hard to say — one of our own comrades... ah, well, whether 'comrade' is even the right word is up for debate."

"—?"

Reaching for the fourth corner, he hesitated for a moment over how to continue.

It was because he couldn't settle on a clear answer, even in his own head, for how to describe the woman who now stood, unintentionally, as that fourth corner.

"Acquaintance" didn't feel far off. Their bond wasn't so thin as to be mere allies-of-circumstance thrown together like enemies sharing one boat. But had they truly shared enough fate to be called comrades? At least for Subaru, maybe the best description was an unwitting, unofficial master-and-disciple bond.

But what about for Emilia and the others?

But into that gap of Subaru's hesitation—

"Comrade — did something happen to Shaula?"

"—"

"—? Subaru?"

"No, it's nothing. Yeah, that's right. It's Shaula."

Slipped in so casually, so matter-of-factly, that Subaru just nodded back at Emilia. And it wasn't only her — neither Beatrice nor Ram showed any sign of finding Emilia's assumption strange.

Regardless of whether Emilia's "memories" were intact or not, there was no discrepancy in their perception on this point. Proof that, in their minds, Shaula's standing had been etched in as something friendly.

"Am I an idiot? No — I am an idiot."

Tripping over the obvious was a bad habit of Natsuki Subaru's. He reflected on it deeply. And turning that deep reflection into fuel for doing better — that was one of Natsuki Subaru's good points.

For every one criticism, one compliment. Ideally, for every one criticism, a hundred compliments.

That was about the right balance. The true ideal was zero criticisms and ten thousand compliments.

"Anyway, it's about Shaula, but... before that, do you remember the rules inside the tower? There was an explanation of the things you're not allowed to break, right?"

"Yes, there was, wasn't there. Not completing the 'Trial,' not keeping to the 'Trial's' rules, failing to respect the library, damaging the tower... those were the rule violations, weren't they."

"Exactly right."

Snapping his fingers at Ram, who'd rattled the list off fluently, Subaru pointed downward with that same finger. Then he circled his fingertip to indicate the whole tower,

"Those rules, several of them... breaking one of them is what makes the fourth corner appear. I don't know specifically which one gets broken, but one of them will be. And when that happens..."

"Just as she said herself, it bares its fangs at Ram and the others. ——Troublesome."

In her usual pose of hugging her own elbows, Ram closed one eye, picturing the worst-case scenario.

Just how high Shaula's combat prowess ran was something Subaru knew all too well, having lived through a loop where she'd been the cause of his death, and something Emilia and the others understood just as well, having endured her long-range sniping across the sand sea without end.

"That scatterbrained girl has quite the ability under all that, doesn't she. Better than being shot at from a distance with no way to fight back, I suppose, but it's still bad news if she turns enemy, I suppose."

Arms crossed, both Ram and Beatrice creased their brows.

Quick understanding saved a lot of trouble with explanations — a relief. Amid the sour faces around him, only Emilia, her face utterly free of creases and still the cutest in the world, raised her hand,

"But it's Shaula we're talking about, right? Can't we talk it out somehow, persuade her? Shaula's suuuper attached to Subaru, and if Subaru talked to her really hard..."

"That peaceful resolution is what I want too, but it seems like it'll be tough getting Shaula to the negotiating table. As for her being attached to me... that part hasn't changed, in a broad sense, I guess."

"In other words, Barusu gets targeted intensively. Having the target narrowed down that much is a very good thing."

You could take it as "the weak point gets targeted," but Subaru agreed with Ram's thinking regardless.

Having her lurk about the tower unpredictably, with no telling who'd be targeted — that would be far worse than a situation where Subaru got targeted single-mindedly; the latter was much easier to plan countermeasures for.

Summarizing the problem, then—

"—Two of the 'Gluttony,' the swarm of witchbeasts, and Shaula turning on us. That makes four corners, doesn't it."

"Two of those four corners are already being dealt with. That's thanks to Bea-ko's handling of things."

While Subaru had been curled up in the Taygeta library, the clever and adorable Beatrice had taken charge of everyone, running herself ragged dealing with the damage assailing the tower.

Sending Meili to the balcony overlooking the witchbeast swarm, joining up with Emilia and Ram who'd gone to bring Rem out of the green room — all part of that effort. Unfortunately, along the way, Emilia and the others had run into Lye, and things had ended with Emilia's "name" being stolen from her.

"Mm-mm, it's fine. Since Subaru remembered me, it's totally no big deal. You always forget your promises, but you remembered me properly, and that made me suuuper happy."

"Nnh, why the sudden knife in the back?"

"It seems your everyday conduct is catching up with you, I suppose. That girl seems to understand Subaru quite well too, doesn't she — even if not as well as Betty."

"Don't go competing, don't go competing, you adorable thing."

Petting Beatrice's head, Subaru nodded again and again at the palpable sense of being loved. And to Subaru, lost in that sentiment, Ram called out, "Barusu,"

"You said two of the corners were being handled. I understand Meili's holding down the witchbeast swarm, but what about the other one?"

"—Julius."

Answering Ram, Subaru gripped the front of his own clothes and let his awareness sink into his heartbeat.

The power of "Cor Leonis" was still very much active — beyond the burden he took on from Ram, it also relayed the presence of his comrades scattered throughout the tower to Subaru in real time.

Among them was, without doubt, a light that seemed to be Julius, and its position was—

"—On the second level, in the middle of fighting an enemy."

"The second level... don't tell me."

"Yeah. That's exactly what you're thinking."

Nodding back at Emilia, whose color had changed in an instant, Subaru lightly lifted his gaze upward.

Blocked by the ceiling, he couldn't see directly beyond it. But far past the line of his sight, he could sense, without doubt, a fierce battle unfolding.

—The rematch between Julius Juukulius and Reid Astrea had already begun.

"—"

In the end, it seemed, a reckoning between the two was unavoidable.

In place of Subaru, who'd been on his knees, unable to stand, it was Beatrice who'd given the orders to their comrades. And even under Beatrice's command, Julius's opponent had ended up settling on none other than Reid Astrea.

That was, of course, partly due to the fact that Reid — having hijacked the body of Roy Alphard, the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony, and won his freedom — was seeking a reckoning of his own with Julius.

But there was something Subaru understood, having stacked up death after death, having walked through countless possibilities whether he wanted to or not.

—It was that, infuriating as it was, "fate" really did exist.

Whatever path led there, an unyielding, unchangeable reality that lay waiting at the end of it — that was what people called "fate."

There existed swells of history so powerful you couldn't help but believe they'd been predetermined — as if the result "fate" presented came first, and every process along the way was merely arriving at it.

It was, in truth, the result of countless individual wills tying together, each striving to fulfill its own purpose — and precisely because of that, they were entangled so tightly they'd never come undone.

But couldn't that, at the same time, be said to mean there was a ray of hope?

The reckoning between Julius Juukulius and Reid Astrea was unavoidable. If so, then—

"Reid... he's fused with one of the 'Gluttony,' and the takeover's basically complete, but leave settling things with Reid to Julius. We'll deal with the remaining three."

"You're sure about that, Barusu?"

"—? Sure about what?"

Subaru tilted his head, quite genuinely puzzled, at Ram's pointed question. But seeing that reaction of his, Ram muttered a short "I see," and then—

"I'm thoroughly convinced now of just what makes Barusu, Barusu. Ram's getting rusty too, having no choice but to rely on that plan of his."

"I don't really get it, but I can tell I'm being made fun of."

"Eh? No, no, she's complimenting you. She said she's suuuper relying on Subaru. Fufu, guess I've gotta try hard too, huh."

"...Emilia, Ram's going to want a word with you later."

At Ram's sullen words, Emilia answered with a cheerful "Okaay," looking a little pleased.

Even in a situation like this, it was probably that Emilia found it refreshing that Ram no longer used polite speech with her, and that surely made her happy. Now that their master-servant relationship no longer applied, the way Ram treated Emilia was like that toward a friend, or a troublesome little sister.

Given that her actual little sister was anything but troublesome, Ram didn't get many chances to play the capable older sister. Though, that this could even be read that way was thanks to Emilia's abnormally positive outlook.

"Anyway, that covers the tower's situation! Now all that's left is assigning the right people to the right places. This sorting business is Natsuki Subaru's biggest moment to shine!"

"That's way, way too plain, I suppose..."

"Plain or not, it's important work! This is what you call unsung, understated brilliance. Work only I can do... yeah, something only I can do! This is my battle...ow!!"

"Enough. Hurry up and sort us already."

For dragging out the preamble too long, Ram slapped him across the face. Bearing that damage, Subaru looked around at the three — plus one earth dragon — and said, "All right?"

From here on out was total war, total war in the truest sense. The distinction between human and earth dragon barely mattered in that — and to begin with, Patrasche was more useful than Subaru on any ordinary day anyway.

And with Super Subaru thrown into the mix, the odds doubled — no, doubled again on top of that.

"One for all, all for one. Everyone for the one, and the one for everyone. We can't grab victory unless everyone becomes one. I don't want to lose even a single one of us. We're going for the win without losing anyone. Because we..."

"—came to this tower to take things back without losing anything, didn't we."

"That's the deal."

Snapping his fingers and nodding at Emilia, Subaru steeled his resolve again.

And then—

"First, about dealing with Lye Batenkaitos of 'Gluttony'—"

"—Barusu."

"Hnh?"

Just as the meeting wrapped up and everyone was about to head off to their own posts, Subaru had his collar grabbed and was forcibly stopped in his tracks. That said, there wasn't enough force behind it to actually choke him.

There was probably a reason for that consideration. Namely—

"Being careful not to break me — that's not like you at all, is it, big sister."

"If you were trying to examine a gnat on the palm of your hand, you'd take pains not to press too hard, wouldn't you. It's the same thing. ...Though Ram wouldn't bother putting a gnat on her palm in the first place. Disgusting."

"Don't say it and then take it back yourself."

Subaru shrugged at the very Ram-like turn of phrase. In front of him, Ram opened and closed her own palm lightly, checking the sensation of it again and again.

There was a faint trace of bewilderment in that gesture, and to Subaru — who thought of the always-aloof Ram as her natural state — it seemed like a rather rare sight.

Getting to see a flustered, bewildered Ram was a nice perk of the job. It was exactly that sort of small pleasure that made it possible to endure this crushing, full-body fatigue.

"—"

The burden of being "Hornless" that constantly tormented Ram's body — Subaru took that on by activating "Cor Leonis," granting her enough spare strength to move at full capacity.

That was one absolutely indispensable measure for conquering this tower.

Never mind the detailed principles behind it — Ram had been told the gist of what was happening.

In short: Ram's burden flowed into Subaru, and her body grew lighter by that same amount. But it was rough on Subaru, so he wanted this settled sooner rather than later. Something like that.

"If it were simply a matter of Barusu suffering, Ram wouldn't give it a second thought no matter how long it dragged on, though."

"But it's joint work between me and big sister. Thinking of it that way, you want it over even a second sooner, don't you?"

"True. It makes my skin crawl."

"'Skin crawl' is a bit much..."

Well, that level of venom was more like Ram, at least.

Suppressing a wave of nausea, Subaru accepted as much. In front of him, Ram hugged her own elbows in that familiar pose and glared this way.

"—Barusu. Ram isn't going to hold back."

"—"

Naturally, the more fully Ram operated at her best, the more the load would swell in counter-reaction. Even so, if he wanted Ram kept at peak condition, Subaru was prepared to take on all of the increased burden himself.

Just Ram standing and breathing was more than painful enough to complain about. But that was the suffering she tasted continuously, day in and day out.

And then—

"'Big sister is too kind,' huh."

That was the assessment of Ram that Rem, still asleep even now, had voiced so often. He hadn't properly understood it back then, but now he understood what her words meant.

To put it in Ram's own style of speaking: that habit of prefacing things like that was exactly what made Ram, Ram.

"Barusu?"

"It's nothing. —Of course, don't you dare hold back. Whether you're going all-out or not, I can measure by how much it hurts me. What kind of torture is that."

"So the more well Ram feels, the more Barusu suffers and writhes — that's the mechanism, is it."

"Feels like handing the nuclear button to the one person who really shouldn't have it."

That said, if Subaru had to hand the nuclear button to someone in his inner circle, Ram was the only one he could really imagine doing it.

"Otto was a good contender too, but there's a real worry he'd trip and hit the button by accident... in terms of using it appropriately, big sister's the surest bet."

"Hah! Flattery won't get you anywhere. ...Make sure you look after Lady Beatrice and Emilia properly. Barusu can end up however he ends up. Ram already knows it's going to be rough on you anyway."

"Yeah, yeah, I know. As for Rem..."

"—Ram is the one who worries about Rem more than anyone else in this world."

Ram declared it flatly, as though stating an obvious truth.

At the force behind those words, Subaru held his breath, and Ram narrowed her crimson eyes,

"Barusu can go on worrying second-most in the world, in his own Barusu way. That should be enough to make sure you won't even think of putting so much as a single hair's worth of a wound on Rem."

"...Well, that'd make me want to die."

With just that much exchanged between them, Ram turned her back on Subaru.

There must have been mountains of things she wanted to say, things she wanted to get off her chest, yet she drew a line under the endless words and switched herself back over without delay.

That, precisely, was the true reason Ram was Ram.

"Barusu, you'd better be careful. If you die, you won't be able to see Rem anymore."

"Yeah, I love you too."

To Ram's less-than-honest concern, Subaru responded with a joke of his own in kind.

And so, seeing off Ram's back as she ran away without another word, Subaru fell in alongside Emilia and the others, who'd been waiting, marking time in the corridor.

"Sorry to keep you waiting. Let's get moving too."

"Yes, let's. Is Ram going to be okay?"

"Probably, surely, Ram will be fine. ...If I'd said something really stupid, there's no way she'd have kept quiet about it."

"That's quite a lot of trust to put in Ram, isn't it."

"I love you the same, or more. Don't get sulky."

"Ram isn't sulking, I suppose!" Beatrice puffed her cheeks out. Taking the hand she held out, Subaru glanced over at Emilia right beside him. He'd thought she might be worrying about Ram going off alone too, but there was little anxiety on her profile,

"Emilia-tan?"

"Mm-mm, it's nothing. Just, somehow, I feel suuuper reassured."

"—. What a coincidence — I actually feel the exact same way."

Emilia's baseless remark felt, to Subaru, like the best possible push forward.

Anticipation outweighed anxiety. Entrusting was stronger than merely handing off. Believing let you face forward more than clinging did. Not a single problem had actually been solved yet, and still—

"Trusting your comrades is only natural, and I can trust myself for relying on them too. Since coming to this sand-choked tower, right now is when my view's clearest it's ever been."

"—That face. That's Betty's favorite Subaru, isn't it."

Seeing Subaru vouch for himself so forcefully, Beatrice smiled fearlessly. And at that remark of hers, Emilia let out a "fufu" and put a hand to her mouth.

Beatrice pointed out Emilia's reaction with a "What is it, I suppose."

At that, Emilia shook her head,

"Beatrice doesn't seem to remember, but I was thinking the exact same thing."

Fiercely, the massive body, seized by a hind leg, was swung around and slammed, mercilessly, into the floor, into the wall.

"—"

At the sheer feat of superhuman strength, the massive frame, taking heavy damage, let out an unsightly scream. It was a dissonant noise that groaned through the air, like countless infants wailing in unison.

"—!!"

The one letting out that despairing cry was a hideous witchbeast of grotesque appearance — Natsuki Subaru, drawing on his own knowledge, called this witchbeast a centaur. But whoever it was that had once known this witchbeast before had given it a different name. —The "Starving Horse King."

"—!!"

That massive body of the Starving Horse King was thrown, and it tumbled head over heels across the balcony of the tower of sand. Battered by the impact, this king of the sand sea nonetheless rose at once, spewing flame from its mane.

Then, in an instant, its humanoid upper body conjured two blades of flame, and wielding a dual-sword technique quite unbecoming of a witchbeast, it tried to drive a red-hot strike into whatever had attacked it.

But then—

"—"

As the Starving Horse King's blazing mane roared and it moved to swing its flame blades, white light plunged into its torso. The impact sent that massive body reeling back hard. But it endured. It held on. Just like that, the Starving Horse King planted its foot to launch a counterattack. —An instant later, the white light lodged inside it burst.

The white light became a bed of light-blades inside the Starving Horse King's body, tearing the witchbeast apart from within, limb from limb.

No matter how vicious a creature's vitality, having every vital organ destroyed from the inside made death unavoidable. The Starving Horse King was no exception, and its life scattered violently before that light.

"—"

And the woman who had accomplished this — Shaula's slaughter did not stop.

Her distinctively braided brown hair whipping about, Shaula kicked hard off the floor and shot horizontally across the balcony. Light wrapped around both her outstretched hands, and she caught two witchbeasts directly in her path — enemies with the bodies of monkeys and the scales of lizards — and there, the light burst forth.

Had Subaru been watching, he would have called it a pile bunker strike. The two monkey-things, their faces seized in her palms, had their heads blown clean off by the discharged white light before they had any chance to retaliate. On top of that, Shaula spun sharply in place; her long leg shot down another monkey lunging at her, her toes driving its face into the floor before its head, too, exploded.

The stage on which Shaula waged her battle against the witchbeasts was the fourth level of the Pleiades Watchtower. The balcony facing the outer wall sat at a height of over a hundred meters, but no small number of the witchbeasts had wings, or could scale the wall with ease.

Shooting down these climbers one after another, the terrace had already become a sea of blood.

"—"

Having accomplished slaughter on that scale, Shaula's breathing hadn't even quickened.

The total number of witchbeasts swarming the Pleiades Watchtower was so vast that counting them felt pointless, but the fact that they hadn't come pouring fatally into the tower's interior owed a great, undeniable debt to her overwhelming combat strength.

But that precarious balance, held together by Shaula alone—

"—Shaula!"

"Guh, uuuuugh...!"

A high voice called Shaula's name, but she gave no answer. She only twisted her cheeks in pain as she grabbed a winged witchbeast flying at her and slammed it down at her feet.

White light plunged into the ones following behind it; red flowers bloomed wildly in midair, and the witchbeasts' pursuit ceased. But even having driven them off, Shaula's state didn't change. She clutched at her own face, stamping her feet in anguish.

Through the gaps between Shaula's fingers — the dark-irised eyeball visible there was splitting apart, beginning to pulse with a deep red. It meant the emergence of compound eyes: Shaula's transformation.

"Someone... someone broke the rules...su..."

Covering her face with her palms, she fought off the encroaching witchbeasts in this hampered state — sweeping aside their attacks with her long legs, shattering an enemy's torso with a sharp back-kick.

"This is bad. She's at her limit. Meili! How are things on your end—"

"As you can seeee, I'm full up just dealing with the ones flying in! And if the naked lady over there stops being able to fight too, we'll be short-handed for suuure, no doubt about it!"

"Figured as much. This is rather brutal."

Watching Shaula's agony with a grim expression was the girl with pale-lavender hair rippling in the wind, Anastasia — or rather, Echidna, borrowing her body. Beside her stood Meili as well, her deep blue braids swaying as she managed, through her own Divine Protection, to somehow keep the situation balanced.

Shaula, Echidna, and Meili — these three made up the team fighting an all-out defense against the witchbeasts swarming the Pleiades Watchtower. Though, unlike Meili with her "Divine Protection of Demon Manipulation," or Shaula with her purely firepower-heavy approach, Echidna's presence there amounted to next to nothing.

Returning to "Taygeta" felt like hesitating, but retreating into the tower's ominous interior and becoming an unwelcome burden there was something she wanted to avoid too. It was a passive, wait-and-see kind of situation.

But then—

"Well now, it seems I can't just sit back and say that anymore..."

Moving her slender fingers, Echidna turned her awareness inward — strictly speaking, toward the Od within Anastasia's body. With a flawed Gate that couldn't draw in mana, the only thing Anastasia's body could use to cast magic was her own innate Od.

Which was to say, it amounted to shaving away at Anastasia's own life. Which was exactly why, even for Echidna, this was a measure she'd rather not resort to except when life was truly on the line.

But rather than sitting back and waiting for death, better to act before it came to that. At least, that was how the Anastasia Echidna knew would think.

Everyone plays with the hand they're dealt. Which means victory or defeat comes down to how you play the cards in it.

"—"

Whatever was afflicting Shaula was probably caused by something rooted deep at her very core.

And it existed as a kind of shackle that could never be overturned. Echidna understood this, being in a similar position herself as an artificial spirit. It was something no amount of willpower could overcome.

If what she'd said earlier was true, then one of the rules of the Pleiades Watchtower had been broken. So, in order to correct that, she had to act as the watchtower's guardian.

And the fact that she was resisting it at all was because Shaula genuinely, from the bottom of her heart, wished not to hurt the people here — no, most likely, wished not to hurt Natsuki Subaru specifically.

"...What an ironic thing."

Because she had kept on being the watchtower's guardian, she'd been able to reunite with someone she wanted to see again.

But because she continued to be the watchtower's guardian, she couldn't defy an order to hurt that very someone she wanted to see. —Truly, the sorrow of a created being.

In the truest sense, she thought, perhaps she herself, and Beatrice — likewise a created being — were the only ones who could understand the suffering Shaula carried.

So—

"—Deryaaaaaah!!"

At the sight of the figure that leapt out from the passage connecting the tower to the balcony, along with that spirited shout, Echidna went wide-eyed and speechless, catching her breath.

The instant he leapt out onto the balcony, Subaru was struck by how different the scene before him was from the one he remembered, and at the same time understood that a battle this fierce had been unfolding here.

"—"

The fourth-level balcony, littered with the corpses of countless witchbeasts, had turned into a sea of blood.

The reason this viewing platform overlooking the sand sea had been stained with blood was that the countless witchbeasts swarming the watchtower wouldn't stop their reckless charges. Left unchecked, they would push into the tower and the chaos would only spread.

And the one whose contribution had, above all others, kept that damage from spreading in the first place—

"—Shaula!"

"Master...?"

At the sound of Subaru's shouting voice, Shaula turned, her eyes going wide, frozen — her eyeballs splitting apart, on the verge of separating into six compound eyes, three to each side.

It was a transformation Subaru had already witnessed once with his own eyes before. It meant that a rule of the Pleiades Watchtower had been broken, and Shaula's duty as the watchtower's guardian had come due.

As a result, against her own will, Shaula would transform into the giant scorpion and begin moving to annihilate the challengers within the tower — meaning Subaru and the others.

So—

"Master, order me... order me, su...!"

"—"

"Someone... broke the rules, su. If, this keeps up, ah'll... hurt Master...! Before that happens, or else, ah'll... hurt Master, Master...!"

Hugging her own body, Shaula begged Subaru for an order with a desperate expression.

Instinctively, it seemed, Shaula had had the function of taking her own life stripped away. What she was asking for here was the final push needed to bring harm to herself.

If it came from Subaru's mouth, Shaula would throw away her own life without hesitation. If Subaru simply gave the order, it was fair to say the threat of the giant scorpion would be removed.

So Subaru nodded firmly,

"Shaula."

"Y-yes... ah'll, for Master's sake..."

"I've said it before, plenty of times — I've got no memory of you ever having a reason to call me Master."

"—!"

For an instant, at Subaru's answer, Shaula's compound eyes were dyed with despair.

After four hundred years of waiting in vain, only to finally be reunited with the one she'd waited for — was she now going to be denied even that, and have her last wish trampled underfoot besides? Shaula quailed like a frightened child at the thought.

Watching that fear, Subaru breathed in, and out.

"So — whether or not I'm your Master, whether I admit it or not, that's a conversation for later."

"Eh...?"

"I'm not going to tell you to die. I'm not going to let you go on crying like this. I'm not going to let your four hundred years end here."

Having taken the time to gather back memories he'd once lost, Subaru understood something now.

People in this world were far too patient. You shouldn't wait single-mindedly for someone for four hundred whole years. You should grab them by the scruff of the neck if you have to, and drag out the someone you want to see.

"I'll do that for you! Like hell I'm going to meekly, obediently do whatever someone else tells me! Bring it on, Lord Fate!"

"—"

Fist raised, Subaru declared it, again and again, with all his strength. Seeing this, Shaula was left speechless. Even as this happened, an unbearable impulse was probably still exploding somewhere inside her.

But in this moment, at least what mattered to her wasn't the tower's rules, nor the impulse swelling up as if about to burst from within, but—

"Master... ah loves you, su."

Probably, nothing but an uncontainable surge of love for the person she'd yearned for across four hundred years.

"—"

The instant she finished saying it, Shaula's transformation began in earnest.

Her slender white hands swelled into great pincers, her voluptuous, feminine body burst apart, and flesh and blood that had scattered came flooding back like a tape playing in reverse, reassembling her body. Jet-black carapace formed, blazing red compound eyes glared down upon the world. Multiple legs planted themselves into the floor, and the tower of sand's caretaker stood complete—

"—!!"

The giant scorpion's imposing form let out a shrill cry, warning the offender who had broken the tower's rules. What its compound eyes caught first was, ironically, the black-haired boy its predecessor had been so obsessed with.

Just as a merciless blow was about to be unleashed straight at him—

"—Eyaaaaaaah!!"

—a magnificent flying kick slammed into the giant scorpion's body, straight from the side.

"—"

Under that tremendous force, the giant scorpion's massive body was knocked clean off the floor and sent flying. Rolling spectacularly across the stone floor, dragging witchbeasts caught in its path along the way, its huge frame went right over the edge of the balcony and was flung out into the sky above the sand sea.

But the giant scorpion wasn't about to just take it. Its tail stinger, thrust out on reflex, plunged into the watchtower's wall, hurling its huge body back upward as if flinging it. Twisting itself in midair, the giant scorpion tried fiercely to make its way back to the balcony.

"—Muraku."

What obstructed that return was a single incantation, spun out in a single voice.

Directional mana interfering with reality brought about an effect on the world that would never occur naturally. The result it produced this time was the obstruction of the giant scorpion's return.

Caught up in the wind, the giant scorpion tried to land on the balcony in one grand motion. Its body, however, was carried along by the buffeting sandy gale, veering wildly off its trajectory toward the balcony.

For a body as massive as the giant scorpion's — surely no less than several hundred kilograms — to be swept along by the wind, just how strong would that gale have to be? But that wasn't what was happening.

"Muraku," a form of dark magic, was a spell that manipulated the gravity acting on its target.

In short: if the giant scorpion's weight became as light as dust, it stood to reason that its bulk would no longer move the way its size suggested it should. That's all that had been done. Nothing more.

"—!!"

The giant scorpion let out a pained cry, thrown back out beyond the balcony once again. In that instant, it swung its tail stinger, giving up on returning and instead trying to snipe at this side from atop the tower.

But that, too, was a beat too late.

"Wingdrake-chan!"

At the girl's high-pitched command, an attack came pouring down on the giant scorpion as it was swept away by the wind. Diving in was a witchbeast that had sprouted something like bird feathers, its head mostly turned to horn. It slammed into the giant scorpion's outer shell again and again, dealing no real damage, but the impact shoved the giant scorpion far off into the distance, further and further from the tower.

"—"

Even the mighty giant scorpion couldn't keep itself airborne without support. Carried along by the wind, the moment the spell's effect wore off, it plummeted headfirst toward the ground.

A fall from several hundred meters up — of course, not enough to finish off an opponent like this. But it was more than enough to buy time. Using that brief window—

"Meili! Echidna!"

"Natsuki-kun! You've recovered—no?"

At Subaru's call as he ran over, Echidna raised her face and knit her brow. Wearing Anastasia's pensive expression, she went on, "Could it be—"

"By any chance, has your 'memory' come back?"

"You catch on fast! But how'd you know?"

"Oh, I'd know. Not with Beatrice right beside you wearing a face that smug."

Where Echidna jerked her chin, there was Beatrice, hand in hand with Subaru, wearing an utterly triumphant look.

Sure enough, seeing this, the turn for the better in Beatrice's heart was plain as day — and that honest inability of hers to hide anything was truly lovely.

Of course, there was no doubt Echidna's insight was excellent too, and with that same insight she looked past Subaru to Emilia, who'd kicked the giant scorpion flying,

"And as for her, the one I don't recognize..."

"It's Gluttony's Authority. What happened to her is the same as what happened to Julius — everyone around her forgets, but she herself remembers. Her name's Emilia. My tough, adorable princess."

"Thanks for the quick rundown. Still, things are in serious chaos both inside and outside the tower. Mind if I ask what the plan is?"

"Yeah, I've already got the answer ready for that."

Behind Subaru as he nodded deeply, Emilia spread both arms wide and deployed countless shards of ice into the sky above. Loosed all at once, that tremendous force struck the ground and the witchbeasts flying through midair alike.

Subaru couldn't see it, but somewhere below, a follow-up strike was probably lodging itself in the falling giant scorpion too. Still, even that was a drop in the bucket against the sheer numbers of witchbeasts—

"Echidna, your job's the inside! For now, join up with Julius, who's fighting on the second level! I've already told Emilia what to do from there!"

"—. Going with her is fine by me, but what about you and the others, Natsuki-kun?"

"Betty and Subaru's job is already decided, I suppose."

Only a moment's thought, and Beatrice answered Echidna's question. She glanced up at Subaru's profile beside her, tightening her grip on his hand,

"We'll be taking on that giant scorpion right here, I suppose. Since we have to hold back and be careful not to actually finish her off, honestly, it's a real chore, I suppose."

"Talking big, this one. That's my Bea-ko for you."

"Ehe, silly me, I suppose."

Having talked big enough to fire up both herself and her contractor, Beatrice stuck out her tongue.

Echidna's eyes went wide at that exchange between them, and then she shook her head. Better, it seemed, to entrust herself to the method she believed best than to spend time arguing about it.

That knack for cutting her losses and moving on — well, no wonder, it was right on par with Anastasia's.

"At every turn in this tower, it's been Natsuki-kun's judgment that's saved us — that much is certain. So betting on you here, too, is our best shot at winning... or so I judge."

"That's an honor. Makes me all the more sorry you happen to be a creature named Echidna."

"As for how strongly my creator's influence shows through — once this whole affair's settled, I'd like a proper sit-down to discuss it. —Any message for Julius?"

"—"

Trading light jabs, Echidna, just as she turned to go, added that last question.

Even now, through the effect of "Cor Leonis," he could feel it without end — Julius, still fighting one level up. Thinking about it, this meant he'd now linked up with every ally in the tower except Julius himself.

Julius, too, had been one of those worried about Subaru after he'd lost his "memories," so Subaru felt he really ought to report that they'd come back—

"Nah, nothing in particular."

As things stood, there were no words Natsuki Subaru needed to give to Julius Juukulius, currently fighting Reid Astrea.

To begin with, Subaru's whole strategy, under these worst-case circumstances, was about putting people in their optimal positions. He was going around calling out to people because reassignment was needed — but there was no such need for Julius.

—Julius's post was none other than Reid Astrea himself.

"On that point, I guess you'd have to say he's something else. Even without me saying a word, he's the one who ended up going to face Reid, after all."

Thanks to that, he'd been able to exchange words with Shaula before she became the giant scorpion.

In that final moment, the strength Shaula's familiar declaration of love had given him was boundless. An absolute, unshakeable resolve to save her lit a hot fire in his chest.

So—

"—Whatever I ought to say, the me from a little while ago, and the me before that, already said all of it. There's nothing left to add at this point. That guy's Julius Juukulius, after all."

That an unyielding "fate" truly existed in this world — Natsuki Subaru had, without question, come to accept that.

The many troubles surrounding this watchtower were unavoidable, certain to bare their fangs at Subaru and the others. But being unavoidable and being insurmountable were two entirely different things.

If it was "fate" that Reid Astrea stood in Julius's way, then what came after was fated too.

The one to cut down the resurrected Reid Astrea would be Julius Juukulius.

"—. Understood. I'll pass that along, just as it is."

"Ah, actually there is one thing. Everyone else has got their hands full too, so wrap it up fast and go help cover for someone else."

Waving a hand, Subaru had a part in the wry smile on Echidna's face as she ran off. Watching her go, Subaru turned his gaze to where she was headed, toward Emilia, who was cutting down two witchbeasts with a blade of ice.

"Emilia-tan! Just like we planned! I'm counting on you, Emilia-tan!"

"Right, leave it to me! And Subaru, you'd better not go dying, okay!"

"You bet!"

At Emilia's altogether obvious words, Subaru nonetheless raised his fist, putting into it a conviction well beyond the ordinary.

I don't want to die. —That basic principle remained unchanged, but layered with reasons beyond it, the danger of "Return by Death" under these circumstances was beyond imagining.

If the restart point hadn't changed, then Subaru would return to a stage before he'd separated Rui Arneb, Witch Factor and all, from himself. If he returned to that point, exactly what kind of trouble it would cause was something neither Subaru nor anyone else could know.

Had Rui vanished from inside Subaru, or not? The "Corridor of Memories" was a kind of extraterritorial zone, but how would the effects of "Return by Death" play out there? Everything about it was hazy.

In other words—

"—This time, I'm putting an end to it!"

Following Subaru's declaration, Emilia grabbed hold of Echidna and left the balcony.

They to their battlefield. And Subaru and the others to theirs.

And so—

"So theeen? I'm totally lost here 'cause nobody's explained a thiiing, but you're gonna properly explain it to meee, right?"

Meili, who'd held her tongue patiently and hadn't butted into the conversation with Echidna, timed it so that once only Subaru and Beatrice remained on the balcony, she called out to him like that.

Giving Meili a thumbs-up, Subaru bared his teeth in a wicked grin.

At this eleventh hour, there was only one reason Subaru hadn't said anything to Meili — same as with Julius, she too was already exactly where she needed to be, on the battlefield best suited to her.

"Sorry, but you're stuck with us, Meili. The three of us — me, Bea-ko, and you — are going to hold down the witchbeast swarm and the giant scorpion — two of the corners!"

"That's not an explanation at aaall!"

"—Here it comes, I suppose!"

There was no time left to give a detailed explanation to Meili, who shook her braids and puffed her cheeks out. The very next moment, as if answering Beatrice's cry, a massive form leapt up from the tower's outer wall onto the balcony.

It was a being with a jet-black shell, savage great pincers, and red compound eyes—

"So. Four hundred years a shut-in, huh."

Breathing just slightly hard, Subaru scooped Beatrice up beside him in one motion. Then, standing alongside Meili as she ran up right next to him, he faced the giant scorpion head-on.

Facing the enemy glaring silently back at him, he twisted his cheek into a grin,

"This is a shut-in showdown. She's got four hundred years, solo. We've got four hundred and one, between the two of us."

"Wouldn't four hundred and twoo, between the three of us, be more correeect?"

"Point is, we win!"

"Nothing about that made sense except the winning part, I suppose!!"

In his arms, Beatrice barked back at the well-matched banter between Subaru and Meili. And then, kneaded by just the right amount of tension, Subaru glared at the giant scorpion and let out a breath.

With this, the situation settled into the matchup they'd expected. All that was left—

"—I'm counting on you for real, Emilia-tan. You're the key to all of it."

—The battle against the "Four Corners" surrounding the Pleiades Watchtower now began in earnest.
