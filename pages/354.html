Arc 5 Chapter 38: "The Witch Cult's Demands"

Raising its head, the eyes that stared back at him left Subaru forgetting even to breathe.

"For the most part this is the reaction I expected, but being stared at with this much shock isn't exactly a pleasant feeling either. Believe it or not, I too am, gender-wise... female? No, wait—whether Spirits even have a distinction between male and female is a tricky question, so perhaps I should say I lean female in spirit?"

White fur. Narrowed black eyes. A voice with an androgynous timbre, and a manner of speech that was excessively formal and roundabout. That its form could be expressed entirely in shades of white and black—yes, it did indeed match the appearance of that witch he remembered. But even so—

"What kind of sick joke is this. You're Echidna...?"

The 'Witch of Greed,' encountered during the 'Trials of the Tomb' in the 'Sanctuary,' who had invited Subaru to her tea party. Seducing the guests of her tea party with sweet, gentle words, turning them into puppets bent to her will, all in the service of a curiosity so voracious it craved every outcome she herself could never witness. He'd been so sure he would never lay eyes on her again.

"And now you show up in the shape of a fox? Don't joke with me. In a situation this dangerous, what are you scheming this time?"

"Whoa, hold up. I'd rather you not blow your top all on your own before hearing me out."

Recovering from his initial shock, Subaru moved to close the distance on the white fox claiming to be Echidna. But before he could take a step forward, Anastasia—still wearing the white fox around her neck—stopped him with a reproving voice. Subaru redirected the hostility he'd aimed at the fox toward Anastasia as well.

"Don't tell me not to blow my top, that's asking too much. You too, standing there with that thing... are you being deceived? To begin with, that thing shouldn't even be able to leave the 'Sanctuary.'"

"I don't know what you're talking about, but Ucchi first met Echidna more than ten years ago now. And ever since, she's barely left my side, right up to today. What Natsuki-kun's saying doesn't quite add up, does it?"

"More than ten years...?"

Anger welled up at the thought—had she been wandering the surface world all this time, doing as she pleased? Come to think of it, that Echidna couldn't leave that dream castle had only ever been her own claim to begin with. If he'd been deceived from that very point—

"Has this whole time been you laughing behind everyone's backs, at people who knew nothing?"

"...Good grief, that's a remarkably deep-rooted dislike. This misunderstanding sounds troublesome to untangle, but it has clarified one thing at least."

"Seems that way. Just like Echidna said."

In contrast to Subaru, whose anger toward Echidna only deepened, Anastasia and the white fox exchanged murmurs of mutual understanding. Subaru knit his brow at this, and the fox shook its head in an oddly human gesture.

"I'm sorry to say, since you've repeatedly voiced your distrust of 'Echidna' just now, but I have no memory of any of that. Or rather, the matter is more serious than that."

"What do you mean?"

"It's simple. I know nothing about any 'Echidna' besides myself. I have the self-awareness that I am an artificially created Spirit, and I remember my name. But that's it. Beyond that, I have nothing with which to even guess at myself. That's all there is to it."

"...Huh?"

Just when he wondered what it would say next, the white fox spouted nonsense unbecoming of Echidna. No—if this really was Echidna, then whatever she said was surely some scheme meant to toy with him. An artificial Spirit, claiming the name 'Echidna,' making contact with Subaru in a scene like this—how could he possibly believe it had no memories?

"Wow, that's a face that doesn't believe a word of it, Natsuki-kun."

"Of course it is. Knowing what kind of person Echidna was, that's the natural reaction. Though I suppose you're going to claim you don't know either."

"So you've decided not to believe it from the start. And with so little time to spare, too—what a troublesome one you are."

Even as Subaru made his wariness plain, Anastasia never lost her composed, unhurried air. As someone allied with Echidna, Subaru's guard toward Anastasia had shot up. Sorry as it was to Julius and Ricardo, he'd probably need to revise how much he trusted them too. He wasn't at ease enough to entertain any hopeful assumption that his head and his limbs weren't in agreement here.

"If you have no memories, why did you come forward and reveal yourself to me? Unless you were certain I know 'Echidna,' there shouldn't be any reason for you to do that."

Revealing her name, exposing her identity—wasn't that already proof enough that she was the 'Echidna of the tea party'? And if she was the 'Echidna of the tea party,' then she was a different person from the 'Echidna of the coffin' that Roswaal sought, and without doubt the very 'Echidna' Subaru knew. There was a mountain of things he wanted to ask. Even knowing full well this was no time for that kind of conversation. But to Subaru's impatience, Anastasia let out a tired sigh.

"It's getting complicated 'cause you haven't heard about the broadcast, huh. What a pain."

"Broadcast? What does the Witch Cult's broadcast have to do with any of this?"

"—Because one of their demands is 'hand over the artificial Spirit.'"

Plainly, the white fox answered Subaru's question. At those words, Subaru's thoughts went blank for a moment before understanding caught up. The broadcast, the Witch Cult's demand, an artificial Spirit. That was—

"No way..."

"It's not a demand I'd like to believe either. They're demanding the artificial Spirit—that is, either me, or the girl traveling with you. So it stands to reason we'd want to discuss this, don't you think?"

"How do you know Beatrice is an artificial Spirit?"

"I can only say I can tell just by looking. This isn't the sort of sense I can explain. I imagine she noticed the same about me, once she saw me move and speak."

The white fox calmly and methodically dismantled each of Subaru's objections one by one. At the very least, Subaru hadn't heard any mention of the fox stole from Beatrice, who should have encountered Anastasia at the inn. Though, given how Beatrice tended to bottle things up, it was possible she simply hadn't voiced information she wasn't certain of. As Subaru mulled over why she always had to carry everything alone by herself—a thought that would probably boomerang right back at him if he ever said it to Beatrice's face—

"I don't think it's any fault of your companion's that she didn't notice. I'm quite flawed as a Spirit, you see. I can't even mimic a proper Contract, and I can barely defend myself with Magic. In exchange, I'm rather confident in my ability to hide my presence. Though even that confidence is looking a bit shaky after today."

"Can't form a Contract... then, with Anastasia, what—"

"The relationship between Ucchi and this child isn't a Spirit-user's relationship. It's fundamentally different from the Contract exchanged between a Spirit and its contractor... You could say we're more like accomplices."

"I want to see where this child's road leads. That's why I've been tagging along, unreasonably, for years now, on and off lending an ear as a sounding board when needed."

As the white fox said this with a smile, Anastasia's fingertip tickled its throat. From how affectionate the two of them looked together, it was clear a considerable trust had been forged between them.

"Why are you confiding this sort of internal matter to me? That you have no fighting strength, that kind of thing."

"If I want the other party to trust me, disclosing information about myself is only natural, isn't it? Especially now, with the city's very survival at stake—an emergency demanding serious commitments from everyone—I'd rather avoid any strange squabbling among allies."

"Ucchi did try to stop her, told her to think it over a bit more, but this one doesn't listen once she's made up her mind. At least I got everyone else to clear the room."

Hearing the fox's claims—claims Subaru might have nodded along to readily enough, if only it hadn't called itself Echidna—and Anastasia's supplementary remarks, Subaru finally realized that Anastasia's coldly detached attitude from before had been a deliberate act, meant to set up an environment for speaking with him one-on-one. Drawing Subaru's attention to herself, in order to clear the room of Julius and Garfiel.

"Going along with the Witch Cult's demands is out of the question. But if Ucchi and Natsuki-kun keep playing dumb, everyone else is gonna be left not knowin' what an 'artificial Spirit' even is. Even to refuse, if nobody understands the situation, we can't get everyone else to accept it."

"I agree we can't give in to a terrorist's demands. ...But that doesn't mean my suspicions are cleared up just because you say that fox is Echidna."

"You're really stuck on the name, huh. Was this 'Echidna' you know really that nasty a person? I don't much like this one being lumped in with her."

"If she were a completely different person, I'd apologize. But the way she talks, the timing of all this—it's too much like the real Echidna for me to find anything to deny it on. And besides..."

"And besides?"

Anastasia tilted her head, and Subaru hesitated over whether he should share more. Even Subaru wasn't entirely clear on what, concretely, an 'artificial Spirit' was. All he knew was that Beatrice and Puck were examples of it, and that the witch Echidna was the one who had made them. Naturally, if it was true that the white fox before him was also an artificial Spirit, then there was little doubt its existence, too, had been crafted by the witch Echidna's hand.

Desiring eternal life, Echidna had devised a mechanism for creating duplicate bodies. At this point there was room to doubt how much of that claim had even been true, but supposing it had been true, arriving at Spirits as the conclusion was a rational one. Losing one's physical body, freed from the concept of a lifespan—a Spirit. If one could sublimate one's own existence into something like that, it would surely satisfy a witch's greed to know absolutely everything.

So Subaru had no doubts that this white fox was an 'artificial Spirit claiming to be Echidna.' The only real point of contention was the truth or falsehood of its claim to have lost its memories. Anastasia said it had been more than ten years. But that alone wasn't reason enough to believe it. It wouldn't be strange at all for someone to spend more than ten years preparing, given enough time. All of it, perhaps, purely to ensnare Subaru here, in this very moment—

"Ah, this is bad. Seems like you got put through something real awful."

"Being disliked to this degree almost makes me curious about this other Echidna of yours... but let's set that aside for now. If you can't trust me, that's fine too. Feel free to let my words pass right through you. Ana, if you would explain."

Falling silent, sinking into suspicion, Subaru drew a resigned murmur from the white fox. Prompted, Anastasia shrugged and clapped her hands as if to reset the mood.

"Right, right. Anyway, let's set that suspicion aside for now. What matters is the Witch Cult's demands—that takes priority. The Witch Cult's put forward three more demands besides that one."

"Three more... so four in total?"

"Yep. They gave the city hall back to us, so... well, it's just the number of remaining control towers, I guess. They're all ridiculous, but don't go gettin' mad, alright?"

Being warned they were ridiculous demands hardly mattered at this point. His impression of the Witch Cult was already the worst of the worst, piled on top of worse still. He was confident that whatever they said now, it wouldn't budge his impression of them by a hair.

To Subaru, who silently urged her to continue, Anastasia stroked the white fox's tail with her hand. Then she lightly wet her lips with her tongue.

"First, one of the demands is to hand over the 'artificial Spirit.' That's the demand from 'Gluttony.'"

"Gluttony...!"

"I know you've got plenty of feelings about that, but later. Next up is the demand from 'Wrath'—the recovery of the 'Book of Wisdom' brought into this city."

"—?!"

Hit once more by a shock he hadn't seen coming, Subaru's eyes went wide. Seeing his reaction, Anastasia narrowed her pale blue-green eyes slightly.

"Judging by that face, seems like Natsuki-kun's heard that word before."

"............."

"Ah, good, that's a relief. This one had us stumped too—Ucchi didn't know, and Echidna said she didn't either. Naturally, neither did Julius or anyone else."

"...You really don't know? That fox? It's the 'Book of Wisdom.'"

At having stumbled onto an unknown demand, Anastasia smiled as though she'd found a lifeline. Of course, there was surely nothing so charmingly heartwarming lurking behind that smile. Still, the puzzled look on her face at Subaru's follow-up question was probably genuine.

"No, I don't know. That much is true as well. ...I take it, from your reaction, that this 'Book of Wisdom' has something to do with the Echidna you know."

"Yeah, that's right."

"Well, in that case, it seems there's no use playing dumb any longer. Just who is this Echidna Natsuki-kun knows? She's not unrelated to the Witch Cult, is she?"

To the point that Subaru wanted to suspect they were feigning ignorance, Anastasia and the white fox's understanding seemed astonishingly limited, despite their position being close to that of the parties involved. And yet, it was only natural that they'd think this way.

If the white fox's claims were to be believed, the name 'Echidna' referred to nothing more than the individual name of this particular fox. Subaru was the only one who knew that the creator of artificial Spirits was Echidna—and more than that, none of the witches' names, save for the 'Witch of Envy,' had survived widely enough to be commonly remembered at all.

Only those among the Emilia Camp who knew of her existence, even fragmentarily, would feel any sense of danger at that name.

"—Echidna is the name of a witch from long ago. Besides the 'Witch of Envy,' there used to be other witches in the past. She's one of them, and she herself died long ago. But—I've had the experience of meeting what remained of her, just a soul. That's why I'm on guard."

"Natsuki-kun, you got a fever or somethin'? Heard you took quite the injury."

"I'm not half-asleep and I haven't lost my mind. Witches are just that kind of strange bunch. If they can artificially create Spirits, something like that isn't out of the question either."

"I see. ...So this Echidna is also this me's birth parent, in a manner of speaking."

Through Subaru's careless, irritation-laced reply, the white fox read between the lines. It wasn't as though he'd been deliberately hiding it, but the fact that he hadn't stated it outright quietly brought a twinge of guilt. Perhaps that inner feeling showed on his face, because the white fox let out a small chuckle in its throat.

"Stumbling onto the secret of my own birth in such an unexpected place—that too is one of life's little pleasures. If the chance arises, I'd very much like to hear the details."

"...For a Spirit, you sure don't know much about your own circumstances. Even our Beatrice knew a bit more than that. No, actually—I've never even heard about your existence from Beatrice at all. Just how did you come to be born?"

"Unfortunately, I don't know that either. I have no idea of the circumstances of my own birth, let alone any sense of purpose. I've simply been sticking by this child, who caught my interest, ever since."

At Subaru's remark, the white fox looked toward Anastasia. Under that gaze, Anastasia tilted her head slightly, then slowly turned to look at Subaru.

"The connection between Echidna and the witches is somethin' Ucchi's curious about too. It's probably tied to the Witch Cult somehow, no doubt. So, I'd like to bring the talk back to the 'Book of Wisdom'..."

"The 'Book of Wisdom' is—according to what I was told—the prototype of the gospels the Witch Cult carries. The complete original version they were based on. A book of prophecy that records the future, working on a mechanism similar to that Dragon History Stone thing."

"Similar to the Dragon History Stone... if that's so, sounds pretty credible. And this book, was it maybe handmade by the witch herself, by any chance?"

"That's what she claimed, at least. But the two copies that existed should have burned. Aside from those, I've never heard of—don't know of—any other copies existing."

The two who had been entrusted with the 'Book of Wisdom' by Echidna were Beatrice and Roswaal. It had been confirmed that the two copies each of them had possessed had both burned in the incident a year ago.

Beatrice's 'Book of Wisdom' had turned to ash along with the burning forbidden archive, and the 'Book of Wisdom' Roswaal had owned had been burned by Ram's own hand, out of fear of what the future held. With both gone, there shouldn't be any surviving copy of the 'Book of Wisdom' left.

"But doesn't that all come with the caveat that you only heard it from that witch Echidna's own mouth?"

"...Yeah."

Anastasia cut cleanly through Subaru's assertion. He could only manage an unconvincing reply, because he himself was aware of just how thin that credibility was. And yet, it hadn't felt to him, in that moment, like Echidna had been lying. This much, he suspected, only he—who had spoken with the witch directly—could really appreciate.

"————"

"You don't trust that person. But you trust what that person says. That's an awfully troublesome relationship you've got there, Natsuki-kun."

"Yeah, I thought so myself. It's true. I mean to distrust her, but on this one thing, I believe her—it's a contradiction."

Echidna's every act had been staged, meant to turn Subaru into her puppet. And yet, not everything had been coated in falsehood—was that just Subaru wanting to believe it? Was his heart still being swayed by that witch, even now?

"Never mind how you feel about it, Natsuki-kun—that leaves three possibilities for the 'Book of Wisdom.' One, that witch was lying, and there's still another copy of the 'Book of Wisdom' out there somewhere."

"Two would be that the Witch Cult idiots are just kicking up a fuss without knowing the 'Book of Wisdom' already burned... right? But what's the third?"

"If there's a possibility the 'Book of Wisdom' survives that isn't a lie, there's only one reason for it—the 'Book of Wisdom' didn't burn all the way through. Maybe some of it survived the fire, no?"

"—Wha—"

The possibility that the 'Book of Wisdom' had partially survived the fire—that was something he'd never even considered. As Subaru fell speechless, Anastasia raised a finger and wagged it.

"I don't know the actual thing, but it's a book the witch made, right? Maybe it doesn't burn easily, or, who knows, maybe it even restores itself back to how it was... can't rule it out completely, can you think?"

"I— sure, I can't say that's impossible. I can't say that, but... but realistically, even if it did restore itself, who'd even have it—"

"Someone who watched it burn and managed to recover it... maybe? I think Ucchi might be overthinking this one, honestly, so I'd guess the second possibility's more likely. Either way, we're not about to give in to their demands regardless."

Mulling it over, listing out possibilities, Subaru put a hand to his mouth in thought. The position from which someone could have retrieved the 'Book of Wisdom' as it burned. The one from the forbidden archive was impossible—that one had been swallowed up when the mansion burned down along with the archive itself. But what about Roswaal's copy? He'd heard the result was that Ram burned it with Magic, but after that, the ashes should have been scattered somewhere in the 'Sanctuary,' buried under the snow, by Emilia's own mana. If that had somehow survived the fire. And if, on top of that, the book truly did have some restorative ability. It wouldn't be strange for someone to be holding onto it. It wouldn't be strange, but—

"If anyone could've brought it here, it'd be someone close to me. But there'd be no reason for them to keep quiet about finding a book like that. The 'Book of Wisdom' isn't in this city."

"I see. Well, that's fine by me then. Ucchi was just feelin' uneasy about a book I don't even really know much about, that's all."

Certainty—calling it that would be giving too much credit to what was really just an opinion weighted too heavily by his own wish to believe it. But Anastasia didn't point that out, nor did she try to drag the topic of the 'Book of Wisdom' out any further. Instead—

"So, next demand. The remaining two, from Greed and Lust, are pretty straightforward, plain enough there's hardly any point discussing them, but..."

"Those two... no, well, Gluttony and Wrath were bad enough already, so it's a bit late to act surprised, but—what?"

He had nothing but the worst impressions of both Capella of 'Lust' and Regulus of 'Greed.' For Capella, the memory of what had just happened; for Regulus, the fact that Emilia was still being held. His right leg's transformation, and the fact that he'd heard no further word about Emilia—both weighed heavily on his chest.

"Now, try not to get too mad hearin' this, okay..."

"Depends on what it is."

To Anastasia's uneasy preamble, Subaru answered immediately. At his answer, Anastasia let out a breath, as if resigned, muttering "Figured as much..."

"Lust's demand is different from the others—it's just plain harassment. Um, it went somethin' like, 'Deliver twenty pairs of men and women who are mutually in love to the control tower. We absolutely will not harm them.'"

"That's got to be the biggest load of BS I've ever heard. 'Won't harm them,' my ass. Has that bastard forgotten he's the one who turned people into flies and dragons?!"

At Capella's unwaveringly wicked demand, Subaru's anger flared openly. Twenty pairs of men and women in love—it was horrifying even to imagine what that monster, singing the praises of such a hideous love, would do faced with couples like that.

"From the way an Archbishop of Sin thinks, I suppose changin' someone's appearance don't even count as harm. I can just picture him brushin' it off with 'I didn't leave a scratch.' And now, for Greed's demand..."

"————"

Silent, anger still burning in his eyes, Subaru waited on Anastasia's words. At his manner, Anastasia faltered somewhat, unable to bring herself to spit out the main point. Into that gap—

"The demand from the one claiming to speak for Greed is this: 'Hold my wedding to the silver-haired bride. Do not interfere in any way with the preparations for the ceremony.'"

"Echidna..."

"Ana was having trouble getting it out. Forgive me for overstepping."

In Anastasia's stead, unable to bring herself to say it, the white fox guarding her throat spoke up first. The exchange between the two of them, each looking out for the other, didn't register with Subaru now.

—Hold a wedding with the silver-haired bride.

There was no need to think about who the bride was that 'Greed'—that man, Regulus—intended to wed.

"—Cut the crap, you bastard!"

And so, Subaru's outburst, the anger erupting straight onto his tongue, was entirely justified. Anastasia grimaced, and the white fox's fur bristled. That was how pure, how straightforward, Subaru's fury was.

The white-haired man surfaced in his mind's eye. The one who had taken Emilia away, who had declared without hesitation that her worth lay entirely in her appearance—an utterly loathsome madman wielding transcendent power. A deluded man who merely happened to possess overwhelming strength—what nonsense was he spouting now?

"Beatrice, the 'Book of Wisdom,' Emilia too! Not one of them, not a single thing, goes to those bastards! Don't screw with me, that's enough already!"

"...Figured you'd react like that, but seein' it play out exactly as expected feels almost satisfying."

Watching Subaru raise his voice in a fit of passion, Anastasia laughed, almost delighted. But that smile hadn't sprung from any cheerful sentiment. Underneath it burned another kind of passion entirely, one of the same nature as Subaru's own.

"With this whole artificial Spirit business, Ucchi's in the same boat—can't hand over Echidna either. But even setting that aside, there's no way we can afford to lose to them mentally. Crusch-san and Emilia, Ucchi's the one who invited them to this city. Hurt like this, made a fool of like this—there's no way Ucchi can just sit quiet about it."

"You're quite the fighter, Ana."

"Just know when the moment to make my stand is, that's all. Know I can't run from this one. Gotta round up every last one of the Witch Cult and make 'em pay for the damages."

Missteps, humiliation, profit and loss. Dressing it up in every kind of language, Anastasia never once let her fighting spirit slacken. In a situation where even Julius, Garfiel, Wilhelm, and Ferris had grown pessimistic, one could say this was just big talk from someone who hadn't personally felt the terror of an Archbishop of Sin on her own skin.

But Subaru didn't say that. There was no point in saying something that would break their allies' morale in a situation like this. If anything, right now, her presence, burning with the fire of retaliation, was without question something he could rely on.

"Everybody's shrinkin' down small. But if we let 'em take the first move, and the second move too, and just let the whole board end like that—who's gonna be satisfied with that? Save your excuses for losing for the afterlife."

"————"

"Long as you're alive, there's a now and a next. Don't throw your life away. That's just too pitiful for yourself."

Her face gentle, yet with a fierce, savage smile spreading across it, Anastasia declared it like a proclamation. The ferocity radiating from that small girl's frame was enough to make one forget she wasn't the type meant to stand on a battlefield. No—she was, in fact, a battle-hardened veteran. In the field of her profession, waging war amid the flames of conflict, she was a soldier tempered by a hundred battles.

"Crusch-san, and the people who got their bodies changed—there's a real good chance 'Lust' himself can fix 'em, if he's the one who did it. Just catch him and put him in the mood to. And Natsuki-kun, you're not plannin' on just leavin' your precious princess taken from you either, are you?"

"Damn right I'm not! Emilia's gonna be my bride. I'll beat Gluttony to death and get Rem's memories back. Sirius keeps comparing himself to me, so I'll knock him flat. I'll make Capella grovel and turn everyone back to normal. And that's how we drive 'em back!"

"Both Ana and Natsuki-boy, tryin' to push through the impossible like it's the obvious thing to do. That's probably the biggest reason I find the two of you so likable."

Subaru answered Anastasia's rallying cry, and seeing this, the white fox nodded, satisfied. Setting aside, for now, the sunken feeling he'd carried since waking, and through the start of this whole conversation, Subaru let himself be swept up in Anastasia's fighting spirit.

So what if they'd lost the first round, the second round. There was a next time. No one was dead yet. He wouldn't let anyone die. Win in the end, win and survive. If that could be achieved, then Subaru and the others would have won.

"Ricardo's out scoutin' the area, lookin' for Joshua and the other kids. Should be back before too long. Once he is, let's have ourselves a proper strategy meeting, all together."

"We'll need to check on the current state of the town, and what's going on with the control towers too."

"No slackin' off there. Seems like your head's startin' to work again."

The task had been decided—and so, his resolve had been decided too. That was all there was to it.

Nodding along to Anastasia's words, Subaru turned his gaze toward the world outside the city hall. Through the window, which showed only one face of the city, he could see the control tower rising high above.

He couldn't tell which of the four—north, south, east, or west—it was. But somewhere among them was Emilia, was Rem's memories, was the sworn enemy, was the monster.

—The fight to save the city, to save the people who mattered to him, was still going on.
