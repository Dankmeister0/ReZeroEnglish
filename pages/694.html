Arc 8 Finale: "Priscilla Barielle"

—The Great Disaster had been driven back, and the Sacred Vollachia Empire had at last escaped its brush with ruin.

Centered on the imperial capital, the armies of the Undead that had spread across the empire lost their unity and crumbled, scattering in flight as the battle wound toward its close. But even with Sphinx, who had commanded them, gone, and even with the flow of mana sustaining the Sacrament of the Immortal King cut off from the "Stone Mass," the Undead who had already been revived would go on struggling through what remained of their new lives until death was dealt to them once more.

Even once the fight against the Great Disaster was decided, the scars it left behind would never simply vanish.

No doubt, in the Vollachia Empire, troubles caused by Undead who had slipped away and fled would keep flaring up for some time to come. And leaving that aside, war itself is never something that wraps up neatly the moment the fighting stops — nothing bound up in it gets tidied away so cleanly.

Of course, the cities and lands that had become battlegrounds, the people and things that had been sacrificed — all of it needed to be accounted for, and set right, brought back to how it had been before the war, or better.

And sadly, for that kind of postwar cleanup, a banner reading "Heroic Fantasy" does you no good at all.

"So yeah, it's true that I've got surprisingly little to do…"

"—What. Have you some complaint to lodge with me?"

"Not a complaint exactly, just, I dunno, it feels weird?"

Grumbling that, Subaru chose his words carefully as he answered the beautiful woman walking beside him — Priscilla's — profile. At his answer, she hid her mouth behind the fan in her hand and gave a small "hmph" through her nose.

Already used to that reaction of hers, Subaru, walking alongside her, tilted his head in puzzlement all the same.

—Right now, Subaru and Priscilla were walking together within the walls of the fortress city of Garkla.

Foremost among the empire's fortresses, its walls had taken considerable damage in the crush against the horde of Undead, and restoration work — soldier and citizen alike — was proceeding through the night without pause. That Subaru found himself walking such streets side by side with Priscilla was quite the strange twist of fate.

Both Subaru and Priscilla had taken part in the decisive battle against the Great Disaster, Sphinx, in the imperial capital of Lupugana, but concern for the safety of those left behind in the fortress city, along with how ill-suited the capital's devastation was to settling down in, were the main reasons they'd since moved on.

"Even the Crystal Palace, said to be the most beautiful in the world, is ash now, and who knows when the reservoir's water will overflow again and flood the city. At this rate, the capital's restoration will take a hundred years."

"That's a heck of a scale you're — I mean, you're the one who turned the palace to ash, and you're saying that?"

"Once it took humanoid form and ran amok, there was no salvaging it as a castle whether I lifted a hand or not. So I simply made grand use of it, as a pyre to send off the thing that had fulfilled the Great Disaster's role."

"—A send-off fire for Sphinx, huh."

Priscilla answered without a hint of remorse, and Subaru lowered his eyes, thinking of the enemy that had been Sphinx.

This was something he couldn't say to any of those who had faced the Great Disaster, but at the very last, to bring out the effect of Spica's "Star Devouring," Subaru had confronted Sphinx head-on.

In that moment, it could be said that Subaru and Sphinx had bared even their souls to one another. Perhaps that was why Subaru couldn't bring himself to hate Sphinx as pure evil, deep down. At the very least, he'd felt she was different from an unforgivable enemy like the Witch Cult's Sin Archbishops.

And he'd thought that Priscilla might have felt something close to that too—

"That was a fine, grand pillar of fire. There is truly something stirring about watching a thing burn."

"Wait, is that just because you like burning stuff!?"

"What, so noisy. Do not suddenly raise your voice like that. You sound like Al."

At a glance, it sounded like an insult, but Priscilla's lips were smiling as she said it. Their relationship was hard to parse, but in her own way, she did care for her attendant Al. That much was beyond doubt, considering the reunion between Priscilla and Al in the capital.

"So the way things looked, Al doesn't want to leave your side, but where's he off to now?"

"Just as you say, since he would not leave me be, I ordered him to run errands. No doubt he is, even now, slaving away drooling at the thought of my praise. Much like you, besotted as you are with the half-demon."

"Can't deny it and won't deny it, but Al's kind of pitiable, getting jerked around like that…"

"—Priscilla-sama!"

At the voice that called out "Oh," Subaru raised his eyebrows, and a young boy came trotting up the road toward them — Schult. The short-panted boy attendant, breathless, skidded to a hurried stop in front of Priscilla and said,

"Um, Priscilla-sama, I'm so glad you're safe! I, I was so very — mmph."

"There is no need for a child to fuss over such trivialities. If you were worried for me, then stand tall about it."

So saying, Priscilla drew the halted Schult into an embrace, the boy's head buried in her chest. Buried literally, no figure of speech about it, which left Subaru feeling like he'd witnessed a difference in life-form rather than merely gender.

Like Al, Schult was one of Priscilla's attendants, and one who had worried himself sick over her not returning from the capital; his face was flushed nearly to melting at the contact.

Indeed, given how overjoyed he was that the capital group — the members of "Team Save the Vollachia Empire from Ruin" — had returned, it had been worth pushing the dragon carriage to hurry back.

"Guess it was worth getting smacked by Patrasche the second I showed up in this body again…"

"Natsuki-sama, please tell Emilia-sama thank you as well! And also, I want to be even more useful to Priscilla-sama, so I'd like to ask the secret to sudden growth spurts!"

"I'd love to see Emilia-tan's proud face over being thanked any number of times, but my sudden growth spurt was a total foul play move. If you grew this fast through the normal route, I think the growing pains alone would kill you."

"To begin with, you have no need to stretch your height needlessly. Stay as you are, for now."

"Uu, I'm caught in the middle, I am…"

Torn between his own wish and his master's, Schult's face was one of agony. Subaru understood the mix of fondness and admiration Schult felt for Priscilla too, so he sympathized with the boy's turmoil.

"Well, sympathizing with you doesn't give me any actually useful advice, though!"

"Rather than trivial, I would call it pitiable, this man. Schult, are you out wandering alone?"

"No, that's not it! Utakata-sama is with the rest of the Shudrak, so I…"

Buried in Priscilla's chest, Schult glanced back over his shoulder. Following the gesture, Subaru looked down the street and spotted a familiar sour face there — Heinkel.

Subaru knew he had come to the empire and helped repel the Great Disaster, but even so.

"Schult, that old man's not bullying you or anything, is he?"

"No such thing! Heinkel-sama is a very kind person!"

"That's absolutely a lie."

Even among family, spreading a mistaken impression is no good. Saying Heinkel was kind was like calling Roswaal an honest good-natured soul.

In fact, Heinkel himself seemed to find Schult's assessment bitter to swallow.

"Runt, don't go saying unnecessary things. I'll leave you behind."

"Do not speak such foolishness, you mediocrity. How Schult is treated is for me to decide. Rather, you ought to fear being left behind by the empire yourself."

"Guh…! This time around, I did my share… my share, of…"

"If you mean to boast that you were useful, at least say so plainly."

Priscilla told the scowling, tongue-tied Heinkel this with a bored look in her eyes. Then she turned the Schult in her chest around and pushed him toward Heinkel.

"Schult, I have many people yet to see. It is late. Do not stay up."

"I-I understand! Somehow I'm all wide awake now, but I'll do my best to go to sleep!"

To Schult, whose excitement clearly hadn't worn off, Priscilla nodded, "That's fine." Then, looking at Heinkel — left speechless as Schult pressed close to him — she said,

"You did well, Heinkel Astrea."

"Ah?"

"Your way of living is unsightly beyond bearing, but the blade you have honed alone has something worth watching. Take heed — even should it go unrewarded, never forget to hone yourself further."

"…Whether I'm rewarded or not is up to you, isn't it, Priscilla-jou."

So saying, Heinkel turned his face away and roughly scratched at his red hair before walking off. To that retreating back, Subaru called out "Old man," too.

"Garfiel made it. —Our little brother owes you one."

"—Keh."

Ill-tempered, Heinkel walked off without even breaking his stride. Subaru watched Schult, bowing deeply, hurry off after that retreating back, then shrugged at Priscilla.

"Last time you wouldn't give me a straight answer, but why are you with Reinhard's old man?"

"There is no man who is 'someone's father.' A question not even worth answering."

"Ugh… when you say something proper like that, it makes my chest hurt."

Pressing a hand to his own chest at that, Subaru got rapped on the head with Priscilla's fan and a "Fool." As he rubbed the spot where he'd been struck, Priscilla lightly jerked her chin.

"There, are you trying to bore me? Play the clown's role properly, Natsuki Subaru."

Called by name once again like that, somehow he found he had no will to refuse.

"Have the two of you come together just to carve a wrinkle into my busy brow?"

At the sight of Subaru and Priscilla poking their heads in, Abel — true to his word — carved a wrinkle into his brow.

It was a room in the great fortress, not quite grand enough to be called an office, but Abel seemed to be buried in work through the night, needing to swiftly establish a plan for the postwar response.

Same as Priscilla, he too should have been one of those who'd wielded the Sunblade and fought the moving castle.

"It really is off-putting how tough you are. Guess I don't get to have a catfight with you anymore."

"To begin with, I never intended to indulge in such a suspicious ritual with you. If you've come to bother me over some worthless matter, get out at once."

"You say get out if I'm going to bother you, but…"

At the emperor's brush-off, Subaru glanced at the sofa beside the desk where he worked. There, shoulder to shoulder, slept Flop and Medium, peaceful and content.

The safely reunited O'Connell siblings were a heartwarming sight, but the contrast with the emperor beside them was stark.

"You're not kicking these two out, huh?"

"…I merely know that waking them would only cause more trouble."

"How very like my elder brother — ever indulgent where the things dear to him are concerned. That I was permitted to flee abroad was likewise due to my brother's affection."

Fanning her face pat-pat with her fan, Priscilla said this so casually that Abel fell silent. It was a silence that could be taken either as "whatever I say, it changes nothing" or as one struck by a truth hitting home.

"Sis-con emperor…"

"I know not the meaning, but I can guess that whatever it is, it's the height of disrespect."

"Enough with these idle threats you cannot carry out. Because of this Great Disaster, the empire has run up too great a debt to both the Kingdom and the city-states. Especially to Natsuki Subaru's camp."

"Priscilla… it's not my camp, it's Emilia-tan's camp."

A correction rare enough from Priscilla, but Subaru made sure to fix the important part all the same. At which Priscilla gave an exasperated "Well-trained, aren't you," while Abel quietly pressed his fingers to his brow.

Seeing Abel close both eyes as he did so, Subaru thought, "Huh."

"—What is it, Natsuki Subaru. Do you intend yet more disrespect toward me?"

"Nah, it's not that. Not looking for a fight either, so if you're busy I'll head out."

Pursing his lips, Subaru didn't point out what he'd noticed. He didn't think Abel was doing it unconsciously, either. There had to be a real reason, a real turning point, behind the change.

And it seemed Subaru wasn't the only one pleased by that change.

"Priscilla, you as well. I have much yet that must be done."

"The empire over your sister. After ten years, has the scale finally settled within my brother's heart? Then—"

"What?"

At Priscilla trailing off, Abel's brow furrowed faintly. Before him, Priscilla pinched the hem of her dress and dropped into a deep bow right there.

And then—

"—Sacred Vollachia Empire's seventy-seventh Emperor, His Excellency Vincent Vollachia. From the bottom of my heart, I offer congratulations upon your ascension to the throne."

"————"

"Do try your best to keep the pack of sword-wolves in line. No doubt uneasy days will continue for you a while yet."

Priscilla offered her congratulations with a serious expression, and her warning after with a fearless smile. At his sister's freely shifting tempo, Abel — Vincent Vollachia — let out a sigh.

Then he glanced briefly at the O'Connell siblings sleeping shoulder to shoulder on the sofa, and said,

"Priscilla. —You are the only sister in this world I have ever been able to let my guard down around."

"How very my elder brother, this roundabout confession of affection."

Priscilla interpreted Abel's remark that way while hiding her provocative smile behind the fan she'd opened with a snap. On that point, Subaru, present in the room, was entirely of the same opinion.

Only, if there was one thing to add as a third party—

"Personally, I think you two are annoyingly alike as siblings."

At Subaru's remark, the two so-alike siblings glared at him with matching displeased eyes.

"Were you speaking with His Excellency Vincent, Priscilla?"

"Hm, Mother?"

Having thoroughly savored two flavors of imperial sibling dynamics — royal family and merchant family — Subaru and Priscilla ran into Yorna in the fortress corridor, accompanied by Eugard.

The two seemed headed for Abel's room, apparently having business with him just as Subaru and Priscilla were leaving.

Yorna narrowed her sharp, slanted eyes and looked Subaru and the others over closely. Subaru figured she found this pairing rare, but that wasn't quite it.

"This be the first I've laid eyes upon it, but a child with such stretched-out limbs, that's not a sight I'm quite used to, is it."

"…Ah! Come to think of it, I've only ever shown Yorna-san the crossdressing and shota versions of me!?"

A shocking realization surfaced, and Subaru was struck by just how many variations of himself he'd shown off in the empire. At Subaru's astonishment, Yorna laughed, the back of her hand against her mouth.

"Your Excellency, this one here be the kingdom's knight. Til not so long ago he'd shrunk into the form of a child, but when I first met him, he wore the form of a fair maiden."

"Hm. That my Star should be called fair to behold — well, I confess I am not entirely without interest."

"No no no, please don't. I mean, sure, Natsumi Schwartz is a noble lady you could show off to anyone without shame, but…"

"For a mediocrity, a technique worthy of praise, but your self-assessment is skewed."

At Subaru's answer, wavering between calm and passion, Priscilla commented with exasperation. Then she turned her attention lightly toward Abel's room.

"For what reason do Mother and the 'King of Thorns' come to call upon my brother?"

"My own remaining time is surely little enough. Before it runs out, I wish to speak of my child's children, and of my Star's wish."

"Remaining time…"

At Eugard's answer, Subaru's eyes drooped as he looked toward Yorna.

—Even as he stood there so naturally with Yorna, Eugard too was one of the Undead. Like Sphinx, his appearance was indistinguishable from that of the living, but that fact didn't waver.

"No doubt my own sense of fulfillment in life affects my appearance. For my part too, I would rather this than embracing my Star with a body that carries no warmth."

"Though it be a bond of soul rather than blood, to boldly proclaim love for Mother before her daughter — that is fitting, indeed, of the 'King of Thorns.' So, what is Mother's wish?"

"—. It concerns the binding of my soul, which keeps being reborn."

At Yorna's quiet words, Subaru knew only the barest outline of what she meant.

Yorna had once been a girl called Iris, and her soul, each time she died, returned to the surface without receiving the baptism of Od Lagna, being revived through rebirth into a new body, again and again. So what Yorna wished of Abel, in return for her contribution to this civil war and against the Great Disaster, was—

"—A revocation, if it please you. Of the empire's decree of extermination against the Wolf Humans and the Mole Humans."

"Eh…"

"To begin with, that was ever my wish… though when I became one of the 'Nine Divine Generals,' I prioritized receiving a demon city to welcome my Beloved Children."

"—I see. What is used to bind Mother's soul is the flesh, blood, and lives of those two races, Wolf Human and Mole Human, accumulated since ancient times. Cut off that supply, and the Curse ought naturally to vanish."

"And at the time, it was I, none other, who issued that decree. Should I ask the current Emperor to revoke it with my own mouth, neither my Star's name nor that of my child's children need bear any stain."

So saying, Eugard turned a gentle gaze upon Yorna, who held her own arm.

In that moment, Subaru understood that behind the unchanging expression of the "King of Thorns," regret and shame had smoldered for many long years.

"Iris and the King of Thorns" — within this world's long-told tale, what drama had unfolded between him and her, Subaru could not know.

But if, supposing, it had ended in tragedy, then this was its epilogue.

"Then I think it's fine if there's at least a little bit of a happy aftertaste to the ending."

"A difference of opinion. Later, needless deeds may well prove nothing but a superfluous addition."

"You— !"

Since the complicated party involved, who was nonetheless supposed to be Yorna's daughter, seemed set to stir up trouble, Subaru was about to scold Priscilla to read the room. But before he could, she continued with a "However"—

"Between Mother and the 'King of Thorns,' surely there'd be no such crude a thing done."

"—. Indeed, there is still a trace of Teriola in you. Do you not think so, my Star?"

"Now that you mention it, indeed… no wonder I find her all the more dear."

"Do not speak of such things comparing me to anyone else. Only for Mother would I permit it."

Rebuked sharply by Priscilla, Yorna and Eugard's lips softened into smiles.

It was a strange relationship — not truly what it appeared, and yet from outside the three of them looked for all the world like a close-knit family. Subaru, feeling somehow ticklish at the sight, scratched his cheek.

At that gesture of his, Yorna smiled, "Seems I've bored you," and said,

"His Excellency and I shall be off. Priscilla, don't you go quarreling with the child, now."

"Do not treat me as a child. First of all, should this mediocrity anger me, I'll simply take his head."

"That's a scary thing to say! Well then, Yorna-san, Eugard-san, see you around."

Amid the giggling, mother-and-child-like exchange between Yorna and Priscilla, Subaru called that out to Eugard. He'd said "see you around," but somehow it felt like this was the last time.

The other must have thought the same. The former Emperor looked straight at Subaru and said,

"May you pass good days with those you love. May fortune favor you, and your Star."

"W-wow, Subaru and Priscilla together — that's suuuper surprising."

"Really, it is. There's still lots left to do, and if it rains tomorrow, that'll be a problem."

So saying, Anastasia gazed out the window at the night sky, and Priscilla gave a "hmph" through her nose.

Having found himself unexpectedly having many chances to talk with people, Subaru stopped by the break room for water and ran into Emilia and Anastasia with their heads together over a table.

Ever since the expedition to the Pleiades Watchtower, this wasn't such a rare pairing anymore. As Emilia had been surprised to see, it was Subaru and Priscilla together who were the far more surprising sight.

Still, physical labor and mental labor — what were these two, with such different roles, doing here together?

"Um, actually, Otto-kun scolded me and told me I'm not allowed to work any more today…"

"To begin with, it's not good for us to be sticking our noses too far into the empire's postwar cleanup, is it? So we figured we'd chat a bit before turning in… but Emilia-san's bragging about Natsuki-kun just wouldn't end."

"Eh, about me? Emilia-tan was? I wanna hear, I wanna hear."

Hearing there'd been an eager girl-talk session, Subaru leaned in with interest. But his collar was grabbed from behind, and a "Gweeh!" scream slipped out of him.

Naturally, the one responsible was Priscilla, who was less about choking Subaru and more capable of taking his head off entirely. She released his collar and fixed Emilia and Anastasia with a warning look.

"A half-demon and a vixen, purportedly candidates in the Royal Selection no less, meeting face to face, and the topic is a mediocrity's exploits? Do not waste your time on such worthless things. Counting stars would be a somewhat better use of it."

"Ah, I get that. When I was alone in the forest, I used to pass the time like that sometimes too…"

"Me, I spent more time counting up saved coin than that, myself."

"Hey now, that outrageous remark of Priscilla's just now isn't a good direction for the topic to expand in."

Telling them to go count stars was practically on the level of "give up everything and go to sleep" — that outrageous a remark. Of course, Subaru too could kill infinite time star-gazing by hunting constellations, but that wasn't the point.

As he headed off that potential expansion of the topic into the night sky, Emilia suddenly gave a soft "fufu" and laughed.

"But somehow it's really strange, isn't it. Anastasia-san and I and Priscilla — we're all candidates for king of the Kingdom, and yet here we all are in the empire."

"If Natsuki-kun hadn't gotten himself carelessly blown off from the tower, none of this woulda happened."

"That was unavoidable, and if I hadn't gotten blown off, the empire probably would've fallen, no question."

That didn't erase the incident that had started it all, but considering the scale of the Great Disaster, it wasn't entirely wrong either. He wouldn't say it was a good thing he got blown off, but it meant something — or rather, he'd made it mean something.

Though Priscilla might well scoff at that kind of talk—

"—Indeed. Without the lot of you, the empire's history would have ended yesterday or today."

"Wow" "Huh" "Oh?"

"Do not, all together, make such foolish noises. I merely stated an obvious fact."

Merely that, Priscilla said, but that she'd even said merely that was surprising enough to Subaru and the others.

At Priscilla's unexpected words, Anastasia gave a knowing little laugh, "What, what's this?"

"Now that's unexpected. That you'd admit so honestly that we helped you all out."

"Do not misunderstand. The empire was Priscilla Benedict's home country, and it is no longer mine. Responsibility for the empire's survival lies with the Emperor and the soldiers who serve him."

"And then this happens. Yes, this, this is how our princess ought to be — no fun otherwise."

"Now, now, Priscilla's just being contrary, so don't tease her, Anastasia-san."

"Contrary — don't hear that word much these days…"

At that Emilia-like yardstick of a word, Subaru gave a wry smile, half exasperated and half impressed.

Still, he understood a little of how Emilia and Anastasia felt. Once, Priscilla had been too much of an unknown to Subaru — like some other creature he couldn't comprehend.

Her insight and strength he'd acknowledged, but her humanity had seemed like that of a carnivorous beast whose language he couldn't understand.

That impression of Priscilla had, somehow, changed quite a lot over these days in the empire.

"At this point I think even Priscilla counts as a proper Royal Selection candidate, same as Emilia-tan and Anastasia-san, and Crusch-san and Felt too."

"Why should I require your recognition. Mind your tongue, mediocrity."

"There she goes talking mean again… but actually, we were kind of talking about that too. Right?"

Clasping her hands before her chest, Emilia called out, and Anastasia smiled a soft, amused smile in response.

"See, remember that thing at Priestella? At the time, there wasn't really a reason to call for her, so we didn't invite the princess…"

"So next time, let's invite Priscilla too. After all, she's a fellow Royal Selection candidate."

"Mediocrity, it seems your master doesn't know what sort of event the Royal Selection is."

"Isn't she cute. That's my angel."

That gathering of candidates at Priestella hadn't been meant as a vacation either, but from Emilia's perspective, it seemed she'd felt guilty about leaving Priscilla out.

Still, better a vacation than another incident with the Witch Cult running wild.

"If we're doing it, let's make the next one somewhere we can do a swimsuit vacation."

"Natsuki-kun sure is honest about his desires. Well, I wouldn't say no to considering it. Course, if the princess says not to bother inviting her, we won't."

"Priscilla… what do you say?"

A provocative Anastasia, and a timid, small-animal-like Emilia. At the invitation from these two beautiful girls, both Royal Selection candidates, Priscilla merely shrugged.

"Do as you please. If it amuses me, so be it; if not, so be it. There is no particular reason for me to refuse."

"—! Yes, let's do that. We're all in difficult positions, and we've all got plenty of things to worry about… but I don't think that's a reason we can't get along."

"Once Emilia-san gets ahold of something, she softens it right up, no matter what. Real troublesome, that."

Priscilla's answer, given in a way meant not to sound eager, came out through Emilia sounding like enthusiastic agreement — and Subaru nodded along with Anastasia's take on it.

Priscilla, too, seemed to have no particular wish to correct that impression, and looked up at the night sky—

"—A slum girl and a duke, and then a vixen from a city-state, and a half-demon with silver hair and violet eyes."

"—? Priscilla?"

"It's nothing. Only, listed out like that, I merely thought it sounded like the cast of a rather poorly made tale."

"Poorly made is uncalled for. Besides, I don't think you're really in a position to talk."

Even Priscilla, seemingly lost in thought, held a position as the supposedly-dead princess of the empire. As titles go to liven up a story, that one was flashy enough and to spare.

The moment he'd said it, Subaru's heart clenched, thinking that might have been enough to anger Priscilla after all. But that fear proved unfounded — no, more than unfounded.

"—Indeed."

So saying, Priscilla laughed, the tip of her fan pressed to her lips.

It was, of all things, exactly like a girl laughing amid idle chat with friends — a natural, unguarded laugh.

Somehow having lost the chance to part ways, Subaru kept walking the fortress city alongside Priscilla.

It's a bit much coming from him, but Subaru had worked too hard today. And the same could be said of Priscilla, and of the great many people still moving energetically through the city.

Of course, most of them, like Subaru, were probably simply too worked up to sleep.

"Don't tell me everyone's actually tireless Undead or something…"

"—What foolishness are you spouting now, you."

"Uhyowai!?"

Idly gazing down from the stone footbridge at the streets below, commenting on the people bustling this way and that — he'd gotten thoroughly overheard.

And by someone he didn't want overhearing him, no less — glaring at Subaru with narrowed eyes was Rem.

A bucket of water in hand, Rem too, it seemed, was one of the sleepless.

"Are you still out helping tend the wounded or whatever? I get being fired up, but overworking yourself isn't good either. Take a page from your sister — rest the moment you're tired."

"I'll return that advice right back to you. No, in fact, if you have no work, isn't your job to go back to your room and rest quietly?"

"Sharp and on point! …It's not that I don't have work exactly, it's more like there's nothing I need to do."

Poking his fingers together in front of his chest, Subaru mumbled his answer to Rem. At that attitude, Rem's pale blue eyes sharpened, and Subaru's courage shrank all the more.

Then, as if throwing Subaru a lifeline — no, simply looking as if she found the sight unsightly — Priscilla sighed and said,

"It is nothing much. As you can see, his limbs have stretched out somewhat compared to his child form. He merely finds it hard to face those he had dealings with while a child."

"Getting bigger but staying short-legged is uncalled for! Though it's all true, either way!"

Her heart matter-of-factly laid bare by Priscilla, Subaru bit the sleeve of his shirt in a "gah—!"

The great task awaiting Subaru — as Priscilla said, it was telling everyone in the Pleiades Battalion that he'd returned to his original form.

Ever since the Isle of Swordfighting Slaves, they'd been companions who had worked together the whole time, but he'd never once confessed to any of them that he'd been shrunk. And for that matter, he hadn't revealed the truth about the whole "Emperor Abel's secret love child" cover story either — meaning he'd been lying nonstop this whole time.

"If I confess and everyone despises me, I won't be able to recover… I'd almost rather it be recorded that Natsuki Schwartz died an honorable death in battle…!"

"Tell a lie like that, and I'll despise you myself. Would that be all right — being despised by me?"

"I don't want that either. If Rem hates me I can't go on living…!"

Caught between Rem before him and the Battalion behind him, Subaru whined, his heart getting pummeled from both sides. Rem, sighing at the sight, suddenly noticed Priscilla's gaze on her.

Arms crossed in a way that emphasized her chest, Priscilla called out "Rem" to her,

"Your expression has changed since we parted a while ago. No doubt the healing magic that once vexed you so has proven useful."

"Yes, I've had many opportunities to put Priscilla-san's instructions into practice. …And, thank you. After the star was shot down, that gentle fire that lent all of us its strength… that was something you gave us, wasn't it, Priscilla-san?"

"Hm. What makes you think so?"

"—. Intuition, I suppose. Perhaps because, however brief the time, I spent it with you, Priscilla-san."

Drawing the water bucket close to her chest, Rem gave Priscilla that answer.

At Rem's smile, so contrary to her words yet brimming with conviction, Priscilla too gave a small "Heh" of a laugh.

"Good. It seems a core of diamond has taken shape within you. I shall commend you for it."

"Um, um, Rem, actually, the thing with the star when the city was in danger, it was me and Beako who shot it down, so what about that? What about that?"

"What?"

"Sorry for butting in, my bad, please praise Beako later."

Wanting to be praised too, not to be outdone by Priscilla, Subaru got glared at by Rem with eyes just short of contempt. Retreating meekly, still, there was a point to what Priscilla said.

Rem now carried herself with a poise befitting the expression "a core of diamond." No doubt her reunions with Ram and Petra and the others, and the presence of her friend Katya, had a great deal to do with it.

"After — while I'm not around, you gotta tell me in detail what you and Priscilla got up to."

"…After everything is settled, all of it, then."

"Ah, in that case, let me help tend the wounded too. You can put me to work for that."

It wasn't that he wanted to put off talking to everyone in the Battalion, but leaving Rem to work herself busy while exhausted, without lending a hand, was unthinkable.

At Subaru's offer, Rem started to hold out the water bucket to him with a "In that case—"

But—

"—No. Never mind, it's fine after all."

"Eh!? D-did I do something to upset you!? Was I too considerate and it got creepy!?"

"It's not quite that. Not quite that, but… if you come with me, Priscilla-san will be left alone."

"No no no, then just have Priscilla help tend the wounded too."

"Are you in your right mind?"

"I feel like that was too harsh but I wasn't exactly in my right mind either when I said it!"

Given a look as though something unbelievable had been said, Subaru reflected on himself too. Still, it seemed strange to have his help declined by Rem on Priscilla's account.

To begin with, the only reason Subaru was with Priscilla right now was mere happenstance, with no deep reason behind it.

If Beatrice or Spica had wanted his company, the night wouldn't have turned out like this.

"But Beako and Spica both got caught up by people wanting to talk spirits and Authorities, so…"

"Then all the more, at Priscilla-san's side—"

"Rem, needless consideration is unnecessary. Besides, what of it, if I go about with a mediocrity in tow."

"It's a bit weird to say 'see, told you so' but you're the one who started dragging me around in the first place!"

Dragged out to play the clown, and now reduced to shouting like this — yes, this was truly the role of a clown.

But setting aside Subaru's protest, Rem stared straight at Priscilla, unwavering. Then, for a while, Rem seemed to search for words that even she couldn't quite put into shape, before saying,

"I don't want you to be alone, Priscilla-san."

"—. That's the answer that comes out after all that searching? You're a girl I don't understand either."

"But right now, this chest of mine isn't aching, and I'm grateful for that."

That answer of Rem's was one whose meaning was lost on Subaru.

Probably, it was a fragment of some exchange between Rem and Priscilla, shared only between the two of them, something Subaru knew nothing of. Receiving it, Priscilla paused a beat, then gave a small snort through her nose.

"Do your best to be strong, Rem. Daughter of an oni. —There will surely be a role only you can fulfill, a wish only you can grant, a future only you can achieve."

"—. Priscilla-san?"

"Come, mediocrity. For Rem's sake, I shall specially take you along."

"Not entirely convinced, but, where to?"

Magnificently ignoring Subaru's own concerns, Priscilla gestured with her fan toward the far side of the footbridge. Not down at the streets Subaru had been watching, nor up at the star-strewn night sky, but somewhere in between — the city walls.

The city's walls had all taken damage in the siege, but fortunately, that particular wall retained its original shape. Even so, it didn't look like a place anyone or anything in particular was waiting.

"Rem, follow your own heart. Your heart is roiling, but those ripples are not the least bit unsightly."

"—Thank you very much."

After indicating their destination, Rem and Priscilla exchanged yet another conversation Subaru couldn't follow.

Only, Rem's calm expression gave Subaru no reason to pry any further.

It seemed the area had been cleared of people, for there were no sentries atop the city wall.

Having only just finished the fight against the Great Disaster, with not every Undead yet dealt with, it seemed a decision more than a little lacking in tension—

"Well then, who exactly's got the courage to defy the princess's orders, is what I say."

So said Al, greeting Subaru and Priscilla, sitting cross-legged atop the empty wall. Beside him sat a bottle of what looked like expensive liquor, along with two glasses.

"Getting drunk celebrating the victory?"

"Don't talk nonsense, I wouldn't start without the princess. Anyway, didn't expect the two of you to be together, bro. Super rare pairing, isn't it?"

"Everyone I run into says that, and even I've had an itchy backside about it this whole time."

Though self-declared not drunk, Al's voice carried a distinct spring to it. Perhaps that wasn't the power of alcohol, but the light-headedness brought on by the afterglow of victory.

In fact, having walked all over the city with Priscilla, Subaru keenly felt the same thing himself.

"Everyone — it's not just that they've got things to do, is it. They don't want this to become a dream."

"Quite the poet, aren't you, bro. But I get it, I do. Morning, don't come yet, that kind of thing."

Rather than the negative fear of falling asleep, waking, and finding the victory had been a dream, it was more the feeling of not wanting to let the day end — a day everyone had united to seize the victory on.

As Subaru and Al shared that pre-dawn sentimentality, the backs of their heads were suddenly rapped with a fan. Giving matching "Gyaah!" screams, the two turned to find themselves met with Priscilla's exasperated look.

"A clown and a mediocrity together, spouting nothing but foolishness. More to the point — Al, did you retrieve it as I instructed?"

"Oh? Oh yeah, no worries. Asked the old chancellor to clear the wall guards, and got some booze off a fancy senior countess besides. Bet the price on this bottle'd make your eyes pop."

"I get it. The label and how old the bottle looks kind of exude the price. Not that I drink."

"I get it. The box it was stashed in was doing good work. Not that I drink much either."

Both ignorant of liquor, Subaru and Al pointed at each other in mutual, endearing agreement. At their manner, Priscilla shrugged, and instead took up the bottle with practiced ease, popping the cork.

Then, liquor laced with sweet fumes was slowly poured into the two glasses.

"There are but two cups. The two of you may share one between you."

"Ah, if it's gonna be that, I'd rather have an indirect kiss with the princess, sorry, my bad, I'll shut up."

Al retreated sheepishly, but Subaru couldn't blame him for wanting to throw in a bit of teasing — he understood the impulse. In any case, as Priscilla put her lips to her own glass, Subaru, after a moment's hesitation, decided to put his lips to the glass he'd been handed.

"Oh, underage drinking."

"It's legal in this world… kohh, gahh!"

The moment the liquor crossed his tongue and its aroma cut up through his nose and throat, Subaru choked. Laughing it off with a "wahaha," Al took the glass from Subaru and drank as well.

Lifting his helmet's chin with the hand holding the glass, pouring the liquor through the gap—

"Kohh, gahh, gobeh!"

"You're choking worse than me! Get it together, of-age drinker!"

"So loud, the both of you. Not even able to appreciate a fine vintage — the 'Witch' who lost would be lamenting it, I'd wager."

"Weird how that gives me guilt, stop it…"

It wasn't as though Sphinx would actually say something like "you must apologize" for not appreciating the victor's fine wine, but Subaru still felt a pang of dejection at the thought.

In the end, while Subaru and Al shared the liquor in their glass little by little, Priscilla, alone, drained nearly half the bottle herself. Subaru had no idea of the exact proof, but the liquor had struck him as fairly strong, and yet Priscilla's face remained utterly unruffled after that much.

"The princess being a heavy drinker — that's about what you'd expect, isn't it."

Al said this with a note of pride, and Subaru couldn't argue with that assessment.

As Subaru and Al's shared glass finally emptied, Priscilla, as if she'd been waiting for the moment, said,

"Dawn draws near. I find myself somewhat moved. —Al, join me."

"Kehh… eh? Join you, what for…? Whoa!"

So saying, Priscilla set the empty glass and the bottle, its contents diminished, upon the railing, then took Al's hand and pulled him to his feet, guiding him to the center of the wall.

Drawing the bewildered Al close, she smiled at Subaru, who was watching wide-eyed, wondering what was happening.

"Natsuki Subaru. Sing."

"That's asking way too much!"

"Your tongue ought to be well-moistened by such fine liquor. There, if you won't be of use, I'll tell Rem on you."

"Guhh, that's dirty…! Fine, fine!"

Rather forcibly swept along by the current, Subaru crossed his arms and began building a setlist in his head. Meanwhile, Al looked Priscilla up and down.

"Princess, I'm short one arm here, y'know?"

"What of it. You have two legs, and loyalty toward me. —It begins."

That wasn't meant as a cue, but Priscilla's words and the start of the singing coincided all the same.

"————"

What Subaru chose wasn't a hit song from his original world, but rather a song from this other world, one that had taken root here — and one that Subaru himself loved.

"Sword Demon Love Song" was a bit too long, and he'd probably cry partway through singing it.

What Subaru chose was—

"—The Sky Overtaking the Morning Glow."

Slowly, the Vollachia Empire's night came to an end, pushing aside the star-strewn yesterday, as a new today came bustling in.

This song, which celebrated that, was Subaru's favorite among all the songs of this world he'd heard.

"Heh."

There was no instrumental accompaniment; the only thing carrying the tune was Subaru's own a cappella voice.

Compared to Liliana, the traveling bard whose trade was song, it made for a poor showing, but the dance Priscilla began, receiving it, felt like the highest praise Subaru's singing could ever hope for.

"There, dance, dance, Al! Do not bore me!"

"Argh, damn it! Screw it, I'm doing this! Bro! Bring the beat up!"

—Listening to Subaru's singing voice, Priscilla and Al whirled round and round atop the city wall.

Priscilla's dance was gorgeous — she'd probably be furious if he said this out loud, but it had something in common with Abel's dance when he'd seduced Zikr in Guaral. Al's ungainly dancing looked something like a bon-odori, clumsy in technique but fun to watch.

More than anything, both Priscilla and Al, master and attendant alike, were plainly enjoying themselves.

"————"

Before he knew it, Subaru — who was supposed to have been dragged into this against his will — found himself laughing as he sang.

The Sky Overtaking the Morning Glow — a song singing of the dawn that was bound to come, a blessing for a world reborn, seared by that dazzling light. In a sense, fitting for a Vollachia ruled by flame.

And Priscilla, who lived like fire itself, was the most Vollachian woman there could be.

"————"

Not knowing how to end it, he repeated it a second time, a third. As he did, the two dancers' rhythm began to sync, and once more he lost the opportunity to bring it to a close. Over and over.

While they went on like that, the true light of morning began to gently illuminate the city wall.

He'd had some liquor too, and today — no, yesterday — he'd worked himself grandly to exhaustion. No doubt, once he fell asleep today, he wouldn't wake again for a good while.

Glittering, in the dazzling morning light, Priscilla dancing with Al seemed to shimmer strangely bright—

"—Princess?"

Sudden — it was a sudden call from Al.

"————"

Up until then, it had been a call laced with unconcealed high spirits and an affection he couldn't quite hide either. But this one carried a different note entirely, and Subaru's singing voice stopped dead.

Then Subaru blinked, and rubbed his eyes, again and again.

Even having rubbed his eyes—

"—That songstress, not quite up to Liliana Masquerade's level, but it was not a bad song."

So, tucked into Al's chest, held from behind, Priscilla spoke that praise.

—Priscilla's body, showing through in the morning sun, was faintly beginning to vanish.

"————"

Wrapped in the morning mist of the fortress city, Subaru stared at Priscilla atop the wall, aghast.

The blood-red dress, the orange hair catching the sunlight, the crimson eyes like flame — all of it, everything that made up the existence called Priscilla Barielle, unchanged, and yet.

"Pri, Priscilla…?"

"You too must know already. My enemy, Sphinx, to flaunt before me the empire's ruin, sealed me away in a different phase. To escape it, there was no other way than to burn that place to nothing."

"—Ah."

Priscilla was clever. So, from Subaru's trembling voice alone, she understood what Subaru was thinking, what Subaru wanted to ask, and offered up the answer to his question herself.

With that orderly answer, Subaru understood too. Understood, and couldn't unknow it.

"————"

The same as Eugard Vollachia, the ancient emperor who'd been with Yorna.

Priscilla Barielle had returned from another dimension in exchange for her own life.

The reason her figure showed through in the morning sun, blurring, fading fleetingly away, was plain as day.

—Because the Priscilla Barielle standing here was already one of the Undead.

"No way… there's no way that can be true!!"

Standing there dumbstruck, unable to say anything, it was Al who screamed instead.

Still holding Priscilla from behind, his voice shook hard enough that even through the helmet, it was plain his expression inside was twisted, and with just his one right arm, he held her tighter, and tighter still.

As if to keep from letting go of Priscilla, who seemed to be slowly killed by the rising morning sun.

"No, no, this isn't right, princess, this can't— b-bro!"

"—! Right. Right, this is wrong, this is a mistake. Wait, Priscilla, I'll—"

At Al's cracked, desperate call, Subaru snapped his head up and turned his eyes toward the railing.

Having ended the Great Disaster, the poisoned packet tucked in his back tooth had already been spat out. So if he was to choose a way to try again right now, jumping from the city wall was the fastest—

"Stop."

"Stop what, why!? There's no reason to stop! I can't just—!"

A voice held back Subaru, who'd leapt at the railing, about to throw himself off. But paying it no mind, Subaru moved to challenge the Great Disaster once more.

Except—

"Your Authority and Al's have the power to overturn even the logic of fate itself. But remember this: even with all your power and all your prayers, there are things that some would wish not to be changed."

"What, are you— what are you saying? This isn't the time for that! Right now, I'll—"

"Natsuki Subaru."

Teeth clenched, Subaru's hands on the railing split at the nails, blood seeping out.

He should ignore everything Priscilla said, all of it, and jump. That's what Subaru's heart, which had spent life after life fighting the Great Disaster to claw out the empire's victory, screamed.

But something told him, just as strongly, that he should listen to what Priscilla had to say instead.

At Subaru, frozen in the clash between that contradictory reason and emotion, Al screamed, "Bro!" Still holding Priscilla, refusing absolutely to let go, he trembled like a child throwing a tantrum,

"Bro! Please… please! Don't ask anything! You don't need to ask! Just do it! Save the princess… save Priscilla!!"

"Aldebaran."

"—! Stop it, stop it, Priscilla! I won't listen! I refuse to listen!"

Screaming in anguish, pleading with Subaru, Al shook his head furiously when Priscilla called his name.

Priscilla used, for Al, a name that was familiar to Subaru's ears, but one he'd never before heard used toward him — and gently stroked his neck.

Held from behind, stroking the neck of the man behind her — the scene looked like nothing so much as a single painting: fragile in the sense that it couldn't be kept in reality, that was precisely how it felt.

"You two saved the empire. Of course, others' efforts contributed too. But no one expended anything to match what the two of you spent. That, I shall commend."

And so, smiling with a beauty maddening to behold, Priscilla continued.

Neither Subaru's protests nor Al's screams could stop Priscilla's gently spoken words.

Those crimson lips, as if to sear away every past impression of Priscilla, as if to burn it into something that had always been true, set the heart ablaze with a convenient flame of fondness.

"I know that you two have done this many times over already. You've always placed yourselves no higher than others, and lived that way until today. And so, perhaps not once have you ever received a fitting reward for it. That, I shall grant you."

So saying, Priscilla closed her eyes once, then reflected Subaru in those crimson eyes.

And then—

"—Well done, Natsuki Subaru. You are, truly, a knight."

The instant that single line was bestowed upon him, the strength drained from Subaru's knees.

"—Ah."

Legs buckling, Subaru couldn't stand back up where he sat. His lips trembled uncontrollably, his head a jumbled churn of every emotion at once, understanding unable to catch up.

And yet his legs gave out because he'd already understood. —His soul had understood.

That Natsuki Subaru could not save Priscilla Barielle.

"Aldebaran, to you as well…"

"I told you to stop it, didn't I! I'm not giving up! There's no way I could give up! Because — because that's how it is, isn't it!? If I, if I give up, then you… princess, you'll…!"

Averting his eyes from Subaru, sunk to his knees, Al still clung to Priscilla. Having given up relying on Subaru, he raised his voice with all his might, trying desperately to change fate.

But that voice gradually lost its strength and momentum, growing weak, punctuated over and over by the sound of sniffling. To Al, unable to finish his words, Priscilla smiled. Like a compassionate mother.

Ironically, that smile overlapped with Yorna's, proof of the two of them being mother and child in more than name. And so, as if soothing a wailing child of her own, Priscilla said,

"What, a great hulking man wailing and blubbering like this — quite the handful."

"—!"

"Ha ha, there, there, I can hear it. Your voice, crying and begging me to become your bride."

"…Ah."

At Priscilla's laughing words, Al responded in a thin voice, nodding. Nodding, he held Priscilla's body even tighter still, refusing to let go of the thing he could not bear to let go of, and confessed.

That was, without question—

"Ah, become it, princess. My princess…"

That was a confession of love no one, whatever they might say, could deny.

All the fondness a man had packed into every corner of his being, telling the woman in his arms to receive the whole of it — that love. Receiving it, Priscilla's crimson eyes wavered, and—

"—There, you see, I win again."

Having made him say it, the quality of Priscilla's smile shifted, changing into the one so familiar to them.

This was Priscilla Barielle, through and through — that triumphant expression, strong-willed and haughty, a beautiful woman who declared without hesitation that everything belonged to her, arrogance itself given form.

The one whose brilliance seared the eyes of all who beheld it, forcing every soul to acknowledge her existence — the "Sun Princess"—

"Remember this, all of you who chose to shoulder the 'Heroic Fantasy' upon yourselves, who hold the means to defy the fate laid out for you."

"You will, from here on, keep taking on the wounds of many, sharing in their pain, drinking down their tears. But most of those you meet will not be good people. They will not even be noble. —Nor will they be perfect."

"There will be days you regret your own deeds, unable to affirm them. There will be nights you lament your own decisions, brought to your knees. There will be mornings you cannot raise your face, having betrayed your own wishes."

"The parts you cannot love in those you love, the parts you can love in those you cannot — you will see them again and again, and stumble over the same things each time. —And each time, remember this."

"That I, Priscilla Barielle, a perfect woman, once praised you."

—From Priscilla, speaking so, neither Subaru nor Al could tear their eyes away.

Atop the wall with no one else there, she had given up the greatest thing she had to fight the Great Disaster, and smiled without a single regret for it, as her form grew fainter — and they could not look away.

Because they couldn't look away, they saw it. Priscilla's existence dissolving into light.

And then—

"How beautiful the world is. And so — the world is arranged, for my convenience, exactly as I please."

True to those words, without a lie in them, the figure of a woman loved by the world, and who had loved the world even more, vanished.

Sung as the "Sun Princess," who lived like flame — Priscilla Barielle. Among the candidates in the Kingdom of Lugunica's Royal Selection, deciding the next king, she was the first to fall.
