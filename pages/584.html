Arc 7 Chapter 79: "The Crimson Guffaw"

—Priscilla, appearing without warning, had stirred up a great whirlwind with the sheer power of her spoken words.

"―――"

In that instant, an unmistakable silence and blankness ruled over the great hall, and everyone felt the illusion that time itself had stopped. It was a statement impossible to mishear, and yet no one could quite grasp what it meant. It was the sort of impression where the sound reached the ears, but its meaning refused to follow. A stretch of time passed as everyone searched their minds for the word that the sound "Hahaue" was meant to represent. But then—

"—? Is Yorna Priscilla's mother?"

Emilia tilted her head, puzzled, and the moment a different sound—"Kaasama"—burst from her, "Hahaue" and "Kaasama" connected, and the words took on the substance of "Mother" and "Mama." Suppressing the sense of his own thoughts scrambling, Otto compared the two who had drawn everyone's attention—Priscilla and Yorna. Yorna stood frozen, eyes wide, while Priscilla, staring her down, remained utterly composed.

The two of them differed in hair color, in eye color, and to begin with, in race itself. Of course, in the case of mixed blood, which parent's traits come out stronger varies by individual, but there was not a single resembling feature between Priscilla's and Yorna's appearances. If forced to name one thing, both possessed a sultry, striking beauty—but that was it.

"If that little were enough to call people family, the whole world would be one big family."

Reaching that conclusion, Otto reined in his own confusion. That said, hearing of the relationship between Priscilla and Yorna did clear up a few things. Priscilla's fierce bearing and personality, now that he thought of it, did carry a strong flavor of the Vollachian way.

"I feel an unpleasant gaze sizing me up, but very well, I shall allow it."

Reading the meaning behind that stare, Otto caught his breath at the warning in Priscilla's eyes. But paying him no further mind, Priscilla turned her gaze toward Emilia.

"Half-devil, there is no mistaking what you say. To think she has become one of the 'Nine Divine Generals.' I thought as much back in her childhood days, but what a curious state of affairs indeed."

"Yeah, it surprised me too. But now that you mention it, maybe the two of you really do look alike."

Priscilla propped her chin with the fan in her hand, and Emilia nodded at her answer. It was a bit concerning that Emilia had reached the exact opposite conclusion from the lack of resemblance Otto had just privately denied, but that could wait for now. The problem was—

"—Wait, if you please."

Struck by an unexpected accusation in Priscilla's words, Yorna herself called for a pause. She closed her eyes once, wiping the agitation from her expression before it could show,

"I'll admit I was a touch surprised, but that's no way to speak, if you please. Why ever would I be milady's mother..."

"Cease your pointless evasions. If you mean to deceive me, you'll need to change the very nature of your soul before it's worth discussing. However much your form may change, these crimson eyes of mine cannot be fooled."

"—!"

The composure Yorna had put on stiffened once more at Priscilla's resolute reply. Priscilla's words were abstract, and on top of that, carried not the slightest willingness to meet others halfway. That made their precise meaning hard to grasp, yet they seemed to land on Yorna like a fatal blow. Yorna was clearly shaken by the sharpness of Priscilla's gaze and tongue. However, Yorna was not the only one in the room to betray strong agitation—

"—Impossible."

Astonishment at the scene unfolding spilled out, unmistakably. It was faint—faint enough that one might easily have missed it—but the one who uttered it made all the difference. Otto did not miss the single voice that slipped out from behind the demon mask. That single word from Abel, a man who seemed less calm than simply cold, a man Otto had thought had no connection to agitation at all. "Priscilla," he said, hiding the faint agitation from a moment ago behind his mask as he called out to her,

"Are you speaking in earnest? That Yorna Mishigure is Sandra Benedict?"

"—I see, so you didn't know either, Abel. Not that I blame you. There's no way to know such a thing save by her own confession."

"But Priscilla noticed without anyone telling her, right?"

"Do not interrupt. Hold your tongue, half-devil."

"You don't have to put it that way..."

Priscilla, who had answered Abel's question flatly, glared Emilia into silence for interrupting. Regardless, this was an exchange that left behind everyone from the Kingdom's group—Otto included—as well as those on the Empire's side who didn't know the full circumstances. The subject delved into Priscilla's personal history, and as someone whose own camp also backed a Royal Selection candidate, Otto found himself curious—but—

"—Save your damn unexpected reunion for later. Right now it's this brat we gotta deal with first."

Unmoved by the shift in the air, Garfiel bared his fangs and growled that out. Those emerald eyes of his skewered the one still being shielded by Yorna and Medium—Louis. As Garfiel said, until her treatment was decided, there was no moving forward or back. Of course, Otto's own conclusion—that Louis should be restrained and stripped of her freedom—remained unshaken.

"I ain't sayin' snuff her out. But tyin' her up and keepin' her down is a line I won't budge on... 'Keep Tinos's limbs at arm's length,' as they say."

"—That's probably not somethin' our brother would want, though."

"Ahh?"

Garfiel, who shared Otto's opinion, clacked his fangs at that remark. The one who'd cut in was Al, who had come up to the top floor together with Priscilla. Looking as bizarre as ever, he scratched the back of his neck with his one arm, seeming somewhat reluctant, as he said,

"First off, lemme apologize to you, missy. —Sorry for draggin' you all the way out to Chaosflame and comin' back without our brother."

"Al... no, thank you for apologizing. But I'm not the only one who's disappointed we couldn't see Subaru—Beatrice is the one who's taken it hardest."

"Ahh, then I owe her an apology too, and everyone else along with her."

Al bowed his head, voicing his apology over the matter of Subaru's absence. Setting aside Al's usual flippant manner, there seemed to be no falsehood in his remorse. That said, a sincere apology didn't necessarily lead to forgiveness or a good impression.

"If anything, an easy apology just gives more room to be taken advantage of. In fact, if you truly feel apologetic, I'd rather you not put in your two cents at all."

"Harsh. But like I said before, doin' anything to this little squirt ain't somethin' our brother—Natsuki Subaru—would want."

"So why the hell not!? If you're just spoutin' nonsense..."

"—Because I tried to kill that little runt myself, and our brother got in my way."

Garfiel's heated words were shut down by Al's dry remark. At the sharp intake of breath from those around him, Al shrugged and gestured at Louis with his chin,

"You lot are like that too. So it's only natural I thought about killin' her, right? And yet..."

"Natsuki-san refused to let you?"

"Honestly, I couldn't believe my ears. At first I figured he was just draggin' along some kid he didn't know, but then it turns out she's a Sin Archbishop. And he goes and puts his body on the line to protect her anyway. I thought I had a handle on how messed up our brother's thinkin' could get, but this went beyond anything I imagined."

As he spoke, Al's gaze pierced through his helmet toward Louis. Under that stare, Louis gave a small groan of "Uu..." but without hiding behind Medium and the others, met it head-on. The light that dwelled in those blue eyes was neither servile nor weak.

"—"

Watching the scene unfold, Otto weighed Al's testimony. That his words weren't idle bluster was something anyone in the camp who knew Subaru well could agree with. Otto himself understood the reason Subaru couldn't bring himself to strike Louis down as his "softness." That "softness" which both Subaru and Emilia carried was, at once, a weakness and a strength. Even if it felt unwieldy—something that, once let go, could never be recovered—Otto never once wished it gone.

"Judgments untouched by that kind of 'softness' are exactly what those of us around them should be making."

"I hear you, big brother. But it's already too late for that, ain't it? After all, we're talkin' about this with this many people watchin'. There's no takin' it back now."

"...Otto-sama."

With a scrape of her shoe against the floor, Frederica called out to Otto's profile. What made her beautiful eyes waver was concern and unease over stirring up trouble here. Otto, too, felt that bringing up Louis's true identity here had been a mistake. —No, if he was going to say that, the real problem was that Louis had already been accepted by those involved before this. The conclusion Taritta and Mizelda had voiced had already come to be accepted as the norm within the walled city. Hence—

"Then you mean to leave her free to roam?"

"Of course, if she'd done somethin', I wouldn't have shown mercy either. But..."

"Louis-chan's been tryin' to protect Subaru-chin this whole time. She never once did anything bad while she was with us. And she won't from now on, either!"

Taking up where Al left off, Medium raised her voice desperately. Her argument was wishful thinking; Louis's past conduct could not guarantee her future conduct. And that was precisely the crux of the whole problem.

"I... I've watched the master do it, so maybe I could do the oath's curse mark..."

"—Let's not. For a moment I thought that might work, but no."

Petra had timidly proposed it, but Otto rejected the idea. The "curse mark" Petra spoke of was a binding of the soul meant to force its target to keep a promise. It was the very thing Roswaal bore carved into his body—one that would cost him his life if broken. It was something Roswaal had carved into himself as a show of surrender to the camp, in the wake of the harm he'd schemed to cause at the Sanctuary and the old Roswaal Manor.

As the characters themselves indicated, a curse mark was, unmistakably, a "curse." It was a superstitious sort of notion, but there was a saying that curses eventually rebound upon the one who casts them. If one grew complacent with the convenience of binding others through curses, that bondage would, in time, come to sear one's own soul as well. Otto had no intention of saddling Petra with that kind of karmic burden, and even if a curse mark binding Louis's actions could actually be carved, it would amount to nothing more than an insurance policy.

"Because we don't even grasp the full picture of what exactly we'd need to bind to buy ourselves any real peace of mind."

No one knew the full extent of a Sin Archbishop's Authority. Since there was no telling what hidden trick might spring out, ultimately, no matter what was bound, Otto's guard against Louis would never fully come down—not unless her life was taken. If that was the case, then not having a curse mark that might breed carelessness would keep his vigilance more stable.

"It galls me to say it, but that's my conclusion."

"Otto-nii! Is that really okay!? She's a Sin Archbishop, damn it!"

Hearing Otto's reasoning, Garfiel raised his voice in what sounded almost like a scream. Otto understood the urge to lament being forcibly silenced when one was certain one was in the right. He wanted to sympathize with his little brother figure's anguish, too.

"But arguing about it here won't win any agreement. As things stand, there's still more we need to hear, and I'd rather avoid a falling-out with everyone here."

"So then, if force is to be used, it'll be after all the talking is done. How shrewd of you."

"I have no intention of doing anything so dangerous, but I'll take that as a compliment all the same, Priscilla-sama."

Faced with Priscilla, who had guessed at his true intentions with uncanny accuracy, Otto at least put up that much of a brave front. Regardless, however galling it was to admit to Garfiel, that was indeed the reality of the situation.

There was no way to break down the position held by Abel, who wielded influence in this city, or the unyielding stance the People of Shudrak had shown regarding Louis's treatment. Beyond the fact of her being a Sin Archbishop, there was no other grounds to justify eliminating Louis. Once that argument failed to hold, there was no option left to their side except force. But even killing Louis was no guarantee the effects of the Authority of Gluttony would vanish, and was it truly worth taking on the liability of turning the entire Empire into an enemy afterward?

"Even if we fought about it like that, it'd only ease our worries a little. That's what you mean, right?"

"—"

Garfiel ground his fangs, and Otto shut one eye. Tracing the flow of the conversation to reach that conclusion, Emilia's violet eyes wavered. In her arms, Beatrice, being held, called out her name: "Emilie."

"Sorry, Beatrice... Ojou-sama. I know you're worried about me."

"...If you truly understand, then it's fine, I suppose."

Exchanging glances and words at close quarters, Beatrice lowered her round eyes. Taking her feelings to heart, Emilia nodded and turned her steady gaze toward Louis and Medium. The girl huddled close like a sister met that gaze in return.

"That girl... Louis might be a reeeally dangerous girl. That's just like what Otto-kun and the others told us, and you understand that, don't you?"

"...Yeah, I understand."

"But you've never seen Louis be dangerous, or do anything bad. Did Louis... protect you too? And Subaru?"

"Yeah, she tried to protect us. Really, it's not a lie. Abel-chin and Al-chin know it too, and Taritta-chan, and Yorna-chan, right?"

Looked at straight-on by Emilia, Medium was choosing her words with all her might. As she chose them, she sought agreement from those who had returned together from the Demon City. Abel and Yorna, still not fully recovered from the exchange with Priscilla a moment ago, responded sluggishly, but Al shrugged, and Taritta gave a nod.

"Ye-es, Louis certainly did try to protect everyone. Especially Subaru — I believe she was quite attached to him."

"Not exactly the mood to joke about him living up to his reputation as the 'Little Girl Wrangler.'"

"...Betty doesn't much care for that nickname, I suppose. You'd best be careful."

Beatrice's low, displeased voice had softened its fighting spirit from just a moment before, if only slightly. She had likely decided to leave the matter of Louis's treatment up to Emilia's judgment. Even reaching the same conclusion, Emilia smoothed over what Otto and Beatrice had arrived at into something gentler. And so, Emilia turned to look at Louis, saying,

"You were worried about Subaru. I don't think that was a lie. So, just like this girl here who's believing in you with all her might, I want to believe in you too."

"—Ngh, Emilia-sama, that's..."

"But Garfiel, you snapped at us reeeally fiercely at first too, didn't you? And now we're friends, aren't we?"

Emilia's argument was a bit unfair—Garfiel and Louis stood in different positions, under different circumstances. But it was also true that, once Emilia declared it so plainly, it was hard to find a rebuttal. With circumstance lending its own weight, Garfiel's cheeks stiffened painfully. At that reaction from Garfiel, Emilia gave a small "sorry" and continued,

"It might be a hard thing to do. But instead of just charging in and smashing at it from the start, I think it's best to begin by really talking, if that's possible. Of course, there are times you just have to clash head-on right away, without any of that, but..."

"—"

"I want to get along with everyone here, if I can. And to do that, I want to properly tell them I want to be friends. So first, I have to open the hand I've clenched tight, don't I?"

As she spoke, Emilia looked down at Beatrice in her arms. At that gaze, Beatrice's eyes widened slightly, and then she gave a small nod. Seeing Beatrice's nod, Emilia slowly stepped forward.

Right in front of Yorna, past Garfiel who stood glaring at her, and up to little Medium, who was shielding little Louis behind her back. And then—

"This is going to be reeeally roundabout, but... I trust Zikr-san and Mizelda-san and everyone who was so good to Subaru and Rem, and I want to trust Medium-chan and the others, since all of them trust her too. So, please let me believe in you, the one Medium-chan believes in."

"...Ah, uu..."

"It's not about us—it's for the sake of these girls who've been so very good to you. Having someone out there who believes in you is a reeeally happy thing."

As she said this, Emilia gently held out her right hand to Louis. With Beatrice still held in her left arm, Emilia lowered herself toward Louis. At Emilia's action, Medium, who had been trying to protect Louis, turned around too,

"Louis-chan."

she called out to her. Whether it was that call that served as the trigger, or whether Emilia's words had worked some meaning even upon a Sin Archbishop devoid of a human heart, Louis timidly reached out her hand. Louis laid her own right hand atop Emilia's outstretched one.

For an instant, Otto worried about the possibility of the Authority being unleashed on Emilia from that contact, but he recognized that he ought to thoroughly despise himself for even harboring such a worry. In any case—

"Uwau."

"Mm, I'm worried about Subaru too."

Hands clasped together, Emilia's smile as she answered became the conclusion, both for Otto and for the Emilia Camp as a whole. A conclusion that was, strictly speaking, one of "suspension." —But even landing at the same place, the way of getting there would have been quite different had Otto said it instead of Emilia, and he found himself proud of her for it.

△▼△▼△▼△

"—I ain't takin' my eyes off you, got that."

Regarding Louis's treatment, a "suspension" was settled on for the time being. In response, Garfiel—who insisted to the very end that he couldn't accept it—declared as much firmly to the girl who had shaken hands with Emilia. Garfiel's concern and vigilance were only natural, so Otto didn't interject.

"Because I can't even imagine a member of the Witch Cult—let alone a Sin Archbishop—having a change of heart."

Otto's dry, realistic thinking arrived at that conclusion. Even if there had been a moving, historic exchange between Emilia and Louis just now. And yet, even as he thought it felt unlike him, Otto also thought this:

—The subjugation of the White Whale, the Great Rabbit, and the Sin Archbishops of "Sloth" and "Greed" had all once been unimaginable too.

It was Natsuki Subaru who had made the unimaginable happen again and again, over the course of little more than a year. The world at large saw these as achievements of the Emilia Camp, but everyone within the camp understood that Subaru's contribution had been the greatest of all. Hence, Otto couldn't help but let his thoughts wander to the possibilities. That perhaps, once again, Natsuki Subaru had gone and done something outrageous.

"Ugh, I hate this..."

When it actually came to light, how everyone around him would judge Subaru weighed heavily on his mind. He felt other people ought to hurry up and learn Subaru's true character already—but.

"Now that the discussion has settled down a bit, might I have a moment?"

So saying, Zikr raised his hand and began to speak into the great hall, where the tension of debate had eased. Once he'd drawn everyone's attention, he prefaced his words with "if I may be so bold," and continued,

"Lady Priscilla and General Yorna, the relationship between the two of you certainly piques my great interest as well, but there remain several matters I would like to confirm with Lord Abel."

"—Speak, then."

"Yes."

With the topic's point now turned toward him, Abel, who had kept his mouth shut, gave a nod. He hadn't interjected on the matter of Louis's treatment, but it seemed he had put a temporary end to his earlier shock. At the address, he looked toward Zikr, his black eyes urging him to continue.

"I've come to understand that General Yorna's cooperation was secured, and that the Demon City of Chaosflame collapsed... and that, because of this, Lady Natsumi came to face grave danger. Given that, what I wish to ask about is a rumor that has spread these past few days—"

"—A rumor."

"Yes. —A rumor that His Excellency the Emperor's own child, a black-haired crown prince, is said to exist."

Bowing his head respectfully, Zikr informed Abel of this. That rumor—hinting at the existence of a child of Emperor Vincent Vollachia—had reached Otto's ears as well. What's more, the story went that this son of the Emperor had raised a flag of rebellion against him, serving as the figurehead of the rebel army—that is, the group now gathered here in the walled city of Guaral. Not that Otto could recall having seen anyone in this city who fit that description.

"According to reports, this rumor flowed in from the eastern lands. It has already spread throughout the Empire, but as for its source..."

"As you suspect, it was spread from the Demon City. —Threatening the throne requires power. And power gathers only beneath a just cause."

Shaking his head slowly, arms crossed, Abel answered flatly. The answer made sense, but to begin with, an attempt to threaten the Emperor's seat wasn't a matter their camp particularly wanted to get involved in. For his part, Otto's focus was on how to head off the possibility of Emilia declaring that she couldn't abandon people she'd once come to know. Because of that, he could say he'd been slow to notice a possibility he would ordinarily have caught. Hence—

"...A black-haired crown prince."

It was Petra who murmured that quietly, as though lost in thought. A small crease formed between her brows on her lovely face as she mulled something over. Then, timidly, she put that thought into words. It was—

"Um, from what Medium-san and the others were saying earlier, Subaru's turned small, right? About the same size as me, or Beatrice-chan."

"So they said. It's hard to picture, but if it's Subaru-sama, the possibility of something like that happening to him isn't... ah."

"—!"

Nodding at Petra's words as she organized the information, Frederica's eyes went wide. Almost at the same moment as Frederica's realization, Otto too understood what Petra had been thinking. And along with it came the damnable realization of just how off the mark his own thinking had been.

"Could it be... that the rumored child of the Emperor is Subaru?"

In place of Otto, who was cursing that very possibility, Petra cut straight to the heart of it. The girl's eyes stared fixedly at Abel, and his black eyes, seen through the demon mask, met them steadily. Utterly unshaken, Abel gave a quiet nod of his chin in response to Petra's pressing question, and said,

"That's right."

"—! But that's...!"

Her eyes sharpening at the brief answer, Petra tried to raise her voice. But faster than Petra's reaction came—

"—Ku, hahahahaha!"

A voice, thoroughly delighted, rang out through the hall. Laughter oblivious to the situation shattered the atmosphere of the great hall. Yet no one could immediately voice objection to such an outrage, because that great laugh belonged to Priscilla. She held her fan to her mouth, hiding her own laughing teeth from view, as she said,

"Of all things, calling that mediocre fool a crown prince—how it makes me laugh. Ah, Abel, do you mean to kill me with laughter? You've changed your methods considerably, I must say."

"Princess?"

"What, you should laugh too, Al. Ah, no—you had a hand in this scheme, didn't you. In that case, well done for making me laugh this hard. As for playing the fool's part, I must admit it was masterfully done."

Turning to the startled Al, Priscilla, her eyes crinkling, voiced her praise. The sheer unexpectedness of Priscilla's reaction drained the heat from the great hall for the moment. Of course, that didn't make all the confusion disappear.

"Um, wait, what does this mean? There's no way Subaru's the Emperor's child, right? Because Subaru came from beyond the Great Waterfall..."

"Emilie, that was one of Subaru-sama's jokes."

"It was? Then... is it really true?"

"As for whether he's really the Emperor's child, that part's a lie. A falsehood."

"Eh? Eh? Eh?"

Utterly bewildered, Emilia looked like her head was spinning. Her confusion was understandable. In short, the situation of Subaru's Infantilization had, quite conveniently, meshed together with Abel and the others' aims.

"To be precise, I think it would be more accurate to say they made use of it to become that way."

Otto agreed with the way Frederica put it—this was an effective exploitation of circumstance. In raising their rebellion, Abel and the others had needed a convincing just cause. And as a figurehead for that purpose, nothing carried more persuasive weight than a child of the Emperor. Of course, using something that didn't actually exist as a figurehead could well prove a double-edged sword.

"But since Natsuki-san actually exists, I suppose even a double-edged sword can be wielded properly."

"That's an insolent way of putting it, but not unpleasing to me. Then you understand it too, don't you."

"—Much as it galls me to admit it."

By rights, the proper reaction for Otto and the others would have been outrage at Subaru's situation being exploited. And yet, there was no falsehood in his having called it galling; it was a fact that a certain value could be found in the flow of events Abel had engineered. And that was because—

"—It greatly reduces the chance that Subaru-sama, whose whereabouts remain unknown, will lose his life."

"...That's right."

At Frederica's understanding, Otto nodded with a bitter feeling. Emilia and Garfiel lagged behind Otto and Frederica's grasp of the matter. The two of them, confusion written on their faces, tilted their heads and asked, "What do you mean?"

"I really don't get what's going on at all..."

"I get that the boss got saddled with some weird title. But if he's bein' made the head of a rebellion, ain't that even more dangerous?"

"No, it'll draw attention, but the danger to his life, greater or lesser, actually thins out. Because this 'crown prince's' value only takes effect so long as he's alive."

The "crown prince"—a destabilizing element threatening the reign of Vincent Vollachia. This figure, the rebel army's just cause, held value for both allies and enemies of the current Emperor to exploit. It went without saying for those siding with the rebellion, but even for the Emperor's side, there was more use in keeping him alive. Whether executing him to sap the rebel army's momentum, or scheming to strip away its just cause, either required a living "crown prince" to make use of.

"In other words, no matter where Natsuki-san was sent, or who takes him into custody, there's a high chance he'll avoid being killed on the spot. In exchange, though—"

"In exchange?"

"...Any path of escape from the Vollachian succession struggle, from this great upheaval, has vanished."

To receive a benefit was also to be burdened with the responsibility that came attached to it. Whether he wanted it or not, Subaru had been placed right in the middle of the great upheaval of the Vollachian Empire—and set upon the very highest palanquin at that. And because of that—

"—Well then, how could one not laugh?"

And so, Otto finally grasped the reason behind the spiteful laughter of Priscilla, who had already arrived at the answer ahead of everyone else.
