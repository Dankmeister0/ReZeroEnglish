Arc 4 Chapter 23: "The Step Forward"

"Alright, I'm off. Won't be back till tomorrow... but like I keep saying, you don't have to push yourself and take the 'Trial.' You should rest up a little."

"I knoooow, I get it. Honestly, Subaru, don't make such a worried face. I'll do just like you said and behave myself and rest today."

Saying so, Emilia pouted and looked up at Subaru on the carriage with a sulky little gesture. Subaru wore a wry smile at her adorable manner, all while consciously masking the ache he felt inside so it wouldn't show on his face.

It was just before noon, the same day he'd finished his morning discussion with Garfiel and stopped by to see Emilia—now a few hours later.

"Natsuki-san, preparations on this end are done. All that's left is departure."

"Damn, that was fast work. Your knack for packing up a place and skipping town deserves some applause. Mind if I start calling you 'Midnight-Flit Otto' from now on?"

"I don't know what this 'midnight flit' expression means, but I'm fairly certain it carries an unflattering ring to it, so I'll have to decline."

Otto, who'd called out to him, twisted his lips at Subaru's answer, looking displeased, and this time Subaru gave a wry smile with no hidden agenda behind it. Looking past him, Subaru could see a line of dragon carriages other than the one he and the others were riding. Six in total—capacity for forty-two. That included the evacuees from Arlam Village and the traveling merchants who'd accompanied them. Which meant—

"Still, it's almost anticlimactic how easily they let us take everyone. ...Honestly, I was braced for a lot more pushback."

"That's because Barusu cleverly cleared things with Garf beforehand, is it not. Ryuzu-sama is a reasonable person, and there's no way negotiations would drag on if Garf doesn't stick his nose in."

Ram tossed the words up from beneath the carriage, dressed in her serving attire, as Subaru scratched the back of his neck. Beneath her pink hair, she looked up at him with her usual haughty expression.

"For Ram's part, she'd like to lodge a complaint about the fact that no negotiations were made to bring Roswaal-sama back to the manor."

"He was way too perfect a hostage-of-hostages. Even if I tried to work him into the negotiation, I couldn't come up with anything clever to say. Just be glad we got everyone from the village back—that's already a win."

"We'll count it as 'well done, for Barusu.' Be grateful to Ram."

"Isn't saying thank you here basically me losing?"

Trembling slightly at Ram, who was claiming every scrap of credit under heaven and earth for herself, Subaru turned once more to Emilia, who was fidgeting beside her. Her violet eyes had been harboring worry and unease, but the moment she noticed Subaru's gaze on her, she chased those feelings from her eyes.

"Thank you, really. If Subaru hadn't spoken up, I might never have noticed any of this myself."

"Emilia-tan's got a lot on her own plate, so just leave the surrounding stuff to us, okay? First things first—the emergency right in front of us. I'll make sure everything else gets handled well, so you can relax."

He thumped his chest forcefully, and Emilia pressed a hand to her mouth, letting out a small laugh. Then, scooping up the tears welling in her eyes with a finger, she nodded, "Mm," and said,

"Got it. I'm counting on Subaru. So..."

"I know, I know. Soon as everyone's delivered, I'll turn right back around. If there's a favorite stuffed animal you left at the manor, want me to bring it back too?"

"I graduated from that a long time ago. And it's not like I asked you to hurry back either. I mean, of course I'd be happier if you came back soon, but..."

"So what were you gonna say?"

"...Be careful. It's nice if it's fast, but if nothing bad happens, that's better."

"Ugh, my heart just skipped."

Feeling his heart give a little throb of love, Subaru pressed a hand to his pierced chest and staggered back. Emilia tilted her head, unaware of what she'd said, and Subaru held up his palms toward her, taking repeated deep breaths at her gesture.

"OK, OK, understood. I'll come back quick, and safe, to you. Pinky swear?"

"Like the one you did with Petra, about lying? ...Okay, got it. Let's do it."

Subaru bent his knees for Emilia as she stepped closer, and their fingers intertwined, from carriage to ground. He recited the customary little chant, and their pinkies locked, sealing the promise. Staring intently at that linked finger, Emilia then lifted her violet eyes to meet Subaru's.

"Subaru. For a Spiritual Arts User, a promise is..."

"Super important, right. I've learned my lesson, and felt it in my bones too. I won't break any promise with you again, Emilia-tan. And I'll do my best not to break promises with other people either, as much as I can. Please just settle for that."

"Mm, well, I suppose it can't be helped."

Emilia's smile spilled out. Having seen it through, Subaru straightened his knees, swung his hips around broadly, then raised both hands to the sky and looked up,

"Alright then, this time we're really departing! Destination: Arlam Village, total headcount: forty-four! Quite the big move, but everybody, let's do this!"

A rousing chorus answered Subaru's call, and the great move began. —The journey back to the village, bringing along the hostages freed at the end of the negotiations.

After finishing his talk with Garfiel, Subaru had watched over Emilia's sleeping face, crushed as she was, and made his decision—to accept Garfiel's proposal, and on top of that, to negotiate the release of the hostages from the "Sanctuary."

After putting Emilia to bed, Subaru waited for everyone to wake, then gathered the key members at Ryuzu's house and laid out the proposal. Given the verbal sparring match with Garfiel, he'd expected considerable pushback—but in reality, Ryuzu did nothing but nod along to Subaru's suggestions, and the negotiation even produced several concessions rather than objections. In the end things went exactly as he'd hoped, but he couldn't shake the feeling of having the wind taken out of his sails.

Afterward, he explained the situation to the now-awake Emilia, then gave the same explanation to the evacuees gathered in the great cathedral. The villagers froze in shock at first, but once they understood they could safely return to the village, their joy was so genuine that even Subaru found himself sincerely glad. Originally, it was Subaru's own idea that had forced them into this place to begin with, and yet the villagers' gratitude poured entirely onto Subaru as he explained their release—a rather embarrassing scene—but past all that, this present journey had finally come to be.

"Still, this was unexpected, wasn't it."

The one who broached the subject was Otto, gripping the reins beside Subaru. Just as on the way there, Patrasche and Frufoo pulled the carriage while the two of them sat side by side on the driver's seat; Subaru responded to his words with an "Ah?"

"Weren't you just asleep?"

"I was just lost in thought, don't go reading into it like that. Anyway, I was half out of it so I didn't catch what you said—what was it?"

"If you're going to bluff, at least commit to it till the end, hm? ...Anyway, like I said, I'm saying this was unexpected. This whole situation."

Subaru, as ever, answered Otto rather carelessly. By now Otto seemed to have grown fully accustomed to Subaru's temperature, for he showed no great reaction, only turning his head as if to elaborate on what he'd said.

"I didn't expect them to release the village people this easily. From a merchant's standpoint, it's generally better to keep hold of any leverage you can, for the sake of what comes later."

"Is that really a merchant's standpoint talking? Not a corrupt magistrate's? Can something be done about the way you keep job-changing into shadier and shadier professions in my head?"

Thinking back on it, Subaru couldn't recall many instances of Otto acting properly merchant-like. What he'd self-reported as his merchandise had been a massive quantity of oil, and Subaru wondered if he wasn't secretly an arsonist or something of the sort. A bit late in the game, but he cast a suspicious glance at Otto's true nature.

"What's with that look, like you're looking at some suspicious character?"

"It's exactly the look you give a suspicious character. Are you really a merchant? Was there ever a single time you acted the part in front of me?"

"You had me forced into organizing document after document full of confidential information right in front of you, Natsuki-san, and managing ledgers and who knows what else! Do you have a weak memory or something!?"

"No, but if I think of all that as some elaborate spy operation via clever manipulation, then honestly 'agent' fits better than 'merchant,' for certainty's sake—"

"Trying to steer your unpredictable train of thought would be easier if I just became a god, honestly."

"Hey, you hear that, Patrasche? The guy next to me just said he's gonna become a god... that's a little scary."

"Ah, damn it! Why does it always turn into— ah! Even Patrasche-chan is starting to pity me! Would you please stop!"

When Subaru called out to her, Patrasche gave a small cry, and hearing it, Otto clutched his head with a look of utter despair. It seemed that single cry had carried some opinion of Patrasche's own regarding Otto. The Divine Protection of the "Spirit of Speech" really was a troublesome thing. And then—

"You bastards don't change no matter where you go, huh, oi."

So muttered Garfiel, running alongside the moving carriage, sounding exasperated. The line of carriages was still moving along a game trail through the forest, so their speed wasn't especially high. Still, even so, an Earth Dragon was far faster than Subaru at a dead sprint. Garfiel, keeping up effortlessly with that speed, had been accompanying the evacuee group ever since leaving the "Sanctuary." Without so much as losing his breath, he kicked lightly off the ground, advancing as if flying, and said,

"If ya'd just act a little more like the 'Tale of Baqim the Horse-Chaser,' ya'd at least be kinda cute, y'know."

"Normally, idioms exist to convey your point to the other person clearly, in a short phrase... but I don't feel like I've managed to have a single conversation with you yet."

"Ha? What're you on about, you bastard."

Garfiel wrinkled his nose, looking displeased. At that carnivorous-beast expression, Subaru shrugged, then called down from the carriage to Garfiel as he kicked along the ground, "Oh, right—"

"You volunteered to guide us, but you're not showing any sign of actually leading the way—did you abandon your post?"

"Ain't my intention. It's just, your Earth Dragon's too damn smart. Only ran this road once, and she's got it damn near perfectly memorized."

"Well, she's the pride and joy of our stable, so that's nothing to her. If you want, I could also have her jump through rings of fire, or balance on a ball, or ride a unicycle... whoa, please don't swerve like that, Patrasche-san!?"

Subaru flailed in a panic atop the carriage as Patrasche expressed her refusal to perform any outlandish acrobatics through her driving. At the sight, Garfiel let out a rough snort, "The hell are you even doing," and glanced over at Otto holding the reins.

"So, that brother over there seemed to be sayin' something interesting. Better off keepin' hold of leverage, was it?"

"Yes, that's genuinely what I think. I don't think it's a great idea, but... the more hostages you have, the more options open up, I'd think. Especially if you wanted to use them to stoke a sense of crisis, the more you have, the more moves you can make..."

"Oi oi oi, is this brother really a merchant? He's definitely some shady type run out of his own country for doing dirty work somewhere!"

"Why does my reputation always end up going that direction!"

Even Subaru had to tilt his head at that—surely this time it was Otto's own fault for how that conversation went. Then, to steer things back, Subaru prefaced with "Anyway,"

"I did clear things with Garfiel beforehand, for what it's worth. Framed it as accepting his condition... well, that's how this whole thing avoided getting tangled up."

"Condition... meaning?"

In place of Subaru, who looked reluctant to answer, it was Garfiel who lifted the corner of his mouth at Otto's question. Opening his mouth to reveal his sharp canines, he said,

"Simple story, ain't it? Instead of that crybaby princess, our man Subaru here's gonna take on all the rest of the 'Trials,' and get the 'Sanctuary' freed up quick."

"That's..."

Otto's eyes widened at Garfiel's words, and he glanced sidelong at Subaru. Still hedging, he opened and closed his mouth several times before finally,

"Is that alright with you, Natsuki-san?"

"Alright how, exactly?"

"Well, there's the burden on you of taking the 'Trial,' for one thing... but mainly, is it alright to take that role away from Emilia-sama?"

At the very thing Subaru himself had agonized over, Otto cut in bluntly despite his reluctance. Subaru knit his brows at the words, and Otto pressed on further,

"Of course, I don't fully understand what the 'Trial' entails, and I think I understand well enough that Emilia-sama's current state isn't good. But when you consider Emilia-sama winning through the Royal Selection, she'll need the support of the 'Sanctuary' and the territory's people. To needlessly throw away a chance to earn that..."

"The time it'd take is... uncertain. If her support base wavers in the meantime, it defeats the purpose. And more than anything, Emilia herself..."

"Is at her limit? I don't think so. I've watched her this whole time, and Emilia-sama strikes me as someone with a solid core. Sure, right after coming out of the tomb having finished a 'Trial' she gets shaken, but outside of that, doesn't she keep her composure?"

At Otto's reasonable argument, Subaru bit his lip. Yes—from Otto's point of view, Emilia's condition probably didn't look nearly as cornered, as close to her limit, as Subaru perceived it. —Not realizing that it was a mask, one she could only maintain when someone other than Subaru was present.

It had been the same during this scene of sending off the evacuees—when a third party besides Subaru was involved, Emilia could behave with her usual composure, just as before. But in exchange, the moment she was alone with Subaru, she would suddenly lose her grip on that composure she'd been maintaining, and sink into the same unstable state as this morning. No one could easily dismiss that as her simply relying on—being spoiled by—Subaru.

Otto, seeing Subaru fall silent, still seemed to want to press further. But it wasn't Subaru who cut off his momentum, but—

"That's enough talk. We're gettin' close to the 'Sanctuary' border. Past that point, I can't follow."

"...Just for reference, out of curiosity—what happens to you if you try to force your way through anyway?"

"Never tried it, so I dunno, but thinkin' about what happened to the princess, my mind'd probably get taken, I'd guess. Well, don't matter none anyway."

Subaru latched gladly onto the change of subject, and Garfiel, tilting his neck, leapt. With that agility, he landed soundlessly on the cargo bed of Subaru and Otto's carriage, then jabbed a finger at the two of them as they turned around.

"As for the condition—the folks in back go back to the village, and you two head right back yourselves... ah, though that brother over there, I don't really need him."

"Don't say something so ridiculous, I'm coming back too! After all, I still haven't properly managed to get an audience with Margrave Mathers!"

"Huh? Really? Haven't I introduced you yet?"

"That's right! I've been here almost a week, and no matter when I go, either the timing's bad, or he's resting... Ram-san has turned me away over and over!"

Otto's shoulders heaved with an indignant huff. Given his remarkable knack for bad timing, Subaru half-suspected it was some kind of divinely ordained bad luck—but,

"Well, it's probably deliberate. Ever since the day of the 'Trial,' they've been keeping me from seeing him too, with one excuse or another."

Subaru murmured that guess under his breath. Ever since the day he'd first taken on the 'Trial,' Subaru had barely properly seen Roswaal face to face at all. Ostensibly, the story was that, same as with Otto being kept away by Ram, Roswaal was devoting himself entirely to recovering from his wound—but it was obvious that wasn't the whole story. After all, the moment Subaru had first spoken of taking on the 'Trial,' he'd practically been thrown out.

The way Roswaal's expression had changed upon hearing that Subaru had taken the 'Trial,' and that Emilia had failed to clear it, had left a deep impression. It was, after all, an expression Subaru had never seen from him before. That perpetually composed clown's mask of his had peeled away, and behind it, for one instant, a flash of intense emotion had shown through—something that was both anger and sorrow and yet neither, something complicated. Since then, Subaru hadn't properly faced him even once.

"Sorry to interrupt your thinkin', but anyway, if you're comin' back, use the same road. I'll be careful not to attack you this time, but in the worst case, don't forget the password."

"Password?"

"When you cross this border, if you say the password properly, you won't get treated as an attacker, y'know? The password's 'Byra-byra lies beneath the grimoire.'"

"Huh? What? Bye-bye Grimoire?"

The worst possible pattern—a meaningless idiomatic phrase as the password. Subaru grimaced, instantly guessing who'd come up with this password. Garfiel, arms crossed, wore an expression of complete obviousness, while Otto hurriedly jotted the password down. Leaving the memorizing to him, Subaru let out a breath toward Garfiel.

"Anyway, we're good as long as we say that, and you'll let us through, right?"

"It ain't just me on watch duty, y'know. There's folks in there with a lot more hot blood in 'em. To types like that, you lot make for perfect pawns to use as an excuse to interfere with the 'Trial.'"

"...Come to think of it, you mentioned that before too."

The faction, led by Ryuzu, that wished to be freed from the "Sanctuary." And, opposing them, another faction whose goal was to remain within the "Sanctuary." What Garfiel was warning him about, probably, was not to carelessly give that latter faction any opening to exploit, in dealing with them.

"Got it, understood. Anyway, thanks for all sorts of help... though I guess we'll see each other again in about half a day, but thanks."

"It's the old hag's request. Don't sweat it. More than that, uh..."

At Subaru's thanks, Garfiel waved a hand, then let his words trail off. Subaru knit his brows at his uncharacteristic manner. Then Garfiel continued with an "ah—"

"When you get back to the manor, that Frederica woman's gonna be there, right?"

"Yeah, she should be. Come to think of it, you said something like you two know each other. What's the deal?"

"It's like some cursed bond, or somethin'. Ain't got nothin' in particular I wanna say..."

"I'll let her know you were thinking about her. She'll probably say something back, I bet."

"...Never asked you to."

Averting his gaze with a huff, Garfiel then leapt again as if to shake off Subaru's smirking look. Jumping down from the carriage to the ground, he broke away from the evacuee group in front of the border between the "Sanctuary" and the forest, and, hands on hips, saw them off, calling out,

"Don't you run off on me, Subaru! Keep your promise even if it kills ya! That's the one and only condition on which I'm lettin' you lot outta here!"

"Yeah, don't worry. My promise-keeping power's been pretty damn strong lately."

Subaru answered the parting words that rang through the forest with a grin, raising a fist toward Garfiel as he grew distant. Garfiel raised his fist in turn, matching him.

Garfiel kept his fist raised, watching them off, all the way until he vanished into the forest's shadow.

—After passing the border and leaving the forest, the journey proceeded without any major issues.

The road from the "Sanctuary" to the manor took roughly eight hours, barring interference. They stopped to rest twice along the way, but the villagers' desire to get home won out, and they cut those rests short each time, pushing on at a pointlessly forced pace. Thanks to that, the evacuee group made it back to Arlam Village in exactly eight hours. Having departed past noon, it was now several hours after the curtain of night had fully fallen over the world.

"My butt hurts from sitting the whole time... but, thank god."

Climbing down from the carriage and rolling his hips, Subaru let out a sigh of relief. All around, cries of joy at reunions echoed through the nighttime village, and some among them were even weeping with happiness. Considering the disaster that had struck this village, Subaru understood that such a reaction was hardly excessive. So there wasn't a single person laughing at it. Arlam Village, after days without its people, had its vitality restored now that the villagers had returned, even at night. The villagers who'd worn such gloomy faces in the "Sanctuary" now wore smiles, all of them. The remaining villagers who'd stayed behind to greet them also seemed to breathe a sigh of relief now that their family members had safely returned.

"Natsuki-san, are you heading right back this instant?"

Otto came jogging over, having been looking around at his surroundings, to where Subaru stood apart from the surrounding commotion, watching over everyone. Otto, slightly out of breath, was met with Subaru shaking his head, "Nah,"

"That'd be too rushed even for me. Let's take a short break first. Besides, I need to stop by the manor and explain the situation to Frederica and Petra."

"Ah, that's true, isn't it. Well, I need to hash things out with my fellow merchants too."

"Hash things out with your fellow merchants?"

Where Otto pointed, standing there were the owners of each of the six carriages. They'd been hired under the pretext of evacuating the villagers, and had, along with the villagers, met the unfortunate fate of being placed under house arrest in the "Sanctuary." Each of them first breathed a sigh of relief at having been freed from that, and then turned their gaze on Subaru with a look that seemed to say—

"Is it just me, or do I see dollar signs floating in their eyes, even in another world?"

"The original condition when we were hired was payment for the goods plus 'other terms negotiable.' On that front, I want to get us all on the same page about how much we're actually going to claim. Ah, of course, I won't suggest anything outrageous. But they've shouldered a fair burden from this whole hostage business too... so brace yourself for it to sting a little."

"Well, it's not my wallet taking the hit. If you go easy where you can, wouldn't it be nice for once to make Roz-cchi's face go pale?"

"As expected, Natsuki-san! You get it!"

Clapping his hands, Otto bounded off in high spirits toward his fellow merchants. At Otto's triumphant return bearing good news, the merchants' cheers rang out through the nighttime village. It seemed, if anything, slightly louder than the villagers' joyous reunion cries, but Subaru made a conscious decision to ignore that and rose to his feet. For the time being, the village folk should be fine. As for the merchants' claims, Otto would compile them, and Roswaal would be the one to process them. Looking forward, just a little, to whatever unpleasant face Roswaal would make at that, Subaru turned his steps toward the manor.

Fifteen minutes on foot from Arlam Village—at the end of that walk stood Roswaal Manor, all alone. In the darkness of night, only the manor's lights asserted its presence, floating in the shadows, and seen from a distance like this after sundown, it did have a certain eerie atmosphere, Subaru thought. Taking in that impression before the gate, he idly gazed at the manor. Naturally, most of its lights were out, with light only spilling from the entrance hall and the servants' quarters. And one more room, near the top floor—Roswaal's study, if he recalled correctly.

"Otto handled the paperwork, but it's probably piled up again since then, over this past week."

Frederica displayed her all-purpose maid skills in that department, possessing clerical abilities rivaling Otto's, but that wasn't the only work she had to do. Even with Petra added as manpower, maintaining the entire manor required considerable effort. Thinking of her toiling away at deskwork like this into the middle of the night, Subaru could sympathize with the hardship.

"I really do need to drag that bastard Otto down into the depths and work him like a beast of burden, as the Emilia Camp's document-processing machine. Let's turn him into a clerical machine."

Thinking of how the young man was probably at this very moment loosening his cheeks over the scent of big money back in the village, Subaru pondered how he might cleverly lure him into that trap, as he pushed open the gate and stepped into the manor grounds. Heading to the entrance, he knocked with the hawk-shaped bird knocker on the door,

"Sorry to bother you this late. I'm here from the fire department—" he trailed off.

The sharp knock echoed in the night darkness, followed by his usual casual greeting. Having said it, he found himself wondering what kind of response this world had for fires and disasters. Tilting his head, Subaru sank briefly into that pointless line of thought. But—

"No answer, huh."

He'd fully expected someone—Frederica most likely—to answer at wind-like speed, so this reaction left him rather deflated. He waited a little while longer, but judging that no one was coming to greet him, Subaru gave up waiting around. He boldly pushed the door open instead,

"Yoo-hoo, I'm home. Food! Bath! Bed!"

Striking a pose, he declared the three demands, playing the domineering husband. But the reaction to this too was, once again, zero. Tasting the nostalgic sensation of dying alone on stage, Subaru decided to head upstairs for now—toward the servants' quarters—to look for Petra.

"If it's the study, Frederica should be there. For now, I'll see Petra first... and, I need to find Beako too."

The three people currently remaining at the manor rose one after another in his mind. Reuniting with precocious Petra and courteously-rude Frederica was one thing, but as for reuniting with that ringlet-haired girl, Subaru would need to steel himself somewhat. Last time, the way they'd parted had been the way it had been.

He hadn't managed to get a single answer to the questions that mattered, and instead had been thrown out amid tears and a crumpled face, and that had been that, ever since.

"Apologizing... would be a weird thing to do, I guess. I don't feel like I did anything wrong, so..."

Still, he'd thought that meeting and talking would change something. He felt he'd managed to move forward a little, by putting one more piece of the past to rest. In his current state of mind, he felt he could face her again, in some different form. For that purpose too—

"First, a preliminary skirmish... or so I thought."

Knocking, then flinging the door open with force to surprise her—another letdown here as well. He hadn't exactly been expecting to stumble onto some blushing, embarrassing changing-time scene, girl or no girl, but as it turned out, he encountered no such scene at all—the room was empty. A room reflecting Petra's tastes, decorated with cute little knick-knacks, yet neatly organized—only, the room's owner was nowhere to be found. Under the light of the crystal lamp, Subaru tilted his head in the room,

"Leaving the light on when you go out doesn't seem like reliable Petra... but if she's not here, don't tell me she's studying in the study?"

That would be plausible, given Frederica's Spartan streak. Perhaps, alongside her serving duties as a maid, she was drilling Petra in clerical work too, aiming to turn her into an all-purpose maid. If that came to pass, it would be an enormous help, but Subaru, already lagging behind Petra in household skills, would truly have nowhere left to stand.

"No, no, I should still have the edge when it comes to basic arithmetic! Don't underestimate modern Japan's compulsory education, you hear!"

Muttering that, he bounded up the stairs two at a time, reaching the top floor—straight down the middle of the hallway to the double doors, where he cleared his throat once more before delivering a knock. A hard sound rang out, one that must surely have reached inside the room. And yet, again, no answer came.

"——"

Even accounting for everything, this was too strange, Subaru thought, raising his guard yet another notch. Covering with idle chatter, Subaru's gaze swept from one end of the hallway to the other, then to the door of the study. He pressed his ear to the door, straining to catch any sound from within, but no sound reached him through the thick door from the other side. The chance of gleaning any information this way, from outside, seemed low.

—Petra's room hadn't been ransacked. It remained neat and orderly, the bed made as if she'd been about to go to sleep. The rest of the manor, too, showed nothing out of place as far as a quick glance could tell. It bore the traces of Frederica's characteristically meticulous work, throughout—not a speck of dust left even on the windowsills. So Subaru's guard had only risen on account of the two of them being nowhere in sight, but—

"——!"

Applying slight pressure to the door, he opened it without a sound. At once, light spilling from inside the room streamed into the hallway, and by that light, he swept his eyes over the interior. An ebony desk. A leather-upholstered chair. A bookshelf against the wall, and a draft blowing through—. The window was closed. Yet he felt the sensation of a cold wind blowing through. Instinct told him that was wrong.

Slipping into the room, Subaru chased after the source of that draft—and noticed. A shelf at the back of the room had slid sideways, revealing a hidden door, normally concealed, set into the wall, just large enough to pass through. Beyond it lay a spiraling staircase, one that continued long, long down, far below into the depths—

"That's right. There was a hidden passage like this. I remember, I remember it."

It was from a previous loop. After the villagers of Arlam Village had been slaughtered to the last by the Witch Cult's hand, and, in the depths of despair, he'd found Rem and Ram's bodies at the manor, Subaru, his ego on the verge of collapse, had made his way here. And then, passing through this hidden passage, he'd gone down underground, and there—

"Got encased in ice by Puck, I think."

He had no certainty about it. Only that, presumably, there had been a row of Witch Cult members, frozen solid in ice, who seemed to have pursued Emilia and the others as they fled through that same passage, and Subaru remembered meeting the same end as them, dying and undergoing a "Return by Death." Afterward, it had been an underground passage he hadn't thought important enough to check on again, and had even forgotten existed. But—

"Why is it, now, of all times..."

The fact that it had been used at all meant that, at the very least, there'd been a need to evacuate. And as for who had used it—naturally, it would be someone at the manor who knew of this passage's existence—Frederica, most likely. The simplest conclusion was that she'd taken Petra and fled somewhere through this passage. The question was—

"Fled from what?"

Given how sharp she was, her decision must have had some solid basis. And given that there were no signs of an attack within the manor itself, that meant whatever danger had been approaching must have been detected beforehand. At that information, Subaru briefly let the word "Witch Cult" flicker through his mind, but he shook his head, chasing it away.

"If it were that, it's way too strange that Frederica wouldn't leave so much as a note. And the people of Arlam Village hadn't noticed anything either... if something as dangerous as the Witch Cult were coming, she'd have taken action to avoid dragging the villagers into it."

At the very least, Frederica had never harbored any doubts about supporting Emilia, whom Roswaal had thrown his lot in with. So she should be taking whatever measures were best, within what she was capable of. And the fact that the villagers knew nothing of this meant it wasn't the Witch Cult. Anyway—

"Frederica and Petra are probably out of the manor. ...In that case, I..."

For a moment, he took a step toward heading through the passage to try to catch up with Frederica and the others, but what stopped his foot was a girl whose name hadn't come up in his thinking so far. Even supposing Frederica had judged that fleeing through here was necessary, would that girl really have gone along with such a flight?

"The Beatrice I know isn't some brat who reads the room like that."

That cheeky, ringlet-haired girl would surely have rejected Frederica's suggestion. She'd shut herself up in her own private forbidden archive, insisting nothing could touch her no matter what happened, kicking away all concern or consideration from others—and then, without fail, wear a lonely expression. Because that was without a doubt what she'd do—

"I'll drag her out...!"

Even if no one else pulled her out, Subaru resolved that he, at least, would do it. It didn't matter how impregnable she believed her own castle to be, alone within it. Knowing danger was closing in on this place, there was no way he could leave a little girl behind here alone.

"Now that that's settled—!"

Turning his back on the hidden passage, Subaru let out a sharp breath and burst out of the study. The surest way to find Beatrice was to go through the manor opening every single door, one after another. With Subaru, he'd have some vague sense along the way of which doors seemed to lead to Beatrice's forbidden archive. Relying on that, all he had to do was find her. First, starting with the top floor's doors, one after another—

"Whoa?"

Just then, his feet, driving forward with momentum, suddenly tangled, and Subaru tripped. More than any sense of pathetic-ness at such a poor start, embarrassment came first. Just when he'd tried to look cool, this happened, and it fell rather flat. Bracing his hands against the hallway carpet, Subaru turned to look back at whatever he'd tripped on. Looking, he saw something lying just a little ways past the study door.

The pink thing was quite long, extending all the way to Subaru's feet, several paces away. Tracing it, wondering just how far it went, he followed it back—and there was nothing complicated about it at all. —It was spilling out of Subaru's own torn side.

"—Huh?"

The left side of his clothing had been cleanly torn open, and pink innards were spilling out from within. It started from just outside the study door, and now, at Subaru's feet, tangled and tripping him up—which meant, somewhere along the way, without his even noticing, his abdomen had been cut open.

"...Ohb."

The instant that registered, a clot of blood surged up, blocking his throat, and his vision was dyed crimson. Unable to withstand the pressure in his abdomen, his innards spilling out, he tried with trembling fingertips to push them back inside, but lacked the strength, and collapsed to his knees on the spot. His body couldn't hold itself up, and his upper body toppled over.

He had no idea what had happened. Just now, Subaru had, without question, been running.

"—I told you, didn't I? We made a promise, didn't we?"

A voice suddenly reached him. From straight ahead, from somewhere above the head of the collapsing Subaru, someone was casting down their voice.

He had no strength left to lift his face. His consciousness, amid the spilling innards and flowing blood, was desperately trying to grasp hold of a world slipping further and further away. His body temperature dropped rapidly, and each time he coughed, his face was smeared with the clot of blood blocking his throat, as he strained with clouding eyes to make sense of the world.

Instinct told him: this is the end. Understanding that somewhere in his heart, Subaru nonetheless scolded himself that he couldn't let it end like this. He had to seize hold of something, or he couldn't let it end. He had to grasp at even just one thing, or he couldn't be allowed to end. Something, something, something, something, something, anything at all.

Footsteps sent ripples through the pool, and a black silhouette stood over the blood-red-stained hallway. Black attire. Slender build. Black hair. A sultry gaze, looking down at him with something like affection. He recognized them, and, remembering the sensation of "having his stomach cut open," Subaru understood.

Not the Witch Cult—but certainly, a real threat. It was—

"Until we meet again, do take good care of those intestines for me."

A declaration of love beyond all reason. In that, Subaru grasped hold of something certain—and his consciousness faded.

Fading, fading, fading, dimming, dimming, and then, at last. Everything vanished, ended, and then—began again.

—The fourth loop raises its curtain.
