Arc 2 Chapter 11: "After-Meal Chat"

Once the drawn-out breakfast finally wrapped up, I'd have thought everyone would naturally drift off into their own time. But—

"Fi-i-irst off, Emilia-sama, please keep to your usual schedule. Ram, please show him — Subaru — around the mansion. Rem, carry on as usual... but before that—"

Roswaal, briskly issuing orders, radiated all the presence you'd expect from the master of a manor — though that grandeur was rather undercut by the sheer size of his collar. As if he'd read Subaru's thoughts exactly, he grinned,

"Before tha-a-at, though, introductions are in order, don't you think? Luckily, every resident of the mansion happens to be here right now."

"Much obliged, much obliged... wait, seriously, that's it? The whole household, minus me, is only five people and one animal!?"

"Counting Nii-cha as 'one animal' — there's a limit to how rude you can be, I suppose."

Cutting into Subaru's surprise was Beatrice, plainly irritated. Subaru raised a hand at her, sounding put-upon, "Yeah, yeah—"

"—minus me, that's four people, one fluffball, and one drill-thing... there, happy now?"

"...I've heard that word a few times now — what, exactly, is this 'drill,' I wonder?"

"A drill is the soul of a man. Pure romance."

"I was a fool for even asking, I suppose. Roswaal, may Betty go back now? I'd like some quiet time with Nii-cha, I suppose."

Rubbing her eyes with a weary air, in a gesture quite unbecoming of a young girl, Beatrice turned back to Roswaal. In front of her, Puck — still perched atop the table — puffed out his chest with his paws on his hips and declared, "One fluffball!" It was, somehow, rather endearing.

"I ge-e-et that you two don't exactly click, but you're going to be living together from now on, so try meeting him halfway a little more, plea-ease?"

"Betty believes she has already made every concession she's willing to make, I suppose. Ask for more than that, and even the head of House Mathers had best be prepared for what follows, I suppose."

Beatrice's threat came with a sudden quieting of her tone. At that abrupt shift in atmosphere, Subaru felt the air in the room grow heavy. The cause was Beatrice, of course — but the fact that it was "of course" was itself the strange part. As it had been at their first encounter, this chestnut-haired girl was, despite her childish appearance, more than a little "off." It wasn't just her cheeky way of talking, or the fact that she was drinking alcohol at her age. Something more fundamental simply failed to mesh. And so—

"Sorry to interrupt the intimidation act, but what exactly are you, anyway? You're sitting here like it's the most natural thing in the world, but I have zero idea where you fit into all this."

"Betty is not in a good mood right now, I suppose. You'd best mind how you speak, human. If Betty felt like it, she could turn you to stone and shatter you where you stand, I suppose."

Beatrice answered Subaru's remark — deliberately made without reading the heavy mood — with her brows knitting downward. Subaru instinctively caught his breath at the sheer menace radiating off the little girl, the kind that made his body want to brace itself.

"Betty's the librarian of the forbidden archive here in Roswaal's mansion~"

"Nii-cha!?"

But that heavy air dissolved instantly at the leisurely interjection from the gray cat. The little cat nibbled at the edge of what was most accurately described as a rusk — something made, apparently, by taking what looked like bread crusts, coating them in a sugar-like seasoning, and frying them —

"Sweet! So good! Nyah!"

"Sorry to interrupt while you're losing all higher brain function to sugar, but let's get into that in more detail — mofu mofu mofu mofu mofu—"

Subaru had leaned in to ask his question, but his hand, reaching out without thinking, brushed Puck's ears and the mofu-ing began. Getting mofu'd to his heart's content, Puck went on wolfing down rusks at a blistering pace,

"Roswaal's a 'reasonably' accomplished mage, you know. House Mathers has quite a history, and there are all sorts of books lying around that really shouldn't be seen by just anyone. Betty's job, under Contract, is to keep those books out of sight. Isn't that right?"

"Yes, that's right, I suppose. Whatever Nii-cha says is always correct, I suppose."

"So going through Puck is how you actually get through to her — mofu. Guess it just goes to show, everyone's equal before a mofu-critter — mofu."

"Your sentence endings are turning into 'mofu,' you know."

Called out by Emilia with a cold look, Subaru hastily let go. Without meaning to, he'd just proven the addictiveness of mofu-ing on himself. A tragedy born of loving to mofu too much — how ironic.

"Guess the mofu-critter's addictiveness backfired on me... wait, hey!"

In front of Subaru, still playing the tragic actor, Beatrice had timidly reached out and touched Puck's ears. She confirmed the sensation with cautious fingers, and at the bliss of the softness returning to her fingertips, her lovely little face went soft and melted into a smile. Subaru caught his breath despite himself at a cuteness that matched her appearance perfectly — while Puck, indifferent to having his ears played with, went right on devouring rusks. Subaru crossed his arms at the sight, turning his face away while sliding his eyes toward the pair,

"H-hmph. It's not like I'm upset just because Puck's two-timing me or anything!"

"Don't be so cold, Subaru. You and I are closer than that, aren't we, nya? A girl like her, I'll just milk her for treats and then toss her aside."

"Nii-cha's so mean, I suppose... mofu mofu."

"Oops, sorry, cute little Betty. Of course that was a lie. You're the most important thing to me, obviously, so you're the only one who's special."

For a palm-sized pet cat, he was behaving like a master manipulator, toying with both their hearts. Between the two of them, both swept up in sweet emotion at his faintly roguish act, Emilia cut in with her hands on her hips. Reaching out, she pinched Puck's head between her fingers and lifted him, wringing out a "nyaun" cry precisely calculated to seize the heart of any cat lover,

"Puck, don't go getting carried away either. And you shouldn't eat so many sweets. If you get all fat and round, I'm not letting you ride on my shoulder anymore."

"Fufufu, jealous? You just can't stand it if you're not number one, can you, Lia? Don't worry, rest easy. My heart's home will always be with you. Even if I drift on the wind like a stray cloud, the place I return to will always be... nya-nya-nya-nya!"

Right in the middle of his cheese-tastic sweet talk, the fingers pinching his head tightened, grinding down until he gave up. Emilia sighed at the sight of Puck gone limp and unmoving, then turned a reproachful look on Subaru.

"Subaru's bad influence is rubbing off on my boy..."

"Is that really on me? Isn't that just how he naturally is!? And correcting it with an iron claw hold — I just witnessed some real Journey to the West-style domestic darkness."

It felt exactly like watching Tripitaka and Sun Wukong go at it from the sidelines. That said, from everything Subaru had seen so far, he'd have guessed their parent-child dynamic ran the other way around. — Well, outside of serious moments, this was probably just how Puck normally acted. As long as he came through when it counted, there was no real problem.

"Well, judging by yesterday, when it actually counted, you were useless, so I guess that doesn't hold up... hey, at this rate you're just a talking cat with a loose mouth."

"That's just fine for Nii-cha! Just being here is enough, I suppose! Everything else is only a small part of Nii-cha's many virtues, I suppose!"

"Your level of worship is pretty demonic in its own right! Anyway, setting that aside — 'guardian of the forbidden archive' really tickles my inner middle-schooler. Let's hear the details."

He formed both hands into finger-guns and pointed with both at once. Twirling one of her drill curls with an expression of pure distaste at the gesture, Beatrice began speaking, haltingly and with obvious reluctance.

"Nii-cha's explanation just now covered almost all of it, I suppose. To put it more plainly... that room you wandered into, that's it, I suppose."

"Ah, the one buried in books."

Recalling the sheer volume of books — enough to worry the floor might give way — Subaru thought, well, no wonder they called it a forbidden archive. Then again, if every single one of those was a banned book forbidden from ever leaving the premises, didn't that start to feel just a little bit criminal?

Forbidden books — a wonderful phrase that seizes a man's heart and shakes it, and never lets go. Looking back, in his middle school days, when the blue flower of budding sexuality was in full bloom, he and his friends used to worship those treasured tomes that let loose such blue impulses, calling them "forbidden books" with great reverence. The kendo club Subaru had belonged to, in particular, boasted an inexplicably overwhelming stockpile of such forbidden books — so much so that there'd been a dedicated storage space built beneath the floor of the club room in the activities building. Then came the long-running feud with the baseball club, an information leak during contact with other schools at the culture festival — and, right before graduation, the utterly unthinkable incident where the forbidden book storeroom caught fire. That was the incident that led to the kendo club room being abolished; the survivors among the treasured books, left homeless, were said to have scattered far and wide.

"Thinking back on it now, I feel kind of bad for the underclassmen. All that history of the kendo club's forbidden archive, passed down generation after generation. The neatness of it all, sorted by era and genre — I miss that, honestly..."

"Betty went to the trouble of answering your question, and I can tell you're off thinking about the single most pointless thing in the entire world right now, and it makes me furious enough to die, I suppose."

"Don't get so prickly, eat some sardines. Calcium calms the nerves, and who knows, maybe you'd even grow a little taller? Personally, I think a height difference around Emilia-tan's size is just right for hugging and rom-com shenanigans..."

Under the guise of giving well-meaning advice to a fuming Beatrice, he shot furtive glances toward Emilia, staking a claim on his interest in her. But she seemed to have flatly dismissed his little delusional outburst outright, and instead raised a finger as though something else entirely had caught her attention.

"Wait a second, Subaru. Didn't you just say you went into the forbidden archive?"

"Yeah, I went in. I went back to my room to sulk myself to sleep, and this drill-loli here had gone and remodeled it into her own workplace. And on top of that, she went and sucked a huge chunk of life force right out of me with a shloop... what is that, some kind of yakuza shakedown?"

At Subaru's complaint, his sanpaku eyes narrowing further, Beatrice turned her face away, all pointed ignoring. Emilia turned the topic her way instead,

"Beatrice... don't tell me you invited Subaru into the archive?"

"As if, I suppose. Why on earth would Betty go out of her way to invite in a lowlife like this? ...He just went and guessed the answer to my 'Door Crossing' on his own, uninvited, I suppose."

"Your manner of speaking seems to be losing its refinement there, my lady. Ohohohoho."

Beatrice seethed in silent fury at Subaru, who laughed elegantly behind the back of his hand. Behind that one-sidedly heartwarming exchange between the two of them, Roswaal, who had been quiet until now, touched a finger to his nose and said, "Heh,"

"Even I ha-a-ave a hard time reaching the forbidden archive by chance, and yet you got connected there without Beatrice's permission at all... You two must be quite compatible, hm?"

"Haaah!?" "Haaah!?"

Both of them scowled and whipped around toward Roswaal at once. Then, in perfect unison, they turned back to glare at each other, and in the same motion, both twisted their lips into a frown.

"Your expressions are mirror images, both of you."

"Cute, adorable Betty, compatible with this? That's a joke that isn't funny at all, I suppose."

"Ditto, I'm telling you. I don't even have a loli fetish to begin with. Say something like that to me and I can't laugh anymore. I just... can't laugh anymore..."

"Why are you acting so serious about it, I wonder!? I've had enough, I suppose! The rest of you can just do whatever you want without me, I suppose!"

At Subaru's display of fine craftsmanship — actually shading his face for dramatic effect — Beatrice's patience finally snapped, and she erupted completely. She rose from her seat, strode briskly to the dining room door, and shoved it open with a bang. And then—

"Huh? Where's the hallway?"

It was only natural such a question slipped out. Because beyond the door — which should have connected to the hallway leading to the center of the mansion — stretched the very archive Subaru had entered once before. Beatrice stepped into that room, a forest of bookshelves packed so full it seemed to declare "not one more volume could possibly fit," and turned a somehow triumphant face toward Subaru,

"This is 'Door Crossing,' I suppose. Burn its magnificence into your eyes, and tremble accordingly, I suppose. — For a while, I don't want to so much as look at your face, I suppose."

The door slammed shut with full force, and the small girl's figure vanished beyond it. Subaru blinked, utterly bewildered. What surprised him even further was Ram's action — reopening the closed door without being asked to do anything of the sort. The door, which had just been violently shut, now swung slowly open again, revealing beyond it the very corridor Subaru had walked down on his own two feet. It was hard to believe that, just a moment before, that same door had connected to the archive.

"I see. So basically, it's magic that can connect any door in the mansion to her own room. Perfect for a shut-in, huh."

"You catch on fa-a-ast. Though I'm not quite sure what a 'shut-in' is."

"It's a parasite that holes up in its room and never comes out, except when it needs food or a bath."

"I see-e-e. Yes, well, then Beatrice is a shut-in, isn't she."

"—I can hear you, both of you, I suppose."

The door Ram had shut opened just a crack from the other side, and Beatrice peeked her face through, glaring this way. Right after declaring she didn't even want to see his face — and now this. Subaru put both hands on his hips and did a little shuffling step, flapping his arms like a bird taking wing.

"Well, well, well? What's the matter? What's the matter? You just stormed off with all that momentum — did something happen? Forget something? Now that's a real pickle, huh. Hey, hey, how's it feel? How does it feel right now?"

The NDK Dance — "Ne-e, Do-nna Ki-mochi" ("Hey, How's It Feel") — exploded into full swing. The reply he received was simple but devastating: Beatrice thrust a palm out through the door toward him and blasted him clean off his feet with raw magical force.

"Bonjoru!"

Struck full-force by an invisible mass of compressed air, Subaru's body — which should have been anything but light — went sailing through the air with ridiculous ease. Tossed about in a whirl that stripped away all sense of up, down, left, and right, he sailed clean over the dining table and slammed into the wall. The maids, arriving in an instant, deftly steadied the wall-hung paintings and furnishings as they shook. Then they mercilessly stomped on Subaru where he lay collapsed at their feet. Nice follow-through.
