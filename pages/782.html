Arc 10 Chapter 23: "Seeking the Optimal Path"

—The Royal Lugunica Castle, which he had looked up at countless times, was far off in the distance, its chalk-white structure looking as if it were about to be swallowed by the evening sky.

"—Emilia."

As Subaru murmured those words under his breath, a heartbreaking ache filled his chest. Emilia, now imprisoned inside that castle atop the royal capital. Settling her upon the throne in that place was Subaru's dream—never had it been to see her bound in a cell.

"...Emilia is a candidate for the Royal Selection, I suppose. At the very least, on the surface, she should be treated with utmost courtesy, I suppose. She would not be thrown into a cell, I suppose."

"I know. I know that. But that's not what I mean..."

"――――"

"Ah, sorry... You're just trying to cheer me up."

"It is quite alright, I suppose. Encouraging a Subaru with no room to spare is a partner's privilege, I suppose. Petra and Rem would weep with frustration if they knew, I suppose."

"Setting Petra aside, I'm not so sure about Rem. Doesn't seem like she'd be frustrated about it."

Subaru offered a wry smile, surprised at his own ability to smile at a time like this. Up until a moment ago, his heart had felt as though it were tearing apart from his own powerlessness and the thought of having no choice but to leave Emilia behind, but Beatrice's presence was truly grand. When he stole a glance at her, the girl in question gave him a cute, smug look, causing him to leak a small sigh once more.

—Subaru, Beatrice, and the rest of the group gathered at the Six Tongues staging area were preparing to move according to plan under Russell's instructions.

The staging area had been set up—daringly enough—in the largest warehouse in the commercial district. Located near the protective wall adjacent to the noble district, it gave Subaru a vivid glimpse into the impressive nature of the Six Tongues' covert operations—their ability to blend seamlessly into the royal capital and exist as if entirely natural. At present, Subaru and the others had moved to the rear of the staging area, gazing up at Royal Lugunica Castle, where Emilia should be, until the very last moment before the escape arrangements were completed.

"――――"

The main group consisted of Crusch, who remained unconscious and asleep; Rachins, who, unlike Subaru and the others, had shown no resistance to escaping the capital; White Sheep and Black Dog; and Russell, who led them all. Besides them, however, there appeared to be multiple other operatives handling disguises and information disruption. As expected of an intelligence organization operating directly under the Kingdom, everything they did was on a grand scale.

"Even with all that power, though, they were completely bested, I suppose."

"Yeah, but we're not gonna stay beaten down. That's why we're escaping—this is just taking a running start for a comeback."

"A running start, I suppose? ...That is a little unexpected, I suppose."

As Subaru voiced his determination not to lose, Beatrice, holding his hand, suddenly murmured those words. When Subaru tilted his head at her "Unexpected?", Beatrice slightly knit her brows and said,

"That Subaru was able to decide to leave the royal capital so quickly, I suppose. Leaving Emilia behind and not joining up with Otto and the others—both must have been painful choices, I suppose."

"That's... definitely true, and it still hurts right now. Before I headed over to Miklotov-san's place, I was talking with Emilia-tan about what to have for lunch, you know? She told me she was making boxed lunches today with Rem and Petra, so I should look forward to it... And now, here we are."

"Subaru..."

"It's painful, and it sucks. But the fact that you didn't object means you know this is for the best too, right, Beako?"

As Subaru dropped his voice with a pathetic tone, Beatrice gave a small nod after a brief silence. She hadn't said anything back then. Beatrice, too, had been certain that dragging their feet in the capital would only lead to a losing battle. Even so, her refraining from rushing Subaru's decision had been her own consideration for him—so that Subaru himself could come to terms with it. Not giving up reluctantly because someone else told him to, but allowing Subaru to choose for himself.

"My Beako is cute, but strict."

"What is more, this cuteness and strictness come with a lifetime warranty, I suppose."

"What a bargain. I'm so glad I contracted with Beako Insurance."

Rubbing her head gently, Subaru felt his heart cleansed by his partner's reliability. Normally she would quickly squirm away saying "Stop that, I suppose~!", but letting him have his way now was no doubt another act of Beatrice's kindness, looking out for his feelings. Just then—

"We will be ready to depart shortly. Are both of you alright?"

The one who called out to them was White Sheep. Over her black suit, she wore a white robe—the same perception-blocking kind as Subaru and Beatrice—and had let down her tied-up hair, subtly changing her appearance. A combination of perception blocking and a mild disguise to conceal her identity.

"Huh."

"W-What is it? Is there something strange about me?!"

"Ah, sorry, sorry, no. I just thought your impression changed quite a bit compared to when you were only in the suit. If anything, your current outfit feels much more like 'White Sheep-chan.'"

"That... might be true. Before Silver Fox took me in... back when I was at Mama's place, I wore clothes like this more often."

"Ah, sorry. I made you remember bad stuff."

To Subaru apologizing for making her bring up unpleasant memories, White Sheep gave a faint smile and said, "No." She then stroked her long, navy-blue hair and—

"...I'm the one who should apologize."

"—? What are you apologizing for?"

"For rushing you... when it was all for our sake. By rights, Natsuki Subaru-san must have wanted to take more time to decide..."

Seeing White Sheep cast her eyes down, speaking as if she were the one suffering, Subaru couldn't help but give a wry smile. Though she had left Capella's subordinate group, the Beloved Children, and was now a member of Russell's Six Tongues, her innate goodness truly made her unfit to be either a villain's henchman or a spy's accomplice. After all, she was trying to take responsibility even for the time Subaru took to make his choice.

"You don't need to shoulder my burdens too. I don't feel like I was rushed at all. If anything, I was grateful to be given the information I needed to make a judgment."

"Natsuki Subaru-san..."

"If you still feel awkward about it no matter what, then stop that."

"Eh?"

"Calling me by my full name like 'Natsuki Subaru-san'. Natsuki-san or Subaru-san is fine, but I'd prefer one or the other. Calling me by my full name makes me tense up."

Shrugging his shoulders as he made the request with as much casualness as he could muster, White Sheep opened her eyes wide and blinked her lips open and closed for a moment. Beside Subaru, Beatrice let out a grand sigh at White Sheep and said,

"There is no need to read too deeply into it, I suppose. He is literally saying only what his words mean, I suppose."

"What's with that unnecessary disclaimer... What other meaning could there even be?"

"I wouldn't know~, I'm not telling, I suppose~"

"That's totally the tone of someone who knows!"

As Beatrice covered her ears and turned her head away, Subaru pouted in annoyance. Perhaps disarmed by their banter, the tension suddenly drained from the shoulders of White Sheep, who had been standing there dumbfounded until now. And then—

"—Would 'Subaru-san' and 'Beatrice-san' be alright?"

"Yeah, that works. Hell, you can even call her Betchin."

"Betchin?! Not even Subaru has ever called me that, I suppose?!"

"I-It doesn't sound like she would respond to that, so I'll stick with this..."

Giving an exceedingly modest response, White Sheep relinquished the historic right to call Beatrice "Betchin." However, showing no lingering regret over it, she instead gazed at Subaru with eyes containing some other sort of sorrow. Just as Subaru knit his brows at her expression, her lips moved—

"Um, Subaru-san, Ti—"

"—Hey! You bastards, how long are you gonna linger around?! Get over here already!"

However, at that moment, Rachins barged in, his rough voice raspy and harsh. Under Rachins' gaze, his sharp sanpaku eyes narrowed even further, White Sheep shrank back with a frightened "Eek!". Shielding White Sheep behind him, Subaru glared back at Rachins and said,

"Man, that robe really does not suit you at all."

"Shut up, leave me alone! Besides, why the hell do I gotta be bossed around by Russell Fellow? He should just make the guys under slave contracts do the work!"

"I-I-It's not a slave contract!"

"White Sheep-chan, flustering like that makes it sound real..."

Chiding White Sheep for taking Rachins' foul mouth so seriously, Subaru looked toward the distant castle once more and let out a long breath—simply praying for Emilia's safety.

"Emilia is surely praying for the exact same thing for Subaru's sake, I suppose."

"For our sake. I'd be happier with that than just for me, and that's more like Emilia."

Responding to Beatrice, who could read his inner thoughts with just a single glance, Subaru turned his back on the castle as if shaking off his lingering regrets, and began walking. They would head straight through the staging area and board the dragon carriage parked outside. Just then, inside the rapidly clearing staging area, he spotted a robed back attempting to carry out a box roughly the size of a full embrace. Although he didn't know the contents, the person appeared to be struggling under its considerable weight—

"Should I carry this to the dragon carriage outside?"

"Ah..."

Subaru quickly reached out and lightly lifted the box in their stead—no, "lightly" was a lie. It was quite heavy, requiring a proper heave. Fortunately, he managed to lift it by himself, preserving his dignity. As Subaru inwardly breathed a sigh of relief, the person who had been struggling with the box—a figure with their hood pulled down low over their eyes—showed a bewildered reaction.

"—? Are you alright?"

Subaru tilted his head at the bewildered figure. Their face was obscured by the hood, but they were of slender build. Still, in terms of unknown faces, Black Dog was far more suspicious, so that was no particular reason for doubt. Rather than suspicion, what Subaru found odd was the person's awkward reaction. However, before he had time to press the issue, the figure bowed quickly and hurried off.

"To not even offer thanks, what a rude fellow, I suppose."

"Nah, some people are just shy. Not everyone is friendly and cute like Garfiel, after all."

"Why bring up Garfiel there...? Well, I suppose I understand what you mean."

Charming qualities show through not just in appearance, but in attitude and actions. As Subaru and Beatrice nodded to each other in shared agreement on their camp's common view, White Sheep noticed Subaru carrying the luggage and called out, "I'm sorry,"

"Making Subaru-san carry that... I'll mention it to Blue Snake-san later."

"No, no, something like this is nothing... Wait, that person just now was 'Blue Snake'? If I remember right, that's the person who healed my burns, right?"

"Eh, ah, yes. A healer with exceptional mastery of healing magic."

When Subaru reacted to the codename—Blue Snake—White Sheep affirmed it. After Sheep, Dog, and Fox, having a healer be Snake was quite stylish. In Subaru's knowledge, snakes and medicine were inextricably linked. In Greek mythology, the Rod of Asclepius, the doctor, was even said to have a snake coiled around it. Though the connection between that and the name 'Blue Snake' was almost certainly a coincidence.

"In that case, I'm the one who needs to thank them. Carrying a box isn't even close to repaying the favor."

"Ah, but that might be difficult. Blue Snake-san doesn't seem to like talking to others very much... Silver Fox told us to respect those wishes, as well."

"Hmm? An antisocial doctor, huh? ...Sounds even more like a brilliant physician now."

Highly skilled but eccentric was practically a staple trope for brilliant doctors in manga and anime. Accepting White Sheep's point, Subaru gave up on contacting Blue Snake for now. Instead, he decided he would help out whenever he saw them struggling with heavy loads, repaying his debt bit by bit. At any rate—

"—Let's go. So that we can return here again."

As if convincing no one other than himself, Natsuki Subaru declared those words, and with Beatrice by his side, stepped outside the staging area.

△▼△▼△▼△

"—The principle behind Perception Blocking is to subtly alter the impression, aura, and manner in which an object presents itself, minimizing any notice it might attract at any given moment. In short,"

"In short?"

"Under a strict security net, it is nothing more than a minor comfort. Please do not over-rely on it."

Facing Russell inside the dragon carriage, Subaru gave a small nod at his words and pulled his and Beatrice's hoods down a bit deeper over their faces.

The Six Tongues' preparations were complete, and they were now in a line of dragon carriages disguised as a merchant caravan leaving the capital. The dragon carriages provided were not high-class, but rather plain, standard vehicles stripped of ostentation—the kind handled by peddlers on the open roads, not just in the royal capital. To maintain the merchant caravan disguise, the convoy consisted of around ten carriages rather than one or two, which gave Subaru a lingering anxiety that it might draw eyes in its own right, but—

"Rest assured. The royal capital is currently in a state of minor tension. Many choose to temporarily leave the capital out of aversion to this. We are simply taking advantage of that flow."

"That state of tension..."

"Naturally, it stems from the fear that a dangerous fugitive may be hiding within the city. A considerable number of Kingdom soldiers have been mobilized, conducting heavy-handed investigations. While Lord McMahon's death is being kept from the general public, the atmosphere of an emergency cannot be hidden."

"Well... that makes sense. Everyone must be filled with anxiety."

Having his expectations of the capital's mood confirmed, Subaru felt pity for the people of the capital caught up in the secret maneuverings of 'Lust'. Seeing this, Russell narrowed his eyes slightly and said,

"Natsuki-dono, do you worry so deeply not only for Emilia-sama and your camp, but for the citizens of the capital as well?"

"Eh? No, well, it's not like I'm worrying endlessly, but I do wonder if they'll be okay. Even Capella wouldn't pointlessly swap citizens around with her Authority... or rather, the fact that I can't say for sure she won't might be why I feel this way."

As part of taking control of the country, swapping members of the Council of Wise Men or high-ranking nobles who held decision-making authority in the royal castle was undoubtedly the optimal strategy for a coup. Compared to that, using her Authority on ordinary citizens was nothing more than harassment, but Capella was the sort who might do even that with glee. Of course, her actions in Priestella had likely been aimed at intentionally advertising her presence and the effects of Lust's Authority to keep the Six Tongues' hands full, but—

"――――"

"...Wait, Russell-san?"

Just as his thoughts reached that point, Subaru noticed Russell had gone silent. In response to Subaru calling his name, Russell, who had been stroking his beard with a finger and closing one eye, offered a brief "No," and continued,

"Natsuki-dono's concerns are quite reasonable. I do not know if it offers any comfort, but the Six Tongues are not pulling out of the capital entirely. A significant number will remain behind to monitor the situation and conduct disruption operations. Please trust in their efforts."

"Yeah, sorry, but I have no choice but to leave that to you guys. If I had ten or twenty arms and five or six bodies, I could be of more help, but..."

"What an outrageous metaphor, I suppose. Subaru as he is right now is pretty much purr-fect, I suppose."

"I appreciate the 'pretty much' rating. ...Anyway, you guys are the only ones we can rely on over there. Show us the true strength of the intelligence agency Six Tongues."

Showing a tightly clenched fist, Russell nodded his chin in understanding. Finding that response reassuring, Subaru directed his awareness toward the back of the dragon carriage—toward one of the other carriages. As mentioned earlier, the Six Tongues' disguised merchant caravan consisted of over a dozen dragon carriages, but Subaru and his group were riding in the lead, with personnel dispersed among them. What weighed on Subaru's mind in particular was Crusch, who had not woken up even once at the staging area.

Ever since falling asleep in the sewers, her consciousness had not returned. Just before slipping into sleep, Crusch had mentioned the influence of the 'Dragon's Eye', but that had been her own self-diagnosis; in reality, no one understood what that 'Dragon's Eye' truly was. Even though Beatrice had assured him that there was no factor other than exhaustion—

"Is Crusch-sama on your mind?"

"Well, yeah. But the same goes for you guys, right? If you want to borrow Crusch-san's strength to fight the Beloved Children, shouldn't you be anxious too?"

"Anxious, indeed. However, an individual who has no equal when it comes to medical diagnosis has stated that there are no concerns regarding Crusch-sama's physical body itself. Of course, there remains concern regarding this 'Dragon's Eye' you mentioned, Natsuki-dono..."

"So for now, we just have to treat her gently and quietly wait for her to wake up, huh?"

"Indeed."

Having his concerns answered so courteously, Subaru sighed at how many things lay beyond his power. In truth, the ones prioritizing Crusch's safety above all else were the Six Tongues. There was no doubt they would exert every effort on her behalf. Right down to assigning a skilled healer—

"Speaking of which, was it 'Blue Snake-san'? I ran into them briefly earlier—are they looking after Crusch-san?"

"—...You met Blue Snake?"

"Eh? Ah, yeah, just passed by them right before leaving. Didn't get a word out of them. White Sheep-chan said so too, but they're pretty shy, huh?"

"...Yes. That is largely correct. Blue Snake is indeed tending to Crusch-sama. Rest assured."

Perhaps surprised that the shy or antisocial Blue Snake had encountered Subaru, Russell took a moment to reply for the first time, a reaction Subaru found refreshing. However, while reassured to hear that Blue Snake was attending to Crusch—

"Ferris must be terribly worried too."

Ferris had been dismissed from his position as her foremost knight by Crusch, and had brushed off Subaru's offer to mediate between them. Since parting ways at the capital's cemetery, his whereabouts were unknown, but he was surely still in the capital. Naturally, news of this disturbance must have reached his ears as well. If only he had met up with Wilhelm and wasn't doing anything rash—just as Subaru was thinking that:

"—Did we stop?"

Suddenly, feeling the carriage come to a halt, Subaru involuntarily stiffened. He instinctively wrapped an arm around Beatrice's shoulder, preparing for any sudden emergency. However, seeing Subaru's reaction, Russell smiled across from them and said, "Rest assured,"

"It appears we have arrived at the waiting line for the checkpoint at the capital's main gates. It is normally crowded, but we may be kept waiting for quite some time."

"Kept waiting for quite some time? Night's about to fall, are that many people leaving the capital?"

"Though limited to those quick to hear, quick to judge, or sharp about profit and loss, day or night makes no difference to such people. Tomorrow, if this atmosphere spreads a bit further, even greater numbers may move."

"It is just like a repeat of the Empire, I suppose. Back then as well, a great crowd fled the Imperial Capital, I suppose."

"I was thinking the exact same thing. I really don't want to make this like the Imperial Capital."

Nodding at Beatrice's words, Subaru recalled Lupugana, the Imperial Capital of Vollachia. The mass exodus of citizens from the Imperial Capital—turned into a city of the dead by Sphinx, who led the Great Disaster—under Abel's command was still fresh in his memory. But that had succeeded because it was Vollachia, where powerful top-down authority functioned; demanding the same in Lugunica would be difficult. All the more so because, unlike the undead, there was no way to distinguish Capella's Beloved Children from regular people.

"That is what Crusch-sama's Divine Protection is for, and why we are here. —Natsuki-dono, though I am sure it goes without saying for you, please always search for the optimal path."

Subaru looked straight back at Russell, whose tone had dropped slightly. Russell spoke not with the face of a merchant, but with the face of the head of an intelligence organization—the commander of the Six Tongues. Words that were likely a self-admonishment, reminding himself of what mattered above all else.

"Searching for... the optimal path..."

"Yes, precisely. For the Kingdom, for the future, for your cherished ones—whatever it may be, to reach your ultimate destination, always pursue the best course. So that, if fulfilling it requires a choice accompanied by pain, you may still choose it."

"――――"

His manner of speech was calm, devoid of heat. Yet that did not mean it failed to touch Subaru's heart. To be sure, passionate words that appeal to emotion resonate deeply. But the reason Russell's words lacked heat was because they had already been struck and forged into a shape that would never change again. One strikes iron while it is hot to forge it into the desired shape. —Yet there is no meaning in heating, striking, and deliberately warping iron that has already taken its final form.

"—It appears we have arrived at the checkpoint. I shall handle it; please wait here."

Stopping, advancing, stopping again. Called by the driver through the small window of the dragon carriage after repeating that cycle, Russell stood up and stepped down from the passenger compartment. Watching his back leave, Subaru let out a deep breath. At that moment, Beatrice beside him said,

"Goodness gracious, I suppose. That man... being Roswaal's friend makes total sense now, I suppose."

"As expected of Beako. I thought the exact same thing. Just pointing in a different direction, but the angle of their vectors feels identical. ...Searching for the optimal path, huh?"

"Subaru is always doing that, I suppose. Betty gives her absolute guarantee, I suppose."

Beatrice looked indignant, as though Subaru had been accused of slacking off. Of course, Russell surely had no such intention, and Beatrice knew that too. At the same time, she understood that capable people always carried that sort of frustration within them.

"—The checkpoint looks pretty heavy-duty."

Standing up, Subaru opened the small window leading to the driver's seat slightly to peer outside. Side-by-side with Beatrice, who peeked out with an "I suppose," they looked out at the regular window set up at the main gates of the royal capital, as well as a temporary watchtower and staging area constructed separately. At the window, travelers were being asked to submit transit permits and cargo manifests; usually, merely entering or leaving the capital was never this strict. The security level normally set higher up—at the entrance to the noble district—was being applied all the way down here.

"Yes, we are deeply concerned. Therefore, moving in haste is standard practice for merchants. All necessary permits and manifests are prepared. The cargo already carries stamped tags, but would you care to inspect it again yourself?"

Looking over, Subaru saw Russell speaking just like that to the guard on duty at the checkpoint. Beside him was White Sheep; Russell was thrusting document after document held by her—decked in her perception-blocking robe—at the guard in rapid succession. Before his quiet yet resolutely forceful pressure, the guard appeared utterly dizzy.

"No flaws in the paperwork, and the Commerce Guild stamp is authentic. The items on the manifest... are primarily medical supplies?"

"Speed is paramount; transportation time directly correlates with human lives."

Leveraging his public title as representative of the Commerce Guild, and appealing to moral sensibilities through the nature of the cargo, Russell left the guard no room to nitpick. And for the finishing blow—

"Please! Without this medicine, everyone in my village will...!"

Trembling with emotion, White Sheep took the guard's hands, pleading in a manner that felt far from acting. Driven to that point, whatever doubt remained on the guard's face finally peeled away. With his hands still held by White Sheep, the guard coughed with a dignified look and said,

"U-Understood. I see no particular issues. Transit is permit—"

"—Hold it! Who said you could skip procedure!"

"—!"

Just as the guard on duty was about to grant permission and White Sheep was on the verge of expressing overwhelmed relief, an order to wait cut in. Striding in from the side and intruding on the spot was an older man. Likely the superior officer of the guard on duty. Bursting out of the guard house, the senior officer snatched the papers from his subordinate's hands and checked the contents, saying:

"Commerce Guild certification and stamped cargo, is it? However, rules are rules."

"An admirable mindset, but is this a situation where rigid bureaucracy applies?"

"Spoken like a merchant, but just as merchants have their ways, guards have their pride."

"Is that so? —Understood."

The subordinate guard stood at attention at his superior's words, and seeing that, Russell gave a nod. Even from a distance, White Sheep's anxious suspense was clear, but the same went for Subaru and Beatrice. Even knowing they had all the necessary documents and dummy carriages in place—

"T-This might be bad... Beako! Into my pocket, right now!"

"You are panicking far too much, I suppose! No matter how minimally pretty Betty is, she will not fit, I suppose! Rather than that, act more dignified—"

Panicking at the possibility of the carriage being inspected, Subaru and Beatrice flustered with each other. Perhaps picking up on Subaru's chaotic energy, Russell happened to look back and offered a light wave of his hand to reassure everyone in the caravan. At that gesture, Subaru breathed a sigh of relief, realizing Russell had a backup plan—and immediately after:

—A massive tremor, the sound of shattering, and multiple screams echoed from the back of the waiting line.

△▼△▼△▼△

At first, Subaru almost mistook the shaking for the carriage setting off, the sound for the body creaking, and the voices for some kind of cheering—confusing it all together. That was how sudden and without warning it was.

"What's that?!"

Turning pale, Subaru scooped up Beatrice and jumped onto the carriage seat, looking out through the large window toward the rear of the caravan—where the commotion had come from. What he saw was a disrupted convoy, plumes of dust rising into the air, multiple figures bursting out from the smoke in utter panic, and a building that appeared half-crumbled inside the haze.

"Is that... an accident?"

"So it appears, I suppose. From the look of it, a dragon carriage breaking an axle or something triggered a chain-reaction collision, I suppose."

"And a building along the road got rammed into, huh? That's bad, looks like there'll be a lot of injured people."

As Russell had noted, the congestion caused by the capital's unrest was likely the root cause. Happening right in the middle of a massive traffic jam in the waiting line, pushing and shoving broke out everywhere. There was no telling how many were hurt, but someone needed to help get people out of the crash site immediately—

"—Natsuki-dono, permission has been granted. The carriage is moving. Please be seated."

Returning to the interior, Russell declared those words to the flustered Subaru and Beatrice. Hearing that, Subaru went to call out "Russell-san—" but paused.

"Eh...? What did you just say? The carriage is moving?"

"Yes. The personnel on duty have rushed off to respond to the accident behind us. Seeing as our paperwork has no flaws, we were told we may pass. It is also necessary to clear the blocked convoy line."

"No, but..."

"There are injured people, I suppose. You mentioned the cargo contained medical supplies, I suppose."

"A disguise. The cargo does not consist solely of what is listed in the manifest. Unpacking it will expose that as well. I believe I told you—always search for the optimal path."

There was no harshness in his expression or tone, yet the sharp edge of his words cut deep. Faced with such an all-too-soon test of putting those words into practice, Subaru stiffened his cheeks. —He understood Russell's argument. Even here, he understood, but—

"—White Sheep, explain it to Natsuki-dono."

"—Kh"

Sighing at Subaru's head-bowed, teeth-gritting silence, Russell called out behind him. Standing there was White Sheep, who had just peeked into the carriage. Gasping in her throat, she turned her moist, round eyes toward Russell. But Russell did not turn back to her, calling her name once more: "White Sheep." Hearing that, White Sheep boarded the carriage as if shaking off hesitation, placing her hand over Subaru's hand resting on the window frame—

"Subaru-san, please listen to him here. It's okay. The guards will properly handle it. —It'll be okay."

"...White Sheep-chan."

Obeying Russell as he made the ruthless decision, White Sheep appealed to him like that. For a moment, a fierce resistance threatened to surge up within Subaru, but the trembling of White Sheep's voice and hand conveyed loud and clear that ignoring this was far from what she truly wanted either. Desperate, earnest, yet still trying her absolute best to do what was right. —In that case, what was Subaru supposed to do here?

"—Subaru."

From within his arms, Beatrice called his name. In her eyes burned trust in Subaru and the light of resolve. No matter what choice he made, she would stay with him—it was the exact same shine as when she had promised in the sewers to follow him all the way to hell. Even if it meant sharing in a sin together, Beatrice would not waver.

"For now, from here..."

"Yes, precisely. A wise judgment, Natsuki-dono. That is the optimal choice for this moment."

Subaru slowly lowered his hand, which lay on the window frame beneath White Sheep's. Seeing Subaru move like that, Russell spoke as if relieved. Yes, that was right. What Russell said was correct. He should follow it here.

"――――"

An accident could perhaps be called a kind of godsend. The carriage was about to be inspected, and Subaru and the others were on the verge of being found. Now the guards' hands were diverted to rescue efforts, allowing them to escape this place.

—All to save the royal capital, to save the Kingdom.

Therefore—

"—Ah?"

Just as he tried to compromise with himself that backing away was the right choice, unable to tear his lingering gaze from the view outside the window, Subaru saw it.

—Fluttering the hem of a white robe, running straight toward the accident scene, was the figure of Blue Snake.

"――――"

—... —...... —......Are you an idiot, Natsuki Subaru?

"...Searching for the optimal path—that was what you said was important, right, Russell-san?"

"—? Indeed. Your judgment is correct. Let us depart at once. We—"

"Then!"

"—!?"

"Then my optimal choice is not abandoning anyone here I can save!!"

Declaring it at the top of his lungs, Subaru placed his lowered hand back on the window frame. And leaving a "Sorry!" to White Sheep, whose overlapping hand was shaken off as her eyes widened, and to Russell beyond her—

"Let's go, Beako! Lend me your power!"

"Abso-lutely and loud and clear, I suppose!"

Holding Beatrice tight, Subaru threw himself out of the dragon carriage window. Behind him, Russell called his name, but he didn't stop. He couldn't stop. An accident was a godsend? Ridiculous. An accident was a tragedy. It was absolutely no salvation.

"You're the one who's right! I was wrong!"

If he hadn't seen the back of Blue Snake running off, he wouldn't even have realized that. One hundred percent, Blue Snake was right. Looking at that retreating back, sheer shame surged within him.

How stupid are you, Natsuki Subaru? Why couldn't you understand, Natsuki Subaru? Even if there were people willing to throw their lives away, his own standard would never change.

—Nothing was ever going to change.

"What the?! Hey, it's dangerous here! Anyone who isn't a guard—"

"There are injured people, right?! I'm helping! We're pulling everyone out!"

Amid the swirling dust right in front of the accident scene, a guard covering his mouth—the older superior officer who had insisted on procedure earlier—gaped wide-eyed at Subaru as he ran up. A man appearing out of nowhere, holding a cute little girl in his arms. The guard's bewilderment was only natural. However, that bewilderment was no reason to stop them.

"We can use magic! It'll be useful! Let us help!"

"—Kh, we need all the hands we can get. Come!"

A moment of hesitation—that was a paper wall before the elder guard's commitment to saving human lives. Piercing right through it in a single breath, instructed by the guard, Subaru and Beatrice stepped into the accident site. In the space where dust billowed and moans of agony and children's cries echoed, it was, as confirmed from afar, a chain reaction started by a carriage with a broken axle, accompanied by the partial collapse of a building caught in the impact.

Those bleeding, and those lying limp and motionless. Agitated earth dragons stomping their feet, with rescue calls and angry shouts flying left and right, creating utter chaos. Witnessing the scene, Subaru let his gaze wander, wondering where to begin—

"—Over here! Help me over here!"

That voice was noticeably loud, cutting through the din of overlapping shouts and piercing Subaru's eardrums. Looking over, deep in the lingering dust, he saw the white robe—Blue Snake—crouched on the ground, holding hands over a victim trapped beneath rubble. Sprinting over to Blue Snake, Subaru saw the victim's condition—a severe wound where part of a broken beam had impaled their torso, spilling blood profusely. And then—

"Use Beatrice-chan's magic to make the beam lighter and pull it out! You can do it, right?!"

"Yeah, we can! We can, but why do you know that..."

"Ah, geez! You're so slow!"

Roughly snapping in irritation, the next moment, Blue Snake—who was applying healing magic to the severely injured person's wound—roughly pulled off their hood. Emerging from underneath was neck-length flaxen hair, and demi-human cat ears asserting their presence as they parted through it—

"—Ferris?"

Dumbfounded, Subaru's lips murmured that name. A familiar face he hadn't expected to be there—the person who had rushed faster than anyone to a dying life, Ferris the 'Blue', working desperately before his eyes.
