Arc 2 Chapter 31: "The Clown's Wail"

—Sickening.

"Oho, Ram-chi! Did you see that just now!? Hasn't my knife skill gotten crazy refined in just a single day!? Has my talent finally bloomed!?"

—Sickening. Sickening.

"Rem-rin, look, look! The skill to make such delicate work possible—at this very moment, a miracle truly resides at my fingertips! Appliqué!"

—Sickening. Sickening. Sickening.

"Emilia-tan, stop stirring up my heart every single time I meet you or catch a glimpse of you! Seriously, being this sinful is downright guilty-ing!"

—Sickening. Sickening. Sickening. Sickening. Sickening. Sickening. Sickening. Sickening. Sickening. Sickening. Sickening. Sickening. Sickening. Sickening. Sickening. Sickening.

Plastering on a smile, playing the goofy fool, keeping his tongue spinning light and fast at full speed as if freshly oiled. Tackling every assigned task with all his might, bravely challenging them without fear of failure, and wandering around in search of events the moment his hands were free. Mobilizing all his memories, digging through the four days he had repeated four times until they burned into his mind, carving himself into every possible event, no matter how trivial.

It had to be this way. He had to do this. He couldn't waste even a single second. Examining every potential possibility, simulating the success or failure of every necessary event.

Just think of it as a game. Thorough flag management. It was supposed to be his forte. The more times he met them, the higher the chances grew. He should be able to smile better. He should be able to make them laugh better.

Act like his head was empty, yet never stop thinking. Meaningless, wasteful, exaggerated actions. Make them see him as a fool unworthy of vigilance. Avoid being judged as too uselessly stupid, though. Conflicting thoughts and conclusions. If there was time to worry about contradictions, act before thinking. But before moving, question the meaning of his own actions. Always stay alert so nothing seemed unnatural. Don't let down your guard for even a fraction of a second, let alone a whole second. Failure was not an option—because it couldn't be.

—Can't fail can't fail can't fail can't fail can't fail can't fail can't fail can't fail can't fail can't fail can't fail can't fail can't fail can't fail can't fail can't fail can't fail can't fail.

Over and over, alarm bells kept ringing in his head. An alert signaling danger. Coming to another world, he hadn't made a shred of progress, but he took pride in the fact that this single sense of his had sharpened. Danger was approaching. An error was about to be corrected. He didn't know where it was coming from. Lightly, vigilantly, step and pivot his body.

"Whoops, Ram-chi, I'm not slacking off, alright? I'll carry out my work nice and tight, through and through. Senior sister, you could even kick back in your room, stay put, and enjoy a siesta for all I care!"

Dodge, dodge, evading the situation with flippancy and a superficial smile. Was he doing it right? Was he properly playing Natsuki Subaru? Had he avoided raising suspicion? Even if he pulled it off in front of Ram, if his true colors were exposed in front of Rem, the key person, everything would go up in smoke.

Feign Natsuki Subaru naturally and effortlessly. It was simple; it was about himself. All he had to do was return to when he was a lazy pig, completely oblivious to the inner thoughts of the people living in the manor, innocently, brazenly, impolitely, recklessly enjoying whatever was handed to him.

Knowing nothing, understanding nothing, noticing nothing, doing nothing. It was supposed to be his specialty. It was supposed to be all he was capable of doing. It should be easy. He should be able to do at least that much with a smile on his face.

Grinning stupidly, he walked along with a mask of a smile plastered on his face. He was inside the manor. There was no telling where or who he might run into. Know that there was no freedom in free time. Spend every blank moment recalling the past and making plans for future actions.

"O-Ugh, egh..."

A sudden surge of nausea. Only a groan leaked from the corner of his mouth, yet Subaru never let his smile break. Keeping that rhythm, his feet skipped, dancing and gliding as he slipped into a nearby guest room. Then, heading toward the washroom inside the room—

"...Bleh. Gag, ough...!"

He threw up into the sink, vomiting whatever remained in his already empty stomach. Ever since he put food or drink into his body, he had vomited every last bit back out. All that came out was yellowish gastric juice, and as it wrung him out so violently it felt like it was torturing his internal organs, even that gastric fluid had completely dried up.

Still, to satisfy the unyielding nausea, he gulped down water from the tap to fill his stomach, only to spew it right back out immediately after. Over and over again, it went on as if washing out the contents of his stomach.

"Haa... haa, haa..."

Roughly wiping his mouth with his sleeve, Subaru breathed heavily, his face pale. The crushing pressure felt enough to kill him on its own. If this time without a moment's rest continued like this, he felt like he might die of pure exhaustion alone.

He sneered at his own put-the-cart-before-the-horse condition, yet he couldn't even muster a dry smile. All that welled up inside him was pure anxiety and despair rising from his chest.

—Am I doing this properly?

Looking back, it seemed his relationship with the people of the manor had been at its best during the first loop, when he was completely ignorant. In that case, should he follow the first loop and try tracing the same circumstances?

No, doing that would be nothing more than tracing the second loop. The second loop had been tracing the contents of the first, only to have Rem's iron hammer added on top of the weakening Magic. It would be nothing other than taking a path that was already proven wrong.

If the third and fourth loops were out of the question, then the one Subaru needed to learn from was still the first. Changing his approach from the second time, aiming not to trace the first, but to do better than the first.

The first time, his competence at every job had been low, but in exchange, he had tackled his assigned work head-on. The second time, his attempt to mimic the results of the first time had been seen through, resulting in him being judged as slacking off. Then this time, just like the first time, he would give it his all—and on top of that, achieve even better results than before.

If he did that, neither Ram nor Rem would write him off. As long as he strayed from the route where they purged him, one of Subaru's concerns would be eliminated. But the problem was—

"I still don't have a damn clue where the Curse Arts User is."

Given that Rem had been killed, the culprit was likely an outsider. Someone targeting the manor—if it was someone related to the Royal Selection, then for Subaru, who wasn't familiar with those political webs, it was practically a dead end.

To expose the identity of the Curse Arts User, the cooperation of the manor's residents was absolutely indispensable. At the very least, raising an alarm wouldn't be useless. But then again, how much influence did current Subaru actually have in this manor?

"With zero trust gained, who's gonna listen to my advice...?"

On top of that, Subaru was bound by the restriction that he couldn't reveal his source of information. Hence, he had to accept the frustration and taking the long way around, enduring a suffocating tightness that felt like it would burn a hole in his stomach as he passed the time.

Not enough time. The lack of time was agonizing. He lacked the confidence to endure this painful time any longer. He wanted it to end quickly. He couldn't let it end. His words wouldn't reach them. Making them reach required time. There was no time. He had to do something. Sickening, painful.

His thoughts strayed into the same dead end they had reached countless times. Last night as well, he had been swallowed by this endless spiral and couldn't sleep a wink. The powerlessness of trying to fumble around and brush off an anxiety whose cause was perfectly clear, yet for which no solution could be found.

He wished he could just choose the option of endlessly screaming "It's dangerous!" Even if they thought Subaru had lost his mind, if that could save everyone's life in the manor, it wouldn't be a bad gamble.

Measures would be taken in advance, and everyone in the manor would reach the fifth day safely. Facing a worsening situation, the Curse Arts User would choose to retreat, and that would bring a happy ending.

—But he couldn't bear the thought of not being in that place himself.

Subaru was neither enlightened nor saintly enough to be satisfied with the detachment of "as long as they're saved safely, that's enough." Subaru's desire was for everyone in the manor to survive, and beyond that, to overcome the fifth day together in peace.

A spirit of self-sacrifice was something he had never possessed to begin with. Greedy, extravagant, stubborn, and vulgar to the extreme—that was his true nature. That was why—

"Ah, damn it... so uncool."

Even after steeling his resolve and choosing suicide back then, now that the chance was right in front of him, he couldn't stop shrinking back. Once picked back up, life was precious. If something you thought you had thrown away returned to your hands, you wouldn't easily think of letting go of it again.

Subaru was the exact same. A life he was supposed to have discarded, a life that was supposed to have ended.

Having won the gamble and returned, what struck Subaru as he realized it was back in his hands was the terror of losing it all over again.

He couldn't fail; there was no second chance. This was the fifth time. And Subaru did not have the kind of optimistic personality that could assume he would still be able to "Return by Death" after this. He had managed to return this time. Next time, it might not work. The mental attrition of being forced to constantly remain aware that the current him might be the last, that he was standing on the edge of a cliff—the saner a person was, the more it backed them into a corner.

He wasn't crazy enough to throw himself into desperation, nor brave enough to make the decision to risk everything and resist.

Thoroughly mediocre, thoroughly mundane. He was forced to realize what a small fry he was, to the point of despising himself.

"Do you really have time to be whining, you idiot...?"

If he had time to spill even a single complaint, it was more important to crack a joke and score points for impression. Shaking off the nausea that had faded before he realized it, Subaru slapped his stiff cheeks to spur himself on. Then, he headed out of the guest room.

Currently, it was free time after completing his assigned work, but even such time was precious right now. In any case, search for Ram or Rem—

"Found you at last."

Just as he leaned out from the door, a voice called out to him. Turning around, he saw Emilia catching her bouncing breath.

The moment her silver hair fluttering in the breeze entered his view, Subaru's consciousness switched gears with a loud click. Adapt to the situation, forget the stomach pain, the lump in his throat, and the feeling of entrapment. For now, turn back to Emilia and twist his cheeks into a lecherous grin.

"Oh my, oh my, Emilia-tan calling for me herself? How delightfully embarrassing and rare! Say anything, command anything! For your sake, I'd dive through fire, through water, even through the darkness of the Loot House!"

Pointing a finger to the heavens, swaying his hips, posing to a rhythm. Even he felt amazed at his own rapid shift in demeanour. However, facing him, Emilia's reaction was different from what he expected.

He had braced himself for an exasperated face or a sigh, but—

"Subaru..."

"Whoa whoa whoa, that's no good, Emilia-tan. Ignoring the killer joke I stayed up all night thinking of? Once used, a joke loses its freshness and can never be used again. Do you have any idea how it feels to have the child born from racking my brains murdered by Emilia-tan!?"

An action of biting his sleeve and crying in frustration. But Emilia's response to this was equally meager. Emilia betrayed every single reaction Subaru had predicted, merely gazing at Subaru with eyes that held a mixture of sorrow and heartache.

—Bad, his instincts screamed in alarm.

"What's wrong, Emilia-tan, falling silent like that? If a beauty like you keeps quiet, I might mistake you for a work of art, take you home, display you in the corner of my room, and give you good morning and good night kisses every single day!"

Something's wrong, his own inner voice screamed over and over in his brain.

A reaction unlike any he had anticipated. Neither exasperation nor anger—this reaction was completely unexpected. If she were angry, he could still figure out how to respond. But for her to look at him with such pained eyes meant...

Didn't it mean that the crude clown's mask he was wearing had been seen right through by her?

The moment that anxiety pierced him, Subaru remembered the presence of the gray cat that was always by her side. Recalling the traits of that cat who called himself a Spirit, the utter pointlessness of everything Subaru had been doing hit him hard.

The sheer foolishness of attempting to polish up a superficial facade in front of an entity that could read hearts.

The moment he was forced to realize this, Subaru's bravado crumbled. The smile plastered on his face vanished without a trace, replaced by a frail expression like a young child waiting to be scolded. The sheer embarrassment of continuing to dance in front of someone who saw through everything, believing they hadn't noticed. And above all, his tiny pride—the desire to keep it hidden from her, of all people—was painfully wounded.

A silence fell between the two of them. Subaru no longer had any leeway to crack a joke, and Emilia's eyes wavered as if searching for words to address Subaru's expression.

—To have Emilia disillusioned with him. That was the one thing he couldn't stand. Yet he didn't even know what to say to make an excuse. Even here, he lacked the means to break through the situation.

Attempting to open his mouth again and again, Subaru couldn't take a step forward because he couldn't find the crucial words. Watching Subaru burdened by such frustration, Emilia suddenly muttered a quiet "Alright," and—

"Subaru, come."

"...Huh?"

"Just do as I say."

Grabbing Subaru's arm with a tug, she headed right back into the guest room Subaru had just exited. Dragged back into the room he had only half stepped out of, Subaru floated question marks over his head, unable to grasp her intention. However, paying no heed to Subaru's confusion, Emilia placed her hands on her hips, looked around the room, and—

"Now then, sit down, Subaru."

She pointed at the floor, speaking in her unchanged silver-bell voice. Following her finger, he looked at the ground. The floor was covered with a carpet, and though no one was using the room, it was thoroughly cleaned. Of course, such effort was put into it that even lying on the bare floor would be fine, but...

"If I'm sitting, wouldn't a bed or chair be fine? Why go out of your way to sit on the floor..."

"Just sit!"

"Yes, as you command!"

Spoken to in an unusually firm tone, he instinctively obeyed by sitting upright in seiza. Seeing Subaru seated, Emilia nodded satisfiedly and stepped right beside him. Naturally, he ended up looking up at her from a low position, but right now, even holding wicked thoughts didn't cross his mind. He was merely desperate to read Emilia's true intentions.

"...Mm."

The one who muttered softly was Emilia. Breathing in as if to make sure or to convince herself, Emilia sat in seiza right next to Subaru. Flustered by her beautiful face being close enough to touch, Subaru stared intently at her fair profile to see if he could read any emotion. Suddenly, he saw her pale profile flush, her ears turning slightly red.

"This is... special, okay?"

"—Eh?"

Question marks floated in his mind at her admonishing words, but before he could utter a response, something pressed against the back of Subaru's head. Naturally, his body in seiza couldn't resist the force, leaning forward with the momentum—and was welcomed by a soft sensation.

"The position is a bit off. Plus, it's prickly."

Something squirmed under his head, and Emilia's shy voice could be heard right beside him. Raising his eyes in surprise, his eyes widened further as disbelief layered upon disbelief at the scene before him.

Right above him, so close that their faces could almost touch, was Emilia's face. It looked upside down, prompting a vaguely distant thought: Ah, I'm upside down.

At this distance, upside down, with a soft feeling beneath his head. —Those keywords gathered together, forming a shape and taking on meaning within Subaru. In other words, this was—

"A lap... pillow?"

"It's kind of embarrassing, so don't say it out loud. Also, looking this way is forbidden. Keep your eyes closed."

Tapped lightly on the forehead, his vision was blocked as her palm covered his eyelids. But Subaru removed her hand with his own and—

"Emilia-tan acting bashful is supreme too, but... what kind of situation is this in the first place? When did I accomplish something worthy of a reward?"

"That kind of strange bluffing isn't good, you know."

He was tapped on the forehead again. But this time, keeping her hand resting on his forehead, Emilia scooped up Subaru's inverted bangs with her fingers and—

"You said it before, didn't you, Subaru? That if you were exhausted, I should give you a lap pillow. So I'm giving you one. I can't do it all the time, but today is special."

"Special or not, it's only day two, you know? If I look completely exhausted and groggy already, then I must have an unrivaled weak constitution through all of history..."

"I can tell just by looking at you that you're crushed. You probably won't tell me the detailed circumstances, right? I don't think something like this will make things easy for you, but... this is the only thing I can do."

Cutting off Subaru's words, Emilia spoke with an affectionate gaze. Before he knew it, her fingers combing through his bangs were running through his hair, beginning to stroke his head gently as if soothing a young child.

Laughing softly, Subaru tried to brush off Emilia's action. He intended to keep maintaining the bravado he had resolved to keep up in front of her—to claim that she had it all wrong, that he wasn't doing anything so uncool.

"Haha... Emilia-tan, really... I'm..."

And yet, his voice squeaked, his throat choked up, and the next words wouldn't come out. For some reason, he couldn't detach his awareness from the soft feel of her fingers stroking his head.

"Are you tired?"

"I-I can still keep going. I'm totally fine..."

"Are you in trouble?"

"If you're nice to me, see, I'll fall for you... Doing that, again... like that... haha."

To her brief questions, Subaru's responding words sounded hollow. Such emotions that even he knew were empty words.

And Emilia softly leaned her face closer to such a Subaru, and—

"—It must have been so hard, hasn't it?"

"――――!"

It was said with compassion. It was said with care. It was said with deep affection. With just that, with just those words, the tattered dam inside Subaru collapsed. Breaking, bursting, everything he had bottled up spouted outward all at once. It was a collection of intense passions that he had intended to seal away, yet hadn't been able to erase even a fragment of.

"It was... hard. It was damn painful. It was damn terrifying. It was ridiculously sad. It hurt so much I thought I was gonna die...!"

"Mm."

"I worked hard! I was working so hard! I was desperate! Desperately trying to make everything, all of it better...! It's true! Really, truly, I've never worked this hard in my entire life!"

"Mm, I know."

"Because I loved it... this place... because I thought it was important to me... this place...! That's why I was desperate to get it back! I was scared! I was so damn scared! What if... what if they looked at me with those eyes again... thinking that way made me hate myself so, so much...!"

He couldn't control his emotions. Once exploded, they overflowed like a burst dam, grandly staining the tears onto the face of the coward wearing a smiling mask.

Tears wouldn't stop. Snot trickled down. Indescribable fluid overflowed inside his mouth, turning Subaru's sob-filled crying voice into something even more unbearable to hear.

Pathos and disgrace. A grown man wailing on a girl's lap while having his head stroked. So pathetic he wanted to die. So filled with warmth that he thought he might die from it.

Emilia's responses as she listened to Subaru's weeping were gentle. Even if she said "I know," it was perfectly obvious that not a single fragment of Subaru's experience had reached her. And yet, Emilia's voice carried a weight that couldn't be laughed off.

He didn't know why. Maybe he just wanted to believe that. However, it was an undeniable fact that Subaru felt as though he had been saved by that inexplicable warmth.

Shedding tears in torrents, Subaru continued to cry on Emilia's lap. Crying, crying, wailing, until before long, his pathetic weeping faded into the distance, leaving only quiet sleeping breaths falling over the guest room.

※※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※

—It was when Emilia was gently stroking sleeping Subaru's black hair that Rem visited the guest room.

Opening the door without a sound, Rem was about to open her mouth upon spotting Emilia inside—

"Shh."

—and closed her mouth at Emilia's gesture of placing a finger against her lips. Swaying her short blue hair, Rem surveyed the inside of the room casually. Then, stepping up beside Emilia, who was sitting directly on the guest room floor with Subaru's head resting on her lap—

"Is Subaru-kun merely sleeping?"

"Yes. Hee-hee, look, he's like a child, isn't he? When I stroke his head, he gets a face like he's relieved."

Stroking Subaru with amusement, Emilia sought Rem's agreement. Rem responded to her request with a quiet shake of her head—

"It seems Subaru-kun will not be capable of any more work today."

"That's right, today is a day off. Taking a break just two days after starting work—he's a veeery bad boy. Once he gets better, make sure to punish him."

Laughing softly, Emilia returned to the task of playing with Subaru's face. She apparently had no intention of moving the sleeping Subaru to free her legs. Interpreting Emilia's stance as such, Rem gazed down quietly at Subaru.

In his innocent, sleeping face, both tension and frivolity had vanished. For some reason, whenever he interacted with her sister and Rem, even before putting on a smile, a subtle tension would fill him. Feeling something deep behind that attitude, Rem had been thinking of stepping up her surveillance on him.

"Seeing him sleep like this, even that desire fades."

Following Emilia's example, Rem parted his bangs lightly with her fingers and murmured. Defenseless as a baby. Looking as though he were utterly ignorant of the world, that sight was almost pitiful, causing Rem to relax her lips just a fraction.

"Rem will go inform Sister that Subaru-kun is useless today. The division of labor must be reworked."

Bowing, Rem spoke politely before turning her back. Her destination was where her sister was. Closing her eyes, she could instantly tell where that location was. Head straight there, and carry out the exchange just as she had stated. From behind her—

"Rem."

Stopping her steps at the call, Rem turned around completely. Her line of sight was at a vastly different height from Emilia sitting on the bare floor. Yet even so, Rem felt a mysterious pressure as if she were being looked down upon by Emilia.

Unaware of Rem's subtle surprise, Emilia spoke softly—

"—Subaru is a good boy, you know?"

At those words, Rem responded with a deep bow. Then, without leaving even a single glance behind, she headed to the door and left the guest room, leaving the two behind.

Walking down the corridor, she chewed over Emilia's words. Even Rem herself didn't notice the subtle tremor that ran across her expressionless profile.

—Only the faint lingering scent of evil left a tiny lump of doubt in Rem's heart.
