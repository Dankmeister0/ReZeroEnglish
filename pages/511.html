This story is an April Fools' Day project.

First, please read School Re:Zero! 1st Period!, School Re:Zero! 2nd Period!, and School Re:Zero! 3rd Period! before reading this.

This is Re:Zero transplanted into a modern high school setting as a complete IF scenario.

Although it uses a modern setting, character names, hair colors, and the like are accepted naturally without any sense of discomfort.

Because it heavily follows galgame tropes, it may rub some people the wrong way.

However, no one becomes unhappy.

Bearing the above in mind, if you have a sense of humor and can forgive the characters being happy, please read on with your understanding.
