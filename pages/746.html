Arc 9 Chapter 50: "Decisive Battle on Ice"

The six-colored light that had pierced through heaven and earth slowly unraveled and vanished.

The sky, which had faded to ashen white and forgotten its true color, gradually regained its hues, and before long the blue firmament began to clear to a pale transparency, as though it had remembered why it was called blue in the first place.

"——"

Before that scene, Roswaal saw tears spill from the eyes of Ezzo beside him. Whether from joy or from frustration, it was a sight that would move anyone to tears regardless. Even though it had taken the two of them together, Roswaal and Ezzo had accomplished a feat never before recorded in the history of magic.

Extreme magical control divided between four colors and two, a flawless construction of spellwork that permitted not a hair's breadth of error, and an ultimate harmony that would have failed to hold with even an instant's discrepancy — that was what had surpassed the dragon-glow of the "Divine Dragon." A hyper-spell that could never have been realized without both Roswaal's years of refinement and Ezzo's spirit of inquiry — either one missing, and it would not have come to be. And yet——

"Someday, I will construct this incantation by myself, alone...!"

Roswaal laughed at the sight of Ezzo declaring this so boldly even as endless tears streamed down his face. It was not exasperation, nor was it mockery. No — that, precisely, was what a mage was. Imagination is what matters in magic. Whatever you can imagine, magic can achieve.

"Isn't that right, professor?"

Muttering this, Roswaal's body tilted and began to collapse toward the ground. Ezzo reached out on reflex to catch him, but sadly, being far more exhausted himself, he toppled first. Roswaal, still falling, was about to crush Ezzo beneath him when——

"——I made it in time. Good news."

Just in time, that flatly composed voice and a pair of powerful arms saved both Roswaal from his fall and Ezzo from being crushed by it.

"——"

Roswaal exhaled, looking at Clind, who had caught him. The all-purpose steward who, during the battle, had flown off into the far heavens and even returned after shattering a star. His face now wore its monocle once more, seemingly restored without anyone noticing, and the black horn atop his head had retracted — his usual, unremarkable state. Though it seemed even he had not managed to restore his everyday impeccable grooming, and the rare sight of his rumpled clothes remained.

"Even so, coming out of that with nothing but your butler's uniform worse for wear — dragonkin really are unfair, aren't they-e-eh."

"Milord, if I may be so insolent as to offer a correction. Correction. It is not because I am Dragonkin that this is so — it is only because I, Dragonkin of the 'Divine Dragon,' am something special. Affirmed."

"...You're bragging, aren't you? And here I am in this state because of your vessel."

"Do not point at me for that! My hips— no, my entire body has gone slack, that's all, and I cannot stand!"

Still lying face-down where he had fallen, unable to rise, Ezzo grumbled his sore-loser's excuse. Clind shrugged as if to voice his displeasure at that — really, what a character.

"You've changed. You used to be more the detached, transcendent-species type, I thought."

"In that case, allow me to return those very words to you. Returned. — Weren't you, milord, supposed to devote yourself entirely to being a clever, cowardly, mediocre mage? Confirmed."

Clind narrowed his eyes behind his monocle, and struck precisely at what Roswaal had been thinking. Certainly, that had been his intention. And yet, Roswaal had been unable to do so. Unable to, and in the end he had risen and done something reckless beyond even his own imagining. But that was because——

"I just... realized something, that's all. That through all these days, I hadn't been walking alone-e-eh."

"——"

At Roswaal's answer, Clind fell silent. For some reason, so did Ezzo.

"'For some reason' is laying it on a bit thick, isn't it-t-teh."

Of course Clind, with his long years of acquaintance, would know. And as for Ezzo — during the activation of that hyper-spell just now, their consciousnesses had come to the very brink of merging into one. Whatever feelings, whatever prayers had been within Roswaal's heart at that time — there was no hiding them from him.

"Speaking of which, I'm used to this business of consciousnesses mixing together, so I understand why I'm fine, but why is it you're all right, Ezzo-kun? Normally one's mind would break, wouldn't it-t-teh?"

"Don't say something so terrifying so casually! ...It's probably thanks to the 'Book of the Dead.'"

"The 'Book of the Dead' from the Pleiades Watchtower?"

"That's right. While I was waiting at the Watchtower, I read more than ten 'Books of the Dead,' and what that required was mastering a technique for emptying the mind..."

"I see, so this is what a mage is. Noted."

Roswaal felt he'd somehow been handed a rather rude conclusion by Clind, but he wouldn't pursue it here. He didn't particularly think what Ezzo had done was strange, in any case. In any event, what mattered now was——

"Milord, we have indeed carried out the duty entrusted to us. Completed. However..."

"Ah, I know, I know-o-oh."

Taking the words out of Clind's mouth partway through, Roswaal quietly let out a breath and looked into the distance. Clind, and Ezzo down on the ground, both turned their eyes the same direction — beyond the horizon. There, in the rocky wasteland whose very landscape had been utterly transformed by the battle with the "Divine Dragon," and further beyond that still, lay the final destination of Aldebaran's group——

"——The Grand Mogolade Geyser."

What crossed Roswaal's mind as he murmured this was the final exchange between the demon and the dragon, an exchange that had torn heaven and earth asunder. The "Divine Dragon," having watched off the Dragonkin who had ascended to the heavens to intercept the falling star, had weighed on its scales two choices: eliminating the two mages who were forging the hyper-spell, or fulfilling its own duty.

There had not been a shred of the arrogance of certain victory in the "Divine Dragon" in that instant.

And so the "Dragon," following the convictions of the personality dwelling within its Dragonhusk, chose one of the two — and with its released breath, took aim at a colossal hole in the earth, far from the battlefield.

Why Aldebaran's group was headed for that place remained unknown. But it was impossible that the "Divine Dragon's" action had nothing to do with that group's purpose, and the "Divine Dragon," resolved even to being slain itself, had prioritized striking that hole with its breath.

What meaning that would bring was surely soon to be proven. All that remained was——

"——whether what you've cultivated can surpass your opponent, Subaru-kun."

——Irregular.

That, Rem told herself, was the role expected of her, the duty she was meant to fulfill. Al, who had broken through every obstacle standing in the way up to now using the outlandish Authority of "Time Regression" — what was needed to stop him was precisely that: an irregular.

"Sure, that helmet bastard's Authority is a terrifying thing. As far as I know, I've rarely come across a power so beyond reason. — But no matter how powerful, it's still a human using it, nothing more."

"Pile on the endless unexpected, make that helmet bastard keep using his Authority. The time he can regress has its limits. Layer it on, layer it on, layer it on till it breaks."

"Once you whittle down his reserves, even that helmet bastard will make an irrecoverable slip. — That's the trick to seizing a chance at victory against him."

So had gone the proposal from old man Rom, who had once driven Al to within a single step of defeat. Against Rom's proposed strategy, none of the tactically astute among them — Ram, Roswaal, Clind — had raised an objection. And so Rem believed in that proposal wholeheartedly. What must be hurled at Al was the unexpected, piled high enough to make even him throw up his hands. And if what was called for was a war of attrition, then Rem was hardly one to be dismissed lightly either. After all——

"——I've watched that man up close."

If asked what her greatest chance of victory was, that was it. Perhaps fortunately, Rem had, until quite recently, been forgotten by everyone. During the time she had no memories, her points of contact with Al in the Empire could hardly be called many. Before that — if one were to speak of her contact with him regarding the Royal Selection — even less so. But precisely because of that, Rem's actions had a chance of running counter to his expectations. However——

"Yaaaaah!!"

Roaring, putting her whole body into it, the iron ball released from Rem's swung arm shot fiercely through the sky a beat later. Straight and true, the spiked iron ball's target was the red-haired Shinobi, Yae Tenzen — on the stage of the Agzadd Canyon, some five thousand meters in total height, the battle continuing as they fell had shifted to its next stage against this astonishing Shinobi.

A disc of ice conjured in midair, and a contest of raw strength using it as footing. Even that had been the result of Rem and Emilia working together to devise a way to force the irregular upon their foe, though there was something frustrating about having been driven into that situation in the first place. It wasn't only Al and his group who'd had an irregular thrust upon them. This side had too.

"——"

With a rumbling roar, the blow that stifled the wind even as it let out its own rising howl was heavy and sharp. The spiked mass of iron — no craft, however sharp its edge, could deflect it merely by bundling thin threads together. That threat surged forth all at once, aimed at Yae the irregular.

"——Kh"

A small catch in her throat at the approaching threat, Yae abandoned interception and cartwheeled aside. The iron ball passed right beside her as she rolled across the ice disc, and shot through empty air——

"There we go!"

A sharp shout that somehow sounded carefree met the iron ball with an impact. It was Emilia, on the far side of Yae, kicking the iron ball hurtling toward her away with a foot clad in an ice boot — the satisfying crack of it. Emilia's long, slender leg swung around, sending the iron ball bouncing back by sheer force — chasing after Yae's slender frame as she fled in her cartwheel. The iron ball bit mercilessly into Yae's side without mercy——

"That won't be happening."

The instant she said this, the surface of the ice disc Yae had planted her hand on while cartwheeling was peeled away by steel thread to cover her, becoming a shield that caught the iron ball chasing her body. Of course, it was too thin a shield to stop the blow entirely. In half a second it cracked, in half of that it splintered, in half of that again the spike thrust through to the other side, and in exactly one second it was punched clean through. But for a nimble Shinobi, that one second was more than enough to make her next move.

"What!?"

It was Emilia who let out an involuntary shriek. But Rem felt the same. In the two women's field of view, Yae, having escaped the follow-up with her improvised ice shield, gave a light leap and set her bare feet on the fully extended chain of the Morningstar, running along it in an instant. Emilia's eyes went wide at the acrobatics and balance, and Rem's at, on top of that, a gait across the chain that betrayed not the slightest sense of weight — but the surprises kept coming. In time with the motion of Yae's swinging arm, blades of ice and stone rained down one after another from overhead toward the two of them.

"This is——"

Twenty seconds ago, weapons of ice and stone that Emilia and Al had conjured in midair while fighting, and simply left there. Yae had pulled them down with steel thread stretched to the sky, recycling them as a dangerous rain.

"Because I didn't clean up properly!" "Al-san didn't clean up either!" "Having a master who's bad at tidying up is hard on everyone around them, isn't it?"

Reflection, backup, and provocation overlapped, and Rem and Emilia found themselves scrambling to deal with the falling blades. Emilia batted them away with her twin ice swords in both hands, but Rem was in trouble. Her right hand was occupied by the Morningstar, and that Morningstar was, at this very moment, being used as Yae's footing — she couldn't carelessly yank it back. She would have to block with nothing but her bare left hand.

"In time——"

—she wouldn't make it, she braced for impact, in that instant. Someone shoved Rem's shoulder aside, and that someone who had shoved her was run through the chest by a falling stone blade.

"——"

Rem's eyes went wide at that act of self-sacrifice, and deepened further still when she saw who had done it. It was a heroic figure — run through the chest, and still gamely giving Rem a thumbs-up——

"——Subaru-kun!"

Before Rem's unwittingly shouting eyes, what had been run through the chest was an ice doll made in the likeness of Natsuki Subaru. The absurdly finely crafted ice Natsuki Subaru had protected Rem, and, wearing a valiant smile just like the real thing, collapsed.

"Ehh? Was that... don't tell me, Emilia-sama?"

"Yes, that's right. — And that's not all, because Subaru isn't alone!"

The instant she answered Yae's scowl, six more figures leapt out from behind Emilia. Every one of them an ice statue identical in appearance to the Natsuki Subaru who had just fallen. All of them, as if each had a will of its own, drew with distinct flair the weapons that had been knocked loose and stuck into the ice disc, and leapt as one toward Yae running along the chain. If even one of those icy fingertips landed, it would be a foothold to corner the swift Yae——

"Unfortunately, multi-opponent battles are my specialty."

In that instant, twenty dancing strands of steel thread bound the leaping ice Natsuki Subarus. The threads caught around necks, torsos, limbs were drawn taut, and the six bodies were mercilessly torn to pieces.

"Subaru——!!" "Subaru-kun!!"

Rem and Emilia's screams overlapped at the pitiful wreckage the ice Natsuki Subarus had become. Beyond the scattering shards of the shattered ice statues, still running along the chain, Yae bore down on Rem. In a split-second decision, Rem let go of the Morningstar's handle, stripping away Yae's footing. About to drive a sharply twisting back kick into the Shinobi who would surely be scrambling in midair——

"I'd really rather not take a direct hit from an oni's kick, if it's all the same."

As she said this, Yae's eyes caught the edge of her buckling knees adjusting to the slack in the chain. Lowering her stance, Yae kept her balance in midair without fail, and a lethal thread released from her fingertips looped like a noose around Rem's neck. Her head about to fly, or be strangled unconscious — an instant before that, the chain at Yae's feet snapped taut, overturning her expectations.

"Wha—"

Yae's eyes went wide, and it was no small feat that she instantly flicked the chain aside with her toes and leapt clear on reflex. Her eyes caught precisely what had pulled the Morningstar's chain in Rem's place. It was the ice Natsuki Subaru, the first to have fallen, run through the chest by the stone sword. — Even having fallen, the ice Natsuki Subaru who had refused to give up on the fight had saved Rem. Truly, this person — always, always doing something reckless for someone else's sake——

"He's not even the real thing, you know!"

"Even so, it counts!"

Into the center of Yae's twisted lips, Rem's full-strength back kick drove home, straight and true.

——Irregular.

Stopping that was the role expected of her, the duty she was meant to fulfill — so Yae told herself. Within Yae, her assessment of Al as a capricious and terrifying monster would not be overturned. — But that which would wear him down, that would load him with burden, that was the irregular, piled up again and again.

"Well, sure? Even I've gone up against people stronger than me plenty of times and nearly died, you know? Man, the world's just crawling with monsters, it's enough to make you sick. — But in the end, I'm the one still standing, so I guess there's no such thing as someone you can't kill."

"When both sides are at their absolute best, there's a gap that just can't be closed, y'know. That's why we do our fiddly little work, to close that gap — that's the cowardly trick we polish bit by bit."

"Strip away the other guy's absolute best, and a chance to slit the throat of someone above you comes around eventually. — That's our bloody way of getting by in this world."

So went the counsel of the village elder, the "Vile Old Man," who had drilled into Yae the Shinobi's way of life and craft. Yae had no objection to the elder's thinking. But as it happened, the chances to test it had not been especially many. After all, most opponents were weaker than Yae. She didn't even lose in a straight fight. Her first defeat had come at Al's hands, and it had twisted the very shape of Yae's being. Now, this person whose mere presence made her whole body shudder with terror — she wanted him gone, even a second sooner. For that, Al had to fulfill his wish, his prayer. So——

"——I don't want anything that would corner that person to get close to him."

Twisted, bent, thoroughly warped. Yae was aware of it herself. But it couldn't be helped. Yae liked spicy food and disliked sweets. But that was an inborn temperament, a fate she couldn't change herself. Having been born this way, she would carry through living this way.

Yae had her own ideal ending for how things between her and Al should conclude. And to achieve that, removing the irregular was Yae's particular twist of devotion. However——

"——Kh"

Struck directly by the kick of Rem, the horned girl who had been so named, Yae suppressed a groan deep in her creaking body, dispersing the received impact through her limbs via body control. It was a Shinobi technique that transmitted will even into muscle and organ — a survival art. But even with that, it wasn't as if she took no damage at all. The tips of her hands and feet went numb.

It wasn't that she had underestimated the power of the Oni Tribe. But for Yae, who could roughly grasp an opponent's strength just by watching their stance and how they moved their limbs, the Oni's racial trait of absorbing surrounding mana through their horn to explosively boost their physical capability had exceeded her expectations. On top of that——

"Emilia-sama's improvisation is too troublesome, isn't it~"

Enduring the bitter feeling, Yae murmured while keeping a composed expression. In truth it had hurt quite a bit, but she couldn't let that show. A Shinobi never lets the opponent gather information. If information is to be gathered, let it be false information meant to mislead. With that much impact felt through her foot, Rem, of course, and Emilia too, having watched, would assume Yae had taken considerable damage. And if, despite that, Yae remained unfazed, it was only human nature to hesitate before following up.

"Emilia-sama!" "Yes! Keep going!"

However, that only held if the opponent were a proper, seasoned combatant. After landing one blow, paying no mind to the composed expression on this side, the two shadows closed in relentlessly on the sent-flying Yae. It wasn't thoughtless, she wouldn't say that. They were desperate, doing their utmost. That, right now, was the most troublesome thing of all.

"In that case, I'll answer with dirty hands and dirty feet!"

Countering, Yae spread the toes of both feet wide, and together with both hands manipulated twenty strands of steel thread. The several blades of ice and stone planted in the ice disc that served as their footing — she caught them up in thread and set them dancing, meeting head-on Rem and Emilia's stance of pursuit.

In time with her deft finger-work, blades whirling and cutting the wind, and invisible, silent steel thread became a double attack of heavy and light, and the rending winds that arose from the margin of error in response ran wild across the ice. Chase the blades with your eyes, and your limbs get caught by the steel thread and lose combat strength. Al had ordered her not to kill, but if it came to slicing off one or two fingers, or all four limbs, so long as life itself was preserved, the demand could be considered fulfilled. With that thought, Yae let killing intent surge forth. However——

"Haaaaaah!!" "Yah! Tei! Taah!"

Rem's roar and Emilia's ferocious defense swept aside every one of Yae's attacks. A combo play — Rem handling the blades, Emilia the steel thread. Rem wound the chain of her beloved iron ball short around her arm as an improvised gauntlet, and Emilia reshaped her twin swords into a massive halberd, and without exchanging a word, their sure combination completely blocked this side's attack.

"How did you..."

Blocking visible blades takes only capability, but blocking invisible steel thread requires a reason. Doubting that reason, Yae caught in her vision her own murmur clouding faintly white, and realized. — Beyond the cold that pierced her skin more than even the super-high-altitude fall and the vast ice surface.

"—kh, ice fog."

Minute shards of ice flickered and danced across her vision, their glimmer tracing the path of the steel thread. Yae felt quiet admiration at the method Emilia had used to spot the invisible steel thread, and at the sense that had made it possible. And also at the possibility that this could become an even worse situation.

"——Icicle Line."

The instant the woven incantation was complete, the air began to let out a high, shrill scream. The ice particles composing the ice fog linked with their neighbors, and the cold that dyed the world white bared its fangs all the more savagely. The first prey to fall to it was the steel thread, inferior in mass. The advantage of the steel thread, whose lightness and thinness were its greatest feature, was crushed by the freezing world. Steel thread could cut through wind, but it could not cut through a blizzard. — And so, the decision came at once.

Yae kissed the ring on her finger, and in an instant, flame ran along the steel thread, birthing an explosive blaze upon the ice. A heat wave that pushed back even the freezing rending wind — it gave up the steel thread's advantage of being "invisible," but in exchange, clad it with power that would not lose to the cold even within the ice fog.

"Please keep me company for my Flame Dance!"

Whipping the flame-wreathed steel thread wild, unleashing scorching threaded strikes, the dance of the "Crimson Sakura" was performed. Against that blazing flame dance lighting up the canyon, two figures trailing crisp, clear ice and snow — no, together with seven ice statues, nine shadows came leaping in.

"I'll take you up on that invitation!" "Sorry if I step on your feet!"

On the ice, into the range of Yae, who spun a whirlwind of raging flame, Emilia slid in with her ice twin swords in hand. She glided across the ice sheet on bladed ice boots, drawing a circular arc, and in time with this side's flame dance, launched a loosely curving yet sharp, never-careless attack. Alongside Emilia's supremely modulated assault, Rem's choice — charging in scrummed together with the ice statues — was straight and overwhelming——

"Aaaaaaaahhh——!!"

Roaring like a beast, arms flung skyward, Rem rained down blow after blow of her white fists. A hail of blows, a barrage worthy of the name, poured down like a storm, and Yae twisted, crouched, arched back, straddled and ducked in repetition, dodging by the barest margin with the coordination of a dance.

"With bare fists alone, you'll never catch me, not for all eternity."

Even with an Oni's physical prowess, if limbs extend from a torso, the flow of force traveling through muscle is plain to see, and Yae's eyes didn't miss it. Rem's Oni-Tribe strength was astonishing, but it didn't surpass Yae's reaction speed the way the "Sword Devil" had. By that same logic, the seven ice statues weaving in feints between Emilia and Rem's attacks with anatomically inexplicable movements were the greater irregular, the one that betrayed Yae's expectations. At this rate, true to her declaration, Yae could keep facing the two of them forever——

"——No."

Yae, on the verge of drifting toward the easy choice, put a stop to herself. This battlefield was chosen by her opponent. Letting the opponent do as they pleased played right into their hands. To stop an irregular, she too would have to become an irregular to her opponent. For the time being——

"This footing——"

The ice disc that Emilia and Rem had cooperated to create, dividing Yae and Al. Its clarity marred by the wind it took, Al's figure beyond the whitened, clouded ice sheet was hazy. Judging this footing unfavorable, Yae parried Rem's flurry of blows, slipped past Emilia's attack from behind, and used one of the tackling ice statues as a springboard to leap into the air. Then, sending steel thread from both hands and feet flying in all directions across the disc, she poured her full strength into twisting her entire body. The result——

"I'm changing the situation. Was this within your calculations too?"

In time with Yae's full-body exertion, the battlefield that was the ice disc changed — the disc began spinning like a top, and tremendous centrifugal force bore down on everyone on the ice. The battle shifted to its next stage.

——"Irregular."

That meant something one hadn't even imagined, and piling that up was their weapon now — so Emilia roused herself. Suddenly startled, Emilia couldn't immediately begin her next action or thought. The same could be said of Al. Al surely had thought a great many things through in planning all of this, but he couldn't read all the way into what was inside Emilia and the others' heads. That was what the "irregular" was for.

"When Lia's in a pinch, she immediately tries to power through everything with a big push, right? It's only when that doesn't work that she starts to wonder what to do for the first time... but I don't think stopping there suits Lia, and I don't think it's what she's cut out for."

"My cute daughter has the power to shove aside any problem with a bang. So instead of trying just once, why not keep piling it on, again and again, and give it your best?"

"It's okay, when the time comes, Lia's body will naturally tell her what she needs to do. Lia's my proud daughter, carrying on the strongest bloodline there is."

Yes, something Puck had once told her gently revived itself in Emilia's mind. These days, it was always Subaru's words she remembered as her source of reassurance, but now, knowing Subaru himself was in danger, it was Puck's teaching that came first to encourage Emilia. The attack she and Rem had launched on Al together was struggling, interrupted by Yae cutting in — they had already passed two more marks carved into the cliff face, and there was only about thirty seconds left before they reached the bottom. There was a great deal of anxiety in her. But she mustn't let it beat her down. It wasn't something she was especially good at, but she needed to push this feeling onto Al and the others. To stop Al — no, to properly talk with Al, for real this time. Because——

"——There's no way Priscilla would want something like this."

Emilia understood that saying this was a cruel thing to do to Al. Even now, thinking of Priscilla, saying her name brought an ache to her chest and a prickle at the corner of her eyes — that feeling was something Emilia had too. She wanted to make Al let it all out. Before, Emilia had once been saved by Al. Setting that aside, Emilia wanted to be of help to Al. Not just because they shared the same pain — not only that, but as an instinctive sense apart from it, Emilia wanted to save him. So——

"——Kh, Subaru!"

Fiercely, a heat-laden wind coiled and swirled, and the ice disc, with Yae at its center, began to spin. Atop the disc, which had begun rotating with the vigor of a spinning plate, the ice soldiers modeled after Subaru, unable to withstand the added centrifugal force, went flying without any way to hold their footing. They were helplessly flung off the disc, or, worse, met their end smashed against the canyon's rock walls. But before that happened, Emilia didn't forget what they'd accomplished — grabbing her hand when the rotation nearly swept her off her feet, preventing her fall, saving her.

"Rem is——"

Gliding across the spinning disc on ice skates, Emilia searched her vision for Rem, who should have been driven into the same predicament. Surely she hadn't been flung off like the ice soldiers— worried at that thought, before Emilia a roar and a particularly large burst of flame rose up.

The collision occurred at the disc's center, where Rem and Yae clashed, and flame billowed up. Rem, having taken back into hand the iron ball she'd thrown skyward, swung it down, and Yae skillfully deflected that downward blow using the tension of her bundled threads. As a result, Rem's large swing was halted, and Yae's toe drove straight into her.

"Kah"

Letting out a pained groan, Rem, knocked back, rolled across the ice disc, sliding toward the outer edge. With the disc's rotation and Yae's leg strength combined, there was no way to stop Rem's sliding body. Realizing this was bad, Emilia considered leaping toward her——

"——No looking away allowed."

The instant she kicked off the ice, Yae's harrying attack came flying straight at her like a thrust. A blow from the stone dragon-blade bound in burning steel thread, and a follow-up threaded strike arriving after it — a two-stage attack demanding two stages of evasion across a wide range. Emilia hesitated — tried to — but abandoned that in favor of remembering the words of her two reliable allies, Subaru and Puck.

Trusting sense over thought, Emilia leapt toward the knocked-away Rem. — Literally, she flew toward Rem, wings beating.

"Wha—"

Yae froze, eyes wide, dumbfounded by the astonishing sight. It was the first opening she'd shown, utterly defenseless — but unfortunately, Emilia had no room in her mind at that moment to turn her attention there. Simply, straight and true, she dove toward Rem, scooped her up just as she was about to be flung off the disc, and rose.

——On her back, wings made of ice, borne by Emilia.

"Ah, thank you very much, Emilia-sama... these wings?"

"Subaru drew them for me once. Wings growing from your back, he said they're called an angel's. He mentioned it sometimes, and when I tried it, it worked!"

Answering the scooped-up Rem's question, Emilia's cheeks relaxed. Though of course, this was only an imitation, wings resembling the shape but nothing more. Unable to flap like a real bird's, gliding on the wind was the most she could manage. Even that was a very rare success of magic, one that only worked under circumstances this special. If she showed Subaru, he'd probably be delighted, but for that, he'd have to fall about five thousand meters together with her the same way. For that very reason too——

"Emilia-sama! Let's strike back!"

"Right!"

In Emilia's arms as she spread her ice wings, she nodded back to Rem's valiant voice. What crossed her mind at the words "strike back" — she believed, on instinct, that it was the same thing Rem was thinking, and together with her, Emilia dropped altitude straight toward the ice disc. There, releasing the wings, Emilia and Rem, cast into the air, spun midair — and simultaneously, the two brought their heels down together, sending the horizontally spinning ice disc into a violent vertical spin as well.

"——"

In retaliation for having the disc spun horizontally, they spun it vertically. Very simple, but an outlandish counterattack that couldn't have been managed alone — it must have taken even Yae, still atop the disc, completely by surprise. The footing's top and bottom flipped, then righted, only to flip again immediately, spin upright, flip again and right again, over and over, tossing everyone about. On top of that——

"The horizontal spin's still there too!"

As a result, the ice disc, spinning wildly in every direction, became a battlefield of pure randomness, tracing not a single predictable trajectory. It was just like the rickety, unsteady "attraction" that Subaru and Garfiel had built behind Roswaal Manor. And it was precisely because she thought of it that way that Emilia was able to run through the ice disc's "attraction."

"Yah! Ta! To... soya!"

Changing the direction of the ferocious load bearing down on her every fraction of a second, Emilia relied on nothing but the feel of her boot soles, accepting it and using the momentum to leap forward. She managed to run, relieved — though worried, on the other hand, whether Rem was alright.

"There's no need to worry about me!"

Answering thus, Rem drove her iron ball into the ice disc, and using it as a pivot, traced the chain to pull herself in one motion toward the center. Her momentum fell short of Emilia's, but in terms of certainty, it was far more stable. If anything, it was Emilia who needed to be careful not to let her feet slip.

"That girl is..."

Amid the disc's disordered vertical and horizontal spin, above or below it, striving desperately not to be thrown off, Emilia blinked her violet eyes searching for the figure of Yae, whom she needed to pursue. There she was. Yae remained, as ever, right at the center where the disc's centrifugal force, both vertical and horizontal, was weakest. Even so, she should still be getting tossed about all the same — Emilia, drawing back her leg to extend it, realized.

"——"

Yae, crouched down on the ice with her knees folded, was not being thrown about by the footing's spin. The reason was plain — she had hollowed out the center of the ice disc with steel thread, creating a safe zone unaffected by the rotation, and had been lying in wait for Emilia and the others there.

——In that instant, flame-wrapped steel thread bundled together, and an extra-thick, burning silver flash cut across their field of vision.

△▼△▼△▼△

——A burning, crimson silver flash.

If someone had observed it from above, if that someone had possessed knowledge of the technique, they would have realized that what Yae Tenzen unleashed was a steel-thread iaijutsu. No one would imagine that something so thin, so lacking in any consistent form, could take the shape of an iaijutsu. Still less, in the long, long history of the Shinobi, was the Steel Thread Technique a phantom art that, for all but a handful of geniuses, was beyond even reaching the basics of, let alone mastering. That the "Crimson Sakura," possessing the greatest Shinobi talent in history, had honed that finishing move to even greater heights — not a single soul, Aldebaran included, knew this fact. And so——

"——"

——And so, that Rem was able to block that burning silver flash could only be called a miracle.

"——Priscilla-san."

What her lips wove was the name of the woman who bore part of the reason Rem had risen to her feet, who had guided her this far. And she was, at this very moment, the benefactor who had left behind the sign that let Rem snatch back her life.

——She had felt it. A certain killing intent from Yae, the like of which she had never once felt until now.

Human emotion dwells within mana. Those with a keener sense for perceiving that mana find it easier to sense the emotions of others. It is thought that this sense may be the reason Spirits can vaguely read the thoughts of those they face. And Rem, who through her transformation into an Oni was absorbing the surrounding mana through her horn, was perceiving, with a sharpness several times keener than usual, a sense she normally could not feel.

"Until now——"

She hadn't been able to feel it. — No, it was a sense she hadn't noticed even while feeling it. There were various factors behind why she'd become able to sense this now. That she had regained herself. That she bore the sense of mission to take Subaru back. But the greatest factor lay elsewhere. The greatest was the influence of the days she had once spent together with Priscilla in the walled city of Guaral.

In a span of time that was never long, Priscilla had repeatedly tossed riddle-like words at Rem, giving her the chance to confront herself deeply. At the time, out of frustration at her own inadequate healing magic and her own uselessness, Rem had only tried to trace the surface of Priscilla's words.

"Only now, at last, do I feel I understand just a tiny bit."

Even now, she couldn't say she understood the full answer to those words back then. She thought it was homework for a lifetime. And there was no longer any way to check her answer. That was sad. Lonely. It ached in her chest.

"——I want to talk with you right now, Priscilla-san."

Murmuring this, she closed her eyes. And when she opened them, Rem's gift as one of the Oni Tribe awakened. What she keenly sensed was the faint hostility, killing intent, intent to attack mixed into the mana — in the place that seemed to take on color, Rem aligned her iron ball without the slightest error.

Into the position from which a single swing could sweep away the burning silver flash bearing down to bisect herself and Emilia both, she drove the Morningstar, repainting the unleashed finishing blow into something that would not kill.

A high, ringing clang of steel striking steel resounded, and, returning a fierce shock into her hands, Rem's counterattack struck head-on against the flash Yae had unleashed.

"——"

At that fact, Yae drew a faint breath, and still, crouching low, prepared her next move. A myriad of raging silver flashes — Rem sensed them turning to killing intent and racing through the air an instant before their release, and took the initiative, tracing that line, driving in her iron ball, her chain, her magic. A few instants, moments that felt almost simultaneous, overlapped, and Yae's attack was blocked by Rem.

Ah, a breath escaped within her chest. Ah, so this was the scenery the true Oni Tribe had seen. — The moment Rem, more than ten years behind, finally hooked her fingertips onto the edge of the realm Ram had reached as if born to it.

"——Rem!!"

As Rem set her iron ball dancing, continuing to sweep away the storm of burning steel thread, Emilia called out to her. The urgency packed into that voice was not gratitude for being protected, but a warning telling of a change in the situation — and not a good one. Catching Emilia at the edge of her vision, she saw her, long legs stretched out, clinging to the ice disc, her eyes sweeping the spinning field of view, and—

"Al's not here! Nowhere!"

"——Kh"

At Emilia's urgent voice, Rem too finally realized the fact that Al had vanished from view. Al, who should have circled beneath the ice disc, in a position where he could only watch Rem and the others' battle, was nowhere in the sky now that the rotation had removed whatever had been blocking their line of sight. Where, she wondered, and the instant after, it struck Rem like lightning. — She strained her eyes toward the distant sky.

Beyond the long, great crack splitting the earth of the falling Agzadd Canyon, far off in the distance from the falling Rem and the others, a receding shadow could be seen. It was, just like Emilia moments before, a figure with wings on its back — wings made of stone, catching the wind, gliding away at ferocious speed: Al.

In that instant, what crossed Rem's mind was the blow she and Emilia had struck against the ice disc. It had sent the footing spinning vertically, shifting the battle with Yae to the next dimension — but that had been exploited.

"When Emilia-sama and I kicked off the footing, in that same moment——"

At that time, Al had clung to the back of the ice disc, and using the momentum of its rotation from the force of Rem and Emilia's kicks, gained the propulsive force to leap all at once into the distant sky.

"——"

If Al escaped like this, the plan would collapse. They needed to pile on the irregular, wear down Al's reserves, and only then reach the trump card that must be thrown at him. That must not be allowed to happen — an instant decision was made — and Rem and Emilia's eyes met.

"——Rem"

And then, Emilia's intent, which shouldn't have been audible, reached Rem, carried on the mana. What the Oni's horn perceives is not only the sign of hostility. By taking in unwavering trust and expectation as well, it grants Rem boundless strength. So much so that she almost felt that bearing that sentiment, more than absorbing mana itself, was the true source of her power. Yes, because she had truly been given a share of that strength——

"——I'm off!" "Please take care!!"

Raising it up, bringing it down with her whole being, Rem struck the ice disc, sharply accelerating its vertical spin, and with that momentum, Emilia, who had been clinging to the ice, was launched all at once into the sky. Clad in glittering ice, spreading her ice wings on her back once more, Emilia caught the wind and momentum, pursuing Al as he flew off into the distance, soaring, soaring, soaring across the sky of the Agzadd Canyon.

And so, leaving the pursuit of Al to the flying Emilia——

"——I won't let you chase him."

"That's my line, isn't it~"

——They passed yet another mark on the rock wall of the Agzadd Canyon; twenty more seconds until the bottom.

The two maids left on the falling ice disc — Rem and Yae — faced each other, and the final clash between Oni and Shinobi, between the "Hero's Second" and the "Crimson Sakura," began.
