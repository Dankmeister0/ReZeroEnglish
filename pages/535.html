Arc 7 Chapter 37: "Master of the Crimson Lazuli Castle"

——The Crimson Lazuli Castle towering at the center of the demon city of Chaosflame.

A townscape built from disorder plastered over with chaos, like some wedding cake with no sense of unity whatsoever — beams and scaffolding of differing materials and styles strung throughout, a demon city that could only be understood as possessing some peculiar species of artistry. Within it, the castle where red and blue glimmer in alternation stands out with a brilliance all its own.

To begin with, "lazuli" is properly a shade of blue, and to crown it with the word "crimson" is a contradiction in a word. And yet, one glance at the exterior of the Crimson Lazuli Castle is enough to convince anyone that the name fits precisely. What is used in abundance for the castle's foundation and framework is, quite literally, lapis-lazuli-colored stone. A gemstone giving off a deep, lustrous blue sheen, within which red swirls slowly like a drop of blood let fall into water — and at times, the lazuli that ought to be blue wraps itself in a light red as blood.

A fickle-hued castle of chaos that refuses to settle on any single tone. That is the Crimson Lazuli Castle of the demon city, the residence of its castellan, Yorna Mishigure.

"――――"

Summoned to the castle keep's uppermost floor of that Crimson Lazuli Castle, with the long-awaited meeting with Yorna now imminent, Subaru's back is drenched through with cold sweat. The cause of that cold sweat pouring out of him is the party waiting for Yorna in that same keep — specifically, that a face which should never be present is among them.

Leading three men who appear to be his bodyguards, standing there with easy composure, is a familiar black-haired demonic visage — none other than the venerable face of Abel, who ought to be waiting back at the inn. But there is no way he should be encountering him here. Precisely for that reason, Subaru and the others had left him behind and come ahead to the Crimson Lazuli Castle as emissaries first. Which means the reason he, who should not be here, is here is—

"—A fake, disguised as the emperor."

Muttering this under his breath, Subaru twists his cheek at how hollow the words sound even to himself. He had been told beforehand that there existed someone who took on Abel's exact appearance and manner. The precision of it and the suddenness of the encounter had startled him, but that is exactly why there's no need to doubt it. One of the Nine Divine Generals, the emperor Vincent Vollachia's body double—

"Huh, Abel-chin? Why's he here...... mmf"

"Whoa there, you okay, Medium-chan? Al-chin's right here, y'know."

Behind the shock-frozen Subaru, a precarious moment unfolds. Spotting a familiar face among those already present, Medium had been about to call out to him with easy familiarity. Al hastily claps a hand over her mouth and pulls her back. Medium is taller than Al, but despite her surprise at the one-armed man's outrageous act, she obediently goes along with it. Just in the nick of time, Medium's action is cancelled.

"——?"

Even so, that little exchange apparently struck the other party as more than a little suspicious — never mind the man wearing Abel's face, even his bodyguard, a person with yellow-green hair, shoots them a glare. Still——

"Al, nice save there."

"Ah, yeah, that was some godly reflexes if I do say so myself. ...Still, ain't this surprise a little too effective?"

"......Indeed. I think so myself."

Beside Al, who has drawn Medium back, Subaru nods gravely as well. An unforeseen collision with the other party — and, of all the possible collisions, the worst one imaginable. In hindsight, it's a small mercy amid misfortune that Abel didn't come along in person. Had he been here, it would have meant the sudden opening of the true contest to determine the real Vollachia Emperor.

"Perhaps... that might even be an option."

Glancing at the three bodyguards attending the Abel-faced man, Subaru feels a flicker of temptation. There's no telling how many soldiers might be lurking within or around the city, but at the very least, the force he has with him here numbers only three — a false emperor who, back in the capital, is guarded by tens of thousands of imperial troops. Perhaps now is the perfect chance to tear off that mask.

"——No, that would be hasty."

If Abel's account is to be believed, the emperor's position is never a cradle of peace and safety. It's precisely because he is forever on guard against having his throat slit in his sleep that Abel won't even close both eyes in front of others. For someone in a position where that has become the norm — or even someone merely impersonating such a person — exposing an unguarded moment in public is unthinkable. Which means either the three attending him as bodyguards are formidable indeed, or some other countermeasure is in place.

"Wanna take a poke and find out?"

"......If we could easily hit continue, maybe that'd be worth trying. But I'm more afraid of an irreversible turn of events. Let's not do anything rash."

"Yeah yeah."

However serious he'd been, Al doesn't seem to mind having his suggestion shot down. Al's opinions tend to get rejected fairly often, but that's precisely because he throws out, ahead of time, the rash ideas Subaru himself might come up with, buying time to assess things calmly. Thanks to that, the detours in Subaru's thinking are reduced, and he can spend more time scrutinizing the legitimate options. At the very least, for now, they should be seeking a way to get through this without incurring the displeasure of the false emperor's party. Beyond that, he'd like to pin down the purpose of the other side's visit.

Why show up in Chaosflame at this particular timing? And moreover, in a form where the emperor himself makes the journey, even if the reality differs.

"Nn~! Nnn~!"

In the midst of such deliberation, Medium, her mouth still covered, lets out some kind of groan. Having been pulled back without explanation, and with her mouth kept covered the whole time, her indignation is only natural, and Al hastily releases her with an "sorry, sorry."

"Pwah, that was rough!"

"My bad, didn't notice. Wasn't doin' it on purpose just 'cause your cheeks are soft, Medium-chan."

"The more you explain, the deeper the suspicion of sexual harassment grows."

"I dunno about all that, but more importantly!"

While Subaru gives Al a cold look over his excuse, Medium, having caught her breath with her whole body, snaps both hands up and points a finger at the two of them. As Subaru and Al both flinch back at her intensity—

"So, um, I still don't really get it, but... that Abel-chin, is he a different person from the Abel-chin we know?"

"Oh, yeah, yeah, that's exactly right. I mean, the real one oughta be back at the inn in the low town, lounging around waiting for us to get back, right? Which is why we're in a real bind right now......"

"Then isn't that bad? I mean, Natsumi-chan's..."

"Me? What about me...... ah."

Even while saying she doesn't get it, Medium's point cuts straight to the heart of the problem. Named as the target of that point, Subaru furrows his brow, then immediately grasps her concern.

Medium's point, what she means by it, is obvious. And it's something Subaru and Al should have noticed for themselves before she had to say it. Of course, even having noticed, there likely wouldn't have been time to devise a countermeasure. Still, they should at least have had the mental preparation to discuss how to handle it——.

"—Lady Yorna Mishigure will now enter."

Allowing them no such opening, the one who declares time's up is the deer-human girl serving as their guide. Having reappeared, the girl lowers her head with its large antlers and bows to both Subaru's group and the false emperor's party. Then she opens the doors at the front of the hall and calls out, "This way, please." And so, a figure slowly steps into the room — and witnessing that figure with his own eyes, Subaru succeeds in putting into words the impression the deer-human girl had given him.

The impression Subaru had of the deer-human girl is that she resembled a kamuro. A kamuro, in old-time Japan, refers to a young apprentice courtesan-in-training who worked in the pleasure quarters, tending to a courtesan's daily needs in the glamorous red-light district while learning etiquette and the arts. It was likely her surprisingly stylish way of wearing her kimono and her hair ornaments that made Subaru think of it. More than anything, though, the clear reason lies with the person who led that girl in—

"――――"

The reason Subaru succeeded in putting the word "kamuro" into language lies precisely with the person who has just appeared. Forgetting to breathe, eyes wide, utterly absorbed in the sight of the other — this is the instinctive reaction of a human whose heart has been eaten away, whose consciousness has been seized, by something beautiful or overwhelming.

"—My, my, we've got quite the crowd of guests today, don't we, ainsu."

So she says, narrowing those long, slitted blue eyes ever so slightly — a tall woman. Her slender, statuesque frame is wrapped in a vivid kimono adorned with a floral pattern, and her hair, deepening in color from white to orange toward the tips, is elegantly and beautifully done up. Adorning that hair are hairpins fashioned from carved animal bone and horn, and beyond that, a whole array of hair ornaments made from fangs and scales serve to delight the eyes of anyone who looks upon them.

But these are merely accessories — nothing more than beauty shaped by human hands. For their charm to be truly brought to life, what matters is the quality of the person they adorn, the substance within. And on that count, the quality of the person wearing the kimono was beyond question.

"――――"

Moving her slender body with fluid grace, advancing at a leisurely pace, is a beauty that takes the breath away. Draped in a somewhat languid air and yet refined in every gesture, her every movement seemingly calculated down to the last degree with an awareness of being watched — a glimpse, one might say, of the optimal solution to the very act of drawing the eye. What makes that swaying, sauntering gait all the more striking is a fox's tail that looks too large for her slender frame — no, tails, nine of them, each with a rich, full coat of fur. Combined with her upswept hair and hairpins, and the beast ears standing up sharply amid the hair ornaments, the information seeps into the brain like the intoxication of drink: this is a beautiful fox-human woman, wrapped in a kimono.

"You've come such a long way. Welcome to my castle, ainsu."

So saying, the beauty seats herself in the cushioned chair prepared at the head of the hall, casting out her long, supple legs and letting her weight sink onto the armrest. As she extends a hand, the kamuro attending her places into her white fingers a fine pipe painted in gold. The beauty sets fire to the tip of the pipe she now holds, draws the rising purple smoke into her lungs, and smiles alluringly. All while positioned at the head of the hall, looking down upon the guest received at the foot of it — the Vollachia Emperor.

"――――"

That commanding, resplendent bearing and manner of speech, the way she wears her kimono with her slender shoulder deliberately exposed, together with the presence of the kamuro she leads, calls to Subaru's mind the words "courtesan" and "oiran." Of course, Subaru has never actually seen a real pleasure quarter or courtesan himself. It's knowledge gleaned only from period dramas and other works dealing with old eras, but nothing else comes to mind besides that.

—No, wait, to say there is no other word to describe her would be an overstatement. In this setting, there is unmistakably another word that describes her. It's true she has the air of a courtesan, and is beautiful besides, but before any of that, she holds the seat of "Shichi" among the Nine Divine Generals—

"—Yorna Mishigure."

The beauty who has appeared — Yorna Mishigure — hears her name called and looks at the man who called it. Above the wooden-plank floor, meeting Yorna's blue eyes is the false emperor — to distinguish him from Abel, let us call him by the name Vincent instead — none other than him.

"As expected, the same voice......"

Though it was only a single word he uttered, it was, down to the last nuance, the exact same timbre as Abel's. It seems what has been replicated isn't just his form and appearance, but his voice as well. Though, given they've gone so far as to copy his very appearance, it's only natural the voice would be matched too, and it comes as no great surprise. More than that, Subaru finds himself holding his breath at the sight of Vincent and Yorna facing off against each other.

Silently regarding one another, exchanging glances — the sight of the two of them together is as magnificent as a single painting. A beautiful, ravishing woman of alluring charm, and a demonic-visaged emperor who miraculously possesses both dignity and majesty. What sort of event will this meeting between the two of them, on this day, come to be recorded as in the annals of the empire's history? Whether it follows the path of amity or of bloodshed will depend on what comes next.

"......Just what, exactly, is your purpose?"

For coming to the demon city, that is — this is, at the moment, the one point Vincent's party cannot fathom. Their reaction to the encounter with them and to Yorna's entrance had taken priority, but the purpose behind Vincent's visit — what reason could he have for coming to the seat of the Nine Divine Generals who has repeatedly raised rebellion against him? There must be some legitimate justification for it, given the need to play the role of the true emperor. And for that matter, why has Yorna, whose relationship with the emperor should be a hostile one, welcomed him into her castle? Though of course, one could simply say that, given Yorna's position, she has no standing to refuse.

"Well, well, Your Excellency, it's been quite some time, ain't it."

Before Subaru, still puzzling this over, Yorna lets a smile pull at the corners of her eyes and brings the pipe to her lips. Then, exhaling purple smoke, she closes one eye in a gesture of utter insolence,

"I am honored beyond measure to be granted the privilege of gazing upon your noble countenance, ainsu. No matter how many times I invited you, you never once deigned to come to the demon city."

"An invitation, you say?"

Vincent, ignoring both improprieties — her posture and the smoke — knits his brow in displeasure. He crosses his slender arms and, as though pondering, taps a finger against his elbow,

"By your invitation, do you mean the repeated occasions on which you've raised troops against me? If so, I believe I returned my answer in a form visible enough."

"Yes, quite so. And yet, my head remains, as you see, still attached to my neck, ainsu. And what's more, today you've come without that troublesome brat in tow, ainsu."

"――――"

"Could it be, I wonder, that my feelings have finally reached you? My heart flutters at the very thought, ainsu. Do forgive me, won't you."

Clicking her tongue softly, Yorna gives a little "kufu" of laughter. But at that bewitching tone and smile of hers, Vincent doesn't so much as let his expression waver. While Subaru is struck by the sheer perfection of Vincent's performance as the false emperor, he's also drawn to Yorna's manner — the passion laced into the gaze and words she directs at Vincent. It's only the briefest of exchanges, but to Subaru's eyes, the two of them look——

"No way that lady's staging a rebellion just to get Abel-chin's attention, is she?"

"......I'd rather it not be that."

Al, apparently having arrived at the same guess as Subaru, speaks up, and Subaru grits his back teeth at the words. He doesn't want to believe that one of the empire's greatest fighting powers is someone who moves an army out of personal grudge, nor does it seem plausible that anyone could hold feelings of affection for that man who treats other people as less than human. Those are the main reasons Subaru's cheeks stiffen — but there's something even more pressing.

To begin with, there's the pretext under which Subaru and the others had requested an audience with Yorna in the first place. That had been staged under the banner of "Abel is infuriating," meant to catch her attention — and it was never meant to reach someone favorably disposed toward Abel. If Subaru's group's unpleasant guess proves correct, this must be an unwelcome turn of events for Yorna. And yet, despite that, she had let them into the castle. And moreover, in a form that seats them alongside none other than Vincent's own party.

"General Yorna, is that posture of yours not disrespectful, no matter how you look at it? What are you thinking?"

"Hmm?"

While Subaru and the others, wary of the murky situation, look on, the conversation among the emperor's party proceeds. In place of the silent Vincent, the one who makes Yorna raise an eyebrow is the person with yellow-green hair who appears to be Vincent's bodyguard.

A man with short, spiky hair, part of it grown out long like an antenna, he looks to be around the same age as Vincent, perhaps slightly older. Over a black light suit of armor, he wears a sand-colored cloak, and his face and build give off an impression as sharp as a length of wire. His thorny gaze bores into Yorna as though passing judgment.

"His Excellency's attendant is..."

"Kafma Irulux. I've been ordered to accompany His Excellency on this occasion. I believed I understood my role to be that of bodyguard, but... your conduct is beyond the pale."

"My conduct, is it, ainsu? And what might you mean by that?"

"All of it!"

At Yorna's words, which sound almost leisurely, the man who has named himself Kafma flares with anger. Still glaring at Yorna, he thrusts a hand toward Subaru and the others, who are quietly watching the situation unfold,

"To begin with, why have you let these outsiders sit in on this audience! This may be your castle, but it is also, at the same time, a territory of the empire...... have you forgotten even that much!"

"That could never be, ainsu. I remain, properly, His Excellency's own, ainsu."

"That is not what I'm speaking of! And you there — you as well, all of you!"

"Ueh!? Us!?"

At having the brunt of Kafma's anger turned on them, Subaru's shoulders jolt. If at all possible, he'd have liked to be left alone, outside the man's notice, but that had been too much to hope for. Still, if attention has been drawn to them anyway, there's a way to make use of it instead.

"Um, are we perhaps intruding? If so, we could come back another day......"

"That would be a trouble, ainsu. My time in a day is limited, ainsu, and if we let today slip by, there's no telling when the next chance might come."

"Do not detain them! They must be feeling most uncomfortable as it is!"

"Yes, yes, quite so indeed."

For some reason, Yorna is trying to keep Subaru's group here, while, of all things, Kafma finds himself in agreement with them. Given that they're accompanying the false emperor, their standing puts them on opposing sides from Kafma — and yet, in this moment, his words and conduct feel so reasonable that he seems like their sole ally in the room. However——

"Yorna Mishigure. What exactly are you thinking?"

Abruptly, the one who cuts through that air is Vincent Vollachia — the false emperor himself. The moment he speaks, even Kafma, who had been working himself up, immediately backs down. Despite the familiar voice and familiar face, Subaru too feels his insides clench. Even knowing it's a fake, the intimidating presence is real.

"Answer me. What is it you are thinking?"

Silencing both his attendant and the uninvited fellow guests, Vincent poses the question to Yorna once more. At the question and the sheer force of will behind it, Yorna narrows her eyes ever so slightly. Gently bringing the pipe to her lips, drawing the trailing purple smoke into her lungs, exhaling a sweet breath, she draws out her answer, savoring the delay. At this further disrespect of making the emperor wait, Kafma's cheek twitches faintly, but—

"Of course, ainsu, I have always thought only of His Excellency... His Excellency the Vollachia Emperor, ainsu."

"――――"

"Kufu. What a cold look you give me. But it might be best not to send those guests over there away, mightn't it? I daresay His Excellency would be pleased for it, ainsu."

With an air of certainty, Yorna gives a small laugh and juts her chin toward Subaru and the others. Only then does Vincent's attention finally turn toward them with interest. He must have judged that the exchange just now had some reason behind it other than mere caprice on Yorna's part. To sever this dangerous turn of events, Subaru, bracing himself for a scolding, clears his throat with an "ahem,"

"My apologies, but allow me to propose this again. It seems we're rather out of place here, and there appear to be matters we oughtn't be privy to. If we might be permitted to withdraw for now......"

"My, my, how faint-hearted of you, ainsu."

Bowing at the waist, attempting to take his leave of the scene with all due courtesy, Subaru's words are cut off. For a moment, Yorna's gaze, half-hidden behind the purple smoke, takes on a glint of childlike mischief, and seeing it, Subaru realizes with an "ah" his own blunder. If they meant to make a swift retreat, the decision should have been made the instant they laid eyes on the man who was already there. Having misjudged that moment, he now finds himself paying the price in regret. For the reason is——

"I heard it from Tanza, ainsu. ...... You all came to invite me, however presumptuously, to become an enemy of His Excellency the Emperor, didn't you, ainsu?"

And with that, every last bit of their intentions is laid bare before them all.
