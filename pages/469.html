Arc 6 Chapter 64: "The Second Obstacle"

—A silver-haired stranger.

"Huh?"

That unexpected way Ram had phrased it opened a small gap in Subaru's mind.
If she had simply said "the silver-haired person," it would have been a roundabout way of putting it, but it wouldn't have struck Subaru as strange to this degree.
There was no doubt that Emilia possessed beautiful silver hair. Her violet eyes, too, were lovely. Every part of her face, of course, and every part that made up her entire body, was as adorable as a divine work of art.
But the moment you added that one extra word—"stranger"—the meaning changed enormously.

"A silver-haired... stranger..."

"Yes, that's right. Someone I've never once seen inside this tower. At least, she didn't seem hostile toward us. ...I judged the situation and pulled back once. But—"

"—Whoever it is that's joined our side as reinforcements, if the enemy is 'Gluttony,' that's another matter entirely."

Nodding at Subaru's murmur, Ram turned her eyes toward the direction she'd come from. It was Julius, his expression turning grim at the mention of "Gluttony," who picked up where her words left off.
He touched the knight's sword at his waist, pressing his lips into a hard line.

"An unexpected encounter, but having crossed paths with them here as enemies, there's no letting them go. Our purpose from the start was to find a way to undo the damage inflicted by the Archbishops of Gluttony and Lust. If they've shown themselves, then we'll simply have to hear it straight from their own mouths."

"I agree. Ram has no intention of letting him leave here alive, either. He has to be made to regret showing his face so brazenly."

"W-wait! Wait a second! I get how fired up you both are! I do, but—!"

Faced with the two of them baring such fierce hostility toward "Gluttony," Subaru couldn't help but call for a halt.
Julius, whose very existence had been severed from the world; Ram, who'd had her beloved little sister devoured from the inside out—he understood why both of their motivations to defeat "Gluttony" ran so high. In fact, Subaru himself felt like a golden opportunity and a terrible crisis had arrived at once.
But the problem here was—

"Emilia's name is missing from what you two just said. What's up with that?"

"——"

With a bad feeling creeping over him, Subaru cut straight to the question.
Ram's unnatural phrasing, and the lack of any reaction from Julius or Beatrice and the others to her explanation. When he looked around, neither Beatrice nor Echidna showed any sign of finding anything strange in it.
They had taken Ram's remark about "a silver-haired stranger" completely at face value.

"—Who's Emilia?"

"—!"

Ram tilted her head, not bothering to hide her puzzlement, and the question caught in Subaru's throat with a jolt of shock.
Looking around, he saw Julius and Beatrice, even Echidna, staring back at him with utter incomprehension in their eyes. —He couldn't hide his shock at that.

"But—"

It had barely been a minute. Maybe not even that.
Right up until that moment, Subaru had been talking with Julius and the others about "Emilia." Wasn't the very reason they'd all gone running from the balcony into the tower his anxiety over Emilia and Ram not rejoining them, his prayer for the two of them's safety?
So how could it all vanish in an instant like this—

"—Subaru, don't tell me."

Suddenly, Beatrice, who was holding Subaru's hand, seemed to notice something from the change in his expression. She was the first to catch on, but the rest of the group were sharp enough to follow close behind.
They immediately understood that the name Subaru had spoken—a name with no place in their own memories—must carry great weight for them.

"Emilia... that's the name of that silver-haired woman?"

"—. Yeah. If there was a girl with silver hair, that's her—her name's Emilia, and she's one of us. That's why she told Ram to run, and she stayed behind. She's still fighting right now."

"So such a thing really can happen. I myself, of all people, know that feeling firsthand."

"——"

At Subaru's faint, drained answer, Julius touched his own forelocks, looking as though he'd heard something almost too hard to believe.
Unable to hide his shock, Julius nonetheless helped Subaru come to truly grasp anew the reach of "Gluttony's" power, its speed, the sheer viciousness of it—and at last, he felt the horror of it in his own bones.

Honestly, even knowing that his own memories had been stolen, Subaru had never felt it very keenly himself.
Of course, the misunderstandings and paranoia born from that loss of memory, the countless negative feelings he'd directed at Emilia and the others—those weren't things he could easily forget, a chapter of his personal history he'd rather have erased forever if he could.
But even so, the reality of it had always felt thin to him. Groping around for something that wasn't there, trying to feel it as though it had once existed—it was an uncertain struggle, like fishing in a pitch-black sea at night where you can't see a thing.
That was why it had never felt real to him. But this—this was different.

Forgetting Emilia—someone he'd remembered clear as day only moments ago, a companion who had overcome hardship after hardship at his side—forgetting her in a single instant. —Was there anything more monstrous than that?

A usurper of memories, a violator of recollection, a habitual offender in the act of devouring bonds—that was "Gluttony."

Having finally witnessed it with his own eyes, Subaru understood at last.
"Gluttony" was nothing more than a vile criminal who, in the excess of seeking their own happiness, had trespassed into territory that should never be trespassed upon.

"—Guh, hh."

"Ram!?"

As Subaru struggled to swallow down his shock, in front of him, amid the group equally stunned at having had a companion's memory stolen, Ram suddenly dropped to one knee.
Beside Patrasche, she leaned against the black earth dragon's leg, breathing hard.

"What's wrong? Are you okay?"

"...It's just a slight headache. That, and thinking about that stranger I don't know..."

"About Emilia...?"

Ram put a hand to her head, shaking it slowly with a pained expression, and Subaru's brow furrowed.
Probably, if "Gluttony" had stolen Emilia's memory, then Ram, who had been traveling with her, must have witnessed it happen right in front of her. Was that what was affecting her now, Subaru wondered.
But Beatrice, patting his shoulder and saying "Subaru," shook her head slowly.

"It's best you stop making her remember any more than that, I suppose. She might be missing too much, I suppose."

"Missing too much...?"

"It's the sloppy part of 'Gluttony's' Authority showing itself, I suppose. There's too much that simply can't hold together without the person who was stolen from the memory being there—it creates a kind of contradiction, I suppose."

"——"

At Beatrice's words, Subaru was speechless for a moment.
But he quickly grasped what she meant, understanding: so that's how it was.

"Gluttony's" Authority had taken effect, and Emilia's memory had been erased from Ram and the others.
Ram's position had been as Emilia's attendant—a relationship of mistress and servant, so to speak. The bond the two shared, hard to parse from outside as it might have been, held a genuine warmth.
And now that had been ripped away wholesale, leaving a void in Ram where "Emilia" had once existed.

Memory is something like a keepsake tucked away in a drawer.
Ordinarily you're not conscious that it's there, but when you go to remember something and open the drawer, you pull it out and it shows you all sorts of faces. —Without that, a life simply doesn't hold together.
Which meant that for Ram, searching within herself right now for Emilia's name was equivalent to opening drawer after drawer, endlessly, and finding nothing no matter how many she opened.

"I'll keep an eye on her."

"Echidna...?"

Beside Ram, whose face twisted in pain, Echidna stepped up, having raised her hand. Subaru startled at her stepping forward, and Echidna shrugged her slender shoulders.

"There's no time to be arguing here now. Especially not when 'Gluttony' has arrived, and one of our companions is fighting on even as her name is stolen from her. We mustn't stop moving."

"Echidna, I'll leave Lady Ram and her little sister to you. Stay back from the fighting, together with Patrasche."

"Ah, leave it to me. —Julius, this is the moment of truth, but don't let yourself run hot."

"I understand. My fighting spirit is ice-cold, just like this blade."

Julius accepted Echidna's proposal at once, turning his resolute face forward. There, a sharp fighting spirit blazed so intensely that Subaru hesitated even to interject.

"Ram."

"It's frustrating, but if Ram went now, she'd only be a hindrance. Leave her behind. Only—leave 'Gluttony's' life intact. The rest of him you can have, Barusu."

"That's a pretty cow-or-pig way of putting it, but anyway, just stay put and rest. We're going!"

"—Yes. Go on, then."

Leaving behind a frustrated Ram, Subaru nodded to the two of them and to Echidna, who would stay behind. Then, before running off, he stroked Patrasche's neck and peered at the profile of the Sleeping Beauty riding on her back.

"——"

As ever, Rem lay with her eyes closed, breathing so faintly and quietly it seemed almost as if she weren't breathing at all, still lost in a dream from which she would not wake.
For now, that was fine. The words he needed to receive from her, he had already received.
The rest was—

"Wait for it, 'Gluttony'...! I'm sick and tired of letting you eat any more than this!"

—Honestly, if he started listing them, the questions just kept coming, endless.

Why hadn't the effects of "Gluttony's" Authority appeared in Subaru?
Even now, with Emilia's name stolen and erased from Beatrice's and Ram's memories, Emilia's name, her face, her voice remained clearly, distinctly present in Subaru's mind.
Even his faint feelings for her—unforgotten, still certainly there in his chest.

"Is it because... I came from another world...?"

Maybe that was why the rules of this world simply didn't apply to Subaru.
If the memories of this world were records of lives shaved away from the souls of the dead in the "Corridor of Memories," then it might well be possible, as a peculiarity of Subaru's existence as someone who'd come from another world, that "Gluttony's" power to hijack such memories simply didn't reach him.
If that were so, then supposing Subaru were to die, would the memories of "Natsuki Subaru" ever be carved into this world's "Corridor of Memories"? Or perhaps—

"—Is that why I can't do that... that's why I keep 'Returning by Death'?"

It was a conclusion so chilling that just thinking it through made him shudder.
If that were indeed the answer to the mechanism of Subaru's "Return by Death," then his life would go on forever without ever being drawn into this world's spiral.
Put simply, he'd spend decades upon decades in this world, unable even to die of old age.
If Subaru wanted to live out a life that didn't fit this rule, then perhaps it would have to be in a world where Natsuki Subaru himself could truly entrust his memories—

"—Ice Brand Arts!"

"—!?"

In that instant, the sharp, silvery-bell voice sliced through his train of thought.
Lifting his head, looking straight ahead, Subaru felt the passage leading to the green room—the destination of his run—frozen white, greeted by a ferocious blast of cold air against his skin.

And the source of that wind was not some ice fairy dancing amid the swirling diamond dust, but Emilia, whirling with her silver hair streaming behind her.

"—Eiya! Hyah! Ei-ei! Yah!"

Wielding a pair of ice swords, Emilia unleashed a furious assault, crying out battle cries as she went. Her shouts were somehow a bit deflating, but the speed at which her ice blades tore through the air left no room for cuteness.
Each slash struck true, lancing toward the enemy before her, aiming to cut them down in a single stroke.

"That's—"

Around Emilia as she danced with her ice blades, the passage that had become her battlefield lay frozen white and blue, presenting a landscape utterly unlike a tower buried in sand—another world entirely.
It was likely the effect of the ice magic Emilia wielded, radiating outward. Its power was formidable enough to feel out of place even in this world, and Beatrice and Julius, seeing it, caught their breath.
But more than that—

"Ahahaha! You're good, you really are, aren't you just, aren't you though, you sure are, and since you can, you go and do it! We're going to have quite the appetite for you two!"

Facing Emilia, deflecting the ice blades she loosed with easy grace, laughing as he did—the presence there was thick with menace.

"——"

It was a boy with chestnut hair grown out long and ragged, his smile tinged with a somehow gloomy madness.
He looked to be in his mid-teens, dressed shabbily, even filthy. His appearance was hardly hygienic, but what provoked even greater disgust than that was the look in his eyes—eyes that betrayed a bottomless contempt for others, no hesitation whatsoever about feeding on them, a glint of despair and hunger for "life" itself.

Subaru knew at a glance. No one needed to tell him.
The unbearable fact was that there was another being besides Rui Arneb who bore eyes like that.

"—The Archbishop of Gluttony!"

"Ahaha! A guest! No, not that! The main dish, big brother! We've been dying to meet you too. Seems our little sister gave you some trouble, huh!"

Hearing Subaru's roar, the "Gluttony" who had just parried away Emilia's blade deepened his malicious grin. Subaru felt a chill of dread at that ominous smile, and at the same instant Emilia noticed Subaru and the others behind her, letting out a startled "Eh!"

"Ah, everyone! Um, you might not know who I am, but that guy over there's the enemy! A bad person! Leave this to me... even if you don't know who I am!"

"——"

Seeing her companions behind her, Emilia correctly grasped her own situation.
Naturally, she must have been shocked to have her memory lost by the others. That shock—Subaru, who had only ever forgotten and never been forgotten, could scarcely imagine.
And yet she had bravely sent Ram to safety, and even now, while continuing her fight against "Gluttony," she was worrying about Subaru and the others who had come running to help.
A surge of overwhelming emotion rose up in Subaru, and he shouted.

"It's okay, Emilia-chan! I haven't forgotten you!"

"——"

"I'm never going to forget you again! No matter what happens, I won't forget you!"

Thrusting his fist upward, Subaru called that out to Emilia's back.
The moment she heard it, Emilia's eyes went wide, then narrowed a beat later.

"——"

What emotion passed through her heart in that moment, Subaru couldn't say for certain.
But judging by the smile that came over her face right after, it was certainly not one that led anywhere unkind.
With that, Emilia turned straight back to face "Gluttony," hurling her twin ice swords, then pulling an ice spear up from the floor, dancing an icy performance as she hurled herself at her enemy once more.

"You really have no idea how much that one line of yours just affected her, do you."

"Huh?"

Right beside him, watching the same scene, Julius murmured that with a faint smile. It sounded oddly loaded with meaning, and when Subaru turned to look, Julius didn't respond.
Instead, he drew the sword at his hip, tracing a beautiful arc as he took up his stance.
And then—

"Needless to say, but she's on our side, isn't she, Subaru."

"Yeah, that's right. No way someone that cute could be our enemy!"

"—Understood."

Responding with a nod, Julius's figure blurred at the edge of Subaru's vision. —No, that was an illusion.
In the next instant, accelerating with a single step, Julius plunged into the icy melee, his thrust catching "Gluttony," who crossed both arms in front of his chest, and sent him flying hard backward.

"Whoa there, big brother's—"

"I've been waiting for this moment, 'Gluttony'—!"

Julius's razor-sharp blade struck the smirking "Gluttony" with great force. But "Gluttony" killed the impact by leaping backward himself, planting his feet on the ice-coated wall and letting out a low, malicious chuckle.
Then, "Gluttony" let his long tongue loll out provocatively.

"Hey now, don't come at us like that. Sorry to say, but 'we' and 'us' don't share everything we've eaten, you know. Big brother doesn't ring any bells. That's probably something Roy did, not us, huh?"

"—!"

"Well, I guess you could say it doesn't make much difference. Still, setting Roy aside, we're not all that interested in you, big brother. Doesn't really meet our eating standards, if you catch my drift?"

"Eating standards, you say?"

"Ah, yeah, yeah. That's..."

Both hands hanging loose at his sides, blades fixed at his wrists, "Gluttony" eyed Julius, seemingly about to expound on some thoroughly repulsive philosophy of his own.
But—

"There you go—!!"

"—!?"

Into the middle of that came a block of ice hurled without the slightest hesitation—Emilia, swinging both hands downward.
Her strike was merciless, easily filling the width of a passage ill-suited for combat in the first place, specialized entirely for landing a blow that left no room to escape.
Mid-monologue, that magnificent strike must have been more than "Gluttony" had bargained for, his face going pale as he ducked under the block of ice, barely managing to save his own life.

"Tch! I knew this from what we ate, but you really don't hold back, do you, Emilia! Keep attacking like that and people are gonna think you're scary..."

"That's enough out of you! I'm used to people thinking I'm scary—you should know that better than anyone! What matters is how I feel about everyone! And besides..."

Ducking under the ice block, "Gluttony," breathing hard, took a white knee strike straight to the face from Emilia. He caught it on his arm, and the impact sent him flying backward.
Still, roaring fiercely, Emilia glanced back at Subaru just as she finished speaking.

"The one person I most wanted to remember me—remembered me! Right now, I'm feeling super pumped!"

"This is exactly why I don't do well with the instinct types. My least favorite kind."

"—Is that so. But on this, I happen to agree with her."

"Gluttony," his cheeks twisting with irritation, found Julius's tall frame sliding in behind him. A downward slash came, and "Gluttony" caught it hastily with an arm thrown back.
But the imperfect block failed to fully stop the blow, and his forearm was sliced deep, blood spraying. "Gluttony" cried out in pain, and still the barrage of strikes continued—

"—There was a time I felt as though, having been forgotten by everyone and having lost my own footing, my very life had been denied. But the ground on which I stand never needed to waver in the first place."

"Tch! Ah, ah, gyaah!"

Voicing his quiet resolve, the sharpness of Julius's blows steadily increased.
"Gluttony" couldn't withstand it fully, his wounds mounting little by little, until at last he took a solid blow to the chest and screamed.

"Subaru."

"I know. I can't jump into the middle of that."

"...As long as you understand that, it's fine, I suppose."

Julius pressed his relentless assault, and Emilia too struck at "Gluttony" without mercy. Caught between the two of them, "Gluttony" was forced entirely onto the defensive.
Of course, if he could, Subaru wanted to join in there too, to help drive "Gluttony" into a corner, but he knew all too well he lacked the skill to do so.
For now, all he could do was stand here and believe in, and bear witness to, the moment Emilia and Julius's combo brought "Gluttony" down. But—

"Not bad for a slapped-together duo! But how about this, huh?"

"What?"

"—Ice Brand Arts."

Bleeding, but still not losing his composed smirk, "Gluttony" muttered that, staring down. Subaru and Emilia raised their eyebrows in surprise at hearing it; Julius and Beatrice frowned, not understanding its meaning. But what came next shocked all four of them equally.

Right after, an ice spear thrust up from beneath "Gluttony's" feet—Julius cartwheeled to dodge it, while Emilia transformed the spear in her hand into an ice hammer, forcefully smashing it away to block it.
But even having avoided the blow, that didn't erase the shock, nor the exchange that followed.

"Hah! I wonder how it feels to get a taste of your own signature move! How about it, how's it feel, how do you like it, what do you think, I wonder how it is, I wonder how it'd be, I wonder how it'll go, no matter how you look at it, how does it feel—gluttonously! Voraciously!"

"—! All of a sudden—"
"His movements changed!?"

Emilia and Julius cried out in shock, and before them, "Gluttony" pulled an ice weapon up from the floor. Seeing it, Subaru caught his breath at the finely detailed craftsmanship of the icy armament.
Because it was—

"Th-that's Excalibur!?"

"Ice Brand Arts itself might be Emilia's move, but recreating it is something we can do, since we ate you, big brother! Knowledge is a weapon! We're the intellectual Archbishop, you know!"

"—!"

Saying that, "Gluttony" swung his self-recreated ice holy sword, unleashing a radiant-looking slash at Emilia and Julius. Of course, it was merely a weapon that recreated the appearance in ice, and no actual energy of light was released from it.
But in that same manner, "Gluttony" went on creating one weapon after another that shouldn't exist in this other world, catching Emilia and Julius off guard and driving them steadily into a disadvantageous position.

Of course, Emilia and the others quickly regained their footing, shaking off the initial shock and attempting to counterattack against "Gluttony," but each time, "Gluttony" changed his fighting style as though he were a different person entirely, and in the process of having to adapt to that, they found themselves pushed back onto the defensive again and again.

"——"

Watching that exchange with "Gluttony," Subaru came to understand the sheer menace of incorporating multiple people's memories into oneself.
Emilia's technique of conjuring ice weapons one after another was formidable enough on its own, but add the imagination of whoever wielded it, and it transformed into something with an utterly different face. In particular, the knowledge Subaru himself had brought from another world—the weapons recreated from it—were exceptionally troublesome.

There was a mountain of weaponry that didn't exist in this world and yet was plenty practical—all because of the embarrassing chapter of Subaru's middle school days when he'd devoured book after book on weapons from every era and every corner of the globe.
On top of that, "Gluttony" had presumably, over the course of his life, absorbed countless warriors into himself, resulting in a state where he had, so to speak, installed the combat prowess of numerous fighters all at once, on an ongoing basis.
Which meant that simply by pulling out whatever memory best suited the moment from his stockpile, he could instantly transform into an expert with that weapon, freely trading blows in attack and defense.

And there was another reason besides that for why the tide was turning against Emilia and the others.

"Julius! Not there!"
"Ngh...!"

Emilia's scream-like warning overlapped with Julius stumbling in pain.
There, cooperating to try to bring down a single enemy, was a coordination too clumsy to call teamwork.

Emilia and Julius simply didn't mesh well as a pair.
That owed a great deal to the fact that Emilia fought by relying on her instincts—an "intuitive type"—while Julius was a "technical type," the product of endless accumulated training.
But that sort of mismatch, once two fighters reached a certain level of strength, was ordinarily something easily corrected simply by learning each other's habits.
Yes—provided the relationship between them ran deep enough to learn each other's habits in the first place.

"It's kind of sad, isn't it. Did you two really think you could actually coordinate? Knowing someone, versus just trusting them for the moment—even if you end up facing the same direction, those are completely different things, aren't they? Can't sync up because you don't know how the other thinks, can't adjust because you don't know their habits. And in the end, you just collide and get in each other's way... ahaha, how pathetic, you two!"

"Ngh..."

"Knowledge is power! Memory is bonds! Sacrificing memories to soar higher, we do! Stronger, we get! Flying ever higher, ever farther, that's how it goes!"

Leaping up, "Gluttony's" unleashed kick caught both of them at once.
Still small in stature, his legs not particularly long, the soles of his feet nonetheless struck both their shoulders squarely, sending the two of them, crying out in pain, flying backward all at once.
In the meantime, "Gluttony" nimbly somersaulted through the air, landing on all fours on the icy ground.

"Troublesome! Impertinent! Quite the struggle! How about it, this is what we can do! Big brother over there, just watching—satisfied just chewing on your own frustration?"

"You bastard..."

"You went on and on about not forgetting and all that, but how much comfort does something like that actually give you? In the end, experience is what counts. Piling up superior insight—that's exactly what enriches a life, what makes a person a winner. Which means, obviously, we're the best there is!"

Spreading his arms wide, baring sharp fangs, "Gluttony" laughed, saying whatever he pleased.
So this, then, was his philosophy—the words "Gluttony" had just spoken. It differed somewhat from the "philosophy for attaining happiness" that his supposed little sister Rui had once voiced, but in the end it amounted to the same vile ideology: using the lives of others as a stepping stone to fatten oneself.
Subaru thought, from the bottom of his heart, that it was an ideology worth despising, and—

"—Oi, what the hell are you gettin' so cocky for?"

"Wha—"

"——"

"Gluttony," who until then had been laughing loudly as though he owned the world, went wide-eyed the instant he heard that voice. And that shock was shared equally by Subaru and the others.
It was an ashura who had barged into the scene so suddenly, so casually, so brazenly.

Slowly, treading the icy passage in his zōri, a tall, red-haired figure with one shoulder of his kimono-like robe bared came into view down the passage—wearing a savage grin, appearing from the direction opposite Subaru and the others, with "Gluttony" caught between them.

And then—

"Ain't no way the best is some twisted little brat like you. Best, strongest, greatest, finest—every single one of those words exists for me and me alone."

Saying that, having descended from the second level he shouldn't have been able to come down from, Reid Astrea stood there wearing a savage grin.
