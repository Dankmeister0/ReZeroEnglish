Arc 3 Chapter 82: "The Gospel Called Love"

—Ghastly. Grotesque. Malevolent.

At the sight of the presence closing in from behind, those negative feelings flitted through the back of Subaru's mind. That was how far beyond the pale Petelgeuse's current form had gone.

The body that had been crushed under the cave-in was half-destroyed. The right side was smashed, bone exposed; the scalp had been torn from the head, scattering red and white in patches. The right leg no longer existed below the shin, and the limbs hanging limply showed not a trace of life remaining.

It was a corpse. A cadaver. The death-throes of a dead thing. This was a warped Spirit that hijacked another's body and forced it under its own control—and this was that Spirit's last, desperate grasping after life, clinging on to the very end.

Because—

"—My boo-dy, my flesh, my body—give it BAAACK!!"

"You went inside me and it went horribly for you—don't you remember!?"

Even as he said it, Subaru was appalled at himself—"went inside me" was an awful thing to say out loud. Though compared to Petelgeuse's staggering tenacity, his own capacity for appallment ranked a distant second.

The corpse that had been the anchor's body had ceased all life functions. Petelgeuse—or rather, in this case, the true form of the Spirit dwelling within that body—was extending its 'Unseen Hands' into its own flesh, binding the limbs and forcing them to move. It couldn't even control the force properly; every motion ground bone against bone and wrung the flesh to ruin.

If this act of self-destruction continued as it was, the thing risked losing its possessed vessel entirely and dissipating on its own. But—

"Waiting for the clock to run out is asking a lot... damn it!"

Subaru slammed a fist against the rocking edge of the cargo bed, grinding his teeth and spitting out his frustration.

To break free of the forest, to flee the presence closing in from behind, Patrasche and her partner pulling the carriage had picked up even more speed. With the Divine Protection of Windshield behind them, that speed was nothing short of the wind itself. And yet Petelgeuse, tearing through the forest in pursuit, outpaced even that.

Perhaps it was life's last flare—like a candle flame burning brightest right before it gutters out—for the wicked black flame was guttering and surging violently.

Pitch-black demonic hands bound the limbs, and a palm thrust into the chest was likely serving in place of a heart that had surely already stopped. And extending along the ground as an extension of the collapsing body, it was no longer anything but a mass of shadow—a monster born of obsession and a warped will to live.

"This is a Spirit...? What part of this is a Spirit...? Aren't they supposed to be something more sacred?"

The image of a Spirit that Subaru held was of something far brighter, far grander, adorned with a fragility and beauty one would hesitate to even touch.

So then what was this? This dim, obsessive, revolting thing that he couldn't even stand to look at—this warped, ugly presence with not a shred of anything worth revering.

"Now I really get how kind Puck and the Lesser Spirits look, compared to this."

"—Natsuki-san! What's coming up behind us!?"

Otto's shout overlapped with Subaru's irritated click of the tongue. Unable to see straight behind him from the driver's seat, the nightmare closing in remained, for him, still outside the realm of sight.

And that, Subaru thought, was probably a mercy. He held out a hand to stop Otto from leaning out to look back, and raised his voice to keep the thing behind them out of view.

"Just a big black beast chasing us, is all. You probably stepped on its tail somewhere without knowing. Its roar's nasty and its face is scary, so better not to look."

"Are you seriously trying to keep me from seeing it!? While saying every single thing to make me want to look!?"

"Just drive! If it bites me, you're next!!"

"Uhh—that's terrifying!"

Spurring on Otto at the reins, Subaru urged the road forward. But no matter how fired up Otto got, it was the two Earth Dragons at the far end of his reins who did the running. Already at full speed, there was no pushing them any faster.

Which meant—

"Holding you off is my job, huh. Big showdown at the climax—how many times am I gonna have to do this climax!? What part of you is 'Sloth,' you useless workhorse!!"

"The Wiiitch... Satelllaaa...! Meee, meee, love, love, love me, love, love, love, be loved, love, love, lovelovelovelovelovelove, aiaiaiaiaiaiaiaiaiaiaiaiaiiiiiiiiiii!!"

"Neither of us is loved by anybody! There's no rom-com where the love interest tries to crush your heart! I withdraw my application!"

Petelgeuse lifted his head, and a scream echoed within a face that was nothing but a death mask.

A body on the verge of death, betrayed in truth, and still he screamed of an unchanging 'love' for the Witch. To Subaru, for the first time, it looked pitiful.

The scream demanding a body, the shriek demanding 'love' from the Witch—behind both of them lay a Spirit without a body of its own, one so rarely perceived that its hunger to be loved, an unhealing thirst, had been eating away at his existence without end.

—Not that this could ever justify or excuse the thing's existence.

"No finishing move, no super magic. But it's me you're up against. I'm not letting you past me, and there's no way in hell I'm letting you meet the girl waiting up ahead...!"

"Natsuki-san, you feel that strongly about me...!"

"Can you just shut up for a second!? I'm trying to look cool right now!"

Shouting back at Otto—whether he was joking around or serious was impossible to tell—Subaru turned to face Petelgeuse as the distance closed.

Thanks to Julius's interception, the total number of usable 'Unseen Hands' had dropped. And since some were still being used to keep the body alive, the number of demonic hands available to attack looked, at a glance, to be seven—same as at the very start.

Inch by inch, dragging his body as though clawing at the earth, Petelgeuse closed in. A raised demonic hand snapped a branch and split the ground as it slammed down from above. Its fingertips grazed the rear of the cargo bed, gouging into the edge with no regard for its sturdiness.

If the next blow landed dead center on the cargo bed, the carriage would flip for certain. Thrown clear, Subaru would be swallowed by Petelgeuse, and this time he really would be devoured head-first.

"Once he realizes he can't take over my body, he's definitely going for the murder-suicide plan."

"Natsuki-san, we're clearing the forest—!"

Simultaneous with Otto's voice, the green of the surroundings opened up all at once. Breaking through the tree line, they shot out and accelerated down the slope of grassland beyond, sliding as if in free fall. Chasing after them, clawing at the earth, Petelgeuse—his mass of shadow swallowing fallen trees and boulders whole—became warped malice itself as he bore down on the slope.

—They'd cleared the forest and entered the highway. Before long, they'd catch up to Emilia and the others.

He couldn't bring Petelgeuse before them. Before her. Even without knowing the details of the Archbishop of Sin's 'Trial,' tragedy was a certainty.

So, therefore, if that was the case—

"Forest's behind us. —I'm not holding back anymore!"

"Love IT! Love, LOVE is ALL that MATTERS—!!"

Weeping tears of blood, mouth open to show teeth broken and missing, Petelgeuse cackled.

Listening to that deafening voice, Subaru threw open part of the cargo in the back of the bed. He hauled out something heavy, and a pungent, brimming smell struck his nose. Then, cradling it in his arms, he leveled a finger at the blood-smeared face.

"—Burn to ash, Petelgeuse."

"——!!"

The rising 'Unseen Hand' aimed skyward, drawing back to unleash a cascade of destruction. But before it could fall, Subaru moved faster.

Lifting the jar with both arms, a savage grin spreading across his face—he hurled the oil jar.

The black demonic hand came falling.

Meant to crush the carriage bed and all, to blow Subaru apart.

Arm extended straight out, index finger leveled like the muzzle of a gun.

At that fingertip, a red glow—the borrowed Lesser Spirit of 'Red,' lent to him by Julius.

"Lending me your strength, Julius Juukulius."

"YOOOU—!!"

Petelgeuse screamed, the arm bearing down from directly above. The sight of it was the worst thing imaginable, an unnamable, evil-god's silhouette.

Subaru's fingertip crackled and burned; the name of the Spirit lending him its power was 'Ia.' So—

"Ia! Ia! Hastur—!!"

An incomplete incantation from an incomplete caster, and an uncontracted Lesser Spirit besides.

Incompleteness multiplied by incompleteness—but the will behind it, at least, was unified into one.

And to intervene in the world required nothing more than a single spark.

Mana on the verge of running dry interfered with the atmosphere through the Spirit's power, and a bursting spark seared the air, rushing toward Petelgeuse. The malevolent face, smeared with blood and oil, opened its mouth wide—

"—Aaaaaaaah!!"

—In an instant, the view before Subaru's eyes was swallowed in a vivid, swelling red.

Petelgeuse's entire body, the oil doused over him serving as kindling, burst into flame with tremendous heat. Rippling fire scorched his whole body, and a scream beyond words tore at the air.

For Subaru, whose only means of physical attack was his bare hands, and who had no effective way to strike a Spirit like Petelgeuse, this was the greatest, and last, blow he could manage.

At worst, he'd considered that fire without mana behind it might be useless against a Spirit. But given how Petelgeuse kept demanding his body, Subaru judged that an attack on the nearly-dead flesh wouldn't be wasted.

Watching the agony and heat-shock wring a death-scream from him, that judgment seemed to have been correct.

Without telling Otto—who believed he'd already unloaded all the cargo at the village—Subaru had smuggled aboard an oil jar for some other purpose entirely, and it had proven useful here. Given how effective it was, he thought he might as well have pilfered two or three more, but there was no time to dwell on the regret.

His voice shaking with agonizing pain, Petelgeuse's raised 'Unseen Hand' came swinging down like the scythe of the god of death.

His vision seared by flame, Petelgeuse shook his head, unable to take aim.

Subaru instinctively rolled sideways, an emergency dodge to the unstable edge of the cargo bed—an instant later, the arm slammed down at point-blank range, grazing the sole of his foot.

The wooden cargo bed buckled under the impact, the section struck by the demonic hand crumpling as easily as if it were made of paper. Bathing half his body in flying wood splinters from the U-shaped gouge torn into the bed, Subaru gritted his teeth and dragged his right leg deeper into the cargo bed.

"—Agh! Aaah, gahaah!"

The grazing fingertip had carved deep into his calf, and the heavy bleeding sent cold sweat pouring off him. His vision flickered and flashed with the pain of shredded flesh and the sensation of blood draining away. He instinctively pressed a palm to the wound to stem the bleeding, but with no tourniquet, it was a drop in the bucket.

And above all—

"Give it to meeee, hand it over, offer it uuup..."

Burning black fingertips hooked over the edge of the violently rocking carriage.

Slowly, Petelgeuse hauled his upper body up, forcibly dragging his nearly severed lower half along, and with a stomach-churning wet sound, the pitch-black mass fell onto the cargo bed.

The writhing demonic hands formed a bloated, misshapen body, the slight, bony-framed original host's form now unrecognizable. 'Unseen Hands' burst from his back and sides; aside from the shredded right arm, four more demonic hands had sprouted apart from the original left arm—a vile visage.

He dragged along a lower half that was dead from the knee down, an arm jutting from mid-thigh serving in place of the lost leg.

The face hanging limply downward showed the whites of its eyes, even the tears of blood half-charred by the flame. Still licked all over by tongues of fire, only the priest's robe he wore remained barely identifiable—and that only made the grotesqueness stand out all the more.

Because it blatantly proclaimed that this ghastly monster wore the skin of a human being.

"You're a real mess, you. ...Though I'm not really in a position to talk."

Twisting a face that threatened to seize up with pain, Subaru forced himself to his feet through sheer stubbornness. The bleeding from his leg was ongoing; he didn't have much time to spare. Then again, the same was true for his opponent.

Petelgeuse's whole body had already rotted away, and on top of that he'd been doused in fire, on the verge of burning to nothing. He couldn't want a long fight either. This confrontation would be over in an instant—it had to be.

The cards Subaru had left to play weren't many. If anything, they were few. All he could manage was the incomplete Shamak spell, borrowed lesser-than-lower-tier fire magic through the Lesser Spirit's power, and beyond that, bare-fisted combat while ignoring the pain. The options were so limited it was almost funny—but he had to break through this situation using whatever he had. He'd wrung every scheme dry. All that was left now was sheer cunning.

"Fleeesh, boo-dy, won't disappear... can't disappear, no maaa'am..."

"That's what I'm saying! You go inside my body, all that happens is you get hurt! What does the Witch even matter? You and me, we're both just being jerked around!"

Dragging himself forward, demanding Subaru's body in halting words—Petelgeuse's stubborn refusal to give up drew a furious shout from Subaru, aimed at breaking the madman's spirit.

But Petelgeuse's reaction to that shout came in a form different from before.

"—The Witch. Satella."

He murmured suddenly, with clear diction, and lifted his face.

Half-collapsed, its cheek gone to expose what lay beneath, the face bore an eerie resemblance to an anatomical model Subaru had seen in school. With the skin beneath still exposed, Petelgeuse's rolled-back eyes swam back into place.

Unfocused pupils wavered, and gradually, an image of Subaru resolved within them.

"You are... dangerous. Da-dangerous. Da, dadada, danger, dangerdangerdanger, DANGER dangerous DANGER—!"

"Ha—!?"

"Receiving her favor, receiving, receiving it, and yet—! Denying love, and me, meee, meee, meeeee, driving me this far, this far, driving death, death deathdeathdeathdeathdeathdeath, hiii—"

"—!"

Head shaking violently, Petelgeuse rattled off incoherent words. From that body, an arm suddenly shot toward Subaru; unable to react, it grazed his shoulder and side and plunged into the wall behind him. —It wasn't so fast as to be invisible. There was simply no telegraphing motion, so he couldn't react.

Petelgeuse's thoughts had scattered so thoroughly now that no ordinary nerve response could gauge his movements anymore. The danger of not knowing what he might do the next moment had always been there, but once things reached a level where even the next motion couldn't be predicted, Subaru had no way to cope.

The 'Unseen Hand' remained extended with Subaru caught between its reach, connecting a straight path between the two facing each other at the front and back of the cargo bed.

If Petelgeuse simply closed the distance inch by inch like this, Subaru wouldn't be able to resist and would be swallowed.

The instinct from before—when the urge to claim a body had run wild—had probably been, in some primal sense, a more straightforward attack on Subaru. Hearing the Witch's name had restored some fraction of intellect to him, and that was exactly what had produced this situation. The nature of it was too vicious for words.

"To the Witch, Witch, Satella, to Satella, love, love, love it! I love her! I am loved! Satella, you, you made me, made me what I am! Not for a moment have I forgot, forgotten... Even if you've forgotten, I—I have not, forgotten!"

Tears spilled. Not tears of blood—real tears.

Truly, for the first time at this point—for the very first time, Petelgeuse screamed words of sanity.

Words, will, were dragging Petelgeuse down from that maddened darkness into reality. Petelgeuse's clouded eyes now held a definite will as he glared at Subaru.

"You are dangerous! One day, you will be a threat to the Witch Cult! Before that! Before you can reach out your hand to Satella! Here! Right here, right now! By my own hand! With my diligence! To part ways with 'Sloth' within me, to make my love true... I will die!!"

Rocking his upper body wildly back and forth, blood gushed in torrents from all over Petelgeuse's body. It was a sign that the energy of the 'Unseen Hands,' pent up within him, was tearing through his flesh from the inside, about to burst outward. Petelgeuse was no longer trying to steal Subaru's body—he was trying to kill Subaru so as to leave no lingering trouble behind for the Witch he worshipped.

That was a thought with will, with intellect, drawing a clear line between itself and a mere beast, and—

"If you'd stayed a monster to the end, I'd have lost."

Subaru drew his hand from inside his coat, and at the sight of what was gripped in it, Petelgeuse's eyes went wide.

Seeing that reaction, Subaru felt a pang in his chest. But he looked away, refusing to dwell on that fleeting sentiment, and swung his arm up high.

From the tip of the hand raised straight overhead, a book bound in black—the Gospel—was thrown.

"Ah... Satella."

That word, spilling quietly from Petelgeuse's mouth, came in a low, hushed tone—the very voice one would use to call out, in a moment of peace, to someone unbearably beloved.

Gazing up at the sky, Petelgeuse raised his fleshly arm toward the heavens, and following that arm, the pitch-black demonic hand launched upward, aiming for the Gospel spinning through the air. Fingertips caught the cover; the instant they closed around it, it happened.

The book, hurled far above the cargo bed into the sky, fluttered in the wind, on the verge of being blown away by its own momentum. Which was to say, it was taking the full brunt of wind and momentum head-on—and the act of reaching a hand out for a Gospel in that state meant—

"——!?"

Petelgeuse's body, which had been looking upward, tilted sharply backward under a ferocious gust of wind. The dragging leg smashed through the gouged plank flooring, and half his body sank as though flung away.

It was the effect of his fingertip escaping the reach of the Windshield Divine Protection that the Earth Dragons were born with, one that extended its influence even to the running carriage.

Once before, in a carriage bound for the royal capital, in the aftermath of some foolish antics, Subaru himself had fallen into the same situation. In a carriage's cargo bed with no Windshield, taking the full wind pressure and shaking of full speed directly with no Divine Protection—there was no way to keep one's posture under that.

"—O, ohhhhhhh!!"

The instant Petelgeuse tilted violently, Subaru let out a roar and lunged forward.

Forgetting for now the pain of his gouged leg, the pain of his torn shoulder, he sprang forward as if released from a spring. There was no decisive technique, no overwhelming move that could turn the tide. So he made sure, at least, not to miss the one moment that mattered.

Having crossed the boundary between life and death time and again, the one instinct Subaru had gained from it all.

He broke into a run; Petelgeuse, facing the charging Subaru, lifted his head and opened his mouth to scream something. Subaru heard nothing. He simply, recklessly, dropped his stance low and drove headlong for Petelgeuse's chest.

The 'Unseen Hand' launched. The speed of the thrust-out palm was slow, almost eerily so, as if frozen in an extreme state of concentration.

He tilted his head. His body wouldn't move the way he wanted. He could avoid a direct hit from the palm, but it was on a course to graze his cheek and neck for certain. He knew the pain was coming. A pressure so intense he almost instinctively wanted to shut his eyes.

In the midst of a situation like that, there was something he found himself remembering. A memory of pain inflicted, over and over, relentlessly. Days gone by that, until now, he'd only thought of as meaningless time.

"—There were two things Wilhelm-san taught me."

The palm arrived. The surface of his tilted neck, part of his cheek and ear, burst with pain as though pressed with red-hot iron. His thoughts scorched with the pain, his vision flashing white.

He'd dodged it. He drew breath. Not over yet.

"That I have not one shred of talent for the sword—"

Pain searing hot, relief slack with release—two sensations fighting to seize control of his mind, and caught in the gap between them, Subaru looked straight ahead.

Beyond the palm he'd avoided, another palm now, this time, aimed for Subaru's face—

"—and the guts not to shut my eyes when I'm about to get hit!!"

He shouted, ducked his head down low. The palm tore across the back of his neck with a jolt of pain, but he gritted his teeth through it. Straight ahead, Petelgeuse's eyes went wide with shock. In a body that no longer held human shape at all, only in that face—bone and muscle fiber exposed—did some vestige of humanity seem to remain, Subaru thought.

"——!!"

A full-swing fist punched through that face—a fatal blow against Petelgeuse's already-tilting body. The body, driven back by the shock of the strike, missed its footing badly on the broken flooring, and was thrown clean out of the carriage. And then—

"Ohhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh——"

Hanging upside down in midair, Petelgeuse was dragged along by the carriage. A torn piece of his priest's robe had caught on the broken edge of the cargo bed, keeping him tethered to the carriage even as it pressed him against the ground and ground him down.

Blood scattered, flesh burst apart, even the 'Unseen Hands' that had made up his body peeled away and fell off, and the being called Petelgeuse crumbled apart. And still, upside down, Petelgeuse lifted his collapsing face and glared up at Subaru on the cargo bed.

"Not, not over, not, not over, not over, not, no, no, no, no!?"

"—No. It's over now."

Looking down at the impossibly tenacious Petelgeuse, Subaru suddenly noticed, in the corner of the cargo bed, the Gospel lying there—the one Petelgeuse had reached for and surely grasped.

He picked it up, flipping through the pages. Near the end, he came upon a blank page.

"The Gospel, that, that is... it affirms my future, my actions, gives me the means to repay love with love, my love is... ah!"

"So that's what you were following, huh. —Then, in that case."

Petelgeuse shifted his shoulder as if reaching for the Gospel. But his right arm was already gone, and his left, which had been on the verge of falling off, had lost everything past the upper arm to the friction against the ground. Limbs lost, even the 'Unseen Hands' gone, this was the last thing the vanishing Petelgeuse clung to—a Gospel.

Subaru opened it to display that blank page, scooping up with his fingers the blood flowing from his own neck. Then, as if striking it down, he carved a mark of red into the Gospel.

"Here, you—it's 'The End'!"

Large and bold, on the blank spread, the words 'The End' were etched in the red I-script.

Seeing that, Petelgeuse's eyes went wide, lips trembling. The wave of emotion spreading in his eyes was too complex for Subaru to read anymore.

And before that raw emotion could become words, the end arrived.

The carriage lurched, and the hem of Petelgeuse's priest's robe, caught on the cargo bed, came loose. That sent Petelgeuse's falling body crashing to the ground—and the further torn, trailing robe, before it could hit the earth, caught in the spinning wheel of the carriage.

Dragged in by the entangled robe, Petelgeuse's body—light now, having lost blood and limbs—closed the distance to the wheel. The end was in sight. The sound of tearing cloth mixed with the scattering of blood, and in the final instant, Petelgeuse looked up at Subaru and screamed.

"—Natsuki Subaruuuuuuuuuuuuuu!!"

The scream echoed, and turned, just as it was, into a death cry.

Still screaming Subaru's name, Petelgeuse's body was swallowed by the wheel, dragged in and ground apart, blood and fragments of flesh scattering as his life came to an end.

The body vanished, and with it, the Spirit's presence that had dwelt within, swept up in that violent, spectacular end, dissipated as well.

At the very last, one of the 'Unseen Hands' extended before Subaru's eyes.

The palm stopped just short of grabbing Subaru's face, then dissolved away, spilling from the fingertips into nothing. That, in the truest sense, was the proof that Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti had ceased to exist.

"This time, for good, just sleep... Petelgeuse."

Certain that it was over, Subaru sank down onto the cargo bed.

At once, the pain he'd been able to ignore until now came roaring back, and Subaru rolled over, half-crying from the pain—no, crying outright, tears streaming.

"It hurts, this is bad, I'm dying, I'm dying, this hurts, this is bad, this is bad...!"

The tears wouldn't stop. Neither would the pain. Throbbing, aching, the bleeding wound pulsed, and that pain stimulated every last inch of his body. So the ache in his chest, too, was surely nothing more than sympathy pains from the wound.

There was nothing to pity here. The madman, the mad Spirit Petelgeuse, deserved not a shred of sympathy. He'd rampaged in his own self-indulgent way, and met his end because of it.

Screaming a blind love, behaving selfishly through it all, and dying alone in ruin.

No one needed to feel a scrap of pity for a fate like that.

—No one but Subaru, alone, needed to feel that way at all.

"No one's ever going to understand you. You deserved to die. Of course you had to go. No one—no one at all—will ever forgive you. —So I'll pity you. Just that much, at least."

Understood by no one, unable to give love to the one from whom he demanded it—a solitary monster.

Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti, this time, truly vanished.

Leaving nothing behind in anyone's heart, anyone's chest.

Except in Subaru's chest alone, where a wedge of pity was driven in—this time, for real.

"Natsuki-san, you're covered in wounds like nothing I've ever seen—are you all right?"

"Course I'm not all right, I just cried my eyes out big-time. Haven't bawled like that since the anesthetic wore off in the middle of a cavity filling. Right now every single wound on me is doing a group chorus of 'ow ow ow.'"

Having moved from the half-wrecked cargo bed to the driver's seat, Subaru gingerly tended to his wounds with the first-aid supplies Otto handed him. Travel by merchant caravan apparently came with its share of danger, and it seemed keeping such medicine and tools on hand was standard practice. Subaru was grateful for that foresight.

"No idea how well medicine from another world's gonna agree with my body, though. This ointment—is it gonna be okay for my skin? I'm actually pretty sensitive, skin-wise."

"It's medicine that activates the mana in your body to speed up wound closure. Seeing you that covered in wounds is pretty painful for me to watch too, so hurry up and treat them. Quickly."

"Fine, fine. Geez, what's with the— OW! Wait, what is this, whoa, this is bad! It hurts! It stings! Hey, it stings, I'm dyiiing!"

"Effective medicine stings like hell, that's just how it is. The pain will fade soon enough. —Until then, though, you'll be tasting hell on earth."

Watching Subaru writhe from the sting of the wound salve out of the corner of his eye, Otto finally wore an expression like he'd landed a hit of his own, smirking. Seeing a bit of the devil in that profile, Subaru wiped away the tears welling up again with a finger.

"So, how far behind are we?"

"We're not behind at all. If anything, we're making good time, thanks to both Earth Dragons pushing themselves to the limit in the face of an absurd crisis. ...Seriously, what was chasing us back there?"

"A slacker, don't you know? Hangs upside down from tree branches, has really long arms, that kind of animal."

At Subaru's evasive answer, Otto let out a small sigh and, seeming to give up on pressing further, glared ahead. Following his gaze, Subaru too looked straight ahead, at the grassland of the Lifaus Highway. A road that stretched to the far horizon—the shadow they sought was nowhere in sight yet, but—

"We'll catch up. No question. This time, I'm going to save you."

Murmuring, he slapped his own cheeks to renew his resolve. A sharp pain shot through the wound, but—perhaps thanks to the medicine—the bleeding had already stopped, and it only left him half in tears.

"Do you think we'll make it in time?"

"We'll make it in time!"

That wasn't weakness on Otto's part—it was a question testing Subaru's resolve. Probably there was also a sense in which, hearing Subaru's answer, he wanted to settle the direction of his own heart.

Subaru sniffed, twisted the corner of his mouth, and let a grin baring his teeth show through as he said—

"It's about time Rem got tired of waiting for good news. Wouldn't be much of a man if I didn't live up to her expectations."

"The name of the woman you're in love with?"

"The name of the girl who's in love with me!"

Hearing Subaru declare that with neither bravado nor embarrassment, standing firm and proud, Otto looked momentarily taken aback. But then, almost immediately, his face broke into a grin.

"Ah, well, in that case, I suppose there's no choice but to look cool doing it!"

Otto shouted cheerfully, snapping the reins hard with a dry crack.

The two Earth Dragons cried out, and the carriage, cutting through the wind, picked up even more speed.

Racing on, racing on, racing as if flying down the highway, the carriage went on.

Beyond the horizon, to find the precious thing that had been slipping further away.

Time passed, moment by moment. But Natsuki Subaru looked ahead.

Natsuki Subaru did nothing but look ahead, single-mindedly, without end.
