Side Story: "Rem's Maiden Heart Is Super Complicated"

Every now and then, Rem seriously suspects that the man she's fallen for might be testing her.

For example, that's when she catches sight of him dozing off during a break.

"Subaru-kun, if you sleep in a place like this, you'll catch a cold."

On the terrace where the warm sunlight streamed in, Rem approached the napping Subaru and murmured to him softly, in "a voice as faint as a mosquito's whine."

With this, Rem secured her justification: she had tried to wake him, but he simply hadn't woken up.

"Honestly, Subaru-kun really is hopeless."

Saying this happily, Rem decided that she, too, would sun herself right beside Subaru. And once that was decided, preparations had to be perfect. From there, Rem's movements were too quick for the eye to follow.

She pulled a nearby chair up right next to Subaru's, draped a blanket over the napping Subaru so he wouldn't catch cold, set out tea and sweets on the table in case he wanted some when he woke, asked her sister Ram to go do a bit of work down in the cellar so she wouldn't come wandering by, delivered tea and snacks to Emilia's room so she'd rest there instead, gathered up a stack of unapproved documents and submitted them to Roswaal so he'd be shut up in his office, and — just to be safe — sealed every door within reach so that Beatrice wouldn't put in an appearance. Only then did Rem finally sit down beside Subaru.

Even then, she never forgot to strictly observe the rule she had sternly imposed upon herself: Never step on Subaru-kun's shadow. She was also doing her utmost to hold back from breathing in the air he exhaled.

Even so, a storm of irrepressible impulse demanded that Rem spend every possible moment as close to Subaru as she could get. Concretely speaking, Rem kept staring at him single-mindedly, with an intensity fierce enough to bore a hole through him.

"Haah... Subaru-kun is so cute..."

With an utterly melted expression she would never show in front of others, Rem savored the sight of his sleeping face.

His sharp eyes, which needlessly intimidated other people most of the time; his lips, which spun out glib remarks as though he'd die if forbidden to talk; his hunched back, which curved the instant he stopped consciously straightening it — the moment all of that turned into this defenseless sleeping figure, it came at Rem's sanity like a merciless barrage.

"...Rem?"

Before she knew it, Rem had leaned her face in so close that her breath must have reached him, and her eyes collided with Subaru's sleep-dazed ones. Her heart nearly burst, but Rem hid it behind a smile and let nothing show.

"Subaru-kun, if you sleep in a place like this, you'll catch a cold."

"Mm, ah... ahh, guess I dozed off. My bad, my bad, gotta be careful... fwaah."

At the sight of Subaru yawning so hugely that his hand couldn't even cover it, Rem reaffirmed the warm feeling in her chest, and—

"Honestly, Subaru-kun really is hopeless."
