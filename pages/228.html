Arc 4 Chapter 62: "The Tragedy of Roswaal Manor"

Seeing that woman standing at the doorway, a shudder ran through Subaru's entire body. Even for Subaru, who had experienced countless dire straits and even lost his life multiple times since being summoned to another world, she was an existence that brought an utterly distinct brand of terror.

She wore a black cloak, her voluptuous figure tightly wrapped in pitch-black garb. Just like Subaru, she had long black hair—a rarity in this world—braided neatly, and a seductive smile graced her gentle features with down-turned eyes. Were it not for the blood-dripping blade held casually in her hand, she was a woman beautiful enough to be hailed as a painting—a serial killer, the worst calamity to Subaru. The First Killer. The "Bowel Hunter," Elsa Granhiert.

"Wh-why... are you here!?"

"—My, I was wondering whose scent I caught, and it was you? How have you been feeling since then? Are you taking good care of the insides of your belly?"

Subaru gave voice to his shock. Elsa raised an eyebrow and tilted her head, as if only just noticing his presence. Her response to his question already failed to qualify as a conversation. Cherishing one's own entrails was an incomprehensible act that no ordinary person could ever perform. In other words, the woman before him, who demanded such things as though it were the most natural matter in the world, was unmistakably a lunatic. And a lunatic of an entirely different vector than Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti.

Tensing his entire body, Subaru focused all his senses on Elsa's every move. The combat prowess of the madwoman before his eyes was on a level where even the "Sword Saint" Reinhard had failed to finish her off. No matter how finely Subaru honed his nerves, she was not an opponent whose precursors of action he could even begin to grasp.

"—Whose permission did you get to enter here, I suppose?"

Suddenly, a voice rang out from behind Subaru, directing a question at Elsa. The one posing the emotionless inquiry was Beatrice. The young girl stood motionless in the exact posture she had held while confronting Subaru earlier, having merely wiped any trace of tears from her expression. To the young girl's question, Elsa lightly brushed her long hair with a hand,

"It didn't appear to be locked, so I simply opened it and came in, you see? If you were in the middle of an important discussion, you had best not forget to lock it next time."

"That is not what Betty means, I suppose. This is Betty's Forbidden Library, and entering without permission... aside from that man there, is not something that can be done easily, I suppose. Just how did you..."

"Ah, that was a simple matter."

As Beatrice glanced at Subaru—the exception to Door Crossing—Elsa nodded as if finally understanding the meaning of the question. Gesturing toward the open door with her hand,

"Your magic that partitions space... uses doors as a medium, does it not? A magic across doors that connects a closed room to another closed room."

"That is correct, I suppose. Unless Betty grants permission, one should never be able to reach the Forbidden Library, which connects to any door in the manor, I suppose. Just how did you..."

"Like I said, it is a simple matter. Since it uses closed doors as a medium... if you leave every single door wide open, the alternatives will eliminate themselves on their own, won't they?"

"――――!"

Elsa laid out the method to bypass the restrictions of Beatrice's Door Crossing with utter ease. Indeed, what she said was correct. Beatrice's Door Crossing was, strictly speaking, a spatial transfer magic that connected closed doors to one another. That meant it could not connect to an open room. If limited to the doors within the manor, opening every single door in the estate made it self-evident that the remaining door would connect to the Forbidden Library. However, to accomplish that—

"You'd have to open all the doors in the manor... There's no way nobody would get in your way..."

As he spoke, Subaru truly realized his own stupidity. Or perhaps it was the result of averting his eyes from a reality he did not want to see.

"You... whose blood is that knife wet with?"

Fresh blood clung thickly to the blade of the kukri knife Elsa gripped. Dripping from the tip, it looked as though it had just ripped through prey only moments ago—and coupled with his earlier deduction, it was more than enough to make Subaru imagine the absolute worst. As Subaru trembled with drained, bloodless lips, Elsa traced the flat of the raised knife with her finger, licking away the blood that stained her pale fingertips.

"Whose do you think it is?"

"You bastard..."

"A hint. She was wearing a maid outfit."

Three people remained in the manor. All of them wore maid outfits. No, Rem, who slept in her bed, wore a nightgown. She was excluded from the candidates. That left two.

"Hint number two. Her hair wasn't long."

Petra with her medium-length hair, and Frederica with her long, flowing golden hair. —Subaru's throat drew in breath for an impending roar.

"Hint number three—she cried and died while saying, 'Subaru, Subaru.'"

"ELZAAAAAAA--!!"

Baring his teeth and dropping his posture, Subaru charged headfirst at Elsa. Elsa gracefully extended her slender limbs to intercept him, aligning the tip of the knife in her right hand toward the approaching Subaru—

"—My?"

"Who'd charge head-on against a monster like you!"

Elsa's voice sounded as if she had swung at thin air. Directly in front of her, the low-crouched Subaru swept his arm upward from the floor in a sweeping arc. Caught in the trajectory, the scattered pages of the Gospel flew into the air. A dozen or so white pages briefly obstructed the field of view between Subaru and Elsa. In that split second, inhaling deeply, Subaru concentrated power in the center of his stomach, wringing out his mana—

"—Shamak!!"

Black smoke erupted, and total incomprehension filled the distance between Subaru and Elsa. Though Shamak had little effect on Julius, its decent effectiveness against Elsa had already been proven in a previous world. Unlike before, when it had completely drained every drop of mana in his body, his improved mana control meant it only resulted in an intense sluggishness throughout his body.

"Beatrice!"

Spinning around, Subaru grabbed the arm of Beatrice, who had been watching the clash from behind. For a moment, her tensing arm moved as if to reject his palm, but she could not break free from Subaru's firm grip. Roughly pulling her light body close and scooping the small girl into his arms, Subaru dove headlong into the Shamak without a second thought.

—Incomprehension. Seeing nothing, hearing nothing, feeling nothing; there was only darkness.

Only the sensation beneath the soles of his feet was Subaru's everything right now, the entirety of the world. In the span between his right foot leaving the ground and his left foot touching down, he was entirely abandoned by the world. His left foot hit the ground. Contact with the world gained. His left foot lifted. Isolation. Right foot landed. Contact. Isolation. Contact. Isolation. The sensation of something squirming in his arms. Won't let go. Won't let her be isolated. Contact. Contact. Contact.

"—Gwah!"

The next instant, the darkness cleared with the abruptness of breaking through the surface of water. In his newly revealed vision, Subaru stood in the manor's hallway. Being in a hallway laid with a reddish carpet could only mean he had leapt out through the Forbidden Library's door. He had successfully bypassed Elsa, who had been standing at the doorway, and dashed straight out.

"Figured you'd do that--!"

He had reasoned that if Shamak were deployed right in front of her, a battle-hardened fighter like Elsa would circumvent the shroud of darkness to target Subaru and Beatrice on the other side. Taking advantage of that, Subaru dared to plunge straight through the darkness, passing right through the doorway Elsa had gone out of her way to open.

"I'd love to brag about pulling one over on her, but..."

He was not afforded the time to gloat. The duration of Shamak was unknown, but once Elsa realized Subaru and Beatrice were not on the other side, she would instantly turn around and pursue them. He roughly kicked the door shut with his foot, interrupting Door Crossing. It depended on Beatrice, but this should make it impossible to know if the Forbidden Library was still connected right in front of them.

"Beatrice! Connect the library to a different room from this one..."

"Betty is doing so without being told, I suppose. More importantly, you had better go around shutting open doors if you want to buy time, I suppose."

"Dammit, you're right!"

Responding to the spiteful words of the girl in his arms, Subaru roughly shut the door to the neighboring room and the one next to it as well. Before closing them, he took a quick peek inside, but found no conspicuous traces. He didn't know how much of Elsa's words he could trust, but—

What do I do, what do I do, what do I do, what do I do, what do I do, what do I do--

The questions were endless. Why was Elsa present here right now? In this loop, he had returned to the manor faster than ever before. Day six, day four, and this time, day two—on every single occasion, Elsa launched her attack on the very first day Subaru returned to the manor. Regarding this matter, a certain thought had already raised suspicions within Subaru. Between this occurrence and Elsa's statements upon stepping into the Forbidden Library, it was turning into a certainty. Yet if so, then why, all the more—

"Why couldn't you wait just one more day... no, just a few more hours!"

Once night fell, Petra was promised to be sent back to the village. Elsa's assault was a half-expected tragedy. Petra's figure losing her life in the midst of it last time remained burned into Subaru's eyes and refused to leave. That was why this time he had taken measures to distance her from that tragedy. And yet, malice approached at a speed exceeding his countermeasures, and fate did not let the young girl escape.

"Not yet. Why do I need to give up? There's still the possibility that she was spouting total bullshit. Right? As if I'd abandon hope...!"

"That is not hope, but lingering attachment, I suppose..."

"Shut up! Just keep quiet and let me carry you! She's planning to kill you too. She's a pervert who gets off on splitting people's bellies open with that knife to look at their guts!"

"To want to see a spirit's bowels is beyond bad taste, I suppose."

I agree, Subaru muttered under his breath. Just then, Beatrice slipped out from his arms and landed on the hallway floor. Brushing off the skirt of her dress,

"So, what do you intend to do, I suppose?"

"There's no 'what' about it. Confirming Petra's safety and checking on Rem are top priority. After that... in any case, I'm taking you with me and escaping the manor. Or what, are you gonna fight that woman for me?"

"...If that thing came to kill Betty, then Betty's wish might just be granted if I offer no resistance, I suppose."

"I had a feeling you'd say that, which is why I dragged you along by force. If you're still set on doing that, I'll haul you over my shoulder and carry you. What's it gonna be?"

Looking down at Beatrice across their height difference, Subaru pressed her for a decision. Not that he intended to give her a choice. If she stubbornly insisted on staying, he was fully prepared to drag her along by brute force. Subaru's intentions must have gotten through to her. Beatrice let out a sigh,

"Even if I cannot choose where to die, I would at least like to choose who kills me, I suppose."

"We'll have that talk properly once we change locations and calm down. Let's go!"

Calling out to Beatrice as she showed willingness to follow, he took her hand and began to run while pulling her along. As Subaru sprinted, Beatrice followed with pattering, tiny strides in her hard-to-walk-in dress. After advancing roughly ten meters, Subaru immediately could take it no longer,

"Aaagh, dammit! Hurry up and come here!"

Tugging her arm, he scooped the young girl entirely back into his arms. Beatrice's body was lighter than she looked. Whether this was related to her being a spirit or simply due to stunted growth, he didn't know.

"...Let Betty go, I suppose."

"If I match your speed, she'll catch up in a flash! It's faster if I carry you and run! Besides—"

Beatrice, cradled against his chest, voiced words of rejection. However, her hands were insecurely pinching the fabric at the chest of Subaru's clothes; catching sight of this, Subaru cut his words short, offering neither denial, affirmation, nor mention of it. For now, he felt that was fine.

"Anyway, before Elsa comes out... I've gotta find Petra and Rem!"

"Betty thought there was one more maid remaining in the manor, I suppose."

"Frederica... right now, it's better if we don't meet her. Probably."

Muddling the end of his sentence, Subaru shook his head while picturing the blonde maid. Beatrice furrowed her brow at Subaru's attitude, but said nothing. Right now, he wanted to avoid meeting Frederica face-to-face. It could wait until after confirming the safety of the other two. Meeting her and hearing what she had to say could wait until after that.

Because if Subaru's deductions were correct, that was surely what this meant—

※※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※

—Petra was laid out on the dining room table, alongside the tableware.

The white tablecloth was dyed a dark red, radiating out from Petra lying in the center. Time had already passed; the spilled blood had begun to discolour into a deep black, shifting into a hue that impressed the gruesome nature of the scene even more intensely upon any onlooker.

"Pet... ra..."

Staggering with unsteady footsteps, Subaru approached the table. Petra lay face up in the middle of the table. Her lovely expression was frozen into a tearful grimace of agony and terror, and her eyes were wide open in despair at whatever she had seen in her final moments. Spilled blood trailed from the corner of her open mouth, and the fatal wound, clear without even looking closely, was a deep gash from a blade that had split her abdomen wide open. Her maid uniform, which she had prettily swayed the skirt of before Subaru like a fairy—was torn vertically from beneath the chest down to the lower abdomen, the spilled blood and entrails making the young girl's body even lighter.

"――――ugh."

Something welled up from the back of his throat. Not nausea, but a sob. Feeling a searing heat behind his eyes, Subaru instinctively reached his hand toward Petra's face before it could overflow. Her face remained contorted and stiffened in fear. Making sure to at least close her eyes, Subaru draped his removed jacket over her body, just like before. How many times was this now that he had offered such superficial make-do for a Petra he couldn't save?

Knowing it would come to this, yet still unable to reach her, how many times would Subaru let this child die? Just because she got involved with Subaru, how many times did this child have to suffer like this?

"I'm sorry... I'm sorry, I'm so sorry... Petra..."

Even the words of apology he uttered sounded utterly hollow. Unable to rely on anyone, Petra had encountered a terrifying serial killer right here, and was cruelly tormented and slaughtered while calling out Subaru's name. His chest felt ready to crush from sorrow. His whole body burned hot enough to scorch with hatred. If hatred could kill, Subaru wouldn't be satisfied even after killing Elsa ten thousand times. What she did was that deeply sinful. Someday, without fail, he would make her pay.

"And for that very reason, I can't just let this loop end like this..."

"This loop...?"

"Just talking to myself. I don't wanna leave Petra like this, but... there's nothing I can do right now. Rem comes first. I'll take Rem, and we'll leave the manor as is. Can you not use Door Crossing?"

"It requires passing through the Forbidden Library, I suppose. And besides, the door is likely left wide open right now, I suppose."

"—I see."

The way to seal Door Crossing was simple, but that was all it took. If every door in the manor was left open, that alone narrowed down the candidates. And if the door to the Forbidden Library itself was propped open, it could shut off access from any door other than the one it was connected to at that moment. If it was kept wide open with something like a doorstop, even Beatrice would not be able to summon the Forbidden Library.

"Do you know where the Forbidden Library is connected to right now?"

"Of course, I suppose. —Currently, it is connected to a guest room on the third floor of the west wing, I suppose."

A guest room in the west wing meant it was some distance from the dining hall in the central wing. However, the fact that the door was open there meant Elsa had, at the very least, exited the Forbidden Library. With that killer's sense of smell, she would find Subaru and Beatrice before long. There was no time.

"No time to dawdle. Anyway, we gotta hurry to Rem's room...!"

Wiping away the tears welling up with the back of his hand, Subaru turned his head toward their destination. Rem slept in one of the servants' private quarters lined up in the east wing. If Elsa started on the west side, it was safe to judge that she hadn't reached there yet. However, that was only true for Elsa, and—

"There's a high chance Frederica is there..."

Knowing that Subaru placed immense importance on Rem's safety, she would easily deduce that Subaru would head there after evading Elsa's attack. In that case, there was a very strong possibility she had gotten ahead of him. Beatrice narrowed her eyes at Subaru, who rested a hand on his chin in thought.

"Betty has been wondering since earlier, I suppose. ...Are you suspecting that large maid, I suppose?"

"...I didn't want to suspect her, though."

To Beatrice's question, Subaru nodded weakly in affirmation. Elsa's assault on the Roswaal Manor—Subaru was half-convinced this was likely due to Frederica's betrayal.

This was the third time Subaru had faced Elsa at the Roswaal Manor. His past encounters were on the sixth and fourth days, both occurring after a few more days had passed. But this time, it was a return and encounter on the second day after arriving at the Sanctuary. Every single time, Elsa appeared as if lying in wait for Subaru's return. If there was a trick behind this, he had no choice but to suspect the existence of an insider.

"She's being guided so Elsa can enter on the very day I return to the manor. I was sure her going to the mountain cabin was part of that scheme, though..."

Subaru had initially suspected that Frederica was ushering in Elsa, who had been hiding out in the mountain cabin. For that reason, upon returning this time, he had asked Frederica, "Aren't you going to the mountain cabin?" while being prepared to arouse her suspicion. In Frederica's answer and attitude back then, there had been no signs of cover-up or suspicion. That was why he had tried to convince himself that it was merely his imagination, but.

"It's not like that was her only method... and it's not like I can see right through to what's inside her heart. If I got deceived, I wouldn't even know."

With her putting up a skillful facade, Subaru's vigilance had failed to perceive her true nature. On the contrary, she had likely realized Subaru was wary of her, causing her to act even more cautiously. It was Subaru's blunder, and as a result, it had claimed the young girl's life twice over. Petra falling victim was Subaru's responsibility for failing to get her away despite knowing the danger. Subaru was the one who had killed Petra.

"Petra was so fond of you too, dammit...!"

He recalled the sight of Petra looking up to her senior maid, calling her "Frederica-nee-sama." Even if it meant putting Petra—who had adored her that much—into the hands of a serial killer, did Frederica's heart feel no pain? Just what was she thinking, taking part in something like this?

"The clincher is the way she broke Beatrice's Door Crossing. Honestly, I'm amazed it could be broken by such a childish trick."

"...Betty never imagined it would be broken by such a method either, I suppose. It is hardly a method one would easily come up with, I suppose."

"Given enough time, I feel like even I could think of it... but that time is the problem. That's a method you wouldn't come up with unless you knew about your existence and the Door Crossing gimmick beforehand. There's no way Elsa conveniently thought of it on the spot. That has to be because she was tipped off internally too."

"You seem to be acquainted with that deranged woman, but where did you meet her, I suppose?"

"I had my gut sliced open a bit in the Royal Capital. ...Ah, she's the culprit from the very first time you treated me."

Thinking back, Elsa was involved in the circumstances behind Subaru being carried into the Roswaal Manor in the first place. At those words, Beatrice nodded in understanding with an "Ah." At any rate, even the time spent reflecting like this was precious right now.

"Regardless, we're going to Rem right now. If Frederica is there... can I count on you?"

"Do you not even have the backbone to protect the woman you love by yourself, I suppose? Just imagining being held as number one by a man like this makes Betty shudder, I suppose."

"If feelings alone could bring down a whale, I'd bring it down, but the world just isn't built that soft."

Beatrice's spiteful remark was half a sign of consent. And bringing about this bickering was likely Beatrice's own way of showing consideration for Subaru. She was managing things so as to steer Subaru's mind away from Petra's death, even if just a little. Unlike Subaru, who had remained oblivious to the sorrow Beatrice harbored and still couldn't offer a solution, Beatrice was far too clever. That was why he ended up relying on her.

"Let's go."

"Mm, understood, I suppose."

Reaching out his hand in a natural motion, Beatrice took Subaru's hand and slipped into his arms without objection. Scooping up her light frame, he began running again in the same posture they had used to get here. Even while knowing it was meaningless, Subaru did his best to deaden his footsteps as he headed toward the east wing. There was no sign of Elsa having gotten ahead of them; sprinting up the stairs, he reached the second and then third floor. Fearfully poking his head out into the hallway, he tried to check if there were any figures between him and the target room—

"――――"

Spotting a figure in front of the room, Subaru pulled his head back. As Beatrice in his arms looked up at him wordlessly, he nodded back, jutted his chin, and checked slowly once more—in front of the door, a tall figure was leaning against it. Even from a distance, the long golden hair and maid uniform were unmistakable. It was Frederica. Just as Subaru had predicted, she was standing motionless before the room where Rem slept, vigilantly waiting for Subaru.

"What do we do...? Pretend not to notice and call out to her innocently? If she hasn't met up with Elsa yet, before she realizes I suspect her..."

"If your earlier guess is correct, the moment that maid ushered in the enemy, she had no reason to curry favor with us, I suppose. Besides, when you last parted with that maid, you entered the Forbidden Library, I suppose. The moment you are outside, there is no excuse, I suppose."

"In that case, our only option is a frontal breakthrough."

Frederica's weapon—as far as Subaru had seen, her weapons were claw-like gauntlets equipped on both hands. Judging from her agility and her position as Garfiel's older sister, though he hadn't clearly confirmed her combat ability, there was no doubt she was vastly stronger than Subaru. What chance did he have in a head-on fight?

"If I blast a Shamak at the opening and pull off a surprise attack, maybe Beatrice's long-range magic gives us a shot...? Even if we can't incapacitate her, as long as we can force her to retreat..."

"A naive notion against a traitor, I suppose. Against an opponent coming to take your life, it is out of the question unless we approach them with that exact same resolve, I suppose."

Beatrice's cold remark. Even knowing she was entirely right, Subaru hesitated to do that. Even knowing the opponent had betrayed them, she was still someone he had interacted with warmly. As long as those times lingered in his memory, Subaru could not simply compartmentalize it so easily. Even if, in the end, Frederica had been deeply involved in Petra's death.

"Beatrice. I'll block her sight with Shamak, so give her a light blow."

"—With that naivety, you will soon suffer bitterly, I suppose."

"It's because there are things I want to ask her. Just consider it nothing more, nothing less."

Beatrice made a face as if wanting to say even at a time like this, but when Subaru bowed his head, she let out a small breath through her nose and said nothing. Admonishing himself for relying on her indulgence, Subaru took a deep breath and opened and closed his palm. While a faint sluggishness still lingered, if it was just one more Shamak—

"Hold out, body of mine. —Shamak!"

Tumbling out into the corridor, black smoke gushed forth from Subaru's outstretched palm before Frederica could turn around. Without missing its mark, it completely engulfed the space between Subaru and Frederica, creating the exact same situation as in the Forbidden Library. Immediately, his head grew heavy from the excessive mana expulsion, and Subaru dropped to his knees, his posture crumbling heavily. Stepping out beside him, Beatrice moved forward with both hands raised in front of her. In front of Beatrice as she muttered something, the atmosphere distorted, a hole opening in space, from which a stake cloaked in faint purple flames emerged. The violet stake hovered, its burning tip shimmering against the atmosphere. —Subaru nearly cried out at a type of magic that was clearly aimed at inflicting a fatal wound, but paying no heed to Subaru's reaction, Beatrice moved to drive that stake into the other side of the darkness—

"...How strange, I suppose."

Beatrice murmured softly, tilting her head. Subaru was bewildered by her reaction, but while keeping the stake suspended, Beatrice raised a finger on her outstretched right hand and swept it swiftly from right to left. With just that, the darkness of the Shamak Subaru had unleashed was cleared from the corridor. It was the result of Beatrice, whose mastery of Yin magic far surpassed Subaru's, intervening. Subaru was left speechless at how easily the result he had bought at the cost of his body's exhaustion was erased. However, any sentiment regarding that was instantly wiped away. Because Subaru also understood the reason Beatrice had halted her attack.

"――――"

He moved straight down the hallway cleared of darkness toward Frederica. Dragging his sluggish body from wringing out mana, Subaru approached her at a snail's pace. But there was no need for caution. There was no reaction from her. None at all. —For all eternity, there would never be another reaction from her.

"...Why."

Frederica stood frozen before Rem's bedroom. A kukri knife pierced her abdomen, pinning and impaling her against the room's door. Gauntlets were equipped on her limply hanging hands. Having engaged in combat, her clothing was disheveled in various places, making it readily apparent that it had been a fierce battle. Frederica's body was already cold, and it had been long since the soul had departed from her expression. Her face with stiffened cheeks held a look of bitter mortification, and from it, Subaru could infer how desperately she must have fought to defend this door to the death. In other words, Frederica had struggled frantically to prevent the assailant from entering this room.

There was no reason for a person who had guided the attacker inside to do such a thing.

"After suspecting her all this time... this is how it ends?"

Covering his face with his hands, Subaru accepted the hard-to-accept reality before his eyes. Frederica, having sustained blade wounds all over her body and lost her life. Facing this sight, how could he possibly say she was an insider who had betrayed them? Did it end up like this after a falling-out among comrades? Having to go so far as to frame Frederica as a villain was nothing more than self-satisfaction to ease the guilt of having suspected her. He had to admit it.

"—Was I... wrong?"

Frederica had not betrayed them. She had fought desperately to protect Rem, and met her end. Judging from the coldness of her body, there was even a possibility that she had died before Petra in the dining hall. There was certainly no time for her to guide Elsa to the Forbidden Library.

"...Rem is..."

A gaping void opened inside his skull, leaving him unable to gather his thoughts. Managing somehow to assemble his chaotic thoughts, the first thing Subaru thought of was the beloved girl sleeping inside the room Frederica had stood firm to shield behind her back.

Frederica's body, where rigor mortis had begun, was heavy, and moving her while the blade was driven through her into the door was arduous work. To avoid damaging her body any further, he had to exercise the utmost caution. And yet, his fingertips, his heart, were racing with impatience. Yet, as if betraying Subaru's consciousness,

"—Found you at last."

From the end of the corridor, a black shadow emerged as if gliding. Swaying her braid, Elsa held a low posture, resting a hand on the floor while turning eyes greedily dripping with murderous intent toward them. Trapped whole by her gaze, Subaru froze. Not missing that opening, Elsa's shadow closed the distance down the corridor in an instant. A soundless advance. It was unmistakably nothing less than the approach of "Death."

"If you think you can kill us so easily, you are sorely mistaken, I suppose."

However, there was an existence that obstructed Elsa's advance. Beatrice lightly raised her hand, aiming the violet stake—which she had summoned earlier and kept on standby without dispelling—at Elsa. The stake whose launch had been aborted earlier shouted with glee upon finding an outlet for its destructive power.

"For such a little girl, you play with quite an extreme toy, don't you?"

"Whether it amounts to a child's toy or not, you had best test it with your own body, I suppose."

Elsa smiled in the face of the atrocious magic. Overwriting that smile with a sneer, the violet stake was unleashed from Beatrice's hand. The velocity of the fired stake exceeded the speed of the volley of icicles in Emilia's magic that he had seen before. Though a single shot, the stake approaching from the front traveled on a collision course directly toward Elsa, who was heading their way at equal speed—yet Elsa completely dodged it in a posture that laid her flat on her stomach, her chest touching the floor.

"Too bad. Your speed and aim were too soft to pierce the target."

Even as she felt the deadly weapon graze the back of her head, Elsa muttered with an attitude lacking the slightest trace of fear, aiming for Beatrice with the reverse-gripped kukri knife. A dull glint closed in on Beatrice's body, threatening to slice her small frame clean in two—

"The thoughtless one is you, human, I suppose."

Just before it did, Beatrice clenched her open palm. Right then, the violet stake swelled up. The stake, which had missed its mark and was on a trajectory to disappear down the hallway, expanded and burst like a popping balloon behind Elsa, whose movements had halted.

"—What is this!"

The fragments of the burst stake became miniature stakes, surrounding Elsa in all directions. Even calling them small stakes, their size matched the index finger of Subaru's hand. Countless numbers of them filled the air, pointing their tips at Elsa. And,

"The punishment for vandalizing Betty's Forbidden Library, I suppose. —Torn to shreds, you had best meet your end, I suppose."

Triggered by that merciless declaration, the violet stakes were fired.
