Arc 1 Chapter 2: "'Don't Get Ahead of Yourself,' Said God"

Subjected to the men's contemptuous and mocking glares, Subaru was assessing them right back. They looked to be in their mid-twenties. Dressed in filthy rags, they radiated an air of vulgarity that seemed to manifest directly on their faces. They didn't appear to be Demi-Humans, but there was no way they were good people either.

"Crap, a forced event triggered."

Facing the smirking men, Subaru wiped his face and hastily scrambled to his feet. They were clearly muggers—and given the world building here, there was a real chance they wouldn't just steal his belongings, but his life as well. Mission 1: 'Repel the Muggers' had begun. Clear condition: defeat all enemies. Defeat condition: Subaru's death. Something like that.

A chill ran down his spine, but Subaru slapped his own cheeks to brush it off. Taking the offensive was his best option right now; hesitation meant risking his life. Decisiveness—that was one thing he had confidence in.

"Besides, I was summoned to another world! If this follows the standard overpowered protagonist pattern, maybe I'm insanely strong in this world. What if gravity is like one-tenth of my original world...? Yeah, thinking about it like that, my body actually feels lighter! I might totally be able to do this!"

"The hell is he muttering over there?"

"Guess he doesn't get what kind of situation he's in. Maybe we oughta teach him."

Compared to Subaru's soaring excitement, the men's reactions were rather cold. Undeterred by their attitude, Subaru puffed out his chest—

"Whoa, hold on, acting tough won't last you long. Let me tell you, a guy like me fantasizes about getting cornered by thugs in back alleys like this all the time! I'll mow you down one by one and turn you into my fuel for tomorrow, you little EXP fodder!"

"I don't get a damn word you're saying, but I know you're making fun of us. We're gonna beat you to death."

"That's... my line!"

With that declaration, Subaru launched a preemptive strike before the men could even move. Diving inside their guard, he threw a right straight with all his might. It scored a direct hit right on the lead man's nose—and the impact against his front teeth drew blood from Subaru's own knuckles.

—I just punched a guy for the first time! And damn, punching someone hurts way more than I thought!

Although he had simulated it endlessly, this was his first actual fight. The punched man collapsed onto the ground, unmoving. Riding the emotional rush, Subaru leapt toward another startled man.

"Take this! A high kick made possible by my post-bath stretching routines!"

"Guh!"

The tip of his arching foot slammed into the side of the man's head, throwing him against the wall and leaving him writhing in agony. With his surprisingly effective performance, Subaru's belief in his 'overpowered protagonist status' was fast turning into certainty.

"I knew it! I'm set to be super strong in this world! Adrenaline's pumping hard, victory is mine—"

High on confidence, Subaru spun around and crouched, ready to beat down the last man. However, the moment he caught a glint of a knife in the last man's hand, his crouched body sank lower—

"I'm sorry, I was completely in the wrong! Please forgive me, just spare my life—!"

Dogeza. The ultimate yet lowest display of the Japanese spirit, indicating absolute surrender to one's opponent. Where his earlier excitement had gone, he couldn't say; listening to the sound of all the blood draining from his body, Subaru desperately rubbed his head against the ground to beg for mercy.

Because knives are a hard no. If he got stabbed, it was over, and it wasn't like he had any anti-knife skills.

Before he realized it, the two men he thought he'd knocked out with single hits were back on their feet. One was clutching his nose as blood trickled down, and the other was shaking his dizzy head, but otherwise they looked fine.

"Huh?! What do you mean my overpowered attacks barely dealt any damage?! What happened to the summoned-to-another-world tropes?!"

"The hell are you blabbering about?! You've got some nerve doing that to us!"

A boot stomped down from above onto his face as he bowed, scraping his forehead against the pavement and drawing blood. From there, kicks rained down on his face, and blows battered his body as he curled into a desperate ball.

—Crap, this hurts like hell. I might actually die, no joke.

Having been attacked first, the thugs showed no mercy. On top of that, unlike back in his original world, there was no guarantee these delinquents wouldn't kill him. There was a very real chance they would beat him to death right here. Should he just go wild and go down fighting? Before taking all this damage, that might have been an option, but now that he was one step away from critical injury, it was doubtful he could even land a single blow. That said, dying like this was unacceptable too.

"Don't move, dumbass!"

"Ow-ow-ow-ow-ow! Ouch, ouch, ouch!!"

He tried to get up, but a heavy boot stomped down hard on his palm, wringing a scream out of him. Spittle flying as he yelled, the man's face flushed red with rage. Subaru saw him shift his grip on the knife into a reverse hold.

"I'm gonna make sure you can't move before stripping you clean. Trying to pull that funny stuff with us..."

"I-If you're after valuables, it's honestly a waste of time. I'm completely broke...!"

"Then your weird clothes or shoes or whatever'll do fine. You can be food for the giant rats in this back alley."

Ah, so there are rats in this world too. Sounding like some trash mob monster.

Watching the knife about to swing down, that fleeting bit of reality-evasion crossed his mind. No flashing lights of his past life passed before his eyes. No phenomenon of time slowing down. Just a sudden end, like a thread snapping—and then:

"Hey, out of the way, out of the way! You guys over there are totally in my way!"

Shouting in a panicked voice, someone came running into the alley. Following the startled glances of the thugs, Subaru raised his eyes with what little mobility his battered body allowed. A young girl darted across his vision.

She was a petite girl with swaying, medium-length blonde hair. She had determined eyes and a mischievous snaggletooth peeking out. Her features had a slightly cheeky look, but taken at face value for her age, she could be considered cute. Dressed in filthy, worn-out clothes, the girl had stumbled right into the scene of an impending mugging and murder.

At this impeccably timed arrival, the dying embers of Subaru's hope struck a victory pose inside him. This was it; this was the development he'd been waiting for. By all narrative logic, this girl would be full of righteous chivalry and rescue the flickering, faint flame of Subaru's life—

"Looks like something crazy is going on, but sorry! I'm in a rush! Stay strong!"

"Wait, huuh?! For real?!"

But alas, that hope shattered into pieces in an instant. The girl, whose eyes met his, raised a hand to him apologetically, maintaining her momentum as she sped through the narrow alley. She ran right past the thugs toward the back, where a dead end should have been. Striking a wooden board leaning against the dead-end wall, she lightly grabbed onto a ledge and vanished over the roof before anyone could register what happened.

Once the girl disappeared from view, silence naturally fell over the alley. She had swept through like a sudden typhoon. While everyone present was stunned, the reality remained that Subaru's dire situation hadn't changed at all once he snapped back to his senses.

"Any chance that just knocked the edge off your mood and made you change your minds?!"

"If anything, that buzzkill ruined my mood even more. Don't think you're getting off easy."

The glint in the knifeman's eyes was deadly serious, making Subaru realize that this time, it really was over. His hand was still pinned under the man's heel; even if he tried to stand, he would be a beat too late. Naturally, resignation washed over his heart.

Was it going to end? Here, without having accomplished anything? It was true he could hardly claim to have lived a proper life, but even so, meeting an end like this was far too cruel. A meaningless death where, if he were to ask what he had done to deserve this, the only reply would be: You did nothing. It wasn't the pain. It was something else that brought tears welling up in his eyes. It wasn't a matter of being afraid of it ending, or hating the thought of dying. He simply couldn't stand the thought of ending up like this—empty, having achieved nothing.

"—That's far enough, villains."
