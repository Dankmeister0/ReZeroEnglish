One Day II Part 2: "Young Souls and Those Who Watch Over Them"

When it came to the matter of siblings, it had been established that Ram was of no help at all, and yet also that she was still trying her best to be a "good big sister." With that revelation, however, matters made no further progress, and only time passed by.

"Still, don't you think Ram's being a little coldhearted about it?"

"Don't say that. Ram has her own reasons. ...She's known those two much longer than we have, so maybe that's just the difference between us."

Walking down the manor's hallway, Emilia gave a wry smile at Subaru, who pouted with his lips pursed.

They'd just left Rem's bedroom, where Ram had been, and were now walking along holding a little post-mortem of the discussion. What surfaced in Subaru's mind was what Ram had said just before they parted ways.

"Garf and Frederica's relationship? You may as well just leave it be. They're not children... well, Garf is a brat, but he's not thoughtless. Even if his thoughts miss the mark more often than not. Whatever's between the two of them, the two of them will sort out on their own."

It was a rather merciless assessment, coming as it did about someone who held such affection for her.

Then again, to Ram, Garfiel was perhaps something like a younger brother. A cute little crush—well, that description falls a bit short of feelings so rough and passionate.

It really was rough going, falling for someone with walls as impenetrable as Ram's.

"—? What? What's wrong?"

"Nah, it's nothing. Just reflecting on how Garfiel's not the only one stuck facing a high wall."

"—?"

Emilia tilted her head, her pretty face puzzled.

The fact that she couldn't connect his gaze to the meaning of his words at all was maddening. Though the fact that he could forgive her for it anyway was, he supposed, just a symptom of being smitten.

"Anyway, if Ram's a dead end... who should we ask next, I wonder."

"Eh? You're still not giving up?"

"Of course not. Nothing's been solved, and giving up just because I stumbled on my very first step wouldn't have a shred of manliness to it. You want to see those two patch things up too, don't you, Emilia-tan?"

"Well, yes, but... Ram's the foremost authority on those two, and if she says so, maybe that really is for the best."

"Maybe time would solve it if we left it alone, but don't forget it took Garfiel and the others ten years just to get from parting ways to now. If it takes another ten years to make up, I don't think I could stand it. I want to give them a push from the outside and get them on good terms already."

To Emilia, who seemed subtly swayed by Ram's words, Subaru insisted on pressing forward with the plan. Of course, part of it was his genuine wish to see the two reconcile for certain—but part of it, too, was that he didn't want to let slip this situation of throwing himself into something together with Emilia.

After all, this time, no matter how Subaru handled things, there was no chance of it escalating into bloodshed. Could there be a lighter feeling than pouring time into trial and error like this?

"What's wrong? You're suddenly grinning..."

"Ah, well, I was just thinking how happy it is to fret over something without risking my life. Isn't it amazing! No matter how it turns out, nobody dies, and there's not even any blood."

"Subaru..."

Looking at Subaru, who flashed a thumbs-up and a gleaming grin, Emilia's face turned deeply pitying.

Wondering if he'd said something strange, Subaru replayed his own words in his head—then was struck with dismay at how grim, how ominous, and how pathetically small a wish it revealed.

"Th-that's not it, Emilia-tan."

"It's all right. It's been hard on you, hasn't it. I'm sorry I couldn't understand sooner. Here, why don't you go back to your room today and get some rest..."

"No good, this is the reaction I get when she doesn't understand at all!"

Subaru raised his voice at Emilia, who was gazing at him with such gentle, sympathetic eyes.

And it was just as the two of them were having this exchange—

"...I thought I heard something noisy, and here you two are, doing who knows what, I suppose."

The voice was exasperated, tinged with a sigh. Turning toward it, Subaru found a girl in an extravagant dress standing there—Beatrice.

The girl, who had been walking down the hall, looked over the faces of Subaru and Emilia as they stood there talking, and creased her lovely brow.

"How wonderful that the two of you get along so well, I suppose. Your bickering was loud enough that I thought I could hear it from the other side of the manor, I suppose."

"That's a whole lot of sarcasm right there. If you're feeling left out, just say so honestly. Come rack your brains together with us over our deep and profound discussion topic."

"Who said anything about feeling left out, I suppose! Don't go putting words in my mouth, I suppose!"

Crossing her short arms in a haughty manner, Beatrice flushed and fumed. At an attitude so perfectly "her," Subaru and Emilia couldn't help but exchange a glance and laugh.

About a week had passed since Subaru and Beatrice had entered into a Contract with one another and formed a partnership as Spiritual Arts User and Spirit. That said, it wasn't as though their relationship had changed all that dramatically.

As ever, Subaru teased Beatrice, and Beatrice overreacted to it. Theirs was a relationship that endlessly repeated this same unchanging exchange.

Though, with the loss of the Forbidden Archive, there had come to be more occasions of Beatrice simply out and about, and now and then, as though suddenly remembering to, she'd taken to holding Subaru's hand and staying by his side more often.

"You say all that, but you came here because you missed me and wanted to hold my hand, didn't you? Oh my, aren't you just precious."

"Stop twisting things as though there's some strange intent behind Betty's actions, I suppose. The reason Betty touches you—Subaru, I mean—is due to unavoidable circumstances, I suppose."

"The way you start to say 'you' and then correct yourself is super cute."

"Subaru."

Before Beatrice's face could turn bright red, Emilia's warning cut in first. Subaru stuck out his tongue in apology to Emilia, then held out his hand to Beatrice.

She touched her fingers lightly to his offered palm, as if pinching it, and then, as though reconsidering, timidly took hold of it properly with her small hand. That was Beatrice's usual habit.

The feel of another's small fingers against his palm—ticklish, itchy in a way—was itself the fruit of what Subaru had so desperately sought.

This time, however, no such response came. Beatrice only stared down at Subaru's outstretched palm, hesitating.

"What's wrong? I do wash my hands properly after the bathroom, you know."

"That wasn't remotely what I was worried about, and now that concern is going to keep flickering through my mind from now on, which makes me feel awful, I suppose! And that's not it, anyway..."

Narrowing her eyes at Subaru's unnecessary remark, Beatrice glanced warily behind her. As he tilted his head, puzzled at this behavior, the answer came from down the hall.

"Beatrice-chan, where'd you go~?"

From beyond the hall—around the corner—came a voice searching for the girl standing right there before them. It was a high, girlish voice, full of fondness and affection.

And yet, the very one being called for jumped, her shoulders leaping,

"Myu!"

letting out a sound like some small animal, before her eyes darted about and she dove into the nearest room. Then, poking her face out through the gap in the door at Subaru and Emilia, who stared wide-eyed,

"Tell her Betty was never here, I suppose. I'm counting on you, I suppose."

"Hey."

"Counting on you, I suppose."

With that reminder, the door shut without a sound. Subaru shrugged, baffled, and beside him Emilia's brow furrowed in equal confusion. Then—

"Ah! Subaru!"

rounding the corner, the girl who appeared lit up at once and came trotting over.

Her still-new maid uniform's long hem swaying as she came at a light run, it was the reddish-brown-haired girl, Petra.

Petra, too, was one of those now taking shelter in this manor following the burning of Roswaal Manor. For his part, Subaru had urged her to return to the village, given the danger of continuing to be involved—but she hadn't listened to a word of it.

Even now, staying at this manor, she was diligently honing her maid skills by helping out with chores alongside Frederica, just as before. He thought her a highly driven, dependable girl.

As though to underscore Subaru's private thoughts, Petra, having run up, bowed politely to Emilia and said,

"I apologize, Emilia-sama. I'm sorry for raising my voice like that."

—such was her manner.

The girlish air she'd shown toward Subaru just a moment before vanished, replaced by conduct so conscious of her role as a maid that it was Emilia who ended up blinking in surprise.

"Eh, um, it's fine. Don't worry. Thou art pardoned."

"Emilia-tan, 'thou art pardoned'?"

Emilia's limited experience with being shown such deference was, given her position, something of a task for the future.

In any case, setting aside that small, heartwarming exchange for the moment—

"So, what's up, Petra? Something happen?"

"No, it's not that, but... I finished my work, so I thought I'd go spend some time with Beatrice-chan. But I've been looking and I can't find her."

"Beatrice-chan... huh."

Emilia's breath caught, and then she pressed a hand to her mouth, trying to hold back laughter.

Glancing sidelong at her, Subaru fought the same battle against a smirk. Of all people, Beatrice, who ordinarily put on such haughty, high-and-mighty airs, was being called "Beatrice-chan" by a girl like Petra.

The first time Subaru had witnessed the two of them together, he'd burst out laughing.

"Emilia-sama, is something wrong? Did I say something strange?"

"No, it's nothing. It's just—I found it super-duper a little bit funny."

"Super-duper, but only a little?"

Now and then, the part of Petra that hadn't quite shed the mannerisms of a young girl showed through. Smiling at her, Emilia shot Subaru a sidelong glance that asked, "What are we going to do?"

At that silent exchange, Subaru pretended to mull it over,

"Well, let's see. Beatrice, huh. She's such an attention-seeker, so I bet fussing over her is just endlessly fun for you, Petra?"

"Yeah, that's right. Beatrice-chan's not honest with herself at all, so being with her is really, really cute. I don't think a kid like that should be left alone."

"Why do you think that?"

"Because she seems like she'd get lonely. I can't just leave her be."

At Petra's smart, simple answer, Subaru nodded. "Yeah, that's right."

Whatever fine words he'd spent so much time on, in the end, the reason he'd dragged Beatrice out of the Forbidden Archive boiled down to much the same thing. Children had a knack for cutting to the truth. Or rather—maybe it meant that he and Beatrice had been operating on the same childlike logic all along.

"Even you, Subaru—why are you laughing?"

"I'm not making fun of you. I just think you're too much of a genius, Petra."

"Really? Ehehe."

Subaru nodded, patting the head of a Petra who clearly didn't mind being praised one bit.

Then he reached for the doorknob of the door behind him and threw it open in one motion.

"Wagh!?"

There was a hard thud, and the loli who'd been eavesdropping on the other side went flying.

Tearing up, the loli sat back up, her forehead flushed bright red where the door had struck it.

"What were you doing?"

"You're the one who should answer for what you just did to me, I suppose! It hurts! It hurts a ton, I suppose! And on top of the pain, you broke our promise too..."

"I never promised anything, and I never said 'leave it to me' in the first place. After careful consideration of whose side I wanted to take, I decided siding with Petra would be more fun..."

"You just said 'fun'! You said it, I suppose! The absolute worst, I suppose!"

Beatrice protested, rubbing her forehead, while Subaru covered his ears and pretended not to hear. Then a girl slipped smartly into the middle of their exchange. Meeting Petra's eyes, Beatrice opened her mouth, her ringlets trembling.

"Ah, um, that is, this isn't what it looks like, I suppose... I—I wasn't hiding or anything..."

"Now, that won't do, Beatrice-chan. You'll get scolded if you play hide-and-seek in someone else's house, you know? I get wanting to play at your age, but..."

"Wha—!? Th-that's quite enough treating Betty like a child, I suppose! To begin with, Betty, unlike her appearance, is properly... um, properly—"

"Properly?"

"...Never mind, I suppose."

At Petra's question, Beatrice's words trailed off weakly before she raised the white flag entirely. This drew a look of surprise from Emilia, and even Subaru squinted at the unfamiliar sight.

What made the relationship between Beatrice and Petra so amusing was this mysterious upper hand Petra held.

Ordinarily, Beatrice kept up a bold, haughty front no matter who she was dealing with. The exceptions had been Puck, and more recently Subaru—but into that short list, Petra had come charging with a flourish.

For some reason, Beatrice alone couldn't manage her usual attitude when it came to Petra. She herself didn't seem to understand the reason for it, and Subaru had seen this scene play out many times now: Beatrice, looking utterly unconvinced, letting Petra take hold of her hand anyway.

Whatever was going on inside Beatrice, from the outside it was simply two young girls—both already showing hints of the beauties they'd grow into—holding hands, which made for a heartwarming sight.

Petra looked subtly the elder of the two, and being treated like a little sister by her was perhaps one more reason Beatrice struggled to handle the situation gracefully.

"Come on, let's go. We shouldn't get in the way of Subaru and Emilia-sama's work. Besides, Clind-oniisama prepared something sweet and said to share it with Beatrice-chan. We can eat it in the dining hall."

"A-all right, I suppose. I understand, so... there's no need to pull so hard, I suppose."

Beatrice, being led by the hand out of the room, shot them a troubled look, but Subaru answered it mercilessly with a thumbs-up. Seeing Emilia give a small wave beside him, Beatrice stuck out her tongue and, still wearing an angry expression, let herself be dragged off by Petra.

Whatever unavoidable circumstances required her to touch Subaru, it seemed, would have to wait for later.

Watching the girl doing the taking and the girl being taken with warm amusement, Emilia touched a finger to her lips.

"Somehow, that's super surprising. I never would've guessed Beatrice was weak to Petra like that."

"Right? I was floored the first time too. I don't say anything about it since it's cute to watch. Besides, I think Petra's got the right idea, through and through."

"That she'd get lonely if left alone?"

"I could fuss over her round the clock myself, sure, but then there'd be no point in her leaving the Archive. Even when it comes to making memories, if we're doing it anyway, the more people showing up in the pages of her album, the better."

After all, there were four hundred years' worth of blank pages that needed filling. Filling her memory album with nothing but Subaru alone would be sort of funny, but far too much of a one-note joke.

Better that all sorts of people, with all sorts of expressions, burn themselves into her memories. Subaru figured he could just spend his time beside Beatrice as she clicked the shutter—sometimes as the subject, sometimes just barely cropped out of frame.

"Subaru... every once in a while, I think you're really, super cool for stuff like that."

"Eh, what, seriously? What's gotten into you, did some jackpot mode suddenly kick in!?"

"It really is only every once in a while, though."

Subaru scratched his cheek and grumbled "aw, come on" at Emilia's suppressed laughter.

Still, joking or not, there was no way praise from Emilia wouldn't lift his spirits. He wanted to hold onto this feeling and remember it every time he teased Beatrice for lending a hand. Which meant, for that very purpose, he'd have to keep teasing her.

"Feels a bit like the ends and means have swapped places, but that happens sometimes. Well, now that we've seen a heartwarming scene, let's just..."

"Indeed, it was a heartwarming scene. Two girls with lovely souls, walking hand in hand, laughing together... a vision of beauty upon this earth. A feast for the eyes."

"Eek!?"

At the voice that addressed them just as the two of them, pulling themselves together, were about to move on, Emilia let out an involuntary shriek. That's how sudden and unexpected the speaker's appearance was.

If the appearance itself was unexpected, so was its location. The man was standing so close behind Subaru that his breath practically brushed the back of Subaru's neck, and he'd slipped into the conversation with an utterly unbothered face.

"My apologies for startling you so. Though, I confess, I could not suppress the servant's heart within me that wished to offer up a surprise. Misfire."

"C-Clind-san?"

"Yes, Clind. I do hope I haven't offended you. How do you find yourself? Contrition."

The one bowing at so perfect an angle as he spoke was a slender, tall, beautiful young man.

His blue hair was trimmed to just brush his eyebrows, and he wore a monocle over his left eye. His crisply starched black butler's uniform seemed to perform to its fullest, as if delighted to be worn by him, and the refined bearing behind his every motion made one's own spine straighten unconsciously.

Wilhelm, too, was the sort of man whose mere presence made you straighten your posture, but what Clind gave off was of a different order from the aura that swordsman-turned-fiend radiated.

If Wilhelm's presence called to mind a finely honed blade, then standing before Clind was like standing before the flow of pure, clear water. Physical beauty and conceptual beauty were two different things entirely—even if both, alike, brought a stillness to the heart.

"Suddenly standing right behind me like that, you've got a mean streak, Clind-san... I thought my heart was gonna stop."

"In that event, we on our part are prepared to devote every ounce of our strength toward reviving you. So please, Natsuki-sama, do not trouble yourself with worry. Time of death."

"Huh!? That means I didn't make it, right!?"

Clind, without ever breaking his elegant bearing, was giving answers that had gone completely off the rails.

Yet one couldn't judge his character or capability from this behavior alone. As his bearing suggested, Clind possessed extraordinary skill as a servant, and held the position of steward overseeing all the household staff here at House Miload.

Despite his youth, he carried himself with the dignified confidence of one who managed an entire household.

And beyond that, it was said he could also wield a sword when the occasion called for it. His skill was such that, upon their first meeting, Garfiel had snorted and picked a fight, muttering, "This guy... he's damn good..." Though his invitation to spar had been politely declined.

And yet, for all his outstanding ability, Clind had a number of flaws in contrast. One of them was this streak of mild mischief he'd just displayed. And then—

"Is Petra causing you any trouble, Clind-san? We kind of forced her into your work, and that worries me a little."

"No need to worry at all. For a girl her age, Petra is exceptionally accomplished. Both her ability and her beauty—I cannot help but look forward to her future. Envy."

"Is that so, well, that's good—"

"However, in time, Petra's limbs will lengthen, and she will grow into an adult... That, I find, is a source of the deepest regret. Regret."

Clind rubbed the bridge of his nose, pronouncing this with a face of utterly sincere regret.

This was the other of his flaws—and the greatest one at that.

Clind harbored an intense interest in young girls like Beatrice and Petra.

To put it bluntly, he had a taste for young girls—one might as well call him a Lolicon.

"Is something the matter, Natsuki-sama? You're looking at me the way one looks at a criminal, or someone on the verge of becoming one. Have I committed some blunder? Confirmation."

"The fact that you jumped straight to that exact conclusion makes me think you're pretty self-aware about it. I'm not really into the younger stuff myself, but even so, girls that age seem like a bit much..."

"It would seem Natsuki-sama labors under a rather severe misunderstanding. Wry smile."

Despite saying "wry smile," Clind didn't so much as crack a grin. Clouding his handsome features with a look of lament, he adjusted his monocle and turned to face Subaru fully.

"May I explain? The reason I hold Annerose-sama, Petra, and Beatrice-sama in such high regard is not because they are young. It is because I am drawn to the youthfulness and promise of their Souls. To be drawn to an innocent, pure Soul is only natural. And it simply happens that the possessors of such Souls tend, more often than not, to be of a young age. In short: misunderstanding."

"Huh... is that right."

Clind had denied it all with quite the speech, though Subaru had let half of it go in one ear and out the other. But that attitude evaporated at Clind's next words.

Looking at Emilia, who stood beside Subaru, he prefaced his remark with, "As proof of this," and continued,

"my eyes perceive in Emilia-sama the same richness of Soul as in those I mentioned before. Purity."

"Me?"

"Clind-san, you're amazing!?"

Emilia tilted her head, puzzled, while Subaru could do nothing but marvel at the sharpness of Clind's eye.

That Emilia's mind was, in truth, far younger than her appearance suggested was something no one could have noticed without knowledge of her origins and upbringing. And yet Clind's eye had seen straight through it, discerning that Emilia was, at heart, a loli.

A Lolicon's sense of smell truly was fearsome, and Subaru could hardly be blamed for gaping in astonishment.

"Then, does that mean Ryuzu-san is maybe..."

"She is an exceedingly lovely person in appearance, but her Soul has matured completely. To speak of 'future promise' to someone who has already settled so firmly upon her way of living would be an overreach for one as inexperienced as myself. An outrage."

"Insane..."

Subaru gave a genuine gasp of admiration at eyes keen enough to see through even a loli-baba.

Emilia, meanwhile, having witnessed this whole exchange, didn't seem to take much interest in Clind's razor-sharp predilections.

"By the way, Clind-san, there's something I wanted to ask you..."

"Yes, please, ask me anything at all. Query."

"Frederica worked here both before and after working at Roswaal's mansion, didn't she?"

"...That's right. Affirmative."

For just a moment, Subaru could have sworn Clind hesitated, and his brow knit.

Emilia, too, blinked at that hesitation, but carried the topic forward regardless.

"In that case, have you and Frederica known each other a long time, too?"

"Frederica and I have known one another for ten years now—when the margrave brought Frederica to House Miload, I myself had only just begun working here as a manservant. I suppose our acquaintance dates back that far. Old friends."

"I knew it! Well then, I want to ask you a bit about Frederica. What she likes, what she dislikes, that sort of thing—I want to give her and Garfiel a chance to make up."

"A chance for reconciliation, is it. Deliberation."

Clind put a hand to his chin, deep in thought. Even his pondering looked like a painting, and Subaru, fiddling with his tracksuit zipper, lamented the gulf in aesthetics with a muttered, "Guess handsome guys get away with being Lolicons..."

After a full minute lost in thought, Clind nodded and let out a small breath,

"For Annerose-sama's next month-birthday, let us make chicken the main course. Planning."

"What about Frederica!?"

"...Ah, forgive me. Whenever I try to think about her, my brain simply refuses to cooperate, no matter what. This, I'm afraid, is beyond even my own discretion. My apologies."

"Could it be that you and Frederica aren't actually on good terms, Clind-san?"

"Not at all. Denial."

Clind shook his head at Emilia's concerned question, denying it.

"She's a superb maid—reliable, quick, and considerate in her work. There is but a single point on which I have any complaint at all: that she ruins, through her appearance, the very duty a maid holds to beautifully and elegantly adorn womanhood. Setting that aside, I have nothing whatsoever to say against her. No connection."

"Um? I feel like I just heard an opinion that was basically a giant lump of prejudice, but was that just my imagination? Right, Subaru?"

"No, it's not so much your imagination, Emilia-tan, as it is Clind-san's fault."

Clind's merciless prejudice, it seemed, stemmed from Frederica's appearance. Certainly, Subaru himself had been startled by the impact of that first impression, but for all that, Frederica was diligent and possessed a personality full of feminine grace. As far as he could tell, there was nothing wrong with her as a woman aside from her looks.

"I sense that Natsuki-sama has just arrived at the very same conclusion as I. Perceived."

"Is that true, Subaru?"

"I'm literally in the middle of trying to quit my habit of hunting for people's flaws, so cut it out, would you!? Your looks are my super critical weakness, Emilia-tan!"

"That's not even what we're talking about, honestly... But, um, thanks."

Her cheeks faintly coloring at the compliment on her looks, Emilia murmured her thanks.

It was refreshing to see that compliments, which had once had no effect on her whatsoever, now seemed to work at least a little on the Emilia who had returned from completing the Trials of the Tomb.

With Puck's habit of fashion-checking her gone, Emilia now dressed herself day to day, and it seemed she was experimenting quite a bit with her own sense of style.

Though when she'd once wavered over whether to chop off her long silver hair, everyone who caught wind of it had united as one to talk her out of it.

In any case, it didn't seem like they'd be getting any useful opinion about Frederica out of Clind. Wondering if they'd already hit a dead end, Subaru and Emilia let out deep sighs.

Watching this, Clind picked up with, "From what I can surmise..." and continued,

"—judging by your earlier conversation, it seems you wish to see the sibling relationship between Frederica and Garfiel-sama improved. Inference."

"Yes, that's right. But neither Subaru nor I have any siblings, so we can't really think of what to do. That's why we've been asking around..."

"Given that Frederica has no faults besides her appearance, my own opinion is that any problem unconnected to her looks would resolve itself if simply left alone—but I suspect that answer wouldn't satisfy you. So, allow me to offer one. Suggestion."

"A suggestion?"

At Clind raising a single finger, Subaru and Emilia tilted their heads in perfect unison.

At the sight of the two of them mirroring each other, Clind, for the first time that day, let his lips curve into a smile, and said,

"If their relationship troubles you so, then surely it would be best to hear from the person closest to the both of them. Wouldn't the true foremost authority be not Ram, but someone else entirely? My opinion."

"Foremost authority... ah!"

At Clind's words, Emilia clapped her hands, her eyes widening at a realization that had come far too late. Beside her, Subaru arrived at the same conclusion—but there was something that bothered him even more.

And it was this:

"That thing about Ram being the foremost authority—I feel like we said that a good while back. Just how long have you been eavesdropping on us, Clind-san?"

"I am, after all, the steward entrusted with House Miload's affairs and its peace. Declaration."

It was an answer that both answered the question and didn't, somehow.

Without waiting to see Subaru grimace, Clind bowed reverently at the waist.

It was a gesture so perfectly that of a servant that it overwhelmed anyone watching.

With the words he'd been about to say cut off, all Subaru could do was pull a sour face.
