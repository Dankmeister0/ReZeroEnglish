Arc 5 Chapter 76: "Battle for Priestella Result 3"

The Sin Archbishop of "Wrath." The bandaged monstrosity who called herself the wife of the madman that was "Sloth." Sirius Romanée-Conti was bound head to toe in the very chains that had once been her weapon, held captive and unable to move in a room within the shelter.

"No one came to see me, no one even came close, and I was getting bored. But it seems you went to go fetch him. Thank you, and I'm sorry, okay? Thanks to all of you, it looks like I've gotten a happy reunion. ...Though it seems there are some pests along with it."

Sirius's voice lifted at the sight of Subaru stepping into the room. Though the tail end of those words carried a fierce hostility directed at Emilia and Beatrice.

"――"

As ever, Sirius still seemed to mistake Subaru for some vessel possessed by Petelgeuse, or something along those lines. Needless to say, it was a case of mistaken identity Subaru wanted nothing to do with. Pressed by the manic gleam of the clearly deranged monster's eyes, Subaru shrugged as if to brush it off, and said,

"You've got a lot of nerve acting so relaxed for someone who's been captured. I don't know what whim made Priscilla decide to catch you, but there's no way in hell you're getting released unharmed."

"And yet you can't just dispose of me so easily either, can you? Thank you. I understand that you're worried about my well-being. But I'm sorry, okay? As much as I appreciate your concern, it's ultimately meaningless to me. Isn't that obvious?"

Sirius interpreted Subaru's threat positively, through her own peculiar logic. The monster kept her posture quietly upright on the chair, only her cracked and rasping voice trembling as she spoke.

"As long as there is 'love' in anyone's heart — thinking of others, longing for others — no one can deny me. That's true even of that arrogant girl."

"...Your Authority didn't work on either Priscilla or Liliana. It's not like there's no one out there who can hurt you."

"But that's not you. Anything given to me by someone who isn't you is, ultimately, meaningless to me no matter what it is. Thank you. I'm sorry, okay?"

"――!"

Subaru ground his teeth at the sight of Sirius, bandaged mouth loosening into what looked like a smile. The conversation seemed to be functioning on the surface, but in truth, no equal exchange of understanding was taking place. The firm values entrenched within Sirius refused to accept even a fragment of outside stimulus. The harder Subaru struck, the more it was Subaru himself who came away wounded.

"Subaru, it's pointless, I suppose. Expecting reflection or sympathy — that sort of human feeling — from a creature like this is meaningless, isn't it. These things are nothing but malice given shape."

"...A spirit wearing the shape of a woman shouldn't come near my precious Petelgeuse."

When Beatrice tugged at the sleeve of the teeth-grinding Subaru, Sirius's demeanor turned openly hostile. At the monster's words, Beatrice gave a small snort and pulled Subaru's arm even further toward herself.

"How unfortunate for you, I suppose. Betty belongs to Subaru, and she's doing this because he wants her to. You're the one who shouldn't call Subaru by that hideous name. And here you don't even know the true meaning behind that name."

"Don't get carried away, you little brat. Whether in body or in spirit, the place at that person's side is mine to occupy. Don't you dare direct your one-sided, deluded infatuation at him. Shall I set your backside aflame and burn out everything inside you, and make you fertilizer for Od Lagna?"

"You two, don't just get worked up and start fighting on your own. I'll get angry too, you know."

As the air between Sirius and Beatrice turned hostile, Emilia's gaze sharpened as she stepped in to intervene. Surrounded by three women, his arm being pulled at — but Subaru had no room right now to joke about the situation. Being near Sirius weighed that heavily on the mind. Whether that came from the Authority this monster wielded, that much wasn't clear.

"Emilia, Beatrice, step back. I'm probably the only one who can hold a conversation with this thing. If anyone else is around... well, it's questionable whether it's even working with just me."

"But..."

"Please. This is a chance to talk with the Witch Cult that just fell into our laps."

Without circumstances like these, there was no way to sit down and have an actual conversation with the Witch Cult. At Subaru's plea, Emilia let out a breath, exchanged a glance with Beatrice, and stepped back so as not to interfere with the conversation with Sirius. Left in charge of the situation, Subaru turned back to face the bound monster.

"Just as you wanted, I'll talk with you. So stop that rattling of the chains you've been doing this whole time. If you break free of your restraints, I'll have no choice but to take you down."

"You have your position to think of too, don't you. I understand. It's fine. These chains won't break or come loose that easily. Thank you."

At Subaru's attempt at dialogue, the captive Sirius looked thoroughly pleased. It wasn't as though she couldn't see Emilia and the others — she seemed to have simply decided to expel the two of them entirely from her awareness.

"So, what shall we talk about? Between you and me, there's hardly anything left to say to each other... perhaps just an exchange of 'love.' Just kidding. I'm sorry, okay?"

"Your objective — right, your objective. The reason all you Sin Archbishops targeted this city at once. I don't need the pretense about the book, or the artificial spirit. We already understand perfectly well that none of you had any serious intention of actually taking those."

"To say we had no serious intention would be a misunderstanding. But it's true that I myself didn't desire it. I can't speak for the others, but I merely followed what was written in the Gospel."

"The Gospel... that again. Same as with Petelgeuse. Why do all of you follow such a ridiculous book? Even Petelgeuse followed it, and—"

In the end, it cost him his life. That the Gospel's writing showed its owner the future path they were meant to walk — even knowing that much, it was clear such a thing wasn't omnipotent, given how that madman had met his end. Subaru knew that seeing the path of the future wasn't everything. And yet—

"Why do you Witch Cultists let yourselves be so obedient to a book like that? Is it because that book helps bring about the 'Witch's'... the revival of your beloved 'Witch of Envy'?"

"――Don't misunderstand, you."

"Misunderstand?"

At Subaru's strained voice, the delight suddenly vanished from Sirius's voice. Within her bandage-wrapped face, the monster stared straight at Subaru with those gleaming eyes, twisting her lips to reveal yellowed teeth. And then, she said:

"The one I love is you. Only you. You alone. The 'Witch' means nothing to me. All of it is merely necessary to reach you."

"――"

"The other Sin Archbishops are much the same, you know. Every single one of them clings only to their own pointless, trivial, loathsome desires, latching onto their own Authority. Unlike me, whose only reason is 'love,' or you, whose very being I adore. I'm sorry, okay? Everything about it is different."

――The Witch Cult's objective was the revival of the "Witch of Envy."

From Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti's conduct and words, and from the doctrine and atrocities of the Witch Cult he'd heard about up to now, Subaru had believed this without question. But now, its very foundation — the reason for the Witch Cult's existence itself — was shaken.

Of course, Subaru himself had encountered and exchanged words with Regulus Corneas. The thought of that self-absorbed man, who looked down on everything but himself, being devoted heart and soul to nothing but the "Witch" — the moment he considered it, the sense of wrongness only grew stronger. Now that it was pointed out, it was a natural, inevitable conclusion to arrive at. But if that were so, then what was the Witch Cult even for?

"Then what in the world are you all keeping up this Witch Cult thing for—"

"Because you're here."

"――"

"That's my only reason. I'm here to exchange 'love' with you. I know nothing about the others. Though I suppose you'd understand, if we became one."

Becoming one, presumably, meant merging hearts through the power of her Authority. But that wasn't understanding — it was forced conformity. Pushing one's heart onto another by force, binding them to the same sensation — that couldn't be called mutual understanding, let alone becoming one.

"What are the other Sin Archbishops' objectives? What is the Witch Cult ultimately after?"

"Who knows, really. Sorry. Unfortunately, I have no interest in that."

"Where does the Witch Cult usually gather? Is there some kind of leader?"

"...No. There are no particular rules like that. You already know as much yourself."

Maintaining her manic smile behind the bandages, Sirius dodged Subaru's questions with slippery ease. No — she probably wasn't even trying to dodge them at all. The monster believed, in her own twisted way, that she was earnestly responding to the questions of her husband, "Petelgeuse." From her behavior thus far, there was no doubt that Sirius harbored an abnormal, obsessive devotion toward Petelgeuse, and was, on top of that, utterly dependent on him. Which meant the monster, quite literally, truly knew nothing.

"――Still, I suppose it really is true, isn't it."

"――?"

Peering up at the pondering Subaru from directly below, Sirius let that comment slip. The coldness in those words made Subaru's reaction lag, just slightly. The monster seized on that momentary gap.

There was a small creak as the chair tilted, and Sirius's face closed in on Subaru's own. Subaru gasped despite himself as bloodshot eyes glared at him from point-blank range.

Sirius had tilted the chair, and even bound at the ankles, had used only the barely-free tips of her toes to balance, tipping her body forward with enough force to lean into Subaru.

"...Ah—"

"I've thought it strange ever since our reunion at the Time Tower, but now I'm certain. Within your eyes, that day's fierce passion is nowhere to be found. ――You've been swallowed, haven't you?"

"――"

"To be devoured by the spirit within a body you only meant as a temporary lodging, unable to move as you wish... you truly are someone who cannot do without me."

Breathing out a feverish sigh, Sirius's long tongue slid eerily across Subaru's cheek. Tasting the rough texture of that tongue-tip against his skin, every hair on Subaru's body stood on end. The revulsion welling up exploded within his chest, and the back of his eyes flashed crimson. It didn't feel like a reaction caused simply by the vileness of the act alone. It didn't feel like that, but he had no room left in his emotions to consider it further. Right then—

"Ice Brand Arts!"

"Gah— fuh—"

A diagonal blow from a hammer of ice slammed into the torso of Sirius, still pressed against Subaru, sending her flying, chair and all, into the wall behind her. With a crash of impact, Sirius, struck defenselessly by the ice blow, toppled over. Dust rose in the cramped room, and fragments even pattered down from the ceiling.

"Wh— whoa...?"

Beside Subaru, who dropped to his knees on reflex, stood Emilia, dispersing the ice hammer she'd conjured. Only belatedly did he realize that this unceremonious blow had been Emilia's doing, and Subaru let out a long breath. He hadn't been able to fully grasp what had happened in that instant.

"Subaru, you're a huge idiot, I suppose."

"――! Beako?"

At the dry crack and the impact, Subaru blinked, realizing he'd just been slapped across the cheek. It was Beatrice, pressed close beside him, who had struck him. She glanced sidelong at Emilia and said,

"If Emilia hadn't stepped in just now, Betty would have done the same thing. Letting your guard down that much against something like that — worst case, your throat could've been bitten clean through, I suppose."

"――"

At Beatrice's words, Subaru became aware of his own carelessness. There was no way he could laugh it off as an overreaction. In fact, Sirius really had licked his cheek with her tongue. Setting aside the vileness of the act itself, if that tongue had turned to fangs, and it had been his throat rather than his cheek, Subaru wouldn't have been able to stop it.

"Don't try anything strange. I'm clumsy, so I can't hold back well. Next time, it'll definitely be an even more painful hit."

Wary of the fallen Sirius, Emilia issued her warning with no mercy intended. That such excessive wariness would be directed at an opponent as thoroughly restrained and immobilized as a captive — that alone was proof of just how vicious a being this monster was.

It was easy to forget in the face of the threat posed by her Authority, but this Sin Archbishop of "Wrath" stood out even in raw combat strength alone. At a glance, it might seem that the strongest of the Witch Cult was "Invincible" Regulus, but in reality, "Greed," who relied entirely on his Authority, posed a low threat in the truest sense. Strength independent of Authority, and the threat of Authority itself — in that sense, the other Sin Archbishops were far more formidable than Regulus.

"Subaru, you should understand by now, I suppose. There's no getting anywhere talking to this thing. It's not an opponent you can have a proper conversation with. I don't know what she knows, but whatever you're trying to get out of her, you're not getting it through a sane conversation, I suppose."

"If talking won't work then..."

"Making the body talk, or torture, that's what it comes down to, I suppose. And that's not something Subaru should be the one doing. That's for the Kingdom to handle as it pleases, once they've taken custody of her, I suppose."

Delivering her cold-blooded assessment, Beatrice pulled at Subaru's arm and hauled him to his feet. Torture — the word alone stirred an indescribable revulsion in Subaru. It was a word he neither heard nor spoke in ordinary daily life, more so even than death or violence. Even without knowing the reality of it, he could grasp, within the bounds of imagination, just how gruesome such things could be. And the suffering of the person subjected to it, too.

"I don't feel good about it, you know."

Even Subaru wasn't naive enough to believe blindly in the inherent goodness of people. He didn't think that every conclusion to a fight had to end in "death," and at the base of his being, there was always the wish to avoid killing wherever possible. That was an ethic dragged over from his original world, the very softness Subaru couldn't cut himself free of.

――And yet, even so, there would always be conclusions that crossed past that ethic.

If he could avoid killing, he wanted to avoid it. But that thought, in the end, meant this: an opponent who had to be killed, had to be killed. The Sin Archbishops — Petelgeuse and Regulus had both fallen into that category. And that held true for the other Sin Archbishops as well — Sirius, Capella, and "Gluttony's" Alphard, no different. There was hatred, there was a desire for revenge. But apart from that, in some other part of him, there was a conviction that these were people who had to be killed.

"I'm done talking to you. And if we part ways here, I doubt I'll ever get the chance to talk to you again. I don't pity you, and I don't feel sorry for you either. But hurry up and say what you have to say, and get it over with. ...That'll be a relief, for everyone."

Telling someone to their face to "die" carried its own kind of suffocation. Having said that much, Subaru, with nothing more to add, turned to leave the room. Just as Beatrice had said, if he wanted to pry any more details about the Witch Cult out of Sirius, there was no way left but to make the body talk. That wasn't something Subaru could do — it was someone else's job.

When Subaru showed his intent to leave the room, Emilia and Beatrice both let relief show on their faces. They'd both opposed entering the room in the first place. It was a rather pathetic outcome — going through an unpleasant experience for no gain — but he should take it in stride: at least now he understood there was no understanding these people's thinking. That much should count for something.

"――"

Get too close, and there was no telling what would happen. Leaving Sirius toppled over from Emilia's blow without helping her up, Subaru and the others headed for the exit. Hardly a commendable attitude, but with this, it should be—

"――"

"...Wait."

Just as he stepped toward the entrance, Subaru stopped short, an unease clawing at his skull. His gaze snapped back to the fallen Sirius. The root of the discomfort lay there, with the toppled Sirius. The monster lay on her side, pressing her face against the cold floor, breathing hard through her nose. A sound grating on the ears, snagging at the edge of consciousness.

――It was only right before leaving the room that he realized it was humming.

"Stop that singing. What are you playing at?"

"――"

The off-key, wildly out-of-tune and off-pitch dissonance didn't stop. That was Sirius's answer to Subaru's words — nothing more. Which was to say, refusal, rejection. And then—

"I said stop it! That song's grating right through my skull!"

"――. I'm sorry, okay? Ah, but songs are wonderful, aren't they. That's what I was taught — that songs are beautiful. So I just felt like singing, is all."

"Liliana...!"

Sirius must have heard singing when she faced off against Priscilla and Liliana. How exactly the song had sealed her Authority in that fight, Subaru didn't know. Rather than come to hate song through that battle, the monster had learned something from it instead. But the monster's understanding of song was decisively at odds with the feelings Liliana poured into hers. Twisted, and forever something ominous and dreadful.

"Don't lump your singing in with hers. Yours is different. It's something else entirely."

"――The same could be said of you. You are different. You're wrong. Decisively different from that beloved person of mine. The same, and yet different."

"What?"

"Petelgeuse is inside you. Mind melting into mind, body mixing into body — it simply takes time for my beloved to surface. What I must do is help with that. To watch over his awakening, by his side."

Still fallen on the floor, Sirius craned her neck to look up at Subaru. What swirled in those manic eyes was a storm of raging passion, endlessly churning. Anger, joy, sorrow, and an unconcealable devotion, all swirling ceaselessly within Sirius's eyes.

"I will drag him out from within you. ――Thank you, and I'm sorry, okay? Until that day comes, please do take good care of your body and heart."

"――!"

Sirius had, indeed, understood that Subaru and Petelgeuse were different. And yet, as if to overwrite that very understanding, the monster layered a convenient delusion on top of it — that the Petelgeuse sleeping within Subaru would, someday, come to fetch her.

No such thing existed. No such thing was possible. That Petelgeuse's Witch Factor had been absorbed into Subaru was, in all likelihood, fact. But that guaranteed nothing about Petelgeuse's spirit surviving. What commonality could the monster possibly be finding between Subaru and Petelgeuse, to keep repeating such nonsense?

――Or did she mean that, from the outside looking in, there was some shared quality between that madman and Subaru?

"Just one thing — a warning, so things don't end in something so pointless."

"...A warning? You, to me?"

"Yes, from me, so I don't lose my beloved you. ――Watch out for 'Gluttony.' 'Foul Taste,' 'Gourmet,' and 'Overeating' too — every one of them will try to steal you away. If that happens before your awakening, no one will be able to remember you anymore."

"――"

From an unexpected quarter, "Wrath" had let slip information about "Gluttony." Granted, the content itself wasn't anything novel — it was information Subaru already knew. But — no, wait.

"Hold on. 'Gourmet,' and what did you say?"

"'Gourmet,' 'Foul Taste,' and 'Overeating.' To be devoured, absorbed, lost without even realizing it — such an atrocity against 'love,' which ought to melt together, mix together, become one. Please, do kill 'Gluttony' if the chance arises. They're a nuisance."

Casually letting slip the name of a fellow Sin Archbishop, and then, just as casually, wishing for that same person's death. That there was a fatal rift in the relationships among the Witch Cult's high-ranking members — fine, set that aside for now. The problem was the "Gluttony" — no, the "Gluttony's" — that Sirius had just spoken of.

"When Otto ran into 'Gluttony,' I figured it was because the 'Gluttony' at the control tower was wandering around, same as 'Lust' had been, but..."

If that wasn't the case — if it had only been one of two other "Gluttony"s. If all three "Gluttony"s were lurking within the city, and the one Otto encountered had only been one of them.

If the "Gluttony" responsible for the "Gluttony" control tower had continued guarding it the whole time.

"――! Damn it, I need to check, now...!"

Clawing at his own skull for his stupidity, Subaru kicked off the floor and made for the exit. There was no time to keep up the conversation with Sirius. He had to confirm, with his own eyes, the safety of everyone who had taken part in the defense of the city. He had to make sure there wasn't someone out there, gone — their name devoured by "Gluttony."

"Emilia! Beatrice! We're heading back to the shelter from before, right now. I have to check something."

"Subaru? I don't know what she told you, but calm down..."

"After this is over! I'll calm down all you want. So let me calm down — let me do what I need to do to calm down. This is important."

To Emilia's hand on his shoulder, Subaru answered in a rapid clip. At his attitude, Emilia caught her breath, then nodded, "Okay." Beatrice, wearing an exasperated look from the start, made no move to interject on Subaru's actions. Subaru, leaving Sirius behind entirely now, dashed straight out of the room.

"Wait, Subaru. I'm coming too."

Emilia hurried after him, and the sound of two sets of quickening footsteps faded into the distance. Listening to it, Beatrice turned back at the doorway, looked at Sirius still pressed cheek-to-floor, and raised her palm toward the monster.

"Honestly, I can't help but think it might be more correct to just blast you to bits right here, I suppose."

"――Then why don't you? Lecherous spirit. If it hastens my beloved's awakening, I'd welcome it gladly."

"――"

At Sirius's provocative words, Beatrice let out a breath and lowered her palm. With that same lowered hand, she gripped the hem of her dress, and, filling her round eyes with fierce emotion, said,

"If you ever make Subaru grieve, Betty will kill you without fail, I suppose."

"Of course. My beloved Petelgeuse's revival should be greeted with nothing but pure joy, after all."

However much of it was understood, whether the conversation was even functioning at all — even that was uncertain — but with it over, Beatrice left the room and shut the door. Right before it closed, Sirius's twisted humming slipped once more into Beatrice's ears.

A melody warped beyond recognition, playing with — trampling upon — the very concept of music, a violence and harassment of sound that clawed at the eardrums. An entirely new kind of music meant to implant revulsion in others — "Resenment," a song of hatred.

The door shut, and the resenting song cut off. And yet, no matter what, that deranged melody lingered on in the ear. Tasting that unpleasant sensation, Beatrice followed after Subaru and Emilia with quiet steps.

Bursting out of Sirius's confinement room, Subaru rushed down the corridor to Al, who stood waiting. Al, standing by with a dao in hand, startled at the fierce look on the charging Subaru's face, said,

"Yo, bro. I heard a hell of a loud noise — don't tell me you killed her? Punching and kicking counts as abuse of a prisoner too, y'know, not exactly something to brag about."

"I didn't kill her, and as for the prisoner abuse, I'll make my excuses properly later — more important than that, I need to check something. Al, no one died where you were, right?"

"――? Well, hell if I know about the whole city. But at least me, and the cat-eared bro, and the Kararagi-dialect girl are all fine. You know that already, don't you?"

"I know, but... ah, damn it. This kind of question isn't gonna get me anywhere!"

It was only natural the answer came out unhelpful. Just as Subaru feared, if someone had fallen victim to "Gluttony," that person would have to have vanished from everyone's memory, just like Rem. In that case, a question like "is there anyone you don't remember" wouldn't even make sense as a question. The simple method would be to have Al and Emilia go through every single name, one by one.

"――!"

That was frightening. That was terrifying. Even though this wasn't the time to be scared, having it told to him from someone else's mouth was terrifying. Racing back to the shelter and confirming everyone's safety with his own eyes would be far easier.

"I'm heading back to the shelter. Don't let your eyes off this thing, not for a second, until Reinhard gets back."

"Sure, that's fine, but... eh, whatever. I won't ask what happened in there. Too scary."

Waving a hand lazily, Al didn't press into the meaning behind Subaru's attitude. Saved by what was less consideration than a wish to avoid trouble, Subaru retraced his path down the corridor, out of the shelter. Passing, as ever, the bored-looking Priscilla out of the corner of his eye, he counted her off — "number two."

"Hah. A mediocrity's small vessel makes even trivial matters a great ordeal for their heart, does it not. If you're to flail about, at least put effort into making it a spectacle worth watching."

"Not gonna lie, being relieved you haven't changed a bit is kind of unsettling. Later."

With no time to spare, Subaru hurried past Priscilla at a quick clip. It might have been a disrespectful enough attitude to sour Priscilla's mood, but she said nothing about it, only fanning herself and murmuring "how dull," nothing more.

"So Subaru, what do you want to do? What is it you need to check?"

Back at the shelter, packed with the wounded, Emilia called out to Subaru as he scanned restlessly around them. At her call, Subaru hesitated for a moment over asking for her help. Given Rem's case, Emilia had no resistance to "Gluttony's" feeding. Subaru hadn't forgotten the shock of learning, from her own mouth, of the loss of Rem's name.

Bracing for the possibility of tearing open that wound again, it took courage to explain the situation to Emilia. Because this, after all, was a blade of unconsciousness against which Emilia had no way to guard herself.

"――"

Of those who'd fought alongside him in the defense, Subaru had so far confirmed only a handful. Beatrice and Anastasia first. Then Garfiel and Mimi joined them, followed by Wilhelm and Otto, then Liliana and Kiritaka. From Otto's words, Felt's existence was also confirmed. Going by their accounts, Reinhard and Ferris should both be fine too. And then there were Priscilla and Al, whom he'd just been with. Which meant the ones whose safety he still hadn't confirmed were—

"――Subaru, glad to see you've made it back safely."

"Reinhard?"

A cool voice called out from the side to Subaru, his thoughts racing. Turning, he saw the red-haired young man raising a hand toward him — Reinhard. It had been only a few hours since Subaru had last seen him safe and sound, after he'd gone off to reinforce the other camps following the fall of Regulus. Still, having been searching for the faces of people he knew, seeing Reinhard's face brought honest relief.

"I'm glad to see Lady Emilia and Lady Beatrice have safely regrouped as well, above all else."

"Thank you, Reinhard. You're the one who should've been running all over the city, though. I'm glad you're safe. Yeah, really."

"No, it was nothing much. And even without me, everyone fulfilled their own roles admirably. My meager contribution was only a small part of it."

Answering Emilia politely, Reinhard then turned to look at Subaru. His eyes, reflecting the blue of the sky, narrowed, as if seeing straight through to the depths of Subaru's heart.

"So, Subaru, did something happen? You looked anxious just now."

"Whether something happened is exactly what I need to check right now. ――Reinhard, have you seen Felt? Felt and the others... um, Rachins and the rest?"

Even the trio of bumbling misfits ought to be counted, at this point, among the people who mattered — the circle of comrades. He'd been told, secondhand through Otto, that Felt was safe, and likewise that her retainers were safe, but he hadn't confirmed it by naming all three retainers specifically. He couldn't feel at ease. At the desperate edge in Subaru's question, Reinhard lightly touched a hand to his chin.

"Ah, they were fine. Lady Felt, and all three of Rachins, Gaston, and Camberley, are each safe. Rachins and Gaston have some injuries, but nothing that needs serious worry. Though I do think Lady Felt will need to reflect later on acting on her own without permission."

"Thanks to that Felt, there's a decent chance my own chamberlain got saved, so go easy on the punishment for that one, would you... anything else? Nothing off?"

"Something, meaning?"

"Something meaning... no, sorry. That's not a specific question at all. Um, after we split up, was there anything? Any problems, anything that stood out?"

Even reconsidering it, the question still failed to come out specific, and Subaru felt pathetic about it. But Reinhard didn't laugh it off, instead pondering quietly before shaking his head.

"No, I'm sorry, but nothing comes to mind. After I parted ways with you and Lady Emilia, nothing that struck me as a problem happened. That's how I'd put it."

"I see... sorry. That's not it. Um... right, there's a lot I want to talk about, so if you could, could you gather Felt too? I want to consult with everyone involved about this whole affair and what comes after. Can I leave that to you?"

"――. Sure, since it's your request. Though I did just ask Lady Felt to please, this time for real, stay put and behave at the waiting area, so I imagine I'll get an earful for this."

"...Sorry about that. I'll apologize to her myself later, so for now, please."

Reinhard gave a wry smile at Subaru's words, glanced briefly around, then swiftly moved off from the spot. Bursting out of the shelter, a faint shadow could be seen leaping clear over buildings in a single bound — reuniting with Felt would surely come soon. The problem was—

"Subaru, Ferris is over there. You wanted to talk to her, right?"

"Ah, yeah. Right, I wanted to talk to Ferris too."

Called by Emilia, he looked in the direction she pointed. There, in a corner of the shelter, he spotted Ferris, eyes darting restlessly about. The cat-eared healer was unsteady on her feet, her complexion terribly poor. Likely the result of a healing circuit led by Reinhard. Having pushed her healing magic hard, she must have taken on a considerable toll. And yet, she wasn't resting — she seemed to be pacing about, searching for the next patient. Or, no, that didn't seem to be it either.

"――Ah!"

Looking around, Ferris spotted Subaru and the others and cried out. She hurried unsteadily toward them and, practically collapsing, grabbed Subaru by the collar. Catching her light frame, Subaru called out, "Whoa?" To which—

"Tell me..."

"Huh?"

"The Sin Archbishop! You caught her, didn't you? Get her to spill everything she knows, get her to tell us how to cure Crush-sama! So tell me where she is...!"

Eyes wide, glaring up at him from point-blank range, Subaru froze under Ferris's stare. Ferris's blazing passion was, purely and simply, born from concern for the master she adored. And her resolve to show no mercy toward anyone who might hold the key to saving her, should the chance arise.

"F-Ferris, calm down. I get how you feel, but rushing like this won't get you results any faster. Right now, first, we need to talk things through..."

"Don't say whatever you want! You get how I feel? There's no way you get it!? Talking so leisurely, while all this time, how much Crush-sama is suffering... if you understood that, could you stay so calm!? Don't just say whatever!"

"――"

Shoved in the chest, a finger jabbed at him, Subaru fell silent. Flared at over his careless words, Subaru couldn't even talk back. Crusch's condition remained unchanged, still ravaged by "Lust" Capella's blood. More than anything, from Ferris's current outburst, Subaru realized she hadn't forgotten Crusch — and felt relief at that, in spite of himself.

The same black corruption afflicting Subaru's right leg and the palm that had touched Crusch was present here too. But there was no way that fact could bring Ferris's heart any comfort.

"I have to save Crush-sama. I'll do anything, everything necessary for that. If I have to torture the Sin Archbishop, I'll do that too. I know how to heal people. So even if I break something, I can fix it. So, so..."

"Ferris. ――That's enough, stop."

To Ferris, scorched by desperation, Subaru had no words to offer. It was the old swordsman, unable to bear watching the scene, who called out to her from behind. Wilhelm addressed the knight who served the same lord, his voice tightly controlled.

"I understand how you feel — painfully so. But that act would demean Lady Crusch more than anything else could. First, calm yourself. Calm down, and then act."

"Saying you understand is just empty comfort...!"

"――I understand."

To Ferris, about to snap back, Wilhelm pushed the words out low and firm. Then Wilhelm's gaze went to the jacket he held to his chest, wrapped around a set of ashes. Ferris's face showed she'd immediately grasped who slept within it, and she bit her lip.

"That's... not fair. Not fair, not fair, not fair, not fair, Grandpa Wil...!"

"I know. It is I who am at fault, presuming on your tolerance and kindness. Forcing this on you, who rejects the pain of others more than anyone. You may resent this old tree all you like."

"Uh, uuuuhh...!"

Ferris hung her head, holding back tears. Cradling her head, Wilhelm nodded to Subaru. Leave this to me, it seemed to say. Once she calmed, Ferris too would need to stand in for Crusch in the discussions to come. The matter of what to do with Sirius would need to be discussed then as well. But for now, first, those who understood each other's circumstances should speak among themselves.

Wilhelm's calm, quiet eyes conveyed that much to Subaru. Leaning on that with a pang of shame at his own helplessness, Subaru bowed his head and left the spot.

"Even Wilhelm-san must want to cry, and yet..."

Why couldn't everything just work out? Was there really no way for everything, everyone, acquaintance or stranger alike, to be resolved happily? However much Subaru struggled, however hard he tried, however carefully he chose the best move to somehow make things right — would that conclusion ever be within reach — or was even that unknowable?

He'd now confirmed the safety of Reinhard and Felt anew, along with Ferris and Crusch. What remained were Julius and Ricardo, who'd gone to take the "Gluttony" control tower. And Priscilla's page, Schult, and — an unwelcome party, but — Heinkel too. Come to think of it, Julius's younger brother Joshua too, ever since this whole affair had broken out—

"――Ah?"

Just as he was thinking that, Subaru spotted a figure peering into the shelter from outside. Finely tailored white attire, a slender knight's sword at the hip. A tall, well-composed profile, and hair of a purple so lustrous it was almost obnoxious — there was no mistaking him.

It was Julius. The very person whose safety he'd been wanting to confirm was right there.

"Hey, Juli—"

"――"

Reflexively raising a hand, Subaru called out to Julius, who'd shown half his body through the doorway. But the moment Julius noticed Subaru's gaze on him, he spun around sharply and stepped outside, then began walking briskly away from the shelter.

"Huh?"

At this unexpected behavior from Julius, Subaru let out a dumbfounded voice. The reaction was completely unforeseen. Whether Julius would respond obediently to Subaru's call was, admittedly, a matter people might disagree on — but even so, this reaction was beyond anything he'd imagined. Not an honest response, not a snide remark — but to be ignored outright?

"Is that bastard screwing around?"

A slow-building irritation, all the frustration up to this point, boiled over at once, and Subaru took off after him. It wasn't that he'd been worried. It wasn't that he'd been worried, but for someone who'd been searching, needing to confirm whether he was safe, to be met with that attitude — that shouldn't stand. He needed to catch him and press him for an explanation, whatever he was playing at. This wasn't the time for games, and he had a thing or two to say about it.

"Um, Subaru? What's wrong?"

"Julius, that pompous bastard, was right there and just ignored me. I'm going after him!"

"Huh?"

Leaving Emilia's startled voice behind, Subaru broke into a run after Julius. Bursting out of the shelter's entrance, he saw a back disappearing around a corner up the street. A movement clearly meant to avoid notice. But if he wasn't running, catching up would be easy enough.

"If you're fine, just say you're fine, damn it, one word would do..."

Muttering curses, Subaru broke into a run toward the corner up the street. A quick walk against a run — the distance closed inevitably. Rounding the corner, he caught sight of that back at once and raised his voice.

"Hey, you bastard! What's with wandering off on your own when everyone's busy? Not being able to see your face makes people worry, y'know. I mean, that's just, generally speaking."

"――"

At Subaru's rough voice, Julius's feet stopped. Turning only his head, Julius glanced back at Subaru with those yellow eyes. Subaru frowned at the silent look, but Julius, still in that same posture, said,

"――Forgive me. I was looking for someone, but it seems they weren't inside. I'd like to go check another shelter right away. Excuse me."

"Wait wait wait wait, what are you talking about. Whoever you're looking for, it's Anastasia-san or someone, right? She was right there in that shelter. You just missed her 'cause you were in a hurry. Not like you."

"――!"

Leaving only a perfunctory word behind, the back began to walk away — Subaru called out to stop him. At Subaru's words tossed his way, Julius showed a dramatic reaction. His shoulders jumped, and he turned back around, his face startled.

"O-oh? What's wrong?"

Subaru's own voice came out cracked with surprise, unbidden. And no wonder. The expression on Julius's face as he looked at him was colored with an astonishment Subaru had never seen before. No — astonishment wasn't the only thing in that expression. There was something clinging, something almost desperately bright, in it.

At an emotion so utterly unlike Julius, Subaru found himself unable to continue for a moment. Julius, catching his breath at Subaru's reaction, his cheeks stiffening, said,

"...Subaru. You... remember me?"

"What kind of question is that. I'm not such a thin character I'd forget you after just a few hours. What's 'The Finest Knight' Julius Juukulius even talking about, being ridicu—"

Shrugging, Subaru gave a slightly mocking reply to Julius. But partway through the exchange, he realized his own stupidity, and the words stopped in his throat. Julius's question just now was clearly, obviously wrong somehow. And that wrongness was something he should have caught, had his imagination reached even one step short of the worst-case scenario Subaru had been dreading.

"Subaru! Don't just go running off on your own, I suppose!"

Subaru, his throat freezing over, faced off against Julius. Into the scene of the two of them confronting each other on the street, Emilia and Beatrice arrived, having chased after him. Seeing the two of them locked in a silent standoff, they blinked their wide eyes, and—

"Um... you're in the middle of something, right?"

Sensing the strange atmosphere, the tension in the air, Emilia tilted her head uneasily. Her reaction — especially the way she looked at Julius — gave Subaru a bad feeling. And then, pointing at Julius, Subaru said,

"...Ah, yeah, that's right, but that's not it. Emilia-tan. Beako, too, um—"

"――?"

Emilia and Beatrice both furrowed their brows in confusion at Subaru's halting words. There was something — probably something decisive — he needed to ask. Swallowing hard at the thought, Subaru glanced sidelong at Julius. At Subaru's gaze, Julius, his face set with a hollow kind of resolve, drew his chin in and said,

"I found Julius. So I can bring him along to the discussion, right?"

"――Julius."

At the question, Beatrice looked toward Julius. And then, Emilia, hesitantly pursing her lips, said—

"Is Julius-san... someone Subaru knows?"

— as if replaying a nightmare from before.
