Arc 5 Chapter 2: "Blood of a Swindler"

Faced with Mimi, all aflutter and waving both hands, Subaru cocked his head. "Party" was a pretty flashy word to come flying out of nowhere.

"An invitation to a party... from Anastasia-san? I mean, what, all of a sudden? Did something good happen or something?"

"Something good? A celebration? Dunno! But it doesn't matter, does it, eating yummy things and drinking and having fun sounds fun! Sooo, fun!"

"Drinking, huh. There's no way you're old enough to drink, however you look at it."

"Heheh. Mimi's grown up properly this past year! The Danchō said it's okay, so it's okay! Ojō-sama says no, though."

Mimi puffed out her chest, the bell on her braid-tying hair ornament jingling. Subaru's eyes went wide at that answer.

"Okay to drink— wait, you're an adult!? No way, how old are you!?"

"I turned fifteen the other day! So Mimi's an adult, an adult. A grown-up woman!"

"Does a grown woman get this excited like she's in the upper grades of elementary school!? Still..."

Calming down a little and putting a hand to his chest in relief, Subaru finally recalled that the treatment of adulthood in this world differed from his original one. Basically it was the equivalent of a coming-of-age ceremony. In this world, fifteen marked adulthood — meaning alcohol and tobacco became permissible.

"That's the right understanding, right, Petra?"

"Yep, that's right. To be a bit more precise, boys leave home and start working at fifteen, and girls, if it's early, get married around that age too. Even if it's not marriage, that's about when they go off to work somewhere. Like me right now."

"So Petra left home pretty early, huh. What a precocious one."

"Ehehe, I'm precocious— that... doesn't feel like a compliment."

While being glared at by a sharp-eyed Petra, Subaru made his way over to a thoroughly worn-out Beatrice. Freed from Mimi, Beatrice's drill curls drooped somewhat dejectedly as she looked up resentfully at the approaching Subaru.

"Keeping up with Subaru's training and now getting dragged around by the cat girl on top of it, Betty's worn out, I suppose... Subaru, carry me."

"Keeping up with my training — you were just watching..."

Making the tactless retort anyway, Subaru scooped up Beatrice, who was holding both arms out. He'd been training more than before, and she was light as a feather, so the weight wasn't an issue. The optical image looking a bit like a single father with a kid was a bother, though.

"Oooh, the little one's getting carried! Lucky! Mimi too! Mimi too!"

"Maybe your Danchō could manage it, but with your build, no way. Rejected, rejected."

"Eeeh, unfair! Unfair! That's so unfair! Unfaair!"

Mimi scampered around and around Subaru, who was holding Beatrice. For some reason Beatrice wore a triumphant "hmph" sort of face. Eventually Mimi grabbed the hem of Subaru's tracksuit.

"Then, fine! Mimi'll just climb up on her own!"

"Idiot! Stop, you'll knock me over! Petra, stop her too... what are you doing?"

"Ah, um, it's not that I got jealous or anything. It's not that, but, may I also climb up on Subaru-sama?"

"It's not fine, is it!?"

Carrying one little girl in his arms, clung to by a cat girl and a young girl, Subaru went on making a ruckus in the entrance hall, the conversation showing no signs of progressing. Then—

"—I was wondering why you never came back. What are you doing at the entrance?"

An icily unemotional voice rang out, and Subaru and Petra both straightened their backs in unison. Mimi, meanwhile, sparkled with curiosity at the new voice, while Beatrice sighed.

The voice fell from atop the grand staircase overlooking the entrance hall. Turning his gaze that way, Subaru saw a single girl standing where she could look down on the four of them horsing around. Pink hair, a maid uniform altered to be short — that was the figure. Her pale pink eyes held a chillingly composed emotion, and her otherwise cute features carried not a trace of charm. It was Ram, Petra's colleague and head of staff of this manor.

Ram surveyed Subaru and the others with cold eyes, gave a "hmph" through her nose, and said,

"Lewd."

"You're the lewd one, coming up with that idea from this lineup and this scene! Whether it's wholesome is up for debate, but it's nothing but heartwarming!"

"Twisting facts to suit your own convenience like that, as always. But Barusu, you'd do well to remember — Ram's judgment is based entirely on what Ram's eyes see."

"Could you maybe start by removing the filter that makes things look weird?"

Judging by how her gaze drifted away, uninterested, she had no intention of listening. Ignoring Subaru's sullen face, Ram's gaze turned toward Petra. Instantly, Petra's small shoulders gave a great startled shudder.

"Petra. I believe I told you to bring Barusu here even if you had to grab him by the scruff of his neck, since we have guests. Why are you making a racket together with him at the entrance?"

"I— I'm sorry, Ram-neesama."

"It seems you didn't hear me, Petra. Ram believes she asked why you were playing around."

"Don't talk like some demon mother-in-law. It was me who was fooling around. Petra didn't do anything wrong."

"There's no way she's not at fault. I'll slap you silly, Barusu."

"Only a little bit at fault!"

Only once he'd conceded did she seem satisfied, and Ram gave a small jerk of her chin to indicate behind her.

"We can't keep Emilia-sama and the others waiting forever. Barusu, get yourself up to the parlor at once too. Petra, to the dining hall. Beatrice-sama, with Barusu."

"Naturally, I suppose."

"What about Mimi? Hey, onee-san, what about Mimi?"

Petra, given her destination, reluctantly let go of the hem of Subaru's clothes, though the other cheerful kitten hand still held on firmly. At Mimi's question, Ram brushed back a strand of hair against her cheek with a finger.

"Your guests are also invited to join Barusu and the others in the parlor. Your companions seemed rather unsettled without Mimi-sama present."

"Ohh, then I guess I gotta go back, huh? What a hopeless bunch!"

Ram behaved politely enough toward the actual guest. Mimi laughed at that, cackling — but Subaru had picked out something in there he couldn't just let slide.

"You said companions, right? But didn't you say earlier you came here alone or something?"

"I did say that, and it's not a lie! Really, Hetaro and Tivey and Danchō and Julius and Ojō-sama aren't with me! But Joshua's with me. Mimi came alone as Joshua's Goe— Goe?"

"Escort?"

"Yeah, Goe!"

Mimi puffed out her chest, looking pleased with herself. After petting her head, Subaru turned once more toward Ram up above.

"Sorry about that. I figured it was just Mimi, didn't think we had someone waiting."

"So it seems. Fine, come quickly. Emilia-sama's patience is likely wearing thin about now."

"That's scary. Well, Petra, see you later. Let's go, Mimi."

"Yep!"

Joshua — an unfamiliar name, but who could it be? Probably someone from the Anastasia Camp Subaru didn't know. Given they'd gone to the trouble of sending him as an envoy with Mimi as an escort, he should hold some corresponding position. Though given how casually Mimi dropped names, the fact that she called him by name alone probably wasn't much of an indicator either way of his rank, considering how indiscriminate she was about that sort of thing.

"I'll bake tarts once things settle down, so please try them, Subaru-sama."

As she parted, Petra whispered that and trotted off at a light run. Waiting in the dining hall was probably Frederica or someone like that. Whether Petra would be assigned to serve in the parlor was unclear, but either way, it looked like eating Petra's tart was going to be pushed back quite a while.

"Who's in the parlor besides Emilia-tan?"

"Roswaal-sama hasn't returned yet, so Otto and Garf are with her. If whoever's come is an assassin posing as a guest, Garf alone would be more than enough."

"I'm not particularly worried about that kind of direct attack. I've told Emilia-tan that if it ever comes to that, she should use Otto as a shield."

"Ram will start doing the same from now on, whenever she senses danger."

As expected of Otto — his treatment really is something else, Subaru thought, though it was hard to say that out loud. In any case, if the three of them were the ones handling this, Otto was probably diligently working overtime about now. Better hurry over and lighten his load, or they might lose a valuable minister of internal affairs.

"That one's unrewarded too, I suppose. It's a mystery why he's friends with Subaru."

"Me and Otto are bound by an unbreakable bond of manly friendship that no outsider could ever see. Rock solid, yo."

"Ooh! Rock soliiid!"

Beatrice's musing drew reactions from both Subaru and Mimi, and led by Ram's sigh, they arrived at the parlor. It was the first room one entered upon climbing the grand staircase in the entrance hall and stepping into the manor's interior. When Ram knocked on the door, it was pulled open from within. And the face that appeared was—

"Oh, the boss showed up, huh. Was startin' to think I'd have to go fetch ya myself, seein' as you're so damn slow."

"If you'd all trooped out here on the pretext of looking for me and left just Otto inside, that would've been funny, but I guess it didn't turn out that way."

"Ah, that would'a been hilarious. Can just picture Otto-nii flailin' around in a panic."

Trading delinquent grins with Subaru like that was a boy with short blond hair, sharp canines, and a distinctive white scar on his forehead — Garfiel. Arms crossed, apparently having been standing right by the door, Garfiel jerked his chin toward the inside.

"Get in here. The guest says talk can't move forward without the boss around. Otto-nii and Emilia-sama are tryin' to entertain 'em, but it just looks like some kinda comedy duo routine."

"That's a show I'd like to keep watching for a while, honestly."

"Quit sayin' stupid things and get in already. There's people waitin' behind you."

"Guack!"

Kicked hard right in the middle of his back, Subaru stumbled forward and was shoved into the room. Several gazes in the parlor pierced Subaru as he barged in off-balance in an awkward posture. The emotions rising on each face were relief, exasperation, and suspicion.

Swallowing the urge to offer an excuse for his lateness to the relieved and exasperated pair, Subaru turned first toward the owner of the suspicious gaze.

"——"

The one whose gaze crossed his was a handsome young man with fine, delicate features. His slender frame was wrapped in a well-tailored formal outfit, and his pale purple hair was grown long and tied back at the nape of his neck. He had a scholarly air about him, made more pronounced by the striking monocle he wore. His yellow eyes were the upturned type, and the expression he wore, peering at this new face with his lips pressed together, looked almost displeased. Their first impressions of each other, it had to be said, didn't seem to be particularly favorable on either side.

"This is...?"

The staring match went on a while before the visiting young man spoke first. His gaze left Subaru and turned to the two people seated across from him. In response to the young man's question, the one who nodded was a beautiful girl with silver hair flowing down her back.

"Yes. Sorry to keep you waiting. — This is my knight, Natsuki Subaru."

At the girl introducing him like that, a wave of emotion shot up Subaru's spine. That was simply because the resonance of "my knight" was a piece of kotodama so wonderful it left him spellbound no matter how many times he heard it.

"F-for some reason you seem to be wearing an entranced expression..."

"Subaru. Stop making that weird face, I suppose. The other party will get the wrong idea about... eh? Wait, your arm's really strong. Wai, the arm hugging me, Suba— Subaru! Ow! It hurts, I suppose!"

"—Huh! Ah, sorry. Got a little lost there for a second."

To cover up the feelings welling in his chest, he'd nearly strangled Beatrice without realizing it. A Bea-hug, or Beatrice hug for short. So that's what that meant. In any case, before the guest's look of bewilderment, Subaru cleared his throat and set the still-wincing Beatrice down beside him.

"I am Natsuki Subaru, as I've just been introduced. I serve as knight to Lady Emilia here. Pleased to make your acquaintance."

"——"

His outfit — a tracksuit — didn't exactly lend itself to dignity, but his form alone followed the perfect etiquette of a knight. There was a time he'd viewed the posturing stance of a knight with a cynical eye, but actually trying it himself, it "fit" more than he'd expected. It wasn't about whether it suited him or not — it was the sensation of drawing himself closer to the existence of "knight." There was meaning in earnestly, deadly seriously pursuing what could be laughed off as a misguided fever. And so, under the tutelage of Garfiel, who lived up to the name "knight freak" with his thorough knowledge, Subaru's formal etiquette had come to a standard worthy of a proper knight.

Glancing briefly toward the door, he saw Garfiel watching on, looking satisfied. Noticing Subaru's glance, Garfiel responded by flipping him the bird. Subaru was the one who'd taught him that gesture, but this wasn't the occasion for it. As an instructor Garfiel was fine, but as a student, he was a failure.

"Thank you for such a courteous greeting. I— this one has come as an envoy of Lady Anastasia Hoshin. My name is Joshua Juukulius."

"I see, Joshua-san. That's a fine name. In any case, I'm terribly sorry for the delay. Please allow me to apologize again... Juukulius?"

Midway through offering pleasantries to the young man who'd introduced himself — Joshua — Subaru cocked his head hard, unable to shake a word that rang a bell. At Subaru's puzzlement, Joshua nodded "yes," and to supplement that,

"That's right, Subaru. Joshua's Julius's younger brother, apparently. Both brothers cooperating with Anastasia — isn't that somehow really wonderful?"

Dispersing the somewhat refined air she'd carried until now, the girl smiled with a down-to-earth remark. Of course, this face was the girl's true face. Subaru gave a small sigh and, fully aware it was rude, stared intently at Joshua's face as he sat down in the seat across from him. As a matter of course, right beside the girl — Emilia.

"I see, Julius's younger brother, huh. Now that you mention it, the features really are similar. Snide... no, sharp eyes. Sarcastic... no, elegant mouth. And unlike the demon... I mean, that lovely hair color."

"Could you please refrain from commenting if it's going to take that much strain each time?"

Covering up words that seemed liable to develop into a problem here and there, the one who cut in without hiding his cold sweat was the gray-haired young man — Otto. He held the title of chief minister of internal affairs for the Emilia Camp — or rather, the title existed because he was the only one who could fill that role — but as mediator, Otto's nerves were considerably frayed.

"Did you lose weight or something?"

"The days here are far too stimulating, you know! Especially when scenes like this happen back to back, the mental fatigue leaves me skin and bones from lack of exercise, skin and bones!"

"Skiiin and boones! Skiiin and boones!"

Raising her voice happily in rhythm, Mimi stirred Otto up without even meaning to. Ignoring Otto's face, unable to say anything given he was a guest's companion, Mimi ran over on her short legs and dove into a seat beside Joshua. And Beatrice, set down by Subaru, also eyed the seating arrangements in the parlor. The seats greeting Joshua's group were filled in the order Subaru, Emilia, Otto. If squeezed, there'd be room for Beatrice too, but that would look awkward. So without hesitation, Beatrice sat on Subaru's lap. Subaru, too, as a matter of course, wrapped an arm around Beatrice's belly to keep her from falling.

"Now then, let's get to the main topic that's been delayed until now—"

"W-wait, please wait a moment! Who is that girl there?"

The one flustered by that way of entering the conversation was Joshua. He pointed at Beatrice on Subaru's lap, leaning forward with enough force that his monocle nearly slipped off. It seemed he didn't have as composed a personality as Julius. As Subaru was thinking that was, if anything, preferable, the one who spoke up instead was Mimi, seated beside Joshua.

"Geez, Joshua's behind the times. That's Bea-ko, Subaru's kid. You can tell just by looking. Next to him's Okaa-san, and next to her is Osandon?"

"Who's a kitchen maid, would you please not say that in front of me when I'm not even entirely confident of my own position here!?"

"Osandoon! Osandoon! Sounds like a demon beast's name, amaziing!"

Mimi's pure innocence rendered even Otto's heartfelt outcry powerless. Everyone unanimously abandoned any reaction to Otto's slumping shoulders, and Subaru placed a hand on Beatrice's head on his lap.

"Sorry for not introducing her. This posture had just kind of become normal, so I tried to move the conversation along without thinking it was odd."

"Otto-kun forgot to point it out too, huh. I was surprised myself."

"'Surprised myself' isn't exactly common phrasing these days..."

Emilia's remark was as ever, but Subaru agreed with the content of it. That Otto had forgotten to make his usual sensible retort meant this scene had become that routine here. What a luxurious sort of problem to have, in a way.

"This girl is Beatrice. Like Mimi said, she's mine and Emilia-sama's child."

"Whaat!?"

Joshua's face was one of shock.

"No, that's not right, Subaru. Joshua's surprised. I did kiss Subaru, but a kiss alone can't make a baby, I studied that."

"Ah, sorry, Emilia-tan. I feel like some incredibly candid private matters are getting exposed here. It was my fault, let's introduce her properly."

"It's because you use Betty as a prop that this happens, I suppose. You should reflect on it."

Subaru gave a forced smile toward both the innocently oblivious Emilia and the displeased Beatrice at once. Incidentally, Emilia's misunderstanding about how babies were made remained frozen right at "a kiss alone can't make a baby." Pushing any further than that was, of course, beyond Subaru's ability, and the other women seemed to be watching for the right moment, expecting a shock, on the principle of "once Emilia-sama has matured a bit more mentally." Basically, everyone was overprotective.

"Um, so then, what exactly is this Lady Beatrice's actual position...?"

Straightening his slipped monocle, Joshua was already reeling under the baptism of the Roswaal Manor crew. Subaru wondered idly whether the Anastasia Camp, with the "Iron Fang" among its members, wasn't much the same in terms of rowdiness.

"Sorry the conversation keeps derailing. Beatrice looks at first glance like just a cute little girl, but her true identity is my Contracted Spirit. A loli-baba."

"That's right, a Spirit, I suppose. Also, I saw that 'loli-baba' remark as being made fun of, I suppose."

Brushing off the hand on her head, Beatrice instead pushed up at Subaru's chin from below. Having grown fluent in Subaruspeak from living here, she no longer let slips of the tongue go easily. As for "loli-baba," it was simply a combination of the already-explained "baba" and "loli," so it was only natural she'd catch on.

Though it was, to any onlooker, a heartwarming exchange between Subaru and Beatrice, Joshua's reaction opposite them was dramatic indeed. For his expression, which up until then had carried an impression of somewhat stiff but likable youthfulness, changed sharply into something cold.

"—I see. A Spirit, is it."

The emotion packed into that low murmur was something no one who heard it could gauge. It wasn't that he was suppressing his feelings. Quite the opposite. There were simply too many complicatedly tangled waves of emotion, so much so that it was impossible to tell which was the core feeling. Still, everyone present could tell it wasn't a particularly favorable murmur.

"Yo, guest. Somethin' wrong with our boss havin' a Spirit with him?"

Playing the role Subaru himself used to play — barreling ahead with disrespect toward anyone — was Garfiel. Where everyone else agonized over how to broach the issue, he plunged right in without hesitation. At Garfiel's words, Joshua gave a short shake of his head, saying "no."

"It's nothing significant. It's just that I thought Natsuki-dono must be a Spirit Knight as well. As you likely know, my own brother is also a Spirit Knight. It's a title that's practically unique in this entire kingdom."

"Ah, of course I know. That guy, during the fight against the Witch Cult, he... uh, well. Helped... helped me out, ngh. Helped me out, so..."

"Are you really that reluctant to admit you were helped that much!?"

That wasn't quite it. It was just that recalling fighting alongside Julius and reflecting on the things he'd blurted out made him itch with embarrassment, on top of the old wounds inflicted at the training grounds acting up.

"I'd heard there was a knight besides Betty and Subaru who employs a Spirit, I suppose. That it turns out to be your brother is quite the strange coincidence, I suppose."

"What sort of meaning do you intend by 'coincidence,' Lady Spirit?"

"Obviously. It's decided that the one who came first will eventually be surpassed by the one who comes after, I suppose. At best, he can serve as decoration along Subaru and Betty's glorious path... nyanya!"

"Don't go picking fights the second you meet someone. Besides, me and Julius are on a totally different level to begin with. No point competing in the same field. Our way of winning isn't challenging a puzzle expert to a puzzle match — it's challenging a puzzle expert to a kart race."

Ruffling Beatrice's hair, Subaru bowed his head to Joshua. In doing so, he also pushed down the head of the now hair-mussed Beatrice along with his own.

"Sorry about that. I've got no intention of mocking your brother. I mean, honestly, I admit I'm the weaker one, ability-wise. This Spirit's just a bit vain."

"That's a commendable thing to say, but also the correct judgment. It's only natural to compare yourself to my brother and acknowledge where you fall short."

"Huh?"

Just as things seemed headed back on track through mature give-and-take, Joshua suddenly said something high-handed, and the mood grew murky again. Not noticing Subaru's puzzled frown, Joshua's monocle gleamed with an eerie light.

"Yes, my brother is an amazing man. At merely twenty years of age, he now holds what is effectively the second-highest position in the Royal Knights' most glamorous unit, the Praetorian Guard. He's currently stepped away from his duties as a Praetorian Knight in order to serve Lady Anastasia, but once Lady Anastasia's cherished goal is achieved, his seat as commander of the Praetorian Guard is all but assured. He shares a deep friendship with Reinhard-sama, the current Sword Saint, and his conduct is flawless both publicly and privately. Strict with both himself and others, he never forgets his ambition to improve. And his elegant appearance, which captivates so many women, is matched by an equally admirable character. Yes, my brother is an amazing man. Compared to him, you're simply not worth discussing."

"...O-oh."

Joshua lined up flowery praise with such passion that his face flushed with excitement. Subaru could only manage that single reply to those words, and Beatrice on his lap looked utterly appalled. Garfiel and Otto too, uncertain what might prove fatal to say, kept their mouths shut, while Mimi was too absorbed in devouring the tea snacks she'd been served to even offer support — or to be listening at all. In a room in that state, there was only one person capable of countering Joshua's torrent of words.

"Fufu. Joshua really loves his big brother Julius, doesn't he."

An angel who, as a rule, took everything in a favorable light sat right there. At those words, Joshua realized what he'd just blurted out, and his face flushed red — not from excitement this time, but from embarrassment. Still, clearing his throat, he managed to compose himself somewhat.

"F-forgive me. I got a little carried away. When it comes to family, I seem to lose my self-control."

"No, it's fine. I actually wanted to hear more about Julius from Joshua's own mouth. I've only met Julius a few times in the royal capital, so I wanted to know more about him."

"I-is that so! Then, allow me to share a few memories of my brother and I—"

"Let's save that for another occasion, and get to the main topic now, don't you think!? Right, don't you agree, Otto! Garfiel!"

"Eh!?"

Called on and dragged into it, the two responded with faces and voices that said "Don't drag us into this!" But they quickly nodded in agreement with Subaru all the same. At that, Joshua seemed to realize, to his credit, that the conversation had strayed too far off course.

"W-well then... let's save the tale of my brother's glory for another time. I too— that is, this one must complete the errand swiftly and rejoin Lady Anastasia."

"Right. I'm really looking forward to it. So, about the main topic we've been putting off this whole time... what would that be?"

Facing Joshua, who managed to feign composure despite the lingering awkwardness, Emilia shifted naturally into her role as a candidate for the throne. As her tone took on a somewhat solemn quality, the air of the parlor tightened accordingly. This ability to command such tension was proof that the self-awareness of a ruler-in-training had begun to take root in Emilia.

"—As envoy of Lady Anastasia Hoshin, I convey to Lady Emilia the words of my liege, Lady Anastasia."

Touched by that skin-prickling tension, Joshua's expression too regained its initial temperature. The young man reached into his coat and drew out a single letter, placing it on the table. Opening it, his eyes dropped to the characters written in black ink, and he said,

"Lady Anastasia wishes to invite Lady Emilia and all her associates to the city of Priestella."

"An invitation to the city of Priestella... Priestella, that's the water gate city, right? The one near the border between the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica and the Kararagi City-States, the big city."

"That is correct. Lady Anastasia is currently residing there rather than in the royal capital, and... wishes to invite Lady Emilia and her party."

Affirming Emilia's question, Joshua bowed his head quietly. Turning her gaze from him, Emilia glanced sidelong at Subaru with a look that of course meant, "What do you think?"

Naturally, the sense Subaru got from this conversation was close to Emilia's own. To begin with, it was common knowledge that Anastasia had a manor in the noble district of the royal capital for her stays in Lugunica. When he'd first heard from Mimi about a party invitation, he'd naturally assumed they'd be invited to that manor.

"Priestella is a scenic place, and given the city's peculiar nature, it's also famous as a tourist destination. Lady Anastasia herself takes delight in saying that just strolling around and looking at it soothes the heart."

"If it's just 'it's a nice place so I want to show you,' that'd be welcome enough on its own, but... that's not quite it, is it, no matter how you look at it? Emilia and Anastasia aren't exactly friends, after all."

Moreover, as Subaru recalled it, in their exchange at the royal castle, Anastasia had been one of those who'd shown a particularly harsh stance toward Emilia. Among the candidates, it was really Priscilla and Anastasia who'd taken a caustic attitude toward Emilia. Crusch didn't discriminate by race out of personal feeling, and Felt owed Emilia no small debt. So while there had been cooperation during the White Whale incident and against the Witch Cult, Subaru personally didn't hold a particularly favorable impression of Anastasia herself.

So naturally, he had a premonition that this invitation, too, wouldn't come without strings attached. As if to confirm Subaru's suspicion, the corner of Joshua's mouth curved into a smile. And then—

"Lady Anastasia says this invitation comes purely out of goodwill this time. Apparently, she happened to see, in Priestella, a certain precious item that Lady Emilia has been searching for."

"Something I've been looking for?"

Emilia leaned in eagerly, and at that instant Otto's expression turned into one that said, "We've been had!" Before he could call out to stop her, the initiative had already been seized — Subaru understood that much from Otto's reaction, but the core of the matter still eluded him. That delay in judgment meant the initiative was handed over to the other side completely.

Still smiling deeper, Joshua went on to say this:

"—At the shop of a certain magic-mineral merchant in the city of Priestella, it seems there lies an ultra-high-purity, colorless magic mineral, exactly the sort Lady Emilia desires. A catalyst for housing a Great Spirit — that's what you're currently searching for, is it not?"

—And the moment the initiative was handed over, the Emilia Camp's departure for Priestella was, in effect, already decided.
