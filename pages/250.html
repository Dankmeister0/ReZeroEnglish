Arc 4 Chapter 82: "Mutual Deception"

"So it really wasn't my imagination, or some kind of mistake after all..."

Placing his hand against the crystal before him and confirming he felt no response from it, Subaru pressed his forehead against the hard, cold mineral and groaned as if in lament. Inside the crystal, the girl sleeping there—the original Ryuzu Meyer—gave no reaction to Subaru's despair. Even under normal circumstances, she who slept an eternal slumber would never answer Subaru's call, but setting that aside, the reaction that should have occurred failed to happen. Namely—

"Because my qualification was taken away, even touching the crystal won't transfer the authority to command Ryuzu-san's clones to me..."

The authority to command Ryuzu Meyer's clones was a privilege granted only to an Apostle of Greed. Having rejected Echidna and been stripped of his qualification to challenge the Trials of the Tomb, Subaru had evidently been deemed unworthy of that role.

Entering the facility filled with sickening air and confirming this fact, Subaru held his head in his hands as the light at the end of the tunnel seemed to slip away. At worst—at the absolute worst—until he confirmed this, he could have thought it was simply the trauma of being shown the second Trial that made him hesitate to step inside the tomb. If that were the only reason, the problem would have been solely in Subaru's own heart. He could have used drastic measures to challenge it again and again, beating down his own weak will.

However, once things were beyond Subaru's reach, his hands were completely tied. Being unable to even set foot inside the tomb meant he could not even negotiate directly with Echidna to regain his qualification. More than that, whether the Witches would ever appear before Subaru again after parting like that—he instinctively understood that it would never happen.

And understanding that meant Subaru's chances of clearing the Trials were gone, leaving the liberation of the Sanctuary in hands other than his own. In other words—

"You're saying... I should make Emilia do that?"

Putting into words the situation he feared most, Subaru felt a deep desire to unleash a torrent of curses at Echidna for her malicious scheme. Echidna had been able to read Subaru's memories. She alone should have been able to observe the worlds Subaru had repeated in the exact same way. So Echidna should have known how Emilia had broken down and been crushed after continuing to challenge the Trials alone. She should have known, too, how desperately Subaru had thrown his own life away just to prevent that from happening.

Even so, did the Witch of Greed still intend to force such torment onto Subaru and Emilia?

"What do I do, what do I do, what do I do, what do I do what do I do what do I do what do I do..."

Burning through his thoughts and setting his brain ablaze, Subaru searched for a breakthrough. Yet no matter how much he overworked his brain until his synapses sizzled, no second-best plan presented itself right after the glimmer of hope he had almost grasped had unraveled. The situation was steadily worsening, and the people he could rely on were only dwindling.

Someone who understood the circumstances and would listen closely to Subaru's words with empathy. Someone who could swallow the countless pieces of unmentionable information and still hold a meaningful conversation.

Brooding over it alone would yield nothing. Echidna could no longer be relied upon. If so, there was only one person left with whom Subaru could share this problem.

"To think you would visit me-e-e so-o-o late at night, I never would have guessed."

"...For saying that, you sure look fully prepared and waiting for me. Forget putting out the lights to sleep, it looks like you just replaced the candle."

"Aha-a-ah, quite ha-a-arsh. We-e-ell, you're not wro-o-ong, though."

Propping his upper body up in bed with his pillow as a backrest, Roswaal loosened his cheeks in a welcoming smile toward Subaru. Although the main lighting in Roswaal's bedchamber had been extinguished, the flame in the candlestick on the small bedside table had just been replaced, its flickering orange light brightly illuminating the room.

In the bewitching, flickering glow of the fire, Roswaal's sickly white face cast eerie shadows. Witnessing this, Subaru swallowed lightly, steeling himself for the conversation ahead. If the knowledge Subaru possessed was the truth, then this conversation with Roswaal was—

"A-a-and so? You've gone out of your way for a late-night visit. Did you bring so-o-ome sweet words to pique my-y-y interest, I-i- wonder?"

"...I suppose the part about winning you over isn't wrong. Roswaal, is there a way to get past the Sanctuary without the Trials?"

"――――"

With just that single sentence, Subaru felt the nature of Roswaal's smile take on a chilling coldness. Indeed, the clown's smile split wider across his lips. Smoothing down his indigo hair, Roswaal nodded repeatedly and fixed his yellow eye upon Subaru.

"Subaru-kun. —Which time is this for you now?"

That question served as a check, confirming that both parties were aware of the surface layer of each other's secrets. Roswaal obtained the information that Subaru was using Return by Death, and Subaru obtained the information that Roswaal was in a position to know that. From there, Subaru had to correct Roswaal's information while extracting concessions from him.

Exhaling a short breath, Subaru deliberately made a show of shrugging his shoulders with an affected demeanor.

"Sorry, but it's a number too ridiculous to even bother counting. I've had so many of these probing matches with you that I don't even remember which one this is."

"I... se-e-e. I see-e-e. For you to speak to me with such a detached, pragmatic approach... may I-i- take it to mean that's the case?"

"Well, I wonder."

Averting his gaze, Subaru held back the rest of the conversation, keeping him in suspense. However, during the exchange, Subaru had not missed how Roswaal's cheek had stiffened at his words. Nor had he missed the fact that he had likely seized the initiative with his opening move.

—Roswaal knows that Subaru loops, but he cannot carry over memories of the loops like Subaru does.

That was the information Subaru had learned in the loop where it was revealed that Roswaal knew about Return by Death. He had also learned of Roswaal's incomprehensible obsession: that even while understanding his own death and demise, he intended to reach his desired outcome through Subaru's Return by Death.

Even now, Subaru could not help but feel disgust and rejection toward that way of thinking, but—

"I'm in the middle of trying various things through trial and error right now. I'd like your cooperation, too."

Roswaal's detached mindset—which could even be described as slighting his own life—gave Subaru the idea to put on an act to make Roswaal believe 'Subaru was moving just as Roswaal intended.' At this stage, there was no doubt that Roswaal was far more knowledgeable about the inner workings of the Sanctuary than Subaru. When it came to future events, it was difficult to align their information since Subaru did not know how much was written in Roswaal's Gospel, but recalling Roswaal's muttering upon seeing the Great Rabbit, Subaru deduced that it probably did not contain that much detail.

In other words, as long as he did not choose the wrong words, Subaru believed it was entirely possible to deceive Roswaal while extracting information from him.

"And a part of that trial and error is... escaping the Sanctuary without challenging the Trials, is that it? I-i-if so, aren't you being rather fa-a-ainthearted? With the Authority you possess, after endless attempts, you ought to be able to overcome any difficulty. I do not know how many times you have tried... but to-o-o give up and seek another method, isn't your resolve lacking just a bi-i-it?"

"I don't think being a stubborn idiot who only fixates on overcoming the Trials is a particularly sharp way to do things. If there's a smarter way, you choose it; that's all there is to it. Getting hung up on formality and losing sight of the essence is foolish. In short, all that's necessary is the fact that we managed to escape this place, and that the credit for it is given to Emilia... Am I wrong, Roswaal?"

While maintaining an outward veneer of cold composure, Subaru analyzed in real time whether there were any flaws in his statements. Scrutinizing his words right before placing them on his tongue and weaving them together wore down his nerves, but without exerting that much mental and physical strain, deceiving Roswaal would be impossible.

Ruthless pragmatism—that was likely the exact attitude Roswaal desired from Subaru. There was no doubt Roswaal wanted Emilia to become king, and wanted Subaru to aid in that endeavor. It was easy to imagine that the more Subaru used self-sacrificing methods, the more favorably Roswaal would view it.

And, sure enough.

"I-i- see... That is indeed a response quite to-o-o my liking."

At Subaru's reply, Roswaal smiled with satisfaction. Subaru barely managed to keep his eyes fixed on Roswaal's clown-makeup-painted white face as it turned sinister, biting the inside of his cheek to maintain his expression. Roswaal's unnerving gaze was that of someone looking at one of his own kind. In other words, Roswaal had judged that Subaru now stood on the same stage as him—the man he could not comprehend above all others. —A feeling of visceral disgust welled up from within.

Even with the self-awareness of his own twisted nature, he could not help but wonder where the line was that made them so different.

"While I find the shift in you-u-ur mindset quite gratifying, answering your question is rather tri-i-icky. There is no precedent. After all, the barrier has not been breached once since it was cast. I have never even considered whether there might be a flaw, and considering who cast it in the first place, one should cast aside any optimistic assumptions that she would make an oversi-i-ight."

"Echidna's barrier, huh..."

"Qui-i-ite so. The tomb in the Sanctuary is a gravestone for her, and the barrier is meant to prevent the half-bloods she used in her experiments during life from escaping. ...Though I-i- imagine you've alre-e-eady investigated this much on your own."

"Well, obviously. Just so you know, I also know about Ryuzu Meyer in the facility in the forest. I know about the clones, and that Garfiel holds the command authority."

"Aha-a-ah, look how quick this is go-o-oing."

Seeing Roswaal's willingness to disclose information one after another, Subaru also carefully laid his own cards on the table. In response, Roswaal closed his eyes and let out a sigh in a contemplative posture.

"The liberation of the Sanctuary is an indispensable achievement to make Emilia-sama king. Unorthodox, brute-force me-e-ethods would never satisfy the residents of the Sanctuary or the villagers of Arlam Village."

"As long as we can escape to the outside, there are plenty of ways to smooth that over. Besides, this isn't the only chance to make a name for ourselves. If we judge the timing is bad, we could just look for another opportunity..."

"Another opportunity? Why-y-y on earth would you say something like that?"

Seeing Roswaal widen his eyes in genuine bewilderment, Subaru realized he had made a slip of the tongue. A Subaru who could redo things as many times as needed and had hardened his resolve to sacrifice himself—that was the persona Subaru was currently playing. With that cold, ruthless mindset, if there were a chance to seize an achievement right in front of him, there would be no reason not to take it.

To keep his regret from showing on his face and before Roswaal's suspicion could deepen, Subaru said, "Think about it," and continued:

"Just as you recognize, I can hustle as much as needed to make Emilia king. After all, I can study all future information in advance and bring it back. To put it bluntly, the liberation of the Sanctuary is just a minor event with few people involved. We should devote our heart and soul to handling events with far greater impact, like the White Whale or the Witch Cult. —This isn't a stage worth investing that much effort into."

"...No-o-o, the liberation of the Sanctuary is essential. Allow me to make that a non-negotiable line. Because I must say, I am still rather skeptical regarding your po-o-ower."

"Skeptical...?"

As Subaru tilted his head at the unexpected turn in the conversation, Roswaal nodded, "Indeed."

"A-a-after all, I cannot confirm with my own eyes your Authority to redo things. It is not impossible that you might be leading me on with sweet ta-a-alk. Of co-o-ourse, if the results follow, I would have no choice but to be convinced, though."

"............"

"I state with certainty that my power... the cooperation of House Mathers is indispensable for Emilia-sama to become king. By liberating the Sanctuary and convincing me that your power to aid Emilia-sama is the real deal, it will lead to a fruitful relationship in the future... That is what I would like you to thi-i-ink, you se-e-e."

Faced with Roswaal's sound logic as he narrowed his eyes, Subaru was rendered completely speechless. Everything Roswaal said was a fact, and Subaru could not come up with an effective counterargument.

Backing was indispensable to prop Emilia, a royal candidate, up onto the stage of the Royal Selection, and there was no other influential figure in their vicinity capable of doing so besides Roswaal. It was also true that what Subaru was currently trying to scramble together was merely a stopgap to survive the immediate situation, offering no future prospects.

Following instructions so as not to displease the patron was the proper course of action. Considering the validity of Roswaal's opinion and the urgency of the situation, it was all too correct. Yet, even as he was tossed about by these waves of logic, Subaru sensed a feeling of incongruity.

It seemed as though Roswaal was far too fixated on the liberation of the Sanctuary itself.

"I think this might change the subject a bit, but..."

"Hm? Wha-a-at is it?"

Subaru raised a single finger to draw his attention, prompting Roswaal to tilt his head. Staring straight into those heterochromatic eyes of his, Subaru said:

"Do you have a reason why you absolutely must liberate the Sanctuary?"

"――――"

In response to the question, Roswaal remained silent. In silence, his smile slowly deepened, and his yellow gaze pierced Subaru.

—Once again, Subaru felt the atmosphere shift against his skin.
