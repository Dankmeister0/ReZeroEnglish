Arc 6 Chapter 35: "A Relationship on Thin Ice"

"—Subaru knows exactly how painful it is to be forgotten. That's why he would never say something like forgetting someone—not even in jest."

"—————"

Just before stepping back into the room, the voice carrying from within made Subaru catch his breath without meaning to.

He couldn't see her, but that beautiful, silvery-bell voice belonged to Emilia. The faint tension in her voice, and the words themselves, made the cheeks he'd arranged into a polite smile go stiff.

A strong trust was woven into that voice, matched by an equally great plea.

That trust was something Emilia directed toward "Natsuki Subaru"—not, under any circumstance, toward the Natsuki Subaru she'd always known. He felt that just as keenly now as he had in their earlier exchange.

"...Not even in jest. That's true. It's rare, but Lady Emilia happens to be right, for once."

The one who caught that voice and murmured it back self-mockingly was Ram, walking alongside Subaru. Unlike Subaru, who carried a bucket of water, her hands were empty; she hugged her own elbows, nails digging into her pale skin.

It was a self-admonishment, a regret, the painful continuation of a vow that would never reach its mark.

"Ram..."

"It's nothing but useless nonsense. Let it pass. As for what happened earlier—it would only worry Lady Emilia and the others. For Ram's own honor, it's best kept quiet. If word got out..."

"—————"

"...You really don't remember, do you."

Had she meant to say something like "it'd be scary if word got out"? Or was it Subaru who should have beaten her to the punch and thrown out that kind of joke first?

Just as before, when she'd sought some way to hold onto her sister's existence, Ram looked at Subaru with a faint, frayed despair lodged in her pale pink eyes. But that sentiment vanished again in the blink of an eye.

Whether it was Ram's strength or her weakness that wouldn't let him say anything more, he couldn't tell.

The depths of her heart were beyond him.

All that remained was the faint trace of where she'd once clung to the front of his chest.

"—Sorry to keep you waiting. We're back."

Without giving him even a moment to think it over, Ram resumed walking and stepped into the room. That very stubbornness was, no doubt, a display of her unbending will. And so Subaru showed her the consideration of not bringing it up.

When Subaru returned to the room a step behind her, he found the atmosphere inside still carrying some strain.

Still, it was a good deal better than right after Subaru's shocking confession.

As if to prove it, after Emilia gave the returning Subaru a "thanks for your trouble," the one who spoke up next was—

"...I'm sorry for the disgraceful display earlier. Might I start over, properly this time?"

"Oh—oh. No, it's on me too, I kinda... surprised you and all. Ah, no, sorry, I shouldn't cut you off. Go ahead."

"You needn't be so stiff about it. It's rather unsettling to have you speak to me so formally."

So he said, a faint smile rising on his face—the purple-haired, handsome young man. Julius, was it.

He was the one whose face had gone pale right after Subaru's confession earlier. Subaru had no idea how hard Emilia and Beatrice had worked to talk him around while he'd been off fetching water, but from the look of things, Julius seemed to have regained at least enough composure to hold a conversation.

Still, there were Ram's words in the corridor. When she'd heard about Julius, she'd called Subaru cruel. What exactly had she meant by that?

"Allow me to introduce myself properly. Julius Juukulius. I serve as knight to Lady Anastasia here... You and I are friends—or something along those lines."

"I see, nice to meet you... Why so unsure about that last part?"

"Unfortunately, it wouldn't be strange if you and I held slightly different views of each other. I considered us friends, but as for how you felt about it..."

"Literally lost to memory, huh."

"—. That's about the size of it."

There was a certain refinement Subaru could sense in the edges of his words, and it made him twist his lips.

According to Emilia and the others, they'd traveled together through a fairly grueling journey—and as the only other man among the group, it stood to reason they'd built up some measure of mutual trust.

That aside, there was Julius's thoroughly knightly bearing and what he'd just said. Even the current Subaru could tell that his first impression of Julius, before losing his memory, must not have been a good one.

"You don't need to worry about that at all. Subaru and Julius are reeeally good friends. We were there the whole time, so we can vouch for it."

"Yeah, well. There's really no need to worry about that, is there~? 'Cause what's actually a problem for Mister Knight over there... is a lot more than just that, y'know~"

Emilia cut into the exchange between the two men with her hands on her hips, while Meili chimed in with a mischievous glint in her eye.

Emilia's words were purely out of concern for the first meeting between Subaru and Julius, but what lay behind Meili's were something else entirely.

When Subaru frowned at that, Julius nodded as though he understood what she meant.

"Yes... indeed. Just as Meili says. Subaru, we do need to discuss the change that's come over you, but that isn't the only problem we're facing. Allow me to explain the rest here as well."

"Is this related to Lady Anastasia acting strange since this morning?"

"...Astute as ever, Lady Ram."

Julius answered Ram's remark, leaning against the wall, with his eyes lowered slightly. At that, Ram turned her narrowed eyes toward Anastasia and let out a small sigh.

"Barusu, hand over the water bucket. Ram's going to get breakfast ready."

"...Aren't we in the middle of something important?"

"We're in the same room. Ram can hear just fine. ...It's easier to keep her mind off things if her hands are busy."

Saying so brusquely, Ram slipped the water-filled bucket right out of Subaru's hands. She headed straight for the luggage in the corner of the room. Just as she'd said, Subaru watched her small back set to work preparing breakfast.

"Sorry. Ram's not usually like that, but..."

"No, there's no need for concern. I understand how she feels. —Not when the information she wanted so desperately just slipped right out of reach before her eyes."

Slowly, Anastasia shook her head at Emilia's apology. At that response, both Emilia and Beatrice's faces turned troubled.

Subaru couldn't tell what was catching in their minds—but the answer came a moment later, from Anastasia's own mouth.

"Now then—I do hesitate to pile yet more shocks onto a morning that's already brought a great deal of confusion... but continuing to conceal things would breed nothing but discord. So, trusting that the ground here can bear it, allow me to lay it bare."

"...That's a rather pompous and roundabout way of putting it, I suppose. Get to the point already."

"There's no need to be so on edge, Beatrice. —You and I are, most likely, something like sisters with no shallow bond between us. Just as you've guessed."

"—————"

At Anastasia's words, Beatrice's cheeks stiffened. Naturally, the girl standing beside Subaru reached out and gently pinched the cuff of his sleeve, as if seeking something to hold onto.

Catching sight of this out of the corner of his eye, Subaru hesitated a moment before taking her hand. Her fingertips trembled faintly with surprise, but the small palm soon accepted the warmth.

"...The relationship between you and your Contractor is heartwarming, ideal, even. It was my ideal too, to build that kind of relationship with Ana—but at the moment, that isn't going well."

"You speak of Anastasia like she's someone else entirely. So you really are..."

"Yes, your guess is correct. —The will currently dwelling in this body isn't Ana's. Anastasia Hoshin has fallen into a deep sleep somewhere in the depths of this body. The one exchanging words with you like this is a mere ghost, borrowing the flesh as its temporary master."

Emilia's breath caught, and the grip on Beatrice's hand tightened. Julius's face turned grave at the confession, while Meili wore her usual bored expression. Beside Meili, only Shaula, sitting cross-legged, had her attention fixed not on the confession but on Ram, busy preparing the meal.

With her back turned this way, absorbed in her task, Ram's expression couldn't be read.

And as for Subaru alone—

"...I have absolutely no idea what you're all talking about."

Left behind in the conversation as a matter of course, all he could do was wear a troubled expression.

—The body of the girl who called herself Anastasia Hoshin was currently ruled by an entirely different presence, its true spirit fallen into a deep sleep.

In short, that was the gist of what Anastasia—or rather, the Spirit calling herself Echidna—had explained.

The fact seemed to have shocked even Emilia and Beatrice, but for Subaru, it wasn't so much shocking as it was simply one more thing accelerating a situation he already couldn't make sense of.

After all, Subaru had no memory of Anastasia to begin with. So being told that the person introduced to him as Anastasia during their first greeting had actually turned out to be someone else entirely—

"...O-oh, is that so. That's... uh, that's rough, right?"

—was about all the reaction he could muster, and it came out sounding like he was talking about someone else's problem entirely.

Of course, he could guess from everyone else's reactions just how serious the situation actually was. To begin with, the very reason they'd come to this so-called tower made that clear.

They'd come to this tower seeking a way to save a girl locked in endless sleep, and people apparently afflicted by some kind of illness. —Or so he'd been told, at least.

"And now, right as we've finally reached the tower and things are about to begin, the very people we need most are battered and broken... Barusu's lost what little memory he had, and Lady Anastasia's consciousness is somewhere in the abyss."

"I... don't see a single bright spot in any of that..."

At Ram's blunt summary, Subaru felt like burying his head in his hands.

The problems were piling up—for one, Subaru himself hadn't even finished coming to terms with his own problem. In a situation like that, having one difficulty after another thrown at him left him with nowhere to move.

At this rate—

"I don't think it does any good if we all just keep staring at the ground. I get wanting to worry about it a lot, I really do. But... we can't just let ourselves stay down."

"—————"

"We came all the way to this tower carrying so many people's hopes on our shoulders. What's happened to Subaru and Anastasia right now is, frankly, really, really bad. But—"

Emilia clapped her hands together, drawing every eye in the room, and paused there.

Then, after a beat of silence, she swept her violet eyes over everyone present, and said—

"We can't stop moving. —I've always been taught that giving up isn't allowed."

Speaking with strong conviction, Emilia looked around the room, one face after another. When her gaze finally settled on Subaru, pierced by that beautiful violet, his throat tightened.

His chest warmed on its own. He didn't know what to say. But there was expectation in that gaze. Feeling it, Subaru knew he had to move—and he tightened his grip.

"Ow! That hurts, I suppose! Hey, Subaru!"

"Ah, sorry... no, actually, I'm not sorry. This is proof I'm fired up."

"It doesn't matter how good a face you make while saying it, it still hurts, I suppose! You'd better feel sorry!"

"S-sorry. My bad on the pain part. But being fired up isn't wrong. Yeah, that's right."

Subaru apologized to Beatrice's protest over having her delicate hand nearly crushed, and shook his head.

He understood the urge to wear a gloomy face in a situation like this. But brooding endlessly over a problem with no answer wouldn't get them anywhere. That much, at least, Subaru knew for certain from experience.

If he'd been alone, Subaru was certain he'd be at a total loss in a situation like this. But Subaru wasn't alone. Even with no memory of them, he had Emilia and the others speaking up for his sake, right beside him. If that was the case, then—

"Sure, I'm sorry my memory's gone clean out of my head and I'm causing everyone trouble. But that doesn't have to add up to hopeless. Look at it from a different angle. The way I am now, ideas that aren't weighed down by any needless baggage might come pouring out of me like water from a spring. Maybe that could be the hint we need to break through this whole situation, right?"

"...That's quite the optimistic take."

"What good does looking backward do anyone? What matters is up ahead. They always say the goddess of fortune only has hair on the front of her head, right? And besides, conquering this tower? What it needs might just be flexible thinking. The kind of thinking from another world entirely, unshackled from this world's fixed ideas!"

Riding purely on momentum, he pushed back against Anastasia—no, Echidna's—wry smile with even more force. It was undeniably empty bravado talking, words tossed out on the spot, but the mood in the room needed breaking.

At Subaru's thinking, a change came over the others who'd been wearing troubled expressions.

"...Mm, that's right. Subaru's always leapt over so many hard moments before. So I'm sure he'll get past this one too."

"Oh, that's the spirit! ...Though I guess that means I'm the one who has to do the hard work here, but hey, since I'm being counted on, I intend to do my level best. Especially with a cute girl cheering me on."

"Thank you, Subaru. —Yeah, I'm glad. Subaru really is Subaru, after all."

"—————"

At Emilia's murmur, gently pressing a hand to her full chest in relief, Subaru was caught completely off guard.

—Subaru really is Subaru, after all.

At that word of heartfelt relief she'd let slip, Subaru too felt himself relax.

It seemed like this would be alright, he thought.

Little by little, he could close the gap between himself and the Natsuki Subaru that Emilia knew. If he did, surely this stiff, awkward relationship of theirs could smooth out too.

"...Good grief. How optimistic."

"Oh?"

That was Julius, speaking up unexpectedly to Subaru, who'd just found relief in Emilia's words. He shrugged his shoulders, and at Subaru's glance, loosened his lips into a small "oh, it's nothing," before continuing—

"I only meant that, memory or no memory, you haven't changed one bit in how you raise your voice without being able to tell bravery from recklessness apart. Could one say it's precisely because you've forgotten how immense the obstacles standing before us are that such thinking is even possible?"

"You, on the other hand, wear that snide streak awfully well. So that's the real you, huh... no, the real you. Mr. Julius... no, Julius, is it."

"...I see. Perhaps, just as Lady Emilia says, the presence or absence of memory really is a trivial thing. In any case, it seems a person's core nature doesn't change so easily."

"I'm starting to get a pretty good picture of what kind of relationship we had, too. Good thing we're both such easy types to read."

It wasn't sharp enough to call hostile, but too thorny to call friendly—the exchange they traded. Even so, Subaru could feel certain that this was exactly the distance he'd always kept with Julius.

His first impression hadn't been wrong. When Subaru and Julius first met, neither could have made a good impression on the other. What sequence of events had led them to accept traveling to the tower together after that was a story for later, but—

"Nice to meet you again, Julius. Suffer appropriately until my missing memory comes back."

"Ah, it can't be helped. I'll accept this too as duty, without complaint. —Memory really is a trivial thing. Yes, indeed. ...Just as you say."

At Subaru's friendly greeting, Julius gave a quiet nod, and the conversation drew to a close there.

Memories lost, and problems cropping up one after another besides. It was true that this was hardly a situation anyone could face with a bright expression—and yet, there was strength in still lifting one's head regardless.

"The fact that the person at the center of it all isn't taking things too seriously might be the one saving grace here, if you could call it that."

"It's just not showing on the outside—inside, my heart's caught in a full-blown storm. Well, I'll have Emilia-chan comfort me gently later, just the two of us."

"—? Should I give you a lap pillow?"

"Ah, n-no, sorry. That's, uh, maybe a little too soon for that."

Puffing out his chest, Subaru had gotten carried away—only to instantly wilt. Of all things, Emilia's straightforward, gentle willingness to comfort him made his heart quail.

A lap pillow, no less. His gaze drifted, unbidden, toward her soft-looking white thighs—

"It's ready. Come help carry it, you perverted Barusu."

"Owww?!"

A brisk kick landed behind his knee, and Subaru crumpled to the ground with a yelp. Looking down at him sidelong, wearing an expression that seemed to say she found him filthy, was Ram.

"Being pointlessly upbeat is one of Barusu's few good points. Put it to use and tackle meal prep with the same enthusiasm. Cleanup too, and tidying, and every last chore there is."

"Isn't that just Ram wanting to slack off..."

"Ah, Master, I'll help too, ssu~! Meal! Meeeal!"

His protest against Ram's exercise of authority was drowned out by Shaula, who dove for the food as though she couldn't wait another second. Watching Shaula start carrying plates, Subaru gave in and joined the preparations himself.

"So the menu is... yeah, this really has that preserved-food look to it."

"That's because it is preserved food, in fact. Even the fresh ingredients we managed to bring thanks to Lady Emilia have run out. From here on, it's nothing but bland meals."

"Well, all the more reason to clear this tower as fast as we can and get back to civilization."

He'd heard somewhere that peace of mind only comes once clothing, food, and shelter are all in order. In that sense, it was doubtful just how "in order" life in this tower really was.

Especially where meals were concerned, combined with his lack of memory, this bland preserved food was being carved into Subaru's mind as the baseline for the "taste of another world." That was rough.

"Sorry to interrupt the meal, but there's something I'd like to confirm with you, Natsuki-kun."

"I'm hungry, so hungry... wait, me? What is it you want to ask?"

Subaru, who'd been role-playing as someone summoned to another world, sold into slavery, and grumbling about his meager meals, stopped eating at Echidna's words.

"Well, I do feel a bit awkward doing this right after we've all united to face things positively, but I'd like us to share what information we have about what happened to you, Natsuki-kun. After all, the only anomaly that's occurred since coming to this tower is your memory. To put it the other way around, none of us know when or where the same thing might happen to one of us too."

"Fair enough, I get that. Coming from the guy it happened to, sure, but memory loss really is a pain in the ass."

"Honestly, saying that about your own situation, I suppose..."

Beatrice wore an exasperated look at Subaru nodding along in agreement.

Still, Echidna's concern made sense. And in truth, Subaru needed to know the circumstances of how his own memory had been lost. Any lead on getting it back was surely tied to how it had disappeared in the first place.

"—————"

Beatrice sat at Subaru's right during the meal, Emilia at his left. The two of them had been by his side when he woke without any memory—and he wanted to know just what memories lay behind the trust and affection they showed him, trust and affection whose foundation he couldn't see.

—No, because he'd taken it upon himself that he had to remember.

"Still, from what I hear, I just woke up and my memory was already gone, so I don't really get it myself. ...How exactly did you all end up finding me, Emilia-chan?"

"That was... in the morning, I didn't see Subaru in the dragon carriage, and he wasn't in the room on the fourth floor either, so I got worried..."

The word "dragon carriage" caught in his mind, but Subaru held back from cutting in, wary of interrupting. It was probably related to that lizard in the green room—the earth dragon. He guessed it was something like a horse-drawn carriage. He shelved his curiosity to see the real thing for later.

"Betty wasn't quite as frantic as Emilia, I suppose. Still, it's a real bother to lose track of one's Contractor in a tower this unknown. So Betty went searching all through the tower together with Emilia..."

"Searching, and?"

"We found you collapsed in that white library on the third floor, and carried you to the green room."

At Emilia and Beatrice's joint explanation, Subaru let out a long "haaah."

"The third floor is one of the several levels spanning this tower. The level we're currently on is the fourth. We're working our way up toward the topmost level, the first floor. The third floor has already been cleared—thanks to your wits, no less."

"Thanks for filling in the details. ...My wits, huh."

Julius filled in the details that had been subtly missing. Even so, the content struck Subaru as somewhat lacking in credibility.

But at Subaru's reaction, Emilia followed up with a "no, really—"

"That's a completely true story. The rest of us were totally lost, but Subaru went and solved it all by himself, just like that... It was reeeally cool."

"Ha ha, thanks. ...'Total gibberish,' huh? Don't hear that one much these days."

"—————"

"Did I say something weird?"

It had just been a joke to cover his embarrassment at being praised, but for some reason Emilia had gone completely silent. For an instant he saw something deep stir in her eyes, but he couldn't tell what it was.

In any case, he couldn't catch up to whatever it was, like a ripple disturbing the surface of water, and so—

"By the way, about carrying me, collapsed like that, to that room full of plants..."

"That room is protected by a special Spirit. That Spirit has the power to heal people's wounds... that's why we've been letting Rem sleep there too."

"Okay, okay, got it. So that's why I got carried in there too. ...By the way, is there any chance that room is what caused my memory loss?"

"That's..."

Subaru closed one eye at Emilia's reaction, which suggested she hadn't even considered that.

This was purely Subaru's own suspicion, but though he had no idea what this so-called Spirit was thinking, if it took the form of a plant, wasn't some kind of cruelty conceivable?

Just as a flower uses its beautiful form and sweet nectar to have insects carry its pollen.

Or like a carnivorous plant, luring insects in through mimicry and draining every bit of their life force.

In exchange for healing the body, it drained away memories instead. —Wasn't that a possibility?

"Quite the leap of imagination, but that seems unlikely. If that were the case, I should have been the first to show some abnormality—I entered that room earlier, and for longer, than you."

"...Is the original owner of your body not waking up unrelated to that kind of abnormality, then?"

"Ana's situation is a problem that predates our arrival at this tower. There's next to no connection to the green room. As for the rest, let's see... did the earth dragon in that room seem to have forgotten you?"

"What?"

"That earth dragon was terribly attached to you. If the Spirit in that room really did have a nasty habit of draining memories, she might well have turned her back on you."

At Echidna's words, Subaru pictured the earth dragon that had nuzzled up to him right after he'd woken.

True, that earth dragon had been unusually friendly toward him. If the reason was that she'd originally been his earth dragon, that would explain it. But at the same time, there was something else that surprised him.

"That earth dragon was a female, huh..."

"Let's get back on track. It seems unlikely that room did you any harm. If you ask me, the room where you actually collapsed is the more likely culprit. You woke in that Taygeta library on the third floor, as I recall."

"Yeah, that's right. Subaru was collapsed on the white floor in that room..."

Recalling that moment, Emilia clenched her own wrist tightly.

"We rushed him to that room and were about to go call everyone else, but..."

"He woke up while we were calling out to him, and then he was already like this. So going by timing alone, it doesn't seem likely to be the green room's doing, I suppose. ...It really must be that library."

If there had been a problem, the cause lay in that library.

It seemed they'd all arrived at that shared conclusion, but it didn't quite click for Subaru. Probably because his imagination couldn't stretch far enough to picture what kind of room this "library" even was.

"That, uh, Taygeta? What kind of room is the Taygeta library, anyway? Somehow the name rings a bell..."

"That library is a room where the 'Books of the Dead' exist."

"'Books of the Dead'... that sounds like something out of a middle-schooler's fantasy, man."

Setting aside his curiosity over the name for now, Subaru latched onto the phrase "Books of the Dead." At his reaction, Julius drew his chin in and continued with "though nothing's confirmed," saying—

"That library housed row upon row of books, each bearing the name of a deceased person from every nation in the world. Reading a book requires some kind of qualification. Most likely, one can only read the book of a person with whom they share some connection."

"What tasteless business... what's written in these books?"

"The life of that deceased person, roughly speaking. An overwhelming rush of thought and feeling comes searing into your brain. It isn't something I'd care to check again by choice."

There was a weight to Julius's explanation, the kind of gravity only someone who'd actually lived through it could convey.

To put it plainly, having a dead person's thoughts sear themselves into your brain didn't sound like the kind of shock anyone would want to experience. And if he'd been found collapsed in a library capable of that—

"—Did I collapse from reading one of the books too? Don't tell me that's what fried my brain?"

"One can't say it's impossible. What does our 'Sage' make of that possibility?"

"...Wait, are you talking to me, ssu?"

Nodding along at Subaru's guess, Echidna turned a meaningful glance toward Shaula. As much as the meaning behind that glance mattered, the title "Sage" fit her about as poorly as a title could.

If anything, it would be fair to say Shaula was the person in this room furthest from anything resembling a "Sage."

"No matter how many times you ask, my answer doesn't change, ssu. I don't know a thing about this tower besides the rules, ssu. I'm just told to come down hard on rule-breakers. Whatever Master does in this tower, it's no business of mine, ssu."

"To begin with, I still have no idea why Shaula calls me Master in the first place..."

"You can rest easy. Barusu's answer to that was the same even before he lost his memory. He's just using it without a word because it's convenient. ...How low."

"Don't go jumping to your own convenient conclusions about me!"

At Shaula's gaze, which it would hardly be an exaggeration to call "maxed-out affection," all Subaru could do was voice his bewilderment.

Being doted on by a glamorous beauty might normally seem like something to be happy about, but without knowing the source of that affection, it only bred endless confusion.

Besides, there was something strangely off about her fondness and affection—something fundamentally different in nature from the earnest feeling Emilia and Beatrice directed at Subaru.

Whether that was some side effect of his missing memory, the present Subaru couldn't say.

"Anyway, that library's the suspicious one. If there's a chance some clue to getting my memory back is there, it's worth going to check it out."

"I agree, we should. It's a situation with more than enough difficulties piling up as it is. Fewer problems troubling us is always better. And besides, a situation like this makes me realize something plainly."

"Realize what?"

"—Just how much you'd been making up for, all this time."

At Julius's words, Subaru, one eye still shut, gave a small snort.

It wasn't out of embarrassment—it was a genuine, wry laugh. That was giving him far too much credit. Having to rely that heavily on Natsuki Subaru was about as dire as dire could get.

When it rains, it pours—it must have been quite the burden. Subaru became newly aware of just how much of an unwitting drag he'd apparently been.

"Anyway, once we're done eating, I want to go check out this library. If my memory's scattered around in there somewhere, I'd like to gather it up and stuff it back in."

"Honestly, saying it in such a weird way. You really are Subaru, through and through."

"That 'you really are Subaru' thing—that's probably not a compliment, is it?!"

Emilia said that with a faint smile at Subaru, who'd deliberately let his tone go light. At that, the air in the room loosened, if only a little.

With a course of action decided, there was a sense that, in a real way, they'd managed to become at least a little more forward-looking.

"I hate to break in on that mood, but there's one more thing I'd like to ask you, Natsuki-kun."

"Well, we've come this far, so go ahead and ask whatever. What is it?"

"Oh, it's nothing, really—just a question born of my own curiosity, not much to do with your memory or clearing the tower, but..."

With that preface, Echidna ran her fingers through her loosely waved hair. Then, amid her lovely expression, with only her pale blue-green eyes taking on a deep, intellectual light, she said—

"This word you keep using, isekai—what exactly does it mean?"

  • The source draws a wordplay distinction between "ナツキ・スバル" (katakana) and "菜月・昴" (kanji) — both read identically as "Natsuki Subaru" but represent, respectively, the current amnesiac persona and the "true" original self Emilia knew. This distinction is untranslatable in English script; I rendered it descriptively ("this 'Natsuki Subaru'... not... the Natsuki Subaru she'd always known") rather than inventing a visual marker not used elsewhere in the chapter.
  • "タイゲタ" romanized as "Taygeta" (a Pleiades star name, consistent with the constellation-themed naming convention likely used for this tower's floors/libraries); flagging for wiki verification since it isn't in the provided terminology list.
  • Shaula's verbal tic "〜ッス" is rendered as a trailing "ssu" throughout, per her established speech pattern of casual, clipped diction.
  • "ちんぷんかんぷん" (an old-fashioned idiom for "incomprehensible gibberish") — Subaru's joke that it's an outdated phrase was adapted as "'Total gibberish,' huh? Don't hear that one much these days" to preserve the joke's shape rather than translate literally.
  • Meili's elongated-vowel speech pattern (e.g., "なあい," "ってえ," "だしい") was rendered with informal contractions and trailing tildes ("~") to approximate the drawn-out, teasing cadence, since she is not in the provided quirk list but clearly has a distinctive one.
