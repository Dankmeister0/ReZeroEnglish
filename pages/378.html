Arc 5 Chapter 61: "Victim of the Domain"

Capella cocked her head slightly at the one-armed man who leveled his green dragon blade and spat his defiant words.

A long black braid spilled over her pale shoulder as she crossed her arms, leaning forward to emphasize her ample chest, and cast a bewitching glance at the helmeted man.

It was a seductive, alluring gaze — the kind of demonic charm that tickled a man's instincts even when he knew it was a trap.

Though whether such witchery actually worked depended entirely on the man in question.

"Sorry to interrupt the sexy routine, but if you're going for seduction, it ain't gonna work on me. I've got an ironclad virgin's mindset — gettin' turned on by some random lady I just met is way too high a difficulty setting. Cracks me up just thinkin' about it."

"Self-deprecating jokes mixed with a bit of restraint... whether that trick works on me, aside — you've got the air of a man who's hard to read. Playin' the clown to lure me into lettin' my guard down — what exactly were you plannin' to do with that thick thing of yours?"

"Don't go spoutin' dirty jokes left and right, you're a woman. Honestly, it's a real turn-off."

"—"

Capella's provocative gaze went blank with astonishment at the man's reply. But the surprise faded quickly, and she let out a throaty chuckle, thoroughly delighted.

"Kyahahahaha! What is that — that look, that age, and that line? Sounds like the dreamin' maiden lives nowhere but inside your own head. Guess you're just another resident of la-la land, only with a different face and address. Kyaa, makes me wanna dirty you up."

"Hate to spoil the fun, but don't make me say it twice. I ain't got the motivation today. Ask me when I ever do have it and I'll come up short, but today especially, I don't."

Capella kicked her feet back and forth with a gesture that didn't suit her current appearance at all, while the man — Al — responded with an attitude that was thoroughly listless and put-upon.

It wasn't an act — he genuinely seemed reluctant about his current role. Quite different from the other two upstairs, Capella thought, her expression growing ever more puzzled.

"No motivation, too much trouble, and yet you won't budge from this spot. Your words are full of contradictions. So why bother goin' along with this whole debauched circus, then?"

"You're the one callin' it a debauched circus?"

"'Course I'm callin' it that. Way I see it, everything that happens in this world is nothin' more than a way to kill time. All of you, in the end, belong to me. My love is generous, merciful, and asks for nothin' in return but love for me... everything else, you're all free to do as you please."

Spreading both arms wide, Capella smiled a coquettish smile, her expression like a poisonous flower in bloom.

Watching that smile, Al lowered the tip of his green dragon blade with a soft breath. He cracked his neck and let out a long, drawn-out sigh.

"Y'know, you're somethin' else. Pretty different from the Sin Archbishops I know."

"Oh? You know some of the other lowlife meat, then? The perverted grudge-lovin' female flesh? The tiny-vesseled virgin bastard? The lowborn glutton brat with rotten taste? Or maybe the delusional, self-satisfied spirit who's got everything backwards? Whichever it is, sounds like terrible company to keep! Didn't your parents ever tell you — choose your friends carefully?"

"...Unfortunately, my friends were exactly the type parents warn you about."

"My condolences, then. But even so, my magnanimous love could still wrap around you, y'know? Take off that helmet, show me your true face, hold me, love me, devote yourself to me!"

No matter how coldly or curtly she was rebuffed, Capella pressed on with her courtship — the ultimate love-brained mindset, if calling something this extreme and one-sided, this utterly disregarding of the other party, an act of plundering affection could rightly be called "love" at all.

Naturally, few responded to a courtship that wasn't so much clumsy as it was devoid of any real humanity.

Al raised the green dragon blade he'd lowered back into position and slowly shook his head.

"Sorry. I'm flattered, but we barely know each other, and it'd be embarrassing if my friends started gossiping about it, so I'll have to pass."

"Worryin' about what people think — you've got a cute side after all. Am I really that bad? — I'd have thought I was right up the alley of a masochistic scrap of male meat who likes bein' jerked around by women."

"...Ah?"

"Insolent, overbearing, with a sharp look in her eyes. A figure that's almost violently voluptuous. Shorter than I am right now, and the type who shows a fair bit of skin. Moody, talks a lot, but not haughty for no reason — her arrogance is backed by intellect and confidence. Leaves a lot to her subordinates, but rarely leans on them. Not an easy position, and not an easy woman, but not one you dislike, either."

As she rattled the description off, Capella's form began to warp and shift visibly.

Her voluptuous body remained but her height shrank, her clothing changing into a dress that boldly bared her shoulders and back. Her droopy eyes sharpened into an upward tilt, her gaze growing keen and brimming with self-assured confidence. What emerged was a beautiful woman with long blonde hair let down.

She wasn't the living likeness of anyone actually connected to this city — and yet, somehow, she carried an air reminiscent of a woman close to Al.

"—"

"Oop, blonde, is it not? In Lugunica, that ought to cover the most candidates... well then, hup, hup, hup — red... hm, no, orange."

Watching for the slightest shift in Al's reaction, Capella changed her hair color one after another. She tried black, brown, green, blue — and the moment she moved into the red family, she paused to gauge his reaction before settling on orange.

With just that one change, the impression she gave grew all the more strongly reminiscent of that familiar woman.

"Creepy... where the hell did you see the princess?"

"Never seen her, never spoken to her, never even given her a thought, y'know? I just guessed the face and body you like from your reactions. I'm a woman who gives her all, see? Of course I'd go all out to be liked by the one I want to like me."

"Reactions or whatever, but you can't even see my face..."

"Your voice, your gestures, the pauses in your speech. The angle of your neck tells me where you're lookin', your attitude. Our conversation tells me your personality, your nature, your likes and dislikes."

"—"

"I don't miss a single move you make. Puttin' my whole soul into bein' loved — that's how I give myself. I'm givin' this much of myself, so... look at me. Look only at me. Don't spare a glance for anyone else. My face, my body, my voice, my attitude, my gestures — every single bit of it! It's gotta be dead center, right in the sweet spot of your taste!"

As she spoke, Capella — now transformed into a likeness of Priscilla — raised her voice. Her assertion was almost refreshingly direct, but so relentlessly direct that it became a thorny, piercing courtship aimed straight at its target.

Al no longer bothered shaking his head or answering in words — he simply answered Capella by radiating pure fighting spirit from every inch of his body. At that silent reply, Capella's face filled with disappointment and hatred.

"You selfish scrap of male trash... just what is it about me that you don't like, huh?"

"Don't get the wrong idea. I don't like you or dislike you — you're nothin' to me... Sorry, that's a lie. Turns out I do dislike you, 'cause you're creepy."

"—! You fickle, rotten scrap of trash meat—!!"

Stamping her foot in fury, Capella's right arm transformed from the shoulder down into the head of a giant wolf. The beast's head, snarling ferociously, lunged at breakneck speed toward Al, who stood rooted in place. Row upon row of blade-like fangs closed in to devour his entire upper body — but at the very last instant, Al slipped through the gap between the closing fangs and dove sideways out of the way.

"Don't think you've gotten away that easy!"

"I don't think that! To the side! And next is behind me!"

As Al rolled, an enormous palm came crashing down over his head next. The massive arm's five fingers were each the size of a human torso — being caught by it would be no different from being seized by a giant serpent.

But Al dodged this attack too, calmly backstepping out of range. Then, swinging the green dragon blade around behind his back, he firmly blocked the wolf's fangs as they swept wide in an attempt to bite his waist in two.

"O-o-ohhhh, Dona!"

Unable to fully kill the beast's momentum, Al's body slid forward even as he held it back with the blade. But midway, the earthen wall he'd chanted rose up from the floor, thrusting up into the transformed right arm from directly below.

The wolf's jaw was knocked upward, and it let out a pained howl; thrown off balance by the sheer mass of the right arm, Capella's main body staggered. Into that opening, Al — having dodged the left arm's furious assault — lunged forward with a vengeance.

"Dona! Over here too, Dona!"

"—!"

The Dona spell Al kept spamming was the lowest-tier earth-attribute magic.

Both its power and its durability as a wall lived up to its billing as the lowest tier of magic, nothing more. And yet Al wielded this spell as the best possible tactic in a life-or-death fight.

Create obstacles, block sightlines, generate footholds — exactly as he was doing this very instant.

"Hyaaaaah!"

The rising earthen walls blocked the paths of Capella's writhing right and left arms at a distance. Further still, an earthen wall rose right in front of Capella's main body, blotting out her view.

With everything set in place, Al's body launched into the air — using the force and speed of an earthen wall surging up from the ground to catapult himself like a spring-loaded trap.

The instant Capella looked up at his battle cry, the green dragon blade flashed across her slender neck, and her head went flying.

The face resembling Priscilla's tumbled through the air as a tremendous gush of blood sprayed from the wound. Capella's blood carried some unidentified, poison-like effect — the very cause of Crusch's suffering.

Naturally, there was a rule against touching that spraying blood at all costs—

"Like hell I'll fall for that, you fraud!"

—but Al stepped mercilessly into the pool of blood and thrust his green dragon blade forward.

Without hesitation, the blade's tip drove into the back of the now-headless Capella and burst out through the center of her cleavage, dealing a further fatal wound to an opponent he'd already believed dead. But it didn't end there.

"Hyah! El Dona!!"

Kicking the impaled body and sending it flying forward, Al chanted with full force — a spell one tier above Dona, El-class magic, activated inside Capella's body, originating from the green dragon blade.

Naturally, Capella's flesh couldn't contain the mass swelling from within, and it burst.

With a comically stupid poomf, Capella's body blew apart. Limbs tore off and scattered, pink innards and vivid red splattered across the underground chamber. In the cold air, the scraps of flesh steamed, and the grisly end result was more than enough to make one look away.

"...How about that! Hah, hah, that oughta do it—"

Panting hard, shoulders heaving, Al called out to the scraps of what had once been Capella.

How about that indeed — no living creature could possibly survive destruction on this scale. There shouldn't have been anything left to answer Al's declaration of victory.

"Ahh, that's just cruel, ain't it? Didn't need to go quite this far."

"Damn it."

Al's irritated click of the tongue was aimed at that same breezily malicious voice, unchanged as ever.

But the voice wasn't coming from the scattered scraps of flesh — it came from the direction of the head he'd first sliced off, from the spot where Capella's severed head had landed. There, Capella's head lay rolled onto its side on the ground, thoroughly enjoying Al's reaction.

"Cuttin' off your head, smashin' your heart — and it's still not enough? That's straight-up cheatin'..."

"Bein' fine even after my head's cut off and my heart's smashed is just who I am, y'know... Though it's rare to see you get this merciless right out the gate. I was seducin' you with a face dead in your strike zone, wasn't I? Did I misjudge? Or are you the type who expresses love by hurtin' the object of it?"

As Al let out a breath heavy with futility, Capella's severed head lifted itself up.

The cross-section of her neck writhed, and black flesh far too voluminous to have fit inside came pouring out. It formed the base of a new neck, and the flabby lump of flesh gradually took shape as limbs; the black surface was concealed beneath pale, fair skin, and it returned to its former self — no, it completed itself as the golden-haired little girl.

"...And what about the flesh scattered around here?"

"Don't need it, so it just melts."

When Al glanced over with an exasperated look, the scattered scraps of Capella's exploded body were dissolving with an audible sound. The innards and limbs turned into a sludgy black mire, bubbling and foaming before vanishing entirely.

Even the way it disappeared felt like some kind of harassment, and it was thoroughly draining just to watch.

"Still, you came straight for the beheadin' without a shred of hesitation. The one upstairs... seems like that was a fake, but don't you have comrades who got put out of commission from bathin' in my blood? Gettin' yourself all soaked in it like that — aren't you scared?"

"Don't try to bluff me. I don't know what conditions are involved, but it's already been proven that just gettin' splashed doesn't send you straight to the grave. All that dodgin' I did was a waste."

"—? I didn't see any sign of you dodgin', though."

"It's somethin' that happened while you weren't lookin'. If the neck and heart don't work, guess the only thing left is smashin' that severed head to bits. Wonder how many times it'll take."

Al's sigh, unusually exhausted, was heavier than the one he'd let out right before the fight began. No doubt some of that came from having truly grasped how troublesome Capella was, but it seemed to be weighing far more heavily on his shoulders than this exchange alone could account for.

In any case, having finished regenerating, Capella showed not the faintest trace of damage from the clash just now.

On top of her mutation and transformation abilities, a regenerative power that rivaled immortality — unable to be killed by beheading and destroying the heart, two of the most reliable methods of execution, she was truly a monster.

But being hard to kill wasn't synonymous with being unbeatable.

"There's still options — freezin' your whole body solid and sealin' you away, or chuckin' you into the Great Waterfall."

"Usually when someone realizes they can't kill me, fallin' into despair is the customary response, but you're a real unbreakable scrap of meat, aren't you. Still, all that bravado's fine, but can you actually pull it off? I've seen you're good at runnin', hidin', and pullin' little tricks, but I don't figure you've got the arm to actually kill me."

"True enough — even if I died a hundred times over, whether I could actually kill you is anybody's guess. Truth is, I'm already about halfway there... But aren't you forgettin' somethin'?"

Tapping his shoulder with the flat of the green dragon blade, Al rattled the metal fittings of his helmet. When Capella tilted her head at the question, the blade's tip pointed upward.

What it indicated wasn't the city hall — but somewhere further out still.

"I've already figured that you thought you outmaneuvered us by leavin' the control tower unguarded. Which means the folks who were headed for 'Lust's' control tower are gonna come rushin' straight back here. Long as I keep stallin' for time, you're gonna find yourself checkmated, naturally."

"—"

"I'll have you know, when it comes to buyin' time, I'm somethin' else. I'll mock you every which way and pin you down right here. So if you're gonna run, now's your chance."

Facing Capella alone, Al pressed her to withdraw, citing the arrival of reinforcements as his reason. At his words, Capella furrowed her brow, puzzled — and at her suspicious reaction, Al let out an "Mm?"

"What's with that reaction? Got a complaint about my flawless recommendation to retreat, or—"

"You really think I'd bother marchin' solo into enemy territory without already havin' arranged somethin' to hold back the forces you sent out?"

"...Mm?"

"I left a couple of things behind along the road for the muscle-bound lump and the lady swordsman — two of the finest dolls among ours, no less... You really think those reinforcements are gonna break free anytime soon?"

"Gah!"

Groaning at this unexpected turn, Al's face turned to look up at the hole above. As though reading the expression hidden behind his helmet, Capella continued, "And by the way—"

"—that communication mirror you all carry, I've gone ahead and sealed it off, y'know? So no matter how frantically you try to get in touch, it won't connect to anywhere."

"How the hell can you even do that!?"

"Seems that Magic Vessel was made by a 'Witch,' see. And the Witch Cult, still holdin' onto that connection, has all sorts of backstage secrets layin' around — includin' how to scramble a communication mirror's wavelength."

In the face of such thoroughly prepared spitefulness, Al's stated aim had already missed its mark.

Al had no way to verify the truth of Capella's claim, but in fact, outside the city hall, the two who had gone to bring 'Lust' to justice — Wilhelm and Garfiel — were both currently engaged with her forces. The odds of them making it back in the middle of a fight between a monster and an eccentric were, it had to be said, slim.

In other words—

"If you're out of moves in a spot like this, how about we just wrap things up already?"

"...I wouldn't quite call it a dead end."

At Al's evasive reply, Capella smiled faintly.

Then, the very instant after that blink, the smile dissolved. Once again, the little girl's form transformed into a shapeless mass of flesh, and Capella's mass began to swell explosively.

Faster and faster, her form shed the constraints of a human shape and grew monstrously huge.

The petite little girl, the bewitching woman, the dashing young man, the rugged warrior — with a force incomparable to any of her previous transformations, her body swelled, and a roar thundered through the underground chamber.

And what appeared after that blink was a black dragon, its dark hide blending seamlessly into the darkness.

"...Right, guess you can turn into a dragon too."

With no walls dividing the space, the underground chamber was somewhat more spacious than the floors of the city hall above. But even in a space like this, the black dragon's massive body could not easily fit.

At Al's muttered remark, the dragon's golden eyes narrowed, and Capella opened her jaws as if in reply. From within, a merciless, searing breath was unleashed.

"—!"

The blasting flames scorched the underground air, and Al dashed through the whitened, glowing chamber.

His chant, drowned out by the roaring blast, conjured an earthen wall within the range of the breath. But before the overwhelming heat, it failed to serve even as a momentary shield and was incinerated instantly — but that wasn't Al's actual aim.

"Gah, gu—"

The black dragon's jaw, still discharging its breath, took a direct hit once again from an earthen wall thrusting upward. The mouth breathing white-hot flame was forced violently shut, and the black dragon reeled backward as its own fire seared its nose and throat.

However, the breath already expelled before the interruption continued its effect. As Al ran to escape its range, green flame caught onto his back—

"Damn it! Dona! Gubuh!?"

His half-desperate chant thrust up an earthen wall, which slammed straight into Al's side. The blow sent him flying, and he tumbled into the waterway with his backside still burning. But the instant his back touched the bottom of the channel, another chant made an earthen wall spring upright, launching him straight out of the water's surface.

"—Shh!"

Drenched, Al burst out of the water. In that same instant, the black dragon's sharp claws gouged into the bottom of the channel. Water sprayed up, and the earthen wall that had launched Al was torn apart underwater. A hair's-breadth escape — but the ferocious assault on Al continued unabated. Every single attack, he dodged, dodged, and dodged again.

He slipped through unbelievably narrow gaps, took attacks from behind he shouldn't have been able to see coming, made up for the evasive ability his physical body lacked by casting magic attacks on himself, and avoided every fatal blow.

"Gyiiiiin!"

"D-d-d-d-Dona—!!"

Losing patience, the black dragon spun its massive body, unleashing a sweeping blow of its tail.

Against that blow, which arced through the air whipping up a violent gale, Al deployed five layered earthen walls simultaneously in front of him with overlapping chants — reducing the force of the strike to the absolute limit while leaping back hard himself and taking the blow on his green dragon blade; even so, he was sent flying, tumbling across the ground.

Rolling extravagantly to dissipate the impact, Al tried to stand, using the green dragon blade as a cane. But the damage hadn't fully cleared.

He hastily tilted his helmet, and from the gap at its neck, his jaw let loose a violent spray of vomit.

"Gehh, geholl... Damn it, this time I'm the one on the receivin' end... seriously outta luck..."

"I don't think that's quite it, though. Given your actual skill level, you're on a run of luck so absurd it's hard to tell what exactly is workin' in your favor..."

To Al, lamenting his current predicament, the black dragon Capella offered a puzzled sort of admiration.

Even from her perspective, there was a strange, nagging sense of wrongness clinging to Al's desperate defense. Attacks that shouldn't have been readable, follow-ups that couldn't have been predicted — in every case, Al survived by using every ounce of his own ability to its absolute maximum. As though he'd understood each choice before it was ever made.

But neither of them had time to dwell on that.

Because—

"...Did I, perhaps, go a bit too wild here?"

That was Capella's mutter as she looked up — prompted by a faint tremor traveling through the ground, and by the fatal creaking sound coming from the city hall above, which had been taking damage the whole time.

The building, its foundation already gravely damaged by repeated fighting, had just been dealt the finishing blow by the battle in its underlying foundation — this very underground space. As a result, the hole on the first floor that had been prepared to drop Capella through began widening before their eyes, cracks spreading not just through the floor but through the entire structure.

What awaited at the end of that was a conclusion even a child could see coming — collapse.

"You gotta be kiddin' me! If the Domain unravels, I'm dead!"

Seeing a chunk of the floor above start to fall, Al forced himself up through the pain. On unsteady legs, he threw himself straight into the flowing waterway.

A splash rose, and his body vanished, carried off on the current flowing beneath the city. Having watched him go, Capella, still in her black dragon form, looked up at the crumbling ceiling and—

"Hmm, well, that's killed the mood. Tired myself out playin', anyway. Guess that's enough for now."

Muttering, sounding bored, the black dragon yawned.

That vanishingly rare sight, witnessed by no one, was swallowed up by the building's collapse — pouring down like a landslide — and swept away.

"—Ugh."

At the site of the collapse, a faint groan rose up.

The sound came with the noise of rubble being pushed aside, and a somewhat larger chunk of stone tumbled down off the pile of debris. With that impact, the pile began to crumble, and a pale arm peeked out through the resulting gap.

The slender arm thrashed and flailed as if reaching for something, breaking down the pile of rubble bit by bit from the edge.

A few minutes later, what finally crawled out from the pile of debris was Ferris, looking utterly battered.

"Kohh, kohh."

Coughing, spitting out the mouthful of dust and blood that had gotten in.

The gritty stuff filling the gaps between tongue and teeth — was it gravel or blood? Either way, it hardly mattered to Ferris. Though a good rinse of the mouth would've been nice—

"Never thought the city hall would actually collapse... I went and died pointlessly once already...!"

Ferris wiped the blood off his face with the lining of his soiled clothes. Once the grime was cleared away, what showed underneath was a strikingly well-formed, lovely face.

It hardly looked like the state one would expect a few minutes after being helplessly crushed in the collapse of a five-story building.

"That doesn't matter right now... That's right, Anastasia-sama!"

His cat ears standing on end, Ferris spoke the name and looked around.

Anastasia had volunteered to serve as Crusch's stand-in, cooperating with the dangerous decoy operation. Just as planned, they'd managed to respond to 'Lust's' assault — but the result they'd gotten out of it was, in a word, disastrous. Still, an ally was an ally.

Confirming whether she'd lived or died was necessary — not least out of a sense of obligation to Julius.

"If she got crushed just like I did..."

He glanced around anxiously, peering under the rubble close by. At the moment the building had shown signs of collapse, Anastasia had been in the same room as Ferris.

When the floor gave way beneath them, she must have been swept up just as helplessly as he had been. As long as she hadn't died instantly, Ferris's healing magic would be more than enough to save her.

"If she'd just make a sound, it'd be so much easier to find her!"

Disaster rescue work of this sort wasn't something Ferris's slender arms were built to carry out.

Saving the wounded might be his specialty, but the process of getting to them was not his forte. Combined with the fact that they'd failed to get any information out of 'Lust,' a helpless frustration built up inside Ferris. And just then—

"Ah, so Ferris-san made it out okay too."

"—!"

There was the sound of footsteps crumbling through rubble, and when Ferris spun around with a start, he saw a person in view. Her dyed green hair coming loose in strands, smiling softly, was Anastasia — the very person he'd been searching for.

Swaying the hem of her hard-to-move-in kimono, she approached Ferris with tentative, cautious steps.

"Anastasia-sama, you're all right?"

"As you can see, yeah. And that's my line too, y'know. You're the one who really managed to come out of that collapse okay, Ferris-san."

"Well, that's..."

Strictly speaking, he hadn't been fine, but Ferris avoided answering right away. He wasn't obligated to explain, and it wasn't exactly a pleasant thing to talk about. Not pressing the matter as Ferris fumbled for words, Anastasia instead swept her gaze over the rubble underfoot and the collapsed city hall together, and said,

"Good thing we got everyone evacuated. This here's turned into a real incident, hasn't it."

"An incident..."

At Anastasia's troubled frown, Ferris likewise turned to look.

An incident, she called it — but was this really a situation you could talk about with that kind of tone? It was certainly an incident, no doubt, but her way of putting it lacked a certain gravity.

To begin with, the very cause of this collapse was—

"I'm guessin' it was the fight between Al-san and 'Lust' down below..."

"—"

"It's all buried real good, so diggin' it up's gonna be a real chore, I'd think. If he managed to catch that underground waterway right, I'd like to think he survived, but..."

Of course, that also meant they needed to consider the possibility that Capella had survived as well.

From what Ferris had seen, Capella's regenerative power was beyond human comprehension. Having half her face blown away by Anastasia's magic and still remaining unfazed was simply abnormal. Though since Ferris's own runaway life-force magic had worked on her, it presumably wasn't the result of some unnatural constitution.

"Come to think of it, Anastasia-sama, where did you learn a spell like that?"

"...Is that really relevant right now?"

"I'd heard you couldn't fight, so it was a bit of a surprise."

At the meeting to divide up their fighting strength, she had declared herself as having no combat capability at all — neither she herself nor the artificial spirit she carried possessed any such power, she'd claimed.

But then, that spell that had burned Capella's face—

"—That's somethin' I'd like to hear about too, actually."

"—"

As Anastasia fell silent at Ferris's question, a third voice cut in.

Where the two of them turned to look, kicking rubble aside as he approached, was a drenched Al. He came back tilting his head, water pouring out from the gap in his helmet.

"Good to see you made it out okay. Managed to jump into the waterway just right, huh?"

"Felt like I got crushed about three times over, though. Well, that ain't important right now. There's somethin' more important to talk about."

As Anastasia welcomed back the hero of the hour, Al said this and drew his green dragon blade. Its tip was aimed straight at Anastasia, and she drew her well-shaped brows together.

Al and Anastasia stood facing each other. Ferris found himself positioned between the two, the three of them forming a triangle.

"Just what exactly is the meanin' of this? If it's a joke, it ain't a funny one."

"I ain't plannin' on makin' it a joke either. Puttin' the cat-eared bro aside, you comin' out of this alive doesn't sit right with me. And on top of that..."

"I just had an ace up my sleeve, is all. I feel bad for keepin' quiet about it, sure, but I can't exactly go layin' everything out in the open either..."

"That's one answer, sure, but the real problem is your attitude. Barely survivin' a collapsin' buildin' with your life, and then makin' small talk with an acquaintance in the ruins... that attitude doesn't match your expression or your feelin's at all. You know what you call someone who talks to people wearin' a face like that?"

"—"

Under Al's rapid-fire interrogation, Anastasia's words stopped. And yet her expression remained fixed in that same ambiguous smile.

And as Ferris watched, Al spoke the words he'd left hanging.

"You call someone like that a 'Witch.'"

"...Good grief, what a thing to say."

The voice that replied sounded worldly, oddly parched of emotion.

The soft, gentle smile peeled away, replaced by a weak, sardonic one. Though that sarcasm seemed aimed not at anyone else, but as self-mockery turned inward.

"As far as I'm aware, 'Witch' is never a word used in a good sense."

"You think I said it meanin' it as some friendly greetin'? You've got the meanin' pegged exactly right, you conniving fox."

"What's going on here?"

Glaring at the two of them, who seemed to understand each other perfectly well — Anastasia with her changed manner of speech, and Al — Ferris sought some room to insert his own opinion into the moment. At Ferris's attitude, Al shrugged his armless left shoulder, his gaze still fixed on Anastasia,

"Point is, this little lady right now might look like the little lady, but what's inside is different. That wicked fox hangin' from her neck — Echidna — is in there."

"It's simply the result of prioritizing Ana's survival, nothing more. Being spoken of so disparagingly, as though it were some malicious act, is rather disappointing."

At Al's hostile words, Anastasia — Echidna — replied with a troubled look. Al snorted "Hmph!" from behind his helmet.

"Don't go actin' high and mighty when you're the one tryin' to take over her body."

"Take over — that's a misunderstanding. Where exactly did you see any sign of that?"

"Your terrible Kararagi dialect and pretendin' to be the little lady is the proof, ain't it. Unfortunately, your true colors showed right through, on account of bein' a 'Witch' who can't understand human subtleties."

"That's quite enough! This conversation isn't going anywhere!"

With Al snapping back at every single thing Echidna said, Ferris finally exploded.

It seemed certain that Al knew something about the situation, but what mattered right now was Anastasia. It seemed the vessel was currently under Echidna's control.

"First off — is Anastasia-sama actually all right? It's not like you just protected the body and tossed her insides off somewhere, is it?"

"She's fine. Ana needs to remain intact in both body and mind. Otherwise there'd be no point in resorting to a forbidden method at all."

"A forbidden method...?"

"I mean this state, right now. Ana's Gate is a somewhat complicated thing, you see. She can't operate it freely on her own. I can handle it, but the strain is considerable. That's all there is to it."

Touching her slender chest, Echidna clouded Anastasia's expression.

Al still held his blade at the ready, looking thoroughly unconvinced, but Ferris stepped between the leveled blade and Echidna, continuing on.

"And pretending to be Anastasia-sama when you spoke to us just now?"

"It would've been unnatural not to check on the well-being of a fellow collaborator, wouldn't it? I disguised myself as Ana herself so as not to show you our weaknesses. As I've just revealed, Ana's body is unstable. I wanted to keep my fusion with her hidden... though it seems I was found out anyway."

"Julius doesn't know about this power... right? He didn't even know you existed. What are you going to do about that?"

"That's a matter between Ana, Julius, and the Iron Fang. I don't believe I'm obligated to answer your questions with speculation of my own."

It was a reasonable enough line of thinking. The sense of wrongness Ferris had felt around Anastasia amid the rubble made perfect sense if attributed to the discrepancy of dealing with a different presence — Echidna — inside.

Between the fact of having been saved by her magic and the track record of having protected Anastasia's body from the collapsing building, at least Ferris's own doubts were cleared.

"But it looks like someone over there still isn't ready to lower that sword?"

"...Tch."

At Ferris's question, Al clicked his tongue and reluctantly sheathed his green dragon blade. Watching him do so, Ferris took a fresh look at his battered body.

Burns, scrapes — he looked to have taken quite a beating.

"Here, let me see, I'll patch you up quick. Whoa, this part's just gouged right out...! Hyah, what is this, this gash — how'd you even survive this?"

"With my skill level, turnin' a fatal wound into just a deep one is about my limit. If I hadn't lost the bet, it'd probably have been better than this... eh, whatever."

"—?"

Tilting his head in confusion, Ferris's palms healed every last one of Al's wounds.

The pale blue light's healing power was impressive as always, and in no time at all, the countless wounds all over Al's body closed cleanly. Confirming this, Al offered a brief word of thanks, and,

"Same as me, you two ladies survived too. No doubt about it, 'Lust' is definitely still alive somewhere. Bein' buried under some rubble ain't gonna set my mind at ease much."

"About that, actually."

At Al's call for caution, Echidna raised a hand. In her raised palm was a communication mirror, and she gave its surface a light tap with her other hand.

"Actually, right up until the building collapsed, the communication mirror had gone completely silent — not a peep out of it."

"Heard that already. 'Lust' — or rather, the Witch Cult, huh? Sounds like the Witch Cult's got some method passed down for messin' with Magic Vessels. So they were jammin' our means of contact..."

"That's likely what happened, yes. Though if it truly was the work of the Sin Archbishop, it seems we've escaped that particular crisis, at least for now."

"...You're beatin' around the bush."

"Apologies, force of habit. In short — the communication mirror's function has been restored."

At those words, Al and Ferris both looked startled at once.

Al, at the possibility of being able to reach the other others carrying communication mirrors. Ferris, at the possibility that whatever had been jamming the mirror's use had been eliminated.

"What does that mean? 'Lust' stopped the jamming... you mean she died?"

That a monster boasting such regenerative power could have died simply from being buried under rubble seemed far too implausible. Just as Al had said, the three of them here had survived too.

It was hard to believe that an undying monster specialized in raw vitality would meet its end from something like this. If there were any other conceivable possibility, it would be—

"She sustained damage severe enough that she can no longer continue the jamming. Or else, by stopping it, she's luring us into letting our guard down while she lies low, waiting for her chance..."

"That doesn't seem to match that wicked thing's personality, somehow."

"Yes, I agree. Rather than lying low, something bolder would suit her better... For instance — how about the idea that she's disguised herself as one of the three of us here right now? The real one already dead, and she's slipped neatly into their place. Wouldn't that be just the sort of trick 'Lust' would favor?"

Ferris felt a chill run through him at Echidna's speculation.

He remembered the monster that had disguised itself perfectly as Crusch, before the collapse. Back then, he'd watched the transformation happen right before his eyes, so he could believe without a shred of doubt that it was a fake. Of course, Ferris was confident he could see through a disguise of Crusch — but with these two here, he couldn't be sure.

He shuddered at the possibility. But—

"Don't go enjoyin' yourself sayin' complicated stuff like that, you wicked fox. If there's no response, this is simple. She tucked tail and ran. Got bored, went home. That's the type she is."

"To say something so blunt and unsubstantiated..."

"Right before I got smashed, that monster had a completely deflated look on her face. That's my reason."

Al was the one who'd faced Capella right up to the very end.

If it had been anyone else, it would've been words to scoff at — but with a Sin Archbishop as the opponent, common sense couldn't be relied upon to measure anything. Of course, Ferris still thought their guard shouldn't be lowered.

"More importantly, if the communication mirror's workin' again, that means we can reach the princess, right? That's priority number one. Connect me."

The two groups who'd left the city hall carrying communication mirrors were the Wilhelm-Garfiel pair and the Priscilla-Liliana pair. Al, originally assigned to Priscilla's group, had reluctantly joined this group staying behind on his mistress's own orders — but his desire to confirm his mistress's safety was something Ferris understood all too painfully well.

"I know. No need to rush so much... Hm?"

Pressed by Al, Echidna, mirror still in hand, furrowed her brow.

At that reaction, Ferris turned to look, wondering what was wrong — and in Echidna's hand, the communication mirror began to glow faintly. A call from another communication mirror.
