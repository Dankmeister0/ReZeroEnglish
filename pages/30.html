Arc 2 Chapter 6: "The Master's Return"

"Ahaa, so you've woken up. Good, good."

The tall figure with long, deep navy hair let that slip out, gazing at Subaru with evident delight.

He was a tall person. His height surpassed Reinhard's, reaching somewhere near a hundred and eighty-five centimeters. His build was slender, seemingly unacquainted with manual labor — the impression was less "lithe" than plainly gaunt.

His eyes were odd-colored, left yellow and right blue, and combined with skin as pale as an invalid's, they lent him a fragile sort of harmony.

By ordinary standards, he was handsome enough to be called good-looking without qualification.

Though it had to be said — even the most handsome face, shown from a distance close enough to feel breath on your skin, was bound to become tiresome.

"Face too close!!"

"Sorry, sorry. See, when they first carried you in, you were covered in blood, drained of all color, like you'd already died. So seeing you up and walking around so lively now — it stirs something in me, you know?"

Slapping both of Subaru's shoulders in a friendly manner, the tall man — still looming over him at point-blank range — was the owner of the mansion, who had introduced himself as Roswaal.

Subaru guessed him to be somewhere in his mid-twenties, and from his whole bearing, the phrase "profligate noble" drifted unbidden across his mind.

"No, no, wouldn't it be just my luck that a guy who looks like this turns out to be surprisingly sharp underneath—"

"Ahaa, what a delightful assessment. Go ahead, look me over more closely if you like. Do I strike you as sharp? Well? How about it?"

He struck a pose in front of Subaru, spun round and round, and settled into a model's stance. Faced with that unabashed display, Subaru was stunned to find his own feet had carried him several steps backward.

"C-could it be... that I'm actually the one forced to retreat!? Having a character even more overwhelming than me — that's not normal! It'll interfere with daily life!"

"From where I'm standing, you're both as bad as each other, honestly."

The sigh came from behind him, and in place of the retreating Subaru, Emilia stepped forward. She faced Roswaal and gave a bow of the head.

"Welcome back. Were you unharmed?"

"Fine, fine. Ahaa, how delightful. To think you'd speak to me first — it's been about four days, three hours, and nineteen minutes since the last time. I simply must write this in my diary."

At the wriggling of the tall man's fingers, a pen and notebook were held out to him from either side. Bowing respectfully were the peach- and blue-haired twin maids. Using the paper and pen handed to him, Roswaal began writing with furious energy.

"The month of Tammuz, the fifteenth day. — Emilia-sama spoke to me of her own accord today. Roswaal is so happy. At this rate we'll surely grow closer, oh yeah. ...There."

Closing the book with a satisfied expression, Roswaal handed it back to the maid and turned around.

Waiting to greet him were Emilia, her smile faintly strained, and—

"No, seriously... that's kind of horrifying."

From the glimpse Subaru caught sideways, the notebook Roswaal held — more like a pocket diary, really — was packed dense with writing from cover to cover. And it looked to be already well into its second half; if it was filled with more of the same sort of content the whole way through, that was nothing short of terrifying.

At Subaru's answer, stripped bare of any tact, Roswaal slapped his knee.

"Horrifying! What a wonderful word! First time I've heard it, but I like it! Hnfu — the pleasure of having one's unique sensibilities go unappreciated by others... ah, splendid."

"Ugh, the fact that I can sort of relate to that is the worst part. Hey, and I'm supposed to get along with this guy?"

Before the eccentric, hugging his own shoulders and squirming with delight, Subaru turned to Emilia with a complaint of "Are you seriously putting me in the same category as this thing?"

She pressed a finger to her lips, looking faintly troubled, and murmured, "Mmm..."

"Even you, Subaru, don't quite measure up to a nationally certified pervert, I'd say... probably?"

"That's way too close for comfort! You're telling me it's that razor-thin a margin!? Between me and that!?"

They say to look at others and correct yourself accordingly, but being treated as on the same level as the writhing individual before him was, frankly, deflating.

Resolving to pay the utmost care to his words and conduct from here on, Subaru said,

"Anyway, come back here, come back. And then let me thank you properly. Deepest apologies for the trouble I've caused you this time, and thank you very much for lending me a bed. Sincerely."

"That's just a mess now..."

Subaru's attitude was far too high-handed to pass for gratitude, but Roswaal seemed unbothered, whistling in response. It was Emilia, caught between the two of them, who put her head in her hands. The twins, meanwhile, showed no interest in butting in and stood quietly by, while Puck, for his part, remained buried in the silver hair, whereabouts unknown.

"We-e-ell, no-o-ow the-e-en..."

Circling back around with an unnecessarily unsettling gait, Roswaal looked Subaru over from head to toe with great interest. Subaru scowled at the discomfort of it, and Roswaal said,

"Y'know, you seem rather ordinary. That much, at least, is a bit of a disappointment."

"Hey now, are you calling me ordinary? Ordinary — a word never applied to me in either a good sense or a bad one... I find that evaluation an insult! I demand a retraction!"

"Was that really something worth getting so angry over...?"

To a question, he answered with resistance.

For Subaru, this was a line he wouldn't concede. Faced with such a spirited stance, Roswaal waved a hand and said "Ahaa, sorry, sorry,"

"By ordinary, I meant in the racial sense, is all. See, I'm rather well known as a pervert noble with a 'taste for demi-humans.'"

"The way you can declare that about yourself so plainly is pretty demonic, you know that? Ugh, I hate that I'm starting to like you a little!"

Roswaal watched Subaru clutch his head with evident amusement, then reached out and drew the twin maids standing on either side of him close, an arm around each.

"These two are part of it, and my supporting Emilia-sama is for the same reason. Though — on that front, I sense you might be a kindred spirit yourself, hm?"

Stroking the chins of the twins he'd pulled close, Roswaal began giving off a strangely villainous air. The twins, submitting without resistance, flushed red in the cheeks — already seemingly under his spell — and, struck by an ominous feeling, Subaru couldn't help but glance at Emilia.

Perhaps sensing the intent behind his look, she waved her hands hastily.

"Don't get the wrong idea. I have no interest in perverts whatsoever."

"Normal's the best, huh. Once I graduate high school I'm gonna go to college, become a regular salaryman like it's the most natural thing in the world, and work for my family."

Reversing course, Subaru let his mind wander to a thoroughly conventional, on-the-rails life. Still, he reminded himself — every dad walking the world's ordinary path has it rough in his own way.

Then, something in Roswaal's earlier remark caught his attention, and he picked it up.

"Anyway, what do you mean, 'supporting' Emilia-tan? To begin with, I have no idea what the relationship between you two even is."

"Ohh? You don't know the circumstances? Hnfu, how curious."

Releasing the twins, Roswaal folded his hands behind his back and glided smoothly forward. Once again the tall man loomed over him at close range, but this time Subaru didn't retreat — he met him head-on.

Their gazes clashed, sparks flying — or, well, not quite that dramatic, but Roswaal peered at him with a probing, searching look, plainly trying to divine his intentions, and it was an unpleasant sensation.

Still, looking away would feel like admitting defeat, and that rankled.

Against that penetrating gaze, Subaru steeled himself and met it head-on, unyielding.

Staring, staring, the distance between them gradually, gradually closing. Won't lose, can't lose. With that resolve he poured strength into his eyes, glaring, glaring—

"Mwah."

"HGYAAAAAAAAAAAH!!!"

The tall man, unable to stop himself, planted a peck on Subaru's forehead, and an uppercut flew before he could think better of it.

The blow, delivered with zero mercy, sent the tall man reeling backward; the twins scrambled to catch his falling body. Subaru, not even sparing a glance to see the outcome, scrubbed at his own forehead as if trying to rub it raw.

"Wha— you— that— what the hell was THAT for?!"

"Ahaa, ow, ow. — Ahh, well, you were looking at me with such pure, trembling-maiden eyes, I couldn't help but get a little worked up."

"My asshole just clenched right up! Stop it, that look! I mean it!"

His forehead burned as if scalded; Subaru changed the hand rubbing it into a fist and squared up in front of Roswaal, only to be blocked by the twins, hostility plain in their eyes.

It seemed that even they weren't prepared to laugh off their employer being struck. Openly marked as an enemy, Subaru's advance faltered despite himself, but—

"Now, now, Ram, Rem. This time the fault was my own poor joke. He's not to blame — if anything, being too much of a maiden about it is the closest thing to a fault he has."

"Calling my look 'maiden-like' means your eyesight's going, noble-sama. I was cute as a flower right up through kindergarten. Ever since then it's been a straight line to a buzzed head."

To a son who hated being mistaken for a girl, his parents' answer had been simplicity itself: a shaved head. Even now he couldn't settle unless the back of his head had that prickly, close-cropped feel to it, but back then it had made him cry plenty.

—Man, my personality really got shaped hard by my parents.

Mostly by a mother who never listened to anyone, and a father who pushed his own convictions too far.

At their master's intervention, the twins withdrew, though they still looked displeased. Then Roswaal, rubbing his struck jaw, got to his feet.

"That one was my fault, I apologize. And, as a way of putting that apology into form — would you care to join me for breakfast? My treat."

"Just so you know, I may not work, but I sure do eat. If it's a free meal, count me in double."

"A fine sentiment. Eating well is tied to living well, you know. Hnfu, not a bad line, that. I must write it down."

Roswaal held out his hand again, and the twins handed over the notebook and pen.

Watching him begin scribbling away deftly, it seemed that notebook's contents weren't some bizarre "love diary about Emilia-tan" sort of thing after all.

"So if it's just him jotting down his own day-to-day stuff, I guess it's basically a collection of poems or something. Bet he'll cringe himself to death rereading it later, but if he's still keeping it up at his age, there's probably no hope of a cure."

A resident of a flower garden surely doesn't think it sad to be living in one.

Subaru talked himself into accepting that thought, and then turned properly to answer the earlier invitation to breakfast.

"Anyway, I'll gladly take you up on breakfast. Forget breakfast — I don't think I've eaten anything since sometime before noon yesterday."

"Well, that's excellent. Then I'll have the two of them show you the way. Ram, Rem, if you would."

"'Leave it to us, Roswaal-sama,'" the twins replied in stereo, flanking Subaru on either side. Each took hold of one of his arms, pinning him firmly in place.

"Hey, hey, what is this, some kind of prisoner transport vibe? You don't need to do this, I'm not gonna run, and the dining table's not gonna run either... wait, ow, hey, that hurts, a little."

"This way, if you please, honored guest," said one. "C'mon, we'll show you the way, guest," said the other.

"Hey, listen, and my elbow's about to— so you two haven't forgiven me for that uppercut at all, have you!? But c'mon, however you slice it, that one was his fault, not mine, ta-ta-ta-ta-ta! Elbow! My elbow! Don't both push in opposite directions at once, hey!"

Amid a great deal of shrieking, Subaru was hauled off to the dining table, joints locked tight.

Emilia watched him go with a look of pity, and once the three of them had vanished from sight—

"—So, how did it actually go, Emilia-sama?"

The way he spoke was the same, yet the atmosphere behind the question had subtly shifted.

Emilia accepted the change without surprise.

"Same as what you figured. Puck's opinion matches too. Right?"

At her voice seeking agreement, the cat, poking just its face out from within her silver hair, nodded. Satisfied with the answer, Roswaal narrowed his eyes, and Emilia, with a faint twinge of guilt, quietly pressed a hand to her own chest.

"Having to suspect even the man who saved me... it really is a terrible thing to do," she murmured, self-mockingly, letting the words slip out.
