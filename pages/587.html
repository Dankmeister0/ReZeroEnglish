Arc 7 Chapter 82: "The Rebel Faction's Intentions"

"—In the end, if you hold me hostage, you can seal off the Dracroy domain's movements. You really did take a fine wife, didn't you, Roswaal."

"...It's grown tiresome counting how many times I've had to correct you already, but Ram is my retainer, you know-ow."

Given the situation they were in, Serena's remark showed an uncannily steady nerve, and Roswaal answered bitterly, feeling like burying his head in his hands.

Two days ago, they had visited Serena's domain, the Dracroy territory, to beg for her help in their search through the Vollachia Empire — to secure the whereabouts of the missing Subaru and Rem.

Serena held the rank of Upper Count and was well-connected throughout the Empire. If they gave her the condition of "a boy with black hair and black eyes who inevitably stood out," there was hope she could obtain reliable information.

No matter where he'd been flung to, it was hard to imagine Subaru staying quiet, so in that sense too, expectations had been high.

So what had happened was, in a sense, inevitable — and at the same time, unexpected.

"Ram, I'll ask just to be sure — the reason for this outrage is...?"

"—Natsumi Schwartz."

"Tha-a-t figures."

The sound Ram's lips had woven was a name Roswaal himself couldn't ignore.

It was a distress signal, sent by Natsuki Subaru — flung into the Empire and left isolated with no help — aimed at whoever would understand the meaning behind that name. Roswaal and Ram had grasped his intent immediately.

Signaling that he was here — Subaru's judgment in doing so was, unusually, nothing but praiseworthy. Yes, there was nothing at all wrong with Subaru's actions themselves.

If there was a problem, it lay in which "side" had sent up that signal flare of a distress call.

Because of that, Ram had resorted to a method that could only be called a surprise assault, or an outrage.

The result was the scene now before them: Serena, sat down on the sofa with Ram's staff leveled at her. And yet, one couldn't simply blame this entirely on Ram running wild.

Because—

"—So the 'black-haired maiden' siding with the rebels, Natsumi Schwartz, is the one you're searching for?"

"Strictly speaking, that one is a bonus, as far as Ram is concerned."

"But not so for Roswaal. And if it isn't so for Roswaal, then it must matter to you more than most anything else."

At Serena's words — even with her movements restrained by the staff pointed at her — Ram fell silent.

Even with Ram holding her in check, Serena's mind hadn't slowed a bit. Keeping her composure even in this situation, she had a rough grasp of their circumstances.

—"Natsumi Schwartz" was something like a false name that Natsuki Subaru went by.

To be precise, the name carried a more complicated meaning, but inferring the circumstances behind why Subaru had deliberately taken it up in the Empire, the intent he'd packed into the false name was clear enough.

Roswaal, too, wanted to respect that intent if at all possible, but—

"To think it would be the rebel army's side..."

Even if he could grasp the intent behind the false name, the direction from which the signal flare had risen was unexpected.

The rebellion breaking out while they were in the middle of illegally entering the Empire was already a headache in itself. To say it hadn't just sprouted but had bloomed into a poisonous flower wouldn't be an exaggeration.

For better or worse, things never going according to their own designs was so very like Subaru.

"Ram, you wouldn't happen to be catching some of Subaru-kun's bad influence, would you-u?"

"That's uncalled for. Not so much as a breath's worth of Barusu's influence goes into Ram's actions."

"And yet this whole situation came about because of his movements in the first place?"

"Roswaal-sama, please speak with Serena-sama."

The moment things turned inconvenient for her, she coolly changed the subject and shoved the reins of the conversation right back onto him.

Closing one eye at Ram's bold conversational sleight of hand, Roswaal turned back to face Serena once more.

As things stood, Roswaal and Ram had been summoned to her office, and had just heard the topic of the "black-haired maiden" from her own mouth, of all people.

They had given her Subaru and Rem's descriptions as the people they were searching for, so from her perspective, it was probably no more than a piece of conversation fodder, focused only on the "black hair" part. But for Roswaal and the others, it happened to carry a meaning they couldn't afford to let pass.

It was right after Serena had declared, "Regarding the trouble that occurred in the walled city, I too, as an Upper Count, must make some manner of statement of intent" — that Ram had moved to subdue her.

It was Serena's bold nature working against her, too, that she hadn't posted a guard in her office.

Her private soldiers stood waiting in the hallway outside the room, unaware of the tension within. Of course, the instant Serena raised her voice, they would come pouring into the office.

"In that case, Ram will tear Serena-sama to pieces."

"Oh, how frightening. Knowing it's no mere threat but genuine intent — that's exactly my type. Roswaal, you—"

"Not a spouse — a retainer... Let's be done with that topic already. More importantly, what I'd like is for the three of us to make the effort to return to an unchanged, peaceable relationship, wouldn't you say-y."

"'The three of us' — what an admirably earnest way to put it. Seeing you, who used to treat everything as someone else's problem, trying to take this one on as your own — it strikes me as refreshing."

At Serena's stifled laugh, Roswaal sighed, one eye still closed.

Indeed, just as she said, he couldn't treat this as someone else's affair simply because it was Ram who'd caused it. That wasn't a matter of the master-servant relationship or where responsibility lay — it was a matter of feeling, too.

Besides, Ram's actions had certainly been hasty, but at the same time, one could say it was an outrage born of valuing speed over polish.

After all, if Ram hadn't restrained Serena—

"—your prized 'Flying Dragon Squadron' would have burned the rebel elements to ash in the blink of an eye."

"—"

At Roswaal's words, Serena Dracroy narrowed her eyes slightly.

The "Flying Dragon Squadron" possessed by Count Dracroy — it was the Empire's foremost strike force, commanding the many flying dragons that inhabited Vollachia and soaring through the skies at will.

Flying Dragon Riders, who possessed the technique of "Dragon Handling" to command flying dragons that otherwise wouldn't take to humans, displayed overwhelming combat power on the battlefield to begin with. Roswaal, who could fly himself and had the ability to shut most opponents down completely, understood this well — that command of the air was the ultimate move for controlling the tide of battle.

Hence, in the Vollachia Empire, the houses with excellent Flying Dragon Riders tended to rack up the greatest achievements in war.

Count Dracroy's house had excelled in that direction since before the previous generation, and by Serena Dracroy's time, it was said to possess the strongest "Flying Dragon Squadron" of all.

If she moved the "Flying Dragon Squadron" in earnest, most any rebellion would be nothing to speak of.

"Unexpectedly, the ones you're searching for turned out to be with the rebel army, so it's a problem for you if I move my troops. But you can't keep glaring me down forever, can you? So how do you intend to bring me to heel?"

"Serena-sama, what of your family?"

"If you're looking for kin, that's pointless. I haven't had good fortune in marriage since Roswaal turned me down."

"Roswaal-sama?"

"Don't take her joke seriously. Besides, a marriage between great nobles of the Kingdom and the Empire — given the relations between the two countries, that's simply not possible, is it-t. Serena, this isn't the time for tomfoolery."

Setting aside the relationship between Roswaal and Serena, Ram right now could hardly be called patient.

It wasn't easy to tell at a glance, but Ram deeply regretted what had happened at the Pleiades Watchtower. She blamed herself for losing sight of Subaru and Rem and letting them be separated.

There was no doubt that behind this extreme action of hers lay that same impatience.

"Don't needlessly provoke Ram — let's aim for a good landing point for both of us, shall we."

"A good landing point for both of us, is it. Then, concretely, what is it you want?"

"—"

"There's no fool who flies a dragon without settling on a destination. They're poor on fuel, and riders are precious besides. If you're going to talk about 'needless,' that's exactly the word for it. You understand my troubles too, don't you? Hm?"

Resting her chin on one hand, Serena looked over at Roswaal as if testing him.

Given that Rem was highly likely to be with the rebel army Subaru was thought to have joined, it was no wonder Ram had made such a snap decision. And blaming her for it was pointless anyway, since time couldn't be turned back.

If they were so inclined, there was a way to harm Serena and, taking advantage of the ensuing chaos, escape the territory.

But that would mean abandoning their original goal and doing nothing but making needless enemies — sheer folly. Even in an Empire where might made right, the soldiers weren't so foolish as to be pleased about their lord being struck down by treachery.

Weakening Count Dracroy's power by taking out the house that held the "Flying Dragon Squadron" might be welcomed from the rebel army's standpoint, but whether that act was worth the price of an old friend's neck was doubtful even from Roswaal's perspective.

Of course, if it were a sacrifice necessary for his cherished goal, Roswaal wouldn't hesitate even at the cost of Serena's life.

But if it was a sacrifice he didn't need to make, he had no wish to make it needlessly.

And so, the words Roswaal offered Serena here were—

"—Here's a thought: why don't you join the rebel army together with us?"

"Ha."

"That way, the friction between you and us would be resolved. If our goals become the same, there'd no longer be any reason for Ram to point her staff at you. Everything would settle neatly for both sides."

Serena's eyes went wide at Roswaal, who pressed his hands together in front of his chest as he declared this.

What passed through her eyes was surprise, exasperation, and a measure of curiosity. In the sense that he'd caught her interest, it wasn't a remark entirely wide of the mark.

Relying on that, Roswaal set about closing the distance in their relationship.

"You say it'd settle neatly, but that's only true here, within these walls. What waits at the end of your invitation is an invitation to civil war. My domain has been guaranteed by His Imperial Majesty — what gain could there be in needlessly stirring up trouble, of all things—"

"—You have no complaints against His Imperial Majesty. Is that really so?"

Serena had spoken logically, laying out the security of her own footing. But Roswaal cut her off partway through, looking straight into her eyes.

Being an Upper Count of the Empire, she might look bold and carefree, but she was also skilled at concealing her true intentions. Precisely because of that, this was a conversation that had to be had while looking her squarely in the eye.

"When you're talking to someone, you look properly at their face while you talk!"

Those words suddenly crossed Roswaal's mind.

Feeling unexpectedly pushed forward by them—

"I haven't been spending these past two days here in idle peace either, you know-w. While you were busy, I made sure to keep my ears as pricked up as I could."

"So instead of keeping me company with drinks until morning, you were pouring your energy into eavesdropping."

"With that workload of yours, I'd recommend reforming a lifestyle of drinking until morning every single day."

He supposed the conditions were naturally different on a day they had guests than on an ordinary day, but even so, wishing she'd cut back on the drink was his heartfelt plea as an old acquaintance.

In any case, setting aside his worry for Serena's liver, Roswaal now unveiled the fruits of his eavesdropping. The circumstances this Dracroy domain secretly harbored were—

"—I heard that the attempted assassination the year before last, the incident rumored to have involved one of the 'Nine Divine Generals,' has been dragging on for quite some time."

"—"

"The perpetrator of that incident, the 'Nine Divine General' who lost his life — a Flying Dragon Rider, I heard... and one who was formerly under your command, no less. His name was..."

"—Balleroy Temeglyph."

Serena cut Roswaal off, saying it in a low, quiet voice.

She kept her chin propped on her hand, but the heat held in her eyes shifted from what it had been until now. Somehow, the hint of enjoyment she'd taken even in this situation vanished, replaced by a stillness reminiscent of the surface of a windless lake.

With those quiet eyes still in place, she looked past Roswaal to Ram standing diagonally behind him.

"Lower the staff, Ram. I'll talk even without it."

"But..."

"Ram, do as she says. Serena doesn't go back on her word."

When Roswaal held up a hand to check her, Ram reluctantly lowered her staff.

Of course, she kept a posture ready to raise it again instantly, but Serena didn't fault her for the wariness. Instead, she turned her face toward the window, looking out at the scenery spread there — no, gazing up at the sky.

The sky where flying dragons beat their wings and soared about freely.

"Do you think it's free? This sky."

"...By the sound of it, apparently not-t."

"In the Empire, the water, the earth, the air, even the flesh and blood of its people — all of it belongs to His Imperial Majesty. Not even the sky is an exception. —Who talked?"

"I'd think it'd be hard to pin it on any one person-n. After all, it's what everyone's thinking."

At Roswaal's shrug, Serena answered shortly, "I see."

The name she had spoken — Balleroy Temeglyph — was one of the former "Nine Divine Generals," and the perpetrator of the attempted assassination of the Emperor that had occurred in the imperial capital the year before last.

He had originally been a talent Serena valued highly in the Dracroy domain, and by all accounts, an up-and-comer who had risen through the ranks by his own merit to become a general on the strength of his extraordinary ability and outstanding skill as a Flying Dragon Rider.

Naturally, with Balleroy having turned his blade against the Emperor, Serena's standing — as the one who had recommended him — had worsened as well.

Though she hadn't even had her rank of Upper Count stripped from her, she'd fallen out of favor in the capital, and the expectations placed on her Flying Dragon Squadron had evaporated, with no chance given to redeem herself.

And the crowning touch was the person who had taken Balleroy's place among the "Nine Divine Generals"—

"—the 'Dragon General,' Madelyn Eschart. What a spiteful choice of successor, that-t."

"A being with no military service to speak of, who rose to general on the Chancellor's recommendation — and her true nature, astonishingly, turns out to be a Dragonkin. To a Dragonkin who can handle flying dragons without the secret art of 'Dragon Handling,' Flying Dragon Riders who go to all the trouble of raising up riders must seem like nothing but a roundabout waste of time."

"That leaves no standing at all for Serena-sama, who holds the Flying Dragon Squadron."

"Whoa now, don't you go belittling my Flying Dragon Squadron. Not needing a rider has its ups and downs too. Sure, it saves the trouble of training, but a flying dragon without a rider has nothing to rely on for tactics but instinct. On that point, Flying Dragon Riders bring understanding and application of tactics."

"—"

"Though I hear that particular Dragonkin crushes even that under sheer weight of numbers."

"Dragon Handling," Vollachia's secret art — the details of the technique for commanding flying dragons, savage and unfriendly to humans by nature, weren't something even Roswaal had a grasp on. But he'd heard that training a single flying dragon required a talented Flying Dragon Rider to stay with it constantly.

In other words, a Flying Dragon Rider had to be paired one-to-one with a single flying dragon.

By contrast, the existence of the "Dragon General," who could command countless flying dragons without spending the time, was a natural enemy — a thorn in the side — to Serena, whose strength lay in her Flying Dragon Squadron.

"Balleroy was a confidant of mine for a long time. For him to defy His Majesty, and worse, to level a spear at him — it's only natural that blame would fall on me for it. But—"

"Whether you're content to swallow that humiliation is a separate matter, isn't it?"

"—. You have a way of tickling the bottom of my belly with every last word."

"You flatter me."

Ram bowed with a flat tone of voice, and at her words, Serena let out a deep breath.

Serena didn't deny the points Roswaal and Ram had raised, pressed in their own distinct tones. The moment she'd had Ram lower her staff, she had already made up her mind to a certain resolve.

It was a sign of her stance — not disclosing her circumstances because she was threatened, but talking on equal footing.

Namely—

"—Even so, I hadn't intended to stir up any trouble while you two were here, you know?"

So saying, Serena Dracroy touched the white sword-scar on her face and grinned savagely.

That wild, untamed smile was the same one young Roswaal had seen when he first met her — the ambitious smile she'd worn before she wrested headship of the house from her father.

But this time, the smile she wore wasn't directed at a father whose eyes had clouded over as his daughter stole away the loyalty of his retainers — it was directed at the fearsome being who ruled this vast Empire—

"—I see. So that's it, Serena. You—"

Seeing that smile, Roswaal belatedly realized his own misunderstanding.

He'd tried to guide Serena's thinking by clarifying the position the Dracroy domain was in, hoping to avoid the situation worsening because of Ram's snap decision — but that had been a mistake.

There had been no need to guide her toward siding with the rebellion at all.

Even the rise of the rebel army with the "black-haired maiden" mixed in was, for her, nothing more than a trigger. Serena had already decided her position, and in her own way, she'd shown consideration for Roswaal and the others.

Consideration, in her own fashion, not to drag in an old friend who'd come searching for someone, knowing nothing of it.

But Roswaal and the others had blundered right into her camp, boots and all, without noticing. Without even realizing the magic stone had already caught fire, they'd gone and poured in extra mana on top of it.

That is to say—

"I was as considerate as I could be. This one time, you can resent having taken such a perceptive wife. Or, going all the way back around, resent turning down my proposal."

"Serena."

"Set your mind at ease. If your true identity comes out, I'll be suspected of colluding with the Kingdom. Fighting a two-front war after that would be nothing short of madness. If I'm going to do this, I'll fight a battle I can win."

The initiative in the conversation had been seized from him, and the balance of power with Serena had reversed.

Now that their positions overlapped, Roswaal and the others had no reason to harm Serena. Meanwhile, Serena had the leeway to do as she pleased with Roswaal and the others, given their circumstances.

"Roswaal-sama, could it be...?"

"Ah, it seems we walked right into the beast's hunting ground, hook, line, and sinker-r. —Serena, if we hadn't spoken up..."

"In that case, I'd have entertained you for a few days as suited, and gotten you out before the civil war got fully underway. But when an old friend invites me to fight alongside them, there's nothing to be done about it, is there."

"How brazen of you..."

Whether she'd intended to answer this rebellion's call, or had meant to take some separate action of her own, wasn't clear. But Serena had already decided to resist.

Whether that was anger at the Emperor's cold-blooded treatment of her, or connected to the fact that her confidant had lost his life, was something he couldn't tell — which of the two it was.

If there was one thing certain—

"There are exceedingly many who've built up grievances against Emperor Vincent Vollachia's peaceful reign. Once the flames rise up in earnest, they'll spread all at once."

"...Those flames — would you happen to have some idea where they'll come from-m?"

"Who knows. Shouldn't it be you who has some idea?"

Serena, with her stifled laugh, looked as though she had some trick up her sleeve Roswaal knew nothing about.

For his part, Roswaal, too, had someone in mind — a singular point who, left unchecked, surely wouldn't let things end quietly, though how much power he could wield in this unfamiliar land remained unclear.

If it were him, no matter what dire straits he fell into, he'd find some way through. The only question was who he'd meet within that struggle, how much he'd wish to save them, and how far he'd let that influence ripple outward.

Or perhaps—

"—might you set the entire Empire ablaze too, I wonder, Subaru-kun."

If, hypothetically, that were to happen, how should Roswaal act?

Of course, for the sake of the cherished goal he had to fulfill, he needed to return to the Kingdom with the full roster he couldn't afford to be missing a piece of. Even setting that aside, there was a promise he'd made with Subaru.

It was something he'd one-sidedly forced on him, but Subaru wasn't the sort to break it. A future where someone was missing — that was something unacceptable to a man who'd resolved to pick up absolutely everything.

Even leaving aside his promise with Roswaal, he'd struggle for exactly as far as his outstretched arm could reach.

So at the very least, all Roswaal could do was act so as to reduce the number of times that struggle had to happen.

It was simply a matter of not letting Subaru realize things had taken a shape he hadn't wanted.

"Well now, thanks to Ram putting my life at risk, the ill feeling between us has ended up clearing away. Shall we forget official duties for today and crack open some celebratory drinks?"

"It's a strange thing for the one who pointed a staff at you to say, but — are you sure that's alright?"

"The two of us now need to cultivate a stronger sense of unity, heading toward the same goal. So we drink, and deepen our bond. Considering who we're up against, it's an entirely natural thing to do, wouldn't you say."

With a face that had utterly forgotten the outrage from moments before, Serena calmly invited Ram to drink.

Having laid herself bare without reservation, with no more need to hide anything from Roswaal and the others, Serena wore a refreshed look — and Ram, unusually, seemed at a loss before such largeness of character.

That said, he didn't think Serena would resort to something so shallow as sweet-talking Roswaal and the others into letting their guard down, only to slit their throats in their sleep.

If she did such a thing, in exchange for Roswaal's freedom within the Empire, Serena would be putting at risk both the military strength she needed to achieve her goal, and even her own life.

"So then, will you wait for the flames to rise, or will you become the flame yourself?"

"I know my own position well enough. No need to rush — it won't be long. —Or is the one you have in mind the sort to sit quietly?"

"...No, that he is not."

"Thought so. Same here."

They were each no doubt picturing a different "flame," and yet the impression they each held of the one they had in mind seemed to be much alike.

Keeping that fact in mind, Roswaal crossed his arms thoughtfully. Beside him, Serena, entirely unruffled, turned to her own display cabinet and began choosing a bottle to open.

"Roswaal-sama, to the walled city—"

"Positionally, Emilia-sama and the others, attacking from the east, are closer. The same rumor is bound to reach their ears too, so if joining up is possible, that's surely what will happen. Of course, I imagine it's frustrating for you."

"...No."

Shaking her head, Ram answered while hiding the frustration within.

In truth, she surely wished to be reunited with Rem as soon as possible. If a rumor of Subaru had arisen in the walled city, then Rem being there was the most likely possibility.

On the other hand, even making full use of Roswaal's flight magic, the blow of coming up empty was something to fear.

Searching for people across the vast Empire demanded a certain rationality — one born of suppressing the heart.

"That's exactly why we should wait. For what she calls the rising of the flames."

"—Yes."

Biting back some measure of hesitation and reluctance, Ram nodded gamely.

—A few days later, what Serena Dracroy called the "flames" rose up.

The momentum of the rebel army, bearing as its banner the Emperor Vincent Vollachia's own child, spread across the entire Empire all at once, propagating outward to rebel factions in every region.

Among them, it was Upper Count Dracroy who declared her joining of the rebel army especially early — and at the valiant lady lord's side, it's said, stood an unfamiliar, handsome man and a lovely retainer.
