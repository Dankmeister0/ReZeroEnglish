Arc 6 Chapter 72: "■■・■"

—At the abrupt visitation of "death," Natsuki Subaru hears the sound of his ■ cracking.

And yet, each time that inescapable sound echoes, he thinks this: This is surely proof that his steps haven't stopped. This is the footfall of drawing closer to the future. However small the stride, however slight the distance covered, still—he is walking forward.

"—Subaru."

"————"

The instant his consciousness was led toward waking, a pair of blue eyes with their distinctive pattern filled his vision up close. Peering down at him anxiously was Beatrice. Beatrice's hand rested gently against Subaru's cheek, and he caught his breath.

Just moments before, he had parted from a tearful Beatrice. His mind couldn't quite make the switch. Just now, he hadn't been able to exchange anything so proper as a farewell—but she had unmistakably been there, right in front of him.

"...Ah."

"Is your mind properly with you, I wonder? It took you a while to wake up just now, so I was worried, I suppose. Perhaps I ought to check you haven't carelessly dropped any memories. As a start, do you remember Betty...?"

"Bea...trice..."

"—It seems you remember properly after all, I suppose. Good boy, good boy, I suppose."

Saying so, Beatrice moved the hand from his cheek up to his head. As her small palm stroked his hair, his consciousness slowly caught up to reality.

"Count, one, huh..."

"—?"

At his muttered words, Beatrice tilted her head. But its meaning was lost on her, and he had no intention of explaining it. Even if he explained, she likely wouldn't understand—and he had no intention of making her understand.

She was kind. Beatrice was very kind. So if she ever learned just what Subaru was attempting, she would absolutely object—of that there was no doubt. And that was true not just of Beatrice.

"So, how did it go? Did you accomplish what you meant to by reading the book, I wonder?"

"—Ah, about that..."

Beatrice asked this of Subaru, who still had the book resting on his lap. The moment before the moment before—setting aside Subaru's own felt sense of time—he had just been challenging Reid Astrea's Book of the Dead, seeking a way to overcome him. That attempt had stalled for complicated reasons, and any number of events had been packed in between—enough that it now felt like a distant memory—but that was a matter for Subaru alone.

What he had to do, what needed doing, was already decided. So—

"Beatrice, things are about to get a little hectic. —Lend me your strength."

"—Well, of course, I suppose. Betty is Subaru's partner, isn't she."

With no explanation at all, Beatrice promised her cooperation to Subaru who'd simply blurted that out. Her presence was, truly and utterly, ■-strong.

"Hah! Hahaha! You're good—good, aren't you, not bad, maybe good, doing great, did great, and it's exactly because you're doing great that— Binge drinking! Gluttony!"

Using the corridor's walls and ceiling alike as footholds, the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony, Lye Batenkaitos, employed one endlessly shifting tactic after another. Chasing him down at high speed, setting aside her body's ailments to move with brisk agility, was Ram. Her slender body danced through the air, and the sound of her blows mercilessly sent Gluttony's body flying. And into that opening, as though she'd been waiting for exactly this moment, came—

"—Whoosh, and then, bam!"

Emilia, swinging a massive ice hammer in both hands like a home-run batter. The hammer's striking head, as large around as a person's torso, landed a full-bodied blow square on Lye's back with tremendous force. Amid a thunderous roar, his diminutive frame bounced away like a rubber ball.

—No, he'd bounced too well for that. He'd instantly kicked off the ice hammer and leapt forward on his own to kill the momentum.

"Ahahaha! You're good, good, good! But but, that's not gonna cut it! I can't go and lose to something like that!"

"—Ngh, he's so slippery!"

At Gluttony's acrobatic display of bodily skill, Emilia's pretty face twisted in frustration. Having consumed masters of every martial art across all times and places, the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony had literally become a walking compendium of every fighting art there was—Lye's combat strength, or perhaps it should be called adaptability. It was on a wholly different level, the precision with which he produced the optimal, peak technique for any given instant unmatched.

"If 'memories' encompass all the effort, all the training someone's ever built up..."

Infuriatingly, Gluttony probably didn't even need the conscious intent to "draw it out." His Authority was one that produced, in any and every situation, the sensation of your body having already moved before you noticed.

—The "memories" stolen encompassed even the body's accumulated experience.

That much was obvious from the fact that Subaru couldn't recall how to use the whip he carried, not in the slightest. It seemed the whip had been his main weapon before he lost his memories, but he couldn't remember anything about it—not the proper grip, not how to swing it, nothing at all. For that matter, why had he chosen a whip of all things as his main weapon in the first place? No doubt it had been some shallow idea like "being nimbly resourceful will serve better than wielding a sword or spear." He had no objection to the idea itself, but—

"I really wish you'd thought to prepare for the case where you lost your memories...!"

Of course, he was well aware that was an unreasonable demand, but Subaru cursed anyway on purpose. Doing so let him forget, if only for an instant, his own unmanly jealousy toward the vanished "memories." Jealousy toward the vanished "memories"—toward "Natsuki Subaru."

"Subaru, I'll ask you again, I suppose. Are you truly all right?"

Beatrice, beside him, called out to Subaru's profile. Supporting the body of Subaru, who was down on one knee, she asked him this, and Subaru nodded, "Yeah,"

"But it's necessary. You understand, don't you, Beatrice..."

"That's—"

"Lend me your strength. I'm asking you again... please."

Taking the utmost care not to let his voice shake from the ceaseless chills wracking him, Subaru cornered Beatrice with a cowardly appeal. At Subaru's cruel request, Beatrice's face clouded and she lowered her eyes.

"Lend me your strength"—it was exactly what he'd said back in the Taygeta archive. But back then, Subaru had not yet activated Cor Leonis, and Beatrice hadn't known just how badly the situation at the Pleiades Watchtower would deteriorate. So this was a thoroughly vile tactic on Subaru's part, one that preyed on Beatrice's kindness.

With a troubled expression, Beatrice weighed Subaru's proposal. The connection between Subaru's sudden physical deterioration and Ram's corresponding recovery of combat strength was plain to see—Ram, Beatrice, even Emilia had all noticed Subaru's stubborn bravado. And yet none of them could bring themselves to tell him to stop, because they all understood.

Hypothetically, if Subaru, out of self-preservation, released Cor Leonis here, Ram would collapse first, Emilia would have her "name" devoured by Gluttony, their coordination would fall apart, and they would lose. And once Gluttony was free, the power balance within the tower would spiral into an irrecoverable state, and in the end everyone would die in defeat—game over.

"...In the end, me shouldering it is simply the best option available."

Subaru, who couldn't even use his largely useless whip, could take on Ram's burden and thereby raise their overall fighting strength dramatically. That was a non-negotiable requirement for conquering the tower. That said, there was a trap in Cor Leonis's effect that couldn't simply be resolved. Namely, Ram herself—whether consciously or not—was holding her combat strength in check.

"————"

With overwhelming instinct and body technique that defied common sense, Ram had complete command of the field. And yet she couldn't push it all the way through, because Ram was keeping one final limiter on her own combat power. —Behind that lay a hesitation born of concern for Subaru's condition.

If Ram moved in earnest, that much greater a burden would fall on Subaru in turn. And Subaru couldn't withstand it. That possibility was what stayed Ram's hand, stayed her feet.

In fact, something close to that had happened in the loop where he'd headed for Julius and Reid. He'd already confirmed that taking on Ram's ailments through Cor Leonis was possible even without joining up with her—but under conditions where Ram didn't know that her own condition was linked to Subaru's, she wouldn't hold her combat strength in check. The result was an unstinting backfire that scorched Subaru, threatening to bring them both down together.

That would mean the end of Ram's window of invincibility too—the worst possible kind of mutual collapse. You simply can't keep sprinting at full tilt for long while starved of oxygen. That was all it came down to.

So, in raising Ram's combat power through Cor Leonis, her own judgment to hold back was, as a result, the correct one. Making it last thin and long was the best move. But even against that improvement in Ram's fighting power, Gluttony could still just barely keep pace, drawing on his plundered, boundless martial training. Emilia made up for the parts they couldn't fully shake him off in, but even so, two against one, Gluttony clung on, refusing to let go with an abnormally high survivability. And so, into that—

"—Miinya!!"

Purple crystals, deployed thick enough to fill the corridor, were unleashed all at once. Answering Beatrice's chant as she raised her small arm and brought it down, a concentrated barrage of the magic known as Shadow Magic assailed Lye, who had his back turned this way, with tremendous force. An instant later, a riotous dance of purple light swallowed his diminutive frame, and the sound of shattering glass filled the corridor.

"————"

Amid the dancing, scattering purple shards, it was Emilia and Ram who leapt clear of the attack's range. Escaping the line of fire on instant judgment, the two of them evaded with agile grace befitting their prowess. Lye, on the other hand, under the same conditions but with his reaction delayed by having his back turned, couldn't escape as easily as they had.

It was a terrifying magic that crystallized whatever it struck, turning it into something fragile and fleeting before shattering it. Even a graze from Miinya could prove fatal, and with it unleashed this indiscriminately within so confined a space, it would be difficult for even a transcendent foe to come through unscathed. When the riotous dance of light stirred up by the purple arrows finally stilled, the figure of Gluttony, surely having taken tremendous damage, was—not there.

"—?!" "Eh?! Where did he—"

Beatrice caught her breath in shock, and Emilia's face stiffened as she cast her eyes around. Amid the confusion, it was Ram, as ever, who grasped the situation fastest.

She placed a hand over her right eye and turned around with only her left eye open. Subaru, connected to Ram by something like a path through Cor Leonis, could tell. Ram was activating some manner of spell formula. Using that special power, she pinpointed exactly where the vanished Gluttony had gone. His location was—

"Barusu!"

"————"

Ram opened her pale pink eyes and shouted. In that instant, Subaru understood from the direction of her gaze that a dangerous presence had appeared right behind him. It was aiming at Subaru—no, at Beatrice's back. Was this in retaliation for Miinya's interference just now, as though it had sullied the fight somehow? Whatever the reason, what was about to happen wouldn't change much either way.

"—Yeah, I gotta say, if you keep that up back to back, it's rough on me."

Along with those mocking words, the dagger strapped to his arm flashed. It was Lye's strike, having escaped the purple arrows' concentrated barrage by leaping through space. It was the peculiar ability of a figure known as the "Leaper," used frequently alongside that of the "Fist King"—short-range warping, so to speak. From what he could tell, there seemed to be some limit to the distance, but even so, the combination of a space-leaping ability and a surrounding attack was the worst possible matchup. "Gah—"

He regretted it, but there hadn't been any other suitable shield. Just imagining what might have happened if he'd hesitated on that snap decision and Beatrice had been cut down was chilling enough. Compared to that, becoming a shield of flesh was nothing. It hurt—it hurt, but if it was only pain, only a wound to the body, it would pass in an instant. Maybe not even an instant. His consciousness sank toward the bottom of a deep, dark pit, too deep even for such glib jokes to surface. Vivid pain and a sense of loss seared his brain.

"—Gh."

He gritted his teeth and choked back the scream that had almost torn its way out of him. Screaming wouldn't make it easier. Not screaming wouldn't make it easier either. But if Subaru screamed here, there would surely be someone whose ■ that scream would shatter. That, he could not allow. And because he couldn't, die silently, Natsuki Subaru.

"—Nice, big bro. Gonna go on to the next one just like that, huh?"

A voice looked down on Subaru as he crumpled powerlessly, still holding back his scream. He'd held back the scream. But he couldn't stand this mocking voice.

"————"

Unable to move a single limb, he forced strength into his trembling arm and moved his fingers. He raised his middle finger, and his lips moved.

"Drop dead, idiot."

And with that, his consciousness went dark—

Julius called for vigilance against a surprise attack from the now-vanished Gluttony, but Ram, shaking her head, was calmly assessing the situation. Subaru agreed with Ram as well. Just as Lye had said on his way out, there was no reason whatsoever for him to keep fighting under conditions this unfavorable.

Lye Batenkaitos was a Sin Archbishop, not a warrior. He had no disposition for fighting to the bitter end or savoring a hard-fought battle. Going by Rui's words, what people like him wanted was the best possible life, the path of the optimal solution. —They would never willingly court the peril of a hard fight or defeat.

Emilia murmured this blankly, as the ice hammer in her hands dissolved back into the air. There was no relief in her words at the enemy's departure—rather, they carried a disappointment, not simply at having let the enemy slip away, but something else entirely. The answer to that disappointment was—

"—We were so close!"

"————"

"One more step, just a little more, and we'd have had the one who did that to Rem...!"

It was Ram, down on one knee, who raised her voice, thin and high. She struck her fist against the corridor floor, her voice trembling with rage at Gluttony, who had gotten clean away.

Everyone present understood her frustration painfully well. The reason Emilia had trailed off, the reason Julius lowered his yellow eyes, the reason Beatrice beside Subaru hunched her shoulders—it all came down to that same thing.

It had been a lead that dropped into their laps out of nowhere. But unlike relying on knowledge about the Watchtower, questioning Gluttony directly about the damage his Authority had done seemed like a direct, reliable means of getting answers. To have let it slip through their fingers right before their eyes—of course they'd be disheartened.

"...Does this actually count as having driven Lye off?"

Even while understanding the ■ feelings of Ram, lost in grief, Subaru had a different sort of doubt. Seeing three-against-one as unfavorable, Lye had tried to withdraw from this place. But whether that meant he'd withdrawn entirely as a threat to the Watchtower was another matter—hardly certain.

Ram had told Julius that being on guard against a surprise attack was unnecessary, but that only applied to this particular spot. The fact that a dangerous Gluttony was still wandering somewhere within the tower hadn't changed, had it? If so, it could hardly be said that one of the five obstacles had been removed. If anything, they'd simply left an enemy loose, one they had no way of knowing where they might collide with again.

"—Ngh, whether to chase the one who fled, or rush to some other problem, in any case right now..."

"—Situations are the kind of thing that move around without giving a damn what you were thinking about your opponent."

"————"

Just as he braced himself, thinking he had to move, a voice from behind made every hair on his body stand on end. When everyone spun around at once, what was approaching from down the corridor was the sound of sandal footsteps. A true master could easily walk without making a single footstep, and yet the man in question chose not to. Why?

"Why the hell would I need to worry about anyone else while I'm walking through my own garden?"

The man who declared this boldly, without a shred of shame—Reid, having descended to the fourth floor—and at the sight of him, Subaru sensed the situation had gotten even worse. They had put Reid off to deal with Lye Batenkaitos first, but in the end Lye had gotten away, and they'd utterly failed to stop Reid's resurrection—

"Since when has this tower become your garden?"

"Oi oi, don't get the wrong idea, hot stuff. Let me tell you, when I say garden, I don't mean some shabby little tower like this. When I say my garden, obviously I mean the whole damn world."

"...Coming from a man who became legend with a single sword, that's not a claim one can dismiss as a joke."

At the appearance of Reid, who shouldn't have been able to leave the second floor, Emilia and Julius each spoke up despite their shock. Reid's answer to them was blunt in a way that could only be described as quintessentially him. To this overwhelmingly powerful being beyond human comprehension, the very notion of caring about his footsteps—no, not just footsteps. The very concept of considering others was utterly foreign to him.

"—Why are you down here? You shouldn't be able to leave that floor, Examiner."

"I got no taste for talking to some woman who's down on her knees sulking. If you want an answer, try begging for it. I don't dislike a strong-willed woman. Don't dislike a weak-willed one either, mind you."

"I see. —You're scum."

Deciding early on that a proper exchange couldn't be expected, Ram cut off the back-and-forth with Reid. In the next instant, Ram kicked off the floor hard enough to seem to burst it, charging Reid in a straight line, a whirlwind of pink hurtling toward the standing Sword Saint.

Making full use of her small frame, she unleashed a high-speed spinning kick, heel aimed straight at Reid's neck—without exaggeration, a kick with force enough to sever his head. A blow so fierce that a direct hit would physically take his head off, and even a glancing one would blow his consciousness away. Naturally, a corresponding backlash flowed through Ram and into Subaru, pain racing through his whole body like lightning.

"Ghh, guuh..."

He'd heard that a ruptured artery causes pain beyond anything of this world. What just tore through Subaru's entire body, he could confidently claim, was something close to that. The result of Ram's strike, delivered at such a cost, was—

"Not bad. But that's all it was. Tricia would've cracked the ground with one hit."

"...Monster."

"Don't lump me in with some monster. That'd bring my value down, wouldn't it."

He caught her kicking leg, letting the impact bleed off into the ground. That same physical-attack-nullifying trick he'd seen before—it cracked the corridor's floor and walls, yet perfectly blocked off any damage to Reid himself. And then, into the torso of Ram, whose movement had halted,

"There, try and take this."

Spinning his body around in a whirl, Reid's thick, powerful leg snapped up. The sole of his sandal caught Ram's body squarely and sent her rocketing straight up with brutal force.

"Kah—"

Air wrung from her lungs, Ram's eyes bulged as blood spewed from her mouth. Reid, not even bothering to dodge, took it full in the face, his uncovered eye narrowing, his lips twisting. But almost immediately, that narrowed eye flew wide open, and a voice slipped out: "The hell?"

"You slipped up."

In that instant, Ram grabbed the leg buried in her own torso and, using her whole body, locked the knee joint. Her attitude was as if the blow that had driven into her own body meant nothing at all. Seeing this, Reid's expression turned suspicious—

"—The hell. This too, huh, small fry."

"————"

He said this in a cold voice while watching Subaru, whose molars nearly cracked from agony like a bowling ball being screwed into his solar plexus, gasping for breath.

"—! No! Don't get any closer to Subaru!"

Right after that, catching sight of the dangerous emotion flickering through Reid's blue eyes, Emilia stepped forward. But against her advance, Reid swung his arm irritably, snatched away the ice sword driven at him, struck Emilia's shoulder mercilessly with its hilt, and kicked her down with brutal force as she fell.

"Ahh!"

"You're in the way."

Struck by the kick, Emilia was sent flying, the physical distance between them blown apart. Terrifyingly, Reid had done this with the very leg whose knee was supposedly locked. In other words, he'd kicked Emilia away while still leaving Ram clinging to his leg. And even as Ram put more force into it, Reid's knee didn't budge an inch.

"Ngh, this is getting us nowhere!"

"—Prepare yourself!"

Abandoning her advantage in the joint lock, Ram leapt back, and Julius stepped in, the two of them closing in at once. But the result of their combined attack was equally disastrous.

Kicks, knife-hand strikes—every single one shot down Ram and Julius, warriors who should have stood at the very pinnacle. Ram was struck in the neck, Julius had his leg broken, and neither of them could stand back up.

"—Shit."

And so, down a corridor littered with his fallen comrades, Reid advanced, obstacles literally cleared from his path. Ahead of him now stood only Subaru, groggy from having taken on the damage of Emilia and the others, and Beatrice, spreading her arms wide as if to shield him.

"B-Bea... it's no good, so..."

"Whether it's no good or reckless doesn't matter, I suppose. That's Subaru's own specialty, isn't it... In which case, it should be Betty's specialty too, as Subaru's partner."

Beatrice's bravado rang oddly hollow just now. Even as he tried to answer Beatrice's spirit, Subaru's hands, his legs, wouldn't respond to his fighting will. This was damage severe enough to incapacitate his companions even with Subaru shouldering the burden. Three people's worth of it—the mere fact that he was still conscious at all was the product of sheer stubbornness.

"Reid, why are you targeting Betty and the others... targeting Subaru?"

"'Because it's part of the Trial'—that's a pretty tacked-on reason, honestly. If we're talking my original objective, it was that beauty over there..."

"But, what, I wonder."

"Seeing that one there in person changed my mind. He creeps me out, so I'm gonna whittle him down."

Judgment based on some idiosyncratic sensibility of his own—against a mind like that, reasoning got you nowhere. At the same time, with any chance of him changing his mind now dead, Beatrice gritted her cute little molars and glanced back at Subaru, collapsed and unable to move.

"————"

She held her breath for just a moment because she'd resolved to fight, shielding Subaru. He wanted to tell her to run. But all that escaped Subaru's mouth was a rasping breath.

"No need to go rushing headlong into death, little runt."

"I'm afraid living like I'm already dead is something I've had quite enough of, I suppose."

"Heh, that so. —Well then, guess it can't be helped."

A hero worthy of the name wouldn't raise a hand against women and children. As if to say such a notion was pure fantasy, Reid's gaze didn't waver in the slightest. It declared, plainly, that nothing would change even if the one standing in his way were a small child. And so Beatrice, too, expected no ■ leniency, and had gone all-out from the very start.

"Ur Miinya."

Countless purple arrows rose up, filling the corridor and sealing off any avenue of escape for Reid completely. Even Reid, seeing at a glance that Miinya's very nature meant a graze alone could prove fatal, didn't flinch in the slightest, his lips twisting.

"Natsuki Subaru's great spirit, Beatrice."

"Fine by me. —'Stick-swinger,' Reid Astrea."

They declared each other's names, and the air drew taut. Beatrice had laid the space thick with purple crystals; Reid picked up Julius's knight's sword, fallen at his feet, and readied it.

"————"

Right to the very last, Subaru couldn't get out a single word. But his eyes stayed open. He didn't close them.

He didn't look away from the results of his own decisions, his own choices. He couldn't look away—

"Beatrice—!"

"—Fwah?! W-what's wrong, I wonder?! Subaru?! Subaru?!"

The instant he opened his eyes, he found himself pulling the girl in front of him into an embrace before he even realized it. Startled by the suddenness of it, the girl—Beatrice—went stiff, her small body tensing.

A reunion with the girl who, until just moments ago, had steeled herself with tragic resolve. It filled his chest with relief and unbearable guilt both, sending a painful wind gusting through his ■.

"Beatrice..."

He was glad to see her again. And at the same time, he was sorry for having put her through something like that.

"Subaru?"

"—No, it's nothing. I remember everything just fine. You're Beatrice, I'm Natsuki Subaru. You and me, best-friend partners, let's keep having fun together, all berig."

"B-berigoo, I suppose..."

At Subaru's rhyming words, Beatrice, bewildered, gave a thumbs up. It wasn't an expression this world had, so Beatrice picking it up was surely the result of Subaru's bad influence rubbing off on her. Stroking her head, Subaru drew in a long breath and let it out short. Then, telling himself he needed to grasp the situation right away, he shifted his focus—

"Calm down, would you, Subaru."

"Huh?"

"Tell me properly, in full, what happened, I suppose. What happened inside the book? Did you get to see Reid's memories? And not just about Betty—what about Emilia, everyone else? Make sure you really remember, I suppose. Make sure you recall every single precious thing, properly."

"T-that's... ah, yeah, you're right."

Cupping Subaru's cheeks in both hands, Beatrice pleaded with him face to face. Hearing her words, Subaru reflected with regret that, in his rush, he'd been neglecting the very girl right in front of him. It was a phenomenon that often cropped up in stories about time loops.

For an individual accumulating loop after loop, things experienced over and over lose their freshness. Because of that, a gap opens up between his own accumulated experience of events and the people around him, who always react as though experiencing it for the first time—until eventually, he stops being able to see them as people at all—

"...I never imagined I'd genuinely end up in that position myself."

And besides, in works like that, falling into that kind of mental state was always something that happened after dozens, hundreds, or some even more vast number of attempts against a wall too high to overcome. Compared to that, Subaru was still only—a mere fifteen times.

Was he seriously claiming that after a mere fifteen times, his ■ had already grown so desolate he could no longer see people as people?

"Am I an idiot? No, I am an idiot."

Spitting out the weakness, the fragility, the pathetic wretchedness of his own ■, Subaru steeled himself. He'd only died fifteen times, that was all. He'd simply gone on squandering his life pointlessly, without any progress to show for it. What right did someone like that have to say he was tired? No such right existed. None at all.

Stand up. Lift your head. Clench your fist. Natsuki Subaru. There's no one but you. No one but you.

—If it were "Natsuki Subaru," he would never make a mistake like this.

"—Subaru was never some superman who could do everything from the start, I suppose."

"————"

Beatrice suddenly declared this to Subaru, caught in the middle of his own repeated self-interrogation. At those words, Subaru caught his breath, eyes widening. Staring straight into Subaru's black eyes, Beatrice shook her head, sorrow welling in her blue ones.

"I'll say it as many times as it takes, I suppose. Subaru isn't some superman who can do everything. He's always desperately fighting whatever's in front of him, getting hurt for everyone else's sake... and he's not even good at bearing pain. Just an ordinary boy, I suppose."

"T-that can't be true. It can't. Because if it weren't like that..."

"If it weren't like what?"

"If it weren't, then how could..."

He thought of the wound on his arm, the wounds all over his body, his arm grown just a little sturdier, Emilia who relied on him, Beatrice, Ram, Echidna, Julius, Meili, Shaula—turning them over, letting them sink in, and gripped his own chest tightly.

His ■ was pounding loud enough to be a nuisance. More, perhaps, than even the moments he'd stood at death's door—it might just be enough to avert Subaru's ■.

"Natsuki-kun, you're back. ...Did something happen?"

"Geez, big bro, you had everyone worried sick and this is what we come back to? C'mon, don't tell me you're fighting with Beatrice-chan now."

An argument between Subaru and Beatrice—no, it wasn't anything so fierce as that. But at the sight of the two of them facing each other, Echidna and Meili, who had been patrolling the archive, came back. He couldn't quite look either of them straight in the face.

"Beatrice, what happened to Natsuki-kun? Was the shock that severe, after all?"

"...I don't think that's entirely wrong, I suppose. But I think Betty and the rest of us haven't been thinking nearly enough about Subaru, who's going through something so awful."

"That's..."

Echidna's brow knit as she watched Beatrice, gazing at Subaru, lower her eyes. But she seemed to grasp the gist of it almost at once, giving a small nod,

"Shaula, who ran off, and Julius, who chased after her, haven't come back. I'm worried about Emilia-san and Ram-san too, off to bring Rem-san back. At the very least, I'd like to join up with one group or the other..."

"...Echidna and Meili, head for the balcony, I suppose. Betty will go look for Emilia and the others, who headed for the green room. Be careful, all of you, I suppose."

"Sure, but what about big bro?"

As Beatrice issued her orders briskly, Echidna and Meili's eyes both turned to Subaru. Under their gazes, Subaru naturally started to say he'd go with Beatrice, but—

"—Subaru will stay put here and behave, I suppose."

"Wha—! Don't say something stupid, Beatrice! I'm fine! Sure, coming back from the book I had a bit of a fever, but it's already settling down—"

Said by Beatrice as she stood, Subaru snapped back at her words reflexively. He put strength into his legs at once, meaning to fall in line beside them. He put strength into his legs—but—

"—Huh?"

"Settling down, and then what?"

At Beatrice's question, Subaru looked down at his own trembling legs in disbelief. However much he tried to plant his knee and force strength into it, he couldn't keep his balance. No matter what he did, his posture collapsed, and he couldn't get to his feet.

"Why..."

"Isn't it obvious, I suppose? Subaru's been pushing himself far too hard, this whole time."

"No, wait, wait a second! Something to this degree shouldn't be enough to—"

"————"

"There's no way. I'm..."

He shook his head in denial, trying again and again, but the result never changed. It wasn't as though there were pain, or suffering, or exhaustion—yet Subaru's fighting spirit simply wouldn't reach his hands, his legs. Even as he struggled, Beatrice took a step, moving toward the stairs.

"—Subaru, whatever's lacking, Betty and the others will make up for it, I suppose. So you don't have to think you need to do absolutely everything by yourself. Because—"

"Beatrice..."

"Because that's the Natsuki Subaru way, I suppose."

Leaving a smile for Subaru, who stared in stunned disbelief, Beatrice took off running. As though keeping pace with her momentum, Echidna and Meili turned their backs on Subaru too,

"Natsuki-kun, I have expectations for you. There's no need to come chasing after us right away. Even so, you might just end up being that final push, mightn't you."

"Personally, I think it'd be fine for you to just rest. But you're the one who talked all big at me, big bro, so you'd better try a little harder on that front, okay?"

Leaving Subaru behind, having said their piece and taken his without letting him get a word in, the three of them vanished from the archive. If they went down the stairs from the third floor, they should run into Julius, who ought to be about due back around now. Subaru even found himself half-hoping Julius might drag him out along with them—but—

"There's no way he's coming... not after Beatrice said that."

He would honor her wishes. More than that, Echidna and Meili had both witnessed the change in Subaru after his return from the Book of the Dead. Kind as they were, there was no way they wouldn't worry about it. But that very concern was exactly what could get someone killed. And he never wanted to watch a single one of his companions die before him again.

"Because I'm weak... breaking down over something this small, how could I—"

The real "Natsuki Subaru" wouldn't have stopped in his tracks over something this trivial. Anger at his own spinelessness burned hot in Subaru's chest. But at the same time, another feeling welled up alongside it. It was the words Beatrice had left behind.

'—Subaru was never some superman who could do everything from the start, I suppose.'

'I'll say it as many times as it takes, I suppose. Subaru isn't some superman who can do everything. He's always desperately fighting whatever's in front of him, getting hurt for everyone else's sake... and he's not even good at bearing pain. Just an ordinary boy, I suppose.'

The image of the absent "Natsuki Subaru" that Beatrice had described. Hearing it, though, Subaru couldn't believe it. Could hardly believe it. Because if that were true, wouldn't it mean he was really nothing but an ordinary person?

"Something must have happened. That's what changed you, isn't it, 'Natsuki Subaru'..."

Some kind of tremendous encounter, maybe. Some unbelievable power he'd gained, maybe. Some experience beyond anything the present him could even imagine, maybe.

There must have been something that let him break free of being a hopeless Natsuki Subaru—that must be exactly why "Natsuki Subaru" had won everyone's trust in this new world.

"Otherwise..."

Still trembling, he struck his own legs and rolled up his sleeve. There, a black, hideous growth—one whose cause existed nowhere in Subaru's memory—etched its pattern into his skin. Could someone go through an experience that left him carrying something like this curse on his body, and still remain fundamentally unchanged at the root? There was no way. So "Natsuki Subaru" must be—

"Natsuki Subaru, at your service..."

What slipped from his mouth, unbidden, was the very origin of his distrust toward this world's "Natsuki Subaru." A message, at times carved into his own arm, at times scrawled across an entire room like a curse, from an entity that shouldn't have been here. It felt almost like a curse aimed at whoever had stolen his role.

"If you're really in there, inside me... then come out...!"

Gripping his own arm hard, squeezing with enough force to make the bone creak, Subaru pleaded with himself—no, with the absent "Natsuki Subaru." A being that had kept up its meaningful silence all along, one capable of achieving the ideal that the Subaru standing here could never reach. If "Natsuki Subaru" truly existed, then come out, he demanded.

"Come, come, come...! Show yourself, 'Natsuki Subaru'!!"

If his arm wasn't enough, he thought, and put his hands to his own throat. Of course, strangling one's own neck with one's own hands isn't easy, and it doesn't work well either. To begin with, something as convenient as "Natsuki Subaru" showing his face the moment his life was in dire straits simply wasn't going to happen. If that were the trigger, Subaru had already pulled it nearly twenty times over. And in all that, exactly how many times had "Natsuki Subaru" shown his face?

"Damn it, damn it, damn it...! Me! I'm no good! I need you, 'Natsuki Subaru'!"

Giving up on holding down his trembling knees, Subaru slammed his fist into the floor. His fist ached against the archive floor, whose material he couldn't even name, but it was nothing compared to the helplessness gnawing at his chest.

He wanted to save them. Everyone. He truly wanted to save them. None of them were bad people. None of them wanted to hurt each other. He understood that now. He could believe, without hesitation, who to save, who to love. He wanted to save every one of them like that, and yet he lacked the power to do it.

"Isn't this exactly when I need you...? And yet, why is it me? A coward, a spineless wreck like me can't save everyone... and yet..."

Even now, his legs wouldn't move. Because of that, he'd sent Beatrice, Echidna, Meili, and—not even here right now—Emilia, Ram, Julius, Shaula, off into a place of death. Because Subaru could do nothing, he couldn't save them from a fate that led toward death.

"...Cor Leonis."

Murmuring weakly, Subaru activated his own Authority, in order to be hurt by it. The power of the "small king" dwelling within him instantly conveyed the whereabouts of his companions throughout the tower. And with unmistakable clarity, it also conveyed the final ends they might meet, and the end this tower itself might meet.

Far off, a light apparently locked in a ferocious battle was Emilia. This time, the light that should have been Ram at her side was some distance away instead, alongside another light—Patrasche, and Rem. She was accompanying the two of them. On the balcony, Shaula was fighting hard, and Meili had likely joined her there. Julius and Echidna seemed to be moving together, and Beatrice, acting alone, could be seen heading toward Emilia's group.

Everything was flowing naturally, exactly as Beatrice had instructed, exactly as the situation Subaru already knew. At this rate, this loop too would end in failure—

"—?"

In the midst of his mind, dominated by such pessimistic thoughts, Subaru felt an odd sense of wrongness. Turning his head, Subaru surveyed what lay behind him—the shelves holding a vast quantity of books, the great archive containing countless Books of the Dead, Taygeta.

"————"

It was a strange sensation. A first-time sensation, without a doubt. After all, ever since Subaru had become aware of his Cor Leonis Authority and started returning by death to this very spot, he had activated the Authority here, over and over, without fail. Every single time, Subaru had rushed off to his companions, trying one approach after another, running himself ragged to change the situation, burning through his own life in the process.

So activating the Authority here in Taygeta had always been the natural thing to do. But, as Beatrice had told him, this might well be the first time he'd actually stopped in place here and, one step removed from the core of things, taken a bird's-eye view of the tower's state. Was that, perhaps, why?

"—This reading is..."

He could feel it faintly, so weakly it seemed like it might flicker out at any moment. Compared to the far-off but unmistakable presence of his companions within the tower, it was a sensation far, far weaker—but a sensation that was, without a doubt, there.

Forcing his trembling legs to move, Subaru advanced at a crawl, slow as a caterpillar. Then he leaned his body hard against the bookshelf, and with all his strength, hauled his upper body upright. Somehow, leaning his weight on the shelf, he got to his feet. Then he reached out toward that faint, flickering light that seemed on the verge of vanishing, and grasped it. The book he'd grasped—a single Book of the Dead—he pulled it out—

"————"

He caught his breath. A single book, black-covered, with nothing but a title on it—an utterly plain, dry, unremarkable-looking book. And yet, it held truly enormous significance for Subaru. Because—

"—Natsuki Subaru."
