—It was a button misaligned that could have been.

With a single gear out of alignment, the story twists greatly, and the rails of destiny easily shift direction. When the flow of what should happen changes, the torrent of fate tosses people about like leaves in the wind.

Even so, if one were to reach that unchanging far shore without ever submitting... Surely, it would only be when an unshakeable, resolute desire completely deceives its heavenly mandate.

That once-in-a-lifetime lie is the very meaning of the man's existence.

"Who... are you..."

Whether to take it as sincerity or clumsiness, the girl could choose neither. Only slowly, just like the child who had lost consciousness first, reality drifted away.

"Let's go, Oni child."

"I... am..."

She felt her body being scooped up by the woman, but she could not resist. As her consciousness gradually faded into white, her lips trembled feebly. An Oni—even being called that meant nothing to her. Yet, as for any other name—

"—Rem."

That was the name spoken by the black-haired boy wrapped in that terribly sickening stench. It was probably just an illusion. —That, to her empty, hollow self, that name had somehow felt so familiarly warm to her ears.

If he could deceive the Observers, trap 'The Great Disaster', and fully deceive Vincent Vollachia, what Chisha wanted would surely radiate brilliance. Therefore—

"――――"

Pushed away, a slight hardening of cheeks was recognizable through the demon mask.

A face whose true intent many would misread. Being overly wise, the Emperor frequently omitted words that shouldn't be omitted, and even that expression was taciturn.

Likely, the only one who could correctly receive the meaning of that expression was himself, having spent long years with this Emperor.

"Ah, yet—"

Perhaps, or just maybe, he couldn't help holding expectations. That there might be someone who would look straight at this wonderfully wise Emperor Vincent Vollachia—this single man who was far too foolish and possessed far too low self-evaluation.

Thinking so couldn't be helped.

"――――"

Breaching the castle wall, avoiding the pushed-away Vincent, as light pierced the chest of Chisha wearing the Emperor's 'Mask', he saw it outside the window.

—The accomplice who was supposed to have been sent far away, riding the back of a flying dragon, diving toward this location.

Pierced through the heart, no one could survive. Thus, it was an inevitable occurrence, the final destination of destiny that ought to arrive.

"――――"

The man died.

As Cheshire Trim, becoming Chisha Gold, and as Vincent Vollachia, the man died.

Before the eyes of the master he pledged loyalty to, and the accomplice who aided in deceiving that master, he died.

—That was everything that occurred at this moment.

△▼△▼△▼△

—And so, the story converged toward its proper form, welcoming the time of 'The Great Disaster'.

The army of revived corpses, led by the 'Witch' seeking the Empire's destruction, burned the world. Assembled 'Nine Divine Generals', taking positions different from canonical history, burning their lives to exert the maximum of their martial prowess while even life and death altered endings. Crossing to the neighboring nation to search for the precious boy separated from them, those girls witnessed the grand turning point arriving in the Empire, burning their bonds without losing hope. And the sun-like woman possessing one half of the two 'Yang Swords' that shouldn't naturally exist, facing the 'Witch' venting rage at herself over the corpses whose way of being was distorted, shone with her unshakeable arrogant beauty, burning her soul.

At the center of that story colored in flames, flames, and flames, those who witnessed the death of the man who had fully deceived everything raised hoarse voices, shed tears of blood, and cracked their souls, burning destiny.

"—Stand up! Don't give up! Giving up is something I'll never forgive! You already know full well what he lied for!!"

"Silence, fool. What do you know of me? I am—"

"You are!!"

""—Seventy-seventh Emperor of the Sacred Vollachia Empire, Vincent Vollachia!""

Shaking knees about to buckle, the man stood up. Holding his heart together as it threatened to sob uncontrollably, the boy clenched his fists. Blaming herself for lacking strength, the girl raised her face knowing there was still something she could do, and the child holding close to her pointed at 'The Great Disaster' shaking the capital, roaring.

At the large hole in the shattered castle wall, looking down at the streetscape, the men lined up. Lining up and exchanging glances, Natsuki Subaru and Vincent Vollachia—two whose karma should have crossed much earlier, two entrusted by the man who had fully deceived destiny—faced forward to destroy the surging 'Great Disaster' and grasp the future that man had desired.

"I am your Emperor, Vincent Vollachia. —One of the Sword Wolves of the Empire!"

"My name is Natsuki Subaru, a passerby as sudden as thunder, but accomplice to that big liar Chisha Gold!"

The two declared their positions, echoing their voices loudly and forcefully into the capital sky. Facing the approaching 'Great Disaster', the terrifying horde of enemies, Subaru (as Natsumi) distorted her cheeks, knowing Chisha had constructed the board state so combat power would concentrate most in the capital when they surged. And then—

"I received the baton passed to us without asking. —Hey, Fate, bring it on!!"

△▼△▼△▼△

—It was a button misaligned that could have been.

With a single gear out of alignment, the story twists greatly, and the rails of destiny easily shift direction. When the flow that shouldn't happen changes, the torrent of fate tosses people about like leaves in the wind.

Even so.

Even so, if one were to reach that unchanging far shore without ever submitting... Surely, it would only be when an unshakeable, resolute desire completely deceives its heavenly mandate.

That once-in-a-lifetime lie is the very meaning of the man's existence—

"—I shall lower the curtain on this, my one and only grand stage, as such."
