Arc 3 Chapter 62: "A Reckoning of Past and Future"

Patrasche swung her head around with a gesture like wrinkling her muzzle, baring her fangs at the blue-skinned Earth Dragon glaring back at her from up close.

Stroking her neck lightly with his palm, Subaru gave a small murmur of agreement to Patrasche's rising temper—"Yeah, I feel the same way."

Theirs had been a short acquaintance, but the bond now forged between Subaru and Patrasche already carried the strength of having crossed the line between life and death together. Through the reins, it almost felt to Subaru as though Patrasche's thoughts were flowing straight into him—

"Forgive me for interrupting the moment, but would you mind not seducing my Earth Dragon? Yours is quite something too. Mine may well follow after her."

"Wait—Patrasche! You little—are you hitting on his dragon?! Who said we felt the same way about anything?! Getting all worked up right before a life-or-death battle!"

"That dragon's got no room to talk to big brother about that, not after the show he put on before we left."

Subaru shouted at Patrasche's unexpected betrayal, and beside him Ricardo, chin propped on his hand, let that comment slip out with an air of exasperation.

Subaru's throat caught with a strangled "ugh," and while he stood there speechless, someone slipped past him and stepped forward—

"Joining up in a place like this—must be nice, being so high and mighty, Julius. Just a few hours ago we were fighting for our lives over here, y'know."

"You have me there; I can hardly hold my head up. But allow me to correct you on one point, Ferris. I am not a man named Julius. Let's see... I suppose I'll go by Yuri."

Julius shrugged off Ferris's narrow-eyed sarcasm and spouted that nonsensical drivel with a straight face. Naturally, everyone's eyes narrowed at the meaningless alias, but he brushed off their looks with a cool, composed smile.

"Hypothetically speaking, of course—it would be improper for a man holding the rank of knight to join a band of hired hands and lower himself to the status of a mercenary. There is no fact of a man named Julius Juukulius joining the Iron Fang. Here stands only a man named Yuri."

"Nya~. Honestly, the chivalry of a proper noble house is such a pain, isn't it. Good thing Feri-chan's family's just fallen nobility."

"I don't consider chivalry a pain at all. Though I do think it's a problem that I have to go to such lengths merely to lend a friend some help. —As an aside, I'll also state plainly that the suspension handed down to Julius Juukulius was lifted as of midnight last night, when the date changed."

"Setting up some pointless little safety net, aren't you... Is there even any point to a fake name at that rate?"

Subaru clicked his tongue and grumbled that insult while eavesdropping on Julius and Ferris's exchange. The way he looked away, lips curled, had all the hallmarks of sulking—and since that was, in fact, exactly what he was doing, there was no excuse to be made.

Catching Subaru's small mutter of complaint, Julius suddenly looked his way. He urged his Earth Dragon forward and positioned himself directly in front of Subaru.

"You look livelier than I expected—that's a relief. —How is your condition?"

"—!"

Julius's inquiry after his health made something click sharply inside Subaru's brain. Whether meant as mockery or sarcasm, the question was more than enough to summon up memories of a few days ago for Julius—though for Subaru it felt like nearly two weeks past.

Taking the provocation—for that was surely all it could be meant as, a probing jab—Subaru felt curses rising all the way to his throat, and somehow managed to swallow them back down.

He cleared his throat, took a deep breath, arranged his face into practiced composure, and smoothed his short bangs back with an affected flourish.

"Ah, well, it was just a scratch, y'know? Spit on it and it healed right up, that kind of thing. More to the point, aren't you a little slow showing up, for someone calling himself reinforcements? What, were you too busy writing up an incident report for the higher-ups because you got serious against amateurs?"

Inferring the background from the earlier talk of a suspension, Subaru fired back with his specialty—provocation. Julius's expression stiffened slightly in response.

"That's not what I meant to bring up—I wanted to speak of your honorable wounds from the Witchbeast subjugation... though it's a relief to see those have healed as well. They were never as flashy as they looked, to begin with. You, who are so skilled at winning sympathy, were rolling about in exaggerated agony, as I recall."

"Ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha." "Fu fu fu fu fu fu fu."

Dry laughter crossed back and forth between the two of them, and an air of imminent violence began to settle over the scene.

One might expect the people around them to try and defuse the situation, but Ferris and Ricardo simply stood by watching with the eyes of people enjoying a good show, while Mimi had wandered off into the crowd on the other side to look for her brother. Which left, inevitably, the duty of settling things to—

"Rekindling old ties is well and good, but is now really the time for it, the two of you?"

Stepping forward to offer that gentle rebuke was the old swordsman astride his Earth Dragon—Wilhelm.

The treasured sword entrusted to him by his lord at his hip, Wilhelm faced Julius and narrowed his eyes, quietly brimming with dignity.

"I cannot thank you enough for this reinforcement. Our forces were considerably worn down in the battle against the White Whale. ...As the one who dragged everyone into my own self-indulgent cause, I confess I was anxious."

"Wilhelm-san, that's not—"

Subaru cut in, his voice dropping, as Wilhelm spoke on in that subdued tone. The Subjugation of the White Whale had been, for Subaru too, one of the walls he'd needed to clear for various reasons of his own. Subaru's own self-righteous will had played an undeniable part in it, and there was no need for Wilhelm to feel any burden over it at all.

It galled him that he couldn't explain everything, but Subaru wanted at least to try and lift that guilt from the old man's shoulders. But before his words could take shape—

"You've come to wear a good face, Wilhelm-sama."

Julius murmured that, quietly, looking at the old man.

Before Subaru could even look up, Julius had already caught the light in Wilhelm's eyes—the look of a man freed from some long-clinging possession.

"You seem an entirely different man from when we last met. ...Reinhard, too, will find some measure of relief in this, I think."

"So... he shall."

Wilhelm set his hand to his chin and lowered his eyes.

In that brief moment of hesitation before the words came, how much inner conflict must have churned within him? Subaru, ignorant of the details, could only knit his brow at the exchange, and once again the eyes watching them were the eyes of those who understood.

Left behind and none too pleased about it, Subaru watched as Wilhelm lifted his lowered face.

"I could not face him honestly. I knew he bore no fault, knew he meant no ill—and still I could not forgive him. ...In time, I shall answer for that."

"That alone should be more than enough for him to gain something from."

Though Wilhelm's voice carried a bitter edge, Julius dipped his chin in agreement all the same. Then, slowly, he turned that perceptive gaze toward Subaru.

Naturally straightening, Subaru braced himself for the verbal sparring to resume—but instead—

"I must offer you my thanks."

"—Huh?"

Before Subaru could stop the sound of surprise from escaping him, Julius lightly dismounted from his Earth Dragon. Then he looked up at Subaru, still astride Patrasche, and bent at the waist.

"This Subjugation of the White Whale was, by rights, a burden the knights should have borne. For putting an end to a calamity the various nations had left unaddressed for so many years, my thanks—"

Offered such elegant, flowing gratitude, Subaru—who only moments ago had been racing ahead on ill will toward Julius—found himself unable to respond on the spot. Beside him, watching his flustered hesitation, Ferris cut in with a "wait, wait, wait—"

"The credit for taking down the White Whale belongs squarely to Duke Karsten's leadership—it's Crusch-sama's achievement, so don't go getting the wrong idea, 'kay? It was Vil-jii who struck the final blow, too."

"I am well aware. That he himself lacks the power to slay the White Whale, I've heard directly from the man who crossed swords with it—Julius."

Still refusing to break his pretense of being someone other than Julius, Julius took in Ferris's remark and continued, "However—"

"There's no question his presence played a great role in the Witchbeast's subjugation. Ferris, surely you'd agree with that much yourself?"

"Nya! Well, that's... true, but..."

Fingertips pressed together, Ferris mumbled and shrank back, defeated. Having thus talked the cat-ears down, Julius turned his attention back to Subaru.

"Thanks to you, people can now forget the days of living in fear of the fog. —Anastasia-sama, too, will be overjoyed, I'm sure."

"If it'd just been the first half I could've taken that at face value, but tack on the second half and it's hard to give you a clean yes."

"And my friend's long years of regret... will finally reach a turning point as well."

Julius closed his eyes and let the words escape like a sigh.

Subaru understood well enough that this "friend" probably referred to the red-haired hero, but he had no idea what long-held regret a man like that could possibly carry. Could there really be a past weighing on someone like him, something requiring regret?

In any case, being thanked so plainly was of course not an unpleasant feeling. Wilhelm's long-cherished wish coming true was something to be genuinely happy about, and Subaru was well aware his own cooperation had, at least a little, helped bring it about. And yet, Subaru's inner feelings before Julius remained thoroughly complicated.

Though he put up a brave front, the first thing that surfaced in Subaru's heart at the sight of this handsome man was the weak, cowering part of him that wanted to flinch away. And even overcoming that weak part, what waited next was a defiance shielded by the bluster meant to hide that same weakness. He did feel genuine gratitude for the reinforcements—but the resentment he harbored toward the poor character of their lord, who'd see fit to station another force here, Julius of all people, was enough to fill volumes if he ever started listing it out. It was, in short, a murky mess of feelings.

But Subaru let none of those negative feelings show on his face.

Swallowing down that tangled, complicated emotion, what Subaru did instead was just one thing.

"Behold."

And with that, he twisted his cheek into a smug, provocative grin, full of swagger.

"Ugh, villain face~" "That grates on the nerves." "Nah, that's a face beggin' to get hit." "His nostrils are all puffed up!"

"Hey, hey, that's some brutal booing from my own side! I try to look a little smug about my achievements and this is what I get?!"

Spitting his outrage at his harshly critical audience, Subaru turned back to face Julius, breathing hard. With a loud voice and a deep breath, he steadied himself.

"So in the end, what is it you're here to do? What'd you come for?"

"—You really did pull it off."

"Huh?"

Ignoring Subaru's question, all Julius offered was a murmur that sounded strangely wistful. Before Subaru could ask him to repeat himself, Julius shook his head. "Never mind," he said, and continued.

"I ask to confirm your understanding—the Iron Fang, bound by contract to Anastasia-sama, has been lent out to Crusch-sama... no, to you, only for the duration of the White Whale's subjugation."

"Huh? Was that the deal? But I'm pretty sure the young miss said..." "Big sister, please be quiet."

Hearing Julius's remark, Mimi tried to interject, but was blocked by the cat-type demihuman who'd somehow appeared at her side without anyone noticing. Taking in that exchange out of the corner of his eye, Subaru frowned and listened to Julius's words.

Seeing Subaru's expression, Julius raised a hand lightly and continued, "In other words—"

"Now that the White Whale has been felled, our reason for cooperating no longer exists. Our job here is done. —And yet, here you are, about to do... what, exactly?"

"Julius is such a forgetter-guy~. The young miss said all sorts of things before we set out, y'know~. Mimi doesn't remember though." "Please be quiet."

The kittens' back-and-forth kept nagging at him, but Subaru was finally starting to grasp what Julius was getting at. In other words—

"You're saying I gotta choose, right here—pull out or keep the reinforcements going?"

"I've been instructed to sell our services at a high price, you see. Or perhaps this is a scene where our strength isn't needed at all?"

Julius gestured pointedly behind himself, pressing Subaru for a decision.

Even so, this wasn't a moment for careless judgment born of irritation. It would be easy enough for Subaru to send them packing out of anger right now, but doing so would be a foolish act of whittling down his own strength before the greatest wall still waiting ahead.

That said, meekly accepting whatever "high price" Julius named was also a problem. Continuing to issue empty promises was about the worst move one could make in a negotiation, and more than that, what hung on Subaru's judgment here was the lives of many people, along with the future of one particular girl.

As Subaru fell silent, he glanced sideways to see Wilhelm and Ferris waiting quietly, showing no sign of wanting to interject. If Subaru asked for their help here, they'd likely find some way to hire the Iron Fang on their own, framing it as an approach from the "Crusch Camp" in these negotiations. But that would only mean shifting the debt he owed to a different party. As things stood, Subaru and Crusch were in a balanced state with one favor owed on each side, and it would be unwise to upset the current arrangement—where his own favor mattered all the more precisely because Crusch valued her debts so heavily.

Next, glancing toward Ricardo's mercenary band, he saw Ricardo standing with arms folded, dark eyes closed, adopting a wait-and-see stance. Mimi mimicked him, eyes shut too, though every so often her orange cat ears twitched and her cute little nose quivered.

Earlier, Ricardo's reasons for going along with the fight against the Witch Cult had seemed a bit thin to Subaru—but now that Julius had brought up this negotiation, it made sense. Knowing full well this was a negotiation Subaru couldn't refuse, Ricardo's attitude had simply meant: do us the favor of playing along.

"Filthy. Real fitting for a money-grubber, filthy through and through..."

"That's quite a way to look at a man. I'll have you know, this ain't even my idea. Takin' advantage of a fella's weak spot like this—well, that's just the sorta thing that happens when you love money more than most, is all..."

"Your inner conflict runs pretty shallow! Not that I was counting on you for anything else!"

He had no intention of asking help from the enemy's boss—even if only a boss in name—Ricardo. In any case, the maddening thing was that this negotiation left Subaru with only one possible answer: yes.

That would balance the books with the Crusch Camp, but it would also mean incurring a one-sided debt to Anastasia on top of it. It was a bitter decision, but since turning down the reinforcements wasn't an option either, Subaru could only grit his teeth and swallow the frustration.

If only there were some way to accept the reinforcements while sidestepping the whole debt problem entirely—

"Fog... White Whale... the highway and the deadline, and the end of it... the Witch."

Thinking furiously, Subaru muttered a word that had flickered across his mind. Those disconnected fragments of thought began to link together, guided by some faint thread, and then—

"The Subjugation of the White Whale isn't over yet... how about that?"

"—Now that's an interesting statement."

At Subaru's half-improvised remark, Julius narrowed his eyes and said as much. Behind him, the beastfolk mercenary band, and more so the members of the subjugation force—Wilhelm's reaction in particular, wide-eyed—brought a pang of guilt to Subaru's conscience.

But he'd noticed a problem that, separate from the old man's long-cherished wish being fulfilled, still could not simply be left alone. Namely—

"The White Whale, the Witchbeast that produces the fog—there's a chance it's a pawn of the Witch Cult. I know someone who said something that sounded exactly like that."

—That had been, if he recalled right, the third world, the very last scene of his previous loop.

Surrounded by the Witch Cult in the forest, unable to fully dodge Petelgeuse's Unseen Hand, beaten down and hollowed out by helplessness as Emilia's corpse was kicked and trampled before him.

At that moment, that madman, hurling filthy abuse at him, had indeed let something slip.

The highway blockade is complete. No one can cross the fog, he'd said.

How did that man know that? Why did he speak of it as though it were his own doing? And then, the crowning touch—afterward, the beast that would freeze the world at its ending had let slip one more line.

"Some know-it-all-looking guy said something about devouring the White Whale. If that's true, if it really is one of the Witch Cult's hands... then it showing up has to have something to do with where I was headed."

If Petelgeuse had summoned the White Whale to the highway to obstruct passage into the Mathers domain, then his purpose could only be one thing: their mad cult's designs. Which meant the White Whale's blockade of the highway had been meant to attack the mansion—to attack Emilia.

"We gotta make the Witch Cult pay up for that little stunt they pulled. This time's worth, and four hundred years' worth, all together. Only once that's done can you really call the White Whale subjugation complete."

"————"

"You've got a job here, paid for by your employer, mercenary—don't go throwing it away halfway through. Or you want to pay a penalty and pull out?"

Speaking with bravado, Subaru watched to see how Julius would respond.

Privately, he was almost impressed with himself at how thin the grounds for his claim were, but putting on a brazen, confident face despite it was something Subaru had learned to do by now.

It was all speculation drawn from scattered fragments of information about the future—and this time, drawn from even more scattered fragments than usual, things he'd overheard while barely conscious, so even he found the credibility thin. He'd stitched it together into something that sounded plausible, but he had no idea whether it would actually be convincing. Still, if it at least gave him a foothold to keep the negotiation going—

"—I suppose we'll call that a passing grade, more or less."

"Huh?"

"I'll polish it up a bit to sound better on our end, but broadly speaking, I'll go along with your claim. It'll spare Anastasia-sama's reputation as well."

"H-hold on a second!"

Subaru raised a palm, trying to stop Julius. But Julius took Subaru's flustered reaction in stride.

"Is something the matter? The mercenary band will continue its cooperation. The compensation has already been paid by Anastasia-sama, as arranged. There shouldn't be any issue, should there?"

"That was too easy... I mean, what's with you being so understanding all of a sudden?! You're—"

He nearly spoke the rest, but caught himself, painfully aware of the ugly part of himself surfacing.

He realized he harbored a wish that Julius—reading the situation, willing to go along with his clumsy line of argument, trying to help—might turn out to be something other than the decent man he appeared to be.

He realized that, somewhere inside him, Subaru wanted Julius to remain an unlikeable person he could never see eye to eye with.

"Well, it's not like there's no reward in it. If you were some dim-witted fool, I'd just wring you dry once and be done with it—but with a sharp one like you, there's no end of chances to keep wringing."

"So in the end, the wringing part never changes..."

Seeing Ricardo cut into the conversation, Subaru gladly latched onto it as a lifeline. Feeling himself run, again and again, toward the easier path left a sour taste in his mouth.

Subaru's self-loathing tangled together with his resentment of others, and the atmosphere turned one-sidedly sour. But facing that, Subaru let out one long, deep breath.

"I'm... the one in the wrong. Damn it, my bad. Ugh, dammit. This isn't even what I want to say. Even back then, I was the one who—"

Hand pressed to his forehead, Subaru struggled to force out some rational reply. But the words wouldn't come easily. In his head, he understood it well enough.

This was a moment to be grateful to Julius, who had brought reinforcements and shown his intent to join the fight. What had happened before, too, had been a result Subaru's own rashness had brought about—now that he could look back on it calmly, it was painfully clear who'd been in the wrong.

Or even—what the consequences of Julius's actions back then had ultimately brought about.

"————"

Julius said nothing to Subaru's halting words, simply waiting.

He surely understood what Subaru was trying to say, and he could easily have spoken the answer himself before Subaru managed to. But he didn't do that—and the fact that he didn't made Subaru hate him all the more. If only he could just keep hating him, plainly, and leave it at that.

"I was... in the wrong. Sorry. I... apologize."

In a low voice, wrung out from somewhere deep inside, Subaru put it into words.

It was a memory Subaru hated even to look back on—but a scene he had to face someday if he ever wanted to shake free of it, standing before someone he would eventually have to settle things with.

Hearing Subaru's wrung-out apology, Julius quietly closed his eyes. Then he dipped his chin slowly.

"And I as well must apologize for my discourtesy. I won't take back everything I did and said that day, but for having belittled you—that much, I regret, sincerely."

Julius answered Subaru's apology in kind.

There was such sincerity overflowing in his words that Subaru felt, with startling ease, the ugly feelings that had knotted up inside him begin to dissolve.

Knowing that, Subaru dismounted to the ground just as the "knight" standing before him had, and faced him at eye level, matching his tall frame.

"My bad. But."

"Mm."

"I still hate your guts. —I feel bad about it, and I'm grateful you came now, but I hate you. Really, from the bottom of my heart, ridiculously, I hate you!"

He broke the last line into clauses, pausing after each one, and hurled it straight at him.

Struck head-on like that, Julius looked briefly stunned—then suddenly his composure cracked, and he laughed, sweeping a hand through his hair in that same insufferably stylish way.

"That's fine by me. I can't say I've had much sense, myself, of us ever becoming friends."

For once, both sides needed to sort out what was in their heads.

They formed a circle, the main players gathering to discuss what came next. Subaru stood at the center, ringed by Julius, Ferris, Ricardo, Wilhelm, and the others.

"Ah, honestly, I'm not really good at running things like this, so having everyone stare at me all serious like that is kind of embarrassing..."

Surrounded once more by people with far more force of character than himself, Subaru found himself shrinking back. Back in his original world, he'd been headed straight toward a life of shut-in reclusion, after all. He had no experience standing in front of people, and he was well aware he wasn't the sort of person of character fit to stand above others.

And yet, they all looked at him with a certain measure of trust despite his hesitance, and Subaru, for his part, didn't find that an unpleasant feeling. Which was, annoyingly, the problem.

"Anyway, let's sum things up. So, from here we're headed to the Mathers domain... or rather, to Roswaal's mansion. And there, probably—no, definitely, the Witch Cult's going to show its face."

"The Witch Cult... is it."

At the mention of the Witch Cult's name, each face took on a note of gravity. Given how the conversation had flowed to this point, most of them had likely braced themselves to some degree already, but it seemed actually hearing it confirmed still struck something in them.

Subaru had no idea how the existence of the Witch Cult was generally known in this world, or how these people regarded it—but to Subaru himself, their existence could be summed up in one word: "worst."

Judging from everyone's reactions, it seemed safe to assume that much was shared common ground.

"Subaru. How did you come to realize the connection between the White Whale and the Witch Cult?"

It was Julius who addressed him so casually as "Subaru." Ever since that earlier confession of true feelings, Julius's manner toward him had grown oddly familiar. Honestly, Subaru had complicated feelings about that too, but—

"Damn thing is, I've had the misfortune of running into Witch Cultists before. Didn't come out unscathed, and I've got a headful of nasty memories from it... but one of them let something slip."

"I see. ...So the knights' guess wasn't wrong, then."

"Yeah, that's right. Sounds like whatever records Vil-jii dug up landed on the same conclusion too."

"You knew about it?"

At Subaru's words, Julius nodded in acceptance, and Ferris voiced agreement. Subaru showed surprise at their reaction, but Wilhelm gave a slow shake of his head.

"Noticing the connection was mere chance. The distribution of the White Whale's appearances and the record of the Witch Cult's activities seemed to line up with an unnatural frequency. —Not that it's anything approaching confirmation."

"The White Whale's appearances were the main thread, and the Witch Cult was more like a side note to it, y'know. Even Feri-chan was only half-convinced when she first heard it, though~."

"There was talk to that effect within the knights as well. Though it was treated as little more than idle rumor... on the level of a tall tale, really."

Julius shrugged, and Wilhelm let out a breath, saying, "It couldn't be helped." Listening to their exchange, Subaru scratched his head.

"Anyway, it's lucky for me that there was already some groundwork in place for people to believe this. Even so, trusting something a Witch Cultist said... feels pretty thin on credibility too, but I'm pretty sure the White Whale's connected to those guys. Witchbeasts are creatures the Witch made in the first place, right?"

"So it's said. The existence and origin of Witchbeasts is a mysterious thing. Some breed like ordinary creatures, while others, like the White Whale, simply spring into being without warning. Though exceptions like the White Whale are really only the Black Serpent and the Great Rabbit."

"Feels like some words just flew by that I really shouldn't let slide, but I'm scared, so can we move on?"

Seeing everyone nod that there was no problem, Subaru cleared his throat and continued.

Prefacing it with the warning that they'd be facing off against the Witch Cult, the next thing he needed everyone to understand was—

"The Witch Cult's target is Emilia, and they're planning to burn down not just the mansion but the whole nearby village too. So we've gotta drive those bastards off, one way or another."

"Drive them off. That's a pretty soft thing to say, isn't it."

Ferris narrowed her eyes seductively and drew out the end of her sentence meaningfully, looking at Subaru. It was the kind of gesture that sent a chill crawling up one's spine—except the one doing it was a man.

"What d'you mean, soft?"

"Wouldn't it be better to just cut every last one of them down? Going by the records so far, that's the correct way to deal with those people, y'know?"

"—————"

Hearing Ferris casually declare a massacre, Subaru's mouth fell open in shock.

It wasn't Ferris's extreme statement that startled him—it was the fact that he himself had been the one to say something "soft."

He'd been the one repeating in his own head, over and over, that they should kill those people, that they deserved to die—and yet here he was, surprised by his own shift in mindset, having used such gentle words.

That was surely because, in the end, what Subaru prioritized most had changed.

"If we can protect the mansion and the village, that's enough for now. Drive off the Witch Cult, blast 'em, crush 'em, beat 'em to a pulp, wring 'em out and flatten 'em and burn 'em down and grind 'em to dust..."

"O-okay, I get it. I understand well enough how angry you are at them."

"—Ah! Damn it. No, wait, that's not it. It's not like I'm all fired up with rage and hatred, ready for battle or whatever. And it sure ain't like the reason I got close to Emilia-tan was 'cause of some twisted grudge, that's just an unfounded accusation!"

"No one said anything like that, nya!?"

While he was talking, his anger had bubbled back up all on its own, and as a result he managed to spook both Julius and Ferris. Still, some unnecessary over-explaining did yield one useful result.

Apparently, the very motives behind Subaru's actions that had been doubted in the previous world weren't under suspicion at all in this timeline. Subaru wondered what could be different, but—

"After pulling off a plan where you risked sacrificing yourself to bring down the White Whale, who's gonna have that kind of ulterior suspicion about you now? Honestly, Subaru-kyun, you've got a surprisingly distrustful streak, y'know."

"Distrustful my ass..."

In truth, he did have experience of being doubted by exactly these two—Ferris and Crusch. But the man now laughing merrily seemed, in a way entirely unlike pretense, to genuinely hold no suspicion at all. Was that, too, a result of Subaru's will and actions having changed?

In any case—

"That the Witch Cult will move is beyond doubt at this point. Given their activity, it was already to be expected the moment Emilia-sama entered the Royal Selection."

Julius, hand to his chin, nodded in understanding. His manner was familiar by now, and since this was a matter Subaru had never had the chance to ask about, he raised his hand.

"Hey, quick question—where's this confidence coming from, that the Witch Cult's gonna move just 'cause Emilia-tan's name got out there? Everyone just seems to accept it, which is kinda strange to me... I heard a lot about the Witch Cult's actual nature is still unclear, and stuff."

"You know the Witch Cult's going to act, and yet you're saying something like that?"

Ferris tilted her head, looking exasperated at Subaru's question. Being laughed at for his ignorance was the reaction he'd expected, so Subaru held up a palm toward Ferris's face, saying, "Now, now—"

"We don't have much time. Let's move things along. So, what's the deal there?"

"It's kinda strange for the one who don't know to be bossing people around like that, but... y'know, the one the Witch Cult worships so preciously is the Witch of Envy, Satella. You know that much, right?"

"That much, yeah. Honestly, that's about all I know—just what I picked up from a picture book."

"'Course it's just what you picked up—hardly anyone's still around who's actually seen her in person. Even I'm just going off hearsay. Well, as long as you know the Witch Cultists worship this Satella, that's fine. And do you know that this Witch, Satella, was a Half-Elf?"

"That too, more or less."

The picture book Subaru had read hadn't gone into that much detail, but he'd heard it from Beatrice when he'd asked her about the Witch. And in the royal capital too, Emilia's appearance and origins had frequently been compared to the "Witch of Envy," coming up as a topic again and again. Subaru had been indignant, thinking it wasn't something she should be blamed for.

"Emilia-tan's looks, her features—they're a dead ringer for the Witch, right? But that's no reason to blame the girl for it. That's misdirected resentment, plain and simple."

"Most people don't see it that way. All Satella did was exist looking like that. And getting back to the Witch Cult... to put it simply, they find the very existence of Half-Elves inconvenient."

"Huh?"

A startled sound burst from Subaru's throat before he could stop it. He turned his head, trying to confirm the meaning of what had been said, but the reaction around him showed no special attention directed at Ricardo. In other words, it was shared common knowledge.

"Why? I mean, thinking about it normally... though tracing the 'normal' thinking of guys like that is disgusting just on principle, but thinking normally, wouldn't the natural line of thought be that they'd never persecute a Half-Elf who shares looks with the Witch they worship..."

"Precisely because they worship her, precisely because they think of her as beyond compare, they can't stand something that's similar yet different. Similar, but not the same—a mere imitation. —That existence."

The voice was chillingly cold, laced with a killing intent that seemed to sink straight to the bone.

Subaru lifted his head, involuntarily turning to look at whoever had spoken. And that person was already looking at him too, so their eyes met, tangled together. Subaru found himself pinned in place by a gaze that felt as though it were peering straight into the depths of his heart—but the very person doing it—

"Nya~n, just a little theory Feri-chan came up with, maybe?"

—broke that expression easily, sticking out her tongue, brushing the whole mood away as though it had never happened.

Subaru couldn't find words for that abrupt, almost transformative shift, but Ferris, feigning complete ignorance of his unease, leaned forward.

"It's not exactly news that the Witch Cult's reasons for doing anything are out of their minds, so let's just leave it at that, yeah? The real question's whose lead this particular Witch Cult group targeting Emilia-sama and the others is under, don't you think?"

"An Archbishop of Sin, is it."

"—!? You know that name?"

Ricardo agreed with Ferris's change of subject, and Subaru latched onto the term that came up in it.

Archbishop of Sin—that was the title Petelgeuse had claimed for himself, further explaining that he was the one in charge of "Sloth."

"Are the Witch Cult's Archbishops of Sin something well-known?"

"Just that people like that exist, is about all. Way back, before the Witch of Envy went on her rampage, so the story goes, there were Witches other than Satella too."

"Pride. Wrath. Sloth. Greed. Gluttony. Lust. —Six Witches bearing the names of the deadly sins, is what's said. All of them, it's told, were swallowed up by Satella, who took the name of Envy."

Witches bearing the names of the deadly sins—that, too, was something Subaru had heard somewhere before.

In this world, "Witch" meant the Witch of Envy, Satella, and no other Witch bearing another deadly sin was said to still exist. But—

"Whether it's right to call them the Witch Cult's leadership or not, I've heard that those in that position have taken on the names of the deadly sins in place of the lost Witches. Envy is the symbol of Satella, whom they worship. Which means the other six—the six Archbishops of Sin—"

"Six of them..."

Julius's explanation made Subaru catch his breath, breaking into a cold sweat at the sheer unfathomable depth of the enemy Witch Cult.

The moment Petelgeuse had claimed "Sloth" for himself, it had been predictable that other figures existed to hold the other sins. The Seven Deadly Sins—to Subaru, it was a term steeped in subculture familiarity, dripping with the kind of chuunibyou flavor that usually made his heart race with excitement. But the bad impression left by his firsthand experience with "Sloth" was far too strong to let him receive the word as anything thrilling now.

Still—

"The White Whale, which must've been 'Gluttony'—we already brought that one down. Another Archbishop of Sin's bound to show his face at the Mathers domain, where we're headed next. Sounds like a good chance to tip the whole Witch Cult over in one go."

"Ooh, bold talk. But well, Feri-chan's with you on this being a good chance to crush a Witch Cult whose true nature's been such a mystery. Those guys have been getting away with an awful lot."

"In the sense that we've seen no sign of their activity for so long, the knights, too, have long endured no small bitterness at their hands. Not just myself—many knights would say the same. To be given this opportunity is a welcome thing."

Ferris and Julius voiced their agreement with Subaru's opinion, Ricardo grinned with battle-hungry relish, and Wilhelm simply gave a solemn, quiet nod.

With that settled, what Subaru needed to do now was draw up a plan making use of their current forces and the information about the future he carried. —Though the plan itself was quite simple, and the groundwork for it had already been laid.

"Worst case, this was a plan I had to run with half the manpower I've got now, but with Julius joining up, the numbers problem's gone. I think we can pull it off."

"There's just one thing I'd like to correct—my name is Yuri. It's true I'm on close terms with the eldest son of House Juukulius, but I'd appreciate it if you kept that distinction in mind."

"That whole setup's useless outside of official occasions! And besides, if you were serious about pretending to be someone else, you wouldn't show up dressed as a Royal Guard knight! Your cover story's paper-thin!!"

Shouting down Julius for his shallow attempt at concealment, Subaru, breathing hard, swept his gaze across everyone's faces. Then, with one cough to clear his throat—

"Alright then, let's begin the simple, even-a-monkey-could-follow-it guide to Witch Cult hunting."

Twisting his cheek into a villain's grin, Subaru laid out the plan.

The moonlight began to slant across the plain—the last night before the clash with the Witch Cult was giving way to dawn, and the morning of the final day of this loop was about to begin.
