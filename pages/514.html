Arc 7 Chapter 16: "Circumstances of the Empire"

―― The walled city of Guaral.

That was the name of the first civilized city the group had reached in the Empire. Until now, they had spent their time in an Imperial soldiers' camp and a settlement of the People of Shudrak, environments that were hardly different from outright camping outdoors, so the sight of civilization struck them with an especially deep sense of emotion. That said, it wasn't as though he could unreservedly welcome this civilized change of environment. Because――

"...A checkpoint."

Following his line of sight, looking at the checkpoint being conducted at the city's entrance, Subaru wet his lips with his tongue. Guaral, surrounded on all sides by defensive walls, restricted entry into the city to two great gates, one to the east and one to the west. The checkpoint was permanently set up at that great gate, and even from a distance he could make out the sturdy gate guards, their stern faces lit with sharp, wary eyes.

"'They'll spot us Shudrak on sight, y'know. Can't come with ya as your kin, but if you ain't carryin' anything suspicious, you'll be fine.'" "'No need to be jumpy — just act natural, and you'll be fine. Make sure to properly protect Rem and Rui.'"

Those were the words of Kuna and Holly, who had parted ways with them before they'd arrived at the city. Both were advice not to psych himself up more than necessary, but telling him to forget his tension and anxiety was a rather unreasonable request. After all, in the Empire, Subaru and the others were foreigners with no known affiliation and no proof of identity.

"But even I, an otherworlder with no common sense, can tell that Lugunican people wandering around inside Vollachia right now wouldn't exactly be welcomed."

If he honestly stated who he was, he'd undoubtedly earn the gate guards' displeasure. Being literally turned away at the gate would be one thing, but if he carelessly ended up captured, that would be unbearable to watch. That particular development had already played out once, at the Imperial soldiers' camp.

――And an ending where a great many people were burned alive because of another mistake of Subaru's was absolutely not to be allowed again.

"――――"

"...If you keep making such a stern face, the people around you will start to find it suspicious."

Reflecting his tangled feelings, Subaru had been frowning, brow furrowed, and now his eyes went wide at that voice. The voice came from behind him――from Rem, riding on the carrier frame on his back. Given the structure of the frame, the two of them faced back to back, so Rem shouldn't have been able to see Subaru's face.

"But how'd you know I was frowning if you can't even see me?"

"Even if I can't see it, I can hear your breathing. And through the frame, our backs are touching, so I can feel your heartbeat too. ...Your tension is coming through to me as well."

"Ugh...my bad. Both the frown and the noisy heart."

"Yes. Please quiet them down."

"That's not, like, code for 'silence my heartbeat' as in stop it entirely, right?"

In response to his just-in-case confirmation, Rem only responded with a silent sigh. That exhale of Rem's carried a temperature somewhere exactly between exasperation and indifference toward Subaru. Since it wasn't clear-cut disgust or hostility, he found himself feeling just the tiniest bit relieved. Though if someone told him his aspirations were pathetically low, he'd have nothing to say in rebuttal.

"Uー, uー?"

"...I know, so don't rush me. You're one of the things I'm worried about too, you know."

Tugging insistently at Subaru's sleeve, pointing at the line, was Rui, her long hair tied up. Carrying the Witchbeast horn entrusted to them by Kuna and the others――wrapped in white cloth――on her back, Rui apparently couldn't fathom why Subaru wasn't simply getting in line. But he couldn't be as reckless as Rui, joining the line without thinking anything through.

"Right now, we've got no way to prove our identities, and no bribe money to slip the gate guards. Which means charging in through the front door the honest way just isn't going to work."

"Would it not work to get in line and try talking to the gate guards?"

"Pleading sincerely is a valid option, but I'm scared of the possibility that there's no recovering if it fails. ...Right now, we don't have anyone we can rely on."

Hearing Subaru's concern, Rem made a faint sound in her throat and fell silent. Without her 'memories,' Rem might find it hard to imagine all sorts of things, but given the place and the nature of this country, there was no such thing as being too cautious.

――Unexpectedly, it had been nearly ten days since Subaru and the others had been flung from the Pleiades Watchtower into the Empire.

The initial chaos had considerably faded, and the precarious, one-touch-from-explosive relationship with Rem and Rui had been temporarily set aside, but it still couldn't be said that the entire situation had improved. To begin with, the period during which Subaru, Rem, and, incidentally, Rui had acted alone, just the three of them, had been only that single moment of fleeing from the hunter and Elgina right after being flung here. Outside of that, whether at the Imperial soldiers' camp, the Shudrak settlement, or on the journey to Guaral, someone who knew the Empire had always been acting alongside them. He hadn't built friendly relations with every single one of them, but at least he hadn't felt helpless.

"But from here on, it's different."

They were now at odds with Todd and his men, had parted ways with Abel, and had separated from the People of Shudrak as well. From here, the three of them had to walk through the uncharted territory that was the Empire without a walking stick to lean on. Like it or not, that meant being cautious about his own judgment calls.

"So then, what do we do? Whether we agonize or hesitate, the goal doesn't change, does it? Surely we can't just pass this city by either..."

"Even if we give up on the checkpoint here, there's no guarantee we won't run into the same thing at another city. If this were Lugunica, security wouldn't be this tight...no, wait."

Shaking his head, Subaru considered the Empire's circumstances. Having been driven from the imperial throne, that was how Abel, before they parted, had described his own situation. If that were true, the Empire could currently be described as being in the midst of internal strife. Abel had said the matter of the emperor's absence was being hidden from the general public, but there's no stopping people's mouths, and an atmosphere has a way of spreading without anyone meaning it to. In fact, back at the camp, even Todd had seemed to sense something was off about this particular deployment.

"If that's the case, is the checkpoint being this strict Abel's fault...? Damn it, I resent that guy."

There were aspects in which Abel had helped them, but on balance and as a matter of feeling, Subaru wanted to keep him filed under a slight negative. Given that personality and attitude, he could easily imagine a Nobunaga-esque reason for his subordinates plotting against him.

"He really seemed like the type to embody 'the ruler who doesn't understand people's hearts.'"

"...Are you perhaps not seriously worrying about this, but instead wasting time thinking about something that doesn't matter?"

Stroking his chin, as Subaru muttered that, Rem's voice turned cold and hard. It was a tone that went beyond exasperation or indifference and contained honest anger, so Subaru hastily protested, "No, no, no, no."

"Wait, wait, don't worry. Sure, my round-table-worthy musings there were a waste of breath if anything, but it's not like I'm just standing here blankly staring at the line with no plan at all. I actually have a real idea."

"Do you feel no shame as a human being, telling such a bald-faced lie?"

"Show a little faith in me here?! Judging it a lie with zero deliberation is premature!"

Rem's opinion of him showing no sign of leveling off, Subaru was desperate to restore her trust. In truth, he hadn't been spouting complete nonsense――he did have an actual alternative plan for breaking through the checkpoint. Just the two of them――three of them――couldn't pass the gate guards' check through legitimate means. So then, all they had to do was not be just the three of them.

"In other words..."

"Natsuki Subaru, showing off the true power of one of his hundred and eight special skills――'Reliance on Others.'"

Thumb raised, teeth gleaming, that was Subaru's answer. In response, even though her face shouldn't have been visible to him, Subaru somehow felt, through their backs, that Rem――whose face he equally couldn't see――had scowled.

Passing through the uniquely tense checkpoint, they went through the towering great gate that made you crane your neck to look up at it. With the view now opened up, the stone-built streets brimming with exotic atmosphere spread out before them, and Subaru thrust both fists into the air on the spot, overcome with a sense of accomplishment.

"We made it through!" "Aー Uー!"

Hearing Subaru's vigorous voice, right beside him Rui raised both her hands the same way. There was something to be said about her acting like she was practically one of the group, but for the moment, the joy of clearing the first hurdle to entering the city washed that thought away. Annoying as it was, it was also a fact that Rui's presence had helped them get through the checkpoint.

"Still, it really is different from Lugunica. The royal capital, Priestella too, had bits and pieces that were different, but..."

Gazing at the city's atmosphere, Subaru compared it in his mind to the streets of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica. The one that left the deepest impression on him personally was the town of Costuul near the new Roswaal Manor, but even so, the change of being in a different country seemed to be a significant one. The royal capital, Costuul, and Priestella, each with their own distinct character, had had something like a common thread of atmosphere unique to the Kingdom. That was something he couldn't sense here in Guaral.

Compared to the Kingdom, which had that classic sort of otherworldly-fantasy atmosphere, the Empire's streets were far more rugged and had less color to them. The impression was one of practicality over ornamentation. Rather than gaiety, the entire city had a unity of feeling that emphasized strength and refinement, and the leveled, bare-exposed ground seemed to reinforce that impression.

"In Lugunica it'd be cobblestone, but over here this seems to be the standard."

Given the city's size and population, Guaral could be considered a city above the Imperial average. It was probably about the right measuring stick for comparing various parts of the Empire.

"Well, since I don't want to stay too long anyway, not needing to use that measuring stick would be for the best."

"――Um, could you set me down soon, please?"

"Oh, sorry."

Behind Subaru, lost in such reflections, Rem in the carrier frame asked to be set down, and Subaru apologized to her as he slowly lowered himself to one knee. Unhooking the shoulder strap and setting down the frame, Rem, holding her cane, stood on the ground. During the long journey she'd ridden atop the carrier frame, but once inside a city she wanted to walk on her own two feet. With no more need to hurry, there was no reason not to grant Rem that wish.

"So this is..."

Leaning on her cane, Rem turned around and finally took in the same sight Subaru and the others were seeing. Her brows lifted slightly, and in her pale blue eyes there was surprise mixed with a faint sense of emotion; seeing Rem's positive reaction, Subaru's cheeks loosened just a touch.

"Well? Surprised?"

"That's...yes, I am surprised. It was the same at the checkpoint line, but...this is the first time I've seen this many people, or a proper city, at all."

"Ah, right, of course. For Rem, this is the first real settlement, isn't it."

As mentioned before, up until now, everything Rem had experienced had been exceptional places and treatment. With no 'memories,' Rem had no memory of living with anyone either. Life with Subaru, Rui, and the People of Shudrak was the entirety of Rem's experience.

"――――"

Seeing Rem, eyes narrowed, looking deeply moved, Subaru closed one eye. For the time being, he folded up the carrier frame he'd taken her off of, and waited for her wave of emotion to pass.

――As a city, it was hard to say that Guaral was brimming with vitality.

The line-forming checkpoint, the rugged streets with their dark, drab colors dominant. All together, it was a place far from a gaudy impression. The city's scale was probably only a few thousand people. Even so, the worth of the emotion that had flickered across Rem's eyes wasn't diminished by any of that.

"Want to take a bit of a look around, if that's the case?"

"――. No, that's all right. I don't want to waste any more time than necessary."

"For your sake, nothing would really count as 'unnecessary'..."

To Rem, who'd shaken her head, Subaru answered while scratching his cheek. In truth, if it meant a bright sign appeared in Rem's heart, a bit of sightseeing around the city wouldn't be a problem. If they were in dire straits it'd be one thing to ask her to hold off, but for now, their prospects had settled somewhat. And that, too, was a stroke of good fortune that Rem's quick thinking had created.

"Without Rem, we wouldn't have been able to get along so well with Mr. Flop and the others. You're allowed to be a little selfish, you know."

"...What I'm saying is that it's not good to keep Mr. Flop and the others waiting. We're already relying on them far too much as it is — are you trying to rack up an even bigger debt?"

"Ugh...when you put it that way, that's, yes, sorry..."

His gaze, which had been turned toward the townscape, now snapped back as Rem's sharp look made Subaru clutch at his chest. Behind the two of them, slowly, with the heavy rumble of wheels, a cattle-drawn cart approached――a Falo cart, drawn by the Brave Ox called 'Falo.'

A 'Falo' was a domesticated working animal, like the Earth Dragon or the Large Canine called the 'Liger.' Like Earth Dragons and Ligers, they were often used to pull carts, frequently employed for hauling heavy loads. Their speed was slow, and lacking the Earth Dragon's 'Divine Protection of Wind Evasion,' he'd heard they were generally operated mainly within cities. And then――

"Yoo-hoo, sorry to keep you waiting! Got held up with the confirmation of the cargo, you see. Honestly, the trouble one has with officials and their work."

On the driver's seat of the Falo cart, shrugging his shoulders as he spoke, was a young man with long bangs. A person wrapping brilliant golden hair and fair skin, a slender build, in a wide-sleeved robe――the person who had put in considerable effort to get Subaru and the others into the city. At the Falo-cart youth's appearance, Subaru bowed his head, saying, "Thanks for your trouble."

"Sorry, Mr. Flop. You let us go through first even though we're just tagging along."

"Not at all, I don't mind in the least. Cargo inspection is such a tedious job. Not the sort of thing worth watching, let alone something you'd need to trouble yourself keeping me company for."

At Subaru's words of apology, the youth on the driver's seat――Flop――gently shook his head. With that graceful gesture, his long bangs swayed like a mane, and Subaru thought he could almost see a sparkling effect there. A quirky person, but his responses were fairly sensible, creating a striking gap. Before Subaru's such impression, Flop stroked his own bangs again and said,

"Yes, a tedious job indeed. Not the sort of thing that ought to be left to you two, journeying toward the future, nor even to me...but rather to my little sister."

"Bro, bro, I can hear you, y'know!"

"Ha ha ha, I'm not saying it so you can't hear, sister. Don't underestimate your brother's vocal projection."

Flop's response, its focus subtly off, was overheard by the woman walking alongside the slowly advancing cattle cart. A tall woman with the same hair color and closely resembling features as Flop. Her clothing boldly bared her shoulders and legs, and she had an eye-catching hairstyle, her voluminous hair split into many bunches. Such an appearance was distinctive enough on its own, but what drew the eye most was the pair of falchions sheathed at the small of her back. That they weren't mere props for show or intimidation was clear from the well-worn condition of the tools. Her name was Medium, the amicable O'Connell sibling who traveled together with her brother Flop.

The two of them were people Subaru's group had gotten to know in the checkpoint line, but they were also, one could say, benefactors who had helped Subaru and company clear the checkpoint when proof of identity and bribe money alike had been out of reach. Of course, it wasn't out of pure kindness that they'd cooperated with Subaru and the others. To begin with, it was Subaru who had set his sights on the O'Connell siblings as a means of getting past the checkpoint.

With no money and no connections, Subaru and his companions needed someone else's cooperation to get through the checkpoint. And so, Subaru had judged that Flop and his sister were people with whom there was room to negotiate. The reason Subaru had come to that conclusion was――

"Amazing, bro! So you saw through my whole plan from the start!"

"Naturally. If one can't read ahead, one can't win in trade. Our O'Connell Company runs on my brains and your brawn!"

"As expected of ya! I don't understand a word of what you're saying, though!"

"Ha ha ha ha, even so, my own sister seems to be enjoying life a great deal, and that's simply splendid!"

Puffing out their chests at each other, the O'Connell siblings laughed loudly together. Their trade was peddling. Strictly speaking, Flop handled the trading business as the merchant, while Medium was in charge of guarding her brother and the goods, both on the road and within cities.

――A merchant.

That was the condition Subaru had chosen when picking whom to rely on for breaking through the checkpoint.

"The experience of talking things over with Otto and Anastasia paid off here..."

Otto, one of his own people, and Anastasia, who'd shared a long journey with him――the latter not being the woman herself, but rather Echidna's performance impersonating her, though in terms of knowledge she must have been on par with the real thing. Having received such tutelage, mainly from those two, Subaru had a certain kind of trust in merchants. Namely, that even one operating across a national border, a merchant would listen to what someone had to say if presented with a benefit.

Literally throwing himself at it with the resolve to fail spectacularly if need be, launching an all-out charm offensive, Subaru had struck out with four groups of merchants before Subaru and the others had run into the O'Connell siblings. And then, sweet-talking the somewhat quirky pair, he'd magnificently secured their cooperation in getting past the checkpoint. As the bargaining material for that, what had strongly caught their interest was――

"Still, was giving up this carrier frame really okay as payment? I mean, sure, it's got some ingenuity to it, but the homemade feel is off the charts, isn't it?"

"Its rusticity is undeniable, I grant you. However, the fact that it's collapsible, and the mechanism devised for portability, caught my interest. It would surely be useful for hauling heavy loads too, wouldn't it?"

"...Well, if that works for you, Mr. Flop, then I suppose it's fine."

With a bit of hesitation, Subaru handed the folded carrier frame over to Medium. Taking it, Medium made a "hohh" sound as she looked it over closely, tilted her head, and then shoved it into the back of the cattle cart. It seemed she personally couldn't see any value in it. In truth, even Subaru himself wasn't sure exactly what part of it Flop had appreciated.

The condition for the O'Connell siblings' cooperation had been the handover of the carrier frame Subaru had built. Seeing the frame with Rem riding on it, Flop had been impressed, calling it a unique and interesting construction, and had promised that in exchange for it, he'd put in a word to get them through the checkpoint. Honestly, Subaru had braced himself for the possibility of being asked for the Witchbeast horn as bargaining material, so if anything, this condition felt almost anticlimactic to him.

"Once we got to the city, I was planning to rebuild it with better materials anyway, and worst case, there's also a chance Rem wouldn't even want to ride it anymore."

"It is true that I'd like to walk on my own feet as much as possible. When I'm riding the carrier frame, your stench drifts along on the wind toward me."

"Sorryyy..."

Leaning on her cane, Rem quietly poked at the matter of the Witch's Lingering Scent. Still, one had to give her credit for enduring it throughout the journey and only bringing it up after they'd reached their destination — that was rather compassionate of her. Watching this exchange between Subaru and Rem, Flop shrugged, saying "Now, now, that won't do." It was a gesture as if to say how regrettable it all was, but there was a reason behind that attitude.

"You two ought to get along better. A husband and wife are meant to support one another. That display just now, of mutual support in its truest form, was quite beautiful."

"Well, it was you who took the carrier frame off him, bro!"

"That's exactly it! What right do I have to say such a thing, I wonder!"

Pressing a hand to his forehead, Flop, and pressing a hand to her stomach, Medium, laughed loudly. Loosening his own cheeks along with the siblings' boisterous laughter, Subaru cast a glance sidelong at Rem's profile. Not laughing in the slightest, Rem had Rui clinging to one hand. To that profile of hers, Subaru hesitantly called out, "Um..."

"So, um, Rem? Are you...upset, by any chance?"

"Huh? Why would I be upset? Do you have some reason to think I would be?"

"No, it's just, even though it's just a cover story, I figured being called my wife might not sit well with you..."

Fingers pressed nervously together, Subaru cautiously gauged Rem's feelings. As far as Flop and the others knew, Subaru and Rem were understood to be a married couple. That had come about when, while negotiating for help getting past the checkpoint, the siblings had asked about the relationship between Subaru and the others, and he'd found himself at a loss for a snap answer. ――No, "at a loss" wasn't quite right. He'd had an answer prepared, but it had been doubted, leaving him with no choice.

"I can't believe our sibling-travelers cover story didn't get believed..."

"Mr. Flop and Ms. Medium are in the same situation, and the two of them closely resemble each other. Between me and you, and this child besides, it was rather a strained lie."

As a merchant, someone who survived in a world where lies and truth flew back and forth freely, Subaru had to admit that his skill simply hadn't been polished enough to deceive such a person. Though, in fairness, it was actually Medium who had seen through the sibling lie, loudly declaring, "You two don't look alike at all, bro!"

In any case, defeated by that keen eye, the epic that had branched off from Subaru's prepared sibling setup――a spectacular saga about journeying to a distant mountain from their hometown to dispose of a cursed ring――had been cancelled. The shock of that had also left Subaru at a loss when answering their question. What had saved him from that predicament was Rem's quick-witted explanation: "I'm his wife."

"The thing about me and Rem being married, and this one being a child of Rem's older sister we're looking after..."

"You're the one who told them I have an older sister. You said we were twins, so I find it hard to believe there'd be a child this big already, but... I'll turn a blind eye to that part."

"Uー?"

Being patted on the head by Rem, Rui gave a small, ticklish-looking growl. This seemingly harmless gesture and demeanor of Rui's also seemed to be one of the factors that had kept Flop and the others from being wary of Subaru's group. Setting aside Subaru's shifty-looking eyes, there was Rem with her bad leg and the defenseless Rui. This trio simply hadn't struck Flop and the others as villains up to no good within the city.

"Why..."

"Hm?"

"Why did you get so flustered for an answer back there? Normally you're rattling off all sorts of nonsense so easily."

"That sounds like a complaint disguised as a compliment..."

Still, it was true that Subaru's inadequacy had nearly put Rem in danger. If it hadn't been for Rem's quick thinking in the moment, there was a good chance Subaru and the others would still be stranded outside the gate, staring at the line.

"――――"

There was a limit to how far he could deflect with a joke. Not wanting to damage Rem's trust in him any further, Subaru gave a small "ahh" groan under Rem's steady gaze, and then said,

"I surprised even myself, but the instant I realized the lie had been seen through, my whole body just tensed up something fierce. Maybe it's some kind of trauma from getting my shoulder gouged at the camp after saying something careless."

"Ah..."

"I got worried that if I answered badly, Mr. Flop and the others might suddenly turn on us. Pathetic, I know, but that's what froze up my thinking. Sorry."

Sharing his pathetic self-analysis, Subaru honestly bowed his head to Rem. A clever head and a glib tongue――those were among the few weapons that set Natsuki Subaru apart from others. And yet, when his head and tongue stopped working, he became nothing more than a liability. He was entrusted with Rem's life. ――He couldn't afford a careless failure.

"――. I understand the circumstances. I don't think it could be helped."

"...Really? Even after a blunder like that?"

"Anyone's body tenses up after a painful experience. At least, that's what I believe."

Having fully expected to be coldly berated, Subaru was caught off guard by Rem's reaction. Of course, he already knew that deep down Rem was kind, capable of consideration for others. What surprised him was that she'd directed that same consideration toward him as well.

"...You're not mad?"

"I'm not mad. Just, next time, please consult with me beforehand. I don't think I'll always be able to come up with a good excuse either."

"Ah, yeah, got it. ...Are you really, truly not mad?"

"I am not mad."

"Really, really?"

"I already said I'm not mad, didn't I...!"

Out of excessive worry, he'd ended up earning Rem's anger after all. Clutching his head under Rem's sharp glare, Subaru shrank down and ultimately begged for forgiveness. With that little scene as a bridge, Subaru and the others had finally made their entrance into Guaral.

△▼△▼△▼△

"――Finally, I feel human again!"

So saying, Subaru threw himself onto the inn bed, stretching out his arms and legs to savor the freedom. A reasonably good-quality bed and sheets, a room with a decent sense of cleanliness――as far as inn grades went, this was upper-middle class, a room whose price matched accordingly. Given his position, he probably shouldn't be indulging too much, but he chalked it up as a necessary expense to buy safety.

"'If you're going to stay somewhere, you'd best cross cheap inns and sleeping outdoors off the list of options. If you had self-defense capability like Lady Emilia or Garfiel, that'd be one thing, but for someone like me or Mr. Natsuki, we're easy prey for anyone scheming mischief.'"

That had been one of the topics of conversation, if he recalled, back when he and Otto had been shooting the breeze in Otto's room at some point. Thinking calmly about it, it was an obvious precaution, but when funds were scarce, safety tended to get pushed down the priority list. Still, given Subaru and company's current situation, it was a necessary expense. Though――

"The room assignment was quite the ordeal, though..."

Muttering that, Subaru rolled onto his side on the bed and glared at the wall. Beyond that wall, in the next room over, Rem and Rui should be together. ――Having taken lodging in Guaral, the rooms had been divided by gender, but Subaru found the outcome unsatisfying. Even at this point, Subaru's suspicion and wariness toward Rui hadn't been resolved. He felt reluctant about leaving the two of them together where he couldn't see them. Still, the proposal for all three to share a single room was one Rem herself hadn't accepted. So he'd had no choice but to settle for this arrangement――

"Well now, husband dear. You're looking quite worn out, splayed out like that. Splendid, splendid!"

So saying, the one who'd entered the inn room belatedly was Flop. He tossed his hand luggage onto the other bed, separate from the one Subaru was sprawled on, and winked while smoothing down his long bangs. Composed as ever, Flop hadn't barged into the room uninvited or anything of the sort. This room had been rented with the plan of him and Subaru sharing it. Naturally, his sister Medium had headed to the room with Rem and the others.

"Ah, sorry. I owe you for everything, top to bottom..."

"Not at all. You defended yourself through wisdom. The teaching of 'Let the Empire's people be strong and valiant' doesn't only apply to those who stand on martial prowess."

"...Hearing you say that puts my mind at ease a bit."

Sitting cross-legged on the bed, Subaru gave a wry smile at Flop's words. Not just with getting through the checkpoint――the siblings Flop and Medium had been an enormous help to Subaru's group in the time since as well. The recommendation for a lodging where they could buy safety was one thing, but the most helpful of all had been the Witchbeast horn.

"If it'd just been us, there's no telling how badly we'd have been lowballed."

The Witchbeast horn Rui had been carrying on her back was already out of Subaru's group's hands. Following the advice from Kuna and the others, they'd had it exchanged for cash at a shop in Guaral. And in that price negotiation, Flop, who had for some reason tagged along, had helped out. The fierce price negotiation exchanged between him and the shop owner had been about as intense as a courtroom battle would feel to someone with no knowledge of law――that much Subaru could gather.

In any case, thanks to that, the Witchbeast horn had safely been transformed into Imperial gold coins. A correspondingly heavy pouch of gold coins――that was now Subaru's group's war chest within the Empire. Since future travel expenses would be covered by this, considerable caution was necessary.

"Can't have ourselves getting taken advantage of again like earlier, after all."

"Ha ha ha! Anything worth doing is worth trying with confidence, first and foremost. If you hold your chest high and your spine straight, the other party won't so easily try anything on you. Before long, even baseless confidence might become the real thing. And if it does, isn't that a bargain?"

"Hmm, hearing that from someone who actually practices it makes it pretty convincing..."

For better or worse, Flop was brimming with confidence. Depending on the circumstance, his frank way of speaking seemed like it could well invite unnecessary friction, but given that he'd made it this far in life doing things his way, that was proof enough that his approach worked. In fact, having been helped by that very way of his, Subaru found himself honestly admiring it here.

"But you carried your wife on your back on a long journey, didn't you? Why not rest well tonight and save what we discussed earlier for tomorrow?"

"That's...a tempting offer, but I'll pass. We need to reach our destination even a single day sooner, so I can't afford to waste time."

Tempted by the soft bed's allure, Subaru somehow managed to tear himself away from it. To Subaru, who rose from the bed as he said that, Flop puffed out his chest, declaring, "Splendid indeed!"

"That's the spirit. There are times you must move your weary feet for the sake of someone important to you. Just as I do for my sister, and as you do for your wife, husband dear."

"Hearing you put it that way is a bit ticklish! ...So, could I get you to show me the way?"

"Of course. It was I who proposed it in the first place, after all!"

Thumping his own chest, Flop readily took it upon himself. Receiving that reply of his, Subaru once more steeled himself and pried his spirit off the bed, then, accompanying Flop, headed for the room next door. Knocking on the door, he called for Rem inside the room.

"Rem, everything okay in there?"

"Yes, we're fine. Right now we're discussing which bed Rui should sleep in, mine or Ms. Medium's..."

"...I see."

"Just now, your face said you wanted to suggest having her sleep on the floor, didn't it."

"I didn't say it 'cause I figured you'd get mad..."

Given the danger Rui posed, sharing a bed with Medium wasn't something he could recommend either. Truthfully, the best option would be for Subaru himself to keep watch all night, but in his current physical condition, he'd likely fall asleep within a few hours of starting to keep watch. He had no choice but to trust in the fact that, over these past ten days, she hadn't done anything.

――Trusting an Archbishop of Sin was an absurdly foolish thought, he knew.

"Like I mentioned earlier, I'm heading out with Mr. Flop for a bit. I'll buy something for dinner outside, so let's eat together."

"Understood. ...Now that I think about it, there are so many people here, so there's no longer a need to rely solely on you, is there."

"Why are you suddenly bringing up something that's gonna make it harder for me to leave?!"

"...I wonder why. I just said what I thought, that's all."

Though reluctant to leave, drawn back by Rem's words, Subaru prioritized following through on his original intent. Waving to Medium, who was inside the room playfully having her hair pulled by Rui, he said,

"Ms. Medium, I'm counting on you for those two. If they give you any trouble, feel free to smack them without holding back."

"For real? My fists hurt like crazy, y'know?"

"Yeah, if it doesn't hurt, they won't learn."

Still, if anything were to happen, Rem would surely stop it beforehand. With Rui currently playing a part in keeping Rem's mental state stable, it was both frustrating and a relief. At the very least, unless he could acknowledge that she was fulfilling some kind of role, Subaru couldn't even draw out the tiniest shred of justification, even for himself, for bringing Rui along.

"Quite the difficult journey it seems, husband dear."

Leaving Rem and Rui at the inn, having stepped back out into the streets, Subaru had his shoulder tapped by Flop. Called a difficult journey, he almost wanted to collapse to his knees and say "You said it," but doing so would only put Flop in an awkward spot. Even without that, he and Medium were already being relied upon completely. He wanted to avoid piling any more worry and burden onto them beyond this.

"Even if 'Reliance on Others' is one of my hundred and eight special skills, there's a limit to everything."

"Now, now, so long as one doesn't become entirely a burden, I don't think there's anything wrong with relying on others. My sister and I travel as two, but we make up for what the other lacks. Otherwise, both she and I would likely die easily enough, each for our own respective weaknesses. That, too, is the Imperial way."

"The Imperial way..."

Waving his hand lightly, then using that same hand to smooth his bangs, Flop spoke. The phrase "Imperial way" that had leapt from his mouth weighed heavily on Subaru's chest. Probably, that was precisely the source of the shapeless dread Subaru feared――a difference in values. In that sense, Flop's thinking was easy for Subaru to understand, close to his own. And yet, it was fairly different from the impression of the Vollachian Empire he'd formed up to this point.

"Not everyone is suited to a martial way of life or existence. What matters is finding one's own accommodation within the framework one's been given."

"Finding an accommodation, is it."

"As I said before, when viewed as individuals, my sister and I are, frankly, beings with quite a lot of weaknesses. But when the two of us join forces, we hide those weaknesses and become something rather formidable. In fact, the reason my sister and I have survived to this day, one could say, is precisely because that was our winning strategy."

"――――"

"Remember this, husband dear. The reason I, my sister, you, and your wife have survived to this day is because we've won every fight we've been challenged to. ――Now then, aren't I an Imperial man too?"

Cheeks relaxed, Flop's profile seemed to be seeking praise. Gazing intently at that, Subaru felt as though he'd received an unexpected revelation. Making up for each other's shortcomings――that was no different from Subaru's own values. Though he had also thought that perhaps, in the Empire, thinking like Subaru's simply wouldn't hold up at all.

"So it's not all just hopeless stuff like that, huh."

"Even so, more often than not, most people's version of the 'Imperial way' tends to be about brute strength! This idea of mine is mocked as the buzzing of a coward's wings. It's by no means the common view."

Beside Subaru, who'd arrived at a certain kind of understanding, Flop cautioned him not to overestimate it. And yet, having said that much, he continued with "That said," and added,

"The notion that one can put a price on that kind of skill is also a valid one. Just as I'm about to introduce you to someone, husband dear."

"Got it, appreciate it."

Nodding, Subaru renewed his gratitude for Flop's proposal. That was, on top of getting through the checkpoint and recommending the inn, the purpose of their current outing――to secure the introduction of transportation and a bodyguard, something Subaru's group wanted in order to smooth the road ahead for their journey.

This time, just as Kuna and Holly had served as guards along the way to Guaral, protecting themselves from the dangers on the road would be difficult without a skilled bodyguard. There was also a limit to how far Subaru could keep carrying Rem on the frame while walking. Ideally, an Earth Dragon; if that proved difficult, then a Brave Ox or Large Canine――in any case, they needed a mount for travel. Both of those would need to be managed within their limited war chest.

"I'm really grateful Mr. Flop has some idea of who to ask..."

"Though calling it 'some idea' is a bit much — really it's just that I know of a tavern where people of that trade come and go. As for mounts, I can offer some advice there too. ――Yes, such as a Falo cart!"

"You're really pushing the Falo hard, huh..."

"It would hardly be an exaggeration to say I was raised on Falo milk. I'm quite the Falo partisan."

Laughing loudly, Flop launched into an enthusiastic speech on just how recommendable Falo were. Still, unless one were hauling a heavy load, the compatibility with Subaru's group, who wanted to travel long distances, wasn't especially good. Their speed was quite slow too, so a Falo would be a last resort.

"In any case, it's best to take whatever safety measures we can along the road. ...Just between us, things seem to be rather noisy in the imperial capital these days. There's no guarantee that won't spread this way like sparks."

"――The imperial capital is noisy."

Subaru's brow twitched up, but Flop, seeming not to notice his reaction, folded his arms and answered, "That's right."

"The Empire's the sort of place where embers are smoldering somewhere or other year-round, but there's concern that this particular ember might spread into a real blaze. I don't want to worry your wife, so I'm only telling you this."

"Appreciate the consideration. ...By the way, does that have anything to do with the emperor, by chance?"

"With His Excellency Vincent Vollachia?"

As though hearing something unexpected, Flop's eyes went wide. Then, promptly, he shook his head, saying, "No, no, no, no."

"I've never once heard talk of anything concerning His Excellency the Emperor himself. If nothing else, His Excellency has magnificently pacified the country up to now."

"But didn't you just say embers were smoldering?"

"That it's stayed at just embers is thanks to His Excellency's skill. His current Excellency ascended to the throne seven or eight years ago, but before that, the Empire was in far more turmoil."

"――――"

"This latest disturbance, too, His Excellency is personally directing. Before long, our homeland, with its embers ever smoldering, will surely return to normal."

"Huh?"

Flop remarked airily that a homeland with smoldering embers was simply home. But something about his words caught in Subaru's mind――the part about the emperor personally directing things.

"The trouble in the imperial capital — is the emperor trying to deal with it himself?"

"When it's in his own backyard...truly, in the deepest sense of 'backyard,' His Excellency would move, wouldn't he. A proclamation was issued that anyone trying to exploit this opportunity would be shown no mercy."

"The emperor's proclamation..."

There was no sense of a lie in Flop's matter-of-fact explanation. To begin with, there was simply no reason for Flop to be trying to deceive Subaru about the trouble in the imperial capital, so at the very least, he must be speaking what was fact as far as he knew. And yet, if that were so, something snagged at Subaru.

"If that guy Abel really is the emperor, then it was him who issued the proclamation...?"

――No, the timing felt off, somehow. Of course, issuing an imperial proclamation might well be something a minister could do in an "it was my secretary's doing" sort of fashion, even without the emperor himself present. For that matter, if Abel's account was to be believed, the people who remained in the emperor's castle were none other than the very people who'd driven the emperor out. They'd trample all over respect for the emperor and make use of his name without hesitation. Still――

"I'll keep the possibility that Abel's just some raving lunatic in mind too, just in case."

Believing everything he saw exactly as it appeared wasn't a way to survive on a raging sea. This wasn't a raging sea, nor was it always raging, but that was roughly the mindset with which he wanted to approach the Empire――a statement of his own resolve. Still, having personally confronted and spoken with Abel, there was a part of him that thought that wasn't the kind of presence a mere self-proclaimed emperor could project――

"――Husband dear, that stern face won't do."

As if concerned about Subaru's state, Flop, circling around to stand in front of him, said that. Before Subaru, who stopped in his tracks without meaning to, Flop pointed at his own brow, between the eyes, and said,

"Fortune doesn't visit those without a smile and composure. From here, aren't you looking for traveling companions for your journey? Then you must seek a good match."

"That's..."

"So then, erase the crease between your brows, loosen the corners of your mouth, and project composure. That's the mark of a capable man."

Poking and rubbing at his own furrowed brow with a finger, Flop then loosened his own cheeks with both hands. Witnessing that gesture of his firsthand, Subaru held his breath. Then, as instructed, he slowly worked at his own brow and cheeks with his fingers.

"Ah, right, I forgot. My eyes already look shifty enough as it is, so I should at least try to soften the overall vibe."

"My apologies! There's nothing even my power can do about your eyes!"

"Don't take that so seriously, it's fine! It's just something I got from my parents, so honestly it doesn't bother me as much as you'd think!"

Having been lamented over dramatically, Subaru responded in kind dramatically, but even that was surely part of Flop's consideration, meant to loosen the tension knotted in Subaru. Trading such banter, Subaru and Flop arrived at the street they were aiming for. What Flop indicated with his hand was a hideaway-style tavern at the end of an alley.

"If this were a large group, we ought to hire guards by the numbers, but with it being just you, your wife, and your little niece, three people total, it's not exactly a large household, is it. Regrettably, even I and my sister can't accompany you all the way outside Guaral!"

"But I feel like if we handled it right, Ms. Medium might actually come along with us..."

"If my sister gets poached away, with no one to make up for my weaknesses, I'll have no choice but to simply die!"

Weighing trust and safety against each other, Flop and Medium honestly weren't a bad option as a choice, but he couldn't afford to pile up any more debt and inconvenience than he already had.

"No, worst case, we could take the two of them and make a clean escape straight to Lugunica. And then have the two of them achieve the merchant success story Otto never managed to..."

"Hey now, husband dear? Are you all right?"

Before Subaru, lost in thought, Flop waved a hand, checking on his condition. At his reaction, Subaru said "Sorry" and scratched his head. It had been a spur-of-the-moment idea, but it was starting to feel like not such a bad proposal after all. As much as it pained him, given how far Flop had already guided him――

"Hey, Mr. Flop, there's actually something I wanted to ask you and Ms. Medium――"

――he was about to say, at that very moment.

"――――"

――Somewhere, there came the faint echo of something hard striking.

△▼△▼△▼△

"――Husband dear, that stern face won't do."

"...Huh?"

Suddenly, as if he'd blinked, his field of vision switched, and Subaru's consciousness gained a blank void. Before Subaru, Flop was pointing at his own brow. As if lecturing Subaru, who had stopped in his tracks, he poked and rubbed at his own furrowed brow with a finger, saying,

"Fortune doesn't visit those without a smile and composure. From here, aren't you looking for traveling companions for your journey? Then you must seek a good match."

"――――"

"So then, erase the crease between your brows, loosen the corners of your mouth, and project composure. That's the mark of a capable man."

As he spoke, Flop began working his cheeks loose with both hands, kneading them. That gesture of his――Subaru had seen it before. More than seen it before――after all, it had been mere minutes ago. He was repeating the exact same conversation they'd had just minutes before.

"Hey, hey, hey, hold on a second..."

As if ambushed, a cold sweat broke out on him, and Subaru covered his own face with his hands. Seeing that, Flop raised his voice, saying "Don't hide it, loosen it!" but Subaru no longer had the composure to respond to those words.

Looking around, the surrounding street looked familiar too. It was his first time in this city, and he wasn't familiar with Guaral. Even so, his memory wasn't so poor that he'd forget a scene he'd just witnessed moments ago. In other words――

"――Did I just 'Return by Death'?"

Amid this seemingly mundane situation, without even understanding what had happened to him, Subaru muttered that, his cheeks tensing for a reason entirely different from before.
