Arc 9 Chapter 25: "Emilia Was Enraged"

"I'm sooo angry right now, so even if it's you, Al, I won't go easy on you."

So saying, the Witch of Ice — who had turned the area around the Prison Tower into a snow-covered landscape — fixed him with a stern look. At the anger lit in those violet-indigo eyes, Aldebaran felt a dryness catch at the back of his throat.

That too, even in the Empire — the way she'd stretched her long, slim limbs as far as they'd go, beautiful — That was why it was the first time.

"—"

—for Emilia's eyes to be filled with nothing but anger, glaring so fiercely at someone. Much less had he ever imagined a moment where that anger, in full, would be turned on him.

"...Guess saying I never imagined it is a bit too convenient of me."

He was an enemy of the world — having hijacked the Dragonhusk of the Divine Dragon Volcanica and set the Witch of Envy in motion for the sake of tying down the Sword Saint, Reinhard. Even before that, the moment he'd sealed away Natsuki Subaru, there had already been no shortage of people who — world-scale threat or not — had become Aldebaran's enemies. Emilia was chief among them. This confrontation was the price of a decision that could only be called inevitable.

"Huh, that's weird, isn't it? Seems like you've gone and made her real angry, old man. That lady's scary once she's mad, y'know that?"

"Shut it."

"Ohh, scary, scary."

Slung over Aldebaran's shoulder, unable to move, Roy laughed mockingly. The circumstances of Roy's capture had been the battle against Natsuki Subaru and the others at the Pleiades Watchtower — naturally, Emilia had been present there too. It made sense that he'd know her. Even before that, Roy had devoured many "names" and "memories" in Priestella as well, so it was no wonder if some of those included someone who knew Emilia. So what mattered right now wasn't Roy's sticky flippancy, but —

"—. Yo, fancy meeting you here of all places, kid —"

Struggling to choose his opening line, Aldebaran opened his mouth. But the effort he'd put into picking that first word turned out, unfortunately, to be meaningless. Because —

"—!"

In the middle of Aldebaran's opening remark, meant to feel out her reaction, Emilia lunged forward. The thin layer of snow beneath her feet burst outward as she kicked off the ground, her silver hair dancing beautifully. In his snow-dusted field of vision, the instant her dancing silver hair seemed to shimmer —

"Hyah!!"

— a merciless punch from an icy gauntlet had already driven straight into Aldebaran's solar plexus.

—Time rewinds slightly, to just before that blow to the solar plexus.

"Subaru and the others —?!"

"That's what I heard from one of my own people. Says everyone at the tower got taken down."

The one who'd brought her this news was Felt, who happened to be staying in the royal capital at the time. It was their first reunion since the water-gate city of Priestella, but there was no time to catch up on old times before the report came. To begin with, Emilia's reason for visiting the royal capital hadn't been a warm one at all — she'd come to report to the royal castle the fact that Priscilla Barielle had lost her life in the Vollachian Empire.

Bringing back news that would have a huge impact on the Royal Selection, and that would grieve so many who'd known and loved Priscilla, weighed terribly on Emilia's heart. The fact Felt had just conveyed was a devastating blow on top of that for Emilia.

"Al..."

Emilia couldn't bring herself to say something so insensitive as "why would you do that." If Al had gone and done something now, there was no way Priscilla's death wasn't connected to it. Al's exhaustion after losing someone so precious to him had been almost unbearable to watch, and that was precisely why Emilia and the others had listened to Subaru's plea and sent them off to the Pleiades Watchtower. It was because they'd believed that there, Al might recover — no, that he might at least find a chance to start looking forward again. And yet —

"—Lady Felt, might I ask you to tell us a bit more in detail?"

Beside Emilia, who bit her lip and clenched her fists tight, it was Otto — who'd come to the royal capital with her — who spoke up to Felt like that. In contrast to Emilia, her heart in a fluster over news she didn't want to believe, Otto's profile as he gazed at Felt was calm, his voice composed too. But Emilia knew — that Otto acting like this was Otto at his angriest. Otto's manner of anger was well known to everyone in the Emilia Camp, and Felt seemed to feel the quiet pressure of it too. "Guess so," she said, folding her arms.

"Word is the knight and the little spirit girl got themselves caught by that helmet bastard. Dunno the method, but sounds like he's not planning to kill 'em."

"No intent to kill? ...Do you know about the others at the tower?"

"Flam, the one who got word to me, is fine. Meili and Emilia's maid are safe too. But my teacher and that Garfiel guy got worked over pretty bad. Doesn't look like they lost their lives either, though."

"—. I see. Thank you."

"Ain't something you gotta thank me for. Knowing and not telling you would just leave a bad taste in my mouth, that's all."

After laying it all out without holding back, Felt looked over at Emilia. Emilia, who still hadn't managed to unclench her fists, caught her breath at the heat in Felt's gaze. Then, to Emilia, her cheeks stiffening, Felt continued.

"Our plan's set. From here, we're using Reinhard to head straight back to the territory. Gotta talk it over with Gramps Rom — there's a mountain of stuff we need to do."

"The territory — that's the Astrea domain, right? That's close to the Augria Sand Dunes too, so we could come with you, Felt —"

"—No, let's not do that, Lady Emilia."

"Eh?"

Otto's calm voice held back Emilia, who'd been leaning forward, her feelings racing ahead of her. But not understanding why she'd been stopped, Emilia was confused. After all, right now, they needed to rush straight to the tower where Subaru and the others were in such terrible trouble.

"Is it maybe because it'd be harder on Reinhard if there were more of us? That's... um, it'd be hard for me to get sooo much lighter starting now, but I'll try...!"

"It ain't like adding you and the Zodda Bug guy would change his burden all that much anyway. But that ain't what this is about, is it?"

"Setting aside how Lady Felt sees me... yes."

As Felt and Otto nodded at each other, question marks flew about above Emilia's head. But Emilia never doubted that the reasoning behind these two, thinking she couldn't quite keep up with, was something they'd thought through carefully and that it was right.

"Felt, be careful. Tell Reinhard too. And, Al is..."

"Can't make you any promises about the helmet bastard, though. Sorry 'bout that."

"—. No, I'm glad you're telling me straight that you can't promise that."

Their reunion had been short, and nothing about what they'd discussed had been happy. Even so, Emilia hugged Felt at the end, and sent her off from the royal castle despite the odd face Felt made at that. After this, Felt and the others were to fly to the Astrea domain by Reinhard's hand, where they had all sorts of things — many things — that needed doing, or so the plan went.

"Subaru..."

After Felt left like a storm, Emilia hung her head in the room left behind in the royal manor. Even now, the strength wouldn't drain from her clenched fists. By Felt's account, everyone they'd sent off to the Pleiades Watchtower had been dealt a painful blow. But among them all, the one Emilia was most worried about, above anyone, was her own knight, Subaru.

Even in the Empire, he'd kept working so hard for Rem and Spica's sake, fought alongside Abel, shrunk and grown by turns, and still hadn't been able to save Priscilla. To punish himself for that, he'd had so many friends in the Empire hit him, and it had been almost unbearable to watch. And now, that same Subaru had had the kindness he'd thought up for Al's sake betrayed —

"...I..."

At the strange sensation simmering and kindling deep in her chest, Emilia became aware of the heat in her own heart. Not quite sadness, not quite an ache in her chest, it was a feeling Emilia rarely held, and precisely for that reason it left her thoroughly bewildered. The last time she'd felt her chest, the backs of her eyes, the very center of her heart boil and roil like this had been that time — back in Priestella, when she'd faced off against Regulus. At Regulus, who'd bound Sylphy and the other brides' hearts for such utterly selfish reasons, Emilia had truly, genuinely lost her temper. And now, an anger the same as that time, or greater.

"Lady Emilia, thank you for hearing me out — it's a relief."

"Otto..."

Realizing the passion inside her had grown too large for her to contain, Emilia intuitively understood that Otto, with his quiet expression, felt the very same. Of course he would. Otto too was one of those who'd gone all the way to the Empire together with Emilia and the others, worried sick about Subaru. Something like this, there was just no way —

"Hyah!"

With a sharp smack, Emilia slapped both her own cheeks with her hands. It was so sudden that it startled Otto into an "Eh, Lady Emilia?" Feeling a bit sorry about that, Emilia lowered her hands from her stinging cheeks.

"My head was all in a whirl just now, and I didn't think I could talk properly. But this'll fix it! ...Well, saying I'm fine is a lie, but I can talk properly now."

"...I see. Then I should too —"

"Eek!"

As if mimicking the nodding Emilia, Otto smacked his own cheeks too. He put so much force into it that the sound was loud enough to make it seem like his nose might bleed, and it startled Emilia badly. But Otto didn't get a nosebleed, and with cheeks surely just as red as Emilia's, he said,

"—. I'll explain why we didn't go with Lady Felt. It's because there'd have been no point in us going... and because we need to prepare for the worst-case scenario."

"...Go on, tell me the rest."

With his hands still pressed to his cheeks, Otto continued, and Emilia didn't interrupt his explanation. She had questions. There were things she wanted to say. But first, she'd hear the other person out completely. Whatever Emilia might think of, Otto surely had a proper answer prepared for it already.

"If Reinhard-san is standing at the front line, then it doesn't matter how many people you send in terms of military strength. Lady Felt surely understands that too. And yet she still went back to the territory, which means —"

"—That's the preparation?"

"Exactly. Just think about it. Setting aside Natsuki-san and the others, if someone attacks people connected to Lady Felt who are at the tower, it's self-evident that Reinhard-san will step in. And yet Al-san... that man went through with it anyway."

At last, Emilia was starting to understand what Otto was getting at. Reinhard was incredibly strong. Probably, among everyone Emilia knew — no, including people she didn't know, Reinhard was the strongest in the world. Puck had said so too, so there was no mistake. It would be strange for Al to go in with no preparation at all, knowing full well he'd clash with Reinhard. So Al surely had prepared something, and even Reinhard might be in danger.

"So Felt went back to prepare because she's worried about Reinhard. ...But then what about us? If it's preparation, couldn't we come along with Felt and the others too...?"

"We're the preparation that comes after Reinhard-san — the preparation for Lady Felt and the others."

"—. I see. If Al knows Reinhard's coming and is preparing for that, then preparing preparations for the preparation of that preparation... um —"

"That's right. Of course, if we start saying 'preparation for the preparation for the preparation,' there's no end to it. The reason that man resorted to such a reckless act stems from what happened in the Empire... I don't think he could have worked out that thorough a scheme, or spent that much time preparing. He should only have had as much time as the rest of us."

"...Mm, that's... right."

Thanks to his careful, detailed explanation, Emilia's understanding finally caught up with Otto and Felt's. While admiring the depth of his thinking, Emilia couldn't help but feel a keenly painful emotion at how Otto had switched to calling Al "that man." Otto's sense of camaraderie ran very strong. Surely the strongest among all of them. As reassuring as that was, it also forced him into a very painful kind of resignation. But even so —

"...I don't know how I'm supposed to feel either, about Al."

There were reasons. Of course there were. But even with reasons, there were things that couldn't be forgiven. She was worried about Subaru. Was Beatrice all right? She'd heard Garfiel was hurt. Petra and Meili, too, must be filled with anxiety. Even Patrasche, whom Subaru loved so much, might be crying. — A blaze kindled, hot, deep in her chest.

"—It's my failure."

Right as that fire was kindling in Emilia's heart, Otto suddenly murmured this. Not having the faintest idea what he meant, Emilia's eyes went wide and she found herself at a loss for words. Otto had covered his face with the hand he'd had pressed to his cheek, and was staring up at the ceiling. With him taller than her doing that, Emilia couldn't see his face. Still, she could tell that the tremor in his voice wasn't simply from anger.

"I thought Natsuki-san was too invested. I forced myself to accept that if he acted for the sake of what the late Priscilla-sama left behind, it might distract him and even help him find some relief. I tried to convince myself that if Beatrice-chan, who'd been apart from him, was by his side, he'd be fine. That Petra-chan would gamely take care of the caretaking and follow-up too, I told myself."

"Otto..."

"Above all, I left it all to Garfiel alone. Something all of us should have done together, and I made that straightforward, honest, sensitive Garfiel carry it by himself."

"Otto!"

The instant the tremor in his voice was on the verge of a full breakdown, Emilia grabbed both of Otto's arms, made him lower his hands and his face, and faced him head-on. And then, she saw. —Otto's bluish eyes, wet with tears the like of which she'd never seen from him before.

"Dammit... why am I always so thoughtless like this...!"

"—"

"Hands that can't reach, feet that can't make it in time — what's lacking, you can only make up for with your head. Even though I know that, even though I go around acting so clever about it, at the times that matter most, I always —"

"—!"

She didn't want to hear any more of Otto's voice blaming himself with that face on the verge of tears. So Emilia pulled the arms she'd been holding toward herself and drew Otto's head to her chest. She held his head there, forcing him into silence. Otto seemed so shocked by what Emilia had done that he froze without a sound. In that instant —

"Don't talk about yourself like that! Otto's been trying so hard! All of us know that. And because we know it, I want to properly listen to what Otto has to say!"

"E-Emilia-sama..."

"I know! Even if I say this, you already understand that too, don't you, Otto? But I only have my own words, and I can only say what I've thought myself, so all I can do is at least say it with my whole heart put into it. So —"

"—"

"Nothing's over yet. We can still try sooo much harder!"

Still holding Otto's head tight to her chest, Emilia declared this firmly. It was as if she were telling herself the same thing, but at the same time, it was words she truly believed. It was nothing other than what her own knight, Natsuki Subaru, had made her believe time and time again. It was the greatest pride and trust that Emilia had built up together with Subaru.

"We're just sooo bad at giving up, after all."

It just wasn't something they were good at — giving up, clutching their heads, curling into a ball, and running away, telling themselves it was already hopeless. Because they were the kind of people who couldn't do that, all that was left was to keep going without giving up. Perhaps Emilia's plea had gotten through. Otto's body, which had been stiff until then, suddenly went slack, and then —

"...Please let go of my arms. Natsuki-san will kill me."

"No, nobody's going to die! That's exactly why we're —"

"I understand! I understand, so please, let go of me!"

To Otto, thrashing about, Emilia reluctantly loosened her arms. As she stared hard at him to see if he'd really recovered, Otto wiped the sweat from his brow with a handkerchief and sighed. An exceptionally big, deep sigh, at that.

"This isn't really the time, but I just remembered something."

"Remembered? Remembered what?"

"—The reason I lend my strength to Natsuki-san and to you, Lady Emilia."

Wiping his eyes with the handkerchief to erase any trace of tears, Otto said this with a calm expression. It was different from the deeply angry face of moments before — now his face looked brave, as if he'd resolved something.

"Our opponent has abducted Natsuki-san and Beatrice-chan. There must be a reason behind that. Normally, the aim of an abduction is to threaten someone connected to the victims, or else the target is the person themselves..."

"So it means they have business with us, or with Subaru."

"Yes. If the Divine Dragon is truly involved, then this is a matter for the entire kingdom. Let's fulfill our own role here in the royal capital."

"—. Yes, let's do that. We'd better get moving briskly."

Listening to the outlook Otto laid out for the road ahead, Emilia desperately held back an impulse.

"—"

It was an impulse that had arrived late — the urge to dash off to Subaru and the others' side right this instant. It was a feeling, strong, so strong, that tried to shove aside all the proper reasoning Otto had worked out for her, things like Reinhard being there, or Felt preparing. She wanted to touch him. She wanted to gaze at him. She wanted to hear his voice. When he was hurt and surely about to hang his head, she wanted to be there beside him.

"—Subaru."

Emilia still didn't know the name of this feeling scorching her chest — a feeling separate from anger.

"Hyah!!"

Charging straight in, delivering an all-out blow to Al's solar plexus without hearing him out first — all of this was on Otto's instructions, the one who'd sent Emilia here.

"Please hold back the urge to hear the other side's excuses or circumstances. There'll be plenty of time for all that once you've made sure he can't turn his hand against you."

Otto, who'd pushed Emilia to dash off, had used his Divine Protection of the Spoken Word to its absolute limit to locate Al and the others, and his face — already fair to begin with — had gone pale as a sick man's. She didn't want him pushing himself recklessly. But when it was time for recklessness, there was nothing to do but be reckless. Emilia herself hadn't lived a life restrained enough to stop him from that. So, to bring that recklessness to an end even a little sooner, Emilia was giving it her all.

"With this —"

Just as she was about to say "how's that," Emilia felt something off in the sensation from her ice-hardened fist. Her fist should have struck Al's bare body, but what came back was a rebound like she'd struck a hard, sturdy wall —

"That's sudden...!"

Al, forcing the words out through gritted teeth, had his torso protected by a suit of stone armor. It had been the preemptive strike Otto told her to land, and it had failed. But this was no time to mope over it. If one blow hadn't gotten through, she'd just try a second, a third.

"Not even gonna hear me out, huh. That's cold."

"That's — that's rich, coming from you, Al — mmph."

"Mmph?"

"I promised — not to talk —!"

As Al, taking Emilia's punch on his stone armor and getting knocked backward, said something like that, Emilia — forcing her own mouth shut partway through — swung a hammer of ice she'd formed in both hands in a horizontal sweep. The one-armed Al had a small companion slung over his right shoulder. Thinking it a bit unfair to exploit that handicap, Emilia nonetheless deliberately drove her blow into Al's left side. A full-force swing like that would hurt terribly if it landed. But that wasn't all.

"Tch!"

Al blocked that unfair blow of Emilia's with his left arm — a stone arm he'd conjured with magic. But even having blocked it, Al clicked his tongue and immediately shattered and discarded the left arm he'd just made. The reason was that ice had begun spreading from the point of impact, and it had nearly reached Al's own body.

"Setting aside the matter of feelings, to rescue Natsuki-san and the others, we need to take that man alive. On that point, your fighting style, Lady Emilia, is well suited. That said —"

"—I promised not to be reckless like Subaru!"

With the exchange and the promise she'd made with Otto before setting off held close to her heart, Emilia conjured a new pair of ice dual blades. Emilia's Iceblade Arts had gained yet another variation in how they could be used. The ice weapons she created froze whatever they struck, robbing both body and weapon of their freedom of movement. It took a bit more mana to use than making an ordinary weapon, but as long as she wasn't fighting a great many battles, that was within the margin of error. To stop an opponent without taking their life — this was the new possibility in martial technique that Emilia had wished for.

"Ahahaha! Old man, aren't the odds lookin' pretty bad for you?"

"Shut up!"

Al, having tossed away his half-frozen conjured arm, snapped this at the companion he was carrying, who kept mocking him. Confirming with her own eyes that the person Al kept carrying, even like this, was Roy Alphard — the one who'd been held captive in the Prison Tower — Emilia bit her lip. Roy was one of the Sin Archbishops that Emilia and the others had worked so hard to capture at the Pleiades Watchtower, in cooperation with Anastasia and her people. Julius in particular had worked hardest of all, but Emilia thought very well of both his decision not to take Roy's life, and Anastasia's courage in accepting that decision.

"And yet, here you both are, so disappointing...!"

Whatever his purpose, Emilia would stop Al right here. And along with him, she'd hold back Roy, whom Al had brought along, too.

"Ain't got any reason to go along with that plan of yours."

Right after saying that with such spirit, the ground beneath Al's feet suddenly heaved upward, launching the two of them into the sky in one motion. It looked like a move to put distance between himself and Emilia and flee the scene as-is. Caught off guard for an instant, even if Emilia stretched her ice out now, she wouldn't be able to catch up to him there. But —

"—Icicle Line."

The barrier of ice and snow she'd spread all around the Prison Tower wouldn't allow that escape. "Guoh?!" A scream rang out in the sky, blocked by a canopy of ice that had spread out to completely cover the surrounding area, and Al's escape attempt ended in failure. As Al, having struck his head on the ceiling of ice, came falling back down upside-down, Emilia moved into position beneath his landing point and struck at the airborne Al with her twin ice blades —

"—?!"

The twin blades were knocked back by a hard sensation, and Emilia's eyes went wide as she caught her breath. If Al had simply blocked it again with a conjured arm, like before, she wouldn't have been this surprised. Al had indeed made a new arm, but that wasn't all he'd made. —What he'd conjured was a pair of stone dual blades.

"Guess you could call it Earthblade Arts."

"Copycat...!"

"Natsuki Subaru's the one who thought this up, right? Then I've got just as much right to use it."

Before the wide-eyed Emilia, Al spun with the recoil from the clash of ice and stone blades, put distance between them, and landed again on the snowy ground. Around Al, stone weapons began materializing one after another with a clattering sound. Emilia braced herself as she realized Al could use earth magic to do the same thing as her own Iceblade Arts. Al, with a stone-made arm and stone-made weapons in hand — on his shoulder, where Roy should have still been, there was no sign of him.

"No way..."

"Ah, if I could say I let him go in all that confusion earlier, that'd sound cool, but it ain't like that. He was just in the way, so I dumped him. Over there."

To the startled Emilia, whose shoulders jumped, Al shrugged and jerked his chin. What Al was pointing to was the pillar of earth that had heaved up earlier — Roy was pinned upside down partway up it. Pinned to the pillar with a blanket of earth over him, Roy sneered at Emilia's gaze.

"Do your best, lady. If you don't win, we've got no choice but to keep doing whatever that old man says. Huh? But wait, even if you win, we go right back to the Prison Tower... hah, guess it doesn't matter who wins, huh?"

"...If you can reflect on it while you're awake, then do that — stay awake and reflect."

Answering Roy, who kept staring at her, like that, Emilia felt relieved she hadn't let him get away, and turned back to face Al once more. It was very dangerous that Roy's magical seal, freezing him solid, had come undone, but leaving him frozen through time itself would mean he could never reflect on anything either. At the very least, preparing an environment he could absolutely never escape from and making him reflect while awake there was Emilia's compromise.

"All of that, every bit of it, comes after things here are properly settled."

"Sounds fine to me, but... what, you feeling like talking to me now?"

"Ah! ...From here on. From here on, I'm not talking anymore."

"Cold. I figured if I just talked to you, kid, you'd understand — whoa!"

To Al, still wanting to keep talking, Emilia sent a spike of ice flying as a declaration of intent. As Al leapt sideways to dodge it, Emilia circled around to his flank and drove a spear of ice into his side. The tip of the ice spear was caught by the blade of a stone sword, and both shattered apart simultaneously. Instantly, Emilia formed her next ice rapier, and Al formed his next stone sword in turn, and the two began exchanging blows, a chain of battle where weapons shattered and were remade, remade and shattered.

"Nice, nice, real nice, ain't it nice, might be nice, isn't it nice, sure is nice — and it's exactly because it feels so nice that— Gorge! Guzzle!"

Watching the clash of ice and stone blades between Emilia and Al, Roy's voice could be heard, smacking his lips. Deliberately ignoring that, Emilia unleashed a series of sharp attacks, and felt something off about how precariously Al dodged them, grazing past what she was certain had landed, time after time. They seemed like they should land but never quite did. It felt somehow like kicking at shapeless water. Above all —

"—Don't much wanna compliment it, but that's a well-made technique. Real mutual love there, ain't it. Envious, kid."

At that outrageous remark — Emilia was enraged. "He resolved that the wicked and tyrannical king must be done away with, without fail." — recalling that stock phrase Subaru had once told her was the customary formula for whenever someone got angry, Emilia was enraged. She hadn't wanted Al, of all people, to talk about her and Subaru like that. And what made Emilia even angrier was that Al hadn't even meant it seriously — he'd said it purely to make her angry. And besides —

"I've kind of thought this for a while now, but why do you always look so lonely whenever you talk to me, Al?"

"—. Thought you said you weren't talkin' to me..."

"Don't bring that up only when it's convenient for you! And one more thing!"

Raising her voice, Emilia swung down a great ice sword she'd drawn back overhead, and Al caught it with his stone sword braced above his head — and just then, someone latched onto both of Al's legs and his waist. Al caught his breath — the one who'd grappled onto his body was the owner of icy arms: an Ice Soldier. At the presence of the Ice Soldier, modeled after Subaru, that Emilia relied on, Al gritted his teeth. "This is the worst...!" There, Emilia raised her leg, shod in an ice boot —

"—When you're talking to me, look me in the eye properly!!"

This time, her all-out kick broke straight through the stone defense and slammed home into Al.

"—"

Felt, sharp-eyed, didn't miss the faint twitch of Yae's eyebrow. It wasn't that she had any certainty about it. It was just that, in this pathetic situation where she could do nothing at all, she'd decided that a little more indignity hardly mattered, and resolved to cling to every scrap of hope she could grasp. One such hope: recalling the time before when she'd been "made to steel herself for death," she held out hope that something insignificant might be listening in on her and Yae's conversation.

"Quiet, please."

Opening one of her usually narrowed eyes, Yae stepped toward the door of the room. The unquestionable weight behind those words carried enough force to make Felt believe that if she moved carelessly, the thread around her neck would carry out its punishment. So, leaving Felt, unable to move, right where she was, Yae reached her hand out to open the door — and in that instant, a sharp swordpoint pierced silently through the door and lunged mercilessly at Yae's throat.

"—!"

Felt gasped — in her field of view, Yae's pale throat was pierced by the sword — no, it wasn't. The blade's tip had stopped right at the very last instant, just before tearing into Yae's throat. It wasn't that their attacker had stopped. Yae had barely managed to block it by wrapping thread around the blade's tip. It was truly a hair's-breadth escape from certain death, but Yae's crisis wasn't over there. —A second sword, different from the one she'd stopped, flashed once, and the door was cut down, slashes of light running wild.

"—"

Against that surging storm of blades, Yae leapt back a great distance while lashing out with her threads. Sword on one side, thread on the other — and yet the clash between the two weapons rang through the room with a sharp sound like steel striking steel and scattering sparks, the flash of blades searing the eyes of Felt, who could only watch. Skillfully working both hands, Yae unleashed a barrage of thread strikes that filled the room, tearing apart its lavish furnishings. But her opponent put on an overwhelming display of swordsmanship with a blade in each hand, cutting straight through that onslaught head-on, and forcibly swept aside the threads trying to entangle his swords —

"Dyaaah!!"

"—Guh."

A front kick shot out, driving straight into Yae's slender waist. Twisting her body on reflex, Yae was sent flying in a half-spin, planted her feet against the wall, landed on the floor, and pressed a hand to her stomach. Then, she fixed her gaze straight on the intruder who'd done this.

"Kicking a lady in the stomach right out of nowhere — what a merciless guest you are~"

"—Is that necessary? Mercy, that is, for a Shinobi such as yourself."

"Isn't that discrimination against Shinobi? That's not something a gentleman would do, you know?"

"Unfortunately, I have no memory of ever being worthy of the appraisal 'gentleman.' No matter how far I go, I, whose only skill is swinging a sword, can be nothing but a swordsman."

So saying, the figure shook the threads free from the swords in both his hands and turned quiet blue eyes on Yae — at that self-assessment, Felt muttered under her breath, "Don't talk nonsense." Whether he was a gentleman or not, sure, she didn't know. But "swordsman" alone wasn't a fitting enough evaluation either. Felt knew that plenty of people wouldn't stand for this man being described as merely that. Because the man who called himself a mere swordsman was none other than the "Sword Demon" who had once triumphed at the very summit of the sword —

"—Wilhelm Trias. Hearing that the kingdom faced a crisis grave enough to bring it to its knees, I have come, for whatever little good my strength may do."

Listening to that self-introduction, Felt retreated to the corner of the room, letting out a sigh. Feeling a deeply complicated mix of emotions at this unexpected reinforcement —

"...Guy really doesn't pick and choose his methods when he's serious, does he, that Zodda Bug fella. ...What in the world is going on over at Emilia's place."

—so she muttered, half exasperated, half impressed.
