Arc 7 Chapter 26: "The Dancing Round Table Conference"

"—Hey, c'mon? Anybody listening? I'm seriously about to bite it here!"

The tense atmosphere shivered under the idiotic voice, but that didn't loosen the strain any.
The taut standoff between Abel and Priscilla was still ongoing, and Subaru, caught at the edge of their line of sight, found himself pinned in place, unable to move.

"――――"

To move carelessly would be to earn the displeasure of Priscilla, mistress of the half-wrecked great hall. Fearing that, even pointing out that Al was on the verge of falling to his death felt like something to hesitate over. To set this situation moving, it would take the next word from either Abel or Priscilla—

"—Don't move."

But it was neither of them who set things moving.
The sudden voice came from the direction of the staircase connecting the great hall to the floor below. Glancing that way, Subaru saw a newly arrived figure nocking an arrow to a bow, eyes fixed on them.

Standing there, her sharp eyes burning with fierce hostility, was Taritta.

Acting on Abel and Mizelda's instructions, she had left the city hall before the assault on Arakiya, and now she'd returned.
Naturally, seeing the upper floors of the city hall blown away, Taritta would have realized an enemy attack had taken place. The problem was that she had no grasp of the circumstances. Looking at Priscilla standing over Subaru and Abel amid such a tense atmosphere, there was no room for doubt that Priscilla must be the one who'd assaulted the city hall.

"Move, and I'll put an arrow through that head of yours."

"Oh? Through my head? And this from one who arrived too late to save her own comrades from their predicament?"

"—!"

Taritta's cheek stiffened at the needless provocation, and Priscilla turned toward her with unhurried grace. Her bearing was so utterly composed that the threat to shoot if she moved was rendered moot before it had even settled — if anything, Priscilla looked ready to stride straight over to her.

"That's far enough."

But Abel halted Priscilla's advance before it could go further.
At the quiet words, Priscilla's crimson gaze swung back to Abel. Meeting that gaze, Abel, still down on one knee, looked up at her and said,

"Taritta is more capable than you credit her. She won't be easily dealt with."

"Hearing you say that — do you imagine I'd overlook such insolence?"

"I doubt you would. But Priscilla... how many times have you drawn the 'Sunlight Sword'?"

Alongside his surprising assessment of Taritta, Abel's question struck at Priscilla. Frankly, Subaru couldn't grasp the intent behind it. The 'Sunlight Sword' she'd held — a treasured blade blazing crimson — its overwhelming power went without saying.

But now, having cut Arakiya down, she had already returned it to its empty sheath. Of course, should the need arise, she would draw it again, but—

"Taritta, lower your bow. This one is not your enemy."

Without waiting for Priscilla's response, Abel went on to tell Taritta to put away her weapon. But being told so gave Taritta no reason to accept it. Still with her arrow nocked, she pressed back with a "But—!"

"That woman is clearly dangerous."

"I say this knowing as much. But what you need right now isn't us."

"—?"

To the still-wary Taritta, Abel said this while shifting his gaze to the side. Guided by the gesture, Taritta, skillfully keeping Priscilla in the corner of her eye, turned her attention that way — and drew a sharp breath.

"S-Sister...!?"

Where her gaze was guided, Taritta saw Mizelda receiving treatment from Rem. Half her body scorched by flame, and further impaled through the abdomen by a branch, Mizelda was gravely wounded; Rem was tending to her desperately, but the sight remained no less harrowing for it.

"Taritta-san, please speak to her. Call out to her...!"

"Sister, sister...!!"

At once, the tension of a moment before dissolved, and Taritta rushed to Mizelda's side. Following Rem's instruction, she gripped her sister's hand and began calling "Sister" over and over.
With that, the one keeping Priscilla in check was gone once more.

"――――"

Once again, the staring match between Abel and Priscilla resumed. Or so it seemed.

"—Fool. You there, go retrieve that one. All this noise is spoiling my mood."

"Eh..."

So saying, Priscilla jerked her chin toward the shattered balcony.
The one singled out was Subaru, and the content of the order seemed to be: go rescue Al, who had fallen. Wherever that razor's-edge tension from a moment ago had gone, Subaru found himself holding his breath at how casually she gave the order.
Of course, he wasn't reckless enough to voice his indignation with blunt honesty and risk souring Priscilla's mood. More than anything—

"I'll keep an eye on that man. Don't fret — I won't let him die. No need to trouble yourself over it."

Pointing at Abel as she said this, Priscilla had addressed the very thing gnawing at Subaru's unease, and that pushed him toward compliance. There wasn't enough trust built up between him and Priscilla for him to take her words wholly at face value, but he could believe that she wouldn't go back on her own declaration.

"Staring each other down accomplishes nothing. For now, do as she says."

"...Fine. Not that I'm your subordinate or anything."

Taking Priscilla's words as a cue, Abel added this to a still-deliberating Subaru.
Somehow, he disliked the feeling of moving because Abel told him to, so he tossed out that bit of pushback and immediately set off to rescue Al, who was caught on the streetlamp outside the building.

"――――"

Inside his head, everything was a jumbled mess — Mizelda and the Shudrak's safety, the handling of Guaral going forward, the relationship between Abel and Priscilla, a tangle of factors all at once. Clenching his back teeth hard, he set that aside for now and focused on what was right in front of him.

Just as Rem was desperately fighting to save the life before her.
So too was Subaru, working to bring the 'bloodless conquest' he'd envisioned even a little closer to reality.

The rescue operation for Al, caught on the city hall's streetlamp, turned out to be surprisingly difficult.

After all, Al was a man who'd dedicated one of his arms to another world. Trying to haul up someone dangling in midair with only one arm to work with left precious few options to choose from.

"This is what you'd call literally 'having your hands full.' ...Nah, maybe 'wishing you had an extra pair of hands' is the better way to put it? Whaddya think, bro?"

"Shut up! If you actually want to be saved, don't kill my motivation!"

Firing back at the ill-timed wisecrack, Subaru tore the curtain Abel had been dangling from, fashioned it into a loop, and lowered it down to Al hanging in midair.
Al threaded his arm and body through the loop to secure himself, and preparations for the hoist were complete. Then, to haul up the middle-aged man swinging back and forth,

"...There, up you come, up you come. Phew, saved my own skin. Much obliged."

After a few precarious moments, they finally managed to haul Al up.
Having crawled back onto solid ground, Al dramatically clutched his chest in relief and plopped himself cross-legged on the wrecked floor of the great hall. Watching him, Subaru, having finished the job, wiped the sweat from his brow.
Then he turned to the one who'd helped with Al's rescue — Medium.

"Thanks for the help, Medium-san. It would've been rough going alone."

"Don't mention it, don't mention it! More importantly, sorry for going down right when it mattered~!"

Still in tattered condition but laughing cheerfully all the same, Medium apologized for the earlier clash.
She seemed to feel bad about being unable to help during Arakiya's assault. But being apologized to over that was misplaced — to begin with, neither Medium nor Flop had any obligation to be dragged into this fight at all.

"Both me and bro still got a long way to go in our training! Gotta reflect, gotta reflect!"

"No, it's not your fault at all, Medium-san! The reason you couldn't move was because you were shielding Flop-san and Utakata and... Rui."

Choosing his words carefully, Subaru praised Medium's achievement.
When Arakiya's tornado had torn through the great hall, Medium had protected Flop right beside her, along with Utakata and Rui, whom she'd been carrying on her shoulders.
As a result, she'd taken a blow to the head and been left unable to move. But thanks to her efforts, all three of them were safe, and Medium herself had escaped further harm.

"Hmm, I'm happy Natsumi-chan says so, but that's no good. I'm bro's bodyguard, so I gotta be able to do my job properly!"

"Medium-san..."

"Next time! I won't show you something that pathetic again! Look forward to me and bro tomorrow, Natsumi-chan!"

"—. It's reassuring to hear you say that. But you two really don't need to keep pushing yourselves this much..."

Subaru tried to continue, saying they didn't have to stick with them any further.
But his halting words were easily swept away by her bright, sun-like "Ah!" as her eyes went round.

"I'm gonna go help bro over there! He's such a softie, he'll have a hard time even carrying the wounded by himself!"

"Ah, ah."

"Well then, see you later, Natsumi-chan! Glad you didn't fall, mask-mister!"

Waving her hand wildly, Medium dashed off without a moment's pause.
She went straight to helping Flop and the others treating the wounded, throwing herself into carrying and tending to injured members of the Shudrak and imperial soldiers alike.
Without even properly accepting Subaru's words of thanks, his sense of indebtedness toward the O'Connell siblings only kept growing.
Would a day ever come when he could fully repay a debt so one-sidedly received?

"Big and cute, that gal. Guess even crossing borders doesn't slow you down none, bro."

"I have zero idea what you mean by that, and it's honestly unsettling. Though I do agree Medium-san is big and cute. More importantly..."

"Hm? What's up, bro?"

Cracking his neck, Al kept up his usual banter, and Subaru glared sidelong at him. Packed into that glare was the frustration he hadn't been able to direct at Priscilla, venting itself out of spite now.
Fair enough, really. This whole time, Subaru's questions had been left hanging.

"Don't make that scary face. You're ruining all that nice makeup and hairstyling."

"Unfortunately, the makeup and hair were already ruined by all the chaos just now. The real Natsumi Schbach is way cuter, so don't get the wrong idea."

"Natsumi Schbach, huh."

Hearing Subaru's glowering alias, Al gave a low, meaningful chuckle in his throat. Subaru eyed him suspiciously at that, and Al shook his head. "Nah, nah."

"Just impressed, is all — sharp fake name. The cross-dressing suits you too. Maybe that's your secret hobby nobody knows about, huh?"

"Don't joke around. I'll give you the real story on Natsumi some other time. More importantly, answer me. —Why are you and Priscilla here?"

"――――"

"It's a huge help for us, but it makes zero sense. And I don't mean just being in Guaral — I mean why you're in the Empire at all."

At Subaru's earnest question, Al closed his mouth on the banter and fell silent.
No matter how much he thought about it, the intervention made no sense.
That Priscilla and Al were standing here in imperial territory, bold as anything, simply didn't add up. Of course, the same could probably be said of Subaru's group from their perspective—

"That one I'll let slide for now. Whoever asks first gets the answer."

"That's a bold policy. But that's not actually what you really want to ask me, is it, bro?"

"What?"

"Why me and the princess are in the Empire — who cares. What you actually want, bro, is how to get back. How to get from this scary, dangerous country back to the home you're used to. Ain't I right?"

Countered like that, hit where it hurt, Subaru fell silent this time.
The way back from the Empire to the Kingdom — that was the information Subaru wanted most right now. Put that way, he had no words to deny it.
He had questions. Resentment over the unfairness of it all too. But if the real question was whether solving a bigger problem mattered more—

"Then — do you have the answer? A way for us to cross the imperial border and get back to the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica...?"

"Nah, sorry. Hate to build you up and then let you down, but I got nothin'."

"You—!"

"Wait, wait, don't get mad! To be precise, gettin' out of the Empire as it stands now is hard. Gettin' in's one thing, but gettin' out's turned into a real ordeal."

Toyed with so casually, Subaru bristled, and Al raised his palm in a placating gesture. As ever, his manner of speaking gave nothing away of his real thoughts. Do I look like this to other people too? If so, he had a lot to reflect on.
Anyway—

"You can get in but not out... why's that?"

"C'mon, that much oughta be obvious. It means someone's made it easy to get in but doesn't want their target slipping out again."

"—. So that means you know Abel's identity too."

Taking in Al's answer, Subaru naturally arrived at that conclusion.
He'd heard before that entry and exit from the Vollachian Empire were strict, but if border restrictions had been tightened under the exact conditions Al described, the cause could only be Abel — no, the presence of Vincent Vollachia, Emperor of Vollachia.

Tightened border security to prevent Abel, driven from the throne and now a fugitive, from escaping to another country.
In the end, for Subaru's group to make it back to the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica, they'd have to overcome a formidable obstacle.

"Al, if you've got information more detailed than ours..."

"Whoa there, zip it from here on out. Don't wanna go runnin' my mouth and get chewed out by the princess. You got somethin' to say, say it to her. Though, that said—"

At that, Al cut himself off with a self-important pause, raised the right hand he'd been extending, and tapped it against the metal fitting of his helmet, clicking it a few times.
Then he continued.

"Whether the princess'll actually tell you straight — even I can't guarantee that one."

"Princess, I'm back. How's the talk on your end goin'?"

"However it's going, it's hardly progressed at all. I've merely been hearing accounts, one by one, from whoever woke first."

Waving his hand casually to ask about progress, Al got his answer from Priscilla, who replied with her cheek propped on her hand.
The upper part of the building had been destroyed by Arakiya's assault, but the sturdy, solidly built foundation remained intact, and fortunately the interior of the city hall had been kept safe.
Thanks to that, there was no need to relocate, and the meeting hall for the crucial discussion could still be used.

In the meeting hall with its great round table, Abel and Priscilla sat at opposite ends. Besides them, Zikr and his subordinate imperial soldiers, Kuna and members of the 'People of Shudrak,' and other faces had been gathered.
All of them were people who'd gotten off relatively lightly, and if Priscilla's words were to be believed, this had apparently been an interrogation session of sorts.

"Yo, glad you're okay, Natsumi."

"Kuna, I'm glad you're awake too. How are your injuries?"

"Holly's big ol' frame came in handy. Honestly, I ain't too fond of bein' stuck in here either, wanna get outta here fast as I can, but..."

Seated at the round table, Kuna noticed Subaru and made a sour face.
Looking at the other Shudrak faces besides her, Subaru understood the reason for Kuna's sour expression. Simply put, she was the one holding things together for the Shudrak in this meeting.

"Taritta can't leave the chief's side, and it ain't like makin' Holly talk is gonna do any good. So somehow this job fell to me..."

"Nah, I think Kuna's good at it — you keep a level head and watch things closely. So, how's the discussion going?"

"Mostly just finished gettin' everyone's read on the situation. If you wanna call it a real 'discussion,' that's about to start now."

At Kuna's shrugging answer, Subaru nodded, "I see."
Right after, he found himself unsure where in the room he ought to be. To begin with, his position within this group was somewhat unclear.
Nominally, he held the role of the one who'd drafted the plan for Guaral's fall, but even that, thanks to Arakiya's and Priscilla's interruptions, hadn't exactly turned out to be a success.

"Natsumi-jou, if you're troubled, would you care to sit beside me?"

Just then, the one who pulled out a chair before the still-hesitating Subaru, rising swiftly to his feet, was Zikr.
Short and stocky, Zikr looked at Subaru and smiled with gentlemanly grace. At that gaze, Subaru pointed at himself.

"Um, you've noticed already, right? I'm cross-dressing."

"If you are affecting the appearance of a woman, then I am, in a sense, affecting the appearance of a man. The image of manhood I believe in is one that behaves with gentlemanly conduct toward a woman, whoever she may be pretending to be."

"T-this is what a 'true lover of women' looks like..."

Not the derogatory sense of 'womanizer,' but the honorable sense of 'one who reveres women' — the loftiness of the mindset was on an entirely different level.
Feeling a touch of embarrassment over his own half-considered resolve to cross-dress, Subaru accepted Zikr's courtesy and took the seat he'd pulled out.
On top of that, as Zikr returned to his own seat, Subaru called out to him, "Excuse me,"

"Also, thanks for shielding me up there earlier. If you hadn't stepped in, things might've gone badly."

"No, that was merely my body moving on instinct. Whether I could manage the same again, I cannot say. After all, I am 'the Coward.'"

Hearing Subaru's thanks, Zikr called himself 'the Coward' with what seemed like a note of pride.
That was likely because it was the epithet by which the emperor he'd sworn loyalty to remembered him — a title from General Zikr Osman, one of the Empire's 'generals,' and his very way of being.
And the emperor who commanded such a fine man's respect, one known as Abel, said—

"—The fortress city of Guaral has fallen, and General Zikr Osman, commander of the garrisoned troops, has come over to our side. In addition, the 'People of Shudrak' from the Buddheim Jungle."

"Hardly enough. Even I have heard of the ferocity of the 'People of Shudrak.' Even so, that leaves you woefully short of the strength needed to take on the Empire."

"A fair point. Priscilla, what of your own forces?"

"My private soldiers I have not brought into the Empire. Setting those aside, all I can call my own forces here are that iron-helmeted clown, a drunken swordsman, and a page whose only merit is being adorable."

"――――"

Off to the side of Subaru's entrance and his exchange with Kuna, Abel and Priscilla pressed on with their discussion.
Confirming each other's forces and circumstances, the two of them held deep contemplation in their eyes — but Subaru cut in with a "Hold on" to that.
He'd been jerked around quite enough as it was. Being left behind here too was out of the question.

"What is it, fool. You were here?"

"I'm here, and I know it's a weird thing to say myself, but you seeing me right now and not being impressed is out of the question. Last time she saw this, Beako was having nightmares for a while."

"I believe I already offered praise regarding that attire. Surely you don't imagine you deserve a reward merely for hauling Al up with a filthy rag?"

"I'm not expecting that! Well, a little. At least I'm hoping you might lend an ear to what I have to say."

Planting his hands on the round table and leaning forward, Subaru met Priscilla's narrowed eyes.
Her gaze seemed to size him up, but Subaru didn't flinch from it. Unlike before, Kuna and Zikr were here now too. Said that way, it sounded like a pretty pathetic sort of breakwater, though.

"Anyway, why are you here like it's the most natural thing in the world? Asking Al gets me nowhere. Tell me straight, from your own mouth."

"What a tiresome question. —To speak with that man there, Vincent Abelux."

"――――"

Jutting her chin, Priscilla answered without so much as a ripple of concern. At that, Abel, seated with arms crossed, closed one eye, dark emotion swirling in his black eyes.
Whether that emotion belonged to joy, anger, sorrow, or delight, or to none of them at all — it was the sort of strange thing that could have been read as any of them.

"With Abel...? But how did you find out where he was?"

"In the imperial throne at the capital of Lupugana, there lies a mechanism for transferring the emperor. One with the ability to sense a coup would flee east through that mechanism. —To the burial ground where emperors are laid to rest, generation after generation."

"The emperor's... graveyard?"

"A mechanism for transferring there. Is that not so, Vincent... no, it seems more convenient now to call you Abel."

Turning the thrust of the conversation from Subaru to Abel, Priscilla let the flame in her eyes burn red.
At that scorching gaze, Abel sighed and nodded, "That's right."

"Call me Abel for now. At the very least, I have no right to claim the title of emperor, stripped of my throne as I am."

"Commendable, dutiful, honest to a fault... in any case, a remarkably soft way of thinking. Seems that sitting on the imperial throne made you forget how to stand up again."

"Look at me and tell me I've forgotten how to stand — quite the thing to say."

At Priscilla's merciless mockery, even Abel could no longer hide a dangerous edge. In an instant, the two gazes clashed, charged with heat, until the air of the meeting room nearly smelled of scorching. It seemed the talks would break down here, sparks flying, and there'd be no avoiding it—

"Now, now, settle down, you two. Fighting won't do either of you any good, right?"

But there was a man who, bold as brass, went strolling into that near-explosive powder keg like he was puffing on a cigarette while doing it.
Slipping easily into an atmosphere that admitted no interruption, grinning easily, was Al. Still standing rather than seated, he basked in the scorching gazes of both Abel and Priscilla.

"I know it don't look it, but the princess has got a cute side too, y'know? The second she found out she had to book it to Guaral express, she had the flying dragon going full throttle like she meant to wreck it. That's how eager she was for this touching reunion, that's the— gah!?"

"Fool."

While spinning this tale, Al had circled around behind Priscilla to defend her character, but his mistress showed not a shred of appreciation for the thought, driving her fan into his gut with furious force.
With a cry of pain, Al doubled over into an L-shape and dropped to one knee on the spot.

"To presume to speak on my behalf — what unbearable conceit. Since when did you rise so high, that you'd forget your place? Clown you are, so know it."

"B-but flyin' off the handle like that just proves it, don't it...? Still, you did come to help — that's the truth, ain't it?"

"――――"

Narrowing her crimson eyes, Priscilla let her displeasure at Al's words show plainly.
But the fact that she didn't outright deny it in words was proof that Al's words had struck true.

"Priscilla, came to help Abel...?"

That felt so hard to accept that Subaru couldn't shake off the sense of wrongness.
Of course, judging by results and actions alone, there was no doubt Priscilla had helped Abel and Subaru's group. But the character of hers that Subaru knew stood in the way of that acceptance.
Even if she'd cut down an enemy she found displeasing, was fighting to protect someone truly a logic that could hold within the person known as Priscilla Barielle?

"You've got the look of someone thinking unpleasant thoughts. Care to have them gouged out, fool?"

"Don't say scary things while staring right at someone like that. Honestly, it feels like a question of how far I can even trust this, but..."

"—In any case, I understand now that you used the mechanism in the throne to fly east. Then I suppose it follows that I would join the 'People of Shudrak' and come to the fortress city as well?"

"...Follows?"

Subaru would grant that Priscilla possessed insight beyond the ordinary, but the ease with which Abel had accepted the circumstances was something Subaru found hard to swallow. Certainly, the conditions given seemed to satisfy that inference, but—

"Don't muddy the waters, bro. The smart folks are satisfied. If we wrap it up here, we avoid trouble that don't need startin'."

"You're fine with that...?"

"Fine or not, ain't much choice but to swallow it. Push back and it's you who loses out. —'Cause either way, me and the princess win."

"Uh..."

Sliding closer on his knee, Al murmured that declaration to Subaru under his breath.
Honestly, Subaru found it surprising that Al would flatly declare they'd win. Originally, Al had seemed less modest than simply drawing a certain casual line between himself and the world around him.
In truth, the knights among the other royal candidates were all men of renowned skill — and neither Subaru nor Al was special enough to afford overconfidence in the face of that.
It was that kind of shared feeling, in a sense, that Subaru had come to hold toward Al.

Which was exactly why Al declaring it here, like that, struck Subaru as unexpected.
Even in someone who seemed outwardly unchanged, breezy as ever, change did come after all. Through the master-servant bond with Priscilla, and the days of battle tied to the Royal Selection that came with it.

"Well, mind you, the only reason I get to talk big like that is 'cause it's about someone we've got even a sliver of a shot against. Point in case, that Arakiya gal's class was rough. Couldn't spot a single angle to win."

"...Well, the 'Nine Divine Generals' are supposed to be the Empire's strongest members, right? In the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica, that'd be like Reinhard or Julius."

Most likely, if one were to select the strongest members within the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica, many would be drawn from the ranks of skilled knights. Reinhard and Julius went without saying, but it might also include Marcos, commander of the Royal Guard, or Roswaal, the chief court mage.
For his part, Subaru would like to throw Wilhelm and Garfiel into the mix too, assembling an all-star lineup to go up against the 'Nine Divine Generals.'

"Nah, that ain't quite it. And however much you call 'em the 'Nine Divine Generals,' there's no way there's someone like Reinhard in there..."

"—Unfortunately, there is."

"Wha?"

To Subaru, who'd been trying to gauge the strength of the 'Nine Divine Generals' as the most dangerous threat, what Abel said was something he could hardly believe his ears over.
What had he just said? —Someone on par with Reinhard exists?

"You're saying there's another bug-tier character like Reinhard?"

"I'm not familiar with that word, but if you mean an equal, then yes. Above Arakiya in the 'Nine Divine Generals' stands the 'First.' That is the one."

"The 'First' of the 'Nine Divine Generals'..."

"—Cecilus Segmunt."

Beside a gaping Subaru, Zikr quietly spoke the name.
Subaru understood that it was a person's name, and moreover, the name of the very 'First' in question. The person behind that name was the Empire's strongest, Vollachia's proud bug-tier character—

"Known as the 'Blue Lightning of Vollachia,' he is a swordsman of the highest rank. A presence spoken of in the same breath as Lugunica's 'Sword Saint,' Kararagi's 'Divine Adorer,' and Gusteko's 'Mad Prince.'"

"Uh... I feel like I've heard that title before somewhere... so, seriously?"

"Cross him, and your head flies in the blink of an eye. That's the kind of man he is."

At Subaru's question, his cheeks stiffening, Abel nodded with arms crossed.
In this setting, neither Abel nor Zikr had any reason to lie or joke, which meant what he'd just heard was simple fact.

Cecilus Segmunt, the Empire's strongest man, a match for Reinhard.
A swordsman of the highest rank whose very description carried an unmistakable sense of danger — just how fearsome and violent a foe could he possibly be? Subaru felt a chill run down his spine.
It was already a battle with a massive gap in numbers as it was. If they were outmatched in quality on top of being outnumbered, just how were they supposed to aim for victory at all—

"...But thinkin' about it that way, y'know. Managin' to knock out the gal holdin' the 'Second' spot here — that's a hell of a haul for us too."

"Al..."

Against the grim mood weighing on Subaru and the others, Al's voice rang out bright and cheerful.
It was a genuinely forward-looking take, though the truth was, it wasn't easy to simply hop on board with it. Still, taking Al's spirit to heart, Subaru let out a breath. "Yeah, you're right."

Dwelling only on the bad here wouldn't lead anywhere.
In truth, Subaru's group had managed to take the fortress city while keeping losses to an absolute minimum. Isolated and without reinforcements, they'd even managed to add allies in the form of Priscilla and Al — even if calling them friend or foe outright was hard to say.
The wounded Shudrak, too — Rem would surely fight hard to heal them. Mizelda would return to the front lines and no doubt regale them again with her energy-sapping theory of the all-capable handsome man.
So—

"Like Al says, taking down one of the Nine Divine Generals — and a big one at that — is huge. If it's a rank near the top in the Empire, chances are she's got information on the others too."

"Ah, good point! Sharp thinkin', bro. Information's worth more than gold bars in the middle of a war. Might as well make good use of her while we're at it — let's hear what she's got to say."

"Right. If there's some good lead to be had..."

Forcing his own spirits up, Subaru spoke, and Al matched his pace. In that tone, the conversation began drifting toward extracting information from the captured Arakiya.

"Hold up, Subaru, tin-mask. Gonna have to say I'm against that."

But there, an objection came from Kuna, present as the Shudrak representative.
Subaru and Al turned, wondering what was wrong, and Kuna, stroking her green-dyed hair, said,

"That woman was dangerous as hell. No tellin' what she'll pull if you give her an openin'. Better off just killin' her quick, and that's a fact."

"That's... I get it, but that's way too rash. Killing her, that's just—"

"Our chief got put through that. Whatever Subaru says, me and the others ain't gonna feel right till that woman's executed."

Glaring at the still-pressing Subaru, Kuna brought up Arakiya's execution head-on.
Confronted with the reminder of Mizelda's condition, Subaru had no choice but to fall silent. Her safety was entrusted to Rem, but even if the wound healed, the fact of the injury wouldn't disappear.
If the Shudrak couldn't forgive that, then Arakiya would have to atone for it.

"――――"

Searching for an answer to give Kuna, Subaru glanced briefly toward Priscilla.
He wanted to see what reaction Priscilla, who'd shown some extraordinary relationship with Arakiya, would have to Kuna's insistence on execution.
But—

"My stance toward Arakiya is already decided. To begin with, it was I who cut her down from behind. Are those black eyes on your face there just for decoration?"

"Gh..."

"I have no intention of pleading Arakiya's case. If her fate runs out here, then that too is Arakiya's path to walk. —Though it would spoil the fun."

"...I don't get you at all."

Subaru shook his head at Priscilla, who spoke of weighing Arakiya's life so flatly, so matter-of-factly.
Looking at how things had ended, the two of them seemed to be on hostile terms, and yet judging by Arakiya's demeanor, they'd surely once been close. And yet Priscilla's attitude was so coldly detached.
As an outsider, Subaru couldn't understand the relationship between the two of them.

"But if she dies, everything's over. A life doesn't come back."

"You would lecture me on the value of life? Do you imagine I would misjudge the worth of another's life?"

"—. Even you aren't infallible. You must make mistakes too, sometimes."

Staring straight at Priscilla, Subaru answered almost without pause.
The moment he said it, the air in the room went taut.

He could see Kuna and Zikr holding their breath, and Al pressing a hand to the brow of his helmet. Subaru himself was aware he'd said something reckless, carried by momentum.
This was the pattern where, only after the words were already out, he'd replayed them and realized: saying this could cost him his life by earning Priscilla's displeasure.

In the next instant, that gleaming red treasured sword might take his head clean off.
Even so—

"I'm not wrong. And you're bound to be wrong too, sometimes."

Doubling down, Subaru repeated words that could well cost him his life.
For an instant, Subaru had the illusion that Priscilla's eyes narrowed with a cold, ringing sound before his very eyes. And then, the scorching 'death' that would make him pay for his recklessness—

"—Even I err, do I. How infuriating."

—did not come.

"Eh..."

Even he himself was surprised, eyes widening, and Subaru let out a breath from lungs gone stiff.
Glancing once at Subaru's state, Priscilla snapped her fan open with a crack, and let her gaze sweep past Subaru, over toward Kuna.

"Before her head is taken, it might be worth considering some use for her."

"—! You tryin' to give us orders? You, an outsider?"

"Ignore me if you're able."

Having withdrawn her earlier stance, Priscilla's gaze swept over Kuna's slight frame as if scorching it.
Unconsciously wrapping her arms around herself, Kuna ended up proving, all on her own, the pressure she'd felt under that gaze. A pity, but Priscilla and Kuna were simply not in the same league.

"Honestly, had me sweatin' bullets there. But hey, that settles it!"

At that, cutting through the tense atmosphere, Al slapped his hand down hard on the round table with a loud crack.
Having drawn everyone's attention that way, he sent a glance toward Subaru from behind his iron helmet.

"Glad bro here didn't kick the bucket, but even bigger news is the world didn't lose an erotically cute gal for nothin'. So once she wakes up, let's have a talk—"

"—This is bad, no!"

Amid pointless banter, just as Al was about to wrap up the matter of Arakiya's disposition,
bursting into the meeting hall with hurried footsteps and a flustered voice, squeezing her large frame through the doorway, was Holly.

Drawing everyone's attention, her body and breath ragged, she said—

That was—

"Two imperial soldiers came bustin' in, and the Nine Divine General we caught got away, no!"
