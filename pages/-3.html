Side Story: "Emilia's First Date"

1

The promised day of the date began with a morning blessed by a cloudless, clear sky.

"Lately my favorite look on Lia is braids, but it feels like a waste to show Subaru. The outfit should definitely be white as the main color. Then an accent hair ornament, and then, and then..."

In front of the silver-haired girl sitting on the bed—Emilia—the one bobbing frantically through the air was Puck, the cat spirit who had made a contract with her.

Watching the little gray puffball fuss about, Emilia pressed a finger to her cheek.

"You don't have to worry so much about it—I think the usual would be fine..."

"No way! Whoever the other person is aside, this is Lia's first time doing this. You can't compromise on being the cutest in the world!"

"Honestly, you're so dramatic."

So as not to let Puck—who for some reason was burning with fighting spirit—see, Emilia quietly sighed.

The cat spirit, her stand-in parent and as good as family, had been like this since last night. At this rate she was going to be worn out from preparations before she even left the house. —And today was supposed to be her "date" with Subaru.

"Listen, Lia. About today's outing—if Subaru tries to hold your hand or anything like that, you absolutely must not allow it. Never let yourself get swept along. A girl needs modesty, a bit of bashfulness, and a little sense of making him wait. I always say so, and Lia understands, right?"

"I get it, I get it. Honestly, if you're that worried, you could just come along too."

"I want to respect a boy's courage. I could never do something so uncouth."

"But then what you're saying and what you're doing seem kind of backwards..."

"Inside me, the part that wants to cheer him on as a fellow male is at war with the part that, as a parent, refuses to allow it. ...Ugh, Lia, go now, while I'm still holding myself back...!"

"Yes, yes."

Was Puck's theatrical banter a bad influence from Subaru? He'd always been a bit like this, she felt, but somehow he seemed to have gotten even worse.

Quickly straightening her appearance, Emilia beckoned to Puck. Readily lured over, Puck settled on her shoulder and gave her cheek a fancy little pat with his paw pad.

"All right, Lia. I'll be sleeping inside my vessel, so if Subaru ever does anything that goes against common decency, call me out."

"You don't need to worry about anything that awful. Now then, good night. Rest well."

Seen off by a softly laughing Emilia, Puck's form dissolved into particles of light. The faint glow was drawn straight into the crystal at her chest, and with that, the noisy chaperone fell silent.

So Emilia gave the crystal a flick with her finger, and stepped out of her room with a brisk, confident stride.

Somehow, she was aware of a certain buoyancy in her chest—even Emilia herself could tell.

2

"Did I keep you waiting?"

"No, I just got here."

In front of the tree marking their meeting spot, Subaru gave that answer to Emilia as she arrived.

Hearing his answer, Emilia nodded, then glanced back toward the mansion.

"Meeting outside the mansion, wanting to have this exact exchange just now... can I ask what the point of it all is?"

"Both are for setting the mood of a proper date! Leaving the house together just doesn't have that date feeling, and if you meet up outside, that exchange we just had is essential."

He must have arrived quite a while earlier, judging by the number of Puck drawings—apparently scratched into the ground with a branch or something to kill time—left scattered beneath the tree.

Seeing this, at the very least, "I just got here" was clearly a complete lie.

"Hmm, is that so. I really don't know anything about that sort of thing."

"By the way, the fact that I'd say 'I just got here' even if Emilia-tan showed up incredibly late—that's where my sweet, gallant heart is hiding."

"Doesn't that mean Subaru was also incredibly late...?"

"There are things you shouldn't say out loud even if you notice them, you know!?"

It seemed to be some kind of standard bit. As she humored Subaru insisting on its importance, he finished his little lecture, put a hand to his chin, and stared intently at Emilia.

"Huh, what's wrong? Is something about me weird?"

"Setting aside how 'love' and 'strange' look kind of similar written out, there's barely anything weird about Emilia-tan, and even that little bit is charming, so it's no big deal. More importantly, Emilia-tan's outstandingly cute today too! ...Did you dress up a little more than usual?"

Subaru was quick to notice the small differences from her usual look—her hair, her accessories. Emilia was surprised that he'd correctly guessed the very parts Puck had put so much effort into.

"You could tell? Puck was incredibly fired up about it today."

"Oh, GJ! Nice work, Puck. Here I figured he'd be dead against his daughter's first date, his fatherly feelings getting in the way and all."

"Yeah, so he really was conflicted about it, but he's asleep in the crystal now, so don't worry."

With a wry smile, she showed him the crystal at her chest, and Subaru's expression relaxed with an "I see." Then he cleared his throat and gave a formal, deferential bow.

"Well then, shall we begin our first date, mine and Emilia-tan's? I am honored to be permitted the privilege of escorting you."

His butler's uniform was crisply, neatly arranged from head to toe. Subaru had had no other choice, owning no other formal wear, but to Emilia it seemed to suit his current bearing perfectly.

So obligingly, she stood beside Subaru, who had crooked his elbow, offering his arm for her to take.

"Um, is this all right?"

She gave a small tug at the hem of his uniform and asked, peering up at him. Holding hands had been forbidden by Puck, so she was quite anxious about whether this was acceptable. But in response to her gesture, Subaru—

"Not what I expected, but a performance beyond my expectations! Seriously, your cuteness is on a whole other level..."

Covering his face with his hands and writhing, Subaru at least didn't seem to be angry. Reassured by that, Emilia said "Come on," tugged at his sleeve, and started walking.

"So, what do we do on a 'date'?"

"First, the village. Then let's visit the graves... and go see a flower field. Slow and easy."

Basking in the warmth of the sunlight, Emilia looked at Subaru's profile as he smiled and said that, and replied "That sounds fun too," smiling so charmingly it was almost mesmerizing.

3

"I'm glad the children seemed so lively. And Subaru, you were incredibly popular with them too."

"Whether I can honestly be happy about that is something I sometimes wonder, Emilia-tan."

Muttering with a deeply world-weary expression, Subaru was already brushing off his butler's uniform, which had quickly gotten smeared with mud and dust.

Having arrived in the village, the two of them first went to pick up flowers for the grave visit. The bouquet was one Subaru had arranged in advance, having asked the children to make it for him.

There was no trouble with the handover itself, but trouble arose right after.

Even the noble cause of an important date carried no weight whatsoever with the innocent children. Poor Subaru got tangled up with them as usual, and ended up, as always, in a thoroughly wretched state.

"Even so, it's probably because Emilia-tan was here that they held back at all. If not, I'd be in a far worse state by now."

"Don't be so dramatic about it. Those kids just really like you, that's all."

"Normally they'd be wiping their snot on my back. But today I actually managed to dodge it."

"Is that what usually happens to you!?"

Suddenly, Subaru started to look rather pitiable to Emilia.

Faced with that sympathetic gaze, Subaru made a complicated expression, turned around with a sigh of "good grief," and then said "Ah." Drawn to look back as well, she saw the children who had followed them from the village.

"Hey, quit following us. Emilia-tan and I are having ourselves a fun, naughty little rendezvous, remember? Scram, scram!"

"Aww, you're being a tyrant." "Aww, you're being violent." "Aww, you're being stubborn."

"I'm not being stubborn. We're getting close to the barrier you've all been told not to go near, aren't we. If you don't want to sit through the village chief's long, boring sermon with no punchline, get on home. Go play freeze tag like I taught you—something wholesome."

Waving his hand as if shooing away insects, Subaru sent the children retreating, grumbling all the while.

Emilia watched the exchange with a warm smile, but then noticed that one of the retreating children—a pretty little girl—was looking at her.

She was a cute girl with reddish-brown hair and cheeks as red as an appa. The moment her eyes met Emilia's, she stuck out her tongue with a "Bleh!" and dashed off.

"What's with that attitude, Petra. I'll set her straight tomorrow. Emilia-tan, are you okay? If you're too shocked to walk I could carry you princess-style."

"No, I'm used to it, so I'm fine. Something like that doesn't bother me at all."

"Nobody says 'doesn't bother me' like that these days... And also, me, getting hurt by the whole 'used to it' comment..."

"It's fine. Come on, back to the 'date.' Let's go."

She said this to Subaru, whose face looked like he had something stuck between his back teeth, and set off walking briskly. Subaru seemed to hesitate for a moment, but in the end said nothing and quickened his pace to walk beside her.

At his consideration, she felt a small twinge of self-loathing for indulging in it.

"—Ah, here we are, here we are."

After walking a little further like that, Subaru stopped and pointed at the trees.

At first glance it looked like an unremarkable row of trees, but blue crystals were embedded in the upper part of each trunk, and the evenly spaced trees formed a barrier that reacted to the bad mana in the air—miasma.

The witchbeast known as an Ulgarm that had lived in the forest had been wiped out, but some other witchbeast was bound to move into the vacant territory eventually. The barrier would never become unnecessary, and so it had been set up anew.

"We can't go inside, so this'll have to do, sorry."

Choosing a spot just before the barrier where no one passed through, Subaru set the bouquet at the base of a tree. Then, kneeling on the ground, he pressed his hands together, closed his eyes, and bowed his head as if in prayer.

It was an unusual way of praying, one she'd never seen before. Perhaps a custom from Subaru's homeland, which he never spoke of in detail. Glancing at him, Emilia too placed a hand over her chest and prayed for the repose of the departed souls.

Even for witchbeasts, she had heard that when a life that had died returned to Od, its soul was purified. When it once again took form and was reborn into the world, she hoped that this time they could be creatures who lived alongside one another.

That was the teaching about life that Emilia knew—though who had taught it to her, she couldn't quite recall.

"Sorry for dragging you into my own self-satisfaction. It's fine now, all okay."

Brushing off his knees, Subaru stood and smiled at Emilia. Coming back to herself, Emilia made sure his smile wasn't forced, and nodded, "Mm."

"So, next is the flower field... right?"

Picturing the itinerary of their "date" in her mind, Emilia named their next destination. At her confirmation, Subaru snapped his fingers with a satisfying click.

"Yep, the flower field. And let me tell you, it's really something."

So saying, he grinned with a mischievous look that seemed calculated to stoke her anticipation.

4

Passing through the forest, her bangs caught in the wind, Emilia couldn't hold back a gasp of admiration.

"Ah, ah... wow!"

Led on by Subaru through the forest, the moment came when her view, until then blocked by the green foliage, suddenly opened wide. The vivid, colorful sight filled her vision, painting it beautifully from edge to edge.

"Well? I was surprised too, the first time... Pretty impressive, right?"

Unable even to respond to Subaru's proud voice beside her, she found her gaze utterly transfixed.

Red, yellow, white, and blue flowers bloomed in profusion, none crowding out the others, forming a field in perfect harmony. That such a garden could have come about naturally could only be called a miracle.

"Apparently it's a secret garden even for those little brats, so it was a real struggle getting them to spill about it."

"Such a wonderful place... how did you get them to tell you?"

"Heh, well, it was all for my date with Emilia-tan. Having my dignity toyed with by those little devils for a few days is a small price to pay."

As a result of that bargain, just how much dust and snot had Subaru had to be smeared with, she wondered.

Even as she pitied him for it, she couldn't imagine ever forgetting the sheer emotion of the sight before her.

Was this, too, a kind of indulgence toward him—this heart of hers filling up with such a gentle emotion?

"—And this, too, is a present from me to Emilia-tan."

So saying, Subaru reached out toward Emilia, who had been touching her own chest. Emilia's reaction came a beat late—"Eh?"—as Subaru's fingers brushed her silver hair.

"Is this... a flower ornament?"

"What you'd call a flower crown. It might be a bit shabby compared to the real thing, but think of it as a dress rehearsal for the crown Emilia-tan will wear as king someday? Eventually I'd like to give you an accessory dripping in jewels, made by my own hand... but for now, this is the best I can do."

He said it in a deliberately smooth, showy tone, and gave Emilia a wink.

At that gesture of his, Emilia felt her heart give a small beat. For an instant, her chest fluttered strangely. But what that beat had been, curiously, it wouldn't repeat a second time.

Still, it had been so warm a feeling that it naturally brought a smile to her lips.

"—Thank you, Subaru."

The brightest smile of the whole day bloomed on her face, and Subaru was left so entranced by Emilia that he couldn't even speak.

To that Subaru, Emilia poured in a feeling of gratitude beyond compare.

"I hope we can 'date' again like this sometime, Subaru."

And with that killer line, she struck Subaru's heart clean through.
