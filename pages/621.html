Arc 8 Chapter 5: "Forgiveness"

"――It hasn't been long since those guys turned into zombies. There are even some mixed in who were alive just a few hours ago. The ones who died in this battle are getting resurrected one after another, top to bottom……though I still can't say that for certain."

"But I get the part about them being newly-made zombies. Everyone seems like they don't know what their own weaknesses are. And it's not just us who don't know much about zombies……"

"Same goes for them. That said, if they've got a head that can talk, they've probably got a head that can think, too. The more time you give them, the more they fill in those unknowns, and whatever weaknesses might exist disappear."

"Which is exactly why we hit them before the enemy's at full strength――ah."

"What? What'd you notice?"

"――――"

"Don't drag it out, spit it out."

"I'm not dragging anything out. It's just, zombies don't really understand their own bodies that well either. So if we turn it around and teach them――"

△▼△▼△▼△

Rui clung to his back, and he hugged Beatrice tight against his chest.
The same basic posture as when riding on the Galewind Horse's back—except the situation they'd been placed in now was fairly extreme, leaving no room to spare a thought for the girls' comfort.

The surrounding scenery switched in an instant, and immediately after, Subaru and the others appeared in midair beside a house being pulverized by a furious blow, taking up a position behind the hostile zombie.

Tanza throwing a house, and Idra throwing rubble—a wave of attacks that, frankly, was a fairly ridiculous combination of words, but the ridiculousness wasn't limited to the words; the visual was just as absurd.
It wasn't a matter of why a child was throwing the bigger thing instead of an adult. To begin with, the very tactic of throwing a house was ridiculous.
And yet, still——

"If I have the honor of receiving Lady Yorna's favor, this much is nothing."

Once she took it on with that much reliability, the visual impact could be shelved for the time being.
In truth, the mysterious empowerment affecting the entire Pleiades Battalion applied to Idra as well, but compared to Tanza, who had Yorna's blessing stacked on top of it, he was at a disadvantage. It was a matter of putting the right tool to the right task.

From that standpoint, the wave of attacks with projectiles both large and small—drawing the enemy's attention with that, then having them focus on Todd for a moment, before Subaru and the others teleported in for the surprise attack.

"――――"

The zombie twisted its body in midair—the enemy, distinctive for the single eye on its face, had turned the white of that one large eye at the center of its face completely black, its golden pupil gleaming with malevolent light.
Carved into the corner of its mouth was a savagely cruel, blood-scented grin of malice that sent a shiver down the spine.
Whether he'd originally been the type to grin savagely in the middle of battle, or whether becoming a zombie had changed him from who he'd been in life—there was no way to know which.
Only——

"El――"

Swiftly, Subaru and Beatrice raised their hands at the same moment, aiming them at the enemy in front of them.
If the opponent threw caution to the wind here and moved to dodge Subaru and the others' attack with everything it had, then the plan they'd laid would fail.
Most likely, even giving it everything they had, they couldn't hope to match this enemy.

But Subaru was betting that it wouldn't come to that—no, Subaru and the others were holding their breath on it.

"――ッ"

Sure enough, the zombie turned around with startling agility, its huge single eye catching sight of Subaru and the others, but instead of taking evasive action, it took up a stance to swing the war axe gripped in its hand.
A tactic of taking the attack in order to land the finishing counterblow—the traditional strategy of cutting one's own flesh to sever the bone—but the desperation packed into it was different in kind.

A zombie's body regenerated wounds it took almost instantly.
That was precisely why the enemy believed it could take Subaru and the others' attack at no risk, and follow through with its own counterattack.
And that was——

"Minya――!!"

——that, without the enemy ever realizing it was the very path to defeat that Subaru and the others had carved out.

With its war axe raised for the counterblow, the enemy took the purple arrows loosed from Subaru's and Beatrice's outstretched hands full-on.
Three brilliantly gleaming arrows of violet-indigo crystal buried themselves in the left half of the Cyclops's body.

"A fine tactic, but――"

Paying no mind whatsoever to the purple arrows lodged in it, the enemy bared its teeth and roared at Subaru and the others, its prey.
Strangely enough, a zombie's flesh, while retaining a certain amount of toughness, breaks in a manner exactly like shattering porcelain when it does break.
That broken flesh would mend itself as if playing a video in reverse, and the zombie, as though nothing had happened, would go right on with its attack――

"Wha, t……?"

――it couldn't.

The violet-indigo arrows that pierced the zombie's flesh didn't shatter the flesh from there—instead, they remade the flesh into the same violet-indigo crystal as the arrows themselves. Cracks then formed in the crystallized part, and next, this time the zombie's body did shatter—but that part did not regenerate.
Shade Magic's special effectiveness against zombies—that was the enemy weakness confirmed over the course of this retreating battle so far.

——They hadn't had an abundance of chances to confirm it, over many trials and a long stretch of time, but several traits common to zombies had been identified nonetheless.

For example, a zombie's fatal wound was closer to being in the heart than the head.
Though, since being run through the chest didn't actually kill them, "fatal wound" might not be the right expression. Even so, it was a fact that among the zombies who regenerated the wounds inflicted on their flesh with abnormal recovery power, wounds closer to the heart healed more slowly.
Losing an arm or a leg didn't cause bleeding, so presumably the heart wasn't fulfilling the role of sending blood through the body—but it apparently hadn't lost its function as a vital point of the human body.

Amid the process of clarifying these traits of zombies that fell short of being able to fell them, the one thing confirmed to have the effect of truly being able to finish a zombie off was Beatrice's Shade Magic.
According to Beatrice's explanation, the Shade Magic spell "Minya" had an effect like an instant-death spell that froze its target's time, and that seemed to be especially effective against zombies, whose existence was premised on regeneration.
According to Todd, if you burned a zombie's body down to ash, that would apparently halt the enemy's regeneration just the same as hitting it with Minya, but there was no point wishing for what they didn't have.

"This is the strongest way to use the cards we've got in hand!"

No matter how great or small the enemy zombie's strength, hitting it with Beatrice's Minya would finish it off.
So then, the measure they'd devised for landing that hit was to make the enemy—who kept exercising the abilities it had in life—understand, whether it wanted to or not, the merit of having become a zombie.

"You don't get hurt even when you're attacked. Once you realize you'll heal right up even if you take a hit, wouldn't anyone start relying on that power?"

"If you'd been like this before you became a zombie, you'd have dodged that easily, I bet."
"Aww!"

That was Subaru's and Beatrice's, and then Rui's, declaration of victory—or rather, it was both praise and pity directed at the enemy.
This warrior resurrected as a zombie had undoubtedly been quite the formidable fighter in life. Even as a zombie, his combat prowess hadn't dimmed a bit, but something had warped in his mindset going into battle.
If only he hadn't harbored the arrogant belief that taking an attack was nothing to worry about.

"Taking a hit like that head-on—that should never have happened."

"――――"

Spinning round and round from the shock of the magic it had taken, the zombie's eye went wide.
At the swirl of complicated emotion crossing that eye—astonishment mixed with the understanding of having grasped its own defeat—Subaru bit his lower lip and cursed the unreasonable fate that had befallen this warrior.
Even if he were cursed out—what right do you have, crystallizing my left half and making me taste death a second time——

"――Lord Schult!"

It was the instant right after a pang of sentiment crossed his heart.
Called by name by Tanza in a voice on the edge of desperation, Subaru snapped back to reality with a start and noticed it—the change in color of the enemy zombie's eye as it spun in midair, turned its back, and faced them again.
It wasn't that the golden eye peculiar to the dead had changed.
It was only that the astonishment and understanding that had been floating there had faded, repainted with fierce hostility.

For a moment, this enemy had been on the verge of accepting its own defeat with a shock.
And then its eyes had changed color. ――Most likely, triggered by Subaru's last words.

"I'm not――"

"――dead yet!"

Before Subaru, catching his breath at that glint in its eye, the enemy—crystallization progressing across the left side of its body—kicked at something in the air.
It was a fragment of the pulverized house-cannonball. Fragment though it was called, it was the size of a person's head, and kicking it sent the impact shattering the zombie's left arm from the shoulder, sending it flying.
In exchange, the kicked fragment came flying straight at Subaru's face, aimed for it――

"Uwaugh!"

An instant before it hit, Subaru had a vision of himself with his skull split open by the fragment.
What would likely have been a fatal blow, however, never reached Subaru. A shadow cut in before it could hit him—Rui had become his shield.

"Rui――!"

Rui, clinging to his back, forcibly pushed Subaru's body down, moving him out of the fragment's flight path, and slipped into its line of fire herself instead.
As a result, Rui took the fragment defenseless, and was torn away from Subaru's body, sent flying.

"Subaru! Don't look away!"

As Rui went flying and Subaru's awareness threatened to follow her, Beatrice called out to him. Still entrusting her body to Subaru, she stretched out her hand and launched a follow-up attack on the enemy.
But the opponent wasn't easy enough to take a second fatal blow either.

"――ッ"

Sending the fragment of its arm flying through the air, the enemy skillfully read Beatrice's follow-up Minya and took it with what little was left of its left shoulder.
It had already offloaded the damage onto a part already crystallized, minimizing how far the cracking would spread.
That was, without question, the skill of a masterful warrior――

"Uwah"

The instant Subaru's eyes widened at that skill, his collar was seized by an arm stretching toward him.
It had let go of the war axe it had been gripping—no, it had lost the strength to swing the war axe—a manifestation of the enemy's desperate resolve not to let Subaru escape.

"Ohhhhhhh!!"

"Gah!"

Hauled in by brute strength, Subaru's body, cradling Beatrice, was forced down onto the ground. As the pain of his back slamming into the hard pavement knocked the breath out of him, he saw the enemy's face looming right up close as it bore down over him, and instinctively tried to bring his hand up to aim.
But his gaze locked with the enemy's death-determined expression, and his movement stopped.

――At that figure, one that shouldn't have belonged to a body already dead, brimming with fighting spirit and will to battle.

"――っ"

"Subaru!"
"Lord Schult!!"

His throat being strangled, desperate voices struck Subaru's eardrums as he gasped for breath.
Even as he heard those frantic voices, and the fatal creaking of his own neck bones, Subaru's arm still would not rise. It wasn't that his strength had been stolen from him—it was that his heart wouldn't rouse itself to act.

Even knowing that if he didn't act, he would be the one acted upon.
Even knowing that if he didn't kill, he would be the one killed.

"Minya!"

In place of the immobile Subaru, Beatrice, stirring, fired off her spell at the enemy.
Looming over Subaru meant looming over Beatrice in his arms as well. Naturally, the magic struck at what could only be called point-blank range.
And yet the enemy endured it. ――No, it protected only its right arm, the one strangling Subaru's neck, at all costs, willing to let its torso be crystallized so long as it could at least take Subaru down with it.

"This is what comes of showing strange mercy."

The moment those flat, detached words were spoken, the enemy—about to destroy Subaru with eyes burning like fire—had its head severed at the neck.
In its place, appearing on the far side of where the vanished enemy's head had been, was Todd, his face cold.

"――――"

Todd, having swung the axe in his hand through in a full arc to sever the zombie's neck.
The severed head, cut off by Todd, tried to direct its hatred at him, but,

"How dare you!"

—Tanza's kick, leaping in, sent that head flying without mercy. The single-eyed head bounced down the street with all the momentum of a soccer ball.
And then, left behind, abandoned by its own sent-flying head, the torso remaining on the spot slowly turned entirely into violet-indigo crystal, and crumbled.

"Are you unharmed, Lord Schult!"

"……geh, cough, y-yeah, I'm fine. Sorry, thanks for that."

Shaking off the violet-indigo fragments of the enemy's crumbled torso, Subaru raised himself up and lifted a hand to Tanza, who came running over, letting her know he was all right.
At Subaru's answer, Tanza relaxed with relief—and then Subaru's expression turned to a start.

"Rui! Where's Rui? She protected me……"

"She should be fine. Though it seems the fragment hit her, and she's taken a concussion."

The one who answered the flustered Subaru was Idra, crouching a little ways off.
At his feet, Rui lay collapsed; her upper body propped up, blood flowed from her forehead, and her head lolled unsteadily.

"A head wound is bad news……! Beatrice, please!"

"――. Of course, I know that, I suppose."

"Please……!"

Taking in Rui's state, Subaru hastily got to his feet and pulled Beatrice's arm along. There was a moment's hesitation in her manner, but Subaru didn't notice it.

Beatrice was the only one on this battlefield who could use healing magic.
The other user, Rem, had been asked—so that the all-out battle against a formidable enemy could proceed without worry—to circle around the battle zone toward the city together with the non-combatants.
Since they'd drawn the formidable enemy away from the city wall, there was a good chance those with her could reach outside the city safely, but here, it had created a shortage of hands for the role of healer.

"Rui, it's going to be all right, okay……!"

Crouching beside Rui together with Beatrice, Subaru held Rui's hand as Beatrice held her hand over the wound on Rui's forehead, activating her healing magic.
Weakly, she squeezed back, and Subaru repeated an apology, murmuring, "Sorry……"
An apology was all he could offer. What had just happened had been entirely Subaru's blunder.

"So that girl can use healing magic too. There sure are a lot of rare talents around you."

It was Todd who spoke up as Subaru watched over Rui's treatment.
Resting on his shoulder the axe he'd used to sever the enemy's neck, he fixed his gaze on Subaru's faintly tensed profile, and,

"And yet with you in that state, it's a waste of talent. You're aware of that, aren't you?"

"Todd……"

"Anyone who entrusts their certainty that they won't die to something other than their own strength is fragile."

"――――"

"That logic doesn't just apply to the Cyclops I just killed. It applies to you too."

The reason Subaru couldn't talk back to his flat manner of speaking was, unmistakably, because he'd been hit right where it hurt.
The instant he'd witnessed that enemy shaking off imminent defeat, clinging to victory, weakness had crossed Subaru's mind. Just as Todd said, it could be called the result of being overwhelmed by a craving for life.
It wasn't as though Subaru was insensitive enough to brush that off as unavoidable, but――

"Isn't that kind of talk rather rude?"

"――――"

"Lord Schult is the one who made this battle possible. To say that to him after everything he's done……"

"Don't snap at me. I'm giving him credit where it's due. I'm just saying it wasn't enough. Also, the biggest contributor here is probably you, or that girl lying over there."

Jerking his chin toward them as he said it, it was Tanza whose cheeks stiffened at Todd's words.
The girl who had shouldered a major role against a formidable enemy paid no mind to the praise directed at herself, worrying instead about Subaru, at whom the heartless words had been aimed.

Though her expression showed little change, the feelings she kept hidden within ran deep, if anything.
Ever since the Sword Slave Isle, having deepened her bond with Subaru no small amount, that she'd show a stance of getting angry on Subaru's behalf at being insulted was understandable.
The fact that Katya, Todd's fiancée, wasn't present here was also lending force to the sharpness of her tone.

"Do you think you're always right about everything? If that's the case……"

"So don't snap at me. What I said is just fact. There are freak monsters out there certain they won't die because of overwhelming strength, and there are people who think they won't die for some other reason. ――Neither you nor I are either one of those."

"That's……"

"When it's the former on your side, things go smoothly, but you don't want the latter on your side. If it's an enemy, it becomes an opening to exploit, like just now. Your kinsman's strategy was on the mark."

"――ッ, don't you dare talk about Lord Schult that――"

Perhaps she'd meant to snap, don't speak of him that way.
Her face flushing faintly with anger, Tanza moved to bite back at Todd. But before Subaru could try to stop her, Idra cut in first.
Still supporting Rui's body, he called her name, "Tanza,"

"That's enough. It's not like you. Being calm no matter what is your strong suit, isn't it?"

"……I'm simply bad at expressing fluctuations of emotion. Besides, surely you understand as well, Lord Idra."

"Understand what, exactly……"

"I and Lord Idra are connected to Lord Schult. However he's feeling, it should be conveyed to us, at least somewhat."

"――――"

At Tanza's protest, still with her back turned, Idra knit his brow and fell silent. Seeing that expression on Idra head-on, Subaru couldn't help catching his breath.
The blessing of the Pleiades Battalion receiving unprecedented empowerment through the effect of Subaru's "Cor Leonis" was a boon, but for the members with a deep connection—Idra and the others from the same "Gou," and Tanza, whose ties ran deeper than the rest—such shortcomings, too, got conveyed.

There was some measure of relief in the fact that Tanza and the others didn't regard that as a failing or a moral fault of Subaru's.
At the same time, there was also a feeling of shame, and a feeling that it still wasn't enough.
Just as Subaru was thinking that——

"Ah—, u"

Suddenly, squeezing back the hand Subaru held, Rui let out a faint voice.
As always, he had no idea what detail her still-unformed words meant. Only that she was worried about Subaru could be understood. Even though she was the one who'd been hurt.

"If she can talk, there's no need to worry, I suppose. Once treatment's finished, we should carry her outside and have Rem take a look at her, I'd say."

"Right. Yeah, that's right. We've finally got a way opened up. If we dawdle here……"

It was just then, as he braced against the very self-defeating possibility of other zombies gathering,

——that a strange sound cut through the wind—fwip.

"――――"

It was the sort of sound you'd hear from swinging a cord with something light tied to it, but what happened right after that sound of wind being cut was by no means light.

"Kah"

With a short, choked groan, Tanza's body trembled.
And as her trembling body looked down at itself, her round eyes went wide. ――Piercing through her back and stomach, something like a sharp-tipped tentacle jutted out.

The tentacle that had run through Tanza's small body stretched from a place much farther away than the rest of them, from the direction of a street collapsed by the aftershocks of battle.
The tentacle, stretching out like a writhing snake—its point of origin was——

"……huh?"

——there stood the figure of the zombie, an abnormal torso sprouting from the head that had been kicked away, tentacle extended toward its target.

"――――"

For an instant, everything froze; a sight that trampled all over imagination and understanding dyed Subaru's thoughts blank.
But so that instant would not prove fatal, a voice rose up.

"Subaru!"

Beside him, Beatrice, her face drained of color, cried out, and it was that which dragged Subaru's awareness back to reality.
Hearing a sound like blood rushing to his brain, Subaru once more caught the sight before his eyes—the single-eyed zombie standing amid the destroyed street, that enemy now grotesquely transformed—in his gaze.

From the neck up, it was the same as before—a black eye with a golden pupil floating in it.
But below the neck was no longer the sturdy body of a warrior; instead, both arms and both legs, each pair mismatched in thickness and length from its counterpart, formed a monster that could only be called grotesque.
From its right arm, the monster had a tentacle—no, that wasn't right. It wasn't a tentacle; it was a finger. One of the monster's outstretched fingers had grotesquely elongated, and it was that which had pierced Tanza's body.

"――う"

The instant he grasped that, the monster withdrew its long finger, and Tanza's body shuddered once more.
Her torso pierced clean through, her body spurted blood and sank powerlessly to its knees on the spot.

"Tanza――!!"

"――っ! This is bad! Schult!"

The instant Subaru cried that out, and had half-started to run forward with his hand outstretched, Idra's palm, thrust out from directly beside him, shoved him with all its force.
From that empowered shove of Idra's, Subaru's body went sailing lightly through the air. But his reversing field of view immediately caught proof that Idra's shove had been the right call.

Right where Subaru had been standing an instant before, the monster's whip-like finger struck.
The terrifying sharpness that cleaved the street and buildings along its path vertically, destroying the city—had he not been shoved by Idra, Subaru's body might have been cut in two as well.
And then, Subaru's body, sent flying with force――

"Tch! Don't tell me it regenerated from the severed head."

——was forcibly hauled in by an arm reaching up from directly below, Todd holding Subaru fast.
Stiffening at Todd's voice raining down from overhead, Subaru muttered, "No way," at the meaning of his words—regeneration from a severed head.

"We crystallized the torso and smashed it, and the thing's still moving!? How is that even possible!?"

"Even if I don't want it to be possible, it's happening right in front of us, so I've got no choice but to accept it. That creepy appearance is……"

"The technique for regenerating its body has bugged out, I suppose!"

To Todd's question, as he held Subaru, it was Beatrice who offered up the answer.
Her cute face harboring anxiety, she glared at the grotesque monster, and,

"The result of it refusing death from damage that should, by rights, have already killed it, I'd say!"

"――. In other words, greedier for life than the other zombies……no, wait, they're already dead, so greedier for death might be more accurate."

"Guh……! Beatrice! Idra! Hurry, get to Tanza……!"

At Subaru's desperate plea, Idra clenched his teeth, raising his voice.
He was worried about the wounded Tanza just as much as Subaru was. But moving carelessly would draw the monster's attention. As they'd just seen, the monster's attacks already reached the Pleiades Battalion.
If Idra were taken down too, their chance of winning would vanish.
That was――

"No, to begin with……"

Rui had been wounded taking the fragment, and now Tanza too was, at a glance, critically wounded.
The zombie they'd thought they'd defeated hadn't been finished off after all—worse, it had transformed into this grotesque thing and now stood in their way. Wasn't this, at this point, entering a hopeless loop with no way out?

"――Don't talk nonsense."

Subaru cut off the foolish thought that had begun to cross his mind for an instant.
There were times when you ran into situations in the dead-end box of fate that a loop could never break through. Todd, holding him now, had in fact once brought about just such a situation for Subaru.
But Subaru was here. ――More than that, Subaru had been saved.

He could give up on himself, but he couldn't give up on everyone else.

"――What a revolting pair of eyes."

"Huh?"

"The eyes of someone who values his own life differently from everyone else's."

Subaru, having audibly shifted gears within himself. Watching that expression of Subaru's up close, what was going on in Todd's heart as he muttered that, Subaru couldn't tell.
There, unmistakably, should have been distaste bleeding through.

"Let's go."

The instant he said that shortly, Todd, still holding Subaru, kicked off the ground hard.
And—of all things—turning his back on both Tanza and Beatrice and the others, he set about fleeing the scene as fast as he could.

"Wha!? What are you doing!? If we split up from everyone else……"

"You'd better take a good look yourself. That thing's after the two of us."

Saying so, Todd tilted his body and dove into a side street.
In that instant, a finger was loosed as if to bore through the street, grazing Todd's afterimage, and the smell of scorched air tickled Subaru's nostrils.

For an instant, he caught a glimpse.
That grotesque thing, its single eye glowing with malevolent light, chasing after them.

"Apparently the targets are me, the one it killed the first time, and you, the one who killed it a second time."

"……! So that's why you drew it off, to get it away from everyone else?"

"If others get caught up in it, your head stops working right."

Saying that, Todd looked down at Subaru with those green eyes of his.
Being shaken about as he was held, Subaru, pinned by that ice-cold gaze, felt a chill run down his spine equal to or greater than what the pursuing monster inspired.
Even in this situation, the eyes Todd turned on Subaru were nothing more than an appraisal.

"Can you think of a way to take that thing down without a partner?"

"And if I can't?"

"Then, much as I'd hate it, we'd have no choice but to leave it to luck."

Subaru sensed that this by no means meant exactly what the words said.
Given that the conditions for being targeted were the same for himself and Subaru, if things turned unfavorable, Todd would surely take measures to use Subaru as bait and escape. That he wasn't doing so right now was because the odds of getting away even using Subaru as bait were low.
Put the other way around――

"――If there's a way, you'll lend a hand, is that it?"

"――――"

At Subaru's question, Todd's expression tightened again.
Skillfully making use of obstacles, hiding from the monster's line of sight, continuing to dodge its attacks—Todd kept it up. Still held by him, Subaru swept his gaze around, searching within the imperial capital for the building he had in mind.

It was a building that had come up in conversation from Idra's mouth on the way as they fled.
A plan he'd thought up separately from the strategy of hitting it with Minya—one he'd struck from the options because he had no way to lure the enemy all the way to that building――

"Can you pull it off?"

"――I'll do it."

"――――"

At the instant answer to his question, Todd's eyes widened slightly, and then the corner of his mouth twisted into a cruel, sneering smile.
And then――

"Good. ――I'll ride along with your plan."

――Chasing after the fleeing targets, the thoughts of "Giant Eye" Izmail were scattered in a thousand directions.

"――――"

His vision flickered on and off, his thoughts scattered to pieces, his movements a clumsy thing far removed from anything refined.
Dragging limbs of differing lengths against the ground, with the unsteadiness of a newborn beast, colliding against walls and against the ground, he stretched out his hand, his finger, toward the retreating backs.

Why he clung so obsessively to the two fleeing enemies, Izmail could no longer recall.
The grotesque monster—who was already on the verge of forgetting he had ever even been Izmail—grew new limbs from the cracking wounds each time it fell, its hideousness increasing all the more with each one.

――That was, precisely, the runaway regeneration ability that Beatrice had pointed out.

But this mutation, occurring in the body of Izmail—resurrected by another's will, and exceptionally kept far from death—was something not even the caster himself could control.
Once it had finally cut down the two targets it clung to, what would become of the monster then?

"Ohhhhhhhh……"

Something of utterly no interest whatsoever to the monster, letting out a hollow, ominous howl.

"――――"

The two targets—the bigger one carrying the smaller one, fleeing—kept forcing repeated near-misses, its backs nearly caught only to slip away, attacks that never landed.
But the opponent couldn't keep fleeing forever. From continuing its ungainly run, new limbs kept sprouting wherever the falling body had cracked, and now Izmail no longer fell.
Since it no longer fell, it stood to reason that the one with more limbs would run faster.
And so, Izmail closed the distance to the targets trying to escape, little by little, little by little, little by little, little by little, little by little, closing, closing, closing, closing――

"Aoh"

With a short roar, Izmail's finger gouged into the fleeing target's back, its shoulder.
Blood sprayed, the enemy's scream sounded, and a savage grin rose on Izmail's face. It felt good. It was fun, so much fun, he wanted more—more blood, more screams, he wanted to hear them.

Turning down streets at random, more than before, he sensed the presence of the opponent growing desperate, unlike before.
Chased by Izmail, caught by the monster, hating the thought of being torn to shreds, the target fled desperately. Its limbs, its upper body especially, were targeted, wounded.
If he aimed for the legs, the target would no longer be able to flee. And then, this would end.

If it ended, he'd be lonely. If it ended, it would be a shame.
So, forever and ever, so that it would never end――

"Ohhhhhhhhn"

Letting out a low howl that shook the very air, Izmail smashed through buildings, crushing streets underfoot, kicking off the water-soaked ground in pursuit of the targets.
The distance closed all at once, and at last, whether the fleeing enemy's stamina had run dry, bleeding, clicking his tongue, he dove into a nearby building.

A foolish choice, diving into a dead end with no way out.
Sensing intuitively that this was the end of the hunting hour, Izmail—no, the monster that was no longer Izmail—smashed through the closed door and slipped inside.
With limbs multiplied, its bulk swollen far beyond comparison to its original body, it couldn't simply open the door and enter the building smoothly.

And then, entering the dim building the target had fled into, the monster looked around inside.
Light was scarce, but even with this much transformation, that special eye—the one thing that hadn't changed—switched how it perceived the world with a blink, searching for the hidden prey.
Searching, the monster noticed.

"――――"

――Inside the building it had dived into, powder was drifting thick enough to dye the monster's entire body white.

It wasn't only the darkness making his vision uncertain—it was because powder was drifting, filling the inside of the building. Looking closer, rows of shelves lined the interior, and on every shelf sat sacks packed full to bursting.
Those sacks had been torn open, sending a great cloud of powder billowing up inside the building.

"What's gonna beat you isn't magic, and it ain't some finishing move."

The powder-covered monster noticed the state of the building's interior at the same moment that voice came from behind it. In the turning monster's field of view was a small, crimson-red shadow.
It was one of the fleeing targets—no, at some point, the fleeing target had become just one. Separated from the bigger one, the smaller one, whose form had gone unseen, was there.
And then――

"Eat this—the essence of science: a dust explosion!!"

The instant those words rang out, the area around the small shadow was all at once dyed red as well――

――A tremendous explosion blew the flour mill to pieces, and crimson flame swallowed the monster whole.

△▼△▼△▼△

"――Look, Schult, let's use that flour mill as a marker. The building with the waterwheel beside it."

Idra, leading the non-combatants and keeping watch on their surroundings while driving the Galewind Horse, had apparently been the son of a flour miller before being sent to the Sword Slave Isle.
Subaru wasn't precisely familiar with what sort of work a flour miller's job entailed either, but he'd been told it was something that used the power of a waterwheel to mill wheat and the like into flour.
In other words――

"If we use the materials inside the mill, we can set off a dust explosion."

A dust explosion is a phenomenon where flammable powder is scattered through the air, and a spark flying into that ignites it in a chain reaction, all at once catching fire and exploding.
Add to a flour mill's enclosed space a large amount of wheat flour drifting through the air, and then have a spark fly into that with clear force behind it――

"……I never imagined it would have this much destructive power."

Leaning his back against a wall left standing, Subaru muttered that between coughs.
Before Subaru's eyes now was the flour mill, blown to bits by the dust explosion that had come off, and the remains of the monster, caught in the blast and burned to nothing.

When it had come down to him and Todd, the two of them, needing to lure the monster in and finish it off, the plan that had crossed Subaru's mind was this dust explosion using the flour mill.
Of course, a dust explosion wasn't something that succeeded easily, and to begin with, there'd been no certainty they could successfully lure the opponent in as planned.
So the fact that it had gone this well wasn't due to Subaru's power alone.

"……You alive, Todd?"

Blown back by the shock of the explosion, Subaru shook his head, his vision still flickering with spots, and stood up.
In the end, the division of labor had gone like this: until the flour mill was ready, Todd had taken on the role of luring the monster and running circles around it, while Subaru had secretly slipped into the mill and filled the interior with the dust. Then, once Todd judged the timing right, he'd dive into the mill, luring the monster in, and a spark would be thrown in. Todd had prepared that spark, too.

"Todd, Todd……!"

"……You don't have to call for me in that mournful a voice. I'm alive and well."

"――っ"

Calling out around the blast site, where the smoldering remains still asserted their presence, Subaru heard a faintly hoarse voice and turned his feet that way.
Looking, he found Todd down on one knee, on a street where the blown-away mill wall had collapsed. Seeing Subaru approaching, he spat the saliva from his mouth, and,

"You nearly killed me along with it, you know that, you."

"……That's slander. I did tell you it'd be incredible force, didn't I?"

"Accidents like that do happen every now and then, but hearing the principle behind it, I get it now."

Breathing ragged as one would expect, Todd plopped himself down onto the street. Still like that, gazing over at the blown-apart mill, he asked,

"The enemy?"

"Blown up. Properly gone……look."

Saying so, Subaru pointed at a part of the blown-apart monster visible from just where they were standing. As he did, the part being scorched by the remaining fire crumbled like sand, losing its shape entirely.
Just like the other zombies' manner of scattering—the result of having completely crushed its regenerative ability.

"And your wounds?"

Confirming this time, for certain, that they'd defeated that formidable zombie enemy, Subaru asked Todd.
In dividing up the roles, since they couldn't afford to be caught by the enemy, there'd been no other option but for Todd—who could keep fleeing for a while—to serve as the decoy. But he'd volunteered for that role himself.
Betraying Subaru's expectation that he would absolutely avoid any plan that endangered his own life.

"If I go around treasuring every scratch and end up losing my life over it, that'd be pointless, wouldn't it. We each just did the job that was needed. That said……"

"Ugh……"

"I got gouged in quite a few places. I'm feeling pretty short on blood."

Saying so, Todd raised one hand, and the extent of his injuries was considerable.
Though there was no wound deep enough to call severe, blood oozed damply at his shoulder and thigh, and his face, too, had lost its color from the amount of blood lost.
Left untreated, it would undoubtedly endanger his life.

"I'll go get――"

Subaru moved to fetch Beatrice.
With Subaru and Todd having broken off from the group back there, Beatrice was surely in a panic by now. But she was the sort who'd surely prioritize tending to Tanza first.
He was worried about both Rui's and Tanza's safety too, and needed to hurry to rejoin Beatrice and the others.
And then――

"――After doing this much, even Katya can't have any complaints, I'd say."

"Huh?"

"I told you back at the mansion. She seems to think I'm a lazy bum. I got everyone to safety, took down a formidable enemy properly, and got an honorable wound to boot. You'd better not go and hog all the credit for yourself, you hear?"

Shrugging, saying that with a face drained of blood, Todd left Subaru stunned for a moment, and then he let out a breath, deflated of tension, with a "Ha."
Even in this battered a state, what he still bothered to worry about was Katya. Or maybe it had been an attempt to loosen Subaru's tension too. Either way,

"You really do treasure Katya-san, don't you."

"Of course. She's my fiancée. Katya is my life itself."

Todd said it flatly, without a shred of embarrassment.
At that answer, Subaru took a deep breath, held it partway, and psyched himself up with a "All right."

Even now, Todd still scared him. What he'd done had left a wound on Subaru's heart that remained.
Even so, the fact that they'd cooperated to bring down that monster here, too, didn't change. Battered to pieces, Todd had given everything for the sake of everyone's, of Subaru and the others', survival.
If that was so, then this time, it was Subaru's turn.

"Here……no, wait, if we do it here, someone might hear the explosion and come. Let's head back partway together for now. Once we find somewhere decent, wait there for me."

"Ah, got it. Just, please, don't make it so you never come back."

"I wouldn't do that!"

That was what Todd had made him feel like he could get away without doing.
Though, even if he'd still been standing in a hostile position toward Todd, whether Subaru could have left him there half-dead—that, too, was a doubtful matter.

"No, overthinking it just digs the hole deeper. Anyway――"

Even lending Todd his shoulder, their difference in height was too great to make it work well. If nothing else, if there was something he could use in place of a cane, Subaru turned his eyes to the area around the blown-apart mill.
And then, finding among the smoldering remains a support post blown off a shelf――

"――――"

――In that instant, a hard sound rang out.

"……Why."

The instant that hard sound rang out, Subaru hung his head, clenching his fist tight. His lips trembling, his shoulders shaking, his black eyes trembling too, he murmured.

"Why."

"――――"

There was no answer to the question, and Subaru let out a breath and turned around.
Before Subaru's turned-around eyes, the blade of a swung-down axe had stopped its motion just barely short of crushing Subaru's skull.

――Held by the restraint of the "Unseen Hand," invisible to anyone but Subaru.

"Tch, missed, missed."

Clicking his tongue softly like that, Todd let go of the axe and leapt backward. From that agile movement, no trace of blood loss or injury could be felt, and Subaru gritted his teeth.
Even that had been an act. The agony of his wounds, seeking Subaru's help, and even talking about his feelings for Katya, softening Subaru's heart—all of it.

"Why, damn it……!"

Asking the same question a third time, Subaru glared at Todd with a face on the verge of tears.
Glaring at him, feeling tears welling up from sheer frustration,

"Even now, I haven't done anything yet. I haven't killed anyone. I could have forgiven you!!"

"Slowly, quietly, you eroded my trust."

Listening to Subaru's cry, in place of the axe he'd let go of, Todd drew a knife and readied it.
That was the declaration of an irreparable parting between Natsuki Subaru and Todd Fang.
