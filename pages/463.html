Arc 6 Chapter 58: "That's That, This Is This"

—Let's search the "Taygeta" archives for any book of Reid's.

That was the matter Subaru wanted to propose as necessary for clearing the second floor, the most immediate goal in conquering this tower.
The moment they heard it, the expressions of the group sitting in a circle turned puzzled.

"A book? ...Are you serious?"

"Oh, Emilia-chan, that was a book-serious pun there. Nice one."

"Ugh, Subaru!"

Emilia arched her brows at Subaru's reaction, her cute cheeks reddening with anger. Feeling a swell of happiness at her adorableness, Subaru said "Listen up" and looked around at everyone's faces.

"Finding Reid's book — I think that's the fastest way for us to clear the second floor. Doesn't everyone agree?"

"You said that before, but why? It's not that I'm against looking for a book. I just don't understand why it has to be Reid's book. And besides..."

"—To begin with, is there really a book of Reid Astrea's in that archive?"

At Subaru's proposal, Emilia tilted her head, and Julius picked up the latter half of her thought.
Drawing everyone's gaze, Julius lifted his eyes—framed by long lashes—toward the ceiling, as if peering through it at the second floor that lay beyond.

"Hard as it is to believe, the illustrious hero who left his name in history, Reid Astrea, awaits our challenge on the second floor under the guise of a 'trial.' That he existed four hundred years ago, and that he is one and the same person, is beyond doubt... but his death is, at present, a matter of some question in my mind."

"Seeing him so lively, you're wondering if he actually never died? I hadn't really considered that theory much, but I guess it's not impossible..."

In truth, Subaru only had his knowledge from Emilia and the others that Reid was a dead man from centuries past.
If you met him without knowing the circumstances, you'd never believe Reid was a dead man. To begin with, he was far too full of vitality. An excessively energetic corpse.

"Still, that's a theory we can basically set aside. I doubt anyone could live hundreds of years — normally he'd be dead by now. Right, Beatrice?"

"Betty, for one, has lived four hundred years, I suppose."

"I'm about a hundred years old too, I think?"

"For me as well, counting from my year of birth, it'd be around four hundred years. Though the time I've actually spent awake is short."

"Ashi too! Ashi too, Master! Ashi's been waiting here for four hundred years too! I was lonely! I demand four hundred years' worth of hugs!"

"There sure are a lot of long-lived characters around here!? Emilia-chan too!?"

He'd meant to ask for agreement, and instead his mouth fell open at the unexpected pushback.
He never imagined half of his party members would turn out to be long-lived characters. The average age of the party shot up at a rate that was no laughing matter.
Especially Emilia's and Beatrice's ages left his jaw hanging.
Still, there was a part of him that thought, ah, that makes sense.

"R-Right. Emilia-chan is a half-elf... no wonder she's such a devastatingly beautiful girl. Half-elves being beautiful and long-lived, that's practically a given, isn't it."

"Um, yeah, that's right. ...Subaru, even without your memories, half-elves don't scare you?"

"Scary or not scary, if I had to say — that cuteness is scary. Seriously a lethal weapon. If I saw it half-asleep with my guard down, I feel like it'd blind me. Even now, honestly, my eyes are kind of stinging."

"...Ugh, idiot."

Scolded by a faintly blushing Emilia, Subaru somehow felt like that had been a good moment just now, and cautioned himself against making such a foolish misunderstanding.
Emilia was so kind that if he wasn't careful, he might start thinking she had feelings for him. Don't waver, my heart. Don't flutter, my heart. No wait, fluttering is fine.

"Haah... in that regard, being around you feels like coming home to my parents' house, Beatrice."

"Somehow that doesn't sit quite right with me. ...Well, but since you're petting my head, I suppose I'll let it slide."

The heartbeat Emilia had thrown into disarray settled down as he stroked Beatrice's head. Beatrice's mood seemed to improve too, so it was killing two birds with one stone.
As that digression played out, Echidna raised a hand and said, "May I?"

"I don't dismiss Julius's concern, but I'd like to make the case, plainly, for the theory that Reid really is dead. Just an impression I got from meeting the man himself."

"And your reasoning?"

"First, as Natsuki-kun says, I don't think Reid Astrea could be a long-lived species. He's an outlandish figure in many respects, but he's human, all the same. Second, his personality."

"Personality? You mean, that super-energetic side of him?"

"I'd call it energy tipping over into recklessness. I can't imagine a man with that kind of exuberant personality staying quietly cooped up in a tower for four hundred years. Given he never even met her — Shaula — that would mean he stayed in that one room on the second floor the entire time. Very hard to believe. Just my own impression, but I'd wager he'd have walked out within three days."

"Ahh."

At Echidna's shrugged-off opinion, Subaru let out a sound mixing agreement and resignation.
Emilia and the others exchanged glances too, recalling Reid's restless demeanor. They seemed to find Echidna's opinion credible as well.
Seeing this, Echidna turned her pale blue-green gaze to Julius.

"So, that's the impression from my end — but will you accept it, Julius?"

"I can hardly do otherwise. Indeed, thinking of the actual impression Reid Astrea gives — he does not strike one as a man content to linger long in one place. That he cannot do so should be attributed, I suppose, to his present self being bound to the tower's 'trial'..."

"Bound to the tower, his very existence, huh."

Listening to Echidna and Julius talk, Subaru recalled how the previous loop had ended.
Amid the chaos that had engulfed the tower, Reid had wandered about freely, even boasting he'd head outside. Without a shred of hesitation, he'd dropped down from the second floor to the fourth, even going so far as to flick the back of Subaru's head.
To Subaru, that hadn't looked remotely like a man whose freedom was bound.

In fact, if not for one last lingering regret, Reid surely would have marched straight out of the tower on those very legs. —The reason he hadn't...

"—? What is it? Something on my face?"

"No..."

"Heh. I believe I too have eyes, a nose, ears, and a mouth on my face — is something amiss with them?"

"Ah, unlike Emilia-chan's, yours aren't cute, so you didn't make the cut. Anyway..."

The conversation had strayed way too far from the main topic, Subaru thought, and looked away from Julius.
Then he brought the subject back around to the original matter — "Reid's book."

"So, now that we've settled on 'that lively guy is a dead man' as our working theory, back to the main topic. 'Taygeta' has the 'Book of the Dead,' that much's not in question either, right?"

"That's the working assumption for now. When someone reads it, the deceased's memories flow into their head... that much Barusu and Julius confirmed. Unfortunately, it seems those memories slipped away too."

"My bad, don't hold a grudge. —Anyway, that's the crux of this."

Snapping his fingers, Subaru pointed the tip at Ram. Perhaps his gesture was irritating, because Ram twisted the pointed finger, and Subaru let out a pained "Gwaah!"
Amid that exchange, Beatrice let out a small "Ah."

"I see, is that how it is, I suppose!"

"Beatrice, you understood what Subaru's getting at?"

"I understood, I suppose. I see, is that how it is. —In short, it means using Reid's 'Book of the Dead' as a guidebook to secrets for conquering Reid, I suppose."

"That's it."

Waving his twisted finger, Subaru affirmed Beatrice with a wicked grin.
Hearing the explanation, Emilia widened her violet eyes and murmured, "I see."

—Using the "Book of the Dead" to conquer the deceased person himself.

Put simply, the "Book of the Dead" was, at the same time as being a record of that person's life, also a "strategy guide" meticulously recording just how that person had died.
And speaking as a veteran player of "death" who'd already died four times over, Subaru could say this much — a cause of death is not something easily sidestepped.

"So if we read the 'Book of the Dead,' we'll learn the guy's cause of death. That's a legitimate strategy right there. Maybe the 'Book of the Dead' exists for indirectly that very purpose, who knows?"

"That's... a blind spot I hadn't considered. But now that you mention it, indeed. They went out of their way to place a dead man as the examiner. It wouldn't be strange if that were the aim all along."

"Well, I mean, you don't have to take it that seriously..."

Subaru gave a wry laugh at Echidna, who widened her eyes and showed more admiration than he'd expected.
Still, since it was hardly a situation likely to come up often, it was genuinely a tricky call whether to treat this as a strategy or classify it as a loophole.

"But there's one thing I can say with confidence — even the me who has no memories right now, and the me who did have memories, I think either version of me would've tried this approach."

"That's... believable, I suppose. There's no way Subaru would pass up a loophole like this, I suppose."

"Not the righteous path, the crooked one. Very much a Barusu thing to do. Ram approves as well."

"Mm, that's right. Subaru's really, really good at this kind of underhanded trick."

"'Underhanded trick,' now there's a phrase you don't hear these days..."

"—!"

Scratching his cheek at the unwavering assessment of "Natsuki Subaru," Subaru was startled when Emilia's eyes suddenly lit up. But she immediately pinched her own cheek with her fingers and said,

"Oh no, mustn't, mustn't. Subaru's the one going through the hardest time. I need to keep it together..."

"Emilia-sama, I understand the feeling, but your cheek will turn red."

Ram took Emilia's hand, which had suddenly started tormenting herself, and cautioned her against it.
There had been these oddly sensitive reactions from Emilia and Beatrice a few times now, and it was probably because there, somewhere, they sensed a trace of the "Natsuki Subaru" they knew.
Honestly, it made him feel bad. He wanted to give Emilia and the others back their "Natsuki Subaru" as soon as possible.

Anyway, just as Emilia and the others' impression suggested, it was highly likely that the Subaru of up until yesterday would have arrived at this same strategy using the "Book of the Dead."
Hearing Subaru's train of thought, Meili put a hand to her mouth and said, "Ah,"

"...Now that you mention it, last night I saw big brother spreading out a bunch of books in 'Taygeta.' Was that what that was about, I wonder?"

"Last night, Subaru was in 'Taygeta'? Hmm..."

"By the way, could you not see what books I was reading?"

"Um... I couldn't tell that much. Sorry."

Sitting on Shaula's lap, being petted vigorously on the head, Meili lowered her eyes.
Subaru waved a hand at her, saying "Don't worry about it," and carefully cross-referenced this against the memories of "I" that weren't his own, arriving at the same conclusion.

In the memories of Meili he'd confirmed via the "Book of the Dead," there had been a scene of Subaru spreading out multiple books, but nothing beyond that — no detailed content.
So it had been a long-shot question, and his disappointment wasn't severe. Still, the fact that he'd been in "Taygeta's" archive at all confirmed that something had happened to "Natsuki Subaru" around that time.
Either right before spreading out the books, or perhaps a little after—

"Or perhaps the act itself is related to the loss of memory?"

"Spreading out a lot of books, hm. ...Don't tell me — could it be that by reading more 'Books of the Dead' than his capacity could hold, the space to store memories filled up and overflowed?"

"I'd like to think not, but I can't rule it out. After all, amnesia!"

Puffing out his chest and pointing at himself, Subaru drew a simultaneous sigh from Julius and Echidna.
He was getting rather good at brazening out his amnesia, but Subaru himself didn't doubt much that the cause of his memory loss lay in a book.
So even if he did find Reid's "Book of the Dead," he wanted to avoid having anyone else read it.
If someone were to read it, it should be Subaru — whose memory had already been erased once and would suffer less from being erased again — no, wait, now Subaru too had far too many memories he couldn't afford to lose.

"————"

The events of the previous loop, the events that had occurred over all the loops before that.
And on top of that, what he'd resolved in his heart this time around, his promise with Ram, his vow to Meili.

What was this — he'd only repeated a mere few days four times over, and already there were so many things he mustn't forget, his arms were already full of them.
That was why memories were precious, hard to let go of. —They must not be forgotten.

"Subaru?"

"Ah, ah — I'm fine, fine, all good good. Um, ahem."

Emilia called out to his suddenly silent profile, and Subaru hastily put on a composed face.
Then he cleared his throat, surveyed everyone's faces, and said,

"So anyway, I propose we head to 'Taygeta.' Guys who occupy the position of past hero or legendary so-and-so — their great deeds get passed down to later generations. But that also means their failures and weaknesses get left behind too, and we're going to take that as the price of fame and dig right in."

"You sound awfully confident, saying things like you'd already know."

"Heh heh, I never imagined I'd actually get a chance to use this strategy in real life. If we learn the guy's true name, we can pin down his cause of death and his relic weapon too. This is what you call modern-knowledge cheating...!"

"True name?"

"What's a relic weapon...?"

At Subaru's words, delivered with a tightly clenched fist, Emilia and Beatrice tilted their heads in unison.
That part was just idle rambling, so he wouldn't explain in detail, but there was no doubt that in this world, Reid Astrea was a person famous enough to have left his name in history.

"In other words, his weakness is the price of fame... isn't that a pretty novel weak point?"

"—. I understand Barusu's aim. I accept it too. Not that I'm without misgivings."

"It's worth trying, at the very least. Yare yare. Just thinking about how difficult it'll be to find one specific volume among that vast a quantity of books makes my spirits sink already."

Letting Subaru's glib remark pass, Ram and Echidna rose to their feet. Following their lead, Emilia, Beatrice, Meili, and Shaula got up as well.
"Alright," Subaru said, slapping his knee and standing too, then looked at Julius, who was slow to follow.

"What's up. You against this?"

"...No. There's no other way forward either. I'll grant that it's effective, your proposal included."

"But you've got something on your mind?"

"—. This is a problem of my own. I'd rather you not concern yourself with it."

Shaking his head slowly, Julius too rose smoothly to his feet.
Being told not to worry and genuinely not worrying about it was an impossible task, so for Subaru it was, if anything, more than a little concerning—

"I'll set that aside for now. —By the way, aside from the 'Book of the Dead,' how famous is Reid, exactly? He seems like a pretty huge deal."

"It must have been an unusually vivid memory. Given how the memory came apart in fragments, it's surprising how much of it remains intact. ...Reid Astrea is one of the Three Great Heroes who defeated a 'Witch' who once existed."

"The 'Sage' Shaula, the 'Divine Dragon' Volcanica, and then the 'Sword Saint' Reid..."

"Not ashi — the 'Sage' is Master, y'know."

"By your logic, does that make me hundreds of years old too? I've had way too eventful a stretch of time between stepping out of the convenience store and waking up this morning..."

Taking Shaula's remarks at maybe ten percent value rather than fifty, Subaru dug deeper into the legend of Reid as told through the Three Great Heroes.
On that topic, Emilia and the others' gazes turned to Julius.
Receiving that gaze, Julius touched his fingers to his forelocks and said,

"Indeed, the legends Reid Astrea left across various lands are too numerous to count. Among the famous ones... a battle in which he slew a hundred dragons, and a record of six thousand consecutive victories, unbeaten, at the arena on the Sword-Slave Isle. There's also the odd tale of him besting something called an Oni god in a drinking contest."

"All of that sounded like nonsense to me, but after actually seeing the guy in person..."

"I sense no exaggeration in it. Knowing his strength, he was undeniably... no."

"—?"

"As far as I know, most of the anecdotes about him speak only of his extraordinary achievements. His humanity, his human failings, records of defeat — I have no memory of anything like that."

Brushing his forelocks aside with his fingers, Julius closed out his display of knowledge there.
Hearing that, Subaru felt a small shiver at the fact that no record of defeat remained. It would be one thing if it just hadn't survived, but — could it be he'd never lost, period?
A lifetime undefeated — entirely plausible, Subaru told himself with a shudder.

"Ah, we're here."

As the conversation reached its natural pause, they arrived at the room with the stairs leading to "Taygeta."
Straight up from here awaited the third floor, "Taygeta," the archive buried in "Books of the Dead," ready to welcome them—

"—Ram, could you lead everyone on ahead for a bit? I've got something I want to talk to Julius about."

"With Julius?"

Ram stopped and knit her brow at Subaru's proposal.
Julius seemed equally surprised at the words, but for the moment said nothing, offering no objection. Seeing that, Ram narrowed her pale pink eyes, peered into Subaru's black ones, and let out a breath.
A sigh that carried something like resignation, or perhaps acceptance, as she said,

"Don't take too long about it. If your memories end up dropping out like Barusu's by the time you come up, things will get out of hand."

"Don't say scary things like that. Though if Ram lost her memories and turned out all meek and demure, that'd be a sight to see..."

"Ram doesn't intend to forget anything else, not anymore."

"...Yeah. Please don't go getting close to any weird books, okay."

Leaving that behind, Ram shrugged and trudged up the stairs at the front of the group.
For now, if he left things to her, there was probably no worry of her making a wrong call. In terms of that kind of trust, among this group he rated Ram the highest.
And along those same lines—

"Meili, act like a lady, would you. Go hold Emilia-chan's hand or something."

"That's really uncalled for, y'know. You don't need to treat me like some troublemaker. Besides, I've already got the naked lady's hand gripping me too — feels like I'm captured without even being caught."

Puffing her cheeks out with a "hmph," Meili was led off toward "Taygeta," her right hand held by Shaula, her left by Emilia. Beatrice followed at her back, and Echidna, bringing up the rear, set a foot on the stairs and turned back.

"Natsuki-kun."

"Hm?"

"Go easy on him, would you."

Leaving that behind too, Echidna promptly climbed up to the floor above.
Watching her back go, Subaru scratched his head. It occurred to him that Echidna might have somehow seen right through his reason for keeping Julius behind.

And so, once the women had all cleared out of the area entirely, only Subaru and Julius were left standing before the stairs,

"So, what did you want to talk about? Going out of your way to send Emilia-sama and the others off ahead — I take it this is something significant?"

"Something significant... well, yeah. Probably."

"That's a rather noncommittal tone."

"It's the kind of thing that's hard to make committal, this is."

Standing with his back to the stairs, facing Julius, Subaru scratched at his black hair roughly.
The reason he'd called Julius to stay behind here was that there were a few things he needed to confirm. It was also a matter of confirming something that had snagged him during the earlier conversation—

"————"

"Subaru?"

"Wait. I'm still sorting through my head a little."

Untangling a thread of a problem more convoluted than he'd expected, Subaru mulled it over.
He'd left the book search to Emilia and the others and called Julius to stay behind. The biggest focus of that, naturally, remained fixed on "Reid Astrea," without wavering.
At the same time, what crossed Subaru's mind was a scene from the chaotic battle of the previous loop.

—The duel Reid, having gained his freedom, had chosen as his final unresolved matter: the one-on-one with Julius.

Even amid a situation crowded with interference from many quarters, that had truly been what deserved to be called a one-on-one duel. And yet, the gap in strength between them — likely due in part to a mental issue as well — had been absolute.
What's more, from what he'd heard, Julius had already fought Reid once before and suffered defeat. Even in that state, Reid had held some manner of fixation on Julius.
Just where, in the person that was Julius, that fixation lay — that felt to Subaru like it might be a key to reading Reid as well. —But how should he go about conveying that?

"Ah, so — how do you feel about Reid? Like the guy?"

"—. What meaning could that question possibly hold?"

"Nah, I was just trying to loosen up the stiff air a bit. The real question's phrased a little differently. — The real question is: do you intend to beat Reid?"

"—!"

Closing one eye as he said that, Subaru watched Julius's yellow eyes go wide. Seeing intense unease shaking within those eyes, Subaru let out a short breath.
There was a part of him that thought, just as I figured. But at the same time, there was also a part of him thinking, wait a second.

"Whether you're aware of it or not aside... it's not unreasonable to be spooked, given the situation. I've heard once you catch a losing habit, it's apparently hard to shake."

"Subaru, you..."

"Sorry. Honestly, I think I ought to spend as much time as it takes doing this and that to fix your caved-in heart properly. I think that, but we don't have that kind of time. You get it, right?"

"————"

At Subaru's question, Julius's cheeks stiffened, his breath catching.
Subaru and Julius had different notions of what "no time" meant, but even so, Julius carried a similar sense of urgency.
And surely, this was something the Subaru of up until yesterday hadn't been able to do. Something he couldn't say, out of consideration for this wounded man who hadn't even noticed the urgency in his own heart.
Probably, what "Natsuki Subaru" hadn't been able to do, Natsuki Subaru would do for him now.

Believing that would become, right now, a solid blow to break open this stalemate of a situation—

"I'll say it straight, Julius. Why? Because right now, I'm invincible."

"Invincible, is it... that's quite the bold claim."

"No baggage weighing me down, so I can take a big step forward. Watching you look at me, look at Echidna, hear about Reid, and shrink into yourself — I can't stand by and watch that. I'm the type to get dragged down by things too, so I'm hardly one to talk, but I'm going to shut my eyes to that and say it straight."

"—I'm listening."

Holding his breath, straightening his posture, Julius looked at Subaru.
Receiving that straightforward gaze, Subaru continued.

"That's that, this is this."

"—Huh?"

At Subaru declaring that so boldly, Julius's face went blank with surprise.
Facing that Julius head-on, Subaru spread both hands wide.

"I get that you go all stiff and awkward looking at me. The Subaru of up until yesterday probably did something to you. And what that Subaru of up until yesterday did to you isn't going to vanish from this world just because — but it's vanished from my head."

"That's... true. That's exactly right. But I..."

"Hear me out to the end. So, given that state, the relationship between you and me needs to be built again from scratch. At least, that's the case with the me right now. The Subaru of up until yesterday — set him aside for a moment."

"————"

Swept up in a wave of confusion he couldn't recover from since a while back, at the rather forceful line of reasoning.
It was a terribly high-handed piece of logic. Not even close to a hundred percent of what he meant to convey got across.

In truth, it was a fact that Subaru was borrowing the strength of "Natsuki Subaru's" accomplishments up to now — the influence he'd had on Emilia, Beatrice, Julius, and the others.
But right now, he was going to borrow only the good part of that influence, and toss aside the bad.
Because—

"Out of our whole party, you're the strongest. So it'll be you who ends up going toe-to-toe with Reid. Even if we manage to find the strategy guide, the fight's still going to be left to you."

Of course, there was also Reid's fixation, the enemy's own wish for a one-on-one duel with Julius, to consider.
But Subaru had no intention of yielding on this point. Setting aside that Emilia had already won her own bout, there was no way he could let Ram, Shaula, Echidna, Meili, Beatrice, or Subaru himself take that moment from Julius.

"I get that you're spooked. I get that you're at a loss too. The Subaru up till yesterday, I'm sorry for whatever he did, really. — Take all of that together, flip the switch, and fight."

"...I've already lost to him twice."

"I know. But win this time."

"————"

He'd lost one more time than he knew.
But that, at this point, was no longer relevant — an extra detail, beside the point.

"If you don't win, my whole calculation falls apart. I've got all kinds of matchups running through my head, but before we get to 'the girls need to do their best,' us guys need to do our best first, or it's a disgrace to the name of knighthood."

"—A disgrace to the name. To me, as I am now, a disgrace to the name of knighthood, is it."

At Subaru thrusting his fist forward, Julius lowered his eyes and murmured low.
Startled, thrown off balance, wounded, struck, and finally, by logic that grabbed him roughly by the collar, Julius was tossed about by Subaru's words.
It was something that changed that elegant expression of his more drastically than the impression Subaru had held of him—

"Just as Lady Ram says, I'm starting to doubt whether you've truly lost your memories. Or perhaps — are you pretending to have lost your memories like this, in order to stop me, who was cowering, thinking of throwing away my sword and fleeing?"

"Even at the cost of clouding Emilia-chan's smile? Idiot, I wouldn't pull off something that roundabout! Besides, you don't need any of that — you're not going to let go of your sword, and you'll fight for everyone, regardless."

"That's... a contradiction. You are, at this very moment, trying to tighten my cowering heart back up—"

"Wrong. That's not it. What you're lacking isn't courage. Courage's packed right in there, plenty of it. — What you're lacking is fighting spirit. The will to not lose."

Closing the distance a step, Subaru drove his outstretched fist into Julius's chest.
At the words and the fist together, Julius caught his breath.

"————"

There was no lie in Subaru's words just now.
In the previous loop, in a hopeless situation against the witchbeast and Reid both, Julius had never let go of his sword, had never turned his back on despair, and had said to Subaru, "Leave it to me."
Turn that around, and what else was there to take from "Leave this to me" except "I've got this covered"?

Julius, in that situation, had said it plainly.

—Leave Reid Astrea to me.

And the last Subaru had seen of him was after he'd declared that.
So—

"...I never saw how it ended. I don't have my memories from yesterday either. So I've never once known you to lose to Reid."

Julius — Julius Juukulius — had not lost.
In front of Natsuki Subaru, not once had this knight, this man, ever lost.

So Natsuki Subaru, no matter what anyone said, would not concede the outcome.
He would keep expecting that Julius Juukulius would strike down Reid Astrea.

"I'm leaving Reid Astrea to you. Beat that most troublesome enemy of all. In exchange, I'll... reach out for everything else, in my own way, once again."

"————"

"Can't you hear me, Julius. Live up to your friend's expectations."

Where before it had been like entrusting his expectations, now it was like striking with all his hope, full force.
Subaru's fist struck Julius's chest a second time.

At that blow, Julius touched his own chest.
Then, keeping his hand pressed to his chest, he stepped back and let out a long breath.
Long, long, deep, deep, he exhaled, and then—

"...How is it that you, having lost your memories up until yesterday, can place so much expectation in me?"

"That's... well, it's an impression. A vibe, how you come across. How you look, how you talk, how you carry yourself, what you carry, how you dress, how you eat, how you walk — some kind of composite art of all that stuff, I guess."

Unable to touch on the events of the previous loop, Subaru gripped his own chest and forced out the pained answer.
Without either of them quite intending it, Subaru and Julius stood facing each other, each with a hand pressed to his own chest. Julius, keeping his hand there, straightened his spine and slowly bent at the waist.
Just as a knight out of a storybook would, with a beautiful, natural motion.

"An impression, is it."

"Y-Yeah. That's right. How you come across. All of you makes me expect that."

"I see. ...It was my own vanity, then, that made you think so."

Still bowed, the tone of Julius's voice changed.
In that voice color of his, which until now had felt somehow untouchable, the faintest measure of strength returned, a softness settled in, a warmth seemed to take root.
Giving Subaru that impression, Julius raised his head and faced forward.
And then—

"Forgotten by the world, forgotten even by you, the one person who alone remembered, unable to confirm my liege's existence, even I myself had become an uncertain thing, uncertain of where I stood. And yet, even in that state — not everything I have cultivated up until now has been lost. That is what you mean to say, is it not?"

"Wasn't quite that cleverly put together in my head, but yeah, that's the gist."

Julius took Subaru's clumsy, unpolished words and received them, refined into something clever and sophisticated.
Subaru had just been thinking, moments ago, that a hundred percent of what he meant to convey wouldn't get across. But he felt now that the listener had chosen something close to that hundred percent anyway.

"Feels like it was kind of an abstract thing, all mental-game talk, but since it seemed like the issue was mostly psychological, I figure I was on the right track?"

"Heh. Why go weak-kneed now, of all times? Weren't you supposed to be invincible?"

"I mean, even Mario with a Star dies if he falls in a pit..."

Julius furrowed his brow at the analogy that didn't land, but didn't press further.
It seemed that, from his dealings with the "Natsuki Subaru" of up until yesterday, he'd come to an understanding that meaningless quips were best let pass unremarked — a strange feeling, that.
Anyway—

"You a little more forward-looking now?"

"Well, who's to say. Ultimately, your words carried a great deal of vague sentiment and little specificity, and it's not as though the many things that have happened to me have dramatically changed."

"Come on, man..."

"Only,"

Cutting himself off there, Julius narrowed the eyes fixed on Subaru.
Then, his lips loosening into the faintest smile, he said,

"—That's that, this is this."

And with those words, uncharacteristic of him, he brought the exchange to its close.
