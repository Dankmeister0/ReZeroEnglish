Arc 4 Chapter 42: "The Value of a Life"

—A golden tiger crouched low, its sharp gaze sweeping the surroundings with disdain.

The great tiger's body measured roughly four meters. Viewed as a quadruped, it boasted a frame surpassing by a full size the lions and tigers Subaru knew of as ferocious beasts. Thick arms, thick legs—fangs too long to fit within its closed jaws jutted out, their tips slick and stained red with blood, swaying side to side as if flaunting themselves.

"Is... that..."

Before the question of what is that could even fully form, the dragon carriage—flung upward—crashed back down to earth. A sound of destruction rang out, wood scattered, screams echoed like tolling bells. The passengers, swallowed by the impact, were thrown to the ground—those still conscious groaned in agony; those who weren't lay motionless, their bodies stretched out in pools of their own spreading blood.

—Instinctively, Subaru's mind raced: I have to help the wounded.

But before he could turn that thought into action, violent motion sent him reeling. Wheeling around, ignoring Subaru's startled cry, Patrasche tore up the earth as she bolted, trying to cut a straight line through the forest to flee the monstrous beast.

"Wai— ! Hey, Patrasche!?"

Subaru called out against his mount's disregard for his own will, but just as on the road that had brought them this far, there was no sign she meant to heed him. Leaving behind the other dumbstruck, frozen dragon carriage passengers and Otto, Patrasche—with Subaru on her back—surged faster and faster. But—

"————!!"

A roar thundered, tearing through the forest, chasing after their retreating backs. A torrent of fury and bestial malice tore down the path, and it finally speared even Patrasche's instincts, which had been trying to carry them away, freezing her in place. The sensation of a mighty being—one with the power to threaten life itself—turning its hostility upon you. Subaru had tasted that feeling more times than he could count, and dread and terror climbed his spine at the recognition. Precisely because Subaru knew that sensation so intimately, he felt no urge to blame Patrasche for freezing at this critical moment. He felt no urge to blame her—but the result was plain regardless.

"—Ah"

Turning to look back, catching sight of the source of that terror, Subaru witnessed savagery itself.

A forepaw swept out as if brushing something aside, flipping the dragon carriage as easily as a toy box, scattering its contents as it hurled it into the cluster of forest trees. If you took the fat crack of a chopstick snapping and multiplied its volume a hundredfold, perhaps you'd get the sound of wood and human bone shattering together, all at once, just now. Faced with a literal monster, knowing that doing nothing meant only having your life taken, no one could move from where they stood. They feared that moving would provoke the giant beast, drawing its claws, its fangs, its wrath toward themselves.

Even though instinct told them that would only be a matter of whose turn came next.

"Could that thing be... the reason 'Sanctuary' was left empty...?"

Teeth chattering atop his crouching mount, Subaru burned the sight of the monstrous beast into his eye with grim resolve. Its golden coat was beautiful; its face, though savage, held something keen and dignified. Its too-sharp eyes gleamed with hostility and rage, and the fangs it clacked together were sharper than any blade could adequately describe. —If this, perhaps, were the thing that had attacked "Sanctuary"...

"—Eh?"

Straining not to miss any gesture, any posture, any motion at all, Subaru noticed something off about the way the great tiger slowly advanced. Narrowing his single eye, seeking the source of that wrongness, he realized what it stemmed from.

—Something was jutting from the great tiger's back, off to the left.

Given the creature's massive frame and seemingly inexhaustible stamina, the wound was surely trivial—its gait showed no sign of even registering the injury; it was probably no more than a scratch to it. But it carried enormous significance for Subaru. Not the wound itself—the object that had caused it. He recognized it.

—That was Ram's cherished staff, the one she always carried.

The small implement was what Ram used to chant her spells; he'd never once seen her invoke magic through any medium but it. He'd seen it in her hand just moments ago, facing off against Garfiel—there was no mistaking it.

Why was the thing he couldn't possibly mistake now buried in the great tiger's flesh? The answer was—

"An... atavism..."

A memory flickered across Subaru's mind—a scene from some time past. The day Garfiel had told him a piece of his own origins, showing off the peculiarity of his body. That time, he'd turned only part of himself—just part of an arm—into a beast, to demonstrate he was half human, half demi-human.

"If this is what they call a wear-tiger..."

Then this monstrous beast was Garfiel, transformed entirely, head to toe, into a beast. That would explain why Ram's staff—the one reason she'd have stayed behind to hold Garfiel off—was now lodged in this tiger's body. But that fact implied something else too, by the same logic. —That Ram, having lost her staff, had failed to hold back the beast that was Garfiel.

"Garfiel, you bastard... what did you do to Ram."

"————"

"Answer me, damn it! What did you do to Ram! Garfiel!!"

The monstrous beast only scowled at his shouting, giving no answer. It merely shook its head irritably at Subaru's voice, raising and lowering a forepaw to display its claws. Caught on the tip of one claw was a scrap of black cloth, all too familiar—and that alone told Subaru the fate of the pink-haired girl.

"Garfieeeeeel!!"

Screaming with rage, Subaru let go of the reins and leapt down from Patrasche. He botched the landing, tumbling gracelessly, and glared ahead on all fours as he rolled. Both parties—one man, one beast—faced each other on all fours, putting distance between them. The disparity in strength was obvious; he had no chance of winning, and probably no room to talk his way out either.

"You hate me that much...?"

"————"

"You dislike me that much, huh...?"

"————"

"If you want to kill me that badly, then just come after me alone, damn it!!"

"————!!"

In response to Subaru's furious cry, the beast lifted its head to the sky and roared. The air shook, the forest trembled its leaves in fear, and every living thing cringed, terror spreading from one to the next. But Subaru shrugged it off and bared his own teeth.

"Raising a hand against the woman you loved, and you call yourself the fang of 'Sanctuary'? Don't make me laugh!!"

"————!"

The next instant, the great tiger, provoked by Subaru's abuse, charged furiously. It blew away with tremendous force the wreckage of the dragon carriage that stood between them, and the golden beast came barreling through a haze of splinters and fresh blood.

Its speed was tremendous, its mass enormous. A direct hit would rival that of a large truck; he would likely die instantly, without even time to gasp. "Death" bore down before his eyes. Feeling that inescapable end draw near, Subaru's brain began spinning at a speed it never had before. His brain cells burned, connecting directly to his rage, and emotion exploded. Something pitch-black writhed through his chest, riding the flow of his blood to course through his entire body. Hot, black, dark, faint, murky, hollow, formless, ghastly—something he could—grasp.

Subaru's single eye snapped wide open, and he felt the sensation of seizing hold of something within himself. He looked ahead. The beast's fangs closed in, near enough now that he could almost feel its breath. Before that gaping maw, deep enough to glimpse the reddish-black gullet beyond—the very entrance to death—Subaru's body reacted beyond its own limits. Namely: he raised his arm, wedging it between the fangs and his chest—

"————!?"

In that instant, wind whipped up, and the giant beast's body vanished from before Subaru's eyes. No—vanished wasn't quite right. Carried by its own momentum, the great tiger's body spun through the air, pivoting a half-turn head over heels. It slammed back-first into the ground, bouncing with a howl of pain and incomprehension, its enormous mass sent flying with a great impact.

"Wha...!?"

Turning to look behind him, Subaru could hardly believe his eyes at the sight of the tiger tumbling head over heels. Just now, he'd been half-certain his life had truly been taken. The same bewilderment had struck the beast as well; the creature lay sprawled on the ground with an expression suggesting it couldn't comprehend what had just happened to it, shaking its head to try to right itself, only to collapse again as its inner ear failed to catch up with its awareness.

Both parties, neither understanding what had happened, floundered in incomprehension. But while Subaru's upper body slumped under a sudden wave of exhaustion, the great tiger shook its head irritably and rose to its feet. After this one encounter, it was plain as day which of them held the advantage. He still couldn't grasp what had just happened, but—

"If it took the bait, that means... it's Garfiel, no mistake."

Scolding his trembling arms into raising his torso, Subaru bit through the edge of his lip to keep his consciousness tethered. Ahead of him, the giant beast swayed its body slowly side to side, wary of him. It was probably trying to puzzle out the inexplicable phenomenon from that brief clash just now.

Even if told to do it again, there was no way he could reproduce that baffling sensation.

He didn't even feel like he'd truly landed a blow. The enemy's wariness was almost comical, but Subaru—who'd lost even the grounds to mock it—was no less a clown himself.

The giant beast crept closer, seemingly half in disbelief that Subaru showed no sign of further action—but then it suddenly stopped moving, and—

"————!!"

"Ah"

A roar. Subaru covered his face against the illusion of being struck by a gale, shrinking back, pinned in place by the sound. And through the crack of stillness that opened up, the sound of a footfall rang out.

Cracking the earth, the beast's body soared through the air, hurtling straight at Subaru. Its weight, its claws—neither could be dodged. This time, that earlier sensation didn't surface, didn't slip through his fingers again. This was the end. The end was drawing near. And then—

"————!!"

"————!?"

Charging in from the side, Patrasche slammed her head into the giant beast's flank. A pained cry rose up, and the great tiger convulsed, bent double. Patrasche whipped her tail, striking the sprawled tiger's face and robbing it of its sight. Immediately, she wheeled around, scooped up the slumped Subaru, and rode the wind, trying once more to flee—

"Patra—"

His call was cut short by the sound of flesh tearing. Subaru had been gripped by the waist in Patrasche's mouth, but he was suddenly flung to the ground as her body was violently whipped about. Looking over, he saw that Patrasche's tail—which had been trying to carry him away—had been caught in the giant beast's fangs, and her body, weighing well over four hundred kilograms, was being thrashed about as roughly as if it were made of paper. Patrasche's anguished cry. Flesh tore, blood sprayed, and the instant her tail was ripped away at the base, a burst of fresh blood dyed half of Subaru's body crimson.

Powerless to stop it, Subaru watched Patrasche get flung away, tumbling across the ground. Taking in her horrific state, he kept up his ragged breathing as he glared at the great tiger closing in on him. Hatred kindled in his single eye, Subaru called out to the one who might once have been called a friend—

"Ga... fiiiiiiiiiiiiile..."

He called that name, carrying nothing but endless, unquenchable hostility. There was no answer. Perhaps it had lost the organs of human speech when it turned into a beast—the great tiger's throat, reaching its massive forepaw toward him, gave voice to neither hostility nor murderous intent. It only expressed that instinct through action.

—Would its neck be snapped? Or would its head be crushed between those jaws?

Whatever the pain, whatever the ending, he would swallow "Death" down whole. He would devour everything, make it fuel, make it a reason to seize the right future.

"You think you'll be in that future...?"

Watching the claw slowly close in before his eyes, Subaru spat out his final grudge, meaning it as his last words. Then he closed his left eye, sealing his sight in darkness.

His only regret was Patrasche's devotion, and his own failure to save her in return—

"————?"

Subaru braced for the end that should have come, but it never arrived. Furrowing his brow in suspicion, he felt an inward flash of irritation that "Death" had missed its timing. And then, slowly, he opened his left eye.

"Huh?"

Before him, the claw that should have been on the verge of tearing him apart had been pulled away, and there was the face of the beast that claw belonged to. The reason his throat let out a foolish sound was simply this: the beast's face—the one that should have been glaring at him, drenched in murderous intent—was staring fixedly in a direction that wasn't toward him at all. The giant beast's gaze had shifted away from Subaru, turning to the left from his perspective. Following its line of sight, Subaru saw something come flying from that direction, strike the beast's body, and roll along the ground.—A stone.

An utterly unremarkable stone, the kind you'd find lying around anywhere, small enough to fit in a palm. Tracing the arc of its throw back to its source, his gaze landed on a figure standing at the edge of the forest.

Short hair, hollow cheeks, gangly long limbs. Not a particularly eye-catching figure, but Subaru knew him. One of the villagers of Arlam Village, one of the members of the village's youth corps, one of the refugees who had fled to "Sanctuary," one of the people who'd been riding in the overturned dragon carriage—and even now, blood streaming from his forehead, his body swaying unsteadily on his feet.

He bent his body with slow, sluggish movements, picked up a stone lying close at hand, raised it with a feeble motion, and hurled it at the beast's body. It struck. Its force amounted to essentially nothing. But—

"Get... away from Subaru-sama. You monster..."

Squeezing out the words, groaning through his pain, there stood a man showing his own will regardless. The instant his words struck, a shudder ran through Subaru's entire body. It traveled all the way to his fingertips, and, at a loss for words, unable to find what to say, Subaru kept watching as—

"————"

From all around, stones, boards, branches, shoes came flying one after another, thrown at the beast's body. Looking closer, Subaru saw it was the clumsy, feeble, achingly fragile resistance of people who, until just moments ago, had been crushed beneath the giant beast's overwhelming pressure.

"H-hey..."

What are they doing, Subaru thought vaguely. What meaning could such acts possibly have? Did they really think such actions could do anything against this monstrous beast? Could the force pierce through its fur, reach the flesh beneath? There was no way. It wouldn't leave so much as a scratch—it would only produce futile resistance.

"Stop..."

The right thing, the smart thing, would be for everyone to cover their heads and flee together. They should all pile into whatever dragon carriage remained intact, turn it around at once, and escape this place. Flee into "Sanctuary," or cut straight through the forest—either was fine. They should act in whatever way kept even a sliver of hope for survival alive. And yet, why were they—

"Everyone, what are you doing!? Hurry, run..."

"Subaru-sama saying that carries no weight at all, does it!"

Waving his arm, trying somehow to convey his intent, Subaru's words were cut off. The one who'd raised his voice was an older man, who swung an arm like a withered branch with all his might, spinning a stick through the air to strike the great tiger's snout. Breathing hard, he looked at Subaru and said,

"Abandoning the one who saved us, running off to save our own necks—how could we ever face our sons and daughters again? Especially when you're the one who came all this way just to save us."

"That's..."

"If you mean to die, then we'll die here too. That much, at least, we all agreed upon together, as a minimum condition for leaving that place."

Subaru's face went rigid with shock. Somehow, in the face of that shock, the old man broke into a gentle smile. It was so utterly out of place—the smile of someone basking in sunlight, as if he'd forgotten the threat looming right in front of him.

The assault resumed. Everyone hurled stones at once at the monstrous beast—at Garfiel. As ever, the force of it didn't even make him flinch, let alone itch. Lifting his massive frame with a slow, lumbering motion, Garfiel began to move. Ignoring Subaru, who was right within easy reach of his claws, he turned instead toward the forest—toward the wounded young man who had thrown the first stone.

"————"

Beast and young man faced one another. Before that overwhelming pressure, the young man lost his voice, but even so, he didn't stop what he was doing—he drew a dagger from his waist,

"Take this, then—!"

He thrust the blade forward, driving it in, its tip sinking into the space between Garfiel's brows—only for it to snap in half with a sharp, ringing crack an instant later. However tough that golden fur was, it wouldn't let a crude blade like that through. And then, in response to the young man who had carried out his final act of resistance, the raised forepaw swept around—

"Stop it—!!"

Subaru's scream. Even that was nothing but a hollow echo before the outcome that followed.

There came the sound of flesh being crushed, and the young man's thin body, from head to toe, was flattened by the impact and force, transformed into a lump of meat. Blood gushed like a fountain from between Garfiel's claws, and where the forepaw had been lifted away, only a swirl of reddish-black flesh remained on the ground.

"————"

This time, Subaru's throat finally let loose a scream of pure fury. Letting out something like a shriek, Subaru clawed at the ground, forced his body up, and hurled himself furiously at the beast's back. But he was struck by a hind leg, casually raised, and convulsed in agony. Immediately after, the whipping tail caught him, sending his body flying as lightly as a rubber ball, and he crashed back-first into a great tree—his whole body groaned, and he heard the sound of bones shattering.

"Gah... aaah—!?"

Writhing on the ground, he spat out great gouts of pain and blood clots rising up his throat. His whole body refused to obey him. His right arm hung at an unnatural angle from his shoulder. His spine, too, no longer held anything like a proper shape after that last blow. Weak, fragile, unable to change anything.

"————!!"

"Aaaaaaah—!!"

The beast roared. It leapt, its fangs and claws swinging at the refugees standing nearby. Blood danced through the air, screams echoed, and he could feel lives being snuffed out somewhere beyond his sight. Someone's scream. A voice like a beast's. Beastlike, yet a human voice. Confusion ran through the monstrous beast. Whose voice was it? What cry was it? Subaru, dealing with the grave error within himself, couldn't tell.

"—Tsuki-san! Survive, please!"

A voice calling out a name. A voice he knew belonged to someone, but the path to the part of his brain that could place it was blocked by pain. His thoughts flickered, the world grew hazy, his own emotions blurred, dream and reality grew indistinct, and hatred and agony scattered them all to pieces.

This isn't the time to lie here. I have to stand up. Stand, get up—if anyone's going to die, let it be you before anyone else.

"Everyone... don't you dare die... let it just be me who dies... that's... enough...!"

Squeezing blood and groans from his throat, Subaru forced his body up with all his might. His right arm was useless, so he desperately moved only his left. Through the vision of his one remaining eye, a red curtain had descended, and he realized his forehead had split open, blood streaming from it. He wiped roughly at his eyelid with his shoulder and, gritting his teeth hard enough to crack them, raised his head.

"————"

Devastation spread before him.

Every time the beast swung a limb, a person went flying, blood scattered, and the flame of a life burned out. Every single one of them, bravely, with the resolve to die, drove resistance that could never possibly reach the giant beast. All that stained its golden coat was their own returning blood, and their deaths carried no meaning at all. They only defied it, and were snuffed out. No meaning, none at all. If there was anyone who could give that "Death" meaning, it could only be Subaru.

"Stop, stop, stop, stop it, stop it... please... stop..."

If it wanted to kill, it should kill him before anyone else. He had been its target from the very start. There was no reason at all for these brave, kind-hearted people to be hurt, to have their lives taken. Or was it that even the deaths of these people were an outcome brought about by Subaru's own foolishness? If that were true, then it was simply too—

"—Ugh, ah!"

Subaru's body, teeth clenched, head bowed, was lifted up. The one who hoisted up Subaru—who'd only managed to prop his upper body up gracelessly—from behind was Patrasche, streaming an enormous amount of blood from her whole body. Even in a state half dead from Garfiel's fierce assault, she had crawled desperately, dragging herself all the way here. Seeing her in such a wretched state, Subaru couldn't hold back what welled up from behind his eyes, and as it spilled over, he said,

"That's enough... that's enough now. It's alright now, Patrasche..."

Calling out to her like that, Subaru reached his hand toward Patrasche's blood-soaked jaw. But she shook her head at his voice, showing a gesture that seemed to declare, Nothing is alright yet. Then, without further ado, she slipped her head under his belly and, giving him no chance to resist, hoisted him onto her back in one motion. Subaru's cry of surprise. With him now on top, Patrasche broke into a run. Her stride was powerful enough to make him wonder where such strength had been hiding. It may well have been, quite literally, everything she had left, spent all at once.

"————"

And yet, even spending what remained of her life force, giving everything she had, it still fell far short of full speed. And escaping the beast's fangs remained, likewise, far out of reach.

Fangs sank into Patrasche's hind leg, and her throat let out an anguished scream. Subaru was nearly thrown again; just as he felt himself lifted into the air, about to be slammed into the ground, Patrasche stretched her neck out and caught him in her jaws. Then, bending her neck back with all her might, she flung Subaru's body, using every last bit of strength she had, deep into the cluster of trees.

"————!"

Subaru understood, in that instant, that this was Patrasche trying—if only by the smallest margin—to put distance between him and the fatal threat. At the same time, he realized something else. The reason she had cut across the road, taking a shortcut after parting ways with Ram, to rejoin the group of refugees.

—Patrasche had already sensed this monstrous beast's presence.

Sensing it, she had fled toward this place, where there were multiple other targets, in order to raise Subaru's odds of survival even slightly. All of it, for the sole purpose of protecting Subaru.

Slammed into the ground, Subaru's body bounced once, twice. And right after he groaned through the third bounce—a sensation of floating took hold of his entire body.

"—Ah"

A sudden slope opened up in the mountainside, and Subaru's body tumbled, rolling straight down it all at once. Without even the composure to cry out, his skin scraped again and again against gravel and branches, he bounced in short, repeated impacts, over and over, tumbling down, down—

"————"

As he slid, head over heels, his view spinning wildly, Subaru just barely managed to catch sight of what lay above him. And he saw a sight he never wanted to see.

"————Patrasche"

Patrasche's body, caught between the great tiger's jaws, was crushed by the tremendous force of its bite, torn by fangs, pierced through, and split clean in two amid a massive spray of blood. Without even a chance to cry out in her final agony, the loyal dragon died having served Subaru to the very last.

"————"

His throat burned. His throat tore. His mind boiled with rage, a sensation of his blood catching fire. Bouncing, rolling, sliding, falling, scraped raw, Subaru kept falling.

—A hard bounce, and once more, that floating sensation.

Under the impact of slamming down, his consciousness was swallowed whole. His body still wouldn't stop. And yet, his consciousness slipped free of it, leaving his physical form behind.

—Only the undying voice of his grudge kept swirling, unceasing, within his chest.
