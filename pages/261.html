Arc 4 Chapter 93: "Each Other's Proposals"

'If the contract between me and that girl were severed, would it be alright to leave what comes after to you?'

'...That depends on the reason for doing it.'

Wetting his lips with his tongue, Subaru faced Puck, ignoring the thirst his throat was crying out for. Inside the crystal stone, Puck's expression—hidden from view—couldn't be seen from here. But the swirl of emotion woven into his words convinced Subaru that they hadn't been spoken lightly.

Still, this was talk of a contract. And not just any contract—the kind exchanged between a spirit and a spiritual artist, one that both parties valued above all else. Words that declared, unilaterally, that this contract would be "broken"—their true meaning wasn't something Subaru could easily fathom.

'The contract between Emilia and you... basically, it's an agreement between a spirit and a contractor, right? Breaking it would naturally come with some kind of penalty, wouldn't it?'

'Of course, that's right.'

'If I'm imagining this right, a contractor is someone who borrows power from a spirit by upholding the contract. If that contractor can't uphold the contract, then obviously, the spirit loses its grounds for lending them power. ...In other words, breaking the contract means—'

'It would result in the loss of the bond that exists between me and Lia, I'd say.'

Puck neither denied nor corrected Subaru's reasoning, simply acknowledging it. But what that affirmation meant was—

'If she can't borrow your power anymore... Emilia loses her power to fight. You'd be reducing her to just an ordinary girl.'

'That's not really something that matters much to you, is it? Whether or not Lia has power, you're the type who doesn't want her fighting regardless. Though it seems you've been down about the disconnect between that and what Lia herself actually wants.'

'Ngh... that's not wrong. But my own sentimentality is a separate matter from this. The whole fighting-power thing isn't the root issue here, like you said. What matters in this case is... what happens to Emilia once you're no longer by her side.'

A situation without Puck was, for Emilia, tantamount to losing the pillar that supported her heart. If the telepathic exchange with Puck up until now had been true, then even now, deep down, some part of her heart was aware that Puck wasn't really asleep. That faint, thin connection hadn't actually been severed. And yet, even in that state where the bond barely still held, a cornered Emilia had grown so exhausted—so much so that she'd come to genuinely depend on Subaru, telling him not to leave her side.

If that bond with Puck were truly, in the real sense, to be cut off—

'It wouldn't be strange if Emilia lost her mental balance right then and there. That has to be one of the top three sights you never want to see, doesn't it? What are you thinking?'

'There's no "what am I thinking" about it. I've decided I'll only push things toward whatever form would be best for Lia. I won't do—and can't do—anything that girl doesn't want.'

'You're saying Emilia wants to sever her contract with you?'

'No, Subaru. Losing the contract with me is just a byproduct of Lia's wish being granted. What Lia wants right now, without a doubt, is to overcome the Trials of the Tomb. That much you don't need to doubt—you can believe in it.'

On that point, even Subaru had no doubts. Even if he believed Puck's hypothesis—that Emilia, unable to fully face her own past, kept repeatedly failing the Trials by continuing to see a false past. That she wasn't the sort of petty person who would merely "pretend" to try overcoming the obstacle standing before her—that, more than anyone, Subaru believed.

Subaru's inner affirmation shouldn't have reached Puck in the form of words. And yet, as if that very fact conveyed Subaru's true feelings all the more, Puck lowered the tone of his mental voice slightly, and said:

'If Lia learns I'm gone, I think she'll certainly fall apart. To the point of crying, wailing, falling to pieces like a child, I'd bet.'

'—————'

'But you know, I think that's fine. The state she's in now—believing on the surface of her heart that I'm gone, while knowing deep down that I'm still here—that's the unnatural part. Only once both the surface and the depths of her heart understand that I'm truly gone... once that clear-cut shackle sealing away her past disappears... can Lia finally face her own heart.'

There was a quiet but overwhelming depth of feeling packed into Puck's words. It was affection, it was sorrow, it was joy, and above all, it overflowed with a devotion willing to give everything to what he cherished most.

'So, in order for her to face herself... you're going to strip away her bond with you, and push her forward?'

'Yeah, that's right. I'm sure there'll be a lot of pain involved, but I believe Lia is a girl who can bear it.'

'You won't be able to stay by her side. Doesn't that worry you? That bottomlessly kind-hearted daughter of yours, who always puts herself last and loses out because of it—don't you want to keep protecting her, right there beside her?'

Subaru himself was starting to lose track of what he was even saying. If Puck's proposal produced the result he intended, it should bring about exactly the outcome Subaru had hoped for. As Puck himself had said, whether or not Emilia retained her combat power meant nothing to Subaru, who was struggling to keep her off the battlefield in the first place. So Subaru had every reason to welcome Puck's proposal, and none at all to try to stop it.

'I thought lately you hadn't much liked me being by Lia's side.'

'That's not wrong. ...Given everything that's happened lately, your stock's hit rock bottom with me, and it's not going to bounce back that easily. One choice to sacrifice yourself for Emilia's sake isn't going to wipe away all the distrust that's built up.'

'That's quite the thing to say. It makes me a little sad.'

'But—'

There was no mistake in the way they'd taken each other's measure. Subaru, as he was now, couldn't simply take Puck's words at face value, nor did he think his poor impression of him could change so easily. There was no falsehood in the words he'd spoken. And yet—

'I can picture Emilia's face, grieving once you're gone. I understand better than anyone, painfully well, just how huge a presence you are to Emilia. And because you're that kind of presence, I...'

'—————'

The words wouldn't continue, and only a hazy jumble of feeling kept piling up inside Subaru. Puck, too, remained silent within their mental link, quietly waiting for Subaru's vague intent to take shape. But the more he rushed, the more the answer lost its certainty, slipping away into uncertainty.

'I'm—I'm, you...'

'The reason I can make a decision like this—it's largely because of you, Subaru.'

Cutting ahead of Subaru's words, which refused to come together, Puck's voice murmured softly. At the whisper resounding directly inside his skull, Subaru raised his head and stared blankly at the green crystal stone.

'Just as you said, Lia matters more to me than anything. I want to watch over her forever, to lend her my strength at her side. Even now, having judged that it's better for her if I'm not by her side like this—that feeling hasn't changed.'

'Then why...'

'Because you're here, I suppose.'

'—————'

For a moment, Subaru was gripped by the sensation of his breath catching.

'In this place... no, in this world, you're the only one who can stake your life for Lia's sake the same way I do. Over the time we've spent together, you've proven that much. And Lia too... setting me aside, you're the one she relies on most. That much is certain. You can believe it.'

'E-even so... I don't have amazing power like you, and I'm not overwhelming enough to blast away every obstacle standing in her path by brute force. All I can do is, at best, hold my head in my hands and worry alongside her, let her vent what's hurting her... that's about it. Can you really leave what comes after to someone like that?'

'You seem to be misunderstanding—I'm not asking you to be my replacement. What only I can do, only I can do. The reverse is just as true, and it's the part that only you can do that I'm counting on to help Lia.'

To Subaru, who fell silent, Puck kept going without pausing his words. Sealing off Subaru's escape routes, so as not to let the moment of decision for Emilia's sake slip away.

'Even supposing I'm gone, Lia is far stronger than you—certainly in the sense of "strength" that you mean. But as you know, that girl is weak—certainly in the sense of "weakness" that I mean. So it's that weakness I want you to support.'

'...If the contract's severed and the bond's cut off, what happens to you?'

'The only reason I can manifest like this is thanks to my pass with Lia. If that connection is cut, I'd have to stay materialized at all times just to keep existing at all. ...But for me to stay materialized means devouring the surrounding Mana endlessly, without limit. If you saw my true form, Subaru, I bet you'd be pretty surprised.'

The "true form" Puck spoke of was surely that towering, massive body he became. The pinnacle of the four-legged beasts, a raging beast of blizzard fury. The Beast of the End. Indeed, if he had to remain in that state constantly, then surely he couldn't go on existing.

'Then you're... saying you'll disappear?'

'It's not quite disappearing. I'll just go back to being the small being I was before I contracted with Lia. Somewhere I have deep ties to... probably the Elior Forest, I'd guess. There, I think I'll sleep inside something that becomes my Anchor, and wait for the time I'm woken up.'

'Woken up... by who?'

'By Lia, of course. —Here, the contract between me and that girl comes to an end. But when the time comes that she needs a new contract again, and if she chooses a spirit as her partner... I believe she'll choose me again. I truly believe that.'

It sounded to Subaru as though it had been said in a bright, clear voice, and he found himself holding his breath. Even as he was about to make a decision that might end in his own disappearance, there wasn't a shred of anxiety in Puck's voice. Regardless of whether his innate personality happened to be optimistic, that voice surely could never have carried a trace of unease.

For him, that Emilia would choose him again was beyond doubt. Emilia, having lost her contract with Puck, would be forced to look her own past in the eye. He hadn't even imagined the possibility that she might fail to fully accept that past and break down. Emilia would overcome her past, and when she sought a contract again, she would choose him.

Within Puck, that was a settled matter. Without doubting Emilia's strength, without doubting the accumulated weight of the time they'd spent together—precisely because of that, he was able to choose the option of severing the bond between them.

"—————"

To Subaru, who did nothing but waver, it was a bond so strong and unshakable it was almost blinding. Puck's deep love and trust for Emilia were the product of a heart of steel.

'And comforting Emilia once she falls apart over it—that's what you're dumping on me, huh?'

So Subaru threw out those words half out of desperation, with something like a grudge behind them. In response, Puck made a sound like a throaty chuckle, his tone carrying a smile, and said:

'That much, I really do feel bad about. But... since I'm entrusting you with my precious daughter, I'd like you to get through at least that much together with her.'

'...Is that your roundabout way of acknowledging me as Emilia-tan's partner—'

'If I blast you into oblivion right here and now, I'd have to rethink all sorts of problems, wouldn't I.'

'Every single one of your paybacks is terrifying, you damn cat!!'

Firing back an unfiltered retort at the outrageous remark, Subaru let his expression ease, if only a little. In deference to the depth of Puck's feelings for Emilia, his heart had eased—just a little, but enough to let them trade banter the way they had before all the rifts had formed between them.

And hearing that he intended to sever his contract with Emilia and leave this place, something else occurred to Subaru. If, hypothetically, this went well—then the Balance of the wager should tip, at least somewhat, in their favor.

'I understand your plan. Whether it actually goes the way you intend is a little uncertain to me... but I'm guessing you want me to play dumb and help steer things along too.'

'I wonder what it feels like, manipulating the mind of a girl you love.'

'Cut it out, I'll be crushed under the guilt. Besides, if Emilia genuinely understands things deep down like you say... then afterward, she's bound to realize she was steered into it, too.'

'If that happens, you and I might get hated together as accomplices. Getting cold feet?'

'Obviously. The way a teenage girl gets mad at her dad, going "Don't wash my clothes with yours!"—that's a totally different vector of being hated than what you and I are looking at.'

If Puck being disliked was, at most, a family matter, then Subaru being disliked cut into something far deeper and more fatal. He believed that if he spoke to her with sincerity, Emilia would come to understand. But even if she came to accept his true intentions, learning that her own heart had been conveniently manipulated wasn't something Emilia was likely to feel good about, either. —On that point, he probably wouldn't be forgiven.

'A little late for that, isn't it. —I've already piled up plenty of unforgivable things, made that girl cry more times than I can count. There's no way I can say, at this point, that I'm not prepared to carry one more.'

'—————'

'I accept the terms, Puck. I'll clean up after you. If Emilia breaks down crying tomorrow morning... it'll be in my arms.'

'—Alright. Then I'll leave it to you. Though it looks like I'll be dumping plenty more trouble on you from here on out too.'

At Subaru accepting his proposal, a faint trace of guilt crept into Puck's words. At that note, Subaru closed one eye, and prefacing it with "in that case," said:

'That means there's room for you to consider a proposal from me too, right?'

'...A proposal.'

'That's right, a proposal. Don't worry. Same as you, I'm acting with the goal of bringing about a future that turns out well for Emilia too.'

Thumping his own chest, Subaru spoke to Puck, who conveyed his assent through silence.

'There's a few things I want to ask, and something I want to try once I've heard the answers. —Emilia might wake up, so let's not take too long.'
