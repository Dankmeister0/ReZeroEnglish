Arc 3 Chapter 57: "The Battle to Conquer the White Whale"

The Earth Dragon's tremors traveled straight up through his seat, and as he fought to control the bouncing motion, Subaru licked his lips, which had begun to go dry.

Right on schedule, his cell phone alarm rang out, announcing that the White Whale would appear on the darkened Lifaus Highway. Roughly a minute, give or take — sometime in that window, the beast would show itself. The trouble was that neither the time nor the place could be pinned down to the second or the meter.

He swept his gaze around, honed his nerves, and braced for that moment. Then, just as he thought the moonlight had been blocked by a cloud, he realized that "cloud" was the underbelly of something far too vast — and Subaru watched his own memory come true before his eyes.

At the same instant, the others around him began to notice the presence just as Subaru had, and as the first ripples of unease began to spread, everyone waited for the call to launch the coordinated first strike, exactly as planned. But it was Subaru and the others who moved first — by half a heartbeat, no more — ahead of the moment Crusch caught her breath.

"—Let 'em have it!!"

"—Al Huma!!"

In answer to Subaru's shout, Rem gave directional shape, through incantation, to the mana she had already been gathering. The four massive ice lances that formed boasted the savagery of bundled great trees, and they drove into the Witchbeast's colossal torso with the force of piercing wind.

A sound rang out — the tip of the ice pillar crushed against something hard — but just before it could be shattered completely, its point sank slightly into the Witchbeast's belly, forcing the wound wider and boring inward, spraying blood.

The White Whale's scream thundered across the plain, and as Subaru and Rem's Earth Dragon rode out the air's numbing tremor against their eardrums, it broke into a full sprint.

—To state it plainly: this was in no way Subaru and the others jumping the gun.

Had they failed to move in that instant — had their movement lagged by even a fraction of a second — this preemptive strike would have been sensed by the White Whale. That split second was the watershed. And even knowing that the faintest hesitation could mean the difference between life and death, even someone of Crusch's caliber caught her breath before the White Whale's overwhelming presence.

Even half-convinced that it was coming, when the fact of it actually happens, ripples form in a person's heart. However small, those ripples warp thought; the warp invites stagnation; and stagnation invites defeat — the opening clash had teetered right on the edge of starting to their disadvantage.

And yet Subaru and the others made it in time regardless — in a word, because of the "power of love." Trust in the Magic Vessel's existence and its function — the fraction-of-a-second delay in Crusch and the others' judgment stemmed from a lingering, if not quite certain, distrust of that very thing. There had also been impatience at the Witchbeast's refusal to show itself, and it was no wonder that a faint shadow had crept into their judgment as a result. But Rem had not doubted Subaru's words — his claim that the White Whale would appear at this precise moment — not by a shred, not by a hair. And so, timed exactly to the moment Subaru had given her, she had woven the greatest firepower spell she could muster, ready to unleash it the instant she confirmed the White Whale's appearance.

What else could one call that, if not Rem's love?

"—Analyzing it like that is so embarrassing—!!"

"Subaru-kun, please hold on tighter. You'll be thrown off!"

Rem, gripping the Earth Dragon's reins, shouted this at Subaru as he analyzed the opening of the battle in his own way. Her words marked part of the plan — the second stage, to follow the detonation of the first strike.

"Everyone — follow those two idiots!!"

Behind them, half a step behind Subaru and the others as they charged, the subjugation force responded to Crusch's command and ignited their cannon barrels one after another — the packed magic-stones fired, burst in midair, and converted into destructive force keyed to their color, striking the White Whale's body in rapid succession.

Flame, ice, and clods of earth became blade, spear, and hammer, hammering down to widen the wound Rem had carved open; blood-mist erupted from the writhing colossus, raining a filthy black shower over the highway. Dodging the fine crimson spray that veiled their sight like drizzle, the Earth Dragon swung wide with agile motion, aiming to circle around behind the White Whale. It was exactly as planned.

"Make it aware of me, so it lets the subjugation force take its back as a rule—!"

"The Dark-Dispelling Crystal is about to shatter — close your eyes!!"

Already deep in battle-mode, a pure white horn now peeked from Rem's forehead. Looking up, baring her teeth, she gave the order, and Subaru scrambled to obey, ducking his head and shutting his eyes — and the next moment, the world flickered.

White light exploded in the sky, searing the world with brilliance in an instant. Even through closed eyelids, the intensity of the light pierced through, all but assaulting the optic nerve, and Subaru's throat locked with shock. Then, seconds later, he cautiously opened his eyes and looked around—

"Whoa, just like they said. Amazing."

The night's darkness had been swept away, and a world lit as if at high noon spread out before him. Overhead, shining in place of the sun that had already set, was the "Dark-Dispelling Crystal Stone" launched simultaneously with the preemptive strike on the White Whale. It was a crystal that, when shattered, produced a pseudo light source to banish darkness — though by all accounts, it was meant to cast only the faintest glow, just enough to dim the dark a little.

"So they went and bought a mountain of them, throwing around money and connections, and smashed them all at once — instant fake sun. That's the idea."

"If it dives back into the night, even the White Whale's massive body won't be easy to find. — Now then, this is where it really begins!"

It was the combined effect of crystal stones gathered by the two great merchant houses of the royal capital, who had joined forces and scoured the land for them. The Lifaus Highway, which had been sunk in night, was now filled with daylight brightness, throwing the world — until then visible only by moonlight — into vivid clarity. Which meant, at the same time—

"That's...!!"

—it laid bare, for the first time under clear daylight, the full form of the Witchbeast known as the "White Whale."

"————!!!"

Whether it felt fury at being exposed under the light of day, the White Whale opened its massive jaws and let out a roar. The thunderous sound it produced went beyond mere noise, closer to an act of destruction in itself. The air itself shook, a violent howl that struck even the disciplined Earth Dragons' wild instincts with fear.

The monstrous form producing that sound bled from all over its enormous body, yet its movements showed not the slightest dulling, and it looked down upon the humans daring to challenge it.

"What... size..."

His throat, which he had no intention of letting tremble, trembled anyway, and Subaru couldn't stop the sensation of his limbs going numb as if paralyzed. He was struck with the clear realization that everything he had seen, touched, and had taken from him regarding the threat called the White Whale had been nothing but the barest fraction of its true existence.

The White Whale — true to the name, its Witchbeast form was covered entirely in white. Countless white hairs bristled thick across skin as rough-hewn as bedrock, and their toughness meant that half-hearted attacks couldn't carry damage through to the inside. Seen from a distance, its overall shape did indeed closely resemble the whale of his knowledge — but its size outstripped his expectations by two full orders.

As far as Subaru's knowledge went, the largest whale in the world was the blue whale — roughly thirty meters in length, and literally the largest and heaviest mammal alive. But the White Whale's body, seen with his own eyes, easily surpassed thirty meters, approaching a scale of nearly fifty. At that size, it was less a living creature than a single mountain. One white mountain of rock, swimming leisurely through the sky, as if it were some kind of joke.

"Subaru-kun."

A voice called to Subaru as his teeth chattered under the grip of terror, his fingertips on the verge of trembling. It belonged to the girl with her back to him, her small waist wrapped in Subaru's arms. Right in front of him, close enough that he could almost hear her breathing, she did not turn to look at him, and said,

"Are you scared?"

Not a taunt — trust, calling out to him. Subaru gritted his teeth, forcibly twisting his mouth into a grin, and said,

"Yeah, I'm scared. — Scared of how bright my future's gonna shine once I take that thing down and get showered with praise!"

Answering the girl's expectation with a wisecrack, Subaru reached forward and slapped the shoulder in front of him.

"My life is all yours! Now come on, let's run like hell!"

"Rem's life is yours too, Subaru-kun. — Then, let's do just that."

At Subaru giving his valiant order to flee with a thumbs-up, Rem smiled softly and snapped the reins. The Earth Dragon whinnied in response and, showing no fear even before the monstrous form ahead, kicked up dust and tore across the earth. Their path aimed straight for the White Whale's lower right, as it faced them — a diagonal dash meant to swing around toward its tail.

Racing ahead, closing the distance, Subaru and Rem drew the White Whale's massive eye toward them. Jaws wide enough to swallow a house whole opened, and the maw lined with millstone-like teeth drew in air, ready to loose another roar in their direction. The roar that had earlier struck their nerve rearing to come again, Subaru and the others braced themselves — and above their heads—

"To look away like that — I've been taken awfully lightly——!!"

An invisible blade swept sideways in a single flash, slicing a shallow line straight across the White Whale's open-mouthed head. Without even the scrape of blade against stone, the strike that shaved cleanly across the rock-hard hide made blood erupt once more from the White Whale's massive body.

Turning, tracing the source of the blade, Subaru saw — following behind them, at the head of the subjugation force — the Earth Dragon that Crusch rode. Standing atop the back of the sleek black Earth Dragon, the beautiful figure who had unleashed the strike along with a valiant declaration — her follow-through arm held—

"Nothing... She's not holding anything...!?"

"An intangible blade that ignores range — famed as the Hundred Men, One Stroke. It's Crusch-sama's swordsmanship."

Rem answered Subaru's astonishment in a low voice. He knew nothing of the tale she mentioned, but the words alone gave him a rough grasp of the situation — and made all too much sense of how someone who looked unarmed could wield such combat power.

Its initial motion crushed by the invisible strike, the White Whale, its movement stalled, took a follow-up assault. The subjugation force's members loosed their magic-stones one after another, and with firepower concentrated, damage from the impacts landed on the White Whale's colossal body in succession, its writhing form losing altitude.

Until then level with the clouds, the White Whale's massive body sank, and once its height no longer required tilting one's neck straight up to see, that was—

"—Within striking range."

The Earth Dragon leapt as if bounding, climbing into the sky with an agility that belied its own great bulk. Even so, set against the overwhelming might of the White Whale, the difference in mass was plain. To the White Whale, the Earth Dragon hovering near its snout must have looked exactly like a mere insect — and yet,

—a sword-flash, driving straight and true, cut deep and vertical into the Witchbeast's snout.

At the sight of silver slicing effortlessly through white rock-hide, the din that had roared across the battlefield genuinely fell silent for a moment. This was not magic, nor the power of mana-infused ore, nor destruction wrought by a formless blade — it was proof of a solid mass of iron, wielded by a human hand. Proof that the pinnacle a man had spent the greater part of his long life pursuing had truly reached the snout of the Witchbeast that birthed mist and painted the world white.

"—Fourteen years."

Driving his sword into the split snout, the silhouette crouched, murmuring low. Bracing himself with the sword opposite the one he'd swung driven into place to hold his stance, his trained back — flicking off the blood that wet the blade after the strike — wrapped itself in a bloodlust so sharp it warped the very air, and,

"I have done nothing but dream of this day."

As the figure straightened its back, the White Whale writhed. As if trying to shake the thing perched on the tip of its own nose, its massive body twisted in midair, mowing the air aside in a barrel roll. A fierce gale tore across the sky above the highway, and everyone caught their breath, eyes wide, at the outcome of the giant body's flight. But—

"————!!"

Its twisted body returned to its former position, the White Whale's throat shuddered with pain, blood spilling as it thrashed its tail wildly. To the vertical wound cut earlier, a horizontal slash was now added, carving a cross-shaped wound onto its forehead, and the silhouette, with light footsteps, now stood upon the White Whale's back.

—The Sword Demon grinned, twisting his wrinkled cheeks into a merciless smile.

"Fall here, and bare your corpse. — You mere lump of meat."

Spitting the words out, Wilhelm's body, sword held in both hands, cut through the wind. Racing from the head toward the tail, the blade he swung along the way carved the White Whale's rock-hide to shreds without mercy. Slicing effortlessly through the hide that should have been hard and tough, sending black-tinged blood-mist scattering across the sky as he ran — that figure was, without question, the "Sword Demon."

Its body under assault, its colossal form thrashing, the White Whale had no effective counter against Wilhelm. Trying to shake off the old swordsman running so lightly across it, it once again wrapped itself in a whirlwind and swam through the sky in a spin, but—

"Coming all this way just to be cut — how obliging of you."

An instant before the White Whale's body spun in midair, Wilhelm made a short leap, driving his sword down to keep himself aloft. As the White Whale spun once in place, the planted blade traced a clean line through it — the White Whale, in effect, offering its own body up to the blade. Bathed on one side by the spray of blood-mist, dyed mottled red, the Sword Demon laughed. Laughing, his aged frame swung both swords up overhead and toward the beast's flank. The descending blades carved vertically down the rock-hide of its side, and when swung out into a V-shape, sheared the flesh away. A scream tore through the sky, and as Wilhelm fell, the White Whale's tail came at him in a sideways blow. But an instant before the direct hit, Wilhelm's Earth Dragon rushed in, scooped up the old swordsman, and slid clear of the attack's range. And as the White Whale, driven by fury, tried to give chase to Wilhelm—

"Shaddup, you dumb beast. You've got us to deal with too!!"

A single blow from a great cleaver plunged into its mouth, gouging out from the root one of the White Whale's teeth, large enough to embrace with both arms, and with a dull crack, a yellowed molar went flying. Cutting diagonally across the White Whale's face like that was Ricardo, astride his Liger, whooping with cheers. The Large Canine, said to be more nimble than the Earth Dragons, showed off that agility without reserve, running circles around the White Whale's body high in the air, its master still on its back.

"C'mon, c'mon, we ain't done yet!!!"

Atop the running Liger's back, spitting curses and saliva alike, Ricardo brought his great cleaver down. Smashing through rock-hide, gouging the flesh beneath, scattering blood — the beastman and his loyal Liger, wielding fang and claw freely, widened the resulting wounds deep and sharp. And, following Ricardo's furious charge,

"Here we go, here we go!!" "Onee-chan, don't push out too far! Everyone, now's the time!"

At the command of the kitten beastman twins — the vice-captains, astride smaller Ligers, spreading out and giving orders — the beastman mercenary corps' Ligers latched onto the White Whale's body one after another, running circles across its vast flesh, beginning to overrun it.

Spears and swords swung and struck, most deflected off the rock-hide, but damage reliably passed through wherever the outer skin cracked. The sight was exactly like a beast swarmed by venomous insects. Clinging to it all over, its body swung about by enemies steadily multiplying its wounds however small, the White Whale exposed the weakness of its colossal bulk's poor maneuverability, and in the middle of all this,

"—Everyone, fall back!!"

Crusch's furious roar cut through the battlefield, and the mercenary corps clinging to the White Whale sprang away from its body all at once. The Ligers flying through the air landed lightly on the ground, and seeing this, the White Whale swung around widely, intending to counterattack from there — but that judgment was a mistake.

"You've bared your flank—!"

Crusch's second strike from overhead — a diagonal slash — tore into the White Whale's side, and following that stroke, a further assault landed close behind. It was the attack of the unit that, until then, had not joined the fighting at all, focused entirely on chanting spells.

"————!!"

Overlapping incantations gave shape to destruction, manifested from painstakingly gathered mana. In a paradoxical scene where sun and moon existed together in the sky, a second sun now appeared, low in the heavens. Even understanding it to be a bundled mass of fire magic's firepower, it was impossible to tear one's eyes from the sheer violence of the world being scorched instantly by its heat.

The great fireball, exceeding ten meters across, seared skin even from a distance, threatening to burn away the moisture in one's very eyeballs behind closed lids. As that fireball wavered and gained its initial momentum,

"—Whoaaaaaaaa!!"

The initial momentum instantly turned to acceleration, and the great fireball struck the White Whale's flank-exposed body directly — burning flesh through the resulting wound, boiling the organs beneath through transferred heat, the White Whale's scream and the blast's roar thundering out into the bright night sky.

The shattered fireball scattered burning fragments across the plain, and the mercenaries running below scrambled to evacuate. Subaru and Rem, mixed in among them, kept their eyes fixed on the White Whale's form, its white fur bursting into flame, even as they pulled away. That overwhelming result — a surprise attack succeeding beyond all expectation — left the White Whale unable even to mount a counterattack. At this rate, could they possibly get through this with zero casualties, without letting it land a single blow?

"Feels like that really did some damage! We could keep this up, couldn't we!?"

Putting distance between themselves and the reach of the flames' aftermath, on the back of the Earth Dragon now halted amid a cloud of dust, Subaru pumped his fist in triumph. In truth, they had pinned down the White Whale completely up to this point, dealing no small amount of damage. Whether it was that the previously organized subjugation forces had lacked the strength, or simply that their own preparations were thorough enough to meet this moment properly, he couldn't say — but there was already an exhilarating sense that victory was within reach. But in response to Subaru's words,

"No. — If things had gone as they should, we wanted to bring it down to the ground with that last strike."

Shaking her head, Rem glared, frustrated, at the burning beast floating in the sky. Drawn by her words, Subaru looked up too, narrowing his eyes suspiciously as he studied the White Whale. Its whole body was being seared by the fire that had caught in its white fur, and it writhed, but showed no sign of the flames dying down. Wounds from slashes and magic-stones spread across every part of its body, and the sight of it dripping blood was visibly pitiful. And yet,

"Its altitude... isn't dropping."

The White Whale's body still hung in the sky overhead. It wasn't beyond a Liger's leaping reach, but it was still far too high for a lone human to challenge directly. More than anything, unless they brought it down to the ground, they couldn't move on to the next phase of the plan.

"We played every card we had right off the bat. If it still ain't fallin' even after that, guess that just means the other side's tougher than we figured."

Shouldering his great cleaver, his fur soaked with the enemy's blood, Ricardo came up alongside them. He snorted through his dog-like nose, whiskers twitching, looking up at the White Whale.

"From what I felt landin' a hit, gettin' an attack through that thick hide ain't easy. Gotta go at it with brute force like my weapon, or skill on Wil's level, or you're just gonna wear yourself thin."

"Physical attacks maybe, but magic attacks look like they're getting through, don't they?"

"That's iffy too. It looks flashy on the surface, like the attacks are landing hard, but the white fur on its skin scatters the mana and cuts the power down. Even Rem's attacks aren't getting through as much as it looks."

Rem spoke bitterly of how her own maximum-firepower spell hadn't gotten through. When Subaru turned to look at her words, he could indeed see that, while the White Whale's body bore many shallow wounds, none of them seemed severe enough to hinder it in battle. But at the very least,

"That fire magic just now burned off the white fur, and it looked like it actually got through."

"Burn off the fur that scatters the mana, then the scorched skin underneath — a blade can cut through that. Simple enough."

Ricardo agreed with Subaru's insight, baring his fangs ferociously. With that, he lowered the great cleaver back into his hand, slapped the Liger's back, wheeled around, and headed for the front line again, picking up speed. As he went,

"Gonna wear it down some more, same as before! Tell Miss Crusch to save that big one-shot of hers for when it counts!!"

Making his own demand as he went, he plunged in beneath the White Whale, leaping once more to aim for its back. Looking around, Subaru saw that even Wilhelm, who had once put distance between himself and the beast, was heading from the tail side back toward the top of the White Whale, and the subjugation force, having reached the same conclusion as Subaru and the others, was already moving swiftly into action. In other words — the all-out assault would continue.

"With the firepower this concentrated right now, if we get closer, we'll just get in the way. Rem, can't you fire off some magic?"

"Something on the same scale as before would take too long to chant, and... the mana still gets scattered, so Rem's magic won't get damage through. Anything weaker than that wouldn't have enough firepower to begin with."

Following the same logic Ricardo had laid out, Rem too, morningstar in hand, diving into the front line and forcing physical attacks through that bedrock-like flesh by sheer strength, would likely stand a better chance. But making that happen would mean Subaru becoming a liability, and if they were to follow the plan of action Subaru himself had devised for what came after, then Rem and Subaru could not afford to be split apart here.

"Frustrating as it is, guess we've got nothing to do but watch till something changes...!"

"It's frustrating on my end too, y'know."

As he spoke, another Earth Dragon drew up alongside Subaru and Rem's, moving at a slow pace. Astride the heavily armored Earth Dragon, fitted with Earth Dragon armor, was a lightly-equipped knight in women's clothing — Ferris. He narrowed his eyes playfully, gazing at Subaru, and said,

"Ferri-chan's kinda short on ways to attack, so basically I just watch, don'tcha know~? I'm used to it, I guess you could say I'm used to it, but the frustration's always there too."

"That's exactly why you're the lifeline of a subjugation force built around recovery. We can't have you out front. Just do your job, only that, thoroughly — I'm begging you."

Subaru answered Ferris, who hadn't dropped his usual manner even now. At Subaru's response, Ferris tilted his head, looking a little surprised, and said "Oh?"

"Feels like there's been a change of heart or something, ya know? What happened with you, Subaru-kyun?"

"If I had to say — I got to be a slightly better man."

Eyes darting over the shifting battle, Subaru answered with a bitter, sour-faced grimace. Ferris, at that answer, hummed "Hmm~," touching a finger to his lips and nodding,

"Could it be that Rem-chan made a man outta you, Subaru-kyun?"

The answer to that was both yes and no. Subaru bristled at the sight of Ferris grinning crudely with a raised thumb. Was this really the time for that? — he opened his mouth, about to snap at the knight, but—

"It's Wilhelm-sama——!!"

At Rem's cry, his gaze snapped forward in a panic, and he caught sight of the old swordsman running across the White Whale's back.

Sword held point-down, vertically, Wilhelm was slicing the White Whale's back open lengthwise with the blade. Trailing behind Wilhelm's shadow as he ran from tail to back, blood erupted like a fountain, chasing after him.

A one-man rampage — this was surely what that phrase truly meant. The subjugation force's morale soared at the brilliance of Wilhelm's blade-work, unbelievable for a single man, and the continuous barrage of magic-stones and the beastman corps' Ligers' coordinated tactics gained momentum.

Writhing in midair in pain, letting out broken, fragmented cries, the White Whale was utterly unable to respond to any of it. The mist Witchbeast — at the pitiful sight of the monstrous form that had tormented the world for so long, Subaru felt certain that the wind was now blowing firmly at their backs.

"Cheeeeeaaaaahhh!!"

With a burst of spirit, Wilhelm's sword strike split the White Whale's head clean down the middle, and his aged frame, running the whole length, leapt lightly off the tip of the White Whale's snout. Spinning in midair, he turned upside down—

"Heave-ho!!"

Ricardo, who had leapt straight up, swung his great cleaver. The flat side of the whirling blow was aimed not at the White Whale but at Wilhelm, upside down in the air. Wilhelm met the strike with the soles of his feet,

"Hah——!!"

Wilhelm's body launched as if shot from a sling, and the swords held in both hands raged as they cut through the side of the White Whale's face. The beast's snout to cheek was mercilessly shredded to pieces. Not content with that wound and the blood alone, Wilhelm regripped both swords and unleashed a thrust—

"————!!"

The sword buried itself deep in the White Whale's massive left eye, the lens flowing out from the depths of the eyeball. Wilhelm instantly released the blade, sunk in to the hilt, and in the same moment drew two more swords from behind his waist with both hands and flashed them in a single stroke — strikes closing in from left and right slicing horizontally across the top and bottom of the eyeball, and the blades, instantly reversing, split the wound's left and right edges vertically. As a result,

"The left eye's coming off—!"

Gouged deep by the square-shaped cuts, the White Whale's left eye was severed and fell. What someone had voiced became reality, and the falling eye, spilling red blood and white fluid, struck the ground with a tremendous roar, shattering the earth. A half-beat later, Wilhelm landed right beside the eyeball where it had fallen. He drove his sword into the rolling eyeball as it was, and lifted it up so that the White Whale, still overhead, could see it, saying,

"—Pathetic."

He said it, the corner of his mouth curling into a grim, merciless smile.

Before the Sword Demon's ferocious display, the White Whale, thoroughly outmatched, had no recourse. One of its eyes gouged out so cleanly, it was plain that the gap in combat power had nothing to do with the difference in size. But that act of provocation—

"The White Whale's eye color, it's...!"

"It's coming!!"

"Subaru-kun, keep your head down——!!"

The instant they noticed the change, Ferris shouted and Rem accelerated the Earth Dragon. Buffeted by wind, his body thrown by the sudden acceleration, Subaru scrambled to steady himself, and even through the violent shaking, he burned the sight of the White Whale into his eyes. — That change, that transformation, it was—

"————!!"

Letting out a roar, its remaining eye, furious at having one gouged out, turned a deep, blazing red. Glaring down with that blood-colored eye, the White Whale tilted its body toward the subjugation force, who scrambled in a panic to put distance between themselves and its frenzy. And then, a change came over the White Whale's body.

—The instant it first began, Subaru could not suppress an indescribable revulsion.

The White Whale's mouth opened. No — that description, while it seemed correct, wasn't quite right. To state the fact precisely, it was this:

—Countless mouths sprouted across the White Whale's entire body, and all of them, at once, bared their teeth and opened.

"————!!"

A shrieking, ear-splitting roar shook the plain's air to a high pitch, delivering to the minds of anyone within its reach a discomfort like claws raking directly across the psyche. At that roar, not just the humans but even the animals — the Earth Dragons, the Ligers — felt their spines shudder. Something that spoke straight to instinct, it made legs buckle, and naturally, turned exposed, defenseless prey out of them all.

And then,

"——Ah."

From every single mouth across the White Whale's entire body, "mist" poured out, dyeing the world white. In the blink of an eye it rained down across the plain as far as the eye could see, stealing back the light they had secured, painting the world pure white.

—The mist Witchbeast's roar rose up, and the difficulty of the subjugation battle leapt sharply upward.

The battle to subjugate the White Whale — its end was, even now, nowhere in sight.
