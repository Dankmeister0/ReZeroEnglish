Arc 4 Chapter 20: "Test Results"

—Seated at a desk right in the middle of the classroom, the white-haired girl tilted her head slightly.

Under her gaze, Subaru quickly scanned the room to confirm no one else was present. Stepping halfway out into the hallway, he checked all the way to both dead ends—and, once again accepting that there wasn’t a single soul around, he scratched his head and let out a sigh.

"First off, there’s something I gotta say."

"Yes, by all means, let's hear it. What you think, what you feel, what you have to say—I am intensely interested in all of it."

"You look good in that uniform."

Subaru pointed at the Witch, whose eyes sparkled with curiosity, and gave his honest assessment. Hearing this, the Witch looked blank for a single moment, before bursting into laughter she could no longer hold back.

"Haha, thank you. If you think so, then recreating it from your memories was well worth the effort. It was the attire most vivid and frequently seen within your mind. Would that happen to make it your favorite?"

Standing up from her seat, the girl—Echidna—pinched the edge of her gray skirt and spun around on the spot. Her shoulder-length white hair swayed along with the movement, and her playful demeanor made her look like nothing more than an ordinary girl her age. A gray skirt and a deep navy blazer. The red ribbon adorning her chest signified that she was in the same grade as Subaru, its contrast against the white shirt beneath striking and dazzling. Though, if he had any complaints to lodge—

"I prefer longer skirts over short ones. The longer it takes to flip, the more it stirs the imagination."

"I see. In that case, the next time you flip it, I shall make sure to wear a long skirt so as to meet your expectations."

"Like you'll ever get the chance! Besides, it's not like everyone wore uniforms just 'cause I loved 'em. It's the dress code around here. Royal Guards and knights have 'em too, don't they?"

Echidna giggled, bringing a hand to her mouth. Her amused attitude suggested she was only half-listening to Subaru's excuses, and even if he pressed the matter, he wasn't likely to get the answer he wanted. Shrugging, Subaru walked toward the back of the classroom—the second seat from the rear by the window, an unremarkable middle spot—pulled out the chair, and plopped himself down.

The feel of the hard wooden chair. The English letters carved into the edge of the desk by previous occupants. The creak of the desk legs when he leaned his weight against it to sleep. The rusted interior of the desk compartment. Every single one of them was a fragment of the daily life Subaru had left so far behind.

"I had expected you to be rather more surprised, though."

"If you were trying to hide it, you should've put a bit more effort into the background. There's no way in hell you'd have a situation where there isn't a single living soul around, not on the way here, not on the streets."

Even taking into account that it was a weekday afternoon, the route Subaru had walked was far too devoid of any human presence. It was as if everything in the world except the information Subaru needed had been neatly excised.

"It was a world way too convenient for me. I'm sorry to say, but you didn't get to see whatever pathetic state you were hoping for—so serve yourself right."

"No, no, that too is quite entertaining. To attempt something and obtain a result—that alone is true bliss for me. The nature of the result matters very little in that regard. Though, of course, the conversation shifts a bit if we consider whether it leads to what comes after."

Despite having her expectations thwarted, Echidna waved her raised hand dismissively, offering that remark without the slightest hint of sore loserdom. Seeing not a shred of genuine negative emotion in her, Subaru suppressed the urge to click his tongue and asked,

"So, what is this world? If I remember right, I went into your tomb while the so-called 'Trials' were going on, and then..."

"You possessed the qualifications and entered. Naturally, that simply means the 'Trial' began for you as well. Did you not hear it? First, face your past."

Offering words that affirmed Subaru’s deduction, Echidna clasped her hands behind her back and slowly walked over toward him. The beautiful girl’s hair swayed in the wind; inside the classroom where a crisp, cool breeze blew in, her uniformed figure blended in without the slightest dissonance. Feeling as though a trap was laid within each and every one of her casual gestures to ensnare his heart, Subaru deliberately averted his gaze. Then—

"Everyone harbors regrets about their past. So long as one lives day to day, there is no being who does not accumulate regrets. Today one regrets yesterday, yesterday one regretted the further past, and come tomorrow, one will surely regret today. —For humans possess the function to regret."

"Don't go thinking so pessimistically. Turning that regret into reflection, using yesterday's reflection to make it through today, and using today's reflection as a breakthrough for tomorrow—that's a human function too, isn't it?"

"—Precisely!"

Clapping her hands together with a dry smack, Echidna leaned her face in close, making Subaru recoil on instinct. Unbothered, she leaned forward to pursue Subaru’s retreating face, bringing her black eyes within breath's distance.

"Mere wordplay, nothing more than a slight difference in perspective. However, whether one is pessimistic or optimistic about the past radically changes the conclusion one reaches. Most people view their past pessimistically, looking back only upon the bad memories they retain, denying the path they have walked. And hating to lay eyes on what they have denied, they put a lid on it and pretend it never happened."

"Hey, your face is... too close...!"

"It cannot be helped. Because yesterday's self was unconditionally more ignorant than today's self. Because today's self unconditionally possesses less knowledge than tomorrow's self. Be it the sum total of knowledge or the count of a single memory, the past is inferior to the present and the future. That is a fact!"

Oblivious to Subaru feeling overwhelmed, Echidna’s impassioned speech continued. Then, abruptly pulling back, she slammed both hands onto the desk with all her might.

"Thus, when confronting the past—or when encountering the past one ought to confront—people waver, lose their way, grieve, suffer, lament, despair, and only then do they arrive at an answer. If it is an answer reached through that process, I shall validate it no matter what that answer may be. Whether it is an answer reached by turning your back, or an answer seized by reaching forward, it is undeniably proof of overcoming the past."

"So that's the purpose of this 'Trial'?"

"Indeed. To face your own past, and to deliver some form of answer to it. If one does nothing but fear delivering an answer, abhor it, and clutch one's head in agony, one can never overcome the 'Trial.' But if you can fully affirm the past, or completely deny it, then I shall see you off with praise. That is the first 'Trial.'"

Nodding to the now-understanding Subaru, Echidna suddenly seemed to return to her senses. Her cheeks flushing faintly, she gave a small cough.

"M-my apologies. I became a bit overly excited. Forgive me for showing you such an unsightly display."

"I don't really care. If your breath stank, that'd be one thing, but luckily it smelled like citrus. More importantly..."

Feeling like he'd witnessed a rare sight at the bashful Echidna, Subaru pulled his chair and shifted to sit leaning forward.

"If what you said is the condition for passing the 'Trial,' does that mean I can consider this 'Trial' cleared?"

"I was allowed to watch the entire sequence from start to finish... and I believe you achieved a more than sufficient result."

Placing a hand over her chest, Echidna took a deep breath as if savoring the rich aroma of fine tea, wearing an utterly satisfied expression.

"The symbol of your past trauma, and the Anchor of your past guilt—to both of them, you delivered your answer. For that, I wish to send you off with praise."

"Start to finish... Wait, does that mean you saw me bawling with snot running down my nose, too?!"

"Even my eyes welled with tears when you said, 'I'm thowwy.'"

"Shut up!! Don't you dare tell anyone, that's embarrassing!"

Knowing that his emotional parting with his father had been voyeuristically watched was thoroughly unpleasant. Above all, it felt like an insult to the emotions Subaru and Kenichi had shared in that moment. Whether she understood his feelings or not, Echidna maintained her cheerful demeanor, giggling softly.

"The only regrettable part is that I could not more deeply savor your anguish upon confronting your past."

"Huh?"

"While I appreciate an answer being reached, I also believe that the struggle along the path to finding that answer holds praise for knowledge. To agonize, to struggle, and only then reach an answer—that was what I had looked forward to, but..."

She cast a sidelong glance at Subaru, narrowing her eyes as if peering into the depths of his black pupils.

"Unfortunately, it seems this 'Trial' was a bit too late to enjoy that. For it appears you had already reached an answer within yourself regarding the negative emotions you held toward the past."

"Ah... I get it. If that's the case, then my condolences."

Understanding the reason for Echidna's disappointment, Subaru let out a long breath through his nose. The way she had wanted him to face the 'Trial' was for Subaru to reunite with his parents—the source of his past trauma—realize his weakness through the days and hours spent with them, agonize over it, decide whether to run or face it, settle matters, and only then come here. However, in order to do that, Subaru had already—

"There was a girl who called an irredeemably hopeless guy like me a hero. So without even needing to face the past now, I'd already accepted just how hopeless I was."

"In a form distinct from resignation, yes. As for me, having my expectations thwarted is utterly displeasing. If you see that girl outside, do tell her that a Witch holds a grudge against her."

Subaru was about to make a lighthearted quip about that being a chilling threat, when he suddenly noticed something. An incongruity in what Echidna had said.

"Hey, you said earlier that you recreated this based on my memories... If you peeked inside my head, shouldn't you already know who that girl is?"

Or perhaps, rather than a realization, it was a feeling akin to clinging to hope. Even if it was through the means of peeking into Subaru's head, could there be someone who knew of Rem, who had been lost from that world—Rem's appearance, the existence of that lovely girl? However—

"Pardon me for dashing your hopes. Even for someone as Greed-driven as myself, there is a line between what is acceptable and what is not. While I drew out the information necessary for this 'Trial,' the rest remains completely untouched. What fun is there in simply gouging out knowledge and stealing a peek? I have no intention of throwing away the pleasure of having it recounted to me."

The answer returned was a brand of Witch's pride that Subaru couldn't comprehend. Call it Echidna's conviction; it was a logic that could only be described as the height of brazenness, yet having it said so bluntly left Subaru speechless. Still—

"You said you only extracted the parts necessary for the 'Trial'... Then why the hell did you pull out that uniform...?"

"Why, that was merely information extracted because it was necessary to recreate this building called a school, of course. It is most certainly not as though I learned of the unknown scenery of another world, wondered what kind of clothes the girls living there wore, and extracted it hoping to see if it would suit me, not at all."

"Are you... actually what they call a smart idiot?"

Sighing at Echidna, who was the very textbook definition of talking herself into a corner, Subaru shook his head. I see. It seemed he wouldn't be getting the answer he hoped for from her. And there was also one thing he had become certain of. That was—

"I probably don't even need to ask, but... As I thought, this world is..."

"Yes, indeed. This is a fictional world reconstructed by faithfully tracing reality as closely as possible, relying on your memories. Therefore, of course—your real parents are likely still worrying about their missing son, completely unaware of where you are or what you are doing."

"――――"

"Information you didn't know may have been presented to you... but did you truly not know it? Can you state with certainty that you never once laid eyes on a letter sent from a mutual acquaintance of your father and mother? Have you truly never met the elderly person who knew your father in his youth? Had you truly never once envisioned an image of your father that differed from what you thought?"

Echidna pressed on, barraging him with words. She then added, "Or perhaps—"

"Did you truly believe you had successfully concealed the inner feelings you thought were unknown? Did you have any certainty that your true desire—to have them know and be set at ease—had remained tightly sealed without leaking through the cracks of daily life? Can you declare with absolute certainty that you were not seeking that selfish desire to be loved, even so, from a fictional father, a fictional mother?"

Leaning in toward the silent Subaru, Echidna finished in a whispering tone that bewitchingly tickled his heart. And within breath's distance, she spoke.

"Do you not think—that it was just far too ideal, far too convenient?"

"――――"

Gently gouging Subaru's heart with her soft fingertips, Echidna smiled enchantingly. Unlike her smile from moments ago that had seemed somewhat fitting for her age, this one was utterly sinister through and through—the very embodiment of a 'Witch' as spoken of in legends. With his mind violated by her permeating words, Subaru squeezed his eyes tightly shut. Behind his closed eyelids. In that pitch-black world, the final images of his father and mother surfaced—

"Don't you go disparaging my parents just to get back at me for things not going your way, Witch."

"...What?"

"I told them all of my answers. Dad and Mom both accepted them. I said everything I couldn't say before, and they told me to do my best. They said, 'Take care,' to me."

Standing up from his chair, Subaru placed his hands on the desk and brought his forehead right against Echidna's, who had been leaning in toward him. Watching the Witch's black eyes widen in shock, Subaru spoke.

"Their voices back then, their smiles, every single bit of it blew past anything my imagination could come up with. —My parents aren't small enough Vessels to fit inside my imagination. Don't underestimate them."

"――――"

"I was able to convey everything to Dad and Mom. I settled things, and I came back. —I'm not gonna be swayed by your damn words."

Pushing back with his forehead, Subaru forced the Witch back and dropped into his chair once more. Crossing his legs with utter roughness, he leaned back arrogantly and looked up at Echidna. Echidna wore a dumbfounded expression at Subaru's attitude, but—

"Good grief... To not even show me a glimpse of yourself agonizing over the validity of the answer you reached—what a heartbreaker of a human you are to a Witch. Truly, magnificent."

"Being praised makes me blush. What's more, I'm the type of kid who grows when praised, so I'm just gonna get stronger, you know."

"Ever the sharp tongue... Ah, but it is enough. More than enough. For it is a joyous thing that an answer so steadfast has been reached that it permits no wavering."

Echidna shook her head in resignation and smiled. Turning the chair at the desk in front of Subaru toward him, she sat down.

"In the true sense, the 'Trial' is over. You have narrowly escaped the clutches of the Witch. In praise of that... before you return, is there anything you would like to ask? I intend to answer."

"Alright, then just one thing for now."

"Yes, go ahead."

Echidna nodded. Subaru thrust his raised finger straight at her.

"You're supposed to have said you had nothing to do with the 'Trials.' ...How the hell do you figure that?! You're not just heavily involved, you're the damn mastermind! What do you mean, 'no involvement'? What kind of nerve do you have to spew a pack of lies like that?!"

"Taking a Witch's words at face value shows a sheer lack of defensiveness and vigilance, does it not? I told you when we parted, did I not? That I am a wicked Witch."

"Yeah, sure. In that case, I don't trust a single word from such a wicked Witch-sama, so I don't have anything I wanna ask. ...With this, is the seal on the 'Sanctuary' broken?"

"Shifting your stance in the latter half without caring for appearances—truly splendid. Unfortunately, the 'Trials' are not so simple that it ends with this. There are three 'Trials' in total. Though, having overcome this first 'Trial,' I doubt the rest will prove terribly difficult for you."

Parrying the enraged Subaru's momentum, Echidna raised three fingers and answered nonchalantly. Hearing that, Subaru muttered, "Three, huh..." under his breath.

"Either way, the seal on the 'Sanctuary' has to be broken. It'll be lifted even if I'm the one who clears the 'Trials,' right? You can guarantee that much?"

"That is why I granted you the qualification. Naturally so. Whether it be you or any other qualified half-blood, if you overcome the 'Trials,' I shall grant my Blessing in the form of liberating the 'Sanctuary.' In exchange, how you will overcome the remaining two 'Trials'—I shall look forward to the answers you deliver."

Seeing Echidna's affirming nod, Subaru replied, "Got it," and stood up. He had nothing left to ask her. Even in this fictional world, there was nothing more to be gained by lingering. Though feelings akin to nostalgia tugged at him from behind, he had already said his farewells to what he had regretted most. Even if, just as the Witch had said, it was nothing more than a transient illusion.

"Hey, Echidna."

"What is it? Could it be that you wish to punch me once before leaving? Well, I am aware that I have committed acts deserving of such. If you desire it, I am not entirely unwilling to accept it meekly. However, I am still a girl, after all. If possible, I would prefer you avoid the face..."

"Thanks."

"――――"

Stunned silence. Echidna, who had been rambling on with unnecessary excuses, lost her voice at Subaru's words. Feeling a sense of satisfaction from her reaction for the very first time, Subaru said,

"Even if it wasn't the real thing, even if it didn't reach the real two, it's thanks to you that I was able to say what I wanted to say to them. Even if it was the result of your damn filthy curiosity, I got to meet the people I thought I'd never see again, and say goodbye."

To those two, whom he had continuously disappointed with his hopelessly small and pathetic self, he had been able to show himself looking up a little, acting a bit more decently, and standing with his chest held a little higher.

"For that alone, I'm grateful. So, thank you."

"...I cannot comprehend you as a human being; it is immensely fascinating. Terrifying, even."

Echidna looked at him with eyes that held genuine dread, devoid of any joke or falsehood. Subaru gave her a smirk, curling the corner of his mouth.

"Catching a tiny guy like me, and the great Witch-sama turns so timid. Well, whatever. So, where's the exit?"

"It is nothing difficult. The disappearance of this world has already begun. Aside from this building, nothing else is properly constructed. —Once you leave the building, you should find yourself right back inside the original tomb."

"Well, that's pretty convenient. —Alright, see you after the next 'Trial.'"

Waving his hand, Subaru left his seat and headed for the classroom exit. Feeling Echidna's gaze on his back, he walked forward without looking back again. The world outside the window, the scenery where a blue sky should have spread, was beginning to blur in the distance. The fictional world had fulfilled its role and was fading away to who knew where.

His father who had pushed Subaru forward, and his mother who had seen Subaru off, would also vanish along with the disappearance of this world. They would be nowhere to be found.

"...I've already been taught everything that matters, after all."

Emotions welled up within his chest. Feeling heat surge behind his eyes, Subaru rubbed his eyelids hard once with his sleeve. And when he lifted his face, not a trace of lingering tears remained in his eyes. Looking straight ahead, Subaru's feet carried him toward the exit of the ending world.

Ahead of him, slowly, slowly, the world turned white, grew distant, and then—

"—He left, did he? My, my. He is rather tougher than expected."

—The classroom after Subaru's departure. Inside the room lined only with empty desks and chairs, the left-behind Echidna ran her fingers through her bangs, leaning her weight against the desk in solitary amusement.

Lightly, the Collapse of the world was beginning. The world recreated relying on memories, having lost the one it relied upon, was returning to fictional dust. Feeling that fading world on her skin, Echidna's consciousness was nevertheless not directed in the slightest toward the ground beneath her feet that threatened to vanish at any moment, nor toward the atmosphere. Her consciousness was focused on a single point—in front of the blackboard, where the teacher's podium stood. There—

"As expected of the one upon whom you lavish your affections, should I say?"

"—My beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved to my beloved"

"Shutting yourself in your own castle, and even when you occasionally meet, you frighten him and make him forget. For such a pathetic state of affairs, you certainly assert yourself quite loudly. I simply cannot understand it."

"Unnecessary things unnecessary things unnecessary things unnecessary things unnecessary things unnecessary things filling his head filling his head filling his head filling his head filling his head don't talk don't talk don't talk don't talk don't touch don't touch don't touch don't touch don't touch mine mine mine mine mine mine mine I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you"

Words of sheer madness were uttered incessantly, causing Echidna to knit her brows in disgust. Before her eyes, in front of the podium, a single silhouette—the shadow of a woman—had formed. A shadow clad in a pitch-black dress, long silver hair fluttering. However, from her chest up, a sinister black shadow akin to a curse covered her entirely, preventing her face from being seen. Though, from the words she spoke and the madness she exuded, even letting one's imagination wander was nauseating.

Following Subaru's exit, Echidna accepted the entity that had abruptly materialized out of thin air as a matter of course. As though she had known all along that it would appear here.

"It is only natural, I suppose. I did step into the heart of the one you are so infatuated with without wiping my feet. I intended to avoid touching upon your territory, but... even so, it seems mutual non-aggression is out of the question?"

"Not a single fingertip not a single layer of skin not a single cuticle of a nail not a single strand of hair not a single drop of sweat not a single drop of saliva not a single word not a single breath not a single fragment of emotion everything everything everything everything everything everything everything everything everything everything everything everything everything everything everything everything everything everything everything everything..."

"All belongs to you, is that it? Good grief, standing before you makes even me want to surrender the title of 'Greed.' To go that far for a single person is utterly unfathomable."

"I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you I love you"

Hence, 'Envy' would never forgive Echidna for touching the taboo. The shadow took a single step forward. With just that, the space she had previously occupied was swallowed into shadow and disappeared. The front of the classroom, the wall where the blackboard had been mounted, and the desks up to the third row were all dragged into the Witch's shadow at once and masticated.

Echidna, who had barely managed to leap backward, escaped the damage, but the shadow took the shape of an arm and surged upward as if to pursue her, aiming for her throat. Facing the fangs of the writhing pitch-black serpent, Echidna let out a single breath.

"Having myself erased here leaves far too many regrets. I shall resort to a slightly underhanded trick—"

The moment Echidna spoke and lowered her posture slightly, the shadow that should have been aiming for Echidna's throat was struck by an impact and blown away. At that sight, the shadow's advancing footsteps halted. In front of the shadow with both arms hanging limply, standing in the spot where Echidna should have been, was—

"Being called out this frequently, haah. I can't even sleep in peace, fuuh."

Sighing listlessly while plopped flat on the ground was a woman with reddish-purple hair—the manifestation of the 'Witch of Sloth,' Sekhmet. Confirming her presence, the shadow nevertheless attempted to step forward, having no intention of changing what it had come to do. But—

"Haah, it's useless, you know."

The shadow's upper body recoiled as if struck by a tremendous impact. Under the force of the blow, the shadow retreated, sinking halfway into the darkness it had generated. Raising its head sluggishly at that outcome, the shadow lifted its right arm and aimed it at Sekhmet. Instantly, from the shadow that had swallowed half the classroom, black unseen hands enough to blanket the entire field of vision were unleashed all at once. A black shadow of death sealing off all directions from every quarter—yet even that—

"I'm telling you, it's useless, fuuh."

All of the swirling black hands were obliterated in an instant, and the relentless shockwaves continuously slammed into the shadow's entire body. The vibrating body of the shadow was pinned against the wall by the impact, and Sekhmet, looking up at it with a lethargic expression, remained completely motionless with her knees hugged to her chest. Yet, even still, Sekhmet's attacks continued to be driven into the shadow's entire body, gradually beginning to shatter its amorphous form.

Listening to the sounds of impact and watching the writhing shadow, Sekhmet roughly scratched her hair.

"With your power mostly sealed away, haah. On top of that, inside the castle made by that nasty-natured Echidna, fuuh. With your base strength inferior, haah, there's no way you can beat me, fuuh."

Sekhmet suppressed a yawn. The strikes ceased, and the battered shadow fell to its knees on the spot, collapsing—whereupon an impact from directly above mercilessly crushed it into the ground. Sinking into the black shadow, the vanishing 'Envy' looked up at Sekhmet.

"Why why why why why why why between me and between me and between me and between me and him him him him him him interfering interfering interfering interfering interfering interfering interfering—?"

"Haah. —Even answering is too much trouble."

Spitting out those biting words, Sekhmet gave a small downward wave of her raised hand. At that moment, half of the school building collapsed from the impact, and the tumbling earth and rubble swallowed the shadow of 'Envy,' plunging it into the depths of the earth. In a world whose disappearance had already begun, falling past such a collapse left no way to ever return.

"Even after dying, why, fuuh, do I have to keep dealing with that sort of thing, haah."

A world whose collapse had accelerated due to her own actions. In a corner of the vanishing classroom, the 'Witch of Sloth' slid her rear along the floor toward whichever corner seemed slightly better amidst the crumbling surroundings. Leaning her back against the wall, feeling the sensation of being retracted along with the ending world and the end of her role, she looked up at the sun outside the finally shattered window.

"Nothing ever goes the way one wants, fuuh. Not for Witches—nor for those enchanted by Witches, haah."

Immediately after that weary sigh escaped her lips, the world vanished along with the light.
