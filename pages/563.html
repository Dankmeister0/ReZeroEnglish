Arc 7 Chapter 57B: "Call Me a Fool and Welcome to It"

――When Natsuki Subaru's whereabouts became unknown, Emilia panicked more than she ever had in her life.

"Please, Subaru. Please be all right, together with Rem."

The violent shadow that assailed the Pleiades Watchtower had swept away not just Subaru and Rem — no, to be precise, not only the two of them, but one more uncertain factor along with them. That Emilia hadn't fallen completely to pieces over it was because Beatrice and Ram, who shared a connection to both Subaru and Rem, could at least guarantee that the two of them were alive.

Of course, having their lives didn't mean they weren't in danger, or facing something frightening. If anything, since Subaru would have to rescue Rem sleeping in an unfamiliar land, it was an absolute certainty that he'd push himself recklessly. And yet neither Emilia nor Beatrice could be there at his side — which was more than enough to send her into a panic.

"We have to hurry and find Subaru and the others...!"

With that resolve, Emilia and the group that had gone to conquer the Pleiades Watchtower sprang into motion. First, they had to find some way to sneak into the Vollachian Empire to the south — the country that currently forbade travel to and from the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica — since that seemed to be where Subaru had been blown.

"We'll try pushing in from the Kararagi side. Kararagi's probably got looser watch than Lugunica and Vollachia, what with those two glaring at each other like always."

So said Anastasia, who had promised to help search for Subaru. Anastasia and her group, including Julius and Echidna, had also been through an intense ordeal at the Pleiades Watchtower, and were surely in no easy position themselves. Even so, that she offered this for Subaru's sake made Emilia truly happy.

"I'm glad I got to be friends with Anastasia-san."

"Well, I don't think 'friends' is quite right. You and I are still rivals, same as ever. But—"

"But?"

"Once everything's settled — Natsuki-kun, the Royal Selection, all of it — let's be friends. Same as you, I don't have that many friends myself."

Saying so in an easygoing tone, Anastasia promised to lend her strength. Even knowing this wasn't the time for it, Emilia found herself looking forward to the day that promise would be fulfilled — not just the search for Subaru, but the promise to become her friend. And then—

"If Anastasia-sama's arrangement is to cross from Kararagi, then our best bet here would simply be to find a way across the Vollachian border, wouldn't it, no-o-ow."

Roswaal, rejoining Emilia and the others upon their return from the Pleiades Watchtower and grasping the urgency of the situation, respected Emilia's wish to search for Subaru at once. However, even using Roswaal's position and power, entering the Vollachian Empire apparently wasn't a simple matter. Even trying to fly in, they'd be bitten out of the sky by flying dragons.

Even with Roswaal's backing, getting into the Empire wouldn't be easy. Above all, the Empire itself was vast. Even if they somehow got in, there was no telling whether they could simply stumble upon Subaru and the others. In a situation growing ever more desperate, the breakthrough came, as always, from someone reliable.

"As for a way into the Vollachian Empire — it's not that I have no idea at all. Though it's not exactly a method to be proud of, and there's danger involved."

The one who hesitantly broached the subject was Otto, who had returned to the mansion along with Emilia and the others. He had been recuperating in the water-gate city of Priestella, and returned together with Garfiel, who had stayed behind with him — and now he offered a path of hope for the problem that had them all so troubled. That method, to put it plainly—

"—Smuggling ourselves in. From Picoutatte, one of the five great cities south of Lugunica, and the town where my family home is, I might be able to arrange a way in."

Smuggling meant crossing a border secretly, skipping the proper procedures and paperwork. As Otto said, it wasn't a method to be proud of, and considering what would happen if they were caught, there was no denying it was a very dangerous option. But if there was no other way—

"We have to do everything we possibly can for Subaru and the others."

With firm resolve, Emilia conveyed her decision to everyone. Ram and Otto raised various objections to Emilia's determination, but no one said "let's not." Everyone was praying for Subaru's safety. That, to Emilia, was the thing she was proudest of, more than anything.

"No matter what, we bring Subaru back safe. Let's get this done, shall we."

"Yes, quite right. — Let's do our best."

Squeezed tight by the small hand of Beatrice, who was anxious being separated from Subaru, Emilia gently but firmly squeezed back in answer.

And so, amid an incident with an old acquaintance targeting Otto's life in his hometown, Petra's bold negotiation with the group guiding them across the border, a bridal uproar surrounding Frederica's special constitution, and more, the group crossed the border in search of Subaru and the others—

"—That's far enough."

So declared Emilia, bringing snow down upon the frenzied, storm-wracked walled city.

"――――"

Countless buildings had collapsed, and the once-beautiful streets had crumbled beyond recognition. And still, watching from a distance as great shadows crossed back and forth through the sky, using their wings to smash apart people's lives, Emilia's anger only grew.

Why could anyone do something this terrible? Wasn't there some other way? Had anyone even properly tried to look for one? But even as she thought this, Emilia herself couldn't think of any way to stop the fighting in this moment other than using her own power — and that frustrated her. So Emilia let herself feel that frustration to the fullest, resolving not to forget it. To that end—

"—Ice Age."

At Emilia's murmured words, heat began draining from the world around her at a furious pace. The air slowly cooled, and from the chilling sky white flecks of snow crystal began to flicker down. Swiftly, inescapably, Emilia blanketed the area around the city in cold.

Careful not to overdo it, she adjusted the power of her magic with great care. She couldn't afford a slip that would freeze this city solid like her homeland, the Elior Forest. And of course, even considering that it could be melted later, cooling it enough to induce a state of suspended animation — like what Regulus's brides had suffered — was absolutely forbidden.

What Emilia had to do was bring an unbearable ice season to a land of the Empire that knew no cold. A biting, freezing wind; falling white snow crystals; a season of magical ice where every breath turned white — all of it blanketing this walled city struck by the Flying Dragon Cataclysm. If she did that—

"Dragonkind is weak to cold. All the more so for flying dragons, who know nothing of freezing chill — the harshness will tell on their wings all the more plainly."

So said a voice, and with a light footfall a figure leapt atop the half-collapsed city wall where Emilia stood. Glancing that way, Emilia nodded and said, "Yeah."

"Petra-chan told me flying dragons are weak to cold, before we set out. She's super studious, and got real knowledgeable about the Empire so fast."

"So you relied on your attendant's wisdom. No wonder — for a half-demon lacking the wit to plan for herself, I found myself thinking you'd struck a decent enough strategy."

"You don't have to put it like that. And besides, how do you even know I'm a half-elf...? Huh?"

Emilia, pouting her lips at the way the other spoke, suddenly widened her violet eyes. Clad in a red dress, long orange hair streaming in the cold wind — the hairstyle was different from the one Emilia remembered, but there, standing before her, was—

"Eh, Priscilla? Why are you here? This isn't Lugunica, you know?"

"I could return that exact question to you. Though I have some notion of why you set foot in the Empire... You've quite the nerve, crossing borders like that."

"Ah, the method's a secret, okay? I'm not supposed to say. And apparently I have to hide who I am too. So be careful. I'm..."

At this unexpected reunion — Priscilla's figure, someone Emilia never expected to see in this foreign land to the south — Emilia responded with surprise mixed with a strange sense of nostalgia. Still, she couldn't afford to be surprised for long. The method by which Emilia had entered the Empire was against the rules, so to speak — a bad thing to have done. She needed to make that clear to Priscilla properly. But—

"—! Priscilla, watch out!"

Cutting off her words mid-sentence, Emilia called out a warning. The next instant, Priscilla clicked her tongue and leapt back, and where she'd been standing was gouged out by a furiously spinning blade — a Flying Winged Blade. The battered, near-collapsing wall was cleanly shaved by the blade's edge, and by a stroke of miraculous irony, was thereby spared from total collapse. Priscilla, having dodged the blade, landed with her dress hem fluttering, this time much closer to Emilia — right beside her. And then—

"One after another, what is it with you people?"

Catching the returning Flying Winged Blade in one hand, a girl's voice, thick with fury, reached Emilia. Looking down, in the midst of the ruined streets stood a horned girl alone on the road, her eyes gleaming as she glared up at Emilia and Priscilla side by side. It seemed she was quite angry too. But Emilia was quite angry as well. And so, Emilia pointed sharply at the girl and declared,

"My name is Emili— Emily! Just a passing Spiritual Arts User!"

She nearly blurted out her real name from the momentum, but managed to give the false name they'd arranged beforehand instead. At Emilia's bold declaration, Priscilla narrowed her eyes, while the named girl's eyes sharpened with wariness as she murmured, "A Spiritual Arts User..."

"Same as that Dog Human, is it?"

"If you're thinking of Arakiya, that one is a 'Spirit Eater,' not a Spiritual Arts User. You're mistaken, Madelyn Eschart."

"Guh...!"

As if reading the thoughts of the girl — Madelyn — Priscilla's remark made her cheeks tense. Still, there was something in what was just said that Emilia couldn't let pass.

"What's a 'Spirit Eater'? What do they do to spirits?"

"Just as it sounds — they eat spirits. Taking that power into themselves and using it to the fullest. The art has all but died out; even in the Empire, only Arakiya practices it, more or less."

"They eat spirits!? That's so awful..."

For Emilia, a Spiritual Arts User herself who had Contracts with numerous minor spirits, it was an unbelievable thing to hear. If Puck or Beatrice ever encountered a "Spirit Eater," would they get bitten into as well? Just imagining it was pitiable. But more than anxiety over something that hadn't happened, the problem in front of her took priority now.

"You're Madelyn-chan?"

"...Don't look down on dragons, half-demon."

"Then let's talk properly, Madelyn. You're the one behind the flying dragons attacking this city, aren't you? I want you to stop. Right now."

Casting her gaze afar, Emilia looked past the range of Madelyn's rampage to the far side of the city, where a swarm of flying dragons continued their assault. Their movements seemed, ever so slightly, more sluggish than before. Emilia's power was dropping the temperature, beginning to affect the dragons' vitality. If this continued, it might be possible to bring every last flying dragon down to the ground. But she'd rather not do that, if she could help it. She didn't want to let even the flying dragons die.

"If you care about your flying dragons, I want you to listen to my request."

"――――"

"Madelyn?"

Emilia had meant to convey her words as sincerely as she could. Perhaps Otto, Ram, or Petra could have spoken more skillfully. But when they saw the city under attack by the swarm of flying dragons, Emilia was the only one who could rush there in time. Having been the first to arrive, Emilia would do everything within her power. The result of which—

"For a dragon to be petitioned — how utterly presumptuous, human...!"

So it was, even if it meant setting off Madelyn's fury.

"—Neither you nor anyone else could talk her down. That one is dragonkin; she is not so gracious a creature as to lend an ear to the words of one rooted to the earth, to begin with."

At Madelyn's furious retort, Emilia caught her breath. Priscilla, who had listened silently to the exchange between the two, offered her assessment of the failed negotiation. It felt almost as if she were encouraging her, and Emilia's eyes widened.

"Priscilla, I always thought you were just someone who liked to be mean to me."

"To state that the unfit is unfit is not, in my eyes, spite. Do not twist my merciful words. — Now then, what shall we do."

"Right."

Nodding, Emilia conjured an ice hairpin in her outstretched left hand. Offered the hairpin, Priscilla closed one eye dubiously. Seeing Priscilla's reaction, Emilia nodded and said,

"Let's stop that girl together. No being mean, okay?"

"Do not demean my conduct as some show of childishness, you half-demon upstart."

Grumbling all the while, Priscilla took the hairpin from Emilia's hand, gathering her own long, loosened hair and pinning it up. At the sound of the ice clasp clicking into place, Priscilla's bearing returned to the one Emilia knew, and Emilia turned back to face Madelyn below. And then—

"—Fools unafraid of dragons are tiresome indeed."

"I just got into a fight with a big Dragon a little while ago. So."

"—!"

"Even if you want me to be scared, I'm not going to be scared of you!"

To Madelyn's threat, baring white fangs, Emilia answered with clear conviction. Immediately after, Madelyn's eyes narrowed like an Earth Dragon's, and the Flying Winged Blade in her hand was hurled toward them with terrifying speed and force.

Watching it spin closer, Emilia conjured a great ice hammer in each hand. Timing her swing to meet the spinning blade, Emilia swung her ice hammer up from directly below, trying to knock it away.

"H— hnngh!!"

A fierce recoil jolted through both her arms as Emilia planted her feet and forced the hammer upward with all her strength. Barely, the blow deflected the blade's trajectory, and it passed over the heads of both Emilia and Priscilla. But at the unbelievable force of the impact, the ice hammer shattered to pieces, and Emilia looked down at her own hands.

"That's amazing strength... maybe way stronger than me...!"

"Of course it is! Comparing dragon and human on the same scale is a mistake to begin—"

Stepping forward with her small frame, Madelyn crowed triumphantly over the pained Emilia. — Onto Madelyn's head, from above, fell a mass of ice larger than the city hall.

"—!?"

Her throat catching at the sudden appearance of the ice mass and its sheer scale and weight, Madelyn's form was crushed beneath it. A cold shockwave erupted outward from her in every direction, a white gale blasting away rubble and half-ruined buildings, wreaking tremendous destruction.

"And you, with what face do you tell others not to destroy the city?"

"Eh! But that part was already destroyed, so..."

"You take a crude view of things. Your attendant must find that quite the headache. Still — now, perhaps, you finally understand."

"Finally understand?"

Gazing at the spot where the ice mass had fallen, Priscilla crossed her arms and narrowed her eyes. Emilia questioned her profile, but the answer came at once, from both Priscilla's lips and the scene before them. The ice mass that should have crushed Madelyn — driven into the ground — trembled faintly, and a great sound, as though the world itself were cracking, began resounding from within the ice itself. It originated from the point of impact, where a girl now thrust her arms upward from within the fallen ice.

"—Just how troublesome a being dragonkin can be."

The moment after Priscilla's words, with a thunderous roar, the massive block of ice split clean in two. The shock of the destruction rippled through the whole, the shattered ice turning to dust of mana from its edges. In the very center of that scene of scattering ice shards, a roar rang out, and the incarnation of the Dragon, the dragonkin, leapt at the two of them.

――A freezing wind raged, and amid a battlefield ruled by the impulse toward destruction, two lovely figures danced.

Had anyone watched that battle from afar, they would surely have doubted their eyes at its sheer majesty. Was this truly reality, or something out of a dream?

"—!!"

Putting enough force into it to crack the ground itself, the small-statured dragonkin hurled a spinning "death." Drawing an arc, the destructive blade that mowed down everything in its path cut and sliced the world beautifully, in stark contrast to its tyrannical menace. Had this been a single painting, it might have appeared, to the discerning eye of an ordinary mortal, not as destruction but as pruning meant to beautifully trim the world. That was how refined, how instinctively magnificent Madelyn Eschart's fury was. And yet—

"Priscilla! Here!"

Conjuring footholds of ice in midair, dodging the hurled Flying Winged Blade as her silver hair danced, the heavenly beauty swept her hand toward the ground below, and the streets, already stripped of their original form, froze over. From the freezing earth, rising up, came an ice sword of beautiful ornamentation. A running beauty, sweeping past, plucked it from the ground as she passed,

"Were I anyone but myself, my fingers would drop off. — But the ornamentation isn't bad. I'll make use of it."

A pale blue slash of absolute ice raced out, cutting diagonally across Madelyn's body. But whatever power that small body concealed, the single blow only made Madelyn reel back lightly, while the ice sword that struck her shattered instead. Undeterred, Madelyn reached her hand toward the approaching Priscilla, but—

"Toryaa!!"

Not permitting such an outrage, Emilia's legs, dropping straight down from above, struck Madelyn. Spinning as she brought down both legs clad in ice greaves, Emilia's merciless attack, meant to crush the girl's shoulders, forced even the sturdy Madelyn to stagger a step. In that instant—

"Please!" "Do not give me orders."

Landing, Emilia planted both hands on the ground, and the twin ice swords thus conjured were snatched up by Priscilla, who leapt over the crouching Emilia to slash at Madelyn. Right hand and left, the ice blades Priscilla wielded came at Madelyn from different angles, and she caught them on the claws of her outstretched hands, a shrill ringing sound cutting through the air.

"Don't get carried away...!?"

"Eryaa!"

Batting away the twin swords and moving to counterattack, Madelyn found an ice spearhead thrust at her face, passing right beside Priscilla's head. Madelyn tilted her body to dodge on reflex, and in that opening Emilia, switching places with Priscilla, thrust an ice spear forward at a speed too fast to follow with the eye.

"Switch."

As Madelyn managed to fend off that spear, next to display a dance of ice blades was Priscilla. Her red dress hem flaring, wielding twin blue-gleaming ice swords with fluid grace, Priscilla's sword dance in the white, frozen world battered Madelyn's whole body, forcing her back.

"—Why, why, why why why, why is this happening!?"

Pressed by Priscilla's twin swords, forced onto the defensive, Madelyn screamed at her own helplessness. But Priscilla's onslaught didn't let up. And worse still for Madelyn, like a nightmare, the moment Priscilla's sword-dance paused,

"Toryaa! Soyaa! Eitaa!!"

Emilia leapt in without a moment's rest, conjuring one ice weapon after another, wielding them with unreasonable adaptability and slamming them down with all her strength.

——Priscilla with skill, Emilia with power, together sealing off Madelyn's every attack and defense completely.

Mowing down with the Flying Winged Blade, striking, swinging, hurling it. The physical prowess dragonkin were born with was beyond comparison to feeble humans. Even Demi-Humans, somewhat sturdier than humans, were like dead branches before dragonkin. And detestable as it was to admit, the same held true even for the Oni Tribe, often spoken of alongside dragonkin.

Dragonkin and Oni were sometimes spoken of together as the strongest races, but that was absurd. In the end, the Oni Tribe were nothing more than superior blood among Demi-Humans. Dragonkin were fundamentally different from such things.

Dragonkin were not Demi-Humans. They were a species utterly different at the root from those descended from the human race. Dragonkin were not human. — And yet, why, then, was nothing going right?

"What are you two... what ARE you!?"

Her rage-driven attacks kept missing, again and again, until at last even her outpouring of fury ran dry. And what remained after the dragon's wrath was spent was an ugly, cracking lament of a dragon. Swinging the Flying Winged Blade she'd caught in a horizontal sweep, Emilia ducked and Priscilla leapt to dodge it. And then, split above and below, the two answered Madelyn's lament simultaneously.

Asked what they were—

"—Emily!" "I am myself."

A raised great ice axe and two flashing blades of ice struck Madelyn directly, each in turn. Struck sharply on the horn atop her head by the ice blades, and taking a savage blow from the ice axe upon her torso, Madelyn was sent flying backward without a chance to break her fall. In the midst of battle, upon ground thinly blanketed with unceasing snow, Madelyn's body bounced high once, twice, and with that same momentum plunged into a mountain of rubble, sending up a great cloud of white dust with a tremendous roar.

"――――"

Leaving Madelyn's hand, the Flying Winged Blade flew on to crash into the wall behind, and the wall that had continued protecting the walled city from threats to the south at last finished its duty. With the loud crash of the collapsing wall as the final note, Emilia slowly straightened up, watching the mountain of rubble that had buried Madelyn without letting her guard down.

"...Did we get her, I wonder."

"There was a fitting response. A pity there was no bite to it, though."

"Bite...?"

Beside her, Priscilla stepped forward; watching the twin swords in her hands turn to dust, Emilia tilted her head. She glanced, somehow, at Priscilla's mouth, but nothing had been eaten. Priscilla met Emilia's gaze with her crimson eyes and said,

"I mean that for dragonkin — and one holding the rank of one of the 'Nine Divine Generals' at that — this was rather underwhelming."

"That, and the Nine Divine Generals thing — but what's a dragonkin? They're a Demi-Human, right?"

"A race said to have died out in ancient times. Supposedly they held power to commune with dragons, no longer seen anywhere in this world — though perhaps that was mere superstition."

"Dragon... does that mean she can talk to Volcanica too? He's kind of senile now, though..."

At Emilia's hesitant question, Priscilla's brow rose slightly. Then, pressing a finger lightly to her lips, she said,

"To think such words would come from your mouth. Have I misjudged you somewhat?"

"—? Ah, are you thinking that's a joke? It's not a joke at all. Actually, I met Volcanica a little while ago, and from being alone so long he's gotten kind of senile..."

At Priscilla's unconvinced reaction, Emilia hastened to protest. But she quickly remembered "oh no" — something more urgent —

"If we beat her, we have to get Madelyn to call off the flying dragons!"

"In this cold, the sluggish flying dragons will all be hunted down before long regardless."

"But there are people in danger in the meantime! Subaru and Rem might be in danger too, for all I know..."

"—Hmm."

Darting past Priscilla, Emilia headed for the rubble that had buried Madelyn. At the exchange over her shoulder, Priscilla's shapely brows furrowed. Not that the reaction reached Emilia at all — she strode over the rubble with her long legs, conjuring an ice digging pole and driving it into the debris to try and dig out the buried Madelyn.

Meanwhile, watching her back, Priscilla let out a small sigh. This one gambit, landing unexpectedly well, and the result it brought — that much, she would allow, was good. But the tremendous damage Madelyn and the flying dragons had wrought could not simply be undone.

At worst, she'd have to consider relocating their base to the demon capital of Chaosflame, or relying on their collaborator, Senior Count Serena Dracroy.

"In any case, first we must see their wings folded."

Pausing her thoughts on what lay ahead, Priscilla looked up at the white, clouded sky. Just how vast was the mana Emilia possessed — even within a limited range, wielding power equal to shifting the very climate; depending on how it was used, there were any number of options to choose from. Of course, what was prized in Vollachia was not necessarily valued in Lugunica. Were she to decide, instead, to live in Vollachia, she need not waste her time in vain. Still, setting nature aside, given Emilia's character, she likely couldn't bring herself to live in Vollachia—

"――――"

So thought Priscilla, tracing thoughts across the sky, when her eyes flickered faintly. Her jewel-like crimson eyes wavered because she caught a slight wrongness in the vast sky she gazed up at. And even as Priscilla came to understand the nature of that wrongness—

"Found her! Madelyn, I'll get you out now. But you have to behave and listen to us..."

"—Ah"

"Eh?"

Finding the girl buried in the rubble, Emilia used her ice digging pole to clear away the building debris. Covered in dirt-stained snow, her clothes soiled, the dragonkin girl lay on her back. Her lips moved, and a hoarse voice escaped — Emilia strained to listen. She hoped for words admitting defeat, words meaning she'd call off the flying dragons. But, contrary to Emilia's hopes, what Madelyn's lips wove was not a declaration of surrender.

What she murmured was a single word — a name.

"—Mezoreia."

――The next instant, from above the clouds, a "Dragon's" roar rained destruction down upon the earth.

The instant white light was unleashed from the heavens, Emilia and Priscilla were already moving as one.

Without a sound, without a signal, a tower of ice sprang up, and at its peak, rising from the ground, two beauties — silver and crimson — leapt into place. Atop the tower, which had shot up several dozen meters in an instant, Emilia raised both hands to the sky, conjuring a massive dome of ice over the area where the white light was predicted to land. Then, inside that dome, she conjured another dome, slightly smaller, and inside that one still another, smaller yet, repeating the process until six layers of ice domes covered the sky.

And while Emilia constructed her defense of ice and snow, behind her Priscilla extended her smooth fingers into empty space and drew the "Sunlight Sword" from its sheath of void. A radiant treasure sword adorned with brilliant red light and countless eye-catching jewels — yet the light spilling from within it was, compared to its full glory, pitifully weak. Even so, Priscilla raised the treasured sword and looked up overhead.

The next instant, the falling white light struck the outermost dome directly, and in less than a second, the first dome, then the second, the third, were pierced through, the light bearing down toward the ground. But to say that the pierced, shattered domes had utterly failed in their purpose would be false.

Receiving the light, in the instant of being pierced, the angle of the light shifted, ever so slightly. The first, second, and third domes caused only minute changes, but by the fourth and fifth these became modest shifts, and by the moment the sixth was breached, the light's trajectory had been altered for certain.

Straight down, the white light fell toward the ice tower, toward the two standing atop it. Meeting it head-on, a crimson flash of beauty and brilliance was unleashed, a single defiant slash cutting through the air—

△▼△▼△▼△

"――――"

All sound vanished completely, and a silence never before experienced lasted, by feel, some ten seconds. When that stillness — which in reality had probably lasted less than a single second — finally burst, the shockwave spreading from the southern side of the city swallowed the walled city whole in every direction, turning it upside down.

The ground was torn up, buildings ripped from their very foundations, unsheltered people sent tumbling like dead leaves, and even the swarm of flying dragons swimming through the sky was battered by the storm. Mercilessly, indiscriminately, the shockwave that swallowed everything struck everyone in the city.

Naturally, the wounded were carried in, and inside the largest mansion in the city, now turned into a makeshift infirmary, Flop, who had been rushing about tending to the injured, was also caught in the shock and thrown against a wall, back-first.

"—Ugh."

Perhaps a few seconds, perhaps a dozen, or perhaps even minutes had passed. Taking that fierce impact square in the back, he felt a sensation as though all his internal organs had been turned inside out. There wasn't much pain, but that might mean it was coming later — or that whatever he'd suffered was too severe to register as pain at all, which was not out of the question.

If that were actually the case, he'd like to just go on living an unremarkable everyday life, watch his little sister Medium settle into a safe and happy life, savor the satisfaction of having gotten his revenge against the world, and remain unaware of it right up until the moment old age finally claimed him. If it went that far, no one would even be able to tell whether he'd died of his wound or of old age.

"There, there, probably nothing too serious, I don't think..."

Hedging his bets upon bets, he carefully assessed his own condition before pushing himself up. His arms and legs moved, and all his fingers were accounted for. Fortunately, his ears and nose seemed intact, and his eyes weren't blown out either. Caution was still warranted, but there was no use being overly pessimistic either.

Shaking his head, Flop got to his feet, wiped his blurred vision clear, and looked around. What had once been a solemn and splendid décor had been utterly transformed; the inside of the mansion had become a hellish tableau ruled by the reek of blood and groans of pain. Now, with this latest shock added on top, the wretched scene had grown even more vivid. Some of the people he'd only just finished treating might have had their pain flare back up from this, or worse. He needed to ease their suffering and lend a hand once more.

"I wonder if the butler-kun and Utakata-jō are all right..."

Having left dealing with the encountered Madelyn to Priscilla, Flop and the others had rejoined Rem and the rest at the mansion, helping tend to the wounded wherever they could. Even Schult, his eyes still ablaze with flame, worried about Priscilla but helped out all the same, and had finally managed to steady his heart somewhat. For that to be undone by a single shock like this one — it would be a shame, to say the least.

"Just what in the world..."

As Flop surveyed his surroundings, a pained voice rose in a groan. Looking over, it was Rem, peering out from the depths of the hall. She, too, who had been tending to a seriously wounded patient requiring healing magic, was one of those left reeling from the earlier shock.

Leaning on her cane, Rem frowned and peered out the window to see what had happened. Flop grew wary too, wondering if something even more terrifying than the swarm of flying dragons or one of the "Nine Divine Generals" rampaging with all her might had occurred. The very next instant—

"—This is bad."

Her eyes widening, expression turning sharp, Rem hurried toward the mansion's door. Flop called out "Ma'am!" after her retreating back as she dashed outside, not saying what she'd seen. Pressing down against his aching side and knee, Flop chased after Rem outside. There, he found her crouched beside a figure lying collapsed in the mansion's front garden.

Someone else, apparently blown here by the shock outside — another injured person. As Flop approached where the crouching Rem was checking on the person's condition, he found himself struck by the sheer scale of the earlier shock, something he hadn't been able to grasp from inside the mansion.

"This is..."

White snow drifted down over Guaral. Thick layers of cloud hung over the southern sky, with sunlight pouring down as if piercing straight through them. It was a sight almost fantastical, and Flop, exhaling a white breath, shuddered.

Suddenly, the surrounding temperature had dropped dramatically, and on top of that, this cataclysmic, otherworldly spectacle was unfolding. Even in a dream, such a sight would hardly ever manifest. Which meant, paradoxically, that this wasn't a dream — Flop shook his head at the thought.

"Flop-san, please help me. We need to carry this child inside..."

"Right, got it. Even under a sky this bizarre and wondrous, all we can do is what we can do. Let's give it our—"

Turning to help Rem, meaning to say "all we've got," Flop's blue eyes suddenly widened, his words cut off mid-sentence. The reason was plain, standing right behind Rem, who had called for his help.

Behind Rem — the figure blown here by the earlier shock, the one she'd suggested carrying inside for treatment — was small. Small, lovely, with two black horns upon her head. And whether from clouded consciousness or otherwise, the girl, slowly pushing herself up behind Rem, was carelessly swinging an arm equipped with sharp claws.

"――――"

Once again, Flop felt sound vanish and the flow of time seem to slip out of sync with reality. Rem, looking his way, hadn't noticed the threat closing in behind her. And even the threat herself, the girl, didn't seem driven by intent to harm Rem so much as by sheer defensive instinct. She must have been through something truly awful. He could sympathize — but sympathy changing nothing about the situation was the cruel, frustrating part of it.

"――――"

Kuna and Holly, in charge of guarding the mansion, were probably also still taking time to recover from the earlier shock. They were nowhere in sight, and no help could be expected from them. Schult, his eyes still burning, Utakata, holding back her fighting spirit to help with the wounded, Heinkel, still unconscious and not waking — none of them could be relied upon either.

Closing his eyes, behind his eyelids he could see his little sister's face, beaming fit to burst. Vowing never to let her brother suffer pain again, she'd truly grown strong — his proud little sister. And the benefactor who'd given him the chance to set out into the world with her, his sworn brother — all sorts of faces flickered past.

——Value your own life, Flop. Self-sacrifice is something only an utter fool would do.

Once, his benefactor had laughed at him like that, for living so recklessly. In this very moment, suddenly remembering that time, Flop decided to say the same thing back.

"I don't mind being called an utter fool."

Because all the people he loved had clapped their hands and laughed at that answer of his.

"Flop-sa—"

Stepping forward, reaching out a hand to push the slender shoulder, he shoved Rem out of the way. And in the path of the swinging claws, one utter fool stepped in to take her place.

――Blood scattered, and Flop O'Connell collapsed onto the cold ground.
