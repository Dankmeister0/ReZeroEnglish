Arc 4 Chapter 53: "Overlapping Questions"

Moving from the tomb, the result of the "Trial" was discussed at Ryuzu's house. The content was almost no different from the loops so far. Emilia had failed to break through the "Trial," and had declared that she still had no intention of giving up.

She kept her lips sealed about the particulars of the "Trial," and when Ram, sensing this, smoothed things over, the gathering broke up on the pretext of Emilia's condition. Everyone filed out of the room, reflecting on the night's events, and once only Emilia, Subaru, and Ram remained inside,

"Ram would like to help Emilia-sama get ready for bed now, but is Barusu, who wasn't invited, simply unable to notice that without being told?"

"Thanks for that roundabout-yet-not-roundabout way of putting it. Given Emilia's condition, sure, I want to let you get on with your job — but I need a little time. Just a bit. Could you leave me and Emilia alone for a minute?"

"How lewd."

"You're the lewd one for jumping straight to that thought!"

Subaru shouted at Ram, whose eyes had filled with distaste, and the pink-haired maid covered her ears and put on an air of innocence. Then Ram cast a sidelong glance at Emilia, who sat on the edge of the bed,

"What would Emilia-sama like to do? If you don't even want to exchange words with Barusu, Ram will have him removed."

"What is with you? Sweet, compassionate Emilia-tan would never reject me. Right?"

"Tonight I'm reeeally tired, so... I think I'd rather pass on our usual chitchat..."

"'Chitchat,' nobody says that these days. ...Besides, that's not what I'm after right now. Wooing Emilia-tan is for another time. Right now I just want to talk about the tomb a little."

Even with their usual back-and-forth exchanged, Emilia's expression didn't brighten. Still, when Subaru hinted at something related to the "Trial," Emilia closed her eyes once, then turned her indigo gaze to Ram and said "sorry," prompting her to leave the room. Obeying the instruction without a word, Ram bowed and headed for the door. On the way, passing by Subaru's side, she murmured in a low voice,

"See that you don't make her talk about anything too burdensome."

— a stern order she did not forget to leave behind. When the door clicked shut, only Subaru and Emilia remained in the bedroom. Subaru could see that Emilia's eyes held a note of earnestness as she looked up at him from the bed. Receiving that gaze, he gave a light shrug and said,

"I could only check you over quickly inside the tomb, but — really, nothing wrong with your body? Given how abnormal that place was, I'm worried."

"That's fine. Yeah, thanks. Really, there doesn't seem to be anything strange interfering with my body or my head. And if you're worried about that, shouldn't it be about you rather than me?"

"How so?"

"Not 'how so.' Subaru, I'm glad you came into the tomb after me when I didn't come back, but something must have happened to you in there too. Ram said it was nearly thirty minutes between when you went in and when you came out with me."

Emilia's gaze carried a note of reproach, and inwardly Subaru stuck out his tongue. Inside the tomb, he'd managed to smooth things over with momentum, but thanks to Ram's unnecessary remark, it seemed his inconsistency had been noticed. The time it took him to step inside, and the time lost before he brought her out — Emilia's expression began to tense as she imagined what might have happened in between.

Naturally, he could tell the content of the "Trial" was rising in her mind. The first "Trial," which forces one to face the past. In the very first loop, Emilia had been terribly distraught, wondering if he'd learned what it contained. This time she was keeping her composure, but it wasn't hard to imagine that if she reached the same conclusion as before, that composure would be lost. So —

"Subaru, could it be... that you also went through the 'Trial' in there...?"

"Nah, that's not it. Even I wouldn't be so reckless as to go tossing people willy-nilly into the 'Trial.' I lost time in there because it took a while to wake you up."

"Me?"

"Yep. You were having nightmares, so I was frantically trying to wake you, but you were sleeping stubbornly tight, like you'd been tied to that dream with a double knot. I thought about just carrying you out as you were, but I had a really bad feeling about it."

He embellished the truth with lies, throwing in some overly dramatic gestures. No words of denial came from Emilia. Of course, she had no way to confirm it. If Subaru said that was how it was, there was no evidence to suspect otherwise.

"I see... Sorry, for doubting you like that."

"It's fine, really. Well, if I'd gotten to go through the 'Trial' too, maybe we could've synced our stories and worked together to clear it."

"...I wonder. There's no guarantee you and I would see the same things..."

Her words trailed off weakly, but Emilia showed no sign of pressing Subaru on his lie. For Emilia, the fact that she had witnessed her own past, and that she'd been unable to overcome it, was something she didn't want known. If Subaru insisted he didn't know the contents of the "Trial," she was in a state of mind where she wanted to believe it. Here too, Subaru rolled Emilia's intentions around in the palm of his hand.

"Anyway, what did you want to talk about? That wasn't the main topic, was it?"

"Right. That's not the main thing. I have something I want to ask."

"Something you want to ask?"

Emilia tilted her head. Watching the waterfall of silver hair spilling from her shoulders, Subaru continued, "Yeah, something I want to ask," and said,

"I haven't seen Puck's face around lately... still no response from him, huh?"

"Y-yeah... that's right. Puck still won't answer me. I keep calling out to him, but it's like he's asleep inside the crystal."

Hearing the name come up unexpectedly, Emilia answered with her eyes lowered. Her white fingers searched at her chest and grasped a beautiful crystal glowing green. The crystal that the great spirit Puck supposedly used as his anchor — its glow appeared to have faded.

"You don't know the cause, huh?"

"Sometimes this happens, where my voice can't reach him like this, but usually he comes back in two or three days. But this time it's already been about a week... I'm getting a little anxious too."

The existence of the little gray cat who boasted that he protected Emilia. As Subaru pictured that small shadow in his mind, what crossed it was the Great Disaster that occurs in the "Sanctuary," and Emilia's fate when caught up in it.

When the Witchbeast called the Great Rabbit assaults the "Sanctuary," probably every last resident becomes its prey — that was Subaru's imagining, from the loop when the Great Rabbit appeared. Among those victims would surely be counted Garfiel, Ram, Roswaal, and the rest. And of course, Emilia too.

Bitten apart, chewed, the sensation of one's own existence becoming meat settling into another creature's stomach — the thought that Emilia and the others had tasted that too made Subaru's guts churn with rage and grief. Setting that surge of emotion aside for the moment, what he thought of was the useless spirit who never showed his face even when Emilia was in a situation where she might come to harm.

It had been the same last time, too, when Emilia's body had been taken over by the Witch. Emilia, cloaked in an ominous atmosphere, overflowing with shadow enough to blot out the world. Even though her body was being manipulated at the Witch's whim, the great spirit who was all talk hadn't come to help even then, either. All talk and no action — words without deeds, mouth and nothing more, taken to its ultimate extreme.

"So far, that guy's only big moment has been the fight with Elsa and the whole 'Sapporo Snow Festival with me' thing. Can't count on him... maybe I should keep that in mind."

Hand on his chin, Subaru cursed under his breath as his thoughts spun. The calamity visiting the "Sanctuary" was, by extension, synonymous with the danger closing in on Emilia. If so, he'd hoped to add appropriate fighting strength to what Garfiel and Roswaal alone couldn't counter — but Puck didn't seem to fit that bill.

"You don't have any idea how to call Puck back, do you? If he could be around, it'd help move a lot of things along."

"――. I don't really... have any idea, maybe."

To his low-expectation question, Emilia gave the answer he'd expected. Still, something about the brief pause before her reply bothered him, but before he could press her on it,

"What did you want to ask Puck? Did something happen?"

"Hm? Oh, well, if that place is the Witch's tomb, then it's been there since four hundred years ago, right? So I figured if I could talk to someone who knew about that era, it might open up a breakthrough."

He delivered the answer he'd prepared, word for word, and Emilia nodded, convinced. Then she seriously considered Subaru's current proposal.

"That's true, isn't it. Puck might know something... okay, got it. I'll try calling out to him more than usual, too."

"Okay. For now, I'll leave that hope up to you, Emilia-tan."

Emilia was motivated toward the "Trial." And she didn't think Subaru had gone through the "Trial." There shouldn't be any drop in motivation. Puck, holed up in his crystal, still hadn't shown his face, and it seemed difficult to hope for his appearance in this loop. The fact that he wouldn't lend even a little help in adjusting the difficulty on this end, combined with the bad impression left by their last exchange, only made Subaru's opinion of him worse.

"Well, staying too long isn't good, and I don't want to give Ram more reasons to tease me by making her suspicious, so I'll clear out. Unless Emilia-tan wants me to sleep beside her..."

"Don't need you to sleep beside me, though?"

"When you say it that flat, all I can say is, oh boy..."

Subaru gave a wry smile at Emilia's puzzled expression and headed toward the door. For tonight, at least, that was probably all the words that needed to be exchanged with Emilia. As for her own problem, there was currently little Subaru could approach from his side. What Subaru could do was create an environment where Emilia could focus on the "Trial," and use his own hands to break through the bad circumstances surrounding it.

Emilia would challenge the tomb, and Subaru would challenge the "Sanctuary." And Subaru's hardships must not be sensed by Emilia. Because for her, that would mean an increase in her burden.

"Well then, good night, Emilia-tan. If you have a scary dream, feel free to come running to me anytime."

"If I came all the way to your place, wouldn't that startle the villagers? ...Hey, Subaru."

"Hm?"

Just as he was about to part with a light quip, Emilia called his name to stop him. Subaru turned back with his hand on the door, and Emilia's lips trembled; a moment of hesitation surfaced in her eyes before she said,

"No, never mind. Good night. Take care."

"That last part isn't really a joke, you know. Night."

Waving his hand, Subaru left the bedroom with Emilia's smile burned into his mind. Closing the door behind him and lifting his face, he found Ram, seated in a chair in the living room connected to the bedroom, tilting a cup of steaming tea. Even though Ryuzu, the homeowner, had thoughtfully vacated the house, the maid maintained her usual insolent attitude. Subaru gave a wry smile.

"I was going to say sorry for making you wait so long... but seeing how completely at ease you are, I don't feel like saying it anymore."

"I'd like some credit for the restraint of not even touching the tea sweets. — There wasn't anything rude done to Emilia-sama, was there?"

"What a dumb question, to someone who always puts Emilia first. You're the one who shouldn't be putting weird strain on a tired Emilia. ...Though I do trust you on that."

How Subaru behaved toward Subaru wasn't the same as how he behaved toward Emilia. Given that Ram sized up whoever she was dealing with, her methods might be called malicious, but precisely because of that clean-cut pragmatism, one could call it trustworthy. Ram scoffed at Subaru's murmured remark, emptied the contents of her cup, and rose from her seat. Watching her figure as she moved to pass by him, heading for the bedroom,

"Oh right, could I get a minute with you too?"

"How lewd."

"Is that how you see me in your head, some beast with no restraint?"

Ram hugged herself and put some distance between herself and Subaru. Of course, she probably wasn't serious, but being met with that kind of reaction from a young lady genuinely stung.

"I'll say this much — I don't think I've ever looked at you with lewd intent, practically speaking."

"That 'practically' is exactly the angle from which the beastliness comes spilling out — hardly trustworthy. Besides, ever since coming to the 'Sanctuary,' Ram's been sensing something odd in Barusu's gaze."

"What's that supposed to mean. That's an accusation I have no memory of earning — aren't you being a little full of yourself?"

"Then call it unconscious. You get this strange, distant look sometimes while looking at Ram. Through Ram, Ram doesn't know who it is you're looking at."

— That was, truly, a blow struck at a thought Subaru hadn't been aware of himself.

Tasting the illusion of being punched, Subaru realized his thoughts had frozen solid. His expression stiffened, his eyes wandered. Regretting that he'd reacted that way, he tried to recover quickly, shrugging his shoulders,

"W-what could you possibly be talking about, I have not the faintest notion, verily."

"The fact that you only notice once it's pointed out makes it quite seriously abnormal. It's just that the color of that gaze isn't unpleasant, so Ram hadn't said anything until now."

Ram let out a small breath at Subaru's agitation. The absence, there, of her usual attitude of mildly mocking Subaru only made it hurt all the more, right now. Identical in appearance, yet utterly different inside — that was supposed to be his understanding of Ram, and yet when treated gently by her like this, the sleeping girl and Ram overlapped in his mind. Ram's observation was accurate; her eye for these things was sharp. Through Ram, Subaru was seeing the figure of a girl who was her spitting image. He couldn't help it. And Ram had just made him painfully aware of that.

"...So, wasn't there something you wanted to ask Ram?"

"Uh?"

"It's not that Ram's goal is to deflate Barusu. Rather, deflating Barusu is just a side benefit, quite low on the priority list. Ram wants to help Emilia-sama now and get back to Roswaal-sama's side. Should Ram just ignore this and leave, then?"

"That'd be a problem. ...Er, well, y'know. I wanted to ask about Garfiel."

Leaning on Ram's continued, unusual show of consideration, Subaru somehow squeezed out the question he'd wanted to ask. Hearing the content, Ram raised her eyebrows in surprise, her expressionless face cracking,

"Did something happen with Garf?"

"Nothing so far, but that doesn't mean it'll stay that way. As things stand, he's the one I run into most in this 'Sanctuary,' and it looked like you two go way back, so I figured I'd ask."

"I see. ...Well, I'll let you have that explanation."

Saying something that suggested she'd seen right through his inner thoughts, Ram gently rested her hand on her own chin.

"So, what do you want to know about Garf?"

"I already know he's crazy strong, so that's not it, but, let's see... if Garfiel were to leave the 'Sanctuary,' what kind of condition do you think it would take?"

"...That's an oddly specific thing to worry about."

"Figured there was no point beating around the bush. Just consider it already priced in that you'll find it strange."

Unlike Emilia, whom he wanted to keep in the dark about the behind-the-scenes circumstances, Subaru didn't mind Ram noticing his covert maneuvering to some extent. In fact, given that he'd eventually need to trigger events heading outside the "Sanctuary" — like the liberation of Arlam Village's refugees — it would be more convenient for her to know he was already moving early.

"I don't know what you're scheming, and I have little interest either, but getting Garf to leave the 'Sanctuary'... well, if Ram were to beg him, the chances aren't zero."

"The weakness of a man in love, huh. I can't really argue with that one, since I've got some experience with it myself."

In fact, there was no doubt Ram's persuasion would move Garfiel's heart more easily than Subaru's. Still, he recalled Garfiel, who had even transformed into a giant tiger to chase him down and stop him from leaving. At that time, Garfiel had gone after Subaru with his claws bared even at Ram, the very woman he supposedly loved. To put it in the most extreme terms, for Garfiel, protecting something in the "Sanctuary" ranked higher in priority than Ram herself.

"But, Ram. That probably means..."

"That's right. It wouldn't work, would it."

To Subaru's opening, which he'd taken pains not to phrase as though he already knew the answer, Ram affirmed his suspicion with breezy candor. Before Subaru, blinking in surprise, Ram swept a hand through her pink hair,

"Garf is certainly infatuated with Ram, but within him, there's a fixed, unshakeable order of priorities. The same way Roswaal-sama is that, for Ram."

"So for Garfiel too, there's something that comes first, separate from that...? And you know what it is?"

"Ram knows. But Ram won't tell."

Turning her gaze away with a huff, Ram gave no answer to Subaru's pressing. He glared at her with narrowed eyes, demanding she continue, but Ram, keeping her composure, let out a breath,

"Presuming to gauge someone's heart all on your own — how terribly conceited of you. Garfiel's heart belongs to Garfiel. If you want to know, ask him directly."

"Look who's talking, being the poster child for that exact conceit. Do you know about the book Roswaal has?"

"...Wherever did you hear about that."

Tit for tat. Even as he was talked down by Ram's sound reasoning, a rebellious streak that couldn't let it slide allowed the counterattack. Ram's reaction to this was fierce; she pierced Subaru with narrowed eyes,

"Depending on the circumstances, you might find yourself in some pain."

"If you did that, wouldn't that go against what Roswaal has in mind, exactly? Don't, Ram. You shouldn't be able to do that."

At least within the "Sanctuary," Subaru had never once been harmed by Ram. If anything, there had been a time she'd said she was lending him a hand of her own will, not on Roswaal's orders. The provocation was made banking on that experience — a remark made in anticipation that there would be no physical response from Ram. Hearing it, Ram's expression turned somewhat sharp, but,

"You've started making unpleasant eyes, Barusu."

"Huh?"

"Ram doesn't know what you saw in the tomb, but it must surely be something not good. Enough that, with how you are now, Ram doesn't feel like continuing this conversation."

"...What I saw inside the tomb is, for me, a kind of hope. Don't slap a label like 'not good' on it."

Recalling the encounter that surfaced in his mind — with the white-haired Witch, Echidna — and feeling as though it had just been implicitly denied, Subaru pushed out his lips in a pout. The meeting with Echidna had brought about last time's tragedy, but even so, it had brought Subaru returns to match the setback he'd suffered. Just having someone he could confide in about "Return by Death," someone he could discuss it with — that alone was worth more than enough to offset most anything else.

"――――"

For a moment, Subaru's black eyes and Ram's pale pink ones held each other's gaze. Her eyes wavered faintly, and Subaru stretched his awareness, trying to see through to what she held deep within them, but before the image could take shape, she turned her gaze away and it dissolved into mist.

"Go on now. It would be a shame to keep Emilia-sama waiting any longer than this."

"...Sorry for holding you up. I know well enough that what you're saying isn't wrong."

Lastly, he apologized for the harsh words that had slipped out of his mouth. Ram paid it no mind, turned her back to Subaru, and headed toward the bedroom. Watching the hem of her serving dress vanish beyond the door, Subaru let out a deep sigh and left Ryuzu's house.

Stepping outside, the wind blowing through the "Sanctuary" brushed Subaru's bangs and tickled his forehead. Breathing in the night air, smelling the scent of grass, Subaru — having stayed quite late already — set off walking through the "Sanctuary," heading toward the cathedral that served as his bed.

Treading over the grassland, making his way by moonlight, Subaru's mind searched for how to use his time in this loop. Now that he had confirmation that there was effectively no limit to the number of "Return by Death" repetitions, Subaru had a means he hadn't had before — namely, throwaway loops purely for gathering information. If he could purely and simply resolve to throw his life away, there was no more efficient way to make use of the trait called "Return by Death" than that.

"If I try a different approach every time, countermeasures for each individual obstacle I have to clear should start coming to me one by one..."

After that, all that was left was to stitch those countermeasures together so everything could be carried out in a single run. Knock down every obstacle standing in the way, and achieve a perfect victory where everyone together seizes the future. Who to include in the picture of that perfect victory — that, too, was a seed of worry for Subaru.

"――――"

Suddenly, Subaru's feet stopped. Because his sense of smell, catching the scent on the wind, had picked up something other than the grass beneath his feet.

Subaru lifted his face. Ahead, on the road bathed in starlight, a young man stood planted like a guardian statue. Arms crossed, spiky blond hair fluttering in the wind, teeth gnashing — it was Garfiel.

"You're quite the timely bastard yourself, aren't you."

"The hell, ain't like you're all that surprised or nothin'. Well, easier to talk if you ain't gonna go flailin' around actin' weird about it."

At the appearance of Garfiel — a topic that had come up in his conversation with Ram just moments ago — Subaru scratched his cheek and gave a voice of admiration. Garfiel jerked his chin, some kind of signal directed at Subaru.

His small, sturdy back set off walking. Off the path, tramping heavily toward the forest. Watching that retreating back leap over the knee-high overgrown grass and enter the woods, Subaru gave a light stretch and turned toward the cathedral —

"That there just now meant 'follow me,' obviously, didn't it, oi!"

Garfiel came charging back and hurled abuse at Subaru, who had been about to walk off just like that. Subaru raised both hands, shrugging his shoulders,

"Just a bit of a stylish joke. Relax, it's not like I missed the point."

"That's exactly what pisses me off. Just get over here already, or I'll eat you whole."

"Isn't this normally the part where you're supposed to say something reassuring, like 'I won't actually eat you'?"

Garfiel stomped off roughly without responding to Subaru's remark. Feeling an unpleasant premonition at his refusal to deny the part he wanted denied, Subaru nonetheless followed the back that was, this time, properly leading the way, jogging along behind.

Off the path, into the forest, and after proceeding a while, Garfiel gave a snort. A small clearing opened up slightly among the standing trees, just about the right size for four or five people to sit in a circle. Garfiel turned back toward him there,

"Now then... what the hell did you see, in that tomb?"

"...You too, huh."

The third questioner, after Emilia and Ram. Unlike the previous two, there was a condition here — his answer to Garfiel needed to be handled with caution — and being asked one after another was starting to wear him down a little.

Oblivious to Subaru's inner thoughts, Garfiel spat, muttering "You too, huh?" with displeasure. Gnashing his teeth, he turned back, narrowing the pupils of his golden eyes,

"Dunno and don't care what you talked about with who, but I ain't just gonna let it slide past me. Like they say, 'Doubt even your own kin before you trust 'em.'"

"Sorry to disappoint, but I was just having trouble waking Emilia up inside the tomb. That I saw something in there is nothing but a guess."

"That's not just hollow, it's downright see-through. You, reekin' of Witch-stink from head to toe like that, and you think you can talk your way past me, huh?"

"――――"

Wrinkling his nose, Garfiel spat the words out with open hostility. Hearing his argument, Subaru found himself at a loss for words, yet also felt a nagging sense of wrongness.

Garfiel's reason for treating Subaru as an enemy — that was the Witch's Lingering Scent. It had always been so, and this latest remark bore that out as well. The problem was the timing of when he'd brought it up this time — that was what felt off.

The moment they'd left the tomb, Subaru had braced his whole body, thinking it wouldn't be strange at all for Garfiel to attack him instantly. Right after a "Return by Death," and for a cause of death that was what it was — in terms of the Witch's Lingering Scent, there could hardly be a time when it hung thicker on him than now.

And yet, contrary to Subaru's expectations, Garfiel hadn't suddenly launched an attack. If anything, he'd seemed, fairly genuinely, relieved that Subaru and Emilia had made it back safely. Because of that, Subaru had gone along carrying a lingering sense of puzzlement, attending the results discussion at Ryuzu's house, even seeing Garfiel off as he left.

The very man who'd been ordinary right up until they parted ways had done a total about-face in the mere few dozen minutes before their reunion. He couldn't fathom why. Could it be — was it that the stench had been so overpowering his nose had gone numb to it, and only now, as time passed, had it faded to a concentration he could actually detect, causing this reaction?

He lifted his own arm and sniffed it, but all he could sense was the accumulated fatigue of the day. Resolving to wipe himself down with water and a towel later, Subaru cut in toward Garfiel.

"That Witch's Lingering Scent thing — I get pointed at about it pretty often, actually, by all kinds of people."

"...Heh, that so. Then everybody before now's been mighty leisurely about lettin' it slide, huh, oi. With a stink strong enough to bend your nose out of shape, why the hell would they."

"Body odor aside, it's probably because they judged based on watching my actions. It'd help if you did the same. At the very least, right after we left the tomb, you did let it go, didn't you."

"――――"

"I have no intention of harming Emilia, of course, or you, or the 'Sanctuary.' I just want you to believe that much, at least. If you could, I'd appreciate it if you'd just keep watching me as things stand."

He knew it was a convenient thing to say, but judging from the fact that Garfiel wasn't throwing punches out of nowhere, he was still being rational. Subaru judged there was a path to settling this peacefully through dialogue. In fact, the eyes Garfiel had turned on Subaru wavered faintly with hesitation. To begin with, he wasn't so simple-minded as to think of eliminating Subaru on the Witch's Lingering Scent alone. It was when the foul reek surrounding Subaru combined with some other condition that he took action. This time, that trigger — whatever it was — hadn't been pulled yet. And Subaru needed to figure out exactly what that trigger was.

"...You ain't answered my first question."

"Hm?"

"What'd you see, in that tomb. Whether I let you off or not depends on that answer, is what I'm sayin'."

Stripping the momentum from his tone, Garfiel repeated the question while glaring at Subaru. There were two possible answers — answer honestly, or lie, same as with Emilia and the others. It was worth confirming which choice was correct here too, but —

"In that case, let me ask you one question too."

"Don't be gettin' your position mixed up. I'm on top, you're on the bottom. Wanna get torn to shreds?"

"Don't get so mad. Just relax your shoulders and answer easy, that's all I ask."

Facing Garfiel, who bared his fangs at him, Subaru gave a light shrug of his own shoulders and drew a breath. Holding the question at the back of his throat, he fixed his eyes on the glaring Garfiel, and —

— Now, this is one of the make-or-break moments.

Bracing himself with that thought, he said,

"— I saw a girl who's the spitting image of Ryuzu-san. Does that ring any bells for you?"
