Arc 6 Chapter 44: "Badge of Blood"

In the stone-walled room, Subaru stared blankly at his own two hands, the girl sprawled limp before him, her limbs thrown out in every direction.

"――――"

He couldn't keep up with what had happened. Not at all.

There was a wound on his wrist, his throat felt raw, and his heart was pounding absurdly fast. But none of it connected to any memory — it all felt like some bug born in a blank patch of his brain, nothing more.

Apart from that physical evidence, his spirit, his soul, had been left behind by the situation.

If there was one thing he could say for certain, it was this—

—the flame of life in the girl lying before him had already gone out.

"—gh, ah, it's— it's too soon to give up."

Breath ragged, Subaru shook his head and scrambled to the side of the girl lying on her back. Each step forward felt leaden, his shoe soles as if glued to the floor, his movements sluggish. Somehow he tore his foot free and dropped to his knees beside the girl — beside Meili.

"――――"

Meili's mouth hung open in a small, vacant gape, her thin white limbs thrown out, not so much as twitching. Her expression looked almost stunned, and her lightless eyes stared vaguely into some distant, unfocused point. She'd always given off an elusive, hard-to-pin-down impression — but now that impression was only stronger.

"M-Meili... hey, Meili!"

He called out to her. No response.

If his voice wouldn't do it, Subaru shook her shoulder, lightly slapped her cheek with his palm. Still no response. Her open eyelids didn't so much as blink.

"Me—"

Cutting the call short, Subaru held his breath and tried every resuscitation measure he could remember. He set both hands on her thin chest, pressing down with his weight — chest compressions. He recalled hearing the heart was two finger-widths from the solar plexus, or something like that, and pressed his palms in accordingly.

"Haah... haah...! Meili! Hey, Meili!"

"――――"

"Damn it!"

No answer. Her pale, bloodless face, her body swaying limply with each push. Cursing at the sight, Subaru tilted the girl's head back, secured her airway, and tried rescue breathing. CPR was chest compressions and rescue breaths, over and over.

"What else. What else is there. Something, there's gotta be something, damn it, damn it, damn it!"

He clawed desperately through his memory, struggling for anything else he could do. But in the end it was nothing more than half-remembered scraps from TV, amateur knowledge picked up in passing. The harder he tried, the more it felt like reaching into fog — a futility that tormented him.

In the end, all he could do was doggedly repeat it: compressions, breaths, compressions, breaths, again and again and again.

Under the palm pressed to her chest, no heartbeat. Even pressing his lips to hers and forcing air straight into her lungs, he got no sense that it reached her, that it stirred any will in her body.

And so he kept at it, kept performing CPR until his body simply had nothing left—

"—Damn... it."

Sweat beading on his forehead, breath ragged and torn, Subaru collapsed backward onto the floor.

"Haa, haa... damn it, damn it...!"

He gripped his own bangs in his fist and, in bitter frustration, could do nothing but curse.

Meili did not draw breath again. Just as his first impression had told him.

The girl's flame of life had gone out, and it would not be lit again.

—Meili was dead.

Fate cruelly and mercilessly stole away the life of an innocent child — no, that kind of comforting phrasing didn't belong here. Whatever had killed her, it wasn't fate. It was something far more definite. Malice had killed her.

As proof, an ugly bluish-black bruise marred Meili's slender throat.

"――――"

Steadying his breath in silence, Subaru — carrying his own regret — looked down at his own palms.

Both arms, which only moments ago had been struggling desperately to save Meili's life — on both wrists, left and right, jagged claw marks were carved into the skin. Wounds that looked as if they'd been clawed at with all one's strength, as if for dear life.

He wasn't dull enough not to understand what that meant.

"――――"

Sitting up, Subaru arranged Meili's body. He straightened her disheveled clothes, folded her hands over her chest, and closed her vacantly open eyelids. Having paid the dead that much respect, if only in form, he gently reached out and touched her slender throat.

Bracing against a wave of revulsion, he traced his fingers over the bluish-black bruise on Meili's neck.

"...It's a perfect match."

A dry, listless mutter, offering no surprise — only fact given voice. The bluish-black bruise on Meili's neck — the marks that looked like fingerprints — and the shape of Subaru's own fingers laid over them matched perfectly, even to an untrained eye.

There was no longer any room to doubt the fact.

Meili had been strangled to death.

And the culprit was none other than—

"—gh, gehh."

The instant that undeniable fact struck him, nausea surged up Subaru's throat. He turned his face away on reflex, rolling to the side so as not to defile Meili's remains.

What spilled out was the breakfast he'd eaten only a short while before. He emptied it spectacularly onto the floor, retching until his stomach had nothing left, and then—

"I really do... nothing but puke, huh..."

He wiped his mouth roughly with his sleeve, muttering the words with self-mocking bitterness.

Honestly, he couldn't remember how many times this made. Maybe it was the first time since this new day had begun, but the previous day had been a disaster in that regard too. It felt like he hadn't properly digested a single thing he'd put in his stomach. Subaru felt like he owed every farmer in the country a heartfelt apology.

If he didn't occupy his mind with such pointless thoughts, his heart would crack and shatter to pieces.

"――――"

The circumstantial evidence was too complete.

The two of them alone in the room, and the fingerprints on Meili's neck matching his own. Subaru wasn't optimistic enough to think he could talk his way out of this.

Nor did he think he'd been framed.

Creating this situation required no other hand. This place existed beyond the realm of "a situation someone else could have engineered with help." If there was a hand needed to conjure this nightmare, it numbered only two.

—Natsuki Subaru's two arms alone were more than sufficient.

"...It doesn't make sense."

Confronting that fact head-on, Subaru voiced his own incomprehension. Baffling, incomprehensible, a breakdown of the situation itself — whatever words he used, this was a scenario impossible to reproduce. And yet he could draw no conclusion other than that Natsuki Subaru had committed this act.

But he himself — the Subaru standing here — retained not a single fragment of that memory.

"What happened, what happened? Remember. Remember, remember, remember...!"

Standing, pacing round and round the room, Subaru forced his memory open.

He'd died, woken up, met Emilia, Beatrice, Patrasche. He'd hidden the fact that he had no memories and eaten breakfast, pressed Julius about his unnatural reaction, embraced Shaula who'd been secretly keeping watch over him, and then run into Meili in the corridor.

And Meili had said she had business with him, brought something up to discuss—

"Something about... last night... what did she say..."

Frowning at the hazy, blurred memory, Subaru turned over Meili's words. But right after she'd stated her business, his consciousness had cut out, and whatever she'd said afterward was as scattered as thoughts on the verge of sleep.

"Night, she asked about the night. That much's certain. Before I woke up, or before I fell asleep? Either way, she asked me about one of those... and then?"

Beyond that, he didn't know.

All he knew was that whatever Meili had asked him about, it had to be connected to the "nighttime actions" Subaru himself wanted to know about.

According to Emilia and the others, this morning Subaru had been found collapsed in the archive. What he'd done in the dead of night, before collapsing, was entirely unknown. At the very least, whatever had happened during that time had to be connected to the loss of his memory.

And surely Meili had realized some connection to that lost memory of Subaru's — which was exactly why—

"She died. And I... I strangled her...? Why would I do something like that..."

Looking down at his hands, he felt a visceral sensation return to his palms despite having no memory of it.

Arms that had strangled a young girl by force until her breath stopped. The marks on his wrists were surely traces of Meili's desperate resistance as she was choked.

Looking closer, blood clung to the fingernails of the girl's folded hands — solid physical evidence of a struggle. The claw marks carved deep into his arms felt to Subaru like the voice of the girl's lingering resentment—

"—Ah?"

Just as his imagination ran wild, a rasping breath suddenly caught in Subaru's throat.

A stunned exhale — its cause lay in his own arm. Not the wrist wounds he'd been glaring at hard enough to bore a hole through them. His gaze had shifted to the nails of his own right hand.

Packed under those nails, just like under Meili's, was blood from clawing someone, and gouged flecks of skin.

"――――"

Suspicious of the inexplicable bloodstains, Subaru looked back at Meili's remains. Her death-pose, arranged out of respect for the dead. It could hardly be much comfort, but aside from the bluish-black bruise on her neck, the girl's remains retained the delicate charm she'd had in life.

At least, there were no claw marks anywhere he could see. No trace remained on the girl's body of the ugly, painful gouges his nails should have left.

Then what was this blood and skin under his nails — this trace of malice — from?

"—No way."

Looking down at his own throbbing, aching arm, Subaru's lips trembled with a bad premonition.

The grotesque black mutation running from his right elbow down was still fresh in his memory, but the pain, the throbbing wound-like ache, was coming from his left arm.

Following the insistent ache spreading from the inside of his elbow, hidden beneath his sleeve, Subaru slowly began rolling it up. The faint sensation of fabric sticking was that of dried blood peeling away.

Holding his breath, Subaru shoved the sleeve up to his shoulder in one motion.

And seeing his own left arm, bleeding a fair amount, he let out the breath he'd swallowed.

"――――"

He scraped away the reddish-black blood staining his arm with a finger, narrowing his eyes at the throbbing wound.

There, just as he'd feared, were scratches that looked to have been carved by his own right hand. The painful marks running from the inside of his elbow up to his upper arm didn't read as mere wounds, though.

They were letters.

Crude, distorted characters carved into flesh with fingernails.

And they read:

—"Natsuki Subaru Was Here."

"—Ha."

The moment he made it out, an unbearable breath escaped him — just one. Thinking it might be some trick of the eye, Subaru wiped at the wound on his left arm with a finger, scrubbing at it again and again, until the blood that had once stopped began to flow again, just to be sure.

But there was no other way to read it. However he looked at it, it clearly said "Natsuki Subaru Was Here." Written so Subaru himself could read it, in ugly handwriting, in Japanese: Natsuki Subaru Was Here.

Straightforward. An extremely straightforward declaration of self. Apparently some criminals leave evidence at the scene to prove the crime was theirs — this was the same thing. Leaving his name behind, making unmistakably clear whose doing this was. What consideration. What a thirst for recognition. What a display of self-assertion—

"You... what the hell are you!?"

Confronted with an unacceptable truth, Subaru screamed in anguish, his voice cracking. He flailed his inseparable left arm, turned his face away as if to push the filth away, and his feet tangled beneath him.

He fell. Landing hard on his backside, Subaru gritted his teeth and slammed his left arm against the floor. Again and again he struck it, and from the corner of his clenched mouth, blood trickled from pain that rang all the way to the bone.

But even that changed nothing about reality. The wound on his arm didn't vanish either.

"It wasn't there! This wound was definitely not there before!"

Shaking his head in denial, Subaru insisted — in a room with no one left but a Meili who would never move again — that the wound on his left arm must have appeared after his consciousness cut out.

The throbbing wound on his left arm was fresh, certainly not something he'd simply failed to notice until now. Whoever had strangled Meili must have inflicted the same wound on him too — no. Wrong.

No, no. That's absolutely wrong. Mistaken. In error.

Just admit it already. Understand it. He already knew. It wasn't "someone." This wasn't the work of any other being. This was Natsuki Subaru's own crime.

A "Natsuki Subaru" who was not Subaru had killed Meili, and, as if flaunting the achievement, carved a badge of honor into his arm.

"It's... insane..."

His head wasn't right. It was deranged. He couldn't think of it as anything but madness.

—Was "Natsuki Subaru" the name of some incomprehensible monster?

"――――"

This wasn't the first time Subaru had felt distrust toward "Natsuki Subaru."

Starting with last night's unnatural behavior — still unanswered — "Natsuki Subaru" had clearly been repeating actions that made no sense. On top of that, there was the illicit affair with Echidna he'd been told of when he pressed Julius, and whatever scheming had psychologically cornered Julius of all people — "Natsuki Subaru" had become, by now, a being similar to but fundamentally different from the Subaru who possessed nothing but the life experience of a convenience-store-going shut-in.

Honestly, he'd find it far more convincing if someone told him some entirely separate entity had been possessing his body all along.

"But that's not it..."

Infuriatingly, focusing on the wound on his left arm made that suspicion impossible to maintain. His own name carved into his arm — naturally, the handwriting was nothing like what a brush or pen would produce, but even so, habits show through in handwriting. Specifically, the stops and hooks of the strokes carried quirks Subaru recognized as his own. No one else could match that closely without consciously, deliberately imitating it.

Which meant this was, without doubt, the handwriting of a "Natsuki Subaru" who shared the same roots as Subaru himself.

And the moment that became clear, another fact came into focus.

Namely—

"—While my consciousness was cut off, did 'Natsuki Subaru' come back?"

And that "Natsuki Subaru," having fallen out with Meili in some fashion, had killed her, left the fact carved into Subaru's arm, and then hidden away again in the gap between consciousness?

That part, he genuinely could not understand.

"He's got his own body, so why does he need... no, wait, what am I? What are you trying to do? Who even are you..."

Gripping his own face hard enough to claw at it, Subaru murmured in a trembling voice.

In a friendless other world, in a situation where he couldn't even tell friend from foe, Subaru had finally been forced to exclude even himself from the category of harmless beings.

"――――"

The ground felt unsteady beneath him, anxiety threatening to make it impossible to even stand properly, dominating his chest.

But Subaru resisted that feeling, and let out one long, deep breath.

He couldn't stay calm. His heart remained in complete disarray.

But he refused — refused to be dragged around like this, at the mercy of "Natsuki Subaru," understanding nothing.

So—

"—Who are you?"

Muttering his hatred toward an opponent he couldn't glare at without a mirror, Subaru dug the nails of his left hand into his own right arm — the skin marked with that grotesque pattern.

With a soft tearing sound, from the splitting black skin, drops of red blood surely welled up.

—Even from this black skin, the blood that flows is red, he thought, with bitter irony.

I chose an idiomatic English equivalent conveying the same boastful "I was the one who did this" self-assertion; a more literal alternative would be "Natsuki Subaru Was Here!" or "Presenting: Natsuki Subaru."
  • "血の勲章" in the chapter title rendered as "Badge of Blood" (勲章 = medal/decoration/order of merit, tying to the "刻んだ勲章" (carved medal/badge) image near the end referring to the self-inflicted wound-as-trophy). Flagging in case wiki convention prefers "Medal of Blood."
