Arc 3 Chapter 4: "A Rough Start to the Journey"

The morning after the heartwarming, worry-tinged exchange with Ram, who was staying behind at the mansion — among the assembled faces gathered before the front gate of Roswaal Manor, Subaru found his throat trembling with sheer emotion.

"Whoaaa, so this is it!"

Before Subaru's eyes, voice and expression brimming with delight, stood a presence of majestic, unhurried bearing. Hard green skin, a body so huge you had to look up at it, sharp yellow eyes — despite having glimpsed it several times before, he'd never had the chance to get this close, and now here it was: the form of a mighty lizard. Sitting in stately repose at the front gate of Roswaal Manor was the guide for the road ahead, the one who would lead Subaru and the others to the royal capital — an Earth Dragon, harnessed to its Dragon Carriage.

"Its body's huge! Its skin's tough! Its face is scary as hell!!"

Big creatures — or really, anything of overwhelming size — have a way of sending a guy's excitement through the roof; that's just a universal male sensation. Subaru, of course, was no exception, utterly smitten for the simple reason that it was big, and combined with the fact that it was early morning, his excitement was at full throttle. He bounced around the lizard, hopping this way and that, openly thrilled at the sight of it.

"Honestly, getting all excited like a little kid."

Watching him, Emilia loosened her lips into a sigh of exasperation. She shrugged, as if seeking agreement from Rem standing beside her. But—

"Subaru-kun... is adorable. Don't you think so too, Emilia-sama?"

"Adorable, sure, I guess... but Rem, you've really been poisoned by Subaru, haven't you."

Emilia sighed again, watching Rem watch Subaru with dreamy eyes. Unaware that such remarks were being exchanged behind his back, Subaru reached out toward the Earth Dragon without a shred of restraint, patting at it and letting out strange noises.

"This is insane, I'm so moved right now! Total fantasy vibes! If I had a digital camera I'd totally snap a pic! Caption could be 'Me and the Dragon That Crawls the Earth—' how about tha—"

Just as his excitement gauge was starting to peg out, and his touches began shifting from pats to full-on grabs, the lizard's own gauge pegged out first. To put it plainly—

"Dosutoefusuki!?"

"「Ah——」"

The lizard's tail whipped around with a roar of displacement and slammed full-force into Subaru's shoulder. The impact knocked him clean off his axis, and he went flying at a speed that practically left an afterimage. Tumbling through the air in something like a side aerial, Subaru plunged headfirst into the soft bushes beside the gate, and after a moment of silence, somehow managed to extract himself.

"—Wh-what was that about?"

"Subaru-kun. Earth Dragons are very intelligent creatures — even without words, they can generally understand your intentions. So you have to be careful and polite when handling them."

"You could've told me that sooner!? That's the closest I've felt to death since the mayo bath incident... which, wow, wasn't that long ago!"

Ever since coming to this other world, moments of brushing up against death had never really stopped coming. And just when he'd thought he was finally settling into some peaceful days, this happens.

Rotating the shoulder that had taken the hit, Subaru trudged his way out of the bushes and back to the front gate. Sneaking a wary glance at the lizard, he saw it narrow its yellow eyes ever so slightly and let out a hard breath, as if to say, "That's what you get for touching me without asking." He was used to being rejected by humans, but having even a creature from this unfamiliar world express its disapproval so plainly gave him a fair bit to think about.

"From now on, I'll be a little more careful about how I speak, how I carry myself, what I do, and my general conduct..."

"That's a lot to reflect on. Though I do think it's very you."

"One of the things Emilia-tan likes about me?"

"I might think it's better if you picked your moments, your audience, and your circumstances a bit more carefully."

"Noted, I'll take that to heart."

He raised a hand and obediently complied, and Emilia laughed, hands on her hips, saying "Good." Just then, the mansion's front door suddenly opened. Out came Roswaal and Ram, arriving a little late for the appointed gathering time. Subaru tapped lightly with his finger at the wrist where he wasn't wearing a watch.

"Hey now, hey now, hey now, you're late, what's going on? Wasn't it Rozwaal himself who set the time? People who are loose with time tend to be loose with everything else, right, Rem?"

"That's right! Today, it was Rem who had to wake Subaru-kun up when he didn't get up even after it was time, but you're welcome to praise her for it."

"There there there, hush now, Rem."

Subaru petted and hushed Rem as she let slip that unnecessary remark. Emilia's cold stare pierced right through him for it, and Subaru hastily launched into an excuse.

"It's not what it looks like! There's a reason! A reason bluer than the sea, greener than the mountains!"

"That's quite the leafy excuse... let's hear it."

"I was too excited to fall asleep!"

"You're not a child."

Seeing Emilia's shoulders drop in resignation, Subaru concluded that he'd won this round by default. To avoid any further pursuit of the topic, he quickly redirected the conversation toward Roswaal and the others.

"Anyway! What's the deal, Rozwaal-chi, why were you two late? It's not like you weren't both there at breakfast, right? And packing's basically Rem's job anyway."

"Oh nooo, sorry, sorry. See, Ram's staying behind to look after the house, so we won't be seeing her for a whi-i-ile. So-o-o, we were just having a li-i-ittle bit of an extra-thorough farewell, is all."

Wagging a raised finger as he offered this explanation, Roswaal straightened his own collar. Beside him, Ram hurriedly smoothed her hair and clothes — just watching the gesture conveyed more than enough of the raw truth.

"Nope, doesn't count! Never mind, didn't hear that, pretending it didn't happen, roll call with a straight face. One, me. Two, Emilia-tan. Three, Rem. And, I guess, Rozwaal-chi too."

"'I guess' is a bit much, don't you think — the Dragon Carriage happens to be my property, you know."

Chuckling softly, Roswaal reached out and gently touched the temperamental lizard's jaw. Subaru couldn't help but flinch, picturing Roswaal getting smacked and sent flying — for one moment his heart even leapt at how hilarious that would be — but that faint hope dissolved just as quickly. Far from shrugging off Roswaal's touch, the lizard nuzzled its snout against him, showing affection much like a horse would toward its owner. Quite the contrast to how it had treated Subaru just moments ago.

"Somehow... I'm not satisfied with this."

"It's Roswaal-sama's tame dragon, of course it's attached to Roswaal-sama. Not like Barusu, who goes around touching things carelessly and gets rejected."

"'Tame dragon' is a wild way to put it... and anyway, where'd you even see my humiliation?"

"Oh, was that not true?"

Subaru wrinkled his nose at Ram, who casually admitted she'd just been bluffing. She pointed at his head, then gave a spiteful little "Hah" through her nose.

"There are leaves stuck all over your head and body. And on top of that, there are dirt smudges too... I'd rather you not get the inside of the carriage dirty."

"You're not even the one who has to clean it..."

"Roswaal-sama will be riding along too. Making sure he isn't inconvenienced is part of a servant's duty. But then, coming from Barusu... well, I suppose it's only to be expected."

"Don't use my name as a synonym for 'no good,' I'll actually cry."

And with that, he flopped down onto the ground like a spoiled child throwing a tantrum, flailing his arms and legs in a display of pure shame-play. Though, if anything, this only risked giving Ram even more ammunition for contempt and mockery.

While they bickered on, Rem had briskly finished loading the luggage onto the carriage. With the round of idle chatter winding down, the mood shifted toward departure. As everyone said their goodbyes to Ram, who would be staying behind, Subaru suddenly remembered something and murmured under his breath.

"Come to think of it, Bea's never even come out to see us off, huh."

What a heartless little loli character, Subaru thought of the cream-haired girl. Of course, he'd anticipated this might happen, and had spent the entire previous day thoroughly teasing Beatrice and savoring their farewell in advance — but now that the actual moment of departure had come and she was nowhere to be seen, it still felt a bit lonely. Man, she really lacks flair for occasions like this, Subaru thought, privately annoyed, pouting his lips as he glared at the mansion.

"—Oh."

In the shadow of the door, cracked open just the tiniest sliver, he caught the eye of a figure in a dress secretly peering out at him. The other party flinched for just a moment at being noticed, but quickly composed herself as if to brazen it out — and yet continued her sneaking mission, still hidden behind the door. Subaru gave a wry smile at the stubborn girl and raised his hand.

'If I make it back safe, there's something I want to tell you.'

With that sentiment tucked into the gesture, he mimed it toward her. In response, the girl, wearing an unimpressed expression, gave a perfunctory wave, as if shooing him away. Having done just that much, she shut the door firmly, as though declaring her obligation to see them off fulfilled.

"—Subaru? What's wrong?"

Turning around, he found Emilia leaning out from the carriage's cabin, looking down at him. It seemed everyone had begun boarding without him noticing, and now Subaru alone still had his feet on the ground among the departing group. Flustered, he reached his hand toward the cabin door—

"Here."

Before his hand could reach the handle, pale fingertips stretched toward him first. For just an instant he hesitated, but then Subaru took the hand. A strength unbefitting the slenderness of that wrist pulled him, drawing his body into the cabin.

Having confirmed Subaru had boarded, Rem gave a nod to Ram, who was being left behind alone. No words were needed between the twins for that exchange of understanding; the gesture alone was enough to convey their thoughts. Rem pulled the reins, and the Earth Dragon, obeying, took its first step forward.

Feeling the carriage body creak and begin to move, Subaru turned toward the window. Sticking just his head out, he faced Ram, who was looking up at them from below.

"Well then, we're off! Take care of yourself!"

"If something happens, Barusu, you can at least serve as a shield against arrows. As for talent as bait — well, yes, I'll grant you that much."

"I've got more going for me than that, probably!?"

That undignified exchange of words served as this morning's farewell greeting. The Dragon Carriage picked up speed, rapidly accelerating. In an instant the distance from the mansion widened, and even Ram's figure standing by the front gate grew smaller and smaller.

Before she disappeared from view entirely, Ram pinched the edge of her skirt and gave a small curtsy. Bowing deeply, she saw Subaru and the others off just like a model maid.

"...If only her actual work skills matched that, it'd be perfect."

As the road entered a slight incline, Ram's figure vanished from sight completely. Confirming this, Subaru finally sank back into his seat in the cabin and let out a breath. Then, shifting in his seat, he found himself testing the ride of a Dragon Carriage for the first time, one way or another. He figured the feel of the seat itself would depend on the quality of the cabin's construction, but the ride itself was surprisingly, unexpectedly comfortable.

Judging by feel, the road wasn't exactly well-maintained. The wheel technology for Dragon Carriages presumably hadn't developed nearly as far as it had back in his original world, and yet the vibration reaching Subaru's body through his seat was far weaker than he'd expected. Honestly, it held up fine even compared to riding in a car back in his old world.

"Seems the Dragon Carriage is quite the novelty to you-u-u."

"Not so much novelty as, I dunno, a fresh kind of surprise — first times are always like this, right? I'll probably make some noise here and there, so bear with me."

Answering Roswaal's question while creaking the seat beneath him, Subaru felt the vibration traveling up from the floor of the carriage through the soles of his feet. If they'd been moving at a leisurely pace, it might have made sense, but the scenery rushing past the window was moving anything but slowly. In fact, the speed seemed comparable to a car, and by his rough estimate, they might well be pushing close to a hundred kilometers per hour. That being the case, his thoughts turned to Rem, seated alone outside on the driver's box.

"Hey, with this much speed going on, is it really okay for the driver's box to just be exposed like that? Not that Rem would get thrown off or anything, I trust her on that... but by the time we get to the capital, won't her hair and clothes be all messed up?"

"The Dragon Carriage is protected by a Divine Protection, so there's no need to worry about that."

Emilia answered Subaru's rather belated question with a puzzled look on her face. At her answer, Subaru tilted his head and said "Divine Protection?"

"That's another word I keep hearing here and there. It doesn't come up as much as, like, Mana, so I've never had the chance to ask — what exactly is that, anyway?"

"A Divine Protection is a Divine Protection. A blessing given by the world at the moment you're born... wait, you seriously don't know?"

Pointing both index fingers at Emilia and rocking his body side to side, Subaru gave an embarrassed laugh and admitted "Well, nope," confessing his ignorance. Emilia tapped her temple lightly with a finger.

"A Divine Protection is, like I just said, a Gospel bestowed by the world. There are all sorts of them, so it's hard to generalize, but there are some Divine Protections that a given species is always granted. The Earth Dragon's Divine Protection of Wind Avoidance is one of those."

"'Wind Avoidance,' huh."

"When an Earth Dragon runs across the land, it's completely unaffected by wind resistance. And the effect of that Divine Protection also extends to the Dragon Carriage it's harnessed to. That's why this cabin isn't affected by the wind either."

"And that means Rem outside is the same, then."

Chewing over Emilia's explanation in his head, Subaru arrived at a satisfied nod. Emilia smiled and said "Well done," and Subaru gave her a grateful wink in return, which only made her look faintly disgusted. Meanwhile, Subaru turned his thoughts back over the Divine Protections he'd heard mentioned so far.

There was Felt, absurdly fast on her feet, with her Divine Protection of Wind. And Reinhard, who'd shown off his Divine Protection of Arrow Avoidance during the standoff at the Loot House. So, huh, there really were all sorts. Which meant—

"What about me? What about me? Hey, Emilia-tan. Don't I have any kind of Divine Protection?"

A special Divine Protection granted the moment you're born — wasn't that exactly the kind of cheat ability Subaru had been longing for ever since he was summoned to this other world? Sure, its application seemed somewhat limited in the cases of the Dragon Carriage and Reinhard both. Maybe whatever Divine Protection had been granted to Subaru just hadn't blossomed yet, on account of its use being similarly restricted. Subaru's gaze, brimming with rising hope and expectation, met with—

"This is hard to say, but... a lot more people are born without any Divine Protection at all. And besides, people who do have one are usually aware of it themselves, without anyone needing to tell them..."

Her voice trailed off, and Emilia looked at Subaru apologetically. That alone conveyed everything she was leaving unsaid, and Subaru felt the tower of hope he'd only just rebuilt come crashing down all over again. Just a fleeting hope, huh, he thought, twisting his mouth into a self-mocking, cynical smile.

"Nah, it's fine. That would've been too much of a luxury for me anyway. Because I've already gotten something way more precious in this place. Yeah — the miracle of meeting you, Emilia-tan, is a Gospel that beats any Divine Protection out there."

"Uh-huh, sure. We'll get to the capital in about four hours, so behave yourself till then, okay?"

"Emilia-tan's so cold! But it's frustrating how happy even that reaction makes me...!"

Through his daily interactions with Emilia, Subaru was steadily acquiring a rather advanced form of perversion. While Emilia and Roswaal began discussing their plans for after arriving at the capital, Subaru, told to behave, glanced restlessly around the inside of the carriage.

The Dragon Carriage's interior, despite its ornate exterior, was surprisingly cramped — a snug, four-seat affair like something you'd see rich people riding in the movies. The seat material was quite high quality, prepared for long-distance travel, so there was no worry of a sore backside even after hours of sitting. But the trade-off was a cramped cabin, and Subaru, notorious for his lack of patience, was already starting to grow restless.

"Emilia-tan, Emilia-tan, switch me seats by the window—"

"What's wrong? Oh, did you get carsick even though it barely shakes at all? That happens a lot when you're not used to riding. Okay, got it, I'll lend you Puck."

"I appreciate the thought, but that's not quite it. And also, I don't get why handing me Puck would help if I were carsick — am I supposed to use him as an emergency barf bag?"

"Even Puck would get mad if you did something like that, I think..."

It was still early morning — not yet within the waking hours of the cat-shaped Spirit — and just imagining that mild-mannered creature actually losing his temper over such an act sent a shiver down his spine. Then Emilia said "But" with a breath, looking back at Subaru.

"If that's not it, then what?"

"Well, see, Emilia-tan and Rozwaal-chi are busy talking about future stuff, right? And I'm all lonely with nothing to do, and going back to sleep feels lame, so I figured I'd just soak in the scenery and zone out."

"Oh, sorry, I didn't notice. Here you go, then."

At Subaru's request, Emilia shifted, and the two swapped seats in the cramped cabin. Since Subaru had naturally been sitting right beside Emilia, various parts of their bodies brushed against each other as she moved past above him, and he let his cheeks loosen at his good fortune. Once the seat swap was safely completed—

"Now then, behave yourself properly, understand?"

"Yep. For a while I'll just quietly savor the warmth of this indirect sit-down with you, Emilia-tan."

"I have no idea what you just said, but it sounds really gross, so tone it down, please."

Saying that, Emilia glanced back a few times at Subaru — who, for some reason, was squirming his backside — with a look of concern, then returned to her discussion with Roswaal. Answering her glance with a smile and a wave, Subaru turned to face the little window visible from his seat. Set above the boarding door, the small window offered a cropped, streaming view of the scenery rushing by outside at high speed. Viewed at a downward angle, blue sky dominated most of the frame, and with no clusters of tall trees in sight, he figured they'd already passed through the forested area near Roswaal Manor.

"Still, I've got no idea how far it even is between the mansion and the capital..."

Emilia had mentioned about four hours earlier, but until he knew just how fast the Dragon Carriage was actually going, that was only a reference figure at best. Lifting himself from his seat to press his face against the little window, the scenery streaming past sideways seemed to move far faster than he'd expected. Despite tearing along at a good clip over a rough road, the fact that no vibration reached the cabin remained something of a mystery.

"I'll just chalk that part up to 'Divine Protections are amazing,' vague as that is... but man, this lizard's legs are fast. Looking at it, we're doing close to a hundred kilometers an hour, aren't we?"

The speed of the passing scenery seemed comparable to something he'd once seen from inside a car on a highway. If this Dragon Carriage could sustain this kind of speed continuously, it was easy to see why they'd become so widespread. Sure, there was probably some cost involved in caring for and feeding the lizard itself, but the same went for a car made of steel. Which one came out on top in terms of convenience was probably just a matter of preference.

"And personally, I'm not into machines, but I am the type of sad boy who gets excited over big creatures."

So, by preference, he'd declare the Dragon Carriage the winner. Having settled the matter of cars versus Dragon Carriages in his own mind, Subaru let his gaze wander with a "well now." Just sitting here, boredom upon boredom was piling up fast. Subaru rapped lightly on the door with the back of his hand, drawing both Emilia's and Roswaal's attention toward him.

"I kinda want to go see Rem — think it'd be okay to open this up or whatever?"

"No-o-ow that's an interesting thing to say, Subaru-kun. Since we're protected by the Divine Protection, as long as you're in contact with the carriage, you won't feel the wind. Working your way around the carriage body to Rem's spot isn't particularly difficult... but if you fall, you'll die, you know?"

"Hey now, Rozwaal-chi, don't underestimate my athletic ability, okay? If you gathered up every after-school-club-less high schooler in the country and ran the numbers, I'm pretty confident I'd rank near the top — anything but long-distance running!"

"At the ve-e-ery least, let's give Rem a heads up first, shall we?"

Giving a thumbs up and flashing his teeth, Subaru watched as Roswaal, resigned, knocked on the small window connecting to the driver's box — a window just big enough to pass items through by hand. The little window opened from the other side, and Rem's voice alone reached them: "Yes, is something the matter?"

"Ah, Subaru-kun says he's bored and wants to be by Rem's side. Wow, someone's popular."

"Subaru-kun does? What should we do, should we stop for a moment? Though if we come to a stop, it'll take a bit for the Earth Dragon to get back up to speed again."

At Rem's words, Subaru tilted his head with a "hm?" and Roswaal touched his chin.

"Divine Protections aren't omnipotent either, you know. In the Earth Dragon's case, once it's exerted the effect of its 'Divine Protection of Wind' like this, it takes a li-i-ittle bit of time before it can activate the Divine Protection again. We could just make it an early lunch break, if you'd prefer?"

At Roswaal's explanation and suggestion, Subaru nodded in understanding before shaking his head to decline.

"Nah, it's fine, it's fine. I'll just loop around and come over there, so Rem, just sit tight and wait. Oh, and please, whatever you do, don't have the Earth Dragon drift or anything — the laws of inertia will send me flying."

Drifting, triple axels, anything of that sort of motion — those were things he'd very much like to be spared. Given that he had no idea how far the effects of this so-called Divine Protection actually extended, his honest wish was to avoid any needlessly tricky maneuvers.

"Understood, I'll be waiting. I'm leaving the seat next to me open. Quickly, quickly."

From the other side of the little window came Rem's voice, feigning composure while clearly unable to wait. Answering with a wry smile, Subaru raised a hand toward Roswaal.

"Well then, guess I'll be heading over."

"My-y-y, you really have tamed Rem quite thoroughly, haven't you. Almost makes me jealous."

"Being jealous of me isn't exactly what I'm going for from you, Rozwaal-chi... now if Emilia-tan got jealous, that I'd welcome with open arms!"

Tossing off a casual reply to Roswaal's quip, Subaru then turned energetically toward Emilia. But she didn't seem to have caught his remark at all, busy pulling something out from the wall of the cabin. Then, saying "Here," she handed the thing she'd pulled out to Subaru.

"I won't stop you since it's not that dangerous, but make sure you hold onto this properly."

"A cord coming out of the wall of the room... like a seatbelt kind of thing?"

"There's a chance the Dragon Carriage could tip over or something, so belts like this are equipped just in case. It's cut long, so hold onto it like a lifeline. Once you get to the driver's box, just take it off and I'll reel it back in."

Gratefully accepting Emilia's thoughtfulness, Subaru wound the handed-over belt firmly around his right wrist. As he worked on fastening the lifeline, he belatedly scolded himself for how slow he'd been to realize he should've just sat in the driver's box from the start. Though if that were the case, the sensible thing would've simply been to sit quietly in his seat and wait to arrive — but the fact that option never even crossed his mind was ample proof of just how strong-willed Subaru truly was.

Sent off by Emilia's deeply worried gaze, Subaru opened the cabin door and leaned his body out into the open. As before, the Dragon Carriage was still moving at high speed. The fact that its speed seemed to have eased ever so slightly was probably a result of Rem's consideration. Even though he could visually confirm the speed they were moving at, the fact that he felt no wind resistance whatsoever was endlessly bewildering. It was like the illusion of being moved along while enclosed inside a transparent box — and as he nearly got swallowed up by that sensation, Subaru reached out for the carriage body, grabbed hold of a handhold, and carefully began working his way around toward the driver's box.

With not a shred of wind resistance to deal with, the movement itself was remarkably smooth. All Subaru felt was the mild annoyance of making his way along a narrow, uneven path.

"This is genuinely a bizarre sensation. Is this what it's always like with a Divine Protection around?"

Experiencing the world's strange phenomena firsthand, Subaru glanced at the scenery streaming by and thought to himself. A world moving at roughly eighty kilometers an hour — what flitted through his mind just then was a fragment of an urban legend he'd once heard back in his original world. Namely:

"They say the wind resistance you feel at eighty kilometers an hour has the exact texture of a pair of BOOBIES...!"

Frustratingly, thanks to the Divine Protection's effect of blocking the wind, there was no way to verify it. If it were actually true, then in a world without Divine Protections, placed in his current situation, Subaru would've been able to fully experience, with every inch of his body, the softness of a woman's chest.

"And what's more, if I just shift my gaze a little, there's Emilia-tan inside the carriage. —Wait, what if I look at Emilia-tan while trying an air-BOOBIES simulation — wouldn't that be a victory of imagination!?"

A flash of revelation struck him at the strangest moment, and Subaru's face twisted with even greater frustration. Unable to fully let go of his lingering regret, he stretched his hand out into empty air, his fingertips clawing uselessly beneath the blue sky. And then—

"That's right, Subaru-kun. I forgot to mention this, but please try not to stick your body out too far from the carriage. —You'll end up outside the range of the Divine Protection."

That was the exact moment Rem said it.

"—No way."

The instant his fingertips closed around empty air, a gust of wind struck Subaru with enough force to nearly rip his hand right off. Caught off guard by the unexpected impact, the hand gripping the carriage body was wrenched free with ease, and his body, having lost its support, was flung straight sideways. As a matter of course, he was hurled clean out of the Dragon Carriage.

Just now, exactly the development Rem had warned him about had come to pass. Subaru had no leisure to muse about how quickly that setup had paid off, or how it was all too predictable. Caught up in the wind, he was tossed about in the gale so violently he couldn't even tell up from down.

"Abababababa—!? This is bad, this is seriously bad, don't tell me this is it... I'm gonna die!?"

Just as he was on the verge of slamming into the ground — at that very moment, his body swung up, hanging parallel to the carriage, anchored at his right arm. A tearing pain shot through his right wrist; looking down, he saw the lifeline Emilia had tied for him doing exactly what it was meant to, quite literally saving the flickering flame of Subaru's nearly-extinguished life. But even as it did, his right hand screamed in protest, the skin from the wrist down already beginning to turn a bruised blue-purple in an instant.

"Reel it in! Can I even reel myself in!? The shaking's way too bad, this is kinda impossible— gah! My tongue! I bit my tong— hgeh."

Subaru, on the verge of turning a moment of pure lechery into a full-blown disaster. His right wrist and its endurance had already reached their limit, and a dark, defeated feeling of just letting everything go began to take hold in his chest. But just then, a chain of high-pitched sounds struck his eardrums through all that resignation.

He lifted his face. Straight ahead, he saw a writhing silver snake. The tip of the snake's body was large and round, and covered all over in spikes.

"—Feels like a trauma's about to resurface."

The snake, ringing out with its high-pitched sound as it writhed, wound itself around Subaru's body. He let out a pained groan at the constriction, tighter than he'd imagined — but in exchange, it held him firmly. His airborne body was hoisted up in one motion, carried past the carriage and up above the lizard itself, before the hold released. Thrown back out into the air, in the dizzying blur below him, Subaru saw Rem. Gripping the iron handle and the reins in one hand, Rem had her other hand outstretched, ready to catch Subaru at his point of landing.

Somehow, looks like I've managed to keep my life, he confirmed, and only that—

"From now on, I'm gonna live a little more quietly..."

Words he'd been told over and over by all sorts of people — for perhaps the first time, he took them properly to heart — and without ever confirming the outcome of his fall, Subaru sank one step ahead into his 'second nap.'
