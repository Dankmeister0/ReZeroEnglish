Arc 3 Chapter 38: "Return to Reality"

Chained to the wall, cut off from Petelgeuse's one-sided conversation, Subaru had been left alone for several hours now.

The cold floor stole his body heat, and Subaru, lying prone, could no longer make his body obey him. His mind was hazy — the result of losing his stamina and his body heat both, leaving him in the grip of a fever. He repeated ragged, gasping breaths, drool trailing from the corner of his mouth. But there was no one here to nurse him through it — no one who even spared him a thought.

"Mobilize every remaining finger of the Left Hand, desu. Clearing the road of onlookers will be handled by the Right Hand alone, masu. Should that prove more than they can manage, we'll need to consider moving up the timetable and blocking the road instead, desu ne."

In stark contrast to Subaru, wasting away at the edge of the cavern's great hall, Petelgeuse — clapping his hands together in the center of the hall as he barked out orders in a high voice — was tirelessly energetic. For all that he looked like nothing so much as a skeleton wearing a human's skin, his voice, his eyes, his every movement brimmed with vitality.

Again and again, his conduct lived up to the word "diligence" that he himself kept repeating, and it was plain he was doing it deliberately. That such a man should bear the role-name "Sloth" was almost comical — but there was no one here who could laugh at it.

One after another, in an endless rotation, black-robed shadows appeared before Petelgeuse where he sat in the center of the cavern. Not one of them stirred the air with so much as a whisper as they delivered their reports to him, and once they'd received his instructions in turn, they melted back into the darkness as shadows once more.

Petelgeuse alone remained still; there was no way to tell one visitor from the next, but if each of them was truly a different person, their number easily exceeded twenty.

A cluster of black-robed figures, sprouting up out of the shadows as if grown from them — the image of it needled at something in the back of his mind, as though tugging at some memory.

"—Ah, that's right, you were still here, weren't you, desu. So? Have you found it in yourself to talk to me yet, desu ka?!"

Once every last black shadow had vanished and the two of them were alone again, Petelgeuse moved. He strode over to Subaru without a shred of hesitation and looked down at his fever-flushed face.

"Is it hard, desu ka? Does it hurt, desu ka? Are you sad, desu ka? Are you angry, desu ka? If you were a fellow believer, I wouldn't begrudge you my sympathy, desu ga — but is that what you want, masu ka?"

"——"

"Ahh, how cold of you, desu ne!"

Subaru answered the madman leaning his face in close by silently turning his own face away.

Seeing this, Petelgeuse slapped his knee in delight, leaned back, and tilted his head.

"But desu, you're finally starting to show reactions like a proper one, aren't you, desu ka. Just one more push, just a little more wavering, and I feel I'll understand you, desu yo."

"——"

"What is it I need to give you, desu ka ne. What must I bring you, desu ka ne. To make your heart tremble, to answer your love with love — what must I, do! ...desu!"

Petelgeuse worked himself up all alone, shouting with a look of ecstasy, leaving Subaru far behind. And just as he was about to sink back into his own private world as before, wallowing in rapture and spouting delusions, an interruption arrived.

"Oh myyy?"

Before Petelgeuse as he turned around, black shadows rose one after another out of the ground. Black robes that looked for all the world as if they'd sprouted from the shadows themselves — numbering perhaps a dozen or more — knelt where they stood and looked up at Petelgeuse, keeping silent. At their appearance, Petelgeuse cocked his hips, his neck, his eyes, all at once, and said—

"Now what could this be, desu ka ne?"

"——"

"What, the Left Hand's encirclement — broken? Single-handedly? Isn't that magnificent, desu ka! Outnumbered, wounded, and still she clings on with all she has… how pathetic, how slothful of my fingers, by comparison! Don't tell me she slipped past you entirely, desu ka?"

"——"

"She hasn't fled? Not running outward to seek help, but pressing further inward? Wounded from head to toe! Toward a place where she can't even be sure of the numbers waiting for her! Ahh, that is, that is that is that is—is-is-is-is-is— what a deeply loving, diligent creature she is, desu ka!!"

Clapping his hands, his face and eyes lit with delight, Petelgeuse spun around toward Subaru with a bounding motion. Then he crouched down to meet Subaru's eyes, and with a child's smile—

"You should rejoice, desu! To be loved so fiercely! A diligent soul is staking her very life on you, desu! Ahh, my brain, my brain, my brain, my brain is trembling!"

Spraying spittle in his excitement, fouling Subaru's face, Petelgeuse rose to his feet and paced busily among the kneeling shadows.

"The girl is coming, desu ka? Ahh, so that's why you've come back. That's good! Very good, desu! By all means, by all means, by all means, by all means, I want to welcome her. I must go out and greet her with my own two hands, desu!"

"——"

"Yes! Let's do just that, masu! Mobilize every finger of the Left Hand, and guide her all the way to the entrance! If she falls along the way, so be it — and if she makes it here without falling, then I shall go and welcome her myself, masu. Ahh, ahh, ahhhhhhhh, isn't that just splendid, desu, ka!!"

Petelgeuse's joy burst forth. But his words, their meaning, failed to reach him.

Yet Subaru writhed, and with his fever-flushed face still fixed in place, he opened his mouth. From lips that could produce nothing but a groan, guided by some incomprehensible feeling welling up from within.

But—

"——!"

No sound came out, as though his mouth had been sealed by something invisible.

It was different from the sensation of his throat closing up with fear or some other emotion. It was more distinct than that — the feeling of his mouth being physically blocked by something.

Subaru's eyes went wide at the smothering sensation, as if an unseen palm had been pressed over his mouth. And Petelgeuse, glancing back up at him, cackled.

"Well now, no need to rush… there's plenty of time, desu kara."

Ke-ta-ke-ta, ke-ta-ke-ta, Petelgeuse's dry laughter echoed through the cavern. At that reverberation, that unpleasant droning that struck his ears, Subaru's mouth — even freed of its stifled feeling — could weave no words. He could not manage a foolish laugh, nor could he shed tears; he could only repeat his ragged breathing and wait, motionless, for time to pass.

—The change came less than an hour later.

The black-robed figures remained kneeling in silence as before. Petelgeuse wove a zigzag path among them without a word, and only the sound of his footsteps and Subaru's ragged breathing stirred the air of the great hall.

The first to raise its head was the black shadow kneeling nearest the passage leading into the hall.

As though following that one's lead, the fanatics raised their heads one after another. Every gaze turned uniformly toward the direction of the cavern's entrance, and Petelgeuse, noticing their movement, looked that way as well — and broke into a smile of such joy it seemed the corners of his mouth might split.

"It seems she's come, desu ne."

His murmur, colored with delight, was drowned out by a thunderous roar that rang through the cavern.

The sound of some tremendous mass being shattered with bomb-like force, being destroyed, violently shook the cold air of the cavern. The chain of impacts traveled through the solid ground and reached even Subaru where he lay, and everyone present sensed that the entrance had just been thrown open by a most violent sort of knock.

The shadows rose to their feet with a sway, drawing crosses from within their robes and holding them at the ready.

For a dozen-odd people to move about in a melee, the cavern could only be called cramped. Even this great hall, which had a fair amount of space to it, was at most the size of two school classrooms.

There wasn't room enough to fly about, to run about, to really do anything. And for the intruder who was outnumbered, that was, if anything, a favorable condition.

After all, her prey was—

"—Found you."

Even from a distance it was plain to see: a roaring iron ball swept aside a cluster of black shadows lunging at her, smashing them into the wall and turning them into red stains.

With that first blow alone, the iron ball had easily claimed three lives — a killing weapon that would strip away life root and all on direct impact. Evasion was the only option against it, and even that was difficult in so narrow a cavern.

The iron ball, having fallen to the ground, shattered the bare rock, and its spikes — slick with blood and scraps of flesh — dragged with a heavy sound. The girl who stepped forward had dyed her once-blue hair a murky black, and still her gaze, undimmed, swept the hall and found the boy lying collapsed within it.

Her lips, trembling with tenderness, drew a small breath, and—

"Thank goodness, Subaru-kun—"

The girl who called Subaru's name and let the tension drain from her shoulders in relief — Rem.

Her appearance was so gruesome that it plainly showed just what a ferocious ordeal she had fought her way through.

There was not a single part of her body left unstained by blood. Her blue hair had turned a murky black, and her scorched apron dress no longer had a scrap of white left on it. Beneath her torn, ripped skirt, both legs showed a number of deep lacerations, and worst of all, her left arm was missing entirely from the elbow down.

Blood dripped from a gash in her forehead, sealing her left eye shut, and Rem — smiling faintly, wreathed head to toe in the scent of blood and death — had pushed a body that was more than merely battered and bruised to make it all the way here.

"Ahh— what a splendid thing this is, desu ka!"

And before her ghastly state, Petelgeuse raised a cheer.

He'd already forgotten the fact that his own comrades had just been slaughtered before his eyes; if anything, he took it as further fuel to work himself up, and stepped a merry, delighted little dance on the spot.

"A girl! A single girl! Wounded this badly, and still she presses on, desu! And for what? For this boy's sake, desu! To go this far to save a boy favored, favored by the Witch — you too are blessed with love, and live for love, desu!"

"Spare me your sermon, Witch Cultist…"

Petelgeuse stood as if to block the space between Subaru and Rem, cheering so hard that foam gathered at the corners of his mouth. Rem regarded his display of madness coldly.

"You are nothing but a pack of fools committing outrages within Margrave Mathers' domain, without so much as Roswaal-sama's leave. In the absence of my liege, Rem will mete out his judgment."

"In that state — that battered, battered-battered-battered-battered-battered state, desu ka? You should stop talking as if you could do something you plainly cannot, desu. In the first place, you've only come here to take this boy back, desu. Stop dressing it up in fine-sounding words, desu yo."

Crouching down, Petelgeuse seized Subaru's head and wrenched his face up. To Petelgeuse, who kept a merry grip on his hair, bobbing Subaru's resisting head up and down—

"…on't touch…"

"What was that, desu?"

"I said don't touch him!!"

Rem's body, roaring with fury, sprang forward, her wounded frame pushed to its limits as she leapt into the air.

As if chasing her leap, the black shadows still standing on the ground also bounded up into the air. Two shadows lunged at Rem. Black robes wielding cross-blades that seemed to melt into darkness pursued her body as it sailed in a straight line.

"——!"

The upraised blade aimed to skewer Rem's torso from directly below. But against the attack from beneath, she swung her left arm — the arm missing from the elbow down — to spin her body, and dodged the blade with barely a graze in midair, before—

"Rraaah!"

—swinging her right arm, the iron ball moving in tandem with it crushed one black robe's face. At the same moment, her fist, clenched around the flail's handle, struck another's head directly, caving in his skull and knocking him down.

As the two corpses fell, Rem's body landed in the center of the great hall. It was right in the middle of the gathered fanatics, and everyone thought she'd made a fatal misjudgment.

Indeed, the fanatics readied their blades and rushed at the girl as she bent her knees to land.

The number of blades thrust at her exceeded even the fingers of both her hands, and taking even one would surely deal a fatal blow. But—

"—El Huma!!"

In answer to Rem's cry, wrung out as if she were coughing blood, the corpse fallen beside her leapt. No — it was the blood gushing profusely from the corpse that changed shape under the influence of mana.

A spear of crimson blood shot up from the ground as if growing there, skewering the black shadow that had carelessly rushed in. The frozen blood-spear was brittle, snapping at its base and losing its shape the instant it struck home, but—

"—Ahh!"

—it bought more than enough time for Rem to blow apart the heads of the fanatics who'd stopped in their tracks.

Blood, brain matter, fragments of skull scattered everywhere, steam rising into the cavern's cold air. Every time Rem, mass-producing death, swung her arm, another corpse was born, and the pile grew higher.

The black robes in the great hall had numbered around fifteen. Most of them had already lost their lives to Rem's attacks, and the ones remaining seemed unlikely to stop her in her fury.

Rem's advantage was beyond doubt. Wounded, missing an arm, and still her strength overwhelmed that of the black robes.

And yet — why was it?

"Ahh, ahh, ahh…"

Petelgeuse pressed a hand to his face, watching his followers sink beneath her violence, and let out a hot breath.

—the unease kept growing, all the more because it was plain his trembling came not from grief, not from fear, not from anxiety, but from pure, unadulterated excitement.

Beside Petelgeuse, Subaru watched Rem's rampaging battle. Its meaning, the reason for this spectacle, the sight of her fighting — all of it was sinking into his brain.

He didn't understand. He didn't want to understand. He wasn't trying to understand. And yet something was getting through to him. At the sight of her, bleeding, wounded, and still fighting on, an impulse welled up from within his chest.

If he put that unease into words, perhaps he would have to—

But if he did, he might lose himself. He would have to agonize over what was right and what was wrong, over why things had to end up this way.

Out of sheer fear of that, out of putting his own precious self first, Subaru—

"My brain… is trembling."

So saying, Petelgeuse rose to his feet.

The hem of his black vestments swaying, Petelgeuse strode forward with unhurried steps. Unlike his followers, his hands held nothing at all. If anything, with his hands loosely open and swinging at his sides, he advanced with an air so relaxed there wasn't a scrap of fighting spirit in him.

A body built of nothing but skin and bone, moving in a manner that looked utterly divorced from strength.

Rem noticed his approach, and having just punched down yet another black shadow, she leapt. Hanging upside down from the ceiling, she glared down at Petelgeuse passing below.

Her bent knees stored up explosive force; in the next instant, her launched body, her arm, would surely smash Petelgeuse's slender frame to pieces.

But — and yet — why? Why was such an awful premonition churning through his heart—

"—m."

A hoarse voice crept faintly out from the back of his throat. It was a meaningless scrap of a word, carrying not the faintest trace of what he wanted to convey. But gasping, lifting his face, forcing his dulled eyes wide open—

"…Rem."

In a faint, whispering voice, he spoke her name for the first time in who knew how long.

"—Ah."

That voice, a voice so faint it seemed likely to vanish entirely — had it reached her, and her alone?

Hanging upside down in the air, the blood-soaked girl's face took on a faint, tender emotion. Her lips loosened just the barest amount, and her eyes, fixed on Subaru, trembled with joy—

—when countless crosses skewered her body and drove it down to the ground.

Seeing the blood spread from Rem's fallen body, Subaru lost his voice.

Groans of agony, moans of pain chained one after another, and the sound of something sharp driving into flesh repeated without end. It was the sound of the cross-blades stabbing Rem's fallen body over and over again.

Blades pulled themselves free and floated in midair, searching for skin not yet pierced before driving in once more. The masterless blades numbered as many as eight, and they repeated the cycle of stabbing Rem's body, pulling free, and stabbing some other spot, again and again.

"The Authority of 'Sloth' — the Unseen Hand, desu."

Petelgeuse murmured this quietly, looking down at Rem as blood gushed from her body. He waved both his hands, raised before his face, thrusting them out as if to display that they held nothing—

"To make a hand reach where no hand can reach. To accomplish something with a body that does not move. To labor diligently with a slothful body — ahh, at my own 'Sloth,' my brain, is trembling, I think, desu!"

The crosses, drawn free all at once, plunged into her body all at once again. Rem's body, no longer even managing a groan, lay face-down on the ground without so much as a twitch.

Watching Rem's final moment in a daze, Subaru had no voice left. Eyes wide, even forgetting to breathe, before the blue-haired girl's end sinking into the darkness — before an unacceptable reality once again — his consciousness began to fade. But—

"Running away is not permitted, desu."

His escape was cut short by Petelgeuse, who seized his bangs and wrenched his face up roughly.

To Subaru, wincing at the pain and shock, Petelgeuse brought his face close, his wide-open gray eyes — looking fit to pop from their sockets — darting about wildly.

"Look, look at her, you must look, desu. The girl is dead, desu. She died a martyr to love, desu. She fought through her wounds, pressed forward against her fear, and in the end accomplished nothing at all, desu."

"Ah, ahh…"

"You must look, desu. You must sear it into your mind, desu. The result — of what you have done."

"—Ah?"

Petelgeuse yanked his face forward with all his strength, refusing to let him look away from Rem, sunk in her sea of blood. Even as Subaru still tried to escape, Petelgeuse pressed him down against the ground, pinned his face in place with both hands, and breathed his fetid breath over him.

"This is the result of what you have done, desu. You did nothing, you were 'Slothful.' And so the girl died, desu! You — killed her, desu."

"…you did."

"With my arms! With my fingers! With my body! You did, you did, you did-you-did-you-did-you-did-did-did-did-did… killed her, desu!!"

There came the sound of something tearing apart with a wet snap. Right before his eyes, within Subaru's field of vision, Rem's body jolted violently.

Her left arm had been torn off at the shoulder. The severed arm was flung aside like garbage, rolling exposed across the cavern's cold floor.

"Snap. Rip. Sn-a-a-p."

"…op it."

Petelgeuse voiced cheap little sound effects, and with each one, Rem's body was further destroyed. An ear was torn off, her right ankle twisted and severed, and the embedded blades trembled, widening the wounds.

Rem was being violated. Her dignity, right before his eyes, was being defiled so easily, so cheerfully.

That sight, that scene, a sight so terrible that the thought of looking away didn't even occur to him—

"—Petelgeuuuuse!!"

—was enough to bring back to himself the Subaru who had been afraid to look reality in the face.

He stretched his neck toward the man right beside him, meaning to tear out his throat with his teeth. But the shackles held him back, and he fell just short. Pitching forward, he crashed face-first into the ground from the momentum. Looking down at him, Petelgeuse laughed with delight.

"Ahh, you've finally called my name, haven't you, desu ka. I'm so happy, desu yo!"

"I'll kill you, I'll kill you… I'll kill you, kill you, I'll kill you, I'll definitely kill you. I'll kill you. I'll kill you! Kill, kill… die, let me kill you, die, die, diiie!"

"To hate someone in order to live! Ahh, how twisted, how splendid, desu yo. It makes it all worthwhile — my diligence, and the diligence of my fingers, desu."

"I'll kill you. I'll kill you. Rem — you — I'll kill you. Kill you, kill you, kill youuu. Ahh! I'll kill you! I'll kill you! Die, you bastard! You bastard, ahh, diiie!"

Spraying spittle, scattering curses, he raised a howl of resentful fury. The shackles binding his arms creaked, and the ones biting into his legs drank the blood welling up around them, gleaming dully.

Let his arms tear off. Let his legs tear off. If he could just get free of these shackles right now, right here, and kill the man in front of him, that would be enough. He hated him, hated him, hated him past all bearing. He should die. He must not be allowed to go on living. This man had to die — certainly, right now, in this very instant.

"Well, this place has gotten quite filthy too, so shall we be saying our farewells soon, masu ka?"

Slapping his knee, Petelgeuse suddenly wiped the mad laughter from his face and let out a breath. Paying no mind to Subaru, whose body kept shuddering with fury, he beckoned the surviving believers to gather round, and said—

"We'll abandon this place, masu. Faster than cleaning it up, desu kara. The Trials of the Tomb are scheduled for the day after tomorrow, desu, at the location where the Right Hand's index finger is lying low. The rest of you will continue on carrying out your duties as the Left Hand. However, once all five fingers are gathered, divide up the numbers equally — understood, desu ne?"

"Die! Go and die! Die, die, diiie!"

Having quickly issued his orders, Petelgeuse clapped his hands. At that signal, the believers turned to shadow and returned to the dim darkness of the cavern.

And so, through the cavern whose number of survivors had been greatly reduced, Petelgeuse walked slowly.

The high click of his shoes echoed through the cavern, and at the retreating sound, Subaru hurled a blood-spitting scream—

"Wait, you bastard! I'll kill you! I'll kill you! Die right there! Die now! Hurry up and die! Die! Die! Diiie!!"

"Oh, I nearly forgot."

His feet stopped, and Petelgeuse turned back with an easygoing air. To Subaru, glaring up at him from below with hatred in every line of his face, Petelgeuse gave a single nod.

"As for your position, desu, I honestly can't say. So I think I'll judge it by whether or not a Gospel comes to bear on the matter, masu."

Raising a finger, Petelgeuse tilted his head ninety degrees and laughed a grim, ugly laugh.

"Bound hand and foot and left here, all that awaits you is death, desu. But if, hypothetically, a Gospel should come to bear on you here, in this place — then you will surely be saved."

"——"

"If you're saved, then you are a fellow believer. If you're not saved, then you were nothing but a passerby. Well then, how does that sound, desu ka ne?"

As if to say what a brilliant idea, Petelgeuse laughed cheerfully, and this time he truly turned his back on Subaru. At his retreating back, Subaru went on hurling curses, striving with words, with will alone, to kill Petelgeuse, pouring out every ounce of hatred he had.

Showing not the slightest effect of any of it in his stride, Petelgeuse headed for the exit. And on the way, he stopped beside Rem's mercilessly destroyed corpse, and said—

"Ah, that's right, that's right, desu ne. You — tried so very hard."

Murmuring this quietly, he straightened his back before Rem's corpse and made a gesture as if crossing himself.

"You died a martyr to love, and resisted with everything you had. And yet, falling short, defeated, your love lost its place to go, and your wish drifted unfulfilled into the void."

His tone, which had sounded as if praising her, turned on its head — his words now speaking of the futility of her efforts, of how they had all come to nothing — and crouching down, Petelgeuse let a mocking smile rise to his lips.

"Ahh, you… you were 'Sloth,' weren't you, desu ne."

In the worst way possible, he insulted the existence of the girl named Rem, and walked on.

"——!!!"

A roar, a scream, rang out through the cavern. Rage thick enough to choke his throat, passion too great for words, regret bitter enough to weep tears of blood — all of it tore an inhuman cry from Natsuki Subaru.

Hearing it, Petelgeuse laughed as though he'd just received the highest praise. Ke-ta-ke-ta, ke-ta-ke-ta.

His footsteps did not stop. Stopping his retreating back was impossible, of course — and so was stopping the breath in his lungs.

Ke-ta-ke-ta, ke-ta-ke-ta — his laughter went on and on, endlessly.

Ke-ta-ke-ta, ke-ta-ke-ta. Ke-ta-ke-ta, ke-ta-ke-ta.

—Ke-ta-ke-ta, ke-ta-ke-ta, ketaketaketaketaketaketa.

I rendered this by keeping "desu"/"masu" as trailing verbal tics in his English lines (a convention borrowed from similar quirky-speech characters in other translated works), rather than translating them away. Flagging this as a stylistic substitution for review.

I approximated the truncation effect in English ("—m." and "…on't touch…") rather than translating the full words, to preserve the halting-speech effect; exact phrasing is a judgment call.
