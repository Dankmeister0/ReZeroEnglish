Arc 3 Chapter 58: "The Threat of the Fog"

The Lifaus Highway rang with raucous laughter.

The White Whale let its massive body drift leisurely through the sky, a high-pitched shriek pouring from every mouth that dotted its body. Unlike the earth-shaking roar it had unleashed before, the sound leaking from that uneven multitude of oral cavities was sharp, like wind being clawed apart.

Subaru couldn't suppress the shudder of revulsion that ran through his whole body — a sensation distinct from having his eardrums pounded.

That first strike had been thrown with everything they had, and between Wilhelm and the fervent efforts of the Beast Mercenary Corps, the damage dealt to the White Whale was hardly negligible. The total firepower alone would probably be enough to kill Subaru a hundred times over with change to spare, and if you scaled the comparison down to something more like ordinary Witchbeasts, it looked like enough force to wipe out a pack of Ulgarms ten times over.

And the creature had taken all of that, full in the body, in rapid succession. Even if it hadn't been enough to finish it off, Subaru had expected it to take commensurate damage and drop to the ground —

"Oh, hell, the fog's—!"

Even as it let out that shrieking cry from every part of its body, the White Whale's countless mouths were simultaneously spewing "fog." Across a broad stretch of the highway, the fog descended from the sky and encroached upon the world, gradually, gradually repainting the field of view a whitish haze.

This was the "fog" Witchbeast showing its true colors. Fog filled the highway, blotting out visibility and throwing coordination between allies into disarray. And more than that — despite its body being a colossal, towering mass, that white form began to dissolve, melting away into the white sea the fog created.

"You've gotta be kidding me...!?"

"—Subaru-kun, please entrust your life to Rem!!"

Subaru's throat trembled at that shocking display of hidden skill, and in front of him Rem leaned forward and cried out. In answer, Subaru sank himself deep against her and clung to her waist from behind. The Earth Dragon obeyed Rem's swift handling of the reins and spun its body, tearing at the ground as it broke into a sprint. Felis, who had been beside them until a moment ago, likewise made a sharp turn, pointing his Earth Dragon's head into the fog. Once the White Whale entered combat mode, a counterattack was inevitable. Naturally, casualties couldn't be avoided. And when that happened, that was exactly where the one called "Blue" would have his moment to shine —

Immediately after,

"—All units, fall back!!!"

A furious bellow rang out from beyond the fog, checking those who had been about to plunge into the white darkness. The voice was unmistakably Crusch's. Before Subaru could even lift his head to ask what?—

"Whoa!?"

In an instant's judgment, the Earth Dragon changed its direction of travel, and Subaru's body, thrown about by centrifugal force, shifted to the left. Ahead of them, Felis's Earth Dragon, wheeling just as sharply, swung to the right. And through the exact middle of the path Subaru and the others had split into, left and right — a dense, massive fog swept through in a single burst.

The surging fog's reach was wide enough to blot out the whole field of view; there was no doubt that if their evasion had been even a moment slower, it would have swallowed them and their Earth Dragon whole.

It's just fog, why all the fuss — he might have laughed it off if he hadn't seen it with his own eyes, but having actually witnessed its abnormality firsthand, no glib remark was possible.

The fog gouged into the plain it swept over as though melting the very ground, swallowing everything in its path root and stem, and literally dispersing it into mist. If he had taken a hit of that, he would have been erased to somewhere that wasn't anywhere in this world.

"So this is the real deal — the 'fog'..."

Among the several things they'd been told beforehand at the subjugation force's briefing about the terror of the White Whale, one was the power of this destructive "fog." There were two kinds of fog released from the White Whale's mouths: one was a purely diffusive fog meant to blanket visibility and expand its own range of movement. The other was the annihilation-type fog that had, just now, gouged the earth clean away right in front of them.

The latter was what the White Whale used as an offensive weapon, and the fog's true horror lay not merely in its unstoppable power and the damage it caused, but somewhere else entirely. That was —

"——Haaah!!"

A spirited shout, a valiant cry, arrived from beyond the fog, and the next moment the fog that had been covering their view was abruptly swept away. Looking over, beyond the cleared field of vision, Crusch stood on the back of her Earth Dragon in a follow-through stance, arm fully extended. She had likely applied her invisible blade technique to slice through the dense fog and clear their sight.

She roughly wiped the sweat clinging to her brow, dropped into a seated crouch on the back of her galloping Earth Dragon, and scanned the surroundings. As though using her as a landmark, the scattered subjugation force began gathering, and to those at the front of the pack, Crusch spoke.

"—How many were taken?"

"My unit numbered twelve — three are missing."

"...Who was taken?"

"We don't know...!"

The older squad leader answered Crusch's question, then shook his head at the one that followed. It was an exchange that made no sense. A squad leader who kept track of his unit's numbers, and yet he still couldn't say which member had dropped out.

But—

"Ours is fourteen, one missing." "Our unit, two. Also unknown." "Six...! Our apologies! We were positioned deep in, we couldn't avoid the fog...!"

Similar reports rose one after another, and none of the squad leaders could produce the names of their vanished comrades.

That very abnormality was the true, terrifying nature of the "fog" the White Whale wielded. In other words—

"The fog of annihilation...!!"

Quite literally, any being that took a hit of the "fog" and vanished was erased from the world, existence and all. Who had disappeared — the fact remained, but it lingered in no one's memory. That was the true reason Crusch had organized each squad of the subjugation force into units of exactly fifteen.

When a squad lost a member to the "fog," no one could even tell who had been taken. So that the fact of the loss itself, at least, could still be recognized, the squads were kept at matched numbers.

When Subaru had encountered the White Whale in a previous iteration of the world, a strange phenomenon had occurred. It was when Otto, the traveling merchant who'd accompanied them, had cleanly erased from memory both the existence of a fellow merchant and the existence of Rem, who'd stayed behind to buy them time. At the time, Subaru had simply assumed Otto had erased an inconvenient memory out of sheer terror — but it all made sense if that had actually been under the influence of the White Whale's "fog."

The merchant and Rem, erased from the world by the fog, had vanished from everyone's memory. Just as, when they returned to the mansion, even Rem's twin sister Ram had had her memory of Rem erased.

And yet, once again —

"...Only I remember."

Stunned, Subaru voiced that undeniable reality. Just as Subaru hadn't forgotten the vanished merchant, or Rem, who'd sacrificed herself to let Subaru escape, in that loop — only Subaru remembered.

Among the veteran soldiers serving as squad leaders gathered around Crusch — of those fifteen faces, two had already been swapped out for different ones. The original squad leaders had taken the "fog" and vanished, and the recognition had shifted to accept that the deputy who took their place had been the squad leader all along. And so no one noticed the sudden change in personnel.

Faced anew with this abnormal state of affairs, Subaru was reminded that this fog-wielding Witchbeast had a strong connection to the "Witch." And that the black hand binding Subaru to this world was surely not unrelated either.

"Now that we've been infiltrated by the fog, there's no telling from where it might come to erase more of us. Staying clustered is a poor strategy too. — Disperse, and use the fog-clearing crystal stones."

Crusch surveyed the gathered members of the subjugation force and cut the discussion short. As Subaru watched everyone nod in agreement, he noticed with a start that neither Wilhelm nor Ricardo were present in the group.

Don't tell me those two were erased as well.

But that inward shudder of Subaru's was disproven by a shadow emerging from beyond the fog.

"You've returned, Wilhelm."

Slowly parting the thick fog was Wilhelm, wielding a blood-soaked blade from the back of his Earth Dragon. Drenched head to toe in the enemy's blood, he wiped what had splattered onto his face with his sleeve as he rejoined the group.

"I overextended myself. — What's the extent of the damage?"

"Seventeen in total... roughly one full squad's worth erased. We can no longer even properly honor the names of the fallen."

Annihilation, quite literally, meant erasure of existence. For those people, remembered by no one, all that remained was a blank space — the sense that someone must have been there.

Where did the bonds, the feelings, the love that had once existed there go?

Looking over, a pack of Ligers appeared from behind Wilhelm, and among them Ricardo could be seen riding a large Liger, along with the two vice-captains. It seemed the group that had been clinging to the White Whale, fighting up close like Wilhelm, had sustained comparatively fewer losses.

"A troublesome fog to have come out. Our stock of fog-clearing crystal stones is nothin' to feel secure about. ...Use 'em wrong, and it's over."

"If we land another concentrated barrage like before, it should drop to the ground. With its location lost to us, this is also the moment to use them to avoid an ambush. Any objections?"

Everyone silently signaled their agreement to Crusch's words. Receiving that, she nodded and turned her gaze to the support unit led by Felis.

"Felis. Launch the fog-clearing crystal stones. We only have enough for two uses. Handle them carefully."

"Preparations are already complete, nyah. Whenever you give the order."

Crusch dipped her chin in response to Felis, who thumped his chest, and once more, before battle resumed, she swept her gaze across every face present.

"This is where it counts! That our attacks reach the White Whale is proven by the resistance still lingering in your hands! Indeed, it is mighty. Its nature is unknowable. Our deaths may, at worst, be remembered by no one. But!"

Crusch, for whom the sword at her hip — House Karsten's treasured blade — would be a useless ornament given that she fought with unarmed strikes, drew it anyway and raised it to the sky, her voice ringing out.

"For the sake of the dead whose names could not remain upon any gravestone, and for the sake of the weak who will yet be exposed to the fog's threat in the world beyond this — though it cost us sacrifice, we shall slay it. Follow me!!"

"——Ohhh!!"

Each of them raised their weapons skyward and let out a unified cheer. A tremendous surge of morale shook the very fog, igniting and stoking the fighting spirit that had begun to sink.

"Fog-clearing crystal stones — launch!!"

At Crusch's command, the members under Felis's direction all raised their launch-tubes skyward — the next instant, with a thundering boom, the crystal stones launched beyond the fog.

"The fog's clearing—!!"

Light burst from the shattered crystal stones, sweeping away in one stroke the fog that had blanketed their vision. That said, it wasn't as if the fog covering all four directions of the plain had been entirely cleared. It had only lifted the state that had thinned everything out and made securing visibility so difficult. But even that alone was more than enough of an effect.

The "fog" the White Whale released was apparently a mutation of the Mana the White Whale itself possessed. In other words, mana given directionality and rendered visible was what corresponded to the "fog." "Fog-clearing crystal stone" was a nickname; its proper name was demon-warding crystal — an item that, by possessing it, granted a one-time substitute-defense function against magical attacks. Breaking multiple such crystal stones at once produced the effect just seen. It was a risky gamble, though — without fog filling the air, it would even dampen the power of their own magical attacks.

"Not enough to clear all the fog away, huh."

"On the other hand, it has no effect on our magic either. Rem is at full readiness."

Rem answered, lightly clenching her fist, the horn on her forehead giving off a glow. Feeling the sensation of mana filling the air around her against his skin, Subaru dipped his chin, acknowledging that Rem was already beginning to weave magic again.

"—Alright! No time to be scared. We haven't been any use at all up to this point. Let's make this our turn to step up!"

"Yes! Let's go!"

The reins jangled beneath the Earth Dragon's neck, and its whinny set off a bounding jolt. On the back of the galloping Earth Dragon, clinging to Rem's waist, Subaru let his gaze wander overhead through the thinned fog, searching for the White Whale.

The subjugation force, having charged out at Crusch's command, likewise scattered in every direction, eyes darting as they searched for the colossal form, the tension of not knowing when battle would resume beginning to dry out the inside of Subaru's mouth. Still no sign of the White Whale's appearance. It resembled the feeling from before battle had even begun — waiting in the depths of that darkness for the White Whale to appear —

"—Fog."

Suddenly, an unpleasant premonition raced through Subaru's mind. There wasn't any particular basis for it. As he mentally ran back through the effect of the fog-clearing crystal stones, the use of magic once that effect had lapsed, and even the circumstances of their previous encounter with the White Whale, that unease had simply welled up out of nowhere.

—The effect was different from the fog of annihilation, but if the fog covering this whole area was also something the White Whale had breathed out, was its threat really limited to just disrupting visibility?

But before that question could make it past his lips —

"————!!!"

A high, high roar echoed across the thinned fog of the Lifaus Highway, faster than his doubt. And then, madness spread like a contagion.

The first sign of trouble appeared in the group of Earth Dragons running alongside them.

The shrill roar was unlike anything the White Whale had let out before — not something that shook the air with a low rumble, but something that carried the unpleasant sensation of eardrums being clawed apart mercilessly by sharp talons.

As Subaru's throat clenched at that violence of sound, enough to raise gooseflesh across his back, and even Rem's body went rigid, Subaru suddenly noticed the abnormality.

"Hey! What's wrong!?"

From the Earth Dragon running beside them, riders were being thrown off one after another, flailing. Hearing Subaru's shout, Rem, noticing the abnormality too, U-turned their Earth Dragon toward it. Weaving through the riderless Earth Dragons careening in all directions, Subaru made his way to the fallen men.

"You okay!? Falling off like that, that's not just a scrape—"

Concerned about the severity of their injuries, Subaru called out — and found the words catching in his throat. The state of the men who had fallen wasn't even on the level of assessing the depth of an injury.

There was a man foaming at the mouth, eyes rolled back, convulsing. There was a man groaning, drooling, clawing desperately at his own arm until it bled. There was a man gritting his teeth until they cracked as if enduring pain, slamming his head against the ground.

The symptoms weren't consistent, but one thing was clear.

"This is..."

"Directly on the mind... it resembles Mana Poisoning, but far more malicious...!"

Rem answered, pained, hand pressed to her forehead. At her state, Subaru muttered "Mana Poisoning..." under his breath, then snapped his head up and looked around — at the fog.

"Don't tell me — this is the fog's doing!?"

Enveloping the enemy force in fog thick enough to fill their entire field of vision, then, from a state of inescapability, unleashing an area-effect status ailment. — Its power and results spoke for themselves.

It seemed unlikely the damage was limited to the handful of people around Subaru and the others. In fact, looking as far as his eyes could reach, other groups elsewhere had likewise ground to a halt.

"Some are resistant and some aren't, huh... I don't feel anything."

"Rem felt a little... from the excessive density of mana. It's settling now."

Subaru left Rem be for a moment as she began taking deep breaths, then dismounted the Earth Dragon and went to the men under the fog's influence. He tried at least to stop them from harming themselves, but—

"Hey! Stop that, any more and the wound's gonna— whoa!"

"Aaaah! Ahihihiahhhhh!"

He reached an arm out to stop the man and had his upper arm raked without mercy. Wincing at the pain of flesh shallowly gouged, Subaru instinctively leapt back to put distance between them. The man, in turn, didn't pursue further and simply resumed his attack on himself. Blood scattered, and a sobbing, whimpering voice echoed on and on.

"'Painful,' hell, this looks seriously bad, doesn't it? Left alone, he might not stop till he's dead, this!"

"Subaru-kun! Your wound!?"

"Stings a bit, could almost cry, but it's nothing serious! More importantly, we've gotta do something about this or everyone's gonna destroy themselves! Is there any way?"

He asked Rem, who had settled her breathing, but she looked at the knights writhing on the ground and shook her head, her expression grim.

"I'm afraid Rem's healing may only go so far... If it were simple physical trauma that would be one thing, but this is interfering directly with the inside, through the Gate. Mana contamination this strong requires someone at least on the level of Felix-sama's skill..."

"How much resistance do people even have to this mental contamination, anyway? Aside from me and you, our side's basically wiped out!"

The unit that had been heading the same direction as Subaru and Rem was nearly annihilated — only the squad leader and two others seemed unharmed, and like Subaru and Rem, they were frantically trying to stop their self-harming comrades. With more than half of about ten people contaminated, the extent of the effect was—

"If even Felis, who's crucial here, got hit with the contamination, we're totally screwed. What do we do..."

If this was the state of just Subaru and Rem's group, it was hopeless to imagine every other squad was in a similar state. If the main force — Crusch, Wilhelm — and the linchpin of support, Felis, went down, there'd truly be no way to recover.

"Those who can still move, get the wounded to the great tree's roots! A little forceful handling can't be helped!"

Crusch's voice rose again from beyond the thick fog, followed immediately by voices responding. It seemed she hadn't been affected by the contamination, but even so, it was clear she was scrambling desperately to bring the situation under control. It was an about-face immediately after ordering an all-out attack. The frustration bleeding into her voice came through clearly, and Subaru even felt a flare of anger at the White Whale's methods — not simply forcing them out of combat outright, but this.

"I've heard that inflicting injuries is more effective at collapsing a battle line than killing outright... but a monster pulling that off?"

"Felix-sama seems unharmed. If his treatment can reach everyone, it should at least strip away the contamination's effect, but..."

Subaru understood what Rem, trailing off, meant to say. What mattered was that doing so would tie up Felis's hands, and that hands would need to be spared to recover the wounded. And above all else—

"We don't have time. We can't just sit here defenseless waiting on the Whale's next attack the whole time it takes for Felis's treatment to reach everyone."

"At worst, the White Whale might swallow us all whole in fog while we're gathered like this. I don't want to believe it has that much intelligence, but... given that it's already engineered this situation, we can't afford optimism."

"Might just be acting on instinct... but either way, we can't afford to underestimate how a wild predator hunts."

Crusch was probably prepared to rally the subjugation force and have Felis treat them, danger and all. She surely understood, too, that something had to be thrown out to buy time — some kind of breakthrough. Whether something effective would actually come of it was another matter, and a difficult one at that.

"—Whew."

He let out a deep breath, emptying his lungs. As he pushed the air from his body to its limit, he could feel, in the chest that naturally tightened, his own heartbeat keeping a slow, steady rhythm.

He gave a wry, surprised laugh at himself, finding he was unexpectedly calm. He'd always been swept along by circumstance, tossed about by whatever was in front of him, his heart racing out of control as if mirroring the state of his mind. So why, now, facing a decision, could he be this composed?

"...Even borrowed, courage is still courage, I guess."

He struck his chest and drew in a deep breath. Held it a moment, closed his eyes, then let it out and opened them. He faced forward. Directly ahead, Rem, mounted on the Earth Dragon, was looking down at him — waiting, as if in anticipation, for whatever Subaru was about to say, whatever he was about to ask.

"Rem, come with me to the most dangerous spot there is."

"Yes. — Anywhere at all."

Rem accepted Subaru's request without hesitation, even with the hint of a smile. At that, Subaru dashed to the Earth Dragon and leapt onto its back as if flying, then turned to the knights holding back their thrashing, self-harming comrades on the ground.

"Rem and I will draw the White Whale off. While we do, all of you get treated by Felis. Anyone who looks stable enough, hand them off to Felis and then regroup!"

"Draw it off!? How exactly...?"

"Like this."

Subaru grinned at the veteran soldier who'd voiced his doubt, drew in a breath, and opened his throat.

"—Anyone who can hear me, cover your ears!! Those who can't, just stay as you are!!"

His full-voiced shout rang out through the fog. Rem listened to Subaru's bellow with what looked like enjoyment, then put both hands to her ears. The nearby knights hastily covered their ears too, and presumably the rest of the subjugation force, wherever Subaru's voice could reach, did the same.

Just as Subaru had asked, in the briefing before the operation. And then—

"—I'm going to di—"

The instant he spoke it, welling terror coiled around Subaru's heart. If his plan misfired, if that black hand reached instead for the comrades around him, for Rem right in front of him—

But—

—I'll give up my own heart, so lend me a hand!!

Eyes wide open, forcing down his weakness, Subaru screamed it inwardly — and the next instant, it came.

"I love you."

It was a faint, small, frail voice, as though whispered right beside his ear. And yet, what was this — this heat-laden feeling packed within it, enough to make his chest tremble? Subaru couldn't hold back the emotion that welled tears at the corners of his eyes, unbidden. He chased after the fading trace of that voice, seized by the urge to catch it in his arms right then and there. In the overwhelming heat of adoration filling every corner of his being, his consciousness burned pure white—

"—I'm back."

After that instant's encounter, Subaru's consciousness woke into reality. The sensation that had dominated Subaru's mind until a moment ago receded, and he could no longer even recall what feelings he'd found there. Only a strange sensation remained — as though the searing pain he'd braced for hadn't come after all. Even so—

"Rem, how is it. Do I smell like the Witch...?"

"Yes, you stink!"

"That's what I wanted, but couldn't you say it a little nicer!?"

With Rem's seal of approval, unsatisfying as the delivery was, the goal was accomplished. Subaru clenched his fist and scanned the surroundings.

"We're leaving here right away! I'll try to keep away from the roots as much as I can, so make sure you regroup properly with Crusch and the others!"

"R-right! Good luck!"

"You too!"

Sent off, Subaru clapped him on the shoulder, and Rem set the Earth Dragon running. At present, Subaru's body was surely giving off a fresh Witch's Lingering Scent — a phrase that, written out, was riddled with contradiction. The question was how much effect this would have on the White Whale.

"Thinking back to the time with the Ulgarms, it had an effect covering practically the whole forest, but how's it gonna go this time... honestly, it's an unknown."

The last time he'd encountered the White Whale in a previous iteration of the world, the creature had doggedly targeted Subaru after he'd transferred to Otto's dragon carriage. Considering that at that point Subaru hadn't even said anything related to the Witch, the Subaru now giving off a stronger scent than back then ought to be a prime piece of bait for the White Whale —

And right as that thought crossed his mind—

"—!?"

The advancing Earth Dragon jerked its head up sharply as if noticing something, then, on its own judgment, wheeled around at once — Subaru, nearly thrown by the centrifugal force, let out a "gyaah!" and hastily clung to Rem in front of him for dear life.

"What's—!"

"The White Whale!!"

Rem, pressed close against him, shouted, just as a massive maw suddenly tore through the fog right beside them. By a hair's breadth, missing Subaru and the others who'd swerved off its path, the White Whale's jaw slid past just to their left, chewing up the ground, swallowing whole the stretch of grassland in its way.

Grazing past that rock-like, sturdy hide, Subaru and the others cut right past it, and behind them the White Whale lifted its jaw and swallowed down whatever was in its mouth in one go. Then the Witchbeast, confirming its intended target hadn't been mixed in with what it had chewed, spun its massive body around and turned its head toward Subaru and the others as they fled at full speed. And then, a roar came chasing after them.

"Waaaaaaah!?"

The pressure of that overwhelming mass bearing down from behind. Chased by a crushing sense of pressure at his back, the Earth Dragon carrying the shouting Subaru kicked desperately at the earth. But the pursuing White Whale's speed was nothing short of abnormal. That mountain-sized bulk swam through the sky, closing the distance at a pace fast enough to outrun the wind.

Steadily, relentlessly, the White Whale closed in with the momentum of something swallowing the world whole. Its snout drew close enough that its very breath reached their backs, and—

"Rem!!"

"Ur Hyuma!!"

In response to Rem's incantation, three spears of ice thrust up from the ground all at once. True to their aim, they pierced the White Whale chasing them from directly below, striking its underbelly — comparatively softer even within that tough hide — impaling it, attempting to halt its movement.

But—

"It's not stopping——!!"

The ice spears, thick as a hundred spears bundled together, snapped off at the base, the crystal shattering with a shrill, ringing crash. Destroyed, the ice spears reverted to mana, and blood gushed from the wounds now left unplugged in the White Whale's belly — but its movement showed no sign of faltering. Wounded that badly, bleeding that much, and still showing no loss of vigor — where was the limit to this endurance? Subaru shuddered anew at just how high a hurdle the task of bringing down the White Whale truly was.

And yet—

"Unlike the Ulgarm, this ain't a one-on-one fight, you bastard!!"

"————!!"

Subaru raised his middle finger, taunting the White Whale as the distance opened between them. The Whale, even without understanding what the gesture meant, opened its mouth and let out a roar as if sensing his defiance.

That massive body—

"Riaaaaaaa—!!"

—was split vertically by a slash from Wilhelm, who leapt in from the side and landed on its back. Landing squarely in the middle of the White Whale's back, Wilhelm's blade drove deep into the flesh, and bright blood sprayed from the opened wound, forming a mist of blood. And climbing toward that blood-soaked back of the White Whale came two Ligers. Riding their backs were two adorable, near-identical kitten Beastfolk children—

"Onee-chan, together!" "Here we go, Hetaro!!"

The two Ligers rose to the back from left and right, crossing paths, and from their backs Mimi and Hetaro leapt down, joined hands, and landed above the wound Wilhelm had carved. Then, looking at each other, they opened their mouths in unison—

"Wa—!" "Ha—!!"

The next instant, every single wound across the White Whale's body began bleeding again all at once, and its massive body shuddered violently, dropping altitude in one sudden plunge. Writhing in agony, letting out a voice as if enduring pain, the White Whale barely avoided crashing. From its back, the twins riding the Ligers leapt down and, catching their breath—

"Trump card, complete!" "Captain, please!!"

"Alright, alright, leave it to me! If the little ones did their part, I gotta step up too!!"

Swapping places with the two descending children, the large Liger began climbing the White Whale's body from the tail up. Peeling back the rocky hide along the way, carving out wounds as it went, Ricardo's Liger raced like the wind — and the fog closed in on it. Targeting Wilhelm and Ricardo atop the body was the dense fog spat out from the countless mouths dotting the White Whale's form. That especially deep-colored fog was surely the "fog of annihilation" that erased anything it touched from existence.

Its speed alone wasn't so great that it couldn't be dodged, but with as many mouths as it had, that many barrages opened up at once. Ricardo's Liger twisted its body, struggling to weave through, and Wilhelm too dodged the fog with swift, agile movement, his sword's slashes and the great cleaver's blows continuing their dance atop the White Whale's back.

A vertical slash tore through a mouth mid-warped, cackling laugh, and the great cleaver ravaged the interior of another oral cavity, crushing its function. The Ligers' claws, too, kept up minor harassing attacks, and above all, the Beast Mercenary Corps' cannon crews, having finally caught up, began once more bombarding the White Whale's whole body with magic-ore explosives.

The White Whale, pressed once again by the subjugation force's combined offense, twisted its massive body — and just as its mouths seemed to be preparing to spit fog again—

"Rem!!"

Faster than Subaru's call, Rem had already wheeled the Earth Dragon around, and Subaru, trailing the Witch's scent, sprinted circles around the White Whale's snout. Its concentration disrupted, the White Whale reflexively turned its head toward Subaru and the others — only for a slash to thwart that intention.

"————!!"

"Looking elsewhere — how cold of you. I have been infatuated with you for fourteen years now, without fail."

The thrust drove into the White Whale's forehead, the blade sinking into the hard portion, halting Wilhelm's movement. But he immediately abandoned his third sword, kicked the hilt of the discarded blade with full force to drive the blade in even deeper, and drew a fifth sword to hold in his right hand, hacking mercilessly at the White Whale's head with blades in both hands as he made his way toward the back.

Silencing, quite literally, every mouth in his path with his blades one after another, he made his way to join Ricardo, who was tearing about wildly, sending his Liger running every which way. The dog-faced Beastman grinned with an expression drunk on blood, delighted.

"This is gettin' fun! Tougher than I figured, but nothin' we can't handle!"

"No... it feels a little too easy, actually."

Answering Ricardo's cheer with a low reply, Wilhelm bit his lip as he kept carving into the back with light, stepping movements. Ricardo, swinging his great cleaver upward to tear a protrusion off the White Whale's back and then thrusting the flat tip into the opening to gouge it out, closed one eye.

"Somethin' botherin' you?"

"That my wife — the Sword Saint — should fall behind against a Witchbeast of this caliber is hard to believe. Even accounting for our seizing the initiative, and for the fact that we weren't split apart by the fog at the very start..."

Even as Wilhelm mused this while swinging his blade, the White Whale suddenly made a great motion. Its writhing, which had until then been aimed at shaking off Wilhelm and the others clinging to its back, abruptly changed. The White Whale turned its head upward and, gaining altitude in one sweeping motion, made for the sky.

As the footing beneath them tilted sharply, Ricardo directed his Liger, skillfully racing down the rocky hide, managing to disengage before reaching a dangerous height. And Wilhelm—

"Before I climb down, I'll take one more—!"

Spun his body and, with a light, nimble motion, the old swordsman leapt across the colossal form. As the White Whale's body rose, Wilhelm plunged downward, climbing the ascending body upside down. Through weight shifts and forceful posture control achieved by driving his blades in, he descended the White Whale's body using the accumulated skill of long years of experience, and Wilhelm's slash, upon reaching its terminus, swept in a great vertical arc — one of the White Whale's dorsal fins, one bearing several mouths across its surface, was blown clean off at its base.

"————!!"

Listening to the White Whale's scream, Wilhelm kicked off the rocky hide and made for the ground. A fall from such an extreme height would, in ordinary circumstances, be a scene almost certain to end in death — but just before hitting the ground, Wilhelm pulled something from his coat and hurled it straight downward: a small-scale blast from a magic-ore charge created an updraft directly beneath him, providing enough deceleration that an Earth Dragon swept in from the side to snatch him up.

"Wilhelm-san!!"

"————"

Subaru called out, trying to confirm he was unharmed. But Wilhelm paid it no mind, his gaze tilted straight upward, fixed overhead. Following his gaze upward, Subaru caught sight of the White Whale's tail swimming far above in the sky. Blood dripped from where the dorsal fin had been sheared off, falling like rain under the violent momentum, dyeing the plain's grass crimson — and beside it all, Wilhelm, silent, showed no diminishing of his fighting spirit.

Subaru didn't think for a moment the White Whale was simply going to flee as things stood, but the creature's intent, having gone up into the sky, remained unclear for the moment. The Beast Mercenary Corps too began regrouping individually, and the state of the wounded gathered at the base of the great tree was a matter of concern —

"It's coming."

Wilhelm murmured quietly, gazing up at the sky. At the sight of the old swordsman narrowing his eyes and regripping the swords in both hands, everyone's wariness spiked to its absolute maximum at once. And, holding their breath, waiting to see what would happen — they would come to regret it.

They should have scattered immediately, without waiting to see what the White Whale floating overhead would do.

"—The fog's coming down on us!!"

Shouting at the top of his lungs, and now wordlessly, Rem wheeled the Earth Dragon around in one motion, pulling out of the battle line. The Earth Dragons and Ligers around them likewise broke into a run, but there was no longer any margin left to look up and check on anyone else's safety.

—A "fog of annihilation" swelling with enough force to blanket the entire sky was falling, aimed straight at them.

Vast as a falling cloud itself, there was no way to dodge it except to escape its range entirely. Using rocks or trees as shields was futile against destruction that would swallow them whole, shields and all. There was nothing to do but run, and pray they'd make it in time.

Even looking up was terrifying; there was only the pressure of a soundless end bearing down from directly above. Clinging desperately to the back of the Earth Dragon, keeping his posture low, sprinting to the absolute limit—

"Did we make it through!?"

A brightness spilled in, like emerging from beneath a black cloud, and Subaru, turning his head back, saw it. Above the ground being crushed beneath the fog, there were several silhouettes that hadn't made it in time, being swallowed.

Desperately, faces carved with fear and fury, people were swallowed by the fog from the head down and vanished. Along with their Earth Dragons, they disappeared entirely; nothing remained after they fell to the ground and dispersed into mist. No memory, not even a name, would remain in anyone's mind. Only Subaru would remember their deaths.

"Guh... ah."

Subaru let out a small groan, and ahead of him, the group scattered and separated by the fog seemed distant. Their numbers, too, had clearly dropped considerably compared to before the previous assault. The subjugation force, of course, and the Beast Mercenary Corps as well — neither had come through unscathed.

At least let the main fighters be safe, Subaru thought, scanning the area, and—

"Wil...?"

He spotted Wilhelm, barely hooking one hand onto the back of his Earth Dragon, having escaped the fog's range. Subaru was about to call out to him at the sight — when he noticed.

—From beyond the thick fog, a Witchbeast with its great maw open was closing in on Wilhelm.

"Run——!!"

"Nu—!?"

Subaru's shout and Wilhelm noticing the threat closing in from behind came at almost the same instant. But it was already a reaction that came too late to matter.

Approaching without a sound, the White Whale's maw swallowed Wilhelm and his Earth Dragon whole, ground and all. Gouging into the earth, a full five-meter square of ground was torn away entirely, all of it now inside the White Whale's mouth.

"Ah..."

Faced with that shocking sight, not just Subaru but even Rem was struck speechless with shock. Knowing the old man's tenacity as they did, the sense of loss was nothing ordinary. And above all, with the loss of a primary combatant, the situation only continued spiraling toward the worse —

"No good!!"

Then, close at hand, another voice rose up. Before Subaru could even react to that voice, their Earth Dragon was sent flying, struck from the side by a Liger charging in with tremendous force.

"Whoaaa!?"

Letting out a pained cry, the Earth Dragon and Subaru went tumbling to the ground together, bruised in several places, wincing at the pain. The perpetrator of this sudden violent act was, judging by the voice, Ricardo — but before Subaru could question his intent—

"——"

Subaru saw a burst of red bloom right in front of his eyes.

"Eh?"

The Liger's mangled corpse, torn to shreds, flesh scattered everywhere, rolled across the plain. And the large Beastman who should have been riding astride it had vanished, leaving behind only a great spray of fresh blood. The White Whale, its tail drenched in Ricardo's blood, swam through the air, its massive body swaying.

Had he been protected? What had become of Ricardo?

Various questions surfaced in his mind, but a fact too undeniable to ignore pressed in on Subaru even more urgently. The White Whale swimming right in front of him, the one that had swept Ricardo away with its tail. And then—

"No... way..."

Turning back, he saw the White Whale that had swallowed Wilhelm, ground and all, beginning to chew. Ahead of him, and behind him — and even overhead, looking up, there remained a fish-shaped shadow still darkening the sky.

—Three White Whales, trembling every mouth across their bodies, let out cackling laughter, stirring up despair.

Bit by bit, bit by bit, Subaru felt despair tightening around his heart once again.
