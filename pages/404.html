Arc 6 Chapter 2: "His Own Kind of Hospitality"

It's a bit late to mention this, but Roswaal Manor, the Emilia Camp's home base, is no longer the burned-down mansion of before — its location has since shifted to the main residence of the Mathers domain.

That said, the floor plan of the mansion is unchanged from before: the main building at the center, flanked on either side by the familiar west and east wings. Since the size of the mansion itself is also almost identical, walking around inside rarely causes any confusion.

The only trouble is that, precisely because it's a little too similar, it's easy to forget that Arlam Village isn't nearby anymore. Subaru himself, more than once, has tried to head to the village for morning radio calisthenics on pure half-asleep momentum, only to realize partway down the long road and be left standing there at a loss. Each time, Beatrice comes chasing after him in a fluster, and he gets thoroughly scolded for it.

In any case, having returned to Roswaal Manor, received the same warm welcome from Petra and Ram as always, they entered the parlor where the master of the house, Roswaal, was waiting —

"We-e-ell now, welcome home. So glad to see you both back safe and sou-ound."

Seated on the parlor sofa, Roswaal clapped his hands in welcome. Subaru and Emilia couldn't help but be taken aback by his beaming, delighted greeting. Seeing his oddly friendly smile, the two exchanged glances.

"...Emilia-tan, did you write something weird in your letter or something?"

"I—I don't know what you mean. Besides, didn't you send him some strange gift, Subaru? I mean, you and Roswaal do sometimes have those little whispered conversations..."

"No way! If I had the time and money to go out of my way to give Roz-cchi a gift, I'd spend it thinking up ways to repay my daily gratitude to Emilia-tan, Bea-ko, Petra, Frederica, Patrasche, and, well, I guess Ram too."

"That was almost entirely women's names, I suppose."

"Giving gifts to Otto or Garfiel would just be embarrassing, guy-to-guy!"

In response to a displeased Beatrice, Subaru laughed awkwardly to cover himself under Emilia's narrowed-eyed stare. Incidentally, leaving Rem out of the list was a deliberate choice, so as not to make everyone around him feel awkward — no offense intended. In any case, watching this exchange, Roswaal shrugged.

"Wha-a-at an unwelcome reaction. Is there something so strange about me worrying over your safety and rejoicing at your return unharmed? It's only natural, after all."

"That's true, but... Roswaal, you've never really shown us that side of yourself before..."

"Over the course of this year, I too have undergone various changes of thought, you might say. Surely my becoming more cooperative can only be a welcome thing for Emilia-sama, no?"

"Hmm, I suppose that's true."

At Roswaal's shameless declaration, Emilia — with her characteristic generosity — simply accepted the change. Watching him nod at her and wave his hand about, Subaru still couldn't quite bring himself to trust the man —

"Trying to understand what that one is thinking is pointless — he's too different from ordinary people to make sense of. Subaru... Subaru's also a bit odd, but that one's oddness is on another level, I suppose."

"Hey, that sentence had way too many vague pronouns in it."

Patting the head of Beatrice, who was talking like some kind of senile old woman with all her "that one, this one," Subaru followed Emilia into the parlor. Roswaal's good mood — suspicious as it was — Subaru had a rough idea of the reason behind it. In truth, Roswaal's real intentions were exactly as he'd just stated aloud.

Roswaal was genuinely delighted that Subaru and Emilia had returned safely. Now that the Book of Wisdom was lost, Subaru's actions — breaking through whatever obstacles stood in his way — were the single best means of reaching Roswaal's still-unspoken goal.

"If that's really how you feel, it'd help if you were a little more cooperative about it."

"Regardless of whether I lend a hand or not, you will accomplish your own goals. It is precisely because I believe that, that my cooperation lies in the areas beyond your reach. A truly equal relationship, wouldn't you sa-ay?"

"I feel like I'm the support character in an RPG who's useless the rest of the time — living out the cliché."

It's a classic trope: the support character who's too strong for the current point in the story only ever shows up for guest appearances. Roswaal's non-participation in combat was much the same sort of arrangement. In his case, the more cornered Subaru became, the stronger the evidence for his faith in the power of Return by Death to alter fate — so he'd no doubt keep gleefully watching Subaru scramble for a way out, more delighted than ever. He's still an impossible character to read, Subaru thought, wanting to click his tongue.

"—Would it be alright if you introduced us to your guests soon?"

"Oh, sorry to keep you waiting. Roswaal, we have guests. Budge up and sit down properly."

"I heard everything. Quite the interesting lineup, though."

At the voice from the girl left waiting in the hallway while the conversation went on at the doorway, Subaru offered a quick apology and waved Roswaal, still seated on the sofa, further back with a gesture. Roswaal rose and settled deeply into the seat of honor. Then, offering the guest sofa to their two visitors, Subaru and the others slowly took their seats across from them.

"Welcome, having come such a long way. This is the first time we've properly sat down face to face and spoken since the commendation ceremony, isn't it-t?"

"That's true. And even then, we didn't really have a proper talk, did we... In a sense, I'd say this is basically our first real conversation."

The commendation ceremony being referred to was the joint ceremony held for the Emilia Camp, Crusch Camp, and Anastasia Camp when the subjugation of the White Whale was officially recognized. It had been a small-scale ceremony held at the royal castle in the capital, but naturally the representatives of each camp had gathered there, meaning that every surviving member from immediately after the resolution of the Sanctuary incident had attended.

Otto's face pale with tension and Garfiel's eyes shining at the sight of the castle remained vivid memories, but Roswaal, showing up cool as anything after everything he'd pulled, was quite something too. Well, in terms of nervous fluster, Emilia had put on just as good a show, so it wasn't really Subaru's place to talk. From Emilia's perspective, she'd been praised for accomplishments she had no memory of earning, so it was understandable enough.

In any case, it was their first meeting since then, and greetings were exchanged in short order. Then the discussion moved straight to the main topic: the report on the city of Priestella —

"—So, as your letter said, Otto-kun and Garfiel were both wounded and had to withdraw. I-I see, understood. Truly, a commendable achievement."

Having finished hearing the report, Roswaal let out that remark along with a sigh of admiration. At the content of it, Emilia raised her eyebrows and said, with a slightly sharper tone, "Now wait a minute—"

"Those two were badly hurt — hurt so badly they couldn't even come back with us. And you call that commendable?"

"Please, do-o-on't jump to conclusions. Their injuries are certainly a tragic thing, and being forced to withdraw for a while is undoubtedly a blow. But on the other hand, no one in the camp suffered a fatal loss. Given that you encountered multiple Sin Archbishops, including alongside other camps, and still ended up with this result — it's truly a miraculous stroke of fortune."

"——"

"Of course, the wounds left by the Sin Archbishops are matters requiring urgent attention. But Emilia-sama and the others who fought to defend the city acted in the best way possible, keeping the damage to a minimum. That's something you may fairly acknowledge for yourselves, at least a little, wouldn't you sa-ay?"

"...Right. Thanks."

To the glib-tongued Roswaal, Emilia offered thanks with an unconvinced expression. As a matter of fact, his words carried a strong ring of truth — but the impression left by the speaker mattered a great deal. It had sounded suspicious to Subaru too, so it probably had to everyone.

"We-e-ell, how you all feel about me is beside the point for now, so let's move the conversation forward. — Incidentally, it was Anastasia-sama, was it not, who invited you all to the city of Priestella, where such a disturbance then occurred... How do you feel about that point?"

"I do feel bad about draggin' you all right into the middle o' the trouble. If you want me to apologize, I'm ready to do it properly, mind."

"And so, with a sincere bow of the head, you needn't pay a single coin in compensation... Ah, no need to apologize to me. Emilia-sama has already declined such an apology herself, has she not?"

"Well, if the Witch's Cult caused trouble, then the Witch's Cult is the one at fault, right? Anastasia only invited us — there's no way she should be held responsible. Besides, in our case, we accomplished what we went to Priestella for in the first place..."

Glanced at sidelong by Roswaal, Emilia touched the pendant at her throat. Set within it now was a new great demon crystal, within which the great spirit still slept on and on, quietly awaiting the moment of her revival. Obtaining a high-purity crystal stone to serve as Puck's Anchor — that had been the objective, so the camp's goal had indeed been achieved.

"Besides, maybe it was only because we happened to be there that we were able to drive the Witch's Cult off. If you think about it that way, it might actually have been to Anastasia's credit, more than anything—"

"For you to go that far in your thinking would mean showing too much favoritism toward another camp, so I'll stop you the-ere."

Somehow, Emilia's forward-thinking optimism ended up being what saved Anastasia. Still, Roswaal's probing just now had only been a bit of testing pressure on his part. In truth, the greater part of the Witch's Cult's objective may well have been something Subaru and the others had themselves carried into Priestella. If that point were pressed, then no matter how undeniably at fault the Witch's Cult was, the mood would grow heavy. So it was best for everyone to unite and firmly agree: the Witch's Cult is the absolute worst!

"On that front, the other three camps are already in agreement with us. One Sin Archbishop dead, one captured... the Witch's Cult must be in shambles by now."

"As for their organizational strength, that remains in question. That lot has never had anything to do with words like cooperation or unity. Even if every last ally vanished and only one of them remained, the madness that defines a madman would never lift."

"...On that much, I agree with you."

Roswaal's view aligned with Subaru's own, and it was likely correct. The organization called the Witch's Cult was, by now, less an organization than a gathering-house for lunatics. Even if it ceased to exist, they lacked the decency to simply lie low and behave. The remaining outrages of "Gluttony" and "Lust" would surely never cease either.

"That's exactly why we need a countermeasure. And that's what today's talk is about."

"...Contact with the 'Sage,' Shaula, is it-t..."

"I know it'll probably be difficult. But since we can't find any other means, we've got no choice but to try what we can try. If there's supposedly a sage who's all-knowing and all... well, all-knowing at least, then it can't hurt to at least go hear what she has to say, right?"

"That's exactly what led any number of people, ove-er the centuries, to pay with their lives and suffer a very great loss indeed, when they tried to do just tha-at."

Stated as common knowledge like that, it was, admittedly, a hard argument to push back against. But they did have a proper hidden card up their sleeve for this. At Subaru's glance, Anastasia gave a magnanimous nod.

"And that's where we come in. Lucky for you all, we've got a means of reachin' that 'Sage's' watchtower safe and sound. So there's no need to worry about gettin' lost along the way. Feel better now?"

"Being told to simply take your word for it is a bit much, isn't it-t. If such a means truly exists, there would be no shortage of people willing to pay a fortune for it. Why is it only you who possesses it?"

"I'm a merchant, and o' course money matters to me. — But there are things that can't be bought with money either. This here's one of those things, you could say."

Placing a hand over her slender chest, Anastasia — Eridna — declared this with brazen confidence. Despite impersonating Anastasia, there was, strangely, real force behind that one line. Even Subaru, who knew she wasn't truly herself right now, felt somehow overawed by it.

Met with Anastasia's direct gaze, Roswaal closed one eye. Then, surveying her for a moment with only that yellow-eyed stare, he said,

"We-e-ell, well. This is why people who've weathered mortal combat and the cutthroat marketplace alike are so difficult to deal with. And this, too, I suppose Emilia-sama has already consented to-o?"

"I know I decided this on my own, and I do feel bad about that, okay?"

"But not bad enough to stop deciding things on your own. Very well, that's fine. You are the sort who deliberately chooses the thorny path. And it's precisely there that he will follow you."

Roswaal's tone was reluctant, but in the end, his interest always came back around to that very point. The harder the path Emilia chose, the higher the hurdles Subaru would have to clear. For Roswaal, that had become, by now, the substitute hope in place of the lost Book of Wisdom.

"Anyway, with Anastasia guiding us, we'll cross that whatchamacallit desert. Might take a few more days, though."

"Not 'whatchamacallit desert' — the Augria Sand Dunes. You've been corrected on this more than once; perhaps it's time you finally learned it."

At Subaru's fuzzy recollection, Julius, sighing, cut in. Seated on the sofa beside Anastasia, having silently watched over the discussion until now, he turned his intelligent gaze toward Roswaal.

"For you, Roswaal-sama, I imagine this is a deeply unwelcome request. But even now, in the city of Priestella, there remain countless people, exposed to the Witch's Cult's violence, wounded in both body and spirit. If our actions might serve as some small aid in healing their hearts, I would ask that you please permit this."

"Quite an elega-ant turn of phrase. From what I can see, you're a man of no small ability... and yet, it troubles me that I have no memory of you. That means, I take it, that is exactly the case?"

"——"

From the mere fact that he had no memory of Julius, Roswaal understood the circumstances at once. As Julius lowered his eyes faintly, Roswaal rested his elbow on the armrest and propped his cheek on his hand. Then, his lips curling into something almost pleased, he said,

"The frustration of being forgotten by everyone through the Authority of 'Gluttony,' left behind by the world. You seek out the 'Sage' Shaula, pursuing a slender hope, in order to overturn that very situation you find yourself trapped in. You need not, you know, make excuses about doing it for someone else's sa-ake."

"—! I would never act out of such self-interest!"

"I'm not accusing you of anything. If anything, it's only natural, and I find it the more preferable motive, myself. In most things, people fight far more desperately for their own sake than for another's. Even if others end up saved as a result, there's no need to deny the part of your heart that finds satisfaction, accomplishment, or even a sense of superiority in the process of being saved yourself."

To a stiff-cheeked Julius, Roswaal went on and on. Behind his clown's makeup, his smile deepened further still.

"And besides, as things stand, if you are saved, there's a high likelihood others will be saved along with you. Bearing a noble cause while acting on it — surely that leaves not the slightest twinge of guilt, does it-t?"

"I..."

"—Margrave Mathers, that'll be about enough, if you don't mind."

Raising a hand to stop the faltering Julius, Anastasia stepped in to face Roswaal in his stead. She smiled softly and tilted her head with an air of charm. And then,

"Truth be told, I don't have any memory of it either... but even so, this boy here seems to be my knight. Seein' him bullied over somethin' he can't help — that don't sit well with me."

"So the bond between lord and knight lives on, even once lost from memory... is that it?"

"Who knows, really — even I don't fully understand that part myself. But it felt only natural, and not at all unwelcome, havin' Julius look out for me along the way... and besides that—"

Anastasia gently lifted a finger and pointed it across at the sofa opposite.

"I figure I've gone and stopped that camp o' yours from crackin' apart, from you carryin' on with your needless mischief for too long, don't you think?"

"—Well, well."

Roswaal shrugged at Anastasia's pointed remark — and at the sight of Subaru, on the verge of erupting. That Subaru was about to shout meant, naturally, that Emilia and Beatrice were in the same state. Seeing this, Roswaal raised his hands as if surrendering.

"Yes, yes, I-I was in the wro-ong. Though I was only pointing out that such an aspect exists as well."

"An unnecessary point to make. Seriously, cut it out."

"Is that so-o? Regardless of how much or little a person benefits from a given outcome, there will always be those who harbor such prejudices — who make unkind assumptions and grumble spitefully. Since it seemed you weren't aware of that, I thought I'd simply offer the warning in advance."

Not for someone else's sake, but for your own. Having his actions cast in that light was, in fact, an old wound Subaru carried within himself, one that still ached.

Once, at the royal castle, Subaru had told Emilia that he was acting for her sake, and she had thrown back at him that it was really for his own. That pain from back then was hard to forget, and, in the end, it had probably been true. But whether or not it was true, there would always be people who said such things with the intent to wound. And yet, even so—

"That doesn't mean the number of people who see the true meaning of what was done shrinks. Is this guy really that kind of person? Cut it out."

"It can't be helped, can it-t. Unlike you, I don't know his character. ...Though even you don't seem like someone who's merely traveled together for a few days."

At the fired-up Subaru, Roswaal lowered his gaze. His eyes, framed by long lashes, filled — just for that one moment — with a genuine sorrow, and he said,

"So once again, it's only you who remembers. Just like with Rem, isn't it."

"Whatever twist of fate that is, I dunno."

"That is proof of how special you are. Treasure it — treasure it well. There are, after all, plenty of people who want such a thing and cannot have it."

The latter part of that murmur was barely audible to anyone but Roswaal himself. Still, Subaru quickly gave up on pressing him further, certain he'd never reveal what he meant no matter how hard he was pressed. In any case, what needed to be conveyed had been conveyed. As for the rest—

"Just like I said in the letter. The locked chamber, and—"

"You'll be taking out the 'Sleeping Beauty,' was it. Quite the bold move. And here I thought you hated even being near that girl."

"There might be a way to figure out how to wake her up. So I'm gonna try it. Obviously, right?"

"What surprised me was that you'd try that method on her first of all. You like to play the egoist, but underneath it all, you're self-punishing. Somewhere in your heart, in some corner of your mind — don't you think, perhaps, that you shouldn't be the first one to be saved?"

"——"

Struck right where it hurt, Subaru fell silent without meaning to. In the lead-up to this expedition toward the Pleiades Watchtower, right up until they arrived at the mansion, Subaru had continued agonizing over whether to bring Rem along as a case study of a "Gluttony" victim. That was, of course, not out of any aversion to the idea of waking Rem herself. If there was any chance of Rem waking, he wanted her to wake up as soon as possible — even a day, even a single second sooner.

But whether Natsuki Subaru himself deserved to be saved by it was another matter entirely. In the city of Priestella, there were so very many people who had suffered the very same fate as Rem. Their families didn't even know they'd lost them; the sleeping victims themselves had been robbed even of the right to be worried over. The more he thought about it, the more some strange wedge drove itself into Subaru's heart.

The guilt of being saved ahead of so many others. And so, truly, right up until the very last moment this time too, Subaru had hesitated to bring Rem along —

"That's something I properly talked Subaru into. So there's no problem."

"...That's even more surprising."

In place of the silent Subaru, it was Emilia who spoke to Roswaal. Roswaal, looking dubious, closed one eye at the proudly straight-backed Emilia.

"Forgive me for saying so, but wouldn't her awakening be rather inconvenient for you, Emilia-sama? However you look at it, Subaru-kun clearly holds deep feelings for that girl — feelings that might, depending on how things go, rival even his feelings for you, Emilia-sama—"

"That's true, I think that's true. If Rem wakes up, Subaru will probably be preoccupied with her for a while, and maybe he won't care about me anymore."

"No, I mean, that's really—"

Not going to happen — that was for certain. His feelings for Emilia had never once wavered, and he could vow they never would. But separately from that, it was also a fact that he held strong feelings for Rem. And, just as Emilia said, if Rem woke up, there was no question he'd be preoccupied with her.

But to that very Subaru, Emilia simply said, "It's fine," and continued,

"If that means Subaru turns away from me for a while, then I'll just have to work hard to get him looking my way again. It's way too late for me to be in trouble without Subaru around. So no matter how cute and absorbed he gets with Rem, I'm still going to make him come back to me too."

"E—Emilia-tan!?"

"That's my resolve, and my decision. Besides, no one's going to complain about Subaru being saved. — So it's fine. Let's wake Rem up."

With firm words, Emilia pushed Subaru's decision forward. At the almost confession-like ring of those words, it was Subaru who was left startled, his knees trembling. There had been a few times before when Emilia had said something that sounded like affection. There had, but every one of those had never gone beyond the bounds of mere fondness—

"Me, Rem, Beatrice, Petra, Patrasche, and Otto-kun and Garfiel! Subaru is so, so lucky, and that's just how it is."

"There was an Earth Dragon and a couple of guys mixed into the back half of that list, though?"

Also, there'd been a loli mixed in the middle there too. In any case, in response to all this, Subaru got jabbed in the side by Beatrice, seated beside him. When Subaru let out an "Ouch" and glanced over, Beatrice, wearing her usual sullen expression, said,

"It's not like Ram — I mean, it's not like Beatrice has any complaint to make about Subaru's flirtatiousness at this point, I suppose. ...But one hand should always stay free. That's a privilege that belongs only to Betty, I suppose."

"...You're stupidly cute, you know that?"

"Naturally, I suppose. Betty's cuteness resounds to the very heavens and earth, I suppose."

Whatever the gods of heaven and earth thought of it, Subaru couldn't say, but it certainly resounded through his heart. Pushed forward by both Emilia and Beatrice, Subaru committed himself to Rem's awakening. — Now, at least, there was no hesitation left in doing so.

"So that's how it is, Roswaal. Sorry for being so loved. I'm taking Rem with me."

"Both Emilia-sama and Beatrice — I find myself continually surprised by how much you two have changed. Very we-ell, do as you like. I never intended to stop you in the first place."

"Then what was all that back-and-forth just now for?"

"Merely confirming, out of an old man's excessive caution, whether you truly understood the meaning of your actions. My apologies as well, to our nameless knight over there-e."

"Not at all."

To the very last, Roswaal's manner remained mocking. In response, Julius drew a small breath, then looked back and forth between Subaru and Anastasia in turn, and said,

"I am Julius Juukulius. At present, I exist only within his memory, but I am — was — a Knight of the Royal Guard of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica. I am not so immature as to be shaken by a matter of this magnitude."

Declaring this firmly, he brushed off the wicked mage's wicked provocation with admirable poise.

"...Though you were shaking like a leaf."

"Whose side are you even on? I feel like I've just been stabbed in the back."

And so, this exchange at the very end — I'll just quietly add that it happened, too.

With the discussion over, Subaru and the others filed out of the parlor. In preparation for the coming push toward the Augria Sand Dunes, they were headed to the "locked chamber" in the mansion's east wing, to make contact with a figure who might hold an important key. There, a presence had been kept confined, chained up ever since roughly a year ago, brought there at the same time the residents relocated to this mansion after the previous one burned down. It was not, by rights, a relationship in which contact was advisable — nor a safe one, either —

"What sort of result will they bring back with them, I wonder. Being led to feel expectation over something like that — I suppose that means even I-I have been influenced by it all."

Letting his weight sink deep into the sofa's backrest, Roswaal murmured this to himself. Left alone in the parlor, the mage, savoring the aftertaste of the discussion just past, let out a small chuckle — whoever it was that had come to mind —

"I wonder if this means I, too, have joined the ranks of those favorably disposed toward him. What do you think?"

"...I suppose you're about to say something unpleasant. In your case, since you actually could go through with it in earnest, I suppose there's no word for it but frightening."

"Even I'm not so lacking in restraint as to make advances in this body, I-I assure you."

"You have been a woman before, I suppose. I'll keep my guard up plenty, thank you."

As Roswaal laughed with ever greater delight, the girl who'd answered him — Beatrice — kept her face primly composed, tugging idly at one of her ringlets. It was a habit of hers, either from having nothing to occupy her hands, or from irritation.

"Subaru-kun and the others are headed to the locked chamber, aren't they? Shouldn't you be at his side? Isn't it dangerous otherwise-e?"

"A stray dog with its fangs pulled is far less trouble than a house dog polishing its own, I suppose. So I thought I'd go ahead and drive the point home properly."

"'House dog' — how cruel a term. Though if it were 'the Teacher's,' I'd have no objection."

"An irredeemable pervert, I suppose. ...Roswaal, don't provoke Subaru too much, I suppose. Betty won't allow any needless stimulation."

At Roswaal's faintly lascivious smile, Beatrice, her expression souring, said this abruptly. Roswaal, in turn, withdrew his smile and, closing one eye — the blue one, as usual — gazed back at Beatrice with only his yellow-eyed stare.

"You and I stand in different positions. Unlike you, so quick to give your heart away, I am singularly devoted."

"And seeing as you haven't so much as grazed Mother's notice for it, I suppose that means Betty — who gets to hold Subaru's hand — is hundreds of times better off, which is why it doesn't even irritate me, I suppose."

"You've grown quite bold in your speech. You, playing at being the librarian of the Forbidden Archive — do try, at the very least, to be happy."

"Naturally, I suppose. Which is why I intend to act to protect that happiness, too, I suppose."

Roswaal, still seated on the sofa, and Beatrice, standing before him, met each other's gaze at the same eye level, sparks all but flying between them. As if trying to peer into the round-eyed girl's very heart, Roswaal narrowed his eyes and said,

"One Sin Archbishop has been defeated. That should mean a second one has now entered Subaru-kun."

"...There ought to be candidates besides Subaru, I suppose."

"But none of them come as close to him, nor overlap with him, as much. Between one who merely grazes and one who truly overlaps, which will be chosen is self-evident. Do spare me the tedious pretense."

"—I won't let it go any further than this, I suppose."

To Roswaal's words, Beatrice answered in a voice steeled with resolve. The girl gripped tightly at her own sleeve and glared up at Roswaal.

"Betty belongs to Subaru, I suppose. So Subaru will stay Subaru, I suppose."

"I have no intention of interfering with your efforts. As for me, the fewer there are remaining, the more it helps my own cause. That's all there is to it-t."

"—!"

With one last sharp glare at Roswaal, Beatrice turned toward the door. The lingering conversation was over now, too.

"Beatrice."

To the retreating girl's back, Roswaal called out. Beatrice stopped in her tracks. But she did not turn around.

"I do, you know, wish for your happiness — at least far more strongly than I do for any of the other faceless masses. You are... something like a little sister to me, after all."

"—Not a pleasant thing to hear, I suppose. And besides, it's nothing compared to how I feel about Mother, I suppose."

"Isn't that what love is, after all-l?"

There was no reply. Only the sound of the door opening, then closing, echoing quietly through the parlor. After that, no further sound reached the room.
