Arc 3 Chapter 76: "Backstage × Scheming × Pinky Promise"

—Time rewinds a little, to the war council held en route to the Mathers domain.

"We don't tell the villagers or Emilia-tan that the Witch Cult is closing in. As for the reason for the evacuation... I figure something vague, like bandits or brigands sneaking into the area nearby, should do."

"The grounds for concealing the truth... I suppose that goes without asking."

Hearing the opening of the plan Subaru proposed, Julius let out a sigh of understanding. Subaru could sense similar reactions coming back from the others connected through the Nect, and continued:

"Thanks for the world-weary reaction. ...Just as everyone's probably already guessed, if it comes out that the Witch Cult is behind this whole mess, there's a good chance the victims' resentment will turn on Emilia-tan. I didn't want to believe people could think that way either, but..."

That things wouldn't turn out the way Subaru hoped was something he knew all too well, having seen it with his own eyes. Even to those good-natured villagers, Emilia's lineage was no exception — nothing more than an accursed bloodline, one it was only natural to shun.

His outrage and defiance toward that twisted prejudice could go on without end no matter how much he spoke of it, but that wasn't something he could undo here and now. Subaru set aside, for the moment, the deep-seated irritation smoldering in his chest, calmed himself with a long breath, and said:

"'Bandits' — let's just go with that across the board. Some troublemakers who'd been stirring things up around the capital slipped in over here, and Crusch-san's camp, our allies, showed up to lend a hand. How's that?"

"It's trite, but not as full of holes as it might sound. The problem is whether Emilia-sama and the villagers will be fooled by it..."

"Honestly, between having this many people gathered and the merchant convoy that went ahead of us, I think that's plenty to divert attention from anything suspicious. Besides, I'm not planning to fool absolutely everyone. There's someone I have in mind who won't be misled on that front."

At Subaru's confident air, Julius closed one eye and answered, "Is that so." In his place, the one who fired off a question at Subaru's plan was—

"I get that you don't want Emilia-samanya to become a target of criticism, nya, but even so, that alone doesn't explain Subaru-kyun's idea from just now, nya, you know?"

"Doesn't make sense... what part?"

"The part where you're not planning on a touching reunion with Emilia-sama, Subaru-kyun."

The one who mercilessly jabbed at the part of this plan that troubled Subaru's heart the most — that was Ferris. Undeterred by Subaru's tongue-tied hesitation, he pressed the point further, as if to pin down the doubt:

"You sealed an alliance with Crusch-sama, helped subjugate the White Whale, and now, right at this very moment, you're bringing reinforcements back to defeat the outside threat menacing the domain — that's the stuff of some kind of heroic saga, nya. I heard you two parted on bad terms, but this sounds like more than enough to wash away any bad name, nya~"

"Listing it all out like that, it sounds like some kind of dumbly heroic streak. ...Well, considering the bill I actually racked up with what I did, I don't think anything less than that kind of feat would make up for it."

"Ferri-chan's just gonna go ahead and say maybe Subaru-kyun ought to look back on his own actions a little more objectively, nya~"

When Ferris grumbled that in response to Subaru's self-flagellating remark, a sense of agreement seemed to reach him from the others as well. Grateful as he was for their consideration, remembering the foolishness he'd committed at the time of his parting with Emilia left no room for his self-mockery to let up. That much folly, that much abuse hurled — how great a feat would it take to pile up before it could be made as though it never happened?

"There's no making it as though it never happened. That was — that was my true feeling, back then."

Acting selfishly, getting carried away, greedily demanding the heart of the girl he loved just as he pleased — that shameless self. Looking back on it now, he wanted to avert his eyes from its ugliness — but that, too, was him.

"That's exactly why, if I'm honest, what I really want is to bring back some kind of achievement and apologize for everything that happened back then, but..."

"You can't do that, nya?"

"If she found out I was here... no, if she found out I was staying in the village to fight the outside threat, she's not the kind of girl who'd meekly run off and leave it at that. Nine times out of ten, she'd stay and fight alongside me. And even if she didn't, she'd turn back partway. That's the kind of girl she is."

"But you two are on the outs, aren't you? Even so..."

"If she were the kind of girl who could leave someone she'd had a falling-out with behind in a place of death and just sigh with relief that it was over and done with... I wouldn't have fallen for her this hard in the first place."

His failure last time around came down mostly to two things: that even though he'd anticipated Petelgeuse's counterattack, he'd been careless about guarding against just how vicious it would be, and that he'd let Emilia and the madman meet, when he should have known better than to let that happen at all.

As for the former, working through a handful of deductions had brought a strategy into view — ironically enough, thanks to having died last time. And as for the latter—

"Not letting her realize the outside threat is the Witch Cult, and that it's after her. Not letting her know that someone connected to her — meaning me — is with the subjugation force fighting the threat. These are the essential conditions for keeping Emilia-tan away from the battlefield. I'll say it flat out."

"Do you really have to be that cautious about it, nya? I think Emilia-sama wouldn't be a drag on the subjugation force even if she joined in, nya. She's got that Great Spirit-sama with her too, after all..."

"Thinking about Crusch-san's example, having her personally lead the charge would be the best way to build up Emilia-tan's reputation, I know... but I'll pass on that this time. I can't let Emilia-tan face a Trial by the Witch Cult — by a Sin Archbishop."

At Subaru's resolute declaration, Ferris gave a shrug and a look of acceptance. Taking that as his cue, Subaru glanced around to see if there were any other questions, and, confirming that no one raised any doubts, let out a sigh of relief.

"For talking the villagers around, it'll probably put them more at ease if it's me and... well, if they know it's Crusch-san's army backing us up. I'd like Ferris and Wilhelm-san to help out with that."

"Understood." "Rooogerrr!"

Listening to the two voices of assent, Subaru pulled a white robe out of his luggage and pulled it on, though its sleeves and length left him a bit uneasy. Made a size large for its original owner, it covered Subaru's body without any trouble, and—

"The faint scent of Emilia-tan lingering on it is boosting my motivation—!"

"I'll pretend I didn't just hear that... but from the looks of it, that robe seems to carry quite an unusual enchantment."

"Well, I'll tell you why — it's the miracle cure that's been protecting me from Emiliase deficiency these past few days. On sleepless nights, I've secretly pressed it against my face to work off all my pent-up feelings... hey, what's with that look? Can't help it! Getting all worked up over the belongings of the girl you like is a road everyone walks down!"

Subaru felt, whether it was his imagination or not, that Julius's Earth Dragon running alongside him had put a little more distance between them, and it irritated him. Julius let out an exasperated sigh at Subaru's reaction, and prefacing with "Now then," said:

"To return to the subject... that robe? The moment you put it on, it seemed as though your presence grew faint."

"Ah, apparently it's woven with some kind of 'Perception Obstruction' enchantment or whatever. It's one of Roswaal's handmade convenience items... originally it belonged to Emilia-tan. It just so happens, purely by chance, to be in my hands right now. I didn't steal it, okay?"

"I'll press you on its origins some other time, but for now... ah, I see how it is."

Midway through responding to Subaru's answer, Julius gave a small nod of understanding, dipping his chin. There was something unpleasant about the gesture, as though his thoughts had been read outright, and Subaru narrowed his already-sour eyes further and glared at the handsome knight. "What." But the knight in question, cool as ever, said:

"I suppose I've simply come to understand anew what a self-defeating disposition you have. Frankly, it isn't rational at all. ...You're the exact opposite of Anastasia-sama, you know."

"I intend to get absolutely everything I want in the end, so if anything, there's hardly anyone greedier than me. Every bit of praise for Emilia-tan, all of it, belongs to me, whole and entire. Now I'm fired up!"

At Subaru gripping the reins and grinning with bared teeth, Julius let out a sigh — he'd lost count of how many he'd given already — and, facing forward, murmured in a voice no one could hear:

"Every now and then, like right now, there are moments I find myself cursing the fact that I'm a knight."

—or so he muttered.

—Entering the Mathers domain and immediately wiping out the Witch Cult, whose location was already known — that had been the policy up until now. But this time, they couldn't afford to kick off with a shootout against the Witch Cult and put the enemy's guard up. They had to make their way down the highway keeping as low a profile as possible, cut through the forest track into the village, and persuade the villagers about the evacuation.

Honestly, at this point, being ambushed from the side by Witch Cultists would have been a fatal turn of events for the flow of this operation. Once they came to blows with the Witch Cult even once, there would be nothing left to do but wash away every carefully laid plan. That said, throughout the march across the domain — undertaken with the greatest caution he'd ever mustered — no such crude interruption fortunately occurred, and—

"Safely arrived, and... whoa, whoa, the tension here is thick."

By the time the subjugation force reached the village entrance, the atmosphere there was already thick with suspicion and unease. They should have made it straight to the village at quite a clip, but judging by the cluster of Dragon Carriages parked at the edge of the village, the merchants had probably arrived sometime around midnight the day before.

Considering that Subaru and the others had fought the White Whale right in the middle of the highway and only set off after resting up, it was only natural that the merchants who'd gone on ahead — even having taken a detour — would gain several hours' advantage. In other words—

"The blank letter of introduction had already been delivered to the mansion by now..."

Seeing that letter, Emilia, on the receiving end, must have been thrown into no small confusion — and the person standing at her side who'd surely seen the same document—

"First a merchant brings a blank letter of introduction, and now an armed group shows up... It seems whoever this is doesn't understand whose domain this is."

With her hands on her hips and an insolent expression, she looked up at Subaru and the others tromping into the village and greeted them with open hostility. A girl with short-cropped pink hair, her small frame wrapped in a servant's uniform, with an adorable face — though Subaru knew that beneath that cat-like cuteness lurked a sharp-edged, cunning personality.

"So, what business do you people have with this village? This commotion... I doubt it's unrelated to the merchants who suddenly showed up in the middle of the night."

"I figure there's a lot of crossed wires behind this, but don't glare at me with that much hostility. It's been a while since we've seen each other — feel free to greet me with a smile and a little tap dance, you know?"

"Such a self-serving thing to say... nonsense I recognize all too well."

To the sharp-edged look, Subaru responded with a playful gesture from atop Patrasche. At that reply, the girl — Ram — raised an eyebrow as though she'd noticed something. Staring intently, she fixed her eyes on Subaru wrapped in the white robe, and—

"...Barusu?"

"Y-yep, correct. Man, but seriously, this robe is impressive. Even talking with someone you know, they have to eye you suspiciously and stay half-convinced before figuring it out. That's downright uncanny."

Flipping the hood back to bare his face, Subaru let out a breath of admiration. Then he twisted his body and swung down smartly from atop the Earth Dragon.

"Anyway, let's talk about the situ— guh."

His landing failed. A dull crack sounded from his ankle, and sharp pain pierced straight through his brain. He crouched down on the spot, half in tears, and—

"Talk— let's talk, talk this over...! Let me explain just what's behind this whole picture here...!"

"The way you never quite manage to look composed — that's so very Barusu. I heard you'd fallen out of favor and been left behind in the capital... what face did you put on to come crawling back with?"

"You're seriously merciless, you know that!? I can't really argue back, but yeah, I crawled back here with this same face, shamelessly! Though I didn't come back empty-handed."

Rising with enough momentum to forget the pain in his ankle, Subaru waved his hands in front of Ram, showing off that he was alive and well. And as Subaru and Ram stood face to face, talking—

"—Hey, the one at the head of that group." "Isn't that Subaru-sama talking with Ram-sama?" "Ah, it's Subaru! Did he come back?"

The villagers, who up until then had been watching the situation unfold with anxious expressions, leaving negotiations entirely to Ram, now began pointing this way and talking all at once. No doubt Subaru's appearance had upgraded the unknown, mysterious group into "a mysterious group led by someone familiar."

"So, what business brought you back with this many people in tow? If this is meant as retaliation, Ram, as the one entrusted with holding down the fort, will have no choice but to fight you off to the last."

"Am I really thought of as that ungrateful!? If I do something wrong, of course I deserve to get scolded for it. Holding a grudge over that or sulking about it... well, I did do some of that, but."

"I knew it. Ram will take Barusu hostage and force a path through to the mansion."

"That's way too quick a conclusion! And believe me, I'm not holding a grudge or sulking right now!"

Warding off Ram, who'd drawn a small staff and looked liable to go after his neck in dead earnest, Subaru held out his palm, turned upward, and said:

"Retaliation is completely out of the question, and I didn't drag this many people along just for a prank either. ...Did you get the letter of introduction from the merchant?"

"...If a blank sheet can be called a letter of introduction, then yes, Ram received it. Though Ram took it to mean something more like a declaration of war."

"Were you always this hot-blooded? Don't take everything so aggressively — eat some small fish, get some calcium in you, and calm down a little."

Subaru gave a wry smile at Ram snapping back at every little thing, then explained that the blank letter had been the result of a mix-up. Ram nodded, though reluctantly, and said:

"Well then, setting aside the contents of the letter, I suppose I can trust it was legitimate in form. In that case, what was the original content supposed to be?"

"Why, of course, it concerns the very reason I stayed behind in the capital in the first place — the sealing of an alliance between Emilia-tan's camp and Crusch-san's."

Flaring his nostrils at Ram's question, Subaru gestured grandly with his arm at the ranks of the subjugation force lined up behind him. As if his gesture were a cue, two figures stepped forward from within the group. Ferris and Wilhelm. They came up alongside Subaru, and, each in their own manner, offered Ram a greeting:

"I am Wilhelm Trias, dispatched on this expedition as the representative of His Grace Duke Crusch Karsten."

"Likewise, I'm Ferris, Crusch-sama's first knight~ The old man leading the group behind me is Vil-jii, and Feri-chan's more like the vice-captain, nya? Nice to meeeet you~"

In contrast to Wilhelm's solemnity, Ferris's greeting was frivolous through and through. Faced with these two contrasting greetings, Ram blinked, looking taken aback in a way rare for her, and said:

"Thank you both for such courteous greetings. I am Ram. I have the honor of serving as head steward at the mansion of Margrave Roswaal L Mathers."

Switching her demeanor in an instant, she pinched the hem of her skirt and gave a courteous bow. Watching just this gesture, one could catch a glimpse of a truly flawless maid's education. Mind you, once she actually got down to work, her abilities as a servant left plenty to be desired.

"The reason I brought you two and the others along this time is no different from what you already suspect. Ram's probably already noticed... it's reinforcements to drive off the suspicious lot lurking in the forest."

"...And where did Barusu hear about that?"

"It's actually kind of a famous story in the capital, you know? A margrave's domain, snubbed by everyone around after he came out in support of a Half-Elf — no better place for up-and-coming bandits to pick as a temporary hideout, they say."

Lowering his voice, Subaru answered Ram's words with a bald-faced lie, cool as anything. At his reply, Ram narrowed her eyes thoughtfully, and Ferris and Wilhelm, who'd been listening, each shifted the lines of their faces in their own way. But—

"That's the official line, anyway."

"Huh?"

"Keep your voice down — don't go raising it in surprise, I don't want anyone else to hear this. ...What's in the forest is the Witch Cult. Their target's Emilia, and they mean to slaughter everyone in the village too."

—!

Leaning in close, Subaru told Ram the truth in a hushed voice. At that fact, Ram's eyes widened just slightly, and her breath caught as though her throat had closed up. Then—

"H-hold on, hold on, hold on, what's going on here? This isn't what we agreed, nya. Weren't we supposed to not tell anyone and go with the bandit story, nya?"

To Ferris, who cut in looking startled at the sudden change of plan, Subaru shrugged and shook his head, saying, "Hey, hey."

"There's no way a trick that flimsy is going to fool everybody. I meant to tell Ram the truth from the start. If there's no one on the evacuating side who's in the loop about our situation, we won't be able to handle it if something unexpected comes up."

"—And she can be trusted, I take it?"

This time it was Wilhelm who asked, with a sharp gaze, as Subaru spoke of his true reasons for revealing the truth. Subaru took the Sword Devil's piercing look head-on and nodded confidently. "Yeah."

"There's no one better suited to be in on this with us than Ram. After all, if our story doesn't hold together, Emilia-tan ends up in trouble... and that's the same thing as being bad for Roswaal. You wouldn't get that judgment call wrong, would you?"

With words that tested her and a provocative smile, Subaru turned back to Ram. At his words, Ram closed one eye, and, letting her lips curl slightly, said:

"My, my, Barusu. In the little while I haven't seen you, your head's gotten quite a bit cleverer at scheming."

"I learned the hard way, body and all, that if you stop thinking, nothing good comes of it. So, what's the actual answer here?"

As Subaru raised his hand and wagged a finger at its tip, Ram let out one small, long breath, then, as if resigning herself, slowly shook her head and said:

"—If it serves Roswaal-sama's benefit, Ram will do anything, cooperate with anything. Satisfied with that answer, Barusu?"

"Yeah, perfect. You're a model servant, Ram."

For the sake of the master she truly served, Ram accepted a compact to deceive her nominal one. Subaru grinned at her with the face of a mischievous kid setting up a scheme — no, a prank.

"I'd already thought as much for a while now, but it seems Barusu really is a fool."

—That was Ram's first remark after Subaru had laid out the entire outline of the operation and she'd finished listening.

She'd interjected questions here and there along the way, but after hearing Subaru's plan through to the end, she showed understanding and agreement — and tacked on that remark at the very last.

"Praise me that much and I'll get embarrassed."

"Keep telling yourself that. ...Are you truly certain about not telling Emilia-sama anything?"

"Liiike I saaaid! Would everybody quit dragging up my lingering feelings already. I'm fine, no problem, no worries! If anything, isn't the whole 'swoop in unnoticed and save the girl I care about from a crisis' situation kind of super thrilling?"

At Subaru, who wouldn't drop his flippant attitude no matter what, Ram let out a sigh of resignation. Then she looked over at Wilhelm and Ferris and said:

"It's just like our apprentice servant — I sympathize with your troubles."

"His actions, words, and displays of emotion are a bit conspicuously outlandish at times, but Subaru-dono is a man of real promise. Even at my age, there's much I've learned from him."

"Feri-chan hasn't been won over quite as much as Vil-jii, nya, so maybe I'll just take Ram-chan's words at face value? Well, I do think he's gotten a little better, nya~"

Wilhelm's high praise, and Ferris's faint hint of softening. At their reactions, Ram looked at Subaru and said:

"Seems you've wormed your way in quite skillfully."

"You really do rate me abnormally low, you know that? It's fair, I guess, in a way, but it still gets me down."

And with that, the conversation with Ram came to a pause. With her accepting Subaru's plan without objection, that concern was now all but gone. What remained was—

"It's a little sudden after not seeing you all for a while, but can I get everyone's attention for a moment!"

Stepping out to the center of the village, Subaru waved his arms broadly to draw everyone's eyes. The merchants, looking ill at ease; the villagers, anxious amid a village full of strangers; the members of the subjugation force, ordered to stand by — all of them fixed their attention on Subaru at once. Even though it was exactly as he'd intended, having every eye gathered on him at once ratcheted up the tension. Subaru managed to push the pressure aside with a sense of duty, forced a smile, and said:

"Anyway, this whole commotion is on me. Seems like word had a hard time getting through to a lot of places properly. I'd like to sort it all out — that okay with everyone?"

Holding up a finger, Subaru posed the question to them all. Only silence came back in answer, and the feeling of being left out was severe. Getting zero reaction from this many people after calling out to them was mentally grinding. Back in his old world, dozing off during homeroom — he suddenly wanted to apologize to his homeroom teacher for how cruel that must have felt. Just as Subaru was sinking into that bout of nostalgia—

"—Let's hear what you have to say, then."

So said someone who, alone, stepped forward toward Subaru. None other than a familiar figure with a distinctive shock of white hair, emerging from among the villagers — commonly known as "Muraosa," an ordinary elder with no actual connection to the office of village chief. Muraosa, whose presence alone carried the weight of a representative, combed his fine chin whiskers with his fingers and said:

"We didn't understand the situation, and it had us uneasy. You mean to explain it to us... and coming from the village's benefactor no less, well, we can hardly refuse to listen."

"Benefactor of the village, that's a bit much. I didn't really..."

"Now that's far too much modesty, Subaru-sama. If you hadn't gone into the forest that night and brought the children back, who knows what would have happened. And everyone in the village knows about everything that came after, too."

A young man with a close-cropped haircut stepped forward and cut off Subaru, who'd started to give a self-deprecating reply to Muraosa's words. A member of the village youth association, he scratched roughly through his short hair with his fingers and said:

"You don't have to look so anxious — everyone's going to hear you out. We're just as indebted to you, Subaru-sama, as we are to Roswaal-sama."

"Don't go seeing through my little rabbit heart when I'm trying to act tough. ...I'm happy, but man, this is seriously embarrassing! Now I look like an idiot for putting on my cool face!"

Showered with unexpectedly warm words, Subaru, unable to bear the sensation of his ears turning red, clutched his head. He spun in place for a moment, cooling off. Meanwhile, the villagers gathered around him one after another, settling into position to listen. Naturally, they fell into a circle, just like during radio calisthenics, and—

"Whoa, whoa, drilling group formations really does come in handy for anything. I'd love to ride this momentum straight into radio calisthenics after so long, but we're short on time, so let's get right to it."

The sheer number of eyes on him was about the same as before, but the gazes directed at him now felt familiar. Treated with that friendly warmth, which felt exactly like the personalities of the people Subaru knew, joy welled up inside him. Last time around, he felt he'd brushed against the darker side of these people here, and he'd been anxious about whether he could carry off this explanation well — but,

"If there's a good side, there's a bad side too. They're only human, after all — of course there is."

Murmuring that quietly, Subaru closed his eyes and, gritting his teeth, let go of the unease he'd carried until then. Then, reclaiming his usual bold, ill-mannered brat's face, he stamped his foot on the spot and said:

"Alright then, lend me your ears for a bit, nice and quiet. It's a little bit of a shocking story for you all, living your peaceful, easygoing lives — but please, stay calm and hear me out. See, actually..."

—Slipping in a bit of stylish humor here and there along the way, Subaru relayed the fabricated "bandits have arrived" story to the villagers. He adjusted his volume so it would also reach the merchants, who seemed to be eavesdropping, and made it all the way through his explanation without letting anyone squeeze in a question, finishing with:

"So, that's the situation — with the lord away, I went ahead and took matters into my own hands. The subjugation force that came here is one of the capital's finest elite units, and on top of that, they belong to a duke's household! Hardcore professionals through and through, so you can trust them and rest easy... how about it?"

With a light, breezy delivery, he pitched reassurance like a salesman, and at the very last, tilted his head to gauge their reaction. He'd done his best to keep an optimistic air and avoid stoking any unease, but even so, given the nature of the matter, the villagers' agitation couldn't be hidden.

"Even if you tell us that all of a sudden, we've got livestock to look after..." "There's the fields to tend too. Couldn't we settle this without leaving the village?" "With this many people gathered, couldn't we just fortify the village's defenses so the bandits can't touch us?"

Opinions along those lines began to be heard here and there. Since every one of them had at least some point worth nodding along to, persuasion was no easy thing. Subaru began to open his mouth, hoping to somehow wrap things up with a bit of sophistry, when—

"It seems you're all under a misunderstanding, but you're not being given a choice here."

"H-hey—"

Overtaking Subaru, who'd been trying to coax and cajole his way toward some kind of compromise, Ram cut in with a voice so cold it verged on heartless. As the villagers looked up with a start, she crossed her arms, gave a small huff through her nose, and said:

"It seems that because Barusu is the one talking, it feels like a proposal to you — but this comes from a higher intent than that: an order from the lord himself, Roswaal-sama, acting through his will. Your safety, your livelihoods — all of that is secondary. Obey the lord's representative."

"That's no way to put it! I know you've got a blunt way of talking, but I can't just let that slide here. These people have their lives to—"

"Barusu is just as lacking in self-awareness here."

Ram pierced Subaru, who was about to press further, with a sharp gaze. Undaunted, but unable to gauge her intent, he knit his brows, and—

"Here and now, the only one who can claim to speak as Roswaal-sama's representative is Barusu, who was entrusted with the negotiations in the capital. As the lord's representative, fulfill your obligations to the people of this domain."

"Obligations, huh..."

"A lord has an obligation to protect the lives and peace of his people. If evacuating them to protect their safety is the lord's duty, then compensating for whatever harm comes with it is likewise the lord's duty."

Declaring it flatly, Ram looked up at Subaru and pursed her lips, as if to say don't make me spell it out this far. At her attitude and the substance of her words, Subaru scratched his head and shook it dramatically, letting out an "Uwaah!"

"You are seriously hard to read. What is it with you? Or is my level at reading a girl's heart just too pathetically low to even count? Man, this is such a pain."

"It's an outrage to think Ram could be understood so easily. Every part of Ram belongs to Roswaal-sama. Ram doesn't even want to be understood by the likes of Barusu."

A curt reaction paired with a remark utterly besotted with her master. Taken on its own, it made him think, yes, she really is Rem's sister, in ways that had nothing to do with looks. And with that, memories of exchanges with Rem came pointlessly flooding back, threatening to make him blush, so Subaru cleared his throat and turned back to face the villagers. To the anxious crowd watching the two of them argue right in front of them, Subaru gave a nod and said:

"Ah, sorry, Ram's way of putting it was harsh. But honestly, my opinion is the same as hers. Having everyone evacuate... that's already decided. I'd rather not go around giving orders and all that if I can help it, but as for whether staying here keeps you safe, it's dicey. My opinion is that we should put in whatever effort it takes to avoid that risk."

—

"Of course, I get everyone's side too. Livestock to look after, fields to tend — even if we say pack up and go all of a sudden, you probably can't properly prepare for that. So as for making up for it... it's not like I've got any real authority, but I've only got the one head to bow, so I'll bow it and beg Roswaal on your behalf. I'll do everything I can to make sure it doesn't come back to hurt any of you."

As he spoke, Subaru realized all over again just how short his own reach was. Feeling pathetic about it, he nonetheless resolved to show his sincerity properly, at the very least, and knelt down right there where everyone could see him. A murmur of surprise spread through the crowd, and even Ram's eyes went wide. Only Wilhelm and Ferris, standing by behind him, watched over Subaru's gesture with an air of quiet resignation.

"—Please, everyone, I know you've got plenty you want to say, but I'm asking you to swallow it down. And on top of that, just this one thing — let us protect all of you."

—

"Please. That's the whole reason I came back."

The villagers, falling silent, held their tongues as though something had lodged in their throats. And no wonder — Subaru, their benefactor, was pressing his own forehead to the ground and pleading to be allowed to protect them.

It had the roles turned completely upside down. To an outside eye, it would have looked like a scene that made no sense at all — but it was, without a doubt, a sight that was distinctly Subaru.

"—Ah, geez, what are we gonna do with you, Subaru-sama."

Subaru heard someone say that, as if the words had burst out of them. That set it off, and the urge to laugh spread through the crowd around him just like that, and—

"Well, if you're going to try that hard just to say you'll protect us, what choice do we have." "Oh, honestly. I don't know why, but my chest's gone warm for the first time in a good while." "Subaru-sama really is a hopeless one, isn't he."

Timidly, Subaru raised his face. The dazzling morning sunlight burned into his eyes from beyond the ring of people, and for just an instant his vision washed white. Shading it with his hand, he turned his eyes to the world that opened up before him, and—

"...Thanks, everyone."

"—That's our line, Subaru-sama."

Surrounded by troubled, smiling faces, Subaru laughed with an expression on the verge of tears.

※※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※

—And so, the persuasion of the villagers reached an amicable resolution, through the strangest of conversational turns in all the world: the ones meant to protect, bowing their heads to the ones being protected.

They gave the merchants the same explanation, with the "bandits" scenario, and directed everyone to split into two evacuation groups, one bound for the Sanctuary and one for the capital. With Dragon Carriages and villagers assigned and everyone about ready to board and set off—

"There's just one favor I'd like to ask."

—So Subaru raised his hand and drew the villagers' attention. Even with the earlier discussion having wrapped up on relatively good terms, receiving their eyes on him again made Subaru hesitate to voice his next proposal. Honestly, it was something he himself wasn't entirely sure was even a necessary request. Still, there were two reasons he wanted to see this proposal through. They were—

"Emilia, at the mansion... I'd like someone to ride along with the Half-Elf girl."

At Subaru's proposal, the bustle that had filled the air a moment ago fell silent in an instant. As an awkward hush began to settle over the village once more, Subaru struggled not to let a feeling of disappointment — a resigned I knew it — show on his face, and said:

"A proclamation went out in the capital, so I think you've all already heard about the Royal Selection. That our lord, Roswaal... sama, is a supporter of one of the candidates, and that the candidate he's supporting is that Half-Elf girl."

—

"I know everyone's uneasy about it. I understand there's plenty that's hard to swallow, too. But even so, I want to ask this of you. Don't let her ride in the Dragon Carriage all alone."

He wasn't confident his voice hadn't carried a trace of anxiety or grief. Hearing Subaru's words, the villagers exchanged glances and wore thoughtful expressions. Still, that they were even considering it at all felt, in itself, like something different from before. Even so, sensing the atmosphere wasn't one where he could expect a positive answer, Subaru was quick to raise his hand to cut his losses with a "sorry about that," when—

"—Petra?"

Before Subaru's raised hand could stop things, a small palm had already gone up. Startled, Subaru looked at the owner of that hand, and she puffed out her red cheeks, looked around at the surrounding adults, and said:

"Why won't anyone listen to him? Subaru's so troubled, asking with a face like he's about to cry, and you won't even try to help him?"

It was a terribly honest, unadorned thing to say — and precisely for that reason, it cut straight to the heart. Free of every entanglement the adults carried, it was the single strongest blow in the world, one only a child was permitted to deliver. The adults grimaced with guilt even greater than before and searched for words to soothe the girl, but—

"The big sister from the mansion — that's the big sister who's always wearing white clothes, right? She's the one who brings the stamp during radiooo caaalisthenics."

"Huh, ah...! Yeah, that's right. The stamp big sister."

Looking back, remembering those peaceful days, Subaru's mouth curved into an unbidden smile. Back when everything had been tranquil, when he could simply enjoy the ordinary days. Every morning, he'd bring Emilia down to the village, do radio calisthenics with the villagers, and stamp everyone's cards with the potato-carved seal he'd made — that had been his daily routine. Yes, memories of happy days — and beside him in every one of those days, she'd been there too.

Perhaps some fitting image had surfaced from a corner of their memory, because the adults too began to catch the meaning behind Petra's words. And in the gap left by that hesitation stopping their mouths, the children around Petra began raising their hands one after another, saying:

"Me tooo! I'm riding with her tooo!" "If Petra's riding, it can't start without me, right?" "No way I'm letting my big brother be the only one who looks good — I'm going with Petra too!" "Subaru looks like he's gonna cry, so let's help him out!" "Yeaaah, let's do thaaat!"

The children stirred and clamored all at once, and at the center of it all, Petra smiled proudly. At an expression far too cute to really call smug, Subaru placed a hand over his chest, feeling as though he'd been saved.

"Can I ask this of you? That girl acts tough and stubborn and pig-headed, but she's lonely underneath, and I worry if someone's not there watching over her."

"Well, I guess it can't be helped — I'll watch over her in Subaru's place."

"Whew" — a breath of relief slipped from Subaru's mouth. Then Subaru walked over to Petra, who'd made him that promise, and held out his pinky finger in front of her. Smiling at the bewildered-looking girl, he said:

"Like this — you hold out your pinky, and we hook them together. Then you sing 'pinky swear, cross my heart' while you cut it off — that's how they seal a promise back where I'm from, nice and now. Think you can do it?"

"I-I'll do it!"

The reply came back with far more spirit than he'd expected, and Subaru laughed, "That's the spirit," and hooked his finger together with the girl's. Then he slowly taught the still-flustered Petra the little rhyme:

"Pinky swear, cross my heart—" "—if I lie, I'll swallow a thousand needlesss—"

"—Pinky's cut!"

Their fingers pulled apart, and a promise — a compact — was sealed. Petra swore to Subaru that she'd never leave Emilia alone, and Subaru swore that he would, without fail, protect the safety of her and the village.

—This promise, this time, no matter what, he would never break.

"This time, let's go without a single regret trailing behind us. Let's do this, everyone!"

Thrusting his fist into the air, Subaru raised his voice, and everyone there thrust their fists up too. Just as it seemed like a rousing cheer was about to burst out at full volume—

"—Ah! This is supposed to be a covert operation, everyone quiet down, quiet down!"

—and so everyone found themselves scrambling to clamp their mouths shut, which rather undercut the moment.

—As an aside, though.

"Ehehe..."

Subaru, who'd called out the order, was stroking the girl's head with his free hand, the one not clasped in the pinky swear. That the girl being stroked had her cheeks flushed red, and was gazing up at Subaru with intensely heated eyes — no one noticed, save for the cat-eared knight and the pink-haired maid.
