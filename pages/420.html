Arc 6 Chapter 18: "The Keeper of the Sand Tower"

—Consciousness lay within a dark, dark stagnation.

It had been a while since Natsuki Subaru had last visited this place. He felt like he'd been here several times before—forcibly summoned here, it seemed, each time he underwent a Return by Death—a black world with no end in earth or sky, where only darkness spread.

Come to think of it, this too was a strange kind of space. After all, Subaru had never once managed to pull the memory of having been here back out into the world beyond. This wasn't his first time passing in and out of it, and yet the moment he left, he forgot he had ever entered at all. It was like the fleeting sensation of a bubble of foam, a fragile dream.

Amid the pale darkness and the hazy, indistinct border of memory, the consciousness that outside was called Natsuki Subaru groped through the void, slowly swimming through the pitch black.

It rose as consciousness alone, and it had grown used to the sensation of having no body, of course, and no hands, feet, eyes, ears, or mouth. The helplessness of not knowing up from down, left from right, the unease of seeing no depth, nothing at all—none of that mattered to a consciousness ruled by only one single thing.

At the core of that consciousness, or perhaps forming the consciousness itself, was an unbearable, ceaseless passion—no, it was affection, it was devotion, it was deep love.

Coming here meant he could meet the source of that love. The consciousness learned only that much, and rejoiced at having been able to visit here again. Before a reunion he hadn't tasted in so very long, a chest that shouldn't exist swelled with anticipation, and lips that didn't exist filled with a joy like song.

But such feelings of that consciousness—

"It seems you're getting quite carried away with yourself, DESU."

"Seriously, this isn't funny at all. Getting a little lucky and acting all triumphant about it, and then staying stuck up like that afterward—unbearable. There's a limit to how shameless someone can be, you know? I really wish you'd take a more objective look at yourself. Then maybe you'd realize just how brazen and shameless you're being."

He was interrupted by voices that suddenly reached him—voices that shouldn't have been able to reach him at all.

『――――』

The consciousness turned around. In a world with no face, no body, no fixed direction, what meaning could the act of turning around possibly have? It wasn't a search for a meaning that didn't exist—it was an act meant to create a meaning that didn't exist. Turning around required a body. Think it deliberately, and put it into motion, and the pitch-black darkness had no choice but to grant it to a Natsuki Subaru who had nothing at all.

And so the turning consciousness became Natsuki Subaru, granted a turning body and sight enough to look around. But that alone was still not enough; completion as a body was still far off. This Subaru was a flawed thing, nowhere near a true reproduction of the real Natsuki Subaru.

Would hands be enough? Would feet be enough? Or perhaps a nose, a mouth? —No. Creating a single human being, reproducing one, was not so simple a thing.

To make Natsuki Subaru, Natsuki Subaru knew far too little about Natsuki Subaru. Natsuki Subaru was not so easy a thing that it could be completed with the knowledge and memory of that one person alone.

And so, as things stood, it was impossible to build any more of Natsuki Subaru than this. But the incomplete Natsuki Subaru could not notice that lacking state. And so this incomplete Natsuki Subaru, given only a provisional body and provisional sight, stood without even his feet firmly on the ground, and turned to look around—and noticed.

Floating in the darkness, the presence of "consciousnesses" that were not Natsuki Subaru.

"How ghastly, how contemptible—this is nothing but the product of pure self-satisfaction, DESU! Ah, how sinful, how filthy a way to be! It deserves nothing but scorn, DESU!"

"Below average? No, below human—incomplete doesn't even begin to cover it. And a thing like that had the nerve to trample all over me, when I was perfectly content? What was it even thinking? If it knew its place, it should've been obvious from the start! Standing before me, standing in my way, getting in the way, blocking me! All of it! It's not enough! It's lacking! If you think about it logically, like a human would...! Even a thing beneath a human, a mere livestock-of-a-man...!"

The "consciousnesses" floating in the darkness, in unison, hurled abuse at the incomplete Natsuki Subaru. One raved madly, the other dripped malice, each flinging their hatred at Natsuki Subaru.

Unfortunately, though, the consciousness-only Natsuki Subaru could not comprehend that hatred. This Natsuki Subaru had not been given a heart, a brain, with which to understand. To try to understand what these "consciousnesses" were saying would require creating an organ for understanding, the same way a body had been prepared, the same way this sight had been created.

『――――』

—But somehow, it felt like no such organ was needed.

At the very least, to understand what these two "consciousnesses" were saying, giving himself such a function felt of little use, and above all, he felt no desire for it. Dragging something into being out of nothing is no easy task, either. Unless Natsuki Subaru's consciousness truly desired it, in the real sense, it could neither obtain nor produce it. So the words to answer those two "consciousnesses," and the mind to understand them, would simply have to wait, for now. No, truly—there was no ulterior motive in it.

"What insolence! What contempt! What derision! And after I have appealed to you with such diligence, you can't even be bothered to try to understand?! What a thing—ah, ah, ahh...! Sloth! You are Sloth, aren't you!"

"How much longer are you going to keep making a fool of people, you livestock bastard...! Listen. Who do you think I am? I am the most fulfilled being in this world, a man who wants nothing but days undisturbed by any event, a man who needs nothing but a desireless, ordinary happiness. And you get in my way. That's a violation of rights. That's an act of evil. There's a limit to how much you can trample someone else's happiness underfoot...!"

Feeling this was going nowhere, he turned his back on the two "consciousnesses." Having a body was convenient—it let him show, through gesture, that he had no intention of engaging in dialogue. Behind him, the two streams of abuse still seemed to echo, but if he minded it, he might end up seeing it, so he chose not to mind. Sight worked even without turning to look. In this case, what was probably needed wasn't "sight" so much as the existence of "eyeballs." If the chance arose, he wanted to keep that in mind to create as well.

But right now, he had no time to bother with such trifles.

『――――』

Because before Natsuki Subaru's consciousness, the shadow of his goal was beginning to show itself.

『――――』

In the pitch-black darkness where nothing should be visible, the shadow that wore a blacker black within the black was almost vivid. The pliant arms and fingers that sometimes froze Natsuki Subaru's heart and made him tremble. A slender yet somehow soft-seeming figure, wrapped in a dress the color of darkness. As always, everything above the neck was indistinct, as if shrouded in thick fog, but Natsuki Subaru understood with his very soul that there, in that place, was a "someone" who inspired in him a beloved feeling.

That figure was clearly more distinct than at their previous meeting, and closer, too. At the previous meeting, only the arms and the build of the figure had been visible; now he could make out the dress she wore, and even the bare feet peeking from beneath its long hem. Almost the entire body had been fully reproduced; the only thing Natsuki Subaru still could not see was the face, hidden in darkness.

It was frustrating. But for now, that was fine. He felt her presence more strongly than before, closer than before. But Natsuki Subaru himself was not yet ready to welcome her as she grew more distinct.

For now, it was enough simply to rejoice that she was so close by. Someday, without fail, he would touch those uncertain fingertips, draw that slender waist close, and exchange love with her.

『—I love you』

At those words, Natsuki Subaru dipped the chin he had only just finished making, and answered. Just that much was enough to make the shadow glad, and a feeling of guilt began to sprout in him.

Next time, so that he could speak words of love back to her himself, he would need a "mouth" and a "tongue." With that thought in mind, Natsuki Subaru's consciousness drifted away from the shadow's garden—

※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※

Subaru was self-aware that he was a good waker, but on the flip side, he also knew perfectly well that he was a poor sleeper.

To Subaru, waking from sleep felt like breaking the surface of water for air. Anyone who's been underwater never forgets to breathe once their face breaks the surface. To Subaru, that breath was the very awakening of consciousness—and being able to draw that waking "breath" was simply a given.

"I'm jealous that you're good at waking up. I'm reeeally bad at it..."

That had been Emilia's comment, from a conversation they'd had once before. As for Emilia's own waking, incidentally, that statement was neither exaggeration nor modesty—it was genuinely quite bad. It matched the image one might have, in a way; Emilia had extremely low blood pressure, and it took her nearly an hour after waking just to get out of bed.

Sit up in bed in the morning, drift in and out of half-sleep waiting for consciousness to fully surface, finally climb out from under the covers, wash her face, fix her hair—that was her style. On the other hand, unlike Subaru, she fell asleep as easily as a child—a real contrast.

"In the forest, there was only Puck besides me, and even Puck wouldn't come out much once it got dark, so... there was nothing to do but sleep..."

That, too, had been Emilia's comment, from a conversation they'd had once before. As for how Subaru responded to that, the details shall remain undisclosed. He has someone to stay up late with now, after all, so the past should be left in the past.

In any case, Subaru sincerely envied her ease of falling asleep. No matter what, once Subaru got into bed and closed his eyes in the dark, all sorts of thoughts came crowding in. What he thought about most were regrets—if only he'd done this, if only he'd done that. Sometimes it was about that one day; sometimes about something further back. Regret assailed Subaru without regard for time or method. Fighting against it made it impossible to fall asleep. That was the cause of his poor sleep.

—And so the events beneath the sand sea, too, were sure to become fresh seeds of regret for Subaru, seeds that would greatly disturb his peaceful sleep for a long time to come.

"...Where... is this?"

The instant he woke, Subaru understood that this was an awakening different from a Return by Death. First, there was light around him. Unlike the underground of the sand sea, where he'd woken in a world of darkness, being able to look around him was proof of change. What's more, the chill in the air had faded, and his body was cradled in a sensation utterly different from lying on sand. The soft feel against his back was, most likely, a bed. What was beneath his head, too, was something that should be called a pillow, which meant that Subaru was—

"...I'm sleeping in a bed..."

Saying it aloud to confirm it, he found a white towel blanket draped over his reclining body. The feel of the pillow and the bed was not, on closer thought, unfamiliar. In fact, it was something he'd grown very used to lately, and he quickly realized it was the bedding fitted into the Dragon Carriage. Which meant this was inside the Dragon Carriage they had used to attempt the Pleiades Watchtower.

"Wait, why would that be...!?"

Realizing he'd been laid down inside the Dragon Carriage he should have been separated from, Subaru sat up. At that instant, he noticed that his raised right hand was being gripped by someone. Turning reflexively, he found, right beside him—kneeling on the floor beside the bed, her body sprawled forward onto the mattress, asleep—a girl. Her expression somewhat relieved even in sleep, gripping Subaru's hand, was Emilia, her silver hair tied back in one.

A faint breathing, and the warmth conveyed through her palm. At the certainty that she was truly here, Subaru let out a hoarse breath and went limp with relief.

"Ah, ha... Emilia, right? This is really... you're okay..."

Emilia, snuggled right beside him—he couldn't quite believe it, and felt lost. Since his right hand was still held, he reached out his left and touched her cheek. Her white cheek was hot with fever, her skin unnaturally smooth and soft. Just touching it made an explosion of tenderness well up in him, and he kneaded her cheek as far as he was allowed, just to make sure.

"Ohh, no mistake, it's Emilia. ...Cute. Soft. Warm."

"—You shouldn't tease her so much, I suppose. Emilia was worried about Subaru quite a lot, you know. She hasn't slept in two nights, I suppose."

"Whoa-ohp!?"

While savoring the little groan Emilia made as he toyed with her cheek, Subaru's shoulders jumped at the sudden voice. Whirling around in a panic, he found, at the door connecting the sleeping area to the living space, a small girl leaning against it with an exasperated look.

"Bea—"

"Shh, I suppose. Ram doesn't like a Subaru who won't listen, I suppose."

『――――』

He'd nearly let out a cry of joy at the reunion, but Beatrice stopped him just in time. Reflexively clapping his left hand over his mouth, Subaru gently looked down at the still-sleeping Emilia. She mumbled her lips, her expression somehow happy, cheeks loosened.

"Close call... So, Bea-ko. Come here. Let me hug you."

"What foolishness are you... w-well, I suppose it can't be helped."

If he couldn't share his joy at their reunion in words, there was nothing for it but to share it through gesture. When Subaru beckoned, Beatrice, sighing, approached with a truly reluctant air. Fiddling absently with one of her ringlets, the girl came to his side, and Subaru drew her in with his free left arm, holding her firmly against his chest.

"Thank god... seriously, thank god. I really, really was worried."

"...That's Betty's line, I suppose. With Subaru and the sisters' elder sister nowhere to be found, it was Betty and the others who had it far worse—it was a chilling ordeal, I suppose. It felt like Betty wasn't even alive, I suppose."

"Yeah? You were worried enough to feel like you were dying?"

"It's not particularly like that... or maybe it is, I suppose. Yes, Betty was worried, I suppose. Really, truly, really..."

As he held Beatrice's light body, the girl trailed off mid-sentence and lowered her face. Subaru stroked the head of the girl now rubbing her forehead against his chest, and for a while they silently confirmed their reunion. Then, after a moment of silence had passed, Beatrice lifted her face from Subaru's chest with a refreshed look, and hopped down beside the bed.

"Anyway, Betty has to let everyone besides sleepyhead Emilia know that Subaru's woken up, I suppose. Subaru was the last one, so everyone was worried, I suppose."

"Everyone means... right, everyone's okay, right? The ones who got separated, and the ones who were with me—all of them."

"You can rest easy, I suppose. Everyone's accounted for safely, I suppose."

"Really... really, huh...!"

At Beatrice's reassurance, the anxiety gripping Subaru began to fade. Hearing that everyone was safe, his heart settled at the reply beyond his hopes. But almost immediately, Subaru felt an unpleasant sense of déjà vu about that reassurance, and lifted his head.

"Wait, Bea-ko. I don't want to get my hopes up for nothing. Is everyone really okay?"

"Hmph, it's insulting that Subaru would doubt Betty, I suppose. There's no reason for Betty to lie to Subaru about something like this, I suppose. This is no joke, I suppose."

"I get why you're pouting, but I'm not doubting you specifically. Even if you're not trying to deceive me, it's still possible I end up deceived as a result. ...We just learned that the hard way in Priestella, after all."

『――――』

Hearing that much, Beatrice seemed to grasp what Subaru was wary of. During the battle against the Witch Cult in Priestella, too, Subaru had received a similar report after the operation ended. Everyone was safe, he'd been told, and factually, in terms of understanding, that had been correct, but—

"Let me confirm. Me, Emilia, Beatrice. Rem, Ram, and Patrasche. Anastasia, Meili, and Giant... and last, Julius. That's everyone."

『――――』

"Is the 'everyone' you mean the same as this everyone... right?"

"...It's fine, I suppose. That's everyone. There's no one Betty forgot, someone that only Subaru remembers, I suppose."

"I see... I see. Then... it's really okay to be happy, huh..."

Having confirmed what he needed to, Subaru carefully considered whether there were any other pitfalls. Judging that he'd overlooked nothing, the words "everyone's safe" finally took on real weight.

"Thank god. Ahh, thank god...!"

"Honestly, Subaru's being too dramatic, I suppose. The moment Subaru, the most precarious one, turned out fine, it was obvious enough that everyone else would be safe too, I suppose."

"Idiot, that's not the point. Even knowing that, worry is still worry, anxiety is still anxiety. You were crying too, once you knew I was okay, weren't you?"

"Ram wasn't crying, I suppose. Betty was pressing her face into Subaru's chest, so there's no way such a thing could have been seen, I suppose. It's impossible to prove, I suppose."

Beatrice puffed out her thin chest, putting on a brave front with a "hmph," but the remark itself practically confessed to the very thing it denied. On top of that, Subaru pointed at the back half of his own bed—about half the mattress's worth of space—where there were traces of someone besides Subaru having slept.

"There's evidence right here that someone snuck in next to me and slept here. Even looking at this irrefutable evidence, you're saying you weren't worried?"

"That would be Betty, wouldn't it?! No, this is a complete frame-up, I suppose. Betty doesn't know anything about it, I suppose!"

"Who else but you would do something so shameless? Don't be shy about it."

"Anyway, it's not true, I suppose! Ugh, honestly, Emilia's going to wake up at this rate, I suppose."

As they traded their usual banter, Beatrice forcibly redirected the conversation. Wryly smiling at her red face, Subaru let out a long, truly long and deep breath.

Everyone safe—hearing it in words at last brought a measure of relief. On the dunes, separated while fleeing from a pack of Witchbeasts, Subaru had repeated his baffling Returns by Death beneath the sand sea, with no idea what had become of Emilia and the others, wondering what he would do if things had ended in irreversible tragedy—

"...Come to think of it, is that strange paranoia gone?"

"—?"

Beatrice tilted her head at Subaru, who was, with a look of astonishment, kneading his own face with his left hand. It was a meaningless action to Beatrice, but a meaningful one for Subaru. No—the action itself had no meaning, but whether or not he still felt those symptoms was an important factor.

"Bea-ko is... the usual Bea-ko. Emilia's cute as always, too."

"What are you suddenly saying..."

Beatrice looked exasperated, but Subaru felt no strange discomfort or irritation at her words. The same went for the sleeping Emilia—all he felt toward the two of them was affection. The incomprehensible hatred that had tormented Subaru down in that underground place was nowhere to be seen, not even a trace.

"I guess it's because we're away from the Miasma now. Cause unknown, treatment unknown—leaving something that scary unaddressed is unsettling, but..."

"Subaru, if you're still feeling unwell, or if you have any concerns about your condition, you can keep sleeping, I suppose. There's no need to rush up right away, I suppose. Betty and the others can manage the trial on their own for now, I suppose."

"...What?"

With a hand to his forehead, Beatrice fretted over the downcast Subaru. Amid her considerate words, there was an unfamiliar term, and Subaru couldn't help but catch on it.

"What did you just say?"

"So, you can rest, I suppose. It's not skipping out—it's fine to sleep when you're genuinely unwell, no one's going to be angry, I suppose."

"That's a real 'you sound like you've said this exact thing before' kind of low-fever pep talk, but that's not the part I mean. After that—did you say 'trial'?"

"Ah... yes, I suppose I did say that, I suppose."

At Subaru's pursuit, Beatrice made an "oops" kind of face. It wasn't the look of someone who'd wanted to hide it from Subaru so much as consideration born of wanting to let him rest a while longer. But Subaru wasn't so mild-mannered a person that he could stay calm after hearing that much, and Beatrice knew that too.

"Bea-ko."

"Ram— Betty knows, I suppose. Betty will explain properly, I suppose. Really, Betty thought it would be better to talk about it more calmly, once things had settled down, but... it slipped out, I suppose."

"Not being able to keep secrets is one of your cute, good points. —Anyway, I skipped over something I should've asked first."

Subaru gave a faint wry smile at Beatrice's thoroughly caught-off-guard expression, and then the smile vanished from his cheeks at once. He lowered his voice slightly and voiced the question that should have been obvious all along. That was—

"Where is this? And don't give me some little joke like 'inside the Dragon Carriage,' okay?"

"Subaru's probably already guessed, I suppose."

Beatrice let out a wistful sigh, then crossed her arms. And the girl looked up at the ceiling—at what lay beyond it—with a gesture, lightly clicking her heel, and said—

"—The Pleiades Watchtower."

『――――』

"That watchtower at the far end of the dunes—Betty and the others are inside it, I suppose."

That was what she said.

※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※

—Carefully unclasping Emilia's hand, laying her, thoroughly asleep after two sleepless nights, down onto the bed, Subaru stroked her forehead before slipping out of the Dragon Carriage.

The inside of the Dragon Carriage, where they'd spent over a month, seemed unchanged from just before they'd been separated. There were no major scratches on the body, nor did anything appear to have been damaged.

"Unlike Subaru and the others, Betty and the rest, swallowed by the crack in the 'sand wind,' were safe, I suppose. We were sent flying to a place different from that flower field... and it was when we realized Subaru and the others weren't with us that everyone's faces turned even paler, I suppose."

"That includes you too, right."

"—Emilia and Julius were the same, I suppose. It wasn't only Betty, I suppose."

With a slight, sulky look away, Beatrice opened the door leading out of the Dragon Carriage. The door creaked open, greeting them with a dim space and a dry wind. The feel of the wind was close to that of the dunes; the darkness of the space was close to that of the underground of the sand sea. But compared to the dunes, the wind carried no sand mixed in, and compared to the underground, the darkness was somewhat softened. The cause was likely the magic lamps set here and there throughout the space, and the faintly glowing moss clinging thickly to the walls. He recalled having seen that kind of moss before, in the tombs of the Sanctuary. Given how it seemed to grow anywhere, it was a rather convenient sort of plant.

"This is..."

"The sixth floor of the Pleiades Watchtower... well, I suppose that means the lowest floor. Judging by how the tower looks from the sand sea, the sixth floor seems to be the part buried underground. You emerge above the sand starting from the fifth floor... though, I suppose you can barely tell the difference, I suppose."

Beatrice explained to Subaru, who looked around the circular space with wide eyes. Her explanation was a bit unclear, probably because Subaru had no familiarity at all with the interior or structure of the Pleiades Watchtower.

As far as he could see, the interior of the Pleiades Watchtower spread out into quite a vast space. The place Beatrice called the sixth floor had a floor entirely of stone, with the Dragon Carriage sitting alone right in the very center of the space. The circular space spreading out around that carriage looked to be, roughly, two or three hundred meters in radius. If the tower had been built as a precise circle rather than an ellipse, the precision of it, and the sheer enormity of the labor involved, left him speechless.

"...Thinking about pointless efforts from ancient times, pyramids come to mind. Those are quite the pile of waste for just one person's grave, too. Though, I have heard it was treated kind of like a public works project, and was apparently a real lifesaver for people without jobs."

"Grumbling on again about something incomprehensible, I suppose."

"I'm talking about gravestones. Living funerals are trendy too—thinking ahead about what happens after you die and taking preemptive measures is the same idea people have had since ancient times."

"...Talk about what happens after death is inauspicious, I suppose. It's ridiculous, I suppose."

At Subaru's tactless remark, Beatrice looked away, sulking. Realizing his own slip of the tongue, Subaru scratched his cheek, then gave Beatrice's head a light pat. Then, as if suddenly remembering something, he circled around to the front of the Dragon Carriage.

"Oh, Giant! Good to see you're okay too! Giant, don't go dyin' on us!"

"Why are you suddenly saying something so pointless!?"

"No, I just felt like I had to get that out of the way. But I mean it—I really am glad you're okay."

Tethered right in front of the Dragon Carriage, close beside its body, was the dependable Earth Dragon Giant. The Desert-model Gairasu breed prided itself on toughness, and true to that reputation, Giant had shown a stubborn will to live and made it through that brutal ordeal splendidly.

"Still, you being the first one I confirm is alive, ahead of everyone else... that's not exactly the most heroic ending."

『――――』

"Hey now, don't make that pitiful face. I'm happy, really. Here, I'll give you extra feed later, so forgive me, alright?"

『――――』

"What, seriously, you're such a mercenary guy."

"It's about time you wrapped up the little skit, I suppose. Ram's getting tired, let's go, I suppose."

At Subaru trading quips with Giant, Beatrice looked like she couldn't keep up. Subaru twisted his neck with a "sorry, sorry" at her reaction, then said—

"I don't mean to trouble you or anything, but there's something I want to check."

"What is it, I suppose."

"No... it's just, Giant's here, but Patrasche's nowhere to be seen."

Both being Earth Dragons, if Patrasche were safe, it would be normal for her to be tethered here too. But checking the far side of the Dragon Carriage, that beautiful, pitch-black form was nowhere in sight. Subaru grew anxious at the absence of a form that should have been there, but—

"Don't jump to conclusions, I suppose. That Earth Dragon... Patrasche can't be kept on the sixth floor, so she's on the fourth floor, I suppose."

"Fourth floor? You mean up there? Why?"

"Because she's injured and being treated, I suppose. The sisters' younger sister... Rem's figure wasn't in the carriage either, was it, I suppose. Anyone whose condition isn't good is being kept on the fourth floor, I suppose."

"Being treated... Rem, is she going to get better?!"

Hearing this unexpected news, Subaru couldn't help lunging at Beatrice. Beatrice looked a bit startled at Subaru's intensity, but quickly pinched his nose and pushed him back.

"C-calm down, I suppose...! There's no need to panic, I suppose...!"

"How can I calm down?! Rem... will she recover? And what's this deal with Patrasche being treated too!?"

"To cut to the conclusion! It's not something that'll be resolved right away, I suppose! It's just that it's a better situation than leaving them lying there, I suppose! It's the same for the Earth Dragon too, I suppose!"

"...I see."

At Beatrice's response, Subaru's pressing manner deflated, and Beatrice's expression grew pained at how his energy drained away. But the girl clenched her small fists and said,

"Come now, this isn't the time to be moping, I suppose. There's surely a way to do something about it, I suppose. To find out, everyone's tackling the watchtower's trial right now, I suppose."

『――――』

Trying, in her own clumsy way, to rouse Subaru's spirits, Beatrice's words took some of the sting out of him, and he lowered his chin, muttering, "Yeah, you're right."

"Sorry, I've been kind of negative. Sorry for making you worry so much."

"If it's worry caused by Subaru, well, Ram supposes she can allow that much. But if possible, it would help if you'd let Betty worry where she can see you, I suppose."

"I'll think about it."

Touched by Beatrice's consideration, Subaru ruffled her hair in a show of gratitude. Beatrice's formidable hairstyle was sturdy enough that not even that could muss it. In any case, bolstered by Beatrice's encouragement, Subaru turned his eyes upward, toward the top of the tower.

"So this is the sixth floor... anyway, we just need to go up, right?"

"Julius and the others are probably in the middle of the trial right now, I suppose. So for now, it'd be better to meet with the 'Sage' first and hear the details, I suppose."

"The Sage, huh... oh, wait, come to think of it—"

Mid-sentence from Beatrice, Subaru snagged on the word "Sage." Right—the whole reason they'd come to the watchtower in the first place had been to meet this "Sage," and yet Beatrice hadn't really said anything about the "Sage" up to this point.

"Bea-ko, you met the Sage, right? What were they like? Did you complain to them about pelting us with all those white beams of light or whatever?"

"...I'd rather not talk about the Sage much, I suppose. No, that's not it, I suppose. It's probably faster to just meet the Sage directly, I suppose."

"I don't have good memories of meeting someone normal after being told that. I think I remember Emilia-tan saying something similar the first time I met Roswaal."

It had been over a year ago now, but he remembered that conversation clearly. As he recalled, about Roswaal, the explanation had gone something like, "It's easier to understand if you meet and talk to him, and I'm sure you two will get along." In practice, he supposed the impact wouldn't have come through without meeting him directly, and their wavelengths did match, in a way—well, that was all in the past now.

"You'll understand once you meet, I suppose."

"...Fine. So I just need to go meet them. How do we get up there?"

"The stairs, I suppose. Look closely, and there are proper stairs by the wall, I suppose."

Squinting at Beatrice's words, Subaru could indeed make out a raised step—something like a staircase—along the circular outer edge. But it turned out to be an enormous number of steps, forming a spiral staircase that wound its way around the vast circular tower.

"Eh...? We're climbing this?"

"We're climbing this, I suppose. If you work hard, it won't take too long, I suppose."

"'If you work hard,' she says... how many steps are there to the top!?"

Faced with the prospect of an endless repetition of climbing, Subaru let out a scream. His voice echoed hollowly through the tower, likely carrying quite far up, and faded away sadly. Ignoring Subaru's lament, Beatrice shrugged her shoulders with a sigh, and said,

"It can't be helped, I suppose. If you could use a bit of magic, there might be various means to skip the stairs and go straight up... but Subaru has no other option, I suppose."

"No, come on, like, Muraku or whatever. How about using that to just zip on up in one go, effortlessly?"

"It's not impossible, but Ram worries about running out of Mana gas at a crucial moment, I suppose."

"Even climbing the stairs normally, I'm worried about running out of stamina gas at a crucial moment!?"

Even though his stamina had improved compared to his shut-in days, Subaru still hadn't escaped being a frail boy by other-world standards. He had no idea how high the fifth floor above the sixth floor, the lowest one, actually was, but at least as far as he could see looking up, the ceiling was nowhere in sight. Judging it to be that high, this was practically mountain-climbing territory.

"Damn it! But if I don't climb, I can't confirm Rem and Patrasche are safe. Guess I gotta do it. Ugh, dammit."

"That's the spirit, I suppose. Fight, I suppose. Betty will cheer you on too, I suppose. —It would help a lot if you called for her once you reach the top, I suppose."

"Not happening, no shortcuts for you!? You're coming too. Walk! Work up a sweat!"

"Unfortunately, Betty is a cute Spirit, so sweating just isn't—"

As Subaru braced himself reluctantly to tackle the stairs, Beatrice tried to weasel out of it. But mid-argument, Beatrice suddenly cut herself off and looked up overhead.

"Bea-ko?"

"This is bad, I suppose. Subaru, get back—!"

At the same moment Subaru called out at her sudden change in demeanor, Beatrice went pale and leapt at him. Catching the Beatrice who'd flung herself into his chest, he scooped her up. Beatrice blinked, dumbfounded, then roughly slapped Subaru's chest.

"Idiot! That doesn't mean pick me up, I suppose! Get back, I suppose!"

"It was a reflex, I just—wait."

Still holding Beatrice, Subaru leaned his body lightly backward. He had no idea what was happening, but Beatrice's alarm was real. He meant to brace for it—but it was too late.

—The very next instant, a tremendous pressure erupted overhead, and Subaru caught his breath.

The source of that oppressive force came hurtling down, and in the next moment slammed into the ground right before Subaru's eyes—a violent tremor shook the massive tower, and the resulting blast of wind and sand nearly swept Subaru off his feet. Had Giant's massive frame not happened to be right behind him, he'd surely have gone tumbling. Protected by the weight of the carriage and the Earth Dragon, Subaru forced his eyes open against the driving sand and gale.

Then, beyond the billowing smoke, the sand haze at the point of impact was suddenly swept away. A fierce gust tore through the smoke like ripping cloth, and beyond it, a figure emerged, standing tall and unhurried. At the sight of the figure appearing through the swirling sand, Subaru's cheeks stiffened, frozen in place.

That figure, that appearance—he recognized it.

"You're..."

Striding forward, treading firmly across the hard floor, was a tall woman. Her hair, a brown so dark it was nearly black, was tied back in a ponytail; her outfit could fairly be called half-naked, boldly exposing her arms, legs, stomach, and even her back. Covering only the bare minimum of her chest and lower abdomen, she wore a black cloak draped over that already quite eccentric attire.

In Subaru's terms: a lewd woman in hot pants and a black bikini top, with a cloak thrown over it.

Long, slender white limbs, and a full bosom swayed without the slightest hesitation. Her height was about the same as Subaru's, perhaps a bit taller, and there was no comparing the height of her hips to his. Atop her white shoulders sat a well-formed face—a languid yet strikingly compelling beauty.

—It was, without question, the last face Subaru had seen just before he lost consciousness.

The one who had shown no mercy to that centaur, cutting it down. And the countless bolts of light unleashed on that centaur had been an attack Subaru recognized, too.

"...Are you the 'Sage'?"

『――――』

To the woman approaching in silence, Subaru managed only that much, his throat dry. But the woman said nothing until she had come right up close to Subaru, stopping at a distance where a reach of the hand would touch him, only looking him over intently.

That gaze seemed to lick over his entire body, and frankly, Subaru found it unsettling. He hadn't even heard so much as her voice, and on top of that, her strength had made short work of a centaur, and she'd jumped down from an unimaginable distance without any apparent effect—an inhuman level of power. Make one wrong move, and it wouldn't be strange for him to be reduced to cinders on the spot. Having no read at all on how such an opponent might act left him with nothing but dread.

『――――』

In Subaru's arms, Beatrice remained stiff, still held tight. He could feel through her small body that she, too, was tense. At least it was fortunate they'd run into this while in contact with Beatrice. In the worst case, engaging in combat wasn't impossible. Of course, it was best if it didn't come to a fight. That effort shouldn't be spared.

"H-hey, are you... listening? Look, um, I heard my friends were being taken care of by you, so, you're not planning to be hostile or anything... right?"

『――――』

"Um, if you just stay silent it's making me nervous, so if you could say something... or wait, could it be you're one of those types who communicates by telepathy instead of words? If so, that'd be a problem. Ha ha."

『――――』

The presumed "Sage" only stared at Subaru, unresponsive to his words, scrutinizing him. Discomfort, taken to its extreme. The extent of Subaru's confusion and bewilderment could be gauged from just how flustered his speech had become. At this rate, he might well be killed by the sheer awkwardness of the atmosphere.

—That anxiety of Subaru's was suddenly shattered.

"...Three."

"Huh?"

『――――』

The woman, who had been staring intently at Subaru, suddenly muttered something like that. Her voice was somewhat hoarse, but if he could keep listening, he suspected it would prove to be a beautiful, husky voice. Still, somehow, it struck him as oddly endearing, for the voice of this mysterious woman whose feelings were impossible to read. And then, before Subaru, the woman quietly exhaled and said,

"Eh, whatever, that's fine. More importantly, found you."

"Um, sorry?"

The woman suddenly said something as if tossing it out carelessly, and her expression changed. What had, until then, been a serious look, as if trying to peer into Subaru's very depths, now slowly, over a long span of time, began to melt like something frozen thawing. The woman's mouth widened sideways, taking the shape called a smile, and

then the woman, gazing at Subaru, said—

"—Master."

"...Huh?"

A word he had absolutely no memory of hearing, and an emotion he had absolutely no memory of earning. Stunned by such an unexpected reaction, Subaru turned to look behind him. Naturally, there was nothing there but Giant. So then—was Giant the "Master" in question?

"Did you just come here and suddenly set up an important-character flag on him!?"

"There's no way that's it, I suppose! Get real, I suppose!"

As Subaru pressed in on Giant, he got scolded by the Beatrice in his arms. Her words were reasonable, but Subaru's head wasn't keeping up. With no idea whatsoever what he was being accused of—or credited with—he turned back to face the woman,

"No, sorry, but that's probably a case of mistaken identity or something like that... mmph!?"

"Master! Ahh, ahh, ahh! I've been waiting, y'know~!"

Mid-sentence, he was seized in an embrace by the woman, who came flying at him, overcome with emotion. Or rather, he took the full brunt of her flying tackle and was knocked flat on his back. Falling backward onto the floor, Beatrice, caught between them, let out a shriek of "Mugyuu!" but the woman, giddy with excitement, showed no sign of letting go of the wide-eyed, blinking Subaru.

Her long ponytail whipping wildly, the woman clamped down on Subaru with an abnormal, monstrous strength, repeating, over and over, in a delighted voice.

"Master! Master! It's been so long! I missed you so much! I thought I was gonna spend my whole life just sniping at anyone who got close! For real!"

"W-wait! Wait wait wait! What is it?! What are you talking about?!"

"How can you say something so mean! Master's the one who gave the order! Keep anyone who approaches the shrine from getting through... well, the method's a separate matter, I guess!"

"No, not that— I'm the Master?! What are you even saying?!"

Despite being in direct contact with the woman's soft skin, he had no room in his heart to savor the lucky perverted moment. Still caught in her abnormal arm strength, Subaru desperately tried to escape. But the woman, for her part, seemed to have her own case to make, and showed no sign of releasing him. As a result, Subaru and the woman ended up rolling and grappling on the floor, with Beatrice squashed between them the whole time.

"You, seriously, get off me... this isn't getting anywhere...!"

"No way! Absolutely not way~! Say that, and the second I look away you'll just disappear again, I bet! Master hasn't changed one bit!"

"How should I know?!"

The stubborn woman, whatever trauma she was working through, refused to let go of Subaru no matter what. Grabbing her face roughly to push her away, Subaru shouted, almost snarling,

"Who even are you in the first place! What are you!"

"What are you saying?! I'm Shaula! Star Watcher of the Pleiades Watchtower! Master's adorable disciple, Shaula, that's who!"

"Never heard of you—!!"

The woman who named herself—Shaula—but that name was supposed to belong to the tower's "Sage." There was no way the esteemed "Sage" residing in the Pleiades Watchtower could possibly be some incomprehensible woman like this.

Subaru resolutely raised an objection to that idea. But into that scene of tangled, going-nowhere struggle, a sudden change burst in. It was—

"—This is bad! Really bad! I woke up and Subaru's nowhere to be found! I need to tell everyone quickly and go look for him..."

"Ah."

And out from the Dragon Carriage door, Emilia came tumbling out, her hair a mess of bedhead. Full of anxious urgency, the moment she stepped out of the carriage, she found Subaru and Shaula tangled up together—and, in fact, Beatrice too—and her eyes went wide.

"Emilia... tan! I'm so glad you woke up! Actually, this girl—"

"There."

"Ow!? Emilia-tan, why did you just kick me?!"

"I don't really know why, but I got reeeally worked up inside."

Including the now-awake Emilia, the noisy shouting match continued for a while longer—

In the end, the desperate struggle with the (self-proclaimed) "Sage" of the Pleiades Watchtower went on until Julius and the others, noticing the commotion, arrived to join them.
