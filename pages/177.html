Arc 4 Chapter 11: "A Prideful, Slothful Wrath"

In a space where even the ground beneath his feet had become uncertain, Subaru stood face to face with a void that would surely mean "death" if he fell into it — and yet he spared it not a single thought. Right now, what dominated Subaru's mind was only the girl before his eyes — a witch with white hair, white skin, black clothes, and black eyes.

That overwhelming presence, that crushing sense of intimidation, the sheer difference in caliber of a being who had transcended the mortal realm. All of it wound invisible fingers around the eyes, the heart, the very soul of the insignificant existence that was Subaru, toying with him at will. Faced with a truly inescapable "terror," this was how thoroughly a person's emotions could be sealed away.

He couldn't breathe. He couldn't even register the beat of his own heart. Not a drop of cold sweat rose to his skin; he couldn't so much as blink without her permission. An absolute separation stood there.

"Oh dear, have I frightened you too much? Even back then, whenever I got carried away, my tongue always ran away with me like this. What a troublesome nature, being a witch."

Still seated in her chair, Echidna suddenly seemed to notice the heat in her own words and reflect on it. But the pressure emanating from the witch before him showed not the slightest sign of easing. No — the crushing weight that, until just a moment ago, he had somehow managed to consciously ignore, had now settled itself at the edge of his awareness the instant he'd noticed it, and refused to leave.

Whatever friendliness there had been in their exchange was gone now. Subaru could no longer see the girl before him as a girl. Not in any real sense — only as a "witch."

"This happened often enough in life, too. Like when royalty from various nations would come seeking my wisdom... I take it you can no longer help but look at me warily."

Shaking her head as if to say "what can you do," Echidna looked at Subaru with those black eyes. The moment Subaru flinched at the sight of his own expressionless reflection in those black irises, she smiled.

"Well then, perhaps this next diversion will be more to your liking?"

"——!?"

The change came without warning. Just as Subaru's furrowed brow showed his incomprehension at Echidna's murmured, smiling words, her smile dissolved into darkness — and the very instant Subaru, holding his breath, blinked—

"Whatcha starin' at, huh?"

"...Huh?"

"Don't go starin' like that, y'hear?"

Saying so, kicking her legs back and forth, a girl sat before Subaru with her cheeks puffed out. She had deep green hair cut even at the shoulders and cheeks as red as apples. Her tan skin suited the white, dress-like garment she wore, and she radiated an unabashed, childlike charm all around her. A hairpin shaped like a blue flower, fastened in her hair, stood out as a distinctive touch.

However you looked at her, she seemed nothing but a harmless, innocent little girl — and now she sat in the place where Echidna had been, staring fixedly at Subaru.

"Ah, eh, wha—? H-hold on. Wait, Echidna...? Where'd she go?"

"Dona? Dona went off somewhere, but who're youuu?"

"M-me? My name's Natsuki Subaru. I'm just a lost soul who wasn't called or invited, on my way home after a bit of tea... though I'm kind of in trouble since the host of the house just up and vanished..."

"Heh. Then you're Baru, I guess."

Faced with something too adorable to really call hostility, and unable to keep up with the situation, Subaru introduced himself honestly all the same. At that, the girl broke into a happy, silly grin — and despite everything, it warmed something in Subaru's chest.

The situation was utter chaos, but the moment Echidna vanished, he'd been freed from that crushing pressure. Thinking it over calmly, the girl in front of him might, like Subaru, be some kind of victim dragged here from somewhere else too. Somehow, working together with this little girl, he might manage an escape from here — thinking so, though he had no idea how much help she could actually offer, he tried to lift his head, and—

"Alright, anyway, while the cat's away, let's use the chance to work things out and come up with an escape plan. There's barely any footing to think on, but first, little lady, your name—"

"Say, Baru, are you an evildoerrr?"

"I was just about to ask for your name... wait, what?"

Subaru's brow furrowed just as he held out his hand, flashing his teeth in a grin. Before him, the girl swung her legs, which didn't reach the ground, rocking the chair back and forth as she pouted her lips with childish impatience and said, "So-o, liiisten—"

"I'm askin' if you're an evildoer or not! Well? Which is it?"

"All people are born sinful creatures who must sacrifice something just to live. So maybe we're all sinners from the moment we're born into this world. But even so, people go on living. Because even if it means sacrificing something, we know that value can only be built atop that sacrifice... not that having a philosophical discussion with a little girl is gonna do much good, but is that the kind of thing you mean?"

"Hmmm, I don't get it even hearin' it. Well, whatever — I'll just find out for myself."

As Subaru tilted his head, the girl tilted hers even further. So saying, she gave Subaru's outstretched palm a firm little squeeze. Her small hand fit snugly inside his own, its distinctly girlish softness coming through in the touch. Subaru renewed his resolve — no matter what, he had to get this girl safely out of here.

"Being around Petra and everyone made me realize it — turns out I actually like kids more than I thought. And here I always figured they were just noisy and I hated them..."

"—Sin is atoned for only through pain."

"Nnh?"

Suddenly, in a quick, quiet murmur, the girl said something under her breath. Unable to catch the words, Subaru raised an eyebrow and tried to look at her — then felt a slight jolt. Along with a sensation like his arm being lightly tugged, there came a strange, weightless feeling of relief, as if a burden had been lifted. Wondering what had happened, Subaru turned his head to look around.

Nothing about the world had changed. The only space allowed to Subaru and the girl was just enough for the two of them to sit facing each other; no sound, no wind, could be felt. And before Subaru, who stood there without sitting, the girl remained seated, swinging her legs. In one hand, she held a man's arm, torn off at the shoulder—

"——!?"

"If it doesn't hurt, that means you're not an evildoer. Good for youuu."

Realizing something was terribly wrong, Subaru looked at his own right arm — the right side of his body that should have been there — and discovered the fact that it had been torn off, exposing a ragged cross-section at the shoulder. There was no pain, no bleeding — no sensation at all, in fact. From the cross-section, pink flesh containing bone and blood vessels peeked out, calling to mind cuts of meat lined up in a butcher shop. That is, if he set aside the unacceptable reality that this was happening to his own right shoulder.

"Ah, AAAAAAAH!! M-my arm... my arm's—!?"

"It doesn't hurt, does it? Don't yell so loud. If you thrash around too much, you'll fall and won't be able to come back."

"Y-you, you, YOU!? You tear a person's arm clean off and then say that!? Give it back! Give it baaack!"

Screaming as he touched the stump of his right arm, Subaru then lunged at the girl, who gave a little snort, his face contorted with desperate intensity. He had to snatch back the arm gripped in her hand and reattach it right now. Of course, just pressing the severed arm back against his shoulder wouldn't actually reconnect it, but even that much didn't register in Subaru's mind right now. But—

"—The transgression becomes a wedge, and never lets go."

The instant the girl blurted out those words again, Subaru's stance collapsed completely. No — to be precise, the leg he'd been about to plant on the ground shattered from the knee down like a piece of glasswork. Having lost his right shoulder and now both legs from the knee down, Subaru's body pitched forward with the momentum. What caught him was the lap of the girl, who still sat in her chair. The girl gently caught the falling Subaru and, with the touch of a mother soothing her beloved child, slowly stroked the trembling Subaru, saying—

"You're not an evildoer, but you think of yourself as a sinner. You're a kind kid, huh. Poor thing. Must be rough, huh."

"W-what... are you... exactly..."

There was no pain from his right arm, none from his shattered legs. No bleeding either. He couldn't understand. He couldn't accept her existence. The girl before him — the being he'd thought, until just moments ago, was someone he needed only to protect — was now close enough to touch, and yet hopelessly, impossibly far away. The girl tilted her head at Subaru's question, and said—

"Typhon is the Witch of Pride, y'know."

"Guh...!?"

At the shocking declaration, Subaru's thoughts ground to a halt once more. Any awareness of anger, of fear — all of it was completely blown away.

Just a moment ago, Subaru had been in the company of Echidna, the Witch of Greed. So why, all of a sudden, was he now face to face with the Witch of Pride? Witches who should have perished, who should already be dead, appearing one after another like this—

"—Hoo. Guess it's my turn next. Hah, I can't be bothered with this."

A languid voice fell from above, and all Subaru could manage was a strangled groan of shock. Once again, Subaru had only blinked. In that instant, the color of the world hadn't changed. His condition — missing an arm and legs — remained the same. And yet—

"Hah, you're heavy. Even with your arm and legs gone you should be a bit lighter, and still this. Hoo, this is why men are... well, men and women both are just lumps of waste to begin with, so maybe this is the better version."

The one whose body Subaru now leaned his entire weight against had changed from the girl who called herself Typhon to a different woman.

This time it was a languid, beautiful woman with reddish-purple hair grown to an extraordinary length. Her skin and lips were sickly pale. Her lowered eyes were narrowed — not so much sleepy as lacking any will to live — and even the way she breathed seemed a chore, spreading a gloomy, oppressive air all around her. She wore loose black priestly robes, but they were stained and frayed here and there, giving off a strong impression of someone who literally never changed out of the same clothes.

Looking down at the speechless Subaru, she let out another languid sigh and said—

"Hah, you really are unlucky, aren't you. Dragged around by that Echidna, and now meeting Typhon and me one right after another... Hoo, meeting three witches in a row like this, only that fool Flugel or that sword-swinging Reid would manage something like that."

"You're... a witch too? And that girl before, and Echidna..."

"Hah, I'm Sekhmet. Hoo, it's a bother, but people call me the Witch of Sloth, or don't, or whatever. Hah, I never asked to be called that — what a nuisance. Hoo, talking's a chore, so mind if I stay quiet now?"

"Give me a break. I feel like I'm losing my mind here. If I'm not talking to someone, I'm scared I'll actually see the reality of my situation. Please, just tell me what the hell is going on."

With his one working hand, his left, Subaru gripped her robe so as not to fall, and craned his neck to look up at Sekhmet. At his gaze, Sekhmet let out a sigh without hiding how bothersome she found it, then, her eyes still lowered as ever, said—

"Your right arm's gone from the shoulder, hah, and both legs from the knee. Hoo, this kind of handiwork's Typhon's, isn't it? That girl doesn't understand other people's pain. Hah, innocent and merciless, and still just a child through and through. Hoo, it's pitiful, really. Hah."

"My... arm, my legs... will they go back to normal?"

"Hoo, that I couldn't say... Ah, good timing, actually, hah. I was getting tired of this anyway, hoo, so I'll leave the rest to that girl and go take a nap. Hah, even breathing's a bother. Don't you ever think, if you could just pump a lifetime's worth of air into your lungs all at once, you'd never have to breathe again for the rest of your life? Hah."

"Do that and your lungs would just explode and you'd die. Forget that, what about my situation—"

Without ever dropping her languid pose, Sekhmet tried to lose Subaru in a haze of absurd remarks. Please, take this a little more seriously, Subaru was about to plead, when—

"——Did you just say the word 'die' in front of me?"

He heard a voice, cold and cutting.

Right before him, faced with a shock he'd lost count of by now, all Subaru could do was freeze. Once again, the person before him had changed. The witch with that immense volume of hair had vanished, and in her place, what filled Subaru's vision was—

"...Boobs?"

"——! W-where are you looking, huh, WHERE!?"

Trying to look up from the soft lap he lay on to see the person's face, Subaru's view was blocked by a large, jutting bust before it ever reached her face. The feel of the lap supporting his body was, unlike Typhon's or Sekhmet's, quite voluptuous — put plainly, a body rich with womanly curves.

Subaru experienced all of this quite literally with his entire body. That body of his was suddenly lifted by the arm of the voice's owner as it reached out — with one hand, effortlessly, even though Subaru, missing an arm and both legs or not, still weighed about as much as an adult woman.

"When you talk to someone, look them in the eye, the EYE! Honestly, men are always like this, it's unbelievable!"

The one venting her huffy anger as she spoke was a beautiful blue-eyed girl with wavy golden hair swaying about her. She was dressed head to toe in easy-to-move-in clothes, a short skirt among them, and even accounting for the fact she'd been sitting, she was quite short in stature. And yet, with her large bust and an overall abundance of womanly curves, she gave off an undeniably provocative air. Since her own manner was so brisk and straightforward, perhaps it was best described as a wholesome kind of allure.

She then gave the Subaru she'd lifted a single glance, her eyes tinged with anger. Before Subaru, left utterly behind by the situation and blinking in bewilderment, she swept back her bangs and said—

"Right arm missing. Both legs missing from the knee down. No bleeding, no pain... This is Typhon's punishment! That girl... doing something like this on her own again, it's just too much!"

Looking at Subaru's painless wounds, her blue eyes clouded with intense emotion. Her words carried anger, her manner carried fury, her every gesture carried passionate emotion — and yet, faintly welling in her eyes as she carried on like this, were tears.

"A-are you... crying?"

"I'm not crying! I'm just angry! That's right, I'm angry! At Typhon for inflicting a wound like this and leaving it be! At the world that makes that girl do such a terrible thing! At the sheer unreasonableness of people fighting, hurting each other, tormenting one another!!"

Raising her voice in a shout, shaking her head as if to fling her hair loose, the girl declared it all with fierce conviction. Then she raised her arm and, without warning, hurled the body of Subaru she'd been holding up into the air.

"Huh?"

"That's why I won't forgive it! Pain! Conflict! Wounds! As if I could just leave them be——!!"

The next instant, the girl's fist, thrown with enough force to tear through the wind itself, struck Subaru directly. Tremendous speed and power slammed into his cheek, and his body was sent flying as easily as a falling leaf, literally. But—

"Bwuh—!?"

He should have gone flying endlessly, but the edge of the world arrived almost at once. In this space, its boundaries limited by Echidna's hand, the distance the force of that punch could carry Subaru was only the barest sliver he was allowed. The shock of slamming into an invisible wall tore through his entire body, and Subaru hung there in midair as if plastered to it, his head spinning. And into that—

"——Don't think! You're getting home in one piece!!"

The girl came leaping after him and, on Subaru's body just beginning its fall, unleashed a further rain of blows. Fists danced wildly, driving into his body one after another, sandwiching Subaru's flesh between the invisible wall and her knuckles. Shock and sound bored into his entire body in unbroken succession, transmitting their force into the wall and beginning to shake the very foundations of the world.

Battered by the shockwaves, tossed about until he could no longer tell up from down or left from right, Subaru's mind went blank white. All that filled his vision were the fists driving into him and the face of the girl, her cheeks streaming with tears she no longer seemed to bother hiding. Watching the tears scatter glittering through the air, he tried to complain that he was the one who wanted to cry — and got another fist to the face for his trouble. An endless hell with no telling when it would end — and then its end came without warning.

"My fists will remake the world! My anger will cleanse the world! My wrath, the healing power of this fist — that's my answer——!!"

The next instant, the world abruptly shattered. The wall that had existed as if to hold Subaru in place, unable to withstand the shock of the girl's rain of blows — transmitted through Subaru himself — was smashed to pieces. At once, Subaru was freed from the sensation of being flung about.

The rain of fists stopped, and then came a soft sensation. Before he knew it, Subaru's body was laid out on the ground — and there, it was the very meadow where the tea party had been held, just before the space had sealed itself off.

Raising himself up and looking around, Subaru could only stare blankly. Landing gracefully beside him, the girl smoothed down her golden hair and glared sharply at Subaru, saying—

"Right hand!"

"Huh!? Ah, yes!"

Reflexively raising his hand at her call, Subaru then realized — the fact that his right arm, which should have been torn off at the shoulder, had regenerated completely, right down to the fingertips.

"Both legs!"

"Th-they're there. I can stand, I can walk, I can moonwalk!"

Springing to his feet at her next check, Subaru showed off a few steps on the spot, moonwalking in place. Watching Subaru glide backward across the green of the meadow, the girl crossed her arms and nodded with satisfaction. As she did, her ample bust swayed in a way that emphasized it, and Subaru burned the sight into his memory before saying—

"T-thank you, that saved me. But, going by how this has gone, you're also...?"

"I am Minerva, the Witch of Wrath! Though I'm hardly worth introducing!"

"You just introduced yourself!"

"Stop it, I haven't done anything so great! I just can't stand it when someone's being hurt, or already hurt, right in front of me, that's all! It's nothing worth being passed down through the ages!"

"And on top of it, she's decided on her own that what she does counts as some great deed! This girl's the type who doesn't listen to anyone — exactly the kind I'm bad with!"

Waving his newly healed arm to express his confusion, Subaru watched as Minerva spun on her heel and turned her back to him, saying—

"Anyway, now that your wounds are healed, there's nothing left for me to do! Don't you go getting hurt again, not even so much as an insect bite! It's a promise with a witch, understand!"

"I couldn't manage that even living in a sterile room! Don't go making promises on your own! And 'a promise with a witch' — breaking that definitely comes with some seriously heavy penalty, doesn't it!?"

"No such thing. It's just that, when that happens... I'll heal everyone."

"Don't phrase it like you're gonna slaughter everyone, that's terrifying!"

Even so, it was an undeniable fact that the missing parts of Subaru's body had been healed. A drastic cure — in this case, healing that came, quite literally, exactly as the word implied. To be healed by being beaten that badly was a mysterious phenomenon well beyond reason. Being healed by getting smacked around made him feel like an old television set or something.

"——Now then."

With that, the girl, who had seemed ready to stride briskly off somewhere, turned back around. White hair swayed with the motion, spreading prettily over black clothing — Subaru saw it. She tilted her head and looked over at him with obvious, deep amusement, saying—

"I had you meet the other witches to prove my own harmlessness — so, how was it? If your attitude toward me softens even a little, it'll have been worth waking them from their sleep."

Echidna, revealing the truth behind the disastrous experience Subaru had just been through. Before her, Subaru let out a long, deep breath, then slowly raised his head, and—

"You are, without a doubt, a witch. ...That's not how a human being thinks."

was all he could manage to spit out.

  • Title: 傲慢で怠惰な憤怒 literally reads "a wrath that is prideful and slothful," using the Witch of Pride's and Witch of Sloth's epithets as adjectives modifying "Wrath." Rendered as "A Prideful, Slothful Wrath" to preserve that structure as closely as possible.
  • The two katakana incantations Typhon murmurs (ツミハタダイタミニヨッテノミアガナワレル / トガハクサビトナッテケッシテノガサズ) are written in katakana in the original, marking them as strange, chant-like utterances rather than normal dialogue. I've translated their meaning ("Sin is atoned for only through pain" / "The transgression becomes a wedge, and never lets go") in italics but could not preserve the visual/phonetic oddity of the katakana rendering.
  • Minerva's line 「皆癒しにしてやる」("I'll heal everyone") is a pun on 皆殺しにしてやる ("I'll wipe everyone out") — the two phrases are near-homophones in Japanese. Subaru's reply plays off this near-miss ("Don't phrase it like you're gonna slaughter everyone"), but the pun itself doesn't carry over phonetically into English, only conceptually.
  • "Dona" (ドナ) and "Baru" (バル) are Typhon's childish nicknames for Echidna and Subaru; kept as informal name-clippings rather than translated/explained.
  • Reid Astrea's epithet 棒振り ("stick-swinger/sword-swinger") is rendered as "sword-swinging Reid" — a descriptive nickname, not a catalogued wiki term, so flagging for verification.
