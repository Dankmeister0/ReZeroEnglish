Arc 3 Chapter 65: "The Result of Sloth"

As the sound of laughter rang out around him, Subaru fought desperately to keep the shudder of dread from climbing up his throat, forcing his slackened thoughts to spin back up to full speed.

—It made no sense. Why was the woman standing before him calling herself Petelgeuse? He'd killed the man—blown his corpse to pieces, utterly. He should be dead. Were there multiple of them? Ridiculous. One unreasonable nightmare like the White Whale was more than enough!

"You bastard... what did you just say...!"

"Oh myyy, you do seem quite surprised, isss that so. If I can astonish you that much, then it was worth coming back like thisss!"

The woman snapped her fingers, then pointed at Subaru with the hand opposite the one she'd just been chewing on. A mad grin plastered across her face, she opened her mouth wide enough to show the back of her throat and ran her tongue over her lips.

In that spine-chilling smile, Subaru saw clear echoes of the madman he was certain he'd killed. The manner of speech, the way she laughed, and above all, the presence of the 'Unseen Hands'—

"Wait... you—you only had seven arms... didn't you...?"

As far as Subaru knew, the total number of Petelgeuse's black demonic hands had been seven.

At the very least, Subaru had never seen more than that, and even Petelgeuse—who should have gone all-out against Puck—hadn't manifested more arms than that.

But the number visible before him now easily surpassed seven, and the arms stretching out already numbered close to twenty.

"You... you can see my 'Unseen Hands'? Is that so."

Narrowing her eyes, the woman let the words slip out in a voice edged with danger.

In the previous world too, Petelgeuse had harbored a fierce hostility toward anyone who could see his demonic hands. That much apparently hadn't changed, even now that he'd become someone else entirely—if anything, Subaru's words seemed to fill her with even greater hatred than before, as she said:

"The answer to a love granted to me, and me alone... why should you be able to see it? Could such a thing be possible, isss it?! You there, disciple of favored love—could it be that you...!"

"Get away from Subaru-dono—you wretch."

A sharp voice suddenly cut in on the woman as her lips trembled.

Where the woman's bloodshot eyes glared, the one who had spoken was—

"Wilhelm...-san..."

Wilhelm, his throat seized by a black demonic hand, his feet lifted slightly off the ground.

Bound by the unseen arm, the old swordsman struggled to pry it loose, yet the fierce ferocity in his eyes hadn't dimmed in the slightest.

Should the 'Unseen Hand' ever release its hold on him even for an instant, the old man would close the distance in a single bound and send the woman's head flying, casting Petelgeuse back down to hell once more.

Before the overwhelming killing intent radiating off the Sword Devil, the woman—it still felt wrong to call her Petelgeuse, but Petelgeuse it was—twisted her face, which would have looked plain yet handsome as a girl's had she kept it still, into something hideous, and said:

"Ahh, you, you seem a diligent one, isss that not so. I understand. I understand indeed, how could I not understand?! Splendid—beautiful—oh, what a noble, unshakable spirit I sense in you! One who believes! One who sees it through to the end! Proof that something firm and unwavering has taken root within you! Goo—soooo good, isss it!"

"Stop spouting nonsense, madman."

"Madman! An entirely correct assessment! Yes, I am mad with love! Mad with reverent love, with bequeathed love, with compassionate love, with grateful love, with craving love, with gracious love, with esteemed love, with love for kin, with utmost love, with private love, with pure love, with cherished love, with affectionate love, with dear love, with trusting love, with deep love, with humane love, with carnal love, with regretful love, with desperate love, with exclusive love, with hateful love, with loyal love, with favored love, with meager love, with partial love, with blind love, with fraternal love, with pitying love, with love, with love, with lovelovelovelovelovelovelove loveee eeeeeee!!"

Eyes bulging, tongue lolling, back arched, Petelgeuse clawed at her own head.

Wilhelm regarded the mad display with eyes that seemed to be looking upon something abominable. Looking around, the other captives, their faces twisted in agony, shared the same revulsion at the madwoman's warped way of expressing love.

Standing at the center of that whirlpool of negative emotion, Petelgeuse repeated her twitching grin again and again, sweeping her gaze around the surroundings, and said:

"Ahh, how lonely it is not to hear the praise that ought to be sung, isss it not. ...Well then, shall I not spare a hand, and show you a bit more generosity!"

"No way—stop...!"

Raising both hands lightly, Petelgeuse made an absurd demand of the unresponsive crowd around her. Imagining the worst about what the madwoman intended, Subaru tried to call out to stop her—but her smile came far faster than his voice could.

"Now then—be 'Sloth.'"

In a form other than the worst Subaru had imagined, 'Sloth' ran rampant through the village as a calamity.

Until then, everyone who'd been immobilized had shared one thing in common despite their agonized expressions: they'd kept their voices suppressed. Part of that was likely a defensive instinct to avoid drawing Petelgeuse's attention, but the bigger reason was simply that their throats were being crushed.

The many outstretched arms gripped the throats of everyone they'd bound, lifting their bodies as though forcing them onto their toes. Breath wasn't cut off entirely, but it wasn't enough to allow the single breath needed to move for a counterattack—a truly vicious act.

And now, with Petelgeuse's single word as the trigger, that restraint was suddenly released—

"—AaaaaAAAAAH!!"

Throughout the village, the screams of those freed from the pressure on their throats overlapped and echoed.

There was a man drooling, smashing his head against the ground.

There was a woman, eyes rolled back white, beginning to foam at the mouth as her body refused to obey her.

There was a traveling merchant, covered in blood from self-inflicted wounds that outdid even Petelgeuse's own.

There was a knight, his mana running out of control within him, bleeding from every orifice in his body.

In the midst of a scene of carnage as though everyone had gone mad, screams and shrieks echoed throughout the village.

"This..."

It closely resembled the phenomenon that had occurred just a few hours earlier, during the battle with the White Whale.

The one where, on the fog-shrouded road, those who'd heard the White Whale's roar lost their sanity and collapsed where they stood, unable to move.

But this situation, triggered by Petelgeuse's single word, was far more severe than that one had been.

"You resisted more than I expected, isss it."

As she spoke, Petelgeuse tilted her head rapidly from side to side, watching with delight the crowd who kept up their throat-tearing screams.

Faced with such carnage, she looked ready to hum a little tune—leaving Subaru no room to doubt that this was one and the same being as that man. And yet—

"Why the hell are you... here..."

"We were in the middle of our conversation, after all, and I still hadn't carried out the Trial that was my reason for hurrying to this place. —Although, thanks to you all crushing nearly all of my fingertips, this feeble body is all that's left to me, isss it."

Speaking in a viscous, cloying tone, Petelgeuse ran a hand sensuously over the curves of her own body. That lascivious gesture produced nothing but revulsion in Subaru, who glared at the demonic hand still gripping him and refusing to let go, reaching out to try and pry it off somehow. But—

"Damn it, I can see it, but... I can't interfere with it?!"

"So it's true, you really can see the 'Unseen Hand,' isss it. Most unpleasant, I must sayyy, but if you are 'Pride,' then that would explain it..."

The madwoman observed the struggling Subaru, leaning toward him with keen interest.

Her judgment was that, with his body lifted off the ground and unable to move, there was no danger in approaching Subaru. And indeed, that judgment was correct. There was nothing Subaru could do.

—Subaru—

"Do it—Patrasche!!"

"Do wha—"

The instant she started to speak, the Earth Dragon, reacting to Subaru's call, lowered her body and slammed into Petelgeuse from the side, sending her flying with tremendous force.

The 'Unseen Hands' had reached out to grip the throats of every survivor present, but they had focused solely on restraining humans. They hadn't interfered with the Earth Dragons left behind near the village entrance, and Patrasche had been circling around to come at Petelgeuse from behind—Subaru, whose restraints happened to be looser than the others', had been the only one to notice, and he'd bought the time needed.

"Guh—!"

Even granting that this was a different body now inhabiting her, the frailty of its appearance was no less real.

The charge of an Earth Dragon weighing several hundred kilograms carried more than enough force to send a small frame like hers—one that had tried to dodge—flying with ease.

Knocked off her feet, Petelgeuse bounced across the ground and slammed into a nearby house. A cloud of dust rose, and the instant her figure vanished from sight, leaving a pained groan behind—the 'Unseen Hand's' grip released Subaru.

"It let go—now... Wilhelm-san!"

"...I've managed, somehow. But this..."

Rubbing the bruise that had formed painfully on his neck, Wilhelm—who had apparently escaped the effects of the mental contamination—looked around at their surroundings. The 'Unseen Hand's' grip had been released for the moment, but the mental contamination that had spread through the area hadn't let up.

Even now, those suffering continued to bleed and scream from self-harm, nightmares, and a fatal deterioration in their condition. If this was the same in nature as the fog the White Whale had emitted, then—

"It can't be treated without Ferris?!"

"Or perhaps it's the kind of curse that breaks if we strike down its source."

As Subaru clutched his head at the hopeless situation, Patrasche, who had just saved him from danger, pressed her muzzle against his shoulder as if to say, "Calm down."

Taking a deep breath at the Earth Dragon's gesture, Subaru looked at Wilhelm's profile as the old man drew his treasured sword, and said:

"Is there a chance that beating her would fix this? Worst case, maybe we should pull back and go get Ferris..."

"With Ferris here, it would certainly be reliable. But could you choose to withdraw and leave that thing here? If we retreat into the forest, there's no doubt where she'll head next—Roswaal-dono's mansion. —Danger would reach Emilia-sama."

"—!"

That much, at least, absolutely had to be avoided.

He didn't know why. He didn't know, but Subaru had a certainty that Emilia and Petelgeuse must never be allowed to meet.

If the two of them crossed paths, it would, without a doubt, spell disaster for Subaru.

That much, he would resolutely refuse to accept.

"It seems your resolve is set. Subaru-dono, please stand back and watch."

"Her whole trick is binding people with invisible hands. Even you, Wilhelm-san, against that..."

"Once one knows there are invisible arms, there are ways to fight them. More importantly, Subaru-dono, are you certain she is 'Sloth'? I do recall cutting down 'Sloth' myself."

"...I don't understand how, but I'm sure of it. Worst case, like with the White Whale, maybe this one's the real body and the one from before was a fake. I wouldn't have known that."

"I never doubted you."

Wilhelm gave a wry smile at Subaru's excuse-laden words. At the elder's magnanimous attitude, Subaru felt ashamed that even in a moment like this, his own smallness had shown through.

But before he could offer any reply—

"—Ahh, it hurts. It hurts. It hurts. My brain, it's trembling. Tremblingsss, isss it."

An arm stretched out from the collapsed wall of the house, and Petelgeuse, half her body stained with blood and mud, peered out. As she sluggishly crawled out, several deep lacerations were visible on that half of her body, vividly telling of just how much damage she'd taken from Patrasche's unguarded charge.

And yet, even so, not a trace of vitality had faded from her gleaming eyes—if anything, it only served to emphasize the grudge-laden madness within her all the more.

"Good. Earth Dragons truly are good, isss it. Familiar with humans, loyal, and above all, wise and diligent! Splendid! Adorable! Diligence! Yes, to be diligent! That is a virtue to be praised above all else, above anything else, isss it! If sloth is the most shameful of sins, then diligence is the most praiseworthy of virtues! If sloth is a faithlessness unworthy of trusting love, then diligence alone is the conduct befitting a disciple worthy of favored love!"

Waving her arms, shaking her head, stamping the ground, tongue lolling, drooling all the while, Petelgeuse showered Patrasche with unstinting praise, a mad grin spread across her whole face.

As for Patrasche, the one being praised, it seemed even the gulf between species couldn't make her accept such bizarre behavior—she showed as much revulsion as her hard-to-read reptilian face could manage, and pressed herself close to Subaru as if choosing him over the alternative.

A cute, dependable partner indeed.

"My hardworking partner here says she's not flattered, you pervert. ...Guess it's 'pervert broad' now? I get that there's a market for TS stuff, but who the hell wants your TS, you piece of shit."

"Tee-ess? I haven't the faintest idea what you're talking about, isss it, but whether the one I lavish with praise is pleased by it or not is of no concern to me! I found it splendid. This emotion welling up in my chest—to leave it unspoken would be a sloth I could never permit myself. It cannot be permitted, must not be permitted, permitting such a thing must never be allowed, isss it. And so I spare no words, love, love, love-love-love-love-love-loveee!!"

"One who cannot hold a conversation is troublesome in any age. —There's no longer meaning or purpose in wasting words. Wilhelm van Astrea, here I come."

Lowering his stance, Wilhelm gave his name and let his speed ride the wind.

In a single bound, the Sword Devil's body tore across the ground, closing in on the slow-moving Petelgeuse. But even she, it seemed, was wary of that opening strike.

"Ahh, how frightening. You too are a diligent one. And so, allow me to do this, isss it."

The 'Unseen Hands' welled up from the ground, covering the space in front of Petelgeuse like a wall. Charging straight in would mean being entangled, whole body, by an unavoidable demonic hand.

Wilhelm, unable to see it, had no way of knowing of that threat—so Subaru, straining his throat raw, shouted:

"Arms, straight ahead!! Fall back—!!"

"—Understood."

Slamming his right foot down to brake his momentum, Wilhelm dodged the wall closing in before him. Leaping sideways, the old swordsman spun his body, raised his drawn blade vertically, and—

"Tch—"

The ground, struck by a diagonal slash, split open, and a rain of dirt poured down toward Petelgeuse.

Had it been a strike meant to shatter the earth and smash its force into her, it might have carried some meaning as an attack, but—

"That's just harassment..."

"A meaningless act, isss it, but surely you aren't 'Sloth,' are you."

Apparently reaching the same conclusion about Wilhelm's actions, Petelgeuse let out a sigh, talking over Subaru's hoarse voice. Without answering her disappointed attitude, Wilhelm kept racing across the ground, his swung blade sending an unbroken rain of dirt pouring down.

Waving an arm carelessly at the irritating motion, Petelgeuse muttered, "That's quite enough, isss it," and said:

"Even if diligent, an incompetent hard worker is a being just as contemptible as sloth, isss it. If you still refuse to give up your futile resistance, there's nothing else for it—before the favored love bestowed upon me, be torn to pieces and vanissshh, vanissshh, vanisssshhhh, isssssss!!"

Shadows exploded outward, and Petelgeuse's 'Unseen Hands' surged toward Wilhelm.

Their number reached somewhere past thirty—incomparable to the Petelgeuse Subaru had known. Subaru, who had been trying to call out the approaching demonic hands one by one, found his throat seizing up in the face of a situation that calling out one or two of them wouldn't change.

And that fatal hesitation led straight to Wilhelm's life being on the line—

"Wilhelm-sa—"

The 'Unseen Hands,' which mercilessly destroyed anything they touched, closed in on the old swordsman's entire body—a threat he had no way to sense. Subaru had a vision of those fingertips touching down and trampling the old man's body, and nearly cried out at his own helplessness. But—

"I told you already."

Ducking to dodge the outstretched fingertips, spinning his body to evade the demonic hands raining down one after another, slipping like water through the gaps between the arms, the old man dodged every last bit of the surging threat, a combative smile rising on his cheek, and—

"—that once one knows there are invisible arms, there are ways to deal with them."

The Sword Devil had just proven, right before her eyes, that there was no falsehood in his earlier words.

"Impossible. Impossible, impossible, impossible-impossible-ssible-ssible-ssible-ssible-na-na-na-na-na-na—"

It was Petelgeuse whose throat trembled at Wilhelm's overwhelmingly stoic declaration.

The madwoman scowled at the fact that her trump card had been dodged with such ease, and as she raised her right hand and crushed its fingers one after another between her back teeth, she rolled her bloodshot eyes in every direction, and said:

"Impossible impossible impossible-possible-possible-ossible-ossible-ssible-ssible-ible-ible-ble-ble-e-e-e-e...... don't tell me even you can see my 'Unseen Ha-a-a-a-ands'?"

"I can see them. —Once the trick is known, it's nothing more than a cheap children's game."

Speaking with an air of boredom, Wilhelm swept the tip of his spun blade through the dirt once more.

The falling rain of dirt struck the still-trembling Petelgeuse, serving as the ultimate provocation to the madwoman.

But through the repetition of that act, Subaru realized what Wilhelm was actually aiming at. And he could only marvel at the Sword Devil's terrifying tactical eye.

Wilhelm hadn't been raining down dirt all this while simply to provoke Petelgeuse's short temper.

By raining down dirt, Wilhelm had created a situation in which the trajectories of the resulting 'Unseen Hands' could be seen visually.

The 'Unseen Hands,' which physically interfered with the human body, were a mysterious phenomenon that genuinely existed—they simply couldn't be seen. Most likely some technique interfering with mana was being deployed to produce them, Subaru guessed, but Wilhelm had turned that very fact against her.

In other words, he judged that wherever the rain of dirt broke off, an arm was passing through, and he faithfully avoided those spots. That said, even so, the onslaught produced by more than thirty arms wasn't something easily dodged in its entirety—it was a feat of god-like skill made possible only by Wilhelm's superhuman reflexes.

"Now then, you've been surprised quite enough. —Are you prepared to be cut down?"

Pointing the tip of his blade at her, Wilhelm intoned the threat in a low voice.

The freezing killing intent packed into those words sent a chill down even Subaru's spine, though the blade wasn't pointed at him directly. Naturally, Petelgeuse, who found herself directly exposed to that killing intent, must have felt even further from alive.

In fact, Petelgeuse, who until then had shown a mad demeanor as though she'd lost all sense of the boundary with reality, now seemed to regain that sense and flustered, said:

"Wait, wait, isss it. This shouldn't be. This situation, this turn of events, this predicament—it shouldn't be happening to me, isss it! I was diligent! I refused to be slothful, all to answer love! To love! To dear love! As a disciple answering favored love, I was more pious and diligent than anyone! And yet I..."

"'I did this, so I'm loved. If I just do this much, I'll be loved.' The shallowness of the love you speak of is enough to rot my ears."

Wilhelm cut through Petelgeuse's unsightly cries as though he'd just heard some ravings truly worth spitting on. In his eyes burned a tremendous sword-intent, distinct from mere killing intent, as he said:

"Love is not something to be forced, nor begged for, nor torn away by force. What you speak of isn't love—it's nothing but self-satisfaction."

"—What would you..."

Ignoring Petelgeuse as she started to speak, Wilhelm, as though this were the last stroke, swept up dirt in a great swing, showering Petelgeuse in the falling rain. But within that dark-colored rain, Petelgeuse carved a triumphant, mad grin, and said:

"Then how about thisss!!"

Shouting, Petelgeuse thrust both hands out toward them.

As if answering her arms, the 'Unseen Hands,' sliding across the ground, hoisted up the bodies of the knights who lay suffering from the mental contamination.

Every one of those sturdy men, clad in equipment matching Wilhelm's own, were survivors of the subjugation force—comrades bound to Wilhelm by unshakable ties. That was exactly what she had set her sights on.

Her eyes gleaming with madness, Petelgeuse opened her throat as though cheering her own act, and said:

"Now, how about this, isss it! With your friends, your colleagues, your subordinates, your comrades, your brothers-in-arms taken hostage like this, what sort of unsightly show will you—"

"—Don't underestimate a knight, you scum."

Leaving that murmur behind, Wilhelm's body shot forward like a bullet.

His planted foot gouged the earth, and in the blink of an eye, his aged body flew forward, leaving even time itself behind. Petelgeuse, her face slack with shock, made a snap decision to draw demonic hands from the shadows—but it was too late.

The blade, driven deep, pierced through her lower abdomen and jutted out from her back, and at close quarters, face to face, the two of them exchanged expressions.

One wore a face frozen in stunned shock, the other burned with an anger too quiet for words, and—

"I took up a sword to protect others, and the moment I became a knight, I stopped valuing my own life. Insulting my—my comrades has its limits."

"Ahh... this... can't..."

A trembling hand reached toward her own wound, touching the blood trickling down the blade, and her eyes went wide. Then Petelgeuse raised that blood-soaked hand toward Wilhelm's face, and the old swordsman didn't dodge it. He, who had cut down countless lives, could tell she no longer had the strength left to do anything at all.

"You... are the one... ahh... truly, genuinely, rightly diligent..."

As though cherishing him, Petelgeuse's fingertips patted softly at Wilhelm's cheek.

The old swordsman let the madwoman do as she wished, and slowly drew the blade of his treasured sword from her abdomen. Blood came pouring out in a great rush, and Petelgeuse, all strength drained from her, collapsed to her knees.

"Do you require a finishing blow?"

"—Unnecessary, isss it. Ahh, the sensation of a life slipping away is splendid. The blood drains out. The source of life flows away. The diligence that has supported my body until now—lost, gone, vanishing, vanishing, vanisssshing—ssshhh—ssshhh—shhh..."

Toppling onto her side, Petelgeuse watched with delight as the ground was further stained with blood. Gleefully, gleefully, her lips trembling with delight, her eyes lost their focus, and at last the light in them went out.

Having watched it through to the end, Subaru let out a breath—"ha." Faced with a battle so intense he'd forgotten even to breathe, his body seemed to have forgotten how to move at all.

"Is—is it... over?"

"Her breath has stopped. And the others, it seems, as well."

To Subaru's timid question, Wilhelm, wiping down the blade of his sword, answered quietly.

Looking around at his words, indeed, the people who'd been tormented by the mental contamination until moments ago had calmed down; though wounded, it seemed no one had died.

Even those last taken hostage still clung to life, if only barely.

It seemed that this time, at last, it was truly safe to feel relief.

"So in the end, what the hell was going on with her?"

He stamped his feet on the spot to loosen legs that had gone completely stiff, and once he confirmed they'd obey him, he turned toward Wilhelm.

In front of the old swordsman standing there, the fallen second Petelgeuse lay completely silent—if one looked only at her lifeless form, she seemed nothing more than the corpse of some unrelated woman.

But in reality, this woman had, until just a moment ago, been Petelgeuse.

That mad display, that Authority known as the 'Unseen Hand'—there was no way those had been some kind of illusion. Whatever had happened, he had to force himself to some satisfying conclusion about it.

"Like with the White Whale, are there multiple 'Sloths'? If so, that would explain the stories of her going wild all over the world up until now, but..."

"I've never heard of there being multiple Archbishops of Sin bearing the same sin... but those people's true nature has always been murky. I can't say for certain it's impossible."

Bringing up the White Whale had made even Wilhelm's tone drop slightly.

In truth, if that theory were correct, it would be a grave situation indeed. The mere possibility that multiple beings like Petelgeuse might exist was too terrible to even contemplate.

"Worst case, maybe even this woman wasn't the real body... like with the White Whale—could there be yet another true body out there? If we don't even know whether it's shown up this time, there's no way to deal with it."

"I'm also worried about the others searching the forest. Here, thanks to Subaru-dono, we were able to deal with the invisible arms, but over there, they have nothing."

"—! That's right! That's bad, we need to get back and regroup right away!"

There were two remaining groups of Witch Cult stragglers left in the forest. Subaru didn't want to think that another Petelgeuse might be lurking in either of them, but he couldn't rule out the possibility.

Meaning to turn right back, Subaru spun around to grab the reins of Patrasche, who was right nearby. But—

"Anyway, we need to get back right away... Patrasche?"

But Patrasche did not welcome Subaru as he reached out an arm to leap onto her. Quite the opposite—the Earth Dragon lowered her head slightly, letting out a savage growl as she glared at Subaru.

Her attitude was as if she were looking at a hostile enemy—

"Don't tell me... possession...?!"

Struck by that possibility, Subaru leapt back, dread still gripping him.

If that were true, then Petelgeuse might be capable of taking over even a being of a different species entirely, like Patrasche—

"Wilhelm-san! This is bad, that bastard's gotten into Patrasche—"

"Splat—"

With a splash, a nauseating stench of blood was flung across his face as he turned around.

"Huh?"

Stunned, doused in the lukewarm liquid, Subaru blinked, mouth hanging open in a daze.

Right before him, where the old swordsman should have been standing, there was no sign of him. No—that wasn't quite true either, but the shape was different from the one Subaru knew.

That tall frame, at least ten centimeters taller than Subaru, that finely honed body of Wilhelm's—above those broad shoulders, there was no neck at all.

A jagged, torn-looking cross-section was exposed, and beyond the torso that sprayed blood upward, Subaru could see a rising black haze writhing and dancing.

"Eh... heh... Un... seen... Hand...?"

He stared blankly ahead.

There before him was Patrasche, the one he'd suspected had been taken over by Petelgeuse.

Her neck too was violently torn off right before Subaru's eyes and flung aside with a heavy thud. Her massive body collapsed to the ground, a considerable tremor running up through the soles of his feet.

At the same moment, behind him, Wilhelm's torso toppled sideways as well—before and behind Subaru, the lives of the dependable companions who, just moments ago, had been sharing in victory, were both lost.

"This... eh? What... is... eh?"

His mind went completely blank, unable to form a single coherent thought.

He turned and looked down at the fallen Wilhelm. Everything above the neck was simply gone. There was no way to stop the surging blood, nothing to do but watch as the red stained the earth.

Suddenly, before he knew it, he'd pulled the treasured sword from Wilhelm's corpse.

Stretching his arm out as far as it would go with that long blade, he aligned its tip toward his own throat.

With effort, he somehow managed to steady his aim, and Subaru tilted his head.

"What... happened... anyway... isss it?"

He understood nothing.

Understanding nothing, he pulled his outstretched arm back with all his strength.

A hot sensation tore through his throat, and the strength drained from his entire body.

He understood nothing. He couldn't comprehend it. But there was one single thing he did understand.

—Ah, that he had died.

  • 剣鬼 rendered "Sword Devil" (standard fandom epithet for Wilhelm) and 大罪司教 rendered "Archbishop of Sin" — both common conventions but not present in the provided terminology list, flagged for wiki verification.
  • The extended list of "○愛" (kinds of "love") wordplay was translated as a series of "[adjective] love" compounds, with the climactic phonetic collapse into pure "ai" sound adapted freely into repeated/slurred "love" in English, since a literal phonetic match isn't possible.
  • 寵愛 was rendered contextually as "favored love"/"favor" — the exact referent (the Witch's favor vs. a more abstract "grace") is ambiguous in the source.
  • "TS" (a Japanese otaku genre term for gender-swap/body-swap fiction) was left untranslated as slang, matching the joke's register.
  • The single instance of Subaru's own line ending in "isss it" (his final line before the cut) mirrors Petelgeuse's tic in the source text — likely a deliberate, ominous signal not fully explained within this chapter.
