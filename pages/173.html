Arc 4 Chapter 7: "Testing Grounds"

The instant the thug's foot came down and dug into the ground, Subaru had the illusion that the world had tilted. In reality, no such thing should have been possible. The weight of the earth itself was not so cheap a thing that its very foundations would shake merely because something the size of a human had stamped upon it.

Therefore, the world tilting was Subaru's illusion. In truth, the ground had simply peeled up starting from Garfiel's sole, and by the principle of a flipped tatami mat, the Dragon Carriage had been sent bouncing upward along with it.

"No—way—that's not—!"

Directly across from the improvised seesaw, the sensation of being thrust violently upward reached its peak, and wrapped in a feeling of weightlessness, the carriage slid through the air. A combined weight easily exceeding a ton, including Patrasche and the other Earth Dragons, went flying, and Subaru, inside the cabin, could do nothing but pull the Emilia he'd been holding in close against him. The carriage slammed back down onto the ground, groaning violently throughout its frame as the impact reverberated within and without. It was a high-quality carriage. Its luxury wasn't limited to outward splendor alone—it was proof of superior livability and durability, and that quality was put fully to the test, sparing it from total destruction. But a carriage tipped half onto its side was hardly in a position to simply drive off.

Which meant, in turn, that against whatever being had just accomplished such a feat, the option of flight had been closed off, and there was nothing left but to face it head-on.

"Damn it, what the—!"

Shaking his head, Subaru pressed a hand to his throbbing forehead, still poking out from the driver's seat. His head had struck something hard in the fall and ached faintly, but fortunately there was no sign of a cut or bleeding. On sudden thought he looked down—Emilia in his arms seemed unhurt as well. Relief welled up at that, but it was quickly replaced by mounting alarm as he remembered the cause of all this. Anxious, he raised his eyes—and there, before him, was the very sight he'd feared.

"Patrasche—!!"

Baring its fangs with a whinny, the jet-black Earth Dragon launched its massive body at the small, slight figure. Taking advantage of the loosened coupling to the carriage, Patrasche had twisted free of her restraints and, with swift agility, attempted to counterattack the assailant. Her sharp fangs held an edge like a blade itself, and the strength of her jaws was more than enough to shear through flesh and crush bone in a human body. Riding a gust of speed, she aimed for the base of Garfiel's neck, moving to tear it clean off without hesitation. But—

"Now that's a decision that'll numb you good. Good Earth Dragon... nah, ain't you a good woman, huh? 'The sound of breaking bones is proof of love, too'—well said, whoever said that."

"————!"

Into the jaws closing to bite clean through, the tip of an outstretched arm had been thrust. Garfiel had deliberately offered his right hand for Patrasche to swallow. The dragon, taking the bait as intended, clamped down on the arm, meaning to gnaw off its tip and then lunge for the body—that was how it should have gone. And yet Patrasche could not move. Not only her body—even the jaw holding the arm in her mouth did not so much as twitch.

Had some special power been used, or was it magic? As that question raced through Subaru's mind, Garfiel casually supplied the answer through action alone. Namely, the muscles of the arm thrust into her mouth swelled, and with raw strength that simply outmatched the force of her jaws, he pried her mouth open.

"Not bad, you. Actin' fast was good, and not givin' up even now is even better. Pass."

"————!"

The jet-black Earth Dragon lowered her body, twisting at the hips even as her mouth remained pinned. With the man's right arm sealed within her jaws, her tail whipped around with a roar, aiming to blast his face clean off. It was the same tail strike Subaru himself had once taken, but seeing Patrasche move now made it instantly clear just how much she'd been holding back before. This literal all-out blow carried enough hostile force to easily knock a human body flying. And yet even this was stopped effortlessly by Garfiel's left hand, thrust into its path.

A dry, cracking sound rang out, and after the impact, the tip of the tail remained caught in his left hand. With his right hand pinning her head and his left pinning her tail, Garfiel bared his fangs like a beast and grinned.

"Ain't gonna hurt you none. Just take a lil' nap."

Swinging his arm in a great arc, the circular motion sent Patrasche's massive body sliding through the air as easily as a joke. Spun sideways on the spot, with a look of bewilderment in her eyes at a weightlessness even she had never imagined, Patrasche was gently thrown down onto her side on the ground. There was barely even a tremor as her huge body struck the earth—and in that silence, Patrasche was defeated. Faced with this sight he could scarcely believe, Subaru's throat, gone dry, managed only a rasp.

"Y-you threw Patrasche...?"

"She's an honest sort. Threw her nice and gentle, so it don't hurt none. Better to finish it before she gets back up anyway!"

Talking right over Subaru's shock, Garfiel, corners of his mouth lifting, leapt lightly up onto the driver's seat. There, on the tilted platform, Otto had lost his footing; he sprang up as if flying the instant the attacker climbed aboard.

"Ngh... but don't underestimate me! Even I'm a merchant, for what it's worth! I keep in mind the possibility of being attacked by thugs in the course of business. Now then, if you'd rather not fall prey to the Suwen family style of thug-repelling technique, I recommend surrendering at on—gah!"

"You're loud, you damn amateur. No way you're beatin' me with some half-baked technique. Take a nap."

Just as he struck a spirited fighting pose, Otto's forehead was flicked—by a finger, Garfiel having strolled up to him at an unhurried pace—and he crumpled. That flick—more like a simple forehead-flick, really—nonetheless produced a sound of tremendous force as it sent Otto's thin frame toppling backward onto the driver's seat. Judging by the sight of him clutching his forehead, writhing in silent agony, his life was probably not in danger—but with that, there was nothing left standing between them.

"Well now. Looks like you two're all that's left."

Snorting, the thug murmured, his gaze sharp enough to slice through them where they stood. The distance from him, standing on the driver's seat, to Subaru was roughly four long strides—but to a man who had closed the distance to Otto in a single unnoticed instant, that was as good as no distance at all.

Holding his breath, Subaru cast about for a way out, but nothing came up in the search results. Of their current forces, the only one who might stand a chance against the man before him was Emilia, but she remained unconscious, felled by some unexplained collapse. And above all, he had to protect her.

"I..."

"'No matter how you flip it, still blue underneath'—not listenin' to that!"

The instant he heard the light tap of a foot against the floor, in the blink of an eye Garfiel's small frame appeared right before Subaru's eyes. The raised arm had its five fingers spread wide, and the future in which he was effortlessly torn apart by those five descending claws came easily to mind. And against that vision of his own body being scattered to pieces, there was only one thing Subaru could do. Just make sure the damage didn't reach the Emilia in his arms—shield her with his own body, and nothing more.

"————!"

—Had a few seconds passed? Eyes shut, shielding Emilia with his whole body, Subaru felt something strange in how unnaturally long the time before the expected impact was stretching. Cautiously opening his eyes, he found the five spread fingers before him, motionless. And beyond them, the thug, eyeing him with a dubious look. He looked down at Subaru, who'd unconsciously stopped breathing, and cracked his neck loudly.

"Not fightin' back, prioritizin' protectin' the woman—the hell's that about. If you go down, she's gettin' dragged down with you either way. Ain't that a bad call?"

Hit with sound logic he had no wish to hear, Subaru found himself speechless in more ways than one. As if further irritated by his silence, Garfiel waved his claw-tipped palm dismissively.

"And on top of that, slow gettin' back up. Bein' able to move at all means you ain't hopeless, but... no denyin' you're pretty damn useless."

"Y-you..."

"Ah?"

Garfiel handed down harsh judgment on points Subaru didn't even follow. Somehow forcing his frozen throat to work, Subaru tried calling out to him. At once, twisting his already vicious expression into further displeasure, Garfiel leaned his face in close.

"Can't hear you. Say it louder. The hell you want."

"You're Garfiel—right? The one Roswaal and Frederica know."

"—Frederica?"

At Subaru's confirmation, the aggressive intent vanished from Garfiel's expression for the first time. He blinked, bare-faced, with a glimpse of the kind of charm you'd see in a Carnivorous Beast that had forgotten about blood for a moment—then quickly masked it again with a bitter expression.

"Why's that name comin' outta— no, wait a sec. That woman you're holdin', that's the silver-haired... half-demon?"

"Half-Elf. Don't talk like that in front of her."

"—Hah. What, what, suddenly gettin' all fired up on me?"

Looking down at Emilia, the moment that slur left his mouth, Subaru's tone exploded. Pure anger came first, all his former fear gone, and hearing it, Garfiel gnashed his teeth together with obvious delight.

"So that means this here's the famous Lady Emilia, huh. Ain't no half-demon comin' 'round here these days 'cept through Roswaal, after all."

"You..."

Ignoring what Subaru had just said, and deliberately stressing the word "half-demon" again, Garfiel prompted Subaru to rise—but he was stopped short, forestalled by the man's outstretched palm.

"'A fool who bites hot iron just gets burned.' You can't beat me. Know the gap in strength and behave yourself. —Don't wanna get hurt, do you?"

Making a fist with his outstretched palm, he cracked its knuckles menacingly at Subaru. The difference in fighting strength between them was plain, and with no way of knowing how the man would act, needless resistance would only worsen the situation. The wise move here was to swallow his anger and wait for a chance at revenge later. That was, without question, the correct and clever judgment.

So, naturally—

"Go to hell."

"Ah?"

"I don't wanna get hurt. I'll probably get the crap kicked out of me by you. But—that's still no reason to let some insult that'll hurt this girl slide."

Gently laying Emilia down in the cargo bed of the carriage, he brushed the hair from her forehead with his fingers. Then, standing, Subaru glared at Garfiel, who watched him quietly, at a distance close enough their foreheads might collide. Close enough to feel his breath. Close enough for his hand to reach.

"Take back that stupid nickname, and never use it again."

"...You want me to listen to you, you're missin' a whole lotta somethin', ain't you? Side of the face, gut, shins—want me to work over the whole lot, huh?"

"Try it. I won't go down for free. I'll bite the hand that punches my face, cling to the arm that punches my gut, spit on the foot that kicks me, and land at least one hit back."

Answering threat with threat, Subaru gave in to the passion boiling in his chest. Before him, Garfiel's quiet hostility sent chills coursing through his whole body. Literally, if the man felt like it, Subaru would be swatted aside in an instant. The exchange just now had made that much clear.

Garfiel was an individual who held enough combat power to rank among the outrageous powerhouses Subaru had encountered in this other world so far. Not quite reaching Reinhard at the summit, perhaps, but reaching into the tier just below him, alongside Wilhelm and Julius. Even the pitifully small retaliation Subaru had voiced stood almost no chance of actually being carried out. And yet, Subaru still met Garfiel's gaze head-on. Knowing he would lose was no reason to back down. The reason he mustn't back down was the one he carried behind him.

"—Hihaha."

"—Ah?"

It was a strange, meaningless burst of breath that suddenly slipped out. In the midst of their standoff, it escaped as if let loose, cutting abruptly into the space between them. As Subaru let out a suspicious sound wondering what it was, Garfiel moved in response.

"Hihahaha! Now that's a good line, ain't it! Turns out you got it in you after all!"

"What are you—ow! Wait, what, ow, hey, that hurts!"

Shaking his shoulders with great laughter, Garfiel began slapping Subaru's shoulder hard, over and over. It was an act utterly divorced from hostility or aggressive intent—pure, unrestrained contact—but with no holding back at all, it did nothing but grind down Subaru's stamina gauge.

"Good, you pass. I'll let you through. Half-demon... Half-Elf, I still don't like it, but if you're gonna get that worked up guardin' your woman, I'll give you credit for havin' guts."

"Glad you corrected yourself, but—also, I said that hurts! How long are you gonna keep hitting me, are you trying to kill me?!"

Shaking off Garfiel's still-unrelenting arm, Subaru stepped back lightly to put some distance between them. Seeing this, the thug cocked his head, then crossed his brawny arms.

"Cold. Just let bygones be bygones already, forget about it. A man with a small vessel's got small everything else too, y'know?"

"That's the first idiom-sounding thing you've said that actually rings a bell, but mind your own business! Any—way!"

Shaking his body in small motions, Garfiel jabbed both fingers at Subaru in a lock-on gesture. Tilting his chin up slightly, he faced Subaru, who pressed on with the same momentum, voice rough.

"You're Garfiel, an acquaintance of Roswaal's, right? I got spooked by the sudden confrontation, but we're good now—no more hostile intent, right?!"

"Quit hollerin' 'peace' at me, you're noisy. Ain't gonna eat you up even if you don't hurry."

"Who's supposed to believe that after the way you were acting a second ago...?"

Sticking a finger in his ear, looking irritated, Garfiel nonetheless conceded to Subaru's protest with an "I guess that's fair" sort of attitude. There were parts of his thinking that were hard to read, but Subaru relaxed somewhat at the realization that communication wasn't impossible. Then, remembering he'd just escaped a crisis, he spoke up.

"Th-that's right... this isn't the time! Emilia suddenly collapsed. She was talking normally just a second before."

"Collapsed, that Half-Elf did? Well, o' course she did. The hell d'you think this place is? Nothin' to panic over."

Rushing to Emilia's side, Subaru checked her expression—her breathing, even now, remained ragged as she slept. As Subaru worried over her pained state, Garfiel merely shrugged with a faintly mocking air. At that knowing look, Subaru demanded, "What do you mean by that?" Garfiel, brow furrowing in puzzlement, replied,

"You asked Roswaal and Frederica what this place was, didn't you? Then this oughta be expected... don't tell me you don't know?"

When Subaru shook his head before even getting into an explanation, Garfiel clicked his tongue irritably. Muttering under his breath something like "that damn pervert..."—leaving little doubt who he meant—he bit back the rest of the curse.

"So Frederica didn't say nothin' either. That wicked woman—guess after not seein' her for a while, she's gone and picked up her master's rotten habits. Hopeless."

Shaking his head, Garfiel let his irritation out in a rough huff through his nose. Then, noticing Subaru's questioning look, he raised a hand lightly and said, "Fine, fine,"

"Looks bad, but there's nothin' life-threatenin' about it. But if you don't wanna keep seein' that pained face, best you get outta here quick. I'll guide you to the village."

"If we leave here, will she wake up?"

"That's what I just said, ain't it. C'mon, let's move. Oi, how long you plannin' on sleepin', get up."

Apparently having no intention of explaining beyond his woefully insufficient explanation, Garfiel turned back without hiding his rough manner, headed for the driver's seat, and kicked the still-fallen Otto. Taking the kick, Otto—still writhing in agony—let out a pained yelp.

"You're the driver, ain't you? I'll fix the carriage bein' tilted, so it's on you to get us to the village. Keep dawdlin' and I'll kick your ass in till it caves."

"Wait, what even is this situation?! Going by how this conversation's gone, I feel like I got completely shafted here!"

Boiling over at an argument that was, frankly, unreasonable in the extreme, Otto stood and spoke up to Garfiel. Considerable courage, given the beating he'd just taken from the man moments before—but seeing this, Garfiel, seemingly of the same opinion, turned to Subaru.

"Hey. This guy always this lively?"

"I'd really rather you not ask him for an evaluation of me, since I doubt it'll be fair! Look at me with your own eyes and judge me as I am! Anyway, apologize! Apologize to me!"

"Ah?! The hell, all of a sudden gettin' all peppy on me, you mockin' me?! I just went a bit overboard hittin' someone I didn't need to hit, cut me some slack!"

"Both of you shut up! Emilia-tan's still suffering this whole time, so hurry up and take the reins already! And you, get the carriage back upright!"

The three men set to squabbling noisily, hurling insults at each other atop the driver's seat. Left in the cargo bed, Emilia still did not wake from her still-unexplained loss of consciousness. And yet, her fine features furrowed faintly at the brow, as if pained by the noise, and in a small voice, murmuring as if in her sleep, she said,

"...Too loud."

"Come to think of it, never introduced myself properly. I'm Garfiel... ah, just Garfiel's fine. The strongest man there is. Nice to meetcha."

"Right, I'm Natsuki Subaru... eh? What was that just now? Did you say strongest? With a straight face?"

Facing each other in the cargo bed of the now-moving carriage, Subaru and Garfiel exchanged introductions. Shaking the offered hand, Subaru found himself taken aback, and Garfiel, in turn, gave him a puzzled look.

"Oh, I said it. Somethin' weird about that?"

"No, it's just, honestly, I never expected there to be someone who'd come right out and say 'I'm the strongest' to your face. Even so, isn't that a pretty big sign to be carrying around?"

"You sayin' I ain't fit to be the strongest?"

"I'll admit you're probably crazy strong, but when it comes to arguing over whether you're the strongest... there's this one guy who just keeps flickering into my mind no matter what."

Picturing the red-haired knight, Subaru mentally compared him against the Garfiel before him. Judging by their exchange just now, Garfiel too was a formidable bare-handed fighter, but surely he couldn't blow away a house with a single kick—no, wait, Garfiel had just caused the ground itself to cave in and flipped the carriage over with a single step. In terms of sheer absurdity, maybe he really was in the same league as that Sword Saint. Even thinking that through, the image of Reinhard's superiority still refused to fade in Subaru's mind. Even he himself didn't understand why he held that one knight in such special regard.

"Hah, well, whatever. That mistaken idea o' yours, I'll prove you wrong with my own two hands and overturn it eventually. For now, just think of me like some 'red-nosed, cold-sensitive reindeer' or whatever."

"I have no idea what you just told me to think of you as..."

Tilting his head at yet another expression that failed to land, Subaru got no explanation—Garfiel apparently had no intention of clarifying, crossing his arms behind his head and leaning back against the seat to relax. With the conversation stalling, Subaru gazed out the small window, and, taking advantage of the lull, idly combed his fingers through the silver hair of the still-sleeping Emilia on his lap. Though still unconscious, her expression had grown more peaceful than before. As Garfiel had claimed, moving away from that place did seem to be having a good effect, at least. Thinking on that, though, something else nagged at him.

"Hey, sorry I didn't get to ask before, but you're an acquaintance of Roswaal's... right?"

"You've at least heard my name before, ain't you? Might add 'supposedly' in front of it, but among Roswaal's associates, I'm the strongest, hands down."

"Not really following... I recall hearing something about an 'influential figure,' though."

Could it be that he'd been called an "influential figure" simply because of his combat strength? If so, then Garfiel, contrary to Subaru's expectations, wasn't a political ally so much as a muscle-headed one. Having managed a friendly encounter with someone who, on the approach to the "Sanctuary," should have been treated with caution, was a stroke of luck—but the fact that it had also added to his list of headaches was a concern all its own.

"Now I've got even more questions to ask Roswaal about the 'Sanctuary.' I'm supposed to be working toward solving problems, but it feels like all I'm doing is adding more of them. What's going on here?"

Holding his head in his hands, Subaru's expression clouded at the thought of yet more difficulty added to an already difficult road ahead. But hearing this, Garfiel gave a small click of his tongue, sharp canines peeking out faintly, and said,

"The 'Sanctuary'—huh."

At the meaningful mutter, Subaru looked up, and Garfiel gave a small wave of his hand. Then he stood, turning to face the direction they were headed—that is, toward their destination, the "Sanctuary"—and said,

"You're just swallowin' whole whatever Roswaal told you, callin' it that. Not knowin' is one thing, but not tellin'—that's some shitty business."

"I honestly agree with you, but badmouthing someone behind their back is a level of bad character we don't need to get into, so let's not... anyway, did something rub you the wrong way?"

Garfiel had clearly grown irritable the moment the word "Sanctuary" came up. Subaru checked to see if he'd said something wrong, but the man's response was blunt and to the point. Namely, forming an ill-fitting, sardonic smile on his lips, he said,

"'Bout time for the princess to wake up, I'd reckon. We've gotten pretty far from the barrier by now."

"Barrier, what barrier—oh, Emilia-tan?"

Before Subaru could voice his confusion, he noticed Emilia stirring in his lap and called out to her. Her eyes fluttering faintly open, she looked around the inside of the carriage with a hazy gaze. Still not fully conscious, she looked up at Subaru with those violet eyes and said,

"Morning, Subaru..."

"You're super cute even half-asleep, but this isn't the time, Emilia-tan. Does anything feel off? Any headache?"

"Umm, not at all? Nothing feels weird...!"

Whether it was fully waking up mid-answer, Emilia sat up abruptly, and Subaru hastily jerked his head back. The two of them narrowly avoided a head-butt, but Emilia, seemingly unaware of the close call, turned to Subaru.

"A-are you okay, Subaru? I said I'd protect you and then I just collapsed...!"

"No need to worry about that, I managed to get through it somehow! The fruits of dialogue-based contact, right there. People really can connect through the communication tool called words—and here we are, taking that very first step. Even though I've got social anxiety."

Touching Emilia's shoulder to calm her as she leaned in close, Subaru observed her state while saying whatever came to mind. She could stand and walk, her eyes and complexion looked fine, and there was no slurring in her speech. Also, super cute. Same as always.

"See? Told you so, didn't I."

As if waiting for Subaru's relief to settle, Garfiel laughed. At that, Emilia seemed to notice for the first time that there was now an unfamiliar presence in their midst, startled, and quickly positioned herself as if shielding Subaru from behind, facing him.

"—Who are you?! I'll have you know, I won't let you lay a finger on Subaru."

"Emilia-tan, it's fine, really! And please, for the love of god, stop making statements that keep cementing my position as the heroine here! My gauge is about at its limit!"

Clinging to her from behind to disarm her defensive stance, Subaru turned back to Garfiel and gestured at him.

"That's Garfiel. Right after Emilia-tan collapsed, he attacked—er, I mean, boarded the carriage. Not exactly a welcoming party, but for now he's accompanying us to the 'Sanctuary.'"

"Garfiel... this is him? The one Frederica mentioned?"

"I'm curious what she said about me, but let's save that for later, yeah? 'Cause we're just about to reach the village."

To Emilia, feeling the same sort of realization Subaru had felt earlier, Garfiel gave no time to sort through the situation, jerking his chin forward. As he indicated, ahead the forest seemed to open up, and the outline of their destination village was coming into view—

"Welcome, Lady Emilia and company."

Honorific attached—though there was not a shred of respect or goodwill in it, laced instead, more than anything, with a thick tinge of contempt. Unconsciously, Subaru's gaze sharpened, while Emilia's expression clouded with confusion. Under the weight of both their stares, Garfiel, still not dropping his manner, spread both arms wide.

"To what Roswaal so grandly calls the 'Sanctuary'—the end result of a dead-end testing ground where a ragtag bunch of half-breeds live out their days."

"Testing ground...?" "Half-breeds—"

As Subaru and Emilia each fixated on a different part of that, Garfiel raised a hand to his mouth, where his prominent canines showed, and laughed as if to mask some deeply complicated emotion.

"Us folk who live here, we call it the 'Witch of Greed's Graveyard.' Ain't that a laugh, huh. Yeah. Oi."

A self-mocking laugh echoed faintly through the carriage. Low, quiet—like a curse, and like a blessing—it rang on.

Listening to it, Subaru simply, quietly, worried for the girl beside him, and thought. If the Witch meant to obstruct her path once again, then it had to be himself who scattered the sparks that came flying from it—he told himself so, firmly, forcefully, as if to drive the thought home.

The "Sanctuary" draws near.

—The place that will greatly change the path of both Natsuki Subaru and Emilia.
