Re:Zero EX: "Repeating Life in an Alternate World from Zero"

This is the annual April Fools' project! This story is not canon, but an IF world tale! The content branches off from Chapter 74 of Arc 4 of Re:Zero − Starting Life in Another World, so please be careful if you haven't read up to that point yet.

Please note that this story takes place in a world completely different from the main canon, so character relationships and other elements have changed dramatically. Please enjoy only if you understand and accept this!

A dream—he was having a dream.

A dream that wouldn't fade, a dream repeated over and over, an endless dream, a dream he wouldn't allow to end—

Over and over, layering upon itself; over and over, starting anew; over and over, erring; over and over, setting things right. Woven a thousand times. Connected ten thousand times. Exceeding a hundred million times. Before he knew it, he had forgotten how to count.

There was agony, there was shock, there was confusion, there was ruin, there was hatred, there was madness. Worn down, shaken, twisted, decadent, driven to lamentation, his heart shattered to pieces.

Even so, there was a place he wanted to reach. Even so, there was a wish he wanted to protect.

So that even if no one knew the shape of the tragedy repeated and layered, he would not forget it. Even if no one else ever noticed, so that he alone could never forget.

—Even if the person he wanted to save wept, he still wanted to save them.

Therefore, he had no regrets about taking that hand, even now. If he had anything to regret, it was his own weak, fragile heart that failed to reach the strength of steel, holding hesitation and doubt before taking that hand—and that vexed him.

—When awakening from a hollow dream, the first thing he always felt was a dull headache.

"――――"

Opening his eyes while resisting the fingertips of sleepiness, he blinked several times to bring his consciousness to the surface. Vacantly, an ambiguous fog remained over his mind, but his poor blood circulation lasted for only a few seconds. Soon, his consciousness detached from sleep, and awakening brought vitality to his body.

"Ah, aaah..."

In contrast to his body's awakening, what leaked from his mouth was a drawn-out voice. At first glance, it might seem like someone groggy from sleep, but this too was one of his important morning rituals. Lying flat on his back, making this sound allowed a stream of information to flow into him.

The tone of his voice, the establishment of self-awareness, the safety of his limbs, the organization of his memories, the execution of his daily routine, the presence or absence of his life—

By performing a fixed set of actions every single morning without fail, all of those things could be verified.

That was the proof that Natsuki Subaru had welcomed the morning without incident.

"Phaaah."

With a yawn, he pushed off the blanket and swung his legs down, bringing his upper body up. Scraffily scratching his head, he looked around at the familiar room—the interior of his own room, furnished with somewhat luxurious furniture and a canopy bed, came into view. However, where Subaru woke up was not the canopy bed, but on the sofa placed in the back of the room. Wrapping himself in the blanket there and curling up to spend the night was Natsuki Subaru's sleeping habit of late—no, for the past few years.

That said, there was no grand reason for not sleeping in the bed. He was simply agonizing over trying to prepare even a slightly better sleeping environment. As a result of such trial and error, he learned that he felt a greater sense of relief sleeping on the sofa than on the bed. Ever since, he had kept doing it. That was all there was to it.

"――――"

Rubbing his eyes, he stepped off the sofa he used as his bed and headed to the washroom to get ready. Washing his face in the washroom adjacent to the bedroom, he reflected his wet face in the mirror and carefully inspected his features. A face with lingering exhaustion and eyes that somehow suggested a sense of desolation. Coupled with his sloppily slackened cheeks, he looked utterly drained, lacking all three essential qualities. To tighten up that mug, Subaru slapped his own cheeks with all his might using both hands. At the dry sound and numbing pain, tears welled up, and he doused his face with cold water as if to wash them away. Then, reflecting his wet face in the mirror again, he thoroughly kneaded his expression, his gaze, and his cheeks, praying inside:

"Smile, me. If you can't, die."

Reciting the magic spell, Subaru then contorted his mouth into a sinister, foul-tempered smile. A smile showing white teeth and narrowing his sanpaku eyes—the villainous face he had been acquainted with for over eighteen years. His eyes, his expression, his complexion—he was able to 'mimic' them all without issue.

"Aright, good, keep it up."

Checking that smile, Subaru wiped his face with a towel and quickly set about changing his clothes. When spending time in the manor, Subaru's attire was not a servant's uniform—it was somewhat high-class formal wear for noblemen. Even so, he had taken off the stiff jacket and rolled up the sleeves of his white shirt, dressing down casually. However, such externally appropriate attire was now required of him as a matter of course. Though it was thoroughly suffocating, it couldn't be helped. Wearing clothes appropriate for his standing was a duty, and it was a position Subaru himself had desired and obtained.

"It's about time for the usual hour."

Having finished dressing, he looked up at the Magic Clock Crystal installed above the room's door and muttered. Glowing a deep green, it indicated the arrival of morning and that the scheduled wake-up time was close at hand. It seemed he had spent a bit too much time forming his smile this morning. In just a few minutes, a girl meticulous about time would knock on his door right on schedule. Before that, he had to finish what needed to be finished.

"――――"

Switching gears mentally, Subaru parted the chest of his shirt. Hanging there, unremoved even while sleeping, was a pendant connecting a 'black crystal' with a thin chain. Grasping the darkly shimmering black crystal in his palm, Subaru closed his eyes. The cold, hard feel of the crystal stone—once wrapped in Subaru's palm, it immediately increased its presence as though holding heat, beating like a living creature.

Naturally, his unconscious mind began counting the rhythm and number of those strange beats in his palm. A crystal stone beating faster and higher than a pulse after running at full speed. Before long, those pulsations overlapped with Subaru's own heartbeats, until the beat of his heart and the pulse of the crystal stone became identical. When his brain understood that, Subaru's consciousness was released from the shackle of his flesh, drawn into a different space that could not exist in reality.

In an instant, sound and presence vanished from the world, and instead, a dazzling light wrapped around his consciousness.

"――――"

His consciousness, painted over in light, was slowly guided to awakening. Opening his closed eyelids, what poured down was sunlight so bright it stung his eyes. And spreading before his blinking eyes was a magnificent grassland swept by the wind.

A vast, endlessly stretching green field. A sea of grass extended as far as the eye could see, and the clusters of short grass swayed softly in the gentle breeze. Overhead in the sky was a cloudless, clear day, and the pouring sunlight sparkled like jewels. The blue of the sky and the green of the earth continued endlessly, faintly intersecting at the far horizon. A perpetual silence suited for a phantasmal world lacking reality rested there.

Grand, clear, and empty space—yet in such a world, there was a single incongruous detail.

"――――"

Standing in the center of the grassland, Subaru turned around. Directly behind him was a gentle slope leading to a knoll. Atop the hill was a modest flower garden, and right beside those lovely flowers stood a parasol catching the sunlight and a white table.

"――――"

Silently, Subaru walked up the hill and stepped beneath the parasol. On the white table rested a steaming cup, its ceramic bowl warmly filled with an amber liquid. Two served cups, and white chairs placed across from each other at the table. Without checking to see if one was prepared for him, Subaru sat in a chair, put his lips to the cup that seemed to have just been poured, and moistened his throat with tea.

—As always, it was a strange tea that tasted neither good nor bad.

However, taking a sip of this tea first was an absolute requirement. It was a promise made with the master of this place, a greeting in place of a strange relationship. Except—

"Ah, drank it all down. Thanks for the treat. Now then, let's get down to business."

"—It's true I said taking the first sip was as good as a greeting. However, I don't believe that meant you were free to forgo an actual greeting altogether."

Subaru tossed his cup onto the table after draining it. Hearing his words, the person sitting opposite him across the table lodged a complaint with a faint smile. At that reprimand, Subaru scratched the bridge of his nose with a finger.

"...Do we really need greetings? To you, watching inside my head twenty-four hours a day, meeting or parting probably doesn't mean a damn thing anyway."

"That is a completely separate matter. In the first place, I'd appreciate it if you didn't use such untoward phrasing as me peeking at you twenty-four hours a day. Even if that were true, I am a maiden at an age where even flowers blush. If word got out that I was staring at the boy I fancied the entire time, wouldn't that be a dreadful blow to my reputation?"

"'A maiden at an age where flowers blush'? Did the concept of how words are used change while I wasn't looking?"

"How harsh. Well, perhaps calling myself a maiden is a bit of a stretch age-wise."

Saying so, the mouth of his companion loosened with amusement, contrary to the content of her complaint. An exchange of banter they had grown thoroughly accustomed to; his counterpart was quite tolerant of Subaru's rude attitude. —Rather than merely tolerant, she seemed to enjoy even such exchanges of words.

Being smiled at like that, Subaru's heart couldn't help but accumulate a warped sense of guilt. Even so, he didn't even know what the right way to treat her was. Because against the person before his eyes alone, his means of pinpointing the optimal solution was entirely useless.

"You really are an unpleasant woman."

"I'm delighted to hear that. For someone as gentle and greedy as you, rather than becoming one of the mediocre many you hold dear, or an indifferent stranger you see no value in reaching out to save, becoming a hateful someone who cruelly wounds your heart and drives an unremovable wedge into it is far more pleasing to me."

For someone so thoroughly experienced, sarcasm and spite had no effect. At that response, Subaru snorted with an unpleasant look, but that only resulted in delighting his counterpart even further.

"Now then, having any more of your antipathy directed at me to make you hate me would hurt my heart. Shall we fulfill the purpose of this morning's rendezvous soon?"

"Weren't you just saying you wanted to be hated?"

"It's what you'd call a girl's cute bravado. I'd like you to see through it and accept it at least that much."

"Cute...?"

To Subaru, who tilted his head in genuine suspicion, his companion gave a slight, wry smile. And then—

"Truly, you are a person who remains undaunted through and through. As the 'Witch of Greed,' I find that part of you quite to my liking—Natsuki Subaru."

Fitting for a contractor of her caliber, the 'Witch of Greed'— Echidna narrowed her eyes bewitchingly and smiled with heartfelt delight.

"Today's the fourteenth day of the Month of Kisdum. ...There's no mistake about that, right?"

"You can rest assured. Last night, you went to sleep without incident, and this morning, you woke up without incident. The worst-case scenario did not occur overnight. You don't need to worry so much."

"Don't be stupid. Thinking nothing's wrong like that and going to sleep, only to wake up dead in the morning—that's happened before. No matter how much I stay on guard, it's never enough to know when or where I'll die."

Tsking at the Witch's optimism, Subaru recalled his 'deaths' where he had tasted a devastating sense of loss. Just mindlessly believing tomorrow would surely arrive without a doubt—only for it to be stolen without warning, the reality of abruptly 'Returning by Death' and being forgotten by the people he had spent time closely with—he could never forget how much terror and despair that brought.

"Indeed. I was careless just now. I apologize."

"...You're strangely honest today."

"When I think I'm in the wrong, I apologize. I intended to show you properly that I am not such an unreasonable woman."

To Subaru, who reacted sensitively and felt awkward, the girl in black winked as she spoke. When attempting to describe this girl, the first things that caught one's eye were her demonic beauty that stole one's gaze and entangled one's heart, and the astonishing fact that her beauty was expressed using a mere two colors.

Long, snowy-white hair reaching down to her waist, and a slender, flexible body wrapped in a dress resembling black mourning attire. Sitting in the chair with her long legs crossed, a somewhat decadent allure wafted from her figure, and the sight of her thin lips swallowing the liquid in the cup possessed an abnormally enticing charm. It was a perverse, demonic visage that intuitively told one destruction was unavoidable if touched by force—yet despite that, tales remained of countless people who continuously challenged to touch her; a Witch of disaster who brought about the end.

That was the true identity of Echidna, the white girl clad in black with whom Subaru had formed a contract.

"—Stare."

"—? What is it? Staring at my face like that. Is there something on it?"

"Yeah. Eyes, a nose, a mouth, ears, and hair."

"...I wonder why. Even though you're stating the obvious, I feel strangely insulted."

Echidna knit her brows at Subaru's response, making a dissatisfied face. To her, Subaru held up his hand, saying, "Hold on, hold on."

"I didn't mean it as an insult or anything. I just thought, for all the hype Witches get, you're not really that much of a freak show."

Although he had used various expressions like 'decadent' or 'perverse' earlier, talking to her like this in reality, she was just an ordinary girl who, while having a slightly peculiar side, could even be called friendly. Of course, he didn't deny she had warped aspects fitting for a Witch, though.

"Oho, that's quite a rare opinion."

At Subaru's impression, Echidna loosened her lips with evident amusement. Setting her cup on the table, she recrossed her long legs and ran a hand through her white hair.

"Even so, I am one of the Witches who left various tales across the lands four hundred years ago. Catching me and calling me 'ordinary' is not something I can easily overlook."

"You're sounding like a middle schooler yearning to be treated as special."

"Adding 'by you' would bring it much closer to my true feelings, though. But that isn't all. I believe I told you about that when we first met as well."

"Ah—what was it again? Right, something about your appearance making people without tolerance throw up on first sight."

"If you pluck out only that part, I feel my image as a person will be severely misunderstood."

Her majestic aura as a Witch instantly vanished, and Echidna pouted at Subaru's reply. Subaru gave a casual "There, there" to her reaction and set about adjusting the derailed topic. Even so, the content of their conversation was more than enough for a morning routine.

"We don't have time to idle around with nonsense. I'm heading back to reality."

"Going already? No one will complain if you kill a bit more time here. As you know, time spent here does not affect reality. If it gives you peace of mind, as much as you like... I am expecting that as well."

Echidna stopped Subaru as he stood up from the chair and stretched. To the Witch's sweet words, Subaru cracked his neck.

"Yeah. Honestly, I know I'd be saved by choosing to linger here, but..."

Cutting his words short, Subaru turned around and looked down at Echidna. The Witch's black eyes and Subaru's dark pupils met directly, intertwining. However—

"I have no intention of leaning on you like that. You should know our contract wasn't made with that intention."

"Good grief, how dishonest. Even though I intend to be your accomplice, the only one in this world who can share your suffering and sorrow."

"—I have no intention of entrusting my suffering or sorrow to anyone. This is mine alone, and keeping it to myself alone is the reason you and I contracted. Am I right?"

To Echidna, who shrugged with her pouting face remaining, Subaru declared in a low voice. At that answer, Echidna lowered her eyes and quietly put her mouth to her cup. It was a declaration of intent that she had lost the words to argue back, as well as the reason to continue the conversation. Seeing that through, Subaru turned his back on her and took a step toward the bottom of the hill. But stopping before he did, he looked back at the Witch.

"By the way, you didn't deny peeking at me twenty-four hours a day earlier, did you?"

"...I believe I said 'even if that were true'?"

"In my Japanese comprehension, saying 'even if that were true' is contextually admitting it's true under the surface."

"――――"

"――――"

At Subaru's grilling, Echidna let out a small sigh. Then she put her mouth to her cup again. It was a declaration of intent that she had lost the reason to continue the conversation—

"You better not think doing that'll make me back off. You stop peeking when there's an ethical problem, right? Hey, you do, right?"

"Of course. Even I am not so vulgar as to peek at you bathing or relieving yourself. However, unless I am certain nothing will happen during those defenseless moments, as your contractor, I feel I cannot look away either. This is purely out of a sense of duty as the 'Witch of Greed' abiding by our agreement..."

"From now on, I'm taking off the pendant whenever I take a bath or use the toilet."

Subaru said to the Witch making rapid-fire excuses, biting back his embarrassment as he walked down the hill. At the bottom of the hill, where Subaru had first appeared in the grassland, a door had materialized before anyone noticed. A standalone single door—it was the sole door allowing entry and exit to the outside world.

"――――"

Arriving before the door, Subaru reached for the knob and casually glanced back. Upon the hill, Echidna stood looking forlorn, holding down her hair as it fluttered in the wind, gazing down at his back. Noticing Subaru looking up at her, she hesitated slightly, then softly waved her hand at him. Without returning the gesture, Subaru left behind only a sigh and stepped through the door.

—Immediately after, his consciousness trapped in the dream world was released, returning to reality.

"――――"

As his consciousness swept back to reality, Subaru remained standing stiffly in the middle of his room, still clutching the black crystal stone. Exhaling a long breath, he turned his eyes to the Magic Clock Crystal above the door. The color was green; its shine unchanged. The time spent in the dream world was a mere few seconds in real time—that was the proof.

"Man, I really can't get used to this..."

Heading to the dream world and spending time at tea parties with Echidna was a daily morning routine. However, returning to reality from there, the discomfort born from the gap between perceived time and actual time was not easily assimilated by the brain. It was a sensation distinct from 'Return by Death' as well. Subaru lamented over that. Then—

How about flipping your thinking and getting used to not being used to it? It's nothing more than cold comfort, but it might make things a little easier, don't you think?

As if whispered right into his ear, the voice of the Witch he had just bidden farewell to echoed. It was not actually whispered into his ear. That voice was a thoughtwave from the Witch flowing through the crystal stone hanging from his neck via their contract. Even without going to the dream world, a path remained connected between Subaru and Echidna due to the contract. Therefore, the Witch was capable of speaking directly to Subaru from the dream world like this. However—

"I told you not to talk to me recklessly when there's no business."

There's no one around. I don't think it'd hurt for you to pay attention to me when you're alone in your room, though.

"What if I accidentally do the same thing in front of people and they think I'm a creep talking to someone invisible?"

I feel it's a bit late for that assessment. Besides, it's not unusual for Spirit Arts Users to converse with their contracted spirits via thoughtwaves. Talking to yourself won't stand out that much either.

"In a Spirit Arts User's case, it's thoughtwaves on both sides. In my case, it's thoughtwaves from you, and talking out loud from me. From the outside, I look way creepier."

Yes, yes, I understand. As you wish, I shall remain silent unless necessary. Wanting to make a Witch obey your every whim—you really are quite the domineering husband.

"Shut. Up. Al. Rea. Dy."

Goodness me. If you have need of me, please do not hesitate to call, Lord Contractor.

Mixing anger into his tone, Echidna withdrew after leaving behind theatrical words. Even so, the Witch was surely peeking at the world through the pendant, but setting that aside for now, Subaru let out a sigh that had become a habit. From now on, he would definitely stuff the pendant into his back pocket during baths and toilet breaks. —Just as he was thinking that:

"—Subaru-sama, are you awake?"

The room's door was knocked on from the outside, announcing the arrival of his usual wake-up time. At the soft voice calling out, Subaru cleared his throat lightly. Then, placing a hand on his cheek and consciously making the smile he had verified in the mirror, he gave a relaxed reply, "Yeah, I am~" Receiving that, the door was slowly opened wide, and a young girl entered the room.

"Good morning, Subaru-sama. It's a pleasant morning again today."

The one who said that with a smile was a girl in a maid uniform—Subaru's personal maid, Petra Leyte.

Having been a young bud when they first met, she had entered a growth period and was now in the process of blooming beautifully. Compared to before, her height and limbs had grown, and her cute maid uniform suited her more and more. Her conduct and behavior were in no way outdone by her lovely attire. Originally, she was a clever girl with good memory and quick perception. The current Petra had reached top-tier status even as a maid.

The blossoming fourteen-year-old—to Petra's thoroughly matured smile, Subaru responded with a smile as well.

"Morning to you too, Petra. A pleasant morning... yeah, I agree. Getting to see Petra's cute face first thing in the morning today too makes me totally satisfied."

"Oh, Subaru-sama, saying things like that again. ...But there are clouds hanging over the sky beyond the forest, so it seems the weather might turn bad starting in the afternoon. We're scheduled to go shopping in town today, so I hope it stays clear."

Tilting her head at Subaru's greeting, Petra murmured as she gently cast her gaze toward the window. A faint glimpse of childishness remained at the edge of her words, causing Subaru to chuckle softly.

"Ah, oh dear, Subaru-sama."

"No, no, just now, your shell as a fine maid cracked just a tiny bit. Very like you, Petra."

"Geez, please stop treating me like a child like that. I've grown into a full adult now. I've been doing maid work for two years already... I'm a full-fledged professional."

Swaying her reddish-brown hair, Petra flushed her cheeks in embarrassment and pouted her lips. Seeing the adorable figure of his younger-sister-like companion, Subaru patted the girl's head repeatedly with a smile. At that touch, Petra narrowed her eyes happily, then let out a lingering sigh.

"So, what will you do this morning? It's still early for breakfast, but..."

"Ah, for now I'll just finish the usual routine, and if it looks okay, we'll all have breakfast together. If it looks tough... well, the usual."

"...Yes."

Slightly lowering her eyes at Subaru's answer, Petra wore a troubled expression. Unable to bear leaving such a cute girl with a face like that, Subaru scratched his head and said:

"Anyway, thanks again today. For coming out of your way to wake me up."

"――――"

"Petra?"

"Subaru-sama, you're really clumsy at things like that."

Assessing Subaru for his terrible topic changes, Petra let out an exasperated sigh. Then, shaking her head, the girl formed a radiant smile like a flower blooming and said:

"N-No, this is my job after all. Besides, Subaru-sama never oversleeps, so it's very easy. ...Even though it'd be fine if you were a little sloppier."

"Hmm?"

"It's nothing. Well then, I'll see you later. Please excuse me."

Finishing her daily duty as Subaru's wake-up caller, Petra politely pinched her skirt and performed a curtsy. Then, seen off by Subaru, she left the room. As the door closed, Subaru nodded in satisfaction at Petra's growth.

"Petra's really grown up nicely. As her big-brother figure, I'm proud of her."

She doesn't seem to think of you as a big-brother figure at all, though.

"...You just said you'd stay quiet unless necessary and then this? Lady Witches break promises pretty easily, huh."

With his monologue chimed in on, Subaru was exasperated by the Witch's lack of patience to endure silence. But quickly leaving behind a sigh of resignation, he turned his eyes toward the door where Petra had disappeared.

"I'm her big brother. And Petra is my super, super cute little sister. That's why I'm gonna make sure she's happy. —That's an absolute."

Make sure she's happy, absolute... That might be an unexpectedly terrifying phrase, you know.

At Subaru's firm voice, Echidna said that without teasing. Washing away the Witch's words, Subaru stretched lightly and left the room. The hallway of the manor was chilled by the atmosphere of early morning. Shivering slightly, Subaru began walking, cutting through the cold air with his shoulders.

—Because he had to get to work on his daily 'routine.'

For the past few months, a fierce 'fight to the death' had been unfolding at the Roswaal Manor in the mornings.

However, calling it a 'fight to the death' might be offensive to the parties involved. Yet, to Subaru, who observed the offense and defense from the side, exchanges so intense were taking place that he could call it nothing other than a 'fight to the death.' In fact, it was certain that at least one of the two clashing parties was charging in with the intent to kill. But sadly, the other party facing him lightly brushed off that ferocious fighting spirit. The difference in skill existing between those two was hopelessly clear to anyone's eyes. Of course, that was precisely why such a dangerous routine was being continued every single morning.

"RU, AAAAAH—!!"

Roaring fiercely, a kicking leg exploded with momentum that blew away the turf of the courtyard. Stepping in and gaining intense propulsive force, a golden shadow charged straight ahead, slamming beastly claws toward the enemy's silhouette. It was a heartless strike that could tear apart thick iron plates and easily turn a human body into minced meat—the beastly claws raged into countless continuous strikes, attacking as if sealing off the prey's escape routes. However—

"Unfortunate, but your opening is too large."

"GAH, AH—!?"

Dodging the pouring frenzy of fatal beast claws with minimal body shifts, the shadow avoided them completely. Immediately after, with a single vocalization, a long leg snapped upward, piercing the beast's torso straight from below. A groan of anguish rose at that strike, and the attacker's body was launched straight upward—

"GAH, DZU-O—!?"

—Or so it should have been, but it didn't go that way. The body thrusting straight up was intercepted by a heel swinging down as if to welcome it, smashing into the ground. That impact created a circular crater in the turf ground, and the beast sank into complete silence spread-eagled, unable to move.

"Shall we continue?"

"—Ah, oof."

To the loser groaning in pain, the victor asked about his intention to continue. It was neither sarcastic nor ironic, but words thrown purely at his fighting spirit. That was precisely why it deeply wounded the loser's soul. Therefore—

"You might not be aware of it, but that's nothing but sarcasm. Give him some samurai mercy and call it off."

Interrupting the scene where victory and defeat were already decided, Subaru called out while scratching his head. At those words, the young man who had been looking down at the spread-eagled opponent turned around and showed a smile. The young man with fiery red hair raised a hand to Subaru:

"Ah, good morning, Subaru. You're early again this morning."

"Not as early as you guys. Even so, every single day, whether you don't get tired or don't learn... You don't need to dutifully play along with him every time, Reinhard."

"His desire to become stronger is not wrong. Besides, it's a fact that he's growing stronger day by day. Before long, a time when he catches up to me might come too."

"Is that so? ...That's kinda hard for me to imagine, though."

The red-haired young man—Reinhard's words carried an air that was neither joke nor modesty. At his self and other evaluation, Subaru closed one eye and looked at the person still lying on the ground.

Lying there was a boy with short golden hair. Gasping in pain, he was still in an incapacitated state, but the fighting spirit residing in his green eyes had not faded in the slightest. However, his body failed to obey his will, and he merely gnashed his teeth in frustration. Having understanding for that vexed feeling, Subaru reached out a hand to the boy.

"Here, can you stand, Garfiel? Don't get so down. You..."

"...Don't try to touch me so casually. I can stand up just fine without borrowin' your hand, asshole."

Swatting away Subaru's extended hand, the gold-haired boy—Garfiel bared his fangs. However, it was clear to anyone's eyes that it was mere bravado. Pain was thick on his face, and his breathing was rough. Still, Subaru wasn't childish enough to mock that bluff. Shaking his swatted arm lightly, Subaru let out a sigh as Garfiel raised his body, and said:

"Well, if you say so, do as you like. But go take a shower and wash off the sweat and mud before breakfast. Otherwise, Ram's gonna hate you."

"...Shut up. I know that without you tellin' me."

Shaking his head at Subaru's advice, Garfiel grimaced as he stood up. His knees were still trembling, but not so much that he couldn't walk. Recovering with terrifying regenerative ability, Garfiel glared at Reinhard, who stood coolly.

"Next time, I ain't gonna lose."

"I'm looking forward to it."

To Reinhard's answer, which lacked even a fragment of lie, Garfiel snorted. Then, dragging his legs, the boy glanced at Subaru as he passed by his side:

"—Tsk."

Giving a bitter click of his tongue, he retreated from the courtyard into the manor as he was. Seeing him off with a wry smile, Subaru shook his head with a "good grief." His pride was wounded, and there must be awkwardness as well. Even so, if he could wash away those bad feelings along with his sweat before breakfast, that would be fine. Really, he was at a difficult age.

"Sorry for dumping him entirely on you. If you're tired too, take a bath... Wait, it'd be awkward if you ran into him in the bathroom."

"Indeed, it would be bad for him. Fortunately, I haven't moved enough to work up a sweat, so I'll refrain. It'd be pitiable to bring Garfiel out covered in mud in front of Ram-san, too."

Seeing off Garfiel, Reinhard smiled at Subaru who had called out to him. True to his words, the 'Sword Saint' showed no trace of being affected by the morning practice just now. He had completely treated Garfiel, who attacked with momentum that could kill, like a child. On top of that, with a cool composure, he flattened the crater in the turf created when Garfiel was slammed down using his foot. Then, by whatever principle, the turf returned to its beautifully pristine state. Was this too the power of the countless Divine Protections he possessed? —It made him an even more terrifying person to imagine opposing.
