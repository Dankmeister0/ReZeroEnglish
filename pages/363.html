Arc 5 Chapter 47: "Prelude to the Recapture Operation"

"For now, let's leave the matter of 'Wrath' to Priscilla and her group. As for Liliana's Divine Protection, Reinhard's given his seal of approval on it."

"Since it's a protection she's not used to wielding, it might not carry much weight, but... I do think that if it's Liliana-sama's song, it could genuinely serve as a countermeasure against 'Wrath,' once he's made to hear it."

At Subaru and Reinhard's remarks, the gazes of everyone at the round table turned toward Liliana. She was holding the ends of her tied-up hair, placing them under her nose and playing at having a "mustache," when—

"Whyyy, leave it to me. Once this Liliana has taken a job on, I'll see it through properly. Rest assured. All I do is sing. In a place where I'm needed! Called upon to sing, I sing! How joyous a thing that is! And if I get a little pocket change tossed my way too, all the better!"

"There ain't gonna be no pocket change, so quit it with that capitalist pig mentality first."

"Oinkk!"

Liliana, who'd been getting into her groove, deflated, and in her place Priscilla gave a snort. Her crimson eyes swept back and forth between Subaru and Reinhard.

"And here I wondered what the two of you men were whispering about—what a needless consultation, how tiresome. This songstress's true worth, I have already confirmed for myself. Some witless fanatic will be crushed to dust by it."

"Even so, verifying things for the sake of certainty is necessary, isn't it..."

"Trivial. It is I who will stake my life on this singer's song. Why, then, would I—the one who first proposed her—entrust my life to something uncertain? I would never commit such recklessness."

"——"

Put that way, Subaru had nothing to say back. Indeed, it was Priscilla who'd first raised the possibility of Liliana's usefulness, and it was Priscilla who would be trusting that possibility while facing 'Wrath.' Contrary to her words and manner, she was known to be a woman of formidable cunning and caution.

"Princess, don't go bullyin' my buddy too much. Let's just keep quiet, us."

"What is it, Al. Are you still sulking over having your counsel rejected by me? A grown man, moping like some woman. Do not lower your worth before me over such trifles."

"...It ain't like that."

Turning his gaze away with a huff, Al propped his cheek on his right arm and settled into spectator mode. Priscilla, snorting at her attendant's attitude, leaned her weight back against her chair and stopped interjecting. At long last, it seemed the discussion could move forward.

"So, that's how it'll be—Priscilla's group takes on 'Wrath.' As for the other groupings... it's about 'Lust's' place. His side—I'd like to put forward Wilhelm-san."

"Naming Wilhelm-sama specifically? May I ask your reasoning?"

"It was I who requested this of Subaru-dono, Julius-dono."

To Julius's question, Wilhelm answered by raising his hand. The old swordsman, gathering everyone's gaze, turned his own lightly upward—toward the floor above.

"As you are all aware, my lord Crusch-sama is currently suffering under the vile influence of the Witch Cult's 'Lust.' As Crusch-sama's retainer, I bear the duty to fight on my lord's behalf. And that is not merely a matter of obligation—it is my true, heartfelt wish."

"If possible, I'd like to capture the Archbishop of Sin and get to the bottom of what's causing that condition. That's Wilhelm-san's aim as well, isn't it?"

"Just as you say. It is for that reason that I ask you entrust the subjugation of 'Lust' to me."

Blue eyes charged with fierce resolve, accompanied by a bursting aura of sword-intent, bore down upon the gathering. Faced with the sheer force of will radiating from Wilhelm—the resolve he held, and the debt of gratitude to his lord—no one dared voice any objection to his wish. No one, save one person bound to him by blood.

"Honestly, I'm against it."

"...Reinhard."

While everyone else was cowed by that sword-intent, Reinhard alone did not so much as change expression. Keeping his usual earnest countenance, he fixed his eyes on Wilhelm.

"Right now, Grandfather, you have lost your composure. Of course I understand the hostility you feel toward the Archbishop of Sin who committed such an outrage against Crusch-sama. But I do not believe the objective can be achieved in that state of mind."

"You say that a lack of composure would hinder the achievement of the objective? Is that what you're telling me?"

"For Crusch-sama's sake, failure to capture 'Lust' cannot be permitted. If that is so, then let me take on that role. At the very least, mentally, I should be able to face him from a position of greater advantage."

Reinhard's words were sound reasoning, a line of thought devoted as much as possible to ensuring the matter's certain resolution. There was no mistaking that Wilhelm, too keyed up, had lost his composure. But at that opinion of Reinhard's, Wilhelm's lips loosened.

Not into anything gentle—but into the ferocious smile of a wild beast.

"—It is only natural that I have lost my composure, Reinhard."

"Grandfather, but—"

"Who do you think I am—your grandfather? I am Wilhelm the Sword Devil. A half-formed man who sought to be nothing but a single blade, yet failed at even that, and loved a woman instead. But precisely because I am a man of half-formed nature, I have never once been half-hearted about what needed doing."

The ferocious smile repainted the mild, transparent impression Wilhelm usually gave. What surfaced from beneath it was a devil who burned his life on blood and steel, whose heart was captured by the gleam of a blade. And yet, even as that devil, a trace of warmth remained in his eyes.

"When I resolve to swing my sword, my heart soars. Losing my composure is an everyday matter for me on the battlefield. And even so, I have lived on to this age. This time, too, I have no intention of withering away without fulfilling my debt to my lord. Your excess concern is unnecessary."

"Isn't that logic just spiritualism, plain and simple...?"

"Push spiritualism far enough, and it becomes conviction. Push it fourteen years, and even a blunted sword can avenge a wife's murder."

Hearing that this was the very conviction with which Wilhelm had avenged his grandmother in the battle against the White Whale, even Reinhard could find no further words to continue the argument. And yet, Reinhard still lowered his eyes, unconvinced. At his grandson's stubborn stance, Wilhelm added, "And besides..."

"The battlefield that needs you is not here. The scene where you are needed lies elsewhere."

"A battle that needs me..."

"—Subaru-dono. Please take my grandson to your battlefield. To recapture Emilia-sama, you must fight 'Greed,' must you not? Let this Reinhard become the sword for your sake."

Suddenly named, Subaru's eyes went wide. Yet, as if pulled along by Wilhelm's nodding gesture, Reinhard's gaze too turned this way. Seeing this, Subaru scratched his head, resigned.

"Honestly, I wanted to settle the 'Lust' matter first before getting into this. ...But yeah. That's right. Your strength, honestly, I want to borrow for the fight against 'Greed,' which is my assignment. I think it's that troublesome bastard, of all of them, who needs your power."

'Greed's' Regulus rose in his mind's eye. Of the other Archbishops of Sin, they only had fragmentary knowledge of their abilities, but among these repulsive freaks, Regulus's Authority was dangerous on a whole different level. He couldn't state it for certain, but as things stood, all he could imagine of Regulus's ability was something as absurd as "Invincibility." Of course, he didn't even want to consider the existence of something simply, purely "invincible." He wanted to believe there was some kind of weakness or limitation to it.

"To break through Regulus's 'invincible' defense, we need combat power that can actually go toe-to-toe with him. In terms of raw offense and defense, simply compared, I think he's the strongest of the Archbishops of Sin. That's why I want to borrow Reinhard's strength for taking him down."

"An opponent against whom attacks don't connect... certainly, I would be the right fit for such a monster. But—"

Even hearing of Regulus's unreasonable Authority, Reinhard's hesitation didn't clear. But into that uncertainty of Reinhard's, another voice rose. It was, of none other, from right beside Subaru.

"—Well then. I'll be goin' with the old man Wilhelm, I will."

Coming forward, Garfiel bared his sharp canines, clacking them as he glared at Reinhard. At Garfiel, Reinhard made a surprised face. "You?"

"The boss is recommendin' you. I know your skill well enough, myself. Ain't like I'm needed for gettin' Emilia-sama back. Ain't that right, boss?"

"No, Garfiel, it's not that I mean anything by that, I just—"

"Don't need no comfortin'. And I ain't sayin' this outta sulkin' either. If anythin', it's the opposite. This time 'round, more'n anythin', there's somebody I got business with."

Frowning at Garfiel's heated manner, Subaru belatedly realized something. That's right. Among the city hall staff who'd been transformed by 'Lust,' there was someone who seemed to be an acquaintance of Garfiel's—someone turned into a black dragon. For Garfiel, 'Lust' was never an opponent without a grudge attached. And on top of that—

"That damn woman, sure, I can't forgive that neither, but that ain't all there is to it. Them first two bastards who came at us with attitude, they're probably with 'Lust' too."

"——"

"There's a fool who got hurt for nothin', because of how pathetic I was. I gotta pay that back, for that guy's sake too. So I'm goin' along with the old man."

At Garfiel's sharp glare, Wilhelm quietly dipped his chin. Here, the intentions of the old swordsman and the young warrior united into a single form—fighting together for the sake of retribution. And underlying that was a shared point: both fighting for the sake of a woman they held dear. At Garfiel's declaration of will, Subaru had no objection to voice.

"I know it's a bit late to say this, but 'Lust's' Authority is transformation and metamorphosis. He can transform himself, and he can transform others too. And then there's the blood. Never, under any circumstances, let his blood touch you. That's the source of what's causing Crusch-san's suffering. As for personality... all of them are the worst, but this one's especially bad."

"I am well aware."

"I'll stomp him flat."

At Subaru's final confirmation, neither Wilhelm nor Garfiel showed the slightest sign of backing down. Finally, Subaru turned his eyes toward Reinhard for confirmation, but it seemed even the Sword Saint himself had no intention of raising objections to a matter this firmly resolved.

"I don't mind. Garfiel's skill is certain too. If Grandfather is there alongside him, I can believe they'll cut down any enemy, whoever it may be."

"Comin' from you, that don't sit right somehow. Like sayin' 'the tempura's modest, but the flavor's exquisite,' or somethin'."

"I mean it sincerely. I believe in your victory, and Grandfather's. I will be Subaru's sword."

Garfiel, looking none too displeased, scratched his cheek, and Reinhard nodded to Subaru. At the Sword Saint's reassuring words, Subaru felt as if he'd gained the support of ten thousand men.

"Sorry for the selfish request, Reinhard."

"It's fine. Whatever the situation, I will do everything within my power to the fullest. If that's what saves you, then it's exactly what I want."

"I keep relyin' on you and it really does feel bad. This is a spot where I'm relyin' way too much on you bein' strong, but... I'll make up for what you're missin' somehow, so look forward to it."

"——"

At those words, Reinhard suddenly blinked and fell silent. At his unusual reaction, Subaru tilted his head, and Reinhard quickly gave a small laugh, saying "No, it's nothing."

"It's nothing, I suppose, to you. —Ah, well. I'll allow myself to look forward to it, then. To you trying to fill in the part I can't reach."

"—? Well, yeah, look forward to it plenty. 'Cause I'm counting on it. So, with the flow of things so far, I think you can guess, which necessarily means the last one, 'Gluttony,' is—"

"—To be handled by myself and Ricardo, I take it."

Picking up where Subaru left off, it was Julius who answered, in a low voice. At the harshness in his voice, unlike him, Anastasia turned a concerned gaze toward the knight.

"Julius, you sure you're okay? You've had bad color in your face for a while now."

"My apologies for causing you concern. But I am fine. If we're going to talk about being in good or bad condition, I can hardly afford to voice any weakness in front of Subaru."

"The hell's that supposed to mean?"

"Naturally, I say that mindful of your leg, and the severity of the position you're in. Please don't snap at me. I have no intention of quarreling with you, even at a moment like this."

"Hmph..."

Thrown off by the unexpected attitude, Subaru fell into an odd sense of unease all the same. Subaru shared Anastasia's suspicion about Julius's manner, though he still couldn't pin down what it was—still, even so.

"The remaining assignment, 'Gluttony,' Ricardo and I will take it on. Originally, it should have been you or Wilhelm-sama, Subaru, who had the grudge against him and thus wished to take that role. Even bending that wish to be entrusted with this duty instead—I will absolutely see it through."

"...Yeah, that's right."

Just as Julius's resolve suggested, the subjugation of 'Gluttony' was the role Subaru had wanted to see through himself. That was surely the same for Wilhelm, and for Crusch, even now suffering upstairs. Thinking of Rem, who had fallen victim to 'Gluttony's' Authority of devouring memories and devouring names, and who continued to sleep because of it—Subaru had wanted to grind 'Gluttony' to pulp with his own two hands. To beat him, kick him, stomp him underfoot, make him regret every single thing he'd done, force him into a tearful prostration—he'd wanted to do all of that, as much as he pleased. To hand that role over to someone else—

"Honestly, I don't want to leave it to anyone. Rem—I want to get her back. I wanted to get her back. I believed doing that was my duty."

"............"

"Even so, if I have to leave it to someone, then here, now, I'm leaving it to you. Don't get me wrong. It's process of elimination. ...It's definitely process of elimination, but I'm leaving it to you. To me, you're one of the few people I can grudgingly hand this duty to and still put up with it."

Rem, held hostage in both memory and existence. Emilia, whose person had been stolen away, even now waiting for the moment she'd be rescued. Both were people precious to Subaru, both were important people Subaru had to bring back, and so he wanted to show his best to both of them.

But Subaru was Emilia's knight, and Rem's hero.

"I'll defeat 'Greed' and bring Emilia back. Punching out 'Gluttony,' this time, I'll hand over to you. ...Don't you dare screw it up."

"—I will answer your expectations. This time, this time for certain."

Bowing deeply, Julius took on Subaru's trust. The 'Finest' knight then looked to Wilhelm, and gave a small dip of his chin.

"Wilhelm-sama."

"What I would say has mostly already been said by Subaru-dono. That my feelings toward 'Gluttony' are far from calm is a fact... which is why I, too, will leave it to Julius-dono. This city has rather too many insolent, overconfident scoundrels in it."

At the refined sword-intent, Julius seemed quietly encouraged. Ricardo, who had been silently watching this exchange up to now, spoke up.

"Well now, ain't nobody botherin' to ask my opinion, and here things go on movin' right along. Not that I mind none! I agree that the current lineup's the best one, myself."

"Ricardo's an attention-hog, so. A body that big, and still sulkin', ain't cute at all. ...Look after Julius for me, yeah?"

"Relax. Have I ever told you a lie? Ana-bou."

"Quit callin' me that, already. I'm Ricardo's mistress, y'know."

At Anastasia, puffing her cheeks in a sulky face, Ricardo laughed boisterously. Looking down at Anastasia with his dark eyes, Ricardo's gaze was terribly gentle.

"Well then, that settles the lineup."

—At Subaru's words, everyone seated at the round table nodded.

"For the subjugation of 'Wrath's' Sirius, Priscilla and Al. And Liliana, the three of you."

"To think of dominating hearts and minds while setting me aside—how laughable. I shall teach that empty-headed fool that this was the wrong place, wrong time, and wrong opponent to get carried away with."

"Just singing, just singing. I'm that, a lump of flesh that just sings. Don't spare your life, spare the stage. Alright, I feel like I can do this. Right now, I feel like I can do this!"

"——"

Priscilla fanned herself with her fan, while Liliana cast some mysterious self-hypnosis upon herself. Al, showing no expression, still radiated an air of dissatisfaction from his entire body. A somewhat unreliable trio, but confidence, at least, was strongest right here.

"Next, the subjugation of 'Lust,' Garfiel and Wilhelm-san."

"Yeahh. Gonna grab that noisy woman by the scruff of her neck and make her cry and apologize."

"Leave it to us. —The reckoning with that pair as well, without fail."

The two with the highest fighting spirit, one might say, were these two. The Sword Devil Wilhelm, for the debt owed to his lord, and for the wife he had never once forgotten, even for a moment. Garfiel, for the settling of an emotion within himself that hadn't yet taken shape, one that shook his heart. The two warriors would perhaps each seek their own answer in the fight, as they went to face it.

"And then the subjugation of 'Gluttony,' Julius and Ricardo, you two."

"A role entrusted to none other than the two of us. I will see it through, without fail. Only by doing so can I deny him."

"My family got done in by them damn bastards too, y'know. No need to tell me twice. I'll knock him flat and make him cry."

These two had the thinnest connection to the Witch Cult of anyone. And yet, that they could be trusted with expectations no less than anyone else's was because both could be confirmed, without doubt, to be worthy of trust. Already, the two had overcome hardship together once before. There was no need, anywhere, to doubt a comrade-in-arms. Precisely because of that, the decision to hand over the hated 'Gluttony' could be made.

"And the last one, 'Greed,' is me and Reinhard, the two of us. Counting on you, yeah?"

"—Yes, leave it to me. I'm counting on you too, Subaru."

At Subaru's request, Reinhard dipped his chin with his usual resolute bearing. And yet, somewhere, the air about him seemed to have softened—perhaps unbecoming before a battle, but it struck Subaru as somehow more human. Why he looked so relieved in that way, Subaru couldn't quite say.

"Well then, that settles the fighting assignments. Dialogue mirrors for keepin' in contact between locations, three in total. I'll keep one here at city hall, and... what about the rest?"

"Personally, I really want 'Wrath' to have one for sure. The other one, I think, should go to either 'Lust' or 'Gluttony.'"

"And the reason for that?"

"'Wrath' is the one whose influence reaches the whole city. Whether he's defeated or not will completely change the situation this city's in. So I want to share that news fast."

Regarding the handling of the dialogue mirrors, everyone nodded at Subaru's proposal, seeing the sense in it. And the reason for giving the other one to someone other than 'Greed' was—

"Not to say too much, but Reinhard's the one at 'Greed's' place. Since we don't know 'Greed's' ability, I'm being optimistic, but there's a chance it could end in an instant too. If that happens, I want to be in a position where I can send Reinhard out as reinforcements."

"But if there's no dialogue mirror at 'Greed's' location, wouldn't that information not be shareable? Natsuki-san's reasoning does make sense, though."

"That part's simple. Use the broadcast Magic Vessel. Announce citywide, and have whoever needs help say so. Anastasia-san gathers the information through the dialogue mirror, and takes on the role of relaying it to everyone. How's that?"

"I think that's wise. My, aren't you sharp, Natsuki-kun."

Anastasia laughed, looking impressed, then tossed one of the dialogue mirrors to Priscilla. Priscilla caught it with her fan and rolled it in front of Liliana.

"Wa, wa, wah!?"

"Songstress, you hold it. I cannot carry anything heavier than tableware."

"That fan of yours is about as heavy as tableware. Don't be lazy about it."

"Don't talk nonsense—look at this design. It's extravagantly gilded, and thus carries that much more heft and gravity. Don't lump it together with mere tableware."

"Sounds heavier than tableware to me..."

In any case, it was settled that Liliana would carry it. Ignoring Liliana, who busily fixed her bangs in the mirror, the last dialogue mirror was offered before Wilhelm.

"Given the number of enemies, the mirror should go to 'Lust's' side rather than 'Gluttony's.' I trust the two of you, but if it looks dangerous, please contact Anastasia immediately."

"Consider it agreed upon, shall we say."

At Julius's consideration, Wilhelm tucked the dialogue mirror into his breast pocket. And yet, it was a decision with a lingering trace of unease. It was entirely possible that, once heated up, Wilhelm might not follow those instructions after all. And needless to say, Garfiel was the type to fly into a rage over the smallest thing.

—Still, in any case, the preparations for battle were now complete.

"Let's take a bit of time. Once that's done, we depart—the beginning of the operation to recapture the control tower."

At Subaru's words, everyone returned their respective nods. But the quiet, tense mood everyone wore struck Subaru as somehow ominous.

"Don't you feel like, if you make a gloomy face, bad stuff comes creepin' closer?"

"It seems Natsuki-san's about to say something strange again."

"It ain't strange. It's important. However big an army you assemble, if morale's low, it can turn into a disorganized mob. I don't think fighting spirit's low, but I don't think putting up a bad front is a good thing either. So, let's raise our voices."

Clapping his hands, Subaru stood up. And, thrusting his fist up so everyone could see, he said—

"Let's do this, everyone! With this battle, we'll sweep the troublesome bastards out of the city! We'll beat the Witch Cult senseless and collect our happy ending!"

"——"

At Subaru's bold declaration, everyone exchanged glances. Then, nodding—

"Oooh——!!"

—came the rousing response. If they could produce words that forceful, surely they'd be alright. This many members, this much fighting spirit—not something easily assembled.

—The battle to recapture the city was about to begin in earnest.

"—This battle, it's our victory!!"

Subaru's very typical declaration served as the closing line to this round-table meeting.

Feeling a somewhat stifled sensation, consciousness slowly returned to reality. As reality seeped in from the sensation of drowsiness, the feel of her limbs naturally came back to her. Gradually the sense of control spread through her whole body, and the first thing she felt was the softness enveloping her.

Warm, like being wrapped in the fur of some great animal, a feeling of security. She'd tasted a sensation similar to this once before. A long time ago, when she was still very small, when her heart hadn't yet caught up with her body, when she was afraid to sleep alone. It had been quite a long time since she'd drifted away from that feeling of fur.

"—Ah."

A nostalgic sensation, welling up like tears. Cutting away her lingering reluctance to savor the warmth where she lay, her eyelids slowly opened. Her long lashes trembled, and violet-blue eyes dimly made out the hazy world.

A high ceiling, and a space with unfamiliar decoration. She realized her own body was on a bed, wrapped in a towel blanket made of quality fur. And that the person seated beside her had been wiping her face with a damp cloth.

"Who are you...?"

"——"

Looking down at her as her eyes opened was a beautiful woman with a pale face. A face drained of color to an almost sickly degree, fixed with a doll-like, beautiful expressionlessness. A beauty that, were she to smile, would surely brighten everything around her—yet it remained rigid as a Noh mask. The woman, clad in a black dress, stood up just like that, and, letting her long blond hair sway, promptly left the room. Emilia tried to call out to stop her on reflex, but hesitated, unable to recall a name to call her by, and by the time she did, the door had already closed. She was left behind, alone.

"Where is this place?"

Fumbling, she pushed herself up. There was a faint sluggishness, but no pain, no sense of illness. The sluggishness was the kind of symptom that came when her body couldn't support the strain of overusing magic she wasn't yet used to wielding. Having thought that far, she immediately recalled what had happened to her.

"That's right. I was fighting some strange person in the plaza..."

One after another, the memories from just before she lost consciousness came rushing into her mind. The bandage-wrapped monster—the person Subaru had called 'Wrath'—had turned on her with terrifying combat strength and a chilling, dreadful wrath. For a time she'd had the upper hand in the fight, but she'd lost to the force of those blazing flames and been blown away—

"And then I must've just fainted. But, I'm alive."

There was no mistaking that she'd been at a disadvantage, in an absolutely desperate situation. Someone had probably saved her. Since Subaru and Beatrice had been there, maybe the two of them had rescued her. Even so, her chest felt like it would crush from the shame of it. After talking so big to Subaru, after cutting such a bold figure, to lose and then need saving on top of it.

"No, I can't keep being down about it. Someone as behind as I already am doesn't have time to stop and reflect. I'll do my reflecting while I walk."

Pressing a palm to her pale cheek, she quickly pulled herself together. There was no time to waste being sunk in gloom. This bed, this towel blanket. Someone even nursing her—this place must be the home of someone well-disposed toward her. And if she'd been carried somewhere other than an inn, maybe she'd been in quite a bad state.

"But there's no pain in my body, and whoever's skilled did it... huh?"

Trying to stand up then, Emilia only now noticed the state of her own body.

"I'm naked."

The moment her bare feet touched the floor, she realized she wasn't wearing so much as a scrap of cloth. Emilia tilted her head, and for the time being wrapped the towel blanket around her body and got off the bed. Looking around the room, she searched for anything else she could throw on, but unfortunately found nothing.

"Um, what should I do. Going out of the room dressed like this seems kind of indecent, but..."

Before leaving the forest, from Puck, and after leaving the forest, between her studies, and after learning various things, thoroughly from Annerose too—she'd been lectured extensively on matters of modesty in that regard. One shouldn't bare their skin in front of others so casually. Going by that thinking, her current appearance was completely out of the question.

"But, I'm worried about everyone, and it's an emergency, so it can't be helped, right?"

She had to find out, as soon as possible, how the fight against the Archbishop of Sin had turned out. Using that as her justification, Emilia left the room wearing nothing but the towel blanket. Stepping into the hallway, it was indeed the interior of an unfamiliar building. Only, unlike the place she'd imagined, outside the room was a strangely rough, cold hallway.

"I thought it'd be somewhere like a mansion, but it's totally different. Hmm, is it just this room that's odd?"

Turning back, she looked at the room where she'd been laid to rest. A large bed, a modest wardrobe. But looking closer, it was hard to shake an impression of imbalance somewhere. As if the bed and other furniture had simply been brought into an otherwise empty room and set down haphazardly.

And that impression might not be wrong. Judging by the atmosphere of the hallway, this place clearly wasn't meant for people to live in. This was a place where people worked. Straining her ears, she could faintly make out the sound of water and some kind of operating noise. Tilting her head at that, Emilia—and then—

"Ah, seems you're awake. Good, good. Nothing better than knowing you're safe."

A voice called out, and Emilia turned around. A young man had appeared from the far end of the hallway. Gazing at Emilia with a smile was a young man with white hair. Wearing an amiable smile, he approached her with an easy, familiar air.

"But, you know, wandering around right after waking up isn't something I can really approve of. So much happened right before this, it'd be a problem if something happened to your body. I really want you to take proper care of yourself in that regard. It's not just your body anymore, after all."

"Um, and you are...?"

Blinking, Emilia looked back at the young man speaking to her. His manner of closing the distance in one leap had something in common with Subaru. But what set him decisively apart from Subaru was that his attitude held not a shred of consideration for the other person. That was Subaru's timid virtue—but the young man before her had none of it at all. As if he felt not the slightest twinge of conscience over his own conduct toward others.

—And at the same time, Emilia felt an indescribable sensation toward the young man before her.

"Ah, right, sorry, sorry. I was gazing at your sleeping face, so it feels like I already know you, but this is the first time you're seeing me, isn't it. I haven't even introduced myself yet. No matter how close you and I are, being rude like this won't do. This is my mistake, rushing things too much. I'll admit it honestly and apologize. Because I'm the kind of person who can do that."

"Ah, um..."

At the young man's fluent, rambling speech, Emilia's response felt heavy. That was partly because she was overwhelmed by his manner, but it carried a more serious meaning than that. It was that Emilia's own instincts were crying out to her.

—That she felt, somehow, that she'd seen this young man somewhere before.

"Such a nice moment, and what a waste that it's such an unromantic setting. But someday, you'll be able to look back on even this and remember it as a special instant. Thinking of it that way, it's not such a bad thing either. Life's path can be lit up plenty just by the small, everyday happinesses. With you, I'm sure I'll feel that especially strongly. Not just the bad parts, but I'd like to look at the good parts like that too, and decide how to live. Don't you think so too, Emilia?"

"I don't recall ever telling you my name, but... so, who are you?"

"Oh, sorry about that. When my feelings swell up, I tend to lose sight of what's around me, that's a bad habit of mine. There are times I even hate my own loving nature for it. Maybe it's because you make me too infatuated. Ah, right, my name."

Going through a truly roundabout detour, the young man finally arrived at the main subject. Her skin practically scorched by the mysterious sensation she felt toward him, Emilia didn't take her eyes off the young man's movements. Her intuition understood that she wasn't in a safe zone. And that the cause of that intuition lay with the young man before her.

"My name is Regulus Corneas. I hold a position like an executive in a certain organization, but that's not important to you. There's only one thing that matters to you. I am your husband, and you are my seventy-ninth wife."

"...Eh?"

The meaning of what the young man—Regulus—had said, so solemnly, made no sense. Emilia, bewildered, furrowed her finely shaped brows. But Regulus paid no mind at all to Emilia's less-than-favorable reaction, and looking at her, her body hidden by only a thin sheet of cloth—

"That outfit is bad for the eyes. I'll have someone bring you a change of clothes right away. You can relax. They're my wives, in the same position as you. They're quite used to dressing brides, after all."

"Wedding clothes, what does that mean? No, not just that. Me being your wife, what are you—"

"Ah, right. I forgot something important! How careless of me, that was a close call."

At the rapid-fire development, Emilia tried to interject, but Regulus had no ear for it. Clapping his hands, he gently gripped the shoulders of Emilia as she tried to press forward. At the abnormal strength in those fingertips, Emilia grimaced. Bringing his face close enough to nearly touch foreheads with her, Regulus peered into her eyes.

"I forgot an important, important question. The marriage ceremony comes after that. Emilia, it's important, so please answer with care. It's something crucial to our future."

"——"

At the abnormal pressure bearing down on her, Emilia held her breath, silent. Perhaps taking Emilia's attitude as consent, Regulus smiled. He smiled, and said—

"Emilia, are you a virgin? That alone, truly, is a very important thing."

He smiled, and said just that.
