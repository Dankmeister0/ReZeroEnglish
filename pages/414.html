Arc 6 Chapter 12: "Baptism of the Watchtower"

The lethargy and the agony of scorching heat were both lifted away at once, and Natsuki Subaru returned.

"――――"

He'd like to be commended for not screaming at the sight before his eyes. Having passed through his second 『Return by Death』, Subaru had been dropped back into the flower field once more surrounded by Witchbeasts, and as he hastily clapped a hand over his own mouth, he genuinely thought so.

"Tsk tsk tsk tsk."

In the space dominated by the violent fragrance of flowers, what could be heard was the sound of Meili clicking her tongue. The oiran bear blocking the way in front of the Dragon Carriage had its gaze stolen by the rhythm released from her lips and the fingers swaying quietly before it. Everyone held their breath, watching over this deadly stand-off. Just a few minutes ago, he'd stood through this very scene twice already — and now he'd been dragged back into it again.

No way. Seriously?

Even as he traced the events that had happened to him, Subaru's brain began spinning at high speed. He urgently needed to switch gears. He had to forget that, until just a moment ago, this had been a desperate battlefield being chased by a swarm of Witchbeasts, and deal with the problem right in front of him now.

Right in front of him — what was the problem in front of him. What was about to happen? Tsk tsk tsk tsk — a sound that, oddly, had the pastoral air of soothing a kitten. The actual situation had nothing to do with anything that cute, but between that and the flowers' fragrance, his thoughts were being obstructed.

Smell, sweetness, noise, nuisance, itch, pain, heat — which one was it?

"――?"

His brain churned to the point of boiling over, yet no concrete plan surfaced. His brain couldn't keep pace with the discrepancy between the situation he'd been in just moments ago and the one he was in now. Subaru, desperately trying to adapt to "now." A faint impact against his chest.

"――――"

Looking down, he saw it was Beatrice, held against him, who had let her weight settle there. She was tucked snugly into Subaru's chest, and lightly raised the reins she'd grabbed in his place, as if to show them to him. She'd apparently caught them for him — the ones he must have dropped from the shock right after the 『Return by Death』.

The moment right before his last 『death』 came back to him, along with Beatrice's crying face and tearful voice.

"――!"

Right, that's right. That's how it was. This was already the third time Subaru had arrived at this situation. He had died here twice already. The first time, a death he couldn't comprehend; but the second, a death whose shape he could, to some degree, imagine. Even so, the agony of the moment of death wasn't clear to him. His organs melting, his thoughts stewed to mush in a simmering pot — was that—

No, that's not what needs priority right now. What needs priority right now is—

"Tsk tsk tsk...... tch."

Just as Subaru's thoughts caught up to his second 『death』, Meili's provocation of the oiran bear reached its conclusion. Drawn by the clicking of her tongue and the movement of her fingers, the Witchbeast's gaze slowly drifted off the Dragon Carriage, guided toward the right side of the group. If the oiran bear would just lumber off as it was, there'd be no problem. But Subaru knew it wouldn't go that way.

"――――"

The Earth Dragon standing right in front of the retreating oiran bear's nose — Jaian — was breathing hard. The pressure of being made to face a Witchbeast at point-blank range, and the violently lingering scent of nectar — these two kinds of pressure were tormenting the Earth Dragon's composure, and the taut thread was about to snap without warning. And if the Earth Dragon started kicking up a fuss, the agitated pack of Witchbeasts would attack all at once. If that happened, it would be nothing but a repeat of the two 『deaths』 so far.

"――――"

But no one in the group besides Subaru could notice Jaian's agitated state. So focused on the immediate threat of the Witchbeast, even Julius, who held the reins, had let his attention slip from the Earth Dragon. Which meant Subaru alone could deal with it. But how was he supposed to deal with it?

He couldn't make a sound. He couldn't rush over and touch it either. Calling out to Julius to have him calm the Earth Dragon carried too much risk. Before any of that — if he couldn't decide right now, it would be too late. What should he—

"――――"

Subaru squeezed his eyes shut, using up his last moment for thought. If nothing struck him in this instant, he'd gamble everything and call out to Julius. The two previous 『deaths』 came back to him, the Witchbeasts' onslaught and the light from the watchtower reviving in his mind. The moment of his second 『death』, Beatrice sobbing and clinging to him, saying "Don't leave me alone" — that too pierced deep into Subaru's chest, and — he realized it.

"Bea-ko, I love you."

"――!?"

He wrapped his arms around her small body from behind and murmured softly into her ear. Beatrice was stunned by the sudden declaration of affection, but he clamped a hand over her mouth so she couldn't cry out. In her place, Subaru extended a 'hand' toward Jaian diagonally ahead.

A 'hand' meant to gently soothe, just as it had wiped away Beatrice's tears right before his 'death.'

—Invisible Providence.

Since his mouth was busy whispering endearments, he unfortunately had to chant the spell's name only in his heart. The instant he did, something stirred at the center of Subaru's body — a sensation distinct from the one where mana was drawn out through his link with Beatrice — and something black roared its approval at being summoned. Well summoned, indeed, it seemed to declare loudly — this was the 『Unseen Hand』 that did not exist in this world, acting in place of the "slothful" Natsuki Subaru to accomplish his aims.

"――――"

The black palm that was born of it took Subaru's chest as its point of origin, slowly manifesting into the world. Naturally, its existence was visible to no one but Subaru. Since he was holding her, the 『Unseen Hand』 existed passing straight through Beatrice, but it apparently didn't function on unintended contact. While impressed by that fact, he could also feel something within himself being shaved away with every passing fraction of a second. Whether it was called the Soul, or whether it was called sanity, Subaru truly didn't know. Still, he couldn't afford to take much time over it. Nor did he want to.

The black demonic hand extended smoothly, reaching for the throat of Jaian, who looked ready to cry out any moment now. Then the palm slowly stroked the thick neck protected by heavy scales. The Earth Dragon's massive body flinched at the contact of something unknown, but instinct must have sensed there was no hostility in that palm. The Earth Dragon calmed its ragged breathing, tension draining from its limbs.

"――Hm?"

Then, perhaps sensing the faint stirring, Julius, noticing Jaian's behavior, pulled the reins and began in earnest to calm the Earth Dragon. As expected of Julius, he was well practiced at it, and in no time at all had settled the Earth Dragon that had been on the verge of a fit. Confirming this, Subaru immediately severed his connection with the 『Unseen Hand』. Robbed of its freedom, the black palm dispersed in an instant, blending into the parched wind of the sand sea as if it had never been there at all.

"Ha, phew......"

Having averted the immediate crisis, Subaru let out a long breath of relief. At the same time, he wanted to exhale the black storm cloud that had settled in his chest as well. Of course, things weren't so simple — the sense of loss that was the price for wielding Invisible Providence still clawed at his soul.

Yet even as that sense of loss gouged at his chest, Subaru thought to himself. Invisible Providence — the price for wielding the 『Unseen Hand』, the breathlessness and sense of loss from its effects — was clearly becoming 'easier' compared to before.

The first time Subaru had used Invisible Providence was when he fought Garfiel in the 『Sanctuary』. Back then, having unleashed the power in a desperate frenzy, Subaru had been struck by a sense of loss so severe it felt like half his body had been taken from him, and an intoxication dizzying enough to destabilize his consciousness. That was an abnormality no excuse could explain away — not revulsion toward the person who'd wielded the same power before him, nor simply having been beaten too badly by Garfiel.

That abnormality had, endlessly, been warning Subaru of the danger of using this power — including the very circumstances by which he'd come to wield this special ability, no, this Authority. Which was why he'd never actively considered making effective use of this power up to now. Subaru's Invisible Providence had nowhere near the power or the number of moves that the original madman's 『Unseen Hand』 had possessed. At best, it could manage wiping away tears or patting a head. For his soul to be shaved down for that was terrible cost-performance. Thinking so, it was a method he'd more or less kept sealed away — but.

"It'd better not be that I'm just getting used to it."

At the decrease in his sense of loss and revulsion, Subaru felt unease rather than relief. Having more cards in hand is better — that was Subaru's personal philosophy, but there's no benefit in holding onto multiple Jokers in a game of Old Maid that never gets matched. Of course, outside of Old Maid, it was undeniably a powerful card to hold, but—

"Mm, mmm, mmm."

"......What exactly have you been doing this whole time, I wonder."

"Huh? Doing what......"

While Subaru groaned softly to himself, lost in thought, an abruptly displeased voice sounded. Wondering what was wrong, he opened his eyes to find Beatrice's narrowed gaze meeting his, her face turned toward him. And as for Beatrice — she had become a strange sight indeed.

"Bea-ko, what's with that hair and getup?"

"—It's the result of Subaru endlessly kneading and mangling Betty's hair and clothes this whole time, that's what it is! Why must Betty suffer such humiliation, I wonder!"

"Huh, me?"

Beatrice fumed furiously, keeping her voice low, while Subaru tilted his head, utterly without malice. Her once-splendid ringlets were now a tangled mess, and her lavish dress and the white cloak meant to protect it from the sandy wind had been arranged into something bizarre — running at the cutting edge of fashion. Though it seemed no one else was following that particular trend.

"—It's infuriating that you have no self-awareness, I suppose! Betty let it slide thinking you were anxious, and you just kept getting more and more carried away...... At this rate, I'd wager you don't even remember what you said just now — that d-declaration of love, either, I suppose."

"Nah, I remember that one. Bea-ko LOVE is my honest feeling."

"Nya!"

At the blunt words, Beatrice's face flushed red, and she buried her head entirely under her cloak. The sight was endearing in its awkwardness, but now wasn't the time to keep playing around with just Beatrice.

As a result of Subaru's unseen efforts, Jaian didn't kick up a fuss, and the Dragon Carriage's march, relying on Meili's ability, was about to resume. But Subaru wove through the gap between oiran bears, brought Patrasche alongside the driver's seat, and—

"Julius, Meili. Temporary retreat. — The odds are too bad."

—and, citing the two 『deaths』 as his reason, proposed reworking the plan.

"I'm disappointed in you, mister. Getting so cold on your feet just from a little unease, of all thi~ings."

"In the end, Barusu is Barusu, I suppose. I thought becoming Emilia-sama's knight might have improved you a little, but I suppose your true nature doesn't change so easily after all. Premature ejaculator."

"There's no reason I should be wounded this deeply just for suggesting a safety-first policy!"

Having somehow backtracked through the flower field and returned to the strategy meeting spot after breaking through the 『Sand Hour』, Subaru — who had made the call to retreat — was skewered by harsh assessments from the vanguard critics. Meili, who had tamed the oiran bears and opened the way. And Ram, who had clung to the carriage window, braced for emergencies. Amid the group, having stopped the carriage and gathered face to face on the sand, Subaru — who had halted the advance on his own judgment — had shrunk into himself.

"But I think Subaru's judgment wasn't wrong. If we'd just kept trying to push straight through the flower field the whole way, I think something like what just happened would keep happening. And then there's no guarantee we'd get through as smoothly as we did just now......"

"Are you doubting my ability, big sister? Does that mean you trust mister more than me?"

"We've come this far, after all. I trust both of you. But especially with what Subaru says, I want to believe it — that's my opinion. Sorry."

"E·M·M......"

Emilia-tan is truly a goddess, standing tall to counter Meili's childish rebuttal. Subaru, pressing his palms together in worship beside her, watched as Beatrice carefully fixed her tangled hair and clothes, and said,

"So, what exactly do you intend to do? If the Witchbeast girl's Divine Protection is unreliable, pushing through this flower field is reckless in the extreme, I suppose. ...... If Nii-cha were here, wiping out this many Witchbeasts might have been no trouble at all, but that's not an option, I suppose."

"If Puck were here, sure. Maybe I could try my best?"

"That's a card I'd really like to save for the very last resort. Besides, even if we wipe out all the oiran bears here, I feel like other Witchbeasts would just move in, and it'd be an infinite spawn. What do you think, Meili?"

Prompted, Meili — who had been sulking — averted her eyes. She rested her chin on her drawn-up knees, in a manner utterly childlike, and said,

"What's the'at?"

"What I'm saying is that maybe those oiran bears weren't the only things around that flower field. To put it bluntly, even if the oiran bears disappear, it'd probably just become a home for some other Witchbeast."

"...... That's not not right, maybe."

"Which is it."

"That's ri~ight, mister's right. But even so, what are you gonna do about it?"

While affirming Subaru's point, Meili turned the question back on him. At her sullen gaze, Julius shrugged at Subaru, whose mouth twisted into a frown.

"Miss Meili's point is understandable as well. Subaru's concern is reasonable, but that said, being overly cautious about every unforeseen event gains us nothing. If something can be anticipated and dealt with, it isn't called an unforeseen event to begin with."

"That doesn't mean charging in blind and relying entirely on Divine Protection without thinking is right either. Besides, if you're the one proposing that, that's out of character for you. That's more like — I dunno, Garfiel's job or something."

"Naturally, I am by no means advocating recklessness without a plan. What should be done ought to be decided after constructive discussion. Advance, or retreat — once again."

Just as he had right before entering the flower field, Julius raised two fingers and said this. At the gesture, Subaru snorted, and each of them turned their thoughts to the situation.

"What about having Barusu act as bait, and breaking through while the Witchbeasts devour him?" "Get on the same page as everyone else." "Ram and the others are saved, and it removes the nasty bug from Rem too. A splendid plan." "I'm the one being devoured!"

"I mean, maybe I really should just try hard and beat them all up......" "Emilia-tan, you're surprisingly brawn-over-brains at times like this, huh." "Brawn?...... Eh, wait, why so sudden? That's embarrassing." "Rare for me, but that wasn't an unconditional compliment for Emilia-tan, you know?"

"Relying on Miss Meili's Divine Protection, pushing through the flower field as far as possible...... and when we reach the limit, launching a preemptive strike and dashing for the watchtower. That carries too strong an element of gambling." "Can't really explain it well, but that'll fail. Can't explain it well, but for sure." "Hm...... you speak as if you have absolute grounds for that." "Well, who knows. Can't explain it well, though."

Trying to shoot down the charge-in plan without touching on the taboo of 『Return by Death』 was a chore. And so, in the middle of a discussion that could hardly be called a strategy meeting for how little progress it was making, Subaru had a realization and clapped his hands.

Having prioritized dealing with the initial Jaian shock and the subsequent backtracking through the flower field, he'd forgotten to sort it out, but — the cause of death in the two 『deaths』 that had triggered his returns in the first place. The first was too sudden, and the second amid too much confusion, for him to say anything very definite — but Subaru's cause of death, in both cases, was related to the tower straight ahead lighting up.

"Did anybody see that tower light up?"

"The watchtower...... lighting up?"

At Subaru's question, everyone tilted their heads, puzzled expressions on their faces. At the unpromising reaction, Subaru groaned, realizing after all that no one had seen it. In a way, that was only natural. Frankly, the times Subaru had seen that light were right before and after his 『deaths』. If that light carried hostile intent, anyone who saw it wouldn't live to tell the tale. He should think of it as an attack that had achieved a guaranteed kill on sighting the enemy.

"So, Natsuki-kun, you're saying you saw that tower light up?"

"Huh? Ah — I don't think I was seeing things, but, maybe it lit up."

"When was this?"

"...... While we were inside the flower field, I think?"

Under Anastasia's pressing questions, his answers kept coming out as guesses. Still, since there had been circumstances preventing him from raising his voice inside the flower field, his having stayed silent about the tower's light didn't seem to arouse suspicion. Since every time Subaru had seen the tower's light was tied to a time period connected with 『Return by Death』, this too could, depending on how things went, brush up against the taboo. It was a bit regrettable that he could only convey the facts in such a roundabout way, but—

"If the tower was lighting up, does that mean the Sage inside was looking this way?"

"Even without that, it's nighttime, isn't it — makes sense they'd be using some kind of light, no?"

"I see. If the 『Sage』 has noticed us, it might be worth reaching out from our side to signal that we bear no hostility."

However, because Subaru had deliberately kept the details vague, Emilia and the others' thoughts drifted in a different direction. Naturally so, since this was a journey seeking friendly contact. If the cause of that light was the Sage, it was only natural such opinions would arise. But to Subaru, who knew that light was by no means friendly toward them, that was nothing but a reckless charge straight toward a cliff edge.

"Wait, it's a bit late, but let's organize the information first. Whether to call out to the Sage comes after that."

"Organize the information?"

"Right, organize it. I mean, look — that light might not necessarily be the Sage's household lighting. It could be more like...... dangerous, some kind of searchlight-type light, right?"

"Sa-chi-rai-to......"

"In other words, Natsuki-kun, you're suspecting the light you saw might be something like a defense mechanism the 『Sage』 set up, not just everyday illumination?"

"That's it!"

While Emilia furrowed her brow at the unfamiliar loanword, Anastasia = Eridna managed to grasp the true meaning behind Subaru's words. Hearing this, Julius fell into thought. The knight murmured "I see" in a somewhat lowered voice, and said,

"Whether the Sage has, without exaggeration, lived on for several hundred years is unknown. Or perhaps some legacy the Sage left behind is functioning as a device to protect the world."

"O-oh...... hadn't thought that far, but that's possible."

"If that's the case, it would explain why not only the ruffians scheming to revive the 『Witch of Envy』, but even the kingdom's envoy delegation, have been kept away. Why the Sage, who ought to be friendly to the kingdom, would engage in what might as well be called outright violence — that's something that's puzzled me all along."

At Julius's words, which sounded somehow relieved, Subaru was struck with admiration in turn — so that's a way of thinking about it too. Indeed, it was a common enough scenario for a defense system prepared by some great figure of the past to remain endlessly in place even after its master's death, continuing to reject intruders thereafter. In a world with Magic Vessels, it wouldn't be strange for such a system to exist.

"Though in that case, I'd appreciate it if this whole journey didn't end up being for nothing......"

"It's fine even if the Sage is dead, so long as there are documents the Sage left behind. It's too early to despair of the world, Barusu."

"I can't hold a candle to how ruthlessly you plunder ruins, sister."

If Julius's speculation turned out true, the very foundation of this journey would be overturned. But even against such a possibility, Ram's stance didn't waver, didn't shake, didn't change. Impressive, as ever.

"Anyway, staying awake and keeping watch for several hundred years without sleep...... wasn't realistic, but it seems there's a chance of it, I suppose. And I don't think we need to be so pessimistic about whether Sage Shaula is alive or dead, y'know?"

"Really? Why?"

"For a long-lived race, it's nothing to be surprised about that someone's still alive even now. Take my Echidna — she herself was born hundreds of years ago too."

Pulling out the white scarf tucked inside her robe, Anastasia laughed. Emilia blinked at this, but soon touched her own pendant and said,

"W-well, my Puck is long-lived too. He's, um, not going to lose to Echidna."

"Why are you competing, Emilia-tan. Well, my Bea-ko's a fair Lolibaba too, though. Right?"

"Do you truly think Betty would laugh and answer that? It makes Betty's skin crawl, I suppose."

At Beatrice's furious-laughing rebuke, Subaru shrank his shoulders. At that point, Ram clapped her hands, noting the conversation had strayed.

"Whether the Sage is alive or dead is outside the scope of discussion. More importantly, the question is the true nature of the light Barusu saw. Whether it's something dangerous, or a delusion on Barusu's part — we need to reach a conclusion."

"Could we maybe reach a level of relationship, at some point, where you don't have to question whether my reports are lies or true?"

"The fact that Ram isn't assuming it's a lie from the start is already Ram making a concession."

That might be true, Subaru thought vaguely, half-convinced — was that why there was no progress? In any case, the discussion circled back around to the true nature of the light.

"I think...... that it's something dangerous. At the very least, not something friendly."

"Your basis is — just a hunch?"

"...... Well, yeah."

"I see. — Troublesome."

Subaru was convinced that the watchtower's light — that it was the cause of his two 『deaths』 — but conveying that fact was extremely difficult. So he'd thought pushing the reasoning through on a mere 'hunch' would be a stretch, but surprisingly, Ram took his answer seriously and fell into thought. And it wasn't just Ram — Emilia and Julius were the same.

"Eh? That, it's a hunch, you know? Shouldn't you doubt it more?"

"If it were just an ordinary hunch, maybe I'd think that, but it's Subaru's hunch, isn't it? In that case, rather than doubting it outright, there's meaning in taking it seriously."

"You needn't belittle yourself so much. You too have crossed through your fair share of dire straits up to now, haven't you? There exists a certain kind of intuition that only works in people like that. Call it an empirical rule if you like...... but it's by no means something to scoff at."

"Field mice change their nests before a heavy rain. Barusu's hunch isn't something to scoff at either."

"That's plenty scoffable...... wait, huh."

Midway through his conditioned-reflex retort, the sting drained out of him, and Subaru felt his shoulders loosen somehow. The absurdity of a situation where he couldn't reveal the source of his information, forced to hide the truth even from his own companions. Subaru had run into this same situation many times before, and each time, he was saved by the consideration and trust of those around him this way, only to repeat the same thing again.

In the royal capital, Rem; in the 『Sanctuary』, Otto and Ram; in Priestella, Emilia and Beatrice — they had believed that Subaru was Subaru, and now, once again, here it was.

"Even if we tried calling out in a friendly way, we don't have a means to do it. If I tried something like shouting real loud, before the Sage even hears it, it'll be 'welcome, Mr. and Mrs. Bear' instead."

"That's something we'd want to avoid. In that case, the realistic option would be...... having Emilia-sama's magic reach it, I suppose?"

Gazing toward the watchtower, Julius extended his right hand and closed one eye. It was a gesture just like a painter gauging distance. And after remaining silent like that for a while, he said,

"Roughly speaking, it's about ten kilometers from here to the watchtower — sending Emilia-sama's magic from just before the flower field isn't a realistic range."

"By the way, how far can Emilia-tan's magic actually reach?"

"Distance? Um, I've never tried measuring it so I don't know...... but I don't think it could reach all the way to the tower from here. It'd fade out before it even hit."

Compared to the silhouette of the watchtower visible before the 『Sand Hour』, the silhouette from their current point was clearly much closer. In fact, on the loop where they'd cut through the flower field, they'd approached quite close right up until the end. Even so, a plan to aim at the tower from before the flower field wasn't realistic.

"To begin with, if just conveying our presence were enough to be let through, even Reinhard should be able to get through, right? Couldn't he, like...... fire off some slash that reaches ten kilometers or whatever?"

"Whether that's possible or not can only be answered by asking him, but when Reinhard made his attempt, he likely didn't follow the process of passing through the 『Sand Hour』. The reason we've been able to get this far is because Anastasia-sama — no, Echidna knew about it."

"Guess that's true too...... Ah, damn it. Isn't there anything else you know?"

With his idea shot down, Subaru vented his frustration on Eridna. In response, Echidna, playing at Anastasia, skillfully worked her scarf-form self, and said,

"You say that, but it troubles me. Being able to assert that a path exists on the far side of the 『Sand Hour』 is already an unprecedented achievement in itself. Shouldn't you be praising me more?"

"You're only a guide once you actually arrive somewhere, aren't you? 'I remembered the way partway there' — are you some newspaper delivery boy who half-remembers his route? That's not doing your job."

"Hmph, a fair point."

At Subaru's rough logic, Echidna didn't object. The scarf fell silent, and that pensive posture seemed to spread to Anastasia as well. Then Anastasia suddenly lifted her head and said,

"That's right. In that case, it'd be best to nail down what we know at this point, wouldn't it."

"What we know, meaning......"

"Natsuki-kun's suspicious tower light, that too, and the Witchbeast flower field spread out before the tower, that too. Then there's the three 『Sand Hours』, and...... anything else?"

The information confirmed so far, laid out by Anastasia. Building a logical path, paving the road to a conclusion, was important. Subaru crossed his arms, recalling the journey up to now and the danger he'd glimpsed just a little further ahead.

"That right now is the spot where we chose to enter during the nighttime 『Sand Hour』. And that pushing through that flower field puts too much strain on the Earth Dragon. Also, Meili's brought a few Witchbeasts along without telling us, hasn't she."

"Ueh!?"

At Subaru's words, counting off on his fingers, Meili's shoulders jumped. At her blatant reaction and the way she shrank in on herself, everyone's eyes turned to her. At this, Meili hurriedly shook her head and said,

"W-wait, wait a second. It's true, mister's right, but...... how does mister know that?"

"Just a guess. That was a fishing expedition just now."

"But it seems Subaru's guess was correct. — What should we do?"

Julius quietly asked how to handle Meili, the traitor. Meili's face paled at his tone, but Subaru had no intention of making a big deal of it. He held out a hand to stop Julius and met Meili's eyes.

"Relax. I'm not saying this to scold you. I get that it's your own kind of insurance policy. Well, it did become a reason for suspicion, though."

"So...... should I let 'em go? If I do that, you won't be mad?"

"No need to let them go, either. In fact, if you think it's necessary, use them. It'd be a huge help, like, when we're surrounded by a pack of oiran bears and being chased."

"――――"

Perhaps thinking it was a joke, Meili blinked wide-eyed, then nodded quietly. Watching this through, Subaru turned back to Julius, whose wary gaze hadn't loosened yet.

"She's a kid doing kid things. Cut her some slack."

"If she were a child who could only manage things befitting her treatment as such, I too would gladly do so with all my heart. But her power is too great to trust unconditionally. Especially in this land of Auguria, which one could call a garden of Witchbeasts, no less."

"All the more reason, then. You two are neighbors in the carriage, so make sure to get along."

At Subaru's words of persuasion, Julius, after a moment's hesitation, sheathed his fighting spirit. Then, in place of the now-silent Julius, Emilia raised her hand.

"So, Subaru. About what we were talking about before—"

"Mm, yeah yeah. The rest of it."

"If pushing through the flower field is out, then...... what do we do? Try the same thing with a different 『Sand Hour』 and see if we come out somewhere else?"

"That's one option, but...... there's something I want to try."

"Something you want to try?"

Unable to guess at Subaru's thinking, Emilia blinked, puzzled, tilting her head. Matching the angle of Emilia's tilted head, Subaru turned his gaze toward the flower field's direction — the sea of flowers stretching all the way to the watchtower — toward its edge. Of course, that edge couldn't be seen at all — but,

"If the flower field breaks off somewhere, then at least we won't have to worry about being targeted from all four directions. Isn't it worth testing whether there's an edge to it?"

—Everyone ended up joining the plan to go check the edge of the flower field.

"If we act separately and get split up, that defeats the purpose. And besides, I'm not comfortable having anyone advance right alongside the Witchbeasts without Miss Meili's assistance."

Julius's reasoning had won everyone's agreement. Subaru agreed with this too, and chose to prioritize safety over the ease of going alone.

"Well then, it's about time we headed out, but......"

Each of them finished their preparations for departure, boarding the carriage and the Earth Dragon respectively. Heading in the direction of left, with the agreed-upon rule of always keeping the watchtower's silhouette on their right, the group's exploratory direction would be north, roughly speaking. Though that depended on whether the rules of east-west-north-south still held within the sand dunes.

"Thinking calmly about it, this place is pretty much a bizarre pocket of space-time too, huh. 'We passed through the middle of the 『Sand Hour』 and ended up here' is pretty close to a 『Door Crossing』......"

"I don't disagree with what Subaru's getting at, I suppose. Indeed, warping space against space to blur physical distance is, in a sense, an application of 『Door Crossing』. Betty pulled something like that on Subaru once before, I suppose."

"You did? To me? When?"

"...... How could Betty forget — the very first time we met, I suppose."

"The first time we met...... ah, wait! At Roswaal's mansion, when I one-shot-cleared that hallway that would've looped infinitely! Sorry about that, back then! You worked so hard preparing it, and I went and trampled all over your effort...... my bad, okay?"

"It genuinely infuriates Betty that you're apologizing so genuinely! As if Betty would forget, I suppose!"

"You're the one who brought it up......"

"Argh! I suppose, I suppose!"

Riding double with him, Beatrice suddenly flew into a rage for some reason, and Subaru could only furrow his brow at the particular difficulty of handling girls. For now, he hugged her to calm her down, then had Patrasche walk over toward the carriage. And then,

"How's it looking on your end?"

"No problems. All that's left is for Emilia-sama to do her part."

"Right."

Nodding to Julius, who'd finished his preparations at the driver's seat, Subaru looked up at the roof. Just as during the march through the flower field, Emilia's position was fixed atop the carriage roof. Raising a hand to Emilia, who somehow looked rather pleased with herself standing up high, Subaru requested the setup of a 'marker,' just as before.

"Emilia-tan, right here please. For now, this is our base camp. As quietly as possible...... you know, in a way that doesn't provoke the Witchbeasts."

"Mm, got it. If we wake the Witchbeasts up with this, there'd be no point in having quietly gotten away, right? Quietly, quietly, quie~etly."

As Subaru made slow, broad hand gestures, Emilia mimicked them exactly, and before their eyes, a tower of ice steadily took shape on the sandy ground. The judgment to proceed quietly didn't seem to be a lie — indeed, the ice tower being built up rose at a gentle pace, without the usual tension-charged sound in the air. Some hesitance crept in, resulting in a slightly slender finish, but it looked like it would fulfill its role without incident.

"Is it...... okay?"

"OK, no problem. As expected, Emilia-tan."

Making a big O-K sign to reassure the anxious Emilia, Subaru glanced warily toward the flower field. Fearful that the Witchbeasts might be sensitive to the working of mana and react to this too, he checked cautiously, but fortunately, there was no reaction from the flower field beyond the sweet fragrance of nectar. They seemed to be quite dull creatures.

"A bit late to ask, but what exactly are those things trying to do, disguising themselves as a flower field like that......? If it's meant to be camouflage, isn't it weird that they're doing it on top of sand in the first place?"

"You shouldn't expect Witchbeasts to have that much fine intelligence, I suppose. They basically can't do anything, and won't do anything, beyond what's carved into their instincts. That's the kind of creature they are, I suppose."

"I see. Well, that's quite a—"

Beatrice, nestled against him, delivered a scathing assessment. Subaru began to nod along to her words, but as he turned his gaze away from the flower field to give the order to depart — he noticed. At the edge of his field of vision turned toward the flower field, there was a change in a place different from the flower field.

The watchtower had lit up.

"—Emilia!!"

"—!?"

Subaru cried out on impulse, calling up to Emilia on the roof. At the urgency in his voice, Emilia's body went rigid. But the result of the light surpassed even that.

"—!?"

With a sharp, piercing sound, the ice tower was struck by something and blown to smithereens. Slender, and over ten meters tall, that ice tower had been anything but fragile. And yet the attack shattered the ice as easily as crumbling a sandcastle, reducing it back to mana.

—Once again, he hadn't seen it. But he understood.

"So it's true, that light is an attack—"

"Subaru! The next one's coming, I suppose!"

Showered with fragments of shattered ice, Subaru turned his head at Beatrice's cry. Right after, the watchtower lit up once more, and he understood instinctively. The next strike of light had been fired aiming at Subaru.

—The light bore down on him at a speed that allowed not even a blink, gouging out a vital point with a single strike.

The extent of that hopeless power, Subaru already knew from having died to it once before. And so, the instant his brain judged he'd been marked as the target by that light, his 'death' was already decided.

"――――"

The light thrust straight for Subaru's forehead. Leaving even the wind behind, silently skewering its prey — it was 'death' itself, plain and simple. Natsuki Subaru was once again, helplessly, mercilessly, killed by it.

"—E·M·M" "I suppose!"

—Had Natsuki Subaru been all alone in the instant he was targeted—

A crisp sound rang out, and the attack that should have struck Subaru directly was deflected. The cause was, quite simply, that Subaru's durability had exceeded the power of that attack.

"E·M·M" was one of the three original spells Subaru had developed together with Beatrice. And this was one of them — the "Absolute Defense Magic."

While E·M·M was active, Subaru became unable to move, but in exchange, he became impervious to all interference from outside. It was the result of mobilizing the full extent of Beatrice's Shadow Magic technique and knowledge to warp, in a limited fashion, the time and space surrounding Subaru.

"Kind of a bummer knowing it has a similar effect to that scumbag's power, but— buah!?"

The Archbishop of Greed, Regulus, had stopped the flow of time on his own body through the transfer of hearts. E·M·M could, in a way, be called a downgraded version of that ability. However, it carried none of the drawback of the heart stopping, and so long as his mana didn't run out, he could remain invincible for as long as he wanted. Though in this situation, it offered no measure beyond buying time.

"Still, goeh!? Buying time gruoh!? Something important buer!?"

"Wait! Subaru, all you're doing is screaming, I suppose!? Are you okay, I suppose!?"

Beatrice's worried voice rose as she circled behind Subaru, using his body as a shield. And no wonder. After all, even with defense active, sound and impact still got through. After E·M·M activated, the watchtower's light — as if puzzled that Subaru wasn't dying, or perhaps testing to confirm it — began pouring shot after shot after shot in rapid succession.

"Go!? Ba!? Rodo!?"

The speed of that rapid fire and the accuracy of its aim were beyond ordinary. The pain and impact force of being hit didn't remain in Subaru's body, but sadly, it's human nature to let out a pained groan on conditioned reflex whenever something makes contact.

"Subaru――! Oh, geez...... enough of this!"

Watching Subaru take the concentrated assault, Emilia moved to cover him from atop the roof. As if to shield Subaru from the watchtower's attack, a thick, massive ice wall rose up from the sandy ground. Presumably the highest-hardness defensive wall Emilia could produce in an instant. It served, unerringly, as a shield against the attacks fired at Subaru — but,

"No way!?"

"Even that ice wall can't hold up against it!"

Just as Emilia's scream and Julius's astonishment indicated, the ice wall was shattered in a single hit. Not just unbelievable power — either. Piercing through the ice wall, the light, its momentum somewhat diminished, slammed into Subaru. Bouncing off, several of them stuck into the sandy ground, sand hissing up in white smoke. Looking closer, the marks of destruction on Emilia's ice wall looked less like they'd been punched through than melted away.

"Heat? Did the heat burn through the ice?"

"That thing that fell to the ground was...... a needle?"

Seeing the details of the attack, Emilia and Julius arrived at different conclusions simultaneously. But even as they did, the attack continued. Subaru was taking a barrage of hits at an insane rate, exceeding even the limits of his invincible state.

"Subaru! There's a problem, I suppose!"

"Don't want to hear it, buhu—! rippo!"

"At this rate we won't get anywhere, and mana's about to run out soon, I suppose!"

"Already!?"

"It's tight, I suppose!"

At Beatrice's cry, Subaru gritted his molars while still under fire. Naturally, what Beatrice was trying to convey wasn't "mana's running out, so it's time to pay the piper." It was "mana's running out, so we should counterattack."

"Can you pull it off!?"

"What a foolish question, I suppose!"

To the question, Beatrice didn't answer that she couldn't. At his reliable partner's answer, Subaru released his gritted molars and steeled himself.

"The third one's incomplete...... we're going with the second one!"

"The instant we take the hit, we cut E·M·M, I suppose."

There was no time. Subaru shot Emilia a glance, and Emilia, receiving it, nodded. There was no time to discuss the specifics of the plan. But he trusted it had gotten across.

"—Goh!?"

The light flew, and once again Subaru took a direct hit. While E·M·M was active, that attack couldn't get through — that was about to be released here. Each of the original spells was a limited trump card usable only once a day. Once E·M·M was released, Subaru would lose his defense against that light.

And before the next shot arrived, he had to execute his counterattack, but—

"The light—"

As if it had read Subaru's actions, the speed of the light's next shot came faster. The speed and power, different from before, were clearly aiming to finish Subaru off. Naturally, with Subaru's paper-thin defense, a single direct hit would send him to the afterlife. That, however—

"Stop right there——!"

A multitude of magical vortexes deployed in layers before Subaru, forming ice walls on the sand one after another in rapid succession, creating six defenses at once where one alone hadn't been enough. The light was drawn straight into those six walls. The first was breached easily, the second just as effortlessly, the third as if it wasn't even there. But the fourth showed slight resistance, and by the fifth, it barely held on for a fraction of a second. And at the last, the sixth wall, the light's speed clearly slowed. — But it broke through anyway.

Piercing through the sixth ice wall, the light thrust toward Subaru's defenseless forehead. —An instant before impact, a knight's sword traced a half-circle, catching the slowed light and cutting it away.

"Unfortunately, there are several women who would grieve if he were hurt—"

Rushing in from the carriage, disregarding the danger, Julius had swept the light aside with his blade. Weakened by Emilia's defense, stopped by Julius. Feeling a faint pang of jealousy at their combination technique, Subaru's preparations reached completion.

"Here we go! Round two! Absolute Negation Magic — 'E·M·T'!!"

Flipping back the hood he'd worn against the sand, Subaru opened his mouth wide and chanted. At this, Beatrice, hidden behind his back, drew mana out from within Subaru, combining her own magical power with his to create a new spell.

"――――"

The next moment, a faint light expanded, centered on Subaru and Beatrice, forming a spherical field over ten meters across that encompassed the carriage. What resulted looked, to the eye, transparent — as if they had simply wrapped themselves in a sphere of light. And yet its effect was tremendous.

"This is......"

Before the astonished Julius, light was fired once again from the watchtower. Catching it in the corner of his eye, he instinctively readied his sword, but soon his eyes widened. The light shot straight for Subaru, plunging into this field of light, and the moment it did, it lost all momentum. That overwhelming speed, surpassing even a bullet's, vanished without a trace, leaving behind in reality nothing but a strange, white, slender object that had lost both speed and power. It was easily deflected by Julius's swinging sword, falling to the sand and dissipating.

"A needle, is it?"

"Seems that way. No, more than that, Subaru, this magic is......"

"Wait wait wait wait! I've got a ton I want to talk about, but retreat comes first right now! Get on the carriage now! We're getting out of here! This won't hold for long!"

"—! Understood! Let's hurry!"

At the urgent call, Julius swallowed his questions and leapt onto the carriage. Grabbing the reins immediately, he turned the carriage's head toward the other side of the 『Sand Hour』. Heading toward the watchtower now would be suicide. He had no choice but to trust this was the right call.

"Here we go! Don't get too far from me! Make sure you stay inside this light!"

"Wow, Subaru, amazing! Even with so many lights flying in......"

Atop the moving carriage, Emilia, clinging to the roof, stared at Subaru in astonishment. It seemed that even after realizing it had no effect, the light kept pursuing Subaru regardless. But no matter how many times it attacked, the light lost all its former force the instant it entered the E·M·T field, simply falling to the sand. Neutralization, complete.

"Even so, what tremendous power. Is this your and Beatrice-sama's trump card?"

"One of the trump cards! I'll tell you now, I'm not planning on spoiling anything! More than that, right now just focus on getting away from the watchtower as fast as possible! No, seriously."

"I imagine it's an application of Shadow Magic, but — could it be nullifying the effects of mana within its range? If so, it's the natural enemy of mages...... no, for those who use mana to supplement their skills, you are a fearsome existence indeed."

"I'll tell you now, this just makes it look like I've got a relative advantage, it doesn't mean I've gotten stronger. I'd lose flat-out to anyone strong in a plain fight. This is a power I developed thinking I'd give Roz-cchi a taste of his own medicine someday."

Seeing Julius genuinely impressed, offering his praise, Subaru felt a bit pleased himself. Of course, that was aided by the relief of having escaped the watchtower's attack, but in any case, it was nothing but sheer 'carelessness.' Still, that no one — not even Subaru himself — could have realized the consequences that 'carelessness' would bring at this point meant no one could be blamed for it.

"Subaru! Are we heading back to the dunes in front of the flower field like this?"

"Bit rough, but let's do it! Anyway, once we work out a countermeasure—"

Saying that, as he rushed back toward the swirling sandy wind of the 『Sand Hour』 — the instant the E·M·T field made contact, 'the world' tore open.

"Ah—?"

"Oh no, I suppose!"

Beatrice, who until then had let her body relax in relief at having survived the assault, jolted upright. She glared at the scenery ahead — the night dunes tearing apart — her voice trembling.

"The warp in space is being nullified by E·M·T, I suppose!"

"Eh?"

"Betty told you already, I suppose! That the warp here is similar to the 『Door Crossing』 Betty showed you at the mansion! Meaning, the distorted space is returning to normal, I suppose!"

"What does that mean it'll—"

Subaru's question never made it to the end, and all that remained was the sensation of Beatrice clinging to him. Just like that, 'the world' was torn apart in a jumble, like paper being ripped, unraveling, and within that fissure, Subaru and the others, along with Patrasche, and Emilia and the others, along with the carriage, were swallowed whole.

"This is bad...... Emilia!?"

"Subaru——"

Flung into darkness, losing all sense of gravity's pull, Subaru screamed. Up, down, left, right — all sense of them vanished, and he had no idea where the carriage was. But he heard Emilia's voice, answering his cry, grow distant.

"This is——"

Before he could finish saying it was bad, he was flung out to the other side of the torn space.

"――――"

The boundary between the flower field and the sand sea tore and frayed, and a small group could be seen swallowed within it.

"――――"

Watching it from afar, from afar, a shadow stirred within the tower. Moving away from the window it had been leaning against, it stepped across the flagstones, descending the spiral staircase. Its footsteps were slow at first, but gradually quickened, growing impatient.

"—Found you."

It let slip a hoarse murmur, as if it hadn't spoken in years. But that its resonance was one of joy — no one could possibly mistake it for anything else.

"Found you."

Only that much was certain.
