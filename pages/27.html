Arc 2 Chapter 3: "I Can't Get Married"

At Emilia's sudden visit, Subaru couldn't help but feel his heart leap.

Her surprisingly active outfit was a fresh, pleasant surprise, but even more pleasing was simply the fact that she was here with him.

Having been thrown from one stranger to the next—especially given that the very first of them had put him through hell—he felt something special for the girl who had been the first to show him kindness after being summoned to another world.

"At this point, this feeling's basically imprinting. I don't want to be apart from you for even a moment, Mom!"

"I heard Beatrice played a prank on you while you were low on blood, so I was a little worried... but I guess that was wasted worry."

"Being a disaster right after waking up is just how I was born! And, well, I'm kind of scared to ask, but..."

To the dubious-looking Emilia, Subaru poked his two index fingers together and, peering up at her timidly, began.

"So... um, do you actually remember me properly?"

"That gesture—I really don't like it. And what a weird question. I don't think I could easily forget someone as memorable as you, Subaru."

Her mouth curving into a small smile as she said his name made Subaru's shoulders sag with relief. And on top of that, the fact that a girl was calling him by his first name with no honorific left him blushing with a sincerity rare for him—red enough to reach his ears.

Setting his slightly bashful, novice-like attitude aside for the moment, the twins—who had been chasing after Emilia's entrance—came running up to her side. The two of them circled around behind Emilia, then peeked their faces out from either side to eye Subaru.

"Please listen, Emilia-sama. Onee-sama suffered a terrible humiliation at that man's hands." "Listen up, Emilia-sama. That man confined and violated Rem."

"As if he'd pull something like that on you two... Well, Subaru might try, but there's no way he could actually manage it. Ram, Rem, don't tease someone who just got out of a sickbed too much."

"Yes, Emilia-sama. Onee-sama is reflecting on her actions too." "Yes, Emilia-sama. Rem thinks she's reflected as well."

Called by name as Ram and Rem, the two declared their "reflection" without a shred of actual remorse showing. Whether from being used to their attitude or not, Emilia didn't seem particularly bothered and turned to look at Subaru.

"So, is your body all right? Nothing feels strange anywhere?"

"Hm, oh—now that you mention it, before I fell asleep my whole body felt like it was on fire and I thought I was going to die, but that feeling's completely gone now. If anything, I slept so much I'm a little sluggish."

"That's right. It's been almost a full day since I met you, Subaru, and you spent more than half of it asleep."

That can't be right, Subaru tried to protest, recalling everything that had happened up to now—but the words caught hard in his throat.

By his own reckoning, the total time it had taken him just to survive through "yesterday" probably added up to more than twenty-four hours—a contradiction in itself.

From her perspective, though, her encounter with Subaru couldn't have lasted even a full hour. And the words they'd exchanged couldn't be called many, either.

This was no time to be satisfied with a good night's sleep. He needed to go through the conversation events with her that he should have gotten in the first place.

"Sleeping too much and feeling sluggish is a problem in its own right... And on top of that, waking up in the morning is super rare for me."

"What kind of life do you have to lead for that to happen...?"

"Well, you know, nighttime's quieter, no extra noise, so games go a lot smoother, right? Honestly, when you're a shut-in, broad daylight hours are basically just sleeping time."

"Somehow the more I hear, the sadder you sound, Subaru."

She probably hadn't understood most of what he'd said, but she seemed to have grasped the essence of it, expressing her exasperation with an easy-to-read sigh.

Finding something satisfying in each of her reactions, Subaru suddenly turned the topic to her casual outfit.

"Come to think of it, you're dressed pretty differently from usual. What were you up to?"

"Ah, please don't bring up the clothes. Even I'm not happy about it... As for what I was doing, I was just about to head out for my morning routine."

Emilia pressed a hand to her forehead, her words trailing off as if reluctant to say more. Her evasiveness was a little concerning, but that wasn't what Subaru pressed her about.

"Morning routine?"

"I borrow the mansion's garden and talk with the Spirits a little each morning. It's one of my Oaths."

"Chatting with Spirits, huh—how fantastical. I don't really get this 'Oath' thing, though..."

Hearing "Spirit," the first thing that came to mind was, naturally, a cat with gray fur.

He hadn't had much contact with that world this time around, but Subaru realized he was still looking forward to seeing him again—mostly to restock his fluffiness quota.

Without noticing, Subaru's hands started wiggling and grasping at the air. Emilia had already shifted into ignoring this odd behavior of his.

"Well, it's good that you're energetic, but a lot of people are still asleep at this hour, so don't make too much noise. Though telling you to behave yourself after you've been asleep this whole time is a bit harsh, I suppose."

"Yeah, I bet. Oh, but if it's a garden, is it on the larger side?"

"It's closer to a formal garden than just a 'garden,' really. Why do you ask?"

"Nah, well, if the condition is that you wouldn't mind me doing something in a corner of the garden while you're chatting with the Spirits, Emilia-tan..."

"As long as you're not running around shouting or anything, I don't really mind—wait, what did you just call me?"

"Alright, deal. I'm coming too."

"Hey, what did you just say? What's 'tan'? Where did that come from?"

Subaru dodged Emilia as she flustered and demanded an explanation. Taking a slightly spiteful tone as payback for earlier, he spun around toward Ram and Rem.

"Hey, maid sisters. You wouldn't happen to know where my clothes are, would you? Somehow I ended up in what looks like hospital wear, and I figure you're holding onto them here somewhere."

It hadn't come up until now, but Subaru's current outfit was something like a thin, reddish-brown work robe. The fabric was a bit too stiff to really call it hospital wear, but it was cooler and more comfortable to wear than it looked—quite excellent, really.

"But when it comes to moving my body, it still loses to a tracksuit. So, is it around somewhere? Though it might be ruined from the blood, for all I know."

"Would you know, onee-sama? Perhaps that filthy gray scrap of cloth?" "I know, Rem. Probably that mouse-gray rag all grimy with blood."

"You two are pretty bold, huh... yeah, that filthy, grimy rag. If it's still in one piece, please bring it here."

The twins looked to Emilia as if seeking permission. When Emilia's eyes signaled that it couldn't be helped, the twins nodded and pattered out of the room.

"Are you really okay, health-wise? Even to my eyes, it wasn't a shallow wound."

"But really, it's already perfectly, perrrfectly closed up. Besides, I don't want to let my body get lazy. I've already skipped a day of muscle training, and it'll take three days to make up for it—oh, right, that reminds me."

As if remembering something, he straightened his posture, tidied himself up, then turned to face Emilia properly. She looked puzzled by his behavior, but Subaru bowed his head to her.

"You're the one who healed my injuries, right, Emilia-tan? Thank you, you really saved me. Dying really is scary, honestly. Once is plenty."

"I'd think most people only die once, normally... No, wait, that's not it."

After delivering her routine retort, Emilia gave a small shake of her head.

Then, with a serious gaze—her violet-indigo eyes—she looked straight at Subaru. Captivated by their brilliance despite himself, Subaru fell silent.

"I'm the one who should be thanking you. Back there, you risked your life to save me even though you barely knew me. Treating your wounds is the least I could do."

With her sincere gaze conveying her gratitude so directly, all Subaru could do was let out an involuntary "auu."

He resented himself for being unable to give even a single sincere reply at a time like this. It seemed like he couldn't face her without joking his way through, deflecting.

How much easier it would be if he could reply "That's not it" to her claim that he'd saved her. That it was Emilia who had saved him first—but by now, the remnants of that other world existed only within Subaru himself.

"—Well then, how about we call it even—plus and minus canceling out to zero, since we helped each other out?"

"Plus and minus...?"

"No debts owed either way! So let's be friends, bro!"

If this had been someone from the slums, he could've easily thrown an arm around their shoulder here, but all Subaru could manage was to bluff his way through his embarrassment with sheer momentum.

At Subaru's bravado, Emilia let out a small laugh, and then—

"I don't think I need a weird little brother like this."

"That's a pretty harsh comment!?"

And on top of that, the letdown of being casually treated as her junior. Though, since he hadn't shown a single shred of anything reliable, he had no choice but to accept it as a fair assessment.

"We've brought it, honored guest." "Brought it for you, honored guest."

While all this was going on, the twins brought back his tracksuit—pink for the jacket, light blue for the pants. Fortunately, it seemed they'd had it washed with no trace of fresh blood left.

Subaru felt relieved seeing the tracksuit return looking unchanged at a glance. Then the two who'd rushed over eagerly started trying to strip his clothes off him.

"Whoa, whoa! I got it, I can do it myself! ...Though I'm a little happy about it."

"My, his true feelings are showing—pleasure winning out over humiliation, onee-sama." "Oh, happiness winning out over degradation—that's a pervert through and through, Rem thinks."

"The pink-haired one's subtly the more foul-mouthed one! I'm changing, so LET. GO!"

He shook off the twins' hands as they mobbed him and reclaimed his tracksuit.

Just as he was eagerly about to change into it, he suddenly noticed something.

"Come to think of it, who changed me into these clothes?"

"That was me. Ram and Rem were both out, so I was the only one available to change you while treating your wounds. Oh, and I wiped your body down properly too, though just lightly."

Emilia said this without the slightest hint of self-consciousness.

Subaru quietly peeked inside the bottoms he was wearing and confirmed the fact that even his underwear had been changed. He crumpled right where he stood, covering his face with his hands.

"I can't ever get married now..."

"If our genders were reversed, that'd be an understandable thing to say... Anyway, we don't have much time, so if we're going, let's go quickly."

Saying this with a briskness almost manly in its own right, Emilia clapped Subaru on the shoulder.

She was so utterly nonchalant about it that Subaru decided pressing the matter further would only dig his own grave, and let it go.

A beautiful girl had changed his clothes for him and seen everything. Well, that's not so bad—in a way, you could even call this a reward. Positive thinking.

Quietly wiping away his inner tears, Subaru stood up on his own two feet.

When he picked up his clothes and gave a meaningful glance, the women, sensing his intent to change, hurried out of the room.

The twins left the room first, and last was Emilia, her silver hair being cut off by the door. Then, just before her figure vanished beyond the door, her pale profile suddenly turned back.

"You don't need to worry. —You were quite impressive, after all."

With just that left behind, the door clicked shut.

Left alone in the room, Subaru got changed for the time being. The work robe's construction was hard to figure out and gave him some trouble, but he somehow managed to finish changing.

He folded the robe neatly and set it on the bed. Then he checked the feel of the tracksuit sleeves he'd put his arms through for the first time in hours, stretched with an "mmn," and preparations were complete.

"Now then..."

Swinging his hips in a big circle to crack his bones, Subaru looked at the window of the room.

Beyond the lightly drawn curtain, he could see the morning sun beginning to rise, blessing the still-early world. Narrowing his eyes at the warmth of that sunlight, Subaru then walked over to the bed and collapsed onto it.

"I can't ever get married now."

Pressing his face into the pillow, this time Subaru cried as a boy truly would.

That last bit of reassurance had hurt his heart the most of all. He thought he might die. He even thought he'd rather just die and start over.

But a heartache alone wasn't enough to kill him, and so Subaru spent a while longer crying like a girl, for reasons entirely a boy's own.
