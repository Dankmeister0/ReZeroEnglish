Arc 8 Chapter 6: "I Won't Beg for Forgiveness"

Blood poured from his gouged shoulder and his thigh, his ragged breathing burned with fever, and the color draining from his face was proof his life's countdown was already ticking away.

If he wasn't treated immediately, if that countdown wasn't stopped, his life would be in danger.

And yet, if healing magic were used, he would definitely survive. It was, without question, a wound that could be healed.

"So then, why...?"

Beside the blown-apart mill, amid the scorched-smelling smoke rising from scraps of wood still smoldering with embers, in the imperial capital where the floodwaters were slowly rising, Subaru stood face to face with an "enemy."

Yes—with Todd Fang, someone he could now call nothing but an "enemy."

—He hadn't wanted to call Todd that. An "enemy."

Ever since they'd first met, Todd had put him through hell, again and again. His life had been targeted more times than he could count, actually taken from him more than once, death and terror carved into him over and over, a grudge growing between them all the while.

Each time, Subaru had Returned by Death, dealt with the attacks Todd sprang on him, and in the end erased the massacres Todd caused, over and over.

The forest where the People of Shudrak lived had been burned, bringing them to total annihilation; in the surprise attack on the walled city of Guaral, Flop had been killed over and over; and in the operation to take that same city's town hall, he'd been made to feel like someone had set fire to the rope he was tightrope-walking across.

Worst of all was the massacre on the Sword Slave Island of Ginunhive. Tanza, Idra, Hiain, Weitz, Gustav, old man Null, and so many other companions from that isolated island had all been slaughtered—and yet, Subaru had overturned even that.

It wasn't that he'd deliberately set out to erase what Todd had done.

It was simply that, as a result, whenever Subaru Returned by Death and fought to protect what mattered to him, whatever Todd had caused ended up wiped away.

And so the reasons Subaru had for regarding Todd as an enemy existed only within Subaru himself, more and more.

And now, finally, with the imperial capital forced into a retreat under this unprecedented calamity, Todd had shown a willingness to join hands and cooperate with Subaru's group in order to protect his fiancée.

In fact, without Todd's help, how many retries would it have taken just to find an effective countermeasure against the overflowing zombies, let alone achieve everyone's escape?

The same was true of that battle against the one-eyed warrior, that particular zombie.

Defeating that enemy after it had transformed into a monstrous shape had required Todd's cooperation too. Both of them surely understood that if either one had been missing, neither would have survived.

So they'd been one step away.

One step away from Subaru being able to keep walking alongside Todd, lid still shut over feelings he couldn't quite resolve.

And yet—

"Why...!"

"Don't play dumb with me. You managed to stop that just now because you'd already half-figured it out. It's not that you reacted to killing intent—you'd just been on guard the whole time, hadn't you?"

Todd closed his bloodshot right eye at Subaru, who protested with a trembling voice.

With the expression of someone dealing with an unreasonable child, his gaze went to the axe floating in midair—or so it would appear to Todd's eyes: a weapon gripped by a black hand.

In Subaru's mind, the axe was synonymous with Todd.

He'd had his skull split open by it more than once or twice. Even having wrested it from Todd's hand, it did nothing to put his mind at ease, and with it at the edge of his vision, Subaru clenched his back teeth.

Todd had hit the mark. Subaru had been wary of him the whole time.

That was why, when he'd turned his back on the injured Todd, he'd activated Invisible Providence to protect his own head. If Todd were going to aim anywhere, Subaru had figured it would be the head—the one strike guaranteed to kill.

But he'd wanted Todd not to aim at all.

He'd wanted nothing at all to happen.

If that had been so, then—

"Slipped up, slipped up. I walked right into your little scheme."

"That's not it..."

"I figured now was the moment, with you separated from the others and the enemy dealt with... You're quite the actor."

"That's not it...!"

"Or were you hoping to blow me away too, in that explosion just now? If so, tough luck—your gamble didn't pay off."

"That's not it! I—I really meant to work with you...!"

"...Don't lie to me."

At that one cold word, a hiss, Subaru's throat made a sound of its own.

Still with one eye shut, Todd peered into Subaru's eyes with a gaze devoid of heat or warmth. In that gaze there was no longer any trace of the appraising look he'd worn toward Subaru until just a moment ago.

Todd had already finished weighing Subaru's worth. This was the result.

"Deep down, you must have known too. You and I can't understand each other."

"..."

"I doubt other people, and I do it forward-looking. You trust other people, and you do it looking backward. No matter how many words we line up, our values are just different."

Todd shifted his footing little by little, wary of Subaru with the floating axe as the pivot point between them. Subaru bit down hard on his lip, glaring at Todd through the hovering axe with anguish.

Todd had said they could never understand each other. Subaru kept turning it over—was that really true?

Had there really been no way to make it work?

"If you kill me here, how are you going to deal with the zombies..."

"Don't be such a poor loser. Once I'm out of the capital, I can manage just fine. Thanks to cooperating with you people, I got Katya as far as the city wall. From here on, I'm better off without you."

"Why?! I never meant to hurt Katya! I never meant to fight you either! And yet—!"

"...But you're under the impression you get to choose whether or not to save Katya, aren't you?"

"Ah...?"

Facing Subaru, who had raised his voice, Todd let fly that one cold remark.

Feeling as though he'd been doused with cold water by Todd's claim, Subaru's cheeks went rigid.

He didn't understand what that meant.

To begin with, it didn't line up with what Subaru was saying. Whether to choose or not to choose—Subaru had no intention of harming Katya in the first place, so that kind of accusation was flat-out wrong.

And yet, Subaru's thoughts came to a dead stop.

That single instant was one Todd did not let pass.

"...I won't beg you for forgiveness."

Slipping through the gap left by Subaru's momentary loss for words, Todd lowered his stance and lunged.

Every cell in Subaru's body screamed at the sense of the distance between them closing, and on instinct he commanded the Unseen Hand stretched out into the air to hurl the axe it had seized at Todd.

But—

"Gh—gaaah?!"

In that instant, at the searing sensation piercing the thigh of his right leg, Subaru's vision was dyed a deep red.

A knife was buried in the thigh of Subaru's right leg.

It was the one Todd had been holding until just moments ago—thrown in the instant Subaru's thoughts had stalled, right before he lunged in, and Subaru had taken it right in the leg.

With his thoughts scattered by the pain, the movement of the Unseen Hand that was supposed to throw the axe was interrupted—the airborne axe was snatched back, and Todd's front kick blasted into Subaru's chest.

"Oof—"

There was no way he could brace himself on the stabbed leg, and Subaru's body toppled backward with a groan of pain. His skull and back struck the ground hard, and his already-scattered thoughts scattered further still.

His leg, his chest, his head and back, and then—the blade of the axe falling right in front of his eyes—

"Aaaaaahh!"

Screaming recklessly, he wedged a black hand into the path of the descending axe.

The overworked Unseen Hand, jutting out from Subaru's chest, caught the axe just as it closed in on the tip of his nose, and mobilizing his own two hands as well so as not to let the trembling blade fall, he resisted the murderous intent with three arms.

And to force that contested blade down, Todd, gripping the axe, pushed with all his strength as well.

His stabbed leg hurt. His kicked chest hurt. His bruised back hurt. His head hurt just the same. And if this axe drove into his face, it would hurt worse than any of that—hurt enough to die.

"You're—so damn stubborn... Just die already, would you...!"

"No... I refuse... I absolutely refuse...!"

Pressing down on the axe's spine with both hands, Todd threw his whole body weight into killing Subaru.

Subaru resisted with three arms, including the Unseen Hand, but a child's two hands and an Authority whose only real advantage was invisibility weren't enough to push Todd back.

Seen from the outside, it was a killing match that was nothing short of comical and pathetic.

In the battle for the imperial capital of Lupugana, where countless soldiers and warriors had pitted honed skill and weaponry against one another, now at the moment of ultimate crisis, the fight between Subaru and Todd was graceless and clumsy.

No honed skill, no special weapon, no trump card to overwhelm the other.

By the standards of the Vollachia Empire, it was a life-or-death struggle on far too low a level—and that was the decisive battle between Natsuki Subaru and Todd Fang, two people who could never understand each other.

"You're so damn inconsistent! You go around with eyes that say you don't care if you die anytime, and you put other people's lives on your own selfish scales—and yet the second it comes down to this, you resist for all you're worth. It's disgusting!"

"D-don't say such... presumptuous things...! I don't think it's fine to die anytime! I've never put anyone's life on any scale...! I don't want to die!"

"Then die!"
"No!"

Todd's eyes, chilled through with pitch-black murderous intent, tried to freeze Subaru's heart, which was pounding like an alarm bell. The axe's blade strained between the two of them in a grinding stalemate, but this ferocious shoving match couldn't go on forever.

Because—

"Gh, ngh..."

Subaru, choking back a groan in the back of his throat, had blood trickling from his earlobe.

It was the backlash from overusing the Unseen Hand's Authority. It was eating away at Subaru's body, sounding an alarm. But he had no choice but to rely on it. Otherwise, the falling blade would split his face open.

"That strange trick of yours looks like it's about to hit its limit."

At the desperate look on Subaru's face and the bleeding, Todd, too, understood the backlash of the Unseen Hand. If this dragged on, Subaru would reach his limit first.

No matter what, before that happened—it was the very instant he thought that.

"...!?"

As Subaru and Todd strained against each other over the fatal axe, their attention was stolen for an instant by a tremendous roar.

It was, far off from Subaru and the others, away from the capital's center—the death cry of the reservoir at the innermost part of the city, beyond the Crystal Palace, the one holding back a vast quantity of water, as it finally reached its limit.

Along with the roar, cracks spread across the entire retaining wall, and the force and volume of the water pouring into the capital surged all at once. It was nothing less than a great wave crashing into a world without an ocean, and the surging waves sweeping the capital away shook the city, their aftershocks reaching all the way down to where Subaru and the other were locked in their killing struggle.

And then—

"Guh, aaaaahh—!!"
"...!"

In the split second when Todd's attention veered toward the roar, Subaru wrung out every last bit of strength he had.

Moving his body from where he lay on his back, forgetting even the pain of the knife in his leg just for this moment, he flipped himself sideways with all his strength, letting the axe blade gripped by the Unseen Hand slip past to strike the ground beside his head.

Todd, who had put his full weight into the axe, couldn't resist that momentum either. The axe fell right beside Subaru's head, and Todd collapsed onto the ground with it.

"Haah, haah... bwahh..."

Subaru kept rolling sideways with the momentum, putting distance between himself and Todd and the axe.

The knife in his leg struck the ground again and again, sending stabs of agony through him, but rather than the pain, it was the need to flee from what he wanted to escape that drove him on, and he kept rolling, crying, gritting through it.

Eventually, after what must have been ten or twenty rolls, Subaru's body slammed into the wreckage of a building, forcibly stopping him. There, somehow bracing himself on his arms, blood streaming from a cut on his forehead, he pushed himself up—and saw it.

"...You really are the most detestable thing, through and through."

Todd, kneeling, muttered it with loathing. In his left shoulder—turned now toward the distant Subaru—the axe blade was buried deep.

"..."

When Subaru, with a desperate resolve, had dodged the axe, Todd, conversely, had collapsed right onto it.

The axe blade had sunk in deep, splitting the collarbone and gouging into the flesh beneath. It was a wound severe enough that, depending on circumstances, it might have reached the heart—yet as Todd wiped his mouth and spat out blood, there was no weakness of the dying about him.

He was simply—yes, simply too tough.

He'd been carved up all over his body serving as bait against the zombies, taken damage from being caught in the dust explosion, and now on top of all that had an axe buried in his own body—and still he was perfectly composed.

The reason for that attitude of Todd's finally became clear even to Subaru's eyes.

It was—

"...So you saw it. Slipped up, slipped up."

With none of his usual flippancy or composure, Todd's tone carried a clear irritation.

A voice holding fierce anger—at himself, and above all at the other man—over the blunder of having something he must never let be seen, something he hadn't wanted seen, seen after all.

Seeping that anger, Todd roughly yanked the axe out of his own shoulder. The wound, roughly handled like that, spurted blood hard once—and then the bleeding stopped almost immediately.

It was the regenerative power of a race that had evolved so that the drawbacks of injury wouldn't work against them too much in combat or the hunt—a regenerative power Subaru had witnessed more than once in this other world.

There was nothing in his outward appearance that set him apart from Subaru, and yet he was a being with decisively different blood running through him.

A Demi-Human. And moreover—

"...Are you a half-beast?"

"Close. But that alone isn't the right answer."

In front of a stunned Subaru, Todd narrowed his eyes and confirmed only half of the suspicion. To Subaru, who caught his breath without understanding the real meaning, Todd supplied the rest of the answer.

Twisting his cheek in a cruel expression, kindling a blood-colored anger in his eyes—

"...I'm a Werewolf."

In this world, there are a handful of things loathed simply for existing.

The existence of Half-Elves occupies a position of particular prominence among such loathed things; as kin to the "Witch" who once nearly destroyed the world, their misfortune is shared across every nation.

Beyond that, in the Kararagi City-States, Demi-Human races without fur are considered ill-omened, while in the Holy Kingdom of Gusteko, the closer one's hair or eyes come to black, the more one is shunned as disliked by the Spirits.

And likewise, in the Vollachia Empire, where various Demi-Human races live intermingled, there are two races whose very existence is loathed—the Mole Humans, and the Wolf Humans.

In ancient times, it is said, the two races—Mole Humans and Wolf Humans—betrayed the woman most beloved in the Vollachia Empire and drove her to her death, and for that sin, they bear a guilt that can never be forgiven, for all time.

As a result, the Mole Humans abandoned their homeland, digging through the earth to flee beyond the nation's borders. The Wolf Humans, meanwhile, who knew no way to flee, were hunted down until they were wiped out—hunted to the very last one.

This empire's wrath extended even to other nations—should a Mole Human or a Wolf Human ever be found within another country's borders, they would be sent across the border to the Empire to be executed. This is, as it's commonly known, the history of the mole hunts and the wolf hunts.

In the present day, the Mole Humans are said to make use of their own gift, living hidden away beneath the earth where no eyes can find them, while the surviving Wolf Humans conceal their true nature by passing themselves off as Dog Humans to hide their origins—so much so that, across the entire world, there is only a single person who openly proclaims himself a Wolf Human: Halibel, "the Adulator," said to be the strongest in the Kararagi City-States.

The Empire that hunted the Wolf Humans down so relentlessly bears, ironically enough, a national crest of a wolf pierced by a sword.

The Sword-Wolf is, in the Empire, the thing most to be revered—while at the same time, the wolf that fled in fear of being pierced by the sword is the thing most to be spat upon.

For that reason, in this world, both the existence of the Wolf Humans and the existence of the wolf half-beasts who inherit the Wolf Humans' blood—the "Werewolves"—continue to bear a Curse that can never be forgiven.

"A... Werewolf..."

Subaru was struck speechless by Todd, who spun the axe he'd pulled from his own shoulder in his hand as he made that declaration.

Learning this unknown secret of Todd's, the man he'd clashed with so many times, left him unsure whether he should feel something positive or negative about it—but more than that, what he felt was a chill of dread.

It was an instinctive revulsion toward the very sound of the word "Werewolf" as it reached his ears.

—Subaru knew nothing of the history of the Wolf Humans' persecution in this world.

He knew nothing of the fact that the Wolf Humans and Mole Humans are loathed in the Empire, nothing of the reason being a betrayal against a woman named Iris who lived in ancient times, nothing that this Iris's true identity is Yorna Mishigure, nothing that the emperor she loved was not Vincent Vollachia.

He knew nothing of the background.

But even knowing nothing, Subaru's Soul understood—just how alien a thing called a "Werewolf" was, in this world.

"That's not quite the reaction I expected. You look surprised that I'm a half-beast, but not surprised that I'm a Werewolf specifically."

Watching Subaru fall silent, Todd knit his brow, his expectations thrown off.

"But now you can do this without any strange hesitation, right? Werewolves are meant to be strung up. That's the kind of Curse that flows in the blood. You too—"

"Why"

"Hm?"

"Why does it have to be that. I... you..."

He couldn't say he'd never once wanted to kill him.

But that wasn't about the blood that ran in Todd's veins, or his origins—it was because Todd's own actions were irreconcilable with Subaru's, and clashing was the only option left.

And yet the way Todd was putting it now—

"Don't talk like your blood is the reason you became my 'enemy.'"

"..."

"The reason you and I clashed, over and over, is a problem between you and me! It's not because of what you really are, and it's not because your blood set me off!"

Grinding his back teeth, pressing his fist into the ground, Subaru pushed his body upright.

The searing pain of the knife in his leg raced through his whole body with tremendous force, but if anything it cleared his mind, and made the anger boiling and roiling in his chest all the more distinct.

"Going and becoming a Werewolf on your own, like it's nothing...!"

"Hey now, 'on my own'? That's hardly something you get to order me around about..."

"Shut up! Why—why are you like this?!"

Not a single thing about him went the way Subaru wanted; whenever Subaru held some positive feeling, he would go and act in some negative way, and whenever Subaru felt positive, he would cause some negative problem.

He'd helped save Katya, lent a hand in rescuing Rem and the others, joined forces to defeat that formidable zombie—and just when Subaru thought that, he tried to kill Subaru, tore into his way of thinking, and on top of it all, turned out to be a Werewolf.

"Why is it like this?!"

"Don't go running your mouth about a man's birth. That's just how it is. Maybe my mother slept with a dog. Come to think of it, there's been this big dog living behind my house for as long as I can remember—maybe that's my old man?"

"That's not it at all! You're the one running your mouth about birth here!"

"..."

"The reason I'm glaring you down like this...!"

Subaru had no idea what kind of life Todd had walked, as a Werewolf.

He didn't even want to know. If he knew, he had a feeling he'd go looking for some reason to forgive Todd on account of it. So he didn't want to know, not at all. Subaru didn't want to forgive Todd for some unavoidable reason.

So—

"...I've decided."

Wringing the words out of himself, his mind on the verge of becoming a mess of anger and pain, Subaru muttered it.

Catching that murmur of Subaru's, Todd narrowed his eyes. Without a word, the silence pressed him to go on—what had Subaru decided?

Yielding to that pressure, Subaru told him.

"I won't kill you. I won't let things go the way you want."

"..."

To that quiet declaration of Subaru's, Todd said nothing in return.

But it wasn't that he kept silent. He laughed.

"Ha, hahaha, hahahahaha!"

Raking his hair back with the hand not holding the axe, Todd opened his mouth and laughed mockingly. Heedless of the blood smearing his hair and forehead, Todd roared with laughter, mouth wide open.

Then, after laughing his fill, he slowly shook his head from side to side,

"You're the monster here. Far more of one than that zombie ever was."

He glared at Subaru with emotion showing more plainly in his eyes than ever before.

Subaru knew what that emotion was, the one hiding behind the pitch-black murderous intent, glimpsed now for the first time. He had seen it many times before—in the mirror.

It was fear. Fear of a thing that brought incomprehensible "death."

"What makes it worse is you don't even realize it. You're picking and choosing lives. Deciding freely who to save and who to let die. Anyone who flatters you and shows you their belly, you dote on—anyone who doesn't, you don't give a damn about. Me, I don't hesitate to flatter anyone, to show my belly to anyone, but..."

"..."

"...There's no way I could ever get along with someone who decides other people's life and death on a whim, based on nothing but preference."

That was Todd Fang's ultimatum.

"..."

Having spat that out to the last word, Todd's form began to change, accompanied by the creak of bone.

The wounds visible through his imperial soldier's uniform, its jacket torn in places, closed up, and fur the same color as his hair spread to cover his skin. The nose on his face pushed outward, his mouth split wider as if tearing open, and the white teeth that grew in began sharpening to points, his appearance transforming into that of a ferocious beast.

It was exactly the form declared by the word "Werewolf."

A being that stood on two legs, wielded tools with two arms, and crushed its opponent's life between savage fangs—that was the transformation into such a being, or rather, it was his true form laid bare.

"Die."

Brief, once more setting that will upon words, Todd stepped toward Subaru.

That one step was huge, the speed of his hind legs kicking off the ground exploding outward. His aim was to cut Subaru down—the mindset of an enemy standing against an object of unfathomable fear, resolved not to let live the one who now knew his true identity.

Understanding that, Subaru's own fighting spirit exploded in turn.

"...Invisible Providence."

Toward the murmuring Subaru, Todd, now a Werewolf, raised the axe high and leapt.

Into the axe's path, Subaru wedged a chunk of rubble lifted by the black arm extending from his chest, deflecting it aside before the axe could overtake Todd's own head.

"...!"

But even without the axe, Todd now had another weapon—his fangs.

And having used the Unseen Hand to block the axe, Subaru couldn't rely on his Authority to guard against those fangs.

Instead, Subaru used his own hand to yank the knife from his leg.

"Gh, gugigaaahh!!"

Screaming at pain he could never grow used to, Subaru gripped the knife he'd pulled with both hands and wedged it between the gap of Todd's fangs as they closed in to bite.

The sharp fangs snapping shut were stopped by the knife, and as Subaru was pushed down onto his back again, drool spilled from the Werewolf's mouth as it bit down on the blade, dripping wetly onto his cheeks.

"...Ghh!"
"Aaaaaaaahh!!"

Pressed down further like that, the fangs closed in on Subaru's throat.

Straining desperately to hold them back, Subaru screamed through the pain.

"I... won't kill you...!"

On the very verge of being killed, an instant before the tips of the fangs would gouge into his neck, he screamed it.

The one on the verge of having his life taken, shouting it at the one trying to take it—out of sync, mismatched, all the same.

Even if he were overpowered like this and lost his life with his throat torn out, even if he were sent back to some point in the middle of this battle.

Even if it meant regarding Todd as someone who despaired of the world and resented it for reasons Subaru didn't know—

It was at that very moment.

"...That's far enough!"

That voice arrived like a surprise attack, resonating as though it were clawing at Subaru's very Soul.

Amid the beast's breathing and the intermittent rumbling of the earth, the capital, still consumed by chaos in every corner, was a hell of noise where every sound ran wild as it pleased.

Even in such a world, that voice went unimpeded by anything.

As though Natsuki Subaru's very existence were made to resonate with that voice.

"Guh—"

Immediately after, Todd's body—pressing down onto Subaru's, fangs on the verge of reaching his life—was sent flying by a tremendous impact striking from the side.

"..."

Right before his eyes, the Werewolf-turned Todd's body was knocked away, and reflected in Subaru's now-open field of view were shards of ice scattering and glittering—and the ice hammer that had scattered them.

An ice mallet big enough to need both arms to embrace—Todd had been struck and sent flying by it with all the wielder's strength.

And swinging that ice mallet through with both hands, her long silver hair dancing, was a girl clad in white, with a profile of such staggering beauty—

"...Emilia."

The name that flashed through his mind like lightning was one his mouth traced unconsciously.

The girl turned around. Her blinking violet-indigo eyes met Subaru's black ones. Captivated by the beauty of those eyes, Subaru unconsciously held his breath—

"You there, this way!"

"Eh?"

Whether he'd forgotten to breathe or simply caught his breath, it was forcibly cut short.

White fingers shot out with a quick, forceful motion, pulling and lifting Subaru's body up with a strength impossible to guess from her looks. Without a moment's hesitation, she scooped up the blood- and mud-caked Subaru—the girl, Emilia—and bending her knees with a little "hup,"

"Hyah!"

—with that shout she leapt, kicking off the wall of a nearby building and springing further upward still. Emilia landed atop the building's roof, and cradled in her arms, Subaru blinked in bewilderment, stammering "Eh? Eh? Eh?"—but understanding caught up to him only belatedly.

Below, in the street beneath the building where Emilia, holding Subaru, had landed, he could see a raging torrent surging through with tremendous force, swallowing up everything in its path.

"That was close... A little longer and you'd have been swallowed up by the water."

At the overwhelming sight, Emilia let out a relieved breath, hand to her chest.

Emilia's reaction didn't convey much sense of urgency or tragedy, but the sight of it all—the ruins of the mill blown apart by the dust explosion, the street where Todd had dragged the zombie around while they'd waited for things to be ready—all of it being swallowed up together by the flash flood was a spectacle of overwhelming despair.

Had it been even a little later, just as Emilia said, Subaru too would have been swallowed by the flash flood. And if that had happened, there would have been no way to survive it—

"...! What about him?!"

Todd, who had been sent flying by Emilia's blow an instant before biting Subaru to death.

If he'd failed to escape in time, had he too been swallowed by the flash flood? Caught up in a current with that much force, even Todd surely wouldn't have any way to survive.

Forgetting even the pain in his leg, Subaru swept his gaze around on instinct, searching the streets of the capital being swallowed by the torrent for any sign of Todd. Honestly, he didn't even know the answer to whether he wanted Todd to be found or not.

"Hey, calm down! Who are you looking for... Ah! Now that I look, that's a terrible wound! We need to treat it right away..."

As Subaru strained his eyes desperately, Emilia noticed the wound on his leg. She, who had been about to set him down on the roof, hurriedly sat him down at the edge instead,

"Wait here! I'll get someone who can use healing magic right away..."

"There's no time for that! If we just leave it..."

"You're one to talk—take care of your own leg properly—"

Emilia raised her brows at Subaru, who brushed off her words of caution and tried to stand. But her scolding never made it to the end—it was cut short.

Her large violet-indigo eyes went wide, and the sight reflected in those beautiful eyes could be seen.

Behind Subaru, seated at the edge of the roof, the Werewolf had surfaced amid a splash of water and was opening its jaws wide, about to sink its teeth into the back of Subaru's neck.

"..."

Whether he'd be dragged into the water still bitten, or have his neckbone crushed without mercy—either way, it was an instant even Emilia, right there in front of him, couldn't reach in time.

Todd's tenacity defied even the flash flood itself, straining to tear Subaru's life away between his teeth.

In that instant—

"...That person—"

A hard footstep stomped against the rooftop floor, and the whistle of something cutting through the air rang out.

Neither the utterly exhausted Subaru, nor Emilia, already pulling away, could stop Todd's violent act.

Leaping in instead was a girl, her pale blue eyes blazing with fury.

"Don't you touch him—!!"

Unleashed together with that roar was an axe, swung with every ounce of strength she had.

The very axe Subaru had knocked away when the Werewolf lunged at him. By whatever twist of fate, it had ended up in the girl's hands, and now it returned to its owner, the one who'd tried to sink his teeth into Subaru.

"...Guh"

Right over the wound where the axe had already sunk deep into his left shoulder once, a second strike now drove in. The impact met the Werewolf's momentum head-on, and his massive body was sent flying, slammed into the raging torrent.

With a great splash and a pillar of water thrown up, this time for certain, the Werewolf—Todd Fang—sank beneath the surface.

"Haah, haah, haah..."

Breathing hard, shoulders heaving, the girl who had swung the axe pulled Subaru's head to her chest. Then, letting the axe drop from her trembling hand into the water, she sank to her knees right there.

And so, the girl—Rem's pale blue eyes met Subaru's black ones, straight on.

"You seem to be safe, it seems."

"...I'm not really safe, though."

Subaru answered haltingly to Rem, whose eyes softened with the faintest relief at the corners.

Then he looked behind him, searching for Todd, submerged by Rem's blow. He couldn't find him. This time, surely, there was no way he would be found. And even if he were found, he—

"...!"

A welling up of frustration and bitterness, and Subaru pressed a hand to his own chest.

Not a single thing had Subaru managed to make Todd understand, or been made to understand in turn. Not the things he'd wanted Todd to understand, not the things he'd thought Todd didn't need to understand—nothing at all.

That was maddening. Maddening, unbearably, achingly maddening.

Carrying that helpless frustration with him still—

"Rem! Thank goodness. Heal this child's wounds for me! I need to hurry and find Subaru..."

"Wait, please! This is him! This is the one! This is the child calling himself Natsuki Subaru..."

"Eh!? This child is Subaru!? But, he's cute, isn't he...?"

Right in front of Subaru, whose strength was draining away bit by bit at the edge of the rooftop, the two who had saved him—Emilia and Rem—were raising their voices back and forth about him.

And the thought crossed his mind—just how long had it been since the two of them had exchanged words like this, right in front of him?

"Todd, you huge idiot..."

With that frustration and sense of defeat as his last thought, Subaru's consciousness cut out, abruptly.
