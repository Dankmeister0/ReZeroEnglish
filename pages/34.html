Arc 2 Chapter 10: "The Wicked Negotiation"

Blue and yellow — a pair of eyes each a different color. Meeting them head-on threatened to drown his thoughts in their depths. He held his breath, clenching his teeth so as not to be swallowed by the atmosphere.

He rested his clasped hands on the table, propped his chin atop them, and said,

"You've got quite the eye for your surroundings, don't you. And the way you've followed along so well since a mo-oment ago — frankly, that's not the least bit normal for an ordinary townsman."

"It is truly my honor to receive such kind words, if it please you — so answer my question, you indigo-haired Longhair. Or else..."

"Or else what?"

At Roswaal's provocative counter, Subaru clicked his teeth in frustration.

"I'll take this hand that's petting Emilia-tan and, with a heart torn to shreds... spend it on sexually harassing this girl instead! I'll make her go ahn, ahn and do the do!"

"Eep!"

At words delivered with enough force to nearly bite through his own lip, Emilia flinched with a start, and the shudder stirred the sadist in Subaru's heart. For an instant, a reckless thought threatened to explode — that maybe he wouldn't regret turning that statement into reality — but it was cut short by the sound of a hand clapping.

What pulled Subaru's mind back from the brink of tipping the scales of his heart toward sexual harassment was Roswaal, who'd made that dry, cracking sound.

"Whew, that was close. Saved me."

"Now why-y am I the one being thanked? ...Honestly, you're a strange one. Negotiating by means like that — I've never even heard of such a thing."

"Heh. Your mistake was putting the highest-ranked girl in this room within my reach. Whatever scheme you're plotting, it's useless. Even a single instant, even the briefest fraction of a meeting — the gene that stretches that fraction into eternity through a bit of jury-rigged modification lives on in my people."

"An instant into eternity... the pinnacle of shadow magic...!?"

At the baffling nonsense, Emilia — still having her head stroked — was left speechless.

He couldn't deny he was being treated like some kind of time-manipulating esper, but given the actual circumstances, it wasn't entirely wrong either, so he chose not to explain.

Both the fact that he'd been Returning by Death, and the fact that it was the sheer idiocy of the Japanese national character — capable of jury-rigging a single instant's delusion into astonishing quality — were, after all, no lie.

Setting aside Emilia's flabbergasted reaction, Subaru kept his true thoughts hidden and glared at Roswaal. Never forgetting his fearless grin, he charged headlong into villain territory.

Longhair, for his part, met that villainous face without losing his gentle expression. He kept up a soft, unreadable smile, and for his side too, the dignity of a nobleman was very much in evidence.

A silent exchange of gazes, each sizing up the other, sparks all but flying in the wordless stand-off. It was Subaru who won it, making Roswaal lower his eyes first.

Watching Subaru, who'd begrudged even a single blink and ended up with dry eyes, Roswaal raised both hands slightly and said, "I surrender, I surrender."

"You've go-ot me. I'm impressed by that stare of yours. Well then, it wouldn't be fair to yo-ou if I didn't make my position clear."

"Ow, ow, my eyes hurt. Emilia-tan, rub them for me gently, would you?"

"Is that really what you want as your reward?"

"I'll do it myself! That was way too close! This girl's surprisingly sharp!"

Having nearly let go of his negotiating advantage, Subaru leapt back and rubbed both eyes. He blinked to confirm the sting had faded, then squared up to face Roswaal once more.

"Though, it's not really anything I need to hi-ide, either. My tongue slipped a bit, but I stand in the position of supporting Emilia-sama as a candidate for queen. A backer... well, a patron, to put it nicely."

"A patron, huh."

So the representative of her backers was this Roswaal standing before him, then.

Subaru studied the man's tall, unreadable figure once more, then turned to Emilia hesitantly.

"This is hard to say, but... Emilia-tan, don't you think you should be pickier about who you associate with?"

"I didn't have a choice. There's no one I can rely on in the royal capital, and to begin with, Roswaal is about the only eccentric willing to help someone like me..."

"I see, so it's process of elimination."

"Both of you, saying such things right in front of your patron — you really don't know fear, do you-u."

Even spoken ill of this much, Roswaal didn't so much as act displeased — he didn't even let his gentle expression change. Whether it was because his Vessel was immeasurably large, or—

"—or he's a textbook masochist."

"M?"

Subaru curved both arms overhead, joining his hands above his crown to form the shape of an "M."

Showing off a human-body letter that no one in this world could possibly understand, Subaru tried to explain it to Emilia, who tilted her head—

"—No, wait, now that I think about it calmly, this is a repeat joke. It loses its freshness, so doing the same bit on the same person a second time is kind of..."

"Don't just convince yourself and move on — what does that even mean?"

"The you from yesterday knew. That's all. Live strong."

Patting her head lightly, as if to console her, Subaru gave a nihilistic laugh.

Leaving Emilia gaping, utterly lost as to his meaning, Subaru's heart felt clear — he hadn't told a lie, after all.

Though, granted, the Emilia from the world he remembered existed nowhere now. She slept quietly within Subaru's heart, together with a debt of gratitude toward her he could never fully repay.

He gave a thumbs-up to Emilia, who pouted with dissatisfaction, then turned his hand — still in that same shape — toward Roswaal. And then flipped it over.

"Now why-y does flipping it over alone make that gesture feel so humiliating?"

"You think so? Where I'm from, it's a sign of affection. It means something like, 'I love you so much I want to let you lick my shoes.'"

"In Subaru's homeland, licking shoes is an expression of affection...?"

"It's the highest expression of love — that everything that makes up you is dear to me. If I felt like it, I could eat Emilia-tan's hair without hesitation, too."

"Please, I'm begging you, never feel like it."

Subaru shrugged at being shot down. Roswaal, unable to hold back, burst out laughing at their exchange, while Emilia looked put out by their reactions.

Each of the three reacting in their own way — but the conversation hadn't progressed one bit since earlier.

"Look, this is a serious conversation, so could you stop derailing it?"

"That's the last thing I ever wanted to hear from you, Subaru, of all people!"

Brushing off Emilia's lament with an airy "yeah, yeah," Subaru turned his gaze — reset for what felt like the umpteenth time — back to Roswaal and got down to business.

"So, the main topic. I get that Rozzy here is Emilia-tan's patron. Well, given how a bit of country-bumpkin charm peeks through the edges of her behavior — which is cute, by the way — acting alone in the capital must've been pretty unusual for her, huh?"

"...Practically her first time doing so, I'd think. Ram was suppo-osed to be with her, though."

Roswaal gave a wry smile, fiddling with his collar as he turned the topic toward the pink-haired maid. When Subaru looked back at the maid behind him with suspicious eyes, the pink-haired one had flipped her hair part over, calmly pretending to be the blue-haired one. Though with her hair color being different, it was obvious at a glance.

"That smug 'heh, I fooled him' look on your face really ticks me off."

"Sister, Sister, the guest is saying such awful things." "Rem, Rem, the guest is saying something empty-headed."

"You're both foul-mouthed enough that I can tell you apart anyway!"

After an exchange that showed zero intent to actually fool him, Subaru shouted roughly, then slumped his shoulders. But it was Emilia who responded hesitantly to his raised voice.

With an awkward expression, she raised her hand a little.

"It's not Ram's fault. It's, um... I let my curiosity get the better of me, I guess. I wandered off and got separated from Ram."

"What kind of moe-character excuse is that, some kind of ironclad defense? Setting that aside, the fact remains you couldn't keep your master's orders, right? So, what do yo-ou think about that?"

He asked the question, jutting out his lips for a proper pronunciation, and Roswaal responded by tilting his head. He prefaced his answer with "You have a point there," and continued,

"Certainly, Ram's lapse in oversight might be my responsibility as well. Bu-ut, setting that aside, what exactly are you trying to say?"

"It's simple. Emilia-tan forgot her homing instinct and wandered off, and her attendant failed to track her down — that's a bitter failure indeed! And so, in the end, I'm the villain character who took advantage of it. Which means squeezing every last drop out of whoever I can squeeze is the proper form of vice, isn't it?"

As if something had clicked into place, everyone's expression in the room changed.

Emilia's face stiffened, the twins glared at Subaru with eyes where apology and hostility coexisted, Beatrice tipped her glass with a face of complete indifference, and Puck — apparently having slipped in front of his egg dish — face-planted headfirst into the yolk in a total disaster.

And Roswaal, with a smile that seemed to say he understood completely, nodded again and again.

"I see-e. Certainly, compared to Emilia-sama, whose personal fortune amounts to next to nothing, I — her patron — would be the more suitable party to demand a reward from, wouldn't I."

"Right? And you shouldn't be able to refuse. Be-cause! I'm the man who saved Emilia-tan's life, and the hero who kept her from dropping out of the Royal Selection! Which basically makes me some kind of savior to Team Emilia in the whole Royal Selection thing!"

"I'll grant you that, since it's true. And so, on that basis, let me ask you something."

Roswaal rose from his seat, looking down at Subaru with his tall frame.

Not to be outdone, Subaru glared back up at him. The composition was similar to their first meeting's surprise forehead-kiss, but the weight of the atmosphere the two of them carried now was on an entirely different level.

Anxiously, Emilia clasped her hands together as if fearing where the negotiation would lead. Feeling her pleading presence at his back, Subaru nodded.

"I'm listening. Dig out your ears and pay attention."

"What is it you want from me, I wonder? As things stand, I can't refuse you. Whatever gold or treasure you might desire. Or even something else entirely — some decadent, hedonistic turn of events. To conceal the fact that the emblem was lost, I'll do anything."

"Heh heh heh, as expected of a noble. You catch on quick, don't you."

Poke, poke — Subaru jabbed an elbow into that thin chest, putting on a lecherous grin.

It pained him to hear the sound of his likability plummeting behind him, but all of that was foreshadowing for this very moment. Keeping up his villainous act, he spread both arms wide.

"The reward is mine to name! And you can't refuse it! That's a promise between me and Rozzy. Break it, and you'll swallow a thousand needles."

"Sounds like a story where I'd die around the hundredth needle. Well, yes, I promise."

"A man doesn't go back on his word, right!?"

"What a remarkable phrase. I see. A man shouldn't make excuses. No second word."

A negotiation with each of their manhood on the line.

A promise made on those terms was worth trusting, Subaru decided, crossing his arms self-importantly.

"Then, hire me at the mansion."

Contrary to the long, long build-up, Subaru declared it cleanly and simply.

At his proposal, it was the women behind him who wore looks of blank astonishment. The twins, on their otherwise nearly unchanging faces, showed traces of bewilderment, while Beatrice scowled with what looked like genuine distaste. Emilia, most of all—

"I-I know it's not my place to say, but that's a little..."

Her beautiful face was busy opening and closing her mouth like a goldfish, eyes rolling in confusion. With that much agitation, her air of mystery had thoroughly faded and peeled away.

Turning back to her, Subaru shrugged, looking regretful.

"No, honestly, I knew that for Emilia-tan this would be a repeat joke and wouldn't be funny. But going by my experience points, sticking with a method that worked once more was the only strategy I had left..."

"That's not what I'm talking about...! No — you're just far too unselfish!"

Emilia grew angry as though it were her own affair, slamming the table and closing in on Subaru. Her outstretched finger poked his chest again and again.

"Listen. It was the same with Puck, and it's the same now... this is wrong. And it was the same, to begin with, when you asked for my name back in the capital."

She listed off, as far as she knew, every occasion where Subaru could have claimed a reward. Knowing the full weight of all he'd accomplished, Emilia shook her head, truly unable to understand.

"You don't understand how grateful I am. With something like that... that's nowhere near enough to repay the debt of having my life saved..."

Her words trailed off weakly, the force of the finger poking his chest fading, until she simply left her palm resting against his chest and lowered her face.

Hearing her quiet, grieving voice, Subaru cursed his own thoughtlessness.

She must have been carrying this sense of debt the whole time — how poorly matched the price demanded was against what she owed.

But the same was true for Subaru as well.

Subaru, too, had been carrying a debt toward her all along. And it was the kind of debt he could never ask her to repay again. The same went for Puck.

Beside the two of them, silently clashing wills, Roswaal crept stealthily over toward Beatrice — or rather, toward Puck, who sat beside her, and spoke to him.

"What's this? Has the Great Spirit-sama given that boy a reward too?"

"Yep. Apparently he wants free rein to pet my body. Says he's a first-class fluff enthusiast."

"On Nii-cha's fur!? What a detestable man... he ought to be thrown out, I suppose."

An unnecessary plot to banish Subaru was progressing off to the side, but he consciously ignored it and placed a hand on the slender shoulder in front of him.

Violet-blue eyes, glistening with moisture, looked up at Subaru, her lips trembling as if wanting to ask something.

—Damn, she's too cute.

He beat down, with everything he had, the impulse to steal her lips on sheer momentum, and miraculously restrained himself. Then, with as sincere a manner as he could muster, Subaru told her his true feelings.

"You don't get it, Emilia-tan. I mean it, truly, from the bottom of my heart — at each and every moment, I ask for the thing I genuinely want most right then."

"—Eh?"

"Back then, I wanted to know your name. So badly you could write it 'serious' and read it 'for real.' I was starving like crazy, and shaking in my boots with anxiety in a whole new world, and I probably should've asked for all sorts of other things — if I'd stopped to think it through calmly. —But I'm a man who doesn't lie to himself."

He'd died three times over, for the sake of that.

He'd spent it all just to learn the smile and the name of the silver-haired girl before him.

In that moment, there could be no reward he wanted more.

"It's the same with my request to Roswaal. Honestly, right now I'm broke through and through, top to bottom! To trade that away for a moment's pleasure — my wallet and my head are both in a bit too rough a shape for that. This is the best option for me, don't you think?"

"...You don't have to be hired, though — being treated as a boarder would be fine too, wouldn't it?"

"—There was that option!? Roswaal!?"

At the tearful counter-argument he spun around as if startled, looking to Roswaal with a thread of hope. But Roswaal crossed both hands in a large X in front of his face.

"The first request stands. A man doesn't go back on his word, after all."

"Aaargh! Right, of course! A man doesn't say things twice, does he!?"

Someone's earlier statement had come back to bite him, and, in tears, it was rejected.

Retreating dejectedly, Subaru mused on his coming life of employment.

"I want to rewind to a second ago and say 'support me' instead..."

"Just now, for a moment, you looked so-o serious... guess it was just my imagination."

"And on top of that, this is Emilia-tan's assessment of me! Talk about adding insult to injury!"

Subaru had lost the establishment of an ideal isekai shut-in environment, all through his own slip of the tongue. And if he'd lost favorability points with a beautiful girl on top of that, there was nothing left to salvage.

In an agony that felt, emotionally, like weeping tears of blood, Subaru bit his lip and glared pitifully at Roswaal.

"Anyway... that's how it is. It must be tough keeping the mansion running with just Ram-chi and Rem-rin, so I'm counting on you for a manservant-type position."

"It's true enough that it's a pressing problem. ...Still, just as Emilia-sama says, I too think it's really an awfully unselfish request, you know."

Slipping a hand into the sleeve of his jester's costume, Roswaal, for the first time, let a deep shade of wry amusement color his otherwise gentle expression.

Subaru wagged a raised finger at him and answered with a "tsk, tsk, tsk."

"I'm an incredibly greedy man. —I mean, think about it. I've rationally secured a spot under the same roof as a super cute, super my-type, super gorgeous girl. And having landed a position as a servant, I might even get blessed with a lucky perverted chance or two. The possibilities are endless, and for once my heart's watching tomorrow with real excitement!"

"...I see. That's certainly true. A workplace where you get to be near a woman who's your type — that's a rare thing indeed. What a sweet deal, truly."

"Well, and besides that—"

He stopped waving his finger and brought it up to his head instead, scratching carelessly at his black hair. Averting his gaze from everyone, he looked up at the ceiling.

"A guy like me, who doesn't make any sense — better to keep him close than leave him running around not making sense somewhere else. And on top of that, figure out for yourself whether I'm useful or harmful to Emilia-tan."

It was something he said thinking that if things had come to him leaving the mansion without laying down any safeguards at all, it surely wouldn't have ended well.

Honestly, this didn't go beyond what he'd expected, and it felt a bit like he was testing Roswaal's character too, which made him a little uneasy.

If Roswaal had had nothing to hide, it must have sounded like a baseless accusation, the kind that would leave a man speechless. But contrary to Subaru's uneasy sentimentality—

"I'll do just that. —If I may hope for it, I'd like for us to get along we-ell, wouldn't you say?"

Roswaal shot that back instantly, and behind those two eyes of differing color, not a trace of emotion could be read.

As an aside, Subaru — who'd inwardly been blushing over having, in the heat of the moment, blurted out something that sounded almost like a confession — turned back and peeked hesitantly at Emilia's expression.

"You really are hopeless, Subaru. ...What's wrong?"

Being answered with such a composed attitude left him somewhat bewildered.

Being this beautiful, he supposed, meant she was thoroughly used to being flirted with — flippant remarks, smooth talk, and all — and her battle-hardened composure left him shaking in his boots.

"Can I really keep hitting on Emilia-tan on pure momentum after this...?"

Subaru couldn't hold back a sigh at the rocky road ahead.

While Subaru worried, from a rather skewed angle, about this uncertain life in another world stretching ahead with no end in sight, Emilia murmured quietly to herself, almost too soft to hear.

"Wanting a workplace with girls in it — that's not very pure of you. ...I wonder which of Ram and Rem is more Subaru's type."

With a finger to her lips, Emilia let her imagination run wild on an idea that was completely off the mark.
