Arc 7 Chapter 23: "The Musician Natsumi Schwarz"

"――――"

In the crude mirror, gazing at what was reflected there, the being who had once called himself Natsuki Subaru narrowed his eyes and checked, carefully, again and again.

Dark eyeshadow, and lashes painstakingly curled. Using white powder to refine the texture of the skin — the point where the difference between male and female showed most starkly — and adding a vermillion tint to make the lips look dewy and fresh. The costume had been quite the challenge, but by wrapping the body in generous folds of fluttering cloth, the build could be disguised, while also laboring to match the style to the climate of the south. After all, this time it wasn't the role of a noble young lady, but a far more loosely-footed, gauzier position — yes, he had to act the part of a traveling performer. Which meant that, whatever the mannerisms, the appearance had to push "woman" harder still, or it wouldn't match the concept.

"And then, padding while gathering the flesh of the body to its very limit toward the chest……"

Marshaling every technique available, he pictured in his mind all the encounters up to now. Starting with Emilia, then Felt and that hateful Elsa, on to meeting Ram and Rem, plus Beatrice and Petra — Meili would be set aside for the moment. After that, Priscilla and Crusch, Anastasia, and here he pressed his palms together toward Ferris, whom he ought to revere as his spiritual master, and further, Frederica, and the Witches whose appearances alone were infuriatingly well put-together — from all the beautiful girls and women, and beautiful-girl-like beings he'd met in this other world thus far, he gathered up the imagination of "beauty."

What I picture is always the most beautiful version of myself.

There was no need to think of anything superfluous. Beyond that lay the answer he was picturing. And so, playing the best hand available from his current cards, he had arrived at the place he needed to reach. And so——

"――This is me."

Stepping away from the mirror, he drew several slow, deep breaths, then turned around. Having ended the time spent fighting alone, in solitude, he finally pushed the door open. Beyond it waited his comrades, holding their breath, awaiting the pass-or-fail verdict that would decide this operation's fate. They turned their gazes toward the figure who had opened the door and stepped out.

"――――"

Silence was born, and the air visibly grew taut. What emotion lay behind that silence — sudden anxiety refused to let him understand. True, there were several cards missing from the hand he needed to win this fight. If only he had that, if only he had this — the regrets never ceased.

But so what if they did? Hadn't he resolved to brazen through anyway? One could never go into battle in perfect condition every time. Wasn't life, in any circumstance, nothing but playing the hand you'd been dealt? Just as he was stringing together excuses within himself, as though he'd already lost—

"――Magnificent."

A faint bursting sound in the air, and at that he lifted his face on reflex. Looking, the source of that bursting sound was nothing mysterious at all. It was the sound made when hand met hand — the sound of applause. A sound mingling blessing, admiration, and respect.

"M……"

Just before he could call out to the one applauding, he stopped himself. Still imperfect. He lightly brought a hand to his throat, and let a small breath bounce there. And once the gears meshed within himself, he smiled.

"Mizelda-san."

"――! Surely not, even your voice? Just, just how far…!"

"Once one has decided to do a thing, giving it one's all is one's duty. There are lives protected by my not holding back. If that is so, there is only one thing for me to do."

"Oh――!"

Erasing the smile, he raised a single finger toward the heavens. The sun was blocked from view by the thickly growing trees, but what needed to be shown off was not the presence above, but the many comrades gathered here.

Behold — from head to toe. From the front, from behind, from the right, from the left — from anywhere at all, it made no difference.

What I picture is always the most beautiful version of myself.

And if he had succeeded in tracing that image, if he had managed to reproduce the ideal, then there was nothing left to fear. Here, by this authority——

"――Natsumi Schwarz, reborn."

Thus declared Natsumi Schwarz — not valiantly, but demurely. Before her, Mizelda, who had stopped her clapping hands, bowed her head deeply. Then she stepped up beside Natsumi, supporting her back with a hand, and said,

"My world was narrow, it seems. You were right, Subaru……no, Natsumi."

"So you understand now, Mizelda-san."

"Ah, it galls me, but I understand. ――Beauty can be made."

Her eyes softening, Mizelda smiled gently, and Natsumi smiled back. Witnessing that beautiful scene, the Shudrak gathered there exchanged glances with one another. Then, as a belated blessing, applause rained down. The falling applause showed no sign of stopping, celebrating Natsumi's manifestation. And then——

"What is this." "Whaaat."

Those who couldn't quite ride the wave watched the scene unfold, their cheeks twitching.

"And so, thus was born Natsumi Schwarz. The makeup tools were quite different from what I'm used to, and it threw me off, but I managed at least this much."

"Are you kidding me?"

"Eh!?"

Having returned to the meeting hall and set up a proper council once more, Natsumi — no, Subaru's words were met by Rem with absolute-zero eyes and tone. Even having shown off the results, Rem's attitude did not budge, and at that Subaru pointed at her own face.

"After all, false eyelashes are simply necessary, aren't they……The wig, I somehow arranged by borrowing the black hair of the Shudrak ladies……"

"That isn't what I'm talking about. The makeup and wig, I think they're very well done. But there's no need to keep talking like a woman forever."

"That's true, but……feelings matter, don't they? If I don't stay mindful of it at all times, who knows where the cracks will show. Besides……"

"Besides?"

"If I get complacent about being a man, the goddess of beauty will turn her back on me……ow ow ow ow!"

Even though he'd been speaking so earnestly, Rem gave his ear a rather forceful tug. Tears sprang to his eyes despite himself, and he beat a hasty retreat from the spot. Then Subaru sidled up to Mizelda, who had been watching the proceedings unfold.

"Mizelda-neesama, Rem-san is scary……"

"Mm, don't cry, Natsumi. ……Strangely, even knowing it's Subaru, you look cute. Taritta, take note."

"Elder Sister!?"

While stroking Subaru's head, Taritta's eyes went wide at Mizelda's heartless demand. And yet, the expression on Taritta's face as she responded was, compared to usual, considerably softer. And no wonder — the fierce war-paint-like makeup that the People of Shudrak normally applied had been arranged by Subaru, adjusted to look cute.

As Shudrak, they showed off beauty through valor and toughness, but what was called for this time was a bewitching allure that combined glamour and danger in equal measure — the kind that drove men mad. Once the direction of beauty was set that way, doing Taritta and Kuna's makeup had been fun.

"Taritta-neesama, you have a much younger face than I expected. The makeup took wonderfully too. Playing up cuteness once in a while is lovely too."

"D-don't tease me. In the first place, I could never be as cute as you……"

"Well, even your lack of confidence is adorable. Isn't it, Mizelda-neesama?"

"Indeed it is, my dear little thing."

Snuggling close and speaking softly, Subaru made Mizelda's cheeks loosen. At the sight of the two of them, Taritta, flustered, could only look down while her cheeks faintly reddened. Her brown complexion made a blush hard to notice, but the makeup on her eyes and lips accentuated the faintly-reddened cheeks, making the charming change plain to see――

"How long are you going to keep fooling around!"

And with a single sharp cry, Rem shattered that garden-like scene. As though hit by a sudden gust, Subaru and Taritta shrank down, and Rem glared at the two of them with her pale blue eyes before turning to look toward Kuna, at the edge of the room. Kuna, whose makeup and hair had likewise been arranged like Taritta's, shifted uncomfortably.

"W-what, I'm telling you, I didn't do nothin……"

"Kuna-san, you're the one I'm relying on. As you can see, these two are like this, so you're the only sane one……are you seriously going through with this operation?"

"I get that I'm the only sane one, but……"

Kuna, her hair done up in twin tails, looked at Subaru and Taritta with an unmotivated gaze. It was a rather unwelcome assessment, but Subaru had enough composure to watch how things unfolded. Unlike Taritta, who was trembling with shock at being called insane. At their reaction, Kuna let out a small sigh.

"The reason the plan couldn't work is gone now, right, Rem gets that much? Natsumi……Subaru, he's fine. That one's more of a woman than the chief."

"I think that's incredibly rude to Mizelda-san, but……"

"Anyway, I don't think it's a problem. Now, about the results from over there――"

Just as Kuna was about to wrap up her exchange with Rem——

"――It seems everyone is assembled."

"Oh, speak of the devil……"

A haughty voice, making no attempt at concealment, wrapped around the meeting hall, and a new figure appeared. Kuna's gaze turned that way, and Rem, with no other choice, turned the same direction. Naturally, the gazes of Subaru, Mizelda, and everyone else gathered there too——

"――――"

In that instant, time stopped for everyone. ――No, to be precise, for everyone except the figure who had appeared, and Subaru, who had applied that figure's makeup.

"I left the fine adjustments to you, but……you're skilled to an almost spiteful degree."

"Is it pleasant to be praised for the deftness of one's decoration? Still, I was struck by your skill. Even the finished product transforms remarkably. It is more presentable than I had imagined."

"Ngh, the composure of the victor……!"

Even as she spoke, Subaru quietly bit her own pinky in frustration. Of course, there was no doubting the conviction that beauty could be made. She was proud that she herself had proven it with her own body, using what few cards she had. And yet, even so, there was still a difference in raw material that simply existed. And that was——

"A-Abel-san, is it?"

"Who else would it be. Do not pose such foolish questions. Though, if the change is so great as to provoke such surprise from you, then perhaps your astonishment serves as appropriate reference."

Answering Rem's trembling words thus, clenching five slender, white fingers, was Abel — no, the being born of makeup, wig, and a change of costume: Bianca.

Her raven-black hair was long and lustrous, framing beautiful, narrow eyes. Standing there was a beautiful dancer who had reproduced the utmost pinnacle of beauty — the finest joker card indispensable to this plan.

"――――"

At the sheer completeness of Bianca, even Mizelda and the others lost their voices. Unlike Subaru's transformation into Natsumi, where the shock came from the sheer magnitude of the change, what Abel-turned-Bianca carried was the pure shock brought by the sheer perfection of beauty itself.

Even the dancer's costume and the various Shudrak ornaments decorating the long hair remained merely accessories, serving only to set off a genuine beauty. Subaru recalled the smile Mizelda had shown when lending them out, calling them her "special reserve." Recalling that smile, for some reason Subaru's chest gave a twinge of pain. But Subaru had no option of holding back out of consideration for Mizelda and the others.

How could she ruin this perfection with her own hands? Such a thing, Subaru's ego as Natsumi Schwarz would not allow.

"That you had no resistance to the makeup was also unexpected, from my perspective……"

"What, did you think I'd feel it a disgrace to disguise myself as a woman? I'll have you know, this is not my first time at such a thing. Indeed, I experienced it many times over in my childhood."

"As a child?"

"Yes. Given my position, one ought to keep broad means of self-protection at hand."

Arms crossed with none of the expected charm, this Bianca-who-was-Abel made Subaru twist her lips at the attitude. Likely, the experiences he'd had in the past were tales of the shadow-struggles from before he'd taken the throne, back when he was still only a candidate for emperor. It was easy to imagine that in Vollachia, where imperial succession apparently proceeded far more violently than in Lugunica, an assassination contest for the next emperor's seat had likely been waged.

To survive such harsh days, he had disguised his own appearance, at times even his gender, in order to act. Abel's willingness to sacrifice anything, including himself, seemed to have been shaped by an accumulation stretching back to his childhood days, long before he won the throne. And now, having come full circle, that showed here, in this cross-dressed Abel-turned-Bianca.

"Even so, somehow it irritates me that you're self-aware of being beautiful……!"

"That is a foolish thing to say. In a position where one must survey one's own nation broadly, how could one who cannot even view himself objectively serve as its head? You seem to have overturned that objective assessment through technique, but I require no such petty trickery."

"Ngh……!"

"Do you understand why the tiger is strong? The tiger is strong because it is the tiger."

It was a theory Subaru had heard once before, from Garfiel, and it struck her down all the same. The theory that the tiger is strong because it's the tiger — flip it around, and it means that Abel is beautiful because he's Abel, and that explanation settles everything. It was nothing short of logical violence. It was the same as saying Reinhard is strong because he's Reinhard. In other words, it was an opinion with no room left for argument.

"Fine, fine……Flora probably thinks the same thing!"

"Why does the fire spread to me, danna-kun!?"

Behind Abel, Flora — that is, Flop, who had come along with them — was startled. He too, disguised as her, was the type whose finished beauty simply came from making the most of his natural material without much fuss. In fact, unlike Subaru and Abel, he'd barely changed the styling of his originally-long hair, so if anyone's natural flavor came through the strongest, it was his. In truth, she was confident that, standing side by side, she wouldn't look terribly outclassed. She was confident, but――

"The effort, the sheer effort put in is simply different……divine favoritism……!"

"I'm not quite sure what you're lamenting so grandly about, but still, danna-kun, you're amazing! I hardly recognized you! You're no longer danna-kun, but danna-san!"

"……Such a compliment isn't nearly enough to satisfy me anymore."

"Was that just now a compliment? I couldn't tell," Kuna said.

Flop's bluntness hadn't changed even as Flora. Still, in his case, that was fine as it was. Though some acting guidance in line with the plan's direction would likely be needed. In this whole matter, the one whose role mattered most was Abel.

"Flora and I can handle instruments. So your role is……"

"The dance, obviously. Even unsaid, the plan is in my head. As is the importance of my part in it. And besides."

"Besides?"

"Not so much as my late sister, but I too am skilled at dance."

Even his cheeks twisting into a triumphant, haughty smile was beautiful. Abel's self-assured bearing was reassuring, and at the same time it seared Subaru's chest with anticipation. In truth, Abel had displayed his bewitching charm without reservation, completing Bianca. If that self-assessment was correct, then hope could be placed in the dance too.

"――. Very well. Since you speak so boldly, show me that skill of yours. At the very least, do not fail to swallow the spit you've spat."

"The spit I've spat……hmph, meaning spit that falls on the clouds, is it. A roundabout turn of phrase, but very well. I shall teach you."

"――――"

"That is the least reward I can give you, who devised this scheme."

Even cross-dressed, Abel's arrogance showed no sign of waning, doubting himself not at all. Feeling both reassured and threatened by that, Subaru quietly glanced at Rem. Regrettably, she could not bring Rem along on this plan. But even so——

"Please pray for my safety. For your sake, I shall do my utmost."

"――. ――――. ――――――――. ――――――――――. Yes."

"That took quite a while to pray, didn't it!?"

At the belated reply from Rem, whose face had gone truly, solemnly serious, Subaru raised her voice like that. Hearing it, Louis, at Rem's side, quickly pulled her head back. It seemed even she hadn't been able to recognize Natsumi as Subaru. For now, it was best to take that reaction as reassurance, a good omen for the operation.

――"Operation Kumasotakeru."

That was the name of the operation targeting the "woman-lover," Imperial General Zikr Osman. The operation's name, modeled on the Kojiki, had been Subaru's proposal, and fortunately, no one had objected. Whether that was out of respect for Subaru as the planner, or because no one cared about the operation's name to begin with, was unclear, but avoiding a squabble over it was a good sign.

After all, there were points in the plan where an element of luck simply could not be avoided. Of course, no effort would be spared in raising the odds of success, but stockpiling good fortune was difficult. So it wasn't a bad thing to feel a tailwind, even in small matters.

――Leaving the Buddheim Jungle, the group set their course for Guaral.

The active unit was confirmed as five: Subaru-as-Natsumi, Abel-as-Bianca, Flop-as-Flora, Taritta, and Kuna, while the remainder, led by Mizelda, made up the "People of Shudrak," formed a separate detachment. Rem, Louis, and Medium would be included on that side. Those lying low in a location apart from the city would, if it came down to it, need to take on the job of attacking the fortress city's walls and drawing attention as a diversion. However, that was strictly a fallback. An insurance policy, in case the plan failed, that couldn't be expected to accomplish much. If the plan did fail, by that point Subaru and the others would likely already be in a fairly critical situation. In any case——

"Personally, I think the first checkpoint will be the biggest hurdle."

Subaru murmured, looking up at the great front gate that stood boldly facing forward, looking imperiously down from on high. They were once again challenging the front gate they had forcibly broken through once before, even if some days had passed since then. Naturally, given what had happened before, the checkpoint's vigilance level had risen compared to last time. In preparation for the re-infiltration of troublemakers, particular scrutiny was being given to dragon carriages, ox carts, and any vehicles capable of concealing people among their cargo. Cargo was being overturned, straw was being carefully prodded with swords, and the screams of merchants and travelers whose wares were being handled roughly could be heard.

Honestly, Subaru felt considerable pity for them. Still, thinking of the chaos about to break out in the city ahead, she thought that turning back here might actually be the wiser choice for them. Of course, there was no way to give them such advice——

"――Next, you lot?"

As Subaru was lost in thought, a gruff man's rough voice struck her eardrums. Looking up, she saw the one waving them forward was a burly, bald-headed guard. Called forward by him, the group stepped up, and at the front, Subaru bowed her head respectfully.

"We are a troupe of traveling performers. We have drifted down from the north and come to this city."

"Ho, traveling performers……"

Raising an eyebrow with curiosity, the guard also turned his gaze to the group behind Subaru. Flora, carrying an instrument on her back, waved; Taritta, carrying luggage, bowed; Kuna held a parasol over the person beside her, whose face was veiled, betraying no expression at all. The guard seemed to be impressed at what a striking-looking troupe they made.

"――――"

Meanwhile, while maintaining her ingratiating smile, Subaru's heart within was strung taut with tension. This guard — she had seen him before. He was one of the ones who had been in charge of the front gate the first time, back when Subaru had first met Flop and the others and entered Guaral. In other words, he could not have been a more fitting opponent for this first checkpoint.

"Still, coming from the north must've been rough. Word is that over there, the 'Scorching Duke's' territory has gotten lively. Flying Dragons flying every which way, they say."

"Yes, yes, that's right. So we couldn't stay too long……in that respect, this is a fine city protected by sturdy walls, surely it's safe here?"

"Ah, well, that's……"

Striking a coquettish pose, smiling, Subaru watched the guard's face turn awkward. The reason for that reaction was surely that they too were aware of imperial soldiers, their camp burned, fleeing into the city. On top of that, given the disturbance Subaru and the others had caused the other day, the imperial soldiers within the city were likely on heightened alert, the atmosphere tense. That, without doubt, was the reason the guard's words had trailed off. If so, there was no reason not to make use of it.

"……Could it be that there's some anxiety here in this city too?"

"……No, well, hiding it wouldn't do much good anyway. Actually, right now there's an imperial army garrisoned in this town, apparently having some trouble with an eastern tribe. So……"

"Oh my! Then the people of the town must be feeling quite suffocated."

"That's exactly it."

It seemed pent-up frustration had built up in him, and at Subaru's words the guard leaned forward. Without any point of contact, one couldn't tell the difference, but fundamentally, guards and soldiers occupied different positions. A city's guards belonged, ultimately, to that city. Soldiers, on the other hand, belonged to the nation — in this case, the Empire. Their chains of command differed from the ground up.

The guard before her, too, held a post protecting the town as one of its own people. And yet, in the current state of affairs, with the imperial army occupying the city hall and issuing this and that order, it couldn't be pleasant. There was no reason he should have to be ordered around by imperial soldiers.

"On top of it all, those guys go around calling it "night patrol" and just occupy the town's taverns for themselves. Bad tippers too, so the shops can't do business. I can't stand it."

"Oh my, oh my, how terrible! My heart goes out to you. Just between us, we too have had quite the ordeal with the high-handedness of imperial soldiers."

"Huh, is that so. Even someone as lovely as you……"

At the deep nods and raised voice of agreement, the guard's attitude softened all at once. In fact, feeling considerable warmth toward this man, Subaru said "Yes" while dropping her tone slightly, averting her gaze, and looking down. At that meaningful gesture, the guard, flustered, said "M-miss?"

"Are you all right? If you don't want to talk about it, you don't have to force yourself……"

"No, I'm all right. It's an old, old story. It's only that, before, we were forcibly seized by imperial soldiers, put in cages, treated like slaves……"

"That's……"

"Tripped on purpose, made to fall, and at the worst of it, threatened with blades……"

"T-that's unforgivable……!"

At Subaru's tearful recounting of the past, the guard clenched his fists, driven by righteous indignation. Seeing the man's ears reddened with anger, Subaru murmured softly, "How kind you are."

"N-no, it's not kindness. Anyone would feel the same way you do."

"Then, say, Sir Guard. If you would take pity on me, please, let us in. If you do that, then……"

"Then?"

"This atmosphere of bloodshed the imperial soldiers have brought — I shall renew it entirely."

Her voice was small, but the fervor packed into it was hot. Hearing that, the guard's stern face colored with surprise. His gaze turned to the troupe of traveling performers lined up behind Subaru.

"……Miss, just what kind of performance can you show us?"

"Of course, we have traveled to many lands, acquiring various arts along the way. I myself dabble in song and music, some small sleight of hand. But……"

"……gulp."

"The greatest sight of all is, without question, the dance of the troupe's flower, our dancing girl."

So saying, Subaru gestured toward the figure standing at the center of the group, shielded by the parasol. Her face hidden behind a veil, she revealed a serene bearing — the troupe's true star attraction. Bathed in the gaze from beyond that veil, the guard's eyes went wide and he froze stiff. Though nothing had been revealed at all, his throat had frozen solid.

"――――"

Small wonder he was captivated, unable to move. It was a masterpiece to make even Subaru feel a pang of frustration — she had boasted that "beauty can be made," but true beauty, after all, was a creation of something divine. In the end, all Subaru had added was, at most, a single extra touch. But even that single touch had been enough to further hone that bewitching charm.

"In addition to the dancing girl, we too shall sing and play for you. Please, do grant us permission to stay in Guaral."

"――――"

"Sir Guard?"

"……Ah, ah, right. Especially since there don't seem to be any suspicious belongings……"

Poked lightly on the shoulder, the guard, coming back to himself, murmured that. Then he placed a hand on his own chest, breathing as if to confirm the beating of his own heart, and surveyed the troupe. And, making every effort to avert his eyes so as not to be captivated by that bewitching beauty, he said,

"Go on in. If you're going to perform somewhere, better to stay away from the front gate. I'd recommend the main street in the middle of town. Steer clear of the city hall — too many soldiers there."

"My, how kind. ……By the way, when does Sir Guard get some free time?"

"Wh-me? I get relieved at dusk and go eat……"

"Then do tell me which establishment. I'm sure your colleagues would come along too, and we could properly repay your kindness to us."

Leaning in gently, Subaru whispered into the guard's ear. The guard, startled, blinked wide-eyed, and Subaru smiled at him and stepped back. Then she turned to the troupe, waiting eagerly through the checkpoint,

"Now then, let's go, everyone. Let's clear away this town's troubled air for them."

So saying, she led the group, who had been standing still, past the guard's side. Covering his ear, the guard, dumbstruck, watched their backs recede, and Subaru and the others, without any trouble at all, passed grandly through the front gate and into the city. Watching them go, the guard let out a quiet breath and murmured,

"……Today, let's finish up work quickly."

"Well now, that was a magnificent thing, danna-san! I might even think you have more of a talent for merchanting than I do!"

Some time after passing the front gate, in the midst of the group, each carrying only minimal luggage, Flora — that is, Flop — eyes shining, praised Subaru's performance at the checkpoint. There was no falsehood in his frank words, and Subaru, in good spirits, nodded, "Yes."

"I'm honored by your praise. But me, a merchant? Not at all. I have things I must do."

"Things you must do? Such as?"

"Of course, being a proper lady……no, rather, becoming a strength for someone dear to me."

"――――"

Hearing Subaru's words, Flop's eyes widened slightly. But he soon loosened his mouth and nodded, "I see, I see."

"Then, we must succeed at this plan as swiftly as possible, and set our wives' minds at ease."

"Flora mustn't make Medium-san worry either, you know."

"Hahahaha! There's no way I'd worry about my sister. My sister, after all, is one tough girl, in mind and body both. I can't even imagine her being down!"

Though he lacked ladylike grace, Subaru agreed with Flop's cheerful laughter. Just like Flop, it was impossible to picture Medium looking down either. The siblings had surely helped each other through many a rough wave together. That was precisely why Subaru bore a responsibility toward the two she had dragged into this.

"All the more reason we mustn't fail."

"W-wait, please, Subaru……n-no, Natsumi."

To Subaru's back, tensed with resolve, came a somewhat weak voice. Looking, it was Taritta, who was carrying the group's luggage. Unlike her usual self, having left the jungle, Taritta had changed her makeup and costume. Rather than the Amazoness-like Shudrak attire, she wore more fluttering cloth, an exotic, ethnic-style dancer's fashion. If anything, it exposed less skin than her usual outfit, and yet Taritta, blushing bashfully, kept glancing around,

"D-doesn't it feel like we're getting a lot of attention? Is there something odd about me or Kuna……"

"Don't drag me into this too. Besides, if we're talking odd, no one's odder than Natsumi. What was that just now at the checkpoint. Way too good at handling men."

Beside the fretting Taritta, Kuna, in her usual low-energy tone, brought up Subaru's performance just now. Hearing that, Subaru said, "Oh my," placing a hand to her cheek.

"This isn't my first time dealing with gentlemen. And besides, don't worry, Taritta-san. It isn't that you or Kuna stand out. What stands out is all of us. ……Because we're beautiful."

"Because we're beautiful!" Flop agreed.

Nodding deeply, both Flop and Subaru concurred with the opinion. At that answer, Taritta's face went, "beau, tiful……" as though she couldn't believe it.

"T-that kind of talk, is more for Elder Sister……"

"What are you saying, Taritta-jō! Your elder sister's charm and your own charm are entirely different things. In the first place, using words of praise for you doesn't drain the well of praise for Mizelda-jō. Those are separate matters, separate stomachs, as they say!"

"――!"

Flop suddenly took Taritta's hand, saying so with a beaming smile. At that force, Taritta's eyes went wide, her mouth opening and closing wordlessly. Her cheeks reddened by the moment, so much that Subaru said, "Oh my, my," a hand to her mouth.

"Give her a break. Taritta's always been glued to the chief, so she's barely even left the forest. Because of that, she's got no resistance to this stuff."

"My, how endearing. But you're quite composed, aren't you?"

"I snuck out of the forest pretty often, and I never had a scary, splendid big sister like that. ……Though I did have someone like a handful of a little sister."

In Kuna's mind, closing her mouth on the subject, was surely a picture of Holly. The two of them shared a distance somewhere between sisters and close friends, or perhaps parent and child, in an endearing way. Still, Taritta's words couldn't simply be ignored either.

"――――"

Around them, indeed, having passed the checkpoint, attention was gathering on Subaru and the others in their unusual attire, walking the street. On top of their unfamiliar appearance, the whole city was on edge, strung tight by wariness of the imperial soldiers. Naturally, the townspeople's eyes would be harsh toward outsiders too. If so, that meant——

"――An opportunity."

"Only in the sense of curiosity, mind you."

Beneath the parasol, a sharp gaze wrapped in a veil pierced Subaru's profile. Warned not to say anything foolish, Subaru answered with a sulky, "Yes, ma'am." Still, on the judgment that this was an opportunity, Subaru agreed with the dancing girl. And so——

"Following Sir Guard's advice, let's have our first showing on the main street!"

"Right you are, danna-sa……no, Natsumi-jō!"

Declaring on the spot her sudden decision for an impromptu performance, Subaru pressed forward, and Flop followed suit. The two shed part of the costumes they wore, exposing shoulders, midriff, and back, and stepped forward, each taking up an instrument, positioning themselves beside the main street. And then——

"Now then, gather round, come and see! What we are about to show you is song and dance handed down across the ages, across worlds, from beyond the vast waters, from the Great Waterfall far to the east! Presenting the traveling troupe that has come to this city this very day!"

Subaru sang out her ringing sales pitch, and at the eye contact, Flop began to play the stringed instrument in his hands — a lyulyre. The light notes wove together into music, and hearing it, the crowd's attention grew all the more.

"Oh, what's starting?" "Traveling performers, they say! Music, music!" "Huh, quite the lookers, this bunch……"

Drawn by Subaru's spiel and Flop's music, people walking the street stopped one after another, or poked their heads out from within buildings, attention gathering on the troupe. Subaru and Flop handled the main instruments, while Taritta and Kuna played simple percussion, in a castanet-like supporting role. Even so, it was more than good enough, and Flop's skill turned out to be better than expected, so the music posed no problem. More than anything, the surest bet was waiting in the wings.

"What we are about to sing and dance for you is a tale from a distant, distant, different world! A tale woven together by man and man, Dragon and demon, and beings so strange no words can capture them! Please, everyone, take your leisure to the very end, and savor it to your heart's content!"

Swelling expectations with a smile and a heart-stirring spiel, Flop's accompanying performance beside Subaru approached its first climax. Everyone was enchanted by the skill of the playing and the beauty of the musicians, but the true main event came right after this. And then, just as the music swelled to its peak——

"――――"

Unhurriedly, shedding the sheer silk she had worn, the black-haired dancing girl stepped out into the street. Lifting the veil with her hand, as her face was revealed, the gazes of the people who had been enraptured by the music turned that way, and a shock as if struck ran clean through them.

Yes, that's it. That reaction, that face, that agitation — that was exactly what she'd wanted.

"Honestly, how infuriating."

Playing her instrument, Subaru put into words her inner sense of having been outdone. Deep in her chest there was a feeling of defeat. But being outdone this thoroughly was almost satisfying. A lie. A feeling of defeat was a feeling of defeat, and it was, in a word, infuriating. But this stillness right before it turned to fervor was precisely the crux of this whole plan. Then let her, to her heart's content, enchant the people of Guaral with that dance.

"Now, it's your turn, Bianca――!"

"――――"

After a brief sidelong glance this way, Bianca — Abel — slowly raised an arm. Just a single motion of raising an arm. And yet, in that gesture there was an elegance welling up from the very depths of her being. It dissolved into the air, and in an instant, dominated the main street.

A flowing, beautiful, solemn dance began. The dance, matched to the melody being played, was something Abel had originally learned, with Subaru's own arrangement added — something that would appear, to anyone's eyes, entirely new. Though, in truth, such minor trickery hadn't been necessary at all. Subaru's trickery had only finished off something already rock-solid into something even higher.

"――――"

Captivated by the lovely dance, the audience forgot to breathe. Not to look away for even an instant — it was a lament of beauty appealing to instinct itself. To put it grandly: know that this was precisely what eyeballs existed for. Abel's dance thrust that notion at its audience, arrogantly, imperiously.

――Look at me, it said.

At the sight of the enraptured, dazed audience, even Subaru, who had proposed this, felt something terrifying. If there had been a thief here, the standing, frozen people would surely never have noticed even if their pockets were rifled through in plain sight and their purses stolen. ――No, that premise didn't even hold. Because even the thief, surely, would be unable to tear his eyes from Abel's dance.

"――――"

Just one song, barely five minutes in length, and the dance came to an end. Those playing the music had been concentrating so as not to fail, but had they stumbled, would anyone even have noticed? Honestly, that was doubtful, in Subaru's true opinion.

The music ended, and Abel's heels and toes settled onto the ground. Seeing that posture, the audience finally seemed to realize that the dance had already ended, and that Abel simply stood there now. A moment later, thunderous applause and cheers broke out, wrapping the main street.

Exactly as expected, exactly as aimed for, a resounding success beyond doubt. But unlike Flop, who raised his instrument and answered the cheers, Subaru, while keeping up a smile for the audience, felt something wonderfully complicated within. After all——

"――Hmph."

Abel wore just such a triumphant smile, and Subaru could not think of a single retort.

The troupe's reputation in the fortress city of Guaral was sealed by the resounding success of that first dance. It had been a kind of demonstration staged on the main street, but the response had far exceeded expectations, and the troupe's subsequent activity became considerably easier. Still——

"Never forget that this spotlight comes first and foremost from novelty. In the end, our art is but a stopgap……for now, it's merely that our trump card is well-received."

"Yes. Which is precisely why daily, unceasing effort and awareness are necessary!"

At Subaru's clenched fist, spoken with such force, Flop, sitting dutifully in formal seiza, nodded with a smile. Loud-voiced and crisply responsive, Flop truly was a good listener. Thanks to that, Subaru's talk kept gaining momentum without her noticing, but that was a charming quirk to be forgiven. Still, the one Subaru truly needed to be lecturing wasn't Flop.

"I'm talking to you, you two over there! Don't let your guard down!"

"Whoa, fire spread over here."

"U-us……!?"

Pointed at sharply by Subaru, Taritta and Kuna looked put out. Inside the inn, the two had shed their dressed-up clothes and returned to something close to underwear — their usual Shudrak style. While active in town as a troupe, they properly dressed the part, but once inside the privacy of the inn, it was straight back to this.

"I won't say we're not slack, but what's with you, on the other hand……"

"What do you mean, 'what'? Conversations without a subject are unpleasant, I'll have you know."

"Faking myself……?"

At Subaru's questioning tone, as though she had no idea what they meant, Taritta and the others looked startled. Even Subaru had to concede that far, saying, "I'm joking," deflecting their surprise.

Flop, a student worth teaching, gave an answer worth full marks. Though no matter how much Subaru tried to correct it, his first-person pronoun as "boku" while dressed as a woman never quite got fixed, and Kuna shot Subaru a protesting look about it, which she deflected with a smile.

――At present, Subaru and the others, the traveling troupe, were staying at an inn in Guaral.

Three days had already passed since their infiltration of Guaral began, during which they'd given a total of ten performances, earning a response better than adequate. Originally, thanks to the payment from selling Elgina's horn, they weren't hurting for money, but with each performance bringing in a decent amount of tips, it felt like they could manage well enough on that alone besides.

"Still, I think it's about time for the next move."

Running a finger gently along her chin, Subaru wished for some change to this stalled situation. In truth, things were going almost too well as they stood. Every performance was a great success, and their favorability among the townspeople, including the guards, was excellent. That was largely thanks to the perfection of Bianca's dance and the conversational skill of Natsumi and Flora. Bianca, whose mystique was also part of her appeal, showed her face in public almost only while dancing, so naturally, gathering information fell to Natsumi and the others. Since Bianca — Abel — couldn't speak in a woman's voice, that task was meant to fall to Natsumi-Subaru or Flora-Flop.

"It's strange how Flora never arouses anyone's suspicion. Even without changing his voice."

"Hahaha, well, I don't have a special skill like you do, Natsumi-jō. But I did travel with my sister, just the two of us, for a long time. Maybe some of her habits rubbed off on me, and that connects to a certain womanly quality?"

"From Medium-san……?"

Flop proposed his new theory cheerfully, but the Medium that surfaced in Subaru's mind — beautiful in appearance, her expressions changing charmingly moment to moment — made the theory that Flora's perfection came from her influence sound dubious at best.

"――Idle chatter, how leisurely of you."

"Mm."

At that, a cold, frozen voice was thrown at Subaru and the others exchanging such words. It was Abel, seated in the chair by the window, the troupe's dancing girl who had never once joined their conversation, now without the wig on his head.

Day after day, Abel danced as the star performer at each venue, and while his profile tried to show none of it, a faint hint of fatigue peeked through. He himself would surely never admit it, but spending time in Guaral placed a heavy burden on mind and body alike. He was constantly on guard within enemy lines. Even Subaru, repeating admonitions to herself as Natsumi like this, did so to avoid letting her guard down. If one's guard slipped in the midst of the enemy, there was no telling what tragedy awaited.

To Rem, too, and to Mizelda, this had at first seemed like reckless foolery. But Subaru had proposed this scheme in earnest, and had gone over the discussions repeatedly to raise its odds of success. She wasn't understanding enough to simply give up and say "oh well" if it failed. Which was exactly why——

"Bianca, shouldn't you rest a little yourself? Only now do I realize, I've never once seen you sleep."

Flop, about to laugh off Subaru's remark, widened his eyes as he realized he couldn't. Paying no attention to Flop's reaction, Abel merely snorted, showing no intention of engaging. Abel's fierce wariness continued unabated; he trusted no one, not even Subaru and the others, with his heart. Of course, Subaru understood it wasn't easy for someone in his position to let his guard down with others. But even so, not being able to show even a moment's relaxation to comrades risking their lives alongside him in this operation was more than simple fatigue. As ever, he never even blinked both eyes at once. As if someone here would do something the instant he closed both eyes at the same time.

"Doesn't it tire you? Living like that?"

"――. You would say that to me?"

To Subaru's frank statement of what she thought, Abel frowned and shot back. Hearing that, Subaru couldn't tell what he meant by it. Abel's way of living was certainly suffocating — it was simply a matter of Subaru's own not being that way.

"I'll keep on living whether you tell me to or not. As for you……"

"To close both eyes is to entrust the right of life and death to the other party. I do not think so lightly of myself as to permit that, even for an instant."

"――――"

"Was it not you yourself who said not to let one's guard down?"

Even the mouth that wanted to argue back, to say "well then why do you always contradict yourself," was sealed shut. Glaring bitterly at Abel's profile, Subaru found she was no longer being paid attention to at all. With the wig and ornaments removed, Abel kept even his inn attire unified as women's clothing, so that he could transform back into Bianca instantly should the need arise. And so, despite the current imbalance of being wig-less, he still cut a fitting figure even without one.

That, no doubt, was thanks to the mindset of "always on the battlefield" — the very thing Subaru kept impressing upon Flop and the others — being already perfected in him, lending him that taut beauty. And, harboring such an impression at Abel's cold profile――

"――There's been movement."

"Eh?"

Murmuring so, Abel rose from the chair and headed for the bed. Quickly putting his own wig back on, which had been set there, he left Subaru dumbfounded. But the meaning of his action became clear at once.

Because from downstairs came loud, unrestrained footsteps climbing up, and the sound of someone pounding roughly on the door to their room.

"This where the traveling troupe is? Open up. Errand from the city hall."

"Ah, ju-just a moment, please!"

Subaru, breath catching involuntarily, body stiffening, was met with Jamal clicking his teeth. His one good eye, the other covered with a patch, surveyed the room, his uncouth manner making no attempt at concealment. His gaze landed on Taritta and the others, still in their underwear, slow to react — a bitter blow.

At the taut tension, Subaru had interjected, and Jamal swiftly grabbed her by the throat with a hand. Not with the intent to snap it, merely a soft touch to seal her words — even so, the gap in raw power evident in his movement and grip strength was overwhelming. Indeed, Jamal, contrary to his looks and poor manners, was skilled in combat. For now, at least, among the imperial people Subaru had seen, he was probably the most skilled of them all. ――Though scary and strong were two different things.

"H-how kind of you to say, I'm, honored……!"

Bringing his face closer, Jamal looked Subaru over with a lecherous gaze. Even at this close range, he showed no sign of noticing Subaru's true identity. That itself was welcome, but in the opposite sense, it seemed he'd struck a chord with him. Invitations of that sort had, of course, come before, but so far they'd been dodged with smiles and clever talk. With an imperial soldier as the other party, though, dodging it would be difficult――

"――Oh."

Wig readjusted, standing serenely with arms folded, was the black-haired dancing girl stirring up Guaral. Catching sight of her full on, Jamal, remarkably, gave a whistle. While most people would be so entranced they couldn't move, that took quite the nerve.

"Ha-ha, this the dancer everyone's talking about. No wonder they said drag her here no matter what."

"Wha-"

Letting out a breath of surprise and admiration, Jamal released Subaru, then strode straight toward Abel. Of all things, he grabbed her face with his palm and turned it to face him. Unknowingly, he was gripping the imperial visage of the emperor himself, flashing a crude grin at it. Ignorance truly is bliss, Subaru thought, momentarily speechless.

"――――"

"Unlike that one, you've got some guts, woman. Well, fine."

Still held by the chin, Abel calmly stared back at him. Amid Subaru's frantic worry, Jamal snorted, having just committed the empire's highest level of disrespect. And then, still gripping Abel's chin, Jamal turned around.

――Hooked, Subaru clenched a fist inwardly. Jamal's self-important words leapt clean past the disrespect of the scene, bringing Subaru a jolt of surprise and joy. General Zikr Osman — precisely a report that the target fish had been reeled in.

"Th……"

"But of course! We would love nothing more than to deliver song and dance to the imperial soldiers! It is, indeed, our very purpose!"

"Good answer! I like it."

Subaru's momentary reaction was overwritten by Flop's even more momentary answer. But Flop's charm, capable of opening the hearts of any opponent, was on display here too, even toward Jamal. "Get ready quick. I'm taking you to the city hall."

"Ha ha ha, don't worry about it. Ordered to bring you no matter what. This is our job too. Go ahead and change however you like. I'll have those guys behind me carry the luggage."

I don't intend to let you out of my sight, said Jamal's lecherous gaze, sweeping over everyone in the room. It seemed there was no choice but not to displease him. Fortunately, Subaru, Abel, and Flop had all completed at least the minimum of their cross-dressing. Taking advantage of Abel's inability to speak, Subaru delivered a scathing assessment of Bianca. At that, Abel's gaze pierced her way, but Subaru let it slide off. And so, briskly tidying the room's belongings, changing into attire for the trip to the city hall, she dared to behave exactly as Jamal wished.

Let him whistle all he liked, savoring the sight of her changing. Once the operation succeeded, the shock and regret awaiting him would be the greatest of his life. Which was exactly why——

"No! You needn't wiggle your hips deliberately, Natsumi-jō!"

Even to an amateur's eye, nothing resembling military equipment could be found within the city hall. Since the building was, in peacetime, used for the city's administration, it was only natural that no defensive facilities had been prepared here. Still, at present, with over three hundred imperial soldiers within the city, and over five hundred combined with the guards, the defensive force was ample. Still——

"――On our end, we've successfully slipped into the enemy's bosom, at least."

Inside the building, sketching the map in her head, Subaru allowed herself a smirk. Operation Kumasotakeru was proceeding more smoothly than expected. If a troupe of pretty faces became a topic of talk within the city, the expectation was that the general known as a "woman-lover" would take interest — the ultimate goal being to be invited to a banquet and thereby seize him. And now, being invited directly to the city hall like this, the opportunity for realizing it had come into view all at once. But at the same time, another problem had surfaced.

"To decide to carry out the operation tonight……"

There had been no chance to convey that to Mizelda and the others in the separate detachment. Jamal, who had come to fetch Subaru and the others at the inn, hadn't given them a chance to make contact with the outside — not out of vigilance, but out of his own lecherousness. His instincts were bad, but his skill was real — Jamal was, in any case, a troublesome person. Even inside the city hall, Subaru and the others' movements were restricted, and along the way, they'd had no chance to slip off separately under some pretext, so they still hadn't been able to convey tonight's decision to the separate detachment. Which meant——

"We must make contact with the outside from within the city hall."

It was partly meant as insurance in case of failure, but even if Subaru's plan succeeded, the imperial soldiers within the city would still need to be disarmed. Naturally, that would require a certain amount of force, so regardless of the success or failure of Operation Kumasotakeru, there would be no point unless they were in a position to coordinate with the outside. To that end, various trial and error was underway. But——

"Whatever Bianca is thinking, she can't be relied upon……"

It sounded fine to say he was concentrating for the night's banquet, but ever since being invited to the city hall and isolated in what was called a waiting room but was in truth a room of confinement, Abel had remained silent. Of course, he surely understood that lack of coordination with the outside was a condition that could decide the plan's success or failure, but even to Subaru's consultations, there was no response whatsoever. Flop, too, was tremendously cooperative and honestly a great help in every way, but he was hardly a suitable partner for discussing the strategy of bringing down the city hall. In the first place, choosing a method that would place further mental strain on him was not something Subaru wanted either. In other words――

"――I have to figure something out myself."

Clenching a fist, Subaru steeled her resolve inside the restroom she'd been shown to. Subaru and the others had been ordered not to leave the waiting room, but surely no one would obstruct even a trip to the restroom. Still, this city hall, currently, was a frightening place where imperial soldiers would come rushing at the first sign of suspicious behavior — reckless action was out of the question.

She'd glanced at the window, but unfortunately, it was fitted with iron bars, and the waiting room's floor was located on the third floor of the building, so she couldn't rashly slip outside either. It wasn't that they were being especially wary of Subaru and the others in particular. It was simply that the idea of leaving anyone entirely unwatched wasn't in play. That, perhaps, was a manifestation of imperial rule. At worst, the thought crossed her mind that they might need to reconsider carrying out the operation this very night, but,

"……Even if I could feign illness to skip tonight's banquet, I doubt I could dodge the next invitation."

They were a troupe of traveling performers with pretty faces gathered. To begin with, it was easy to imagine that the other side's aim, when going after someone called a "woman-lover," was the dance and performance, and beyond that, nighttime pleasures. That much was obvious from the moment they'd set out to bring him down. She could dodge invitations from ordinary townspeople, but dodging an invitation from an imperial soldier, and a general at that, would surely be difficult. And there was no countermeasure for what to do if dragged into his private chambers. In the end, a disguise was just a disguise; there existed no all-purpose stratagem beyond that point.

"Or rather, if it comes to that, it's game over from a bad choice."

So, one way or another, it had to be decided tonight. Furthermore, even if by some miracle tonight's invitation could be avoided, if Jamal's account was to be believed, it seemed a banquet with ordinary soldiers, rather than just the generals, was slated for tomorrow. Ordinary soldiers — naturally, that would include the one person Subaru least wanted to meet.

"――――"

Honestly, when Jamal had barged into the inn, she'd thought her heart might stop. She'd felt relief that he hadn't recognized Natsumi as Subaru, but that didn't mean every danger had been dispelled entirely. ――Though there was also the possibility he'd taken an arrow and died.

It was a difficult feeling. She never wanted to see him again. But she couldn't quite bring herself to wish him dead either. It was complicated. Subaru knew there was evil in this world that couldn't be allowed to go on living. The Witch's Cult were such beings, all of them people who affirmed unforgivable vices. So, if it were one of the Witch's Cult, Subaru could wish for their death without hesitation. But he wasn't that. He was merely the object of Subaru's fear, not evil itself. In the first place, if she were to say that, what about Louis, whom she'd kept letting slide by default?

"――. No good. I need to focus on the plan in front of me right now."

Fretting now over how ragged her own footing was — how foolish. Besides, if she started listing it, it wasn't just her footing that was ragged. Right now, her body and heart were held together by makeup, but every part of her was ragged, ragged all over. Making skillful use of a threadbare hand of cards — that was Subaru's one redeeming talent.

"I can't linger here any longer."

Having thoroughly searched the restroom, she'd regrettably concluded there was nothing usable to be found. To make contact with the outside, she'd need to find some other method. The watchman waiting outside the restroom door — assigned to keep her from wandering the city hall — she wanted to avoid arousing his suspicion too. For now, a fresh start. If the situation grew pressing, perhaps even Abel would show some willingness to meet her halfway.

"Mm, ah, no need to worry. I happened to have someone to talk to just now."

"Someone to talk to?"

Stepping quietly out of the restroom, the waiting soldier answered so. It seemed he'd been talking with someone passing by, and following his gesturing chin, she looked that way——

"――Huh, so you're the dancer called for tonight's entertainment."

――There, was a face she absolutely never wanted to see, and Subaru's heart froze solid.

"Ah, k……"

"Hm? What's wrong. Making such a surprised face. Hey now, I'm not gonna eat you."

Seeing Subaru's throat trembling on reflex, the man laughed, making light of it as a joke. The joke he'd made was one she'd heard, just a short while ago, from Jamal's own mouth at the inn. But unlike Jamal, whose remark could be waved off as mere lechery, this time it could not be waved off. Because she wanted, genuinely, to ask back whether he really had no intention of eating her.

"What, did you do something, Todd?"

"Me? Don't be stupid, there's no way I could do anything. My wound's still bad, been bedridden this whole time. Only just started being able to walk around again."

"That's true too. Then, maybe it's the physiological type, just didn't like her face?"

"That one kinda stings too, you know……"

Scratching his head, the man having a friendly exchange with the watchman soldier — Todd. The being who made Natsuki Subaru most wary, the one whose death she wished for besides the Witch's Cult — that nightmare, chatting casually right before her eyes.

"――Ah."

Something, she had to say something, Subaru's brain spun at high speed. Silence was bad. She mustn't show even a sliver of anything that could arouse suspicion. Todd would find it in a flash, and use it as grounds for attack. He needed no evidence or proof. If there was doubt, he tried to crush it. Which was why——

"You, seriously, what's wrong? Did we meet somewhere……"

"I-I'm terribly sorry……it's just, um……"

"Just?"

Simply having the same word repeated back to her, quietly, was enough to make her heart flip. She wanted to end the conversation by simply saying "I just feel unwell." When "Return by Death" Subaru had tried to use his knowledge, merely by intending it, Todd had sensed the shift in her heart and driven a knife into her. Lies get found out. Found out.

Lies had to be avoided. It wasn't that she felt unwell. What made Subaru, right now, breathless was——

"A little, well, scared……"

"Scared? Of me?"

"You too. I don't want to say this, but……I was brought here somewhat forcibly."

Averting her gaze, avoiding letting Todd look her in the eye, each individual motion churned within Subaru with the question of whether it was succeeding or failing. If he looked her in the eye, it felt like the lie would be exposed. If she told a lie, it felt like it would be seen through. It wasn't a lie. That she feared Todd was fact, and she hadn't distorted that.

Hearing Subaru's desperate answer, Todd closed one eye, and,

"Brought here forcibly, huh — who was it that brought these kids here?"

"Uh, Corporal Aurélie, I believe it was."

"Ah, Jamal, huh. That explains it. Well, sorry for scaring you, miss."

"Eh……"

Having reached his own understanding through the exchange with the watchman, Todd apologized to Subaru just like that. At that unexpected attitude, Subaru's eyes went wide, and Todd, scratching his cheek with a finger, said,

"Not that he's a bad guy……well, I won't say that either. Jamal's got a foul mouth and a foul temperament, and he's not too bright either. But he means no real harm. That's just how he is, plain and simple."

"I-I see……"

"If you could, would you find it in your generous heart to forgive him? Believe it or not, he's gonna be my brother-in-law. Not a bit alike, but I've got an angelic fiancée who happens to be his sister."

At Todd's wry-smiling words, Subaru found herself bewildered at every turn. From what she could tell, Todd showed no sign of harboring suspicion toward Subaru's words or conduct. If anything, he seemed to sympathize with Subaru, harassed by Jamal, even showing concern for her.

Here, of all places, Subaru couldn't hide her astonishment at how thoroughly her honed skill at cross-dressing, and the fact that she'd been saved by Jamal's habitually crude and troublesome behavior. The days spent pursuing beauty, even knowing it held no particular meaning, had not been in vain after all. That even the sexual harassment from Jamal would prove useful here was entirely unforeseen.

"――Ah, there you are! Oi, Todd, why the hell are you out and about!"

Amid such thoughts of Subaru's, unrelated to them, an angry voice rang through the corridor. Raising his voice, coming with rough, thudding footsteps, was Jamal. Noticing Jamal's approach, Todd said "oops," a hand going to his forehead.

"Found me, huh……"

"'Found me, huh' my ass! A guy with a hole in his gut oughta stay put and rest! You, don't tell me you figured you couldn't trust it unless you saw it with your own eyes."

"No, no, I trust you. You're surprisingly diligent, you do your job properly. But even doing the job seriously, screwups happen to those who screw up, don't they?"

"That's exactly what I mean by not trusting you……!"

At Todd's reply, hands raised and shoulders shrugged, Jamal clicked his tongue. But Jamal, approaching with flared nostrils, noticed Subaru standing beside Todd, and said "Oh," his expression shifting from anger to a grin,

"Ain't that the entertainer girl. Out of that whole bunch, you were the most my type. Hey, if you don't get called on tonight……"

"Ah, yeah yeah, cut it out, cut it out."

Jamal, his eyes lecherous, reached out to grab Subaru's shoulder. But the one who stopped that hand of his was, of all people, Todd. Todd grabbed Jamal's wrist, and to his twisted-cheeked glare, said "cut it out" once more,

"You, it's exactly this attitude of yours that scares people. Scaring a girl you just met right off the bat, and I'm the one who got hurt feelings over it. What are you gonna do about that?"

"What am I gonna do, nothing to do. Anyway, why the hell are you butting in. ……Don't tell me, you and this woman……!"

"Cut the jokes, Jamal. I'm devoted only to your sister. You know that, right?"

"Devoted only to my sister, hearing that as her brother is kinda complicated, you know……"

Muttering that, as though the fight had gone out of him, Jamal shook off Todd's hand. Then, he glanced Subaru's way once more, but at her stiffening expression, seemed to lose any further inclination to bother her. Surprisingly, it seemed Jamal had some sense of restraint after all. Or perhaps, one should say, he had a heart capable of being hurt when someone he'd taken a slight liking to showed fear of him. Either way——

"Anyway, let's get going. Either way, we're not part of tonight's banquet thing."

"Even so, I'm getting tired of you fussing over plugging up every escape route."

"It's not about getting tired of it. Insurance, insurance. ――That's absolutely where it'll come from."

Laughing it off breezily, Todd's sharp remark clawed at Subaru's chest. As she'd thought, the idea of using a back route had been seen through — she inwardly praised herself for that judgment. And having praised herself for that——

"By the way, miss, what's your name?"

"――――"

Had he seen through her having let her guard down? At the feint of a question, Subaru genuinely thought her consciousness might blank out.

Her name, he'd asked. Why, how — questions raged in a storm. A name — surely one would ask that much. ――No, Todd wouldn't ask. He shouldn't be interested. Rem had been wary of Todd from the very start, precisely for that reason. This time, their relationship had gone badly, so Subaru hadn't exchanged names with him either. Should she give it? Or not? What should she do?

"Come on, tell me."

Pressed again, Subaru held her breath. Judging she couldn't stall for an answer any longer, she forced as much composure as possible into a smile, and,

"――I go by Natsumi Schwarz."

So, she gave her name. There had been no option here but to do this. All that remained was to pray and hope this was the right answer, and simply wait for his reaction. Hearing that answer, Todd said "Hmm," nodding while stroking his own chin,

"There you go, Jamal. Good thing you got her name."

"Shut up! Let's just get going already!"

"Yeah, yeah, got it. ……Ah, mind if I come along outside too?"

"You really are the least trustworthy guy I know……suit yourself!"

At the enraged shout of Jamal, his face reddening, Todd, shrugging his shoulders, followed after. And with that, the two of them, without ever once looking back, vanished from Subaru's sight. They turned the corner of the corridor and disappeared from view. Disappeared. Disappeared.

"……really, gone?"

"Y-you okay? Your color's awful."

Watching them go, wondering if they'd truly not come back, Subaru let out a breath. Just how desperate that breath had been, even the sole remaining watchman soldier noticed enough to worry over Subaru's complexion. To respond to that, Subaru no longer had even a shred of composure left. Still, somehow managing to smooth things over with the watchman, she brought him along and returned to the waiting room, rejoining Flop and the others who had been waiting inside.

"That took quite a while, Natsumi-jō! ……Are you all right? Your complexion——"

"I've already handled that part……for now, I've weathered the storm. But the problem remains."

Returning to the waiting room, seeing Flop's concerned face, Subaru finally regained enough composure to notice her own racing heartbeat and ragged breathing. Slowly, with deep breaths, she corrected her disturbed physiological state, calming herself.

Yes, she had — presumably — escaped the storm that had suddenly struck. She still couldn't let her guard down until the very last moment, but for now, the storm had, presumably, passed. And yet, the great wall that had been the original problem had not yet been overcome.

"If we can't make contact with the outside, then even if the plan succeeds after all this effort……"

"Ah, don't worry about that, Natsumi. Kuna and I already sent up a signal so Elder Sister and the others would know."

"Huh?"

Told so simply that the problem weighing on her had already been resolved, Subaru's eyes went wide. At that reaction, Taritta looked apologetic, while Kuna wore an expression of complete indifference. Subaru's gaze demanded an explanation from the two of them, and,

"It's just, that……after Natsumi drew away the watchman, Bianca told us to keep the remaining watchman occupied and get the job done."

"W-w-wha……"

Eyes widening, Subaru looked toward Abel, in the corner of the room. At that gaze, Abel narrowed his eyes and slowly shook his head side to side,

"Rather than have you act unnaturally after being told, I simply used you as a natural decoy. Fortunately, it seems you fulfilled your role adequately. I commend you."

"Quiet, you! Do you have any idea, any idea what a terrifying experience I just……!"

"C-calm down, Natsumi-jō! Look, your lovely face is being ruined!"

"Shut up already!"

Subaru moved to grab hold of Abel out of pure outrage at his unreasonable attitude, only to be restrained from behind by Flop in a full nelson. She genuinely wanted to punch that arrogant, bewitching face, but with the operation impossible without that very face, Subaru had no choice but to calm herself. Taking her own face hostage — what unbelievable villainy.

"You. I'm absolutely punching you once this is over……!"

"That you're already thinking of what comes after it's over suggests admirable composure. Then surely you'll manage a flawless performance as well."

"Flora! Let's rehearse before it's time!"

"A-alright, alright."

Whatever you say to that, he twists it right back — the very extreme of it, Subaru thought, gnashing her teeth in open anger. Whatever came of it, having made contact with the outside now, all that remained was to wait for the appointed hour.

At the very least, yes, at the very least——

"――If this plan doesn't succeed, it'll be unforgivable."

She muttered it like sour grapes, and yet, from the past several days' experience, Subaru was certain that this would not, in fact, end as mere sour grapes.

It was truly infuriating to admit, but she was fairly certain that no one — save perhaps the Witch's Cult themselves — could fail to be captivated by this arrogant dancing girl.
