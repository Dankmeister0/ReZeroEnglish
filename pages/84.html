Arc 3 Chapter 8: "The Outcome of the Gamble"

—Spinning end over end through the air, the coin rose.

The copper coin bore a dragon emblem on its reverse marking it as Lugunican-made, and the year of its minting stamped on its face — in appearance it wasn't so different from the coins of Subaru's original world. The only difference lay in whether the technology behind it was mechanical or magical.

The spinning coin's ascent reached its peak, and for a fleeting instant the copper coin hung motionless in midair, as if pinned there. In that instant, Subaru held his breath, and as he watched the coin enter its fall, he began moving both hands at a tremendous speed.

He punched rapid-fire into empty air, the resulting whirlwind veiling the coin's line of fall from view. Rather than catching it the instant it dropped straight before him, he bent his knees and crouched down in time with the falling coin, buying himself a little more falling time, and just before the coin struck the ground, Subaru crossed both hands as if to scoop it up—

"—Now then! Right hand? Left hand? Or perhaps my mouth!?"

"No need for bluffing. It's in the right."

"Oh my goddess!!"

He opened his trembling right fist, holding out the coin in his palm before the triumphant girl. Watching her smile deepen, all Subaru could manage was to hang his head, shoulders trembling with humiliation. The bag of Appa passed into her hands, and from it two Appa were set down neatly in front of him.

"Now then, two remain. Coming up short on heads or tails, and short again on the catch. What manner of trick will you pit against me next?"

Whether or not she doubted her own victory, the girl seemed purely interested in what wager Subaru would bring next. It was an attitude that could only come from an unshakable core conviction that she would win, no matter what was proposed. Subaru, meanwhile, had lost even the coin-catch wager he'd been confident about, and his ten chips had been whittled down to two — he was in a state of total, imminent defeat. Mentally, he was already at a decisive disadvantage.

"No way... My coin-flourish, honed through nothing but manga-fueled practice, failing to work? How could such a thing be possible? Me, the one they called 'Subaru the Mesmerizer'..."

"Let me tell you, your little tricks are useless. To begin with, I wasn't even watching that coin-flourish thing you were doing. There was no need to."

Seated on a discarded wooden crate against the wall, one knee drawn up, the girl tilted her head as if troubled. The same smile still played at her lips, but it had begun to take on a sadistic tinge — the pleasure of tormenting the weak. Faced with such an arrogant opponent, the man in Subaru burned to somehow bring her down a peg. But not a single means of doing so came to mind.

"To begin with, I lost with the coin-flourish I thought I'd win with... and it was treated as meaningless besides! At this point, I'll just leave it entirely to fate—"

"Have you decided on a method? Or you're welcome to simply offer up your two remaining Appa to me as they are."

"Hey now, deserting in the face of the enemy gets you ordered to commit seppuku for a failure of samurai spirit back where I'm from. Don't underestimate the strictness of the demon vice-captain's code. —And don't think for a second I'll end this as a mere loser."

The girl's demeanor was arrogance itself made flesh. And yet her bearing suited it so perfectly that it carried an intimidating force nearly enough to make one prostrate before her. But Subaru shook his head against it, jabbing a finger at her and putting up a bold front.

"The contest will be... yes, the ultimate decisive method handed down as tradition in my homeland: 'Rock-Paper-Scissors'!"

"Rock-paper-scissors?"

"Whaaat, you don't know rock-paper-scissors? You don't know it? Oh come on now, just how sheltered a hothouse baby do you have to be to end up not knowing that, whyyy?"

"A dull provocation. Lacking in taste. I won't deny that I'm 'sheltered' as you put it, but I won't fall for such a low-grade lure."

He mixed teasing with mockery in a scheme to somehow rattle the girl's composure, but it didn't work on a girl who could see through his intentions with a single exhale. To begin with, such provocation could only work if the other party stood in a position of "facing" Subaru at all. Her self-awareness was established so firmly, and she regarded Subaru as no different from a pebble by the roadside — precisely because of that, the pebble's insults couldn't so much as ripple across her heart.

"I-I-I'm not scheming anything, okay!? Just 'cause I've been on a losing streak doesn't mean I'm trying to scrape together a win with some rotten trick, that's totally small-time spirit, don't underestimate me!"

"A waste of time. Now, hurry up and explain this 'rock-paper-scissors' thing. Then we're done with this."

She waved a dismissive hand, mocking Subaru, who — outmaneuvered instead — couldn't hide his agitation. Biting his lip in frustration, Subaru held out his hand.

"Rock-paper-scissors is a dueling method where you call out a chant and throw your hand into one of a set of fixed shapes, and victory is decided by which shape beats which. There are three shapes: 'Rock,' 'Scissors,' and 'Paper.' Rock beats Scissors, Scissors beats Paper, and Paper beats Rock. Got it?"

As Subaru explained, twisting his hand through the various shapes, the girl gave an "Oh?" and tucked her chin.

"I see, understood. A fairly amusing conceit. And this chant?"

"You throw your hand on the 'pon' of 'Jan-ken-pon.' By the way, if the same shape comes up on both sides, you immediately redo it with the call 'Aiko de, sho.'"

"Is that all of it? Very well, understood. Then I shall throw Paper."

"Bluffing already!?"

Having had the advanced technique of "declaring one's hand" used against him, Subaru trembled at his opponent's sheer comprehension and adaptability. The instant after having the rules explained to her, she'd grasped them and immediately chosen the standard tactic of seizing psychological advantage. Here, unlike the purely luck-based contests until now, was skill on display — the ability to select the best move for victory.

"My plan of explaining the rules and immediately pushing into a round while my opponent's still stunned enough to fumble the outcome — crushed already!?"

"Then let's begin. Now — jan-ken—"

"Ah, wait, timeout, hold on, I haven't even decided what hand I'm gonna—"

His petty scheming had been seen through and turned against him, and Subaru panicked. But there was no stopping a chant once it had begun, and seeing the girl raise her hand, Subaru raised his in time as well.

"Pon!"

The hand thrown with the chant was, just as she'd declared, Paper. And Subaru—

"And here I went out of my way to let you save face... it seems that, whatever excuses you conjure up, you're simply desperate to offer your Appa up to me."

"That's not it! Statistically speaking, there's data showing that when humans are suddenly pushed into rock-paper-scissors, they instinctively clench their fist like 'n-nooo, it's coming out—'! In other words, it's force majeure!"

Before the girl waving her open palm about, Subaru raised his firmly clenched Rock hand and voiced his sore-loser excuses. Subaru had played to perfection the drowned man who'd schemed himself right into drowning in his own scheme.

"Now then."

With a soft exhale, the girl's pale fingers reached out in front of Subaru — plucking up one of the placed Appa and spiriting it away into the bag beside her. With that, only a single chip remained in Subaru's hand.

"And I shall take even that last one, and teach you, who knows not your place, that I stand at the very top, while you are suited only to crawling along the bottom."

"Hey now, isn't it a bit extreme to lose a bet and get treated as the bottom rung of the human-worth pyramid? I'm just a guy who bet it all with no chance of winning, is about to get stripped bare, and still can't back down because of pride, magnificently ruining himself... ah, yeah, that is the bottom rung!"

"Rest easy. Everything beneath me is the bottom. In this world, there is only me, and what lies below me."

"A pyramid with only two tiers — no room to sleep in it, that's actually kind of refreshing!?"

The base resting on the ground would be enormously wide, yet there'd be only a single point at the apex. It sounded like it would make for a pyramid with a brutally steep angle toward the top. While Subaru struggled to figure out where he'd even sleep inside his imaginary pyramid, the girl clapped her hands with a "Now then," pulling his mind back to reality. And—

"Let us wager that last one, and have our contest, shall we not?"

"...How about, around now, you take pity on me and let that last one slide?"

"Every Appa in your possession shall become mine. —I have already stated my decision. All that remains is for you to devise the means, and pave the road by which you offer your Appa up to me. Watching you has been amusing enough in its way... but it has grown a touch tedious."

Her tone hadn't changed a bit, but it was, in effect, a death sentence — she had no intention whatsoever of letting Subaru off the hook. For a girl who showed not a trace of doubt in her own path, obtaining the Appa was already a settled matter. And if that was so, Subaru had no way out.

"How about the final round is rock-paper-scissors too?"

"—Hm, wagering yourself again on a contest you were just soundly beaten at. That, too, is a kind of decision, though whichever you choose, it shall prove futile."

"No more speeches from either of us. And no more of that setup from before — I'm going into this round straight."

Thrusting out his fist, Subaru first sealed off the girl's verbal maneuvering. She raised an eyebrow, seemingly surprised by his attitude, but apparently concluded it wasn't significant enough to affect the outcome, and shook her head.

"Very well. Then let us begin promptly, and end it promptly."

Having confirmed the girl's assent, Subaru widened his stance and drew back his fist. He covered his drawn-back right fist with his left hand, hunching his body and twisting at the waist, wringing his entire body taut like the draw of an iaido strike — building a fighting spirit fierce enough to tear silk.

"—Aaaaaaah!"

A war cry spilled from his mouth as he channeled all his focus into the moment. Meaningless motions, meaningless gestures, meaningless mental preparation, meaningless tension. No matter how much strength he poured into his whole body, victory or defeat wouldn't hinge on any of that. Even knowing this, Subaru still poured his entire soul into the right fist on which the match rode.

The air of the back alley changed, and even the girl's expression stiffened before Subaru's fervor for this final round. Then, the instant their swelling fighting spirit reached its peak, the two of them moved—

"Jan-ken—PON!!"

The chant. With it, their fists shot forth as if released from a spring, and for a fleeting instant, all sound vanished. The air seemed to split before the advancing fists, an illusion of light bursting and flickering through the ruptured space — and looking at the fist thrust out before her, Subaru saw agitation spread through the girl's red eyes.

The girl's clenched fist was Rock. And facing it, the shape of Subaru's hand was—

"Th-this is..."

"Hear it and be astonished, see it and be dumbfounded. This is the lethal weapon of rock-paper-scissors, handed down since ancient times."

Subaru's murmuring right hand had formed a shape beyond the girl's comprehension. —An irregular hand with the thumb, index finger, and middle finger all raised. People called it the ultimate forbidden move, and had trembled in fear before it—!!

"That's right, this is the ultimate technique — 'Rock-Scissors-Paper'!"

"What is that!? I never heard of any such hand existing!"

"Shut up, dammit! I didn't mention it, sure, but it's your fault for not asking! This part here's Rock, this part's Scissors, and this part around here's Paper. Which means my hand already beats your Rock, see."

"If that argument holds, then part of it is losing, though..."

"Aaah, laaa, laaa! Can't heaaar you!!"

Covering his ears and shouting, Subaru tap-danced on the spot as he scooped up the Appa, clutching the one last piece he hadn't lost with the utmost care.

"My win! My win it is! I won't hear any complaints! Unlike a simple coin toss, you failed to notice there was a hidden catch in the rules — that's on you for not trying hard enough! In other words, my sheer will to win beat your monster luck! No objections! I'm positivar!"

At Subaru's ugly claim — a distillation of sophistry, sore-loser excuses, pettiness, and every cowardly trick paraded at once — the girl groaned "grrrh" in frustration. It was the first expression leaning toward the negative that the girl — who had maintained an unflappable composure this whole short while — had shown.

"—Very well then, one last round."

"Huh? What are you on about, Miss Bunny. The round we wagered that last piece on ended in my win, curtain closed. I've got nothing — nada — no more reason to keep playing!"

"I'll wager the single Appa you hold against every Appa I have won."

The girl declared this, her eyes gleaming brilliantly, at Subaru as he tried to cut and run with his winnings. At her words, Subaru too went still, staring back at her intently. In his dark eyes, the girl's expression already showed her composure restored — no, more than that, an intensity of conviction was visible.

"The contest, once again, is rock-paper-scissors. However — each of us may play that 'Rock-Scissors-Paper' hand only once."

"Are you serious? I'll tell you now, all ten Appa are already yours..."

"I believe I said hand over all of them. If it's not all, it's the same as none."

A reasoning so self-righteous it was almost refreshing. The girl tilted her head, demanding "well, your answer?" and questioning Subaru's resolve. In response, Subaru silently declared to himself, "This girl's a serious idiot." Getting heated over a bet like this and losing track of when to quit was the height of foolishness. For Subaru, who had little attachment to victory himself, it was without question a hard emotion to understand. More than anything, he couldn't fathom the greed that would drive someone to pour their entire soul into taking what belonged to another. Therefore, having already secured one Appa, and having felt, even if only once, that he'd taken her down a peg, Subaru had no reason to take her up on this bet. And yet—

"That old man's scarred-up face is pushing me forward. —Take back the fruit of our bond. And knock that arrogant woman's nose down a peg, and do something naughty-oh-my while you're at it!!"

"I'll take that as a yes."

At the girl's low-voiced confirmation, Subaru dipped his chin to signal agreement. Then, raising their fists together, the two entered the sequence of the final round.

—The mere seconds before their hands would come out stretched into a world of frame-by-frame slowness, his thoughts accelerating.

As a given, "Rock-Scissors-Paper" could only be played once. And since it was a guaranteed win, it would naturally, as a matter of course, be the first card played. If that ended in a tie, the match would shift from there into pure, ordinary rock-paper-scissors.

The girl's tremendous luck operated with a precision that could only be called a threat by now. Seven consecutive correct calls on the coin toss, and the identification of which hand held the coin in the coin-catch trick. It was enough that he'd almost believe her if told she could see the future. But foreseeing the future couldn't possibly be real. To know what was coming and act to overwrite it — that would be far too much of a cheat. Such an absurd ability—

"Ah, wait, that was literally me!"

"Here we go — jan-ken—"

Aiming for the moment his concentration broke, the girl's hand swept down. Seeing it, Subaru instinctively followed suit, thrusting out his fist. And then—

"So Rock-Scissors-Paper comes out, just as expected. But you know — too soft."

Seeing the Rock-Scissors-Paper hand thrust out before him, Subaru's cheek twisted into a smirk. Before Subaru's confident air, the girl once again blinked in bewilderment.

"What is that hand?"

A voice, somehow gone icy cold, was directed at Subaru as he thrust a thumbs-up into the field. But Subaru, oblivious to the chill in her tone, let out a snort of "hmph" and said,

"This is the hidden ultimate technique that goes even beyond the forbidden move — the 'Bomb.' The strongest finishing move, one that blows away even Rock-Scissors-Paper. Bam, boom, and the other guy's a dead sweetie."

"What is that!? Is anything allowed!?"

"This is genuinely, truly my last hidden card! You confirmed how Rock-Scissors-Paper works, sure, but you never asked about other hands, so I didn't answer, see. The Bomb's effect deals direct damage to you! Shattered! Blown to bits! I take a standing ovation!"

Clapping his hands wildly to drag the mood of the moment onto his side, Subaru loudly proclaimed his victory. The girl bit her lip in frustration and pointed a trembling finger at Subaru.

"A-are you saying anything goes as long as you win!? Have you no pride as a man, you!?"

"Winning's all that matters, winning! Don't get it twisted! I don't like fighting! I like winning!"

Declaring, with full-blown conviction, lines straight out of a two-bit villain's playbook, Subaru stamped his feet. Then he covered the finger pointed at him with his palm from above.

"By going through with this last round at all, you basically indirectly acknowledged my earlier win with Rock-Scissors-Paper! Which means you built the flow yourself where you now have no choice but to acknowledge this win too! Ha-ha, rock-paper-scissors really is hell!"

"Grrrh..."

The girl groaned in frustration before the triumphant Subaru. But she quickly shrugged with a "there's no help for it" and, putting her hands on her hips, threw out her chest.

"As you say, having let it proceed, I cannot back out now. Therefore, it may be said the bet is your win. Very well — do as you wish."

"There," she seemed to say, stepping forward toward him. Her posture emphasized her generous bust, and — with nothing to support it — her dress-clad chest swayed lightly. Looking closely at it right in front of him now, it was a bit beyond what he'd imagined. An encounter with what one might call "big-bust talent" had never once happened in Subaru's life before now. At Roswaal Manor, the bust rankings had Emilia's lovely chest at the top, and the twins below her couldn't be called generous even as flattery. Beatrice was excluded from ranking to begin with. So, before the girl's body thrust toward him as if it were a formal reward, Subaru swallowed hard.

"H-hey, hey hey hey, that's awfully decisive of you. You could show a little more of a 'oh nooo, I couldn't possibly do something so embarrassing, how mortifying' act, you know? Then maybe a man like Natsuki Subaru wouldn't hesitate to show a little mercy..."

"Once a contest is decided as a loss, making excuses on top of it is not permitted. Nor is sore-loser talk. As you say, it was my own fault for sitting complacent atop my Divine Protection and forgetting my drive to win. —Though I suppose, for one who has never once been defeated until now, it may be called a valuable experience. So there is nothing to regret. Take it."

"Go on," she seemed to say, stepping even closer. Overwhelmed by her momentum, Subaru retreated exactly as much as she advanced. At his behavior, she furrowed her brow.

"Don't tell me... now that it comes down to actually touching my chest, you've gotten cold feet?"

"Hah!? W-what are you even talking about, I seriously don't get it! Who's g-g-getting cold feet!? Where'd you get that info!? What time, what minute, how many times did the earth even spin!?"

"...A man who throws off one's rhythm something fierce, honestly. Though I suppose, getting cold feet like that, it's endearing in its way."

Pressing a palm to her forehead as if exasperated, the girl regarded Subaru with a sultry gaze. A chill ran up his spine at that look, and Subaru instinctively hugged his own body the way a woman might.

"Okay, whatever, I don't even care anymore, please just let me go. I just wanna take my Appa and head back. I'm separated from my seriously cute travel companion right now... though, honestly, this is probably a total time-out already."

Emilia was surely, right about now, desperately searching for the now-missing Subaru. His plan of getting back before she left the guardhouse and insisting he'd been waiting all along like a good boy no longer stood a chance. Not that Subaru — who'd gone and wasted his time getting distracted by the allure right in front of him — had any right to make excuses.

"And on top of it, now that I actually have the right, I'm being a total wimp and hesitating to exercise it. No, look, it's the pure-hearted part of me talking here. It's saying my first boob-touch in another world has gotta be with the girl I actually like, no ifs ands or buts."

"A rather moldy set of values, if you ask me... though I suppose, in its own way, I don't much care for that either."

Caught between the question of touching and not touching, the situation between the two of them began to flip on its head. Subaru, showing off his virginal nature, chickened out at the very last moment, while the girl, for her part, apparently couldn't let her pride allow her to withdraw the body she'd offered up, treating this as a convenient out. Right in the midst of this thoroughly baffling back-and-forth stalemate, the change came not from the two of them, but from outside.

"—Hm, this looks like it's about to become troublesome."

The girl abruptly turned her gaze from Subaru toward the entrance of the street. Drawn along, Subaru turned to look that way too.

"Huh, is that... a bunch of not-so-savory-looking folks strolling in?"

"And leading them, I believe, is a piece of trash I recognize. Honestly, a bunch of fools without a shred of amusement to them."

The girl shook her head, wearing an expression that hid neither exasperation nor contempt. Beside her, Subaru scrambled to his feet and glared down the alley in the opposite direction from the approaching group. He didn't sense any group closing in from that side, though he had no assurance it wasn't a dead end either—

"Either way, if we get caught, the vibe screams straight to BAD END! Coming back after hearing Reinhard's name, what are those guys even thinking!?"

"They must have realized you laid the bluff about knowing the Knight among Knights on a bit too thick. They have their pride too. They've gathered numbers, erased their weakness, and come to challenge us. That's what you were calling drive to win a moment ago, isn't it."

"What a loser spirit! I can forgive myself for having no pride, but I'm the type who can't forgive other people acting without pride!"

Subaru and the girl, both saying fairly self-serving things. The addlepated fool walking at the head of the group noticed the pair of them. Shouting something, he signaled to the group behind him, and the tough-looking men began to break into a run. Seeing this, Subaru—

"Dammit, is today seriously just an unlucky day!?"

Starting with the near-fall from the dragon carriage, then getting separated from Emilia, and now this — if things went badly, it could end in a sword-slash incident. He really hadn't had a decent moment all day.

Subaru forcibly grabbed the hand of the girl standing next to him, stuffed the Appa into the bag, and, with everything he had, stepped out toward the darkness behind them — toward a destination he had no idea of.

"Hey, what do you think you're doing, don't touch me so casually."

"Is this the time for that!? If you don't wanna get caught, you run too! Look at the lecherous faces on those guys! If we get caught, it's gonna turn into some seinen-magazine development, eroticism explosion!"

Dragging the girl, who had little inclination to run, along by force, Subaru pressed forward with powerful strides, punctuating each syllable — "And. On. Top. Of. That!"

"Right now, ownership rights to your boobs belong to me! Which means letting those guys touch them is an utterly unspeakable oppekepe! Got it? Then dash! B-dash!"

Pulling her by the arm, tearing along the rough ground, Subaru made for the depths of the alley with the girl in tow. Feeling the men's curses and footsteps chasing after them in a chain behind him, and consciously ignoring the sensation of his heartbeat already climbing and his lungs beginning to ache, Subaru went on running through the narrow, narrow alley, deeper and deeper in.

It was twenty minutes after leaving Subaru behind that Emilia was finally released from the guard post and able to return to the original street.

As for her original objective — making contact with Reinhard — despite the long time it had taken, the conclusion, put simply, was that it had ended in failure. When she'd brought up the topic of Reinhard to Julius, who had been guiding her,

"Reinhard, is it? He's been quite busy lately, it seems, and hard-pressed to find the time... though, if it's a message from you, Lady Emilia, I would be glad to relay it to him."

Julius had offered as much, and Emilia had somehow managed to decline, her purpose derailed early on — she'd planned simply to head back to the street, but—

"That took up more time with Julius than I expected."

"He didn't mean any harm, and he was considerate about it too, so it's not like I minded. Besides, I don't dislike his rich, refreshing Mana one bit."

Puck answered from within her silver hair at Emilia's words as she finally stepped free onto the street. At his reply, Emilia nodded with an "Mm."

"I know he's not a bad person. And I think the conversation itself was worthwhile, so it's not that I have complaints about that, but..."

"Well, if nothing else, he does talk about himself a bit too much. ...If Subaru were here, wouldn't he call this 'not reading the room' or something?"

Emilia let out a breath at Puck's easy laughter, then looked out over the street. Twenty minutes was more than enough time to grow weary of waiting. She honestly felt sorry for making him waste that much time doing nothing. But what unsettled Emilia's heart even more than that was—

"How should I put it — this feels exactly like what I expected."

"...I figured he wasn't the type of kid who could sit still and wait for a full twenty minutes, but..."

Her unpleasant sense of foreboding had proven right after all, Emilia thought, running a hand through her silver hair and bowing her head. Nowhere along the street before the noble district, visible from where she stood, was there any sign of Subaru, whom she'd told to wait. She wondered if he might be waiting for her somewhere in the shadows, but after spending several minutes in front of the guardhouse without him showing himself—

"He doesn't seem to be on this street anymore... Puck, can you track him?"

"I'll give it a shot, though. Subaru's Mana feels kind of thin all around right now, so don't get mad at me if it doesn't work."

"That way of talking — you're starting to sound like Subaru. —Anyway, please."

"'Kaaay," Puck answered in a small voice, and pushed his way out through her silver hair. Slipping out of her hood, he settled onto Emilia's shoulder and twitched his pink nose, sniffing out Subaru's scent — in this case, the lingering trace of Mana filling his body.

For Mana belonging to someone he knew reasonably well, Puck's nose was quite reliable. The issue was how much time had passed since Subaru had vanished. And the matter of his Mana shortage, given his current physical condition. Both cast a faint shadow of worry over their chances of reuniting.

While Puck put his nose to work, Emilia too looked around, trying to get a sense of where Subaru might have gone. The street, serving as an entrance to the noble district and, above all, standing right before the guardhouse, saw very little foot traffic. Passage in and out of the noble district was quite strictly policed, and for anyone with no business there, the area in front of the guardhouse wasn't exactly a place one would want to loiter around. So aside from Emilia, the figures on the street could be counted on one hand—

"...I wonder what that person's doing."

Precisely because of that, the strange behavior of a figure she happened to spot across the street caught her eye all the more sharply. The spot was several dozen meters from the guardhouse, along the road leading toward the commoners' district, opposite the entrance to the noble district. Partway along it, someone was leaning against a wooden garbage-collection box set beside the road, head and arm thrust inside, rummaging through its contents.

"Maybe he's looking for something he dropped."

Emilia thought as much, a finger to her lips. Glancing at her shoulder, she saw Puck, eyes shut, still diligently twitching his nose, in the midst of chasing down the trace. He hadn't yet gotten a lead to act on. Having confirmed as much, Emilia murmured a small "alright" to herself, then stepped forward, heading toward the suspicious figure.

If Subaru had been present, or if anyone else had, surely they would have stopped her right then. That was how much revulsion the act of approaching someone earnestly rifling through a trash box ought to have inspired. But there was no one present to stop Emilia, alone as she was, and Puck, the only one who might have offered a warning, was too absorbed to notice her actions right now. And so, unhindered, her approach carried her all the way to the figure's back.

"Huh, not here either, huh, Princess. This is rough, seriously. What am I gonna do..."

As she reached just behind him, the sound of the figure grumbling reached her ears. It was a man's voice, as she'd already been able to tell from the figure's back. The man had his upper body thrust into the wooden box, dressed in somewhat grimy light gear. And yet the broad-bladed sword fastened sideways at his hip made a very strong impression all its own. Seen from behind, the build of his back down through his lower body was sturdy as well — no doubt he was someone who possessed a fair amount of fighting ability.

From the roughness of his tone, too, he was the sort one would normally rather avoid making contact with. But then, standing there was, as ever, a Half-Elf whose good nature strayed a bit outside the bounds of normal sense.

"Um."

"—Hn?"

When she spoke up without hesitation, the man let out a startled sound and jerked his head back—

"Ngah!"

Right as he was pulling his upper body out, the back of his head slammed hard into the box's opening. He doubled over on the spot, and the momentum sent his chin crashing into the outside of the box next. Struck top and bottom in quick succession, the man crouched down.

"Oww, ow, that hurts...! What the hell, what am I even doing, a grown man like me..."

"A-are you all right?"

At the man's oddly plaintive tone as he reflected on himself, Emilia, feeling guilty for having spoken to him, reached out and touched his shoulder. And—

"Eek!"

The man shot to his feet with such force that Emilia, her hand still on his shoulder, startled. Then the man spun around on the spot, coming face to face with Emilia standing behind him.

Since the man was taller, Emilia's gaze angled slightly upward. And the instant she found herself face to face with him, her violet eyes went wide with astonishment.

Pointing a finger at Emilia, whose throat had closed up with shock, the man said—

"Hey, don't go touching me so carelessly, missy. Touch me and you'll get burned."
