Arc 5 Chapter 53: "The City in Chaos"

"—Check whether that thing's heart is beating!"

It was intuition that drove Subaru to act—an impulse born from that very thought. He had no conviction, no grounds for it. He simply dared to believe it wasn't meaningless.

Sin Archbishops, the names of stars, Kararagi where traces of another world lingered, another otherworlder besides Subaru himself. If they had left their mark on this world, and if that trace was etched into the Witch Cult as well, then the connection Subaru sensed between star names and their legends wasn't a notion to be tossed aside.

If Regulus Corneas's Authority couldn't simply be dismissed as "Invincibility," then the idea needed to crack that shell open existed nowhere but inside Subaru. And so, praying it wasn't unrelated, Subaru raised his voice. It happened an instant later.

"—!"

A thick, crushing pressure surged over him, and Subaru felt the illusion of losing all sense of up and down. The very air turned murky, palpably so. It was a discomfort, a revulsion, hard to put into words. The revulsion of a scab peeled off without care, the displeasure of having a fetid breath blown across his skin, the horror of being licked persistently by some sticky tongue on his bare flesh.

The source of that thoroughly foul presence was the white-haired madman, now turning to look back at them. The instant their eyes met, Subaru's body shook without his willing it.

Those blank, expressionless eyes pierced Subaru's heart like a curse. It felt as if he were being clawed at with rusted needles, as if even his lungs and heart had frozen over with fear. But even as Subaru froze under the weight of that sensation—

"Looking elsewhere isn't very polite of you. I believe I'm your opponent!"

For Regulus to turn toward Subaru meant, by definition, turning his back on the Sword Saint facing him. What Reinhard raised overhead in both hands was a chipped signboard and a scrap of iron junk, the kind of debris that looked to have simply been lying in the street. Junk that would otherwise have ended its existence as mere noncombustible waste—Reinhard's arms elevated it into a masterwork rivaling any legendary blade.

The blade that cut through the air struck diagonally, slamming into the back of Regulus's tuxedo. The shockwave that burst outward spread through space—the delayed shriek of air that only just realized it had been cut, and the water of the great canal, thinly crusted with ice, swirling and spraying upward. And that was merely the aftershock of the strike. It would have been no surprise at all if the madman had been reduced to pieces. And yet, it still fell one step short.

"Don't get the wrong idea, Sword Saint. The only reason I've been playing along with you is because my generosity and composure allowed it. But even kind, sweet me has limits, you know."

"—!"

Regulus brushed lightly at his back where the blade had struck, tilting his head. That gesture set off Reinhard's alarm, and he discarded the shattered fragments in his hands, moving to leap far backward—only for his feet to stop.

Reinhard's supernatural senses. A terrifying intuition that sensed any threat or attack bearing down on him before it arrived. That razor-sharp sense refused to let him evade backward. He tried at once to search for another option, and his bending knee froze as well.

"The air all around here—I've already touched every inch of it."

His too-sharp senses had produced paralysis, and in this instant Reinhard was defenseless. An unseen trap lay wide behind him, encircling him. Reinhard's decision was to press forward and slip past the madman's side—but that required a check, a blow to hold him back first.

"Hah!"

The blow he unleashed carried an edge sharp enough, ordinarily, to pierce straight through solid rock. The hilt of the dragon sword drove into Regulus's chest, yet the madman himself took it with an untroubled face.

"Such a wasted effort—well done, though. Best you pray your wounds turn out light."

"It seems Subaru was right after all... your heart isn't beating here."

"—!"

Regulus's easy smile froze, and he looked down at his own chest. The sword's hilt was still lodged there, and Reinhard had strained every nerve not to miss even the faintest pulse of life through it. Realizing he'd been played for a fool, Regulus's leg snapped up in a furious kick.

A direct hit—it looked like a rerun of the scene from before. Reinhard brought the dragon sword's sheath up to meet the incoming toe of the kick, but his body, unable to disperse the impact, was blown backward. This time, though, what followed was different.

"Reinhard!!"

Just as declared, countless traps of Regulus's breath lay waiting behind Reinhard. What would happen if he plunged in defenseless—the result made that plain enough.

Reinhard's white attire was dyed with blood, his whole body torn open as his tall frame flew through the air. How much he'd managed to lessen the injury was impossible to tell; he crashed once more into a cluster of ruins, his body widening the city's collapse, too fast for the eye to follow. The one thing certain was that Reinhard had answered Subaru's request.

"You did great, Reinhard...!"

"Subaru!"

"It's fine. Reinhard's probably fine! Worry about him later!"

"I know that already! What should I do?"

Subaru had been certain she was worried about Reinhard's safety, so her answer caught him off guard. But the look Emilia fixed on him was serious, and it was clear she understood exactly what role she needed to play here. It came from her trust in Reinhard's strength, and perhaps, from her trust in Subaru as well.

"Reinhard took on something that reckless because he believes in you too, Subaru. You've figured something out about Regulus, haven't you? Tell me."

Her trust weighed on him. Her expectation weighed on him. The simple fact of being believed in weighed on him—and that was exactly why it fired him up. He owed Reinhard his thanks too. He'd make sure to pick up the pieces for him later.

"You two, muttering to each other over there—why don't you try despairing a little more plainly? You've made me angry with your cruelty and your underhanded conduct; you're in a position where you ought to be punished, aren't you? Isn't that so? Won't that be so? Infidelity and impropriety—either one's a capital crime deserving ten thousand deaths."

Having kicked Reinhard away and rid himself of the nuisance, Regulus sneered. Facing him across the canal, the madman's malevolent aura only kept swelling; honestly, Subaru would rather have declined even standing here face to face with him. But running away here simply wasn't an option. Natsuki Subaru couldn't repay Emilia, couldn't repay Reinhard van Astrea, if he did.

"Accusing the purest heroine this century's ever seen of infidelity—that makes you the impudent bastard here, you idiot."

"Ahh?"

"Sorry to interrupt while you're busy inventing reasons for us to be scared, but it's you who needs to put that empty head of yours to work a little."

At Subaru's sudden burst of bravado, Regulus's eyes bulged. Subaru tapped his own head with a finger, ostentatiously, and said:

"I don't know how smoothly you've ridden the waves of life up till now, and I don't wanna know either... but have you noticed? Right now, you're being cornered. For sure."

"Cornered? That makes so little sense I can't even laugh. What exactly are you trying to say? Actually, never mind—there's a good chance nothing you say will come out as a language I can understand, so if it's nothing, feel free to say nothing."

"Come on, don't say that, just listen. You've got the right to hear it. A right—your favorite thing."

"My right...?"

At Regulus's furrowed brow, Subaru put on a smile mixing equal parts flippancy and mockery. Then he continued, "Yeah, because—"

"—losing without knowing how you lost would leave you with regrets, wouldn't it."

"—Just disappear already, you!!"

Subaru's shrug of the shoulders served as the trigger, and Regulus's body launched itself from the canal's edge. Lacking sufficient leaping power, his body landed on the water's surface. But once submerged, his body suffered no loss of mobility whatsoever from the water—he simply leapt upward again, ignoring the water's resistance and everything else about it.

Watching up close as Regulus's body sank beneath the surface, Subaru clapped Emilia on the shoulder.

"Emilia, now!"

"Ul Huma!"

Following Subaru's cue, Emilia released the mana she'd been gathering, transforming it into ice pillars. Ice spears massive enough to make one look up rained down upon Regulus, driving into the ground all around him just as he rose from the water's surface, forming a cage of ice.

"And here I thought you'd do something clever—but it seems you never learn no matter how many times it proves useless! What is this, defiance? Sitting there smug without even that much wit, always looking down on people as you hand them the same futile effort over and over? Don't get carried away, you imperfect thing!"

Baring his teeth, Regulus swept the ice cage aside, stomped it, and shattered it to fragments with overwhelming force. As easily as knocking down a wall of sand, Emilia's ice prison, forged with all her might, returned to mana. But that was fine. That was exactly as intended.

"No good—it's not even buying us time after all."

"That's not it, Emilia-tan."

To Emilia, her face clouding at seeing her power fail to hold, Subaru shook his head. It was a difference in perspective. Subaru's actual objective had already been achieved, and achieved fully, by that alone.

"Given how spiteful he is by nature, he can't be satisfied unless he's stepping on someone and looking down on them. Even an obstacle he doesn't need to destroy, he won't feel like he's won unless he tramples right over it."

Regulus, who proclaimed himself complete, who boasted of being fulfilled. The turbulence of his heart, the smallness of his vessel, his bloated vanity—all plain to see.

"By rights, that guy doesn't need to do anything at all to get past an obstacle. And yet, without needing to, he takes one extra action. Even a single second, even a fraction of one—that's a result."

"Can we beat Regulus with that one second?"

"Stack them up, and I swear I'll make you win. I'll strip off that mask of his."

Reinhard had already laid the groundwork for that. He'd confirmed Regulus's heartbeat and told Subaru there was no sound of one at all. No warmth flowed through him, no pulse beat in his heart, no breath moved in his lungs, and nothing around him affected him. That was, without question, "Invincibility"—but its true nature was not "Invincibility."

"Emilia! This way!"

Pulling Emilia by the arm, Subaru dashed with her through streets pocked with holes. Emilia matched her pace to Subaru's while glancing back to aim ice pillars at Regulus. Seeing the two of them flee, Regulus's face grew ever more furious as he gave chase with fierce intent.

"After all that big talk! What's the meaning of running away?! Calling me an idiot, saying you've cornered me—you just keep making a fool of me, don't you?! Who do you think you are?! Coward!"

Since his physical abilities weren't anything extraordinary, Regulus's pursuit speed wasn't much different from Subaru's own running pace. If anything, he was even a bit slower. But blessed with an Authority that never ran out of stamina, the madman would eventually catch up if he kept up a full sprint.

"Subaru! Where are we headed?!"

"The church! The target is Regulus's wives! Somewhere among them—"

Subaru answered Emilia's call, his voice cracking. Glancing back mid-sentence, checking behind them, he said:

"—Don't you dare figure out anything extra, you."

"Whoaaa!?"

Where had that earlier distance gone? Regulus appeared right before him the moment he turned back. Regulus closed the gap in a single bound, and Subaru barely ducked his head to dodge the palm strike coming down on him. He felt as if the back of his hair had nearly been taken clean off; pulling Emilia tight against him, he closed on the wall to his side, planted his foot, and scrambled straight up it.

"Wh-wh-whoa, Subaru, amazing—"

"Emilia-tan, just hang on tight!"

Emilia's eyes went wide at Subaru's acrobatics, and her arms wound around his neck, her legs around his waist. She was soft and smelled nice. It filled him with motivation. He kicked off each foothold, clearing more of the wall. His parkour training was paying off. If he could keep this up and put distance between them—

"See, that's the thing—all that effort you mediocre people put into closing the gap is wasted."

As he spoke, Regulus ran his palm across the base of the wall Subaru was climbing. A ponderous sound like a millstone grinding rang out, and the stone wall was gouged out as easily as if it were tofu. Losing its support, the stone wall toppled—and of course, so did Subaru and Emilia, who were clinging to it.

"Gaaah!"

Mid-fall, Subaru yanked out his whip and flung it upward more or less at random, with no particular target. The tip caught on something, and Subaru hauled his body up on it by brute force. He swung his legs, and the instant the soles of his shoes touched the wall, he kicked off hard. Using the recoiled centrifugal force of the whip, he twisted his body even further, and, still holding Emilia, Subaru pulled off a stunning climb.

Looking around, the two of them had made it to a warehouse that had lost about half its footprint. Slumping down on the watchtower jutting from the roof, Subaru looked at his own palm.

"Whoa, no way, I pulled off some adrenaline-fueled superhuman strength there...!"

"Subaru! We just need to get to the church, right?! Which way?!"

Wincing at the sight of his palm, its skin scraped raw, Subaru looked around at Emilia's call. Fortunately, thanks to the watchtower's height—and, ironically, Regulus's handiwork—the view of the surroundings was excellent. Far off, he could make out what looked like the wreckage of the church, collapsed by Reinhard's first strike. Somehow they'd ended up a considerable distance away.

"Crap! We ran the wrong direction! What do we do?!"

"That way, right?"

"Yeah, that way, but how are we gonna—"

"Like this!"

At Subaru's answer, Emilia clapped her hands, and an instant later a bridge of ice extended out from the watchtower. The bridge, wrapped in a mystical pale blue glow, took shape in midair, starting from the watchtower and cutting a great shortcut through the air toward the street that led to the church.

"What!?"

Looking up at that ice bridge, even Regulus, who had been braced for the two of them to come falling down, was stunned. At Emilia's decision to force the solution through with sheer power, Subaru clapped his hands and stood, setting his foot on the stairs leading up to the ice bridge. But the madman interfered with the two fugitives' escape from directly below.

"Like I'd let you go!!"

Circling beneath the ice bridge, Regulus hurled a stone he'd picked up at it. The hastily conjured bridge took massive damage from that single blow alone, and cracks instantly spread across its entire length. It shattered from its worst-damaged point, the bridge turning cruelly into crystals of light. Watching this unfold, Regulus smiled cruelly. But—

"That wasn't the real plan—the bridge was fine to break!"

"Wha—!?"

Above the shattered, scattering glitter of ice dancing through the air, Subaru and Emilia glided along on the ice pillar Emilia had launched. Using the bridge's slope as a launching ramp, the two of them flew like ski jumpers, soaring over the canal and the streets in one motion, leaving Regulus behind as they headed for the church.

"Emilia-tan, you've gotten crafty!"

"Maybe some of you rubbed off on me."

"That's not exactly how you say something as a compliment, you know!"

He did have a means of ranged attack, but for Regulus, that range still ended at the reach of his own shoulders. Keeping his rage-twisted face at a distance, Subaru narrowed his eyes at the rapidly approaching church.

"What are we going to do when we meet those women at the church?"

"I don't know if they're being threatened or if they're devoted to him, but..."

In response to Emilia's question, Subaru put a hand to his chin and paused. What rose in his mind were the women at the church, trembling before Regulus's conduct. He prayed with all his heart that the terror on their faces then hadn't been an act. And if it hadn't been—

"Then it'll mean tearing the heart out of them against their will. —In the literal sense."

Bathed in a rushing wind, the ice pillar lost altitude, and the church drew close. Regulus was behind them. Reinhard's safety was unknown. A faint chance of victory had come into view, and yet the desperate situation still went on—

Was everyone else doing all right? He had no room to spare for such thoughts, and yet he couldn't help thinking them.

The plaza before the control tower assigned to "Gluttony." The battle between the Julius-Ricardo team and Alphard.

"—El Krauzel."

Borrowing the power of six quasi-spirits of six colors, a rainbow-hued radiance burst from the tip of the knight's sword thrust forward. Unleashed as a preemptive strike, it was a blow delivered with lethal intent and no restraint whatsoever.

"Krauzel" was a magic technique of the same lineage as "Chrysta," which had damaged even Petelgeuse's true body—but unlike "Chrysta," which wreathed the sword itself in destructive brilliance, this was a ranged technique that discharged that destructive brilliance from the sword's tip.

The impression of Sin Archbishops etched in Julius's mind was, inevitably, dominated by Petelgeuse. The emotion Julius held toward that madman, the Sin Archbishop of Sloth, was that of a great criminal who had tormented the world for many years—but at the same time, and to a greater degree, it was the fighting spirit one holds against an existence of an utterly alien mode of thought: an unfathomable foreign enemy.

Julius Juukulius, celebrated as the "Most Superior Knight," was often misunderstood because of his manner and speech, but he carried within him a certain naivety—a belief in what might be called the fundamental goodness of human nature. He believed there was a reason behind any human's actions, and even when it came to the causes of wrongdoing, he was inclined to consider the influence of circumstances beyond the individual involved—a softness that, from a humanitarian standpoint, went too far. Even for Julius, a Sin Archbishop like Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti, or Witch Cultists like dolls devoid of self-awareness, were more than he could handle.

Enemies he could not understand—enemies for whom even abandoning the effort to understand was considered forgivable. To Julius, the Witch Cult was the worst kind of malice, one that shook even his own sense of what it meant to be a knight.

Hence the trump card. Hence the hidden hand. From the very first move, Julius hadn't hesitated to play his ace.

A superhuman technique that borrowed the strength of six quasi-spirits, wielding six colors of mana all at once. Not even the slightest disparity in mana output was permissible. It was made possible only through the bond between a Spiritual Arts User and his quasi-spirits, together with the talent and effort of a genius like Julius himself. Even Roswaal L Mathers, standing at the pinnacle of magecraft, likely couldn't pull off exactly this same feat with ease. It was an original technique that Julius alone had devised, and Julius alone could wield.

On first sight, none could grasp the terror of this spell—and they would vanish without ever grasping it. Julius gave priority to blasting away, root and all, the source of the unease still lodged in his chest, rather than to first confirming what that lump of misgiving even was.

The destructive brilliance tore up the cobblestones and painted over a small silhouette standing with both arms hanging limp. The long, dark brown hair, the grimy rags, the dull gleam of a dagger—all swallowed by the rainbow light—

"Big Brother, you know, you actually have this weak side where you turn your eyes from unpleasant things, don't you?"

"—Wha—!?"

Muttering that in a voice audible even from here, Alphard's body collapsed to the ground. In a stance so low he was nearly flat on his belly against the pavement, the blasphemer, tongue lolling out, kicked off the ground. The speed of the discharged brilliance, though it couldn't reach the speed of light itself, was by no means slow. Dodging a surprise attack approaching at arrow-like velocity would require physical ability rivaling Reinhard's—or else—

"You were our idol, Big Brother. A magic technique you desperately worked out, you who hates letting people see your effort—there's no way we wouldn't know about it!"

"What are you talking about?!"

"Not understanding—Big Brother Julius really is heartless. But that's exactly the part we love about you! Gyahahahaha!"

With movements that suggested he'd known the trump card was coming, Alphard bounded across the ground, evading the spell. It was a motion that had even anticipated Ricardo, who had already broken into a run to strike the fleeing Alphard the moment the mana was released.

The dagger flashed, striking sparks head-on against the machete Ricardo brought crashing down. In sheer physical strength, Ricardo overwhelmingly had the advantage. Yet Alphard deflected that difference in power with a blade technique of skill beyond imagining. The machete slid past the blasphemer's side and split the cobblestones. At the same moment, Alphard's slash cut across Ricardo's shaggy torso.

"There, a stab of dog meat! Tough, sinewy meat—stab it stab it stab it, tenderize it, make it easy to eat, easy to chew through, gentle on the stomach, easy to turn into waste, easy to turn into fertilizer, easy to turn into manure, and it becomes vegetables, and the vegetables get eaten by the meat, circulate circulate circulate circulate, the food chain! Ahh, magnificent!!"

"Ngh, gah, guh!?"

Rattling off nonsense at breakneck speed, Alphard swung the daggers bound to both arms at a speed that was anything but ordinary. A frame that hadn't even finished growing, a body without much apparent training—physical ability that far betrayed all appearances was slicing into Ricardo's flesh as he fell back on defense.

"Ricardo!!"

Wire-like fur and thick muscle. Ricardo's body, which compared to a human's was like wearing a layer of armor, was left helpless before Alphard's attacks, taking wound after wound. Julius, eyes wide, stared in shock at Ricardo's bleeding wounds.

Each of Alphard's blazing-fast strikes was precisely targeting the thin patches of fur and the joints. However tough Ricardo might be, if his vital points were struck, he would be wounded, he would bleed, and his life could indeed be lost.

"—"

The instant he saw his first strike miss, Julius broke off the barrage and called his quasi-spirits back. He ordered the two of them—Ia of Fire and Aro of Wind—to wreathe his sword in flame and wind for a searing slash. A crimson, fire-wrapped strike came in at Alphard from the side.

"Yep, we knew about that pattern too!"

"Wha—!?"

"That way of looking surprised has no style! It's not even enough to whet an appetite!"

But Alphard blocked this too, effortlessly, catching and deflecting it with one arm with an accuracy that made it seem he had eyes in the back of his head, and slammed a kick into Julius's now-open torso. His abdominal muscles driven in by the heel, Julius bent double with a groan of pain. In front of him, Ricardo's counterattack was parried, and a rising toe caught him under the jaw and sent his head snapping upward.

"Nice, nice, this is getting fun! Against that Big Brother! Against that Ricardo! The two of us putting up a great fight against both of them! A place we, weak in body as we are, should never be able to reach, never see, never know—we'd resigned ourselves to that completely, and yet! Ahh! Something this fun is unfair, unfair, unfair!"

Julius and Ricardo dropped to their knees at the same moment. As if mocking the two of them, Alphard eased off the pursuit and turned somersault after somersault across the cobblestones. Bounding across the paving stones with a bright, cheerful face, he looked every bit the child his appearance suggested. That is, if one set aside his unbelievable skill and his innocent cruelty.

"We already knew talk wouldn't get through to him. But man, that thing's just creepy. The way he talks, the way he phrases things, all of it together, it's just plain unsettling!"

Bearing no small number of wounds all over his body, Ricardo grumbled as he licked at a cut on his arm, breathing hard. Julius, standing back up as he steadied his breath, shared Ricardo's anger entirely.

"The same as at the city hall... no, his words and actions make even less sense than then. He may be trying to fluster us, but it's only having the opposite effect."

"But even so, you can't help but be curious, can you? Sentimental Mr. Ricardo, and Big Brother who's actually sentimental too but hides it? We know that peeerfectly well!"

"You..."

Clapping his hands, Alphard laughed shrilly. Julius sent Qua, the quasi-spirit of Water, to Ricardo's side to give him rough first aid while he himself stepped forward.

"Hey! C'mon, Julius! Don't go stealing a march on me!"

"You just stay put there and behave until the bleeding stops!"

Leveling his knight's sword ahead of him, Julius charged Alphard. But the speed of his advance was clearly different from before. At the sharp advance and slash, Alphard, taking the first strike, raised an eyebrow slightly.

"This is..."

"The power of In, the quasi-spirit of Sun. And at the same time—"

"Huh?"

Alphard's bewilderment mingled with the responding voice. Julius forced through the blade lock against the blasphemer, whose cheek stiffened, and his long leg snapped up, slamming into the side of Alphard's head. This time, the defense didn't come in time. The arm that had risen too late hung limp, and Alphard, seeing stars, rolled desperately away.

"Waugh! What was that just now, huh?"

"On myself, the quasi-spirit of Sun. On you, whose sword met mine, the quasi-spirit of Shadow. A combination that mutually raises my physical ability and lowers yours. This must be your first time seeing it, isn't it?"

"...Uhihi, as expected! Master Julius, wonderful! Charms neither we nor us know about yet, and there's still so many more of them, aren't there!"

"—!?"

Cheeks flushed red, Alphard gazed at Julius with a dazed, entranced look. The instant Julius grimaced at that heated gaze, Alphard removed the daggers bound to his arms and threw them down. A shrill sound rang out as they struck the cobblestones. An instant later, a heel shattered the pavement.

"Seems like a sword can't surprise you anymore, so this time we're gonna dazzle you with our fists!"

"Guh—!"

Closing the distance in the blink of an eye, Alphard twisted at the waist and unleashed a palm strike. Julius took it reflexively on his open left arm, but the shock ran straight through arm and chest alike. The push off the ground and the twist of the hips added destructive force beyond all common sense to the palm strike, and Julius's slender frame, no exaggeration, crumpled and was thrown into the air.

If Subaru had witnessed that scene, he surely would have thought of a car accident. Specifically, the violent sight of a runaway car with no brakes plowing into a defenseless pedestrian.

"Our fists, which have beaten eighty-eight handsome men to death, one after another... I wonder if it rang right down to the marrow of your bones, Big Brother?"

Julius had no room left to respond to Alphard's insane smile. His breastbone creaked, his organs were crushed, and coughing up a clot of blood, his tall body went flying. The one who caught him on reflex was Ricardo, still receiving treatment.

"Julius, hang in there!!"

Ricardo wrapped Julius up and shielded him fully, just as he was about to smash headfirst, defenseless, into a wall. The impact swallowed even the Dog Human's massive frame, and the two of them shattered stonework and were flung straight into the building.

Amid the billowing dust, Ricardo shook his head and rushed to Julius's side. He turned Julius's blood-spewing head to the side, letting the blood drain out fully so it wouldn't block his throat.

"Spirits! Dunno if you can hear me, but your master's in a real pinch! Give it your all! Don't worry about me, that can wait!"

Whether Ricardo's desperate call got through or not, the blue light poured its power into Julius's body. The look of death faded from Julius as he coughed painfully, and Ricardo, granting himself only a moment of relief, shouldered his great machete and rushed back outside in a panic.

"Weeelcome back! Will it be dinner? A feast? Or, per-haps, a ban-quet?"

"Mocking me rotten, you little brat... I'll teach you what happens when you make fools of grown-ups, same as I do with my own little ones—spank you till you cry."

"No thanks, no thanks, let's not. We don't have a taste for dog faces, thank you. If sword and fist haven't been enough fun for you... how about something like this?"

As Alphard, wearing a faint smile, spread both hands wide, Ricardo's fur instantly stood on end. Bulging his eyes to see what was happening, Ricardo ground his teeth in irritation.

—Behind Alphard, the canal's current spiraled and rose, looming over Ricardo like the neck of a Water Dragon.

"Swordplay, martial arts, and now magic too. What the hell even are you?"

"We're just a lowly, no-name mage, a nobody our own family couldn't be proud of. Just kidding!"

The instant Alphard stuck out his tongue, the head of water came crashing down toward Ricardo. Mere water it might be, but its force and mass could crush a living body with ease. With Julius behind him, evasion wasn't an option.

"Bring it on. Wa—haaa—!!"

Slamming his great machete into the ground to brace himself, Ricardo opened his mouth wide and unleashed a roaring shockwave. It was the original version of the roaring shockwave that two of the three sibling deputy captains of the Iron Fang produced together. According to Ricardo, the version Mimi and her siblings used was originally copied from the one he himself had devised, and he insisted the true originator was him. However, compared to Mimi and her siblings' version, which spread the load by splitting the point of discharge, a roaring shockwave produced by a single person placed a much greater burden on the body.

Feeling his own body creak as he clung to the great machete, Ricardo let a destructive roar burst from his throat to meet the muddy torrent of water crashing down.

"Wooow, amazing!"

Without even hearing that admiring exclamation, Ricardo's roaring shockwave collided head-on with the torrent. The shockwave struck the spray of water head-on, and several tons' worth of weight scattered into mist and was made to evaporate. Seconds later, the remainder of the torrent, having pushed through, pattered down over the plaza like rain, and Ricardo, atop the flooded cobblestones, collapsed, letting his weight fall onto the machete.

"Man, been a while... and it's rough... corner of my mouth's split open."

The strain of both the long time since he'd last done it and the prolonged effort left damage on Ricardo himself, the one who'd unleashed the shockwave. Breathing hard through heaving shoulders, Ricardo nonetheless summoned his grit and stood back up. Alphard, unharmed, was still dancing in place, showing no sign of fatigue.

"Amazing, amazing! It's been a while since we got blocked. So long we and us can barely even remember it. Nice, sure, great, of course, maybe, why not, right, isn't it, of course it is, and precisely because it is—!"

"—That's enough empty talk."

"Oop, Big Brother's back. Scaaary, cuuute, so envioouus."

As Alphard shook his head, Julius came to stand beside Ricardo, facing him. His face was pale, his knight's attire soaked in blood. There was even a faint tremor in his breath—hardly a state one could call fully recovered. Hardly, and yet, even so—

"Sorry to trouble you, Ricardo."

"You sure did. You'd better give a proper report to the young lady later about how hard I worked, and get me a bonus for it, or it just won't add up."

"On that point, allow me to put in a good word myself as well."

Regripping his knight's sword, Julius clapped Ricardo's shoulder and then glared at Alphard. Under that gaze, the blasphemer smiled, flushed, and twisted his lips in irritation, all at once. His expressions, his words, even his fighting style—all had the uncanny quality of a mismatched patchwork. Perhaps that very quality had something to do with the Authority of "Gluttony."

"Having mastered the sword, the martial arts, even magic to that degree—why did you fall to evil? Surely you could have found some other use for that power, somewhere else entirely."

"Somewhere else, hm. For instance, what would you nominate as a candidate, Big Brother?"

Even the mere manner of being called "Big Brother" carried a taint of revulsion. Every time Alphard said it, with a leering gaze, a fawning tone, a clinging manner, the value of the word itself was defiled somewhere inside Julius. That, too, was a strange sensation, if a sensation was what it was. —After all, he shouldn't have had any family who called him that.

"For instance, a knight. For instance, a mercenary. For instance, a hero. Power without conviction falls easily into evil, and strong ability transforms readily into violence. So..."

"Knew you'd say that! Just knew you'd say that, Big Brother! The Big Brother we know, the Big Brother we believe in, we knew you'd say exactly that. We knew it!"

Abruptly cutting the conversation short, Alphard leapt, closing in on Julius. Julius held his knight's sword upright, batting down the diving kick. Whether there was an iron plate fitted into the sole of Alphard's shoe or not, a hard sensation absorbed the blade, and the slash failed to cut through. Spinning round and round on the spot as if dancing, Alphard whipped his small frame around sharply, unleashing kick after kick, and step by visible step pushed Julius's footing backward. Under that ferocious onslaught, even Ricardo lost his chance to cut in.

"Do you remember, from when we were children! When we, always sickly, took a turn for the worse, and begged Big Brother for the appa growing on the garden tree!"

"Say what you like...! There's no way I could remember. Stop trying to force your self-serving delusions onto someone else!"

"We and Big Brother were both still small, and Big Brother said at first it was impossible, said to give up on it! Remember? Or maybe you don't, huh? But the more Big Brother refused, the more we wanted that appa! If we could do the thing Big Brother said was impossible, that'd make us amazing! We'd be able to have confidence! That's why!"

"What—what are you talking about?! I don't know anything about that... I don't know!"

Toe kicks, heel strikes, roundhouses, front kicks, low sweeping kicks, crescent kicks, somersault kicks, back spin kicks—Julius took them all, his knight's sword groaning with the strain. His arms went numb, his still-wounded organs ached, and he tasted blood in his mouth. No—that taste of blood wasn't from coughing up blood. He was biting his own lip. He himself was, somehow, right now. Unable, no matter what, to turn his ears away from Alphard's raving.

"Because of what happened after that, we—! Us—! Big Brother—!"

"—!"

"We thought it all along! We felt it all along! That we were different! That we were a burden! And now look! Look at us now! This feels good! So this is what it feels like! Ahh, this must have felt wonderful for you! Finally, we understand!"

"I don't understand anything about you, nothing at all!!"

Pushed past his limit by everything being forced on him, it was Julius who finally snapped. Putting his full strength into his knight's sword, he shoved back, and in the instant Alphard's stance broke, he drove in an attack. A downward cut, a thrust, a kick, a slam, a sweep, a swing.

A slash steeped in fury and hostility cut through the air, and several locks of Alphard's long hair, his read of it coming a beat too late, were severed and fell to the ground. And beyond that, the slash wasn't the only thing demanding attention.

"Ia! Qua! Aro! Iku! Mea! In!"

Woven together like an incantation were the names of the quasi-spirits bound to the Spirit Knight. The six quasi-spirits, called by name, blazed brighter, converting the affirmation of their own existence into power and pouring ruin down upon the knight's enemy.

—From six directions, six colors of light closed in on Alphard, leaving no escape.

"This ends it—!"

Certain of victory, Julius drove a thrust at Alphard, who had no way left to escape. It plunged straight for the very center of Alphard's chest—

"Ultimate Palm."

Black palms pressed together before his chest caught the knight's sword's blade between them and shattered it to pieces. The fragments scattered, and the lethal thrust lost all its effect. And yet, the spells still closing in from six blind directions—

"Twilight Mage."

Mana surged and swelled behind Alphard, shooting down every single one of the six approaching forces of magic. Spell of the same color struck spell of the same color head-on, and every last one was cancelled out. And on top of that, at the very end, Julius's eyes went wide, every means of attack now sealed off.

"Serpent of the Twin Swords."

What Alphard's toe flicked up were the very daggers he was supposed to have thrown away. With the pretense of retreating under Julius's attack, he had, in fact, expertly maneuvered him right to where the daggers lay. Catching the spinning blades in both hands, Alphard's body spun. A storm of slashes raged, and Julius instinctively raised the sword whose blade had already been shattered.

"——"

"Big Brother got us that appa after all. That's why we hated him so much."

With a keening sound, an arm severed at the elbow spun through the air and fell, striking the cobblestones with a thud.
