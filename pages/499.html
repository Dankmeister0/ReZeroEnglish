Arc 7 Chapter 2: "Animal Tracking"

"—Listen up, Subaru. From now on, you need to understand that you're always in a state of half-dying."

"C'mon, that's a pretty unfunny joke coming from you, little miss princess."

"I'm not little, and it's not a joke, I suppose! Listen seriously, would you!"

Saying so with her face reddening, Beatrice made Subaru nod and say, "Okay, okay, I got it."

Sitting properly on the beautiful carpet with her short arms crossed, he was right in the middle of receiving a lecture from Professor Beatrice, but it wasn't progressing very well at all.
Mostly because Subaru kept interjecting comments in between—

"I mean, it's all Bea-ko's fault for being so cute, isn't it..."

"Just when I thought you were being serious, what are you saying now! ...Well, it's common knowledge that Betty is cute, so I suppose that can't be helped..."

"You, your tsundere index skyrockets the moment you clear the event flag..."

"That is the height of pulling the ladder out from under someone, I suppose!"

Beatrice stamped her feet, openly furious. It looked the same as always on the surface, but Subaru's feelings toward her were vastly different now.
Before, they'd clashed often, and she'd given the impression of a neighborhood kitten that just wouldn't warm up to him. But now, to Subaru, Beatrice was—

"Like an irreplaceable pet cat I want to spend my days burying my face in the belly of."

"Listen! Seriously! I said!"

"Zen-kokyuu!"

Subaru, murmuring wistfully, was blown away by the shockwave from Beatrice's raised hand. As he slid down the wall and crumpled to the floor, she gave an exasperated shrug.

"Don't make me say it over and over, I suppose. This is important. If you miss it, it's truly, truly a dangerous matter, I suppose. —The matter of your Gate."

"...The Gate, huh."

Subaru slowly pushed himself up and sat cross-legged on the spot.
He gently touched his own chest, thinking about that organ—unseeable, untouchable—inside it.

The Gate was an important organ said to exist inside living things, necessary for using Magic in this world so different from Subaru's common sense. The quality of one's Gate apparently affected one's aptitude as a mage, and in Subaru's case, he'd had a Gate that wasn't particularly excellent.
Past tense, because that Gate had already been lost.

"Strictly speaking, you didn't lose it—you broke it, I suppose."

"Because I overused it after being told not to. Man, next time I see Ferris, it's gonna be way worse than just a scolding. I wasted all that hard-earned treatment."

"That's an awfully rosy kind of worry, I suppose. ...There's no guarantee there'll be a next time, I suppose."

It amounted to completely ignoring the warnings of Ferris, who had been in charge of repairing Subaru's overworked Gate. Subaru braced himself, imagining being scolded like a demon with that cute face and voice, but Beatrice's gaze was far more grave than that.

"As a result of your Gate breaking, you can no longer properly expel Mana, I suppose. And yet, as long as you're alive, Mana is generated—or absorbed—and accumulates inside your body. But since you have no way to release it..."

"Don't tell me you're gonna say I'll swell up and explode or something."

"——"

"H-hey, Beatrice?! It's scary when you go quiet on me!"

Subaru's voice rose at her silence, her expression turning awkward as she looked away. Seeing him finally take it seriously, Beatrice raised a finger and said, "Listen well, I suppose,"

"No joke, that understanding is correct, I suppose. If this continues, the gradually accumulating Mana will cause your health to deteriorate, and eventually you'll go pop from the corrupted Mana, I suppose."

"That's terrifying! Isn't there some way to deal with it?!"

"That's where Betty comes in, I suppose. Fortunately, Betty formed a Contract with you—with Subaru—and connected our existences through our Souls, I suppose. As I've told you, Betty is a little different from other Spirits in that she needs to receive Mana from others. In other words..."

"—You mean you can do something about the Mana piling up inside me!"

Hearing Beatrice's come-from-behind-home-run idea, Subaru sprang to his feet and scooped up her small body all at once.
Holding the shrieking "Nkyaa!?" Beatrice aloft, Subaru spun around and around on the spot,

"Amazing, Bea-ko! Man, I really can't hold my head up around your very existence! With this, in name and in fact, you and I are in it together for life!"

"T-that's not all it means, I suppose! From now on, every time you put Betty in a bad mood, remember that your own life will be in jeopardy, I suppose. If you don't want to die, you'd better devote your entire body and soul to keeping Betty happy, I suppose."

"Heh, so basically, keep doting on you with everything I've got. Well, that just depends on how much you keep shining, doesn't it. Heavy responsibility, Bea-ko."

"Why does Betty have to be the one told that! I don't accept it, I suppose!"

And so, held aloft, red-faced Beatrice shouted, while Subaru listened, still spinning her round and round with him.
Round and round, round and round.

Round and round, round and round, round and round——,

Yes—the spinning sensation in his vision cut off abruptly, and consciousness surfaced.
The drowning-like agony from just before vanished suddenly, replaced by the pain of his breathing rhythm having broken and the vast solidity of earth against his back.

"—Ko, ah, cough"

Rubbing his throat, he pushed his body up and recalled what had just happened to him.
At the same time, looking at the center of his chest, the arrow that should have been lodged there was nowhere to be found. Along with that, the scrape-like marks from being dragged through the trees had also vanished.
Of course they had. He'd had his chest pierced by that tremendous blow.

"I... died... didn't I..."

A chill ran through him, a sensation of uncertain ground crumbling beneath his feet, and Subaru's blood ran cold.
In terms of events, it had been barely a dozen or so minutes since he'd been swallowed by the black shadow at the Pleiades Watchtower and blown away from it—that alone had been enough to cost him his life.
Understanding once again just how precarious his position was, he stood up. Steadying his unsteady body as best he could, Subaru looked around.
And then—

"Damn it, this is the worst..."

Confirming that he was in the middle of a vast, open green grassland, and that the figure he'd hoped to see was nowhere in sight, he cursed his own bad luck.
This place was, without a doubt, the grassland Subaru and the others had been blown to from the tower.
The problem was that Rem and Rui, who'd been blown here together with him, were nowhere to be found. In other words, the point in time Subaru had "Returned by Death" to was—

"After Rem strangled me and knocked me out...!"

The restart point was right after being choked unconscious by the Rem carrying him on her back.
The fact that Rem's awakening hadn't been undone, and that the final loop at the Pleiades Watchtower hadn't been undone either—that itself, he wanted to call good news.
Or rather, if he'd been able to return to the tower's final loop, there was a faint hope he might exchange words again with the girl who had vanished—that hope hadn't been entirely absent, but—

"Am I an idiot. No, I am an idiot."

If he was going to harbor such lingering regrets, he should have made more time while there was still time to talk, room to reach out, leeway to hear her feelings out.
Natsuki Subaru, who hadn't done that, had no right to lament like this.
Right now, in any case—

"—I'll find Rem."

He had to chase after the fled Rem and clear up her misunderstanding.
If the reason Rem had run was the Witch's Lingering Scent clinging to Subaru, then now, having "Returned by Death," that scent was surely even stronger.
The chances of her refusing to hear him out were even higher than before. But Natsuki Subaru's one year of history wasn't light enough to let him give up on her just because of that.

"Besides, someone killed me. ...There's no way that arrow was Rem's attack."

Worst case, there was a possibility Rem might attack Subaru, but he couldn't imagine her gathering the tools for it in such a short time.
Especially a bow and arrow—not something easily or quickly prepared.

"The arrow that hit me had a head and fletching too... maybe because it went straight through my heart, I didn't feel much pain..."

If attacked by that powerful bow, even Rem wouldn't stand a chance.
With her legs not free to move, escaping wouldn't be easy for her either. No matter what, he had to save her before she fell prey to that arrow's poison fangs.
Even if the memoryless Rem didn't think of Subaru as an ally.

"Here we go, Natsuki Subaru. —Show me how great you are."

He slapped his own cheeks, temporarily setting aside the shock of his recent "death" and the sorrow of being hated by a girl precious to him. Whether he could reunite with her even after finding her was unknown, but first, he had to find her.
Grieving and raging could both wait—everything depended on being alive.

"——"

Letting out a deep breath, Subaru once again followed the trail of trampled grass marking Rem's path, plunging into the forest.
Only, he greatly hesitated over whether he should call out loudly while searching for her.

That baptism of arrow-fire earlier had likely happened because the enemy had spotted an unguarded Subaru.
The other party's identity and purpose were unknown, but given that they'd come to kill with a single blow, it was hard to think of them as friendly. If found, it meant "death"—he had to regard them as that kind of enemy.

"Still, using a tool like a bow and arrow means it's not a Witchbeast or something—it's a human opponent."

If the other side was human too, there was a chance things could be resolved through negotiation rather than a fight to the death. Still, whether the other party would even come to that negotiating table was unknown.
Roughly speaking, among all of Subaru's "Return by Death" experiences so far, the ratio of causes of death between humans and Witchbeasts was about even, maybe slightly leaning toward humans.
Just because the other party was human and could understand words didn't mean he could assume a friendly relationship could easily be formed.
More hands would raise the odds of finding Rem, he thought, but—

"—Cor Leonis."

Closing his eyes, Subaru banished distracting thoughts and activated his own inner Authority.
The new power that had run rampant inside the Pleiades Watchtower, "Cor Leonis," was a detection technique that pinpointed the location of allies supporting Subaru's "Small King" nearby.
He clung to the hope that he might use this to pinpoint Rem's location.
However—

"...No good, no response. Did she get really far away, or is it because Rem doesn't think of me as an ally?"

With "Cor Leonis," whose exact range was unknown, no faint light of a presumed ally appeared within its effective range.
The reasons he couldn't locate the other party's position—the possibilities he could think of were distance and relationship.

Emilia and the others, who had once been subject to the effect, he couldn't sense at all now.
Even for familiar people, he hadn't been able to locate Reid or "Gluttony" inside the tower.

The activation of "Cor Leonis" was certainly confirmed.
To put it plainly, the ally radar's power was on, but there was no response. The reason for that: Emilia and the others were outside the effective range, and Rem, who should be within the effective range, didn't count Subaru as an ally.

—Probably, the fact that he couldn't locate Rui's position either, moving together with Rem, was proof of that.

"There's no way I'd ever, in a million years, treat that girl as an ally. That's why I'm not showing up on the radar of a guy who's got a hopeless crush on Rem."

At this point, he regretted the poor communication he'd had with Rem.
As long as Subaru wore the Witch's Lingering Scent, he had no idea what words he could have chosen to win Rem's trust. Still, even if it was deceitful, he hadn't been able to say things like "I'll protect Rui" or "let's bring her along" back then.

"Damn it, damn it all... why, why is this happening...! After Rem finally, finally woke up, why do I have to..."

...go through this game of tag with her.
He'd longed for the day she'd stand up and move about freely on her own two legs, and now that she actually had, Subaru couldn't help but curse it.
Thinking about who he should hate for this state of affairs, only anger toward the Sin Archbishops of "Gluttony," including Rui, welled up.

"——"

Carrying his circling thoughts with him, Subaru cautiously advanced through the forest.
Keeping low as he went was a desperate measure to avoid re-encountering the one who'd once killed him—for convenience's sake, he'd call them the "Hunter."
He thought he ought to do more—smear mud on his face, camouflage his clothes with leaves, apply some kind of camouflage effect—but unfortunately, he didn't have time to spend on that.
If only he knew precisely how much time had passed since he got separated from Rem, his search would go more smoothly.

"Think, think... using my sly little head is exactly what this moment calls for. Rem's forgotten everything, doesn't remember anything. But she still had the ability to pin me down and sense the Witch's Lingering Scent. Which means it's a loss of episodic memory."

"Amnesia," often featured in manga and games, in most cases corresponds to what's called a loss of episodic memory.
That was the same state Subaru himself had been in inside the tower, after losing his memory once from his encounter with "Gluttony"—no, that Subaru had only lost his memories from after coming to this other world, so strictly speaking it wasn't exactly the same state, but it was close.

In other words, a state where you remember the names of objects and body-ingrained reflexive actions, but have lost your own and others' names, and the contents that would count as memories.
The fact that Rem referred to herself as "I" and instinctively grew wary of Subaru because of his scent was proof of that.

"Rem must be confused too. She can't keep running forever. If I put some distance between us, she'll take time to calm down and reflect on herself. Especially if that Rui girl is with her."

Praying for something like this was ridiculous, but he'd rather hope that Rui was grandly dragging her feet and slowing Rem's escape.
If she whined, sometimes refused to walk, and generally gave Rem trouble, that would give Subaru a chance to catch up to her.
Or, it would be fine if Rem gave up on Rui.
If Rui proved too unmanageable, too much trouble to look after, then that too—

"...I don't know."

He didn't know whether Rem would abandon a girl who at least looked like a helpless small child.
Rem, having lost her episodic memory and in a sense being nobody, could be said to have lost the inferiority complex born from her lifelong relationship with Ram, and the establishment of her own self.
Ram's stability had been abnormal—even with Rem's very existence missing, nothing about her had changed—but would the same thing happen for Rem?

A Rem who wasn't Ram's sister, who had nothing to do with pride or inferiority as a member of the Oni Tribe, who felt nothing at all about Natsuki Subaru—that Rem—

"—Ngh"

The moment he imagined it, feeling as though his chest were burning, Subaru stamped his foot down hard.
A branch he'd trodden on out of spite let out a crack and snapped, and nearly slipping on the muddy ground, Subaru leaned forward and swerved around the tall grass in front of him,

"—Ah?"

The forest suddenly opened up, and once again Subaru found himself stepping out into grassland.
For a moment the blood drained from his face at the thought that he'd been going in circles through the forest and come out at the same spot, but looking closely around, he realized that wasn't the case.

It resembled the first grassland, but the height of the grass underfoot was different.
Compared to the first grassland, this one had slightly taller grass. And while that one had been surrounded by forest a full three hundred sixty degrees, here there was only the forest Subaru had burst out of.
Directly ahead of Subaru as he emerged from the forest, visible through the break in the grassland, was a great horizon. The sky looked strangely distant and high, and a strange illusion of being sucked upward made his footing feel uncertain.

But it wasn't only that high, distant sky that caught Subaru's eye.
Closer than that, there was a small clearing carved out of the grassland, and camping equipment placed there—in short, traces that someone had been here.

"——"

In an instant, wariness surged through Subaru's entire body, and his field of vision narrowed sharply.
Fortunately, there was no human figure in what could be called a simple campsite. There were only traces that camping had been done there, and it seemed unlikely to be a trap.
The question was who had made this campsite.

"The most likely possibility is the guy who killed me... right."

There was a high possibility that the Hunter who'd taken Subaru down had set this campsite as a base camp for hunting in the forest.
If so, Subaru should leave here immediately and secure his safety.
Whether encountered in the forest or in the field, there was no doubt the Hunter was a dangerous individual.

There were stories of mountain hunters mistaking deer for people and shooting them by accident, but mistaking someone calling out loudly while searching for an acquaintance for a deer was inconceivable, given that marksman's skill.
He should conclude the Hunter was a dangerous enemy and act accordingly.
However—

"...If I could just swipe a knife or something."

The density of trees here, dense enough to be called a jungle—he wanted some tool to help him push through it. Unfortunately, Subaru's only possession was his beloved whip—the Guiltywhip, made from a formidable foe's materials.
His outfit, suited for tackling the sand sea, was subtly useful for tackling the jungle too, but still, having even one blade to cut through the trees and advance would make a dramatic difference.
So, if he could get his hands on even one likely-looking tool from the campsite, things would go a lot differently.

Thinking so, Subaru finished a brief deliberation and stepped toward the campsite.
There was also the option of turning right back and returning to the forest, but given his goal, he wanted to avoid going back empty-handed.
Paying attention to his footsteps and his surroundings, Subaru moved into the campsite.

"Traces of a fire, and though it's crude... is this a bed?"

At the center of the campsite were the remains of a campfire, and beside it, what looked like a bed made of arranged cut grass. Without a doubt, someone had been staying here.
Above that was empty too, and there seemed to be nothing else, but—

"If I could at least find a knife or something like one—"

"—Ho, so you want a blade? You've turned up at a remarkably convenient moment."

Just as he finished a quick inspection of the campsite and was about to look for any usable tools, Subaru froze, feeling a voice from behind him and something cold pressed against his neck.
Holding his breath, slowly lowering his gaze, he saw that what was pressed against the right side of his neck was the blade of a sword, its blade polished to a beautiful sheen.

"——"

Swallowing hard, Subaru understood that he'd handed the other party the right of life and death over him.
But at the same time, his mind was in chaos over having been addressed from behind. —He'd been on guard. It was, after all, a life-or-death situation, so of course he'd been wary without any ulterior calculation.
Of course, he knew that among the superhumans living in this other world, there were those who moved at speeds his eyes couldn't follow, and even ones capable of instantaneous movement.
But—

"To draw that rare exception here of all places? ...Just how unlucky am I?"

"Fool. Who said you could speak. Choose your every word and deed with care. Do not forget that your life is in this hand—in mine."

To Subaru cursing his own bad luck, the voice from behind showed no mercy.
As the man said, if Subaru made any strange move, his head would surely fly off immediately. Feeling the certain pressure that this would absolutely happen, Subaru desperately racked his brain for a way to turn things around.

Judging by the voice, the other party was a man.
It had a fairly young ring to it, so probably around Subaru's age or a bit older. His word choice had a certain quirk to it, but strangely, it didn't feel out of place.
And above all—

"You keep your mouth shut while turning over this and that in your mind, it seems. But you make no attempt at a counterattack even at the cost of your life. ...Hmm."

There was insight there, reading Subaru's inner state from his silent demeanor.
The other party, letting out a thoughtful breath, seemed to mull something over regarding Subaru's back, and,

"That's not a style seen around here. Nor does it seem suited to Badheim's climate. Pale limbs too... not a local."

"I'm... whoa!"

"Silence. Who said you could open your mouth. If you displease me again, let's see if you can still talk after your head's parted from your body."

He was made to understand, with a shallow cut to the side of his neck, that the man had no intention of engaging in conversation.
A stinging pain and a trickle of blood ran down his neck, but still the man's scrutiny of Subaru showed no sign of ending.

"That whip at your waist, too, is hopelessly impractical for use in a forest. Your arms and legs are trained to some degree, but not enough to stand among the ranks of wolves. ...You don't seem to have been chasing me."

"——"

"Why the silence. If you won't explain yourself, shall I have you die here?"

"Eh?! Now that's fine to say?! This makes no sense!"

At the unreasonableness of it, Subaru protested, and the sharp gaze stabbing into the back of his head grew even keener.
Subaru stiffened, realizing he'd said something unnecessary, but that stiffness finally eased as the sword pressed to his neck was drawn back.
However—

"—Turn around slowly. If you try anything strange—"

"You'll take my head off?"

"No. I'll sever your limbs, gouge out your heart, and burn it to ashes before your very eyes."

"That's way too vicious a threat!"

Against the threat, from which only sheer menace came through, Subaru raised both hands, proving he had no intention of fighting back, and slowly turned around.
And, looking squarely at the one who'd taken him from behind—,

"...Seriously?"

—he found himself face to face with a man whose face was hidden by wound rags.

△▼△▼△▼△

It was, in truth, an extremely strange-looking man.
His height was a bit taller than Subaru's, and his build looked vaguely slender. Standing with long, slender limbs, he held in his hand a sword with a thin blade like a saber or rapier—the very one that had shallowly slit Subaru's neck.
His clothing was of a fine, aristocratic style—arguably even more out of place here than Subaru's own outfit. Looking closely, the rags wrapped around his face had probably originally been a cloak.
Whether he'd suffered an injury to his face, or simply didn't want to show it, that much was unclear, but—

"What is it? That vacant look on your face."

"I might have a vacant look, but my face is my face since birth, so that's kind of an insult... 'what is it' nothing, seeing your getup, of course I'd end up like this."

"Says the one doing as he pleases. I'm reconsidering whether you're an assassin after all, looking at your face again."

"A job doesn't get decided by the look in someone's eyes! Besides, my role is exactly the opposite of an assassin. I'm not the attacking type, I'm more the protecting type."

The man—no, the masked man—kept his sword trained on Subaru without loosening his guard.
Responding to that suspicious voice, Subaru frowned as he took in the man's equipment and the baggage placed behind him.

Besides the outfit unsuited for camping or forest activity, what caught his eye was the pack placed behind the masked man—it looked exactly as though it had suddenly appeared there, just like the man himself had suddenly appeared behind Subaru.
And in addition to that, the masked man's equipment—there was no bow and arrow.

"...Seems like you're not the Hunter, huh."

"Hunter?"

"Never mind. By the way, just to ask... could it be that you can teleport, or maybe turn invisible?"

"——"

At that question, the black eyes visible through the mask's opening narrowed.
But that reaction wasn't one of anger or dismissal—it was one of interest in Subaru.
In fact, the masked man let out a small "Ho" of breath, and,

"What led you to such a notion? Explain your reasoning."

"...For what it's worth, I was on guard before I came near here. Of course, I get that there are plenty of guys out there who could blow right past my vigilance, but you're not one of them."

"Why?"

"Don't get mad hearing this, but I happen to run into a lot of really skilled types—so-called superhumans. Compared to those out-of-common-sense guys, the vibe I get off you is... well, normal."

If the other party had been a proper warrior, that remark would surely have earned his wrath.
But facing the man, that was exactly what Subaru felt. The air coming off the man in front of him was, sure, that of someone who could handle a sword, but it was of an ordinary sort, one who'd undergone a fair amount of training.
Looking at him through eyes that knew Reinhard, Garfiel, Wilhelm, and Julius, Subaru could only say he came up lacking.

"So that rules out the theory that you came flying in from beyond my perception. That leaves either teleporting behind me, or..."

"—Or using 'Cloaking' to become unseen, is that it?"

"—?!"

Right after that, the man who should have been standing before him vanished from Subaru's sight, and he gasped in shock.
But the surprise didn't end there.

"He's gone... but he's right in front of me?"

"—Correct. 'Cloaking' cannot erase one's presence."

In response to Subaru's question, the moment the man answered it, the masked man returned to the exact same spot, in the exact same situation.
So it truly was that he hadn't vanished, merely become unseen. And the fact that he'd returned the instant he answered Subaru's question meant—

"It gets cancelled if you touch someone, or if they become aware of you?"

"It's a fine tool for holding one's breath and lying in wait, though."

Saying so, the masked man lowered the sword pointed at Subaru and turned it toward the edge of the campsite. Toward where the pack sat.

"That bed is a decoy. I was holding my breath a little way off from it, over there. I saw you sneaking up the whole time. It was rather comical."

"A guy on guard looks super stupid from the outside, huh... but never mind that! You lowering your sword means..."

"You're not a pursuer. I have no idea what your reason or intent is, but you're plainly a lost traveler. In which case I have no cause to loudly condemn you for it. Nor any need to drive the point home with a blade."

As if to show he had no intention of fighting, the masked man sheathed the sword he'd drawn at his waist. Seeing that, Subaru finally felt the tension drain from his body.
And the instant it did, he remembered his original purpose.

"Oh, right, this isn't the time to be relaxed. Hey, sorry for all the questions, but—have you seen a girl with blue hair? I got separated from her around here."

"Blue hair? No, I haven't. If anything, the first thing I laid eyes on when I set foot in this place was your mug. What are you going to do about that?"

"I'm not gonna do anything about it?! I'm not, but... damn, a dead end here too. Hey, this is a long shot, but... would you maybe help me search for her?"

"——"

"Yeah, figured..."

The eyewitness lead was a dead end, and even his last hope was cut down flat in front of him.
Taking the masked man's cold, could-cut-you-just-by-looking gaze as his answer, Subaru turned to head back to the forest to make up for lost time, when—

"Wait. If you got separated in this forest, reuniting won't come easily. I'd think prioritizing your own survival is the wiser course, no?"

"—. Sorry, but that's not gonna happen. That girl means more to me than my own life. No matter what, I'm going to reunite with her. No, I have to bring her back."

"More than your life, is it. If it weren't heard from your own mouth but sung by some minstrel, I'd take it for empty words. And yet, what's interesting is your eyes."

At the man's tone mocking his recklessness, Subaru's eyes sharpened, and the man pointed directly at those very eyes. Subaru flinched back, thinking for a second he was about to have his eyes jabbed, and the masked man gave a small laugh,

"Those aren't the eyes of one speaking falsehood. Whether it'd hold if actually weighed against your own life on The Balance, I can't say, but at the very least, it seems you're not spouting deceit here."

"So what... so what does that mean, huh? If what I'm saying is true, then what?"

"—Then it piques my interest, somewhat. I'll lend you my wisdom."

Tap, tap, the masked man tapped his own temple with a finger.
Hearing that answer, Subaru considered shouting "quit messing around" at him, but no such curse left his throat.
Strangely—and this itself was a strange thing—no feeling of suspicion toward the man arose in him. No, there was something shady about him. But there was a persuasiveness that outweighed it.
That was probably the man's innate, natural charisma.

"Tell me the particulars of what happened. I'll find you a way to search."

"...Me and that girl got suddenly sent flying here."

Before he knew it, Subaru had begun answering the man's questions, bit by bit.
It wasn't that he trusted the masked man, or had come to believe in him. —It was simply that he was grasping at straws. In a state of mind like that, even if the other party was less trustworthy than straw, you still wanted to cling to them.
Probably, that was all there was to it.

△▼△▼△▼△

"You made a mistake."

That was the first, blunt remark from the masked man once he'd finished hearing the circumstances.
Subaru, prompted by the masked man's questions, had carefully explained everything that had happened between him and Rem—omitting the complicated surrounding circumstances.
That Rem's memory was in disarray, that she'd knocked Subaru out and fled, and that a dangerous little girl was traveling with her too, all included.

"I know. That I was a huge idiot. But don't tell me that's all this is gonna end with. Digging up my recent black history and mocking me and calling it done..."

"Fool. As if I'd waste my precious time deriding a clown like you. —That girl, her head turns fairly well, doesn't it?"

"Ah, yeah, probably."

Pressed by the masked man's presence, Subaru nodded honestly.
Rem, who was active in all sorts of everyday scenes as an all-purpose maid, didn't earn that kind of evaluation just from being good at housework.
Placing and moving the right personnel with an eye to their capabilities. —Even in combat, she'd read Subaru's breathing and matched him without a word being said.
Rem was clever. Even with her memory lost—.

"If so, there's a high chance you were outmaneuvered."

"Huh, outmaneuvered? You're saying I got tricked? What do you mean by that..."

"At this point, whether memory is present or not is beside the point. What matters is that the girl is aware she's being pursued, and has the ability to think through countermeasures against her pursuer. For instance—"

He cut off there, and the masked man's black eyes looked Subaru up and down.
Under that gaze, feeling as though his own thoughtlessness were being reprimanded, Subaru shrank his shoulders slightly.
Watching Subaru like that, the masked man narrowed his eyes,

"For instance, leaving traces in the grassland herself, disguising the direction of her flight."

"——"

"Knocked unconscious by her opponent, she must have panicked considerably upon waking. When the moment came that she needed to search for that opponent at once, what would she do if there were conspicuously obvious footprints?"

Drooling, chasing after the trail like a mutt, he'd gone running off.
That was exactly what had actually happened, and it was the answer behind the mocking look in the masked man's eyes. Of course, he could argue back that it wasn't true, but—

"—. Sure enough, the trail vanished partway into the forest from the entrance. But I just figured it was because the ground was bad there..."

"You don't look away from facts inconvenient to yourself, do you."

"I've known I'm a stupid dunce for a long time now. But adaptability and guts—those are the two things I've got going for me, at least that's the shared opinion between me and my other self."

Even though it wouldn't get through to him, Subaru answered the masked man like that.
In truth, the masked man's deduction was reasonable. Thinking back on it, the traces left on the grass had been far too "convenient," too on the nose.

Wild animals like foxes and rabbits have techniques of deliberately leaving footprints and then leaping into the underbrush partway, to throw off predators.
If the traces Rem had left on the grass were the same kind of thing—a cunning trap to deceive Subaru—

"Sending me off in a different direction, while going the opposite way herself...?"

"In that case, more often than not, the direction the other party chooses is the exact opposite. It's psychologically rational too, to choose the path that puts the most distance between you. —Understand?"

"...Frustrating, but I get it. Damn it! That Rem!"

As the masked man said, if she'd deliberately left an obvious trail as a decoy, there was a high chance she'd fled in the opposite direction.
But once he had that guess, there was also the prospect that heading to the opposite forest instead might actually let him catch her. It made him feel like the villain somehow, but—

"I'm used to first impressions being bad because of my face and my body odor. I will catch up with her, no matter what!"

"Spirited, aren't you. —Here, take this."

"Ugh?!"

To Subaru, who'd stood up and was about to dash off with no time to lose, the masked man rummaged in his pack and tossed something at him.
Catching it reflexively, he saw it was a small knife fitted in a leather sheath.
Eyes widening in surprise at this, the masked man shrugged and said,

"This isn't a forest one can face down with just a whip. Make good use of it, at least."

"That's super appreciated, but... are you sure? I haven't given you anything back."

"No matter. I simply feel like giving alms once in a while. Or would you rather try using that knife to take every last bit of my belongings from me?"

It was said in a joking tone, but it was a fact that he'd given Subaru the means to actually attempt it.
The masked man's skill was decent, but not so overwhelming that there was no possibility of an accident against Subaru. In that sense, this was something of a gamble on his part.
However—

"—My name is Natsuki Subaru. This is, without question, a debt I owe you. I'll definitely repay this favor. I won't be ungracious about it."

Tucking the received knife securely at his waist, Subaru bowed deeply. Seeing that, the masked man gave a small "Hmph" through his nose, and,

"I've already shown you the way. Get going, quickly. Do your best to use whatever means you have, and win back the trust of the girl who fled from you."

"Really, that's exactly it. You saved me! Oh, wait, and one more thing—"

"What is it."

Waving his hand in gratitude, Subaru stopped just short of dashing into the forest. At his hurried manner, the masked man's voice took on a note of exasperation, but Subaru pointed at the forest ahead and said,

"I'm gonna pass through here to get back to the original grassland, but you'd better not use this place too much. There's a scary Hunter in there. He picks people off with a bow from far away, so I don't think you've got enough lives to spare. If you're heading anywhere, I'd recommend going around this forest."

"—. I see. Understood. I'll keep that in mind."

Hearing the masked man's reply, Subaru succeeded in avoiding the bad aftertaste of his benefactor suddenly falling to the Hunter's hand.
And then, dashing headlong into the forest, he ran off at full speed toward the first grassland.

"—Great edge on this!"

Fortunately, getting back to the first grassland wasn't much trouble.
The knife he'd received from the masked man was sharp, and it proved a great help in cutting down the branches and leaves blocking his way as he advanced.
Normally, a knife of this size would be expected to chip badly with heavy use, but he felt no such inconvenience. Perhaps it was quite the renowned blade.

"His gear looked expensive too... who even was that guy...?"

Wondering about that, Subaru hurried back to the original grassland. Then, deliberately searching the area where he'd been knocked over—

"—Found it. This must be the real deal."

Right in the direction opposite the conspicuously left trail, he discovered footprints left unevenly in the grass. It looked like an attempt had been made to erase the traces as much as possible, but Rem's had probably been erasable while Rui's couldn't be completely erased.
Given the evidence of effort made to erase them, it was unlikely this was yet another decoy.
In other words—

"Finally, I've got your tail, Rem...!"

Making the very villain-esque remark he'd worried about earlier, he eagerly pursued the trail that would serve as his new marker. The trail continued sporadically all the way to the forest's entrance, same as before, but this time the broken branch at the forest entrance and the footprint in the mud couldn't be faked.

"Found it! With this—"

Thinking he could catch up to Rem, Subaru moved to follow the muddy footprint with eager momentum.
And then, the very next instant—

"—?!"

At Subaru's feet, as his attention was fixed on the muddy footprint, a tied vine was cut. It launched a supporting branch with tremendous recoil, and a powerful blow struck Subaru's side from an angle.

"Gehh!"

Taking a horizontal swipe from a branch as thick as his own arm, Subaru's body went flying spectacularly. Rolling across the mud, he let out a groan of pain, unable to get up for a while from the impact.
His vision flickered, and struck by the unexpected surprise attack, Subaru's consciousness spun and flashed like a police car's rotating light, from pain and shock.

"Th-that was... no way..."

After a while, as the pain eased, Subaru finally managed to stand up. He could tell his knees were still shaky, and that there was substantial damage inside his flank.
But there was a greater shock than that.

"—A trap?"

Even while fleeing, she wasn't just fleeing.
That was the fearsomeness of the girl—Rem—who, even having lost her memory, used every ounce of strength she had.

Natsuki Subaru finally realized.
—This was the beginning of the second true battle with Rem since coming to this other world.
