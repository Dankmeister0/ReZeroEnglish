Arc 10 Chapter 1: "Friends"

"—Sounds just like that princess. Selfish to the very end, doing exactly as she pleased."

Felt said this with her lips against the rim of her teacup, and Emilia's eyes softened.

Across from her, Felt sat on the parlor sofa with one knee drawn up against her chest. She'd listened to Emilia's story all the way through without interrupting, and mourned Priscilla in a way that was entirely her own.

Felt's eyes held a red just faintly different from Priscilla's own, and no great grief or sense of loss passed through them. Emilia didn't think that made her cold-hearted. She simply thought: she's strong.

That included the fact that Felt had come here herself, of her own will, to learn the details of Priscilla's death.

Right now, Emilia and Felt sat facing each other in a room of the Royal Guesthouse in the royal capital of Lugunica. It was the parlor of a residence set aside for important guests staying in the capital, and there Emilia had welcomed Felt.

They hadn't arranged this in advance. Felt happened to be staying in the capital, and when she heard that Emilia had brought news to the royal castle, she'd come to see her like this.

Honestly, Emilia was overjoyed that Felt had come to visit like this.

To begin with, the Emilia Camp owed Felt a great debt — Emilia had heard from Annerose that Meili had been very well looked after while they were away from the kingdom.

Annerose, Roswaal's relative and one of Emilia's supporters, had been asked to handle the various negotiations concerning Meili and the Pleiades Watchtower. Apparently Felt and her people had lent considerable help in seeing those negotiations through.

For that reason too, Emilia had meant to make time to go thank Felt herself, so getting to meet like this was a huge help.

Still, if only she could have brought back better news to share—

"—That's the kind of face you're makin', Emilia-neechan."

"Huh, was I really that easy to read?"

"Pretty much. Scrunchin' up your brow and starin' at the floor like that, anyone could tell."

Following Felt, who tapped her own brow with a finger, Emilia rubbed at hers too.

She'd often been told her thoughts showed easily on her face, but to have it come through so specifically startled even her. It worried her too — she'd been telling herself she'd fully recovered, that she was fine now, that there was no need to make everyone worry about her.

"And yet, no good. At this rate Subaru and the others are going to scold me."

"That guy gettin' mad at you, Emilia-neechan? Can't picture it."

"That's not true. Subaru would be in trouble if I didn't pull myself together, so he makes sure to tell me when something about me isn't good."

"Huh. We'll see about that."

Laughing, Felt rested her chin on her raised knee, a snaggletooth peeking out.

Emilia pouted a little at Felt's faintly mischievous, teasing smile, and continued, "But—"

"—it seems like you could see right through what I was thinking, Felt-chan... Don't you feel that way too?"

"A bit convoluted, but is that s'posed to mean — it's been a while since we've seen each other's faces like this, and you're sorry the topic's the princess's death?"

"Um, yes... that's right. Yeah, that's it."

"About that, I'm actually glad I got to hear it from you, Emilia-neechan."

"From me?"

Caught off guard, Emilia asked again, and Felt nodded, "Yeah." She scratched her own head in front of Emilia, whose eyes had gone wide.

"Honestly, when I heard that princess died, I figured it had to be some joke. She was the poster girl for women you couldn't kill even if you tried. If it'd stayed like that, my head would've just kept swellin' up with all kinds of half-baked guesses, and the truth would've gotten buried under 'em."

"—"

"So gettin' to hear it from you, Emilia-neechan, who was actually there — it let me put it behind me. ...Nah, more like it got me ready to put it behind me. Tch, now I'm the one talkin' all convoluted. My bad."

"—. No, it's fine, I think I understand what you mean, Felt-chan."

Felt made a sour face at not being able to explain it well, but her feelings still reached Emilia.

If their positions were reversed — if Emilia heard that someone she knew had died somewhere far away, without her knowing it was happening — she too would end up imagining all sorts of things about that death.

Her head would fill up with all those different imaginings — some right, some wrong, none of it something she could ever confirm — until she could no longer tell what was actually true.

So even if it was something very painful, having that room for doubt cut away for her was a kind of salvation.

"And if it's someone I trust who tells me, that makes it even better, I think."

"...Doesn't that mean I trust you, Emilia-neechan?"

"Yes, and I trust you too, Felt-chan... isn't that how it is for you too? Isn't that why you came to see me like this?"

"For the record, we're rival candidates, so it'd be weird for me to just nod along here!"

Felt lowered her feet from the chair, propping her chin on her knee with an exasperated look. Even so, she never shook her head, and Emilia was able to smile with relief.

Thinking of Priscilla still made a dull ache settle in Emilia's chest. But she could at least feel glad that Felt's murky feelings hadn't been left buried unanswered.

"—"

Just then, a faint breath of amusement broke into the exchange between the two of them. Felt's sharp ears caught it instantly, and she snapped, "Oi," in an irritated voice.

"What're you laughin' at?"

"—No, forgive me. It's just that seeing Felt-sama bested so thoroughly by Emilia-sama is such a rare sight, I couldn't help it."

"'Couldn't help it,' my ass. What kind of knight just grins while his liege gets put in her place?"

"Forgive me. I hesitated to interrupt the two of you."

The one who said this, hand to his chest and head bowed, was Felt's knight — the man with the striking red hair and blue eyes, Reinhard.

Of course, Felt would never have come to visit Emilia alone, and he too had been listening to the whole conversation alongside her. At the friendly back-and-forth between Reinhard and Felt, Emilia said, "Oh, honestly," by way of preface.

"You don't have to be so considerate. Even our Otto-kun has been joining the conversation since a while ago... huh? Wasn't he?"

"Right. Thank you for noticing. Believe it or not, even I have the good sense not to interrupt a conversation between two people of such exalted standing."

Beside Emilia, who raised her brows in surprise, the one who gave that wry laugh was Otto, who'd been standing there the whole time.

This time, Otto was the only one who'd accompanied Emilia to the capital, so naturally he'd been at her side for the report to the castle and this meeting at the guesthouse alike — everywhere, really. Otto's mere presence was so reassuring that Emilia had grown completely at ease, even when he wasn't saying anything.

"Sorry, I didn't notice. You love to talk, Otto-kun, so staying quiet must have been reeeally hard for you."

"If I may say so, I only speak what's necessary when it's necessary — I'm quite sure that doesn't make me a chatterbox!"

"Oh-ho, not very convincin'. Comin' from a guy who can chat up not just people but bugs and Water Dragons too, sayin' you don't love to talk."

"I'd appreciate it if you didn't mix up my Divine Protection with my personality."

Otto pressed down the green hat on his head and answered the easygoing Felt like that.

Since Otto could speak with all sorts of living creatures through the power of the Divine Protection of Word Spirits, Emilia felt Felt's remark had struck right at the heart of it. Come to think of it, wherever he went, Otto was forever talking to animals or insects or any creature at all, widening his circle of friends.

"Otto-kun has reeeally a lot of friends. I don't have very many, so I really admire how he can get along with anyone."

"Calling it 'friends' isn't quite right, though. More often than not, what I bring to them is negotiation or trade — a different sort of know-how from making friends. Thinking about it that way, I probably can't claim to have that many friends either."

"Ain't somethin' you gotta agonize over the definition of a pal. Since you're at it, why don't you go be friends with my Reinhard here. This guy's short on friends too."

"Eh, really? That's reeeally surprising."

At Felt's words, one eye closed as she pointed to Reinhard standing behind the sofa, Emilia found herself looking up at the red-haired knight.

Reinhard was affable and quite famous — surely everyone in the kingdom knew his name and relied on him. Emilia herself had had nothing but kindness from him since the moment they'd met, to the point she'd half-assumed he was friends with everyone in the kingdom.

Under Emilia's surprised gaze, Reinhard went on, "Unfortunately—"

"—setting aside how I feel about it, it seems I make people shrink back around me more often than not. Aside from those in the Knights or my camp — Subaru and Ferris excluded — I may have few friends."

"Is that so. Even Reinhard has that kind of worry... Say, Otto-kun, if you don't mind, would you become friends with Reinhard?"

"That is an absolutely outrageous request you're making of me — wouldn't this actually be dishonorable for Reinhard-san!?"

"Not outrageous at all. I'd welcome it, actually. Will you be my friend?"

"Isn't this a pincer attack — no, more like an ambush!?"

Pinned between Emilia's gaze and Reinhard's smile, Otto agonized, groaning, "Grnhh."

Only Felt watched the whole thing with a smirk. After agonizing for a long while, Otto let out a long, deep breath and said,

"As Felt-sama said a moment ago, right now we're members of rival camps. Until the Royal Selection ends, we probably ought to avoid getting too close."

"I see. Regrettable, but if that's your wish, I'll respect it. Even though we're not friends."

"Yes, please do. Since we're not friends."

Otto said this lonesome thing with his shoulders drooping, while Reinhard, oddly enough, answered with a smile that showed no hurt or disappointment at all. The two of them, sadly, hadn't become friends — and yet, to Emilia's eyes, they somehow already looked exactly like friends.

And on top of that—

"Well, well, the tea's gone completely cold. I'll go brew a fresh pot. Please continue enjoying your chat, Emilia-sama. I'll just—"

"—. In that case, perhaps I'll join you. We may not have become friends, but why don't I show you the effects of the 'Divine Protection of the Tea Ceremony.'"

"You're surprisingly the type to hold a grudge, and having the Sword Saint hold one against me is not a comfortable feeling at all."

Chattering on like that, the two of them left the room together.

Watching their backs disappear behind the closing door, Emilia turned back to Felt.

"That looked reeeally like friends just now, didn't it?"

"Heh, yeah, I thought so too. Usually that attitude of Reinhard's just pisses me off, but when it's someone else on the receiving end, it's hilarious."

"Ah, there's your naughty face. But... because of our Royal Selection, making Otto-kun hold back from becoming Reinhard's friend..."

"Heh, that was just that guy usin' what I said as a convenient excuse. Not like I don't get why he did it... but somethin' like 'we can't be friends 'cause of the Royal Selection' — that ain't somethin' you gotta take seriously."

"Not... take it seriously..."

Felt seemed so pleased with herself she looked about ready to start humming, and after thinking it over for a moment at that sight, Emilia said, "All right," and stood up on the spot.

Otto had thought of the Royal Selection and given up on becoming Reinhard's friend. As sorry as she felt about his decision and consideration, Emilia had made up her mind.

Because of what happened with Priscilla, she didn't want to have any more regrets.

"Hey, Felt-chan, is it all right?"

"Ohh? What's up?"

Emilia strode over and dropped down beside Felt, who was looking up at her, facing her head-on at a distance close enough for their shoulders to touch. At Emilia's sudden closeness, Felt blinked her red eyes in surprise.

Come to think of it, Emilia's first meeting with Felt had begun, of all things, with Felt stealing the badge that proved her eligibility for the Royal Selection. Her mind had gone completely blank at the time, and she'd even wanted to take her frustration out on Puck for not being flustered at all despite the situation — in any case, meeting Felt had never been a good start.

Even so, now, Emilia thought this about Felt, her fellow candidate for the Royal Selection:

"I want to get close with you, Felt-chan. Not just ordinary friendly — I want us to be reeeally close friends."

"...Hah?"

"Actually, I made a promise with Anastasia-san that once the Royal Selection is over, we'd become friends. We traveled together and talked about all sorts of things... it wasn't the real Anastasia-san the whole time, but we talked a lot, and that's how we made the promise."

As Felt's big eyes wavered in confusion, Emilia pressed on briskly.

The promise she'd exchanged with Anastasia — since Anastasia thought of herself so strongly as a merchant, she'd surely keep it. Unlike with Subaru, a promise with her felt secure.

"Subaru breaks promises right away so I worry, but with Anastasia-san I feel secure. But, even setting the promise aside, I get the feeling I can't become friends with Subaru anyway, and I don't feel like I even want to become friends with Subaru either... oh? What was I talking about again?"

"The one about how you and Subaru can't become friends, Emilia-neechan?"

"Yes, that's right... no wait, not that — my story about you and me, Felt-chan!"

Emilia felt a chill of alarm at having nearly lost track of what she was actually talking about. Right at that narrow escape, in front of Emilia, Felt suddenly looked up. Following that motion, Emilia looked up at the ceiling too. There was nothing in particular to see.

"Felt-chan?"

"Ain't lookin' at the ceiling. Just thinkin' about what to do here."

"Um, oh, right. Subaru and Petra-chan often tell me that looking at my face makes them reeeally cheerful. What do you think?"

"You tryin' to tell me your good points or somethin'?"

"That's right, because I want to be friends with you, Felt-chan."

Over this past year and a bit, Emilia had grown used to looking at her own face in the mirror.

Since Subaru and Petra, and even Ram now and then, complimented Emilia's face, she thought it might work on Felt too, and gave it a try with nothing to lose.

At Emilia's desperate approach, Felt, still with her face turned upward, said,

"Why d'you wanna be friends with me so bad? Ain't like things aren't fine as they are..."

"I wanted to become friends with Priscilla."

"—"

"But I kept putting off telling her that. I'm hopeless, aren't I. I knew perfectly well that someone important to me, who was fine just yesterday, could be taken from me all of a sudden."

Pressing a hand tightly to her chest, touching the mana crystal hanging from her neck, Emilia murmured.

It was the same with Puck, who kept on sleeping, and the same with Emilia's precious family, frozen in ice in Elior Forest. Partings come suddenly, no matter how important the person is to you.

Priscilla's death had reminded forgetful Emilia of that sad truth.

"I don't want to forget that again. So I've decided not to hold back anymore. To tell the people I love that I love them, and to tell the people I want to be friends with exactly that."

"—Damn it, that princess, pullin' something like this even at the very end."

"—?"

Emilia had put her feelings into words as honestly as she could. Felt, who had listened while still looking up, slowly lowered her gaze.

Felt's expression was a smile — not one of frustration, nor of bitterness, but something else entirely.

"Got it, fine. I'll be friends with you too, Emilia-neechan."

"Really!? Does that mean, after the Royal Selection ends—"

"Don't gotta wait for that. If it's gonna happen either way, I'll become friends with you before Anastasia-neechan and make her sore about it."

"Ah, don't say that. Anastasia-san is going to be my friend too, you know."

"Bein' already friends, ain't I ranked higher in your heart than Anastasia-neechan, who ain't your friend yet?"

"Oh, stop teasing me!"

At Felt's cackling laugh, snaggletooth on display, Emilia couldn't help but let her own lips curve into a smile.

Felt had given her a happy answer to her offer. With the real feeling of having become friends with Felt, Emilia's chest slowly filled with something warm.

Laughing together with Felt like that, Emilia thought — of Al, who even now hadn't been able to fully accept Priscilla's death, and of Subaru and the others staying by his side.

"With Al too—"

Someday. It didn't have to be right now — but someday.

Emilia wanted to pray, from the bottom of her heart, for the day to come when she could talk about Priscilla — the person she'd wished to be friends with — wearing some expression other than tears.

That was what Emilia thought, shoulder to shoulder with her friend.

"—So that's why you even gave me that look. You have something to discuss with me, don't you?"

Leaving Emilia and Felt alone in the parlor, once they'd made their way some distance down the hallway, Reinhard brought it up unhurriedly, just like that.

Carrying a silver tray laden with a tea set, Otto nodded without turning around. "That's right."

"I'm grateful you picked up on it. Felt-sama's visit finally seemed to let Emilia-sama laugh naturally again, so I didn't want to disturb that atmosphere."

"So Priscilla-sama's death really did weigh on her a great deal, it seems."

"Honestly, I doubt anyone who took part in that battle came away unaffected. Even someone like me, who barely had any connection to her, feels a sense of it I can't quite shake."

Of course, whatever shock Otto had felt was nothing compared to Emilia, who'd held Priscilla in some fondness, or to Subaru and Al, who had watched her vanish right before their eyes.

Even so, for Otto — who'd been working to bring back as many of the allies who'd fought against the Great Disaster as possible — the death of someone connected to the kingdom, a Royal Selection candidate, weighed on him considerably. On the other hand, he couldn't deny that part of him had coolly analyzed the elimination of a rival candidate with such absurd charisma.

"...That's surprising."

Reinhard murmured that, as if he'd heard the voice inside Otto's chest. Glancing back at him, Otto saw Reinhard's brows raised, looking exactly as surprised as his words suggested.

At being reacted to that way, Otto loosened the corner of his mouth in a slightly self-mocking way.

"Is that reaction because it surprised you that Priscilla-sama's death pained me? Certainly, it would be a lie to say I feel no relief at all at a rival candidate's elimination..."

"No, I was surprised that you spoke of your own weakness so honestly. You don't have to go out of your way to paint yourself as worse than you are, you know."

"—"

"Or, was speaking your true feelings a sign you've let your guard down around me... a sign we might become friends?"

"It seems I chose my words poorly, then."

Teased that way, Otto squared his shoulders slightly and resumed walking.

It seemed his heart was in worse shape than he'd realized himself. Because Reinhard had understood that, he let the whole slip pass as just a moment of banter.

"Get it together, Otto Suwen."

Telling himself this wouldn't do, he pulled his own reins tight again.

However little hostility Felt held toward Emilia, she was still a rival candidate. There was nothing better than not showing weakness to the other side. Being in a state of mental imbalance was practically at the very top of the list of things one must never show.

Keeping that firmly in mind once more, Otto set about fulfilling the reason he'd brought Reinhard out here in the first place.

It was this—

"—I'll go ahead and share what I know about Heinkel Astrea."

"—"

At the topic he'd raised, the air in the hallway clearly changed.

Whether it turned dry or damp, the humidity shifted. Whether it rose or fell, the temperature shifted. And the only thing certain was that the one causing it was the Sword Saint walking right beside him.

"As for Heinkel-san, he took part in the battle in the Empire as a member of Priscilla-sama's camp, and made it back alive. But after Priscilla-sama's death, he was seen in a terribly distraught state, and after that his whereabouts became unknown."

"...Was he taken captive by the Empire?"

"No, that seems unlikely. It's a nation that can be utterly ruthless when practical gain is at stake, but exhausted as they are now after that battle, they're not so blind to their situation as to make an enemy of the kingdom — of the Sword Saint, Reinhard van Astrea."

"Then he vanished of his own will, is that it."

Otto heard Reinhard murmur that, listless, eyes lowered slightly.

He hadn't meant it as payback, but the result was the same kind of situation as Otto's own earlier slip — throwing the other party's mental balance off. Even so, he wasn't in any mood right now to seize the chance and dig into or probe at the other's weakness.

Honestly, he thought it wasn't really suited to him either, but—

"Heinkel-san... your father, I mean — are you worried about him?"

Asking the other man that came from genuine concern, in return for the concern shown to him.

In truth, it was probably a hard question for Reinhard to answer. Otto didn't know the details of Reinhard and Heinkel's relationship, but it was already common knowledge that this parent-child relationship was cracked and broken.

What's more, Otto had personally witnessed, in Priestella, the scene where Heinkel held a sword to Felt's throat in an attempt to bind Reinhard's movements.

For that matter, the very fact that three generations of the family each supported a different Royal Selection candidate could itself be said to speak plainly to House Astrea's dysfunction.

In any case—

"Whether or not this offers any comfort — according to Garfiel, your father withstood even a blow from the Dragon. So I don't think there's much danger of him having been attacked and having his life put in jeopardy."

As he said this, Otto deliberately kept quiet about the possibility of Heinkel taking his own life.

From what he'd heard, Heinkel, distraught over Priscilla's death, had gone so far as to switch the direction of his loyalty to Emilia, begging to be taken on as her subordinate. Refused by Emilia and left with nowhere to go, the possibility that he might do something desperate couldn't be entirely ruled out—

"—That won't happen, Otto. My father would never, under any circumstances, take his own life."

"...Was I showing it on my face like Emilia-sama does?"

Having his inner thoughts pinpointed exactly, Otto rubbed his brow with one hand. Reinhard gave a wry smile and said, "No,"

"I could tell you were deliberately not voicing the worst possibility, out of consideration. But that worry is groundless. My father has a purpose he's determined to live to accomplish. There's no possibility of him choosing death before he's seen it through."

"If that's so, it's reassuring on one hand, but it stokes another worry — driven into a corner, who knows what he might do... Might he not, for instance, try to approach Felt-sama next?"

To begin with, from Heinkel's perspective, that would surely be a far more viable option than clinging to Emilia.

Of course, that choice would erase Heinkel's one and only absolute advantage — his ability to exert some form of coercion over the Sword Saint, Reinhard. But even setting that aside, it was an option with a high chance of getting him taken in.

Having lost Priscilla and now been rejected by Emilia as well, Otto thought it entirely plausible that Heinkel would choose Felt as his next host to latch onto.

And yet—

"—That won't happen either, I think."

That came in a tone even more certain than before, and it sealed off whatever Otto might have said next.

"—"

Into his eyes, which reflected the clear blue sky, mixed a shade as if night had fallen across them. Even the faintest trace of a smile vanished from his lips, and Reinhard walked on in silence beside a now-quiet Otto.

Two steps, three steps — unable to say anything, the two of them closed the distance to the kitchen. It seemed as though the conversation might end there in silence, but then—

"My father wants me to give up being the Sword Saint. That's why, in a Royal Selection he absolutely must win, it's unthinkable that he'd lend his strength to Felt-sama, the one I support."

"...He wants to win the Royal Selection. But he doesn't want you to win it, Reinhard-san?"

"That's exactly it. On that one point, my father and I are irreconcilable. However my father wishes it, I am the Sword Saint. — I have to be the Sword Saint."

Everyone has their own circumstances, surely.

He understood that well enough, so Otto had no intention of poking his nose into some other family's business. Even while feeling that this was, without a doubt, a warped relationship for a father and son, and even though that last line had sounded like Reinhard was trying to convince himself of it.

So the only thing Otto could do was make his own stance clear.

"—. Well, well. That was quite the scene, wasn't it. I never imagined my life would include a moment of small talk like this with the Sword Saint."

Shrugging his shoulders like that, Otto showed Reinhard his stance of not prying further into the matter.

It was a survival skill honed as a traveling merchant, meeting people of every station in every kind of place. Whatever circumstances came to his ears, he'd declare himself harmless by making an exaggerated show of not using it.

Most of the time, that was how he managed to leave a town without incident.

"Every now and then you get someone who, carried away over drinks, lets slip information they absolutely shouldn't have, and then tries to silence whoever heard it out of sheer unreasonableness..."

So even in a tavern — the ideal place to gather information on the road — you have to take care not to carelessly pick up more critical information than necessary. That this bit of merchant's wisdom ended up proving useful even in his involvement with the Royal Selection just goes to show you never know what life will bring.

In any case—

"Thank you for sharing what you know about my father's matter. I'll keep it in mind."

Reinhard too nodded, seeming to accept Otto's declared stance. In response, Otto shrugged as well and continued, "Sorry about that."

"Did I just add to your troubles unnecessarily? In a position like the Sword Saint, you must already have plenty to worry about as it is."

"It's a Divine Protection and a position I sought for myself. I suppose that makes it my own responsibility to bear."

A Divine Protection is something one is granted.

Otto thought it an odd way to put it — "sought for myself" — but he quickly realized that the Divine Protection of the Sword Saint alone was acquired, inherited by whoever proved worthy of it.

Reinhard too, he remembered, had inherited it after the previous Sword Saint's death.

"For anyone with a Divine Protection, the worries of coming to terms with it never end. From what I can see, though, you seem to have mastered yours quite well, Otto — or perhaps not so much?"

"Mastered it, not at all. I've been at the mercy of this unmanageable Protection since the day I was born. If I could hand it off to someone else, I'd gladly do so."

"Ha ha, you shouldn't say something so careless. Plenty of people envy those with a Divine Protection. Of course, I imagine there's no need for me to tell you that."

"Honestly, I don't think it's the kind of thing anyone should envy at all."

Just as Reinhard said, for the blessed, worries over their Divine Protection never end.

It was the kind of problem with no model answer, one that couldn't even be shared between two people with different Protections, let alone with someone who had none at all — and yet you had to go on answering it regardless.

His answer to Reinhard's flattery, too, was unmistakably Otto's true feeling. He had never once been so conceited as to think he'd mastered the Divine Protection of Word Spirits. Probably never would.

"Unlike your Divine Protection of the Sword Saint, Reinhard-san, mine isn't the sort of thing that gets your name written into history."

"I wish you wouldn't tense up over 'Sword Saint, Sword Saint' like that. In the end, it's still an immature position that gets tossed around by its own title. I have no intention of putting on airs about it."

"They say the position makes the person. You may simply not realize it, but you might be intimidating those around you with that very 'Sword Saint'-ness, Reinhard-san."

"Intimidating with 'Sword Saint'-ness... could you tell me more about that? It's possible that's the reason Felt-sama and Rachins and the others say all sorts of things to me."

"I said that as an idle joke, please don't come at me so eagerly with it!"

Caught off guard by how eagerly it was seized upon, Otto let out something like a yelp at Reinhard's barrage of questions.

Shifting into this lighter, thinner exchange, Otto let his guard against Reinhard drop just slightly, making a little room and ease in his heart.

At least for now, Otto had no reason to rush into political maneuvering against Felt or Reinhard at the cost of his own standing with them. His own condition, too, was far from good.

More than anything, right about now, in some distant place, Subaru was in the very thick of wearing himself down for Al's sake.

"Even someone like me feels a pang of conscience scheming behind the scenes while Natsuki-san and the others are trying to save someone's heart. —Though Margrave Mathers is another matter entirely."

Unlike Subaru and the others, who had headed to the Pleiades Watchtower with Al, Roswaal had brought along Schult, Priscilla's attendant, and accompanied him to her territory of Barielle. There was no doubting that behind his show of concern and consideration lay the aim of using this opportunity to interfere in the Barielle domain.

As a party involved in the Royal Selection, that conduct was correct, diligent, and worthy of contempt. — And at the same time, Otto found himself feeling that he, who couldn't bring himself to be quite so heartless, was both too soft and yet somehow better off for it.

"Well then, shall I brew the tea? Allow me to show off a bit."

"Please, don't hold back — I'll be watching to see what you can do."

The moment they reached the kitchen, Reinhard snatched the tea set from the tray and began preparing it. Watching him, Otto let out a breath, taking a moment's rest even as he thought of what lay ahead.

But he had a feeling. A feeling that things were about to start moving again, soon, in some way or another.

"...Some discussion is being prioritized at the castle, one important enough to postpone Emilia-sama and Felt-sama's audience. Whatever could it be about?"

Beside Otto as he murmured that, a small shadow lurking behind a pillar stirred — a Zodda Bug, with whom he'd struck a deal to report back if anything stirred at the castle.

—Otto's premonition would prove right, in two senses.

One was the report from the Zodda Bug that the discussion at the castle — deliberately held without the Royal Selection candidates present — had reached some conclusion. The other was the report from Grassis, the girl from Felt's camp, who came to the Royal Guesthouse.

It was news of a complicated upheaval: trouble had broken out among Subaru's group at the Pleiades Watchtower, and after taking Al into custody, Subaru and the others were now heading for the royal capital.
