Arc 4 Chapter 52: "A Slight Change"

When Subaru wakes, the first thing he confirms is whether now is reality.
He's crossed back and forth between dream and reality so often lately that the boundary tends to blur. Especially when the point his 'Return by Death' brings him back to is right after regaining consciousness — his brain is often still half-asleep, and it's not uncommon to fall into a state indistinguishable from ordinary waking.

"—Ugh, gah."

Consciousness comes flooding back into reality, and the first thing he registers is the taste of grit mixed into his mouth.
Spitting out the bitter taste along with a mouthful of saliva, Subaru pushes himself upright and looks around. A dim space, damp cold air, a silence unsettling enough to unnerve — inside the tomb.

"I made it back... huh."

Opening and closing his raised hands, Subaru mutters that while checking the feel of his body.
He does it while replaying, in his mind, how he'd died right before this 'Return by Death.'

"When I was about to get swallowed by the shadow I wondered how it'd turn out... but it was worth killing myself, I guess."

Recalling the sharp sensation of something piercing his throat, Subaru touches the Adam's apple that should have borne the wound and lets out a breath of relief.
Blood welling up, blocking his throat, flooding his lungs — the suffocating agony of drowning. The fading consciousness and the sense of loss don't fade in color no matter how many times he's already tasted that 'death.'
No matter how many times he tastes it, 'death' always deals Subaru a fresh kind of suffering. And still—

"...It's a lot better than a situation where I couldn't come back at all — something irreversible."

Feeling some measure of satisfaction that he'd made it back, that he'd been able to choose 'death' without hesitation in that moment, Subaru sets his examination of 'Return by Death' aside for now.
It's still too early to feel moved that he's safely made it back to this point in time.

"No time to be getting sentimental about making it back. First, sort out what needs doing, do what needs to be done, and then..."

And then, he needs to question his own resolve.
Closing his eyes, Subaru takes a single deep breath. And by the time he opens them again, no trace of confusion remains in his eyes. Only a focus on what must be done.

Standing, brushing the dust off his body, Subaru surveys the room and spots a girl lying on her side a short distance away.
Emilia. In the middle of her 'Trial,' she's likely still facing her own past this very moment, an expression of anguish on her face.

He rushes over, reaching out a hand to wake her. He has some plan already for getting her out, meeting up with Ram and the others outside the tomb, and what comes after.
But right as his thoughts reach that point, Subaru suddenly notices — just before his arm touches Emilia, his fingertips are trembling.

"...What?"

Eyes widening at the tremor in his fingers, Subaru brings his hand up in front of his face, bewildered. He sends a command from his brain to still the trembling, but the fingers, now aware of their own shaking, ignore the order and keep trembling. And then, belatedly, Subaru notices something else.
His teeth are chattering, clacking against each other without meeting properly.

"My hands and my teeth are shaking... what the hell is this..."

Even as he's startled by the change happening in his own body, some part of Subaru already understands what it means, deep down.
The instant he'd reached to touch Emilia, a scene had flashed through his mind — that's the reason for the trembling.

—The moment before he died, Emilia's face looking down at him, her emotions frozen solid.

The Witch of Jealousy — that very presence — had descended upon the 'Sanctuary' at that moment, he thinks. And for some reason, it had cloaked itself in shadow while wearing Emilia's body, and in that final instant, Subaru had witnessed it firsthand.

Most likely, the Witch had possessed Emilia's body as it slept within the tomb.
Knowing Petelgeuse — a being who'd possessed the power to take over another's body — Subaru could swallow that possibility without much resistance.
And the reason the Witch had targeted Emilia's body was simple.

Subaru had blithely let a forbidden word slip at the tea party. The Witch, manifesting to punish him for it, hadn't been able to step into the tea party itself. Instead, what caught her eye was Emilia, sleeping beside him.
The Witch had then seized her body, filled the 'Sanctuary' with shadow, killed Garfiel, and tried to swallow Subaru in shadow as well — that, presumably, was how it had all played out.

"Even understanding all that... my body's still shaking, huh..."

Even with the composure to calmly retrace those events, there's a weak part of his heart that can't forget the terror of standing before that monstrosity.
If Subaru's current reasoning is correct, that whole tragedy occurred because Subaru had been invited to the tea party right after his last 'Return by Death.' Which means, this time around, having not attended the tea party, he hasn't stepped on that landmine.
—There's better than a ninety percent guarantee that the Witch isn't dwelling within Emilia.

And yet his body still reacts to fear with rejection — that's nothing but plain cowardice.
He simply can't shake off the worst-case imagining.

Namely — whether the Witch of Jealousy would really stop chasing Subaru just because a 'Return by Death' now stands between them.

"——"

It's the Witch of Jealousy who's causing Subaru's 'Return by Death.'
That's Subaru's conclusion, and Echidna affirms this view as well. Considering the Witch's past appearances and how the last loop ended, it's likely correct.
For whatever reason, the Witch doesn't want Subaru's story to end in 'death.' He can be grateful for that much, at least. That much, and only that.

The problem is only this: would that Witch of Jealousy — so obsessive, wielding that much power, her fixation growing strong enough to interfere directly with reality — really give up on Subaru?

"——"

Even supposing the Witch has power over time itself, it's mere wishful thinking to assume she couldn't apply to herself what she's applied to Subaru.
Just as Subaru has rewound the world through 'death,' what's to say the Witch hasn't likewise gone back through time, chasing after him?

A heart shrinking in fear, and an answer that won't come — and that answer is lying right in front of him now.

"............"

If he touches Emilia, wakes her from the 'Trial,' everything will become clear.
If she rises and calls his name in that familiar silver-bell voice, Subaru will be freed from this fear.
But what if that doesn't happen?

"...Then, I guess that'd be the end of it."

If the Witch manifests before him every time he loops back, then there's simply nothing to be done. The Witch of Jealousy's strength is absolute — faced with the power that blanketed the entire 'Sanctuary' in shadow, he can't picture any vision of their current forces standing a chance.
Even Garfiel had been struck down without a fight in that nightmare — what countermeasure could there possibly be?
In other words, this is the watershed moment.

"If it's not even certain I can die and come back... then whether the Emilia in front of me right now is really Emilia herself or not is uncertain too, huh. God... I'm an idiot."

Grasping anew the situation he's in, Subaru lets out a soft breath.
He notices, only now, that the trembling in his fingers and the chattering of his teeth have both stopped. Realizing it, he's finally able to see clearly.

Everything's ambiguous, uncertain, undetermined — and so what.

"That's just... something that could happen to anyone. Nothing unusual about it."

Not knowing the future, being anxious about even a second ahead — that's normal for anyone.
Just because he happens to have a glimpse of what's a little further ahead, what is there to be afraid of?
A fear so foolish it's practically equivalent to fearing life itself—

"...Ugh, no... I don't want this..."

Compared, right now, right in front of him, to the sight of the beloved girl nearly crushed beneath the weight of her own past — how small, how trivial his hesitation looks.

"—Emilia."

Calling her name, Subaru gently touches her cheek with fingers that have stopped trembling.
Her pale cheek. Smooth skin, its warmth seeping into his fingertips as though it might melt. Her downcast eyes, long lashes trembling faintly, and then from beneath them, violet-indigo eyes flicker open with a faint, weak light.

Emilia, pulled back into reality. She blinks several times, then notices Subaru right in front of her, and—

"...Su, baru?"

Her trembling eyes find Subaru, focus on him, and his name is spoken.
That voice, the color of her eyes, her manner — all of it was the Emilia Subaru knew, and so—

"—Yeah, that's right."

Subaru could feel the shadow of anxiety that had been wrapped heavily around his entire body until just a moment ago vanish all at once.
A long breath follows the words he'd somehow managed to answer with, and as he supports her back with his hand while she tries to sit up, he feels himself go slack with relief.

In contrast to Subaru, Emilia, now sitting up, looks around restlessly, her eyes darting here and there. Her head still seems a little heavy; after confirming where she is, she presses a hand to her forehead and murmurs, "Um..."

"This is... I was, just now..."

Still troubled by a lingering ache, Emilia closes both eyes, her mind drifting back to her memories from before she fell asleep — recalling what happened while she slept.
Remembering that her memories had been laid bare, Emilia's eyes widen, and then, her pink lips trembling, she looks at Subaru.

Violet-indigo eyes swaying with a wave of emotion. Her mind must be in utter chaos over the past right now. Subaru knows that Emilia, shaken awake from the 'Trial,' tends to become distraught. So he could have simply watched her quietly, right before that distress hit.
Gently embrace her while she trembled, choose his words carefully so as not to hurt her, comfort her, reassure her, and then—

"...Subaru?"

But faced with Subaru, who had prepared himself with that in mind, Emilia's reaction was completely different from what he'd imagined.
Her eyes, which had been on the verge of falling into distress, regained their composure, and her trembling lips pressed together as if held in check by some stronger emotion. Then Emilia gently lifted her hand and reached it out toward Subaru.

"Why do you look like you're about to cry?"

"...Eh?"

Emilia's fingertips stroked Subaru's cheek, then slid up toward his eyes, wide with surprise. Seeing her white finger brush past the corner of his eye, tracing over what had nearly overflowed there, Subaru realized that he himself had been holding back a welling of tears, on the verge of crying.
There's no time to ask himself why, even as he becomes aware of it.

"Ah, uh, eh?"

The trembling came, abruptly.
A trembling on an entirely different level from the shaking of his fingers and teeth earlier — one he couldn't hold back.

A trembling that shook his whole body, sapping the strength from every limb. Kneeling face to face with Emilia, Subaru couldn't resist it, could do nothing but curl in on himself, holding his shaking body.
Even as his body went slack, holding himself, Subaru understood the root of the trembling.

If, right before he touched Emilia, that tremor had been fear that Emilia might have been replaced by the Witch—

"It's okay, Subaru. It's okay, it's okay. I'm here—"

Saying that, Emilia gently embraced Subaru's trembling body from the side.
Through the thin fabric of their clothes, they could feel each other's warmth. Feeling her quiet heartbeat coming through her entire body, Subaru felt more fullness in his heart than heat in his body.

—Having been gripped by fear at the possibility that Emilia might have been replaced by the Witch, and now understanding that fear was unfounded, Subaru found himself unable to move, overwhelmed with relief.

Even though his heart thought it had steeled itself, his body hadn't reflected that at all.
Neither a heart of steel nor a body strong enough to protect that heart — both were far beyond Subaru's reach.

Feeling Emilia's warmth, her heartbeat, her gentleness, Subaru gritted his teeth at his own pathetic state, and still, he couldn't help but feel relief.

Quietly, quietly, quietly, the two of them remained there in the tomb, holding each other for a while.

"Feeling calmer?"

"Ah, yeah... um, sorry. I made a weird fuss over nothing."

The embrace continued until Subaru's trembling subsided.
At its end, Emilia asked him that, and Subaru, flushing with embarrassment at his own pathetic display, offered an apology. Hearing it, Emilia shook her head. "No,"

"It's fine. Lately, I feel like I've been the one relying on you all the time, Subaru. So when you show me your weaker side like this, once in a while... it's kind of a relief."

"That's a killer line. ...If I could help it, though, that's not really the side I want Emilia-tan seeing."

"Why not?"

"Because I always want Emilia-tan watching me act tough and cool. I don't want you knowing that deep down I'm actually weak and pathetic and hopeless."

"I don't think I'd start seeing you that way just from a little glimpse of your weak side."

Emilia's words are kind, but Subaru's pride rejects them.
It's not that she isn't that kind of person, or that showing weakness would disappoint her — that's not the real issue at all.
This is simply — a matter of Subaru's own, a boy's, concerns.

"Baring your weak side completely, letting someone know the real you... that's actually a scenario I'm pretty fond of, as far as tearjerker scenarios go."

"Tearjerker... what?"

"Never mind. I want Emilia-tan to only see my strong side. It's a matter of a man's pride, this."

Washing away the awkwardness from before with his usual pointless chatter, Subaru gives a wry smile at Emilia tilting her head in confusion. Then he tightens his expression and—

"So, I wanted to ask about the 'Trial'..."

"—Okay."

At the question he ventures out cautiously, Emilia pauses a beat, then nods, dipping her chin in response.
For a moment, Subaru feels a brief flicker of surprise at that reaction. Her demeanor upon hearing the word 'Trial' was different from any before.
Probably because, in the process of confronting reality right after waking, still carrying the shock of failing the 'Trial,' Subaru's unintended, pathetic little episode had washed some of it away.

That period of embrace had given Emilia, if only briefly, time to settle the shock of failing the 'Trial.' That's likely part of why she's managing to stay calm now.

"So my being spineless comes in handy sometimes, huh..."

"Subaru, why are you here? This place is supposed to be somewhere only I can enter..."

"Oh, I..."

Subaru, about to answer honestly, cuts himself off there.
And he thinks. —What, in fact, is the most correct thing to say here?

It would be easy to honestly tell her that he'd earned the qualification, that he'd overcome the 'Trial.' But if he chose that, it seems like what would strike Emilia is self-reproach over failing her own 'Trial,' along with a sense of inferiority toward Subaru, who'd overcome his.
That inferiority would lead to impatience, and Emilia would suffer, caught between self-loathing and a sense of responsibility. If that happened, it would negate the meaning of her current composure in this moment.

Given that there remains a possibility Emilia might face the 'Trial' in a different way than before, shouldn't Subaru respect that possibility of hers?
Setting aside whether that would actually change anything essential — whether it would let Emilia confront and overcome her 'past.'

—It's worth trying, Subaru thought.

"I got worried when you weren't coming out for so long, so I rushed in without thinking. I managed to hold onto my consciousness somehow on the way in... but once I got this far, it seems like the same thing happened as during the day."

"I see... sorry, for worrying you."

"Nah, in the end I ended up sprawled flat on the ground in here too, so if we're talking about who's worrying who, it's probably the folks outside worrying about me right about now, present tense."

The others outside must be just as anxious, given there's been no response since Subaru dove in. Realizing this, Emilia looks up with an "Ah,"

"W-well, anyway, I need to get back to everyone... you must have worried them too, Subaru."

"Whether I live or die is the least of anyone's worries, so if we don't at least let them know you're safe, Emilia-tan, things are gonna get bad."

"...You shouldn't say things like that."

At Subaru's teasing remark, Emilia gives him a reproachful look. Shrugging slightly with a "sorry," the two of them head together toward the exit of the tomb.
Along the way, Subaru adds, "By the way,"

"Can I ask? About the 'Trial,' I mean..."

"...I'm sorry. It looks like I failed the 'Trial.'"

"That's... yeah, I kind of figured, from how you were acting."

Emilia looks away apologetically. Not looking directly at her, Subaru acts as though he's just now learning a fact he already knew.
There's guilt in taking that stance, but Subaru kills that hesitation with a single shake of his head.

"So then, is this it, all over... is that how it's gonna be?"

"It doesn't seem like it. ...I can try as many times as I want to. It's strange, but I just know that. But..."

The nature of the 'Trial,' something Echidna had told Subaru directly. It was information already known to him, but it seems Emilia has been informed of it on some unconscious level as well.
Cutting herself off at the end, Emilia hesitates for a moment, then—

"No, never mind. Looks like today's a lost cause, but I'll try again tomorrow."

"You gonna be okay? If it's rough, you could space out the days a bit... or even, come up with a better strategy and countermeasures before trying again, that'd raise your odds too—"

"Thanks. ...But I know that's not the kind of problem that gets solved by doing something like that. I know that."

"...Might feel better if you talked about it, or something — I'll go ahead and say something like I understand, even though I don't."

Glancing sideways at Emilia as he says this, she looks up to meet his gaze, her lips trembling. For a moment it seems the mixed emotions welling in her chest might spill over, but right before that, she seems to catch herself, closing her eyes.

"—Sorry. I don't want to show you what I'm feeling right now, Subaru."

"There's nothing about you I'd end up hating, no matter what side of you I saw."

"It's not about being scared of what you'd think of me. No — well, that's part of it, but... there's something scarier than that."

With that, Emilia falls silent. Still, seeing the strength of will in her violet-indigo eyes remain unshaken, Subaru confirms he's succeeded in stoking that resolve.
If Subaru shows a supportive attitude like that, Emilia won't lean on it to bare her weakness, let alone let herself fully depend on him.

And loathing himself for rolling even Emilia's heart around in his palm with that knowing attitude, Subaru feels almost sick with self-disgust. —If only he could just tell himself it's necessary and be done with it.

"—Emilia-sama!"

Still choking down his self-loathing, what pulled Subaru, still walking, back to reality was the sudden dazzling moonlight and a voice calling out to the girl beside him.
Pale moonlight illuminates the tomb's entrance, and a cool breeze greets the two of them as they step outside. Glancing down, he can make out the group who had been waiting for Emilia to return from the 'Trial,' relief spreading across all their faces.

It seems Ram was the first to call out to Emilia. Having confirmed Emilia's safety, Ram, uncharacteristically, lets out a sigh of relief, hand to her chest, and then looks at Subaru standing beside Emilia,

"And Barusu, well done."

"Whoa... never expected words of appreciation from you, seriously shocked. What's with the sudden virtue, it's not like you."

"When you actually manage to accomplish something properly once in a while, Ram will offer her appreciation too. At the very least, Ram will give you credit for bringing Emilia-sama back safely. Roswaal-sama will be pleased too."

Most of the relief seems to stem from having something to report to her master, but being praised so plainly still gives Subaru a fresh sort of joy. Grinning sheepishly, Subaru lets his gaze, feigning nonchalance, drift past Ram — toward the blond young man standing apart from the rest of the welcoming group.

Garfiel, who'd had his back against a tree trunk, uncrosses his arms and walks over with a reluctant gait. There's nothing suspicious in his manner or bearing, but if he wanted to, distance would mean nothing to him in an instant.
Having just come out of the tomb, and given the very reason behind this particular 'Return by Death,' Subaru — dreading to think what state his own currently-imperceptible-to-himself 'stench' might be in — kept himself on maximum alert toward him.
And Garfiel, arriving before a tense Subaru, opens with,

"Thought for sure somethin' was gonna happen when you pulled that reckless stunt, chargin' in there. Glad to see ya made it back safe. Even thinkin' 'the Gafgaron fruit don't fall in the wind,' it still had me on edge."

"Ow! Hey, wait, that hurts, I said it hurts!"

Saying that with a laugh, he slaps Subaru's shoulder roughly, again and again.
With an impact that rattles clear to the bone, Subaru briefly wonders, "Could this actually be Garfiel's assassination attempt disguised as casualness!?" — but there's no malice to be felt from him as he grins, baring his fangs.
It's a manner that genuinely seems to be welcoming the two of them back safely. Given yet another reaction different from what he'd expected, Subaru can't hide a certain sense of anticlimax.

"That's... it?"

"Ah? Whaddaya mean. You want me pattin' your head as a reward for doin' good or somethin'?"

"Coming from Emilia, maybe, but from you? Who even wants that. That's not it, I mean..."

Subaru starts to bring up that letdown, then reconsiders — no need to go poking a hornet's nest. Whatever's going on in Garfiel's head, it's a relief that he doesn't seem inclined to strike immediately.
Either way, the conditions under which he bares his fangs are becoming increasingly clear, even to Subaru. If the worst-case condition — no questions asked, attack on sight — has been overturned, that's something to welcome.

"How I'm even supposed to deal with you is a headache in itself."

"The hell're you goin' on about now, can't make heads or tails of it."

"That's another one of those things I'm keeping to myself. Anyway, let's settle down somewhere and talk details properly. First, I want to let Emilia rest. We can sort out the schedule going forward there too."

Holding up a single finger, Subaru makes the suggestion, and no one objects.
After Emilia offers everyone an apologetic "Sorry," Ram takes the lead, holding her hand, and the group makes its way through the 'Sanctuary' — settling, as before, on Ryuzu's house as their destination.

Emilia hadn't fallen into distress. Garfiel had maintained an unwaveringly friendly attitude toward Subaru, fresh off a 'Return by Death.'
Faced with several conditions different from before, Subaru keeps searching for what the best course of action might be, what the best way to meet 'death' might be.

There's too much he needs to know, too much he needs to test.
How many more sacrifices will he have to make, to seize the best possible future?

Without even factoring his own life into the calculation, Subaru doesn't notice how calculating, how mercenary, he's become.

"——"

Nor does Subaru notice Ryuzu, standing behind him, watching him intently.
He remains, still, unable to notice.
