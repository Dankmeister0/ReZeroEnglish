Arc 5 Chapter 25: "Theater of Leo"

A mysterious young man clad all in white suddenly burst into the battlefield where ice and flame raged wildly.

His white hair was neither long nor short, and his features—handsome enough, yet without anything that stood out. His build was neither muscular nor slight, average in both height and frame, and he gave off such a faint impression that he seemed like he'd vanish into a crowd the instant he stepped into one.

But—

"A Sin Archbishop... of 'Greed'...!?"

The self-introduction from this thoroughly unremarkable, run-of-the-mill young man struck Subaru like a shock. Unless his ears had made some fatal mistake, the man had indeed just called himself a Sin Archbishop of the Witch Cult.

And in all likelihood, that declaration was no lie of any kind.

Otherwise, how could one possibly account for the abnormality of him taking the full brunt of that hellfire Sirius had unleashed, and yet showing not the slightest sign of harm?

"Anyway, I'm glad I made it in time. My bride very nearly became a pile of ash. I do pride myself on not asking much of others, but even I have at least the wish that my bride keep her human shape. Though I'd call it less a wish than plain common sense. I don't happen to have the kind of perverse taste that lets a man whisper sweet nothings to a heap of cinders."

With the fear still refusing to fade, and Subaru trembling before him, the young man—the Sin Archbishop who called himself Regulus—looked down at Emilia in his arms and kept talking. His tongue ran on fluently, glibly, but the content amounted to little more than empty nonsense.

Emilia, on the receiving end of that drivel, didn't so much as twitch in Regulus's arms. She seemed to have completely lost consciousness, her slender body left limp in his hold.

Regulus toyed with the white hair falling over his brow.

"I'm glad she's unharmed, but sadly it seems my bride didn't get to see my little display of heroism just now. Rescuing someone from a hopeless crisis like that—I'd think that's a pretty important moment for bringing two hearts closer together. Well, since it's already decided that we'll end up together in the end, it's only a difference of how quickly we get there. Nothing to make a fuss over, really."

"Y-you, what are you even—"

"Hm?"

Regulus, who had been laying out his self-serving logic, noticed Subaru and knit his brow. Then he let out a weary sigh.

"You, you don't really understand manners, do you? I mean, I introduced myself first, right? And you know why I introduced myself? Because that's the most important thing when starting a relationship with someone. No matter what kind of relationship it is, you have to start by knowing yourself and each other, don't you think? And I happen to be the considerate type, so I'm always trying to be as friendly as possible with everyone. Plus, there's always the chance the other person is shy, right? Even if they want to get along, they might hesitate to introduce themselves first. So partly out of consideration for people like that, I make a point of introducing myself first whenever I can, to lay the groundwork that puts the other person at ease. Of course, I don't go around bluntly announcing all that to everyone from the start like I'm doing them some huge favor. But I do think that if you've lived long enough to reach a certain age, you ought to be able to sense that that's the meaning behind why I introduce myself first. I mean, you can tell, right? Or have you been living your life having conversations with people you've just met without ever exchanging names? If so, then I guess my sense of common sense and your culture are just different. In that case I think we'd need to reconcile our sensibilities, but even so, shouldn't you have said something up front so as not to cause a misunderstanding? Just taking the other person's kindness for granted like it's the natural thing to do, without making even that one small effort of consideration—isn't that a little off? Or rather, that's downright rude, isn't it? That's rudeness itself. And to fail in courtesy toward someone means you only see them as worth that much. And to misjudge someone's worth like that is nothing less than an infringement on their life, on their way of living. An infringement of another person's rights. An infringement of my rights, against me, who is selfless and rational."

"O-oh...? M-my bad. ...My name is Natsuki Subaru."

Watching Regulus's eyes gradually take on a tinge of madness as he rattled on, Subaru's heart screamed out in alarm. Obeying that fear, he managed, trembling, to give his name.

At hearing Subaru's introduction, Regulus narrowed the eyes he'd had wide open, and said:

"...Yes, that's how it should be. By respecting others, you in turn are respected. Ordinary consideration is what makes the world livable for everyone. By not asking for too much, and finding a happiness that fits one's own vessel, everyone gets to spend their share of happiness. Don't wallow in selfish desire—just accept things as they are, and be satisfied with your daily bread. That's the peaceful way to live."

Regulus laid out such placid, unimpeachable truisms that it almost sounded like a joke. But the eyes with which he spoke of his ideals proved it was no joke at all—it was exactly what he believed.

If you took her words out of context alone, Sirius too had sounded perfectly reasonable. And from Regulus's behavior and words, Subaru couldn't help but sense that same thinness, that same distortion, he'd felt from Sirius.

"I'm talking to you here, so isn't giving at least one little nod of acknowledgment what it means to actually listen? Why can't you manage something as basic as that, as a human being? How is it you don't even realize that, day after day, unconsciously, unknowingly, thoughtlessly, you keep hurting other people's hearts in small ways? Even a small wound is still a wound, right? Something bad can get in through it, and it might turn into a sickness that threatens someone's life. It's the same with the body and the heart. I really hate people who don't understand enough to realize they're unknowingly threatening someone else's life. Are you out of your mind or something?"

"…………"

"Being flawed as a person is one thing, but not being able to recognize it—that's no good, right? And it's just as wrong that the people with ordinary good sense are the ones left to bear the burden for it. Why can't you see that the world turns smoothly along only because the vast majority of people uphold common decency without even having to think about it? Before you go trampling over the consideration other people extend to you without even thinking about it, shouldn't you first become conscious of your own crooked, deficient way of being, and change it? Otherwise you'll just keep stomping through people's hearts with your shoes on, won't you?"

Displeased, it seemed, by Subaru's stubborn silence, Regulus kept pressing on. His words gradually sped up, and it was plain his voltage was rising. Even so, Subaru still couldn't respond.

His heart had frozen too stiff for that.

"Even after all that, you're still ignoring me..."

"Thanks for the lecture. —Now hurry up and burn, char, and disappear!!"

From behind Regulus, who stood there stock-still, flames came crashing down like a waterfall. Sirius, raising both arms, sent the flame rising from them slamming down mercilessly on the man who at least claimed to be a fellow Sin Archbishop. Subaru had watched that atrocity unfold with his own eyes, and it was precisely because of that he couldn't cry out in the moment.

"Subaru..."

"I—I know. But it'll be fine."

Behind him, Beatrice's voice, as her grip on his shoulder tightened almost painfully, was trembling too. He knew that voice was worried about Emilia—the one in the arms of Regulus, who had vanished into that waterfall of flame.

It wasn't as if Subaru himself felt no fear that Emilia might get caught up in that atrocity. But even so, he had a certainty. And that was—

"—Hey, pulling something like this in the middle of a conversation, just how bad at reading the room are you? If you had something to say, you should've spoken up first. If you wanted to say something, raise your hand. Do you really think I'm incapable of the basic courtesy of waiting for someone to finish talking?"

With an annoyed wave of his arm, Regulus dispersed the swirling column of scorching hellfire. The heat wave vanished as if by sleight of hand, and Regulus, standing right at its center, showed not the slightest change. Naturally, the same held true for Emilia in his arms.

Despite having been swallowed whole by that much fire, there wasn't so much as a burn, let alone a single drop of sweat, on either of them.

"And on top of everything, I'm a Sin Archbishop just like you, you know? I already know your head's not screwed on right, and I have the kindness to overlook a bit of rudeness. Luckily, there's no harm done either way. Except..."

Turning around, Regulus lowered his voice and glared at Sirius. Under that gaze, Sirius closed the front of her coat, hiding the girl from view once more, and kept grinding her teeth, both arms hanging limp at her sides.

"Just now, you meant to burn this girl to death too, didn't you? Letting that slide is, well, honestly kind of impossible, don't you think? Or rather, it is impossible. Since time immemorial, no matter what story or what character, when someone you love gets hurt, burning with rage is only natural, isn't it? That's an ordinary right everyone has, and so it's the ordinary right of revenge that I have too."

"Rage! Ha, so it's rage now, is it! Don't make me laugh! Don't you dare speak so lightly of rage and the like, you thin, small excuse for a man! That's mine! It's the one thing, more precious than anything, that he gave me. And you dare to—"

"Huh, is that so. You're still hung up on that fool who got ahead of himself and died? Ugh, gross. That's neither productive nor rational, is it? Once you're dead, it's over. That's just how it is, isn't it obvious? And you can't even accept that much, clinging to memories instead... Yeah, you really are a defective human being. When the one you love dies, you find the next one. For all your noise about love, love, love, you won't even exercise your rightful due. Disrupting the world's natural cycle like that—you really are trash, you know that?"

"You, who mocked his death, dare to talk so high and mighty!!"

Doused in that emotionless stream of abuse, Sirius flew into a rage, spittle flying. In her fury the monster's charging step shattered the flagstones, and she unleashed the flame-wreathed chains from both arms toward Regulus at a terrifying speed. The scorched iron tore through the air with a shriek, becoming the worst kind of weapon—one that would burn and gouge any wound it struck—as it bludgeoned Regulus.

There came a dull thud as the chain struck flesh, and Regulus's face—still standing stock-still—snapped to the side. But neither Sirius's rage nor the chain's blows would be satisfied with just a single strike.

Left and right, above and below, front and back and every angle between—Sirius's bronze-red chains battered Regulus's entire body without pause. And the moment Subaru realized that the chains, flying in at tremendous speed, were tracing trails of heat that had formed into a cage of flame—

"Disappear, disappear, disappear, disappear, disappear! Turn to charcoal, you and that damned half-devil both!!"

The cage of flame collapsed inward toward its center, and a ferocious pillar of fire rose up, engulfing Regulus. The heat was so intense it melted the flagstones—where Regulus had been standing, both the stone and the earth beneath it vaporized, leaving a crater behind.

Sirius stood there panting hard, watching the result of that scorching heat. Around the monster, the crowd—sharing in her fury, blood streaming from their eyes and noses in their frenzy—gathered, shrieking strange cries. But—

"Hey, seriously, how many times do I have to say the same thing before you get it?"

Treading over the red-hot, molten flagstones, Regulus reappeared as if nothing at all had happened. Not so much as a scratch on his white hair, his clothes, or on Emilia in his arms. His expression carried only a petulant, childish note of displeasure, as he said,

"You know, I think about this. People who can't understand something no matter how many times you tell them—they just aren't putting in enough effort to understand what's being said. And not putting in that effort means they're not being considerate enough toward the person telling them. They're looking down on them. So they don't engrave what they're told into their hearts, don't take it as a lesson, don't reflect on it, don't turn it into nourishment for tomorrow—they just forget it entirely and make the other person say it all over again. That's belittling the other person, sure, but it's also belittling the bad influence they themselves have on others. Belittling their own worth and the other person's worth, root and all. And that's... well, it involves neither words nor actions, but it's violence all the same. And so I think—"

"You goddamn insect...!"

"One of the earliest doctrines of the Witch Cult went something like this: 'If struck upon one cheek, offer the other, and only then ask the reason why.' A doctrine showing the nobility of coming to mutual understanding. But personally, I think this: sometimes, you have to strike back at the person who struck your cheek. Especially—for someone who refuses to understand pain, that's exactly what's needed."

If you listened to the words alone, they sounded sound enough.

But there was something twisted, somewhere, in the way Regulus himself existed.

"—!"

Stepping out of the crater, Regulus wore a sinister smile directed at Sirius. It was by no means a friendly smile—closer to the lip-licking look of a predator that has just spotted its prey.

Just how Regulus was warding off Sirius's flame and chains remained unknown. There was even a chance he was purely defensive, that the Authority of "Greed" possessed no means of attack at all. And so there should have been no way to conclude that whatever Regulus did next would bring about a fatal result.

—And yet Subaru could say it with certainty. At this rate, Sirius would die.

The possibility that Regulus's power was purely defensive was next to nothing. If that young man made any move at all, Sirius would die without question. And on its own, that would pose no problem whatsoever. If anything, it would be cause for celebration—one less Sin Archbishop, thanks to some infighting.

Cause for celebration it would be—except that Sirius's death would drag most everyone around into it. That included Subaru, of course, along with the crowd caught in Sirius's madness, and surely Lusbel and Tina, the two who had offered themselves up while praying for each other's safety.

"…………"

Even now, fear that refused to fade coursed through every inch of Subaru's body. His knees, buckled beneath him, kept trembling, and even his breathing felt unsteady. But even in a situation like that—

"Subaru."

Close to his ear, an uncertain voice called his name. A voice that couldn't hide its own trembling fear, yet was straining with everything it had to tell him: rely on me. It reached him through his back, carried on warmth.

Biting down on his own dread, Subaru rose unsteadily to his feet. He couldn't leave everything to that light weight on his back. And yet he didn't have the strength to manage well without borrowing that power either. And so Subaru refused to face this alone, and refused to leave it all to someone else alone, too.

If Subaru had been alone, he surely would have stayed there trembling in fear, unable to stand. The reason he could stand now was that he wasn't alone. Unlike everyone else swallowed by madness, only Subaru had someone pressed close against him—he wasn't by himself. That felt sense of connection was what let Natsuki Subaru resist his fear.

"Beatrice."

"I know, I suppose."

With just that single call, Beatrice nodded, accepting everything. Neither felt the need to confirm whether their communication had truly gotten across. All that mattered was giving their all to their respective roles—that alone should draw the result they needed.

Both Sin Archbishops, Sirius and Regulus alike, had entirely forgotten Subaru existed. Each could see only the other. Let them tear each other apart, and welcome. If Sirius alone managed to burn Regulus to death, that would be the best outcome—but that possibility was, in all likelihood, one that could never come to pass. So what Subaru aimed for instead was stopping Regulus's savagery.

Draw the man's attention over here, and head off the massive damage before it could happen. And more than anything else—

"Don't you dare go on touching Emilia so casually...!"

An inexhaustible love shoved his fear aside and lit a fire under him. Or so he told himself, bluffing. He had to start by deceiving his own heart first—otherwise there was no way, none at all, he could bring himself to challenge this.

Ahead of him, Regulus—his back turned—moved sluggishly. And Sirius, facing off against him, likewise seemed to lack any real sharpness. For Subaru, that was tremendously convenient.

"—Shamak!"

The instant he scolded his trembling legs into motion and broke into a run, Beatrice, on his back, cast her spell. Shamak's black mist—the strongest of spells—veiled Regulus's body from the world, brilliantly hindering his footing. Into that, aiming for the spot he'd been standing an instant before he vanished from sight, Subaru swung his whip with all his might.

His target was the neck. Snag the whip around that slender point and yank him down in one go. Even Regulus, riled up as he clearly was, surely couldn't help but turn his attention to Subaru once handled that roughly. Or perhaps, the moment Beatrice's Shamak hit, he'd already been plunged into complete disorientation—

"No resistance!"

"Watch out, I suppose!!"

The whip, which should have connected for sure, gave back almost no reaction, and no sooner had Subaru cried out in a voice edged with panic than Beatrice's warning followed. The next instant, the white-haired young man had burst through the black mist, kicking off the ground straight at Subaru.

"You've been busy, back and forth, back and forth. You rapist of my right to a modest peace. Would you mind scattering into pieces and disappearing?"

"Mi—no, wait, Muraku!"

Cradling Emilia with his right arm, Regulus casually reached his free left hand toward Subaru. In a snap judgment, Beatrice abandoned the idea of an attack and instead laid a gravity-manipulation spell onto Subaru's body. Subaru instantly affirmed her call, evading Regulus's closing fingertips with a leap.

Muraku, a type of Shadow magic, has the effect of loosening the pull of gravity on its target's body. Subaru's body, now light as a feather, soared high into the air, leaving behind Regulus, who'd tried to chase after him.

"Why would you dodge, I wonder?"

"Of course I'm gonna dodge, you're terrifying!"

As Regulus looked up at Subaru escaping overhead, Subaru brought his whip cracking straight down. This time his aim didn't miss, and the whip struck the crown of Regulus's head dead-on. His white hair lifted with the impact, and on his head there ought to have appeared a painful gash from the whip—except there was none. Not a scratch. It was as if nothing more than a breeze had stirred his hair.

"What are you going to do if you hurt my bride? Being gentle with a girl is just common sense, don't you think, without anyone having to teach you that? Can't you even manage that much?"

"Don't talk nonsense! She's the one person in this whole world I want to be gentlest with. And anyway, what do you even mean going on about 'my bride, my bride' like—"

"Isn't it obvious? Fate. —After all, we promised each other in a dream."

At Regulus's smiling answer, Subaru—clinging to the wall, propping himself up with one hand—was left dumbfounded. It was a somewhat absurd tableau, but neither party seemed to care, and Regulus went on:

"She and I are meant to be together. It's fate. I'm basically satisfied with myself, and I don't go around wanting things. I don't, but I'm not so small-minded that I'd refuse something given to me. All the more so when fate is the one bringing it to me. I don't ask for much, but whatever's within my reach, my world, I intend to protect no matter what. At the very least myself, and the person precious to me. So—"

"…………"

"I'll protect her. I'll take her as my wife, love and be loved, and enjoy the peace that should rightfully be ours. For that, I won't hesitate to wield whatever power I've been given."

"A-and where... where does her own will factor into any of that? You two haven't even confirmed each other's feelings, and you're already running so far ahead—"

Regulus's forceful assertions carried the bearing of a strongman powerful enough to back them up. It was a straightforward, upright, humble, ambition-free way of thinking—and yet fatally wrong. What exactly made it fatally wrong, Subaru couldn't explain. If forced to put it into words, something had gone wrong right at the starting point. Because something had been broken from the very beginning, everything that followed came out inverted.

Fear, and something beyond fear alone, made Subaru's voice shake. At that question, Regulus laughed, as if he'd just been told the most trivial thing in the world.

"Are you worried about me? If so, thanks. But it's fine, really. Fate is inevitable—and above all, love and friendship can never be one-sided affairs. If I've been told she's my fated bride, then obviously she must have been told, in turn, that I'm her fated groom."

"...Something is seriously wrong with him, I suppose."

At Regulus's broken logic, Beatrice let slip a mutter dripping with disgust. Subaru agreed completely. What Regulus was proposing was, at its core, a stalker's reasoning dressed up on the surface with sound arguments and pretty words. And true to every troublesome stalker, he clearly didn't harbor the slightest doubt that he himself might be wrong.

"I've heard enough. Besides, there's no way I'm ever going to see eye to eye with a rival in love. Not that I even want to admit he's my rival—the guy makes my skin crawl."

Letting go of the wall, Subaru dropped back down to the ground of the plaza. Seeing him land without a sound, Regulus gave a small nod, as if something had clicked into place.

"I see, so that's how it is. Hey, I feel bad saying this, but... you can't butt in on fated lovers. Don't you think unrequited jealousy is a pretty ugly thing?"

"Shut up! Emilia-tan is my wife. Like hell I'd hand her over to the likes of you."

"Oh, so her name is Emilia. Nice name. Suits her perfectly—the sort of name you'd want to whisper the way you coo over a little songbird, for such a lovely girl."

"You didn't even know her name... What could you possibly have seen in her to spout nonsense about her being your bride...!"

"Her face."

Speechless. Subaru's throat closed up from sheer fury. Misreading that silence as something else, Regulus tilted his head and said,

"Her face is cute. Isn't that basically all love is?"

"Drop dead."

"He really ought to die, I suppose."

Beatrice echoed Subaru's fury, rejecting Regulus's far too flippant notion of love. Charging forward, Subaru's body carried the ongoing effect of Muraku. Riding the wind, he closed the distance in an instant, and Regulus, startled, opened his eyes wide.

He probably couldn't understand why Subaru would still choose to close in for melee combat against him, even after all this. Subaru understood it too—the sheer folly of closing the distance on Regulus. Unlike Sirius, Regulus carried no obvious weapon. That wasn't a sign he lacked the means; the correct conclusion was that he simply had no need of one. Closing in on Regulus, then, was truly a life-or-death gamble. And yet there was exactly one reason Subaru still chose to engage him at close range.

—Because his own trump card, too, could only be used in close combat.

"I really can't understand why you'd throw yourself in here. Not that I need to, but if there's some meaning to it besides sheer desperation, I'd love to hear it. See, I'm the kind of person who never forgets the consideration of wanting to understand someone all the way to the end—even someone about to scatter into pieces."

"Thanks for the lecture— Beako!"

"Ready, I suppose!"

As Subaru closed in, Regulus casually reached out his left hand toward him. The five spread fingers were, in all likelihood, each a lethal weapon capable of reaping a life on its own. Faced with them, Subaru drew a breath, and shouted.

One of the fruits that he and Beatrice had built up together over this past year.

"—E! M! M!!"

"...What?"

That roaring, howled incantation resonated Mana from within Subaru's broken Gate through to Beatrice, and from there manifested a wholly new kind of spell, unlike anything else in this world. It was the activation of one of only three original spells the Natsuki Subaru and Beatrice combo had ever created—the absolute defense spell, "E.M.M."

An invisible field of magic enveloped Subaru's body, "shifting" his existence half a step out of this world, and thereby completely nullifying any attack against him, physical or magical alike.

Regulus's fingertips, too, reached Subaru without inflicting the slightest damage. Witnessing that with his own eyes, Regulus's face finally froze in genuine surprise, for the first time. Aiming for that very cheek, Subaru swung his left fist with everything he had.

"Take this!"

"—!"

Regulus's cheek snapped back from the blow. Subaru's face twisted at the full-force impact he felt in his own fist, but Regulus's face, springing right back as if nothing had happened, showed not even a trace of redness. Total damage nullification. It seemed as though something functioning much like what Subaru himself had just done was permanently protecting Regulus's body.

"Is the charge not ready yet, I wonder!"

Before Regulus's counterattack could land, Beatrice shouted that the condition for their next move hadn't yet been met. At close range like this, Subaru needed to dodge whatever Regulus threw at him. Evading, defending—either option was difficult. Which meant shaving away at his soul instead—

"Don't get too full of yours—"

"Invisible Providence!!"

An invisible fist came up from directly below, striking Regulus's irritated face as he turned toward them. His words cut off in astonishment, Regulus went flying. Watching him fly, Subaru spat out a mouthful of bloody phlegm welling up in his throat and wiped roughly at his mouth with his sleeve.

To eyes other than Subaru's, that invisible will of god must have been unseeable. But Subaru could see it clearly—a black arm thrusting out from his own chest, a torrent of accursed power that might as well be called a third arm.

His whole body groaned, his soul shaved away, and he coughed up blood gone black as if poison had spread through him. Paying that much of a price was what it took just to land one all-out blow. And even that "all-out" was only relative to Subaru; as a rock-shattering punch, it wouldn't come close to Garfiel's level. Even so, an invisible strike ought to carry some effect of its own.

"Subaru, are you all right, I wonder?"

"Cough... somehow. Besides, that guy—his power looks terrifying, but his offense is pathetic. Out of everyone I've faced so far, he's about on par with Rachins."

Coughing up blood that clung to his throat, Subaru pointed out how low Regulus's fighting ability was. Regulus's combat technique was crude enough, amateurish enough, that even Subaru could hold his own against it. Even those lethal fingertips might be something he could keep dodging, so long as his concentration held out.

"…………"

A tap on his shoulder. A silent report from Beatrice that the charge was complete.

Given how powerful and effective the original spells were, they couldn't be used in rapid succession. On top of that, all three types of spell could, in principle, be used only once a day each—any more than that, and the body itself would be lost.

"E.M.M.'s used up, but at this rate I can close in even without magic. And if I can land that again..."

"There might be a chance of winning. A light's starting to show, I suppose."

"There's no such thing anywhere. Sorry if I gave you that impression, though. Even I don't find it particularly fun to keep being made a fool of. Actually, 'fun' isn't even the issue. My will not getting through—that's just no good. It's an infringement of my rights. I've already let you land two hits on me, so isn't it about time one of my attacks connected too? Otherwise it's just unfair, don't you think?"

Landing back down from midair, Regulus glared at Subaru and Beatrice as they steeled their resolve anew. His expression had lost its initial composure, passing beyond mere displeasure into outright indignation. At last, it seemed, Regulus too had come to recognize Subaru as an enemy. With that, their original objective— and it was right about then that—

"Whoa!?"

"...What do you think you're doing?"

Between Subaru and Regulus, glaring at each other, the ground between them suddenly burst into flame. Subaru recoiled, feeling the searing heat wave scorch his face head-on, while Regulus took the resulting hot gust with a scowl.

Naturally, both their gazes turned toward the one responsible—Sirius. For whatever reason, the monster had, up until this point, watched Subaru's fight with Regulus purely as a bystander. What intent lay behind her silence, no one knew, but it would have been best if she'd simply kept staying quiet—and now, out of nowhere, she'd jumped into the fray.

Subtly, Subaru had no real means of defending against Sirius's flame, which honestly made her someone he wanted to deal with even less than Regulus, who was limited to melee. On top of that, she was an opponent whose conditions for defeat he hadn't even worked out yet. As the situation threatened to spiral further out of control, Subaru held his breath.

But the situation worsened far beyond anything Subaru could have imagined.

"—Found you."

"—?"

Sirius stood stock-still, staring fixedly at the two of them glaring back—no, at Subaru alone. The monster had left no attention whatsoever for the murderous Regulus; her gaze bore down single-mindedly on Subaru instead. That gaze carried a tinge of madness, and Subaru's throat went dry in an instant. And then the monster raised both hands, which had been hanging limp at her sides, and cupped her own cheeks between them, saying:

"Found you. Found you. Found you. Found you. Ah, aaah! Aaaahhh! Yes, it really is you, after all! I'm sorry, I'm sorry I didn't notice sooner, okay? I'm sorry? Oh, thank goodness. That's right, isn't it. You really did come back to me, didn't you!?"

"What are you...?"

"There you were, all along!? I looked everywhere and couldn't find you, I even tore apart every one of your spares and you weren't anywhere, I searched and searched and searched and searched and searched and searched and searched and searched and searched and searched and searched and searched and searched and searched and searched for you... no, it's because you noticed I was searching like that, that you came back to me!"

Her voice, cracking as it climbed high, carried an unmistakable fever. Still cupping her cheeks, writhing, swaying her body side to side, Sirius's voice bubbled with elation. Just what state that was, was hard to put into words looking at the bizarre spectacle of the monster before him. But if forced to name whatever it most closely resembled, it was this—the behavior of a woman blinded by love, who has just glimpsed the face of the lover she's been searching for.

"Because my feelings reached you! Because you finally noticed how I'd always, always wished to become one with you! Because my 'love' reached you!"

"…………"

"I've been waiting, waaaiting, only for you, this whole time... my beloved, beloved Petelgeuse!"

So saying, with a manically blissful smile, Sirius Romanée-Conti called out the name of her late husband, and turned eyes brimming with passion upon Subaru.
