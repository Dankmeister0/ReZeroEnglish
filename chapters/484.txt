Arc 6 Chapter 78: "Four Corners"

"—Listen up. Right now, this tower's in serious trouble. How serious? Picture an Othello board where all four corners have already been taken."

Ram's expression darkened as she watched Subaru hold up a single finger and launch into his explanation with a grave face. She wasn't the only one turning serious — Emilia and Beatrice, listening to the same explanation, did too.

"Othello" — strictly speaking, "Reversi" would be the more diplomatically correct name to use — is a board game in which players use black and white pieces to fight over control of an 8×8, sixty-four-square board.

Naturally, it was a fragment of knowledge from the original world Subaru knew, but since it was relatively easy to reproduce here, it had been brought to Roswaal Manor fairly early on, sparking a minor sensation — and earning particularly rave reviews from Emilia.

Which was why, even with this quick explanation, the gravity of it came through loud and clear to Emilia and the others. In Othello, having all four corners taken meant the game was as good as over.

For what it's worth, Ram hadn't played much, but on the rare occasions she did, she was frighteningly good — quite literally demonic, in her case, and small wonder.

Anyway—

"All four corners gone is an outrageous situation, I suppose... Even as a handicap, that's simply too much, I suppose."

"If I got that many corners, even klutzy me, all in a flap, might actually win..."

"'In a flap'? Nobody says that anymore..."

"Later. Later."

Emilia had latched onto Subaru's obligatory quip, and now looked disappointed at having her request turned down. A pang of guilt sprouted in him at the sight — but as he kept telling himself, flirting could wait.

Breaking the current deadlock came first. And to that end—

"The four corners... are the two Gluttony Archbishops inside the tower, and the swarm of witchbeasts they've called in. And then — this one's hard to say — one of our own comrades... ah, well, whether 'comrade' is even the right word is up for debate."

"—?"

Reaching for the fourth corner, he hesitated for a moment over how to continue.

It was because he couldn't settle on a clear answer, even in his own head, for how to describe the woman who now stood, unintentionally, as that fourth corner.

"Acquaintance" didn't feel far off. Their bond wasn't so thin as to be mere allies-of-circumstance thrown together like enemies sharing one boat. But had they truly shared enough fate to be called comrades? At least for Subaru, maybe the best description was an unwitting, unofficial master-and-disciple bond.

But what about for Emilia and the others?

But into that gap of Subaru's hesitation—

"Comrade — did something happen to Shaula?"

Regardless of whether Emilia's "memories" were intact or not, there was no discrepancy in their perception on this point. Proof that, in their minds, Shaula's standing had been etched in as something friendly.

"Am I an idiot? No — I am an idiot."

Tripping over the obvious was a bad habit of Natsuki Subaru's. He reflected on it deeply. And turning that deep reflection into fuel for doing better — that was one of Natsuki Subaru's good points.

For every one criticism, one compliment. Ideally, for every one criticism, a hundred compliments.

"Exactly right."

Under that tremendous force, the giant scorpion's massive body was knocked clean off the floor and sent flying. Rolling spectacularly across the stone floor, dragging witchbeasts caught in its path along the way, its huge frame went right over the edge of the balcony and was flung out into the sky above the sand sea.

But the giant scorpion wasn't about to just take it. Its tail stinger, thrust out on reflex, plunged into the watchtower's wall, hurling its huge body back upward as if flinging it. Twisting itself in midair, the giant scorpion tried fiercely to make its way back to the balcony.

"—Muraku."

What obstructed that return was a single incantation, spun out in a single voice.

Directional mana interfering with reality brought about an effect on the world that would never occur naturally. The result it produced this time was the obstruction of the giant scorpion's return.

Caught up in the wind, the giant scorpion tried to land on the balcony in one grand motion. Its body, however, was carried along by the buffeting sandy gale, veering wildly off its trajectory toward the balcony.

For a body as massive as the giant scorpion's — surely no less than several hundred kilograms — to be swept along by the wind, just how strong would that gale have to be? But that wasn't what was happening.

"Muraku," a form of dark magic, was a spell that manipulated the gravity acting on its target.

In short: if the giant scorpion's weight became as light as dust, it stood to reason that its bulk would no longer move the way its size suggested it should. That's all that had been done. Nothing more.

"—!!"

The giant scorpion let out a pained cry, thrown back out beyond the balcony once again. In that instant, it swung its tail stinger, giving up on returning and instead trying to snipe at this side from atop the tower.

But that, too, was a beat too late.

"Wingdrake-chan!"

At the girl's high-pitched command, an attack came pouring down on the giant scorpion as it was swept away by the wind. Diving in was a witchbeast that had sprouted something like bird feathers, its head mostly turned to horn. It slammed into the giant scorpion's outer shell again and again, dealing no real damage, but the impact shoved the giant scorpion far off into the distance, further and further from the tower.

"—"

Even the mighty giant scorpion couldn't keep itself airborne without support. Carried along by the wind, the moment the spell's effect wore off, it plummeted headfirst toward the ground.

A fall from several hundred meters up — of course, not enough to finish off an opponent like this. But it was more than enough to buy time. Using that brief window—

"Meili! Echidna!"

"Natsuki-kun! You've recovered—no?"

At Subaru's call as he ran over, Echidna raised her face and knit her brow. Wearing Anastasia's pensive expression, she went on, "Could it be—"

"By any chance, has your 'memory' come back?"

"You catch on fast! But how'd you know?"

"Oh, I'd know. Not with Beatrice right beside you wearing a face that smug."

Where Echidna jerked her chin, there was Beatrice, hand in hand with Subaru, wearing an utterly triumphant look.

Sure enough, seeing this, the turn for the better in Beatrice's heart was plain as day — and that honest inability of hers to hide anything was truly lovely.

Of course, there was no doubt Echidna's insight was excellent too, and with that same insight she looked past Subaru to Emilia, who'd kicked the giant scorpion flying,

"And as for her, the one I don't recognize..."

"It's Gluttony's Authority. What happened to her is the same as what happened to Julius — everyone around her forgets, but she herself remembers. Her name's Emilia. My tough, adorable princess."

"Thanks for the quick rundown. Still, things are in serious chaos both inside and outside the tower. Mind if I ask what the plan is?"

"Yeah, I've already got the answer ready for that."

Behind Subaru as he nodded deeply, Emilia spread both arms wide and deployed countless shards of ice into the sky above. Loosed all at once, that tremendous force struck the ground and the witchbeasts flying through midair alike.

Subaru couldn't see it, but somewhere below, a follow-up strike was probably lodging itself in the falling giant scorpion too. Still, even that was a drop in the bucket against the sheer numbers of witchbeasts—

"Echidna, your job's the inside! For now, join up with Julius, who's fighting on the second level! I've already told Emilia what to do from there!"

"—. Going with her is fine by me, but what about you and the others, Natsuki-kun?"

"Betty and Subaru's job is already decided, I suppose."

Only a moment's thought, and Beatrice answered Echidna's question. She glanced up at Subaru's profile beside her, tightening her grip on his hand,

"We'll be taking on that giant scorpion right here, I suppose. Since we have to hold back and be careful not to actually finish her off, honestly, it's a real chore, I suppose."

"Talking big, this one. That's my Bea-ko for you."

"Ehe, silly me, I suppose."

Having talked big enough to fire up both herself and her contractor, Beatrice stuck out her tongue.

Echidna's eyes went wide at that exchange between them, and then she shook her head. Better, it seemed, to entrust herself to the method she believed best than to spend time arguing about it.

That knack for cutting her losses and moving on — well, no wonder, it was right on par with Anastasia's.

"At every turn in this tower, it's been Natsuki-kun's judgment that's saved us — that much is certain. So betting on you here, too, is our best shot at winning... or so I judge."

"That's an honor. Makes me all the more sorry you happen to be a creature named Echidna."

"As for how strongly my creator's influence shows through — once this whole affair's settled, I'd like a proper sit-down to discuss it. —Any message for Julius?"

"—"

Trading light jabs, Echidna, just as she turned to go, added that last question.

Even now, through the effect of "Cor Leonis," he could feel it without end — Julius, still fighting one level up. Thinking about it, this meant he'd now linked up with every ally in the tower except Julius himself.

Julius, too, had been one of those worried about Subaru after he'd lost his "memories," so Subaru felt he really ought to report that they'd come back—

"Nah, nothing in particular."

As things stood, there were no words Natsuki Subaru needed to give to Julius Juukulius, currently fighting Reid Astrea.

To begin with, Subaru's whole strategy, under these worst-case circumstances, was about putting people in their optimal positions. He was going around calling out to people because reassignment was needed — but there was no such need for Julius.

—Julius's post was none other than Reid Astrea himself.

"On that point, I guess you'd have to say he's something else. Even without me saying a word, he's the one who ended up going to face Reid, after all."

Thanks to that, he'd been able to exchange words with Shaula before she became the giant scorpion.

In that final moment, the strength Shaula's familiar declaration of love had given him was boundless. An absolute, unshakeable resolve to save her lit a hot fire in his chest.

So—

"—Whatever I ought to say, the me from a little while ago, and the me before that, already said all of it. There's nothing left to add at this point. That guy's Julius Juukulius, after all."

That an unyielding "fate" truly existed in this world — Natsuki Subaru had, without question, come to accept that.

The many troubles surrounding this watchtower were unavoidable, certain to bare their fangs at Subaru and the others. But being unavoidable and being insurmountable were two entirely different things.

If it was "fate" that Reid Astrea stood in Julius's way, then what came after was fated too.

The one to cut down the resurrected Reid Astrea would be Julius Juukulius.

"—. Understood. I'll pass that along, just as it is."

"Ah, actually there is one thing. Everyone else has got their hands full too, so wrap it up fast and go help cover for someone else."

Waving a hand, Subaru had a part in the wry smile on Echidna's face as she ran off. Watching her go, Subaru turned his gaze to where she was headed, toward Emilia, who was cutting down two witchbeasts with a blade of ice.

"Emilia-tan! Just like we planned! I'm counting on you, Emilia-tan!"

"Right, leave it to me! And Subaru, you'd better not go dying, okay!"

"You bet!"

At Emilia's altogether obvious words, Subaru nonetheless raised his fist, putting into it a conviction well beyond the ordinary.

I don't want to die. —That basic principle remained unchanged, but layered with reasons beyond it, the danger of "Return by Death" under these circumstances was beyond imagining.

If the restart point hadn't changed, then Subaru would return to a stage before he'd separated Rui Arneb, Witch Factor and all, from himself. If he returned to that point, exactly what kind of trouble it would cause was something neither Subaru nor anyone else could know.

Had Rui vanished from inside Subaru, or not? The "Corridor of Memories" was a kind of extraterritorial zone, but how would the effects of "Return by Death" play out there? Everything about it was hazy.

In other words—

"—This time, I'm putting an end to it!"

Following Subaru's declaration, Emilia grabbed hold of Echidna and left the balcony.

They to their battlefield. And Subaru and the others to theirs.

And so—

"So theeen? I'm totally lost here 'cause nobody's explained a thiiing, but you're gonna properly explain it to meee, right?"

Meili, who'd held her tongue patiently and hadn't butted into the conversation with Echidna, timed it so that once only Subaru and Beatrice remained on the balcony, she called out to him like that.

Giving Meili a thumbs-up, Subaru bared his teeth in a wicked grin.

At this eleventh hour, there was only one reason Subaru hadn't said anything to Meili — same as with Julius, she too was already exactly where she needed to be, on the battlefield best suited to her.

"Sorry, but you're stuck with us, Meili. The three of us — me, Bea-ko, and you — are going to hold down the witchbeast swarm and the giant scorpion — two of the corners!"

"That's not an explanation at aaall!"

"—Here it comes, I suppose!"

There was no time left to give a detailed explanation to Meili, who shook her braids and puffed her cheeks out. The very next moment, as if answering Beatrice's cry, a massive form leapt up from the tower's outer wall onto the balcony.

It was a being with a jet-black shell, savage great pincers, and red compound eyes—

"So. Four hundred years a shut-in, huh."

Breathing just slightly hard, Subaru scooped Beatrice up beside him in one motion. Then, standing alongside Meili as she ran up right next to him, he faced the giant scorpion head-on.

Facing the enemy glaring silently back at him, he twisted his cheek into a grin,

"This is a shut-in showdown. She's got four hundred years, solo. We've got four hundred and one, between the two of us."

"Wouldn't four hundred and twoo, between the three of us, be more correeect?"

"Point is, we win!"

"Nothing about that made sense except the winning part, I suppose!!"

In his arms, Beatrice barked back at the well-matched banter between Subaru and Meili. And then, kneaded by just the right amount of tension, Subaru glared at the giant scorpion and let out a breath.

With this, the situation settled into the matchup they'd expected. All that was left—

"—I'm counting on you for real, Emilia-tan. You're the key to all of it."

—The battle against the "Four Corners" surrounding the Pleiades Watchtower now began in earnest.
