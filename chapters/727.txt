Arc 9 Chapter 31: "One Hundred Percent Effort"


　――Accompanied by visual effects and a roar so immense one might think the heavens themselves had split open, a colossal mass of rock plummeted.

　There was ample room for doubt as to whether it could simply be called a "mass of rock."
　After all, it was a chunk of stone easily over a hundred meters in size. Rather than a mere boulder or rock, calling it a rocky mountain suspended in the sky was far more fitting.
　Fissures spiderwebbed across that rocky mountain, shattering the hundred-meter mass into pieces, which rained down upon the ground as countless smaller—yet still five- to ten-meter-wide—stony projectiles.

　The impact site of these stony projectiles was the cityscape of the Royal Capital, with casualties conservatively estimated at anywhere between one thousand and ten thousand.
　If one were to commit mass murder on such a scale, the only ones in the present era who could compare in terms of kill rate would be the "Blue Lightning" Cecilus Segmunt, or perhaps the Archbishops of Greed and Sloth.
　However—

"If you don't limit it strictly to the current era, there used to be quite a few back in the day."

　His voice steeped in exhaustion with a tinge of exasperation, Aldebaran gazed down upon the scene from atop the ramparts.
tPlan B—the tactic of dropping the rocky mountain in question—was a cowardly scheme used by Aldebaran after he was cornered, all to create an opening for his escape.
tConsidering the circumstances that led him to rely on such a tactic, Aldebaran should not have lingered leisurely; he ought to have turned tail, committed fully to running away, ignored the agonizing screams, and dashed out of the Royal Capital.
tHowever, Aldebaran could not do that. —He had a responsibility to see it through to the end.

"—Impressive."

tDeep inside his helmet, his narrowed dark eyes reflected a sight that drew a raspy murmur of praise from his lips.
tMatching the sound and spectacle when the mountain began its heavenly descent, the screaming of the world was overwritten just as flashily, as beautiful towers of ice rising from the ground intercepted the countless boulders one after another.
tDespite their cold, frozen appearance, they were hands of salvation filled with warm mercy and affection. At the same time, the ice towers, gleaming pale blue as they caught the light, seemed to reflect the deep sorrow of their caster.

"Whaaat, isn't thinking like that a bit too poetic, pops?"

tThus Roy, carried over Al's shoulder, mocked Aldebaran as he watched the scene unfold.
tThough Roy's limbs had been snapped to render him immobile, he used his back muscles to deftly raise his upper body, taking in the cataclysm striking the Royal Capital over Aldebaran's shoulder.
tIt was exasperating how he ran his mouth so glibly despite being unable to do anything.

"You're the one getting all poetic with your commentary on my inner thoughts. I don't know either of you all that well, but doesn't that style suit your dead counterpart who played at being a 'Gourmet' better than you, the 'Bizarre Eater'?"

"Haha! Isn't attacking someone over their dead family proof of a terrible upbringing? In truth, the one who liked to rate and review what he ate was Lye, not us. Isn't it strange? Even though the destination for everything we eat ends up in the same 'Stomach of the Soul' anyway."

"Talk about a thief being brazen—or in your case, a gluttonous thief."

"Aww, don't go treating us like a thief. We just want to eat a tiny bit more than a full stomach... We're just cute little epicures of that level, you know."

tAlways having an answer for everything. Given the nature of the Authority of Gluttony, the depth of vocabulary and rhetorical strength of this talkative "Bizarre Eater" were among the best not just among Archbishops, but in the entire world.
tIt was easy to be misled by his childish appearance and his speech pattern that laid bare his rotten nature, but one must never forget that the creature carried on his shoulder was a monster of collective intelligence that had devoured countless stores of knowledge and experience.
tWhich was precisely why Al had given up on verbal persuasion and chosen instead to force submission through violence and curse marks.

"...At any rate, our business here is done. Let's go."

"Yeah, since there's no need to bail out the big sister who let some rocks slip through anymore. Hey pops, don't people tell you pretty often that you're an oddball—or rather, a total freak?"

"Thanks for the overall evaluation of my past two or three days."

tRoy had correctly deduced the reason for Aldebaran's unannounced delay and tacked on an unnecessary comment at the end; having lost any desire to engage with him properly, Al gave that dry response.
tAnd so, after confirming the state of the Royal Capital where damage had been contained thanks to Emilia—specifically the nobles' district, one section of which had been completely flattened—Aldebaran hastened his steps once more.

t—Through his battle with Valga Cromwell, Aldebaran had revised his mindset, recognizing that even against his own Authority, which he had judged to leave him with no equals, there were formidable foes who could bite back.

tEven now, his conviction that no one could defeat him remained unshaken.
tHowever, he also admonished himself that to elevate that conviction into an absolute truth, he could not rest on the laurels of his Authority and stop thinking.

tHence, during the jailbreak operation for "Gluttony" after sneaking into the Royal Capital, he did not choose easy retries, but instead approached every single Aldebaran life with painstaking care—like squeezing out the last drop of toothpaste from a tube—tackling each attempt with everything he had, finally bringing the count to a close at 6,724.

t—Whether one considered that count high or low depended on the individual, but fundamentally, Aldebaran viewed whatever count he reached as "appropriate."

tNo matter the number of digits in the count, not a single life was wasted; each was an attempt required to overcome the obstacle.
tOn extremely rare occasions, there were attempts spent idly just to let his heart catch its breath, but even those mental breathers were indispensable attempts.
tTherefore, regardless of the count, so long as he achieved the desired outcome, Aldebaran would insist that those 6,724 attempts were the "optimal" number of tries.


"—Yo, damn helmet bastard."

tThe prearranged rendezvous point—located a good distance away from the Royal Capital—was a long-abandoned quarry, making it an ideal meeting place for villains wishing to avoid public gaze.
tIn that quarry, leaning her back against a crumbling stone wall, Felt greeted Aldebaran with that harsh hail, her red eyes gleaming bright with rage and hostility.
tHer lips curled to reveal a sharp canine, her arms crossed, she displayed her utter contempt for Aldebaran. Even her address for him had been demoted from "helmet bastard" to "damn helmet bastard."
tFelt jerked her slender chin, pointing toward the Royal Capital visible in the distance.

"How many people do ya think would've died if Emilia-nee didn't stop that giant rock? What happened to your rule about not lettin' anyone die? Ya gonna bend your rules every time ya hit a wall? You couldn't be any lamer."

"...Yeah. I'm glad to see you're safe too, Miss Felt."

"Haah?"

"It's a joke. Or casual banter, or a total flop of a gag."

tAldebaran answered feebly as Felt grew even pricklier at his response. Perceiving his attitude as insincere, Felt leaned forward, ready to snap at him, when Roy's voice chirped from Al's shoulder:

"I'd love to say 'keep it up, keep it up,' but if you're gonna keep nagging pops, we've already done our prep work on our end. So why not change the flavor with a different seasoning?"

"Change the flavor my ass. ...So you're the 'Gluttony' he captured. The older brother? The younger brother? Either way, you've got the same annoying face as your brother."

"Ah, so you know about us? Well, I guess that makes sense. We heard from Rui, too, you know? That Lye got indigestion because of you, Felt-chan."

"You talkin' about when he picked a fight with me over my name or whatever? Served him right."

tFelt, whose venomous tongue grew even sharper, had previously encountered "Gluttony"—Lye Batenkaitos—in Priestella. Though her comment mocked what had happened then, including his deceased brother, Roy simply laughed merrily, "Haha!", at her sarcasm, just as he had with Aldebaran.

"By the way, among us, we don't even know which of us was the eldest brother. For us, there were times we felt like being the eldest son, and times we felt like being the second son, so maybe that's why."

"Don't go spouting nonsense that makes no sense. The order of siblings isn't decided by mood. Choose whether to give or receive before something's missing."

"Hahaa! You and pops both think you can say whatever you want just because you're talkin' to a hated Archbishop, don't ya? It's fine, it's fine, that's everyone's shared consensus, after all, isn't it?"

"Hey, damn helmet bastard, I ain't plannin' on having a friendly chat with this guy. Shut his jagged-toothed mouth up right now and answer my question."

"I agree he's noisy, but hold on a bit with the Q&A. Before that..."

tCutting off Felt's demand, Aldebaran turned his head, surveying the quarry. Sensing the intent behind his gesture of scanning the quarry, Felt clicked her tongue with a "Tch" and jerked her chin. Guided toward the back of the quarry, where a shabby shack barely retained its original shape—

"—Al-sama."

"――――"

tJust then, Yae emerged from the shack, and her eyes met Aldebaran's.
tYae was accompanied by a faintly warm aroma; she had likely been preparing a meal inside the shack. Her thoughtfulness was appreciated. Appreciated, but—

"Judging by your appearance, did you get beaten pretty badly?"

tWith a single glance, Aldebaran sensed the contrast between her current state and her usual aloof, elusive demeanor.
tHer custom-made maid outfit infused with Waso style was torn in various places, and her red hair, which she never failed to groom even on the battlefield, was disheveled. Above all, it was her expression.
tSolemn, perhaps; an air devoid of playful innocence detracted most from her usual self.
tAs proof of that—

"...Thank goodness."

tYae gently placed a hand over her chest and murmured those words upon Aldebaran's arrival.
tSlightly taken aback by the emotional hue in her voice, yet choosing not to address it directly, Aldebaran shrugged his shoulders with an "Oy, oy."

"That's surprisingly meek for you, Yae-san, our all-purpose useful maid. For you to be unable to support me, what kind of surprise guest were you flirting with?"

"—. Calling it flirting sounds terrible, Al-sama. As you well know, Yae-chan's heart is completely under your thumb."

tErasing the brief hesitation in her response, Yae spoke in her usual business-as-usual manner. Stroking her chest gently with a finger, she continued:

"But, but... it is true that I spent a passionate time with a gentleman~. The one who came to the mansion was the 'Sword Demon' Wilhelm van Astrea... no, Wilhelm Trias-sama."

"The 'Sword Demon' old man, huh. Well..."

tAn unexpected assassin like that must have shocked Yae as well. However, he was hardly the worst possible adversary.
tTo be sure, he was undeniably one of the Kingdom's top fighters, but as a swordsman, he was a far easier opponent for Yae to handle than a mage. What's more, the Barielle detached villa had been rigged with her traps, making it an ideal hunting ground for the "Red Cherry Blossom."
tYet, since "Aldebaran" had ultimately rushed to her rescue, the "Sword Demon's" sharpness had surpassed Yae's. Judging by that expression of hers, it wasn't even a close loss, but a complete defeat.
tOr perhaps the reason for that razor-sharp performance was—

"That old man moping over there isn't unrelated either, is he?"

tWhere Aldebaran directed his gaze, sitting beside the shack on a discarded wood-chopping stump, hugging one knee with his head lowered, was the red-haired man—Heinkel.
tLimp with his head hanging low, Heinkel hadn't even wiped away the red blood staining his entire body. Whether it was his own blood or blood splattered from his opponent was impossible to tell from here.

"What happened?"

"...My consciousness was hazy, so I did not see it clearly with my own eyes, but..."

"But?"

"Heinkel-sama slew the 'Sword Demon'-sama."

"――――"

tAt Yae's uncertain answer, Aldebaran gasped and looked back at Heinkel once more.
tIt went without saying that Aldebaran was well aware of the relationship between Heinkel and Wilhelm. In short, it was a confrontation between father and son—and what's more, a story of the son slaying his father.
tHe also knew about Heinkel's fatal flaw as a swordsman, but—

"Stuck perpetually between your son and your father, huh, old man?"

tEven Aldebaran didn't know the core of the feud within House Astrea.
tHe merely understood that the existence of the Divine Protection of the Sword Saint and the title of "Sword Saint" had generated friction across three generations—parent, child, and grandchild—that could never be smoothed over. And that friction had surely become even more absolute through this incident.

"――――"

tHaving placed himself in complete opposition to both Reinhard and Wilhelm in just the past few days, Heinkel's mental state was something Aldebaran and his group had no right to worry about.
tThey were the ones who had dragged him into this to use him. Asking if he was okay would sound hollow and hypocritical. Therefore, Aldebaran turned his gaze back to Yae and asked:

"So, what about the old man? If you say he was slain, and that blood on the old man is splatter..."

『—I gave him the bare minimum treatment, for what it's worth. We couldn't stay long, so literally the bare minimum.』

"Whoa?!"

tStartled by the sudden voice, Aldebaran jerked his shoulders and looked around. However, there was no sign anywhere of the "Aldebaran" who was presumably the owner of that voice. It was the Dragonhusk of the "Divine Dragon." It shouldn't be a size you could miss—or so he thought, right at that moment.

『Sorry, sorry. Canceling optical camouflage for a sec.』

tNo sooner had he said that than in the space where nothing had been before—chin resting on the stone wall where Felt leaned her back—a massive dragon body appeared, leaning limply. Watching it emerge as if the blurred air were gradually taking on color, Aldebaran widened his eyes in surprise:

"Optical camouflage, huh? So you were turning invisible by refracting light. You can actually do something like that?"

『Adjusting it in real time while moving is impossible, mind you. But if it's strictly while staying still, it pretty much erases the drawback of this massive body, doesn't it?』

"Yeah. An invisible 'Dragon' is pretty damn terrifying, I'll give you that."

tWhile magic to bend light refraction was achievable by combining Yang and Wind Magic, maintaining it constantly consumed far too much energy. Blending into the surrounding scenery required extremely precise magic control, making it a feat with a high probability of falling short of the desired standard.
tIn fact, because even "Aldebaran" found it difficult to execute, he had been made to wait on standby high in the sky over the Royal Capital, concealed within the clouds.

"The fact that you've managed to do it now... means it must've been quite a fierce battle."

『No less than yours. Honestly, if it hadn't been for the old man's backstab, a streak of weakness crossed my mind thinking I might've lost.』

"A backstab, huh..."

tSlowly shaking his head, Aldebaran processed his twofold surprise.
tOne was the sheer terror of the "Sword Demon," who had forced "Aldebaran" to imagine defeat. Though the software installed in him was different, his hardware specs were unmistakably those of the "Divine Dragon"; to reach that level even after fighting consecutive battles including Yae made him a total monster.
tAnd the other, of course, was Heinkel's method of intervening in that fight—

"Even winning head-on wouldn't have neatly solved things, but settling it with a backstab... the old man's mental state must be completely shattered."

"Well, I believe that is undoubtedly true~. After all, from the moment we fled the Royal Capital until now, he hasn't spoken to us even once."

"I see..."

tReceiving confirmation from Yae that Heinkel's condition was just as severe as expected, Aldebaran let out a long breath and looked around at everyone present once more.
tA disheartened Yae down from defeat, and "Aldebaran" shaken by the "Sword Demon." Heinkel crouched in shock after stabbing his own father, and Felt making no attempt to hide her hostility. Plus Roy in high spirits atop his shoulder, and Aldebaran himself—a heinous villain barreling straight down the path of cowardice.
tA terrible group of people, and in pretty battered shape to boot.

"—Even so, everyone's alive."

tThat everyone gathered in the quarry had survived brought a looseness to Aldebaran's shoulders. Quietly, he deactivated his Domain and restarted a new matrix.
tThe restart point updated; with this, the events in the Royal Capital were etched permanently into the history of this world.
tWhich meant that even the tears that flowed from her blue-purple eyes were now beyond undoing.

"But that's fine. The moment I decided to betray everything, making a convenient exception for just one person was off the table."

tNo compromises. No allowing his heart to waver. Any slip-up, and everything would be ruined.
tTo conclude that all the pain endured by this body and heart had meaning, Aldebaran and his group could neither fail nor stop in their tracks.

"Yae, if you've got food ready, give me some. I'm starving."

"I couldn't bring food out from the mansion, so I stole from shops along the way. I took notes on which shops I took what from, so please pay them back later."

"You're shrewd as ever... Got it. Also, take this guy."

"Should I tie him up and hang him? If so, I've already done it."

tAs Yae spoke, moving her raised fingers smoothly, Aldebaran's shoulder suddenly felt lighter. Roy's body, which he had been carrying, was suspended by steel threads and hung against the quarry's stone wall.

"What's this, what's this, this treatment again? Well, I guess it's better than hanging upside down, though."

"Stay like that for a while. Once I figure out how to discipline you, I'll fix your limbs. However—"

"'Don't forget the curse mark,' right? Sounds good! We'd never say we won't scheme anything even if our mouths were ripped open, but for now, we'll behave and evaluate you guys, see!"

"Evaluate us, huh."

"Maybe it's because Lye died? We don't have the slightest intention of taking over as the 'Gourmet,' but we thought we'd take a bit more interest in what happens before things land on the plate, that's all!"

tBound hand and foot, suspended in a cramped, arched position like a bent shrimp, Roy swayed his body. Shrugging at the creepiness of it, Aldebaran exchanged glances with "Aldebaran."
tThe possessor of the same self pulled back his massive jaw, promising not to take his eyes off him.

"By the way, Plan B was a lifesaver. Thanks to that, I managed to get away."

『Ah, so the Origin went with that name. I was thinking 'Second Plan.' Well, I'm glad our ideas landed in the same spot.』

"Origin?"

『Calling us 'the other me' is getting confusing, right? So I thought 'Origin' for the original. How about 'Direct Line' for me?』

"Original and Direct Line... sound like ramen shop styles."

"You knew pretty quickly that it was Miss Emilia who stopped that giant rock."

"Huh? You makin' fun of me? Ain't nobody gonna do somethin' that stupid except Emilia-nee."

"With a phrasing like that, makes me wonder who's really making fun of who... but you're right. I dropped that rock banking on Miss Emilia catching every bit of it."

"...You're gonna get more than just a kick in the ass for this."

"...What happened between the old man and the 'Sword Demon' isn't your fault, Felt-chan."

"I don't think for a second it's my fault. It's definitely yours... yours, and Reinhard's dad and granddad. But I'm still pissed off, and just thinkin' about what kind of face that idiot's gonna make when he finds out makes me even more pissed."

"――――"

"The moment I made that guy my knight, pretendin' that stuff involvin' him has nothin' to do with me don't hold water. Remember that, damn helmet bastard."

"Even if you did somethin' thinkin' it's your own responsibility, there ain't no way people won't group it together with that princess. What you're doin' is kickin' a corpse."

"Priscilla isn't anywhere anymore. Not even a corpse remains."

"—Tch, that's not what I mean...!"

"That IS what it means. To me, that's what life is."

tHis dry response silenced Felt, who was venting her rage.

"Do your absolute best while both you and the other person are alive. That's all I've got to tell you."

"—. Damn helmet bastard... what the hell are you, really?"

"...I'm a chaser star."

"A chaser...?"

"A defective version of the star that shines brightest in the night sky."

tWith that, Aldebaran turned his back on Felt, abandoning the conversation. He walked toward the chopping block, approaching Heinkel.

"――――"

"I'll keep my promise. Once this is done, the Dragon's Blood will definitely be yours."

"—!"

"Without fail...!"

"――――"

"I know. I don't intend to end the world. That's what Gluttony is for."

tFeeling a sharp pain at his neck, Aldebaran answered calmly. While doing so, he lightly raised a hand, waving back Yae, who had begun to react to Heinkel's violent outburst.

"Dammit!"

tFor the time being, he had grasped everyone's condition.

"For now, we should be able to settle down here for a bit. Let's eat Yae's food, rest up a little, and then resume moving. The chaos in the Capital is still continuing—"

tUnable to clap his hands, Aldebaran snapped his fingers instead to gather everyone's attention.

t—That is, if a giant rock hadn't been dropped on them from the sky, cutting off Aldebaran's words.

"Wha—"

tJust as he thought a shadow had fallen from thin clouds, Aldebaran was crushed under immense weight.
tOnly, he felt as though he heard the buzzing of insect wings far in the distance.

t—Otto Suwen was furious.
tHe resolved that he must eliminate that treacherous and tyrannical king. —Otto was furious to the point that the nonsense Subaru had once said about that being the cliché opening phrase when someone got angry flashed through his mind.

tThe cause and target of that rage were already beyond the realm of needing explanation.
tAl—Aldebaran, who had become a heinous criminal—had committed unforgivable deeds. This was true regarding how he betrayed the hearts of those who worried and cared for his mind and body, as well as how he broke the laws set by the Kingdom at a pace approaching nearly a hundred serious offenses alone.
tHowever, what enraged Otto above all else was that Al had turned against Otto's inner circle.

tOtto was well aware that he was a pacifist.
tHe fundamentally disliked conflict, and his creed was to avoid trouble whenever possible. Indeed, back when he was a traveling merchant making nimble journeys with just Frufoo and himself, except when unavoidably dragged into something, he almost never stuck his nose into trouble on his own, nor did he choose violent solutions.

tHowever, after joining the Emilia Camp and stepping into the role of civil official, circumstances changed.
tThough he did not accept Subaru teasingly calling him a "combative civil official," the principle of aiming to resolve matters peacefully and gently was no longer the top priority in his current position.
tCompared to his nimble days as a merchant, the malice directed at him differed by orders of magnitude in both quality and sharpness; continuing with half-baked, passive responses would leave him unable to protect what he wished to protect.

tOn the other hand, he also exercised self-restraint, knowing he could not let go of the awareness that he was heavily relied upon by one of the Royal Selection candidates, keeping it as a self-admonition.
tAs the camp's civil official involved in managing the Mathers domain, Otto possessed the power of status and title that his past self could never have imagined. To avoid getting drunk on it and misunderstanding his own place, Otto disciplined himself daily.

tHowever, that restraint—the product of Otto's constant efforts—came undone the instant someone laid a hand on those close to him.

"Everyone has their reasons. But I have no consideration for an opponent who has no intention or preparation to sit at the negotiating table for them."

tIf they wanted pity, he would pity them. If they wanted charity, he would bestow it.
tThat kind of compassion and kindness was possessed by everyone in the camp, led by Emilia. Therefore, Otto respected the wholesome nature of their hearts to the utmost degree.
tIf, despite that, the enemy trampled on them and exploited them, there was no helping it.

tIf they wanted grace, he would give it. If they wanted support, he would offer it.
tAnd if they wished to be enemies, then he would oppose them to his heart's content.

"Thanks to Emilia-sama, no damage was done to the city. The evacuation guidance for the noble district was also completed in advance. The Royal Capital's Knights are quite competent."

tSorting through the damage reports of what had transpired, Otto clenched his fist.
tBecause he needed to allocate his mental capacity to grasping the situation and checking progress, he could not indulge in outrage. There was merely a deep, painful mortification in his chest—toward his own pathetic, disgraceful showing.

tWhen struck by the initial weight of the unfolding crisis, Otto had miserably lamented his own helplessness, and to make matters worse, was comforted by Emilia. It was completely upside down. Such a thing should never have happened. Otto was in the position where he should have relieved Emilia of her anxieties.
tAnd yet, in reality, Otto had been half in tears while being comforted by Emilia, forced to raise his head by the words of the person he was supposed to support, even having his back patted physically.

tThough he had jokingly told Emilia that Subaru would kill him, in truth Otto felt like strangling himself with his own hands.

tHowever, dead men never get a chance at redemption. Thus Otto did not do anything as foolish as placing his hands on his own neck, but instead racked his brain with all his heart and soul. Fortunately, crying half a bucket of tears had cleared his head a bit. —Although thunder still rolled inside his mind, lightning flashes are useful for straining one's eyes into the depths of darkness.

tThinking back, long ago when it was just Frufoo and him, rushing through a thunderstorm, lightning had struck his cargo bed, burning their entire shipment to ashes.
tWhen Otto lamented that massive misfortune, his beloved dragon had said, "Shouldn't we just be glad that neither I nor you, Young Master, got hurt?" That opinion was correct.
tIf one travels through a thunderstorm filled with continuous flashes of lightning, one must be prepared to be struck. —Whether one's life escapes being scorched by lightning at that time depends on one's own luck.

tTo shave away as many elements that could be overturned by luck as possible, and force a way through the thunderstorm.
tThat was the essence of a furious Otto Suwen's ruthless way of fighting—for that purpose, he would cut cards that might be cursed as heartless without hesitation.
tThe manifestation of that was—

"Military force exceeding Reinhard-san's, or combat power exceeding Felt-sama's, are things we staying in the Capital cannot prepare. In the first place, by the time our turn arrives, force and numbers can no longer serve as an absolute metric to stop them."

tSomehow, Aldebaran had evaded the "Sword Saint" Reinhard, brought along the "Divine Dragon" Volcanica, repelled Felt's forces, and reached the Royal Capital.
tAgainst Aldebaran's incomprehensible capacity for execution, Otto and his group—having just returned from the Vollachian Empire and acting separately from their companions—had no straightforward means to compete.

tHence, sending out Emilia with instructions to capture him alive, or calling upon Wilhelm and the others who happened to be staying at the Royal Manor, were not aimed at delivering a decisive blow.
tOf course, it would be best if Emilia or Wilhelm and the others could achieve victory, but Otto anticipated that could not be counted upon. And Otto's group currently possessed no offensive means beyond throwing their maximum available fighting power at the problem.

t—Otto Suwen possessed neither Reinhard van Astrea's combat style nor Valga Cromwell's strategic intellect. Nor did he rate himself so highly as to believe he had the talent to compete with them.

tOtto expected no such thing of himself as being a person capable of doing things beyond his abilities. —At most, all he could do was exert one hundred percent of his existing abilities.
tAnd what Otto Suwen could accomplish with that one hundred percent effort was—

"—An abandoned quarry west of the Royal Capital."

tAt the report delivered alongside the buzzing of wings, Otto narrowed his eyes as he marked a map. Holding a handkerchief stained with blood to his nose, he nudged his fading, hazy head.
t"Overheating" of his Divine Protection—recalling how Garfiel's eyes had shone at the strange sound of the word when Subaru called his exhaustion from overuse by that name, Otto's lips relaxed into a faint smile.

"Overheating is perfectly fine. I won't stop even if my head splits open..."

tMurmuring thus while bleeding from his nose, Otto snapped his fingers at the gathering buzzing wings—the Zodda Bugs—and instructed them on their next action and to continue surveillance.
tZodda Bugs possessed good-natured temperaments and weak individual consciousness. Sharing policies as a swarm, convincing a single bug produced the same effect as convincing the entire swarm. And the compensation they sought for requests was almost always "a peaceful habitat."

tOtto would secure that reward by exploiting his position as a civil official. In return, they would obey instructions that endangered individual lives and perform unreasonable tasks impossible for humans.

t—Such as clinging to the fleeing "Divine Dragon" to pinpoint the meeting location, dropping a massive rock onto the enemy's head with the force of a swarm exceeding one hundred thousand, recovering without fail the message left by the captive Felt—everything.

"...Grand Mogolade Geyser."

tThat was the message left by Felt—who was being dragged along as a hostage by Aldebaran's gang—believing it would reach them, which had been recovered from the destroyed Barielle detached villa. Slipping past watching eyes to entrust it to the Zodda Bugs showed that Felt certainly had guts.
tSuccinctly noting only a certain place name in the Kararagi City-States, it was likely Aldebaran's gang's next target or destination. If they planned to move toward it, they were free to move as much as they pleased. —However, Otto would not take his eyes off them.

tWherever they went, wherever they hid, in exchange for ringing ears and bleeding, Otto would continue watching every single move of Aldebaran's group.

"Do not expect to sleep ever again. —The entire world is now your enemy."

tHe possessed no power to win outright. What he could do was continuously whittle away at the enemy's strength.
tThat was Otto Suwen's way of fighting—giving one hundred percent of his strength as the Emilia Camp's civil official, distinct from both the "Sword Saint" and the "Great Strategist."
