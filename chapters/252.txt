Arc 4 Chapter 84: "Denial × Denial × Denial"

――Crumbling, crumbling, collapsing away from beneath his feet.

Having lost sight of the footing that should certainly have been there, Subaru felt as though he were falling from some great height.
In reality, Subaru stood frozen in the middle of the room, eyes wide open, rigid.
And yet he was gripped by that illusion, because Roswaal's confession had dealt him a shock too great to bear.

"Somewhere out of reach, you said…"

"You already know the answer, don't you? When two precious things are driven into crisis at once, a person is forced to choose. To choose which is more precious, and to let slip the one not chosen. And so, paring away everything but that single, singular precious thing — that is what completes you, the chosen being that you are."

"That's insane!! A completed being!? All I see is a scarred, brainless idiot standing alone in some windswept wasteland!"

"But the precious thing can be held, undefiled, in a place overflowing with green. Is that not a higher priority than your own freedom from injury?"

At Roswaal's question, posed with something like composure regained, Subaru found himself at a loss for words.
It wasn't that he'd been talked into a corner, nor that he'd lost the thread of a counterargument.

It was only that the sheer volume of emotion surging up in him was too great to even become words.
――Never before had he understood the phrase "too appalled for words" so viscerally.

That incomprehensible logic, that gospel arraying its self-serving events — was the result of abandoning thought and simply obeying it the tragedy at that mansion?

Was Frederica going to die for that? Petra? Even Beatrice? For such selfish reasons?
For the sake of "completing" Subaru — some worthless goal like that — were they going to be betrayed by the master they'd trusted, and lose their lives?

"Roswaal… you're… really, seriously out of your mind, aren't you?"

"…Indeed I am. I've been out of my mind for a long time now. Ever since those eyes bewitched me, four hundred years ago, I have never stopped being out of my mind."

"Four hundred—!?"

Unable to swallow the meaning of the words tossed at him, Subaru parroted them back, his face twisting in confusion.
Four hundred years ago, again — but there was something far too unnatural about Roswaal speaking of it. He shouldn't have any way of knowing about four hundred years ago. And more than that, the way he spoke of it now made it sound as though he himself had existed continuously since then—

"Natsuki Subaru-kun."

A voice called out from close by, startling Subaru out of his bewildered daze.
He looked up to find Roswaal, who had risen to his feet at some point, standing right in front of him. His breath nearly brushing the tall man's face, Subaru gasped and tried to step back — but a hand seized his collar, and he couldn't retreat. He was pulled in, forehead knocking against forehead.

"You called me out of my mind. I'll agree with that assessment. Without question, I am out of my mind. Not in my right senses. My heart was carried off long, long ago."

"A-Ah…"

"Why is it, I wonder, that you are not out of your mind enough-h? Like me — no, more than me — you ought to be out of your mind. Without being out of your mind, you cannot reach the place you must challenge. Because where you must go is far higher, far more distant than where I must go. To walk a path of solitude that no one understands, that no one can understand, requires no human heart. You must make your heart strong, hard, like steel — mustn't you?"

"A-ah… sh-shut up!"

With every word sinking into his mind, there was a force in Roswaal's voice that seemed to drag him down into the depths of darkness. Subaru shook his head as if to fling it off, and shoved Roswaal's chest away from where they stood pressed close.
Pointing a finger at the tall man as he staggered back a step, Subaru spoke, his voice trembling with bravado he couldn't quite hide,

"However it is, whatever it is, your whole scheme fell apart the moment my qualification was stripped from me! Whatever you've been plotting against the mansion — it's all wasted effort, a meaningless sacrifice! Once you understand that, call off this insanity right now!"

"I refuse. Seeing your resolve is still lacking has only made me more keenly aware of the necessity. You have no need to remain human. I shall drive you, wound you, until your very humanity is stripped away and lost, until you depend utterly on Emilia-sama. And Emilia-sama, in turn, shall be made to drown in a love that is nothing but dependency, believing you an indispensable being. I shall sink the two of you into a relationship where only each other matters, and steer the course of your desperate, drowning selves. That is the one and only path by which my purpose is achieved."

"Wh-what possible meaning is there in that…! No matter how much I'm worn down, the qualification I lost isn't coming back! It's a wasted effort — you'd get nothing for it!"

"Whether that is truly your honest feeling, you yourself must know better than anyone."

Roswaal's icy voice struck down Subaru's shout.
Subaru's heart gave a hard, sudden lurch — because he understood the true meaning behind Roswaal's words. There was nothing to it, really. It was a simple matter.
As he'd thought just moments before — Echidna, if Subaru truly, seriously reconsidered and reached out for help, would extend her hand to him. If all he needed to do, when truly and utterly cornered, was simply keep moving forward, then that method still remained open to him.
And that fact—

"If a being such as you were to return, Echidna would gladly reissue the qualification, or anything else you like. Given her nature, that much is only natural — even I understand that much."

"…………"

"Don't flatter yourself, Natsuki Subaru. You are not the only o-one who understands Echidna."

It was a tone unlike Roswaal — one saturated with hatred and resentment.
At the sheer force of the ill feeling hurled at him so nakedly, Subaru went rigid. Only after chewing over its meaning did he feel he finally understood Roswaal's purpose.

"Is prioritizing the liberation of the Sanctuary… because it's what Echidna wants?"

"…………"

"Making them clear the Trials of the Tomb she rigged up, freeing the Sanctuary… you think that's some kind of memorial for her, or something like that?"

"…While she lived, Echidna was concerned with what sort of ending this place would meet after her death. That is why she left devices in the tomb, and lodged her soul there. And yet, the ending she sought has not come to this place, even after four hundred years."

Four hundred years since the barrier enclosing the Sanctuary was raised, and in all that time, the barrier had never once been broken.
The ending Echidna wanted, wished to see, still had not come. Was what Roswaal sought to show her that ending — to lay her soul to rest?

That notion itself wasn't incomprehensible. Subaru had never once believed in the existence of spirits back in his original world, but in this world he had come into contact with Echidna and the other Witches.
He had feelings from that contact, and if their unresolved regrets truly lingered in this land, he had something close enough to gratitude that he'd want to see them fulfilled.
But sacrificing other precious people living in the present for that purpose — that was unspeakable.

"That's one thing. This is another. Roswaal, I'll arrange another chance to lay Echidna's soul to rest. I can promise to make the effort for that. ――So for now, pull your hands off the mansion."

"I refuse. I will see my wish, Echidna's wish, through to completion. I will take whatever measures are necessary. I will entrap others as needed, wound you, and take on all the mud myself."

"Don't drag other people into your self-satisfaction! If you've got something to say to her, drag her out and say it yourself! Don't you dare sacrifice someone trying to build a future, a girl who still has so much future ahead of her, a girl shut away because she can't believe in a future!"

Frederica, Petra, Beatrice — none of them needed to be sacrificed to such a scheme.
There was no reason for them to be dragged along in Roswaal's self-indulgence — let alone for a reason having nothing to do with their own humanity, like hurting Subaru.

"I refuse. The only thing I would accept is the argument 'do as Roswaal says.' Everything else, I shut out entirely. Their sacrifice is inevitable."

"Don't screw around. I could expose exactly what you're scheming, and what'll happen as a result, you know."

"You're the one who ought to think ahead before acting. What would that accomplish? Even if my misdeeds were made public, the situation surrounding the Sanctuary wouldn't change one bit. Emilia-sama would lose her backing to challenge the Royal Selection, and worse, her relations with the refugees and the residents of the Sanctuary would undoubtedly worsen. Do you imagine Emilia-sama could challenge the Trials and produce results while carrying a bomb of ill will on her back? How many times have you watched Emilia-sama collapse with your own eyes?"

"Ga-Garfiel too — if he hears that Frederica, his sister, is a sacrifice in your plan, there's no way he'll just—"

"If you're pinning your hopes on that one, your judgment is clouded further still. Garfiel would never leap outside the Sanctuary to save Frederica. He's a pitiable, foolish being, paralyzed by imagined threats — worrying what might happen to the Sanctuary in his absence. Narrow-minded, stubborn, and possessed of nothing but brute strength. And because his head works just well enough, he can't help but consider the risk of what would become of the Sanctuary should he lose me. Which is why that one cannot move to your advantage. ――That one is nothing but a child, desperate only to protect his own weak, fragile world."

A desperate child, that phrase struck at his chest.
It was the same appraisal the Witches had made at the tea party, of Subaru single-mindedly trying to sacrifice himself. And apparently, that was how Garfiel appeared to Roswaal as well.
Not knowing he was seen that way — or perhaps knowing it and still — was Garfiel single-mindedly charging ahead toward the purpose rooted in himself?

"Garfiel will not side with you. I have no intention of abandoning my scheme. All you need do is wear your heart thin on circumstances beyond your reach, be honed sharp, and be completed. Nothing more is required. Do resign yourself to it, Natsuki Subaru-kun. ――Resign yourself to the fact that it doesn't matter who dies, so long as it isn't Emilia-sama."

"Don't screw around! I! I won't become like you! A way of thinking like yours, like yours — I'll never think that way! That's not — that's not how a human being thinks!"

"…………"

"I'm human. No matter what incomprehensible power gets handed to me, no matter how much pain or how much anguish I go through, that doesn't change. ――I'm human. And I'll keep on being human."

Declaring this to the silent Roswaal, Subaru stepped back, putting distance between himself and the tall man. A flicker of complicated emotion crossed Roswaal's composed features for just an instant, before he shrugged.

"We-ell, that's fine too. As long as you have infinite chances, that holds true for me as well. This time, let's just say I'll give up on persuading you. I'll leave it to the ne-ext me."

"Even if this time fails… even if there's a next chance, and the one after that, and the one after that — I will never nod along to your proposal. I won't become you."

"――Leave the room. I no longer have any reason to go on living this life."

Returning to his bed and pulling the covers over himself, Roswaal left only those words behind, and shut Subaru out of his awareness. His closed eyes, feigning sleep, refused communication with Subaru just as his words declared.
Subaru opened his mouth at Roswaal's attitude, but no words came out, and—

"――――"

Subaru left the room in silence, carrying a weight of defeat.

Having left Roswaal's chambers, Subaru wandered aimlessly beneath the moonlight.

"――What should I do."

What came from his mouth was a question addressed to a problem with no visible end, and the exact same sentence repeated itself in his chest, but no answer ever came echoing back.
The question, cast out into nothing, reached no one, vanishing into the empty air and leaving only a knot of emptiness lodged in his throat.

Literally, there was no other way to put it — he was boxed in on all sides.

Neither Roswaal's cooperation nor Echidna's seemed obtainable any longer.
Echidna's cooperation was physically impossible; Roswaal's, emotionally impossible.

As a possibility, the thought had been sitting in the back of Subaru's mind.
That Roswaal was Elsa's employer, and that the attack on the mansion had been staged for the sake of a "Trial" aimed at Emilia — in this case, a Trial actually directed at Subaru.

Elsa's appearance had been timed as though she'd been waiting for Subaru's arrival at the mansion. Frederica's death, which someone could have arranged access for. Knowledge of the hidden passage and the existence of Beatrice's forbidden archive. Putting those together, the only suspects who came to mind were Ram or Roswaal.
And since there was no reason for Ram, devoted as she was to Roswaal, to do anything that would put Roswaal at a disadvantage, the process of elimination left only Roswaal. ――More than simply hoping he'd be wrong, it was a possibility Subaru had kept confined to the very edge of his mind, out of a sense that it must not be true.

"If Roswaal really is Elsa's employer, then…"

Did that mean even what happened on the day Subaru was first summoned — the theft of Emilia's insignia in the royal capital — had also been orchestrated by Roswaal's hand?
If the gospel had foreseen Subaru's existence, the existence bearing the Authority of Return by Death, and had recorded that this being was necessary for Emilia's victory, then the uproar of that day would have been necessary to draw Subaru onto their side.

Had everything — his desperate running that day, dying three times over trying to save Emilia, even learning her name alongside her smile — all been within the palm of Roswaal's hand?

"Everything, exactly as foreseen… then does that mean Rem having her existence stolen, and me being cornered here in the Sanctuary like this — all of it, exactly as someone planned…?"

If so, then was even Subaru's will to keep struggling like this nothing more than an extension of some thread someone else was pulling?
Was the answer for the future nothing but Subaru cutting away everything except Emilia and pressing forward? Now that the path leading there had been severed, was he simply at a dead end?

"Am I an idiot… no, I am an idiot. That kind of thinking, giving up and letting your mind stop — that's exactly what's become of Roswaal, isn't it… what am I doing, letting myself get swallowed up by it too…"

That a gospel wasn't absolute — no one understood that better than Subaru, who had rewritten the contents of the one Petelgeuse possessed.
Even the gospel's account, laying out the path to the future, wasn't omnipotent. In fact, when events occurred that diverged from what was written, Roswaal had given up on the world and thrown his hopes toward the next loop instead—

"――Eh?"

Just now, something had felt strange, he thought.
Slowly working through the sequence of events, in the middle of turning his thoughts over Roswaal's gospel, Subaru had definitely felt something off. He couldn't put a form to what exactly was snagging at him.

"What is it? What… what's wrong. But something's wrong here…!"

Feeling like he'd been handed a riddle with no visible answer, Subaru searched for a light within the fog.
Roswaal's gospel. Actions that matched what was written. Beatrice's gospel. The Witch Cult's "Gospel." A prophetic book whose holder dies, leaving what follows unwritten. Blank pages. Results as prophesied. Results that didn't match the prophecy — diverging from what was written, now.

"No good. ――One more step, and something won't come out."

Among the scattered pieces, several snagged at him, but none of them coalesced into any concrete shape before dispersing again. Still, this snag was not the kind of thing that could be overlooked.
Every dead end he'd faced before now, he had pulled an answer out from some small foothold, prying the path open and passing through.
This time too, surely each of those fragments would connect to an answer—

"――Subaru?"

"Eh?"

Called by name out of nowhere, Subaru's consciousness, sunk deep in the sea of thought, was pulled back up.
Surfacing into reality, he found Emilia, her shimmering silver hair swaying in the moonlight pouring down through the faint darkness, gazing at him.

At this unexpected encounter, his chest gave a sharp throb, and Subaru raised a hand, unable to hide his surprise.

"Ah, Emilia-t-tan. What are you doing out here? It's pretty late already, isn't it?"

"That goes for you too, doesn't it? If you stay up too late, you won't grow any taller, you know."

"I'm pretty sure I'm already past my growth spurt, so that worry's a bit late for me…"

Emilia, in her usual way, tossed out a topic slightly off the mark. Her response settled him somewhat, and Subaru found his feet naturally carrying him to her side.
The place was the center of the Sanctuary, a spot that formed a modest little plaza. Emilia settled herself against part of a moss-covered stone object — something like a dried-up fountain — her silver hair swaying in the night breeze as she cast a sidelong glance toward Subaru standing beside her.

Her violet eyes, tinged with melancholy, seemed to glisten with a certain allure, bringing to Subaru's weakened heart a sweet, aching impulse.

"I couldn't sleep, so I'm taking a little walk… but what about you, Subaru?"

"…Nah, same kind of thing for me. I'm the type who can't sleep easy when the pillow changes, and also Otto's snoring's surprisingly loud."

"I never would've guessed you had such delicate nerves, Subaru. That's really surprising."

Emilia laughed softly, a hand at her mouth. Watching her profile, Subaru realized this was the first time he'd reunited with Emilia on the night of the very day he'd Returned by Death.
Generally speaking, in places where Subaru wasn't involved, even across loops, other people's actions tended to trace roughly the same course. Which meant Emilia, on the night of the first day she challenged the Trial, always came out here for exactly this kind of sleepless wandering.

While Subaru had been invited to tea parties, beaten up by Garfiel, learned the truth of Ryuzu and the others at the facility deep in the forest, discovered the fact that Roswaal was the mastermind, and generally caused all manner of chaos — Emilia too had undergone changes.

"…Subaru, you don't seem to have much energy."

"Is that so? I didn't think I was like that."

"Nuh-uh, that's a lie. If it were the usual you, you'd be way more… out there, wouldn't you?"

"Nobody says 'out there' these days…"

Feeling a certain relief at how it had been a while since they'd had an exchange like this, his cheeks loosening as he replied, Emilia said "See," pointing at Subaru's cheek with a smile,

"You finally smiled. Subaru, you always try to show me a smiling face, but just now, you weren't even managing that."

"――――"

"Did something… painful happen? If you're troubled about something… I'll listen, if you don't mind it being me."

Called out on how his stiffened cheeks had loosened, and then met with words of concern on top of that, Subaru felt the backs of his eyelids grow damp and hot, and fought desperately to hold it back.
Her gentle, tender words seeped into every part of his body. Boxed in on all sides, with no move left to make, even the glimmer of light he thought he'd seen now cut off — in this state, he nearly found himself clinging to the beloved hand gently extended to him.
And at his own inability to hold fast even to the resolve he'd only just hardened moments ago, at his own wavering conviction, he felt pathetic, and frustrated.

"This is… my own problem. I can't go dumping a burden like this on you, Emilia."

"…………"

"More than me, it's you who's got it rough. After all that happened with the Trial, you were pretty shaken up… are you, um, okay now?"

"Yeah, I'm sorry for the trouble back then. Making such an unsightly fuss, sorry about that? …It felt like I'd been hit with a problem I wasn't prepared for at all."

Turning her face away, Emilia responded to Subaru's deflection with a weak, breathy laugh.
Then she leaned back against the stone object, tilting her face up toward the night sky,

"I really… wasn't prepared at all, and that's what it made me realize. That I'd been running from things I needed to face, all this time, right up until now."

"I don't think that's a bad thing, personally. What's wrong with running from something you hate? If you keep facing something you hate, will you eventually overcome it? Do you have to overcome it? If you run and find a different path somewhere else, and decide to walk that path instead… is that something people get to point fingers at?"

"Subaru…?"

Emilia knit her well-shaped brows at Subaru, who was talking fast, the words slipping out without quite coalescing into what he meant to say. Emilia looked puzzled, but without noticing her reaction, Subaru went on,

"Echidna, who rigged the Trials in the tomb, and Roswaal, who dragged us here knowing full well what they were, and Garfiel, who gets in the way knowing full well we have to get past them — every single one of you is so damn selfish. We're the ones dragged along while you all do whatever you want, so why do we have to be the ones getting jerked around? And then when we try to handle it our own way, we get complained at for not doing it the way you wanted… so what am I even supposed to do?"

"――――"

"My head feels like it's about to burst, like something's gonna break, and yet more and more and more, the problems just keep piling up like they're stacking on top of each other… and on top of all that, the reason for it is supposedly me? Give me a break. Give me a — give me a—"

His emotions surged, and at the very moment he felt something like dizziness from the incomprehensible fury welling up in him—

A soft palm came around the back of his head, and with the sudden sensation of his head being pulled, his body was drawn forward. He plunged face-first into a soft warmth right in front of him, and Subaru, without meaning to, held his breath.

An intensely warm, soft sensation covered Subaru's face.
Beyond the warmth pressed against him, he could hear a heartbeat, and his thoughts, gone blank white, slowly registered the present situation — that he was being held against Emilia's chest.

"A-Ah—?"

"Slowly. Quietly. It's fine to take your time, just listen to the sound of my heart."

"――Mm."

"Let yourself go with the steady beat, breathe in quietly, and out… over and over. When you've calmed down, tap my back. Until then, it's all right to stay like this."

The voice whispered close to his ear sent a shiver of pleasure skittering down his spine, and Subaru's breath caught unbidden. The surge of emotion was blown away by the shock of it, but in its place he was assailed by a sensation like his entire body's blood was coming to a boil.
What, exactly, had led to this situation? Listening to Emilia's calm heartbeat, his own heart hammered against his ribs in a completely different rhythm, sharp and frantic.
But even that pulse, shouting with agitation, was gradually eased and loosened by Emilia's breathing and the palm softly stroking the back of his head, growing naturally calmer. Following her instructions, breathing deep, repeating the inhale and exhale, Subaru steadied his breath and his heart.
Then, quietly, he tapped Emilia's back. At that signal, the hand resting against the back of his head withdrew, and Subaru, holding back a pang of reluctance, raised his body,

"Feeling calmer?"

"…Somehow."

Before the violet radiance right in front of him, Subaru let out a small breath.
At his answer, Emilia's face bloomed into a relieved smile, saying "Good." Enduring the embarrassment threatening to redden his face at her consideration, Subaru gave a small shake of his head,

"Sorry, for losing it like that. I didn't want to cause you trouble like this."

"It's not trouble at all, I really don't think that."

"But you've got it way harder than me, that's the truth of it, isn't it. …If I could, I wanted to help you, going through something that hard… that's what I was thinking, but…"

"Subaru…"

Even now, there was a part of him that always wanted to appear cool and capable in front of Emilia.
In truth, he was unsightly, weak, self-indulgent, lacking in everything — and yet he always tried to push himself to his absolute limit, wanting always to stand by her side.

"Nothing's going right, and honestly I… I was just now going at it with Roswaal. Trying to see if there's some way to get through the Sanctuary without the Trial."

"Eh?"

"What would've really been best is if I could've taken the Trial in your place… but that doesn't look possible. So I at least tried desperately to find some kind of loophole, but that's tough too. I don't know what to do… sorry for being so useless."

"Subaru—"

He lowered his head. No matter how many chances he'd been given to redo things through Return by Death, his own shallow thinking, unable to find even a single clever way through, felt pathetic.
If he'd handled things better, surely there were plenty of cases where a sad world like the one he'd been made to see in the second Trial wouldn't have had to come into being at all.
Even a scene of grief like this one — surely there was a way to have handled it, if only he'd found it—

"But I'll figure something out, somehow, I promise. I'll make sure you don't have to go through anything painful, anything awful. So, please, believe in me."

"…Subaru."

"Yeah."

Emilia looked up at Subaru, her eyes glistening.
Gazing back into those damp eyes, Subaru, amid a heart still wavering, steeled his resolve so that at least the most important part of it would not bend.
Protect Emilia. Overcome the Sanctuary. Save the mansion. Take everything back.

A path with not a single glimmer of light in sight, and yet, surely, somehow—

"I'm happy about how you feel, Subaru. Really, truly happy. ――But I can't accept that kindness."

And yet, the resolve that should have hardened was flatly denied, head-on, by the mouth of the beloved girl whose eyes now held a fierce conviction.
