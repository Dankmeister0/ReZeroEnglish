Arc 7 Chapter 22: "The Bloodless Surrender Plan"


—Time now rewinds, to before the dancing girl danced in the Guaral city hall.


"—A bloodless surrender, you say?"

Natsuki Subaru had come storming into the meeting hall and declared it forcefully before everyone.
Abel, his demon mask having been forcibly torn off, looked up at Subaru and repeated in an icy voice the "pipe dream" that had just rattled his eardrums.
There was no scorn in it—no, no note of mockery at all. What lay in it was pure doubt, the sort of breath one lets out when they cannot fathom what the other person has just said.
Subaru understood the intent behind it well enough.

"You may scheme to keep the casualties to a minimum, but the idea of zero casualties never even crosses your mind in the first place."

"Naturally. Whether you admit it or not, what we're engaged in right now is a war. However things go, deaths are unavoidable. I can conceive of avoiding the waste of human resources—but that is all."

"That whole 'human resources' way of thinking—I don't like it to begin with."

Subaru clicked his tongue, looking down at Abel, who sat cross-legged directly before him.
"Human resources"—to Subaru's mind, it was a vexing phrase, one that jammed together two words, "human" and "resource," that should never have been combined in the first place.
Framing it that way put one degree of distance between the viewer and "humanity," until people looked like nothing more than numbers.
Or perhaps that was simply the sense of balance a ruler like Abel could not do without—

"—but that's not something I can accept. Isn't that right, Flop-san?"

"...And so, in its place, out comes a bloodless surrender? Quite the grand claim to make—coming from you, of all people, who only moments ago looked so utterly worn down."

"I won't deny I made a pathetic, ragged mess of myself. Honestly, ever since I got blown into this country, it's been nonstop disaster after disaster."

Subaru laughed at himself over Abel's remark and stared hard at his own palm.
His right hand, oddly pristine now—the fact that this arm had been swapped out for a brand-new one could itself be counted as one of those "nonstop disasters." Though barely, perhaps the exchange of his right hand counted among the rare strokes of luck.
The exchange of his right hand, Rem waking up, and meeting the siblings Flop and Medium—those, perhaps, could be counted as unqualified good fortune.

Everything else, in any case, was luck and disaster fused as two sides of the same coin.
The Abel before him now, the People of Shudrak, the imperial soldiers including Todd—all of it alike—

"It's been one hardship after another, enough to wear a soul down to nothing. But—"

"—————"

"—all that friction was enough to strike a spark."

Subaru clenched the palm he'd been staring at and declared it clearly.
It happened just as Rem, arriving after Subaru, reached the entrance to the meeting hall. Leaning on her cane, she appeared with Utakata and Rui at her side.
She must have come to see how things turned out.
After all, it was her selfish plea that had lit the fire in Natsuki Subaru's soul.

"You heard about the secret passage from Flop-san and figured on a strategy to slip into the city... a surprise attack, is that it? Something like that is never going to work."

"Oh? And why not? It's a walled city, protected by ramparts. Is there some other way over them?"

"If there's a wall, looking for a secret passage is the first thing anyone would think of. Sure, the passage itself might be cleverly hidden, but..."

A secret passage built for entering and leaving a walled city was, naturally, presumed to exist for smuggling through people of uncertain identity or goods of suspicious origin.
No doubt it was hidden well enough to escape the guards' notice. But that was only good enough to slip past peacetime vigilance.
Subaru doubted it could get past anything beyond that—say, a wartime security net.
More than anything—

"The guy who cornered us in that city isn't the type to overlook something like that."

Quick-witted, sharp-eyed, and decisive without hesitation.
Now that Todd knew Subaru and the others were alive, a secret passage of that sort was exactly the kind of "hole" that would draw the most suspicion. Their enemy wasn't kind enough to leave it be.
He'd seal it off as a matter of course—or, worse, leave it open only to turn it into a trap.

"That's why a surprise attack is completely out of the question. The moment you step out of that passage, someone could be waiting there to split your skull open with an axe."

"Wh-why are you looking at my face when you say that, danna-kun!? That's a terrifying thing to imagine!"

Subaru had only meant to warn everyone of the danger, but he'd ended up staring hard right at Flop's face.
What came to mind was the sight of Flop, again and again, falling before a murderous blade—no, a murderous axe—right in front of him. Seeing that again was something he'd refuse even in death, and he couldn't let it repeat.

"...If it's a mere surprise attack, then I agree that the enemy would be on guard for it."

"What?"

Subaru's eyes went wide at the unexpected agreement. After all, the one who'd just agreed with his opinion was none other than Abel himself.
Abel raised an eyebrow at Subaru's surprised look and went on, "What."

"Is it so unexpected that I'd affirm something?"

"Well, yeah... You go around acting like everything in this world is exactly as you say, and now you're just casually admitting you were wrong..."

"Fool. When did I ever admit I was wrong? I merely agreed with the notion that a plan to use the passage for a surprise attack would not succeed."

"—Huh? Then what were you planning to use the passage for?"

There was a note in Abel's denial that didn't sound like the sour grapes of someone who'd merely had his plan guessed correctly.
To Subaru, pressing him for the grounds of it, Abel closed one eye and said,

"There's no reason it has to be a person passing through. If all you want is to cripple the function of the soldiers holed up in the city, simply smuggling something in would suffice."

"Something...?"

"—Poison."

"All the more reason I can't let that slide!"

The flat, matter-of-fact way Abel spoke of it only made Subaru feel the unwavering resolve to actually carry it out all the more, and he raised his voice in protest against the plan before he could stop himself.
Using poison was simply beyond the pale. —Indeed, it was precisely because Subaru had experienced firsthand the power of the poisons the People of Shudrak could wield that he insisted it should never, under any circumstances, be used.
That agony, that hell, was a fate more miserable than dying in battle.

"Wasn't the whole point, to persuade Flop-san, that we were talking in terms of minimizing the damage, reducing it? How is a plan that uses poison anywhere close to..."

"What are you even saying. Even with a surprise attack through the passage, there's a chance our own forces would suffer casualties during the raid. With poison, there is none."

"—————"

"Reducing the losses among the forces at hand—that is what minimizing the damage means."

Faced with Abel's flat assertion, Subaru held his breath.
And once again, he recognized just how vastly different his own standing was from Abel's—the horizon of a ruler versus everyone else. The gulf between them exceeded what Subaru had understood.
In other words, Abel wasn't factoring in harm to the residents of Guaral at all. —No, even setting aside harm to civilians, he wasn't giving a single thought to casualties among the imperial soldiers.
That much had been obvious ever since he burned down the imperial camp that had come after him.

"My thinking was still too soft. You're..."

"Yes, your thinking is soft. And so, you have no place here—"

Glaring at each other, Subaru and Abel's gazes struck sparks.
Their opinions collided head-on, and the talks were on the verge of falling apart—

"—Now now now, wait a moment, you two! There's no need to glare at each other and snap like that!"

It was Flop who forced his way between the two of them and pushed them apart.
With a soft, easygoing smile, he glanced back and forth between Subaru and Abel's glaring faces, clapping his hands in front of his chest. "Let's all calm down and talk this through!"

"Honestly, my discussion with village-chief-kun here had been running in parallel lines going nowhere! And then danna-kun came striding back in, all fired up. I would love nothing more than to hear this 'bloodless surrender' story danna-kun mentioned! If it's really true, wouldn't that be like a dream come true!"

"Flop-san..."

Faced with Flop's beaming, hopeful smile, Subaru swallowed the anger he'd just been feeling. It seemed Abel, too, had had the wind taken out of his sails by Flop's manner.
Abel gave a small "Hmph" through his nose and said,

"...Very well. Let's hear it, then. If this plan of yours manages to fool this merchant well enough to pry the location of the passage out of him, so much the better."

"Wha—!? Danna-kun!? Don't tell me you're..."

"No, no, no! We didn't cook this up together! Hey, don't drag me down with you just because your own plan isn't going to fly!"

It was a remark bound to invite Flop's needless suspicion, yet Abel showed not the slightest bit of shame over it. At the sight of him calmly keeping his poker face, Subaru muttered a curse: "You don't even need that demon mask anymore..."

"So then..."

—and into the gap that opened up in the conversation, a faint voice slipped.
It was Rem's voice, from where she stood rooted at the entrance to the meeting hall, staring at Subaru's back.
Undistracted by the mood of the room, Rem kept her eyes on Subaru alone and went on.

"So then, what would you do? Can you find some other path—one that's neither a faint dream nor a blood-soaked reality?"

"...You sure have a way of putting things that gets my blood up every single time."

"—————"

Subaru gave a wry smile at the sight of Rem's steady gaze, her lips pressed firmly together, and steeled his resolve anew.
Then he swept his gaze once more over everyone in the meeting hall. Gathering the eyes of Abel, Flop, and the Shudrak led by Mizelda all upon himself, he said,

"My plan needs no secret passage, and no bloodshed. That said, I will need Flop-san's help."

"No need for the passage, yet you need my strength...? But danna-kun, you know as well as I do that I'm nothing more than a powerless peddler with a bit of a glib tongue, don't you?"

"Yeah, of course. But Flop-san isn't just a merchant—you were also born with a natural gift. —You've got a good face."

"—Huh? A face?"

At that, Flop's eyes went round and he pressed both hands to his own face.
Likewise, everyone else in the hall who'd heard it tilted their heads. "A face?"

"Is that so. Certainly seems that way."

"—! Sister, have you noticed something?"

"No. I merely agreed with what Subaru said, that he has a good face."

"Sister..."

Taritta, sour-faced, hung her head at Mizelda, who nodded with her arms crossed.
But to a greater or lesser degree, no one could quite grasp what Subaru's remark meant, and an air of confusion spread through the room. That went for Flop, and even Abel, alike.

But Mizelda's attitude, given her keen interest in other people's looks, wasn't off the mark. That absurdly lax aesthetic sense of hers was exactly what had given Subaru his big hint in this situation.
After all, it was Mizelda's love of a pretty face that had given birth to the idea for this "bloodless surrender operation."

"The proof's in the pudding. —Flop-san, humor me, just try it out."

"Try it out? I don't mind, but just what am I trying...?"

To Flop, whose confusion hadn't cleared, Subaru insisted, "Just trust me," pushing the matter through.
Then he turned his gaze toward Mizelda.

"You dye your hair and paint patterns on your body, so you must have makeup, right? Mind lending me those tools for a bit?"

"..."

After a while, they returned to the meeting hall, and everyone present, shown the "results," was left speechless.
But Subaru could tell this wasn't silence born of negative feelings like confusion or exasperation—it was something closer to pure astonishment, admiration, even something like being moved.
He could say with his chest puffed out that the result was impressive enough to warrant exactly that kind of shock.

"Given good enough raw material, even I can pull off something like this."

"..."

Rubbing under his nose as he said this, Subaru got the same non-reaction as before. With everyone still speechless and nowhere near recovering, an anxious voice spoke up. "D-danna-kun."
It came from the one person present unrelated to this shock besides Subaru himself—

"I'm not entirely clear on what the results actually are, but what exactly is going on here? It doesn't seem like a very good feeling, somehow..."

"Hey, hey, nothing to worry about, Flop-sa—no, nothing to worry about, Flora."

"Flora!?"

Flop—no, Flora—widened his eyes in shock. But even that expression, dyed with surprise, was charming, Subaru thought, nodding with full confidence as he stroked her cheek.
Then, once more, he gripped Flora's slender shoulders and turned her to face forward.

He'd combed the long golden hair out soft and gentle, applied eyeshadow to make the eyes stand out more than usual, groomed the lashes so their length showed clearly, added a faint touch of red to the cheeks to set off the naturally fair skin, painted the lips red, and changed the outfit.
With a frame as slight as his, just a little ingenuity was enough to pass him off as "her."
In other words—

"—This is the key to the bloodless surrender, the finest plan I can muster!"

Raising a fist high with force, Subaru presented Flop—now Flora—to everyone in the meeting hall.
Short on time as it was, he was proud to call it a gem of a finish that made the most of the raw material. And don't be shocked—this was made under a shortage of proper tools, meaning this was, in fact, the least cute version of Flora possible.
Once the proper tools were gathered and the person herself learned the right mannerisms, that charm wouldn't just add up—it would multiply and shoot skyward all at once.

"Beauty can be manufactured."

"Are you joking around?"

"Eh!?"

A cold voice stabbed into Subaru just as he'd shown off what could not have been a more heartfelt masterpiece.
Looking over, he found the voice had come from Rem, her eyes gone cold. Whatever hopeful gaze she'd turned on him just moments before had vanished, replaced by the same look she'd worn toward him ever since she woke up.
Taken aback by how razor-sharp it was, Subaru gasped and thrust out a hand. "Wait!"

"I-I'm not joking! I'm not joking around at all, so don't look at me like that!"

"This isn't joking around? Please, stop with the jokes. I was a fool to have even tried to trust you, even a little."

"That's a fast conclusion! I never noticed it before, but the way you cut people off like that—you're the spitting image of Ram!"

"Huh?"

That would mean nothing to the current Rem, but her speed at writing someone off would have made even Ram proud.
Subaru felt a warm little pang at the thought that, yes, they really were sisters—but right now, winning back Rem's lost trust took top priority.
In truth, Subaru wasn't fooling around at all. He was tackling all of this in earnest, thinking it through seriously.
The reason he'd gone to the trouble of transforming Flop into a woman named Flora was—

"—Your target is Zikr Osman, is it?"

—it was Abel, hand at his chin, who was the first to arrive at the answer.
Unlike everyone else, who couldn't hide their shock at Flop's transformation into Flora, Abel had likely fallen silent in order to gauge Subaru's true intent.
And indeed, he'd seen right through Subaru's aim.

—The target: Zikr Osman.
Holder of the rank of Imperial Second General, and commander of the imperial troops stationed in the walled city of Guaral. A capable officer who favored solid, unadventurous tactics, and, moreover—

"I heard he's an absolute skirt-chaser. Seemed to be well-known among the imperial soldiers."

"That he's known by that as a byname is common knowledge."

"'Skirt-chaser'... That doesn't give a very favorable impression."

Listening to the exchange between Subaru and Abel, Rem made a sour face.
Subaru shared that impression. Hearing that a military officer was a skirt-chaser, anyone would picture some lecherous, base-natured man. But that was exactly the opening they could exploit.

"At the imperial camp, there was even talk of offering up captured women to this Zikr fellow as a gift. In other words, a harmless-looking woman would have a good chance of getting close to him."

It wasn't something he much wanted to remember, but back at the camp, Jamal had thrown it in Subaru's face that Rem might be offered up to Zikr.
Since Rem was a beautiful girl, that meant Zikr's aesthetic sensibilities were, evidently, quite ordinary.

"In that case, with Flora's power here, it ought to work."

"D-danna-kun? You've been calling me Flora with such fervent hope in your voice for a while now, but what exactly is happening to me? I don't understand any of this and it's terrifying!"

"Don't worry, Flora. Of course I'm not going to make you do this alone. I intend to fight the same way, right alongside you."

"That is much too reckless, Subaru!"

Mizelda shot to her feet at that remark, right as Subaru was soothing a bewildered Flora. Clouding her strong-eyed expression, she gripped Subaru's shoulder and shook her head.
Then, as though breaking a hard truth, she dropped her voice and said,

"There is charm enough in your eyes, sure. But what you are born with..."

"Hey, hey, come on, Mizelda-san. Weren't you listening to what I said?"

"What was that?"

"—Beauty can be manufactured."

Subaru laid his hand over Mizelda's on his shoulder and declared it firmly.
At that, Mizelda's eyes went wide, and she caught her breath. Then she looked at Flora, narrowing her eyes as though the makeup on her face were something dazzling.

"You have won this one. ...Go on, then. Show me what you're capable of."

"Yeah. Watch closely."

"I got no idea what you two are even talking about."

Mizelda entrusting her faith, and Subaru accepting it. Kuna watched the exchange between the two with an exasperated look, but no comment on that front.
In any case, the real problem now was—

"Say we exploit Zikr Osman's tastes—then what's your plan? He's a wolf too. Not some dog that'll snap at any pretty bait you dangle."

"Well, sure, just dangling it aimlessly won't get us anywhere. So we need some ingenuity to make him bite. Luring him to a party, for instance."

"A banquet. But he won't be so easily drawn out. Naturally, until reinforcements arrive from the imperial capital, he has no reason to come out from behind the walls. He won't take a suspicious invitation."

"Yeah, that's true. I'm still working on narrowing down the options for that part..."

"W-wait, please!"

In the midst of the back-and-forth between Subaru and Abel, Rem raised her voice.
With shock still plastered across her face, she looked back and forth between Subaru and Abel.

"Are you... are you serious? Building the whole plan around some cruel prank on Flop-san?"

"Eh, a prank on me? What is really happening to me here? So from the wife's perspective, it looks like I'm the one fooling around...? Niece-kun, what's going on?"

"Auu? U! Uu!"

Beside a tense Rem, Flora—who still hadn't gotten a single look at her own face in a mirror—turned to Rui for rescue. But Rui, flustered by meeting Flora for the first time, hid behind Rem instead.
In other words, from Rui's point of view, Flora and Flop were two entirely different people.

"Whether or not that girl's reaction is a fitting touchstone is beside the point—I don't consider this a prank. He's finally put forward a plan worth debating."

"So you admit it too, then. Flora's beauty."

"...What I admit is the idea you came up with, one I would never have thought of. A rather crooked one, at that."

At Abel's stubborn response, Subaru twisted his lips to voice his displeasure.
But Abel paid it no mind, resting a hand at his mouth for a moment in thought. Then he turned his sharp gaze on Subaru and asked,

"Natsuki Subaru, let me ask you one thing... Does your makeup only work on the merchant?"

"..."

For a moment, Subaru was caught off guard by the question.
But he chewed over the intent behind it in his head, then shook his head.

"I told you already, didn't I? If we're going ahead with this plan, I'll be standing in the same position!"

"Fool. Who would expect anything from you? Go look at your own face in a mirror before you go running your mouth."

"Watch your mouth!"

Abel spat the words out, brow furrowed, dripping with genuine contempt. Ignoring the way that remark wounded Subaru, he put a hand to his own chest and said,

"The merchant alone will not be enough. —Then I shall follow suit."

"A-Abel is going to!?"

At that bold self-nomination, the air in the meeting hall stirred with murmurs, Mizelda foremost among them.
Naturally, Subaru too was startled by Abel's declaration. He'd never have dreamed Abel would volunteer for it himself.

"...Honestly, I figured the biggest challenge would be talking you into it."

"In peacetime, this would be a foolish plan not even worth a moment's thought. But as things stand, our hand is thin, and the measures we can take are limited. If it will prove effective, cutting into my own flesh becomes an inevitable necessity."

"Tch, what an obnoxious way to put it. This is exactly why 'charisma' types are..."

Even driven from his throne, his being Emperor remained unshaken.
That, surely, was Abel's creed—the one principle he'd never bend. Shown a vivid glimpse of it, Subaru could do nothing but admit his honest admiration.
A textbook example of a bad ruler would be one so concerned with saving his own skin that he'd fumble everything as a result.
But ever since his first encounter with Subaru in the jungle, Abel had kept winning high-stakes gambles, the Lifeblood Ritual included, wagering his own life as the chips.
This time was no exception; he clearly had not a shred of intention to back down.

"A clever ploy only proves effective when it's sprung from outside the enemy's expectations. Exploiting the general's tastes, lying in wait within his inevitable carelessness—it is worth considering."

"Yeah, it's even written in this book called the Kojiki. Cross-dressing is the ideal way to go after an enemy commander's head."

"Taking the word of some dubious book at face value..."

It was, in fact, a quotation from a venerable old text, but there was no use trying to explain the Kojiki's credibility to the current Rem—it would fall on deaf ears. As things stood, Subaru couldn't think of any way to swiftly win back the trust he'd lost from her.
Still, since his real worry had been getting flatly shot down, Abel's reaction was as welcome as it was unexpected.

"When you first brought up poison, I wondered if you were the type who wanted to wipe out anyone who crossed you..."

"If necessary, I would not hesitate to do so. But sentiment is useless baggage where future decisions are concerned. To begin with, the city of Guaral is not the only thing we must secure."

"—————"

"If we can seize the general, Zikr Osman, then we can arrange to obtain what we want without a scratch. And if it also reduces the risk of a crack forming from within, all the better."

"...Is that so. Coming from a guy who got cracked from the inside and lost his own place, that's some line with real depth to it."

It was a jab thrown out partly as a probe, but Abel's expression didn't waver in the slightest.
Perhaps sarcasm and insolence of this level weren't even worth reprimanding. —Or perhaps, just as he himself had said, he wasn't so stubborn as to refuse to acknowledge his own blunders.
Either way—

"If you're willing to cooperate, nothing better than that. As for a name... Abel, Vollachia... how about Bianca?"

"I have no attachment to a false name; call me whatever you like. More to the point, you, I, and the merchant alone won't be nearly enough hands. Let's see..."

Crossing his arms, Abel swept his gaze over the faces gathered in the hall.
Then, with a "Hmm," he closed one eye and said,

"Kuna and Taritta look to be the usable ones, I'd say."

"Hey?"

"It's only a reasonable precaution. Even if we manage to lure Zikr Osman out successfully, we'll need enough hands to hold him down. That said, we want to avoid anyone who'd be identified as one of the People of Shudrak at a glance."

Subaru could follow the reasoning behind the jerk of Abel's chin as he said this.
Taritta and Kuna, bewildered at being singled out so suddenly, were relatively speaking the two among the People of Shudrak who gave off the least dangerous impression.

Mizelda, who looked every bit the die-hard warrior at a glance, and Holly, who made a distinctly unusual impression at first sight, wouldn't suit an operation where they needed to hide the fact that they were People of Shudrak.
What was needed, first and foremost, was a femininity that wouldn't put the target on guard—

"That's within the range I can fake with makeup and styling, I'd say."

"—Me too."

"Rem?"

It was Rem who smoothly raised her hand at that.
Rem, who'd made no secret of her distrust of Subaru since the whole Flora business, seemed to have found something worth considering in the earnest way they kept deliberating, and her expression turned serious.
Then, lighting her pale blue eyes with resolve and determination, she said,

"Let me come along too. I promise I'll prove useful."

"Rem... sorry, but that's impossible."

"—! Trying to keep me needlessly away from danger again..."

Her resolve on the verge of being crushed, Rem glared hard at Subaru.
It was true, Subaru did carry a degree of overprotectiveness that would irritate Rem. His wish to keep her far from danger, to have her spend her days safe and peaceful as if in a cradle, was no lie.
But that wasn't the only reason he stopped her from joining the fight here.

"It's true I'm worried about you. But the reason I'm turning down your joining is purely that it would lower the plan's chance of success. ...You've had your face seen by the imperial soldiers in the city."

"—Ah."

"Same as when you were captured at the camp, and same as when we fled the city. For the same reason, I can't borrow Medium-san's help either. We went and made too big a scene."

Having stood out that much, the checkpoint guards weren't likely to forget the faces of Rem, Medium, or Rui besides.
This operation's whole success hinged on how well they could slip in behind the enemy's expectations. Since that was the very foundation at stake, he couldn't take Rem along.

"But—but if it's about having your face seen, then you're in the exact same position!"

"Yeah, but it's different for me. Because the one walking through Guaral's front gate next isn't going to be me—it's going to be Natsumi Schwartz."

"Huh?"

Perhaps thinking she was being brushed off again, Rem pressed the point, anger kindling in her eyes. But this, at least, wasn't something words alone could explain.
Just as Flop had transformed into Flora, Natsuki Subaru would simply transform into Natsumi Schwartz. —For this, there was nothing for it but to show, not tell.

"Anyway, the reason I can't bring Rem is as I explained. But Taritta-san and Kuna, too—since this is going to be a dangerous operation, I want them to agree to it first before..."

"No. This is interesting. I give my permission. Take the two of them along."

So Mizelda cut off Subaru just as he was about to go check with Taritta and the others on their intentions.
He turned in surprise, and found Mizelda's eyes gleaming bright with curiosity as she watched him. It was the eye of a hunter, as though she meant to peel back his skin and peer at what lay beneath.
For a moment, Subaru even felt a shiver of dread run through him.

"Mizelda... san?"

"Subaru, you and Abel already proved your valor. The Shudrak take pride in valor as our highest honor. But that does not mean we dismiss wisdom and cunning as without honor. A warrior above all others excels at both strength and wit... So then. Prove it."

Mizelda's cheeks relaxed, as though she found this roundabout, back-door sort of plan very much to her liking.
Subaru had assumed, out of pure preconception, that Mizelda and the rest of the People of Shudrak would dislike a plan like this. That was why he'd meant to ask Taritta and the others whether they wanted to take part.
But once Mizelda answered that way, Taritta and Kuna nodded along as though it were only natural.

"If that's what the chief says, I got nothing much to add."

"I will follow Sister's judgment. ...I'm curious about the makeup, too."

Kuna gave her consent with her hands laced behind her head, looking not terribly interested. Taritta shared the same view, but her eyes kept flicking, again and again, toward Flora.
It seemed she'd taken an interest in Subaru's skill with makeup. It struck him as a bit lacking in tension for the preparation stage of a life-or-death operation, but—

"If there are no objections, then let's move quickly into preparations. We must settle this before the coward holed up in this walled city gets a push at his back from the capital."

"...Right, got it. If everyone else is fine with it. Rem, can you swallow this too?"

"...You don't intend to listen to me anyway, do you."

Rem glared steadily at Subaru, holding back her own mortification. Sorry as he felt for her resolve, weighing her safety against the plan's success on the Balance left no room for the option of bringing her along.
So Subaru lowered the corners of his brows, resigned to bearing the brunt of Rem's anger.
But—

"But, it was I who asked you to do something about this in the first place."

"Rem?"

"So there's no way I have any right to butt in, is there. ...Please, make it succeed."

The frustration in her expression hadn't changed, and Rem hadn't forgiven Subaru. But she'd made clear, looking away as she said it, that she would at least respect his judgment.
That alone was enough to make the storm clouds inside Subaru feel as though they were clearing.

"Only when it comes to Rem, I guess I'm cheap... no, maybe not so cheap after all?"

The smallest hint of goodwill from Rem right now was enough, all on its own, to make Subaru feel happy enough to fly.
But then, if Emilia smiled at him, that alone would send him soaring to the heavens, and if Beatrice explained something to him with that smug look on her face, his chest would go all warm and fuzzy too.
Huh, turns out I'm way cheaper than I thought, Subaru realized, a bit late in the game.

"What's wrong, getting cold feet now, of all times?"

"'Now, of all times' isn't quite the right direction. ...I'm just slowly starting to catch fire, that's all."

"Oh? Then go on and show it through your work, for what it's worth."

Abel snorted at Subaru's answer, given courage by Rem's single remark.
He didn't need to be told—that was exactly his intention. —If he could pull off this bloodless surrender, then this time, a path back to the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica might finally open up.
Even setting that aside, there was no way he'd let poison get scattered anywhere within Subaru's sight.

"—Heeey, big brooother! I figure we oughta let Botechin rest soon, poor thing, so I wanna find somewhere to park him—"

It was Medium who poked her face into the meeting hall, its mood building with resolve, thrusting herself right in.
Even next to the Shudrak, many of whom had sturdy builds, Medium's height—a full head taller than most—stood out remarkably. Standing out as she did, she swept the meeting hall with her round eyes and said,

"Huh, where's big bro?"

"Oh, little sister! Losing track of your own brother, how heartless can you be! I'm right here!"

"—?"

At Medium's tilted head, Flora stood up to assert her own presence. Hearing Flora's words, Medium knit her brow, deep in thought.
Then, after staying silent for a while, she suddenly cried out as though something had clicked.

"Big bro, turns out you were actually big sis all along!"

"Danna-kun!? Just what in the world is happening to me!? This is scary!"

If he could fool the eyes of Flop's own blood-related little sister, that would make for a considerable foothold toward the operation's success.
