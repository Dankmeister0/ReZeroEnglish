Arc 8 Chapter 16: "The Undead Disaster"

—Pale skin drained of blood, and golden pupils floating within black eyes.

The undead army that had appeared out of nowhere threw, quite literally, a bucket of cold water over both the Imperial Army and the rebel forces locked in their battle for the imperial capital of Lupugana.
The battle between the living, contesting for hegemony over the Empire, had at some point transformed into a survival struggle between the living and the dead, and its brutality was only deepening.

"AAAAAAAH——!!"

Raising a great shout, he brought his greatsword down in a sweeping arc onto the enemy before him.
The man opposing him raised a ragged sword—scavenged from who knows where—to block, but the reckless, all-out blow snapped the brittle blade and drove straight on into his enemy's skull.
The sensation of the skull shattering—but what followed wasn't the ghastly sight of blood and brain matter spraying out. Instead, it was more like a ceramic pot breaking, fragments scattering in a way that no human death should ever look like.

These inhuman things were inhuman not only in appearance but even in the manner of their deaths.
As a man who had lived his life in the Empire, he'd had occasion to see races with outlandish physical traits—different numbers of arms, different numbers of eyes—but even so, the way they died had never differed from anyone else's.
Things with life in them were bound, by rights, to end that life as living things do.
But these undead had none of that. Perhaps that was why they inspired such fierce revulsion.

Even in an Empire that spent its days at war, where life itself was said to be cheaper than in other nations, no one trampled on the very concept of life the way these undead did.

"Hold the line! Get the Emperor and the citizens out!"

"OOOAAAH——!!"

When one of the Generals bellowed the order, the soldiers around him cried out as if fevered.
With the undead army's assault and the runaway rampage of the Cloudy Dragon, and the destruction of the sluice wall of the reservoir in the capital's north, His Imperial Majesty had made the decision: abandon the capital, and withdraw the entire army.
The Imperial Army, united as one, was pouring everything it had into supporting the evacuation of the Emperor and the capital's citizens. Ironically, the arrival of this third faction had rendered the fight against the rebels moot. Now, without distinction between regular troops and rebels, the lines were drawn between the living and the dead, and the fierce battle raged on.

The imperial soldier who had just cut down one of the undead was one of those holding the line; he and the men around him were fighting with everything they had, desperate to delay even one more of the undead, to drag it down into death alongside them.

"I haven't the faintest idea what these things are. But—"
"His Majesty the Emperor will—!"
"He'll surely find a way to wipe every last one of you out!"

No matter what the enemy might be, their faith in Vincent Vollachia, the Empire's summit, did not waver. That was the greatest reason the soldiers could hold their ground without fear.
Vincent had continued, without fail, to produce results. The Imperial soldiers took the blood he had spilled from his enemies, and the mounting pile of corpses, as the grounds for their trust in him.
Given the absoluteness of his rule, their will to fight was of a different purity than that of men who had lightly plotted rebellion.
And that held just as true for their will to fight against the undead now shaking the Empire's foundations.

"—! Everyone, bring that one down!"

The General's cry rang out again, this time directed at the ferocious onslaught of an unusually large undead.
A bald, massive undead was swinging both arms, sending the imperial soldiers who stood against him flying like scraps of paper. If left to run wild, it would tear a hole in the line, and the defensive position they had worked so hard to build could crumble from that breach.
To stop it, the soldiers who followed the General's order leapt at the huge body all at once.

"Ngh, OOOAAAAH!!"

The soldier who leapt in first was struck, his crushed corpse sent flying; the imperial soldier ducked beneath it and, staying low, slashed at the giant's leg.
The blow landed, and the enemy's stance broke. But then golden eyes turned down to meet the gaze of the imperial soldier who'd pulled off such an impudent trick, and the tip of a sword—stolen from someone else—flashed dully.
An instant before that point could run him through, a figure cut in front of the soldier.

"Do... it... nnngh...!"

Pierced through the chest, it was the General who cried out amid a spray of bloody foam.
The imperial soldier the General had shielded caught his breath, then immediately sprang out from behind that back and, with all his strength, swung his sword to crush the massive undead's head.
The impact snapped the sword halfway through, but the giant made no counterattack—its entire body simply shattered apart.

With a thud, the General fell backward to the ground, taking the huge undead down together with him in a mutual kill.
The General, run through the chest, had already breathed his last; the imperial soldier, praising his bravery, borrowed a replacement for his broken sword from the General's hand.
Then, turning his head to search the battlefield for the next enemy—

"Goddamn it..."

Muttering that, the imperial soldier pulled down the helmet he wore with his fingers, his cheek twisting.
Having just crossed the threshold of death himself, what came into his field of view was the next undead—and it wore the same gear, the same face, as the General who had just died shielding him.

The General's corpse lay fallen beside him. He himself gripped that General's sword.
And yet, standing right before him was that very same General—pale-faced, golden-eyed.

"Goddamn it."

A battlefield where even comrades, the moment they died, turned undead and joined the enemy.
In that waterlogged killing field fit to be cursed, the imperial soldier raised his sword and kept fighting for all his life was worth.

—Having withdrawn from the imperial capital of Lupugana, the Coupled Dragon Carriages made for the fortress city of Garkla.

Within one of those carriages, the historic alliance struck between the Emilia Camp and the leadership of the Vollachia Empire had, unexpectedly, been interrupted by an unwelcome intrusion.
Though as for the cause of that intrusion—

"What's with that face? Here we were, worried sick about ya, Natsuki-kun, and you look at us like we're guests nobody invited. That's just plain sad, ya know."

"I'm not making that face! I'm glad we got to see each other properly. Anastasia-san, of course, and even Julius's cool, composed face—"

"Composed isn't quite the word for how cool my state of mind is right now. And the cause of that, none other than your current appearance."

The two who had suddenly joined the Coupled Dragon Carriages, Julius and Anastasia, looked the shrunken Subaru up and down with keen interest.
Naturally, running into the two of them here was a bolt out of the blue for Subaru.
The degree of his shock rivaled the moment Priscilla had suddenly dropped out of the sky at the fortress city.
And yet Subaru wasn't so dense that he couldn't guess why the two of them were here in the Vollachia Empire.

"I mean, it's one thing that Emilia-tan and the others are looking for us, but for Anastasia-san and everyone too... did you seriously come all the way through Kararagi just for me?"

"Yup yup. Went the whole roundabout way, three whole countries—quite the adventure, wasn't it? Crossing the border into Vollachia isn't exactly a walk in the park either, and we ended up on quite the expensive little trip."

"Ugh."

"Not that I wanna sound cheap about it, but I'd appreciate it if you didn't forget the price of the bones we broke gettin' here."

Being blown into the Empire had been beyond Subaru's control, but hearing about the worry it caused made his chest tighten all the same. Caught neatly in Anastasia's gaze and words, Subaru found himself on the verge of being handed an enormous debt of gratitude.
But then, from around Anastasia's neck, a voice called out to her—"Ana"—

"You shouldn't bully Natsuki-kun without cause. Given his current appearance, no matter how you look at it, you're the villain bullying a child here, Ana."

"O-oh..."

At that voice, Subaru raised his eyebrows, and its owner swished a white tail in response.
It was the fox stole draped over the shoulder of Anastasia, who was dressed in a kimono. In other words, it was Echidna, the artificial spirit Anastasia kept as her companion.
Unlike the Witch of the same name, Echidna—whom Subaru recognized as a comrade who'd made it through the crucible of the Pleiades Watchtower alongside him—drew a sigh of relief from Subaru with that single remark.
That meant that, of the companions he'd been separated from at the Watchtower, the only one Subaru still hadn't been able to show his face to safely—aside from letting Patrasche outside see him later—was Meili.
In any case—

"Anastasia-san! Julius, you made it to Vollachia safely too!"

"I'm just as glad you and the others are safe, Emilia-san. As for the story of our troubles along the way... well, since we didn't find Natsuki-kun before you all did, I can't exactly brag about it."

"No, that feeling alone makes me really, really happy. Thank you."

Emilia's cheeks relaxed as she welcomed Anastasia's appearance too.
Given they were already in the Vollachia Empire, and on top of that there'd been a civil war followed by this undead disaster—it was probably fair to say it was a genuine relief that they hadn't accidentally missed crossing paths with Anastasia and the others in the capital.
And then, once the joy of that unexpected reunion had settled a little—

"Have you finished warming up old acquaintances sufficiently? As you're aware, time for war council is limited."

The one throwing cold water on the moment was Abel, arms crossed with characteristic insolence.
He had shown miraculous tolerance for him in reading the room, but it seemed even his patience for staying silent had finally reached its limit. Grumbling his complaint, Abel glanced sidelong at Subaru.

"Another acquaintance of yours, is it. To think you'd extended your reach all the way to Kararagi."

"That's actually kind of complicated—Anastasia-san and everyone are with Kararagi, but also kind of not with Kararagi, so... how does that work, exactly?"

Subaru's puzzled question stemmed from the presence of Halibel, standing beside Anastasia and the others. His gaze went to the wolf man puffing on his pipe, whose title as the strongest in the Kararagi City-States raised the question of just what position he indicated for Anastasia's group.

"Allow me to take that explanation. Specifically, the circumstances of how we came to ride here directly aboard a flying dragon ship."

Julius answered Subaru's question, wiping a finger under the fresh wound below his left eye.
The new white scar on the handsome man's profile, combined with his unfamiliar wafu attire, only accentuated Julius's dignified, sharp bearing, and Subaru felt a strange sensation looking at him.
He wouldn't say the effects of his Infantilization had nothing to do with that impression, but it was likely, in fact, the change on Julius's side that was the true cause.

Somehow, it felt as though Julius now had more ease about him than before.
Even before, there had been something like the grace of the "Sword Saint's Knight" about him, but that had been different from ease—if anything, it seemed to have sat at the very opposite pole from it.
Whether his current bearing, as if he'd finally shed armor he'd long worn, was the result of his experience at the Pleiades Watchtower, or had some other cause, wasn't certain.

"—Your Imperial Majesty Vincent Vollachia, first and foremost, I am relieved beyond words to find you safe. To be granted the honor of an audience with you once again is a glory beyond anything I could have hoped for."

Oblivious to the impression forming in Subaru's mind, Julius stepped forward and delivered this with a bow, fluid and graceful.
Abel, standing in the position shielded by the large-framed Goz, knit his well-formed brows at Julius's greeting. Subaru, too, felt something off about the word "again."

"You said 'again.' Yet I recall no audience with a swordsman of the City-States, nor do I recognize your face. On what grounds did you speak of 'again'?"

"With respect, I have had the opportunity of a single glimpse before. Though at that time, it was not as a swordsman of the City-States, but as a knight of the Kingdom."

"Of the Kingdom—"

Even facing Abel, the Emperor of Vollachia, Julius showed no fear, and at his response Abel narrowed his black eyes thoughtfully, but nothing in his memory seemed to strike a chord.
Surely Julius, carrying himself with such composure, couldn't be lying. Which meant the two of them had genuinely met before, and Abel had simply forgotten it.
In other words—proof that the effects of Gluttony, which had erased Julius from the memories of those around him, still hadn't fully recovered.

"Were this a scheme, your bearing makes little sense for it. —Berstetz."

"Yes. I myself have no recollection of ever having seen this gentleman either."

"An occasion where Berstetz was not present at an audience is rarer than one where he was. Which means this is not a matter between you and me alone, but a wider-reaching problem."

With a single question to Berstetz about whether he remembered Julius, Abel had already seen through to the discrepancy in recognition between himself and Julius, and deduced that some special circumstance was tangled up in it.
Watching Abel's conduct, Subaru couldn't help muttering to himself, "Is this guy for real?"

"Normally, would you just accept something like that so easily? This ridiculous a story..."

"We are in the midst of an emergency where the dead have risen and the Emperor has been driven from his own capital. Measured by scale, your body shrinking is not so far removed from that. The point is simply whether or not one can conceive of the possibility."

"I'm not saying my shrinking is exactly an everyday occurrence either, but..."

Being told, essentially, that any mystery in this world could be accepted so long as one could conceive of it, wasn't the kind of logic Subaru could just nod along to and accept.
As Subaru furrowed his brow at that, Beatrice, holding his hand, used her other hand to knead at the crease between his eyebrows.

"Best not to think about it too much, I suppose. Besides, someone with the capacity to hear such things out is far more convenient than someone who refuses to understand, I suppose."

"...That's fair. Also, that feels nice, so keep going a little more."

"Roger that, I suppose."

While Subaru continued having his brow massaged by Beatrice, Julius—having had the question raised during the greetings resolved—moved on properly to the matter at hand.
Which was—

"First, the lady here is Anastasia Hoshin-sama... I gather Emilia-sama's identity and standing have already been disclosed, but Anastasia-sama holds the same standing as Emilia-sama."

"Ho, is that so, alongside Lady Emilia. So the Kingdom's next-generation candidates for the throne stand side by side here in a foreign land. Well now, that is quite an amusing and rare situation indeed."

Serena, twisting the scar on her face into a pleased expression, spoke, and Julius nodded silently in response. Taking that as confirmation, the gazes of the Imperial faction turned to Emilia, and at those looks, her lips curved into a smile.

"Yes, what Julius said is true. Anastasia-san and I are both Royal Selection candidates, and we're friends."

"Well, technically our friendship contract kicks in once the Royal Selection's over, mind ya. —As introduced, I'm the representative of the Hoshin Company of Kararagi, and one of the Royal Selection candidates of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica."

"On top of such an impressive title, yet another person from the Kingdom."

"Sorry if the title surprised ya, but actually, that's not the only title I've got right now. And that's the reason I went to the trouble of showing up here on a flying dragon ship, y'see?"

Just moments earlier, a friendly alliance had been struck with Emilia as the Empire's leadership. Into that, the fact of another Kingdom's person becoming involved had Abel starting to show displeasure, when Anastasia held up a hand to stop him.
At Anastasia's mention that there was yet another title not yet spoken of, Emilia tilted her head, puzzled, finger to her lips.

"But Anastasia-san is the company representative, and a Royal Selection candidate, and... is there still more? Oh, maybe you're the employer of the mercenary band Mimi and the others are in?"

"'Iron Fang' falls under the Hoshin Company's umbrella, so I don't bother addin' that on separately to my title as company rep. This here's somethin' else entirely, a whole separate matter."

"Separate..."

Told by Anastasia that she'd guessed wrong, Emilia murmured in a cute, puzzled voice.
Beside her, Anastasia gave a wry smile at Emilia's manner, and Julius gestured toward her with his hand.

"On this occasion, Anastasia-sama has been appointed as an envoy—of the Kararagi League of Cities—and has come to the Empire in that capacity."

"—The Kararagi League of Cities!?"

At Julius's answer, it was Otto whose voice cracked.
Having broken his silent observation without meaning to, Otto looked embarrassed. Taking the opportunity, Subaru asked him, "What's the League of Cities?"

"I've heard Kararagi's under the banner of a 'city-state,' a country made of a bunch of cities stuck together, but..."

"—. That understanding isn't wrong. Broadly speaking, the Kararagi City-States is a nation formed from roughly ten major cities banding together. Each city has its own mayor as representative, and the council formed by those representatives together is what's called the League of Cities."

"Huh... kind of like Priestella's Council of Ten?"

"If anything, it's more likely Priestella's Council of Ten took inspiration from the League of Cities. The same goes for the 'Water Wing Inn'—being along the border, Priestella sees a great deal of cultural inflow from Kararagi."

"Ah, now that you mention it..."

The 'Water Wing Inn' where they'd stayed at Anastasia's group's invitation had that wafu style spread throughout its interior and exterior, and the story was that it had come over from Kararagi.
And going back further still, Subaru privately suspected that the wafu style in Kararagi itself was knowledge brought over from Subaru's own world—but that was a matter to set aside for now.
In any case—

"Not merely a Royal Selection candidate of the Kingdom, but having entered the Empire as an envoy on the League of Cities' authority—that is no small matter."

"True enough. Not quite as bad as with Lugunica, but Vollachia can hardly be said to be on friendly terms with Kararagi either. Well, more like the Empire's basically at odds with just about everybody."

"Probably because the Emperor's got such a prickly personality. Might round out a little going forward, though."

"Silence."

Riding on the bluntly satisfying way Anastasia spoke her mind, Subaru chimed in, and Abel, his expression unchanged, hurled an insult back at him.
Subaru stuck his tongue out at that, but Julius looked startled at the exchange between him and Abel.
He turned a look of surprise on Subaru, but since explaining would take too long, Subaru just stuck his tongue out at Julius too and let the moment pass.

"Word of Vollachia's messy state of affairs reached our ears back in Kararagi too. But we didn't come to show our faces here to take advantage of it, y'know."

"How commendable. Though surely that isn't entirely without some intention behind it."

"Well, ya gotta be a little shrewd to get anywhere in this business. Wouldn't it be more uncomfortable bein' helped for free anyway?"

"—. A fair point."

At Anastasia's mischievous eyes, Abel's gaze flicked toward Emilia for an instant.
Emilia herself looked puzzled at that glance, but from Abel's and Anastasia's perspective, Emilia's goodwill was likely the far greater miscalculation.
Even Subaru wasn't about to say Abel and the others should be helped for free—if he said something like that, Otto would probably unleash a thousand sermons' worth of hell on him.

At that point, a raspy voice asked, "May I?" seeking a chance to speak.
Having received Abel's silent assent, Berstetz turned his thread-thin eyes toward Anastasia and Julius.

"It's a bit puzzling, but might I ask the reason the City-States sent an envoy to the Empire in its current state? Surely not because the Empire's various troubles have affected your nation, and this is some manner of inquiry?"

"Even in Kararagi, where the sayin' goes 'time and money are of equal value,' we wouldn't come talkin' money in the middle of all this chaos. Meddlin' in another country's internal affairs without plenty of preparation just ends up costin' you."

"You're being roundabout. I believe I already said—time is limited."

"My, aren't we impatient. Alright, alright. —Halibel."

"Hm? I'm allowed to talk?"

To the Imperial leadership eager to move the conversation along, Anastasia called on Halibel beside her.
Leaning against the wall of the dragon carriage, blowing smoke rings from his pipe, Halibel stroked up the fur on his chin at the attention gathering on him from all around.
It really was a fine, fluffy coat of fur, or so it looked to Subaru's eyes, at least.

"Well then, I suppose I'll tell ya... actually, somethin' odd's been happenin' in Kararagi for a while now. The League of Cities has been on my case to sort it out, y'see."

"Something odd?"

"You lot probably got some idea already, don't ya? —The hotoke-san have been movin'."

"Hotoke?"

At Halibel's casual, faintly self-important way of putting it, Subaru caught his breath. But Emilia and Goz, hearing an unfamiliar word, tilted their heads.
At that reaction, Halibel gave a wry "sorry, sorry," and continued.

"Right, right, this word's Kararagi dialect, wouldn't make sense to ya. 'Hotoke-san' is what we call a dead body in Kararagi. In other words..."

"—You mean the City-States has also suffered damage from the undead?"

"That's about the shape of it, yeah."

At Halibel nodding so calmly, the same shock Subaru had felt spread once more through Emilia and the others.
The undead army that had launched its flanking assault on the Battle for the Imperial Capital—if the same thing had been happening in Kararagi too, then this was a calamity that went beyond the Empire alone.

"Th-then, worst case, is this happening on a global scale? What happened with that zombie thing in Kararagi, anyway?"

"Zombie?"

"A moving dead person! Easier to talk about if it has a name."

"Hohh, I see, I see. Funny, a word I don't know but it rolls right off the tongue."

Bobbing his pipe up and down at Subaru's answer, Halibel replied in a somehow good-humored tone. But that reaction of his only frustrated Subaru, who leaned forward. "Halibel-san!"

Moment by moment, if the undead disaster were still expanding—

"There's not a second to lose. This isn't the time to be here in the Empire..."

"No need to worry, the zombie business back in Kararagi, I already took care of it nice and proper. So there's no need to fret about Kararagi."

"Ah..."

"Sweet kid, aren't ya. Want a candy? ...Ah, don't have any candy on me, actually."

Without even so much as reaching into his sleeve as if to check, Halibel soothed Subaru in a light tone. Feeling, unexpectedly, as though his leaning forward had just been gently scolded, Subaru pouted.

"According to Halibel-san's investigation here, tracing the course of how the undead—no, zombie—damage occurred, it seems the harm was gradually moving toward the Empire. Hence, the League of Cities, in order to investigate the incident within its own borders and to alert your nation—"

"I hired Halibel here, who knows the details, and took on the job of League of Cities envoy myself. That's how I got help crossin' the border too, see."

There, there, Anastasia summed up the circumstances of how she'd taken on the role of League of Cities envoy.
She might have been trying to keep things from turning too grave, but unfortunately, no amount of consideration could keep the content of the story from carrying weight.
In particular, the fact that the undead disaster had struck the City-States first—

"The matter occurred in the City-States before the Empire. Given that, one could say this calamity of the undead was brought into the Empire from your nation, could one not?"

"That's one way to see it, but we've got another theory too."

"Oh? If it isn't shirking of responsibility, let's hear it."

"Kararagi bein' the place where the first hotoke-san... zombie damage showed up, that part's true. But the real target was Vollachia all along, and Kararagi was just a practice run before that, I'd wager."

At Abel's spiteful way of putting it, Anastasia answered with the same unchanged manner.
Contrary to her easygoing demeanor, the hypothesis she put forward was fairly harsh in content. The view that Kararagi was merely practice and Vollachia the real target was almost too cruel to consider.

"Why do you think that, Anastasia-san? That what happened in Kararagi wasn't the real thing, and Vollachia was the actual goal?"

"Scale of the damage, that's why."

Anastasia answered the very question Subaru himself had been holding, which Emilia had voiced aloud. She met Emilia's violet eyes with her own pale-blue ones as she spoke.

"Information's been all tangled up, but the first reports of the capital's damage reached us at Garkla. Even if it wasn't accurate in every detail, it's true that the capital was taken by zombies, isn't it?"

"An event where the very heart of the Vollachia Empire, the imperial capital Lupugana itself, is seized, and the wholesale evacuation of its citizens carried out, is no ordinary matter. Hence, there can be little doubt that the intentions of the 'enemy' behind this event were directed at the Empire."

"The reasoning holds."

Abel responded briefly to the inference put forward by Anastasia and Julius.
But the Emperor, plotting deep schemes behind his quiet black eyes, prefaced his next words with "By the way," turning his gaze toward Halibel.

"This matter you say you quelled in the City-States—what actually happened? Regardless of the state of affairs in the Empire, I have heard nothing of the City-States being destroyed by the undead."

"Right. I stopped it before it got to that point... though, mind you, it's not like their aim was to wreck Kararagi to bits. More like, just as Ana-bou said, it was practice, I reckon."

"What does that mean, exactly?"

"Simple as this: the zombies I ran into had swapped places entirely with the people of the town, every last one, and were livin' on same as the folks still alive, no different. So nobody'd notice the swap."

"Wha..."

At the fact Halibel revealed, Subaru found himself at a loss for words.
And it wasn't only Subaru who was shocked. The clever ones among them all wore thoughtful expressions in unison, while those in the same camp of shock as Subaru had gone speechless.
Small wonder. What Halibel had just described diverged far too much from what anyone had expected.

"You mean to say those undead are even capable of impersonating the living!? Certainly, Lamia-denka spoke just as she had in life, but even so...!"

"If the assumption that they'll attack anything living on sight breaks down, that changes things. Even a single one with intelligence enough for this is troublesome enough, but if that's the case, the way we treat them as an enemy has to shift far too much. This is becoming rather interesting indeed."

"What's interesting about it, Count Dracroy!? There is a limit to how far your callousness can go!!"

At this embodiment of shock over how far reality had diverged from expectation, Goz and Serena clashed in opinion.
But that reaction of theirs was only natural. In fact, Subaru found himself in complete agreement. An enemy that couldn't simply be fought head-on, but that also used underhanded tricks besides—that was a serious matter.
And more than that—

"Um, Halibel-san, you said the whole town got turned into 'zombies'... how many people lived in that town?"

It was a question someone had to ask.
Subaru regretted having let Emilia, rather than himself, be the one to bring it up. That regret only deepened, heavier, once he heard Halibel's answer.
Because—

"—Roughly, about two thousand people, I'd say."

"That many..."

With Halibel's answer, the scale of the damage was rendered into a concrete number.
The scale of destruction it forced them to imagine cast a grim shadow over Emilia's expression, and drove a cold, sharp thorn into Subaru's chest as well.
But there were also those who, rather than merely being wounded by Halibel's answer, looked further ahead.

"If a scale that large could pass itself off as the living, one must call it troublesome indeed. What was the trigger that exposed it?"

"Reason it got found out was the flow of supplies goin' in and out of the town. They'd faked it to some degree, I reckon, but the numbers don't add up between livin' folk and dead ones. Hotoke-san don't need food or drinkin' water, so that's where the gap showed."

"The boundary between the living and the dead. —No end to this."

Unlike Subaru, who let emotion stop his feet, Abel and Anastasia didn't let their feet stop.
While Subaru felt something like frustration at that difference, he also didn't want to forget what he'd once told Abel—that this frustration wasn't something to discard, that it was necessary.

"Berstetz, you'd stationed a force in the western Empire, hadn't you."

While Subaru gritted his back teeth over that, Abel turned a question toward Berstetz.
Berstetz gave a short nod, "Yes," at the Emperor's question, then continued.

"In the west, along the border with Kararagi, we had received reports of unsettled movements. This was before the signs of civil unrest had even begun to spread, but fearing a two-front situation alongside countermeasures against the rebel army, we maintained a force there as a check. —Surely not."

"The army entrusted there should have been under Groovy Gumlet, should it not."

Berstetz's thin, thread-like eyes widened slightly, betraying his shock. As if agreeing with the Chancellor's realization, Abel let out a small breath.
What that breath spoke of was, if Subaru recalled correctly, one of the "Nine Divine Generals"—a figure whose face Subaru still hadn't had the chance to see.
The reason Abel narrowed his eyes with such melancholy upon speaking that person's name was—

"Your Majesty! Surely not... surely not! General Groovy is—!"

"Stationed to check unrest along the border with the City-States—if, using that as pretext, he has not joined us here even now, at this point, we have no choice but to assume the worst."

"The worst assumption, is it. Even I can hardly call that an amusing thought."

Goz's face reddened, and even Serena's thin smile vanished from her lips.
Nodding at how the senior officials reacted, Abel spoke on.

"—Groovy Gumlet and his army have already fallen into the hands of the undead. Should he fail to rejoin us going forward, that possibility must be accounted for."

"—!"

At Abel's words, Goz's whole body trembled, and he ground his back teeth so hard they cracked.
That wasn't a figure of speech—he had genuinely cracked his own back teeth. Overwhelming indignation tore through the warrior Goz Ralfon's entire body; the possibility of having lost a comrade struck him hard.
Even for Subaru and the others, who'd never known the man in person, it landed as a quiet blow all the same.

"Your Majesty, if the undead truly can impersonate the living as you assume..."

"Reports from every region can no longer be trusted on their own merits alone. Without raising the certainty of our information, throwing ourselves into the fight to reclaim the capital would be an extremely difficult undertaking."

Among the reports being delivered, there was no way of knowing which came from a living ally and which from a dead enemy.
Faced with that terrifying possibility laid bare, Subaru caught his breath.

"So that's the reason the League of Cities entrusted Anastasia-san with the role of envoy..."

"Glad you're sharin' the sense of urgency. This is somethin' that absolutely can't be allowed to spread any further. That's why we only let a small, trusted group come ahead like this."

At Anastasia's nodding words, Julius and Halibel each gave their own confirming nods.
In other words, the two of them had been selected as trusted individual combat strength for beginning the fight against the undead.

"What about Ricardo, and Mimi and the others?"

"Buildin' up a joint front with the League of Cities at the border. Basically, they won't be comin' into the Empire. If we can't stop it here in the Empire, Kararagi's gotta be ready too."

"Bit of an ominous thing to say, but bear with it. Well, Ana-bou's always had a sharp tongue, nothin' new there."

Having answered where the absent "Iron Fang" was, Anastasia looked at Halibel with a smile. In an elegant, unhurried gesture, she placed a hand to her cheek and, still fixing her gaze on Halibel, said—

"Let it slide the first time, but would ya quit callin' me Ana-bou? I'm not a kid anymore, y'know, and I'm the City-States' representative besides."

"Not a kid, sure, but ya don't look much different from when you were one. And your nature hasn't changed much either, from back when you used to run wild with Ricardo..."

"Halibel."

"Alright, alright, my bad, my bad."

Waving a hand back and forth, Halibel raised the white flag to Anastasia.
The apparently close, easygoing relationship between the two of them was intriguing in its own right, but compared to the grave matter at hand, it could only be called a trivial thing.
Indeed, given the situation—that this unprecedented undead disaster had to be stopped—

"To restate it properly, I'll convey the thinking of the Kararagi League of Cities. In order to halt this undead damage—this zombie damage—the City-States wishes to offer the Empire whatever support it can."

"—. As for the details of that support, we shall hammer those out separately. But tell me—do you have any idea of a means to stop this situation?"

"About that—well, it's not like hotoke-san just get up and start walkin' around as some kinda natural phenomenon, right? No doubt about it, somebody's nasty scheme is behind this. Which means—"

"—Call it the mastermind, the caster, the ringleader, whatever you like—but we bring them down."

A grave problem threatening to plunge the entire world into crisis, and the means to stop it.
The one responsible for causing this—stopping that unseen "enemy," whose face and form no one had yet laid eyes on, was the top priority shared among the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica, the Vollachia Empire, and the Kararagi City-States.

"—The 'enemy' of unknown identity."

Softly, picturing that "enemy" whose true nature remained unclear, Subaru murmured to himself.
Just what shape, what form did that "enemy" take, and under what ideology had it made ready to carry out such a horrifying atrocity?

Just imagining how much hardship and how many obstacles lay in wait before they could strike down that as-yet-unseen "enemy" was enough to send a cold sweat down his back.
But even so—

"—Look at the members we've got gathered here."

What roused Subaru's heart, on the verge of quailing, was the array of faces around him.
Emilia and the others, who had come all the way to the Empire searching for him and the others; the Empire's leadership, who had steeled themselves to unite as one to protect this Empire; and the reliable reinforcements from Kararagi.

"No matter what the 'enemy' is scheming, there's absolutely no way we can afford to lose——!"

That was Natsuki Subaru's unfeigned declaration of war against the "enemy."

—Now, around the same time Subaru was rallying his spirits inside the Coupled Dragon Carriages.

Furiously, the water of the reservoir poured out from the destroyed sluice wall, and the capital was being swallowed by water.
Watching from below as the raging torrent swept away buildings, tearing apart people and trees alike without distinction, the blue-haired boy who had fled to higher ground shaded his eyes with a hand and gazed out over it.

"Ah, well, I did try to buy as much time as I could, but I wonder if everyone managed to get away safely. Hope the bosses didn't let the blood rush to their heads and misjudge the call to retreat, of all things."

Murmuring that lightly was Cecilus, dressed in a kimono with zōri on his feet.
In this grand Battle for the Imperial Capital, the greatest wildcard involved, in every sense, in the sudden reversal of circumstances—Cecilus had pinpointed it with his signature intuition and story-brain, and the world's leading man had rushed to the scene.

In fact, had it not been for Cecilus's own willful, unilateral action, the capital would have been swallowed by water far sooner, with far more people left behind in the escape and far greater casualties as a result.
In that sense, Cecilus's actions had been unexpected to everyone, and at the same time had brought about a fortunate outcome for a great many people.
And yet—for Cecilus himself, the one directly involved, it wasn't quite that simple.

The undead army that had appeared out of nowhere, the shift in the tide of the Battle for the Imperial Capital, the crude interloper of a third "enemy" trying to snatch victory out from under everyone—Cecilus had leapt into the fray meaning to astonish it.
Truly, it had been an exploit fit for dazzling the whole world—or so he could pat himself on the back for it.
And yet—

"Still, this turned out to be a complete surprise, I have to say."

Murmuring that, Cecilus, amid the capital being swallowed by the raging torrent, scratched his cheek with a finger at the sight of the undead who, just like him, had fled to higher ground and still hadn't given up their obsession with the living.
There was no doubting that these things were beyond the natural order, brought back to life by some technique even Cecilus didn't know.
In fact, he had even encountered the one who had fashioned them into such things.
And yet, despite events having unfolded exactly as far as Cecilus's intuition had run—

"—Who would've guessed that even taking down the caster in question wouldn't settle the matter!"

Raising his voice at that, at Cecilus's feet lay collapsed the caster who had revived and led the undead.
Beautiful pink hair and long ears, an appearance young enough to seem not so different in age from Cecilus himself—it was the "Witch" who had faced off against Cecilus, the "Blue Lightning," and had already breathed her last.
