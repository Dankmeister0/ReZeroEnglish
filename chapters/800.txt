Arc 10 Chapter 40: "Let's Do This Together, Yeah?"

"—Got a quick little moment, handsome? I'd like a word with just the two of us, if you do-o-on't mind?"

Called to a halt in the corridor, Subaru turned to find Black Dog standing before him.
Having finished their conversation with Melty and parted ways with her as she headed off to see Russell, Subaru and Beatrice had been on their way to check on Crusch, who had fallen into a deep sleep from the exhaustion of battle.
And there was Black Dog, with timing that made it seem as though he had been lying in wait—

"Actually, was that exactly what you were doing? Waiting for us?"

"What, what's up? You look shocked and surprised. Can't get over how unexpected and unforeseen it was that I didn't barge into your little talk with 'White Sheep'?"

"Pretty much, yeah. So you can wait your turn and read the room."

"Well, that's a hurtful little assessment, thank you very much. So, what's your answer to my offer?"

"My answer? You mean talking alone? I don't mind, but..."

As he spoke, Subaru raised his hand, still joined with Beatrice's.
The Beloved Children had only just subjected them to a scheme to split them up. Between their depleted mana and the need to replenish his Beatricium, he did not want to be separated from her right now.

"So, since Beako's with me, we can't make it just two. Two and a half at best, if we're being generous."

"And which of us are you counting as half a person, Betty or Subaru, I suppose? Besides, with Subaru beside her, Betty is as dependable as a hundred people, in fact."

"Hear that? So it'll be just the hundred and two of us. That's a pretty big crowd for a whispered conversation, but if you're fine with that, I'm in."

"Thanks for the sophistry and the hair-splitting. Fine, fine. Rather count you two as a package from the start than have you tattle to a hundred people afterward. That's better and preferable. Understood."

The word 'tattle' made Subaru feel a little guilty, as though they were doing something wrong, but there was no point throwing cold water on Black Dog now that he seemed willing to talk. Taking advantage of the empty corridor, Subaru stayed there with Beatrice and continued the conversation.
Whatever it was Black Dog had wanted to discuss badly enough to lie in wait for them—

"—'White Sheep' told you all about this and that and everything else, right? About how and why we wound up working under that unpleasant bastard."

"There's still plenty I don't understand. Melty-chan said herself that her memories were full of holes. But we heard the most important part. —What she wants, and what she's prepared to do."

"And Betty and Subaru promised to help her, I suppose."

"Oh-ho. How kind and magnanimous of you. Lovely. To think you'd get through that distrust of people."

Distrust of people did not seem to suit Melty, yet he could not say it was entirely wrong, either.
Born with the power of 'People-Charmer,' Melty could never fully believe in other people's goodwill. She could not tell whether the kindness directed toward her came from the person themselves or was caused by her power.
Whether there was any point in making that distinction was not something outsiders had the right to casually debate. —Perhaps Black Dog, too, had felt frustrated by it.
Either way, he paid close attention to Melty.

"But if that's how it is, then you're looking more and more like..."

"A tragic man increasingly likely to occupy a rather pitiful position, in fact."

"What, what's up with those looks you're giving me!? Don't go pitying me however you damn well please! First off, me and 'White Sheep' aren't like that. Sure, when she's down in the dumps, I get this itchy, restless feeling I just can't stand, but..."

"Staaare." "Staaare."

"Stop it, cut it out, you idiots! That's not what I mean! To me, 'White Sheep' is... yeah, she's like a pet. It's irritating when you can't train them the way you want. You see all these clever ways they could do things, if they'd just do this or that, but they can't. So I end up sticking my hands and my nose into it."

"That sounds less like treating someone as a pet and more like..."

Black Dog looked as though he had successfully put their relationship into words, but to Subaru, it sounded like something warmer than the bond with a pet—something closer to a parent's point of view.

"—Melty told us that you have neither 'memories' nor a 'name,' I suppose. Is that true, in fact?"

As Subaru hesitated over whether he should point that out, Beatrice took over the conversation and put the question to Black Dog. He fluttered both hands.

"If you mean neither I nor anybody around me remembers who I am, then yeah. Funny, isn't it? No memories, no records telling me who the hell I am or where I'm from. Was I born a baby this damn big?"

"That must be rough..."

Black Dog made a joke of it, but perhaps the closest comparison to his lonely circumstances was Rem just after she had woken up in Vollachia.
Even then, Rem had had Subaru, who remembered her, right by her side. His clumsy handling of things had caused plenty of friction between them, but the thought of Rem being thrown into that situation all alone, without even that much, sent a chill through him.
And Black Dog had been left in exactly those chilling circumstances, with no one to turn to.

"Haven't you got any clues about yourself? Nothing?"

"...Thanks to joining the 'Six Tongues,' I found out and established that the Sin Archbishop of 'Gluttony' was definitely involved. But that's it, that's all. Might've been different if I'd gotten to talk to the 'Gluttony' they had locked up, but before my chance to meet the guy came around..."

"Roy was killed, huh. And now you're stuck traveling with us."

"Exactamundo-o."

Black Dog was getting quite adept at using modern expressions. Perhaps the very fact that he had neither 'memories' nor a 'name' was what let him absorb that sort of knowledge without finding it strange.
Even as that fleeting impression crossed his mind, Subaru frowned. "But..."

"What exactly did you set this time aside to talk about? Is this the part where we get to know each other, now that we've heard about your situation too? I mean, I do feel like it's bringing us closer as comrades."

"Hah! 'Comrades'—now there's a nice turn of phrase to make a man laugh and squirm. Sure, we're comrades, all right. —Comrades getting used however 'Silver Fox' pleases."

"...Used by Russell-san?"

Black Dog's voice had dropped a little. His words seemed to mean something beyond the two months they had spent with no choice but to accompany the Six Tongues.
Of course Subaru knew that Russell was making use of their position and abilities. Since they were borrowing the Six Tongues' strength in return, he had considered it a mutual arrangement.
However—

"'Silver Fox' is a much, much, mu-u-uch nastier piece of work than you imagine or think. He's careful and thorough about controlling the information he gives his little pawns. For example..."

"For example?"

"That princess you think is sitting quietly in the castle? Turns out she ran away with another princess in the same boat. Your whole camp's wanted now, in other words. That's the sort of thing 'Silver Fox' hasn't been telling you, i-i-isn't it?"

"Wha—!?"

At Black Dog's exaggerated tilt of the head and the words that accompanied it, Subaru's eyes widened in shock.
Emilia was supposed to be under house arrest in Royal Lugunica Castle, in the capital. —He had never really expected her to sit quietly, but her escape was news to him.
If she had escaped, it meant she had decided there was something she needed to escape from. That was as good as saying she was in danger. He had to find out whether she was safe, right now—

"Calm yourself, Subaru, I suppose. —'Black Dog,' what grounds do you have for that claim, in fact? Roswaal's friend 'Silver Fox' is suspicious enough, but you are hardly above suspicion yourself, I suppose."

"—Beako."

Making an effort to remain calm, Beatrice held Subaru's impatience in check. At her question, Black Dog tilted his head from side to side. "Hey, hey."

"Listen to you, little one. Such accusations and suspicions. You just healed my wounds and sent me off to help 'Ashen Star' and the others, didn't you?"

"One acts differently in an emergency than one does at other times, in fact. Melty spoke frankly with us, I suppose. It is high time you did the same."

"—Right and unassailable. A perfect example of a sound argument, indeed."

Acknowledging Beatrice's firm demand, Black Dog let his shoulders slump. Then he pushed back his hood, took off his mask, and faced them.
At the sight of the face he so rarely revealed, Subaru blinked.

"...What's with you, getting all formal out of nowhere?"

"Pretty good-looking, handsome face, isn't it? Must be a feast for a sucker for a pretty face like 'Ashen Star,' but spare me the compliments and the admiring stares. I don't like this face. That's why I wear this thing."

As he spoke, Black Dog twisted his uncovered lips into a coarse grin.
With wavy light-brown hair and dark skin, the unmasked Black Dog was a handsome man who gave an impression of rugged strength. Subaru had seen his face a few times over the past two months, on infiltration missions and the like, but he could never get used to the gap between those features and his usual behavior.
Even now, without that animal grin baring his teeth, Subaru might scarcely have recognized him.

"Is speaking without your mask your way of showing sincerity, I suppose?"

"I'm not saying you should trust and believe me just because I showed my face. Though it does look like it might work on you two. But that's not the main point. It'll just make what comes next easier to explain."

"What comes next..."

"We'll get to my mug later. First, those grounds you want and need to hear about. All the information the 'Six Tongues' gather from around the country goes to 'Silver Fox.' Then he selects what to pass on to us himself. With me so far?"

"That much... I get. Russell-san isn't showing us all his cards. But keeping quiet about Emilia crosses the line, no matter how you look at it."

However ruthless he might be for the kingdom's sake, if he knew what Subaru and the others valued most and did that anyway, there could be no trust between them.
As for whether it was true, Black Dog pulled a folded sheet of paper from inside his clothes.

"By the way, I found out about the princess by stealing one of those Bird Mail messages he's so fond of. He's a sneaky, persistent bastard who mixes false intelligence into coded messages, but you can probably believe this bit."

Black Dog fluttered the scrap of paper.
Bird Mail was this world's equivalent of carrier pigeons, though it involved specially trained birds carrying letters that would burn unless the proper procedure or prescribed method of opening them was followed. The Six Tongues frequently used it to exchange information more efficiently.
Naturally, intercepting or reading the Six Tongues' Bird Mail had to be among the hardest such feats in the kingdom. If Black Dog had pulled it off to obtain this information, it was probably reliable.

"You can have this message. Decode it however you like later, back up my story, whatever. Point is, 'Silver Fox' can't be trusted. But that's his character we're talking about, not every last piece of information he has."

"...I'm still not seeing what you're getting at. You're not trying to get us on your side so you can push for workplace reform this late in the game, are you? And it's strange that you got your hands on this message in the first place."

"Strange? What's odd and out of the ordinary about it?"

"Well, think about it. It's not like picking up something someone happened to drop. Finding out about Emilia and the others was incidental for you. You must've decided to snoop through the Bird Mail because there was something you wanted to know yourself. If that's the case..."

"—Quick on the uptake, and you keep things moving. That's why you two are just right, the perfect fit."

In an instant, Black Dog seized Subaru's shoulder and shoved his back against the corridor wall. Subaru caught his breath involuntarily, and Beatrice cried, "Subaru!" her voice breaking.
She squeezed her small body between the two men.

"What do you think you are doing!? If you mean to harm Subaru..."

"What'll you do? How'll you act? You just screwed up by being that slow to decide, you know?"

"—Muraku!"

Gritting her teeth at Black Dog's provocation, Beatrice cast her spell.
A faint light spread out, acting on Black Dog's body as he held Subaru against the wall. It would make him lighter, light enough for even Beatrice to push him aside with ease—

"—!? The effect vanished, I suppose!?"

"Kuhahaha! Thanks for the meal!"

Answering Beatrice's shocked cry with laughter, Black Dog raised his right hand to his mouth, caught the tip of his glove between his teeth, and pulled.
With a pop, his palm came bare, and he held it out for them to see.
There, on it—

"...An eye, in your palm?"

"An Evil Eye, in fact. And judging by what it just did..."

"Right. It sucked up, absorbed, and swallowed your magic. An Evil Eye of 'Magic Absorption,' they call it. Naturally, inevitably, that gives you an idea where I'm from, doesn't it?"

"—Faradar?"

"Bingo, correct, ding-ding-ding! Probably!"

Black Dog took a step back and waved his hand with his usual clownish air.
In his palm, an eye with greenish whites and a reptilian pupil—the thing called an Evil Eye—stared out, insistently asserting its presence.
In other words, that sudden act of violence had been—

"A demonstration to show off your Evil Eye? You could've just said so!"

"Just a teeny little surpriiise. First and foremost, it's your own fault for letting your guard down. You do realize we're up against monsters who can squish and shapeshift, don't you?"

"That is an entirely separate matter from whether your prank was justified, in fact! Besides, you have used up even more of Subaru's mana when we were finally charging him back up, I suppose!"

"And now I'm brimming with it instead. It's in reserve, so call it good. Now, where were we, how far did I get... Ah, right! Me and 'White Sheep' come from the same place. Maybe."

"I guess you can't help it, but you're not sure about that either, huh..."

Black Dog's account was rather short on certainty, but there was nothing to be done about that. —At any rate, if he belonged to the Evil Eye Tribe, it was very plausible that Faradar, which Melty had only just told them about, was his homeland too.
And while both Black Dog and Melty seemed to have fallen victim to Gluttony during their time as Beloved Children, it made sense for them to have been together if they had already been partners.
They had been attacked by Gluttony in the same place, lost their 'memories' and 'names,' and still built something close to the relationship they had had before. —No, perhaps there was more to it.

"Melty-chan and 'Black Dog'... their eyes..."

Recalling the face of Melty, whom he had been facing just moments before, Subaru took another long look into Black Dog's golden eyes. He had already known they were the same color, but knowing now that the two came from the same place and were both of the Evil Eye Tribe lent that fact a new significance.
And that significance invited the thought that they might be more than fellow countrymen—perhaps something closer, perhaps even blood relations.
If so, much about the way Black Dog and Melty usually interacted suddenly made sense.
But Black Dog waved a hand. "Stop that, cut it out."

"Annoying and ridiculous. Listen, all right? That's not what I want to tell you, what I want you to hear. It's about Faradar, but I'd actually rather 'White Sheep' didn't hear about it."

"...And that brings us back to why you were waiting for us at the start."

"Right you are. Took the long way round, a real detour. Anyway, that place, Faradar, is apparently where I'm from... but it doesn't exist anymore, and everyone connected with it is probably dead."

"—How do you know that?"

"How or why, this is just a theory I've put together from the information I got. Seems Faradar, land and all, fell into the hands of some nasty people. But then they couldn't figure out what they'd originally been using it for, so they disposed of it. Or so it looks."

At Black Dog's casually delivered explanation, Subaru and Beatrice exchanged a look.
He sounded utterly unconcerned as he explained how his homeland had apparently been destroyed, but true to his warning that it was only a theory, the account was full of gaps and rough edges.

"What do you mean, they couldn't figure out what they'd originally been using it for?"

"There's the theory they were just too stupid, too damn idiotic, but there's also this one. Originally, they secured Faradar because of a certain someone, but then everybody forgot that someone, and all that was left was a liability with no apparent purpose... Something like that."

"—You mean they were hostages to keep you and Melty-chan in line?"

"And once me and 'White Sheep' disappeared, there was no point to it anymore. Now, here's the thing. I've got business with whoever disposed of Faradar."

"...Don't tell me you want revenge? You, when you don't remember anything?"

Catch the person who had disposed of Faradar and hear what they had to say. Seek a clue, a possible connection to his own past—Subaru had not thought of anything so peaceable because he was looking straight at Black Dog's ferocious expression.
What dwelled in those golden eyes was not the look of a supplicant seeking his past, but something fiercer, filled with dark flames of rage.

"Even a guy who doesn't remember a thing has his own way of settling things. You don't have to understand. Not like anybody, like any damn person, could."

"...No."

"—?"

"I do understand. Maybe not perfectly, but I do."

Subaru, too, had once lost his memories and become empty. Admittedly, he had only lost his memories of the time since coming to this other world, rather than being reduced entirely to zero.
Even so, there had been a wellspring of feeling inside him then, and the things he had built up in that emptiness had unquestionably become the foundation that drove him onward.
Black Dog was probably the same. —He might have started at zero, but he was not at zero now.

"Why do you not tell Melty that, in fact?"

"Hey, hey, didn't I tell you? Didn't I explain? She's from the same place as me. Which mea-a-ans! She's in a position to have the same motive. Imagine if she woke up to a thirst for revenge. She'd steal the reason to live I've finally gotten all to myself."

"...Well, Betty shall let that be your story, I suppose."

"What, what's with this little one? There's a limit to how cheeky you can get, you know?"

Beatrice folded her short arms and gave a long sigh, making Black Dog's cheek twitch. But there was probably no need to worry about him doing anything more. —There was no great mystery to it. At its heart, this confession was the same as the conversation Subaru and Beatrice had just had with Melty.
His foul mouth, forceful manner, and clownish antics made it hard to see, but Black Dog had chosen to trust them in his own way. That was why he had told them. That was all there was to it.

"So, after all that going around in circles, what does it come down to? Beako and I are about to take this Bird Mail and storm Russell-san's place. Do you want to tag along and grill him about Faradar while we're at it... something like that?"

"Actually, with 'Emerald Beauty' asleep, now's our chance. None of the other 'Six Tongues' are aboard this castle either. We can force 'Silver Fox' to talk if we have to. Buuut, you two would get all worked up if I didn't tell you first, wouldn't you? That's why."

"I see. ...Melty-chan just went to see Russell-san, though. If you don't want her hearing about Faradar, now's not the time."

"We'll pick our moment, pick our moment! You get the general idea, don't you!?"

It was hard to tell whether Black Dog was being thorough or thoughtless, or quite what he was thinking. But he had told them something important, and in his own manner, he had shown them trust.
They would change course and head straight to Russell. Before that, though—

"I get that you want revenge. I'll help as much as I can. As long as it doesn't go against Emilia's wishes, our goals, or our promise to Melty-chan."

"Hearing you say it aloud, you are quite thoroughly tied down, in fact."

"Yeah, but I chose to get myself tied down..."

Adding Black Dog's share to that would make the chains heavier still, but Subaru's inability to pick and choose was nothing new. Recognizing their weight, then untangling them one by one—that was his way of making his way through life in another world.

"————"

At Subaru's answer, Black Dog lowered his gaze to the Evil Eye in his palm. —Was the feeling that briefly crossed his eyes a longing for the lost homeland he himself could not remember?
He closed his hand, breaking off his awareness of the Evil Eye that had met his gaze, and spoke.
The name of his enemy. It was—

"—Sakura Element. I'll kill that bastard with my own hands. Definitely. Absolutely."

It was a declaration of resolve that ran counter to Melty's vow to bring someone back, yet was every bit as unshakable.
