Arc 10 Chapter 34: "When It Comes Down to It"

—Truth be told, what was known about the 'Beloved Children' was frustratingly little.

They were a group directly under the command of Capella Emerada Lugunica, the Sin Archbishop of Lust, and apparently had no sense of belonging to the Witch Cult themselves — they thought of themselves strictly as Capella's subordinates. Standing in a different position from the perpetrators behind the "Doll Swap" incidents occurring across the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica right now, only a select few among Capella's underlings were counted among the 'Beloved Children,' or so it seemed.

And the condition for being chosen as one of that select few 'Beloved Children' was—

"—Having a special power that no one else has. Mama... that person called it the children's 'talent.' She said someone special like her deserved a special child to match..."

"The more I hear, the more it sounds like garbage logic, and it lines up scarily well with what I'd guess. So, White Sheep, what's your basis for saying there are 'Beloved Children' here, and more than one?"

"It's a guess based on what's been happening. The fact that we couldn't hold back our emotions, and the fact that sound disappeared... those are clearly two separate things happening."

"Is there a chance it's just one person with multiple powers—like some multi-talented type?"

"I can't say it's absolutely impossible, but I don't think a 'Beloved Child' would act alone. They're made to watch each other, so none of them can run."

Even her answer to that question was smeared thick with Capella's ugliness—it turned his stomach.

Still, Subaru himself had already figured the odds of that were low. After all, the only people he could think of who held multiple unique powers were Reinhard and the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony. Both were outliers on a whole different scale, practically the extremes of good and evil—it was hard to imagine a bunch of people like that scattered around.

One power per person—just as with Divine Protections and Authorities. For now, he'd operate on that assumption.

"Which makes me start wondering what the hell I am, but let's set that aside. White Sheep, do you have any idea who the 'Beloved Children' here might be?"

"I'm sorry. Actually, even among us 'Beloved Children,' we barely know each other... The only one I know is an older woman who used to talk about how her body was really hard to kill."

"Hard to kill... well, I guess that counts as a unique power in its own way. But that's no help for right now... Oh, wait! If you're a 'Beloved Child' too, White Sheep, doesn't that mean you've got a special trick of your own?"

"—!"

Maybe there was a breakthrough to be found there, Subaru had thought—but in front of him, White Sheep caught her breath. Seeing her round eyes tremble and her cheeks stiffen, Subaru realized he'd picked the wrong topic.

She clearly didn't think well of her own power's existence. Which was only natural—it was because of that power that Capella had fixated on her, forcing her into a position she never wanted, even now.

"U-um, Mr. Ashen Star, I—"

"No, sorry—you don't have to say it if you don't want to. There are still moves we can make."

Shaking his head at her strained voice, Subaru glanced back at the corridor crushed in total silence behind them. Beatrice should be somewhere beyond that rubble—he'd been separated from her and the others, but—

"Deep breath... and focus! —Cor Leonis!"

Letting out the breath he'd drawn in, Subaru focused his awareness on his chest and activated Cor Leonis, the Authority of Greed—the power that pinpointed his companions' locations and sensed their presence. Ideally he'd be able to sense Emilia and the others clear across the distance, but that was too much to ask of his ability.

So for now, he'd settle for just the positions of his companions within Kugelschreiber Castle—

"—Beako and Black Dog are together. Crusch is moving alone. Way off in the distance, there's a pair—that'd be Russell and someone. And then..."

From the rough distance and movement, and the color of the light he sensed as each companion's presence, he could confirm the relative positions and safety of everyone in the castle.

One by one, he confirmed their safety and let relief settle in—and then, he found it.

"—Ferris."

Cut off from both Subaru's group and all the others, isolated at a point set apart all alone, he could definitely sense Ferris's presence there—faint and frail as the light was.

"There—there he is! It's Ferris! He's—he's alive!"

"Eh? That's wonderful news, but how would you know that, Mr. Ashen Star...?"

"Actually, I've got this sensor-like power that lets me feel out where my companions are! So I was checking on where everyone was, and Ferris showed up too. He's safe—well, no, 'safe' might be a stretch. His signal's pretty weak. But he's alive. And if he's alive... we can link up with him!"

At the glimpse of that ray of hope, Subaru clenched his fists so hard his nails bit into his palms.

At the restart point of his Return by Death, Ferris had already been silently spirited away from Subaru's group's line by then. In the worst case, it was possible that even at the restart point, it was already too late.

That possibility was always there. And Subaru could not bear it.

"But it's not—it's not like that. Ferris is... down below? Damn it, I should've had Beako draw me a map of the castle! Anyway, we need to go help him right now—"

"Please calm down, Mr. Ashen Star! Just the two of us alone can't do it!"

Alarmed by Ferris's uncertain condition, Subaru was on the verge of dashing off when White Sheep spread both arms and blocked his way. She stared straight at Subaru as he stumbled to a halt, and said,

"I believe you're not lying. But please, calm down. With neither Black Dog nor Emerald Beauty here, if we go up against the enemy, we'll just get taken down!"

"That's... ah, damn it, that's a fair point! I let my blood rush to my head and lost sight of things again...!"

White Sheep had doused Subaru, whose vision had narrowed with panic, with a splash of cold, sobering words. His sight cleared, though the scale of the problem hadn't changed. Only the order in which to deal with it had.

"White Sheep, can you fight? I've got some cunning and a bit of whip work."

"I—I've got staff techniques, and a little magic!"

"Fighting with this hand would be reckless... Alright, then let's link up with Beako and Black Dog, since they haven't moved from their spot. There's no way we'll catch up with Crusch—she's too fast on her feet."

"—Understood. Um, you can tell where they are with your power, right?"

"Yeah, let's go! I don't know the way exactly, but it's this direction!"

Without breaking his focus, keeping Cor Leonis active, Subaru took the lead and set off running with White Sheep behind him.

Ferris's safety still weighed on him, but for now he could only believe this was the best course. Link up with Beatrice's group fast, heal Black Dog, and head out to rescue Ferris. If possible, he wanted to pick up Crusch and Russell's group too, and go as one whole team.

Of course, if too many people clumped together, there was a risk they'd be unable to counter that emotional phenomenon best called 'Rupture,' so whether that was really the best plan was doubtful—but—

"—?"

Running along while thinking this over, Subaru frowned at a sudden sense of wrongness.

Inside the unfamiliar castle, weaving around broken walls and corridors with gaping floors, Subaru should have been sprinting along as close to the shortest route as possible, heading toward the light he sensed from Beatrice and the others. Of course, he was moving cautiously, well aware that running into an enemy along the way would be the worst outcome.

That was the idea, at least—

"Um, Mr. Ashen Star... there's something hard to say, but—"

"...Don't say it. No, wait, go ahead and say it. I want to face reality too."

Subaru made that request with a bitter look, to White Sheep as she hesitantly broached the subject.

In response, White Sheep looked around at their surroundings, her body stiffening slightly—

"—We've already passed through here once before. We're going in circles."

"Yeah, right! I figured as much too. That broken pillar—I saw that one before."

At the T-junction ahead, turning left, a broken pillar crushed the corridor. There were plenty of similar sights around, but this one was undoubtedly the same. Because the distance to Beatrice, whom he should have been heading straight toward, hadn't closed even a little.

"Don't tell me—on top of 'Silence,' who erases sound, and 'Rupture,' who sends emotions spiking, there's also something that turns people into a 'Lost Child'? Don't tell me every single 'Beloved Child' is here...!"

It was a remark said in the heat of the moment, but given that all the Sin Archbishops had once gathered together in Priestella, it wasn't something he could dismiss as an unlikely joke. But if that really were the case, their side's fighting strength was far too thin compared to back then. At the very least, he wished he could trade Rachins for Reinhard.

"I'll trade myself out for Garfiel too, and let's bring Julius or Roswaal along while we're at it. Actually, why not call in Ceci as backup and flip the whole table..."

"I—I don't think it's very likely that every 'Beloved Child' is here... but if the enemy means to settle things here, I can't be sure. There was that hidden door leading to the laboratory too, so it's not zero percent that we're just being confused by some trick built into this castle..."

"If that's true, then it says a lot about Beako's priorities that she chose sentimental reminiscing over telling us about the castle's security—talk about a Fielder's Choice. Besides, there are two things bugging me. Both of them have to do with Crusch."

At Subaru holding up two fingers, White Sheep blinked. "Ms. Emerald Beauty?"

Hearing "corridor of confusion," the first thing Subaru thought of was the prank Beatrice had once pulled on him at Roswaal Manor, the corridor that looped infinitely. That had been a kind of illusion, but Crusch's Dragon's Eye ought to have been able to see through it at a glance.

On top of that, after clashing with Black Dog amid the discord brought on by 'Rupture' and cutting him down, Crusch had said she'd go find Ferris and run off back along the original route. —That was strange too.

"If she was going to look for Ferris, she should've turned back the way we came. He was taken by 'Silence' because he was walking at the very back... In fact, I think Ferris is underground, back that way."

It was hard to pin down the relative positions precisely, but Cor Leonis was telling him that Ferris's current location was underground, closer to the entrance than where Subaru's group, having pressed deeper into the castle, now stood.

Even with her blood up from 'Rupture,' Crusch should have understood that much.

"But she ran forward. She didn't seem to feel anything was off. If the same thing that happened to her is happening to us right now..."

Meaning to head toward their destination, yet unable to close the distance at all. —Their sense of distance and direction was being warped. As if running on the track right next to the one they thought they were on.

Because of that, Subaru's group—no, probably none of their companions—could ever reach their destination.

"...Hey now, this is quietly terrifying."

He'd inferred the enemy's abilities from what had happened, but even though none of them individually seemed all that threatening, combined with the right timing and interplay, could they really be pushed this far into pure harassment?

Leave it to Capella's children—when it came to doing things people hated, every single one of them was top-notch.

"Ah, but you're a former one, White Sheep, so you don't count—separate category. Black Dog's still on probation, though..."

"—It might be better to reconsider our whole premise."

"Huh?"

"If we just keep blindly trying to link up like this, we're never going to get there. We need to make some move of our own, somewhere... Mr. Ashen Star!"

With a hand at her mouth, White Sheep, who had been deep in thought, suddenly looked up. "Yes!" Startled by the sudden force of it, Subaru watched as she gestured with both hands to indicate the whole castle.

"You can sense where your allies are, right? Anyone will do. Try pointing."

"—Okay. Beako's over there. Black Dog too."

Wondering if she had some idea, Subaru followed White Sheep's instruction and pointed toward the light he sensed. Confirming the direction, White Sheep nodded. "Understood," she said, and then:

"Okay, let's try heading that way. Ready, go!"

"Wait, you can't just say that out of nowhe—huh?"

Rushed by White Sheep without warning, Subaru hurriedly took a step—in the opposite direction from where he'd just pointed, toward Beatrice.

At the undeniably abnormal movement of his own body, his throat clicked audibly as he swallowed.

"Don't tell me my brain's wiring is being scrambled like this? If so..."

"—No, this time try closing your eyes. Then walk toward wherever you feel the Librarian's presence."

"—? My eyes? But how would that change anything..."

He didn't think it would, but all the same, he shut his eyes and focused his mind on Cor Leonis. Still sensing Beatrice by Black Dog's side, he stepped forward from the darkness behind his eyelids—

"—Just as I thought."

At the sound of White Sheep's voice, Subaru opened his eyes and checked his own position.

He'd only moved a single step, but unlike the misdirected step from before, this one had properly carried him toward where he sensed Beatrice.

"W-what was different? Could it be some kind of illusion that comes in through the eyes..."

"I think your power just now overpowered whatever this 'Lost Child' effect is, Mr. Ashen Star. The power balance between Divine Protections, Evil Eyes, and unique powers is really strict, so..."

"Unique power... right, because Cor Leonis is an Authority."

If one power could outrank another in the hierarchy of abilities, an Authority could overwrite the effect of the opponent's power.

If he closed his eyes and gave Cor Leonis one hundred percent of his focus, the obstacle of never being able to reach his destination because of 'Lost Child' could be broken through—

"That's a huge win, White Sheep! This'll work! Alright, follow me this time for real!"

"Ah, wait, please! It might work, but—"

"No, we've already lost way too much time! From here on, we're making up for i—gubwof!?"

Following the breakthrough he'd finally found, Subaru closed his eyes and boldly set off chasing after Beatrice's light—only for his body to slam headlong into a solid wall and flip spectacularly onto his back in the corridor.

He'd smashed his forehead and nose hard. And when he fell, he even failed to break the fall properly.

"Gaaah... o-of course... walls, pillars, none of that disappears...!"

"I—I'm sorry, I couldn't warn you in time!"

"N-no, it's not your fault. It's mine. My head's the problem. ...But damn it, what do we do."

Even though he'd finally found a way to avoid being tricked by 'Lost Child,' the fact remained that this was Ballpoint Pen Castle, a maze of dead ends and detours. To avoid repeating the tragedy that had just happened, the only way forward was for Subaru to keep his eyes shut and inch along, timidly feeling out walls and floors as he went—

"I could guide you from behind, telling you what the path looks like—that might help a little, but..."

"Even so, it'd still be slow going either way, huh. And on top of that, thanks to getting led astray so much, the distance between us and Beako's group has widened. Getting all the way there..."

"—Isn't realistic right now? Is that it?"

Catching up to the conclusion Subaru's thoughts were heading toward, White Sheep asked as much, and he nodded.

For the moment, the need to hurry and link up with Beatrice's group hadn't changed, but even using the method they'd found, there was no telling how long it would take to actually reach them.

And even as they stood there deliberating—

"—Crusch's movement just changed. This is... she's fighting...!"

Crusch, who had been moving swiftly through the castle, came to a stop, and began making movements other than running, right where she stood. From the shifting light and its intensity, Subaru sensed the feel of her in combat. What's more, he could even guess who she was fighting.

Because—

"She's not that far off, but I can't hear any sound of fighting."

Turning his face toward where he sensed Crusch's presence, Subaru could feel an unnaturally heavy stillness drifting from that direction, like the hush of a Japanese garden. —Without a doubt, Crusch was fighting 'Silence.'

Without realizing it, Subaru put a hand to his own stomach, remembering the broad-bladed weapon that had once run him through from behind. It wasn't hard to imagine that had been 'Silence's' weapon.

"—————"

In that instant, two thoughts sparked against each other in Subaru's mind.

The reasonable thought that if Crusch was fighting the enemy, he should rush to her side.

And the humiliating thought that if the opponent was 'Silence,' then Subaru and White Sheep's presence would only be a shackle holding her back.

"—Against 'Silence,' we've got no way to coordinate with her."

If they had the kind of wordless, breath-perfect synchrony that let two people coordinate without speaking, that would be one story—but unfortunately, Subaru and Crusch's relationship hadn't reached that point, and to begin with, their skill levels weren't remotely matched.

Therefore, a careless attempt to join her could itself end up benefiting the enemy. —That was what made Subaru hesitate to link up with Crusch in this moment.

And there was one more reason pushing Subaru toward that conclusion—

"—From here, Ferris is close."

Yes, that was it. Relying on Cor Leonis's readings, within a realistic distance that a blind, guided Subaru could actually reach, both Crusch and Ferris were there.

Crusch was locked in combat with an enemy; Ferris had either been taken captive, or was injured and left unable to move. Either way, both of them were in a different kind of crisis.

But if there was one thing worth considering, it was that right now, 'Silence' wasn't beside Ferris.

If 'Silence' was fully occupied with Crusch right now—

"Besides, if we have Ferris with us, our side's margin of safety goes way up. If we can link up with Black Dog, he should be able to get back into the fight fast too. So..."

"So your opinion is that we should go save Mr. Blue Serpent, is that it?"

Faced with Subaru's reasoned argument, White Sheep pressed her lovely lips together and fell into thought.

He understood her concern. Whatever else you could say, Subaru and White Sheep were both non-combatants, and even if they pushed things and saved Ferris, that would only make three non-combatants—far from any real boost in fighting strength. Even with 'Silence' absent, at minimum, 'Rupture' and 'Lost Child' remained as enemies. In the end, they hadn't found anything resembling a real countermeasure against those.

If they wanted to avoid risk, they should aim to link up with Beatrice's group, even if it took time.

"—But if you never take a single risk, you'll never get a single thing you actually want, either."

"Mr. Ashen Star..."

"Honestly, I know I'm asking something pretty tough. And I know you've got your own reasons, White Sheep—reasons important enough that you had to get into the Six Tongues to accomplish them. I bet failure isn't even an option for you, huh? But... but still!"

If you feared failure and stood frozen in place, you'd end up unable to take a step forward even in the moment that mattered most.

Subaru had learned that lesson through countless failures, and every single time, he'd been crushed beneath soul-rending regret and grief.

Precisely because he was that Subaru, he could say it. —The moment it comes down to it will always arrive.

"Let's show our resolve here. So we can face our precious companions without shame."

"—I..."

He didn't know if this was the best choice. Whether it was a decision worth dragging someone into the same fire and blood together with him.

But he did know that if he didn't choose this here, he'd regret it to death, in the truest sense.

And to Subaru's words, White Sheep nodded back with a look of resolve—

"I'll choose too. —So that I can face her without shame."

△▼△▼△▼△

—Always. Always, always. Always, always, always. That's what he thinks.

Why is he so useless? Why is he so pathetic? Why is he such a failure?

Why, why—only the voice tormenting himself never fades. —That, too, was a kind of self-indulgence.

"Honestly, I disgust myself..."

The moment he woke and understood the situation he was in, he wanted to die of shame. Of course, he couldn't die that way. By any method, this life of his couldn't be taken so easily.

Because that was Felix Argyle's specialty—miserable enough to make him want to curse it.

"—Hey, there's something I've always, always thought. Will you listen?"

Just as he was cursing himself, a shrill voice rang out from directly in front of him. Glancing up, he saw a long-haired woman standing there, wearing a black hat and a black dress.

She pressed both hands together in front of her face, and looked at Felix with dreamy eyes.

"Don't you think the right hand and left hand feel like they were born to fit together perfectly like this, forever—like fated beings? The right foot and left foot are different. I mean, they never press together snugly like this, right? So the feet aren't in love... but the right hand and left hand!"

"—————"

"No wait, hold on. If you look even closer, it's not just the hands—the fingers overlap perfectly too, don't they? The thumbs, the index fingers, the middle fingers! And the pinkies! Even the pinkies!"

Whatever it was that thrilled her so, the woman writhed and squealed with a flushed face. Her manner grated on him terribly, and he wanted to throw cold water on it.

"...Some people are born without one hand, you know. And some people lose a hand or fingers to injury."

"—————"

It was an extreme thing to say, but she'd been the one to throw out an even more extreme argument first.

Maybe he'd touched a nerve and would pay for it. But even so, he had a little bit of a reckless feeling that it'd be fine as long as it got her attention off track.

But—

"...That's the best thing I've ever heard."

Far from angry, the woman let out a rapturous, heated breath, her eyes going misty as she looked at Felix. Then she raised both hands over her head and began spinning in place, right there.

"Because love burns hotter the higher the obstacle! Status gaps! Racial differences! Age, gender, ethics, morals, the law! All of it, every bit of it, is fuel to make love blaze up! And do you know what the greatest obstacle of all is... hehe, ehehe, do. you. get. it?"

"—————"

He regretted trying to throw cold water on her, instantly.

This was a vein of ore that yielded nothing decent no matter how deep you dug. Or maybe it was more like he'd struck a spring that only gushed poison. —Actually, "poison" wasn't far off the mark at all.

"...You've been doing something to me this whole time, haven't you? What is this?"

"Something...? Do you mean your arms? If so, that wasn't me, that was Reborn. Honestly, such a brute. I think someone easygoing and steady, older than her, would suit Reborn well—or on the flip side, someone younger who needs more looking after than she does! What do you think!?"

"It's not about my arms. I mean you messing with my head."

"Ah, that? Hehe, don't get the wrong idea. I'm not only doing it to you. I'm doing it to everyone in this castle... oh! Could it be you wanted to be treated specially, just you? But if that's true, doesn't that mean you like me? I like you too!"

At the woman crying out about love with dreaming eyes, Felix gave up any hope of a sane conversation.

Even setting that aside, only a fool would show their hand to the enemy. As a matter of fact, Felix could feel outside interference on his own body, and his body continuously healing against it.

The special formula woven through Felix's body was a continuously active spell that kept his physical condition perpetually optimal. Thanks to it, Felix could respond to and recover from any abnormality—even changes he himself hadn't noticed—through shifts in his flesh and his Od.

Even now, it seemed to be endlessly healing against whatever strange power the woman was working on him.

There was a flaw to this power, though. —Felix's own physical fragility.

"—————"

Ignoring the woman writhing about, he took in his surroundings—undoubtedly somewhere inside Kugelschreiber Castle, though it was a space he didn't recognize. It looked like the kind of landing you'd find in any castle or manor, but the high walls had not a single window. Probably underground.

In that basement, Felix was a captive. —His arms, raised above his head, were literally skewered and pinned to the wall.

"Frustrating as it is, that's a perfect way to take someone down..."

As mentioned, Felix's body kept healing itself to a perfect state at all times, but even so, there was no way to heal a wound while a blade still ran through it, without dealing with the blade first.

And Felix, skewered through the arms and left hanging, didn't have the physical strength to pull the blades out and win his freedom.

Because of that, he had no choice but to keep hanging there like some kind of spectacle—

"Don't make that face, Felix. You're ruining that cute face of yours. And besides, besides, you know? I don't think this is such a bad thing for you either."

"Huh? Why's that?"

"Actually, right now it's really hard for anyone to reach here! Everyone can only take the 'Detour,' so there must be plenty who get discouraged along the way, plenty who end up crying. But but, if someone manages to overcome all that and make it here anyway... that would be your fated match! I, Lycoris of the 'Ring Finger,' guarantee it! Blazing love will wrap you right up!"

Because he'd reflexively answered a conversation he never meant to engage in, he'd ended up subjected to a long, rambling stream of worthless delusion, and all he'd gained out of it was the woman's name—Lycoris.

What's more, what Lycoris called hope was, without question, Felix's despair.

"...A knight taken hostage, that's the worst."

Even if his skill as a knight was lacking, even if the title was mostly for show, for someone whose position was meant to protect others to end up waiting for someone else's rescue was nothing short of a nightmare. All the more so if it meant becoming a burden to the very person he never wanted to burden.

And for Felix, the most unbearable part of all was—

"—If it were Reinhard coming for me, I wouldn't mind at all."

He almost wanted to say it in a voice on the verge of dying, but his throat and tongue stayed in perfect condition, so it wouldn't come out that way. Because he was the one who'd set up the spell to keep it that way.

Whether she knew that or not, the one who answered let out a small "hah" of a laugh, breathing it out,

"Glad to see you're a lot livelier than you look, Ferris."

Whether on purpose or just because his spirits were riding high, a boy with fiercely gleaming eyes appeared on the landing, his footsteps ringing out unnecessarily loud, all but dancing his way in.

From the entrance of the basement, the boy—Natsuki Subaru—fixed his gaze on Felix, pinned to the far wall of the broad space, then glared at Lycoris standing beneath him where he hung.

Then, with that same sharp look, he snapped a finger out to point straight at her.

"Yeehaw! Finally get to meet you, 'Beloved Child'! I've been itching for the day I'd get to see your ugly mug!"

"—————"

At Subaru, eyes flung wide, hurling out a blustering tirade at the top of his lungs, Felix frowned. Even for his usual forced bravado, this was a bit much.

"...Is this that kind of thing."

If whatever was happening to Subaru right now shared the same cause as what Felix's body kept endlessly healing against, then he could guess what Lycoris was doing.

She was scattering it into the air—a 'poison' that drove people mad.

Lycoris, displaying her literal poisonous-woman nature, hugged herself as though trembling in fear at Subaru's tirade, and said,

"Just as I thought! Exactly as I thought! Natsuki loved Felix all along! There's no other way you could've made it here past Tornel's 'Detour'! Just as I thought, just as I hoped, just as I wished, just as I dreamed, dreamed, dreamed, dreamed, so precious——!!"

Stamping her feet in place, Lycoris writhed in a rapture that had blown right past its limits.

Seeing her for the first time, Subaru didn't bother hiding his utter revulsion as he readied his whip. He seemed prepared to throw down against Lycoris then and there—but—

"...Where's Beatrice-chan?"

"Me and Beako split up! Not just her either! But don't worry, you're not alone! Neither am I! I've got a reliable ally with me! First off, the whip!"

"A whip! What are you planning to use it for!? Is that love too!? Can I watch from the side? Or should I join in too? Is that love? If it's love then I'm fine with it!"

"And courage! When it comes down to it, that's what I can count on! And I've got an ace up my sleeve too——"

He'd probably meant to say "I've got one."

At the nightmarish sight of Subaru and Lycoris carrying on together, Felix had even begun to doubt whether any of this was real.

That was why—in the next instant, his understanding couldn't keep up with what happened.

"—By 'ace up your sleeve,' do you mean me? If so, I'm sorry."

Suddenly, a figure appeared behind Subaru as he shouted at the top of his lungs, and threw its arms around him. —No. It only looked like an embrace. The reality was different. It was a full-body collision.

Why? Because that way, it wouldn't miss. It would drive in deep. —Because the sharp knife, gripped with both hands and braced low at the hip, could carve neatly into the vital spot at Subaru's side.

"Wh-White... Sheep... chan...?"

Looking at the knife driven into his own side and the one gripping it, Subaru called out that name, that nickname. Because neither he nor Felix knew her real name.

Coughing out overflowing blood with a wet "buh," Subaru sank to his knees. And looking down at Subaru as he knelt there, she—White Sheep—spoke, with a coldness in her eyes neither of them had ever seen before.

"—I made my choice. So that I, too, can have my wish granted."
