Arc 10 Chapter 44: "Close Enough to Touch, and It Hurts When We Do"

"—Subaru-sama, please stop."

Subaru had been waiting outside the guest room, trying to contain his impatience and dread, when the voice beside him made his eyes go wide. "Huh?"
He blinked and found Crusch peering at him, her brows drawn with concern. An eyepatch covered her left eye, and worry clouded the one amber eye he could see. She had reached out gently to stay his right arm. Without realizing it, he'd been digging his nails into the skin as if to claw it off.

"Ah... Sorry you had to see something so gross..."

"Please don't speak about yourself that way. Nothing you might show me could ever disgust me, Subaru-sama. I would only worry for you."

"Right. ...Yeah, sorry."

"Subaru-sama..."

It was concern he heard in her voice, without a trace of reproach, and that made him feel worse. He squeezed his arm until it hurt instead of digging in his nails.
Still worried about him, Crusch glanced at the guest room door close by.
Inside, Beatrice and Ferris were doing everything they could for Emilia. She had gone limp and lost consciousness in Subaru's arms so soon after their reunion on the Milesta Dragonback Bridge.

"—!"

The instant he thought of it, Subaru's chest seemed to tighten deep inside, and he gritted his teeth.
When he'd arrived in Kugelschreiber Castle, Emilia had been fighting to protect the great bridge across the gorge, as lovely and earnest as she'd been when they parted. He'd made it just in time to help and had barely had a moment to be glad, from the bottom of his heart, that she was safe.
Why? How could this happen? The questions and accusations he hurled at himself went around and around without end.

"...Are you feeling all right, Crusch-san?"

"Subaru-sama, this is hardly the time to concern yourself with me..."

"Please, tell me. If I just stand here keeping quiet, the frustration and helplessness are gonna kill me. I need some good news about somebody I care about."

"—Thanks to Ferris and all of you, Subaru-sama, I'm quite all right. I was terribly worn out, but I've recovered after some rest."

Granting Subaru's awkward request, Crusch touched her chest as she spoke.
Using the 'Dragon's Eye' against the Beloved Children had put her body under considerable strain. And when circumstances had forced her to cut off Ferris's arm, that must have taken a heavy toll on her too.
Crusch had pulled herself back together after all that. "And one more thing," she continued.

"I must confess that I have deceived you and everyone else for these past two months and more, Subaru-sama. I gave the impression that the 'Memories' the Sin Archbishop of 'Gluttony' took from me had returned, and that I was once again the same Crusch Karsten I had been before. ...That was an act."

"...So that's what it was. I was pretty out of it during the fight, so I didn't catch everything you said."

Being stabbed by Melty as part of the plan had cost Subaru so much blood that he'd been drifting in and out of consciousness.
But from the way Crusch had acted and spoken since, he'd had the feeling he was seeing the woman she'd been without her 'Memories': a woman whose quiet resolve stood out more than her dignified valor.
He hadn't been wrong about that. And he could also hazard a guess as to why she'd wanted to pretend.

"Was it because you felt like you weren't good enough? Like the old you would've handled things better than you could now?"

"...I'm surprised. Can you really see right through me, Subaru-sama?"

"It's nothing that impressive. I'm just speaking from experience. I've agonized over the same thing, that's all."

Crusch's eyes widened. Subaru shook his head at the memory of his own conduct.
Crusch had followed exactly the same line of thought as Subaru when he'd lost his 'Memories' at the Pleiades Watchtower. You were desperate to close the gap between who you were and who everyone took you for, so you hoped that if you passed yourself off as your old self in every particular, that absurd gap might disappear.
Crusch had done the same thing. She was simply a much better actor. Subaru hadn't lasted a day in the tower, while she'd managed to keep it up for two months.

"Ferris saw right through the performance, apparently. ...Of all people, the one person I most wanted to convince was the only one who noticed. I made quite a fool of myself, didn't I?"

"No, you were a star. Ferris caught on because he was your first knight, Crusch-san. That's all there is to it."

"Ferris, my... But I..."

Crusch quietly lowered her eyes, troubled by the conflict those words had stirred in her.
Whatever she'd pretended about her 'Memories,' the decision to dismiss Ferris as her knight had been Crusch's alone. What Subaru had just said might only have made it worse for her. But his feelings were complicated. As someone who'd lost his 'Memories,' he sympathized with Crusch. As a knight, he wanted to side with Ferris.
Either way, he knew there was only so much an outsider could say about it.

"I simply couldn't..."

"Hm?"

"I simply couldn't bring myself to accept it. Ferris entrusted me to the Divine Dragon Church and cut off my chances in the Royal Selection. ...His decision went against our vow."

Crusch let the words fall so quietly that Subaru held his breath.

"—It's been on my mind for a while. I heard you and Ferris made a vow to the late prince, Crusch-san. That was why you dismissed Ferris... But if you haven't gotten your 'Memories' back, why?"

"————"

"Why use that vow as the reason to dismiss him as your knight? I don't know if I should even say this, but without your 'Memories,' Crusch-san, the prince is..."

"—His Highness Fourier Lugunica ought to be a stranger to me?"

Having her say it first threw Subaru off. But he recovered quickly, nodded, and said, "Right." He waited for her answer.
Under his gaze, Crusch narrowed her one visible eye a fraction.

"I just apologized for allowing you to believe my 'Memories' had returned. It hardly seems right to say this in the very next breath, but... I do remember His Highness."

"You remember... Wait, what? You do have your 'Memories'?"

"Strictly speaking, I should say I recalled something. More precisely, I recalled a few memories: the helplessness I felt after losing His Highness, and the vow I made with Ferris afterward. The rest of my memories are still gone."

"————"

"This is the honest truth. Though I can hardly prove it on my word alone."

"I don't think you'd lie to me now, Crusch-san."

It must have been painfully hard for her to tell him what she'd been keeping to herself. Subaru had no intention of doubting her or reproaching her for having deceived him before.
Her answer did explain what Subaru had been wondering. It also left him with another question.

"...Why did your 'Memories' come back? And why only some of them?"

The one thing that came to mind was what had happened to Roy Alphard, brutally murdered in the Prison Tower.
They still didn't know who'd killed him, but if Roy had spat up any 'Memories' or 'Names' before he was murdered, those might have returned to their owners.
Yet in all the known cases of 'Gluttony's' Authority, no one had recovered only part of their 'Memories' or 'Name.' There had been exceptions: Rem had awoken from her 'Sleeping Beauty' state without recovering her 'Memories' or 'Name.' Subaru, for some reason, had been unaffected by the Authority and hadn't forgotten Rem. And Emilia, whose 'Name' had been eaten, had recovered it as soon as Lye was defeated. Could he count what had happened to Crusch as another exception?
Even if he did—

"It seems awfully selective, the way my 'Memories' returned. You think so too, don't you?"

"Well... yeah, I do."

"I can think of two possibilities. One is that those 'Memories' were deliberately chosen to be the only ones I got back. The other is that they were the 'Memories' I most desperately wanted to recover."

"————"

"Either way, remembering His late Highness means a great deal to me. ...And what I've done since is my own responsibility."

Crusch shook her head slowly as she finished.
Subaru was about to nod when he finally realized what Crusch meant and felt like an idiot. She had deliberately drawn a line for his sake.
She meant to take care of her own trouble, and spare him the burden when he already had his hands full with Emilia.

"Crusch-san, I—"

Subaru was about to say something in answer to her thoughtfulness. Just then, the door to the guest room where Emilia was being treated opened.
What emerged wasn't a fully recovered, bright-eyed Emilia. It was Beatrice and Ferris, the two who had examined her.
At the sight of them, Subaru couldn't wait a second longer.

"—! How's Emilia?"

"Physically, she's all right for now. Her condition's stable, and I healed all the little injuries too. Her life's in no danger. For the moment, anyway."

"...You say it like I can't just thank you and leave it at that."

A fresh wave of dread made him feel as if he was being kept in suspense. He looked at Ferris, who sighed softly and shut the guest room door behind him.
Then, as if to deliberately cut off contact with Emilia in the room, Ferris said—

"I talked it over with Beatrice-chan, and we're both pretty sure. —It's a curse mark."

"A curse... A curse mark? You mean one of those curse marks? Really?"

Unable to believe the diagnosis, Subaru asked again and again. Beside him, Beatrice tugged at his sleeve. "Subaru."
But Subaru kept pressing Ferris, who stood in front of the door.

"A curse mark, like the ones the 'Six Tongues' people and Roswaal have on their bodies, right? Something that looks that bad for you, why would Emilia have one?"

"I don't know either. I asked Beatrice-chan, but it hadn't already been on Emilia-sama, had it?"

"Of course it hadn't! I'd never let Emilia do anything that dangerous...!"

"Enough, Subaru. There's no sense taking it out on Ferris, I suppose. Ferris has done very well, in fact."

Beatrice still had hold of his sleeve as she rebuked him for lashing out. Subaru came to his senses. "Sorry..." he told Ferris.
Beatrice was right, of course. Ferris was hardly to blame. Faced with Subaru's embarrassment, he gave a little shrug. "It's fine."

"You're hardly the first person to lose their head when someone's dear to them is in danger, Subaru-kun. It happens all the time when you work as a healer. I'm used to it."

"Being used to it doesn't make it okay. You even patched the hole in my back, and I never thank you enough."

"Right. A hole in the back really ought to kill you. Though I won't let you die."

Ferris narrowed his eyes in a glare. Somehow Subaru got the feeling he was being blamed for something other than getting in Ferris's face just now, but he couldn't think straight for worry about Emilia.
A curse mark, of all things.

"Honestly, all I've ever heard about curse marks makes them sound bad and scary..."

For starters, the people Subaru knew who used them most were Roswaal and Russell. As far as he was concerned, a curse mark was a system for enforcing absolute obedience to a vow, meant for people so committed they were ready for anything.
Roswaal, having reflected on his past behavior, had put a curse mark on himself that would burn him up, soul and all, if he harmed the Emilia Camp. The kind of thing that made you want to say, "You didn't have to go that far."
Russell had done the same to members of the 'Six Tongues' to prevent leaks or betrayals, and Melty and 'Black Dog' were two people who'd borne the consequences.

You could say a curse mark was proof of a resolve that left no room for turning back. Perhaps that was precisely what it was meant to do. It was a different matter when the person who carried one was Emilia.
He had no doubts about her resolve. Of course, as far as he was concerned, he never wanted her to prove it by doing something like this. The question was—

"—Who put a curse mark on Emilia-sama, when, and how?"

Crusch voiced Subaru's exact question, narrowing her eye in thought. Neither Beatrice nor Ferris seemed to have an answer.
As if afraid of the silence, Subaru called to his partner. "Beatrice."

"I've never asked how curse marks actually work. It doesn't seem likely, but can you just put one on someone or take one off whenever you want?"

"Of course you can't, I suppose. A curse mark is a contract bound directly to the soul of the person who bears it. You can hardly make one on a whim or undo it with a wave of the hand, in fact."

"...Figures. Can you be a little more specific?"

"The soul that bears the mark is the Od, I suppose. A promise inscribed directly on one's own Od. It goes by the understanding of the person who bears it, so there's no fooling it or talking your way around it, in fact. Break it, and you might suffer pain or lose strength. In some cases, it might cost you your life, I suppose."

"Pain, and your life..."

Both were exactly what the words 'curse mark' brought to mind.
And when Subaru had been reunited with Emilia, he had watched her succumb to unbearable pain and collapse before him. He'd caught her as she fell and called out to her, even after she'd lost consciousness. Each time, her pain had seemed to deepen. It hadn't let up until Ferris came running and told Subaru to let go.
If that was any indication of the rule imposed by Emilia's curse mark, then—

"—Contact with Subaru-kun is forbidden."

"————"

"It's the only explanation. Beatrice-chan and Ram-chan can touch her without a problem. And I could touch her too, so it's probably not about the opposite sex. Assuming Emilia-sama thinks of me as a man, anyway."

"...Yeah, she does. I don't think it's about gender either."

Ferris had taken it upon himself to say what nobody wanted to. Subaru covered his face with both hands.
Emilia wasn't the sort of person to get that wrong, and Ferris probably hadn't meant it seriously either. He was just ruling things out. The first guess was the one that fit. Contact with Subaru was forbidden.
The curse mark on Emilia forbade contact with her own first knight, Natsuki Subaru.

"...We finally get back together, and this is what happens."

"Subaru..."

He couldn't help it. The bitterness slipped into his voice before he could stop it. Beatrice took a worried pinch of the hem of his tracksuit, and he couldn't even respond to that.
Even Subaru couldn't help feeling the weight of this latest, unforeseen blow.

"...Of course you'd be upset, Subaru-kun. You went as far as threatening 'Silver Fox' to meet up with Emilia-sama and the others."

"—Threatening is an ugly word, in fact. All Betty and Subaru did was explain the meaning of standing together, I suppose. And whose castle this is, in fact."

"I'm not blaming you. Serves 'Silver Fox' right to get some pushback for once."

Ferris shrugged at Beatrice, who had answered for Subaru, then looked over his shoulder at the closed door. Emilia lay beyond it.

"Like I said, I think the curse mark forbids contact between Emilia-sama and Subaru-kun. It seems to have activated when you touched her directly, but from now on it could act up whenever you get close."

"Whenever he gets close? How close would that be?"

"Hugs and holding hands are obviously out, but even talking face to face might be hard. It could be enough for her just to see him. You should probably have someone pass messages between you."

"That's terrible... Is there anything you can do?"

"—If I could do something, I would have."

Ferris looked away as he answered. Crusch, who'd put Subaru's frustration into words for him, lowered her eyes. "...I'm sorry."
While the two spoke, some of the initial shock finally began to wear off, and Subaru could think again.

"...Okay. I get it. Beako, could I ask you to be the one to go between us?"

"L-leave it to me, I suppose! Betty shall be your messenger Great Spirit, in fact."

Hearing Beatrice do her brave best to help, Subaru took a long, deep breath and let it out as if he could squeeze out some of the heaviness that had settled inside him.
Of course, it didn't ease the gloom in his heart in the least, not even a little, but—

"There's nothing else wrong with Emilia, right? Apart from the curse mark?"

"Mhm. If you trust me when I say I checked everything I know how to check, anyway."

"When it comes to a diagnosis, what's more reliable in this world than your eyes? Of course I trust you."

"—Hmm."

"How long before Emilia wakes up?"

"She's just worn out now, so whenever she's recovered enough. Give her two or three hours."

"Right. Got it. In the meantime, then... Crusch-san."

With that assessment from Emilia's physician, Subaru turned to Crusch. She nodded when he called her name, her face telling him she knew what he was about to say.
Crusch had been asleep, and she'd originally been woken to do something else. Emilia had needed urgent care, so that had come first.
Which meant—

"————"

Before he spoke, a trace of reluctance drew Subaru's attention back to the closed door. But he told himself that right now, even holding Emilia's hand while she slept would only make her suffer more.

"Let's check the identities of the others who joined us. Time for you and Ferris to do your thing, Crusch-san."

"—Barusu, is Emilia-sama safe?"

The moment Subaru and the others appeared on the terrace, Ram got to her feet and asked him.
With the hood of her 'Perception Blocking' robe down and that tense look on her face, she was the Ram Subaru knew. She must have worked herself to the bone as Emilia's only ally these past two months. He was about to reassure her that Emilia was safe, apart from the curse mark, when—

"—'Ashen Star,' please refrain from saying anything until 'Emerald Beauty' and 'Blue Snake' have finished their checks."

"Ngh. Got it. ...So, give me a minute, Nee-sama."

"I see. I understand. Very well. —Ram will remember this."

Subaru had been told to hold his tongue, and Ram didn't look the least bit inclined to make allowances for the circumstances.
Honestly, that reaction was so Ram it seemed proof enough she was genuine. But Russell would say that exploiting assumptions like that was exactly how the Beloved Children, and Capella in particular, operated. He'd been fighting them in the shadows for over thirty years. It was hard to dismiss what he had to say.
Subaru could understand why Russell was watching so sternly and insisting on the checks.

"————"

Russell, Melty, 'Black Dog,' and Rachins were all already on the terrace. Now Subaru and the others had joined them. He wouldn't have left Emilia alone in the guest room if he'd had a choice, but everyone who'd been outside her door had needed to come.
In other words, everyone in the castle except Emilia was there. That meant Ram and all the others they'd picked up at the Milesta Dragonback Bridge too.

"————"

Lined up at the edge of Kugelschreiber Castle's terrace as it flew were the people who'd escaped Royal Lugunica Castle with Emilia. No matter how many times he looked, it was an odd group.
Ram had been looking after Emilia, and Felt had been imprisoned for the same reason. Those two made sense. Then there were the other three: Filore, Heinkel, and Marcos.

"Counting Crusch-san, we've got every Royal Selection candidate apart from Anastasia-san..."

"Hah, you're right. Why not call Big Sis Anastasia too? Get all the candidates together and we'd get things done faster, wouldn't we?"

"Well, with the whole kingdom in trouble, I guess it's an option."

Felt folded her arms with a snort, and Subaru scratched his cheek as he answered her.
Checking how everybody was doing had been put off when Emilia collapsed, but Ram and Felt seemed to be doing reasonably well after their time under house arrest. Ram needed regular replenishments of mana, and Emilia had probably taken the initiative there.
Which made what had happened to Emilia painfully ironic.

"Subaru, please tell us! Ram's worried sick, and so are we. Is Emilia all right? My very best friend!"

"U-um, please calm down, Filore. Once the necessary checks are done, we'll have a proper chance to talk..."

"I do appreciate you being kind to me, but who are you?! You act as though you know me well, and I don't know where to put myself!"

"Ku ha ha! What a mess. This is taking forever. Come on, let's get it over with!"

Filore was flapping her arms and legs. Things looked complicated between her and Melty. They apparently had known each other before Melty's 'Name' was eaten, and Subaru wanted to get them talking as soon as possible too.
He turned back to Crusch and Ferris, who'd come with him.

"Everybody's asking for it. 'Emerald Beauty'-san, if you would."

"Certainly. —I'm going to ask you some questions. Please answer yes or no."

At Subaru's prompting, Crusch stepped forward and looked over the five people in line. The air grew a little tense as all five looked back at her.
With all eyes on her, she turned first to Ram at the far left.

"Are you Ram-san? —Do you have any connection to the 'Beloved Children'?"
"That's right, you're talking to Ram. Ram doesn't know what the 'Beloved Children' are, but she has nothing to do with them."

"Are you Felt-san? —Do you have any connection to the 'Beloved Children'?"
"Yeah, that's me. Never heard of any 'Beloved Children.'"

"Are you Marcos-san? —Do you have any connection to the 'Beloved Children'?"
"I am Marcos Gildark. I am not one of the 'Beloved Children.'"

"Are you Heinkel-san? —Do you have any connection to the 'Beloved Children'?"
"...Yes to the first one. Don't know a thing about the second."

"Are you Filore-san? —Do you have any connection to the 'Beloved Children'?"
"Yes! I'm Filore! I'm the 'Saintess,' not one of the 'Beloved Children'! P-probably still, anyway!"

The same questions were put to each of the five, one after another.
Their answers differed, but they each affirmed their identity and denied any connection to the Beloved Children. The bearer of the Divine Protection of Wind Reading considered every answer carefully.
Everyone's tension rose bit by bit until—

"—They are all who they say they are, and none of them is lying."

"...Right. Okay."

Crusch shook her head slowly, her lips relaxing with relief, and the tension went out of Subaru's shoulders too.
He'd believed they were genuine, of course, but it was a relief to have Crusch vouch for them. Melty and Rachins and the others seemed to feel the same way.

"If 'Emerald Beauty' can vouch for them, we can lower our guard a little for the time being. I had intended to throw them off the terrace at the first sign of suspicious behavior..."

"I figured as much, but there's no need to say it out loud. You'll only make yourself look bad."

"Knowing what will happen if it comes to that can be a deterrent in itself."

"You don't sound much different from Roswaal when you talk like that."

"—Hm. I'll bear that in mind."

In the midst of all that relief, it was Marcos who quietly reproved Russell.
He was the captain of the Royal Guard, which put him in charge of Reinhard, Julius, and Ferris, but Subaru could hardly remember exchanging a word with him.
Maybe they'd said a quick hello at the ceremony honoring the defeat of the White Whale and 'Sloth.' That was about it.

"You seem rather friendly with 'Silver Fox,' I suppose. You make it sound as if you know Roswaal too, in fact."

"Good point. In that case, are you in Roswaal's mysterious little circle, just like 'Silver Fox'...?"

If so, there might be some shady business in how the Royal Guard was run. While Subaru was entertaining that thought, Marcos caught his eye and raised an eyebrow.

"Ah, pardon the confusion, Natsuki-dono. Russell Fellow over there and I go back a long way. That's why we may sound familiar. We are most certainly not friends."

"Precisely. We recognize each other's usefulness, and our interests happen to coincide."

"I see. So with Roswaal that's a best-buddy triangle..."

Whatever the two of them said, they were too much in sync for anyone to believe they weren't friends.
Subaru could practically hear the three of them, if Roswaal were there, trading grown-up wisecracks until the end of time.
In any case—

"Barusu, you've finished talking about what you needed to, haven't you? Out with it. What's happened to Emilia-sama?"

"Yes, exactly! Where's Emilia's room, for that matter? I want to visit. I'll use my Sacrament if it comes to that!"

"I dunno if you oughta go using that thing whenever you feel like it, but what's goin' on, big bro?"

Ram and the other women crowded forward, all their attention on Emilia. With Emilia herself absent, their patience was probably at its limit.
Subaru wanted to do something about it too. He was tired of sitting there worrying.
So—

"Just hear me out. First, Beako and Ferris looked at her, and Emilia's life isn't in danger. She'll wake up soon. But..."

"But?"

"—There's a curse mark on her, and it seems to be causing trouble."

With that, he told them what he knew and asked for their thoughts on how it might have happened.



"————"

Subaru stumbled through an explanation of the curse mark on Emilia, but it didn't take long.
There was so little they knew. He could have done with an explanation himself.

"Beako says curse marks aren't something you can put on someone easily. You can't just do it in their sleep like a tattoo. Ram, did anything like that happen while you were stuck in the castle?"

"Do you think Ram would miss an outrage like that? Even if there was a moment when Ram took her eyes off her, do you think Emilia-sama would sit quietly and let someone do it?"

"That seems extremely unlikely, I suppose."

Subaru and Beatrice nodded to each other. It was hard to argue with that.
Ram was that sure of herself. Left behind in enemy territory with Emilia, she'd have been on constant alert to keep her safe. If she'd had any reason to leave Emilia's side, she'd have thought nothing of using 'Clairvoyance' to keep watch.
But if that was true—

"Then we still have no clue how the curse mark got onto Emilia."

"True enough, but there's one thing that's starting to make sense to me."

"Something makes sense?"

It was Felt who spoke. She'd been scowling ever since she heard about Emilia's curse mark.
All eyes turned to her to see if she'd found something that might move the discussion forward. She raked her fingers roughly through her shining golden hair.

"Hey, cat ears. You're the one who spotted the curse mark on Big Sis Emilia, right?"

"That's right. Why?"

"Could you check me the same way? I bet I've got one of those curse things on me too, same as Big Sis Emilia."

"What...?!"

Felt had said it so suddenly, and with such certainty, that a murmur of alarm swept the terrace. Standing at the center of all that surprise, she wore a decidedly mixed expression.
"Hey." It was Rachins, from her own camp.
He probably hadn't seen Felt for two months either, just as Subaru and the others hadn't seen Emilia and her companions. Rachins clicked his tongue, showing hardly any of the warmth one might expect from such a reunion.

"Knowing you, you didn't just pull that outta nowhere and you ain't expecting the worst for the hell of it. What is it?"

"It's simple. —Reinhard."

"Reinhard?!"

Rachins's voice went up in bewilderment at the name of the first knight who hadn't come with them. Felt didn't laugh it off. She sighed, looking thoroughly disgruntled, and went on.
What she said was—

"I knew something had to be up for that bastard to go two whole months without showing his face around me. And that curse mark would explain it perfectly, wouldn't it?"
