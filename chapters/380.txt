Just as I trailed off, Priscilla-sama whispered that in a low voice.

That was, how should I put it, um, it sounded like an oddly tempting proposal.

"You told me, did you not. That song needs no qualifications to be enjoyed. That choosing neither place nor partner is the very essence of song. Then with that very essence, will you try to save these wretches?"

"............"

"You've entered within my 'Sunlight.' And I also said this: if anything happens, rely on me. It's an early card to play, but... that, too, would be amusing. For the sake of hearing your song, I might just be willing to stop here a while."

Provocatively, Priscilla-sama crossed her arms. Whether out of habit or not, it lifted her bust.

I could see the cleavage of that violently ample bosom. I touched my own chest. *Pat.* Stared at my hand.

Sweat had beaded on that palm.

"Can I... do it?"

"Doing it won't necessarily change anything. —If that's what you believe, then it would be pointless to even try."

If I think song has no power, then yes, that would be so.

It would be so, it would be so, but it isn't so, is it—!

I unslung Lyulyre from my back and gripped it firmly in both hands.

People who weren't yet my audience, harboring rage in their bellies, were watching. What is this even about! I'm about to sing here!

—When people are about to burst into joyful song, please, just, don't fight!

"Schult, wait a moment. You'll see something interesting."

"Y-yes, ma'am, I will."

Priscilla-sama and Schult-kun stood behind me as I readied Lyulyre.

What kind of faces were they making? Argh, damn it, I don't even need to look to know!

Woman, Liliana! Crossing the world with nothing but Lyulyre, a bard destined one day to sing of heroes!

If I back down here, what kind of woman, what kind of life would that even be!

"I've had an inspiration! Please listen. —The Water-Gate City, Swaying on the Water's Surface!"

Come back to your senses, all of you, and then go wild for me!

If you've got time to be fighting, singing along is a far, far better use of it!
