Arc 6 Chapter 31: "A Recommendation for Communal Living in the Tower"

――In the end, the conclusion of the second-floor "Electra" strategy meeting was postponed.

Partly this was because the discussion hadn't produced any concrete breakthrough, but the biggest reason the conclusion got pushed back was Subaru's stomach.

"Now that I think about it, I came back from being unconscious for two whole days and immediately started tackling the tower... no wonder my stomach's stuck to my back."

Right as the conversation stalled, his stomach let out a magnificent howl, and only then did Subaru realize just how starved he'd let himself become.
It's not quite "you can't fight on an empty stomach," but hunger does affect one's thinking. As a result, prompted by the protest of Subaru's stomach, the meeting broke up for the time being.

"Honestly, part of me's relieved my stomach growled when it did..."

Setting aside the plan for the second floor, they'd gotten some sense of the pattern behind the Watchtower's "Trials" — and of the sheer nastiness of whoever set them. But at the same time, that had brought into faint relief just how dangerous Shaula was. Her combat strength should have been treated as a hazard from the start, but her empty-headed attitude and overly familiar manner toward Subaru had let his guard slip.

"『Why don't you just stick around here and have fun with me!』"

However many days, however many years, however many hundreds of years it took — Shaula had declared it without a shred of hesitation, and that alone had been enough to jog his memory of just how dangerous she really was. Perhaps that's the right way to put it.

"Not that I'm planning to, but apparently there's some rule that if we abandon the tower and try to bail partway through, she turns hostile for sure..."

Several rules about clearing the Pleiades Watchtower, the ones Shaula had mentioned, crossed his mind. Leaving a "Trial" partway through, breaking a "Trial's" rules, disrespecting the archive, destroying the tower. Break any one of them, and Shaula would turn hostile again, just as she had when she'd barred their entry to the Watchtower.
That was something to avoid — for the sake of their fighting strength, and for the sake of everyone's peace of mind.

"Still. Like Shaula said, the idea of spending years here isn't remotely realistic, and honestly, no thanks..."

Turning his attention to clearing the tower, anxiety naturally reared its head from every angle.
They'd already spent over a month just on the journey to the Pleiades Watchtower. Even if they cleared the "Trials" in rapid succession and finished conquering the tower, factoring in the same amount of time to return to Priestella meant a round trip of nearly three months at the shortest.

Of course, he had no intention of pulling out midway just because things dragged on — that included the matter of turning Shaula hostile — but the Royal Selection that Emilia and Anastasia were part of had a deadline.
Three years total — a little over one had already passed, leaving less than two years remaining.
Both the time they could spend and the time they still needed to build were finite; neither was infinite.

"But there's no use worrying about the day after tomorrow all the time, I suppose. First, what matters is tomorrow, built on today. And for that..."

"Right now it's 'eat till you're full,' huh."

"That's the one, I suppose."

Beatrice snapped her finger up at Subaru's words.
With the meeting cut short by his stomach's protest, Subaru was spending the time before the meal was ready touring the tower — making rounds of the fourth floor, designated the living quarters.
Walking alongside him, hand held snugly in his, was the aforementioned Beatrice.
They held hands periodically so that Beatrice, his contracted Spirit, could draw Mana directly from Subaru, whose Gate was malfunctioning. But setting that pretext aside, Subaru wanted to hold Beatrice's hand anyway, and Beatrice never refused it either.

"Besides, weren't you anxious too, these past two days while I was laid up? Today, feel free to relax and cling to me all you want."

"Aren't you saying something foolish. This is just Ketty making up for the Mana collection she slacked on while Subaru was bedridden. Especially inside this tower — Betty wants to always be in top condition. Being unprepared is something to avoid, I suppose."

"And yet, even when you weren't collecting Mana, you were holding my hand the whole time I was out cold?"

"That has nothing to do with Mana, and everything to do with Betty's peace of mind, so it's irrelevant, I suppose."

Beatrice puffed out her chest, insisting it had nothing to do with Mana collection, but Subaru chose not to point out that this made it, if anything, both more endearing and more embarrassing.

In any case, Subaru wholeheartedly agreed with Beatrice's reasoning.
There was the matter of the second floor's "Trial." He didn't want to think that even the floor above it, the first floor, might demand combat strength too, but the possibility couldn't be ruled out.
Besides, the strength of the Subaru-Beatrice combo wasn't limited to combat. He wanted Beatrice in top form for the sake of the tricks he'd need to pull off outside of combat, too.

"Alright, Bea-ko. Forget about me. Suck Mana out of me and get nice and fat...!"

"No matter how much Mana Betty collects, she won't go getting all plump, I suppose! Besides, no matter how fired up you get, everyone knows Subaru's own store of Mana isn't much to speak of, I suppose. It would be a problem if you collapsed from having what little you have wrung out of you."

"Hey now, then what am I supposed to do?"

"That's — why — I'm saying! At the very least, eat until you're full, rest properly to recover your stamina and store up Mana, and devote yourself to keeping Betty company. That's Subaru's mission, I suppose."

"You sound completely convalescent, and honestly there's a little bit of 'I was lonely being left alone' mixed into that, isn't there..."

Half exasperated, half charmed, Subaru made an artfully complicated face. His feet stopped as he sensed someone approaching from the far end of the corridor.
Footsteps sounded, and Emilia appeared from the other direction. The moment she noticed Subaru and Beatrice, her eyes went round with an "Ah," and she came trotting over.
In her hand was a silver metal container — a bucket.

"A bucket, huh. Look at you, Emilia-tan, working hard even at a time like this. Singing practice?"

"Fufu, what are you talking about, Subaru. It's true Professor Bucket always helps me practice singing, but you know as well as I do this isn't the time for that."

"Fair enough. So why the bucket, then?"

"That's because I wanted Professor Bucket to do his actual job for once."

Emilia beamed proudly at Subaru's question and thrust the bucket forward. Sure enough, it was brimming with water — ah, Professor Bucket restored to his original duty. Though the question of how that duty had even been fulfilled remained.

"Is this water springing up from somewhere? I thought there was nothing around the tower but the sand sea?"

"Ah, that's a misunderstanding, Subaru. If you go far, far, far enough past the tower, there should be the Great Waterfall. There's soooo much water there..."

"You went all that way just to fetch one bucketful of water? For me?"

"I'd gladly do that much and more for Subaru, but that's not it. Actually, the Spirit in that green room makes clean water for us."

"Isn't that amazing," Emilia said, sounding oddly proud of herself, but what pleased Subaru more was the remark she'd made just before revealing the truth.
That she wouldn't have minded fetching water from the Great Waterfall for his sake — that made him happy.

"While I savor that happiness, though... that Spirit in the room really is something. Not just healing wounds, but doing that too?"

"Just making water, Beatrice and I could probably manage with magic, but..."

"The area around the dunes and the Watchtower is too thick with the influence of Miasma, I suppose. Using Mana that's been in prolonged contact with that Miasma for drinking water is something we'd rather avoid, if we can help it, I suppose."

"That's what it comes down to."

"I see."

Beatrice supplemented Emilia's explanation, and Subaru found himself convinced by it.
Throughout their journey so far, securing drinking water — something that should be a major concern on any ordinary trip — had been handled almost entirely by relying on magic.
So long as you had Mana, you could conjure water freely just by preparing a container, without needing to haul around the necessary quantity of heavy water. The convenience of magic, taken to its ultimate extreme.

"Not quite air pollution, but I guess you could call it Mana pollution caused by Miasma. Does taking it in through drinking water and the like actually hurt you?"

"Whether there's a dramatic change to the body, I can't say, I suppose. But the more you take in, the more Miasma accumulates within you. In the worst case, you might end up with a constitution like Subaru's, drawing in Witchbeasts, and that wouldn't be strange at all, I suppose. It gives Betty the shivers, I suppose."

"I hate to say it about myself, but this constitution makes life pretty rough..."

Subaru had a habit of putting this constitution to use at critical moments, but outside of last-ditch situations it was basically never helpful. If anything, there was a real danger that a casual hike up a mountain could get him surrounded by Witchbeasts before he knew it.
A constitution that attracts Witchbeasts — obviously, not having one would be far better than having one.

"So, we're using spring water purified by the green room's Spirit as much as possible for our water. During the two days Subaru was asleep, that's how we managed things too."

"Huh, is that right."

Subaru was thoroughly impressed by this explanation of the Pleiades Watchtower's hitherto-unknown living conditions.
And hearing about the spring water brought up, belatedly, an obvious question in Subaru's mind — one not unrelated to his earlier wariness about Shaula.

"Water's fine, but what about food? We did stock up in town before crossing the sand sea, but there was trouble along the way too."

"Don't worry. Even with all that commotion, the food didn't go flying off anywhere. It's all still safely in the Dragon Carriage. But..."

"However cleverly we ration it, it'll only last about a month, right?"

Even setting aside drinking water, there was a limit to how much weight the Dragon Carriage could carry. And it wasn't only the passengers, Subaru and the others, who needed food. Patrasche and Giant, their Dragon mounts, needed to eat too. Accounting for all that, the food they'd loaded was provisionally enough for one month.
It wasn't simply a matter of "as long as we escape the tower alive, that's fine." The deadline of one month was meant to let them properly conquer the Pleiades Watchtower in the true sense of the word and make it safely back to the nearest town.

That was the deadline within which Subaru and the others had to conquer the Pleiades Watchtower.

"――――"

"Though, I don't plan on staying here a whole month anyway."

"Subaru..."

Subaru smiled at Emilia, whose eyes had briefly clouded with unease.
There was a deadline, and no shortage of difficult problems, but flinching from them wouldn't get them anywhere.

"After all, in just one day — well, since I got a late start, technically it's my third day at the tower — but in that time we cleared the first 'Trial,' and Emilia-tan breezed through the second 'Trial' too."

"It wasn't exactly breezing through..."

"This is a moment for bluffing, so it's fine to say breezing through."

Subaru wagged an important finger at the earnest Emilia, then pulled Beatrice's hand, still linked with his, drawing her to stand in front of him, and rested his chin atop her head.
Then the two of them, Subaru and Beatrice, looked up at Emilia together.

"Doesn't matter if the opponent's the strongest 'Sword Saint' in history. That eyepatch-wearing sexual-harasser chopstick bastard — I'll wreck him to pieces with my tricks and Bea-ko's power, and we'll blow right past him."

"Isn't that right. Wrecked to pieces, I suppose."

"Wrecked to pieces..."

"Nobody says 'wrecked to pieces' these days."

"That's unfair! Just now! Both Subaru and Beatrice said it!"

Caught in the coordinated trap sprung by Subaru and Beatrice, Emilia flushed red with indignation.
Having a new pattern mixed into their familiar routine, Emilia trailed a faint trace of dissatisfaction, then let out a sigh as if to say there was no helping it.

"Mm, okay. Understood. Somehow, when Subaru says it, it sounds like it'll be easy. But that's really, really reassuring, you know."

"Yeah, feel free to believe in me, expect things from me, rely on me, and love me. I'm your knight for exactly that purpose."

"That's true. I'm counting on you, my knight."

"Wait, the 'love me' part didn't get denied, so now I'm the one flustered..."

"――?"

The sweet nothing he'd slipped in among the banter had sailed right past her, unremarked, leaving him deflated. Though if she'd taken it seriously, he'd have been flustered by that too — which meant the simple solution was just not to say it in the first place.

Anyway—

"A little late to bring it up, but it's kind of odd that Emilia-tan's out here fetching water and all. Isn't this — well, not exactly knight's work, but it should be the servant's job in a master-servant relationship?"

"It's fine. Subaru's my knight, but I'm not particularly trying to make it a master-servant relationship. I want Subaru by my side. As long as you protect that much, you can be spoiled and lazy while you're recovering."

"What is with you, Emilia-tan! If you spoil me that much I might just die of happiness!"

"Besides, I'm in charge of dinner today! I want to do everything from start to finish myself!"

"Both of those sound like her genuine feelings, that's the tricky part about Emilia, I suppose."

Beatrice sighed at Emilia's declaration, fired up and raring to go. Then, with Subaru's chin still resting on her head, she waved a hand and said,

"There, Emilia. With you around, Subaru never settles down. The meal preparations keep getting delayed too, so you'd best hurry along, I suppose."

"Really? Okay. Then, see you both later. Look forward to it, okay?"

"We'll keep it as something to 'look forward to,' I suppose."

In Subaru's place, thoroughly disarmed as he was, Beatrice saw Emilia off. In doing so, Beatrice's reply to Emilia's words reflected a subtle difference in how the two of them understood things.

"When dinner's ready I'll call you, so don't wander too far, okay?"

"We'll probably still be on the fourth floor, I suppose. Whether you'll be able to hear us, though..."

"Got it. I'll call really loud, then."

"...Understood, I suppose."

Having exchanged this thoroughly heartwarming little back-and-forth, Emilia waved with her bucket in hand and disappeared down the far end of the corridor. Watching her go, Beatrice looked up at Subaru's face resting atop her head.

"Now then, it would help if you'd settle down a bit soon, I suppose."

"...Yeah, I'm fine. I don't really get it myself, but Emilia-tan just feels unbearably precious right now. Like I passed through a world where everything but Emilia-tan looked faded and colorless, and finally got to reunite with her real smile."

"I have no idea what you're saying, even more than usual! Besides..."

"Besides?"

Subaru had just delivered his faint feelings for Emilia in a poetic, self-satisfied tone with a look to match. Beatrice leaned her back against him in response, settling snugly into his chest, and said,

"It's unfair that only Emilia gets that. Subaru is Betty's partner, I suppose. I'd like you to properly recognize that and act accordingly, I suppose."

"You're seriously adorable, you know that?"

"Migyaaa, I suppose!"

She said something so unbearably cute that Subaru scooped Beatrice up, nuzzled his cheek against hers, and began spinning round and round in the corridor.

"It's fine, don't worry. You're precious to me too, properly. I don't know how to put it, but right now the whole world looks beautiful to me, and out of all of it, you're the standout. Bea-ko, I love you!"

"W-wai, wait, understood, I understood, so put me down, I suppose! I said I understood, I said I understood, I suppose!"

Beatrice shouted, face flushed red, but Subaru didn't set her down.
Round and round and round he went, Subaru kept spinning happily, happily down the fourth-floor corridor with Beatrice in his arms.

The two of them kept up their happy racket for quite some time, until Emilia's loud call of "Dinner's ready!" finally put a stop to it.

Now, the time before dinner wasn't spent entirely on spinning around.
Beatrice's presentation — a tour and guide of the fourth floor — was carried out quite thoroughly by her, who, having two extra days' seniority in tower living over the unconscious Subaru, made for a proper senior guide.

That said, this was a place originally named a "watchtower," after all.
There was no reason to expect any consideration for comfortable living, and in fact, even the fourth floor, treated as the living quarters, had no amenities whatsoever meant to improve livability. The only special features were the green room, used for treatment, and the large chamber housing the staircases connecting to the third and second floors respectively.
Aside from that, save for the grand staircase leading to the lower floors, the place was simply dotted with any number of small rooms at irregular intervals.

"There's no map, so I can't say for sure, but if there were one, it'd definitely be a nasty layout, this tower. I really hate buildings that feel all haphazard like this at the design stage..."

"What are you even saying... In the first place, given how spiteful the nature of the 'Trials' already is, there's not much point worrying now about the twisted personality of whoever built it, I suppose. It's far too late for that, I suppose."

"Ah! That's you insulting Master, right now! This shrimp just insulted Master, who built the tower! Is that okay with you, Master?! If you go easy on her just because she's a shrimp, she'll get carried away! You oughta scold her hard, no adult-restraint level! And whatever leftover scolding there is, give it to me! Bring it on!"

Shaula, seizing on Beatrice's remark as if she'd caught the devil himself. Not that Beatrice's remark was any kind of gotcha to begin with, but explaining that would be a hassle.
He couldn't very well lecture her, one by one, on the difference between Subaru, "Master," and the "Sage." Especially not while he was in a position to happily exploit that misunderstanding.

"Now, now, don't keep fooling around and making a fuss. Shaula, behave yourself too."

"Ehh, I don't accept that. Discrimination. Shrimp discrimination~"

"If you'd really done something bad, Subaru wouldn't spoil Beatrice — he'd scold her properly. The fact that he doesn't means that's not what's happening. Besides, it's only natural for small children to be doted on. Both me and Shaula just have to bear with it."

"Being treated like a child as a matter of course, even though I'm younger than Betty, is something I can't accept, I suppose..."

"Now, now, this is your chance to show off some senior generosity."

Subaru smiled wryly, soothing the discontented Beatrice.
At Emilia's call that the meal was ready, everyone gathered in the fourth floor's large chamber — the place where cargo unloaded from the Dragon Carriage, food included, had been stored.
Everyone except Rem and Anastasia, unable to attend due to being unconscious, was gathered for the meal, faces all present together.
Everyone besides those two, that is—

"――Before we eat, may I say one thing, Lady Emilia?"

The one who spoke up, arriving in the room last of all, was Julius.
Julius had been thrown into the green room to focus on treatment, but the reason he'd shown his face at mealtime now surely wasn't hunger.
He'd come here even after declining the suggestion to have his meal brought to the green room instead.
In response to Julius's question, Emilia, who'd been running the show, nodded, "Sure," and said,

"Of course, go ahead. Though there's no need for you to ask my permission, really."

"With Lady Anastasia absent, you are the one to be honored most in this gathering. And I have already caused enough trouble through my own selfish actions. I cannot, at this late stage, presume otherwise."

Receiving Emilia's words, Julius shook his head, his eloquence as smooth as ever.
His formal manner and dutiful thinking were very much like his usual self. But there was one person present who couldn't receive that statement so favorably.

"How commendably conscientious of you. I'd have preferred you'd known that much beforehand."

"Ram..."

"Reckless and stubborn is more than enough with just Barusu. Especially when someone I'd counted on to be sensible goes charging ahead on their own, disappointment is only natural. Going forward, I'd like to believe there won't be a repeat."

The one who so scathingly assessed Julius's unilateral decision was Ram, wearing her usual cool, composed expression.
The coldness in her voice and eyes was as always, but the stiffness of her expression seemed stronger than usual. Even in her harsh words, the consideration typical of her seemed diminished.

"Ram, that's too much."

"...My apologies, Lady Emilia. I'll be mindful of it going forward."

Chided by Emilia for the harshness, Ram apologized at once and bowed her head slightly to Julius as well. Once she withdrew like that, there was no room to press further.
And it would be wrong, too, to blame Ram for having less composure than usual. She didn't hate Julius, nor did she resent him. She simply, earnestly, wished to save Rem, and wanted to devote the whole of her heart to that end.

"I've caused Lady Ram and everyone else a great deal of trouble."

Knowing this, Julius took Ram's harshness as a rebuke he deserved for his own actions, and bowed his head without arguing back, settling the matter.
Taking this time before the meal to make this reckoning — that was what Julius had wanted to do.
Subaru had some sense of the true intent behind Julius's unilateral action. That was why Subaru could forgive him for it — but whether Julius could forgive himself was another matter entirely.
As a first step toward that, this ritual had been necessary.

"Right! Julius apologized. I accept the feelings behind that apology. So as far as I'm concerned, the matter of what was wrong here is closed."

Emilia clapped her hands and said this in response to Julius's apology. At her words, Subaru of course, and Beatrice too, nodded along.

"Well, I already said what I wanted to say, so beyond that, mercy of a fellow warrior, I guess."

"Betty too, mercy of a fellow warrior, I suppose. He can make up for it with how he performs from here on, I suppose."

"――Forgive me."

At the two of their responses, Julius, eyes closed, murmured just that.
Following Subaru and Beatrice, the next to react to Julius's expression of gratitude was Meili. Sitting cross-legged on the floor, fiddling with one of her braids with a finger, she said,

"Well, you didn't end up dyin', so isn't that good enoughh? I don't really care one way or another about mister knight."

"Master said it's a no-problem, so this one's no-problem too. No-pasoo."

"That's not how you use 'no-pro'..."

Meili's apparent indifference sounded less like considerate tact and more like genuine sentiment. Shaula, tagging along with her, probably didn't feel much of anything about it seriously, either.
Setting aside Shaula, someone tied to the tower who showed no sign of opening her heart to anyone besides Subaru and, to some degree, Meili — Meili's own position was a complicated one too.
And then—

"――――"

Only Ram, who'd said nothing even to the direct apology, neither forgave nor withheld forgiveness.
She simply drew the front of her white robe closed and lowered her eyes to her meal.
And Julius quietly accepted that too. As did Subaru and the others, besides Julius. This much was a matter between the two of them alone, not something outsiders should intrude on.

"――Well then, let's get properly started on the meal. Today, Ram and I prepared it."

"We weren't allowed to use fire, so aside from being a bit rough around the edges, it should be decent."

"Yes, that's right. It's soooo decent. ...Isn't 'decent' a weird way to put it?"

Emilia, having regathered herself, took the lead with this announcement. At the follow-up remark after it, Emilia tilted her head, but Ram offered no support whatsoever.
In any case, through this exchange, they gathered around a precious meal in the Watchtower.

Most of the food loaded onto the Dragon Carriage was preserved food — dried meat, smoked goods, and the like. Naturally, some blandness and a lopsided quality to the meals was unavoidable — but here too, magic came to the rescue.
By applying Emilia's specialty ice magic, they could operate a makeshift refrigeration setup built into the Dragon Carriage, making it possible to keep fresh ingredients on hand as well.
Thanks to this, fruit and fresh produce could be preserved to no small degree, so their food situation maintained considerable stability.

"Even so, we have to use up the ingredients that spoil quickly first."

"Even having that to worry about is a luxury, honestly. Seriously, E.M.G. — Emilia-tan's Marvelous Goddess-of-Food status! Thanks to this trip, Emilia-tan's cooking skills have come along a ton, and if I could, I'd want you to make me miso soup for the rest of my life."

Thanks to that, the girl who used to char her Daisukiyaki pitch black was nowhere to be found here anymore.
That was because she'd mainly been assigned to dishes that didn't require using fire.

Incidentally, among this trip's members, the ones who could actually cook properly were Subaru, who'd picked up cooking skills during his year as a servant; Julius, who handled anything he was given without a hitch; and, surprisingly, Ram, who could also cook properly when she put her mind to it — three people in all.
For reference, on the road to Priestella, the three men — Subaru, Otto, and Garfiel — had decided cooking duty via rock-paper-scissors.
Setting that aside though—

"The reason Ram doesn't stand in the kitchen at the mansion isn't because she can't — it's simply that she doesn't. Steamed Potatoes aside, she has no intention of applying her skills to ordinary cooking. That job, she happily hands over to Frederica and Petra."

Ram wore a troubled expression, as though she'd raised a question about her own remark to herself. Watching her profile, Subaru too let out a faint sigh of his own.

But ever since Rem's memory had been stripped from the world, Subaru had come to realize that relationship hadn't meant exactly what it appeared to on the surface.
In truth, Ram surely could do a competent job at anything she turned her hand to, not just maid work. And that fact and the loss of Rem were, in terms of raw ability, probably unrelated.

In other words, Ram had been just as capable of doing things the way she does now even back when Rem was alive and well. That she hadn't — that likely wasn't because of some innate laziness on her part.

"――――"

That was something Subaru had no intention of deliberately digging up.
Something Ram herself surely couldn't understand right now. And its true meaning, he felt, was something that needn't be spoken of even after Rem safely returned. Still, that aside—

"I figured as much, but there's not a shred of manners in the way you eat."

"Mmf mmf... Ah? Did Master say something just now?"

At Subaru's frown, Shaula blinked, cheeks stuffed full like a squirrel's pouches.
In this world, there was no shortage of people who squandered their own good looks needlessly, but Shaula was top-tier even among them. She could rival Liliana, honestly.

"Don't talk with your mouth full while you're eating. Pick one — eat or talk."

"You just said a minute ago that water made with Miasma-tainted Mana isn't fit to drink."

"So, did it work out?"

"Seeking to improve the food situation and so forth, various twists and turns of trial and error were reportedly repeated. But no stable results were ever achieved. Of course, it's not that eating it is poisonous, but..."

"But?"

"Apparently there's a considerable problem with the taste."

At the mention of a taste problem, Subaru's face went slack with disbelief.
Surely even the worst taste could be masked with enough spices thrown on. He'd heard that meat from omnivorous animals tends to be gamey, sure, but if it wasn't poisonous, there should have been more that could be done about it.

"This is purely what I've read in literature. Lady Shaula, from actual experience, what would your impression be?"

"Tastes like sand soaked in sewage."

"Ah, that's the kind that spices can't save."

"If Master wants to try it, I'll roast a whole Gama-oh for you next time. Disgustingly awful, but addictive. Just kidding."

The taste seemed beyond imagining, which made it hard to blame Shaula's enthusiasm for her meal.
Subaru politely declined the offer of a whole roasted Gama-oh. Not that it mattered, since that unsettling Witchbeast had been engulfed in flame from head to toe even before you got to the roasting part.

"Still, the food situation was already something to worry about..."

Subaru muttered this, holding his head in his hands.
Before his eyes were Shaula, stuffing her mouth full of food with a beaming, on-the-verge-of-tears smile, and Emilia, her maternal instincts stirred by Shaula's hearty appetite, looking dangerously close to bringing out seconds.

One month had been the time limit calculated based on their remaining food supply.
But if Shaula kept up eating at this pace, Subaru thought, it might dwindle even faster than that.


※ ※ ※ ※ ※ ※ ※ ※ ※ ※ ※ ※


Once the meal ended and they'd finished washing up with the spring water (mostly just wiping themselves down), the day broke up and bedtime arrived.

Given the circumstances, staying up all night hashing out strategy might be the proper thing for a party trying to conquer the tower to do, but it seemed unlikely that would actually produce a breakthrough as things stood.
Leave tomorrow's problems to tomorrow's self — a bit of a convenient way of thinking, maybe, but.

"Realistically, it's not the kind of problem where a solution's just gonna pop out of nowhere. I'd rather take the time."

The nature of the "Trials" being what it was, in the worst case they might have to challenge the second floor again tomorrow with no real plan. Rather than overthinking it, a "throw yourself at it and see" approach — though he'd really rather not literally get thrown anywhere — seemed reasonable, at least given that the examiner, the first-generation "Sword Saint," didn't seem to actually intend to kill them.
Or perhaps they might find some thread toward a breakthrough through conversation, the way Emilia had managed to draw out a concession through talking, in the end.

"Even to do that much, gotta leave some room for my head to keep turning. So I've gotta eat properly, rest properly, and go in at full strength."

Slapping both cheeks with his hands, Subaru chewed down his various anxieties and thought this through.
More or less, Emilia and the others agreed with Subaru's view too — or rather, it was less agreement than a resigned sense that, for now, this was all there was to do.
And so, tonight they broke up. Each would return to the Dragon Carriage serving as their bedroom, and go to sleep there in preparation for tomorrow.

"Subaru, Betty's staying in the Dragon Carriage with Emilia and the others tonight, I suppose."

"Oh, got it. Sorry about that, Bea-ko. Don't stay up too late. If you don't grow any taller you'll stay small forever... that's actually kinda cute. Alright, stay up late, Bea-ko."

"No need to worry, Betty isn't getting any bigger than this anyway, I suppose. She'll stay cute forever, I suppose. So going to bed early won't matter one bit, I suppose."

Yawning, Beatrice waved a hand lightly and parted ways with him. As she left, Beatrice took Emilia's hand, and the two of them bid Subaru goodnight and departed.

"Emilia-tan, take care of Bea-ko. See you tomorrow."

"Mm, see you tomorrow. ...Subaru, don't stay up too late either, okay."

Without chiding him for staying up at all, Emilia simply said that much and headed for the lower floors down the grand staircase. Watching her off, Subaru gave a light stretch and made his way down the fourth-floor corridor, footsteps echoing.
His destination was easy enough to guess. The green room, with its vine-covered door.
And there—

"Subaru?"

"...Oh, it's you."

In front of the room, Julius, whom he'd run into, widened his eyes in surprise at Subaru's appearance.
He'd apparently been just about to enter the green room himself, and at the sight of Subaru arriving, he narrowed his eyes for a moment before nodding as though something had clicked into place.

"I see. It seems you've come to this room for the same purpose as me."

"Though the person we're here for is different. ...Should I let you go first tonight?"

"No..."

Shaking his head slowly at Subaru's offer, Julius turned his gaze to the closed door. After a moment of silence, his golden eyes slowly returned to Subaru.
Then, taking a step back as though yielding the doorway, Julius said,

"This time, I should be the one to yield to you. Come to think of it, you were unconscious for two whole days, until just this morning. You only just managed to report that you were safe — surely, she too must have been yearning through the night."

"...Well, if you're offering, I'll take you up on it honestly."

Scratching his head at the elegant turn of phrase, Subaru glanced sidelong at Julius.
There was no sign of strain on his face, but Subaru had never been good at reading other people's feelings to begin with. If the true intent was hidden behind an expression, he wouldn't be able to see through it.

"Are you really okay with that? You'd probably rather be by her side."

So, with no other choice, Subaru let out a sigh and spoke his honest thoughts.
Julius, hearing this, gave a faint smile and said, "That's true,"

"It's a fact that, if possible, I'd like to watch over Lady Anastasia by her side when she wakes. ...It's just also a fact that I'm hesitant about what to say to her first, once she opens her eyes. Pathetic, I know."

"Your first line should be 'I was worried, I'm glad you woke up,' shouldn't it? The problem starts with the second line. That one... well, that's up to you."

"Heh."

"Why are you laughing? I answered pretty seriously there."

It had been a reasonably earnest answer, but it apparently didn't sit well with Julius. At Subaru's affronted look in response, Julius turned on his heel, presenting his back.

"Your way of thinking is unconstrained. ――That, I envy."

"That sounds like you're calling me an idiot and it kind of stings. Hey, where are you going?"

"I'm yielding this place to you. I'll return to the Dragon Carriage and rest. I'm a bit tired today, you see."

Raising a hand over his shoulder, Julius said this as he walked away.
Whether he'd recovered enough to obliquely refer to the "Trial" as "a bit tiring," or whether that was just bravado, was, as usual, hard for Subaru to tell.
Hard to tell — but even so—

"――Julius, still, you really should wait for Anastasia-san to wake up. Once I'm done with my business, I'll come wake you, so do that."

"――――"

"Just so you know, I've racked up way more regrets than you have, I bet. That's advice from someone with that track record. Take it a little to heart, would you."

Subaru called this out to the retreating back disappearing down the far end of the corridor. He got no reply, so there was no telling how Julius took it. Still, he doubted Julius would let it roll in a bad direction. That much trust, at least, Subaru held for him.

"...Coming in."

Shaking his head, Subaru cut off his concern for Julius there, pushed the door open, and stepped into the green room. The interior, dimly lit by soft, pale light, was as dominated by abundant greenery as ever, and on the beds woven of vines lay two girls asleep.
Anastasia on the nearer bed, and Rem on the farther one.

"And all the way in the back, there's you."

"――――"

Looking up at Subaru, who'd come to visit, was Patrasche, who let out a small sound from her throat. The jet-black Earth Dragon's manner was so natural, it was as if she'd known all along Subaru would come.
Perceptive as ever, Patrasche had probably sensed his arrival in advance. Naturally, she'd already opened half of her own vine bedding, leaving space for Subaru to sit.

"You really are something... your wounds look like they're healing up nicely too."

Smiling wryly, Subaru traced a palm over Patrasche's black scales.
The marks from her battle with the Gama-oh underground had healed up quite well after three days of treatment. Patrasche naturally tended to hide her own discomfort from Subaru, but this time it seemed less like bravado and more like she really was recovering steadily.

"Seriously, thank the heavens for this room's Spirit. I can't thank them enough. If this room hadn't been here, who knows how bad things could've gotten."

He wouldn't dare call Patrasche's injury a stroke of luck, but it was a fact that the damage from crossing the Augria Sand Dunes had been kept to a minimum. The only injured members of the party were Subaru and Patrasche, just the two of them. And even that damage was within a range this green room's effects could reliably recover — better than a good outcome, all things considered.

Including the treatment of Julius and Anastasia, injured in the second floor's "Trial," Subaru had been saved by this green room's existence over and over. It was almost as if someone had prepared it just for this purpose.

"Now then."

Having thoroughly reaffirmed his affection with Patrasche, Subaru took a light, deep breath, then headed toward the far bed — Rem's.
When Subaru stood beside the bed, the vines gathered with a rustling sound, forming a chair for him to sit right beside the bed.

"There's such a thing as being too attentive."

He'd just been going on about endless gratitude for the green room's Spirit, and now this. It was as if he'd been buttering them up on purpose to be treated well.
Assuring himself that hadn't been his intention, Subaru settled in beside the bed.

"Julius said something about Rem yearning through the night, but..."

That was wrong.
Because there was nothing to it, really. The one who'd been yearning for this time, to speak with Rem undisturbed by anyone, surely wasn't her — it was Subaru himself.


※ ※ ※ ※ ※ ※ ※ ※ ※ ※ ※ ※


――What alerted Subaru to something wrong was the sensation of his shoulder being gently shaken.

"――Uh?"

Lifting his bowed head, Subaru blinked several times where he sat. Contrary to his body's reflexive motion, his consciousness lagged behind unusually badly.
Slowly, his awareness surfaced. It caught up to reality, and he realized.

"Was I... asleep?"

Hand to his chin, Subaru was startled to find that his consciousness had slipped into sleep.
He was in the green room, and it seemed his body had dozed off sitting in the chair beside Rem's bed. But his memory of the chair's shape and its current form were completely different.
The chair he remembered, woven of vines, hadn't even had a backrest. But the chair now had grown a large backrest to gently support Subaru as he slept, transforming into an egg-like shape that seemed to envelop his whole body. The Spirit's hospitable nature had gone into overdrive.
Thanks to that, it had been too comfortable, and his little doze had nearly turned into a full night's sleep.

"Guess I was fairly worn out myself... wait, Patrasche?"

Muttering about the fatigue he hadn't even realized he had, Subaru turned to look at the cause of his waking — Patrasche, who had stretched her long tail out to touch Subaru's shoulder.
The beloved Dragon, curled up in her bed of grass, had woken him with her tail as if trying to convey something urgent. Wondering what she was trying to say, Subaru furrowed his brow, and—

"――No way."

Following Patrasche's gaze, Subaru leapt down from the chair. He rushed toward the bed by the green room's door — the bed where Anastasia should have been sleeping.
Past tense, because the girl who should have been there was nowhere to be found. At that fact, Subaru felt the blood drain from his face in an instant.

"After — after all that big talk I gave Julius..."

Because Subaru had dozed off, he'd failed to let Julius be there for Anastasia's awakening. That, even just on the surface of it, was more than embarrassing to admit.
――No, the problem wasn't limited to that.

"She woke up, and then...? Where'd she go? Bathroom? Without waking me either?"

It wasn't the fact that she hadn't asked him to accompany her to the bathroom that puzzled him. What puzzled him was that Anastasia — Echidna, to be precise — would slip out of the green room alone, without a word to sleeping Subaru.

He didn't want to think that, right after the second floor's "Trial," she'd vanished off somewhere — surely not gone off to challenge Reid a second time, just like Julius.

"The bed's still a little warm. ...Gotta go find her."

A faint trace of Anastasia's warmth still lingered in the vine bed where she'd been lying. Patrasche had woken him for this reason too.
Presumably, it hadn't been all that long since Anastasia had left.

"Patrasche! Watch over Rem for me! And — thanks for waking me!"

"――!"

Waving a hand at the short response, Subaru tumbled out of the green room.
His mind racing, he had no idea where Anastasia might have gone. If it were Subaru himself, the very first thing he'd want to confirm would be Emilia and Beatrice's safety — following that logic, would she have headed for Julius after all?

"No, wait, right now it's Eridna inside her. That'd be too straightforward for her. In that case..."

They were short-handed. Setting aside the embarrassment of his own dozing off, right now, above all, he needed to call out to the others too and find Anastasia's whereabouts—

"――Huh?"

About to head downstairs to call for people, Subaru held his breath.
It was a breath that escaped him out of sheer stupefied, blank amazement — the kind of dazed sound one makes upon seeing something that shouldn't be, something impossible.

"――――"

Something crossed languidly through Subaru's blankly widened field of vision.
It was a bird, spreading a pair of white wings, flying gracefully down the not-especially-wide corridor.

"Why... is there a bird, inside the tower?"

At the sight of a bird that shouldn't exist, Subaru murmured this blankly.
There wasn't a single thing resembling a window connecting to the outside anywhere on the walls of the Pleiades Watchtower. The tower was a structure completely sealed off from the outside world, and the only thing connecting inside and out was the great door on the fifth floor.
At least, that was how Subaru understood it, and Beatrice, who'd guided him around the fourth floor, hadn't so much as hinted at the existence of any window connecting to the outside either.

"――! W-wait!"

Sensing something seriously amiss, Subaru hastily began chasing after the retreating bird.
For a moment, he hesitated. Should he chase the bird here, or should he go call someone, reveal Anastasia's absence and the bird's existence, and ask for help?
But Subaru chose to chase the bird. Following an instinct he couldn't quite put into words, that letting the bird out of his sight here carried the greater risk.

"――――"

Naturally, a flying bird had no kindness to spare, no inclination to rest its wings at Subaru's call. Languidly, as if leaving Subaru behind, it soared on down the corridor, its form growing more distant, further and further in.
He chased after it desperately, chased and chased, and eventually—

"――! It disappeared? No way."

Reaching the very end of the corridor, Subaru's voice cracked.
The fourth floor's corridor curved in a circle following the tower's shape. But it wasn't connected so as to form a complete loop — about halfway around, it was blocked by a wall.
Picture a clock face: there was a wall at the twelve o'clock position, where you'd be stopped whether approaching from the left or the right.
Knowing this, Subaru had figured that unless the bird could open a door, it couldn't slip into any room, and he was bound to catch it — and yet—

"No sign it hit the wall and fell. What's going on here...?"

Unable to track the vanished bird's trail — or rather, its wing-trail — Subaru looked around in bewilderment.
Unfortunately, there was no room in sight the bird could have fled into. Its appearance had been sudden, and now its exit was just as sudden. It felt like being led astray by smoke, as if he'd been dreaming the whole thing.
Except, it was quickly proven that it hadn't been a dream.

"――This is a feather, right?"

Backtracking a little down the corridor, Subaru found a white feather lying on the floor. Not certain proof, but given the circumstances, this was surely a feather from the bird he'd been chasing.
This would serve as proof the bird had really existed. He could bring it back and tell Emilia and the others, "There was a bird inside the tower!" — but that wouldn't solve anything.
As for clues to the vanished Anastasia, there was nothing—

"No, wait... if a feather fell here, that means—"

There had to be something.
Thinking this, Subaru searched every inch of the floor and walls around where the feather had fallen. He patted along the stone floor and ceiling, checked nearby rooms, even tried pushing against the wall.
But there was no trap or mechanism to be found anywhere, and he began to feel a rising impatience as time slipped away for nothing. Just as he was thinking he really ought to go call for help—

"Ah――!?"

He'd been sweeping his palm across the spot where the feather had fallen. The instant he thought his fingers had brushed the nearby wall, they slid clean through it.
It wasn't an optical illusion. Reaching out cautiously, he confirmed — his hand couldn't touch the wall.

"But I should have checked here already..."

Not that he'd failed to check it — when Subaru touched the wall again to make sure, he found that above his own waist the wall was solid, while below that point, an illusion had been cast over it.
An illusion made to look as though it blocked the entrance — he recalled a similar device once, in the hideout the Witch Cult, led by Petelgeuse, had built inside a rocky cavern.

"――Nothing ventured, nothing gained, as they say."

Crawling on all fours, low to the ground, one could slip through the illusory wall.
After a moment's hesitation, Subaru crawled through the wall and pressed on to the other side. Presumably, the bird had passed through here in low flight and gone on ahead.
If this led outside, or perhaps to some other part of the tower—

"Pwah!"

The darkness on the other side of the wall didn't last as long as he'd expected.
Passing through the illusory wall and emerging on the far side, Subaru found himself gasping for breath as if breaking the surface of water. Without meaning to, he'd been holding his breath the whole time, as if diving into darkness.
And then he noticed it — the outside air, a cold wind, brushing against his face.

"――Oh."

Opening his eyes, he let them slowly adjust from darkness to the world outside.
Spread out before him was a view of the night-shrouded dunes, glimpsed from a height beyond imagining. Above, looking down over it all, was a black sky glittering with stars.
And then—

"――――"

In what could only be called the Watchtower's balcony, there stood Anastasia, her purple hair streaming in the wind, surrounded by a countless flock of birds, waiting for him.
