Arc 4 Chapter 4: "The Next Place"

Bodily ejected from the forbidden archive by Beatrice, Subaru trudged back to the others with nothing to show for it and a heavy dose of self-reproach weighing on him.

Returning to the living room, Subaru was greeted by the same faces waiting in the same arrangement as when he'd left. The one difference was that Otto now occupied the spot where Subaru had been sitting, and there were signs he'd been having some kind of conversation with Emilia across from him.

Subaru made a sour face at the sight of another man getting cozy with Emilia, and without bothering to hide that sour face, he turned it on Otto.

"You'd better drop dead for having a flirty little chat with my Emilia-tan while I was gone."

"Waiting in silence or wasting time idly just isn't in my nature. And honestly, is that really the first thing you say the moment you walk back in? Getting your bad mood taken out on me is a bit much, frankly."

"D-don't act like you understand me and see right through me! The second you hand over the payment for the oil, that's the end of it between us, got it? Don't get the wrong idea!"

"Could you not phrase it like there was something between us that could even *give rise* to the wrong idea!?"

Subaru struck a tsundere pose, turning his face away, and by the time Otto finished shouting at him, Subaru had already lost all interest and turned back to Emilia.

She'd been waiting for their exchange to end without joining in, and now, meeting his gaze while still seated, she looked up at him.

"—Did you get to see Beatrice?"

An anxious question. In other words, she was asking, without quite asking, whether Subaru had accomplished what he'd left the room to do.

The answer to her literal question was yes; to what she actually meant, he could only say no.

He'd made contact, but hadn't managed to bring up the one thing that mattered. It was pathetic enough to make him want to laugh it off as some indecisive, spineless protagonist out of a dating sim.

Though, he thought, there wasn't really enough distance between him and that description to laugh it off.

"Nah, that part didn't work out."

"I see. Well, it can't be helped. Once Beatrice vanishes through one of her doors, there's no finding her no matter what. Even Ram and I never managed to see her, not even once…"

"Nn-uh, no, I did get to see her. It's just she was in a mood, kind of sulky, and wouldn't answer my questions. Pretty lame, I know."

"……You did see her?"

He cut off Emilia, who'd been assuming no contact had been made at all, and she opened her eyes in surprise at his answer. Picking up on something faintly odd in her reaction, Subaru nodded and said "yeah."

"My razor-sharp instincts led me right to the room she was holed up in. Well—I got to see her, but my conversational skills were too weak to actually land the point."

"I've thought this before, but you and Beatrice… you get along, don't you."

Emilia pressed a finger to her lips, murmuring the thought in a low, contemplative voice.

Catching that remark, Subaru made an exaggeratedly sour face—not an unconscious expression, but one he crafted with at least some deliberate intent.

"Please, don't lump me and Bea-ko together like we're friends. We're mortal enemies, from the moment we met. That girl sucked my mana dry the instant she laid eyes on me. There hasn't been enough time for that first impression to wear off."

"But you made up with Julius after everything that happened there. Sometimes you dig your heels in over the strangest, most pointless things, Subaru."

"I'm the kind of pathetic guy who's under the delusion that a man proves himself by digging in over pointless things to the bitter end. And also, for the record, I have not made up with Julius. Him, me, hate, forever."

"Uh-huh, sure."

Subaru protested this unfair assessment, but Emilia gave a small laugh and breezed right past it. He twisted his lips in a show of dissatisfaction, though inwardly he felt relief at how smoothly the subject had shifted.

The words he and Beatrice had exchanged still hadn't sorted themselves out in his head. Combined with the sorrowful look she'd shown him at the end, he had no idea how to make sense of any of it.

"Speaking of, where'd Frederica run off to? I don't see her anywhere. Leaving Otto and my Emilia-tan alone together—that's a pretty serious lapse in judgment, don't you think?"

"Whose I am is a conversation for another time… Frederica's gone to prepare one of the guest rooms right now. —A room for Rem to rest in."

"Ah. Right."

Subaru's reply came out a note lower, and Emilia's eyes narrowed with a pained look.

Regretting that he'd made her show such an expression, Subaru still couldn't hold back the ache that filled his chest whenever he thought of Rem. But with a single blink and shake of his head, he wiped the sentiment from his face, and to spare Emilia—who was worried about him—any further grief, he softened his lips into something like a smile.

"Guess I'd better go get Rem out of the dragon carriage soon, then. Can't just leave her there forever, that'd be cruel… Oh, right, sorry about earlier, Otto."

"No, it's fine, don't worry about it. To Natsuki-san she's… ah, well, she's clearly someone who means a lot to you. I can hardly tell you not to get worked up, given the state she's in."

"Just the thought of my Rem being touched by fingers stained with Otto's filthy money—I really couldn't hold it in. Genuinely sorry about that."

"That is *not* what someone who's genuinely sorry sounds like!? And another thing—I don't think someone who just called a completely different woman 'my' should be allowed to say that line!?"

"I was using you as a pawn in my adorable little romantic strategy to make my Emilia-tan jealous, obviously. Don't make me say it, idiot."

"You just said it yourself!"

Subaru grinned at Otto's habitual overreactions, then glanced sidelong at Emilia. Watching the exchange between Subaru and Otto, her lips had loosened slightly, the earlier gloom gone from her face—confirming that, Subaru let out a small breath of relief. And then—

"You know, you and Otto-kun seem awfully close. It hasn't even been that long since you met."

"Wha—you're jealous of *that* one!? Compared to what I've got going with Emilia-tan, Otto's just a fling, a fling. What I want is a serious, all-consuming romance with my Emilia-tan."

"Why am I the one getting cast off here? Forget whether it's true or not, that's the part I object to!"

The two men only heated up further, and Emilia burst into laughter.

Hand over her mouth, shoulders shaking with laughter she couldn't quite suppress, Emilia offered a small "sorry" before continuing.

"I don't think this is really the time to be laughing like this, but I can't help it. You two might end up being friends for a really, really long time, don't you think?"

"I'm a traveling peddler, you know? Once my business is done, it's a quick goodbye… Actually, I can't stand the thought of a man with no confirmed pairing besides me getting close to my Emilia-tan."

"I hate that after this short an acquaintance, I already know you're saying something that makes no sense and is also completely pointless—!"

Otto clutched his head, his face going pale, and Subaru snorted hard through his nose and twisted his mouth in response.

It was somewhat exaggerated as far as delivery went, but the sentiment behind it was unmistakably genuine. Having confessed his feelings to Emilia and now pursuing her with unstoppable momentum, Subaru found any other man's approach toward her absolutely intolerable, jealousy blazing.

Both possessiveness and jealousy—no doubt he had more than his fair share of both.

The rambling exchange in the living room finally wound down when Frederica returned, having finished preparing the guest room.

"I think I'll go take a look at the state of the village nearby. As for the buy-back of the cargo, I've got the deeds for the other merchants' goods with me too, so I have some discretion to distribute a portion to the village myself. Though the margrave will be footing the bill later, of course."

With that, undeterred by the fatigue of the long journey, Otto energetically set off toward the village.

Though he let a bit of his calculating merchant's face show through, his proposal was nothing short of support for Arlam Village, where sixty percent of the population were still refugees and the village had yet to recover its normal functioning.

Watching him hide that generosity behind a pose of feigned self-interest, Subaru couldn't help but feel a rush of gratitude—along with the urge to spit, just to cover it up.

"I've prepared one of the servants' rooms as you instructed. …Curiously enough, there was one room that showed signs of having been very thoroughly tidied already."

"Thoroughly… the room at the far end of the second floor?"

"—Yes, that's right. Just that one room, tidied up as though everything but the bed had been thrown out entirely… Do you know something about it, by chance?"

As Frederica brought up the now-prepared room, Subaru fought hard to keep the sorrow from showing in his eyes.

The room she described—at the far end of the east wing's second floor—had originally been Rem's room. Hearing that its contents had been cleared out, Subaru was struck anew by the sheer strength of Gluttony's power to erase one's existence.

"…Nah. Just felt like it, is all. Doesn't mean anything."

Sensing something in his tone, Frederica said nothing more in response.

She, too, seemed to possess an exceptionally fine sense as a maid. Rem and Frederica—between the two of them, the operations of the far-too-sprawling Roswaal Manor had likely been run. Excluding Ram, of course.

Circling around to the back of the manor toward where the dragon carriage was tethered, a small whinny called out to Subaru.

Looking over, he spotted an unfamiliar warehouse-like building—likely serving as a garage to house dragon carriages. Beside it, in an adjoining stable-like space, he found Patrasche tethered.

The jet-black-hided Earth Dragon noticed Subaru's approach and let out a spirited cry, leaning her head toward him as he drew near to show her affection.

Touching his fingers to the nose brought close to him, tickling the hard hide, he said,

"Sorry I didn't even properly thank you for your hard work earlier, Patrasche. Bit late given everything that's happened, but I'm counting on you going forward too, partner."

"————"

Patrasche answered Subaru's words—tongue stuck out—with the rough texture of her tongue against his palm. Watching the man and dragon get along so well, Frederica tilted her head.

"She's quite taken with you. It's admirable, taming an Earth Dragon this fine—even at a glance, one can tell she's excellent—so thoroughly."

"It's not like I did anything to tame her? If a normal Earth Dragon is hard to win over, then Patrasche's probably just got way more affection in her than most. Or maybe she's just putting up with me because I'm so unreliable, and she feels sorry for me."

Subaru offered this assessment of Patrasche's devotion with neither modesty nor exaggeration.

It had been barely three or four days since they'd met. In that time, how many times had this soft-hearted Earth Dragon saved his life? Having given nothing back in return, all he could say about Patrasche's loyal-dragon behavior was that meeting her had been sheer good fortune.

As if sensing Subaru's self-assessment, Patrasche stretched the head that had been licking his hand and rubbed her nose against his unsuspecting cheek. Startled by the sudden move, Subaru gave a wry laugh at the sensation of having his cheek sanded down.

"I think I understand a little of what kind of person you are now, Subaru-sama. —You have your struggles too, don't you."

"————"

Rather than speaking to Subaru, who was too busy handling Patrasche, Frederica addressed Patrasche herself with a soft gaze, watching her play with him.

At Frederica's deeply-felt remark, Patrasche paused her movements for just a moment, turning her reptilian eyes toward the woman once before resuming her teasing of Subaru.

Without Subaru even noticing, it was, in its own way, a moment where the hearts of the two women had somehow connected.

In any case—

"Sorry to keep you waiting, Rem. Cramped and dark in there, wasn't it? I'll carry you to your room now."

After spending a good while playing with Patrasche, Subaru finally made his way to the dragon carriage in the garage—that is, to where Rem still lay sleeping inside.

Unchanged in condition, deep in her unbroken slumber, Rem had no complaints to offer Subaru. She wouldn't sulk over being left behind, turn her face away and puff out her cheeks, pout that "Subaru-kun is a mean person"—nor would she smile at him when he apologized. None of it.

"—I'd heard about it, but seeing it still surprised me."

Behind Subaru, lost in that bout of sentiment, Frederica—seeing Rem for the first time—couldn't hide her astonishment. When Subaru tilted his head at her shock, she gave a small shake of her head and said, "No, it's nothing."

"It's just, her features are the very image of Ram's, as I know her. The only difference is the hair color… So the story about them being twins really was true."

"With her memories erased, I figured you'd be half-convinced either way, so I'm glad you believe it. I'd be even happier if you believed it wasn't some ill-natured prank, and that's why you're remembering it."

Nodding at the reason for Frederica's surprise, Subaru reached out and laid his hand against Rem's cheek.

Strangely, he felt neither warmth nor cold. There was no doubt her body was sustaining life, and yet nothing of what had once dwelt within that body remained.

Confirming again something he'd already confirmed so many times, Subaru inflicted the same unhealed wound on his heart once more. Knowing that, he still couldn't help confirming it anyway.

"Subaru-sama. I could carry her for you, you know…"

"I want to do it, so let me. I want to be the one who carries Rem back to the manor… back to her room. Sorry for being so selfish about it."

"No, that actually made my heart skip a little. You have such a murderous look about you, and yet you're so kind."

"Even my heart can be hurt by an offhand insult like that!"

Retorting to Frederica's words, Subaru shifted his hold on Rem. He'd already carried her like this several times while moving her—she was light.

She had stood before Subaru with this very body, fighting desperately to protect a useless Subaru. The more he thought of that, the more the tenderness welled up in him.

"I'll wake you up soon, I promise. So, for now, get mad at me for feeling how soft your body is against my fingers like this."

"That sounds like it should be a nice line, but somehow it just ruins the mood."

With Frederica's exasperated remark as the final word, they left the dragon carriage. With a single nod, Subaru bid farewell to Patrasche, who poked her head out from the stable, and then, following Frederica's lead, headed into the manor.

To the servants' quarters in the east wing—to what would become Rem's bedroom, the room that had originally been hers.

"You did get to speak with Beatrice-sama, didn't you."

The words came suddenly, as they walked.

Careful of the steps, Subaru was climbing the stairs and looked up at Frederica's back ahead of him. She narrowed the pupils of her sharp eyes, looking down at him with an expression that could only be read as intimidating. Though Subaru—who had, thanks to his own similarly narrow, sharp eyes, been misread by people his whole life—understood well enough that it wasn't meant that way.

Sensing that Subaru's silence was affirming her question, she went on.

"Is she doing well? I still haven't been able to see her face myself since I returned to the manor."

"Like I told Emilia-tan, well is well, I guess… probably. She was in a worse mood than usual, though, so we didn't really get to talk that much."

"I see… is that so."

Perhaps out of some concern, her expression didn't brighten at Subaru's answer.

Watching that expression, Subaru found himself struck by a sudden question—about the girl named Beatrice, and her position within this manor.

Up until now, Subaru had never pressed into her standing or background.

A girl who dwelt within Roswaal's manor, occupying the forbidden archive—that mysterious space conjured by magic—and who was treated by both Rem and Ram with something between the deference shown a guest and that shown someone of noble standing.

And yet she also had this childlike side, adoring Puck—the Spirit contracted to Emilia, a Royal Selection candidate—like an older brother. And then there was her behavior when facing Subaru directly, which seemed no more than what her apparent age would suggest, along with the meaningful, suggestive attitude she'd shown just now. Nothing but mysteries.

"Hey, um, has Frederica worked at this manor a long time?"

"Oh my, are you interested in me now? Emilia-sama, the girl in your arms… and Beatrice-sama besides—you really do have a lot on your plate, don't you."

"Don't casually toss Bea-ko in there, I've got no attraction to younger girls. You can see for yourself my hands are already full with Emilia-tan and Rem, right? Frederica's… honestly, in this short time, I think you're just not my type."

"Oh dear, so I've been disliked."

"It's that whole thing where you try to play me like a fiddle—that's exactly the kind of thing that makes you feel like a real Roswaal servant, and it's a dealbreaker. Ah, but that's purely a personality-compatibility thing, it's not that I hate you or anything."

Frederica blinked at Subaru's follow-up, then, hiding her mouth full of fearsome fangs, laughed.

"I wasn't worried about that at all. You really are quite the worrier yourself, aren't you, Subaru-sama."

"I hurt you the first time we met. You laughed it off, but at least half of that was genuine, wasn't it?"

"————"

At Subaru's words, Frederica blinked in genuine surprise this time, the smile fading from her face as she stared at him. The gleam of her yellow eyes slid into his own, and he let it, allowing her to try and see through to what lay beneath.

With a small exhale, Frederica said,

"I don't have much experience with people seeing through to my heart like that. I'd appreciate it if you didn't press any further."

"I just tidied up what I trampled on, that's all. I'm hardly one to talk about bad eyes myself… well, in my case, it runs in the whole family."

Both his father and mother had had sharp, unfriendly eyes, so it was only natural the son born of them turned out the same way.

At dinnertime, the family gathered together, silently doing their my-mayonnaise mayo-smooches—it probably looked, to any objective onlooker, like some kind of black magic ritual being performed at the table.

Watching that memory play out objectively and making a sour face at it, Subaru drew another sigh from Frederica.

"It's not that it's unpleasant—you're a strange one, though. I can understand a bit better now why Emilia-sama behaves the way she does around you."

"What about Emilia-tan?"

"It's nothing. I really will be scolded by Emilia-sama for real this time. Now then, why did you want to know how long I've worked here?"

Shaking her head, Frederica steered the conversation back on track.

Feeling something didn't quite add up, Subaru nonetheless shifted gears, prefacing with an "um—"

"Basically, I wanted to talk about Bea-ko… Beatrice. If you've spent a long time as a maid at the manor, I figured I'd ask how long ago Beatrice showed up."

He hadn't exactly asked a direct question, but Frederica's age was probably a few years above Subaru's—somewhere around twenty-three or twenty-four, he'd guess. Even if she'd been a maid for ten years, working backward from Beatrice's apparent age of around twelve, she seemed likely to know the answer to his question.

But in response to Subaru's question, Frederica shook her head.

"I'm sorry, but I don't know. Beatrice-sama had already shut herself away in the forbidden archive long before I came to work at this manor."

"Ah, well, guess that can't be helped. It's not like your years as a maid directly line up with how long she's been at Roz's place. If she's been raised within the manor from the start—"

"No, that's not it, Subaru-sama."

Frederica cut him off clearly, denying the possibility Subaru had thought was settling into place.

Standing straight before Subaru, who furrowed his brow, she carried a plainly visible sorrow on her fearsome features.

"I have worked as a maid at only the one manor—my master's. And I was first brought here as a servant when I was twelve years old. That's already more than ten years ago."

"…Wait, hold on, that's weird, isn't it? Because working backward from then, that would mean Bea-ko's been holed up in that musty room since she was still toddling around."

"Haven't you already worked it out?"

As if reproaching Subaru for his reluctance to accept the obvious, Frederica shook her head.

Her attitude seemed to affirm the question that had been forming in Subaru's chest, and he now clearly grasped an answer he'd avoided thinking too deeply about until now.

In other words, the girl in that forbidden archive was—

"Her appearance hasn't changed. …She's not human after all, is she."

"A librarian bound by the covenant to guard the forbidden archive since the very founding of House Mathers—that is who she is. The Great Spirit, Beatrice-sama."

There was no hint of falsehood in Frederica's declaration, and Subaru had no choice but to accept it.

The true nature of the girl he'd been dealing with all this time existed on an entirely different plane from his own.

"Great Spirit… same title as Puck, but she's pretty different in looks and everything else."

"The absence of a contractor, and matters concerning the covenant… no, this is a subject that goes beyond what I should be saying. Please forget I said anything."

"Yeah, no way that's happening."

Not for the first time, ignorant Subaru found himself jerked around by the suggestive hints of someone in the know.

Frederica, unmoved by his sullen glare, seemed to have no intention of touching further on what she'd already sealed her lips about. Faced with her manner, Subaru sighed, judging it unlikely he could push the conversation any further.

Then, remembering that the two of them had been standing still talking this whole time, he said,

"Frederica."

"Please forgive me, Subaru-sama. I let my tongue slip more than I should have. I suppose I was unusually glad to see someone show concern for Beatrice-sama. Please, forgive me."

"That's fine and all, but my arms are about to give out."

Trembling arms shaking, Subaru looked up at Frederica with a strained expression.

He'd bragged about how light Rem's body was, how love would carry him through and all that, but matters of arm strength and muscular endurance had a way of ignoring such sentiment and coming for him regardless.

"Oh my, my."

"Why—please, quickly, get out of the way, I'm begging you!"

Vowing not to commit the folly of setting Rem down on the floor or handing her off to Frederica halfway there, he brushed past Frederica's offer to switch places and hurried toward the guest room.

He heard the light patter of footsteps behind him—Frederica following along. Reflecting a little guiltily on how abruptly he'd cut off their conversation, he arrived at the door to Rem's room, where—

"—That took a while, didn't it."

Emilia was waiting there, looking rather at loose ends.

He laid Rem in the bed, pulling the covers back up to her chest.

Her chest indeed rose and fell, breathing steadily; the heartbeat within told of life being sustained. Until the moment she someday woke, how much time would she spend here?

"That's all up to how hard I work at it, I guess."

Renewing his resolve, Subaru brushed the bangs from Rem's forehead with his finger, then turned back to Emilia, who stood waiting behind him.

"Sorry to keep you waiting. Got to talking with Frederica a bit too much. My biceps are paying the lactic-acid price for it now, though."

"If you two got to talking, that's a good thing, isn't it? What were you and Frederica talking about?"

"Doing a little fieldwork before rehabilitating a certain shut-in young lady. There's a close relationship between how someone became a shut-in, how long it's lasted, and how you should approach them afterward, you know."

"Huh, is that so. You really know a lot about that 'shut-in' thing, Subaru. Impressive."

"Emilia-tan's innocent comments really do stab me right in the chest sometimes, you know that? Like just now."

It only made it worse that she seemed to be purely and genuinely praising the depth of his insight.

Emilia tilted her head at Subaru's grimace with an expression free of any malice, but when he responded with a wry laugh, she gave a small shrug.

"So, you couldn't get anything out of Beatrice, right?"

"Tight-lipped, no give at all. By the way, since I keep asking—what about Puck?"

"—No good, no response from him either. Sometimes he goes quiet like this, but this time the timing is really, really bad. I don't know what to do."

Emilia reached into her chest and pulled out a green crystal stone. The pendant-style gem glowed with a faint light, an eerie shimmer wavering within it, marking the presence of the Great Spirit housed inside.

That was the anchor for Puck, Emilia's contracted Spirit, and Subaru knew that he ordinarily materialized from and returned into it day to day.

But as their exchange just now made clear, these past several days, his form hadn't been present within the crystal. Absent—strange a word as that was to use—unresponsive even to her calls.

"Does this happen sometimes? But even so, doesn't it put Emilia-tan in a bind like this?"

"He always comes back when I really need to borrow his power. So I don't think he's not watching over things. But he's never once told me what he's doing when he's gone, no matter how many times I ask."

At Emilia's apologetic words, Subaru scratched his head and responded with an "nnh."

But he couldn't hide his inner disappointment at the fact that even Puck—who seemed to know quite a lot—had clammed up here too. This meant that all the key witnesses to the various questions that had arisen had now, together, fallen silent.

"Both Puck and Bea-ko, all sealed up tight together… man, this is rough."

"Really. …Hey, Subaru, what should we do?"

Emilia asked, seeking his judgment, as he stood there with a hand to his forehead, lost in thought.

Glancing down at her call, Subaru saw the clinging trust in her eyes—genuine trust placed in him—and couldn't help feeling glad about it even in a moment like this. He was beyond saving.

That she relied on him—that fact registering within him, even amid this suffocating sense of being stuck.

"With both of them clamming up when they seem to know a lot, I think we've got no choice but to move on to the next one. …Well, even knowing where he is, whether he'll actually talk is a different story."

"Roswaal, right."

"I think it's about time we sit him down and get him to lay it all out, backroom dealings and everything."

At Emilia's perceptive words, Subaru nodded in agreement.

That she'd immediately landed on the same conclusion suggested it matched the direction Emilia herself had been thinking. Emilia visibly relaxed at Subaru's agreement, placing a hand on her chest with relief.

"I'm glad, that you're in agreement too, Subaru. I was worried what I'd do if you objected, like Roswaal and Ram did."

"Depending on what it is, I might object too, but basically I'm Team Emilia-tan, all the way, you know? Even if I did object to something, I'd want you to believe it comes from love for you."

"L-love, honestly… you really do say the smoothest things sometimes, Subaru."

At Subaru's shameless flirtation, Emilia turned aside, caught off guard. Seeing the faint blush rise to her cheeks, Subaru clenched his fist, while Emilia, still looking away, said,

"So, I have a proposal for you, Subaru, since you're on my side."

"Heyhey, let's hear it, whatever it may be."

Placing a hand on his chest and bowing with mock reverence, Subaru prompted her. Emilia closed one eye and turned back to him, muttering "smooth talker" with her lips pursed.

Then, after a single breath, she looked straight into Subaru's dark eyes and said,

"There's things we need to talk to Roswaal about, and things about the villagers who fled, right? So, I've decided I want to go to the 'Sanctuary.'"

"The 'Sanctuary'…"

It was a place whose name had come up repeatedly here at Roswaal Manor.

As for its location, Subaru—having 'never made it there'—unfortunately didn't know, but Ram's Team B of evacuees had made it their destination in fleeing the Witch Cult.

At the very least, now that Petelgeuse's Witch Cult had been wiped out, the threat to the 'Sanctuary' should have been lifted, same as it had for the manor.

"I've been told it's a place I'll eventually have to show my face at, and I figure now's as good a time as any. I've decided I'll really talk things over with Roswaal properly this time, too."

"Wait wait wait, hold up! You're not planning on leaving me behind, are you?"

"Eh?"

Subaru held up a palm toward Emilia as she spoke of her resolve, throwing cold water on her declaration. But even so, he had to say it.

"I get that you're fired up, and I'm on board with the plan, but leaving me behind is a no-go. I know I'm weak and not too bright, but I still hate the idea of not being able to work hard by your side. I know I'm being selfish, but still!"

Emilia's eyes widened at Subaru's desperate plea.

But it was nothing less than the plain truth. Subaru would follow Emilia. If he wasn't by her side, he couldn't protect her. He couldn't act for her sake.

Not out of conceit or anything of the sort—surely his presence was necessary to help save her. Not as a stance seeking something in return from her, but as the meaning behind what Subaru himself wanted to do.

Emilia remained wide-eyed at Subaru's intensity. And so, judging that now was the moment to press forward and settle her intentions before they could waver, Subaru continued,

"Stopping me is pointless. I'm following you, Emilia-tan. Being left behind—no thanks. Whether it's the 'Sanctuary' or Roswaal himself, no obstacle stands before my blazing love—"

"I'm not going to leave you behind. Come with me."

"Being left behind, no, no, no— wait, what did you just say?"

Subaru, who'd been on the verge of actually flopping onto the floor and kicking his limbs, froze halfway into a crouch and looked up at Emilia.

At that, Emilia raised a hand to her mouth, her face slightly flushed.

"So, come with me. Because if it's just me alone, I'll be too anxious to stand it."

"E-Emilia-tan…"

"I rely on you, Subaru. I don't think you're weak, or that you're not smart. I need your strength."

"————"

The shock Subaru felt at those words was beyond description.

Subaru fell silent, mouth hanging open without meaning to, and anxiety flickered across Emilia's expression at the sight. She let her raised hand hover uncertainly, as if unsure whether to reach out and touch him.

"Ah, um, so, um, what's wrong? Did I… say something strange again…"

"Turns out my motivation switch is in Emilia-tan's hands. Turning it on, turning it off, it's all automatic, one word from you. Unbelievable."

Covering his face with his palm, Subaru toyed with Emilia using words like that.

In truth, unable to read the intent behind Subaru's remark, a bewildered Emilia responded with a confused "Eh? Eh? What do you mean?"—and Subaru wanted very much to say *serves you right*.

Because he himself was thoroughly, thoroughly experiencing being tossed about even more than she was.

"—It sounds as though the two of you have come to an understanding."

"Wahyah!?"

The exchange between the two of them—which, from the outside, could only look like flirting—was cut abruptly short by Frederica's entrance, knocking on the open door.

Emilia showed no surprise at Frederica's appearance, but Subaru, hiding his racing heartbeat, couldn't help but glare at her. Frederica, for her part, surely understood exactly what Subaru was feeling, yet showed not a trace of consideration on her cool, fearsome features.

"I have no objection to the two of you going to the 'Sanctuary.' However, I'll need about two days to prepare."

"Prepare? Frederica, are you coming with us too?"

"No, I have the manor to manage, so I can't accompany you. Instead, I'll teach the route to the 'Sanctuary' to the Earth Dragon you brought with you, Subaru-sama."

"To Patrasche?"

Subaru's eyes widened at the unexpected suggestion.

At his reaction, Frederica gave a matter-of-fact "Yes."

"Earth Dragons are intelligent creatures, so if properly taught, they're fully capable of grasping a route without needing a guide. That one especially seemed clever. There shouldn't be any issue."

"Patrasche just keeps getting to be more and more of a catch. Seriously, how'd I get so lucky with her?"

"More importantly, there are a few things I need to tell the two of you."

Subaru cocked his head at his exceptional partner, but Frederica didn't respond to that, straightening her sturdy frame and looking the two of them square in the eye.

"Since you're going to the 'Sanctuary,' there are a few things I want you to keep in mind. Emilia-sama especially, given the matter of your origins, please take care."

"—Yes, I'm prepared for it. I've heard it's a place with quite the history, one way or another."

At Frederica's almost threatening warning, Emilia set her jaw with a look of firm resolve in her eyes.

Subaru, too, stepped up beside her as if to honor that resolve.

"To be honest, I don't know much more about the 'Sanctuary' than the name… but helping Emilia-tan is my number one priority. Tell us anything."

"That's an almost refreshingly pure sort of impurity, isn't it."

Exasperation and admiration flickered through Frederica's eyes at once, and then, with a single blink, she crushed both sentiments down and raised a finger.

"Then, let me tell you about the 'Sanctuary.' And there's one thing I want you to remember."

"Remember?"
"What thing?"

Subaru and Emilia tilted their heads in unison, and Frederica nodded, "Yes," her voice dropping slightly in tone as she said,

"—Please be careful of a person named Garfiel. In the 'Sanctuary,' he is the one person the two of you must handle with the utmost caution."
