Arc 3 Chapter 29: "Things Set in Motion"

When Subaru, led by Rem, burst into the drawing room, the people waiting there were Crusch, Ferris, and Wilhelm — who had made it back into the mansion a step ahead of Subaru.

Crusch watched Subaru come charging into the room, breath ragged, shoulders heaving, and narrowed her eyes within that dignified face of hers.

"Judging by your state, it seems you've already heard."

As if glad to be spared the trouble of an unnecessary preamble, Crusch said this with a keen brilliance in her sharp features that betrayed no emotion.
Unable to deny the feeling of having been beaten to the punch, Subaru gave a small shake of his head.

"Not the details, no. Even Rem only seems to have a vague sense of it."

Turning only his head, Subaru shot a glance at Rem, standing just beside him. Catching his meaning, Rem set her jaw, her expressionless face showing just a hint of tension.

"What Rem felt was, at most, something that came through Sister's senses. Unlike with Sister — Rem didn't see it concretely. If Rem could use clairvoyance, it would be a different story."

Frustrated, Rem knit her brow and bit her lip, chagrined at her own lack of power.
But watching her, Crusch let out an "ah" of understanding, as if something had clicked into place.

"I had heard tell of the twin maids in Margrave Roswaal's keeping. I see — so even separated by the distance between the royal capital and his domain, a certain degree of information exchange is possible."

"As I said, it is vague. For one of my meager talents, that is the best I can manage."

Rem bowed at the waist, deprecating herself in the face of Crusch's praise. Watching their exchange out of the corner of his eye, Subaru stepped forward and waved a hand. "Hold on, hold on."

"That side of things doesn't matter right now. I want to hear the important part. From how you two were talking earlier, you know a bit more of the details, don't you?"

"Even on our end, the information's not exactly rock-solid, y'know? It's the kinda thing that's threaded through the gaps of a whole buncha different people before reaching us."

Ferris turned his usual mischievous gaze on Subaru, who couldn't hide his impatience. Subaru shot back a glare in response, and Ferris pressed a finger to his lips, tilting his head to one side with an adorable little motion, and said, "Buuut—"

"That vague sorta feeling, when you put it together with Remmy's sixth sense from earlier, starts to come together into somethin' kinda niiice?"

"Quit stalling. Get to the point."

Ferris kept up his teasing attitude no matter what, and Subaru planted his hands on the reception table, thrusting his face forward as if to intimidate him.
But it wasn't Ferris who responded to Subaru's demand by clapping to draw attention — instead it was:

"I understand your impatience, but—Natsuki Subaru."

Calling Subaru by his full name, it was Crusch who looked his way, her green hair swaying.
Bathed in the sharpness of that gaze, Subaru had, for a moment, the illusion of having been doused in cold water, and he unconsciously caught his breath and drew back.

The difference in height between the tall Crusch and Subaru was, at most, five centimeters — Subaru was still the taller of the two, without question. And yet Subaru felt a pressure as if he were being looked down upon by her, as though she stood above him despite them standing on the same level.

It was likely the difference in the vessels each of them possessed that produced this difference in how they perceived one another. Subaru, forced in an unnecessary moment to recognize the difference in rank between them as living beings, found Crusch regarding him with an attitude of feigned ignorance toward his inner turmoil.

"What if I told you flatly that we have no reason to disclose information to you in the first place?"

"Wha—?"

"Surely that's a response you should have anticipated. You are, at present, a guest under my house's care. That standing guarantees nothing beyond itself. There is no reason a mere guest should be involved in the comings and goings of the Royal Selection, my own house included."

Struck down head-on by an argument too correct to refute, Subaru twisted his lips, unable to muster a comeback.
What he felt at the same time was anger at his own rash, shallow complacency.
Hadn't he only just recently come to realize this himself? That this was, after all, enemy territory — a place that would bare its fangs against him without hesitation should anything happen.
Naturally, it stood to reason that Crusch and Ferris would take a stance toward him in keeping with that.
There was no way that showing his face here and asking to be handed information, with such spoiled logic, would fly.

"Lady Crusch, Sir Ferris — that will be quite enough jesting."

Subaru, his inner thoughts a blaze of hostility even as he cast about for how to pry the information loose, had his train of thought brought up short. It was Wilhelm, who had until then kept silent throughout, who did it.
He turned a reproving eye on his lord and her attendant.

"In peacetime, there might be room for such leisure, but at present both sides understand this to be urgent. Needless bickering now will only leave regrets later."

"If there is a later, that iiis."

"Sir Ferris."

"Fiiine, I'll be quiet. Mmh, you're so scaaary."

Puckering his lips, Ferris flopped down onto the sofa as if bouncing, then promptly covered his ears and eyes and fell silent. Wilhelm let out a sigh at this childish display, while Crusch suppressed a laugh.
Unable to keep pace with their manner, Subaru blinked, but—

"I owe you an apology for the discourtesy, Natsuki Subaru."

"Huh?"

"What just happened was, in part, a test of you. It carried no great significance. But I do want you to bear in mind that this is a place where you have no choice but to be conscious of such things."

Crusch offered this apology to Subaru, who responded with a foolish sound, but she did not neglect to add her warning. At her words, Subaru anew became conscious of his own standing, gaining a kind of jolting clarity of awareness.
In the end, whether he wished it or not, Subaru now stood in a position where he could not simply be content to rest within the environment he'd been given.

To say he "steeled himself" would be putting it a bit strongly, given how unsettled he still felt inside, but even so, seeing Subaru shift his mindset, Crusch gave a satisfied nod, as though this was just as she'd intended.

"There has been some troubling activity observed near Margrave Roswaal's domain. Word is that the domain has entered a state of alert by the margrave's order..."

"Troubling activity? State of alert?"

At the ominous words that had come flying out, Subaru raised his eyebrows and voiced his confusion.
In response to that question, Crusch nodded and crossed her arms.

"To begin with, this state of affairs was one that had been anticipated. From the moment the margrave put forward Emilia as a royal candidate — that is, the moment he declared his support for a half-elf."

"What, is it—are the domain's residents exploding with complaints or something?"

"Naturally, that too, no doubt. Given how widespread the notoriety of the Witch of Envy has become, being a half-elf makes it unavoidable to have to contend with such prejudices."

The concern that had sprung to Subaru's mind he voiced without thinking, only to have it affirmed all too readily.
Here too, Subaru could not forgive the fact that Emilia's origins should become a shackle upon her. It wasn't as though she herself had done anything wrong, and yet how much did the world drag at her feet?
And the faceless "domain residents," who knew nothing of her personality and spoke only from prejudice, made him hate them so much he could hardly stand it.

"It is a road she walked with her eyes open, resolved to bear it herself. Your indignation is misplaced."

"The hell it is. It's not about her being resolved to hear people badmouth her, or being used to it, none of that. I'm saying there's no reason she oughta have to be badmouthed in the first place."

Subaru shot back at Crusch's words with a hard expression, and then said "Anyway," pulling the conversation back on track.

"So that trivial nonsense is the reason there's trouble brewing on Roswaal's domain? If we're not careful, it won't stop at a little smoke — it'll turn into a full-blown fire?"

"An amusing way to put it, but you've hit the mark. Setting aside the particulars, that's the broad shape of it, correctly stated. Wouldn't that also account for what your attendant sensed?"

At the question thrown her way, every gaze in the room converged on Rem.
Keeping her poker face, betraying no expression, Rem met the nearest gaze — Subaru's — and, as if responding to the intent behind it, nodded.

"What came through from Sister was a measure of impatience, and a great deal of anger... I believe it wasn't something she meant to convey — it slipped through despite herself, because she couldn't hold it in."

"This empathic sense thing — do you two feel each other that often?"

"No. To some degree, we consciously suppress it. Even so, in the case of an especially strong emotion, it can overcome that and get through to the other regardless."

As she went on, the strength drained little by little from Rem's words.
To organize what she'd said: the wave of emotion that had reached Rem from Ram this time was irregular. If Ram had used their shared sense as a means of calling for help, it would be natural for it to arrive in a clearer form, and intermittently at that.
And yet this time, the shared sensation that had slipped through had come only once, with nothing following after. Which meant—

"She's trying not to get her involved... is that it?"

Muttering the words only within his own mouth, Subaru felt an emotion that seemed as though it would burn him alive at his own conjecture.
If Ram's intent was as he imagined, it was not hard to guess who had ordered Ram to do so, or by whose designs it might be.

Subaru was, at present, someone receiving treatment at House Karsten. Rem was there attending him. For it to reach Rem was tantamount to it reaching Subaru.
—Was that how badly she didn't want to involve Subaru in her own path?

"But you're in trouble, aren't you...?"

The matter had reached even as far as Crusch, situated in the royal capital.
And on top of that, this was a situation where Ram — who ought to have had several times the self-control Rem did — had been unable to hold back her emotions and had let them leak through.
As ever, there were few they could rely on, and their enemies were, for her, unreasonably numerous. In a situation like that, how many were there who could be an ally without any hidden agenda?

Yes, that was it.
So then—

"I've got to go help her."

Subaru raised his head and murmured this quietly, and once again every gaze gathered on him.
Crusch closed one eye, Ferris narrowed his mischievous eyes just slightly, Wilhelm showed no ripple of emotion across his deeply lined face as ever, and Rem—

"Y-You mustn't, Subaru-kun...!"

Tugging at his sleeve, Rem's lips trembled faintly as she opened her large eyes wide.
What floated in those eyes was impatience and confusion, and a color of pleading so desperate it bordered on life-and-death.

"We must abide by Lady Emilia's, by Lord Roswaal's instructions. Subaru-kun was told to devote himself to treatment at Lady Crusch's residence. Rem — Rem agrees as well. Healing your injured body is what should take priority for Subaru-kun right now—"

"And what happens if something irreversible happens while we're sitting around doing that? Then it won't matter what eyes or nose or ears we've got left to worry about. It's a question of which one to choose."

Pushing past Rem's clinging manner, Subaru stepped forward and looked at Crusch's face. Meeting her amber eyes that quietly gazed back, he straightened his posture.

"You heard, didn't you, Crusch-san. Rem and I are going back to Roswaal's mansion... to Lady Emilia's side. Treatment's on hold until this is settled—"

"—If you leave this place, then you become my enemy."

Cutting off Subaru as he tried to talk about what would come later, Crusch declared this with utter coldness.
At the sheer sharpness with which those words cut, Subaru had the illusion that he had actually been slashed. And as meaning began to spread the way pain spreads from a cut—

"Wh-what do you...?"

"Let me correct one thing in your thinking. The only reason I have treated you as a guest and had Ferris administer your treatment is, purely and simply, because of a contract."

"A contract...?"

"That's right," Crusch nodded at Subaru's questioning echo, and she raised a single finger at the end of her crossed arms.

"The contract that was concluded is between Emilia and myself — mediated through Ferris — but its content is your treatment, and what is given in return. With mutual consent, it was made to stand, and I have received you as a guest on those terms. But there is a reason I am honoring this contract."

"—"

"I honor my contract with Emilia because it was concluded before the Royal Selection began. As of the Royal Selection, Emilia and I stand as political enemies to one another. Therefore, there is, in principle, no mercy to be shown to a member of Emilia's camp out of sympathy. But a contract is a contract."

Repeating it again and again, Crusch used the word "contract" to drive home her point.
Listening, Subaru felt the word ring in his ears like the word "promise," and felt the discomfort of that word leading straight back to the last conversation he'd had with Emilia.

To Subaru, who had failed to keep his "promise," Crusch, who gave priority to the fulfillment of a "contract."
Toward Subaru, who tried not to let the awkwardness of his own guilty conscience show on his face, she sharpened her gaze all the more.

"Should you leave this house, then that contract, too, shall be considered abandoned midway. With no lingering grudge on either side, after that Emilia and I will be complete enemies to one another. And you, acting as a member of Emilia's camp, will naturally become an enemy to me as well."

"So if we leave, treatment's cut off — is that it... That's a pretty low-down thing to do, isn't it. Basically you don't want us to go, because it'd be a problem for you if we helped Emilia out of her pinch, right?"

"I'd rather you didn't misunderstaaand."

Just as Subaru, with a provocative bit of glibness, was trying to paper over his own inner turmoil, that was when it happened.
Ferris, who had until then watched the course of events unfold in silence, cut in, his gaze carrying a slight edge, his chestnut-colored cat ears standing sharply erect.

"What Lady Crusch is doing for you and your friends right now isn't spite, it's kindness, y'know. It's not like it'd hurt us one bit if you and your friends went back to help out Lady Emilia. And yet she's holding you back anyway—"

"Ferris, stop."

"Nooo, I'm gonna say it. You're being juuust a little too wrong about this, so somebody's gotta set you straight on that."

Shaking his head at his lord's attempt to hold him back with a short word, Ferris looked at Subaru anew.
Then, slowly, as if chewing over each word, he said:

"It's not like the situation changes just 'cause Subaru-kyun goes. Going would be pointless. The promise of treatment Lady Emilia paid a price to arrange would go to waste too. If you're gonna do something like that, you'd be better off for your own sake just sitting tight, watching how things play out, and letting your body heal."

—There was a sound.

Subaru heard something snap inside his head with a small pop. And the moment he realized that it was the mouth of the sack that had been holding back his temper, Subaru came close enough to biting through his own lip at an unimaginable humiliation.

"I've decided."

Without letting any of those emotions show on his face, Subaru quietly spun out those words.
The raging passion still blazed within his chest, but that heat, rather than choosing to erupt outward, scorched Subaru's inner self and, in doing so, arrived at a single conclusion.

"I'm going back to the mansion... to Emilia's side. It was a short while, but thanks for everything."

"Subaru-kun!"

At Subaru's clear declaration of parting, Rem cried out in a voice that seemed to cling to him. But Subaru turned his palm toward her to silence her, and looked down at the Crusch camp arrayed before him.
Hearing Subaru's words, Crusch closed her eyes, her inner thoughts unknowable. But beside her, Ferris, letting out a long, deep sigh, wore an unmistakably sour expression.
He shook his head and looked at Subaru without troubling to hide his exasperation.

"Taking good advice to heart is also a measure of a person's vessel, y'know?"

"Your advice is exactly what let me make up my mind. Thanks a bunch."

At Subaru's clearly sarcastic reply, Ferris shrugged, seeming to give up on forming any further words. Taking over the conversation in his place instead was:

"Natsuki Subaru."

Calling his name, it was Crusch, looking up at Subaru with wide-open eyes.
Pinned by that dignified gaze, Subaru steeled his resolve so as not to yield to the force of it. Whatever might be said to him, he had no intention of retracting words he'd once spoken. Even if he were reviled as powerless and foolish, what of it?
But—

"Unfortunately, all of this house's long-distance dragon carriages are currently out on assignment. All I can lend you is a slow-footed one meant for hauling cargo, or one built on the premise of swapping out earth dragons over a mid-range distance."

While Subaru seethed inwardly, unaware, what Crusch said was an entirely different topic — no, in a way, words that affirmed Subaru's decision.

For a moment, Subaru blinked, uncomprehending of what had been said. Crusch looked at him with a hint of puzzlement, then turned back to Ferris beside her.

"Ferris, did I say something strange?"

"Ferry-chan's always dizzy from how fast Lady Crusch switches gears, buuut... this time, well, isn't it 'cause Subaru-kyun didn't think you'd even go so far as to offer to lend a dragon carriage?"

At Ferris's answer, given while pressing a hand to his cheek and squirming, Crusch gave a small "ah" of understanding and inclined her chin. Then she looked at Subaru once more.

"I respect your decision. Whatever the judgment, seeing through what one has decided for oneself carries grave responsibility. And if one is to bear that responsibility regardless, then one ought to do what one wishes to do. So as not to be ashamed before one's own soul. —Is that not so?"

"...Yeah, that's right. Exactly right. My soul's kicking up a fuss about not wanting to be a coward. Saying I shouldn't just be lounging around convalescing while that girl's in a pinch."

Responding to Crusch, who affirmed Subaru's intent, Subaru accepted the change taking place within himself. Having gained unexpected reinforcements, Subaru's judgment would no longer waver.
That much must have gotten through to Rem as well. As if reproaching herself just once, she closed her eyes and chose silence, and by the time she opened them again, her usual expressionless face had returned.

"On my lord's behalf, thank you for your kindness up to this day."

"It's no trouble. There was benefit to us in it as well. Now, about transportation to the domain—"

"Brazen as it is to ask, we would be grateful for your assistance. Right now we wish, as swiftly as possible, to return to the domain and be of use to our lord."

Bowing her head, Rem sought to receive the favor Crusch extended.
Realizing from the preceding conversation that this must be about a dragon carriage to use for the journey, Subaru raised a hand and unthinkingly interjected.

"Actually, quick question — how long is it from the royal capital to Roswaal's mansion?"

If Subaru's memory served, the trip from the mansion to the royal capital had taken roughly seven or eight hours. Having set out in the early morning, they'd arrived sometime a bit later than past noon, so that ought to be about right.
Given that, the travel time, even generously reckoned, should have been at most half a day, but—

"Accounting for switching between dragon carriages along the way... it should take two days, or perhaps three."

"Three days?! But it didn't even take half a day coming here!"

There had been mention of the lack of long-distance dragon carriages, but even accounting for that, the difference in time was too great. Even running continuously, day and night, it was hard to imagine such a gap arising.
But Subaru's natural question was denied by a shake of Rem's head. Within her finely shaped features, she drew her brows together just slightly, forming a troubled expression.

"The Lifaus Highway that we used to get here can't be used now. Unfortunately, it's entered the season when the 'Mist' occurs... so we'll have to detour around the highway."

"What's the mist got to do with anything. If we just push straight through—"

"The one that gives rise to the mist is the White Whale, y'know? If you were unlucky enough to run into it, you'd lose your life. That's common sense you shouldn't even need to be told."

Cutting in, Ferris dismissed the idea in a tone as if it were only natural.
Subaru frowned at yet another unfamiliar term, "White Whale," coming up, but leaving him behind in his lack of understanding, their discussion proceeded smoothly.

In the end, the negotiations between Crusch and Rem settled on: "borrow a dragon carriage from House Karsten, and travel back by swapping carriages at the villages along the way."

If only they could keep running without rest, Subaru thought with frustration, but since it was a living creature pulling the carriage, there was no escaping the reality that it would grow fatigued.
It wasn't like a car that could keep running as long as you put fuel in it. In points like this, he was made to keenly feel the difference in convenience between the world he'd originally come from and this one.

In any case, matters had begun to move.
There was no longer any room to waste time fumbling about.

—Once a course of action had been decided upon like that, things moved quickly.

To begin with, the luggage Subaru and Rem had brought to House Karsten was minimal. When the time came to leave the mansion they'd entered practically with nothing but the clothes on their backs, the two of them had only enough hand luggage that Subaru could carry it all with both arms.

By the time they'd quickly gathered their things and headed outside the mansion, a dragon carriage had already arrived beside the gate — the only point along the row of iron fencing separating outside from in.
Wilhelm, seated in the driver's box, noticed the two of them approaching and, reins still in hand, lightly leapt down from the box, landing on the same level ground. Straightening his appearance with a comb of his fingers, he said:

"This is the fastest earth dragon among the dragon carriages remaining at this house. Even so, it falls well short of the caliber of earth dragon the margrave uses — I ask your forgiveness for that."

"Just having it lent to us is more than we could ask for. We'll be sure to return it... though I guess after all this, that might not be so easy, huh?"

Lowering his voice, Subaru took the offered reins and looked at Wilhelm.
Already, the only one seeing the two of them off at the gate was the old man alone — Crusch and Ferris had bid their farewells back at the point they'd left the mansion's entrance hall, and there had been a clear color of parting in it.
At the very least, it had unmistakably been an attitude that would make it awkward to simply, smoothly, go return the borrowed earth dragon once matters were settled.

At Subaru's words, that unease showing on the surface, Wilhelm lowered his deeply lined face, prefacing with "Indeed," and continued:

"Given my position, I have no choice but to follow Lady Crusch's judgment. Once you have left this mansion, the relationship between my lord and Subaru-dono's lord will become as was stated earlier. —Consider the dragon carriage, along with the treatment and swordsmanship instruction left unfinished, as at least a small parting gift."

"That's... I don't remember hearing a word of that when we were leaving the mansion, though."

The words that lord and vassant had thrown at the two of them as they left the mansion had been a farewell fitting of them, if anything, almost too fitting.

"Fight well. Take care, above all, to make a choice you need not be ashamed of before your pride and your soul."

"You're driving away someone as kind as Lady Crusch has been to you, so you'd better hurry up and make up with Lady Emilia, or I'll really be annoyed. Go on, get going already."

The most memorable part was probably each of their final lines.
Though there hadn't seemed to be any of the consideration in them that Wilhelm had just described.

"I too am one who serves Lady Crusch. I have some notion of my lord's thinking."

"By the way, how long have you been working here?"

"Roughly half a year now, I should think..."

"That's a lot shorter than I expected?! Are you two even communicating properly?!"

If he took Wilhelm's word at face value, borrowed the dragon carriage, and that ended up deepening the rift between Crusch and Emilia, it wouldn't even be funny. The dawn of the "Borrow-and-Bolt War." He'd hate for that to end up carved into a history textbook somewhere.

While Subaru and Wilhelm had been talking, Rem, having dashed up to the dragon carriage, was sparing no effort in checking the vehicle and the earth dragon itself.
Snatching the reins from Subaru's hands, she leapt up onto the driver's box, then immediately climbed back down and busied herself checking the condition of the earth dragon pulling the carriage.

From a quick glance, the earth dragon on the borrowed carriage seemed nearly a size smaller than the one that had set out from Roswaal Manor. The carriage itself was correspondingly small too, so there shouldn't be a problem with horsepower, but the difference in speed was still a concern.

"—Understood? Then behave. That's right, good boy, good boy."

"Rem, how's it looking?"

The earth dragon lowered its head in response to Rem's instructions. She reached her hand toward the lizard's snout, gently stroking its hard, reptilian hide, and turned back to Subaru.

"It's a bit of a hot-tempered one, but Rem has made clear who's in charge, so there shouldn't be a problem. It should follow Rem's instructions."

"O-Oh yeah... you've got the hierarchy straight then. Kind of a jock, huh."

While Subaru had been talking with Wilhelm, it seemed Rem had also been having a "chat" with the earth dragon. He lacked the courage to press for details so the particulars remained unknown, but something like sympathy stirred in him for the earth dragon that was about to be worked to the bone.
Though of course, that sympathy left no room for mercy in the pace of their march.

"If we avoid the mist and detour around the plains, we should pass through two villages before reaching the margrave's domain. Of those, at the one closer to the domain — a village called Hanumas — we should be able to arrange a carriage to switch to."

"By the way, how far is it to this village, Hanumas?"

"Roughly fourteen or fifteen hours, I should think. If you're prepared to push the carriage you switch to until it drops, I believe you could reach the domain from there in about half a day more."

Either way, it was, without question, a maddening journey that would take a day and a half or more.
Scratching his head, gnashing his teeth at the vexation of it, Subaru bowed his head to Wilhelm.

"Thanks for everything. Come to think of it, you went to the trouble of training me and we didn't even get to finish that either..."

"I believe I conveyed the single most important thing. Beyond that, if you wish to improve further with the sword, there is nothing for it but to keep swinging, relentlessly. Take care of yourself."

Shaking the offered hand in return, Subaru and Wilhelm exchanged a firm handshake.
Then, following after Rem, who climbed up into the driver's box a step ahead of him, Subaru hoisted himself up onto the cargo bed of the carriage. Looking back at the mansion from atop the cargo bed, he could make out, from a distance, two figures standing on a terrace that overlooked the gate across the garden.
From their silhouettes, he guessed they were Crusch and Ferris.
Thinking they probably couldn't see him, Subaru waved a hand at the two of them anyway, then called out once more, one last time, to Wilhelm.

"Well, we're off. If our paths cross again, let's get along."

"If a welcome of being thrashed half to death with a wooden sword suits your taste, anytime."

Smiling like a gentleman, Wilhelm then bowed politely at the waist to see them off.
The earth dragon let out a cry, and with a slow initial burst of speed, the carriage bit into the ground and began to move. The mansion and its inhabitants began, little by little, to grow distant.
Watching the white-haired figure, growing ever more distant, dutifully continue to bow until they were out of sight, Subaru resolved in his heart, "Next time, I'm definitely landing at least one hit."

Down the slope, out through the entrance of the noble district, past the guard post, straight along the main road of the lower district, and out through the great front gate connecting the royal capital to the outside world — and they would be on the highway they sought.
Perhaps thanks to the earth dragon's Divine Protection, Subaru felt only the faintest of vibrations beneath him as, unable to contain his impatience, he let his gaze wander over the scenery outside the small window.

Since he'd been unconscious upon arriving at the royal capital, this was his first time seeing the area around it. Even so, once out on the highway, the scenery around the royal capital wasn't so different from what he'd just seen leaving Roswaal Manor.
At most, the width of the well-maintained highway was greater, and far behind them, the royal capital's skyline could be seen with its strong presence. Even that vanished completely after only ten-odd minutes of travel.

Once his field of view was dominated by nothing but the green plains stretching out and the blue sky, Subaru suddenly found himself with nothing to do, left with no choice but to lean his back against the wall of the carriage.
On the trip there, there had been a fellow passenger inside the carriage, so boredom hadn't been much of an issue, but this time his only companion was Rem alone, seated in the driver's box. He couldn't very well distract her while she was concentrating on handling the earth dragon, so all Subaru could do was sink into his own thoughts.

For a carriage used by someone of a duke's household, the feel of the seat on the cargo bed where Subaru sat was not particularly good. This smaller one was probably not meant for someone of Crusch's high standing to ride in, but rather the type used by the people who served as her hands and feet.
Thinking of how her mansion had seen ceaseless comings and goings, it made sense that the long-distance carriages would all be out on assignment.

Participation in the Royal Selection alone gave rise to that much involvement with those around her.
For a ducal house, the sheer number of dealings would likely be considerable, and if one aimed for the throne, one would need an even wider, greater network of connections.
Such political matters were ones Emilia, too, would from now on have no choice but to step into. And before that hardship, an unnecessary trial awaited her.

"That's why I've got to hurry and get there."

Of course, Subaru did not think so highly of himself as to believe he could personally take part in matters of politics or in the challenge of securing powerful backers.
Even in his original world, he'd been someone who held not a shred of interest in fields like politics or economics. Even when Japan's prime minister changed, Subaru had been the type to think, "with how often the top changes, it's probably the same no matter who's in charge..." and had no further interest beyond that.

Therefore, there was nothing concrete in Subaru's mind, no specific vision, about rushing to Emilia's side.
It was only that a slight sense of urgency, one Subaru himself was not even aware of, was averting his eyes from those questions he ought to have been thinking through.

"If I'm not there, it's no good... if I go, she'll understand."

There was nothing there but a groundless conviction — no, only a wish.
Emilia was in a desperate situation. If only he himself rushed to her side, then surely everything would somehow work out — such was the faint, flickering hope that seemed as though it might be blown out at any moment.

He wanted to prove his worth, needed to prove it.
If Emilia was up against hardship, then that was something Subaru could do.
No — it had to be that way. So that Subaru could find his own worth, so that he could make it known — she had to be in a desperate situation—

"That's right... I've gotta be there. Definitely."

Muttering to himself, Subaru sank alone into thought.
What rose in his mind was a beloved girl with silver hair. Her smile, covered by some unfathomable darkness, being buried beneath a malice trying to crush that noble heart of hers.

Envisioning that scene, Subaru bit down on his lip and shut his eyes.
Alone on the cargo bed, Subaru quietly kept waiting for his time to come. Alone, in a place where, save for Rem up in the driver's box, he could feel the presence of no one but himself.

Head bowed, eyes closed, Subaru sat.
That the corner of his mouth had, ever so slightly, twisted — of that, in the end, not even he himself took any notice.
