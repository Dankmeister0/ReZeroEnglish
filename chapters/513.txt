Arc 7 Chapter 15: "The Thing I Want to Protect"

"——!"

The instant a sharp, heavy report cracked out, animals in the distance all kicked off the ground at once.
In a small stand of trees they'd passed partway across the plains, deer-like animals had been grazing in the shade. Their proud, well-developed antlers and black coats had earned them the name "Black Deer" — a species that also lived in the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica, and could be called a fairly common animal in this other world.

And in the herd that had bolted apart at the sound and shock, one black deer had been left behind, collapsed in the grass.
A thick arrow was buried in the deer's body, its heart shattered by a single blow.
The twitching of its limbs was nothing more than a death spasm, one that would soon still.
In any case—

"Got some meat, noh!"

"...Meat? At least call it a black deer."

"Eh? What'd you say just now, noh? Kuna's voice is too quiet, I can't hear her well, noh."

The one who'd let out the shout of triumph was Holly, who'd loosed the powerful bow.
With a cheerful smile still on her face, she hadn't caught the mutter of the girl beside her — Kuna — and tilted her head in puzzlement.
But seeing that, Kuna pursed her lips and said,

"It's nothin'! Let's bleed it out! Hurry it up!"

"Ah, wait up, noh!"

Kuna stomped off, and Holly hurried to follow after her — but before she could, Holly stopped short with a "whoa" and spun around.
At the end of Holly's gaze stood the shapes of their traveling companions—

"Since we're here, let's take a little break, noh. That okay with you too, Subaru, noh?"

"...Ah, yeah, sure, that's totally fine, go for it."

Subaru answered Holly's suggestion of a break while sweating like a waterfall.
Seeing him, Holly said "Good, noh" and chased after Kuna's back. Watching her go, Subaru slowly sank to his knees right where he stood.

Behind the utterly exhausted Subaru, Rem — riding in the carrier on his back — let out a small sigh, and

"...Stubborn."

she murmured, careful that Subaru wouldn't hear.


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△


"Man, I seriously underestimated that. If I wasn't the eldest son, I'd have been whining. Being the eldest, I could take it, but if I were a second son or the youngest, no way."

Bundling up the dry branches he'd gathered, Subaru made excuses in the cool breeze.
Rem listened with a flat, unimpressed look, while Rui listened with a vacant expression that gave no clue what she was thinking. Still, at the content of it, Rem sighed as if exasperated.

"That makes no sense. To begin with, does whether or not you have siblings have anything to do with patience?"

"That was a kind of standard joke just now, but I do feel like there's actually a correlation between endurance and having siblings. You know how there's that thing where the eldest gets raised strictly by the parents and the youngest gets spoiled?"

"I wouldn't know, even if you say 'you know.' And it wouldn't apply if someone's an only child, would it?"

"In that case, you end up raised strictly while also being spoiled... which is exactly the case for me, a man who fits the only-child trait of being both the eldest and the youngest son."

Considering how close his parents were, it was rather strange that Subaru had no siblings. He was well aware he'd monopolized his father and mother's love, so he couldn't help but wonder what it would've been like with brothers or sisters — though wondering wasn't going to change reality.

"Besides, there's no guarantee a little brother or sister hasn't shown up while I'm not around right now..."

"——"
"Ah-wah-ah."

While Subaru entertained that terrifying thought, Rui was fooling around on the lap of Rem, whose eyes were lowered.
While traveling, the carrier was fixed to Subaru's back, but it was clever enough to double as a chair once set down on the ground.
Thanks to that, Rem wasn't forced to go through the trouble of climbing in and out.
Though it might well gnaw at Rem to be constantly relying on Subaru's help — that was a humiliation she'd simply have to endure a while longer.

"Siblings..."

"Hm?"

"Family born before me, or after me... did I have any?"

"Oh—"

Asked that out of nowhere by Rem, Subaru involuntarily held his breath.
Startled, he looked up to find Rem twining her fingers in Rui's hair on her lap to keep her entertained, her blue eyes turned toward him.
He couldn't read the emotion wavering faintly in that light. Probably, he thought, even Rem herself didn't fully understand it.
Still—

"This is a first. You, trying to ask me about your memories."

"Where this is, who you are, who I am, what you intend to do, with what face... I think I've asked plenty of times before."

"Not that kind of negative stuff. Also, I'm pretty sure you hadn't said the 'with what face' one yet..."

Subaru gave a wry smile at Rem's needling way of speaking, but even so, he felt a faint sense of relief.
Just as he'd told her, this was the first time Rem had asked Subaru something in a positive sense. Subaru took that as progress in their relationship.

Honestly, ever since leaving the Shudrak settlement, Subaru had been anxious the whole way here.
After all, Rem — who had shown such open hostility and suspicion toward him — hadn't rejected his proposal to spend several days traveling to the town.

Frankly, he'd been sure the biggest obstacle to leaving the Shudrak settlement would be Rem's stubborn refusal to lend an ear to anything he said.
And yet that same Rem hadn't opposed his plan, had been cooperative. — Rather than believe in a miracle, he'd half wondered what kind of disaster it was a sign of.

But as it stood, Rem remained cooperative, and hadn't given Subaru a hard time with unreasonable demands from atop the carrier. If anything, she'd scold Rui whenever the girl squirmed about trying to disrupt the march, even trying to lighten Subaru's burden.

"——"

"What? Are you saying you don't intend to talk?"

"No, no, that's not it. That's jumping to conclusions. It's just, you know, my relationship with you has been kind of awkward, right?"

"It still is. And it's not 'awkward,' it's 'strained.'"

"I'm saying that strain has eased up a little, I think!"

Rem looked at him like he was something filthy, and Subaru felt his heart wounded all over again. But he carefully filed that wound away too, as something given to him by Rem, and scratched his cheek with a finger.

"I don't know everything about you either. But I know more about you than you know about yourself right now. If there's something you want to ask, I'm willing to answer as much as I can. But..."

"Whether I can believe it is up to me..."

"Mm."

Nodding shortly, Subaru glanced at Rem to gauge her reaction.
She combed her fingers through Rui's hair, brows knit as if lost in thought. After a while, Rem met Subaru's eyes again and said,

"I don't understand it."

"Don't understand... yourself, you mean?"

"You. ...I don't understand at all what kind of person you actually are. Because what I feel and what I see don't match up."

Rem pressed her lips tightly together, her gaze taking on a cold heat.
It wasn't that the distance between their hearts had widened — it meant her seriousness had deepened. Rem's eyes grew more intent as she tried to take Subaru's measure.
That was proof, at least, that she'd decided his character was worth examining.

"...Compared to being flatly treated as the embodiment of evil, that feels like huge progress."

"Even now, I think you're only a hair's breadth from being the embodiment of evil. ...It's just that I've come to think I might allow a single thin sheet of paper between us."

That one added remark felt like a handshake of concession, offered from Rem.
With relief, Subaru reached out to shake that invisible offered hand in return. Rem looked at him suspiciously as he shook hands with thin air, but—
Anyway—

"So, now that a sheet of thin paper's been slipped between the thin ice of our relationship... what now? Is there anything you want to ask?"

"...Let me think about it a little more."

Subaru, for his part, was ready to open up — but hearing that, Rem shook her head.
Rem's readiness — or more precisely, her readiness to trust Subaru — wasn't there yet.
Perhaps Rem worried that if she heard the truth now, in a state where she couldn't fully trust him, she wouldn't know what to do with the information.
Honestly, it would be a lie to say he felt no frustration at Rem's caution.

"—Got it. I'll wait until you're ready."

"...Don't say it like it's someone else's problem. I think it's fair to say it also depends on your everyday behavior."

"I see... so basically, the more I rack up affection points or trust with you, the sooner the route opens up, huh."

"I don't understand what that means, but I understand you just said something unpleasant."

At Subaru's satisfied nod, hand to his chin, he could practically hear the sound of Rem's displeasure meter filling up again.
And then, to Subaru and the others carrying on this conversation—

"Sorry to keep you waiting, noh. The black deer's all nicely dressed and done, noh."

Holly came back, beaming.
She looked thoroughly pleased with herself, the dressed black deer hanging from the end of a branch slung over her shoulder. Behind the cheerful Holly, Kuna — who apparently had been the one doing all the actual butchering — wore a face of exhaustion.

"Why the hell do I have to do all of it..."

"'Cause Kuna's better at it, noh. If the meat got ruined, I'd stay hungry no matter how much I ate, noh."

"Why's that gotta follow!? Whatever you eat oughta stay in your stomach, you weirdo!"

Kuna shouted at the easygoing Holly, but Holly just laughed it off.
Then Holly's eyes fell on the bundle of dry branches Subaru had gathered.

"Oh, you gathered a nice bunch, noh. Impressive, impressive, noh."

"Can't exactly hunt. Figured I'd at least do this much. ...Mind if I learn how to start a fire?"

"Learn? Never heard that word before, noh."

"It means gettin' taught, probably..."

Holly, having tilted her head, brightened at Kuna's correction and laughed, "That's right, noh."
Then Holly pulled a small black stone from her belongings, struck it together quickly where Subaru could see, and lit a branch on fire with the spark, easy as that.

"Oh, oh— whoa, amazing. That's a craftsman's touch."

"It's a firestone, noh. Once you get the knack, it's easy, noh. You should try it too, Subaru, noh."

"You'll let me? Then let me borrow it for a sec..."

Holly tossed him the firestone, and Subaru tried copying her motions from before by eye. He failed the first three times, but on the fourth try, a spark finally came.
After that, doing the same thing over the dry branches—

"It's lit!"

"Well done, noh! Now whenever the meat's ready, we can grill it up and eat any time, noh!"

"When she says 'meat,' she means the animal before it becomes meat, so don't take her seriously."

Holly and Kuna gave wildly opposite evaluations of Subaru's small sense of achievement.
Keeping both reactions in mind, Subaru watched the black deer meat being roasted over the fire and brought up, "By the way, though—"

"When we found that herd of deer, Holly's quick draw was amazing. By the time I noticed, the arrow was already loosed."

"That's thanks to Kuna spotting the herd, noh. Thanks to her, we got fresh meat, noh."

"I just said there was a herd there. ...Quick-draw aside, any Shudrak can loose an arrow."

"Except Kuna, noh."

"Ugh—"

Struck in a sore spot, Kuna's expression went bitter and stiff.
At Kuna's reaction, Rem's eyes widened. "Is that so?"

"That's surprising. I'd heard from Mizelda-san that Kuna-san has sharp eyes, so..."

"...Havin' sharp eyes don't matter none if your aim's bad."

"When it comes to archery, Kuna even loses to Utakata. Cute, noh."

"Shut it!"

Rated below even Utakata, Kuna drove a knife-hand strike into Holly's belly. But Holly's ample body simply bounced the blow right off.
Rem watched their apparently routine exchange with a warm smile, but hearing about Utakata's archery skill left Subaru with rather complicated feelings.
After all, Subaru had once lost his life to one of Utakata's poisoned arrows.
In terms of raw power, Utakata's lack of strength probably couldn't kill Subaru outright — but coat the arrowhead in poison, and lethality was hardly handicapped at all.
Though from the standpoint of hunting and taking game, poison probably wasn't very welcome.

"Besides..."

Fingers to his lips, Subaru found himself thinking a little about that powerful bow.
That is, the existence of the "hunter" who had attacked Subaru and Rem in the forest before they were captured at the imperial soldiers' camp. A master archer who had once protected them from a Witchbeast, and once killed Subaru.
That hunter's identity still hadn't come to light.
So when Holly had shot through the black deer, the blood had drained from Subaru's face.
Still, hearing this talk of how the Shudrak handled bows—

"—Is it pointless to think beyond 'someone from the Shudrak'?"

At that point in time, Subaru and the others had been suspicious troublemakers who'd wandered into the forest.
It wouldn't be unreasonable for someone to judge Subaru, shouting loudly as he searched for Rem, to be a suspicious threat worth eliminating. The same held for the battle against the Witchbeast that followed.
To begin with, the hunter had protected them from the Witchbeast. — It could be said there wasn't much basis for flatly declaring them an enemy.

"Holly-san and Kuna-san get along well, don't you."

While Subaru was lost in thought like that, Rem's conversation with Holly and the others had picked up.
What Rem had ventured into was the topic of Holly and Kuna — the two young members of the People of Shudrak accompanying them.
However, only Holly laughed at being called close — Kuna, for her part, made a face of distaste and even stuck out her tongue with an "ugh."

"What's with that reaction and that face? Neither one's something a pretty girl should be doing."

"You just reminded me we're stuck with each other for life. I've been nothin' but worn out by her..."

"Ahahaha, Kuna's such a worrywart, noh."

"Wh-o-se-fault-d'you-think-that-is!"

Kuna, furious, grabbed onto Holly and shook her shoulders violently back and forth. But the difference in their builds was large, and Holly — nearly twice the mass of the slender Kuna — didn't budge an inch.
In the end, Kuna, worn out for nothing despite doing as she pleased, dropped her shoulders and ground her teeth in frustration with a "Dammit," and that settled it.

"Me and Kuna were born on the same day, noh. We're neighbors, practically sisters, noh."

"I'd refuse someone like you as either an older or younger sister..."

"Ah, it's about time this is getting nicely cooked, noh."

"Listen to me!!"

Against Holly's thoroughgoing, unshakeable pace, Kuna's protests only rang out in vain.
Watching the scene, the sight of Kuna being jerked around somehow overlapped, in Subaru's mind, with a certain hard-luck martial-arts-inclined domestic affairs officer.

"Kuna's got her hair dyed green too, so their image colors overlap... that guy asserts himself even in scenes he's not in."

It was the kind of baseless accusation that, if the man himself had heard it, would probably have prompted a "Now that's a false charge if I ever heard one!?" — but since he wasn't present, Subaru filed it away as an auditory hallucination.
Still, watching Holly and Kuna bicker back and forth about how well-done the meat was, Subaru heard Rem, her eyes softening just slightly, murmur, "I envy that."

"Having someone you can speak so frankly with, like that..."

"...Ah, Rem, mind if I say one thing?"

What had been packed into that murmur was a longing both plain and desperate.
For Rem, without her memories, everyone around her — Subaru included — was like an invader emerging from the darkness; there was probably never a moment when her heart could truly find peace.
Whether this would serve to heal that taut, strained heart of hers, he didn't know, but—

"What is it?"

"I said I'd stay quiet until you wanted to ask, but I'm going to let one thing slip."

"——"

"You have an older sister. Your twin sister, and she cares about you more deeply than anything. ...So no matter where you are, you're never truly alone."

Hearing those words from Subaru, Rem opened her round eyes even wider.
Having said it, Subaru asked himself whether he should have held back — but he quickly resolved to tell himself this decision had been the right one.
It was the kind of act that deserved to be cursed as going back on his word before the words were even dry — Rem had only just said she needed to wait until she could trust him.
But it wasn't just Rem's anxiety — Subaru himself had reached his limit too.
At the very least, surely it was fine to tell her that Ram existed.
Ram, who was surely, even now, far away in the land of Lugunica, worrying over her little sister. — Ram, who through their shared senses could probably feel Rem's presence.

"I wouldn't know, but if you close your eyes and think of her, you might be able to feel it. That's what they call the twins' shared senses."

"Shared senses..."

With some hesitation, Rem tentatively placed a hand to her own chest and closed her eyes.
Just like that, still and silent, seeking the other half of herself — the twin sister born on the same day from the same mother — Rem's consciousness began reaching a hand out toward a dark, nighttime sea.
But—

"...I can't feel anything."

"I see... I guess it's tough without being able to picture her, huh."

Shaking her head slowly, Rem reported the failure of the shared sense.
For a moment, a flicker of anxiety welled up — had something happened to Ram, that they couldn't connect? — but he judged that the distance lying between them, both physical and mental, was simply too great.
Honestly, if the shared senses had connected properly, it felt like a lot of things might have suddenly turned around for the better, so he felt quite a pang of regret — but he couldn't let that show.
The one who felt it most bitterly was Rem.
For now, at the very least, he had to support her through that frustration—

"—Ah."
"Uu?"

But while Subaru was still choosing words of comfort, a faint breath slipped from Rem's lips.
The reason was the hand she'd placed on her chest — over which Rui had laid her own hand. Still resting her head on Rem's lap, Rui had placed her hand over Rem's as if worrying for her from right below.
At that gesture from Rui, Rem's lips loosened into a gentle smile, and

"Thank you. I'm all right."
"Ahh."

When Rem smiled bravely, Rui, seeing it, laughed happily too.
Watching the two of them and their warm, heartwarming atmosphere, Subaru gritted his back teeth at having been too slow, and at having his role stolen out from under him.

"Damn it... so you really are my enemy after all...!"

"Why does it come to that? Don't you think that's childish?"

Seeing Subaru glaring at Rui, Rem's opinion of him dropped yet again.
Unaware of that, Rui, receiving Subaru's glare, flailed her arms and legs about in high spirits.
And then, oblivious to Subaru and the others' strained relationship—

"It's cooked, noh!"
"It's still half-raw!!"

—the two of them, whose relationship was already well-established, were the ones raising their voices.

—Taking four days in all, the group safely arrived at "Guaral."

"That's Guaral... protected by some pretty impressive walls."

What could be seen in the distance was a walled castle town, encircled by tall ramparts.
Faced with a sight far grander than what he'd pictured on hearing "the nearest town," Subaru felt both the surprise of having his expectations upended and the sensation of unexpected good fortune falling into his lap.

"Pretty intimidating atmosphere for a city... are giants coming or something?"

"The Giant tribe? I heard they were nearly wiped out a long time ago."

"Is that so? Then I wonder if the old geezer I know is the last of the Giants..."

To Kuna, who answered him seriously, Subaru let slip that pointless remark.
It was surprising that Old Man Rom, the only Giant Subaru knew, might occupy such a rare position. Then again, he'd heard from Rem that the Oni Tribe was also quite small in number, so perhaps the competition for survival among races in this world was fairly harsh too.

"Now that I think about it calmly, I've never run into anyone Elf-related besides Emilia-tan, so maybe Elves are pretty scarce too."

As a standard trope of fantasy stories, it was a common thing for long-lived races to have weak fertility in exchange for their long lifespans, unable to grow their numbers much.
On top of that, this world's fear of the Witch of Envy meant that Half-Elves were, in practice, loathed and shunned. Naturally, it was conceivable that this same superstition weighed down on pure-blooded Elves as well, as the very soil from which Half-Elves were born.

"Thinking of Emilia-tan in a place without Emilia-tan. Damn, I haven't seen Beako in a while either — feels like my Emiliase and Beatromin deficiency is getting pretty serious."

For both nutritional deficiencies, the prescribed remedy was contact with Emilia and Beatrice.
In all seriousness, the mental fatigue of ongoing anxiety and tension was considerable, so just being able to hear the voices of those two — his sources of reassurance — would have made things a good deal easier.

"I miss Ram's voice, Petra's, Frederica's too... at this point, even Roswaal's voice would do."

"Um, could you please cut out that pointless inner turmoil already?"

"Ah, my bad."

Rem, on his back, caught him on the mutterings that kept spilling out of him.
Over the course of the journey, Subaru had carried Rem in the carrier the entire way without handing her off to anyone else. On the first and second days he hadn't yet gotten the knack of carrying her, wasting a noticeable amount of stamina, but from the third day on, having found his pacing and balance, his progress picked up considerably.

"Man, when it comes to carrying you on my back, nobody's got anything on me."

"Please don't compete over something so unflattering. Also, Holly-san and the others—"

Since they were positioned back to back, whatever Rem pointed out was behind Subaru. Following her words, he turned around to find Holly and Kuna there.
And then, beside Kuna, who was scratching her head listlessly, Holly stepped forward toward Subaru and the others.

"Well, since we made it here safe, this is where we part ways, noh."

"Ah... you two aren't coming into the city?"

"No point us goin' in. Our job's to see you two safely here."

"I see... You two really helped us out."

At the sudden farewell, Subaru nonetheless reminded himself that this had always been a given.
The two of them had, after all, only come along out of the goodwill of the People of Shudrak. Having been helped by them countless times along the way, he'd grown thoroughly used to leaning on them.

Holly, who'd eased the journey with her hunting skill and cheerful nature.
Kuna, whose earnest willingness to answer whatever he asked, and surprising breadth of knowledge, had been so reliable.

Parting from the two of them, Subaru now, at last, faced a journey of three — himself, Rem, and — no, Rem and Rui.
At the imperial camp, and at the Shudrak settlement too, there had always been someone else around. This time was different.

"...Geez, quit makin' such a pathetic face."

"Sorry. Pathetic face, huh... whoa!?"

Seeing Subaru's black eyes wavering with anxiety about what lay ahead, Kuna thrust something at him.
He caught it reflexively, and the unexpected weight pitched him forward. What had been handed to him was a long, white bundle — the very thing Holly had been carrying on her back the whole trip.
He'd simply assumed it was some necessity for their travels.

"Come to think of it, I don't think I ever saw it opened once... what is it?"

"It's yours, Subaru, noh. The chieftain told us to hand it over once we got you safely to town, noh."

"Mine, and once we reached town...?"

Not grasping the true meaning behind Holly's words, Subaru knit his brows in puzzlement. But Kuna roughly urged him on: "Just open it already."
At that, Subaru set the carrier down on the ground and opened the white bundle.
Inside was—

"Ah..."

"Is this... a horn?"

In Subaru's arms was a white mass roughly the size of an armful. Rem's murmur at the sight told Subaru what it was too — a horn. A Witchbeast's horn.
It was something he recognized. He'd seen it twice; the clear sighting had been during the Lifeblood Ritual.

"Wait, is this Elgina's horn?"

"That's right, noh. You're the one who broke it off, Subaru, so it's yours, noh."

"It's valuable stuff. A size like that'll fetch a good price."

"—!"

Hearing it would fetch a good price, Subaru caught his breath at what the two of them had arranged.
In other words, Holly and the others were telling him to trade this Witchbeast horn for the travel funds he and the others would need to return to the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica.
And they'd carried this baggage for that very purpose, all without saying a word about it.

"That must've been pretty heavy, though..."

"You went and carried Rem the whole way too, didn't you."

"Besides, besides, I'm super strong, noh. So it was nothing, noh."

At Subaru's voice trembling over what they'd intended, both Kuna and Holly wore faces as if it were nothing at all.
At their consideration, Subaru was, quite literally, at a loss for words.

"——"

He'd been helped along the way, and now they'd even arranged this much toward his travel funds.
And yet, despite that, Subaru would part from them and return to his own country. — Holly and the others would rejoin their Shudrak companions and join Abel in the fight to retake the imperial capital.
Along that road, producing a great many deaths and survivals alike—

"I..."

"—Don't go thinkin' anything stupid."

"——"

"Fight to protect what you want to protect. Same goes for us."

Subaru, on the verge of blurting out some impulsive words, was sharply cut off by Kuna.
Still wearing her usual languid air, she glared at Subaru as if he were a nuisance.
Kuna, full of complaints, always irritated with Holly. And yet not once had she shown any sign of wanting to leave the Shudrak, or of truly disliking Holly.
As a member of the Shudrak, she took it as a given that she'd fight alongside Abel.
—That, surely, was proof that what she called "the thing she wanted to protect" was already settled.

"Don't go draggin' your feet. I got sharp eyes. If you go and do somethin' stupid, I'll spot it right away."

"And then if Kuna tells me, I'll go blam with my bow and arrow, noh!"

"...Yeah, that's terrifying, all right."

Subaru understood from their words that he was being gently pushed away.
If he acted impulsively here, he'd be treating the kindness of these two — no, of the People of Shudrak — with contempt. That was something he couldn't do, not to the people who'd called him a comrade.

"I'll gratefully put this toward the journey, then. Thank you both, for everything!"

Reading their intent, Subaru swallowed down the feelings welling up in him.
At that, Holly and Kuna each nodded back in their own respective way.

"Holly-san, Kuna-san, thank you for everything on the road. I won't forget my gratitude to you both, and to everyone of the People of Shudrak."

"See that you don't. Sounds like you're the type to forget things a lot."

"I think that's a bit much, noh."

Wanting at least to say a proper goodbye, Rem climbed down from the carrier and lingered over her farewell to the two of them.
Surprisingly, Rui too showed signs of not wanting to part from Holly and Kuna. She seemed to have grown especially close with Holly, who treated her without any reserve, and for a while Rui clung to her belly, refusing to let go.

"Well then, Subaru. Don't forget — I'm watchin' you."

"Noh!"

"Yeah! Thank you, really! Thanks for everything!"

Turning his back on the two of them as they waved broadly, Subaru and the others set off on their journey of three.
The Witchbeast horn he'd been entrusted with was wrapped back up and left for Rui to carry on her back. It was a bitter decision on Subaru's part, given his hands were already full, but perhaps Holly and Kuna's words had had some effect — Rui, seeming determined not to drop the bundle, followed along obediently.

"That just means that girl is watching all sorts of things too, you know."

"...I already know she's a lump of curiosity."

Told that by Rem on his back, Subaru answered with a bitter feeling.
Rui Arneb, the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony, had devoured every kind of life in search of the one best suited to her — an explorer, to put it kindly; an omnivore, to put it unkindly.
So merely being shown a bit of admirable behavior wasn't going to change Subaru's impression of her.
It wasn't supposed to change.

"Let's go."

Feeling Rem's sigh reach him from his back, Subaru started walking.
Following after him, Rui's footsteps could be heard as well.

Just the three of them, as it had been at the very start of their push into the Vollachia Empire.
At last, in a form where everyone could be said to be facing the same direction, the three of them advanced.

And so they passed through the gates of the walled city, Guaral.
