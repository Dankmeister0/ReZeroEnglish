Arc 6 Chapter 62: "The Rumbling of Collapse"

—The white world peeled away, and beyond it, a world of color began to reassemble itself.

An enormous, wild, overwhelming brush of god painted color onto the world.
It was a strange sensation, almost like being sealed inside a colorless space and watching, from within, as color was smeared thickly across the world. He'd heard the story that God made the world in seven days, but seeing this sight, it seemed less like God had spent seven days stacking building blocks to shape the world, and more like He had painted the world onto a blank canvas with a brush.

—The cradle of Od Lagna. Or, the "Corridor of Memories."

From that hyperdimensional otherspace called by that name, the existence of Natsuki Subaru was being peeled away.
Leaving behind the space that did not belong to this world, his fragmented consciousness gradually knit itself back together, and little by little, bit by bit, the self that was "himself" reformed—

"—Subaru."

"Nn……"

Groaning at the call, Subaru opened his eyes, aware of the dryness in his throat.
His vision was hazy; after blinking a few times to bring the world into focus, he noticed he was resting his backside and back against something hard and cold. He seemed to be sitting somewhere against a wall.
Right after that, he became aware of a presence right in front of him.

"——"

Peering into Subaru's face from the front was a girl whose distinctively patterned eyes were filled with worry—Beatrice, with her lovely features and flashy dress that suited her so well.
Seeing the depth of concern in her gaze, Subaru realized he'd truly returned from the "Corridor of Memories" to the original world, back inside the Watchtower.

"—Ah."

"Are you conscious? Properly, I mean? You'd best make sure you haven't gone and dropped any memories carelessly. For starters, do you know who Betty is?"

Pat, pat—Beatrice touched Subaru's face and chest, attempting an examination.
He didn't think it was something you could tell by touch, but being worried over was ticklish and yet pleasant all the same. Letting himself be handled in that spirit, Subaru, his cheeks squished about, answered, "I'm fiiine, I'm fiiine."

"No problem. I remember. My memories are intact, and you… Beatrice, I haven't forgotten you either. Or anyone else, for that matter."

"…Well, I suppose I'll praise you for calling Betty's name first."

Beatrice, who had been kneading Subaru's cheeks, relaxed the corners of her eyes in relief at hearing her own name called. Seeing her reaction, Subaru's lips loosened too, and he absentmindedly stroked her head.
Feeling, through his palm, Beatrice narrowing her eyes and accepting the gesture, Subaru took a deep breath and checked—truly checked—that his memories were intact.

He'd declared to Beatrice that he "remembered," but gaining actual certainty of that was difficult.

The Sin Archbishop of "Gluttony" he'd just encountered—Rui Arneb.
It was certain that she was a monster who devoured the "memories" and "names" of countless people, but exactly how the power to eat memories manifested was still an unknown to Subaru.
Did it require an actual bite? Did merely declaring "I'll eat it" suffice? Or was some more complicated procedure required? It wasn't clear—though it's the standard rule for this sort of power that the more complex the restrictions and oaths, the conditions, the greater the power gained. He hoped that wasn't the case here.

In any event, Subaru had no way of knowing whether such conditions had been met within the "Corridor of Memories." In fact, there had been physical contact—his arm had been bitten, licked. If that had been the condition, then he'd been swept up in the mood and thoroughly done in.
So there was every possibility his memory had been reduced to a moth-eaten mess without him even noticing—

"—Probably, I'm fine. My promises, my feelings of love, they're all still here in this chest."

Thinking of Emilia made his heart race; caring for Beatrice made him want to pat her head. Being able to wholeheartedly wish for his companions' safety was proof, too, that Subaru hadn't lost the self he currently was.
If he could keep hold of just that much, at least he wouldn't repeat the disgraceful spectacle of before.

"—Natsuki-kun, have you come back to us?"

A voice slipped into Subaru's eardrums as he checked, deep within himself, for any missing memory.
Raising his head, he saw Echidna, smoothing back her pale lavender hair, approaching him where he sat with Beatrice at his side. Belatedly, seeing her walk over, Subaru noticed something.

"…Emilia-chan, and the others?"

"Good, it seems your ability to properly observe your surroundings hasn't been lost. Do you remember what you were about to do, just before?"

Echidna asked in that self-important way of hers, and meeting her pale blue-green eyes, Subaru understood—looking past her, searching for the figures he wanted to see—that several of his companions were missing.
It would be too charming a story to say they were merely hidden somewhere among the many bookshelves of the archive around them. On his lap, he touched the closed, thick black binding, and let out a breath.
The members who remained here with him now, that was—

"Beatrice, and Echidna, and Meili…?"

"Oh my, has Onii-san finally woken up? You had me sooo worried, you really oughta cut back on the oversleeping."

"Oversleeping…"

Hands on her hips, Meili made a show of indignation, and Subaru held his head in his hands.
Somehow calming his own heart, Subaru tried to work out the details of what exactly was happening.

Inside the room, Emilia, Ram, Julius, and Shaula were nowhere to be seen.
Only the three aforementioned and Subaru remained—which was to say, just four non-combatants. That alone was more than strange enough, but stranger still—

"This isn't a scene right after I opened the book, is it? How long was I out?"

"—About an hour, I suppose. Since up till now the books had only taken a few seconds, Betty was worried sick something might have happened to Subaru."

"An hour…"

Hearing Beatrice's answer, Subaru understood that there wasn't a great gap between the time he'd spent in the "Corridor of Memories" and time in the real world.
Everything about this was different from his previous attempts at the "Book of the Dead," after all. That "Corridor of Memories" truly existed somewhere, and the time spent there hadn't been an illusion either.

He had encountered Rui, had his heart toyed with and licked at by her, and then, trying to strangle her, had denied "Natsuki Subaru" himself—only to have that denied by gentle yet stern words.
It wasn't a bad thing to be able to think that time hadn't been some convenient dream of Subaru's own making. But as a matter of fact, he couldn't overlook that finite time had been shaved away.

"Why aren't Emilia-chan and the others here with you?"

"While you were, quite literally, absorbed in a book, various irregularities began occurring on this end as well. The four who aren't here are running around dealing with them."

"Irregularities…?"

"Shaula was the first to notice something was wrong. In that respect, perhaps it's a testament to her position as this Watchtower's guardian."

Though the content was delivered in a light tone, Echidna's voice wasn't nearly as calm as her words. Suppressing a sense of urgency that still bled through in her voice, Echidna answered Subaru.
She gestured with a hand toward the room's entrance, toward the stairs leading down.

"Shaula said something was approaching from outside the tower. After that, she moved fast. She rushed off to check the cause of the disturbance before there was even time to stop her…"

"And that dashing onii-san chased right after her. Meanwhile, the silver-haired onee-san and the maid onee-san went to go fetch their sleeping little sister, that's how it went."

"So that's why all four left. …Honestly, it's lonely, but that's the right call. Especially in a situation like this, I don't want any family member whose whereabouts I don't know."

Taking in Echidna and Meili's reports, Subaru untangled the confusing situation and let out a breath.
What Shaula had noticed was concerning, but he was grateful for Julius trying to back her up, and for Emilia and Ram's actions.
If the blue-haired girl, Rem, could be brought to safety, that would eliminate one source of worry.

—In the face of whatever emergency was about to unfold.

"—That reaction. Natsuki-kun, do you know something?"

"——"

Echidna raised an eyebrow at Subaru's suddenly thoughtful expression. Bathed in her sharp, intelligent gaze, Subaru was momentarily at a loss for words, but he quickly shook his head.
There was no need to dodge the question, no need to cover it up. He should just speak the halting words as they came, and worry over them together with his companions.

"Let me tell you what happened, starting from the conclusion. —The plan to read Reid's book and dig into his past failed. I didn't get to see his past. It turned into something else entirely."

"You didn't get to see his past, I suppose? What in the world does that mean?"

"Something got in the way. —A Sin Archbishop. Of 'Gluttony.'"

"—!"

He paused a beat, then dropped the name with full weight behind it.
Hearing that word from Subaru's mouth, Beatrice and the others widened their eyes in shock. The term "Sin Archbishop" itself was something Subaru had only just heard for the first time from Rui's own mouth—but for them, it clearly wasn't the first time.

"It's hard to believe on short notice, but—the Sin Archbishop of 'Gluttony' was inside the book… though whether it's right to call it 'inside the book,' in this case, is a question in itself—but she was there?"

Echidna knitted her brow thoughtfully, crossing her arms with a difficult expression. What lodged in her eyes was suspicion, and beyond that, unease. And that unease, moreover, was directed at Subaru himself.
The target of that unease being Subaru's memory was more obvious than words could make it.

"Like I already told Beatrice—my memories aren't missing… I don't think. I haven't forgotten my companions' faces, and my love for Patrasche is the real thing too."

"I don't understand why the Earth Dragon-chan's name comes up there, thoughhh…"

Puffing out her cheeks, Meili grew annoyed at Subaru's particular way of putting things.
Her irritation was justified. In fact, Subaru had a prior record of losing his memory once already. The presence or absence of memory was an ambiguous thing to begin with, and a memory deficit with no self-awareness of it couldn't be proven by anyone.

—However, only Subaru, in this very moment, could say with certainty that this wasn't the case.

"I appreciate you all worrying, but I haven't forgotten a thing. I can guarantee it."

"I'm doubting whether that guarantee of yours has any value… but given you're that confident, there must be some kind of basis for it?"

"—Because Rem saved me, when I was about to be beaten down by 'Gluttony.'"

"——"

The name that followed carried weight only for Beatrice, here in this room.
So instead of Echidna and Meili, whose puzzled expressions didn't budge, it was Beatrice—caught completely off guard—whose eyes went wide.
Subaru nodded deeply at her. The reason Subaru could boldly claim that not even the smallest fragment of memory had been taken by Rui was her presence alone.

The girl who, in that place, had encouraged Subaru when his heart was on the verge of breaking, and made him stand back up.
He believed that her presence was what had denied "Gluttony" that opening.

"Subaru, I suppose I'll have you tell me the details of what happened. And also…"

Fixing her gaze on Subaru's black eyes, Beatrice asked for an account of what had occurred. But at the end of her sentence, where it trailed off, a faint tremor overlapped it—the sensation of the tower, shaking.
Strangely, there was no sign of the bookshelves trembling, but there was no mistaking that the tower as a whole had shaken unnaturally. And that this was likely the very irregularity that had sent Shaula rushing off.

So Beatrice blinked once, and continued.

"As briefly as possible, I suppose."

"—The moment I dove into the 'Book of the Dead,' instead of Reid's past, I got taken to a white place with nothing in it. And there, there was this girl from 'Gluttony'… a Sin Archbishop, and she called herself Rui. According to this Rui, that place is the cradle of Od Lagna, or the 'Corridor of Memories,' something like that."

Inside the tower, while a faint tremor like a rumbling of the earth traveled up through the soles of his feet, Subaru began explaining what he'd seen inside the "Book of the Dead."

The girl he'd encountered in the white space, and the fact that the place had been called the "Corridor of Memories." The involvement of an overwhelmingly powerful, transcendent existence referred to as Od Lagna.
To Subaru, these were just a collection of words and phenomena whose meaning he didn't grasp, but to those present who understood both the common sense and the uncommon sense of this world, it ought to be valuable information.
Sure enough, hearing Subaru's explanation, Beatrice murmured "Od Lagna…" with meaning.

"Rui said something about it being a mechanism to keep the world from breaking. Does that ring any bells?"

"Betty doesn't know much about it in detail either, I suppose. But I do know it's said to be the central point of this world… the place all mana returns to, I suppose."

Shaking her head at the question, Beatrice offered this much. She slipped a finger into one of her vertical ringlets, fiddling with the soft strand of hair as she continued.

"All mana returns to Od Lagna, and circulates there… in other words, Od Lagna might also be an existence deeply involved in the death and rebirth of spirits, I suppose. So it's not a matter entirely unrelated to Betty and the others, I suppose."

"Though, given our origins, we're a bit special. Our existence doesn't pass through Od Lagna. In that sense, perhaps we're able to maintain a more objective view of Od Lagna than ordinary spirits."

"More than ordinary spirits, huh… I see. So that's where that talk about you two living longer than you look comes from."

Echidna picked up where Beatrice's words left off, and hearing her say it, Subaru understood. It had come up as an idle aside, but it seemed true after all that Beatrice and Echidna were beings who had lived for centuries.
The secret behind their longevity was, apparently, their very origin as spirits.

"Personally, I think Beatrice is less 'spirit-cute' and more 'fairy-cute.'"

"…Someone's told Subaru that before, I suppose. Subaru wouldn't know, being ignorant as he is, but around here, the word 'fairy' isn't a compliment. So, well, I suppose I can't quite be pleased with it, nor can I quite be angry at being mocked, and it's all rather unsettling."

"Hm, is that so? Sorry about that then."

Come to think of it, he'd heard that overseas, in the heartland of fantasy, beings like fairies carried a strong image of bewitching and harming humans.
If that notion applied in this world too, it made sense that "fairy" wouldn't be a compliment.

"Sorry. Let me rephrase—I meant it in the sense of being cute enough to eat."

"Wanting to eat someone and thinking they're cute shouldn't go together, it's strange, I suppose!"

"Huh, is it? Don't you sometimes just pop something cute in your mouth without thinking?"

"Now that you mention it, with bad animal-chans, sometimes they accidentally put their own babies in their mouths and bite them to death, you know."

At Meili's unnecessary commentary, Beatrice shuddered, visibly unsettled.
In any case, the conversation had drifted from the main topic yet again. What mattered here was—

"—That cradle of Od Lagna. What kind of place the 'Corridor of Memories' actually is."

"It even came with a fancy title—a place that filters souls, or something?"

"Filters souls… meaning purifies, cleanses them. Considering that Od Lagna's role lies in the circulation of mana, that's not something to laugh off as a joke, is it."

Subaru agreed with Echidna's assessment.
Having seen that space firsthand, he couldn't bring himself to laugh it off as an exaggeration.

All the souls of the dead reach the "Corridor of Memories" and are thereby circulated.
There, souls wash away the grime of memories and history built up over the long haul of a lifetime, becoming pristine existences once more, and are granted a new life.

"I actually find that explanation pretty convincing. A mechanism for recycling the souls of the dead… and the 'memories' washed away there become the books in this archive, the 'Books of the Dead.'"

"So it's not simply erased into nothing—the world, as a matter of fact, remembers the dead. 'What a poet,' I might have said, but having seen this archive, I can't say it so lightly anymore."

The archive recorded everything that had been born into this world and had reached the "Corridor of Memories."
In other words, the "Corridor of Memories" was the compilation room for creating the "Books of the Dead." There, memories arranged into the form of a book were stored in this archive as a "Book of the Dead."
And "Gluttony" was doing harm by forcibly interfering with what should rightfully be an individual's own memories and history.

—That was the true nature of the power possessed by "Gluttony."

"Normally it should've been Od Lagna doing the work of filtering souls, but she's been doing it herself, without permission. And putting the 'memories' she skims off into her own stomach, literally feeding on other people's 'lives.'"

Putting it into words made just how vile the act was all the clearer.
And Rui had declared it, no less, to be "trial and error in the pursuit of happiness." Sampling every kind of life like a snack, and from among them, seeking out the happy life best suited to herself.

That was equivalent to drawing lots endlessly until you hit the jackpot.
Having gained the right to draw infinite lots, Rui—and her siblings—had chosen the path of seizing happiness by feeding on others. —That was what terrified Subaru.
Not their judgment itself. It was that he couldn't convince himself their thinking was some especially aberrant outlier.

Given the right to draw infinite lots, wouldn't anyone choose to keep drawing until they hit the jackpot?
So Rui Arneb's actions were, in the end, an entirely ordinary human desire—

"Even so, normally, one shouldn't be able to keep drawing lots knowing full well that someone else will be sacrificed for it, I suppose."

"Beatrice…"

"Continuing to draw lots knowing there will be sacrifices isn't sane, I suppose. So there's no room for sympathy toward the Sin Archbishops, I suppose."

Reading Subaru's thoughts, Beatrice cut in decisively like that.
To call it a cold, detached judgment would itself be a lack of feeling. Beatrice was simply standing by Subaru's own feelings, voicing the words he couldn't bring himself to say.

Yes—there was no room for sympathy.
The circumstances one was placed in, the lack of any place to belong—perhaps the person themselves wasn't responsible for that. Even so, taking that first wrong step, and continuing to stray from the human path afterward, had been their own choice.
Whoever it was, they had to face the consequences.

"But it's a strange story, isn't it. Why would a book that shows who a person was in life connect to some place like that, that doesn't even make senseee?"

"That's… because the souls of the dead get recycled in the 'Corridor of Memories,' so the book that got shipped out from there connected back to the original supplier, or… no, that doesn't explain anything."

Subaru couldn't offer a clear answer to Meili's question.
Why had Reid's "Book of the Dead" ended up connected to the "Corridor of Memories"?

At the very least, the Subaru from before he lost his memories must have read the "Book of the Dead" once already. He'd heard Julius had read it at the same time, and in loops that had already passed by, he'd even had the experience of reading Meili's book.
But in none of those experiences had the reader's consciousness ever been flung off into that space.
So what if Rui's intervention was the cause?

"But 'Gluttony' didn't seem to have any intention of summoning me there. Even for her, my showing up seemed to be outside her calculations… there shouldn't have been any trick rigged into Reid's 'Book of the Dead.'"

"—There's one hypothesis, I suppose."

In front of Subaru as he pondered, Beatrice held up a single finger.
Watching that fingertip, Subaru pressed, "A hypothesis?"

"Let's hear it. What possibility comes to mind?"

"Suppose the 'Books of the Dead' in this archive are transcribed records of what was skimmed off a soul in the 'Corridor of Memories.' Normally, reading it in the form of a book is this archive's function… but what do you suppose would happen if that record was being put to some other use, I suppose?"

"The memories of the dead, put to some other use? But what else besides a 'Book of the Dead' could even…"

"—Ah, I see, that's it. Clever, Beatrice."

Hearing Beatrice's words, Subaru knit his brow in suspicion. But leaving Subaru aside, it was Echidna who clapped her hands as if it all clicked into place. And not only that—following Echidna, Meili too said "Ohh," a hand to her mouth.

"Could it be, is that what it is?"

"Wait, wait, don't you two go putting on knowing faces. You're really competitive, aren't you. It's nice discovering a new side of yourself like that, but you're in a position where it's totally fine to just say, like a kid, 'I don't know' when you don't know."

"It's Onii-san who's the competitive one, not meee. I only say I know something when I've properly thought it through and figured it out, myself. …It's the redheaded onii-san upstairs, isn't it."

Pushing away the closing-in Subaru with a hand, Meili confirmed this with Beatrice. Beatrice let out a sigh.

"Meili's guess is right, I suppose. …It's Reid, I suppose."

"Um, meaning?"

"We couldn't reference Reid's past from the 'Book of the Dead' because right now, Reid Astrea's record isn't in that book. Reid's record is currently being used in another form—to recreate Reid Astrea in this tower as the examiner."

"Ahh!"

Hearing Echidna's chewed-down explanation, Subaru finally arrived at understanding.
If that were the case, it all made sense. It matched, without contradiction, the events that had actually occurred when Subaru read the "Book of the Dead."

"Reid's memory is being used for recreation, not viewing, I suppose. That's why I'd guess Subaru couldn't read it earlier, I suppose."

"And that also explains how, yesterday's me had my memories eaten by Rui in the 'Corridor of Memories.'"

There was no doubt that the Subaru from before—yesterday's Subaru—had noticed the same strategy for beating Reid that today's Subaru had. And that, trying to test it, he'd read Reid's book in the archive and ended up in that white world.
In fact, Rui had said her encounter with Subaru was the second, following the previous night. On the first contact, she'd eaten Subaru's memories; on the second meeting, she'd been trying to finish off even the leftovers.

"But thinking about it that way, this book will never become a strategy guide for beating Reid, will it. …No, worse than that—it's a dangerous volume that forces you into an unwanted one-on-one with 'Gluttony.'"

"Indeed. Even so, though it came in an unexpected form, I'd call it a stroke of luck that we've grasped the cause of Natsuki-kun's lost memories. The memory loss wasn't part of the tower's function. …The trap of being made to encounter 'Gluttony' likely isn't the tower's intent, for now."

"That's, well, yeah, I guess so."

Whether the tower's creator had even anticipated Subaru being transported right before an instant-death trap was unclear. He preferred to think of it as an accidental occurrence.
If not, then the person who'd outfitted this tower's facilities had to be either possessed of a catastrophically twisted mind or a devilishly bad personality.
Subaru had no memory of it himself, but the fact that Shaula spoke of Oshi-sama as being one and the same existence as Subaru made the reputational damage all the more frightening.

"Reid was recreated, and the book itself was an empty 'blank' page. So that 'Book of the Dead' was connected directly to Od Lagna's doorstep… is that about right?"

"For now, I can't think of a hypothesis beyond that, I suppose. Why one of 'Gluttony' was hiding there in the first place is still a mystery, I suppose. But there's one thing I can say, which is…"

The chain of inexplicable events surrounding Reid's "Book of the Dead."
One hypothesis had now been strung together for it. Of course, questions with no answer still remained. But unfortunately, Subaru and the others didn't have time to examine every one of those questions.
As proof of that—

"—Echidna! Is everyone all right!?"

Bounding up the stairs, a lone knight appeared at the "Taygeta" archive. Seeing him, Echidna, turning around, widened her eyes.

"Julius, you seem quite flustered."

"Something unexpected is occurring. I wanted to hear everyone's thoughts right away… hm."

Answering Echidna, the handsome man walked toward them—Julius.
His cheeks were stiff with faint tension and wariness, but when his eyes met Subaru, sitting cross-legged on the floor, his yellow eyes widened slightly.

"You've woken, Subaru. That's fortunate. Do you know who I am?"

"Right, that's the first thing to check, huh. Um, you are…?"

"—As I feared."

"That was a lie! A joke! You're Julius Juukulius! What's with the serious face, 'as I feared'! I'm properly back, all right!"

"Heh. That was a joke on my end too. Consider it payback for your incurable childishness. —Because after this, I'm afraid I'll have to tell you something rather unfunny."

Gu, groaned Subaru, bested and pulling a sour face. Echidna scoffed at his reaction, and then Julius immediately drew his expression tight.
Just as he'd said, it seemed there was a report to make that wasn't the least bit amusing.

"I'd like to hear Subaru's report from returning out of the 'Book of the Dead' as well, but there's an urgent matter. —I went to confirm, outside the tower, what the disturbance Lady Shaula sensed actually was."

"I don't see Shaula around, though…"

"She's currently responding to the threat from the tower's outer wall. But there are, simply, not enough hands."

Subaru glared sharply at Julius over his roundabout way of putting things. Meeting that pointed gaze, Julius let out a small breath, and prefacing with "forgive me," said:

"You've likely all felt the faint rumbling in the earth. This is footsteps."

"Footsteps?"

"Yes."

Subaru and the others tilted their heads at the unexpected word. Julius dipped his chin toward them, and after a beat, announced:


"—The witchbeasts scattered across the Augria Sand Dunes are converging on this tower, all at once. Lady Shaula is fighting them off, but it's only a matter of time before they're pushed inside."
