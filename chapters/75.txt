One Day I Part 1: "Withdrawal Symptoms"

This story takes place after the conclusion of Arc 2. It's best read after Arc 2, so if you haven't read Arc 2 yet, we hope you'll read that first.

— It was a morning some three days after the incident in the Witchbeast forest had been settled, and the promise between Subaru and Emilia had been made.

"—I can't take it anymore. This is my limit. I can't endure it. I'm going home to my parents!"

He shouted, slammed the table, and stood up.

The sudden outburst interrupted the quiet breakfast, and everyone present looked up at Subaru.

Receiving everyone's stares, Subaru spread both arms wide and—

"Ahh, I just... can't."

He muttered weakly, pressed a hand to his forehead, and collapsed limply back into his chair.

The one who deftly caught Subaru as he abruptly sank down was Rem, who had been waiting at his left side. She pulled the chair back at just the right moment to catch him, and said,

"That's dangerous, Subaru-kun. You're still recovering, so please don't overdo it and worry us so."

She gently patted his slumping shoulders, and from behind trailed a finger along the line of his jaw as she voiced her devotion.

It was a somewhat excessive show of physical affection, but this was simply how Rem had been treating Subaru these past few days—it was normal by now. For a girl who had self-appointed herself "the one in charge of looking after Subaru, who still isn't quite back to normal," this much was merely the opening act.

At the pretty maid's affectionate touch, however, Subaru curtly brushed away the finger tracing his jaw, shaking his head and saying, "No, no, that's not it..."

"It's not that... It's more serious than that."

He declared this in a grave tone and slumped limply against the table—though not before shoving aside the plates that had been set out to clear himself a space first. It was only natural that Emilia, having by now grown thoroughly used to Subaru sitting beside her, judged there was nothing urgent going on.

"Are you all right? If something's wrong, tell me. I'll do anything for you—that kind of thing... You're not gonna say that, Emilia-tan?"

"If I reacted to every single thing you do like that, I'd wear myself out. Keep that up too much and pretty soon nobody's going to want anything to do with you."

"Guh, harsh. But even your profile is cute right now... dammit."

Emilia cut him down flatly while carrying something salad-like to her mouth, as Subaru rested his face on the table, glancing up at her.

As Subaru pouted, plainly displeased, Rem raised her hand energetically.

"Me, me! Rem will listen to anything. Rem will do anything. Just give the order. And once Rem's done it well, you're welcome to pat her head, too."

Rem practically radiated the image of a puppy wagging its tail. To the maid appealing so excessively to be praised, praised, Subaru extended a trembling finger toward the table.

And then—

"But... he really does seem to be in pain right now."

At the sight of Subaru's paling face and the sweat beading on his brow, even Emilia—or rather, Emilia, who for all her bluster was fundamentally kind-hearted—couldn't help but react.

The finger on the table was visibly trembling too; it was clear something was not normal.

"Could it be... some aftereffect of the curse...?"

Voicing the worst possibility, Emilia and Rem exchanged glances. Then, worry welling in her eyes, Emilia reached a hand into her silver hair and plucked out a small gray kitten from within.

The kitten, who had apparently been indulging in a lazy nap, was pinched between two fingers and set on the table. Rubbing his eyes, Puck was placed sitting right in front of Subaru's face, sniffing away with his little pink nose.

"Hmm, I don't get the feeling it's anything for Lia to worry about? His mana's empty so his vitality's still weak as ever, but other than that, he seems just like normal."

Puck reported this in a leisurely voice, patting the cheek of Subaru, who was slumped over the table, with his paw pad. Subaru's trembling finger reached toward him, and Puck's small body was soon enveloped in his palm, the petting session beginning.

Setting aside Puck's "auauaa"-ing as he was petted, Emilia let out a sigh of relief just from hearing the report and seeing Subaru's undiminished petting form.

But then, that left Subaru's current state unexplained—

"Trembling fingers, shallow breathing. It's ju-ust like withdrawal symptoms from some highly addictive drug, isn't it?"

Seated at the head of the table, diagonally to the right in front of Emilia, who furrowed her brow in confusion, Roswaal murmured this while resting a hand on his chin.

Glancing at Subaru's symptoms, Roswaal gave a small nod and continued,

"When one uses the sort of drug that easily induces dependency, symptoms like these appear once the drug's effects wear off. Its use is strictly forbidden even in the royal capital, thou-ough."

"I always thought you were some fellow of unknown origins, but I didn't expect you'd gone and gotten your hands on illegal drugs, Barusu. Rem, move aside, I need to kill him."

"You just wrote me off instantly. Though I guess that's very 'Ram' of you."

Ram was riding along with Roswaal's speculation, taking it a step too far for fun.

Subaru gave a wry smile at that, while privately impressed that this world had drug-like substances too. It seemed the way they were shunned was the same everywhere.

Of course, Subaru had never lived the kind of underground life back in his original world where he'd dabble in such things. His life had simply been that of a lone underdog, nothing more.

So no such drug had anything to do with the change in Subaru's body.

It had nothing to do with it—but Roswaal's observation of "withdrawal symptoms" hit remarkably close to the mark.

In fact, this was, without exaggeration, the kind of symptom that could rightly be called withdrawal.

Because Subaru's body—his very soul—would not stop craving it.

"...-nnaise. Not enough."

Bathed in the gaze of everyone in the dining hall, Subaru, still slumped over the table and still savoring the fluffy feel of Puck in his right hand, whispered in a voice on the verge of vanishing.

"—Huh?"

The only one who managed to catch even a fragment of it was Emilia, who had leaned in close beside him. On the other side, Rem couldn't hear due to the angle of her neck, and pouted, clearly displeased.

Not noticing Rem's reaction, Emilia leaned her ear even closer to his mouth, trying not to miss a single one of Subaru's words as they threatened to spill away, straining to catch his faint breath.

Betraying her devotion, Subaru's eyes suddenly snapped wide open, and his body abruptly sprang upright.

Everyone around him startled, a multitude of colored eyes looking up at him. Amid them all, Subaru cried out at the top of his voice:

"—There's not enough mayonnaise!!"

At the appearance of a word none of them had ever heard, everyone tilted their heads in bewilderment.

* * *

—Natsuki Subaru was a mayoraa through and through.

A "mayoraa" is the name given to people who love the condiment mayonnaise beyond all reason—a legion of heretics who hold it righteous to top any and every food with mayonnaise.

Whether the verb "mayoru"—colloquially, "to mayo" something, meaning to douse it in mayonnaise—was truly what brought the term "mayoraa" into being is uncertain, but going by that understanding, Natsuki Subaru was most certainly a mayoraa.

His relationship with mayonnaise, in fact, traced all the way back to his nursing days.

To begin with, the Natsuki family Subaru belonged to was a clan utterly captivated, every member of it, by the charms of mayonnaise—a household for whom a dinner table without mayonnaise on it was simply unthinkable.

Naturally the fridge always kept a spare bottle of mayonnaise in reserve, and each family member owned their own personal, name-labeled tube of mayonnaise—putting their mouths directly to the tube for a "mayo-suck" was practically a nightly ritual after a bath.

Mayonnaise on fried chicken. Mayonnaise on salad, of course. Mayonnaise on dumplings, on fried food, even on dessert. Mayonnaise whenever there was trouble. Mayonnaise whenever there was a spare moment.

—Such were the living habits of Natsuki Subaru, a mayoraa through and through, one who even took pride in being one.

That life necessity—that soulmate, so to speak—mayonnaise, Subaru had gone without ingesting for roughly a week in real time since plunging into this other world—though by his own subjective sense of time, it had already been nearly a month.

His soul wearing thin, his fingertips trembling, his tongue wandering in search of fat and calories, his eyes beginning to lose focus—the conditions for a mayoraa's withdrawal symptoms had, as a matter of course, begun to eat away at his body, dragging him into a sea of agony.

"That's what it is—the white lover. Mayonnaise."

Subaru finished recounting the long, long story of how he'd first met his "lover."

He bowed once to everyone who had listened attentively, then collapsed back into his chair once more.

Overwhelmed by the sheer passion of it all, everyone present nonetheless did their best to digest what they'd just heard. Emilia, the first to return to reality, crossed her arms and said,

"So basically, you're throwing a tantrum because you don't have a seasoning you're used to eating?"

"You don't get it at all, Emilia-tan!!"

At her exasperated tone, Subaru flew into a rage and slammed the table. In the process, Puck, still clutched in his hand, let out a shriek of "Myaa—!" but Subaru, his emotions boiling over, didn't notice.

"Do you have any idea how agonizing it is for a mayoraa to go day after day without tasting mayonnaise? It's already been twenty—no, a whole week now. A normal person would've gone insane by now. It's only thanks to my rock-solid mental fortitude that I've held out this long... but I'm at my limit."

"Isn't that... isn't that a bit much? Going insane, really..."

"You think my hands and feet would be shaking like this just for show? Believe me, Emilia-tan. My body can't survive without mayonnaise."

By popular theory, the fat contained in mayonnaise triggers the release of endorphins—pleasure chemicals—in the brain, and this is sometimes said to be the very factor that produces mayoraa.

In other words, Roswaal's earlier remark, while not exactly on target, wasn't far off either. For Subaru, running out of mayonnaise was synonymous with falling into a state of addiction withdrawal.

Pressed by an intensity from Subaru she'd never seen before, even Emilia found herself at a loss for words. Subaru, who never dropped his flippant attitude in front of her, was now appealing to her this desperately, with a look of do-or-die on his face. In the face of that passion, that conviction, Emilia felt ashamed for having been about to laugh off Subaru's claim as exaggerated. She was, completely, in the palm of his hand.

"Even without mayonnaise, the food here is good. Really, it's good. This bread-like stuff, this soup-esque liquid, even this salad-ish assortment, paired with a drink that might or might not actually be water—it's all delicious, truly delicious—but this isn't mayonnaise."

He gestured with his hand at the lavish spread of dishes lined up on the table, then buried his head in his hands. Puck, still in his right hand, ended up jabbed into Subaru's short hair and complained, "Ow, it's prickly—," but Subaru's grief was too deep even to notice.

Human desire knows no limit.

What Subaru had wished for in this world was, purely and simply, peaceful days.

At Emilia's side, surrounded by the smiling faces of the people around her, if he could just live out ordinary, uneventful days—wasn't that all he needed? That had been his wish, straight from his inveterate NEET disposition, and it had served as the very vitality that carried him through even the most painful of times.

But now that he'd fought through that hardship and arrived at the days he'd longed for, all that assailed Subaru was an insatiable hunger whispering, "this isn't enough."

Hopeless. Human greed truly was, hopelessly, beyond saving.

Hadn't he meant to be satisfied with just these days? Hadn't the only thing he wished for been to obtain what he'd sought, what he'd desired, and then simply sink into idleness, content with what he'd been given?

—No. Absolutely not.

A voice rang out loud and clear, holding back Subaru as he tried to sink into that sea of languor.

Whose voice could it be but his own? Within his chest it echoed—hot, strong, fierce—a voice of craving, pushing at the back of the Subaru who had come to a halt, urging him onward with nothing but the single word: *Yes.*

Onward, onward, onward—down the road of insatiable desire.

Forward, forward, forward—if the day would come when he reached what he sought, then let him aim for the summit that surely lay still further beyond.

And so Natsuki Subaru would not stop wanting.

He had broken free of the loop of death, safely carried the twins—and, incidentally, Roswaal and the rest—through their crisis, and even managed to secure a promise of a date with Emilia.

So it was about time, then, to start dreaming the next dream.

"For a guy with no purpose, no reason to live, this is the next dream I'm going to have. ...And that dream is bringing mayonnaise to this other world. So what's wrong with that?"

A white fairy upon the dinner table—that was Subaru's next objective.

The one who listened attentively to his quiet resolve and thumped her own chest was—

"Understood. Please leave that request of yours to Rem, Subaru-kun."

—Rem, her blue hair swaying, her eyes sparkling brightly.

These days she wholeheartedly affirmed every one of Subaru's requests. There were surely various reasons for it—a sense of debt among them—but leaving Subaru to face hardship alone simply wasn't an option for her now.

"To begin with, overseeing the manor's kitchen is Rem's duty. If your palate isn't satisfied by Rem's cooking, then it's Rem's obligation to take appropriate measures. —Isn't that right?"

"You understand... no, you'll actually do it, Rem?!"

To Subaru, who looked up at her with eyes moist with emotion, Rem thumped her chest once more to show her resolve. Then she spread her hands and said,

"Rem will show you she can do it. Now then, Subaru-kun, the recipe for that seasoning..."

"—Huh?"

"—Huh?"

Face to face, Rem and Subaru both froze, unable to move.

Mouths hanging open, they simply stared at each other like a pair of fools.

Beneath Subaru's hand, which he'd braced on the table, Puck—now squashed flat—

"You're heavy—"

—was all that dropped into the quiet dining hall, a lone voice of complaint.

* * *

"To declare such a grand thing as 'I can't live without it,' and then not even know how to make it—that's a laughable story."

"You say that, but the question is whether you actually know the recipe for something you love to death yourself. Can't take back what you've already said, you know."

"Everything Rem cooks is what Ram loves. As long as Rem knows the recipe, there's no need for Ram to know it too. Not like you, Barusu, demanding some 'mayo-whatsit' that nobody's ever even heard of."

The time was after breakfast; the scene remained the dining hall, though with fewer people present now.

While clearing away the table after the meal, Subaru and Ram kept up a halting back-and-forth that fell somewhat short of a real argument.

Subaru gathered up the dishes lined on the table and handed them back to Ram, who pushed a cart behind him. With practiced motions, Ram transferred the plates onto the cart, accompanying Subaru's slow, plodding work without complaint about the pace itself.

Viewed objectively, it was a wildly inefficient arrangement, but this was part of Subaru's rehabilitation, and assisting with his work was something Ram had volunteered for herself.

As his body recovered, Subaru was slated to eventually return to his post as a servant of Roswaal Manor. Taking part in simple daily chores like this, repeating the work to check both his grasp of the tasks and his physical recovery, had been his assigned task these past few days.

Incidentally, Rem had originally volunteered enthusiastically for the role now occupied by Ram, but it had been rejected on the grounds that it would cause the manor's overall workload to grind to a significant halt.

Compared to the drawback of assigning just Ram to Subaru, the drawback of assigning Rem alone to him was on an entirely different scale. With the manor currently running on ninety percent Rem, any reduction in her available effort was practically synonymous with only ten percent of the manor's work getting done.

It was only natural her candidacy was rejected on the spot.

"If she could just get a proper handle on that about herself, it'd probably give her a lot more confidence, though."

Gathering plates and wiping down the table with a cloth, Subaru let out a light breath and thought to himself.

Most of the manor's work depended on Rem. It wasn't as though Ram was slacking off, but there was no denying a considerable gap had opened up in terms of ability.

Even so, Rem's tendency to belittle herself hadn't shown much change. Perhaps it counted as some growth that she'd at least started seeking validation for her own accomplishments externally—mostly from Subaru—rather than keeping it all bottled up inside.

"Still, having her ask 'How is it?' 'Am I doing okay?' 'Is it strange?' 'Is it good?' 'If you don't mind, please praise me' 'Praise me, praise me, praise me!' around the clock takes its toll mentally, too."

Since it had been Subaru's own words that started this, he couldn't very well take it back now, but the sheer weight Rem had decided to lean on him with was no small thing.

He wanted to believe it was just everything she'd bottled up until now exploding out all at once, and that it would settle down after this initial overflow—but if she kept up this pace, his still-recovering body wouldn't stand a chance against it.

That said, Subaru had grown fond enough of her by now that he couldn't just coldly brush her off, either.

"...That girl's stubborn habit of underrating herself runs deep. It's because Ram was a little too excellent when they were young."

"Doesn't it sting a little to describe yourself as a genius, past tense, in your own words? Just how much good air do you have to be breathing to come out with a line like that?"

"If it were the Ram of the past you were dealing with, that comment just now would have cost you everything above the neck, Barusu. ...Honestly, why must I go and pin my hopes on a man like you, of all people?"

Ram let out a weary sigh. Puzzled by her words, Subaru tilted his head, wanting to hear more. Ram gave him a sidelong glance, pointedly exasperated, and said,

"Until now, Rem's world consisted of nothing but Ram, Roswaal-sama, and this manor. Emilia-sama and Beatrice-sama were included in that world, I suppose, but I doubt they were weighted all that heavily within it. And anyone outside that, even less so."

"Ah, well, yeah, I can see that kind of tunnel-vision image fitting Rem, honestly."

The way she jumped to conclusions and acted rashly was probably a strong manifestation of that same tendency.

Being the type who withdrew inward, her conclusions tended to resolve entirely within herself. As someone who had personally been a victim of just such a conclusion, the analysis hit close to home for Subaru.

Though he couldn't help but wish that, if she understood all this, she might have handled things a bit more skillfully.

"What's that look supposed to mean?"

"Interpret my dead-eyed stare however you like, Sister dear."

"Stop looking at me with such lecherous eyes. Since we're identical in appearance, you may as well direct that at Rem instead."

"That sounds beautiful on the surface, but it's not beautiful at all, this sisterly love of yours!"

"Well, never mind that."

Deflecting Subaru's shouting without a fuss, Ram plucked the cloth from his hand as he wiped the table and smoothly usurped the wiping duty herself.

She seemed to have noticed that Subaru's arm had stopped from fatigue and that he'd been trying to use their conversation to disguise a break. Feeling a bit awkward, Subaru turned his face away from the working Ram and said,

"So, tell me the rest of what you were saying before. It's bugging me."

"Asking when you already know the content—Ram thinks that's rather mean-spirited of you."

"Wouldn't it be worse if I assumed and got it wrong instead of just asking straight out? I'm a man well known for not understanding other people's feelings, after all. Round-table-shattering level."

Though the meaning of his analogy surely hadn't come across, Ram answered Subaru's words with a sigh and let the rest of what she'd been saying reach him on a whisper.

"—Into Rem's narrow little world, a new foreign body has intruded: you, Barusu. Perhaps that foreign body will thrash about and cause enough chaos to widen that girl's narrow walls, even just a little. That's the sort of fragile hope this beautiful girl called Ram can't help but cling to."

"Your comedic style's starting to overlap with mine—watch out for that. —Also, don't pin too much hope on me widening anything or whatever. I only do what I can do, and honestly, I'm the type who doesn't even do most of what I could do in the first place."

Subaru's personal motto was: "When in doubt whether to do it or not, don't."

Because he'd held that as his creed, there was no telling how many opportunities he could have seized but let slip through his fingers instead.

Subaru wore a smug look about his own uselessness. But at that, Ram snorted "Hah!" through her nose, openly mocking, and said,

"Unable to abandon the village children, let alone leave Rem to fend for herself, and on top of it all volunteering as a decoy to let Ram and Rem escape—and now you try to play the bad boy after all that? It gives me goosebumps."

"Don't go listing off my achievements like that! Looking back on it makes my face burn with embarrassment! Ugh, seriously, what was wrong with me. I must've been completely off my rocker—that's not even my character..."

Pressing his reddening face and crouching down, Subaru shook his head in shame as he reflected on his own conduct. Even granting that it was the result of him pouring everything into breaking through his second loop, what on earth had all those actions of his, scene after scene, even been? What hot-blooded hero did he think he was—it was enough to raise goosebumps for sure.

"Not the kind of thing befitting Natsuki Subaru, the cool, sharp, 'well, whatever' type of guy. If I'd swept in and settled everything with style, sure, but all I actually did was blow black smoke out of every part of my body and then mooch off Rozzy's leftovers, didn't I?"

"Well, put in the most extreme terms, that might well be the case."

"Yeah, that's super lame after all! I went around feeling like I'd pulled it off, and it's pathetic, me!"

It was an ending arrived at through repetition upon repetition.

He would never say Subaru hadn't struggled along the way to get there. He wouldn't say that—but to eyes that hadn't witnessed the process, Subaru's part in it might well have looked like nothing more than "happening to keep getting caught up in the thick of things at just the right moments."

And on top of that, since he hadn't performed anything that could really be called a decisive blow, Subaru's role amounted to little more than "Bystander A, who just happened to be there."

Still—

"You were there in that moment. I think you, too, ought to know that just being there is sometimes enough to save someone."

The words spun gently, softly, from Ram's lips. There was none of the sarcasm or dismissiveness she usually wove into her speech—only a warmth built purely out of goodwill.

However—

"Come to think of it, I didn't do anything even when I was with Reinhard, did I?! The only thing I accomplished was standing in front of Emilia-tan at the very end—and even that, old man Rom could've done just as well as a human wall, so it didn't have to be me at all! Ugh, I'm crushed!"

Unfortunately, it never reached the ears of Subaru, who was busy reviewing the details of his past achievements and crumbling under the weight of it all.

As Subaru clutched his head and lamented as though the world were ending, Ram, brow furrowed in displeasure, unleashed a low kick that sent his knees buckling, dropping him to the dining hall floor.

Looking down at him, Ram's expression turned nearly foul enough to spit, and she said,

"It really does seem like a waste to pin any hopes on a washout like you, Barusu."

"Suddenly so harsh?! But honestly, I don't think you're wrong. Not that I'm saying don't hope for anything from me at all, though."

In response to her scornful gaze, Subaru snapped his fingers, shaped both hands into pistols, and shot at Ram. He blew away the imaginary gunsmoke drifting from his fingertips and said,

"Widening your world or whatever—you don't need to rely on some total stranger for that, you can move on your own. You're the one holed up in this cramped manor too, you know. You don't even know the names of the kids in the village right next door, and you're going on about far-sighted wisdom? Ha, don't make me laugh."

"...What did you—"

"Both you and Rem, the two of you have narrow fields of view. Both of you stand there facing each other, staring at nothing but each other's expressions, always trying to read each other. If you're going to be together anyway, stand side by side and look at all sorts of things together instead of facing off. That'd be a lot more constructive and worthwhile."

To the silenced Ram, Subaru snapped his fingers once more and flashed his teeth.

Then, adjusting the angle just so to hold his very best signature expression, he said,

"So, how about it? That line of mine just now—pretty smooth, wasn't it?"

"About as much as I'd expect from someone who was just wailing about running out of seasoning, yes."

*Harsh,* Subaru thought, answering Ram's rebuttal with a wry smile.

Then he looked around the dining hall and said,

"Well, let's take the long view and work at it gradually. Rem's bound to start seeing things clearly eventually too. There'll probably have been all kinds of comparisons and worries along the way... but someday, if she stops and thinks it through calmly, she'll realize, 'Compared to me, Sister isn't really all that impressive after all.'"

"...Yes. I do hope that day comes soon."

What had been meant half as a tease came back to him laced with something like genuine longing, and Subaru found himself faltering.

There was no bravado or falseness in her words. It was a way of speaking that conveyed she truly meant it.

"So really, what'll you do when the day comes that your little sister actually outgrows you like that?"

"Even so, the one who was born first—Ram—will still be the one who's great. —That fact alone will never change."

At a declaration delivered with such refreshing conviction, Subaru realized how graceless it would be to press the matter further.

Though he had the feeling he'd already been sticking his nose into graceless situations well before this, he set that thought aside for now.

Looking around, he saw that Ram, having taken over his task, had also finished her work.

In the end, he'd left nearly the whole final stretch to her, which called for some self-reflection. His physical recovery still wasn't complete, either—and neither was his immaturity at failing to hide it.

"Well, can't let Emilia-tan or Rem see this side of me, anyway."

He didn't want to show weakness to the former. He didn't want to make the latter feel guilty.

Both were matters of a boy's pride—things that didn't even require deliberation over whether to do or not do.

"Even so, having Rem's workload increase for the sake of some mayo-whatsit doesn't sit well with me as her sister, you know?"

"Rem's current direction is too vague. She's trying too hard in every direction at once, and she's gonna blow herself out from overwork before long. If you give her just one clear goal, at least, she'll focus on that for a while. She's got a narrow field of view, after all."

For all she looked calm and composed, Rem was someone who, once convinced of something, charged straight ahead without deviation.

Right about now, her mind was surely fixed on nothing but completing the unknown seasoning Subaru had spoken of. Her focus on everything else would drop accordingly, and the risk of her overexerting herself would, to some extent, ease off as well.

At Subaru's answer, Ram raised her eyebrows, seemingly impressed.

"I'm surprised. You'd thought that far ahead with such nonsense."

"If you're gonna praise someone, I feel like it should be sincere, not just going through the motions. And what I want from the bottom of my heart right now is the white lover—so it's a win-win for both me and Rem, right?"

"Win-win?"

At Subaru twisting his hips and arms to do a "win-win" gesture, Ram wore, unusually for her, a look of pure confusion.

Conversation with Ram, where he didn't have to particularly guard against showing guilt or weakness, wasn't tiring at all.

Enjoying both a pleasant sense of fatigue and their conversation, Subaru lifted his hand off the table he'd been leaning his weight against, and said,

"Well then, guess I'll get to the next job. Now, what's left—"

The dining hall cleanup was finished, and he figured he'd go help with Rem's work for now—it was at just that moment that the dining hall door was flung open roughly.

"Subaru-kun! Sister!"

Beyond the open door stood Rem, out of breath, her face lit up with a bright smile.

Panting, her shoulders rising and falling, Rem nodded in satisfaction upon seeing that the dining hall had been cleaned up, and said,

"The dining hall cleanup is finished too, I see. —Then, the morning's work is done."

Ordinarily there would still be nearly two hours left before the morning's work was scheduled to be finished. Without saying it outright, Rem was telling them she'd worked through all of it and skipped straight past every remaining task.

Imagining the whirlwind speed that must have taken, Subaru and Ram couldn't hide an inward shudder. To the two of them, Rem spread her arms wide, her smile unchanged, and declared,

"So then, let's get straight to 'mayonnaise'-making time! I worked hard, so we can try as many times as we want until lunch!"

The blue-haired girl clapped her hands together, happy and full of energy.

Above her beaming smile, peeking out from behind her white headdress, was a single beautifully lustrous, pure-white horn.

"You've driven her all the way to her oni form... The very means you chose to keep her from overworking is laughable, honestly."

"Nah, I'd say the way you raised her had something to do with it too."

Subaru and Ram, faces turned toward each other, pettily pointed out each other's shortcomings.

Faced with the two of them going back and forth like that, Rem, hands clasped together, tilted her head in puzzlement.

Atop her tilted head, the white horn reflected the light, shining dazzlingly.
