Arc 3 Chapter 54: "The Alliance Negotiation"

In life, you have no choice but to play the hand you're dealt.

Whether it's birth, appearance, talent, skills honed through effort, or virtue cultivated over time — every kind of meaning boils down to the same thing.

Subaru is well aware that he himself is lacking in every single one of those categories.
By some strange twist of fate, only Rem affirms Subaru unconditionally, but he knows all too well how far he is from the ideal image of Natsuki Subaru that she affirms.
Compared to the Natsuki Subaru of that ideal, the cards in Subaru's hand are fewer in number, and inferior in quality besides.

But when you're standing at the table facing a showdown, nobody wants to hear that kind of whining.
Everyone has no choice but to challenge the game with the cards they were dealt.

All that's left is when to play a card, how to play it, and how to bluff while doing it.

"—The White Whale."

The card in his hidden hand that would surely have the greatest effect. At the sound of that word, the expressions of everyone in the room shifted, each in their own way.

Crusch narrowed her eyes with interest and murmured to herself; Ferris glanced sideways at his liege with a look of concern. Russell, ever the merchant, showed his distaste for the hateful name in the furrow of his brow, and above all else—

"—!"

For an instant, everyone must have felt it on their skin — a dark, dense sword-intent that swept through the room.
Crusch, skilled in the martial arts; Rem, belonging to the race of oni; Ferris, a member of the Royal Guard — of course the women who dealt in martial matters felt it, but even Subaru and Russell, the two with little connection to swordsmanship, plainly felt the oppressive pressure, as if it were reaching directly through their skin to touch their very organs.
And the source of it was—

"My apologies. I am still quite the novice, it seems."

Wilhelm, who had until then kept his eyes closed, opened one eye and offered his apology without any change in expression. The old swordsman, whose honed sword-intent had brushed every corner of the room, touched the hilt of the sword at his waist as if embarrassed by everyone's attention, and said,

"Forgive me for interrupting the conversation in such a manner. I would not mind excusing myself from this room, if—"

"No, stay, sir knight. I want to hear your opinion."

Cutting off Wilhelm's quiet suggestion of bowing out, Crusch sent a meaningful glance toward Subaru.
She propped an elbow on the armrest, rested her cheek on her palm, and said "Well then?" by way of preface.

"The White Whale — that's rather a sudden word to bring up. The White Whale you speak of — am I correct in assuming you mean the 'Mist Witchbeast'?"

"Yeah, that should be right. The one that spreads mist and swims through the sky, a giant whale. That White Whale. I know where and when it's going to appear next. I want you to consider that information as a bargaining chip for the alliance."

How about it? Subaru tilted his head toward Crusch, leaving the judgment to her.
Crusch put a hand to her chin and took a moment to deliberate. But before she could hand down even a provisional judgment—

"May I ask something?"

It was Russell who raised his hand to request the right to question, first.
When Subaru nodded permission with his eyes, Russell said, "Then, if you'll excuse me," and held up one finger.

"First, there's something I must confirm... Subaru-dono, do you correctly grasp the value of what it would mean to be able to know of the White Whale's appearance in advance?"

"...At the very least, it'd cut down on the number of merchants and traders caught up in the White Whale's appearances. You could reconsider cargo transport plans ahead of time, and I figure you could reduce casualties too."

"That is far, far too shallow a way of thinking."

Russell's tone sharpened as he spoke over Subaru's uncertain answer.
He swung his arm up dramatically, gesturing toward the great window of the hall, indicating the outside as he spoke,

"Do you know how many lives have been lost to the White Whale, that creature born of the Witch's hand? Caravans swallowed by the White Whale's mist by sheer bad luck, never heard from again. The former Kingdom's Knights, who set out to subjugate it only to be routed halfway through. The White Whale is not merely some existence that is huge, that is merely terrifying."

There was heat in Russell's manner as he spoke of the White Whale's terror. Subaru now understood that it was an expression of a weakness turned into self-discipline, a desire to share a negative emotion.
When faced with an "enemy" too overwhelming, people try to protect their own fragile hearts by adopting an attitude that all but praises the enemy's overwhelming strength.

"Not encountering it at all is what matters most. If nothing else, for merchant houses, for whom the White Whale is a symbol of calamity, the ability to foresee its appearance would hold value beyond ten thousand gold pieces!"

Clenching his fist, Russell fervently expounded on the value of the information Subaru held.
But then—

"However,"

He suddenly let the passionate light fade from his eyes, opened his clenched fist, and waved his hand lightly.

"That value of ten thousand gold only holds meaning once the reliability of that information can be trusted. Subaru-dono, you claim to know the location and time of the White Whale's appearance. But if you have no means of proving it, this is nothing more than idle wordplay meant to raise hopes."

"More or less, what we'd like to say has been said by Russell Fellow. I too would like to hear the grounds for your claim, sir knight."

Following Russell's lead, who shrugged with a smile devoid of malice, Crusch raised the question of the truthfulness of the information Subaru had presented.

Hearing this, Subaru felt cold sweat trickle down his back.
But he managed, somehow, to keep his outward bold smile from betraying any hint of weakness. Bearing it, he played the next hidden card against the women who had taken the bait.
He played it carefully, exactly as he had prepared beforehand.

"The reason I can know when the White Whale's going to appear is because of this."

Saying so, he pulled it out from inside his coat and slammed it down on the table.
At the center of the hall, in the middle of the table everyone was gathered around, the object slid into view, and everyone's expressions stiffened slightly. — A moment later, confusion rose uniformly in their eyes.

"Natsuki Subaru."

"Yeah."

Responding to Crusch calling his name quietly, Subaru puffed out his chest without any hint of fear.
Without remarking on Subaru's brazen attitude, Crusch pointed at the thing sitting in the center of the table.

"What in the world is this?"

At last, the vividly glossy black item from another world, thrust forward.
— Pointing at the cell phone, she tilted her head at Subaru.

That Subaru could know the precise time of the White Whale's appearance was thanks to a mischievous trick of fate, built up from a stack of coincidences.
Ordinarily, the mischief of fate tended to summon fatal situations for Subaru, but this time, and only this time, it had worked in his favor.

The moment he'd encountered the White Whale in the third loop of the world, Subaru had been holding his cell phone.
Searching for a light to check a map on the driver's seat of the dragon carriage in the darkness, he'd just managed to dig it out of his luggage and turn it on.
The phone, with barely any battery left, had cut brightly through the darkness and admirably served its purpose.

Of course, immediately afterward he'd ended up encountering the White Whale, and checking the map — let alone securing the dragon carriage or any means of transport — had all become wasted effort.

"That moment, that instant."

The dragon carriage running alongside him had vanished, and Subaru had held up his phone to strain his eyes against the dark.
And then, beyond the darkness, a giant eye had appeared as if peering in at him, and their gazes had met.

Immediately after, thanks to Rem's preemptive strike, they'd been plunged straight into combat, but as he was sent flying through the air by the White Whale's roar and the blow of its tail, a series of images — the world sliced into fragments like a frame-by-frame photo sequence — had been burned into Subaru's mind.
And within that sequence of images, there had been the sight of the cell phone slipping from Subaru's hand as he tumbled through the air — and on the screen facing him, clearly displayed, had been "15:13."

Day and night had flipped the moment he was summoned to this other world, so that time, in a purely literal sense, couldn't serve as an indicator of the actual time. But if he considered it as a marker relative to some other reference point, it carried great significance. Above all—

"No wonder you don't know it. This is something excavated back where I'm from — what you'd call a Magic Vessel. This is the grounds for my claim."

—The cell phone of unknown origin, in itself, could become a useful weapon in the negotiation.

At Subaru's words, eyes widening, it was Russell, naturally, who reached for the phone first. Just before his finger touched it, as if realizing he'd gotten too eager, he asked Subaru's permission: "May I touch it?" Subaru nodded, and once he saw Russell pick it up—

"This is a remarkably strange material, in texture. It resembles metal, and yet it doesn't. The surface is smooth... and here — does this open?"

Opening Subaru's flip-style Galapagos phone, Russell let out a small gasp of surprise as light leaked out from within.
The wallpaper displayed on the screen would normally have been an anime character's picture, but Subaru had switched it to an ordinary clock face before this meeting began. Even fiddling with it randomly, all that would come up was the contacts screen, which had very few entries.

"It glows, and the picture changes... no, but, I can't make out the content. Characters I've never seen before — no, are these... pictures?"

The screen displayed the blinking animation of the second hand moving, but to people of this world, where the very concept of a clock's appearance was different, the meaning of the clock face displayed made no sense. The same went for the numbers showing the time at the bottom of the screen — at best, they'd look like geometric patterns writhing about.
It was only natural, given that Subaru himself felt the same sense of bewilderment every day.

"It's special writing. I doubt anyone can read it, right?"

"But you, sir knight, can make use of it... is that it?"

"Not that I can use all of its functions, mind you."

Choosing his words with the utmost care in response to Crusch's question.
In facing Crusch — no, in any scene where Crusch was present — there was exactly one condition Subaru absolutely had to avoid.
To avoid stepping on that landmine, Subaru's mind was working at full capacity to select his words.

"In other words, what you're saying is this — that this so-called Magic Vessel serves a role something like a 'warning stone,' alerting to the approach of the White Whale."

"Never heard of a warning stone before, but I think that's about right."

Judging from the name, it was probably some kind of magic ore that served as this world's substitute for an alarm.
He guessed at it being a tool that reacted to sound, presence, or the movement of mana, giving off light or sounding a warning of some kind.

"A Magic Vessel that alerts to the presence of the White Whale as it approaches... is that it? What's your appraisal, Russell Fellow?"

"Honestly, I'm at a loss. Magic Vessels vary greatly from one specimen to another, and it's rare for identical ones to be excavated. Things like communication mirrors, for which a method of replication has even been established, are the exception among exceptions. ...Therefore, as to the truth of this Magic Vessel's purported use, it's difficult to say."

Asked to judge a tool outside his own knowledge, Russell nevertheless avoided voicing baseless speculation. At present, Russell's position had not yet swung toward either Subaru's side or Crusch's side — he remained, strictly, in the position of a well-meaning third party.
The course of the negotiation would greatly affect his own standing as well. Naturally, his eyes were sharp as he judged, in the middle of determining which side — Subaru's or Crusch's — it would benefit him to side with.

As a result, refused an answer by Russell, Crusch furrowed her brow in difficulty.

"In that case, there's no way to confirm the truth of the matter. Then it will be difficult to simply take your claim at face value. So, what shall we do?"

"Yeah, that's a real problem, all right. If only there was some way to prove it, at least."

Spreading both hands, Subaru turned back to Crusch with a gesture as if to say he was at a loss. She closed one eye, dubious of Subaru's attitude and words, and after letting out a small "hmm" from her lips,

"Shall we actually bring it near a witchbeast and see if it sounds? Or is there someone who could prove that this Magic Vessel reacts to witchbeasts?"

"One correction, if you don't mind."

Holding up one finger, Subaru wagged it at Crusch with a bit of a retort in mind.

"The Magic Vessel doesn't react to witchbeasts themselves. If it did, it'd go off indiscriminately, what with witchbeasts living all over the place."

"—In other words, it reacts only to a witchbeast's threat, is that it?"

Crusch responded to Subaru's clarification, then went on to say, "Such a convenient story," as if about to laugh it off.
But before her reaction could fully form—

"—Ah."

Someone let out a small voice, tinged with something like realization.
Who else could it be but Rem, whose fingers still rested on Subaru's sleeve.

She put a hand to her mouth as if ashamed that her own opinion had intruded into the conversation, but before her apology could be heard,

"That was an interesting reaction, Rem. Do you have some idea of what this might mean?"

At Crusch's probing voice, Rem's eyes flicked briefly to Subaru's profile. Seeing gratitude and concern rise there, Subaru nodded to reassure her.

"If there's something on your mind, feel free to say it, all right?"

"—Yes. If Subaru-kun says so."

Given permission, Rem stepped forward and turned to face Crusch, gesturing with her hand toward the cell phone on the table.

"Since it concerns matters within the domain, I cannot go into great detail... but the other day, there was an incident involving a witchbeast. In that matter, it was Subaru-kun who acted swiftest to bring the situation under control — even our liege Roswaal-sama would attest to that."

"An incident involving a witchbeast — and you're saying he noticed the sign of it in advance, through this Magic Vessel?"

"It would have taken far too keen an intuition to notice it for no reason at all."

Timidly, Rem tilted her head slightly and glanced toward Subaru.
No doubt, privately, she had spent time wondering to herself just how Subaru had sensed the Juggernaut incident coming.
Her doubt — or rather, her pure question — was being resolved by the "Magic Vessel" Subaru had presented.

"—"

Quietly, having heard Rem's answer, Crusch's gaze pierced into her.
That sharp gaze poured its focus into Rem's eyes, giving the illusion that it was trying to see clean through to her very depths.
Only a few seconds passed by the clock, yet it was a span of time that seemed to sap one's strength under such intense pressure, and then—

"—You're not lying."

Crusch said, showing a certain degree of understanding and trust in Rem's answer.
Hearing her words, Subaru struggled not to let obvious relief show on his face, while inwardly, he couldn't help clenching his fist in a triumphant gesture.

As for the function of the Magic Vessel "cell phone," what Subaru had said was, of course, nothing but complete and utter nonsense.
It was a farce built by piling bluff upon bluff, and if the fact that it was steeped in falsehood were known, it would be rudeness in a scene he himself had called an equal negotiation — an outrage that would not be strange to be cut down for.

And yet Subaru had managed to get through this dire situation by carefully choosing his words and steering the topic.

In response to Crusch's questions, Subaru had not once told an outright lie.
The phone was not a tool that would sound off indiscriminately at the mere presence of a witchbeast, nor had Subaru, who hadn't even bothered with the mail function, mastered its features.
Wherever he couldn't get through without lying, he'd covered it with silence and by swapping the topic, and as for the most difficult hurdle he'd anticipated — a third party affirming the function of the "Magic Vessel"—

That, he had managed to accomplish by steering Crusch's interest toward Rem, who had unwittingly spoken in Subaru's favor.
Subaru had predicted that if the topic of the "Magic Vessel" reacting to a witchbeast's threat came up, Rem would connect it to the Juggernaut incident. And indeed, Rem had linked the cell phone to the Juggernaut incident, and that reaction had drawn Crusch's interest splendidly.

Rem, in fact, had only spoken what she herself believed to be true.
Which happened to be an understanding that differed from the actual truth—.

"You speak as if you could tell whether or not the other party is lying."

"If I may boast, that's exactly right. 'A discerning eye,' one might call it for appearances' sake, but in truth, it's the blessing of the Divine Protection of Wind Reading granted to me."

To Subaru's remark, meant to fish for information, Crusch responded with an answer beyond his imagining.
Subaru had assumed all along that the "ability to see through lies" Crusch had mentioned back in a previous world was simply a matter of her own keen eye for people, but—

"To read the wind is to take as one's basis for judgment things that cannot be seen with the eye. Naturally, my eyes can perceive the 'wind' that surrounds a person. Beneath one who speaks falsehood, naturally, such a wind blows. — With Rem, there was none of that whatsoever."

"O-oh, huh, is that so. Didn't know that, ha—"

"There's a wind of agitation blowing, Natsuki Subaru. Well, it would be terribly unfair for you not to know of my Divine Protection of Wind Reading in a negotiation, at any rate."

At Crusch's breezy delivery, and the sheer unkindness of it, Subaru couldn't fully hide his inward agitation, and a strained smile crept over his face.
A Divine Protection that could see through the truth of another's words whether one liked it or not — it was practically a foul move. Even a simple YES-or-NO discernment could become a wicked weapon in a scene where words were the cards being played, in a negotiation.
In any case,

"Whatever your attitude just now, there was no falsehood in what Rem said. At the very least, it serves as grounds that you, sir knight, do possess some means of sensing a witchbeast's threat in advance."

Questioning the truth of Rem's words, and placing her trust in her own Divine Protection of Wind Reading, Crusch overlooked the cunning loophole Subaru had prepared.
Subaru felt guilt at deceiving so upright an opponent, but he did not let a shred of that inner pain show on the surface. He understood that "I didn't intend to throw an outright lie at the core of it" would not even serve as an excuse.

They had each made their positions clear and resolved to face each other across the negotiating table. In that case, he had no right to feel guilty for choosing to play the cards he could play in the way that showed them off best to his opponent. Much less lacking the resolve to see a "lie" through to the end — that would be the height of rudeness.

"Will you believe in the effect of the Magic Vessel?"

"That, too, would be premature. Even granting that I can see there is no hidden connection behind the scenes, it remains a matter of one insider vouching for another. This is a decision that could affect the future of the Royal Selection — and by extension, the future of the Kingdom. I cannot act rashly."

Subaru's hope of sealing the deal here was, understandably, swept aside.
As for the White Whale's appearance information — it seemed he'd gained at least a minimal level of trust, but only enough for it to continue being used as a topic of discussion without being laughed off outright.
To raise that level of trust and draw out the answer Subaru wanted—

"—Would you mind if we let Ucchi in on this Magic Vessel deal too?"

Suddenly ringing out in the room was a voice unlike anyone else's who had been present until now.
Startled, Subaru turned around in a hurry, and there before his eyes, leaning her back against the open door of the hall, was a figure making a show of knocking on the door with the back of a raised hand.

"Funny how the very person who called me here looks the most surprised, don't you think, yourself?"

Seeing Subaru's dumbfounded face, she loosened her soft features into a mischievous smile and gently ran her fingers through her wavy, pale purple hair.
Her soft hair, reaching down to her waist, was like cotton fluff, and her mild, gentle features naturally gave others a sense of ease. And yet, deep within the eyes forming that smile, an unguarded gleam looked down upon them, and inside her head, a monster of the economic world, where numbers lined up as if competing with one another.

"—Anastasia Hoshin."

Quietly, in a low voice, Crusch murmured the name of the visitor.
At this, Anastasia, hearing her name called, smiled and said "Much obliged,"

"Geez, I got called out here in a hurry, and here you all are starting the negotiations without Ucchi, how unfair is that. Such an interesting-sounding money-making story... won't you let Ucchi in too?"

Saying so cheerfully, in contrast to the content of her words, Anastasia crossed the hall toward its center.
Holding his breath as she walked, Subaru shot a brief, uneasy glance behind her. The open door slowly swung shut, and other than her, no one else had come—

"Julius isn't coming, so don't worry, 'kay?"

"—!"

Seeing through Subaru's inward unease, Anastasia let this slip quietly as she passed by his side. Catching Subaru's startled gaze on her profile, she said "Now, now," shrugging her shoulders as if troubled,

"Right now Julius is under house arrest by order of the captain. He's right in the middle of being punished for doing something to someone else's kid without his liege's orders. Poor, troubled knight."

"House arrest..."

Told of a fact he hadn't known, Subaru couldn't hide his surprise as he parroted the word back.
That incident at the training grounds had been the collective decision of the Royal Guard, and Subaru had simply assumed his own disgraceful defeat would be quietly buried without ever coming to light.

Glancing sideways at Ferris, he saw him smile back at Subaru with a face feigning ignorance. If he'd known and simply hadn't told Subaru — that was rather harsh of him.
Or perhaps, had this been the Subaru of even half a day ago, he would have simply laughed at hearing of Julius's plight, thinking it served him right, without ever considering it a warning to himself.
That knight might have understood as much, though.

In any case,

"Called out... does that mean it was Natsuki Subaru who summoned you, sir knight?"

"That's the cute girl at his side, actually. Normally I'd have been turned away at the door, but... hearing that it concerns the Royal Selection, and on top of that, that someone's selling information about the 'White Whale' — well, there was no way Ucchi could stay away."

Anastasia, responding to Crusch's question, looked at Subaru.
Subaru gave a small nod of acknowledgment to her gaze and felt the situation shift decisively.

—With this, everyone Subaru had wanted to invite to this negotiation had gathered in one place.

Crusch, as an equal partner for the alliance. Russell, added in as a representative of the capital's merchants, so as to be able to hold his own against Crusch. And now, adding Anastasia, Crusch's rival candidate and someone who also vied with Russell for standing—

"Excuse me, but there's something I'd like to ask, Subaru-dono."

"Yeah, what's up, Russell-san?"

Raising a hand, Russell posed his question while keeping Anastasia in view.
At Subaru's nod, he touched his own beard and said, "Surely this isn't the case, but," by way of preface,

"I would like to know your true intent in summoning Anastasia-sama to this place. As a candidate for the Royal Selection, and, moreover, as the Hoshin Company has considerable influence, upstart though it may be, among the merchant houses of the royal capital gathered here — as my own standing in this meeting has now become unclear, I cannot help but ask."

"You'd get mad if I said it was to play you off each other on a scale, huh?"

Closing one eye as he answered so, the air in the room shifted all at once.
Russell's face showed displeasure at having his concern confirmed, and even Crusch's closed-eyed expression took on something severe. Only Anastasia showed no change in the atmosphere she'd carried in, and the predatory gleam in her eyes — like a carnivorous beast sizing up its prey — that she'd had from the start didn't waver in the slightest.

"In other words, are you saying this — that you'd have Anastasia Hoshin and my house compete over which would pay more for the 'White Whale' information, and choose your alliance partner based on that?"

"—"

"If so, that would be far too shallow a choice, Natsuki Subaru."

Striking Subaru's silence with her force of will, Crusch rose from her chair and stood before Anastasia. Naturally, given the difference in their heights, the standoff between the two ended up with Anastasia looking up, but—

"Ah, that's nice, Crusch-san. Someone standing at the top, afraid of being pushed down from below... seeing that expression gives me a thrill."

"That's not what I'd call a fine taste. Though I suppose, for you, who takes your own desires as the righteous path, it might well be the natural judgment. ...But my own way of being does not change."

Receiving Anastasia's provocative words and manner with a straight face, Crusch turned her gaze away from her and skewered Subaru with a sharp glare instead.

"You heard her, Natsuki Subaru. If you were hoping a bidding war would break out between my house and the Hoshin Company, let me tell you that's misguided. My house has no reason to be as attached to the White Whale's information as the merchant faction. So there's no reason for me to go along with your scheme..."

"Ah, hold on, hold on, you're both jumping to conclusions, calm down for a sec."

Cutting her off decisively, with Crusch on the verge of shutting down not just the White Whale matter but the alliance negotiation itself, Subaru held up a palm toward her, saying so while inwardly quite flustered.
Taken aback by Subaru's words, Crusch fell silent despite herself. Behind her,

"Jumping to conclusions — does that mean your intent wasn't to have the candidates drive up the price against each other?"

Russell, who had been surveying the scene with eyes gleaming at the prospect of profiting as a bystander, tilted his head. "Yeah," Subaru said, nodding in response to his question,

"I don't think I could make anyone dance in the palm of my hand that easily. I know full well how small my own hands are. ...I've got just enough to hold on to one person's hand, at most."

Idly swaying his empty hand, that hand was gently enveloped from the side by a warm palm. Rem. She wrapped Subaru's trembling fingers softly, and as Subaru turned to her, she nodded, cheeks faintly flushed.

"A-and, well, that's about it, holding one person's hand is about all I can manage..."

"Ahh, yeah yeah, thanks for the show. So, where's this conversation headed?"

"My apologies for that. Um, so, in other words..."

Flustered by Rem, whose physical affection had increased noticeably since confessing her feelings, Subaru drew courage from the warmth of her palm and resumed the topic.

"I played the White Whale card, called in two merchants representing the royal capital, and built up this whole grand situation... and on top of all that, there's something I want to propose."

Snapping his fingers to draw everyone's attention, Subaru bared his teeth in a fierce grin.
Boldly, confidently, showing not a hint of weakness or hesitation, Subaru slowly pointed the finger he'd snapped toward Crusch.

"Will you hear me out?"

"—It was I who interrupted you and stated a mistaken conclusion. Then it falls to me to give your proposal due consideration. Speak."

Standing as she was, arms crossed, Crusch faced Subaru with dignified bearing.
The wind of pressure blowing off her had only grown stronger, and Subaru couldn't help the gooseflesh rising on his skin. Anastasia, too, was looking at Subaru in the same way now, and the pressure bearing down on him was simply doubled — had it been just one of them, he'd surely have laughed it off and fled.

"—"

A sensation returned to his clasped palm, a soft squeeze.
It wasn't that his name had been called, nor that they'd exchanged some kind of signal. Simply, purely, Subaru was glad for Rem's feelings.
That alone was enough that Subaru felt he could fight even a Witch.

Closing his eyes, holding his breath once, he felt thought and oxygen circulate through his brain.
She'd take the bait — she had to. He had thought, and thought, and thought it through. He had replayed the first, second, and third worlds over and over in his mind, gathered together the information he'd obtained, and formed a picture, a prediction.
It wasn't certain. He hadn't heard it from anyone's mouth. But there was information to be gained from this very negotiation too, and that picture suggested one possibility.
Whether it was a convenient fantasy of his own making, or a miracle that would make his three deaths not meaningless — now, it was time to find out.

"Crusch-san, you—"

"—"

"The information I have would absolutely be of use to the subjugation of the 'White Whale' that you're plotting."

—Subaru's information about the future, and the objective Crusch had been harboring.

The correspondence between two people, each aiming at the "White Whale" — that, precisely, was the grounds on which Subaru had judged her — Crusch Karsten — worthy as an alliance partner.

—For an instant, the silence that fell over the hall gave each of them a moment for thought.

Crusch, Anastasia, Ferris, Wilhelm, Russell.
Each of them, upon hearing Subaru's words, closed their eyes as if weighing the content.

That silence, which by the clock could only have lasted a few seconds, bore down on Subaru's spirit with tremendous pressure.
His stomach churned, his innards screamed, and a high-pitched pain, as if his skull were being struck directly with an iron rod, made the roots of his teeth tremble.

He'd staked everything on it. He'd made his move.
From here on, unlike the flow of conversation up to this point, this wasn't a part he could clearly simulate. He couldn't predict the other party's reaction; all he could do was adapt to the flow of the moment.
First, right out of the gate — how would Crusch respond—

"Let me question one thought of yours, Natsuki Subaru."

Breaking the silence, it was Crusch, after all, who spoke the first word.
She unfolded her crossed arms and pointed just one raised finger toward Subaru.

"Where did that outlandish notion come from? Why do you judge that my house is plotting such a thing?"

Her toneless voice betrayed no agitation, no confusion — no emotion came through at all.
The weight and dignity of a ruler filled her bearing, and Subaru, naturally overawed, let his eyes wander and said, "Um," by way of preface,

"Rem."

"Yes."

"Slap my back, hard as you can."

"Yes."

Having said it, he thought, "Ah, 'hard as you can' was overdoing it," but it was too late.
A tremendous impact, a sharp, dry crack, and Subaru had the illusion that the force had blown clean through him, that all his organs had dropped right out of his stomach.

Of course, she'd surely held back to some degree in her own way, but Subaru's back would no doubt bear the vivid imprint of her small palm, a red mark etched into his skin.
Setting that aside, Subaru steeled himself against the pain and heat, and turned back to face everyone who was watching this exchange in surprise, wondering what on earth was going on, and said,

"Sorry, that was unsightly of me. Just needed a bit of a wake-up call."

"...Correcting one's own flinching before a challenge is something anyone can relate to. I won't fault you for it. Before I myself face something great, I too was once taught to write the character for 'enemy' on my palm and swallow it, believing it would let me swallow my opponent instead — a superstition I still sometimes rely on."

"Crusch-sama, Crusch-sama. That was just something Ferri made up as a joke, a good while back. You still remembered it?"

To Ferris, who stepped closer and confessed his past mischief in a hushed voice, Crusch showed a touch of surprise in her brow and said, "What... "

"Was that a lie?"

"It's just that there's nothing else quite like it — but if it helped clear away the doubts in your heart, Crusch-sama, then it wasn't a lie, was it? Ferri's happy to have been of help to you, Crusch-sama."

"I see. So it was out of consideration for me. Then I forgive you."

Watching Crusch fall so easily for Ferris's smooth talk, Subaru felt somehow encouraged, as if the difficulty of this negotiation had just dropped a notch.
In any case, watching this master-and-servant exchange with its odd little gaps out of the corner of his eye, Anastasia too said, "Hmm," touching a finger to her lips and tilting her head slightly,

"Ucchi's got a little ritual before an important deal too. Stuff gold, silver, and copper coins in a bag and jingle it by my ear — gives me courage... what, what's that face?"

"Just thinking this really doesn't feel like a conversation between people vying for the top of the nation."

Chalking it up as a minor matter before the main event, Subaru let their remarks slide.
Anastasia pouted, dissatisfied with Subaru's reaction, fidgeting with the same fur stole she'd worn at the Royal Selection venue that day.

"Well, that's enough of a breather... let's get back to what you were saying before. Ucchi wants to hear it too. Mind telling us, properly this time?"

"Yeah... sorry to make you wait on my account."

He murmured the latter half at a volume barely audible. Anastasia responded only with a small wave of her hand, not remarking on whether she'd heard it.
It was clear that this exchange with her just now had been arranged to buy Subaru time to properly gather his thoughts. As for Crusch and the others, it was more likely their reactions had been genuine, but grateful for the consideration all the same, Subaru organized his thoughts once more and opened his mouth.

"The first time I felt something was off was when I heard that iron goods — weapons and armor, in other words — were selling at high prices."

Slowly, chewing over his words carefully, Subaru gathered up the scattered pieces of information.
It was information he'd come to know across the first world, the second world, the third world.

"There was somewhere buying up huge amounts of weapons and armor that used to go for next to nothing. That it was Crusch-san's people doing it — well, you'd figure that out easily enough just wandering around Merchant Street."

Was someone preparing for war, one of the traveling merchants he'd been with had joked.
In fact, Subaru had read it as Crusch preparing for war.

The difference between his thinking and theirs was that the enemy wasn't human, but a mighty witchbeast.

"Given that it was enough to affect the market, seems like you were collecting at quite a pace. In such a hurry, and on top of that, gathering weapons in the capital instead of your own territory. Kind of natural to think something's up, don't you think?"

"To connect that to the 'White Whale' is far too great a leap. There's not even a hint of the White Whale in any of that. It's true my house is gathering weapons, iron — but you can hardly think that's for the purpose of subjugating the White Whale, can you? It might well be that I'm purely gathering military strength, planning to ignore the outcome of the Royal Selection and seize control of the nation by force."

"There's no reason for you to resort to such an outrage, and I know enough to tell you're not that kind of person."

If the person he was facing was truly great, then he liked to think he had at least enough of an eye not to misjudge them.
There probably weren't many people whose character remained as unwavering, even seen from a distance, as Crusch's. Sincere, upright — an embodiment of those very words. How could he possibly hold such a suspicion of her?

At Subaru's answer, Crusch let out a short "I see," then looked at Subaru with an interested gaze,

"Even so, I find myself a bit surprised. I had assumed all along that your going down to the lower district during the day was simply to pass idle time, a way to kill boredom — it seems I'm the one lacking an eye for people. Forgive my having underestimated you."

"Ah, uh, yeah, that's right. I mean, I wasn't just goofing off, you know."

Guilt pricked at Subaru at the sight of Crusch letting out a breath of admiration. In truth, her assessment had been quite accurate. Subaru's character, justifying itself after the fact this way, could well be sufficiently affirmed by the very contempt she'd previously held.

Shaking his head, collecting himself, Subaru continued, looking at Crusch.

"Once I figured out you were gathering weapons, the first thing I thought of, of course, was war preparations. The question was what you were planning to fight... and there, a certain merchant let something slip."

"A certain... merchant."

Picking up on the thread from talk of wandering the lower district earlier, Subaru deliberately kept vague the part where he'd gotten his hint. At that vagueness, Crusch closed one eye and, with the other open, glanced meaningfully at Russell.
Under that gaze, Russell drew back as if to say "how could you suspect me," waving his hand and shaking his head to deny the accusation. Crusch, apparently finding no lie in his reaction, only let out a breath, somewhat unconvinced.

In fact, it would have been impossible to detect any hint of a lie from Russell as things currently stood.
But the suspicion wasn't wrong. It was none other than Russell who had spoken the words that made Subaru suspect Crusch was after the "White Whale."

Subaru's first meeting with Russell in a previous world had been just as he was leaving Crusch's estate after concluding a discussion.
At that time, the talks between him and Crusch seemed to have fallen through, but Subaru hadn't forgotten what Russell had said in answer to Wilhelm's question as he took his leave.

—That if Crusch's scheme succeeded, it would be a happy thing for them, as merchants, too.

If that remark had been made in anticipation of Crusch becoming queen, it didn't sit right that the talks had apparently ended in failure. But if there was some other possibility by which it would benefit Crusch and Russell — and by extension, the capital's merchants—

"Crusch-san is running almost solo at the top of the Royal Selection, but it seems the merchants don't think too highly of her. A fruit seller I know was grumbling about it."

"I won't deny that. Pulling funds from where they exist is only sensible. I admit that commerce in my territory is subject to taxation."

Though, she added, acknowledging Subaru's point before continuing,

"I do intend to return that in the form of guaranteed public order — though I'm well aware that, from an outsider's view, that benefit doesn't come across easily."

"Yeah, actually, unlike the people in your own territory who get that benefit, the merchants in the capital have no choice but to judge your character based on surface-level information."

That she, as a ruler, managed her own territory well was surely a fact.
But anyone who wasn't in a position to actually see her skill with their own eyes had no choice but to decide their assessment of her based only on superficial information they'd heard secondhand.

Just as Emilia was shunned for the mere fact of being a half-elf.
Crusch, too, was shunned by those who saw only the negative side of her fierce way of living.

"So here's what I thought. Crusch-san, you probably don't much care for people who judge others by surface impressions alone, but there are times you'd need even those people on your side. So then, how do you turn their impression around..."

"If people are judgin' by bad surface talk... then all you gotta do is repaint that surface with good talk, that's the idea, right?"

Anastasia picked up the tail end of Subaru's sentence and stated the conclusion. Then she glanced up slightly, as if reviewing what had been said, and let her lips curve,

"Well, that's a real convenient story, isn't it. Things don't go that easy, and there's a good chance you've just got it all wrong from the start. Sounds like a pretty self-serving interpretation to me, don't it?"

"Can't deny that. That Crusch-san's gathering weapons, and trying to do something big to win the merchants over — that much I'm sure of. But whether that connects to the White Whale is wishful thinking. Might just be that, since I know the White Whale's coming soon, I'm tying it all together to fit that."

But, he continued, and looked straight at Crusch.
Crusch's expression, receiving Subaru's gaze, showed no emotion, and he couldn't see through to what lay within. And yet, no word of denial came from her. If that was so—

"Let me say it plainly, then. Regarding the alliance between Emilia and Crusch — what the Emilia Camp can offer is a share of the mining rights to the magic ore in Elior Forest, and information on the time and place of the White Whale's appearance. In short — the honor of subjugating the witchbeast that has troubled the world for so long!"

"—"

"If I'm off the mark, if this doesn't suit your intentions at all, then cut it down without mercy. And if I'm wrong, it's fine to just make this a straightforward deal for the White Whale appearance information alone."

Even with just that information, the two merchants present would surely be able to turn it to profit skillfully, preserving Crusch's honor. The capital's merchants' view of Crusch, too, had every chance of shifting favorably.
But—

"But, if your aim and my hope happen to line up—"

Spreading his hand and holding it out before him, Subaru made his request of Crusch.
For her to take that hand, to prove the value of the future Subaru had seen, and to tear down the wall between them.

"Let's subjugate the White Whale. — Let's go hunting."

That grotesque existence, that nightmarish, mighty witchbeast.
The symbol of calamity for traveling merchants, the seed of hateful memory for Subaru.
Subaru proposed to Crusch the subjugation of the Mist Witchbeast.

"Let me ask one thing."

Looking down at Subaru's outstretched hand, Crusch raised one finger toward him.
Subaru instinctively sensed that this question would be the final gate he had to clear to win her over.

Up to now, Subaru had slipped past the power of her "Divine Protection of Wind Reading." And on top of that, he had succeeded in drawing out the objective, the critical juncture of the negotiation.
All that remained was the final showdown — what would she ask? Subaru held his breath,

"Are you certain — that you know the time and place the White Whale will appear?"

—Ah, this won't do, Crusch.

Murmuring so quietly to himself, Subaru added words to the breath he'd held and let them pass through his throat,

"Yeah, it's true."

—That means you can't let me lie.

"I guarantee the time and place the White Whale will appear. I'd stake my life on it."

Literally, it was information he'd obtained by staking his own life, paying with the lives of others.
He had no doubt about its certainty, and this wasn't a moment to hold back.

"—There are some points I still question, but I suppose I should say you saw through our intentions impressively."

Letting out a small breath, Crusch answered as if resigning herself, closing her eyes.
At first, Subaru couldn't quite grasp what that answer meant. But as the words slowly sank into his mind and took shape, their outline became clear,

"Then..."

"There is doubt. There is suspicion. Many points don't sit right with me, and it's difficult to nod along immediately. But—"

Crusch closed the hand she'd been holding up with one finger raised, and extended that hand toward Subaru. His outstretched hand was grasped from above by hers,

"I will trust in the resolve you've shown in creating this situation, and in these eyes of mine."

The negotiation was settled.
Watching the two of them shake hands, one person let out an enormous sigh of relief — Russell. He let out an exaggerated breath and shook his head as if to say "what a business,"

"I was made to sweat a fair bit there, but I'm glad it's settled. Subaru-dono, as for the promise made before this meeting..."

"Yeah, sorry for making you draw the short straw so much, Russell-san. Once the White Whale subjugation's done, I'll hand over the cell phone to you, just as promised."

To Russell, who loosened his cheeks at the two shaking hands, Subaru responded with an equally sly grin. Hearing that exchange, Crusch, right in front of him, scowled openly,

"So you two really were in on this together."

"I'm the one who called him here, aren't I? After the talks between you and Russell-san broke down in the evening, I went and had a word with him, and we'd already worked out this much beforehand."

"I hope you won't think ill of us. In truth, we didn't mean to show any unnatural favoritism. This is, after all, a relationship looking ahead to after the alliance is formed."

To Subaru and Russell, speaking so nonchalantly, Crusch closed her eyes and snorted.

After finishing his exchange of information with Rem before the negotiation, Subaru had gone straight to make contact with Russell. He'd caught him just as the talks had fallen through and he was leaving Crusch's estate to head home, and had put this proposal to him.
To someone whose lies he needn't worry about being seen through, Subaru had proceeded from the start acting as though he already knew of "Crusch's plan to subjugate the White Whale," and secured the passive cooperation of one of the very few people in the capital, outside the Crusch Camp, who knew of that scheme.

The talk of using the cell phone as a witchbeast alarm had also been arranged in advance — though, of course, the fact that the phone didn't actually serve as an alarm at all was something he hadn't told even Russell.

Having received these answers, Crusch now turned her gaze slowly toward Anastasia. Anastasia met Crusch's gaze with an easy, gentle smile,

"What's the matter, Crusch-san?"

"I understand the connection between Russell Fellow and Natsuki Subaru. But that leaves your own position unclear. Why were you summoned here, sir knight?"

"Well, one reason is just to pad out the persuasiveness, I'd guess."

Looking around the room cheerfully, Anastasia touched the fur of her stole,

"Two Royal Selection candidates, and one of the capital's leading merchants. You can't gather that many important people together for an alliance negotiation and go making careless claims or statements. So, just by Ucchi being here, this fella's words carry more weight, right?"

Am I wrong? Anastasia tilted her head, having read Subaru's intentions.
Inwardly breaking into a cold sweat, Subaru had no choice but to answer with a silent, awkward smile. In truth, that had indeed been part of his aim in calling her here.
If she'd figured that, gathering Royal Selection candidates, he surely wouldn't dare spout a pack of outright lies, and had come along thinking that much, then it had gone as planned. That she'd seen through it that clearly and still chosen to participate suggested Anastasia, too, was no ordinary opponent.

"Then, what's the other reason?"

"That one's much simpler. — Ucchi—"

Putting a hand to her mouth with a sly smile, Anastasia stepped forward, practically bouncing.
Then, she gently laid both her own palms atop the hands of Subaru and Crusch, still clasped together,

"I'm giving my full support to the subjugation of the White Whale. For us merchants, the White Whale's existence is a matter of life and death, so if you can kill it, that'd be a huge help. And if, as a little extra, you'll show some favor to the Hoshin Company on supplies and the rest — nothing more I could ask for, right?"

"If I may. On that point, the royal capital's Commerce Guild ought to take priority. Anastasia-sama, if you're going to cut in, I would ask that you observe proper order."

Cutting in, it was Russell who took issue with Anastasia's merchant instincts.
As the two merchants exchanged glances, sparks all but flying between them, Crusch, having heard their remarks, looked at Subaru with a measure of suspicion in her eyes. And then,

"Wait. Listening to the two of you talk, it sounds as though there's precious little time to spare?"

"Didn't hear the important part, but the way you were leading the conversation felt like that. Actually, you're in a bit of a rush, aren't you?"

Watched by that narrowed, sidelong glance, Subaru couldn't help catching his breath.
But at this point, concealing information any further would only cause more problems. Deciding that much, he said,

"Yeah, that's right. — According to the Magic Vessel, the White Whale will appear about thirty-one hours from now. The place is... near Flugel's Tree."

"Thirty-one hours...!"

"Flugel's Tree."

Crusch gritted her teeth at how little time remained, and Anastasia tilted her head at the location.
Yes, this was a race against time. The information about the White Whale's appearance time and place held value only up until the very moment before it appeared.
If the goal was to subjugate the White Whale, then,

"We must deploy the subjugation force to the Lifaus Highway within thirty-one hours, and take down the White Whale in an instant, the moment it appears."

"For that, what we'll need is..."

Quickly grasping the situation, Crusch responded to Subaru and looked around at the personnel in the room. Then, stepping forward as if picking up where Subaru's words left off, was the elderly swordsman — Wilhelm.
Breaking his silence up to this point, he said,

"First, the formation of the subjugation force. This itself has already been proceeding smoothly for several days now. Naturally, the preparations were made to align with the timing of the White Whale's appearance. That it happened to coincide almost exactly with the start of the Royal Selection, I believe, is thanks to Crusch-sama's good fortune."

"That's fast! The timing of the White Whale's appearance, you mean..."

Is there a pattern to it? Subaru voiced his surprise.
In the worlds up to now, judging from the way Otto and others spoke, the White Whale's appearance seemed to be completely random in both place and timing, which was precisely why it was feared as an unpredictable, elusive monster.

"It's the fruit of Wil-jii's sheer obsession, nya. He's been at nothing but that for fourteen years now, doing all sorts of things about it."

Answering Subaru's question as he spoke was Ferris. He came to stand beside Wilhelm and touched the arm of the broad-shouldered old man,

"No need to worry about training and morale, since Wil-jii's here, but there's no denying preparations aren't quite complete, nya. If word got out that Crusch-sama came to the capital leading an army, all sorts of places would start making a fuss, so we had everyone gather quietly, nya."

"Indeed. As of now, I cannot say our preparations of weapons and equipment are complete..."

Wilhelm nodded at Ferris's words. But the old man turned his sharp eyes toward Subaru, then toward Anastasia and Russell,

"That, I take it, is the reason for these two's presence here. Subaru-dono."

"Well, yeah, figured something like this might come up."

Scratching his head, Subaru answered Wilhelm's words with a somewhat sheepish, modest tone.
Receiving Subaru's passive affirmation, Russell touched his own chin,

"We've already set the Guild in motion, and preparations are underway. By tomorrow past noon, we'll gather what's needed from every shop across the capital."

"Same goes for the Hoshin Company. Anything outside the Guild's territory is our turf, and we've got plenty more stock to offer besides, so expect good things."

Following Russell, Anastasia too declared her firm cooperation.
She then glanced toward Crusch, who seemed to have something to say, and stuck out her tongue,

"Not missing a chance for business is what makes a merchant a merchant. That's the reason Ucchi answered your call. Ahh, there really is nothing like the moment of selling someone something."

Not hiding a shiver, Anastasia smiled with an ecstatic expression, hand on her cheek.
With her flushed cheeks and her lovely features, it made for quite the picturesque sight — but knowing that at the root of it lay pure miserly greed, one could hardly just find it endearing.

"Goods are one thing, but when it comes to selling, gratitude's the real prize. No shape to it, no loss on it — and no price tag on it, either."

"You're on our side right now so I guess it's fine, but hearing it put that way, this merchant is seriously terrifying!"

At Subaru's cry, Anastasia said "Oh come on, come on," in high spirits. Watching this exchange, Crusch let out a breath as if the venom had been drawn from her, and said,

"Blocking the path before the negotiation, was it. I see. In this scene, it seems it was I who lacked sufficient resolve. I'm impressed, Natsuki Subaru."

"Just a case of preparation and review paying off. Honestly, I'm relieved down to my bones."

The first stage of the negotiation — that is, one of the walls he had to clear — had been breached.
Even though it was the result of preparations he'd laid the groundwork for in advance, it still felt like a success by the narrowest of margins. That the irregular had worked in Subaru's favor was, in itself, one point to reflect on.
Even so,

"Somehow, I managed to keep some face for what's left in the royal capital, huh, Rem?"

"—Yes. As expected of you, Subaru-kun, you're wonderful."

Saying so, Subaru gently squeezed back with the hand opposite the one extended to Crusch and the others — the hand that had been held all along — sharing the victory of the negotiation with Rem.
Thinking back on it, she was surely the one happiest, more than anyone, about how this negotiation had turned out.

Back when she'd first remained in the capital, unable to confide in Subaru, she must have faced the negotiation with Crusch alone. Fighting to the very limit of the authority she'd been granted, she had still been unable to maintain the alliance. If it had fallen through, the Emilia Camp's dire straits would remain unchanged, and even if there was hope for treating Subaru, there was no telling what would come after.
There was no doubt that all manner of anxieties had worn down her mind and body.

Had he managed to return to her, even a little, something equal to that anxiety, to the worry he'd caused her all this while?
If he had, then that alone was enough to make Subaru glad, for now.

And then—

"—Subaru-dono."

To Subaru, sharing this joy with Rem, a voice suddenly called out.
Looking over, he saw Wilhelm, who had walked up close, looking at Subaru with a serious gaze. Naturally feeling his spine straighten under that look, Subaru looked up at him, and the old man, after a moment's pause, said,

"My thanks."

Saying so briefly, he knelt right there and took a bow of gratitude.
At this sudden gesture, Subaru couldn't hide his surprise. But he was the only one reacting that way — everyone else showed expressions of a certain understanding toward Wilhelm's action. Even Rem, even Anastasia.

"My gratitude, equal to that which I owe my liege, Crusch-sama. My thanks, for granting this unworthy body of mine the chance for vengeance."

"Um, that, um... eh?"

"You, being the wise Subaru-dono, have surely already seen through it, but allow me to state it once more."

Ignoring Subaru's bewilderment, Wilhelm removed the sword from his waist, sheath and all.
Then he set the sword upon the floor and, placing his hand atop it in the deepest form of reverence, said,

"Trias, the name I once gave, is an old family name from the past. Having taken the previous Sword Saint, Theresia van Astrea, as my wife, and having thereby sullied the lowest seat of the Sword Saint's lineage — that is who I am, Wilhelm van Astrea."

Drawing a breath, Wilhelm let a light full of fighting spirit fill his eyes,

"My thanks for the mercy of granting this old body the chance to strike down the hateful witchbeast that took my wife from me."

Deep, deep, Wilhelm's words sank in profoundly.
Everyone in the room listened intently to those words, and all waited, simply, for Subaru's response.

Pushed on by that expectation, Subaru drew a small breath, and said,

"A-ah, yeah. O-of course I knew. Obviously, I figured that was part of why Crusch-san was jumping on the White Whale subjugation—"

"Natsuki Subaru."

Crusch cut in on his faltering, stumbling answer.
Fixing her gaze on the flustered Subaru, still in the posture of clasped hands, she said quietly,

"There's a wind of lies blowing, from you."

And so, for the first time, she saw through the lie Subaru hadn't quite managed to cover, proving the effect of her "Divine Protection of Wind Reading."
