Arc 4 Chapter 15: "Qualification and the Trial"

The next morning, while dew still clung to the leaves, Subaru walked through the grass and looked up at the entrance of the old ruins — the tomb he'd visited the day before.

Yesterday, pressed for time, he hadn't had the leisure to take in its appearance properly, but looking at it now, he could see it was surprisingly well kept. Vines had crept up the entrance and outer walls in untended tangles, but the trees and grass around the ruins had been carefully trimmed, and the tomb itself showed signs of past repairs to wall damage that would otherwise have come from years of weathering.

"I still can't quite figure out how these people feel about this tomb."

Running a hand over a patch of wall slightly discolored by sun exposure, Subaru muttered the words absently. He was turning over in his mind the conversation from the night before, held in the room where Roswaal was under house arrest, with Garfiel and Ryuzu present. They'd asked to have Emilia undergo the Trial, and as a result, to be released from this confinement here in the "Sanctuary."

"Going by that logic, the Witch ought to be nothing but a nuisance to Garfiel and the others — and yet they take such good care of her tomb..."

"Isn't it the other way around, though? The reason everyone here is bound to this land is because of this tomb. Which means they can't afford to let it fall to ruin until someone clears this 'Trial' of theirs. If tearing it down would set them free, that Garfiel fellow would surely have done it ages ago."

The one filling in the gaps in Subaru's reasoning, tilting his head as he was, was Otto — standing behind him, scrutinizing the tomb with great interest. Subaru had woken him with a surprise attack at dawn, exactly as he'd warned he would, since Otto had been sleeping in the dragon carriage as declared, and dragged him along on this forced tomb-touring expedition. Otto had grumbled about it at first, but—

"Still, getting the chance to lay eyes on something as rare as the tomb of the 'Witch of Greed'... it's almost worth having come along. While we're at it, is there anything lying around that looks 'Witch of Greed'-ish? It'd fetch a good price."

"Didn't you say that selling something as a Witch-related item would get you hounded relentlessly by the Witch Cult? If word gets out this place is the source and they burn it to the ground, I'm the one who won't be able to sleep at night, so cut it out."

Pinning down Otto, who was scanning the surroundings with a merchant's tenacity, Subaru turned back to face the tomb once more. Stillness had settled over the area; the only sounds were the chirring of insects filtering through the gaps in the trees and the wind stroking the leaves.

Clear morning air drifted through the "Sanctuary" — plenty of reward for a morning stroll, all on its own.

"That said, this isn't just about breathing some good air and calling it a day, is it? If you really just wanted to enjoy a morning walk, you'd have called out Lady Emilia instead of me."

"Actually, Emilia-tan's kind of weak in the mornings. Sleepy Emilia-tan is dazed and unsteady and adorable, but... well, let's wait till things settle down. Yesterday's talk probably left her a little on edge, so let's let her sleep a bit longer."

"So while the princess sleeps, the menfolk gather to scheme. You really are a villain, sir."

Subaru shrugged at the perceptive Otto, whose eyes narrowed with obvious relish. But his read was, in fact, entirely correct. Subaru really had come to this tomb to do something better left unseen by Emilia or anyone else.

"So, what's the plan? I'm not particularly confident in my magic either, but with a little application of Wind and Water magic I could muffle footsteps, or vanish you for a split second with a flash of light... oh, or make footsteps sound somewhere else entirely."

"Just from the applications you're listing, that magic sounds like it'd come in real handy for a sneak thief. Am I imagining things?"

"How rude. I'd never steal anything. Eavesdropping's a different matter, though."

Otto bared his white teeth and spoke with breezy shamelessness; Subaru narrowed his eyes and let out a breath. Then, in front of the still oddly high-spirited Otto, Subaru raised a finger and pointed it at the tomb.

"I'm about to go into this tomb for a bit. If things go the way I expect, the lights inside will come on and my future will be blessed. If they don't, I'll probably keel over, so I need you to pull me out cleanly."

"Is there anyone who'd hear that explanation and just nod along with a 'sure, got it'!? What do you mean, 'keel over'? Stop it, that's terrifying!"

Otto pleaded in the wake of Subaru's casual bombshell. Subaru looked at him the way one looks at an unreasonable child.

"Listen. This is the tomb of the 'Witch of Greed.' Bluntly put, anyone who enters without the Witch's permission gets their consciousness scooped clean out the moment they step inside. That happened to me yesterday, in full. Since this place carries that kind of risk, you're not to go in, got it?"

"If it didn't work for you yesterday, it's not going to work today either! Why would you go in knowing you'll collapse? This is just going to cause trouble, let's not. Actually, in that case, how exactly am I supposed to get you out?"

"Question after question after question... keep expecting someone to answer everything for you and you'll turn into a spoiled brat."

"Don't you dare use fancy words to dodge the fact that explaining it is a hassle!"

Subaru clicked his tongue, his expression souring at having his inner motives read so cleanly. Otto had apparently picked up the knack of dealing with him in short order. Subaru shook his head, resigned.

"Here. I borrowed a rope from the carriage. I'll tie this around my waist, so if I keel over inside, pull me out gently, quietly, and with love."

"No matter how gently, quietly, or lovingly, I'm pretty sure you're going to end up covered in dirt, dust, and scrapes."

"It's the circumstances, all right? Consider it a favor owed."

"How is this me owing you a favor!?"

Ignoring Otto's clamoring with a weary look, Subaru handed him the other end of the rope tied around his waist. Otto took it reluctantly, checked the knot at Subaru's waist carefully, and confirmed it would hold even under a hard pull. For all his complaining, he was a conscientious man.

"For a merchant, this gullibility and this handiwork... I do worry about this kid sometimes."

"You do realize I'm literally the one holding your lifeline right now, don't you, sir."

Otto muttered dryly, gripping the rope, at Subaru's mother-hen sort of remark. Subaru gave him a wry smile, then bent his knees lightly on the spot, and turned with a swing to face the tomb entrance head-on.

The tomb quietly exhaled its dusty air outward. As it had the day before, the morning sunlight reached only a few meters past the entrance. Beyond that, the passage was total darkness, still looking as precarious as ever — as if the whole thing might collapse the instant one stepped in.

"Well, it's not actually going to cave in, and if I'm gonna collapse it'll be from step one, so that's oddly reassuring."

"Whenever you're ready, Natsuki-san, just give me a signal when you're actually going."

"Alright, going."

Subaru never hesitated over decisions like this. Worst case, he'd pass out on entry and just get invited back to the Witch's tea party. Not that it had gone well for him last time — arm torn off, beaten and then healed, forced to drink bodily fluids — nothing about it had been pleasant, but—

"Still way better than the do-or-die situations I've dealt with before—!"

Declaring as much, Subaru gave a light hop and stepped over the tomb's threshold. Whether "threshold" was the right word was debatable, but the moment his foot crossed the line where the outside daylight met the tomb's shadow, he cautiously opened his tightly shut eyes.

Yesterday, the instant he'd taken that first step, he'd been struck by a feeling of weightlessness. But this time—

"Ground's solid. I'm not falling through."

At least there was no failure on the first step. Whether that counted as progress from yesterday was a fine line, but he felt relief at the change regardless. Taking a breath, he took the next step. As his second step carried him fully inside the tomb, Subaru's body was completely swallowed by the ruin's shadow.

From outside, Otto probably saw Subaru's figure blur, then vanish.

"Natsuki-san, are you still there? When you collapse, signal me the same way you did going in, okay?"

"That's gonna be a bit tricky. Admitting I'm collapsing is annoying, so if I'm about to go down, I'll yell that I don't want to collapse."

"That's needlessly complicated and also an unnecessary bit of vanity, cut it out!"

Exchanging their by-now customary banter, both took comfort in being able to confirm each other's position by voice. Buoyed by that reassurance, Subaru's body took a third step, then a fourth—

"—Oh."
"Ah..."

The next instant, the scene before Subaru's eyes opened up all at once. No — the shadow filling the ruin was swept away in a single moment, exposing its interior to the intruder.

On both walls of the passage, at about the height of Subaru's shoulder, flames burned at even intervals, their faint, wavering light bringing the ruin's corridor into view. The stone passage, built of the same material as the outer walls, was just wide enough for two people with outstretched arms to walk abreast — barely. The ceiling was low enough that a jump would knock your head against it. Some enormous bald old geezer somewhere would probably need to bend his back to avoid setting his scalp on fire from friction.

"...The lights came on. What does that mean, condition-wise?"

"Apparently, this is how they welcome someone who's qualified to undergo the Trial at night. ...Worst case, I figured there was a chance this was all just some daydream I hallucinated like an idiot."

Confirming his newly secured field of vision, Subaru stared intently at his raised palm. Then he touched his own forehead, recalling the sensation of the fingertip pressed there in the dream.

"So I really did get my souvenir from the tea party. Looks like the price got taken without my say-so, but for now, I'm not planning on telling anyone."

"I can see inside from here too. What do you suppose would happen if I went in along with the momentum of all this? Think I could pull off some grave-robbing on the 'Witch of Greed'?"

"The last wicked mage I know of who tried to get in here started coming apart at the seams just from trying, and he's laid up recovering as we speak. No idea what would happen to you."

"That sounds like a terrifying place!"

Listening to Otto's shudder at his back, Subaru confirmed his objective had been achieved. He could go on deeper, but Otto might well follow, so he gave up on that idea. Either way, what Subaru had wanted was confirmation of his own qualification to cut in on the Trial — and, incidentally, confirmation of how much weight to put in what Echidna had told him in the dream.

In any case, having managed to step inside the tomb like this, he could probably take it that everything Echidna had offered him — the information, the conditions, the price — was fact. And likewise the characters of the Witches he'd encountered in that daydream.

"But then, four hundred years ago, all those Witches were running around doing whatever the hell they wanted, raising hell here and there? What kind of apocalypse... I'm honestly glad I got transported to this era instead."

Thinking of an age when the Deadly Sin Witches and the Witch of Envy had run riot filled him with an appreciation for the relative calm of the present — or it would have, if the wickedness of the Sin Archbishops hadn't immediately surfaced and canceled it right back out.

There was simply no helping how six-of-one, half-a-dozen-of-the-other both the Witches and the crowd wearing the crown were.

"Anyway, my end of the prep is all done. Now it's just a matter of waiting for night and seeing how Emilia-tan's Trial goes. Maintain a high degree of flexibility, adapt as needed."

Voicing a plan that sounded good but amounted to no plan at all, Subaru turned and headed back to the tomb entrance. Returning to where an anxious-looking Otto waited, Subaru untied the rope from his waist and tossed it aside near the tomb's entrance.

"It might come in handy for something — actually, might need it again tonight out of nowhere, so let's hide it here. Sorry for dragging you into this, Otto."

"No, I'm glad you're safe... but more to the point, you're empty-handed. You went into a tomb and came out with nothing to show for it — what exactly did you go in there to do?"

"I genuinely can't tell how much of what you say I'm supposed to take seriously — is that just your comedy style? I'm irreligious but devout, I wouldn't go pillaging graves, you know?"

Having no particular god he believed in and yet paying due respect to the eight million gods anyway — that was the incomprehensible thing about the irreligious nation of Japan. You could call it the grand headquarters of religious hodgepodge. Or, alternatively, a gathering of faint-hearted worrywarts hypersensitive to the possible existence of inconvenient ghosts and curses.

"Obviously that was a joke from start to finish. More to the point, for something this simple, you didn't really need me along, did you? Why drag me into it?"

"Well, whether I'd even be able to get in was a bit of a gamble, and whether it ended with me collapsing and being a burden, or bursting apart and scarring someone for life — either way, I figured you'd be the best victim..."

"What kind of options were those!? I feel painfully aware I was chosen by process of elimination!"

"Idiot, when I thought about who I'd want to inconvenience, you were the first name that came up. Don't make me say it, it's embarrassing."

"You should be embarrassed as a human being for that kind of reasoning!"

Otto's retort echoed high and clear at Subaru's brazen way of putting it. The forlorn exchange rang out through the morning forest, off the ruins. The only ones listening to this ritual besides the two of them were the insects and animals hidden in the woods—

"Damn noisy this early in the mornin', ya know? Hollerin' at each other right on top of a Witch sleepin' like a log — that's exactly the sort of thing 'A Yohororoi hollers loudest at dawn' is talkin' about."

"What a nuisance, this Yohororoi. Next time I see him, I'll give him a piece of my mind."

With that, Subaru turned his gaze slightly from the tomb entrance to look at the one who'd cut into the conversation from the woods off to the side — Garfiel, scratching at his short golden hair and baring white fangs. He wiped roughly at his sweat-beaded brow.

"I'll have ya know, runnin' into you two was pure coincidence. I run a lap 'round the outside of the 'Sanctuary' every mornin', and you two just happened to be here. Don't get all wary on me."

"It's not exactly wary. Besides, we weren't talking about anything that'd be a problem to overhear. Right, Otto? Nothing you'd mind him hearing at all, right!"

At Garfiel's not-quite-a-warning, Subaru shrugged, then clapped Otto roughly on the shoulder. Struck and then thrown an unreasonable demand in one move, Otto made a confused "Wheh?" face before responding.

"Y-yeah, that's right, nothing shady going on here that'd be a problem at all, no sir. Just a little grave-robbing rehearsal, or, uh, a light reconnaissance, that sort of thing!"

"Wow, Otto. I've never seen a guy who's this good at digging his own grave right in front of a tomb."

Subaru jabbed at Otto, whose face suggested he had no idea what he'd just said in his momentum, while stealing a glance to gauge Garfiel's reaction. But Garfiel had completely let that little confession slide by.

"Nah, ain't gonna tattle or nothin', ain't mad either. Long as you don't go bustin' things up or doin' anything that'd hurt the 'Sanctuary,' I ain't got a reason to raise a hand against ya."

"Yeah? That guarantee's honestly appreciated. Unfortunately, I can't picture myself winning if it came to a fight with you."

"Sayin' that before we even fought is pretty pathetic... is what I'd like to say, but I'll let it go. Can't be helped, seein' as I'm your opponent. After all, I'm the strongest there is!"

Garfiel declared with eyes shining, brimming with confidence. Having actually witnessed his strength firsthand, arguing the point here would only make Subaru look foolish. Though as someone who knew a different dimension entirely called Reinhard, he did have a thing or two he wanted to say.

Swallowing what he wanted to say, Subaru lightly raised a hand in farewell.

"Emilia-tan should be waking up around now, and I wanted to be there for her morning routine, so I was just about to head back. You're mid-run too, right? Here we—"

"Headin' back to the cathedral? Then I'll walk you that far."

Subaru had been about to say his goodbyes and hurry off, but was interrupted by this unexpected offer from Garfiel. Surprised, Subaru started to decline with an "ah, no—"

"Just do as yer told for once. Actually, quit wanderin' the 'Sanctuary' by yerselves so much to begin with. Never know what might happen."

"—Huh? What's that supposed to mean? That's a weirdly ominous thing to say. It's like you're saying there's danger in here."

"That's exactly what I'm sayin', ain't it."

Not following the meaning of his words, Subaru blinked in confusion. Garfiel clicked his teeth with a "can't be helped," stepped closer to the two of them, and lowered his voice.

"Hate to air the family's dirty laundry, but the folks livin' in this 'Sanctuary' ain't exactly of one mind."

"What do you mean by that?"

"The old lady's — the village chief's — plan is to hold Roswaal and you humans hostage, get Lady Emilia to take the Trial, and break the Witch's contract that way. Most of the folks backin' the old lady are goin' along with that plan... but there's some who ain't."

At that, Garfiel dropped his voice further still.

"Far as the ones who wanna stay holed up in this 'Sanctuary' forever are concerned, you lot bein' here is a hindrance. No tellin' what they might pull to stop Lady Emilia from takin' the Trial."

"You don't mean... there's a chance they'd come after me, or Otto, or someone like that?"

"I'd figure there's a chance they'd go after Lady Emilia herself directly, too. Well, it's like they say — 'even the meek get bold in front of this hole in the ground.' Long as I'm around, though, I ain't lettin' those folks pull anything troublesome."

He said it lightly enough, but Subaru — having left Emilia behind — couldn't feel at ease at all. If anything, he despised his own stupidity for not having considered that possibility sooner.

There were divided views among the people of the "Sanctuary," Garfiel included, and among the villagers of Arlam. If that was true, why hadn't it occurred to him that there might be factions within each side as well? There was no way everyone would simply raise their hands in unanimous support of the "Sanctuary's" policy. Surely some people didn't want the situation to change. Subaru understood that feeling all too keenly.

"I need to get back right away...!"

"Ah, guess I scared you more than I meant to. No need to panic that bad — those folks ain't gonna stir up trouble in broad daylight, and besides, Lady Emilia's at the cathedral right now, ain't she? They ain't gonna try anything with other people millin' around. If they're gonna act, they'd go after folks wanderin' around in small numbers, like you two and that noisy fella there."

"Mm... yeah, that's fair. Twisting the will of the majority faction would put them in a bad spot too, so they wouldn't move rashly, huh."

The edge of his panic subsided somewhat, and Subaru let out a breath, bringing his racing pulse under control. Then, noticing something, he raised an eyebrow.

"Hey, wait — is it possible you thought it was dangerous for me and Otto to be wandering around the two of us alone, and that's why you showed up to help?"

"...Ahh?"

Subaru cocked his head and asked; Garfiel took a beat before answering. He turned his face away, keeping his expression hidden from the two of them.

"No way that's it. Coincidence. It's just a coincidence, I'm tellin' ya."

"Look at that, Otto. This is what a proper tsundere looks like. Remember his ferocious tsun from when we first met yesterday? Makes this dere all the more endearing, doesn't it?"

"Hmm, having actually been on the receiving end of a finger to the forehead, I have some trouble accepting that part outright, but I can't deny there's some part of my manly heart that's getting suckered into thinking, 'huh, this guy's not so bad after all.'"

"Wow, you really are gullible."

"You used me as material!!"

Otto's cry of injustice echoed through the morning forest, and startled birds took flight. The noisy Otto and the surprisingly likable Garfiel — dealing with the two of them, Subaru felt, for the first time in this world, like he'd gained something like a proper pack of rowdy friends he could treat as genuine equals.

"Guess I'm pretty gullible myself, huh."

He said it with a small laugh, cheeks loosening into a grin.

When night fell and Subaru stood once more before the tomb's entrance, he could feel against his skin that the wind blowing now was clearly different from that of the morning.

"Coming here at night, it really does feel like a tomb. It looks creepier than yesterday."

So said Emilia, her silver hair stirring in the wind as she gazed at the tomb's entrance. Fidgeting the end of her braid between her fingers, she glanced sidelong at Subaru.

"Do you think it's okay to go in now?"

"If it said something like 'Opens at 7 PM' next to the entrance, that'd make it easy, but there's nothing written. If it's just a vague 'Trial starts at night' kind of thing, it's dark enough around here now that we should be well within range."

"Right. Then, I guess I'll go."

She let out a small breath, and yet, for all she said, Emilia still seemed unable to quite bring herself to step forward. Waiting beside her for her courage to steel itself, Subaru tilted his head and looked back.

Besides Subaru, only four people had come to watch Emilia's challenge to the Trial: the "Sanctuary" team of Garfiel and Ryuzu, Ram standing in for Roswaal, and — for whatever reason — Otto, who'd shown his face here too. If you counted this as the "Emilia faction," then adding Emilia and Subaru made it, in this gathering, the largest faction present, but—

"On the other hand, looking at the whole remaining population of the 'Sanctuary,' we're suddenly the smallest faction. Feels like the real world, or, well, guess we're already in it."

"What are you muttering about? I'm super curious now."

"Nothing, just talking to myself. Emilia-tan, make sure you're mentally prepared for whatever's ahead. Honestly, since we couldn't find out anything about the contents of the Trial, I'm pretty anxious about it..."

"The people who've challenged the Trial before didn't know either, right? So I can't exactly go and cheat by myself. Same conditions as everyone else — and I'll still do my best."

Emilia clenched a small fist, fired up, and Subaru felt almost dazzled enough to shield his eyes. It only highlighted his own pettiness in having gone and asked the Witch to let him cheat first thing. In contrast, how splendidly aboveboard Emilia was being.

"That's so E-M-K (Emilia-tan, seriously, knightly) of you."

"Ah, I feel like I haven't heard that one from you in a while."

Emilia burst into a small laugh, her expression blooming at Subaru's habitual line. Seeing her smile, Subaru nodded to himself, thinking his own bad jokes weren't so worthless after all.

"Anyway, since we don't know what's in there, shout out the second you feel any danger. If you call my name, I'll come running instantly."

"And then you'll immediately flop over and pass out, right?"

"Nobody says 'flop over' these days..."

Emilia pouted her lips; Subaru scratched his cheek in response. Then she added, "But—"

"Thanks for worrying about me. ...Puck won't show his face at all either, and honestly, I really am super anxious. I feel like I'm leaning on you a lot right now."

"Go ahead and put your full weight on me, then. Emilia-tan's light as a feather, so I need to keep touching you constantly just to be sure you're actually real."

"That finger-wiggling is gross, so no thanks."

Emilia gave a wry smile at Subaru's fingers writhing suggestively. She then stretched lightly on the spot, her posture loosened by the release of tension.

"I feel like my shoulders got a little lighter, somehow. It's been like that since we first met — Subaru, are you maybe doing that on purpose?"

"If I had first-rate therapist skills like that, I wouldn't have ended up a lonely kid with too much time on his hands, mastering origami and making things like the 'Ryuutan-sha' all by myself."

*I flatter myself to have touched, however slightly, upon the pinnacle of the art my fingertips could produce. Naturally, I had no one but family to show it off to.*

Emilia tilted her head cutely at Subaru's excessively bleak trip down memory lane, clearly not following, but since she was adorable, Subaru crossed his arms and nodded as if that settled everything. Still looking not entirely convinced, Emilia turned her face toward the tomb.

"—Alright, here I go for real this time. Wish me a safe trip back."

"I'll pray hard enough to make even Buddha's ears bleed."

With that send-off, Emilia's back disappeared into the tomb. At once, light flickered on from the entrance — just as it had when Subaru had stepped in that morning. Emilia's footsteps echoed as she moved deeper into the tomb. The Trial apparently took place still further within the passage. That morning, even straining his eyes, Subaru hadn't been able to see past the passage's depths, but—

"You're wearing a troubled face, boy."

To Subaru, unease welling in his eyes, came a voice from the side — Ryuzu, a little girl in appearance but a full-grown adult within. She wore a smile that didn't suit her adorable looks at all, tinged with something world-weary.

"No need to worry so. It's called by the grand name of 'Trial,' but it's nothing that'll cost you your life."

"You know what's in the Trial?"

"I've taken it myself. Half-elves are judged qualified — naturally, aren't they? As a result, I couldn't clear it, mind you... but look, here I am, still hale and hearty."

Ryuzu hopped lightly on the spot to demonstrate her continued good health. In a somehow endearing gesture, she seemed to be trying to dispel Subaru's anxiety, and he was grateful for the consideration.

"So that's how you ended up with a curse that keeps your appearance a loli while only the inside rapidly ages into an old lady. I could love Emilia-tan even like that, but... I think I prefer the real her, personally."

"That's the kind of reply that makes me feel foolish for having taken you seriously. Same as Gar-bou — don't you know anything about respecting your elders, Su-bou?"

"That's not true at all? Honestly, it did put my mind at ease a bit. Thanks for looking out for me, sorry about that."

At Subaru bowing his head, Ryuzu shook her own with a lament of "why couldn't that have come out first," and dabbed at mock tears with her long, hand-swallowing sleeve in an exaggerated bit of theater.

Watching her, Subaru glanced over the others standing by: Garfiel, arms crossed, wearing a hard expression as he silently watched the state of the tomb. And, to his surprise, Otto and Ram were exchanging some kind of conversation, and it looked — to some degree — to be going amicably.

For Subaru, who felt he had precious little experience of a friendly conversation successfully happening with Ram, the sight was a matter of grave concern.

Some kind of countermeasure would be needed later — specifically, he secretly vowed to learn from Otto the art of steering a conversation without getting scolded by Ram — and then turned his attention back to the tomb.

Before he knew it, he found his own hands clasped together in front of his stomach, thumbs rubbing against each other as if in prayer.

Being the one left to wait was hard. If he was just going to stand here waiting on the results like this, it might have been far easier on his nerves to have challenged the Trial himself. Just as that thought — one that could be taken as arrogance — crossed the edge of Subaru's mind, the change came, almost simultaneously.

"————!"

Witnessing that change, everyone present gasped reflexively. The reflexive blinking was the body's automatic response to the world falling into darkness as the light source vanished, adjusting to the sudden dark. In other words—

"The lights in the tomb just went out!?"

"As long as the Trial is ongoing, the lights are supposed to stay lit..."

"So you're saying something irregular's happening?"

He looked to Ryuzu, who should have known the details, but confusion showed plainly in her childlike, sharp eyes — her demeanor made clear this situation wasn't as it should be. Garfiel had already uncrossed his arms and was rushing over; Ram, brow furrowed, and a flustered Otto looked equally unlikely to offer any reliable advice.

If that was the case, then—

"Su-bou!? You shouldn't be able to enter — you don't have the qualification—"

"I already got properly certified, qualification and all. I'll go look inside. Whatever state Emilia-tan's in, I'm dragging her back out!"

There was no time to stand around wringing his hands and waiting. The instant Subaru charged forward and stepped into the tomb's entrance, the passage lit up again, just as it had when Emilia went in.

Feeling Ryuzu and Garfiel gasp behind him, Subaru threw himself into the tomb before anyone could get out a word to stop him.

The passage inside was, as before, filled with dusty air, and every breath brought a sensation like it was slowly ruining his lungs. Kicking hard off the floor, his footsteps echoing sharply, Subaru ran deeper into the passage — toward the depths of the tomb.

"Damn it, I screwed up. Shouldn't have played it like some ace up my sleeve — should've gone in together with Emilia from the start..."

Spilling regret from his mouth, Subaru ran on. Then, abruptly, he noticed light spreading at the far end of the passage — he'd reached a small chamber.

Skidding to a stop, Subaru swept his gaze around the small room. "Room" was a generous word — it was little more than a bare, square-cut space. Nothing was placed within it; the only feature was, at the far end, a door rimmed in pale blue light.

And then — lying collapsed on the floor before that door, he saw a silver-haired girl.

"—Emilia!!"

Crying out, Subaru rushed to the fallen body. He immediately scooped up her slight frame, thinking only of getting them both out of this place at once—

*"—First, face your own past."*

The next instant, a sensation of something being whispered by his ear shook his consciousness. There was no time even to wonder what that voice had been.

His knees buckled, and without so much as a chance to brace himself, Subaru's body toppled like a puppet. Carried by his own momentum, he rolled across the floor, ending up sprawled spread-eagle — landing, by chance, right beside Emilia.

And then, beside the unconscious Emilia, Subaru too was dragged down into unconsciousness—

Waking from sleep always brought Subaru a feeling like breaking the surface of water, gasping for air. It felt like rising up out of the sea of sleep, seeking the air of reality to breathe—

"GOOOOOOD MOOOOOOOORNING, my boy!!"

"Code of Hammurabi!!"

That kind of poetic morning awakening arrived instead in a devastating burst, smashed apart by a tremendous impact. Tasting the agony of having the air crushed clean out of his body under the weight bearing down on him, Subaru, still half-asleep, threw the weight off with all his strength and coughed violently.

"Hey hey hey, what's wrong, what's wrong, what's wrong. A loving diving press to start the morning, that's the usual routine, ain't it! Careless boys catch fire, y'know — burning with negligence!"

"Guh — cough — what kind of — high expectations do you have — for someone who's asleep — and, wait, what—"

Subaru raised his head, eyes watering, wondering what had just happened. Leaning halfway off the bed, he looked up at the figure standing before him, who turned his head and said—

"What's with that face, again? Looks like you just saw a stark naked middle-aged man first thing in the morning or something!"

Striking a pose as he said it, the stark-naked-since-morning middle-aged man — Natsuki Kenichi — laughed uproariously, celebrating his son's awakening.
