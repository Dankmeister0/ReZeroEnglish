Arc 5 Chapter 34: "Swordplay and Melee"

——On the rooftop of the city hall, the black dragon spread its wings and sneered down at Subaru and the others below.

Opening its mouth to reveal sharp fangs, flicking its long red tongue, narrowing its golden eyes, its ear-grating, shrill laughter would not stop.

The black dragon's face was more or less what Subaru had imagined a dragon's face to look like. It resembled Patrasche and the other Earth Dragons in its sharp, dignified features, but its great mane and, above all, its sheer bulk were different. Where Earth Dragons on average fell within a size comparable to horses, the winged dragon overhead rivaled an elephant.

And it was hauling around that much bulk in the air. Subaru wanted to believe it couldn't possibly fly, but if that were true, he'd have to insist those magnificently spread wings were just papier-mâché props. There was no way that could be the case.

"Don't go gettin' all worked up sendin' me hot stares like that, is what I'm sayin'! What, is it mating season for you lot?! Ahh, gross, gross, bein' ogled by a bunch of things that only think about livin' to squirt their juices is way too much of a disaster for little ol' me. That's exactly why I didn't wanna come out here!"

As it beat its wings toward the ground, the gale the black dragon kicked up buffeted their bodies. The black dragon—Capella—looked them over with a long, lolling tongue, its reptilian face twisted into a shape unmistakably a grin.

A winged dragon disturbingly rich in expression.

There were things made wonderful precisely because they couldn't be understood, precisely because room was left for imagination. Not being able to exchange words with Patrasche was a prime example of that. It was precisely because of her composed bearing, showing no emotion even on her face, that he could love her without reservation.

By contrast, the winged dragon before him stirred nothing but revulsion with its human-ish quality—in the worst sense of the word.

"...A little late to ask, but can dragons actually talk?"

"Dragons that have lived long years and developed intelligence can understand human speech. Volcanica, the Divine Dragon who holds a Covenant with the kingdom, apparently communicates with us through words as a matter of course. ...That said, I've never heard of one so brazen about baring its emotions."

Julius answered Subaru's hushed voice from a short distance away. The Sword Saint, shrugging, never once took his eyes off the winged dragon. Neither did the other four—nor, of course, Subaru.

Below, two swordsmen of superhuman skill. Above, a black dragon calling itself "Lust." A situation that was already a mountain of unease had, in an instant, turned into a wall of it.

"If it were just the two swordsmen alone, that'd be one thing, but..."

A woman warily leveling a longsword, and a giant of a man testing the heft of his greatsword with practice swings. The woman's abilities were an unknown quantity, but the giant had taken Ricardo's attack straight to the body. Granted, that had only been possible because they knew he'd regenerate afterward—it wasn't that his attacks couldn't land. Ranged attacks, then—that conclusion hadn't changed since earlier. But that was only under the assumption that all six of them threw everything they had into cornering him.

"Anyone here have experience fighting a dragon...?"

"——Yes."

"Wilhelm-san, seriously?"

Subaru had figured it was a long shot, but Wilhelm answered his question in a low voice. The old swordsman gave a small nod to the surprised Subaru.

"Nearly forty years ago, I took part in the subjugation of a dragon known as the Blight Dragon Valgren, in the south of Lugunica. Near the border with Vollachia—I recall it caused considerable diplomatic tension, concentrating our forces there."

"Setting aside the diplomatic issues, could you give some advice for fighting a dragon...?"

"In the battle with Valgren, one in ten of the five hundred knights who took part died, and four in ten were incapacitated. The subjugation succeeded, but it was, in the end, a disaster. Its breath, its inexhaustible stamina, and the helplessness of swordsmen against flight—those are the things to be wary of, I should think."

"Unable to fight it with sheer numbers, and now the hopeless factors just piled up...!"

Subaru's face paled, but Wilhelm continued, "That said—"

"Valgren was a formidable foe, deserving of the name 'dragon' even among winged dragons. That one, by contrast, is too small to be called a dragon. Sever its neck, and it should die in a single stroke."

"Valgren didn't die in one stroke?"

"It had three necks to sever."

Wilhelm regrasped his sword, looking back on what must have been a desperate battle in his past. If there was only one neck to sever, that was reassuring indeed. At Wilhelm's readiness for battle, Subaru too regripped his whip, and the others braced themselves as well.

Seeing Subaru's group's spirits unbroken, the black dragon Capella spoke in a tone of surprise.

"Aahh-hah, what a stubborn bunch, ain't you? Normally, after gettin' beat up that nicely and then havin' reinforcements show up—and a Great Sin Archbishop no less!—wouldn't runnin' away be the natural instinct for pathetic little bugs like you? Am I mixin' you up with some other kinda insect, or what? Kyahahahaha!"

"Shut, up, alreadyyyy! Doesn't matter who I'm fightin'! Doesn't matter how many're standin' in my way! I'm gonna mow down every last one of you pests and stomp you flat, ya hear!"

"Kyahahaha! Somethin' about a loser dog's howl is real loud in my ears, is somethin' wrong with 'em? Oh wait, my bad, that ain't right. Way I see it, you ain't a loser dog, you're more like a loser cat! Nyaa nyaa nyaa nyaa, don't go gettin' all red in the face just 'cause your kitty-cat mate up and died!"

"Wha—?!"

Garfiel, who had shouted back at Capella's abuse, choked on his own counterattack. What the black dragon had said could only be referring to Garfiel's defeat. That it could describe in such detail what had happened to Mimi meant Capella had witnessed it. And there was more—something else that shocked Garfiel.

"You, how the hell did you know I'm a Were-tiger..."

"Ha, askin' 'where'd you hear that' is way too full of yourself, ain't it? I ain't got a speck, a shred, a hair's worth of interest in a thing about you. That you're some filthy half-beast, I can tell just by lookin', so don't insult me by askin'! And if you weren't tryin' to insult me and actually said that, then you're the idiot! Too stupid to live, drop dead!"

Cursing foully, Capella sniffed the air and fixed its gaze on each of them in turn.

"Ugh, gross, gross! Every last one of you rotten meats reeks of nothin' but garbage. Wrinkly old garbage meat! Garbage meat playin' at bein' smart! Hairy beast garbage meat! Some garbage meat that pisses me off for no reason I can name! Oh, wait, though—"

After delivering that thorough round of insults, Capella's gaze fixed on one point—Crusch. Its sharp eyes narrowed, and under that sticky stare, Crusch instinctively hugged her own body. Seeing this, Capella growled with even greater delight.

"Oh, there's a not-bad piece of meat mixed in here after all. Pretty, cute, top-grade meat, just my type! Smells nice too! The sense of depravity is just too much! That face, that body, that beauty... I wanna get my hands on you and just wreck you all to pieces."

"——That's enough."

"Ahh?"

Was it in raptures, perhaps? With an expression that could only be called ecstatic, the black dragon looked Crusch's body up and down as if licking it with its eyes.

Into that moment cut a low voice, its anger barely restrained.

"——"

The black dragon, its attention on Crusch, lifted its head irritably. And there, where its eyes widened, stood Julius, the tip of his sword swaying like a conductor's baton.

"Be scorched by six-colored light, El Prielum!"

Six semi-spirits traced a circle above Julius's head, and shining lights of six colors merged as they were loosed in a single beam. The rainbow radiance turned white at the point of impact, and Capella, taking the direct hit, screamed.

"——Kyaaaaaaa!!"

"Payment for the long speech. Even so, it was drivel deserving far more than this in return."

Following Julius's direction, the semi-spirits' destructive light continued to be unleashed without mercy. With Capella's heart-rending scream as their backdrop, the two swordsmen, who had gone silent until now, once more kicked off the stone pavement and closed in.

"Stop them!"
"I won't let you!"

Garfiel and Wilhelm cut in, shouting as they intervened. Wilhelm clashed blades with the swordswoman, and Garfiel caught the two greatswords fast between his two shields.

"——"

"I won't let you retreat—let me confirm this for myself!"

Wilhelm's blade flashed at the woman, who tried to fall back after her first strike was stopped. The old swordsman's all-out advance and the storm of slashes driven furiously up and down overwhelmed her; the length of her longsword worked against her, and her guard fell behind Wilhelm's ferocious blade speed. Even so, what made the woman terrifying was that she made her evasions in time even when her guard could not keep up—footwork and body balance so refined it would not disturb the flow of a river.

Hers was a body honed purely for swinging a blade, seemingly shaped for nothing but gripping a sword. Against Wilhelm, unleashing a swordsmanship no less than what he had shown in the battle against the White Whale, the woman kept pace through sheer, exceptional skill and talent alone.

"Nnn, ohhhh!"

"——"

With a cry, a spirited shout tearing through the air, the rotation of Wilhelm's slashes accelerated. Who could laugh at an aged body producing this? The old swordsman's chain of blows captivated the eyes of many young men who wielded swords, a peak of swordsmanship that made them think, *I want to become that.* The flashing blade cut the wind, ran through the air, scraped the earth, surging toward the woman's flesh.

The woman who met it did so in silence, receiving head-on, deflecting, parrying the old man's blade honed by years. Without a voice raised, without a cause proclaimed, the woman was like a doll made for war. Blindly, following the fighting instinct etched into her flesh, she swung her blade as though turning like a gear. Cutting the wind, running through the air, scraping the earth, she coldly batted away the blade surging toward her.

It was a clash of steel on steel that did not sound like one—a quiet resonance of blades meeting. It wasn't that the woman's sword was light. There was no way the old man's sword was light either. It was simply that each of their honed blades held no wasted destruction beyond cutting down their prey.

That was the beauty of the domain of the "blade," a realm that reaching was itself an honor for the tool called the "sword" and for those who lived as "swordsmen."

"Ohhhhhhh——!!"

"——"

Blades flashed, and the two swordsmen clashed their quiet fighting spirit against one another.

——It was a sanctuary of the sword itself, one that no intruder was permitted to enter.

Meanwhile, close by, another battlefield unfolded.

"Ohhhraaaaaa——!!"

"——"

A battle cry, muscles bulging, a stomping advance that cracked the earth, a blow that gouged the opponent's flesh. Struck by an incoming blow that made his vision spin, roaring at the feel of striking back, coughing up blood at the sensation of crushed organs, the opponent reeled from an impact powerful enough to shatter bone.

The exchange of blows between Garfiel and the giant was, in stark contrast to the swordsmen's elegant duel, savage and brutal. Broadly speaking, the giant too was undoubtedly a swordsman handling twin greatswords. Undoubtedly—and yet his manner of fighting was far from refined, no different from the reasonless violence of a barbarian or a beast.

"Ha, ruaaaa!!"

Garfiel, facing him, likewise knew nothing of a well-mannered fighting style. Garfiel's combat was streetfight-craft, a product of his own self-taught methods. Since coming under Subaru's tutelage, he had taken to calling it "Garfiel-Style Battle Shield Form," but in truth it was violence without order that only Garfiel himself understood by instinct—impossible for anyone else to imitate.

And Garfiel's brand of violence meshed remarkably well with the giant's barbarian style. A straightforward, savage duel where they simply pounded on each other until one or the other reached his limit and fell—and precisely because of that, victory or defeat would be conveyed to any onlooker in the purest possible terms.

"——"

The falling blow of the greatsword was heavy; blocking it with one arm alone would wreck the elbow. But blocking with both arms meant less attention on the other greatsword, risking an unguarded hit. So against a single swing of the greatsword, Garfiel executed a deflection with the shield on one arm. Receiving the blade at an angle, supporting the shield with sheer arm strength, he let the momentum of the blade's tip slide off and away.

Terrifyingly, this giant wasn't simply swinging his blades blindly. Barbaric as his fighting style was, his cuts were astonishingly honest—straightforward, to put it plainly. Talent alone couldn't produce this. It was skill attainable only after swinging a sword tens of thousands, hundreds of millions of times. Against a blade swung so straight, no half-hearted block was permitted. If Garfiel let his guard down even for a moment while receiving it, the silver shield would be cut clean through by a greatsword with next to no edge, and he'd take the full brunt of the blow.

"You've got, to be, kidding——!"

So Garfiel had to give his all, constantly, against the greatswords' violence. A downward swing—deflected. A horizontal sweep from the side—deflected. Immediately after, a greatsword swung up from between the legs—deflected. A blow from another arm exploiting the opening—struck. Struck back.

Troublingly, the giant had six arms besides the ones swinging the swords. These broke through Garfiel's guard at critical moments to land blows, and at times the giant would even grip a greatsword with three hands instead of two—his habits were relentlessly dirty.

In speed, Garfiel had the edge, but in the power and variety of his blows, the giant surpassed him. His jaw was knocked, he deflected a greatsword, drove a kick into a knee, uppercut a ducking face, took four backfist blows and staggered back—only to be driven into the ground as he caught a slash coming straight down from above.

Blood sprayed, bones split, and a savage battlefield ruled by groans of pain and cheers alike. A clash of titans that set any onlooker's blood boiling, impossible to watch without shouting.

The ringing clash of shield and greatsword sounded like the beating of percussion instruments, and combined with the scattering sparks, it had something of the quality of a stage drama.

"——"

The quiet battlefield of Wilhelm, and the thunderous battlefield of Garfiel. Subaru and Crusch held their breath, unable to intervene in either fight. It wasn't only that there was no room to cut in, skill-wise—they were captivated, their feet simply wouldn't move.

But unlike Subaru, swept up in that reverie—

"Ah, crap, he's about to move."

Ricardo, who had been watching Julius's magic overhead intently, shook his massive frame and stepped forward. The instant Subaru moved to say "eh" at that motion—

"Subaru-sama!"

"Get down, would ya!"

Grabbed by the collar, Subaru was pushed down by Crusch, who had been right beside him. Ricardo stepped in front of the two of them as if to shield them, tilted his head upward, and opened his mouth.

"Waaa, haaa——!!"

The roar generated a wave of sound; the trembling air resonated, and the voice itself carried an invisible force of destruction. The unleashed roar-wave was the same kind of blow Mimi had shown once before, cooperating with her brothers in the battle against the White Whale—a blast powerful enough to damage the White Whale's massive body and interrupt its attack. Terrifyingly, Ricardo seemed capable of unleashing it alone.

What clashed head-on with that roar-wave was black flame, pouring down to the ground through the white light. The pitch-black hellfire rippled ominously, its uncanny nature more chilling than its heat. The black flame, met by the roar-wave, shattered with almost anticlimactic ease, scattering its embers across the plaza. But its true horror manifested after it fell.

"What is that, that fire... it's not going out?"

Nothing sustained the black flame's burning where it had fallen on the stone pavement. And yet the flame remained there, writhing, extending tongues of fire outward. What was terrifying was the fact that even where it fell into the canal, it remained on the water's surface. Like oil dripped onto water becoming a source of ignition, the flame kept insisting on its existence right where it was.

"Boys, how long you gonna stay like that? Actually, ain't it usually the other way around?"

"Subaru. Being shielded by a lady is, I'd say, questionable form."

At Subaru's inexplicable chill from the black flame's persistence, Ricardo and Julius each tossed their comments his way. Following their gazes, Subaru realized he was lying flat on the ground, with Crusch positioned right above him, covering him.

"Ugh, I look so uncool!"

"I'm glad you're unharmed. Don't worry—I won't tell Ferris or Emilia-sama."

"That I'm relieved about that makes me look even more uncool!"

To make matters worse, he had to be helped up by Crusch's hand, adding another ten percent to the uncoolness. Dusting off his backside, Subaru looked up at the source of the black flame—naturally, the black dragon that had spat it out—and grimaced at what he saw perched there.

Nothing but disgust, plain and simple.

"Ugh, gross, gross, don't go tremblin' with sexual excitement just from lookin' at me, huh. Stop it, don't look, don't rape me with your eyes! Kyahahaha! I told you no touchin' the dancer, and you're sayin' since it's magic it don't count as touchin'? Kyahhahaha!"

"What the... is that?"

Having taken Julius's magic head-on, Capella still appeared to be alive and well. However, that was by no means to say it was unscathed. Quite the opposite—Capella's form had sustained tremendous damage from Julius's magic, cast without holding back.

The right side of the winged dragon's prized wing was burned raw and pitiful, dripping blood and flesh. It had probably tried to shield its body with the wing, but the wing couldn't possibly have protected the torso given that state. The magic's force had punched through the wing and dealt damage to the black dragon's body as well. Its abdomen had been seared clean through by the heat, its innards boiled to mush inside. The right side of the dragon's head had been blown away too, its madly cackling tongue torn off, its eyeball dangling loose.

This was well beyond "half dead." It was a corpse, plain and simple. And yet what made Subaru hold his breath, made even Julius and Ricardo furrow their brows, and drew an involuntary, distinctly feminine scream from Crusch—was not that gruesome damage.

——It was the regeneration of that flesh from such gruesome damage.

Blood vessels writhed, flesh swelled up, bones creaked, torn fibers stitched themselves back together, and Capella's destroyed body regenerated at a ferocious pace. At this deranged sight, the heat generated burned the blood, sending up red steam.

"So, did you all get your fill lookin' even at my beautiful insides? You lot must be such perverts drowned in lust that you wanna see even the asshole of the garbage meat you like so much. Kyahahaha! Satisfied? Hey, did you get satisfied and squirt your juices?"

"What—the hell—are you?"

"Ain't it just plain stupid to ask somethin' you can see with your own eyes? But since I'm so merciful, I'll answer anyway. As you can see, I'm obviously immortal, ain't I?"

Immortal—a blunt, maximally clear word of absoluteness. At Capella's self-proclamation, Subaru instinctively caught his breath. He had an urge to laugh it off as ridiculous, but was that truly because he thought it ridiculous from the bottom of his heart? Wasn't it rather that he didn't want to believe it, and so wanted to laugh it off?

"That's just... some kind of super-regeneration ability, arbitrarily calling itself—"

"Feel free to imagine whatever you like, huh? There's idiots out there who go around callin' themselves invincible too, so I ain't gonna go so far as to say I'm amazingly great, or nothin'."

"——"

"Aww, gone all quiet, how cute! Just kiddin', idiot! Drop dead! All you garbage meat, drop dead! Everyone but me, die out! To, to, to, to!"

Spitting that out with venom, Capella cut itself off abruptly mid-sentence. And then, as if in afterthought, the black dragon roughly slapped the city hall rooftop with its freshly healed wing and hauled its heavy body up. Just as they braced themselves, thinking it was finally about to attack them directly on the ground—

"Time's up, seems like. I gotta go do the broadcast, so I'll be headin' back inside. Talkin' to you lot was such a waste, seriously! An infuriatin' waste! So, you all just go get chopped up by that slightly-better meat over there and die and rot, 'kay?"

"Huh, what?"

Its tension dropping abruptly, Capella opened its huge mouth in a blatant yawn. Then—she, though whether that was even the right word was hard to say—Capella truly turned and, with heavy, lumbering footsteps, withdrew into the depths of the city hall, out of sight from Subaru's group.

It might be a tactic to lure them into carelessness by saying that, but—

"We should assume we're being lured in, but... it will broadcast regardless."

"If the demand actually gets through, the town, currently in an uneasy lull, will panic. Damn it, so we've got no choice but to go inside. In this situation! Someone go after it!"

Nothing but bad feelings. To begin with, how was Capella, with that huge body, even fitting inside the city hall? The size of the broadcast room was purely a matter of imagination, but it seemed like everything would be blown apart the moment Capella so much as shifted its weight. Or perhaps it had only had Witch Cultists set things up and was merely projecting its voice from outside—but there was no time to sit and think about that.

"Well then, that settles it. Leave the ones out here to me and those two. Julius-bou, boy, and Crusch-jouchan, you three make up the assault team."

Ricardo laid out the plan for Subaru and the others, who were still deliberating. Subaru hoped there was some basis for such an unhesitating decision.

"Ain't got a basis, really. Just, tryin' to take on those doll-like things would be rough for Crusch-jouchan and Subaru-bou. And I move badly indoors. Julius-bou can handle both situations. That's about the size of it."

"A reasonable assessment. I reach the same conclusion. To be honest, leaving Wilhelm-sama and Garfiel behind worries me, but I'll leave that to you, Ricardo."

"Leave it to me. I'll keep things runnin' smooth."

Before Subaru or Crusch could get a word in, Julius and Ricardo had already nodded to each other in agreement. Being of the same camp, perhaps something had passed between them in just that exchange. Subaru couldn't muster any counterargument to the plan, and after roughly scratching his head, he called out—

"Garfiel! Don't you dare lose! Once you and I blow away that 'Lust' bastard, we've gotta go save Emilia, you hear me?!"

"Ain't got, time to answer, right now, boss!"

Subaru called out to Garfiel, engaged in his slugfest, and nodded to Julius beside him. Next to him, Crusch put a hand to her mouth and called to Wilhelm.

"Wilhelm, I'm leaving this to you!"

"As you wish!"

To his lady's brief words, Wilhelm answered just as briefly. For a proper lord and retainer, that exchange alone was enough. With Crusch's assent given, Julius took the lead as Subaru's group made their charge toward the city hall.

The three cut across the middle of the plaza, dashing for the city hall. The two shadows, sensing this movement, moved to block their path as if forgetting the opponents they faced—

"Linin' yourselves up in a straight line—that's just plain the wrong call, ain'tcha—waaa——!!"

The roar-wave tore up the stone pavement, and the spreading wave of destructive sound assailed the woman and the giant from behind. The dispersed roar-wave had lost some power, but it was still enough to stop both their feet in place—and there, their neglected dance partners caught up to them.

"That's a cold thing to do. When I only have eyes for you!"

"Don't you go turnin' your ass on me mid-fight, I'll rip your ass hair out and crush it, orraaa!"

"——"

Slash against slash, fist against blade clashed violently, and the plaza battlefield admitted no outsiders. Listening to the sword clashes exploding behind them, Subaru's group didn't slow their pace, kicking down the front entrance of the city hall and plunging inside.

"Where's the broadcast room?!"

"I don't know, but likely somewhere upstairs. It would be more convenient for projecting the voice."

"There might be an ambush. Be careful!"

The place they slid into from the entrance was the city hall's reception lobby. A place that in peacetime would have been bustling with people and a cute receptionist would have been stationed was thoroughly ransacked, the lights out, a dim atmosphere hanging over everything. It seemed they had at least avoided the sight of corpses strewn about or numerous Witch Cultists lying in wait, but—

"Anyway, up, let's go up. There's probably a floor guide or something showing where the broadcast room is too!"

"If possible, I'd like to confirm the safety of the people who were in the city hall, but... it seems that may have been asking for a bit too much."

"What—!"

Subaru glanced into the reception area, confirmed no one was there, and pointed toward the stairs. Julius responded quietly, but he shook his head while glaring toward the back of the lobby. At his expression, Crusch's brow furrowed, and her face immediately paled with dread. Seeing Crusch's reaction, Subaru also circled around from the reception desk toward the two of them—and, noticing what they had noticed, held his breath.

With soft, padding footsteps, a single figure appeared. Peeking out from beside the staircase, a figure wore a grin like a mischievous boy.

At a glance, it looked like a small child. Partly because of the small build, and partly because the face that came into view seemed, even before "young," downright childlike.

But that impression lasted only until Subaru saw the boy's eyes.

Long, disheveled, dark brown hair, and clothing so crude it looked like nothing more than cloth wrapped around the body. The childlike features and mischievous grin were paired with eyes glinting with a rot as though every poison that existed in this world had been boiled down into them—eyes that were never, ever those of a sane human being. And in this current situation, if there were an insane human present, it was obvious what that meant.

It was—

"I'm happy, so happy, so very happy, happy indeed, happy so much that I can think it, feel it, sense it! Gluttony! Gluttony!! The longer you make something you've waited for go hungry, the more the emptier it stays, the more unbearably delicious that first bite becomes!"

Looking utterly delighted, utterly pleased at heart, the barefoot boy padded forward, step by step. From a mouth remarkably nimble in its speech peeked fangs just a little too long. That mannerism, that attitude, and those overly self-assertive lines set Subaru's brain boiling.

If this imagined suspicion, this seething emotion, was correct, then this thing was——.

"Hey, kid over there. If you just happen to be some brat who was playing hide-and-seek and got left behind, some over-the-top chuuni-brained troublemaker, confess right now. If that's it, I'll really, truly let it slide, whatever this was. But if not, then give me your name."

"Ahaha, mister, what's that? Your face looks so irritated. Could it be you've got some kind of grudge against us, us guys? If we could remember, we'd like to remember, but us, we've got bad heads, and us guys, we've got bad memory, so..."

Restraining the urge to raise his voice, Subaru tried to keep his composure at all costs. As if to grate on Subaru's nerves, the boy twisted his mouth and laughed darkly.

"Why don't you check whether that irritation's really directed at us, or whether it's actually meant for someone who isn't us at all?"

"That's enough, I get it. You're—my enemy."

"We are the Witch Cult's Great Sin Archbishop in charge of 'Gluttony,' Roy Alphard."

The instant the boy declared himself "Gluttony," Subaru's whip cracked as if launched by a spring. Cutting through the air, it struck the opposing enemy's face without mercy. That—

"Well, us eating grudges is a pretty common thing, though."

"Gluttony" caught the tip of the whip strike between its teeth, and said so with brazen nonchalance.
