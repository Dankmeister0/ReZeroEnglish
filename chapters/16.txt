Arc 1 Chapter 15: "That Man, Regarding the 'Sword Saint'"

Thinking it over, quite a lot of the contact he'd had with the fake Satella had come down to sheer coincidence.

The first and third times were the patterns where he'd run into her, but the only common thread between them was that both occurred somewhere not far from this main street (the turban-wearing shopkeeper had called it "Merchant Street," or so he'd heard).

It would've helped if he'd made a habit of checking the time, but he wasn't in the business of logging the exact moments he happened to bump into the fake Satella like some kind of stalker.

As a result, the fastest way was to try recalling the pattern from the third loop, but—

"How long was I moping around next to that vegetable stall, anyway?"

How much time had passed while he sat there dizzy and collapsed, hugging his knees?

It felt like it could've been a few minutes, or it could've been the better part of an hour spent wallowing. Whichever the answer, the honest answer here too was simply "I don't know."

"Guess I'll just wander around and search Merchant Street at random... pinning my hopes on the beautiful-girl radar."

Or maybe the silver-haired beautiful-girl sensor.

A skill with no hope of activating back in his own world, but in this one, surely it'd come in handy.

Subaru raised both hands, fingers up in front of his forehead, murmuring "myon myon" while drawing plenty of strange looks. He felt like he sensed something, vaguely, and let himself be led along for a while on the march.

Gradually, his surroundings started to feel oddly familiar, and just as he was thinking, "Huh, my sensor's sensitivity isn't half bad after all!?" — he suddenly noticed something.

—Somehow, without realizing it, he'd wandered off the main street and into a narrow alley.

"Is this... the place I first ran into the fake Satella and them...?"

It was a similar-looking alley, but he wasn't all that confident. The slum's passageways didn't differ much in atmosphere to begin with, and as for the alley of that first encounter — he hadn't managed to pass through it again since the very first loop.

The second time, it had clearly been a different alley, and now that he thought back on it, the third time too had been a different dead end. Even if the fake Satella having her badge stolen by Felt was a fixed point, the exact location of the alley Felt fled into afterward apparently depended heavily on circumstance.

The first and second loops might have matched, but in the third, Subaru's involvement had subtly shifted that particular thread of fate.

Having thought that far, Subaru abruptly realized how shallow his own reasoning was.

If he stepped into a familiar-looking alley, he might run into the fake Satella and Felt. But in a certain sense, that also meant another reunion entirely. Which was to say —

"I've had more than enough of you three, Ton, Chin, and Kan."

Turning around with a grimace, Subaru found, blocking the alley in front of him, the same three men as always.

Same looks, same clothes, same faces. Same goals too, no doubt, and same equipment — no progress whatsoever. Not that it was surprising, given they were stuck marking time in the same spot.

"And here I still can't manage to run into the fake Satella or Felt."

The reason he couldn't find the fake Satella or Felt was probably that their movements were heavily influenced by random factors beyond Subaru's control. Conversely, the reason he kept running into Ton, Chin, and Kan every single time was apparently because Ton, Chin, and Kan had marked Subaru as their target from the very start. So no matter which alley he ducked into, no matter how he changed his route, he'd end up encountering them regardless.

"Well, now that I've drawn a conclusion that thrills me not even a little, what do you three want?"

"He's been muttering to himself this whole time — what's he even saying?"

"Probably doesn't get the situation. Should we clue him in?"

The exchange between Ton and Chin traced the same template as always, dragging Subaru's mood down further. Even so, alongside the exasperation, there was a genuine part of him that knew better than to underestimate them. Encountering Ton, Chin, and Kan wasn't a terribly strict clear condition, but that didn't mean a hundred-percent breakthrough was guaranteed either. In fact, they were exactly what had killed him the third time.

He figured he could get through it either by having the fake Satella save him like the first loop, or by a surprise attack of his own like the second loop, but neither was a sure thing. The second loop had gone surprisingly smoothly, but there was no guarantee this time would go the same way. With some big event almost certainly waiting further down the line, taking an injury here was something he genuinely wanted to avoid.

"Then again, handing over the bargaining-chip luggage and running isn't really an option either."

The ideal was to get through this without any loss on his end, like the second loop. Better still if he could avoid the kind of time-consuming struggle from the first loop. Arms crossed, Subaru racked his brain trying to think of some way to dodge them.

Meanwhile, perhaps taking Subaru's attitude as being ignored, the expressions of Ton, Chin, and Kan — who ought to have held the advantage — only grew uglier by the moment. He needed to settle on a stance before they drew their weapons, at the very least.

Lamenting the fragility of Ton, Chin, and Kan's patience, Subaru was on the verge of concluding "Guess it's a surprise attack after all" — when he suddenly recalled the moment of his third death.

In that situation, on the verge of death, Subaru's hearing at least should have kept picking things up. So what exactly had Ton, Chin, and Kan been saying, right at the end? His dying brain hadn't processed it back then, but surely he could understand it now.

—Right, that's it.

"Guaaaaards!!!"

At the sudden, telegraph-free distress signal, Ton, Chin, and Kan flinched, caught completely off guard.

A volume that shattered the alley's silence and undoubtedly cut clean through the din of the main street. The idiotic nerve he'd built up doing kendo had long since stripped Subaru of any shame at raising his voice. Shouting for help cost his pride not a single scratch.

"Somebody!! Send a man over here, quick!!!"

"You... you little—! Quit screwing around! Who just starts screaming out of nowhere like that!?"

"Given the situation, you're supposed to shut up and do what we say or things get ugly for you! Nobody just does this without even hearing our demands, normally!"

"Shut it!! What's 'normal' got to do with anything!! Out of bounds, off the rails, back alleys, dead ends, dirty roads — ALL roads lead straight! I've got no time for the likes of you! I'm putting everything I've got into a golden-and-silver beautiful-girl kyaa-kyaa-ufufu situation!"

Stamping his foot on the ground, Subaru bellowed down at the enraged, brazening-it-out Ton, Chin, and Kan.

Putting on an air of defiance while hurling verbal jabs, but inside, unseen on the surface, his sense of urgency was climbing fast. This whole "call for help" gambit was a hunch built on the shaky foundation of a memory from the very last moment of his death in the third loop.

A flimsy plan riding on nothing but the fact that, right before his consciousness sank into darkness, one of Ton, Chin, and Kan had mentioned the words "guard" and "run away."

The scream had surely carried all the way to the main street, but there was no sign of any response from that direction. It hadn't been a terribly promising hunch to begin with, honestly. When he'd driven off Ton, Chin, and Kan in the second loop, he recalled the people on the main street had seemed to notice Subaru getting mugged and just... let it happen.

"Guess it really was a bust, huh..."

"You scared the hell outta us... Just a little, mind you, but you got us good for a second."
"Only a little, though!"
"Just a tiny bit, seriously!"

With well-practiced teamwork, they denied their own small-time nature in the most small-time way imaginable.

As if trying to reclaim the pace Subaru's opening move had stolen from them, the men took a collective deep breath to steady their nerves, then each began gripping their weapon of choice.

A knife, a rusted machete. And—

"Why's just one of you unarmed? Couldn't afford a weapon?"

"Shut your damn mouth! I'm stronger without one, that's why! Keep running that smart mouth of yours and I'll beat you to death for real, you piece of shit!"

"Man, I wish I had the tape of the second loop's world to show you right now."

Recalling that clean back-throw, Subaru couldn't help swelling a little with pride at his own handiwork, even if it was his own doing. On the flip side, the situation was bad enough that he found himself repeating "This is really, really not good" in his head.

With the weapons already drawn, getting through unscathed had become a lot harder. Forget escaping with zero damage — at this point, he couldn't even guarantee he'd come out with all his limbs intact.

"Come on, cut me some slack. ...I really don't want this to hurt."

Having died three times now, he knew this much: dying is not something you get used to, no matter how many times you do it. Especially since, so far, every single one of Subaru's deaths had come amid searing pain from a blade. A sharp pain is always fresh, every time, and the sensation of it grinding your nerves down to nothing right up to the end is sensational every single time.

He never wanted to die that way again, and more than that—

"Just because I've 'returned by death' up till now doesn't mean it's guaranteed to work again this time..."

How could he possibly claim there wasn't some limit on the number of times? There was no sign of any number carved somewhere on his body, but they did say even a saint's patience runs out after three strikes. If that latter option turned out to be the rule in play, then Subaru's continues were already spent. If he died a pointless dog's death here, that would be the BAD END to his life in this other world.

"...So basically, taking a wound and running for it is the right answer here."

Naturally, the knife had the highest lethality — trusted, proven. The machete looked surprisingly rusted; if he could block it with the plastic bag as a shield, he might get away with just a bruise. And the bare-handed guy — reliable as always, just ignore him entirely.

Watching Ton, Chin, and Kan close in, Subaru made up his mind on the spot.

Focusing his wariness solely on Chin, he began a mental countdown for the moment to slip past between them.

—Three, two, wait, actually hold on, three, two...


"—That's far enough."


The voice cut through the alley's crackling tension, abrupt but unmistakably clear.

There wasn't a shred of hesitation in that composed tone, nor a trace of mercy in it either. It struck the listener with nothing but sheer, overwhelming presence, conveying its intent — a gift that could only be called natural-born.

Subaru raised his head, Ton, Chin, and Kan spun around — and there, standing before them, was a young man.

The first thing that caught the eye, above all else, was hair red as a blazing flame.

Beneath it, a pair of eyes shone blue, so straightforward they defied any comparison but "valiant." Features arranged with an almost abnormal perfection only reinforced that gallant air, and just one glance was enough to announce that here stood someone of no ordinary standing.

His slim, tall frame was wrapped in well-tailored black clothing, and at his waist hung a simply adorned — yet undeniably, uncannily imposing — knight's sword.

"Whatever the circumstances may be, I won't allow any further violence against him. That's far enough."

As he spoke, the young man strode past Ton, Chin, and Kan with unhurried ease, stepping between them and Subaru. His bearing was so utterly composed that neither Subaru nor the men could get a word out.

But it seemed Ton, Chin, and Kan and Subaru had chosen silence for different reasons. Subaru had gone quiet out of shock, watching this unprecedented turn of events unfold with bated breath — but Ton, Chin, and Kan, the color draining from their faces, were silent for another reason entirely.

"C-Could it be...?"

Lips turning faintly purple, trembling, Chin pointed at the young man.

"Blazing red hair and sky-blue eyes... and a knight's sword with a dragon claw engraved on the scabbard."

Pointing at each detail as if confirming them one by one, he finally caught his breath and said—

"Reinhard... 'Sword Saint' Reinhard!?"

"It seems introductions aren't necessary. ...Though that title is still far too heavy for me."

The young man called Reinhard murmured this with a touch of self-deprecation, but his gaze never once softened. Pierced by that look, the men took a step back as if pushed, exchanging glances as though gauging the right moment to flee. But—

"If you choose to flee, I'll let it go this time. Head straight for the main street. But if you intend to resort to force, I'll meet you in kind."

Resting a hand on the hilt of the sword at his waist, he tilted his chin toward Subaru standing behind him, indicating him.

"In that case, it'll be three against two. Numerically, the advantage is yours. I can't say how much difference my meager strength will make toward saving him, but as a knight, I intend to resist."

"Y-You're joking! This ain't worth it!"

Rattled by Reinhard's calm bravado, Ton, Chin, and Kan scattered like startled spiders, fleeing toward the main street without even bothering to conceal their weapons.

Such was their panic that they couldn't even manage the parting insult they'd left the first time around. That alone spoke to just how far outside the norm the young man standing before him was.

"I'm glad we're both unharmed. Are you hurt anywhere?"

Once he'd confirmed the men were entirely gone, the young man turned back with a faint smile. In that instant, the oppressive presence that had dominated the alley vanished completely. Realizing that even that had been something the young man was doing deliberately, Subaru found himself at a loss for words. More than anything—

"Doing all that and still coming off this refreshing... that's not even 'handsome' anymore, that's a whole different tier."

Face, voice, bearing, actions — every single one, so far, had cleared the bar for "handsome guy" at a shockingly high level. If his personality and family background turned out to be just as good, the scales practically demanded he be secretly up to something evil somewhere to balance it all out.

In any case, setting aside that thoroughly jealous train of thought, the fact remained that he'd been saved. Subaru dropped to his knees right there, prostrating himself with a "heh-heh," and said,

"I offer my heartfelt thanks for saving my life on this occasion. This Natsuki Subaru is deeply moved by the purity of your noble heart, and so..."

"You don't need to be so formal about it. They also lost their numerical advantage once it became three against two. If I'd been alone, this wouldn't have gone the same way."

"Nah, judging by how scared they were, they'd probably have run even at ten-against-one, let alone three-against-one... man, this guy. Seriously handsome inside and out. This is a flag for my own route!"

Definitely the kind of scenario that'll make you cry — bring tissues.

Muttering that nonsense to himself, Subaru took the opportunity to properly look Reinhard over again. The more he looked, the more the man seemed like a work of art chosen by the gods themselves — and yet, judging by his outfit, he didn't look much like a guard. Aside from his sheer physical quality, he seemed not all that different from the many ordinary people Subaru had seen along the main street.

"Um, so... Reinhard-san, was it?"

"Just Reinhard is fine, Subaru."

"Whoa, closing the distance real casual there... uh, well, thanks again, seriously, Reinhard. You're the only one who heard my scream, out of everyone. That's genuinely kind of sad."

Surely, with that many people around, he couldn't have been the only one who'd heard. Lamenting the sheer indifference of humanity, Subaru got a faint, downcast look from Reinhard in response.

"I don't much like saying this, but there's a reason it can't be helped. For most people, crossing men like that carries too much risk. In that light, your judgment to call for a guard was the right one."

"The way you're saying that, is Reinhard actually a guard? You don't really look like one."

"I get that a lot. Well, part of it's probably that I'm not in uniform today, since I'm off duty."

Spreading both hands with a wry smile, Reinhard prompted a silent rebuttal in Subaru's mind. The biggest reason he didn't look like a guard was that entire air of his, worlds apart from that kind of grounded, down-to-earth image. If anything were to add to that impression, it'd be—

"I feel like they called you 'Sword Saint' or something..."

"My family's a bit unusual, that's all. Some days I feel like I'm going to be crushed under the weight of the expectations that come with it."

The easy way he shrugged that off suggested he had a sense of humor to go along with everything else. There was no longer any room for doubt — this man was handsome in body and spirit both. Unable to hide his awe at Reinhard's sheer existence-level handsomeness, Subaru found the young man studying him intently in turn.

"Unusual hair, clothes, and name, I thought... where are you from, Subaru? What brought you to the royal capital of Lugunica?"

"Kind of a hard question to answer, honestly. Went with 'small country in the east' before and got shot down for it, so... how about, uh, even further east?"

Even Subaru had to admit that was a pretty lazy answer as he said it. But Reinhard's reaction to it was, surprisingly, quite pronounced.

"East of Lugunica... don't tell me — beyond the Great Waterfall? Is that a joke?"

"The Great Waterfall?"

Subaru tilted his head at the unfamiliar term. Waterfall, he figured, meant some kind of falls or something, but he was pretty hazy on the local geography. He hadn't even grasped the size of the royal capital yet, honestly. As far as Subaru was concerned, his entire sphere of activity in this royal capital of Lugunica began and ended with Merchant Street, the back alleys, and the slums.

Not all that different a level from his original world, where his whole life had begun and ended with his house, his room, and the convenience store.

"Come to think of it, guess I'm holed up in the same kind of spot even in another world. That's basically a talent at this point... I've got it, don't I."

"You don't seem to be evading the question on purpose, so, fine, let's leave it there. In any case, it seems clear you're not from the capital — you must have some reason for coming here. Current Lugunica is in a more complicated state than usual. If I can be of help, I'd be glad to."

"Nah, nah, you're off duty, right? No need to give that up just to help me out. What you already did was plenty... though, there is one thing I wanted to ask, while we're at it."

Shaking his head at Reinhard's offer, Subaru abruptly raised a finger as if just remembering something. Reinhard nodded easily, saying "Ask away."

"Though I'm not exactly well-informed on current affairs, so I can't promise an answer."

"Nah, it's about finding someone, so it's fine, it's fine. Anyway, here's what I wanted to ask — you haven't happened to see a silver-haired girl in a white robe around here, have you?"

Based on his observations of the main street so far, the fake Satella's outfit was distinctive enough to stand out. Her hair color was as rare as Subaru's black hair, and so was that robe embroidered with what looked like a hawk. Now that he thought about it, that white robe had looked fairly expensive too. Given she'd been carrying a jeweled badge, it wasn't hard to guess her standing was naturally quite high as well.

"A white robe, silver hair..."

"Oh, and, on top of that, an insanely beautiful girl. And a cat — well, it's not like I'm just showing off that detail or anything. That's about all the info I've got — ring any bells?"

If a cat-shaped Spirit accompanying her matched too, there'd be no doubting it was the fake Satella, but normally that sort of detail would be buried under all the fuss over silver hair, so that was probably asking too much. Having added the "beautiful girl" detail, it occurred to Subaru that if the drop-dead-gorgeous Reinhard and her stood side by side, it really would make quite the picture. He found himself getting weirdly jealous over a scenario he'd just invented in his own head.

"...What do you plan to do, once you find her?"

"Return a lost item — or, in this case, I guess it's more like a thing I'm looking for on her behalf. Just want to get it back to her, that's all."

Though, in truth, that item wasn't even in Subaru's hands yet — and it was entirely possible it hadn't even been lost yet, either. At Subaru's answer, Reinhard narrowed his sky-blue eyes, fell into thought for a moment, and then said,

"No, sorry. Doesn't ring a bell, offhand. If you'd like, I could help you look."

"Nah, can't put you out that much. It's fine, I'll manage to find her one way or another."

Waving off Reinhard's offer to help, Subaru decided to start by circling back through the main street. If things went well, maybe he'd spot her along the way, like in the third loop.

Or better yet, he could catch Felt right at the moment of the theft and stop her from taking the badge in the first place.

Thinking it through, that actually seemed like the quicker route in the long run, which only made him more anxious to hurry.

"Problem is figuring out how to catch someone as slippery as Felt... something like, block the path with side-to-side shuffle steps, zone defense."

"That's an unusual motion. What does it mean?"

"It's a defensive move — pressure the opponent while you go for the ball. Believe it or not, I've got a solid reputation on defense. Also good at looking like I'm participating in ball movement from a position that has zero actual effect on it."

Even in dodgeball, Subaru was the type to plant himself right at the boundary line, participating in some ambiguous capacity that was neither quite infield nor outfield. It wasn't unheard of for the game to end without the ball ever touching him.

"Anyway, no matter what, Merchant Street first."

"You're heading off?"

"Yeah, heading off. Thanks for everything, Reinhard. I'll pay you back for this sometime. ...Guess I could find you at the guard post or something?"

"That's right, I think giving my name there should do it. Or, on days off like today, I tend to wander around the royal capital."

"Not really into the hobby of wandering a town hunting down some guy... this isn't an otome game."

Answering with a light joke, Subaru snapped up a hand in a quick salute, and Reinhard saw him off to the very end with a cheerful "Take care."

As if pushed forward by those words, Subaru made his escape from the alley without a scratch to show for it.

—Never noticing that behind him, a pair of blue eyes watched his back, as if weighing his worth.
