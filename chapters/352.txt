Arc 5 Chapter 36: "The Starting and Ending Point of Love"

Behind him was a room packed with giant flies. To the side, the wall had been smashed clean through, and outside, the black dragon lay fallen, unable to move.

And before him, a girl stood laughing scornfully, one foot planted on Crusch. Her sinister smile, her mocking words, and above all—if her self-introduction was to be believed—the one standing there was none other than the Witch Cult's Sin Archbishop of Lust, Capella Emerada Lugunica.

—His mind felt like it was about to break.

"Wha—what the hell is this...!"

"Ain't thinkin' about it just a waste of time? You trash meat oughta stop runnin' that extra brain of yours and just accept the reality right in front of you—that's the best way! A pretty little girl tremblin' in fear, cowerin' small—turns out she was a Witch Cult Sin Archbishop all along!"

In front of Subaru, who clawed at his own head, Capella danced, sticking out her tongue and cackling crudely. Beneath her foot, Crusch lay with her eyes rolled back, blood spilling from her mouth at an alarming rate.

There were no obvious external wounds. But the damage was clearly severe enough to be life-threatening. With Garfiel—who could use healing magic—too far away, this could easily turn into the worst-case scenario.

"In the first place, don't you people think somethin's off? This here's city hall, the very center of the city's functions. Don't it occur to you to wonder why some runt meat would be wanderin' around a place like that? Livin' your life without a shred of doubt, just goin' 'oh, a girl in trouble, gotta save her'—that kinda brain-dead mindset is the real mystery to me!"

"Sh-shut up. I've got plenty to say and plenty to ask, but first—get your foot off her."

"Huh? Ain't you just thrilled to see my lovely leg, drippin' with excitement? Or maybe you're all worked up over the female meat desperately lickin' the sole of my foot? She's got a body built for sin, after all. Can't hold it in anymore, little boy? Kyahaha!"

"—! That person! I'm telling you she's not someone you get to keep under your foot!"

Capella ground her heel down, savoring the way Crusch's ribs creaked beneath it. At the outrage and mocking words, Subaru spat out his fury and kicked off the ground.

He lowered his stance and lunged forward. Capella, having provoked him into it, clapped her hands in welcome as though this was exactly what she wanted—but Subaru wasn't foolish enough to charge in blindly.

Even having lost her memories, Crusch was a warrior—one skilled enough that Wilhelm had permitted her to take part in this battle. And in the mere ten-odd seconds Subaru had looked away, she'd been defeated without so much as landing a blow. There was no doubt that Lust's strength far surpassed Subaru's, standing at a height he couldn't hope to reach.

So what Subaru needed to prioritize here wasn't defeating the enemy, but breaking through the situation.

"—"

Recover Crusch, whose life was in danger, and hurry to rejoin Julius and the others. Abandoning this place and withdrawing was the top priority. He wanted to stop the broadcast, but it was clear this wasn't a situation worth throwing his life away over. And the people he was supposed to rescue, who should have been somewhere in city hall—at least on this floor, there was no sign of them. The conclusion was that, at present, they didn't have the strength to retake city hall. So Subaru didn't hesitate.

"Ohh-ho?"

"Tch!"

Capella let out a mock-surprised sound, her eyes widening at Subaru's action. Cracking his whip, Subaru struck not at Capella but at a shelf built into the wall as he ran past. Inside it was a metal humanoid figurine—what it was modeled after, he had no idea—but he snagged the two-handed-sized object with the tip of his whip and, with a deft twist of his wrist, hurled it at Capella.

It was a metal weapon spinning rapidly end over end. With enough force to easily crack a wall, it packed far more power than a single lash of the whip. Whether she blocked it or dodged it, she'd have to move off Crusch to do so. Through that opening, he would take her back—

"Hit her!"

"Sure, go ahead~"

"Wha—!?"

To Subaru's shout of effort, Capella answered with a voice full of composure. An instant later came the sound of something hard piercing flesh and bone, and blood scattered from Capella's struck head. The girl's forehead, hit square-on and undefended, split open; hair and skin tore away together, leaving a gruesome wound, and thick, sticky blood could be seen trickling down her cheek.

Half of the pretty girl's face was crushed and caved in, a pitiful ruin. Her left eye was half-crushed, and as Subaru watched that light-dead eye roll to stare straight at him, the unexpected sight momentarily wiped his mind blank. It was a foolish instant—an action meant to create an opening in the enemy instead created shaken hesitation in himself. And there was no way the Sin Archbishop would let that instant slip by.

"Why is it you people are always so lovably right in the palm of my hand? That hopeless kinda stupidity, I just luuuv it. Kyahahaha!"

Into the gap where his thoughts had frozen, Capella's mocking laughter slipped in. Before the eyes of the rigid Subaru, the girl spun her body, and in the next instant a black whirlwind struck him full from the side and sent him flying.

"Guohh!"

As if struck by a giant's open palm, the entire right side of his body was hammered at once, and Subaru bounced off the floor, tumbling and knocking over desks in the room. Battered all over, head spinning, Subaru leaned against a wall and barely managed to prop himself up, and he looked.

"What's the matter? Struck speechless by my beauty, are we?"

"...What... is that."

"Hmm-hmm? Oh, this? Now then, what do you suppose it looks like~?"

Before Subaru, who forgot his pain and couldn't find his voice, Capella danced merrily, swaying her hips. And from beneath the hem of the girl's short skirt, swaying with the motion, something that shouldn't be there extended out—a thick, black dragon's tail.

At the same time, it hammered home the realization that it was that very tail that had struck him down a moment ago, and the twisted, grotesque sight of a mighty dragon's tail grafted onto a small girl's body. What was that. What in the world was that. What was he even looking at.

"Don't tell me—a dragon that can transform into a human...?"

"Yep, and there we have it—a brain-dead, imagination-starved, unbearable theory straight from the horse's mouth! Trash meat—even after kind ol' me scattered so many hints around, you still don't get it, huh."

"—!"

At Subaru muttering his guess about Capella's true nature, the girl swung her tail as though her mood had soured. The long tail came crashing down from directly overhead, striking the floor and sending a crack racing across it—Subaru barely dodged the attack with a sideways leap and watched it happen. But—

"If you go and relax there, that just ruins everything~"

"Ugoh! Wha—!?"

Where he rolled after hitting the floor, Subaru was struck by a giant left arm. Knocked away, the waiting tail flung his body upward; slamming into the ceiling and falling, Subaru was sliced by a bird's wing like a blade and smashed down onto the ground.

Groaning at the searing pain of a wound slashed across his back, Subaru rolled across the floor, coughing from the shock of the battering, and came face to face with the true nature of the terrible attack that had assailed him. Where he'd dodged the black dragon's tail was a giant, shaggy beast's left arm. As he was struck away, the black dragon's tail launched him up again, and what sliced through him was a bird's wing armed with several blade-sharp feathers—and every single one of them was the flesh of the girl standing before him.

"Reckon you've about figured out the answer by now, haven't you?"

There was no other word for it but monstrous. A human girl sprouting a dragon's tail, extending a beast's arm, spreading a great bird's wings. Faced with such a sight, what other word could possibly come to mind? The only thing that rose up besides words was the visceral revulsion of witnessing a creature in a form that should never exist.

There was nothing to feel toward the monster before him but disgust—nothing else at all.

"Mutation... transformation...!"

"I'm the Sin Archbishop of Lust, Capella Emerada Lugunica. All the love and reverence in this world exists solely to be monopolized by me. Bein' the one most deservin' of love, I can answer any pervert's twisted desire, embodyin' the ultimate ideal of beauty across every possible set of values. I'll go ahead and warp myself, all squishy-squashy, into whatever pretty girl happens to be your type, you know? I'm a woman who gives her all! Kyahahahahaha!"

Saying whatever she pleased, Capella's form truly shifted freely before Subaru's eyes. The monstrous shape reverted to that of a small girl, then her limbs immediately stretched and she became a grown woman with a voluptuous body. No sooner had he registered that than, in the blink of an eye, she changed into a girl with the air of an innocent village lass, and the next moment into a young child with a lewd smile on her guileless face.

"See? So tell me, just whi-i-i-ich me do you like?"

"—"

Subaru was speechless. No words came. All he could feel, keenly, was that this was the worst thing imaginable. It was a blasphemy against values themselves. For all that it was a simple, straightforward power, the Authority of Lust defiled and trampled every conceivable set of values, forcing its target's gaze onto herself.

Looking again, the wound from the figurine's blow had already closed up completely, without even a trace of a scar remaining. That terrifying regenerative power—or perhaps it was the shifting ability concealing the wound. Either way, the trick behind the black dragon transforming into a girl was solved. At first he'd suspected some ability like Petelgeuse's, a kind of possession of another being, but if that wasn't it—

"—Ah?"

If that wasn't it, then what were the flies in the broadcast room, and the black dragon that had been in the room until just now?

"Figured it out, have you?"

"...W-wait. Wait, wait wait wait wait, hold on. Just wait."

As if reading Subaru's inner thoughts from the change in his expression, Capella laughed. Her form shifted into a graceful woman with long, swaying hair, and even her voice was now entirely different. In the midst of changes so rapid he was on the verge of losing track of who he was even talking to, Subaru shook his head.

No, surely not—he wanted to believe it wasn't true. But if he thought of it that way, the explanation fit, the pieces lined up; and if it wasn't that, nothing else explained it.

Capella's Authority of Lust, freely mutating and transforming her own body. If that power could also be exerted on targets other than her own flesh—

"Even that sluggish head of yours oughta be able to figure out the truth behind the flies and the lizard by now, no?"

"Those are... those people were—"

"Hmm-hmm, go on, give me your answer. I'll hear you out. Kyaha."

Capella laughed haughtily, a hand at her mouth. Unable to bear the sheer horror her manner stirred in him, Subaru spoke, his teeth chattering.

"—Those are the people who were inside this building, transformed by you."

"Yep, correct answer, but way too slow. No prize for you. No praise from me either. What are stupid, ugly trash meat even for? Beats me."

"That's my line!!"

Capella affirmed her unspeakably cruel act with a face utterly devoid of remorse. The wordless end state of people, crammed into that dim room. The reason their glowing red compound eyes had all turned to look at him at once. The reason they had beaten wings that could never take flight, filling his ears with a buzzing that wouldn't fade—it was, surely, because they had been begging Subaru for help.

"I don't get it! Are you out of your mind!? Why—why would you do something like that!? How could you!? Turning people into flies... into flies—what's the point!?"

"Horrifying, is it?"

"It makes my skin crawl! You... you, all of you...!"

"So you loathe it. Can't stand how disgusting it is. That's what you're sayin'?"

Words wouldn't come anymore. Spitting, grinding his back teeth hard enough to crack them, Subaru glared at Capella with bloodshot eyes, unrelenting. If a glare alone could kill her, he genuinely wished it could. Turning people into flies, toying with their lives. It was worse than simply killing them. It was the absolute worst.

In these past few hours, Subaru had seen four Sin Archbishops he'd never had the chance to encounter before. Sirius of Wrath, a monstrosity who toyed with others' emotions and forced a self-serving love upon them. Regulus of Greed, a fiend who imposed his own values and forced his self-righteousness through. Alphard of Gluttony, a blasphemer who stole people's "memories" and "names," trampling the path a person had lived. And Capella of Lust, a monster who toyed with human dignity and values.

Every single one of them was insane beyond saving, insane enough to be cursed.

"—"

To Subaru, raging with fury fit to burst the blood vessels in his head, Capella wore a cold, unimpressed face as she piled on question after question, then fell silent. What nonsense would come flying out next? Before Subaru, his vision gone red with fury, Capella's expression—

"—Yes. Horrifying, disgusting, loathsome. That's exactly it."

Taking Subaru's negative emotions head-on, she smiled with a joy that couldn't have been any greater. She clapped her hands and pointed at the room full of people-turned-flies.

"Seein' a bunch of giant flies gathered together, you felt a gut-level revulsion. Thought it was horrifying. That's the correct response, the natural one to have. No matter who it is, that feeling of loathing toward such ugly creatures—can't be helped. Yeah, that's right."

"What... are you..."

"Somethin' ugly and disgusting that anyone could see for what it is. I took mushy, worthless trash meat and turned it into pitiful, filthy bugs. Nobody could love somethin' like that. Course not."

"So!! What are you trying to say!!"

"People are creatures that can't live without lovin' somethin'. But if all that's around is stuff like that, there's no way anyone could love it. So there's nothin' left to do but love somethin' else. By process of elimination. No matter how you struggle, you can't love somethin' filthy, after all."

His mind went blank. He couldn't comprehend the thinking of a monster who tilted her head as if she'd just proposed a brilliant idea.

Listening to the dry sound of clapping, Subaru was overtaken by the urge to flee this place right now. Right now, without waiting even a second, he wanted to vanish from any place where he could sense the monster's presence. He didn't want it in his sight. Didn't want to feel it against his skin. Didn't want to hear its voice. Didn't want its existence lodged in his memory. If this wasn't visceral revulsion, what was?

"Can't stand it on a gut level"—that phrase should really only be used for beings like this. An opponent your instincts reject—that's exactly how terrifying a mere presence could be.

"Merciful and kind as I am, I'm also a woman who falls in love easily, y'know. I've decided to monopolize all the love and respect in this world, but I'd never be so lazy as to skip the effort it takes to be loved. To be loved, I become whichever me you like. So that you'll look at me, I steal your interest away from everything that isn't me. I don't care who you love to start with. As long as, in the very end, you choose me. I never skimp on the effort for that. Raisin' my own charm up and up and up and up and up and up! Bringin' down the charm of every other piece of crap meat down and down and down and down and down and down! So that everyone comes to love me, the most precious and beautiful thing in this world."

"...Just kill me already."

"Why? I'm a humanitarian through and through, y'know, so I could never do somethin' as barbaric as killin'. Besides, no matter how stupid or hopeless a piece of trash you are... as long as you're alive, there's still a chance you'll come to love me. I've got a real strong need for approval, see. So I want as many people as possible, for as long as possible, praisin' me as highly as possible. And if you can't manage that—then, and only then, die! Go ahead and die already! That's the end of my oh-so-gracious sermon."

—. —. —.

"I understand."

"Oh, you finally get it? Well then, go ahead and line up all the words of praise for me, melt away in love into a lump of flesh just how I like it..."

"Die."

No thought came to mind. There was no need to think at all. The thing in front of him was an enemy. The worst kind of enemy, at that. He didn't want to know anything more about it.

He cracked his whip. The monster, caught off guard, stepped back, and the filthy creature finally lifted off of Crusch's body. Subaru surged into the opening and secured Crusch's body. Pulling her close, he hoisted her light frame and leapt backward all at once.

"See, there ya go—that's how it always is, male meat drippin' with want for female meat. Don't you go denyin' it. Don't you go linin' up pretty words about it. You like pretty things, don'tcha. You like cute things, don'tcha. You like soft, good-feelin' things, don'tcha. Quit puttin' on airs, damn it!!"

"—!"

Chasing after Subaru as he leapt back, Capella spat as she extended both arms. Those arms transformed—one into a snake's head, the other into a lion's head—stretching their misshapen necks after Subaru, crawling across the floor of the great room in an attempt to sink their fangs into him.

His right leg began bleeding again. There was no pain. He didn't care if it tore off entirely. Pouring his whole self into protecting the warmth and weight of the person in his arms, Subaru dedicated every ounce of his motion to dodging Capella's pursuit.

"Is your mate's female meat really that precious to you!? Then go on, hold her close like she's the most precious thing in the world, never let go while you hug her! That body that lures men in! Those eyes that invite pity! Those lips that let out a spoiled little voice! That feel-good flesh, flesh, flesh, flesh! You're desperate 'cause you just can't stand it, aren't ya, trash meat! Die! Die! Die right now!"

"Don't spout your bullshit, you damn bastard! It's not like that between me and her!"

"Shut it! When female meat smells like a female, and male meat like you smells like a male, that's all there is to it! You sayin' you've never thought about it, not once? You gonna stand there and swear, proud as anything, that you never had one single lewd thought, not for one scrap, not for one second? The moment you think about it for even one second, that's already a relationship between male meat and female meat! What's different! What's different!! Tell me what's different, huh!!"

The snake, the lion, thrashed wildly about the room as though reflecting Capella's excited will. The sound of fangs snapping shut, the sound of wooden desks being crushed to splinters—writhing appendages that could be called arms, necks, or torsos alike mowed down the furnishings, and sometimes struck Subaru's body.

Groaning, caught up in the destruction, Subaru kept shielding Crusch and dodging. Capella stood on the side of the exit. Even trying to watch for an opening to break out, Capella's main body kept swelling and shrinking, wildly wandering across the boundary between woman, girl, and child, giving off an abnormality that made even approaching her repellent.

"Don'tcha wanna stroke her hair? Don'tcha wanna touch her lips? Don'tcha wanna hold that body? And that filthy, drippin' train of thought—you people go and dress it up with pretty words like 'love,' don'tcha! Don't you go gettin' the wrong idea! Don't you go mistakin' love for somethin' pretty! You're just decoratin' your own lust with beautiful words, all by yourselves, and gettin' off on it, aren'tcha!"

"—Guh, gah!"

"Lust you can't openly spit out! Don't go dressin' it up with a word like 'love.' Or maybe you'll say it anyway, go on and say it, will ya? That same old rebuttal. —I love her because I was drawn to the depths of her heart! Because of her nobility, her kindness, her merciful heart, the largeness of her vessel, her eyes that mirror the sky, the way she lives able to hurt herself for someone else's sake, her strength in never voicing complaint, the weakness she shows only to me, how I can't leave her be when she tries to push through alone, her voice that puts my heart at ease, her eyes brimming with compassion, the gaze that steals my heart away, her lips that call me beloved, the warmth of her hand entwined with mine, the way my heart races every time we touch, her beautiful hair swaying in the wind, because I can believe we were bound together by fate, because she's the only one who's ever acknowledged me, because she stayed by my side when things were hard, because she taught me what truly mattered, because we've been together for so, so long, because I want to keep seeing and feeling the same things together from now on, because we made a promise, because she never forgot our vow, because she found the me that's different from everyone else, because she's the only one who knows the real me, because it's only in front of her that I can be myself without pretending, because I always wanted someone to understand that deep down I was lonely, because she reminded me of my very first feelings, because she taught me what it means to fall for someone, because you wiped away the tears that fell, because out of so many people, you found me, because you held me so tightly I thought I might break, because you were the first one to ever scold me, when no one else ever had, because you were the first to speak words to me that weren't just going through the motions, because you taught me so many things I never knew, because you showed me sights I'd never seen, because you let me out of the birdcage I'd been shut inside, because you pulled me by the arm and led me out of a world that suffocated me, because you stood by my side even when I had no one else, because you're the only one who understands me, because being together with you just feels natural, because I can't live without you, because being with you is my everything, because you love me and I love you, because you make my heart burn, because with you everything looks vivid and full of color, because I can't feel happiness without you, because I can't live alone anymore, because in a life full of lies, this feeling alone is real—"

Reciting the words like a curse, Capella's expression died a little more with every phrase. But after going on and on, endlessly listing layer upon layer of reasons for love, Capella raised her face—now a bizarre, complicated visage where beauty, cuteness, and lewdness intermingled—and screamed.

"—All of it, every single bit, is just pretty talk!!"

"—"

"Quit spoutin' nothin' but stuff that sounds nice to the ear! Goin' on and on about how it's the inside that matters, or personality, or bein' compatible, or how well you get along—shut up already! It's the surface, it's the outside, it's the looks that stir up your flesh, so you're drawn to that flesh, aren'tcha! If you really feel love in your heart, then go ahead and try lovin' the person you dressed up with sparkly words, gazed at with sparkly eyes, whispered sweet pillow-talk to with a sparkly, honeyed tongue—try lovin' 'em after they've turned into a fly! Can you love 'em? You can't, can you!? Horrifyin', ain't it!? Disgustin', ain't it!? Nothin' but revulsion wells up, don't it!? You said so yourself, just now, didn'tcha!?"

A raging storm of abuse, ravings, and paranoid delusion, jealousy, hatred, obsession, and self-preservation. Spitting as she screeched, Capella raved on hysterically as though she'd lost her sanity, destroying the room all the while.

The great serpent's hissing threats, the lion's roars, Capella's screaming—none of it reached Subaru's ears anymore. The noise became a storm, and parts of the room collapsed one after another. Swallowed by the impacts, he couldn't even tell how he was moving, lost amid the billowing smoke. Were his feet even still on the ground? Was the leg that had nearly been torn off still intact? The only certain thing was the heartbeat of the woman in his arms, sending strength coursing through Subaru's entire body.

But even that desperate struggle had reached its end.

"Trash meat, keep your eyes on me!"

"—Gaaah!!"

Bursting through the smoke, the lion's head charged furiously. Its fangs, thrown wide open, sank into Subaru's outstretched right leg, and the leg—already having lost about half its flesh—burst from the thigh in a massive spray of blood.

Beyond the limits of the healing magic Ferris had cast on the wound, Subaru's brain boiled over with the searing agony of losing his leg, and his throat let out a scream that couldn't even take the shape of sound.

Naturally, he couldn't even hold his body upright anymore. He collapsed, and Crusch rolled to the ground in front of him too. Writhing, blood kept gushing out, more and more. This wasn't a matter of some thick blood vessel or another. Blood poured out as if a bucket had been overturned, and he could tell that what remained of Subaru's life was draining away at a terrifying speed.

"Haah, my head hurts. Got a little too worked up there, huh. My apologies. Kyaha."

"—"

Lying on his back, Subaru pressed a hand to the wound, convulsing. Though his palm held the wound closed, there was no sign the bleeding was weakening. No—there was a sign. But it was only because there was less and less left inside Subaru's body to spill out. It would be over soon. That familiar sensation of "death" was drawing very near.

In the span of a few hours, he had suffered wounds severe enough to lose a leg twice over. His complexion had gone past pale, turning ashen, his bloodshot eyes wide open, his breathing ragged.

"Oh my, looks like you're about to die. Watchin' trash meat writhe in agony—for someone like me, who understands the pain of a human heart, it's just too much to bear."

"...Ah... ahh..."

"The female meat's probably gonna die too. Such a shame. She was just my type... I wanted to try her out. See whether she'd lose to the blood or not. Oh, that's right."

Capella crouched down and peered into the writhing Subaru's face. Then the monster smiled, and gently reached her hand out over the wound on Subaru's right leg.

"Let's see just what kind of unsightly lump of meat you turn into, shall we?"

"...Ohh..."

So saying, Capella gouged into her own outstretched wrist with her other hand, transformed into a blade, drawing blood. The flowing blood dripped down onto Subaru's right leg in fat drops, dark, murky blood mixing with fresh red blood, creating a scene of depraved corruption.

It happened immediately after.

"—!? Guh, ahh, gaaoo aoo!?"

"My blood ain't like your everyday, run-of-the-mill blood, y'know. After all, it's got the Dragon's Blood mixed in. Lose to the Curse of the blood, and somethin' real nasty happens. So, will you hold out longer than the female meat over there, I wonder?"

Capella chuckled merrily, but Subaru couldn't answer at all. Half-dead, his very pain already grown sluggish, his body on the verge of death was doused in blood. It invaded through the wound in his right leg, trampling through Subaru's body, eating into him. The sensation of a willful foreign substance flowing in, of his very existence as himself being overwritten by something entirely different, was unlike pain or suffering—it violated Natsuki Subaru with a terror of an altogether different dimension.

He couldn't understand it. He wasn't even allowed to simply die. The monster had said something about who would hold out longer, him or Crusch. Did that mean Crusch was, right now, tasting this same suffering too? He wanted to die. He wanted to die, wanted to die. He didn't want to die.

"Kyahahaha! Well now, I've gone and safely dealt with the intruders. Reckon the time's drawin' near, so I oughta be..."

Glancing back and forth between the agonized Subaru and Crusch, Capella stood up, satisfied. Her form shifted back into that of a girl, and as she brushed off her backside and walked toward the broadcast room, she suddenly turned her face to the side as if noticing something. There stood the collapsed wall where the black dragon that had been in the room had been thrown out—

"Oh my, look at that. Not bad."

"—!"

Having crawled back up from the floor below where it had fallen, the black dragon let out a roar upon spotting its mortal enemy, and black flames burst forth from its open maw, aimed straight at Capella.

—In an instant, the top floor of city hall was engulfed in jet-black flame.
