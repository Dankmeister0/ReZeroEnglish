Arc 3 Chapter 80: "The End of Sloth"

　—A brilliant rainbow radiance repelled the surging pitch-black darkness.

　The clash had already lasted dozens of exchanges. Subaru continued to watch the sword and hands shave away at one another, all while enduring the searing agony shooting through his entire body.

　Julius’s sword—a blade cloaked in the colors of the rainbow—was empowered by a composite spell of all six elements known as "Clarista," which allowed its edge to reach even the Authority of the "Unseen Hand" unleashed by Petelgeuse.

　Every time a slash was unleashed, the cleaved demonic hands dissipated into mist, vanishing from the shadows as dust. Though the exact mechanics were beyond Subaru, it seemed the Unseen Hands could not regenerate before the rainbow blade; with each shadow lost, a flash of pure rage visibly surfaced on the madman’s face.

"It is not amusing, DESU! It is no joke, DESU! It is completely unacceptable, DESU! To try and belittle my love... my loyalty, with such trivial tricks as that...!"

　Spitting foul words of irritation, Petelgeuse extended arms from the shadows that swelled as if bottomless, relentlessly targeting Julius’s arms, legs, and head at random.
　The parrying blade met every strike, or twisted away in evasive maneuvers, as the finest of knights dashed across the battlefield as though dancing—literally performing a sword dance.

"———!"

　Ripping through the air, the number of approaching Unseen Hands consistently exceeded ten. There was no way to sweep them all away in a single stroke; for every hand cut down, for every evasive motion made, unavoidable wounds still tore into Julius’s flesh.
　Miraculously, he had avoided any fatal damage, such as being grabbed or torn to shreds. No, it was no miracle—it was a feat made possible solely by Julius’s extraordinary combat prowess.
　And because Subaru knew he was the one supporting that razor-thin margin of success, he could not tear his eyes away from the scene unfolding before him.

　A pitch-black fingertip grazed Subaru's thigh, and he grimaced at the gouging pain. Clenching his teeth against the torment, he choked back a cry at the sharp sensation of his shoulder being torn open.
　Through "Nect," their senses were shared—Subaru’s and Julius’s bodies and five senses were currently synchronized on a deeply intimate level. Thus, Julius could perceive Petelgeuse’s Unseen Hands through Subaru’s vision, and conversely, Subaru could feel the overwhelming mana surging from the rainbow knight sword Julius held.

　However, setting those benefits aside, it was no exaggeration to say this coordinated state was exceptionally unstable.
　The synchronization of vision caused multiple perspectives to overlap at times, accompanied by the bizarre sensation of seeing entirely different landscapes out of his right and left eyes. The bodily empathy conveyed not only the exhilaration of combat coursing through Julius’s entire being, but also transmitted the pain carved into his flesh directly and vividly to Subaru.

　Constantly bombarded by "two people’s worth" of stimuli—the feel of the wind brushing skin, the soles of feet treading on dirt, the mingled taste of blood and saliva in the mouth, the dull, murky ringing in the ears—the strain placed on mind and body could hardly be described as merely double.
　How much easier would it be if he could just shut down his sense of taste, smell, pain, and touch right now? The dilemma of feeling an itch in a place his own hands could never reach. It was like feeling an itch on the back of someone else's head.

"Wanting to get this over with as fast as possible is my absolute, unvarnished truth..."

　Surrounded by a mass of dissonance engulfing his whole body, Subaru moistened his lips with his tongue and muttered. Julius had likely felt that sensation just now, too. He couldn't even afford to be careless.
　If he could follow his instincts to complain, he would gladly discard all his senses and shut down his consciousness right this second. Even though it was a strategy he had proposed himself, sharing empathy with another person was just that repulsive.
　However, he had no choice but to seal away those pathetic, weak feelings for now.
　Because—

"My body is growing accustomed to this little by little. Subaru, shall I increase the pace?"

"Yeah, I'll keep up, so don't you worry!"

　As he spoke, Julius lowered his stance and charged straight into the massive swarm of shadows. Gliding, practically crawling along the ground, he slipped past the palms, sweeping away three demonic hands at once with an upward slash from directly below. He nimbly wove through the gaps of the dissipating, dusty shadows, immediately shifting his weight into a deep, earth-carving pivot—and the palm that aimed for the tail end of his movement was met by the rainbow blade, bursting into brilliant sparks before scattering.

　Twisting his body lightly, Julius swung his rainbow sword as if shaking off the lingering obsession clinging to it from the demonic hands. Beneath his refined, handsome visage, his eyes remained closed, not having opened even once.

　With their vision synchronized, if both fought with their eyes open, their fields of view would overlap intermittently, reducing the world to an ambiguous blur. Thus, Julius had made the decision to close his own eyes, entrusting the entirety of his visual information to Subaru’s sight.
　Subaru had been flustered by this unexpected development, having almost complained loudly about Julius's choice, but a reason had emerged that forced him to swallow every word.

　First, Julius’s decision to abandon his own sight was the correct one. At the very least, it avoided the folly of hesitating before two conflicting views.
　And second, it was the conclusion of the thought process that led to Julius relinquishing his sight in the first place.

"You idiot, you complete idiot, messing around like that... You really are an infuriating guy!"

　Giving up his own vision and entrusting everything he saw to Subaru was nothing less than putting his life on the line to prove his absolute faith that Subaru would not look away from the battle.
　Furthermore, pouring all his focus into Subaru’s vision was no simple task. Subaru’s eyes belonged strictly to Subaru’s body, not to Julius’s.

　In other words, Julius was currently pulling off the outrageous feat of viewing his own body from a third-person, objective angle from behind, controlling his movements in combat while perceiving himself from someone else's vantage point.

"This isn't a video game, dammit... It's a trash game where a single grab means instant game over. Betting your life on that... we're both totally out of our minds."

　Julius leaped to dodge the whip-like trajectory of an arm, grabbing a tree branch to propel his body through the air. Subaru turned his head so as not to lose sight of him, taking care not to let him get too far away. After all—

"You need not concern yourself so much over there; once I strike *you* down, everything is over, DESU!"

　Petelgeuse's primary target was not Julius, who was putting his life on the line at the front, but Subaru.
　Subaru was the keystone of this battlefield, the one serving as the indispensable "eyes" needed to overcome the Unseen Hand. And above all, to Petelgeuse, he was the vile sinner who had mocked his faith.

"Julius, bad news! Fall back! Get back here! Follow me!"

"Good grief, honestly... What a handful you are!"

　Kicking off a tree trunk, Julius executed an inhuman feat by leaping horizontally, landing right beside Subaru. At the same time, as the Unseen Hands closed in on both of them, Julius completely ignored the palms bearing down on his own back—minimizing his movements to let them merely graze his skin—and thrust the tip of his blade forward, piercing and drilling through the demonic hand that sought to tear off Subaru’s head.
　Next, the rainbow gleam traced an arc, cleanly cleaving through the palms that had grazed Julius. Turning the crisis around in an instant, the knight smiled at Subaru with his eyes still closed.

"I understand you are desperate, but perhaps you could look to your own defense a little? At this rate, I cannot even comfortably face the enemy head-on."

"I'm throwing those exact words right back at you! I can't comfortably watch this either! Can you see this frantic knight through my eyes, huh?!"

"Reflected in them is a handsome man with a melancholic gaze, with features blessed by noble lineage and talent."

"I'm starting to suspect we're seeing completely different things!!"

　Sticking his tongue out at Julius’s self-praise, Subaru scrambled away from the surging wave of palms. Subaru rolled away, while Julius leaped backward, parrying with a sweeping slash that forced the arms to dissipate before stepping forward once more.

　The terrifying thing about Julius was that, even in the midst of combat, he was rapidly adapting to the feat of controlling his body from a bird's-eye view.
　Unlike Subaru, Julius possessed the experience of pushing his body to its absolute limits, along with the way of life of a knight who honed himself on battlefields where lethal intent was traded for lethal intent.

　Subaru’s current limit was to cower in fear of battle, unable to display even his normal capability, let alone surpass it.
　Julius and other true warriors, however, were of the sort who refined themselves within battle, drawing out power far beyond their limits.

　The fact that he was involved in a part of this warrior's struggle was what barely kept Subaru’s cowardly, retreating knees from buckling entirely.

　Grazed and gouged by palms, the pain from his back, thighs, and burst shoulder struck Subaru’s brain like a physical blow.
　Gritting his teeth to endure the agony, Subaru did not look away.

　Dashing, leaping, sliding, pivoting, stepping in, springing back, advancing, slipping under, holding ground, slipping past, circling around, side-jumping, ducking, looking back, charging, leaping upward, kicking—every action grew ever more refined.

　Parrying, severing, thrusting, uppercut-slashing, kicking away, flow-cutting, striking, sweeping, piercing, bisecting, flashing, swinging down, knocking down, lopping off, mincing—the accumulation of strikes and slashes reduced the Unseen Hands back to dust.

　While an eerie, ominous darkness expanded as if to swallow their entire field of view, the figure of the knight performing a sword dance with a rainbow-colored blade in hand was beautiful to the point of unreality.
　It was a scene so ethereal that one almost forgot they were fighting for their lives.

　The reason Subaru felt that way was likely because, through Julius, he could feel the pulsations of the spirits. And he felt something similar in the raining palms as well—.

"This cannot be... There is no way this can be happening...! Why, DESU? Why is this happening, DESU?! How can my Authority be treated like this?! I was supposed to be loved, I am supposed to be loved, I *am* loved, DESU! I love her, DESU! This much, to this extent, to this absolute degree! If that were not so, then why! Why would the Witch have guided my very existence, DESU?!"

"I have no time to entertain your incoherent rambling. The number of your black hands has diminished considerably. And above all, my body has thoroughly adapted to fighting through Subaru’s eyes."

　Relying on his hearing, Julius turned his head toward the shrieking Petelgeuse. Holding his breath for a brief moment, he aimed the tip of his sword at the madman.

"—It is about time I cut you down in earnest. I shall put an end to 'Sloth' right here—one part of the threat that has menaced the Kingdom... no, the world, for so long."

"As if you could! As if I would allow it! To me... I! For four hundred years, I have bathed solely in the Witch's favor, diligently striving to embody her will, DESU! To think that I would be obstructed by someone like you... a fool who merely drags along petty spirits—such a thing is utterly, completely unacceptable, DESU!!"

　Baring his teeth, Petelgeuse revealed his blazing fury at Julius’s declaration.
　Hearing the contents of those words, however, Subaru felt the deduction he had shared with Julius take on intense reality. In that case, that bastard truly was—

"Julius—!"

"I know! Sin Archbishop, prepare yourself—!!"

　Stepping in with a kick forceful enough to gouge the earth, Julius’s body launched forward.
　Petelgeuse opened his mouth, shouting something as he deployed the Unseen Hands with obsessive fervor. Into the sky, into the ground, into the forest—the expanding demonic hands closed in from all directions without a single gap, attempting to swallow Julius’s body whole—

"—Al Clarista!!"

　An aurora of rainbow light incinerated the world, blowing away every single palm that had tried to crush Julius.
　It was the pinnacle spell of Clarista, cloaking the sword in rainbow brilliance.
　Though it was only for an instant, the encirclement Petelgeuse had constructed vanished entirely, carving out an unobstructed path between Julius and the madman—!

　In the aftermath of the rainbow aurora, Petelgeuse lost even the demonic hands he had used to suspend himself in the air, crashing to the ground before rising while trembling with violent passion.
　Julius rushed toward the madman without a shred of hesitation. Holding his blade straight before him, its tip once again took on the rainbow glow, aiming directly for Petelgeuse’s chest.

"I will not let you, DESU!! Ur Dona!!"

　Spreading his arms, Petelgeuse stood as if to meet Julius head-on and chanted. Immediately, a torrent of mana interfered with the earth—earthen walls rose in front of Julius, surrounding Petelgeuse on all four sides to keep the madman safe from the blade.
　Furthermore, curving around from behind the obstruction, a massive cluster of black palms swept in to crush Julius in an embrace.

　At this rate, Julius would need an extra action to break through the wall, and that single movement would delay his response against the demonic hands by a step. That would leave him with a fatal choice: either suffer a mortal wound, or lose the opportunity to corner Petelgeuse.

　—If Julius had been fighting here alone, that is.

"Burn, fighting spirit! Roar, magic pitch! —My full power is 120 kilometers per hour!!"

　Twisting his body, lifting his leg, and stepping in as he swung his shoulder around in a full swing—at a speed that was a bit of a stretch to call a fastball, a magic stone was hurled from Subaru’s hand.
　Though released from the hand of an ordinary person who had never practiced pitching, fueled by the focus of an extreme situation, the throw slammed straight into its target.

"What is... DESU?!"

　The destructive energy sealed within the red magic stone bypassed Julius’s back and collided with the earthen wall acting as a barrier—exploding on impact with light and intense heat, sending up a blast of flames that painted the space in front of Petelgeuse entirely red.

"Do not tell me you planned this from the very start...!"

"The reason for your defeat is that you stepped onto this battlefield entirely alone!"

　Bursting through the flames, Julius’s thrust pierced straight into Petelgeuse’s chest.
　The rainbow radiance penetrated Petelgeuse’s body, the aurora scorching his gaunt frame from the inside out.

　Slammed into the earthen wall behind him, the impaled Petelgeuse flailed his limbs like an insect pinned to a specimen board, foaming bloody bubbles from the corner of his mouth.

"Im... possible, impossible, im-pos-si-ble... My body... like this..."

"A blade binding the six elements scorches your body from within. If your true identity matches our deduction, then vanish into nothingness just like this!"

　Julius twisted the impaled sword, drawing a shriek from Petelgeuse.
　The knight sword shone brighter, and before that light, Petelgeuse could neither unleash the Unseen Hand to keep him at bay, nor chant a spell to overturn the situation.
　Even so, Petelgeuse’s frenzied gaze had not yet given up on life.

"I cannot end! There is no way I can end! It is unacceptable for this to be the end, DESU! I have strived diligently, DESU! To indulge in slothful resignation, to submit to a slothful end—I am not permitted to even contemplate such a thing, DESU! Therefore, in that case, by any means necessary...!"

　Shrieking, writhing, struggling, Petelgeuse widened his own wound, expanding the root of his torment. Matching his movements, Julius pushed the tip of his sword even deeper, shifting the blade to destroy his heart—and then,

"I have lost my Fingers, and now my flesh is ruined like this... But, but, BUT! I still! Have! Another body left to me... DESU!"

　With all his potential vessels crushed in advance, Petelgeuse faced the demise of his final physical body, yet screamed in defiance of surrender.
　Those eyes, filled with madness, fixed straight upon Subaru.
　A terrifying chill ran down Subaru's spine as Petelgeuse’s mad cackle deepened.

"Ah—my brain trembles."

"———!"

　The next instant, Petelgeuse’s body, impaled by Julius, collapsed like a puppet whose strings had been snapped. Light faded from his eyes, and with his head dangling loosely, every trace of life vanished from the flesh.
　The moment he saw that reaction, Subaru judged that the inevitable had arrived. Instantly placing a hand to his chest, he pulled something from inside his coat while shouting:

"Julius! Cancel it!!"

"Understood!"

　Responding to Subaru’s call, Julius kicked Petelgeuse’s corpse aside and turned around, sprinting toward him. His eyes were open, and immediately afterward, the overwhelming pressure of doubled senses that had weighed upon Subaru’s body vanished.
　In its place, another presence barged its way into Subaru’s flesh. That was—

『As expected, your body possesses the aptitude to serve as my vessel! Even you could not have predicted things turning out like this, DESU! Ah, ah! Ah! You truly are Slothful, are you not?』

　Petelgeuse’s mad laughter echoed so close that Subaru wondered if the madman was nestling right alongside his brain.
　Next, against Subaru’s will, his body raised its right hand toward Julius, who was running toward him. His shadow swelled, and the Unseen Hands erupting from within aimed straight for Julius, who had lost the means to see them—

"Gwah!"
『—Ah?』

『If I know your location through the mirror, this much is simple, *ne*~. Honestly though, Subaru-kyun, coming up with a plan that relies on your own sacrifice... you really are a s-i-l-l-y b-o-y♪』

Exactly as Subaru had instructed—the moment Petelgeuse began his possession, Ferris had transformed Subaru’s body into a "puppet," just like the Witch Cultist they had interrogated.

"If you can't... keep a sane state of mind, you can't bring out the Unseen Hand either, right?"

"The Witch-sama you've been waiting for, of course."

　—Come on, bring it.

"—I can Return by Dea—"

　Having lost even the energy to play the madman, Petelgeuse looked up at Julius with a dazed expression. His gaze then shifted toward Subaru—and suddenly, a spark of passion flared in his eyes.

　Tears welled up, streaming down Petelgeuse’s cheeks.
　Unlike the tears of ecstasy seen so many times before, these were the proof of a hopeless, irredeemable delusion, overflowing purely from bitter frustration.
　Petelgeuse wept, his voice trembling, reaching a hand toward the heavens as if trying to grasp something that was not there as he screamed:

"O Witch... O Witch! O Witch! O WIIITCH! When I have offered up this much to you! When I threw away everything for your sake! When I dedicated every conceivable thing to you, why, DESU?! Why is this happening, DESU?! Why do you forsake me, DESU?! Why, DESU?! Why, DESU?! O Witch! If that is so... then why did you grant me love... your favor... DESU?!"

"What you offered wasn't faith or love, and it certainly wasn't yourself. It was just the bystanders who happened to be walking around you."

　Subaru unequivocally cut down Petelgeuse’s pleading lament.
　It wasn't even worth lending an ear to; Petelgeuse's feelings were nothing more than self-righteous, self-absorbed delusions. It was just as Wilhelm had said—presumptuous to even call it "love."

"———!"

　Julius surged forward, closing the distance to Petelgeuse’s gaunt frame.
　Against the raised blade, Petelgeuse merely turned dry, hollow eyes—and once again, the tip of the knight sword pierced his chest, unleashing an explosion of light.

　A rainbow aurora that scorched the spirit residing within the flesh—the collective mass of mana—eradicating the existence known as Petelgeuse from the world down to the roots.

　The blade was pulled free, and Petelgeuse stared blankly down at his bleeding chest.
　Then, turning his unfocused eyes toward the sky, his hand still reaching toward the heavens—

"—My, brain, trem, bles."

　A single Unseen Hand shot from his shadow toward the sky, reaching endlessly toward the dazzling sun.
　Yet, the palm grasped nothing; heading into empty air, it eventually gouged deeply into the face of the cliff, violently tearing away the rock and sending fissures running through it.

　—It likely wasn't an intentional act. It held no meaning even to Petelgeuse; it was merely an action giving form to his lingering delusion.

　A collapse occurred directly above Petelgeuse, as the gouged cliff face peeled away in massive boulders. Directly beneath them stood Petelgeuse, still gazing at the empty heavens he could not grasp.

"I am... loved—"

　The falling rock crushed his body from directly above, the sound of splintering bone and flesh resounding grandly.
　Tremors and plumes of dust erupted in succession, and in an instant, Petelgeuse’s gaunt frame was buried beneath a grave marker of rubble of his own making.

　Julius, having avoided the collapse, walked over to the spot where Petelgeuse had stood. Ahead of his gaze, a massive pool of fresh blood flowed out from beneath the boulder. Seeing this, he shook his head, sheathed his drawn knight sword, and turned around.

　Subaru also stepped forward without a single word.
　His footing was unsteady, and combined with the rough terrain, his progress was agonizingly slow. But upon reaching the rubble marker, Subaru let out a quiet breath.

　There was no awe, no sense of accomplishment, no satisfaction.
　All he felt was an empty emotion opening wide within his chest.

　Subaru did not commit the tactlessness of speaking of concepts like victory or defeat here.
　He merely gave voice to the sole thought crossing his blank mind.

"Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti."

　With that single name, an end was marked to this battle.

　—The Sin Archbishop representing "Sloth," Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti.
　—The battle fought by the finest of knights, Julius Juukulius, and the self-proclaimed knight, Natsuki Subaru.

　Standing before the grave marker of rubble, Subaru took a small breath and spoke.

"—You really were 'Slothful'."
