Arc 4 Chapter 83: "Mutual Confessions"

Feeling Roswaal's pressure prick at his skin, Subaru understood that what he'd just said had touched the very core of Roswaal's scheme.

Roswaal's smile was the same quality as the one he'd worn back in the snow, moments before being torn apart by the Great Rabbit, when he'd half-revealed his own intentions.
An expression that was neither resignation nor delight—it was more than enough to put Subaru on his guard.

"Now wha-a-at makes you think that?"

At this point in the game, Subaru clicked his tongue at Roswaal's continued evasion, his refusal to let the conversation proceed smoothly. Then, prefacing with "what makes me think that, he asks—"

"It's the way you shot down my proposal. It didn't feel honest. When you tack 'releasing the Sanctuary' onto the very front as a condition for everything else, of course I'm going to suspect there's something behind it."

"I believe I gave you a perfectly reasonable explanation for tha-a-at. To begin with, if we're to keep cooperating with each other going forward, you need to prove yourself. The grounds for continuing to lend Emilia-sama my strength—namely, that you, capable of deriving the optimal solution, remain at Emilia-sama's side. I want you to make me believe that. And the condition for doing so is the release of the Sanctuary."

"It doesn't have to be release specifically—I figure the more means we have of getting out of here, the better. And there'll be plenty of chances down the road to prove myself…"

"I'd like to ask you the opposite, actually."

As if countering Subaru's persistence, Roswaal raised a single finger. When Subaru fell silent, his words cut off, Roswaal nodded magnanimously.

"Aren't you the one who seems awfully reluctant when it comes to the Trials? It's almost as if you're the one with a reason not to want the Sanctuary released."

"There's no way I don't want it released! I want to pry that barrier open right now and drag the problem we can't solve inside out into the open, I really do… but."

"But?"

Subaru caught himself about to add more, and realized he was being drawn into Roswaal's pace. If he raised his voice here and spoke without thinking, the game of deception would go to Roswaal by default.
He forced himself to stay calm, to choose his words.

"I don't want to watch Emilia get her heart wounded by challenging that Trial."

"All the more reason for your Authority, isn't it? If Emilia-sama stumbles at the Trial, you simply take her place. There's no problem there. What matters is the fact of its release—just as you yourself said."

"Nn, ngh…"

Caught neatly by his own words, Subaru bit his lip, searching for a way to continue. But a claim built on piling one impossibility atop another wasn't easy to make coherent.

"I know it doesn't matter whether it's me or Emilia who breaks through the Trial. Having her past wounds torn open is too much for her. That I should be the one to take her place—that too, I get… only."

"Surely you're not saying something so naive as 'the Trial is hard on me too, so let's find a way around it'?"

Roswaal's gaze sharpened, the edge in his pressing words growing keener.
As Subaru faltered, Roswaal went on with a "surely not, surely not"—

"Loving yourself, finding it hard on yourself… if that's the reason you go groping for some other method, then that's the extent of your feelings for Emilia-sama, isn't it—?"

"That's not—!"

"Not what? Really? How can you say so with such certainty? Who can you convince? If you truly care for Emilia-sama, shouldn't you be able to swallow all of it—the pain, the hardship, the suffering? If you love Emilia-sama, shouldn't that be a given? If Emilia-sama matters more than anything, if you place Emilia-sama above all else, if you can accept that everything besides Emilia-sama is a trivial thing to be spent in service of raising Emilia-sama to greater heights… then there shouldn't be any problem at all, should there?"

Roswaal's fluent, relentless string of words, delivered as if reasoning with a child, nearly swept Subaru up in its current.
It was an extreme argument, pure and simple—but for anyone who understood Subaru's Return by Death, it was one of the conclusions he was bound to arrive at eventually.
Or rather, it was the conclusion that the Subaru from before—before Satella exposed his true feelings at the Witch's Tea Party—might well have ended up agreeing with.

—Ah. Yeah. That's probably true.

Subaru had meant it as a declaration to Roswaal: that he wouldn't become like him, wouldn't discard everything except the one thing that mattered most, the way Roswaal had told him to.
But if, hypothetically, he had taken Echidna's hand at that later tea party, he probably would have ended up living exactly the life Roswaal wished for him.

Abandoning thought, crushing every option, chasing only results, treating everything else with contempt.
No matter how badly he was hurt himself, if the future he wanted lay beyond it—Emilia's smile, everyone's smiles—he'd thought that would be enough.
But would the Natsuki Subaru who chose only to endure that much hardship still be able to smile alongside them, the same as ever, in that final moment?

—Or would he be forcing himself to smile, driven by nothing but a sense of obligation that he *must* smile, wearing a dry, hollow grin?

"…What you're saying is right, in a way, Roswaal."

"In a way? What do you mean?"

Roswaal closed one eye, tilting his head at Subaru's loaded reply.
Meeting that yellow gaze, Subaru spoke as if forcing the words out.

"You're right—if I threw everything else away and kept running just to protect Emilia and nothing else… I probably could save her, and only her. But that's not enough."

"Not enough…"

"I'm going to save Emilia. But I can't be satisfied with just Emilia. Rem, too. Beatrice, too. The people of the Sanctuary, the people at the mansion, all the people who looked after me in the royal capital… I want to save all of them. Together."

"————"

"I can't live the way you do—satisfied with just one person. Whether it's you or me who's the greedy one, I honestly don't know."

To love one person and go on loving them, to throw everything else away for that alone—there's something beautiful about it, in its way.
Call it "living for love," and there's no denying it's a noble act, nothing less.

Roswaal's way might be, as one man's way of living, a completed thing in its own right.
But to imitate it required a staggering resolve, a willingness to whittle away everything precious as if shaving down his own soul, and Subaru didn't think he could do that.
As ever, his own vessel was small—Subaru remained, still, a selfish child.

"…It seems your resolve hasn't been honed sharp enough yet, after a-a-all."

"…………"

"For a moment there… yes, just for a moment, I let myself hope. That perhaps you might see the place I'm hoping for. But… it seems that's not to be, after a-a-all."

Roswaal shook his head, murmuring "how unfortunate."
In this exchange, it had been laid bare to Roswaal that Subaru had not yet settled on the resolve Roswaal's scheme required of him.
To Roswaal, who demanded of Subaru a merciless resolve in tackling the challenge before them, the present Subaru was a defective, disappointing thing. And that, in turn, meant nothing less than glimpsing the end of his own life's purpose.

"Just how many more times… I wonder, will you disappoint me?"

"If that's how you feel, maybe you should be a little more cooperative with me. If you'd just lend me a hand without holding back, most of our problems would be solved."

Subaru said it almost mockingly to Roswaal, who made no attempt to hide his disappointment.
In truth, with Subaru having no real plan on the combat front, Roswaal's strength was something he wanted badly. The overwhelming magic that had rained fire down on the witchbeast forest, the skill that had precisely cut down the Great Rabbit's assault—against the threat of the Great Rabbit, Roswaal's cooperation was indispensable.
Put the other way around: if he could just win Roswaal over to his side, half the problems on the Sanctuary's end would be solved.
But Roswaal shook his head at Subaru's simple but reliable demand.

"Unfortunately, that's not how it works. Cooperating with you as you currently stand offers me far too little in the way of merit. Suppose—yes, suppose you did overcome this crisis by borrowing my strength. With your resolve left ambiguous, you and Emilia-sama would push forward and inevitably hit a wall somewhere down the line… and when that happens, would you rely on me again? Putting off the resolve you should have settled here, only to flounder helplessly once matters became irreversible?"

"…………"

"Subaru-kun, I… for the sake of my own purpose, I cannot decide to lend my strength to someone I can't entrust with my trust. A relationship of pure dependence has no place in my purpose. That's why I want you, by whatever means necessary, to convince me—to show me your resolve to move forward."

"Your purpose…"

"This time, it seems that wasn't to be. Unfortunate. I'll look forward to the next opportunity. To when you can accept your own power, correctly, in the true sense."

As if to say the conversation was over, Roswaal let the tension drain from his body and lay back down on the bed.
For Roswaal, the meaning of his "current" self's living had, with this, been lost. He probably intended, with the mindset of watching a meaningless dead-rubber match, to see failed Subaru redo it all, and then simply expire.

If the conversation ended here as it was, Subaru would leave having accomplished none of what he'd come for.
Unwilling to slink away with nothing, and watching Roswaal's hand gesture urging him out of the room, Subaru scrambled his mind into motion.

"…What would it take? What way of showing my resolve would actually convince you?"

"Hmm… to be honest, that's the sort of thing I'd rather you sensed for yourself, through continued contact with me, but going through that many redos just for that purpose would be a needless waste of effort, wouldn't it."

Roswaal reacted to Subaru's continued words, sitting back up and putting a hand to his chin.

"In broad strokes, it comes down to the release of the Sanctuary. However, reaching the release of the Sanctuary requires action on your part, and that requires the resolve to keep redoing it however many times it takes. The Sanctuary's release being achieved is, naturally, the proof of the result of your having settled that resolve."

"Why does it have to come to that? Sure, maybe that's the closest thing to an answer, but if that's all it is, then… clearing the Trial alone shouldn't connect to some whole story about my resolve to throw everything away. There's still the chance Emilia clears the Trial herself…"

"That's impossible."

Roswaal's remark felt too extreme, and Subaru reflexively strung together a rebuttal. But the answer that came back was curt, and ice-cold.
Subaru, faintly taken aback by its sharpness, watched Roswaal wag his raised finger.

"Your fleeting hope will never bear fruit. There's no possibility of Emilia-sama overcoming the Trial. Her heart isn't strong enough to manage such a thing."

"…'It'? You called her 'it'?"

"Indeed. I'm sure the environment she was raised in and old customs play their part, but that girl is no good. Unable to stand alone even for a moment, weak, fragile, a small child. Watching her driven to act by guilt and self-reproach is so pitiably earnest it stirs something close to contempt."

Subaru was struck speechless by Roswaal's flat, dispassionate assessment of Emilia—unlike anything he'd heard from him before.
Earnest, hardworking, kind to a fault, doing her best—that was the Emilia Subaru knew. Right now her luck was bad, and she hadn't yet found an answer to the Trial, but Subaru believed that given time, she would surely overcome her past and possessed the strength to lead the Sanctuary to its release.
Still, the reason Subaru had thought to take on the Trial in Emilia's place regardless was that they couldn't secure the time it would take, and because he couldn't bear to watch her get hurt.
It was never, under any circumstance, because he'd given up and resigned himself to Emilia being unable to break through the Trial.

"And yet you say Emilia can't do it… then why, why are you even here!"

"Because you exist. With your presence, even a powerless, feeble Half-Elf can aim for the throne. No—she can take the throne. Naturally. Every path that leads elsewhere, you will eliminate, and you will see her wish through to completion. You possess exactly that much power. If Emilia-sama has any value, it's that she's acquired you—the strongest card there is."

"Me… the strongest… card…?"

Reeling from Roswaal's dizzying barrage of an argument, and from being called the strongest with words that sounded like a pipe dream, Subaru was thrown into confusion.
It was an adjective utterly foreign to the powerless person he knew himself to be. And more than that, Roswaal's words were so insulting to the very existence that was Emilia that Subaru couldn't let it pass.

"Don't screw with me! Do you have any idea how hard Emilia's tried, how much she's thought things through, how much she's suffered just to keep challenging that Trial! Having her worst memories dug back up, and still she—she's desperately doing her best! And you—!"

"If no result comes of it, then it's all meaningless. And that lack of a result—you, far more than someone like me, understand it perfectly well, don't you? If Emilia-sama's efforts had actually borne fruit, there'd be no reason for you to have come back here at a-a-all."

"————ッ!"

Even flaring up in fury, raising his voice, did nothing against Roswaal, whose composure never wavered. If anything, it was Subaru whose words got choked off, as if cold water had been dashed over his overheated thoughts.
The fact was, Roswaal's argument pointed at a piece of reality.

As far as Subaru had seen, there wasn't a single loop where Emilia had overcome the Trial—not even the First Trial. She kept challenging it with all her heart, but the wall of her past stood in the way of every attempt, breaking and wearing down her spirit each time.
Unable to lean on Puck, the one she could trust from the bottom of her heart, Emilia, worn thin, would eventually mistake her growing dependence on Subaru for love, and break.

Because he knew that future, all Subaru could offer against Roswaal's words was an emotional rebuttal. But he couldn't just silently let words that insulted Emilia slide, either.

Roswaal, looking down on Emilia, pinning excessive hope on Subaru's Return by Death—how could he knock that arrogance down a peg? The moment it came to him, Subaru was already shouting.

"I get your point loud and clear! But! Your scheme's not going to pan out!"

"Oh? And how is tha-a-at…"

"You want me to break through the Trial in Emilia's place, right? Well—Echidna stripped me of my qualification to take the Trial! Whatever result you're hoping for, you're not getting it out of me anymore! Too bad for you—!"

Hand on his chest, Subaru declared it loudly to the composed-faced Roswaal.
It was a painful blow to Subaru too, but it should have been a devastating one to Roswaal's own scheme as well. Even Roswaal, Subaru figured, couldn't stay unshaken by this—and he was about to flash a nasty grin at that thought when—

"—Stripped… of your qualification?"

The sound that slipped out, murmured under his breath, was so weak and hollow that Subaru was slow to realize it had come from the Roswaal in front of him.

There, propped against the bed before his eyes, Roswaal went rigid at Subaru's words. His mismatched eyes flew wide, frozen, and his lips trembled faintly as he stared at Subaru.
His usual composed bearing, that air of detachment as if he'd seen through everything, that breezy, cunning, unnerving edge—all of it seemed to have peeled away at once.

"What… do you mean…"

"What do I mean, nothing—it's exactly what I said."

Addressed in that hoarse voice, Subaru's own voice cracked despite himself.
Overwhelmed by a tone that sounded nothing like the Roswaal he knew, Subaru wet his parched throat with a swallow of saliva.

"Echidna took my qualification away. Right now I don't have command over the Clones, let alone anything else—just trying to enter the tomb makes my head spin and drops me to the ground. …I'm in the same position as you now—trying to get in, and getting refused."

"Wh—why… no, how did that happen. You're supposed to take the Trial inside the tomb and… if not, then the Sanctuary's release, her true wish, would—"

Hand at his mouth, Roswaal muttered hollowly, as if he'd seen something he couldn't believe.
At this unexpectedly intense reaction, Subaru realized it had landed with more than the effect of mere retaliation, and lost his own words. Roswaal's shaken state—it wasn't just something Subaru hadn't seen this loop, it was something he'd never seen since first meeting the man, period, and Subaru couldn't tell what about this exchange could have dealt him such a shock.
Still, Subaru swallowed and pressed on.

"Does your Gospel say something in there about me breaking through the Trial?"

"————"

"I know that if things don't go the way it's written, you're ready to throw the whole thing away. So if you've been assuming, deciding, that breaking through the Sanctuary is my role… that's not happening anymore."

Just as the breakthrough of the First Trial had carried over, just as the witches had carried shared memory across Return by Death, that castle of the Witch of Greed had existed outside the world's usual laws.
Even through a Return by Death, the memories and feelings exchanged in that castle never faded. That was precisely why Subaru had felt something like salvation in that place, why he'd come to hold no small amount of goodwill and expectation toward Echidna. —And that was why he understood, now.

Even if he died right now and returned to the tomb, the qualification wouldn't come back to him.
Recovering what was lost required Echidna's permission, and to get Echidna's permission he'd need to enter the tomb—and without the qualification, he couldn't enter the tomb.
—In other words, right now, Subaru's path to challenging the Trial in the tomb was completely closed off.

"Is there… a way to get the qualification back…"

"If there is, you'd know better than me. If you say you don't know, then I don't either."

Even as he answered Roswaal's faint, thin voice, Subaru privately recognized the possibility.
Echidna, most likely, was even now watching Subaru's struggles and torment from within the tomb. Stripping his qualification was probably her way of getting back at him for refusing her hand—a way of watching to see what Subaru, having taken Satella's hand instead, could actually accomplish.
And if, in the course of that, Subaru piled up enough failures that he came crawling back to Echidna in tears, with no moves left to make—that witch would probably extend her hand to him again.

—But if he took the hand Echidna offered next, it would mean throwing away everything: the words he'd spoken to Satella, and the feelings he held in his heart at this very moment.

An ending where he delivered Emilia to the best future and then simply expired.
Even so, that might be somewhat better than an ending where he shattered somewhere along the way.

"What about your redo—going back to before the qualification was stripped…?"

"Seems like you're misunderstanding something—it's not that all-powerful a power. It's not something I can use casually enough to go back without a price… and besides, the point I'd go back to is already too late. If I did go back, I'd return to after it was stripped. Not being able to enter the tomb wouldn't change."

"I see… so that's how it is…"

Roswaal's voice, answering Subaru, was feeble, and to Subaru it looked as though the man had aged all at once, right before his eyes.
Until now, Roswaal had always carried about him not youth exactly, but a certain ageless, indeterminate quality. Now, with his shoulders slumped small like this, even that air had vanished.
What remained was a face that looked all too human—the face of someone in anguish over a long, long-nurtured obsession being blocked, just out of his reach.

Seeing that expression, one his clownish makeup couldn't hide even now, Subaru, for the first time, felt he could sense that Roswaal was the same kind of being as himself, a fellow human.
Though, of course, that recognition solved nothing, and was probably nothing but unwelcome to Roswaal himself.

"I'm stuck too, same as you, Roswaal. I think you and I need to sit down and talk this through properly, find a way out."

"————"

"Getting the result that lines up with that Gospel of yours might be tough now, but the thing can't just end there—there's got to be more written than just that. If we just follow the broad strokes… I know that might not satisfy you, but if there's some room for compromise…"

"…wasn't enough."

"—Huh?"

Offering a compromise, trying to use words to wring some concession out of Roswaal, Subaru pressed on. But Roswaal, his eyes hollow as if he hadn't heard a word Subaru said, murmured something under his breath. Startled by the sound of it, Subaru's mouth fell open, and he took a step forward.
Wondering what Roswaal had just said—whether he'd misheard.
And then, as Subaru stepped closer, Roswaal's murmur crept into his ear. It was—

"—That my method of driving you wasn't enough."

"What did you say?"

"I don't know the particulars, but for Echidna to have changed the rules like that, there must have been some corresponding exchange between the two of you. Under normal circumstances, before a rift like that could ever open between you and her, you should have hardened your resolve and gone to the Trial… yes. I fell short."

"————"

"If I had driven you harder… shown you, made you understand, that reaching for this and that both means dropping what matters most… none of this would have—"

"Wait. Roswaal. Wait."

Sensing that whatever Roswaal was about to say was something decisive, Subaru found himself hesitating to hear the words he knew he had to hear.
If he heard what came next, he was sure he wouldn't be able to keep standing here.

He had a certainty about it.
No—rather, it was something he'd suspected before, a part of him that had wondered if this might be the case.
And yet, even so, the reason he'd never given that nagging suspicion form was partly that his mind had refused to consider it, telling himself there was no reason for such a thing—and partly, however faintly, a trust in Roswaal.

That trust was now, decisively, about to be severed by the words about to leave his mouth.
Before that happened, Subaru had to say something. And yet, no matter where he searched inside himself, he couldn't find the words, and time flowed on, mercilessly.

"Knowing full well what Garfiel's nature was, and still calling you and Emilia-sama to the Sanctuary—that too."

"————"

"Knowing full well how terrible the Trial is, and still letting Emilia-sama challenge it, showing you her wounded state to spur you into action—that too…"

"Wait, wait, please. Wa—"

And then—

"That through losing something precious, somewhere beyond your reach, you would be completed—that too… all of it, still, wasn't enough."

—Roswaal was confessing that it was he himself who had pulled the trigger on the mansion's massacre.
