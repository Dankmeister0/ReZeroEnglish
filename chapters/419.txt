Arc 6 Chapter 17: "King of the Sand Sea"

By unanimous vote, Team "Non-Combatants" decided to take the left path at the fork.

It was obvious that taking the right path would mean being swallowed by miasma, breeding discord that could escalate into bloodshed. Given that, choosing the left path was the natural, unmistakable choice.
For Subaru, it was a question with an answer already worked out. But then, if the left path wasn't necessarily the safe option either, whoever had set this problem was being needlessly cruel.

If the right path was a psychological trap, then what waited on the left was a physical one.
That grotesque, flame-wreathed Witchbeast — the "centaur" — was an encounter Subaru would avoid if at all possible.

It wasn't just the obvious gap in fighting power — the fact that they'd stand little chance even if they fought. The real reason was that, in his current state of mind, Subaru had no confidence he could genuinely cooperate with Ram and Anastasia if they ran into the Witchbeast.

"Actually, I saw this insane Witchbeast from a distance out on the dunes. Body like Patrasche's, but from the neck up it's a human torso, and right across the chest and stomach of that torso there's this huge mouth. And where the head should be on the human part, there's this big horn growing instead..."

"Ehh, what is that... that's so gross..."

"Frankly, I'm repulsed."

Following the pattern from their last Witchbeast conversation, Subaru had segued naturally into explaining the centaur's ecology. The two women's disgusted reactions — three, counting Patrasche — were exactly as expected.
Not that Subaru enjoyed explaining it. But as long as they kept heading down the left path, he had to assume there was a seventy-percent chance they couldn't avoid running into the thing.

"I figured you'd be put off, but the point is the thing looks seriously dangerous. I'm just calling it a centaur for lack of anything better, but it's not only the look that's gross — the mane growing down its back was blazing away like crazy... it looked ridiculously strong. Putting it mildly, I don't think we could win."

"Why would you spot a Witchbeast like that and just leave it alone? Do you want to die?"

"Ah?"

He'd tried to keep his emotions level while he explained, but Ram's usual flippant remark sent anger surging up in him immediately. Who did she think she was, when he'd gone out of his way to warn them?
Always looking down on people so high-and-mighty — Subaru felt his simmering resentment starting to curdle into hatred, and he took a deep breath. He fought hard to smother the flare of emotion and calm himself down.

"Damn it... this place is the worst."

"How troublesome. Ram thinks she's being relatively careful with her words, too."

"If that was you being careful, it's not doing a damn bit of good."

Toward Subaru, unable to handle his own emotions, Ram's voice and gaze took on a note of sympathy.
Except that attitude made him feel pitied, which only irritated him further — it had to be said, at this point, everything backfired.

"It's easy to just say it's the miasma's influence... but whether that's really all there is to it, that's the scary part, isn't it. After all, Ram-san and I aren't being affected."

"What's that supposed to mean? If you're thinking 'I'll be fine, it won't happen to me,' you'd better drop that rose-tinted nonsense right now. Scratch the surface and you and I are the same kind of human."

"That's a prickly way to put it. It's not like I have that much confidence in myself either. Judgment, decisiveness, and the strength of one's mind and body are two different things, y'know. ...It's just, pinning everything on how scary the miasma is — that seems a bit reckless, don't you think."

In her roundabout way, Anastasia offered a critical word about Subaru's current state.
Her words were reasonable enough, but their vagueness irritated him. Walking at the head of the group, treading the sand of the passage underfoot, Subaru signaled with his attitude for her to get to the point.
Taking the hint, Anastasia gave a small cough, and continued,

"About the miasma... I'm not exactly an expert myself, so forgive me if this is just secondhand knowledge, okay?"

"The only ones who'd really know the details would be the Witch Cult, I'd think."

"True enough, but there are also folks in this world who go out of their way to poke at the things everyone else finds distasteful. Like, say, some oddball who goes around researching the miasma. My knowledge is, well, hearsay of hearsay of hearsay — take it with a grain of salt."

What Anastasia meant, presumably, was something like a Witch researcher.
It had an unexpected ring to it, but thinking it over, assuming such a thing couldn't possibly exist would be strange too. This world surely had people who functioned as researchers, and it would be unreasonable to expect every last one of them to be so rational, so obedient to the world's conventions, that they'd all steer clear of forbidden inquiry.
In any world, in any age, there would always be some embodiment of unconventional curiosity.

"Now, as for where this secondhand knowledge of mine starts — first, what is 'miasma,' exactly?"

"That's... tainted mana, given off by Witches and Witchbeasts, isn't it? I've also heard that Witch Cultists give off the same thing."

"Ram-san's answer is the common wisdom. The miasma is something the Witch gives off, and the Witchbeasts she creates leak the same stuff... but, have you heard this one?"

"—"

"The story that Witch Cultists and Witchbeasts actually hate each other's guts."

Anastasia = Eridna spoke fluently, warming to the topic.
Given who had made her, maybe she simply enjoyed showing off her knowledge. Anastasia's veneer was peeling back, letting the true nature of the white fox beneath show through.

Contrary to Subaru's private thoughts, Ram's eyes went wide at Anastasia's words.
Witch Cultists and Witchbeasts were both generally thought of as allies of the Witch of Envy. That the two were actually on hostile terms might well come as a shock.

"That's hard to believe on the face of it... but is it actually true?"

"Unfortunately, it's not like I've got any Witch Cultist or Witchbeast acquaintances myself, so I couldn't tell you for sure. That's why I said up front it's just hearsay. But, if it turned out to be true — wouldn't you find that interesting?"

"Interesting...?"

Subaru reacted, feeling that a word had just come out that had no business being used here. At his reaction, Anastasia gave a short nod. "Right."

"Everyone in the world thinks the 'Witch,' the Witch Cultists, and the Witchbeasts are all one big happy family, right? And yet, nobody knows that the two things beneath the Witch actually despise each other. A misunderstanding like that's been getting passed around for four hundred years... and not a soul knows the truth of it."

"That's... true enough..."

"Sorry, this is hearsay too, but apparently there's a lot of misunderstanding about the miasma as well. For instance, the relationship between Witchbeasts and miasma is supposedly the exact opposite of what's commonly known — Witchbeasts actually hate the miasma. And deep down, they hate the Witch too, so the story goes."

"—Even so, surely that's going too far."

Drawn into Anastasia's story, Ram kept piling on questions.
Behind their exchange, Subaru understood that Anastasia = Eridna's words were built out of elements he couldn't really deny.

That the relationship between Witch and Witchbeast was actually hostile, that Witchbeasts loathed the Witch — for Subaru, who had used the Witch's Lingering Scent so many times before, that possibility rang very true. Otherwise, why would Witchbeasts seethe with such hatred, chasing after Subaru with such frantic desperation whenever he trailed the Witch's Scent?

On top of the Witchbeast incident right after he'd first been summoned to this world, he'd even used the Lingering Scent to bring down the White Whale. With this much evidence piled up, it was impossible to ignore the connection between the Witch's Lingering Scent and the miasma.

—The "Witch's Lingering Scent" clinging to Subaru must be the miasma itself.

"No way every single Witchbeast has a yandere streak like Petelgeuse, right? Can't forgive somebody who isn't the Witch smelling like the Witch — like catching the scent of another woman on someone."

That was a horrifying enough image in its own right, but the earlier possibility made more sense as an explanation.
In other words, Witchbeasts hated the Witch's miasma and viewed her with intense hostility.
To begin with, most people probably had no idea that the rumor claiming the Witch of Envy created the Witchbeasts was actually false.

It wasn't the Witch of Envy who created the Witchbeasts — it was the Witch of Gluttony.

Because that fact hadn't gotten around, nobody had noticed the relationship between Witchbeasts and the miasma either.
As for the source of Anastasia's knowledge — whether some researcher actually studying the Witch really existed, Subaru couldn't say, but if such a person did exist, they had a good eye for what mattered.

"...What a bald-faced farce."

Having thought that far, Subaru sighed at his own naivety, exasperated with himself.
Rather than doubting the existence of some researcher who might not even exist, it was far more natural to conclude that Anastasia = Eridna had simply produced knowledge she'd already possessed from the start.

Thinking that, a different kind of resentment gradually welled up in Subaru.

Why did Subaru have to go this far to conceal Anastasia = Eridna's circumstances? There was consideration for Julius and the Iron Fang, sure, that much he understood.
Julius, in particular, was currently stretched to his limit, having slipped from the world's memory.
Julius was trying his best to act as usual, but even he had blind spots he couldn't cover. Given that, unnecessary mental strain was something to be avoided.
It should be avoided — but then why did Subaru have to be the one shouldering that burden in someone else's place?

"—"

Every single one of them was so self-centered.
Why was it always Subaru who had to run himself ragged, cleaning up after people like that?
It was infuriating. Maddening. Maybe he should just spill everything, all of it. Anastasia's true nature, Subaru's Return by Death, all of it—

"...Barusu. Suddenly shoving your face into the sand is suspicious. Stop it."

"It's a voluntary defensive measure on my part, to keep relations from getting worse. Ptooey."

With his resentment about to hit its limit, Subaru had jammed his face into the sand wall before he could spit out an insult. The wall of sand was more crumbly than expected — he could probably have dug straight through it with his bare hands.
In exchange for that discovery, Subaru spat out the sand that had gotten in his mouth. He had thoughts about Ram's cold stare, but since this was a consequence of his own actions, he kept his objections locked away in his chest.

"Is Natsuki-kun's strange behavior also the miasma's influence... I wonder?"

"No, Barusu's behavior is just how he naturally is."

"It is not how I naturally am. It's secondary damage from the miasma's influence."

"Well, we can sort out the truth of that later — anyway, getting back on track... Witchbeasts actually hate the Witch. Given that, doesn't it explain why Witch Cultists and Witchbeasts don't get along?"

Deliberately using a bit of banter to create a pause before circling back to the main topic, Anastasia tilted her head. But Subaru's agreement couldn't quite catch up to her words. Instead, Ram nodded, and said,

"Whether or not they're on bad terms, I've never heard of Witch Cultists and Witchbeasts acting together. Then again, rumors about the Witch Cult don't spread much to begin with."

"Right... they're supposedly some secret organization operating in the shadows. Why is it I don't get that vibe from them at all?"

"It's because Barusu and Emilia-sama keep running into that lot far too often."

The Witch Cultists Subaru knew were, without exception, self-absorbed and desperate for attention.
Petelgeuse's activities could hardly be called quiet either, and the other Sin Archbishops had gone as far as seizing an entire major city and broadcasting it to boot.
What nerve would it take to call that a covert organization operating in the shadows?

"Oh, but wait. What about the White Whale? It cooperated with the Witch Cult... though now that I think about it, that's a bit shaky, but there were signs of it."

"Who knows? Even if it's true the Witchbeasts hate the Witch Cult, whether the Witch Cult hates Witchbeasts back, I really couldn't say... well, I don't much care if I turn out to be wrong, either."

"You're the one who brought it up in the first place..."

Anastasia offered a halfhearted rebuttal to Subaru's objection, then abandoned her own theory without a fight. That lack of attachment made it seem plausible that it really was secondhand opinion after all.
Still, having used up the topic as conversational material, Anastasia closed one eye at the end and said,

"In the end, it's all just a story about how nobody really understands the miasma. Like Ram-san said, there's the idea that it's tainted mana... but then, is there anyone who could actually explain what it means for mana to be tainted in the first place?"

"—"

"No matter whose body it passes through, mana is mana. You can change its properties for a purpose through Magic or magic-stone crafting, but coloring mana itself — that's not something anyone can do."

"That's..."

"So why is it only the Witch of Envy who can taint mana, I wonder? And the Witch Cultists who've adapted to that tainted mana — just what are they for, anyway?"

At Anastasia's rapid-fire questions, Ram fell silent, an unusual thing for her.
It was rare for her to find no rebuttal and choose silence instead. At the same time, Subaru found himself tasting déjà vu and distaste in Anastasia's manner of speaking.

The vagueness of it all, the way she left every conclusion hanging in the air.
The whole evasive, smoke-and-mirrors attitude overlapped with Echidna in his mind, and it left him with a bad taste.

"Oh-oh-oh, seems like we let the idle chatter run on a bit too long, hm."

Setting aside Ram's silence and Subaru's private musings, Anastasia said this abruptly.
Her tone shifted completely from a moment before, and the sudden change of subject left the other two behind. Leaving them there, Anastasia raised the lantern in her hand and lit up the passage ahead.
Following her lead, Subaru stopped as well, and raising his own lantern, he noticed—

"—"

Ahead, the passage curved gently to the left, and a faint breeze was blowing through.
The sand walls looked the same as they had everywhere else, but that sudden bend, and the smell of something "scorched" mixed into the drifting breeze, struck a violent chord of recognition.

"That's a burnt smell. Like meat being cooked."

Ram offered that curt observation on the faintly heated breeze.
The smell of blackened meat, cooked well past done, was drifting out of the depths of the passage.
That this scent was proof someone was busy cooking, that friendly contact might be possible — that had been a well-thought-out notion, ignorant as it was, back then. If anything, he must have been far crazier back then than he was now.

"Do you think there's a chance it's Emilia-sama and the others, carelessly lighting a fire while they rest?"

"I agree Emilia's the type to carelessly light a fire, but after telling you about the centaur myself, there's no room for me to imagine that. I'd like to believe your head's not full of flowers either, y'know?"

"I'll look forward to hearing how you excuse that abuse once the miasma wears off."

He'd answered Ram's question with more spite than necessary, and she'd struck right back with venom of her own.
Snorting at that, Subaru reminded himself that his hostility was aimed at the wrong target.

There was no longer any need for doubt at this point.
Beyond this scorched-smelling breeze lay a great cavern, and in it waited that blasphemous, flame-wreathed Witchbeast.

The time it had taken to get here was probably somewhat shorter than the loop before last. Even so, if they were bound to run into it, that place was likely the Witchbeast's den.
He couldn't hold out the faint hope that it would obligingly relocate somewhere else. If so, then—

"We have no choice but to kill it and pass through, huh?"

"Ram thinks it would be more constructive to come up with a plan that works with the forces we actually have."

"If I play my trump card, I could fight it well enough... but I'd rather keep whittling myself down as a last resort, if I can help it. Some things you can't take back, y'know."

Both Ram and Anastasia objected together to Subaru's dangerous proposal.
It irritated him to be shot down, but for the moment, Subaru had no intention of pushing his own idea either.
In truth, it was undeniably a reckless plan. Team "Non-Combatants" hadn't taken that name for show. Trading blows with the Witchbeast was an option he ought to write off as too rash to consider.

"That said, we can't just turn back and take the right path instead."

"Having watched Barusu's hundred-faced act up to now, I don't think you've got the nerve to head down the right path."

"But then we're stuck at a dead end, aren't we. It's not like we're saintly enough to just head back to the starting point and wait for everyone else to come find us, right?"

Setting Ram's banter aside, Anastasia had a point. If they were going to turn back out of fear of the Witchbeast at this stage, Subaru would have talked his way into staying behind at the start instead.
Since he hadn't done that, his only conclusion was that they had to get past the Witchbeast.
And a method for doing that wasn't entirely beyond reach.

"First, what we need is information on our opponent. If my guess is right, there's a chance."

Ahead of the other two, still uncertain of their course, Subaru laid out his conclusion and said this.

* * *

—About the ecology of the Witchbeast centaur, Subaru knew next to nothing, practically speaking.

After all, their entire relationship consisted of him being burned to death within a dozen-odd seconds of the encounter.
All that had been carved painfully into his soul as lived experience was a visceral revulsion at its grotesque appearance, and the sheer terror of its firepower.
By any conventional way of thinking, it was a death that could fairly be called a waste.

But it wouldn't do for anyone to think they'd taken the measure of Natsuki Subaru by that alone.
When it came to sudden "death," Natsuki Subaru was a hardened veteran of a hundred battles. He had cultivated enough experience by now that he no longer let the phenomenon called "death" end simply at death.

"First, what I should focus on is that centaur's visuals."

An ugly appearance, impossible to forget once seen.
He'd only laid eyes on it for a dozen-odd seconds, but that very strangeness had burned itself vividly into memory, and now it was helping his analysis along.

A horse's lower half, a human torso, a head transformed into a horn, and a gigantic mouth opened in the torso.
Every part of it looked like some clumsy clay figure a child had thrown together at random, but if he studied the memory without looking away, there was something he noticed.

"Plainly put — it has no eyes."

There was no head where a head should ordinarily be. As a result, there was no doubting that no organ governing sight could be found anywhere on that Witchbeast's body.
Or perhaps this was a drawback — a degeneration — that came from operating underground in this sand sea, a place with no light source to speak of without a lantern.

"Something like a mole, then. Adapting to life underground so thoroughly it lost its eyesight."

Or maybe it had never had eyes to begin with — that much wasn't clear — but at the very least, that Witchbeast probably didn't rely on sight to operate.
Back then, the Witchbeast hadn't seemed to notice Subaru's presence by the light of the lantern. What had alerted it to him was, without a doubt, the sound and presence Subaru himself had carelessly given off.
In other words, whatever that Witchbeast had developed in place of eyes was either smell or hearing.

"I've heard moles have good ears, but their sense of smell is supposed to be terrible in exchange."

Not unlike Anastasia earlier, this too was undeniably secondhand knowledge.
Still, it was a far better aid to Subaru's reasoning than clinging to some baseless idea. Deciding the centaur's strength was its hearing, Subaru resolved to act on that assumption.
And so—

"—"

Without a word, Subaru raised his arm and brought it down as quietly, as soundlessly, as he possibly could.
The instant his foot touched the sand, he took the utmost care that no grinding of grains would give him away, and from the tip of his lowered arm, he flung one of the water containers he'd packed in his emergency bag.
It flew true, straight to the edge of the great cavern — and drew the attention of the Witchbeast, blazing red in the center of the space, toward the point where it landed.

"—!!"

When the water container hit the sand with a light thud, the centaur reacted dramatically to the sound. Whipping its burning mane about, the Witchbeast bounded on its horse's body straight toward the source of the sound and slammed into it with a furious, unhesitating body blow.

"—!!"

Sand billowed up violently, and sparks scattered flickering through the cavern.
Whipped about by its own frenzy, the centaur bounded around, grinding the fangs inside its gaping maw together, letting out a grating roar that sounded like countless wailing infants.
Then, still at it, it stomped again and again on the flame-wrapped, crumpled water container, smashing it until it lost all shape, letting more flame spill from its mane onto the sand. Only once everything but the dry sand had been scorched away did the Witchbeast finally stop, seemingly satisfied.

Satisfied, it seemed.
Watching it out of the corner of his eye, Subaru gave the cord tied to his waist a shake, signaling to move. Amid a space filled with the Witchbeast's shrill cries, the careful sound of feet on sand was broken into fragments.
And so, taking the utmost care, step by step, they advanced—

"—"

He pulled the cord with the meaning "stop" behind it, halting the advancing Earth Dragon's feet.
The dragon, who shouldn't have understood human speech, followed the signal with astonishing faithfulness, planting her feet slowly and cautiously into the sand in a manner that belied her massive frame.

—Inside a cavern painted solid in the color of darkness, Subaru and the others pressed on in a march of life and death.

The cavern's size was, roughly, about that of a school gymnasium, perhaps.
In a space littered here and there with the charred corpses of creatures the Witchbeast had burned, Subaru and the others crossed along its edge, holding their footsteps and their breath, trying to slip past the Witchbeast unnoticed.

"—"

It wasn't quite so silent as to be called hushed.
The centaur's absurdly large mouth kept up a ragged breathing, a sound closer to the unglamorous hiss of air leaking from a balloon.
A Witchbeast ugly not only in appearance but in its very ecology — still, right now, that simple, straightforward nature was a blessing. Thanks to it, a plan to avoid fighting altogether had come together.

"—"

Once more, Subaru raised another water flask he held in his hand and threw it behind the centaur. The single-minded Witchbeast reacted to the sound and launched another furious attack on the empty flask.
Flame and infant wails filled the space, the flask burst under the tremendous firepower, and with a dry crack it transformed into black char.
And in the meantime, Subaru and the others kept advancing, successfully closing the distance to the passage.

—Having observed the centaur's ecology, the conclusion Subaru had reached was this simple diversion.

Reasoning from its lack of eyesight, Subaru had judged that the centaur relied on hearing. Leaving Ram and Anastasia behind, he'd drawn the Witchbeast's attention with the same trick of throwing objects, and repeating it several times had given him certainty.
The centaur was a Witchbeast that found prey by sound, and moreover, a creature simple-minded enough to fall for the same trick again and again.

Once he'd confirmed that, everything else was simple.
Gather up material for diversions, have Ram use the wind to read out the position of the passage they'd flee into once they'd slipped past the Witchbeast. After that, all that remained was to carefully brief Patrasche on moving stealthily, steel his heart against tension and anxiety, and step into the gambit.

"—"

In fact, the plan Subaru had drawn up was working almost anticlimactically well.
They'd already cleared roughly half the distance from the cavern to the passage, and despite the tension, the time spent hadn't actually amounted to much. A Witchbeast with no capacity to learn could very plausibly be gotten past by repeating the same trick over and over.

"—"

Of course, the lantern light was doused, and the only thing clearly visible to Subaru and the others' eyes was the centaur's burning mane, carrying on in a great fit off in the distance.
Presumably, a Witchbeast that didn't rely on sight wouldn't notice the lantern's light regardless, but even so, keeping the number of stimulating factors to a minimum was the sort of small, cautious judgment call of an ordinary citizen.

"—"

He felt the cord being tugged, and Subaru's consciousness, which had drifted into thought, was pulled back.
Subaru, carrying out the diversion, held two cords — one connected to Patrasche, the other to Ram, who rode the dragon. The signals to Patrasche were "advance" and "stop," but the connection with Ram had no particular codified meaning to it. It was more like the two of them simply calling out to each other.

Still, this connection through a cord — no faces met, no words exchanged, only awareness passed along — was, surprisingly, a source of calm for Subaru right now.
This way, at least for the moment, he could avoid feeling ill will toward either Ram or Anastasia. Not having to face someone was this much of a relief — that was the state of his heart.

At the same time, it wasn't as though his heart, finding peace in that solitude, was free of unease.
It's the miasma's influence, Subaru told himself, half-convincingly — but Anastasia's earlier story had left large parts of just what "miasma" even was murky. Whether leaving this place would even cure it was, in fact, doubtful now.

If it didn't get cured — if he ended up unable to keep himself from feeling this same loathing even toward Emilia, even toward Beatrice—

"—!?"

The cord was suddenly yanked hard, and Subaru stumbled forward on the spot. It was the cord connected to Patrasche, a forceful decision handed down from the dragon herself to stop.
Reflexively lifting his face to see what was going on—

"—ugh"

A ball of hurled flame passed just a step in front of Subaru.
It was a fireball about the size of a soccer ball, but it flew scattering waves of heat around it, slammed straight into the sand wall several meters ahead, and burst there with a violent roar.
Scorched by the swelling blast of heat against his chilled body, Subaru barely managed to choke back a scream in his throat.

If Patrasche hadn't called him to a stop, he'd have taken a direct hit without question.
Whether it packed enough power to kill him, he didn't know, but it was undeniably firepower he couldn't have escaped without injury. Gritting his teeth at the fact he'd narrowly escaped death, a shiver ran up Subaru's spine at the same time.

Why had the fireball been thrown toward Subaru?

"—"

Reflexively, Subaru turned to look behind him.
Caught by the diversion, the centaur should still have been over on the far side of the cavern, playing with the water flask. But now, the Witchbeast had turned its horn-shaped head this way, growling low.
As if it were certain that Subaru and the others were right here.

"—"

There's no way, Subaru shook his head.
He unhooked another flask for throwing from his waist and settled it into his hand, readying another diversion. The cord connecting him to Ram was calling to him insistently, but he ignored it.
Right now, prying the Witchbeast's attention away from them was the first priority.

He swung his arm, and the flask arced through the air, veering off far to the left of the centaur before falling into the sand. Naturally, the Witchbeast's attention shot toward it, and the ungainly beast leaped at the transparent diversion.
Flame again, an infant's wail echoing out again. And in the cavern where the shrill sound reverberated, Subaru signaled Patrasche to hurry their advance toward the passage.
Repeat, and repeat, doing nothing but the fixed routine.
That alone should have been enough to break through, but—

"—!"

Yet again, a fireball hurled by the centaur passed right by Subaru as he walked.
It came even closer than before, an accurate strike that nearly grazed Subaru's skin. Holding his breath on reflex, Subaru was buffeted by the wave of heat from the fireball's explosion.

"—"

Lit by the fresh orange glow that had sprung up in front of him, Subaru locked eyes with the Witchbeast standing distant. Their eyes didn't actually meet — calling it a staring match would be a misnomer.
Even so, the Witchbeast's attention was clearly turned this way. A Witchbeast with no way of telling them apart except by sound — why, amid its own cries, would it—

"Its own... cries..."

"Barusu, the echo—"

Inside Subaru, question and answer connected at last, and Ram's call over the tugging cord finally became sound.
And the instant the two of them arrived at the same answer, the centaur kicked off the sand.

Hooves kicked at the sandy ground, and the Witchbeast's towering frame leapt lightly through the cold air. The firepower of its blazing mane grew as it ran, surging straight toward Subaru and the others all at once.
The human torso, connected to the horse's body, raised an arm and tore a fistful of its own burning mane away. In the Witchbeast's palm, that turned into a fireball, and in the blink of an eye, an explosive round of flame was complete.

"—! Run, run, run, run!!"

At this point, a clash with the centaur was no longer avoidable.
Throwing away all pretense of stealth, Subaru slapped Patrasche's hindquarters and ordered her to bolt for the passage at once.
Right now, Subaru and the others were positioned right in the middle of the cavern — far in distance from both the original passage and the one ahead, in a position so advantageous to the Witchbeast it made him wonder if they'd been played.

"Were we being toyed with...!?"

"—!"

Subaru's mind was frozen with shock, but the Witchbeast paid it no mind. The centaur hurled the fireballs it had produced, blasting the sand as if toying with the fleeing Subaru and the others.
Their headlong dash for the passage had been read by the thing as well. And running in zigzags to confuse it wouldn't help either — its hearing, which picked up on sound, was more accurate than any little trick they could throw at it.

"Whoa! Wah!"

Fireballs came flying in one after another, grazing his lowered head.
As if toying with the fleeing, panicking Subaru, the sightless Witchbeast ran a wide circle around him, pounding him with explosive round after explosive round, blowing sand everywhere.

"Gah—!?"

The ground beneath his feet burst, and Subaru's body was tossed lightly about by the wave of heat and sent flying.
He shielded his face with both arms on reflex, but the surging blast of hot wind invaded his respiratory tract, faintly scorching his nasal cavity and throat. Breathing hurt, and his mucous membranes melted enough that his sense of smell died, temporarily.

Rolling around amid the frenzy of agony that had erupted at the center of his face, Subaru lifted his tear-filled eyes.
The mouth in the centaur's torso opened wide, and the fang-filled maw let out a grating sound that seemed to be laughing. No — it was laughing.

At the human, a weakling defeated even in a battle of wits against the Witchbeast, now being toyed with by sheer force.

"Damn it, gah...!"

Hatred welled up, giving Subaru the strength to rise.
Compared to unfounded suspicion or needless discord, how much healthier was this hostility directed at a hostile pest before him. Swallowed by something pitch-black, as if his passions had been boiled down into pure sediment, Subaru scoffed at the conceited thinking of the Witchbeast lording its victory.

If it thought it had won, that was a joke.
A miasma-hating Witchbeast, thinking it could beat Natsuki Subaru — who did it think it was?

"—"

He put a hand to his waist and drew his whip.
Settling the feel of the handle into his grip, Subaru flicked the tip lightly against the sand, and the centaur stopped its guffawing, listening with apparent interest to the sound of the whip cutting the air.

Probably, it was a sound the Witchbeast had never heard before.
But that wasn't Subaru's real intention. The whip was, ultimately, just a diversion.

"—Invisible Providence."

The pitch-black emotion coiled in Subaru's chest, sparked by the Witchbeast's outrages.
He gave it direction, converting it into force meant to tear the centaur's limbs from its body. It was a one-trick use, the kind of thing a fool clings to as his only skill, but that was fine. It was a pattern that worked on anyone.

"—"

He whirled the whip overhead, letting it sing through the air at high speed.
So as not to be noticed by the Witchbeast, absorbed in curiosity at the unfamiliar sound, the invisible hand slid through the darkness, crept into the Witchbeast's shadow, and set its aim on the horn atop the human torso.
Whether the vital organ lay in the human half or the horse's half, he couldn't say. And since the head had become a horn, whether a brain was even packed inside was a mystery too. Even so, there was no doubt some fatally important organ existed there. Guessing as much, he crushed the horn in his invisible palm.
If losing the horn made it listen to reason, he could just as well make it kill itself. That would be a lot more amusing, and—

"—!? Gi, ah, gah!?"

He'd thought that far, and it was the very instant he moved to carry out the punishment.
As Subaru fixed his gaze on the centaur and extended his "Unseen Hand" toward its head, an unimaginably violent shock ran through his own head. With a pain like his scalp being peeled and an awl driven directly into his skull, his eyes rolled white in an instant, and Subaru sank to his knees on the spot, foaming yellow at the mouth.

"Gah, aaah!? Gu, aghi!"

Still on his knees, he reached both hands to his head, striking his own temples to fight back against the sharp pain. Rubbing it, pressing on it, nothing eased the pain. To fight pain, he dealt himself a sharper, stronger blow. So he struck, struck, and struck again, and still the pain wasn't something he could overcome.
A thorn-riddled hell had sprung up inside his skull, seeming to writhe as it stabbed straight through his brain, and Subaru writhed in agony on the sand, biting into it without knowing why.

"It hurts! Aaagh! It hurts it hurts it hurts! It huuurts!!"

He screamed, as if coughing up blood.
With a mouthful of sand crammed in, grinding it between his back teeth, thrashing about so his throat wouldn't close up against the incomprehensible agony, Subaru fought the pain. He couldn't fight it. He was losing.

Naturally, Invisible Providence had vanished in an instant.
Dispersed, it could no longer interfere with the centaur at all. The Witchbeast, seemingly deflated by the anticlimax of Subaru's condition, set about turning him into a charred corpse with fireballs instead.

The great fireball it produced drove away the coolness of the space, and a small-scale blast of heat warmed the world.
It would, as it stood, reduce Natsuki Subaru to ash—

"—!"

—but the instant before that, a jet-black Earth Dragon lunged fiercely and bit clean through the Witchbeast's arm.

"—"

The dragon, her body's color blending into the dark, had crept up on the Witchbeast without a sound and struck a savage blow. Having lost an arm and its balance, the Witchbeast dropped the fireball it had been holding aloft.
As a result, the Witchbeast set off the heat of its own fireball right at its own feet, and was blasted away by the shockwave from point-blank range.

Caught in the blast, the Witchbeast was thrown, and the centaur toppled over, blood spilling from the wound on its arm. Paying it no mind, Patrasche, sprinting across the sand, clamped her jaws onto the clothing of the thrashing Subaru and immediately took off running to retreat.
Bitten around the waist and dangling, Subaru was swung side to side, tormented by poor circulation and an unrelenting headache, and looked back over his shoulder.

Behind Patrasche, the centaur was staggering back up.
He saw the wound on the human torso bubble up, and the left arm that had been bitten off regrow in an instant. The monstrous regenerative power worked on the other wounds too, sealing up one after another the injuries left across its body by the explosion just now, and within mere seconds the Witchbeast returned to full health.

With that, there was no longer anything to stand in its way.
The centaur let out a shrill cry at Patrasche for the sneak attack, accelerating to catch up with the fiercely running dragon, its mane blazing as it lunged.

Magnificent as Patrasche's running was, adapted to any terrain, it still fell one step short of the speed of a centaur that had made this its home ground. With the original difference in build as well, the Witchbeast drew level right alongside Patrasche, who still held Subaru in her jaws.
A blazing fireball formed in the Witchbeast's hand, and this time it stretched out lengthwise. Watching wide-eyed to see what would happen, he saw the fireball transform in the Witchbeast's grip into a long-handled weapon, and in the blink of an eye, what had been created was a spear wrapped in flame from tip to butt.

"—!!"

Hoisting the flaming spear, the centaur brought its tip down on Patrasche. The jet-black dragon ducked in time with the spear's arc — evading the blow at a height low enough to nearly bury herself in sand — and accelerated as if slipping through the narrowest of gaps.
But just as it looked like she'd dashed clean through, the Witchbeast's hoof kicked the dragon's flank from the side. The force sank in beneath her tough hide, and Patrasche let out a groan through clenched throat, as if her insides were being wrung. Even so, she didn't drop Subaru, caught in her fangs. The heat Subaru felt around his waist was blood, coughed up by Patrasche from her damaged organs.

That alone told him she'd taken a grievous wound.
And yet Patrasche wouldn't let go of Subaru, and Subaru himself had no room right now to worry over his beloved dragon's injury. All there was, was the endless headache — a torment that felt like it would go on forever.

"—El Fura!!"

"Jiwald—!"

With Patrasche's speed clearly flagging, the flaming spear's second strike came bearing down on her. But before it could reach the dragon's body, a diversion struck in from two directions simultaneously.
One was an invisible blade of wind; the other, a beam of blinding white, converging heat.

Both incantations came in voices Subaru recognized, though the particulars escaped him.
Still, he could tell both had struck the Witchbeast directly, punching a hole clean through its body and slicing diagonally through the human torso. — And that even that wound sealed shut again in the blink of an eye.

"Barusu...! Ugh, honestly, if you're dead, just say so!"

"That thing's a real pain... it's not just that it's a bad matchup for me, it's worse than that."

One girl's voice, its tone unchanged as ever, yet sounding somehow desperate.
The other girl's voice, despite the desperate circumstances, sounding oddly lacking in urgency.
Feeling his beloved dragon's labored breathing and the heat of her coughed-up blood against his skin, Subaru's consciousness was on the verge of slipping away entirely. If he had to be tormented by this much pain and suffering, he might as well just die—

"Don't you dare die, Barusu! Rem would cry!"

"—Oh"

Shouted right beside his ear, that voice reached his brain past even his pain.
But what that voice stirred up was an anger no less fierce than the hatred he'd felt for the Witchbeast.

When she'd forgotten.
When everyone, everyone, didn't even remember that girl.

—Don't talk about me and Rem like you understand anything.

"—Invisible Providenceee!!"

Releasing his emotions in a wave of pure anger, Subaru slammed his jet-black magic hand, with something close to blind rage, at the Witchbeast passing at the corner of his tear-blurred vision.
At once, a fresh wave of agony rose up, crushing his skull — but before it could swallow his consciousness whole, Subaru's "Unseen Hand" struck the Witchbeast's spear head-on and snapped it, landing one blow in return.
—But that was as far as such a feeble resistance could reach.

"—!!"

The reply to his angry counterattack was a savage blow born of even greater fury.
The centaur planted its front legs into the sandy ground, and using them as a pivot, wrenched its massive body around, launching its hind legs like a catapult.
The mineral-hard hooves, gaining weight and speed, exploded toward Subaru and the others — Patrasche, and presumably Ram and Anastasia nearby as well — scattering sand as they struck.

Leg strength enough to blow away part of the cavern exploded outward, and everyone was scattered helplessly by the violence of the sand. At last Subaru too slipped free of Patrasche's jaws, tumbling helplessly across the sand, crashing into the collapsed remains of some charred corpse thrown up by the shockwave.

"Ah, uh..."

A headache beyond words, and a body that had taken the centaur's kick.
Under the relentless assault of pain from both inside and outside his body, Subaru could no longer hold onto consciousness. Yet even as he lay there, rolling limply, he could tell the presence of "death" was closing in, thick and unmistakable.

Annihilation. Wipeout. A wasted death, a death in battle for nothing.
Such merciless words raced through his mind, and yet—

"—"

Even as his lungs forgot how to breathe, Subaru saw someone stand in front of him.
A small, slight silhouette. In a world starved of light, its appearance wasn't clear. But it was a shape he knew well enough to recognize at once. It was Ram. She was standing there, swaying unsteadily.
Arms spread wide, as if to shield Subaru.

—You idiot, it's impossible, it's pointless, stop it.

Even trying to call out to her that way, his throat was dead, unable to produce sound. As if it were stuffed with sand. —No, in fact, it was stuffed with sand. Because he'd swallowed sand like an idiot trying to kill the headache, Subaru couldn't manage a proper voice right now.

"...Wh-why..."

Subaru managed only to squeeze out a thin, whimpering voice.
He'd had so much resentment and anger coiled up inside him toward the people close to him — Ram and the others — and surely they'd felt it too.

"—Because Rem would cry."

To Subaru's words, Ram answered quietly, only that.
For the sake of a sister she shouldn't even remember, to protect that unremembered sister's beloved, Ram stood.
What could drive her that far, Subaru didn't understand.

Even without understanding, there was something he did understand.
At this rate, Ram would die. And Subaru would die too. That much couldn't be avoided.

"—"

The centaur let out a growl, forming two new blades of flame in its two arms. Or perhaps, since they didn't quite look like swords, they were meant to be hammers or axes.
Either way, they were two weapons wreathed in flame. With them, it looked ready to cut down Ram — so tiny compared to itself — and scorch Subaru to ash along with her.

"...Come. Come on. There's got to be... something."

With the approach of "death" right before his eyes, Subaru reached toward the depths of his pain.
Working against the pain, diving down into it, was close to an act of continuing to sink into himself. Seeking help from outside himself — from Ram, Patrasche, Anastasia — wasn't realistic.
So, though it might be dismissed as some kind of dream or delusion, Subaru sought a means within himself. This method, though obviously unrealistic, was still the more realistic option available to him.

Diving inward, reaching into his own thoroughly clouded body, pushing aside the writhing black obsessions, Subaru searched himself for a breakthrough. Not the "Unseen Hand," overused as it already was. Something else, something new, some way to get through this.
But Subaru's desperate search—

"—Ah"

—found nothing, and in front of him the Witchbeast raised both blades overhead.
The flames crossed above the Witchbeast's head, and after a moment's pause, were unleashed toward Ram. Scorching the very air, the approaching slash would mercilessly burn through the girl's slender body, blow her away, and reduce to black char everything — the path she'd walked, every feeling she'd carried — as though none of it had ever existed—

Envisioning that scene, Subaru let out a scream of pure helplessness—

"—"

—and in the next instant, a white light, unleashed at a terrifying speed, had already blown the Witchbeast's upper body clean away.

* * *

The Witchbeast's body, its flaming blades still raised, vanished from the world under that terrifying light.
It wasn't anything so gentle as being cut or crushed — it meant, literally, obliteration.

"—"

The centaur was a monster with a horse's body and a human torso joined where a head should be.
And the light had blown away everything from the chest up on that human portion. Naturally, that included the arms from the shoulders down, the horn standing in for a human head, and the fang-filled mouth gaping in its chest.

At the shock of it, the Witchbeast spilled blood soundlessly from its wound — and immediately after, the wound bubbled up, and the exact same superhuman regeneration as before took place.
Writhing flesh swelled and rose, reforming the lost human torso. A human body was instantly reconstructed, and already, explosive fireballs were forming in both its arms.

"—!!"

The centaur roared, and countless infant shrieks echoed through the cold space.
Wheeling around, the Witchbeast paid no mind at all to Subaru or Ram, and lunged head-on at the enemy who had blown away its upper body.
Generating a multitude of fireballs in its arms, the Witchbeast hurled them one after another as it charged, poised to make its own headlong assault under cover of the blazing heat. But—

"—!?"

Every single fireball the Witchbeast produced was intercepted head-on.
The one accomplishing this was a white light that pierced each fireball dead center with terrifying precision. Its speed was far beyond ordinary, and its power clearly outmatched the fireballs themselves.
Struck through their centers by that light, the balls of flame, dragged along by the force of the light, landed on the Witchbeast's own body — and just as it seemed to have become a hedgehog bristling with white light, flame exploded across the Witchbeast's entire body.

Its arms were blown away, its mouth torn off, the horse's-body portion scorched, and the centaur toppled over. Tumbling violently across the sandy ground, the Witchbeast let out a piercing scream of pure rage.
But its wounds writhed, and the Witchbeast didn't die. Gouged, blown apart, outmatched — as if to say, then simply increase its own strength, change its methods, evolve its way of killing.

"—"

Regenerating its lost parts, the Witchbeast's very appearance transformed.
The shape of its human portion changed — two arms became four, and from the mouth in its torso, sharp, elongated fangs now peeked out. Its horse's lower half sprouted more legs too, eight legs' worth of strength, simply doubling its power by the numbers.
Its scorched skin, moreover, changed into something glossy black and hard, the sort of thing that at a glance might look like armor.
And in each of its now-multiplied arms it took up a flaming sword, a spear, a hammer, an axe — the Witchbeast had, in a mere span of time, undergone an unbelievable evolution, reshaping itself for the sole purpose of facing the wielder of that white light.

"—"

Raising its now four front legs, the centaur let out a thunderous roar. Clacking together the hooves of its raised front legs with a shrill sound, it charged forward, gathering momentum.
Its form, combined with its massive frame, was the equal of a steel-armored freight train. Its weight and acceleration would easily turn anything struck directly into mincemeat, delivering a death gruesome to behold.
And with flame added as the finishing blow, whoever had shamed the Witchbeast would be utterly annihilated.

"—!!"

"—"

And so, in the instant after that tremendous surge of light, nothing remained there any longer.
The Witchbeast that had rampaged with such savage force — every last piece of it, down to scraps of flesh — had been blown away by the light entirely, cast off to somewhere not of this world.All that remained on the sand was the source of the countless beams of light unleashed to erase the Witchbeast — a single, slender white needle. "—"

Watching that in stunned amazement, Subaru had even forgotten his headache.
Before he knew it, a hot, slender body had thrown itself into his arms. It was Ram. He had no memory of it, but apparently, in that final moment, he'd pulled her body close to his own.
Not that it should have made any difference, and Ram, for her part, seemed to have lost consciousness.

Then, in Subaru's ear, he heard the sound of someone's feet treading on sand.
Slowly, slowly, it was undeniably coming this way, toward Subaru and the others.

Right at the very edge of that field of view, someone's foot came into sight.

"—"

Lifting his face, Subaru turned his eyes toward the owner of that foot — presumably, the source of the light.
Slowly raising his gaze, what appeared in his blurring vision was a human.

"—"

A woman.
A woman with a strange air about her.

The legs standing on the sand were boldly bare all the way to the thigh, and all that guarded her lower belly were shorts cut off at the barest minimum of length. Just above that, her waist and navel were exposed as well, and around her slender, narrow torso, only a strip of cloth was wound like a breastband to hide her chest.
Still, she wore something like a cloak over her shoulders, which barely shielded her pale shoulders and the greater part of her exposed body from the wind.

Her hair was a brown so close to black it nearly blended into the darkness, worn long and gathered into a single ponytail.
And the eyes fixed on Subaru and the others were a pair drenched in thick, heavy emotion.
Her thin lips split sideways, and the woman, wearing a smile touched with something feral, said—

"—Found you."

At least the language is the same, Subaru thought.
No sooner had he thought it than his consciousness reached its limit.

Caught by the feel of the sand, Subaru let go of consciousness without a word.
At the very least, holding tight so as not to let go of the girl in his arms.

That much stubbornness, at least, he still had the guts left to hold onto.
