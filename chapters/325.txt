Arc 5 Chapter 9: "The Value of the Songstress"

"Well then, well then, allow me to introduce myself properly. I don't wander quite so much these days, but I once roamed the continent east and west, wherever the wind and my whims took me, making my living on nothing but my Lyulyre and my voice — a bard. My name is Liliana. Pleased to make your acquaintanth—"

"You bit your tongue, I suppose."

Instrument in hand, Liliana bowed with elegant flair, then smiled with a great deal of blood trickling from the corner of her mouth. She'd bitten down with quite some force, it seemed.
At Beatrice's faint recoil, Liliana pressed a cloth to her mouth.

"Beg pardon. I bit down something fierce there."

"I can see that. So — uh, this 'Songstress' who's supposedly famous around town, that's you, right? I can't imagine there's some other singer out there who just happens to share the name, but I figured I'd check."

"Um, er, that title's a bit embarrassing, so if you could refrain from using it, I'd appreciate it, yes. I still consider myself terribly lacking in polish, so being treated as though I'm the pinnacle of song, a 'Songstress' no less — my nose would get so long from all that pride, people would laugh at me, you know."

Liliana fidgeted, blushing across the lower half of her face, which was smeared red from her far-too-careless wiping. Subaru hesitated over whether to point that out, then decided against it, focusing on the conversation instead.
Even so, her reasoning struck him as a rather admirable attitude for a professional to hold.

"Even if it's not what you intended, being evaluated that way is something you should be able to take pride in, honestly. But yeah, I'm impressed that you think about it like that."

"Oh nooo, not at all. It's nothing but daily devotion to my craft! Now then, yes."

"—?"

At Subaru's admiring words, Liliana smiled and held out her hand.
There was nothing in particular in it, and unable to grasp the true meaning behind her gesture, Subaru tilted his head. Still smiling at him, Liliana said,

"You've heard the Songstress sing, so it's only right you pay up. Surely you weren't planning on listening for free, were you, sir? That would be quite the problem, you know."

"Give me back the admiration I just felt! You're squandering the Songstress brand you're supposedly upholding!"

"Whatever do you mean! If anything, you should be praising me for never losing my resolve as a bard who makes her living through song! Or would you have me live on nothing but mist and offer up inspiration for free!? How cruel!"

Liliana clutched her head and stamped her feet.
Her argument was, admittedly, a fair one as far as arguments went, but the impression Subaru had of the title "Songstress" had been in steady decline ever since. It seemed the same was true for Beatrice beside him — the temperature in her big eyes had dropped even lower than his.

"Fine, fine. If paying a tip makes this go smoother, I'll pay. How much do you want?"

"Oh nooo, that's entirely up to whatever your heart desires, sir. Just give me everyfing you've—"

"That's enough out of you."

The instant she grinned, showing teeth mottled red with blood, Subaru seized Liliana's head in a firm grip.
Her face was small enough that it was absurdly easy to grab, and he shook her upper body back and forth before releasing her. Dizzy, Liliana pouted, muttering, "It was just a joke, you knowww."

"Anyway, since you're here on business for the Muse Company, that makes you Kiritaka-san's guest too, doesn't it? In which case, there's no way I'd be so rude as to skim off a bit of pocket change, now would there? Teeheehee."

"We don't know you well enough to think that, and the more we get to know you, the faster your credibility keeps dropping. It'd be great if you could get going already."

The dawdling before they'd even departed was about to exceed the limits of Subaru's patience.
They were already running well behind schedule from having gotten lost, and if their arrival was delayed any further, there was even a chance they wouldn't make it in time for the negotiations to be settled. If things fell through and they got a second chance, fine — but if the deal was already sealed, Subaru would have no leg to stand on.

Shaking her head, her tied-back hair bobbing, Liliana saluted and answered with gusto, "Understood!" before striding off ahead.

"By the way, sir, what business did you have with Kiritaka-san?"

"Nosiness isn't a very endearing trait, I suppose. It seems you still don't understand your position — all you need to do is keep quiet and guide Betty and the others to our destination."

"Piiik!"

At Beatrice's near-threatening tone, Liliana let out a shriek like a little bird. Subaru, chiding her, gave one of Beatrice's drills a tug.

"Don't tease her like that. That said, we're not exactly loose-lipped enough to spill our business to just anyone. I can't tell you that easily, sorry."

"I am connected to Kiritaka-san, you know — I think it's only a matter of whether I find out sooner or later."

"Then it really is just a matter of sooner or later, so there's no problem with me playing it safe for now."

"Say one thing and you counter with another... you're not a very nice person, are you."

"You're one to talk about personality. If you didn't have any talent for singing, I bet things would've turned out badly for you."

He'd heard that geniuses with one outstanding talent tended to be eccentrics, and Liliana was surely one of those. A freedom permitted by sheer skill — though hers cut it rather close.

"But honestly, I am a little seriously concerned about something. I was told to stay outside for a few hours."

"Guess if you were there for serious business talks, you'd be in the way. I get it."

"I get it, I suppose."

"Huh!? Why does that agreement feel different from what I was hoping for!? I'm hurt, you know!"

At Beatrisubaru's shared understanding, Liliana puffed out her cheeks, displeased. Then she gave a light tap with her finger to the Lyulyre in her arms.

"I've got it. Listen to this. — Rough waves, high waves, the waves of the world—"

"No thanks, that's quite alright."

Cutting off the recital before it could even start, Subaru snatched the Lyulyre away. Liliana reached out with an "Ahh—," but with the height difference between her and someone so childlike, she couldn't reach the arm Subaru had raised it with.

"Consider it a hostage — well, an instrument-age. Keep us on track without any more detours, and I'll give it back once we reach the company."

"What villainy! What wickedness! What vileness! What vulgarity!"

"Ha ha ha, that's way too much!"

Being spoken to as if he were the concentrated essence of all the world's evil, Subaru plucked at the instrument's strings with a finger. The sound it produced resembled that of an acoustic guitar.
Incidentally, Subaru happened to be a jack-of-all-trades with a broad if shallow range of hobbies, and could manage a full sing-along set of the seventies folk songs he used to practice back home.
Reviving the classics of yesteryear in this world might just bring about a revolution in its music scene.

"Well, I thought the same thing about mayonnaise, and look how that turned out."

It was a plan that had stalled out before it even started, since he'd never been able to figure out where to begin or what exactly to do.
Back in his old world, whenever isekai stories came up, it was practically standard for the protagonist to bring over modern knowledge and dominate the economy — but when he actually tried it himself, he had no idea where to even start.
Expanding distribution for mayonnaise, establishing a production system — what was he even supposed to do about any of that?

"So the guitar thing's just a hobby, and my fairy tales are only good enough for telling to village kids."

"Wait, wait, stop that, please! If some amateur fumbles around with it and breaks it, that's my livelihood— whoa, you're good!? You're really good at playing the Lyulyre!? And you're playing a song I've never even heard before! Wait, huh, what, what is this!?"

Liliana was astonished at Subaru, who plucked out chords as he walked, playing a folk song. Dragging along the wide-eyed, blinking girl, Subaru and the others made their way straight toward the Muse Company.

"What a handful, I suppose..."

Beatrice followed the two of them from behind, her face weary.
And yet, somehow, her steps seemed to have a bounce to them, keeping time with the melody Subaru played.

The gate of the Muse Company building didn't look particularly different from the buildings on either side of it.
The border between First Avenue and Second Avenue mainly divided those who used Priestella from within the city and those who came from outside it, and the company's building was located right at the midpoint between the two districts.

For convenience of access, it would have made sense to situate the building directly on First Avenue near the main gate, but positioning within the city also carried implicit meaning about the power balance among its owners.
Situated at once at the innermost point and the very front, a contradiction of sorts, the Muse Company's location made clear its aspiration to be involved in the city's core affairs while also conveying just how much it liked to keep people waiting.

"And so, here we are — the long-awaited Muse Company!"

Spinning around as though dancing, Liliana gestured toward the stone building in front of them.
The four-story building was quite large for a company office. At the very least, none of the neighboring buildings for several doors down matched it in height, and it certainly conveyed a sense of presence.
Still, what Subaru found somewhat disappointing was—

"Guess it makes sense I didn't notice it from the main gate... but I was kind of hoping for a surprise twist, like it turning out to be some Japanese-style building, like the 'Water-Feathered Robe Inn.'"

"The Water-Feathered Robe Inn — you mean that peculiar inn over on First Avenue? If that's your standard of comparison, then even Kiritaka-san might come up short, I suppose. But, but, more importantly—"

Swaying her upper and lower body left and right, Liliana pressed in close with exaggerated gestures. She stretched out both hands, opening and closing her palms right in front of Subaru.

"C'mon, c'mon, I've held up my end of the promise, so please give my precious little Lyulyre-chan back! Without her, I won't even be able to scrounge up tomorrow's rice!"

"Yeah, yeah, alright, got it. Here, take it."

He'd grown bored of playing while walking and had ended up using the instrument like a back-scratcher, poking his back and tapping his shoulder with it, but now he returned it. Liliana hurriedly took it — and before even checking that it was undamaged, she nuzzled it against her cheek, breathing hard through her nose as she showered the returned instrument with kisses.

"Ehehe, so glad you're back safe. I'm never letting go again. Mwah, mwah!"

"You're something else. I don't think anyone's managed to creep me out this badly with such ordinary behavior since maybe Petelgeuse."

"Ohh? Not sure who that is, but sounds like quite the impressive fellow, this Petelgeuse-san. If our paths ever cross, he might just become my lifelong rival! Please, do tell me more about his background!"

"He's a Witch Cult Sin Archbishop."

"Ha ha, very funny, you're quite the jokester! Attaboy, that's the spirit!"

Liliana, dressed in an outfit that bared quite a lot of skin like a dancer, produced what looked like confetti from who-knows-where and flung it, showering Subaru's joke with the highest possible praise.
But noticing his lackluster reaction, she tilted her head, and her expression gradually tightened into something more serious.

"...Could it be that you're actually being serious?"

"Exactly-ly. Well, since he's not around anymore, no one's going to be comparing you to him going forward, so just keep on living as yourself."

"Wait wait wait wait wait, please wait!"

Liliana protested with tremendous force at Subaru's offhand remark. From her attitude, Subaru had assumed she must, of all things, object to being lumped together with a Sin Archbishop — but her next words were nothing like what he'd expected.
What she said was—

"The way you just said that makes it sound like you were present at the Sin Archbishop's final moments?"

"...And if I was?"

Unable to read the intent behind the question, and yet sensing it was anything but harmless, Subaru answered warily.
Surely not, he thought. But this was her reaction upon learning Petelgeuse was a Sin Archbishop.
The unfathomable nature of those creatures showed itself even in this — the way they could turn up anywhere, like some venomous insect.
At Subaru's quiet vigilance, Beatrice beside him narrowed her eyes as well, watching. If anything happened, she would act. Playing one's trump card too cautiously only made it come too late.

A faint tension hung in the air.
Depending on what this next Songstress did, the situation could shift right in front of her own base of operations.
And then, Liliana said:

"Could it be that you're... Natsuki Subaru-sama, the 'Cradle-Robber'?"

"Ugh."
"Ugh, I suppose."

At Liliana's exclamation, eyes shining, Subaru and Beatrice groaned in unison.
It was, of all the things Natsuki Subaru had gained over the past year since receiving his knighthood and being permitted to call himself, in name and in truth, Emilia's knight — the single most disgraceful title of them all.

—As the saying went: the Half-Elf's First Knight was a mysterious figure who was always accompanied by a young girl.

"The one who lent extraordinary aid in the subjugation of the White Whale, one of the Three Great Witchbeasts, led by Duke Crusch Karsten, and was even called a benefactor by the Sword Devil Wilhelm himself — an attendant to history at that pivotal moment! Immediately after, he struck down one of the Witch Cult Sin Archbishops who had shaken the world to its core, 'Sloth,' with the help of Duke Crusch and Anastasia-jō of the Hoshin Company! And though the rumor remains unconfirmed, he is said to have also subjugated the Great Rabbit, another of the Three Great Witchbeasts — the newest hero to set four hundred years of stagnant time moving once more!"

"Itchy, itchy, itchy, itchy, itchy!"

"What a squirm-inducing assessment, I suppose."

With the expression of a dreaming maiden, hands clasped together, Liliana rattled off Subaru's accomplishments one after another. Though somewhat excessively embellished, the words hit close enough to the mark that it made the whole thing worse.
There wasn't a single element in the substance of it that could be denied.

Still, being attacked from such an unexpected angle made Subaru itch with embarrassment all over, while Beatrice beside him puffed up her nostrils, looking rather pleased with herself.

"And since then, he's rushed east and west for the sake of the master he serves, active in every scene alongside a young girl who wields powerful magic, or so the rumors say! That's — that's you, Natsuki Subaru-sama!?"

"Hmph. He's quite a fellow who understands the proper order of things, I suppose. Yes, Subaru, Betty's partner, is a man destined to eclipse every great figure throughout history and shine as the very first and brightest star. Revere him more. Worship him, I suppose!"

"Yesss ma'am!"

Beatrice, thoroughly pleased with herself, and Liliana, prostrating herself right there on the spot.
After all that embarrassing commotion, this was the treatment he got. Looking around, Subaru noticed passersby and company employees had taken note of the little skit playing out at the company entrance and were staring intently. The moment they all recognized it was Liliana doing the prostrating, they turned right back to their business as usual.

"Do you spend all your time like this? The way everyone around here reacts with, like, 'Oh, it's just Liliana,' is kind of intense."

"Ah, stop that. If you touch me carelessly like that, I'll run a fever. Ahh-ha-ha-ha, before my very eyes, a legend, a legend stands before me. Uheehee."

Right in front of Subaru, who was shaking her to try to bring her back to her senses, Liliana only grew more unhinged. The girl, grinning with a face that looked about to drool, suddenly changed her expression with a start.

"C-Could it be, by any chance, that the person you're meeting inside the company is exactly who I think it iiis!?"

"...No, it's not?"

"No, wait, now that I think about it, I recall you playing the Lyulyre along the way while going on about some love song dedicated to Emilia-tan or something! If Emilia-tan is a person's name, then that is precisely the figure I'm picturing! Uhyohyoo, jackpot!"

Ignoring Subaru's desperate lie, Liliana bolted into the company building as if flying. Slow to react, Subaru's eyes went wide, and he hurriedly chased after her into the building.

"Subaru! That girl left her instrument behind, I suppose!"

"Does she actually have any feelings about her livelihood or whatever at all!?"

Catching the Lyulyre Beatrice tossed to him, Subaru dashed into the company alongside her. Judging by the receptionist's startled expression as she looked toward the staircase, it seemed Liliana had headed upstairs from there.

"E-Excuse me, are you gentle— er, you two—"

"We're attendants of Emilia-sama, who should be meeting with someone here right now. Did anyone mention she'd be arriving late?"

"Y-Yes, I have been informed. I was just about to show you to the room... ah, but right now Liliana-sama is—"

"I know the direction. Up the stairs, right?"

Seeing the receptionist nod jerkily, Subaru headed for the stairs after Liliana.
For some reason, there was a nagging unease about the idea of Emilia and her meeting — or rather, less unease than the feeling that doing so would somehow lead to something incredibly complicated and troublesome.
What made it even more terrifying was the objective fact that the chemistry between the thoroughly eccentric Liliana and Emilia was probably, for better or worse, not bad at all. For better or worse, Emilia was overwhelmingly lacking in both experience dealing with people and a sense of caution. He wished she'd learn a little from him.

"People who say weird things turn out to actually be weird people after all!"

"That's a lament with a startling amount of feeling behind it, I suppose."

Letting Beatrice's jab slide, Subaru chased the sound of footsteps up to the third floor. Just then, past the landing, he caught sight of the hem of Liliana's skirt disappearing around a corner, and he sprinted after it with everything he had. Parkour, unleashed here of all places.

"Hyah!"

Bounding up the stairs, Subaru dove headfirst into the corridor, lightly planting a hand on the floor and rolling beautifully to the side. Riding the momentum, he chased Liliana's shadow, catching up with her just as she came to a stop in front of a certain door.

"Liliana, stay!"

"Woof!?"

Liliana, who had been on the verge of drooling, let out a shriek at Subaru's sharp command. Catching her as she stood bolt upright, Subaru caught his breath lightly.

"I-I've been caught up to, but my ambitions won't falter here. I won't give up until I win!"

"If you really want to meet Emilia-tan that badly, I'll ask her later and make some time for you. But right now, there's an important discussion going on. Fall back."

"Ngh... yeshir. I got a little ahead of myself there."

Hearing the gravity in Subaru's serious tone, Liliana drooped, crestfallen.
Watching her shoulders slump, Subaru let out a breath, scratched his head, and held out the instrument he'd still been gripping in his hand.

"Ah, thanks."

"It's supposed to be an important tool of your trade, isn't it? Don't go flinging it aside somewhere like that. An instrument used by the famous Songstress — if someone picked it up, it wouldn't be strange at all for them to sell it off for a high price."

"In that case, the eccentric who'd end up buying it would surely be Kiritaka-san, you know."

"Your patron sounds like quite the character!"

Wincing wryly at Subaru's words, Liliana clutched the instrument to her chest as though it were precious. Her expression was serious enough that it was almost convincing — but she'd literally just abandoned that very instrument moments ago for the sake of some immediate whim. With this girl, you couldn't trust even a smile from one second ago.

"So, with the talk with Emilia-sama on hold for now... does that mean Natsuki Subaru-sama might tell me some stories?"

"That title makes my skin crawl, quit it! And also, what stories?"

"Oh, all sorts, all sorts! I want to know, want to hear, want to sing about how much of what I've heard rumored is actually true! And from the inspiration I gain there, I might be able to sing all kinds of songs — yes, perhaps even a heroic epic to be passed down for generations! This gets me fired up!"

Pumping her fist, eyes blazing with fervor, Liliana burned with motivation.
As Subaru was flustered by her intensity, Beatrice, who had lagged behind on the stairs, finally caught up. Seeing Liliana pressing in on Subaru, she said,

"Ah, hey, you strange girl. Don't get close to Subaru, I suppose. Step back, would you."

"Oh, come on, it's not like it'll wear him down or anything. Besides, I did want to hear from the young girl accompanying the 'Cradle-Robber,' too, you know."

"Young girl, young girl — Betty isn't a young girl, she's a fine and dignified lady, I suppose. How exceedingly rude."

"You two are way too noisy, chirping away like that! Cut it out already! Right now, inside, Emilia-tan and the others are negotiating and—"

Caught between the irritated Beatrice and the oblivious Liliana, who hadn't even noticed the lit fuse, Subaru raised his voice, desperate to bring the scene under control.
But it was right at that moment.

"—Subaru?"

Suddenly, the door in front of them opened, and from within, a voice like a silver bell called Subaru's name.
Looking over, inside the open door — in what appeared to be a reception room or something similar — there were faces sitting on a sofa, looking this way with startled expressions.
The Emilia Camp, consisting of Emilia, along with Otto and Garfiel. Seated across from them was a slender young man in finely tailored clothes.
The one who had opened the door was likely a middle-aged man attending him.

"H-Hey there, Emilia-tan. What a coincidence."

"Coincidence or not, making all that racket... huh, Kiritaka-san?"

Emilia frowned, troubled, at Subaru, who raised a hand with an awkward look on his face. But immediately after, the person she called out to was the one seated across from her.
The young man rose to his feet and, with movements like a ghost, snatched something up off the table.
Then, turning toward Subaru, he said,

"D-D-D-Don't you touch my Liliana!!"

His voice cracked, and from the young man's hand flew a blue-glowing magic crystal.
Pure energy detonated right in front of Subaru, standing frozen where he was, and as his vision was engulfed in a wash of blue, swallowed up by the blast, all he could think was, "Ah, pretty."


—And so, on this first day, the negotiations fell apart.
