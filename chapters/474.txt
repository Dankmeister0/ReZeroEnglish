Arc 6 Chapter 68: "The Woman of Scorpio"

——The first time Subaru heard the name Shaula, it wasn't as though it meant nothing to him.

That wasn't because he had any special attachment to the name "Shaula" itself.
It was simply that the sound of it rang a bell.

"Shaula" was the name of a star shining in the night sky Subaru knew — a word meaning Scorpio.

That the true identity of the person bearing the name of Scorpio was a jet-black, carapace-clad giant scorpion was, honestly, an answer with no twist to it at all. So lacking in twist, in fact, that it made him want to question the sense of whoever had named her.
But at present, the leading candidate for that lack of sense was Natsuki Subaru — treated as "Oshi-sama" by Shaula herself.
The trouble was that Subaru couldn't imagine, memory loss or not, that he'd ever had the opportunity to name Shaula, and that right now he had no leisure to calmly verify the matter anyway.

"――――"

Before his eyes, the tag team of Emilia and Ram continued their fierce battle against the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony, Rui Arneb. With that battlefield between them, deep in the corridor beyond, the jet-black giant scorpion clacked its vicious pincers, its red compound eyes fixed on him.
Natural great pincers evolved for slaughter — the weight of the ominous grinding of those blades promised a sharpness that would slice clean through a human body, bone, organs and all, without the slightest resistance.

Red compound eyes, two menacing great pincers, the multiple legs supporting its massive frame, and a carapace boasting a strength that put iron armor to shame — every part of it was a symbol of refined destruction and violence.
And in fact, its combat power betrayed none of that outward ferocity.
Subaru had already confirmed as much, again and again, with his own eyes across the battles thus far, across the worlds that had come before — or felt it with his own body.

"O—"

The instant that realization took root, memories from the previous loop flashed through Subaru's mind.
Just after they'd beaten back the giant scorpion's onslaught and thought they'd managed to force a temporary retreat, Subaru and the others had been caught in the explosion the vicious beast left behind as a parting gift, and it had claimed the lives of both Beatrice and Echidna.

The unbearable rage he'd felt in that moment — that, he could not forget.
The fury he'd held then was anger at his own helplessness, at being able to do nothing. But at the same time, it had also been an uncontainable rage at the giant scorpion that had carried out that slaughter.

——And the giant scorpion that had been the target of that rage — its true identity was Shaula, who was supposed to be their ally.

Now that he knew that, what filled Subaru's heart was a storm of raw, nowhere-to-go emotion and suspicion.

Why was Shaula in the form of a giant scorpion?
Why was Shaula attacking them, trying to kill them?
Why had Shaula revealed her true nature at this juncture, thrusting herself into the fight?
Why, why, why, why, why, why, why, why, why, why, why——

"—Subaru, calm down, I suppose. First, breathe, would you."

"――――"

Subaru's mind was buried under a barrage of shocking facts. It was Beatrice at his side who called his name gently and pulled him back to his senses.
His hand held in her small palm, Subaru realized for the first time that he'd forgotten to breathe. Slowly, following her instruction, he drew a deep breath and let it out.
And then—

"That's it, I suppose. —Is it a fact that that's really Shaula?"

At Beatrice's question, Subaru checked with himself once more.
*Cor Leonis* — an Authority he'd only just awakened to, whose properties he'd only just begun to grasp, but there was no mistaking that it was a power that told him the whereabouts of his companions and took on their afflictions in his stead.
That was why Subaru could vaguely sense the locations of the companions inside the tower — Emilia, Ram, Beatrice, Julius, Meili, Echidna, and the rest.
And that faint, warm sensation he felt from those companions — he felt it from that giant scorpion too.

Given the current positions of everyone in the tower, no other candidate was conceivable.
So that giant scorpion was, without a doubt——

"It's absolutely Shaula. I don't know why, but she's transformed into a huge scorpion..."

"—. The fact remains she was an unidentified girl to begin with, I suppose. Her true identity turning out to be a somewhat oversized insect hardly seems worth being shocked over, does it."

"That's some nerve you've got..."

Beatrice declared the scorpion an insect outright, deflecting the shock not through comprehension but through sheer capacity to cope. Marveling at that method of hers, Subaru glared at the giant scorpion once more.
Its red compound eyes, impossible to tell where they were looking — he stared straight into them, pinning them with his gaze.

"Say something already! We already know who the hell you are!!"

"――――"

"An excuse, licking your chops over your prey, something — you've gotta have a move here...!"

Raising his voice in a shout sent a sharp pain through his sluggish, unwell head. But Subaru ignored the pain and demanded an answer from the giant scorpion.
But the giant scorpion, with its appearance incapable of communication, made no attempt to answer at all.

"Not even gonna give me an excuse, huh..."

Muttering weakly, Subaru thought even as he fought back nausea.
Just as the memories that had resurfaced told him, this giant scorpion had put him through hell over and over.
The Shaula who'd been so innocent and unrestrained, who'd never once tried to hide her affection — or fondness, whatever it was — for Subaru — the fact that he'd been deceived, manipulated by her, struck sharp into his chest.

Had that smile, those words, that manner — had all of it been a fabricated fake?
Had she been a traitor, deceiving Subaru and the others all along?

"No..."

The word "traitor" surfaced in his mind, and Subaru's cheeks stiffened with a subtle unease.
Shaula had betrayed them. —That conclusion itself, given the present circumstances, was unavoidable. But whether truly everything about Shaula had been a lie — there was room to argue that.
Of course, it was possible that all of Shaula's words and actions up to now had been an act, that her smiles, her physical affection, everything, had been deception meant to fool them.
It was possible. But to what end——?

If she'd been acting to worm her way into their confidence, then her goal would be hostility toward Subaru and the others.
But if Shaula had had that intent, she wouldn't have needed to pull a stunt like cutting into the fight at this juncture — she'd have had countless chances to slit their throats before now.
Precisely because she'd burrowed so deeply into their trust, during their time traveling together there had been endless opportunities, in the idle stretches between everything else, to quietly slip in poison.
Why let all those chances slip by, only to expose her betrayal here?
None of it made any sense at all.

What point was there in going to such a roundabout length, pretending to be their companion?
Listening to Subaru's request and helping stop Meili, showing off her incredible magic against the horde of witchbeasts on the balcony — none of it made sense either.
Nothing about it added up.

"Is it that I want to think that way because I've been charmed by the white legs peeking out of her hot pants?"

"If that's what makes Subaru's thinking go soft, then Shaula's strategy isn't such a bad one, I suppose."

"Right. My heart's set dead ahead on Emilia-chan, though... wait, whoa!?"

Just as they'd found common ground over the strangeness of Shaula's actions, Beatrice suddenly yanked his arm. It wasn't a protest — it was a forceful rescue.
White light tore through the space where Subaru's head had been an instant before, and he stumbled back, narrowly escaping with his life.

"Doesn't seem like there's time to be chit-chattin', I suppose!"

As she spoke, she raised the hand not linked with Subaru's, and a faint glow spread around Beatrice.
What unfurled was a mass of crystals wreathed in violet light, their tips honed to sharp points. The purple arrows deployed as if to encircle Subaru and the others, then shot straight out toward the giant scorpion.
The next instant, the white light flying in from the front collided head-on with the purple arrows, and light scattered wildly.

"Uwoooooaaaah!?"
"Get your head down, I suppose! A direct hit would be very bad, wouldn't it!"

The glittering violet light raged, and a sound like shattering glass rang out beautifully, again and again, through the corridor.
But contrary to that dazzling, eye-searing spectacle, the flash of light unleashed by the giant scorpion — no, the strike of its sharp tail-stinger — was taking aim without mercy, intent on running Subaru clean through.

Against that surging assault — not one arrow of killing intent but a hundred — Beatrice was doing her best to protect him, deflecting, batting away, and turning aside each with the magic she wove.
But the sheer volume was overwhelming.

"Gh, this is bad, I suppose...! At this rate, our mana's going to run out soon, isn't it...!"

"What happens if it runs out!?"

"Betty and Subaru get to be skewered together on the same happy needle, I suppose!"

Happy or not, that situation was unacceptable.
Against the storm bearing down on them, that Beatrice's reserves wouldn't hold out much longer was exactly as she'd self-reported. Before they were ground down into a slow, losing battle, they needed to somehow break the deadlock.
Just as Subaru gritted his teeth at that sense of crisis——

"—That's dangerous, whizzing around over people's heads like that! I've had about enough!"

A voice like a silver bell rang out sharply, and a massive block of ice struck the giant scorpion's carapace with a resounding blow. The impact echoed through the corridor, and for a moment the scorpion's emotionless red compound eyes seemed to register shock.
And the one who'd drawn out that reaction was Emilia, who'd kicked off an ice wall she'd conjured in the corridor and plunged decisively into the scorpion's own territory.
Her silver hair streaming behind her, she leapt at the scorpion in a magnificent arc, brandishing a longsword of ice.

"Going after Subaru and the others... bullying someone weaker than you is despicable!"

"――――"

The ice blade came down in a vertical strike, which the giant scorpion parried with one massive pincer before scrambling its many legs into a high-speed retreat. But Emilia, refusing to let it get away, chased after its huge body, and a three-dimensional battle between a beautiful girl and a scorpion began, ranging freely through the narrow corridor.

"There! Hah! Hiyah!"

Longswords, twin blades, spears, and greatswords of ice were conjured one after another, shattering with a light chime as Emilia danced amid a swirl of glittering ice shards.
The giant scorpion's formidable pincers were a sight to behold, possessing the brute strength to crush and sever any weapon they caught with ease. But that weapon-destroying power was of no use against Emilia.

Emilia's Ice Brand Arts was a fighting style that conjured countless weapons from her own mana. Those disposable ice weapons were, to Emilia, no more than temporary tools. No matter how much they were shattered or severed, Emilia felt not the slightest pain.

"There, yah!!"

Dodging the swinging pincers and tail-stinger, Emilia launched an ice stake she'd floated in the air. It collided head-on with the white light the scorpion unleashed, and light scattered.

It was a clash between human and inhuman, between two beings of overwhelming power.
What was astonishing was Emilia's sense for combat — she dove without hesitation into a fight against an opponent whose countermeasures would differ vastly from a humanoid foe, and held her own.
For Emilia, who favored an instinctive style of fighting, the shape of her enemy was never absolute. It wasn't training built up over time but the sheer combat sense overflowing within her that spoke here.

And so the exchange between Emilia and the giant scorpion settled into a back-and-forth, an even fifty-fifty standoff.
But with Emilia fighting the giant scorpion, another battlefield inevitably felt the strain.
For instance, the fight against the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony, Rui Arneb — a fight that had only been holding together because the two of them were pressing the attack together — was now left to Ram alone.

"Ram!!"

"You don't have to shout every single thing — I can hear you fine. Be quiet. It's distracting."

His voice, full of concern for her, was brushed off with a curt, unreasonable reply.
Alone, Ram stood facing Rui at the center of the corridor. With her back turned toward Subaru and the others, she was clashing head-on with the massive body that radiated tremendous pressure.
Naturally, with Emilia pulled away, Subaru expected the battle to have grown harder for her — but——

"Hah!"
"Hyagyah! Sis, that's so mean, so mean!"

The huge body, wearing a sticky grin, shook its head and leapt backward.
It was a retreat to disperse the shock of the elbow strike it had taken to the face. Before that retreating hulk, Ram spun sharply and launched a bold, blisteringly close-range assault on the man who called himself "Fist King."
The martial arts Rui employed carried lethality that lived up to the name "Fist King." And yet Ram was overwhelming that consummate fighting technique through sheer, unhinged sense alone.
Even now, the white fist she unleashed drove mercilessly into her opponent's centerline.

"Amazing! So amazing! You're so amazing, way too amazing! Sis is way stronger than I ever knew! What is this, what is this, why can you move like that!?"

"Don't talk like you know anything. Just shut up and die."

Rui, taking the ferocious barrage of blows, writhed in pain even as he let out cheers.
Right now Rui's right arm had been snapped, one arm rendered useless. In that state, no matter how thoroughly the man had mastered unarmed combat, he couldn't bring his full specs to bear.
The experience the "Fist King" had cultivated in martial arts counted for nothing before Ram, now free of her chronic malaise.
That was how overwhelmingly Ram's innate specs outmatched her enemy.
But the harder Ram fought, the more fiercely she pressed the attack——

"Gh, bl—"

Rising nausea, a body heat that seemed to climb without limit. Amid a blasphemous ringing in his ears and a headache like gongs being struck inside his skull, Subaru fought against the lethargy overtaking his entire body.

This was the utter malaise Ram must ordinarily endure, day in and day out.
And it was mercilessly filing away at Subaru's mind and body. Far from growing used to it, the chill grew heavier with each passing second, its weight becoming ever more pronounced the higher Ram's voltage climbed.

"――――"

But Subaru clenched his back teeth and endured the nausea, the headache, the exhaustion.
Thanks to this Authority, he could take on Ram's suffering and grant her the strength to fight. This much backlash, this much suffering on his part, was a price worth swallowing.
Even as his whole body grew heavy, his limbs creaked, his ears rang unbearably, his slashed thigh throbbed, his beaten stomach cried out in agony, his breath grew ragged from the exertion of wielding the power, and his vision flickered between red and white.
——Put up a front. Act like this is nothing at all.

"—Idiot."

Just then, glancing back at Subaru, who knelt behind Beatrice, Ram's lips moved, murmuring something faint.
But the Authority only took on the burden — it didn't grant him full knowledge of the other person. The brief words she'd murmured went unheard, leaving Subaru only to furrow his brow.
But——

"Hurry up and fall already. Drag this out much longer and my errand boy is going to pass out."

"You're so cold, so cold, so cold, so cold, there's no word for how cold you are! Sis, sis! Let's play some more! Let's have fun! We're sisters, and we've never once wrestled around like this before!"

"—Then I'll grant your wish."

Narrowing her eyes coldly, Ram slapped the side of Rui's cheering face.
Flowing straight from that, she drove an elbow into the same cheek, then, spinning at high speed, hammered a barrage of blows into Rui's whole body.
With Rui reduced entirely to defense, Ram flicked his chin upward from below with her palm, seized him by the collar, slammed his face into the wall to the side with brute force, and drove her lovely knee into the back of his head.

"Pu, geh"

Sandwiched between stone wall and knee, his nose crushed, Rui collapsed, spilling blood. Ram turned her palm without hesitation toward his exposed nape.
In her palm swirled an exceedingly small blade of wind. But do not underestimate its size — that swirling wind blade, small as it was in her palm, carried more than enough force to gouge out a vital point on a human body.
No grand destruction was needed — this was the minimum attack necessary to finish an enemy by carving into a vital point. Take that to the neck, and even the "Fist King's" thick throat, whatever that was worth, wouldn't stand a chance.
And so, just as Ram's blow was about to decide the match——

"—Damn, doin' whatever ya want while a guy's sleepin'. This is why little sisters are no good — so selfish. Big sis, you'd agree, wouldn't ya, from up there as the older one?"

"—!"

The instant the strike to the nape was about to land, "Fist King's" form changed, and Ram's palm strike missed.
The next moment, catching Ram's slender torso head-on was a back kick from the now markedly shorter Rui — no, from Lye Batenkaitos.

"Switch...!?"

"Nope, nope, nope, nope, nope, that's not it! It's not like I planned it that way, it just so happens my sis was losin' the second I woke up. You're a big brother, ain'tcha? You get it, right? Watchin' your sibling suck at a game you're playin' together, and you just can't stand it, so you grab the controller."

Tongue lolling out, the malicious one with chestnut hair — Lye — grinned at Subaru.
Behind that malevolent grin, Ram, having taken a kick to the gut, staggered several steps backward. She twisted her face at the blow, letting out a rough, heavy breath.
At the same moment, Subaru took on, through his Authority, the cause that had stopped her in her tracks.
And then——

"Gh, giyaaaaaaaa—!"

A burning pain erupted in his stomach, and Subaru screamed at the unbearable heat.
It felt exactly as though his belly was being stirred with a fire poker. His vision whitened with the shock, and he could feel every organ inside him screaming at once.

"Hahn? Just as I figured. So the reason big sis, who shouldn't have been able to move at all, could move — it was thanks to you, big brother! And here she was, deepenin' her bond with her little sister's crush while her little sister wasn't around!?"

Spouting nonsense, Lye tapped a heel against the floor while watching Subaru scream. At his heel, a short concealed blade peeked out from a hidden sheath, its tip wet with Ram's blood.
He'd been stabbed. Subaru had taken that pain from Ram and was suffering it in her place.
And this burning heat — perhaps there was some poison smeared on the blade. His body temperature was dropping at a chillingly rapid pace, and copious sweat began pouring from every inch of his body.

"Subaru!?"
"Barusu, stop this!"

Beatrice was stunned at Subaru's scream, and Ram cried out, her expression grasping the situation in an instant.
A stab wound to the stomach, blood flowing from it — if the pain had faded, and his movement showed no sign of injury or blood loss, of course it would raise suspicion. But Subaru desperately shook his head.

This agony couldn't be handed back to Ram. The instant he did, she'd be unable to move.
It was the natural conclusion. All this pain was best kept right here with Subaru. As long as it stayed here, no one would lose yet. They could keep fighting. He could keep helping them fight.

"Not yet..."

"Beatrice-sama! Take Barusu and fall back! He's only in the way!"

At Subaru's delirious mumbling, Ram made a swift decision.
She wrapped the jacket she'd taken off around the wound in her stomach to stop the bleeding, then, on top of that, resolved to continue the fight. In exchange, she urged Beatrice to get Subaru out of this place.
At Ram's judgment, Beatrice tugged Subaru's sleeve, moving to comply.

"Subaru! It's like Ram says, I suppose! Let's get away from here for now..."

"N-No...! If I leave here now..."

Shaking his head at Beatrice's tug on his sleeve, Subaru tried to plant himself in place.
If he pulled away from the battlefield now, *Cor Leonis*'s effect would end, and the front line Ram was holding up might collapse all at once.
And if that happened, what would be the point of him withdrawing from here at all?

"Guh...! Emilia-sama! Come here for a moment!"

"Eh? Ah, okay! Got it!"

Seeing Subaru trying to stay put, Ram called out to Emilia.
At that call, Emilia, who'd been focused on her fight with the giant scorpion, leapt sharply backward. The giant scorpion tried to give chase, but its pursuit was blocked by a massive ice wall that filled the corridor.
Of course, before the giant scorpion's pincers, an ice wall wouldn't last even a second — but for Emilia's retreat, that one second was enough.
And into that——

"Hey hey hey, hold on hold on hold on! You think you can just come back like that and I'll say 'oh, sure' and let you go..."

"—Quiet."

"Uhwe"

Licking his lips, Lye raised a dagger at Emilia's retreating back. From a low crouch, Ram crept in and swept his legs out from under him with a resounding sweep.
As Lye toppled over, Emilia leapt clean over his head as though clearing a hurdle. As a result, when Lye scrambled back up in the corridor——

"――――ッ"
"Tch! Hahaha! What's this, what's this, look what you did!"

The giant scorpion, charging fiercely to catch up with Emilia, collided head-on with Lye, left behind in the battle zone. The scorpion's compound eyes were fixed on its true target, Subaru — but even so, it couldn't simply ignore and pass by an obstacle standing directly in its path.

The giant scorpion's pincers swung, and Lye parried them with astonishing swordsmanship using his dagger. A blade so fragile should have shattered easily against a wild strike like that, but Lye's skill made it happen with ease all the same.
And as enemy clashed with enemy off to the side——

"Emilia-sama! Take Barusu!"

"Leave it to me!"

At Ram's shout as she raced down the corridor, Emilia scooped up the crouching Subaru into her arms. Carried in Emilia's slender arms, Subaru let out an "Uwoah!" of surprise, but—

"Sorry, just stay still for a bit, okay!"

"Being carried by a girl... my pride's taking a hit..."

"You don't have any pride left to take a hit, do you. Just be quiet and let yourself be treated like luggage!"

Carrying the smart-mouthed Subaru, Emilia's speed showed no sign of slowing.
Behind them, the fierce battle between Lye and the giant scorpion raged on; whatever conclusion it reached, both would likely be tied up there for some time.
Even so——

"—Shaula's target should be Subaru, I suppose. If we take our eyes off her, we won't know where she'll strike from, and that's more dangerous, isn't it!"

"That's the problem, Beatrice-sama. —Is that really Shaula?"

Running alongside Emilia, who carried Subaru, Ram and Beatrice exchanged information on the situation.
Ram's question was confirmation of the identity of the giant scorpion, something Subaru was already certain of. In response, Beatrice glanced at Subaru and nodded.

"Subaru's certain of it, I suppose. That attack shooting out light matches too, doesn't it."

"...The attack it showed on the balcony, and when we crossed the dunes."

Hearing Beatrice's analysis, Ram furrowed her brow thoughtfully as well. Then she looked toward Subaru and rapped his forehead as he swayed in Emilia's arms.

"Barusu, I don't know what you're doing, but release it. If you keep this up..."

"W-what, you think I can't take it? Let me tell you, I'm a man. Grinning and bearing it, putting up a tough front — that's a man's privilege..."

"You're saying that while this beaten up and being carried by Emilia-sama?"

"Ugu"

There was nothing Subaru could say to that.
In truth, from the effect of taking on Ram's malaise, his whole body was so sluggish even breathing was difficult. The fatigue mounting by the second felt like a curse eating away at his very life.
At Subaru's bravado, Emilia murmured "I knew it" and said,

"Subaru, you're doing something, aren't you? Partway through, my body suddenly got so much lighter, and the places I got hit stopped hurting... Beatrice, if you're making Subaru overdo it—"

"It's not Betty, I suppose. This is Subaru doing it on his own... with his Authority, I suppose. Even Betty would stop him if she could, I suppose."

"Authority...?"

At Emilia's reproach, Beatrice made a sour face while denying her own involvement. At the word she'd used, both Emilia and Ram furrowed their brows in unison.
Beatrice seemed to have some idea what Authority meant regarding the power Subaru had wielded, but——

"Beatrice-sama, what is this 'Authority'?"

"—. Something like an upgraded version of Divine Protection, I suppose. Subaru's using it quite recklessly. Emilia and Ram's condition — that's the cause, I suppose."

"――――"

Hearing that, Emilia caught her breath, and Ram's gaze sharpened.
Even though her condition should have been easier, what filled Ram's pale pink eyes was a fierce anger. With that gaze fixed on him, Ram glared at Subaru.

"Since when did you get so high and mighty, taking Ram's burden away without permission?"

"Sorry... I'm a gentleman. That's why I gotta smartly carry a lady's baggage for her..."

"And the result is you becoming a lady's baggage instead? Like I keep saying, that's putting the cart before the horse."

The sigh that followed from Ram carried a hint of resignation toward Subaru's attitude. She, too, must have understood that Subaru had no intention of returning this burden.
Carrying it forever, honestly, would be brutal — but still, for at least the duration of the fight——

"—That's it."

Just as his thoughts reached that point, Subaru finally turned his awareness deep within himself — not toward the various organs crying out in distress, but toward some other part of himself entirely.
His awareness passed through the flesh that was himself, greatly widening the range of what he could perceive. And it connected to the states and locations of his companions that he'd grasped through *Cor Leonis*.

As before, Echidna, Rem, and Patrasche were in "Taygeta." A little further off, down a floor, was Giant, and then——

"Just as I thought."

"Just as you thought? What is it that you thought?"

"Meili — she's still on the balcony, holding back the witchbeasts..."

Though somewhat distant, the faint light flickering on the fourth floor's balcony was Meili. Despite lacking Shaula, she was mobilizing every bit of her own power, dealing with the horde of witchbeasts.
Her tenacity was something Subaru genuinely admired and was grateful for, but——

"Then that means that girl hasn't been attacked by Shaula, I suppose."

"Exactly."

Subaru affirmed Beatrice's remark from over Emilia's shoulder.
Right after the fighting had broken out on multiple fronts, Subaru had entrusted the horde of witchbeasts swarming the watchtower to Meili and Shaula together.
Afterward, once Shaula appeared in her giant scorpion form to attack Subaru and the others, naturally, he'd worried about the safety of Meili, who should have been with her — but he could sense that she was unharmed.
In other words,

"Whatever happened to Shaula happened after she and Meili split up. Either way, Shaula hasn't laid a hand on Meili..."

What connection that fact had to the current situation, his unresponsive brain couldn't work out.
And on top of that, even if they kept running like this——

"If we don't solve the problems that are happening, running away is just going to leave us more and more cornered!"

Emilia's lamenting cry perfectly summed up their current situation.
Of the five great problems, Meili was handling the horde of witchbeasts; the giant scorpion and "Gluttony" were, for now, locked in a fight with each other; and there was still no sign of the arrival of the jet-black shadow.
But as for the unreasonable hammer of god, the walking engine of violence that was Reid Astrea——

"—Stop!"

"—!?"

Ram, who'd been running in the lead, thrust a hand back to halt Emilia and the others. Just as the three of them came to an abrupt stop, a shockwave tore through the space they'd just occupied, a beat too late.
The shockwave sliced diagonally through the corridor, which appeared to be made of stone, sending stone fragments and dust billowing up.
The watchtower, which had supposedly been indestructible, was — by Subaru's memory — being destroyed for the third time now, and this was the first time it had happened by means other than the shadow's crushing pressure.
And the identity of that destruction was——

"Ram-joshi... and Emilia-sama and Beatrice-sama as well, is it."

"Julius!?"

Bursting through the billowing dust, leaping backward into view, was a handsome man in white attire stained with blood and grime — Julius Juukulius.
Stamping his feet to disperse the shock, he glanced back over his shoulder with his yellow eyes, and upon spotting Subaru on Emilia's shoulder,

"I thought it had only been a few minutes since we parted, but it seems you've taken quite a beating."

"Shut up... I'm unhurt, technically..."

"That doesn't look like the cold sweat of an unhurt man..."

Julius closed one eye at Subaru, whose face was pale and who spat out spiteful remarks between labored breaths. Even his perceptiveness surely couldn't unravel the full effects of *Cor Leonis*'s Authority.
And unfortunately, there was no time left to sit down and discuss the matter calmly.

"Please, just tell me you took Reid's head..."

"Making a report that differs from the facts is an extremely painful choice for a knight to make."

"...That's practically an answer in itself, I suppose."

Subaru's imploring appeal was quietly denied by that earnest reply. Feeling hope repaint itself as despair, Subaru's face soured.
Against his eardrums came the sound of sandals crunching over shattered wall debris, and—

"Han! So the women got together to greet me, did they. Was just startin' to get bored after messin' around a bit. You lot gonna take me on, huh?"

"...The worst possible development."

Following after Julius, emerging from the dust, was a towering, muscular man with long red hair, one sleeve slipped off his loosely worn robe — the appearance of the red violence, Reid.
At the sight of Reid boldly treading the corridor of the fourth floor rather than the second, Ram wrung out a low voice. At the same shock, Emilia's voice trembled as she said "You..."

"Why are you on this floor? You're not supposed to be able to leave that place..."

"Oi oi, don't make me laugh, gorgeous. I go where I wanna go, cut what I wanna cut, and hold the woman I wanna hold. I ain't followin' nobody else's rules."

"...He's an outlier, through and through, I suppose."

Beatrice, looking shaken, clicked her tongue at the utterly self-serving philosophy that ran through Reid.
But as a matter of fact, Reid had appeared here, standing in their way, determined to block their path.
With this, it was Reid at the front gate, and "Gluttony" and the giant scorpion at the rear. — An absolute maelstrom of chaos.

"Might be pointless to say, but... what's happening in the tower right now is—"

"Ah, I get that it's turned into a big damn mess, y'know? But that ain't got nothin' to do with me. Anyone who barges in and gets in my way, I'll cut down. Anyone who clears the road, I ain't gonna chase after and cut down too. —Ah, 'cept the ones takin' part in the 'Trial' — that's a different bracket. They came pickin' a fight with me once already, no mistakin' that."

"Then..."

"—Oi, small fry. Don't go runnin' your mouth with anythin' more boring than that."

Subaru, trying to talk his way into wringing some concession out of Reid, was silenced by the utterly unhinged killing intent radiating off him.

"――――"

A pressure so intense it caused a physical, tingling numbness swept over Subaru — no, not just Subaru, but all five of them standing in the corridor. Perhaps even the entire tower shuddered with it.
Was this — merely from having incurred the displeasure of a single man — nothing more than that man's intimidating presence?
The Pleiades Watchtower held not only this overwhelmingly powerful man but other threats besides——

"We still haven't found a way to deal with the one 'Gluttony' or with that shadow, and..."

"—Subaru, on that matter, I have both good news and bad news."

To Subaru's weak mutterings, Julius said this, having risen and readied his knight's sword once more. At that opening line straight out of some foreign drama, Subaru's cheeks stiffened.
Good news and bad news — a common enough phrase in fiction, but hearing it spoken aloud like this, for real, carried a ring that clawed viciously at his chest.
Swallowing once, Subaru repeated "good news and bad news" under his breath, then said,

"Then let's hear the good news first."

"The one you've been worried about — the other half of the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony whose form we haven't seen — you needn't be wary of him. I can guarantee that much."

"—? I don't get the reasoning, but that's definitely good news. So what's the bad news?"

Whether Julius had found a way to counter him, or had grasped some proof that he wasn't even coming and going in the watchtower at all, Subaru raised an eyebrow at the remark.
If there was only one Sin Archbishop of Gluttony, that would genuinely mean one fewer problem to deal with.
Comparing their own hand of cards against the enemy's, Subaru turned his mind, in his utterly wretched physical state, to what would be best to do.
To that Subaru, Julius drew a breath ever so slightly, then continued.

"The Sin Archbishop of Gluttony, Roy Alphard, is right there."

"...Ah?"

In a grave tone, Julius pointed the tip of his knight's sword straight ahead.
Standing where that blade indicated was only the towering man with the savage grin of a shark. Nothing else, meaning nothing else. — The man with the shark's grin, simply standing there.

And to Subaru and the others, left dumbstruck, Julius continued further still.

"The man standing before you, the first-generation 'Sword Saint,' Reid Astrea. —He is Roy Alphard."
