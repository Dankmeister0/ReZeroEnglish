Arc 3 Chapter 77: "Prisoner Negotiations and Reunion"

—Time rewinds to the moment Subaru pulled back his hood and revealed his identity to the Witch Cultists.

Snapping his fingers at the end of his raised right hand, Subaru delivered his triumphant declaration, and the Witch Cultists, who until then had only watched the situation unfold, sprang into motion all at once.
The one holding the Dialogue Mirror and the two flanking Subaru on either side thrust their hands into their robes in unison, drawing out cross-shaped daggers—

"—Too slow to decide."

With that single line pointing out the cultists' misjudgment, the Sword Devil dropped down through the gap in the trees.
A silver flash cut the air, and the arm of the believer gripping the dagger was severed at the midpoint of the upper arm and sent spinning through the air. Blood sprayed, and the gleam of the blade tearing through the darkness traced a cruel, beautiful arc.

The sound of the arm hitting the ground and the sound of spurting blood striking fallen leaves came in quick succession. And yet, even as he was seared by the agony of losing his arm, the Witch Cultist decided to retreat without so much as a groan escaping his throat. Bending his knees, he twisted to break away, the three of them scattering in different directions. However—

"My apologies, but the ground beneath your feet is already ours."

The moment their feet tried to press forward, the earth beneath them sank, and all three lost their footing and dropped to their knees. As they tried to right themselves, a blade was set against each man's throat, one apiece.
Wilhelm had struck first; Julius had turned the ground to mud in a follow-up. And then—

"And Feri-chan swoops in for the best of both worlds!"

Decorating his expression with something like a peace sign, Ferris—who had drawn a dagger, a rare sight—was flashing his charm in Subaru's direction.
Setting aside that unserious attitude, Subaru let out a breath of relief that the situation had proceeded largely as planned.

Aside from Subaru, the only ones who had come to this place were these three—the ones of the highest skill and trust. The rest of the knights had stayed behind in the village to guide the evacuation. As for the mercenary band, they'd been sent off on a different task. If this attempt had ended in failure, that would have been the fallback plan to fall back on. But—

"Glad it worked out. If I'd talked that big and then screwed it up, 'lame' wouldn't even cover it. Ferris, can you stop the bleeding on them or something? I'll be in trouble if they die before I get to ask my questions."

"You know, saying that with your face all pale doesn't really land, nya~?"

His voice stiff, his lips parched. He'd meant to hide his expression by resting a hand on his forehead in the dark, but he'd been seen through anyway. At Ferris's remark, Subaru made a bitter face and closed his eyes just once.

The gruesome exchange that had just played out before his eyes.
Different from the Witchbeast uproar or the fight against the White Whale—he still wasn't used to this, a clash of blades between beings in human form. Neither a person's death nor their corpse had lost that sense of belonging to some other world entirely.
Feeling almost dizzy at the unreality of the fallen arm, Subaru gave his head a light shake to chase away his own weakness. And while he worked through his own reconciliation with it, Ferris administered treatment to stop the Witch Cultists from bleeding out, and Julius and Wilhelm bound them one after another.

"Still, nyeat catch, spotting that there was an informant among the merchants."

"It was obvious the moment we heard the letter was blank—that meant it had been swapped out. According to Ram, the messenger from Crusch-san's side who brought it turned right around and headed straight back to the capital the second he heard that... This time especially, since there wasn't much time, whoever picked the local help for transport got sloppy. The world's built to be a little worse than even your worst-case guess."

Answering Ferris's praise flatly, drawing from experience, Subaru let out a deep breath as he looked over the prisoners.
A gaunt man. An elderly man. And above all, the man who'd been holding the Dialogue Mirror, trying to pass their information along to Petelgeuse—that man's face, he recognized.

"Now I remember. You're Ketty, right?"

"—Why."

"Hmm, now why would that be, I wonder. Feel free to agonize over it as much as I did, why don't you?"

Called by name, the man—Ketty, the merchant—asked only that one question. Subaru gave an evasive reply, feeling a mild shock at the fact that someone he knew had turned out to be an enemy, and at the same time a kind of grim satisfaction with how the pieces of this loop had come together.

The man named Ketty was someone Subaru had encountered on the very first go-around of this loop that began in the royal capital.
After the Lifaus Highway had been shrouded in fog, Subaru—left behind by Rem partway through the detour around the highway—had run himself ragged through the village trying to secure transportation, and it was Ketty he'd met along the way. Through him, Subaru had been introduced to Otto, and on that first run he'd managed to make it back to the Mathers domain. Though by that point, everything was already too late—the worst of memories.

And then the second world—that one had been beyond saving anyway, so set it aside—the third world.
Driven by vengeance, Subaru had cut straight across the highway and, partway along, run into a group of traveling merchants. The two managing that group had again been him and Otto. And Subaru, hitting on the same idea as this time around, had hired Otto and the others to secure the village's transport, bid farewell to Ketty—who'd stayed behind saying he was heading for the royal capital—and afterward encountered the White Whale, in which the merchant group had been decimated.

Looking back on it now, this man had been present at every branching point of Subaru's actions, and yet he had, as if by design, always managed to steer clear of danger. Which meant—

"It's because you were in the loop, is what it means. If we'd just left this alone, the location of the sanctuary—which is supposed to be unknown—and the plan to split up and evacuate in two directions, both would've leaked straight through."

"But fortunately, it seems we've managed to snuff it out at the source. I might add, this should also let us seize the initiative against the Witch Cult presumably lying in wait in the forest."

As Subaru pulled his thoughts together, Julius picked up the thread and gave a small nod. Subaru agreed with that, and it had been exactly the outcome he'd hoped for in taking this action, but—

"Still, don't these guys seem like they'd be pretty tight-lipped? If we try to force them to talk, I get the feeling they'll just yell 'Glory to the Witch!' and off themselves."

"In truth, such a fearlessness of death is deeply rooted in Witch Cult custom. Part of why their true depths have remained unknown these four hundred years owes to that very tendency. It is not uncommon for a captured group to take their own lives nearly in unison, the truth of the matter buried in darkness with them."

Wilhelm confirmed Subaru's grim imagining.
At that textbook-perfect picture of fanatic thinking, Subaru stuck out his tongue with an "ugh," anxious that Ketty and the others might self-destruct right then and there.
But as if to sweep away that very concern, Ferris clapped his hands loudly and said,

"Now, now, no need for such a gloomy story, nya. That's exactly why Feri-chan's out here on the front lines, so that doesn't happen, nya."

"I mean, when I said I had a hunch about the informant, you're the one who tagged along all excited. Honestly I was thinking, you've got no combat power, why'd you even come."

"My, how rude, nya. Feri-chan's just going to sweep away that terribly, terribly rude thought of Subaru-kyun's."

Ferris gave a charming wink that left Subaru thoroughly unimpressed, then slowly approached the men. His large cat eyes went round and round as he studied all three carefully. Then, settling his aim on the elderly man among them, he touched the man's forehead with his right palm.
His slender fingers gripped the man's forehead firmly, mercilessly, and he let out a small breath. And then—

"Stir and mingle—"

"—Ngh!!"

Ferris's palm gave off a faint glow, a pale blue light wrapping the man's entire body, centered on his forehead. It was the same sight as when he administered treatment—the very same healing Subaru himself had received countless times to repair the anomalies in the Gate within his body—but the effect was dramatically different.

"Ah... gah... ub..."

Just as his whole body seemed to be wrapped in that pale blue light, the man's limbs suddenly began to convulse, his eyes going wide, his tongue lolling out in evident agony.
His eyes went bloodshot in an instant, drool trailing from the corner of his mouth as he let out a groan of anguish. Dripping to the ground, staining it, the man could only gasp like a fish caught in unending torment.

"Hey! What exactly are you—"

"I'm just interfering with the water inside his body a little, making him suffer, that's all. No need to worry, I'm not going to turn him into a vegetable or anything, don't worry. —Well, into a doll, maybe, nya."

At this sudden act of prisoner abuse—one whose method wasn't even clear to onlookers—Subaru raised his voice, but Ferris himself continued on, humming under his breath with an unbothered expression. Subaru felt a chill of dread at that sight, but Wilhelm reached out from behind and patted his shoulder.

"It appears somewhat brutal, but it is a necessary measure when dealing with the Witch Cult. I ask that you bear with it, Subaru-dono."

"I'm not so much of a nice guy that I'd say my heart aches watching the enemy get hurt or whatever... but seeing someone tormented this much, I'll admit, I'd like an explanation."

Watching the situation out of the corner of his eye, Subaru twisted his mouth to voice his displeasure at the ongoing torment of the unchanging man. Seeing Subaru stand there so discontented, Julius raised a single finger and said, "To put it simply—"

"Under the pretense of treatment, Ferris administered a water-attribute spell to them, did he not? He can apparently interfere with the mana he channels inside them and strip away a certain degree of influence over their bodies."

"...What does that mean, in the end?"

"It means that anyone Ferris-chan touches, if their resistance is a bit weak, turns into a kitten obedient to Ferris-chan."

Furrowing his brow as he asked, Subaru got a lighthearted answer from Ferris, who was contentedly brushing off his knees. Ferris responded to Subaru's suspicious gaze with a breezy wave of his hand and, gesturing toward the Witch Cultist he'd just been touching, said, "There, all done."

"There's a time limit, but I think he'll talk pretty reasonably right about now, nya? Once the effect wears off, you'll have to wait a while before doing it again."

As he spoke, Ferris slowly turned his head to look at the remaining two Witch Cultists, and his smile deepened, alluring.

"There's still two more, so it's totally fine, nya~."

"Sometimes I catch a glimpse of your sadistic streak and it's a little terrifying, honestly. ...You're not being serious, right?"

"If I get good information out of them, no need to get serious, is what I'd say, nya~."

Pressing a finger to her lips and swaying her body, Ferris laughed off Subaru's worries.
At that attitude, Subaru looked to Wilhelm and Julius as if asking for their opinions, but both of them shook their heads in unison.

"It is not an ethically commendable method, but there is nothing for it but to accept it as a small evil for the sake of the larger good. As for the remaining two, if possible, I would like to bring them to the royal capital as they are."

"A valuable source of information, after all. The Witch Cult has operated in the shadows for a great many years. If we are to seize hold of their tail, we must make use of whatever we can."

Both of them stood in agreement with Ferris, and Subaru, judging that this wasn't a situation where he could afford to be picky about methods, closed his eyes to some degree on his own inner discontent.
Then, clearing his throat, he turned to face the Witch Cultist who had, somewhere along the line, ended up with that hollow, broken stare.

"Anyway, let's get to asking what I want to ask. How many Witch Cultists are participating in this attack? How many groups are they split into, hiding in the forest?"

"—————"

"Hey, you said this guy would answer honestly, and now he's clammed right up."

At the question there was only silent response, and when Subaru looked at Ferris with suspicion, Ferris swayed his slender shoulders side to side and stuck out his tongue.

"Even I can't get an answer out of him for stuff he doesn't know himself, nya. If he stays silent, that's your answer that he doesn't know. Switching gears, next, next!"

"Not exactly satisfying, but fine. Then let's talk about the guy leading you—Pete... the Sin Archbishop. Name, appearance, what he can do—talk."

"The bishop's... name is Petelgeuse... A gaunt build, green hair... a man with a face where the bones show through..."

Mumbling, the man answered Subaru's question obediently. Listening to the answer, Subaru gained a strange sort of confirmation—so even his followers think he looks sickly and emaciated—and continued with his questions. But—

"What abilities can he use?"

"—————"

"How many Fingers he's got planted in the forest."

"—————"

"Birthday, blood type, and favorite part of a girl's body."

"—————"

"That's just a total wash!"

Sick of the endless silent responses, Subaru's patience gauge snapped early.
He'd been told upfront that the man couldn't speak on things he didn't know, but interrogating someone whose own organization kept this much undisclosed information from its members was still a grueling task. If anything, there was a real chance Subaru himself held more information than they did.

Scratching at his head, Subaru agonized over what to do, and as if unable to watch any longer, Ferris—who was supposed to have stepped back—patted his shoulder.

"Well, no helping it, is there, Subaru-kyun."

"Easy for you to say, Feri-emon, but these guys are just too clueless..."

"Emon? What's that, where'd that come from?"

"It's a cat-ears connection thing—oh wait, he took the cat ears off that one!"

Judging Subaru's response as nonsense, Ferris let out a sigh and swapped roles with him for the interrogation. Implicitly told he lacked the chops for the job, Subaru, dejected, yielded the floor while—

"Hm... and, this is..."

—he picked up an object that had fallen at his feet, unused, dropped from Ketty's hand: the Dialogue Mirror. Brushing off the grass and dirt and looking it over closely, it resembled quite strongly a folding hand mirror Subaru knew from his own world. A compact, image-wise, was probably the closest match.

"A mysterious item that lets you, what, transform or something if you mutter a magic spell... well, I guess it actually is a mysterious item. Abracadabra-amen-somen."

Muttering nonsense to himself, Subaru opened and closed, opened and closed the compact in his hand. In the mirror inside was his own sour face, which he hadn't seen in a while, and looking closely he could tell that between the dirt, sweat, and blood mixed together, his complexion looked awful. Couldn't let Emilia see this.
Consoling himself over the current turn of events with that judgment, the hand mirror suddenly changed. Its edges abruptly began to glow, giving off heat.

"Wh—whoa... huh? Wait, did I accidentally touch something weird?"

Flustered, he fumbled with the Dialogue Mirror in his hand, but the light steadily grew stronger with no sign of fading. The suspicion that he'd broken it grew stronger by the moment, and along with it came the memory of a conversation with a bald old man about how "Magic Vessels sell for extremely high prices," and cold sweat broke out on his skin.
As Subaru wrestled alone with the prospect of a personal financial crisis, Julius noticed and said,

"Subaru, what in the world are you—"

"I—I'm not doing anything?! I just went 'tekumaku mayakon' a little, nothing special, I swear! But this thing's gone kind of weird anyway..."

"Don't tell me—you unraveled the activation formula?"

At Subaru's flustered excuse, Julius, briefly startled, rushed over. Then, glancing at the Dialogue Mirror in Subaru's palm, he wore the expression of someone seeing something unbelievable.

"The Dialogue Mirror is, among Magic Vessels, one of the more common items in circulation, but linking it to its paired Dialogue Mirror requires following a proper procedure. In particular, since the activation formula is the one part left to the discretion of whoever linked the pair, there are few ways to draw it out besides hearing it from someone who already knows it..."

"Huh, what, so what does that mean?"

"It means that what Ferris was trying to draw out of them just now, you've essentially finished doing yourself. If we can release the Dialogue Mirror's activation formula, we can pinpoint the linked Dialogue Mirror."

Taking the Dialogue Mirror from Subaru's bewildered hand, Julius began manipulating the hand mirror somehow while gathering mana at his fingertips. At a glance, it looked not unlike someone operating a smartphone or a PAD, and Subaru, stuck at the flip-phone stage, could do nothing but wait quietly.
But the operation, which looked so complex, ended in a mere ten-odd seconds.

"—Well done, Subaru. I've just located the other Dialogue Mirror linked to this one."

"Whoa... so basically, this is like a GPS function, right? You can tell that from here?"

"I don't know what a 'GPS' is, but I do know the location of the Dialogue Mirror. There are two places the Dialogue Mirror connects to... one is within the forest, but the other is somewhat further away."

Lifting his face from the Dialogue Mirror, Julius sent his gaze beyond the forest, and Subaru followed suit. Blocked by the green of the trees, he couldn't see it, but if his sense of direction wasn't off, the direction Julius was looking was toward the mountain range—a place Subaru had been before.
The west side of the village—the forest where the pack of Ulgarm had been. That place, and no other.
After Roswaal's sweep of the Witchbeasts, the presence of dangerous creatures there had presumably decreased. Still, with its steep cliffs, it was a place with no small connection to Subaru—he'd once "thrown himself off" there.

"So the Witch Cult's there, huh. ...That one, I don't know about."

Petelgeuse's Fingers—going by the name, Subaru guessed there were presumably ten places where the madman's underlings were hiding. Through two loops' worth of experience, roughly eight of the Fingers' hideouts had already been identified.
And if he counted the three who had been embedded among this time's merchants as the one location that had remained unknown, then the spot just now discovered would be the very last one.

"The Dialogue Mirror shows two locations. Should we assume there's no Dialogue Mirror at any of the other spots?"

"However common they may be in the marketplace, the Dialogue Mirror remains a rare Magic Vessel all the same. The refining method is known, but considering the funds, time, and labor required, it's difficult to call it something that pays off well."

Nodding along at Julius's shrugging gesture, Subaru privately held a different opinion from them.
It wasn't as though Subaru had, in his original world, taken any particular interest in military affairs or war-related information. He hadn't, but even so, he knew at least the importance of the speed and accuracy of information transmission.
Could they really not imagine how much potential for development lay hidden in this technology they were dismissing as "not worth it"?

"Well, I'll shelve my hopes for cell phone proliferation for now—if that's reliable, that's good enough. If meticulous information relay all depends on the Dialogue Mirror, and its holders are in two places, then..."

—One location, he judged, was probably Petelgeuse's own.

It was only natural that the head of the group, the top of the chain of command, would hold a Dialogue Mirror. And if there was one for a spy embedded in an enemy group, and then one more location besides, then that would be—

"Reinforcements, or a hidden card... either way, I've got it now. The tail."

Clenching his fist tight, Subaru bit his lip at this change in the situation—this time, for real.
And then, meaning to share the information with Ferris, still mid-interrogation, and Wilhelm, still keeping watch, he turned to walk toward them.

"You two, lucky break—the Dialogue Mirror worked out. With this, the rest of the—"

"—!"

Subaru approached carelessly, hand raised. The instant the prisoners caught sight of the glowing Dialogue Mirror in his hand, the demeanor of the previously docile captives suddenly changed.
The two who hadn't had Ferris's spell cast on them twisted their faces into murderous expressions and, bound as they were, leapt toward him like springs.

"—Ah."

Frozen for an instant by the suddenness of it, Subaru found Ketty—still bound—baring his fangs at his throat, trying to tear it open. But just before it landed—

"Forgive me—"

A slash cut through the air, and the tall man's torso was split top from bottom and fell.
Spilling entrails and blood in a great flood, Ketty let out a roar like a beast, his lower body gone, convulsing on the ground—and then, with a savage expression still fixed on his face, died.

And unlike Ketty, who had taken Wilhelm's blade, the other man—

"Buh-buh-buh-buh-aaaaah-bahfuh-abababa—"

—still in the posture of lunging toward Subaru, collapsed face-first onto the ground, limbs trembling, tongue lolling out as if his throat had gone hoarse, eyes bloodshot.
His skin turned red enough to be visible even in the dark, veins bulging under pressure that seemed to press outward from within, and green markings spread thick across his entire body.

"...Hey, now."

Muttering, Subaru realized only belatedly that he'd sunk down and sat right where he stood. Julius came up from behind and lent him a shoulder to help him up. Even so, it took quite a while for him to stand, as his knees refused to cooperate.

"In the intensity of his will to fight, I failed to hold back. My apologies."

Wiping the blood from his treasured sword and sheathing it, Wilhelm apologized for having cut down Ketty. Then, crouching down, he examined the other man's convulsing body.

"This one, too, is no longer breathing. —Ferris, that was a cruel thing to do."

Ferris stuck out his tongue at Wilhelm, who had confirmed the death. Not even looking at their exchange, Subaru stared fixedly at the body of the man who had died looking like one giant water-swollen blister.
His face contorted in agony, unrecognizable at a glance. It was a death so utterly monstrous for a human being, and above all—

"You weren't even touching him, but... did you do this?"

"Yep. A little application of water magic. —Even gentle mana that heals wounds turns poisonous if you pour in too much of it. The water inside the body getting too energetic is a problem too, you know?"

Unashamed, the cat ears on Ferris's head twitching, Subaru couldn't hide a chill of dread.
The man's death throes were something that even made Wilhelm—who'd just finished cutting a man down himself—show a bitter expression on his face. That Ferris could do such a thing and feel not a shred of remorse over it left Subaru with an unnerving impression he couldn't shake.
Above all, going by what he'd just said—

"You're..."

"Hmm?"

"...No, never mind. The other one—he's fine, right?"

Shaking his head slightly, Subaru gave up pressing further, judging it better left unstirred.
The question he'd swallowed back was this:

—Couldn't you kill me the same way?

The magic Ferris had described, one that ran the water inside a body wild—if it was limited to those who had received healing mana from Ferris's hand before, then Subaru fit that condition perfectly.
And what he remembered was the overwhelming, agonizing death that had struck him in a previous assault.

His entire body's blood boiling, a violent heat that seemed to make even his brain seethe.
If that had been Ferris's doing, then that death would be exactly the same as the man's in front of him now—

"The one who survived, he's getting taken to the royal capital for questioning... right?"

"This one's still under Ferris's suggestion, so there shouldn't be a problem. Now then, Subaru-dono, you mentioned the Dialogue Mirror could be used?"

His complexion pale, Subaru averted his gaze from Ferris, and Wilhelm responded to him, casually positioning himself to block the corpse and Ferris from Subaru's line of sight.

"If the location has been pinpointed, let us launch a surprise attack. Eventually, should they grow suspicious over the lack of contact at the appointed time, our advantage will be lost. Before that happens—"

"...Yeah, that's... right, yeah. We'll do that. We'll do that, but—"

Nodding along to Wilhelm's proposal, Subaru sorted through his thoughts.
Petelgeuse. The eight hideouts already known through experience. The newly discovered final location. The Dialogue Mirror. Emilia and the others fleeing. The battle. Possession. Spiritual Arts.

"—Ferris."

"Nya-what?"

"You said you can manipulate that guy—so you could also make him use the Dialogue Mirror to talk, right?"

Subaru's mind, which had been racing, opened its eyes and posed the question to Ferris. Ferris blinked once at the question, then, loosening his lips into a pleased smile, said,

"I can, nya? Deliberately feeding false information to whoever's on the other end of the Dialogue Mirror is a piece of cake for me. —Want me to?"

"Yeah, let's do it. Just, don't have him say anything too elaborate. Don't want him to slip up and get suspected. Keep it simple—no change in the situation, continue standing by, that kind of thing is fine."

As long as their own irregularities didn't get through, it was reliably known from experience that the enemy would at least hold off and watch quietly until midnight.
As long as the fact that the Dragon Carriage was fleeing didn't come to light, keeping Emilia and the villagers away from danger wouldn't be difficult.

"Get the method for the regular check-in out of him, and have him relay this information to the two connected locations."

"There might be a code word or something, so I'll keep an eye out for that too. Ehehe, Subaru-kyun might have shed a layer today—Feri-chan's happy about that."

Nodding at Subaru's instructions, Ferris, pleased for whatever reason, took the Dialogue Mirror and turned back to the man in high spirits. Watching his back as he carried out interrogation and espionage while humming a tune, Subaru called over Wilhelm and Julius.

"I've got a favor to ask you two—or rather, as we head into the final stage, I want you both to take on a role, if that's alright?"

Man does what he can and leaves the rest to heaven—in order to win, every hand that could be played had to be played.
Because not once, not a single time, had heaven ever smiled on Subaru while he sat and did nothing.

—Having wiped out the Witch Cultists hiding among the merchants and eliminated the threat from within, the immediate worry was gone.

"May fortune favor you—"

The way Emilia had said that as she boarded the Dragon Carriage, turning a gaze mixed with gratitude and worry toward Subaru and the others staying behind in the village, lingered heavily in his heart.
Wrapped in his perception-obstructing robe, having never revealed his identity to her, Subaru's heartache was hard to put into words. If he were honest, he wished he could tell her everything right now and take her hand.

"Well, wishing's all I can do for now, gotta hold out. I've got the memory of holding her hand and not getting yelled at tucked away in my heart, so I'm not gonna cry or anything."

Deceiving himself with some idle joke, he saw off the departing Dragon Carriage along with the knights.
Several guard knights rode atop Earth Dragons, escorting the carriage as it evacuated. They'd taken every precaution to avoid drawing attention, even splitting into two groups partway.
Ram's group was headed for the Sanctuary, Emilia's group for the royal capital. On the Sanctuary side, Roswaal would be there to greet them; on the royal capital side, Crusch. With safeguards laid so thoroughly, their safety was in some ways far more assured than Subaru's own.

The children who had begged to ride along with Emilia had also climbed aboard the carriage, holding tight to her hand. Remembering how genuinely happy Emilia had looked at that response, Subaru's own heart filled with warmth.
He was a little bothered by the look in Petra's eyes—she who'd ended up sitting beside Emilia as they boarded the carriage—something like rivalry burning in them, but he judged it probably wouldn't affect the bigger picture. Petra in particular was a girl who listened well to what Subaru told her.

"Sweeping her along on a wave of happy feelings, riding the moment to get Emilia-tan to safety. ...I've really turned into a real bastard who toys with people's hearts. Quite a change from the days when the neighborhood used to say I couldn't read a room or anyone's feelings."

He'd also asked the children to make sure, while inside the carriage, that they didn't mention Subaru's name and didn't let Emilia step outside.
Because last time, Subaru had judged that the reason she'd ended up returning to the battlefield had of course been the former, but the latter too.

At that time, if someone had been riding along in Emilia's carriage, someone there to hold her back from her actions—wouldn't things not have turned out that way?
If there had been someone there who truly cared for her, holding her back from the heart, then surely her kind self couldn't have brushed that aside.
That was exactly what Subaru had deliberately engineered this time—by giving the children a role to play, and by keeping Subaru's own presence hidden from Emilia.

If she ever learned the full picture of this later, it might be an act she'd deeply despise him for.
Even knowing the future through Return by Death, deceiving someone's heart because of it—worse, manipulating it for his own convenience—wasn't something to be forgiven. And so—

"I'm never, ever telling anyone about this, for the rest of my life..."

He wasn't quite so shameless a person as to say "not caught, not a crime," but at the very least, he wasn't the masochistic type who'd volunteer to confess it himself either. He resolved to take it to his grave.
And then, to that Subaru—

"Looks like the village folk and the half-devil lass have gone on ahead, huh."

—came Ricardo, the burly beastman, shouldering his great cleaver and approaching with his rough manner of speech, his massive frame swaying. Subaru grimaced at the sight of him, slightly spattered with blood from elsewhere, and let out a small breath.

"Don't call my precious Emilia-tan a half-devil, half-dog."

"Ohh, being called half-dog stings way more than I expected! Learned something today! Masterpiece, an absolute masterpiece!"

Subaru's sarcasm was laughed off so heartily that he could only manage a wry smile himself. Then, his expression suddenly going blank, he lifted his gaze toward the forest.

"So how'd it go? The raid."

"They knew where we were and had basically zero guard up on their end. Screwing that up wouldn't be what a mercenary getting paid for the job does. Went smooth as silk."

Opening his beastly mouth wide, Ricardo grinned fiercely, showing off his fangs.
As if to prove his words, mercenaries came pouring out of the forest one after another, mounted on their Ligers, tearing through the now-empty village to prove they were alive and well.

"Yeehaw, we wiped 'em out!"

"We properly took prisoners, we did. Don't go saying things that make us sound bad, big sis."

Watching the siblings, who'd taken the lead in charging around, having a conversation that was both heartwarming and terrifying at once, it seemed the results had been solid.
Even knowing the odds had been favorable, it had been a scene full of anxiety for the one who'd sent them off. Seeing them all safe and sound, Subaru let out a breath of relief, patting his chest.

"Ah, right, and, well, there's kind of a problem, actually."

But then Ricardo lowered his voice and leaned his tall frame down close to Subaru. Sensing something ominous in that attitude, Subaru furrowed his brow.

"Wh-what is it. I've got a bad feeling, so try to sound as upbeat as possible, please."

"Yeah? Well then, no holding back!!"

Taking Subaru's attempt to deflect his own dread at face value, Ricardo answered with an absurdly loud voice. The volume, loud enough to burst an eardrum, rang through Subaru's skull, and he grimaced. Around them, Mimi, riding her Liger, circled about shouting "Yeehaw! Party time!"

"Shut up! That's not what I meant! If we let our movements get found out after going through all that trouble to evacuate everyone quietly, what then?!"

"Bro! Bro! Your voice's plenty loud too, y'know!"

Ricardo roared with laughter at Subaru's shouted retort.
Left unconvinced by the shift from Ricardo's earlier attitude, Subaru shook his head and said "never mind" before continuing,

"Just tell me. If some problem's come up, I need to hear it before we move forward with the plan, or we can't adjust. We need to cut down on every uncertain variable we can right now, so—"

"Nah, no problem with the plan or anything, probably. Just, catching the Witch Cultists went fine, but... there's a separate issue on top of that."

"Separate...?"

Not understanding what Ricardo meant, Subaru tilted his head, voicing his doubt. At Subaru's reaction, Ricardo nodded, and, deciding it'd be faster to show than tell, raised his arm.

"Hey, bring that guy from earlier over here."

The subordinate given the order nodded and ran off, bringing back a single Liger.
On its back was a person bound with rope, and as it drew steadily closer, Subaru's expression shifted through doubt, unease, and finally certainty.

"Raided the Witch Cultists' den, and found him in the back. Seems like this fella just had rotten luck and got himself caught by 'em."

As Ricardo spoke, scratching his head, the gagged figure let out a groan. Whether it was a complaint about his unjust treatment or a plea for his life, the rather pathetic sight overcame Subaru's initial shock, and—

"Pfft."

—unable to hold it in, he burst out laughing.
Pointing a finger at the bound, immobilized figure, Subaru howled with laughter, and—

"You—here I thought you were missing, and you were just caught?! Otto!"

—shouted the name of the last cast member to appear, the one who, through nothing but bad luck, had been unable to show his face in any scene until now.
