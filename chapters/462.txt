Arc 6 Chapter 57: "Let's Set That Aside for Now"

"Honestly, it had Beatrice on edge, I suppose. But since it was Subaru's request, she managed to grit her teeth and bear it, I suppose."

"Master's an immortal man, y'know. Ashi wasn't worried at all. If anything, ashi was suspecting that the moment she stood behind Master, Shortstuff Number Two would just explode and scatter into pieces, y'know."

"Ah, uh……"

Trading these words back and forth, the faces slowly climbing the spiral staircase came into view, and Meili's pale cheeks flushed red, her lips opening and closing without producing words.

Glancing sidelong at that change in Meili, Subaru raised a hand toward the silhouette on the stairs with a "Yo."

"Thanks for holding the rear guard. I managed to avoid falling to my death, so I'm relieved."

"……No matter how much it was Subaru's order, if you'd actually fallen, it's doubtful whether Beatrice would have forgiven you. So in that sense too, you got lucky, I suppose."

Sniffing through her nose, holding up the hem of her dress as she climbed the stairs, was Beatrice. Behind her was Shaula, hands clasped behind her head, the two of them having climbed all the way back up to the fourth floor together, chatting amiably from far below on the spiral staircase.

"Wh-wh-what……B-brother? What's going on with those two, huh……?"

At the sight of Beatrice and Shaula, Meili turned to Subaru, aghast.

Faced with the girl's dismay, as if she hadn't been told any of this, Subaru crossed his arms.

"Well, I don't want to put it this way, but that plan just now was pretty much a tightrope walk, or rather……I want to think it's not likely, but there was totally a scenario where you shoved me off and I just plain died, right? It just so happened this time that I got to grab your arm in a cool way, so it worked out."

"B-but, didn't Sister keep watch over us too……?"

"Well, sure, I get that Emilia-chan's dangerously cute looks hide way more power than they let on, but there's still the off chance. If Emilia-chan carelessly tried to save me and ended up falling together with me, that would've been no laughing matter."

In fact, at the end of the previous loop, Subaru and Emilia had met with a fall from a great height together. He'd held her close, but hadn't been able to save her. —That was driven deep into his heart.

If the same situation ever happened again, this time Subaru wanted to insist he'd shove Emilia to safety no matter what, but there was no way to gather data on his own capacity for action under extreme conditions, so relying on that wasn't going to help.

"That's why I took measures so a worst-case scenario like that wouldn't even happen. If the two of them down there—Beatrice and Shaula—were watching, then for the time being, we'd be fine, that was the plan."

He'd seen with his own eyes that Beatrice was a formidable mage, and while the details on Shaula were unclear, he'd also confirmed she had unexpected strength that didn't match her appearance.

During the request for cooperation for this plan, he'd had her lift him up just to be sure, so there was no mistake.

Though he did still hold a grudge about being carried princess-style in front of Emilia and Beatrice.

"Going that far……there was a simpler, smarter way, wasn't there, huh?"

Hearing Subaru's explanation, Meili murmured, eyes downcast. The shame of having an embarrassing scene witnessed had faded, replaced instead by something like awkward guilt.

At her words, Subaru scratched his cheek with a finger, saying "Yeah."

"Like you said, I think there was a smarter, simpler way. I think so, but……"

"But what?"

"At the level my head can come up with, simple is just the flip side of compromise, and smart is next-door neighbors with cheating. And I……yeah, I didn't want to compromise or cheat."

"――――"

Hearing those words, the pupils in Meili's eyes narrowed, and she faintly bit her lip.

Watching her with a wry smile, Subaru clenched his fist tight.

He'd wanted to somehow fix everything.

He'd wished to somehow save everything.

So then, in order to somehow fix everything, he wanted to do everything he possibly could.

"That's why I didn't hesitate to ask Emilia-chan for help, or to ask Beatrice and the others either."

"Mm, that's right. I was surprised too, at first, when Subaru told me about this."

The one who said this was Emilia, still holding Meili from behind. She rested her chin on Meili's slender shoulder, and gazing up at Subaru gently, said,

"But I could tell right away that Subaru was really, really serious about it. And besides……"

"Besides?"

"――I was happy that Subaru came to talk to me about it. Subaru's usually the type who, by the time I notice, has already finished getting everything ready to solve on his own."

A smile joined her upward gaze, and Subaru held his breath before the purple-and-navy-eyed girl.

To Subaru, his cheeks stiffening, Emilia in turn nodded, her cheeks and lips softening.

"So I was happy that this time you talked to me about what to do, and let me think it through together with you from the start. Fufu, it's kind of strange, isn't it."

"……There's no point saying it now, but I'm honestly furious at the me from up until yesterday. No, but since seeing this face and hearing this voice is my privilege, maybe it's more fitting, event-wise, for me to gloat over yesterday's me instead……? Emilia-chan, what do you think?"

"Sorry. I don't really get what you're saying."

Still smiling, Subaru had his nonsense breezily deflected, and his shoulders drooped.

Just then, the two who had finally finished climbing the stairs joined the group of three.

"Once again, Beatrice is relieved that nothing happened, I suppose."

"'Nothing happened' is a bit misleading, though. Let's say, considering all the changes going on inside Meili's mind……there's a nothing-happened that's actually something."

"Ooh, as expected of Master! Ashi has no clue what you're saying at all, but when it comes to sounding cool with just the vibes, nobody beats you!"

"Do you actually have any respect for me?"

Shaula seemed to have a habit of being impressed by anything as a conditioned reflex, but when Subaru had first consulted about wanting to save Meili, she'd also been the first to agree.

Of course, it had more the feel of her not thinking deeply at all and just having her mind set on backing up whatever Subaru wanted to do, but it was true that he'd been helped by her presence all the same.

"Um, Beatrice……you're not mad at me, huh?"

"Of course Beatrice is mad, I suppose. But somehow you managed to stop just short of Betty snapping and bursting, I suppose. Given the business at the dunes too, Betty will call this one settled, I suppose."

"――――"

"But! What's being settled just now is only the matter from this trip. You still bear the crime of burning down the old mansion along with Betty's forbidden archive, I suppose. As long as that stands, Betty won't be forgiving you anytime soon, I suppose."

Crossing her short arms, Beatrice answered Meili's question with a stern look. At her words Meili held her breath, but Emilia soon laughed "fufu" and said,

"It's hard to tell, but just now Beatrice said 'anytime soon.' That means if Meili's a good girl, she'll properly forgive you. She's really, really kind, isn't she."

"Emilia! There's no need to say that, I suppose!"

"……I'll try to be careful about it, as much as I can, huh."

Her stern opinion's hidden side exposed, Beatrice reddened and Emilia smiled at her. Watching this, Meili responded in a small voice.

Watching it all, Subaru nodded several times, satisfied.

At last, he could say there had been visible progress.

At the very least, he'd been able to stop the girl from acting on her resolve to kill Subaru. Even so, that was still just one of the tragedies that would occur in this tower——

"――So, Master, is this really okay?"

"Hm?"

While he was lost in thought, Shaula, now standing right next to Subaru, spoke up casually. Beside Subaru, Shaula narrowed her black eyes, watching the scene unfold—Beatrice and Meili conversing with Emilia in the middle.

Then, to Subaru who frowned in suspicion, without changing her tone of voice, she said,

"She's the girl who tried to get Master killed, y'know. Is it really fine with no punishment at all?"

"Don't ask such dangerous stuff. ……It's fine. As for punishment, Meili already got hers earlier. But nobody ever properly taught her why she was getting punished, that's why she ended up like that. That's what I'm going to teach her now, going forward."

"Master said this too, but what if she tries to kill Master again after that?"

"That'll just mean I'm a really bad teacher. But I'm not doing this alone."

To Shaula's probing, Subaru answered like that.

If he were told to single-handedly change the ethical outlook cultivated in Meili since childhood as an assassin, that would be extremely difficult, and honestly, not a weight of responsibility he could bear alone.

But Subaru had no intention of trying to do everything by himself.

Stopping Meili's murderous act, too, had relied on Emilia and the others. He'd have Emilia and Beatrice keep him company going forward as well.

"Naturally, I'll need your help too, Shaula. Talking someone out of a lifelong value system—that's gonna be a long haul."

"……Ashi too, y'know?"

"Of course. You're……well, you're kind of a cautionary example in a way, but I'm not leaving you out. Your body's overflowing with maternal energy, so use that to help crack Meili's stubborn heart open."

To Shaula, who pointed at herself with a surprised look, Subaru said this casually and shrugged.

It was a mystery what she was so surprised about, but since she was prone to a lot of overreactions in general, Subaru didn't press the matter, figuring it was just part of that pattern.

At Subaru's judgment, Shaula pressed her hands to her own face and said,

"Ashi too, huh. Ashi too, together with Master, ashi too……ehe, ehehe. Eheheheh……"

"Uh……what's wrong with you……"

"Nothing! Ashi's decided! Just like Master ordered, ashi's gonna raise that Shortstuff Number Two into a proper decent human being~!"

Her face suddenly brightening, Shaula dashed off toward Emilia and the others. Then she lightly lifted Meili's small body and hugged her against her own ample chest.

"Kyaa!? Wh-what is this, Sister Naked! This is so sudden, it's shocking, huh!"

"It's fine, it's fine, feel free to spoil yourself on ashi as much as you want, Shortstuff Number Two. Ashi's chest belongs to Master, but just for now, ashi'll share some with Shortstuff Number Two too. Like giving to the poor from the wealthy, that kind of thing!"

"Wait, Brother!? You put more weird ideas into Sister Naked's head again, didn't you, huh!"

Meili, being handled however Shaula pleased, raised her voice, deciding it was Subaru's doing.

"Well, think of it as the price for making everyone worry, and let yourself be doted on nicely. It's fine, from what I can tell there's no ill will."

"……Honestly. You're all such hopeless people, huh. Fine, I'll forgive you, huh. But what happens here stays secret from everyone else, huh."

So saying, Meili puffed out her cheeks, sandwiched between Shaula's chest. But at her words, Subaru scratched his head, saying "Ah——"

At his reaction Meili frowned, and Emilia answered in Subaru's place.

"Um, Meili, this is really, really hard to say, but……"

"……I have a bad feeling about this, huh."

Whether Meili's bad feeling would prove correct or not—

The answer became clear immediately, in her reaction to Emilia's next words.

That was——

"――Ram and the others who aren't here right now, they also properly heard Subaru's consultation about this."

Meili scowled magnificently.

The room used for meals and discussions—let's call it the "Great Hall"—was where Subaru and the others returning were first greeted by Ram, standing with her hands on her hips.

"I see. So it was settled without incident. Not bad work. Ram will praise you for it."

Greeting the five of them returning safely, Ram said this with quiet eyes.

Subaru chose to take that as probably her version of the highest praise. In any case, he too had talked big to her about Meili's matter. He was relieved not to end up in a situation of regretting his own grandiose words with an "I told you so."

"It may not need saying, but she stood there just like that the whole time, waiting for your return."

"……That's really something that didn't need saying. Restrain yourself, Echidna."

"The moment I realized it's me controlling this body right now, this is the attitude I get. How agreeable."

The one who said this, making Ram's brow harden, was Echidna, who had been sitting on the floor of the Great Hall. She received Ram's sharp tongue with a smile, then looked at Subaru and the others.

"Mm—"

"So, why is she pouting so magnificently?"

What Echidna pointed out was Meili, being carried piggyback on Shaula's back.

Making a sour face and groaning, Meili didn't answer Echidna's question either, turning her face away in a sulky manner. She had completely retreated into a sulk.

"Don't tell me—she's angry that her own murder plot was thwarted. In that case, leaving her unrestrained does give me some slight unease……"

"Ah, no no, that's not it, not it."

Echidna's somewhat dangerous assumption was likely a manifestation of the wariness she'd shown Subaru even in the previous loop—her desire to protect the woman whose body this was, Anastasia.

Unable to bear having that turn into some strange misunderstanding, Subaru firmly denied it.

"It's simply that her own thinking got seen through, and moreover that everyone found out about it, that's just embarrassing for her. Isn't that childlike and cute?"

"Given what she was trying to do, it does feel odd for the word 'childlike' to come up……hmm. So, even losing one's memories, one's essential nature doesn't easily change. You surprise me, Natsuki-kun."

"If you're enjoying yourself, I'm honored, that's the thing."

Winking at Echidna's gaze, Subaru got a scoffing "Hah" from Ram in response. Then, sweeping his eyes around the room, he said,

"Huh, where's Julius? Where'd he go? Bathroom, maybe……"

"――To be thought of as attending to small business while you're involved in a great matter—that would be unwelcome."

"……You didn't say 'small' specifically, did you. Might've been the big kind of business."

A voice called from behind, and Subaru turned around, twisting his cheek at the sound of it. Receiving that bad grin was Julius, twirling his own forelock with a finger.

Returning from outside the Great Hall, Emilia tilted her head at him.

"Julius, did your stomach hurt?"

"Emilia-sama, please don't take his words too much to heart. Certainly he is your knight foremost, a man to be trusted above all others without question, but on occasion his conduct does go a bit far……"

"Hey hey, don't go putting weird ideas into Emilia-chan's head. Besides, you're the one who wasn't here. Saying stuff like that just because I said 'bathroom' real quick."

"――. Heh."

Meeting Subaru's bite with his yellow eyes, Julius let a beat pass before his lips relaxed into a smile.

A laugh like an exhale, thoroughly dandyish and suited to his appearance, but to Subaru it somehow looked like a smile that didn't quite sit right on him.

"Emilia, and Natsuki-kun, no need for concern. Following our earlier discussion, Julius was keeping watch outside. Given that Meili possesses the power to control Witchbeasts, should she truly attempt to carry things through, the greatest danger to be wary of would be Witchbeasts outside the tower."

"So I was monitoring outside the tower from the balcony we found last night. Fortunately, no abnormalities were seen outside the tower, so I judged that things inside were likely fine as well, but……"

As he spoke, Julius turned his gaze toward Meili. Atop Shaula's back, Meili noticed Julius's gaze and pursed her lips, looking uncomfortable.

But at Meili's demeanor, Julius's cheeks loosened into a faint smile, and he said,

"It seems things concluded safely, which is the best outcome. When you said you'd lost your memories, I felt as though the heavens and earth had half turned upside down……"

"That's way too exaggerated. Well, not that I, having lost my memories, have any right to say that."

"True. 'Heavens and earth turning upside down' is a bit much. A bit, mind you. ……Well, in any case, I'm glad you're safe. If we'd asked even Emilia-sama for cooperation and it had ended in failure, there would have been no way to make up for it."

"Yeah, I'm relieved too. ……We've got the right distance for this back-and-forth of ours, right?"

"――Heh."

At Subaru pointing this out, Julius smiled that dandyish smile again.

This time the smile seemed free of the faint hesitation from before, and Subaru was satisfied.

"――――"

This marked, in fact, their fifth encounter since Subaru had begun losing his memories.

In that time, there hadn't been many opportunities to spend peaceful moments, but originally, the time spent with Emilia and the others should have been mostly made up of such peaceful moments. In the midst of that, surely "Natsuki Subaru" had had his own way of relating to them.

Starting from there, without straying too far off, without making Emilia and the others sad, Subaru wanted to trace "Natsuki Subaru's" footsteps as faithfully as he could.

He didn't want to make Emilia and the others confront the reality that they were gradually losing "Natsuki Subaru." ——Besides, that way, surely they wouldn't have to face that reality at all.

If Natsuki Subaru disappeared, and "Natsuki Subaru" came back, surely.

"So, with that, let's have our newly reborn good-buddies team conquer the tower. Any objections?"

"Yeah, none. Let's do our best."
"That 'good-buddies team' name is a bit hard to accept, I suppose."

At any rate, Subaru's raised-fist opinion got two extreme reactions—full agreement from Emilia and Beatrice. The rest showed no sign of objection either, so he was relieved.

"So we may take it that the matter of her is resolved, no problem?"

"Yeah, it's fine. She shouldn't secretly plot to kill me again, not under normal circumstances anymore. Though whether that holds going forward depends on Meili growing up watching our backs, so make sure none of us shows her an uncool back, or a terrible one."

"I see. A matter of appearances, then. In that case, leave it to me."

Glancing toward Meili, Julius drew in his thin chin, convinced by Subaru's words.

Called "appearances," it might sound a bit off, but showing one's back to another—in essence, meaning becoming a role model—Julius did indeed seem the right man for it.

Subaru couldn't say he'd spent that long with him, even now, but he could still tell that Julius possessed a strong sense of self-discipline and excelled at every manner of conduct.

That was the fruit of his own talent, a manifestation of the good breeding he'd grown up with, and at the same time proof that he himself had made the effort to acquire such things.

Things properly earned through effort. Noble things.

"――Subaru, are you okay? Can you keep talking?"

"Whoa!?"

Suddenly, Emilia touched the arm of Subaru, who'd been holding his breath, and peered worriedly into his face. At that closeness from Emilia, Subaru, snapping back to himself, hastily jumped backward.

Seeing Subaru's overreaction, Emilia let out a small "Ah……" and said,

"Somehow, ever since waking up……no, ever since you said you'd lost your memories, hasn't Subaru just kept being startled by me? Am I really that strange? Is something on my face?"

"No, that's, not at all. It's just, you've got cute eyes, and a cute nose, and cute lips, and cute ears on there."

"Cute……fufu, thanks. But then why?"

"I think it's because Emilia-chan's cuteness doesn't add up part by part, it multiplies and shoots up. Also your voice is cute. Your hair's cute too. This is bad, you're an angel."

Too dazzling to look at directly, Subaru covered his face, peeking at Emilia through the gaps between his fingers. Hearing this remark of Subaru's, the one who reacted sharply was Beatrice.

"――! How would that sound if you said it more Subaru-like, I wonder!?"

"Huh!? Eh, what's, huh!?"

"So, say those words about Emilia looking like an angel, but in a more Subaru-like way, I suppose."

"What kind of humiliation is that!? No way! That's embarrassing! Beatrice, you're cute like an angel too! Don't sulk!"

"It's a fact that Betty is cute like an angel, but that's not what I meant, I suppose……"

Beatrice's shoulders slumped in disappointment. Feeling a pang of guilt at the sight, Subaru stroked Beatrice's head, and everyone formed a circle in the Great Hall.

They'd held discussions like this many times now. Though it was a source of worry that no notable progress seemed to show each time, so it was about time to push the conversation forward more boldly.

"So, with that, once again, Meili has joined our team. For the sake of the newly-inducted Meili's field trip in society, I want to get out of this sandy tower ASAP. Any opinions?"

"That's a way of talking that makes me doubt you've really lost your memories. ……Still, Barusu losing his memories, among serious problems, is on the lighter end, but it's still a problem."

"Yeah, that's right. It's not like it could just vanish out of nowhere, so we have to somehow get Subaru's memories back……"

"Ah, about that—can we set that aside for now?"

Raising a hand to solicit opinions, Subaru put a stop to Ram and Emilia's discussion. At his words, not just the two of them but everyone participating in the discussion went "Eh?" in surprise.

The only one not surprised was probably Shaula, but since she was currently taking Subaru's words at face value and absorbed in caring for Meili, let's set that aside for the moment.

"Subaru……please don't worry Betty and the others so much, I suppose. Just losing your memories was already shocking enough, but saying it's fine even if they don't come back is way too self-destructive, I suppose."

"Yeah, that's right, Subaru. Could it be you think you're being a burden to us? But Subaru, you talked to us about Meili, didn't you. In the same way, we'll think about Subaru's memories together too. Please, okay?"

"That's a persuasion that's fiercely stabbing my heart!! No, but hear me out a sec! It's not that I'm being self-destructive about this either. There's a proper reason!"

Against Emilia and Beatrice's worry, Subaru managed to make excuses even as his heart was pricked. Then, raising a finger for everyone to see, he said "Listen, okay?"

"I feel bad that my memories just up and vanished from my head in this situation, and I'm super grateful that everyone wants to get them back for me. But—is there anyone here who thinks this memory loss has nothing to do with this tower's existence?"

"Master, you knocked your head on the toilet bowl once before and lost your memory, y'know. Can't be trusted."

"Peanut gallery, quiet a sec! That's also something I can't just let slide, but not now!"

Growing irritated at the sideline commentary, Subaru said "Anyway!" and reset the conversation.

"What I'm saying is, there's a connection between the tower and my memories going missing. In other words……"

"――In other words, if we go about fulfilling the conditions to conquer the tower, we'll naturally obtain the cause of Natsuki-kun's lost memories, or at least the key to it. Is that what you mean?"

"Ah, yeah, yeah, that's it. Exactly that!"

Echidna having grasped his meaning and said it for him, Subaru nodded repeatedly. Taking in Echidna's opinion, Julius said "I see," putting a hand to his chin.

"If the tower's mechanism is what stole Subaru's memories, then conquering the tower brings us closer to the answer. Or perhaps the very trigger for Subaru losing his memories was that he got too close to the answer."

"Quite plausible, wouldn't you say. The flash of insight he showed when conquering 'Taygeta' made use of knowledge that, frankly, was utterly beyond our reach. Perhaps it's precisely because of knowledge only Natsuki-kun would know that he got too far ahead of us, and as a result lost his memories."

"Wait wait wait wait, that's giving me way too much credit. I'm just a shut-in, you know? My special skills are, like, making bed sheets look nice and neat, and sewing, that kind of thing."

"Ah, look, Subaru. This embroidery on this clothing, Subaru did it for me. Doesn't seeing it remind you of something? Isn't it cute? It's Puck."

"Hmm, cute cat embroidery. But, I don't really have any recollection of it, sorry."

Handling a conversation with quite a gap in the level of thought involved, Emilia, met with Subaru's denial, dejectedly stroked with a finger the picture of a cat embroidered on her clothes.

Since it even had a name given to it, it was probably a pet cat. It didn't seem to have been brought along to this tower, so he wanted to hurry and get back safely so she could be reunited with her pet cat too.

In any case——

"Barusu noticed something he shouldn't have, and as a result, rushed ahead and had his memory taken……a convincing story."

"There's a barb in the way Ram put that, but Betty mostly agrees, I suppose. And—prioritizing conquering the tower, as Subaru thinks……I hate it, but there's a point to it, I suppose."

"Beatrice……"

Beatrice's dissatisfaction was, after all, likely directed at the situation where Subaru's memories couldn't be made the top priority. He was truly grateful she'd think that way, but Subaru had his own reason why he couldn't put his own memories first. ——If he leisurely went searching for his memories, he wouldn't make it in time to prevent the tragedy of the tower.

What had happened. What would happen. Subaru——no, Subaru and everyone together, had to unite and prepare for that calamity.

"So with that, I want to prioritize conquering the tower. I want everyone to go along with it, for now."

"――――"

"I don't think leaving things as they are is fine either, you know. It's just, my situation is a problem that fell out of nowhere. If we keep putting off the original problem because of it, we're never going to get any closer to our goal, no matter how much time passes. I want to do what needs to be done."

And so, Subaru earnestly appealed to everyone.

He would do his best not to show the downsides of "Natsuki Subaru" not being present here, as much as possible. In exchange, he asked for everyone's help.

At Subaru's request like that, everyone lost their words for a while, but——

"――You really are a fool."

Ram said, letting out a breath and shaking her head, then looked around at everyone with her pale pink eyes.

"It seems that even losing his memories, his sorry excuse for a brain doesn't change. In other words, even if his memories came back, in terms of contribution there'd be no great difference from the current Barusu……so prioritizing Barusu's memories would only be a loss. Let's conquer the tower, and hope his memories come back as a side effect."

"'Side effect,' there's gotta be a better way to put that."

"There isn't. You dropped your memories as a side effect of conquering the tower, didn't you? Then pick them back up as a side effect too. Don't trouble Ram over it."

——I will surely remember, I will get them back, that was the promise she'd made, with Ram.

So when Subaru said he would set his memories aside, the shock it dealt to Ram's heart was immeasurable. And yet, precisely because of that promise, Ram was the first to say what she'd just said.

Because Subaru, apparently a habitual promise-breaker, and she, who had still made that promise with him anyway.

"There's no proactive method to reclaim his memories. I'll side with Natsuki-kun and Ram's position. To speak optimistically, there's also the possibility that time itself will jog the memories loose."

"I'll call mine a reluctant agreement. It won't be top priority. Conquering the tower comes first. But should we find a prospect for restoring your memory, we will prioritize that. ——Emilia-sama and Beatrice-sama should not be made to wear such sorrowful faces for long."

At Echidna and Julius's words, Subaru nodded deeply.

Then he turned his gaze to Emilia and Beatrice as well. Receiving Subaru's gaze, the two of them hesitated slightly after all, but——

"――This time, however hard it is, I'll bear with it. But."

"But?"

"Once in a while, let me be the one most worried about you too, okay?"

"Ugh……sorry."

Realizing this was as good as asking him to put worry about him on the back burner, Subaru bowed his head at Emilia's request.

Seeing the two of them like that, Beatrice sighed and said,

"What Betty wanted to say, Emilia said for her, I suppose. That should have hit hardest, so make sure you properly reflect on it, I suppose."

"――Yeah, got it."

And so, having secured everyone's approval, the policy of prioritizing the conquest of the tower was firmly settled once more.

With that established, there was something Subaru wanted to propose first.

It was——

"――Should we all search 'Taygeta's' archive together, to see if there's a book of Reid's?"
