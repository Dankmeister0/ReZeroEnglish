One Day II Part 6: "One More Loose End"

"Still, I wonder if a plan like that's really gonna work out..."

"Really? I thought it was a suuuper good plan. Anne and Ryuzu-san's acting fooled them completely — Garfiel and Frederica fell for it hook, line, and sinker."

"You think so? ...I was more struck by what a terrible actress Ryuzu-san turned out to be, and how Annerose lost her nerve completely at the crucial moment."

"You're being loud, Subaru."

Waiting in the dining hall for the results of the plan, Annerose cut in on Subaru as he reminisced about what had just happened. Her cheeks were flushed with heat, and watching her blush with embarrassment, Subaru found himself thinking, offhandedly, that she really did look like a girl her age when she did that.

The scheme Annerose had proposed to mend the rift between the siblings was simple enough in outline. The sticking point Emilia had raised, and that had left Subaru scratching his head — giving Garfiel and Frederica a shared goal and throwing them into the same place — Annerose had cleared without much trouble at all, by making use of a "memory" the two of them shared.

The meat pie Frederica apparently made from time to time, it seemed, had been her specialty ever since her days in the "Sanctuary." Whether Garfiel could make it too had been anyone's guess, but—

"Frederica used to complain about it all the time. How this was a dish of memories — Grandmother taught her the recipe, and Mother made it for them. Naturally, it wasn't just Frederica who grew up eating it; her little brother Garfiel would have too, so I figured there was a good chance he'd picked it up from Ryuzu-san as well. From what I've seen, Garfiel was quite the grandmother's boy."

"I got no complaints about the part where you figured that out. What I've got a problem with is everything besides the insight."

"Muu."

Annerose puffed out her cheeks, but that didn't erase the earlier blunder. Things had gone smoothly right up through luring Frederica in and giving an excuse to send her to the kitchen. The trouble was the motive she'd used, and how she'd tried to brush off Frederica's suspicions.

"'You'll drop dead on the spot if you don't eat meat pie' — what kind of bizarre disease is that supposed to be? Apologize to the meat pie."

"It was just a little slip of the tongue. And why exactly should I be the one apologizing—"

"That part, yeah, I'll admit was a little much. Here, look, I'll apologize with you."

"W-well, if you insist. If Emilie says so, then I suppose it can't be helped!"

Her face bright red, Annerose threw herself wholeheartedly into Emilia's offer.
Averting his eyes from that heartwarmingly girl-on-girl scene, Subaru glanced over at Ryuzu, who was making herself small at the corner of the table.

"Ryuzu-san, you're making a face like you feel guilty about tricking Garfiel and getting your way with him."

"That's not... no, no, no, wait! What is that, talking like you're inflating my little bit of guilt just to poke it with a needle. Stop it, would you, it's making my chest hurt."

At Subaru's mischievous words, Ryuzu recovered a bit of her usual expression. Then, realizing that the point of Subaru's remark had been precisely to draw that spirit back out of her—

"It's a bit late to say this, but... I still wonder, even now, whether this was the right thing to do. Of course, it's a good thing those two are making up. But..."

"Then there's nothing to wonder about. Those two would've ended up back to normal sooner or later even if we'd just left them alone. So the outcome doesn't change. All we did was bring about the same result a little sooner. ...But if it can be sooner, I figure sooner's better."

"Why's that?"

"Because time spent dithering is wasted, and happy time is too precious to waste. People are all gonna die eventually, no matter what, so isn't it better to move while there's still plenty of sand left in the hourglass?"

"――――"

At Subaru's words, Ryuzu's eyes went round, and then she let out a weary sigh.

"Su-bou, you really are something. Living without much hesitation, aren't you."

"Not really. There probably aren't many people out there who get hung up and dither over stuff as small as I do. But I don't think I've ever agonized over things that don't need agonizing over, and going forward, I want to keep it that way."

"Things that don't need agonizing over."

"Right. If there's two people I want to see make up, and everyone'll be happier once they do, then there's no need to agonize — just make them make up. If there's someone I want to be with, put off the agonizing, go up to them, and just say 'EMT!' That's how I've been thinking about it lately."

Of course, it wasn't something he could apply across the board. As he'd just told Ryuzu, Subaru was a weak man who agonized plenty over small things. And yet time was limited, and the choices available to him were narrow and few. So at the very least, he wanted to be unhesitating about the choices he did make.

To Subaru's answer, Ryuzu responded with a "I suppose so," and then—

"At my age, there's still far too much to learn, and too much being taught to me. If it had all ended with the 'Sanctuary,' I don't think I'd ever have come to think this way."

"If you're living a normal life, you don't get tired of living all that often. I bet everybody knows that much even without me guaranteeing it."

"Then I suppose I'll make the most of what time I've got left too. For now, I'll look forward to my two cute grandchildren making up and coming to spoil their old granny a little."

"Can't really picture those two coming to you all sweetly, though."

Earnest, straightlaced Frederica, and contrary, twisted Garfiel. Neither of them had the temperament to be openly affectionate with their grandmother. The fact that, deep down, the two of them — no, all three of them — wanted family affection more than anything else made it all the more heartwarming.

"My apologies for the interruption, everyone, in the midst of your pleasant conversation. My rudeness."

Just then, Subaru's ear, loosened by such imaginings, was struck quietly by the voice of a butler who had appeared in the room without a sound. Startled, Subaru's eyes went wide, and Clind came to stand at Annerose's side.

"What is it, Clind? Right now I'm spending a happy time with Emilie."

"I feel it deeply regretful to intrude on such a time. My distress. However, Roswaal-sama has returned, and I must inform you of it as well, Ojou-sama. My counsel."

"Uncle has...? Honestly, that man always comes back as if he's timed it perfectly."

At Clind's words, Annerose furrowed her brow and muttered discontentedly. While Subaru felt a pang of pity for Roswaal's usual conduct at earning that kind of reputation even from relatives, Annerose rose from her seat.

"Since Uncle has apparently returned, I shall go greet him. Emilie, Subaru, and Ryuzu-san, please wait here quietly for the siblings to return. ...Especially you two, Emilie — you're going to get busy."

"Um? All right. We'll wait here, then."

To Emilia's obedient nod, Annerose sent a smile and a gentle look. Then the girl turned back to Subaru and aimed at him a piercing gaze and a negative aura.
While Subaru twisted his lips at the difference in treatment, Annerose left the room, taking Clind with her. At that moment, tea that had somehow been poured without anyone noticing was set before the three remaining people, and Subaru nearly let out a strange yelp.

"Did anybody just see Clind-san put that tea down there?"

"Nope, didn't notice. As always, Clind-san's technique is amazing."

"Mm, the hand of a craftsman. Cooled to just the right temperature for my tongue."

"Mine's just the right heat for someone who can't handle hot food, too... What about you, Emilia-tan?"

"I like it hot, so it's suuuper hot."

"What even is Clind-san?"

Annerose might say to just accept it as "the way things are," but Subaru couldn't quite bring himself to. Was this some fundamental difference between someone who'd been raised in this world and someone living in it now? Wondering that, he glanced over at Emilia and Ryuzu, and the two of them seemed to be feeling their own respective sense of threat from Clind. Yep, definitely beyond the norm. And then—

"Well, if it ain't the whole gang of schemers, all cozied up in one place."

A few minutes after Annerose and the others had left, the door opened and a blond tiger of a man poked his head in. He seemed to have already figured out that he'd been caught in an obvious trap set by Subaru and the others, and his expression carried a heavy trace of complicated feelings. Still—

"From the looks of it, seems like you've figured out we're the bad guys here."

"Fell for it good and proper, didn't I. Real pathetic, huh."

"So, so? Did you two manage to talk it out properly?"

Emilia's face lit up with curiosity as Garfiel gnashed his fangs and walked closer. Beside her, Ryuzu, shrinking her neck uncomfortably, also had her ears trained entirely on Garfiel's words.
Seeing the women's attitudes, Garfiel let out a sigh and said—

"Ah, thanks a whole bunch for the uncalled-for meddling. Me and my sister... well, we talked it out, we did. So there's nothin' more to worry about."

"Really? But then, why didn't you come back holding hands?"

"Like hell I'd do somethin' that embarrassin'! Even after makin' up, it ain't like a brother and sister just go holdin' hands easy as that. Don't make me laugh."

"I wonder. It's not something to be embarrassed about at all, though — it's a lovely thing."

It wasn't teasing, but genuine, sincere disappointment, and Garfiel had no further words for that. Instead he looked at Ryuzu, who still seemed at a loss for words, and said—

"Old hag."

"...What is it, Gar-bou."

"Sorry for worryin' you. I'm good now, and Sis is good too. Nothin' to worry about."

Saying that, Garfiel rubbed the tip of his nose with a finger, and Ryuzu was silent for a moment. Then her lips loosened into a smile — a smile aged well beyond her childlike appearance.

"I see. Then I suppose I can breathe easy too. Don't go putting too much strain on an old woman's heart and body, now. It'll only hasten my time to be called home."

"When you say that, it ain't even a joke, so watch it."

Garfiel snorted at Ryuzu's now-relaxed manner.

"And, boss. And you too, Emilia-sama — sorry for the trouble."

"Don't sweat it. Me and Emilia-tan just took it on as a way to kill time while fixing up the relationships in the mansion. Nothing worth thanking us over. Right?"

"Subaru, was killing time really all you meant by it? It's an important problem for the two of them, so you have to think about it more seriously than that. Naughty."

"Huh!? The 'downplaying it out of embarrassment' pattern backfiring on me!?"

The pattern where Emilia didn't catch Subaru's roundabout consideration. Or so he thought, but Emilia suddenly carved a smile onto her lips and—

"Fufu, just kidding, just kidding. I understand just fine. Subaru's not honest about these things, is he."

"No way... Is this what they call EMK (Emilia-tan Majorly a Kobitsune)? Her charm just keeps going up... are you trying to kill me..."

"What about the part where I said thanks, huh? Where'd that go, oi."

At Garfiel's sullen face, Subaru and Emilia exchanged glances with each other. Then, together, they turned back to face Garfiel and said—

"—You're welcome."

—offering that as their response to his thanks.
Garfiel twisted his lips into a dissatisfied look, and Ryuzu shrugged her shoulders as if to say "honestly." Subaru gave a thumbs-up to Emilia, who was watching the two of them with a warm smile, and—

"Right, Garfiel. Speaking of making up, what happened to the meat pie that was supposed to be the trigger for all this? I'm genuinely looking forward to it."

"Pie ain't gonna bake up that easy. Slow and steady, deep in the oven — that's the secret to good flavor. 'Baumbehm's best when you let it rest' — now that's some words to live by."

"What's Baumbehm? Like a Baumkuchen or something? But I feel like Baumkuchen goes bad before it gets tastier if you let it sit."

According to Garfiel, the pie wouldn't be done baking for about two more hours. At that rate, it would likely collide with dinnertime and end up as one of the dishes served then.

"—Well, if that's how it is, isn't that rather convenient?"

At that moment, having lost any means of distracting himself from his hunger, Subaru was turning his mind to how to kill the next two hours when a voice he recognized made him and everyone else in the room turn around — and all four of them, in unison, made sour faces at the source of the voice.

"O-o-oh my, my. That's a rather harsh greeting for someone who's been away from the mansion, working hard, only to come home to this."

"It's not that we don't appreciate the effort or anything. But you should just accept that making faces like this is a conditioned reflex, and that it's your own fault, so there's nothing to be done about it. Besides, we're still going easy on you. Look at Garfiel — the guy's got veins popping on his forehead."

Veins bulged on Garfiel's forehead, and his eyes began to go bloodshot.
Even under the weight of his piercing glare, the man kept his cool, unruffled expression — easily recognizable by his distinctive way of speaking — the entrance of Roswaal L. Mathers.

He was the mastermind behind the whole affair, including the "Sanctuary" incident, and by confessing to it, his standing with most of those involved had plummeted straight into negative numbers. Among them, the flames of Garfiel's anger were still burning hot, and there was no telling when they might flare up bright red again.
Naturally, Subaru too held complicated feelings toward Roswaal, feelings made all the more tangled by what he'd heard in the continuation of Roswaal's confession.

The fact was that Roswaal hadn't orchestrated everything about the "Sanctuary" and the mansion incident. It seemed Roswaal had disclosed that fact to no one but Subaru. Subaru had no way of knowing his reasons, but he himself hadn't felt any particular urge to share it with the others, either.

Everything about that incident was Roswaal's responsibility — at least ninety percent of it was. As for the remaining ten percent, that would be filled in from elsewhere, not from Roswaal. For now, Subaru had decided that was better than spreading needless anxiety.

"Subaru, are you okay? You've got a suuuper weird look on your face."

"For real? What kind of face was I making?"

"Um, like this — all scrunched up with your eyes going all mean."

"For real? Was I making a cute face like that?"

"It wasn't cute!"

Pulling up the corners of her own eyes with her fingers, Emilia mimicked Subaru's expression. Even doing her best to portray something menacing that way, her cuteness won out — that was her charm.
Emilia pouted her lips while Garfiel, in a foul mood, dropped into a seat. Watching Ryuzu pour Garfiel's share of tea, Subaru addressed a word to Roswaal, who remained standing alone.

"Anyway, welcome back. So, did you accomplish what you set out to do?"

"Ahaa, Subaru-kun's kindness is the only thing that touches my heart here-e. And yes, there's no problem at all. I went out to visit a few of the towns and villages in the domain, and also to see our new place to move to."

"Never mind moving — you went around the whole domain? Was there some purpose to it?"

"Well, there was that whole incident with the lord's mansion burning down, after all-l. If I don't go around letting everyone know I'm alive and well, who knows what sort of ill intentions might crop up. I make it a principle never to slack on keeping the peace in my domain, you know-w."

"About the part where the lord himself was the one plotting the worst of it."

"How harsh-h. No harm came to the domain's people, and I even kept the truth hidden from the residents of Arlam Village. Keeping up such a prickly attitude is bound to cause trouble down the road, wouldn't you say-y?"

"Grgh."

Whatever you said, he'd twist it right back around. However much Subaru tried to needle him, Roswaal, having thrown off his mask, had recovered all his former composure. In truth, revealing that Roswaal had been the one pulling the strings behind the incident carried nothing but disadvantages, both for the Royal Selection and for the management of his domain. Which was why, among the villagers of Arlam, Roswaal remained, as ever, the "capable, upstanding lord."
The one exception who knew the truth was Petra, and even she understood that, given her position, revealing the truth would gain her nothing but self-satisfaction. So it was unlikely she'd take any drastic action. Being too clever sometimes forces cruel choices on a person.

"But that doesn't mean it's fine for you to use that knowingly to your advantage. Forget that, and once Emilia-tan becomes queen, you're getting the guillotine."

"How frightening. Although-h, by that point, my own objectives may well already have been achieved-d."

"It ain't just about achievin' your damn objectives, is it. Keep talkin' that kinda garbage and you'll make Ram cry for real, you bastard."

Surprisingly, it was Garfiel who cut in to stop the still-provocative Roswaal. Roswaal raised his eyebrows in apparent surprise at the reaction, then lightly raised both hands.

"Well, well, all right-t, I understand. I've no wish to pick a fight with you all either, you know-w. I only meant to explain that I'd returned, and yet somehow it's turned into this kind of quarrel. How unproductive-e."

"That's because you're the one talking in a way that makes Subaru and Garfiel mad. I know full well you're doing it on purpose. Enough of that already — stop trying to get people's attention that way. You're not a child."

"――――"

Roswaal, who had been trying to smooth things over from a position above them all, found himself pressed down from even further above by Emilia, hands on her hips. Roswaal's face showed surprise, and Emilia continued on.

"Even if you don't act all anxious like that all the time, none of us are going to forget what you did, or your promise. There's no point deliberately playing the villain and troubling everyone. Honestly, you're hopeless."

Emilia's tone was that of someone scolding a troublesome child.
And yet, at words from Emilia that might otherwise have been dismissed as a misplaced misunderstanding, Roswaal fell silent and offered no rebuttal. If anything, he narrowed his eyes, and the awkward expression on his face looked almost as if he'd been caught red-handed.

Surely Roswaal hadn't really been acting on such childish sentimentality — that couldn't be it.

"Well, I'll say this much — the wind's sure been knocked out of his sails. As expected of Emilia-tan."

"...? Um, thanks. Also, that wasn't the only reason you went around the domain, was it? Roswaal, what else did you do?"

"Ahaa, you've gotten quite sharp-eyed lately, haven't you. This tour was, as I said, to report that I'm safe and sound, and... also to lay the groundwork for the resettlement of the 'Sanctuary' residents."

"Resettlement preparations...!"

The one who couldn't stay silent at those words was Garfiel, who had been resting his chin on his hand. Ryuzu hurried back over as well, and Garfiel slammed the table.

"That mean you're talkin' about somewhere to take 'em in?"

"That's right. Given their connection to us as refugees, it's certainly true that taking them into Arlam Village would be the best option-n. But there's a limit to how many the village can take in. Especially when the number would increase by more than half the original population — a settlement that size simply can't manage it easily. Of course, we could always expand the village, but there's the matter of the barrier there, you know-w."

"The hell's a barrier? You tellin' me you went and stuck one o' those things somewhere else without learnin' your—"

"Wait, wait, Garfiel. That barrier's a completely different thing from the one in the 'Sanctuary.' Out around there, in the mountains, there's a whole lot of Witchbeasts crawling around. To keep them away, there are barriers set up here and there around the village. That's what Roswaal's talking about."

The barrier that surrounded Arlam Village and the mansion was also the cause of the Witchbeast incident. It wasn't exactly coexistence and mutual prosperity with the Witchbeasts, but the two sides kept to their own territories well enough that it worked out — which was presumably why expanding Arlam Village was difficult.

"Just as Subaru-kun explained, that's the gist of it-t. So-o, the 'Sanctuary' residents will need to be split — part of them into Arlam Village, and the rest divided up in small groups among a few other candidate sites. Wherever they end up being welcomed, it wouldn't do for them to keep clustering together as the same old 'Sanctuary' faces forever. Watching my little ones leave the nest is a heartbreak for me too, you know-w."

"Say what you like, you shameless—"

Unable to hold back at Roswaal's mock crying, Ryuzu let a curse slip out.
Roswaal only smiled at those words and continued with "so-o, anyway"—

"That's roughly what my tour of the domain amounted to. Given the distance and time constraints, I could only make a loop of the nearby areas, you know-w. I've sent messengers to the other towns, but there's still a mountain of matters piled up that need handling, isn't there-e."

"Yeah, no doubt about that. If you don't get back to your office soon, I figure Otto's gonna work himself to death. Crushed between heavy labor and a sense of responsibility."

"What a novel way to die. Fascinating-g."

Subaru thought so too, but set that thought aside. Now that Roswaal was back, Ram's energy would probably return as well. Thinking that far, Subaru tilted his head at a sudden sense of unease.

"Come to think of it, I thought Anne went to greet you, Roswaal... wasn't she with you?"

Emilia seemed to have arrived at the same question Subaru had just thought of. At Emilia's words, Roswaal held up a single finger.

"Ah, about that — I asked Annerose to handle one small errand for me-e. Yes, indeed, I thought I might as well take care of one of those pending matters while I was at it."

"A pending matter?"

"The matter of using the great hall. I believe you had already been preparing for it as well, Emilia-sama."

"—!"

At Roswaal's words, Emilia's shoulders jumped in surprise. But that surprise lasted only an instant. Almost immediately, Emilia's expression took on a seriousness, and her violet eyes, charged with firm resolve, glanced sideways at Subaru.
Feeling as if a finger had traced down his spine at that look, Subaru tilted his head, wondering what was going on. But no clear answer was given to his confusion.

"I see. But — are we starting right away?"

"If you're mentally prepared, Emilia-sama, then yes, right away would be fine. There's still time before the meat pie finishes baking as well. If anything, the timing is rather convenient."

"It's such an important thing, and yet you're being suuuper casual about it, aren't you?"

"Deliberately setting aside a block of time is difficult given my current position, you understand. Considering that you'll be busy from tomorrow onward as well, Emilia-sama, wouldn't it be best to call this an opportunity given to us?"

"That's true... all right. I'll go along with it."

When Emilia nodded, Roswaal nodded back, satisfied.
The two of them, despite their opposite expressions, had reached some kind of agreement, but Subaru couldn't follow the conversation at all. Naturally, Garfiel and Ryuzu must have been just as left behind.

"Hey, don't just settle things between the two of you. What's this about? Don't tell me you're planning to make Emilia-tan do something weird again."

"That-t is a cruel misunderstanding, Subaru-kun. And you can rest easy. This isn't a matter concerning Emilia-sama alone — far from it, it's something you're very much involved in as well, you know-w."

"A matter I'm involved in too...?"

Before Subaru could get out a "what," Roswaal leaned his face in close. Subaru instinctively stepped back, and as his back hit the wall, a finger was pointed right at the tip of his nose.
And then—

"—it's the very ceremony you've been waiting for: your knighting ceremony-y."

"Come on, seriously! Keeping the details of a big event like that secret from the person involved until right before it happens — that's just not right! You gonna throw a surprise wedding on the bride and groom? A surprise funeral on the guy being sent off? You wouldn't, right? You wouldn't!"

In the room he'd been brought to for changing, Subaru kept up his string of complaints as he peeled off his tracksuit.
What Roswaal had told him in the dining hall was, for Subaru, an absolute bolt from the blue.

—A knighting ceremony.

It was, in other words, the ceremony by which a lord personally recognized someone as having entered their service as a knight, and announced to those around them that the person had become a knight. The forms and customs surrounding it were varied, and would naturally differ not just from country to country, but from world to world. Subaru had seen it a few times in manga or anime, but there was no way he'd retained which parts were common across depictions and which parts differed.

Much less did he have any knowledge whatsoever of the proper etiquette for a knighting ceremony in the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica.

"And yet the preparations are just proceeding along as if it's the most natural thing in the world. Don't tell me Annerose is jealous that I'm always glued to Emilia-tan and decided to try and embarrass me on purpose...!"

"There's no way that's it. The one being knighted is naturally the main figure of a knighting ceremony, but the lord bestowing the honor bears responsibility too. If Annerose-sama were to hold some petty grudge like that and end up embarrassing herself, it wouldn't just be Natsuki-san who suffers for it, but Emilia-sama as well. Do you really think someone as intelligent as her would do that?"

Shaking his head at Subaru's grumbling in the dressing room was Otto, who was helping him change in the same room. As for what exactly he was helping with in terms of the clothes, it was, of course, matters like how to properly wear formal wear. Naturally, Subaru had had neither the time nor the reason to learn any of that.

"Natsuki-sama. First, please change into this undergarment, and after that, please change your trousers. This is the correct order of dressing. My advice."

"Oh, thanks, that helps. Actually, this outfit fits my body so precisely it's kind of creepy. Has this ceremony been in the works for a while now?"

"At the very least, it was proposed not long after all of you first arrived at this household. This time, it was decided it would be carried out once Roswaal-sama returned... and Emilia-sama has been thoroughly studying and practicing the ceremony this whole time. My report."

"That report's a little late, isn't it!? And why the heck did Emilia-tan keep it secret too!?"

"Perhaps she was too embarrassed? More to the point, are you seriously saying you have absolutely no idea about the etiquette involved? If so, that's a problem in its own right."

Threading his arms and legs through the clothing Clind handed him, Subaru looked bewildered. Otto sensed genuine alarm in that expression and seemed to start considering, as one might expect, that this could actually hinder the proceedings.

"Right? Scary, right? I mean, I'm happy Emilia-tan feels that way, and getting appointed a knight is a huge honor, but wouldn't it be bad if this special ceremony turned into a disaster? Maybe I should throw myself down in a dogeza and beg for a postponement..."

"When you're called, you step forward and kneel before Emilia-sama. Then you draw the sword you're carryin' from its sheath and hand it to her. She takes it, puts the blade to your neck, and speaks the words of the oath... you just gotta swear the vow to that. That's it."

"...Wait, seriously?"

The words of surprise slipped out involuntarily.
Drawing every eye in the room, Garfiel folded his arms and answered, in a rough voice, "The hell?"

"You doubtin' me or somethin'?"

"Not that, more like surprise at why you'd know something like that. It's not that it doesn't fit your character to be well-versed in this kind of formal etiquette or anything..."

"Ain't that, boss. Ain't like I actually know a damn thing 'bout etiquette."

Garfiel waved a hand with an exasperated air, but the fact remained that he'd just described the procedure for a knighting ceremony without a hitch. What did that mean, Subaru wondered, furrowing his brow—

"Knightin' ceremonies, see, that's — it's super cool, so I remembered it."

"Ah, that explains it. Makes sense now."

The reasoning was so overwhelmingly straightforward that understanding arrived instantly.
The great and noble spirit of chuunibyou lending its power even here. Of course Garfiel would know about knighting ceremonies — that explanation carried all the persuasive force in the world.

"So, Clind-san, knowledge-wise, does that match what you know?"

"My learning is shallow, but it does align with what I understand. I bow my head to Garfiel-sama's erudition. Lucid."

"But from what was just said, it kind of sounded like you already knew too, Clind-san... no, never mind. Forget I said anything."

Poking a sleeping snake and drawing an ogre out of it — that was the way Otto lived his life.
Seeing the eyes behind Clind's monocle gleam ominously, no one could blame Otto for withdrawing his opinion.
In any case, Subaru carefully smoothed out the wrinkles in his clothes, put on the jacket, and attached the necessary ornaments one after another.

"Formal wear's really something else, huh. It took forever to get used to wearing a butler's outfit, but I don't see me ever getting used to wearing this stuff."

"When it comes to formal wear, you're not given that many chances to wear it often enough to call it 'getting used to it' in the first place. If it were something like an exalted position, that'd be different, of course... well, though, who knows about your future, Natsuki-san."

"Meaning?"

"Given Emilia-sama's standing, after all. As long as you're accompanying her, I imagine you'll find yourself in that kind of formal setting more often than not. That formal wear was made especially for you, too."

While Subaru was impressed by Otto's words, thinking ahead about it made his spirits sink a little. He pictured some kind of stiff, formal scene, and his restless heart shuddered at the thought of having to just sit still through it. But all those worries were contingent on getting through today's ceremony without a hitch first.

"That bastard Roswaal — don't tell me he hid this from me on purpose just to make a fool out of me."

"No use bein' sulky about it forever, boss. More importantly, make sure you don't forget the steps I just told you — go over 'em again."

"Kneel, pull the sword out of its sheath, hand it over, then the oath words, right? I've been through two graduation ceremonies in my life, I'm not gonna forget steps that simple."

Though, admittedly, those had been the real thing only after going through proper rehearsals.

"By the way, though it's a bit late to ask — since it's a knighting ceremony, does that mean all the Royal Guard and everyone else went through this same thing?"

"Not just the Royal Guard — everyone who bears the title of knight should have gone through it, yes. Though it's rare for someone to skip past all sorts of conditions and go straight to serving their liege like this. Normally, I'd think you'd swear loyalty to the country first, and then choose a liege."

"So it's a difference between serving the country and serving an individual, huh. In that case, the part about me serving an individual is at least correct."

Still, the sound of "knight" applied to himself carried no sense of reality.
Time and again, in a kind of self-declared way, Subaru had insisted he was Emilia's knight. Even hearing that this provisional title was finally about to become reality, there remained a large part of him that couldn't quite come to terms with it. Even having been recognized as a knight, he found himself harboring doubts about just how much, and in what way, things would actually change from before.

"And here they've gone and dressed me up in such proper formal wear. This really does fit me perfectly — when did you even measure me for this?"

"I took the measurements bit by bit, weaving through gaps in Natsuki-sama's awareness, day after day. I did make sure to check the fit properly, so I'm relieved you're able to actually wear it now with your own arms through the sleeves. Much gratification."

"I wasn't that surprised you took measurements, but when did you actually fit it on me? When was I ever dressed in this without noticing?"

Clind, smiling, gave no answer, and instead led Subaru's now-dressed body over to stand before a full-length mirror.
Standing before the mirror reflecting his whole body, Subaru held his breath faintly at the sight of himself.

It was a black formal outfit, decorated just enough not to be excessive, and clearly of a different, more distinguished quality. It was a garment with a kind of bewitching power that made Subaru look as if he cut a fine figure no matter what pose he struck. Carry yourself solemnly, and indeed, it certainly took on the air of ceremonial dress.

And yet, there was no denying that Subaru himself, wearing it, was somewhat outmatched by the power of the clothes. If he had to put it into words, there was a kind of incongruity to it, like a child dressed up for a Shichi-Go-San festival. And yet—

"Hm. Well now, you're wearing it more properly than I expected, aren't you?"

"Well, there's a bit of a feel like the clothes are wearin' you, sure, but you ain't lost to 'em completely. You can relax on that front, boss."

"Yes, it suits you very well indeed. I imagine Emilia-sama's impression of you, Natsuki-sama, will only improve further as a result. Favorability rising."

"Do you two seriously mean that? Hey, is that seriously, sincerely how you feel, both of you?"

Fixing his collar over and over, Subaru cast a doubtful look at Otto and the others, who, if not exactly unreserved, at least weren't mocking his appearance.
And yet even so, not a single muscle in their faces moved, and they looked at Subaru with something like pride. Faced with that, even Subaru had no choice but to fall silent.

"Here, take this, boss."

No matter how much he fussed with it, no dramatic change was coming.
As Subaru turned back with a sigh, Garfiel practically shoved a knight's sword into his hands. Subaru accepted it reflexively, and drew a breath at the slender weight of it.

"Ideally it would be a blade of your own that you're accustomed to using, but since you don't have one, Natsuki-sama, our household has prepared one for this occasion. Should it be to your liking, you're welcome to keep it as it is. A gift."

"A knight's sword... huh. It's a real blade, right? Obviously."

"There's no way a wooden sword would be crafted so finely, is there. The only ones who'd be pleased to receive something like that would be children... hm? Wait, could this be a new business opportunity...?"

Watching what might have been the seed of otherworld souvenir shops eventually stocking wooden swords, Subaru felt the weight of the knight's sword in both hands.
He'd held a real blade in his hands once before.

That had been during the Witchbeast incident at Arlam Village, when he'd gone into the mountains with Ram to search for Rem. He'd borrowed a sword from the village's youth corps and gripped it with hardly a thought in his head. In truth, that sword had snapped before the fight with the Witchbeast even began and hadn't been of any decisive use, but that had been his first, and so far only, experience of driving a blade into a living creature.

And this knight's sword should, in comparison to that one, be somewhat slimmer and lighter.
And yet the weight he felt in his arms right now was nothing like that of that time.

"――――"

Without realizing it, his throat made a sound, and Subaru felt as if something had reached in and squeezed his chest tight.

The weight of that real blade back then, and the weight of this knight's sword in his hands now, were entirely different things.
And it was that very awareness, Subaru realized, that was the meaning of this ceremony.

"—Natsuki-san. I'll come call for you before it begins. I'll check your outfit one last time then, so please try not to muss it up before that."

"...Got it."

Perhaps sensing that Subaru's expression had changed, that he had begun to face the ceremony properly, Otto said those words, and with that as a signal, the others left the room.

"――――"

Left alone in the room, Subaru pulled a nearby chair close, sat down in front of the full-length mirror, and sank into thought, gazing at his own reflection, sword in hand.

Knight — the weight of that title, now looming so close, pressed down on Subaru's shoulders at last.

Had Subaru ever, even once, seriously considered the meaning of that word he had so many times carelessly claimed for himself? Of course, in each moment, Subaru had always been sincere about it. When he called himself Emilia's knight, he didn't think he'd ever used it as armor to hide any shallowness.
And yet—

"Julius. Reinhard."

What rose in Subaru's mind were two men who ranked among the top knights of this country.
One, the "Knight among Knights." The other, the "Sword Saint."

Their very forms were the pride of what it meant to be a knight, the symbol of what it meant to be a knight.
Toward Subaru, who had claimed the title of knight without understanding any of that, Julius had struck reality into him with fierce intensity.

"What a knight needs is loyalty and strength... was that it?"

If those two things were the essential requirements, then Subaru was well aware he still fell short of being a knight.

Subaru's feelings for Emilia were, unfortunately, far from anything as lofty as "loyalty." As for strength, his own raw power was insufficient, and even borrowing Beatrice's help, he couldn't quite rise above being half a person. Both loyalty and strength — he still lacked them.

But there was one thing he now carried that he hadn't had before: resolve.

It wasn't loyalty, but he had a feeling that wouldn't lose to loyalty. He lacked strength, but he had the stubbornness and the resolve to run himself ragged making up for what he lacked.

There was no denying it made for an unpolished figure to be claiming the title of knight, but that, precisely, was what made it Subaru's own. As if a flashy, storybook chivalry could ever suit Natsuki Subaru.

"Hm. Guess there wasn't any need to come after all, was there, I suppose."

Just as Subaru arrived at a kind of resolution regarding the figure reflected in the mirror—
A small figure came to stand beside his own leaning-forward form. Reflected side by side in the mirror was a girl with long hair done up in extravagant ringlets — Beatrice.

"I'm in the middle of getting dressed. You're a lecherous little loli, you know that?"

"Getting dressed seems to be properly finished already, I suppose. Besides, since Subaru kept making a face like he still hadn't made up his mind, someone asked me to come do something about it, I suppose. So I came, unwillingly, meaning to slap him on the back. ...Well, seems there wasn't any need for it after all, I suppose."

"Those guys..."

Who had left behind that bit of meddling? Otto, Garfiel, or, could it possibly be Clind? Or maybe all three of them — the likelihood of that made Subaru give a wry smile.
And indeed, at a moment like this, when Subaru needed a push, there could hardly be anyone more fitting than Beatrice. Best choice. Well then, he'd allow himself to indulge in that consideration.

If only to save face for Beatrice, who looked frustrated at having nothing to do with her hands.

"My back."

"...?"

"If you're gonna hit it, I'd like you to hit it. Honestly, going through all this thinking has left me feeling pretty settled... but I could use one more thing, one last push."

Beatrice's large eyes went round with surprise at Subaru's words.
Finding that expression unbearably endearing, Subaru held back the urge to burst out laughing, and said—

"So, come on. Please."

"There's no need to worry about something like that, I suppose... or, well, Betty doesn't mind, I suppose."

"I'm not saying this out of consideration or anything. Whoever hits my back, I figure it'll give me that final push. And if I get to pick who that someone is, I'd want it to be you."

"――――"

"I want that last bit of strength to become Emilia's knight, from getting my back smacked by you. That way it'll finally feel like something that's actually me."

It might just be a comforting delusion, but so what if it was — nothing wrong with that.
It might just be a matter of feeling, but if so, all the more reason to let himself feel good about it while he was at it.
After all, when it came to showing what was in your heart, the simplest way was always the one that got through.

"Y-you really are hopeless, I suppose. Can't Betty do anything without you?"

"Yeah, that's right. Honestly, I'm no good without you. It's only with you around that I even become a hopeless mess."

"You're already a hopeless mess as it is, I suppose! How rude, I suppose!"

"And then this hopeless mess becomes Emilia's knight and stops being hopeless. So every time I'm about to become hopeless, I'm gonna be counting on you."

Rising heavily from the chair, Subaru reached out and stroked Beatrice's head.
Though her face showed displeasure at the rough touch, Beatrice made no move to bat his hand away, nor did she voice a single complaint.

"――――"

Having thoroughly savored the feel of stroking Beatrice's head, Subaru turned his back to her.
Beatrice, too, seemed to understand the meaning of that offered back.
She drew a faint breath, and Subaru could sense her bracing herself.

"—Hnnyah, I suppose!"

"――――っ"

An adorable battle cry, and the dry crack of an open palm rang out through the room.
The impact transmitted from that small palm brought Subaru a sharper sting than he'd imagined. And an even greater shock, he realized, was running through his entire body, traveling out from that spot on his back.

"You've got more power than I expected."

"It's not for nothing that Betty's been carrying around big, heavy books like that every day, I suppose."

At Beatrice's proud words, Subaru found himself recalling the girl's days spent in the Forbidden Archive. Indeed, Beatrice had always been opening books so large they could hide her small body entirely. The result of days spent enduring that weight had just been demonstrated, right here.
Whether strength training had any effect on a Spirit was another matter entirely, though.

"Turns out you're an unexpectedly physical mage. Massive Bea-ko."

"Somehow I feel like I've just been given a really unwelcome nickname, I suppose."

"Must be your imagination. Anyway, I'm fired up now. Thanks."

"...As your contractor, it's only natural, I suppose."

Her cheeks tinged faintly red, Beatrice looked away from Subaru.
It was the kind of reaction that made him want to stroke her head all over again. But before Subaru could reach out his hand—

"—Natsuki-sama, it is nearly time. Preparations."

Clind, knocking on the door to come call for him, appeared first.
Subaru drew a small breath of tension, feeling that the moment had finally, truly arrived.

And yet, his limbs and face were less stiff than he'd expected. The tautness of his nerves had, in a good way, loosened his movements, and he privately gave silent praise to the effect of Beatrice's slap, greater than he'd imagined.

"Beatrice-sama, you also have preparations to attend, as you will be seated among those present. We too will occupy the lowest seats, so I hope you will understand, Natsuki-sama. My understanding."

"Ah, got it. Just don't laugh if I mess up, all right?"

"As you wish. Solemnly."

Clind took up a guiding stance outside the door, and Subaru let out a breath, cracking the bones of his neck.
Then he turned back to Beatrice, hesitating over what to say, and—

"Well, I'm off."

"You should do that, I suppose."

A simple exchange of words, but that much, surely, was enough.
He had already received more than enough, in both words and actions.

"—Subaru."

And yet, at the very last moment, Beatrice called him back once more.
Just before he stepped out of the room, Beatrice, her face red, looked at Subaru as he turned around and said—

"Does it suit you, I wonder — that outfit."

—and with those words, she filled in even the last bit of confidence Subaru had been missing.
