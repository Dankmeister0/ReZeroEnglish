Arc 3 Interlude: "Natsuki Rem"

— Beneath a clear, wide-open sky, Subaru squinted against the pouring sunlight.

A crying voice could be heard.

It was a girl's crying voice. A full-throated, all-out cry, her small body straining with everything she had to wail as loud as she could.

Watching her pour every ounce of her feelings into that display, Subaru found himself vaguely wondering why kids had so much energy. And the moment he thought it, he was struck by just how unyouthful a thought that was.

"Guess I always kind of knew I acted older than my age, but it hits different when I realize it unconsciously instead of on purpose. And here I thought I was still a fresh, spry young man."

"What d'you mean, 'spry'? Can't you look at yourself objectively for once, huh?"

*Still got it*, Subaru was thinking, gazing at his own empty palm, when the insult came flying at him from the side. Sighing at the familiar jab, he slowly turned his eyes that way.

Beside Subaru, who sat in a chair, stood a boy whose eye level matched Subaru's own even while seated. At Subaru's silent stare, the boy twisted his lips and pressed on, "More importantly—"

"—do something about Spica bawling her head off. Seriously, I can't handle this."

"I'm done for. My naive little heart's been shattered by one heartless word, and now I'm a wreck. Guess I'll go back to being a kid too and start bawling like Spica. Forgive pathetic me."

"A grown man losing it like that in the middle of a public street? No way I'm forgiving that!?"

At Subaru's childish sulking, the boy flung his body about in an exaggerated, energetic retort. And with that motion, the baby cradled in Subaru's arms — the girl called Spica — drew another breath, and while the two men uttered useless syllables like "ah" and "uh," her emotions exploded.

"Waaah——!!"

"Whoa! She's crying! Spica's crying! Oi, Rigel, you do something, you're her big brother, aren't you!"

"If we're going there, shouldn't you be way more able to handle this than me!?"

On a corner of the busy main street, the two males spun in circles with the baby between them, tossing the blame back and forth.

Passersby glanced up at the commotion the three of them were making, but on seeing the two flapping about, their faces settled into an "oh, it's just them again" look and moved on without a second glance.

As a result, the picture of a crying girl and the two men making her cry carried right on. Whether it was heartwarming or bordering on outright public disturbance was a matter of paper-thin distinction. And then—

"A girl's wailing her heart out this hard, and not one person steps up to help...damn, what kind of world is this! Have people's hearts really grown this cold?"

"This isn't the time to be lamenting the state of the world! If we can't get Spica to stop crying before she gets back, who knows what she'll say to us..."

"Who's 'she,' and what about when she gets back?"

"Well, obviously—"

The boy Subaru had called Rigel folded his arms and turned around, nodding along—only for his face to freeze in shock, mouth falling open in a daze. Following Rigel's gaze, Subaru spotted the same person and raised his eyebrows. "Oh."

"Done with the shopping already?"

"Yes, without a hitch. ...Looks like things are rough on your end, though."

"Nah, Spica's just super lively. Once she's old enough to run around on her own, she's gonna grow up into the type who leads men on a merry chase. A future femme fatale in the making—I'm getting all giddy and nervous just thinking about it!"

In the arms of the wisecracking Subaru, Spica blearily opened her cried-out eyes, and on noticing the woman standing in front of her, spread her tiny maple-leaf hands and reached out. The sight of her trying to sit up despite not yet having the upper-body strength for it felt, to Subaru's lonely fatherly heart, like a none-too-subtle declaration that she wanted a change because being held by him just wasn't comfortable.

"Well, if I ignore what she wants and she just starts crying again, we're back to square one. Here, she's all yours."

"Consider her taken."

For all the flippant roughness of his tone, Subaru's movements as he handed the baby over were achingly gentle. The woman receiving her carved a small smile at the sight of his fingertips handling the fragile treasure.

Then, having taken Spica into her arms, she held her securely against her chest and rocked her gently, saying—

"There, there. What a hopeless father and big brother you have. You'd better hurry up and grow big, Spica, so you can scold the two of them yourself."

"Hey now, let's not go planting ideas in her head before she can even understand words, huh?"

After tossing off that foolish remark, he pictured himself sandwiched between two people, hands on hips, huffing with indignation. The image of getting scolded together with Rigel—

"Huh, that's actually not as bad as I'd have thought. If anything it's such a happy vision of the future I might just tear up."

"Well I don't want it. Getting scolded by my little sister? That'd totally wreck my dignity as her big brother."

"You've got no dignity left to wreck, not after flailing around with me like this. I can see it, I can see it... your future, so besotted with your little sister that you spoil her rotten and end up whipped."

"Don't project your own henpecked self onto me! There's no way I end up like that!"

Rigel shot back, veins bulging right on cue, at Subaru's provocative, weirdly complex finger-wiggling. But it wasn't Subaru whose brow furrowed at Rigel's raised voice.

It was the blue-haired woman, who had supposedly been watching the two of them with a fond smile. In a voice that was calm but firm, she called the boy's name. "Rigel."

"The way you've been talking out here in public — it's more than I can overlook."

"Uh, but I mean..."

"Mother doesn't like 'but' or 'I mean.' And besides, what you said a moment ago was also wrong."

She scolded the stammering Rigel without mercy, then turned a smile on Spica, now settled quietly in her arms.

"Mother has never once had Father whipped. Father has always been Mother's number one, after all."

Her cheeks colored red — a declaration more embarrassing than wailing in the middle of a public street.

Faced with a mother who could declare such a thing so boldly, Rigel finally, unmistakably threw both hands up in surrender.

Feeling something like an itch he couldn't quite scratch, Subaru laughed, and she, watching over her family's antics with happy eyes, gently held down her hair.

Rem's beautiful long blue hair, like a mirror of the sky, swayed softly, stroked by the breeze.

In a corner of a provincial city in Kararagi — in an open plaza-like space that left some doubt as to whether it really qualified as a "park" — Subaru sat on an installed bench, lost in a daze.

Before his eyes, Rigel, his short blue hair standing up in spikes, formed a little league of rascals with his friends and dashed about left and right. His manner of speaking to his father left much to be desired, but watching him like this, Subaru couldn't help but think there was something endearing about it too.

"If only we could do something about that murderous glare of his."

"No, that won't do. That sharp glare is part of what makes Rigel who he is. No matter how much fun he's having, no matter how happy he is, to a stranger seeing him for the first time, his face always looks like he's sulking and plotting something. That's Rigel."

"I can hear you, and honestly Mom's version of 'sticking up for me' hurt way worse!?"

Absorbed in a game of freeze tag that Subaru had taught them and made popular, Rigel — caught by the tagger and currently frozen solid — shouted something. Subaru gave him a careless wave in response, and Rem too gave a small wave, careful not to wake the sleeping Spica.

Rigel pouted, his sanpaku eyes narrowing sharply in displeasure — and the resemblance to Subaru's own childhood self in old photo albums was almost too much to bear.

"Which means his future's already locked in, huh. Makes me shudder just thinking about being him... told that in twenty years, he'll turn into me."

"Wouldn't that mean he'll get himself a wonderful, ideal wife — skilled at cooking, a master of housework, devoted to attending her husband and always putting him first?"

"What kind of real-life-charmed nonsense is that, go blow up already. Oh wait, that's me!"

Rem let out a small laugh as Subaru put a hand to his head and stuck out his tongue.

Hand at her mouth, chuckling low in her throat, she glanced sideways at Subaru's face.

"If you keep flattering me to death like that instead of denying it, Rem might get carried away, you know?"

"Was that flattery to death, though? Feels more like I just didn't bother denying your own self-praise. Though it's undeniably true there's nothing to deny."

If anything, if Subaru let himself talk about Rem with all embarrassment and restraint stripped away, an even bigger torrent of praise would come flying out, and things would get out of hand. This being a midday park where children vied for supremacy, there were plenty of families and neighbors about too. Once he started gushing about his wife, he'd surely be grilled for every last detail and end up monopolizing tomorrow's gossip at the well.

*That wouldn't be so bad either*, he found himself thinking — a sign of just how far gone he was — but Subaru deliberately set the thought aside and let his mind sink into the warmth of the family gathering.

Closing his eyes and letting himself go slack in the sunlight, he felt the illusion of his body floating on the sun's warmth take hold. His body, worn from consecutive late nights, was dragged along by drowsiness, his head growing strangely heavy and swaying unsteadily. And then—

"Huh?"

"If you're sleepy, please use Rem's shoulder. Her arms are currently Spica's exclusively, I'm afraid."

Cracking one eye open, he found Rem's body, which had somehow drawn in right beside him without his noticing, supporting his weight. There was a slight difference in their sitting heights, so that when Subaru tilted his head, it settled neatly onto her shoulder.

A little embarrassed, Subaru looked at Spica sleeping soundly in her arms. Black hair inherited from her father, a lovely face inherited from her mother. A beloved little life, knowing nothing of the world yet, living each day in innocence.

"Mmm, that Spica. Beloved daughter or not, occupying my sacred territory makes her a bad girl. I'll sentence her to a tickling later."

"You'll have to wait until nighttime to have Rem's chest all to yourself, you know."

"It's the middle of the day, we're in a park, watch what you say!?"

Subaru whirled his upper body around in reaction to Rem's bold remark. A glance at her profile showed the speaker herself with cheeks flushed bright red. Honestly, what could he even say—

"My wife is super cute."

"I am loved, every single day, after all."

Rem answered Subaru's lovestruck remark with one of her own, and the two exchanged smiles — Rem's a demure *smile*, Subaru's more of a goofy *grin*, if we're being onomatopoeic about it.

Gazing at each other like that grew embarrassing too, so Subaru scratched his cheek with a finger, collected himself, and said, "Well then, if you insist," before settling his head back on Rem's shoulder once more.

Her silky blue hair, swaying in the breeze, brushed his cheek and felt weirdly wonderful. He sniffed at her scent, nose twitching, rubbing his cheek against her shoulder.

"That tickles, dear."

"Oh, sorry. It just felt so good I got carried away. I'll take a page from Spica and settle down. I'll leave the not-settling-down to Rigel. Whoa, look at Rigel go—such a kid."

"I can hear you, you idiot old man! Quit dragging me into it every time!"

"Rigel. Your little sister is sleeping, so please be more considerate."

"This is so unfair!"

Rigel, still frozen, shouted at the injustice of it, but not a single family member came to his defense. To add insult to injury, nobody came to free frozen Rigel either. Through and through, his position was the one everyone teased.

Given how much his personality, way of talking, and patterns of behavior resembled Subaru's own, Subaru figured that at least not being excluded by the kids around him made Rigel a bit more blessed than he'd been. Then again, he vaguely recalled having been reasonably well accepted into the community himself as a kid too, which meant Rigel's future might not be as bright as one might hope.

"Gotta make sure Spica doesn't end up like that. Rigel's a lost cause, but you take after your mom, so your future's bright. All that's left is praying you don't get snagged by some hopeless guy like me."

"There's no one who could replace you, anywhere. Rem's you — you are one of a kind in this world."

Given his wife's stamp of approval, Subaru laughed softly, and then a brief silence fell between the two of them.

Tickled by the soft breeze, feeling Rem's warmth against his skin, Subaru began to drift toward a hazy, empty-minded world.

His body, worn from working hard day after day, carried its share of accumulated fatigue. During this rare stretch of family time, he was basking in the joys of an ordinary, happy life. Bathed in mild sunlight, watching from a distance as his son got teased by his friends, leaning against his wife as she held their daughter, he himself was drifting off into a nap.

—What a sweet, happy time this was.

"Subaru-kun—"

Suddenly hearing his name, Subaru opened his closed eyes.

Glancing toward the voice, he found Rem, her head tilted slightly toward him. Her clear pale blue eyes reflected his image, and her lips trembled as though she wanted to say something.

"...That way of calling me."

"——"

"It's been a while. Lately it's been nothing but 'dear' or 'Father.'"

Subaru winked one eye and let his mouth relax into a smile, while Rem, in turn, pressed her lips together.

That manner of hers called to mind years past — something she'd done often back in the days right after they'd fled here. She'd probably tried not to let Subaru notice, but he'd been watching her far more closely than she ever realized.

He closed his eyes and felt the wind.

It had been Rem who'd suggested today that the family go out shopping together like this. He had a rough idea of what lay behind that invitation. Because—

"It's been eight years today, since that day, hasn't it."

"You noticed, then..."

"Well, yeah — for me... no, for us, it's the day everything turned. Of course I'd notice, of course I'd remember — more than remember, I could never forget it. —There's no way I could forget."

The day he'd bowed to fate. The day he'd thrown everything away and fled.

The day he'd meant to give up on everything, yet found there was one single thing he could never give up.

That day's decision, and her love — it was because of those that Subaru could be here like this now.

"Subaru-kun..."

That nostalgic way of addressing him was one she had consciously stopped using sometime after the two of them fled to Kararagi. He supposed part of it had been to show themselves and those around them the shape of a married couple, and part of it had probably been a wish to make even some small piece of things different from the past.

Up until this very day, he had never deliberately brought it up, and Rem, for her part, had never told him her reasons either.

Calling Subaru by the name that had belonged to those days they'd left behind, Rem, a swirl of complicated emotion rising in her eyes, said—

"Do you regret it?"

"Regret?"

"Yes. Running away. Giving things up. Throwing them aside. Rem..."

"If you're gonna say 'choosing you,' I'm gonna be super mad. I'll take Rigel and Spica and go straight back to my parents' house! Oh, wait, never mind Rigel. I'll leave him behind."

He caught sight of Rigel making a murderous face off in the distance, but Subaru just muttered, "We're having an important talk right now," and dropped his son off a thousand-fathom cliff with a perfectly innocent expression. Turning back to face Rem properly, he continued, "Listen—"

"It's been eight whole years so this is super overdue, and honestly I've said this like a dozen, a hundred times already so I dunno how much effect it still has, but—"

"Yes."

"I love you more than anyone in the world. You're the only wife I've got, and I'm the only man you've got. You're not some cheap woman a guy like me could just settle for and win over."

Sitting up, Subaru flicked Rem's forehead lightly with a fingertip. As she pressed a hand to her forehead, startled, he leaned his face in close and said—

"Just like I swore that day, everything I am belongs to you. I devote myself to you, I offer myself to you, I live only for your sake. —Though now there's also our kids to live for."

He brought his lips close to the tip of her nose, then stole a kiss from her closed-eyed lips.

They shared a kiss that was little more than a touch, and at close enough range to feel each other's breath, Subaru formed the same mischievous grin that hadn't changed a bit despite all the years.

"So — does that put your mind at ease?"

"...I'm sorry. Rem is always anxious. Because — I keep falling more and more in love with you, Subaru-kun. Even when I think there couldn't possibly be a happier time than this, you go and make me even happier still. I'm happy, and I love you, and that's exactly why I'm anxious."

Tears welling in her eyes even as she declared herself happy, Rem shook her head slightly. Shaking her head, she pressed her forehead to Subaru's, and as they traded warmth—

"—I feel like someday I'll lose you, even while I'm touching you like this."

"Relax. I'm not letting you go, and I'm not disappearing either. As long as you don't get fed up with me, there's no way we end up apart."

"There's no way Rem could ever get fed up with you, Subaru-kun—"

"Then we're together forever. I love you, Rem."

Subaru kissed Rem again, as she struggled to contain the emotion welling up inside her.

He pressed his teeth lightly against her soft lips, pushing deeper still into her as she froze in surprise. Their soft tongues entwined, and he felt the heat of her saliva at the tip of his own.

Only then did their lips part. Rem let out a light breath, hers coming somewhat deep and long. Subaru raised a finger at her and said, "Anyway—"

"Quit saying stupid things like 'settle.' What, you saying Rigel and Spica were made out of pity instead of love? Spica's the fruit of our carefully planned-out love, and Rigel's the result of our burning young passion running wild."

"...It really was quite something when Rigel was born."

Rem's lips curved into a smile as she answered Subaru, who stood with hands on hips in full lecture mode.

Lost in fond memory, she traced her fingertips along the past with affection.

"We'd only just moved to Kararagi and finally found a place to live and work — we were supposed to take our time building up a life here slowly."

"Ah well, you know, we were young and couldn't hold back."

"Even though you should've been tired from work, Subaru-kun, come bedtime you'd get so terribly energetic."

"Ah well, you know, we were young and had energy to spare."

"It was almost the exact same time I finally got hired properly and found out I was pregnant. Rem's face went completely pale, back then."

"Some things you just don't want to admit — the mistakes of one's own youth..."

Under Rem's relentless barrage, Subaru murmured with a distant, wistful look in his eyes. Off in the distance, Rigel, having just been labeled a "mistake" by his own father, made a sour, want-to-say-something face, but somehow managed to read the room and hold himself back. Quite the accomplished son.

Subaru nodded at his son's evident growth. Beside him, Rem likewise looked over at their son, still frozen with that sour face, drew a small breath, and said, "But—"

"When Rem found out she was carrying Rigel, she really was truly happy, you know."

"Well, yeah, I was happy too. First time I heard it, snot came out my nose and I leaked a little pee, and I had Rem punch me to check if it was a dream, which ended in bloodshed."

Rem had probably been fairly flustered herself, because he'd been struck with a full swing and slammed into the wall with enough force to make the rented place shudder. If he hadn't managed to fall properly on instinct, he might well have died.

Setting that memory aside, he could recall in vivid detail the moment Rem had told him she was expecting — and every bit of the warm feeling that had welled up in his chest at the time.

But Rem shook her head at Subaru's answer. When Subaru tilted his head, not understanding what the gesture meant, a faint shadow crossed her smile, and she said—

"Rem's 'happy' is surely different from your pure kind of happy, Subaru-kun. What Rem felt as happiness was...the joy of thinking, now I won't have to lose you."

"........."

"Rigel is a bond born in a certain, tangible form between Rem and Subaru-kun. It's a terrible way to put it, but with the baby, something certain and unbreakable was tied between Rem and Subaru-kun. ...That was what made Rem happy."

Perhaps anxious days had weighed on her all along.

They had cast aside everything they'd built up until then and fled into a new land with only each other. In days where each of them had no one left to cling to but the other, Rem had gone on enduring, barely, the fear of not knowing when she might lose Subaru again.

Her lack of confidence was on a level that could give Subaru's own a real run for its money.

For Rem, whose self-deprecation ran to extraordinary lengths, life with Subaru had amounted to time spent being worn down, continuously, between the extremes of happiness and the extremes of fear.

What put a period to that time was the new life created between the two of them.

"You couldn't believe it?"

"No. Rem trusts you more than anyone else in this world, Subaru-kun."

"That's not what I mean. Not that you couldn't believe in me... but that you couldn't believe in yourself?"

At Subaru's words of denial, Rem drew a small breath, then nodded.

Within her, Subaru's presence loomed disproportionately large. Facing it, her own shadow must have seemed all the smaller by comparison, and that was what made her anxious.

—Did she never notice that Subaru always carried just as much of the same feeling himself? Even though Subaru, too, always thought Rem was far, far too good a girl for someone like him.

It made him laugh. Seeing Subaru show his teeth and let his mouth relax into a grin, Rem puffed out her cheeks as if put out, and said—

"It's fine. Rem was being foolish. Foolish enough that being laughed at is only fair..."

"No, no, that's not it. I was just thinking, all over again, that you and I are alike right down to the core — and that my wife is, after all, the cutest in the world."

At Subaru's out-of-nowhere words, Rem froze in surprise for an instant, then her face flushed bright red all at once. Watching her reaction, he could feel all over again, for certain, that he loved her.

He loved Rem more than anyone in the world. He was in love with her. He could shout it out loud if he wanted. In fact, he did occasionally. They were a couple famous among the neighbors for how head-over-heels they were.

"—Rigel. Spica."

"Hm?"

Rem murmured their children's names affectionately, out of nowhere. As Subaru tilted his head, Rem said, "No, it's nothing," and gazed up at him from beneath her lashes.

"Both of them are star names, aren't they. The way stars were named, in the place you used to live, Subaru-kun."

"Yep, yep. My old man was basically off his rocker, the kind of hopeless guy who'd misread 'common sense' as some made-up word, but naming me 'Subaru' was the one good call he ever made. I like my name. 'Subaru' is a star name too."

It must have been in elementary school — some assignment or other about looking into the origin of one's own name — that Subaru came to learn the history behind his. When he found out that one of the stars adorning the night sky shared his name, he'd been, uncharacteristically, thrilled enough to get carried away.

Ever since, though he was the type whose hobbies never lasted long, poring over star encyclopedias was one habit that had stuck with him for a long, long time. He knew a fair range of star names, and naturally, whenever the chance came up to name something, his go-to choice was—

"—to take it from a star name. My online handle's a star name too, and if I ever had to go by an alias, I'd probably grab one from the stars. Guess that makes it a kind of 'sparkly name' too, huh!?"

"I'm not sure what that means, but I think taking it from a star name is lovely. If we have a third one, let's be sure to do the same."

"Isn't it a little early to talk about a third one already? Spica's still a baby, you know?"

"I think it'll be fine if we just leave everything but nursing to Rigel. What do you think we were being so careful about, not having another child until Rigel was big enough, for?"

"You don't get noticed much standing in my shadow, but you really come on strong with Rigel too, huh!?"

Wincing wryly at how their son got treated day to day, Subaru brushed off his backside and stood up. Before Rem, who looked up at him, he arched his back and rolled his hips, saying—

"Let's head home soon, I want to put the shopping down too. Out here I keep worrying about people watching, can't get as lovey-dovey as I'd like."

"That's true. Right now Rem is in the mood to be as lovey-dovey as possible, at full strength, for the first time in a while."

"O-oh boy, wonder if my libido's up to keeping pace with a demon's stamina right now..."

Muttering nervously, Subaru then held out a hand to Rem, seated on the bench. As she slowly took it, he pulled her up to her feet and drew her, letting out a little "whoa," into his arms.

He embraced her, Spica and all, savoring the warmth thoroughly, before saying—

"Well then, let's head home. To our home."

"Yes, dear."

With the shopping bag in one hand, he took Rem's offered hand in the other. Subaru walked ahead, with Rem, hand in hand, following half a step behind and close beside him.

So they made their way to the middle of the plaza, to their son, still frozen solid, and said—

"Oi, son currently starring in the Sapporo Snow Festival. This freeze tag isn't going anywhere and it's supremely boring to watch, so Mom and your sister and I are heading home. You can crash at a friend's place or something."

"You're blatantly trying to kick me out here!? I mean — also, the matter of my parents shamelessly making out in broad daylight in the middle of a park."

"Serves you right, jealous much. Sorry, Rigel. This Rem here is mine and mine alone."

"So annoying!!"

Rigel bellowed, his sanpaku eyes flashing sharp, at Subaru's flared-nostril, boisterous laughter. But seeing how the more he yelled, the happier Subaru looked, Rigel let out one long, deep sigh and shook his head.

"Calm down, calm down, me. Don't let the old man drag you around by the nose. Calm down, calm dowwwn. Okay, calmed down. So, what were you and Mom talking about?"

"Huh? Just talking about how your name comes from a star name, that kind of thing. Your first name candidate was actually Vega, you know..."

"That sounds strong! Why'd you drop it?"

"Right, exactly, sounds strong, doesn't it? Figured you'd grow up strong, get a nasty rebellious phase, hard to handle — so I gave up on it. Even knowing I'd get surpassed eventually, some fatherly part of me that didn't want to lose to his own son whispered that in my ear."

"You were thinking that far into the future about a baby who was only a few days old!?"

Rigel leapt up to snap back at Subaru's razor-sharp wisecrack. And then—

"Ah, Rigel's moving! Can't go breaking the rules of freeze tag, y'know!"

"Ah!"

The tagger, who up until then had seemed to be deliberately leaving Rigel out, seized this chance to call him out for moving. Rigel froze, throat catching. Subaru clapped him on the shoulder and said—

"There's a penalty game for anyone who breaks the rules of freeze tag. Getting tickled by the tagger until you can't even laugh or cry anymore. —Good luck."

"What kind of ridiculous thing are you saying with such a straight face...oi, what, you guys!? Wait a second! Don't take this man's word seriously! Wai— waaaaaaah——!!"

A swarm of children materialized and chased Rigel down. Rigel made a break for it. But he was cut off. He was pinned to the ground, limbs seized, and despite his futile struggling, a barrage of small white fingers descended upon him.

"Farewell, my son. You were a good son. But your father is the one at fault here."

"Rigel. Father and Mother have important matters to discuss, so you're staying at a friend's place tonight. Also, no using your horns. And try not to tear your clothes."

"I— I'll remember this, you heartless parents——!!"

Beset by a mountain of devilish hands, forced peals of laughter burst out of Rigel. Watching her brother's undignified plight through half-open eyes, Spica let out a happy little sound.

Quite the promising sensibility for the future, that. No doubt, her rise would only further cement Rigel's position within the Natsuki household.

Having shown his beloved, dearly loved son a bit of twisted affection, Subaru took Rem's hand and started walking.

Toward the home, filled with peace and happiness, where their precious family lived.

"Subaru-kun."

"Hm?"

Tugged by the hand, Subaru stopped and turned around.

Just then, a strong gust suddenly blew through the space between them. He instinctively shut his eyes, and only after the sound of the wind died down did he slowly open them again.

Rem's long blue hair was swept up broadly by the wind, glimmering as though melting into the sunlight.

Rem, who had taken to growing her hair out long. Subaru had a fair idea now of just whom that had been meant to compete with. And these days, when he thought of a girl with long hair, the very first person to come to mind was already the woman standing before him now — the one most precious to him in the whole world.

Her blue hair flowed quietly, and Rem, cradling their beloved daughter in her arms, smiled at Subaru.

It was, beyond anything else, the smile of the person most beloved to him — the one that stirred the very feeling of love itself in Subaru's heart.

"Right now, Rem is the happiest person in the world——."
