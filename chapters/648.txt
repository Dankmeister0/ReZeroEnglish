Arc 8 Chapter 32: "Selected Members"

—The annihilation of the Vollachian Empire by the "Great Disaster," and the lightning operation to stop it.

Now that it was clear that clashing head-on with the ceaselessly resurrecting army of the undead would only hasten the empire's ruin, what was needed was a swift, decisive strike.
Since pitting great armies against each other in some grand final battle between the living and the dead was out of the question, it was only natural that the mission adopted would be a small elite strike force punching straight into the enemy's stronghold.
If there was a problem, it was—

"—the question of who's going to be the one throwing that punch."

Arms crossed, Subaru sat planted on the floor in the middle of the room and looked around at everyone.
In a room within the great fortress of the citadel city of Garkla, a large chamber perfectly suited to gathering a big crowd, Subaru's allies—minus the imperial parties—stood arrayed in force.
Among them were those who hadn't been present at the earlier meeting due to treating the wounded—Ram, Garfiel, Petra, Frederica, and Rem as well.
And naturally, since it excluded only the imperial side, Anastasia and Julius were there too—

"Halibel, I'm glad you made it back safe."

"Oh, ain't that sweet of you to worry. Anna-bo's always so hard on me, so hearin' you fuss over me like that, I'm honestly touched."

Leaning against the wall with a pipe in his mouth, Halibel laughed as he answered Subaru's words of concern.
This strongest Wolf Human of the city-state was the hero of the victory in that battle among the Coupled Dragon Carriages, having held back the black Dragon that had appeared ahead of the undead's assault—the Dragon Zombie.
Thanks to him keeping the Dragon Zombie away from the carriages, the damage had been kept to a minimum. And on top of that, he'd single-handedly managed to slay a Dragon—an absurd Shinobi indeed.
But at that remark from Halibel, Anastasia, the one he'd teased, made a displeased face.

"What's that supposed to mean? Talk like that and it sounds like I'm some heartless employer. I always pay proper wages for proper work, don't I?"

"See, this is exactly it. Turnin' everything into a matter of coin like that, you've gone and become such an unlovable girl... Bet it's on account of Ricardo's hands-off way of raisin' you, made you all shrewd."

"Ha-a-ali-be-e-el."

"Yeah, yeah, I lose, fine."

The exchange of Kararagi dialect between the two came to an end at Anastasia's lukewarm summons.
Old acquaintances though they were, the easy familiarity of the exchange called to mind an uncle and his niece. Anastasia was small and baby-faced, but her personality was the polar opposite of childish, so it was rare for Subaru to get that impression of her.
Unaware of Subaru's private impression, Halibel—having just been talked down by Anastasia—didn't let the conversation end there, saying "anyway, about that" and continuing on.

"Fightin' 'em taught me somethin'—Vollachia's zombies are a bit different from Kararagi's zombies. Reckon I might be pretty bad at handlin' 'em."

"Different zombies between Vollachia and Kararagi? What does that even mean? Don't tell me there's a separate cause behind a zombie panic happening at the same time here?"

"Nah, I don't think it's that. My impression is... the empire's zombies are tougher customers. Seein' as the one behind this has been messin' with the empire's Great Spirit, might just be a matter of distance."

"Distance..."

Rocking the pipe clenched between his teeth up and down, Halibel revealed his impression as such.
That subtle difference in nuance didn't quite click for Subaru, who didn't know Kararagi's undead. Still, if someone of Halibel's caliber sensed a difference, there was surely a real one there.
He didn't want to think that zombies were really being put to separate uses for separate reasons at the same time, though.

"Rather than distance, I get the impression it's more a matter of degree of completion."

"What's that supposed to mean, Julius?"

"Given that Halibel rates them as formidable, it stands to reason that the zombies appearing in the empire are superior in ability. However, chronologically, the city-state's zombies appeared earlier. In other words, what we can infer from this is..."

"Kararagi's zombies were the practice run, and Vollachia's zombies are the real deal. Is that what you're getting at, Sir Julius?"

Asked by Anastasia, Ram put that question to Julius, who touched the scar beneath his left eye as he expanded on his reasoning. At Ram's words, he tucked his chin and said, "Precisely," then continued.

"If that reasoning holds, it would also explain Halibel's impression. Given that, Halibel—might I ask why you feel you're poorly matched against the empire's zombies?"

"Didn't see it myself, but the zombie that showed up at the dragon carriages could multiply, right? That black Dragon pulled somethin' similar. See, my attacks are all about the one-hit curse-kill—land it and it's over. So somethin' that don't stay dead is about the worst match for me there is."

"—————"

"Huh? What's with the silence?"

"No, it's just that you casually admitted your whole gimmick is a curse-kill, so it kinda threw me."

Subaru could hardly believe his ears at how easily Halibel had exposed his own hand. But at Subaru's remark, Halibel nodded as if in understanding and said, "Ah."

"Ain't like it matters much if folks know. Even knowin', not many people can manage to dodge every single hit of mine, y'know?"

"Hmm, that's somehow both humble and not humble at all. Reinhard and Ceci are the same way—guess that's just something all the strongest people in every country have in common..."

Subaru was struck with genuine admiration at Halibel's brazen declaration of strength.
Cecilus, a walking mass of approval-seeking, went without saying, but even Reinhard—modesty and sincerity practically given human form—held absolute confidence in his own abilities.
In truth, when someone that strong said "oh, I'm nothing much," it carried zero persuasive weight, so on that front, it was much more endearing when they just owned it outright.
In any case—

"So I get that Halibel doesn't fight the 'zombies' all that well... but given that, what do we do? Who would be best to march into the capital?"

"Weren't exactly a strongest-in-Kararagi kind of admission of weakness, but even so, Ucchi vouches Halibel's got the skill. Shouldn't go easy on him—he oughta be in the assault group."

"Well, ain't like I'd cry and fuss and say I don't wanna, now would I?"

At Anastasia's sharpness even without arguing back, Halibel gave a wry laugh.
In truth, even if his curse-kill special attack didn't work on zombies, there was no doubt Halibel had the strength to fight a black Dragon single-handedly. Deciding that he'd be part of the assault group could be treated as settled, Subaru thought, and figuring it was time to get to the main topic, he raised his hand right there and said, "Mind if I say something?"
And then—

"I'll say this first: I'm joining the assault group."

So, faster than anyone else could volunteer, Subaru staked his own claim to a place in it.

"—————"

Attention gathered on Subaru, the self-nominated candidate, and a moment of blank silence followed.
Taking advantage of that silence, Subaru got the opening to explain his reasons for why he needed to be the one going in.

"First, everyone should already understand that Spica's Authority is a zombie-killer—it has the power to nullify resurrection. But as things stand, using it requires me, her guardian, to be there with her. If even Halibel finds the enemy's revival tough going, then Spica is absolutely necessary for cracking this."

Spica's contribution had been instrumental in defeating the formidable foe who'd attacked the Coupled Dragon Carriages, the "Poison Princess" Lamia Godwin.
If she could fully wield the "Gluttony" Authority, she could sever the undead's endless "death-flight." There was no more useful strategy for defeating Sphinx than Spica.

"Neither my job nor Spica's can be filled by anyone else. ...Which means, by necessity, Beako's coming with me to the capital too."

"That is exactly what Betty wants, I suppose. Betty has had quite enough of being apart from Subaru."

"Sorry. Appreciate it."

Beatrice, sitting tucked in right beside the hand-raised Subaru, took hold of his raised hand and squeezed it tight, consenting to accompany him into the assault group as well.
Taking heart in his partner's answer, Subaru looked at Spica—the linchpin of the whole operation—and at Rem, to whom Spica was clinging.
Spica, arms wrapped around his waist in a hug, and Rem, supporting her shoulder, received Subaru's gaze, and—

"...Why are you looking at me with such nervous eyes?"

"Well, it's just—we're gonna be taking Spica somewhere dangerous again, and if that makes Rem hate me, my heart's gonna shatter into pieces..."

"—. It's fine, isn't it? You could just have Emilia-san comfort you instead."

"Huh?"

At Subaru's weak-kneed answer, Rem muttered that while looking away with a discontented expression.
While Subaru was left failing to properly digest the meaning of those words, Ram, standing beside her sister, narrowed her pale pink eyes and said, "Rem."

"What will it be? Shall we tear Barusu limb from limb?"

"Don't just casually say something so violent, sister!"

"I'm joking. Even if we tore him apart now, wouldn't he just come back to life? A corpse that multiplies on top of it—Barusu... what a nightmare. How ghastly."

"Don't kill me on your own whim and then multiply me and act disgusted about it!"

At her sister's utterly Ram-like remark, Rem gave a wry smile and shook her head. Then, saying "Thank you, sister" to Ram,

"I'm glad for your consideration, sister. But like you said, right now anything we do just comes back to life, so let's set that aside for later. More importantly..."

"Uh?"

"Spica-chan, do you think you can do your best alongside that man?"

Rem, the trace of her wry smile gone, looked down at Spica clinging to her and asked as much. At the question, Spica's blue eyes went round, and then her face immediately took on a resolute look.
Asked about her role, the girl—aware of what she needed to do—nodded her head firmly, over and over,

"Aa-u!"

—she nodded, vigorously, with that.
Taking that voiceless voice as affirmation, Rem's expression became a mixture of loneliness and frustration. As if to plainly reveal her true feelings, her voice, when it continued, trembled.

"If it were up to me, I'd want to go along too. ...But I know that if I went along as I am now, I'd only be a burden. Even so..."

"—Then you don't need to worry, Rem."

Rem's earnest plea was cut off by a reassuring voice.
Rem's pale blue eyes widened involuntarily as she said "eh," and there, the one who'd spoken to her, thumping her own chest, was Emilia.
She filled those beautiful violet eyes of hers with determination and nodded at Rem.

"I really, really understand how you feel—worrying about that girl, and about Subaru and the others going with her. So no matter what happens, I'll protect them so it'll be okay!"

"Emilia-san..."

"Leave it to me. I may not look it, but I'm reeeally strong."

Making a fist and flexing her arm, Emilia smiled at Rem like that.
At Emilia's overwhelmingly reassuring words, Rem's eyes darted about, at a loss for what to say.
However—

"No, no, no! What are you saying, Emilia-sama! There's no way I can permit you to go, of all people!"

"Eh!? Why not!?"

"'Why not,' he says—do I really need to spell it out!?"

Emilia stared, wide-eyed with shock, but even more shocked than her was Otto.
Ever since regrouping in the empire, Otto had had a near-permanent scowl on his face, so seeing his familiar flustered expression again at last—well, it wasn't exactly something to laugh at.
In truth, this time, Otto's opinion was far more reasonable than Emilia's.

"This is a mission to charge into the single most dangerous place in this entire empire, no matter how you slice it. There's no way I can just send Emilia-sama off with a cheerful 'go right ahead.'"

"But listen here, Otto-anii. If we're talkin' 'bout dangerous places, ain't the whole point moot the second we set foot in Vollachia to begin with?"

"Hard to argue with that, but there's still such a thing as degrees of danger. As Natsuki-san said, the capital is the enemy's stronghold... their home base. There's no way its defenses are as tight as some village with no strategic value. The capital is, without question, the single most dangerous zone."

"Well, guess that's... true enough."

Met with a proper counter-argument to his proper argument, Garfiel withdrew his objection.
Riding that momentum, Otto turned his gaze toward Subaru and Beatrice and said,

"To be honest, I'm against Natsuki-san and the others going too. This is fundamentally the empire's problem, and risking your lives for another country's sake is out of line."

"Mr. Honest over here. But like I've said plenty of times already..."

"I know. It doesn't matter whether it happens in the Kingdom or the Empire, that's the point, isn't it? Given that there's a chance the 'Witch's' true target is Lugunica, I agree with you there too. Even I'd rather see the empire turned into a wasteland than have this thing show up back home."

"You've stopped bothering to hide just how much of an in-group loyalist you are lately, haven't you."

Otto had always had a hard-nosed streak to begin with, and the cost-benefit balance of getting involved in Vollachia's problems clearly didn't add up for him. Hence his endless reluctance.
But, as Otto himself had come to understand, for Subaru this wasn't a matter of territory.
Subaru disliked the Vollachian Empire too, but even in an empire he disliked, there were people Subaru had come to love. For their sake, he wanted to do everything he possibly could.
And to that end—

"—It's not just about Spica. I need to be there in the capital too."

Of course, part of the reason Subaru volunteered for the assault group was to accompany Spica as her guardian, so she could fully unleash the power of her Authority.
But his greatest purpose was this: the capital, which would surely become the empire's fiercest battleground—there, to overturn someone's unavoidable "death" waiting to happen, and to rewrite fate with his own hands.

On the "Isle of Swordsmen," Subaru had gone to considerable, reckless lengths to win over every last one of the sword slaves to his side.
But it had been worth it. If a reward was promised, Subaru wouldn't hesitate to pay the price for it.

"Betty feels the same as Subaru—not wanting him to do anything dangerous, I suppose."

At that, Beatrice, speaking up in Subaru's place, began stating her own thoughts to Otto, who was looking between Subaru and her.
Beatrice, the distinctive pattern surfacing in her blue eyes, met Otto's gaze as he looked her way, and said,

"But is that girl's power not useful for dismantling the enemy's 'Sacrament of the Immortal King'? And besides, if it should become necessary to seal Sphinx away, Betty's power will surely be needed."

"Seal her away, you mean."

"If Spica's Authority proves ineffective, sealing her is the only way left to stop Sphinx's rampage, I suppose. If she should manage to kill herself and escape, that would only be the start of an endless chase. To prevent that, it will be Betty's magic's turn to shine, I suppose."

With one hand holding Subaru's, Beatrice thrust her other free hand forward.
Seeing that small, round hand characteristic of a young girl, what rose in Subaru's mind was a sight reminiscent of the "sealing" Beatrice had spoken of—the fate of Roy Alphard, the "Gluttony" Sin Archbishop successfully captured at the Pleiades Watchtower.
Roy, his entire body hardened like wax by Shadow magic, had been restrained in a state where not just his movement but even his thoughts were sealed away.

"If I recall, the 'Witch of Envy' is captured under that same kind of seal, isn't she..."

"That's right. A 'witch'-sealing method with a track record of trust and results, I suppose. If sealing Sphinx away is essential to bringing this to a close, then—sorry as it is to you and Petra—Betty and Subaru are indispensable, I suppose."

At Beatrice's backing from a magical standpoint, Subaru glanced toward Otto, and past him, at Petra standing beside Frederica.
Petra, who in the time he hadn't seen her seemed to have grown even more reliable and full of promise, naturally didn't look pleased at the idea of Subaru charging into the capital in that shrunken body of his.
Still, being the clever girl she was, she understood the validity of Beatrice's reasoning, and that if the army of the undead wasn't stopped here, the damage would grow exponentially.
So, with a thoroughly reluctant sigh, she said,

"Guess it's gonna end up this way after all, huh."

"Petra..."

"I'm in the same boat as Rem-san—not in a position to go along either. ...At least it's some comfort that Beatrice-chan's going along to keep an eye on you."

Subaru's chest ached at the fact that he himself was the very source of anxiety for this all-too-understanding girl. Trusted by Petra like that, Beatrice nodded as if taking on a duty.

"Betty understands Petra's feelings well enough, I suppose. As long as Betty is with him, Subaru will not come to harm, I suppose."

"Yeah, thank you, Beatrice-chan."

"Indeed. It reassures me to know Beatrice will be with Subaru, and it makes me happy too. But if the two of you and I were all together, it would be even more reassuring..."

"Emilia-sama..."

"Ugh..."

Following the beautiful friendship between Beatrice and Petra, Emilia tried to press her case in a thoroughly unconvincing way, and was shot down by Otto's stern glare.
Still, as mentioned before, on this particular matter Subaru too thought Otto's argument was the sound one.
Subaru wanted to grant Emilia's wishes wherever he could, but granting this one would mean bringing Emilia along to the single most dangerous place in the empire.

On the other hand, Garfiel's point had some merit too.
To begin with, simply having come this far into the empire already meant danger was a given, and staying behind in Garkla wouldn't guarantee anyone's safety either.
In that sense, the ideal was to keep everyone he knew close at his side—

"—I am in favor of adding Emilia-sama to the assault group heading to the capital."

"My lord!?"

But then, an opinion diametrically opposed to everything up to that point suddenly burst out.
So startled by the opinion that she doubted her own ears, Frederica raised her voice. But it was only that Frederica happened to be the first to voice it—nearly everyone present couldn't hide their shock at that opinion, Roswaal's declaration.
Amid the shock that gripped the room, the one who recovered relatively quickly was Anastasia. Smoothing down her fox-fur stole, she furrowed her brow and said,

"Now that's a surprisin' opinion. I'd have figured everyone on Emilia-san's side agreed on one thing—bein' unanimously against Emilia-san."

"That the representative of a camp, Emilia-sama, should have her opinion go unheard is simply too pitiable... is a joke, of course. I haven't come out against the grain just to be difficult."

"...Let's hear it, then."

At Roswaal's shrug, Otto, his voice and expression carefully restrained, prompted him to continue.
Subaru too, and Emilia, the one whose case had just been championed, waited with bated breath for Roswaal's reasoning. Under everyone's watching eyes, Roswaal closed his eyes, leaving only the yellow one open, and said,

"It's not a complicated matter. In the context of our alliance with the empire, Emilia-sama's strength is a compelling asset we can offer them. If it had been Anastasia-sama proposing to go, everyone should stop her—but Emilia-sama is a different matter e-e-entirely. That much everyone already acknowledged in practice, the moment they let Emilia-sama take part in the great battle before the zombies even appeared."

"Yes, exactly! See, even back then, I ran into Madelyn and Mezoreia, and I still came back just fine, without a scratch."

"The situation is different this time. What's called for is a small elite surprise strike, and I have serious doubts that Emilia-sama's blu— bold approach to combat is suited to it."

"That's well within a margin we can cover. And besides, with Emilia-sama there, even if the operation should fail, the recovery from that failure can be built into the plan from the sta-a-art. She could freeze half the capital solid, could she not?"

Roswaal's outrageous remark, delivered with one finger raised, wasn't actually as reckless as it sounded.
Emilia had an irreplaceable unit performance—an absurd quantity of Mana she could produce entirely on her own, plus the skill of a wide-area attack that made full use of it.
Put simply, Emilia alone could turn a battlefield into a winter landscape and go dashing about cheerfully, unbothered, in the midst of the freezing cold. It was to make maximal use of that overwhelmingly high sustained combat capability that Subaru had devised "Ice Brand Arts" in the first place, and Roswaal too recognized its value.

In truth, there was no doubting that Emilia was one of the camp's top combat assets.
Having repeatedly sent her into the Pleiades Watchtower and the decisive battle at the capital before now, arguing that she was too dangerous to send out this time carried little persuasive weight.
On top of that, Roswaal held a certainty that only he possessed.
And that was—

"Subaru-kun, too, would fight harder with Emilia-sama there, wouldn't he, I wonde-e-er?"

"You..."

At Roswaal's smile, dripping with nothing but ill intent, Subaru couldn't help but bite his lip.
That razor's-edge remark was, unmistakably, a jab at the Authority Roswaal alone knew Subaru held—"Return by Death."
Roswaal didn't know that "death" was the trigger for Subaru's time regression, but he did know Subaru had some such hidden trump card.
And he was certain, too, that for Emilia's sake, Subaru wouldn't hold that trump card back.

Roswaal's calculation was correct.
No matter what happened to anyone else, Subaru would use "Return by Death," but if Emilia were the one who came along, Subaru's desperation, his all-out effort, would rise to whatever level Roswaal wanted—or beyond it.
That said, being persuaded by such reasoning was something only Roswaal was capable of.

"My lord, I too, like Otto-sama, am against Emilia-sama going."

Indeed, even hearing Roswaal's opinion, the feelings of those opposed weren't shaken.
Even Frederica, who within the camp tended more often than not to side with Roswaal, prioritized the danger of the situation and opposed Emilia joining the assault group.
However—

"Of course, I understand that Emilia-sama's strength far surpasses what either Otto-sama or I can manage, but even so, for her precious body to..."

"—I will accompany her. Does that answer satisfy you?"

"Eh?"

Frederica, still protesting that she couldn't nod along after all, widened her eyes.
It was a short, but unmistakably decisive declaration from Roswaal. Raising both hands so everyone could see them, he waved them as he spoke.

"For the assault group heading to the capital—allow me to accompany Emilia-sama and the others as well, won't you? Fortunately, now that there's no more need to hide my true nature, let's give both my grand tricks and my small ones a good-o generous airing."

"Eh, Roswaal's coming too? No, wait—you'll come along with us?"

"Surprised?"

"Well, it's just that Roswaal always struck me as the type who's off relaxing at the mansion."

At Emilia's plainly astonished reaction, Roswaal gave a wry smile.
"Relaxing" was a rather Emilia-esque way of putting it, but the reason Roswaal was so often absent from the scene when problems got solved owed largely to the fact that Roswaal himself was frequently the cause of those problems—and to the simple fact that he wasn't a trustworthy ally to begin with.
That said, there was no reason, situationally, for Roswaal to betray anyone's trust here.
Considering there was also no possibility of Roswaal engaging in some covert scheme within the empire, this was, in a sense, a situation in which Roswaal could be trusted far more than back in the Kingdom.

"Makes me wonder what it says that you're a more trustworthy ally when you're in some other country..." Subaru muttered.

Setting aside Beatrice, who was making a thoroughly sour face at the news of Roswaal's accompaniment, the proposal seemed to Subaru to have nothing but merits.
If they were talking about the camp's top combat assets, then Roswaal, without question, was one of them as well.

"I too—" Otto began to say something for a moment.
But the words he'd started to say were cut off mid-utterance as he gritted his teeth, and after taking a deep breath and letting it out, he said,

"Garfiel! Please accompany Natsuki-san and the others."

"—. You sure about that, huh? Gonna end up with all three of the 'Knights' headin' out, like some kinda 'here come the three knights' routine."

"At this point, a half-measure deployment would be the worse strategy. Though I'll admit, I don't much like that this all plays right into the margrave's hands regardless."

"Otto-kun does have a tendency to overestimate me time and again, doesn't he."

Roswaal wore a composed smile as Otto pressed a hand to his forehead. Seeing his elder brother figure's anguish, Garfiel gnashed his fangs and brought his fists together in front of his chest.
With a dry crack of sound, Garfiel stamped the floor hard and said,

"Fine by me. Otto-anii's worries, Petra's worries, and while we're at it, sis's worries too—I'll take 'em all off your hands and haul 'em along with me."

"Garf, you're leaving out Ram's worries."

"Ain't about to go around haulin' worries about Roswaal with me!"

"Idiot. Nobody needs to worry about Roswaal-sama. Worry about Garf."

Having made his decision with such gusto, Garfiel deflated with a "gaoh" under Ram's blow.
Having thus demonstrated her sinful nature as a woman, Ram turned her gaze to Roswaal, who had volunteered for the assault group, and said,

"Do please indulge yourself to the fullest. Show the empire what Roswaal-sama's power is capable of."

"Indeed. The empire's been rather too dismissive of magic, after all, hasn't it-t-t?"

At Roswaal's smile, Ram pinched the skirt of her travel outfit and dropped a curtsy.
Watching that exchange of trust, neither excessive nor lacking, Emilia—left behind by the flow of conversation—blinked her eyes.

"Um, so in the end, Roswaal and Garfiel are coming too, and I'm going along with Subaru and the others to the capital... is that right?"

"Yeah, that should be fine. Like Otto said—it's our top firepower."

With Emilia, Garfiel, and Roswaal, the three top combat assets of the Emilia Camp, all going together, it could be said they'd be putting on the finest possible showing as an ally.
Whether Subaru, Beatrice, and Spica, the three of them, would end up being a drag on the group was a worry all its own.

"And Halibel's joining in on top of that. And then there's—"

"—Subaru."

Just as he was about to declare the group the undeniable strongest lineup, a call came, and Subaru turned around.
The one who'd called Subaru was a man not among the members just named. And if they were gathering only the strongest members, he was, without question, someone Subaru would have wanted to include.
But the man in traditional dress, his features sharp and handsome, kept his eyes resolute as he stated flatly,

"I will remain at Anastasia-sama's side. Drawing the enemy's attention to the fortress city will also serve to aid your assault."

"—————"

"Of course, whoever the Emperor's Excellency trusts will likely take charge of the city's defense, but I intend to lend my aid as well. More than anything..."

There, he paused a moment, and he—Julius—looked toward Anastasia beside him.
Anastasia, who had pushed herself to rush to Vollachia in search of Subaru and the others after they vanished. She deserved his gratitude, and absolutely needed to return home safely.
For that reason, Julius stated it plainly.

"I am Anastasia-sama's foremost knight."

At those words, spoken with such unclouded conviction of self, Subaru quietly held his breath.
Because he understood that it was both a statement of Julius's own position, and, at the same time, a spur directed at Subaru, who stood in the same position.

—Just as Julius would protect Anastasia, so too should Subaru protect Emilia.

"Just so you know, staying behind here doesn't mean you get to take it easy. Let your guard down and it'll be a repeat of that humiliation with Reid, you know."

"How dreadful. Even now, I shudder every time I see the scar on my face in the mirror."

"Look at you, mouthing off!"

Laughing off Julius's quip, Subaru sprang to his feet with vigor.
Then, looking over everyone gathered, he confirmed it once more.

"Going are me and Beako, plus Spica. Along with Emilia-tan, Garfiel, and Roswaal, and Halibel's part of the team too."

"Mn, let's do our best. Ram and the others staying behind, and Anastasia-san and the others too—please be careful of the 'zombies.'"

"Bit rich, comin' from the folks marchin' right into the enemy's home turf. But how flashy a show we can put on here is gonna decide just how hard a time you all have. Time to show what we're made of."

As if her merchant's spirit had been stirred, Anastasia answered with a wicked little smile.
Taking heart in that, Subaru clenched his fists tight before the decisive battle for the empire that now loomed ahead.

Whatever it took, he would give everything he had to stop the "Great Disaster."
And to that end, they needed to hurry to the capital.
Because—

"—If we're not careful, we'll come back to find that Ceci, left behind, went and casually slaughtered a thousand men or ten thousand, and burned through the Great Spirit's Mana in the process, dooming the empire, and that'd be a sight too awful to look at."

And in that case, whether it would be Subaru's fault for having brought Cecilus along, or the empire's own fault for having left Cecilus unsupervised up to now—there seemed no way to tell the two apart.
