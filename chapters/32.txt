Arc 2 Chapter 8: "The Kingdom's Circumstances and That Girl's Circumstances"

"You really are strange, you know. Here you are, all the way at the residence of Roswaal L. Mathers of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica, and you don't even know the situation. However did you manage to pass the kingdom's border inspection?"

"Well, in a sense, it was kind of like sneaking across the border illegally..."

*I noticed I was in the country before I knew it. Got a problem with that?* That was about the level of Subaru's awareness.
At his slack-jawed answer, Emilia was first shocked, then glared at him with the righteous indignation of someone scolding a small child.

"Unbelievable. Just blurting something like that out so easily — do you have any idea what would happen if we reported that to the management bureau? You'd be thrown straight into a cell and beaten black and blue."

"'Beaten black and blue,' huh. Don't hear that one much these days."

"Don't joke around. Hey, Subaru. Are you really okay? Is everyone around you like this? Or is it just you who doesn't know anything, specifically?"

Feeling bad in the face of Emilia's genuine concern, Subaru scratched his head and reflected on his own attitude up to this point.

"Ah, well, I guess I'm just especially forgetful. So if it's no trouble, I would be most humbly and gratefully obliged to receive your gracious instruction on the matter."

"Hearing you use language like that, you almost sound like you're from a decent family..."

*If I showed up at high society with this half-baked way of talking, my social life would be over on the spot.* That said, Emilia being fooled by mere surface appearances seemed a little suspicious too, so—

"Something's off here. Emilia-tan, are you actually not that deep into this kind of knowledge either? What you just said was honorific and humble speech all mixed up into a mess."

"Ugh... can't deny it."

At Subaru's pointed remark, Emilia shrank in on herself as she answered. Subaru was surprised to see this side of her, since she'd seemed like she knew everything — but it was Roswaal, silent at the head of the table until now, who came to her defense.

"Emilia-sama is still studying that area, you see. Of course, I don't blame you for pointing it out, either."

"Still studying, huh. Does that, by any chance, tie into what you were just talking about?"

"Ahaha, sharp. You're thinking things through, aren't you. And precisely because you're thinking, these remarks that sound like you *aren't* thinking keep tumbling out."

"Thinking things through while you live is just common sense, isn't it? Even with your limbs aching and your nose running and your guts hanging out on display, thinking is a human being's duty."

"That's a frightening metaphor, and yet it's oddly heartfelt..."

*Well, of course — those are words of accumulated wisdom drawn straight from personal experience.*
Perhaps moved by Subaru's words, Roswaal held out a hand, and the twins slipped a notebook and pen into it. He jotted the gist of the conversation down quickly, then turned back to face Subaru properly.

"That was good too. Being around you gives rise to all sorts of inspiration — how wonderful."

"How many times a day do you write in that thing? What number notebook is that in your life?"

"That question touches upon the sacred mysteries of House Mathers. So-o-orry, can't tell you that."

Watching a grown adult of considerable age turn his head away with a pout, Subaru felt thoroughly worn out.
At last, Subaru found himself at a loss for how to deal with someone even more eccentric than himself. It was Emilia, clearing her throat to steer the conversation back on course, who threw him a lifeline.

"All right, back to the topic — does Subaru know what state this country, the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica, is currently in?"

"Not even the slightest bit."

"Saying it that flatly is almost refreshing. Subaru's way of living just keeps surprising me. Now I'm really starting to worry."

Emilia's look had gone past exasperation and was starting to resemble the way one looks at a small child. It wasn't as though Subaru had been running some scheme to tickle her protective instincts and appeal to her maternal side, but the end result was that, through sheer momentum, the emotional distance between them had widened to something like mother and child.
Trying somehow to close that gap in perceived maturity, Subaru rocked his chair and pressed, "And then! And then!"

"You're like a little kid. Um, right now Lugunica is under martial law. Entry and exit with other countries especially is being handled very strictly."

"Martial law... that's not a peaceful-sounding word."

"Ahaha, 'peaceful' isn't quite the word for it. After all, right now the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica has no king."

Roswaal picked up the conclusion. Subaru weighed the words, and once their meaning sank in, he quietly held his breath.
He glanced around at Emilia and the twins, then at Beatrice and Puck. There was no sign of unease in any of them — this must be common knowledge among them. That realization was quickly followed by wariness at the fact that something like this was being disclosed to someone as much an outsider as himself, but—

"No need to worry! It's already common, undeniable knowledge, known even among the common folk."

"Is that so. No, here I was bracing for the 'now that you know our secret, we can't let you leave alive' development."

"If we're the ones who let it slip, that wouldn't be much of a payoff for you, would it. ...Anyway, that's the state of things, which is why the whole country is on edge."

"Martial law is part of that too. In this situation with no king, it's not desirable to bring sparks into another country, or vice versa, either."

*I see*, Subaru nodded to himself.
A kingdom with no king was, structurally speaking, a fatal state of affairs for a monarchy. Whether from illness or some other cause, whatever the reason, the country was reeling from the sudden 'death' of its king.

"Hm? But wait a sec. Isn't the usual thing that the king's kid inherits the throne and everything's solved? If they're too young, you just stick a regent on it, right?"

"Hm, keeping up with the conversation, are you. In any case, ordinarily that's exactly how it would go. But the origin of this matter goes back half a year. Around the same time the king passed away, there was an epidemic that spread through the castle."

An illness announced as afflicting a particular bloodline specifically, Roswaal explained.
As a result, the king and his descendants who had been living in the royal castle were wiped out entirely.

"So the king really is gone, then. So what happens to the country in that case? There's no royal bloodline left — do they hold some kind of popular vote and elect a prime minister?"

"I have absolutely no idea what that second half meant, but as things stand now, the country's governance is being carried out by the Council of Wise Men. It's a gathering of distinguished families whose names are etched into the kingdom's history. Their influence on the actual politics of the nation isn't that significant, though."

Here he paused for a moment, then drew a breath and said "however," his expression tightening.

"—A kingdom without a king ought never to be allowed to exist."

"Yeah, guess so."

Even if it were merely a figurehead with no bearing on governance, an organization without a head simply cannot function. Much less a nation.
Even in Japan, however much the primacy of public opinion gets whispered about, there's still a prime minister at the top. Someone in the position of bearing responsibility — even if that post changes hands with alarming frequency.

"So then, a kingless kingdom has to choose a new king. But the bloodline's basically wiped out. So they have to select a king in a way everyone in the country can accept."

"—Really, Subaru, you're a strange one. You don't know anything, and yet your head works like that. It's exactly like... a wise fool, I suppose."

"Don't go complimenting me like that. I'm not used to being praised, I'll fall for you in a heartbeat."

Muttering that to hide his embarrassment, Subaru turned his face away from Emilia.
He slapped his cheeks to give himself rosy "maple-leaf" color as cover for his blush, then turned back around, holding up a finger toward Roswaal as he organized his thoughts.

"I see, I'm starting to get it bit by bit. In other words, the kingdom has no king, and on top of that it's in an uproar over selecting one. Relations with other countries are shrinking too, basically a mini isolationist state. And into that walks this mysterious foreigner — me. I'm suspicious as hell!"

"And to add further to that, you've made contact with Emilia-sama and gotten entangled with House Mathers as well. If someone were quick to jump to conclusions, that alone might be enough..."

Roswaal closed his eyes and brought the edge of his hand to his own neck in a guillotine gesture.
Watching that gesture out of the corner of his eye, Subaru couldn't stop the cold sweat born of a bad feeling.

"Whoa-oh! From that pervert's little remark just now, I'm pretty much getting the picture, getting the picture indeed!? House Mathers looks the part of a proper, distinguished family just from appearances — could it possibly, just possibly, be connected to the Council of Wise Men, or even something closer to the heart of the matter—!?"

Given the flow of the conversation so far, that was the greatest possibility Subaru could imagine.
Both the way the conversation was being led, as if savoring the reveal, and the way this whole exchange had been constructed — none of it sounded like the words of someone merely observing the situation from outside.
Which meant there was no way to conclude anything other than that he was a party involved — and being the master of a mansion this large, it stood to reason his family's standing was considerable as well—

"F-forgive my disrespect — please, at least accept my little finger... let me offer my little finger as atonement."

"Good news for you, off chasing frightening imaginings like that. You can relax, hm? I am not a member of the Council of Wise Men, nor, for the time being, am I in a position connected to the kingdom's throne. —Isn't that right, Emilia-sama."

Roswaal laughed uproariously, as though he'd just heard the finest joke, at Subaru offering up his little finger on the table. He then turned to Emilia to seek her agreement, and she responded with a sour, silent expression.
For a moment, Subaru nearly erupted at Roswaal's suggestive attitude, but he quickly caught himself, noticing something off, and his thoughts ground to a halt.

Yes — it was something that had been nagging at him repeatedly since a while ago.

"Why... does the master of this mansion address Emilia-tan with 'sama'?"

That was a relationship in which the person of highest standing in the household paid the utmost respect to another.
Watching unease sprout within him and swiftly bloom into a black flower, Subaru turned his gaze to Roswaal to confirm it. And then—

"Naturally, don't you think? Addressing someone of higher standing than oneself with an honorific title, hm?"

Roswaal said this with a mischievous smile, hands folded on the table.
Subaru froze, mouth hanging agape in stunned silence. He turned to look at Emilia with movements so mechanical they seemed like they ought to creak, and she sighed as though resigning herself to it.

"It's not like I was trying to trick you or anything, okay."

"—Um, so, Emilia-tan is, in other words—"

Subaru still, incorrigibly, tacked "-tan" onto her name.
As if delivering the finishing blow to him as he tried to deny this reality—

"My current title is one of the candidates for the forty-second King of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica. Backed by Roswaal, Margrave of this estate, here."

At those words, Subaru felt the hurdle before him rise to the height of a space elevator.

—The person I fell for turned out to be a queen.

That line flickered across his mind. More precisely, a queen candidate, but still.

"Somehow the phrase 'queen candidate' has kind of an erotic vibe to it."

"I don't really get it, but I'm being serious here."

"Ngh, even that cold stare gives me a little thrill... as expected of you, my queen. Don't look at me like I'm some lowly swine."

"I am not giving you that kind of nasty look! And you're one to talk about looks, Subaru!"

Writhing at the scolding delivered at point-blank range by that beautiful face, Subaru collapsed onto the table, trembling slightly. He buried his face to hide his expression, but inside, his heart was pounding wildly.
In any case, the one thing he did not want Emilia to see right now was the pathetic look that must surely be on his face.

A queen candidate — frankly, for Subaru, who had no real familiarity with monarchy, the notion of a world with a king at its apex was something he barely knew outside of manga and games.
Even so, he understood just how distant a being capable of becoming head of state must be from the ordinary masses.
And on top of that, in this world Subaru didn't even have so much as a family registry. He barely qualified to be counted among the ordinary masses in the first place.

The feelings he'd already resigned himself to as hopelessly out of his league were dealt a further blow, now that he knew Emilia's status went beyond simply her beauty.
It genuinely felt like trying to scale the top of a space elevator with his bare hands.

"Oh, Jesus, unbelievable. Even I'm shocked at how rough the road ahead of my love life is. Even Romeo, lover of Juliet, would pity me."

"Um, Subaru, are you okay? You suddenly went quiet..."

Lifting his tear-filled eyes, he saw Emilia's blurred form come into view. The violet looking down at him with concern was amethyst-hued. Against skin white as virgin snow, her pale pink lips let slip a beautiful sound like a silver bell.
She seemed, no matter how far he reached, an existence forever beyond his grasp, and—

"—There."

Unable to help himself, Subaru pulled the girl beside him into an embrace.

"——Eh?"

"Whoa, so tiny. And soft too. Smells amazing."

For a moment, Emilia failed to comprehend that she'd been embraced.
Holding her against his chest, Subaru savored the sensation to the fullest.
For Subaru, with so little experience in relationships, embracing a girl like this was an utterly unprecedented experience. The time he'd shielded Felt at the Loot House regrettably didn't count — there'd been no time to savor it, and besides, she'd been far too skinny.
In any case, Emilia, belatedly registering the situation, began flailing with a reddening face.

"Wa— what!? What's gotten into you!?"

"No, when you got right up in front of me my beastly instincts suddenly went wild. And besides, thinking calmly about it, you were always out of reach anyway, so there's no point getting worked up about it."

"I have no idea what you're talking about at all!?"

"Well, hold on. Let me calmly analyze myself. —Right, in other words, while I was thinking about whether you were within reach or not, there you suddenly were right in front of me, so from the idea of 'well then, let's just check if my reach is good enough,' a spinal reflex kicked in, my inner boy-index spiked its libido, and it led to an embrace, and once I'd gone and done it, I figured I might as well savor it as much as possible or I'd regret it to my dying day, and that conclusion is what triggered this whole happy-for-me, unhappy-for-Emilia-tan surprise snapshot that just occurred... is what happened."

"You explained it in detail and I still have no idea what that meant!?"

Emilia, flailing in his arms, finally shoved off Subaru, who'd been stretching the embrace by stalling with a long-winded explanation.
Lonely at being ejected from the hug, Subaru sat there as red-faced, teary-eyed Emilia pointed a finger at him.

"Don't just suddenly hug girls like that! It wasn't that big a deal because it was me, but if it were an ordinary girl, she'd take it seriously!"

"I wasn't even aiming to do it, okay? Yeah, it was like someone ordered me to, almost. Don't tell me, Emilia-tan, did you use a charm spell on me...? What a wicked woman!"

"I did not do that, that's a false accusation. And stop giving me the thumbs-up!"

His confident thumbs-up rejected, Subaru dejectedly lowered his right hand.
Still, it was true his body had moved on pure spinal reflex. And the words he'd said afterward weren't lies either — but if someone told him to say it plainly all over again, he'd die of embarrassment.

—That it was true his heart had been stolen by the sight of her eyes, or something like that.

"No way, too embarrassing. If someone said that to me, I'd be all, 'How lovely, take me!'"

"My, my, you really are fearless, aren't you."

Hand to his cheek, hips swaying as he went "no, no~," Subaru drew a comment from Roswaal, who had been watching the exchange between the two and was now holding back laughter.
Emilia, face still flushed, shot Roswaal a sharp glare at his remark, but Roswaal, wiping tears of laughter from his eyes, feigned innocence.

"Your partner there is a future queen candidate, hm? Perha-a-aps she'll hold onto this disrespect of yours, tenaciously, for a long time, and once she's taken the throne, have you executed..."

"You're telling me to regret my present happiness for the sake of some uncertain tomorrow? Don't underestimate a victim of relaxed education and its philosophy of living purely for the moment's pleasure."

Rudely flipping his middle finger, Subaru gestured toward Emilia with his free hand.

"Even if she's a future queen, right here, right now, in this moment, Emilia-tan is just one girl. And I'm just one guy. If one man and one woman end up in some scandalous, tangled-up relationship through whatever chance encounter, nobody's got any right to complain about it! So—!"

Planting a foot on his chair and clenching his fist, Subaru declared, held high.

"Even if I get executed tomorrow, I'm going to hug Emilia-tan, and sniff her hair, and live off the afterglow of that memory as my sustenance—!"

At Subaru's blood-spitting declaration, silence filled the dining hall.
Everyone was struck speechless by Subaru's forceful display, swallowing hard as they took in words spoken with such wild, misplaced pride. And then—

"Sorry for interrupting breakfast, everybody. Come on, let's all get back to eating. Let's restart the little family gathering and forget this whole spat ever happened..."

"For a simple perverted remark, that's an awfully grandiose set of principles you laid out, I suppose."

Sweeping his hair back in an attempt to look refreshed, Subaru's plan to salvage the mood through sheer momentum was abruptly demolished.
The one who demolished it was Beatrice, seated some distance away, watching the whole scene with a bored expression. Puck, rudely perched on the dining table, was being stroked along his gray fur by her as she spoke.

"In the end, it's just a story of that little girl over there bewitching him with her charms, I suppose. And to think he could run his mouth that much about it..."

"You really are my natural enemy, you know..."

Thoroughly worn out by Beatrice's blunt remark, Subaru answered only with slumped shoulders.
Beatrice's snort of a response served as the starting signal, and one by one, the rest of the room began to recover their wits.
Which was to say, this marked the beginning of Subaru being condemned for his earlier act of sexual harassment.
