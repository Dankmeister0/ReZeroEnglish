Arc 5 Chapter 6: "Two Spirit Knights, a Greedy Merchant, and a Desireless Angel"

Anastasia's opening salvo betrayed every expectation they'd walked in with.
Still overwhelmed by the presence of the Water Feather Inn, Subaru and the others found their reactions to her lagging behind, as if in pursuit of the welcome they'd just received.

"—Thank you kindly for the trouble. It's a relief to see you came all the way out to greet us yourselves."

Except, that is, for Emilia.
Hearing Emilia—who should have been feeling the same astonishment as the rest of them—answer Anastasia so calmly snapped Subaru back to himself. Looking around, he saw Garfiel wearing a puzzled expression at this unfamiliar Anastasia, while Beatrice had grabbed his sleeve and was tugging at it.
The inn's ambience seemed to have affected the two of them far less than it had rattled Subaru personally. Which was only natural, really. Only those who *knew* what this was would find it startling.

"—My, my. You've gone and gotten a good face on you, haven't you."

Leaving Subaru behind with his exhaled breath, Anastasia murmured this while gazing back at Emilia. There was no mocking glint in her pale blue-green eyes, no scorn in her murmur.
From this brief exchange alone, it seemed some measure of Emilia's year of change had gotten through to her. And well it might—she really had transformed since before.

"Well, her cuteness is the same as ever... no, actually, that's leveled up too."

"Subaru, that's the face you make when you're about to spout nonsense, I suppose."

Letting Beatrice's warning slide past him, Subaru rubbed under his nose with a finger. Anastasia, watching him do it, smiled faintly.

"Been a while, hasn't it, Natsuki-kun—since the commendations after the Subjugation of the White Whale. And how's Margrave Roswaal been keeping, same as always?"

"My apologies for the unsightly display you witnessed back then. Thankfully, things somehow didn't fall apart for our camp either. Give and take, same as ever."

"That so, that so. Well, I think that's for the best too. Glad you two came together this time. Julius has been wanting to see you again too, Natsuki-kun."

At Anastasia's words, hands pressed together, Subaru's face crumpled in plain distaste.
Both Emilia and Anastasia laughed at the reaction, which only made him more uncomfortable. The two of them seemed to have some misunderstanding about the complicated relationship between him and Julius.
Emilia, no matter how many times he told her otherwise, refused to revise her opinion of it.

"Ahh, I don' really get it, but—that woman there's one of Emilia-sama's enemies, right?"

Garfiel, who up until then hadn't joined the conversation, seized the opening to voice the question he'd been holding onto. But the way he put it was crude, hostility written all over it.
Subaru put a hand to his head; Anastasia's already-round eyes went even rounder.

"Garfiel. I mean, that's not *wrong*, exactly, if you put it in the most extreme terms, but could you soften it up a little? We're the ones who got invited today, remember."

"Yeah, well. Ain't we s'posed to end up bitin' each other's heads off eventually? Get too friendly now and it'll mess with my head later when it's time to throw punches."

"That's true. Garfiel's kind-hearted, so that kind of worry makes sense, but..."

"—! Wh-who's kind-hearted!? Emilia-sama, watch what you say!"

Garfiel, who'd just been told off for his own way of speaking, complained about Emilia's newly ironic brand of guilelessness. He was about to move past Emilia entirely and turn his embarrassment on Anastasia instead.

But—

"Ahh! Garf's here! Ojou, why didn't you tell Mimi!"

The inn's wooden door burst open with a loud rattle.
Poking her face out from the other side, beaming with an innocent, adorable glow, was a cat-eared girl—Mimi. She flipped the hem of her robe and vaulted the stone wall as if flying, landing right in front of Subaru and the others.
Before anyone could react, she'd already seized Garfiel's arm in a firm grip.

"Welcome, you must be tired, huh! C'mon, Mimi'll show you to your room! And after that, exploring the inn! It's really somethin' here, y'know! It's different from what Mimi's seen before, that's the amazing part!"

"O-oi, wait, hold up! I still got things to say... your grip's strong!?"

"C'mon, let's go! Right now!"

Small as she was, Mimi swung Garfiel's arm around with all her strength, and Garfiel—who should have had the greater physical power—stumbled off-balance, dragged along.
Some of it was probably her use of martial technique, but a good deal of it was simply Garfiel not being adult enough to shake her off. Not that "adult" was quite the word, given the two of them were supposedly the same age.

"Um..."

Barely able to resist, Garfiel was hauled away, the two of them vanishing into the inn. Emilia looked to Anastasia with a troubled expression, and Anastasia—her brows drooping into an unusually helpless shape—said,

"Haah, Mimi's suddenness gets the better of us all the time too, y'know. Still, that just now was a bit of a surprise even for me."

"Oh, is that so? Good. I was worried it might've just been me who got left behind."

"Nothin' like that, don't worry. Only—"

The softness in Anastasia's expression, born of that shared sentiment, sharpened all at once. Her gaze turned so cold it sent a chill down Subaru's spine, nearly enough to make him step in front of Emilia.
Still fixed with that stare, Anastasia's lips trembled.

"That girl who's got Mimi wrapped around her finger—I'd like to hear all about just what kind of girl she is."

It was the low, ferocious inquiry of a woman looking after her family, quietly burning with anger at the thought of some bad bug latching onto a beloved daughter—or sister.
Faintly, Subaru could feel just how loved Mimi was, and found himself already exhausted, mere moments after arriving.

"...Why does Natsuki-san suddenly look so worn out?"

Otto, having just finished stabling the dragon carriage, arrived to find Subaru looking haggard in front of the inn's entrance, and joined them with those words.

The great hall of the Water Feather Inn had floors entirely of wood planking—the miracle of a banquet hall floored in tatami mats did not, alas, extend to greeting Subaru.

"If they're going to be that particular about everything else, even I have to admit I feel some kind of way about this one."

"What have you been muttering about this whole time? Have you no composure, I wonder."

Seated with his backside on a floor cushion of the seiza-mat variety, Subaru looked around the room, eyes darting this way and that. Beside him, Beatrice sat cross-legged, perfectly composed, while Emilia seemed somewhat at a loss over the whole business of sitting directly on the floor.
Facing them was a long low table, also made of wood, and apart from the absence of tatami, the scene was near-identical to the great hall of a traditional Japanese-style inn.
Still, being something of a professional in these matters, Subaru could identify a few deductions.

"Like, for instance, the sliding doors—the fusuma and shoji—unfortunately haven't been reproduced. And the staff not being in traditional dress is another point off. They've put in a fair amount of atmosphere, but for how much effort's gone into the exterior, something about it feels sloppy."

In the staff's hospitality, Subaru could sense something of the *omotenashi* spirit he knew. But their attire remained resolutely Western—a lingering fantasy-world quality he couldn't quite shake the wrongness of.

"So, overall score: seventy points. Excellent, good, or passable—barely a 'passable' by my grading. Don't let it get you down, keep striving going forward."

"So what exactly are you talking about?"

"Nonsense, foolishness, idle chatter, that sort of thing. Doing this settles my nerves when I'm anxious. ...You can let go of my hand now, by the way. I've calmed down."

"...Just in case, I think I'll keep holding it a little longer, I suppose."

The grip on his left hand tightened slightly, and Subaru said nothing more about it.
Instead, he turned to Emilia, seated on his right, who noticed his gaze as she looked around the room with curiosity.

"It's all so terribly strange, isn't it. Even from outside it was full of unusual things, but coming inside makes me feel it even more. Sitting on the floor, taking off our shoes..."

"No beds in the bedrooms, they lay out futons on the floor instead. I figure it'll be confusing if you're not used to it, so I'll help later... or maybe the inn staff will do it?"

"An offer that, unusually, doesn't seem to have any ulterior motive behind it. ...Actually, come to think of it, Natsuki-san, you seem to know a surprising amount about Kararagi-style customs."

Otto, seated opposite with Beatrice between them, joined the conversation between Subaru and Emilia. At the words "Kararagi-style," Subaru's brow furrowed.

"Kararagi-style, meaning this building and stuff like it?"

"That's right. This sort of wafu architecture is a technique that came over from Kararagi. It's not exactly widespread even over there, but apparently common enough that it's not seen as unusual either—scattered here and there. This Water Feather Inn is probably one vessel for that tradition."

"'Wafu architecture,' huh, that's not something I can just let slide by... why would this inn of all places be under that influence? Well, I guess there doesn't need to be a *why* behind trying to drum up customers."

If it was wafu—not quite "Japanese-style" but close enough—rather than the real thing, there was no room left for doubt now. All Subaru wanted was confirmation. Though what he'd do once he had it was another matter.
As if to fill in that very thought, Otto raised a single finger.

"Probably because Hoshin's influence still runs deep in Priestella. Originally, I've heard, Priestella was one of the very first cities Hoshin was involved in founding. Call it the opening act of Hoshin's rise, before he made his name as the great hero of Kararagi's founding."

"He really did do a lot of stuff, this 'Hoshin of the Wilderness' guy."

"In a different world, it wouldn't have been strange for Hoshin himself to be called the Sage. Well, his achievements were so massive that Priestella's status ended up being a real point of contention between the two nations. It's only now that it's formally Lugunican territory."

According to Otto, up until roughly a hundred years ago, Priestella had been a spark for territorial disputes between Lugunica and Kararagi. Geographically it belonged to Lugunica, but its founding bore the strong imprint of Hoshin, Kararagi's honorary elder statesman.
Lugunica, resenting that influence, had forcibly stripped out Kararagi culture, which stirred resentment among the city's residents who'd lived under it.
From there it had apparently devolved into a mudslinging dispute, and at one point crossing the Great Tigracy River connecting the two nations was banned outright, escalating to something close to a severance of diplomatic relations.

"Fortunately, the crackdown didn't last too terribly long, and things were resolved, more or less by attrition, as the restrictions were gradually eased. The fact that Priestella's Kararagi culture stopped developing during that period of severed relations probably helped put Lugunica's mind at ease too."

"Either way, it sounds like it got settled with an awful lot of hand-waving. Still... does this Kararagi style trace all the way back to Hoshin too, if you go far enough?"

"Seems that way. Hoshin was apparently known even back then for an absurdly innovative streak—ideology, technology, legislation, he supposedly remade all of it from scratch."

"—I see."

At Otto's words, Subaru dipped his chin, having gotten the confirmation he'd wanted.
The great hero of the founding of the Kararagi City-States, "Hoshin of the Wilderness." His true identity was the same as Subaru's and Al's—a being summoned from another world.
That made three now who'd been called to this world—though the timing was different for each. Hoshin, four hundred years ago. Al, twenty years ago. And Subaru, one year ago.
Of this discrepancy in timing, and of why they'd been chosen at all, nothing could be known. He couldn't help but think that pondering it further was probably pointless.
Still—he wasn't alone. Just that fact alone offered his heart some measure of comfort.

"—Sounds like you've been enjoying the wafu inn, from how things are going."

Just as the conversation reached a natural lull, as if the timing had been chosen deliberately, a voice came from outside the great hall. The thin wooden sliding door moved quietly, and from beyond it Anastasia appeared, smiling.
And beside her, having entered alongside her—

"It's been a while, Emilia-sama. As one who ought to have been first among all to greet you, I apologize for the delay in my greeting."

A man's voice, the moment his face came into view, carried an apologetic sorrow across his handsome features.
The gently coaxing quality of his voice was sweet enough that many women, hearing it through nothing but a door, might have swooned imagining its owner's appearance. It would be a funny story if that imagination were betrayed by the voice alone—but unfortunately, the owner of that voice came attached to looks, beauty, and strength in equal measure.
The "Finest Knight," Julius Juukulius, had a way of scratching at other people's hearts simply by existing. At least, that was how it felt to Subaru.

"Yes, it's been a while, Julius. You look well too."

"I'm grateful for your generous heart. And you, Emilia-sama, seem to have only grown more radiant in your beauty. Should your eyes ever dim, it would be a loss not merely to the kingdom, but to this entire world."

The affected turns of phrase, rather than grating, came off polished—which was exactly what made it so irritating. Then he shifted his gaze from the wry-smiling Emilia over to Subaru.

"It's been some time since we last stood face to face. I trust you've been in good health, Natsuki Subaru-dono?"

"...A chill runs down my spine, so don't use some hollow honorific like that. What's with the 'Subaru-dono'? It's insincere as hell."

"You may say so, but it's common knowledge that Subaru-dono has been recognized as a knight under Emilia-sama. Whatever the case in the past, your current standing is not something to be treated lightly. As a fellow knight, I intend to conduct myself accordingly. Is that not so?"

At Julius, resolutely maintaining the demeanor due a peer, Subaru's lips twisted with irritation he couldn't quite suppress.

"'Is that not so'? Cut it out. I'm telling you it pisses me off. Besides, getting recognized for something you don't even mean makes me happy exactly zero percent."

"I see. So it seems that even with a change in standing, the heart beneath hasn't budged an inch. —In that case, there's no need for me to adjust my own attitude either, it would seem."

With that, Julius abruptly dropped the formal bearing he'd maintained until then.
A smile crossed his face, irritatingly well-composed as it was, and he looked down at Subaru sitting on the floor from his considerable height.

"Well then, allow me to start over... it's been a while, Natsuki Subaru. Are you working daily to live up to the knighthood you've been granted?"

"You don't need to tell me that. I've had enough of getting my ass kicked by a certain someone over knowing my place."

"Being described as a beating or a lynching wounds me. On both our honors, I recall it as strictly an equal-footing sparring match."

"You just don't shut up, do you..."

But since it had been Subaru who'd fallen short, insisting loudly on that point would only add to his own embarrassment. So he limited himself to muttering curses.
Watching that attitude, Julius narrowed his eyes with a "hm."

"It seems your tendency toward rash impulsiveness has moderated somewhat. If the self-awareness of knighthood prompted that, then perhaps the judgment behind granting you that station wasn't premature after all. Truly, the discernment of Emilia-sama and Lord Roswaal commands respect. Though, come to think of it..."

"—?"

While pompously assessing Subaru, Julius's gaze slid off him. It landed instead on the person beside Subaru, making herself small—Beatrice.
She, catching Julius's yellow gaze upon her, glared straight back with her pale blue eyes.

"What is it. One oughtn't stare so at a lady, I suppose."

"This is a grave discourtesy on my part. I hadn't imagined a spirit of such high standing as yourself would be present in this company."

"Betty is Subaru's partner, so of course she'd be here, I suppose. A different rank entirely from those nameless minor spirits you keep in tow. Best tremble in fear, I suppose."

Beatrice rose to her feet, placed a hand on Subaru's shoulder, and thrust out her chest proudly.
That look of hers, which could pass for displeasure, was probably a response to Julius's manner toward Subaru. The one point where Subaru, inferior in raw ability, held the advantage over Julius.
That was the rank of the spirit each of them kept as a fellow Spirit Knight.

What Julius kept in tow were six minor spirits, one for each of the six elemental attributes.
In terms of spirit rank, the order ran Spirit, then Minor Spirit, then Lesser Spirit. Taking that into account, it could be said that Subaru, with Beatrice at his side, outranked Julius as a spirit user.
Except—

"If we're comparing actual ability, you and I are both riddled with weaknesses, so don't go pointlessly raising the bar. Besides, I've been on the receiving end of help from his minor spirits before. Cutting him down over that isn't exactly a cool thing to do."

"Hmph. Don't you go stopping me, I suppose. This one's making fun of Subaru, is he not, I suppose. Betty's going to teach him what happens when you look down on her partner. Just because he's the kind of handsome devil who unsettles a spirit's maidenly heart a little, that's no reason to get carried away, I suppose!"

"An unsettling handsome devil, huh!?"

Subaru, caught off guard by the sudden talk of looks, took no small amount of shock from it.
And it was Julius himself—the very cause of the rift—who cut in on this scene threatening to crack the partnership between Subaru and Beatrice.

"You mustn't misunderstand. Your spirit-sama there has no intention of betraying you. It's simply that the Divine Protection dwelling within me is stirring her instincts."

"Your Divine Protection...? Seriously, you've got one too? What kind?"

"Mine is called the Divine Protection of Gathering Spirits—put simply, a Divine Protection that draws spirits' favor. That I, unworthy as I am, have managed to form contracts with minor spirits of all six attributes is entirely thanks to its power. Other spirit users don't tend to look too kindly on it, I'm afraid."

"Betty won't lose to anyone! Subaru's way, way better than you, in, um, how do I put it... yes, decent, I suppose!"

"Thank you! Please don't wound me any further than that!"

He trusted, of course, that there was no possibility of Beatrice ever betraying him—but the fact that no firm, ringing endorsement had come flying out still left him feeling defeated.
Standing face to face with Julius left Subaru's inferiority complex needled again and again. Though that wasn't the whole of it—it was, in fact, the single biggest reason Subaru so disliked him.

"Same as ever, huh—our knight-sama's quite taken with Natsuki-kun."

"Not at all. I merely, as one somewhat senior to him in the ways of knighthood, was sharing a fragment of what that mindset entails. His conduct's effect on the common people could, by extension, shape how people view knights of the Kingdom of Lugunica as a whole."

"That, translated, means Natsuki-kun's name's gotten pretty well known, don't it? Julius, you really aren't honest, are ya."

At Anastasia's teasing tone, Julius loosened his cheeks and bowed his head silently. Better, no doubt, to accept being played with as a conversation piece than to press the point with his mistress. He was clearly well practiced at this.
Meanwhile, Subaru, who'd already had a rough enough time of it, had his left shoulder patted by Beatrice and his right by Emilia, as if in consolation.

"There's no need to worry about it, I suppose. A man's worth isn't his face, but his spirit, I suppose."

"Subaru and Julius are already such good friends, I'm so, so happy."

"Thanks for that vibe that makes it hard to say thank you."

Whatever the people around him were thinking, he received consolations that only made him more anxious about it.
He'd already given up on the matter of his facial construction, and yet, for some reason still uneasy, Subaru reached up to prod at his own cheek. After that, he watched as Anastasia and the others took seats on the opposite side of the table. And then—

"So, is it just the two of you? Mimi and Garfiel seem to be off having some kind of heartwarming little date somewhere in the inn."

"As you've guessed, Mimi and that blonde boy of yours get along real well. Hetaro, who loves his big sister to bits, went chasing after them in a panic, and I made sure to ask Tivey nicely not to get his feathers ruffled about it. That's why those two aren't here."

"Ricardo didn't come? I know that trio of kittens is strong, but it feels a little careless not having a knight escort along."

Beyond the presence of the three Pearlbaton siblings, what caught Subaru's attention was the absence of Ricardo—a walking case of species fraud, a giant kobold whose mere presence made itself loudly known.
And come to think of it, Julius's younger brother Joshua was nowhere to be seen either.

"We're not staying in Priestella for pleasure, you understand. Joshua and Ricardo are currently away from the inn on separate business. They're within the city, so they should be able to show their faces later. Come to think of it, you must have already met Joshua."

"Ah... he was the spitting image of you. If his build were a bit sturdier, you two would be a complete character overlap. Actually, you're welcome to retire from the stage entirely and let him take over."

"I'll keep that as an interesting suggestion, but it's a difficult one, I'm afraid. Unfortunately, Joshua's never been well-suited to physical exertion, ever since he was small. These days he can travel long distances without worry, but in the past it was frustrating enough as an older brother to watch."

From Julius, his gaze and the tone of his voice both dropping, came a sense of genuine concern for Joshua's well-being. Finding the feeling uncomfortably out of place, Subaru scratched his cheek and let the matter drop.
And once, for a moment, a gap opened up in the flow of conversation—a cough filled it. It drew everyone's attention to the man who'd kept silent throughout: Otto.

"Er, well, I think it's all well and good to catch up on old times like this, but since everyone's face is now accounted for, I'd like to suggest we make proper introductions. To each other."

"That's true. I've only heard about y'all secondhand—outside of Emilia-san and Natsuki-kun, this is our first meeting. I'd love to hear about the sharp-witted steward and the great spirit-chan."

"Hey now, picking up bad intel isn't like you, Anastasia-san."

"I can pretty much guess where you're getting the 'bad intel' from, but pointing it out feels kind of self-important, so ugh, honestly!"

Subaru stuck out his tongue and adorably ignored the "sharp-witted" steward's flustered fuss. Watching the exchange, Anastasia gave a small laugh and nodded toward Julius.

"Well then, allow me to introduce myself properly. My name is Julius Juukulius. I belong to the Royal Guard of the Kingdom of Lugunica, and am currently on leave from that post, serving as the First Knight to Lady Anastasia here. A pleasure to make your acquaintance."

At Julius's elegant bow, Otto nodded, looking somewhat overwhelmed. Receiving this, Julius continued, "And—"

"—the one seated here is none other than one of the candidates for the throne of the Kingdom of Lugunica, and the brilliant woman who runs the Hoshin Company based in the Kararagi City-States: Lady Anastasia Hoshin."

"I—I am unworthy—"

"Don't go prostrating yourself!"

"Huh!? Oh no, I got swept up in the atmosphere!"

In front of a thoroughly pleased Anastasia at Julius's introduction, Subaru smacked the back of Otto's head as he reflexively bowed it to the floor.

"Don't let titles get to you! Look here! Our Emilia-tan is just as much a royal candidate, and a fine one at that. She's not losing to Anastasia-san either!"

"That's right. I'm the same kind of candidate. I'll do my best."

"What is this, so cute. Haven't done an E-M-T in a while!"

"There's something seriously irritating about the fact that watching this actually calms me down..."

In the midst of their usual pointless back-and-forth, Otto wore an exasperated expression at finding his composure restored. After that, seeming to pull himself together, he turned back toward the rival camp.

"Thank you kindly. Belatedly, allow me to introduce myself. My name is Otto Suwen, and I currently serve as steward under Lady Emilia... yes, by some twist of fate, I'm serving as steward."

"Sounds like there were a lot of hard decisions along the way."

"I was originally supposed to be a traveling merchant, but somewhere along the line, through some series of events I don't fully understand myself, it turned into this..."

"Sounds rough. If somethin' comes up, feel free to lean on me, y'know."

There was a melancholy air to it, but it was also, subtly, a scene where a steward was in danger of being poached. Subaru held up a hand in a gesture refusing to hand Otto over, and Anastasia stuck out her tongue and backed off. After that, everyone's gaze turned to Beatrice.
Receiving that attention, she thrust out her thin chest proudly.

"Betty is the spirit Beatrice. Subaru's contracted spirit here, I suppose. As you can see, she's leagues apart in both rank and cuteness from your run-of-the-mill spirits, I suppose. Once you've understood your place, Betty requests some fine tea and sweets, I suppose."

"Keep your dignity up to the very end, would you."

Beatrice, whose demands never quite escaped mascot-character territory.
Subaru gave one of her ringlets a light tug to get her to sit back down, and stroked the head of the now-sulking Beatrice.

"Well, that's the situation—Beatrice is the spirit I've contracted with. Never thought I'd be the one with the job overlap between the two of us, kind of a bizarre joke."

"For my part, I always had confidence in your affinity with spirits. It's not as surprising as it might seem. Though I'll admit, I hadn't imagined it would go so far as forming a contract with a spirit as formidable as Beatrice-sama."

"Try not to get our Bea-ko too carried away with herself. She takes after me—when she gets excited, there's no telling what she'll blurt out."

"Mmu. I have a thing or two to say about being handled so carelessly, I suppose."

Yet for all that, Beatrice's expression as she was stroked was far from displeased.
And with that, the round of self-introductions was finally, at last, complete. Which meant it was time to cut straight to the matter at hand.

"I hate to press things along so hastily, it's not a comfortable feeling, but might I go ahead and confirm a few things?"

Whenever it came to keeping the conversation from wandering, the role of steering it naturally fell, on the Emilia Camp's side, to Otto. Responding to the strategist's question was Anastasia, fingers idly toying with her fox-fur stole.

"Sure, go ahead. Entertainin' guests is our job. Ask whatever you like."

"Then, if I may take you up on that. First, could you tell us the reason you invited us to Priestella this time?"

"No need to be so wary. We're not schemin' anything. It's been a year since the Royal Selection started, hasn't it? I just thought it might be good for us to set up a chance to catch up on how things've been goin', that's all."

At Anastasia's mild manner, he nearly nodded along without thinking. But the substance of it wasn't light enough a matter to let a raised eyebrow slide.
Especially not with an opponent who was a battle-hardened merchant of the highest order.

"For 'just catching up,' you dangled some pretty blatant bait in front of us, if you ask me. Well-prepared, or maybe I should say cautious, is more like it."

"Since we're goin' through the trouble of invitin' you, seems only right to offer somethin' worth the trip, don't it? I could've just picked out some souvenirs on our end, but figured you'd remember it better if we gave you somethin' you'd be happiest to get."

"...And how exactly did you find out what would make us happiest?"

"That'd be a trade secret, now wouldn't it. Ah-ah, Natsuki-kun, that's no good. Diggin' around so much into a girl's business. Not with those two sittin' right beside you."

Hiding her mouth behind her sleeve, Anastasia laughed as if teasing Subaru for pressing too hard. Subaru's throat caught and he backed off, and beside him he sensed Beatrice letting out a sigh. Her attitude seemed to say she was disappointed in him for getting so easily managed. It stung.

"It's not like we were hiding it, so I think it's understandable that word got around to someone."

Taking over Subaru's question, Emilia said it plainly, without any fuss.
Anastasia blinked and looked at Emilia. At that gaze, Emilia tilted her head.

"Actually, rather than feel like I got nothing out of asking, I think I should be glad Anastasia-san went to the trouble of telling us exactly where to find what we want."

"...An answer too kind to argue with. And besides, I don't think I've actually told you properly yet what it is you want, Emilia-san."

"But you're going to tell us properly now, aren't you? Thank you. I don't know what I can do for you, but I'll definitely repay the favor somehow."

"————"

At Emilia's smiling answer, Anastasia's eyes went wide and she fell speechless.
At Anastasia's astonishment, Julius beside her couldn't help but loosen his cheeks. Anastasia shot him a sharp glare for it.

"What's so funny, Julius."

"Nothing. Only, it's rare to see you so caught off guard, Lady Anastasia. And I find that unadorned true face of yours quite beautiful as well."

"Sayin' smooth things to wriggle out of it, as usual. ...Guess my guard's still a little too loose."

Recovering her usual composure at Julius's words, Anastasia shook her head. Then, pursing her lips at Emilia's puzzled expression, she said,

"Let me correct myself. Emilia-san, a year's passed, but the roots of you are exactly the same as before. That bein' the case, the people around you must have quite the hard time of it."

"Yes. I still fall short in so many ways, and I cause everyone a lot of trouble. I'm trying hard to catch up quickly, but..."

"Correctin' myself again. Your roots have gotten even softer than before. Makes it sound like I'm the mean one here."

Letting out a long breath, Anastasia then broke into a bright smile. Caught off guard by the sudden shift in attitude, it was Emilia's turn to widen her eyes, and having watched that unfold, Anastasia turned to look at Subaru and Otto.

"You two make sure to properly support her, alright? If things go too far in the too-soft direction, it leaves me with nothin' to compete against, and that's a real problem for me."

"I fully intend to make every effort, but my basic policy is spoiling her rotten with praise to help her grow!"

"Which means the burden falls basically entirely on me. Ha, I don't even know how this happened."

Subaru gave a thumbs up; Otto's eyes had a dull, worn-out look.
Watching the two of them's contrasting reactions, Anastasia straightened her stole.

"Well, that's fine. Doesn't seem like you don't understand the value of a debt owed, at least."

"A debt, is it. A debt's nice, isn't it. No inventory to hold onto, and it never expires."

"Right, right. And more than anything else—"

As Otto chimed in agreement with Anastasia's words, the two merchants exchanged a glance, and—

"—'and you don't have to put a price tag on it either.'"

—they said in unison.
Anastasia clapped her hands together, and Otto's shoulders slumped even further. It was a line he'd apparently heard somewhere before.
An awfully materialistic way of putting things, when it came to the value of a debt of gratitude.

"Well then, shall we move on to the story you've been waitin' for? What Emilia-san and the others want is... right, colorless magic ore. And it's gotta be the kind refined to the utmost purity, wasn't it."

"Yes, that's right. Could you tell us what leads you might have?"

On the road to Priestella, Emilia had carried with her a faint hesitation.
Part of it came from her own awareness that wanting the magic ore was, in the end, purely her own concern. That was precisely why bringing Subaru, Otto, and the others all the way out to Priestella had left her harboring a hard-to-articulate unease at dragging them into it.

And yet, even so, now that the possibility lay before her eyes at last, the light of expectation in her eyes was unmistakable and unwavering.
Once she obtained it, her family would return to her side. Or perhaps, for her, it was also a ritual necessary to begin a new version of herself.

To Emilia, holding her breath, Anastasia—after drawing it out just a little—finally told her.

"The one holding high-density magic ore is the Muse Company, here in this city. The one in charge is the man said to be heir to the Muse Company, the head of the associated company here in town. His name's Kiritaka Muse—a man whose heart's been stolen by a songstress."
