Arc 10 Chapter 35: "White Sheep"

With a thud, Subaru's head dropped to the floor.
Seeing the way his head fell, with no restraint at all, she thought instinctively: *This is bad.*

"――――"

She'd seen wounds and blood to the point of sickness, both at the clinic and on the battlefields she'd been sent to. So she knew. A person who'd suffered a truly bad wound couldn't even manage to protect themselves.
Like Subaru now, unable even to shield his head, left with no choice but to collapse to the floor.

"――――っ"

Blood slowly welled up from Subaru's right side, spreading as it flowed to soak the landing. Seeing blood always made her think the same thing — that it looked just like life itself, draining away.

"Hey, isn't this strange? Red and white make such a wonderful pairing, I could stare at it forever and swoon... but more importantly, you! Why did you go and do something like that? Could it be... jealousy? Which means — love!?"

It seemed a doubtful conclusion to draw from the same scene, but after remarking on the blood flowing from the fallen Subaru and the whiteness of the floor, Lycoris turned both her hands toward her — toward "White Sheep."
The one who had stabbed Subaru from behind, wearing an utterly cold expression — "White Sheep." As she wiped her blood-stained fingers with a handkerchief, she quietly returned Lycoris's gaze and said—

"――――!"

"That's right. It's love. In the end, that's what all our motives come down to, isn't it?"

"You admit it!? Then it really is! Natsuki was head over heels for Felix, and you couldn't bear it, so you stabbed him! Oh, how tragic! You, driven mad by jealousy, and Felix, weeping and screaming as he faces love head-on... which one should I root for!"

The temperature gap between "White Sheep's" response and Lycoris's was enormous. But Felix, taking neither side, was inwardly furious at the sheer absurdity of Lycoris's remark.
The idea that Felix was weeping and screaming — there were limits to how warped one's perspective could get.
He wasn't weeping and screaming. He was only—

"—Subaru-kun! No! Don't die! Come here... let me get to you!"

—only, as a fledgling healer, continuing to call out to someone on the verge of death.

"How tragic."

He shook his body, straining his arms, struggling desperately to reach Subaru. But his body, pinned to the wall, wouldn't budge an inch.
Meanwhile, Lycoris advanced to the center of the landing with the click of her shoes. Right between Felix and "White Sheep," she turned both hands toward the pair and said,

"On one side, someone chased down through blood and tears, even overcoming a 'detour.' On the other, someone who could only cling desperately to that same fleeing back, standing closest of all. And that sinful Natsuki leaves you both behind and departs... this is it!"

"—This being?"

"Love burns hotter the higher the obstacle, doesn't it? Age, gender — those are kindling too. But then, what's the greatest obstacle of all? It's se-pa-ra-tion by de-ath!"

"――――"

"Death do us part! That's the ultimate obstacle to love! Because once you're dead, that's the end! You can never touch the person you loved again. So what could possibly drive two people further apart than death? But flip that around — death is the drug that pushes the love between two people to its absolute limit! Death is an aphrodisiac!"

She was insane. Lycoris, speaking with rapturous eyes and a rapturous smile, was out of her mind.
Whether "White Sheep" agreed with even a fraction of that deranged theory of love was unclear. But she neither laughed at nor scorned Lycoris's ravings—

"—Mama, thank you always. I love you more than anyone in the world."

It was a remark with no context whatsoever — sudden, and utterly nonsensical.

At the very least, to Felix it seemed, just as with Subaru before he was stabbed, that this was some product of the mental imbalance "White Sheep" suffered because of the "poison" Lycoris scattered.
But—

"――――"

To Lycoris, whose face drained of blood and went pale in an instant, it landed as something entirely different.
At the sight of Lycoris like that, a change came over "White Sheep's" cold expression. A smile. She wore a sneer that didn't suit her features at all, and said,

"—I am the 'Beloved Child' Mama sent into the Six Tongues."

"—Ah"

"I infiltrated to grasp the full picture of the Six Tongues and strip our enemy bare. But things were about to come to blows between fellow Beloved Children, so I've revealed myself. Consider this a gift I brought along. He turned out to be surprisingly tough, for someone acting so ordinary."

"This," she said, nudging Subaru's body where it lay collapsed in the pool of blood with the tip of her toe. At that callous gesture from "White Sheep," Lycoris, who had gone rigid, slowly let out a long, deep breath.
It wasn't the breath of the woman who'd been seeing a nightmare until now, but one of profound, profound relief.

"Ohh, ohh, I see. You're a 'Beloved Child' too... not Mama herself, right? Oh, thank goodness. You know how Mama does that sometimes? Wanting to understand us for real, so — there was a time when everyone I met in a single day turned out to be Mama! Just remembering it makes my heart feel like it's freezing over. ...You're not Mama, are you?"

"No, I'm myself. But I understand how you feel. She's done things to me too that left me unable to swallow a bite of food, more than once."

"You too! We're the same, then... is that why you told me? Does that mean it's fate? You don't want to lose me? Me neither!"

At the feet of the two "Beloved Children" chatting amiably, the blood of a boy pitifully forsaken by love kept flowing, little by little but surely, whittling away at his life. With every passing second, the chance of catching that spilling life slipped further away.

"And yet, what are you two dawdling for... I could just—"

Hatred. Fury. Irritation. Anger welled up huge — at these "Beloved Children" who'd set this bloody hourglass running, at himself for being able to do nothing but watch, and — to put it more plainly — at Subaru too, for carelessly charging in here and getting himself neatly stabbed by a traitor.
These past two months, it had always been like this. He'd always, always been furious with Subaru, unable to help it.

—Why did Subaru leave Emilia's side?

Why, once again, had he left the one person he should stay closest to, reaching a hand out to save Crusch from her crisis instead? So that now, separated from both his liege and his companions, he lay in a pool of blood. It was completely backwards.

Natsuki Subaru's actions, his very existence, were nothing but Felix Argyle's own sin, given form.

If weakness meant something was taken from you, and you had no choice but to demand compensation for that fate from those around you, then making Subaru shoulder that burden — all of it was Felix's sin. And Felix's sin, without fail, was always paid for not by himself, but by someone other than himself.

"Don't be ridiculous..."

Once, Felix's very birth had shattered the Argyle family — the demi-human blood reverting in him, the beast ears that appeared, had made his father suspect his mother of infidelity.

Once, Felix's arrogance had made him overlook a chance to repay a beloved mentor. — The mentor, so deeply owed, who had affirmed this power of his, had that judgment doubted, and had their life taken by someone who couldn't bear the doubt any longer.

Once, Felix's negligence had failed to save a dear friend he'd loved from the bottom of his heart. — Praised as "the Blue," he'd let his liege's beloved slip away, his skill never matching his reputation.

Once, Felix's atonement had twice failed to reach his liege, more precious to him than life itself. — And even now, his own insufficiency was about to bring about a third betrayal of that same liege.

"That's just — this is no joke... this isn't funny at all...!"

He twisted his body. His arm wouldn't come free. He put his shoulder into it. His arm wouldn't tear loose. He kicked the wall with all his strength. His arm wouldn't move an inch. Nothing, not one thing, went the way he wanted. Just like Felix's whole life.
"—っ"

Anger with nowhere to aim itself rampaged inside his hatefully shallow chest. Even if his body could stay whole, his heart couldn't. In fact, had his heart ever once been whole? Always emotionally unstable, always this fragile heart shrieking and carrying on inside him.
Being like that, he thought, was why, just like every time before, not a single person—

"—Ah"

Once again, he would be punished. By ruining someone else's life besides his own. — Just as he cursed the fate entwined around Felix's very life and clenched his teeth, that was when it happened.

"――――"

His eyes met Subaru's black ones, Subaru lying collapsed in the pool of blood, face turned to the side.
Limbs sprawled limply, eyes unfocused, repeating thin, blood-flecked breaths — that face was, without question, that of a person on the verge of death, one Felix had seen many, many times before.

But there was one point, just one point, that set him apart from those in death's grip, being called away.
Those being called away died with grief, terror, and regret residing in their eyes. But Subaru was different. What resided in those weak, unfocused black eyes was hope.

—The instant Felix understood what that hope was directed toward, he understood everything.

The terrifyingly foolish act of throwing himself on someone else's mercy, struck by a weak, fragile, powerless Natsuki Subaru.

"Don't be ridiculous...!"

What spilled from him next was different from the aimless fury of a moment ago. It was a crystallization of anger, dark and roiling, curse-like, dwelling inside Felix, now aimed straight and true at the one it should be aimed at.

Even now, at this juncture, Natsuki Subaru believed in Felix Argyle. — This was a farce, staged on nothing but that hope, so foolish it defied belief.

But 'White Sheep'-chan actually has a past as one of the 'Beloved Children,' so you'd have common ground to talk about with them. And if you show up with me as a gift in hand, wouldn't that stand a better chance than a head-on fight?"

It must have been beyond her wildest expectation. "White Sheep" clapped both hands over her mouth, eyes going wide.

But over these past two months on the "Beloved Children" front, he'd seen time and again that the enemy shared less information than expected, each of them fighting hard within the scope of their assigned duties and authority to answer to Capella.
With Capella's dictatorship at the top, incomplete coordination among allies meant to enforce discipline, and a false loyalty bound tight by fear — Subaru figured there was room to exploit that.
Though this would require "White Sheep," the one directly involved, to be prepared to face that fear all over again.

"There's a chance of success... I think. Maybe, for a reason 'Grey Star'-san doesn't know about, it might go better than expected."

And to "White Sheep," bewildered by his grim expression, he said:

"—When we run into a 'Beloved Child,' stab me with that knife. If you go that far, surely that adds a whole lot of persuasive force, doesn't it?"

"This is the first time I've ever made a friend, and also the first time I've ever stabbed one!"

That, indeed, was a valiant declaration of friendship.
And with that, the "Natsuki Subaru Staged Murder Incident" was set into motion—

"Lycoris, are you here on Mama's orders?"

"No, Dandon told me to. No, actually, to be precise, Dandon said to wait. But Reborn ignored that... I bet Reborn just wants to get Dandon's attention!"

"There was apparently a report from an 'Executor.' I don't know the details of how he figured it out, but isn't that mysterious and wonderful too? Who is it his eyes are looking at... me?"

"Lycoris, who's here with you besides yourself?"

"Reborn and Tornel. Actually, Reborn was trying to bring only Tornel, but you can't overlook a rendezvous for two, can you? So I forced my way along. My 'Ring Finger' gets along with anyone... ah, don't go thinking I'm easy! I just want to hand out lots of love, and I want to see it being handed out too...!"

Taking Melty's hand herself, clinging to her as she poured it all out — what flowed endlessly from Lycoris's mouth was the untold circumstances of the "Beloved Children" who'd pushed into this castle.

Of course, some of it was surely the original Lycoris's own carelessness.

But more than that, what loosened her tongue was the ferocity of "People-Charmer" wielded on someone already trusted as an ally from the start — probably, cheating of any kind was flat-out impossible around her. You'd be found out instantly. Made to confess.
It was, without a doubt, proof that Melty Pristis really had once been one of the "Beloved Children."

"――――"

For a fleeting instant, Melty's gaze crossed with Subaru's where he lay collapsed on the ground.
Nodding, giving a thumbs-up, even so much as catching his eye — all nearly impossible, but he confirmed the certainty of her power. It was worth having taken on the burden of that "Breach."
Now, all that remained was—

"Lycoris, actually, there's one more 'Beloved Child' with me. But she's gotten lost inside the castle and can't link up with us, and it's troubling me."

"Eh! You mean, she can't meet the person she wants to meet? What a tragedy... though I do think the longer two people keep missing each other, the bigger love grows..."

"—But that would trouble Mama."

"――――"

Whatever dramatic effect that had, Felix was sure he'd never in his life feel like welcoming the fear and terror that surfaced on Lycoris's profile in that moment.

"Tornel! Undo the 'Detour'! It's too pitiful, two people who can't meet!"

Unable to fully hide the tremor in her voice, Lycoris shouted, her voice ringing through the basement. — At that cue, once, with a click, a blue light spread across the underground.
It traced over Subaru, over Melty and Lycoris, over Ferris pinned to the wall, and further outward still, likely spreading across the entire castle.
And as that light faded, its afterglow receding, he understood instinctively.

"Yes! That should undo the 'Detour.' You should be able to properly meet the person you want to meet now. But if you don't mind, I'd love for the reunion to happen in front of me. It'll be so exciting!"

As if to confirm that instinct, Lycoris, rubbing her cheek against Melty's hand, declared it so.

And with that, one objective of the "Natsuki Subaru Staged Murder Incident" was cleared. If the "Detour" was lifted, then this meant everyone who'd been separated, everyone, would be safe—ee.

"—Don't you dare die, you idiot!! What were you thinking, don't be ridiculous!!"

—In that instant, the scruff of his dying consciousness was seized, and he was wrenched forcibly back into reality.

"—Ah"

"Idiot! You were about to die just now! I can't believe it, I can't believe it, I can't believe it! Why would you do something so stupid! Why...!"

He could hear a tearful voice. Lamenting, crying out, asking why, how could you.
There was no way he could let her go on crying like that. For that, he held his life fast, right at the very edge.

"――――"


—In place of an answer, at the edge of his vision, countless slashes tore across the ceiling, and from beyond the white canopy cut open, a shadow leapt down into the basement. — Silently.
