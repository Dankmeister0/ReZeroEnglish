Arc 4 Chapter 21: "Resolve Renewed"

When he opened his eyes, the first thing Subaru noticed was the sensation of something dusty in his mouth.

Along with the saliva that had pooled inside his mouth, he unconsciously tasted its shape with the tip of his tongue—and the instant he registered the smell of earth and the grit of gravel, he spat it out with all his might. Then, springing upright, he bolted himself into a sitting position.

"Ughh! Ptooey, ptooey! There's some weird rock in my mouth… blegh."

Gagging, brushing the dust from his body, Subaru squinted into the dim light and turned his head around.
The space, cut off from any light source, was filled with a chill, cold air—he remembered that this was the inside of the tomb that selected who could enter. And at the same time,

"Right, I took the 'Trial'…"

Rushing into the tomb, his consciousness had suddenly been seized, and he'd been pulled into a world of dreams. There, under the guise of a "Trial," he had been given the past—though whether it was truly correct to call it the past, in this case, he wasn't sure—an inescapable bond he could never wipe away, and a chance to part ways with what he had left behind. And then, after it was revealed that all of it had been resting in the Witch's palm, he had been sent back.

Counting off on his fingers what had happened while he was unconscious, Subaru confirmed that his memory of it was vivid. In the dream, he had told his father and mother—the two he could never see again—his apologies, his gratitude, and his farewell.
It had given him a strong sense of nostalgia and sorrow, and in their place, resolve and courage.

"It's okay. I haven't forgotten. Everything I told them—I remember all of it."

Given that the worst-case scenario would have been having his memories peered into for nothing, it was a relief that he could remember the whole of the "Trial" like this.
And then, having finished checking on his own body, Subaru's awareness turned to—

"Right…! In the first place, I came here for Emilia!"

Chiding himself for being slow on the uptake, Subaru looked toward the back of the room—and there, just like him, he found Emilia lying on her side.
He rushed over, and in the darkness her silver hair and pale skin still stood out; seeing the breath of life in them brought him a moment's relief. But that lasted only until he saw her expression.

"…! …ah, no… stop…"

"――――"

Her face was twisted in agony, sweat beading on her brow, as she writhed in torment.
But contrary to her suffering, she didn't seem to have been granted the freedom to move her body—her limbs lay rigid and still, and only her expression carried the deep color of her pain.
If she, too, was undergoing the same kind of "Trial" as Subaru, then—

"A past she doesn't want to see… no, does it mean she's facing something she has to settle…?"

He didn't know how much time had passed, but she had entered the tomb more than thirty minutes before him. And yet Subaru had returned first, which meant her "Trial" was proving extremely difficult.
Her pained groaning, that expression, spoke to it plainly.
Normally, he should trust her and keep waiting until the "Trial" ended, but—

"If I could say something that cool while looking at that face, I wouldn't be having such a hard time."

Subaru reached his fingers toward Emilia's profile, which looked ready to burst into tears at any moment, wanting to ease even a fragment of her agony. But the instant his fingertips touched her cheek,

"――――!"

Emilia's rigid limbs convulsed violently. Her face, twisted in pain, went taut, and Subaru, startled, instinctively used the hand that had been on her cheek to support her head instead. Pulling her still-trembling body close, he held her against his chest.

"Emilia!? Hey, stay with me… Emilia!"

Rubbing her back as he held her close, he desperately called her trembling name.
The violence of her convulsions filled him with a heart-shriveling fear, but gradually the trembling of her body slowed and began to settle.

"…Su, baru?"

"—! Ah, yeah, that's right. Are you okay? Can you tell it's really me? Natsuki Subaru, the guy who swore his future to you."

"I haven't agreed to go that far…"

He probed her waking memory, checking that her consciousness and recollection weren't clouded. As he pulled back, her violet eyes slowly brought Subaru into focus.

"Um… huh? Why am I…"

"Take it slow, Emilia-tan. Save the complicated stuff for later—right now, just take a big deep breath. Then try moving your hands and feet to see if they're numb, and if you can stand, let's try standing up."

"Ah, o-okay…"

With the unsteady tone of someone freshly woken, Emilia followed Subaru's instructions and took a deep breath. Then she moved her arms from fingertips to shoulders, and, borrowing the hand of Subaru, who had stood up first, she rose to her feet. In the dimness, she looked around, puzzled.

"In a dark place… alone with Subaru…"

"Cut off right there, that sounds like a pretty steamy situation, but given where we are, it's way too sacrilegious for that."

Scratching his cheek with a finger, Subaru gave a wry smile at Emilia, who was hurrying to grasp the situation. After all, this place was the Witch's tomb. It was sorely lacking in both mood and consideration as a spot for a man and woman to sneak off to.
But hearing Subaru's words, Emilia suddenly hugged her own shoulders. Startled by her reaction, Subaru thought with a shudder, *Did I get too carried away…!*—but then,

"Right… I took the 'Trial,' and then…"

"Mm, yeah, that's right. This is inside the Witch's tomb. Man, I was really thrown. Not long after you went in, Emilia-tan, the tomb's lights suddenly went out. So I panicked and chased after you inside, too…"

"Ah… no… that's not… I, that's not… that's not what I meant to…"

"Emilia?"

*Well, whatever the case, it's a relief there's nothing wrong with her body*—Subaru had been about to continue when he finally noticed the change in Emilia's demeanor, her voice still trembling.
She stood there hugging her own shoulders, her teeth chattering faintly as if she were cold, shaking her head from side to side.

"I… it wasn't me, it's not… I didn't… do anything like that… I didn't do it… even though I keep saying it's not true…"

"Emilia. Hold on, Emilia? Calm down, what's—"

"…No. Don't look at me… like that… no, no, no… no, that's not it… it's not me… why… why are you leaving me alone…"

Subaru's calls passed right by her ears, and Emilia covered her face with her hands and collapsed to the floor. Tears mixed into her voice, and the silver bell of her sobbing was so full of sorrow it ached to hear.
At the sight of Emilia crumpled on the floor, Subaru was struck speechless with shock. All he could do was—

"It's okay. It's okay. I'm here. I've got you. I won't leave you alone. It's okay."

Over and over, as if to comfort her, protect her, cherish her, he wrapped his whole body around her trembling, weeping form and kept gently stroking her back.
All the while, Emilia kept her face hidden behind her hands as if she couldn't hear Subaru's voice at all, and instead called out,

"…help me, Father. Please, help… Puck, Puck… Puck…"

Not to the man right beside her who worried about her more than anyone, but to the Spirit who still would not show himself before her as she wept and broke down—it was his name she kept calling.


※※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※


"—She's calmed down now and resting."

Ram said this with the look one might give an ill-mannered dog, aimed at Subaru, who had turned a questioning gaze on her as she emerged from the room.
Unable to muster any comeback to her thoroughly contemptuous look, Subaru only murmured "I see" in a small voice. At his attitude, Ram let out a small sigh.

"That's not like you, Barusu. Your face is slack enough as it is normally, but if you go and cast a shadow over it too, it'll be even harder to look at."

"'Slack as it is'—mind your own business… Sorry, for making you worry about me."

"…Since when did someone like Barusu get perceptive enough to notice when someone's being considerate?"

Genuinely surprised, it seemed. Subaru stuck out his tongue at her and set aside his earlier gratitude. The first half of what she'd said was probably meant to spur on Subaru, who'd been looking down, but the latter half was undoubtedly just her being blunt.
Averting his eyes from Ram, Subaru's gaze turned toward the door behind her—the one she had just come out of. Beyond it, Emilia should be sleeping now.

"Anyway, sorry for this happening two days in a row. Ryuzu-san must be pretty put out too, huh?"

"If it's about being put out, there's no need to worry about that. To begin with, it's our own selfish wish that has you taking on the 'Trials' in the first place."

Turning around, Ryuzu answered Subaru's remark in a flat voice. This room, connected to the bedroom, might be called a living room, and Ryuzu's house consisted of these two rooms plus a third, a library where books were kept.
For someone in a position like a village chief in a place called the "Sanctuary," she lived a remarkably plain life, Subaru thought. Though, for a woman living alone who sipped tea from a small cup in the corner of the room, this much space was probably plenty.
In any case,

"Ha, ain't you bein' awfully considerate, hag. To be honest, from where I'm standin' it's more like 'homeless on account of Gaunzun's assumptions,' if you ask me."

"I don't quite get what feeling that's supposed to convey, but… I get that it's not a good one."

Seated across from Ryuzu, likewise sipping from a cup, Garfiel said this baring his teeth. Another idiom that made no sense to him—but Subaru guessed it probably meant something like being let down. Then, sensing Garfiel's intent in bringing it up,

"Just so you know, if you're talking trash about Emilia-tan or anything like that, I'll take you on. Go through me first, your manager."

"Ain't got a shred of intent to talk behind anyone's back. Ain't got that shitty a personality. If I've got a complaint, I'll throw it right in your face. With a fist thrown in for good measure, if ya want."

Waving the hand not holding his cup, Garfiel brushed off Subaru's provocation with a ferocious grin. As Subaru kept his guard up against him, the man who had been silent until then—Otto—raised a hand and said "Um—"

"So, is it alright if I ask what actually happened? Honestly, I don't intend to pry too deeply, but since things seem tense, I'll volunteer to moderate."

"Oh, my bad. Yeah, you're the right man for it. You're the one here with no deep ties to anyone, and you're not tangled up in the important stuff, so you're a light little bit player who doesn't have to shoulder any of the responsibility. I'll leave it to you."

"You just drilled a hole clean through any willingness I had to accept that role, and then handed it to me anyway!?"

Otto shouted at Subaru's remark, but at Subaru's gesture—finger to his lips, "quiet"—he hastily shut his mouth. Still, unable to fully accept it, he shook his head and said,

"Ahh, I'm not exactly satisfied, but anyway, let's hear it. First, Natsuki-san, I'd like to hear what happened inside."

"What happened, huh…"

Being the first one put on the spot, Subaru set a hand to his chin and looked up at the ceiling.
What came to mind was what had happened inside the tomb—the "Trial," and Emilia's changed state after returning. Her tearful sobbing, calling out apologies and the Spirit's name like a delirium.

"Inside the tomb, the 'Trial' was definitely happening. I chased after Emilia-tan inside and got put through the same thing. Anyway, I got through my 'Trial' okay, but it sounds like Emilia-tan was struggling. She looked so much in pain that I ended up calling out to her without thinking… and that's when she woke up, and once she was fully conscious, that's the state she was in."

"No, no, no, no, no, no, no, no, no, hold on a second."

Otto thrust a palm toward Subaru, who had answered the question rapid-fire, and pressed in with a flustered look. Wondering what was wrong, Subaru looked up, and Otto repeated "no, no, no, no" again.

"There's a part there I almost nodded right past without questioning it… Wait, what? Natsuki-san, you took the 'Trial' too?"

"Eh, ah, yeah, I took it. My friend signed me up without asking, so I had no choice."

"There's no way someone like you has friends, Natsuki-san. Please, be serious."

"There are things you should and shouldn't say, you know!"

*If you're gonna say that, it's war,* Subaru thought, glaring at Otto in front of him. But a hand slid in between the two of them, and Ram, having interposed herself, looked up at Subaru.

"So, Barusu took the 'Trial.' Is that correct, without a doubt?"

"Ah, yeah. Without a doubt. Once I went inside, I got dragged in by force. It wasn't the kind of thing you could refuse."

"However it started doesn't matter. The problem is that Barusu managed to get through the 'Trial.'"

Ram touched a finger to her lips, closing her eyes as if in thought. Then she turned to look at Ryuzu.

"According to this errand boy of the household, that's what happened—did you sense any change? If the 'Trial' really has been completed, then the ban on the 'Sanctuary' should be lifted."

"…No, there doesn't seem to be anything special I can feel in my body. Though it might be a different story if we actually tried going outside the 'Sanctuary.'"

"I see. Then this settles it quickly. Come with Ram. Let's test whether we can pass through the 'Sanctuary,' and if we can get through, then—"

"Whoa, whoa, whoa, hold up, that's way too quick. And don't jump to conclusions. Some of that's on me for not explaining enough, but you're being way too quick to decide."

Grabbing the shoulder of Ram, who looked ready to drag Ryuzu off that very instant, Subaru turned the pink-haired girl around and threw his words at her. Ram furrowed her brow in displeasure at his words, but quickly recomposed herself and said, "What do you mean?"

"If the 'Trial' really was completed successfully, then it's necessary to confirm whether the residents have been freed in accordance with the Covenant. If what Barusu says is true, the villagers of Arlam Village could return to the village as early as tomorrow, and Roswaal-sama's recuperation could move back to the mansion—"

"Your true feelings are showing through in the second half there—I can see how desperate you are. …And sorry to disappoint, but you can't get out of the 'Sanctuary.' The 'Trial' isn't over yet."

At those words, Ram's eyes widened slightly. Then, as if mulling over the meaning of Subaru's words, she looked away, and once she seemed to have reached some conclusion, she nodded.

"You lied. Die."

"That's way too fast a conclusion and sentence!!"

Ram, already drawing her wand and moving into punishment stance. Subaru threw up both hands in surrender and shook his head desperately.

"And I wasn't even lying! It's true I got through the first 'Trial'! But there are still two 'Trials' left! There are three 'Trials' total in the tomb. So, sorry, but Ryuzu-san and the others aren't getting freed right now."

"That's enough nonsense out of your mouth… How would you even know that?"

"Well, the one who came up with this 'Trial'—"

*The Witch,* he started to say, but Subaru suddenly noticed a chill run through his entire body.
The chill that assaulted him brought a leaden heaviness to the tips of his hands and feet, a sluggishness to his thoughts as if mud had been poured into his head, and a coldness as if ice water had been injected into his veins.
And at the same time, what rose to the surface of Subaru's mind was an absurdly blank space.

He remembered the content of the "Trial," and that two more "Trials" awaited him, and even who had told him about it. The Witch. And yet—

"What she was like—it's just not coming to me at all…"

Touching his temple, Subaru was stunned to realize the gap in his own memory.
He remembered the content of the conversation he'd had with his father and mother. The heat of the tears he'd shed, the warmth of the words spoken to him at their parting—he remembered all of it.
But only the substance of the Witch involved in all of that had vanished, hollow, from his memory.

At the sight of Subaru falling silent, dumbfounded, Ram—who until then had shown no reluctance to resort to violence—stood down from her combat stance. She slid the wand she'd drawn back into her waist, glanced at the still-silent Subaru, and let out a breath. At that,

"Ahh? Just when it was gettin' interestin', you're not gonna do it? I was hopin' to see Ram cut loose for once, first time in a good while."

"There's no way a demure, delicate girl like Ram would do something so barbaric. Besides, I've more or less figured it out from Barusu's current attitude, so there's no need."

"Figured it out—figured what out?"

Neither Garfiel's flippant remark nor Ram's brushing it off quite registered in Subaru's mind. But at Subaru's question, Ram tilted her head slightly.

"That Barusu isn't lying. That much trust from me should be enough for now. Barusu, continue answering Otto's questions."

"Ah, yeah… fine."

Somewhat unconvinced, but reluctantly nodding at the force of Ram's tone, Subaru complied. Otto, who had been watching this exchange, cleared his throat and, prefacing with "Well then," said,

"We got a bit sidetracked, but let's go back to the topic properly. I'll save the deep dive into the content of the 'Trial' for later… As for the matter of Emilia-sama losing her composure, do you have any idea what might have caused it, Natsuki-san?"

"…I do. I think it was probably the content of the 'Trial' that was the issue. The details of the 'Trial' Emilia-tan and I each took were probably different, but I think the broad strokes were the same."

"The content, huh… Is that something you're okay talking about?"

Otto's consideration was directed at him, but Subaru waved it off with a palm. Nodding as Ram and Garfiel watched him with probing eyes,

"The first 'Trial' was about facing the past. Basically, come face to face with your regrets and lingering attachments and settle things—that's the gist of it."

"I—I see… so that's why the details would differ."

Of course, each person's past differs.
For some people, this "Trial" probably wasn't all that difficult. It just happened to strike Subaru dead-on, and it just happened to land a critical hit on Emilia—

"No, when I think about the qualification thing too, I feel like there's a nasty streak baked into the 'Trial' itself."

After all, the qualification Subaru had been specially granted was originally something that should only be attainable by a "Half." He couldn't see all the way into the true intentions of whoever had prepared the "Trial," but in stories, there's a certain fixed pattern awaiting a being burdened with the condition of being a "Half."
It means persecution from one's own kind and from other races alike, or being feared—isolation, in other words. If the "Trial" was specifically selecting "Halfs," who tend to fall into such circumstances, and putting them through it, then it stood to reason that those who challenged the tomb were, as a matter of course, more likely to carry wounds from the past.

"Which means, inevitably, it's a lineup of people who'll have a hard time getting through the 'Trial.' Pretty black-hearted, if you ask me."

"Cursing out the examiner's personality isn't going to get us anywhere right now. More than that… it's hard to say, but when it comes to the circumstances of Emilia-sama losing her composure—"

Otto trailed off. His glances toward the bedroom where Emilia lay conveyed what he meant to say all on their own, and yet Subaru felt somewhat saved by his consideration in not putting it into words.

—Emilia's appearance. Her looks, bearing many similarities to the "Witch of Envy," and her origin as a half-elf. From these points alone, one could sense the existence of the baseless contempt and persecution she must have endured.

Still, all that Subaru and the others, who weren't actually in her position, could grasp was, at best, a surface-level guess at how it probably was.
For that reason, it wasn't something to bring up lightly. Otto's judgment was intensely humane—or put another way, it was a personality fatally unsuited to being a merchant.

"You'll definitely never make it big as a merchant, but—thanks."

"Why are you suddenly crushing a man's dreams!?"

"You're the shy type who can't say thanks straight without mixing in some banter—read the room."

"You read the depths of the wound you just left in my heart a little, would you!!"

As Otto raised his voice and looked about to stomp his feet on the floor, everyone in the room put a finger to their lips in a "quiet" gesture. Otto, seeing this, hastily clapped a hand over his own mouth—but it was too late.

At first, it was just a small sound.
With the faint hush that had fallen over the house after Otto went quiet, that small sound striking the floor rang out unusually loud. Every gaze turned toward the source of the sound—that is, toward the bedroom.
And then, before anyone could speak, the door slowly opened.

"That… I'm sorry, for causing you all trouble."

So she said, shrinking apologetically, as Emilia showed her face, her silver hair flowing down her back.
Relieved to see, from that first line, that no trace of her earlier breakdown lingered, Subaru was quick to trot over to her.

"Good, morning. Emilia-tan, are you feeling okay now?"

"Ah. Yeah, I'm fine. My body's completely fine. Sorry for worrying you."

"Good, that's a relief then. See, when you collapsed the first time, I wasn't with you, so I didn't know if you'd hit yourself somewhere, and I was worried. Guess we really should stick together without ever separating, for both our peace of mind."

"—Yeah, that's true."

"Hm?"

Braced for some retort to his banter, Subaru frowned slightly at Emilia's unexpected reaction. She kept her eyes lowered, staring fixedly at Subaru's hand. Tilting his head, wondering what was wrong, Subaru held that hand out to her.

"What's up? Missing the feel of my palm already? I could hold your hand all night if you want."

"Eh, ah… i-it's fine. It's not that. I was just, um, still half-asleep."

Right before her fingers touched Subaru's outstretched palm, Emilia shook her head as if reconsidering something. The next to speak to her was the maid who stepped forward.

"Emilia-sama. First of all, it's a great relief to see you've woken up safely. If possible, Ram would like to hear how you're feeling, without you putting on a brave face."

"Hey. The way you say that makes it sound like Emilia-tan would hesitate to be honest with me."

"Between women, or in front of someone she wants to look good for, there are things that aren't so easy to say. Show a little tact and leave the room, won't you?"

"I'd love nothing more than to show that tact, but…"

Under Ram's sharp gaze, Subaru dropped his eyes and let his words trail off. Ram, frowning suspiciously at that, followed his gaze, and her expression shifted to one of understanding.
What was reflected in her eyes was Subaru's hand, hanging limply—and Emilia's pale, white fingers, timidly gripping it.

"Eh, ah!"

Belatedly noticing the meaningful glance exchanged between Subaru and Ram, Emilia hastily let go of his hand. Her cheeks reddened, and she panicked as if her own recent action had been unconscious.

"T-that's not— huh, that's strange. I didn't mean to do that… because, I was making sure not to touch it just a moment ago."

"You definitely pulled it back, but then it ended up creeping right back over, you know? Since it worked out in my favor, I didn't say anything, but—Emilia-tan, are you really okay?"

At the question, asked with lingering reluctance over her withdrawn fingers, Emilia nodded firmly. But her cheeks remained flushed, and even Subaru could tell that it wasn't from embarrassment.
Yet Emilia, seeming not to notice this change in herself, said,

"Sorry for interrupting. But really, there's nothing wrong with how my body feels. My sleepy head's properly awake now after that, so I'm totally fine now."

"'Totally fine'—don't hear that phrase much these days…"

"Mmph, there you go again, Subaru."

At his usual remark, Subaru teased her, and Emilia puffed out her cheeks in a sulk. Sensing her usual self in that gesture, Subaru relaxed his guard, thinking it might have just been his imagination after all. But—

"Emilia-sama. It's right after you've woken up, but—about the 'Trial.'"

"—!"

It probably wasn't timed to Emilia's recovery, but at Ram's businesslike words, Emilia's profile stiffened for just an instant. Subaru, who had been watching her closely, caught that momentary change, but it was instantly hidden away beneath a smile.

"Right… um, about the content of the 'Trial,' does everyone—"

"That, we've heard from Barusu. Of course, we haven't gone into the fine details. There must be things Emilia-sama doesn't want asked about, too."

"O-oh, I see. Subaru… eh? Why did Subaru… I mean, Subaru's not a Half, so there's no way he could take the 'Trial'…"

Emilia's astonished eyes turned to Subaru, and the surrounding gazes, sharing the same question, converged on him as well. *Fair question,* Subaru thought, receiving those looks, wondering how to answer.

"I told you before going in, right? I got the qualification. Who from is a bit iffy, but where—probably, yesterday evening, at the tomb."

"Evenin', huh—that's when you collapsed and got hauled back here, ain't it."

"Yeah, that's right, no mistake. I don't have solid proof of why I got it, but… maybe it's this: even someone without the qualification can get in if they push their way inside and go through whatever that baptism thing is. Roswaal might actually be able to get in too, who knows."

"That'd be hilarious if we tried it and Roswaal got bounced right back out. We'd get to confirm 'even droplets of Prinpa are the same color of blood,' or however that saying goes."

Garfiel laughed with his mouth wide open. But at Ram's chilly glare, his face turned sour and he clammed up right away. Then, still not quite satisfied with Subaru's answer, she said,

"In any case, it's a fact that Barusu went inside and brought Emilia-sama back out. And in the process, Barusu also took the same type of 'Trial' as Emilia-sama, and—if it's not delusional talk—claims to have gotten through it."

"'Delusional talk'—harsh, hey."

"Got through it… Subaru, the 'Trial'?"

Subaru shot back at Ram's merciless words, but Emilia's ears hadn't caught that latter exchange at all. Staring at Subaru with trembling eyes, she said,

"Did you really get through it, Subaru. Um… the past?"

"What you and I were looking at were probably different, though. I mean… I didn't get through it on my own strength, anyway."

After all, he'd been helped through it by his father and mother—the very people who should have been the ones standing in his way—who had lent him their utmost support. More than anything, even before challenging it, the answer had, in effect, already been given to Subaru within himself.
It was unfair to Emilia, but from the stage before even taking on the "Trial," their situations had already been different.

"It's not something worth making a fuss over just because the test results happened to go well for me. More than that, the problem is Emilia-tan. From how you looked, I'm guessing today's 'Trial' didn't go well…"

"Y-yeah. That's right… I tried hard, but it suddenly cut off partway through."

"That's probably because I woke you up, so I'm sorry about that. …Actually, wait, is retaking the 'Trial' even a thing that's possible in the first place? I haven't taken the second 'Trial' either, and I came back."

Listening to Emilia's halting words, Subaru turned back to the question that had arisen and put it to Ryuzu. The old woman with the child's appearance, who had been silently watching the discussion until then, touched her cheek.

"It's not something with much precedent, but… challenging it again, that itself should be possible any number of times. I myself never managed to get past the first 'Trial,' but I've attempted it twice. More than that, what concerns me is Su-bo, who gained the qualification."

"Me?"

"For a qualification to sprout up suddenly like that just doesn't happen, not normally. At least, not as far as I know, and I've been here since the tomb was first made… But, well, I have a rough idea."

With that, Ryuzu fell silent again. Subaru felt a twinge of unease at her words and manner, but for now he set it aside and turned back to face Emilia.

"Anyway, we've got official confirmation that a retry is possible. The remaining problem is Emilia-tan herself."

"M-me…?"

"Yeah, that's right. So let me ask—Emilia-tan, do you think you can steel yourself to take on the 'Trial' again?"

"――――!"

At that question, Emilia's throat seemed to catch, and her eyes went wide.
If it had been anger at having her resolve doubted, or something steeped in humiliation, Subaru would have been ready to accept being cursed at, ready to take the mud on himself.
But what dwelled in her trembling, violet eyes was an all too fragile anxiety and fear.
That she couldn't answer immediately meant that negative emotions were eating away at her heart to that degree, and so—

"If you can't take the 'Trial,' then I'll take it in your place."

"—!? But Subaru, that would—"

"At the very least, I've cleared the first 'Trial.' I should be proof enough that the remaining two 'Trials' aren't impossible to get through either. And if, even so, you're hesitant to take the 'Trial,' then I'll go ahead and do it, no shame about it. That's what I'm here for."

"What you're here for… for m-my sake…?"

"That's right."

To Emilia, who almost seemed to want to be told otherwise, Subaru stated it plainly.
Watching her eyes widen further with mounting emotion, Subaru looked straight into her twin eyes and said,

"For your sake, if it's something you're afraid of, I'll do it. Roswaal and people like him might say that freeing the 'Sanctuary' has to be something Emilia-tan pulls off herself, has to be your own achievement… but whatever gets praised as a result of my actions, all of it, I'll give to you. Nothing needs to be left in my hands."

"Why would you go so far… for me…"

"I already told you. Because I love you. Because I love you so, so much."

Emilia caught her breath, and the others in the room each reacted in their own way.
Not even registering their reactions, Subaru kept his eyes on the shaken Emilia and shrugged.

"So anyway, I'm thinking of challenging the 'Trial.' What about you, Emilia-tan? If it's really too hard, you can just stay home and sleep, you know?"

"――――Subaru, you idiot."

Before Subaru, the corner of his mouth curling up, Emilia's lips let those words slip quietly.
Then she lifted her once-lowered face, rubbed hard at her eyes with her sleeve, and let her lips bloom into a smile.

"There's no way I could be told something like that and just sit around waiting, shut up in my room. Really… you're so mean. So very stupid. So very… thank you."

"Eh? What? Didn't catch that last part. Sooo very much in love with me?"

"Not even close! I said thank you so, so much…"

"I see. I heard you, but say it again!"

"Subaru, you idiot!!"

To Subaru, leaning in and pushing his luck, Emilia shouted it out loud.
Even a voice like a silver bell, when slammed into an eardrum at full volume, is a decent acoustic weapon. Nearly reeling, Subaru grinned at Emilia, whose shoulders were heaving up and down.

"That's the spirit. Alright, let's give it our all again. I'm starting from Stage 2, and Emilia-tan's starting from Stage 1, though."

"Hmph. I'll catch up in no time, and pass you right by, and leave you in the dust for sure. I'm not leaving even one achievement of Subaru's standing."

"If there's not at least one left, I won't have a chance to beg Emilia-tan for a reward, though."

At Subaru's incorrigible remark, Emilia pursed her lips and stuck out her tongue.
Going through this exchange, Subaru wondered whether he'd been able to help her get back on her feet, even a little.
Either way, the real "Trial" would surely start from here.

"With me and Emilia-tan's lovey-dovey power combined, we'll twist the 'Trial' right up, easy peasy."

Snapping his fingers, flashing his teeth, giving a thumbs up, Subaru declared his resolve.
At Subaru's words, Emilia simply stuck out her tongue and said,

"I'm plenty enough on my own. I'll prove it by tomorrow—that I'm fine without Subaru babysitting me."

—and put up that brave front.


※※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※


Watching Subaru and Emilia rile each other up into higher spirits like a couple of playful kids, Ryuzu brought her cup of tea, already gone cold, to her lips.

Wetting the tip of her tongue with the faintly lukewarm liquid, she watched the exchange between the silver-haired half-elf and the black-haired boy with quiet amusement.

"I wonder how this'll turn out. …Whether it'll all go just as the Witch-sama intends."

"Keh. Hell if I know. However it shakes out, it's just gonna leave a rotten taste in my mouth, is all."

Catching that murmur, Garfiel said it in a voice that didn't hide his displeasure.
Glancing at the young man's profile, Ryuzu let out a small breath that no one would notice, and said,

"Forcing a burden onto children who are nothing but kind. On and on, endlessly—we, this useless old guard, are beyond saving. —If nothing else, at the very end, I'd rather not be that way."

Those words, murmured over her cup of tea, reached no one; they only stirred ripples across the surface of the tea, the color of tea leaves, lukewarm—and then vanished away.
