Arc 8 Chapter 14: "Kingdom and Empire"

—Priscilla and Yorna had not returned.

The astonishing news, delivered from Emilia's own mouth, was clearly not something Subaru had misheard—no one else in the room raised any objection to it.

In the battle for the Imperial Capital of Lupugana, fought between the Empire's regular soldiers and the rebels who had risen against them, he could well imagine how large a role Priscilla and Yorna had played.

At the same time, Subaru clenched his fist tight at a sudden realization.

"So that's why Tanza looked so gloomy..."

When Subaru had woken up in his bed aboard the Coupled Dragon Carriages, members of the Battalion had come to visit him. Among them, Tanza had worn an unhappy expression the whole time, and it had bothered him.

He'd meant to discuss their future course with Emilia and the others, with Abel and his people, and once that had settled down, to properly ask Tanza what was wrong. But before he could actually act on that, the reason for Tanza's worry had already become clear.

"So we're leaving the Imperial Capital even though those two haven't come back!?"

"—If I began listing everyone who fought in that battle and has not returned, there would be no end to it. Certainly, that woman and Yorna Mishigure's presence carried considerable weight, but I cannot give them special treatment."

"But Yorna-san is—! And besides, you must have some kind of history with Priscilla too."

Looking out the window of the running dragon carriage at the scenery streaming past, thinking of the Imperial Capital falling further behind, Subaru pressed the point to Abel.

He hadn't heard the details. But when Priscilla had come rushing to their aid in the walled city at their moment of crisis, she had surely, quite literally, flown there to protect Abel.

And in their exchanges since, even amid the sharpness of blades turned against each other, there had seemed to be a certain ease between them, the kind shared by people who understood one another.

For that Priscilla to be left behind—surely even Abel couldn't be at peace with that.

Perhaps his agitation earlier, in Subaru's room, hadn't been solely because of the death of the man named Chisha, either.

"You dare speak to His Excellency with such insolence!"

But the one who objected to Subaru's attitude was not Abel himself, but the big man standing beside him.

Goz Ralfon, the scar-faced man clad in golden armor, bellowed with the volume of an erupting volcano as he glared down at the boy who had pressed in on the emperor to whom he'd sworn loyalty.

"Given the full details of the Imperial Capital's situation, His Excellency's abandonment of the city is only natural! Even the soldiers and common folk left behind surely understand the correctness of his judgment!"

"I'm not talking about whether it's right or wrong. What I'm trying to say is..."

"You—!"

"Enough, Goz Ralfon. That one is simply not built to hold his tongue."

Goz's face reddened at Subaru's continued persistence, but Abel raised a hand to stop him.

At Abel's calm tone, Goz closed his half-open mouth and bowed his head reverently. Subaru, meanwhile, looked at Abel with an awkward expression.

"Sorry for the ruckus. But I'm going to say the same thing anyway."

"It is as that fool Goz says. As things stand, remaining in the Imperial Capital is tantamount to throwing away one's life. I cannot make such a decision. Naturally, I am concerned for the safety of Priscilla and Yorna Mishigure, but..."

"Um, listen, Subaru, it's not all bad news. ...No, I mean, even within the bad news, there's real hope too."

The one who placed a hand on the shoulder of Subaru, who had stepped forward, was Emilia, her brows drooping with worry.

She, too, was just as deeply worried about Priscilla and the others' safety as Subaru was. If she said there was hope, then it was hope for him as well.

"Hope, like what?"

"Priscilla and Yorna-san are both really, really strong, but that's not all—they both have the power to affect the people around them, too."

"Affect the people around them... Right, yeah. Though I don't know about Priscilla's case."

Yorna, who ruled the Demon City of Chaosflame, boosted the strength of the entire city through an ability called the 'Soul Marriage Technique,' which linked her own soul to that of every resident.

The same principle had been applied to Tanza, and the incomparable power she displayed, despite looking like nothing more than an adorable deer girl, was something separate from the Pleiades Battalion's overall reinforcement.

It seemed to be a fairly powerful support effect—one that had persisted even on the Sword Slave Island, far away from Chaosflame, and in the battle for the Imperial Capital as well—

"...Don't tell me Tanza's boost still hasn't worn off?"

"Not just that, I suppose—the effect on everyone who joined from the Demon City is still ongoing as well."

"Which naturally means that Commander Yorna, the wielder of that secret technique, is still alive and well. That's the reason our worrywart Emilie isn't panicking."

"Right, that's why I'm not panicking. And it's the same for Priscilla too."

"Priscilla too? The same?"

Beatrice and Ram backed up Subaru's guess. Then came Emilia's remark that Yorna and Priscilla were the same, and Subaru's brow furrowed at it.

Taken at face value, that meant Priscilla too was a wielder of the same skill as Yorna.

"But that doesn't sound like an ability that suits Priscilla at all, and besides, Abel, you told me using the 'Soul Marriage Technique' was ridiculously difficult. Were you lying to me?"

"Fool. What would I gain by lying to you? I care nothing for what you think of me at this point, but why would I go out of my way to earn your resentment here? Use your head."

"If that's your idea of not earning my resentment, you're not cut out to be emperor."

It was the kind of behavior that would surely have provoked a coup in the near future even without this whole business.

Goz's face twisted with anger again at Subaru pointing this out, but Berstetz, standing beside him, smoothed things over before it could become a real incident.

"In any case, I understand your impression of Priscilla-sama, Natsuki-san, but she is indeed a wielder of this so-called 'Soul Marriage Technique.' I saw it myself, with my own eyes."

"Just as Otto-kun says, I confirmed it too. Though unlike Commander Yorna's outrageous, off-the-charts application of it, in her case it seems limited to just her favorite attendant."

"Her favorite—Schult, huh. ...Come to think of it, where's Al?"

With the talk turning to Priscilla's camp alongside her failure to return, Subaru asked about the man in the iron helmet whom he hadn't seen either here or anywhere aboard the Coupled Dragon Carriages.

Neither master nor servant would admit it, but Al's position was essentially that of Priscilla's knight.

He came across as flippant and easygoing, but his loyalty to Priscilla was genuine. Naturally, of anyone, Al would feel the most responsible for her failure to return.

"...That man stayed behind in the Imperial Capital instead of boarding the carriage, I suppose. Just as you guessed, Subaru, he's presumably out searching for Priscilla."

"That guy...! Don't tell me, alone!?"

"His opinion was that being alone gives him more mobility. Ram agrees with him there. It's not as if leaving a large group behind in the Imperial Capital in that state would have been a good idea either."

"Damn it, still..."

He'd gotten his answer about the absent Al, but it was a fact that carved a deep line between Subaru's brows.

As unassuming as he seemed, Al was remarkably quick-witted, and a man specialized in surviving well beyond what his actual strength would suggest. Even so, there was no denying his raw ability fell a notch short of a true powerhouse.

And yet despite being aware of that himself, Al had cooperated with Subaru wholeheartedly. In the Demon City, too, how many times would Subaru have lost his life if not for him?

"As for Priscilla-sama's camp: Priscilla-sama herself, who leads it, is missing. So are her attendant Al-dono and Heinkel-dono, who was traveling with them. Schult-kun is resting in his room."

"Heinkel... that name rings a really unpleasant bell..."

"The drunkard, I suppose."

"That explanation doesn't convey anything, I suppose. ...Reinhard's father, I believe."

"—! That damn old man! Why the hell is a guy like that in the Empire!?"

Eyes bulging, Subaru dredged up a memory that left a bad taste, from deep down in his mind.

Heinkel was the man who had shattered a moment of budding warmth back in the Watergate City of Priestella. Now that he thought of it, the man had seemingly been with Priscilla's group, but that he'd been in the Empire too came as a shock.

Self-centered and selfish as he was, Subaru would have thought he'd never willingly set foot in the Vollachian Empire.

"But Heinkel-san is missing too."

"One more who hasn't returned, same as Priscilla and the others... Damn it, I don't even know how I'm supposed to feel."

Of course, he didn't want the man dead, and he didn't think he deserved to die.

At the same time, the man wasn't someone Subaru could bring himself to like, so beyond not wanting to make Reinhard or Wilhelm grieve, he had no real reason to wish him alive.

Still, the fact that this had come as a result of the man taking part in that battle gave him something to think about.

"—"

Heinkel, a man of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica and father to the 'Sword Saint' Reinhard.

That such a man had been swept up in a war in the Vollachian Empire, and might well have lost his life as a result, left even Subaru—who was hardly close to him—with a bitter feeling.

No matter what, Subaru told himself, he had to return to the Kingdom together with all of his precious companions.

Just as Subaru was steeling his resolve on that point—

"The situation regarding Priscilla-sama and Yorna-san is as I've explained. With that said, before we begin discussing our future course, there's something I want to state clearly."

It was Otto who spoke up like that, in the room now filled with heavy silence.

Standing up where he was and raising a single index finger, he drew everyone's attention.

"As I'm sure you're all aware, we are people of the Kingdom. I acknowledge the fault of having crossed the border at a time we should not have, but I'll say this anyway."

"—Speak, then."

"Yes. —We have accomplished our objective. Once we reach the Fortress City of Garkla, we intend to head north from there, pass through the Kararagi City-States, and return home to the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica."

There, in the Vollachian Empire in the midst of an unprecedented crisis, before the Vollachian emperor facing the danger of his nation's ruin, Otto declared this plainly.

△▼△▼△▼△

"—Natsuki-san, Emilie, please stay quiet."

Said before Subaru and Emilia could get a word out, cutting them off before they'd even started.

Otto, without so much as glancing their way, had beaten them to the punch—Subaru and Emilia, eyes wide at his declaration a moment before, ready to protest furiously, found themselves neatly preempted.

But their reaction was only natural.

With the Imperial Capital struck by such a catastrophe, and Priscilla and Yorna's safety still unknown on top of it, making arrangements to go home now felt like far too much.

"You look like you want to say I have no heart, but Ram agrees with Otto's plan too. Ram has recovered Rem, and, incidentally, short-limbed Barusu as well. That's more than enough of a result."

"Ram...!"

"Giving me that longing look won't help, Emilie. You understand too, don't you? Ram and the others just happened to come away without losing anyone. Anything more than that would be far too dangerous."

In place of Subaru and the others, ordered to keep silent, it was Ram who spoke up, siding with Otto.

Even to Emilia's pleading gaze, Ram gave a cold answer, narrowing her eyes as she sat in her chair holding her own elbows in her usual pose.

They'd merely happened to come through without losing anyone.—Ram's assessment, phrased that way, was correct.

As a matter of fact, they hadn't been able to bring Priscilla or Yorna back with them here. And there was no guarantee anywhere that the next name on that list wouldn't belong to someone from their own camp.

"Dudley, do you share your wife's opinion as well?"

"She's not my wife, but yes, I'm largely of the same opinion as the two of them."

Alongside that exchange, the one who'd turned the topic to Roswaal was the beautiful woman with a conspicuous white scar on her face, the one introduced as Serena.

The 'wife' Serena referred to was presumably Ram. While denying that particular suspicion, Roswaal nonetheless indicated he shared the same view as Otto and the others.

"In truth, if we look only at our own objectives, this counts as a perfect success. His limbs have gotten a bit shorter, and the girl who was supposed to be asleep has turned into a tomboy, but overall this is an excellent result. Withdrawing to the Kingdom here is, I'd say, the judgment least likely to cost me anything."

"For a Barusu who hasn't even got all his teeth in yet, the loss can be called minimal."

"I've got all my teeth just fine...!"

Taking advantage of the fact that no one contradicted her, Ram tore into Subaru's diminished size without mercy. But Subaru, sensing this was a bad turn of the conversation, let his eyes dart around the room.

Otto, Ram, and Roswaal—these three were, within the Emilia Camp, the ones with the most practically sharp minds, and they carried heavy weight in any policy meeting.

Despite carrying the name "Emilia Camp," it wasn't unusual—if anything, it was more common than not—for the opinions of Emilia herself, or her knight Subaru, to be overruled.

"So Bea-ko's the only one on our side, huh..."

"...Sorry, Subaru, but if anything, Betty leans that way too, I suppose. Between you shrinking and everything else, getting any further involved with the Empire is too dangerous, I suppose."

"You've gotta be kidding, you too!?"

At his partner's unexpected betrayal, Subaru's eyes went wide with plain shock.

But though she looked apologetic, there was no sign of Beatrice about to say "just kidding, I suppose." She was saying this precisely because she genuinely worried about him.

In other words—

"Subaru..."

"...It's just me and Emilia-tan."

Emilia clenched her pale hands tight, her violet eyes wavering. At her call, Subaru swallowed hard, breaking into a cold sweat at just how badly outnumbered they were.

If only some of the others not present here had been in the room, things might have been different—

"Just so you know, Petra-chan agrees with us too. Frederica-san leans toward going home as well, if reluctantly... You can just take Garfiel with you."

"Just Emilia-tan, Garfiel, and me!? Give me a break!"

"Uu... somehow that sounds really, really unreliable..."

Their wishful thinking crushed by cold reality, the flimsiness of Subaru and Emilia's position was reinforced all the more.

If this were a meeting decided by fistfights, having Emilia and Garfiel on their side would give them an overwhelming advantage, but for any other kind of meeting, the matchup was thoroughly unfavorable.

"Enough! What is this farce!? I cannot bear to watch it any longer!"

It was Goz who snorted furiously at this exchange between Subaru and the others.

Having already objected to Subaru's attitude toward Abel earlier, to him every word and action from this camp must have felt like disrespect toward the emperor.

Striding forward, Goz pinched the back of Subaru's collar and hoisted him up. Genuinely lifted between two of the man's thick fingers, Subaru let out a startled "Whoa!"

"This is the Empire's problem! If you claim to be citizens of the Kingdom and not the Empire, then cross the border promptly, just as you've planned! We have no need of your strength!"

"W-wait a second! We haven't even settled things on our end yet..."

"Enough of your excuses—!!"

Legs dangling helplessly in the air, Subaru's head spun under the sheer volume of the shout that struck him like a blow.

Even with both hands clamped over his ears, Goz's voice pierced straight through that defense—it was practically a weapon at this point. Still, his argument was rooted in the pride of an Imperial soldier, and Subaru had no reason to submit to it.

And the same held true for every outsider besides Subaru.

"That's enough, Goz-san. First put Subaru down, and let's talk about this properly."

The one who stepped forward firmly, hand on her hip, was Emilia.

She looked up at Goz, who still held Subaru pinched between his fingers, and conveyed her resolute will to him. At her stance, Goz's thick brows knit, and he looked down at Emilia with his scarred face.

"I take it you share this boy's opinion as well! But! The Sword Wolves of the Empire accept no charity! Your assistance is unnecessary! Take your companions and promptly—"

"That! I have a really, really big objection to that!"

"What!?"

"You say you don't need our strength! But! I don't think that's true at all!"

"Getting caught up and raising her voice too, I see, I suppose."

Refusing to be outdone by Goz's momentum, Emilia's silver-bell voice kept growing louder and louder.

Her beauty undiminished but sounding now more like a bell tolling than a bell tinkling, Emilia's voice challenged him head-on, and Goz's face twisted.

Pointing a finger at Goz's twisted expression, Emilia continued.

"Because! Even in the battle for the Imperial Capital, if Priscilla and the rest of us hadn't been there, everyone would have had a much, much harder fight of it!"

"—!"

"We were tough opponents to deal with, weren't we, Berstetz-san!"

Saying this loudly, Emilia turned the point of the conversation toward Berstetz.

The old man with eyes narrow as threads showed no sign of being fazed by the sudden call-out, and toying with the whiskers around his mouth, said, "Indeed, that is so..."

"Even from my humble vantage, it was unexpected that even our Commanders could not press their attack home. If their contribution played a part in that..."

"It's an odd feeling to be speaking up on your behalf, but that white light that was unleashed from the castle... it was the girl in the dress right there who cancelled it out."

"—Well, well, is that so."

Jerking her chin, Serena spoke aloud of Beatrice's achievement. Hearing it, and understanding what that had to do with that light, Berstetz's expression tightened slightly.

That single blast which, just before Subaru and the others reached the battlefield, had nearly decided the whole battle—hearing Beatrice's achievement in canceling it out spoken of made her partner swell with pride.

"So, Goz-san, doesn't that change your whole premise? You keep saying you don't need our strength, but if we hadn't been there, things would've been a whole lot worse."

"Yes! I don't know the specifics, but I think it would have been much, much worse!"

"Emilie, perhaps it's time to lower the volume a bit, I suppose."

Under the wave after wave of attack from Subaru and Emilia, joined by Serena, and with Berstetz ending up giving covering fire as well, Goz's lips trembled.

If it were only Subaru and the others insisting on this, it would be one thing, but having Serena and the others—who were participating in this top-level talk—acknowledge it seemed to hit especially hard on Goz, with his strong sense of being a career soldier who'd risen through the ranks.

However—

"...Very well, I will grant that your contribution is real. But! To begin with, have you not yet even settled the crucial matter among yourselves!?"

"That's..."

"That's true..."

Faced with this entirely reasonable counterargument, Subaru and Emilia deflated.

Even if they won this argument against Goz, it would only mean they'd beaten an easier opponent—it wouldn't mean their own position sailed through unopposed.

If anything, what was truly necessary was persuading a far tougher crowd than Goz.

"I'll say this knowing full well it sounds cold-hearted, but—"

Just as Subaru re-recognized who his real opponent was, that very opponent spoke up, as if he'd timed it perfectly.

Otto, opening with a phrase that promised nothing but a bad feeling, looked back and forth between the pinched Subaru and Goz, who was doing the pinching.

"Just as Commander Goz says, this is the Empire's problem. Unlike a situation where necessity forces our hand, actively involving ourselves from here on would amount to interference in another nation's internal affairs... It could well become an international incident."

"Gh...!"

"In the first place, why is it that you want to intervene so badly, Natsuki-san? It's probably something about obligation or attachment to the people you've come to know here in the Empire up to now, isn't it?"

"Don't talk like you already get it, already get it! Is that supposed to be a bad thing!?"

"It's not a bad thing, no, but here's another way to persuade you. —What if we brought every single person Natsuki-san can't bring himself to abandon out of the Empire with us?"

Otto, who always sought to resolve problems through logic, unleashed the full force of his method here, and Subaru's eyes went wide at the outrageous proposal he'd just floated. Goz, apparently just as shocked, even let his fingers slip, dropping Subaru straight onto the floor.

Naturally, this shocked Emilia too. Helping Subaru up as he sat sprawled on the floor, she looked toward Otto and said, "Otto-kun?"

"What do you mean by that? Don't tell me, really..."

"There's no hidden twist to it—it's exactly the proposal it sounds like. Given the situation, let's go ahead with the recklessness of gathering up whoever wants to come and taking them out with us. Fortunately, we do have the authority to decide."

Otto's gaze flicked briefly toward Roswaal.

Roswaal was apparently concealing his true rank within the Empire, but it remained a fact that he held the greatest authority within the Emilia Camp. If Otto's proposal were to go through, it would fall to Roswaal to guarantee acceptance of those who would end up as refugees.

And in response to Otto's challenging remark, Roswaal closed one of his yellow eyes, leaving the other open, and said,

"At the very least, my employer won't refuse, I'd wager. If it means not having to take on a risk that needn't be taken, then that much counts as a necessary expense."

"Given how childish Barusu's thinking is, most of the people he's caught up on are probably ones without any power to fight. For the Empire, they're likely people it wouldn't mind letting go of either."

Even as this negotiation unfolded at a staggering level, the matter was being settled without so much as a glance at Subaru and the others.

Between Roswaal's outsized self-preservation and Ram's guess born of seeing straight through Subaru's character, there was no room to interject, and the conversation kept advancing while Subaru floundered.

"What do you say, Your Imperial Excellency? If it means reducing the casualties of your own nation even a little, isn't our proposal not such a bad one?"

"—A well-considered proposal. The kind not easily dismissed."

"You're too kind."

With even Abel affirming it as a flawless proposal, Otto gave a courteous bow, purely as a matter of form. Though internally, it wouldn't have been strange if he were sticking out his tongue.

Imperialism and Otto simply did not get along. Not in terms of capability, but in terms of temperament.

Just like Subaru, Otto probably disliked the Empire too.

On that point, they agreed. It was just that Otto, on top of that, was capable of properly sorting out what mattered most.

"Ngh..."

It felt exactly like having the holes in the ship he was meant to sail patched up without his say-so, only to have it remade into an entirely different ship.

That ship spread its sails toward a direction other than the one Subaru wished for, and yet it drifted on and on, aiming for the far shore, all to protect the lives of the people precious to him.

He understood perfectly well that it was something thought through to the very end, for their sake.

He understood, and still—

"So, what do you say, Natsuki-san? This is the maximum concession and consideration I'm able to offer."

Rather than flatly dismissing Subaru's opinion outright, Otto was doing his best to pick up on it while also trying to secure everyone's safety.

Subaru had always thought him a dependable man, but when opinions genuinely clashed, he was far too troublesome an opponent. A man he should have gotten dead drunk before this discussion even started.

Against Otto's overwhelmingly well-armed logic, what could Subaru, armed with nothing but a plain wooden sword, possibly—

"—Hey, Abel, there's something I want you to tell me."

"Emilia-tan?"

Out of nowhere, Emilia—who held the same opinion as Subaru and should have been feeling just as frustrated at being talked down—spoke up to Abel like that.

Seeing her profile, Subaru's eyes widened.

Unlike Subaru, whose face showed frustration as he scrambled to find some way to break through, neither Emilia's eyes nor her profile showed any sign of being at a disadvantage.

At that dignified, courageous call from Emilia, Abel's black eyes turned to look at her.

Prompted by his silence, Emilia's thin lips moved.

"How many people are there in the Vollachian Empire altogether?"

"—The civil strife of this past month has likely reduced the number considerably, but before that, it stood at roughly fifty million."

"I see."

With a furrowed brow, Abel gave that answer to Emilia's question. Hearing it, Emilia let out a short breath, then raised a finger to her own lips and looked at Subaru.

And then—

"There you have it, Subaru."

"—"

So Emilia said to Subaru.

For a moment he failed to catch the meaning of the words she'd handed him, but almost immediately Subaru's eyes went wide and his cheeks stiffened.

And with his cheeks tensed like that, he hesitated for an instant over what to do about it.

"Subaru, Betty already gave her opinion, I suppose. But—"

"Beatrice..."

"Betty's always on Subaru's side, I suppose."

Taking hold of Subaru's hesitating hand, Beatrice pressed herself close beside him.

With Beatrice, whose height barely differed from his own, fixing him with her eyes from closer than usual, Subaru drew in a deep breath, and let it out.

Still receiving Emilia's expectant gaze, he looked at Otto.

Looking up at him, he said:

"Fifty million."

"...I'm sorry?"

At the declaration, Otto's neatly shaped brows knit as he asked him to repeat it.

At being asked to repeat himself, Subaru squared his shoulders, twisted his cheek into a grin, and said it again.

"You said it yourself just now. That you'd bring every single person I can't bear to abandon back to the Kingdom. Well then! The people I can't bear to abandon number fifty million!"

"—! Natsuki-san!"

"I know! You're the one in the right! I'm the one talking nonsense! I know exactly how it sounds, saying this after everyone came all this way to save me and Rem! But still!"

Thrusting a hand out at Otto, whose brows had shot up, Subaru mocked himself even as he refused to hold back.

There was no way he could win against Otto, Ram, and Roswaal by cornering them with logic. So instead, Subaru would do what he could with Emilia and Garfiel on his side.

Which was to say, meeting logic head-on with pure, stubborn, emotional argument.

"This is a completely, insanely dangerous situation! What happens if we pull out and the Empire falls completely apart because of it? Just thinking about it, I can't even swallow food!"

"And what about the risk we'd be taking on? If we save fifty million total strangers only for one of us to die, or suffer a wound that never heals, that would cause far more suffering."

"I won't let anyone among us die, and I won't let anyone bear that kind of burden either. That's absolute! With that made absolute first, this is what comes next!"

Subaru shot this back at Otto, who was pressing him low and quiet, then turned his gaze not to Otto, but to Roswaal, standing beside him with one eye closed.

"Roswaal! You of all people should know. That when I say absolute, I mean absolute."

"...I'm Dudley, Subaru-kun. Still, it would be more than a little dishonest of me, given that I'm the one pinning my hopes on your 'absolute,' not to nod along to that question."

A roundabout way of putting it, but Roswaal was throwing up his hands in surrender to Subaru's argument.

Even without knowing it as 'Return by Death,' Roswaal knew of Subaru's Authority, and precisely because he pinned his hopes on that Authority, he couldn't afford to sour Subaru's mood.

Besides, if he let himself be swept along by Roswaal's smooth talk here, what would become of the oath sworn at the 'Sanctuary'?

"Ram, please..."

"...Emilie, don't you have even one shred of material to actually persuade Ram with?"

"All I've got is trying really, really hard, and wanting to be of help to everyone... so, please."

On the opposite side, Emilia was making an outrageously brute-force appeal to Ram.

Ram, who would have slapped Subaru down with a merciless verbal backhand for trying the same thing, found herself unable to muster an effective response to Emilia's approach.

Ram let out a small sigh, and said,

"As far as Ram's concerned, all that matters is bringing Rem home. But seeing how things are with Rem lately, she's grown quite close to certain people in the Empire... and if Ram were to just abandon that, it would tarnish the dignity of an older sister she's only just started to properly feel again."

"Ram...!"

"Stop it, you're being a nuisance."

Emilia threw her arms around Ram, who had closed her eyes and found her own reason to compromise. To Emilia's overflowing embrace, Ram responded with that bitter remark, expression sour.

And so, through methods that were nothing but sheer brute force, Roswaal and Ram both fell.

However—

"Otto, please...!"

"The same tearful pleading as Emilie? Unfortunately, I'm not like Ram-san. I won't do anything so foolish as signing a contract without knowing what price I'll have to pay."

"Bea-ko, ask him too, please...!"

"B-Betty's asking too, I suppose."

"Whether it's two of you or three of you ganging up, the answer is no."

As Subaru prompted Beatrice, and Emilia, still hugging Ram, moved to join in as well, Otto once again cut them off before they could get anywhere.

With the look of a man utterly resolved not to be moved by sentiment, Otto's stubbornness rivaled the toughness of dealing with Roswaal in the grip of some mad obsession. Though right now, even that comparison, meant as an insult, probably wouldn't land on Otto at all.

"It's nothing but risk—there's no benefit at all in Natsuki-san and Emilia-sama's plan. It doesn't even meet the conditions for a tearful appeal to work. Ram-san, Dudley, please stop spoiling the two of them."

"Oh dear, I've been scolded."

"Awfully cheeky of you, for someone in Otto's position."

As Otto said this sternly, Roswaal shrugged and Ram's brow furrowed. But apparently having no real objection to Otto's argument itself, neither of them talked back any further.

In truth, unless the fortress that was Otto could be brought down, there was no forcing the situation through.

Because Emilia—and the Emilia Camp as a whole—would never accept simply forcing a decision from above on a situation where not everyone was convinced.

And into that—

"—You said there's no benefit. Then if there were a benefit, the matter would change."

"—"

Neither Subaru nor Emilia, but Abel, cut in with that.

At the emperor interjecting himself into the discussion, Otto's gaze sharpened. Unlike Subaru's casual jabs, the look Otto turned on the emperor was genuinely, seriously disrespectful.

"If there were a benefit, you say. Just what exactly do you intend to do? I'll tell you now, even if you offer some kind of reward, there's no way it could possibly be worth—"

"—I will formally request the cooperation of a Royal Selection candidate of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica."

"—!"

At the proposal Abel had put forward, Otto's expression stiffened, and his throat clicked.

At the same time, the ones whose faces shook with the greatest tremor weren't Subaru's group but the people on the Imperial side. At the words, Serena laughed, and Berstetz's thread-thin eyes widened slightly.

And Goz, astonishment plastered across his face, spread both arms wide as if the world were ending.

"Please wait, Your Excellency!! Requesting cooperation from the Kingdom... such a thing! There is no precedent for it in this Sacred Vollachia Empire!!"

"What of it, whether there's precedent or not. If we're going to speak of precedent, is there precedent for an emperor disgracefully withdrawing like this after having his capital stolen by insolent rabble? A pointless thing to cling to."

"But! If you rely on another nation, the soldiers and the people alike will grieve that you lacked strength, that you made a decision unbefitting a Sword Wolf! With all due reverence, it would dim Your Excellency's own imperial authority!!"

"My authority would dim, would it... A worthless concern."

To Goz's raised-voice plea, Abel shook his head and answered so.

Cut off so flatly, Goz's eyes widened, frozen in place. Looking back at him, Abel called his vassal's name: "Goz Ralfon."

"What we require right now—is it a hollow authority with no substance behind it? —No, it is not. What this Empire desires now is victory. It is the victory won only after tearing out the enemy's throat, bathing in blood, and drinking down their lives, that will shape the Empire's tomorrow."

"Y-Your Excellency..."

"Anything that might stand as an obstacle to sinking those fangs in—all of it, without exception, is an 'enemy' under the Code of Iron Blood that I, Vincent Vollachia, lay down. Answer carefully, Goz Ralfon."

"—"

"—Are you my 'enemy'?"

Asked so quietly, Goz's entire body trembled.

There was no trace anywhere of the man who had scuffled with Subaru back in his room—standing so straight now, displaying the full authority of an emperor, Abel possessed real power.

That power pressed down on others through his gaze, his voice, his very presence itself, and Goz found himself receiving the full brunt of it, up close and head-on, alone.

And then, after a span of thought that in real time couldn't have lasted even five seconds—

"Forgive my terrible discourtesy, taking up Your Excellency's time. —I, Goz Ralfon! Am the war-hammer that shatters every obstacle that dares stand before Your Excellency! I am never! Your Excellency's 'enemy'!!"

"Then very well. I am counting on your service. Redouble your efforts."

"Yes!! ...Huh!?"

Nodding at Goz's booming reply, Abel answered by declaring his expectations of his vassal's service. Having received that with full force, Goz let out a sound like a man struck by lightning.

Probably, the cause of his astonishment was the one extra line Abel had tacked on.

"—! I shall exert myself to the utmost!!"

An instant later, tears welled up and poured down in torrents, and Goz wiped his face with a thick arm, swearing anew to Abel in a trembling voice.

Paying him only passing notice, Abel glanced briefly at Serena and Berstetz, and said,

"You two have no objections either, I take it."

"None. That's just the kind of face I like—it's made my loyalty to Your Excellency swell all the more."

"None from me either, humble as I am. Other than being more than a little astonished."

"Hmph."

After snorting at Serena's smile and Berstetz's thread-thin eyes once more, Abel stepped forward and came to stand directly in front of Emilia.

And gazing at Emilia, who was blinking in surprise, he said,

"You heard correctly, Royal Selection candidate. The Vollachian Empire formally requests the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica's cooperation, to bring this situation under control. —Lend me your strength."

"...Um, listen, Abel. I really do want to help you. But I don't know that 'Royal Selection candidate' at all, not even a little, not even the tiniest bit."

"Emilia-tan, that's not it! This is the part where it's fine to tell him!"

"Eh!? Is it!?"

At Abel's offer, Emilia—who had genuinely looked pained a moment ago—widened her eyes.

As Subaru and Beatrice nodded back at her again and again, Emilia looked to Ram, still in her embrace, to double check.

At that, Ram shook off Emilia's arms and instead gave her a push on the back.

"Yes, that's right. —Go on, do as you like, Emilia-sama."

"—Ah."

Called 'Emilia' from Ram's own mouth, Emilia's surprise gave way to eyes lighting up. Then she hid her joy behind a blink and turned back to face Abel properly.

To Abel, who waited for her to reset with his brow still faintly furrowed, Emilia cleared her throat with an "Ahem,"

"I'm Emilia, just Emilia. One of the Royal Selection candidates chosen to decide the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica's next monarch. And right now, someone who wants to help an Empire in danger."

"—This is a formal request. It is unheard of for the Vollachian Empire to ask for cooperation from a citizen of another nation, let alone one bearing such heavy responsibility. For that reason, should this fact become public, it may well work as a tailwind for you as well, in your contest for the throne back in the Kingdom—"

"Oh, enough! That kind of thing can wait! What I want to do right now is this!"

Emilia puffed her cheeks out at Abel, who had started reeling off one stiff, formal justification after another. With that adorable pout still on her face, what she showed Abel was her own hand.

Lowering the hand she'd raised, slowly, Emilia held it out to Abel, and said,

"Let us help save your Empire."

That in the end it was she who ended up making the offer, backward from how it should have gone, was so very like Emilia. Looking down at the hand she held out, Abel glanced, just for a moment, toward Subaru.

At the faint bewilderment rising in those black eyes, Subaru grinned wickedly and nodded.

"Go ahead, Your Imperial Excellency. I'll graciously allow you to take my Emilia-tan's hand."

"Honestly, it's you who's mine, Subaru."

Answering Subaru's glib remark with a shy little reply like that, Emilia drew a sigh from Abel, who closed one eye at the exchange between the two of them.

Then, slowly, his hand closed around the one Emilia had offered.

"—Very well. I shall let you lend me your strength."

That this too, in the end, came out sounding so very like Abel, was how the framework for cooperation between the two nations came to be established.

△▼△▼△▼△

—And so, with Emilia and Abel shaking hands, a historic moment between the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica and the Vollachian Empire was etched into being here. But—

"Um, Otto-san, how does something along those lines sound to you...?"

Subaru and Emilia, Abel and the Imperial side, had all gotten swept up in the moment, an exchange that had ultimately arrived at a handshake—but the very person who'd started this whole discussion, Otto, had been left completely behind.

Roswaal and Ram had found their point of compromise, but Otto hadn't, and yet the conversation had moved forward and left him behind anyway, so Subaru was thoroughly terrified.

Fully aware that he'd done something far too far, he found himself, without meaning to, asking with polite speech and hand-wringing deference.

"—"

There was no reply from Otto to Subaru's timid question.

Too frightened to look the man in the face, Subaru found himself thoroughly at a loss, taking the silence as his answer. Without meaning to, he pulled Beatrice close beside him, pressing their cheeks together, and said,

"W-what do we do, Bea-ko... Otto won't even talk to me..."

"Betty understands how you feel, I suppose. From Otto's perspective, he probably feels like he's been made a total fool of, I suppose. Having his own idea smashed to bits like that—might as well call him a clown, I suppose."

"A clown, huh... he doesn't have to end up resembling Roswaal that much..."

"Stop it, I suppose! An insult that extreme will only make him angrier, I suppose!"

Flustered and fumbling, Subaru cast about together with Beatrice for some way to break the impasse.

He wanted to somehow make up for having crushed Otto's dignity, but how?

"Should I, um, give you a shoulder rub, or a leg rub...?"

"Somehow that's exactly how you'd try to make up with an angry parent, I suppose..."

"Well, I can't think of anything else! Otto, come on, please... hiii!"

In front of Subaru, unable to come up with a decent proposal, Otto—who had been standing there in silence all this while—abruptly pulled out a chair and dropped heavily into it.

Startled by the sudden movement, Subaru sprang back, still clinging to Beatrice. Surely he hadn't sat down just to let Subaru give him a shoulder rub.

And there, in front of the flustered, panicking Subaru and Beatrice, Otto put a hand to his forehead—

"...Well, I suppose it did settle properly into the landing spot I actually wanted."

"Huh...?"

Letting out a long, deep breath, Otto began rubbing at the corners of his eyes. At the words he'd just uttered, Subaru sat there dumbstruck for a moment before blinking in disbelief. "Don't tell me..."

"Don't tell me you knew from the very start it would end up like this..."

"Not that I knew it would end up like this—more that I went in intending to see at least this much through. I had no way of knowing just how far Emperor Vincent would be willing to bend. But since Natsuki-san and Emilia-sama were never going to budge from your position anyway, I had to have a landing spot worked out in advance."

"Ah, oh, uh, eh, ah, oh, uh..."

At Otto's flat, matter-of-fact answer, Subaru's mouth opened and closed uselessly. Turning around in sheer disbelief, he found his eyes meeting Roswaal's and Ram's instead.

And both of them, too, wore the expressions of people who had, broadly speaking, more or less had the whole thing figured out from the start.

"You guys are, you guys are, you're so scaaaaary—!!"

"How insulting! It's your own fault for being so stubborn about it in the first place, Natsuki-san!"

"Ugh, I can't trust anyone anymore except Bea-ko and Emilia-tan...! And Rem! And Rui! And Tanza and everyone in the Battalion and Flop-san's group and Mizelda-san's group, that's it!"

"That's still quite a lot of people, I suppose!"

Hugging Beatrice tight, Subaru could do nothing but stand in awe of just how thoroughly prepared the brain trust of Otto and the rest had been. Perhaps Abel, too, had factored their thinking into his plans to some degree—but even so.

"No, no, that's terrifying, seriously, I can't deal with that. From now on I'm sticking to the emotional route."

"That conclusion makes no sense, but Betty thinks Subaru's better off being honest with his heart too, I suppose. It's more passionate that way, and Betty likes that, I suppose."

"Yeah, I love you too. Man, seriously—"

At last, the shock of Otto's earlier revelation began to fade, and Subaru breathed a sigh of relief.

Just as he'd managed to settle his feelings like that—

—Suddenly, the sound of applause rang out through the room.

"—Ahh, now that was somethin' real amusin' to watch. Amazing, amazing, real impressive stuff."

At the loud sound of clapping, Subaru instinctively swept his gaze around to see who was applauding, and froze, breath caught, at the sight of an unfamiliar figure.

In the corner of the room where no one had been a moment ago, a tall figure now stood, seemingly out of nowhere. That person clapped both hands together in applause, watching the exchange that had just taken place.

—Without letting a single person in the room sense its presence.

"Who goes there!? Just how did you get in here...!!"

"Ah, oops. Here I am clappin' away without even bein' asked to. No good, no good—findin' a kid worth praisin' and just gushin' over 'em, that's my bad habit, that is."

In an instant, Goz shielded Emilia and Abel behind him and dropped into a battle-ready stance.

Before Goz, his face now twisted into a look of fierce, murderous intensity, the figure kept his easygoing manner unshaken, biting down on a golden pipe drawn from his robes, and grinned with practiced ease.

Utterly out of place, the figure's appearance stood out all the more strikingly for it.

Standing nearly two meters tall, covered in black fur, his easygoing, grinning expression full of charm, and paired with his shaggy, unkempt coat, giving off a gentle, mild-mannered impression—a black Dog Human.

Clad in a kimono, a pipe clenched in his teeth, this presence had appeared, seemingly from nowhere, aboard the Coupled Dragon Carriages where the Empire's most important figures had all gathered—naturally, every eye in the room turned to him.

"Aw, look at that, seems like everyone's real curious about me."

"Who are you? What are you doing here?"

Scratching his head with a finger even as every eye fell on him, the Dog Human was addressed by Emilia, sheltered as she was behind Goz's back.

At her question, the Dog Human tilted his head, his mouth lined with fangs twisting into a grin, and said,

"Name's Halibel. Mind if I say a quick hello?"
