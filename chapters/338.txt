Arc 5 Chapter 22: "Answer-Checking on Easy Choices"

Perhaps it should be called an aerial battle.
With a single leap, Reinhard closed in on Sirius, who had taken to the heights, and after kicking his opponent upward, he chased him by hooking his toes once more onto the white rim of the Time Tower, then with nothing more than a light bend and extension of his knee, he shot upward like a bullet.

"Fufufu! Ah, how overwhelming!"

At the sight of the crimson hero hurtling toward him, Sirius raised a voice steeped in ecstasy and swung his arm. What sliced the air with a droning growl was an iron chain, wound many times around Sirius's arm.
A chain that could be used both as a striking weapon while coiled and as a whip when uncoiled — its practicality mattered less than the sheer violence of its appearance and sound, which plainly declared its wielder's departure from the common sense of those who favor such weapons.

The iron serpent, which had surely already tasted the blood of more people than could be counted on one hand, screamed its usual cheer as it tore through the wind, meaning to crush its prey between iron fangs.
But even that dull-grey serpent, closing in at speeds nearing the speed of sound, could not have known.

—That there existed, in this world, a kind of human being unhinged in a way entirely different from something like Sirius.

"A chain, huh. Troublesome," the Sword Saint murmured, brow furrowing before the sound-outpacing blow of the chain.

In the midst of high-speed combat, he murmured it with the same everyday air one might use to let slip a small complaint over some mundane inconvenience.

"Fufufu!"

As though exhaling, Sirius let a breath heavy with heat spill out as a smile.
He must have had no choice but to do so. Whether that smile of Sirius's own was born of desperation or genuine amusement, no one could say — but Subaru, watching from below, and every member of the crowd besides him, all understood the same thing.

Even someone other than Sirius could do nothing but laugh at a moment like this.

"——"

Reinhard, chasing Sirius as he flew skyward, leapt up after him from directly below.
Sirius aimed both arm-chains at Reinhard without missing, striking true. Against that blow closing in at tremendous speed, Reinhard did not reach for the hilt of the sword at his hip.
If what Subaru had heard before was true, it wasn't that he chose not to draw his sword — he *could not*. The Sword of the Covenant that Reinhard carried was apparently built so that it could not be drawn except by one deemed worthy. Which meant he would have to face that terrifying monstrosity bare-handed.
Even someone as formidable as Reinhard couldn't avoid a hard fight — that was the belief Subaru had wanted to hold to, perhaps because he wanted to believe that Reinhard, as an existence, could still be met with as something within the bounds of humanity.

If so, that faith was about to be shattered.

The second strike of the chain aimed at Reinhard was deflected with a piercing clang.
The resulting shockwave and scattering sparks danced so violently that, to Subaru and the others watching from below, it looked almost like a bolt of lightning had flashed.
The speed with which that feat was accomplished was itself proof that Reinhard was a being beyond human comprehension.

Against the first chain-strike closing in on him, Reinhard had blocked it by bringing his long right leg to meet it.
If that alone were all he had done — blocked the attack — it would still have been astonishing, but not quite laughable. What pushed it past mere shock was what came immediately after: with a twist of his ankle, Reinhard snared the chain whose tip had met the sole of his shoe, and made it his own.
Nothing complicated, really. Reinhard intercepted the incoming chain with his right leg, wound the chain around that same leg to arm himself on the spot, and used that very chain to deflect the second blow that followed.

The gap in time was a mere instant — the span of a fraction of a second in which this exchange of attacks occurred.
Naturally, not every one of the ordinary onlookers among Subaru's group could follow it with their eyes. A handful of skilled martial artists grasped the feat at once; most ordinary people only came to understand it once the following exchange made it clear.
The moment they understood, what came over them was an urge to laugh. Breath escaped, and the strength drained from their shoulders. There was no greater relief than knowing he was an ally. Had he been an enemy, by now it wouldn't just be their shoulders losing strength, but their knees and bladders too.

"Fufu, fufufu! Ahaha, fufufufu!!"

Laughing aloud, Sirius swirled his right arm like a tornado.
With the chain on his left arm now claimed by Reinhard, all the monstrosity could manage was strikes from the right. The growling, dull-grey serpent tore through the sky in every direction, but every single blow was shot down by the chain now wrapped around Reinhard's right leg, scattering high-pitched clangs and sparks.

Each time, a small firework bloomed in the blue sky, and over the midday plaza unfolded a scene of instrument-like sounds and red and yellow flames dancing in the air.
One strike, then another — yet even as this exchange continued, the distance between Reinhard and Sirius kept shrinking, until at last the blows began to close to true point-blank range, and—

"No way — it's come to this?! You're just too amazing!"

"Your arm has grown quite skilled as well. All the more a pity, then, that such skill is put to wicked use."

In the instant their paths crossed, the two exchanged words even as they each unleashed a blow.
Reinhard drew back his right leg and instead thrust out his left hand, fingers aligned into a blade. Sirius, meaning to intercept it, swung his arm down with especial force, and the rippling chain bared its fangs.

The sight of that object of supposedly sturdy steel severed by nothing more than a bare hand-blade was, if anything, moving to behold.
Subaru had once, in the past, seen a party trick where someone cut disposable chopsticks using only the paper sleeve the chopsticks came in. If it were Reinhard, surely he could cut a steel sword with paper.
His single strike carried a beauty of blade sharp enough to make one believe exactly that.

Severed clean through the middle, the chain's remains flew off with the momentum still carrying them. It slammed into the white wall of the Time Tower, breaking through the surface and plunging into the building's interior. Watching the smoke and rubble fall across the plaza, Subaru finally came back to himself.

He had been entranced.
By the fight between Reinhard and Sirius — no, by Reinhard's fighting alone. Whether that entrancement had been born of envy or of awe, he couldn't say.

"That, I can leave to Reinhard. Which means I—!"

There was no reason to keep lingering here, simply waiting for the outcome.
As if shaking off the disgrace of having stood there slack-jawed, Subaru slipped through a gap in the crowd and dashed into the Time Tower. With Sirius now fully occupied by Reinhard, Lusbel — set up inside the tower as part of the speech's staging — was surely being left unattended.

He would retrieve the boy and cut off any lingering danger.
So that Sirius could never use Lusbel's captivity as leverage to shake Reinhard, not even by the slimmest chance.

Inside the dim, damp air of the Time Tower, Subaru raced up the spiral staircase at full speed.
His view was brighter than it had been just fifteen minutes ago, thanks to the hole punched into the wall by the chain that had slipped Sirius's right hand a while ago.
Climbing the spiral staircase without incident, Subaru found Lusbel bound at the top floor. Laid face-down, the boy's floor was soaked where he'd wet himself, and his sobbing struck straight at Subaru's chest.

"Lusbel! It's okay now, don't worry!"

Rushing over, Subaru pulled up the gagged Lusbel and held him.
The lukewarm dampness didn't bother him in the least. The boy, tears streaming down, saw Subaru's face and desperately turned away, showing fear.

"Nnn—!"

"Don't get the wrong idea. I'm on your side. That monster's my enemy too, and right now he's got his hands full fighting the hero outside. So I'm getting you out of here while I can."

"—————ッ"

Meeting his eyes with desperate sincerity, Subaru pleaded earnestly. Little by little, the strength drained from Lusbel's squirming body, and his tear-brimmed eyes regained their sanity as he looked at Subaru.
Nodding back at that gaze, the boy began to sob for a different reason than before.

"Hang on. I'll get this off you right now."

Stroking the whimpering boy's head, Subaru carefully began removing the chains, taking care not to hurt him.
Chains wound tightly from shoulder to ankle, and a chain wrapped around his face like a gag. Being careful not to let the chain bite into the boy's tender skin, he finished undoing it all with painstaking care.

"Alright, it's off. Can you stand on your own? If not, I'll carry you."

"I-I'm okay… th-thank you… so much…"

Standing bravely on trembling legs, Lusbel thanked Subaru. Tears still stained his face, but Subaru, thinking *what a strong kid*, stroked his head once more.
Then, casting a glance toward the direction outside where the sounds of fierce combat still echoed on and on—

"It might not even be safe here, come to think of it. Better to get out of the tower for now. Can you walk? Anywhere hurt?"

"My right arm, a little, just now…"

Grimacing, Lusbel obediently showed the wound to Subaru.
On the boy's offered right arm was a bruise as if a snake had coiled around it, along with a sharp laceration. At the sight of the ugly wound, oozing blood, Subaru's face twisted in anguish.

"That bastard, not only did he tie up a kid this small, he went and hurt him too."

"No, that's not it. This wound — it suddenly, just now… while I was tied up, it suddenly started hurting."

"Suddenly?"

Hearing Lusbel say *while I was tied up*, Subaru tilted his head.
At the very least, Subaru hadn't been the one to injure him while undoing the restraints. He'd been careful, and a wound that bad — he would have noticed if he'd caused it.

—Something terribly, unpleasantly, made his chest stir with dread.

"…Let's go. Whatever happens, we can't stay here."

Grasping Lusbel's uninjured left hand, Subaru pulled the boy along and broke into a run. Descending the spiral staircase in one breath to the bottom floor, Subaru and Lusbel emerged together from the tower.
And then, when Subaru returned, the plaza was—

"—Kill him! Kill him! Kill him! Kill him!"

A bloodthirsty chant demanding the execution of the cornered monstrosity roared from the crowd.

"—Kill him! Kill him! Kill him! Kill him!"

Eyes bloodshot, teeth bared, stamping the cobblestones, the people demanded slaughter as one.
Endless hatred toward the atrocity. Loathing for a monstrosity that instilled discomfort in others. Hostility wanting to expel a being one's very instincts rejected. Such negative emotions summon murderous intent.
Do you know what people call it, when all of that is gathered together?

—People call it "Wrath."

"—Kill him! Kill him! Kill him! Kill him!"

Strangers with no knowledge of each other's names linked arms, marching forward toward a single purpose.

"—Kill him! Kill him! Kill him! Kill him!"

Hearts uniting before hardship, the moral fiber of humanity put to the test amid the extraordinary.

"—Kill him! Kill him! Kill him! Kill him!"

If, in an extreme state, people choose to band together, then that is—

"—Kill him! Kill him! Kill him! Kill him!"

"To become one… isn't that 'Love'? If so, don't you think this is undeniably a scene wrought by 'Love'?"

Before a spectacle that could only be called a vision of hell, Sirius whispered in a voice tinged with rapture.
Back pressed against the Time Tower, the monstrosity was cornered by the hero atop the cobblestones. The crowd surrounding them longed for the monstrosity's death, and their champion, the Sword Saint, possessed more than enough power to finish him off.

Cornered, Sirius appeared to have lost the chain from his left hand as well. With both sides now unarmed, the monstrosity had no means left to block Reinhard's hand-blade.
An absolute dead end — and yet, Sirius kept laughing, his manner unchanged.

"Any last words?"

"Thanks. Well then, just one piece of advice. The other Sin Archbishops aren't docile like me, so if you go trying to hear out their dying words, I'm sure you'll have a terrible time of it."

"—I'll be sure to take that to heart."

Before Reinhard's mercy, Sirius spat plainly provocative words. Reinhard nodded dutifully in return and stepped forward, hand-blade raised, to carry out the execution.

"—Kill him! Kill him! Kill him! Kill him!"

The crowd's fervor climbed higher still, and Sirius's fate was already sealed.
And yet — why was it?

At the entrance of the Time Tower, Subaru felt a chill as if his chest were being clawed open.
He couldn't say what the cause was, or what it meant. Desperately trying to put it into words, his mouth wouldn't move. If it moved, Subaru would say something he didn't intend to.

Surely, Subaru too would end up screaming *Kill him!*

"To know one another. To yield to one another. To acknowledge one another. To forgive one another. Becoming one through all of that — that is the proper, true form of 'Love.'"

Heedless of Subaru's mounting anxiety, Sirius voiced his overbearing personal philosophy.
At a glance, it sounded almost righteous — but factoring in what Sirius actually was, the claim instantly transformed into something abhorrent.

"——"

Reinhard, it seemed, had reached the same judgment as Subaru.
He stepped forward, determined not to let Sirius utter another word. But before him, Sirius sneered and thrust an arm toward the sky.
An instant later, a sound like something snapping loose came from the coat's sleeve, and a chain shot out — launched by some mechanism hidden in the sleeve — coiling around the tip of the tower and hauling Sirius upward in one motion.

Fleeing — before that motion, Reinhard stamped the ground without holding back in the slightest.
A blast of wind erupted, and crimson flame surged all at once toward Sirius, borne aloft by the winding chain.

The raised hand-blade was a single fatal stroke, far surpassing any half-hearted edge.
The instant it connected, Sirius's life would be severed.

"—Kill him! Kill him! Kill him! Kill him!"

The crowd's cry — about to be fulfilled.
A tremendous chill raced up Subaru's spine. Driven by impulse—

"Reinhard!!"

He called the hero's name, Subaru shouting, led on by that anxiety.

"—Kill him!!"

The hand-blade struck.
Its trajectory traced a white arc, cleaving Sirius's body clean from the left shoulder to the right side.
The cut was so brilliantly, terrifyingly sharp that even the severed flesh took several seconds to register the blow. Only after that delay did comprehension finally catch up, blood bursting from the wound as Sirius's body split diagonally and collapsed.

"…Ahh, what a kind world."

Entrails spilling out, Sirius's body was parted top from bottom.
The upper half, hauled up by the chain, rose into the sky scattering blood and intestines; the lower half, left behind, spilled crimson like a fountain and rolled across the plaza.

A nightmarish sight, flung apart between heaven and earth.
A scene of carnage that made everyone want to look away. And yet no one looked away.

They were far past the point of looking away.

"…No…"

Landing, turning back, Reinhard murmured in stunned dismay.
His blue eyes trembled with sorrow, and Subaru saw the shadow of despair fall across his handsome profile.

—That was as far as Subaru got to see.

"——"

Subaru too, along with the crowd, now lay scattered across a plaza turned pool of blood.
His wound exposed, sliced clean from left shoulder to right side.

"——"

Blood and entrails spilling out, with no understanding of what had happened, Subaru's consciousness was dragged down into death. Only just before that, he thought he felt it.

The boy's left hand, still held in his own — the boy's left hand, split clean in two just as Subaru's had been — gave Subaru's right hand a faint squeeze, as if pleading for salvation.

He thought he felt it.

"For the get-together after the song, perhaps Natsuki-sama could prepare some snacks? I'm sure if something sweet is on hand, it'll lift everyone's spirits and bring you two closer together in an instant, don't you think? Don't you think?"

"—————ッ！"

"Ow, owowow! Ow! That hurts, Subaru!!"

The instant he blinked, a voice reached him and Subaru's shoulders jumped in shock.
At the same time, just before his consciousness had violently switched over, the impulse to squeeze back hard carried over with him, and his pointless grip strength — strong enough to crush Appa — clenched tight around Beatrice's small hand.
At Subaru's sudden act of violence, Beatrice's eyes welled with tears as she kicked his shin. The pain snapped him back to himself, and Subaru let go of Beatrice's hand, stumbling back.

"W-w-w-what happened? Trying to crush Beatrice-sama's hand all of a sudden… Poor little hand, so precious. S-shall I, shall I lick it and suck on it to heal it, haa, haa—"

"That's quite alright, I suppose! There's suddenly an unpleasant air about you, don't come closer, I suppose!"

Taking Beatrice's hand, cheeks flushed, breath ragged, was Liliana. Beatrice shook the hand free and hid behind Subaru with a frightened expression. That even after nearly having her hand crushed, Beatrice's trust in her partner never wavered — her kindness struck deep.
But now was not the time for that.

"Subaru, are you okay? Your face just went really pale."

"E-Emilia-tan…"

Leaning in close beside him, Emilia placed a worried hand on Subaru's cheek. In her violet eyes, framed by long lashes, he saw his own reflection and let out a breath.

He was back, it seemed.
He patted at his own shoulder and side, the place that had been cleanly bisected. He had confidence in having died plenty of ugly deaths — cut open at the stomach, head crushed — but a proper cleaving in two was a first. Surprise and a sense of loss outweighed pain, the sensation of both body and soul being yanked down all at once into the fact of "death." Even Subaru, sommelier of his own murders, had to admit this was a way to go.

"Not that I can just brush it off like that…"

Once his understanding caught up with the memories he'd carried over, what assailed Subaru was the belated, creeping realization of "death" demanding recognition.
The fact of being cleaved hadn't carried over the pain, but the sense of loss and the shock came crashing down on him regardless. And once that understanding had run its course, what remained was something further beyond even that understanding.

Namely, this time's cause of death, but—

"That's a foul, seriously…"

Needless to say, he understood.
This time's cleaving death of Subaru's had been the exact same, down to the last detail, as Sirius's own death just before. Which is to say, put simply: Subaru had died the same way Sirius had died. Looking back at that very first time, thirty minutes prior, Subaru had died just after watching Lusbel's death amid a fit of ecstasy — and the cause of death that had eluded him then became clear now, thanks to this.

—Somehow, by some principle, Sirius was able to inflict the same "death" onto anyone who witnessed that "death."

It wasn't just brainwashing through the sharing of emotional shifts. It had to be considered a sharing of even the physical changes the body underwent. Not mind control, but — life control, or perhaps soul control, one might call it.
Which meant that killing him would mean harming everyone present at the scene.

"What am I supposed to do…?"

His goal of stopping Sirius's rampage had been achieved by summoning Reinhard.
But the cost was the lives of everyone present. That left the outcome no different from what would have resulted had Sirius simply carried out his own plan. Summoning Reinhard was, at a glance, an answer that looked obviously correct but was in fact wrong. So then, what was he supposed to do?

"Call Reinhard, and have him take Sirius alive…?"

Was that even possible? As for possible or not — it wasn't out of the question.
If Reinhard was capable of killing him, surely he was also capable of merely taking his consciousness. The problem was that even if Sirius were captured alive, there was no certainty that the mind control would be severed.
Just from contact with Sirius, Subaru and the boy had gone mad and died together. If that inexplicable contagion of madness continued regardless, then the man could not be captured and kept alive.

Kill him, and the killer dies along with him.
Keep him alive, and there's no denying the possibility that an irresistible madness will spread.
A being that threatens others merely by existing — a bomb of a man. Truly worthy of being a Sin Archbishop.

"Is there any way…?"

Unable to find a thread to pull toward a breakthrough, Subaru's thoughts hit a dead end.
If he called Reinhard, he could surely defeat Sirius and take him alive. Should that alone be counted as good enough? Turning a blind eye to the possibility of that madness.

"——"

Even as Subaru agonized, time kept passing.
Emilia and Liliana wore anxious expressions at Subaru's silence. He wanted to avoid worrying them, and to avoid having them notice the situation awaiting him, for now.
Subaru hurriedly composed his expression.

"Ah, it's nothing. Just, um… yeah, this morning's grilled-eel-on-rice suddenly came back up on me. My chest's been a little off."

"Ahh, I get it, I get it. I've done that too, mistaken it for a burp and it comes right back up, or mistaken it for a fart and…"

"Stop right there. You're technically a girl, so please, absolutely do not finish that sentence."

Silencing Liliana, who had been cheerfully veering into crude territory, Subaru smiled at Emilia. At that smile, Emilia lowered her gaze slightly, but—

"If you say so, Subaru, I'll believe you. …Just this once, okay?"

"Yeah, thanks. …Alright, I'll go buy some sweets like Liliana suggested. Emilia-tan, enjoy the song for me."

Leaning on Emilia's consideration, Subaru raised a hand lightly and declared as much. Then, taking the hand of Beatrice, who was looking up at him from right beside him—

"Beako. You're coming shopping with me too. Let's go, all lovey-dovey and cozy."

"What's gotten into— …No, never mind. Understood, I suppose."

Beatrice, who had flushed and nearly gone prickly on reflex, caught sight of Subaru's expression and let her softer side show instead. Or rather, she noticed the pleading look in his eyes and accepted it.
Showing a face that leaned on Beatrice and Beatrice alone, Subaru pulled her along by the hand as they dashed out of the park for the fourth time.

This time, without leaving Beatrice behind — bringing his dependable partner along instead.
Even so, he still had no idea what to do, no clue of any way through.

"—Hm."

—And behind that retreating figure of Subaru and Beatrice, a crimson woman watched on with eyes full of hidden meaning.
