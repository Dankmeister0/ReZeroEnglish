Arc 2 Chapter 13: "The Promised Morning Is Far Away"

"So-o-o, then — how's Subaru-kun been getting along since then?"

It was night — the sun had already sunk beyond the western sky, and a moon just past its first quarter hung in the heavens, when this quiet report took place.

It was a spacious room. At its center stood a sofa and table for receiving guests, while at the back sat an ebony desk and a leather chair reserved for the room's master.

Papers and a quill pen lay scattered across the ebony desk, and beside them a still-steaming cup gave off a faint, gentle fragrance.

At a glance one could tell it was a study — a room at the center of the manor's upper floor.

Seated in the leather chair, the one who'd voiced that first question was a man with long indigo hair and a gentle countenance, his eyes an odd mismatched pair of blue and yellow — Roswaal.

The question had been little more than a whisper, but it reached its listener perfectly well. Small wonder — the one sharing this secret conference with him was right beside him, curled sideways atop his lap as he sat in the chair, making her already small frame smaller still.

"Five days since that little outburst of his — no, four and a half, is it? About time something ought to be coming into view, wouldn't you say-y?"

"I suppose so. ...He's utterly hopeless."

The one being whispered to, her pink hair gently stroked, was Ram. Only the two of them, Roswaal and Ram, were in the room — there was no sign of the twin sister she called her other half.

That was simply because it was Ram, not Rem, who held the position of Subaru's instructor.

At her instructor's unambiguous verdict, Roswaal blinked in surprise, then immediately burst out laughing, his face breaking into a grin — enough that he coughed a little.

"Ahaha, is that so-o? Utterly hopeless, is he?"

"Barusu really can't do anything. His cooking's terrible, his cleaning's clumsy, and when you try to hand him the laundry his breathing gets all heavy. None of it can be entrusted to him."

"That's a grave state of affairs for a servant, especially that last one-e."

Looking up at her master, who gave a wry smile and murmured that it couldn't be helped at that age, Ram found herself looking back over the past four days once more. Each time that short, dense stretch of time came vividly back to her, her neat, composed features would visibly crack, the cold mask slipping, plain for anyone to see.

"It's rare to see you make a face like that. Is he really that hopeless?"

"Hopeless through and through. It's not that he's bad at things — he simply doesn't know them at all. I can only assume he was raised somewhere quite privileged. And yet for all that, he's lacking in basic knowledge."

"Ah, I did hear a little about that. Apparently you've been bombarded with all sorts of questions-s."

"And what he asks is so baffling in itself that I hardly know what to make of it."

At Roswaal's raised brow, Ram brought up here a few of the questions Subaru had thrown at her. Examining each one in turn, Roswaal set a hand to his chin with a thoughtful "Hmm."

"It's true, what Emilia-sama likes is rather a mystery."

"Roswaal-sama, Roswaal-sama. That routine of yours is starting to resemble Barusu's."

"Mmm, no-ow that's troubling. To think that I, of all people, should fall behind on the stage of eccentrics, perverts, and oddballs... how vexing."

At her master's furrowed brow, genuinely troubled, Ram let out a sigh he wouldn't notice. Then, shifting her position within his arms, she burrowed her sideways-seated body further inward.

Pressing herself more deeply against Roswaal's body, Ram closed her eyes as if savoring his warmth. A large palm gently stroked her pink hair.

"Now then, Ram, the matter at hand. — What of the possibility that he's a spy?"

His tone unchanged, Roswaal asked without letting his smile slip. The question had no subject, but she knew exactly what answer he wanted.

Eyes still closed, Ram called to mind the face of a man quite different from the warmth before her, and said—

"I can't rule it out, but I'd say that possibility is fairly weak."

"Hmm, and your reasoning is?"

"For better or worse — or rather, especially for worse, he stands out far too much. The means by which he'd have infiltrated this household, and what came after... to begin with, the man himself is—"

Even as she trailed off, the answer came bluntly enough.

Though her words were a denial of the possibility he'd raised, Roswaal smiled as if satisfied with the answer — the smile of a man who'd had his own thinking confirmed. Even though it wasn't directed straight at her, Ram was aware of her own cheeks growing warm.

"I see, that's convincing enough. In that case, is he truly just a well-meaning third party, then?"

As he spoke, the chair creaked as Roswaal turned his body around — from facing the desk head-on to the exact opposite direction, toward the great window where the moonlight shone brilliantly.

His mismatched eyes narrowed at the sight below, the corner of his mouth still loosened into a smile.

"Sti-ill, he doesn't give up, does he-e."

What could be seen from the study window was the garden within the manor grounds. Surrounded by a somewhat tall fence and trees, the spot couldn't be seen from outside, but it was in exchange extremely visible from the manor's windows.

At the edge of the garden, bathed lavishly in that moonlight, a silver-haired girl and a black-haired boy could be seen chatting together.

As ever, it seemed to be the boy doing all the talking one-sidedly, but the girl's expression never once tilted toward displeasure in response.

"It's a heartwarming thing. That sort of passion is something I can no longer hold onto myself."

"A woman's rather pleased when a man chases after her like that, you know."

Answering what might have been meant as a mere murmur to himself, Ram opened her eyes and noticed her master looking down at her from close by.

Her lips trembling faintly, Ram met his gaze with moist eyes. But—

"Could it be that you actually think rather highly of Subaru-kun?"

"...He's utterly hopeless, but I don't think he's a bad sort. As for the work, he's not slow to learn — he simply doesn't know things yet, that's all."

Ram's eyes carried a trace of displeasure. At her somewhat cooled answer, Roswaal gave a vague smile, and the hand that had been combing through her hair traced gently along her cheek.

Answering the expectation of the girl whose eyes wavered as if in a daze, Roswaal quietly closed his own eyes and turned her answer over in his mind.

It was rare for Ram to appraise another person like this.

Unspoken, she was suggesting that given the chance to learn, he'd grow further still. The black-haired boy must have found real favor with the two of them.

That fact, plainly, pleased Roswaal.

"In my position, I suppose I ought to be getting in the way of it-t."

Looking down at the garden with only his yellow eye, Roswaal let that slip. To which—

"They're both still children, so nothing will happen even if you leave them be."

"That much is true."

Faint laughter overlapped in the study, and the curtain was drawn over the window that had looked down on the boy and girl's rendezvous.

—What happened in the study after that, not even the moon was permitted to see.

* * *

At the hour when the moon sat smugly at the center of the sky, as if it owned the place, Subaru was psyching himself up.

He smoothed the wrinkles from the butler's uniform he'd pulled on, checking his appearance once more in the reflection of the window. He was heading into his fourth day wearing it now, and he figured he was starting to get used to the outfit.

"Not bad, not bad at all, me. It's fine, I can do this. You always look fifty percent more handsome in the mirror right after a bath. I've got a feeling that phenomenon's kicking in right now."

Whether he was objectively fifty percent more handsome was anyone's guess, but self-suggestion mattered plenty too.

Wrapped in the mere atmosphere of good looks if nothing else, Subaru took a light breath and stepped forward.

Treading over the neatly trimmed garden lawn, he headed for a corner of green — a spot ringed by tall trees, receiving the moon's blessing more strongly than anywhere else.

There sat a girl, her silver hair glittering in the moonlight, wrapped in a pale glow.

A bluish-white radiance — Subaru now knew that the true nature of this phenomenon, so like the glow of fireflies, was "spirits."

Knowing that fact and all, the fantastical sight still held a demonic charm that seized the heart of anyone who looked and refused to let go.

He stopped in his tracks despite himself, catching his breath. Perhaps sensing his presence, the girl — who had been murmuring with her eyes closed — opened them.

A pair of amethyst eyes turned forward, catching Subaru as he approached within their sight.

Spotted before he could even call out, Subaru's heart skipped a touch faster. As if to keep that fluster from showing, he raised a hand and said—

"Y-yo. Fancy meeting you here, huh?"

"You say that, but you barge into my routine every single morning. And 'fancy meeting you'... we live under the same roof, you know?"

At an opening line that had clearly given up on hiding his fluster, Emilia fell into what was by now a familiar rhythm, starting with a sigh.

Faced with her attitude, Subaru drew a sharp breath, psyched himself back up with an "Alright," and said—

"'Under the same roof' — saying it out loud like that kind of gets me all fired up, you know?"

"You say that sometimes, but what even is 'fired up'? It's got this reaaally creepy ring to it, like it makes my back crawl."

Emilia hugged her own shoulders and shivered as if she'd caught a chill. Subaru broke into a grin at the gesture and settled down beside her, a spot that had by now become a matter of course. The gap between them was a mere three fists' width — a subtle distance that stood as proof of his spinelessness.

Whether she'd simply grown used to sitting beside him like this, Emilia no longer bothered pointing it out at this stage.

With him joining her side every morning for her routine and every meal besides, it was only natural she'd lost any will to remark on it.

That silent acceptance of hers made him unbearably happy inside. Naturally, then, Subaru's voice afterward came out with an uncontainable joy seeping out at the edges.

"So, so, whatcha doing? Whatcha doing?"

"You're like a little kid. ...I'm just carrying on my morning routine. I can meet most of the little ones in the morning, but there are some I can only meet during meibi."

Subaru nodded in understanding at Emilia's answer.

"Meibi" — "dark-day" — was an expression of time particular to this world, referring roughly to the twelve hours from six in the evening to six the following morning. Conversely, the span from six in the morning to six in the evening was called "yōbi" — "sun-day" — and these terms were commonly used in place of the AM/PM divisions of his original world.

Incidentally, a day here still ran to twenty-four hours, much the same as his original world. Human activity hours didn't seem to differ either, which came as a relief to Subaru — at least his body clock wouldn't be thrown off.

There apparently were even finer classifications beyond that, but he hadn't yet heard the details. His senpai, who favored a spartan approach to education, prioritized teaching him the actual work over that sort of thing. She must really want to make her own life easier.

In his heart, a litany of grievances over four days of spartan training began to spill out. But even in the midst of that, Emilia's conversation with her meibi-exclusive friends was still ongoing.

Friends you could only meet at night — somehow that had a suspicious ring to it.

Feeling a strange embarrassment at his own thoughts, Subaru rubbed his nose. Still watching over Emilia's quiet chat with the spirits, he murmured—

"Kinda looks like will-o'-the-wisps or something."

The spirits, gentle until then, suddenly sprang into brisk motion, spreading out to surround the face of Subaru sitting beside Emilia — and a sharp pain abruptly needled all across his face.

"Bwuh! Ow ow ow ow! It's like getting jabbed with toothpicks, it hurts!"

"Don't go saying careless things and make them angry. They'll stop listening to me too, you know."

Whether she sympathized with the angry spirits' feelings or not, Emilia's attitude toward Subaru, under concentrated attack, was distinctly cool.

Though the fact that she casually cast healing magic on him afterward, once the spirits had withdrawn and he was still in pain, muttering "well, it can't be helped," did speak to her good nature.

His pain eased by the pale glow, Subaru, still sprawled where he'd fallen, glanced up at Emilia.

Before him as he lay on the lawn, Emilia was still carrying on her fantastical conversation from before.

Suddenly Subaru realized he was gazing, entranced, at the sight of the beautiful girl performing alongside the spirits.

That had been unbearably itchy somehow, which was why he'd blurted out that flippant remark earlier — but if he simply let his mind go blank, there was nothing to it at all.

The itch vanished into the distance, and with his head and heart both empty, all he had to do was wear a fool's expression before the scene unfolding in front of him.

"It's probably not much fun to watch, is it?"

Perhaps finding Subaru's silence unusual, it was Emilia who suddenly let that slip.

At the faintly apologetic note in the voice of the spirit-wrapped girl, Subaru sat up and waved a hand lightly. "Nah," he said—

"There's no way I'd ever find it boring, being with Emilia-tan."

"Wha—"

At words far too straightforward, Emilia's breath caught and her face flushed red despite herself. Watching her, Subaru too, replaying what he'd just said, went red to the ears.

If he'd said it on purpose, that'd be one thing, but that line just now had come out completely sober, unguarded.

No doubt it was his true feelings, but for it to go leaping out on its own without so much as waiting for his heart to brace itself — that was going rogue if anything was.

A silence abruptly fell between the two of them. Embarrassed by it, Subaru raised his voice with a deliberately loud "A-ah—" before saying—

"Besides, look, we haven't really had the chance to talk properly these past few days, right?"

"Right, that's right. It must've been hard for you, learning the work here at the manor. Yeah, you've been working really hard... yeah, really hard... haven't you."

"The way you're trying to back me up there is so nice and so pathetic at once that I could cry, uu."

He'd dug his own grave with a topic meant to smooth over the mood, and found himself tweeting out his sorrow without meaning to.

As Subaru, mortified at his own pitifulness, began tracing the character "no" in the dirt, Emilia looked on awkwardly, her hand wandering as she agonized over how to comfort him.

Over these four days, Subaru's reputation as he began his new life as a servant, even with a heavy dose of favoritism, generous euphemism, and no small amount of bribes and under-the-table gifts to the higher-ups, amounted to little more than "utterly useless" at best.

Possessing not a single one of the household skills — cooking, laundry, cleaning — Subaru found himself first of all chased down by the need to acquire these essential servant skills of the manor.

As things stood, all three of those skills had earned him a uniform grade of "C."

"The only time I got an 'S' rank was when I re-sewed a button back onto a frayed apron."

"You really are only outstandingly skilled in that one narrow area, huh."

"Well, I was raised on 'be a sharp, pointy guy, not some round, flat, boring one.'"

The fruit of his parents' unplanned, hands-off approach to child-rearing.

That it had produced a knack for sewing of all things was, even by his own admission, a bit much.

"Oh, but I've got confidence in my bed-making skills. Back in my first year of high school, when I dreamed of becoming a hotel man, I burned through half my summer vacation practicing it."

"Could it be that's why my bed's been so perfectly crisp these past three days...?"

"I made it with my whole heart, pouring in more love than anything."

So much so that he'd choked up with emotion muttering things like, "This is the room where Emilia-tan sleeps, this is the bed Emilia-tan sleeps in. And the futon and sheets that wrap around her! Muhaa!" — putting in so much heartfelt sincerity that Ram had visibly recoiled.

He'd put in so much effort, in fact, that a time limit had been imposed on bed-making just for Emilia's room alone.

"I see, is that so. I'm glad. Glad there's something you can feel confident about, Subaru."

Unaware of that little episode entirely, Emilia smiled happily at the fact that there was work Subaru could do with total confidence.

Weeping tears of blood as his conscience was stabbed a hundred times over, Subaru kept silent and said nothing. Emilia, prefacing with "besides," went on—

"You're doing great, not giving up on the other work either. Ram and Rem were secretly praising you too, you know?"

"Seriously!? So my senpai are going soft when I'm not looking — that's some serious tsundere-ation going on! But if you're doing it right, it's gotta be in front of me, or what's the point!"

Get the usage wrong, and tsundere behavior gets mistaken for simple contempt easily enough. It's a double-edged sword you can't recommend when the other party's an amateur.

On that point, though, to a man like Subaru — who'd covered the full breadth of what tsundere entailed through his own numerous romantic history (in virtual space) — someone tsundere-ing right in front of him was as good as synonymous with "fish on."

Though in the eyes of the twins in question, all they likely saw was a pervert of a noble.

"Well, my eyes don't see anything but Emilia-tan's figure either, so I guess we're even."

He shrugged, playing at nihilism, but even he found that last line too embarrassing, and it came out barely more than a mumble. It seemed not to have reached Emilia at all, then, as she tilted her head with a faint question mark and changed the subject.

"But isn't it hard, every day?"

"Super hard, seriously rough. I wanna sleep on a rotating selection of Emilia-tan's arm, chest, and lap!"

"Yes, yes."

Emilia's answer brushed him off flatly. She simply stretched out her arm as she was, letting the pale blue-white spirits wrap around it.

At the sight, as if her white arm itself were glowing, Subaru let out a vacant "Whoaaa" of admiration despite himself. She gave a wry smile at that—

"As long as you're still able to joke around like that, I guess you're doing fine."

"If I ever look like I'm about to break, come sleep next to me, okay?"

"'I'll think about it'... is a scary thing to say, so I'll just say no."

"Damn, missed my chance to pin that down!!"

He clutched his head and threw himself dramatically back onto the lawn.

He lay there, sighing at the cool feel of the grass and the star-filled sky above. A sudden gust of wind stirred his short bangs, and on a whim Subaru found himself gazing at the moon, and said—

"—The moon is beautiful, isn't it."

"It's something out of reach, isn't it."

"Guh—"

"What's wrong!?"

His amore, delivered with all the flourish of pure literary expression, had been shot down by an unintended remark.

As Subaru clutched his chest, groaning in pain, Emilia hurried anxiously to his side.

And then—

"Ah..."

As she drew close, Emilia's voice caught.

Still lying down, he looked up at her, and saw that her violet-blue eyes were fixed on his chest — on the hand he'd pressed there for the sake of the pained gesture.

On his left hand, covered in bandages from a stack of failures at work.

"Oh, whoops, that's not cool. Effort's supposed to stay hidden, huh."

Hiding his embarrassment, Subaru swept his hand behind his back and touched his head with the other, giving a sheepish "tehe" as a sign of contrition. But Emilia's expression stayed serious.

As Subaru wondered what to make of it, waiting on her reaction, Emilia lowered her eyes slightly and—

"I suppose everyone really does have it hard, don't they."

— he heard her murmur that, as if chiding herself.

Hearing that murmured aside of hers, Subaru gave a quiet "ah" of understanding.

He wasn't the only one learning something from scratch here at Roswaal Manor right now. Emilia too was, as a royal candidate, in the midst of absorbing all manner of things she needed to learn.

A single servant, and the queen of a nation. The level demanded of each, and its scope, were bound to carry a weight so disproportionate that comparing them would almost be rude.

Carrying something that heavy, there were bound to be times she grew tired. Perhaps she too was holding onto worries she could tell no one.

Emilia's hand suddenly reached out, and Subaru's reaction came a beat too late. Her hand took hold of the arm he'd swept behind his back and drew it up before her own eyes.

Looking at fingers covered in wounds and the marks of their treatment, Emilia narrowed her eyes as if in pain, and—

"...Should I cast healing magic on it for you?"

— she asked.

The question was gentle, and the warmth of being tended to mid-treatment was hard to put into words. The newborn wounds, small as they were, still cried out their first cries, even now protesting with a faint sting of heat whenever he paid them mind. And yet—

"Nah, it's fine, you don't have to heal it."

"Why not?"

"Hmm, it's kind of hard to put into words, but... yeah. It's because this is proof of how hard I worked."

Gently freeing his hand from her grip, Subaru answered with a broad grin.

Before her beautiful, wide-eyed face, he clenched that battered hand tightly, and said—

"Turns out I don't actually hate hard work, believe it or not. Otherwise I wouldn't keep up muscle training every day. Turning can't into can — that's pure ec-sta-sy."

He swung his hips to the rhythm, spinning as he rose to his feet. With his gaze suddenly rising higher, Emilia's eyes scrambled to follow him — and he pointed a finger straight at them.

"It's hard, and it's seriously rough, y'know? But it's actually pretty fun. Ram and Rem are surprisingly spartan about it, that loli's infuriating, and Roz doesn't say much of anything, so he's got less presence than I expected."

"If you said that to Roswaal's face, he'd probably blow his top."

"'Blow his top,' nobody says that these days..."

He expressed having his train of thought cut off by bending at the waist. Then, springing his spine straight again like a wind-up doll, he thrust out a palm with a "shwip" to keep Emilia in check, and—

"Well, clearing problems one by one like that isn't so bad. Here, I've got no choice but to do it if I want to survive... and if that's how it's gonna be, might as well have fun with it, right?"

In his original world, living "easy" was all he'd asked for. But in this world, that kind of untroubled life wasn't something he could hope for.

So if that was the case, Subaru wanted to demand at least some measure of "fun." It was, in a way, his own stubborn pride against whatever had thrown him unreasonably into this world.

At Subaru's declaration of resolve, Emilia froze as if time itself had stopped. Only her eyelids kept opening and closing over and over, and then, all at once, her expression crumbled.

"That's... right. Yeah, I think so too. Ugh, geez, you idiot, Subaru."

"Wait, huh, isn't that reaction kinda off!? This is the part where you're supposed to fall for me all over again, isn't it!?"

"I wasn't in love with you to begin with. Honestly, you're such an idiot... me too."

Subaru, spelling out "outrage" with his body as a human character, didn't catch that last murmur of Emilia's.

She too brushed off the hem of her skirt where she'd been sitting, rose to her feet, and smiled before Subaru.

At her smile, as if some part of the weight pressing on her until just now had lifted, Subaru could say nothing, only stare, entranced.

When Emilia looked like this, she became something words like "pretty" or "cute" couldn't begin to capture. If he had to put it into words—

"E·M·M (Emilia-tan · Majorly · a Goddess)"

"I'm trying to thank you and you go and joke around again."

Poked on the forehead, just a little angrily, Subaru clutched the spot and backed away. That it felt strangely hot there was, surely, no mere trick of the mind.

At Subaru's reaction, she burst into a bright, satisfying peal of laughter.

"Still though... I know you're working hard, but how exactly did your hands end up this beaten up?"

"Ah, that one's simple. This evening, when I went along with Ram to do some shopping in the village near the manor, I got chomped hard by some little critter the local kids were playing with."

"That wasn't the fruit of your hard work after all!?"

"Guess you could say the bigger injury just covered it up... I'm not usually the type animals hate that much, though."

Back in his original world, he wasn't the sort to get murderous intent from small animals like that. Otherwise how could he ever have called himself a leading authority among fluff enthusiasts?

That said, the constitution he'd displayed without fail even in his original world — of being either wildly liked by children or completely walked over by them — was alive and well here too.

"Those little brats... kicking me without mercy, punching me, wiping their snot on me. It was the worst, damn it. Just you wait till tomorrow..."

"You do seem like you'd be good at looking after little kids, Subaru."

"That's a total misread, Emilia-tan. I'm just softening them up nicely now so I can reap the rewards once they grow up. Call it my Hikaru Genji plan."

"You don't have to play the villain — you could just admit it honestly."

Emilia, not taking the bait, said "well then," looked up at the sky, and stretched her back.

"I should probably head back to my room soon. What about you, Subaru?"

"Gotta go tuck Emilia-tan in, so I'll head back too."

"That particular job description will have to wait until you've polished your skills as a servant a lot more."

"Whoaaa, dreams expa-a-anding!"

Emilia watched with a small wry smile as Subaru raised both hands and howled at the heavens.

Then Subaru twisted at the waist and turned back to Emilia, and—

"Oh right. If you're up for it, wanna come with me tomorrow to get revenge on those brats... I mean, on a lovey-dovey date... I mean, to go see some cute little animals?"

"You corrected yourself quite a few times there. ...But, um, well, I—"

Emilia trailed off, unable to get the rest of the sentence out.

Subaru tilted his head at her reaction. It didn't feel like the sort of reaction where she just didn't want to go. In that case—

"D-don't tell me it's like, 'going together would get me talked about by friends, and that's embarrassing'... or something?"

"I wouldn't give such an awful excuse to turn you down!"

She set her hands on her hips in an angry gesture — the sort you'd give a "hmph!" sound effect to — and then lowered her eyes once more.

"It's not that I don't want to, and I am curious about the little animal, but..."

"Then let's go!"

"But if I come along, it might be a bother for you, Subaru..."

"Alright, got it, let's go!"

"...Are you actually listening?"

"I'm listening! There's no way I'd miss a single word out of Emilia-tan's mouth!"

"I hate you, Subaru."

"Ahhh! Ahhhh! What's with that all of a sudden!? I ca-a-a-n't he-e-ear you!!"

He clapped his hands over his ears and ran in circles, blocking out the sound.

At the sheer decisiveness with which he instantly retracted his own words, Emilia laughed, all the fight drained out of her. Then, prefacing with "well, I suppose there's no helping it," she said—

"On the condition that my studying reaches a stopping point, and you've properly finished your work at the manor, okay?"

"Yes! Roger that! I'll get it done in record time!"

Having secured his date, Subaru answered with a firm fist-pump.

Seeing how transparently pleased he was, Emilia dropped her shoulders and shook her head.

"Watching you, Subaru, makes me feel like my own worries are kind of small."

"That's not true at all!? If everyone was carrying around worries on the scale of maybe-becoming-queen, this'd be one stressed-out society with everyone's stomachs full of holes!"

Emilia finally couldn't hold it in and burst out laughing. Drawn along by her laughter, Subaru began to laugh too.

The two of them laughed together like that for a good while, and their rendezvous that day came to a close.

Lastly—

"Come to think of it, why are you still dressed like that even after work's done?"

"Ah, well, it hit me that I hadn't actually gotten a proper opinion from Emilia-tan on this getup. So, how about it? Pretty sharp, right?"

"Yeah, I guess so. You look like you could really get work done."

"That expectation's so heavy I might get crushed, uu!"

Let it be recorded here that such an exchange took place.

* * *

"Yoo-hoo, you sleeping properly, loli-girl? Stay up too late and your growth hormones won't kick in, and you'll stay tiny forever."

"...You've started breaking the Door Crossing like it's nothing now, I suppose."

To Subaru, who'd picked a door at random, peeked inside, and called out some equally random greeting, that resentful reply came back.

Looking in, he found a girl settled on a wooden step-stool at the back of the library, glaring at him. She held a thick book in her hands, open somewhere around the middle.

"Keep reading manga in the dark at night like that and you'll end up wearing glasses thick as the bottom of a milk bottle, and flunk your college entrance exams big time."

"More nonsense I can't make heads or tails of... Keep fooling around and I'll turn you to stone, I suppose."

"Yeah, yeah, understood, understood. Well then, get to bed already. G'night."

He gave a perfunctory wave and moved to shut the door when a voice called out, "Hold on." Subaru stopped his hand mid-close and turned back, and Beatrice, leaning slightly forward from atop the step-stool, said—

"Didn't you come here because you had some business, I wonder?"

"Nah, not really. I was just gonna say goodnight since I'm heading to bed. I figured I'd give up if I hadn't found you after opening like three doors, but I got you on the first try."

"What kind of intuition does this guy even run on, honestly..."

Beatrice tugged wearily at one of her ringlets. The curl stretched out with a *boing* and, released, bounced back again and again. Watching it stirred something childlike in him just a little.

"Can I do that?"

"The only one allowed to touch Betty is Onii-cha, I suppose. That's enough, now hurry up and disappear, I suppose."

"What, you call me back just for that? Ha, well, fine by me! I'm in a good mood right now, so I'll totally let it slide!"

Making a show of his good spirits, he left the room after carving a scowl onto Beatrice's face. Just as the door was closing, though—

"—It's got nothing to do with Betty, I suppose."

— was all that seemed to reach him, in a voice that sounded faintly lonely, and it left him a little uneasy.

Or so he thought, and when he opened the door again to ask her about it—

— what lay beyond the opened door had changed entirely, reverting to nothing more than an ordinary guest room.

He felt a slight tug of reluctance, but he had no mind to go wandering around searching again. Tomorrow he'd need to wake early and get through his work as fast as he could, after all.

"Gotta head to the village, come up with some excuse, and win those brats over. Oh, but before that I need to scope out good vantage points around there, where the flower fields are... Prep work, that's what matters in any battle! Victory or defeat is decided before the fight even starts, so I've got this."

He flared his nostrils, his chest swelling with anticipation for tomorrow.

Practically skipping back to the room assigned to him — a private servant's chamber — he shed the butler's uniform he'd been wearing and swiftly transformed into his tracksuit.

"Yeah, this is the stuff for sleeping, no question. Kinda nerve-wracking only having the one, though... wonder if I asked Roz nicely, he'd cast a duplication spell on it or something."

It was by now a rare item, the only one of its kind left in existence in this whole world. And yet, for all the utmost care he took handling it, its preciousness only amounted to the casual sort you'd wear as everyday clothes.

If it ever wore out, then that would simply be the garment's natural end. Losing this last trace of his original world would be sad, but there was no point dwelling on it.

"That's why I live painting hope onto tomorrow, guhuhu."

Smirking crudely to himself, Subaru dove into the bed he'd made himself. Caught by its soft embrace, he burrowed under the covers, ready for sleep.

But his anticipation for tomorrow was so great that his eyes stayed wide awake, and no matter what he tried, he simply couldn't drift off.

"Damn it, what the hell, my own body! Are you trying to trample all over my hopes for tomorrow!? Damn it all, so my greatest enemy was myself this whole time..."

"Jeez-us," Subaru groaned, glaring up at the ceiling.

But on a sleepless night like this, there was a secret technique. Subaru smiled mockingly at his own overheated body, and said—

"Behold my secret technique — one Puck fluff, two Puck fluffs!"

For a first-rate fluff enthusiast, picturing the object of one's fluffing in the imagination makes it possible to experience virtual fluffing as vividly as reality.

A gray, fluffy cat appeared in Subaru's mind's eye, and with each passing second its numbers multiplied. Little by little his whole body was wrapped in these fluff-balls, his flesh carried off beyond bliss and self-forgetting — to a distant utopia, an Avalon of his own.

"Puck... one hundred sixty... three fluffs... fluff fluff."

Murmuring, Subaru's consciousness sank down into the world of dreams.

Still picturing his utopia, his awareness arrived at the far end of the fluffing — and, at last, faded away.

* * *

That the awakening of consciousness resembles rising up to the surface of the sea is a joke he's already used once.

Opening his eyes, his sight scorched by the sunlight, Subaru pulled his faintly sluggish body upright.

His head felt oddly heavy, somehow. Maybe some fatigue still lingered. He'd only just started unfamiliar work — this sort of thing was bound to happen.

But today was no day to be saying weak-kneed things like that.

Subaru, a good waker, was already turning last night's promise over firmly in his mind the moment he came to.

"That's right, Natsuki Subaru — today, I become a man!"

He gave a thumbs-up to no one in particular, teeth gleaming in his signature triumphant pose.

The perfect wake-up to mark the start of a day of promised victory, one bound to turn out splendid. But—

— it was snapped in half by the gazes of the twins, watching him with looks of surprise.

Covering his face, pressing it into the futon, Subaru shook his body back and forth, no-no-no—

"Wha-a-at is it! You were there!? That's embarrassing, say something! Ugh, ugh, ugh, seriously so embarrassing!"

Subaru thrashed about, trying to kill his own embarrassment.

But against him, the twins' expressions remained, as ever, scarcely changed. Being pointed at and laughed at would've been bad enough, but this lack of any reaction felt like his joke had bombed, and that was somehow worse.

"No, wait a second, you two. That reaction actually hurts, you know. You just touched on someone's sensitive spot, so there ought to be a little more... something, right!?"

At Subaru's desperate plea, the two of them exchanged a look.

He hoped, at the very least, for the usual sort of cold or unimpressed jab in return. — Which, he thought to himself, was a pretty awful thing to hope for too. And it was right after that thought that—

"Sister, sister, we seem to have been greeted rather familiarly, somehow."
"Rem, Rem, we seem to have been greeted awfully over-familiarly, somehow."

Words that brushed past Subaru's mind carrying a sense of wrongness.

The curtness of the two was certainly their usual selves — and yet something about it felt off.

"Uh, hang on? Something's weird, isn't it? What's up, senpai? If this is you two coordinating a bit, it doesn't really fit the occasion, or something."

Even as he spoke, Subaru was starting to realize what was causing that sense of wrongness.

—Their eyes.

The gazes with which the two looked at Subaru — they had lost the familiarity of last night, and shifted into something distant, formal, like strangers.

And what settled it beyond doubt was—

"Sister, sister, it seems our guest here is a little confused."
"Rem, Rem, it seems something's gone strange in our guest's head."

—Called "our guest," Subaru found himself struck speechless.

For all the elegance of its ring, the word carved into the inside of Subaru's heart with a fierce, razor-sharp edge. He actually mistook it for physical pain and clutched his chest.

He couldn't make sense of it. Their reaction was like—

"Both of you... haha, that's a rough joke. That... kind of thing..."

Wanting to shut out the eyes of the two, forever looking at him like a stranger, Subaru reflexively raised his left hand to block his own sight. But in that instant, he saw something decisive.

—From his own left hand, the bandages were gone.

The fingertips roughened from housework in water, the back of his hand cut from unfamiliar knife work, the bite mark from the little animal amid his roughhousing with the children — all of it, wiped clean.

With things having come to this, even Subaru had no choice but to admit the reality of it.

Abruptly, suddenly, he felt something hot welling up behind his eyes, and Subaru pressed his face into the futon again — for a different reason than before.

He absolutely, positively did not want anyone to see his face right now.

Not these people he loved so much. Not the people he'd been on the verge of coming to love. Not the people he should have already come to love.

He didn't want to shed a single tear, not while being looked at with eyes like a stranger's.

"Why... did it go back!?"

He wailed. Face still pressed into the futon, he bared his teeth at some incomprehensible injustice, and simply howled out his fury.

—It was the moment the loop that had tormented Subaru so relentlessly drew him back into its whirlpool once more.

The second — first day — begins...
