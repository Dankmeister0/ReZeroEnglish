Arc 2 Chapter 5: "Emotions Realized"

Emilia let out a breath and looked down at Subaru, who was prostrate on the ground, then touched a finger to her lips as though remembering something. "Oh, right."

That gesture, one she made from time to time, was strangely childlike and endearing for her.

As Subaru bounced back up onto his feet, Emilia moved the finger she'd had at her lips over toward her own eyes.

"Still, never having seen a Spirit before—is your Mana use unstable or something?"

"Mana use... huh."

"Focusing it into your eyes is pretty basic, isn't it? Something that simple should be—"

"Lia. It's not that simple. It's just that everyone you've met so far has been unusually skilled with Mana, for a human. Well, aside from that, Subaru's circumstances are a *little* bit special too."

"Special?" Emilia tilted her head, and Puck crossed his arms and nodded.

"From what I can see flickering around, Subaru's Gate isn't opening properly. Even setting aside Spiritual Arts or Magic, it's like—way too closed. That level."

"Isn't that strange? That shouldn't happen if you've lived a normal life, should it?"

"Yeah, that's exactly why it's strange. Guess he hasn't lived a normal life."

The two of them locked eyes, nodded to each other, and turned to look at Subaru in perfect unison. Facing their combined gaze, Subaru said,

"A world just for the two of you that I hesitate to interrupt... jealous."

"W-well, I do get the sense that you're not normal."

"The definition of 'normal' is under assault! I mean, looking at a guy's face and going 'normal' or 'not normal'—that's pretty rude, don't you think? Your daughter over here, what's the deal with her?"

"I'm so happy she grew up to be an honest girl without a shred of duplicity."

"Behold, the pinnacle of a doting cat-parent!"

Subaru covered his face with his palm at Puck's attitude—doting on his daughter well past reasonable limits. Watching that overreaction, Emilia said,

"Somehow, you two seem to get along *really* well."

She muttered this while glancing back and forth between them with narrowed eyes.

At that remark, Subaru and Puck looked at each other, nodded, and pressed their palms together (Puck more like slamming his whole body against the palm),

"Yup, super close. Feels like our wavelengths match. Wanna aim for the top of the comedy world together with me!?"

"Say what you like, but you're just after my body, aren't you."

"Love and petting are two separate matters. But I'm sure, in the end, I'll always come back to you. Cut a man a little slack for his wandering heart, pet pet pet."

The banter was interrupted by the sensation of palm on fur. Emilia, seeing Subaru petting Puck with a blissful expression, must have given up—this time for good—on continuing the conversation. She entrusted Puck to Subaru completely.

"Puck, go play with Subaru over there. I have my own business to attend to, keeping the Spirits in good humor. Honestly, don't get in my way."

"Cast aside."
"Cast aside indeed."

The two of them shrugged theatrically, and Emilia silently ignored them. She hurriedly put some distance between them and immersed herself once more in what seemed to be a continuation of her earlier activity with the Spirits she'd gathered.

The sight of her speaking to the Spirits was, as before, a fantastical scene. It was precisely because he'd wanted a taste of that solemnity that Subaru had acted the way he had.

"So it's not like that was my actual intention. Just so you know."

"Being 'just so you know'd by me doesn't do much. Well, but if I keep this up I'll get scolded, so let's hold back. This is the middle of an important ritual. As a Spirit, I can't just overlook it anymore."

"For someone saying that, you were pretty into interrupting it yourself just now. Anyway, what exactly was that all about?"

"The ritual of Contracting with a Spirit. —Fulfillment of an Oath."

Faced with a barrage of unfamiliar terms, Subaru responded with a baffled scowl. Puck, seeming to choose his words carefully in response, prefaced with an "um" before continuing.

"First, a Spirit user can't use Spiritual Arts without Contracting with a Spirit. And the terms of the Contract differ depending on the Spirit. Following so far?"

"Like how interest rates and collateral vary by moneylender. Okay."

"I'm not okay with that comparison, but let's move on. What the Spirit asks for differs from one to the next... but when the other party is one of those minor Spirits like that, it's usually something like what you just saw—physical contact mediated by the caster's own Mana. That kind of thing tends to secure their cooperation fairly easily."

"Sounds pretty low-effort. Though, the way you're putting it, a proper Spirit is different, huh? How about it, Mr. Spirit?"

"It's a relief when a smart kid catches on quick. ...Though when they're too clever, they also tend to go off on unnecessary tangents and the conversation doesn't move forward."

Subaru gave an awkward "heh," scratching his head in embarrassment. Puck gave him a lukewarm look while fiddling with his own whiskers.

"Just like Subaru said, Spirits with wills of their own, myself included, have somewhat stricter requirements. Though in exchange, I intend to contribute that much more to my caster. I may not look it, but I'm a fairly powerful Spirit myself, so the Oath I've placed on Lia is a strict one."

"I've been meaning to say, that nickname 'Lia' is pretty cute."

"Can't beat your 'Emilia-tan,' though. Maybe I should start calling her that too."

"—Please, for the love of god, don't."

Puck egged on Subaru's tangent with mischievous relish, and Emilia cut in with an angry expression.

Looking closer, the Spirit's glow had vanished from around her. It seemed the Spirit talk show had wrapped up.

Subaru stood, brushing grass off his backside.

"Is the get-together over already? That went by pretty easy, all things considered."

"It's because you two interrupted and threw off my concentration that I had to cut it short. Even from a distance, you manage to grate on people's nerves... it's practically a talent."

"Two people who should never have met, met... something like that. I have a feeling something's about to begin. My chest is pounding with excitement, it won't stop."

He beat his own chest, tapping out a rhythm. Emilia held out her palm, and Puck, leaving his perch under Subaru, landed on it.

He looked up at Emilia with round black eyes, and with a knowing smile, said,

"It's fine. From what I could sense, there's no malice, hostility, or ill intent in Subaru. His personality's a bit twisted, but he's a good kid."

"H-hey..."

Emilia was rendered speechless by Puck's less-than-flattering assessment.

Glancing uneasily at Subaru, she jabbed at Puck in a flustered tone.

"Why would you say that in front of him... and that kind of evaluation, too. Even if it's true, it still hurts to hear, doesn't it?"

"Ah, it's fine, it's fine. Poking around to check is only natural, right? A nice guy of unknown origin like me, of course you'd be suspicious. ...Though Emilia-tan's follow-up just now really did hurt!"

Emilia's hand flew to cover her mouth, and Subaru gave a wry smile at her unguarded reaction.

That Puck, in touching him, had been probing him—that much Subaru had already guessed.

They weren't careless enough to accept Subaru, who hadn't offered up a single solid piece of information this whole time, without some kind of vigilance. Even Ram and Rem's abrupt appearance earlier was likely a feat only possible because someone had been keeping watch over Subaru's movements from somewhere.

"Still, I don't have a good way to explain things."

He had his memories but no family registry—that was Subaru's current situation in this world.

Explaining it would be difficult, and there was a high chance he'd be branded insane for trying. If that was the case, he might as well ride on Puck's character judgment instead.

If Puck could read minds to some degree, he'd likely convey Subaru's situation more accurately than Subaru could himself—that had been the calculation behind the earlier exchange.

It had been something of a gamble, but at Subaru's calculation, Puck only laughed.

"It's fine, Lia. I mean, Subaru understands that much himself too. Using my mind-reading like that so shamelessly—what a naughty boy this one is."

"That's a flattering assessment. Keep smoothing things over like that for me, my friend."

At the address, Puck blinked in mild surprise. Then he burst out laughing, thoroughly delighted.

"It really has been a while since I was treated like that. Yeah, I like it."

Subaru sensed something meaningful in Puck's pleased attitude, but before he could remark on it, Puck floated up in front of Emilia's face.

Then, pressing his forehead to hers as she closed her eyes, he created a strangely surreal scene.

"Take thiiiis! Puck-style ultimate secret technique: 'Cat Forehead Impact'!!"

Left out by the silent pair, Subaru felt lonely, and did his own play-by-play commentary to stave off the loneliness. But the two of them, deep in concentration, gave no reaction at all, only deepening his isolation.

"Damn, how powerless am I... I can't think of any more moves to grab their attention. I'm—shedding a tear."

"...Yeah, got it. Huh, so Subaru actually thinks about a lot of things... wait, why are you sitting seiza and weeping pitifully?"

"Don't wor-ry a-bout it! So, what was that just now?"

"It's like a shared consciousness, sort of. Rather than explaining it verbally, it's faster if you just feel what I felt directly, right?"

Answering Subaru's question concisely, Puck returned to his usual spot beside the silver hair. Emilia raised a hand to touch Puck perched on her shoulder and nodded, saying, "So that's how it is."

"It was through Puck, but I understand about Subaru now. ...I'm sorry for doubting you. Even knowing why, it doesn't feel great, does it?"

"Being probed by a beautiful woman—doesn't that sound kind of exciting, written out like that? Next time, please, instead of Puck, feel free to have Emilia-tan directly rummage through my brain."

He gave a thumbs up, teeth glinting. Emilia was rendered speechless at this. Seeing her reaction, Subaru internally broke into a full sprint of regret—*I did it again.*

His bad habit of getting carried away and spouting nonsense had reared its head. And then—

"No, no, no, I didn't mean it in some perverted way, I mean, that... in our industry, that kind of pain is a reward too."

Botching his own follow-up afterward was also one of Subaru's bad habits.

Subaru could feel cold sweat trickling down along with his strained smile. Whether it was sweat, tears, rain, or drool at this point, some liquid that was three-quarters guaranteed to be off-putting ran down his chin.

Emilia tilted her head slightly at Subaru, now fully committed to a filthy-comedian trajectory.

"—Subaru really is strange."

"Huh?"

"Treating a corporeal Spirit like it's the most natural thing in the world, and on top of that, flirting even with someone like me... a Half-Elf."

She laughed lightly, saying, "It's surprising even as a joke."

*If it weren't a joke, would I be doing something to surprise you like that?* was what Subaru said internally, but he forgot even that, entranced by her smile.

Because that smile was, just as pure and clear as the one he'd seen in that back alley. Far from fleeting, far from sorrowful—the kind that made you happy just to look at it.

—And it was only then, watching her expression, entranced, that Subaru first became conscious of his own feelings.

"Uh-oh."

"Huh?"

"No, I mean, this is like, super rare for me, so... yeah. No, seriously."

His mouth went bone dry, his breathing grew labored, his heartbeat quickened.

His vision began to flicker, and he couldn't hold his face up straight. Heat rose from his neck to his face, and his emotions slipped beyond his control.

"What's wrong, are you okay?"

Emilia, asking after him, looked up from below at Subaru's lowered face. Her upturned expression of concern was so unbearably cute that—

"Whoaaaaaaaaaaah!!"

He let out a battle cry and leapt hard, straight backward, attempting to escape the situation with a backflip.

Upside down, he reached to plant his hands on the ground for an elegant rotation—but his hand slipped on the morning dew, and he dropped straight onto the crown of his head. He slid along the grass on the top of his skull, then collapsed after about a meter.

"Wh-what happened all of a sudden!?"

"Owwwwww, ow, that really hurts. Oh no, is my head—is it going bald, going bald? ...Ah, white."

Emilia rushed to Subaru's side as he rolled on the ground clutching his head, hands fluttering uncertainly over what to do. Looking up at her from below, Subaru made his comment.

For a moment, Emilia's face showed suspicion—then, realizing the meaning, her cheeks flushed crimson in an instant.

With her short skirt, and her standing right in front of Subaru sprawled on the ground, well—

"Force majeure, force majeure! I wasn't aiming for it, I swear!"

"And your honest answer?"

"Thank you for the meal."

He sat seiza and pressed his palms together, bowing his head graciously to the silver-haired goddess.

When he looked up, Emilia smiled at him—unlike her earlier smile, this one carried a measure of sadistic color mixed in.

"Even that face was charming. The end."

"—You need to reflect on what you've done."

The chunk of ice that came falling down was, even accounting for restraint, quite large.

"Ah, ow... is this seriously the start of baldness? Are you okay?"

"It's just a slightly bigger-than-average bump. —I think you should be grateful for my kindness."

Subaru sat cross-legged on the ground, rubbing his head. Half-teary, he faced Emilia, who, still averting her face, responded coldly, clearly displeased.

There were several meters of distance between the two of them, the rift created by his earlier act made manifest as real physical distance.

He stole a glance at her white profile from below. She turned away with a huff.

Glance, huff. Glance, huff. Glance, huff. Glance-glance, huff-huff.

"Stop playing around. I'm angry, you know."

"It wasn't on purpose, I swear, it was just a little mishap that snowballed into self-destruction."

The bump on his head was pronounced enough that, in manga terms, you'd want to slap a bandage on it.

At the moment of impact, sparks had genuinely flown behind his eyes. If he'd been unlucky, it felt like the kind of force that could've dragged him back in front of that vegetable stand all over again.

He was fine now, but right after taking the hit, his consciousness had been genuinely hazy. Incidentally, perhaps sensing she'd overdone it, Emilia's flustered watching-over of the fallen Subaru had been small-animal-like and endearing all over again.

Though getting caught watching that through half-closed eyes had earned him her anger once more.

"You've got a tough personality too, huh. You could stand to take it easier."

"I live easy and cut corners, and look where that's gotten me. Man, I envy your position there."

The object of Subaru's envious gaze was Puck, relaxing on Emilia's lap. Having been palm-sized until just a moment ago, he'd now swollen to about the size of a human toddler, resting his head on her slender lap and enjoying the sensation.

"That kind of lewd feeling has nothing to do with me, you know."

"Shut up, and stop reading people's minds. This is the inside of a teenage boy's head, it's a mess in here. Emilia-tan's no exception."

"If you keep thinking indecent thoughts about my daughter too much, I'll erase you."

"Don't threaten to erase someone with the same casualness as 'I'll get mad'!?"

He said it as a joke, but internally broke into a cold sweat—*he's probably serious.*

Still, Subaru prefaced, watching the two of them,

"Never skipping the morning grooming session, huh. Somehow, from what I've seen so far, Contracts and Oaths with Spirits seem kind of… easy."

"That's not true. Depending on the Spirit, the demands can be much stricter. Things like giving several times the Mana of an ordinary person, or, if the Spirit is combative, one kill a day, that kind of thing."

"Your comparison examples are way too grim... and also, you spoil your daughter way too much."

Compared to that example, Puck's terms were absurdly easy. That family gets favorable treatment in Contracts must be true in any world, in any era. Long live nepotism hires.

Mulling over connections-based hiring in his mind, Subaru, uncharacteristically, gazed in blank silence at Emilia and Puck's interaction.

To begin with, the reason Subaru kept chattering away with unnecessary talk was that he felt pain at silence in a space shared with someone else.

Silence while another person was present—that kind of state gave Subaru a pressure like *"You're keeping quiet because I'm not talking, aren't you."*

Normally he'd be tormented by that troublesome compulsion, but strangely, right now, that self-centered anxiety wasn't welling up anywhere at all.

—Silence, soundlessness, stillness.

Perhaps it was because Subaru could somehow sense that the warm scene before him was, in a way, supported by that very silence.

Puck's fur was scooped up and smoothed down by fingers moving with gentleness. Her expression, perhaps beginning to forget her earlier spat with Subaru, had a profile absorbed in the grooming that carried an earnestness while remaining calm—and that alone was enough to captivate him.

*Ah, my eyesight's completely shot,* Subaru thought.

"Heh heh heh."

The mind-reading cat, saying it out loud while looking at Subaru, was infuriating.

That cat, who could read others' emotions without even touching them—he must have grasped the identity of the indescribably bittersweet feeling occupying Subaru's chest right now.

Unintentionally, his weakness had been seized upon. Of course, it wasn't amusing, but—

—*Even I have to admit, I do have a knack for choosing the thorny path,* he thought.

Her beautifully flowing silver hair was fantastical, like drops of moonlight. Her skin, white as freshly fallen snow, seemed almost translucent, and her violet eyes drew in Subaru's consciousness as though casting some captivating curse. Her slender limbs and delicate frame seemed as though they might snap if embraced—yet Subaru also knew she carried, at her core, a strong will that would never break. Noble, beautiful, an ideal person.

Whatever way you looked at it, of course a green shut-in from another world would fall head over heels.

"Just being here like this... maybe that's a luxury in itself."

The strange emotion that grew murkier the more he thought about it.

Subaru stood up, and, as if to shake it off, began swinging his wooden sword again.

The last time he'd felt an emotion this hard to put into words dated all the way back to elementary school. And in the end, even that feeling, after being dragged along for nearly ten years, had ended without ever being conveyed.

Every time those feelings resurfaced, the speed of his swings grew sharper.

"Begone, worldly desires! Drop dead, regret! I don't care if there's a hundred and eight of you! I'll cut down every last one! Every last one!"

He cut down something unseen, and with each swing came a defiant declaration.

That's right, agonizing over some murky feeling wasn't like him. No agonizing—just clear, decisive, pick a reasonably good answer and go. That was the kind of man Natsuki Subaru was.

"Oh-ho, boys will be boys. Fufu, never gets old watching him."

"Yes, yes, hold still. ...But, really, it's true."

Watching Subaru continue his massacre of inner conflict, the two of them offered such comments.

Watching his rampaging back, Emilia gently loosened her lips into a smile. In that profile of hers, there was no trace of the awkward anger from before.

That he failed to catch this, sharp-eyed as he usually was—that was very much Subaru being Subaru.

—And then,

"Is something wrong? You two?"

At Emilia's voice, having noticed something, Subaru too stopped his practice swings and turned his gaze toward the mansion. Coming out of the mansion with graceful bearing were the twin maids, notable for their pink and blue hair. They bowed at the waist with a composed bearing,

"'Master Roswaal has returned. Please come to the mansion.'"

Perfect stereo, without a single moment's lag between them.

He was impressed by their flawless coordination, but what really struck Subaru was the difference in their demeanor from before.

Unlike earlier, when there had been a lightness to their bantering with Subaru, the two girls standing before him now carried a dignity truly befitting servants of a manor.

In short, they'd switched from private mode to work mode.

"I see. Roswaal's back... Well, I'd better go greet him."

"'Yes. And the guest as well. If he's awake, he should come along too.'"

Puck shrank down and sank into Emilia's silver hair. She stroked her own hair to welcome him in, then stood, her expression settling into something slightly more composed.

Feeling a faint sense of unease at that profile, Subaru, having been named specifically, wiped away his sweat. The blue-haired maid stepped briskly to his side, taking over the task with a cloth pulled from within her clothes.

"Sorry about that."

"Not at all, it is only natural duty as a servant, honored guest."
"Not at all, it's just the natural job of a servant, guest."

Even though it was the blue-haired one doing the work, the pink-haired one puffed up her chest as though it were only natural. Feeling a slight relief at the return of the earlier banter's atmosphere, Subaru turned around.

"So, who's this Roswaal person?"

"The owner of this mansion... oh, right, I never explained, did I."

As though realizing her own oversight, Emilia put a hand to her mouth. Then she furrowed her brow, apparently troubled over how best to answer.

"Um, right. Roswaal is... you'll understand once you meet him."

"That's a quick surrender! Is there nothing distinctive about him at all!?"

"'No, quite the opposite,'" all four voices returned in unison.

At the startling quadruple chorus, Subaru was so rattled he nearly dropped his wooden sword.

The blue-haired maid gave the falling sword a light kick, easily catching it as it spun, then gave a solemn bow and stepped back to stand beside her sister. The pink-haired one then gestured toward the mansion with her hand.

"No matter how we phrase it, it would be pointless. Master Roswaal's character is something the honored guest must understand by meeting him in person. Yes, surely it will be fine."

The pink-haired and blue-haired maids exchanged a glance and nodded to each other, and Emilia, too, reluctantly agreed. To the bewildered Subaru, Emilia gave a light pat on the shoulder as she headed toward the mansion.

"—I'm sure you and Subaru will get along. Though it's kind of a headache to think about."

She murmured this in a tone that made it sound like a genuine burden.
