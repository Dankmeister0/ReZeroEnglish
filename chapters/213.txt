Arc 4 Chapter 47: "An Ill-Matched Opponent"

"It's not so much that we've got bad chemistry—I doubt anyone could have good chemistry with someone in this state!"

Faced with a girl in a state of total restraint, Subaru delivered that heartfelt retort.

The Witch of Gluttony—that was how the figure before him had been introduced.
The girl lying inside the coffin propped at a diagonal was roughly a hundred and fifty centimeters tall. Her grayish hair, falling to about shoulder length, was tied into twin bunches. Fair-skinned and slight of build, with a small chest—no, more that she looked like a child of thirteen or fourteen.

"On top of being locked up tight in restraints, both her eyes are sealed shut too... Given how young she looks, I doubt it, but..."

At that age, everyone dreams of things like "supernatural power beyond the ordinary" or "hidden potential, sealed away by one's own will, that others fear as a threat."
Thinking back, in middle school Subaru himself had been influenced by a manga he was reading at the time, developed a longing to be a hidden-weapon wielder, and had gone around tucking sewing needles into various parts of his school uniform.

"Well, in the end I fell over, got stuck all over with needles, and had to give it up in tears."

Reflecting on that dark chapter of his past—one he'd never had anyone to show off to—Subaru couldn't decide how to act toward the witch before him.
To begin with, up until now it had always been the witches who launched an overwhelming first strike before Subaru could even act.

"—————"

Across from him, the witch in the coffin remained silent, showing no reaction whatsoever.
Believing the opening move was crucial, Subaru—unusually unable to decide his first move with someone he'd just met—found the silence oppressive. If only he knew whether she was friendly or hostile, he could at least choose between coming on familiar or coming on provocative.

"............"

With both sides sizing each other up, silence continued to reign over the tea party on the small hill.
Even so, what gnawed at Subaru was the overwhelming pressure radiating from the witch before him. Her whole body bound immobile, her eyes sealed so she couldn't even look his way—and yet the sense of intimidation this small girl gave off was, one had to admit, worthy of a witch.

No wonder Echidna had hesitated to let them meet, despite having so casually let him tangle with the three witches of Pride, Wrath, and Sloth.
Daphne, the Witch of Gluttony, was decisively different from the witches he'd met so far, in some way he couldn't quite place.

"...Mm."

"—————!?"

Just as a bead of tense sweat trickled down Subaru's brow, and he wiped it away with the back of his hand so it wouldn't get in his eyes, movement suddenly stirred from Daphne's direction, and Subaru went rigid with alarm.
Inside the coffin, the girl wrapped in restraints twisted her neck slightly, and the sound of her breathing reached him. What action might follow from this, Subaru wondered, bracing his whole body on guard.
And then—

"...Zzz, mnyaaa."

"—She's asleep!?"

"—Fnyaa!?"

The instant he heard what sounded like sleep-breathing, Subaru stepped forward and shouted his retort.
His foot struck the grass of the hill with a satisfying smack, and the witch, startled by the voice and the sound, jolted inside the coffin and let out a cry of surprise.
Unable to see with both eyes sealed shut, she shook her head side to side.

"Wh-whaaat is it? Heeere I waaas, sleeeping so soundly..."

The words, resentful in content, came out in a bizarrely sluggish, drawling tone.
Whether the airy trailing-off of her words was from just waking, or simply a habit of hers, was unclear—but at the very least, the overwhelming pressure he'd felt only moments ago had vanished.
Had it all just been his imagination? Unable to hide his sudden deflation, Subaru said:

"Ah, uh, sorry. I got worked up without meaning to. Wasn't trying to yell."

"Aww, if you're gonna startle me over something liiike thaaat, Daphne's gonna have a probleeem with it, you know."

"Ngh... I said I'm sorry. So cheer up already. Don't be mad at me, okaaay?"

"I'm noooot mad, not really. If I get maaad, that just makes me hungrier, after aaall. More importantly, who are you, anywaaay?"

When he apologized, she rejected it, and on top of that posed a question entirely at her own pace.
They'd exchanged only a couple lines of conversation, and already the rhythm of it was thrown off. And Subaru began to grasp, if only vaguely, the meaning behind what Echidna had said.

*—This witch was the type whose conversational pace completely failed to mesh with Subaru's.*

Sighing at this uneasy start, Subaru gave his head a light shake to wipe the sour look off his face, then turned toward her with an extremely friendly smile.

"My name's Natsuki Subaru. For reasons I won't get into, Echidna invited me to this witches' tea party... ah, well, we're tea buddies, I guess. Yeah, something like that."

"Heee, so Dona-Dona has a friend after aaall. Subaru-n, don't you think you should be more careful who you pick as friends? If your real friends or faaamily heard you're friends with a witch, they might end up hating you, you knoooow...?"

In response to Subaru's introduction, Daphne offered up her unwanted concern. The way her words trailed off in fragments toward the end was because, for some reason, she was running out of breath as she talked.
Her shoulders rose and fell inside the coffin, clearly exhausted, and Subaru called out to her, "Hey, hey."

"Why are you so worn out? Does that coffin have some kind of effect where just lying in it drains the life force out of whoever's inside?"

"Nooo, not really? It's juuust, Daphne tires easily, or, well, my tummy's growling and I don't have any energy, or... is there, anything, to eeeat...? Haah, haah..."

"Getting out of breath just from talking—that's the very picture of a frail constitution... As for food, all there is on the table is Dona-tea and something that looks like cookies to go with it."

In truth, the tea snack was some mysterious cookie-like confection. Given that the tea itself was, by way of preamble, Echidna's bodily fluid, there was a real possibility that this too was made from her body's cells.
Unlike the tea, which was over in an instant, this was something that would linger, so Subaru had deliberately avoided touching it—but Daphne's reaction on hearing "cookies!?" was bright to the point of being obvious.

"Th-th-th-that's fiiine. It's fine, so give it to Daphne, put it in Daphne's mouth, pleeease. Hurry, c'mon, hurryyy...!"

"Could you maybe tone that down—out of context that's gonna sound really wrong!? Well, I'm not sadistic enough to make someone wait when they want it that badly."

Picking up the plate of snacks, Subaru approached Daphne's coffin, meaning to carry a cookie to the mouth of the girl who couldn't move. But just before he did—

"Ah, but, but, Subaru-n, could you wait just a seeecond?"

"Hah? What is it. I'll warn you, there's probably only one flavor. No sign of chocolate or anything, so it's the plain type. If you say you don't like it, all I can tell you is 'no leftovers allowed.'"

"That's not it... it's about when you feed me, I'd really rather Subaru-n not get too close to Daphne."

"That's a tough order to fill while trying to feed you!?"

Refused permission to get closer, Subaru stood there at a loss, cookie plate in hand.
Seeing him like that, she raised herself up slightly within the coffin.

"I don't want you to get the wrong idea, it's not like I hate Subaru-n or find you gross or anything, that's not what this is about."

"That reassurance somehow made me trust you less! The reason! Tell me the reason, please!"

"It's just, if Subaru-n's smell gets too close to Daphne, I worry it might be poison."

"My body odor's being treated as a toxin!?"

Struck by a remark that only wounded him further, Subaru hastily lifted his arm to sniff it. He didn't detect any particular offensive odor, but then again, people are generally dull to their own smell.
Running his eyes over his own body from top to bottom, Subaru said:

"Do I stink? Do I really? I've been washing up properly even since coming to the Sanctuary, you know? Sure, I don't have anything like proper soap until I'm back at the mansion, but I've kept up at least minimal grooming with Emilia in mind... no wait, this is a mental world to begin with—does the lousy state of the outside world even carry over in here?"

"Nooo, it's not that kind of thing. Come oooon, you know, don't you get it, Subaru-n?"

"I don't get it! Don't talk like I'm the one being difficult here! Just say it plainly!"

Losing his patience, Subaru called out and beckoned her on. In response, Daphne slowly swayed her head side to side, the coffin creaking, and continued in that peculiar rhythm of hers, "How should I put it..."

"If I smell Subaru-n and end up wanting to eat Subaru-n more than the cookies, that'd be a probleeem, wouldn't it?"

"...Eh? Sorry, I don't quite follow what you're saying."

"Daphne likes meat more than vegetables, I guess, or, like, I like tough meat more than soft meat—it's kind of like that."

Subaru suddenly felt a chill race up his spine.
He held his breath and stared at Daphne. Her manner hadn't changed once from beginning to now. Still packed into the coffin, still sealed inside restraints that left her unable to move, her eyes had never once looked at Subaru, shut away in darkness.
If this wasn't some fashion in bondage, then what was it for—

"As far as I can tell from the smell, Subaru-n's got lots of muscle, kind of sinewy meat, and thick-looking bones too, sooo, soooo... I have a feeling you'd be right up Daphne's alley. So if you get close, you smell so good I just want to eat you."

"E-eat me... you mean, sexually?"

"In the raw sense..."

A word with a different pronunciation but a chillingly adjacent meaning came flying back at him, and Subaru caught his breath.
Moving swiftly, he put a good deal of distance between them, and from that slightly removed spot picked up a single cookie from the plate.

"I—I'm gonna throw this at you from here, so sorry if it doesn't make it into your mouth, okay?"

"That's fiiine, Subaru-n. Just toss it kind of vaguely, close enough to hit the coffin. I'll go and get it myself on this end."

"That sounds way too ominous, but... ah, screw it, here goes!"

Winding up, Subaru tossed the cookie with a light overhand throw.
The cookie, about the size of a five-hundred-yen coin, arced through the air and sailed cleanly into Daphne's mouth with an accuracy beyond his imagining. Supreme concentration had granted Subaru control like never before.
With control precise enough to thread a needle, the cookie shot straight into Daphne's mouth. Landing on her tongue, she drew it fully inside in a single motion, and—

"Mnyum, mnyum... mmm, tasty. Tastes like Dona-Dona."

"I don't know if that means it's handmade by Echidna, or if she's actually putting some part of her own body into these cookies in some kind of black-magic way... anyway, here comes more."

"Yesss, I can't wait. More... c'mon, more..."

"Stay quiet for a sec, you're gonna throw off my aim!?"

Cutting off Daphne's needlessly suggestive pleading, Subaru kept throwing cookies one after another. It was a scene that looked like it'd get him scolded for playing with his food, but the sheer seriousness with which Subaru threw put it beyond that kind of reproach.
Though Daphne had to shift her neck slightly here and there, every single cookie made it into her mouth. And just as he was relaxing with relief, thinking he could clear the whole plate—

"—Ah."

A gust of wind blew rather strongly across the hill just then, and the light cookie was buffeted off its arc. It missed its mark badly, sailing over the table toward the slope of the hill. It was about to fall—about to become ant food, Subaru thought—and right after that—

"Noooo... what a wasteee."

Daphne, with her abnormal sense of smell, had noticed the cookie had gone off course. She followed the arcing cookie's fate with a sightless gaze, and the next moment, Subaru saw it.

"—————!?"

With a violent noise, sharp claws gouged into the hill's soil, wreaking destruction.
Earth and dirt flew up, the successive noises spreading the damage wider, until it reached beneath the cookie that had failed to catch the wind and was starting to fall.

"Ah, mm."

Stretching her neck out, Daphne caught the cookie between her red lips and, with a look of happiness, drew it into her mouth. She chewed so quietly not a sound could be heard, and once it had settled in her stomach, her pink tongue slowly licked her lips moist, followed by a sultry sigh of "haah."

Having watched all of this unfold, Subaru fell silent.
Noticing Subaru's silence, Daphne gave a little sniff with her small nose.

"Subaru-n... there are still two left, you knoooow? Don't be meaaan..."

With her cheeks flushed and her lips trembling like a little bird's, the girl looked adorable.
That is, if you ignored the fact that both her eyes were covered, her entire body was bound tight in restraints, and she was lying inside a black coffin—

"...No, there's no way you could not be rattled by this."

—if not for the sheer absurdity of crab-like legs sprouting from that coffin to carry its host's body around.


* * *


"So... that thing—can I ask what it was?"

Still unable to recover from his initial shock, Subaru threw the remaining two cookies as requested. Though the tremor in his fingertips threw off his aim on both, the coffin's swift footwork carried them effortlessly into Daphne's mouth regardless.
Daphne, having savored the sweetness to her heart's content, was humming a happy "mmm" or so, but she noticed the question Subaru had somehow managed to squeeze out.

"That... I dunno what you mean, seeing as Daphne can't seeee."

"That... incredibly, gleamingly beautifully-designed mobile coffin thing. In my narrow, shallow knowledge, coffins don't have legs, and they don't move at high speed like some kind of insect."

Creaking with a grinding sound, the coffin, still holding Daphne inside, slowly returned to its original position. Setting its base down onto the grass with a thud, the crab-like legs were drawn back into the coffin as if sucked inside. It was something like the way a turtle tucks its limbs and head into its shell.
Seeming to catch on to the impression forming in Subaru's mind, Daphne laughed, "Ahh."

"You mean the Centipede Coffin? This little one, Daphne made because I couldn't move and it was so inconvenient. It's usually quiet, and it's a good child, you knoooow?"

"Made it, you said... is that thing... alive?"

Even though it had biological movements and organs, he couldn't be certain its existence fell into the category of "living thing." Of course, it was equally certain it wasn't something mechanical.

"It doesn't eat or drink or anything, but the Centipede Coffin properly absorbs mana and lives just fine, you knoooow? Not getting hungry—that's kind of enviable, isn't it?"

"Feeding on mana... no, let's set that aside for now. More importantly, did you say you made it? You can create living things?"

"Living things, I guess, or, well, Witchbeasts... is more like it. Daphne's intent, or mood, or that kind of hazy, floaty feeling—it seems like that's what gives birth to them."

No words that conjured up anything concrete came from Daphne's mouth as she writhed inside the coffin. And yet, even from that vague air about her, one could sense that something outrageous was being done.

*—Creating living things was, quite literally, the work of a god.*

In his original world, humans had dabbled in such forbidden science through selective breeding and cloning, but bringing something into being out of pure nothingness was the complete province of divinity.
Whether one viewed that as a blasphemy against life or as the achievement of one who had attained the mysteries was, of course, a matter of perspective.

"Wait... don't tell me... Daphne's 'negative legacy'... does it mean, literally, that she gave birth to Witchbeasts?"

"Hmm, hmm?"

"The White Whale, the Black Serpent, the Great Rabbit... you're saying you brought all of those into existence, just like that crab coffin thing...?"

"Hmm, fuu... yeah, all such nostalgic names. That's right. The whale, and the little snake, and the rabbit too—they're all children Daphne made."

"Why!?"

Bristling at her confirmation, Subaru closed the distance between them in one step, spittle flying.
His face red with fury, Subaru jabbed a finger at Daphne.

"Why the hell did you make monsters like that!? Those things, out there in the world, for four hundred years after you died! Do you have any idea how much havoc they've wreaked!? How many people—tens, hundreds of people—have suffered horribly because of them...!"

What rose in his mind was the fierce battle on the Lifaus Highway, the clash against the White Whale.
Wilhelm's cry and his obsession after his wife was killed, and the grief and rage of all the knights who took part in that battle—all of it a tragedy that began with the White Whale, born from the witch who had given birth to it.
As for the Great Rabbit assailing the Sanctuary—if Subaru's efforts going forward didn't bear fruit, every resident of the Sanctuary, Emilia included, would end up devoured.
The Great Rabbit was a natural disaster that had gone on repeating that very thing again and again. And if the cause of it all was this witch right in front of him—

"What was it all for!? Tell me! The reason so many people had to suffer—the monster that made them suffer—why did you make it!?"

"...? Well, bigger creatures are more satisfying to eat, aren't they?"

"—Ah, uh, huh?"

To Subaru's impassioned outburst, Daphne responded with that puzzled expression.
Her words brought him to an unexpected halt, and Subaru, his tongue unable to keep pace, could only let out a groan.
Daphne's face took on a puzzled look in response.

"The White Whale was big, wasn't it? Don't you think if people ate that child, lots of them would get nice and full?"

"What are you... talking about..."

"The Great Rabbit, too, multiplies as much as you like. So, well, if that child's around, it keeps multiplying even if you just leave it alone, and I thought, that way, no one would ever have to worry about running out of food."

"Multiplies... huh?"

Daphne's words reached his ears, but their meaning failed to properly register in his brain.
If this cluster of words rattling around in his head meant exactly what it seemed to, then he had no idea what the witch before him was even saying.
Truly, from the bottom of his heart, what she was saying, its meaning—

"Th-then... what? Are you saying you made Witchbeasts to solve the food problem or something? That you made the White Whale and the Great Rabbit to help people suffering from starvation? Even though, thanks to your 'kindness,' countless people have been eaten alive by them!?"

"...? Trying to eat something while never considering the chance that you yourself might get eaten—isn't that kind of selfish?"

"............"

"Besides, humans and demi-humans, all lumped together, I think there's kind of too many of them in this world. And if those children can thin them out and tidy things up even a little, then Daphne thinks that's fine too."

"Th-the White Whale breathing out mist that erases people's existence, the Great Rabbit devouring whole villages as it pleases... what about that?"

"Daphne doesn't even know how they go about hunting. How those children grow up, how much they eat, where they end up getting eaten themselves... even knowing that wouldn't fill Daphne's belly, after all."

Watching Daphne say this with a smile, Subaru arrived at an understanding far, far too late.
He finally grasped the meaning of what Echidna had said.

She had said that Subaru and Daphne were the worst possible match.
Subaru had judged that this was simply because her utterly self-paced nature failed to mesh with his own impatient one—but what an optimistic, wide-of-the-mark idea that had been.

*—Subaru and Daphne didn't share the same values.*

No, it wasn't limited to Subaru alone. There was no human whose values could ever mesh with hers.
She viewed things from a perspective on an entirely different dimension from either humans or demi-humans. It wasn't even a viewpoint that favored the Witchbeasts she herself had created.
The strong devour the weak—that single principle was the whole of her thinking, and every consideration besides acknowledging the existence of prey, increasing it, and consuming it was treated as a trivial afterthought.

No words came. Her mental structure was fundamentally, categorically different.
Up to now, every witch Subaru had encountered had, whatever their issues, still been someone with whom a conversation could actually take shape—and that had led him astray.
She was a witch. They were witches. The genuine article—one of only seven such beings in the entire world.

"Subaru-n's the same way, but... isn't everyone underestimating 'Gluttony' just a little too much?"

"............"

"To begin with, appetite is the single most important desire for living, you know? I mean, if it's not satisfied, you can't go on living, right?"

"............"

"Even without peace, without being loved, without being able to voice your feelings, without holding onto your sense of self, without getting the things you want, without anything to aspire to—people don't die from that, do they? But..."

"............"

"If they can't eat, people die, you know?"

Among the Seven Deadly Sins, "Gluttony" alone was directly tied to life itself.
"Gluttony" in its proper sense meant pursuing appetite beyond what was necessary. But the true intent behind Daphne's words here pointed to the appetite necessary simply to live.
And Subaru couldn't deny it. Her statement did, in fact, point to one fragment of a truth about living. But to think that fragment was the whole of it—that alone was wrong.

"What you're saying is partly right... but a way of thinking like that..."

"Maybe Subaru-n should try starving to the very limit, just once. Then... I'm sure you'll understand what Daphne's been saying."

It was an attitude and a proposal that could not have been more witch-like.
Swaying, Daphne raised herself up from within the coffin. A sound like tearing paper rang out, and the crossed belts of the restraints that had bound Daphne came undone with ease. Pushing the dulled white restraints aside with her arm as though they were an annoyance, Daphne stepped down barefoot from the coffin onto the grass.
Swaying the limbs of her small frame, she checked the condition of her body, stiff as it was, and said:

"Moving around on my own two feet just makes me hungrier, so I hate doing it, you knoooow..."

Daphne was already out of breath from just that much warming up.
But faced with her, Subaru couldn't move even a single step. Even his breathing was sealed shut.
The small witch—the pressure radiating from her entire body seized Subaru and would not let go. It was a pressure like being gripped, whole body, tight in some enormous palm.

"I could just eat Subaru-n right here as is, but Dona-Dona and Meto-Meto would probably get mad about it, so... hmm, I guess just the left eye will do?"

As she said this, Daphne raised a hand to the eyepatch covering her own eyes, right in front of Subaru.
Don't undo her restraints. Don't touch her body. Don't look into her eyes—every one of these was something Echidna had warned Subaru about before bringing Daphne down.
But she had undone her own restraints on her own accord, and though she showed no sign of touching him, his own movement had been sealed shut by sheer pressure. And now, as for that final warning—

"—————"

A golden left eye.
Nothing strange about it at all—just an ordinary round eye, a girl's eye.

Daphne peeled back only the eyepatch over her left eye, showing that eye off to him.
Subaru's movement was sealed shut as though pierced through by that gaze. Presently, she blinked that eye a few times, and then—

"Alright, that's enough."

As she said this, she moved—still with that exhausted air—back toward the coffin. The coffin shifted and gently caught her as she all but collapsed into it.
Shifting about inside the coffin, Daphne searched for the most comfortable position. Still unable to move, Subaru watched in silence, chewing on whatever was in his mouth.

Yawning, Daphne put the eyepatch she'd peeled back into place, covering both eyes once more. Presently, the lining of the coffin's interior slowly peeled away and writhed as it wound around her small body, cinching it tight.
It was nothing more than this: her restraint had been carried out by her own will, all along.

"Just now, what was... I mean, what was that even for—"

"Still haven't noticed?"

Subaru had been about to ask why she'd restrain herself of her own will. Cutting off his words, Daphne swayed within the coffin as if checking how tightly she was bound.
At her words, Subaru's brow furrowed for a moment—and then, all at once, he noticed.

"Ah, uh...?"

Pain. That's what it was.
Subaru doubled over from a pain that had arisen in his stomach, a pain so severe it felt like a hole might open there.
His stomach wrung tight, crying out with thirst and hunger—it was overwhelming starvation. Groaning at the unbearable pain, writhing, Subaru sank to his knees on the grass.
Drool spilling from the corners of his mouth, he endured the pain with everything he had, desperately. Hungry, hungry, hungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungryhungry.

"Ah, ah, ahh... it's... painful..."

The ferocious hunger, so intense it threatened to blow his consciousness away, scrambled his thoughts and kept him from perceiving reality.
Gasping, writhing, Subaru rolled about on the ground. Even that motion seemed to feed the hunger further, and before long Subaru was trembling in small spasms on the grass like an insect.
Starvation, a starvation maddening enough to drive him insane. Starving. Life-threatening. A hole opening, a hole opening in his stomach. Dying, he might die, if he wasn't filled right now he would die. Die. Die.

"Still haven't noticed?"

Looking down at Subaru writhing in agony—though in truth his image wasn't reflected in her eyes—Daphne seemed to be grasping his condition through his voice and her sense of smell.
He didn't understand what Daphne meant. Notice what—the hunger alone was enough to feel like it was driving him out of his mind. He understood this was something she had done to him, but the hunger outweighed any hatred he might feel toward her for it. Somehow, he had to satisfy this hunger, or he couldn't bear it. And the very fact that his consciousness was barely managing to hold together right now was thanks to whatever it was he'd been chewing on in his mouth this whole time—

"—————"

What, exactly, was Subaru eating right now?

"Did you notice? That's 'Gluttony,' you know."

At Daphne's words, Subaru noticed his own right hand—a hand missing its pinky and ring finger.
Where had the vanished fingers gone? There was no need to search. Right now, in his mouth, his back teeth had just finished crushing a fragment of his pinky finger.
Blood welled up from the wound where flesh had been torn away by teeth, staining the green of the grass a vermillion red.

Watching those drops spill and fall, blankness took hold of Subaru's mind.
And that blankness, as time passed, began to fill in. The feeling that rose up was—

*—Ah, what a waste, all that spilling blood.*

Nothing more than pure disappointment, born of raw hunger, at the thought of that thirst going unquenched.
