Arc 5 Chapter 37: "Mop-Up Duty"

Far away, far away, a voice echoes.

A voice whose owner I couldn't tell. A voice whose source I couldn't tell.
A voice I couldn't tell was a man's or a woman's, from above or below.

"――――"

It was a voice like a battle cry.
It was a voice like a lament.
It also sounded like an accusation.
It also sounded like sobbing.

It was a voice.
The voice poured down like a waterfall, surged forward like a great wave, and gripped like a whirlpool, refusing to let go.
As though finally meeting someone, it were conveying all that had long been held inside.

Swept away by the muddy torrent of voice, I lost track of where I was.
Hands and feet, head and hips, chest and back — all became one. Melting, melting, melding together.
Eventually even the barely comprehensible existence that was myself was swallowed up in the vast, ceaseless outpouring of voice, and my body scattered away, lost.

I could tell that a black stagnation was drinking down the world I sensed.
Just as all that stagnation was about to finish invading the flesh, just as I was about to surrender myself to an unresistable, gradual end, I noticed—

—a thread, tangled so tightly it could never be undone, was refusing that encroachment.
A thread that insisted it would never let itself be blocked — not by being scattered, not by being drunk down, not by being covered over.

Something writhing inside the body. Neither side was necessarily in the right.
It was merely a stagnation, turning a person's body into a battlefield, each side simply asserting its own existence against the other.

And that, eventually, eventually——.


*  *  *


"――――"

The first thing to leap into his ears was someone's shouting voice.
Hearing that piercingly shrill sound, he opened his eyes, and a white ceiling swam into view. At the same time he realized he was sprawled out on a hard floor, limbs flung wide.

"—You're useless!"

Consciousness surfacing, the abuse took shape more clearly than a moment before.
That voice, dressed in an anger unbearable even to hear, was accompanied by action to match its emotional eruption. A dry crack rang out — an open palm striking flesh.

"Stop it! What good does blaming him like that do? This isn't something one person alone is responsible for. You must know that too."

"Shut up! That's not the kind of hollow comfort I want to hear! Stay out of it, you outsiders!"

The sense of a scuffle, and fury that would not subside.
From the way the voices echoed, he could faintly tell it was happening in the same room, though a spacious one. He reached out, and when his left hand touched a wall, he tried using it to prop himself up.
Instantly a sharp pain shot through him, as though a nail were being driven into his skull, and his breath stopped. His vision burst apart as if gunpowder had been packed behind his eyeballs, and his consciousness dyed itself crimson.
Reeling from pain and shock, he forced his upper body up, and there before him the scuffle was still unfolding just as before.

—Grappling in the middle of the room were three men and women. No—three men.

Ferris, eyes brimming with tears, was trying to grab hold of Wilhelm, while Julius desperately held him back. That dry crack from before seemed to have been the sound of Wilhelm's cheek being struck. The old man, his cheek faintly reddened, lowered his head weakly before Ferris's glare.

"...There's no way I can apologize enough."

"Make excuses! Tell me there was some reason, that it couldn't be helped, and make me understand! Apologizing—being apologized to—none of it changes anything!"

"Ferris, that's misdirected. Wilhelm-sama regrets it too, you know."

"Regrets it—!? What good does regretting it do? Useless! Spineless! Every last one of you… why…? Why couldn't any of you protect Crusch-sama…"

His voice rising, Ferris's gaze stabbed at Wilhelm, then Julius, before, as if losing track of where to aim it, he collapsed to his knees on the spot.
At Ferris's tearful accusations, neither man could say a word. Looked down upon by the two of them, Ferris clawed at the floor with his pale hands,

"What good is being 'Blue'... unable to be of use even once, at a time like this...! Useless... useless, useless, useless...!"

Spilling tears onto the floor, Ferris's condemnation continued on like a curse.
But it would have been better still if that anger had somewhere else to land, on someone nearby. Once it became clear that it was, hopelessly, anger directed at himself, it was only natural that no one could find words to insert into his grief.

"――――"

Julius's sighs mingled with Ferris's sobbing voice. Wilhelm stayed silent, saying nothing at all, and a gloomy air hung over the room, centered on the three of them. Just then—

"Yo, boss. So ya woke up, huh."

Garfiel, poking his head through the doorway that had lost its door, noticed Subaru, who had been watching the three of them, unable to do anything. At the sound of Garfiel's voice, Julius and the others turned toward Subaru's awakening and their faces showed relief.

"Thank goodness. It seems Subaru's woken up too. Ferris."

"...I know."

At Julius's call, Ferris roughly wiped his face with his sleeve and stood up. With no trace of the unsightly scene from moments before, he walked over to Subaru, briskly examined his bewildered body, and finally looked him straight in the eye.

"Mm, looks fine. Consciousness is okay too, right? Can you say your name and where you're from?"

"My name's Natsuki Subaru. I'm from Japan."

"Never heard of that backwater. ...I'll be with Crusch-sama."

Making a face as if he'd heard a lame joke at Subaru's answer, Ferris dusted off his backside and promptly left the scene. Unable to respond to his curt words, everyone silently watched his back go.
Only Wilhelm began walking, as if to follow after Ferris. Just before leaving the room, the old man left Subaru with nothing more than a nod of the eyes, and departed together with Ferris.
Once the two of them had left, he could finally tell the tense atmosphere in the room was beginning to thin.
Though in its place, he could feel, painfully, the gloomy atmosphere growing all the stronger.

"Even if your body's fine, don't push yourself, boss."

"...You're one to talk. Your color's the worst I've seen."

Garfiel called out to Subaru, who was leaning his back against the wall with his legs sprawled out. Looking back at his face, Subaru lowered the tone of his voice at the sight of the boy, deeply etched with exhaustion.
Dried blood clung to his face and golden hair, and there were several tears in his clothes as well. His pallor was as bad as, or worse than, when he'd shown up at the shelter carrying the injured Mimi.
Thinking that far, Subaru's delayed thoughts finally reached one conclusion.

"Guess that means... I didn't die."

"Yeah. Both me and you, boss, made it through just fine. Still, that don't mean everythin's cause for celebration. Damn it all."

Affirming Subaru's hoarse mutter, Garfiel clicked his fangs in irritation.
Glancing at him sidelong, Subaru came to grasp anew his own survival—in other words, that Return by Death had failed to trigger—and that he hadn't seen the end of the operation to retake the city hall.
Naturally, since he'd survived, someone must have rescued him, but—

"The city hall... what happened to it? How did I end up here..."

"Nothing happened, in a sense. This very place you're in is that city hall in question. The Witch Cultists abandoned the building, and we retook our target. If you look only at the outcome, I suppose you could put it that way."

Julius, kneeling down in front of him, answered Subaru's halting question.
Seen up close, the state of the "Finest Knight" was quite something too. His hair was disheveled, and marks of being struck were visible on his cheeks and neck. His knight's uniform was stained with blood as well—hardly a look one could call unscathed.
Above all, his handsome features were etched deeply with an unbecoming regret and humiliation.

"First of all, it's a relief you've woken up. If something had happened to you on top of everything else, our morale would have been beyond recovery."

"...Skip the stuff I don't need. What happened. Morale and all that doesn't matter. The Witch Cult abandoning the building—what... what the hell happened!"

"Exactly as I said. The Witch Cultists abandoned the building, and the city hall returned to our hands. If we set aside the hostages transformed into something other than human, and the fact that every last Witch Cultist got away after achieving their goal, we might even have had cause to celebrate."

Against Subaru, whose voice raced with impatience, Julius conveyed the facts flatly, to the last.
But the stiffness in his tone, his lowered gaze—above all, the sheer weight of the facts he spoke could not keep even Julius from letting a trace of righteous anger slip into his voice.
And Subaru, too, could not hide his shock at what he'd been told.

"Something other than human... what does that mean..."

"The top floor of the hall—you saw it yourself, didn't you. That was a nightmare, but it doesn't end as one."

Shaking his head, Julius confirmed the cruel reality.
Vividly, the glowing red compound eyes and the beat of wings that seemed to plead for help rose up again in Subaru's mind. That he managed to hold back a sudden wave of nausea was because it occurred to him that the truth behind that nightmarish sight had been a row of people crying out for salvation.
His heart ached as though squeezed, and a feeling that was neither sympathy nor fear welled up within him.

The abominable monster of the Witch Cult, the Sin Archbishop of Lust, Capella, was the worst kind of existence—one that trampled human dignity and values underfoot and mocked them for it.
What that monster had toyed with was not merely people's lives, nor merely their hearts, but something even more precious.

"The plaza, and then the interior and top floor of the hall. From the way we were split up individually to the way our time was drained away, we were dancing in the Witch Cult's palm the entire time, one might say. If they'd felt like it, they could have slowly tortured every one of us to death. That it didn't come to that was thanks to your quick thinking, and the efforts of the person turned into the black dragon."

"My... quick thinking?"

"The mirror for communication that you carried—from the moment we breached the top floor, you kept it connected to the other two the whole time, didn't you? As a result, the situation at the city hall was conveyed to Anastasia-sama and Ferris. That the Iron Fang's reinforcements and Ferris arrived in time was your doing."

"You think that's supposed to be comforting?"

"...I have no intention of comforting you. I merely told you the facts. How you choose to take it, I'll leave up to your own heart."

Subaru felt a flash of irritation at Julius's every answer sounding so affected. But Julius's distancing response was, itself, proof that he wasn't entirely composed either.
Neither of their mental states nor their situation was good. Subaru let out a deep breath.

"That other thing you mentioned before—the black dragon's efforts. What was that."

"It happened on the top floor. I can't claim to understand it better than you, but... there was someone made into a substitute by 'Lust's' Authority. The Authority's horror aside, what's astonishing is its fidelity of reproduction. The person transformed into the black dragon, though near death, crawled its way up to the top floor and breathed its breath upon 'Lust,' forcing him to withdraw. That you're alive to speak of it is likely thanks to that stroke of providence."

What Julius spoke of could only be the black dragon that Crusch had cut down in the great hall and that should have been thrown outside afterward.
If Capella's Authority was one of mutation and transformation, then that black dragon must have been one of the hostages held in the city hall. Which meant that, ignoring its cries for help, he and the others had tried to cut the person down. And even in a state like that, it had still fought with everything it had.

"That person, the black dragon—where are they now..."

"Ain't gonna let 'em die."

Garfiel suddenly cut in with a quiet voice at Subaru's concern for the person's safety.
Without turning to face Subaru, who raised his eyebrows, Garfiel just stared fixedly up at the ceiling and said,

"Ain't gonna let 'em die. Can't let 'em die, no way. Gotta save 'em... or else..."

"He's been like that the whole time. It seems the person is someone he knows. Even their scent has apparently changed entirely, and yet Garfiel is clearly acting this way regardless. Treatment is finished for now, though there are a few concerns, so we've let them rest in the back."

"Someone he knows—Garfiel's, you mean? Since when. Does he even have acquaintances in this city..."

"――――"

Even at Subaru's surprise, Garfiel, without meeting his eyes, kept his silence.
Still, Subaru felt relief that the life of the black dragon, to whom he owed so much, had been saved. As for the others—the people turned into those flies—

"Whether it's fair to call them 'safe' isn't something I can judge lightly. But their persons are secured. Ferris has already examined them. I held little hope... and, well, as expected."

"So it's not the kind of wound or sickness Ferris can heal. ...Damn it!"

Unable to help himself, Subaru slammed a hand against the floor, thinking of the hearts of those people who had lost their own bodies.
Just how much terror and loss must be assailing them right now. Ceasing to be human carries a different kind of horror and cruelty than losing one's life.
If you lose your life, the self you've lived as up to that point simply ends.
But the loss of one's body was, though equal to that ending, not actually an ending at all.

Those tormented by an incurable curse were likewise gathered somewhere within the city hall.
Thinking of the regret of those people gathered somewhere above or below, Subaru could do nothing but let his thoughts race over the many things he still had to confirm.
Now that he knew he'd survived, the next question that surfaced was—

"Are you and Garfiel both unhurt?"

"As you can see, neither Garfiel nor I have any notable wounds. Ricardo's the same. It's humiliating to admit, but... we were held back against."

"――――"

A hiss of humiliation mixed into his voice, and Julius bit down hard on his lip.
Seeing how his anger came through, and remembering who Julius had faced, the same measure of frustration welled up in Subaru too.

The one Julius had fought was that despised "Gluttony," Alphard.
If he was honest, that one Subaru wanted to tear apart with his own two hands. But even if Subaru himself couldn't have been the one to strike him down, letting him escape entirely was the one outcome he'd wanted to avoid at all costs.
Hearing that this mortal enemy had gotten away, there was no way he could stay calm.

"...I'm sorry. I couldn't even fulfill the duty entrusted to me."

"If we start on that, there's no end to it for me either. ...The broadcast—it went out, didn't it? Since they said they'd achieved their goal."

"That's right. The voice was the same—'Lust's.' Only... no, there's no use dwelling on that. In any case, the demand was made. We need to discuss that as well."

That the demand "Lust" had made over the broadcast was nothing good could be read plainly from Julius's expression. It was something Subaru didn't want to hear, yet he understood full well it wasn't something he could simply cover his ears against. But before that—

"The broadcast, sure... but there's something else. What happened to Crusch-san?"

"――――"

"Crusch-san was on the top floor same as me... no, it must've been even worse for her. 'Lust' did something to her, and she was suffering..."

The image of Crusch, coughing up blood with her eyes rolled back, rose in his mind.
Without any external wound, she had shown herself in such a ravaged state. Something dire must have happened, to the point that her very life seemed in danger.
On top of that, there was Ferris's outcry from before. He wanted to think, surely not, but—

"I feel like Ferris was saying something... something bad. I..."

"Crusch-sama is alive. Of that much, at least, there's no mistake."

"Don't say it like there's something you're leaving out."

For a moment it had sounded like a glimpse of hope, but that only lasted until he looked into Julius's eyes.
From his expression, one enduring something unbearable, there was not a trace of relief at a life having been saved. If anything, it seemed as though something even more terrible were happening.

"Ferris has been doing everything he can, the whole time. But it isn't going well."

"What do you mean, not going well. What's happening to Crusch-san... if even Ferris can't do anything, that's just like the others!"

"Calm yourself. Nothing changes even if you fall apart. Calm down."

Julius called out to still Subaru's agitation as his face paled.
But that composed veneer only grated on Subaru all the more right now.

"Why the hell can you stay so calm!? This is a crushing defeat if there ever was one!? Getting played like that by those bastards, and you're not even angry!"

"—I am angry! I am grieving too! Of course I am!"

Julius's arm knocked away Subaru's, who had reflexively reached out to grab at him. Seeing the eyes of Julius, his voice raised, wavering with passionate feeling, Subaru lost his own voice.

"...I'm sorry for the roughness. If I can't even control myself, then I'm still immature."

Julius supported Subaru, who had nearly fallen after his arm was knocked away, and apologized as though ashamed of himself. Listening to that apology, it was Subaru who felt ashamed of himself. He was sick of his own shallowness, picking a fight with Julius's attitude even though he understood perfectly well.

"Crusch-san, is..."

"...She must have had something done to her by 'Lust.' A foreign presence has dissolved into her body and is rampaging inside her. Ferris's distress is more than one can bear to watch."

With Julius lowering the tone of his voice, the seriousness of Crusch's condition rose vividly before his eyes.
At the presence of something demonic lurking within her body, the utmost agony, as if her flesh and blood, bones, even soul were being whittled away. That was never a suffering a human being should have to taste.
Being unable to do anything about it must be what led to Ferris's behavior earlier.
Around the time Subaru had woken, Ferris had seemed to be blaming Wilhelm. That was surely a rebuke aimed at the old swordsman who had accompanied his mistress to the battlefield and failed to protect her.
Surely both Ferris, who did the blaming, and Wilhelm, who was blamed, understood it was nothing more than a fit of temper with nowhere else to go.
That was precisely why Wilhelm had stayed silent even after being struck, and why Ferris had been sobbing as though hating his own weakness.

The two who had left the room earlier, and the mistress who must still be suffering even now.
Thinking of the three of them, he could feel a sense of defeat pierce his chest all the more.
But to Subaru, his heart aching with such feelings, Julius said,

"—Subaru, there's something I want to confirm."

"What."

"It pains me a little to ask this... but it seems you haven't noticed."

At Julius's roundabout way of speaking, Subaru tilted his head.
Narrowing his eyes at him, Julius gently touched Subaru's reclining body. Wondering what was happening, Subaru looked over to see Julius's hand, having touched his leg, gradually moving downward, toward his right thigh.

Slowly, offhandedly, he let his gaze follow along.
At this point Subaru truly was being offhanded about it. Even while remembering that Crusch had been struck down by Capella on the top floor of the city hall, Subaru had conveniently forgotten about himself.
What had happened to him, right before he lost consciousness.
That he'd felt relief at not having triggered Return by Death could only mean his subconscious had understood he'd come close to dying—there was no other explanation—and yet.

"Wha—?!"

His breath caught, his throat froze, and Subaru doubted his own eyes.
At the end of his gaze was his own right leg. But—

—there, an ugly, blackly writhing seam of flesh, and a right leg being steadily encroached upon by that writhing, was what existed.

"It wasn't Ferris who joined it back. It isn't even healing magic. Your leg, the one that was torn off, joined itself back together on its own, and this is the result. There doesn't seem to be any pain, from what I can tell."

"――――"

Just as Julius said.
This ugly right leg had neither pain nor any sense of wrongness. He might have thought it wasn't even properly attached, yet the knee bent exactly as Subaru willed it, and he could move his toes too.
It was only that the wound where the severed part had rejoined had discolored to a murky black, with mottled vessels stretching outward, extending their encroachment up and down the leg.

"Subaru, I want to ask you again, properly."

".........."

At the harrowing change to his own leg, Subaru couldn't even manage to speak.
To Julius's question, Subaru slowly raised his face, and then—

"Are you truly all right?"


*  *  *


The reattached right leg was, terrifyingly enough, no hindrance at all to standing or walking.

"That Ferris lady said it too—that thing on your leg, boss, ain't a wound or a sickness. So even if I try healin' magic on it, the result's the same. All I get back is the feelin' that it's already healed."

Rolling up the leg of his trousers, Subaru's right leg looked as though black blood vessels had been strung all through it, starting from the wound on his thigh. The pattern that rose to the surface had a slight give when touched, its texture relatively close to that of normal skin. If he ignored the color, he wanted to think he could get away with dismissing it as nothing more than veins showing through.

"It joined back together on its own... hearing that, there's no way I can call that reassuring."

Needless to say, Subaru's body wasn't specialized enough in regeneration for a severed part to reattach on its own. This was the second time he'd experienced having a leg blown off, and the first time, there hadn't even been a hint of it reattaching—so his memory testified.
Naturally, he should assume some kind of interference had occurred, but there was only one thing he could think of.

"That blood Capella dripped on my leg... is that it?"

It was from when his right leg had been blown off, when his consciousness had grown hazy from the pain of massive blood loss.
It wasn't a memory reliable enough to state with total certainty, but he was fairly sure Capella had wounded his own wrist to bleed, and then poured that blood over Subaru.
At the time, he had said something that stuck with him, he was fairly sure. Yes, he was fairly sure—

"He did the same thing to Crusch-san, too."

"You mean, dripping blood onto a wound? It's not a pleasant act, but calling it merely ceremonial in significance... the direct effects it's produced are too pronounced for that. I have heard that Curse Arts and curses sometimes require steps different from ordinary Magic."

"A curse... right, a curse. He called it a blood curse too. No, wait, it was something else... not a blood curse—dragon... yes, Dragon's Blood. That's what he called it, I'm sure of it!"

Under Julius's suspicious gaze, Subaru dug through his memory and struck his hands together.
Capella, he was fairly certain, had watched Subaru writhe after being drenched in blood while saying something about Dragon's Blood being mixed into his own.
Whether it was delusion, nonsense, or a lie was beside the point—it ought to serve as a foothold into the abnormality of it all.

"Dragon's Blood... would that be referring to the Dragon's lifeblood bestowed upon the royal family?"

"I don't know the details. But so there's an item like that? In the castle?"

"There is, so I've been told, that much at least. One of the treasures bestowed in accordance with the Covenant with the Divine Dragon Volcanica. Dragon's Blood, which brings the blessing of abundance to barren land. The legend is a famous one."

"Sounds pretty all-purpose, this Dragon's Blood. ...Whether it's actually connected, I don't know. Might just be some kind of riddle, for all I know."

That Capella had claimed the family name of the Lugunican royal house for himself was also cause for concern. All the more so now that Wilhelm had told him Emerada Lugunica had truly existed.
He wanted to think surely not, but might it turn out Capella really was of royal blood? Why Dragon's Blood would be mixed up in that was very much an open question, though.

"Either way, if there's a possibility tied to blood, that's good news. It might be of some help to Ferris too, who's at a dead end."

"Ah, yeah, you're right. Then, right away..."

"Boss, I don't think ya oughta go."

Information that might finally turn things for the better.
To Subaru, fired up to deliver it, Garfiel, arms crossed by the wall, threw cold water. At Subaru's accusing glance as he turned, Garfiel shook his head.

"Ya haven't seen it yet, boss. If that's the case, better keep the number of people who see it small."

"...What's that supposed to mean."

"Just what it sounds like. She was a real beauty, that lady. Makes it worse, seein' her like that."

To Subaru's suppressed question, Garfiel only lowered his gaze.
Left with nothing but mounting unease, Subaru reflexively turned his eyes to Julius. But Julius, too, shook his head at Subaru with the same look as Garfiel.

"It's true. Crusch-sama, too, surely wouldn't wish to be seen by others right now. Precisely because she is a person of high integrity, she wouldn't want to show a weakened state."

"Is that really the reason? Just because she's weakened?"

"――――"

Julius said nothing. He only quietly averted his eyes from Subaru.
That single gesture alone was answer enough, and more.

"...It's my fault."

"Subaru, that's—"

"It's my fault! I... I of all people should have understood how dangerous they were! I of all people should have known we needed to prepare more, to go in with every bit of preparation we could manage!"

That he could only imagine what had happened to Crusch accelerated his fear.
What that grave self-reproach brought Subaru was anger at his own helplessness, and regret over the recklessness of having gone in without a plan.

The horror of the Witch Cult's Sin Archbishops—he should have understood it down to his very bones.
It wasn't only Petelgeuse. Before storming the city hall, Subaru had run into both Sirius and Regulus, one after another. And yet, why had he been able to take Capella so lightly?
With this much strength gathered around him, even if things turned against them they could always retreat—what was that, if not sheer arrogance? All of it was the price of his own misjudgment.

"All of it, because I..."

"—Yeah, yeah. That's enough whining and sore-loser talk. You're annoying, so hush up."

Subaru, shouldering the full weight of it all, on the verge of sinking into a sea of self-loathing.
What leapt into that Subaru's ears was not a voice gently lifting him back up from where he sank. It was a cold, merciless voice, glaring at the sinking Subaru with icy eyes and letting out a bored sigh.

"――――"

He turned his eyes toward the entrance of the great room.
There, clapping her hands and drawing every eye in the room to herself, stood a woman merchant with soft violet hair and a gentle cast of features.
She glared at Subaru with eyes shockingly bleak for that mild face of hers,

"Anastasia..."

"I get feelin' like it's a funeral after losin' a fight like that, but if all we get outta you is complainin' instead of pickin' apart what went wrong bit by bit, the rest of us are gonna be stuck. How dumb. Complainin' isn't gonna bring back what we lost or the time we lost, so you're gonna have to buck up."

So said Anastasia, chiding Subaru as he lay crushed under his sense of defeat.
For a moment, not understanding what he'd been told, Subaru froze, but the instant comprehension caught up, anger surged up in him. But—

"Anastasia-sama, I must ask you to retract that. Subaru, who came into the closest contact with the Witch Cult's malice, has every right to regret, to grieve..."

"Not like you, Julius. The fire's still burnin', y'know? And here you are puttin' feelings first... if you wanna play buddy-buddy games, Joshua'd do better than you."

"...!"

Julius, who had tried to shield Subaru, was silenced by his mistress's icy gaze. Seeing that, Subaru, whose thoughts had been overheating, felt as though he'd been doused with cold water too, left bewildered, unable to read what Anastasia was up to.
Then, looking over Subaru and the others who had lost their words, Anastasia toyed with the fox stole adorning her neck and said,

"...Well, never mind. Julius. You've got business over at Crusch-san's place or whatever, don't you? Go on and finish that up. And could the blond boy over there step out for a bit too?"

Prompting Julius and Garfiel to leave the room, Anastasia turned her eyes on Subaru.
There were four people in the room; once those two were gone, it would leave Subaru and Anastasia facing each other one on one.

"I won't do anything bad by ya."

Anastasia said so with a smile that held nothing endearing about it.
With that, Julius bowed and walked out first, and Garfiel followed after, wary look and all. Right up until the end, Garfiel kept casting worried glances at Subaru, but when Subaru gave him a nod just before he left, he shook it off and went out.

"Garfiel-kun's got a cute side to him, doesn't he. Peekin' back worried about Natsuki-kun right to the end—makes bein' the big brother figure worth it, don't ya think?"

"...That's not the kind of small talk you kept me here for, is it."

To Anastasia, letting slip her thoughts on the departed Garfiel, Subaru answered with a barb in his tone, what with the exchange just before.
Turning back to him, Anastasia smoothed down her hair while glancing around the half-wrecked room, righted a fallen chair, and lowered herself into it.

"Pushy, aren't ya. Talkin' with Natsuki-kun this leisurely like—feels like we gotta go back to, what, the night before the White Whale hunt or somethin'."

"Even then it was just business talk. Look, isn't now not the time for this? Even if we took back the city hall, nothing about the situation's actually..."

"That's right, nothin's changed. If anything, it's gotten worse. And we gotta do somethin' about that."

Subaru found himself made aware that the carefree voice, which until now had sounded almost pleasantly oblivious to the situation, had suddenly taken on the sharpness of a blade.
Subaru straightened his back involuntarily, and Anastasia, looking over his leg, said,

"Heard it got torn off and stuck back on—is your leg gonna be okay? Lookin' pretty gnarly on the outside there."

"Fortunately, doesn't seem to get in the way of jumping around or anything. Which honestly just makes it creepier."

"Right. If you can still jump and run around, that's what matters most. There's still probably gonna be a whole lot more I need you to do. ...But before that."

Using Subaru's injury as no more than a conversational opener, Anastasia let out a breath.
Sensing she was about to get to the real point, Subaru's brow furrowed, and she pointed a finger at the ceiling—no, upward, and said,

"Did you hear the third broadcast, the one after you and the others stormed in?"

"No, I didn't get to hear it. So I don't know what they said... probably some kind of demand, I'm guessing, but I never heard it."

"Missin' two outta three broadcasts—that's awful careless of ya, Natsuki-kun."

Chuckling with a hand over her mouth, Anastasia made Subaru's lips twist in displeasure. Still, she kept her hand over her mouth and raised her eyes, saying,

"One of the demands, that's probably somethin'—only me and Natsuki-kun would understand."

"Only me and Anastasia-san?"

What does that mean, a question mark rose in Subaru's mind.
He'd never once felt he and Anastasia had anything in common. To begin with, even properly talking like this was practically a first.
With such a tenuous relationship, what method was there to even know what they shared—

"—Honestly, Ana. Sometimes that self-important manner of yours produces nothing but frustration. This is precisely one of those times, wouldn't you say."

"—?!"

A third party's voice suddenly rang against the eardrums of Subaru, deep in thought.
The voice, neither Subaru's nor Anastasia's, carried a tone with a somehow androgynous ring to it.
He hastily cast his eyes around, but there was no figure anywhere nearby that could account for it. Since Julius and Garfiel had left, there had been no sign, no trace, of anyone approaching the entrance.
Then where, exactly, had that voice come from?

"Sayin' that, and now you're just as bad as me. Natsuki-kun's totally lost, all confused."

"Ah, I see. Then I've done something unkind."

"What—with whom—?!"

Anastasia carried on a conversation with that third party's voice as though it were the most natural thing in the world.
It did nothing to resolve Subaru's astonishment, and just as he tried to demand that the source of this confusion be revealed at once, he caught his breath.

"I'm sorry for startling you."

"――――"

Their eyes had met.
The third party who had spoken. The owner of that other voice, which had abruptly cut into this scene.
No—the owner of that voice hadn't cut in at all. It had been in this room from the very start.
It had entered this room together with Anastasia.

"My name is Echidna. Well, how should I put it... I suppose you could call me a kind of artificial spirit."

"Ech—?!"

Before Subaru, swallowed by a double shock, it deftly formed a smile.
The corners of its mouth lifting, its eyes narrowing—that, most surely, in all likelihood, was a smile.

—At Anastasia's neck, the fox that had been disguised as a stole was smiling.

Bearing an unforgettable Witch's name, while proclaiming its own nature to be that of a Spirit.
