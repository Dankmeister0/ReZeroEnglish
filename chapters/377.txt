Arc 5 Chapter 60: "One Ending, and One Battle"

She saw a white light.

A warm, soft, deeply comforting light.

How long had it been since she'd greeted a morning with such a peaceful heart?

Waking from sleep had always been a melancholy thing; in the days she'd spent trapped inside an unending nightmare, there had been no peace to be found anywhere.

She had believed, surely, that the darkness would never lift, not ever.

Perhaps that was exactly why this light sank so deeply into her heart.

"—Hey, wake up."

Someone's voice.

From beyond the white light, someone was calling to her. Led by that voice, as if her hand were being pulled, she passed through the darkness.

The white light that had seemed so far away eventually filled her vision entirely—

"Morning. Time to wake up, sleepyhead."

On the other side of her opening eyelids, a silver-haired girl said this with a bashful smile.

—At those words, tears traced down Sylphy's cheeks.

* * *

A pale blue light rose skyward as the barrier of ice unraveled.

The ice that had enveloped the half-collapsed church turned to light, and the mana that scattered apart vanished, wrapped in the dance of tiny spirits.

The sight of one invisible wonder being swallowed by another invisible wonder carved a deeply wistful sentiment into the hearts of those who watched.

It was the kind of scene that could hardly help but stir the tear ducts, but that alone surely wasn't the whole reason the sobbing women wept.

It was release—release from the nightmare that had bound the most radiant years of their lives.

"Still, Emilia-tan really is something else."

Subaru murmured absently, his lips loosening despite himself.

At the end of his gaze was Emilia, and the brides—former brides—clinging to her in tears, alive and well.

The women in wedding dresses numbered exactly fifty-three, with not a single one missing.

"...When I heard the brides' hearts had been fused with his, I figured there was no way to save them without letting them die."

Take the brides' lives, and thereby erase the whereabouts of the 'Lion's Heart.' Subaru had half-seriously resigned himself to believing there was no other way to stop that madman. He'd braced himself for sacrifice.

Unlike Subaru, Emilia never gave up.

Granted, he'd been locked in a life-or-death standoff against Regulus at the time, but the fact remained—he'd stopped thinking, convinced there was no other way. Emilia, though, never stopped thinking.

She considered what she could do with the cards she had in hand, and she carried it through.

And so—

"This time, Emilia-tan completely stole the show."

"That's not true at all."

Subaru, drained of strength, had slumped against the wall. Catching the sound of his relieved sigh, Emilia came back over. Her white dress was torn, her silver hair mussed from surviving a desperate battle. And yet, even so, Emilia standing there after the fight was beautiful.

Breathing out at that private thought, Subaru tilted his chin toward the others.

"Those people over there still look like they haven't finished thanking you, Emilia-tan?"

"Don't tease me. Besides, I can't act all high and mighty about it. It was a spur-of-the-moment thing, but... I forced everyone into a choice that could've killed them, even if only for a moment."

"But nobody died. Everyone's alive. —That's what matters most."

That result was, above all else, the best possible outcome.

Having gotten the answer he'd sought, Subaru relaxed. Emilia set her hands on her hips and, setting aside her own persistently low self-regard, pouted her lips at Subaru.

"You got covered in wounds and pushed yourself so hard... If you hadn't done your best, Subaru, everyone would've been done for by now. And it was you who noticed the 'Lion's Heart' thing in the first place."

"Lacking a finishing blow is pretty much business as usual for me... though it feels weird to just own that. But you really did well noticing—freezing the brides solid to put them in a state of suspended death."

"Well, I spent a long time frozen myself."

Emilia stuck out her tongue in a sort of teehee gesture. Cute.

Though, honestly, it wasn't really something to laugh about.

Even so, Emilia's actions and quick thinking had, in the defeat of Regulus, achieved the greatest possible result: preventing any needless sacrifice.

Fifty-three precious brides' lives had been saved.

"I wasn't confident I could really pull it off, though."

"But you did it. That's because you've been working hard to master your own power, Emilia-tan."

"But there was a chance they'd have stayed frozen forever. I'm honestly a little relieved I managed to release them properly."

Then, hiding a bashful smile, she touched her own chest.

As if confirming, beneath her palm, the pulse of a heartbeat that belonged to her alone.

"Besides, if you hadn't pulled Regulus's heart out of my chest, Subaru, I'd have had no choice but to cast the same spell on myself. And in that case, thawing both me and Sylphy and the others would've been so much harder than this. It might've taken another hundred years."

"Oh come on, that's got to be an exaggeration."

"..........."

"Wait, it's NOT an exaggeration!? Whoa, that was a razor-thin fine play, then!"

Subaru was aghast at Emilia's wordless, wry smile.

Provoked by Regulus, Emilia had been ready to stop the 'Lion's Heart' even at the cost of herself. Had he let that slide, one wrong move could have meant parting with Emilia for good in this life. Of course, he supposed he would have wandered the earth searching for a way to thaw the ice rather than let that happen.

"Two Sleeping Beauties on my hands would push my jinx status to a whole new level, so please, spare me that."

He grumbled, but still, Subaru breathed a sigh of relief.

Either way, Emilia had been safely saved, and the brides had come through unharmed as well. The battle against Regulus had produced destruction on a scale hardly believable for a fight against a single person—but as a final reckoning, the damage suffered on their side was next to nothing.

At most, there was the various physical toll Subaru had taken on, and an unnecessary grudge now held with the Witch Cult.

And then—

"I wonder if Reinhard's going to be okay—he left without even treating his wounds."

Emilia said this suddenly to a thoughtful Subaru.

Subaru looked up and waved a dismissive hand.

"No problem there. Apparently the minor spirits just go ahead and heal his wounds on their own if you leave him alone. He said so himself."

"Ah, I knew it. There were supposed to be a lot of minor spirits around here, but the moment Reinhard left, they all went with him... Reinhard might actually have talent as a spirit user."

"Don't say that, it'll kill my whole thing!"

Besides, in Reinhard's case, he was already absurdly overpowered even without that. It was Subaru himself who'd thrown Reinhard at Regulus, but the way Reinhard had fought in that quasi-duel at the end could be summed up in one word: horrifying.

Could a human really jump that lightly all the way above the clouds? He didn't seem like the same species as the rest of humanity—and yet, he was a knight in service to a fellow Royal Selection candidate.

"Emilia-tan, I'm weak, but please don't abandon me, okay?"

"—? I rely on you a whole lot, Subaru, you know?"

"Right!? Yeah, exactly! I'll keep serving you faithfully from here on out!"

"Sorry, I don't quite get why you're suddenly coming on so strong..."

Either way, he should stop comparing himself to others. That was exactly the kind of thing that would turn him into a Regulus—someone who couldn't act without measuring himself against others for reassurance.

He'd thought the man had nothing worth learning from, but well, as a cautionary example he wasn't half bad, was he.

"...I wonder if everyone else is okay."

"That's what Reinhard's there for. And honestly, everyone's stronger than me anyway."

To say he'd leave it to them might sound like he was just relying on others to save him, but the ticklish phrase "I believe in them" fit best.

They belonged to different camps, and someday they'd surely clash head-on over a single throne, but Subaru believed in them—all of them. Their characters, abilities, convictions varied widely, but still.

At the very least, enough to wish that they wouldn't lose to the likes of the Witch Cult—those contemptible, hopeless bastards.

"——"

And if someone were to lose, if it came down to a matter of life and death—then perhaps Subaru would have to consider Return by Death. Setting aside his contract with Roswaal, if there was any possibility of saving someone, he knew he'd surely try to.

He hated the pain. He hated the agony.

But grief, surely, he hated even more.

"Subaru."

"——"

Whatever she saw in Subaru as he sat there contemplating the possibility of death, Emilia lowered herself down beside him.

She leaned her body against his left shoulder, gently stroking his head as it began to bow. It tickled. But he couldn't bring himself to pull away.

"Emilia-tan?"

"Right now, I feel the same way you do. I'm worried about everyone, but I'm completely out of strength—I can't even go help. So let me pray together with you, Subaru? For everyone's safety."

"——"

"It'll be fine, I'm sure. Because everyone is so much stronger than us, so much smarter, and such hard workers."

Whether to put Subaru at ease or not, Emilia was choosing her words carefully. The sense in her word choice was so very like her that Subaru's heart eased, just a little.

He would believe. In everyone. In the Reinhard he'd sent off.

The instant Regulus was defeated, Reinhard had run off to aid the others. Whatever battlefield he reached, there was nothing to worry about.

He wanted to greet the morning with no one missing. If that happened, then Subaru would have only one thing left to worry about—

"——"

As Subaru looked up at the sky as if in prayer, Emilia too gazed up at the night sky through the collapsed ceiling of the church. Where Emilia couldn't see, Subaru clutched at his own chest, gripping it tight.

—Along with the growing certainty of Regulus's death, something black and unknowable had slid its way into his chest once more, and it was pulsing there.

This, surely, was the same thing as what had happened with Petelgeuse.

So, so that Emilia wouldn't notice it, quietly—only quietly.

He prayed to the sky, and steeled his own resolve, quietly, only quietly.

* * *

—Time now rewinds slightly, to before Regulus's defeat.

It was half an hour after Subaru and the others of the control tower assault team had departed, and after Otto, having seen them off, had left the city hall to retrieve the Book of Wisdom.

It was around the time Otto made contact with the 'Gluttony' fighting alongside Felt and the others; around the time Garfiel fell into the waterway with Kurgan; around the time Wilhelm knocked back Theresia's hood; around the time Julius frowned upon hearing grievances he had no memory of causing; around the time the church was half-destroyed in the middle of a wedding held without the bride's consent; around the time the waterways in the northern part of the city suddenly burst into flame all at once—it was the very moment an impact struck the city hall building, where only noncombatants remained.

"Ta-daaa! Here I am, making my entrance~!"

On the top floor of the five-story city hall, an enormous mass smashed mercilessly through the wall.

The violent tremor cracked the building's windows, and combined with the damage already sustained earlier that day, the building's foundation suffered fatal harm. The building that should have been the very nerve center of Priestella's civic functions had, in the span of a single day, been driven to the brink of collapse, reduced to a shadow of its former self.

Whether it would stop at the brink of collapse, or go further, would be decided by whatever was about to happen here—and by its outcome.

"Four towers, four floodgates. Get even one of 'em open and the whole city's guaranteed to go under water... Naturally, you gotta snuff out the possibility at every single tower, so you scum-meat had no choice but to spread your forces thin and drag this into an all-out war, wouldn'tcha say."

A voice, grating and chattering on the ear, spelled out the city's predicament.

Then, in response, voices rose up.

"Everyone! Let's make it back alive, and protect this city!"
"We'll take back these beautiful streets with our own hands!"
"Those of us who fight for justice can't possibly lose!"
"Good deeds bear good fruit, evil deeds bear evil fruit. This battle will be our victory—!"

A dignified young man's voice.

A young girl's valiant voice.

There was a battle-cry befitting a grizzled, veteran soldier of a man, and the voice of an intelligent woman in her prime shouting words of rousing encouragement.

Every one of them, strong words backed by will and resolve worthy of what they proclaimed—except that they all came from a single mouth.

"—Now, wasn't that about the sorta thing you were all thinkin', huh?"

And the very same lips that had spoken those powerful words now declared, in a voice caked over with contempt and mockery that betrayed everything said before, with malice too thick to ever wipe clean.

Then the owner of that voice hugged her own small body and swayed her shoulders side to side in a show of distaste,

"Kyahahahaha! No, no, no waaay, quit it already, seriously! Why the hell would I have to go along with some grubby, sweaty-smellin' justice like that? You scum-meat, every single one of you boiled your brains clean out, or what?"

A shrill, clattering peal of laughter rang out.

That coquettish voice of malice—unhideable, and with no intention of being hidden—came from a young girl with a body still small and unformed.

Round eyes, thin lips, chin-length blonde hair, and red-flushed cheeks made for a face of the utmost adorableness, yet upon it floated a sinister expression carrying a lasciviousness unbecoming of a child. What she wore amounted to little more than scraps of cloth resembling underwear, and the excessive exposure of a girl whose body wasn't even fully formed as a woman, let alone as a human being, stirred an equally twisted revulsion in any normal person.

That, no doubt, was precisely the aim of this girl—no, this monster.

The Witch Cult's Sin Archbishop of 'Lust,' Capella Emerada Lugunica—a monster whose aim was to violate human ethics and dignity in every way possible, precisely there.

"Honestly it's a total mystery to me how your brains could ever believe some convenient fantasy where I'm sittin' there all polite, waitin' in a tower! Just how gentle a life have you all been livin', is this some kinda hospitality service!? A fight's about not lettin' your enemy do what they wanna do, and doin' what your enemy don't want done to 'em, ain't it. You flower-garden dwellers oughta know your limits already, you scum, scum, scum, scum, scum-meat, the lot of you."

Even amid a torrent of abuse too vile to bear listening to, the monster Capella's mannerisms remained, somehow, adorable.

The monster, pressing a finger to her cheek and swaying her body, had broken into the city hall and immediately made her way, guided by the presence of people, into the nearest room—and there she had encountered, and had been hurling her string of abuse at, a cat-eared knight. Lying on the bed behind that knight was a long-haired woman.

This was the top floor of the city hall, the room assigned to the master-and-retainer pair of Ferris and Crusch.

"So you're the Sin Archbishop of 'Lust'...!"

It was Ferris, shielding the bed, glaring at Capella in a voice trembling with rage. Capella tilted her head at Ferris glaring at her, then peered past him at the bed and nodded with a look of understanding.

"Ah, yeah yeah, I get why you'd hate me. So she went and lost to the blood after all, huh. Figured she probably would, y'know. I figured it, but actually seein' her lose is still kinda disappointin'. Here I was expectin' something outta that fairly noble Lugunica blood."

"What's your goal, putting Crusch-sama through something like this!? What do I have to do to save Crusch-sama!? Answer me!"

At Capella's mutter—more bored than genuinely disappointed—Ferris, his lovely face flushed red with anger, roared back. Gripped in both his hands was the dagger Ferris always carried.

Carved with beautiful decoration and a lion's crest, it was a piece meant more for display than for actual combat. Combined with Ferris's own unpolished skill, it didn't look like something that could be wielded to any effect.

"That a toy? Some precious gift? Either way, wavin' that thing around with those skinny little hands is dangerous, ain't it, missy. ...Wait, huh?"

Capella, laughing with her tongue stuck out, cut herself off there and knit her brow.

"Ugh, gross. Huh? You've got a real unnatural body goin' on there, don'tcha. A body like that, and you're a guy... how the hell'd you end up like that without takin' anything off first? This ain't some pervert who just dresses like a woman, this is a whole different animal, and I gotta say, I'm creeped out."

"—!"

Having seen through Ferris's sex, Capella recoiled at how unnatural it was. The monster looked Ferris up and down and made an exaggerated show of gagging.

"That getup of yours, is it meant to lure men into lettin' their guard down? If so, then you actually get how pathetic humans really are. Men are idiots, women are trash, and humans across the board are nothin' but scum... that's a conclusion right up my alley."

"Shut up! Never mind that... answer my question! What did you do to Crusch-sama!?"

"Ah, geez, you're noisy."

To Capella, with whom conversation simply never managed to take hold, Ferris shouted again, swallowing his humiliation. Hearing this, Capella shrugged her shoulders, and in the instant after a blink, the girl's face melted.

"—!?"

Before Ferris's held breath, the girl's body melted and changed shape.

The small stature stretched out like a nightmare, and the vivid blonde hair changed color. The sweet features that stirred every onlooker's protective instinct turned dignified, and the underwear-like garments transformed into an indigo dress.

Ferris had only ever heard tell of it, but this was the first time he'd witnessed the transformation with his own eyes. A creator of nightmares, remaking the bodies of anything it touched—its own or another's—however it pleased.

And at that nightmare's manifestation, Ferris was struck utterly senseless.

"Ah, uh..."

"—What are you so surprised about?"

So said the face Ferris loved most of all, smoothing back long green hair.

The figure of Capella standing before him had transformed into the very image of the master Ferris revered. At this, Ferris's face went pale, and the dagger in his grip trembled in small shudders.

"See, all that spirit you had a second ago just up and vanished. The moment this face, this body, this voice stood right in front of you—there it went."

Smiling with a face of Crusch's that Ferris had never seen before, Capella slowly stepped forward.

She walked right up close to Ferris, near enough that an outstretched hand could reach, and—for whatever reason—lined the center of her own chest up with the trembling point of his dagger.

The blade's tip was gently pressed right to the very center of Crusch's ample chest.

Positioned such that a single thrust would drive it home.

"Your hated enemy is right here in front of you. Avenge me, please. It hurts, it hurts so much. Even breathing hurts. I can't keep my eyes open. It's like my heart isn't pumping blood but poison through my whole body. So hurry, avenge me. —That's what she's saying, you know."

"Hah, haah... haah...!"

"Just push the blade in firmly, and pull it out with a good hard twist, like you're gouging the wound wide open. That'll destroy the heart, sever the artery, and the bleeding won't stop. You can kill her."

Ferris's breathing quickened, his gaze unable to settle.

A priceless opportunity—the life of his lord's mortal enemy, offered up right in front of him. Just as she'd said, an attack would surely land right now. He could crush the heart. He could kill her.

Except that she wore the exact same form as the person he revered.

"Stab me, stab me, stab me, stab me, stab me, stab me."

"——"

"Stab me—!"

"Guh, aaaah!!"

Commanded like a curse, Ferris's dagger drove into her chest.

The blade pierced the body easily, destroying the heart within through the gaps between the ribs. The sharp edge was twisted, and with a cruel sound of muscle tearing apart, the dagger was pulled free amid a spray of blood.

"Hah, haahh."

Ferris stepped back to avoid the spattering blood, breathing hard. The dagger clattered from his hand, and he could see it soaking in the blood dripping onto the floor.

"Guh, cough."

Then Capella, stabbed through the chest, dropped to her knees, blood spilling in great amounts from her mouth.

Still wearing Crusch's appearance, her face stained with blood from pain and agony, her moist amber eyes looked up at Ferris with the expression of someone who'd seen something unbelievable.

"It hurts, it hurts so much... why, why would you do this..."

"You're the one who told me to stab you...! Told me to stab Crusch-sama!"

"It hurts, it hurts so much. ...You're horrible, I can't forgive you. After all those times you said you loved me, that you adored me... after we loved each other...!"

"—! Don't say something so stupid! Crusch-sama and I don't have that kind of relationship!"

"Oh, is that so? Well, guess there was a difference of opinion on the staging, then."

With an utterly unbothered expression, Capella wiped the blood off with her sleeve and stood up.

As she did, she brushed a hand over the wound in her chest, and the injury—which had unmistakably been there—vanished in an instant. Whatever had become of the anguished expression from before, she let out a sigh.

"Yeah, thing is, with somethin' like this, there's no point unless you commit from the very start. Gettin' a loving master and retainer to kill each other, with me lookin' just like her—that's where the catharsis of love is supposed to come from... but, failure, total failure."

"What is this farce... what is it you're trying to do? What are you trying to make me do!?"

"Nothin' in particular? There's no meaning to it, and there's nothin' I'm trying to make you do. Havin' a husband kill his wife's likeness is just something to pass the time. And keepin' a knight cross-dressed at your side, well, I just figured that was the kinda taste, the kinda relationship goin' on there."

"The promise between Crusch-sama and me isn't some shallow thing like that!"

"Callin' fetishes or sexual love 'shallow'—now that's a pretty flippant opinion, if you ask me."

Capella said this, tilting her head at Ferris's raised voice. Then she lifted her right hand, and its shape underwent another massive transformation.

Her palm became like the petals of an enormous flower, and extending tentacles swept Ferris's body aside, catching him as he was blown back and slamming him against the wall, constricting him all the while.

"Ka, fuh...!"

"Just like it looks and feels, you've got a weak, skinny little body, don't you. If you wanna be a woman that badly, you want me to make you one? In my hands, it's easy as pie—I can take that thing off and put a hole on you real quick."

"M-my body doesn't matter... more importantly, Crusch-sama...!"

"Ridiculous. Sayin' someone else matters more than yourself—don't feed me that pretty nonsense. And as for how to fix a body that's lost to the blood? Hah, if there's a method for that, I'd love to know it myself."

The tentacles writhed, and Ferris's slender limbs went congested with blood. His eyes rolled back in pain, and the creak of bone echoed through the room.

In place of the right hand now turned into a man-eating flower, the raised left hand formed the sickle-blade of a mantis. Still wearing Crusch's form, with a flower for a right arm and an insect's limb for a left, it was a grotesque sight.

And yet the face alone remained unchanged, still beautiful—which made it all the more horrifying.

"Turnin' you into a mushy lump of meat does sound like fun, but I ain't got all the time in the world either. Before anyone else comes up here, why don't I just go ahead and tidy up both you and your master together."

"—Ugh, guh."

"Still, you're all a bunch of hopelessly dim-witted people. Not only did nobody see me comin', even bein' attacked, the response is slow, slow. Just how long's it gonna take before..."

Saying this much, Capella's expression, loosened with pleasure, clouded over. As though doubting her own words, she leaned her face close to the gasping Ferris.

"Isn't this too slow, even accountin' for everything? Even with me comin' in from above, it's takin' way too long for anyone to come up here."

"...Ah."

"Whatever you're plottin', better spit it out quick, for your own sake. Otherwise, your dear, precious master's gonna transform into somethin' even uglier..."

Capella's sickle-arm turned toward Crusch lying on the bed, forcing a cruel choice on Ferris. At that demand, Ferris squeezed a voice out from trembling lips,

"—Use..."

"Ahn? What kinda beggin' for your life you gonna give me..."

"You... useless waste."

"Huh?"

His yellow eyes, filled with hatred, glared at Capella as he spat the words out.

An instant later, the tentacles binding Ferris's body began to smoke, and the petals lost their color and rotted away. Seeing the corrosion of her own right arm, even Capella's face registered shock.

"Ohh-la, what's this? What'd you do to my hand..."

"Well, guess bein' rotten to the core ain't your exclusive patent, huh."

The tentacles rotted away, and Ferris's body was freed.

Capella, tilting her head at this, was cut off by yet another voice. A lovely voice, with a distinctive intonation. Capella looked toward the bed where it had come from—and in the next instant, light streaked forth.

A beam of white heat so intense it gave the illusion that the room's temperature had spiked for a single instant. That searing light burned into Capella's face, blasting away the left half of it.

With the acrid stench of burning flesh and the charred cross-section of the wound laid bare, Capella staggered a large step backward. A tongue, stretching out like a snake's, licked over the surface of the wound, and she laughed.

"No mercy, even for a fellow member's face. ...Well, it's not unheard of for it to have next to no effect on some folk, but bein' done in this bad, that's a surprise."

"Fellow member, that's a misunderstandin'. We're business rivals... no, competitors. I ain't livin' easy enough to go soft on my attack just 'cause I recognize the face I'm hittin'."

"A conditional ally who's an eventual enemy. So aimin' for the face on purpose, that some kinda stress relief? If so, I'd say your personality's twisted way too far."

"Told ya already, I don't mix business with personal like that. I aimed for the head simply 'cause I was hopin' crushin' it there might just kill ya."

So saying, the figure who climbed down from the bed let out a breath of disappointment.

In Crusch's place, it was Anastasia who had been lying in the bed.

She smoothed back wavy hair dyed green, and returned a soft, elegant smile to Capella, who remained alive and well despite having her face burned off.

"Had my hopes up, but ya just won't die, will ya."

"A woman who's terrifyin' even while smilin'. Burnin' a woman's face without a moment's hesitation—that's past rational and straight into selfish, real rotten bitch-meat, exactly my type!"

"Bein' liked by somethin' like you, no thanks, that's the last thing I need. See, my type's already settled—hairy, nice to the touch, the kind everyone loves."

With perfect composure, Anastasia carried on the conversation with the monster Capella. She walked over to Ferris, who was coughing beside the bed, and pulled him up by the arm to his feet.

To Ferris, still teary-eyed, Anastasia prefaced with, "That's enough now, right?" and continued,

"There's nothin' more to be gotten outta her. At least for now, we're settin' aside the matter of Crusch-san."

"...I know. You went along with such a dangerous gamble for my sake, after all."

"My share of the blame in all this is plenty big too, so we're even."

Even the power of Ferris, the kingdom's foremost healer, couldn't cure Crusch's present state.

To save her, there was no way but to hear directly from the one responsible what had caused it and what should be done. Anastasia hadn't refused that plea of Ferris's.

It was Anastasia who had invited the candidates to the city of Priestella. And this was where things now stood. The responsibility of that fact was what kept Anastasia from turning down Ferris's request.

"After goin' this far, comin' up with nothin' really is disappointin', though."

"Well, it ain't like I'm just brimmin' with regret over not meetin' your expectations, but there was never any guarantee I'd even show up, was there?"

"There was Natsuki-kun's broadcast, wasn't there? Figured hearin' that would get somebody movin'."

Send out the main strike force, leave headquarters empty, and the enemy would surely move. There was no reason for the Witch Cult to meet them fair and square. Just as Capella herself had said.

"Natsuki-kun's a bit soft on that front, though."

A possibility she'd deliberately kept from pointing out—but given the nature of the Sin Archbishops, she'd narrowed it down to either 'Wrath' or 'Lust' being the one to launch the assault.

So Anastasia had set a trap, letting them attack the city hall as they pleased. Naturally, the real Crusch, along with the other wounded, had long since been evacuated to a shelter.

Of course no one else would come up to this floor. The only people in this building were on this top floor, and—

"...Hmm, heh. So there's at least a few of you with your heads screwed on right. But ain't you underestimatin' me here? Neither the cat-ears over there nor the missy here look like they can fight worth a damn."

"Callin' me missy, how embarrassin'. I'll have you know I'm actually one of the older ones, y'know?"

At Capella's remark, Anastasia gave her a wink.

At such composed behavior, Capella's eyes carried both revulsion and interest at once. Then Crusch's half-melted face twisted, and Capella's form reverted once more into the underwear-clad girl.

No one would guess this was Capella's true form, but in this second return to the girl's shape, Capella wore a cruel expression and pointed at Anastasia.

"Decided. I'll leave just that cute little face of yours and turn everything below your neck into a grub. Let's see if you can still run that mouth of yours at me once that happens."

"That sure sounds scary... which is why, time to say goodbye for now."

"——"

At Anastasia's rejection, Capella, baring her menace, made a puzzled face.

Anastasia gave a tug at her still-green hair and looked around the room.

"I told ya, I was waitin' for you to come. —And if that's the case, there's no way I'd be waitin' without any preparations at all, is there?"

The instant she finished speaking, Anastasia tapped the toe of her shoe lightly against the floor of the room.

Twice, sharply, as if it were some kind of signal—instantly, cracks split open in the floor beneath Capella's feet, and the girl's body, thrown off balance, dropped to the floor below.

"Oh my, my."

The floor of the room gave way beneath her, and the floor of the story below, into which she fell, had likewise opened into a hole.

Capella kept falling further down still, plunging through a full four stories' worth of height in one go, dropped into an underground space deeper than even the ground floor.

With a wet splat, Capella's body burst apart.

Struck against the floor unguarded, the girl's body lay crushed upon the cold ground.

Blood sprayed from her whole face, her limbs snapped in a pitiful ruin of a shape. But the body, reduced to a lump of flesh, at once set its limbs writhing, and the girl's body shifted like formless water, standing itself back up.

What appeared there was the figure of a woman of bewitching bearing.

She bared her voluptuous body without reserve in a revealing outfit, her rare black hair worked into a long braid. There was a vaguely dangerous air about her, a beauty that left the heart unsettled.

Neither Ferris nor Anastasia knew whose likeness this form was based on. To begin with, the two of them remaining on the fourth floor couldn't even see the shape she had changed into.

So there was no one to be surprised at her having taken that form.

"Ahh, ahh, seriously, seriously... you've gone and given me all kindsa fun. Kyahahahaha!"

Showing no sign of feeling either the near-death state of her body or the pain of dying, Capella let her throat burst into laughter. That frivolous, coquettish voice, hardly matching her post-transformation form, rang out through the underground space of the city hall.

It was a dim space, cold, damp air drifting through it. It didn't have the feel of a plain basement room—more likely, it was part of some facility for managing the waterways that ran throughout the entire city.

The sound of flowing water came from somewhere, and a breeze drifted in from a location other than the hole overhead.

"Gettin' such a passionate welcome, why, even these breasts of mine that can change size however I like are bouncin' with excitement. Hurry back and hold me, retrain me in your arms so I can't love anyone but myself..."

"Ain't goin' back."

"——"

Someone cut off Capella, who had flushed and was trembling with excitement.

A low, muffled, world-weary man's voice.

At that voice, Capella raised her face, and a figure stepped forward out of the darkness underground.

Seeing it, Capella's expression changed entirely. The rapturous look she'd worn until then twisted into something hateful, and she glared at the figure.

"My sense of aesthetics don't got any mercy for someone tryin' to hide how ugly they are, y'know."

"That so. Relax, then. My sense of aesthetics ain't got any mercy to spare for you either."

The weary voice answered Capella so, and let out a sigh heavy enough to weigh down the spirit.

And then—

"Heard it up there, didn't ya? Your movements got read clean through by the rotten-personality crew on our side. And well... ain't nobody out there with a worse personality than our princess, is there?"

Even as he spoke, there came the weighty sound of a blade sliding from its sheath.

A thick-bladed sword was drawn from its scabbard, its dull gleam catching the light spilling down through the hole overhead.

Standing there was a one-armed man. A shadow in a black helmet. An eccentric in bizarre garb.

The eccentric leveled the dao blade he'd drawn with a single arm, aiming it at Capella.

"Bit early for a welcome, but I'm in a foul mood today. —Before I die, get lost, and quick. Mollusk."
