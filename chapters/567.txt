Arc 7 Chapter 62: "Order of the Isolated Island"

—Not even he knows why he's here, or what the "Nine Divine Generals" are.

A sudden bombshell of a statement — Subaru failed to defuse it, and his mind was swallowed by confusion.
It was a shock far more vivid than being doused with cold water; he blinked rapidly without meaning to.

"W-what do you mean? You don't know why you're here...?"

"There's no deeper meaning than the words themselves. Before I knew it, I'd been thrown onto this Sword Slave Isle, with no context before or after and no outlook for what comes next — I was in quite a fix. Well, this place happens to suit me fairly well, so it's not really as much of a fix as all that, but I figured I'd phrase it that way."

"――"

"Oh? Lost in thought again, are you? Ahh, Bassu really does love to think things over. No, I think that's fine — I'm more the type who isn't too fond of thinking, myself. I won't mock or bother people who like to mull things over. I'll keep my mouth shut!"

It took him quite a while to actually close his mouth, but Cecilus did eventually go quiet. Even so, his feet kept tapping away the whole time, and he fidgeted around restlessly enough to clutter up Subaru's field of view.
He might not have been mocking him, but he was plenty distracting all the same.
But Subaru had no room to spare for pointing that out. If he was going to press the issue, he should press on something that actually mattered.
For instance—

"Why is it that you don't know about the 'Nine Divine Generals,' but you do know you're called the 'Blue Lightning'? That doesn't make sense."

"Ah, the answer to that question is simple. That thrilling epithet — none other than I came up with it myself! Cecilus Segmunt, the 'Blue Lightning' of Vollachia! Well? It's a nickname that leaves a truly vivid and elegant impression, don't you think! Someday I'll make this name resound far and wide and build an age where everyone's eyes are drawn to me, the leading man! That's what it expresses — my ambition!"

"A nickname... you came up with yourself..."

Answered with his chest puffed out proudly, Subaru's shoulders slumped in defeat.
Seeing Subaru like that, Cecilus peered casually into his face, going "Huh? What's wrong~?" But Subaru had lost the energy to answer.

The matter of whether the boy in front of him was the real Cecilus Segmunt or a fake had been settled.
Even the theory that plenty of adults simply can't bring themselves to respect a child didn't have the power to overturn an outrageous statement like not knowing what the "Nine Divine Generals" were.
No matter how you looked at it, there was no covering for a genuine General claiming ignorance of the "Nine Divine Generals."

"Not knowing you're a general of the Empire — that's just way too ridiculous..."

Trying to make sense of logic that full of holes was simply impossible.
And so, in Subaru's mind, the possibility that the boy before him was the real Cecilus vanished. He was nothing more than a boy who'd cried wolf, taking a prank far too far — the fake Cecilus.

With that, the slender thread of hope that had sprouted in this dead end snapped clean through.
If just one seed of hope still remained, it was this: the fake Cecilus was a liar, so perhaps that terrifying story about the island just now had been a lie too—

"—So this is where you were, Segmunt."

"Waaah!?"

In the next instant, a thick male voice was cast onto his back as he sat lost in thought, and Subaru jumped.
It wasn't the fake Cecilus's voice, nor of course Tanza's, sleeping on the bed — and when Subaru turned toward the entrance of the healing room where the voice had come from, his shock only compounded.

—Because the figure standing at the entrance to the healing room was an absolutely enormous man.

"H-huge...!"

The man was tall enough that an exclamation slipped out of Subaru unbidden.
No, it wasn't just his height — he had thickness both vertically and horizontally, and "every part of his body was thick and huge" was the description that suited him best. His neck and limbs were as sturdy as logs, and the black uniform he wore, something like a policeman's or soldier's, was stretched taut, bulging as if it might burst at the seams.

Regardless of the fact that Subaru himself had shrunk, this was a giant he had to crane his neck painfully to look up at.
He easily stood over two meters tall — possibly the biggest human Subaru had seen since coming to this other world. Atop that enormous body sat an extremely rugged face, with fangs that jutted out even with his mouth closed, and blue-tinted skin — there were simply too many distinguishing features.
And even then, the most conspicuous feature of all hadn't been mentioned yet.
That most conspicuous feature was—

"Ah, hello there, Gustav-san! Splendid attire again this morning! Paying attention to every last detail of one's grooming is the basic mark of a gentleman — truly an attitude worth emulating!"

"Flattery isn't necessary. As for you, it's good to see you're as needlessly healthy as ever. I, in my fairness, have strictly judged that an existence such as yours ought to be spent in the arena."

So saying, the giant man — the one called Gustav — let out a long breath at the fake Cecilus's glib words and answered while crossing his thick "four" arms.

Yes, four arms.
The ogre-faced giant, his massive body wrapped in a mass of muscle, had four arms — two growing from each shoulder. Subaru recalled hearing from an old acquaintance that this was a trait of a certain Demi-Human race, the Multi-Arm Tribe.

An old acquaintance—

"Um... right, Wilhelm-san."

Subaru, who struggled even to recall the name of an acquaintance.
Ignoring Subaru's struggle, the fake Cecilus laughed "ahaha" at Gustav's words.

"Much obliged for your concern! I understand what you mean too, Gustav-san! If I'm ever to die, it should be on a battlefield. It's a great help having someone so sensible as the island's master — I really am a man blessed with good fortune, aren't I!"

"Conflating public duty with private feeling is an obstacle to order. There is no reason for this office to commit such folly."

"Yes, yes, of course I understand! My deepest thanks for Governor Gustav's consideration~!"

At the fake Cecilus pressing his palms together with a grinning face, Gustav narrowed his intelligent eyes.
Somehow, that line about "not conflating public duty with private feeling" sounded to Subaru like Gustav personally disliked the fake Cecilus, but there was no point digging into that.
Someone other than the fake Cecilus he could actually talk to had finally appeared.

"Now then, Segmunt. About that boy there."

Right on cue, Gustav turned his attention toward Subaru as well.
Looked down upon from a tremendous height, there was a suffocating sense of being wrapped in pressure just from being gazed at. His four crossed arms ended in fists each roughly the size of a child's head — if he were struck by one of those, Subaru would surely die.
Subaru swallowed hard despite himself, and beside him the fake Cecilus clapped his hands.

"Right, right, he's finally woken up. He was worried about the young lady he came with, so I brought him here. Only, it seems the young lady sleeping here wasn't the one he was hoping for, and all sorts of crossed wires have resulted. He was just riding out that shock, and now here we are, about to get the story rolling!"

"Well explained. But if he had woken, I recall ordering that he be brought to this office first. That was the condition under which this office granted permission for this boy and girl to come ashore."

"Was it? Sorry, I'm forgetful."

"――"

At the fake Cecilus's breezy reply, Gustav silently rubbed his brow with his fingers.
And then, the man so much closer to the sky than Subaru and the others glared up at the ceiling, muttering to himself, "This office is the guardian of order, it does not conflate public duty with private feeling..."
Subaru didn't quite understand the relationship between the two, but the man was probably put through a great deal of trouble.
Realizing that, Subaru felt a little relieved — sorry as he was to Gustav for it.

"So I'm not the only one Ceci's been dragging around."

"'Dragging around' — that's unfair. I did accompany Bassu to get the explanation he wanted, the person he wanted to meet, and the place he wanted to go, or so I thought."

"Yeah, right, I went too far. Though the explanation had a lot of detours, the person I got introduced to wasn't the one I wanted to meet, and I got shot down and told I couldn't go where I wanted to go."

The fake Cecilus probably didn't mean any harm, so there was no point saying it.
Told that by Subaru, the fake Cecilus pouted his lips in discontent with a "tch." Then, as if having safely dealt with his own irritation toward the boy, Gustav turned back to face Subaru once more.
And then—

"Boy, since you've woken, let us speak properly."

"I'd be glad if you would. I wanted to hear things from someone else too. And if it's someone important on this island, all the better."

"Hmm."

As Subaru spoke carefully, Gustav touched his chin with his two right hands.
At a glance he looked intimidating, but his way of speaking and his bearing were very calm and quiet, and there was an intelligent light in his eyes — the impression he gave was close to that of the shaggy-haired Zikr.
In other words, a prime example of an adult one could actually talk to.
As if to confirm Subaru's hopes, Gustav gave a deep, weighty nod, and—

"This office is Gustav Morello, entrusted with the administration of this island by His Excellency Emperor Vincent Vollachia, the seventy-seventh emperor of the Sacred Vollachia Empire. Can you tell me your name and affiliation?"

He gave his position and name politely, meeting Subaru head-on.
Struck by that fact, Subaru lightly widened his eyes, then placed a hand on his chest and bowed his head.

"Th-thank you for introducing yourself. My name is Natsuki... Natsuki Schwarz."

"I see. Schwarz, is it."

Even while overwhelmed, Subaru gave Gustav his name in return.
He'd hesitated for a moment over whether to use his real name or an alias, and the answer that resulted was somewhat halfway, but for the moment he should consider it a decent job.
Honestly, he didn't know whether lying to Gustav was the right call or not.
But it wasn't a good thing for Subaru's real name to spread. It hadn't become a big deal with the fake Cecilus since he was just a fake General, but there was no telling who might be well-informed about the Kingdom's affairs.
Whatever could be hidden should be hidden — even if Cecilus, hearing Subaru's current alias, tilted his head in a big, exaggerated "Hm~?"

"However, if it's Natsuki Schwarz, then why does Segmunt call you 'Bassu'?"

"I think Ceci has his own logic for that. Want to check with him?"

"...No, let's not. Given the duty entrusted to this office, there's no time to spend on fruitless thought. This office is no great intellect, but it does wish to be wise."

Shaking his head, Gustav made what was, from Subaru's perspective too, a wise decision.
Crediting that to the fake Cecilus's ordinary conduct felt somehow irritating, so Subaru decided to chalk it up to his own good conduct instead.
In any case—

"You've answered your name, and from what I can see, there's no apparent physical trouble. According to the healer, your stamina was significantly depleted — are you aware of that yourself?"

"Stamina... I guess I do feel a little tired. But, um, thanks for helping me out and everything."

"Let me correct one thing, Schwarz. It was not this office that helped you, but Segmunt there. This office merely consented to accepting you both, and that only on certain conditions."

"I-I see."

At Gustav's logically consistent replies, Subaru nearly stumbled over his own words.
His manner of speaking and its content weren't particularly sympathetic, and the tone of his voice sat somewhere between cold and emotionless, but the fact that his explanations were easy to follow was, purely and simply, a help.
Compared to the fake Cecilus, the ratio of words spoken to actual information conveyed was on an entirely different level.
Even that last question seemed to be out of concern for the condition of the newly-woken Subaru, and at this rate, maybe if he explained things, Gustav would understand.

That Subaru, and Tanza too, had ended up on this Sword Slave Isle by some mistake.

"Gustav-san, me and this girl... her name's Tanza — it's like a mix-up, or some kind of mistake that brought us here."

"What do you mean, some kind of mistake?"

"—! Well, I don't know the reason. But we were supposed to be somewhere much farther away... you know Chaosflame, right? We should have been in that city!"

According to the fake Cecilus, this island and the Magic City were quite far apart.
That alone should be enough to make him understand what had happened to Subaru and Tanza was an emergency. Being flung all the way from the Magic City to this island was no ordinary occurrence.

"I know it's awfully self-serving of me to say this after being helped, but I can't stay here for long. My friends are definitely looking for us! If at all possible, I want to let them know we're safe and rejoin them right away."

If possible, leaving here together with Tanza was the ideal.
He didn't know why the two of them, Subaru and Tanza, were on this island, but Yorna was surely worried about Tanza. Winning Yorna over as an ally had been the objective from the start, so Abel surely wouldn't be angry with him for doing something that would make her happy.
Besides, Subaru liked Yorna.
If that kind woman would be pleased, there was nothing better than that, he thought.

"So, I want to get out of here even a second sooner—"

"—That's enough. That's enough, Schwarz."

As Subaru leaned forward and tried to raise his voice, an enormous palm stopped him. Silenced by the two right hands thrust in front of his face, Subaru choked out an "ugh" and lost his words.
Past those right hands, Gustav stroked his own chin and head simultaneously with his left hands, and—

"I have understood your claim. I would say I have grasped, roughly, the circumstances you find yourself in. That said, you must also hear this office out. That is what fairness is."

"Fairness..."

"Yes, fairness. Impartial, strict, an even standard without bias... an absolute principle indispensable to constructing what is called order."

Having had his argument cut off, and now hearing these words piled slowly on top of it, Subaru furrowed his brow.
Fairness, impartiality — from the position of someone in a panic, it all sounded terribly leisurely. But Subaru understood that defying Gustav here would accomplish nothing, and so he nodded regardless.
At Subaru's response, Gustav closed one eye and said, "Good."

"You remember what this office said just now, yes? That it was Segmunt who helped you, and that this office's permission for you and the girl to come ashore was conditional."

"That... yes, I remember, but..."

His attention had been on the part about being helped, and he hadn't given much thought to the conditions part. But Gustav had indeed said as much to him, in that heavy voice.
But what were the conditions? The conditions for letting Subaru and Tanza onto the island?

"Schwarz, you possess appropriate judgment; you possess sufficient knowledge to hold a conversation without difficulty; you possess the upbringing to use polite speech with this office; you have an evaluation of good health from the healer's diagnosis; and there is the fact that you yourself report no trouble with your own condition. Any objection?"

"It makes my head spin when you say it all at once, but... I probably don't have an objection."

"'Probably' is a rather unconvincing answer. Is it, or is it not, one or the other?"

"...I have no objection."

Gustav's gaze would not allow Subaru a half-hearted answer.
Overwhelmed by this, when Subaru looked back at him, his gaze was bounced right back off a terrifying, statue-like face whose eye color and expression never changed.

The conversation was proceeding entirely under Gustav's lead.
That fact was making his unease well up and grow, but no immediate countermeasure came to mind. As ever, Subaru had no information. In other words, no options.
In Subaru's place—

"See, didn't I say so? That Bassu would properly measure up in Gustav-san's eyes. We even made it in time for the schedule — nothing to complain about, right?"

"But the girl has not woken."

"Well, as for that, you know, there ought to be ways to adjust or fudge things using your governor's authority and such, right?"

"Indeed. Since the girl has not woken, we shall begin with one fewer person than the regulation calls for."

"Oh dear, did I just stir up trouble for myself!?"

Leaving the bewildered Subaru aside, it was the fake Cecilus who was doing the talking with Gustav.
The main thrust of the conversation was unclear, but Subaru could gather that it concerned him and Tanza, and that the fake Cecilus hadn't managed to steer it well.
What's more, it didn't feel like it would be the fake Cecilus himself who paid the price for that failure.

"Sorry, Bassu. It seems this is about as far as my help can extend. Do your best on your own from here. I believe you can pull it off!"

"W-wait wait wait! This conversation is way too ominous! What was that exchange just now!? Gustav-san! The condition for letting us stay on the island is..."

"—Participation in the 'Sparka.'"

"Spa...?"

An unfamiliar word, dropped on him from on high, made Subaru scowl.
Since it was called "participation," this "Sparka" must be some kind of event. Some sort of event held on this Sword Slave Isle — he had nothing but a bad feeling about it.
To begin with, Al had said that life on the Sword Slave Isle was harsh and painful.
An event held there was bound to be something scary or something painful.

"Wait, Gustav-san! Why do we have to take part in the Sparka!? We didn't come to this island on our own!"

"Prisoners of war, slaves, criminals... the circumstances of those who come ashore on this Ginunhive vary, but almost none of them come willingly. And this office's role is only to impose order upon this Sword Slave Isle gathered with such lawless men, and to devote itself entirely to duty for the sake of His Excellency's wishes."

"His Excellency... the Emperor's wishes..."

"—To remake this Sword Slave Isle into the place where the most meaningful blood in the Empire flows."

It was a declaration so bone-dry that it chilled the blood of Subaru, who had bitten back at him.
He had felt that the color of Gustav's eyes and his way of speaking were cold and unshaken, but that had been a mistake. There was a more fitting word to describe Gustav.
He was, yes, inorganic and cold, just like a robot.

Gasping at this shift in impression, Subaru froze, and Gustav called out, "Schwarz."
And, looking down at Subaru, who was far smaller than himself—

"Call him His Excellency the Emperor. The first time is a caution, the second a warning. There will be no third."

"...Ugh."

"And furthermore, let me state again — refusal of the 'Sparka' is not permitted. Should you refuse to participate in the 'Sparka,' both you and the girl will be made to follow your original fate."

"What do you mean, our original fate..."

"To deny Segmunt's plea and refuse permission to come ashore. —Which is to say, simply becoming prey for the aquatic Witchbeasts that live in the lake."

Without hesitation, Gustav plainly snipped away Subaru's future options.
A giant of a man who plainly told a child that refusal meant death. If there was ever a phrase for "no blood or tears," it was this — Subaru found himself wanting to curse his own bad luck, that even shrunk down he only ever ran into harsh experiences.
Normally, shouldn't people be kinder to children?
Or was it that in the Vollachia Empire, where the strong are great, children being killed one after another simply for being weak was something that passed without a second thought?

"If that's how it is, then a country like that should just be destroyed..."

"The intent is unclear, but I will permit that abuse. Suppress all speech and discontent only accumulates. The seed of great folly is a thing that grows with discontent as its seedbed and resentment as its water."

Slowly shaking his head, Gustav gripped the shoulder of Subaru, who was gritting his teeth.
Caught in a palm that could easily grip even his own head, Subaru couldn't resist. He even considered forcing his way free and fleeing from here, but—

"I wouldn't recommend that approach, Bassu. You'd probably just end up in a much worse spot. This is less my being overly cautious and more a request coming from a stronger feeling of, well, that'd just be boring, wouldn't it."

"Thanks for sharing your feelings. Drop dead, Ceci."

"Wow, and after I properly helped you too, what a thing to say. Still, I do understand the feeling that makes Bassu want to say that, so I won't get angry."

With the exit casually blocked off by the fake Cecilus, Subaru was robbed of his last shred of resistance.
In the end, even hurling foul language at the fake Cecilus — whose loyalties remained unclear — to vent his frustration at the situation he'd been placed in, the man's reaction was like pushing against a curtain: no resistance, no response worth the effort.

In any case, Subaru was going to be made to participate in this rotten event called the "Sparka."
Refuse, and he'd become fish food; go along with it properly, and—

"If you prove your qualification, this office, as governor, will take responsibility for welcoming you as a sword slave."

"...That's just great."

Succeed, and he'd become a slave; fail, and he'd become fish food.
He'd said not one good thing had happened since he shrank — but let him correct that.

—Not one good thing had happened since he arrived in this Vollachia Empire.

"...I hate the Vollachia Empire. I really hate it."

Subaru's venomous, resentful mutter was small, quiet, and thin, as if out of consideration for the still-sleeping Tanza.
And then—


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△


—He could feel, with his whole body, the heat wrapping around the venue.

Unsettlingly, though countless gazes rained down on him, there was almost no clamor.
Call it a quiet excitement, or perhaps madness — he was made to experience the rare sensation of silence itself feeling deafening.
That said, Subaru had no room to feel anger over it, or to voice complaints.

"Shit, shit... you've gotta be kidding me, this is the worst...!"
"I'll do it. I'll damn well do it..."
"Silence! The rest of you, obey my instructions and behave! Do that and you won't have to die!"

Cutting through that deafening silence were three men standing close by Subaru.
Respectively: a Lizard Human with rock-like skin, a bald man with numerous tattoos carved into his body, and a young man with rust-colored hair grown long and well-formed features.
All of them differed in upbringing and race, but they shared just one thing in common.
That was: none of them counted Subaru among their number, and each had life-or-death tension dwelling in his eyes.
And, at this rate, though their birthdays differed, their death days were about to be the same.

"—Gentlemen, first, well done in coming ashore on this island. His Excellency Emperor Vincent Vollachia, in the capital, must surely be pleased as well. As the governor personally entrusted by His Excellency the Emperor with the administration of this Sword Slave Isle, I now grant you an honorable trial."

The one who declared this in a ringing voice, looking down on the arena from a prime seat atop the high wall, was Gustav Morello — governor of the Sword Slave Isle, Ginunhive, and the man who ran the shows held on this land.

And then, down below Gustav — as the gate of the passage that sent warriors out into the arena opened, a ghastly, monstrous shape slowly, slowly emerged from the darkness.

A lion's face fitted with red eyes, and four deer-like legs with hooves. Twisted horns and fangs, a horrifically long tail, and a body so massive it made even Gustav's giant frame look small.
Drooling and growling from its mouth as it lumbered forward, a Witchbeast — no, here it was called a gladiator beast, a fearsome enemy.

That was the very opponent Subaru and the other three would have to face—

"—Prove your dignity as a resilient citizen of the Empire, as His Excellency the Emperor desires!"

—the examiner of the savage trial known as the "Sparka," the test to become a sword slave.
