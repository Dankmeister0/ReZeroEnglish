Arc 1 Chapter 1: "A Serrated Ten-Yen Coin Is Useless"

—This was seriously, seriously bad.


As he stood completely penniless and at a total loss, his mind was filled with that single thought.
Well, calling himself "penniless" wasn't entirely accurate. His wallet was in his pocket, containing his entire net worth, even if it was somewhat heavy on small change and light on paper bills.
It was enough cash to take a trip to the nearest shopping mall back home, buy a book at the bookstore, and grab some lunch. Even so, "penniless" was the only way to describe his current situation.
Because, after all—

"I guess monetary value works totally differently here..."

Flicking a ten-yen coin—a rare "serrated ten"—in his hand, the boy let out a long sigh.
He was a boy without any particularly striking features. Short black hair, an average height that was neither tall nor short. His build was somewhat muscular, perhaps from working out, giving him a sporty look combined with his cheap gray tracksuit.
Only his sharp, sanpaku eyes left a lasting impression, though right now even the corners of his eyes drooped weakly, devoid of any energy.

His appearance was mundane enough that he would be lost in a crowd in an instant.
And yet, the eyes of the people looking at him were filled with a dense, incomprehensible look, as if staring at a curiosity.

Naturally enough—after all, among the people staring at the boy, not a single one had black hair, nor was any of them wearing a tracksuit.
Their hair varied wildly, from blond, white, and brown to green and blue. Furthermore, their outfits—armor, dancer-like garments, all-black robes—were far too archetype-appropriate.

Exposed to a wave of unabashed stares, the boy crossed his arms, forced to accept the reality.

"In other words... this is that, huh?"

Snapping his fingers, he pointed at the people looking his way.

"—Looks like I've been summoned to another world."

Right in front of him, a carriage-like vehicle drawn by a giant lizard-like creature passed by.

His life spanned seventeen years, and telling his entire story would take seventeen years in itself.
Omitting all that to briefly explain his current standing, he was a "third-year high school shut-in."
To explain it in detail: "a hopeless piece of trash who abandoned his parents' expectations and everything else to lock himself in his shell right when college entrance exams were right around the corner."

There was no particular reason for his becoming a shut-in.
It started on a normal weekday when he happened to vaguely think, *Getting up today is too much trouble*, and put skipping school into practice.
From there, his self-imposed absences accumulated seamlessly, until before he knew it, he was a full-fledged shut-in bringing his parents to tears.
Wallowing in laziness all day long, sinking endlessly into internet spaces devoid of actual human interaction—

"And the result of that is getting summoned to another world...? Man, even saying it out loud makes zero sense."

Reassessing his situation once again, Subaru let out another sigh, having lost count of how many he'd uttered so far.

Having moved away from the street where he had been showered with curious gazes until a moment ago, he was now sitting down in a slightly dim back alley.
The ground was paved—shoddy work compared to modern Japan, but not terrible.

"Assuming the current situation is an isekai fantasy, I guess the civilization is the cliché medieval style? From what I've seen, no machinery, and building materials are pretty much unified into stone or wood..."

Recalling the sights he had seen before sitting down in the alleyway, he organized the information in his head.
Thanks to having had plenty of time for daydreams on a daily basis, his mental preparedness for being "summoned to another world" was top-notch.

First, he needed to calmly assess the civilization of this era, the differences from modern Japan in food, clothing, and shelter, variations in physical phenomena, and whether communication with the local "humanoid creatures" was possible.

"Alright, good job, me. Those daydreams weren't for nothing. Civilizational level check is good enough; for now, my money is useless. On the bright side, I could talk to that shopkeeper, so communication isn't an issue."

Upon realizing he had been summoned, the first thing Subaru did was attempt to negotiate at a 'greengrocer's?'. He had tried to buy the 'apples?' displayed at the storefront, only to have his Japanese yen rejected.
From what he saw then, currency in this world seemed to consist of gold, silver, and copper coins. The ease of understanding a worldview where coins themselves possessed value was very typical of an isekai fantasy, as expected.

"Though I bet it'll decline once debased coins, low-quality counterfeits, or 500-won-coin-esque tricks start popping up."

*It'd be way too heavy to carry around anyway,* he muttered inwardly as another carriage drawn by a lizard passed by on the main street. A grand cloud of dust whirled into the air, but the passing people seemed used to it and paid it no mind.

"Still, they're fewer in number compared to cars... Come to think of it, I haven't seen any dogs or cats so far, either."

The giant lizard pulling the 'carriage?' was perhaps a size larger than a horse. Since it was slender, its overall mass might not be much different, but having a reptile that huge felt intensely bizarre.

"I guess it's normal... both the lizards and the way people look."

And then there was the part he had pushed off confirming until last: the unusual appearances of humans in this world.

He could accept the colorful hair. In theory, dyeing could produce any hair color, and being in an isekai fantasy, he had already accepted that aspect.
What he considered an issue was something else—for instance, "animal ears."

From a quick scan, he had spotted 'dog ears' and 'cat ears.' There were 'bunnies,' and as a peculiar variant, he felt like he'd caught a brief glimpse of someone looking like a 'lizardman.'
Yet there were also humans who looked no different from Subaru himself, leading to the conclusion—

"Genre: Isekai fantasy. Civilization: Typical medieval style. Demi-humans: definitely a thing. Wars and adventures: probably definitely a thing too. Animals have slight differences, but no real change in their roles... I guess?"

Having organized that much, Subaru let out a long breath, distinct from a sigh.

Speaking his situation aloud, his eyebrows furrowed at the convenient, trope-heavy setup.
If things went according to his daydream scenarios, he ought to be making full use of modern knowledge from here on to pull off an "OP cheat protagonist" run. But the worldview was subtly different from one where his prepared knowledge would be useful.

"My simulation for being sent back to the Sengoku period was flawless, though... If I'd been sent to Sengoku Japan, I would've helped Nobunaga conquer the whole country."

In an isekai fantasy, the only usable knowledge he had was maybe the ratio for black gunpowder.
Even that would be meaningless depending on this world's level of civilization. If the inevitable 'Magic' of an isekai fantasy existed, gunpowder would probably be treated as nothing more than fireworks.

"Well, magic not being omnipotent is also kind of a trope, I suppose. If there's a chance to contribute to scientific development, I'll give it my all... but the immediate problem is..."

...the fact that he had absolutely no clue about the cause or purpose behind his summon.

He remembered what happened before being summoned very clearly. Leaving home for the first time in a while, he was on his way back from buying cup ramen for a late-night snack at a convenience store. He hadn't felt like riding his bike, so he was walking.
And on the way, he even remembered casually looking up at the night sky and thinking, *It's almost a full moon tonight.*
Then, when he lowered his gaze and blinked, it was suddenly daytime.

From night to day in an instant.

He knew immediately that an abnormal situation had occurred. Though that was all he knew.
He was calm now, but his panic immediately after was a display of wimpiness worthy of being passed down for generations.

"Well, considering I'm the eldest son and now missing, there won't be any future generations to pass it down to anyway."

Muttering to himself, Subaru checked his belongings once again.
In an isekai fantasy situation, initial equipment was far more important than expected. Right now, what mattered was the fact that he was connected to reality, no matter how thin the thread.

First, his cell phone (battery about to die), wallet (full of video rental shop membership cards), cup ramen bought at the convenience store (tonkotsu soy sauce flavor), snack chips from the same store (corn potage flavor), his beloved gray tracksuit (unwashed), well-worn sneakers (two years old)—that was all.

"I'm doomed... Why didn't I have at least a pistol on me? What am I supposed to do with this?"

The only useful thing was probably the snacks to fill his belly. Though that would only stave off light hunger once, and then it'd be over.

"The situation is hopeless. And as expected, the cause is unknown. I don't remember stepping through a mirror or falling into a pond, and above all, if this is a summoning story, where's the beautiful girl who summoned me?!"

The main heroine was absent, so to speak. In the realm of 2D fiction, this was an unacceptable dereliction of duty.
To be summoned and dumped without a goal was practically being used and discarded.
In fact, having finished assessing his current situation, Subaru couldn't even escape into reality and had no choice but to hang his head.

"Seriously, give me a break. What do you expect me to do?"

Weak complaints and whines spilled out; he was already on the verge of breaking. *I want to go home,* was all he could think.
Daydreams were best left as daydreams. Being summoned to another world was fun when you were going on an overpowered cheat rampage inside your head, but if you were actually dropped into one, what else could you do besides cringe and shrink back?

"For now, my goal is just to stay alive, but... can I really make it with a Communication Level of 1?"

Aside from his family, the only people he'd properly conversed with were convenience store clerks. He had been living like that for nearly a year. He had long forgotten how to gauge distance with people.

"I can type as fast as speaking in online chat, though..."

Subaru wriggled his fingers nervously, dreading the future.
Then, his expression changed. The reason was a sound.
Footsteps suddenly echoed through the alley—looking up, he saw three men standing at the alley's entrance, blocking the way.
