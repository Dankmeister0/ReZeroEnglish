Arc 5 Chapter 12: "The Incredible Atmosphere of the Tatami Room"

The faces gathered in the tatami room were, in every sense of the word, an illustrious assembly.

"Still, it's such a surprise that Reinhard and Wilhelm-san turned out to be family. Now that you mention it, they both do seem like they'd be good with a sword."

"It's only natural you wouldn't notice without knowing beforehand, but if you start calling people family based on that kind of similarity, you're gonna end up with a pretty huge family, Emilia-tan."

They sat Japanese-style, on cushions laid over the wooden floor, facing a long low table.
In the middle of that row of cushions, Emilia leaned in to whisper to Subaru in secret. She might have been nervous, but for now, what she whispered was a remark with absolutely no substance to it.

"Betty's on guard too, but there's nothing to be done if someone suddenly makes a move. Subaru had best be careful not to earn cold stares from everyone here, I suppose."

"I get you don't mean anything by it, but it brings back actual memories of being on the receiving end of those cold stares, so cut it out, it hurts."

With Subaru between them, Beatrice sat on the opposite side from Emilia, urging caution. For the record, Emilia sat with her legs folded to one side, Subaru cross-legged, and Beatrice in formal seiza. It was the result of Subaru goading her into it, and her knees were already starting to tremble.

"If it comes down to it, Garfiel will move. Besides, with this crowd, there's no need to worry about anyone suddenly pulling something underhanded."

Setting aside Beatrice's trembling knees, Subaru's gaze drifted to the edge of the room. There, against the wall, Garfiel sat sprawled on a cushion with one knee up. He noticed Subaru looking and gave a light wave, but his hand was quickly commandeered by Mimi beside him, keeping him busy.
At present, the important figures were gathered around the low table in the middle of the room, while everyone else sat lined along the edges, watching the proceedings. Which meant that, beside Mimi, there was also Hetaro glaring daggers fit to kill at Garfiel, and Tivey wearing a face that said none of this concerned him.
Joshua, for his part, could be seen sitting seiza beside them, looking distinctly uncomfortable.

"Thank you for extending this invitation to us. Felt-sama's arrival at the inn has been delayed somewhat, but she is already here in Priestella, so it shouldn't be long now."

"No need to be so formal. Just answerin' our sudden summons is more than enough — though I did have a laugh at how everyone's arrivals ended up overlapping more than I expected."

Anastasia, there to receive him, responded warmly to Reinhard's greeting, given in proper envoy's form. At her words, Reinhard raised his head and turned a smile toward Julius, who had taken his place beside Anastasia.

"It's been a while, Julius. Not since we last crossed paths at your trading company."

"Ah, that's right. I'm sorry for imposing on you with this summons. Still, it's a fortunate thing that we could confirm each other's safety like this."

Exchanging a brief greeting as friends, Reinhard crossed the room and returned to his seat. If the long table were divided by camp, his was the seat furthest from the place of honor. Anastasia sat at the head, with the Emilia Camp beside her. Across from Emilia sat Reinhard — that is, the Felt Camp. And seated across from Anastasia was—

"It has been quite some time since we last met like this, hasn't it."

The one who said this with a graceful smile was a beautiful woman with long green hair.
Her almond-shaped amber eyes held a gentle warmth, and her womanly figure was wrapped in a navy-blue dress — a long-skirted one, naturally — giving her an air of ladylike grace.
Anyone who had known her before would find it hard to believe she was the same person.

"It's been a while for you too, Crusch-sama. Not since the commendation ceremony, right?"

"Yes, that's right. I apologize for the trouble I caused at that time. I've heard about everyone's accomplishments since then. I thought to myself, how impressive."

The one Emilia addressed without hesitation was Crusch, who now answered with a gentler manner. The decisive, dignified bearing she'd once had was lost along with her memories. It still hadn't returned, and stripped of her former sharpness, she remained nothing more than a beautiful noble lady.
If the circumstances surrounding her had allowed it, perhaps this wouldn't have been treated as a tragedy, but—

"Ya really do keep springin' surprises, don'tcha. Same as at the commendation ceremony — huntin' the 'Great Rabbit,' turnin' into a Spirit Arte user... Subaru-kyun just keeps bein' outta this world, a total surprise of a human bein', nya~."

Beside Crusch, the one teasing them like that was a beautiful cat-eared girl — or rather, a young man who looked the part. This was Ferris, Crusch's knight and the kingdom's foremost healer.
It felt odd to compare him to how much his liege had changed, but Ferris alone seemed exactly the same as before. Subaru felt relief at that, even as there remained a part of him unable to fully guess at what Ferris must be feeling underneath.

"Well, my guiding principle in life is to always stay fresh enough that people never get tired of me. As for the Contract with Beako, you already chewed me out about it, but it really is a matter of life and death."

"I told you so many times, and yet Subaru-kyun goes and works his Gate to the breaking point anyway, nya~. If Beatrice-chan weren't there you'd go pop, so you'd better really cherish her, okay?"

"I know that. No one's gonna come along who can make Beako happier than I can."

Teasing tone aside, Ferris's warning was surely a serious one. So Subaru's answer in response was every bit as serious. Beside him, red-faced Beatrice pounded mercilessly on his shoulder, but that too was just part of the price of admission.

"Still... I never expected Crusch-san and the others to be called here too. If I hadn't run into Reinhard out front, I'd probably be surprised enough to have a nosebleed right about now."

"Aw, that woulda been a shame to miss. But everyone endin' up here on the same day really was just a coincidence. That part surprised even me, y'know."

"No particular date or time was specified, after all. That we should all meet like this must simply be the workings of fate. Opportunities for everyone to gather together face-to-face are rare indeed. In that sense, it was a fortunate happenstance."

That was said by the last member of the Crusch Camp.
Crusch sat in seiza, and beside her Ferris sat in the girlish sitting position. Next to them, also in seiza, was the old swordsman Wilhelm, taking a sip of the tea he'd been served.
His attire was the same black butler's uniform as always, and yet somehow the tatami room, the seiza posture, and the tea suited him uncannily well.
As it happened, from where Subaru's group sat, Wilhelm and Reinhard ended up seated naturally side by side — which made for a scene rather hard on the heart.

"They won't even look at each other..."

At Emilia's murmur, spoken under her breath, Subaru nodded silently within his own heart.

Grandfather and grandson, sitting side by side — Wilhelm and Reinhard. The two of them, having had an unplanned reunion in the tea room, hadn't exchanged a single word since their first greeting.
In the tea room set aside as a waiting area, silence had ruled the space, and Subaru had felt deeply uncomfortable. Even the Emilia Camp — a group fundamentally incapable of reading the room, made up as it was of the oblivious, the naive, the chuunibyou, and the childish — had been rendered utterly helpless by the sheer weight of the air.

Subaru would never forget the sight of Joshua, returning to fetch them once things were settled in the tatami room, taking one look and stepping back with a muttered "Whoa..." Nor would he forget the sheer relief of finally being released from that room.

House Astrea. To put it simply by family name, but Subaru had some sense that this was a complicated environment. It was a lineage that had passed down the title of Sword Saint generation after generation, a family whose combat strength was so overwhelming that the two people in front of him and the previous Sword Saint alone were more than enough to prove it.
Subaru also knew of Wilhelm's determination to slay the White Whale, and that the Whale was the enemy who had taken the life of Wilhelm's wife — the previous Sword Saint. Once the pieces connected that far, there was exactly one question that arose.

—Why had Wilhelm relied on the Karsten family rather than drawing on the power of his own house?

To put it plainly: why hadn't Reinhard been present at that battle of vengeance?

Subaru had heard that Wilhelm began pursuing the White Whale fourteen years ago. If this were a matter that arose after the Royal Selection began, he could at least understand why Wilhelm hadn't been able to cooperate with Reinhard, who belonged to a rival camp.
But at the time Wilhelm resolved to avenge himself upon the Whale, the Royal Selection had nothing to do with House Astrea. Of course, Subaru didn't imagine Reinhard at that age could have been much help in hunting the Whale — but factoring in how he grew afterward, why had Reinhard been kept away from the Whale all this time?
Neither Wilhelm's true feelings nor what lay in Reinhard's heart were anything Subaru could fathom.

—Honestly, he wanted to ask so badly it hurt.

He wanted to dig into the wound without restraint, to rub salt right under the scab.

That was Natsuki Subaru's full and honest desire, but he had grown over the past year. He could now judge that doing something like that would, without a doubt, get him hated.
Reinhard and Wilhelm, whatever camp they belonged to, were both precious people Subaru couldn't bring himself to dislike. Their trust wasn't cheap enough to throw away in exchange for mere curiosity.
So instead, he pinned his hopes on the conversation somehow drifting there naturally, leaving it up to fate.

"By the way, Anastasia-san, why did you gather everyone here? I feel like you must have something in mind... or am I wrong?"

Unaware of the turmoil in Subaru's mind, Emilia tossed the question at Anastasia. To Emilia's tilted head, Anastasia laughed and said, "Well, now,"

"Honestly, right now I really just wanted everyone to get together and talk again. Though I figured folks like Emilia-san would get suspicious, so I used all sorts of tricks to get everyone here."

"For us it was the magic ore matter — was it the same for everyone else?"

"Everyone's got their own things goin' on, y'know. Once you know that much, it's simple enough... well, I did ask the ones who wouldn't listen to reason to sit this one out, though."

"Wouldn't listen to reason...?"

Emilia crossed her arms with a puzzled look, but it wasn't really a problem worth agonizing over. After all, it wasn't hard to guess which camp was both absent and least likely to listen to reason.

"You didn't invite Priscilla-sama and Al-dono?"

"That camp's on a completely independent path of their own, and I couldn't find a single weakness worth exploitin', surprisingly enough. If I'm honest, I was hopin' to hear about the ins and outs of territory management, same as with Felt-san's side — that'd have been useful to me."

"As for that, once Felt-sama arrives, that will be up to her discretion. Even so, it is certainly a point of some interest."

To Anastasia's rather casual confession that she'd left one camp out entirely, Reinhard showed his understanding, voicing his agreement. Hearing this, Emilia — agreeing only with the substance of it — raised her hand,

"I'm curious how everyone's doing too. I've been studying a lot, but being in an important position means there's all sorts of ups and downs, doesn't it?"

"'Ups and downs'... nobody says that these days..."

"Mmu, Subaru's being mean."

At the rare jab, Emilia puffed her cheeks and pinched Subaru's right shoulder. His left shoulder, meanwhile, was still being pounded by Beatrice, so between reward and punishment, he had both sides covered.

In any case, Subaru understood the Priscilla Camp's absence and the Felt Camp's summons. What remained was the reason the Crusch Camp had been called here — namely, their weak point.

"The reason we came to Priestella like this is because we heard from Anastasia-sama that we could hear about the matter of 'Gluttony.'"

—

Sensing the intent behind Subaru's gaze, Crusch answered before he could even ask.
But that answer wasn't something Subaru could afford to let slide by, either. As he jerked his head up, Anastasia gave a wry smile and touched her scarf.

"It's not like I was keepin' it a secret from you, Natsuki-kun, y'know? There's just an order to things, that's all. Even if the conditions are the same, Crusch-san's side is in a more urgent spot, so it's only natural they get priority. Am I wrong?"

"Guh, gh... u, ngh. F-fine, I get it."

"You've grown into quite the adult, haven't you."

"Shut up, don't poke at me when I'm barely holding it together."

Pay more for goods of greater value — that's the basic principle of any merchant. Under Anastasia's lecture, Subaru just barely managed to hold his anger in check. He very nearly burst at Julius's wistful remark right after, though.

"Are you my dad or something. Just so you know, my old man's ten times better than me at riling people up."

"Ehh... Feri's scared..."

"Don't actually be afraid for real! You're making me worried about my own home life!"

He shouted at Ferris, who leaned in close to Crusch, but he didn't correct himself and say it was a joke — because Subaru's father, Kenichi, would make it true without either humility or exaggeration.

Still, if that story had been shared with Crusch and the others while Subaru remained kept in the dark, that was another matter entirely. The existence of the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony bore directly on the very question of Rem's continued existence, still asleep at Roswaal Manor even now.
Even if reason could accept being left out, instinct was persuasive enough to make reason throw that acceptance right back out the window.

"No need to bare your fangs like that. I'll tell you properly, so don't worry."

"Wha... seriously?"

"It's no lie. You can thank Crusch-san and the others for that one. They told me there was no point in only they themselves knowin'."

Speechless, Subaru turned to look at Crusch, and she nodded, her expression composed with all the fortitude she could muster.

"It's only natural. Of course, in order to recover my own memories, I want to see the matter of 'Gluttony' settled by my own hand. But I also know that you, Subaru-sama, have resolved to defeat 'Gluttony' for that girl's sake. There's no way I could keep this all to myself."

"Crusch-san..."

"Besides, I believe the more people who share the same resolve, the better. Our opponent is a Sin Archbishop who has slipped away, cunning, again and again. The more hands reaching out, the greater our chances of victory."

At Crusch's words, delivered with a touch of humor, Subaru bowed his head, feeling saved.
In her heart, she too wanted to settle things herself with the enemy who had created the hole within her. And yet she'd set that aside to show consideration for Subaru, who carried the same purpose.
The soul of the woman named Crusch Karsten — fair and upright to her very core — had lost none of its light, even with her memories gone.

"I'm grateful. Thank you, Crusch-san. I'll definitely seize the chance you've held out to me. I promise."

"Even so, we'll surely get there first. That much, I have no intention of conceding."

Before Subaru, who had put his resolve into words, Crusch puffed out her chest, refusing to be outdone.
Ah, so this was how she chose to treat the flames of their resolve — as rival lights racing each other. A smile, oddly out of place, rose at her consideration, and Subaru traded smiles with Crusch.
Watching this with obvious displeasure was her knight.

"Nyaaah. I don't like it, Crusch-sama gazing into Subaru-kyun's eyes like that. Cut it out, you lecher, Subaru-kyun. You've already got a flower in each hand, so be satisfied with that — honestly, you're just too indecent, nya."

"Ferris, isn't that a rather rude thing to say? Subaru-sama isn't the sort of unfaithful man who casts eyes at just anyone."

"That's right, quit it. Sure, Crusch-san is beautiful and cute, but my heart runs straight as an arrow... okay, it splits into two partway through, but it's straight, ow ow ow owww?!"

"That's probably not what people call 'straight as an arrow,' I suppose. Also, if you intend to reflect on your own careless remarks, you'd best not go around saying things like that so easily, I suppose."

Subaru had tried to smooth over the exchange between Crusch and her retainer, but Beatrice yanked his ear with all her might, and he screamed in pain. As he tried to protest with tears in his eyes, Beatrice pursed her lips and pointed at Crusch.
Following her finger, Subaru saw Crusch, cheeks faintly flushed, looking down.
He replayed his own words in his head. Had he said something odd?

"Uh oh, Emilia-tan, did I say something weird?"

"Huh? Hmm, I don't know. I think you sounded the same as always when you talk to me, but..."

"Right? I wonder what it is. Maybe I'd figure it out if I held your hand, Emilia-tan. Can I hold it?"

"Yes, yes. Go ahead and think hard."

Smacked on the forehead by the hand he'd failed to hold, Subaru reeled back with a sulky look on his face.
Meanwhile, Ferris leaned in close to the downcast Crusch's ear,

"See, it's exactly as it looks. Subaru-kyun just does that unconsciously, recklessly, tryin' to look cool for anyone at all. It's a sickness, nya. So you mustn't let it get to you."

"Yes, I'll be careful. Phew, that startled me."

Crusch took a deep breath and pressed a hand to her chest in relief.
There was something charming in the little gestures of her femininity, a kind of gap-moe, Subaru thought. Unaware she was being thought of this way, Crusch bumped fists with Ferris, renewing some sort of vow between them. Seen just like that, they looked like a pair of close friends of the same sex.
And so it was, at that point, that everyone had at last shared their reasons for coming to Priestella.

"Heh, well look at that, everyone's all here. Rachins told me it was just gonna be big bro's group and the Kararagi-accent sis's group."

Thrown open with tremendous force, the sliding door revealed a blonde girl, out of breath.
Dazzlingly beautiful golden hair, and big, round, red eyes. A faint glimpse of a snaggletooth peeked from her smiling mouth, stirring up a mischievous charm within her well-formed features. Small and slight of build, she seemed to have gained just a touch more womanliness than before.
As ever, she showed plenty of skin, favoring mobility over modesty with a midriff-baring outfit paired with short shorts that left her legs bare. Even as a royal candidate, she hadn't shaken her air of scrappy poverty — this was Felt's entrance.

"Huh, you all don't look much different. It's been a year, so I was kinda lookin' forward to some changes — how boring."

"Felt-sama."

Felt looked around at the faces in the room, her shoulders slumping in disappointment. At her arrival, Reinhard, who had risen to his feet, moved swiftly to the entrance of the room,

"What happened to the garments prepared for you in the dragon carriage?"

"Hah! 'Sightseeing' was obviously just an excuse, wasn't it. I sent you off to the inn first sayin' that, and changed clothes the second you were gone. Like hell I'm gonna keep wearin' somethin' that makes my whole body itch just puttin' it on. Get it through your head already what kinda person I am!"

"Honestly, you are truly..."

Reinhard pressed a hand to his forehead as if lamenting the state of things. Felt, having gotten one over on the nation's hero and greatest fighting force, stepped into the room looking thoroughly pleased with herself.

"So anyway, I'm here too. —Thank you for inviting me today. Let's make this a productive discussion. Okay, manners over!"

Felt switched deftly between a mischievous-brat grin and the refined smile and manners of a proper young lady. She dropped a curtsy despite her skirtless outfit, then instantly threw off lady mode again.
Subaru was fairly confident he himself didn't mix well with noble society, but Felt's commitment to rejecting it had only grown stronger over the past year.

"Anyway, this is a weird-shaped buildin'. First time for me too, so it was all kinda novel. I wandered around checkin' stuff out on my way to this room."

Felt plopped herself cross-legged right down onto the cushion Reinhard had been sitting on. Reinhard pulled over another cushion and settled obediently into seiza beside her.
By coincidence, the seat Felt took happened to be next to Wilhelm — meaning she'd wedged herself right between grandfather and grandson, which, if nothing else, was a small mercy.

"Um, it's been a while, Felt-chan. Have you been well?"

"Callin' me 'chan' is kinda creepy, so quit that. Well, I've been fine. You too, sis... fine, or more like reckless? I heard all kinds of rumors, and they were all pretty wild."

"The reckless troublemaker isn't me, it's Subaru. I've mostly just been saved by all the hard work he's done."

"Ah, that! That's the thing!"

At Emilia's answer, Felt clapped her hands, then leaned across the low table to glare at Subaru.

"I've been hearin' some crazy stories about you, big bro. Real talk, how much of that stuff is bull?"

"Don't just assume it's lies from the start. Makes it sound like most of it's fake."

"I mean, no way anyone could believe it. Big bro splittin' the White Whale in half all by himself, punchin' out the Witch Cult brass along with their whole crew, roastin' and eatin' the Great Rabbit too — c'mon."

"The related facts check out, but there's a whole lot of made-up nonsense in how it supposedly all went down!"

If Subaru had really pulled all of that off single-handedly, he'd be sitting on the throne by now, never mind being a national hero. He'd have seized the country by force, made Emilia his queen, and the two of them would be canoodling away.

"—Heh."

Off to the side of Subaru's forceful retort, a small laugh could be heard.
And it had two sources. From the seat of honor and the one across from it — Julius and Wilhelm.
The two of them, who had apparently reacted without meaning to, relaxed into smiles the instant they noticed each other's reaction. Watching this out of the corner of her eye, Felt furrowed her brow in confusion, not understanding the reason.

"Why'd gramps and Julius laugh? Did I say somethin' weird?"

"If we're talking weird, the whole thing was way too weird to begin with. My contribution to the world is way too high. Give me a Nobel Peace Prize already."

He had no idea what actually came with winning one, but it was the go-to example of a prestigious award, at least.
He'd received a handful of medals at the commendation ceremony, but since he didn't really grasp their concrete value, Subaru had little real sense of his own accomplishments.
In truth, the medals he'd received were among some of the most prestigious honors in the kingdom.

"Subaru-dono's contribution to the Subjugation of the White Whale is beyond measure. I myself can say so with certainty — and my Soul, more than anything, affirms it clearly. Without Subaru-dono, my long-cherished wish would never have been fulfilled. There is no need at all for you to be laughed at as exaggerating."

"It's the same with the Sin Archbishop matter. It was none other than him who took the lead and commanded our defense against the Witch Cult's assault. That command was precisely what made the defeat of the Sin Archbishop possible. What little help I and the others lent wasn't nearly enough to claim credit for so loudly."

—

Wilhelm's, and then Julius's, overwhelming support.
Hearing the two of them so plainly stand up for him, Subaru found himself at a loss for words. A beat later, an unbearable heat came surging up.
His neck and ears burned bright red, and he very nearly felt blood shoot from his eyeballs.

"S-stop it already! Don't go puttin' me on a pedestal like that! You two are the ones who know exactly what kinda mess I make of myself if I let it go to my head!"

"No, no, not at all. It's true that your conduct back then was reckless, but you showed results more than sufficient to redeem that disgrace. That, and your subsequent achievements, are entirely separate matters. You're allowed to take pride in it honestly."

"There's no need for modesty. What you accomplished was something no one but you could have accomplished. Until the day my life ends, I will go on taking pride in having run the battlefield alongside you."

"—Ha, aah."

Praised to death. Natsuki Subaru had died countless times up to now.
But this was a first — being killed in this terrifying a manner. Right now, Subaru was being praised to death.
Tormented by embarrassment fierce enough to actually kill him, Subaru looked to Emilia and Beatrice as if seeking rescue. But the two flanking him only smiled sweetly,

"That's right. Subaru worked really, really hard. I'm truly proud that someone like that is my knight."

"W-well, of course, that much is only natural for Betty's partner, I suppose. If anything, everyone else was far too slow to notice how amazing Subaru is. Subaru's only going to grow bigger and bigger from here, so he'd best get used to fame now while he can, I suppose."

A wholesale, unqualified endorsement — the last thing he'd expected. Doted on like this, Subaru nearly spiraled into panic.
And then, surrounding him, everyone joined in all at once,

"Amazing, Subaru. Everyone speaking of you like this is proof you've truly accomplished something rare. As your friend, I'm glad."

"I've heard that if not for you, Subaru-sama, I would have lost a great many loyal retainers. For Wilhelm's sake too, who has supported me until now, please allow me to thank you once again."

"It's true you were pretty hopeless, but it's also true that Subaru-kyun's little bit of a contribution is part of why our hearts didn't break at the White Whale, y'know? After that, Crusch-sama's amazing speech is what really turned things around, so it's not like it changed all that much, nya, but I'll go ahead and thank you anyway."

"Thanks to Natsuki-kun lettin' me in on things, I made out pretty well when it came to the White Whale business. I'm grateful too — on behalf of the merchants who suffered from that fog for so many years, as well."

"Ohh, is this the part where big bro gets praised? Amazing! So cool! Though you're still second to Mimi! And also after Garf!"

"Dunno what's what, but you're somethin' else, boss. That's what makes you my big bro. 'Fame follows right conduct and, uh, whatchamacallit.'"

Riding the wave, everyone present who knew Subaru took their turn praising him one after another.
Every time he was met with warm words, Subaru reddened just a little, and Felt, baring her teeth, laughed a little "heh-heh-heh" at the sight of him.

"Sounds like all kindsa stuff really did happen... but big bro bein' the type everyone loves teasin', and bein' a riot, that hasn't changed one bit. Glad to hear it!"

"Hey, quit praisin' me so much, all of you! I'm gonna end up loving every single one of you!!"

At the scene Felt had wrapped up like that, Subaru's voice finally burst out, unable to hold back any longer.
Whatever tension had lingered over the tatami room shattered completely this time, and laughter broke out from everyone at once.

"Ahh, damn it, that really took years off my life..."

With the gathering in the tatami room finally winding down, Subaru sat slumped in the guest room he'd been shown to.
After everyone had needlessly synchronized their efforts to tease Subaru to death with praise, the various camps had gone on to lose themselves in conversation as though they'd forgotten they were rivals at all.

Of course, it wasn't as though there had been zero exchanges probing at each other's internal affairs and circumstances, but even so, the portion made up of harmless, neither-medicine-nor-poison small talk was by no means small.
Even in a relationship that could turn hostile the moment one stepped back and gained some distance, seen from a wider view, they were simply young women close in age — there was nothing wrong with letting the conversation forget about the kingdom entirely.
In fact, Emilia had seemed to thoroughly enjoy the lively conversation with those close to her own age.

"Well, calling it 'same-age talk' when it comes to Emilia-tan and Beako is kind of a stretch, though."

"Don't go bringing up actual ages as a topic, I suppose. It could start a war, I suppose."

Beatrice's sharp voice pierced straight through Subaru, who'd let that slip carelessly.
Glancing over at the sound, he found Beatrice perched atop the futons stacked in the corner of the room, working desperately to loosen up her completely numbed legs.

"You're a spirit, so your legs can still go numb? It's not like blood's actually flowing through you, right?"

"There's no issue with blood flow, but Betty's body was designed to mimic a human body as closely as possible, I suppose. So whatever's hard on a human is hard on Betty too, I suppose. It's the same as how staying underwater too long makes consciousness fade, I suppose."

"Wait, do you even breathe?"

"Of course I— hey, stop leaning in to smell Betty's breath, I suppose!"

Subaru leaned in close and sniffed, and Beatrice, face reddening, pounded the futon. While he was at it, Subaru seized the opening to flick her numb leg with a finger, and the fading pins-and-needles came rushing right back, sending Beatrice rolling around with tears in her eyes.

"It hurts, I suppose, I could cry, I suppose. Subaru's bullying me, I suppose."

"Alright, alright, my bad, my bad. C'mere, c'mere."

Subaru gave a wry smile, sat cross-legged, and patted his knee, and Beatrice crawled over to rest her head in his lap. Stroking her head, made voluminous by her ringlet curls, Subaru murmured, "Still, though..." and looked around the guest room.

"A private room's fine and all, but it's the interior. They're clearly trying hard to make it look like a proper ryokan... but yeah, there's still something a bit off about it."

It was probably a discrepancy born from differences between wooden construction methods and how that technology had developed here. The Water Feather-Robe Inn had come impressively close to a real ryokan, but the beams, the sliding doors — there was a sense of falling just one step short scattered here and there, which was a bit of a shame.
There might have been real effort put into making the guest room floor into tatami. But all that effort seemed to have spun its wheels, resulting in the bold choice of laying animal hides over a plain wooden floor instead.
The feel underfoot wasn't bad, but it was a textbook case of missing the mark it had been aiming for.

"So, in order to stick to the idea of laying futons right on the floor, they couldn't compromise on the wooden flooring, huh."

"Laying a futon straight on the bare ground is what poor people do, I suppose. Betty won't acknowledge a man who earns so little as her partner, I suppose."

"Guess I'm always putting you through hardship, huh."

"That's the sort of thing you're not supposed to say out loud, I suppose."

"What kinda comedy routine are you two runnin'?"

Beatrice, who had been trading quips with Subaru at a good clip, sprang up as if struck the instant the third voice spoke. But the moment she landed, she remembered her numb leg, botched the landing, and plunged headfirst into the futon in spectacular fashion. Subaru quietly fixed her flipped-up skirt.

"Garfiel, huh. Done exploring?"

"Finished up this mornin', draggin' along with the little squirt. Beatrice, you're sure worked up."

"It's her first trip out in a while, so she's pretty hyped. The way she can't settle down to sleep is crazy cute, right?"

Standing in the doorway, Garfiel bared his teeth and clacked them together with a laugh at Subaru's words.
Since the guest room's door was also a sliding one, its opening and closing made no sound, which was probably why Beatrice had been caught off guard. Subaru, for his part, had been facing the entrance, so he'd noticed Garfiel appear without any trouble.

"So, what'd you come here for? Dinner?"

"Nah, dinner's probably still a bit off yet. Wasn't gonna do me any good sittin' alone in my room, so I figured I'd see what the boss was up to. Otto's not back yet either."

"Well, Otto's not a kid, so no need to worry. Even if he racks up some debt, I'm sure he'll bust his ass to sort it out without dragging us into it."

"Guess so."

Otto — a man for whom taking on debt within just a few hours, and then the whole process of clawing his way back out of it, was simply an expected sequence of events. Garfiel didn't deny it either, so that was about the general consensus on him.
Of course, when it came to relying on him, they relied on him plenty.

"That's a whole separate matter, though."

"The hell are you talkin' about, boss."

Garfiel let Subaru's muttering slide right past him with a practiced expression. Then he jerked his chin toward the hallway,

"Anyway, forget that. I want the boss to come along with me for a bit."

"Come along how? Oh, the bath? A bath, huh, nice. Can't rule out the chance there's an outdoor one somewhere. I checked behind the sliding door in the back earlier and there were actual yukata in there. Just imagining Emilia-tan in a yukata gets me pumped! I'm pumped already!"

Even if tatami and traditional architecture had been hard to reproduce, they'd apparently managed to commercialize yukata just fine. Subaru genuinely wanted to applaud the dedication of whoever it was that had spread "wafu" culture through Kararagi.
But before Subaru, worked up like that, Garfiel's expression was dead serious. Even Subaru couldn't keep clowning around in the face of that, and he furrowed his brow, wondering what was going on.

"Garfiel. What is it? What are you planning?"

"Nah, it ain't nothin' that big a deal."

Garfiel touched the white scar on his forehead and paused there for a moment.
Then he looked Subaru straight in the eye and said—

"I just wanna find out exactly how much the strongest, most famous man in the world's really got in him."
