Arc 4 Chapter 111: "Garfiel's Barrier"

Even to Subaru's eyes, the figure of Garfiel standing there was battered from head to toe.

His whole body was dyed red with tremendous bleeding, his shoulders rising and falling with ragged breath. The lacerations carved into him in countless layers still wept blood, and his small but powerfully trained body was exposed everywhere except where a loincloth-like wrap covered his lower abdomen.

He'd even lost his shoes, standing there barefoot. Before that sight, Subaru lowered the hand he'd raised.

"...That's a pretty different entrance than I was picturing. I figured you'd be furious, but I didn't think you'd be in that kind of state."

"Don't sweat it. Just slipped and fell a little, is all."

Garfiel replied with a sullen look at Subaru's words, his cheek twitching. Of course, that was nothing but a flippant lie, but the sight of him wounded like this couldn't help but stir up ugly imaginings in Subaru.

Garfiel had been running all over the Sanctuary searching for Ryuzu, and by Subaru's scheme, should have been thoroughly caught up buying time for Otto and the others.

Given that, for him to show up looking like this in front of them now — thinking it through, there was only one possibility. Namely—

"That idiot Otto, I told him to spill everything and run the second things got bad...!"

"Real admirable of him. Guy's not the least bit suited for fighting, and I bet he's never even had a proper brawl in his life. And yet he goes and mouths off about doing it for his friends... put up one hell of a fight, he did."

Twisting his cheek in irritation, Garfiel touched the white scar on his own forehead. That alone was an old wound, but could it be that every other wound on his body had come from his fight with Otto?

It couldn't be called a fistfight so much as a one-sided beating, but Subaru — who'd traded blows with Otto himself — knew better than most. Compared to Subaru, Otto was more used to scrapping, but even so, in a world overflowing with people whose combat abilities defied common sense, he ought to be classified as a non-combatant.

For a man like that to go up against a full-blown combatant — Garfiel, who by any measure Subaru knew of stood at the very top — there was no way he could have had a chance of winning.

And Subaru hadn't given Otto any plan beyond stalling with a few tricks.

"...He's not dead, is he?"

An unpleasantly cold trickle of sweat ran down Subaru's forehead.

The worst-case scenario was a scene ending with Otto torn apart by Garfiel's claws. He wanted to laugh it off as an overactive imagination, but looking at Garfiel's state, he couldn't quite manage it.

Garfiel himself had clearly been wounded on this grand a scale, forced into a hard fight. And yet, to demand that he go easy on Otto now would be far too self-serving.

But even so — if Otto had died, none of this would mean anything.

Not Otto's death being meaningless — Subaru's being alive would be.

"...Subaru."

A silver-bell voice called from behind, just as Subaru caught himself holding his breath, on the verge of hanging his head. Fingertips that had hesitated to touch now settled on Subaru's shoulder, which had begun to sag uncertainly. At that faint contact reaching him from behind, Subaru — on the verge of looking down — stopped himself.

Turning only his eyes, he found Emilia standing just behind and to his side, watching him with concern. Emilia, who still hadn't come to terms with the feelings inside her.

To her, a situation where a blood-soaked, furious Garfiel stood before them must make no sense at all. And yet she could clearly sense that this was no ordinary situation. In the eyes turned toward Subaru, there was more worry than unease.

"I'm okay, Emilia. Sorry for suddenly showing you something so pathetic. ...I remembered who was standing behind me, so I've settled down now."

As if bolstered by that gaze, Subaru let out a deep breath and turned back to face Garfiel. The entrance to the tomb had a slight upward slope, so from Subaru's position he was looking slightly down at Garfiel. Hunched, ill-tempered, his sharp glare stabbed at Subaru as though it would strike him dead.

"You lot went and did some real high-handed shit while I was runnin' myself ragged, huh. Sneakin' around, sneakin' around... ahh? Guess you didn't know how much I frickin' hate that kinda filthy underhanded scheming, huh?"

"I didn't know I needed your permission to do anything in this place. Besides, you're easy enough to read that I already figured that's how you'd be. Honestly, I figured you'd get mad, but I also figured it wouldn't go beyond that."

"Ahh?"

Facing down Garfiel's intimidating posture, Subaru merely shrugged and shot back. Perhaps because there was no more tension in his tone than usual, Garfiel wrinkled his nose and growled. Looking down at his irritated expression, Subaru set his own face and asked,

"Garfiel. What happened to Otto?"

"He went and pulled some real smart-ass shit, so... I chewed him up and spat him out. Rest's probably fertilizer for the forest by now."

"――――"

At Garfiel's answer — baring his all-too-sharp canines and licking his lips — Emilia caught her breath. The tremendous rage radiating from Garfiel's entire body alone conveyed just how hard Otto must have fought against him.

And it meant Garfiel, for his part, hadn't been able to hold back either. So—

"So he's alive, then. Man, scaring me like that... I genuinely felt my blood run cold thinking this whole thing was about to fall apart."

"...Ahh?"

"But why would he go and do something like this... don't tell me — you said he got Ram's cooperation, right? Don't tell me she whispered something weird in his ear. Ram's the type who might just do that... she never did tell me how she'd actually move when it counted."

"Oi, oi, don't screw with me."

"So then, is Ram the reason you're this beat up too? Yeah, that'd make sense. There's no way Otto alone could've pulled off something like this. Damn. Here I was starting to think Otto had some hidden power he'd been keeping secret, our friendship almost took a hit..."

"――You! What the hell are you talkin' about! Ahh!?"

Garfiel roared in fury, and the ground where he slammed his surging emotion exploded. Starting from the heel that struck it, the earth caved in, forming a crater centered on Garfiel. At the bottom of the crater, cracks radiating in all directions, Garfiel bared his fangs, smoke trailing from the impact.

"I'm sayin' I beat the shit out of him! That boy fought damn well! Got the whole damn forest on his side with some incomprehensible Divine Protection, made a fool outta me with bugs and rats and all that. And then, at the end, one shot with a Great Spell he shouldn'ta even been able to use. So... I paid him respect too — the guys with the will to fight. With these claws, and these fangs!"

"Got the whole forest on his side... I see. So that's another way to use the Divine Protection of the Soul of Language. That bastard, keeping something that important under wraps..."

"And Ram, who that boy talked into it too! Ram went and butted into the fight, hit me without mercy... so I chewed her up too."

"…………"

Clenching his teeth, Garfiel covered his face with a hand and looked up at the sky. Watching Garfiel's grieving figure in silence, Subaru's mind kept turning over his words again and again.

So Ram, too, had formed a joint front with Otto and faced off against Garfiel. Perhaps with her help, they'd managed to push Garfiel almost to the brink of defeat. And yet, even so, the wall that was this beastman had proven thick.

"I got no mind to chase down the ones who ran off elsewhere while you lot bought time. But I ain't gonna overlook what you're doin' anymore either. Back off from there, don't come any closer. Nobody's gettin' into the tomb anymore. I'm gonna tear it down myself, with these hands."

"If you do that, there won't be any way to open the barrier anymore. ...This Sanctuary will stay a sealed garden forever. Are you really okay with that?"

"I'm okay with that. Everything else is what's wrong."

Declaring it, Garfiel's feet turned from the crater toward the tomb. All trace of hesitation had vanished cleanly from his stride, and it was plain he felt not the slightest reluctance about carrying out what he'd just said.

Blood-soaked, battered from head to toe — even in that state, there remained a genuinely insurmountable gap in ability between Subaru and Garfiel. Even wounded this badly, the man Garfiel's raw strength still exceeded Subaru's.

That much was obvious from the single blow to the earth just now, from the demonic aura he radiated.

But—

"I-I won't let you do that."

In this place, besides Subaru, there was one more person who could stand to stop Garfiel. Emilia stepped out from beside Subaru, blocking Garfiel's path toward the tomb. Garfiel, however, looked up at her with a jaded expression.

"The hell, oi. Weak-ass woman, don't go blockin' my path."

"No, I will get in your way. I won't let you destroy the tomb. I'm definitely going to overcome the Trials of the Tomb."

"No way in hell you can. You've been cryin' and sniffin' and sobbin' every damn day, haven'tcha. And on top of that, your friend's gone and you're lonely as hell, ain'tcha? Go cry into your blanket in bed. Do that, and I won't do a thing."

"――――っ"

At Garfiel's cruel words, something anguished flashed across Emilia's face. But that expression lasted only an instant — in the space of a blink, Emilia swallowed down the pain and said,

"Unfortunately, no matter what you say, I can't step back for you. I have to face the Trial. And confront the past..."

"Every single one of you...!"

Cutting off Emilia's words, Garfiel clicked his tongue in irritation, fury kindling in his eyes. The pressure of the demonic aura he emitted intensified another notch, and Subaru saw Emilia's shoulders tremble. Garfiel caught that faint flicker of fear keenly and snorted.

"Who gives a damn about the past, so what. You're scared shitless of me, and you think you can overcome the thing you're most scared of? No way in hell. Nobody can do a damn thing about it. That witch is just doin' some nasty, twisted shit to mock people, that's all."

"You sure trash-talk the witch pretty hard."

"Ahh?"

At Garfiel's spat-out words, Subaru cut in. He turned the burning gaze he'd been aiming at Emilia toward Subaru instead, pointing a finger at him.

"The hell? You tryin' to defend that rotten witch bitch or somethin'? They say 'there's no night or day at Porttux,' but are you her damn slave through and through, you stinkin' bastard?"

"――――"

At Garfiel's words, Subaru shut his mouth. Garfiel frowned suspiciously at that reaction, but he didn't seem to understand the reason for Subaru's silence.

The witch who slept in the tomb was Echidna. And the source of the witch's miasma wrapped around Subaru's body was that of the Witch of Envy. Garfiel, going only by hearsay and with a nose that didn't function against miasma, hadn't caught up to that distinction. And naturally, having broken after facing the Trial just once, Garfiel didn't even understand the meaning of Echidna's Trial.

"You're half-baked about everything, Garfiel."

"...What'd you just say?"

Taking his words together with his actions so far, Subaru offered that assessment of him. At Subaru's curt, dismissive words, Garfiel growled low in intimidation. But even facing that spirit-crushing menace head-on, Subaru met his eyes without flinching.

"'It's something I couldn't do, so others can't do it either. I think this way, so that guy must be the same kind of person.' — How badly twisted up in your own self-righteousness are you?"

"…………"

"Sure, like you say, Emilia's failed the Trial over and over. Can't deny she's been shown pasts she doesn't want to see and cried her eyes out over it. Same with how pathetically shaken she was after Puck disappeared, and I can't say for certain she's recovered even now."

In front of the silent Garfiel, Subaru jerked his chin toward Emilia standing beside him. Startled by this sudden turn, Emilia looked at Subaru with wide eyes. But something in the expression of the Subaru delivering this harsh assessment of her must have struck a chord, because she didn't interrupt. More than anything, the evaluation Subaru had voiced was one Emilia herself already acknowledged. It was undeniably an assessment she should be ashamed of, but not one she needed to shut her ears to.

Being able to judge that and face it head-on — Subaru thought that, weak as she was, was something admirable about her.

So—

"Even if she challenges the Trial right now, the result might not change. She might lose again today, come back crying again."

"If you know that much, then why the hell do you keep—"

"But Emilia will still challenge it. As many times as it takes. — She's different from you."

"――――っ"

Garfiel caught his breath. Not missing the momentary waver that crossed his sharp glare, Subaru pressed on without flinching, looking him straight in the eye once more.

"Garfiel. Emilia is nothing like you, who lost and ran away."

"――ッ! Don't you get, cocky, with me!!"

The instant he finished speaking, Garfiel screamed in fury and his right foot shattered the ground. The earth peeled upward from the shock of the stomp, taking on — by whatever principle — the shape of a square wall of dirt roughly the size of a tatami mat, which Garfiel's left foot then kicked. The clod of earth spun vertically with tremendous force, wrapped in a violent gale, passing right beside Subaru's left flank before slamming into the wall beside the tomb's entrance, dealing a major blow to the ancient structure.

Dust, tangled vines, and moss came peeling away with a crash from the tomb's wall. Subaru stood there, taking it full on the head. Emilia beside him hunched her shoulders slightly for a moment too, but perhaps because of what Subaru had just said, she made no move to leave the spot.

Seeing the faint but genuine trust between the two of them, Garfiel's eyes went wide. His bloodshot eyes gleamed with a savagery that only intensified.

"The hell?! I don't like it, the two of you together! Ahh! I don't like it! I don't like it at all! Puttin' on that know-it-all face! That face like you got room to spare! If I felt like it, I could turn the both of you into ground meat, and you couldn't even tell whose was whose! You don't get that, do you, ahh!?"

"I get it. I get that you couldn't do something like that even if you wanted to."

Breathing hard, kicking the ground, hurling threat after threat — but Garfiel's blustering intimidation no longer struck any chord in Subaru's heart. Of course not. What lay at the root of Garfiel — through everything that had happened, Ryuzu's story, and the present situation — Subaru had finally come to understand.

For all his display of fury, for all he treated Subaru and the others as enemies, Garfiel was—

"You're not going to kill me or Emilia. No — you can't. Because Garfiel... you've never killed anyone, have you?"

"――――"

"You must have fought Otto and Ram too, but there's no way you could have killed either of them. Otto, maybe, but Ram, absolutely not. If they haven't shown themselves, they've probably been left unable to move... but that's as far as it goes."

At Subaru's words, Garfiel's irritated movements stopped. Holding his breath, Garfiel stared at Subaru. Hearing the words Subaru had thrown at him, uncertainty rose in Emilia's eyes. Having only ever seen Garfiel's usual temperament, she couldn't easily accept what Subaru was saying now.

But Subaru had a conviction. Garfiel — at least while in a human form with his own will intact — could not bring himself to make the decision to kill someone.

――Through the loops until now, Subaru had clashed with Garfiel many times. Over differences of opinion. Or times when Garfiel had abruptly turned hostile toward him. There'd even been a time when, to stop Subaru as he lashed out violently at Roswaal, Garfiel had struck him and taken one of his eyes.

But it was also Garfiel who had treated Subaru after he lost that eye, and even when hostile, Garfiel had, in the ultimate sense, never once brought himself to kill Subaru.

There was one exception. A time when a beastified Garfiel had slaughtered a group of refugees. It was a memory Subaru didn't even want to recall. But to Subaru, it was a memory burned into him, one he couldn't forget, and an event that still stirred feelings in him toward Garfiel he couldn't easily swallow.

Only — looking back on that moment, there was something Subaru had come to notice.

A beastified Garfiel couldn't use words. He became nothing more than a beast, wielding claw and fang purely on instinct. When he'd turned that weapon on the villagers, it truly must have been pure instinct.

But in that moment, when Garfiel killed the first villager — the very first one, the one whose death triggered the massacre — right up to the very last instant, he had hesitated.

At the time, consumed by rage and desperation, Subaru hadn't understood it. When Garfiel had moved toward that first person, it had seemed to Subaru, in his panic, as though everything slowed down. But that wasn't what it was. That had been genuine hesitation.

And it was only after killing that one person, after losing sight of that hesitation, that Garfiel truly became a beast.

Natsuki Subaru remembered, with hatred, the eyes of that great tiger who had learned the taste of blood and life.

"Your eyes aren't like they were back then. You still haven't killed anyone."

"What the hell — kinda proof is that. Ram, maybe, but I got not one shred of a reason to hesitate chewin' up your damn friend."

"Right, Ram maybe."

"H-hey... do you two have some kind of grudge against Otto-kun...?"

Timidly, Emilia brought up Otto, who was getting rather sorry treatment in this conversation. But Subaru, perhaps for the first time consciously, ignored Emilia and pointed a finger at Garfiel, whose fierce fury had drained away.

"Even now, if you'd wanted to land that hit, you could have. You just couldn't bring yourself to want to. You were only using it as a threat. Fashion bloodlust, huh. I wonder which one of us is really the scaredy-cat."

"Oi, oi, oi... watch your damn mouth. Keep it up and who knows what your last words'll end up being."

"Cut it with the threats you can't back up. I've had my fill of guys acting tough while their spine's really jelly on the inside — the loitering idiots back in the alleys of my hometown covered that quota. At least they had the guts to actually stab me."

"Stop it... stop it..."

Grinding his teeth, Garfiel glared at Subaru with a face full of fury. But the one facing him was Natsuki Subaru — and there was no scene where his talent shone brighter than when goading the person right in front of him. Hence—

"Those prized claws and fangs of yours — polish 'em up nice and neat every day just to keep 'em on display, huh? Tell you what, why not get some cute decorations on those nails while you're at it? Back where I'm from, all the girls do it. Should suit you just fine, moping around all whiny like this."

"I said stooop iiiit――!!"

Impact again. The gouged-out earth flew off, grazing past Subaru's head before slamming into the tomb. No need to dodge. There was never any intent to actually land it.

"If you want to play in the dirt, go do it in a sandbox. You get that thing behind you is an important cultural asset? Especially you, who's supposed to be self-appointed fangs guarding this Sanctuary, right? What's behind you is the grave of a witch who died here. She's your comrade — don't discriminate."

"No way in hell she's my comrade! Because of her! Because that damn witch under the dirt exists... I... I...!"

Subaru's tongue was in top form. Against his smooth provocations, Garfiel was gasping for breath. He'd already pushed his battered body this far to get here. On top of that, continuing this exhausting exchange, wasting stamina on pointless posturing without a care — his blood, racing with emotion, had reopened wounds that should have already stopped bleeding.

Panting hard, Garfiel stopped moving and glared at Subaru. Then, suddenly, his gaze shifted to Emilia beside Subaru. As if noticing something, he wrinkled his nose.

"Oi... don't screw with me. What's with that look?"

"…………"

"If you got somethin' to say, say it! Bein' looked at like that pisses me off way more!"

Garfiel roared at whatever he'd seen in Emilia's eyes, watching him silently. Emilia's violet eyes held a complicated tangle of emotion, and she shook her head slightly at Garfiel's demand.

"Garfiel... what are you so afraid of?"

"Me — afraid...? The hell?"

"You're not not afraid. That's why you're shouting so loud, stretching your arms out as far as they'll go, stomping the ground to work yourself up, right?"

"What the hell do you know about me, actin' like you—"

"I do know. Because..."

To Garfiel, whose voice was growing smaller, Emilia paused there, took a breath, and said:

"――I've spent my whole life being afraid of all sorts of things too."

At Emilia's words, using her own weakness as her reason, Garfiel stopped breathing. Touching her own chest, moving her fingers as if to confirm the feel of a crystal stone that wasn't there, Emilia continued, a fragile color rising in her eyes.

"I've walked this far being frightened of so many things. Leaning on Puck, who was always with me, entrusting him with all sorts of things... and walking on without even realizing I was doing it. It's only today, only just a little while ago, that I feel like I finally understood that, even just a little."

"Shut up."

"I still don't really know what's right, or what I have to do. But I feel like I understand that there's 'something.' And that 'something' — I think I can find it inside this tomb. So I can't step aside for you."

"Shut up. Disappear. Don't say anything to me."

"...That 'something' — don't you already have it, really?"

"――――ッ!"

At the question, Garfiel, having reached his limit, jerked his face up as if struck. Bending his knees slightly, he exploded his small frame into a leap, closing on Emilia with tremendous speed. — But just before Garfiel's body could reach her, Subaru cut between the two of them.

"Garfiel!"

"――Tch!"

He stretched out his arms to the incoming Garfiel, shielding her from his trajectory, colliding and grappling with him, tumbling as the impact sent them rolling, wincing at the pain of striking against the ground again and again. After a few sensations of up and down reversing, they came to a stop, Subaru flat on his back. Then Garfiel, claws at Subaru's throat, bared his fangs at Emilia.

"Right now! Get away from this place right now! Or else I'll tear open his throat and dye your clothes red all over again!"

"Subaru――"

Emilia braced herself at the threat. Though she'd lost Puck, she was still a Spiritual Arts user, capable and strong enough to wield magic with the borrowing of a minor spirit's power under contract. Fighting Garfiel, win or lose, was within her ability. So Emilia moved instantly to unfurl her mana.

"Emilia, don't! Forget about me! There's no way he's actually going to do it!"

"Shut up! I'm sick and tired of this! My ears are rottin' off listenin' to you and this woman's nonsense! Should I split open that loose mouth of yours, drop your jaw so you can't run it anymore, huh?!"

"――づっ"

Straddling the prone Subaru, Garfiel raked his sharp claws across Subaru's left cheek. The tips carved sharply into the flesh, and Subaru let out a small groan of pain at the burning sensation. But even so, his eyes did not yield.

"Push us aside, destroy the tomb, and then what? Run and run and run and run — you think you can actually get away?"

"It's your own regret. Who the hell can do anything about it? That thing never had any intention of letting us escape from the start. Why can't you get that!"

"I can't get it, Garfiel. — Because the past, regret — those can be overcome."

"――――"

Subaru's flat declaration. At those words, both Garfiel and Emilia caught their breath simultaneously.

"I gave up thinking it was too painful, too hard, too pathetic to even show my face. But it turns out I was the only one who thought there was nothing to be done — none of that was actually true."

Even if it was a temporary event, false parents, a reenactment of memory — a mere illusion — Subaru had faced his greatest regret and found there one answer, and one farewell. The Trial had certainly inflicted pain on Subaru. What ached in his chest even now at the mere memory of it was, without question, the brand of suffering the Trial had seared into him together with the past.

"But that pain, everything, all of it wrapped together — I swallowed the past. I was able to swallow it. ...It's true the witch is wicked, and I still can't forget being betrayed after choosing to trust her."

The pure white witch, always wearing that eerie smile, rose in his mind. His complicated feelings toward her would surely never untangle, no matter how much time passed.

But the feelings he'd gained in that moment — those, at least, he shouldn't have to betray.

"I'm grateful to the witch. I'm glad I was able to face the past. I ran, and ran, and kept on running... but I'm glad I couldn't outrun it."

"――――"

"Garfiel. — Are you still going to run from the past with your family?"

"Wha—ッ!?"

At Subaru's question, Garfiel's color changed. His face reddened with anger, then paled with shock, and now, turned ashen.

His fangs clacked together — his teeth trembling in small, rapid shudders. With an expression as though feeling a chill, or fear, Garfiel loomed over Subaru up close.

"My... past... who'd you hear that from..."

"More or less, everyone you can think of. You think that's betrayal? Or do you think it's something else?"

"Uh, ah, hff...ッ"

Overwhelmed by surging emotion, Garfiel couldn't even string words together, breathing in broken gasps. Faced with that intense agitation right before him, Subaru pressed on.

"The witch said you're afraid of the outside world."

Garfiel gave no answer.

"Frederica told me you knocked away the hand she held out, asking you to come to the outside world with her."

Garfiel gave no answer.

"Ryuzu-san told me what you saw in the tomb was a parting with your mother."

Garfiel's answer was—

"Haha, oh...ッ"

"I've heard the surface of the situation between you and Frederica. A human mother, and a half-Demi-Human father — half-siblings from different fathers. You two, being quarter-bloods, aren't bound by the Sanctuary's barrier. That's why Frederica went outside the Sanctuary — to build a place that would welcome the people here once the barrier is eventually opened."

"Sis...ッ"

"But you didn't take Frederica's hand. You stayed inside instead. Why? What do you want to do, what are you here in this place for, even now?"

His breath grew ragged. The hand pinning Subaru to the ground began, gradually, to tighten its grip. It wasn't meant to silence him. Garfiel himself was so exhausted that if he didn't hold on to something, cling to it with force, he seemed about to collapse right then and there.

"In the tomb, you saw the past. What you saw was your mother, leaving you and Frederica behind in the Sanctuary and walking out... wasn't it?"

"――――"

"Is that why you loathe the outside world..."

Subaru took his silence as confirmation. Garfiel remained silent, but he looked at Subaru with eyes weaker than Subaru had ever seen — a gaze so drained of strength it could hardly even be called a glare. The face of a small child, afraid of having a secret they'd been holding onto exposed.

Guilt at gouging into another person's wound stirred in Subaru's chest. Crushing that feeling down, Subaru pressed further toward Garfiel's truth. Digging his fingertip into the faintly visible wound, forcing the hole wider, drawing blood—

"Because your mother abandoned you. Because you hate the mother who abandoned you, hate the outside world that took her away — is that why you hate the outside world...!"

The conclusion reached in his discussion with Ryuzu. Echidna's words left behind: 'Garfiel fears the outside world.' The parting with his family — the factor that had lodged in Garfiel's heart and, even now, continued as a thorn causing him pain.

At Subaru's assertion-like accusation, Garfiel shook his head desperately from side to side.

"No! No, no no no no no no no!! What the hell would you understand! Don't run your mouth like you know somethin'!"

"That's right! What I said was guesswork, and I said it like I knew — but your real feelings can only come from you. If that's not it, then where's your real heart!?"

His lungs compressed, Subaru raised his voice without showing pain on his face. At the voice hurled up at him from directly below, Garfiel's expression stiffened and he reared back.

"You reject Frederica, who went out to the outside world! You bind yourself to the mission of protecting the Sanctuary, and get in the way of the Trial meant to open the barrier! What are you afraid of! What are you scared of! Isn't it the outside world you hate!"

"N-no...ッ!"

"Isn't it your mother, who abandoned you both, that you hate! You're afraid of taking the Trial and seeing yourself get abandoned again, aren't you!"

Garfiel's face twisted in anguish. Releasing the hand pinning Subaru down, Garfiel tried to sit up, to escape Subaru's pursuit of his words. He wouldn't let him. Reaching out an arm, Subaru grabbed the back of Garfiel's neck, holding back the body trying to pull away. At a distance close enough to feel his breath, Subaru glared at the blood-soaked, grief-stricken face and appealed to him.

"Answer me, Garfiel! What is it you were afraid of!"

The conclusion of his talk with Ryuzu, Echidna's words, Roswaal and Frederica's attitudes, the way Ram looked at Garfiel — Subaru saw a different answer in all of it. If it truly was so—

"No, I... I'm...ッ"

"What did you really think!!"

"I... I... to Mom, I...ッ"

Catching his breath, looking up at the sky, Garfiel, fangs trembling, said—

"――I wanted her to be happy...ッ!"

"We were in the way, weren't we!? Me and Sis, we were in the way of her being happy, weren't we!?"

It overflowed. Everything Garfiel had kept bottled up until now.

"I know that much! Me and Sis got abandoned. That's just how it is, of course it is!"

The reason Garfiel had carried inside his chest, never confided to anyone.

"We were kids she never even wanted, and mixed with Demi-Human blood on top of it... of course that'd get in the way of living in the outside world! What's so wrong about wanting to leave us behind, wanting to abandon us... nothing's wrong about it at all...ッ!"

His trembling voice, he couldn't hide; his trembling eyes, he tried to hide, covering his face with his palm.

"I know why we got abandoned. So I don't hold it against Mom for abandoning us! Of course I don't! Me and Sis, we were in the way of Mom's life! She left the Sanctuary to become happy, abandoning us!"

A still-young Garfiel had once seen his mother off as she left the Sanctuary, leaving them behind. A feeling he hadn't been able to understand back then — but one that had, without doubt, taken root, and over a long span of time had grown within him into a single blossom of an answer.

That his mother had abandoned them like that, on purpose.

But—

"But that night... I saw. Inside the tomb, in the Trial, I saw it, I saw it! Th-that Mom, who left us behind and went out of the Sanctuary... right after she left, she got caught up in a landslide, carriage and all, buried in earth and died...ッ!"

"――――!"

"Sis doesn't even know this... Sis, she still thinks Mom's out there somewhere, forgot about us, livin' happy. ...But that's not true! Mom died — right after she abandoned us! She just died!"

Fragments of truth, told from Garfiel's mouth as if wailing. The cruelty of what it meant struck down both Subaru, who knew the circumstances, and Emilia, who did not. Before the two of them, left speechless, Garfiel kept his face covered, spilling out ragged breaths like sobs.

"She just up and died... couldn't even become happy..."

Subaru could give no answer.

"Why? Wasn't she supposed to go out to the outside world to become happy?"

Emilia could give no answer.

"Wasn't it 'cause she wanted to be happy that she left us behind?"

Neither Subaru nor Emilia could give Garfiel an answer.

"If she abandoned us and then just died without even becoming happy..."

Garfiel kept voicing the question with no answer. It was, surely,

"Then what am I supposed to do with our loneliness, our sad feelings from bein' abandoned!?"

――A question that had been chanted over and over, endlessly, inside his heart.

"I wanted Mom to be happy...ッ!"

There was strength in his tearful voice. Lowering the palm that had covered his face, Garfiel gritted his teeth and let out a breath — gritted so hard his teeth seemed about to crack, his sharp canines nearly piercing his own lips.

"The sadness! The loneliness of bein' abandoned! I wanted — I wanted someone to tell me it all meant somethin' for her happiness! I wanted — someone to let me hate Mom...ッ!"

His feelings toward his mother lost anywhere to go, and Garfiel's heart, through the tomb, was sealed away into the Sanctuary. The surging emotion, with nowhere to strike, kept burning inside his body, fueled by his own soul as fuel for a flame that never went out.

"But Mom just died...! Me and Sis, all we got was sadness. Mom couldn't even become happy — she died crushed under heavy stone and sand, sufferin'."

Having reached that conclusion, in the ashes of the smoldering feelings inside himself, Garfiel had made his decision.

Rising, Garfiel pulled his body away from Subaru. Looking up at the tomb, Garfiel said in a low voice,

"――I'm never lettin' anyone go to the outside world. Ever."

His voice trembled. It held anger, grief, the residue of surging emotion, and a flame that still burned even now.

Garfiel turned back. Looking down at Subaru, who had sat up, he clacked his sharp fangs together.

"Happiness ain't somethin' you get by leavin' things behind and walkin' out! Tryin' to change somethin' always comes with pain, and not everybody can just endure that pain!"

"――――"

"There's plenty of people who can't do a damn thing about it! Everyone here is exactly that kind! What are they supposed to do, huh! Are they supposed to be sacrifices for somebody else's happiness, feel sad, is that it! Supposed to end up like me and Sis, huh!"

"――――"

"I — I'm gonna protect 'em."

He clenched his fists tight. Stopping his roaring, holding quiet resolve in his eyes now, Garfiel let out a breath.

"I'm gonna protect 'em. Everything within my reach, I'm gonna protect all of it. Protect it, protect it, I'll protect it, I swear... I won't let anyone be lost... I won't let anyone feel what Mom felt...ッ!"

Something that wasn't anger, something that wasn't grief — that was what shook Garfiel's heart. Neither Subaru nor Emilia could move before the feelings Garfiel had carried. Before the two of them, Garfiel spread both arms wide, back to the tomb, and shouted.

"I'm gonna become the barrier! A real one, dividin' outside from inside — a real barrier!"

"Garfiel..."

"So! I'm the one! I'm gonna protect the Sanctuary, protect everyone! Protect Granny! Only I can do it! Only I know it! And that's fine — nobody else needs to know!!"

Garfiel's resolve, his cry, his determination, wrung out as if coughing up blood. Before it, words failed to form.

Garfiel had now steeled every ounce of his resolve. If so, then—

"――Subaru."

"I'm okay, Emilia."

Emilia called out to Subaru as he rose and stepped forward. Waving a hand at her worried voice, Subaru walked out to stand before Garfiel.

Close enough that an outstretched arm would reach, the two faced each other. Words could no longer stop Garfiel. If that was so, there was only one thing left to do.

"You stubborn, hard-headed idiot who won't listen to reason..."

"――――"

"I get your resolve now. I had you figured wrong. And with that, you're still figuring things wrong too. So... I'm going to set it right."

Garfiel lowered his stance slightly, letting both arms hang loose. It looked defenseless, but it was a serious stance radiating an abnormal, demonic intensity.

Before that, Subaru too raised both hands and took his stance. Having decided that words wouldn't reach Garfiel, he would settle it on Garfiel's own ground instead.

"I'm going to beat you down completely and make you understand. — That you're a kind, weak idiot!!"
