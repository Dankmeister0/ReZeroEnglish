Arc 8 Chapter 71: "Heroic Fantasy"


——Let one thing be stated plainly, here.

Had Emilia not acted on her own initiative, on a sudden impulse, the Witch Sphinx's scheme would have succeeded, and the fall of the Vollachia Empire would have arrived in a form impossible to avert.

And what's more, it may have been something no one but Emilia could have stopped.

"—Hyah!"

Letting out a sharp breath, cheeks tensed, Emilia braced herself desperately, batting away the beams and orbs of light raining down from every direction with her polished ice weaponry.

Before her, the magic of her enemy — Sphinx, who looked exactly like Echidna — was formidable through and through; the only way to endure attacks too hard to dodge was to deflect them with ice she'd polished mirror-bright.

Emilia, too, was always being told by Subaru that "creativity matters!", so she took real care over the *details* of the weapons she made from ice — and this time, that care had paid off spectacularly.

Thanks to that habit, she was able to conjure weapons suited to countering Sphinx's magic right away.

"Thanks, as always."

Offering her thanks to the Subaru who wasn't there, Emilia geared up eagerly, clad in ice.

Even now, somewhere in the imperial capital, Subaru was surely holding hands with Beatrice and Spica and doing his best — and small as it was, even without being by her side, he was lending Emilia his strength like this.

Just thinking of Subaru let Emilia forget her fatigue and swing her great ice hammer all the harder.

"—Required: elimination. Immediately, at that."

Sphinx, facing Emilia, kept her distance while unleashing spell after spell. Exposed to that ferocious assault, Emilia had no sense of the impatience her opponent kept from showing on her face.

Sphinx had originally slipped into this deserted place, in secret, to do something. Sensing this somehow, Emilia had come rushing in with a "here goes!" — and that was how the fight began.

Whatever it was that Sphinx had been trying to do, it couldn't be allowed to happen.

With that thought, Emilia — though it wasn't her strong suit — focused single-mindedly on getting in her opponent's way.

—Here too, Emilia was making the one best move that no one else in the capital could have made.

Had Emilia taken Sphinx's life, the Witch would have carried the memory of that death back into her soul, grasped the threat that was Emilia, and manifested a new self prepared with a countermeasure.

But while Emilia meant to stop Sphinx, she had no intention of killing her.

So the situation Subaru had dubbed "death-fleeing" never arose, and against a threat named Emilia that had never been shared, Sphinx would send no new self as reinforcement.

This wouldn't have held true if it had been strongmen like Cecilus or Halibel here, nor sages like Vincent or Roswaal, nor outlaws like Subaru or Al.

No one but Emilia could have pinned Sphinx down here.

"Hyaah!"

Emilia had pulled off this miraculous alignment of circumstance relying on nothing but her own instincts, yet without any awareness of that, she kicked away an orb of light with her ice-shod boot—

"I don't know what it is, but I won't let you do whatever you're trying to do! And tell me where Priscilla is, too! I promised Schult-kun!"

"...One-sided, and greedy. Truly, you are every bit a witch."

Sphinx murmured coldly at the demand of Emilia, who leaned forward eagerly.

But that murmur carried an irritation too plain to fully hide.

—White light fills the vision, and in the next instant, the world ends.

That was the new phase of Return by Death that Natsuki Subaru would go on to taste again and again in a short span of time.

"—"

Tucked under the arm of the flying Roswaal, Subaru gripped hands tightly with Beatrice — held under Roswaal's other arm — behind the small of his back.

Beatrice's presence, Roswaal's warmth, the sensation of the wind buffeting his whole body — all of it was real.

And not only that—

"About that dragon-slaying just now — I don't think competing over who bagged the most heads is a very elegant way to go about it, you know. Honestly, what we really want isn't trophy heads but a thunderous round of applause, right? So for now, how about we wrap it up as 'the two heroes of Vollachia and Kararagi standing shoulder to shoulder'!"

"Fine, fine, that works for me. Standin' out too much has its own problems too — gettin' hauled up for this and that after the war's a hassle, y'know."

Out of the corner of his eye and ear, Subaru caught the undead Blight Dragon, severed from existence, dispersing into mist across the sky, while Cecilus and Halibel — who had literally killed it in an instant — bantered back and forth as they dropped toward the ground.

The two of them were unmistakably at the world's summit, transcendent beings neither inferior to the other, and yet even they couldn't defy the laws of physics and remain aloft forever — there was a certain sense of rightness in that, but—

"—Not now."

He had to grasp what was happening, identify the new threat, and come up with a countermeasure.

He had blocked the Magic Crystal Cannon, cut down the Blight Dragon bearing down on them, and watched a swelling green light in the far sky blow the clouds away — and even after all that, the Witch still had a trick in store.

There wasn't much time left before it activated, before the world turned white, before Subaru and the others lost their lives.

Think, think, think. Think think think think think think think think think think think think think think think think think think think think think think think think think think think think think think think think think think think think—.

"—Subaru."

So went Subaru's racing thoughts, when Beatrice's firm, steady voice reached his eardrums.

Holding his hand as she was, she must have felt it — the intense sense of loss that Subaru's mind and body, no, his very soul, had tasted, and the resolve to do something about it.

And that Beatrice didn't waste words on it besides was proof of her lovely, lofty resolve.

"Won't you say whatever it is? Betty will help with all of it, I suppose."

How much it saved him, that Beatrice said so without asking for more.

Feeling disgusted with himself for always being the one who only ever received, Subaru nodded.

He nodded, and—

"—Take it right to the edge, right up until my consciousness blows out. I'll give the signal, so keep it ready so we can use that thing from before, anytime!"


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△


—Overhead, Subaru raised his voice, and in response, black and blue went racing across the sky.

The specific content of the order hadn't carried this far. But in its place, something else seemed to be heard.

The sound, unmistakably, of the world's mismatched gears catching, meshing, and beginning to turn — in order to spin out a thread of time made of light.

"—"

Whether it was an auditory hallucination made no difference. In fact, that was surely how it would turn out.

Natsuki Subaru had thought it through and made his move as the best possible way to break the deadlock. That meant it could be trusted. —No, there was no choice but to trust it.

That was the last, scraped-together best move of Al — Aldebaran — who had called out to Subaru that the Witch's countless schemes weren't yet exhausted.

"Damn it."

The short curse rang strangely loud, audible only to his own ears within his helmet.

Because of that, he felt as though he'd been made to notice, with emphasis, that he'd muttered it unconsciously.

What in the world did Aldebaran have to be frustrated about, anyway?

He'd known it from the moment he found Natsuki Subaru in this empire: that when it came down to it, he should use Natsuki Subaru without hesitation.

Disliking that, loathing it — in the end, that was nothing but pointless stubbornness.

—His own, who was nothing more than a star chasing after that same Natsuki Subaru, unable to do the same things.

"...Required: reconsideration."

At that voice, heard so suddenly, Aldebaran turned around without a word.

There was Sphinx, her body from the neck down bound fast in tangible black light, sprawled defenseless on the ravaged ground.

Her Gate completely sealed, reduced literally to a state where she couldn't lift hand or foot, Sphinx nonetheless showed neither impatience nor anger on that detestable face, only staring fixedly at Aldebaran.

It was an unpleasant gaze, as if he were the one being observed.

"Reconsideration? You mean changing your mind? About what, exactly? No matter what I do, you never even try to tell me what's going on in that head of yours."

Twisting the corner of his mouth, Aldebaran glared down at Sphinx from behind his helmet.

That much was true, too. —Aldebaran had already interrogated this captured Sphinx again and again, and confirmed that she would not breathe a word about her scheme.

Ironically, among the countless attempts available to Aldebaran, there existed a certain number of outcomes that simply would not bend, no matter what.

No matter how many times he rolled the dice, even into infinity, he could never make it come up zero or seven.

Yes, he couldn't. —Not Aldebaran.

"But you know what, you lot are finished. You made an enemy of someone you shouldn't have. Reconsidering anything now won't—"

"What I reconsidered was my assessment of you."

"Huh?"

"I had recognized you as nothing more than a mere attendant of Priscilla Barielle. ...But you are the same as me, aren't you."

"—"

"A martyr who cannot fulfill the purpose for which they were made, and so struggles on living because of it. —Because I have achieved my own purpose of creation, I cannot help but pity you, still marking time in place."

A captive, a mage with her magic sealed away — and yet Sphinx, without bluster or sour grapes, quietly pitied Aldebaran.

At the sight of Sphinx's utterly sincere gaze, Aldebaran's throat froze solid.

Aldebaran was aware that the cold which had frozen his throat was, in truth, murderous intent.

This sharp-tongued woman wearing a witch's face had to be killed. He had to snuff out her breath entirely. Of all people, she had no right to say such a thing to Aldebaran with that face.

—Just who did she think had made him Aldebaran in the first place?

"You—"

"Al! Lend me your strength!!"

It was the instant his vision dyed crimson red and his thoughts were painted over white.

Not even a second later — a mere fraction of one — and Aldebaran would have killed Sphinx. But he was held back by a wind gliding in with furious speed, together with a voice that struck like a blow.

"I need your strength! Just like you said, the Great Disaster isn't over yet! This is an all-out fight!"

Landing right beside him, it called out to Aldebaran with a face gone pale.

Beside him, bringing along the two dependable figures of the long-haired mage and the spirit in a dress, was Natsuki Subaru — who could reach a light beyond Aldebaran's own grasp, and who could search out as many hands as he needed.

"Even if you tell me to lend a hand......"

"Shut up!"

"—!"

All sorts of awkward embarrassment mixed together, and that was what slipped out of Aldebaran's mouth.

But Subaru, as if he had no idea of Aldebaran's inner turmoil, thrust out his fist,

"I don't have the luxury right now of sitting with everyone's individual problems one by one! So just switch gears and cooperate, right this instant! You said it yourself before, didn't you!"

"I did...... before?"

"That you're counting on me! Well, I'm saying the exact same thing back to you!"

"—Ah."

At Subaru shouting with his face red, Aldebaran caught his breath.

At the same time, what came back to him was the exchange between the two of them, back when he'd reunited with Subaru in this Vollachia Empire — back in the walled city of Guaral, when Subaru had been on the verge of losing sight of his own reason for existing.

Rejected by a girl named Rem, his footing unsteady, Subaru had been encouraged by Aldebaran.

At that time, what had Aldebaran said to him—

"Whatever confidence you've lost, everything — there's no way to get it back except through results. It's all the same as it's always been!"

"—"

At that single call from Subaru, the girl holding his hand, and the long-haired one standing behind him, both let expectation seep into their eyes.

And then Subaru, who gave strength to those around him, reached out his hand to Aldebaran too, just the same, saying — as if returning the favor from back then —

"Come on, Al! —Bear it with me — the Heroic Fantasy!"

—The Witch's scheme, the fall of the Vollachia Empire, "death" itself — it had all come to pass, again and again.

An ending in the form of painless death might, perhaps, be the ideal way to end.

A finale that arrives before you even understand what's happened might be the one kind mercy that could save someone who fears an end that is bound to come, from that very fear.

But still, he thought.

"No, that's not it — my ideal way to go is dying peacefully of old age, surrounded by Emilia-tan and Bea-ko and our grandkids."

Even supposing the Great Disaster's whole final-boss-style philosophy was to save every human being from sorrow and suffering by bringing them an end free of pain and fear, Subaru flatly refused it.

When he thought of the Emilia and Beatrice he'd be leaving behind upon reaching the ideal ending he pictured for himself, a pain like being stabbed over and over with shards of glass ran through his heart — but that was a worry he himself was meant to suffer through and find an answer to, on the way to that point.

There was absolutely no way he'd just dump it all onto some sweet dream someone else handed him.

In the first place, this was all hypothetical anyway, and the Great Disaster's actual aim wasn't some gentle ending — its whole direction was piling up every terrible scheme it could think of to destroy the empire, so the premise didn't even hold.

So there was no compromise in this fight other than crushing the Great Disaster's goal outright.

"......Bit by bit, it's coming into view."

At first, the world had turned white with no means of resistance, and Subaru — without even knowing what it was that destroyed him and his companions — had no choice but to be swallowed by the "death" forced upon him.

What's more, the point he returned to was right after Subaru and Beatrice had been caught under Roswaal's arms and the Blight Dragon bearing down on them had been cut down by Cecilus and Halibel.

Less than a single minute after that, the ending would come.

"—"

The grace period was short, and there was much to be done.

If Al hadn't told him it was the Witch's plot, it would have taken time just to uncover that much. Thanks to him, Subaru had probably cut out five or more Returns by Death.

Even so, it had taken a number of attempts to determine what exactly was happening.

And in the midst of that trial and error—

"The most likely thing is that the 'Stone Mass'...... Muspel gets killed, I suppose. If the enemy has one comeback move left in reserve, that's got to be the highest possibility, I suppose."

"There's a possibility our opponent has prepared a trigger to bring about the collapse of the empire's very la-a-and, that great calamity. ——If it's Sphinx's usual method, some sort of magic circle is possible too."

"Well, for now, Groovy already lifted the 'Curse of Thorns,' which was the biggest problem, so I don't reckon there's much reason to think they've got another curse hidden up their sleeve, no?"

"Yes yes yes yes! From what Al-san said, if Anya dies the whole empire's a goner, so that might be part of it too! Meaning there's a chance a huge army gets sent after Anya! Guess it's time to do the thousand-man slaughter again, been a while!"

"Uu! Uwaau! Aah, uu!"

"So you're safe and well, little one...! I hear Tanza's been ever so well looked after by you, ain't she. If there's anythin' I can lend a hand with, you just say the word, ain't there."

"Schwartz-sama, I have a bad feeling...... Is everyone in the Battalion safe?"

"Young as you are, you have good eyes. My star, too, seems to rely on you — though not so much as I. Say whatever you need, if there is need."

"Boss! I'm happy an' all, but it's too early to be praisin' me! Ain't over yet, is it?! Say whatever ya need, I'll do whatever it takes!"

"......Get lost. Don't lump me in with the rest of you, brat."

'Fadin' away, I am...... the dragon can't do... nothin' more, now......'

"Fading away, I am...... the dragon can't do... nothing more, now......"

"Subaru-chin, thinkin's not really my thing, but I'll think! Subaru-chin, do your best too!"

"Oh, sorry 'bout this, but seein' as I've lost both me hands, you're gonna hafta come back on your own, ain't ya? Don't reckon I can knead your soul back into its original shape like this, kah kah kah kah!"

He wasn't some superman who could rack his own brain alone and solve everything.

Within the limited span of just one minute, repeating it however many times in exchange for "death," Subaru closed in on the board his enemy had built, move by move, move by move, with certainty.

The death of the "Stone Mass" Muspel, the magic circle, the Curse of Thorns, Arakiya's life, Spica's Star-Eating, glad Yorna's safe, the state of the war at the fortress city, the undead emperor cheerfully gushing about his wife, our Garfiel's the best, the hopeless drunk, the dragon who won't talk, the Dragonkin who won't talk, keep trying, please don't say anything that saps the will to keep trying, etc. etc.——

"—Fool. Don't hang your head, Natsuki Subaru."

"Neither you nor I have time to hang our heads. We already decided to struggle on, didn't we."

"I have no intention of giving up. ——Just who do you take me for."

Behind that arrogant way of speaking, just how much genuine self-respect was really there.

He wouldn't do anything so tactless as peer behind that. Fine, then. Let's do this.

Giving up simply wasn't a choice Natsuki Subaru could make.

"—"

The same went for her, the one he couldn't find no matter how hard he searched.

Rather than worrying over that, right now his faith in her won out. ——She might be in danger, but she wasn't the type to sit quietly somewhere safe; surely she was out there fighting to seize the future for them.

So — let's go overturn it. ——The Witch's board.

"Come on, Al! —Bear it with me — the Heroic Fantasy!"

An impossible unreasonableness was moving to overturn the Witch Sphinx's final scheme.

"—Every preparation, complete."

That was how it should have been. Yet every last bit of it was being overturned.

She had obtained the result she wanted, accomplished the supreme proposition of fulfilling her purpose of creation, and for the briefest moment a genuine sense of achievement had reigned within her chest — but one after another, the situation kept twisting out of shape.

What was it that stood in the way of Sphinx, of the Great Disaster?

"—Vincent Vollachia."

"—"

"—Aldebaran."

"—"

"—Natsuki Subaru."

As though drawing close to the very heart of Sphinx, red lips spoke those names.

Sphinx, chosen as the Great Disaster, had begun a fight that staked the very survival of the Vollachia Empire — and clinging in pursuit, determined to overturn her scheme entirely, was a deterrent force trying to set the turning of the stars right.

"How do you assess them?"

At Sphinx's question, she turned to look this way, the metallic clink of chains sounding.

Crimson eyes gazed at Sphinx across the water-mirror — Priscilla Barielle laughed at the question. It was neither mockery nor pity, but a smile.

Anyone who knew Priscilla would have been startled to see it. There was even fondness in that smile.

The fondness that Priscilla Barielle held for Sphinx.

"Why......"

"Why I'd make such a face? Wasn't it you who asked me for my assessment of those men just now? How very like the 'Witch of Greed' — greedy even for answers."

"If it is mockery......"

"You know full well it is not."

Cut off sharply, Sphinx was silenced by Priscilla's gaze.

Neither her eyes nor her words held any color of mockery toward Sphinx any longer. Then what was it that dwelled in those crimson eyes?

It clawed at Sphinx's chest from within. An unfamiliar sensation.

It was unlike the feeling of being driven, again and again, by the sense of duty to fulfill her purpose of creation.

It was, for Sphinx, an impulse both unbearable and impossible to let slip away.

"Remember this. That is what it means to burn with longing."

"Burn... with longing......"

"If you would seize something beyond your own station, you must struggle on even through the agony of being burned by flame. There may be those who do not seek such a thing, but a life like that is worthless."

"—"

"Be burned by the flame, and listen to what your soul cries out. Live as though burning with longing. Love as though drowning. ——This world is made to suit me exactly as I please."

At that declaration, Sphinx truly tasted a sensation like being struck by lightning.

Feeling an absolute, unshakable self, a confidence that would never waver, the inescapable heat of flame, Sphinx — in dying — felt for the first time the real sense of being alive.

And at the same time, she burned with longing. ——To win against this woman before her, this woman like a flame.

And so—

"Required: countermeasures—no. Stop me, if you can."


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△


The imperial capital, enclosed by a star-shaped fortress with five vertices, had a pentagram magic circle laid out to align exactly inverted with those five points — and the Sphinxes tasked with activating it, puzzled by the incomplete casting of the spell formula, accepted its effect regardless.

The target of the magic circle's effect was the now-defenseless magic crystal of the Crystal Palace.

By breaking down that colorless, colossal mass of mana, the Witch Sphinx had schemed to draw the nearly-depleted mana into her own soul and obtain vast power.

Now that she had succeeded in reproducing her soul as the "Witch of Greed," Sphinx could wield the same power as her creator, who possessed knowledge of every kind of magic — if only she could secure the mana for it.

To that end—

"The completion of the magic circle——"

"I'm afraid that stopping just that is the order I received from His Excellency and the Boss."

In that instant, a flash of blade-light, too fast to even allow a reaction, sent Sphinx's head flying.

Her long white hair was cut off level with her severed neck, and in the spinning, whirling view, Sphinx saw the figure of the young man who had killed her.

A stroke too beautiful for words — and yet, still not enough. It couldn't end here.

Even an incomplete magic circle had let her soul catch its breath.

All that was left—

"—Ah, I see. So you, too, are drowning."

At the result of Sphinx's choice in that next instant, Cecilus Segmunt, sword in hand, smiled.

Before Cecilus's eyes, multiple Sphinxes — restored from a soul that had carried home the taste of his blade — stood in his way, determined not to let him stop the magic circle's full activation.

The Sphinx blocking his path tilted her head,

"There is no water source here. What do you mean, drowning? Required: explanation."

"No real river, pond, lake, or even a puddle is needed. This thing called the 'Great Ocean' exists in everyone's heart. Anyone who wants something badly enough, so they say, is drowning in it."

At that way of putting it, Sphinx's tilted head still didn't right itself.

But paying that no mind, Cecilus set his hand to the sheathed hilt of his sword, readied himself, and named himself.

"—Swordsman, Cecilus Segmunt."

"—'Witch of Greed,' Sphinx."

Before they knew it, the words had spilled naturally from the mouths of the Sphinxes.


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△


At the same moment that the "Blue Lightning" and the Witch's arts and skills shook the imperial capital, fierce battles to stop the magic circle's activation were beginning in three other places as well, just the same.

"—'Extoller,' Halibel."

"—'Witch of Greed,' Sphinx."

"—The 61st Vollachia Emperor, Eugard Vollachia."

"—'Witch of Greed,' Sphinx."

"—'Gorgeous Tiger,' Garfiel Tinzel."

"—'Witch of Greed,' Sphinx."

Natsuki Subaru's handpicked elites, serving as the spear to crush the Great Disaster's final scheme, faced off against the Witch and were exposed to a ferocious, staggering onslaught of magic.

If the magic crystal of the Crystal Palace, being broken down bit by bit, were to flow entirely into the Witch's soul, stopping her scheme would no longer be possible.

Because her means would then expand without limit.

Magic, through the imagination and adaptability of its user, could extend the branches of its possibility infinitely.

In other words, by obtaining an extreme abundance of mana, the Witch Sphinx would gain the power of a transcendent being capable of turning nearly every impossibility possible.

And that would hand her the key to overcoming every obstacle and fulfilling whatever wish she desired.

But what was truly to be feared about Sphinx was not her vision of obtaining that overwhelming power.

What was truly to be feared was her thoroughness, the fact that she wasn't singularly fixated on that one goal alone.

"Bea-ko! Roswaal!"

"Minya!"

"Ul Goa."

A storm of violet arrows and blazing shells spread out with no escape, and the surging Undead were blown away.

But even as the vanguard was repelled, the Undead trampling over them and pressing forward had, by this point, had their sense of self utterly destroyed, attacking now as puppets obeying orders alone. ——To kill Arakiya.

Not just the magic circle — stopping this, too, was a necessary condition for Subaru and the others' victory.

"Al!"

"I know that already!"

Breaking through the encirclement, Al leapt at an Undead targeting the girl's life, plunged an earthen broadsword into it, swelled the blade inside its body, and blew it apart from within.

This was the first time Subaru had seen Al fight since Chaosflame, and watching him again, it was so reckless it made his heart race. There was no calling him a skilled warrior, not by a long shot.

Even so, this was an all-out fight, everyone throwing themselves in together, taking on whatever came within reach.

"—A minute had passed."

Having pushed past the fatal line that had claimed him every time before, Subaru felt the progress of the situation for real.

Sphinx, who led the Great Disaster — honestly, the shock of seeing her appearance turn into an exact likeness of Echidna had been no small thing, but it was Beatrice who had held back Subaru's confusion.

Even faced with the sight of the Witch, who had become the spitting image of Echidna — the one who could be called her own birth parent — Beatrice had bravely kept her composure.

"Subaru, that is a different person from Mother, I suppose."

"Indeed so. She bears not the slightest resemblance to the 'Witch of Greed' Echidna. ——Though she is, without question, an extremely, thoroughly unpleasant opponent, mind you."

Pushed forward, for some reason, by words from Roswaal just as certain as Beatrice's, Subaru focused his attention less on the Witch's eerie appearance and more on seeing through her aim.

To begin with, the very fact that the Witch — who should have been burned away by Abel's Sunlight Sword — had survived by changing her form was itself the ultimate counter to both the Sunlight Sword and the Star-Eating.

Cracking that, too, had to stay within view.

But for now—

"If we can completely disrupt the magic circle's activation——"

That was the instant he thought it.

—When, in the sky above the imperial capital, a colossal water-mirror unfurled and cast an image, thrusting before them the utterly desperate moment of the "Fortress City" Garkla, on the very verge of being blasted away by a falling star.


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△


"—Abel-chin!!"

Medium, her face drained of color, called out to Vincent in a voice tight with urgency. She held Madelyn in one arm, and with the cleaver in her other hand, pointed at the sky.

There, a colossal distortion of light seemed to blanket the entire sky over the capital, and Vincent quickly understood it to be a thinly spread mass of water. For an instant he was on guard, wondering if it was the Witch's next prepared attack, but the moment after, when the rippling water's surface cast an image of some scene, his suspicion turned to certainty.

Sure enough, it was the Witch's attack.

Though not one that plainly wounded the body — instead, it was an attack that pierced the spirit, thrusting an inescapable reality before everyone in a form none could look away from.

—What the sky's water-mirror showed was the fortress city, surrounded by a great army of Undead.

He could see starlight raining down from directly above the fortress of the sword-wolves, who — drawing off the Undead army just like Vincent and the others — were waging a fight that staked the empire's very survival.

It was the same beautiful judgment of annihilation that had fallen upon the capital too, seeking to bring ruin.

If that fell upon the fortress city, the walls and the fortress itself would collapse, and most of those holed up inside would die. And that would mean the ruin of the empire, unrecoverable even if the capital's own operation succeeded.

Starlight over the capital, the flame of ruin, the runaway magic core, and now the magic circle targeting the Crystal Palace.

The Witch's ruinous schemes, coming one after another, aimed their point not just at the capital, but at the ruin of the empire as a whole.

A truly wise schemer strikes the genuinely fatal blow in such a way that it's never sensed coming.

And so—

"—Our pessimism has proven correct, Natsuki Subaru."

That was the moment right after Vincent spoke those words.

—When, from a hole that opened in the sky as if smashing through the heavenly water-mirror, the flame of ruin shot straight through, heading north across the capital.


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△


—The genuine, honest-to-goodness trump card of Natsuki Subaru and Beatrice.

The flame of ruin from the Magic Crystal Cannon, sent into another dimension with Al Shamak, was brought back from that other dimension into this one with a second Al Shamak — and it shot down the starlight that should have ended the "Fortress City."

Thanks to the help of Cecilus and Halibel, the trump card that hadn't had to be spent slaying the Blight Dragon instead shot down the Witch's last move, prepared to shake them to their core.

"—!"

Bathed in the shattered water-mirror as it turned into a torrent of raindrops and poured over the capital like a passing shower, Subaru, gripped by a violent, draining exhaustion, reeled with dizziness and his nose burst with blood.

It was the backlash from having drawn on Beatrice's full power, on and on, for hours now.

However priceless the cuteness Beatrice scattered about, the cost of drawing out her competence was steep. And there was a limit even to sharing that burden with the Pleiades Battalion.

That limit reared up right before him, and Subaru dropped to one knee without meaning to.

"Subaru!"

He was held up by Beatrice, who cried out — but though he wanted to reassure her, no voice would come.

Beatrice tried to stem the nosebleed, dripping and dripping without end, with the sleeve of her dress. He wanted to stop her, not wanting her fine outfit stained, but his arm still wouldn't lift.

"Not yet, not yet......"

He couldn't afford to stop here.

There was still a role he had to fulfill, a scheme he still had to stop.

It was none other than Subaru himself who had decided this would be an all-out fight.

For that very Subaru to be the first to run out of strength — as if such a stupid thing could be allowed to happen.

So—

"—Ah?"

"Subaru? You mustn't push yourself, I suppose. Rest, even just a little——"

Beatrice hurried to caution Subaru as he let out a breath — but her words stopped partway. ——Because Subaru had stood back up, right where he was.

"—"

Beatrice stared at Subaru as he stood, her round eyes wide. Bathed in her gaze, Subaru looked down at his own two hands, bewildered.

The exhaustion that had assailed his whole body just a moment before had vanished without a trace. ——No, it was more than just vanishing. If anything, it was the very opposite.

"Strength...... is welling up?"

Subaru murmured, dazed, and just as the words said, vitality was overflowing through his entire body.

It was a sensation similar to, yet distinct from, the way his mind and body's strength surged fiercely when his feelings became one with his comrades of the Pleiades Battalion — this was a sense of omnipotence, of being held in a great flame.

And Subaru understood at once just why it had been brought on.

—The shattered heavenly water-mirror, the flame of ruin trailing a band of light as it pierced the distant starlight. In the capital's sky, which those two had swept across, a new light had been born.

It was small compared to the starlight, smaller still compared to the flame of ruin — and yet, in brilliance alone, it was not merely a match for them but a blaze that overwhelmed them both.

Falling from the heavens was a woman like living flame, clad in a blood-red dress——

"—You have done well."

Everyone had already looked up at the sky when the water-mirror tried to project despair. But this, with a vividness beyond even that, drew the eyes of everyone in the capital up to the heavens and set their very souls alight.

She was falling. Her orange hair streaming, her crimson eyes blazing bright — a lone she-wolf among sword-wolves——

"No need for excess words. ——Simply call my name."

A crimson jeweled sword gripped in her hand, a princess like the sun falling toward a city in dire straits — and as one of those looking up at her, Subaru, without meaning to, found himself carried along by her intent.

That is to say—

"—Priscilla."

So he called out the name of the one descending.

—Her name, called by one who had kindled flame in his right eye, one graced by the blessing of the sun.
