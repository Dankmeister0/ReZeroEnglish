Arc 6 Chapter 14: "Trust Upon the Sand"

The cold sand's mockery, and the warmth of Ram conveyed through the palm touching it.
Both were, for Natsuki Subaru right now, reality — a "present" he had to accept.

"...Ram."

"What?"

"Your fingers are smooth, feels nice... ow, gah!"

"Don't get carried away. Barusu."

"Would you quit using a person's name like it's an insult!?"

Struck across the cheek as the price for his careless remark, Subaru pleaded with tears in his eyes. But Ram, cantera in hand, didn't respond to that — she hurriedly put some distance between them.
Just a moment ago she'd shown a gentleness rarely seen, and now this coldness. Though honestly, if she'd stayed that way, it would've been too embarrassing to even talk — so returning to her usual tone was probably her true intention.

"Where is..."

"Some pitch-black darkness, location unknown. You remember, don't you — the scenery of the sand sea splitting apart, and the dragon carriage getting swallowed into the crevice along with us? I don't particularly want to explain further than that."

"No, that's enough. I've got the general picture."

"...I see."

Ram laid out the gist of the current situation, and Subaru took a deep breath and accepted it.
That he had "Returned by Death," and that the point of that "Return by Death" had already shifted once again.

—In this sand dune, this made three "deaths" Subaru had met.

Two of those had been aboveground, presumably deaths from the light fired from the watchtower. But this third time was entirely different from the ones before — a death underground, attacked by a monster.
Recalling his own death just moments — a mere minute — before, he couldn't suppress a shudder.

"To die like that, of all things..."

This time, Subaru's cause of death was what you'd call burning to death.
His whole body thoroughly scorched by crimson flames, killed by fire while sinking into total incomprehension. The way that scorching tongue had licked over every part of him, and how every part of him had melted rather than merely burned — that was hard to forget.
He'd known in the abstract that the human body was a lump of fat, but did it really melt so easily into a dripping mess? That charred corpses were a wretched sight — well, now that made a convincing kind of sense.

"Barusu, calmed down yet? If you're okay, let's talk."

"Ah, yeah. I'm fine. ...Is it just you and me here?"

"If it were anyone else, Ram would have abandoned Barusu and fled for her own safety. The fact that I haven't done that... you understand, don't you?"

"Did you just unleash round two of your rarely-seen gentleness?"

"That's an extremely disrespectful thing to say to Ram, a font of compassion and mercy. —While we've been wasting time chatting, someone's come back."

She indicated with the cantera to the left from where Subaru was looking — Ram's right — and he could see another light approaching from deeper within the cavity. The cantera swaying at a height one head taller, as he already knew, was the light belonging to Anastasia, mounted atop Patrasche.

"Looks like you and Natsuki-kun finished your talk, Ram-san?"

"...Anastasia, and Patrasche."

The same opening line as before, and Anastasia atop Patrasche smiled at them. At her words, Ram again, just as before, pinched her skirt and bowed.

"Thank you for your consideration, Anastasia-sama. So, how does the area look?"

"I only went a bit further in and looked around, but I didn't find the others. Seems like the ones blown here are just us three... and this little one."

"I see."

"You mustn't let it get you down. Not that it's much comfort."

"Thank you for your concern. Yes, I understand that... I think I do."

Ram, being cared for from atop the dragon, seemed her usual self, and yet was not quite usual. This time the cantera-holder was Ram, so her profile was clearly visible.
Just as Anastasia had said before. From Ram's profile, looking faintly worn out, it came through that she had been distraught over being separated from Rem.

"Right now, the only ones who've regrouped are the four of us. ...It's bad that we've got no combat power."

"You're less shaken than I expected, Natsuki-kun. Or did Ram-san do some kinda special charm on you?"

"Unfortunately, leaving Ram and Barusu alone together produces nothing. Only the following day would Barusu be found as a slashed corpse."

"What is with you? You got some kind of werewolf-from-the-werewolf-game trait? Scary."

It wasn't exactly forced cheer, but Anastasia and Ram remarked on Subaru's composure just after waking like that. It was only because this was the second time and he already grasped the situation quickly — but being able to move right away was, this time too, something he wanted to count as an advantage.

"Ram, just to check — you can't sense Rem's location through your empathic link, right?"

"No, that's no good. There's a connection, so I'm at least certain she's alive... but nothing beyond that. I can only pray she's not alone."

"If good and bad luck have anything to do with how you've lived, then Rem's conduct has definitely been nothing but good deeds. ...I believe she's absolutely fine."

If this theory were correct, it would mean that Subaru meeting with unreasonable ends and dying over and over was because of Subaru's own conduct — but he preferred to shut his eyes to that.

"And besides..."

An enormous anxiety that Subaru held, one he could never reveal to Ram or Anastasia.
The possibility that the things he wanted to save might slip through his fingers, now that the "Return by Death" checkpoint had updated.
Just as he'd been unable to bring back Rem, whose name had been devoured, through "Return by Death."

If, with this update to "Return by Death," some tragedy had befallen his separated companions.

"That's the one thing that absolutely cannot happen...!"

Emilia and Beatrice, not here. Julius and Meili. And Rem.
He prayed that nothing unfortunate had happened to them.
That somewhere beyond Natsuki Subaru's reach, they weren't hurting.

"Barusu. —Are you not connected to Beatrice-sama?"

"I tried, but it's no good. If the pathway were open, Beako should be able to sense me from her end... but even trying to call her out, the connection's thin."

"Might be the Miasma. Spirits can't hold their breath long either, apparently — my Echidna keeps grumbling about it too. ...The only one doing fine was Emilia-san."

"Was Beako like that too, I wonder."

In Beatrice's case, strong-willed as she was, she wouldn't be the type to complain even if something were wrong. In that case, it should have been up to Subaru to properly notice it.
Beatrice's condition could affect the safety of the entire group. Once they were safely reunited, he'd have to give her a proper scolding.

"So for that reason too, we regroup with the others. —Let's go, deeper into the cavity!"

Heading further in raised the possibility of encountering that monster that had burned Subaru again.
But even so, there was no path but forward.

To reunite with his precious companions too, there was no path but forward.

"Natsuki-kun, you're the only one on foot — your footing okay?"

"――――"

Advancing deeper into the cavity, Patrasche gave a small cry toward Subaru, who led the way with the cantera. That cry, sure enough, was the sound she made when she had no particular complaint.
Perhaps because she too understood this was an emergency, Patrasche was a hard worker despite carrying passengers other than Subaru. As expected of the most capable woman in the Emilia Camp.

With Ram and Anastasia riding Patrasche, and only Subaru traversing the sand labyrinth on foot — the arrangement followed the previous loop, and the four of them pressed on deeper into the cavity.
Their departure might have been ten-odd minutes earlier compared to last time, but given they had no idea how long that monster had been in that spot, it was probably within the margin of error.

"That place must be its den... a feeding ground, or maybe even a playground..."

He couldn't quite bring himself to say "feeding ground" outright, because of the sheer number of charred corpses that had been in that space.
The blackened corpse Subaru had ended up grabbing was probably an animal's — from its shape, he'd thought it was the hind leg of some four-legged creature — but there was no trace of it having been eaten. And corpses like that, charred black, had been strewn everywhere in that place, countless in number.
If it were merely using fire to cook its food, you could at least credit it as some kind of gourmet Witchbeast with a food culture worth appreciating. But if it just burned things and left the corpses lying there, then that monster had a cruel disposition that enjoyed watching its prey burn to death, and it seemed more natural to think of that place as existing for that purpose.

That deranged firepower, and that grotesque, misshapen body.
Recalling the moment of his own burning death, Subaru simultaneously felt terror at the Witchbeast itself, a creature existing in a form fundamentally wrong for a living thing.

"...From what I can see, this area's gotta be the sand dunes for sure, but I get the feeling it might be underground. What do you think, Natsuki-kun?"

"Natsuki-kun?"

"Eh? Ah? Ah, right. I think so too."

"Were you actually listening? Giving careless answers is rude, you know."

Lost in thought, his careless nod was instantly caught by Ram. But Subaru, waving both hands and head, insisted that wasn't so.

"No way, I was totally listening. And on top of that, my stance is a full-on affirmative."

"I was saying that when you sleep, not wearing a bra keeps your breasts from losing their shape better, wasn't I. You're affirming that? How lewd."

"You bringing up a topic like that to me is the lewd part!? Or wait, that's not what you were saying, was it!?"

Subaru, aghast, turned to Anastasia for help, and she gave a wry smile. Then Anastasia added, "No need to worry," and continued,

"That was just Ram-san messing around. There's no way we'd talk about something like that in an emergency like this."

"R-right? Yeah, that's right. That scared me. Besides, neither Ram nor Anastasia have breasts big enough to worry about losing their shape anyway..."

"Ahh, oops, sorry, Natsuki-kun, my hand slipped."

"Hot, in the past tense!?"

The cantera pressed against the nape of his neck, and the residual heat of the magic-crystal glow scorched him. The sensation of burning to death instantly revived, making him shudder, but he narrowly avoided rolling on the cold sand screaming.
Still, having fallen backward onto the sand, Subaru glared up at the two on the dragon.

"I-isn't that a bit much, out of nowhere? I'd like at least a chance to explain myself."

"Bringing up a person's physical features to put them down is a no-no. That kinda thing's only okay when you do it to yourself. In Karlagi, you'd lose all credibility and end up penniless for it. Right, Ram-san?"

"Ram isn't bothered by it. Besides, Rem's are large, so it balances out between us. No problem at all."

Something about the beautiful sisterly love of compensating for each other threatened to invite further displeasure if he brought it up, so Subaru kept quiet.
He grimaced at the faint pain on his nape and stood up, brushing off the sand and clearing his throat.

"Anyway, let's talk about the main topic. This being underground, right?"

"See, you were listening. Yeah, that's what I think. The air being heavier compared to the surface, and the temperature being lower — those are my reasons."

"Underground beneath the dunes... let's hope it's not a sand earthworm's burrow."

"...There's a possibility it's something worse than that."

Anastasia, pulling her robe closed to signal the chill, was echoed by Ram. Nodding along to their opinions, Subaru mixed in a bit of the truth.
Of course, even against a sand earthworm, this group with its lack of combat power would find it a supreme challenge to drive one off — but a sand earthworm, against which they had at least some grasp of countermeasures, was still preferable.

That Witchbeast — for convenience's sake, he'd call it a centaur for now — had burned Subaru the instant they met, so he had no idea, of course, of any combat capability against it, nor even how to fight it at all.
If they clashed head-on the same way as last time, he was confident he'd die the same way.

"Actually, regardless of sand earthworms specifically, there's a real chance we run into Witchbeasts. About the ones in these dunes — we should probably discuss countermeasures."

"True. ...Though even so, all Ram has is her own knowledge plus what she heard from Meili along the way. And that's probably just general Witchbeast ecology, at that."

"The Witchbeasts of the Augria Sand Dunes seem to have turned savage from the Miasma's influence, and to begin with there are Witchbeasts here that by rights shouldn't even exist ecologically. Even so, having knowledge's better than not."

When Subaru proposed this, Ram and Anastasia nodded along.
As a result, alongside their search deeper into the cavity, a discussion about the Witchbeasts got underway.

"The sand earthworm stands out for its hideous appearance and stench, but despite how savage it seems, its body isn't all that strong. It's big, but Ram's magic can kill it easily. ...For that matter, even Barusu's whip might not be entirely useless."
"Seriously? The whip can do damage?"
"Its temperament's apparently rather cowardly, so if it feels pain, there are cases where it turns and flees. Depends on the scope of that pain, but the chance isn't zero."

"I'd rather not run into a pouch-kangaroo if I can help it."
"Rat? Despite the name, it's a vicious Witchbeast?"
"Nah, it's got no combat strength. It's just that its way of fighting is nasty. It puffs itself up with air until its body's swollen full, then explodes near the enemy. And it showers you with its own blood and organs."
"...Is that blood poisonous or something like that?"
"Nothing like that. It's just plain harassment... but if one does that and the smell sticks, other pouch-kangaroos come in a pack and do the same thing. You end up drenched in blood."
"Scary!"

Being unfathomable Witchbeasts as they were, hearing about their ecology alone was enough to make one wince more than once. After discussing the Witchbeasts in general terms to some degree that way, Subaru chose that topic at last, with resolve.

"Alright, next up is the Witchbeast I saw at a distance out on the dunes... do you know of a Witchbeast with a horse's body, a human torso attached, that breathes fire from its back?"

"――――"

Amid the flow of discussing Witchbeast ecology up to this point, Subaru revealed the matter of the centaur.
It was such a distinctive Witchbeast. Even supposing it was something specific to this dune, an existing Witchbeast turned abnormally savage, if there was an original form, it might serve as a breakthrough.
If only it had some clear weakness like being weak to water, being made of fire — that would be perfect, but—

"...Sorry, but I've got no idea. Ram-san?"

"Ram is likewise sorry. Though it's a Witchbeast that only breeds revulsion the more one hears of it."

"You don't know, huh..."

But what came back from the two of them was that kind of answer.
Apparently, they had no idea of a generic body distinct from the centaur or its abnormal individual. Still, the one Witchbeast they were certain to encounter was precisely that one. He didn't want to give up exploring for a breakthrough on the strength of just that answer.

"You really don't know? Like, the head part is where Patrasche's head would be, replaced by a human torso, complete with two arms properly attached. And the torso splits vertically down the chest to the belly, and that's the mouth. And then, above the human torso's neck, instead of a head, horns grow..."

"Ehh... what is that? Super gross..."

"Honestly, that put me off."

The more thoroughly he explained, the more the women's favorability dropped. Somehow even Patrasche, used as the point of comparison, looked displeased, and Subaru's shoulders slumped.
It seemed they were genuinely at zero leads.

"To begin with, if you saw such a Witchbeast, you should have reported it right then. Why did you let something that dangerous-looking slide?"

"No, well, that's... it was at night. Maybe because of Meili's Divine Protection, it never came closer, so I only saw it from a distance. It was just about when you were being treated by Emilia-tan inside the dragon carriage."

"Lewd."

"Aren't you just saying that because you want to say it at this point?"

Deflecting with a flimsy excuse, Subaru felt let down that they had nothing to go on.
As for countermeasures against the centaur, that meant there was no advance move to be made. All that was left was for Subaru to warn them of the danger beforehand while searching for ways to avoid encountering the real thing.

"Anyway, I'll just call it the centaur for now... but I think it's probably a pretty seriously dangerous one. A mane grows from the human torso's back, and it was burning like fire. Its expression looked pretty vindictive... though I can't really say since it has no face, but it gave off that vibe, so worst case, there's a chance it's chasing after us."

"Why would you leave something like that alone, and it just keeps getting worse? Do you want to die?"

"Yeah, I think even I feel there was a logical leap in my own explanation just now."

There was no denying his logic had gone somewhat overboard in his eagerness to impress the danger upon them.
Still, having conveyed at least this much, it should let them hold a proper sense of crisis about the crucial matter of a centaur encounter.

And besides, that. Countermeasures against the centaur weren't only about what to do if they actually ran into it.
Subaru was also thinking of a more fundamental method.
And that was—

"Natsuki-kun, Ram-san. —Let's pause the chatting for a bit, shall we?"

Anastasia said so and signaled Patrasche to stop. The clever Earth Dragon had already halted her feet before being told, and now, as if picking up on her intent, lowered her head.
Then, as Anastasia held out the cantera she gripped, out in front—

"A fork in the road."

—They stood before the first step of a plan meant to avoid an encounter.

Persuading the two of them and getting them to advance down the right path — that was Subaru's job to do.
An opportunity gained even through "Return by Death" — he couldn't let the two of them walk needlessly into "death."

"—! Saying something unnecessary...!"

"C'mon, leave this to me, yeah?"

It was a remark from Anastasia that, while considerate of Ram's feelings, was at the same time lacking in consideration. He could hear Ram grinding her teeth at having her hidden inner state exposed, but Anastasia paid it no mind.
She raised a small hand and pointed ahead so Subaru could see.

"Right now, it's better if neither of you talks to the other. When your heart's on edge, no conversation with anyone about anything turns out well. Right?"

"――――"

"Right?"

Galling as it was, Anastasia — Eridna's — reasoning had a point to it.
In his eagerness to prioritize pressing forward and getting safely through the cavity, this time around Subaru had indeed failed to be considerate enough of Ram's feelings. The bill for that had come due, and perhaps it had surfaced here in Ram's attitude.
Thinking of it that way, it would be hasty to assume the problem lay only with Ram.

"...Got it. That's true. My bad."

"Reflect on it."

"—! You know!"

"Now, now, no fighting. C'mon, Natsuki-kun, let's move, let's move."

At Ram's attitude, showing not the slightest sign of conceding, Subaru's nerves frayed. But Anastasia, stepping in between, managed to head it off somehow, showing Subaru the path forward.
She jostled the cantera at Patrasche's neck with her foot, and seeing the shadows of the sandy passage sway within his field of view, Subaru clicked his tongue.

Her attitude was hard to overlook, but there was no point squabbling among themselves here.
Fortunately, he could excuse it as this unpleasant atmosphere acting on their mood swings. As long as he could blame something else, there was no need to sour the relationship over something trivial.
The current bad mood could be talked through once they got out of here safely.

"...Let's go."

Once again, the search led by Subaru resumed.
Still, the pace of advance remained sluggish, refusing to pick up. In the end, the attempt to enter a mentally upward state and shed the physical burden had failed.
Since nothing had improved, there was of course no prospect of the situation getting better either.

Even so, if there was a change, there was a change.
Not on Subaru and the others' end, but in the sand labyrinth itself.

"The path's clearly gotten narrower, no matter how you look at it."

They'd advanced down the right path, and it had probably been close to another hour again.
The sandy passage's spacing, up, down, left, and right, had all been narrowing, and its height and width were reaching a point where it would be impossible for a massive Witchbeast to pass through.
There was barely enough space for Patrasche to get through. But there wasn't enough width to pass the dragon carriage Giant had been pulling, and a sand earthworm or a centaur surely couldn't pass through either.

In other words, at the very least, it seemed they'd be spared the worst-case scenario of a centaur waiting for them further down this path. Compared to that thing, whatever waited ahead, at least they could avoid things tipping in a worse direction.

"But if the path's getting narrower, we probably need to be even more careful. We won't be able to move quickly if something happens suddenly, so stay alert, both of you."

"...!"

"—Hey."

Thinking ahead, Subaru, having said that, raised an eyebrow at a sudden sound. He turned back and looked at the two atop the dragon, illuminated by the cantera.
There was no voice responding to Subaru's call. All he could tell was that Anastasia, riding double in front, covered her face with her palm.

Naturally so, for Anastasia, who wanted to somehow keep things peaceful and press on.
After all, what Ram had just done was click her tongue at Subaru's words.

"You've been at this for a while now — what exactly is your deal?"

"Nothing in particular."

"It's not 'nothing'! I'm asking what you mean by it!"

Unable to hold back, Subaru raised his voice and kicked the sand wall right beside him. The fragile wall's surface crumbled and fell away, but Subaru paid no mind whatsoever to the scattering sand.
Right now, all Subaru could see was an insolent Ram.

"When a person's walking along quietly, I can hear it — tsk, tsk, tsk, tsk! And that wasn't the first click of your tongue either, was it? Hey, come on, what's your deal!"

"There's no deal whatsoever. Ram has nothing to say to Barusu."

"You click your tongue tsk-tsk-tsk-tsk year-round with nothing behind it? You don't, do you? If you're not doing it and yet you are, then there's gotta be a reason you're doing it!"

Contrary to Ram's cold demeanor, Subaru's heat had only been rising since a while ago.
Naturally so. Against Subaru's efforts up to this point, what Ram had shown was far too cold, and far too one-sided.
Subaru had no reason to be criticized, to be looked down upon.

"If you've got something to say, say it! I'll listen, so go ahead and say it!"

"—So I said, there's nothing."

"You're lying! Are you stupid? If you're gonna hide it, hide it completely! Show a little of it and act all smug about it and then say there's nothing? That's the thinking of a total idiot, moron!"

"――――"

Spitting as he cursed crudely, the atmosphere around Ram changed.
Clinging to the back of Anastasia, she tilted her body and glared at Subaru. That positioning irritated him too — hiding behind someone else while looking down on Subaru from above.
Was she thinking herself important, borrowing someone else's strength?

"Barusu seems quite eager to press forward, doesn't he?"

"Of course I am! What do you think we came here for? To meet the Sage, right? What's wrong with going through all this trouble and pushing forward for that?"

"Wrong. —We didn't come here to meet the Sage."

"Huh?"

"The reason Ram and the others came here is to restore Rem back to how she was."

Clearly, staring straight at Subaru, Ram declared it. Pressured by the sharpness and pressure of her gaze, Subaru's boiling thoughts weakened slightly.
Saving Rem = meeting the Sage — wasn't that the same thing?

"That's the same thing! Meet the Sage, save Rem! It's one and the same!"

"It's not the same. Saving Rem comes first, and meeting the Sage comes after. The order of priority is different. ...Yes, the order of priority is different."

At the end of those words, Ram's voice trembled. The intense emotion packed into it was a blazing fury exceeding anything from her emotionless self up until now, combined.
In the cold underground of the sand dunes, Ram continued speaking, her words still carrying the heat of that fury.

"Ram came here for Rem's sake, to bring back her sister's memory. And yet, what are you doing right now? With Rem not even here, dawdling around in a place like this... don't be ridiculous."

"Nobody's being ridiculous...! Since it's come to this, now that it's come to this, I'm doing what can only be done when it's come to this! Am I wrong?"

"Yes, maybe that's true. But before confirming that it's 'come to this' — has Barusu even once worried about Rem?"

"...Ha?"

"Waking up underground, did Barusu worry about Rem? What about Emilia-sama? What about Beatrice-sama? Did you properly worry about the people you couldn't find?"

Hit with a barrage of words, Subaru fell silent, unable to say anything.
Indeed, this time, having woken up prompted by "Return by Death," Subaru hadn't panicked and tried to confirm everyone's safety the way he had the previous time.
But that wasn't at all because he wasn't worried — it was because he knew Ram and the others wouldn't know that anyway. It was Subaru's own kind of consideration. His own kind of concern.
And yet,

"No, you didn't. Barusu didn't. Barusu doesn't care about Rem at all. Your head's full of Emilia-sama and Beatrice-sama. That's fine, I suppose — you're just that kind of man, that's all. But Rem is pitiful."

"...Shut up."

"Wasn't Rem the one who believed in Barusu? Ah, well, that's just a convenient story I've only heard from Barusu, so who knows. Maybe you're just saying whatever's convenient. Always saying whatever's convenient in front of women. Emilia-sama and Beatrice-sama are pitiful too. Deceived by a man like this!"

"Shut up!"

"No, I won't shut up. I'll say it as many times as it takes. —Barusu doesn't care at all about Rem. Thinking, wouldn't it be a relief if she was never found at all."

"—Don't, screw around with me!!"

His vision dyed red, his brain nerves catching fire at the careless words hurled at him.
Looking down from on high, at the arrogant woman flinging selfish words at him, his rage exploded.
Grabbing her, dragging her down — even that much time felt maddening.

"Invisible Providence——!!"

"—Gu, ah!?"

The pitch-black feeling coursing through his head, Subaru sent it straight down into his chest and released it.
The black palm that came into being cried out in triumph, and slithering, stretched itself up onto the Earth Dragon's back, and from there hurled down the pink-haired girl who dared accuse him.

Screaming, Ram tumbled roughly onto the sand.
Against the violence of an invisible demonic hand, Ram's face showed she had no idea what had just happened. Subaru had rushed right up beside Ram, lying on that sand.
And then,

"Don't screw around."

That he didn't care about Rem at all — no way, don't joke with me.
In his overheated thinking, as if fury had made everything come apart at the seams,

"—Gh"


—Subaru was pressing down atop the supine Ram, strangling her slender neck.



Creak, creak.

Creak, creak, creak.
