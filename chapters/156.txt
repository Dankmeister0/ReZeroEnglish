Arc 3 Chapter 79: "The Self-Proclaimed Knight and the Finest Knight"

Subaru thrust out his right hand, middle finger raised, and slammed his declaration of war home together with his fist.

Knocked down by the blow, Petelgeuse wore a dumbfounded expression, unable even to wipe away the blood trickling from the corner of his split mouth, his lips trembling violently.

"Just what— do you understand what you are DOING!? I am a Sin Archbishop, an Archbishop graced with the Witch's Blessing! For you, who receives that same favor, to strike me down like this...!"

"Unforgivable, is that it? Sorry, but I'm sick of hearing that line. I've got no interest in debating you and whatever it is you believe in anymore. The Witch can go to hell, Petelgeuse."

Spitting the words at the madman, whose face had gone rigid with fury, Subaru gave a light, taunting beckon with his hand. Something about the gesture seemed to rekindle Petelgeuse's fury, and he beat the ground as he rose to his feet, his bloodshot eyes bulging as if they might pop from their sockets.

"Very well, IS! I understand, IS! If you—if you claim to have forgotten the Witch's favor, her Blessing, if you insist on playing ignorant, then I shall use the love bestowed upon me to make you atone for such disrespect, IS! I don't understand, don't understand, don't understand, don't understand, don't, don't, don't, don't, don't, don'don'don'don't-t-t-t-t-t-t-t! Why! Why do you reject love! I cannot fathom your true intent!"

*Whether the world's gone mad, or I have. When you're driven into an unreasonable corner, when nothing goes the way you want, it's easy to fall into that dead-end kind of thinking when you hit a wall of thought...*

Half-listening to Petelgeuse's spittle-flecked ravings, Subaru recalled the calamity that had befallen him, and the tangled state of his own mind while he'd been caught in the middle of it. That time he'd told himself he was right, loudly insisted everyone else was wrong, and then simply thrown a childish tantrum, screaming and raging.

How different was that, really, from the behavior of the madman standing before him now?

"Well, I've always thought it's a question without an easy answer, but I can say this much."

"――――"

"You're the one who's crazy. I'm right. —This ends here, Petelgeuse!"

At Subaru's words, Petelgeuse's eyes trembled with a dark delight—the murky joy of a man drunk on his own diligence, meting out punishment to the fool who dared treat his faith so carelessly. At the same instant, Petelgeuse's shadow exploded, countless demonic hands surging up from underfoot straight at Subaru, meaning to tear off his limbs, wrench off his head, and bathe in his blood.

But—

"Why—!?"

"Come and get me—though I'm not exactly relaxed enough to be playing games!"

Twisting his body, leaping wide left and right, Subaru dodged the closing palms by a hair's breadth. The number of reaching arms stopped at seven—not because Petelgeuse was going easy on him, but because he still hadn't grasped how many "arms" had returned to his own hands. Either way, with a manageable number, slow speed, and no element of surprise, dodging the opening strikes wasn't especially difficult.

Of course, if he were simply retracing the pattern he'd followed until now, there'd be no need to risk closing this much distance. He could lure Petelgeuse into a frenzy from far away and calmly dodge the barrage of Unseen Hands at his leisure. But this time, Subaru chose not to do that. Knowing full well the danger, he chose to face the Unseen Hands at point-blank range with Petelgeuse—grazing past hands that could strip the flesh from his bones with a single touch, all to wrench victory from this moment.

"Impossible, this cannot be... Why! You, of my! The favor bestowed upon me! The Authority of Sloth, the Unseen Hand—can you SEE it!?"

"Why don't you ask the Witch who apparently plastered my body with her scent. Oh, wait—unlike me, you don't exactly have a permit for free visitation with the Witch, do you?"

"What do you mean by that...! You speak as though you've—as though you've exchanged words with the Witch! With Satella...!"

"She's got me wrapped tight around her finger. Literally."

His face flushing crimson with rage, Petelgeuse crushed his own fingertip between his teeth to bear the unbearable fury—a wet crunch of nail, bone, and flesh, and the dull sound of teeth threatening to snap at the root.

At the same moment, the volume of shadow gushing forth in response to that emotion began to swell, the difficulty of evasion spiking, and sweat beaded on Subaru's brow at the reality of it. But that fact also brought Petelgeuse face to face with a truth of his own.

"The 'Unseen Hand' has returned!? Why, IS!? My fingertips... what has become of my fingertips!!"

Even though his own arsenal had just increased, Petelgeuse looked shaken, as if confronted with an unacceptable fact. Of course he would be. For the number of Petelgeuse's Unseen Hands to increase meant—

"Just as I figured. The 'Unseen Hand' he had scattered among his underlings—his fingertips—for controlling them or whatever, lost its targets and came back to the main body."

Subaru had confirmed, from felling Petelgeuse and possessing another body, that the number of Unseen Hands the madman could wield tended to increase. And that this number tended to rise the more of his fingertips—his subordinates' hiding places—had been crushed in surprise attacks beforehand.

From that, Subaru had drawn his hypothesis in advance: if he didn't destroy the possession target first, Petelgeuse would simply revive by hopping to a new body. But if he destroyed the possession targets, the Unseen Hands would multiply and his combat strength would grow—a maddening, tiresome catch he'd come to loathe about this man.

And it seemed his hypothesis had indeed been correct—the madman was thrown into confusion by the fact that the number of arms he could use had grown with the loss of his fingertips. Subaru clenched his fist at the rightness of his own reasoning, but—

"Which means the part that actually matters—surviving—just got a whole lot harder—!"

Still not fully recovered from his confusion, Petelgeuse wasn't extending the largest number of demonic hands Subaru had ever witnessed—over a hundred. Still, even so, the number of jet-black palms closing in exceeded twenty, already well beyond Subaru's capacity to handle.

He crouched, slid, spun into a roundoff and leapt hard with a twist. Bad footing sent him tumbling onto his backside, which—by dumb luck—turned into the fine play that let a hand graze just over his head in a miraculous dodge. Pain shot through his tailbone like it might crack, and Subaru, half in tears, rose to put distance between himself and Petelgeuse.

For now, no wounds from the Unseen Hands. A skinned knee from the fall, and tailbone pain that was anything but normal—might even be cracked.

"C'mon, c'mon, pathetic, can't even reach me! Baring your incompetence to fish for sympathy—aren't you ashamed, getting all that favor and blessing and whatnot? Ohhh nooo, you're SO slothful, aren't you!?"

"—There are several points that concern Me."

Even as Subaru tap-danced tauntingly atop the rocky ground, throwing out jabs, Petelgeuse's earlier fury drained away, and he murmured quietly instead. He raised a finger, bloodied and mangled, waving it as if to display it deliberately.

"That I lost my fingertips, IS unavoidable. My diligence fell short, my thinking failed to reach far enough, I was slothful! That much I admit, IS, I cannot help but admit it. IS, IS, IS, IS, but! There is too much I cannot understand no matter how I think, IS! That you can see the Unseen Hand, the skill by which you located my fingertips, and that the one who presented herself before Me was you, one with no direct combat power at all!"

"――――"

"Therefore, I shall think thus, IS. There is no point thinking about what cannot be understood by thinking, IS. Therefore, being not slothful, I shall need to think only of matters for which there is reason, and result to be gained. —Why did you show yourself before Me, drawing My eye with provocative words and deeds? That is—"

*This is bad*, Subaru instinctively read Petelgeuse's line of thought and bit his lip.

He'd figured that if he kept taunting, kept hurling provocations, he could keep Petelgeuse's attention fixed only on what was directly in front of him, and thereby keep his own intentions hidden. He'd been naive. Circumstances had swung too far in Subaru's favor, and the accumulation of setbacks had, in turn, given Petelgeuse the very trigger he needed to step back and calmly reassess the situation.

If Petelgeuse correctly grasped the current state of affairs and arrived at the perfectly reasonable solution, Subaru was finished. And, Subaru's hopes notwithstanding, Petelgeuse arrived at it.

"If the 'Unseen Hand' is visible, and dealing with that takes time—then I shall mobilize My fingertips in full, and settle this with the violence of numbers, IS!"

"He figured it out, just like that—!"

Simply put, the maximum force Subaru alone could draw off was Petelgeuse alone—and even that in an incomplete form, one that would fail the moment the Unseen Hands multiplied beyond count. Right now, thanks to the time Subaru had bought with performances that made his own back itch with discomfort, the subjugation squad, operating as a separate unit, had wiped out Petelgeuse's fingertips one by one. Counting the merchant's and his hidden card's fingertip as well, nine locations were confirmed destroyed.

However, the final location hadn't been touched yet—and as for where that last one was, it was right here.

Directly beneath the cliff face at Subaru's back stood a boulder taller than Subaru himself. Behind it lay the entrance to a cave, and beyond that, the last fingertip remained. If Witch Cultists came swarming out of there, Subaru's forces alone would be checkmated on the spot. Which meant Petelgeuse's flash of insight just now was, for Subaru, tantamount to a death sentence—

"Wa—!"
"Ha————!"

Except that too was a matter of mere minutes' difference.

"What IS this!?"

A resonance wave churned the forest air violently, becoming a raging torrent of destruction that peeled back the rocky ground, raging wild with the center of the cliff face as its final stage.

The cliff section directly above the great boulder tore away whole, the collapse swallowing the cave, opening a great pit, and catching part of Subaru—who'd been right nearby—in the process.

"Whoawhoawhoawhoawhoawhoawhoa clooose—!!"

Grazed on the back of the head by a rock mass that would have caved in his skull had it connected, Subaru crawled out of the sandstorm looking like he'd barely escaped with his life. Pointing at Subaru's undignified state, clutching her stomach, laughing in a high-pitched voice, was—

"Ahahaha—! Big Bro's running away like a bug! Hey, Tivey, look, look!"

"I'm looking, yes, and I think the timing there was just bad. Any other timing, and Big Bro would've been beaten to a pulp, I think."

The little brother scolded his sister, who laughed innocently, having just triggered destruction that nearly swallowed Subaru whole. Subaru rushed over to the beastkin siblings joining the fray, feelings split evenly between "thank god you made it" relief and "I almost actually died" resentment.

"Both of you, glad you finally showed up, you jerks."

"Ohh-ho, Big Bro's got it backwards, doesn't he? This is a thank-yoou! You're welcome! Yaaay!"

"I think that was actually the right thing to say, since it sounded like you wanted to say thanks but also didn't want to. But—"

Tivey narrowed his eyes, his cat-like pupils sharpening as he glared at Petelgeuse. In the direction of his gaze, Petelgeuse gazed absently at the collapsed cliff, his expression as blank as a Noh mask.

His stance, his manner, his appearance—none of it gave off much sense of martial threat. In a contest of strength, a footrace, a fistfight, in any of those, even Subaru could win. Tivey and Mimi, siblings who made their living fighting, all the more so. But along some vector separate from that obvious kind of threat, Petelgeuse's dreadfulness existed. Whether he sensed it or not, Subaru didn't miss the faint flicker of something like fear cross Tivey's expression.

"That's a Sin Archbishop, is it. ...Just looking at him gives me an awful feeling, like it's going to unsettle my mind."

"'When you gaze into an abyss, the abyss also gazes into you'—so said the ancients. Better not to look at him too directly. Recommend viewing from the corner of your eye, or reflected in a mirror, indirectly. And it's best not to get close either."

"Whyyy?"

"His breath reeks something awful, physiologically unbearable. Stings the eyes or something."

Soothing Tivey, who seemed about to be swallowed by the atmosphere, with a bit of banter, Subaru glanced over at Mimi, who was laughing without a care. But she just tilted her head cutely, looking puzzled, apparently feeling no threat whatsoever, genuinely. Impressive.

"Anyway, more than anything, I'm glad you two joined up. Killed the reinforcements, and honestly, I was a hair's breadth from getting done in."

Thinking that if their arrival, this current wave of attacks, had come even slightly later, Subaru's chances would have been snuffed out right then—it really had been a hair's-breadth entrance into the fray. Voicing his gratitude for the narrow escape, Subaru glanced sidelong at the two of them and turned his eyes to the forest.

"So, what about the one who really matters. Still no sign of him joining up?"

"Yuri should be here any minute now, yes. Me and Sis arriving first is basically a rounding error."

"Who's Yuri? Not Julius? If it's Julius, he'll probably be here in a sec. I can smell him coming."

Casually wasting her brother's concern for Julius, the sister pointed into the forest and sniffed. Just how far he could trust her sense of smell, being basically a cat in appearance, was a mystery, but if their reunion was near, so much the better. It was maddeningly frustrating to admit, but without Julius's cooperation, bringing down Petelgeuse could never be accomplished.

"—Something is off, still, IS."

Petelgeuse, who had sunk into silent thought until now, turned his head in a slow circle. His bulging eyes settled on Subaru and widened, and, tongue lolling, drool dripping, he spoke.

"That attack just now—there is no way it wasn't aware of the cave's location, hidden as it was by a visual-discrepancy formula. It also somehow saw through the presence of the fingertips lying in wait in the forest. And it possesses eyes that see through the existence of My 'Unseen Hand' as well. Given that My fingertips were destroyed one after another beforehand, it seems that other matters, too, are known."

Pulling the Gospel from his robes once more, Petelgeuse ran his eyes over the pages. Again and again, reaching the last page only to return to the first, repeating this over and over—and still, as if unable to find the answer he sought, his mouth began to twist.

"The Gospel! My guidepost of fate! Records nothing of this! Then what, WHAT is your existence! One who receives the favor, and yet holds the Witch in contempt! Standing in the way of Me as I conduct the Trials, blocking every scheme I lay...!"

Stamping his feet against the ground, Petelgeuse expressed his unbearable irritation through action. From Petelgeuse's perspective, Subaru's behavior must have seemed utterly incomprehensible. His planned assault, meant to be carried out under the guise of the Trials, had been preempted, the villagers and Emilia allowed to escape; on top of that, his subordinates had been wiped out by surprise attack, his own Authority and even his trump cards exposed—and his opponent, no less, received the very Witch's favor that was his own foundation.

For Petelgeuse, what could this be, if not a nightmare?

Looking back over the sum of his own actions, Subaru almost felt a laugh well up at how thoroughly, almost obsessively, his strategy of crushing Petelgeuse had succeeded. And yet—even having done all this, the man's depths still remained unfathomable. In this loop originating from the royal capital, Subaru had already met death five times.

Five times, Subaru had failed to save anyone, and let the world end. Only by having passed through that much despair, that much experience, could he finally have arrived at this point. What did he care for a madman's nightmare? He had watched nightmares, literally, until it nearly killed him. So now, what stood before him—this, finally, was the reality Subaru had longed for.

"After all, after all, after all, after allll, you are of 'Pride'—"

"My name is Natsuki Subaru."

Cutting off the voice that ground its teeth, about to name Subaru "Pride," Subaru thrust out a finger. And, to the sputtering madman, he declared:

"Silver-haired half-elf Emilia's knight. 'Pride' or whatever, I don't know or care, but that's the only title I want. I don't need anything else."

To make those words real, he had kept running all this while. At Subaru's words, Mimi let out an admiring "whoaaa," and Tivey closed his eyes, dipping his chin slightly. And Petelgeuse, receiving that head-on—

"That will do, IS."

—he said, breathing out with a somehow listless air, shaking his head side to side. Shaking, shaking, shaking, shaking it wildly, shaking it loose, tossing his face up toward the sky.

"Refusing to repay love! Regret this sloth of yours, that fears not even the Witch, IS! What sloth! What sloth! What what whatwhatwhat't't't't't't't... sloooth!!"

Spreading both arms wide, mouth agape in a scream, Petelgeuse's shadow swelled enormously. The number of jet-black demonic hands gushing forth reached the maximum Subaru had ever witnessed—easily surpassing a hundred, stretching skyward, and then the shadow scattered in every direction.

"You've gotta be kidding me..."

The black palms' raw strength snapped trees like twigs, hoisting up felled logs cracked halfway through with ease. From the site of the cliff's collapse, several rock masses the size of Subaru's head were lifted up. Still the empty-handed palms wandered the forest seeking prey, returning skyward bearing vicious objects in their grip. The number, the weight, the lethality of it all—just looking at it, you could die a hundred times over.

"If you can see the Unseen Hand, then how about pure violence, IS! Can you dodge violence approaching a hundredfold, the diligent destruction My Authority displays, all of it!? Come, come, come, come come come come come comeeeee! Are you 'Sloth,' you!?"

Without even the time to call out a warning, that violence was hurled at the three standing frozen. Rocks and fallen trees showering down all at once—a barrage that filled the entire field of view—everyone flinched, shrinking as if pinned in place.

"Wa—!"
"Ha————!"

The instant their prey came into their sights, the siblings' cries met that destruction head-on. A resonance wave shook the air, and the numerous obstacles being hurled cracked apart, scattered, and burst into fragments. Even so, not everything could be broken through, but—

"Ohh!?"

"Don't just stand there!"
"You'll die!"

Pulled backward from both sides simultaneously, Subaru's body was hoisted up and dragged back. Mere instants later, rock masses that hadn't been stopped slammed one after another into the spot where Subaru had just been standing, the impact traveling through the ground.

Beyond the billowing smoke, Petelgeuse's laughter rang out in a cackling peal. And, as if to encourage that laughter, above the smoke another arm was already readying another mass projectile.

"The strategic objective here has already been achieved! Fingertips wiped out, and the last location buried splendidly alive...! Which means—"

"Which means what?"
"What do we do?"

"—Retreat! Retreeeat!!"

At the same moment as Subaru's pathetic cry rang out, the throw was carried out once more. Feeling death's presence at their backs, Subaru and the siblings leapt back and away from the spot. Kicking off the uneven ground, they plunged into the forest to secure cover, and, weathering a stormlike gust of wind, tore through the forest at a dead run given the dire situation.

"Bad, bad, this is bad, this is bad! Damn it, unexpected! That arm can do more than just rip apart bodies!? Where the hell's Julius! Patra——sche!!"

Trampling through the underbrush, Subaru, at the height of confusion, let his mouth run loose. Running alongside him, Mimi and Tivey kept pace, casting wary glances behind them. Unlike the two of them, accustomed to running, Subaru had to watch his own footing or risk falling at any moment. Why was it always like this—that he could never once calmly stroll through a forest? It always felt like he was running desperately, for his life.

"Whoaaa!? What is THAT!?"

Beside Subaru, who was venting his grievances at full volume, Mimi let out a voice tinged with unease. Drawn by her voice, Subaru glanced back—and immediately, genuinely regretted having looked.

Of course Petelgeuse wasn't about to let the fleeing Subaru and company go. Chasing after the three of them as they fled at full speed, he was—but his method of movement was unbelievable.

Knees drawn up, curled into a ball, seated as if in a child's crouch, Petelgeuse had his own rounded body gripped by his 'Unseen Hands,' propelling himself by stretching countless demonic hands out like a spider's legs. The black palms' destructive force remained undiminished—every obstacle in his path was torn out root and all, blasted apart. Coming on dead straight, tearing the forest apart, the malevolent embodiment of 'Sloth' pursued.

"He's floating in the air, but I've never seen such a gross way of floating!"

"Ah, right! That's how it looks to you two!"

The scenery that Subaru, who could see the 'Unseen Hand,' witnessed, and what Mimi and the others—who couldn't—saw, were entirely different sights. To Mimi and the others it must be the nightmarish sight of Petelgeuse flying while seated cross-legged; to Subaru it was a nightmare beyond a nightmare, of countless arms carrying himself along—though it was really just a question of which of two worsts was worse.

And then, the situation grew even worse.

"—Ahhh! Be 'Sloth'!!"

"Not go—!"

Right after the roar from behind, black light scorched the world in an instant. That which blanketed his vision for a fleeting moment had no effect on Subaru whatsoever. But for the two running alongside him—

"Ah?"
"...gh!"

Strength drained from their bodies, and just as the siblings were about to collapse, by sheer luck an outstretched arm caught them both at once. Tucking their small frames under each arm, Subaru ran through the forest carrying the two of them, dazed and half-conscious.

It was the manifestation of Petelgeuse's vicious ultimate technique, 'Spiritual Contamination'—one of Petelgeuse's Authorities, which stripped the will to fight from everyone save Subaru, who had resistance, and a handful of other subjugation members.

Subaru had told Mimi and Tivey plainly about this Spiritual Contamination. That they likely couldn't resist it, and that if hit by it, they'd suffer the loss of their mental equilibrium. But even after hearing that, the two of them had still come to Subaru's aid. Knowing that facing him head-on would sap their will to fight, they had still come to help him.

So—

"Wouldn't it be better to abandon your dead weight and flee, IS!? Those foolish sluggards, who shall sink before the Authority of 'Sloth'! Why do you protect them so desperately!?"

"Shut your yapping, you idiot! I'm a Fluffist! What kind of Fluffist abandons his fluffy kids and expects tomorrow's meal to taste good, huh!?"

There was no way he could abandon them. Still carrying the two of them, Subaru's legs only accelerated further. Petelgeuse, closing in from behind, seemed startled by Subaru's hidden reserves of strength, but the distance between them kept shrinking all the same. Between Subaru, carrying two children, desperately running the forest's rough terrain with feet unaccustomed to it, and Petelgeuse, tearing through rough ground and obstacles alike without a care, the two were on entirely different footing from the very premise of a chase. He couldn't escape for more than a few dozen more seconds.

That hopeless situation—

"I love you, Patrasche—!!"

Leaping over a hollow that had opened in the ground, bursting through the trees, Subaru caught sight of jet-black scales ahead. His partner, who had come running all this way in answer to his call, lowering her sleek, velvety back. Subaru literally flung himself onto that back, hugging his whole body around Patrasche as if to pin the siblings in place, and—

"GO!!"

"—————!!"

With a whinny, Patrasche lowered her posture and, with one sharp stride, instantly rode the wind.

The forest's rough terrain, obstacles of every kind strewn about—Patrasche trampled and broke through all of it with powerful strides. Underfoot wasn't even a game trail, just pure, untouched green wilderness. If an Earth Dragon's existence was analogous to a horse from Subaru's original world, then for Patrasche, trained to run open plains, tearing through the forest's rough ground like this was an outrage that should never happen—let alone without even the leisure for a slow walk, at an all-out sprint.

"You're the best—!!"

Even his earlier call hadn't been shouted with genuine belief that Patrasche would truly come to save him. It had been nonsense that tumbled out of his mouth, driven by desperation in a cornered moment. But Patrasche had answered it, entering the forest of her own judgment to save Subaru. Since the battle with the White Whale, how many times had this Earth Dragon saved him now?

"Everyone, everyone... this time, absolutely, all of it...!"

Mimi, Tivey, even Patrasche—all of them had kept lending a hand to his useless self. So Subaru had to answer. He had to answer what had been given him with more than what had been given. That was the one and only answer to make the flower of trust bloom.

"How far, how far... just how many cards were you hiding, IS!? If even this, even this too was known in advance, then just what are yo—!"

*—That much, I hadn't foreseen.*

Answering Petelgeuse in his mind, Subaru reached for the reins and gave Patrasche a signal. Patrasche picked up on the clumsy instruction keenly, tilting her body slightly and curving in the direction Subaru wanted. Little by little, little by little, so gradually that Petelgeuse chasing from behind wouldn't notice—yet surely—she turned back around.

Leaping over the hollow, slipping through gaps in the trees, bursting through the forest, mud splattering, tearing through the dim green labyrinth where sunlight was blocked out—and there, at last—

"We're back!"

"This is where we were before...!?"

Bursting back out into the open ground, it was the cliffside battlefield where they'd faced Petelgeuse just before. Returning to the site still thick with the traces of destruction, Subaru swept his eyes around the surroundings. But before he could find what he sought, Petelgeuse let out a strange cry.

"Whatever you scheme, whatever plot, whatever you're aiming for, IS not known to Me! But all of it, everything, everything, all of it! Let it be crushed here and disappear, vanish entirely, IS!!"

A demonic hand, separate from the one crawling along the ground, once again lifted a fallen tree and a rock mass, hurling that mass projectile at the group, exposed with no cover in this open space.

Sensing the threat, Patrasche increased her speed, trying to shake it off, but there was no longer any way to dodge the thing bearing down with such ferocity—raising her tail, she tried to shield her master with her own body even a little. At that instant—

"Eldona."

A wall of earth rose up from the ground, an improvised barrier catching the hurled destruction head-on. Cracking under the impact, the barrier, its purpose served, immediately crumbled back into ordinary earth. For one instant, Subaru, unable to grasp what had happened in that momentary exchange of attack and defense, blinked wide-eyed atop Patrasche's back, curled up. And then—

"I arrived at the meeting point to find no sign of you. It gave me a bit of a start."

"...That's 'cause you're too damn slow showing up. Where were you dawdling, to fall this far behind schedule?"

"Carrying the wounded back to the village took time. I turned pale on arrival, but... I'm glad, more than anything, that we've reunited."

Going limp, Subaru stroked Patrasche's flank as the tension drained out of him. At his answer, Patrasche gave her body a shake, as if to say "mind your own business"—but against the relief of the moment, such a protest was no more than a gentle breeze. In any case—

"Sorry for the delay. It seems, as planned, the situation is proceeding well."

Drawing his slender sword, striding calmly across the rocky ground, the handsome man came to stand beside Patrasche. His pale violet hair fluttering in the wind, the hem of his Royal Guard uniform trailing—Subaru couldn't count how many times that figure had made him seethe with irritation. And yet, the stance that had begun as such a hateful memory now, in this very moment, felt maddeningly reliable.

"Of the Royal Guard of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica, Julius Juukulius."

Declaring his name, Julius raised the drawn blade vertically, holding it toward Petelgeuse. At his appearance, Petelgeuse, who had stopped moving at the forest's edge, widened his eyes and waited for what would follow. A quiet breath, silence, and then six kinds of Lesser Spirits shining around Julius.

"I am the Kingdom's blade, come to cut you down."

"A Spiritual Arts User, IS it. ...How far, truly, how far, hmmmm."

More than the declaration, it was the sight of the Lesser Spirits flitting around Julius that stirred fierce rage from Petelgeuse. Watching that reaction, Subaru felt it reinforce the likelihood that the hypothesis he and Julius had shared was correct after all.

"Julius."

"At a glance, it's plain this thing is beyond all normal bounds. It appears to be floating in the air, but that is the power of this 'Unseen Hand' you spoke of, I take it?"

"Yeah. It stretches out arms like a spider and floats on 'em."

At Subaru's answer, "That's frightening," Julius said, giving a small shrug. Then he glanced briefly at the siblings Subaru held.

"Are the two of them... injured? Something seems off."

"Told you, Spiritual Contamination. Take a hit from that and it snaps your mind. ...That's why I told you two not to come. Though it saved my ass, big time."

Expressing his gratitude through spiteful words, Subaru pulled the two of them in tighter against him. In his arms, Mimi stirred, her mouth moving faintly as she tried to convey something. Leaning in gently to catch it, he heard her say:

"If you win here... you'll be so cool."

Wide-eyed, Subaru looked down at her—Mimi wore a clear smile even amid her pained breathing. The shock Subaru felt at that expression, that attitude, those words, was immeasurable. So—

"Let's do this, Julius."

"Are you certain?"

At Subaru's call, Julius spoke as if to question his resolve. Dipping his chin at the question, Subaru lit a resolve in his eyes that would never bend.

"Can't back off. Won't bend. Can't afford to lose. I don't want to lose anyone else. Not anymore."

"I am the man who beat you down, badly. I still believe, even now, that there was reason and meaning behind that act, by my own lights—but that's merely a self-righteous thing, unrelated to you."

At Subaru's answer, Julius spoke of that unforgettable incident between the two of them. The humiliation of that time, the pain, came vividly rushing back, cutting sharp into his chest.

"I'm not asking you to dig up the past, or seeking to atone for any sin. Your resolve carries weight, and your decisions and actions have shaped the path that brought you this far. Which is why I must ask. In this moment, placing my existence at your side—can you carry out your true purpose with an untroubled heart?"

"――――"

"Can you believe in me?"

Julius's question was terribly vague, out of place, naive even. But it was necessary, to make Subaru face and become conscious of the sharp thorn that had gone on asserting its existence, endlessly, in the deepest part of his heart right now.

At the Royal Selection, Subaru had exposed himself to the most humiliating failure imaginable, and rather than redeem it, had gone to the training grounds only to pile disgrace upon disgrace, everything shattered by Julius. The reason he'd been able to stand back up was thanks to the devotion of one girl who had continued supporting him at his side.

And now, having stood back up, when he pictured that old feeling in his chest—how did it look? That time, that moment, that burning passion—what color did it glow now, inside Subaru?

"—I hate you. A lot."

"Ah, I know that much already."

"Your hair's got this elegant look that pisses me off, and the way you talk is way too damn polite, comes off shady. And on top of that you're clearly looking down on me, and now that I think about it, the first time I saw you, you kissed Emilia-tan's hand, didn't you. If I try to kiss every inch of Emilia-tan's body someday, that makes it an indirect kiss with you, screw that."

Looking back, Subaru had hated Julius even before they'd first exchanged words. Given the circumstances of how coldly Emilia had once treated him, his ill will toward Julius had been there from the start. It had swelled in the Royal Selection hall, exploded at the training grounds, and the embers had gone on smoldering ever since. Even in this very moment, it burned hot, scorching Subaru's chest without end.

"Limbs broken, my head split open, even my adult teeth brushed clean out of my skull. Fine, it healed, but normally speaking that's guaranteed trauma, the kind that keeps you from ever walking outside again. Don't you know how to hold back, you bastard?"

"For what it's worth, I was holding back considerably."

"Seriously? That was you holding back. Yeah, you really are the worst kind of jerk."

Subaru, who had called himself a knight and, in doing so, had piled up shame after shame of powerlessness, ignorance, and recklessness. Julius, who had beaten that Subaru down, fulfilled his strength, his skill, his role, and shown what it meant to be a knight. He couldn't help but pity himself, cast as he was in the role of the one beaten—but setting that aside and simply looking at the figure before him, it was, precisely, the very image of a "knight" that Subaru had longed for all along.

"—I hate you, a lot, you 'Finest' knight."

"――――"

"So I'll believe in you. That you're a hell of a knight—because my own shame knows it."

More than anyone here, more than anyone in that place back then, Subaru knew Julius's blade. That's why Subaru would entrust his fate to him. Because he knew the weight of that sword, from that time.

"I'm counting on you, Julius. —I'm putting everything I've got, in your hands."

"――――"

Dismounting from Patrasche's back to the ground, Subaru faced Julius. At Subaru's answer, Julius closed his eyes, and then, a few seconds later, slowly opened them. In those twin eyes, Subaru was reflected. He drew a short breath, held it, and—

"Then I shall answer with my whole self—"

Raising his sword to the heavens, as Julius murmured, the Lesser Spirits blessed it. Circling around the blade, the vividly colored spirits danced through the air, and among them, shining especially bright, were the white and black Lesser Spirits.

The brilliance grew, intensified, and let loose a light bright enough to sear the eyes—

"—Is the farce over now, IS?"

Having crawled out of the forest, Petelgeuse cut in, seeing that exchange as its conclusion. Still floating in the air, Petelgeuse tilted his head. His bloodshot eyes wandered, and, unfocused, he pointed at Subaru and the others.

"What could one Spiritual Arts User accomplish, joining now? For a mere spirit to stand in the way of My path, My love, My loyalty, is presumptuous beyond measure! So be destroyed, vanish, and be gone, IS! IS, IS, IS, ISISISISISISISISISIISSSSS——!!"

Tongue lolling, biting into his fingers, blood dripping from his mouth, Petelgeuse screamed his death sentence and thrust out all his 'Unseen Hands' toward them at once. The surging jet-black palms, blotting out the field of vision like a tsunami, meaning to swallow and crush the group whole—at that instant—

"—Clarista."

A single flash of rainbow light cut clean through the oncoming 'Unseen Hands,' root and all.

Sparkling light danced in a wild flourish, tracing the blade's arc while showering irregular reflections of light. In the path that arc had traced, the black obsession that should have been there vanished, and the destruction that should have occurred never came.

"—Huh?"

Petelgeuse's dumbfounded voice.

In response, Julius swung his rainbow-tinged knight's sword and, prefacing with "there's no need for surprise," said:

"If it can touch this side, then this side can touch it too. If it's a power that can interfere, there is nothing this blade—bearing all six elements—cannot cut through."

Wielding the knight's sword that housed all six kinds of contracted spirits, Julius declared this as if it were nothing at all. But Petelgeuse's confusion lay elsewhere entirely. Trembling, he pointed at Julius with a mangled, blackish-red finger.

"You, you should not, IS. Should not be able to see. 'The Unseen Hand' was cut... more troubling than even that fact IS this! See, seeing, should be seeing, be, be, be, g-g-g, not, not, not IS, not, IS, not, IS."

Stammering in blank horror at the fear of his own foundation crumbling away, Petelgeuse's words failed him. At that sight, that manner, for the first time Subaru felt a kind of sympathy—and, exceeding it, the sheer pleasure of "serves you right." Genuinely, at last, he'd outmaneuvered him.

"I'm the one watching, Petelgeuse."

"—?"

"I'm the one who sees your 'Unseen Hand.' Julius is just seeing what I'm seeing. Weirder feeling than I expected, honestly."

—The true essence of the high-order communication Magic, 'Necto.'

A Magic used for transmitting intent to multiple parties across a wide area—when using it, Julius had told Subaru that "if you heighten the empathic connection too much, the boundary between self and other blurs." Which was to say, it was a synchronization of the senses—and if the connection ran deeper still, then—

"That we could deliberately share and sustain part of our senses—when you first proposed this to me, I doubted your sanity."

"Worked fine though, didn't it? A man's gotta have guts, try anything once."

Synchronized with a portion of Subaru's five senses, Julius could presumably see the scene Subaru was seeing—Petelgeuse, pinned in place by the 'Unseen Hand,' strung up like some monstrous spider. And, in turn, the surge of Mana coursing through Julius's whole body, and the warm pulse conveyed from the Lesser Spirits, was reaching Subaru as well.

"Not to complain, but I don't wanna keep this up too long, this feeling."

"Entirely agreed. Even if asked, I'd like to decline a second time."

At Subaru's words, spoken with a twisted lip, Julius smiled wryly and braced himself. With his rainbow sword before him, facing the pitiable, foolish, yet utterly unpardonable madman whose trump card—the 'Unseen Hand' itself—had just had the key to its defeat seized.

"Damn you... damn you, damn you damn you damn you damn you damn you damn you damn you damn you damn you damn you damn you damn you damn you damn you damn you damn you damn you damn you damn you damn you damn you damn you damn you damn you damn you damn youuuu!!"

Screaming, shattering his shadow into an explosion, scattering demonic hands in every direction with no aim to speak of anymore, hurling destruction wildly on pure impulse.

Before that unsightly display, Subaru resolved never to look away, drawing a deep breath and steeling himself.

"Well, sharing feelings with you is getting old too. —Let's wrap this up, quick."

"Yes, let's."

A rising slash obliterated the jet-black demonic hands raining down, and the returning stroke cleaved apart a palm closing in from the side. Watching the vanished arm dissolve into particles and scatter into shadow, Julius smiled.

"Through your eyes, I will do the cutting. —My friend, Natsuki Subaru."
