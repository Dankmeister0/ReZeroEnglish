Arc 5 Chapter 26: "The Target of Love"

—The situation had taken a major step backward, and what was already bad had gotten even worse.

Subaru shuddered at the sight of Sirius, her eyes tinted with ecstasy, breath escaping in hot gasps.
The monster paid no mind to the heated chains searing her cheeks through their bandages, and fixed Subaru with eyes burning single-mindedly with a mad light—no, not a glare. A gaze that never wavered.

"My beloved... Petelgeuse...?"

It was a madman's name Subaru had no wish to hear. Watching her claim the family name Romanée-Conti and scream out her love for her dead husband, wrapped in fury—to say the connection hadn't occurred to him would be a lie.
Still, he hadn't been able to commit to that conclusion, because he hadn't wanted to combine the existence of the madman Petelgeuse with the existence of the monster Sirius.
He hadn't wanted to believe that the two of them, linked by the Romanée-Conti name, were the worst married couple imaginable.

"Both husband and wife being Witch Cult Sin Archbishops—that's the worst possible combination. Choose your spouse better, man... though I guess if that's the result of choosing, there's nothing to be done."

Truly, honestly, the Witch Cult was made up of nothing but broken people.
Self-righteous Petelgeuse. Sirius, forcing her selfish love on others. Regulus, spouting love through some shallow theory of fate—every last one of them, garbage.

"Come to think of it, their names sound kind of similar too. They all end in '-us'... end...?"

Trying to mask his terror with a useless train of thought, Subaru caught on something in what he'd just said and cut himself off.
He felt like he'd just noticed something strange. But things were too urgent to pin down exactly what was strange about it.

"Don't go quiet on me, Petelgeuse. You really are a mean one. Always so cold to me like that—oh, oh, oh... it's so frustrating!"

However Sirius's mind had interpreted the silence of a thoughtful Subaru, she pressed a hand to her cheek and writhed, twisting at the hips. What made the scene feel nightmarish was that even the crowd gathered around Sirius had been infected by the monster's delusion, and were now displaying bizarre courtship behavior of their own, right there on the spot.

"...Good grief, I can't keep up with this. I'd guess she saw his strange little trick just now and jumped to that idea, but honestly, it's pathetic. If two people are truly fated to be joined, they'll be joined no matter what obstacles stand between them—like me and my brides. And yet here she is, unable to let go even though she and her love were parted by death before anything could bear fruit. That's not just pitiful, it's unsightly."

"Yes, yes, thank you. So sorry about that. I'm rather busy right now, you understand? Understanding matters. So does yielding to one another. If you've already accomplished your goal, why not withdraw promptly? I have business of my own to attend to."

"It's true my goal of taking my bride out of here has been accomplished, but..."

At Sirius—whose madness had come full circle to look almost rational—Regulus, having been told without words that he was in the way, glanced down at Emilia in his arms. Then he turned his gaze to Subaru.

"You, who tried to kill me and my bride, and this one who's been buzzing around getting in the way—I still haven't delivered proper retribution for the violation of my rights. Not that I'm eager to resort to violence for its own sake, mind you. It's just proper retribution. What matters is that this is a righteous act. If I back down, well, that's just me enduring it—but backing down here would mean failing to do justice to what's righteous. That, you see, is a loss for justice. And that's not a precedent I should leave behind."

"Don't—don't dress it up like that every damn time. Bottom line, you just don't like me and want to kill me. And you want to make it sound plausible, arming yourself with some shitty theory just to justify it?"

"It's a surprise, being talked back to. Especially in a way that makes it sound like I'm the one who's wrong. Or is that it—are you the type who dismisses out of hand any opinion that doesn't suit you? If so, I can only say your perspective and your capacity are both small and narrow. People who can't listen honestly to what others say tend to get their legs swept out from under them eventually, precisely because they looked down on others. What do you think?"

"Look who's talking, huh."

At Subaru's reply, Regulus's eyes widened in surprise.
Was it possible he genuinely believed his own words carried legitimacy? No—he must believe it. Otherwise he couldn't stand at the pinnacle of a cult of madmen.
The sense that a conversation was taking place was an illusion. They merely happened to use something resembling the same form of language—think of them as creatures from another world, another ecosystem entirely.
If he didn't, he'd be swallowed whole.

"Sirius. It looks like you've excluded him from your Authority's range. What's going on?"

Rather than the silent Subaru, Regulus turned the point of the conversation toward Sirius.
Furrowing his brow suspiciously at the remark, Subaru belatedly realized that the terror which had been nesting in his heart until just moments ago had vanished.
That he hadn't simply stepped outside the range of her ability was proven by the fact that Beatrice's fingers, still gripping his shoulder, were faintly trembling.
In other words, Subaru alone had been excluded from the ability's targets. The reason was—

"W-well, of course. If I knew he was That Man, and yet still kept trying to be with him, then my feelings would reach him, wouldn't they."

"Don't act bashful, it's creepy. After going on and on about how you love him, love him, love him—what's the point of playing modest now? I don't get it."

"If feelings I can't put into words—feelings I mustn't put into words—were to get through to him too, wouldn't that seem shameless? Feelings like that, I keep locked away until the very, very end—until the moment he and I become one. That's what I've decided. Yes, for the sake of 'love'!"

"Isn't it precisely because you play coy like that, that not a fragment of your precious feelings ever reaches him? You call yourself Sirius Romanée-Conti, don't you? Pining devotedly is one thing, but going and claiming the man's family name for yourself without permission—you'd best realize how creepy that is, and soon. In a way, that's an infringement on Petelgeuse's own way of life, isn't it. Well, he's dead, so I suppose it hardly matters now."

"He and I loved each other!"

At Regulus's exasperated voice, Sirius abruptly exploded.
The monster clawed at her own head, spittle flying as she shouted at Regulus.

"Because our eyes met, again and again, so many times a day! When I touched him, fearfully, timidly, he never scolded me! When I spoke to him, he answered without ever making a sour face! When I breathed in the breath he'd let out, he said nothing! He let me sleep beside him! When I wanted something of his, he wasn't angry even when I took it away! When I burned a half-demon well, he praised me! He gave me a name! He smiled at me! At me alone! At me alone, me alone, me alone, me alone!"

Breathing hard, Sirius wept as she spilled out the full measure of her feelings.
So, now it was clear.
The shape of the monster Sirius's pitch-black, pure love.

"Good grief—this is exactly why people with such strong delusions are impossible to deal with."

Shrugging, Regulus said it as if seeking Subaru's agreement.
From where Subaru stood, he wanted nothing more than to snap back that of all people, Regulus was the last one who got to say that—but the two of them were evenly matched, both in being impossible to handle and in how badly he wanted nothing to do with either.
Standing before two Sin Archbishops, the situation remained one with no glimmer of light on how to break through it.

"No, if anything, things had gotten worse now that Sirius's interest had shifted this way."

Subaru alone being freed from the shared fear didn't mean their fighting strength had shot up dramatically. If only Beatrice's handicap had been the one lifted—but she remained governed by fear, her judgment lacking its usual sharpness.
If only their opponent were Regulus alone—.

"...Hey, Sirius."

"Yes, what is it, darling?"

Sirius responded to Subaru's call readily, with a soft, gentle demeanor.
Surprised inwardly by how startlingly normal the response was, Subaru pressed on.

"I've got some business with Regulus over there. So could you just stay quiet for a while? Please."

"'Could you just stay quiet for a while,' is it."

Subaru's last-ditch gambit was to exploit Sirius's misunderstanding.
It seemed Sirius had mistaken him for Petelgeuse or something of the sort, because he'd used Invisible Providence, a power resembling the "Unseen Hand" that Petelgeuse wielded. An outrageous case of mistaken identity, but for now, he'd use it.

In fact, Petelgeuse had been a spirit that possessed others. If he were still alive, the idea that he might have possessed Subaru's body was, in fact, entirely plausible.
The problem was that it left everything that had happened so far unexplained, but Subaru doubted Sirius, in her current state, would care about such details.
That was the thinking behind Subaru's brute-force gambit to break the deadlock. The result—

"I'm sorry, but no. Even for you, my precious darling, I have to refuse that."

Mercilessly, Sirius rejected his plea.
So that convenient a request wasn't going to fly, Subaru grimaced—and Sirius lowered her head.

"I really do want to do as you say, you know. But you, ever the hard worker, would surely slip right through my outstretched fingers and vanish while I stood there waiting. I know that much. We've been together a long time, after all. I know how you're always, always so earnest, working yourself too hard alone just to produce results. ...But."

It's all in how you phrase it, Subaru thought, genuinely impressed.
To interpret Petelgeuse's so-called diligence that way was, he had to admit, a rather blind way of looking at things. A hard-working spirit that would be welcomed in an ordinary company man amounted, in a professional Sin Archbishop, to nothing but malice inflicted on the world.

"Because we've finally, finally been reunited. A year—a whole year, do you understand? Not once in a year had I ever been apart from you. Not once in a hundred years! And yet you and I couldn't meet for over a year... and now that we've finally met, you tell me to wait? No. I won't. I can't do that. Even while we couldn't meet, I wanted to become one with you, wanted to, wanted to, wanted to, wanted to, wanted to, wanted to, wanted to, so badly!"

"—"

"And on top of that, you tell me to wait even now!? And what's more, you're still dragging around some spirit woman I've never even seen before! Where did you find her!? What's so good about a woman like that!? Small body, insolent face, not a shred of womanliness in her chest or her hips! Is it because she's a spirit!? Because you're a spirit too, so a woman ought to be a spirit as well!? Is what you and I have going to lose out for some trivial reason like that!? I'll burn you, you hear me!"

As she spoke, the direction of her rant grew increasingly unhinged, and Sirius's arms, working themselves into a fury on their own, wreathed themselves in flame once more. The crowd, too, was dragged back from courtship mode into rage mode, and it clearly took a visible toll on their bodies—one after another began bleeding from the eyes and nose.

"And I bet the reason you're even going after Regulus is that half-demon in his arms!? That filthy little half-demon girl! The silver-haired half-demon! Why do you take her side so much!? Haven't you figured it out by now? You wretched, contemptible, miserly damn Witch...! If she's meant to be reborn according to your wish, then I'll burn her right in front of you...!"

"I don't even— none of this makes any sense anymore, you..."

In a blood-vomiting shriek, the monster spewed out her hatred for both Emilia and the "Witch of Envy."
Wasn't the Witch Cult's goal the resurrection of the "Witch of Envy"? That she would hate, above all else, the very object of that resurrection was something Subaru couldn't comprehend at all.
More than that—Sirius didn't share a single shred of sympathy for Petelgeuse's cherished wish.

He'd known she could never be an ally, but did she have to be this thoroughly an enemy?

"—"

A three-way standoff had formed once again.
Regulus intended to kill both Subaru and Sirius.
Sirius meant to drive Regulus off and, from Subaru, sense out the presence of a Petelgeuse who wasn't even there.
Subaru wanted to reclaim Emilia from Regulus, win Tina's release from Sirius, and if at all possible, defeat both Sin Archbishops.

Clearly, Subaru's conditions alone were far too demanding.
Sweat trickled down Subaru's brow at a situation that demanded results no one could achieve alone.

He'd already Returned by Death once; after that, Sirius had appeared, followed by Regulus. And through the ensuing chaos and maneuvering with both Sin Archbishops together, this was, at the very least, the longest he'd managed to survive.
That all the information he'd gained across that stretch of time amounted to nothing but "this is bad" was truly hopeless.

"...Beako."

"Either way, I can manage, I suppose."

From behind him came the voice of his partner, bracing Subaru just as he was on the verge of losing his nerve.
At those words entrusting the choice to him, Subaru steeled his resolve.
That was when it happened.

"—Oh?"
"...!"

Both Regulus and Sirius changed their demeanor at the exact same moment.
Each of them reached into their own robes and pulled out—a single book.

"That's..."

A thick, black-bound book he recognized.
There was no mistaking it—Subaru still had one of his own.
The Gospel, which every last member of the Witch Cult was said to carry.

"—"

Paying no mind to Subaru, whose wariness only deepened, the two Sin Archbishops flipped open their own Gospels together and ran their eyes over the pages.
The two of them then snapped their books shut in the same instant, but the expressions on their faces were opposites.

"I'd guess it says the same thing for both of us. What do you think, Sirius?"

"Be quiet, Regulus. Why... why now, of all times. He's finally, finally within my reach."

Regulus wore a thin smile, while Sirius ground her teeth, frustration bleeding into her voice.
Villain and monster, holding opposite feelings, nonetheless seemed united in intent.
Because the two of them turned to look at Subaru together, and—

"Sorry, but it's time. I no longer have leisure to spend on you. Thank the Gospel... no, that's wrong. That wouldn't be rational, not by any measure. Gratitude toward a scrap of paper is unnecessary, after all. So instead, you should thank me, for obeying the Gospel."

"I'll let the gratitude nonsense slide, but—time, you say? What the hell are you talking about!"

"Exactly what it sounds like. This was free time, before what I actually have to do. As for me, I've spent it meaningfully—getting my bride, just as you see."

"I am left burning with the most terrible incompletion...! To have him right in front of me and be treated like this—there's no worse cruelty. It's far too much. I feel myself about to be swept away by grief. You understand, don't you!?"

Regulus spoke with unhurried composure, while Sirius forced her sorrow upon them. The crowd collapsed where they stood, and behind him, Beatrice fought to hold back sobs.
Yet before all of it, neither Subaru nor Regulus showed the slightest reaction.

"Sorry, but your pushy, shallow little love has no effect whatsoever on someone whose own love, however small, is already complete. It just strikes me as filthy, is all."

Saying so in a voice dripping with condescension, Regulus turned his back on Subaru and the others, still holding Emilia.
It seemed he really did intend, just as he'd declared, to let them go and leave the scene as-is. Regulus's personal departure would have been more than welcome—but it wasn't going to be that simple.

"Wait, you bastard! Don't just decide things on your own! Leave the girl behind! Or else..."

"I've been thinking, you know."

"—!?"

Regulus stopped walking and turned only his head back toward them, smiling.
At that smile, Subaru felt as though a blade had traced down his spine, and he froze, breath caught in his throat.
And so, he couldn't stop what Regulus did next.

"It'd be a shame to have fewer guests on the bride's side, and I think it'd be a bit heartless not to invite you, given how smitten you were with her too. —So, I'll go ahead and not kill you."

Lightly, Regulus tapped the cobblestones with the toe of his shoe.
It looked like nothing more than adjusting the fit of his shoe, but his toe scooped up the cobblestones as effortlessly as a shovel gouging into soft earth.
Following the arc of his toe, the scooped-up debris of stone went flying straight toward Subaru.

Most of it was little more than shrapnel flung without any real aim. But part of it grazed the outside of Subaru's right leg—and in the next instant, his right leg vanished, wholesale.

"—Eh?"

Baring an ugly cross-section as though gouged out by a beast's claws, Subaru's right leg lay neatly opened up—white bone, pink muscle, yellow nerves, and green blood vessels sliced through with a smooth, clean cut.
Incomprehension, then comprehension, and immediately after—agony.

"—!? Gh— aahh! Aaaaahh!?"

His vision went pure white, a sensation like dozens of sharp needles being driven straight into the crown of his skull.
His throat shook as a scream tore free, and Subaru, unable to hold himself up, toppled sideways. He reached out and clamped his fingers desperately over the outside of his right thigh. It was no use. The wound was too large; his palm alone couldn't hope to seal it.

"Subaru!? Subaru! Subaru, wait, I suppose! Right now!"

Beatrice, who'd gone down to the ground along with him, realized the severity of Subaru's wound and hurriedly began chanting a healing spell. At the wretched sight the two of them made, Regulus nodded, satisfied.

"Let's call that fitting retribution for your reckless behavior earlier. I suppose you could reoffend, but I'd like to hold out some hope for your future too. This pain you're feeling now—when you next try to hurt someone, surely it'll be what stops your foot from taking that step. No need to thank me. Just save someone the same way in return, next time, and that'll be enough."

"Aaaahh! Guh— guh, ungh, gahh!!"

It hurts it hurts it hurts it hurts it hurts it hurts it hurts it hurts it hurts—!
His vision blurred. His molars clenched hard enough to crack. Everything was red. He couldn't tell up from down, left from right. Whether he was lying down or standing, alive or dead.
He didn't know. Didn't know, didn't know. He didn't know—but there was one thing he did.

"E-Emili—a...! Gh— hk, ugh, gohk..."

"Subaru, you mustn't thrash around, I suppose! If you're going to be sick, turn on your side, or your throat will—"

Wracked with pain and panic, Subaru thrashed his head while clutching his wound. His heart hammered like an alarm bell, his innards, wrung tight with tension, screamed and made him retch again and again.
Beatrice, small as she was, threw her whole body into pinning down Subaru's thrashing form while wringing out every last drop of her healing magic. But as though mocking her efforts—

"...This is no joke, I suppose."

"I'm sorry. But it really isn't a joke. It's only natural, isn't it?"

Behind Beatrice, who muttered the words with bitter loathing, Sirius answered in a voice steeped in dark, festering passion.
Around Sirius were people screaming in agony, writhing on the ground.
Every one of them clutched at a wound in their own right leg, desperately begging for help.
Just like Subaru, their right legs torn open as though by a beast's claws.

"My beloved Petelgeuse would say it like this: pain is proof of life, and living itself is an act wrought by 'love.' I understand. It cannot possibly be wrong. But I believe there's a form of 'love' still further beyond that. Thank you. And that is becoming one. Because 'love' is wishing to become one! Seeing the same things, feeling the same things, spending time together, ending together—becoming one is 'love' itself!"

Sirius spread both hands wide and clapped them together in a great, explosive burst of applause.
She gazed with envy at the faces of the people gasping in agony, and finally turned a look laced with cold cruelty on Beatrice alone.

"Everyone ought to taste the same fullness of life as he did. But you, and that filthy half-demon—the two of you are the only ones I won't grant it to. As if I'd ever let the likes of you become his equal."

"...Nothing is more unsightly than a woman driven mad by envy, I suppose. Whatever you do or don't do, Betty is already one with Subaru, I suppose. Betty belongs to Subaru."

"—!"

Not to be outdone by the vicious remark, Beatrice shot back just as sharp.
Monster and Spirit locked eyes, but it ended quickly as the monster turned her face away.

"For now, I'll leave him in your keeping. I have to give priority to the Gospel's instructions. Yes, reluctantly. I'm sorry. I'm sorry. Really, I want to take you back right away."

Sirius turned an endlessly mad affection on Subaru, whose eyes had rolled back white with pain. Then, in a single leap, the monster bounded out of the blood-soaked plaza and was gone.
Beatrice had no choice but to give up chasing that retreating back, even knowing full well that letting her escape might mean still more victims.

"—Subaru."

Subaru's eyes had rolled back white, yellow foam bubbling from the corner of his mouth.
Beatrice pressed her palm to the wound, working to treat the bleeding that wouldn't stop. The wound was too large; if she let her focus slip for even a moment, death from blood loss was unavoidable.
Saving Subaru was Beatrice's absolute top priority. And the next thing she had to prioritize after that was—

"I have to heal everyone else's wounds too..."

All told, more than thirty wounded lay scattered across the plaza.
Every one of them bore a wound identical to Subaru's, and infuriatingly, healing Subaru did nothing to share the effect with the rest. Each wound had to be healed individually.
It was a burden severe enough that it would exhaust every last drop of Beatrice's own stored mana, and even then she couldn't be sure it would be enough.

"—Subaru, I'm sorry, I suppose."

Beatrice's voice, straining to stay composed as she desperately healed Subaru's wound, cracked.
A single tear slipped from her wide eyes and ran down her pale cheek.

"I'm sorry. I'm sorry, I suppose..."

Again and again, Beatrice apologized.
Even knowing her voice couldn't reach Subaru, who had passed out from the pain.
Even knowing it would solve nothing at all.

"I'm sorry, I suppose, Emilia—"

Sirius, who had left behind a multitude of victims and departed the scene.
And Regulus, who had done nothing but flaunt his overwhelming power before making off with Emilia.

—Two Sin Archbishops, unleashed upon the sluice city of Priestella.
