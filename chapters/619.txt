Arc 8 Chapter 3: "Three Reasons"

Bursting out of the annex's entrance, Subaru froze before the man who stood there, faintly smiling.

Perhaps that paralysis could be called the most foolish choice possible in front of this man. But even for Natsuki Subaru, who had experienced every kind of hardship, who had experienced even "death" itself again and again, this man's indescribable eeriness turned his thoughts white.

A fear he didn't even hold for the Witch Cult's Sin Archbishops, a sensation of his heart being ensnared by it.

Subaru found himself made to feel that, by Todd Fang.

"――――"

Reflexively responding to the voice he'd heard, confusion arose beside the frozen Subaru.

It was Beatrice, still holding hands with him, made to face the same person as Subaru. Besides her, Tanza and Idra too watched the stock-still Subaru with puzzlement.

His heart urged him to call out a warning to them at once, but—

"—Katya-san!"

—faster than Subaru's trembling lips could move, Rem cried out in a high voice.

Looking over, he saw Rem's pale blue eyes, turned back at the doorway, fixed on Todd—no, not on him, but just behind him.

It took effort for Subaru to tear his gaze from Todd and turn his attention there, but once he managed it, he understood the target of Rem's call.

There, as if hiding behind Todd's back, was a woman in a wheelchair.

Peeking out from behind while pinching the hem of Todd's clothes, the woman—with her dark brown wavy hair and eyes that surveyed the world with suspicion—matched the features Rem had described.

That in itself would be a happy thing—no, the fact that she was beside Todd was the problem.

"Please, get away from her……!"

"Uuu!"

Apparently arriving at the same thought as Subaru, Rem sharpened her voice and glared at Todd. Spurred by Rem's fighting spirit, Rui, who stuck close to her, growled at Todd as well.

Having lost his trademark bandana and let down the orange hair he usually kept up, Todd was different in appearance, but that wouldn't erase his cruelty or his dangerous ingenuity.

Even in this situation, it was easy to imagine him using the woman—Katya—as a shield to bend Subaru and the others to his will.

And of course, from Subaru's past experience fighting Todd, it was clear that "death" was what awaited anyone who ended up obeying him.

"……From here……"

He hadn't confirmed how far back things would rewind if he died now.

Whether it should be called fortunate or troublesome, in the whole sequence of intervening in this Battle for the Imperial Capital, breaking through the city wall, and pushing into the city, Subaru hadn't lost his life even once. Thanks to that—or because of it—he didn't know the starting point of his "Return by Death."

He'd been able to meet Rui and Beatrice, and reunite with Rem and Flop as well.

Honestly, this situation was going almost too well, and he couldn't calculate how many retries it would take to arrange things this favorably again.

Still—

"—I'll do it if I have to."

Once he'd decided who to save, who to let live, who to carry forward into the future, Subaru was firmly resolved to seize that outcome without fail.

That was exactly why, on the Isle of Sword-Slaves, he'd managed to save every single sword-slave he'd gotten involved with, and fought his way through to this day as a comrade of the Pleiades Battalion. —Never give up, absolutely never.

Even if the obstacle he had to overcome was a symbol of fear that froze his heart.

Yes, Subaru wet his half-dried lips with his tongue, resolved to take decisive action—

"W-wait a second! You've got it wrong! I wasn't captured, Rem!"

"Katya-san?"

"It's a misunderstanding……that's right, a misunderstanding! Todd isn't your enemy!"

Raising her voice, Katya tried to roll her wheelchair forward. But Todd pressed down on her thin shoulder, blocking her advance.

Pitched forward by her own momentum, Katya glared at Todd with tearful eyes.

"H-hey, what are you doing! That's dangerous!"

"You're the one who's dangerous. Don't just go rolling forward on your own."

"W-who do you think I'm saying this for……!"

"For my sake, obviously. But that doesn't mean I can just watch the woman I love pull some dangerous stunt."

"Ngh……"

Her face reddening, Katya, who had snapped back with such force, was silenced by that. It wasn't shame or embarrassment that did it so much as the fact that his words clearly conveyed that he was thinking of her.

A completely honest statement with no trace of deception in it—that was the hard thing for Subaru to believe.

"Katya-san, could it be that he's……"

Drawing a small breath, Rem asked Katya that, tentatively. She hadn't clearly stated the content of the question, but the focus of what she wanted to ask was obvious.

Perhaps as a sign of her confusion and unease, the hand Rem had resting on Rui's shoulder beside her was weak. To that question of Rem's, Katya nodded while still biting her lip.

"I've told you before, haven't I. He's my……that, you know."

"Fiancé."

"That's it……!"

Given that hesitant assertion, Katya answered Rem's question clearly.

Hearing that, Rem's eyes wavered, and she was visibly shaken. Struck by the same shock as Rem, Subaru too was violently shaken.

"Fiancé……"

From their first meeting through everything that followed, Subaru had almost no good memories with Todd, but aside from his maniacal caution and mercilessness, one thing that had stuck in his memory was the talk of a "fiancée."

Stationed as an Imperial soldier, camped near the forest where the settlement of the People of Shudrak was, Todd had repeatedly talked about wanting to return to his fiancée.

But after that, through the bloody exchanges that followed with Todd, his expectations for anything resembling humanity in the man had faded, and it had become just a memory shoved to the back of his mind—until now.

"She really exists……"

—was the thought that slipped out of him, so mismatched were the words "Todd" and "beloved fiancée."

It wasn't as if Rem could have heard Subaru's muttering, but she fixed Todd with a hard, suspicious gaze and said,

"Are you really Katya-san's fiancé?"

"Yeah, really. Like I said, quite the twist of fate, huh. Never imagined Katya and you'd end up cozy together, sheltered in the chancellor's mansion."

"――――"

"Don't look at me like that. Sure, I won't say it was a good meeting or a good relationship, but given the circumstances, shouldn't we both let bygones be bygones?"

Shrugging at Rem's silent, hard stare, Todd said it as brazen as could be. Then his gaze shifted—not to Rem, but this way, toward Subaru.

Holding his breath, Subaru met Todd's gaze. Cold sweat ran down his back.

But—

"Just like the kid there says, right?"

"—ah"

There was no hint of hostility in Todd's manner as he winked one eye. True to his words, as if it were an obligation on the part of the one proposing cooperation to escape this situation, he let go of hostility and wariness and extended a hand.

"Husband-kun, wife-kun, what's your connection to this soldier-kun?"

At the sight of Subaru and Rem falling silent, Flop's gentle face took on a note of seriousness. To his question, Subaru, mindful of his current appearance, couldn't afford to say anything careless. Instead, Rem picked up the thread with "As for him—"

"We've crossed paths a few times before. Each time, our lives were put in danger."

"Eh……"

Glancing toward Katya, Rem chose her words carefully while still conveying the truth. Hearing it, Katya's eyes went wide, and she looked immediately to Todd beside her.

Her lips trembling, she questioned her fiancé's profile.

"I-is that true? Todd, you……did something to Rem……"

"If it's about that young lady, it's true. I told you, there's bad blood. I'm a soldier. Sometimes that means pointing a weapon at someone. It's one of those unwanted situations."

"Unwanted, how convenient for you to say……!"

"If it's about the fortress city, it's the result of judging your companion a threat. If it's before that, I was just under orders like any other soldier. I've got no personal grudge against you."

As he spoke, Todd drew the axe at his waist. For an instant, tension ran through Subaru and the others' entire bodies, but he simply tossed it away with an outstretched hand, without any particular effort.

The axe traced an arc, slid across the ground, and rolled to a stop at Subaru's feet.

"Aー-u"

Rui picked up the axe and looked back and forth between Subaru and Rem's faces.

It seemed it wasn't some bomb shaped like an axe—this was a genuine act of disarmament.

Seeing that Subaru and the others had recognized as much, Todd waved both raised hands lightly.

"See, no hostility. But we're both wasting time glaring at each other, aren't we?"

"……Wife-kun, I think I should say something constructive too, what do you think?"

"――――"

Hugging her own arms tightly, Rem glanced sidelong at Subaru.

Only Subaru and Rem shared the sense of just how much of a threat Todd posed. As for actual acquaintance, Rui had met him at the same time as Subaru and the others, and Flop too had been attacked by Todd in the fortress city—but the loop in which that danger had actually played out existed in neither of their memories.

Therefore—

"Betty will go along with whatever Subaru decides, I suppose."

Still holding Subaru's hand, Beatrice, without regard to advance or retreat, gave him only the courage to choose.

And not just her. Tanza and Idra too entrusted the future to Subaru's choice. —Just having these two here in the same place as Todd was enough to make his heart feel it might freeze.

If possible, he wanted to put distance between Todd's presence and themselves as soon as possible, even by a single second—but,

"……Katya-san is Rem's friend, isn't she. I want to get as many people out as possible, without anyone getting hurt. There's no time for us to be at each other's throats here, I think."

"――. I understand, but……"

Looking down, Subaru bit his lip, and Rem too stiffened her cheeks in frustration.

But there was no telling what action Todd might take if they clumsily refused him. That was an anxiety Rem shared as well. Now that they'd crossed paths, they couldn't afford to let him out of arm's reach.

It was the same reasoning as not being able to leave a bomb lying around when you didn't know when it might go off.

"D-don't worry about it! This guy, he's ridiculously hard to kill, so! I'm sure he'll be useful for helping all of us escape, me included."

"Hey now, don't go comparing your fiancé to a Zodda Bug or something."

At Subaru and Rem's uneasy manner, Katya, clumsily, tried to speak up for Todd. Wryly smiling at Katya's defense of him, Todd ran a hand through his hair and said,

"Well, if you're willing to take me in, I've got to repay the favor. Besides, I've got to make Katya think better of me—she doesn't exactly hold me in high regard."

Saying things like that, sounding for all the world like some reliable, upstanding young man, he came to join them, heedless of Subaru's feelings as his throat tightened with tension that refused to ease.


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△


—In this situation, there were three reasons Subaru couldn't refuse Todd Fang's presence.

The first was the existence of Katya, Todd's fiancée.

Having apparently built a friendly relationship with Rem during their time living in this mansion, she was, contrary to her sharp tongue, a genuinely good person—clearly one of those who bore no ill will toward Subaru and the others. From what he could see, she didn't seem to understand the rotten core of Todd's character, but no matter what Subaru and the others might argue here, it would be a losing bet as to whether she'd believe her fiancé or them.

So then—should he choose to part ways with Katya on the grounds of his revulsion toward Todd?

The sight of her entrusting her weight to a high-performance wheelchair—one that lost nothing even compared to the one Subaru had built for Rem—was pitiable, and there was no way he could simply abandon her.

Given that Katya and Todd were a package deal, it followed that he couldn't refuse Todd.

The second reason was that, due to repeated "Return by Death," Todd's acts of violence had all, without exception, gone unconsummated.

Cold-blooded by nature and possessed of ruthless judgment, Todd had, across many different turns of events, cornered Subaru time and again, taking Subaru's life—no, sometimes taking the lives of Subaru's precious companions.

Even now, Flop who traveled with him, and even Tanza and Idra, had been killed by Todd before.

The reason Subaru held such strong revulsion and fear toward Todd was probably that, of everyone he'd met in this world, Todd was the one who, right before Subaru's eyes, had taken the most companions' lives.

But because "Return by Death" had rendered all those events unconsummated, the current state of affairs was one where, even though Todd had turned his murderous blade on Subaru and the others, he hadn't inflicted any further harm.

The great fire in the Shudrak forest, the relentless assault in the fortress city, the massacre of sword-slaves on the Isle of Sword-Slaves—none of these had actually happened. —It was simply that he was capable of carrying them out.

And then, the last, the third reason—

"—Aim above the chest! Going for the legs will only buy time!"

"Tch, Beatrice!"
"I know, I suppose! Miniya!"

At the sharp voice's instruction, Subaru and Beatrice simultaneously raised their hands to the front.

In the direction their palms pointed, several sickly-pale enemies lay felled on the ground—Zombies—and purple arrows conjured there flew at them; amid the swirling dust, the enemies crystallized.

Some had escaped a fatal hit—

"Unfortunately, once the torso's in that state……"

"Even with the head intact, it'll just be smashed."

Tanza's falling heel struck the Zombie that hadn't been instantly incapacitated, shattering its crystallized torso, leaving the limb-blasted Zombie with nothing left to do.

From the rolling Zombie head, no regeneration began, and eventually even the intact head lost its color like sand, crumbling and vanishing.

The sight wasn't without a certain heartache.

"That should clear the obstacles in the back alley. Your magic's a real godsend against these things. How's your reserves?"

"……Plenty left, I suppose. A hundred thousand of them would be no trouble, perhaps."

"That's reassuring. But don't push too far forward. You two are our lifeline."

Having succeeded in eliminating the group of Zombies blocking the road, Subaru and Beatrice felt conflicted at being praised for their handiwork.

Beating down the Zombies one-sidedly was, mentally speaking, the correct choice. On the other hand, the fact that it was Todd praising them for it left a bitter taste.

Meanwhile, unaware of Subaru and the others' feelings—

"Up until we joined Katya at the mansion, I ran into those pale-faced things more than a few times. They don't seem to be on the side of the regular army or the rebels—things you just can't reason with."

"Is that so? But the ones you two took down at the mansion seemed to understand language just fine."

"Understanding language and being reasoned with are similar but totally different things, aren't they? They won't listen no matter what, and on top of that, they don't die even if you crush their heads or cut off their necks. About as troublesome an enemy as you could ask for."

"Undying soldiers……just like what the child accompanying Schultz-sama was saying."

Hearing about the Zombies' formidable traits, Flop and Tanza mulled it over.

However, what they were using as material for consideration was Todd's opinion—and it was, Subaru had to admit, a highly accurate analysis he couldn't afford to ignore.

"In short, you can't beat them with straightforward methods. Swords, spears, weapons are basically just for peace of mind. The real deal is……"

"Husband-kun and wife-kun's……"
"Magic, isn't it."

"The ones I burned to charcoal with the borrowed spirit didn't get back up either. Whether it works with more than just magic—regular fire, say—I haven't confirmed enough to say."

"――――"

"Well, at least the ones you hit above the neck take longer to come back than the ones with other parts broken. Legs regenerate surprisingly fast, so don't count on cutting off their mobility."

Even Subaru, who knew Todd's true nature, couldn't help but be impressed that he'd go so far as to offer this kind of advice.

Once the matter of accepting him into the group had been settled, Todd had shared his knowledge of the Zombies this freely, laying it all out.

Naturally, he too, just like Subaru and the others, had had to move through an Imperial Capital swarming with Zombies to reach Katya. Having accomplished that, Todd might well have effective strategies against the Zombies, or knowledge useful for escaping.

That expectation was precisely the third reason Subaru had welcomed Todd.

"Even so, to think it would come to this……"

The Zombies had appeared just after Subaru and the others had climbed over the city wall.

For a moment, Subaru had wondered whether they themselves had been the trigger for the Zombies' appearance, but he decided that was an anxiety with no answer even if he thought about it, and chose to trample over it and press on.

In any case, from the Zombies' appearance until now, the time available for Subaru's group and Todd to think things through should have been the same—yet Todd's depth of information necessary for survival had progressed far beyond theirs.

It was this refusal to let up his guard—toward Zombies or anything else, toward anyone, anywhere—that was fueling Todd's abnormal decisiveness and ability to act.

"Katya-san, are you all right?"

"Eh, y-yeah, aside from trembling at these unfathomable things. ……What about you? You okay?"

"People with no chance of winning against them, like Priscilla-sama or Madelyn-san, scare me far more."

"That's an unpleasant kind of nerve to have……"

Lagging a little behind Subaru's combat team, who moved slightly ahead to clear the path, Rem and Katya followed slowly from behind. Rem pushed Katya's wheelchair, and Rui, restlessly scanning their surroundings, guarded the two defenseless women.

Because they were choosing paths that avoided the rough terrain and detoured around clusters of Zombies, and factoring in the pace of the fake crown prince's group led by Idra on her Galewind Horse, their overall speed of movement couldn't be called fast.

Even so, they were steadily drawing closer to the Imperial Capital's city wall.

"Husband-kun, you're looking troubled. Is it the soldier-kun you're still worried about after all?"

"Flop-san……"

Ever attentive to his surroundings, Flop saw through Subaru's complicated expression—relief and bitterness arriving at once.

Sharp-eyed as he was, Flop had surely already noticed that Subaru was wary of Todd more than necessary—or at least, more than necessary given the amount of information available at this point.

Of course, since it would run afoul of "Return by Death," there was no way Subaru could confide to the man himself the fact that Flop had been killed.

"Flop-san, like I said before……"

"Pay close attention to the soldier-kun's movements, was it? I understand perfectly well. The reason Tanza-jō stays as close to the soldier-kun as she does is because you asked her to."

"――――"

"Small in stature, your face turned childlike, and yet your way of thinking's grown more adult, more cautious, hasn't it, husband-kun. It's a virtue of yours that you can always agonize with your full effort. But you know, I'm fond of the you who proposed cross-dressing to take the fortress city, too."

At Flop's gentle, mild smile, Subaru was taken aback, then laughed wryly.

Odd, given his current appearance, but he felt keenly the older-brotherly strength Flop possessed, even compared with his original body. It seemed only natural that Medium so thoroughly admired Flop.

"—Ah, Subaru, mind your footing, I suppose."

"My footing……huh, whoa."

Just as that conversation reached a lull, Subaru, his hand pulled by Beatrice, looked down to see water flowing over the sloped ground at his feet, soaking the soles of his shoes.

Beyond the Crystal Palace, the reservoir holding a massive volume of water had been destroyed, and the water level flooding into the city was beginning to rise in earnest. Their enemies weren't just the Imperial soldiers or the Zombies—the water was one too.

If they spent too long dawdling on the retreat, eventually they'd be swallowed by the swelling current—

"If we don't reach the exit before that happens, we're all finished together."

Todd, coming over to join them, put into words with perfect clarity the sense of crisis Subaru himself had felt.

Subaru stiffened slightly; whether or not Todd noticed that wariness, he pointed the tip of the returned axe toward the Crystal Palace and said,

"Commander Moguro looks like he's at a disadvantage against the 'Cloud Dragon' too. Losing's probably just a matter of time. One-on-one might be one thing, but two-on-one is fatal……after the commander goes down, how do you think the enemy will move?"

"How would we know?"

"Setting the 'Cloud Dragon' aside, the Flying Dragon Rider working with it looks like a corpse, from what I can tell. So far, the corpses attack us the moment they spot us. In other words……"

"The Zombies are trying to kill living people."

"Infuriating, but that's about the gist of it."

Cocking his head, Todd made his point, and Subaru understood what he was getting at.

The Zombies' objective was to systematically eliminate any living person they encountered. If there was a means to kill a great many at once for that purpose, it wouldn't be strange for them to choose it.

That stone Giant clashing with the white Dragon right in front of the palace—if it lost, the Zombies would utterly destroy the reservoir's walls and let the water burst forth all at once.

If that happened, Subaru and his group, the people of the Imperial Capital, and even the regular army and rebel forces clashing outside—all of them together would be swept away, roots and all.

"It is infuriating. But that name, Zombie, isn't bad. We need some kind of name for them anyway. From here on, I'll call them Zombies."

"What do you think about those Zombies, you, I wonder."

Suddenly, Beatrice, who had been silent, tossed that question at Todd.

Muttering "Zombie" a few times to get the word to settle in his mouth, Todd made a puzzled face at Beatrice's question.

"What do I think, how do you mean?"

"So far, most of the Zombies we've run into have been Imperial soldiers just like you, I suppose. You can tell from their uniforms. What you think about having struck down your own allies, that's……"

"First off, they're not allies. Same uniform, that's all—once they're dead, they're not soldiers or anything else anymore. No need to think of them as more than a troublesome obstacle."

"――――"

"Whose puppets they are, what their purpose is—that's not something I think matters much for surviving right now. That's the kind of thing the higher-ups can sit around figuring out an answer for."

Answered so matter-of-factly, Beatrice silently agreed to drop the conversation.

At her attitude, Todd shrugged, then looked back over at Subaru and said,

"Reliable partner you've got there, but if you're feeling worn down mentally, you'd do well to talk it out with her properly. Surprisingly, just venting what's in your gut can make things better."

"……I'll keep that in mind."

To Subaru's heavy reply, Todd left him with "You should," and moved on ahead.

He served as scout for the road ahead, worked out the Zombies' positions and how to repel them, and Subaru and the others would consider his findings and act on them to secure their escape route. As things stood, it was a strategy that was working well.

Only—

"Subaru, if you're feeling worn down, tell Betty anything at all, I suppose."

As Beatrice peered up into his face like that, it was in fact Subaru who was the more mentally worn down of the two.

Part of it was from continuing to devote such intense vigilance toward Todd, and part of it was from having his own inner mana drawn on for Beatrice's magic.

But the greatest psychological burden was destroying the Zombies.

Smashing apart Zombies that talked, that retained something close to their will from when they were alive, was painful.

If only he could dismiss them, like Todd did, as mere scarecrows blocking the road—how much easier that would be.

"I think that's a virtue of yours too, husband-kun."

"……I wonder. Rem's probably going to kick me in the butt again for moping."

"Wife-kun wouldn't do a thing like that."

"――――"

"She wouldn't."

Strangely, Flop's repeated words carried a certain persuasive weight.

Flop, who had survived this brutal empire with nothing but words, wielding neither force nor magic. Was it the one and only greatest weapon he possessed—his personal virtue—that made you believe him like that?

"Thanks to you, Flop-san, I feel a bit better."

"Glad to hear it! Seeing as I can't be of any use in any other way, if husband-kun got depressed on me, I wouldn't have the means to help! Oh, but wait."

"But?"

"Because of me, I may have put Miss Dress in a bit of a mood."

At Flop's remark, Subaru turned around with an "Eh?" and found Beatrice there, still holding his hand, her round, cute eyes narrowing into a glare directed at him.

At her reaction, Subaru's cheeks stiffened, and Beatrice, her cheeks reddening, said,

"Leaving Betty who was worrying about you to one side, must be nice being you. Subaru's going to go right on getting friendly with handsome men like that and neglecting Betty again, I suppose."

"That's some serious reputational damage! No, I'm definitely not neglecting you, okay? To begin with, Flop-san and Cecy are just the exceptions—Idra and Weitz have their own charm, but they're not exactly handsome……though Hiain's got a certain likability going for him."

"Excuses are unnecessary, I suppose!"

Turning her face sharply away, Subaru groaned as though his chest had been gouged hard.

Beatrice too had been worried about him, just the same. Even if Flop had simply had a way with words, Subaru felt genuinely sorry for what he'd done.

No matter what, once they'd made it out of this, he'd have to make it up to her sincerely, with everything he had.

"For now, all I can do is keep petting her and rubbing her palms……"

"……What exactly are you doing, in a situation like this?"

"Wawu!"

Startled by a voice calling out from behind him, Subaru turned. Rem, eyeing him suspiciously, kept Rui—who was restlessly glancing around in wariness—clinging to her waist, and said,

"Even turned into that shape, you're still exactly yourself, aren't you."

"Oh? Does that mean you know me well enough to recognize what's 'me'?"

"Correction. Turned small like that, you've become even less restrained than before."

"That part, I'd like you to chalk up to the unfiltered spirit of childhood and let it slide with a laugh."

Even Rem's sharp assessment was, to Subaru—separated from her for a while as he'd been—a welcome stimulus.

Without realizing it, his expression had started to droop at the corners of his eyes; he consciously firmed it up, careful not to make Rem uneasy.

And speaking of someone else he didn't want to make uneasy—

"Hey, is that Todd guy not causing trouble? Is he actually being useful?"

"――――"

Yes—it was Katya, asking that timidly from the wheelchair Rem was pushing.

Freed from her confinement, and compared even to the fake crown prince's group, who followed the retreat under their own power, Katya, unable to move on her own, felt keenly the sense of helplessness—no less than they did.

She clearly harbored an affection she wasn't being honest about toward her fiancé Todd, and it was plain she was deeply worried, not just for herself but for him as well.

To Katya's pure concern, Subaru glanced back toward the alley Todd had gone down and said,

"Yeah, he's being useful. Good nose, good eyes. His instructions are on point too—he's a big help."

"O-oh. That's good then……"

Lowering her eyes, Katya let out a breath of relief.

Galling as it was to admit, that hadn't been an empty reassurance made up to put Katya's mind at ease—it was a fair evaluation of Todd's performance so far.

In fact, Todd's performance as a soldier was beyond reproach, and in their current situation, having to flee while escorting a large number of people who couldn't defend themselves, his scouting and route-selection were contributing enormously to their survival.

He had no effective means against the Zombies himself, but his arrangements for putting Subaru and Beatrice—who did have effective means—into position were outstandingly good. —Aside from the excessive psychological toll on Subaru, there was next to no downside to having Todd around.

"――――"

—No, Rem too, looking conflicted over that exchange with Katya, was, just like Subaru, being forced to bear a mental burden because of Todd's presence.

"If this keeps up……"

Could they really, without incident, escape outside the Imperial Capital with this same group of people, exactly as they were?

As Subaru agonized over a question to which even he didn't know what answer he wanted—

"—Katya, I told you to stay further back. Don't catch up to me."

—Todd, who had gone out scouting, came back, spotted Katya with Subaru and the others, and flung that warning at his fiancée.

At Todd's words, Katya's expression started to show relief at his being safe, then immediately turned to one of resistance against the words directed at her, and then changed expression once more.

Slightly, as if worrying over Todd's manner.

"Todd, did something happen?"

"……Good grief, sharp as a tack only when it counts, this woman."

Saying that, Todd ran a hand through his hair, no longer held up by the bandana, and, looking toward Subaru and the others, revealed what he'd seen.

It was this—

"—Up ahead, there's a Zombie sitting tight that looks like a tough one. That Cyclops Tribe member—probably one that stood out quite a bit in today's battle, I'd bet."
