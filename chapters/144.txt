Arc 3 Chapter 67: "Spirit Knight"

There are countless things that need to be thought through.

As they resumed their march toward the Mathers domain, cutting through the wind, there were two broad tasks Subaru and the others had to accomplish.

*Keep Emilia and the others at the mansion, and the villagers, out of harm's way.*
*Fight the Witch Cult, who were plotting the attack, and finish off Petelgeuse of "Sloth."*

Last time, they had prioritized the latter, hoping that accomplishing it would achieve the former at the same time — but that had been thwarted by Petelgeuse's unexpected tenacity for survival.

Just how far his possession reached, under what conditions, and on whom it worked — none of that was clear. But as a matter of fact, he had gotten ahead of them, lain in wait at that spot, and brought about that catastrophe.

Overconfidence was forbidden — no, unless every possible countermeasure was taken, the best outcome could never be reached.

"So that means... a temporary evacuation, yeah — worst case, abandoning the mansion and the village is a must."

Even Subaru understood just how merciless a decision that was for them. Telling people to throw away the life they'd built, the home they were used to, and go running off with nothing but the clothes on their backs — that wasn't something anyone could just accept. But it had to be done.

*Nothing could be worth more than their lives.*

He knew there were times when something had to take priority even over life itself. But even so, life ought to be the top priority, barring a very few, very limited exceptions. On that point, Subaru had no intention of yielding at all.

"Thinking about the time we've got, the evacuation and taking down the Witch Cult have to happen at the same time."

It was unclear just how thoroughly Petelgeuse had prepared, but given that he'd hidden a group of that size around the mansion, it would be far too convenient to assume he'd notice absolutely nothing unusual happening in the village. Ten times out of ten, they should assume there were watchers posted, just as there had been when their dragon carriage was attacked on the highway.

If they rushed the evacuation, the chances of the Witch Cult catching wind of it and moving would rise. Which meant the Witch Cult needed to be crushed before they could move.

"The procedure should be mostly fine as it was before. ...It bothers me that we only found eight hideouts, but if we can find the rest of them this time—"

Their odds of winning would climb sharply. At the very least, they'd be able to completely eliminate the chance of being ambushed by Witch Cult members they hadn't yet spotted. If the only threat they had to deal with was narrowed down to Petelgeuse, that alone would count as more than enough of a result.

And as for dealing with Petelgeuse — the single biggest concern —

"If I'm not there to counter the Unseen Hand, none of this is gonna work."

That much, at least, was a line he would never give up on.

Last time, he'd dragged out the conversation to draw Petelgeuse's attention, and Wilhelm's surprise attack had let them bring down the first Petelgeuse. This time, following that same flow as a basic template should be fine. The problem was what to do if they encountered a second one, or a third—

"No, wait — should I be thinking about taking him alive instead? If it's a case of 'die, then possess,' then as long as I don't let him die, doesn't that mean I've got him?"

Saying it out loud, it struck him as a surprisingly obvious blind spot he'd missed.

So far, there had been two instances of what looked like possession by Petelgeuse. Both had occurred at the moment the body he was riding died, and Subaru hadn't been able to narrow down the conditions for what happened after that — but if he could simply create a situation where possession *never happened* in the first place, that changed everything.

And if the ambush went well, creating that kind of situation might not be all that difficult.

"That's it. Taking him down doesn't have to mean killing him! Sealing him away... I don't know if there's a method like that, but if it's possible—"

*This could work,* he thought.

If they could knock him out with a surprise attack, all they'd need to do was gag him and tie him to a tree. If they isolated him at a distance even the Unseen Hand couldn't reach, that thing would become nothing but a powerless lunatic, screaming into the void. After that, it'd be even better if they could get Puck to freeze him into a state of suspended animation and mount him somewhere in some remote, human-hands-can't-reach corner of the world as a decoration.

"You seem to be wearing the face of someone plotting something wicked, you."

Julius spoke to Subaru, whose mouth had twisted into a villainous grin now that he saw a ray of hope. Riding alongside him, Julius had watched Subaru sink into thought without a word, but he must have noticed the change in expression that signaled a breakthrough.

His fine-featured profile eased into a faint smile, and he lightly smoothed back the hair the wind had tousled.

"If your thoughts have come together, I'd like to hear them. — The judgment you've made, having decided to fight."

"There might be a hole in it so I wanna tighten it up a bit more... but time's precious, so should we have everyone stop again to hear it?"

Either way, several additions and revisions needed to be made to the explanation from before. Calling out to everyone marching along and gathering them back into a circle again would eat up a fair amount of time. Now, with time weighing on his mind and urgency pressing at him, that felt like it would sap everyone's momentum a little — which he didn't want.

"No, there's no need for that. I'll take responsibility for conveying your words."

Shaking his head slightly, Julius declared Subaru's judgment unnecessary. Unable to read the intent behind that remark, Subaru's brow furrowed faintly. And right in front of him, Julius flicked the fingers of his raised right hand lightly, and the next moment — light spilled out.

"Wha— ! You, that's..."

"A faint glow of will filling the air. Surely you've seen this beauty before, haven't you, if you've spent time at Emilia-sama's side?"

Julius smiled at him, as if with pride. Around him, the faint glow drifted and swam, clinging close to him as he rode atop his earth dragon.

And Subaru knew what that light truly was. Again and again, up close, he'd watched it beside the girl he loved.

It was—

"Minor spirits..."

"More precisely, buds not yet possessed of a spirit's rank. — Beings that ought to be called quasi-spirits."

"Quasi-spirit... don't say it like 'quasi-knight' or something."

Reacting to the deliberateness of that particular naming, Subaru managed a reply through a twitching corner of his mouth.

His expression twitched not because he'd compared the smug look radiating from Julius's well-formed face to his own and felt let down — that particular gripe he'd deal with later. What occupied Subaru's mind was Julius's true nature, given that he had quasi-spirits accompanying him — which was to say—

"You — you're a Spiritual Arts User!?"

"'Also a Spiritual Arts User' would be the more accurate way to put it, perhaps. As a knight, I've never neglected honing my swordsmanship either. If you'd prefer to address me more precisely, I'd ask you to call me this—"

Julius closed his eyes at Subaru's astonishment, letting a brief pause build, and Subaru opened his mouth.

Given this flow, given this setup, the next line about to be spoken was surely—

"Archmage—!!"

"A Spirit Knight, and— hm?"

Wrong.

His voice trembling as he traced out the classic line, Subaru got only a puzzled look from Julius in return. Flustered, Subaru waved his hands to cover the redness creeping up in embarrassment, mumbling "ah, no, that's not it" to paper over the blunder.

"Ah— ahem. Right, Spirit Knight! Yeah, Spirit Knight, huh! I see, uh-huh, that's amazing, makes total sense. Yeah, really. Spirits, huh... a Spirit Knight!?"

"I'm gratified you're so impressed. I hadn't expected quite that much of a reaction, though."

Julius gave a wry smile at Subaru, who'd tried to act composed, absorbed and understood it all, and then choked in astonishment anyway.

Julius gently extended his arm forward, and the shimmering lights began gathering there as if it were a perch. Each of them gave off a different colored glow — six kinds of spirits in all — and recognizing that fact, Subaru realized something.

If he commanded six spirits, each a different color of the six —

"No way, does that mean you can use all six systems of magic?"

"— I see. Without any prior knowledge, that you'd notice that point so quickly is rather sharp of you. It seems you haven't spent your time under Lord Mathers idly, after all."

"Nah, I never really got, like, a lesson in magic from Roz-icchi or whatever... ah, wait, there was that little bit in the bathhouse, wasn't there."

Waggling their respective bits at each other while getting a bit of a magic lecture — that felt like a distant memory now. Come to think of it, that was one of those experiences lost to this world too.

Setting aside that muttering only a time-regressor could understand, Julius showed off the spirits gathered on his arm toward Subaru.

"As you can tell, with these children's cooperation I can handle all six elemental attributes. They're still buds — quasi-spirits — for now, but the day will surely come when they bloom into beautiful flowers in full glory. That will be the moment I truly stand as a Spirit Knight, together with them."

"Hate to interrupt while you're basking in it, but do quasi-spirits even have genders? Once they become spirits, is it, like, a setting that all spirits have super self-assertive bodies like Puck? In his case he seemed pretty conscious of being male, or I guess a 'he.'"

In Puck's case, since he seemed to consider himself Emilia's father, there was probably no mistake about his gender. Although sometimes he seemed to carry a complicated feeling of being a mother too, which hinted at no small amount of struggle on his part.

In any case, at Subaru's question, Julius tilted his head as if the answer were obvious.

"One could hardly fail to treat children of such beautiful light as women. What is it you find so puzzling?"

"Man, I hate that this is one of the few points where you and I might actually understand each other! Can we just move things along, this isn't going anywhere!"

"Only a joke. Do try to cultivate a more elegant heart. — Now then. In and Ness. If you would."

At Subaru's outburst, Julius shrugged his shoulders in an affected way, then addressed the two lights perched at his fingertips — a white glow and a black one — and the spirits suddenly began to intensify.

"Quasi-spirits who preside over yin and yang, In and Ness. By coordinating a combination of systemic magics with interference on the two of their mana, one can accomplish something normally considered difficult."

"Something like what..."

"Will conveyed without need for words — that is, the high-level magic of communication known as 'Nect.'"

He closed one eye in a wink, and the light surged forward, dyeing the whole group in its glow. And then, into the mind of Subaru — swallowed up in the light — something suddenly began arriving—

*I'm hungryyy. I'm hungry I'm hungry I'm hungry I'm hungry. Liger's back-fur is so warm, I'm sleepy, but if I sleep I'll get scolded. Tivey gets mad and takes away my snacks right away, that's so mean, and Mimi's the big sister too, y'know, mrrgh mrrgh mrrgh. I wanna eat Appa—*

"SHUT UUUUUP—!!!"

Thought-waves from a distance and depth of heart that shouldn't have been reachable came pouring into Subaru with tremendous force. There was no need to think about whose heart it was — but the wave, surging in like a tide, didn't stop with just one person. Indeed —

*About two more hours to the Mathers domain, and then the fight with the Witch Cult. Visiting Theresia's grave will have to wait until we're back in the royal capital.* / *Dang, the wound's gettin' rough. Not the time to be whinin', but if I can't hold out, might have to get serious.* / *Onee-chan's making the face she makes when she's hungry. Shouldn't spoil her, but if I don't, it'll be a pain. It's because Onii-chan isn't here that I have to go through all this trouble.* / *Can't help it since it's an order, nya, but I'm worried about Crusch-sama. I wonder if she'll make it back to the capital safely. We beat the White Whale too, so doesn't this mean the Royal Selection is basically Crusch-sama's alone to win, nya? Aah, I wanna hurry back and get praised~* / *All hail the Great Chikuwa*

"Who was that just now—!?"

"You. And — let me dampen the empathic connection a little. It may be that your resistance to mana interference is somewhat weak. Too strong an effect is a problem in its own right."

Seeing Subaru on the verge of overload from the flood of information pouring in all at once, Julius instructed the paired spirits to weaken the flow.

Once the influx of others' surface thoughts had settled somewhat, and Subaru confirmed he could now sort through the information, he turned back to Julius once more.

"So, basically this Nect magic thing is... the reason it's okay that it doesn't stop?"

"It's a magic ideally suited for war councils. Its effect neither lasts especially long nor extends over any great range. I should add — sharing consciousness like this to any degree requires a certain measure of trust between the parties. It doubles as a kind of touchstone for whether hearts are truly open to one another. Applying it to combat, though, is difficult."

"Huh. Being able to hear people's inner voice — if you could use that mid-battle, wouldn't that be crazy strong?"

In manga and anime too, it was practically a given that a mind-reader would rank among the strongest characters.

But at Subaru's easy assumption, Julius gave a faint smile and shook his head.

"I did say it was empathic in nature. Too weak, and thought doesn't reach you, as just now. Too strong, on the other hand..."

"On the other hand?"

"The boundary between self and other grows unclear. Dying because you cut yourself down — don't you find that a somewhat frightening thought?"

"Ugh... so that's how it is."

Butting into another person's thoughts apparently carried more risk than one might expect. For all its convenience, there was a difficulty of that kind too, and Subaru gave a small groan — at which Julius added, "However..."

"It's unusual for In and Ness to misjudge the calibration of Nect. Perhaps, Subaru, you have a high affinity with spirits."

"Affinity with spirits, meaning like... good compatibility?"

"Something like that. This is largely a matter of innate disposition. Whether or not one can become a Spiritual Arts User comes down entirely to being close enough with a spirit that it agrees to form a Contract. Has nothing of that sort ever occurred to you before?"

"You say that, but man..."

Since he was holding the reins, he couldn't cross his arms, so he tilted his head repeatedly instead, mulling it over. But to begin with, in the original world Subaru had lived in, the very existence of spirits wasn't even acknowledged. And since coming to this world, there hadn't exactly been an opportunity for some great spirit to swoop in and save the day in a moment of crisis, complete with a dramatic "I refuse to die" moment. As for actual contact with spirits to begin with—

"Best I've got is peeking at Emilia-tan having her little chats with the minor spirits in the mansion garden every morning. With any other spirit, the only one I've ever talked to is Puck... ugh, my track record's way too thin...!"

"If the opportunity never arose, that would explain it. In that case, perhaps once this business is settled, you might ask Emilia-sama to teach you. I think there's a real possibility."

At Julius's words, spoken with a loosening of the lips, Subaru's brow creased.

At that reaction, Julius asked, still every bit the picture of elegance, "Something wrong?" and Subaru, replying "No," looked away from him.

"Just thinking, you're being weirdly cooperative, what's the deal. You got some ulterior motive or something?"

"Spiritual Arts Users are, quite simply, few in number. Even just finding someone with whom I might share the topic is a precious thing. Hence I don't mind the trouble of lending a hand — I, that is."

"If it was something I could use right this second I'd think about it, but this ain't exactly the time or place. ...Well, I'll gratefully take the goodwill for what it is."

At this unexpected possibility handed to him, Subaru nonetheless set aside dwelling on it for later. Whether with a sword or with magic, he'd always been told he had no talent. Part of it was that he couldn't muster much hope for his own talent as a Spiritual Arts User — but mostly, right now, he simply couldn't spare the time for it.

"Anyway, for now, that Nect thing from before — let's use it again, please. Weaker, in a range where it still keeps that telepathy-level convenience. I don't want other people's heads flooding into mine again."

"I've got a fair grasp of the wavelength from before. There shouldn't be a problem. — Nect."

Julius touched a finger to his lips, and the paired spirits dwelling there began to glow.

Right after his affected incantation, the faint light enveloped the surroundings once more, just as before.

*Ah, ah— how's this, everyone, can you all hear me—?*

Timidly, Subaru transmitted his inner voice. Willing it to reach, praying it as he sent it out — if the magic had misfired by some mistake, he'd just be a poor deluded chuunibyou kid convinced he was a mind-reader.

*I can hear you, Subaru-dono.*
*Comin' through fine, don't you worry.*
*Mmm-mmm! I know exactly what you're up to! Trying to read Mimi's heart! You're a spy for the enemy organization!*

More or less the replies Subaru had expected came back. One particular individual seemed to have descended into something resembling a poor, pitiable chuunibyou patient, but he warmly chose to ignore that.

*This is Julius's magic — it's like telepathy, or something along those lines. There's a few things I want to add to what I said before, so hear me out for now. Questions and everything else, feel free anytime.*

Relieved to feel agreement radiating in from everyone, Subaru began laying out his earlier rough draft. It was an amateur's plan, full of holes — but if everyone thought it through together, it would come out better shaped. Never stop thinking, never stop acting, in the search for a solution, in pursuit of the best outcome.

Unable to swing a sword, unable to use magic, unable to do much of anything — that was Subaru's way of fighting, after all.

*So first, the first change: I want a few people to go into the village ahead of everyone else. There, I want you to link up with the insurance I already arranged.*

*Insurance, you say?*

Words that arrived directly in the mind were hard to distinguish by tone of voice, but instead, one could tell the speaker apart by the wave of emotion that came through with it. Naturally, the calm, composed color of that particular wave told Subaru it was Wilhelm — and, in response to a mere voice in his head, he found himself dipping his chin in a nod.

"Whoa, hey, I just went and nodded without thinking. The whole bowing-on-the-phone Japanese-person thing, cropping up here of all places...!"

*Bro, hate to interrupt your weird moment of self-realization, but use the magic properly, would ya. Julius-bou'll get scary if he gets mad.*

*I'm not so narrow-minded as to get angry over something like this. Though I can't help but lower my estimation somewhat regarding the waste of our limited time.*

*I— I'll do it properly! I was literally just about to! Insurance! Talking about the insurance now!*

Feeling Ricardo's and Julius's exasperation wash over him, Subaru experienced the strange sensation of stammering with his mind, before slowly beginning to lay out the plan's outline.

*Before we left the royal capital, I asked Anastasia and Russell-san to spread word around like this: Lord Mathers wants to hire traveling merchants heading to the capital along the highway. He'll buy their cargo at whatever price they name, in exchange for the use of their carriages.*

*—! Subaru, that's—*

*It'll cost a painful amount, probably... but you can't put a price on the villagers' lives. As their lord, that's just the obvious way to think about it, right? Especially since that bastard's off somewhere unaccounted for, useless at exactly the moment he's needed.*

It was a fairly rotten piece of reasoning — if a problem could be solved with money, and it wasn't his own money, there was no reason to hesitate — but Subaru's scheme seemed to have worked out well. In fact, at the village the last time around, no small number of traveling merchants had gathered, lining up their dragon carriages and earth dragons at the edges of the village, making preparations.

— Preparations to load up Emilia and the others, the villagers, all of them at once, and evacuate together.

*I had a letter sent along, in the form of an official document, carried by a messenger who set out from the royal capital ahead of us — one saying the Witch Cult might attack, and asking them to evacuate on the merchants' dragon carriages. I don't know if there's some finicky business here about verifying handwriting or whatever, but I figure Emilia-tan will be able to judge it properly.*

Even so, he expected there would be confusion at first. In truth, even once the letter arrived safely, Subaru knew full well its contents wouldn't be something easily swallowed. Which was exactly why he thought the real problem lay somewhere else entirely.

Namely—

*To get people to reach a quick judgment on the letter's contents, we should send someone who properly grasps the situation, not just the merchants.*

*Glad you're quick on the uptake. Realistically, it's near certain the villagers and the merchants are gonna butt heads. I want to smooth that over and get the evacuation moving fast. Honestly, I know the quickest way would be for me to go there myself.*

Being a familiar face, and having earned a fair amount of trust from the villagers, Subaru was well aware that persuading them wouldn't take him much time at all. With no combat ability of his own, going as a messenger to the village and persuading Emilia and the others at the mansion — sending Subaru was, without question, the smartest move. It was — except that would be a problem.

If Subaru went that way, the fight against the Witch Cult would necessarily be left to the remaining main force. He didn't doubt for a second that they'd lose to ordinary Witch Cult members. But Petelgeuse was a different problem altogether. To face him head-on—

*To go up against an Archbishop of Sin, it has to be me. As things stand... it's only me and Wilhelm-san who can beat that thing.*

*—Would you mind if I asked for your reasoning? Not out of vanity, but I don't believe my own skill would be a burden to Wilhelm-sama's efforts. Still less do I think it wise to send you into the main battlefield.*

It was Julius who immediately raised an objection to Subaru's words.

Subaru understood both the meaning behind his confidence and what backed it up. Certainly, in terms of pure skill, Julius could probably hold his own against Petelgeuse too. But that wasn't enough. Facing that madman, skill and resolve weren't what mattered.

*I don't know what kind of trick it is, but the Archbishop of Sin uses some messed-up technique. Makes whoever it hits lose their sanity, can't fight anymore. I call it mental contamination — and the only ones I can think of who can withstand it are me and Wilhelm-san.*

*Mental contamination, meaning... like the White Whale's mist, nya?*

Ferris's voice came through, and Subaru sent back a thought of agreement.

With that, he turned — without a word — toward Julius riding alongside him.

*That thing that makes you lose your sanity — the White Whale used the same kind of thing. In that fight, there's a track record of who kept their sanity and who didn't... but you never fought the White Whale, so I don't know what your resistance is like.*

*If I were to lose my sanity in the middle of it, I'd become a shackle on the two of you in that fight... is that what you're saying?*

Julius closed his eyes, looking chagrined, but he evidently couldn't come up with a logical counter. His pained expression and the strong emotion of regret came through directly now, and Subaru could feel, painfully, just how much it weighed on him not to have been able to join the fight against the White Whale.

Sensing that staying in direct contact with that emotion any longer would lead to trouble, Subaru turned his face away.

*Anyway, that's how it is, Wilhelm-san. I want you to fight the demon together with me.*

*This body is now Subaru-dono's sword. You wield it, and I shall cut. — Along that path, it would be uncouth of me to question your resolve.*

*What was that, so cool it's dangerous, I'm getting all worked up*

*What— where? What is it—?*
*Onee-chan, please be quiet.*

Having been given lines cool enough to make him want to be held, Subaru trembled, while off to the side the siblings carried on their own little comedy routine.

It had to be said this was a rather unbecoming turn of events for a war council, but this too, he supposed, was simply their group's particular flavor. To begin with, they weren't functioning as an army — just a band of ruffians pointed at the same goal.

The one meant to stand at the front and pull everyone forward was, after all, himself — a listless, powerless, mediocre man who happened to be the worst possible candidate for the role of leader of men. Which was precisely why it worked out this way.

That was exactly why Subaru didn't hesitate, and didn't feel any more burdened than he needed to.

Entrust what could be entrusted, do what needed doing, and somehow make it all work out.

*All right then, let's get this done. — I'm counting on you, leaning on you, putting my whole weight on your backs.*
