Arc 7 Chapter 38: "An Eight-Year-Overdue Reward"

—"Abel pisses me off."

That slogan, Subaru decided, was the key to cracking the "Kaleidoscope," Yorna Mishigure.

After all, she was said to have repeated her treasonous schemes time and again, to the point where even Zikr, that paragon of good character, had branded her someone with a death wish.

In seeking an audience with her, the approach Subaru had pushed forward was this: "A person of appropriate authority wishes to speak with you privately, in hopes of raising the banner of revolt against the current Vollachia Emperor." The three of them were to present themselves at the Crimson Lazuli Castle carrying that authority's personal letter.

To be honest, not a single word of it was a lie. The "current Vollachia Emperor" was none other than the man who'd driven Abel out and stolen his name and position. And the "person of appropriate authority" who wished to speak privately was, properly speaking, the true Emperor who ought to be sitting on the throne — so there was no falsehood in their claim there either.

Their actual goal was simply to get Yorna to accept Abel's letter. If lying through their teeth — eight hundred or eight thousand lies — was what it took, so be it.

"This is what you'd call Natsumi Schwarz's cunning stratagem — !!"

...or so she'd declared, drawing cheers from Al and Medium for her scheming genius.

However—

"—Rebels against His Excellency, you say?"

The pretext Subaru had spun for Yorna was laid bare, and the air of the great hall turned to ice.

The one radiating hostility fierce enough to be mistaken for cold itself was the man who'd been the hottest-blooded presence in the room from the start — Vincent's bodyguard, Kafma Irulux. One look at the harshness in his eyes told Subaru that a peaceful retreat from this place was no longer on the table.

—Never before had she felt the weight of the saying "the schemer drowns in his own scheme" so keenly.

"But surely the bad timing and lack of information-sharing are both Abel's fault...?"

"Silence your tongue. —General Yorna."

Cornered by this absurd predicament, Subaru — busy cursing Abel — was silenced by Kafma. He then drove his sharp gaze forward, straight at Yorna.

"Did you know, and seat His Excellency with these people regardless?"

"—————"

"I would have you answer!"

Kafma raised his voice, but his fury wasn't directed at Subaru and the others, whose tension only tightened further — it was aimed at Yorna, the one who had created this collision course of a situation.

It wasn't that the responsibility lay with Yorna. It was something simpler than that — a sign that he didn't regard Subaru's group as any kind of threat.

Indeed, the fighting spirit radiating from Kafma as he rose to his feet gave Subaru the same taste of a powerful enemy's menace that she'd felt facing Arakiya at the Guaral city hall. At the very least, there was no doubt he was strong enough to be entrusted with escorting the Emperor. From his perspective, it was probably only natural that Subaru — and even Al and Medium — didn't register as worth being wary of.

"The people actually involved get left out of the conversation, huh? Not sure if that's good or bad..." Al muttered under his breath, and Subaru shared the sentiment, but the tense exchange continued, leaving them out of it. Pressed by Kafma, Yorna put her pipe to her lips and exhaled a curl of purple smoke.

Then, swaying her striking hair ornaments, she tilted her head. "Now whatever might you mean by that, hmm?"

"Do not play the fool...! Just as I have repeatedly advised His Excellency — you are dangerous, after all."

"Ye state the obvious, don't ye. My many acts of rebellion, one after another... could it be that my lord here wasn't even aware, hm?"

"—Tch."

A vein bulged on Kafma's forehead at Yorna's attitude, which could be nothing but provocation.

Strange as it was, watching this exchange, Subaru found herself siding more with Kafma. Honestly, from Subaru's own perspective, Yorna's personality was terrible. She'd braced herself somewhat based on the rumors beforehand, but the reality exceeded her expectations for sheer nastiness.

Still, it didn't seem to be a simple plot to hand Subaru's group — mere rebels — over to Vincent and his party, who'd happened to be visiting. If this were a scene of presenting a traitor to the Emperor, the air would hardly be stretched this taut.

Above all—

"—Your tastes remain as revolting as ever."

Vincent's voice rang out, dismissing whatever game Yorna was playing.

At Vincent's cold words, Yorna raised her shapely eyebrows just slightly.

"Ahh, did that not please His Excellency?"

"Fool. To be so one-sided is no different than setting a hound loose on a hare. I have not yet grown so weary of the Empire that I take pleasure in mere cruelty."

Vincent's remark was flat, yet carried unmistakable pressure. Yorna received it coolly, the fox ears among her tied-up hair trembling faintly — what emotion that betrayed, Subaru couldn't guess.

But then, Vincent's own inner thoughts were equally unreadable.

"—————"

Face to face, confronted directly by rebels — what storm was raging within him now?

Understanding his heart was harder than imagining the real Emperor's thoughts would have been, precisely because the man before them was known to be a fake. Having overthrown Abel, surely the impostor thought ill of him too, but—

"He can't possibly be foolish enough to let that show on his face..."

"—You."

"—!"

Vincent's black eyes pierced straight through Subaru, who caught her breath. Instantly forbidden even to look away, Subaru found herself locked in a staring match with a great enemy, utterly unprepared.

However it had been fashioned, the face was an uncanny match for the real one. Genuine or false, both shared those sharp black eyes that seemed to see straight through everything before them, and that made Subaru's chest itch with an unbearable irritation—

"If you have something you wish to say to me, then say it."

"...What an infuriating look in your eyes."

"Wha—!?"

"Ah! No, I didn't mean— That's not what I meant! That just slipped out on impulse!"

With the worst possible timing, the fake Emperor's question and Subaru's leaking resentment overlapped perfectly. At the outright abuse hurled at the Emperor, Kafma froze, utterly unprepared for such a thing. Subaru waved her hands in a panic, while Vincent himself closed one eye and fell silent.

He looked surprised, but there was also something appraising in his gaze — the same quality Subaru had seen directed at her by the real Abel, back in the Buddheim Jungle, in the Shudrak settlement, and at the Guaral city hall.

"Um, in any case, we..."

Behind her, Al and Medium watched the situation with bated breath. Subaru's shocking outburst must have put considerable pressure on them too — the air of the great hall had frozen solid and taut enough that one more slip of the tongue would shatter it to pieces.

To begin with, the situation they were in was simply too dire. Yorna, of course, but Kafma too — neither seemed like an opponent Subaru's group could hope to match. Wouldn't it be the wiser move, then, to withdraw their claim to Yorna, bow their heads and insist the whole "rebel" business was nonsense, and retreat even if it left the mood sour?

Resolving to do just that, Subaru began to loosen the corners of her mouth into an ingratiating smile—

—and noticed Yorna's gaze, fixed steadily on her.

"—————"

Pipe still to her lips, Yorna watched Subaru's movements in silence. It was an ambiguous gaze that could be read as either indifferent or not — as uncertain as the rising violet smoke, a phantom that vanished the moment one tried to grasp it.

To rely on that was, Subaru thought, thoroughly foolish.

But she sensed instinctively that this was the watershed moment. That look — on the verge of losing interest in Subaru's group entirely — wasn't the look of someone eccentric enough to reconsider something once she'd let it go. Whatever claim was abandoned here would never be scooped back up. In other words, Yorna Mishigure's cooperation would be lost, permanently.

And that was equivalent to sealing off, in darkness, whatever fragile path to victory remained.

Therefore—

"—Answer with care. Tell me what it is you have to say to me."

"That would be—"

At Vincent's prompt, Subaru took a beat too long — far longer than a mere pause — before lifting her face.

The fake Emperor's eyes narrowed; beside him, Kafma too turned his attention fully on Subaru. Behind her, the tension in Al and Medium swelled, and Yorna held the smoke she'd drawn into her lungs. Catching these changes at the edge of her vision, Subaru fixed her eyes on Vincent and spoke.

"Just as Lady Yorna says. We are here to declare war upon you."

Yes — as though clinging to a position she could not retreat from, a piece she could not surrender. Rem, waiting for her return in Guaral; Abel, absent from this place; Al and Medium, braced behind her — all of them had entrusted this judgment to Subaru. So that Natsuki Subaru would not misjudge the weight of that trust, and would not throw it away—

"—————"

Told to his face that he was to be treated as an enemy, Vincent's black eyes trembled just slightly. But whether that corresponded to any of the four cardinal emotions was beyond Subaru's power of insight to read. All she could feel was her own tongue drying rapidly with the heat of the words she'd just spoken.

Of course it would. Unlike Abel, who had lost his actual authority as Emperor of the vast Vollachia Empire, the fake standing before her held that power firmly in hand, like a spear.

In fact, if Vincent hadn't raised a small hand to stop him, the outburst Subaru had just uttered would surely have made Kafma explode, and her life would have scattered to pieces. But that hadn't happened. Vincent hadn't let it happen.

"Kufu..."

Looking down from her seat of honor at the standoff between Subaru and Vincent, Yorna gave a small chuckle in her throat. Smoke leaked from the corner of her mouth as she suppressed her laughter, shoulders shaking — she looked thoroughly amused. Subaru's life-risking declaration had, at the very least, succeeded in relieving her boredom.

"What amuses you, Yorna Mishigure?"

"Firstly, that our guest did not withdraw her bold words. And in addition, these three might well be putting His Excellency's own safety at risk... what will you do about that, I wonder?"

"You are an unmanageable woman. Even you, who make it your creed never to obey me, must understand my intent."

At Yorna's provocative sidelong glance, Vincent responded without a flicker of expression change. He then turned his black eyes once more to Subaru, who had just declared her hostility to his face.

"This is a land of sword-wolves. Only one with the spirit to aim for this very throat may truly call themselves a citizen of the Empire."

"...How very generous of you."

"Hmph."

Vincent snorted, dismissing Subaru's sarcasm with a laugh. Even in that response, Vincent's behavior was a flawless trace of the genuine article. Even if Abel himself had been present, it seemed he would have responded in precisely the same way, without the slightest deviation. If anything, it made Subaru want to suspect that the Abel traveling with her group was the fake one. Though in that case, they'd be completely and utterly cornered, so she'd rather not think about it.

"But my head is not worth so cheap a price that I would hand it over to you so easily."

"—...Then what do you intend to do with us?"

"That is the question."

A statement that restrained Kafma at the edge of eruption and acknowledged Subaru's group, who had shown open defiance to the Emperor's face. But picking up from what followed, there was no change in the cold gaze he turned on his enemies. Even if their spirit was valued, there was no reason to go easy on those intent on doing harm.

Slowly, steadily, Subaru's and Vincent's black eyes locked, and the air of the room seemed to scorch.

"Ahh, I too am a sinful woman. Made to watch men fight over my very body like this, it stirs something in me, it does."

"As though it were someone else's affair... In the first place, there are more women than men over there. 'Men' is off the mark."

"Kufu."

At Yorna's remark — bizarrely out of place amid the glaring standoff between Subaru and Vincent — Kafma's pointed correction earned only a mocking chuckle from her throat. That reaction only stoked Kafma's anger further.

"Your Excellency! Command me! Let me deal with those people—"

"—Now, Kafma, you're the one, ain't ye. Been makin' a right racket for a good while now."

Impatient with the stalemate, Kafma tried a direct appeal to Vincent. But the voice that cut him off belonged to neither Vincent nor Yorna — it was someone else entirely.

"Ah..."

Subaru let out an involuntary breath, turning toward the source of the voice. That was how completely outside her awareness the speaker had been — she was startled just by the fact that he'd spoken at all.

It was one of the three whom Vincent had brought along as bodyguards — not Kafma, but a small figure sitting cross-legged, quite at ease, on the wooden floor.

"When His Excellency does the thinkin', most things turn out better than if we did the thinkin' ourselves, don't they? So if we go on yappin' about it, we're just gettin' in the way, plain and simple, that's the honest truth."

The one who said this was a wrinkled old man with white hair and long, drooping white eyebrows. Both his tone and the content of his words were steeped, deliberately, in an air of "this is such a bother." Looking at him now, his appearance was striking enough that Subaru couldn't fathom why she hadn't noticed him until this moment.

—No, he had been within her field of view. He simply hadn't registered in her awareness.

Presumably, this meant he'd been erasing his presence in the truest sense of the word.

Kafma turned back toward the old man who'd pointed this out. "But surely removing His Excellency's troubles is also a loyal retainer's duty, Elder Olbart!"

"When ya start callin' yerself a loyal retainer, don't that sound like the first step toward gettin' purged for actin' on yer own say-so? I don't much fancy that — carvin' up the neck of a young fella with his whole future ahead of him."

"Guh...!"

Slowly shaking his head, the old man — addressed as Olbart — dug into his ear. Whatever pressure Kafma felt from that gesture, his cheeks stiffened.

But Kafma wasn't the only one whose cheeks stiffened. Hearing the name spoken, Subaru's own cheeks tightened just the same.

"Elder... Olbart..."

"Oh? That's me, but d'ya know me? I'm a fair bit of a somebody, y'know."

"...Well, if you're aware you're a fair bit of a somebody, then I'm sure you are."

Her lips trembling as she voiced the name, the very Olbart in question reacted to it. Seeing Subaru's shocked expression, that reaction alone confirmed it. She'd heard Olbart's name at the Guaral city hall, and then again on the road toward the Mist Capital.

From here on, in Subaru's campaign to bring down the Empire, there was one thing she absolutely could not avoid: the "Nine Divine Generals." One of them was Yorna. And—

"'Vicious Elder' Olbart Dunkelkenn...!"

"That name, I ain't too fond of, y'know. 'Vicious Elder' — bit much, don't ye think? Do I really look like such a rotten old geezer to ye? Well, if someone asked me straight to my face, I couldn't rightly say I don't. Couldn't say that, no — kah-kah-kah-kah!"

Opening his mouth wide, the old man laughed, showing teeth remarkably even and white for his age.

But Subaru couldn't bring herself to laugh at all. —With the goal of winning Yorna to their side, they'd ended up encountering the fake Emperor Vincent, and on top of that, he'd brought Olbart along. Which meant that, at this very moment, Olbart too counted among the enemy's forces.

"Arakiya, and Chisha, and now Olbart..."

Subaru wanted to clutch her head at this apparent sniper-precision targeting of the "Nine Divine Generals" already claimed by the enemy side — and, worse, all of them high-ranking ones at that. The problem wasn't only what lay ahead; the situation right in front of her was deteriorating at an accelerating rate too.

Kafma alone had already been more than they could handle, and now the Nine Divine General Olbart had joined in as well. To gather this many bad conditions together, one would think it took deliberate effort—

"—Hey there, old man. Remember me, by any chance?"

"Ehh, what's that?"

Sweat beading on her forehead trickled down her cheek and dripped from her chin.

Amid that surging tension, a voice caught Olbart off guard enough to raise his eyebrows. It belonged, of all people, to Al, reaching a hand out from behind Subaru.

Al leaned forward, and Subaru's eyes went wide with astonishment. "Al?"

"What's gotten into you all of a sudden... every single move counts right now, you know that?"

"You keepin' up the lady-talk in this situation is plenty weird too, Natsumi-chan, but I'm not just jumpin' out here without thinkin'. Well — not that I thought it through exactly, but... hey, old man! It's me, it's me!"

"That sounds like some kinda 'it's-me' phone scam..."

It would be careless to assume that a con man's patter would work on someone just because he was old. Indeed, at Al's call, Olbart tilted his body and gave a low "Hmm~?"

"Well now, that's a fair peculiar getup, ain't it. Even bein' an old geezer myself, if there was a fella runnin' around lookin' like that, I reckon I wouldn't forget him easy. You and me, seriously acquainted?"

"Might not be 'acquainted' exactly, and last time we met I wasn't wearin' this getup. But I didn't have an arm back then either, and we had ourselves a bit of a chat."

"Me, chattin' with a fella missin' an arm...?"

"Yeah, that's the one. —Little miss Arakiya was there too."

Lowering his voice slightly, Al spoke the name of Arakiya — that terrifying girl, another of the "Nine Divine Generals," who had thoroughly stirred up trouble for Subaru's group. It meant absolutely nothing to Subaru, but the moment Olbart heard it, his eyebrows snapped upright and he let out a bright "Ohh!"

"You're that fella! The one who took back the island alongside Araki! Come to think of it, that get-up does look kinda similar, don't it. Kah-kah-kah-kah, so you're still alive, are ye!"

"Oh yeah, alive and kickin'. Managed to keep the stars turnin' my way, somehow."

"And now, by some twist of fate, ye end up crossin' swords with His Excellency, do ye? Shoulda talked ye up a bit more on His Excellency's behalf back then, maybe. Ah well, my mistake, my mistake."

Olbart laughed heartily, exchanging words with Al in an easygoing manner. Unable to grasp the relationship between the two of them, Subaru could only blink in confusion. Whether things were turning for the better or worse right now was more than she could judge.

"—Olbart. A face you know?"

The one who seemed poised to settle that question was Vincent, cutting in.

At the arms-crossed fake Emperor's question, Olbart answered, "Oh, that's right," and continued,

"Two, three years back, right after His Excellency took the throne, there was that time rebellions broke out all over the place at once, weren't there?"

"Elder Olbart, His Excellency's enthronement was already eight years ago..."

"Eh? Wasn't it more like three years? Whoops, whoops — anything from the last ten years or so just feels recent to me, so I go and mess it up like that."

"Never mind that — continue. Eight years ago, what happened?"

Vincent corrected Olbart's course as he kept veering off on tangents. Following that correction, Olbart said "Right, so," and pointed at Al.

"Back then, there was that rebellion that broke out in Ginunhive... and the ones who put a stop to it were that helmeted fella there, and Araki. That's the way of it."

"Hoh."

Hearing Olbart's account, Vincent's interest turned toward Al for the first time. It was hard to tell which way the needle swung, toward favor or disfavor, but at least it seemed to be work that wouldn't lead straight to summary execution for insolence.

With that, Al took a step forward, coming up level with Subaru.

"With all due respect, Your Imperial Excellency — old man Olbart there probably knows already, but... the truth is, eight years ago, I did you a service, and I still haven't gotten my reward for it."

"Said he didn't need it, that guy did."

"Olbart, be silent. —Continue, jester."

"—I'd like to collect that reward, today."

Subaru had the illusion of hearing the very air of the great hall freeze solid with a sound.

Boldly, audaciously, Al was demanding — eight years overdue — the reward for his own accomplishment. His sheer nerve, combined with the coincidence that the very person who knew of Al's deed happened to be present, made for a gamble genuinely worth staking everything on.

Vincent — the fake Emperor who, upon hearing Subaru's declaration of war, hadn't opted for immediate execution — had both a reason and the resolve to conduct himself as the Emperor of Vollachia. And if Abel's words held true, his creed included the principle of "sure reward, certain punishment."

If so, then—

"What do you seek? My head?"

"If you were willin' to just hand it over, that'd sure make me a hero, but askin' for somethin' like that takes more nerve than I've got. So..."

As he spoke, Al glanced back over his shoulder at Subaru. Sensing what was being asked of her, Subaru pulled the envelope from inside her robes.

The personal letter containing Abel's message — delivering this had been their goal from the very start.

"This letter — delivering it to Lady Yorna is our purpose here. If you would reward my companion's service, I ask that you permit this."

"A personal letter."

"A love letter... from our lord, to Lady Yorna."

Hesitating over the word "lord," Subaru inwardly stuck her tongue out at the words that followed. At the mention of a love letter, Yorna, up in her seat of honor, gave a "kufu" of laughter from her throat. It seemed to have caught her interest. And Vincent's reaction wasn't bad either.

Narrowing his black eyes, Vincent pondered for only a few seconds before speaking.

"Eight years late as it is — well done, on the matter of the labor colony island."

"Oh—"

"If you desire it, I shall grant you a reward. That personal letter of yours — you may give it to Yorna Mishigure."

Jerking his chin, Vincent indicated Yorna.

For a moment, it took time to process the intent behind Vincent's words. But once they realized it meant Al had won his gamble, Subaru's group exchanged glances.

It had been a make-or-break, all-or-nothing wager, and Al's decision to strike had been the right call, they thought—

"—However."

"Uh?"

Just before they could raise their voices in shared celebration, Vincent held up a single finger and spoke once more.

To Subaru's group, who turned to look, the fake Emperor spoke with his black eyes sinking into a deep, dark shadow.

"What I permit is the delivery of the letter. You understand what that means, do you not?"

He said it as though driving the point home.

Hearing that, Subaru's eyes widened, then she immediately clenched her back teeth and turned around. Her gaze went to Yorna, who was watching the situation with a smirk. Delivering the letter to her and receiving her reply — that was their actual goal.

"Should we deliver the letter, when might we receive Lady Yorna's reply?"

"Now, let me see..."

Asked by Subaru, Yorna let her gaze wander into empty space for a moment. Then she turned her pipe upside down, tapping the ash into a jar her attendant, a hairless little servant, held ready.

"I too am a woman... I've no wish to be seen tearin' open a love letter in haste the moment I receive it. And so... once our guests have left my castle, I should like to take my time readin' it through, and pen my reply at leisure."

"—...You'll read it, once we've left the castle?"

"I am mistress of this Mist Capital. I'll speak no falsehood before my own attendants."

The light held in her blue eyes — whether sincerity or mischief, Subaru, not knowing her, had no way to judge. But there was no other option left to choose. If, for instance, they changed the terms of Al's reward to something like safe passage out of the castle, Vincent likely would have allowed that too.

But that plan, just like before, would mean severing any chance of allying with Yorna. In other words—

"Al, Medium-san."

Before her resolve could fully settle, Subaru called the names of her two companions.

For what was about to happen — for what needed to be done — their cooperation was essential. So before taking action, it was only right to secure their consent. Turning, Subaru met their eyes; Al and Medium each gave a nod.

"Well, I did already tell the boss I'd lend a hand."

"And I'm asked by big brother too! Take care of Natsumi-chan, he said!"

Al with his head tilted, and Medium with her forceful reassurance.

Given courage by their answers, Subaru nodded back to them in turn.

Then, Subaru walked slowly forward, toward the front of the great hall — toward Yorna, who leaned against her armrest in the seat of honor, drawing on her pipe.

Up close with that bewitching beauty, amid the drifting sweet fragrance, Subaru held out the letter.

"Here — a personal letter from our lord."

"Much obliged for your trouble. Though mind ye, from here on is where things get truly difficult."

"Yes, I'm well aware."

Graceful fingers accepted the letter, and words laced with a smile cursed Subaru's group's future. But Subaru met those words with composure, and turned back around.

And then—

"—Your Imperial Majesty. With all due, terrible impudence — your throne. We shall be taking it."

She declared it, head-on, an even more unmistakable declaration of war than before.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△

The moment after, what happened was dramatic.

"Well roared, you insolent wretches—!!"

Straight after the declaration of war, Kafma's eyes flew wide open. Receiving Subaru's utterly disrespectful, maximally insolent proclamation, Kafma Irulux moved to prove his loyalty to the Emperor with his own strength.

—The result: the coat sleeves on both of Kafma's outstretched arms burst open, and countless brambles surged toward Subaru.

Deep green thorned vines, writhing like serpents, surged toward Subaru, their overwhelming mass swallowing her entire field of vision. An inescapable area-denial attack — each individual vine equipped with thorns as thick as Subaru's own arm — a killing technique meant to wring its prey dry, squeezing out the very last drop of life.

An assault that seemed to permit no reaction whatsoever, swallowing her body into the brambles as easily as a snake gulping down a mouse—

"—Well said, partner."

Braced for her whole body to be run through by thorns, for agony beyond imagining.

But what struck the tensed-up Subaru wasn't pain — it was the impact of falling backward onto her rear, and Al's voice as he stepped in front of her, catching the brambles on his dao blade.

Shielding Subaru from behind, Al deflected the enemy's attack with the thick, drawn blade. He couldn't block everything — blood was already oozing from his shoulder and side.

Even so, Al had protected Subaru from that overwhelming mass.

"Wait, where's Medium-san!?"

"Whoa-ahh! That was close! If I hadn't done just what Al-chin said, I'd be dead!"

Right beside her, Subaru turned in a panic to find Medium, twin swords drawn, answering in a loud voice.

Looking over, she saw Medium holding her paired blades high and low, slicing away parts of the brambles closing in from all four directions, or blocking them, stomping them down, warding them off completely.

Relieved by both her evident skill and the fact that she was unhurt, Subaru let out a breath. But it was too soon to relax. After all, this was still only the first wave—

"My, what an exaggerated bit of stagecraft."

"—!"

Behind the braced Subaru's group, Yorna — who should likewise have been within range of the brambles — stood there, letter still in hand, having drawn her attendant close against her body, exhaling violet smoke.

Traces showed that brambles had surged toward her surroundings too, but they had — as though deliberately aimed to avoid her — twisted unnaturally, as if a round empty space existed around Yorna alone.

Whether that was Kafma's deliberate bending of the attack, or something Yorna herself had done, was impossible to determine, but—

"Forgive the disturbance. We shall take our leave now."

"Do take care on your way out. Should you three fail to leave the castle..."

"The letter won't be read. Understood, I do believe."

Spelled out once more, however poor her perception, even Subaru understood Yorna's intent. The wager Vincent had deliberately made explicit—

"Our wager is whether we make it out of the castle. —Al, Medium-san!"

"Right!"
"Yessir!"

Right after those forceful replies, Subaru roared out at the top of her lungs.

"Right side!!"

Hearing it, her two companions instantly grasped Subaru's judgment, and three blades swung as one.

The single-armed dao and the supple twin swords tore fiercely through the brambles, blasting away the mass that had blocked both their sight and their path, and the three of them burst out beyond the green.

Landing on the wooden floor, they gained speed and momentum as they ran.

"You think I'll let you escape!!"

But one step into their run, something the thickness of a thumb grazed past Subaru's nose.

Unlike the brambles, in that fleeting instant it looked almost like a shard of white bone. It hadn't been aimed to merely warn her off — it had been a strike meant to pierce straight through her temple. Had Al not instantly thrust his blade into the projectile's path, she would have died.

"Brambles and now needles, is he some kind of shock-value freak show!?"

"That's the 'Insect Cage Tribe'! They attack usin' insects they keep inside their own bodies!"

"Like some kind of hornet clan!"

Trading pointless bickering, Subaru kept her posture low, advancing while covered by Al and Medium. Their destination wasn't the great hall's exit — heading that way would mean retracing the long path back to the castle keep. Staying exposed to Kafma's attacks the whole way there was simply impossible. Which meant Subaru's group needed a drastic shortcut.

Therefore—

"Medium-san!"

"Yessir!"

"You can do it!!"

"—I'll do it!!"

The instant she received that cheer, Medium's whole body — without exaggeration — surged, her momentum increasing.

Her long hair streaming behind her, she spun the twin swords in both hands, and in impossibly complex trajectories fiercely batted away the brambles surging in on her.

Of course, some of that was Medium's own skill. But on top of that—

"Right! Right knee! Nape of the neck! That sexy hip!!"

"Uh! Yah! Hyah!"

Al's blood-curdling screams warned Medium exactly where the danger closing in on her was aimed. Medium responded to each in an instant, successfully deflecting or parrying the thorned attacks a hair's breadth before impact.

However far he could see, Al's uncanny survival instinct — the same ability that had proven effective even against Arakiya — apparently worked not just for himself, but for others as well.

Benefiting from that, Medium reached the great hall's window. With a single swing she smashed through the wooden lattice, and her long leg, kicking upward, blasted a hole clean through the wall.

Below, the great hall of the keep opened up — a drop of a good thirty meters or more to the ground — and the Mist Capital's blue sky spread across their entire field of view, wind roughly greeting Subaru's group.

It wasn't even a matter of "win big or lose big" anymore, but—

"—If we get outside the castle!"

Yorna had already taken the letter in hand. Having accepted that condition, Vincent's side surely wouldn't interfere any further. Perhaps that promise had never actually been put in writing anywhere.

But with nothing else to trust, going along with it was the only goal—

"—Natsumi-chan!"

Called by Medium, who'd just smashed through the wall, Subaru sprinted with everything she had, heedless of appearances.

She silently thanked whatever instinct had kept her from wearing a skirt today. If her cause of death turned out to be crossdressing, she'd never be able to face all the pioneers who'd come before her. Forgetting even to keep up her composed expression in this do-or-die moment, Subaru kicked off the floor hard enough to nearly punch through the boards.

Then, catching up to Medium, she and Medium leapt together from the wall to the outside—

"Sorry 'bout this, but I got a job to do too, more or less, y'know."

"Kah— !"

In that instant, an old man appeared as if sprung from nowhere, cutting in directly in front of Subaru and Medium, a thrusting palm-strike driving straight into both their chests.

Struck by the impact, the breath was knocked from them. Taking a direct hit from a "Nine Divine General" — Subaru's consciousness cracked, on the verge of shattering entirely.

However—

"Natsumi-chan! Nothin's wrong! I'm fine!"

"Eh!?"

Called loudly right beside her, Subaru's consciousness — which had nearly slipped away — snapped back. Glancing down at her own chest in a panic, she found no sign of damage from Olbart's thrust. No blood, and no trace of her heart having been extracted without bleeding either.

"Here we go, Natsumi-chan! Don't bite your tongue, 'kay!?"

Subaru's collar, as her eyes spun with confusion, was seized by Medium, who declared this boldly. She then strode over the broken wall in one great step and, without the slightest hesitation, flung herself out of the Crimson Lazuli Castle.

Subaru and Medium burst out into the blue sky, tangled together; the sensation of ascent from the leap vanished in an instant, and a single second later they were flung into the trajectory of a fall.

"U—, kyaaaaaaa—!"

Letting out a shrill scream, Subaru drew Medium close and fumbled at her own waist. From there she drew out a familiar, hand-worn object and, even in midair, readied her weapon — the whip.

Jumping without any preparation at all would have been nothing but suicide.

It wasn't that they'd been able to prepare many winning odds, but between provoking Yorna, declaring war on Vincent, and the exchange of blows against Kafma and Olbart, the gambles had come one after another. Having barely managed to pick up every single one of them so far, all she could do now was believe she could pick up this very last one too.

"C'mon— reeeeach!!"

Holding Medium's slender waist, Subaru swung her arm and released the whip. Its tip stretched out — not into empty air, but toward one of the many beams extending throughout the Mist Capital, the ones strung like a spider's web all across the city, one of which reached even to the outer wall of the Crimson Lazuli Castle.

Catching the whip on it, she meant to use it as a foothold to swing clear of the castle grounds. That was the best plan Subaru had been able to scrape together amid the chaos of the great hall.

"—!"

She felt it catch against her arm, and with all her might Subaru wound the whip around her wrist. This way, even if her grip strength gave out, at worst her wrist bones would break and still support her weight.

Now all that was left—

"Al's— gyaan!?"
"Sorry!"

The instant the whip snapped taut, Al slammed into Subaru's back with tremendous force. It seemed he too had managed to leap clear of the castle, just as Subaru's group had.

Reuniting passionately in midair, the three of them clung together, their combined weight — nearly a hundred and fifty kilograms — entrusted entirely to Subaru's one arm as they dangled.

"Guh, gaaaaaaaah—!"

Trading a genuine scream in place of the shrieking pain in her arm, wrist, elbow, and shoulder — her entire right arm — Subaru's group traced an arc outside the Crimson Lazuli Castle, pivoting on that single beam.

The best move now would be to wait for the swing's momentum to settle, then climb the whip back up to the beam one at a time. Weighing the time that would take against the risk of her right arm dying, Subaru clenched her back teeth—

"—Ah."

Both Al's and Medium's voices overlapped in that instant, right as she was about to summon her remaining strength.

Before she could figure out what that meant, the strain on Subaru's right arm vanished. —No, more precisely, the strain hadn't vanished; the beam the whip was hooked to had been snapped in two.

By the sheer overwhelming mass of the brambles extending from the breached wall of the keep.

"Uh, aaaaaaaaah—!?"

Screaming, trailing the sound behind them, Subaru's group tumbled through the air, tangled together. With the full recoil of the whip's snap on the beam, their bodies flew in an arc, describing a parabola.

Better than falling straight from the keep's height, perhaps, but even so — dropped from over twenty meters — ordinary humans like Subaru and Al had no hope of surviving physically.

Screaming a trailing wail, Subaru searched desperately for some way to turn the situation around, the faces of Emilia, Beatrice, and Rem rising one after another in her mind—

"—————"

—With a tremendous crash, the three of them broke straight through a roof and, tangled together, plunged into the castle town's stables, landing upon a pile of hay below.
