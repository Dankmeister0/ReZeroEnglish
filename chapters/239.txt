Arc 4 Chapter 72: "Bad End 1, 5, 11"

—How many times must my heart be broken before I'm forgiven?

"Well, that's that, then... quite the thankless job, this one."

In the dim storehouse, gazing at the three corpses sunk in a sea of blood, the beautiful figure in black tilts her head slightly.

Standing in a place saturated with the stench of blood, without a single drop of backspray on her — an abnormal degree of skill — and her monstrous composure, not so much as a flicker crossing her face at the carnage before her.

It was plain to see: here was something that ought to be called a monster, wearing the skin of a human.

Walking across a floor slick with fresh blood, the monster strolls among the fallen bodies, examining each with apparent interest.

A massive old man, his arm severed at the shoulder, blood pouring from his throat. A black-haired boy, his stomach split clean across, dead after thrashing about with his insides spilled out.

—And a silver-haired girl, cut diagonally from the left shoulder down through the right hip.

—How many times had he fought against having to see this sight again?

"As a result of the job, this is about as bad an outcome as it gets... now what to do about it."

Pressing a finger to her red lips, the monster murmurs in a voice so leisurely it feels utterly out of place. In the hand not held to her lips, she idly dangles an ominously shaped, blood-stained curved blade — a kukri knife.

Swinging the weapon that had claimed three — no, four lives in this storehouse, the monster called Elsa wears a bewitching smile.

"—Oh my."

Elsa suddenly tilts her head and lightly leaps backward from where she stood.

An instant later, the spot she'd occupied is pierced by a blade of ice erupting straight up from below, and a chain of ice spikes surges after her retreating feet, thrusting upward like fangs, boring in, closing for the kill.

"Now this is..."

"How dare you — how dare you do this."

Before Elsa, dodging the ice blades, a faint light gathers in the air, forming the shape of a small spirit.

The floating cat spirit — Puck — hardens his expression, his androgynous voice trembling with rage.

"You'll regret taking Lia's life —"

"Oh, that child... he was a spirit user? How lovely. I've never gotten to open up a spirit's belly before. —But still."

Floating icicles hover in the air as Puck readies for battle, and Elsa greets the promise of a fight with a rapturous expression, laughing. But before taking her stance, she closes one eye.

"Why didn't you come out before that girl died? A spirit user and their spirit are supposed to be a set of two — it's such a waste if you can't enjoy it to the fullest."

"Keep talking, murderer. —If I weren't bound by contract myself—"

Shaking his head, Puck's expression twists with loathing. Baring his fangs, he thrusts his small hand toward Elsa.

"I have no intention of exchanging words with you. I'll freeze you solid and offer you up, at the very least, to the peace of Lia's soul. After you, the kingdom, the world, the Dragon, the Witch — everything."

"Ohh, how wonderful — do entertain me, won't you!"

Elsa leaps, scaling ceiling and wall like a spider. Icicle after icicle fires toward her slender form, boring into the storehouse walls, freezing the very air, a shrill scream of atmosphere rising up.

The world goes white with mist, and nothing more can be seen.

Not even the corpses of Subaru and Emilia, fallen on the floor, their fingers entwined together by mere chance.

—How many times must the world betray me before I am redeemed?

"Rem only prevented the situation from getting worse. By the time she found him, Subaru-kun was already beyond saving. —He surely wanted the killing blow delivered as quickly as possible."

"And the result... is Rem saying that ending, that awful ending, was the only way? Subaru was my benefactor, and there were surely so many things we still had to talk about... and you—"

Their voices, quarreling — two beloved girls' voices. One half of the pair stirs in Subaru both tenderness and unbearable grief. The other voice — how many times, standing at the edge of despair, had he wished to hear it, to cling to it, to be touched by its gentleness?

The blue-haired girl and the silver-haired girl face each other, an uneasy air hanging over the room. The setting is the mansion's parlor; seated across the table from one another, the two are on the verge of eruption.

"No-o-ow, now, Emilia-sama, let's not get so heated. Wh-y-y don't we first properly li-i-isten to what Rem has to say?"

"Roswaal... do you even understand? Rem — your servant — let Subaru die. My benefactor, and your guest as well... she let him die."

"I u-u-understand perfectly well. Which is exa-a-actly why we must have a proper conversation about this. —So that neither of our feelings pass each other by, ye-e-es?"

At Emilia's words, Roswaal narrows his yellow eyes in response. The clown then glances at Rem, seated beside him, and sensing his gaze, Rem draws in her chin.

"Late last night, there was an intruder in the east wing — on the floor where Emilia-sama's chambers are. Rem, alerted by the Warning Stone, rushed there at once and found Subaru-kun crawling on the floor."

"By then, Barusu was already under the curse's effects, was he."

"Yes, sister, that's correct. He was weakened, in a state near death. His life force had been drained to its very limit by the curse, and I judged there was no longer any way to save him..."

"And so you beat him to death with an iron ball. —Battering his body that relentlessly."

"Emilia-sama—"

The one turning a stern gaze on Emilia is Ram, holding her sister's hand beside her. But Emilia meets Ram's sharp glare with resolute defiance of her own.

"A fact is a fact. ...Subaru's body — his torso and head — were in a terrible state. If the only goal was to grant him a mercy killing, there must have been an easier way. And yet, why—"

"That, is..."

"...................."

Rem falters under Emilia's pressing. She cannot voice what comes next, both because it is not in her nature to lie, and because Emilia's words strike unerringly at her true intent.

At this point in time, Rem still harbored a deep distrust of Subaru. After the second week at the mansion — after beating Subaru to death within its walls and failing to conceal his death — this meeting had presumably been arranged as a result.

And in that period, Rem's hostility toward Subaru, for his easy familiarity with Ram, had only intensified; it could be said she felt no hesitation in carrying her murderous intent through to the act.

—What had been in her mind, up on the mansion's upper floor, as she raised the iron ball against Subaru?

Perhaps that was something even Rem herself could not clearly say.

"—I wanted to hear that your hand slipped, or that you hesitated... that's the kind of answer I wanted to hear."

"—————!"

Murmuring sorrowfully, eyes closed, Emilia's words make Rem's head snap up as though struck.

How much of Rem's truth those words of Emilia's had actually captured, no one could say. And now, it would remain unknown forever.

"Emilia-sama, where are you going?"

Rising, brushing off her hem, Emilia is met by Roswaal's question, his expression now blank.

In response, Emilia smooths her long silver hair with one hand.

"—I'm leaving. It was a short time, but thank you for your hospitality. Without your backing, I can't take part in the Royal Selection. But... I can no longer trust any of you."

"Even without trust, might we not still be of use to one another? It would hardly be the wisest choice to throw a tantrum and storm off now."

"...Tantrum?"

At Roswaal's choice of words, Emilia, who had stopped in her tracks, freezes in visible shock. Then she strides straight up to him.

"————"

No one could stop the dry crack that rang out. Her pale fingers, swung loose, struck Roswaal's wan cheek with full force.

Emilia is left breathless from that single slap, her palm reddening the cheek it struck. Roswaal, on the receiving end, makes no move at all — but instead it's Ram who, face flushing with anger, half-rises from her seat.

"Ram."

"But, Roswaal-sama—"

"It's fine. Stay seated. Emilia-sama, forgive Ram's rudeness."

"You only ever say that to me... but you say nothing at all to Subaru...!"

Biting her lip, Emilia glares at the icily composed Roswaal. Fierce anger swirls in her violet eyes, but Roswaal, the target of that anger, remains utterly unshaken.

His attitude speaks for itself: he is simply looking at this from a different vantage point.

"Leaving the mansion, returning to the forest — what, precisely, would remain within you?"

"I was wrong to have let myself be swept along by your smooth talk. There must have been some other way — some better way to atone. Because I got that wrong, Subaru died."

Closing her eyes at Roswaal's question, Emilia speaks in a quiet voice. Then she shakes her head slightly.

"I'll take his soul with me, and let him rest peacefully in the forest. —For Subaru, and for everyone else, no matter how long it takes, I will devote my time to laying their souls to rest. That's all. This conversation is over."

With no intention of continuing further, Emilia puts distance between herself and Roswaal.

Roswaal's mismatched eyes follow her retreating back, her silver hair swaying as she walks away. Still seated, he half-reaches a hand toward that departing figure — and lowers it again.

"If this differs from what was written, then perhaps... this is where I was always meant to end up, i-i-isn't it."

"Roswaal-sama..."

At his listless murmur, Ram takes his hand with a voice full of concern. The clown glances sidelong at the girl gazing at him with worry, and manages a weak smile.

"Ram, it seems you've won our little wager, ye-e-es. My aim will likely come to nothing now... which means the contract will be fulfilled."

"...Yes. Yes, Roswaal-sama."

Leaving the two of them to their quiet exchange, Emilia rises and heads toward Rem, who has opened the door. Before passing beside her, she notices Rem bowing her head solemnly.

"Take me to Subaru."

"Emilia-sama, that is..."

"I know it's a terrible state. Please make him look as close to normal as you can... I'm taking him to the forest with me."

At Emilia's deeply sorrowful profile, Rem's expression hardens before she looks down. Her expression seems at once full of regret and of anger.

She must be wrestling, surely, with how things came to this.

—How things came to this, no one could ever say.

"I'm sorry, Subaru — there was nothing I could do."

That was the last thing Emilia murmured, quietly, to herself.

—How many times must my own foolishness be thrust in my face before I understand?

"Death, DEATH! Death-death-death-death, DEATH!!"

A shrill, grating cackle rang out.

Chest thrown back, mouth flung wide, drool dripping from the corners of her lips as she howls with laughter — a young woman, her long red hair whipping wildly.

Behavior one would want to look away from in any woman her age, and worse still, her bloodshot eyes stretched wide, her display of madness utterly devoid of anything human.

"To love! In love! By love! Love above all! To repay love with love — that is everything! Ahh! O Witch! O Witch of Adoration! O destination of my love!"

Kneeling, arms outstretched to the sky, tears streaming in torrents, the woman sings the praises of love at the top of her lungs. Scattered around this raving woman lay corpse after corpse, sunk in a sea of blood — limbs torn away, throats gouged out, human dignity trampled underfoot in every one of them.

Among them lay the body of a black-haired boy, who had taken his own life by driving a sword through his own throat.

Throughout Arlam Village, pools of blood had spread everywhere; every member of the armed subjugation party lay collapsed on the ground, their lives stripped from them down to the last drop.

The moment their main fighting force — the Sword Devil — had fallen to a surprise attack, the outcome had effectively been decided.

What remained was a massacre carried out by the "Unseen Hand," and even that chain of death throes had come to an end within a matter of minutes.

"My diligence has brought low those who wallow in sloth! What else should I call that, if not an act of love! Ahh! My devotion, my faith, my unshakable love! Accept it! Receive it! I beg you, envelop me within it——!!"

Screaming of love, weeping, howling amid a sea of blood — the woman, a monster who steals bodies and corrupts minds, Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti.

The madman had wiped out, to the last one, the group of Subaru and the others who had come to save the village, and even now, having lost his own followers, he still cried love's praises at the top of his voice.

And then—

"—What... happened here?"

From beyond the single road leading to the village, a breathless girl came running down and spoke.

Brushing aside, with obvious irritation, the silver hair clinging to her brow, her violet eyes swept over the carnage. —Emilia's eyes went wide at the villagers sunk in the sea of blood, and then she noticed.

"Su... baru?"

Amid the carnage, a boy she knew so very well lay fallen.

What emotion had flashed through Emilia's mind in that instant, no one could say — not even she herself — for the feelings swirling within her wide-open eyes were far too complicated.

Only her lips trembled, quivering, as she spoke.

"Why... is Subaru lying there... eh?"

"Lia—! This is bad, it's the Witch's Cult! An Archbishop of Sin — at a time like this, why!"

Emilia, dazed, unable to fully accept reality. In her place, driven by a rush of urgency, Puck bursts forward.

Flying about Emilia, he glares at Petelgeuse, standing alone amid the carnage, his black eyes filled with sharp wariness and hostility.

"Lia! Now, right now! Get away from here right now! You should never have met that thing — that Archbishop of Sin! The Trial will begin! Once it's imposed, terrible things will happen!"

"Puck...?"

"I remember now, just now, finally! Meeting that thing — that bastard — and I finally remembered! Why did I forget something like this... there's still so much I can't remember... it must have been arranged so I couldn't remember unless it came to this! If that's the case, then!"

Looking up at the sky, stretching his small body to its fullest, Puck screams.

"This isn't what we agreed — ECHIDNA!!"

A voice thick with urgency, with hatred, echoes loudly, and Puck shakes his head, breathing hard. Emilia, faced with such a sudden transformation in someone so close to her, has lost the ability to speak.

And the madman, having caught that cry, slowly rises to his feet.

"Weeell, weeell... a pleasure to meet you, DEATH!"

Tilting his upper body with a swaying motion, Petelgeuse roughly grabs at his own long hair, tearing it out without mercy, blood beginning to trickle from his scalp.

At the sight of this self-mutilation, revulsion and fear flash through Emilia's eyes.

"Witch's Cult Archbishop of Sin, in charge of 'Sloth' — Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti, DEATH!"

The madman cackles, his throat shaking the very air.

Still laughing, the madman tilts his upper body and head at an angle, running his gaze slowly over the frozen Emilia as though licking her with his eyes.

"...Ma, gni, ficent, DEATH."

He lets out a breath of admiration.

A dry sound — clapping. Petelgeuse claps his hands together, applauding toward Emilia.

"Magnificent, DEATH! What a form, so fitting for a Vessel! What features, so reminiscent of the Witch's appearance in life! If such a fresh, vibrant Vessel can be prepared, there is nothing more to say, DEATH! The Trial! The Trial to determine whether the Witch Factor will take root, DEATH!"

"Shut up, you madman! Take one more step toward this girl and I'll make you regret ever being born——! I mean it!"

"Before love, pain and fear alike are but offerings to be given up... nothing, DEATH, could ever be a reason to stop."

Puck spits out his fury at Petelgeuse's ravings, but the madman has no ear for it. Dragging his feet as he approaches, Puck can do nothing, his body trembling.

"Why, why now, of all... no, that's not it. I'm remembering. No. That's right, no, no! No! I... I—"

"Puck! Wh-what should I... what should I do!? I... Subaru's... he's lying right there...!"

"The Trial! Let this be chosen as the destination for a diligent soul! If the Vessel is already filled, it will affect the soul poured into it! The contents — we don't need them — DEATH!"

Puck clutches his head as Emilia calls out to him desperately. And still Petelgeuse does not stop advancing, closing in on the bewildered pair.

Moving the fingers of both hands in some grotesque manner, licking his lips, Petelgeuse sends every alarm inside Emilia screaming to full alert.

At that deranged look in his eyes, Emilia gasps, and in a cracked voice—

"No... I'm scared, Father...!"

It was a whisper, clinging to something.

A small voice calling for help, one that seemed like it would reach no one.

Petelgeuse ignores it entirely, reaching a hand out toward Emilia. From it, invisible to any eye, surely extended the "Unseen Hand," the Authority of Sloth.

To seize Emilia's frozen body and set his depraved scheme into motion——

"—Don't you dare touch my daughter with your filthy hands!!"

In the next instant, a wall of ice erupts before Emilia's eyes with tremendous density and height.

The wall divides the space between Emilia and Petelgeuse, and as if to widen that space further, more walls burst upward one after another, splitting open the ground. Even Petelgeuse, who had reflexively extended his "Unseen Hand," leaps backward.

"This is——!"

"I finally remembered the one thing that mattered most... To protect this girl, contracts, obligations, none of it matters anymore. I just remembered — I've been bound by something meaningless."

To Petelgeuse, his voice trembling with rare agitation, the small floating cat speaks quietly. Having dispelled the confusion from moments before, the spirit glares at the madman with a face now unburdened.

"I finally remembered what all of this was for. To protect my daughter — that's it, finally... and if this is the price exacted for that, damn that wicked woman."

"Puck—ah."

Emilia's throat freezes as she reaches a finger toward Puck, his face looking like he might click his tongue.

At her chest hangs a crystal stone, glowing green — the Anchor of the spirit Puck, a precious stone connecting Emilia and Puck.

Without anyone touching it, it suddenly shatters into pieces.

"No way... wh-why...!"

"Because I... because I broke the terms, the collection has begun. Was even this factored in from the very start... even so."

Turning around, Puck matches his height to Emilia's face.

Emilia's eyes waver in confusion at Puck's actions. Gazing at her, Puck's expression takes on the color of someone looking at something dear to him.

"Lia, it's time to say goodbye—"

"Th..."

"I broke the terms, and now this body can't be tethered any longer. Being by your side, too, is impossible now — the price for it has been taken from me. —I'm sorry."

"No, I don't want that, Puck... because, everyone, everyone's disappearing... even Subaru, right there... nobody's left. Nobody's left! If Puck disappears too, then... I, I'll be alone forever... I don't want that."

Sniveling like a child, Emilia lets the tears fall unbecomingly, one after another.

Puck wipes away Emilia's flowing tears with his long tail, and presses his lips to the nose of his crying daughter.

"You mustn't say such unreasonable things. Ram is still at the mansion. Beatrice is still there. If it comes down to it, rely on Beatrice. That girl surely won't be able to refuse this request. I know that, and it's cowardly of me to ask anyway, but still."

"I! I don't want anyone besides Puck to..."

"—Go now. The most precious, the most adorable, the most beloved girl in my whole world, Emilia."

"Wa—"

Before Emilia could say another word, Puck's small body pressed firmly against her forehead.

Unable to withstand the unexpected force, Emilia's body reels backward — and in that instant, a rift that had opened in space swallows her slender form,

"Eh—"

And in an instant, Emilia's figure vanished from anywhere in the village.

—Having seen this through, Puck lets out a long breath.

"Sorry to make you overexert yourself, Beatrice."

Words of thanks, directed toward his accomplice in that sudden disappearance just now.

Then Puck turns, looking toward Petelgeuse, who had been watching him intently all this while.

"Just standing there quietly watching... quite the well-mannered fanatic, aren't you."

"Should I make a move, it seems you intend to destroy me on the spot, DEATH. Either way, driving you back to the mansion amounts to the same thing. There's no need to needlessly provoke a tiger, DEATH."

"I see. For a lunatic, your head's working better than I'd have thought. —Scum."

Spitting the word out, Puck vaults over the ice wall and heads toward Petelgeuse.

Petelgeuse, receiving him, doesn't do anything so crude as launch an "Unseen Hand" attack midway either.

The two face off, keeping a fixed distance from one another.

"There's no time. —Let's get this started, and get it over with quickly. As for everything after, I've tossed it all onto my reliable little sister."

"Your demeanor seems to have changed, DEATH. Rather human, for a spirit."

"—I suppose it would."

Rubbing his pink nose with a small hand, Puck laughs cynically.

"I look like this now, sure, but originally I had a bit more in the way of arms and legs, and a handsome face to boot. Makes sense, doesn't it, when your daughter's that cute?"

"...A statement I find difficult to comprehend, DEATH."

"Ah, that's fine. You don't need to get it... because you're going to die right here."

As he speaks, Puck's body, both hands raised toward Petelgeuse, begins to turn white.

His mana is running dry, and he's becoming unable to sustain his physical form. This was partly the effect of his connection to Emilia having been severed, and perhaps also the effect of breaking the terms he himself had spoken of.

Either way, his form, his outline, grows hazy and indistinct—

"Before I vanish, I'll erase you. Dying alongside a fanatic — not exactly a pleasant thought."

"A pity, DEATH, but even should this body perish, I will not be extinguished——"

"Soul and all, I'll freeze you right here. —Then what happens?"

Petelgeuse's expression, which until then had kept its fearless smile intact, freezes over.

At the madman's widened eyes, Puck laughs, thoroughly delighted from the bottom of his heart.

"Ah — that's the face I wanted to see, you idiot."

In that instant, as the spirit's outline comes undone, a white radiance bursts forth——

—Shown one ended world after another, Subaru lay sprawled on the ground.

He no longer even knew clearly where he was.

Was he in reality, or in a dream? Was he watching some repeating nightmare? Was he even permitted to dismiss it simply as a nightmare and be done with it?

Was it merely a world of possibility? Or had it truly happened? Or was this just Subaru's own mind conjuring up convenient worlds again? If so, then what was this world into which information Subaru clearly had no way of knowing kept flowing?

A world born of delusion? Or was one reality encroaching upon a different reality? Whichever it was, the suffering inflicted upon Subaru's heart was immense.

So immense that he could not stand up straight, could not even lift his face.

And so—

"Can you no longer even stand, Subaru-kun?"

He heard someone standing beside him, gently trying to scoop up his heart.

It felt, somehow, like the voice of someone dear to him.

"—————"

Down Subaru's cheek trailed a single, hot tear — one that should never have been able to fall.
