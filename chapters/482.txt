"――kh!"

Fluttering his long hair, the enemy dodged her desperate attacks as if crawling along the ground.
Glaring with sharp canines bared was the Sin Archbishop of 'Gluttony', who called himself Lye Batenkaitos.
Hearing the name of a Sin Archbishop caused a hazy, disgusting feeling to swell within her chest.
That was because about a month ago, in the Watergate City of Priestella, they had clashed with multiple Sin Archbishops, resulting in various conclusions, various postponements of conclusions, and an absolute mountain of terrible hardships.

So many sad things and painful feelings had been born.
Their alleviation and resolution had still not been achieved even now, which was why everyone was trying their absolute hardest.

"Why do you people have to do this――!"

"Why? Why would that be? Why could it be? Why is it? Why do ya think? Why might it be, huh? Why would that be, really? Why why why, 'cause that's just how it is!"

There was no serious ring to his mocking words; the opponent had no intention of offering a proper answer.
Sensing that, she put strength firmly into her cheeks. If they could talk things through, that would be best. But if the opponent wouldn't respond to dialogue, she wouldn't hesitate beyond that.
That decisive shift was clearly made in this very moment.

"――Icicle Line."

"Whoa."

Simultaneously with the chant, the sound of air freezing rang out behind her as she held her ice swords at the ready. It was proof that countless ice stakes with sharpened tips had been born in midair, prepared to be fired.
Looking at that, however, Lye's composure still didn't crumble. Without any sentimentality toward that fact, she simply followed her frozen fighting spirit and initiated the attack.

"――――"

The number of ice stakes unleashed in a single breath didn't quite reach a hundred, but there were enough to draw close to it.
Filling the passage that appeared to be made of stone, ice dust danced as the floor, walls, and ceiling served as points of impact, the white, freezing air dealing immense damage to any existence struck directly.
However――

"Sorry, Onee-san. I won't say I'm sick of seeing that... but I've seen it before. See, we're geniuses, so we can't really let an attack we've seen once hit us!"

Swinging the daggers strapped to both hands, Lye sneered, having completely weathered the tremendous onslaught of ice.
She gasped at his skill, and at the fact that he had endured it unharmed. However, if the first time was fended off, then a second time, a third time, and if that still didn't work, even more.

"Think you can win if you do it a hundred times? Even if you do it a thousand times, you won't reach me, y'know?"

"Then I'll just do it ten thousand times! I won't let you go where everyone else is!"

"Ahaha! Now that's on a grand scale! Boy oh boy, you're really something else!"

Putting a hand to his forehead, Lye let out a sigh split equally between exasperation and irritation. Watching him, she quietly held her breath, intending to follow through on her own words.
They were words spoken in the heat of the moment, but all she had to do was act accordingly.
A hundred times, a thousand times, ten thousand times. What everyone else couldn't do, she would do herself.

So, just as she was about to step forward to do that――

"――Lye Batenkaitos!!"

"――――"

From behind, a voice that belonged neither to herself nor to Lye echoed down the passage, causing her feet to stop involuntarily. Turning around immediately, drawn by the voice, a figure emerging from around the corner leaped into her field of vision.
It was a boy with black hair and slightly scary-looking eyes. With a valiant expression, he came running onto the battlefield, looking at Lye and herself fighting in the passage.

"Ah..."

Instinctively, she thought to herself, *Oh no.*
She had to say something, yet for a beat, a blank void formed in her words. That was caused by all sorts of emotions welling up inside her upon seeing the boy who had rushed to her side.
However, before 'bewilderment' could surface in the boy's black eyes as he looked at her, she panicked, realizing she had to speak, and moved her lips.

"It's dangerous, so stay back! Um, you might, well, not know who I am, but that's the enemy! Leave this to me! Even though you might not know who I am!"

She wanted him to leave this place to her, and she didn't want him to think too much about who she was.
Because at least right now, in this moment, if she thought about anything other than fighting, she felt like she would burst into tears.
Because she didn't want to show such weakness and cause trouble for everyone.
And yet――


"――It's all right, Emilia-tan."


The boy who had rushed over called her name with just a single phrase.
With just that, the anxiety, the sorrowful thoughts, the emotions she couldn't afford to dwell on that she had been trying so hard to keep from spilling out of her chest—all of it burst away.
Because――


"――My name is Natsuki Subaru. Emilia-tan's number-one knight!"


Right now, 'Emilia's' chest was beating so, so hotly.
