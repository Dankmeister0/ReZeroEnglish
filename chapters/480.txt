Arc 6 Chapter 74: "Natsuki Subaru"

Short black hair, a stubby torso and short legs, sanpaku eyes that looked like they could murder someone.
Every last feature of it was familiar to the point of disgust, and it stood there looking down at Natsuki Subaru, who sat slumped on the white ground.

"――"

Blankly, fixedly, he stared at the other's face.
However he looked at it, it was a face he knew — no, it looked just slightly different. That was probably because it wasn't a face he was truly used to seeing.
The face people are used to seeing, day in and day out, is overwhelmingly the one reflected in a mirror or on water. A human face isn't strictly symmetrical, so that alone produces a subtle discrepancy.
Which was why—

"Ah, right. It's not a mirror image, so it feels a little off. In that sense, I guess this is closer to looking at a photo or a video recording of yourself."

"――!"

"――? Oh, wait, were you thinking the same thing?"

At the voice that seemed to have read his inner thoughts, Subaru's cheek stiffened, and the other caught the meaning at once.
That too set his ■ churning, but talking back wouldn't do any good either way. To begin with, what mattered to Subaru wasn't this immediate exchange.
There was something bigger, something that shook everything to its foundations—

"So then, let me start over... yo, brother."

"――"

"No, wait, 'brother' might give the wrong idea. Let's be precise here... yo, other me."

The figure raised a hand and greeted him in a light tone — no, "figure" was far too distant a word for what stood there. Even "close acquaintance" would be too lukewarm a term for this relationship.
Because what stood there was, unmistakably, none other than 'Natsuki Subaru' — wearing the very same face as Natsuki Subaru.

"――'Natsuki Subaru.'"

"...Doesn't that sound faintly weird, though? And calling myself by my full name is kind of... but I honestly don't know what else would be the right way to refer to it. It's a situation that comes up in manga and stuff often enough, but doing it for real, it's actually pretty tricky."

"'Natsuki Subaru'...!"

Anger rose in Subaru at 'Subaru' piling one carefree remark on another, and he pushed himself up to his feet. He lunged to grab the other's collar, but his legs tangled beneath him.
His knees gave out, his posture crumbled—

"Whoa, careful."

"――! Don't touch me!"

'Subaru,' standing right in front of him, caught Subaru's body as it pitched forward. The instant he felt that arm against him, Subaru was struck by an unbearable revulsion and shook the arm off.
He took a step back, then another, putting distance between himself and 'Subaru,' and glared at him.

"Why are you here...? For that matter, where even is this?!"

Keeping 'Subaru' in view, Subaru gestured with his hand at the white world around them.
A white space, an empty place — it was the very image of the Od Lagna cradle where he'd confronted the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony, Rui Arneb.

"Damn it...!"

His mind was a jumbled mess.
In the real time flowing through the tower, it hadn't even been hours since he'd run into Rui.
But to Subaru, who had run through so many attempts against the five trials, it felt like something from the far, distant past.

On top of that, Subaru had relived 'Natsuki Subaru's' life and death through the Book of the Dead more than twenty times over. Some entries in the Book of the Dead raced through life and death in less than five minutes; others held stretches of over a year where nothing happened at all.
He had devoured them one after another in quick succession. There was no way his sense of self could stay intact after that.

The passage of time was hazy, and on top of that, an utterly unexpected face-to-face meeting had been waiting for him.
Subaru's eyes went wide, and he flailed his arm wildly as he shouted—

"Why the hell am I in a place like this?!"

"――That's proof that you caught up to me."

"――"

"You read the Book of the Dead and caught up to me. Everything you didn't know, you should've seen it now, relived it. My life in another world."

"...Huh."

To Subaru, shouting breathlessly, 'Subaru' replied in a flat, even tone. Watching that unruffled attitude, Subaru clenched his back teeth.
That composed face, that air of having everything figured out — none of it, none of it sat right with him.

More than that — what had this infuriating face just said, exactly?

"I caught up to you?"

"That's right. There's not one single thing about me left that you don't know. So—"

"――Don't screw with me!!"

"――"

"I caught up to you? Give me a break! Don't lie to me! Not yet! I still don't understand — the most important thing, the thing that matters most, I don't understand any of it!"

Eyes bulging, Subaru roared and this time succeeded in grabbing 'Subaru' by the collar.
'Subaru' didn't resist the momentum. Subaru hauled him in close, until they were near enough to feel each other's breath, and glared into those black eyes.

"――"

Reflected in the black eyes of that face so exactly like his own was that very same face — and the instant he registered it, a nauseating self-loathing welled up inside him.

Whether that self-loathing was aimed at himself, or at the 'himself' standing before him, he didn't know the answer, and he didn't try to think about it.
He simply glared at point-blank range, baring his teeth.

"Tell me! If you're here, this works out perfectly! Tell me! There has to be a reason, somewhere, that you became you! I haven't seen it. I couldn't find it. So—"

"The reason I became me?"

"That's right! There has to have been a trigger, a moment that made you into you. You, you—"

"――That's something you should've seen too, shouldn't you?"

Still held by the collar, 'Subaru' looked back at him without resisting.
He didn't even try to shake off the arm being used to rattle him. It gave Subaru the illusion of not being taken seriously at all.
It felt, precisely, as if he were being looked down on from one step higher up—

"Stop looking at me like that!"
"――Guh!"

Subaru drove his fist into that composed profile.
A hard impact ran through his knuckles, and 'Subaru' went flying as if struck away. There was no equivalent pain rebounding back onto Subaru.
The pain 'Subaru' took was 'Subaru's' pain, 'Subaru's' wound.
It was something different from what Natsuki Subaru himself bore.

"And you line up all these words like you know everything... I see. Now I get it."

'Subaru,' struck down onto one knee, rubbed his beaten cheek with the back of his hand, and seeing that, Subaru felt something click into place.
This person in front of him, talking as if he knew everything — there was no mistake.
If this place was Od Lagna's cradle — the Corridor of Memories — then wasn't this exactly the opponent he needed to be on guard against, first and foremost?

"You're Rui, aren't you? The Sin Archbishop of Gluttony! It's you again, isn't it?!"

"...Me?"

"Don't play dumb!"

The last time, in the Corridor of Memories, Rui had used every trick in the book to try to sever Subaru from 'Natsuki Subaru,' aiming to devour his very existence whole.
He'd escaped those fangs by the narrowest of margins that time, but if a Sin Archbishop would give up so obediently after one failure, they wouldn't be called Sin Archbishops in the first place.

He had seen, now, just how hideous, how beyond salvation the Sin Archbishops truly were.
Petelgeuse, Regulus, Sirius, Capella — every one of them the worst kind of broken personality imaginable. And Lye, Roy, and Rui too were no exception.

If Rui, who had lain in wait inside Reid's Book of the Dead, had this time used some means to burrow her way into Natsuki Subaru's Book of the Dead instead —
Then it would make perfect sense that she'd be waiting here for Subaru, brazen as ever.

"That's the truth of it, isn't it, 'Gluttony'! Rui Arneb! If you can change your shape and form, then confusing me like this is nothing to you!"

Relying on the 'memories' and 'names' stolen from others, seizing not just a victim's skills but their very shape and form and chewing it up as her own — that was Rui Arneb's Authority.
That vileness had already been demonstrated without restraint throughout the tower. There was nothing strange about the stage on which it was demonstrated simply shifting to the Corridor of Memories.

"Is that it, then? Chewing me up like this to take me over for good this time? You got turned down once and you're still this persistent... You want to Return by Death that badly?!"

He said the words "Return by Death" without hesitation.
Saying them, he relived the memory of tasting hellish agony over and over again — the penalty that came with pain like his heart being crushed in a fist. But if that agony were limited to himself alone, it would still be bearable. — Compared to when it spread to Emilia, to the people around him.

But for Subaru, who had shouted the words while stamping down a trace of fear, no penalty came.
'Subaru' in front of him, too, showed no sign whatsoever of having his heart crushed. For a moment, it seemed he'd been on guard for the same thing, and Subaru could tell that 'Subaru' too felt relief at the demonic hand's failure to appear. That was infuriating.
Wearing the same face as him, harboring the same feelings as him — it felt, all the more, like an insistence that he and the other were one and the same thing.

"――! Get it through your head already! Is this some kind of amazing power? Something worth admiring?! It's nothing but dying and starting over. Dying and starting over... nothing more, nothing less. If the me using it is garbage, then the results are garbage too! So—"

"――"

"So I couldn't save anyone. ...I let everyone die. Because I'm weak, I make everyone grieve. Even now, it's like I can't save anyone at all...!"

――Return by Death is nothing worth having.

It's exactly the shoddy, garbage power it sounds like.
Something like this — he'd be far better off without it. There had been a witch who seemed to want to claim this power was something wonderful, the finest thing in this world, but he couldn't agree with that at all.
Hand an ant a cannon and it still won't know how to use it. In the end, it's simply beyond what he deserves.

Weak and puny, a child crying and throwing tantrums all alone.
What the witches said at that dream tea party was right. It's hopeless. Weak. Can't be picked up. Won't be used. 'Gluttony,' who doesn't understand that, and the Witch of Greed too, are both garbage.

Just how many times does he have to be deceived, how many times does hope have to be trampled underfoot, how many times does his ■ have to be broken before he learns.
Why, after being deceived so many times, after having hope trampled so many times, after having his ■ broken so many times, can he still think to keep going.

Return by Death shows him nothing but the ugly parts of the world.
It shows him unreasonableness, absurdity, fates he wants to look away from.
And yet, why—

"――It's because I love everyone."

At some point, Subaru had sunk down where he stood, dropped to his knees, and was shouting it out like a curse.
At that unsightly, half-sobbing outcry, 'Subaru' shook his head. Shaking his head, he said it again, once more, layering the words on top.

"――I love everyone. That's why I can't stop."

"――"

Wiping the redness of his struck cheek with a finger, 'Subaru' rose to his feet in Subaru's place.
The same clothes, the same features, the same face and name — and yet decisively different from himself, this 'Natsuki Subaru' — looked at Natsuki Subaru and said,

"How rough the position you've been put in is — honestly, just imagining it makes me sick. Everything wiped clean, back to level one, and yet you're starting at stage six. Every bit of the whining you spat out just now, I get it. Because that's a wound I've tasted over and over myself."

It was a face that had tasted its own lack of strength, its own lack of knowledge, over and over. — No, it had tasted it, again and again.
The defeats 'Subaru' had tasted, the agony, the lament of 'death' — Subaru knew all of it. He had seen every bit of it with his own eyes. He had tasted every bit of it with this body, and with his ■.

Precisely because he had tasted it, precisely because he knew it wasn't a lie, he couldn't accept 'Subaru's' words. He didn't want to accept them.

"If I were stronger, if I were smarter, if I were just a little more, a little more... It's frustrating, isn't it."

"Don't act like you understand...! What do you even know about me—"

"I do understand. And you should understand that I understand. This is a pointless argument, really. For me, and for you both."

"――!"

There was no force behind his words of refutation. That was only natural.
A refutation requires the will to reject the other's opinion, to beat it away as unconvincing, to crush it down as wrong. That kind of will is what's needed.
And Subaru, right now, couldn't turn that against 'Subaru.'
Because he knew.

He knew that it wasn't a lie, wasn't deception — that it was the whole of 'Natsuki Subaru.'

"I never should've looked at your memories..."

"You go and read a guy's diary without permission, and now you say that?"

"I never should've looked at your memories!"

He heard 'Subaru's' voice, sounding faintly sulky, and Subaru clenched his fists hard.

Struck, showered with abuse, and yet 'Subaru' still didn't break from his stance of dialogue — it made him seem far more grown-up than Subaru. Maybe that too was only natural.
After all, 'Subaru' should be, in fact, a full year older than Subaru.
And on top of that, he had experienced a dense concentration of life in another world, had gone through 'death' again and again, and on top of all that had woven countless bonds, earning the position he now held as himself.
And even so—

"I never should've looked at your memories..."

Again, Subaru repeated, layering the words once more.
To Subaru, who had once again heaped up the same regret, 'Subaru' said nothing. Not seeking any answer, Subaru went on as if muttering deliriously.

"I wanted to see hope in you. I thought — if you were an amazing guy, and I could figure out the 'something' that was propping up your amazingness, then I could do the same thing too. And yet..."

And yet, he'd understood.
He'd seen everything.

He'd understood that 'Natsuki Subaru' was no different at all from the Natsuki Subaru standing here now — a weak, puny man.

That he was just an ordinary man, who in a time Subaru didn't know had met people Subaru didn't know, had raced through a story Subaru didn't know, and had simply seen scenery Subaru didn't know.

"――I wanted to deny you. I wanted to say something that sounded plausible."

But he couldn't do it.

"Because I understand how you feel. ――Because you're me."

He had seen the world 'Natsuki Subaru' had seen, the world he had tasted.
He had seen every wound 'Natsuki Subaru' had taken in order to fall in love with this world, with the people living in it, with Emilia and the others — and in order to keep on loving them.

He'd had it wrong. He'd misunderstood.
Since this was about himself, maybe "he'd been full of himself" was the more accurate way to put it.
The illusion that 'Natsuki Subaru' was some kind of superhuman had been shattered, cast aside.

"Yeah, I get it! I got it! That you — that the reason you get back up over and over, the reason you don't give up no matter how many times you die, is just because you can't do anything else!!"

Just like Subaru, 'Subaru' too had run headfirst into walls he couldn't do anything about, again and again.
Each time, he had died over and over, piling up 'deaths,' changing the circumstances, changing how he met people, how he connected with them — and that was how 'Subaru' had overcome his hardships.
That was all it was.

"Is there anyone who could hear all that from Rem and still not get back up?! I got decked by Otto and realized I'd been full of myself! I always knew Dad and Mom felt that way about me — of course I did, because they're my dad and mom!"

The scenery 'Natsuki Subaru' had passed through, the triggers that had been necessary to overcome it — not the 'something' Subaru had been searching for, but simply a wish, piled up bit by bit — had certainly been there.

"I love Emilia. I want to protect her. I want to be with her. She told me she wants to make me her knight. I was so happy. That girl who's always afraid of being hated — I want to brag that she's someone who can walk around without a single worry, someone everyone can love! I finally managed to get Beatrice to come out with me. I want to make her happy for every bit of the time she spent suffering. She has the right to be happy — she does!"

So—

"――The reason you can't give up is just that you love everyone, that's all! Damn you! Why aren't you some kind of superhuman?! Why are you still just a stupid kid?!"

"――"

"Be strong like Reinhard! Be someone who can do anything, like Julius! If you can't manage that, then be useful like Ferris! Or master even just one single thing, like Wilhelm! If you could just be any one of those things..."

That was all he'd come to realize, after spending so much time, after suffering so much for it.
That 'Natsuki Subaru' was an ordinary man, holding no different a hand of cards than the Subaru standing here now, clumsy even at playing them, with no luck in the game whatsoever.
Any one of those things, even just one—

"Any one of those things..."

He murmured it, his voice drained of force, its momentum gone.
Any one of those things, even just one of them — his hand, which had gotten hold of nothing, clenched tight.

"...You're really not Rui, are you."

The question, unconnected to anything said before, came out in a hoarse voice.
Hearing it, 'Subaru,' who had been silent for a long while, nodded and said, "Yeah."

"Nope. I'm not Rui. I'm not gonna say 'unfortunately,' though. I'd hate having that girl butt in on this. I don't need to explain how rotten her personality is, right?"

"Yeah... guess not."

He answered small, feebly, and covered his own face with his arm.
The shouting that had all but torn his throat raw a moment before had died down, and now, belatedly, the fist he'd used to hit 'Subaru' throbbed with pain.
If this was the Corridor of Memories, the body Subaru had here shouldn't be his real one — and yet it was an unreasonable thing, feeling pain at all.

――Yes, pain, "death" — they were always unreasonable, always unbearable.

Which was exactly why the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony, Rui Arneb, was wrong.
The idea of wanting that for oneself was, without question, mistaken.

"...Why did you disappear?"

"Hm?"

"Why did you vanish? Do you know how much trouble it caused, you disappearing..."

The incomprehension that wouldn't go away, the trigger that had been the start of everything.
The reason 'Natsuki Subaru' had disappeared, and Natsuki Subaru had been born.
What, exactly, had happened to 'Natsuki Subaru,' who had vanished along with his memories.

"The reason you disappeared..."

"...That was just a dumb mistake on my part. I went into 'Taygeta' meaning to scout out a way to beat Reid. And things went fine, right up until I found Reid's book..."

"...And that's when Reid's book turned out to be empty."

"That's where I ran smack into 'Gluttony.' After that, I don't need to spell it out, right?"

'Subaru' scratched his head, looking back on his own humiliation.
He'd encountered Rui Arneb in the Corridor of Memories, and 'Natsuki Subaru' had his 'memories' stolen from him. And with that, having forgotten his entire life in another world up to that point, unable to understand even his own feelings, let alone those of the people around him — Natsuki Subaru had been born.

"Don't put yourself down like that. ...Though I guess that's a hard thing to ask. Since you're me, after all."

"...'Natsuki Subaru' is weak, and puny, and a hopeless, gigantic fool."

"No arguing with that."

"But—"

"――?"

At Subaru's feeble murmur, 'Subaru,' who had been about to agree with a wry smile, raised his eyebrows.
Watching 'Subaru' like that, Subaru gave voice to what came after the pause.
It was—

"――You're an amazing guy, 'Natsuki Subaru.'"

――That was the honest, unembellished truth, coming from someone who had watched more than twenty Returns by Death.

"――"

Slowly, narrowing his eyes as he fixed his gaze on the man standing in front of him.
A face he was used to, a face he was sick of the sight of, a face he even thought unsightly — but it was himself. Himself, and yet not himself; himself, and yet unable to think of it as himself.
That face was showing, for the first time, a reaction like it had been caught off guard, and it felt satisfying to see.

"...The closest stranger there is, huh."

Not many people can love themselves from the bottom of their heart.
Subaru was especially that type — Subaru hated himself. And probably 'Natsuki Subaru' was no different. — Subaru, 'Subaru,' hated himself.

But as the closest stranger there is, when Natsuki Subaru looked at 'Natsuki Subaru,' embarrassing as it was to say something like this — he'd been able to think, *he's cool.*

"Weak, hopeless, unable to do anything — and yet you struggle on, loving all of them so much. I respect you for that. So—"

"――"

"――That's where the meaning of me reading your Book of the Dead was."

The Book of the Dead hadn't been opened just so he could dwell on his own helplessness, mourn the fact that his lead had run out, and hurl grievances at a self wearing the same face as his own.
And of course it wasn't to trace the roots of some superhuman Natsuki Subaru and fulfill a grandiose delusion of gaining unmatched power.
It was to learn that 'Natsuki Subaru' was just an ordinary human being, and to acknowledge his conduct, his path.

"...You're suddenly being real embarrassing, you know that?"

'Subaru,' who had been frozen at Subaru's words up until then, suddenly moved again.
'Subaru' narrowed his eyes and glared at Subaru — not quite in displeasure, but with a somewhat awkward, sheepish look — and slowly pointed at himself.

"I know it's weird coming from me to say it, but looking back over everything I've done, I'm amazed you can say something like that. If you put it into words, it's basically 'Natsuki Subaru's Isekai Life Starting from Zero.'"

"Yeah, it feels weird when the protagonist's name is the same as your own, huh."

"Still better than if the heroine had Mom's name. ...No wait, that's not the point."

They traded light banter back and forth, and Subaru met 'Subaru's' eyes.
'Subaru,' looking somehow like he'd been let down in some way, gestured at himself with a hand and said,

"I said what I said a minute ago, but are you seriously fine with that? It's not like the possibility that I'm a fake made by Rui has been completely ruled out."

"It's ruled out. I hit you for real, and you didn't revert."

"That only works for Yanagisawa's copy ability, you know..."

Just as 'Subaru' protested, that wasn't really something to use as serious grounds.
But by this point, Subaru had already let go of his suspicion toward 'Subaru.'
That was probably because of something 'Subaru' had said a moment ago.

Even supposing Rui Arneb could devour another person's life whole, from head to tail, leaving nothing behind, Subaru didn't think she could reproduce that face, that voice.
Because she couldn't understand happiness, good fortune, joy.
Which was why—

"Someone who can't love anyone couldn't understand the feeling of Returning by Death because you love someone."

"――"

'Subaru' in front of him fell silent, and Subaru let out a long, pained breath.
Once put into words, it sounded terribly trite, nothing but a pretty phrase that was only pleasant to the ear. And yet, he couldn't find any other answer besides that.
So — the 'Subaru' standing before him was, without question, 'Natsuki Subaru.'

"Why are you here, anyway? Were you... waiting for me?"

He acknowledged 'Subaru' in front of him as a being sharing the same roots as himself.
On top of that, the question that surfaced was why the two Subarus had come face to face here at all.
At Subaru's question, 'Subaru' pressed his foot down against the pure white floor and said,

"The reason you and I came face to face here is that this is the one and only point where you and I connect."

"Where you and I... connect."

"Inside the tower, the only place where I, who has my 'memories,' and you, who doesn't, cross paths, is this loop's Book of the Dead. Not after this point, not before it — anywhere else, you and I could never have met."

"――. Guess I'm back to my first question, then. Why are you here?"

'Subaru's' explanation didn't sit right with him, and Subaru threw the same question again.
Strictly speaking, the two Subarus meeting face to face here made no logical sense. It would mean a Subaru with 'memories' and a Subaru without them existed at the same time.

――He didn't know the precise rules governing how the Books of the Dead were created.
For that matter, the way Natsuki Subaru's book had accumulated volumes equal to every death so far was already a strange thing to begin with. It was a phenomenon that couldn't hold together unless the world observed 'Natsuki Subaru's' deaths from the outside and recorded them.
Was Od Lagna supposed to have been playing the role of that observer?

"But then I don't get why Rui, back in the Corridor of Memories, would be observing my Returns by Death and getting all excited about it like that. If it can be recognized from the outside..."

"She's just squatting in the Corridor of Memories, she's not the ruler of the place. The ruler there is... probably someone with an even worse personality, I'd guess. Judging from this whole situation."

"I get 'someone with a worse personality.' The top candidate is..."

There, both of them paused, and Subaru and 'Subaru' said it together, in unison:

"――The 'Sage,' Flugel."

He was the top candidate for worst personality, no question.
The man who'd been involved in the construction of the Pleiades Watchtower, who'd set up 'trials' that placed the likes of Reid as gatekeepers, and who'd even burdened Shaula with four hundred years of endless, unbroken toil.
Honestly, he felt sorry for Shaula. If he could, he'd like to snatch her away from that so-called master of hers.

"So then, the reason a me with 'memories' and a me without them can exist at the same time is..."

"――. That's the kind of question with no answer. Not as long as I'm here and you're here."

"Is that... really how it is...?"

He felt something catch in his mind, but he took 'Subaru's' words in and drew his chin back.
It really was the kind of problem with no one setting the question. Even if he arrived at some kind of logic for it, in the end it probably wouldn't get him a real answer.
If there were a case where this question absolutely had to be pursued to a definite answer, it would be—

"As long as you're really not Rui Arneb, it doesn't matter."

"You're pretty persistent with the double-checking. Do you actually believe it?"

"I believe it, I believe it. I'll believe it even more if you let me punch the other cheek too."

"I see. Well, that's not exactly a bargain deal, so no thanks."

They traded easy banter back and forth, and Subaru shrugged at 'Subaru's' words.
That kind of underhanded rhetorical trick, trying to shift the core of the conversation — coming from anyone else it might work, but coming from himself, it was far too transparent.
So Subaru deliberately threw the same question a third time.

"Hey, other me. ――What are you here for?"

"――"

"You're not gonna ask 'you should know already,' huh."

At 'Subaru's' choice to stay silent, Subaru narrowed his black eyes and murmured that. Even hearing it, no more banter like before came back.
Instead, what surfaced in 'Subaru's' black eyes was a gleam that had been there, unbroken, from the very start.
Probably, that was what people called — guilt.

"――Hey, so, what do you think happens with the memory integration?"

Which was exactly why Subaru asked it in a deliberately bright tone.

"――For this kind of situation, is there actually an answer for which one of us ends up which?"

"Dunno. Feels like there's no clear, definite answer."

"Realistically, you seem like you'd know more about this place — is there some rule that'd be an advantage to know, something you know that I don't?"

"You read my whole 'anatomy textbook,' didn't you. It's fifty-fifty."

"Fifty-fifty, huh."

"Fifty-fifty."

"...What if, like, you and I accidentally collide and annihilate each other or something?"

"Don't even say that. Whatever makes you anxious pretty much makes me anxious too..."

"Guess that's fair. Okay then, no hard feelings whichever one of us wins?"

"No, I'm gonna hold a massive grudge, personally. And since I'll hold a grudge, that means you will too."

"Well, yeah. Probably, yeah. Hmm, my ■ really is narrow."

"Huh, what?"

"My ■ is narrow... huh, was that weird?"

"...No, I think I probably just misheard."

"Well, we've come this far, say whatever you want."

"Basically, you know more about this world than me... though that advantage's pretty much gone now since you read the Book of the Dead. But there are a couple things that changed too, right?"

"After my 'memories' were gone, me talking with everyone, getting to know everyone... it's the parts of 'Natsuki Subaru's Isekai Life Starting from Zero' that you don't know."

"And there's this huge scorpion prowling around the tower — that's actually Shaula. She's turned out pretty dangerous... but that's not something she's doing because she wants to. Save her too, would you?"

"Oh, right, forgot to mention — when I met Rui, Rem kicked me in the back. At the time I didn't remember Rem at all, so it wasn't until after I read the book that I remembered what kind of girl she is... yeah, she really is my Rem."

"No, she's my Rem."

"No, mine."

"Mine."

"――"

"――"

"Ram said it, didn't she. That once the thaw season comes, the things that had gone unseen would come properly into view, show their face again. ...As expected of her."

"Yeah, she really is."

"As for that guy Julius... well, I don't really need to say anything about him, do I. He's not the kind of unreliable guy that either of us needs to say anything about."

"Yeah, agreed."

"Thanks for apologizing to Dad and Mom."

"Yeah. Mm."

"Thanks for saving Otto and Garfiel."

"Yeah."

"Thanks for saving Petra, and Frederica, and everyone in Arlam Village too."

"Yeah."

"Thanks for bringing Beatrice out, for holding her hand."

"――"

"Emilia..."

"――"

"Thanks for falling in love with Emilia. I love her too. I love her so much."

"...Yeah, I know."

"――Natsuki Subaru. I know for sure that you're an amazing guy, so I know you'll be fine."

"Yeah."

"――"

"Yeah. ...That's right."

"――"


"Yeah. ――The two of us, we'll be fine."


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△


"Fine, huh."

"Yeah. We'll be fine, won't we."

"I really do know that you're amazing."

"...Yeah, but, mm. This — I gotta say this."

"――"


"――My summer vacation... it's over now, huh."


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△


――Pressing hand to hand, a cliché of a gesture, was the very last exchange.

"――"

He breathed in, breathed out.
Most likely, the integration had taken place in an irregular form. — No, to begin with, a process like "integration" was never meant to take place in a location like this at all.
Subaru's very existence, the events that had occurred — all of it had been irregular.
Which is why—

"――"

Standing frozen where he was, Subaru only realized tears were tracing down his own cheeks when a drop fell from his chin.
Somehow, he couldn't bring himself to wipe them away, because it felt as though those flowing tears surely weren't ones he himself had shed.
They were tears shed by someone who, right now, inside Subaru, was slowly melting together with Subaru.
And so, breathing deep, again and again, he sent his awareness circling through his own mind. Burned into his own memory there—

"――!"

Suddenly, twenty deaths that Natsuki Subaru had gone through without conscious awareness flashed back through his mind.
Every one of them — the countless 'deaths' he'd come to taste in the process of struggling, thrashing inside the tower — the part he'd worried over, whether it would carry over or not, had connected.
It had connected, but—

"You've been walking over a plenty crazy bridge yourself, haven't you...!"

That other self, who had heaped so much praise on Subaru — taking in the sheer solitary struggle he'd fought, and the hard-won battles he'd waged once he'd learned to trust his companions, Subaru understood it now.
Indeed, just as he himself had said. — When it came to that guy, he really could respect him.

Taking a deep breath as if to press down both that feeling and his oddly racing heartbeat, he nodded.
Then Subaru lifted his face and looked straight ahead.

It was the same unchanging white space.
A blank zone born within the Corridor of Memories, created by an irregular set of circumstances.
The place where, just a few moments before, another Subaru had been, pressing his hand against Subaru's. — There, looking down at the small, huddled figure slumped in that very spot, Subaru narrowed his black eyes.
And then—

"So. Did you get to see what you wanted to see? ――Rui Arneb."

To the Natsuki Subaru who had read through the Book of the Dead, arrived at this place, and vanished — no, to Rui Arneb — Subaru quietly posed that question.
