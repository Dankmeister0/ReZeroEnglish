Arc 2 Chapter 20: "Twilight, Alone and Unaided"

"Um, well then, thanks for having me for this short while."

In the entrance hall, watched over by every human in the mansion (a mere four people, and that excluding Beatrice), Subaru bore down on a ticklish sort of embarrassment and bowed his head.

The three-day stay, the promised deadline, had arrived — the day Subaru would leave the mansion.

Subaru's outfit was simple: the tracksuit and convenience-store bag he'd had on when he was summoned to this other world, plus the tool pouch Roswaal had kindly given him.

The tool pouch was weighty, apparently holding a fair sum of money, and measured against a noble's sense of currency, it was a little frightening to peek inside. Especially now, when Subaru's means of self-defense were so drastically lacking.

"Are you really going to be okay? If it's a carriage you need, you could just ask Roswaal to have one called out here."

Among the group seeing him off, standing at the center, Emilia's expression showed no sign of shedding its worry. Glad for her concern even as he felt it, Subaru waved the hand he'd raised.

"No, no, no, I'll be fine. I'm gonna take it slow, easy does it. I can't put you out any more than I already have — seriously."

"...I guess Subaru's got his head on straight, so I probably don't need to worry."

"Whoa, hearing you rate me that highly puts a little pressure on me, you know. But it's fine, it's fine. Someday, when I've become a stronger, smarter, richer man, I'll come riding in on a white horse to whisk you away."

"Do you have a handkerchief? And drinking water, and — right, you'll need Lagmite for when it gets dark. And then, and then—"

"Full-on mom mode!?"

Emilia's worrying showed no sign of stopping, and in the end it even reached "Can you sleep by yourself without getting lonely?" Just how starved for company did she think he was?

Or maybe, by some intuition, she was sensing the true feelings Subaru carried in his heart, the anxiety he couldn't quite suppress.

"We-e-ell then, Subaru-kun, stay well. It was a short while, but it was fu-u-un, wasn't it?"

The tall man held out his hand; Subaru returned the handshake with a grin.

"Yeah, thanks for having me too. You even sent me off with souvenirs — you really went above and beyond."

"Think nothing of it. Oh, right, about that souvenir — don't lose it, now. I tucked in just a little extra, to match the memories of our three days together."

Roswaal winked as he gave the signal, and Subaru, catching his meaning, gave a big nod and jangled the tool bag on his back.

"Right, right, hush money. I got it, I got it. I won't blab about anything I shouldn't. Heck, I'll swear on a dragon if you want."

"Being around you, I start to lose sight of the finer points of scheming. And besides, in this country, swearing on a dragon is the highest order of oath there is. I don't doubt you, but do try not to forget that."

Given that extra reminder, Subaru raised his hand — and this time turned that raised hand toward the twins standing behind Roswaal. To the two of them, standing silently and still, Subaru gave a pat on the shoulder with his raised hand.

"You two, thanks a ton as well. Especially Rem-rin, thanks for the good food, always. Ram, uh, well, you're good at cleaning the toilets, right?"

"Sister, sister. The guest's flattery is hopelessly bad, isn't it."
"Rem, Rem, the guest's flattery is fatally lacking in taste, isn't it."

"Aw, quit it, I seriously couldn't think of anything. But, thanks."

Having said his farewells to everyone, he turned his back before the parting could grow too sentimental.

The twins hurried to the entrance hall doors and pulled them open, and before Subaru's eyes spread the road leading straight to the nearby village.

Walk about twenty minutes to the village, then move on to yet another village from there, and from there take a carriage to the royal capital — that was the plan Subaru had explained to everyone.

"Well then, thanks for everything. Hope we meet again sometime."

"Mm, take care. Don't get hurt, okay?"

Leaving a smile for Emilia, who worried about him right to the end, Subaru stepped out onto the road as they saw him off.

The silver-haired girl kept waving in front of the entrance until the mansion passed out of sight.

That distant gesture was endlessly dear to him, and it stoked back into flame the fire in Subaru's chest that anxiety had nearly smothered.

Alone on the road, he walked for a while before stopping.

Subaru checked his surroundings, and once he confirmed no presence of anyone, he abruptly left the road and slipped into the forest. The forest across the road connected to the mountains and ran deep; Ram had sternly warned him not to enter it, on account of how much wildlife lived there.

Ignoring that warning, Subaru pushed his body through the underbrush, deeper into the forest. He climbed several slopes, picking up scratches now and then from branches jutting out, but he never slowed his pace.

He must have pressed on through the mountains for some fifteen minutes like that.

"Alright, here it is."

The forest suddenly opened up, and his field of view revealed a high sky above. After climbing several slopes, Subaru had arrived at what amounted to a small hill amid the mountains.

This was the spot he was aiming for. Miming wiping sweat from his brow, Subaru then set his eyes on the cliff before him — or rather, positioned right beside it, on the building visible down below.

The familiar mansion, its grandeur unmistakably that of Roswaal Manor.

Having circled around the road and come by way of the forest and mountains, he'd arrived at the perfect vantage point — a spot from which to keep watch over Roswaal Manor from high ground. Especially,

"I can see the west side well, where Emilia-tan's room is. If anything happens, I'll notice right away."

From this distance, Subaru's eyesight could just barely make out the window of her chambers. Setting that aside, this was a position that let him take in the entire western side of the mansion and more. If anyone approached the mansion from outside, he judged, he'd be sure to spot them.

The third loop had already passed into the morning of its fourth day. By Subaru's experience, that left roughly sixteen hours until it matched the time of his previous death.

Which meant that whatever abnormality was going to strike the mansion would likely occur around then.

"Now all that's left is to lie in wait for it to happen."

Sixteen hours or so was something Subaru's willpower could manage for a stakeout.

Really, he'd considered setting this up three days ago, right after the loop began, but he'd worried that starting too early would sap his concentration before it mattered, so he'd let three days pass as a grace period.

Thanks to that, his energy, stamina, and motivation were all in full supply.

Detect any abnormality at Roswaal Manor beforehand, or failing that, put himself in a position to leap into action the moment something happened — that was the precondition Subaru had prepared.

If he stayed at the mansion, he himself would be folded into the attacker's target list from the very start, and just as before, some magic would leave his consciousness clouded over.

With his current desperate need for even a single scrap more information about the attacker, that would be fatal.

So Subaru had chosen a strategy: use Emilia and the others as bait, and lure the attacker out.

Of course, since Subaru couldn't bring himself to be entirely ruthless, he'd laid down several fail-safes, ready to bolt straight for the mansion the instant anything happened, to sound the alarm and run himself ragged if need be.

"It'd be great if the enemy's the cautious type who gets spooked by my screaming and runs off."

Voicing that wishful thinking, Subaru set down the tool bag and rummaged through its contents. From inside, he pulled out a long rope he'd borrowed from the mansion's storeroom. From his time as a servant there, he knew exactly where to find equipment like this.

He'd swiped it under everyone's noses and had been carrying it around ever since, just in case of emergency.

He tied the rope to the trunk of a nearby thick tree and fastened the other end around his own body. Of course, if he used it as a simple lifeline as-is, the load would kill him, so he made several knots at various points, preparing cushioning against the shock first.

"And then there's the little knife for cutting the rope... I really hope cutting the rope is all I end up needing it for."

The blade, about the length of a fruit knife, was also meant to serve as a means of self-harm in a pinch, if it came to that.

As a countermeasure against the magic from last time, it might at least blunt the initial onset of that weakening of consciousness. He didn't think he could do anything against the debilitation that followed, though.

"Better than having no countermeasure at all... Besides, this time around is for preparing countermeasures for the next time, anyway."

Muttering as if to convince himself, Subaru shook his head to chase away his faintheartedness.

What crossed his mind, unbidden, were the people of Roswaal Manor he'd just said goodbye to.

This time, Subaru was going to use them as stepping stones, sacrificed in preparation for the next loop. This time too, just as in the loops before, he was doing this to people with whom he was sure he'd forged real bonds.

If he closed his eyes, he could recall these past four days.

He didn't think he'd built as close a relationship as last time, but even so, he'd had plenty of chances to spend time with Ram and Rem, and he'd gone out of his way to see Emilia himself. And he'd done it all while anticipating that it would come to ache in his chest exactly like this, right now.

This was penance. This was a fitting comeuppance. This was punishment he deserved to receive.

It was the kind of judgment Subaru absolutely had to accept — Subaru, who had resolved once again to use them as stepping stones, who had devised his plan on the premise of losing them.

He'd spent time with them thinking how heartbreaking it was.

He'd spent time with them thinking how dear they were.

Sticking his fingers into the wound that had opened, enduring pain like flesh tearing further and bone splitting, Subaru had spent these three days.

All so as not to forget any of it.

"I did say it, didn't I, Natsuki Subaru. Even when everyone else forgets it, after you repeat a loop... you remember it."

So this time's events, too — he mustn't let himself think it was alright to forget them.

Until the very, very last moment, he had to keep chasing the happy ending Subaru wanted.

No one had the right to declare that her existence, or theirs, was some ephemeral bubble destined to vanish in the gap between timelines.

Lying flat, gritting his teeth hard enough to grind them, Subaru hid his body among the trees and grass, watching Roswaal Manor through the gaps in the forest.

His breathing was shallow, and though his body should have been tense, his heartbeat was strangely slow and steady — proof, spreading through his whole body, that it affirmed the resolve Subaru had made.

A sense, more than ever before, of his own body obeying him.

Holding onto that rare, hard-won feeling, Subaru kept waiting, unmoving, for the time to come.

Lying low, he kept waiting for the moment to come.

Tension stiffened his body, anxiety rocked his heart unsteadily, and impatience made his vision conjure up things that weren't there.

Quietly evading such unconscious interference, Subaru kept holding his breath.

Six hours had already passed, and the world that had been approaching noon now poured vivid orange from a sunset sky.

Thinking back, this was around the time when, in the loops before, he'd have been heading to the village to shop with either Ram or Rem.

This time, though, he hadn't confirmed either of them leaving the mansion. Maybe, without Subaru around, that shopping trip event simply didn't happen.

"Guess it's simply that with one less mouth to feed, they pushed the grocery run to tomorrow... Weird feeling, being able to work that out so objectively."

Having stayed silent for over six hours, his voice, when he suddenly muttered, came out hoarser than he expected. Wryly amused, Subaru pulled the water canister from his tool bag and wet his throat with the cold water. It was simple, like a bamboo tube, but strange in that the water inside stayed cold indefinitely. Probably another tool tied to magic somehow, he figured.

"Only six hours in and already slacking, pathetic. No time to be losing focus — get it together, me!"

Slapping his own cheek as he said it, the dry crack of sound helped clear his head.

There was still nearly half a day left before the estimated time. If he was already breaking down at this point, the road ahead didn't look promising.

He tightened his resolve and turned his attention back to keeping watch.

Fortunately or unfortunately, it was at that very moment Subaru reset his focus that it happened.

"――!"

The instant he caught the faint sense of wrongness that brushed his eardrum, Subaru's body flung itself sideways without a moment's hesitation.

An evasive maneuver, planned out in advance, executed with every ounce of his senses thrown into it.

It bore fruit — it saved Subaru's body beautifully from the threat of that surprise attack.

Immediately after, he heard the crashing sound of something heavy snapping a tree half in two. Trees were mowed down, dragging their neighbors with them, leaves and branches shattering and scattering in a chaotic dance.

Through the middle of it, Subaru broke into a run, throwing himself all at once toward the cliff.

"――Uwaah!"

A scream he couldn't fully suppress even with his teeth clenched leaked out faintly, and he tasted the stomach-turning sensation of falling from a height. But after only a few meters of fall, the momentum abruptly halted — the rope cinched tight around his waist bit in, and, groaning in pain—

"Emergency... escape!"

He said it and cut the rope with his knife. Freed, his body resumed falling, his shoe soles biting into the slanted cliff face, sliding as he somehow landed roughly on the ground.

Without pausing even to catch his breath, Subaru chose an all-out, undignified flight. To lighten his load, he threw off the tool bag and the rest, and quite literally ran down the mountainside with nothing but his own body.

Panting as he ran,

"I saw it! Haah... ah! I saw it!"

The object that had mowed down the trees was an iron ball studded with spikes, about the size he could wrap both arms around. Something like a bowling ball retrofitted for lethal use — a wildly romantic weapon connected to its wielder by a long, long chain — its name: Morningstar.

The sense of wrongness he'd felt in the mountains — that had been the sound of the chain linking the iron ball. That sound, one he recognized without question, was the very weapon that had taken Subaru's life that night.

Seeing its power and menace with his own eyes, Subaru's teeth chattered belatedly with fear.

If that mass came flying with that kind of sharpness behind it, a body taking a direct hit could scatter to pieces without it being strange at all. It made sense now, why his left side had been torn clean off.

"But... it came after me!"

Stepping over branches, leaping ditches, crossing bad footing, he spat as he ran.

It was an outcome he'd anticipated. There was as much chance of an attack on Subaru, now away from the mansion, as there was of an attack on Roswaal Manor itself.

If the attacker meant to wipe out everyone involved, there was basically no way Subaru, who'd stayed three days, would drop off the list. And the fact that they'd chosen Subaru as a target meant—

"Which means they must've been watching me stay at that mansion for days already."

The attacker had been surveilling the mansion for several days beforehand.

Which was why they hadn't excluded Subaru from their targets even after he left the mansion. Above all, the fact that they'd tracked down Subaru while he was hiding was proof enough of that.

How else could they have done it, without following Subaru after he left the mansion?

"――Uwah!"

It happened the instant he got too absorbed in his thoughts and let his footing slip.

A tree root caught Subaru's foot, sending his momentum-laden body sprawling spectacularly. He tumbled across the ground, sliding down the slope. And just then, as if it had taken careful aim, the iron ball passed right over his head.

A shudder ran through Subaru's chest at the thought of what would have happened if he hadn't been tumbling right then.

He rolled sideways to his feet, spat out the dirt that had gotten into his mouth, and resumed his flight. He'd completely lost track of any path by now, but he couldn't afford to stop.

His breath was ragged. His lungs ached. To begin with, Subaru lacked stamina.

He had above-average confidence in just about every kind of physical ability, but stamina alone was truly his weak point. A chase through the mountains — he had no chance, just thinking about it. And since he had no chance just thinking about it, actually doing it would go even worse.

Which meant—

"Guess getting cornered was inevitable, huh..."

Ahead of him, a cliff rose up as if to seal him in.

The rock wall, its hard, sharp fragments jutting out, was a natural stronghold that refused to be climbed or used as a foothold. Naturally, Subaru currently had no means of scaling it.

He turned around, drew a deep breath, exhaled, steadied his breathing, and braced himself.

Ahead of him, the darkness within the forest had deepened at some point without his noticing; here, where the trees blotted out the sunset, the place was filled with a desolation as if cut off from the rest of the world.

"If you're coming, then come on..."

Chasing away his weakness with bravado, Subaru opened the front of his tracksuit and stripped off the jacket. He spread it wide in both hands, readying himself, and quietly waited for the attacker to arrive.

He was cornered. Driven into a corner. Subaru was now nothing more than helpless prey caught in a predator's trap.

But he wasn't going to just let himself be eaten for nothing.

He'd paid this much of a price already. At the very least, he was going to get something in return worthy of it.

――In that instant, from beyond the darkness, trailing the sound of chains, violence came flying at high speed.

"Got some gu-u-uts... in me, don't I!?"

Facing a blow certain to be lethal, Subaru's body showed a reflex beyond common sense.

Raising the tracksuit jacket held in both hands, he wound it around the incoming iron ball from directly below, deflecting its momentum just slightly, and by the narrowest of margins avoided a direct hit to his torso.

That said, the jacket was torn from both his arms, and his body — unable to fully absorb the impact and slammed against the rock wall — didn't escape unscathed.

But when he raised his head and saw the iron ball, its aim thrown off, embedded in the wall, Subaru broke into a triumphant grin and firmly seized the fully-extended chain.

Then, turning his gaze toward the other end of that stretching chain — toward the direction of the one holding it—

"Alright, show yourself, you bastard! It took me a whole week to see that face of yours, damn it!"

He roared, hurling foul-mouthed abuse to steel his own nerve.

No matter what came out, he narrowed his eyes and glared into the darkness, determined not to miss it.

It had been a desperate, life-or-death situation, but somehow he'd managed to scrape through alive.

Maybe this time, he wouldn't have to sacrifice everything after all — maybe he could drive off the attacker once they showed themselves, without needing to throw this loop away.

His racing mind conjured up thoughts that weren't quite optimism, but something close to it.

In a situation he'd once nearly given up on, he reached desperately for the glimmer of light he now saw.

Within that light were Emilia, the twins, the impudent little girl, and Roswaal. A sensation that made him forget the situation for a moment and almost let his cheeks relax, and then—

"――There's nothing to be done about it, I suppose."

The chain rang out, and he felt the fully-taut length of it slacken as its owner drew near.

But leaving behind any such trivial sensation, Subaru's eyes went wide.

His lips trembled, and a voice that wouldn't form words leaked from his throat as a groan.

Without his even realizing it, his fingers let go of the chain they'd grasped, and his head shook weakly, faintly, from side to side, as if refusing what he saw.

Treading over the grass, stepping past branches, her figure slowly emerged from the darkness.

A black-toned apron dress, a pure white headdress crowning her head. Her small frame, utterly mismatched with it, gripped the handle of the chain connected to the iron ball,

"It would have been best if you'd finished it all without ever noticing a thing."

Her blue hair swaying, tilting her head slightly with that familiar expressionless face,

"No way... Rem."

The girl he'd meant to protect stood before Subaru, iron ball raised high.
