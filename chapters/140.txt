Arc 3 Chapter 63: "One Flash Against Sloth"

"Among the Witch Cult, the two most infamous are surely 'Sloth' and 'Greed.'"

Riding side by side on their Earth Dragons, Julius said this to Subaru.

Subaru, for his part, was clinging fairly desperately to Patrasche's back, while Julius, accustomed to riding, looked effortlessly dashing. Seen from the outside it made Subaru feel pathetic, so he didn't want Julius riding alongside him — but whether Julius knew of that inner conflict or not,

"The names of the Archbishops of Sin are known well enough, but in the actual records of their activity, those are the two names that come up most. Sloth appears more frequently, but Greed's is the greater in terms of damage caused."

"Doesn't sound like either's much good... how much is actually known about them?"

"Not that many details, honestly."

Julius smoothed back his windblown hair and cast a sidelong glance at Subaru, who was answering him reluctantly. Looking down at Subaru, whose face was rigid as he endured the jolting ride,

"Are you all right for me to continue?"

"No reason to worry about me. Where I'm from, this is just how you ride an Earth Dragon. Right, Patra... Hey, don't shake me around! You lizard bastard!"

"I wonder if it isn't a lack of trust between you and your Earth Dragon. She is your legs, but not your possession. Unless you entrust her with your trust and leave the wind to her, you won't be able to move as one..."

"Skip the sermon. Just tell me the rest of what you were saying."

Subaru stuck his tongue out at Julius's condescension while trying his best to sync his breathing with Patrasche's, urging him to continue. At Subaru's less-than-honest attitude, Julius let out a deep breath, then raised a finger and wagged it side to side.

"The Archbishop of Sin representing 'Sloth' has an extremely high rate of involvement whenever the Witch Cult is active. Roughly, more than half of the damage the Witch Cult brings about across various regions has 'Sloth's' name attached to it."

"So he's a busybody, huh."

"It's presumed he has an extraordinary obsession with being the first to strike. Rather industrious for someone claiming the name 'Sloth.' —Though one wishes that energy were directed anywhere but heretical deeds."

Subaru recalled how Petelgeuse relentlessly insisted on being industrious himself, and forced that same effort upon others.

Though he bore the mantle of 'Sloth,' the man himself seemed to detest sloth in its true sense. Was that rebellious streak the very thing behind the Witch Cult's abnormally high frequency of 'Sloth' activity?

"But if he shows his face that often, how come nobody knows the details? Hasn't the knights' order ever cornered him or anything?"

"Fundamentally, one can't even sense a sign of Witch Cult activity in advance. They're the sort who are only recognized as having acted once you're standing amid the damage they've already caused. Even being present and surviving to tell of it is no small feat."

"But this time, it will not go that way."

Julius looked frustrated at Subaru's question. But the one who forcefully contradicted the elegant man's answer was another rider flanking Subaru on the opposite side from Julius — Wilhelm.

The old man, his eyes holding a quiet fighting spirit, let the scabbard of the treasured sword at his hip slide,

"If we can pin down where he emerges, then it will become, quite properly, a contest of raw strength. With myself, the rest of the unit, Sir Ricardo, and Sir Julius all joining in, I do not believe we shall come up short."

"Yeah... that's true. I think so too."

Coolly weighing the difference in strength between the two sides, Subaru gave Wilhelm an affirmative answer.

Comparing pure combat strength, the fighting power of the Witch Cult under Petelgeuse's command shouldn't be able to contend with their side, which held the numerical advantage. What's more, Petelgeuse's own combat ability wasn't especially high.

If one could just close the distance, he couldn't even dodge an attack from an amateur like Subaru. If it were Wilhelm closing in, he could probably take the man's head off in an instant.

—When it came to defeating him, the biggest problem was, as ever, the 'Unseen Hand.'

Whether or not one could slip past those black-mist-like palms that stretched out endlessly and close the distance to him — that alone, without exaggeration, would decide victory or defeat. And Subaru was the only one who could counter that 'Unseen Hand.'

"That's part of the plan. I'm counting on you, Wilhelm-san."

"As you wish."

Wilhelm gave a solemn nod, his brief reply radiating nothing but reliability.

Beside Subaru, who found himself seized by the urge to entrust the man with everything, Julius nodded several times, saying, "Quite right,"

"I too have no intention of losing, if I can actually bring things to the point of crossing blades. I've been kept from that chance again and again until now, to my great frustration... but this time, it won't go that way."

A quiet anger simmered in Julius's eyes.

As a member of the Royal Guard himself, he too must have harbored no small resentment toward the Witch Cult, which had slipped free of capture time and again.

On that point, Subaru was in complete agreement.

"By the way, we got pretty into the Sloth talk, but what about the other famous one, 'Greed'?"

"Unlike Sloth, there aren't that many reported cases of Greed. But the incident that made his name so notorious was simply too enormous."

"Too enormous, huh...?"

At Subaru's blank response, Julius, the wind striking a serious profile, closed his eyes as if in thought, then said,

"South of the Kingdom lies the Vollachian Empire. He conquered a city there called Garkla, known as a fortress city for its impregnability. —All by himself."

"Conquered!? A whole city!? By himself!?"

"Let the soldiers ever be fierce and strong — that spirit lives on in that land, where even a single foot soldier is an ashura of battle. The Archbishop of Sin who claims the name 'Greed' conquered, single-handedly, a fortress city defended by such soldiers. He even struck down Vollachia's hero, 'Kurgan of the Eight Arms.'"

Wilhelm continued, as if piling on to Subaru, whose mouth hung open in astonishment. Speaking the name of the hero said to have fallen to Greed's hand, he furrowed his whitened brows for a moment, an emotion like nostalgia surfacing in his eyes.

Noticing that look, Subaru fell silent, and catching his reaction out of the corner of his eye, the old swordsman gave a small shake of his head.

"Kurgan and I crossed blades a number of times, you see. —A fine swordsman, that one. I cut off six of his eight arms, and in exchange had my belly run through. We both ended up half-dead, honors even, and it was never settled which of us was the better."

"He just casually dropped a brutal war story—!"

An anecdote from the Sword Devil's active days was, frankly, quite the light-novel material, and it left Subaru anything but calm. Part of him wanted to dig deeper into the details, but even Subaru wasn't callous enough to press the point while Wilhelm was recounting the fact that his worthy rival had since been slain.

That said, the topic of 'Greed' was, purely on its own, a heavy one for Subaru as well.

"Sloth and Greed. And on top of that, four more to go... no, minus Gluttony, three. A rough road ahead."

"—Already looking ahead, are you."

"Reluctantly, yeah, but it feels like a pretty high probability."

With a confrontation against the Witch Cult imminent, Subaru's gaze grew distant, fixed on the future.

The decisive battle with Petelgeuse of 'Sloth' — including the fact that the man would come picking a fight — was unavoidable. And settling matters with Petelgeuse once and for all would mean a definitive confrontation with the Witch Cult.

"Which means, inevitably, a moment will come where I clash with the next Sin-whatever too... It's an infuriating grudge-match kind of development, but hey, beat one delinquent and his buddies show up next — that's a classic manga pattern."

The real problem was that clashing and settling things wouldn't simply be the end of it.

If it wouldn't end until one side or the other was utterly destroyed, then as long as Subaru kept resolving to stand at Emilia's side and face forward, opportunities to confront the Archbishops of Sin would keep coming.

"My stomach's already hurting just from hearing about Greed. Somebody save me."

"It's hard to keep talking when you get that depressed over something that hasn't even happened yet. —Subaru, right now you should focus on what's in front of you."

"I know, I know. I'm just a little nervous with Sloth right up ahead."

At Julius's admonishment, Subaru tucked his chin and steeled himself once more.

Just before that, Subaru heard Julius murmur softly, "Sloth...?" — but he dismissed it as nothing important.

Night was beginning to pale, and beyond the dark sky, sunlight had faintly begun to rise.

They had already set foot within their destination, the Mathers domain, and the anti–Witch Cult coalition — a merger of the subjugation force and the Demi-human mercenary corps — rode their mounts onward with spirits high.

What lay ahead on their course was not the manor of Roswaal, the lord of the domain.

Heading straight for Roswaal Manor and having everyone stand guard over the villagers and Emilia and the others remaining at the manor was one strategy Subaru had considered.

There was a calculation to be made, too, that in terms of protecting them through to the end, that option would carry lower risk. But—

"I'm the only one who knows for certain he's coming, and even the timing would shift the moment we moved in to guard them. Once that happens, the future's a total unknown."

If an armed group entered the manor, even Petelgeuse might reconsider his approach. Knowing that fanatic, he'd probably swallow even that and force his way through regardless — but even so, it would already differ from the shape of the future Subaru knew.

Having brought down the White Whale, the coalition's morale currently stood extremely high. But how long that morale could be maintained in the face of an enemy who might strike at any unknown time was another matter entirely.

Continued fear of the enemy would sap their morale, and it would also place a corresponding burden on Emilia and the villagers, who would be kept under constant guard. It could no longer be called certain.

"So there's only one way to pull this off in line with the future I know — hit first, and take it all in one go!"

Cowering before an enemy whose timing was unknown was not the course a Subaru who had already glimpsed the future should take. He ought to be seeking the way to make the fullest use of the chance he'd gained through Return by Death — a chance that, by rights, shouldn't even exist.

So then, what was that method, that possibility—

* * *

Feeling the texture of fallen grass and leaves beneath his feet, Subaru advanced slowly.

Treading over uneven ground, Subaru was making his way through a dim, shadowy forest.

The sun had already climbed high, its offered light slanting down through the gaps between the trees. The wind blowing through carried a damp weight, and it made Subaru — conscious of his quickening heartbeat — keenly aware of the cold sweat beading on his brow.

—Right now, Subaru was entirely alone.

Walking in isolation through the cold-winded forest, Subaru didn't even have Patrasche, who had run beside him along the highway. His hands were empty, and the way he walked, shoulders swaying as if it were a chore, looked utterly unreliable.

"—I left Patrasche behind. She wasn't going to be able to keep up with this fight."

Subaru gave a small laugh as he said it, slightly out of breath.

He'd already walked quite a distance alone like this. Slipping between slender trees, snapping fallen branches underfoot with a crack, carefully picking his way down slick slopes so as not to fall. This path that was no path at all was hardly even worthy of being called a game trail, and it did nothing but purely, single-mindedly hinder Subaru's progress.

Walking through this forest like this — this made the third time, didn't it.

Both the first and second times, he'd been carrying someone in his arms, so he ought to be far lighter on his feet now than he'd been then. And yet his steps felt heavier now than they had those times — was that a matter of how he felt about where his feet were headed? Or was it because whoever he'd been carrying then had been someone who made him forget his exhaustion?

"Probably both, but... nothing to be proud of either way."

Both the first time and the second, the result had been that he'd failed to protect the person in his arms.

And if this third time, now, was to end with his hands empty, then any further repetition beyond that had to be avoided absolutely. That was precisely the point of now.

"——"

Avoiding a garishly colored mushroom so as not to step on it, he used a nearby tree trunk for support and made a small leap — and the instant he landed, he felt the air change against his skin.

It was different from the sensation before the battle with the White Whale, where tension had seemed to snap taut against the skin. This air didn't snap — it clung, carrying with it a murky dimness, and it forced Subaru, who hadn't been conscious of it until just now, to suddenly become aware of the sweat covering his entire body.

"He's here... it's like, you know, suddenly noticing a cockroach in the corner of a quiet room."

Silently locking eyes with an unmoving black object X. At the end of a lonely standoff with no telling when it might end, a person tends to feel as though time itself is being stretched into something eternal.

A sensation close to that, simply and purely unpleasant, was crawling across his entire body.

When he narrowed his eyes to look, the forest scenery to his left and right looked much the same — and within it, he could make out a strange, familiar sense of having seen this somewhere before.

No — he really had come upon a landscape he recognized.

"Walking this much of a path that isn't even a path and still arriving here every time — my sense of direction, or maybe my instincts, are honed way too sharp. It's almost funny."

Or perhaps it should be said his nose had gotten keen instead.

A hunting dog trained specifically for the Witch Cult — call it that, and it might sound rather impressive, but it was the sort of role he'd rather decline if given the choice. He wanted to hand it back after just this one time.

"—Thanks for the welcome party."

Straining his eyes at the dim shadows ahead, Subaru offered that word of thanks.

There wasn't a shred of genuine warmth behind it, but he was well aware that whatever he was addressing didn't possess the humanity to care about such things one way or another. It occurred to him only now to wonder — just what were they, really?

Squirming on and on without a word, without any assertion of their own, like puppets utterly devoid of will — where did their true intent lie?

"Guess you wouldn't answer me even if I asked, huh, Witch Cultists."

"——"

All at once, Subaru found himself surrounded by men clad in black garb that blended into the darkness.

Somewhere along the way, the sound of the wind and the cries of insects had vanished from the world, and the ground had already been thoroughly prepared for their entrance. So there was hardly any surprise in the encounter. What he felt instead — out of place as it was — was only relief that they had shown up exactly as intended.

"I'll get the details from whoever's in charge of you lot, so never mind that... for now, get lost."

"——"

"Not totally sure how it works, but in the pecking order I'm above you, right? Do me a favor."

He raised a hand, gesturing for them to be off somewhere.

At that, the black-clad men bowed their heads to Subaru as if in respect, and in that same posture slid smoothly back into the darkness once more, dissolving from sight. This too was the reaction he'd expected.

It was a somewhat complicated feeling, but they held no hostility toward Subaru. So long as he showed no intent to harm from his side, or unless Petelgeuse gave the order, there was no need to worry about them attacking from theirs.

"If they'd just take this as their cue to retreat to some other country and never show their faces again, that'd make things nice and easy."

He let out a breath, his shoulders slumping at the thought that things surely wouldn't go that conveniently.

If there were no chain of command like Petelgeuse's, the possibility of that working wouldn't be zero — that was the unfathomable nature of these people whose true form was impossible to grasp — but as things currently stood, Subaru's place in their priorities probably fell below Petelgeuse's. The man would continue, as ever, to be a thorn in his side.

Still, the fact that they'd withdrawn without a fight was good news. The groundwork had gone well — depending on circumstances, the setup could have fallen apart, and hostilities could have broken out right there. Even that encounter, though he'd played it cool, had actually been cutting it fairly close.

He'd grown bold enough to step into a scene like that without flinching — a change in his once-timid self that struck him as faintly amusing. Though by his own analysis, it was less that his resolve was set and more that it was closer to sheer desperation.

"Well, it's not a bad flow, I guess. Just a bit more, c'mon — hang in there, my nerve."

Confirming that the black-clad men had vanished from sight, Subaru set his foot back down on the earth once more.

As he advanced through a world ruled by nothing but the sound of his own breath and footsteps, he felt something close to the illusion that time had stopped, that he was walking alone through an endless, unbroken darkness. But that feeling came to an end fairly quickly, as the trees opened up to reveal a sheer cliff face.

A towering, steeply cut rock face spread out ahead of him, the forest cutting off abruptly as though some vast claw had gouged it away. A great boulder blocked the ground just beneath the cliff, and by whatever principle governed it, if Subaru touched it, the path to a hidden cave would draw him into the cold, dark rock.

But before he could touch the rock, a shadow rose to greet him, its gaze already turned his way.

"—I have been waiting for you, DESU. Oh Devotee of the Beloved."

There stood a man, arms spread wide, his face alight with rapture.

Hollow cheeks, sunken eyes. His deep-green hair had an unhealthy sheen, and the limbs peeking out from beneath his black priestly robes were thin and frail as withered branches. He looked to be in his mid-thirties or so, but the overall impression of frailty was such that being told he was in his fifties wouldn't have come as a surprise.

Only the glinting brightness of his eyes, still fixed unwaveringly on Subaru, burned with a mad light as it poured over him, seeming to lick him up whole.

"I am of the Witch Cult, Archbishop of Sin in charge of 'Sloth.' Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti... DESU!"

Drool dripping from the tip of his extended tongue, the madman — Petelgeuse — cackled as he welcomed Subaru.

* * *

Standing before his mortal enemy Petelgeuse, Subaru became keenly aware of just how calm he himself was.

In his short life, there was probably no one he had hated as much as this man.

He had thought of nothing but killing this man, his vision dyed red, hating him with a hatred that scorched his mind down to nothing but murderous intent.

The worst kind of calamity — one who slaughtered, mercilessly and without pity, the people dear to him, the people he'd grown close to.

He should have been burning with the resolve to snap that neck with his own two hands without fail — and yet, standing before that withered, murderous face now, what Subaru felt was, of all things, a measure of relief.

—The same kind of feeling as when he'd managed to make contact with the Witch Cultists safely just a short while before.

"Welcome, oh Beloved Child cherished by the Witch! Wonderful. Wonderful. Wonderfuuuuuuul! How deep the love that clothes your body! How lofty the love that wraps your body! How ardent the love that embraces your body! That in this place! Someone such as, such as, ssssuch-uch-uch-uch-such! A Devotee of the Beloved should show a new face! How, how utterly wonderful!"

Before Subaru, still lost in that private reflection, Petelgeuse had already shifted into top gear. Shaking his head wildly, clawing at the bony backs of his own hands, spilling copious blood from self-inflicted wounds, Petelgeuse couldn't contain the violent passion of his joy.

The first time it had been fear, the second time hostility; watching this now for the third time, he finally arrived at the ordinary emotion of disgust. Toward Petelgeuse's way of being, all Subaru felt now was an unfamiliar revulsion toward an existence he simply couldn't understand.

Suppressing the urge to let his cheek twitch involuntarily, Subaru let out a deep breath before Petelgeuse. Steadying himself, he lightly raised a hand.

"I'm honored by the warm welcome. Though I gotta say, it's not really sinking in."

"No wonder, DESU! For me too, it began suddenly. Everyone, on some given day, comes to realize that they are 'loved.' And once one has realized it, one can never let go of that love again. —For love is everything, DESU!"

Petelgeuse latched onto the topic Subaru had raised in hopes of finding an opening, delighted. He held out both hands — hands dripping blood — trembling as he spread them wide, and without a shred of self-reflection for how easily he trampled the lives of others, he sang the praises of love. A dreadfully twisted love.

"To love! To the love that has been given! I, we, must answer with industry, DESU! And so, trials — I give trials! I hurl trials! In this world, in this age, in these days, in this hour, in this instant, in this fleeting moment, in order to find meaning in the fact that I have received the Witch's favor, to love, to love, to love, to love, to love, to love, to love, to love, to loveloveloveloveloveloveloveee!!"

"Can't just be lazy about it, huh. Gotta be industrious, to repay love with sincerity."

"Just — so, DESU!!"

At this display of understanding for what he'd said, Petelgeuse threw his head back, his face awash with a joy that couldn't have been greater.

Subaru had no wish to agree with or understand the madman's opinion, but he kept swallowing down the revulsion welling up in him and stayed facing him regardless. For the sake of time, and of gaining even the smallest advantage.

"Uh, so, I'm here to join up with your side... is that about right? Is there like, some procedure, papers I gotta stamp a seal on or something? I don't have my registered seal, so is a thumbprint okay?"

"To join under my banner here, I feel the favor bestowed upon your body shows far, far too strongly... Not even I have been granted a Witch's Scent this rich and fragrant. 'Lust' or 'Wrath,' say, would surely envy it... could it be — might you be 'Pride,' DESU!?"

"Pride...?"

"Of the six seats of the Archbishops of Sin, 'Pride' alone remains vacant, DESU! Until one worthy appears, the Sins would never be complete... but the Witch Factor should already have descended upon the next generation's Pride. —You there, have you received the 'Gospel,' DESU?"

Petelgeuse closed the distance a step toward Subaru, tilting his head a full ninety degrees as he asked.

Whatever the question was aiming for, all Subaru could offer in return was bewilderment. To the question of whether he was 'Pride,' Subaru had no answer. And as for what this 'Gospel' the man spoke of even was, that too lay beyond his comprehension.

Unable to give a careless answer, yet not permitted to let the moment pass in silence either, Subaru found himself falling mute — and Petelgeuse's brow knit in suspicion at that,

"—My brain... is... trembling."

Petelgeuse murmured it in a small, cracked voice.

But from that thin voice, all trace of the trembling, all of the violent rapture from before, had vanished — what remained was an utter emptiness, the kind that stirred anxiety and dread in whoever heard it.

Just standing face to face with him stirred up an anxiety that clawed at him, and Subaru realized, belatedly, that his own breathing had quickened without his noticing.

His heart pounded high in his chest, creaking as though beating against his breastbone until it hurt.

His legs began to tremble in small, quick shivers, and even standing properly threatened to become unsteady unless he consciously willed himself to — within that wind of oppressive pressure, Petelgeuse tilted his head the other way, raised his right hand, and thrust a finger into his own mouth, biting down and crushing it.

"Present your 'Gospel.' Proof of your favor—"

He slid his left hand inside his black priestly robe, and what he drew out was a book bound in jet-black covers.

Roughly the size of a dictionary back in his old world, he opened it deftly with one hand, and as the pages turned, Petelgeuse's bloodshot eyes raced across them. And then—

"In my Gospel, there is no mention of you, DESU. Now then, just why have you appeared here, arrived here — what meaning do you bring to me, DESU?"

"If that book's title is 'Gospel,' then, well. There's kind of a story behind it, but I don't have that book on me right now."

"A story, DESU?"

"Yeah. —Used it as a trivet, got it filthy, and tossed it out."

—This was the watershed moment.

The instant Petelgeuse's ears caught Subaru's flippant answer, his expression twisted into something murderous.

Immediately after, the madman's shadow spread out as if exploding, and from it countless black demonic hands stretched up toward the sky. Every one of those palms was the manifestation of a nightmare, possessing strength enough to tear a human body apart with ease.

They soared high into the sky, and glaring down at Subaru like a snake rearing its head, accelerated all at once, lunging to entangle him.

—Just before that—

"FOLLOW ME!!"

He pulled his hand from his pocket and hurled the magic stone he'd been gripping up toward the sky.

Dyed in red, an aggressive color, its destination wasn't Petelgeuse but the sky itself. Rising high, the magic stone thrown into the dawn-lit sky burst, releasing a flash of light.

At the same moment, Subaru's body threw itself backward in an all-out backstep, putting whatever distance he could between himself and Petelgeuse ahead of him, pulling even slightly out of the reach of the black palms closing in to tear his limbs off.

"Too naive, DESU! Naive, naive, naive, naive, naive-ive-ive-ive-ive-iveeeeeeeeeeee!! DESU!!"

As Subaru shifted into a stance of flight, Petelgeuse stepped forward, extending the 'Unseen Hand' to strike.

Seven demonic hands closed in on Subaru in a swirling motion from above, below, and every side, seething to deliver heaven's judgment upon the traitor who spurned the Witch's favor.

But—

"Awoo—!"
"Haaah—!!"

The overlapping howls shook the air and unleashed tremendous destruction upon the earth.

The rocky ground peeled upward, clods of earth caught in the wind and thrown skyward, and the resulting fissures spread across the earth like a spider's web, carving claw marks into the sheer cliff face.

"Wha—!?"

Before the eyes of Petelgeuse as he turned around, the combined technique of the pair of Demi-human siblings blasted apart the great boulders piled high beneath the cliff. —Which is to say, that act also sealed off one of the Witch Cult's meeting places that had been hidden behind the rock,

"Buried alive, and good riddance. —Suffer, and apologize for what you've done."

Subaru raised his middle finger, baring his teeth as he snarled the declaration ferociously.

Before Petelgeuse's trembling eyes, the rock wall could no longer bear the destruction spreading from the cracks, and most of it collapsed feebly, crushing the Witch Cultists it had been concealing beneath it, all at once.

As dust rose and the shock rolled through the ground underfoot in a rumble, the now-isolated Petelgeuse opened his mouth wide, tears streaming in torrents from his eyes,

"What a... what a thing... to do...!!"

He raised both hands and clawed at his own head, catching hair between his fingers and ripping it out. Hair tore free under the violent motion, blood spilling from his scalp, and still unable to exhaust his fury, he stamped his feet against the ground,

"My Fingers... to be so mercilessly, so pitilessly, so lawlessly, so randomly, so carelessly, so meaninglessly, killed, murdered, annihilated, extinguished... ahh, ahh, ahhh! My brain! My brain! My braaain... is trembling, embling-embling-embling-emblingggg"

"Uhh, that old man's kinda scary~"

"Witch Cultists are like that no matter who you ask, Sis."

Watching Petelgeuse wallow in grief like a child, the two Demi-human siblings — Mimi and Tivey — lowered their voices to each other, sounding thoroughly unnerved.

Having erased their presence and traveled behind Subaru all along, the two of them had burst out at the signal of the magic stone's light and, exactly as planned, immediately sealed off the path for reinforcements.

Petelgeuse slowly turned a glare on the two who had just accomplished a feat worth its weight in gold.

What Mimi saw in that expression — invisible to Subaru, whose back was turned — was something that made her, who had never once shown a hint of negative emotion until now,

"Eeeek..."

let out a small cry, her shoulders trembling as she shrank back, eyes going wide.

Beside her, her brother Tivey, his gaze so similar to his sister's yet more intellectual, mingled disgust and fear in his eyes, his body stiffening without a sound escaping him. And then—

"Be 'Sloth'—"

The instant Petelgeuse spoke those words, black mist spread explosively across the world.

With a force and speed that seemed to blot out his entire field of vision, Subaru — who had no means prepared to resist it — felt his throat freeze at the jet-black mass bearing down on him as if to crush him.

Could it be — some hidden trick beyond even the 'Unseen Hand'—

"Huh?"

A blank, foolish sound slipped from Subaru's throat as he raised a hand and tilted his head, looking at it.

The black mist should have slammed against his entire body. It had unmistakably swept over the whole of him — and yet he felt nothing out of the ordinary.

"What... is it?"

He checked his unaffected limbs, blinking in bewilderment, voicing his confusion. But in contrast to Subaru, who'd taken no damage whatsoever,

"—Guh..."

the two Demi-humans let out pained groans and collapsed right where they stood.

Mimi and Tivey, faces rigid, sweat pouring down them, gasped and opened and closed their mouths as though even breathing had become difficult.

It was the damage from what Petelgeuse had just done.

And then, toward the two of them as they lay motionless, Petelgeuse's towering shadow began to writhe once more, its black demonic hands turning their fingertips toward the pair—

"—Hey, you! Don't you dare ignore me! I'm your opponent!"

"...Ah, yes, that's, DESU, right."

Stamping the ground, spit flying, Subaru roared. Hearing this, Petelgeuse turned back as if reflecting on his own reflexive response, gazing at Subaru with hollow eyes that reflected nothing at all. Those empty eyes dimly took Subaru in,

"So it truly has no effect, DESU."

"Huh?"

Unlike Mimi and the others, Subaru remained unaffected by Petelgeuse's just-uttered declaration.

Petelgeuse's demeanor shifted entirely as he fixed Subaru with a calm expression, his violent passion having passed,

"Good, DESU. That's good, DESU. —That's fine, that's good, DESU! DESUDESUDESUDESUDESUDESUDESUDESUDESUUUUUUUUUUUSU!!"

He bowed his head forward, then snapped his back upright as if bouncing, crying out at the top of his voice before bursting into cackling laughter. After laughing his fill, Petelgeuse pointed at Subaru with a liveliness he'd never shown before, blood dripping from the crushed tip of his finger,

"Good, DESU. I understand, DESU! Let's do this, let us do this, DESU. Between myself and you, let us compete over which of us is more worthy of receiving the Witch's favor, her love, her ardor! Ahh, to love, to love, to loveeeeeeee!!"

"...Hate to spoil the mood while you're all fired up, but."

Tapping the ground with the toe of his boot, Subaru watched Petelgeuse loudly proclaim their contest.

Petelgeuse narrowed his eyes suspiciously at Subaru's attitude, so far removed from any fighting spirit,

"What is it, DESU!? Right now! At this very moment! I! Am about to stake my own fate and take up the trial—"

"Your opponent's already been arranged — someone else."

Subaru told him coldly, cutting off Petelgeuse, who still pointed a finger and tried to press on.

At that answer, Petelgeuse's eyes went wide, and just as he opened his mouth to voice a question—

"GYAAAAAAAAAH—!!"

Threading through the gaps between the trees, a blade fell in a diagonal cut, entering at Petelgeuse's right shoulder and bursting out beneath his left arm — the old swordsman's strike had cloven his body in two on a slant.
