Arc 6 Chapter 30: "Second Floor Post-Mortem"

—A woman. There was a woman.

The woman grieved for everything. She grieved for people, for conflict, for the world, for all things without exception.
To the woman, everything looked small. Everything, everyone, seemed like a small child in need of protection.

Sweep her arm, and everything would scatter into a thousand pieces — ruling over such people, such a world, would have been easy.
But the woman decided to use her arm not for dominion, but for protection.

The woman's solitary struggle began. The trial and error that followed was not easy, even for her.
Guiding people, binding people together, lending people a hand, offering people counsel —
none of it could be reached by a woman alone. Her hand could not stretch far enough. She could not arrive at what needed to be done.

For the first time, the woman came to curse her own lack of power.
Even seeing danger coming, her hands were not enough. Even when someone fell into dire straits, she could not save them. That lack of power.
What lay before her, she could reach; the distance she could not reach, she could not close. Even seeing everything laid bare, she could not change what had already happened. She had no life enough to deal with the calamities that lay far ahead.

She had believed she had strength. It fell far short of her wish.
She had been arrogant enough to believe she had wisdom. It showed her no path toward her hope.

And so the woman despaired. She ground down, endlessly, endlessly, the curse of her own insufficiency.
Struck by every regret imaginable, torn apart infinitely by the grief of others.

Eventually the woman understood. Impossible. Hopeless. It could not be done.
This was a great wish too large for one woman's hands, unbefitting even to hope for.

She knew this. She understood it. She was made to realize its impossibility. —And yet, she did not give up.

If one was not enough, then increase the number.
If she could not do it alone, then by gathering others who held the same wish, the wish that could not be reached could be reached.

Her mind grew clear. She began to see what she could not see before.
The grief that could not be reached, the tragedies that could not be saved, the cruelty that repeated itself without end — she would pluck them out with this wish.

She joined hands with an instigator, gave the Heavenly Sword a battlefield, struck a bargain with a Dragon, and pressed forward toward her great wish.

Someday it would arrive, the conclusion of her cherished desire.
Eventually it would come to pass, the outcome of her sworn vow.
Someday, eventually, without fail, the fulfillment of the great wish would come—.

"I will save them. I will show that I can save them. I can see the path to salvation. —I know the path to salvation."

However much she might be wounded, however much she might lose along the way, she would abandon only one thing: giving up.
Even when the instigator vanished, even when the Heavenly Sword forgot how to smile, even when the Dragon flew off into the far sky, she would not give up.
Even having lost her first ally, her footsteps did not stop. The woman was no longer alone.

"It has to exist. I know it existed once. —I want to know it again."

She walked a road paved with countless corpses. She aimed for the fulfillment of a great wish bought with countless sacrificed lives.
Someday, her hand would surely reach every tear. When that happened, there was a world she would come to understand.

Only then, for the first time, could she reach her original wish.
And so, seeking that, desperately, the woman endured unbearable pain and kept struggling.

—The 'Witch of Greed' burns her body for a wish she cannot reach, and goes on bleeding for her own greed.

"—Julius, you are absolutely, positively going to rest right here until your wounds heal! No arguments!"

Emilia raised her voice as she shoved a battered Julius into the green room.

Subaru and Julius had descended, leaning on each other's shoulders, down the long staircase connecting the second floor, "Electra," to the fourth. This exchange came right after Emilia, having greeted the two and breathed a sigh of relief, rounded on them.
Her quick change of gears was admirable, and her no-arguments-allowed manner was a blessing. It was a swift enough maneuver that it gave no room to ask for reasons, let alone make excuses. In truth, Emilia probably had a mountain of things she wanted to say to Julius, too—

"—Whatever needed saying, I'm sure Subaru already said it for me. So for now, just rest properly, and everything else can wait. Right?"

"…Ah, that's the ruling. I basically surrender unconditionally to anything Emilia-tan says, and this time I don't even have grounds to object, so — same opinion. Get some sleep."

At the entrance to the green room, Subaru shrugged at Julius, who had seated himself on the vine bed. Emilia stood at his side, hands on her hips, breathing hard through her nose. Even like that, she was adorable.
Receiving both their words, Julius touched his forelock and let out a breath.

"Of course. I have already caused you and Emilia-sama far too much trouble. At this point, I would be shameless indeed to defy your instructions. I will obey without complaint."

"You could've just said 'understood' instead of going on and on…"

"Exactly! It's not about how we feel or anything like that. You're the one who's hurt, Julius, so you have to stay still until it heals — that's the whole point! You can trouble us as much as you like. We're comrades."

"――――"

"Patrasche, please look after Julius. If anything strange happens again, cry out loud and call us, all right?"

Cutting off Julius's roundabout gratitude without ceremony, Emilia — leaving him rather taken aback — turned to the back of the room, where Patrasche was healing her wounds, and spoke to her.

The personnel left in the green room numbered three people and one dragon — the unconscious Rem and Anastasia, along with the injured Julius and Patrasche. Since the green room had a limit on how many people it could accommodate at once, Subaru and Emilia couldn't stay behind to keep watch either.
So the better choice was to have someone already receiving treatment inside keep an eye on the others. Normally, that role should have fallen to Julius.

"Being told so plainly by Emilia-sama leaves me no room to argue. I shall obediently hang my head here."

"Lost trust is hard to win back. And on that front, nobody can hold a candle to Patrasche, who always scores top marks in the trust department. If anything happens, feel free to bite him without mercy, Patrasche."

"――――"

Patrasche gave a low sound in her throat, accepting Subaru and Emilia's request — or so it seemed she had, at least.
Patrasche, the Emilia Camp's premier "woman who gets it," had the look of someone who felt responsible for having let Julius go off alone earlier.
Her determined bearing, burning with resolve that there would absolutely not be a next time, was enough to make one bow one's head in gratitude.

"See? Patrasche says, 'There shall be no second time you slip past me,' too."

"It's strange how it really does seem like she's saying exactly that."

"Well, according to our militant home minister's bilingual skills, that's more or less the right nuance. Though since she's a lady, the ending might be more like 'shall'."

Whenever Otto occasionally translated for him via his Divine Protection of the Soul of Language, Subaru was often struck anew by the depth of Patrasche's character and how attached she was to him. Lately he'd started to feel he understood her heart-to-heart even without Otto's help. Though saying that out loud tended to earn Patrasche's displeasure and a swat of her tail, so a maiden's — well, dragon's — heart was complicated.
In any case—

"I shall content myself with devoting myself quietly to treating my wounds. Being surrounded by fair ladies and recuperating at my leisure is a luxury in itself."

"For the record, every woman in this room besides Anastasia-san belongs to me."

At Julius's glib remark, Subaru pointed in turn at Rem on the bed, at Patrasche, and finally at Emilia beside him, and insisted as much.
Hearing that, Emilia puffed out her cheeks with an "Mm," and said,

"I'm not yours yet. …Actually, now that I think about it — you're my knight, so shouldn't it be the other way around, that you're mine?"

"That's a wildly happy and embarrassing way to put it!"

With a finger to her lips, Emilia's remark could, depending on how one heard it, sound rather bold, and Subaru's emotions swung between joy and embarrassment over it. Even so, he turned once more to look at Julius, whom they were leaving behind in the room — as if to check whether Julius had found, if nothing else, enough peace of mind to set foot on the first step of the long climb ahead of him.
It would take time to get over the peak of his anguish. But at least, whether he'd found room in his heart to take that first step.

"Anyway, while you're resting, you might feel like clutching your head and rolling around remembering what just happened, but don't forget Patrasche's watching."

"Rest assured. I won't display such an unsightly spectacle. —It wouldn't be elegant, after all."

"…Look who's getting his groove back."

"Heh."

There was something distinctly Julius-like in his reply, and Subaru's cheeks eased with a measure of relief.

Two defeats — and utterly one-sided defeats at that, where he couldn't lay a hand on his opponent.
That should have carved an unbearable humiliation and sense of helplessness into Julius. But somehow, it seemed he'd weathered that first wave.

It was good that they hadn't left Julius alone on that long staircase.

If that had brought him even a little relief, then Subaru's own experience of being humiliated before a great crowd back at the royal castle had been worth learning from.

"—Patrasche, I'm counting on you."

With a final word of reassurance to his beloved dragon, Subaru left the green room together with Emilia.
As they departed, he sensed Patrasche drawing close to Julius, and Julius wearing a wry smile at her vigilant watch.
As expected of Patrasche — faithful to her orders, a wise dragon indeed.
Between Julius's mental state and Patrasche's loyal dragon spirit, they could probably stop worrying about the personnel left in the green room for now.

"…Should we freeze the door shut and lock them in?"

"Emilia-tan's infinite well of ideas never stops surprising me, but let's save that as a last resort. I don't much like the idea of ticking off the green room's spirit doing that, either."

"Mm, that's true. Fufu, I was just saying it. It was a joke."

Emilia stuck out her tongue cutely in apology for the joke. Smiling at her reaction, Subaru kept to himself the fact that he'd also been holding that as one option in his own back pocket.

—Worst case, he had genuinely considered forcibly restraining Julius by sheer force.
In that scenario, the best-suited person to stop him — both in ability and in standing — was Emilia. Fortunately, Julius's mental state had turned toward stability, sparing them from having to resort to such a drastic option. Subaru was relieved too.

"Anyway, let's leave the green room to the spirit in charge of it. From what I saw, Patrasche's wounds seemed to be healing nicely, and Julius doesn't seem like he'll take that long either."

"Yeah, that's right. Julius's wound… isn't as bad as it looks, so I think it'll probably heal soon. That seems to be Reid's way of doing things."

"…Good at holding back, huh. Better not let Julius hear that."

Subaru scratched his head in agreement with Emilia's carefully worded guess.
That his weapon of choice was a stick was the ultimate in mockery, but for Reid to treat someone of Julius's caliber like an infant with it spoke to just how far his combat prowess outstripped everyone else's.
The first Sword Saint, the man credited alongside the Sage and a Dragon with defeating the 'Witch of Envy' — with a title like that, becoming a legendary hero, well, it made a certain twisted sense.
Whether his personality was fit to be revered as a 'legendary hero' was another matter entirely.

"For now, our discussion is about…"

"—How to break through this second 'test,' before Sir Julius's wound heals. Right?"

"――――"

A cold voice cut into Subaru and Emilia's conversation. Looking over, it was Ram, leaning her back against the corridor wall, waiting for the two of them to return.
Barred by the green room's occupancy limit, Ram had been kept waiting in the corridor. Having her pin his thoughts so precisely, Subaru rubbed at his own cheeks and said,

"Is my face really that expressive, giving away that much of my inner thoughts?"

"Your worries were simply written all over your face, all bundled together. Right now, the only person or reason Barusu has to worry about is in that room. That's all there is to it."

"That's not it at all. It's not limited to the people in there — I'm worried about everyone who's with us. Emilia-tan and Beako, sure, but also about you, Nee-sama."

"Hah!"

At Subaru's thumbs-up reply, Ram scoffed derisively through her nose.
Then, as Ram turned her back and started walking, Subaru pouted, and beside him Emilia laughed softly behind her hand,

"It's all right. Ram's just a little embarrassed. She's not honest with herself."

"That feels like way too generous an interpretation, but if Emilia-tan says so, well."

Glancing sidelong at Emilia's faint smile, Subaru cracked his neck and followed after Ram. Ram was heading toward one of the small rooms on the fourth floor.
Stepping into that room—

"…Aren't you late? You kept me waiting too long. Is Julius all right?"

The voice that greeted him belonged to Beatrice, who was seated flat on the floor in the middle of the room. She looked up at Subaru and Emilia with narrowed eyes, and in a phrasing that came off vaguely like a scolding, expressed her worry over Julius. Subaru gave a wry smile at her less-than-honest attitude.

"Relax. Looks like he's over the worst of it, for now. He's got a needlessly strong sense of responsibility, so he'll probably keep agonizing over this and that, but… he's not going to do anything self-destructive anymore."

"If Subaru says so, well, that's probably good enough to believe, I suppose. That way, we can narrow it down to one problem, which is a relief, I suppose."

Having received confirmation of Julius's safety, Beatrice tipped her chin down. Subaru nodded at her remark, then looked around the room once more.
The small room was one of several open spaces on the fourth floor, used for storing the luggage and belongings of Subaru's watchtower assault party. Sitting in a circle in that room, faces gathered close, were Subaru and Emilia, plus Ram and Beatrice. And besides them—

"I'd like to hear the details about that bastard from you, Shaula."

"Uhii, Master's so scaaary! But-but, being treated so strict like that, ah-shi doesn't hate it either. They say hating it is just another kind of loving it, y'know."

"So she claims, Teacher. Lewd."

"False accusation!"

Hugging her own voluptuously feminine body and writhing about was Shaula, with Meili clinging to her. Ram piled on to her wild antics, but as far as Subaru was concerned, this was a wrongful conviction if ever there was one.
In any case—

"First off, everyone, good work on the Julius search. I know he's going to apologize himself later no matter how much I tell him not to, but anyway — he's safe."

"It's fine. I already told Julius this too, but I was just glad he was found safe. Right? That goes for everyone else too, doesn't it?"

"Please don't lump me together with Emilia-sama."

"Eh!? What do you mean by that!?"

Subaru, trying to take the lead and move the discussion along, found that a rift in opinion had already opened up between Emilia and Ram.
To the startled Emilia, Ram raised a single finger and said, "May I?"

"Going by the report, this second 'test' is something everyone who has come to the tower must clear. And yet, Sir Julius made a unilateral decision to challenge it a second time… If that had gone wrong even slightly, it could have put a crack in our cooperative relationship with Anastasia-sama's camp as well."

"Because everyone's attempt might have failed because of Julius's actions alone?"

"Had that happened, everything up to this point would have been for nothing. It's doubtful whether the examiner on the second floor would have even let us return safely. …Including that self-styled sage over there."

Ram's gaze, which had been explaining things to Emilia, flicked briefly over to Shaula. Perhaps not expecting the finger to be pointed at her, Shaula pointed at herself with an "Ah-shi?"

"Self-styled sage, is that ah-shi you're talking about!? That name, seriously offends ah-shi! Ah-shi never called herself a sage on her own! The only name ah-shi goes by is Shaula, the one Master gave her! Ah-shi's one and only Master, forever!"

"Lewd."

"Stop saying that while looking at me! …Not that your point is wrong, exactly."

Though she may have overstated it somewhat, Ram's guess wasn't wrong if one assumed the worst-case scenario. Julius's actions had put the entire group at risk.
Even accounting for the fact that he hadn't fully grasped the details of the second 'test' — no, precisely because of that, it could be called all the more reckless.

"To say it's unlike Sir Julius is something Ram can't say, because Ram doesn't know him well enough. Even accounting for the matter of 'Gluttony,' Ram thought he wasn't the type to do something like that."

"Yeah, I agree with you there too, but… there's a difference between not understanding it and how I feel about it. It's like a rite of passage for men."

"A rite of passage?"

"Measles works too, if you'd rather."

"――――"

Either way, he meant it in the sense of "an illness everyone catches once."
Calling Julius's unilateral decision this time an example of that made it sound trivial, but measles or chickenpox could do real damage if caught as an adult.
All the more so if it flared up at a critical moment.

"It just so happened it wasn't fatal this time. …So let's just call that a win for now."

"—Ram just doesn't want her legs pulled out from under her by someone else, that's all."

Averting her gaze from Subaru, Ram murmured that in a small voice.
Her cheeks stiffened faintly. Seeing that, Subaru raised an eyebrow.

"The older sister of the pair hasn't seemed to be in very good shape since arriving at the tower, I suppose. Maybe it's the fatigue from the long journey, or maybe the treatment for her horn isn't taking well, I suppose."

"Or there's something else she's worried about… which, yeah, that's probably it."

Sitting right beside him, Subaru murmured back and forth in low tones with Beatrice, who was holding his hand.
Ram taking out her frustration over Julius's rash decision, and her irritation at not seeing a way to clear the floor, on Subaru and Shaula — that was far too unlike her usual self.
The cause of it was probably the frustration of having the means to save Rem right in front of her eyes, yet being unable to reach it.
Of course, the fatigue and the wound on her horn that Beatrice had pointed out were probably not unrelated either.

Being less than whole, in body and mind alike.
That was what was robbing Ram of her usual composure.

"So, do you all wanna fight? Or do you wanna talk? Make up your minds, 'cause I can't keep up with both."

The one who called a halt to the atmosphere just as it threatened to turn sour was Meili.
Snuggled up against Shaula, twirling her own braid, she turned a languid gaze on everyone in the room and said,

"Can we please skip the fighting? I don't like pain, and I don't like being scared, either."

"You're… no, that's a fair point. It helps having you around to play the bystander."

"Really? Ufufufu, then you'd better be thankful."

At Subaru's thanks, Meili smiled innocently, the way a girl her age should. And yet that smile carried a faint hint of something bewitching, quietly hinting at the precariousness of her nature.

Even under such a smile, Subaru held genuine gratitude for Meili's words, exactly as he'd said. Looking back, their retreat from the earlier 'test' had also been thanks to something Meili said.
Depending on how things had gone, escaping a crisis that could have endangered everyone was thanks to her contribution — a companion who nonetheless kept a step back and a clearer view of things.

"Keep it up like that going forward. Having a cool head in the mix matters a lot."

"You're so full of it. Even if you say that, my job's over once we're past the sand sea, right? There's nothing else useful I can do."

"That's not true, though? More heads thinking is always a help, and including how we survived the sand sea, we're all in this together inside the tower. Just chalk it up to bad luck and let me lean on you."

"――――"

Meili was momentarily struck speechless by Subaru's brazen declaration of dependence. Then, after a long sigh, she said,

"…Now I get why Petra-chan never gets any peace."

"—? What about Petra?"

"Nothing. More importantly, you want to hear from the naked lady over there, don't you?"

Turning her face away with a huff, Meili stood and shoved Shaula's back forward, hard. It probably wasn't her thin arms' strength alone that did it, but Shaula obligingly shuffled forward, knelt right in front of Subaru, and bowed her head flat against the floor.

"Ah-shi's an unworthy woman, but please treat her kindly for many years to come."

"I appreciate the humble attitude. So, as part of the betrothal gifts, I want to ask you— ow ow ow ow! Emilia-tan!? Beako!? Why are you both pinching my sides from left and right!?"

"No reason." "It's nothing, I suppose."

Having his sides pinched over "nothing" was more than he could bear, but the looks on Emilia's and Beatrice's faces made him hesitate to press further.
Anyway, Subaru cleared his throat and turned back to Shaula properly.

"The one up top was Reid Astrea, no mistake about it. He admitted it himself. So, I want to hear from you, the living witness from back in his day. What kind of guy was he?"

"A total human trash-heap."

"I already heard that, and I've confirmed it with my own two eyes, too."

Twisting her lips into an expression no pretty girl should make, Shaula reminisced about the deceased. Setting aside the fact that said deceased was currently on the floor directly above them in the same building, it was clearly not a fond memory.
Which made sense, given that just seeing his face was enough to make her foam at the mouth and pass out.

"We absolutely have to get past that guy. I want to squeeze out even one more hint for clearing the second floor's 'test.'"

"Anything that comes to mind, tell me, no matter how small. The Sword Saint Reid's personality, habits, relationships, likes, dislikes, weaknesses. Yes, I want to hear about weaknesses. Talk."

"You're leaning in real hard here! If ah-shi knew any weaknesses, ah-shi would've used 'em for payback already! Which means — there aren't any!"

"Tch. Useless."

"You're bossier than Master, this girl…"

Whether it was pride from once being called 'Sage' or not, Shaula pouted her lips at Ram's rather high-handed, domineering attitude. But at a single glare from Ram, she shrank back and scurried behind Subaru, using him as a shield.

"Why are you hiding? You're definitely stronger than me."

"It's not a matter of strong or weak, ssu. Somehow, instinct just makes ah-shi react like this. Probably 'cause Master's scared of her. That fear's coming through to ah-shi, since ah-shi and Master are of one heart and mind."

"Don't pin your own cowardice on me."

Unsettled by the soft sensation pressing against his back, Subaru grabbed Shaula by the scruff of the neck and forcibly dragged her back to her original spot despite her protests.
And so, the round of questions and answers resumed—

"Um… so basically, Shaula doesn't know anything about the 'test,' right?"
"It's not that ah-shi doesn't know, ssu. It's just that now's not the time to speak of it yet, ssu. Probably, all the answers will be revealed once the tower's mysteries are solved, ssu."
"I see… that's soooo exciting."
"Don't fool the innocent Emilia-tan."

"Even if we don't know the Sword Saint's weaknesses, does he have any habits or tics? If he has some habit when fighting, that might give us an opening."
"Habits, ssu? Oh yeah, come to think of it, when ah-shi tried to kill Reid for payback after his sexual harassment, he kept scratching his own butt while fighting, ssu! Isn't that a habit?"
"That's just him messing with you…"

"To begin with, the conditions for clearing the 'test' feel vague, I suppose. It's beyond doubt that Emilia was recognized, but there must be some other condition — something that makes him acknowledge someone, I suppose."
"Probably, he's weak to pretty women. Ah-shi's guess is that pretty women can just pass through."
"So then it's just me and Julius left behind… that's a grave situation."
"S-Subaru could probably pass too, depending on how you look at it, if you try hard, maybe if you gouge out that man's eyes, I suppose, I can't say it's completely impossible that you might get through, I suppose…!"
"You're so cute, you know that?"

And so, exchanging a fairly unhelpful round of questions and answers, Subaru hugged Beatrice — who had tried her best not to hurt his feelings — and ruffled her hair.
Basking in Subaru's arms, letting herself be doted on, Beatrice said,

"Either way, it was pure chance that Emilia was recognized, I suppose. That man let his guard down, and it just happened to be Emilia's attack that landed, I suppose."

"And the meaning behind that is?"

"If Emilia had struck meaning to kill, that man wouldn't have let it land in the first place, I suppose. So that was the man's carelessness, and Emilia's victory, I suppose."

"Huh? Wait, am I being praised right now?"

"You were praised, I suppose."

"Ah, I knew it. Fufu, thank you. That makes me so happy."

At Beatrice's deduction and the compliment that came from it, Emilia beamed with delight and further petted Beatrice, who was already being petted by Subaru.
It was a fairly muddled scene by now, but a heartwarming one all the same.

"Still, saying it depends on whether he intends to kill you or not sounds cool on paper, but seeing the real thing, it's hard to say how serious he ever really is."

"…We should flip the way we're thinking about it. Like Barusu said, we don't know how serious that man is being, but the point is — we must not let him get serious."

"Not let him get serious?"

Picking up on Ram's murmured, thoughtful remark, Subaru knitted his brow. At his words, Ram, still lost in thought, continued with a "That's right,"

"The reason Emilia-sama was recognized by the examiner was that she drew out a concession from her opponent, and satisfied the conditions on that basis… The conditions for clearing this test are considerably loose."

"I agree it's a fuzzy condition. It reflects the examiner's own temperament."

"Which means the condition for clearing the second floor is to entertain the examiner while presenting terms that qualify as a proper test, and then defeat him on those terms."

"――――"

Taking in Ram's words, Subaru inwardly clapped his hands — of course.
Emilia's victory, secured by setting the condition of "if he moves even a single step," and scraping it out through a massive number of attempts plus a bit of luck — including Reid's own carelessness — was a win achieved under the loosest possible conditions. A victory wrung out by sheer force should probably now be considered impossible.

"Still, it's not like we can just say 'whoever wins rock-paper-scissors' either…"

"We find conditions that convince Reid, and work hard within those… this 'test' really is one we have to think hard about too, huh."

"More than hard, I'd say this is a nasty test in a different way from the third floor."

Having been tested on knowledge (specifically, knowledge that didn't exist in this world), and now expecting to be tested on martial strength (of a world-class caliber) — only to find the real subject lay somewhere else entirely, it seemed. That was the 'test' as best they could guess.
In other words, the second floor's 'test,' while different in flavor from the third floor's, was without a doubt another expression of the 'Sage's' spiteful streak in designing this watchtower.
As for the rest—

"—C'mon, isn't it fine? No need to rush so much, just take it slow, ssu."

"Slow, huh…"

Looking around at the pondering group, Shaula, sitting cross-legged and rocking side to side, spoke up breezily. Everyone frowned at that, but she paid it no mind, her eyes simply glittering with delight,

"For as long as Master and everyone wanna stay, stay forever and ever and ever, that's fine by ah-shi, ssu. Ah-shi's been waiting hundreds of years for Master to come, after all, ssu."

"That's…"

"Take as much time as you like clearing the 'test' properly, step by step, ssu. Ah-shi'll be watching over you the whole time, ssu. —However many days, however many years, however many hundreds of years it takes."

Those words carried a weight that couldn't be laughed off as a careless joke.
In the words Shaula spun in her light tone, with a smile, in a manner that held nothing but goodwill toward Subaru and the others — no, toward Subaru — lay the weight of the hundreds of years she had spent here.

Here lay the weight of a guardian who had continued watching over the tower, obeying the 'Sage's' words all this while.

Shaula had said it herself.
That leaving without completing the 'test' was forbidden. And that if that rule were ever broken, she would show no mercy — even to Subaru, whom she adored and called Master.

Being fond of someone, being friendly toward someone, did not make them an ally.
In the conquest of the Pleiades Watchtower, Shaula too, in her role as the Star Watcher, was—

"—Just stay here and have fun with ah-shi forever, ssu!"

—Not someone they could trust as an ally. That smile drove the truth of it home.
