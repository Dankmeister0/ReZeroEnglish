Arc 10 Chapter 20: "Sheep, Mirror, Dog, Mask"


—Red.
That single color completely filled Subaru's field of vision.

"—!"

The man in the clown mask had appeared out of nowhere. Knowing nothing of his affiliation or his motives, he had hurled a massive fireball at Subaru and the others in place of a hello and a goodbye.
The surge of heat scorched their skin, illuminating the dim sewer as brightly as midday.
However, that only served to stoke a certain kind of despair, making them clearly see the flames spreading across the narrow waterway to a size that left no avenue for escape.

Carrying Crusch on his back, virtually running on empty after using up his daily limit of E·M·T and E·M·M, Subaru had no means to deal with this—

"Shit!"

came a coarsely spat-out curse, followed by a viscous-sounding splash.
At the edge of his vision, Rachins, who should have been beside him, was gone. Having been just as startled by the clown mask's appearance as Subaru, he reacted faster to the approaching flames—diving headfirst without hesitation into the sewer water beside the walkway, the sole escape route.

"Subaru! Betty and the others should also—"

"No—"

Taking a cue from Rachins's emergency judgment, Beatrice grabbed the hem of Subaru's tracksuit. Frail and lightweight as she was, she tried with all her strength to drag Subaru down into the sewer water.
However, Subaru dug his feet in, resisting Beatrice's effort. At his unexpected reaction, Beatrice squeaked, "Subaru?!" her voice turning upside down, but—

"Trust me, Beatrice."

"..."

Her eyes widening, Beatrice stared intently at Subaru, who had spoken with absolute conviction. Their eyes locked, and Beatrice held her breath as her round eyes, filled with their characteristic pattern, trembled.
And before she could say anything, the mass of flames closed in right before their eyes.
That overwhelming firepower mercilessly incinerated the standing Subaru, Beatrice, and Crusch on his back—or rather, it didn't.

"—!"

The flames surged close, scorching their skin, threatening to vaporize every drop of moisture in their bodies—just as the heatwave scorched Subaru's bangs, it abruptly vanished.
As if by magic, as if the sun had been hidden right before their eyes, the brightness of the sewer plummeted in an instant. All that remained was the lamp in Subaru's hand, the weak light produced by the fragments of Lagmite ore lying on the walkway, and the heated, foul sewer air left behind by the extinguished flames.
—No, that wasn't all.

"—Why?"

"Huh...?"

"Why? How? Just now, in that moment, at that place... why didn't you move?"

In the hazy gloom, the clown-masked man's white faceplate hovered.
Tilting his head and angling his mask, the clown-masked man wore a look of bewilderment—in fact, at some point he had swapped the stereotypical villainous mask he wore earlier for a different one, this one featuring X's over the eyeholes.
Finding his foolish antics exasperating, Subaru blew away his singed bangs with a puff of breath.

"You prefaced this by saying you had business with Crusch-san. If so, it'd be pretty weird if you tried to burn her to death along with us."

"—. Heh, ehe, kuhahaha! That's all? You stood your ground for that reason and nothing else?! Even though you risked dragging your Great Spirit partner into it too?!"

"...But I won the gamble."

"What a confident guy~! As expected of the head knight of a Royal Selection candidate! So that's how you've carved your path so far and up to now—by believing in your own decisiveness and insight! Amazing, wonderful, inspiring! I'm utterly jealous!"

Contrary to his loudly praising words, the clown-masked man spoke in a tone that didn't hold Subaru in high regard at all as he applauded grandly. As loud applause tore through the air, echoing hollowly in the sewer, Subaru grit his teeth and silently endured the insults disguised as praise.
It was all to avoid needlessly provoking his opponent, but—

"That was simply the result of Subaru's cleverness outmatching your schemes, I suppose. To ignore that and launch petty personal attacks is truly foolish behavior, I suppose."

In place of Subaru, who hadn't snapped back, Beatrice bit back at their adversary with a sharp glare. In response, the clown-masked man placed a hand over his head with a "Tahaa~!" and said:

"How touching, how loving~! A spirit and their contractor are like a key and lock fitted perfectly together. Neither can exist without the other. Which is preeeecisely why~! A bond can be either medicine or poison~!"

"You are impossible to reason with, I suppose... Such nastiness reminds me of the Sin Archbishops, I suppose."

"Wait, wait, wait, wait, give me a break! Of all things, a Sin Archbishop! I can't stand being lumped in with those freaks, and besides, aren't you the ones being suspected of that in the first place?"

"Suspected? Of what..."

"Of being a Sin Archbishop, that's what! Up above, everyone's abuzz with that rumor, Natsuki Subaru. 'Inside, he might be the Sin Archbishop of Sloth! What should we do, what should we do?!' It's total pandemonium!"

"—!"

Waving his hand, the clown-masked man taunted him as he swapped his mask back to its original expression. Every single thing about his attitude was infuriating, but Subaru had no leeway to address it.
Being treated like a Sin Archbishop weighed that heavily on Subaru's mind.

"Me, a Sin Archbishop..."

"There is a limit to absurd speculation, I suppose. In that case, how do you explain Subaru thoroughly thrashing the Witch Cult until now? Would you call that internal infighting, I suppose?"

"Well, I don't know, I don't care, it doesn't matter! Nobody is interested in the truth—maybe there's someone scheming to spread whatever convenient information works in their favor, spun just the way they like it. Guys like that... are really annoying, aren't they?"

Even while speaking, the clown-masked man swayed his body exaggeratedly, unable to stand still. However, that last added sentence sounded like the true feelings of the eccentric man in front of him.
Sensing that, Subaru took a deep breath in, exhaled heavily, and then—

"In the end..."

"Hmm? Nn-un? Hmmm~?"

"What is your goal? I get that you're trying to take Crusch-san. But for what purpose, and for whose sake? Dressed like that, I don't buy for a second that you're a soldier of the Kingdom."

The fact that he was not only being pursued by Kingdom soldiers but also treated like a Sin Archbishop gave Subaru—who was practically allergic to the Sin Archbishops—an urge to break out in hives, but he put that aside for now.
Focusing the problem solely on the clown-masked man before him, a natural question arose.
His objective was securing Crusch's person. Yet he was no ally of the Kingdom soldiers. —If so, the logical conclusion was that he was an associate of the group that had set Subaru and the others up.

Honestly, even putting Crusch aside, Subaru and Beatrice—running on empty as they were—had no chance of winning a head-on fight against someone capable of wielding magic of that caliber.
They had to drag out information, buy time, and find a breakthrough somehow—and right then:

"—? Hey, what's that?"

"Hmmm~? What is it, what's wrong? If you're trying to buy time... Gegeh!"

At Subaru's pointed remark accompanied by a furrowed brow, the clown-masked man looked down at his own body and groaned.
What Subaru had pointed out was a flashing light faintly visible through the inside of the clown-masked man's black robes, which completely concealed his figure.
The presence of that blinking light rang a bell for Subaru.

"Is that... a Conversation Mirror?"

"Aah! Eee! Uuu! No way, no how, right?! Let me tell you! Just because my belly is sparkling isn't gonna change your fate—"

"The light is getting even stronger, I suppose. It seems they have quite an important matter to attend to, I suppose."

"Nnnnggggh...! You guys! Right there! In that spot! Wait right there for a sec!"

Spitting words with momentum, the clown-masked man turned his back on Subaru and the others right where he stood. He exposed a blind spot so blatantly that Subaru was left dumbfounded.
Having turned around, the clown-masked man rummaged in his robes and pulled something out—presumably the Conversation Mirror—and appeared to start talking to someone through it.
Turning his completely defenseless back implied that Subaru and the others weren't even a threat to him. It was infuriating beyond belief, as if he were explicitly stating as much—but:

"Now's our only chance. Hey, pull me up...!"

A hushed voice struck the ears of Subaru, who had just decided on moving stealthily.
Glancing toward the voice, he saw Rachins floating in the sewer water running right beside them, his head poking out from the black surface of the water.

"Chin! You've been in the sewer water the whole time?!"

"I didn't wanna draw that guy's attention carelessly! Plus, there's a chain submerged in the water, and it's tangled around my foot so I can't climb out..."

"If you had drowned because of that, it would have been the worst way to die, I suppose."

"Shut up! Hurry up and give me a hand!"

Subaru hesitated for a moment at Rachins's raised voice from the sewer.
Naturally, he wanted to help pull him up, but Subaru's hands were occupied carrying Crusch on his back. That meant relying on Beatrice, but whether her weight and arm strength could haul Rachins up was doubtful. In fact, there was a risk of simply adding one more victim to the sewer.
If so, it would be better to use Murak to manipulate gravity and simultaneously open a path—

"Aah, damn it all to hell! I'm doing my job properly! Sure, it's a bit flashy, and I won't say I didn't act on my own discretion, but ignoring orders or deviating from the plan? Don't go smearing my reputation like that!"

"—!"

Raising his voice, the clown-masked man stomped his feet and protested to whoever was on the other side of the Conversation Mirror.
Afterward, whatever his conversation partner said to him made him give a dismissive, defeated reply: "Fine, fine, I got it!" And then—

"Hey, catch! Don't drop it!"

"Huh? Wait, Beako!"

"—Watch out, I suppose!"

Turning around and slumping his shoulders with a sigh, the clown-masked man suddenly threw something. The moment Subaru saw that it was the Conversation Mirror, he called out Beatrice's name.
As mentioned earlier, Subaru's hands were occupied carrying Crusch. Thus, the one who caught the object tracing a gentle arc was Beatrice, who hurriedly extended both hands.

"I-I almost dropped it, I suppose! What are you thinking..."

"The person on the other side of that mirror says they want to talk to you guys!"

"To us?"

At the sulky clown-masked man's words, Subaru and Beatrice exchanged glances.
Then, keeping a cautious eye out in case the clown-masked man made any suspicious moves, the two timidly peeked into the Conversation Mirror in Beatrice's hands.
And there, reflected on the surface of the mirror, the figure of the clown-masked man's conversant emerged—

'—Ah, it worked! Um, can you see my face?'

"..."

Looking at the person reflected in the mirror as she spoke, Subaru was at a loss for how to react.
Because he never imagined that the figure appearing in the Conversation Mirror handed over by the clown-masked man would be a girl with youthful features.

She wasn't a girl he knew. Her age was probably a year or two younger than Subaru.
She had long, glossy navy-blue hair tied high in twin pigtails, and large, round golden eyes that left a striking impression. Within the narrow frame of the mirror, the girl—possessing refined features yet an aura that felt somewhat unreliable—wore a white shirt neatly adorned with a narrow tie at the collar and a pitch-black jacket thrown over it. She was looking through the mirror in a black suit that prioritized practical utility over glamour.
The gap between the girl's earnest gaze and the current situation thoroughly confused Subaru.

"Who... I mean, who are you?"

'Um, I am called "White Sheep." I suspected that "Black Dog" over there... the masked man, might have caused you terrible trouble... I'm so sorry!'

"S-Sorry, she says..."

The girl who introduced herself as "White Sheep" suddenly bowed deeply on the other side of the mirror. As she disappeared from the mirror's view due to her sheer momentum, Subaru's brain couldn't keep up with processing the information.
The clown-masked man—the man the girl called "Black Dog"—was an obvious danger. Yet the girl, who was apparently on the same side as Black Dog, was bowing to Subaru and Beatrice like this. It was so backward and mismatched, it felt like she was deliberately trying to confuse them.

"...To be frank, I don't know what to think of you guys. My own position is ambiguous enough as it is, and my head and heart are in complete chaos. Give me something to judge by."

'Something to judge by...?'

"If you're an enemy, just say so. —I'll smash right through you."
"Just as usual, I suppose."

When Subaru declared that, Beatrice nodded beside him with a fearless expression.
As established earlier, Subaru and Beatrice were severely lacking in mana, running completely on empty, with a hand of cards in absolute tatters. But there was no need to announce that out loud. Even if your hand was total trash, bluffing was the trick to surviving in another world.

'...'

At Subaru and Beatrice's words, White Sheep's eyes widened on the other side of the mirror.
She was likely the type whose feelings showed easily on her face. One could see her efforts to suppress any change in expression as much as possible, but her eye movements and facial muscles were clearly not suited for it.
And that fact seemed to be something she herself was aware of, facing the mirror every morning—

'You certainly lay it out straight... Aren't you scared?'

"I am. But it's better if the crisis is right in front of my own hands. That's way better than hesitating while the crisis rolls out of my reach."

'—. Once again, I am sorry. "Black Dog" meddling with you was due to our oversight, and because he was thinking of me.'

"Feels like you're calling me selfish and self-centered, kuhahaha!"

While White Sheep apologized for her companion's misdeeds, the masked Black Dog literally played dumb.
Ignoring Black Dog, who was hardly worth covering for, the girl's gaze on the other side of the mirror shifted—proof that her attention had turned to Subaru's back, which was visible from White Sheep's side.
As if to confirm it, White Sheep stared at Crusch sleeping on Subaru's back and said:

'We wish to protect... no, secure Crusch Karsten-san's person. We need her power.'

"Crusch-san's power...? You don't mean her left eye, do you?"

Subaru glanced toward Crusch's left eye, covered by her eyepatch.
It was entirely plausible that she was being targeted for the obvious abnormality that had developed in her, or specifically for her "Dragon's Eye." The fact that White Sheep had deliberately corrected "protect" to "secure" was likely because she felt simply promising her safety wouldn't be accurate.
However, betraying Subaru's expectation, White Sheep tilted her head in confusion, repeating, 'Left eye?'

'I haven't heard anything about her eyes. What we need is the power of the "Divine Protection of Wind Reading" that Crusch Karsten-san possesses.'

"—Her Divine Protection."

'Yes. We need that power. —For the fight against the "Beloved Children."'

She uttered that unfamiliar phrase with strong determination and resolve.

—The fight against the "Beloved Children."

White Sheep had said they needed Crusch's Divine Protection for that fight.
Stating their goal was securing her person regardless of the Dragon's Eye housed in Crusch's left eye made Subaru feel a misalignment in the focus of the conversation.
In the first place, he hadn't expected the existence of a completely unknown enemy to be dangled before them here.

"Chaos is starting up again... What are these 'Beloved Children'?"

'They are our enemies. And likely, Natsuki Subaru-san... yours as well.'

"...Can I take that to mean it's related to the current, pressing situation?"

'—Yes. They are the ones pulling the strings behind the current chaos in the Royal Capital.'

Subaru held his breath at the clear assertion that they were the central enemy behind this situation.
Honestly, White Sheep's declaration was more welcome than anything to Subaru right now. The reality was that he was in the middle of total confusion, having no idea who the enemy was or what their objective was. Just clearly presenting the information he wanted was enough to raise his opinion of White Sheep.
However—

"If Crusch-san is needed for the fight against these 'Beloved Children' or whatever, and that's why you contacted us, then picking that mask bastard as your negotiator is an extreme mistake in personnel..."

'N-No, um, the Royal Capital right now is in a pretty tense state, so unless someone is as skilled as Black Dog, walking around carelessly is dangerous...'

"Aaaand it was just pure coincidence that the guy I tailed turned out to be the jackpot—I wasn't chosen as the negotiator~! So don't go thinking it's White Sheep's fault, got it, you guys?"

"How can you sound so full of yourself after saying that, I suppose? Was that supposed to be sticking up for her, I suppose? If so, it's not just your taste that's lacking, but your understanding of the human heart, I suppose."

"Kuhahaha! You little brat in a ditch-smelling dress. How about I shut that mouth of yours for good?"

Due to his threatening massive fireball and behavior, Beatrice harbored intense hostility toward Black Dog. Coupled with Black Dog's mocking response, Subaru also couldn't feel the slightest bit of goodwill toward the guy.
On top of that, there was something concerning about Black Dog's recent remark.

"Did you just say you tailed someone? Does that mean..."

"You're sharp and shrewd, Sir Knight! Exactly, precisely! Searching blindly for a target is a waste of time, energy, and life, right? That's why I used another trick. Meeeaning, I tailed someone who seemed likely to find you! The guy currently floating in the stinky water."

"In the stinky... water..."

"Hey! How long are you bastards gonna chat so peacefully?! Hurry up and save me already!"

Prompted by Black Dog, who answered while swaying his hips from side to side, Subaru looked toward the sewer, right as Rachins—who had been left completely ignored—let out a yell.
Rachins, who was familiar with the underbelly of the Royal Capital, finding Subaru and the others on Felt's instructions was a massive achievement, but it seemed Black Dog and his partner had taken advantage of that. Still, that only diminished Subaru's gratitude toward Rachins by a tiny bit.

"Leaving him treading water forever is a bit much. Can we pull him up?"

"Pull him up~? A guy soaked in such filthy, stinky water? Leaving him submerged right where he is would keep our conversation much more hygienic, if you ask me."

'...Black Dog, if you caused trouble for someone...'

"Fine, fine, I get it! Maybe I bear a teeny-tiny bit of responsibility for him diving in. Maybe I thought—just a tiny, slight bit—that it would've saved us a whole lot of trouble if everyone else burned to a crisp and only the Duchess on his back fell down."

"Before you ruin our impression of you any further with your terrible remarks, hurry up and move, I suppose."

The good impression White Sheep had built up by being reasonable was completely ruined by Black Dog's unnecessary comments. Now that the true intention behind those threatening flames had been laid bare, his level of danger had surged even higher.
Keeping that in mind, Subaru and Beatrice took a step back, remaining vigilant as Black Dog reached out a hand to Rachins in the sewer.
With magic skills like his, simply averting their eyes wasn't enough to feel safe.
And that vigilance of Subaru's—

"—Ah?"

...was useful in detecting a completely different anomaly rather than Black Dog crouching toward the waterway.
*Crack.* Somewhere, a sound like a fingernail scratching thin porcelain echoed. Reflexively, Subaru's gaze darted toward the sound. —Deep in the sewer, illuminated by the faint light of the Lagmite ore Rachins had tossed near Black Dog's feet and left behind, someone's shadow emerged.

"..."

It was a woman.
She wore a cloak of an ambiguous color between black and gray, with wavy dark green hair tucked into her collar, and dark circles under her eyes on a pale, sickly face.
For a moment, Subaru wondered if she was another ally of Black Dog and White Sheep. But before he could demand an answer, the woman moved in Subaru's field of vision.
Limping one hand at her side, she pinched a white, fragment-like object between the fingers of her left hand held before her chest. That fragment was crushed by the movement of her fingers.

—Immediately following, fractures spreading from beneath the woman's feet slithered like snakes across the sewer's flagstones, surging toward Subaru and the others.

"Uwooooah?!"

At that terrifying anomaly, Rachins, who was about to grab Black Dog's hand, cried out.
The running cracks didn't stop at the floor. Passing from the walls to the ceiling, they traveled through the filthy sewer's stonework one after another, turning the ancient, damp framework supporting the Royal Capital's foundation a murky white.
It was as if everything in sight was being replaced with glass.

'—Black Dog!'

Amidst the abnormality, it reached the other side of the mirror, causing White Sheep to scream in a voice bordering on a screech.
Yet sadly, no matter how much she raised her voice, she couldn't intervene on Subaru's side from afar through the mirror. —However, her cry was not without purpose.

"Don't cry or shout in a trembling voice! I know!"

Because with that single shout, the aura of foolish playful antics around Black Dog vanished instantly.
He spun around swiftly and stood bravely in the path of the expanding fractures. Subaru could feel his gaze—unseen behind the mask—piercing sharply into the woman in the dark.
Black Dog clicked his tongue grandly.

"This is the worst of the worst! You're showing up right here, right now?!"

"An acquaintance?!"

"Someone I didn't want to know or ever meet! 'Shatterer' Clarissa—a 'Beloved Child'!"

At the name Black Dog spat out, both Subaru and White Sheep on the other side of the mirror gasped.
The woman called Clarissa gave no reply. She merely pinched another small fragment from the drawstring pouch held in her lowered hand. It looked like a pebble.

"Wait, wait, wait, wait, if you do that here—"

Black Dog shouted in a desperate attempt to stop her, but the crushing of the stone was faster.
*Snap.* A clear sound rang through the sewer—and at that signal, the next wave of destruction propagated.

"Are you serious...!"

New destruction surged through the cracked and fractured sewer. The wall right beside Subaru and the others turned a cloudy white, followed immediately by the collapsing ceiling coming down to crush them.

"Beatrice!"
"I know, I suppose!"

Responding to his call, Beatrice spread her five fingers toward the collapsing ceiling.
Having already used E·M·T and E·M·M, she was in a state running completely on empty where proper combat was difficult. Even so, making do with a meager hand was the foundation of Subaru and Beatrice's partnership.

"Murak!"

The incantation interfered with reality, causing the debris of the falling ceiling to glow with a faint light, reducing its lethal weight to that of mere pumice stones.
Shielding Crusch on his back from the rain of pumice while pulling Beatrice close, Subaru leaped backward with all his might—and in an instant, the weight returned, and the collapse buried half the sewer.

"Not bad, incredible, little kid~! That was a pretty good snap judgment!"

"If you slyly managed to get saved along with us, then spill the information clearly, I suppose! What about that woman?!"

"A user of strange powers! She shatters things! Destroys them! A gloomy woman who's no fun to talk to! I'll kill her right now!"

Having benefited from Beatrice's countermeasure and escaped the cave-in, Black Dog shouted as if laughing, summoning a great gale toward the collapsed rubble and hurling a storm of flying stones toward the back of the passage.
Without missing its mark, it surged toward Clarissa deep in the corridor—but the moment another light *snap* was heard, the stones flying through the air were shattered, turning into dust by the time they reached her.

"Does crushing those stones have something to do with it?"

Straining his eyes at Clarissa's movements and seeing stone dust drop from her crushed left hand, Subaru deduced that the logic behind that abnormal destruction was hidden within that very gesture.
And that the drawstring pouch in Clarissa's hand held plenty more pebbles for that purpose.

"Hey, fire! If it's something without a physical form, she shouldn't be able to shatter it to stop it!"

"I see, good point, but alas! I already used my fire, I don't have it in my hand!"

"Huh?! —Wait, this is bad!"

In the vision of Subaru, who was rattled by Black Dog's incomprehensible rejection, Clarissa smoothly pinched the next pebble between her fingers in a movement like a sacred ritual.
Already, the floor and walls surrounding Subaru and the others were on the verge of total collapse. If Clarissa shattered that fragment, the entire sewer area might crumble all at once.
At this rate—

"—Hey! Over here! Get over here!"

"—! Chin?!"

Just then, a desperate call echoed from the sewer, causing Subaru and the others to turn around.
Looking over, Rachins, floating in the water, was clinging to the side wall of the waterway with one hand while pulling something black with the other. —It was a rusted chain.
Hauling the mud-caked metal up from the bottom of the water, Rachins shouted at the top of his lungs.

"It's a settling tank! Beside the main channel, it's packed full of sludge!"

"Settling..."

Hearing this sudden introduction to the sewer's mechanics, an electric shock ran down Subaru's spine.
A settling tank, accumulated rotten sludge—that meant the worst possible scenario—

"Are you serious...?!"

"Heh! You're quick on the uptake, ain't ya?"

Drenched in foul water, his hair plastered to his forehead in a dreadful state, Rachins—whose Sanpaku eyes glinted wildly—grinned at Subaru for reaching the same conclusion and yanked his arm with all his strength.

The rusted, filth-covered chain clanked heavily—and a dull, heavy groan resonated from the bottom of the water.

Matching that sound, bubbles surfaced along the water's edge near the wall. Starting as tiny pops, they rapidly increased in number, making the black surface boil all at once. —In an instant, the sewer air, already foul-smelling, was completely overwhelmed by a scent resembling rotten eggs.

Subaru understood Rachins's intent.
There was no time to hesitate.

"What about Crusch-san—"

Mindful of Crusch sleeping on his back, Subaru hesitated for a second over what to do. Thinking of what was about to happen, leaving her unconscious was far too dangerous—
The instant Subaru hesitated, Black Dog moved. —He suddenly shoved them.

"You—!"

"Better than getting thrown, sent flying, and shattered—this is the right answer!"

As he spoke, Black Dog hurled himself into the sewer water, dragging Subaru, Crusch, and Beatrice along with him. Just before splashing into the water—his vision was suddenly covered in black, and the expected welcome of foul sewage never came.
Looking closely, it was a massive, half-formed sphere made of earth that Black Dog had created, attempting to completely envelop and encase Subaru and the others—

'Please! Trust us!'

In the heat of the moment, White Sheep earnestly pleaded from the mirror's surface.
Pushed by her plea, inside the sinking earth hemisphere, Subaru entrusted Crusch to Beatrice right beside him and reached his hand outside the closing ball.
And—

"—Come on, Rachins!"

"Don't go bossing me around!"

Rachins, having pulled the chain all the way, kicked awkwardly off the wall and flew toward them. Grabbing Rachins's outstretched arm, Subaru dragged his slender body into the hemisphere in one motion.
They tumbled backward in a tangle. Immediately after, the hemisphere sealed completely, almost simultaneously with the faint sound of a pebble fragment being crushed.

Shaking and a roaring noise echoed from beyond the thick walls of the closed earthen sphere.
However, something greater than that—

"Blow sky high. —Goa."

Lying tangled on top of Subaru, Rachins spat out those words and snapped his fingers. —Immediately after, red light spread through the soil wall, and the sound and shockwave arrived a moment later.

"—?!"

An impact like a punch straight to the gut shook the world, making Subaru scream.
Even though they were underwater, inside an earth sphere, it was burning hot. Blindingly bright. An explosive roar burst forth as if to shatter his eardrums.

"—Ah."

Inaudible screams thrashed wildly inside Natsuki Subaru's body.
Round and round, heaven and earth spun with tremendous force. The earth sphere submerged underwater was swept away by a violent current, colliding with and being battered by huge masses again and again, pushing him to the brink of losing consciousness.

What had accumulated in the settling tank was flammable gas generated from massive amounts of waste.
Rachins had opened the cover of that settling tank, ignited the massive amount of gas spewing out, and blown up the sewer itself along with Clarissa, who had been aiming for them. —No, what happened to the sewer wasn't something he knew. He couldn't see. He couldn't hear. It was beyond his comprehension.

Simply tossed about by the churning water and the bouncing momentum of the earth sphere, Subaru frantically held fast to the familiar small form and the person he had to protect, holding them tightly, so tightly.

Grit his teeth and endure. —That was the absolute best Subaru could do right now.

"..."

He grit his teeth and kept enduring, enduring, and enduring.
Until his consciousness was swallowed by an unseen muddy torrent, he kept enduring on and on—

—When he woke up, the first thing he felt was a smell.

Not the smell of rotten eggs, nor the smell of sewage covered in filth.
What he smelled was the sting of alcohol and the scent of dry cloth. Air warmed without the use of fire passed through his nasal passages, and taking a deep breath of that strangely clean air, he choked.

"Ghah! *Cough* *Hack* *Gag*!"

"Eek, I suppose!"

As Subaru coughed violently, his body jolting, the weight resting on his chest and stomach bounced in the same way, and he heard an adorable scream like that.
Opening his eyes, he adjusted his blurred vision with a few blinks. Then, right beside where he lay, he found Beatrice wearing a sullen expression.
Seeing Beatrice draped in a blanket from her shoulders, Subaru's lips trembled.

"Beako... Are you... safe?"

"Somehow or other, I suppose. It was far beyond a hair's breadth—more like a hundred hair's breadths, I suppose."

"That 'hair' refers to a single hair, so increasing it changes the meaning... wait, never mind that. More importantly, if you and I are safe, what about the others—"

"Calm down, I suppose. Look around before making a fuss, I suppose."

As Subaru tried to lean forward, his forehead was sharply halted by Beatrice's hand. With his forehead pressed by her tiny palm, Subaru moved only his gaze and noticed.
On the bed beside the one Subaru lay on, Crusch was also laid down.

"Crusch-san... Thank goodness. She's alright... right?"

"From what I heard, she isn't harmed, I suppose. But she hasn't woken up even once since then, I suppose. How long the exhaustion from that eye will last is unknown even to Betty, I suppose."

"I see... Ah, Chin is here too. What a relief."

While relieved by Crusch sleeping peacefully with her chest rising and falling rhythmically on the bed, beyond her on a long bench lay Rachins, rolled up like a reed mat in a blanket.
He hadn't woken up yet, but perhaps because the bench was uncomfortable to sleep on, he was knitting his brows in his sleep, muttering incoherently. —Subaru cracked a wry smile, thinking it was rather rough treatment for the key player whose quick thinking had saved them, but—

"...Where is this?"

Drained of strength from relief, Subaru rested a hand on the bed and finally arrived at that question.
Not only the smell, but the appearance was clearly somewhere other than the sewer. The stone room had no windows, with illumination provided only by a small lamp. Beside the bed, a pitcher, bandages, and neatly folded cloth were arranged orderly, bringing the word "infirmary" vaguely to mind.

"Or maybe a hideout clinic? Like an underground doctor's hideout..."

"—! Have you woken up, Natsuki Subaru-san?!"

Right after he voiced those stray thoughts, a voice struck Subaru from his side like a surprise attack.
Turning around, Subaru saw a girl standing at the entrance of the room—the person he had seen through the mirror. A girl in a black suit with long navy-blue hair tied on both sides—"White Sheep."
Facing her in person rather than through a mirror, White Sheep clasped her hands before her chest, opened them, clasped them again, opened them, repeating the gesture several times before—

"I-I'm so glad... I was so worried about what might happen to everyone..."

"That's... thanks for worrying about us, I guess?"

"Yes! No! Um? Which one should I answer...?"

Subaru and White Sheep both short-circuited, unable to decide which order was correct to process apologies, gratitude, and complaints.
There was no doubt they had been saved. However, prior to that, it was also a fact that they had been threatened by Black Dog, White Sheep's companion, leaving endless doubts as to what the right response should be.
And just as he was thinking that—

"—White Sheep, you must not trouble Natsuki-dono. You are supposed to be different from Black Dog. Otherwise, we will need to reconsider our policy."

"..."

That voice came from behind White Sheep, who was flustered at the doorway.
Hearing the voice and seeing the person who stepped into view, Subaru gasped and widened his eyes. Having surely ingested more than enough surprises for one day, here came another addition.
Because the person appearing alongside White Sheep was someone Subaru knew once again.

"You're... Russell-san?"

In a faltering voice, the name Subaru uttered belonged to a person he had formed a connection with long ago—a merchant he became acquainted with at the start of the Royal Selection, whom he had negotiated with to supply relief goods for the Subjugation of the White Whale immediately following. Though they hadn't stood shoulder-to-shoulder on the battlefield, they were allies who fought the same enemy.
Russell Fellow, holder of the title of representative of the Royal Capital's Commerce Guild, was right there.

"..."

Inside his head, filled with supreme chaos and confusion, Subaru simply stared dumbfounded at Russell's calm demeanor. At Subaru's gaze, the gentleman in a well-tailored suit smiled and nodded deeply.
For some reason, Subaru felt a chill from that smile. —A smile that felt laden with far too many hidden motives for a mere merchant to wear.
And Subaru's bad feeling proved entirely accurate.

"Meeting you like this is truly a stroke of good fortune, Natsuki-dono. In the face of this unprecedented crisis for the Kingdom, we would very much like you to lend us your strength and insight."

"An unprecedented major crisis for the Kingdom..."

"Indeed."

Despite a situation where laughing was out of the question, Russell continued, still wearing his smile.
And that was—

"—The enemy's objective is a quiet, yet thorough invasion of the Kingdom. The core of the Kingdom has already fallen into the hands of our long-standing nemesis, leaving it on the brink of ruin."
