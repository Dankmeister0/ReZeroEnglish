Arc 8 Chapter 10: "No One Can Be Alone"

—Fainting and sleep, though both are the same suspension of consciousness, why are the two so different?

Subaru is likely one of the world's foremost experts on fainting and losing consciousness, and yet he doesn't understand in the slightest why the two are different.
What he can say for certain is this: if both fainting and sleep have the effect of resting the body, fainting only seems to manage about half that effect.

It might just be a matter of feeling, but that's how it feels to him.
Surely that's because, unlike sleep, fainting weighs so differently on the hearts of the people around him.
If he had no connections to anyone, if there were no one to worry, then maybe there would be no difference between fainting and sleep at all.
Although—

"—Ah."

His eyelids trembled, and a weak breath leaked from his throat.
Faint and uncertain, drawing reality toward himself with that small gesture, Natsuki Subaru slowly achieved his awakening from the darkness.

"――――"

Raising his face from the shore of reality he'd pulled himself to, Subaru forgot to breathe for a beat.
What entered his field of vision was an unfamiliar ceiling—just how many times had he woken up like this in some unknown place, racking his brain to figure out where he was? Only, what was usually a solitary task wasn't so this time.

"...Finally awake, are we. Subaru's such a troublesome sleepyhead, I suppose."

"Beatrice..."

Blocking Subaru's view of that unfamiliar ceiling was a familiar, lovely face.
Looking down at Subaru with lowered brows and pale-colored eyes was the girl who probably had the most opportunities of anyone to share a waking moment with him—Beatrice.

And, as Beatrice's figure came into view, Subaru noticed the sensation of his hand—apparently lying on a bed—being held, and his lips loosened.

"Don't tell me you held my hand the whole time?"

"Naturally, I suppose. Betty's Subaru's partner, I suppose. Besides, Subaru goes running off wherever he pleases far too much. If chains are too much to ask, holding hands is the most effective method, I suppose."

"You really do know me well..."

At Beatrice's sulking face and words like that, Subaru couldn't argue back and just smiled wryly.
If there's something he has to do, Subaru will find a way even if he's chained up—but letting go of a held hand always aches at his heart.
Subaru's weak to pain, so this is the most effective way to hold him back.

"Um, so where is this? What are we doing right—"

"Calm down, I suppose, Subaru. I understand wanting to fret over all sorts of things... but before that, look around first, I suppose."

"Around?"

To Subaru, eager to know the situation, Beatrice puts a finger to her lips and makes a show of drawing it out.
His impatience and rushing feelings put on hold, Subaru blinked and, as told, turned his eyes to something other than the ceiling and Beatrice.
And then he caught his breath.

—Witnessing, all around the bed he was lying in, a great many sleeping figures breathing softly.

"――――"

It's by no means a spacious room. Just the one bed Subaru's lying in takes up half the room's space. And packed into that room are more than ten faces.
An impossible combination, at that.

"Tanza, and Garfiel? And Hiain and the others, and Rui, and even Utakata..."

"That's not all, I suppose. Betty won one hand, but the other one..."

Told this, Subaru realizes that it isn't only his right hand held by Beatrice—his left hand is the same—and he turns to look. There, gripping Subaru's hand tightly, sleeping with her upper body thrown across the bed, is—

"—Petra."

A big ribbon on her head, a girl whose sleeping face alone was lovely, lay there.
Just how much hardship and how many obstacles had she overcome to hold this hand? Petra, in unfamiliar traveling clothes, was dirty here and there—so unlike her usually fashion-conscious self.
It was proof, too, that she'd come all this way even in such a state.

"But I think what Petra worked hardest for was seizing the chance to hold Subaru's hand like that. Everyone was fighting over it, I suppose."

"Hey now, you're saying everyone was fighting to hold my hand while I was out cold? Come on, that's a ridiculous—"

"It's not a ridiculous story, I suppose."

Beatrice's quiet voice stopped Subaru, who'd been about to laugh it off as a joke.
To the wide-eyed Subaru, Beatrice lifted the hand she still held, squeezing it tight, and said,

"Everyone was seriously worried about Subaru, I suppose. If holding a hand gives Subaru strength, they'll want to do even just that much, and anything else besides, I suppose."

"――――"

"Subaru ought to be a little more aware of how large his own presence is, I suppose. That's something Betty's told him over and over and over, I suppose."

Quiet, in a tone that might even hold a trace of exasperation, and yet Beatrice's words were steeped in too great a tenderness.
Said with those eyes and that voice, there was no way he could brush it off.
Subaru, too, never once thought he had no influence on the people around him, or that he wasn't cherished—

"...I mean, it's because I've shrunk, but you're all being way too overprotective."

"I wonder about that. Personally, before worrying about everyone else's overprotectiveness, I'd rather Natsuki-san reflect on the fact that he's shrunk in the first place."

"—!"

Subaru's shoulders jumped at the sudden voice.
The voice that leapt in unexpectedly came from the narrow room's entrance, where the sliding door had opened to reveal a face—that infuriatingly soft-featured bastard.

"Otto!"

"Yes, it's me... but please, quiet down. Spare a thought for Petra-chan and Garfiel's feelings."

"Petra and the others' feelings, meaning...?"

"They're waiting right beside you for you to wake up, aren't they? Of course they want to be the very first to exchange words with you once you're awake. And yet, if it gets out that I stuck my face in like this and stole that right from them, just imagine how much they'll resent me."

"Oh man, what awful timing, dude."

"You, of all people, don't seem to have lost any of your talkativeness despite shrinking. Good to see."

Putting a finger to his lips and dropping his voice, the young man who'd shown himself—Otto—looked around the room.
Though he'd finally shown his face, the room was packed so tight there was nowhere to put a foot down. Wearing a troubled expression, Otto nonetheless managed to find footing and approach the bed.

"Beatrice-chan, thank you for your hard work nursing Natsuki-san."

"There's nothing tiring about it, I suppose. ...Anyway, is it all right for you to be talking?"

"Of course, I should be informing everyone that Natsuki-san has woken up... but—"

Given a narrow-eyed look by Beatrice, Otto smiled wryly and cut his words short. Drawing out what came after that pause, he looked over at Subaru on the bed and said,

"Given that, by pure bad timing, I happen to be present for Natsuki-san's awakening, I'd like to get my personal complaints in before things get noisy."

"Y-you've got some nerve..."

"Well, I am a merchant."

Putting a hand to his own hatless head, Otto smiled without a trace of spite.
Feeling a chill about as bad as being glared at by an enemy—or as bad as when he's earned Tanza's displeasure—Subaru took a deep breath.
Honestly, Subaru doesn't think he was much at fault for being flung into the Vollachian Empire, or for the trouble that followed—but it's a fact that he caused a great deal of worry.
Whatever grievance gets thrown at him, he'll take it like a man.

"All right, bring it on. But you know, my mental fortitude's been trained pretty hard over here too. Don't think some half-baked words are gonna break me."

"What kind of boast is that. Well, I don't intend to give a long lecture. I'm not the only one who wants to say something, so I'll keep it brief. —Natsuki-san."

"Yeah."

"—I'm glad you made it back safely. Please, don't worry us so much."

"――――"

A hand reached out, landing with a pat on Subaru's small shoulder.
Feeling Otto's fingers—sturdier than his features suggested—tremble faintly on that shoulder, Subaru's throat tightened.
Tightly, furrowing his brow, wrinkling the bridge of his nose as he said it, that was a fraying of emotion Otto—who always views himself from a bird's-eye view—couldn't hold in check.

"Gu...!"

His trained mental fortitude, his resolve to take it like a man, all of it was for nothing.
Otto's decisive one-liner mercilessly shattered Natsuki Subaru's defenses, driving a fatal blow into the bare soul beyond.

Simply put, that was against the rules.

"This is probably what works best on Natsuki-san, isn't it?"

"You, you bastard...!"

In front of the trembling Subaru, Otto tightened his expression—which had just been on the verge of crumpling—and put on a confident, meaningful, unshakeable smile.
Subaru could only redden with the sense of defeat, knowing he'd been completely played. —No, there was one more move left. As payback against Otto for having gotten the better of him, far from a victory, but at least a way to keep him from getting away scot-free with the win.
That was—

"Ahh! I've been done in, done in, total defeat, you bastard Otto!!"

"Gah!?"

Face still red, wearing a wicked grin, Subaru loudly declared his defeat. The instant he heard it, Otto's face went pale and he let out a shriek.
As this exchange between Subaru and Otto rang through the room, naturally enough, the sleeping comrades who heard it began to wake one after another—no, sleeping lions began to stir.
And so, joy and reproach and all manner of mixed emotions exploded into noise.

In the midst of that increasingly raucous room, alongside Subaru and Otto holding their heads, Beatrice—who had watched the whole thing unfold—propped her chin on her hand and said,

"Good grief... everyone's carrying on like children, I suppose."

—all the while watching the scene with a fond smile, playing the lone bystander.

—The mass evacuation of citizens from the imperial capital, Lupugana.

That was the great undertaking carried out while Subaru was unconscious, and Subaru had woken up in the middle of that group, in a passenger compartment of a dragon carriage allotted for moving important persons.
Pulled by numerous Earth Dragons, it functioned as a mobile bedchamber—an excellent arrangement usable for both evacuation and recuperation. Apparently, Subaru had been carried out of the capital with such an awe-inspiringly generous treatment.

"Brother! Glad you're all right! When you went limp partway through, I was seriously, seriously worried about what might happen!"
"I couldn't care less if the bearded bastard died, but if you disappeared, the battalion would be finished... If someone's gotta be a shield, use me or anyone else instead."
"Weitz's way of putting it rubs me the wrong way, but I agree. Schwarz, I'm truly glad you made it back. For the battalion's sake, and for my own."

Those were the reactions of the trio who'd waited for Subaru to wake—the three "combatants" who'd accompanied him from the Isle of Sword-Slaves: Hiain, Weitz, and Idra.
Idra, who'd been acting alongside him during the battle, was one thing, but Hiain and Weitz, who'd been acting separately, must have been worried sick.
And it isn't only these three that Subaru needs to apologize to.

"Sorry. Because I passed out, everyone's Empowerment got undone too..."

"Don't beat yourself up over it... Luckily, that happened right when we were already starting to retreat."

"That's right! The one who nearly died when that ran out was this skull-face here, who happened to be holding up a collapsing pillar at the time. If it weren't for the Governor, he'd have been crushed to death..."

"I told you not to bring that up...!"

Weitz's consideration for Subaru's feelings is, as usual, ruined by Hiain's unnecessary extra remark. With the two of them bickering in the background, Idra shrugged.
Having had his Coeur Léonis unbound right in the middle of the imperial capital and presumably exposed to serious danger, he said,

"As Weitz said too, don't dwell on it, Schwarz. From the moment we decided to follow you, whatever happens after that is our own responsibility."

"But..."

"And I know that even hearing that, you're the type to worry anyway. —I'll leave the rest of this to the two women who've apparently known you longer than we have."

As ever, Idra's good at wrapping things up rationally.
Thanks to that, Subaru—capable only of emotional rebuttal—was left silenced, and Idra, paying him no further mind, took the grappling Hiain and Weitz and left the room.
And as the three left, a small figure was about to follow after their backs when—

"Tanza."

"...I'm sorry I couldn't be of use when it mattered most. I'm truly glad you've returned to us, Schwarz-sama."

Tanza answered, shoulders drooping, in her formal tone.
Thinking back on their parting in the imperial capital, Subaru understood her feeling of regret over her own insufficiency. But it seemed her dark expression wasn't only about that.

"What's wrong? If something happened..."

"—. No, it's nothing I should burden you with right now, Schwarz-sama. Please rest your body slowly, and spend time with everyone."

Leaving a moment's hesitation, and saying nothing more, Tanza too left the room.
Subaru half-reached a hand toward that frail-looking back, but both his hands were occupied. Even so, his heart insisted he couldn't just leave Tanza with a face like that.
He resolved firmly to ask the reason for that expression later, without fail.
And then—

"—Subaru."

Seeing off those who'd left the room before, what followed was a voice like a silver bell, bright as though it had been waiting eagerly for this very moment to call out, gently striking his eardrums.

"――――"

It took a beat, and time, for him to react to that voice, to turn toward it.
That wasn't out of the awkwardness of having caused worry, or the embarrassment of not being able to face her, or any such petty pride.
Purely and simply, Subaru's whole being needed to brace itself.
Because—

"Emilia"

—just calling out that name was enough for Subaru's heart to taste a sweet, numbing sensation.
At Subaru's tension-laced call, the person herself—Emilia—smiled softly, and said,

"Mm, I'm glad we got to properly see each other. ...Still, honestly, Subaru, you sure made a lot of friends even way out here. I felt so, so relieved."

"Friends..."

"Yeah. Everyone was so, so worried about Subaru, and stayed right by your side the whole time."

At Emilia's words, Subaru recalled the state of the room just now and looked down.
Somehow, the feelings of having caused worry always weigh far heavier on him, and every time he's told that's a bad habit of his, he reflects on it.

"I'm a lucky guy, huh."

"I'm sure you are. But I want Subaru to become even happier than that, so much more, so this much isn't nearly enough at all."

"Even after everyone's done this much for me?"

"Then, does Subaru ever, when doing something for me, think 'this much should be enough' and feel satisfied?"

Tilting her head, her beautiful silver hair flowing down over her slender shoulders, Subaru—thoroughly bested—found himself at a loss for words.
She's right. When there's something he wants to do for the people he loves, what he thinks isn't "is this enough" but "isn't there something more I can do."
Seeing Subaru's reaction, Emilia laughed "fufu" and said,

"So? Am I wrong? My knight."

"...Guess not. While we were apart, you grew like crazy—I'm proud, but it's a little lonely too, Emilia."

"Emilia?"

"...I mean, Emilia-tan."

Asked to repeat himself, Subaru reeled back in the address that had drifted away.
Yes, that was the way of calling the girl who mattered so much, so precious, so dear to him, the girl he thought of until his heart went utterly haywire.
The instant he called Emilia that, once, everything clicked into place all at once.
As Subaru savored that sense of rightness—

"Emilia-oneesama! It's about time we get to talk with Subaru too!"

Into the soft air flowing between Subaru and Emilia, cutting in at just the right moment, raising a small hand to assert her presence, was the girl Petra.
Having woken up amid the earlier commotion, and unable to calmly celebrate their reunion in the chaos that followed, Petra, her round eyes flashing with indignation, was absolutely starved for this reunion.

"We still haven't gotten to properly talk with Subaru yet. —Except for Otto-san, who cut in line!"

"Ugh. No, um, it's not that I was trying to cut in line or anything..."

"Oh? Oh? Makin' excuses, Otto-anki? I'm kinda lookin' forward to hearin' what kinda excuse you'll come up with. Even I was hurt, cuttin' in line like that here..."

"Even Garfiel's biting at me now!?"

Dropping his shoulders in dejection, making a gesture as if lamenting the state of things, Garfiel joined in. Faced with this coordinated attack alongside Petra, Otto's eyes widened and he cried out.
And then, delivering an additional blow, Beatrice chimed in with "That's right, I suppose,"

"Betty happened to overhear it. That Otto fellow said himself that he came to talk to Subaru secretly, so Petra and the others wouldn't find out, I suppose."

"Eh, is that true? Otto-kun, I get how you feel, but everyone was equally worried about Subaru, so it's not fair to cheat like that."

"I have no allies! This hurts far more than any petty disadvantage ever could!"

Called out by the traitor Beatrice, and by Emilia who took it at face value, Otto, clutching his chest, received a grand comeuppance for his own actions.
Laughing at the sight of the lively, clamoring group, someone said,

"Setting aside Otto-sama's bad habits, I'm simply so relieved we've truly managed to reunite. Emilia-sama, Beatrice-sama, and of course Petra too—none of us could rest easy."

That gentle voice belonged to Frederica.
She too, like Petra, was in traveling clothes rather than her maid uniform, an unusual sight. But right after, Subaru was shown something even more unusual, and he startled.
Tears welled at the corners of Frederica's smiling eyes, nearly spilling down her cheek.

"F-Frederica-oneesama, tears!"

"Eh? Ah, m-my apologies. I just, that is, I'm truly so relieved that... how unsightly of me."

Startled by Frederica showing tears, Subaru's gratitude welled up all the more.
As Frederica herself said, those who'd rushed to search for the missing Subaru and the others—there's simply no measuring how much hardship they must have overcome to get here.

A border not easily crossed, an empire plunged into civil war, at that.
Overcoming obstacle after obstacle that couldn't be cleared by any proper means, they must have made their way to Subaru.

"Really, thank you, everyone."

"Heheh, it's only natural we'd come, ain't it. Ain't nothin' gonna come together without the boss around, no way, no how."

"Yeah, what Garf-san said. ...Though I was surprised you'd shrunk down so much."

At Garfiel rubbing under his nose, Petra nodded with a smile and added that little jab at the end.
True, this isn't quite the happy ending of everyone reuniting all at once—not given Subaru's current state. He certainly doesn't intend to return to the Kingdom of Lugunica with Emilia and the others while shrunk like this.
Unless he returns to his original eighteen-year-old self, he probably can't stand beside Emilia.

"Well, Beatrice... ahem! Does it not bother you, standing next to Bea-ko like this?"

"Being at the same eye level isn't an unpleasant feeling, I suppose. But Betty's starting to miss being carried by Subaru. So, a big Subaru is preferable after all, I suppose."

"Me too! I think Subaru's better big too. Though I think this Subaru's cute too..."

"Ah, so it really is cute after all. Glad it's not just me who thought so."

At Emilia's slightly off-topic agreement, a fluffy, relaxed air spread through the room.
In that indescribably easygoing atmosphere, Subaru tasted the nostalgia of the Emilia Camp he'd been kept apart from for so long, and felt his soul filling with contentment.

Flung into the Vollachian Empire, he'd spent time with the People of Shudrak, with Flop and Medium, and later formed the Pleiades Battalion with the members from the Isle of Sword-Slaves—but none of those quite matched this feeling of being with everyone here.
Each was precious, but he savored, deeply, the preciousness of this place he'd been kept away from for so long.

"But I guess even so, Roswaal and Ram couldn't make it, huh. Well, I guess just having Emilia-tan here is already pretty dicey on its own."

Remarking on the faces missing from the room, Subaru smiled wryly.
Speaking of absences, Meili's missing too, but given her position, it'd probably be hard for her to cross the border and accompany Emilia and the others. Probably, along with Ram, she's staying behind to hold down the mansion—

"Eh? Nuh-uh, that's not it at all. Ram and Roswaal are properly with us too. The two of them were so, so worried about Subaru as well."

"—Eh?"

Subaru's assumption was shattered by Emilia's puzzled-looking answer.
Subaru, eyes wide with shock, had Emilia's lovely voice echo in his head. Ram, and Roswaal too, had come along.

Roswaal being here is a tremendous surprise, but he'll set that aside for now.
The problem isn't Roswaal, though—

"Ram's here too?"

"She is? Ram-oneesama was worried about Subaru too... ah! You absolutely can't tell her I said that, okay?"

"I won't. I won't, but—I won't, but that's not it, that's not the point..."

Shaking his head side to side, Subaru let Petra's words numb his brain.
No, that's not it—if Ram is here, that means—

"D-did you already... let her and Rem meet?"

"Ah..."

The moment he timidly voiced that question, everyone but Subaru's expression stiffened.
That reaction was exactly what Subaru had feared most.

Emilia and the others' reaction was the kind that said "we forgot to tell Subaru"—which meant, in other words, it had happened while Subaru was asleep.

—The reunion of Ram and Rem, the sisters who'd been torn apart.

"Waaaaahhh—!! I wanted to be there so badly!!!"

"S-sorry, Subaru! But we wanted to let them meet as soon as possible, so..."

"I get it, I get it, but—damn it, me, you idiot—!"

Covering his face with both hands, Subaru, having missed a historic moment, screamed at his own foolishness.
Emilia and the others desperately tried to console him, but since this wasn't really anyone's fault, Subaru's wound was never going to heal.
If anyone's to blame, it's Subaru himself, for being asleep at the crucial moment, more than anyone.
Even so—

"H-how was it? Their reaction..."

"Um, it was a little awkward, like you'd expect... but it felt really, really special! I got a little teary myself, too..."

"Ohhhhhh!"

"Emilia! Show a little more mercy, I suppose! Subaru's pitiful, I suppose!"

"Eeeh! Sorry, sorry!"

Even so, his frustration showed no sign of fading, so Subaru went all out in his frustration.
When you're genuinely about to cry, not being able to hold back the tears—that's the annoying part of this young body, Subaru realized anew, shedding tears of frustration.

"――――"

Right in front of her, from beyond the door she'd half-reached to open, came a loud, loud wailing voice.
Stopped in her tracks by that shouting voice and its content, Rem furrowed her fine brows, her heart troubled by hesitation over what to do.

"Uu, au?"

"...Um, sorry. Rui-chan wants to see that person, doesn't she."

Right beside her, Rui, clinging to her waist, looked up at Rem in puzzlement.
When the unconscious Subaru woke up, Rui too must have wanted to talk with him, but she'd swallowed that down hard and rushed to Rem's side instead.
And then, led by the hand by her, Rem had come all the way to the front of Subaru's sickroom—

"――――"

The voices coming from inside the room belong to Subaru and his companions.
Put that way it sounds terribly detached, but it seems those companions are, apparently, deeply connected to Rem as well. —Same as with Subaru, though, it still lacks any real sense of reality.

"I do understand they're not bad people at all, not in the least."

Led by Emilia, whom she'd met first among them, the group that had come searching for her and Subaru is clearly, unmistakably full of consideration for their comrades, brimming with initiative, and above all, good-hearted.
From what she's heard, they rushed here from a kingdom bordering the empire, despite travel between the two being forbidden, and even joined in a civil war that had nothing to do with them.
And all of that, for the sake of saving Subaru, caught up in the empire's civil war—no, for the sake of saving Subaru and Rem both.
That's the same reason Rem herself cannot escape from this empire's conflict—

"—I can hear Barusu's pathetic voice."

Suddenly spoken to from behind while lost in thought, Rem caught her breath faintly.
In her place, it was Rui, clinging to her waist, who turned to face the speaker. The owner of that voice looked back at Rui looking up at her, let out a small breath, and said,

"Stop glaring at me so reproachfully. What you just heard was love's whip for Barusu... no, there's no love in it, so just a whip. Yes, a purely unadulterated whip."

"...Does that mean you dislike that person?"

"I don't dislike him. I simply have many opportunities to hold him in contempt."

Saying that, Rem too turned toward the person slowly approaching, footsteps sounding.
It took a beat for her to turn that way, because turning to look that person's face straight on required her to summon up courage.
After all, the approaching face was, even to a memoryless Rem, a familiar one.
That was—

"...Ram-san."

"Such a distant way to address me. Ram, for her part, is fully prepared in her heart to be called 'sister.'"

"For my part, everything has been so sudden..."

Standing before her, chest puffed out as though hugging her own elbows—the woman who called herself, and was called by everyone else, Ram—her face closely resembled Rem's own.
What differed was the color of her hair and eyes, and the overwhelming pressure of confidence that filled her from within.

That woman is, apparently, the sister of the memoryless Rem.
Honestly, comparing their features, there's nothing to deny about that. The name of the sister that the persistent Subaru had told her about so many times before was, indeed, "Ram."
More than anything, what dominates Rem's heart, undeniably, is—

"Whatever you think, Rem's heart is feeling it, isn't it. Ram and Rem are sisters... synesthesia shouldn't lie."

"Synesthesia..."

"Something like a bond of souls between twins who share the same blood. Ram has continued to sense Rem's existence this whole time. ...Is Rem different?"

"――――"

Narrowing her pale pink eyes, Ram asked her straight on, and Rem pressed her lips shut.
Asked if she's different—no, not at all. She doesn't know the finer details of this thing called synesthesia, but inside her, there was certainly a strong, strong sense of something out of place.
That was an overwhelming sense of security toward Ram, someone she should be seeing for the first time.

"...But I'm afraid."

"Afraid?"

"That I feel this way toward you, after having exchanged words for only such a short time."

For the memory-lost Rem, the only two present at the moment her current self began were Subaru and Rui. For reasons of her own, she'd kept Subaru at a distance, and kept Rui close, at that beginning.
After that, she'd gotten to know the people of Shudrak, and Flop and Medium, and Priscilla, and Katya too, building various relationships in her own way.
And yet, Ram's presence rushes past all of that in one leap, trying to stand at the very top.

"That's what frightens me..."

This sense of security, one she can't clearly put into words, is the footsteps of the past drawing near.
It isn't as though she never wishes to recover her lost memories. Surely, many of the problems Rem carries would resolve themselves once those memories return—she thinks that, too.
But at the same time, she's terrified of everything changing once those footsteps catch up to her.

If the way she sees things, the way she feels things, the way she thinks about things—if all of it changes.
Rem shut her eyes tight, hard, and pressed a hand to her chest.
And then—

"I see. —Good."

"Eh?"

Hearing an unexpected voice, Rem opened her closed eyelids and raised her face.
Before Rem, who stared, stunned, wondering what had just been said, Ram, still hugging her own elbows, lowered the corners of her eyes slightly and said,

"Ram is the same as Rem. She thought it was frightening too."

"Afraid, meaning..."

"What if Rem accepted this synesthesia naturally, and came leaping into Ram's arms as though it were only natural? —That's what frightened her."

"――――"

Her expression still soft, and yet speaking of fear all the same—Rem found herself unable to understand the feelings of the Ram standing before her. —No, that wasn't it.
She was understanding something she hadn't understood before, and that was what left her confused.

"U!"

Suddenly, in place of Rem, whose eyes wandered in bewilderment, Rui, clinging to her waist, growled.
Glaring up at Ram from below, Rui, sharply attuned to the trembling in Rem's heart, sharpened her gaze as though scolding Ram for causing it.
Receiving that gaze from Rui, Ram narrowed her eyes slightly and said,

"Trying to stand between Ram and Rem, are you? Of all people, you?"

"Ah-uu!"

"I see, you have no intention of backing down. Complicated, but not unpleasant."

Whatever history lay between them, Ram spoke as though she knew Rui too, then turned her eyes back to meet Rem's. Once their gazes met, Rem couldn't tear her eyes away.
Even as she felt her heart scorched by the intensity of that gaze, her soul was drawn in all the same.

The fear kept growing, stronger and stronger.
And just as strongly, growing right alongside it, was the pull she felt toward Ram.
Feeling that keenly, Rem bit her lip hard, and said,

"You're surely my sister. That much I can believe without a doubt. It's just..."

"Your heart accepts it, but your head can't?"

"...Yes."

"I see. Then, for now, that's enough."

At Rem's weak nod, Ram closed the distance by another step.
As she drew near, tension filled Rem's shoulders, and Rui moved forward to shield her. But Rem gently held Rui's shoulder back, stopping the gentle girl with an "It's all right."

And then, of her own will, Rem faced Ram head-on.
Receiving Rem's gaze, Ram unfolded her crossed arms and held out a hand. Toward that outstretched hand, from the one her heart had already admitted was her sister.

"I'm Ram. Your sister, and surely born to love you."

"――――"

"Though I'm also an independent sister who has someone else just as precious to her."

At those words, spoken so calmly, so confidently, Rem's eyes went wide.

"You really are a frightening person after all. Talking with you, I feel like I might just surrender to this sense of security and become completely captivated."

"Can't be helped. Ram is a sister one can't help but want to entrust with complete trust and affection."

"Yes, Ram-neesan."

Puffing out her chest with pride, Ram had her hand taken timidly by Rem, who finally addressed her that way. Hearing it, Ram furrowed her brow slightly.
For Rem, that had been a way of addressing her that took courage, so that reaction was rather unwelcome.

"Um?"

"...It galls me to go along with what Barusu says, but it really doesn't feel right."

"Um?"

"Eh?"

At the single word Ram added after a momentary sour look, Rem's eyes went wide. At Rem's reaction, Ram repeated the same thing once more.

"Call me Oneesama. That fits best."

"...Ram-oneesama?"

"The name's unnecessary."

"—Oneesama."

Obediently bringing her way of addressing her closer to what was requested, Ram shut her eyes tight. Then she drew Rem's held hand toward herself. As Rem let out a startled "wa" and stumbled forward, Ram gently caught her body, and Rem found herself embraced by Ram, face to face.
Just like that, Ram brought her lips close to Rem's ear, and said,

"I felt it clearly, just now. —Ram is Rem's Oneesama."

"—I felt it too."

Just like that, the moment she was held in those arms, what she had feared came pouring out.
What she had called a sense of security was affection for Ram, a pull that came from her very soul. And it swallowed even the memoryless Rem, firmly, gently.

"...It galls me, but are you ready, Rem?"

"Ready to go inside. We need to show it off, of course to Emilia-sama and the others, but to Barusu too. Both to boast of our sisterly love, and to put their worries to rest."

At Ram's way of putting it, Rem shut her eyes.
Still, within Rem, she hasn't found an answer for how she should face Emilia and the others, or Subaru, or how she should carry herself with them.
But at the very least, there's no doubting this sister's feelings, or her own feelings toward Ram.

"Yes, Oneesama."

"Perhaps I should put this off until I've been called that a hundred times, after all."

"Oneesama..."

"That was a joke."

At Rem's nod, Ram made a joke that didn't sound like a joke at all.
At her sister's mischievous manner, despite her own sense of urgency and everything else going on, Rem's heart, for just that one moment, was filled with relief.

"Uau, auaー u?"

"Yes, it's all right. Rui-chan, thank you for worrying too."

"U!"

Caught between Rem and Ram's exchange, Rui, who'd stood by Rem's side the whole time, had her unease dissolved with a smile, and Rem gently stroked the gentle girl's head.
Then, bringing Rui along, she turned halfway to stand beside the sister who'd turned to face her, summoning her courage. Meeting Ram's gaze and nodding, the two of them reached out toward the door before them—

"—Excuse us. We could hear the noise all the way out here."
"A voice fitting for Barusu—like some overexcited little critter shrieking."

And so, sisters together, they stepped into the room where their companions waited.

Subaru himself managing to reunite with Emilia and the others too—that alone is cause for great celebration.

Beatrice, who to the very end hadn't wanted to let go of Subaru's hand.
