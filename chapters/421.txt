Arc 6 Chapter 19: "The Sage's Whereabouts"

An unexpected pitched battle had left the sixth floor of the Pleiades Watchtower awash in the aftermath of fierce combat.

Dust hung in the air, and a man and woman grappled together, smeared with sweat and grime.
And then there were the others, watching the two of them with cold eyes.

"What's with that look?! Are you saying it's my fault?! It's not my fault! It's not my fault—!!"

"Subaru, please don't disillusion me any further."

"I thought there might be some good in you, but I guess in the end, Barusu is just Barusu."

Such were the impressions of Julius and Ram, freshly descended, regarding Subaru as he was thoroughly pinned down by Shaula.

Be that as it may—

"It's a stroke of luck that we managed to regroup safely. Your waking up is welcome news too... but I'm afraid I can't make sense of the situation without a verbal explanation."

"I'd explain if I could... Damn it! Let go!"

"Nooo waaay, ssir!"

Seeing Shaula clinging to Subaru's arm, Julius, who had had his hand on the hilt of his sword, shrugged it off—no doubt having judged she had no hostile intent. Subaru agreed with that assessment, but that was a separate matter from the plain fact that he was having a hell of a time prying her off.

"Don't just stand there, lend a hand peeling her off...! This chick's got monster strength—"

"Your nose is growing, you lecherous Barusu."

"It is not, and don't lump those two things together! Don't extrapolate! Emilia-tan, ow! Pulling my hair isn't really going to help, y'know!?"

"Oh, sorry. I wasn't trying to help at all."

"You're apologizing for THAT part!?"

The stubborn Shaula refused to release Subaru's arm, and Emilia, wearing a strangely blank expression, kept tugging at his hair. Meanwhile, Beatrice, caught up in the tussle between Subaru and Shaula, had her eyes spinning, collapsed in a red-faced heap with a faint "gyuu."

"Well now... after all that trouble, the mood sure's changed fast, hasn't it?"

"Maybe it's because our Subaru-san woke up? I'm not great with noise, but I think it's good he's awake."

Trailing behind Julius and Ram, Anastasia and Meili came slowly down the stairs to join the group. That accounted for everyone except Rem and Patrasche, who were said to still be under treatment.

Now that everyone had gathered, though, there was no way to have a calm, orderly conversation like this.

"E-nough! Everybody, settle down! —Let's talk."

Straining his just-woken throat to bring the situation under control, Subaru got everyone to comply, at least for the moment.

That said, within the circle the group had formed, Shaula remained latched onto Subaru's right arm, refusing to let go, while on his left, Emilia sat in seiza close enough that their shoulders nearly touched. And on his lap, Beatrice had staked her claim—altogether, from an outside perspective, an absurdly extravagant formation.

"Lewd."

"You—did you see the state of things a minute ago? Does it look like I *wanted* my right side to be like this? My bones are creaking something awful. At this rate it's going to go necrotic."

Spitting the words at Ram, Subaru looked down at his restrained right arm.
Put into words, being embraced by a half-naked beauty sounded like a dream come true, but the reality was that instead of savoring the softness of her skin, what actually terrified him was the fully locked-out joint, the wrung-out flesh, and the effect on his bones and nerves. Bluntly put, it was about to pop clean off.

"Anyway, before my arm falls off at the root, I'd like to get things moving—but first, I'm glad to see everyone made it through okay. Not having Rem and Patrasche confirmed yet is scary, though..."

"Still doubting us? It's fine, I suppose. Everyone's safe, I suppose."

"Beatrice-sama is right. That said, your anxiety and concern are entirely justified. You should properly go visit them later. —This time, everyone truly had their hearts in their throats."

Beatrice and Julius both nodded along as Subaru rejoiced at the reunion.
Julius made a sour face, seemingly recalling the moment right after they'd parted ways in the sand sea.

"The instant the space split, all of us except you, Anastasia-sama, and Ram fell beyond that field of witchbeast flowers. From there, after a fair bit of trial and tribulation, we ended up taking shelter in the watchtower."

"But we didn't have you guuuys, and we knew we couldn't fight, so we were worried~"

Meili, pouting, agreed with Julius as he traced back over Beatrice's earlier explanation.
Of course their anxiety was only natural. The moment they'd fallen into the underground of the sand sea, Subaru and the others had been just as worried about the missing girls—it had gone both ways, it seemed.

"Emilia-oneesan got sooo worked up, you know. She really went at that Sage person something fierce. Even now it's kind of scary just remembering it~"

"Wait, Meili. You don't need to go telling him that."

At Meili's sidelong glance, precocious beyond her years, Emilia turned red and protested. That the girl's words weren't exaggerated was made obvious by Emilia's reaction—red all the way to her ears.

It was inappropriate of him, but Subaru was honestly happy to hear she'd worried about him.

"I see, I see, so Emilia-tan was worried about me. Sounds like Bea-ko cried her eyes out worrying too—I'm a lucky man."

"Honestly, Subaru, teasing again... And besides, Beatrice didn't cry her eyes out. It was more like half that. Right?"

"If you're going to be considerate, be considerate all the way to the end, I suppose, you airhead...!"

"—?"

Even after the fact of her tearful eyes had been exposed, Beatrice sulked, but Emilia didn't notice at all. Subaru's cheeks loosened at the heartwarming exchange as he looked up at Julius.

"So that means you must've been plenty scared too, huh. Shame I didn't get to see that face."

"Naturally, I was greatly shaken. Setting you aside, there was Anastasia-sama and Lady Ram—two delicate women, out of my reach. It was agonizing. I'm glad you two didn't see me pale-faced and scrambling about; I'm only now settling my frayed nerves."

"How does a story about being so scared you couldn't move hand or foot come out sounding so elegant?"

Touching his bangs out of habit, Julius gave a faint, wry smile, and Subaru twisted his lips at him. Neither putting up a brave front nor showing any weakness—it was a thoroughly unsatisfying result. At the exchange between the two of them, Meili let out a small snort of laughter.

Wondering what was so funny, Subaru glanced her way; she held her braid to her mouth and

"Nooothing~"

was all she said, laughing it off with a meaningful smile.

"Well, anyway, I get that everyone who got separated was worried about us. Since the result is that nobody's missing, we can look back and laugh about it."

"Only someone as thick-skinned and shameless as Barusu could be that bold. Ram's slender body and tiny heart still feel ready to burst with anxiety and terror just remembering it."

"The onee-san with what I'm pretty sure is the most hair on her heart of anyone I know is saying something?"

In truth, among Subaru's acquaintances, Ram boasted one of the strongest mentalities around. Naturally her remark was something to be brushed off as nonsense, but Subaru found himself staring intently at Ram's face, seated across from him.

Pale skin, pale pink eyes. Needless to say, finely sculpted features and a cool, refined beauty. A lovely face poised between the charming and the elegant, like some bewitching fruit. However he looked at it, it was the same old Ram.

"—Stop looking at me like that. Staring so intently, what are you trying to do?"

"No, I was just thinking, huh, that's Ram all right."

"This one's a lost cause."

"Don't pass judgment with no subject in the sentence! That's not it, I mean—"

Ram dismissed Subaru's words as idle chatter, leaving Subaru fumbling for words.
What rose up in his mind was what had happened just before he'd lost consciousness underground—Ram standing in front of the centaurs, shielding an injured Subaru with her own wounded body.

Wounds all over her, that slight figure facing down a formidable foe with no chance of winning. Seeing her unafraid of getting hurt like that, he'd tasted the fear of loss—and now, here they both were, safely alive.

Trading words with her, as unflappable as ever, was what made that fact truly sink in.

"Yeah, it's this distance between us after all. Things got weirdly hostile, or heavy, at times, but looking back, even that... no, well, that particular memory isn't going to become a fond one, I'll admit."

"I can't tell what you're mumbling on about. Spit it out. Like a man."

"Just glad we're both okay, is all. And—thanks for protecting me there at the end."

"...What a waste of time that was."

"You're gonna say THAT to me!?"

Here a person had honestly conveyed their gratitude, and she was a young lady thoroughly unworthy of the effort.
As Subaru sulked at that, Emilia gave a suppressed laugh.

"It's okay, Subaru. Ram's just a little embarrassed, that's all."

"Sis, embarrassed...? A natural disaster would be more convincing."

"I-it's true! I think it's probably because Subaru kept hugging her the whole time until she woke up, so she's feeling awkward about it. And you let go pretty roughly too."

"Emilia-sama!"

Emilia whispered this to him in secret, but Ram reacted fiercely to the words. Perhaps she'd read Emilia's lips, because—a rare sight—the corners of her eyes rose sharply.

"I don't think it's wise to judge things by your own standards. Ram has no wish to serve a scatterbrain who can't read other people's hearts."

"...That scatterbrain isn't me, right?"

"Indeed. If Emilia-sama were properly able to consider others' feelings, an existence with nothing to do with being a scatterbrain, then this would be an entirely unrelated, nonsensical conversation."

"Right. Um, let me think about that for a second, wait."

At Ram's spectacularly sarcasm-laced tirade, Emilia began earnestly pondering. Meanwhile, Ram fixed Subaru with a blade-like glare and mouthed, silently, "Forget it."

Cowed by the sheer intensity of it, Subaru nodded reflexively—and then recalled that when he'd woken up, there had been traces in his bed of someone having lain there together with him. He'd assumed it was Beatrice, but putting the current conversation together, it seemed—

"Whoa, forgot it, forgot it. Already forgotten, I mean."

"Good. Scatterbrain—no, Emilia-sama. That's quite enough."

"Uu, isn't Ram getting less and less deferential toward me lately...?"

Emilia shot her an accusing look, but Ram deflected it with an air of innocence.
In any case, since Ram clearly didn't want that particular subject touched further, Subaru averted his gaze and turned toward the last of their companions, Anastasia.

She sat neatly beside Julius, fiddling as usual with the pom-pom decoration on her hat, but when she noticed Subaru's gaze, she smiled sweetly.

"Ah, done talking? Natsuki-kun, I was starting to think you'd plumb forgotten about little old me."

"You'll have to forgive a man for prioritizing his own family a little. Glad you're safe too, all things considered. During that last fight underground, I honestly had no idea what was even happening anymore."

"Well, we got thrown into pitch darkness ourselves and had a scary time of it too, y'know? Natsuki-kun and Ram-san and... and Patrasche-chan were putting up a fight, and the witchbeast ignored us entirely. Since we couldn't be much use there, I figured I'd make myself useful in the negotiations instead."

"Negotiations?"

"With the 'Sage' who saved us, right there."

Anastasia pointed at Subaru's right arm, where Shaula was still nuzzling her cheek against it.
Hearing "this" and "negotiations" in the same breath, Subaru made a sour face, but Anastasia gave a wry smile.

"Now, now, we were plenty confused ourselves. I mean, up until just now, this was someone who wouldn't answer a thing no matter how you pushed or pulled, and now she's gone all lovey-dovey over Natsuki-kun like this."

"Quiet? Her?"

"Not 'her,' it's Shaula, sir. Master~"

"So annoying..."

Subaru grew weary at Anastasia's shrug and Shaula's pouting.
Looking purely at the situation, sure, the others had a point. But Subaru had his own side of the story too. In truth, the whole situation had been beyond anyone's expectations.

"When someone suddenly cozies up to you with a hundred affection points, even if she's a beauty, my own affection points are stuck at zero, so it's not exactly a simple case of being confused..."

"—! Did you just call me a beauty, sir!?"

"You've got awfully convenient hearing, geez!"

Once again, Subaru tried using his free left arm to push Shaula's head away and pry her off. But the more he did, the harder Shaula struggled, and her arm strength only grew—Subaru surrendered.

In the end, still left clinging to his right arm, Subaru, breathing hard, shook his head and said "no helping it," then—

"Let's set the trivial stuff aside for now and talk about what needs talking about. I'm done running around in circles because of a lack of explanation. You're going to tell us all kinds of things."

"Yes siree, if it's what Master says, then okey-dokey, sir."

"Splendid, your cooperation is a great help. Then, if I may ask—are you the 'Sage' who has taken up residence in this Pleiades Watchtower? Is that a correct understanding?"

"Pfft, sir."

"Answer him! You just said you'd talk!"

Just when she'd seemed to be laughing amiably, Shaula turned her face pointedly away from Julius's question. At her poor attitude, Subaru barked at her, and Shaula turned a pouting face on him instead.

"Sheesh, what a Master, sir. 'Don't say anything unnecessary to anyone who asks anything, don't tell, don't teach, or I'll skewer you'—that's what Master told me, sir. I'm just faithfully following that teaching, sir. Getting scolded for it is downright unreasonable, sir! I'll sue, sir!"

"That Master sounds like a real piece of work!"

"Yup yup. Master's a super awful person, sir. I demand deep reflection and a formal apology, sir."

"So don't just go calling people your Master without permission—wait, what, Emilia-tan, what's with that look?"

At the bizarre comedy routine between Subaru and Shaula, Emilia was narrowing her round eyes. Fearing another mysterious fit was coming on, Subaru braced himself, but Emilia went on, "No, it's not that," and said—

"It's probably nothing big, but... hmm, how do I put it. Doesn't the way Subaru and that Shaula girl talk—the vibe—kind of seem similar?"

"I don't talk in some lowly flunky's dialect like that!?"

"Not that, more like the phrasing, I mean. See, Subaru, even when you're having a serious talk you still crack jokes, right? It feels kind of like that."

"That's how I'm seen!?"

Subaru was aghast at this offensive assessment popping up out of nowhere, but Emilia didn't seem to mind at all. If anything—

"Hmm... now that you mention it, I suppose."
"You mean her conversational skill is on par with Barusu's? That doesn't bode well for her."
"I quite like the way Onii-san talks, personally?"
"But if everyone's like that, all the time, wouldn't it get exhausting?"

At the surrounding agreement and less-than-flattering assessments, Subaru's jaw hung open, speechless. But in his place, one furious little girl rose up in righteous indignation—Beatrice.

"Honestly, the nerve of you all, saying whatever you please, I suppose. And you—you've been far too familiar and clingy with Subaru this whole time, I suppose. Do you have any idea how much trouble it took Betty to hold Subaru's hand!? Not fair, I suppose!"

The topic had drifted somewhere along the way, but Beatrice's spirit was appreciated nonetheless. Still, Shaula deflected Beatrice's protest with an expression no beautiful girl ought to wear.
At Shaula's utterly, thoroughly expressed disinterest, Beatrice's already-short fuse reached its limit.
But before it could go off, Subaru's chop landed on Shaula's forehead first.

"Enough out of you already."

"Ow... not really, sir, but abuse!? Abuse, sir...! Master used violence against me, sir! See you in court, sir!"

"Shut up! And actually talk to people who aren't me for once! This isn't getting anywhere!"

"...Is that okay, sir?"

"It's fine! Encouraged, even! Get serious already, would you!"

At Subaru's scowling plea, Shaula's expression went blank with surprise. Then, gradually, her expression shifted, passing in sequence through surprise, understanding, acceptance, and finally elation.

And then—

"Uhyaa! Yay, sir~! Permission to talk has been granted, sir~! Now I don't have to play the mysteriously enigmatic beautiful girl anymore~! Hoorayyy!"

"There's not a single trace of that left in you anyway!!"

If she'd had a tail, it surely would have been wagging at full throttle—that much of a blooming smile.
Indeed, Shaula's ponytail whipped about in sheer delight, striking Subaru's cheeks and forehead repeatedly, his arm still held captive as it was.

"—So, you're the so-called 'Sage' everyone's been talking about, right?"

After the commotion had finally died down, they truly, at long last, arrived at the exchange that should have come before all the commotion.
Having freed Subaru's right arm, Shaula now sat cross-legged in the middle of the circle the group had formed, surrounded on all sides. Incidentally, that right arm was numb and wouldn't lift. Wouldn't move at all.

"—————"

"Answer me. You're the 'Sage,' right?"

"Hmm, that question's a tough one, sir."

Met with silence, Subaru repeated the same question again. Shaula's expression soured, as if she'd bitten into a pickled plum, and she gave an ambiguous answer.
Subaru knit his brow in confusion at the response. But it was Emilia who raised her hand instead. Prefacing with "Well then," she said—

"Setting aside whether you're the 'Sage' or not... it's fine to say you're the one who's been watching over the dunes inside this tower this whole time, right?"

"Ah, that part's definitely me, sir. Four hundred years, day after day after day, watching over the sands, endlessly, endlessly—living through days too full of tears to even tell, sir...!"

"You poor thing...!"

"Emilia-tan, don't get swept up in it. And you, quit throwing in unnecessary sentiment too."

Shaula answered with trembling fists, and Emilia's eyes welled up in sympathy. While soothing Emilia, Subaru, taking Shaula's answer into account, exchanged a glance with Julius.
Julius dipped his chin at the glance and picked up the conversation with "Then."

"May we understand that the deeds generally known to the public as those of the 'Sage' were, for four hundred years, carried out by you, Lady Shaula?"

"Aw, the honorific's making me blush, sir. I'm not used to it, so you can just call me plain old Shaula, no honorific needed, sir. Lady Shaula, my my... hehehe."

"Then let me rephrase. —Is it correct that you are the one generally known as the 'Sage,' Shaula?"

"Well now, who's to say, sir? Even if you say 'generally,' I've never once left this tower, so I've got no clue what kind of rumors are going around out there, sir. Might well be some weird version's gotten around out there, sir. Like, that I'm the one being called the 'Sage,' or whatever."

Shaula's slack expression suddenly shifted, taking on a thoughtful cast, changing the whole mood around her. At the depth of that probing gaze, even Julius, facing her, narrowed his eyes.
Even setting Julius aside, this was not the kind of remark one could simply let pass. Because, if what Shaula had just said were true—

"If you are not the 'Sage,' then the entire legend of the 'Sage' Shaula would be a complete misattribution. Or is there another Shaula, one who is actually said to be the 'Sage'?"

"No clue who that 'Sage' Shaula going around town might be, sir, but as far as I know, I don't have any acquaintance named Shaula besides myself, sir. This name was given to me by Master, too, so unless Master gave it to someone besides me... sir."

Shaula's gaze flicked briefly toward Subaru, but neither anyone else present nor Subaru himself had ever given the name Shaula to anyone at all.
Still, it was a fact that the name "Shaula" had a certain resonance. Shaula was also the name of a star—one that forms the constellation Scorpius—meaning "the stinger."

His thoughts naturally drifted toward the true nature of the white light unleashed from the watchtower, but there was no need to press for details on that just now. What needed priority right now was a different matter.

"Since Master doesn't seem to have any idea, I guess Shaula really is just my own name, then, sir. The name Master gave me, mine alone... don't need any other Shaula, sir."

"I see. You seem to be quite fond of this Master of yours. —Whoever this person is, they've quite the sin to answer for."

"Stop looking at me while you say that. False accusation. Presumption of innocence."

"When in doubt, punish, I suppose—that's what Ram thinks."

"Have a little pride in the rule of law, would you, please?"

Interjections aside, there was no hint of falsehood in Shaula's words or manner. If so, then perhaps it really was the legend itself that was mistaken.
Then, off to the side of the discussion, Anastasia began rummaging through her belongings. Wondering what she was up to, Subaru watched as she pulled out some coins from a coin purse.

"Suddenly counting your coins... that's not what this is, is it?"

"It's just a habit of mine—fiddling with coins helps me think—but that's not the point this time. Here, take a good look at the kingdom's coins and you'll see."

So saying, Anastasia tossed the coins in her palm at the uncomprehending Subaru. Catching them in a fluster, he found he'd been handed a copper coin, a silver coin, a gold coin, and a sacred gold coin—four in all.
Surely this wasn't meant as pocket money or a bribe for Shaula. As instructed, Subaru examined the coins and noticed the designs stamped into them.
He'd never really looked closely at money before, but—

"Stamping pictures or patterns onto money—that's the same everywhere, huh. Weird."

"Not really weird, is it? You need to be able to tell where a given currency's from... and besides, currency is always bound up with a nation's history. That's why what gets stamped on it runs deep with the country's history."

"Hmm...?"

Listening to Anastasia's explanation, Subaru turned his attention to the engravings on the coins. Looking closely, sure enough, each coin bore a different design—

"The sacred gold coin is the 'Divine Dragon,' the gold coin is the 'First Sword Saint,' the silver coin is the 'Sage,' and the copper coin is 'Lugunica Royal Castle.' Didn't you know?"

"E-Emilia-tan's saying something like a know-it-all character now...!"

"I mean, isn't it common knowledge? Never even properly looking at your own money—Subaru, are you just spending it on autopilot or something?"

At Emilia's cutting inquiry, Subaru whistled to cover his embarrassment. And upon checking while doing so, sure enough, the coins' engravings matched her explanation exactly.
The sacred gold coin bore a dragon, the gold coin a sharp-eyed man, the copper coin the royal castle—and stamped on the silver coin was—

"A young, handsome guy. Nothing at all like Shaula."

"But the one the world at large thinks is Shaula is the guy in this picture."

What was engraved was a long-haired, striking man with rugged good looks. Naturally, no matter what angle you looked from, there was no mistaking him for a half-naked beauty.
When Shaula herself begged, "Let me see, sir!" Subaru handed it over, and she studied the silver coin closely, then—

"Heh, pretty good work, sir. Spitting image of Master."

"How is it!? Ah, no, wait—if the 'Sage' stamped on there is your Master, does that mean it resembles the Master you remember?"

"Come on now, what are you saying, sir. My Master is just Master, sir."

"Then let me rephrase again. How is it!?"

Comparing the figure on the silver coin with Subaru, Subaru coughed and raised his voice.
The hairstyle was different, the eyes were different, and honestly the two didn't even look like the same ethnicity. About the only thing they had in common was sex. Judging purely by the category of good looks, Julius would be a far closer match than Subaru.
But Shaula gave Subaru's argument a dissatisfied look and said, "Aw, sir"—

"Way I see it, it's captured you pretty accurately, sir. You've got hair, two eyes and two ears, a nose and a mouth too, sir."

"That's the standard you're using!? You're not praising a kindergartner's drawing here!?"

"Even I don't really think this Sage and Subaru look alike..." Emilia added.

Shaula pouted, unconvinced, but of course both Subaru's protest and Emilia's judgment were failing grades. Naturally so. Still, Shaula wore a face that remained unconvinced, and said—

"But see, sir, I'm just not good at comparing people's faces. Aside from whether it's a man or a woman, they all look about the same to me after that. ...Well, maybe size, too, sir."

"Did you just add that on after looking at Betty, I wonder?"

"Bea-ko being small and cute is a one-of-a-kind thing, so that's fine. More importantly, you've got a sense of aesthetics that loose and you still called me your Master!? It's mistaken identity, plain and simple!"

Riding on Shaula's excuse, Subaru moved to clear his name of the unjust dishonor heaped on him so far. It had been a strange claim from the very start, after all. Naturally, there was no need to even consider it—Subaru had never had any point of contact with Shaula—so, with this, relief.

Or so he thought, when Shaula shook her head and said—

"Ah. The way I found Master wasn't about looks, so that's not really an issue, sir."

"Not about looks—then what did you go by? An aura?"

"Smell, sir."

"—————"

"I mean, come on, sir, who else but Master could give off a stench that dark and foul enough to make your nose bend sideways and stay totally fine with it, sir."

"I've genuinely never been insulted that badly before! What, am I really that bad!?"

The moment the word "smell" had come up, Subaru had braced himself for an instant. But Shaula's utter lack of tact in what followed shattered that resolve in one blow.
Face reddening at the outrageous assessment, Subaru looked at her, and Shaula tilted her head in confusion.

"Why're you mad, sir? Ah, is it 'cause I said foul, sir? It's fine, sir! Master's smell is seriously awful, sir, but it's the kind of awful you just can't help wanting to sniff again and again, sir! Not like puke or anything, more like a weird delicacy kind of thing, so it's fine, sir!"

"Don't casually say puke like that, you're a girl! And that's not making it better!"

Subaru covered his face with both hands and crumpled to the floor as if weeping, mortified.

"What is this... I thought I'd started getting used to being talked about like this, but being humiliated this way is just too much. What did I even do..."

"I-it's okay, Subaru. I understand, really, I do. So let's have a good bath together later, okay?"

"You do NOT understand!"

With Subaru rendered useless, sniffling in dejection, and Emilia comforting him out of the corner of her eye, Beatrice sighed. Then, arms crossed, still leaning her back against Subaru, she said—

"So, you understand now why you mistook Subaru for some stranger you'd never met, I suppose?"

"Wasn't a mistake, sir. Cheeky for a shorty, aren't you, sir."

"Unfortunately for you, that shorty happens to be the partner of your precious Master. Oh, but I suppose it was mistaken identity, so he was never your Master to begin with, was he. My apologies, I suppose."

"—————"
"—————"

Quietly, sparks of tension flew between Beatrice's and Shaula's gazes.
The topic had scattered completely off course by now, but there were simply too few reasonably sane people present to steer it back. When it came to that, it was the side of common sense that always ended up paying the price.

"Beatrice-sama, please save the rivalry for after we're done. Once the important discussion is over, you're free to decide who Barusu's owner is, however you like."

"Subaru isn't owned by anyone, I suppose. That's precisely the point."

"...Yes, yes."

With a thoroughly exasperated look, Ram made Beatrice's anger recede. Then Ram narrowed her eyes and spoke to Shaula, whose gaze was still fixed sharply on Beatrice.

"In any case, it's fortunate you're able to talk now. Until a moment ago you wouldn't even engage in mundane conversation, but this time it'll be fine, I trust?"

"Mm, Master gave the go-ahead, so it's okay, sir. Though, that said, I'm not so nice that I'll answer just anything, sir. Well, more like, I don't actually know that much, sir."

"That answer alone tells me you're mistaken about being the sage."

Puffing out her ample chest with pride, Shaula declared her ignorance with almost brazen confidence. As if in competition, Ram puffed out her own modest chest and gave a haughtier sniff than anyone else present.

"You're Shaula, but you're not the 'Sage.' In that case, do you have any recollection of the 'Sword Saint' or the 'Divine Dragon'?"

"Kenseh and Shinryu?"

"Their names are Reid and Volcanica."

"Ughhh, sir."

At Ram's question, Shaula stuck out her tongue as if she'd bitten into something bitter.
It was an easy reaction to read—she recognized both names. When Ram silently pressed her to elaborate, Shaula rocked side to side where she sat cross-legged and said—

"Course I know 'em, sir. Reid the Stick-Swinger and Volcanica the Sarcastic are old pals of mine, sir. Haven't seen either of 'em once since we parted ways, but I bet they're doing fine, sir?"

"One of them—Reid—is dead. Has been for quite some time."

"For real, sir!? He was the type who wouldn't die even if you killed him, and he up and died, sir!? Why'd he die, sir!? Did he eat something weird, sir?"

"His lifespan. No one can defy the mandate of heaven."

"Lifespan... ah, right. That's right, sir. Reid was human, wasn't he, sir."

Informed of the "death" of an old acquaintance, Shaula lowered her eyes with a subdued air. Even her ponytail seemed to droop, somehow, and her shoulders slumped in a sorrowful way.
Whatever her attitude or manner had been up to this point, the sight of someone grieving a friend's death was equally poignant regardless.
Even Subaru, only now beginning to recover his composure, felt a lump in his throat at Shaula's profile in that moment.

"So then, is Volcanica doing okay, sir?"

"That one's a dragon, so."

"Ah, right, dragon, sir. Wish it'd been Volcanica who died instead of Reid, honestly, sir~"

"Wow, harsh words there, geez."

But after showing just the faintest trace of sorrow, Shaula moved right along. Speaking harshly of her other old acquaintance, she wore a thoroughly unbothered expression.
To that thoroughly unbothered Shaula, Ram closed one eye thoughtfully and said—

"You have knowledge of Reid and Volcanica. ...In that case, you should also know your Master's name."

"—————"

At Ram's question, Subaru and the others all held their breath in unison.
Only Shaula, the one being asked, blinked in confusion and answered, "Of course, sir."

Ram's question was, indeed, remarkably effective at unraveling the mystery of the "Sage."
Now that the equation "Sage" = Shaula had collapsed, if there was someone other than Shaula who had accomplished deeds worthy of being called "Sage," then naturally, they would need to meet that person. For some reason, Shaula mistook that person for Subaru, but there was no way to fudge a name.
Surely—surely—there wouldn't be some miracle where the name of a "Sage" called Natsuki Subaru came out here, Subaru practically prayed.

And that prayer was answered. —In a far stranger form than he could have imagined.

"It's a weird thing to wonder about Master's name, sir. The man himself is right there, sir, so wouldn't you all, being Master's companions, already know, sir?"

"Unfortunately, your Master hit his head on a toilet bowl and had a lot of things fall right out of it."

"Does it have to be specifically the toilet?"

"Master did it again, sir..."

"Again!?"

Met with a sympathetic look from Shaula, Subaru suffered an indignity he in no way deserved. But apparently satisfied by the answer, Shaula sprang to her feet as if bouncing up, then—

"Then, announcement time, from my own mouth, sir! Master's name... yes, the name of the exalted great sage! If anyone's fit to be called the 'Sage,' it's Master and Master alone!"

"Skip the preamble!"

"So impatient, sir~. But hey, that's Master for you too."

Shaula, drawing it out with theatrical gestures, stuck out her tongue in a pout at Subaru's demand. Then she pressed a finger to her cheek and said, in an oddly childlike gesture—

"Flugel."

"...Huh?"

"Master's name is Flugel, sir. The great sage Flugel, Shaula's Master, sir."

The instant she spoke the name, Shaula puffed out her chest, beaming with joy.
There, unmistakably, was pure respect, gratitude, and affection—there was no doubting the reverence and love Shaula held for Flugel.

However, the reaction of Subaru and the others to that name was, to put it mildly, mixed.
After all, that name rang a bell.

"...Isn't that the name of the guy who planted the trees?"

And so Subaru cocked his head, at the name of a great figure whose fate had crossed his own, once, long ago.
