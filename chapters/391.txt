Arc 5 Chapter 74: "Battle for Priestella Results, Part 1"

"The cowardly Witch Cult, which had occupied the four control towers and held the city hostage, has been entirely repelled. With this, the city's safety has been secured—the water gate city of Priestella is victorious!"

Subaru heard that announcement, which rang out across the entire city, while hurrying toward the municipal hall together with Emilia.

The jubilant declaration came through a Magic Vessel built to broadcast a voice across the whole city.
There were parts where the sound cracked, but that was no more than the speaker's voice breaking with emotion. As for the content—so worth celebrating—there seemed no room to doubt it.

"Subaru! Just now, was that—!"

"Sounds like it. Somehow, it feels like things have settled down, at least for now."

At Emilia's face brightening at once, Subaru let the tension drain from his shoulders as his cheeks loosened. The reason for that slackening was relief, along with a measure of unease.

"...After all, considering who we were up against."

With Capella, "Lust," possessing the Authority of mutation and metamorphosis, there was—in the worst case—even the possibility that the broadcast was a malicious ploy meant to give the residents false hope. That such wickedness couldn't be entirely ruled out was exactly what made the Sin Archbishops so appalling.

Still, the voice on the broadcast—the one who'd first declared himself Kiritaka Muse—couldn't fully hide a faint excitement and joy beneath his attempt at formality. Hearing that voice, they probably didn't need to worry that he was being "forced to say" something as part of some vile scheme.

"So, that means everyone managed to pull it off..."

The broadcast said that all four control towers seized by the Sin Archbishops had been retaken.
That meant, at the very least, they could consider the all-out-destruction ending—where the city sank beneath the water from the great floodgates opening—averted. On that point, they could honestly breathe easy. If there was a problem, it lay in the other half of what worried Subaru: the matter of human casualties.

"With Regulus, miraculously, we managed without a single victim."

Even though their side had fielded a fairly powerful formation, the enemy consisted of Sin Archbishops, each with their own vicious quirks. That they'd made it through the fight with Regulus with zero deaths was, in the end, only because their opponent had been just as hopeless in combat as Regulus himself.

The cunning "Lust," or the "Wrath" whose strategy remained unclear. And "Gluttony," with its deep-rooted grudge against them—there was no need to belabor how formidable and threatening these enemies were.
Even if they'd won, there was the fear of what the cost might have been.

"From what you told me, Subaru, the other control towers had Witch Cult Sin Archbishops too, right? I wonder if everyone else was okay..."

Sensing the same unease in Subaru, who couldn't quite manage a bright expression, Emilia spoke those words. Subaru shook his head, biting his lip, toward Emilia as she lowered her eyes.

"That's what's worrying me too... but there's nothing to do but trust the others on this one. I want to confirm everyone's safe, and soon."

"Yeah, that's true..."

Easy words of comfort wouldn't dispel the unease Emilia carried.
Given the sheer strength of this enemy, the possibility of losses on their side had been unavoidable. Even so, massive casualties in the course of saving the city could never be called a desired outcome.

Which was why, depending on how things stood, factoring in "Return by Death" as one option had been Subaru's resolve from the very start of this operation.

Fundamentally, Subaru did not consider it acceptable to build his own "Return by Death" into a strategy. That wasn't only out of resistance to choosing to end his own life—it also wasn't unrelated to what he'd seen during the Trials of the Tomb in the Sanctuary, about the world after Subaru's death.

In truth, Subaru didn't know whether the world would go on after he died, but the Trial had taught him that such a possibility existed. That was why Subaru was firmly resolved never to use "Return by Death" merely to increase his number of attempts.
Even so, if there was ever a case where Subaru would choose to invoke "Return by Death" of his own will, it would be when the result waiting for him was one he couldn't bear to move forward from, having lost what he'd lost.

And this time, Subaru was weighing that very possibility.

The Royal Selection candidates, their knights, and everyone else involved who had challenged the Sin Archbishops and sworn to retake the city.
The resolve to repeat, with all the pain and suffering that entailed, so as not to lose the people he didn't want to lose.

"...Subaru, you've got reeeally deep wrinkles between your brows."

"Huh?"

Emilia had been looking straight at Subaru, whose expression had fallen under a grave shadow. Pierced by that gaze right between the brows, Subaru couldn't help but blink in surprise.
To that Subaru, Emilia, her violet eyes filling with concern, said,

"I guess no matter what, worry is worry, huh. Sorry. It was such a difficult time, and it's partly because I got captured that..."

"No, it's not your fault, Emilia-tan. Even setting aside everything about you, Regulus needed to be taken down regardless. And if you hadn't been there, I don't know if we could've saved those brides."

To save "Greed's" brides, it had been necessary to temporarily stop the hearts of the women who'd been saddled with the "Lion's Heart." Among their currently available forces, Emilia had probably been the only one capable of pulling that off. Ferris might have managed it too, perhaps—but that was about the extent of the possibility.
In the worst case, without that, the brides might have had to become a necessary sacrifice for defeating Regulus.

"Well, that's not a choice I'd want to make either, and Reinhard wouldn't have allowed it."

Even framed as a necessary sacrifice to defeat a great evil, it wouldn't have been accepted. That young man, practically a walking embodiment of righteousness, could never tolerate even a small sacrifice. In that case, the battle with Regulus might not have concluded nearly so quickly.

"Or maybe, partway through, I would've died caught up in it all by myself."

To begin with, if Emilia hadn't been kidnapped, the composition of the team assigned to take down the Sin Archbishops might have been significantly different. No one could say what the "correct" answer would have been, so there was little point dwelling on it.
Still, if he could hope for anything—

"After we split up, Reinhard should've gone to join everyone else. I want to believe that helped keep the casualties down..."

"Yeah, that's right. —We need to confirm that, and soon."

Emilia nodded seriously at Subaru's reply.
Then, as she resumed her steps toward the municipal hall, Subaru walked alongside her and quietly placed a hand on his own chest. He felt his heart beating, faintly quickened—and there, apart from his resolve regarding "Return by Death," lay the existence of one more concern Subaru carried.

An unidentifiable black foreign sensation, nestling insensitively right beside his heart.
It was an impure, wicked something that had slipped smoothly inside Subaru the instant he'd confirmed Regulus's "death." Subaru had a vague idea of what that impurity's true nature was.

—A "Witch Factor."

The impurity connecting the Witch Cult to Subaru was, most likely, the thing called by that name.
Subaru had also come to harbor that same unpleasant sensation within himself right after defeating "Sloth," Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti. The one who'd first told him the identity of that foreign sensation—that it was a "Witch Factor"—had been the "Witch" Echidna, if he recalled correctly.

Witch Factors had deep connections to the Sin Archbishops and to the Witches of Sin themselves. And for some reason, they affected Subaru too, in a way that felt like it was eating away at him.
If that was so, then surely this, too, wasn't unrelated to the "Witch of Envy" who caused "Return by Death."
It was certainly nothing he could think of positively, as a good influence.

"No matter how much of this creepy stuff clings to me, I'm still me. ...That's how it has to be."

No matter how much the Witch Factor's influence grew, his own self would never waver. Even if the Witch Factor ate away at Subaru each time he brought down a Sin Archbishop. And besides—

"When Beako wakes up, I'll have to tell her, or she'll get mad at me."

Whatever the Witch Factor was, Subaru didn't need to agonize over it alone.
He had a partner to confide his worries in, someone who would think alongside him to try to solve the problem.
Surely, they'd find a way through this too.

"Subaru? Did something happen?"

As if worried about Subaru, who'd naturally fallen quiet, Emilia turned her eyes to him. Subaru shook his head, saying "Nah," and after thinking it over a little, said,

"I was just thinking, from that broadcast just now, sounds like Kiritaka's safe. When Liliana finds out, she's gonna be so happy."

"Kiritaka-san had gone missing?"

"He got listed as unaccounted for after shielding Otto. For some reason I didn't really get the feeling he was dead, so I wasn't all that worried."

"Then you should be happier for him, or it's not fair to him."

As if kicking away the unease hanging in the air, Emilia pouted her lips at Subaru.
Rather than fretting over the possibility that someone had been hurt, she chose to be glad over the fact that someone had been saved.

Perhaps that, too, was the mindset needed right now.

And so the two of them hurried along, trying as much as possible not to look their unease straight in the eye—but the sight that greeted them allowed for no such easy optimism.

"...Man, this is rough."

Before him, gazing at the municipal hall now collapsed into a mountain of rubble, Subaru was at a loss.
The words that finally spilled from his dry lips were nothing more than a blunt reaction to the sight.

The municipal hall had been a five-story building—a rare high-rise in this world's architecture—but its former grandeur had utterly crumbled, leaving no trace.
The scars of destruction extended all the way to the building's foundation; the ground where the hall had stood was crushed in at the center, sunken as though a great pit had opened its mouth.
Nothing short of a devastating blow to the building's foundation could have caused a collapse like this.

Subaru drew that inference from the wreckage, but Emilia, looking at the same sight, glanced around uneasily.

"That broadcast just now—it was using the Magic Vessel that was inside this building, right? So why does the building look like this...?"

"—! Now that you mention it, yeah..."

At Emilia's concern, Subaru hurriedly grew wary of their surroundings.
The collapse of the hall was no small matter. Without question, it was the result of the Witch Cult's hand reaching in. And the hall should have held not just the Magic Vessel, but also Otto and the others who hadn't joined the teams assigned to the control towers, along with the victims of Capella's Authority.

Given that the municipal hall had suffered this much damage, a battle here was inevitable. If so, what had happened to the hall, where mostly non-combatants had remained?
Even the factual basis of Kiritaka's broadcast now felt precarious.
But just as such unease gripped Subaru and the others—

"Ah, I thought I saw someone coming—so it's finally you two, I suppose."

"...Beatrice?"

A familiar girl's voice suddenly reached Subaru and the others as they stiffened.
Looking up, they saw Beatrice atop the pile of rubble, walking while lifting the hem of her fluffy dress, looking down at the two of them. She descended to Subaru's side, her eyes going wide as she looked him up and down thoroughly, and said,

"Hmph. Betty's relieved you don't seem to be hurt, I suppose. If you got hurt while Betty wasn't around, Betty won't even be able to let you go to the bathroom alone carelessly anymore, I suppose."

"I'm not that much of a handful of a kindergartner... anyway, Beako, why are you here of all places?"

Subaru was startled by Beatrice, arms crossed over her short frame, chin lifted with a haughty, composed expression. That insolent attitude was exactly like her usual self.

"Weren't you supposed to be sidelined from the front lines because of overusing Mana? At least, that's what I heard—that you couldn't take part in this fight."

"Don't call it 'overusing' like it makes it sound like it's Betty's fault, I suppose! If it weren't for Betty's devotion, Subaru's legs would be a size thinner by now, I suppose. Don't you think some gratitude, and comfort, and a hug are in order, I suppose!"

"I know, I know."

Subaru rubbed his hand roughly and familiarly over the head of Beatrice, who was fuming with indignation. Though she'd been puffing her cheeks in discontent, she still closed the distance to Subaru by one more step and quietly savored the head-rubbing.
And then, into that exchange between contractor and Spirit, Emilia gently inserted a word.

"Beatrice."

"...I'm glad you seem to be safe too, Emilia, I suppose. If something happened to you, Onii-cha would be sad too, I suppose. Because of that, Subaru ended up doing something as reckless as fighting without Betty, I suppose. If you've learned your lesson from this, don't go getting captured again, I suppose."

"Yeah, thanks. Sorry for worrying you."

"Betty barely worried about you at all, I suppose!"

Beatrice turned her face away with a huff, and Emilia watched her with a warm, amused look. Then, as Emilia looked over Beatrice's whole body, she narrowed her round eyes slightly.

The lavish dress, and the carefully arranged ringlets.
On both, faint splatters of blood and mud clung. That was proof that this Spirit girl hadn't simply woken from a peaceful sleep.

Subaru's absence, and the collapse of the municipal hall.
Such unforeseen circumstances surely weren't unrelated to Beatrice's awakening.

"――――"

Catching that meaningful look from Emilia, Subaru drew his chin in as well.
Then, looking down at Beatrice, still being petted, he said,

"Sounds like you worked hard while I was gone. Sorry, always causing you trouble."

"It's already a given, built into the equation, that Subaru causes Betty trouble, so there's no need to worry about it, I suppose. No, well, I suppose I'll worry about it a little after all. Worry about it, and be grateful, I suppose."

"Uh-huh, uh-huh. ...But even so, this is a bit much even for hard work. Flattening an entire building is a bit—"

"Wait, did Beatrice do this?"

Emilia pointed at the mountain of rubble, staring at Beatrice with astonished eyes.

"About how long would it take to repair a building this size...? Subaru, do you know?"

"With Beako's allowance, I know it'd take, like, decades—a massive undertaking."

"What are you two so solemnly implying?! It's not like Betty did this, I suppose! Betty's only seen this building after it turned into wreckage, I suppose!"

"I know, I know. You react so strongly whenever someone teases you. What a cute thing you are."

Subaru laughed heartily at Beatrice's protest against the false accusation. Emilia, watching the exchange, kept saying "wait, huh, which is it?"—but for now, that was fine.
If Beatrice was here around the ruined hall like this, that meant—

"Anyway, for now, it doesn't seem like there's any risk of the Witch Cult causing trouble around here. So then, what happened to Otto and the others, who should've been in the municipal hall?"

"Mm, that's a complicated thing to explain, I suppose. But the people who were inside the hall..."

"We aaall got out safe, so ya don' need ta worry, okay?"

A voice cut in on Beatrice's answer—not Kansai dialect, but Kararagi dialect.
Turning at the sound, they saw a small figure approaching, skirting around the mound of rubble. For a moment, something about the person's appearance felt off—it was because the hair she was combing with her fingers was dyed a different color than the one Subaru knew.

"Anastasia-san... is that you?"

"What's with that question mark tone... oh, right, yeah. My hair's a different color right now."

Her soft pale-lavender hair was now dyed a deep green.
That alone changed the impression considerably—this was Anastasia, in her kimono. She fixed her eyes on Subaru, Beatrice, and then Emilia, and gave a satisfied, graceful nod.

"Hm, looks like Natsuki-kun brought Emilia-san back safe and sound, just like he said he would. I heard from the Sword Saint, so I wasn't all that worried."

"Reinhard, he joined up with you all properly, then?"

"Came flyin' in like whoosh. Right now he's out huntin' down Witch Cult stragglers... or rather, more like he's makin' rounds of the shelters with Ferris-san."

"Rounds of the shelters... so the healer's earning his keep, huh."

Even with the Sin Archbishops repelled, restoring the damage the city had taken would require considerable effort. For the sake of quickly restoring the city's functions, Ferris's role would be significant. Reinhard, it seemed, was currently being used as Ferris's legs.

"Sorry for causing you all sorts of trouble too, on my end. ...But what happened on your end, Anastasia-san? The hair, and this building..."

"Right, right. Changing your hair to something easier on the eyes—new look? I think it suits you too, but knowing the original Anastasia-san, it still feels a bit off."

"Natsuki-kun, always flatterin' me for no reason. But this was just dyed for a bit of a plan. And it didn't even pay off—no, more like, just as you can see, we had an awful time of it."

Winding a lock of hair around her finger, Anastasia sighed as she gazed at the wreckage of the hall. Going by her words, it seemed she'd had no small part in the municipal hall's collapse.

"Ferris is safe, right? What happened, and what became of everyone else?"

"Simple story... after everyone went out to take down the Sin Archbishops, someone rotten took the chance ta attack while the place was undefended. We twisted 'em up nice an' easy, is what happened."

"That doesn't look like what 'twisted up nice and easy' would leave behind..."

Contrary to the lightness of Anastasia's tone, it was clear this had been a fierce battle.
An assault on the municipal hall—a vicious tactic exploiting the gap left by the combatants' departure—was probably either "Lust" or "Wrath." But the more likely candidate was—

"Was it 'Lust' who came?"

"Just like ya heard, personality was the absolute worst. Gave me the chills just runnin' into 'em."

That shouldn't have been an opponent light enough to dismiss with mere "chills," and yet there was no trace of fear or terror in Anastasia's demeanor. Quite the nerve, to encounter a Sin Archbishop like that.
He wanted to praise her for it, but more than that, Subaru felt a pang of self-reproach.

"Sorry. Slipping away from the control tower to ambush the municipal hall—I should've caught on to that."

"No need ta worry about it. It's just that with Natsuki-kun short-handed, we did what we pleased on our end. If anythin', I'm more embarrassed it didn't even pay off."

From the way Anastasia spoke, Subaru got the sense that she'd anticipated the ambush. The hair dyed green was probably part of laying the groundwork for it, too.
Putting together the deep green hair and the person Capella might be obsessed with, he could vaguely piece together the plan they'd laid.

"So Anastasia-san disguised herself as Crusch-san, as bait? And if you and Ferris alone drove off 'Lust' with just that, we don't really have a leg to stand on."

"Would've been cool if that's how it went, but there was one more person there. Priscilla-san's knight."

"...Al?"

At that unexpected name, Subaru's eyes went wide.
Of everyone, Al had been the least enthusiastic about the fight to retake the city. And besides, Al should have originally been heading off with Priscilla and Liliana to take down "Wrath."
If he'd stayed behind to defend the municipal hall instead, then the team assigned to take down "Wrath" would be left in a state of nothing but doubt and unease, both in terms of firepower and team composition.

"Just so ya know, the 'Wrath' team made it back safe too, no problem."

As if sensing Subaru's doubt, Anastasia added that. She gave a wry smile and glanced toward the direction of the control tower "Wrath" had occupied.

"Priscilla-san came back without a scratch. And the diva Liliana—came back with, of all people, the prince, and everyone was shocked."

"The prince... you mean Kiritaka? Those two went in together, and came back together with someone who'd been listed as unaccounted for? How does that even happen?"

That Priscilla had returned unharmed from taking down "Wrath," or that Kiritaka and Liliana had joined up—there were far too many mysteries. He'd like to hear more details on all that, but more pressing than that was the bigger picture.

"That broadcast just now, from Kiritaka—can we trust it?"

"――――"

"The control towers were successfully retaken. All that's left is the state of everyone who went in to fight. What happened there?"

The hall had collapsed, and yet the Magic Vessel had presumably been carried out.
If so, there was no need to doubt the possibility that the broadcast was some kind of trap. The only issue left was, in the end, the one thing they'd been carrying with them from the very start: the matter of the casualties incurred.
And to Subaru's question, Anastasia—

"Ya can relax. Natsuki-kun and the rest of ya were the last ones ta come back."

"We were the last... so, everyone?"

"Relax."

To Subaru, faintly on edge, and to Emilia and Beatrice, who'd been watching with anxious looks, Anastasia gave a smiling nod at all three of them, and said—

"Everyone's made it back safe. No one's missing."

—was her answer.

"Boss! You made it back safe, huh!"

In place of the collapsed municipal hall, the nearest shelter had become the new rallying point.
Seeing Subaru and the others arrive there, a blond boy—Garfiel—raised a joyful voice and ran up to them.

"Oh, Garfiel... wait—"

Subaru had started to raise a hand, but froze at the sight of the boy running toward him.
Garfiel, bare from the waist up, was drenched in blood from head to toe. And yet his expression was somehow radiant; while it looked like he'd fought a considerable battle, he seemed to have carried out his role to the end.

Taking that in, Subaru quickly shifted his expression from shock to a grin, and said,

"Hey, you're the one who doesn't look safe. You've got a hell of a mug on you right now."

"Ain't got the right ta say that ta me, boss... though I guess I can't really say that either, huh. But you, boss—you're the one who looks like he showed some real guts. Nice job bringin' Lady Emilia back safe."

"Of course."

Subaru held out his fist, and Garfiel met it with his own.
That alone was enough for the two of them to praise each other's efforts.

"But I heard 'Lust' showed up over at the municipal hall. Where were you fighting, and who with?"

"Obviously, it was Kurgan of the 'Eight Arms.' ...Though I dunno how much I can even keep callin' him by that name."

"—? What's that supposed to mean?"

"What I fought was just a corpse, plain and simple. Probably, when he was alive, he wasn't anything like that. So I don't really feel like I won, much."

A secret technique that manipulated the bodies of the dead, using them as warriors.
It was certain that technique had been used in this scheme of the Witch Cult's. Even so, did that mean a warrior's skill degraded compared to what it had been in life? A warrior on Garfiel's level might be the type to sense such a difference keenly.

Whether that was what was bothering him or not, apart from the victory itself, Garfiel seemed to feel somehow unsatisfied, incompletely burned out.
That sensation wasn't one entirely foreign to Subaru either.

"Are you down because the enemy wasn't strong?"

Unable to grasp that feeling, Emilia, who'd been listening in, tilted her head.
At her words, Garfiel first said, "Glad you're safe," rejoicing in her return, before roughly scratching at his short blond hair.

"It ain't like I'm down 'cause he wasn't strong... it's somethin' I can't really explain. Lady Emilia, you're a woman, so..."

"Something women wouldn't understand? Then does Subaru understand it?"

"Kind of, I guess. But even among men, there's this thing—like a wall you can't see across, between the strong and the weak. ...Still, I think that's a result of Garfiel being strong. Aren't you overthinking it?"

"Overthinkin' it... huh."

At Emilia's puzzled face and Subaru's less-than-fully-affirming reply, Garfiel bowed his head with a somewhat melancholy expression.
Garfiel was always troubled by something like "strength." That was true of his single-handed challenge to Reinhard, and it wasn't unrelated to the harsh baptism he'd received afterward at the hands of the Witch Cult, either.

A problem that no amount of thinking, no matter how hard you thought it over in your head, produced an answer to.
It might well be that kind of thing. So, as for that—

"Hey, Garfiel. Thinking about it like that isn't gonna—"

"Ooh! Garf, there you are! Doon-doon-DOON!!"

"Guah!?"

Before Subaru, who'd been about to offer some kind of advice, Garfiel's body went flying amid a racket of noise. He barely caught sight of a small shadow slamming into Garfiel around the waist, but there was no time to stop him from tumbling with a pained cry.
And looking toward the fallen Garfiel, there sat a cat-eared girl, perched right on his defenselessly sprawled chest, tail wagging happily.
The girl's ears stood straight up, her adorable face full of mischievous delight, as she said,

"Fuhaha! You're too careless, Garf! The real enemy is inside your own heart! And inside your heart, there's always someone important too! In other words, MANPAI!"

"Grumblin' and moanin', sittin' on top of a guy..."

"Hmph, Mimi's doing what Ojou told her to! Keep a man under your thumb, and it pulls in all sorts of affection and stuff...? Something like that? Ojou said something about how that's like a bargaining thing or whatever! She said it, so Mimi tried keeping you under her thumb!"

The one laughing gleefully on top of Garfiel was Mimi.
There was no trace left of the pallor she'd had, drained of blood from an unhealing wound. Seeing the girl's face fully restored to health, Subaru let out a breath of relief.

"Mimi, you're feeling better?"

"Yep, Onii-san, welcome back! Welcome back! While Mimi was sleeping, sounds like all kinds of stuff happened, but good work! Mimi slept super well! But Garf worked reeeally hard too, huh? Good work!"

"G-glad to see nothing's changed. Right, Garfiel?"

Mimi's injury, Subaru had heard, was one she'd taken protecting Garfiel.
Garfiel had carried her back near death, deeply shaken by the sight of a wound that wouldn't heal. Seeing Mimi recovered like this, he must have felt tremendous relief.
And yet, at Subaru's call, Garfiel—still pinned beneath her—rubbed his nose and said,

"Hah, I'm in trouble 'cause nothin's changed too much. I've told ya a bunch already, actin' up like this while you're still recoverin'..."

"Hm, what? Garf, did you say something? ...Ah!"

Mimi, leaning her face close to Garfiel's, suddenly cried out and peered down into her own front. Checking inside her white robe, her round eyes blinked rapidly, and she said,

"Garf, uh-oh! The wound opened up again! There's blooood coming out!"

"You idiot! That's exactly why I keep tellin' ya! Damn it, gotta rewrap the bandage and redo the healing magic! C'mon, over here!"

"Uwaaah! It huuurts! It huuurts!"

Considering the worsening wound, Mimi's hand seemed remarkably relaxed as Garfiel pulled her along toward the back of the shelter. The exchange was so noisy, like a typhoon passing through, that even Subaru was left dumbfounded.

"Pfft... G-Garfiel, at this rate, he might not even have time to keep worrying."

But beside Subaru, who'd been standing there stunned, Emilia suddenly put a hand to her mouth and burst out laughing. Watching the backs of the two retreating figures, she referred back to Garfiel's earlier worry.
Indeed, Subaru agreed with that assessment.

"Somehow or other, they're a good match, those two."

"Mimi's cute, and she seems to reeeally like Garfiel... but Garfiel seems to like Ram too, so I don't think it's a simple story."

"Yeah, that's true... wait, Emilia-tan, commenting on romance between a man and a woman!?"

Even though it was a fairly clear-cut example, Subaru was surprised she could even discuss it.
For Emilia—who'd left even Subaru's own confession on hold, saying she didn't quite understand romantic love between men and women—to weigh in on someone else's love affairs like that.

"Mm, Subaru, I feel like you just said something reeeally rude just now."

"I thought it was a pretty accurate observation, myself. ...Don't tell me, without my noticing, there's been some change in Emilia-tan? Must be the wedding dress!"

"Though I did tear it up pretty badly."

Given that she'd torn the wedding dress apart with the excuse that it was hard to move in, perhaps there wasn't much need to worry on that front after all.

"What a bunch of children, I suppose. All you two do is look like you're trying to act grown up."

Just as Beatrice wrapped things up with a punchline, Subaru cleared his throat. Then, in search of an answer to the question implied by Mimi's recovery, he let his gaze wander through the shelter, and—

"――――"

In a corner where people rejoicing over reunions gathered, he spotted the figure of an old swordsman standing quietly.
Seeing the silent, eyes-closed figure of the sword demon, Subaru quietly caught his breath.

"Subaru..."

"Sorry. I'm gonna go over there a sec."

Answering Emilia's concerned look that way, Subaru left her and Beatrice behind and slowly made his way toward where his gaze had settled.
What to say first—but that worry turned out to be unnecessary.

"—Subaru-dono, is it."

"...Yes, that's right."

As Subaru approached, hesitating over his first words, Wilhelm caught him with one open eye. Seeing that still, blue gaze, Subaru realized the meaninglessness of staying silent.
Wilhelm had been leaning against the cold stone wall, blending into the scenery. Subaru fell in beside him, somehow, and glanced at his figure.

He looked wounded all over, still carrying the aftertaste of a fierce battle.
Traces of sword strikes marked the lightly armored body, his coat removed here and there; the white hair that had been tied back had come loose and spread across his back. Most painful of all was the blood-soaked strip of cloth wrapped around the base of his right leg—clearly a deep wound, one serious enough to be life-threatening.

But what caught Subaru's eye even more than that was not Wilhelm himself.
It was the coat, carefully placed right beside him, wrapped around something and rolled into a bundle.

"Wilhelm-san, that's..."

"――――"

Unthinkingly, Subaru tried to confirm the identity of whatever was wrapped in that coat. At Subaru's words, Wilhelm's gaze turned toward the bundle.
The old swordsman fell silent for a while, then moved his dry lips and said,

"...As you've guessed, it's my wife."

"――――"

"The moment her life ended, her remains turned to a heap of ash. Leaving them exposed to the wind like that felt far too pitiful, so—unsightly as it is—I wrapped them in my coat. ...At the very least, I wish to lay even just her ashes properly in a grave, and give her a proper burial."

A secret technique that moved the remains of the dead—and this, then, was the form its ending took: remains reduced to ash.
It was a desecration of the departed soul, and the shock it must deal to those connected to the person made the object of such a technique was beyond measure. Thinking of what must be in Wilhelm's heart, it was beyond imagining.

"I apologize. It's an unbecoming, meaningless attachment."

"That's not true at all!"

At the self-reproaching tone in his voice, Subaru raised his voice on reflex.
Aware of himself growing heated, Subaru looked at Wilhelm head-on. Wilhelm, too, opened his eyes slightly wider, looking back at Subaru.

"Back at the White Whale, and now too, I've never once thought you were wrong, Wilhelm-san. I respect you—you're an amazing person. What's wrong with treasuring someone precious to you? There's nothing shameful about it. Thinking that way is what's wrong."

"Subaru-dono..."

"Wilhelm-san, you're admirable. Your wife—wanting to properly lay her to rest, to give her a proper burial—that thought isn't wrong at all. I can't put it well, but... it's amazing."

That was his true feeling.
This was, without a doubt, Subaru's genuine, unshakable belief—one he didn't want denied.

At the White Whale, and now again in this sorrowful reunion, fate had been far too harsh to Wilhelm.
And yet the sword demon had fought desperately against fate, carrying through his own will, trying to see his love through to the end. The result of all that might not have been entirely rewarded.
Regret, remorse—those might never run dry. But it had to be right.

Wilhelm's love, loving the one he loved to the fullest—all of it had to be right.

"There's nothing unsightly about it. Please, lay her to rest properly in a grave. And if there's ever a chance, and I wouldn't be intruding, please let me visit her grave too."

"――――"

"That's the least I want to do—and I think she's someone who deserves it."

His words wouldn't come out smoothly, and on top of that, he was being so emotional, that Subaru felt frustrated with himself.
It was a selfish imposition of his own feelings; Wilhelm would have every right to laugh it off, or to rebuff the offer as none of his business.

But Wilhelm's lips loosened slightly at Subaru's words.
A small gap appeared in that tense, taut profile. And then—

"...Yes, please, Subaru-dono. I, too, would like you to say some words to my wife. From you."

"—! Y-yes. I mean, I'm honored."

Rather than permission being granted, it was likely more a matter of Wilhelm's magnanimity.
Accepting Subaru's unreasonable request, Wilhelm let out a faint breath. Sensing from his profile that he wanted no further conversation, Subaru bowed his head.
For a while, Wilhelm and his wife should be left alone together.

Still, before leaving that spot, there was one last thing Subaru wanted to confirm.
That was—

"Wilhelm-san. —With your wife, did you... properly...?"

"――――"

Had he settled things? Had it not ended in a result he hadn't wanted?

Of course, being made to face his wife as one of the dead was never a result anyone would have wished for in the first place. And yet, no one but Wilhelm could have been allowed to face her; having swallowed down such circumstances, Wilhelm had sought to bring things to a close.
If so, at the very least, to hope for that much—surely Subaru wasn't the only one.

"With my wife..."

Stopping his feet, Wilhelm opened his mouth toward Subaru, who was watching him. But the words broke off there for a moment. Faintly, Wilhelm's gaze drifted away from Subaru. It settled on the coat wrapping his wife's ashes.
For an instant, a vast swirl of emotion passed across those eyes, and then—

"—Yes. I exchanged words with my wife to my heart's content, and bid her a proper farewell."

"Words," was likely a figure of speech.
For Wilhelm, the former "Sword Saint's" husband, crossing blades with her had surely been the ultimate conversation—the blade that brought resolution had itself been the words of farewell.
So that resolution was surely the result of Wilhelm's own choice, and—

"I love my wife. —That much, I'm certain, was conveyed."

"I see."

Wilhelm's quiet declaration of love.
Contrary to how softly it was spoken, the intensity of feeling that scorched the listener's heart made Subaru's chest grow warm. Letting out a deep breath, Subaru closed his eyes.

A surging wave of emotion welled up. Pressing down each one, he opened his eyes.
Before him, Wilhelm's mouth looked lonely, and yet it had loosened into something like a smile—and feeling saved by that, Subaru let his own lips relax too.

"Wilhelm-san. Thank you for everything."

"――――"

"Things'll probably get busy again soon, I think, but until then, please rest. I'm gonna go ask around a bit more, hear what's going on."

Not sure if that last line had been the right thing to say, Subaru found himself speaking quickly. Scratching his cheek with a finger, he turned his back to Wilhelm out of self-consciousness.
To that retreating back—

"Subaru-dono—"

"Yes?"

Stopping the foot he'd begun to move away with, Subaru turned at being called. Wilhelm, looking a little surprised himself, quickly shook his head and said, "No, it's nothing."

"Forgive me. It's a trivial matter. Please, don't concern yourself."

"Is that so? Well, now that you say that, it makes me curious instead, but... well, all right then. See you later."

Smiling wryly at a reaction so unlike Wilhelm, Subaru left the spot.
Seeing Subaru returning, Emilia and Beatrice both wore expressions of what looked like relief. That alone told him how different his own expression must have been, between when he'd walked over there and now that he was coming back.

Even Subaru himself was aware of that much.

It wasn't as though the reunion with the dead had been something to be glad about, not at all.
And yet, at the very least, Wilhelm had brought things to a close by his own hand, and seemed at peace with the result. That fact alone felt like it had become a small salvation.


※※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※


The sword demon watched the retreating back of the black-haired boy, eyes narrowed, staring fixedly.

His lips pressed tightly shut, as though holding something back.
It was the collapse of a disguise that, until just moments ago, had concealed his true feelings through sheer force of will—a surge of passion so intense that, were he to let his guard down even slightly, he might bite through his own pressed lips.
That he had gone so far as to hide the depths of his heart entirely from that boy was surely because—

"Subaru-dono... if you—"

In a hoarse whisper, audible only within his own mouth, the sword demon called the boy's name, and—

"If you were, by chance, my—"

Having said that much, the sword demon closed his eyes, as if to sever his own weak-hearted thoughts.
What came after, left unspoken, would never be heard by anyone.

And that it would ever be spoken aloud from the sword demon's own mouth—that, too, would never happen.
That much, at least, the sword demon would never permit himself.
