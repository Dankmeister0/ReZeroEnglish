Arc 4 Chapter 31: "Maid, Maid, Maid"

—For Subaru, this was the second time returning to Roswaal Manor from the Sanctuary.

"Though the first time, I went through hell for it..."

Scratching his cheek, Subaru grumbled as much after dismounting from Patrasche's back in front of the gate.
After parting with Ryuzu, the refugees-with-Subaru party had made it safely back to Arlam Village. Given the track record from before and the trust built up with Patrasche, that part had never been in doubt.

"Village folks overjoyed, Otto sticking around the village for a bit — same as last time. Honestly, I wanted Otto to come with me as a shield, but..."

Insisting he tag along on the return to the mansion felt like something to hesitate over. More than anything, given the real possibility of danger, someone like Otto — who couldn't be counted on to react well in a sudden crisis — shouldn't be at his side. Even if Subaru couldn't beat Otto in a straight fistfight, that didn't make Otto some one-man army. Subaru had no interest in seeing Otto's insides on display in front of the Bowel Hunter.

"Just... don't let anything have happened, okay..."

Last time, Subaru had returned to the mansion six days after the Trial began. This time it was the third day — three days of leeway compared to before. From every angle he could reason it out, the attack on the mansion had almost certainly happened the same night Subaru was killed. The problem was—

"Three days left... meaning, get the full story out of Frederica, hightail it back to the Sanctuary, solve the Sanctuary's problem right then and there, and get back to the mansion — a forced march. Looking at raw time alone, it's not impossible, but..."

Turning a paper plan into reality came with some brutal constraints attached. The road between the Sanctuary and the mansion was roughly eight hours one way. A round trip alone would eat up nearly a full day. Factoring in the inevitable lost time along the way, the hours Subaru actually had free to use would be even more unforgiving.

"There were a few candidates for how to solve the problem, but... the best one, the wish-fulfillment route, might be a bit much to ask for."

Given the prophecy of Elsa's attack, the best outcome for Subaru would be driving off the assassin — and if possible, he wanted a total, crushing victory, one that meant he'd never have to fear her shadow again. What that required was fighting power exceeding Elsa's, and that meant either Roswaal or Garfiel had to be present. As things stood, the odds of bringing either of them back to the mansion in time weren't looking good.

"Guess it's the next-best option, then... huh."

Scratching furiously at his head, Subaru groaned in frustration as Patrasche nuzzled her nose against him. Subaru gave a wry smile at the earth dragon's face rubbing against his shoulder, stroking her rock-textured neck as he did.

"There's a payoff worth the risk, but I can't exactly line up odds worth the risk to match it. Guess it's gotta be the scatter-like-baby-spiders-and-run plan."

The same conclusion that had crossed his mind during the fight against the Witch Cult. Last time, he'd had a full hand of pieces to work with, each one opening up new possibilities. This time his hand was far too thin. Just sensing the coming attack ahead of time and dodging it would be a stroke of good fortune. Though even that came with its own problem.

"The mansion's crew. Rem, Petra, Frederica, and... no telling if Beako's on board with evacuating. Honestly, Rem I could carry piggyback and Petra I could just take by the hand, but talking the other two into it is gonna be a pain."

Of course, in the end, he was prepared to force them into the dragon carriage and haul them off bodily if it came to that. He didn't think he could win an arm-wrestling contest against either of them, but if he just pushed hard enough on the things he wasn't willing to give up, he had to believe it would work out somehow. No — he'd make it work.

"...Phew."

Exhaling softly, Subaru became aware of the weight of responsibility pressing down on his back. How many people's fates hung on his words, his actions, the resolve he carried — that was the question. He'd felt something like this the night before the decisive battle with the White Whale, too.

"No use standing here at the gate getting spooked forever. I don't even know what's happening inside yet. First, let's see everyone's faces safe and sound..."

"See them, and then?"

"Then I can worry about persuading them. Yeah. Since nobody'll know any better anyway, I can just claim it's Roswaal's orders or whatever and bluff my way through—"

"Whoa. Bad boy, Subaru is."

"Dirty and wild, that's the age for admiring that kind of not-quite-bad-boy image, ain't it— wait."

Still talking, Subaru turned at the sound of stifled giggling. There, past the gate in the mansion's front garden, stood a small maid — a familiar girl, Petra.

Subaru raised his eyebrows in surprise, and before him she tilted her head fetchingly, chestnut hair swaying, and said,

"Welcome back, Subaru-sama. You're back sooner than I expected."

"Ah, I'm back... Thanks for the welcome — I can see traces of Frederica's elite education showing through here and there."

Watching her pinch the hem of her skirt and bow made his cheeks loosen with relief despite himself, and Subaru, feeling the tension drain out of him, put his hand on the gate and went in. He guided the following Patrasche's head toward the earth dragon stables, then looked down at Petra walking alongside him.

"—?"

Under Subaru's steady gaze, she made a puzzled face, then hurriedly turned her back to him and smoothed her hair and clothes with her hands. Once she'd finished to her satisfaction, she gave a small, convinced "there," and turned back to face Subaru,

"Is something wrong, Subaru-sama?"

—and smiled, even more radiantly, even more charmingly than before.

Girlish cuteness combined with features that hinted at a dazzling, well-formed future, and in that smile there was a bewitching quality that could capture the heart of anyone her age. A perfect smile, calculated with full understanding of how she appeared to others. Faced with something like that, Subaru let out a small breath and—

"Aah, geez! Seriously, you're just too cute, you know that?!"

"Wa, wah?!"

Utterly oblivious to the true intent hidden behind that smile, Subaru let his feelings take over and wrapped his arms around the girl, hugging her close, ruffling her head with a hand that was both careless and doting all at once. Petra let out a confused yelp at the sudden action, but—

"Sitting there all spaced out without a clue how I was feeling. You, you little— Ahh, damn it all."

"Wh-what, what is it?! C'mon, hold on, Subaru... it's still too soon for—"

"Seriously, damn it."

"—Subaru?"

Her cheeks flushed, Petra had been squirming in his arms, but her expression shifted. Still fully enclosed in Subaru's embrace, she looked up at his lowered voice, and the mix of embarrassment and delight that had colored her face until then faded away.

"Does something hurt...?"

The girl's fingers, reaching out worriedly, touched Subaru's trembling cheek. He gently pressed his palm over her hand from above and shook his head. "No," he said.
He breathed in through his nose, held it a moment. Then he opened his eyes, which had been closed,

"I'm genuinely, truly relieved, from the bottom of my heart. That's all. —I'm home, Petra."


※※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※


—Having returned Patrasche to the stable, Subaru walked back to the mansion hand in hand with Petra, who'd wanted to hold hands. Fortunately, according to Petra, nothing significant had happened since Subaru and the others had left the mansion.

"Frederica-neesama's out checking the mountain barrier right now, so it might be a little while before she's back... sorry."

As Petra relayed the fact that the head maid was absent, Subaru recalled the mountain barrier — the spell that had once sealed away the Juggernaut. The Juggernaut in the mountains should have been wiped out entirely, but the barrier apparently remained active even now. Even without the Juggernaut, the barrier seemed to have the property of blocking things with hostile intent, like witchbeasts, and maintaining it was apparently also part of Arlam Village's — and by extension its overseer Roswaal's faction's — job.

"Once everyone from the village comes back, I think the job of patrolling for gaps in the barrier goes back to them, but since not everyone's returned yet, right now it's Frederica-neesama who—"

"Hearing you call her 'neesama' — didn't realize the two of you had gotten so close while I wasn't looking. Kind of makes me smile. Oh, and the village folks are all back, by the way."

"Really?!"

At Subaru's finger pointing toward the village, Petra's eyes lit up, her voice bubbling with excitement.
Her family had all been among the group evacuated to the royal capital, so her parents should have made it back to the village safely, but that didn't change the fact that she'd been separated from close neighbors and acquaintances all the same. Hearing they were safe, Petra clapped her hands happily.

"Go show your face around there later. Even just seeing you in that maid outfit'll make 'em happy."

"Okay. I'll ask Frederica-neesama's permission, then change and go!"

"Nah, you don't need to change... you're cute as is, so just show 'em like—"

"Ehehe, cute? Cute?"

"Cute, cute. So just show them—"

"Okay! I'll change and go!"

No matter how many times he tried "no," it kept getting drowned out like thunder cancelling him out.
Whatever line she wasn't willing to cross, Petra's answer wasn't budging an inch, and Subaru gave up on pressing the suggestion further.
He cracked his neck, then let out a deep breath and said "well then," coming to a stop.
The spot was the mansion's second floor — sliding his shoe soles across the carpet, Subaru lifted his face and fixed his eyes on a door. Petra, who'd been holding his hand, let her fingers slip free with a touch of sadness. A clever girl, good at reading the room.

"Sorry, Petra. Give the two of us a little time, would you?"

"Sure, I understand. I'll be in the west wing finishing up cleaning, so call if you need anything."

As though she'd known before Subaru even said it, Petra shed her girlish manner right there, putting on her maid's composure, gave a small bow, and left.
Even as he leaned on her thoughtfulness like that, Subaru lightly knocked himself upside the head — here he was, with pressing matters to attend to. He knocked himself, and even so—

"Talk about priorities, and... I end up here anyway."

Pushing the door open, Subaru slowly stepped into the room. A room where time didn't seem to move. On the plain bed set within it lay a single girl. Stripped of her familiar maid uniform, dressed in pale blue sleepwear. Eyes closed, not even the faintest sound of breathing audible. But quietly, her heartbeat continued — the small proof that her life still hung on.

"...Rem."

Who could ever grasp the whirlpool of emotion packed into that short word as Subaru spoke her name? A boundless torrent of feeling directed at the one and only person in the world for whom it was meant.
He'd resolved, in his heart, to steel himself and face any hardship without wavering. For that, he'd decided he wouldn't lean on anyone, that he'd keep his chin up and move forward.

—That resolve, that determination, scattered to nothing the instant he stood before her.

Subaru, who'd told Emilia to leave it to him, who'd taken her hand and said he'd figure something out, who'd carried himself with such forceful confidence — the surface layer of that resolve peeled away the moment he stood in front of her.

"Pathetic... I really am weak, aren't I."

The moment he stood before Rem, Subaru reverted to the weak Natsuki Subaru of before. Sent back to that moment when Rem's devotion had first affirmed him, when he'd first managed to stand on his own.

Slowly, he reached his hand toward her sleeping face, brushing aside the bangs falling across it with his fingers. There was no change in her sleeping expression; there was still no telling when she, who had been 'devoured,' would return. And yet, if he simply left her lying here like this, doing nothing, it was certain that even her vessel would eventually be lost.

"You probably don't mean to, but you're the reason my resolve holds firm."

Weak and fragile, the peeled-away surface of his heart — he could feel it being covered over again with new steel. The sight of the sleeping Rem, her steady heartbeat, the simple fact of her being there — it brought Natsuki Subaru back to that time, to the reborn feeling of that moment.

"Because you told me it was okay to be weak, because you told me to grow stronger together... I can do something about this. I can get back up, as many times as it takes."

However much pain, suffering, and hardship waited ahead, her all-encompassing love was what healed Subaru, and the desire to live up to it was what drove him forward.

"You, and Petra, and everyone else... I'll get every single one of you out safely."

He gently stroked her forehead, holding back the urge to keep touching her sleeping form longer. Then, in the room with the wind blowing through, he sat in the chair beside the bed and simply stayed by her side in silence.
Pouring the entirety of that time — time he could scarcely spare — into her alone was, for Subaru now, the fullest offering of his heart he could give her.

How long did that quiet time pass?
Suddenly, the sound of a knock pulled Subaru's absent gaze away from Rem and back to reality. Lifting his face, turning it toward the door, he called out, "Come in," and—

"Excuse me. —I'm relieved to see you've returned safely, Subaru-sama."

Quietly pushing the door open, a tall maidservant entered.
Her long golden hair swaying, her composed bearing fitting her like a second skin — Frederica.
She glanced at Subaru sitting beside the sleeping Rem, gave a small bow of her head, and said,

"There's a great deal I'd like to ask you... though I imagine the same is true for you, Subaru-sama. Let's move somewhere else. Even asleep, these aren't things she'd want to overhear, I'd think."

"Glad you're quick on the uptake. ...Any idea what I'm gonna want to ask about?"

"I believe so."

Taking in her measured reply, Subaru let out a small breath and rose to his feet. Touching Rem's sleeping face once more, one last time, he clenched his fist as if to cut off his lingering reluctance,

"Your violent, foul-mouthed little brother, and the gap-moe of looking like a loli on the outside but being an old hag on the inside. Then there's the Sanctuary as some kind of testing ground, and whatever Roswaal's got planned. I'm looking forward to finding out just how much you're willing to cough up, under a bit of pressure."


※※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※


"The fact that milord hasn't returned yet means the Trial still isn't over, I take it."

Having moved from Rem's bedroom to the parlor, the two of them settled in.
Setting a steaming cup of tea in front of Subaru and taking her seat across from him, Frederica opened with that remark. Stirring the tea he'd been handed with a spoon, Subaru nodded, "Yeah," and said,

"You really are quick on the uptake. —Though given how much you clearly know about the situation, I've got a few things to say about you sending me off with only that measly amount of information."

"I won't make excuses for it. It's simply a fact that I never told you everything about the Sanctuary, the Trial, or my wayward little brother."

Frederica's voice, stated so evenly, carried none of the guilt one might expect as a matter of fact. And yet it wasn't quite the same as being unashamed, either. It was hard to call it suppressed emotion, either — more like a deliberate mask of emotionlessness, designed to keep her true feelings unreadable.

In type, she was cut from the same cloth as Ram — though given how much longer he'd known Ram, reading Frederica was considerably harder.

"Like I said back in that room, there's a few things I want to ask... but should I even bother hoping I'll get answers to all of it?"

"...I doubt I can live up to that hope, I'm afraid. As long as the Sanctuary remains unliberated, the contract between myself and milord still stands. So long as I abide by that contract, the facts I'm able to share with you, Subaru-sama, remain limited."

"A contract, again... every single one of you."

Subaru pressed a hand to his forehead, gnashing his teeth in frustration.
He wanted to raise his voice and demand she just interpret the damn contract flexibly, but having sworn to Emilia that he'd honor promises himself, forcing that same standard onto someone else didn't sit right with his conscience.

"Can I at least ask some details about this contract?"

"No. It's a contract exchanged between myself and Roswaal-sama, and as long as it stands, the information I can disclose is limited. —That's all I can say on the matter."

"Didn't get a single new piece of information out of that. Damn it, that bastard's pulled some incomprehensible strings again. Seriously, at this point I can't help but think he's working against us this time around."

Clicking his tongue at a reality — and a key player — that refused to bend to his will, Subaru composed himself and brought the tea to his lips. As ever, it tasted of nothing but leaves, but after swallowing it down so many times over and over, he'd finally gotten to where he could tell the difference between expensive leaves and cheap ones. —According to his tongue, this was the expensive kind.

"No time to be dwelling on that. ...Frederica being from the Sanctuary, and Garfiel's older sister — that part's accurate, right? Or is that something you can't answer either?"

"That much is no issue. As for the details... to be precise, I should say I was raised there rather than being 'from' the Sanctuary. I was living in the Sanctuary for as long as I can remember, so calling it my origin is close enough to the truth, I suppose."

"Not from there... come to think of it, Ryuzu-san said something like that too. Something about Roswaal bringing in half-elves — half-bloods — from outside and settling them there."

Those were Ryuzu's words, spoken along the road back in the dragon carriage.
At the time, Ryuzu had refused to elaborate, so Subaru hadn't been able to press further into her meaning.

"Half-bloods can't pass through the barrier, so bringing outside half-bloods there means locking them in. Why would anyone do that... And besides, for people supposedly being confined there, the folks in that place—"

Everyone there had seemed to live peacefully, without any particular discontent, as far as he could tell.
At the very least, that life had seemed entirely free of any sense of suffocation at being forced into that place against their will from outside, or of anger over it.
Which meant even the people brought in from elsewhere had, presumably, come to accept life in the Sanctuary. —What could possibly be the point of that?

"Subaru-sama, are you familiar with the Demi-Human War?"

"...The Demi-Human War. I feel like I've heard the term somewhere, at least."

Digging back through his memory to its very earliest reaches, he thought he recalled hearing that phrase two or three times. More than anything, the term itself carried an impact that told Subaru plainly enough what must have happened, even without details.
Hearing Subaru's uncertain answer, Frederica ran a finger gently through her long golden hair, pressing a hand lightly over her mouth where sharp fangs peeked through, and said,

"To unravel the Sanctuary's reason for existing, and Roswaal-sama's intentions behind it, I'll first need to tell you a little about the Demi-Human War."

With that, she rose and moved toward the back of the parlor. As Subaru's eyes followed her, she retrieved a box that had been sitting on a table in the back and said,

"No need for such wariness — it's merely a little something to go with the tea."

Her lips curving into a small smile, she returned and held the box out to Subaru.
A sweet, a rare treat one could only occasionally enjoy at Roswaal Manor, unique to this world.
Subaru glanced back and forth between the offered treat and Frederica's face. To him, she said,

"This is likely to be a long and tedious story. Please, do make yourself comfortable."
