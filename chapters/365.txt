Arc 5 Chapter 49: "The Battle for Greed Begins"

"――Subaru!!"

Kicking down the entrance of the cathedral, a voice calls Subaru's name from within.
It's Emilia, standing before the altar, her body wrapped in a white wedding dress.

Her long silver hair done up, her form clad in the pure white gown—she is beautiful. Beautiful enough to blind you, practically. If it were at all possible, he would have wanted to be the very first to see her in that bridal gown with his own eyes, but—

"I'll save the 'Emilia-tan's Marvelous Transformation' commentary for later—looks like I managed to crash the wedding partway through, huh?"

"And from the looks of it, the ceremony wasn't proceeding all that harmoniously to begin with. It seems we may be spared the treatment of unwelcome intruders."

Keeping his distance, watching Emilia and Regulus glaring at each other, Reinhard agrees with Subaru's muttering. The ceremony was apparently on the verge of falling apart—perfect timing to butt in.
Hearing the two of them speak, Regulus's expression, already sour, flushes red with fury. He opens the front of his white tuxedo and twists his lips in irritation.

"Sorry to you uninvited guests, but the celebration's about to turn into a memorial. No need to prepare your hearts to switch from wedding guests to mourners, though... ah, right, you won't need that preparation at all. Since soon enough, you'll be going from the ones sending off the dead to the ones being sent off yourselves."

"Whoa there, big talk. Before the bride even gets to slap you with a 'Narita divorce' at the airport, you got your vows rejected right at the altar. You oughta be blushing harder and looking more embarrassed than that. Oh, and by the way—did you not hear who's standing next to me?"

Regulus, lacing his low voice with murderous intent, glares at Subaru, who shoots Reinhard a provocative glance. Regulus eyes that look with suspicion and lets out an "Ah" of a sigh.

"What was it again, Sword Saint? I've heard the term. Wasn't it the title for some guy with no talent besides swinging a sword? And what, you brought him along thinking that'd help? Ugh, gross—thinking you've won just because of some title is honestly hilarious. 'The weight of accumulated history,' 'the pride of bloodline'—that kind of stuff is just the ugly custom of stale traditionalism. Things like that are supposed to get swept away by a new wind and lose pathetically. Is that what you want to demonstrate?"

"'No talent besides swinging a sword'—that's rather well put. In fact, that's the reason for most of what everyone expects of me. There is, however, one problem."

Reinhard responds to Regulus's insolent attitude without taking offense. As he speaks, he quietly reaches a hand to his own waist.
Fastened there is the holy sword, marked with dragon claw-scars, that Reinhard never lets out of his grasp. Reinhard grips its hilt—and shakes his head.

"What's wrong, Reinhard?"

"This is Dragon Sword Reid, passed down from the first head of House Astrea. It has just one flaw: it won't come out of its sheath unless the enemy is worthy of drawing it against."

"Meaning?"

"It seems the sword's conclusion is that the man over there isn't even worth drawing for."

"――!!"

Whether or not Reinhard intended it as such, to Regulus this was a bitterly cutting, humiliating remark. Subaru knows from experience—the fact that the sword hadn't come out even against Elsa—that Reinhard's words aren't a lie.
Even knowing that, though, it likely wouldn't have changed the humiliation Regulus felt.

"What can a Sword Saint do against me without even drawing his sword? Don't flatter yourself, small fry. You and I exist on entirely different planes. There's no comparison between you lot, pathetically flailing about and using your incompleteness as an excuse, and me—a complete, self-contained individual. Pathetic creatures who can only confirm their own worth by comparing themselves to others. Don't you dare presume to evaluate me."

"How should I put this—you're kind of a piece of work, you know that?"

Subaru says, half-exasperated, to Regulus, whose eyes burn with hatred.
Setting aside, for the moment, every one of the man's threats, and just listening to what he's actually saying—

"That's a hell of a boomerang. You're going on and on about being 'complete,' but you're the one who can't stop measuring yourself against other people to feel superior."

"――! A lacking, deficient wretch like you has no business lecturing someone as fulfilled as me!"

Enraged by Subaru's words, Regulus finally puts his menace into action.
The instant the madman kicks the floor before the altar, the wooden planking peels up with tremendous force. A torrent of destruction surges straight toward Subaru and the others, and the splinters of wood and stone caught up in the whirlwind are visibly ground down to sand by its overwhelming power.

"――Whoa!?"
"Subaru, this way."

An instant before the destruction reached him, he's grabbed by the scruff of the neck and yanked sideways in one motion.
A light leap cuts through the wind, and Subaru is pulled clear of the path of destruction almost as soon as it begins. It's Reinhard who did it—scooping Subaru up with one arm and clearing the attack in a single bound.
Setting the dizzied Subaru down gently on the floor, Reinhard simultaneously bends his knees to charge at Regulus. However—

"Don't move! Try anything funny, and the women die."

"――――"

Glaring at the crouched Reinhard, Regulus turns both hands toward the cathedral walls.
There, lined up as wedding guests, stand rows of finely dressed women. Every one of them wears an expression drained of emotion, and none reacts in the slightest to Regulus's move. As if they already knew this was coming, they simply stand in a row, taking in the conflict before them with blank minds.

"Bit late to ask, but—who are those women, exactly? Not that I really want to know."

"Every one of them is my beloved wife. Lovely princesses who love me, and answer my love in kind. Are you saying you'd let these innocent women die? How monstrous, wanting to do something so cruel!"

"Yikes. I had a vague suspicion, but yeah—there's no having a conversation with this guy."

However much of it is genuine conviction is impossible to say—Regulus's logic is utterly incoherent.
The idea that he'd take his own beloved wives hostage makes no sense to begin with. Worst of all, perhaps, is that there's little room to doubt either the "innocent" part or the part where "Regulus will kill them."
And yet the incoherent hostage scheme works perfectly well on Subaru and the others regardless.

"I don't want to let them die. And yet, if you two keep resisting like this, I'll have no choice but to kill them—one by one, starting from the end of the line. Forcing me to do something so cruel is just too much to ask."

"Pretty sure that's pointless, since I don't recall threatening you even once."

"Don't make excuses! Yes, I'm the one who'll be killing them directly. But you two are the ones with your fingers on the trigger. Your murderous intent is what kills them. That makes it murder committed by you, using me as the tool. You are the ones killing them. Don't try to run from that responsibility. You wife-killers..."

Grinding his teeth, Regulus glares at Subaru and the others with eyes full of hatred. The madman, spouting his horrifying logic, doesn't feel even a flicker of wrongness in his own words.
Trying to stall for time through conversation, Subaru shoots a glance at Reinhard. But the trigger for the man's explosive violence is unknown, and there are some fifty hostages lined along the walls. Even Reinhard, skilled as he is, can't save both sides if attacked simultaneously left and right.

"――――"

At this rate, the situation would settle into a stalemate—no, it would move exactly in the direction Regulus wanted.
That's when it happened.

"You won't forget about me, will you?"

"What?"

Beside Regulus, who was keeping them in check, a pale blue light burst forth.
The light permeates the cathedral in an instant, and in the next moment interferes with the world with a high, clear sound. The light, ringing notes chain together, resonate, and the natural music being played fills the cathedral.
Then, in the space of a blink, a great ice barrier forms within the cathedral.

The glittering blue ice barrier spreads through the cathedral centered on Emilia before the altar, creating a thick wall to protect the women among the wedding guests being held hostage by Regulus.
Not only that—the ice freezes Regulus's lower body and pins it to the floor, and at his now wide-open throat, an ice sword—the blade in Emilia's hand—is leveled.

"You let your guard down. I wasn't about to try fighting you without thinking things through at all, you know. I had it all ready to freeze you properly. You lose."

"...Hey, do you not know how to read the room? This was the moment where I was cornering those guys. The important scene where I take a resolute stand against those two cowards and prove I was right all along. And all my wives were believing in and hoping for my victory, and... what exactly do you think you're doing here?"

"Right now, let me and everyone else go. I won't say it's true of all of them, but I'm sure there are people following you out of fear. Cherish the ones who genuinely want to stay by your side, and then..."

"――Honestly, who does she think she's talking to. Good thing I didn't make her a wife."

"Huh?"

"Emilia! No! That won't stop him!"

Under normal circumstances, this would have been a decisive, conclusive moment—it's no wonder Emilia judged it as such.
If there was a difference in the conditions here, it came down to exactly one point: the opponent was not normal.

"――!"

With a sigh, Regulus gives his body a slight twist and moves his leg. That alone is enough for the ice that had frozen him solidly from the waist down to be peeled away, effortlessly.
With the casualness of peeling away half-melted ice, the frozen restraints are shattered to pieces. The instant Emilia gasps at the sight, Regulus's palm closes around her slender throat. And with that, Regulus lifts Emilia's body with a single hand.

"Violent, and on top of that, doesn't know how to let a man take the lead. Even if your mind and body are both virgin, if you're an emotional cheater, none of that means a thing. Whore. A latent whore. Toying with my pure heart, and then trying to threaten me on top of it—unbelievable. I've never seen a woman this wicked."

"K-Kh... uh..."

"That cute face of yours—I wonder how many men you've led astray with it. Everyone was kind to you whenever you smiled, weren't they? A man got over the moon the moment you spoke a word to him. If you so much as touched their hand, they'd have showered you with gifts without limit. Ah, ah, what a filthy woman."

"Stop it! Let go of her, you bastard!"

Gripping and hoisting Emilia up, Regulus reproaches her flatly, hatred dripping from every word. Unable to bear hearing any more of it, Subaru shouts, and those empty, inorganic eyes pierce straight through him.

"You're the idiot here—can't you see the situation? Or is this a case of giving up on the effort to understand? Do I really have to spell it out for you that much? Isn't that just a form of giving up on thinking altogether—clinging to the other person's mercy while conveniently ignoring your own lack of situational awareness? Shouldn't you be thinking things through with everything you've got, putting yourself in the other person's shoes, before doing something like that? Relying on someone else's goodwill instead—as a human being, isn't that just a little messed up, hm?"

"Let go of Emilia-sama. I'll hear your demand."

Beside Subaru, who has gone breathless, Reinhard speaks to Regulus.
The madman raises his eyebrows at the words, apparently deciding Reinhard—rather than the blood-flushed Subaru—is more capable of holding a conversation, and twists his cheek into a smile.

"Yes, yes, that's the humble attitude I like. To bring things around to what both parties want, people do have this tool called conversation, and I'd really like to see it put to good use. It's a real shame how many people misunderstand that and just try to force things through with power instead. It's something you could just explain with words, and yet they insist on flaunting their own strength every single time. Though, funnily enough, it's usually those very types who turn out to be nothing special at all, losing to a pacifist like me—how pathetic."

"No lengthy speeches are necessary. Rather, I'd like to hear your demand. It pains both me and my friend here to see Emilia-sama suffer any further."

"Is that so. Then let me tell you plainly. ――Take off the sword at your waist, and come stand a little in front of the altar."

Emilia's face turns pale, and Regulus lifts her even higher, as if showing her off. Her legs kick, and the ice sword in her hand falls and sticks into the floor.
Seeing this, Reinhard unhesitatingly unfastens the dragon sword from his waist and hands it to Subaru.

"...If it comes down to it, I'll draw this myself and cut him down."

"That's one option, but I'm afraid even you won't be able to draw it. Don't worry. I will bring Emilia-sama back, without fail."

Exchanging words in a low voice, Reinhard follows Regulus's instructions.
As the unarmed Sword Saint stands at the center of the cathedral, Regulus calls out "That's far enough" and has him stop. The distance is about five meters—but for Reinhard, a distance he could close in the blink of an eye.
The problem is the high likelihood that the moment he closes that distance, Regulus—Emilia still in his grip—would snap her neck. And beyond that, the true nature of Regulus's invincible Authority remains completely unknown.

The unbinding of Emilia's restraints, the repeated, inexplicable destruction—somewhere in that, surely, lies the key to unraveling the concrete nature of that "invincibility" of his.

"――――"

Holding his breath, Subaru fixes his eyes on Reinhard's movements.
As things stand, with no breakthrough in sight, Reinhard's judgment is all they have to rely on. Of course, Subaru is scrambling for something on his end too, but at this stage its effect is minimal.

"As you wished—here, then? What now?"

"I could just have you killed right here—that would be simple enough. But even I think that would be a bit much. It wouldn't show sincerity toward the resolve you showed, stepping forward for my wife and for that whore. I don't want to force anything unreasonable on anyone. I don't want to be misunderstood as some selfish man. I want you to properly understand that I'm just an ordinary, small-minded sort of person, satisfied by life's little everyday happinesses."

"――――"

"So there's only one condition for me to release the hostages. You'll stand right there and take one hit from me. Unguarded—no dodging allowed. That alone, and I'll free the hostages. I'll forgive you two for the cowardice of trying to gang up on me two against one."

"One blow, is it."

At Regulus's proposal, Reinhard touches his chin as if in thought.
Watching that thoughtful back, Subaru shakes his head inwardly—what an outrageous proposal. Regulus is putting on airs, but they've all seen the power of his attacks firsthand.
There's no way even Reinhard could withstand a blow so incomprehensible it grinds building materials entirely to dust. And even if he somehow survived it, if he were left unable to fight, continuing the battle would be unthinkable.

"Understood. I'll take it."

And yet, contrary to Subaru's mounting dread, Reinhard accepts it without hesitation. Subaru's eyes go wide at the answer, and Regulus nods deeply.

"Good resolve. I respect you for it. Even as someone who tried to kill my wives, it seems you at least have the minimal pride of a human being."

"An invincible ability-user who's also taken hostages, and you don't have a single doubt about yourself..."

Regulus's words, superficially noble-sounding, make Subaru's skin crawl. His mutter doesn't seem to reach Regulus, who—still holding Emilia in his left arm—points the fingertip of his right hand at Reinhard.

"R-Reinhard... you've got something in mind, right?"

"Subaru, remember our promise. You'll make up for what I lack—won't you?"

"Don't say something so ominous..."

Subaru, hoping there's some winning strategy behind Reinhard's acceptance, gets only an ambiguous answer in return. Before Reinhard can finish answering, Regulus swings his arm at him.
It's invisible. Fingertips slice the air, and something—presumably—is loosed toward Reinhard. But the destruction itself cannot be seen. Perhaps something like an "unseen hand," some unknowable attack, is bearing down on Reinhard.
There's no time to confirm whether that guess is real.

"――――"

The body of Reinhard, who had been standing in front of Subaru, collapses, scattering a spray of blood.
His body, severed as if by a diagonal slash, tumbles and rolls from the impact—and in that tumbling form, not even a trace remains of his usual, keen bearing.

"Wha――?"

From Reinhard's fallen body, a great flow of blood spreads, staining the already-torn red carpet an even darker black. The way the body jerks and trembles is the convulsive shock response a dying body shows in the instant before it loses its life.
And when even that finally settles, the body lying there is completely dead.
It is, beyond any doubt, the death of Reinhard van Astrea.

"Whoever you are, death, when it comes, is such an anticlimactic thing, isn't it. However great the achievements a person leaves behind, however hideous the sins a villain stains their hands with, death arrives for everyone equally and takes their life away. In a world overrun with inequality, that much, at least, is an equal event."

Having killed Reinhard with a single stroke of his own and watched him die, Regulus shakes his head.
The madman, wearing a solemn expression, comments on the result of his own act as though it were someone else's affair entirely.

"Precisely because we know an end will come eventually, those of us who are alive have to pursue happiness while we still can. That's why I feel such deep fulfillment in the fact that the bar for my own happiness is set so low. If, say, I had been 'Greed'—insatiably wanting absolutely everything, unable to be satisfied unless I obtained it all—then perhaps I never could have found happiness while I lived. But fortunately, I was born blessed with a sensibility that's satisfied by the smallest of happinesses."

Pressing the arm that killed Reinhard to his chest, Regulus smiles.
And then—

"I, who am fulfilled, would like to ask: did you, who died, die satisfied? If you did, congratulations. If you didn't—my condolences."

"Ugh, aaaaaah――!!"

The instant Regulus finishes uttering his nonsense, Subaru is already moving, a roar tearing from his throat.
He hoists up a chair from within the cathedral and hurls it at Regulus. Regulus swats the incoming projectile aside roughly with one hand, as if brushing off an insect. The chair shatters to pieces from the light impact and scatters, and Regulus's face twists with displeasure.

"Compared to that one, who died with some dignity, you're awfully loud and unsightly."

"I happen to take pride in being a knight who's not very knight-like!"

Treading over the carpet stained with Reinhard's blood, Subaru reaches a hand behind his waist. He draws the whip from there and sends its tip flying forward with a burst of momentum.
At that movement from Subaru, Regulus raises the Emilia he's holding, as if to flaunt her,

"Are your eyes just for show? Can't you see I have a hostage?"

"――Now that's strange. According to what you said, the hostages were supposed to go free."

"――!?"

The moment that voice rings out, Regulus's expression wavers with shock.
The gaze that had been chasing Subaru as he ran across the cathedral snaps back, and the instant it lands on the tall figure standing in the pool of blood, his throat freezes with the shock of it.

"Wha―"

"――'Divine Protection of the Phoenix.'"

A short phrase answers Regulus's astonishment, and in that instant, three shadows move in a dizzying blur.

Subaru's whip catches the blonde-haired woman crouched behind the altar and yanks her close.
Emilia, still gripped in Regulus's hand, kicks out with her extended leg, sending the ice sword flying toward Reinhard.
Leaping forward, Reinhard catches the ice sword and swings it up toward Regulus.

The woman who would have been caught in the line of fire is pulled clear, and the Sword Saint, blade of ice now in hand, doesn't hesitate for an instant.

"――――"

In that instant, sound vanishes from the world—and a blue light blows the cathedral apart with a shattering impact.
