Re:Zero EX: "Iris and the King of Thorns (Part 2)"


—An ancient tale tells.

It is the story of a lonely king and a single girl.
A story of a king who was alone, who met a girl, and was alone no longer.

Of the lonely king’s struggles, and the beginning of the imperial contest that would decide his fate.
Of the girl’s resolve, wishing to remain by the king’s side, making a firm decision in her own heart.

Yet, the wishes and feelings held by the lonely king and the single girl began to cloud over unending bloodshed, the leaking away of lives, and the piling ends of so many.

The story begins to transform.
Starting with a tragedy that befell a single girl, alongside the lonely king’s fight.


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△


—Before she knew it, the Imperial Selection Ceremony had begun.

That was Iris’s honest impression, and precisely why she felt a deep sense of self-reproach, believing she was entirely inadequate.
She had prepared herself. But she was forced to realize that her preparation had been nothing more than an illusion.

To be honest, Iris had thought the start of the Imperial Selection Ceremony was still far off—that the real struggle for Eugard to become Emperor lay years in the future.
However, it seemed Iris was the only one holding such a naive thought.

"The former Emperor, Radcain Vollachia, has passed away, and the Imperial Selection Ceremony shall commence. —As the Nineteenth Prince, I desire to ascend the throne. I expect great efforts from you all."

Eugard’s declaration, addressed to the entire Elcanti territory, was broadcast across the realm through the effect of a magic tool that Whitaker had managed to drag out from somewhere.
Unintentionally, that declaration served as the trigger for the Imperial Selection Ceremony to begin in earnest. —No, perhaps calling it "unintentional" was something only Iris thought, as well.

In truth, the strategy and policies of the Elcanti faction led by Eugard were not shared in detail with Iris.
It wasn't because Iris, ignorant of military affairs, was looked down upon as nothing more than a rustic village girl—


"...I'm so useless."

Lying limply with a heavy body on her bed, Iris cursed herself.
Her self-cursed body burned with a high fever, keeping her bedridden ever since around the time the Imperial Selection Ceremony began.

Right now, Eugard was leading the army of his territory on an expedition to battle the other princes.
Originally, Iris was supposed to accompany him on the expedition to attend to his personal needs. But contrary to her enthusiasm, her feverish body refused to obey, leaving her behind at the manor to chew on her frustration.
And as if being utterly useless wasn't agonizing enough,

"I've even tied Sensei down at the manor like this..."

"Good grief, a sick person shouldn't worry about such things, *moi*. Besides, I was never going to be much help on the battlefield anyway, *moi*. It won't affect our military strength, *moi*."

"But Sensei, you were planning to make a big contribution with your short claws and big nose, weren't you?"

"I'm only useful for digging holes and scouting at best, *moi*! You should know that, *moi*!"

Shaking his large nose back and forth, Lynek raised his voice as he nursed Iris. Seeing Lynek's reaction, Iris pulled up the blanket and muttered, "I'm sorry."
At Iris's weak voice, Lynek shook his head and said, "A serious illness, *moi*."

As one could see, because Iris came down with a fever, even Lynek was held back at the manor.
Though Lynek claimed it didn't affect the overall situation, she had heard that the Punitive Squad led by Volcas was the core of the Elcanti military. Thinking that Lynek, as a member of that unit, surely had an irreplaceable role, Iris felt her presence was nothing short of aiding the enemy.

"You're overthinking it, *moi*. Don't worry, there are plenty of people who can replace me, *moi*."

"You say there are replacements, but..."

"It's just playing healer on the battlefield, *moi*. Even without me, as long as my teachings and knowledge live on, the squad members should be able to manage, *moi*."

As he spoke, beside Iris's bed, Lynek put several herbs into a mortar and ground them up, formulating a medicinal broth for her.
His practiced movements appeared even more proper than the genuine healer who had examined Iris long ago when she suffered from a severe illness. So proper that it was hard to believe there were plenty of people who could replace him.

"Now, gulp it all down, *moi*. I should warn you, it tastes horrific, *moi*."

"Oh come on, calling medicine made by Sensei bad—*Gag!*"

"I told you so, *moi*."

At the shocking taste that made her feel as if she had been poisoned, she involuntarily screamed. However, as the superstition goes that foul-tasting things neutralize the bad things inside one's body, once she endured it and gulped it down, it felt as though it was working on her body.

"It's just your imagination, *moi*. You'll be in pain again soon enough, *moi*."

"Sensei, please be a bit gentler..."

"I cannot offer hopeful optimism, *moi*. However, beyond piling up this bitterness and suffering, complete recovery awaits, *moi*. In all things, step by step, steadily, *moi*."

"In all things, steadily..."

Washing down the lingering bitterness in her mouth with water as best she could, Iris cast her eyes downward.
What those words brought to mind was not her own physical condition, but Eugard.

"I wonder how His Excellency and the others are doing right now."

"Reports from the front lines are arriving properly, *moi*. Apparently, Volcas is working eagerly and performing brilliantly, *moi*. For your sake, Iris-dono, *moi*."

"...With that attitude about debts and favors, he really is dutiful, isn't he?"

At Lynek's words, Iris remembered Volcas coming by before setting out.
He had mercilessly mocked and ridiculed Iris after she collapsed with a fever, yet he departed promising that the results wouldn't change even without her. She didn't doubt for a second that his blunt attitude was his own way of showing concern for her.
And indeed, actually backing up his words with actions was quite impressive.

"In fact, since he actually does what he says, he's far more admirable than me—"

"Iris-dono!? Are you crying, *moi*!?"

"S-Sorry. My unshakable patheticness just made me tear up. Please continue."

Wiping the welling tears with her sleeve, Iris prompted Lynek to go on. While looking worriedly at her condition, Lynek cleared his throat with an "Ahem,"

"His Excellency and the others are smoothly carrying out their marching plans, *moi*. Originally, the princes with rights to succession divide into two factions in the Imperial Selection Ceremony, *moi*. Princes who fight aiming for the throne, and princes who concede defeat in exchange for the safety of their territory, *moi*."

"...Even if they concede defeat, it's no good, right? Um..."

"—The prince's life is forfeited, *moi*. In exchange, promises can be struck so that no unnecessary sacrifices fall upon the subjects, nor unneeded pressure upon their birthplace, *moi*. Inevitably, houses where the prince reached the start of the Imperial Selection Ceremony while still a child have no real choice, *moi*."

"――――"

"They say they are dealing with the latter princes according to prior arrangements, while engaging in conflict with the former princes and continuing to claim victory over them, *moi*."

Reports of Eugard's victories were not something Iris could rejoice over unconditionally.
For Eugard to triumph meant that one of his brothers or sisters, sharing his very blood, was losing their life, and that he was walking step by step down a bloodstained road.

It wasn't that she loathed him putting on a blood-soaked crown.
Iris had already accepted that Eugard had chosen to walk the path of supreme rule because of her, and she had resolved herself to face that fact properly.
The reason she couldn't rejoice unconditionally was because she was making Eugard walk it alone.

As the "King of Thorns" who started it, and the woman who caused him to start it, she had intended to be soaked in blood together with him.
And yet—

"...If it's making you feel down, I really should set up an opportunity for you to speak with His Excellency, *moi*. Willpower is also required to fight illness, *moi*."

"Sensei, how many times do you think I've suffered from high fevers before? Even without being told, I know how to fight illness."

"That's not funny, *moi*."

Sticking her tongue out slightly, she tried to play it off, but it fell flat with Lynek. Thinking that she probably hadn't managed to make a convincing face at all, she felt annoyed at her poor acting.
Even so, Iris had been refusing Lynek's suggestion—to borrow a "Conversational Mirror" used to keep in touch with the front lines and speak with Eugard in a distant land.
The reason for that was very simple and selfish.

"I don't want to end up talking to His Excellency through a mirror too..."

As the "King of Thorns," kept at a distance by everyone—no, choosing to keep his distance himself so as not to hurt those around him—Eugard had become able to speak with people through "Conversational Mirrors" with Whitaker's help, but Iris could not welcome it wholeheartedly.

It wasn't jealousy that people other than herself could talk to Eugard.
She wouldn't say such feelings were entirely nonexistent, but the reason Iris disliked the mirror was that it felt like a symbol that isolated Eugard even more clearly.
It was convenient. Yet if interacting with Eugard like that became the norm in the future, no one would ever endure the pain to stand before him.

"At least I want to..."

She knew she shouldn't say such things when she was in a position where she didn't know the pain of Eugard's thorny fetters, but Iris wanted to remain the one human who could touch Eugard directly.
For that reason, Iris had refused to speak with Eugard through the Conversational Mirror.

"...That is a matter of your feelings and His Excellency's, *moi*. I cannot say anything pretentious, *moi*."

"I'm sorry, Sensei. But His Excellency felt the same way, too."

Before departing, when Eugard came to say goodbye to Iris in her bed, he had been the one to say that he didn't want to communicate via Conversational Mirrors from his end either.
Iris had felt happy that they shared the same reason and the same feelings.
That was why she wanted to cure her illness even a day sooner and go to Eugard's side on the battlefield—

"Hm, who's that, *moi*?"

Suddenly, there came a knock at the bedroom door, and Lynek stood up, his nose twitching.
Heading straight for the entrance, Lynek vanished from the lying Iris's field of view. But immediately, Lynek's surprised voice rang out: "*Moi*!?"

"Hello, hello. Sorry to intrude when you're feeling so poorly, Iris-sama."

"Huh... Whitaker-san?"
