Arc 5 Chapter 24: "The Conclusion of Ice and Flame"

Crimson flames roared to life on both his arms as Sirius raised his voice in fury, his expression twisted into something demonic.

That impression came through even across the bandages, born of eyes overtaken by a passion beyond all reason.

His pale pink eyes—so clear they might normally draw you in—now burned with a heat like the flames scorching his own arms, glaring down at Subaru and the others in pure hatred.

No—that's not quite right. Because—

"It reeks. It reeks, reeks, reeks, reeks, reeks, reeks, reeks, reeks—you whore...!"

In Sirius's eyes, scattering curses as he was, Subaru's existence didn't register in the slightest.

What Sirius was single-mindedly trying to strike dead with his glare were none other than the two standing in front of Subaru—Emilia and Beatrice.

"What is with him? He's totally different from before..."

Subaru couldn't hide his agitation at the sight of Sirius's fury.

In a short span of time, Subaru had faced off with a—well, whether "normal" was the right word was questionable—Sirius three times now. In those encounters, Sirius, while hardly what you'd call sane, had never been a being who'd lost his reason entirely to be ruled by raw passion.

He was supposed to be, through and through, a rational lunatic who forced his deranged personal theories on others.

But look at the Sirius standing before him now.

Clearly having lost all composure, dominated entirely by rage—wasn't this exactly the form befitting one who called himself the bishop in charge of "Wrath"?

"Like maggots and flies, burn you and burn you and you just keep swarming back up... What grudge do you even have, huh!? Are you saying I don't even have the freedom to grieve, to mourn in peace!?"

"...I don't understand what you're talking about."

"Hah!?"

Emilia answered without flinching, even as Sirius spat and hurled accusations that sounded like nothing but baseless spite. His response was just as fierce, but Emilia didn't back down.

She pointed the tip of the ice sword in her hand toward the crowd lined up behind Sirius.

"If you're angry at me, I'll hear you out. We're the ones who attacked first, so of course you'd be angry. But the people around you have nothing to do with this. Let them go."

"Don't talk down to me like that! If you want me to give something up, show it in your attitude!! Of course I'd be angry? Then apologize! Apologize, beg forgiveness, crawl on the ground, wail and weep and plead for mercy, and then I'll shove fire up your ass and roast your insides to a crisp!"

"Having my stomach burned would be a problem. —So, I'll make this easier to talk about."

Sirius, still raging, tilted his head at the way Emilia's voice dropped low.

An instant later, Emilia leaned her upper body slightly forward and launched her slender frame ahead. Her white arms swung the long ice sword as lightly as if it weighed nothing at all.

The sharp tip, gleaming as it caught the light, was loosed straight for Sirius's shoulder.

"Emilia-tan!?"

"—Tch!"

Subaru's shout of alarm overlapped with Sirius's click of the tongue.

Faced with the swung longsword, Sirius reflexively raised his left arm, trying to counter Emilia's ice blade with his blazing flame. However—

"Damn half-demon!"

"Don't say that so many times. It'll make me feel dirty."

Bathed in flame hot enough to vaporize spit in midair, Emilia's ice sword still didn't melt.

Its pale blue tip, undaunted by the flame, struck true against Sirius's flame-wrapped left arm—except there, chains were wound tightly around it.

A high, grinding shriek rang out as the ice sword and the flame-wreathed arm collided, scattering sparks of mana light. After only the briefest moment of resistance, Emilia's ice sword shattered with a crack.

"S-serves you—!"

Seeing the ice sword reduced to nothing but a hilt, Sirius, face triumphant, swung his arm down at Emilia. The heat-laden chains wrapped around his arm were a vicious thing, capable of searing and tearing apart the wound of anyone they struck.

Emilia's beautiful face was a hair's breadth from being scarred—

"Whoop!"

An exclamation ill-suited to the moment rang out, and this time Sirius's arm was knocked upward from below.

What accomplished that was Emilia's ice sword—or rather, what had been her ice sword.

"Ahh, aaah! Aaaaah! You're so annoying!"

Sirius screamed and crossed his burning arms overhead.

Slammed straight into the center of those crossed arms was the blow Emilia unleashed.

What extended from the sword's hilt, where the blade had been, was now an ice hammer, reshaped from the sword. Under the weight of the two-handed downward blow, Sirius gnashed his teeth and staggered back, Emilia pressing after him.

"Hyah! Tei! Yah! There! Uryah! Uryah, uryah!"

"Damn you! Half-demon! Filthy maggot! Vermin! Infuriating, maddening—!"

Using centrifugal force and exquisite footwork, Emilia displayed close-combat prowess beyond anything one might expect.

Against the blows of the swinging ice hammer, Sirius was reduced to scattering flame while purely on the defensive. Seeing Emilia completely dominating, Subaru judged, from a third party's perspective, that she could win like this. He judged so, but—

"Wait, this isn't the time to be spacing out! Emilia, don't!"

"Subaru, you mustn't draw his attention right now, I suppose!"

If Emilia struck Sirius down, at that very moment, "death" would be shared with everyone around.

It was that sense of danger that made Subaru cry out, but Beatrice rebuked his snap judgment. Wondering what was wrong, Subaru looked at Beatrice and noticed the danger in the direction of her gaze.

"—Damn insect."

"Uh-oh."

There stood the crowd, faces flushed red, glaring at Subaru with eyes gone far beyond sane.

Looking at Subaru and Beatrice and spewing foul curses, they looked exactly like Sirius. It was safe to assume the crowd was now completely sharing in Sirius's "Wrath."

And their fury was, right now, about to turn on Subaru and the others.

"So it's not just sharing emotions—he can brainwash people into his own pawns, too?"

"This isn't the time for talk, I suppose. With no way to break through, all we can do is flee, I suppose!"

Just as Subaru groaned at the sheer awfulness of it, Beatrice leapt onto his back. As Subaru braced her light body with a hand, steadying her, the crowd surged toward him all at once.

"Emilia! Keep him occupied!"

"I can't push it too far, you know!"

With Emilia's spirited answer, Subaru accelerated backward to escape the crowd. Fortunately, the footing of the people who'd lost their sanity was far from steady.

Reaching out with both arms toward Subaru, muttering hollow words of rage, they looked, in a way, not unlike zombies. The differences being that they were conscious, and that their goal wasn't to eat Subaru but to tear him apart.

"If I keep buying time like this, someone's bound to notice something's wrong and..."

"Even if they get here, it's meaningless as long as we can't see the condition to break this situation, I suppose. And if Reinhard were to arrive, that alone might put us in checkmate, I suppose."

"Well, at least there's no worry of him being called in right away."

After all, Rachins, the man tasked with summoning Reinhard, was currently red-faced and in hot pursuit of Subaru. Shoving aside the people around him, he was showing enough drive to be neck and neck for first place.

Perhaps it was some residual impression of Subaru left over from before Sirius's link took hold.

"And while you're off thinking things over—!!"

"Damn insect!"

An arm shot in from dead sideways, nearly grabbing him, and Subaru reflexively ducked his head. As it passed over him, he dove into his attacker's chest, swept his legs out, and sent him toppling, then kicked him forward.

The thoughtlessly charging crowd tripped over the man rolling underfoot and went down in a heap. At the sight of them falling like bowling pins, heads apparently as empty as that, Subaru cocked his head and said,

"You're so mad your heads have stopped working entirely, huh?"

"But I can't really recommend that approach either, I suppose. Given the mood they're in, they don't seem like they'd hesitate to trample their own comrades to death, I suppose."

"That's a problem!"

He didn't want any victims.

That Subaru kept fighting so hard, again and again, was precisely because of that overriding goal.

Of course, Subaru understood there were limits to what his reach could cover.

There were many things he wanted to protect. But there was a limit to what he could protect.

That was simply the natural fact of life for a Natsuki Subaru who was neither omniscient nor omnipotent.

"But I'm not gonna be the one deciding where that 'limit' is!"

"That's exactly what makes you Betty's Subaru, I suppose!"

With the greatest cheer at his back, Subaru drew his beloved whip from behind his waist.

He'd save as many lives as he could. So a little collateral damage, please forgive me—that was Subaru's mindset. Aiming for the feet of the charging crowd, Subaru's whip cracked out, splitting the air.

A sound like a small bolt of lightning striking rang out, and cracks spread across the cobblestones beneath the crowd's feet.

However weak and non-lethal a whip might be, swung with full force and no restraint, it could still pack enough power to strip an opponent of the will to resist.

If, at the sight of that power, the crowd would just flinch back, that'd be ideal, but—

"Guess it's not gonna be that easy, huh!"

No helping it, then.

Snapping the whip back the instant it struck the ground at the crowd's feet, Subaru now took aim at the man in front. Average build, faintly bluish hair, sharp eyes. Wait—that's Rachins.

Someone he knew. Even Subaru's heart ached a little.

"It hurts, but it's still better than some stranger! Sorry, Chin!"

"I'm not Chin, I'm— gbuh!?"

Subaru's whip coiled around the leg of Rachins, who'd shouted his reflexive protest, and yanked it up in one motion. His body spun lightly through a half-turn on the spot, taking the people around him down in a chain reaction like falling dominoes.

Even as he did this, Subaru shifted his own body well to the left, out of the crowd's line of advance, steering the footing of those coming up behind elsewhere so they wouldn't trample the group now toppling like dominoes.

"Since they're lacking in reason, this way even I can buy some surprising amount of time—"

Just as he was about to say "buy," a chill shot up the back of Subaru's neck.

That sensation was like an annoying neighbor who'd taken a one-sided liking to Subaru. A troublesome relationship—one where he didn't want to run into them, and yet every time he did, they taught him something useful.

—In other words, this was what was called the presence of "death."

"Whoa—!!"

"Filthy maggot!"

A terrifying whistle of wind cut through as a greatsword swung clean over Subaru's head.

It came from a beastman who'd leapt clear out of the crowd in a single bound, closing the distance to Subaru's throat in one step. He had pointed canine-like ears and a jutting snout, but looking closer, Subaru realized his sly, endearing features actually resembled a fox.

The fox-man, the rich white tail extending from his waist standing on end, undeterred by his missed swing, unleashed blow after blow trying to sever Subaru's neck.

"Beako!"

"Shamak!!"

"—!?"

Facing him head-on, his limbs would be sliced off within five seconds.

Judging the gap in their fighting strength in an instant, Subaru shouted Beatrice's name. That alone was enough for Beatrice to grasp his intent, and she deployed Shamak centered on the fox-man's face.

His tall, slender frame and greatsword were swallowed by black mist, and in an instant, he lost all combat capability.

"Doesn't that cut his link to everyone else's shared senses!?"

"No luck, I suppose! I can strip his combat strength, but the link itself isn't severed, I suppose! If that pervert dies, I bet the curse dragging everyone down with him is still intact, I suppose!"

"So what do we do!?"

"I'm thinking as hard as I can, I suppose!"

Solving the mystery would have to be left to Beatrice.

All Subaru could do was buy Beatrice time to think while continuing to dodge the raging crowd's grasping hands without troubling her further.

"As for Emilia-tan—"

Worried about Emilia, locked in a head-on clash with Sirius, Subaru turned his eyes that way.

He knew that over the past year, Emilia had kept training herself even while handling her political duties. He was well aware her combat strength clearly surpassed his own, and that her resolve was strong.

Even so, Subaru was worried about Emilia. It wasn't a question of who was stronger. It came down entirely to the fact that Subaru was a man, and Emilia a woman.

The kind of worry some people would flatly dismiss as pointless. And that worry—

"Teryah! Soya! Soi!"

—seemed to be lost on Emilia entirely, who, letting out battle cries as oddly deflating as ever, was pressing Sirius hard with a fierce onslaught.

Spinning her body, Emilia brought the twin ice swords in her hands down on Sirius. Whirling his burning chains, Sirius, shouting something foul-mouthed, knocked them away.

The ice swords shattered with a shrill crack and a spray of ice shards, but when Emilia crouched and thrust both arms straight up from below, an ice spear was now gripped in her hands, and its tip sent Sirius's guarding body flying with tremendous force.

It was a forging of ice weapons meant to break, made possible by drawing on her own vast reserves of mana.

The combat technique Subaru had dubbed "Ice-Brand Arts" carried a fantastical quality, almost as if a fairy were dancing—helped along by the fleeting beauty of the shattering ice.

The many shards of shattered ice scattered through the fight told of just how fierce the clash between Emilia and Sirius had become. On a dance floor strewn with scattered ice, the two warriors, wielding the opposing weapons of flame and ice, continued their fight to the death.

"Hyah, yah!"

Chasing after the flying Sirius, Emilia spun her ice spear and thrust its butt end upward. Catching the blow in midair, Sirius skillfully worked his feet, deflected the strike's tip, and seized the spear's butt.

"Seething, scorching heat! The trembling of the heart! Ah, aaah! Aaaah! 'Wrath'!"

"Kyah!?"

In answer to Sirius's cry, the positive and negative of thermal energy reversed.

Emilia's ice spear transformed into a spear of flame in an instant, and the heat made her instinctively let go. The moment she did, Sirius landed, spear of flame in hand, and launched an all-out counterattack.

"Violet eyes that lure men in! A voice like a ringing bell that lures men in! Soft silver hair that lures men in! Fair skin that lures men in! A face dressed up in cuteness to lure men in! Ah, you harlot! Whore! Filthy! Vulgar! Is that how badly you want to make men lose their minds!? Is that how badly you want to steal that person away from me!? You thieving cat! You thieving half-demon!"

"Hey! Wait, don't say weird things like that!"

Narrowing her eyes against the heat wave grazing past her face, Emilia conjured another ice sword into her hand.

The ice blade, its tip split into three prongs, met the flame spear head-on and stopped it cold.

A fierce grinding sound rang out as the fang-baring monster and Emilia, eyes burning with intensity, glared at each other.

"These eyes, this voice, this silver hair! They're all the same as someone I love dearly! The same as the coolest woman in the whole world! Talking about them like that—I'm angry now!"

"Angry!? You're angry!? Don't make me laugh! That's mine! That's the precious thing that person gave me! This role, this name—all of it, gifts from that person! And you'd just take it from me, selfishly, on your own whim...? Stop it! Stop it, stop it, stop it, stop it, stop it, stop it, stop it, stop it, stop it, just STOP!!"

Toward the end, Sirius's scream cracked, turning raw with anguish.

The monster snapped the flame spear in his arms in two, and with the shortened weapons in each hand, struck out alternately. Against the blows of the flame blades, Emilia countered by conjuring twin ice swords.

But it seemed Emilia, too, had felt something in Sirius's scream—her profile seemed to be losing the fierce sense of purpose she'd carried until just moments ago.

"—This is bad."

Seeing that look on Emilia's face, Subaru sensed intuitively that things had taken a turn for the worse.

He had no grounds for it. And yet, he was certain.

The change in Emilia's expression was, in other words, a change in her feelings toward Sirius.

However kind-hearted she might be, there was no way she'd be so easily swayed by sentiment in the middle of a fight. And before that even—no conversation had taken place that should have moved her to sympathy in the first place. And yet, that reaction had appeared, which meant—

"Emilia's falling into Sirius's trap."

But she hadn't fallen completely under his control just yet.

Against Sirius's attacks, though she was slipping onto the defensive, Emilia was still fighting back. She hadn't lost her sanity like the crowd, and she was still just as cute as ever.

And though it was a thought coming rather too late—

"In the first place, why weren't me, Emilia, or Beatrice affected by Sirius's emotional link right from the start?"

Whether Emilia and Beatrice had resistance the same way Reinhard had been able to resist, whether there was individual variance in that shared-sense effect—that was open to debate. And it was also true that Subaru had chalked up Reinhard's safety to simply "well, he's Reinhard," and stopped thinking about it there.

But if there was some other reason behind it, that reason would be the condition.

The fact that Subaru, who had fallen prey to Sirius's Authority three times now, was managing to resist it like this should also count as a data point toward that condition.

If that could be the breakthrough—

"Bea—"

"Subaru!!"

Just as he tried to pass along the flash of insight, a voice charged with panic struck his ears.

An instant after his eyes went wide in surprise, a shock tore through Subaru's right side.

"Guh—!"

His body nearly folded double from the force of the blow, but Subaru dodged by throwing himself to the left at the last instant. Bleeding off some of the impact, he retched up stomach acid as he took stock of what had just hit him.

Gliding in close was a woman with an eyepatch, moving like a shadow. Her bare palm strike had driven into Subaru's torso while his attention had wandered.

"Subaru! You mustn't die, I suppose!"

"Even I'm not gonna kick the bucket from one hit like that... Ah, damn. But that hurt like hell...!"

His ribs creaked, but Subaru judged that neither bone nor organ had been damaged. Or rather, he wanted to judge that. Probably fine. If there's no blood, it's not an injury. He told himself so.

"Every single one of these people feels like a nightmare the moment they're on the other side..."

"It's not that they're unreliable because the enemy is strong—it's just that you're weak, so they feel strong the moment they turn hostile, I suppose."

"That's... the truth...!"

He snapped his whip at the feet of the eyepatch woman as she tried to close in again, and as her attention dropped low, he flung sand at her face. With her one eye—already at a disadvantage—now caked in sand, she writhed, and Subaru bowled her over with a shoulder tackle.

"The one saving grace is that their combat ability's dropped across the board. If they were fighting at full strength, I'd have been beaten half to death by now."

"...It seems there's yet another reason not to celebrate that, I suppose."

To Subaru, catching his breath after fending off the eyepatch woman, Beatrice delivered yet another unwelcome word. Not wanting to hear it, he tilted his head anyway, and Beatrice, furrowing her brow, jerked her chin in a direction.

The direction she indicated led out of the plaza toward the great canal, and—

"You've gotta be kidding..."

Right before the groaning Subaru's eyes, from the street on the canal side, red-faced people were staggering toward them one after another, footing unsteady.

"I think they're bystanders who heard the commotion, I suppose."

"And they wandered into range and got swallowed by the link... You've gotta be kidding me. So his Authority isn't just area-of-effect, it's contagious too?"

—Panic, madness, spreads from person to person.

Sirius's shared emotions and senses were producing a result that vividly reflected exactly that.

Ah, of course. In sheer viciousness of scale, the damage this could cause even surpassed Petelgeuse.

"The more we run, the more victims pile up... What am I supposed to do!?"

"But there are a few odd points, I suppose. The woman Subaru knocked over, the fox-man who took a Shamak, and Chinkarahoi—none of the damage they took has spread to anyone around them, I suppose."

It was a serious moment, so Subaru didn't bother pointing out that she'd mixed up "tonchinkan" and "chinkarahoi."

And indeed, Beatrice's observation could well serve as a clue for inferring the conditions behind Sirius's shared senses.

"...Should we just try knocking everyone out at once?"

"If you had the combat strength to pull that off, it'd be one option, I suppose. —Using Betty's Shamak to steal everyone's consciousness at once is another option, I suppose."

A brutal method, but also the first one they'd considered.

There was no time to hesitate. They also had to avoid letting Subaru's continued flight rack up even more victims. For now, following Beatrice's plan—

"—Ah, gh!?"

"Emilia!?"

Just as he was about to act, a scream rang out, and Subaru's attention was drawn away.

Looking over, he saw Emilia sprawled sideways on the plaza's stones, while Sirius, arms raised and burning, arched his back to loom over her.

"It grows! It grows! The overflow of 'love'! Numbers are power! The power to bind together! One for all! All for one! People love one another, and become one! Sharing feelings, dividing wishes, multiplying joys and sorrows and living them together! Then this outcome is inevitable! A half-demon who can't even join the circle of 'love'—get crushed like the insect you are and vanish!"

Emilia, who had held the advantage at first, gradually found the balance of power evening out, and before long, she fell into a disadvantage.

Whether Sirius had grown stronger as time passed, or Emilia's strength had waned—faced with a result likely caused by one or the other, Emilia, looking up at Sirius, spoke through ragged breaths, her face twisted with frustration.

"Something—something's wrong. What you're saying sounds right, but it feels wrong somehow... Why is that?"

"Because you haven't reached the truth! Because you're a filthy, whoring half-demon who'll live and die never knowing what 'love' even is! A half-demon's very existence is a sin! Your being born was a mistake, and your father and mother meeting was a mistake! Shit crossed with vermin and out came a shit-covered vermin, and this shit-smeared story ends right here!"

"—!"

At the end of this unbearable string of abuse, the color in Emilia's eyes changed.

Even for kind-hearted Emilia, this was abuse she could not let pass. The vilest kind of slander, degrading not just her own existence, but even the meeting of her parents and its result.

Biting her lip, Emilia slammed the stones and sprang up as though bouncing off them. Then, aimed at the wide-eyed Sirius, a pale blue light shot upward from directly below.

"—"

A blue flash of the blade streaked out, and as Sirius reeled back, the edge shallowly sliced through his closed coat.

As his body staggered backward, Emilia, blazing with anger, pressed forward to finish it. Her raised arms swung the ice sword down to cut through the thin frame before her—

"—Eh."

"Mnnnn—!"

—Beneath Sirius's coat, she saw a girl bound tight in chains, and her movement stopped.

The golden-haired, curly-locked girl was bound just as Lusbel had been once before, blood trickling from the corner of her mouth as tears streamed down in torrents. Her body was tightly lashed to Sirius's thin frame.

Tina—a name he recognized flashed through Subaru's mind.

"—That 'Wrath.' It's wasted on someone like you."

Anger seized both Subaru, who'd realized the girl's identity, and Emilia, who'd seen the girl's tears, at the very same moment. In that instant, Sirius grinned with a viciousness beyond anything before, and a wave of flame, its force redoubled, sent Emilia's body flying with tremendous force.

A thunderous blast and shockwave tore through the air, and Emilia's body was flung backward as lightly as a feather, trailing smoke.

Unable to break her fall, she bounced across the cobblestones and rolled to a stop, sprawled spread-eagle in the middle of the plaza.

"Ah... hn..."

Writhing, Emilia let out a pained groan, her breath barely holding on.

Watching Emilia, Sirius, whose flames had until now burned only across his arms, sent the fire soaring so high it could be seen from far away, and clapped his hands together.

"Don't you dare, insect, hold such a sweet passion. It makes me sick."

"—!"

"Well then—thanks. Sorry about this."

Crossing both arms above his head, the whirlpool of flame Sirius held grew a degree more vicious still.

A hellfire that could scorch even iron with a single touch—if swung down, it would burn Emilia's very existence from this world without leaving so much as a shadow.

He had to stop it right now. He had to save Emilia and get her out of here. He knew that, and yet—

"Move, damn it—move, legs!"

"Nnnnn—!"

Subaru's legs shook and trembled, refusing to move, as though frozen stiff with terror.

This abnormality in Subaru's body had begun the very moment he'd seen the eyes of the girl bound to Sirius's torso. Beatrice on his back, too, couldn't even get her teeth to stop chattering.

The shared senses worked on spirits too, apparently. Absurd. But there was no time to think about that right now—

"E—Emilia...!"

His throat, cramped and trembling, couldn't even properly call out the name of the girl he loved.

Surely that voice would never have reached Emilia anyway.

As she lay collapsed and powerless on the cobblestones, what had Emilia thought, facing the hellfire bearing down on her?

—That too was drowned out by the sound of everything being burned to ash, and now no one would ever know.

A tremendous surge of heat scorched the plaza's stones, and the heat wave, striking like a shockwave, dyed the world in gold.

Before a sight that was almost mystical, Subaru sank to his knees, trembling.

"Su—baru..."

Beatrice, riding on his back, called his name in a halting voice.

He couldn't respond to that voice. Eyes fixed downward, still swallowed whole by the terror gripping his entire body, Subaru kept refusing to face reality.

If he raised his head now, if he looked at the place where the flame had fallen, he'd lose to the fear.

No—his heart, already having lost, would shatter completely.

If he saw the scorched traces of Emilia's disappearance from this world—

"Su—Subaru. Subaru!"

Even so, Beatrice desperately kept calling his name.

Struck on the head again and again, Subaru, his frightened heart afraid even of that, kept slowly shaking his head from side to side.

It's impossible. Even if the monster were standing right in front of him now, Subaru—

"—I made it in time."

But the moment he heard that voice, Subaru's heart, rather than the fear of looking away, gave in to the fear of not knowing.

He raised his head and turned his eyes toward the direction of the voice—the spot where Emilia had been burned away.

There, a lone man stood.

A scene of devastation—scorched black, black smoke still trailing, the crackle of heat-swollen stones popping.

There, a lone man stood, perfectly at ease.

And in that man's arms—

"E-mi-lia..."

—was the girl he'd already given up as lost, vanished into the flames.

Her eyes were closed, her body limp, but not a single part of her was damaged.

She had lost consciousness from the accumulated damage and the terror that had loomed before her, but Emilia was safe. She was safe—thank god.

"You're..."

The figure who had appeared out of nowhere and pulled Emilia to safety.

Even as his fear-stricken heart refused to simply rejoice at Emilia's safety, Subaru called out in a trembling voice to the figure's back.

The man reacted to his voice and turned around.

And then he said:

"I came to fetch her. I'm truly glad I made it in time."

"Fe—fetch her... what do you..."

"—Taking the hand of the woman who's to become my wife is only natural, isn't it?"

At the sudden words, Subaru was struck speechless.

To Subaru, frozen with his breath caught in his throat, the man—a white-haired young man—smiled thinly and said,

"I am a Sin Archbishop of the Witch Cult, in charge of 'Greed.' —Regulus Corneas."

He said it without the slightest affectation, as though stating an obvious fact.

"Just as promised—she will become my seventy-ninth wife."
