Arc 4 Chapter 30: "An Uneasy Feeling on the Way Back"

Roswaal's proposal — releasing the evacuees from Arlam Village.
The proposal itself passed without much difficulty, same as last time. Of course it would, given that there was next to no benefit to the Sanctuary in keeping the evacuees confined here. Where this time differed from before was in the conditions attached to it — or rather, the lack thereof. Last time, the condition had been that Subaru undergo the Trials.

"This time around, the guy who'd attach that condition happens to despise my guts through and through, so..."

"What's wrong, Subaru?"

"Nah, it's nothing. More importantly, are you okay, Emilia-tan? Feeling settled? If I'm in the way, I can compromise and wait outside the room."

Waving his hand with a wry smile, Subaru spoke to the girl beside him — Emilia.
They were in Ryuzu's house, which had been given over as Emilia's quarters, sitting side by side on the bed in her room, passing the time without any particular conversation blossoming between them.

It was already evening, and night's curtain would fall soon.
Subaru had shared a late breakfast with Emilia after she woke just before noon, and after that they'd finished a discussion centered on the "evacuee problem," with Roswaal and Ryuzu also present. The proceedings had gone smoothly, and it looked like the evacuees' release would happen tomorrow — the meeting had been about to break up —

"Well, s'only natural to ask, but... Emilia-sama's still gonna take on the Trials tonight, right?"

— had Garfiel not driven the nail in with that remark, they might have gotten away without addressing it directly.
Suppressing the urge to click his tongue at his words, Subaru glanced sideways at Emilia and saw fear and sorrow flicker across her profile for just an instant. Watching it, he became half-certain all over again that Emilia wouldn't manage to clear the Trials this time either.

Unlike Subaru, who carried his memories forward, Emilia's conditions ultimately didn't change. If she were to somehow overcome the Trials, the environment surrounding her would need to have changed far more dramatically through Subaru's actions than it had. And in this loop, there was no prospect yet of any means to change her environment so drastically in so short a time. — Even if she challenged it tonight, it would probably do no more than wear her down further.

"But that's Emilia-tan for you — refusing to admit weakness even here."

At Garfiel's needling question, Emilia hid the emotion that flickered across her for an instant behind her eyes and declared, resolute, "Of course I'll do it."
Garfiel narrowed his eyes, looking somewhat impressed by her bearing, and Roswaal let out a soft whistle that nearly made Subaru burst with anger.
In any case, there was no taking it back now, and only a few hours remained before tonight's Trials would begin.

After the meeting ended, they'd had lunch not long after breakfast, and it had already been about three hours since they returned to this house. The whole time, Subaru — who'd stayed with her without a break — kept tossing out topics of conversation, but as the hour of the Trials drew steadily closer, her replies grew visibly sparser.
Now she managed only the faintest of murmured responses to his words. And yet —

"Um... that's, a little no."

"Ah, got it. Fine. I'll just focus on breathing in the air you breathe out until you calm down, Emilia-tan, so don't worry."

"That's really, really not okay. ...But, stay here."

Faced with the complexity of a maiden's heart, Subaru shrugged and stayed put as told.
Sitting side by side, too spineless to even work up the nerve to take her hand, but he was honestly glad to be needed — by Emilia, of all people.
Even if, for her, it was merely a substitute for the absence of the person she could rely on most.

Ever since coming to the Sanctuary — no, to put it more precisely, ever since returning to the manor when Puck stopped responding to her calls — Emilia's attitude toward Subaru had been steadily softening.
Part of him was simply glad, taking it as a sign she was opening her heart to him. But another part of him held a quiet unease.
That other part of him wondered: wasn't Emilia, having lost her anchor, in a precarious place right now?

"...Mm?"

"It's nothing. Just thinking Emilia-tan's eyelashes are long and cute, and I want to eat them."

"Subaru, you always say things like wanting to eat my hair, or my eyelashes, or wanting to lick my cheek... um, do you have that kind of... hobby?"

"Where I'm from, that's the ultimate expression of affection, y'know."

Subaru scratched his cheek, sulking a little at Emilia's slightly recoiling reaction.
Wanting to nuzzle and coo at her was, to Subaru's mind, the highest form of a courtship declaration — but if he actually acted on it, he'd surely be met with utter horror. All the more so in this world, where the underlying intent went unread. He really ought to watch what he said. A bit late for that realization, though.

For now, occasionally saying something silly to distract Emilia was about the most Subaru could manage. He now knew fragments of the past Emilia had to face. Speaking of them might trigger some dramatic change different from last time, but —

— However he looked at it, he couldn't imagine it changing things for the better.

Whatever the situation, what was needed, in the end, was always time.
Time for Emilia to steel her heart to face her past. Time for Subaru to offer her fragments of that past and draw out the truth from her own mouth. Time, time, time. There simply wasn't enough of it.

"Why the hell do we have to scramble around on such a cramped schedule. Have I even had a properly relaxed stretch of time since coming to this world?"

Searching his memory, if there had been any period Subaru could call peaceful, it was probably only the few weeks after the Juggernaut incident was resolved.
Before and after that, it had been nothing but a relentless torrent of events — his own work ethic was such that it was a wonder he hadn't died of overwork in this life.
As he mulled over such pointless thoughts —

"— Subaru."

The sudden call made his reaction lag. When Subaru turned toward the voice — toward Emilia — he found her gazing at him from close range, her violet eyes glistening.
The instant he met those damp eyes, his heart pounded so hard he thought it might stop. Subaru caught his breath. Watching him, Emilia's eyes wavered between resolve and hesitation. As though, on the eve of the Trials, she were wavering over whether to confide something in him.

"What's up?"

So Subaru wove his words with every ounce of gentleness he could muster, careful not to rush her. If her resolve was about to bear fruit, he would never get in its way.
But at his reply, Emilia's gaze dropped for a moment, and —

"Ah... mm, sorry. Never mind. I just, wanted to say your name."

"—. I, I see. Just wanted to say it, huh! That sounds like something a couple who just started dating would do!"

"I really should get going soon..."

Her resolve had faltered. Regretting having let the moment slip by, but putting on a show of forced cheer so she wouldn't notice, Subaru listened as Emilia rose to her feet, looked up at the window where the sun had set and night had begun, and said —

"— I have to go to the tomb. Subaru, you can only come partway, right?"

"I'd like to beg Garfiel to let me see you off all the way to the entrance, but I don't know if I can talk him into it. ...Emilia, I know it might be pointless to say this, but—"

"— Pointless. No good, Subaru."

He'd tried to make her stop, tried to tell her there was no need to push herself, but Emilia — having anticipated it — cut him off flatly before he could finish.
Before Subaru, who fell silent, Emilia smiled bravely, pressed a finger to her own lips, and said,

"I know, watching me fall apart like I did yesterday probably doesn't make it seem like I'll be okay. But I'm going to do my best. I want to do my best. I feel like I have to do my best."

She clenched the hand she'd raised in front of her face into a fist, and continued, "So—"

"If you want to say something to me, don't say 'it's okay to stop.' Say 'do your best,' and cheer me on instead. If I can believe that even one person is counting on me like that, I think I can turn it into strength."

"I'm counting on you, Emilia-tan. Honestly, I might be second only to your papa cat when it comes to guys counting on you. — Go get 'em."

"Mm. I will."

That day, for the first time, Emilia showed him an unguarded, genuine smile. Reassured by that smile, Subaru rose as well and followed her out of the building.
In the Sanctuary at night, with the sunlight gone, a wind blew cold enough to raise gooseflesh.

In that flowing wind, Emilia's silver hair danced and shimmered as she walked ahead.
Watching that river of silver catch the moonlight, Subaru fixed his eyes on the back moving forward step by determined step, and —

— Even so, he already sensed: tonight, too, it would be impossible.

The return to Roswald Manor with the evacuees in tow came two days sooner than it had before.
Aside from the date, little else differed from last time. The evacuees each boarded the same Dragon Carriages they'd arrived at the Sanctuary in, and the merchants who'd been hired were released from the Sanctuary along with them. Subaru and Otto rode along as escorts.
If there was one point that differed most from last time, it was this —

"I'm surprised Ryuzu-san volunteered to guide us. Usually this kind of job goes to, I dunno, some underling... someone in that sort of position, doesn't it?"

"What, art thou dissatisfied with me? And here we've shared a pot of tea together — Su-bou, thou'rt cold to thine elders. It wounds the heart."

Said the loli-baba, feigning tears as she spoke. She'd wedged her small frame into the cramped driver's seat, planting herself boldly beside Subaru. The seat was built for two to begin with, and even with her petite frame, having one more body in there made things rather tight.

"Yeah, I really do sympathize. Natsuki-san genuinely has zero mercy and zero restraint about anything, so I guess he must've left all sense of tact behind in his mother's womb."

"Oi, Su-bou. There's a face I don't recognize sitting beside the driver's seat. Who's this?"

"Is that really where I rank in your mind!?"

Otto, about to found a Subaru Victims' Association, had the ladder kicked out from under him. Holding the reins in the driver's seat, shouldering what was literally the lifeline of the whole Dragon Carriage, and yet still treated so lightly as ever.
Casting a shadow over his surprisingly well-proportioned features, he said,

"Ahh... it feels like ever since I got here, all I've done is keep shouting like this the whole time — did I actually manage to make a decent impression on Count Mathers?"

"You showed him your usual relaxed self just now, and he laughed so hard his stomach wound nearly split open, so in terms of making an impression, I'd say that's a pretty strong one, wouldn't you?"

"There are good impressions and bad impressions in this world, but which side would you say 'the impression made when your stomach nearly ruptures' normally falls under?"

"Says the guy who ruptured somebody's stomach in the first place... this guy's hopeless."

"If I'm hopeless, then you're plenty hopeless right alongside me!"

The reason Otto had come along to the Sanctuary — the formal introduction to Roswaal — had concluded without a hitch. That part had followed the same course as last time, and in fact, having watched Otto's character on full display amid gales of laughter, Roswaal's opinion of him was probably not low.
Though the sense that Roswaal had come to see him purely as a merchant was, admittedly, fairly low.

"Well, that's the kind of thing you can hash out gradually as your relationship goes forward. Either way, you already know Mathers territory's top secret, so there's no running away now."

"Guess meeting you was the moment my luck ran out... no, well, I've reached a kind of enlightenment about it at this point, so it's fine."

Truly the mettle of a man who'd charged forward as a merchant despite walking down misfortune's highway without ever giving up. He probably wasn't destined to ride some path to great success, but Subaru would make sure he never regretted the choice he'd made.
Feeling, inwardly, a genuine sense of friendship toward the man who put up with all this, Subaru said,

"I'll keep working you to the bone from here on out too, so take care of me! Otto!"

"What are you saying with such a refreshing look on your face, you person!"

Otto let out a scream as Subaru clapped him on the shoulder and gave him a thumbs up.
Watching Ryuzu, sandwiched between them, mime plugging her ears in annoyance, Subaru once more looked down from the Dragon Carriage before departure, and —

"— Well, I'm off, Emilia-tan."

"Mm, take care."

They exchanged words, Emilia raising a hand slightly before her chest and waving, watching them off with a look of unease.

— Last night, Emilia had challenged the Trials with Subaru accompanying her. The result, as expected, was failure. Since Subaru hadn't been able to go with her into the tomb, the Trials hadn't been interrupted partway through; Emilia, left dazed and vacant, had crawled out of the tomb on her own, her eyes trembling, and collapsed into Subaru's arms before losing consciousness entirely.

Staying by sleeping Emilia's side through the whole night, Subaru could no longer count how many times he'd wiped tears from her sleeping face.
To say he felt no anxiety about leaving her behind carrying that much mental exhaustion would be an outright lie. If he could, he wanted to stay by her side as much as possible and support her trembling body.

"I'll be back within a day or two, so don't push yourself. Once the matter with the village people is settled, there's no need to rush. Let's take it slow and tackle this over time."

"That... I guess so. Yeah, if Subaru says so..."

Unlike the smile she'd shown last night, one that had regained a little strength, this smile made him feel nothing but fragility. And yet, her simply standing here to see them off was already pushing herself. Perhaps by directing her attention elsewhere, she was trying to forget the thing shaking her very foundation.

"Ram, I'm not trying to nag you or anything, but—"

"What else would that be, if not nagging — Ram finds herself questioning that. ...Set your mind at ease. Much as it galls Ram to say, she agrees with Barusu on this. It's a matter that ought to be viewed as a long fight from the start. Unless Roswaal-sama orders otherwise, Ram will keep Garf in check."

"I'm in your debt... nah, that sounds scary. I'll thank you some other way."

"Tch. Sharp instincts, for a Barusu."

"Just now, I secretly dodged a different death flag entirely—"

Ram heard him click his tongue and didn't so much as flinch, offering only a polite bow as she saw them off. She took a step back, and Subaru finally settled into the driver's seat for departure — and spotted, far back among the line of well-wishers, a blond young man watching him with arms crossed.

The instant Subaru noticed him, the other noticed Subaru too, and their gazes locked.
Whatever feelings each of them poured into that exchanged look, there was no telling — but with what had happened to Emilia last night still fresh, not a shred of the hostility between them had softened.

"Just you wait. I'll find some way to crack this thing open, somehow..."

"Natsuki-san? We're about to depart, is that alright?"

"That's plenty alright. Ryuzu-san, lead the way, please!"

"Leave it to me."

With an affected nod, Ryuzu gave the signal, and Otto — working the reins — cued Patrasche and Frufoo. The Dragon Carriage began slowly rolling forward, and the great migration of evacuees began.
The carriage's speed was nowhere near full pace — more like a crawl, in car terms. With mostly children, the elderly, and women aboard, such caution was unavoidable.

"Still, everyone's faces look pretty good now that they know they can go home."

"One's homeland holds that much power, aye. However little there is to see, however dull it may be, in the end the heart always leaves a piece of itself there."

Ryuzu chimed in as Subaru murmured absently, looking back. Tilting his head and crossing his arms at her words with an "Is that how it is," he asked,

"Ryuzu-san, do you feel attached to the Sanctuary too?"

"...Who's to say. In my case, there's the special circumstance that I know nothing beyond that place. I confess there are times I find it frightening even to think of anywhere else."

"Frightening?"

"Stepping into a place unknown is a frightening thing, Su-bou. Especially for an old servant like myself, who has done naught but pile up years to no purpose."

Ryuzu wore a world-weary smile, her eyes drifting somewhere distant. But given her childlike appearance, no matter how seriously she tried to carry herself, it only ever looked like a little girl putting on airs.
Along the way, exchanging bits of conversation here and there, the Dragon Carriage's slow march through the forest continued. It was a long haul — roughly eight hours one way. The Divine Protection of Wind guaranteed a ride comfortable enough to feel like sitting in a royal carriage, but if anything, that only made the passage of time feel slower.

"Quite a clever Earth Dragon, this one. Barely puts a foot wrong even without my guidance."

"She's my pride and joy of a cutie. Bit rich of me to brag, given how far above my level everyone around me is."

Starting with the people of Roswaal Manor and running through everyone he'd met since the Royal Selection began, each one of them had been a formidable figure in their own right. It was frankly pathetic how mediocre he felt mixed in among such an impressive lineup, but at this point he'd simply resigned himself to looking up at them.
He'd started late and was a lap behind, but he had, at least, started running. All that was left was to keep running to catch up — and he'd already been given the strength for that.

"Speaking of which, it's great that you're guiding us partway, but what are you going to do on the way back, Ryuzu-san? All the Dragon Carriages are heading straight to Arlam Village, so there won't be any transportation for you."

"No need to fret, I'll simply walk back on these two legs, obviously. Mind you, these sturdy legs haven't lost to the young folk yet, not by a long shot."

Ryuzu tapped her short, slender legs, swinging idly with the carriage's motion, to demonstrate. Frankly there wasn't a shred of persuasive force to it, but Subaru didn't much feel like crushing the spirit of a little girl so brimming with confidence, so he said,

"Alright, alright... Hey, Otto. You got it in you to carry a little girl on your back and run all the way to the Sanctuary?"

"I don't understand the intent behind that question, so I'd like to decline to answer — is that alright?"

"Hear that, Ryuzu-san? Apparently this guy doesn't have it in him to carry a little girl who has to walk through the dark forest alone. Says one or two little girls' fates are none of his business."

"What a dreadful thing to say. Truly, the hearts of men have grown thoroughly desolate."

"Did the two of you rehearse this!?"

Otto's scream sliced through the forest's silence as usual, and just as he and Ryuzu shared a laugh over it, she suddenly lifted her head and murmured,

"About time, I'd say."

Subaru knit his brows at Ryuzu's murmur. In the next instant, she abruptly leaned her weight against him. Catching her light body, Subaru let out a small "Oh?" and said,

"Otto, stop. Something's off with Ryuzu-san."

"Should we head back to the village?"

At his terse call, Otto worked the reins and brought the Dragon Carriage to a halt. He signaled the same to the line behind them with a hand flag, and one by one the Earth Dragons' whinnies signaled them coming to a stop.
Then, in Subaru's arms, Ryuzu raised a small hand and said,

"...Forgive me, there's no need to turn back. It was merely the effect of coming this close to the barrier. If we press on further out of the forest, though, my consciousness would likely be swept away."

"The barrier... same as when Emilia entered the Sanctuary?"

Memories, already nearly a week old by Subaru's internal clock, of arriving at the Sanctuary.
Back then, jostled along in the Dragon Carriage just like now, Emilia had suddenly lost consciousness, and that was when she'd received Garfiel's rough welcome.
Ryuzu's condition now closely resembled Emilia's back then — if the carriage had continued on at this pace, her consciousness would surely have been cut off just as abruptly.

"Man, this barrier really doesn't discriminate, does it. Neither my sensitive skin nor Otto's dull skin is picking up on anything in particular."

"What do you mean by 'dull skin'? Skin doesn't have degrees of sensitivity like that."

"That's exactly the kind of young man who neglects skincare and, come his late twenties, finds himself steadily plagued by spots and freckles, regretting the ignorance of his youth."

"I've genuinely stopped understanding what you're talking about, but to get back on track — does this mean this is where we part ways with you, Ryuzu-sama?"

Apparently fed up with the banter, Otto ignored Subaru and redirected the conversation to Ryuzu. In response, still looking somewhat pained, she nodded and said,

"Aye, that it does. This is as far as I go. Those of the Sanctuary simply don't sit well with this barrier. I thought I'd try coming this far again after so long, but... it seems there's no helping it after all."

"Was part of the reason you came along to test that, by any chance?"

"An unexpectedly self-serving thought on my part, I admit. The result speaks for itself, as you can see. ...If even I cannot manage it, then indeed, the Sanctuary cannot be released without the Trials being completed. Now thou seest it plainly, Su-bou."

At the glance the little girl shot his way, Subaru realized she'd shown him, through her own body, the true situation of those trapped within the Sanctuary. And at the same time, that she too — quite naturally — yearned to be outside it.

"I wonder if Emilia's gonna end up the same way, once she comes this far too."

"Once one has entered, aye, likely so. Not everyone who lives in the Sanctuary was born and raised there. Roz-bou would, from time to time, bring in others from outside in similar circumstances. Those children, too, became the Witch's property the moment they entered the Sanctuary. Emilia-sama would be no exception, I'd wager."

"...Feels like there's another piece of info there I probably shouldn't just let slide by again."

If Roswaal had, from outside, brought residents into the Sanctuary — and if they, too, were affected by the barrier — that meant those people were, in other words, Halfbloods as well.

"And he's been bringing them in and keeping them trapped there? Hey now, what the hell is he thinking?"

"As to his true intentions... it's not my place to speak of them. Best thou return and ask Roz-bou directly thyself."

Weakly shaking her head, Ryuzu slipped free of the arms of the frowning Subaru. She lowered her small frame nimbly from the driver's seat, then stroked the neck of Patrasche, who turned her head toward her, saying,

"A fine Earth Dragon, this one. Be strength to thy master, now."

Patrasche nuzzled her nose against Ryuzu as if in agreement with her words. More than anything, without meaning to boast, this was the first time Subaru had seen Patrasche take so readily to anyone besides himself. Otto, despite being able to converse with her, got headbutted all the time.

"There's a bunch of stuff to handle at the Sanctuary anyway, and once I ask Frederica what I need to ask, I'll head straight back."

"Best thou dost. ...Call it an old woman's intuition, but I've a feeling matters at the Sanctuary won't move without thee."

"That's a heck of an overestimation... though it is just intuition."

"The intuition of a woman who's lived over a hundred years, mind."

"Whether that's a point in its favor or against it depends how you look at it, I guess."

Replying to Ryuzu with that, Subaru gave her a polite bow from atop the driver's seat. Once she'd stepped back a safe distance from the carriage, Otto murmured, "Heading off," and —

"Well then, Ryuzu-san, see you. Get home safe."

"Aye. Straight on from here and you'll clear the forest. Once you reach the highway, the Earth Dragons will manage the rest well enough on their own. Safe travels."

Waving lightly and seen off by her, Otto signaled with the hand flag, and the Dragon Carriage's march resumed.
Watching them draw away, Ryuzu too turned her back and headed deeper into the forest. Watching her figure sink into the gaps between the trees, Subaru prayed she'd make it back safely — and, in his chest, felt an inarticulable knot of unease.

"...Something felt off, huh."

An unease born from that earlier conversation. Unable to put into words what exactly it was, Subaru let his weight sink into the carriage's rocking motion.
They cleared the forest, sunlight streamed in, the road widened — they crossed the barrier and left the Sanctuary behind.
From here, another long, long road still lay ahead.

Things that needed doing, things that needed saying.
With all of it piled up unresolved, Subaru let the Dragon Carriage go on rocking him along.
