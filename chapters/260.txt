Arc 4 Chapter 92: "Lies"

*"It's been quite a while since we've spoken like this, hasn't it?"*

*"Ever since you vanished... man, it's been almost two weeks now. I couldn't stand watching your owner search around with that sad look on her face."*

Not a sound that shook the air, but words that seeped directly into the inside of his skull—that strange, incomprehensible sensation. And yet, the same leisurely tone of voice as before came through unmistakably, and Subaru couldn't help but let his inward indignation bleed into his reply.

More than any relief at getting the response he'd expected—or hoped for—irritation won out, and he wasn't sure how to take this situation. The rift between them ran too deep for him to accept it at face value.

*"It seems that during these two weeks we couldn't exchange words... you've built up quite a bit of anger toward me."*

*"You know the reason, right? Don't make me spell it out."*

*"Right. Before I stopped coming out... what I said to you in front of that blue-haired girl—thinking back on it, that was awfully thoughtless of me. I've reflected on it a lot."*

*"...! That's not what I'm talking about!"*

Digging up a grievance Subaru had already forgotten about—*that* was the thoughtless part. On top of that, bringing it up in a situation completely unrelated to what should actually be discussed.

Puck responded to Subaru's outburst with a sulky-sounding *"Don't be so angry with me..."* and then—

*"I know, I know. I just wanted to apologize first. If we don't clear away the bad blood before getting to the main matter, we might not be able to push into things properly with each other, don't you think? ...Especially since I'm in the position of having to ask a great deal of you from here on out."*

*"Oh, is that right. If your self-satisfaction's been satisfied, then let's get on with it. The main matter you're so eager to get to."*

Putting on a show of accepting Puck's apology only on the surface, Subaru glared at the crystal stone and pressed for him to continue.

The translucent gleam bounced back the light of Subaru's gaze, glowing a deep green against Emilia's chest.

Subaru clicked his tongue.

*"Anyway, this situation makes talking way too inconvenient. For now, come out into the open. I don't even know when Emilia's going to wake up. Let's move somewhere else..."*

*"Sorry, but I can't do that. —That's actually one part of the main matter."*

Puck kicked down the proposal, made while looking down at Emilia's sleeping face, with his thought-words. At that response, Subaru's face went blank for a moment, as if someone had pinched his nose, before he said—

*"That's not a 'don't want to' kind of refusal. What do you mean, 'can't'?"*

*"Exactly what it sounds like. Right now, I'm in a state where I can't materialize outside the crystal stone—that is, in the outside world. If I weren't, do you think I'd leave Lia alone like this, making her this sad?"*

*"————"*

To Puck's voice, speaking as though stating the obvious, Subaru answered with silence through their shared thoughts.

Thinking back on everything that had happened between him and this Spirit up to now, honestly, there was a part of him that wanted to doubt just how much credibility to give his words.

But setting aside Puck's tendency to fall one step short at the crucial moment, at the fundamental core—the fact that his existence was for Emilia's sake, that his feelings for her were no lie—on that point alone, Subaru thought there was no need to doubt him.

So—

*"There's some circumstance, some reason... and you can't come out."*

*"That's right. I haven't even been able to reach out with my thoughts like this. So it was a stroke of luck that Subaru noticed and called out to the crystal stone. I don't think anyone else could have seized this chance."*

*"Anyone else... meaning?"*

*"Simply put, with Lia unconscious, there's hardly anyone besides Subaru who'd want to get this close to her. And even if someone got lucky enough to touch the crystal stone, whether the thoughts connect or not is a matter of compatibility. I tested it once before, in the royal capital—I confirmed that a path for thought-speech connects between Subaru and me."*

*"...Now that you mention it, that did happen."*

The second time in the royal capital, when he'd had a secret conversation with Emilia—that was when he and Puck had exchanged thought-speech just like this. A reenactment of that moment—a secret talk concerning Emilia—that was true in that sense too.

*"So, what should I do?"*

*"Hmm...?"*

*"You were deliberately timing when someone would speak to you, and I happened to call out to you in this almost miraculous situation. You must have been preparing for such a once-in-a-lifetime chance so you wouldn't miss it. In this short window, this limited opportunity—the words and hints you need to convey."*

*"————"*

A meaningful silence answered Subaru's words. He could vividly picture the invisible little cat Spirit wearing an oddly human-like smile.

True to that image, Puck laughed in a voice that couldn't hide his delight.

*"I really was right to have expectations for you. Though honestly, I can't hide how frustrating it is to think I have to entrust Lia's care to someone other than me."*

*"...If you want, should I tell Emilia how you feel?"*

Subaru lowered his eyes at how the joy in Puck's words had darkened into something more solemn toward the end. What he'd proposed to him afterward was little more than an idea, one he wasn't sure would even be a comfort. But once he'd said it, it seemed, in a way, like a rather good idea.

Right now, Emilia had lost sight of Puck, the core support of her heart, and was wavering. She could endure it for now, but as time passed and the setbacks of the Trials of the Tomb piled up, her mind and body would wear down and her weakness would be exposed. If it was going to come to that, wouldn't it be better to show her one small salvation here—

*"You'd better not. If she found out my words could reach someone else, in the worst case, it might break Lia's heart."*

But Puck's subdued voice stopped Subaru, who had been mentally moving forward with the idea. Swallowing the meaning of those words, Subaru let out a dry "Huh" of breath in the real world, and said—

*"That's... what exactly do you mean by that?"*

*"Exactly what it sounds like. If I convey my words to Lia through you, that means Lia would find out that I'm not asleep inside the crystal stone. For Lia—who's keeping me from coming outside, keeping me from contact with anyone—realizing that my mouth isn't sealed shut could be enough to upset the precarious balance of her heart even now."*

*"Wai—wait a second—!"*

At Puck's rapid-fire words, Subaru shook his head and called for a halt.

With no form to see, only the sheen of the crystal stone in his field of view, Subaru couldn't make out Puck's expression. But at least in his tone of voice, there was no sign of any intent to deceive him, and—

*"Do you even realize what you just said? You just said that Emilia—of all people—is keeping you from coming outside."*

*"............"*

*"What do you mean, sealing your mouth shut...? Emilia's been calling for you like that, crying and screaming for you to help her... so why would you say it like that?! Even though it's not me, not anyone else—when Emilia's worn to exhaustion, it's your name she calls! So why do you...!"*

*"...Ah, I see. You're frustrated that you're not the one Lia relies on first, aren't you, Subaru."*

*"—!!"*

At words that felt like a sleight-of-hand shift of the argument—yet whose meaning struck precisely at the core of Subaru's own heart—his throat closed up with some incomprehensible surge of emotion.

It was true that he kept acting on the desire to be Emilia's number one. And it was also true that, right now, the fact lay before him that he wasn't.

And it was also true that the presence occupying that place as Emilia's number one—a presence that should hold far more power than someone like Subaru—despite that presence declaring outright that Emilia was *his* number one too, wasn't acting for Emilia's sake, and that was maddeningly hateful to him.

Precisely because of that, to be told that the reason, the cause of that fact, lay not with himself or with Puck, but with Emilia herself and no one else—there was no way he could just swallow that at face value.

*"So what, then. Are you saying that Emilia challenging the Trials of the Tomb and being broken, suffering alone and wearing away to nothing, smiling through tears as she remembers her sad past—all of it, every bit of it, is a bald-faced act? —There's no way I could believe that!"*

If those tears, that voice, that anguished wailing, all of it, were an act meant to deceive everyone around her, then Emilia's acting talent was nothing short of a gift from heaven. She ought to aim to become the greatest actress under the sun rather than aim for the throne.

If he could trample and ignore the plain, obvious fact that Emilia had no such talent, no reason to deceive him and the others, then he should be willing to do so. But he couldn't.

*"There's no way that's true... Far from keeping up a lie to everyone around her, she gets crushed with guilt over even the smallest little fib. Emilia's not that kind of girl..."*

*"Subaru, calm down. I'm not speaking as badly of Lia as your worst imagination might think. So calm down."*

*"My worst imagination...? What do you mean by that? Don't you go peeking into my head without permission! That has nothing to do with this. No matter what happens from here on, I would never let Emilia become something like that, ever...!"*

*"—Natsuki Subaru!"*

As his heart was tossed about by emotion, Puck's sharp voice struck through Subaru in his agitated state.

At the strong feeling packed into that short call, Subaru's body shook for an instant and he stopped moving. Looking over hesitantly, there was still no sign of the little cat's form—only the inorganic glow resting quietly at Emilia's chest. And yet—

*"...Have you calmed down?"*

*"...You could even make a voice like that? I always figured you as the type to stay all dazed and fluffy, ignoring how serious things are."*

*"I rarely raise my voice like that. Only for things concerning Lia... and about as much as when scolding an unreasonable child."*

*"An unreasonable brat, huh."*

At the merciless words, Subaru let out a small breath.

The fact that he couldn't fully deny Puck's words was because he was aware there was a problem with his own unsightly attitude up until now.

Even though this was the conversation he'd been longing for, Subaru had failed to keep his composure more than once already. How many times now had Puck had to rein him in and pull the conversation back on track?

His own lack of self-control was pathetic. A heart of iron, the thing he kept longing to obtain—did he not have even a trace of it within him?

*"Still, honestly, your existence—getting this emotional over Lia—is actually somewhat appreciated on my end. For Lia too, you must be giving her no small amount of strength."*

*"—Eh?"*

*"No one has ever stepped this far into Lia's inner heart before. Even Roswaal, who took Lia out of the forest because of the Royal Selection, doesn't interfere with the deep part of her heart. His wish probably lies somewhere apart from what Lia does once she sits on the throne, so I suppose that's only natural."*

*"—Do you know what Roswaal's goal is?"*

*"Tracing the Gospel, isn't it? In that respect, he might be similar to Betty. The standpoint of one who's written into it, and the standpoint of one who isn't. Similar, yet different, I suppose you could say."*

The standpoints of both Roswaal and Beatrice, as far as Subaru knew them—Puck apparently had known the details of these for some time already. He doubted this was information that had even reached Emilia, and he was all the more concerned about what Puck had been thinking, keeping it locked away in his own chest all this while.

Still, Subaru had a vague idea of what would come back if he pressed the question.

*"It's got nothing to do with Emilia, so you didn't take the initiative to move on it... is that it?"*

*"As for Betty, if there's something I can do, I do want to help her. As for Roswaal... since it involves dragging Lia into it, that's something that has to be dealt with eventually."*

*"You knew all along and just kept putting it off, and now the bill's come due."*

*"I've got no comeback for that. Though I do feel bad dragging you into paying that bill."*

Regardless of whether there was ill intent behind it, it should be said that the bad part of him—having no intention of prioritizing anyone but Emilia—had exerted a strong influence here. If that fence-sitting stance of his was what had invited today's hardship upon Emilia, then it was too great a fault to laugh off as some trivial mistake.

*"I'll break Roswaal's goal myself, so never mind that for now. Beatrice too... I don't intend to leave that to you either. The only thing I'm conspiring with you on is Emilia."*

*"That's fine by me. I don't have much strength to spare for anything besides Lia right now either. If I pour effort into something that isn't the main priority and overlook the most important thing, that would defeat the whole purpose."*

*"Tell me. What does it mean that Emilia isn't trying to let you come outside? There's no way I can believe that girl is deceiving me and everyone around her."*

Earlier he'd raised his voice, driven by passion, but that conviction hadn't changed even now. He couldn't possibly know the whole of Emilia's inner heart, but even so, there was no way he could believe she was some being who schemed against the people around her and trampled on their kindness.

At that thought of Subaru's, Puck conveyed through their thoughts a sense of breath, as if in relief, and said—

*"Whether I should say 'you can rest easy' is a hard call. It's true that it's Lia's own will that keeps me from coming outside... but Lia isn't keeping me from coming outside because she's trying to keep me from coming outside."*

*"...Sorry, I don't understand what you're saying."*

*"It's hard to explain. It's all true—that Lia is asking me for help, that she's calling out to the crystal stone, that she can't hear my voice. It's a fact that she's afraid of being alone, that she's lost her support and is trembling. But—"*

*"————"*

*"On an unconscious level, Lia is refusing my materialization and any communication between us. You could say the front and back of her heart don't agree with each other."*

At the expression "the front and back of her heart," Subaru caught his breath.

Surely it didn't mean something like a split personality, but the experience of being betrayed by his own heart was something Subaru himself had tasted many times over, whenever he'd been driven into a corner in this world.

If Emilia now found herself in a similar situation—

*"Can't you reach out to Emilia yourself, from your end?"*

*"That's difficult. The compulsion from the back part of her heart is stronger than the front. It must mean that if I were to come out to the front, it would cause quite a problem for Lia's heart."*

*"Something troublesome for Emilia's heart—do you have any idea what that could be? Something that would be inconvenient for Emilia if you came out..."*

*"You've more or less figured it out already, haven't you?"*

Catching the trailing edge of Subaru's words, a note of teasing mixed into Puck's voice. At that thought-message from Puck, Subaru fell silent for a moment, then lowered his eyes.

*"—It's just a guess, though."*

*"Sure, that's fine. Let's hear it. I told you, didn't I? I have expectations for you, Subaru."*

To Subaru's preamble, Puck gave him a seal of approval that wasn't the least bit welcome. Even so trivial a guarantee made him feel somewhat lighter, and as if pushed forward by it, Subaru went on—

*"If you're around, Emilia..."*

*"Mm-hm."*

*"...has to acknowledge some inconvenient part of the past she's looking at. That's why Emilia's unconsciously refusing any situation where you'd have a say."*

*"————"*

Puck received Subaru's thought-wave neither with denial nor with laughter, but with silence.

If the invisible little cat Spirit had actually been present here, would he still be swaying that long tail of his side to side, with that same leisurely bearing and languid expression?

*"Amazing, Subaru. That's more than I expected."*

After a moment of silence, Puck said so as if in admiration of Subaru's words.

Subaru let out a breath through his nose at Puck's attitude.

*"Being praised—honestly, it doesn't make me happy at all."*

*"It's genuine praise. It's not like you had that much information to go on, and yet you got this far just from guesswork—that's really astonishing. You understand the state of Lia's heart well too."*

Was Puck's awareness, filled with such great tenderness, gazing at the sleeping Emilia? As if drawn in by that voice, Subaru too found himself gazing at her pale, sleeping face.

Emilia, deep in a sleep so profound she couldn't see either nightmares or good dreams. The Trials of the Tomb and the accompanying past that were wearing down her heart, driving her further and further.

And yet, as to just how far that reenactment of the past "faithfully followed the true facts of the past," Subaru was skeptical even of that.

In fact, the past Subaru himself had shaken off within the Trials had come in the form of parting with his father and mother—the symbol of his own past regrets. Of course it had. For Subaru, the past he needed to shake off wasn't one single great incident, but that environment itself, and the idle time he'd spent leading up to building it.

Hence the Trials had created for Subaru a form of time that had never actually existed, urging him toward a peaceful farewell with his parents—the symbol of his regret.

*"The past doesn't faithfully trace the real world. It's assembled into a form suited to the Trials, drawing up the mental landscape of the one who challenges it, along with all sorts of other conditions."*

At the place where he'd finished the Trials, Echidna had told Subaru the broad outline of how the Trials worked. That it gathered up elements from Subaru's own memory—even ones Subaru himself wasn't consciously aware of remembering—and, on top of that, formed an "elaborate fake world."

That the parting with his parents wasn't the real thing, but merely Subaru's own self-satisfaction.

—*So what*, he thought now.

*"The past you see in the tomb is a fake dressed up as the genuine article. That ill-natured woman who rigged up the Trials set it up so the problem gives the challenger whatever answer feels best to them."*

She hadn't said quite that much, but he was certain that was exactly the kind of thing Echidna would do. To that extent, at least, Subaru had gotten a good look at the blackness of that Witch's guts.

So—

*"The past Emilia is seeing has parts that are correct and parts that are wrong. That difference... something that decisively distorts the past—you know what it is. That's why Emilia's unconsciously refusing to call you out."*

*"...I wonder why. If I were there, Lia could see the true, correct past. Knowing that, why does Lia's true heart reject me?"*

*"That's..."*

He almost continued with "simple," but Subaru hesitated to put the rest into words.

The reason for his hesitation was, indeed, simple. To speak the rest was to expose the truth of Emilia's past. And if the cruel, eye-averting time that Emilia herself had spoken of was serving as a cover, hiding the truth within Emilia's heart—then—

*"The real time that Lia wants to forget must be even more hopeless than the fake time she spoke of."*

Puck finished, as if taking up the answer Subaru couldn't voice himself, and giving it form.

Made aware of something he'd managed to avoid consciously acknowledging until he heard it spoken aloud, Subaru's face twisted in anguish as he gazed at Emilia.

People with whom she'd spent close, gentle time together—having malice and hatred hurled at her by those very people, hearing their curses as she greeted a long, long parting within the ice. That was Emilia.

A past that, just thinking of it, felt like it would tear body and soul apart—and yet for Emilia, it served as a gentle cradle to conceal the truly cruel truth.

*"Do you know what Lia really saw?"*

*"...Unfortunately, I don't know that either. I met Lia after she'd already been frozen in ice in the forest. So I have no idea what it is about my existence that Lia is afraid of. I don't know what part of me determines Lia's past."*

Puck murmured, sounding genuinely frustrated, and Subaru bit his lip at those words.

Emilia's true past—but thanks to this, he'd managed to grasp one part of the answer as to why she couldn't overcome the Trials.

Each time, the Trials showed Emilia a past different from the true one. And Emilia herself wished for that fake past to be her own past.

Unless she could give an answer to the true past, she could never overcome the Trials. As long as Emilia was being deceived by her own heart, the past would keep slicing into her with its sweet blade.

*"What should I do?"*

*"I don't know."*

*"I want to save Emilia. I want to be a strength for that girl."*

*"So do I. I exist only for that girl's sake. If I can't be a strength for her, then there's no meaning to my existence at all."*

*"I want to support what she wants to do, and I want to stand beside her."*

*"————"*

Before Subaru's appeal, Puck fell silent, as if lost in thought. Subaru simply waited, still, for the Spirit's answer. And then Puck spoke, his voice steeped in resolve—

*"Subaru. —There's one possibility."*

*"A possibility...?"*

*"It's a method I'd never have considered on my own, and even now, frankly, I have a strong sense of revulsion toward it. If I'd thought of a proposal like this, I never would have said it out loud."*

At Puck's words, as if bracing him for what was to come, Subaru steeled himself for what might be said. At the very least, this was the first time Subaru had heard Puck's voice sound this serious about anything other than calling out to Emilia.

*"What are you going to make happen?"*

*"I'll be the one doing it. Though you'll be the one cleaning up after, afterward."*

*"...I've got a bad feeling I'm about to hear something outrageous."*

*"I never even considered asking something like this of anyone else, myself. It's just... well, you're the only one who seems like he could bet his very life for Lia's sake."*

At Puck's words, as if holding back some great emotion, Subaru drew in a small breath.

The feeling Puck held toward Subaru—that was, beyond any doubt, a fact. Natsuki Subaru would bet even his own life, if it was for Emilia's sake.

So, to Subaru, who silently indicated his assent, Puck surely nodded within the crystal stone.

Then, in a quiet voice, he said:


*"Tomorrow morning, I'm going to make Lia break the Contract. —If the bond between Lia and me is severed, I wonder... how will you comfort Lia as she cries her heart out?"*
