Arc 3 Chapter 20: "Self-Proclaimed Knight Natsuki Subaru"

It was his best power pose, put together with everything he had — but ever since coming to this world, he couldn't really recall a single time his "power pose" had actually accomplished what a power pose was supposed to accomplish.

As far as he could remember, there was the time he'd gotten Emilia to tell him her name, and the time he'd struck some kind of cool pose while spouting a line at Rem so cheesy it made him blush just thinking back on it.

—Come to think of it, those had actually worked out fine at the moments that mattered.

Reaching that private conclusion, he nonetheless reflected that this particular scene probably hadn't called for this particular face, given the TPO — time, place, occasion.

Frozen mid-smile in the hushed, silent hall, Subaru nevertheless pushed through with the outrage.

Once he'd started something, there was no way he could be the one to back down.

Waiting properly for the other party's reaction, and only then working out his follow-up — that was, at the very least, Subaru's professional pride as an entertainer. Except he wasn't one.

And so Subaru's utterly unprofitable, benefits-no-one-whatsoever decision was—

"Baaarcelonaaa!?"

He was cut off with a groan of pain at the sharp, hard impact that slammed into the back of his head.

Stars burst across his vision, and his knees buckled instantly under the force of the blow. A heavy, solid *thunk* sounded as something hard and heavy dropped right beside him, and his brain immediately concluded: he'd been struck.

Then, forgetting the pain in his anger, Subaru whirled around with tears in his eyes,

"Oi, who the hell d'you think you are?! You know I'm Emilia-tan's Number One Knight, under Roswaal's care, and best buds with Reinhard — and you still pull this crap?!"

"That's all borrowed authority—!?"

Subaru spat with the full force of his fury. Someone jabbed in at how thoroughly his outburst leaned on other people's names, but even that didn't reach Subaru's ears right now.

It was partly that the situation—one he could no longer back out of—had suddenly shifted, and he was riding that momentum to cover for having fumbled his line. But that didn't mean he felt no anger at having his declaration of resolve interrupted.

It had been a statement wrung out of every scrap of courage Subaru had.

The way he'd wrung it out had been a little unhinged, and the reaction from everyone around him afterward had been nothing like what he'd hoped for, so his spirit had nearly broken — but even so, he'd wanted, if no one else, to praise himself for it. That much was undeniably the expression of a certain resolve.

And the very person who'd doused that resolve with cold water was—

"And now I'm getting hit with some Dempsey Roll combo starting off a Shamak, and it's Emilia-tan?!"

Spotting the silver-haired girl with her palm turned toward him, Subaru stomped the ground with considerable force as he shot back the retort.

Sure enough, lying right beside Subaru was a chunk of ice roughly the size of an armful. That it hadn't cracked his skull open seemed nothing short of miraculous, and having served its purpose, it dispersed and melted into the air.

Watching it happen, Subaru couldn't hide his shock at this unbelievable betrayal. But the dismay swimming in Emilia's eyes as she looked back at him was far more intense than his own.

She flailed the hand she'd aimed at Subaru,

"T-that's not it! Just now, um, I just thought I had to calm you down somehow, and it just sort of…"

"Going straight to konking me on the back of the head is a dangerous train of thought! You're like some heroine from an old-school manga! Though Emilia-tan is my eternal heroine!"

"Hero—? Ugh, never mind, this isn't the time for that — apologize, right now! I'll apologize with you."

"You're my mom now?!"

Apologizing with a parent in tow after doing something wrong wasn't sincerity, Subaru thought — it was spinelessness. Tickling a woman's maternal instincts and having her face you purely from a guardian's point of view might look similar, he realized, but they were actually worlds apart. Subaru flatly refused to do anything so undignified.

The two of them traded their usual back-and-forth, right there in a hall packed with the nation's most eminent figures.

Most of the onlookers watched the pair with their mouths hanging open, unable to hide their bewilderment. Meanwhile,

"He went and did it—!!"

"Ahahahaha! Look there, Al! A fool — there stands a fool!"

The most arrogant master and servant pair pointed at Subaru — one clutching his head, the other clutching her sides in a fit of laughter.

"Having now seen actual magic in use — the time from incantation to activation was quite fast. She's genuinely skilled, it seems."

"Oh my, Crusch-sama, the way you zero in on things is just so gloriously manly...!"

That other master-and-servant pair, whose personalities were reversed along gender lines, likewise had one of them praising Emilia's talents as a mage, while the other, ignoring that entirely, writhed in delight over their lord's train of thought.

"Ahh, yeah, that guy's really gone off the deep end, huh. Figured as much, but still."

"I see. So that's it. Subaru — you really are Emilia-sama's..."

Even the two who had not yet settled into a master-and-servant bond had their say — one summing up Subaru's behavior in rather rude terms, the other, for some reason, wearing a look of understanding and quiet conviction in his eyes.

And then,

"Huh, an' here I thought some kid in weird getup snuck in by mistake, but turns out he's that little witch girl's fella, is he... Hey, Julius, what's up?"

"No, it's nothing — well, there is something on my mind."

In response to his distinctively-spoken liege's words, the Knight among Knights closed his eyes quietly, meaningfully. He fell silent after that, choosing silence and simply waiting for his moment.

Watching each of the candidates take the mood of the room in stride, without so much as flinching, made it clear that every one of them possessed nerves rather different from an ordinary person's.

In the end, the spat between Subaru and Emilia had only thrown into sharp relief the difference in temperament between the candidates and everyone else present.

Of course, the two of them themselves had no such awareness, and oblivious to the reactions around them, carried on their usual squabble.

"There you go again, Subaru, always dodging with words I can't understand. Just because I'm a little unworldly doesn't mean I'll keep letting myself get fooled every time."

"'Fooled' makes me sound like such a bad guy! My heart's always racing straight for true, going full steam-together down your route, y'know!"

"Ahh, Emilia-sama, Subaru-kuun—"

Emilia crossed her arms and turned pointedly away, while Subaru stamped his feet, refusing to let it go. The moment their war of words stalled for a beat, Roswaal's drawling voice cut in.

The two of them responded with nothing more than a glance, and he, without a word, made a gesture sweeping across the entire hall,

"Ahh."

"Uwehh."

Only then, at last, did they notice that the hall had descended into a fairly chaotic state.

For Subaru, who was always keenly attuned to the air after failing to read it, the current situation was a well-worn kind of trouble — about on the level of an "oops, did it again, teehee." Emilia, on the other hand, had gone pale, and her dismay went well beyond that.

Her fair skin drained of even more color, she immediately looked up toward the dais, and on the way, grabbed Subaru's ear to yank his face around to face the same direction,

"We're terribly sorry for the disturbance we've caused."

"Owowowow! It hurts! My ear's coming off! Off, off it comes, if it comes, when it comes, let it come, come come come—!!"

Since Emilia bowed her head while still gripping his ear, Subaru was dragged along in that direction until he ended up bowing too.

Reduced to near tears twice in such a short span, Subaru nonetheless thrashed his way free of her grip and, rubbing the throbbing spot at top speed,

"That's dangerous! Ears are one of those weak points you can't train up, so you gotta handle 'em with more care — it'll be too late once it actually comes off!"

"If things got to this point, that means it wasn't listening to a word anyone said, right? If it's not even doing its job, it might as well come off — that'd be better."

"That's harsh, even for someone panicking! For the record, when it comes to my eyes, ears, nose, and throat — all my sensory functions — I'm confident I'd score a double-A rating!"

It was just that his brain and personality never seemed to steer those senses toward actually being useful.

Whether she judged Subaru's remark — based on the health checkups of his old world — to be his usual nonsense, Emilia didn't pursue it further, and continued her apology to the Council of Wise Men.

She shut her eyes once, then reset her expression to something serious and looked up at the elders,

"In any case, please forget what was said just now. This child— he is an acquaintance of mine, and a retainer of Margrave Roswaal, but as for what was said before..."

"Wait wait wait! I'm telling you, having it swept under the rug is a problem!"

But Subaru cut in once more, stopping the conversation Emilia had been trying, somehow, to wrap up.

She turned toward him a look that was genuinely running out of patience by now — though to call it a glare would be wrong, since it carried far too much pleading in it; "gazing" was closer to the truth—

"Please, just be quiet. I know you're embarrassed and can't back down now, so at least for now, just behave..."

"I'm not spouting off stuff I don't mean out of spite, and there's no way I've got the nerve to blurt out some hollow nonsense I don't mean in a scene like this in the first place!"

To Emilia, who wouldn't believe a word of his earlier declaration, Subaru raised his voice and pressed on. Even Emilia couldn't keep up her hardened stance against an attitude like that.

Perhaps reading the pause in the conversation from Emilia falling silent, the figure on the dais who had kept his own counsel until now — Miklotov, that is — gave a small cough,

"That gentleman's resolve seems quite firm, it would appear. Hmm, and what might Margrave Roswaal's own view be on the matter?"

Stroking his beard at the exchange between the two, he turned his line of inquiry toward Roswaal. Roswaal, thus addressed, smiled with a suspicious sort of geniality and said, "We-ell, let's see-e-e,"

"Every one of the royal candidates has a knight they place their trust in at their side. Among them all, it's been a matter of concern that Emilia-sama alo-o-one lacked anyone in such a position. Me occupying that role myself is, well, a bit of an a-a-awkward fit, you understand."

"Right, right, exactly, exactly."

"Roswaal!"

Listening to that, Roswaal prefaced his next words with, "Bu-u-ut,"

"that's not to say i-i-it can be just anyone, mind you. If someone is going to call themselves a knight — the Number One Knight, no less, of someone who might one day become the ruler — the-e-en they'll need to show something to match the claim."

"Yes, yes, exactly, exactly."

"Roswaal!"

This time it was Emilia showing her agreement with Roswaal's opinion, while Subaru let his complaint spill out riding on the man's name.

Watching those two opposite reactions with evident, gleeful satisfaction, Roswaal turned his gaze up to the assembled elders of the Council of Wise Men,

"Qualification as a Number One Knight — loyalty toward one's lord, or else strength enough to protect that lord's person. Something that can clear the path for a lord striving to become ruler — without something of that so-o-ort—"

"—That alone will not be enough, Margrave Roswaal."

Whatever he'd intended to have Subaru do, a voice cut into Roswaal's sonorous speech. Roswaal closed one eye and turned, his yellow gaze alone swiveling around — and at the far end of that yellow gaze stood a purple-haired young man: Julius.

He, who alone amid the earlier exchange between Subaru and Emilia had kept his eyes lowered in thought, stepped forward, offered an elegant bow, and then said,

"My apologies for interrupting mid-discussion. But something has arisen that I absolutely must ask that man over there."

The "man," toward whom Julius turned his palm, was — who else — Subaru, of course.

Subaru, on the receiving end of the nomination, scowled at the interruption and squared off against the graceful young man's gesture. To begin with, Subaru held no good impression of him. Nor had anything happened in the course of the Royal Selection to improve that impression — to put it plainly, the man was someone he disliked.

Julius — a man Subaru couldn't shake the impression of as some stock, insufferably slick noble — took even that sour shift in Subaru's expression like nothing more than a passing breeze, and said,

"There's no need to be so wary. There's only one thing I wish to ask, and once that's settled, you may go on doing whatever it is you feel you must do."

What he said sounded almost considerate of Subaru's feelings, yet the expression with which he said it was deadly serious, and, unconsciously, Subaru's own face hardened to match, as if refusing to be outdone.

"What's wrong? You're making such a weird face. When the other person's being serious, isn't it rude to make a face like that?"

"Just now, I was wounded, deeply—!"

Wounded unintentionally by a remark with no malice behind it, Subaru nearly crumpled right there on the spot. But without so much as remarking on Subaru's reaction, Julius went on,

"A clownish performance doesn't suit this occasion. Not if you truly intend to call yourself Emilia-sama's knight."

"...And just what's that supposed to mean?"

"Exactly what it sounds like."

Closing one eye, Julius looked Subaru up and down. Subaru moved his hands as if to shield certain parts from that gaze, but the gesture was lost on most of the people present. The only one who got it was probably Al, who crouched down and shook his head as though he couldn't bear to watch.

He had a thing or two he wanted to say about his fellow countryman's attitude, but more than that, right now he was preoccupied with the intent of the man standing in front of him.

"Sorry, but I'm not exactly quick on the uptake. Back home, I flunked my reading-the-air exam with a flat E. So spell it out plain and simple for me."

Picking at his ear, Subaru answered Julius with poor manners. Julius didn't touch on the rudeness, merely brushing a hand through his bangs with an elegant motion that somehow grated on the nerves, and said,

"Are you aware, I wonder? Just now, you declared yourself a knight — and you did so, with all due reverence, in a place where the full ranks of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica's Royal Guard stand assembled."

Spreading his hands, Julius spoke as though representing the ranks of knights standing behind him.

At Julius's words, the assembled knights all straightened at once, and in a single unbroken motion stamped the floor and raised their swords in salute.

Overwhelmed despite himself by the force of it, Subaru leaned back and, out of pure sore-loser spite, said,

"Tch... hard to believe these are the same guys who were spooked out of their minds just by the way Emilia-tan was walking earlier."

"You go too far, my friend. That knights pay heed even to trivial matters is because they understand the preciousness and meaning of all that must be protected. Do you have the resolve to do the same?"

Subaru's sore-loser jab had nearly wounded the knights' dignity, but with an exquisitely timed save, Julius overwrote it into something that sounded almost like a heartwarming story.

It didn't sit well, being talked circles around like that, but more than that, his question carried a deeper weight.

Before the assembled knights, Subaru had declared himself a knight.

With the dignity of the Royal Guard at his back, Julius, shouldering that weight, was asking after the true meaning — the resolve — behind Subaru's claiming that title.

In short, Subaru was, right now, being tested.

—Yes, the depth of his feelings for Emilia!

"Why are you staring at me like that right now?"

"No, just re-confirming my feelings, and priming the detonator of resolve so my knees don't shake."

Emilia knit her brow as if she had no idea what that meant, but even so, she couldn't hide her worried air as she watched Subaru face down Julius.

Meaning to set her mind at ease, Subaru thumped his own chest, coughed lightly, and turned back to face Julius, who had posed the question,

"*Cough*, in other words, you're... *gack*. My— *hack*—resolve..."

"Take your time; there's no rush. Unfortunately, there's no water prepared here."

To Subaru, who'd unexpectedly done damage to his own windpipe, Julius granted him a grace period without so much as an exasperated look. Gratefully accepting the reprieve handed to him, he rubbed his throat to stall for time while running a simulation of the exchange in his head.

Given that Subaru was now known to everyone present as an acquaintance of Emilia's, whether he raised her worth or dragged it down all hinged on what he did from here on.

Given the palpable sense that his emotions had already run ahead of him and likely cost her some points in the eyes of those watching, he needed to be all the more careful in the comeback from here.

Emilia, of all people, had gone so far as to do something that didn't suit her at all — flaunting her power and voicing her own opinion — in order to see this scene through to the end.

There was no way Subaru, of all people, could be the one to trip her up now.

Not when all he wanted, single-mindedly, was to keep on being the best thing for her.

Well then — hardening his resolve, Subaru cleared his throat and put an end to his stalling. He slapped both cheeks to psych himself up, staggering slightly from having used too much force, and turned back to face Julius.

Julius, waiting on Subaru's move, stood with unshaken composure, resting a hand at his head as if smoothing hair that had no chance of being mussed, despite there being no breeze indoors at all.

As if to counter that graceful bearing, Subaru gave a small hop, spread his feet into a front-back stance, snapped the fingers of both hands, and leveled a pair of finger-guns at his opponent,

"Alright, I'll answer you nice and sharp, crisp and proper. Oh, and sorry to keep you waiting."

At Subaru charging in with all that energy only to immediately stall out, Julius knit his brow for an instant, but then, perhaps fearing he'd get dragged into Subaru's pace, closed his eyes and shook his head,

"No, never mind. Then, shall we resume the question and answer? I ask, you respond. — Whether you are fit to be a knight; whether you possess the qualification to call yourself one before us."

The question was a rehash of before — no, more than that: the knights standing in ranks behind him, too, had had their desire for an answer grow all the stronger for the time they'd been made to wait.

Tasting something like the illusion of a raging windstorm in the intensified pressure bearing down on him, Subaru wet his lips, which had begun to go dry, with his tongue,

"I know full well my own strength doesn't measure up. I can't even swing a sword properly, and my magic's below beginner level. I'm no financial wizard either, and I've got nothing to do with some secret hidden power. Stack me up against the conditions Roswaal listed earlier, and I fall way, way short."

Lacking in strength, lacking in all sorts of ways — he was well aware of every bit of it.

Throwing a tantrum wasn't going to change reality. Natsuki Subaru was, sadly enough, a thoroughly ordinary person, with nothing particular to distinguish him besides hailing from another world.

He knew all too well that none of the abilities he'd cultivated over the course of his life so far would serve as even a single spark of light to illuminate Emilia's path forward.

At that answer, a faint trace of bewilderment surfaced within Julius's composed features.

Naturally so. Here was a man who, having finally worked up to it, had declared himself in this place — and the very same man now sang his own incompetence at the top of his lungs, offering, in response to a clear challenge, what looked for all the world like an easy raising of the white flag.

Anticlimax, or perhaps simple disappointment, was what rose in his eyes.

But—

"But there was that talk about loyalty, devotion, all that. Yeah, sure, my strength's nowhere near enough, laughably so. But — even so."

He turned to look back at Emilia, standing behind him.

She had been watching Subaru with worry — what did she think, on receiving that glance? She only pressed her lips together, wearing an expression that looked either angry or on the verge of tears, and waited in silence for Subaru's next words.

Drawing courage from the sight of her, Subaru turned back to face Julius. And then,

"I don't think it's really the word 'loyalty,' but — I want to make Emilia-t...Emilia-sama the ruler. No — I will make her the ruler. By no one else's hand but my own."

"...Don't you think that's an awfully arrogant answer, yourself?"

At Subaru's words, Julius sighed as if he'd just been told some pipe dream,

"You lament your own lack of strength, and you're aware of your insufficiency in every respect. And in this very place, you weren't even granted the right to speak in the first place. And yet you, of all people, mean to touch the very core of a matter of state? — Isn't that simply conceit?"

"Listen," Julius said, holding up a finger, continuing as though lecturing the silent Subaru.

"A person has, from birth, what is called their station. One might also call it their Vessel. A person cannot obtain anything beyond their own Vessel — nor should they even seek to. The honor of 'knighthood,' which you spoke of so carelessly, is no exception."

Julius unfastened the knight's sword at his waist and struck the tip of its scabbard against the floor, letting the sound ring out. A hard, sharp report echoed through the hall, and an instant later, the ranks of knights behind him struck the same note in turn. That overlapping percussion lent Julius's gesture support, and with the knights' cooperation behind him, he said,

"What is demanded of a knight is loyalty to one's lord and to the kingdom. And strength enough to protect to the last whatever one holds dear. Both are indispensable. Neither can ever be spared if one is to call oneself a knight. But I believe there is something else, beyond these, that matters as well."

"—"

To Julius's words, phrased almost like a question, Subaru answered only with silence.

Julius, too, wasn't really seeking an answer. He simply wanted to voice his own thinking. In fact, before Subaru could answer anything at all, he shook his head and went on.

"What I believe is indispensable to a knight — is history."

"History...?"

The one who echoed back, tilting her head, the word he'd spoken so resonantly, was Emilia. Julius answered her questioning voice with a reverent "Indeed," and continued,

"I bear the name of House Juukulius, a family of counts who have served the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica for generations. We who hold titles carry the pride of having supported and protected this nation, unbroken, through the years."

With a sweep of his arm, sharp enough to snap the fabric of his sleeve, he gestured behind himself.

At his gesture, the knights standing in ranks behind him lifted their heads proudly and stamped their feet to show their assent.

"No one of uncertain origin is ever recommended into the Royal Guard. This isn't born of some exclusionary obsession with bloodline — it's because a certain, verifiable lineage speaks to the weight of a history spent serving the kingdom. It is that accumulated history, and nothing else, that upholds our pride as knights."

Therefore, he went on,

"Here in Lugunica, I do not recognize you, whose very origin is uncertain, nor the man who calls himself Al, as knights. Much less as anyone fit to be the Number One Knight in service to a future ruler."

"That's not something the person themselves can do anything about...!"

"Just so. Which is why I said, did I not? That a person has, from birth, their station. That includes even the house one is born into. People cannot, from birth, be made equal."

To Subaru's voice, wrung out as if squeezed from him, Julius answered with the sort of flat certainty only too characteristic of a noble.

An unbridgeable gulf of values lay stretched between the two of them, and the other man's rejection stood far, far too distant for any voice to leap across it and land.

Even so.

Even so.

"I'm going to make Emilia the ruler."

"—Still saying it? That position is far too lofty for you to reach. If the weight of your blood is lacking, you don't have the strength either, not like the man standing beside Priscilla-sama."

As if scoffing at Subaru's mutter, Julius indicated the jet-black helmet with a tilt of his head. Al, on the receiving end of that, kept up an air of having nothing to do with any of it, muttering, "Don't drag me into this."

Even glancing sidelong at his fellow countryman's cold-hearted attitude, Subaru's answer didn't change.

"Even so, I'm going to make Emilia the ruler."

"You—"

"If you're saying I don't have the qualifications of a knight, then fine, I guess that's how it is. Like I said before, I'm someone with a lot of pieces missing. Before we even get to whether I'm worthy of the honor of 'knight,' I'm well aware there's plenty wrong with me just as a person."

But even a self that fell short, he wanted at least the feeling of caring for someone to be genuine.

A self lacking as a knight, a self too weak to reach — and yet the wish itself was real.

"Even so, I want to be a strength for Emilia. I want to be her greatest strength. I know that standing here as just a retainer isn't enough. If I want to stand here, hold my head up, and be a strength for her — then I guess I have to be a 'knight.' So—"

He lifted his head and looked Julius straight in the eye.

Fine features, a valiant Royal Guard uniform, a finely wrought knight's sword, a bearing of stately confidence.

He was, precisely, the very image of a knight straight out of a story.

Against him stood Subaru, in servant's clothes that felt faintly soiled from crawling around here and there, with a face whose shifty-eyed look was about as far from refined and dashing as could be. Let his guard down for a second and his back slouched, and rather than any fine sword, he didn't even have a bamboo practice sword on hand — bare-handed all the way.

The place he wished to reach was, impossibly, far away. But—

"If that's what it takes for this to mean anything, then I'll be a 'knight.'"

He had no idea how to go about it, and there was so much lacking that he couldn't even pin down what exactly was missing. But even so — because what he wanted was now settled.

"If I have to be a knight to stand at her side, then I'll become one. That's the whole of my answer."

"Why do you wish, that badly, to stand in that place?"

Had he given up on persuading Subaru with words by now?

Julius closed his eyes, and with a small shake of his head, questioned Subaru about the very root of his conduct. What was it he wanted, badly enough to go this far?

Subaru caught his breath, feeling the strong weight of a gaze at his back.

Emilia — the silver-haired girl standing behind him — that girl was watching.

He couldn't turn around. He didn't have the courage.

But at least he could feel her presence at his back, and so, hesitating,

"—Because she's special to me."

was his answer.

At that answer, Julius murmured the same words quietly under his breath. Then, as though he had reached some conclusion, he gave a faint nod,

"Incidentally, might I ask what that means?"

he asked, of all things.

Subaru had only one answer for that.

"—Not here. I don't want to say it here."

He wondered what exactly he thought he was doing, in front of so many watching eyes like this.

It was mortifying, plain and simple. His face burned, and he could tell even his ears had gone red.

And as for Julius, the cause of it all — he shrugged, whether satisfied or resigned, at Subaru's answer, one that was clear precisely because of how unclear it was, and said,

"Qualification or none, you've made me understand your reason for standing there. In that case, there's nothing more for me to say. — Except."

Turning his back to Subaru, Julius made to return to the row where the candidates stood. But partway through, he stopped once, and turned his head alone back toward him,

"I still don't think I can recognize you as a 'knight,' after all."

"What are you—"

"I understand that you've settled on someone you wish to protect, someone you hold dear. But that way of thinking of yours... no — piling on more words wouldn't be graceful."

Shaking his head, Julius looked at Subaru, who wouldn't let it go, with something like pity in his eyes. And then,

"Whatever makes the person you wish to stand beside wear a face like that — that is not a 'knight.'"

The words, once spoken, were gentle — but the meaning packed into them was more scathing than anything he'd said until now.

Subaru felt something almost like a chill run through him, and fearfully glanced behind him. Emilia stood there, and he had no way of knowing what expression she'd worn while listening to what had just been said.

He simply couldn't bring himself to turn and face her — it was too frightening. Her silence left his mind spinning nothing but bad imaginings about what state of heart she'd listened to Subaru's words in.

"K-knight this, knight that — quite the mouth on you, Mr. Knight among Knights (lol)."

So the next thing to come out of Subaru's mouth was nothing but a trembling bit of sore-loser bluster.

No doubt because that much was obvious, Julius's footsteps, carrying him further away, didn't stop. Subaru's words just now weren't worth enough to make him stop.

Feeling as though he'd been judged exactly that, Subaru pressed on all the faster,

"Sure, they're propping you up as the Knight among Knights around here, but out in the world, that title of Knight among Knights already belongs to someone else. ...You think words from a guy like that are gonna scare me?"

"Natsuki Subaru, was it? You should know that speaking words that carelessly belittle others wounds not only your own worth, but the worth of those around you as well."

To Subaru's cheap provocation, Julius responded flatly, without so much as a flare of temper.

By now he had already returned to the row of candidates, settling in at the side of his liege, Anastasia. Neither he nor his lord felt the slightest discomfort standing there.

Looking straight at the two of them standing side by side, and at the two other pairs of candidates doing the same, Subaru felt the blood drain from his head, and it came with an ache.

"Natsuki Subaru. — That is not beautiful."

Summing up the whole of everything Subaru had said and done up to that point, Julius said this.

With just that one line alone, Subaru understood the fact that he himself had dragged his own conduct down to its absolute lowest.

Looking around, even the row of candidates turned cold, disenchanted eyes toward him.

From the ranks of the Royal Guard standing further behind them, no doubt because of the rude remark aimed at Julius, their representative, many directed something close to outright hostility his way.

From the ranks of civil officials arrayed across the way, too, there was no trace of goodwill toward Subaru, capable as he was of nothing but emotional outbursts, and as for the Council of Wise Men behind him, he had no nerve left right now even to turn and look.

To make an enemy of the entire world, if that's what it took, and still stand on Emilia's side.

That kind of resolve, that kind of strength — surely he'd had it, at least for one instant, up until just moments ago.

And yet now, Subaru's body had gone rigid, and he couldn't even move from where he stood.

Far from making an enemy of the whole world, all it had taken was making enemies of the mere hundred-odd people in this hall, and the flame of his resolve had already flickered — that was the self he was right now.

That was so pathetic, so unsightly, that the backs of his eyes went hot.

Even so — even in a state like this, even in a situation where everyone around him had turned into an enemy — even so, if only, at the very least, she alone would stay on his side.

—But,

"That's enough now, Subaru."

Before Subaru could make up his mind to turn and face her, a voice like a silver bell rang out, circling around to him.

At the touch on his shoulder, Subaru was startled by the fact that he'd flinched badly enough to want to look away from himself. But Emilia, reaching a hand out to him, didn't touch on that pitiful display in the slightest,

"My apologies for taking up your time needlessly. I'll have him withdraw at once."

Tugging Subaru's sleeve as she said this, Emilia bowed her head to the Council of Wise Men.

Being written off, just like that, as "needless time," carved into Subaru's heart like a sharp blade. And yet he had no grounds to object.

His resolve, his determination, all of it — there was no denying he'd trampled it all himself.

Dragged by the arm, unable to resist, Subaru was pulled down off the stage. He still couldn't bring himself to look at Emilia's face as she walked ahead, pulling his arm along.

Yet the fact that he couldn't sense even so much as an angry, passionate flare from her, given her stubborn refusal even to look his way, felt like proof of the utter futility of what he'd done, and it was painful.

To that retreating back,

"There were parts of it, at least, that I could judge to have been a worthwhile use of time, Emilia-sama."

From the dais came Miklotov's rasping, and yet somehow carrying, voice.

To the pair who didn't stop walking, Miklotov continued,

"He, at least, has shown that you are not the Half-Elf the world fears you to be. — You have yourself a fine retainer."

"—Subaru is"

Her feet stopped. Emilia turned back.

The line of her gaze went to the Council of Wise Men on the dais; Subaru, standing right beside her, wasn't even at the edge of her field of view. And yet Subaru could see her turned-back face with perfect clarity.

That face wore eyes cold, frozen of all feeling, and, as if severing something cleanly away, in that clear, silver-bell voice, she said,

"He is not, my retainer."

Clearly, plainly, she rejected everything Subaru had said up until then.

* * *

Wandering unsteadily down the corridor outside the hall, Subaru was at a complete loss.

He didn't remember what he'd looked like after making such a spectacle of himself, next to Emilia, in front of so many. He only remembered that the Knight-Captain had suggested he leave the room, and that Emilia, offering neither agreement nor objection, had left the decision up to him.

Entrusting that choice to Subaru had been the last kindness Emilia had shown him.

Subaru, who had gone around calling himself her acquaintance only to make such a spectacle of himself besides — surely she understood, painfully well, that his very existence could only be a liability to her now.

And yet she hadn't put her blame of Subaru into words, and if he had chosen to stay, she probably would have respected that choice too.

Precisely because of that, there was no way Subaru could have kept standing in that place.

Not because he couldn't bear the mortification over the foolishness of what he'd done. It was because he wanted to spare Emilia any more trouble than he'd already caused—

"No, that's just an excuse too."

Not wanting to hold her back — that was nothing more than a convenient excuse. The root of the matter was far simpler, and far more hopeless than that.

Simply put, he just couldn't stand being looked at by Emilia with those cold eyes any longer, that was all.

"What the hell did I even cross a dangerous bridge and come all the way out here for, idiot that I am."

After causing trouble here, there, and everywhere, after being saved even by strokes of luck bordering on the miraculous in his encounters, after everything it took to finally arrive at the royal castle — what Subaru had done was become a grand, sprawling obstacle in Emilia's path.

He'd come to this place to protect her from anyone who might do exactly that to her. His resolve alone had been admirable, but the result was a wreck to look at — the sort of thing where it would've been better not to have tried at all.

He was disgusted with himself. Even though it had been obvious all along.

"Is something the matter?"

Catching Subaru's self-deprecating mutter, the knight walking beside him spoke up, his voice tinged with concern and confusion.

He was the knight escorting Subaru on the way from the hall back to the waiting room. Unlike the ones who had been inside, this man, having been stationed outside the room, hadn't witnessed Subaru's folly. As a result, his manner still carried a measure of respect owed to those involved in the Royal Selection.

Being treated with such courtesy struck Subaru, in his current state, as absurd — and was, in fact, part of why the corner of his mouth twisted into an unintended, crooked shape.

"Nah, it's nothing. I mean, uh, sorry about this. You're in the middle of important work and I'm making you do extra stuff on top of it."

"Not at all. Everyone here knows that what's happening in the throne room right now is a matter of grave importance, one that will decide this nation's future. Only a handful, even among the knights, were permitted inside, but keeping watch outside and guiding dignitaries are both essential duties of ours as well."

Stated so plainly and clearly, it was Subaru, instead, who was left flustered.

Honestly, in his current state of mind, what he really wanted was to shove away even being held up like that by this man. But to someone who hadn't witnessed what had happened, Subaru pushing him away now with a harsh tone would be nothing more than a tantrum. It wouldn't even qualify as taking it out on the right person.

Right now, this man took pride in the fact that his own actions were part of the Royal Selection.

And Subaru, by contrast? Could he take pride in his own conduct, in front of anyone at all?

Even if no one else had recognized him, he'd been rejected by the very person he'd most wanted recognition from.

"—Hm, what's this?"

Suddenly, a commotion brought to a halt Subaru's feet, sunk as they'd been in a sea of self-loathing.

Looking ahead, he saw, beyond the bend in the corridor, several raised voices going back and forth. As Subaru knit his brow suspiciously, the knight stepped forward as if to shield him,

"Please stand back, just to be safe. I doubt it's anything serious, but still."

"Oh, uh, right."

Following the words meant to look out for him, Subaru moved to the wall's edge and held his breath. Then, rounding the corner, the source of the commotion drew near.

It was a group of about six people. A young man clad in knight's armor led the way, guiding the group behind him. The group at the rear appeared to be restraining a person at their center — amid the knights, uniform in their dress, the figure in the middle stood out as clearly out of place.

"What happened?"

Judging it not to be an urgent situation, the knight accompanying Subaru called out to the one leading the group. Noticing him, that knight replied, his face somewhat tense,

"What happened, he asks — a suspicious individual who tried to sneak into the castle. And a bit of a troublesome one, at that."

"A suspicious individual...? Then why are you parading him through the castle? Wouldn't it be better to take him quietly to the barracks?"

"I said troublesome, didn't I. In any case, we need to get the Captain's orders on this."

Cutting the exchange short, the knight gave a brief bow toward Subaru before returning to leading the group onward. Their destination, it seemed, was the very hall where the Royal Selection had just been taking place — a place presumably still in the middle of matters of the highest national importance. What sort of situation could possibly warrant interrupting that?

Turning the overheard conversation over in his mind, Subaru glanced at the group. Surrounded by knights, the person said to have snuck into the castle was, at that very moment, passing right in front of Subaru's eyes.

Thinking that, with one wrong step, he himself could have ended up in that very same position, he felt a chill at just how reckless his own actions had been, to have done something so audacious.

Though that reflection was blown away instantly. Because—

"—Ah?"

Right before Subaru's stunned eyes, being marched along with his limbs restrained by four knights, was a bald-headed old man he recognized — Old Man Rom, whom he was supposed to have parted ways with in the noble district.
