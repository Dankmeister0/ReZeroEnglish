Arc 5 Chapter 3: "Their Respective Views"

"We kind of just went ahead and decided a bunch of stuff on our own — I feel bad for Otto-kun about that."

Returning to her room after the meeting concluded, Emilia let that slip as she offered Subaru a chair. Settling into it, Subaru gave a small laugh.

"Well, Otto's flustered fit is gonna be passed down through history, but basically I agreed with Emilia-tan's thinking too. If there's an uncertain factor, it's that we're charging in where they're probably sitting fully prepared and waiting."

"But it's hard to imagine Anastasia going out on such a dangerous limb when she deliberately sent an envoy like that. Even with Mimi-chan there, if she'd wanted to restrain Joshua-kun, she could have."

"Sending a knight's own kin as the envoy — that means we're properly recognized as an 'enemy.' You know, I used to watch those Warring States dramas back home and wonder, when someone in an important position gets sent as an envoy, 'why don't they just get cut down?' Turns out there's this whole backstory reason for it. Never thought I'd learn it firsthand."

Basically, it came down to a matter of mutual trust with the surrounding world. If word got out that someone had acted against honor, their standing would collapse instantly. No camp could afford to act with that kind of reputational ruin on the line — there were still too many enemies on every side. Or rather, the more a camp fattened itself up, the more potential enemies it accumulated, so it made sense that the more power one held, the more careful one had to be about backroom maneuvering.

—The meeting that had welcomed Joshua Juukulius as envoy was already over, and a languid night had settled over the new Roswaal Manor.

Since it seemed unkind to send them back the same day, Joshua and Mimi had been told they could stay the night, and the reply given regarding this visit's purpose — an invitation to the city of Priestella — had been accepted.
When the conversation had turned to the main subject, Joshua had worn the expression of someone putting on airs of importance, but everyone present had noticed the faint relief that crossed his face once he received a favorable answer. Probably the monocle was some accessory meant to alter the impression he gave others, but even accounting for that, the young man had a streak of being a bit too honest at heart.

"Well, that makes him a lot more likable than his big brother, at least."

"Subaru, you really can't be honest about anyone but Julius, can you. Still holding a grudge about getting hurt at the castle?"

Catching Subaru's mutter, Emilia teased him with an amused look.
There was a time when merely recalling it would flush his face with shame and send something hot and indignant churning through his stomach. But how was it now, after some time had passed?

"I'm not so far removed from it that I can laugh it off and talk about it, that's for sure. I was young back then. It's not that I haven't reflected on it. Though I'd like the same from his side too."

"But I heard at the commendation ceremony that both you and Julius apologized properly and made up. Just forgiving each other on the surface while still stewing underneath isn't cool, you know."

"Mmrgh... but I'm only human!"

Under Emilia's accusing gaze, Subaru still dug in stubbornly.
Emilia glared at his averted profile for a while, but before long she couldn't hold it in and burst out laughing.

"Yes, yes. Honestly, Subaru, you're such a stubborn hardhead. But you can't get into fights with Julius when you meet him in Priestella, okay? You've become a fine knight, and knights don't wield their strength recklessly."

"Heeey, no match for my mistress, I s'pose."

Playing the fool to hide his embarrassment, Subaru rubbed under his nose with a finger.
Then, letting his gaze wander idly around Emilia's room, he suddenly seemed to remember something.

"Oh yeah, Emilia-tan. I don't really know much about Priestella — is it famous, like, what kind of city is it?"

"Mmm, you need to study more, Subaru. Priestella is one of Lugunica's five great cities, right on the border, just across the Great Tigracy River from Kararagi. It's built in the middle of a big lake, and apparently it's famous for having waterways running all through the city."

"The way you say that like it's secondhand info is very you, but so it's one of those water cities, huh? Well, they managed it with old Venice, so I guess it's not impossible here either."

Back in his original world, when it came to a city of water, Venice was the famous one. Canals ran crisscross through the town in every direction, and the stone streetscape mingled naturally with the sight of water. It had been the kind of picturesque place Subaru had always thought he'd like to visit at least once, a spot brimming with romance.
So that was the impression Subaru formed from Emilia's explanation of Priestella. But—

"No, that's not right, Subaru. Priestella isn't a water city, it's a floodgate city."

"Floodgate?"

"Right. Since it's built in the middle of a lake, apparently depending on rainfall, huge amounts of water can flow into the city. To prevent that, they've covered the perimeter of the city with tall walls and placed a number of floodgates to regulate the water level. Those floodgates are supposed to be incredibly magnificent and famous, so it's called a floodgate city rather than a water city."

With Emilia's explanation, the image in Subaru's mind flipped completely — from a lovely city of water to a prison of water. Whatever charm the promise of a beautiful waterscape might have held was rather ruined once you added being surrounded by tall, tall walls.
Subaru tilted his head, wondering just why they'd built such a suggestively ominous structure in the first place.

"I'm not too sure myself, but there seem to be all sorts of theories about how the city came to be. Some say they wanted to challenge the limits of the era's technology, some say they wanted to overcome flood damage without relying on magic or dragons, and the wildest one is that it was built to seal away and finish off some evil witchbeast or something."

"None of those sound realistic, and yet the fact that they all seem faintly plausible is a real testament to human nature."

It wasn't the sort of idea an ordinary mind would easily arrive at, but the kind of people called geniuses simply didn't have the brakes that common sense installed in everyone else. It seemed entirely possible that such people would actually go and put an idea like that into practice.
In any case—

"Not being able to read what's on their minds hasn't changed one bit. ...I don't think they'd go out of their way to kindly tell us where to find what we're looking for out of pure good will."

"Really? Instead of just doubting everything, why not try trusting people's kindness a little more?"

"Unfortunately, every single one of the rival Royal Selection candidates has their quirks. And honestly, there isn't a single lord-and-retainer pairing among them whose intentions I'd call trustworthy."

The Crusch Camp — Crusch's character itself could be trusted, but there was no telling whether Crusch, having lost her memories, still maintained the good-natured young lady she'd been when they'd first met, and above all there was the part where he couldn't let his guard down about what Ferris might do. It would be fine if Wilhelm was reliably putting the brakes on things, but even that Sword Demon wasn't entirely free of his own uncertainties. Weighing all of that, it was hard to trust them unconditionally.

As for the Anastasia Camp, Anastasia's own movements and intentions remained unclear. Even this invitation had no visible true intent behind it. Even granting, a hundred times over, that Julius himself was a knight through and through, living by chivalry — the fact remained that Anastasia held the reins. Her private soldiers, the Iron Fang, were another matter too — the personalities of the members and their professional conduct were two different things. Not to be underestimated.

The Felt Camp — Reinhard and old man Rom might be worth trusting. But the crucial matter of Felt's own feelings remained unreadable. At the very least, as long as she was putting real effort into the Royal Selection, there was no way to face things without guarding against whatever that cunning, scrappily street-smart girl might be scheming. And if she ever moved Reinhard against them for some legitimate reason and turned hostile, standing up to that by force of arms would be a dream beyond dreams.

And then the Priscilla Camp — honestly, this was the hardest to read of all. The biggest factor was that, lord and retainer both, Subaru simply couldn't muster either trust or confidence in them. He and Al shared the same hometown, but the man was fiercely loyal to Priscilla regardless. That wouldn't buy Subaru any leniency, and Priscilla's own capriciousness was terrifying in itself. There was an unreasonableness to her that made it entirely plausible she'd come at you smiling and lop your head off.

In the end, even after roughly a year's time, none of the candidates had truly shown their hand to one another. To know them better than that time in the royal castle had allowed, a bit more direct engagement was probably necessary. In that sense too, accepting this invitation was a valid move.

"Honestly, I'm scared of owing Anastasia a favor. In the first place, where did they even learn that Emilia-tan wants the colorless mana crystal?"

"Since Puck got shown at the castle, it'd be a problem if people found out he can't be freely called on right now. So we should've been moving really carefully about it... but I guess once something gets into people's ears, there's no stopping it from slipping out of their mouths again."

"That's probably how it went, yeah. Even if Emilia-tan manages to get her hands on the mana crystal now, from the other camps' point of view it's just restoring things to how they were. All that's added is the debt we'd owe."

Still, once Puck came back, Emilia's fighting strength would be reinforced by however much she'd be able to use magic on her own again. That said, however much Emilia's personal prowess increased, it wouldn't much affect the overall battlefield of the Royal Selection. At best, it would only lend more credibility to the achievement — currently dismissed as tall tale — of having defeated the Great Rabbit.

—The subjugation, in the Sanctuary, of one of the Three Great Witchbeasts, the Great Rabbit.

Within the Emilia Camp, this achievement, separate from the defeat of the Witch Cult's "Sloth," had regrettably gone officially unrecognized so far.
The trouble was that no one had witnessed the actual moment the Great Rabbit was defeated, and the manner of its defeat made it impossible to present its corpse as proof.

It had been blown into another dimension, sent off somewhere not of this world.
That, in short, was how the Great Rabbit had met its end, but telling that story with a straight face rarely earned belief. After all, the Al Shamak that Beatrice had used was a spell already lost to the present age, and with Beatrice herself short on mana, there was no way to demonstrate it either.

As a result, though the defeat of the Great Rabbit had been reported to the royal capital, its recognition as an achievement remained shelved. To explain the details would naturally require touching on the details of the Sanctuary as well, which would expose the fact that Roswaal had been harboring a hidden settlement within his domain that he hadn't reported. In the end, they'd had no choice but to give up pressing the claim forcefully.

Apparently, if no further sighting of the Great Rabbit was confirmed over a span of a decade or so, the credibility of this report would come up for reconsideration — but by the time the achievement was recognized at that point, it would frankly be well past too late.
Since it was an achievement that had fallen into her lap out of nowhere, though, Emilia didn't seem to care all that much about it.

"Even so, it's still frustrating, or — man, how much is enough. Damn it, how many times did that monster rabbit make me suffer..."

"But whether or not anyone believes us, it's still true that we beat the Great Rabbit. And thanks to that, no one has to get hurt by such a scary witchbeast anymore, so isn't that enough?"

"Emilia-tan, you're too kind and too positive, man..."

If you do the right thing, you want it properly recognized.
Talking with Emilia, who showed such an open-hearted attitude, Subaru couldn't help but be made aware of his own smallness. If only he could actually think the way Emilia said. But in reality, Subaru just couldn't manage that.
Specifically, being denied the recognition he deserved made his chest boil with irritation regardless.

Watching Subaru's sulking face, Emilia's lips softened.
Sometimes Emilia's eyes held a gentle light as she looked at him this way, though Subaru never noticed. Nor did he notice that her expression at such times carried some complicated, indescribable emotion, different from a mother watching over her child or a maternal look toward someone younger.

"Besides, when it comes to achievements, what you worked hard for has been recognized, hasn't it? Defeating the White Whale, and beating the Witch Cult's 'Sloth' — that was all properly acknowledged at the commendation ceremony."

"That was... in my mind it's still strongly just 'got a piece of the leftovers.' With the White Whale, there were people more desperate about it than me who got there first, and I just swooped in and took the good part right at the very end. As for 'Sloth,' I wasn't even thinking that way at all."

The fight against Petelgeuse had been, purely and simply, out of a desire to protect Emilia.
No — even that wasn't quite an accurate view of his own heart. In truth, Subaru's feelings toward Petelgeuse had been a mix of both the desire to protect Emilia and personal hatred toward Petelgeuse as an individual.
It wasn't a matter of which had won out. Both had been Subaru's genuine feelings, both were self-interested.
And so, having the outcome of a fight so close to personal vendetta praised as being for the sake of the world and its people sat uneasily with him.

"It's the same with the Great Rabbit thing, same as how you feel about it. Two witchbeasts that had troubled the world for four hundred years, both defeated one after another in such a short span... it's not really my place to say, but it does seem awfully convenient."

"Yeah, that's true. That I'm involved in both of them too — even I think it's a bit too convenient, honestly. I hope I don't run into the last one."

"...Yeah, that's right."

Subaru, believing there was power in words spoken aloud, prayed with all his heart that they wouldn't cross paths with the remaining one — the so-called "Black Serpent." But Emilia's answer to that was somehow stiff.
It was as if she had some thoughts of her own about the Black Serpent.

"So, about Priestella—"

But before he could press further on that, the subject was changed.
When Emilia took that kind of tone, it was her quiet way of signaling she didn't want to talk about something. Having learned a little by now, Subaru made a point of not trying to force the conversation when that happened.
Though every now and then he forgot that consideration and showed the same lack of tact as before.

"Accepting the invitation is settled, but are you sure it's fine to go with just the members from earlier? I'm planning to consult with Roswaal about it later too."

"I think that's basically fine. Emilia-tan's a lock, and obviously your knight — me — and your partner Bea-ko will come along. Then, being serious for a second, Garf comes as combat strength, and Otto's tagging along too, weirdly eager about it. Honestly, if Petra or Frederica could come too, we wouldn't have to leave Emilia-tan wanting for anything, but..."

"It can't be helped. Roswaal's going to be busy with the meeting of the western territory lords. Petra coming along for her studies was decided a long time ago. Though Petra was really disappointed about it."

"Petra's dislike of Roswaal is dyed-in-the-wool, thanks to the whole Sanctuary business. Ram doesn't say anything about it because Roswaal finds it amusing, but it makes me a little nervous, honestly."

As both a maid and a young girl, Petra was growing up properly and sturdily, but at her core there still remained a fragile immaturity. It showed most notably in her harsh attitude toward her master, Roswaal — it wouldn't be at all surprising if she were secretly wringing rag water into the tea she served him behind the scenes. Still, since Subaru sympathized with Petra on this emotionally, he was prepared to look the other way even if he witnessed something like that.

Once trust broke down that way, there was no way to restore it except over a long stretch of time. At the very least, a single year hadn't been enough for Petra to start listening to what Roswaal had to say.

"So then Frederica coming along as stopper and etiquette role model is basically decided, which means Ram's the one staying at the manor? Man, that makes me a little uneasy."

"I wonder. Since this meeting's going to have Anne there, Clind-san will be with her too, right? Clind-san would probably look after Petra kindly, so Frederica might not need to come along after all."

"Clind-san, huh... honestly, he's another one I can never quite figure out."

Subaru recalled the young man, the all-purpose butler of House Miload, whom they'd lodged with for a while before the manor's renovations.
The refined ferocity of his work was such that it fit the meaningless praise of "too fast for the eye to follow."

Incidentally, the parkour Subaru was gradually mastering technically had its foundational basics taught by Clind. While searching for some way to work on body control and movement techniques that wouldn't be too much for Subaru, who was physically no more than an ordinary man, he'd received Clind's instruction. After they'd moved to the new manor, Clind had accompanied Annerose on her occasional visits and had watched Subaru's subsequent practice a few times — and had proceeded to clear Garfiel's handmade obstacle course without disturbing so much as the hem of his clothes, without breaking a single drop of sweat. A monster.

"Well, regardless of who ends up staying at the manor, worrying about it is kind of pointless. More importantly, we're the ones who need to be careful. That goes for you too, Emilia-tan."

"Yeah. I'm sorry we decided without consulting anyone. I need to apologize properly to Otto-kun later too."

"He's not the type to care much about face, but he is the type to hold onto things. I'll have a word with him too. I'll tell him I scolded you until I cried."

"Fufu, thanks."

Emilia gave a faint smile at Subaru's mock gesture of raising a fist. Then she gently touched her chest, where a pendant hung with a blue crystal set into it.
That crystal was the temporary resting place where the great spirit Puck currently slept.

Far from being able to exercise his true powers, the stone didn't even have the function to allow communication. If Puck so much as stirred inside it, it would easily shatter and release his presence into the world — or so Emilia and Beatrice said.
If that happened, an uncontrolled Puck would bring great destruction to his surroundings, and eventually vanish back to his original place due to lack of mana.

To prevent that, Emilia's vast reserves of mana were constantly being poured into the crystal stone, sustaining its existence. All that remained was to process the colorless mana ore anchoring that existence and create a proper crystal stone for it — then he should be able to revive.
This time, what Anastasia had brought up was talk of mana ore capable of withstanding that use.

"If I can talk with Puck properly again... there's so much I want to say, so much I want to ask. So—"

Emilia fell silent, closing her eyes, and didn't put the rest into words.
Watching her long eyelashes tremble faintly, Subaru quietly scratched his head. He could only vaguely guess at what Emilia was thinking.

"Hurry up and get back here, cat spirit. I've got a mountain of complaints for you too, you know."

And so, as befit her knight, he voiced his agreement with her unspoken wish, laced with a bit of grumbling.

"I'll have you know! I say what I say with everyone's interests properly in mind!"

Slamming his glass down on the table, tonight's Otto Suwen was in a state.
Having finished his talk with Emilia and dinner, Subaru had visited Otto before his nightly routine and had been sitting through, for a while now, the endless griping of an inebriated administrator tipping back drink after drink.

"He's been like this the whole time. Even I'm gettin' my ears talked off, and that's sayin' somethin'."

The one saying so, making no effort to hide his exasperation, was Garfiel, sitting alongside Subaru listening to Otto's complaints. Digging his fingers into his short-cropped hair and scratching his head, he clacked his sharp fangs together as he sipped, almost licking, at his own glass — filled with milk.

Not letting the underage Garfiel drink alcohol was a policy Subaru maintained. Incidentally, this had the endorsement of both Frederica and Ram, and as far as Roswaal Manor was concerned, Garfiel wouldn't be permitted to drink until he turned twenty. Incidentally, Garfiel had once been goaded by a bad influence — namely Otto — into taking a sip of alcohol, and apparently, contrary to his temperament, he turned out to be hopelessly weak to drink.
Ever since, he'd developed enough of an aversion that even the sight of a liquor bottle made him grimace.

Of course, since Subaru had no intention of breaking the laws of his original world either, in practice the only ones who drank in the manor were Roswaal, Otto, Ram, and Frederica.
And in this room, right now, the only one drinking and rambling on was Otto.

"Don't get so sour about it. Even Emilia's reflecting on deciding this on her own without consulting anybody. Says she should've discussed it first. Though I don't think the outcome would've changed."

"Things aren't just about outcomes! Process matters too! It's not exactly rare for the conclusion of a discussion to already be decided before the discussion even happens, but the point is what path it takes to get there! This time especially, walking defenselessly right into their invitation... being played right into their hand — that's the problem!"

Against Subaru's attempts to smooth things over, Otto counterattacked fiercely, spraying spit as he went.
Every point he made was so reasonable there was no way to argue back, but listening to the content of it—

"Man, you really are a full-blown bureaucrat type through and through now... you resisted so hard when you first got roped into this. Guess it's a case of protesting too much means you secretly like it."

"That's the 'tsundere bottom' type, ain't it, roundabout as hell, Otto-nii."

"You two haven't changed one bit since the day I met you, have you, disgustingly enough!?"

Working in perfect tandem to needle Otto, Subaru and Garfiel slapped hands together. At Roswaal Manor, these three, being close in age and sharing a certain kind of friendship, often clustered together. Even the way the conversation flowed to settle into this shape now had become something of a set ritual.

Regardless of his own wishes about it, Otto had more than enough aptitude for the role of administrator. Having received a decent education as a merchant's son, and having grown worldly through his experience as a traveling peddler, he was, on top of that, calculating and quick-witted by nature — an excellent hire. He was surely being treated far better here than he would be if he'd ended up swindled somewhere and reduced to wandering the roads or falling into slavery.
Still, even while doing his paperwork, the man himself often tilted his head, muttering "this wasn't supposed to happen, was it..." A man reluctant to accept his fate.

By now he'd settled comfortably into the role of something like Roswaal's secretary and Emilia's aide, and he was thoroughly steeped in the administration of the Mathers frontier territory, to the point that there was truly no way for him to extract himself anymore.

"What's with that pitying look? You're looking at me like a bird about to have its neck wrung."

"This is more like the look you give a bird kept in a cage, allowed to live as long as it keeps laying eggs."

"That's even worse!?"

"Quit hollerin', you're gonna spill it. Ya shouldn't tease Otto-nii too much either, big man. Didn't we decide before, ten-Otto max per day?"

"What unit is that, exactly!? What in the world is 'ten-Otto' supposed to measure!?"

Otto shouted, his face flushed red, but Subaru and the others responded to it with silence.
This was exactly the problem once the drink started taking hold. These drinking sessions were held periodically for the sake of Otto, whose stress-prone job wore on him — but it was starting to seem like Otto's stress actually built up more during these sessions than otherwise.

"Well, for Otto, shouting like this is probably the best way to blow off steam."

"I refuse to accept that!"

"Yeah yeah, Otto-nii, just sit tight and pour yourself another. So, big man, there's somethin' I wanna check with you too."

"Oh? That's rare of you. Go ahead."

Grumbling under his breath, Otto refilled his glass with the drink he'd gone through and began sipping at it little by little.
Watching him out of the corner of his eye, Garfiel — mouth ringed white with milk — spoke up.

"Straight up, it's gotta be what the enemy's after this time, right? Up till now there ain't been so much as a skirmish with the other candidates, but now they're comin' at us head-on like this. Ain't no way they're not plannin' somethin'."

"So, to you, this feels basically like getting handed a formal duel challenge?"

"'Course it does. They gotta be serious about it too. Forget that scrawny Joshua fella — you saw that cat-girl standin' next to him, right?"

—That girl you keep calling "girl" is the same age as you, you know?
Subaru held back the retort as pointless, but wondered what exactly Mimi had done. To his eyes, Mimi had just looked like her usual self, innocently reaching for tea and sweets the whole time.
Dinner had gone the same way, too, as far as he could tell.

"That little runt, she looks like that but she's plenty strong. And it wasn't just in that room, neither — durin' dinner in the dining hall, she kept starin' me down the whole damn time. No doubt about it, she'd figured out I was the strongest fighter in the room."

"Is that so...? I mean, sure, Mimi being pretty strong is true, and the fact that she's got a subtle battle-junkie streak is pretty obvious too."

She hardly seemed like the type of clever character who could keep such scheming hidden in her belly. If anything, to Subaru she seemed like an open book, frankly a bit of an airhead.

"At the very least, while we're at the manor I'm gonna stay on guard. Once we're over there, if you can manage it, better to avoid you and Lady Emilia goin' off alone. Otto-nii's one thing, but if you two go down there's no comin' back from that."

"For your information, if I disappear this territory's finished too, you know!? Could you two show a little more appreciation and treat me with a bit more care, honestly!"

Though Garfiel urged caution, he wasn't belittling Otto.
He was simply impressing upon Subaru, in the strongest terms he could manage, the need for wariness. Of course, using that as an excuse to keep messing with Otto wasn't off the table either.

"On that point, I can't deny you're a bit unreliable. I won't go on and on about it, but I'm counting on you, Garfiel."

"Yeah, count on me all ya want. Formerly the 'Ultimate Shield,' now goin' by 'Legend of the Guardian' — Garfiel Tinzel!"

Pointing sharply at himself with his thumb, Garfiel declared this with pride, and Subaru nodded along.
Then, sipping the milk in his own glass, he reflected that Garfiel's title might be a bit too cool.
Someday, when Garfiel achieved feats of valor that rang out across the whole kingdom, would he be able to come up with a title to top the current one? Could he really put that much faith in whatever inspiration struck him at the time?

"Whether that same kind of sparkle I had for 'Invisible Providence' will strike me again... how many times the goddess smiles on me is completely up to her whims, man."

"You're thinkin' too hard about it, big man. But you don't need to fret over it. When push comes to shove, you're a man who gets it done. I trust you on that, at least."

Like Emilia, the look of trust from Garfiel carried a fairly strong compulsive force of its own. It naturally made him feel he needed to put in effort worthy of it.
Of course, he didn't intend to mistake charging forward recklessly without a fixed goal as the best way to answer that trust.

"Well, with Garfiel on board, our camp should at least be safe on the strength front for now. Emilia-tan's plenty formidable as an individual fighter too, and with Bea-ko I'm decent enough myself. If there's a problem, it's Otto — you sure you're really coming along?"

"Of course I am! If I don't go, who knows what kind of ridiculous discussion Lady Emilia and Natsuki-san will end up walking away from — I won't get a moment's peace!"

To be trusted this little in matters of negotiation was almost refreshing.
Emilia was exactly as she appeared — honest and pure — and Subaru, despite his bad personality and stubbornness, wasn't especially worldly either. From Otto's perspective, being seen as easy marks was probably only natural.

"Besides, speaking of Priestella, it's also the birthplace of the founder of Kararagi, once known by the name 'Hoshin of the Wilderness.' Being right on the border, adjacent to Kararagi, makes it an extremely significant place for merchants. I'd been thinking I ought to visit at least once myself."

"You already washed your hands of the merchant business ages ago, though. What are you gonna do there now?"

"For your information, don't think for a second that I intend to remain content as an administrator here forever! My ultimate goal remains becoming a great merchant with a shop of my own! Being here is a necessary detour — a necessary detour on the way to my destination!"

"That detour might just end up bein' the place ya spend the rest of your life, though, who knows."

The whole "washing his hands of it" business wasn't very realistic, but between the pull of a place he'd long admired and his role assisting negotiations as administrator, there was nothing but legitimacy to Otto's coming along.
Whatever else was said, everyone in this room — no, everyone at Roswaal Manor — understood that things wouldn't run smoothly without Otto. And precisely because Otto knew he was valued that way, he stayed put even in a situation that, on the surface, looked like poor treatment.

"Well, there's also the possibility you've just got a masochistic streak, but let's set that aside."

"I feel like I'm being agreed with in a somehow very rude way, but maybe that's just my imagination!?"

"It's not that big a deal. With the capable Anastasia coming, there's no telling what kind of unfavorable terms she might bring up. I'm counting on you too. Otto for the pen, Garfiel for the sword. And I'm in charge of comic relief."

"Try harder than that!!"

There was such a thing as the right person for the right job.
No matter how hard Subaru trained himself to death from now on, he'd never become stronger than Garfiel, and no matter how hard he studied himself to death, he'd never catch up to being as useful an administrator as Otto.

"I'll do what I can do, that's my motto. I'll have a thorough talk with Bea-ko about that and handle it proactively and appropriately, let's leave it at that."

"Realistically, with Lady Emilia and Beatrice around, big man'll be fine for the most part. In that case, guess I got no choice but to cover for Otto-nii after all. Take care of yourself."

"Why do I get the feeling I'm being treated like the biggest liability here... I can't accept this."

Subaru, brazening it out, and Garfiel, acting like he'd taken on a babysitting job. Otto grumbled resentfully, sipping at his drink little by little.
And so, as the night deepened at a comfortable pace—

"Well, tomorrow's probably gonna be busy too, so I'll head out now. What about you, Garfiel?"

"I'm gonna drink with Otto-nii a bit longer. Or rather, I figure it's about time I scored a win on the shatranj board. Might manage it now while he's drunk."

Leaving Subaru, who stood up, aside, Garfiel pulled out game pieces and a board from the back of the room. It was a board game called shatranj, with rules close to shogi or chess. Subaru had been struck with admiration that this sort of game existed in every world alike.
As it happened, Otto was quite strong at this kind of match, and though Garfiel kept getting fired up about it, he apparently just kept racking up losses. Incidentally, Subaru had an uncanny talent for Othello, but the moment it came to shogi or chess, he turned hopeless.

"Don't stay up too late. Won't grow any taller."

"Hey, I been told that before and I been doin' what they said, but does it actually work? Feels like I ain't grown much at all this past year."

"In your case, part of that probably got sucked away by Frederica, so who knows."

"That damn sister!"

Garfiel bared his fangs and made a fuss, setting the shatranj board down roughly on the table. And yet right after, he hunched his back and started carefully lining up the small pieces — quite the drop in tone.
Watching him from behind, Subaru raised a hand toward the red-faced Otto.

"Otto, don't drink too much either. If you're useless from a hangover, Petra's gonna end up despising you for real."

"That girl's been kind of harsh toward me lately, I feel — or is that just my imagination? Would you put in a word for me with her, Natsuki-san?"

"You mean your approach is too soft?"

"Could you please ask her to go a little softer on me instead!?"

That's an impossible request, Subaru answered with a wry smile, and left the room behind the two of them glaring at each other across the game board.
From the mage-crystal clock installed partway down the hallway, he could tell the date was about to change. Normally this would be the time he'd be getting into bed in his own room, but—

"Guess it's a little later than usual tonight."

Muttering that excuse-like remark, Subaru didn't climb the stairs to the third floor of the east wing where his room was, but instead headed for the west wing, where the rooms of the women slept in a row.
And then—

"—Pardon the intrusion."

Before entering that room, Subaru always knocked on the door without fail.
He knew there'd be no answer. Even so, perhaps he couldn't help but hold onto some hope.
Or perhaps, by confirming there was no answer, he was making sure not to forget.

—Making sure not to forget the heat of the flame that burned ceaselessly within his chest.

"――――"

Opening the door, he was greeted by a room that had gone completely dark after sunset.
A modest room. Its layout was no different from any of the countless guest rooms in the manor, and if anything, the furnishings placed in it were noticeably sparser. A bed in the center of the room, curtains on the window, and a small, simple table — with a vase of flowers arranged on it the only modest touch of care.
Even knowing that no one would ever complain about it, Subaru disliked the attitude of prioritizing only the practical, purely functional side of things above all else.

Call it sentimentality, call it excessive emotionalism, he still couldn't help wanting human warmth. The day he could dismiss that as weakness might never come for Subaru, not in his whole life.
When he'd confessed this state of mind to the women close to him—

*"If you were the kind of person who could cut things off so cleanly, I don't think — no matter how many times you and I fought — we could ever have understood each other. So I really, really like Subaru just the way he is."*

*"Wanting more than you have is a bad habit, I suppose. If it were just Subaru alone, it'd be hopeless no matter what. ...So since you're not alone anymore now, I suppose we'll manage to do something about it, even if you're greedy."*

"Man, I'm getting spoiled. And also, Emilia-tan, you say way too many suggestive things — it messes with my head."

He wished she wouldn't say things like "like" or "cool" so casually.
Even though Subaru had made his feelings plain, Emilia's emotional growth hadn't caught up to match it yet. In terms of romantic progress between a man and a woman, the two of them hadn't advanced anywhere at all.
Though honestly, even if things suddenly did progress, Subaru's own heart wasn't prepared for it either. Two years, or at least three — no, if possible, even longer — that was the extent of his spinelessness on the matter.

"Man, and here I am coming to your room and going on and on about Emilia and Beatrice — how rude of me. If Petra heard this, she'd really lay into me."

Depending on how you looked at it, Petra might well be, for her age, considerably ahead of everyone else in the manor when it came to matters of romantic subtlety. One way or another, when it came to being bad at love — or rather, when devotion taken too far turns to poison — starting with Roswaal, the people of this manor all seemed to have their weak points there. Garfiel's feelings for Ram hadn't advanced past a middle-schooler's level, and Subaru wasn't exactly one to talk either. Ram's excessive loyalty was hard to classify as romantic love between man and woman to begin with, and as for Frederica, that whole area was a complete mystery. Otto occasionally claimed, over drinks, to have had his share of romances, but everyone unanimously judged that to be bluster.

To be outpaced by a thirteen-year-old girl was, frankly, a complete loss of face for the adults involved.

"Thinking about it that way, I wonder. Even with Rem awake, I feel like that state of things wouldn't really change much. Whether it's because I'm spineless, or because you're the type to respect my pace..."

Pulling out a chair as he spoke, Subaru sat down beside the bed.
The faint moonlight slipping in through the gap in the curtains barely brought into view the sleeping face of the girl lying in the bed.

A pale face bathed in moonlight, cherry-pink lips. Short blue hair, and a body with surprisingly feminine curves wrapped in a thin nightgown, her chest rising and falling with steady, even breaths — a sleeping beauty.
—A girl who had now gone on sleeping like this for more than a year.

"I've got a lot to report today. See, an uninvited guest brought us a hell of a problem. First off, I started the morning like usual—"

Subaru spoke to the sleeping girl with a calm expression on his face.
His way of speaking kept up its usual playful lightness, but the tone of his voice was deeply gentle. In a voice like one soothing a dozing child, he cheerfully related the day's events.

The girl gave no answer. Even so, this quiet rendezvous continued every single night.
On this particular night, with so much more than usual to talk about, this small, quiet exchange of dreams between Subaru and the sleeping beauty continued until the moon had tilted even further across the sky.
