Arc 4 Chapter 14: "Questions and Answers"

"You brought down the White Whale, and then repelled a Witch Cult Archbishop of Sin who targeted the mansion. You forged an alliance with the candidate Crusch-sama, and distinguished yourself in both of the aforementioned battles — hmm."

As if his weight on the bed had grown heavier for it, Roswaal touched his chin and closed his eyes. What he'd just recited was the string of events that had occurred during his absence, which Subaru had related over the course of the night so the two of them could get their stories straight.

Consciously suppressing his own bad habit of letting the story wander off on tangents, Subaru thought he'd managed a fairly objective account, stripped as much as possible of self-congratulation or sob stories. And now, looking back on his own conduct once more—

"...Frankly, it's the sort of track record that makes a person suspect delusions of grandeur. Since when did you switch careers to be the hero of an adventure story, Barusu?"

"Coming from you that's weirdly irritating, but... even I think it's a bit much, myself. This is, what, lightly terrifying levels of contribution whether I grade myself or somebody else does it?"

Results that might send even Zhuge Liang running barefoot. Coming from Ram, said quietly, almost sardonically, and yet acknowledging the sheer magnitude of the achievement, it only made the feeling swell further.

"There's nothing for it but to call it an unlooked-for result, I suppose. I never expected — no, that's not right — *no one* could have expected you'd manage this much, I'm su-u-ure."

Having apparently finished digesting his own astonishment somewhere inside himself, Roswaal drew in his chin and voiced his praise. With rare seriousness tightening his expression, he fixed his mismatched eyes on Subaru, seated in the chair before the bed.

"Fi-i-irst, let me offer you my thanks properly. — Thank you for protecting my domain and my people. And for your tremendous contribution to Emilia-sama, as her supporter, I am beyond grateful."

"O-oh. Yeah. Well. Hearing it put so formally makes me want to shrink into myself a little. It's not really something worth—"

"Barusu doesn't seem to fully grasp the weight of Roswaal-sama's gratitude."

Subaru tried to wave off Roswaal's earnest thanks, but it was Ram, stepping forward, who cut him off. She looked down at Subaru with that same unwavering, penetrating gaze.

"Interrupting the words of a superior, and worse, throwing denial back at his gratitude, isn't something that should normally be permitted. Especially not when Roswaal-sama holds the rank of margrave, a body entrusted with a share of the full authority of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica. — His words of thanks carry far more weight than Barusu seems to think."

"—"

"Given his standing, Roswaal-sama cannot simply bow his head to an inferior, nor hand out gratitude lightly. You'd do well to understand the weight of being given that."

Ram said it flatly, slicing away at Subaru's naive assumptions. Hearing it, Subaru found no rebuttal and dropped his eyes. At that, Roswaal raised a hand lightly, as if to smooth things over. "Now, now-o-ow."

"Ram is exaggerating just a li-i-ittle. My words aren't worth that much."

"Roswaal-sama."

At Ram's concerned prompt, Roswaal nodded, then continued, "But-t-t—"

"Setting aside the weight my words carry from my position, the magnitude of what Subaru-kun accomplished is plain to anyone's eyes. And no-o-ot rewarding it appropriately — well, I can easily imagine the disappointment and discontent that would be directed my way."

"...So what, exactly, are you offering?"

"A fitting reward. — Subaru-kun, do you remember that scene in the hall, at the Royal Selection?"

Watching Subaru's throat catch, Roswaal's mismatched eyes narrowed slightly.

Pierced by that gaze, what flickered through Subaru's mind was a memory so shameful it still burned his chest with embarrassment and self-mockery whenever he recalled it. That declaration, that reckless outburst — all of it had been the ravings of a fool who knew nothing of the world, knew nothing of himself, and had his priorities all wrong.

But even so—

"I remember. There's no way I'd forget. ...I don't think I'm *allowed* to forget."

"Then let me repay your achievements this way. Let us make the words spoken to you in that hall real. — Once you leave this place safely, I will appoint you a knight."

Subaru looked up. For a moment the meaning of the words didn't register, and he blinked in confusion as Roswaal nodded at him.

"A man who fought alongside a duke to bring down the White Whale, and struck down one of the Witch Cult's Archbishops of Sin, has no business remaining unknown. Your name — the name of 'Sir' Natsuki Subaru — ought to be spoken across the kingdom with honor and praise. — Once that happens, no one will ever again be able to laugh at the words you spoke in that hall."

*I'll be of some help to Emilia* — an empty-handed young fool had once roared those words. That dreamer of a boy had been broken again and again against reality, sunk into despair, drowned in madness, driven by vengeance to trample everything underfoot, and finally saved by love — and now, here he was.

Every moment of that time was, through the word "honor" that had just left Roswaal's mouth, proven to have carried real worth.

— Including the achievements of Rem, who now lived on in no one's memory but Subaru's own.

"...I'll accept it, gratefully. If it means those battles mean something."

"An achievement to be proud of. I won't let anyone make a fool of you for it. You've earned the right to stand beside Emilia-sama with your head held high. By your own strength."

"...It wasn't only my strength."

A murmur, barely more than breath, in answer to Roswaal's words. Watching Roswaal's brow furrow faintly, apparently unable to catch it, Subaru closed his eyes once, took a deep breath, then opened them again and gave a deliberately flippant shrug.

"Man, that got serious for a minute there. Let's not keep the character breakdown going too long, or it'll be embarrassing when I switch back to normal. My face's already getting hot."

"...Ye-e-eah. Goodness, doing something so out of character has given me stiff shoulders. A heart-to-heart isn't really our sty-y-yle, is it."

Matching Subaru's loosening expression, Roswaal let his own face relax as well, dissolving the atmosphere of a moment ago. Watching the exchange between master and Subaru, Ram let out a small breath, then picked up the thread. "Well then—"

"Isn't there something Barusu wanted to ask Roswaal-sama, next? That's why you had Emilia-sama sent away from this discussion, isn't it?"

"Your sharpness really speeds along the conversation. ...I'm not trying to treat Emilia-tan like some nuisance, but if she were here, I figured Roz's mouth would clam right up."

Subaru gave a wry smile at Ram's sharp observation. Watching that smile, Ram glanced briefly at the spot where Emilia had been standing until a moment ago, confirming the empty space there once more.

"And so you had her go with Ryuzu-sama to inspect the 'Sanctuary'... I see. Emilia-sama looked rather uneasy, hearing that Barusu would be staying here."

"I'm happy she wants to rely on me, but thinking ahead, I can't just chase after what I want in the moment. Otto'll probably catch up with her partway, so I'm not too worried about Emilia-tan being alone... unless that Otto bastard tries something with her. Emilia-tan's absurdly cute, so now I'm getting worried."

"I don't think it's a very go-o-ood idea to make yourself anxious while you talk. In any case, your reasoning isn't wrong. — In fact, if there's anything I-I-I don't want Emilia-sama to hear, I certainly won't be saying it."

Subaru, rattled by needless worry. And before him, Roswaal shook his head and, brazen as anything, confessed to his own secretiveness. Subaru closed one eye at him and, tongue carrying the thought *knew it*, said—

"You're deliberately restricting what info gets to Emilia-tan. ...What the hell are you thinking, doing something like that?"

"I think being selective about information is necessary, don't you-o-ou? Emilia-sama's importance, as a Royal Selection candidate, far outweighs my own. But her position, her knowledge, isn't yet refined enough to match her qualifications. Pile too much on someone still in the process of learning, and it only breeds strain—"

"That's a nice sentiment for someone who's actually giving her room to learn at her own pace to say. Withholding information that could tank the whole situation if she doesn't know it, when you *do* know it — that's not reasonable. And it's not good for you either, is it?"

Knowing full well what he was doing, Roswaal offered up a shallow deflection anyway; Subaru pressed on, voice quiet even as he swallowed back a click of his tongue. Roswaal closed one eye and looked back at him in silence.

Often, Roswaal would fix Subaru like this with one eye — the left, the yellow one — and stare. Subaru shifted uncomfortably under that gaze, which unsettled him for no clear reason. As if he'd read Subaru's discomfort perfectly, Roswaal laughed.

"We-e-ell, I did think you'd eventually press me on this, you know? Which is exactly why, this time, I made su-u-ure to steel myself properly."

"Steel yourself...?"

"Steel myself to answer Subaru-kun's question honestly, instead of dodging around it too much. Not that I could run even if I wanted to, in this battered state — rather convenient timing, don't you-u-u think?"

He laughed lightly, tapping his own thigh where it lay on the bed as he said it.

For a moment Subaru's throat caught in surprise at how readily he'd given in.

"...What's gotten into you all of a sudden?"

"Being trusted this little is a bit lonely, in its wa-a-ay. Though given the history between you and me up to now, I suppose it's only natural you'd feel that wa-a-ay."

"Sorry for the naked suspicion, but after everything so far, can you blame me? You're a bit too fond of your secrets. ...I can trust you this time, right?"

"Of course."

Roswaal nodded to Subaru's suspicious gaze and spread both hands lightly.

"Over these past few days, the achievements you've racked up have proven more than enough to pry open my closed heart. You can rest easy. Which means I've decided to trust you, and let you into my heart — as an accomplice, so to spea-a-ak."

"Wait. I'm getting a sense I'm about to have some heavy cross strapped to my back. You don't need to open your heart *that* wide, let's just do the surface stuff."

"Oh my, how cold, after all this buildup."

"No, I mean, having everything just handed to me all at once is heavy, it's, like—"

Sounding for all the world like a newly dating couple arguing over mismatched expectations of romance, Subaru cleared his throat, gathered his thoughts, and prefaced with "Anyway"—

"Setting the whole accomplice thing aside for now, let's just get to what I actually wanted to ask. — Why you're restricting information to Emilia-tan. That's where we start."

"—"

Subaru glared once more at Roswaal, who had closed one eye again.

Roswaal's selective filtering of what information reached Emilia — the effects of that were incalculable, even accounting only for the previous loop.

If she had understood that her origins — being a half-elf — were information that provoked the Witch Cult, they might have had far more effective countermeasures ready against Petelgeuse's assault on Arlam Village and Roswaal Manor.

And that, in turn, meant it had affected Subaru's own actions, as he scrambled to deal with the fallout — which meant, in the end, that it may have affected even the rightness or wrongness of Rem's fate.

"Answer me, Roswaal. You want Emilia-tan to become queen — you should be just as troubled as I am if she drops out along the way. So why the hell are you deliberately sitting on information that puts her at a disadvantage? It doesn't add up."

"To that question, I would answer this way. — Your point is entirely correct, and it's precisely *because* it's correct that I restrict the information given to Emilia-sama."

"...!? That makes no sense. You're saying withholding information that puts her at a disadvantage if she doesn't know it is somehow *necessary* to win the Royal Selection?"

"That's right. And I do think it was worth it, don't you-u-u?"

Roswaal's answer scrambled Subaru's thoughts, and his brow furrowed in open confusion. At that reaction, Roswaal creaked the bed as he shifted.

"Subaru-kun, what you're trying to say is essentially this, isn't it-t-t? Word of Emilia-sama's entry into the Royal Selection might set the Witch Cult in motion. And indeed, the Witch Cult *did* move, and struck at my domain. I could have anticipated that possibility, and prepared some countermeasure in case it happened."

"...Y-yeah. That's right. That's exactly right. Anyone would think that, it's basic common sense. I didn't know it, but around here, the connection between the Witch Cult and half-elves is just common knowledge, isn't it? And you obviously knew it too. So why didn't you prepare anything... no, before that, why did you leave the manor and shut yourself up in the 'Sanctuary'?"

"I was under house arrest in the 'Sanctuary' — I didn't leave the manor for days on end on purpose—"

"That excuse doesn't fly. The reason you ended up hurt and under house arrest in the first place is that you went and challenged the crypt to vent the discontent of the people of Arlam. Which means, after I cleared the village of people as a countermeasure against the Witch Cult... before that, you not coming back was your own choice."

"An opponent who's angry with airtight logic is worth arguing with. Tru-u-uly, a good sign."

When Subaru rebutted the flimsy excuse, Roswaal shrugged with an easy air, as if he hadn't really expected to get away with it. Displeased by that manner, Subaru took a step forward, but—

"...Ram."

"Roswaal-sama is injured. Even so, one fingertip would be enough to burn Barusu to ash... but Ram won't allow violence, not in front of her."

"And you're fine with that? You got treated like a sacrificial pawn too, didn't you. You knew those monsters were coming to the village and you ran off alone from that battlefield. Can you really forgive that?"

"There's nothing to forgive or not forgive. Ram accepts everything Roswaal-sama does. However Ram is treated, however she's cast aside — it's all the same."

"You—!!"

Ram's loyalty, incomprehensible in its depth, choked Subaru's throat with raw emotion.

Even so, that he didn't lash out with violence right away was perhaps because some part of Subaru's reason coolly judged he couldn't win against either of the two people before him — or perhaps—

"...Rem was sacrificed for something just as senseless, you know."

"—? I don't know who you're talking about, but other people's names mean nothing to Ram. To Ram, Roswaal-sama is everything, and everything else is trivial."

Subaru's squeezed-out plea found not even a sliver of resonance in Ram's heart.

He'd known it already. Pleading with her about Rem's existence, when she had forgotten it entirely, meant nothing. And at the same time, he understood something else.

He'd thought he already understood Ram's abnormal loyalty toward Roswaal, from the start. But that stubbornness now carried something different — something mad — from the Ram Subaru had known until now. The cause of that, surely, was largely the forgetting of Rem's existence.

Subaru didn't know the details of what had happened in their past. But piecing together the fragments of information Rem had shared, it was easy enough to see that the sisters Ram and Rem had existed in something like a codependent relationship.

Guilt and inferiority toward her older sister — caught between the two, Rem's complex had deepened, and with it her dependence on her sister. That instability had been visible on the surface, impossible to miss; but traces of the same thing had shown in Ram, facing her sister, as well.

Just as most of Rem's world had been made up of Ram, so too had most of Ram's world been made up of the two people, Rem and Roswaal. Having settled her complex once and for all, Rem had begun welcoming various new elements into that once-narrow world — Subaru among them — and had been trying to change. But Ram's world had remained narrow, all the same.

Having literally forgotten the presence that had occupied half of that vessel, Ram's world was now composed of Roswaal, and Roswaal alone.

That was the source of this extreme, even excessive, display of loyalty toward Roswaal.

"Ram, don't provoke Subaru-kun too much either. No-o-ot that Subaru-kun means any violence toward me. His foot just moved forward a little, that's all."

"If Roswaal-sama says so."

"Ye-e-es. It's fine, fine. Isn't that right, Subaru-kun. You look angry, but you're not actually furious. Losing yourself and swinging a punch — that's not a choice you could make, is it, one that would throw the whole discussion into ru-u-uin?"

"What's that supposed to mean..."

"It's simple. The Subaru-kun of before would have, at some point in this conversation, snapped and started shouting, blowing the whole discussion apart. That you haven't done that — that you can keep the argument going while swallowing your anger — that's growing up, wouldn't you say."

At Roswaal's light applause and hollow praise, Subaru felt the pent-up urge to shout burn hot in his chest. But he held himself back, knowing that giving in would only play further into the other man's hands, pushing back the wave of fury with a long exhale.

— He couldn't help but be irritated at himself, realizing that the very act of restraining himself was proof of Roswaal's words.

"No-o-ow then, bullying the young any further wouldn't be very adult of me. Since you've shown signs of growth, I ought to demonstrate some adult composure of my own, don't you think?"

"...Then do it. Anyway, give me a clear answer to that question from before. No dodging this time. Why did you hide the Witch Cult from Emilia? Why, knowing the Witch Cult was coming, did you — the strongest force we had — leave the mansion!"

"Both questions can be answered with one thing. — I did those things in order to avoid facing the Witch Cult myself."

"Huh—?"

Answered calmly and coherently, Subaru's understanding lagged behind, if anything.

He chewed on it, swallowed it, let his brain taste the words — and then the meaning sank in.

"That doesn't make any sense. You avoided fighting the Witch Cult for... what, for what reason? Don't tell me it's some kind of visceral distaste for them! If you'd been there... if you'd been there, wouldn't those bastards have been wiped out completely? The damage, too—"

"Indeed. Truly, if I had been there, I think the damage from this whole incident could su-u-urely have been reduced. I believe I understand my own capabilities correctly, and I'm aware that I'm counted among the top ten most capable individuals in this kingdom. I'll say it plainly. Had I been there, this Witch Cult assault would have been repelled with ease."

"Knowing that, why didn't you—!"

"Because of that."

Roswaal raised a finger to stop Subaru, who was spitting as he shouted, then turned that pointed finger toward the ceiling.

"If I'd been the one to distinguish myself, it wouldn't have become an achievement for Emilia-sama, or for you, would it? There's no poi-i-int in me raising my own name."

"——Eh."

Subaru genuinely could not comprehend what he'd just been told.

He half-hoped it was a joke, some flippant remark, and waited, sentence cut off, for Roswaal to continue. But at Subaru's silence, Roswaal only tilted his head.

"No words of denial? Here was a calamity we knew was coming, practically guaranteed. What reason could there possibly be *not* to make good use of it?"

"Y-you... do you even know what you're saying...?"

"—? I'm the one who doesn't understand what Subaru-kun is making an issue of, here. Is it that, hmm. The damage done to Arlam Village, or the mercenary company and Crusch-sama's private soldiers who lent their strength to repel the Witch Cult... is this about whether something could have been done about that kind of loss?"

Reading through every ounce of Subaru's trembling inner thoughts, Roswaal spoke as though it were simply self-evident.

Hearing that answer, Subaru finally felt his very soul shudder.

Once before, talking with Puck, that spirit had stood before the sleeping Rem and said, "Thanks to this girl, Lia was saved" — a remark that had sent Subaru into a rage. That incident had driven home just how large and unspeakable the gulf was between his own sensibilities and a spirit's. But conversely, the anger he'd felt in that moment had come with an understanding that they were, at bottom, fundamentally different kinds of being.

But Roswaal had no such excuse. He understood the cause of Subaru's anger. He understood exactly what Subaru wanted to say — and even so, he could make such a merciless judgment.

But that was—

"That's — that's hindsight talking. I get, sort of, what you're trying to say. I understand that who takes command against a Witch Cult attack, who gets the credit — that has no small influence on the Royal Selection. ...And that if Roswaal did it himself, you wouldn't get the effect you wanted. But still!"

Baring his teeth, Subaru swung his arm wide.

"How many people do you think died because you were gone, because you didn't tell anyone anything!? Sure, there wasn't huge damage. There wasn't, but it wasn't zero either. People died. Not just on our side — even among the Witch Cult—"

"Even had I been there, nothing would have changed about how the Witch Cult's followers were dealt with. Every single one of them, reduced to ash, and that's all. I'll accept blame for losses among those who fought on our side, but resentment on behalf of those who stood against us seems mispla-a-aced, don't you think?"

"—! Even so, more of them could have been handled more gently... no, that's not — that's not the point! I'm saying it's *all* hindsight! Sure, it worked out. The damage was small, the enemy was wiped out. Emilia-tan's safe, and the people of Arlam Village got evacuated safely. ...But all of that, every bit of it, was just chance. If things had gone the way they should have—"

If things had gone the way they should have, before Subaru could do anything, the village's people, the mansion, Emilia—

"—would have died. Should have died. Instead of things going well like this time, everyone would've been... horribly, cruelly tormented, suffering, and murdered."

Covering his face, Subaru desperately choked back a voice on the verge of tears.

Beyond his closed eyelids rose the unforgettable vision of hell Subaru had once witnessed. A village burning to the ground. Corpses scattered everywhere. The bodies of children. And Rem's corpse, discarded in the mansion's garden. A world freezing over, coming to its end.

— A world that could never have been overturned if Subaru hadn't been able to Return by Death.

"If you'd been there, none of that would've happened. ...You knew, and you let it happen. You killed those people, over and over, again and again..."

"I'll ask you not to misunderstand. It was the Witch Cult who attacked, not I. And the Witch Cult's assault was prevented at your hands, before it could happen — there are no victims of the kind you're describing. — Nothing more than the repetition of delusions."

"—Fine, then."

At Roswaal's chilly words, Subaru dropped his shoulders and answered in a low voice.

Delusions — once dismissed as that, there was no way for Subaru's own words to overturn it. As long as he couldn't appeal to the truth of Return by Death, he had no way, in reality, to condemn Roswaal for "events that never happened."

Only Subaru knew that hell — and it was Subaru himself who had erased from Roswaal any responsibility for having caused the sight of that hell.

"...If I'd stayed the same useless waste of space I was, what would you have done? You want Emilia to become queen too, don't you. The odds were so lopsided this wouldn't even work as a bet. ...The chances were far higher that it would've all ended right here."

"But you overturned those odds for me. — Is that not enough for you?"

"It's not enough. You don't strike me as the type to leave things to chance like that."

There are different kinds of people who take on a gamble. Those who bring nothing but luck to a fight whose outcome they can't predict. Those who arrange every advantage within their power so the outcome doesn't depend on luck alone, leaving only the final sliver to chance.

And then there are those who prepare the entire path from beginning to end in advance, and call a fixed match, its outcome already decided, a "gamble."

"You're not the type to gamble in the first place. So how did you manage it?"

"—I believed in you."

At Subaru's renewed question, Roswaal answered, his voice dropping in tone.

Hearing that answer, an involuntary snort of disbelief escaped Subaru's mouth.

"So you don't even mean to answer seriously."

"Whether you believe what I say or not is a separate matter, but I am telling you the truth, you know? I've decided that tonight, in this place, I won't deceive you. What I cannot say, I will say I cannot say; what's inconvenient, I'll hold my tongue on and simply not speak of. But I swear that what I *do* say will not be a lie."

Roswaal spoke solemnly to Subaru's disillusioned words. But how far that could be trusted was another matter. Having already, over the course of this discussion, drained away nearly all of his goodwill toward Roswaal, Subaru had no room left to take it at face value.

Sharpening his squint further, Subaru watched as Roswaal turned his head.

"I'll say it again. — The reason I made the decision I did this time was that I believed in you. I believed that if you saw Emilia-sama's situation turning bad, you would scramble to make the alliance with Crusch-sama happen, and, on top of that, throw yourself into repelling the Witch Cult and see it through, and rack up achievements doing it."

"Even if that's true, why would you end up trusting a nobody like me!? What do you even know about me! After a month's acquaintance, did I really look like a man capable of earning that kind of trust!?"

At Roswaal, brazenly stringing together flowery phrases, Subaru stamped the floor and shot back. He pointed a finger and shook his head, as if denying his own previous words.

"There's no way. When I parted from you, I was a genuine piece of garbage. That the garbage got even a little better than that was because of what happened after. And what happened after exists nowhere but inside me. — What exactly did you trust about me!?"

Roswaal closed one eye. That yellow eye watched Subaru, uncomfortably.

As if to shake off that gaze, Subaru kicked hard at the floor.

"This is pointless. As things stand, you're basically saying you trusted some empty-headed idiot kid to make everything work out fine, and abandoned your people and everything else to go play around. Betting your own position, your own future, as the stake — I've got no words for how reckless that is!"

"...It seems that's as far as tonight's discussion is going to go-o-o."

In contrast to Subaru's open anger, Roswaal murmured, sounding forlorn.

Catching that murmur, Subaru let his still-unspent irritation ride his tongue with an "Ah!"

"Since you're clearly not interested in talking straight, whatever I say is pointless anyway. Whatever you say after tonight, I'm not going to believe it."

"I find it genuinely regrettable that my standing in your eyes has crashed so badly. ...I don't suppose I need to ask, but tonight's conversation, to Emilia-sama—"

"Like hell I'd tell her. Whether I told it straight or dressed it up, there's no benefit to be had from sharing it. You calculated that far ahead, which is exactly why you spouted all that nonsense so freely."

Whatever Roswaal's true intentions were, friction developing between him and Emilia would be undesirable for the continuation of the Royal Selection. Especially now, when the Roswaal camp — Emilia as its figurehead, the villagers of Arlam included — needed to stand united.

Galling as it was to play along with Roswaal's designs, taking on the Trial would still raise Emilia's standing, relatively speaking. — All of it, events unfolding in the palm of his hand.

"Understanding everything, harboring unbearable anger at me and still... you can't bring yourself to flip the table. Ju-u-ust as I expected of you, after all."

Roswaal's voice reached Subaru as he ground his teeth, holding back his mortification. Looking up, Subaru saw Roswaal twist his expression into something truly unpleasant.

"You truly are fit to be my accomplice — that's what I mean."

"...You're not gonna die a decent death, you bastard."

"I know that already. I'll certainly fall to hell. Which is exactly why, before that happens, I ought to make the most of whatever tyranny I can manage in this life."

Shooting one sharp glance at Roswaal as he spoke, Subaru turned his back without a word and stormed out of the room.

Continuing this conversation further would only be pointless. As long as Roswaal had no intention of speaking his true mind, and Subaru had no way to tear apart his scheme, it would only be a fruitless exchange.

But—

"—Don't think for a second I'll let everyone dance along to your little plan."

Fists clenched tight, Subaru renewed his resolve as he walked the night road.

Tomorrow, Roswaal intended to send Emilia into the Trial, hoping to overturn the contempt for half-elves held by the people of the Sanctuary and Arlam Village alike. In the process, that man wouldn't spare a thought for whatever burdens might fall on Emilia. In the end, however many wounds Emilia bore, however worn down her heart became, he'd just laugh it off and bend things to his own will. Well then—

"I won't let that happen. That girl — Emilia — I'll protect her."

The qualification to face the Trial — if the dream he'd seen in the crypt hadn't been just a dream, then Subaru should already have been granted it.

Whether it was some whim of the Witch's, he would use it to thwart Roswaal's scheme. Whatever pain, whatever tears might arise from that man's disregard for his own allies — Subaru would stop all of it.

"— That's what I have to do, here in the 'Sanctuary.'"

Beyond his raised fist floated a pale moon.

Clenching that fist tight, as if to grasp what lay beyond his reach, Subaru pictured the silver-haired girl he loved, and resolved to clash head-on with the clown's malice-tinged scheme.

"—Was that truly all right?"

In the room, after her fellow attendee had stormed out, Ram, who had watched the exchange in silence, quietly posed the question. Hearing it, her master shook his head weakly.

"I expected that kind of reaction, of course-e-e. Even knowing it, gouging into a young man's heart isn't exactly something that lifts one's spirits."

"You don't need to lie in front of Ram, you know?"

"I appreciate the concern, but that was the truth, just now. What, does even Ram see me as someone who'd do that sort of thing gleefully?"

Turning her eyes away wordlessly served as Ram's answer to her master. As the earlier exchange had disturbed the sheets, she set about straightening the bed. In doing so, her fingers brushed against something hard near her master's side, and she pulled it out from beneath the sheet.

"Roswaal-sama. This."

"Ah, so-o-orry, sorry. If Subaru-kun had seen it, things might have gotten complicated, you see. Though tucking it under my backside like that was rather sacrilegious of me. I should be more careful."

Receiving the item back with evident care, Roswaal gently stroked its surface. Then, "In any case," he said, touching a finger to his chin.

"I've confirmed Emilia-sama's qualification, and I've lit a fire under Subaru-kun. If the Trial begins tomorrow night... what do you think will happen, Ram?"

"There's no way Ram could possibly gauge Roswaal-sama's thinking on that. ...Wouldn't Roswaal-sama already know how it will turn out?"

"It's not quite so convenient a thing as that. Compared to the incomplete version the Witch Cult holds, it's a fair bit better, but it's still nothing more than an imitation, falling short of what she truly sought. Even that exchange with Subaru-kun just now — how much of it followed the script, who can say."

At Roswaal's sigh, as if in self-reflection, Ram raised an eyebrow slightly, then, hesitant and tentative, asked—

"Then, the many things you said to Barusu just now, were they..."

"Some of it was acting, but... mostly, it was the truth, I'd say. I mean, of course I knew Subaru-kun would get angry like that. I knew it, but even I have a few things I'd like to say for myself, don't you think?"

Roswaal waved a hand toward Ram as if in self-defense, then continued, "After all—"

"It would be rather galling to think I'm the only one who feels irritated when the one I fancy gets belittled, wouldn't it? Call it childish. Call it my own little spite."

Roswaal laughed as he said it.

Cradled carefully, so carefully, in his arms was a book bound in black.

Roswaal's fingertips traced slowly, slowly, along that book's binding.

Lovingly, lovingly, slowly, slowly—
