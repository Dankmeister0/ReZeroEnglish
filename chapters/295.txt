Arc 4 Chapter 125: "The Battle for Roswaal Manor"


"――Dona!!"

Of the three of them, only Otto managed to react in time to the threat bearing down on them.
Thrusting both hands forward, he interfered with the world through incantation — a surge of mana shattered the floor of the manor and raised a wall of earth, covering the wide corridor to stand as a barrier against the jet-black shadow.
But—

"In the way."

"One hit!?"

A short voice, and a pair of curved blades swung.
The arcing slash cut through the earthen wall as easily as paper, and the divided remains were kicked by a long leg and collapsed in an instant.
Scattering grains of mana, and the fragile, crumbling remnants of the earthen wall. A sadistic smile taking shape, and a dully gleaming silver blade.

"I suppose I'll have to slit your throat first and seal that mouth shut before you'll behave."

"Don't say something that scary!"

In the one-second gap Otto bought, Subaru scooped up Petra and rolled into the room just off to the side. Otto dove into the same room a beat later, slammed the door shut, then immediately covered his head and dove behind the bed.
A slash cut the door diagonally, and half of the sliding door was kicked into the room.

"Eat this, then――!"

Aiming at Elsa as she slipped in through the gap in the door, Subaru swung down a wooden coat rack. Elsa leaned back to dodge it, and her flashing blade cleaved the coat rack in two. The return stroke aimed for Subaru's throat, but thanks to Petra leaping onto him, it only grazed him.

"Oh, what a naughty boy."

"I'm proud of being a good boy, you bastard!"

Pressing a hand to his neck where blood beaded from a shallow cut, Subaru pulled Petra close and fell back.
Elsa wore a look of ghastly delight and moved to press her attack on the retreating Subaru.
However—

"How about this――!"

Aiming for the side of Elsa's face, Otto reared back and threw a magic stone.
The faintly red-glowing object was something like an explosive shell infused with mana of "Fire" — a literal trump card, purer than the one that had helped against Garfiel in their earlier showdown.
Otto's hidden card exploded at the defenseless Elsa from directly to the side――

Between Otto and Elsa, the shattered magic stone's power burst into crimson.
Sound and light swept the room, and buffeted by the searing wind, Subaru watched — in a field of vision sharpened enough that time itself seemed to slow — as the magic stone Otto had thrown was intercepted by a thrown knife Elsa had flung without even turning around.

The explosion went off at an unintended spot, and Otto, his eyes seared, cried out and reeled backward.
The tip of Elsa's long leg drove into his gut, folding him double and sending him flying into the wall. Without so much as a glance at the crumpled Otto, Elsa turned back toward the breathless Subaru, and her eyebrows rose slightly.

"Oh? You... I've seen that face before, in the royal capital, haven't I?"

"H-honored that you remember me. Given that connection, think you could let this one slide?"

"Once I've set my sights on a set of guts, I make a point of seeing them through, no matter how long it takes."

"Whoa, that's real collector spirit!"

Subaru felt Petra grip the hem of his clothes tight, and his thoughts raced white-hot.
He could feel the Gate inside his body was dead. No matter how he prayed, no matter how he burned his Soul, neither mana nor Gate would respond. Deploying Shamak here was impossible, and before that, he'd collapse like the fool he always was.
Then there was only one option he could rely on — Invisible Providence, and nothing else.

"――――"

The moment he decided to use it, something unknowable and black began crawling through the inside of Subaru's body.
The thing that had lain still now sensed it had been summoned by Subaru and began asserting its presence, clamoring eagerly for the chance to unleash its own power.
A sinister sensation stabbed through him, like feeding some hideous monster.
Deliberately ignoring it, Subaru poured commands into the black power raising its first cry, resolving to carve a path from the inside to the outside world.

At the end of an agony fit to wring tears of blood, there remained a revulsion toward wielding this power.
Even so — clinging to whatever there was to cling to, using whatever could be used, living in order to live and let live.
For no other reason than to save everyone Subaru wished to see saved.

"Ahh... that face. It gives me chills."

"I'll show you something even better."

"Yes, how delightful――"

Aiming for the center of Elsa as she reared back with her two blades, Subaru drew the trigger taut.
All that remained was to release it, and it should be able to tear through that slender body and pierce clean through.

"――Ohhhh"

Something viscous and writhing flowed into his bloodstream and coursed through his whole body.
An illusion that his exhaled breath had taken on color, an illusion of running a high fever, black unknowable claws extending — he could foresee a future in which he split Elsa clean in two.
Just like this, offering up everything, and then――.

"Subaru!"

An anguished voice, and a numbing pain like his side being pinched.
He flinched, and the hideous emotion that had been inside his belly instantly dispersed.

What remained was only a faint residue of black filth, and the same unbroken jet-black killing intent.
Against Elsa, who was already moving to close in, Subaru scrambled to take aim again but wasn't in time — and then—

"――Watch out."

"――You're dodging that!?"

A great gust bearing down from behind — Elsa evaded it with a split-second read.
She broke off her strike at Subaru, twisted her body, and danced clear of a claw-strike that gouged at her back.
Her body, spinning, kicked the ambushing Frederica in the side, and on the same momentum elbowed Subaru, then somersaulted backward through the gap between the two of them as they were sent flying, landing serenely atop the bed at the back of the room.

Pressing a hand to her back, her palm coming away slick with blood, Elsa wore an expression of ecstasy.
Then she looked at Frederica, who had dropped to one knee, and tilted her head with amusement.

"Another one... no, two more guests. What a lovely manor this is."

"Even that ambush didn't land properly... that's not a human's reflexes."

Frederica ground her teeth in frustration, unable to hide her dread.
Subaru, his chest struck hard, coughed as he shuffled sideways over to Frederica.

"Frederica, sorry, and thanks for the save. And you too, Petra."

He called out to Frederica, who had rejoined them, then thanked Petra, whose hand he still held. Petra, eyes brimming with tears, shook her head,

"N-no, I'm sorry too. But Subaru... just now, you had that scary look on your face..."

"Honestly, I think I was about to get swallowed up. If you hadn't pulled me back it might've been bad. Guess I can't carelessly rely on Invisible Providence..."

"In... what?"

That the trump card was a double-edged sword was no longer any surprise to Subaru at this point.
The problem was that its usable moments were growing ever fewer — he could only pray that the current flaw stemmed from consecutive use. At the very least, he had nothing but the sense that relying on Invisible Providence in this fight would cost far too much in exchange.

"Frederica-neesama..."

"Petra, you must have been frightened. But you held back your tears so well."

Still gripping the hem of Subaru's clothes, Petra called out to Frederica. Frederica, praising the effort of the girl she treasured like a little sister, turned a stern expression toward Subaru.

"Subaru-sama, I apologize. My duty was to see to it that Rem-sama and I escaped the manor... and I have failed to accomplish it."

"No, given the situation, it couldn't be helped. Things are apparently even worse outside... Where's Rem?"

"Right here."

Frederica held nothing in either hand, instead equipped with claw-shaped gauntlets.
To Subaru, uneasy at not seeing Rem, Frederica turned her back to him. There, bound securely with cords, was Rem, carried on Frederica's back.

Tightly bound as she was, it made for a rather surreal sight even so.

"I get that it's an emergency, but if you move around too much like this I'm scared Rem's neck is gonna snap off!?"

"Fortunately — though I hesitate to call it fortunate — Rem-sama's body is severed from the normal flow of time. So even somewhat rough handling shows no ill effect..."

"S-still, please handle her as carefully and respectfully as you can, okay...?"

This was, after all, the result of Frederica doing her best within her own judgment.
Subaru had no wish to complain about it without an alternative to offer. Rem would just have to bear with some cramped discomfort for a while.
After all, on their side—

"There's nobody who can fight besides Frederica. Me and Petra are out of the running. Rem's asleep. Otto fought hard, but for all his valiant effort, he's already..."

"I'm not dead!? Could you not say scary stuff like that while a guy's on the floor seeing stars from a head hit!?"

Protesting Subaru's downcast eulogy, Otto revived from where he'd been sprawled in the corner of the room.
Shaking his head, Otto crawled over to rejoin them, shuddering at the sight of the severed door and coat rack.

"I can't believe he shot down my magic stone like that... it worked just fine on Garfiel."

"Different level of combat experience, and probably a different level of brains too. Don't compare them. It's just sad."

"Garf... so he really did grow up looking exactly the way he looks. Because I wasn't watching over him..."

Subaru cut off his own cruel comparison in response to Otto's assessment.
Frederica too seemed to have complicated feelings about Garfiel, whom she'd been reunited with after ten years. Perhaps there was some self-reproach mixed in, over her little brother's thoroughly delinquent-looking growth, for having looked away.
That sort of reconciling would have to be handled well going forward, as they built their sibling relationship, but for now—

"For now, we need to unite and deal with the problem at hand."

"Shall I take it that the pleasant little chat is more or less over?"

"Sorry to make you wait, specially for us. You ready in your heart for a five-on-one beatdown, though?"

"By my count there seem to be three or four whose inclusion in that number I'd hesitate over, don't you think?"

At Subaru's bold provocation, Elsa smiled faintly, accurately counting the non-combatants.
Elsa swayed the curved blades in each hand and lightly dropped down off the bed. Watching her, Subaru noticed something.
――Blood was no longer dripping from Elsa's back.

"Looked pretty deep from what I saw, right?"

"The wound? No need to worry. See, it's already—"

Saying so, Elsa spun in place on the spot.
And just as Subaru had suspected, the wound on her back that Frederica had gouged out earlier had vanished, clean and whole. Since there were claw marks left on the back of her garment, it hadn't been an illusion.

Frederica and the others besides Subaru stiffened, catching their breath.
Subaru, for his part, only let out a deep sigh and cursed the accuracy of his bad premonition.

"I knew you couldn't kill you and have it stick, but... wounds heal too, huh. You're basically a monster."

"I don't recall having renounced my humanity, and I do think saying that to a woman is rather much, even from you. And where did you learn about my constitution, I wonder?"

"The fact that you weren't cut clean in half by Reinhard was enough for anyone to figure something was up."

"Now that was quite the experience. I very nearly was split in two. ――I do wonder what a hero's guts look like inside. I'm terribly curious."

Having witnessed that much combat prowess firsthand, Elsa showed not the slightest sign of having learned her lesson.
If anything, she ought to be pestering Reinhard, who seemed unkillable, instead of persistently harassing Subaru and the others — what twist of fate was this?
It was enough that his grudge against Roswaal wasn't even sufficient to cover it.

"Subaru-sama... if that woman is here, then what of Garf?"

Fearfully, Frederica asked, her expression tightening.
Having seen the abnormality of Elsa's body just now, she grew anxious for the safety of the little brother not present here.
But Subaru had no answer that could dispel Frederica's unease, either.
If there was one thing he could say, it was—

"Unfortunately, I can't explain to you either why she's here. But I also don't believe Garfiel would just get taken out clean in this short a time."

"From what I saw as well, their strength seemed nearly matched... if anything, Garf looked to have the edge."

"That's how it looked to me too, but in the end there's no real answer..."

*No answer to be found,* Subaru thought, his eyes flicking toward Elsa, and he unconsciously held his breath.
Drawn by Subaru's gaze, Frederica and the others followed suit, also holding their breath the same way.
Puzzled by the reaction, Elsa raised her eyes to look up at what Subaru and the others were staring at above her head.

The ceiling of the guest room appeared to be caving in, as though sinking, and—

"Shaaaraaaku, sei――!!"

"Kya, ah――!?"

"That idiot!!"

The instant the ceiling burst open with a roar, Subaru and the others all rushed for the door at once.
Cursing at the voice they heard, the five of them shot through the severed door like they were flying, and directly after, the ceiling of the guest room crushed the entire room, furniture and timber cracking with an anguished sound that echoed through the manor.

An explosive roar and a violent wind tore through, the aftershock spilling out into the corridor beyond the room.
White dust billowed up, and biting down on gravelly grit in his mouth, Subaru rolled clear down the corridor to evacuate the scene. Fortunately, it seemed none of their allies had been caught in the collapse.
And from beyond the smoke—

"Don't go pullin' boring shit like that! There, spin, spin!"

A familiar rough voice shouted, tinged with excitement.
The voice was accompanied by the sound of blows landing, and steel clashing against steel, and before long a shadow, trailing plumes of smoke, came tumbling into the corridor.

"Oh, whoa!?"

Seeing the tumbling shape, Subaru involuntarily let out a cry of surprise.
No wonder — it matched none of the forms he'd imagined. A four-legged beast with thick fur and sharp claws on its limbs — its mottled coat resembling an animal like a hyena.
Except its build was nothing like a hyena's. A massive frame roughly twice Subaru's size.

For a moment, Subaru braced himself at the appearance of such an oversized beast, but he quickly realized there was no light in the hyena's twin eyes — it was already dead. Looking closer, its neck bone was twisted grotesquely, its head facing the wrong direction entirely.
There was no doubting some tremendous force had wrenched its cervical spine.
And right now, in this manor, if there was anyone likely to be hostile toward a beast and capable of doing that—

"Yo, boss. Still in here, huh."

Kicking the smoke aside, it was Garfiel who emerged calmly into the corridor.
He noticed Subaru and the others staring dumbfounded at the hyena's corpse in the hallway, and grinned, mouth open.

"No need to get all scared, y'know. I'm the one who beat the hell outta that thing."

"Oh good, that's a relief... no it's not! What the hell were you doing letting your eyes off it! I thought I was gonna die because of you! I was so scared! I thought I was actually gonna die!"

"My bad, my bad. Wasn't plannin' on lettin' it get away either, not even a little. Got tangled up with some annoying shit partway through and it wandered off somewhere."

"Annoying shit, you say, you..."

Garfiel twisted his face in irritation, teeth gnashing.
Was the "annoying shit" he'd mentioned this hyena-like beast? Putting it together with what they'd discussed earlier, it seemed certain to be some kind of Witchbeast — and it was right then.

"Ugh! Unbelievable! Elsa! Elsa! Do something about him!"

"I'd love nothing more, but wasn't it you who told me to leave things to you and handle the rest yourself? Not that I mind — the more bellies there are to cut, the happier I am."

Voices rang out, one high, one composed — two of them.
Right after, the wall of the room Garfiel had smashed shattered from the inside, and once again, trailing smoke, a shadow emerged into the corridor.
Heavy footsteps echoing, and light footfalls — two sets, though the difference in size was so extreme it made one hesitate to call it "two."

"...What, um, is that?"

Otto, who had been silent up till now, unable to bear it any longer, pointed and asked.
In response, Subaru, feeling cold sweat drenching his whole body, said,

"From what I can see, it looks kind of like an oversized hippo."

"Kind of, you say?"

"Yeah. Hippos are already big animals to begin with."

If an already-large hippo grew about three times bigger, it would probably look something like the creature before them.
Jet-black skin, thick hide as tough as rock. Round eyes glowing red with vicious hostility, and a mouth large enough to swallow even old man Rom whole, with flat teeth like millstones — those were its distinguishing features.
At a glance it resembled a hippo, but its ferocity and viciousness were probably about triple as well.

And the sheer pressure radiated by its massive, imposing presence was nothing ordinary, but—

"The Spotted King-Dog died! He got killed! Poor thing! No! Nooo!"

A shrill, tearful voice mourned the death of the hyena-like beast from atop the massive hippo.
Legs kicking, riding on the hippo's back was a small girl. A girl with brown hair in braids and an unassuming face, openly showing her emotions with childlike innocence.

Subaru recognized that girl's face.

"...The girl from the Witchbeast forest."

That was from before, back when Subaru had been caught in a loop originating from the manor.
The reason Subaru had ended up venturing into the deepest part of the forest, trying to rescue the children of Arlam Village who'd wandered into the Witchbeast forest. And more than anything, the person most responsible for luring those children into the Witchbeast forest in the first place.
After the incident had ended, Roswaal had told him her figure had vanished, but—

"That girl... it's her, from back then!"

Petra too seemed to have caught up to the same realization Subaru had reached.
If it had only been Subaru who noticed, it might have been dismissed as a mistake or something. But if Petra's memory was tangled up in it too, he had no choice but to accept it.

The girl was someone connected to the Witchbeast incident.
And considering the current situation, even the Witchbeast disturbance surrounding this manor might well be—

"Roswaal's... doing...!"

If she was working together with Elsa, then perhaps even the Witchbeast incident was something Roswaal had brought about.
If so, then everything from the royal capital to the manor was all on Roswaal's palm. Was Subaru's every struggle merely a future already laid out by that black prophecy book?

"There's no way I'm accepting that kind of nonsense...!"

He refused to accept that fate was preordained.
At least, not from here on out. He'd interrogate Roswaal about the Witchbeast incident too — one more reason added to the list for punching that clown-faced smirk right across the jaw.

Burning with anger and rebellious defiance, Subaru's stare finally caught the girl's attention atop the beast.
The girl blinked her round eyes, waved a hand toward Subaru, and,

"Ah, it's the big brother from back then. Petra-chan's here too. It's been a while."

"D-don't just talk to us like there's nothing wrong. Do you even understand the situation here?"

Subaru couldn't hide his bewilderment at the girl calling out to him so casually.
The girl tilted her head at Subaru's obviously wary attitude,

"I understand, I'm working right now. I have to properly do my job or Mama will scold me. But Elsa's the one doing whatever she wants."

"Being told to hold the rear like some boring job — she's the one out of line for ordering me around like that. I'd rather savor life far more vividly than being reduced to beast fodder. And I'd rather be the one doing the killing too, don't you think?"

At the girl's sulky remark, Elsa, stepping up beside the beast, redirected the conversation their way.
Subaru let out a breath at the deranged woman's reasoning, then raised a single finger.

"Alright, then let me offer you one supremely cool proposal. You take that knife you're holding and switch it to a reverse grip. Then you stab it into your own stomach. Then you slide it sideways. Guts spill out, everybody's happy, me happy, you happy. Seppuku Challenge. Cool, right?"

"Pfft! Ahahaha! Amazing, amazing! Hey, Elsa, why don't you try it? Elsa, you love guts, don't you? It'd definitely be interesting! Fun!"

"Unfortunately, ever since my body became like this, I've already grown tired of doing that."

Hearing that his supremely cool proposal had already been carried out, a cool chill of dread ran down Subaru's spine.
Either way, there was no mistaking that there were two crises staring them down.

"I don't know the mechanics of it, but I'm guessing we should assume that girl's controlling the Witchbeasts?"

"No mistakin' it. The Witchbeasts surroundin' the front too, and that big-ass dog from before — all of 'em obedient little pets. So whaddaya wanna do, boss?"

Garfiel asked Subaru, seeking confirmation on whether to continue with the original plan.
Honestly, the situation had shifted quite drastically from the original plan. Beyond Elsa alone, there was now a second enemy — and one commanding a massive Witchbeast at that.
As long as the Witchbeasts outside remained, escaping the manor peacefully would be difficult. And more than that, Subaru and the others hadn't yet gotten hold of every person who needed rescuing.

Even if they managed to get Frederica, Petra, and Rem outside, that still wasn't enough.

"Garfiel... can I ask you for something really unreasonable?"

"Try me, boss."

"I want you to hold off both Elsa and that girl, at the same time."

"――――"

Subaru was well aware he was making an outrageously unreasonable demand of the silent Garfiel.
Elsa alone was already an opponent far too tough to face head-on normally. Holding her off while also having to divide his attention toward that massive Witchbeast, too.
Subaru had felt the threat of Witchbeasts painfully, ever since coming to this world.
So—

"Sure. Leave it to me. Gettin' fired up now."

"――!? R-really okay with this? Seriously? You can do it?"

"That's what I'm here for, ain't it. I been runnin' my mouth big time. Ain't no way I'm gonna start whinin' now just 'cause the enemy got bigger or tougher. 'A Miden with a cliff at its back has nowhere to run,' as they say."

Delivering that back-against-the-wall kind of proverb, Garfiel clashed the shields on both hands together.
Subaru could tell clearly enough that it was more bravado than backed by overwhelming confidence.
Even so, right now, there was no one else he could ask this of but Garfiel.

"Garfiel. Like I said a bunch of times in the dragon carriage..."

"I know, I know. Ain't plannin' on dyin' in some place like this either."

Cutting off Subaru's attempt to press the point, Garfiel shoved him along with his shoulder.
His attitude made it clear no more words needed to be exchanged.

To pile on any further would be crude toward his resolve, Subaru swallowed his words and pushed back against his shoulder.
That alone conveyed a heavy weight of trust pressed onto him.

"Alright, get goin' already. If you're in the way, I can't go all out."

Baring his fangs, Garfiel spat abuse at the others still remaining.
In response, Otto and Frederica exchanged glances, then,

"Garfiel, don't you dare die. I'm not cleaning up after Natsuki-san all by myself, no thank you."

"There's still so much left unsaid between us. Understood? You must, all three of us, with Grandmother."

"S-scary-faced onii-chan, do your best."

At the three's words, Garfiel gave a wry smile and nodded.
It felt to Subaru like a conversation stacking up death flags one after another, but he chose instead to take it the opposite way — that stacking up this many was reassurance enough, a survival flag — and cling to that hope.

"So, that means you two are still my opponents. This time, no lookin' away, no cheatin' on me. Get crushed by my claws and fangs and shield, and cry about it!"

Turning back with a stomp, Garfiel let out a roar.
Taking that fierce burst of spirit head-on, Elsa smiled, and the beast the girl rode let out a low, throat-trembling howl.

"Meili. Don't get in my way again this time."

"That's what I should say to you, Elsa! I'm just doing what Mama told me!"

Bickering with each other, Elsa and the girl directed their attacks at the roaring Garfiel.
Bracing himself, Garfiel caught the heavy blow and the sharp one with his two shields, and as sparks scattered, Subaru watched just long enough before breaking into a full sprint the other way.

"Otto! Frederica! Situation's changed! Since we can't just walk out the front of the manor, we're escaping through a different route so we don't get chewed up by Witchbeasts!"

"Different route, sure, but if it just leads out the back service entrance the result's the same, isn't it? If Garfiel's brute-force approach isn't an option either, then what do we do?"

"How about you negotiate desperately with the Witchbeasts using 'Divine Protection of the Soul of Language,' and we escape through diplomatic means as they let us pass? Your time to shine."

"Witchbeasts mostly only say 'ME, YOU, EAT ALL,' so there's no conversation to be had, y'know!"

To the thin-hoped proposal, Otto responded, running alongside him with a pathetic look on his face.
Even when words could be exchanged, there really were opponents with whom fundamental communication simply didn't work. That seemed true whether human or animal. Elsa was proof enough of that.
Given that, there was only one escape route Subaru could think of—

"Subaru-sama. I have one idea for an escape route."

"I know, Frederica. Probably the same place I was about to suggest myself. Except..."

There was a problem with that route.
Just as he was about to point it out, Subaru held his breath as he ran down the corridor.

"Whichever way we escape, this reeks of trouble no matter how you slice it, damn it!"

――From straight ahead, two hyenas noticed them and lunged.


The Battle for Roswaal Manor was changing cast, and still raging on in earnest.
