<<"School Re:Zero! 3rd Period!!">>


—Waking up every morning usually comes in the form of a sudden impact.

"—Come on, hurry up and get out of bed, I suppose!"

"Honduras!?"

A light voice rang out, followed by an impact directly to my stomach that was anything but light. Screaming "Ugyaa!" as I opened my eyes, the adorable face of my little sister was right in front of me today, as always.

"...Yo, Beako. Pretty, cute, and lovely again today, I see."

"Course I am, I suppose. Today as always, Betty is cute as a button, I suppose. Come on, it's a lovely morning, so get up quickly and eat breakfast with me, I suppose."

Beatrice sat atop my stomach, directing a smug grin at me. Keeping her light body perched right there, I nodded, saying "Alright, alright," and curled up on the spot.

"Ah! You don't understand at all, I suppose! Going back to sleep! That's the posture for going back to sleep, I suppose!"

"Ugh, understand me, Beako. I want to cherish the limited time I have in this finite life to eat meals with you, too. But just as much as that, I want to indulge in this decadent morning time..."

"Mukii! I won't allow that, I suppose! Big bro... Subaru! Which is more important to you, Betty or going back to sleep, I suppose?!"

"Asking something like an unreasonable actress in a daytime soap opera... No, wait, that gives me an idea!"

"—? What did you suddenly come up with now... Ukyaa!?"

Reaching up for the little girl swaying on top of my stomach, I dragged her straight into the futon with me.
Stroking the head of my little sister as she fit comfortably against my chest, I dragged Beatrice into my warm, decadent back-to-sleep time. —Talk about killing two birds with one stone: sleeping in AND little sister time!

"With this, Beako can enjoy going back to sleep too. Its power doubles the happiness of normal sleeping in... No, it's no exaggeration to say it goes even higher as an exponent!"

"Spouting stupid things again, I suppose! L-Let go, I suppose! Betty isn't such a child that she needs to sleep with Subaru anymore, I suppose!"

"Ehh, lately Beako won't sleep with me so I'm l-o-n-e-l-y... So I'm gonna take this opportunity to absorb my fill of Beatrise. *Sniff sniff.*"

"Don't sniff me, I suppose!"

Cute Beako squirmed and flailed in my arms.
Incidentally, "Beatrise" is a special brain chemical I came up with that can be absorbed by touching and adoring Beatrice. Without absorbing this, the men of the Natsuki family wither up and die. The sample size is two: me and Pops.
Now then, playing around with my little sister like this had, ironically enough, woken me up completely.

"Guh, damn it... So this was Beako's scheme all along. To use your own cute self as bait to separate me from my sleepiness in a diversionary tactic... You really pulled one over on me..."

"G-Gosh, I have no idea what you're talking about, but let go of me already, I suppose. Ah, my carefully done hair and clothes are completely messed up now, I suppose."

"Don't worry. Even completely messed up, your charm doesn't fade. Messed up has its own messed up appeal. Messed-Up Beako."

"You are completely annoying, I suppose!!"

An angry Beatrice hopped down from the bed and cutely stuck out her tongue at me. Scratching my head at my sister's tantrum, I sat up and tilted my head in confusion. "Huh?"
The reason was the clock. Strictly speaking, the time the clock was pointing to.
Namely—

"Hey now, Beako. You woke me up a whole hour earlier than usual. You're being the ultimate clumsy-chick. Come on, let's go back to sleep."

"Don't spread your arms out, I suppose! It makes me want to dive in, I suppose! No, that's not it, I woke you up an hour early this morning for a reason. So it's correct, I suppose."

"What do you mean, it's correct..."

"Starting this morning, Betty has a meeting for the school trip executive committee, I suppose. So eat breakfast earlier than usual, perk up, and head off to school, I suppose."

"I see, so I'm dragged into this... Why?!"

My dozing-off time having been ruined by Beatrice puffing out her chest to explain her circumstances, I rebelled.
No matter how cute a little sister she was, there were things you could and couldn't do. I maintained a firm stance against such selfish thinking.

"My resentment at having my sleep-in ruined runs deep. What logic could possibly defeat me? Come on, out with it, Beako! Why did you wake Big Brother up?!"

"Because if only Betty woke up early, I wouldn't get to say good morning, or see you later, or eat breakfast together with Subaru, and I'd be lonely, I suppose..."

"Damn it! So cute!"

In the end, a sister-con had no power to reject his little sister's cute request!
To answer Beatrice's honest feelings, I gallantly stepped down from the bed. It was true my mind was wide awake now, and going back to sleep at this point felt off anyway.
Answering his cute sister's plea was an older brother's duty—

"Alright, since that's settled, dress me, Beako."

"Do it yourself, I suppose!"

As expected, she wouldn't go that far for me.
At any rate, since I felt like there had been a mysterious period of being put on ice, properly delivering the requisite comedy routine was an important matter.


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△


"Well then, I'll be taking Beatrice-chan with me!"

"I am being taken, I suppose."

Despite the early morning, Petra-chan, who came to pick her up with a dazzling smile, took Beatrice along as they headed off to school.
The two were close friends who did everything together, and it seemed they were both on the school trip executive committee as well. They couldn't hide the sense of novelty from going to school at a different time than usual.

As an older brother, I worried about my sister who, despite having high all-rounder stats, constantly displayed little bits of clumsiness, but I felt secure knowing Petra-chan would cover for her well. As a duo, those two could probably take over the world.

"It's a little lonely for me, but seeing my sister off with a smile as she walks her path to glory is a brother's duty... If my fave can make it to Budokan, I can die happy..."

"Hey now, you're jumping the gun again, son. Sure, our daughter is a lovely girl who wouldn't be out of place center-stage at Budokan, but Papa won't allow that for another five years."

Seeing off my sister cheerfully at the entrance, slightly teary-eyed, Pops struck up a conversation with me. Pops had a high tendency to be half-naked, but on days when someone else's kid like Petra-chan visited, he made sure to wear clothes.
Printed on his T-shirt was Beatrice's smiling face—a piece that let you feel her loveliness nearby even on days she left early for school.

"Don't worry. The back is properly printed with your smiling face from when you were little."

"I wasn't worrying about that, and watch your boundaries with a teenage son. Beako aside, you never know what could trigger a family breakdown with a boy this age."

"Don't say such scary things. Sure, your smile on my back is from back in elementary school, but I love you just as much now that you're a high schooler. You know that, right?"

"Since I hear it at least once a day, yeah."

Saying "I love you" once a day, and telling those you like that you like them, is our family motto.
I respected Pops for cherishing it, but for a teenage boy, it was a rather tough motto to live by.

"Though I'm not embarrassed saying it to Beako or Mom, so maybe it's just your everyday behavior?"

"Bah! So uncute! Anyway, Beatrice left early, but what about you? You've got plenty of time, so want to wrestle sumo with me until Rem-chan arrives?"

"Waking up early just to sumo wrestle with my dad—since when did I become Hinomaru? Still, zoning out isn't much fun either... Alright."

It grated on me to play into Pops's hands, but I didn't want to waste time on something as fruitless as sumo wrestling with him either, so I quickly put on my shoes at the entrance.
An occasional early morning, a rare early hour. Might as well indulge in a morning stroll.

"They say the early bird gets the worm, so maybe something good will happen."

"I see, a stroll. Hold on a sec. I'll go change."

"I'm not going with you! I'm going alone! Go flirt with Mom!"

"Hmm, I see. My bad then. Feels like you're being considerate."

"Leaving my parents alone isn't called 'being considerate'! Stop acting like you're a classmate nudging a shy couple forward!"

After exchanging that unsettling banter, I trudged out of the house.
Somehow, it felt awkward to return anytime soon. My parents had been married for about twenty years, but their newlywed heat hadn't cooled off at all, making them tricky for a son and daughter to handle. Of course, having parents who got along so well was something to be grateful for.

"A happy home free of fights... Some people say clashing builds bonds, but smooth sailing is always best."

My personal theory is that the fewer worries, misfortunes, and obstacles you have, the better.
So while the Natsuki household was a thoroughly average middle-class family, just having good family relations and a cute little sister meant we were blessed beyond measure.

"Grateful for the miracle of being born in this era, giving a thumbs up with my entire presence, sunshine in the sky brightly lighting the future, don't block my way I'm a runaway train!"

Oh, my energy went up for no reason and I started rapping out loud.
I see, waking up early clears the head nicely. Basking in the cool morning breeze while walking through streets slightly emptier than usual felt quite poetic—

"—Ah, good morning, Subaru. What a coincidence meeting you here."

"Uhyee!?"

Just as I impulsively broke into a skip, a sudden voice addressed me, throwing me into utter chaos.
And small wonder: depending on the timing, she might have heard my rap just now, and above all, the voice that reached my ears was like a ringing silver bell.
There was only one person in my entire personal history who matched that voice, shining brilliantly.

"E-Emilia-tan? Why are you existing so early in the morning?!"

"Fufu. I get that you're surprised, but 'existing' is a bit much, isn't it? I perform normal biological functions even when Subaru's not looking, you know."

"T-That's true, of course. Haha, hehe, caught me off guard, fuhehe..."

Dammit, in the shock of this unexpected encounter, I laughed like a suspicious person.
Yet paying no mind to my deep self-reflection, the girl in front of me—the transfer student, Emilia—directed gentle eyes toward me, smiling like an angel.

A girl with long, beautiful moonlit silver hair and long, slender limbs.
Having just transferred, her school uniform wasn't ready yet, so she wore her old girls' academy uniform at school, but that wasn't the case here.
A thin shirt and a soft, fluffy skirt spreading outward—she was in casual clothes.

"Oh man, casual clothes are so cute..."

"Eh? Really? Thank you. Subaru's casual clothes are... um."

"Ah, this? This is my sweet lovely sister, Beatrice. Cute, right? This is her face with bedhead right after waking up..."

In front of Emilia tilting her head, I proudly displayed the T-shirt I was wearing.
Unlike the one Pops wore, mine had Beatrice's face printed on both front and back. I appreciated Pops's sentiment, but mixing in the impurity of my own existence when adoring Beatrice was very uncool.

"Woah, she's your little sister? Such a super cute girl!"

"Oooh, you get it? Exactly, a genuinely cute sister. Lately she's been going through a bit of a rebellious phase, but back then she'd follow me everywhere saying 'Big Bro, Big Bro'... Not to brag, but I raised Beako."

"Huh?! What about your dad and mom?!"

"Of course they contributed, but even combining both of them, it's a 7:3 ratio in my favor."

"I see. Subaru is a super hard worker..."

It was my usual light banter, but thanks to Emilia gullibly accepting everything, my tongue kept running away with me. Regardless, my initial shock had faded, leaving me enough breathing room to savor the joy of unexpectedly bumping into a beautiful transfer student.

"By the way, what were you up to, Emilia-tan? You're up pretty early."

"Hmm? I was taking a walk. You see, I just moved here to transfer schools, so I don't know the area at all. I have to study the neighborhood for Puck's sake, too."

"Huh, how diligent... Wait, Puck?"

"Yes, Puck. The cat I keep at home... Look, over there."

Following where Emilia pointed, I saw a gray ball of fluff curled up atop a brick wall a short distance away, swishing a long tail.
Squinting closer, I could tell it was a cat.
Noticing my gaze, the cat turned its round, button eyes toward me and—

"Hey there."

"Eh!?"

"W-What's wrong!? Making a sudden noise like that..."

Beside me, shocked by the sudden turn of events, Emilia was shocked by my reaction.
Still, acting like that was normal didn't help me. I mean, just now, that cat—

"It talked, right? It raised a paw at me and said 'Hey there'..."

"Huh? That's impossible. Jeez, saying things like that... Even if you're trying to tease me, I won't be fooled!"

Yet when I tried to confirm, Emilia wouldn't entertain it at all. No, her reaction was actually the normal one. If Emilia had said, "Oh right, Puck talks! Super rare, isn't it?", I wouldn't have known how to handle that either.

"Well, must have misheard or imagined it. Whew, gave me a fright."

"Right, right. Best to leave it at that. Subaru wouldn't want to startle Lia or have her looking at you like you're a weirdo, right?"

"You're talking to me super naturally, man!"

The cat that hopped off the wall—Puck—curled up at my feet and spoke to me in that casual manner. Stunned by his unshrinking non-human presence, I scooped Puck up.
Puck pressed a paw flat against my face with a soft *plop*. What incredible fluffiness.

"By the way, I don't possess any special ability to talk to animals. Meaning the weird one isn't me, it's you."

"Well, if Subaru wants to claim that, I won't stop you, but even if you do, Subaru's the one who'll be looked at strangely. Here, take a look."

Prompted by that, I looked to my side. Emilia was rounding her dark blue eyes, looking back and forth between me and Puck as we engaged in a staring contest at close range.

"It almost really looks like Subaru and Puck are talking. It's rare for Puck to be held by someone he just met without thrashing around, too."

"Is that so? Out of curiosity, how did it look to you, Emilia-tan?"

"Eh? It looked like Puck was meowing cutely, and Subaru was making a mean face saying something..."

"My reputation is terrible! Wait, are you serious about this..."

Judging by Emilia's reaction, it seemed Puck's voice truly couldn't be heard by anyone other than me. Slipping through my hands as I panicked over this realization, Puck landed gracefully on the ground.
And washing his face with a paw—

"Don't worry, it's not like some strange story is starting because of this. It's not as if I'm gonna turn you into a magical girl, grant your wishes, or go on about cosmic entropy, so contract with me with peace of mind."

"I'm not contracting with you! The word 'contract' is nowhere near peace of mind!"

"Just a joke, just a joke."

Giggling merrily, Puck leaped right up to Emilia's chest. Emilia caught Puck's body and petted his back.

"Still, I'm glad Puck and Subaru are getting along. I was a little worried."

"Worried? Why?"

"Because Puck doesn't usually get along well with my friends... Ah, is it okay to call Subaru my friend? Is that too presumptuous?"

"No no no, not at all! Not at all! In fact, I'm deeply honored to be installed as Friend Number One. Eventually, I'd like to aim for a rank-up beyond friend status, even."

"Sorry, I don't really get what you're saying."

Once my mouth started running, I always ended up saying extra unnecessary things—my bad habit.
Anyway, meeting Emilia like this was fate working its magic—

"Since you were taking a walk anyway, mind if I join? We're both heading to school in the end, so let me treat you to a date."

"A d-a-t-e... Outings with a boy! You're right!"

"Whoa, is this your first time?"

Seeing Emilia's eyes sparkle, I remembered she was raised in an all-girls academy going all the way from elementary through high school.
A sheltered schoolgirl wouldn't have had opportunities to hang out with boys. Which meant—

"It's my first time on a d-a-t-e... What do we do?"

"Wait! If it's Emilia-tan's first date, I want to do it properly! At the very least, an outfit with my sister's face printed on the front, back, AND armpits isn't suitable!"

"Hidden Beatrice-chan in the armpits...!"

Besides, I was the type who wanted to do things properly if I was setting my mind on a date.
Since it was a moment meant to be etched into memory, I wanted the stage set right.

"Thinking calmly about it, I don't have date experience either..."

"Really? But Subaru seems to get along with everyone in class..."

"Well, if it's simply hanging out with girls, I do that plenty. I go shopping with my childhood friend Rem all the time, and Rem's elder sister Ram drags me along on errands too."

Still, counting outings with childhood friends felt a bit too lonely, didn't it?
Of course, I'd accompanied Crusch, Ferris, and Anastasia on shopping trips to help the student council, and I'd been dragged around by an annoyed Priscilla when her retainer Al couldn't be caught. I'd played with the six beautiful sisters next door, too—

"Looking at it that way, I might look surprisingly experienced from the outside..."

"The outside and inside don't match. Yep, yep, I get it. Subaru totally gives off that vibe."

"Shut up for a cat! Don't read my mind without permission!"

I shouted at Puck, who was purring in Emilia's arms. But Puck put on an innocent face, while Emilia looked at me in bewilderment. Damn it.

"Gah, anyway! Today's walk isn't a date. This is a stroll-date... right, counted separately from a real date. Let's go with that."

"I don't quite get it, but if you're okay with that, I am too. So, what do we do then?"

"Hmm, let's see. For now, Emilia-tan is getting a grasp of the neighborhood geography while picking a walking route for Puck, right?"

Thinking calmly, unlike dogs, cats aren't usually walked around on a leash by their owners, but since she didn't seem to doubt that, I refrained from poking fun at it.
Now, deciding what route to introduce was the tricky part—

"For me, I'd like a good view where a nice breeze blows. I don't want too much exercise, so avoid steep slopes... Also, per cat treaty, I haven't greeted the alley cats around here yet. So I'd appreciate avoiding the boss cat's territory."

"You're giving me ridiculously specific demands... No, actually, that helps."

"Right? Being told 'anywhere is fine' on a date is the worst."

Frowning at Puck talking like he knew it all, having strict selection criteria did make it easier to narrow down options.
And so, the route I chose for my stroll-date with Emilia was—

"Alright, then let's head for the riverbank. There's a big river flowing right next to it, so it looks grand and feels great."


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△


And so, lured by those words, we arrived at our town's riverbank.
With a famous, large river flowing right beside it, this bank was prized as a New Year's sunrise viewing spot, and on weekdays was known as a jogging course for fitness-minded youth and seniors alike.

Of course, plenty of people visited for the same purpose as us, and here and there dog owners could be seen chatting happily.
Could be seen, but—

"As expected, I don't see any owners walking cats..."

"Maybe it's because cats hate wearing collars and leashes... But Puck often wakes me up saying he wants to go for a walk."

"Huh, really."

"Yep, yep. After all, a new day doesn't feel real unless I'm with Lia."

Even if the goal was walking a pet, the differences in details stood out, making Emilia lower her brows in worry while holding Puck.
Emilia's claim sounded like an over-doting pet owner projecting her own feelings, but hearing Puck's actual thoughts, it wasn't one-sided love. My bias that most cats in the world viewed their owners as servants was apparently wrong.

"Though even for a walk, Emilia-tan has been holding him the whole time."

"—? Yes, Puck doesn't like walking, so I always carry him. But when he gets super pudgy, I make sure he gets proper exercise."

"Is that so? I had this preconception that once an animal loses its shape, it can't go back, but you can pull off Rizap-style transformations like that."

"Well, it's the fruit of my effort."

"Fatness is the fruit of sloth, though..."

I dropped my shoulders at Puck's triumphant face.
Well, since he was currently slender, he had no intention of leaving Emilia's arms. If Emilia wasn't finding him heavy, I had nothing further to say.

"Still, isn't carrying a full-grown cat around tough? Though Puck's relatively small."

"I'm fine. Despite how I look, I'm pretty strong. Even at my old school, whenever there was heavy lifting, people relied on me right away."

"Being called strong by all-girls academy standards doesn't say much..."

Even if she appealed her strength with slender white arms, her upper arms looked so soft that it lacked persuasion. For the record, I also had moderate confidence in being surprisingly ripped under my clothes.
Most issues like this came down to a lack of self-objectivity, so Emilia and I were probably neck and neck.
As we chatted like that—

"—Oh? Subaru-kun, what brings you here?"

Called by an extremely familiar voice, I flinched in surprise.
There was no need to turn around to verify who it was. After all, to me, it was a voice as familiar as family—or even more so.

"Huh? Rem? What are you doing here?"

"I came to accompany Sister this morning. But to think Rem would run into Subaru-kun... Sister's divine favor truly is magnificent."

"Even if you treat her like a shrine deity, Ram is still your sister."

I offered a dry smile at Rem spouting nonsense as usual, answering her back.
Standing on the riverbank in her usual cute casual attire was Rem. She came over to play often on days off, so there was no novelty, but adding the condition of a weekday morning instantly made her appearance seem radiant. Ah, the weekday effect.

"Hey, Subaru. That girl is in our class, right?"

"Hmm? Ah, yeah. This is Rem. My all-around domestic prodigy of a childhood friend."

Nudged in the back by Emilia, I reflected on leaving her out. Stepping one pace to the side, I introduced Rem to Emilia.
And then—

"Rem, as you know, this is Emilia-tan. We ran into each other while walking her pet cat, so we're currently picking a walking route suitable for a lazy cat."

"I see, so that's how it is. —Allow Rem to introduce herself again. Rem's name is Rem. Rem and Subaru-kun have been close since sharing the same first bath water."

"Don't lie. The one who shared your first bath water was your sister."

"That is true. Rem misspoke; Rem meant wrapped in the same swaddling clothes."

"That was also your sister."

Well, I didn't have consciousness right after birth so I couldn't be sure, but since Rem and I had birthdays two months apart, I surmised that hadn't happened.

"Ah, thank you for the formal greeting. I'm Emilia. And this is Puck, the gray cat."

"Sounds like an intro for a delivery service..."

"My, what a cute cat. May Rem pet him?"

"Of course. Come on, Puck, show your tummy."

"Can't be helped, meow."

Puck rolled onto his back looking quite pleased, while Emilia and Rem giggled as they played with him. Watching them with arms crossed, I tilted my head.

"Rem, you said you were accompanying your sister earlier, but why the riverbank? What does Ram have to do with this?"

"That is because Sister is undergoing special training."

"Special training?"

"Yes."

Tickling Puck's belly with a finger, Rem smiled proudly.
Whenever Rem boasted like this, it was invariably about Ram or me. Leaving Ram aside, having her brag about me to others made me feel ticklish, but since I boasted about Rem when talking to others, we were even on that front.

"Rem-san's sister is Ram-san from our class, right?"

"Yes, that is correct. She is a magnificent Sister, far too good for Rem."

"So yeah, Rem's sister-worship is practically a chronic condition, so take it with a grain of salt."

"Muu, that is not true at all."

Pouting her cheeks, Rem turned her face away. Continuing while turned away, Rem began speaking about Ram: "So, you see..."

"As Subaru-kun knows, Sister possesses outstanding athletic ability and is often asked to help out various sports clubs..."

"Ah, yeah. I see her flashy activity in all kinds of clubs. The way she handles herself among guys without any trouble is a god-given talent..."

Ram's strange trait was mastering anything she tried quickly, but in events relying on raw sense, she was extraordinarily good.
So on that point, Rem's exaggerated praise wasn't wrong.

"So, what happened with such an amazing Ram-san?"

"Yes. Being such a reliable Sister, she was asked to assist another club... and wishing to take it seriously, she began morning special training."

"Ram did?! Doing something so admirable?!"

Quite astonishing—a bolt out of the blue to me.
Ram, who felt like she lived solely on innate talent, was basically arrogant and overconfident, though she always produced matching results.
So I thought she was completely unacquainted with anything resembling hard work.

"Besides, isn't she obsessed with taking care of Roswaal-san the novelist right now? She even dragged Rem along to fuss over him, didn't she?"

"That is true, but she is working hard on this separately. Therefore, Rem wished to support her efforts and made these preparations."

"Woah, impressive. That's why you have a setup like a picnic."

Behind an enthusiastic Rem, lined up on a picnic mat, were a picnic basket, thermoses, a first-aid kit for emergencies, towel sets, and the like.
Always making flawless preparations was Rem's virtue, but overthinking things and preparing unnecessary items was a slight flaw that came out at the same time.

"Well, being cared for this much, Ram must be pretty satisfied... But what kind of club substitute role requires Ram to go that far?"

"The girls' judo club."

"Girls' judo club... Did our school even have one of those? Never knew."

Even hearing Rem's explanation, I couldn't picture the girls' judo club.
Our Lugunica Academy was fairly active in club sports, often posting decent results at prefectural tournaments.
So famous clubs would naturally enter one's ears from time to time.

"After all, our handicrafts club and home economics club are regulars at nationals."

"Oh, really? That's amazing... Wait! Nationals for handicrafts and home economics?!"

"Whoa, wondering what they do? Let me tell you, this area is pretty jock-heavy... Calling them cultural sports clubs wouldn't be an exaggeration. Competing over running stitch speed, threading as many needles as possible within a time limit, precise sewing machine setup..."

Surprisingly, when pushed to a competitive level, handicrafts had deep mechanics.
Incidentally, our school was a regular at national tournaments, but usually the handicrafts and home economics clubs entered simultaneously and ended up eliminating each other.
We just accepted that as a proxy war between the tiger and hawk, advisors Frederica-sensei and Clind-sensei.

"Well, the most famous club in our school is the light music club led by Liliana-chan. They even do national tours and are a big deal in the indie scene."

"That doesn't sound like a school club at all anymore!"

"Lugunica Academy is lax with discipline in those areas, so it is permitted. It seems Liliana-teacher's passion reached Principal Wilhelm as well."

"The legendary direct appeal where Liliana-chan's teeth-guitar struck right to Principal Wilhelm's heart... Wish I could've seen it while enrolled."

Liliana-chan and the principal seemed like opposites, but sharing a passion for things they loved made them surprisingly compatible.
Liliana-chan, moved by the principal bragging about his wife, made "Sword Demon Love Song" a smash hit on the light music club's setlist, complete with a photo of the principal and his wife on the cover jacket.

"Anyway, thinking about Liliana-chan is pure nonsense so setting that aside... I get the girls' judo substitute thing, but why is Ram doing special training?"

"That is..."

"—Wanting to know Ram's every move, what a disgusting man."

"Uhyowai!?"

Hearing a cold voice shoot from behind out of nowhere, I jumped in spite of myself.
Cutting off Rem's answer and digging nails directly into my eardrums was my other childhood friend, Ram. Dressed in a hoodie and running shoes, Ram was panting lightly, staring me down with her pale pink eyes.

"And why is Barusu here? Don't tell me you caught wind of Ram's special training and came all this way just to mock Ram?"

"As if I have that kind of free time! As Sister knows, I'm the type who secures sleep down to the absolute limit. That's why I chose the school closest to home."

"And because Barusu chose a school for such a reason, Ram was forced to attend that school as well... Hah. How selfish."

Placing a hand to her forehead, wiping sweat droplets with a finger, Ram sighed.
Behind this exchange between Ram and me, I heard Emilia softly whisper to Rem.

"Hey, why did Ram have to go to the school Subaru picked?"

"That is simple. Because Rem decided to go to whichever school Subaru-kun attended. And Rem wished to attend the same school as Sister, so Rem asked Sister to do so as well."

"I see... Wait, so it was Rem-san's selfish request?"

"Yes. Sister is far too kind to listen to Rem's selfish request."

Cheeks flushing slightly, Rem savored the realization of being loved by her sister.

"Well, sure, I was massively surprised..."

Scratched my cheek embarrassed at Echidna's mischievous playfulness.

"Still, Emilia-tan yesterday, Echidna-san today... throwing exciting surprise events one after another. As expected of Delica-sensei, true entertainer!"

"Guaaaah!!"

Two pretty girls fighting over me, both my shoulders screamed instantly.
