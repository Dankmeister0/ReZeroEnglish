Arc 7 Chapter 29: "How Each of Them Stands"

——The victory condition for retaking the throne: securing the "Nine Divine Generals."

The moment the presented victory condition was revealed, along with the names and epithets of the "Nine Divine Generals" necessary to achieve it, Subaru could feel the air in the meeting hall grow taut all at once.
Naturally, the names of the Nine Divine Generals—names surely familiar to any citizen of the Empire—were, to Subaru, without exception, hearing them for the first time.

"...If this were a manga or anime, the scene where the names of all the top brass get revealed at once would be the kind of thing that gets you hyped."

But when it's happening to you personally, it's not a development you can enjoy without reservation.
The names and epithets of the Nine Divine Generals that Abel had recounted were each convincing enough to sound impressive, each one enough to make you imagine just how troublesome its bearer would be.
Given that Arakiya's epithet, "Spirit Eating," was literally her defining trait, it stood to reason the others' bynames were likewise tied to their peculiar abilities or methods of combat.

"The ones where you can guess what kind of person they are just from the name—that's about 'Curse Tools Master' and 'Flying Dragon General,' right?"

Both of the ones he'd pointed out were surely epithets derived from their fighting style or the tools they wielded.
The rest of the epithets were probably rooted in appearance or some famous exploit. In Chinese works like Romance of the Three Kingdoms or Water Margin, characters often took on bynames for that kind of reason.
Come to think of it, those stories were also about seizing a nation, in their own way. Unfortunately, Subaru only knew the gist of them and wasn't especially well-versed in the details.

"Not the time to be escaping into reality-adjacent trivia. Anyway, about this 'securing the Nine Divine Generals is the victory condition' thing—there's a few things I want to ask."

"What. Is there room for doubt?"

Having recovered from his initial shock, Abel rested a hand on the map spread across the table and raised an eyebrow.
If he thought he'd explained everything with that, then this was a prime example of a clever man's failure to say enough. Compared to everyone else here—not just Zikr and the others, but even the People of Shudrak—Subaru's stock of information about the Vollachian Empire was overwhelmingly the smallest.

"So yeah, there's plenty of room for doubt. If you keep skipping explanations just because you already understand things, the people around you are gonna think you're some guy nobody can figure out."

"I'll take that as advice and let it pass through one ear and out the other. State your doubts."

"Don't let it pass through—keep it lodged in your chest..."

Subaru sighed at Abel, who answered with arms crossed and an air of haughtiness. Then, steeling himself against the sense of gathering eyes, he said "Listen up," holding up four fingers on one hand and five on the other.

"So, the Nine Divine Generals—thankfully they split into an odd number, which makes it easy to understand this is a numbers game. But the situation is: the ones favorably disposed toward us, we don't know if they're alive or dead, while Arakiya and this Chisha guy are definitely enemies. That's already a pretty rough position."

The premise required securing five or more, and yet the other side already had two of the Nine Divine Generals locked down.
On top of that, the fate of Goz, the man who'd been helping Abel, was unknown, so there was a good chance they'd already lost even the one card they could have counted on for certain.

"That makes the odds look even worse. It's nice that the victory condition is clear-cut, but realistically, is talking five of the Nine Divine Generals into joining us even feasible?"

"Whoa now, brother, that's underestimating the Emperor way too much. I said what I said earlier, but the fact that he won the Imperial Selection Ceremony is a guaranteed credential. It's not like they'd go talk to him and get turned away flat. As long as they're not refused at the gate..."

"——The chance to strike off the heads of those who defy me would likewise be secured."

"Get them on our side, would you!"

Al's remark, meant to dissolve Subaru's doubts, was incinerated by his own master's extreme comment. They were discussing how to bring the Nine Divine Generals onto their side, and devising ways to kill the ones who wouldn't join was putting the cart before the horse.

"Still, that iron-helmeted jester's remark hits the mark. In truth, if I say I wish to meet with one of the Generals, there is no General who would refuse. The problem lies in what comes after the meeting."

"'Iron-helmeted jester'—that's me?"

"There's no other iron helmet here. As for being treated as a jester, if you have complaints, start by filing them with Priscilla first."

Silencing Al's needling, Subaru returned to the main topic with a "First of all"—

"I don't get how this Nine Divine Generals system even works. I'm picturing nine generals directly under the Emperor, but doesn't that mean they're not all stationed in the capital?"

"Each General is a pillar of the state. Look upon the needlessly vast expanse of the Empire's territory. Though the capital sits at the nation's heart, could all nine remain there and still respond swiftly to emergencies?"

"Indeed. Under His Excellency's reign the incidents have dropped dramatically, but even so, the embers of internal strife never quite stop smoldering somewhere in the Empire. Guarding the Empire is not as simple as securing the capital alone."

Supplemented by Priscilla and Zikr, Subaru put a hand to his chin, convinced.
"...So the Nine Divine Generals in question are also scattered all over the capital and beyond, is that it?"

By way of a familiar example, Roswaal too had been granted the grandiose title of Margrave of the Western Domain in the Kingdom of Lugunica, and was apparently obligated to act swiftly in the kingdom's moment of crisis.
Of course, there was also permission to raise a private army on one's domain for that purpose. ——Though in Roswaal's case, individual combat strength probably outstripped anything a poorly organized army could manage.
Just flying through the sky raining down fireballs would probably be enough to shut down most opponents completely.

"Thinking about it that way, that guy's a pretty broken unit himself... makes me a little curious where he'd rank if you compared him to the Nine Divine Generals."

"Not sure what yer on about, but ain't the talk driftin', Natsumi?"

"My bad. Anyway, I get that the Nine Divine Generals are scattered all over the country. So then, is it fair to say not all of them were involved in the coup in the capital?"

"From what I have seen, and reasoning it out practically, that would seem to be the case."

Abel nodded to Subaru's confirming question.
Frankly, given that Abel's lack of popularity had only just been exposed, there was some anxiety about trusting his assessment—but since no one else pointed anything out, it seemed to be a topic that could, for now, be swallowed and accepted.

In any case, that not all of the Nine Divine Generals had turned against them was good news.
It also made sense of the condition that securing more of the Nine Divine Generals would bring victory in this fight closer. Given that, if Subaru had one more doubt to raise, it would be—

"Sorry for all the questions, but one more. About the rankings among the Nine Divine Generals—assuming it's simply ordered by strength, then the priority should be going after whoever ranks highest, right?"

"Aye, that assumption is correct."

"Then, since he's apparently on par with Reinhard—Cecilus, was it? Wouldn't going after him be the textbook move... or, I mean, wouldn't that basically settle things?"

The four strongest of humanity, whose names thundered across the Four Great Nations.
If this Cecilus being discussed was a man spoken of in the same breath as Reinhard, then wasn't it practically true that this one person alone could decide the outcome of the fight?
In fact, an unreasonable sort of expectation and resignation had taken root in Subaru—that Reinhard alone could probably win even against an entire kingdom's worth of soldiers.
So naturally, that same expectation extended to Cecilus, his equal in rank.
However——

"...It is true that securing that one would resolve our troop-strength problem all at once."

"Then why the bitter face?"

Abel's brow furrowed, and the expression with which he answered Subaru's question was far from pleased.
Subaru's expectation didn't seem to have missed the mark, and yet the expression wasn't free of misgiving either. Abel revealed the reason with a small exhale.

"Securing the Nine Divine Generals is the victory condition because troops and officers rally to their martial exploits. Bring more of the Nine Divine Generals under your banner, and that many more troops join your ranks. Understand?"

"——? Yeah, I get it. So the point is, you should get the strongest one on your side. Or is it that 'strongest in the Empire' title's a fraud?"

"No, there's no doubt that wretch is the strongest in the Empire. There is, however, a problem."

"A problem?"

"——That wretch has no popular following."

At the single line delivered after all that buildup, Subaru's thoughts froze for a moment.
Wondering what on earth could be the issue, the slowly-delivered words sank into his brain. No popular following—so the information had been correctly digested.

"You're saying that? You?"

"It is fact. He holds the seat of 'One' among the Empire's Generals, and yet that one has no authority whatsoever. Even were I to grant him some, there is nothing he could do with it. All that one is capable of is cutting people down."

"Don't put a guy like that in a general's position!"

"Now now, calm down, brother! That part's just how things are done in the Empire, can't be helped!"

At the assessment of Cecilus delivered by Abel, Al put Subaru, who'd started to lash out, into a headlock. Holding Subaru down with one arm, Al tilted his head to look at Abel.

"Honestly, all he's been given besides the title of 'One' is nothin'. Just imagine if they'd handed him authority to match the position. A smooth-talkin' type coulda used him something fierce, y'know?"

"Naturally, it must never come to pass that that wretch be used by anyone but myself. Were such a danger to exist, I would have disposed of him far sooner."

"But you were out there in the woods with nobody backin' you up...!"

Being told the reins were held, when the actual state of affairs said otherwise, was nothing more than talking big.
To begin with, the very question of whether a man hailed as the strongest in the Empire could fail to gather any popular following, in a Vollachian Empire that worshipped strength above all—was that something to take at face value?

"What about it, Zikr-san? What do you think!"

"Me?"

"Yeah. Go on, give me your honest opinion as one of the Empire's Generals. What do you think of General Cecilus?"

Prompted, Zikr said "Let's see," crossing his short arms in thought.
Then he nodded a few times at Subaru's expectant eyes, and—

"First, there is no doubt that Lord Cecilus is a pillar of national defense, and a symbol of the Vollachian Empire's martial might. He embodies the very ideal that the Empire's people should be strong."

"Ooh, good start. And?"

"As a person, he's unrestrained and approachable, a cheerful and pleasant man whose attitude never changes toward anyone. All told..."

"All told...?"

"Most officers and soldiers regard Lord Cecilus with fear, as a monster impossible to understand, someone with whom deep communication is impossible. I believe His Excellency's assessment to be correct."

"That's a heck of a sudden pivot in the evaluation!"

Coming from Zikr, who up to this point had shown nothing but the face of a genuinely nice person, this was clearly a very carefully worded assessment.
One could read it as Zikr aligning his opinion out of consideration for Abel the Emperor, but the furrow of his brow made plain the effort behind it, and the statement was, most likely, simply true.
In other words——

"To seize the 'One' of the Nine Divine Generals and tip the scales all at once was too lazy a scheme to be countenanced. Even wracking that empty head of his, the jester cannot come up with a decent 'idea,' it seems."

"Shut up! Don't go pinning the guy's lack of popularity on me too! Besides, if neither the Emperor nor the generals have any popular support, no wonder there was a coup!"

"You dare repeat yourself how many times? Do not think insolence will forever go overlooked."

Under the sharp gazes leveled at him from both Priscilla and Abel, Subaru stuck his tongue out at the pair of them.
Still, the fact remained that his scheme had crumbled all too easily. If securing the Nine Divine Generals was equal to securing troop numbers, there was no merit in rushing to secure a General with no popular following.

"If anything, if he's got no following, maybe it's fine to just leave him be...?"

"That too is a problem. That one has, depending on circumstance, the capability to single-handedly change the tide of a battle. Even should we secure every other one of the Nine Divine Generals, there remains ample possibility that this one alone could take my head."

"What a pain in the ass this guy is! He's a total liability, isn't he!"

Little merit in rushing to secure him, and yet leaving him be made him too dangerous a bomb.
It was precisely now, in a distant foreign land, that Subaru truly came to appreciate just how excellent a "property" Reinhard was, whose strength and character came fully guaranteed.
If he just hollered loud enough, would Reinhard come running even from a neighboring country?

"Mind you, fool. As Sword Saint, he cannot cross the border on account of treaties between nations. Best not to entertain such worthless hopes."

"Don't go reading my mind. I wasn't seriously gonna try it. ...Only counting on a friend when you're in trouble ain't the kind of attitude you should have toward a buddy anyway."

Just because he'd come running if called didn't mean it was right to use him conveniently like that.
If things got truly, genuinely dire, Subaru might not be able to keep saying that—but until that moment arrived, he was resolved to hold on to his sense of ethics.

"So then? Has the fool being treated as Abel's strategist run out of doubts?"

"Not run out, and I'm no strategist, but the first part is..."

Sneered at by Priscilla over his position, Subaru answered with a scowl. And, just as he moved to steer the meeting's topic onward——

"——Lady Natsumi, and Mr. Village Chief! Pardon the intrusion!"

Along with a spirited voice, the door burst open, and a new figure appeared vigorously in the meeting hall.
The one who'd arrived was a handsome man with long golden hair swaying briskly, dressed in an outfit centered on blue—Flop.

Flop was supposed to have been tending to the wounded upstairs, where the scars of Arakiya's rampage still remained. Drawing every eye in the room to himself, he looked at Subaru and Abel and nodded, "There you are."
Flop's appearance was stained here and there with blood, throwing into stark relief the grim, field-hospital-like scene of the treatment site.
But the fact that he'd appeared here at all meant——

"Flop-san, how's everyone doing?"

"Just reached a stopping point! ...Hmm? Given how you're acting, is it the husband-kun mood rather than Lady Natsumi right now? Then I'll switch back to calling you husband-kun."

"Um, either's fine. But, I see, a stopping point..."

Taking in Flop's somewhat off-kilter show of consideration, Subaru chewed on both relief and anxiety at once upon hearing that the treatment had reached a pause.
The relief was simple: the treatment of the wounded had concluded. The anxiety was over having to hear the results of that treatment.

The goal of a "bloodless surrender of the city" had already ended in failure.
Seven guards had been sacrificed in the course of Arakiya being carried off. All that remained was whether anyone among the wounded would join the ranks of those casualties.

"No one has died, husband-kun."

"Huh..."

"Everyone was in quite a rough state, mind you. But I'd wager it's thanks to your wife and your niece working so hard. Lady Taritta and Lady Utakata's quickness helped too. And of course, my sister and I gave it our all as well!"

Reading Subaru's inner state from his expression, Flop had given him the answer preemptively.
Pointing sharply at himself and boasting of his own contribution, Flop was refreshingly unabashed about it. The answer he'd given was concise too, and it took Subaru a beat to fully absorb the information.
But once understanding sank in a beat later, a great, great sigh escaped him.

"No one died...!"

"Ah, proof that everyone gave their utmost effort to survive. Why, if my sister hadn't shielded me, I might have taken a blow to the head and died! Hah, I really can't hold my head up around my sister!"

"Yeah, yeah, that's really true. Can't hold my head up around Medium-san either..."

Flop laughed cheerfully, and before him, Subaru bowed his head, shoulders trembling.
No exaggeration, no joke—wholehearted agreement. If Medium hadn't been there—no, if anyone at all hadn't been there—this report might never have come.
Some lives had been lost. But some lives had also not been lost.
And the emotion that brought welling up in his chest was immense——

"——Merchant, what became of the healer."

Oblivious to Subaru's welling emotion, Abel cut into the conversation in a flat tone.
Hearing the unfamiliar phrase "healer," Subaru furrowed his brow, and Flop too murmured "Healer..." pressing a finger to his own lips in thought.

"Could that possibly mean... husband-kun's wife?"

"Who else would it be. Was there anyone else present capable of healing magic besides that girl?"

"No, I can't think of anyone else offhand! Only—"

"Only?"

"Village chief-kun, don't you think you ought to speak of her in a way that would earn more goodwill from the other party? Even in how you refer to her, being mindful of approachability would make things go so much more smoothly!"

A remarkably head-on, cheerful protest—Abel raised an eyebrow at it.
Flop, having said his piece, stood there quite composed, but frankly, for those listening nearby, it was a rather nerve-wracking thing to hear said aloud.
Of course, having Rem reduced to being called by her mere function didn't sit well with Subaru either.

"Coming from you, that sentiment doesn't carry much weight, given the insolence you were just showing, brother."

"Considering the road we've traveled to get here, I figure I've earned the right to talk like that a little... and I guess that means Flop-san's got the same right?"

"Dunno about that, but I'm mighty curious about you brothers' wild road trip."

It was too turbulent a tale to properly recount, and delving into it would have to wait for another occasion.
Whatever way Abel chose to phrase it, Rem's safety was Subaru's top priority as well. Of course, he'd confirmed with his own eyes that she bore no notable external injuries.

"She hasn't collapsed from overdoing it or anything, has she?"

"No need to worry there, husband-kun. Of course, she's quite worn out from having pushed herself so admirably, but that's the sort of thing that improves with rest. You have a truly hard-working wife, and that's a wonderful thing indeed."

"I see... that's a relief, then."

Letting out a breath of relief, Subaru took comfort in Flop's assurance.
Given the circumstances, even though they had to rely on Rem, the thought of her overexerting herself was genuinely frightening. No matter what Subaru said to her, the Rem of now likely wouldn't listen if it was something within her own power to do.

"——Wife?"

Just then, beside Subaru who'd let out that breath of relief, Al murmured quietly.
Tilting his head, hand at the chin of his iron helmet, he said—

"Didn't see her upstairs, I don't think. That mean your gal's here too, brother?"

"My gal—wait, you mean Emilia-tan? No, she's not here. Man, how reassuring would it be if she were here... though part of me also kinda doesn't want her here."

Emilia's gentle nature and the way of the Vollachian Empire were oil and water.
Emilia, who tended to think things through in a somewhat muscle-headed way, might actually mesh well with the Empire's style on some gut level—but the Empire's cruelty went further still beyond that. ——The Empire didn't suit Emilia.

"——A wife other than the gal, huh."

"...I'll have you know, that's just how I've been putting it for convenience's sake. Truth be told, she's someone I've decided to treasure that much. No matter what, I'm bringing her home."

"————"

Hand still at his chin, Al murmured low, and Subaru offered that explanation in response.
As long as they were both in the Empire, it wasn't like some strange rumor was going to spread from Al's mouth—but Subaru still wanted no part of being misjudged. Nor did he want his resolve doused with cold water.

"You there, the pretty boy referred to as 'merchant'... are you Abel's subordinate as well?"

"Ah, not quite a subordinate. My sister and I have, given the circumstances, ended up in a position of cooperating with husband-kun and village chief-kun. Well, perhaps 'newest friend' would be the most fitting description!"

"Oho. A friend, is it."

Priscilla, having exchanged words with Flop, loosened her lips at his answer. Hiding those loosened lips behind her fan, she sent a meaningfully suggestive gaze toward Abel.

"To think you've been busy making friends without my knowledge. It seems the Vollachian Emperor's throne has become quite an easy thing to leave vacant."

"Cease the sarcasm. To begin with, I have no memory of becoming friends with that man there."

"What are you saying, village chief-kun! We're the ones who crossed through a life-or-death crisis together in drag, aren't we!"

"Does crossing through a life-or-death crisis together instantly make one a friend? Then every soldier of the Empire would be counted my friend. And the one who crossed through the closest brush with death alongside me is my enemy."

A perfect rebuttal built on the very position Abel himself occupied—Flop, receiving it, fell silent.
That said, it was also a double-edged sword of an argument; Abel himself hadn't come through it unscathed either.

"Anyway, Flop-san brought us good news. I'd like more news with that kind of energy on our side too..."

"——Wait."

"...What now."

In the midst of a none-too-optimistic meeting, this had finally been a bright topic. Just as Subaru moved to use it for momentum, Abel cut him off and jerked his chin toward Flop.
Guided by that motion, Subaru too looked over at Flop, and—

"Flop-san?"

He noticed then, the reason Abel had singled him out—the change in his expression.
Flop, who never failed to wear a bright and cheerful expression, whose very being had consistently been like the sun—in his eyes now floated a faint trace of hesitation and sorrow.

"Being a merchant, you'd think you'd have grown careful about how you broach a topic. On that point, I find it hard to believe you're suited to being a merchant at all."

"I've received that opinion quite a number of times myself, so I do have thoughts on it, but let's set that aside for now. ...Husband-kun, there was a part of what I said earlier that fell short of the whole truth."

"————"

Glancing sideways at Abel's remark, Flop trembled his long lashes with sorrow as he looked at Subaru.
His finely made features, and the earnestness that surfaced upon them now, squeezed at Subaru's chest. He didn't want to hear what came next, and yet he couldn't help but listen.
In that sense, Flop had a natural gift for making others listen to him. Under any other circumstances, it would have been a talent worth praising to the skies as fit for a merchant.
But——

"It isn't unrelated to you either, village chief-kun. I'd like Lady Kuna and Lady Holly to come along with us too."

Unable to cut off Flop as he said this, Subaru, just now, hated that gift of his as though it were a cursed talent.

"——So Abel and Natsumi came too, huh."

Called by Flop, Subaru and the others left the meeting hall once and headed up to the upper floor of the city hall.
In a space where the wounded had been gathered, now resembling a field hospital, the one who greeted Subaru and the others with those words was Mizelda, her singed black hair cut evenly short.

Of the group that had assaulted Guaral, now routed by Arakiya's surprise attack, Mizelda—both in the initial rampage of fire and in the final clash with Arakiya—had suffered the most severe wounds of all.
Her whole body burned and swallowed by the whirlwind, and afterward her very organs churned by a blow from Arakiya.
No matter how tough her constitution, it must have been a situation that tested the very limits of Mizelda's vitality, to see how far Rem's healing arts—not yet at full strength—could hold out against it.

"Sorry to call ya up herE. But I figured I oughta tell ya as soon as I couLd."

"Mizelda-san..."

Saying so, Mizelda, seated on some object against the wall, smiled faintly.
In Mizelda's smile—she, one of the formidable Amazoness—the wild dignity she'd shown Subaru and the others time and again was nowhere to be found.
And yet the force behind her sharp-eyed expression hadn't been lost. That imbalance was what struck him.

"First, let me say thankS. Thanks to Rem, I got to keep my lifE. A miraclE."

"————"

"And ya took the city too, splendidlY. For what ya've donE, as chief of the ShudraK, I'm truly impresseD. And on toP of thaT, for the sake of the fight ahead, there's somethin' I must declarE."

Praising Rem's devotion and Subaru's contribution, Mizelda straightened her posture.
And then——

"I'm handin' the seat of Shudrak chief over to my sister TarittA. I can no longeR fulfill the dutY."

So saying, she stroked her right leg—lost below the knee—and made her declaration of passing on the role.
