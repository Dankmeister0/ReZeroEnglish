Arc 8 Chapter 24: "How to Walk in the Light"

"—Spica, is it."

The murmured voice looked over the two of them crying their eyes out—Rem, and the girl who until now had been Louis.
Subaru wiped his teary eyes with the back of his hand and turned to see who had spoken: Ram. She sat with her usual composed bearing, chin propped on her hand at the table, her long legs crossed.
She tilted her head slightly, narrowing her pale pink eyes at Subaru.

"Where's it from?"

"...The name of a star. Back where I'm from, anyway."

"I see. Not like Barusu, being a poet. But do you understand?"

Eyes still narrowed, Ram's gaze shifted toward the two embracing figures.
That alone was enough for Subaru to grasp what Ram meant.
It was, naturally, an argument they'd already hashed out thoroughly on the way to this cabin.

"Even if it's for a greater purpose, do you truly understand what it means to make use of a Sin Archbishop?"

"—. Of course I've thought about it. I can't claim to fully understand, but..."

"Then stop."

"—!"

Struck by the cold, hard words, Subaru's throat made a small strangled sound.
But with eyes that showed no consideration for the pain Subaru felt, Ram pressed the point again.

"If you don't properly understand, then stop. —What it means to face these eyes."

As she spoke, Ram extended the hand that had been propping up her chin and touched the slender shoulder beside her.
That was Tanza, who had been watching Subaru's exchange alongside Ram. Asked to serve as a witness, Tanza had been present for this.
Her dark, expressive eyes wavered under Subaru's gaze.

"I understand Schult—Schubalz-sama's feelings all too well. You've pushed through so much that shouldn't have been possible, both in Ginunhive and on the road since. But..."

"Tanza."

"But even for you, Schubalz-sama, who brought even Cecilus-sama along with you—I find myself thinking you shouldn't bring a Sin Archbishop."

Resolutely, correcting the wavering light in her eyes partway through, Tanza looked straight at Subaru and said it.
She didn't flatter him by choosing words pleasant to the ear, but asserted her own honest thoughts. Precisely because Tanza's opinion was young yet upright, it sank deep into Subaru's chest.

"...Yeah, I was an idiot."

Receiving Tanza's words, he once more keenly felt the truth of what Ram had said.
And that was the misguided weight of what Natsuki Subaru had resolved to choose.

"I understand it fully. I've been told so, over and over—so I'll carry all of it."

"Good. But let me say this—no matter what Barusu or Rem say, Ram won't forgive it."

"...! Sister."

"No, Rem. I take pride in your kindness as your sister, but that and this are different matters."

Still hugging Spica tightly, Rem looked at Ram with tear-filled eyes. But Ram shook her head at her younger sister's gaze and firmly declared her own position.
Of course she would. Ram, too, was a victim of "Gluttony," robbed of Rem, her other half.
It would be presumptuous to say her position was the same as Subaru's—she'd never think to say such a thing.
So—

"I haven't even set foot at the threshold of wanting to be forgiven. The only reason Ram hasn't torn that girl—Spica—limb from limb even now is the matter of 'memories.' That's all."

"...Until we know how to restore 'memories,' if something were to happen to Spica—"

"It must never happen that Rem's 'memories' fail to return, not even by the slimmest chance. Rem said it was fine if they didn't come back, but Ram won't accept that. I'll have Rem herself remember Ram, and remember just how much Ram loved Rem, too."

Ram's flat declaration that she would reclaim that love—her conclusion was the same as before.
When the battle at the Pleiades Watchtower had been settled, Lye Batenkaitos was dead, and they had succeeded in securing Roy Alphard's custody, Roy had been spared from having his life taken, as a possible means of saving the victims of the "Gluttony" Authority.
Except—

"The difference is that, compared to that Sin Archbishop, this one's at least cooperative."

"Ram..."

"Stop making that face. Listen. If you want to be forgiven, atonement comes first. That's simply how things work. Though I imagine you've already been told this too."

"—. Yeah."

Nodding at Ram's words, Subaru pressed his small fist against his own chest.
Exactly as she said—right now, what Spica had been granted was a generous suspension. Those who'd had dealings with her were extending the suspension of her punishment on the basis of her conduct and usefulness.
All Subaru could give her was a name as the trigger for her resolve to live anew, and help in persuading more people to agree to extend that suspension.

"For now, Spica... can't you use your Authority to just snap Rem's 'memories' and 'name' back into place?"

"Uu, auu..."

"Yeah, that's asking too much, huh..."

At Rem's shoulder where they embraced, Spica shook her head apologetically.
She opened and closed her own hands, testing them, but freely wielding the Authority—freely returning what the "Gluttony" siblings had gathered—didn't seem to come easily.

"...Can Louis-chan—no, Spica-chan—really do it? Bring back my memories, and everyone else's, that vanished?"

"At the very least, she's the one with the highest chance, and it being possible at all is the bare minimum condition. That itself is separate from the whole 'Stargazer' business."

Rem was anxious too, but it had to become possible.
That was the necessary premise for Spica to bear the cross of "Louis" and keep walking.
To that end, Spica needed to master the "Gluttony" Authority and—

"————"

"—? Um?"

A moment's, an instant's anxiety sealed her words, making Rem's eyes waver.
Knowing the generosity Ram had offered, and the condition necessary to accept it, and the means that had to be cleared for that—knowing all this, the existence of the Authority was terrifying.

The terror that, in order to stop being an unforgivable Sin Archbishop, by continuing to use the Authority that Sin Archbishops wielded, the way of life called Spica would once again draw closer to "Louis Arneb."

To let Spica live meant continuing to fight against that fear.
Having resolved himself to that—

"—I'm asking you to do it. I'm counting on you, Spica."

"Uh! Auauu!"

Her blue eyes filled with unmistakable resolve, Spica nodded vigorously at Subaru's words.
That figure of Spica, and the figure of the vicious "Louis Arneb"—identical in appearance, yet not overlapping in the part that mattered, the heart. That was hope Subaru could genuinely believe in.

"Tanza, sorry I couldn't heed your warning."

"—. Do you think that, as usual, I'll just smile and forgive you?"

Taking his eyes off Rem and Spica, Subaru turned back to Tanza. To Subaru, whose voice had dropped in tone, Tanza responded with a voice hardened by emotion.
At that reply, Subaru shrugged and said, "Nah."

"I don't think that. Because you hardly ever smile for me."

"That's not the point..."

"I get it, I really do. But give me some leeway."

"...If that's what you want, there are any number of methods you could take. You could simply say that her power is for the sake of the Vollachian Empire, and by extension, for saving Yorna-sama. Wouldn't that do?"

Pressing her lips tightly together, Tanza pushed the argument on Subaru.
Setting aside the credibility of the "Stargazer's" prophecy, if he pushed the point that Spica's presence was indispensable to this fight, Tanza—with Yorna's life on the line—couldn't very well refuse.
But that method was one Subaru hated.

"I could probably use that dirty trick to make Tanza fall in line, but I don't want to. I don't want to use dirty tricks on anyone. You're one person I especially feel that way about."

"—. Then it's hopeless for you, Schubalz-sama."

Hugging her own thin arms, Tanza finally averted her eyes from Subaru.
Called a coward to his face, in both gesture and word, Subaru let out a long breath.
Because what Subaru wanted to do was always a path that did nothing but hurt the people around him, the people who cherished him.

"So? How'd sly, cowardly Barusu manage things out there?"

Subaru gave a wry laugh at Ram's way of putting it.
Ram was kind enough not to let Subaru's self-mockery turn into mere self-pity. Truly, as Ram said, it would've been nice if he'd managed things well.

"I didn't manage it well at all. There was a way to keep redoing it until I got it right, but..."

For instance, on the Sword-Slave Island, Subaru had been able to do exactly that without hesitation.
When he'd been about to head down a wrong path, toward a relationship he didn't want, he'd had the proactive drive to try again and again, trial and error, to find a way to make up for it.

"But now that I've been reunited with everyone, I don't want to do that."

As he spoke, Subaru ran his tongue along the inside of his mouth, closing his eyes as he confirmed the absence of that medicine packet—the poison packet—he'd kept hidden behind his back molar for so long.
Even if he ever came to rely on that method again, it must never be used to look away from mistakes in human relationships.
Because he was firmly resolved on that point—

"—Otto punched me hard enough for it, after all."

Whatever sacrifices the path he walked demanded of others, that was not something he could allow himself to repeat.

"Otto-nii, show me your hand so I can heal it."

Hearing that, Otto looked back up at Garfiel's face standing in front of him.
This rough-looking boy was, contrary to his appearance, very delicate on the inside. Considerate and warm-hearted at his core—truly a proper member of the Emilia Camp.
To that offer of Garfiel's, Otto shook his head and said, "No, it's fine."

"You don't need to worry about it that much. It's not something to make a fuss over..."

"That ain't like you, Otto-nii."

"————"

"Look, it ain't like I never think or feel things myself. So I get how you're feelin', a little."

Scratching his own head roughly, Garfiel closed one eye. Then, jutting his chin, he indicated what his gaze was fixed on—Otto's hand.
That fist, painfully swollen a bluish-black.
For a moment Otto tried to turn his body to hide his hand from view, but doing that to his sharp-eyed junior, a skilled soldier, would have been reckless—and more than that—

"Draggin' around a hand you ain't even bothered fixin', into an important fight—that what you want, Nii?"

"...When you put it that way, I have no leg to stand on."

Wry-smiling at the reasonable persuasion, Otto gave up and held out his right hand.
The bluish-black, swollen fist throbbed with a dull, wet pain, and from over twenty years of living, Otto had a feeling the bones in his fist were probably broken.
Or rather, the moment he thought that, the pain only seemed to increase. Even if it wasn't broken, it might as well be.

"Feels like 'the weak-willed Domus dyin' first in the charge.'"

"You mean the sort who's usually timid getting fired up on the battlefield and dying a pointless death?"

"Well, 'timid' ain't a word that's ever had anythin' to do with you, Otto-nii."

As he spoke, Garfiel gently took the hand and cast healing magic on it.
A faint light carried a sensation like being gently warmed by hot water, and within mere seconds, the pain in Otto's fist eased.

"Just knitted back together, so it's still soft—next time you're punchin' somethin', use your left hand."

"I'd rather not break both hands. Next time, I'll leave it to Garfiel."

"If I did it, I'd blow a damn hole clean through. It's 'cause it's your strength, Otto-nii, that it only came to that much."

Gnashing his fangs, Garfiel turned his head to look at the wall of the carriage.
There, just as Garfiel said, was a slightly dented, cracked mark on the wall. That had been made by Otto's fist, and the height of that fist was—

"—Right about where our shrunk-down boss's head is."

"It was lucky Natsuki-san was small at the time. If I'd punched him in that state at his normal size, no matter how I explained it, I'd end up looking like the villain."

"Can't argue with that. If the boss had been full-size, might've been I threw a punch too, right after you, Nii."

Laughing with a low rumble in his throat, Garfiel played along with Otto's joke.
The corner of Otto's mouth loosened at Garfiel's manner, and he said, "Ah, geez," scratching his own head with the hand that had just been healed.
The freshly healed right hand still ached when handled roughly. But the pain was a relief.

"Even if it's a shallow trick, pain works like medicine. ...Hm, guess I should've gone ahead and punched Natsuki-san even while he was small, huh."

"Then wanna go punch him together, right now?"

"No thanks. If I went now, I'd see something I don't want to see."

Garfiel, doing his best to offer some comfort, groaned "Gao..." at Otto's answer and fell silent.
Otto reflected on his own words—he hadn't meant it that way, but it had come out like he was taking his frustration out on someone else. No—had he really not meant it that way?

Could he really claim there had been no calculation in flinging sharp words at Garfiel, someone he was certain would never bite back, someone who was only being considerate of him?

"...I hate this."

Muttering that, Otto once again struck his own forehead, a bit harder than before, with his right hand.
Both the struck forehead and the striking right hand ached with bone-deep pain.

—At this moment, Subaru should be heading to the cabin where Ram and Rem were waiting, to confront "Louis" there and talk things out.

Whatever conversation took place there was, from the bottom of Otto's heart, something he wanted no part of—a scene he could declare, loudly, he absolutely did not want to be present for.
Emilia and Beatrice, worried less about the substance of the talk itself and more about Subaru, who had to have that talk, had gone with him. Whether they'd actually go inside the cabin or not was unclear, but he could easily picture them anxiously waiting for the outcome.
Still, considering those circumstances—

"————"

Garfiel, silent with an awkward look on his face, must have stayed behind out of worry for Otto.
Both Emilia and the others, and Garfiel—both left Otto's heart feeling sour.

—Otto's opinion of the "Gluttony" Sin Archbishop, Louis Arneb, had consistently been one thing: she must be eliminated, no matter what.

Precisely because he thought that way and held to that resolve firmly, there was a fact Otto had deliberately not told Emilia and the others.
That fact was this: from the girl who couldn't speak, called "Louis"—the one he'd had numerous chances to interact with ever since they'd rejoined at the fortress city of Guaral—he had never once heard malice.

"————"

Otto's "Divine Protection of the Soul of Language" was a simple ability, nothing more than allowing communication with any living creature. Exchanging words with earth dragons and insects to gather information was its most common use, but if he set his mind to it, Otto could even converse with an infant.
Back in the hard years of his life as a traveling merchant, he'd once done work caring for a local notable's baby just to earn travel funds.

Even when a baby's voice couldn't form words, the intent behind it could be read.
In the same way, the wordless voice "Louis" emitted—its intent, too, could be read. And there was no malice toward others in it; what filled most of it was affection for Subaru and Rem.
So Otto had sealed up that fact and never once told Emilia and the others.

As long as doubt remained, it kept Emilia and the others from getting excessively close to "Louis."
Once that doubt cleared, there was no need to spell out what kind of attitude the kind-hearted Emilia and the rest would show toward "Louis," what kind of closeness they'd try to build.
That—

"—Otto-kun, Garf-kun, got a moment?"

"————"

Knock knock—lightly rapping on the cabin door and peeking her face in was Anastasia, dressed in a kimono.
At the appearance of her, accompanied likewise by a wasou-clad Julius at her side, Otto tightened his cheeks for a moment before nodding, "Sure."

"Looked like things over there were gonna take a bit longer, and figured it'd be kinda much for a big crowd to just sit around waitin', so we came back. Was worried about your hand too, Otto-kun..."

"Don't need you lot worryin' about Nii's hand—I already fixed it up myself. More importantly."

"Hm?"

"Bein' called 'Garf-kun' by you don't sit right with me."

Wrinkling his nose and protesting, Garfiel made Anastasia's eyes go round. Then she smiled, "Sorry, sorry," and said,

"See, Mimi's always callin' you Garf-Garf this and that, so it just kinda stuck in my head as Garf-kun without me meanin' to. Is Garf-kun no good?"

"Ain't sayin' it's no good, but..."

"Garfiel, you don't need to hold back on my account."

Patting the shoulder of Garfiel, who was grudgingly pressing back at Anastasia, Otto shook his head.
As Anastasia and the others entered, Garfiel shifted his stance to put himself between Otto and them. —To be precise, it wasn't because of Anastasia.

"————"

It was because of Julius, standing right beside Anastasia.
Garfiel had sensed the change in the air around Otto the moment Julius appeared in the cabin. It was out of that consideration that Garfiel had moved. Otto nodded at it.
Then, taking his place shoulder to shoulder with his junior, he showed his right hand to Anastasia and the others.

"Like Garfiel said, my hand's already been healed."

"Ah, is that so. Good to hear. If it hadn't been, I was thinkin' of havin' Julius heal it—guess that woulda been meddlesome of me."

Sticking out her tongue briefly, Anastasia said this with such shameless ease that it was almost infuriating. If she thought he couldn't refuse an offer coming from her, she was sorely mistaken.
In the Augria Sand Dunes, where Otto and Garfiel had been absent, the two of them may well have deepened some rapport with Subaru and Emilia along that road—but—

"Unlike those two, I remember quite clearly that the two of you are enemies."

"—. That's the stuff, Otto-kun. Course, I don't dislike Emilia-san and the others, but... if you didn't react like that, I'd lose all my spirit for this."

To Otto's sharpened gaze, Anastasia answered back with an equally gentle smile. Her expression and tone were soft, but the light held in her pale blue-green eyes was firm, and Otto found himself satisfied by it.
Even having crossed the border deliberately to march into the Vollachian Empire, Anastasia drew a clean line between camps. Precisely because of that, she had joined this coupled dragon carriage as someone representing a position that heavily involved the Kararagi City-States.
On that point, the problem wasn't Anastasia, but—

"Sir Knight Julius."

"Earlier, I overstepped. Allow me to offer my apology for that."

"An apology, is it."

A sigh escaped at the reply from the man he'd addressed in a voice stiff even to his own ears—Julius.
The overstepping remark in question was surely the one he'd made earlier, during the exchange in this very cabin, regarding the handling of the "Gluttony" Sin Archbishop.

In the sense of stating his own thoughts, Julius had merely exercised his right to do so.
That much should have been the very point Julius himself had made to an enraged Otto, back in that moment.

"Have you reconsidered? That you were, after all, an outsider to this?"

"No. Where the Authority of the 'Gluttony' Sin Archbishop is concerned—in the sense that I too suffered harm from it, I am a party concerned. I too have a younger brother who cannot look back on those memories. Considering that as well, I do not think of myself as an outsider."

"...Then on what grounds do you call it overstepping?"

Lowering his voice, Otto asked with furrowed brow.
Despite bringing up an apology, Julius had said he had no intention of changing his position on the point that had caught Otto's ire.
Otto couldn't think of any other reason Julius would have to offer an apology.

To Otto, puzzled by this, Julius spoke.
With sincere contrition, and a certain trust toward him, held in those yellow eyes—

"Otto-dono, I apologize for having taken your role from you."

"————"

"Your reaction told me as much. Had I not spoken up in that moment, the same thing would surely have been said, in the end, from your own mouth. And yet, on the sole grounds that I myself was a party who suffered from 'Gluttony,' I took from you—the camp's man of counsel—your rightful role. For that reason—"

There, he cut himself off, and Julius bowed deeply.
Then, turning the crown of his pale violet hair toward Otto, he said,

"I offer my heartfelt apology. I am sorry."

Shown that expression of contrition, flawless down to the last detail, Otto bit hard on the inside of his cheek.
Had that defensive reflex come a moment later, he might well have bitten through his own lip in front of the man. Julius, with his head bowed, wouldn't have seen it—but Anastasia would have.
That was something he absolutely had to avoid.

"Julius said he really wanted to apologize for that, no matter what. So I figured, if Otto-kun's hand treatment was still goin' on, it'd be easier to bring it up."

"...That so. Well, that was a lousy thing to do to him."

"It's fine. Even without a reason to bring it up, my knight here's the type who can properly apologize regardless."

Setting aside Otto and Julius, the two actual parties involved, Anastasia and Garfiel exchanged such remarks between themselves. All the while, Julius remained bowed, and Otto belatedly realized his own words were being awaited.
That unless Otto answered something, this apology would not end.

"...Please, raise your head."

Slowly, taking his time, Otto finally offered those words to the man before him.
Receiving them, Julius likewise slowly raised the head he had bowed. Looking at the face of the wasou-clad knight, the scar beneath his left eye lending it a striking sharpness, Otto sighed.
And then—

"I don't doubt that your remark carried no malice, and that it came from a stance of cooperating with Natsuki-san. But you are an enemy. Still, unchanged."

"Otto-dono."

"I'm no knight, so there's no occasion for us to cross swords. You're neither merchant nor civil official, so there's no occasion for us to cross words either. —And yet, in a capacity that is neither sword nor word, you are my enemy, and I am yours."

Clenching tight a fist that ached when squeezed hard, Otto declared this to Julius outright.
Receiving Otto's words head-on, Julius's eyes widened. That it wasn't the blank astonishment of surprise directed at someone he hadn't thought of as an enemy—that much, at least, salvaged Otto's pride in this situation.

"Anastasia-sama, I'll tell you this now in advance—whatever Natsuki-san or Emilia-sama's intentions may be, the reason for putting the Sin Archbishop to use lies with the Empire. If there is to be censure over the Sin Archbishop's existence, it is the Empire that ought to bear that responsibility."

"—. Mm, no objection from me there. Even settin' that aside, I've got no intention of turnin' this into a card in my own hand. I mean, that's only right, ain't it?"

Turning around, Anastasia closed one eye at Otto, who presented that fortified logic.
Stroking her white fox stole, she turned her gaze in another direction—likely toward the carriage where Subaru and the others were—and said,

"No matter what the plan is, the moment anyone allows a Sin Archbishop to get involved, folks around are gonna make sour faces about it, no matter how deep or shallow the connection. That's just natural. And that goes not just for the Empire, not just for Emilia-san's lot, but for us too—somethin' none of us can bring out into the open."

"—If you understand that much, I'm satisfied."

Nodding at Anastasia's answer, Otto let a little of the tension drain from his shoulders.
Once a Sin Archbishop was tangled up in something, there was no scenario in which it could be received favorably. That was the framework of this world, an immovable truth.
No matter what the victim Julius might say, no matter what path the kind-hearted Emilia might show for forgiveness, no matter what the earnest, high-aspiring Subaru might wish for—that remained true.

So they had to hold it against each other.
Knives pointed at each other's throats, each side pressing on the other's fatal weak point.

"—. Excuse me, there's something I need to attend to. I'll take my leave here."

Having confirmed that shared understanding, Otto said this abruptly.
Hearing it, Anastasia tilted her head, "Oh?" Beside her, Julius was still digesting Otto's earlier declaration, his expression of surprise tightening—but it was unbearable to keep looking at that for long, and Otto turned his back quickly.

"Nii! I'll come too—"

"Garfiel."

"Ahn?"

"I'll be fine alone."

To Garfiel, who moved to follow at his back as Otto tried to leave the cabin with quick steps, Otto held up a hand to stop him and said this as if biting down on the words to make them clear.
It wasn't that he'd be fine alone—it was a plea to be left alone. And his considerate junior obediently took it as such, nodding and seeing him off.

"————"

Quietly closing the cabin door, Otto strode off with long steps, putting distance between himself and the room.
He'd left Garfiel behind, alone with Anastasia and Julius. He was worried what kind of conversation might unfold with him gone, but he had no room left in his heart to go defend anyone.

He hadn't run, but his heart was anything but calm—calm enough that he wanted to.

"Taken my role...?"

Julius's words, bowing his head and saying he wanted to apologize, echoed in his mind, and he ground his back teeth.
That Julius could apologize like that—how very like him, a knight worthy of Anastasia, sincere and earnest in character. No such carefree sentiment came to him.
There was only, purely, a very bitter feeling toward Julius's misunderstanding.

Julius was making a huge mistake.
If Julius hadn't spoken up in that moment, Otto would never, ever have said anything that would poke a hole in Subaru's dead end.
It was true, as Julius said, that he'd be lying if he claimed the same thought hadn't crossed his own mind—but on top of that, what he could state with certainty was that Otto would never, not even on pain of death, have voiced it aloud.

It wasn't because his role had been taken from him that he'd flared up like that.
It was because something that shouldn't be said—no, something he didn't want said—had been said, that Otto had erupted at Julius in that moment. Julius didn't understand that.
And yet, that he'd make such a mistake—

"No wonder. Birds of a feather, I suppose."

At the root of it, it was proof that Julius, too, was an idealist much like Subaru.
Believing that the fundamental core of a person is good. But that wasn't out of easy optimism. It wasn't from not knowing reality. Even knowing reality full well, he could still declare it so boldly.

That was the same way of walking in the light shared by Natsuki Subaru and Emilia.

"—!"

"—Otto-kun, don't do that."

Suddenly, feeling his own arm grabbed, Otto was pulled back to reality.
Wondering what was happening, he found that without realizing it he'd raised his arm, and someone had reached out from behind to stop it.
Probably, Otto had been about to slam it against the wall out of pure emotion—that very right arm Garfiel had only just healed.

"That healing—that's Garfiel's magic, isn't it? He only just fixed it, and here you are about to break it again right away—even you'd feel awkward about that, wouldn't you?"

"...I admit it would've been a foolish thing to do, so would you let go of me?"

His voice came out low, unconsciously, and it surprised even himself a little. But the other party, as though used to hearing it, didn't remark on it and released Otto's hand, letting him go.
Standing there was Roswaal—someone he'd rather not have met just now. Though honestly, he'd rather not meet him at any time, but this was especially true right now.

"That face says you don't even want to exchange words with me, doesn't it-t?"

"That you can tell and still speak to me anyway—you really are hardened through and through, Margrave."

"Not like you, to have sarcasm with so little edge to it. Seems you're bearing more of a strain than I'd thought."

"————"

Naturally, Roswaal too had been present at the discussion in the cabin just now.
He knew that Otto had been boiling with anger there, and that he held a hardline opinion on how the Sin Archbishop should be treated.
Knowing all that and still adopting this attitude—his aim was blatantly obvious: to provoke Otto.
If that weren't the case, then he was simply hopeless at dealing with other people.

"I imagine you understand this too, Margrave, but I have no composure to spare right now. If you'd rather not be gnawed all over by mice I've bought off, I suggest you not rub me the wrong way."

"So if I'm ever gnawed by mice hereafter, you'd be the culprit, is that it. Useful thing to hear, that... but I am concerned for you."

"...For me?"

Otto repeated it back with suspicion, and Roswaal nodded.
At that response, Otto understood that the man was harassing him from a new angle. Being subjected to that when he had no room to spare made him genuinely sick to his stomach, which only made it all the more detestable.

"What are your own thoughts on this, Margrave?"

"Mine? Why, of course, I take the same stance as you, Otto-kun—that it wouldn't trouble me one bit if the Empire fell to ruin, don't I-i?"

"————"

"Oh, was I mistaken? A Sin Archbishop is nothing but poison that can never serve as medicine. Rather than rack one's brains over how to make use of one, better the Empire simply vanish. Just as you proposed to Subaru-kun—bring back only the members whose consciences would trouble them for abandoning it."

Shrugging as if to say "what a fine idea," Roswaal's manner made Otto feel sick, seeing his own opinion vividly reflected back at him.
Speaking as if in sympathy, only to deliberately bounce Otto's own methods back at him—Roswaal's rotten personality had reached its peak.
At the same time, Otto was equally disgusted with himself, for thinking, even while knowing it was realistic, that it was the best option.

Roswaal peered into Otto, self-loathing and all, and said,

"Just this much I'll tell you—I think you're doing plenty well, Otto-kun."

"...Without makeup, even the Margrave's tongue seems to have lost its edge. It almost sounds like you're genuinely trying to be considerate of me."

"I have no talent for being considerate, so I've no intention of trying. In any case, you are doing plenty well. But this is also a matter of no matter how you struggle."

"—. No matter how... I struggle?"

Caught by a strangely nagging turn of phrase, Otto's brow twitched.
At Otto's mulling it over, Roswaal nodded deeply. Placing a hand to his own thin chin, he closed his eyes on his makeup-less face, leaving only the blue eye visible, and said,

"This matter now is a fine example, is it not. In most cases, whatever Subaru-kun or Emilia-sama wish for tends to come to pass. Everyone rallies around to smooth the path, so that it comes to pass."

"...What are you saying?"

Otto furrowed his brow, taken aback that Roswaal would suddenly say such a thing.
The idea that whatever Subaru and Emilia wished for would come true was absurd beyond reason. If that were true, Emilia would long since be queen, and would have become Natsuki Emilia.
That it hadn't turned out that way meant it simply wasn't so.

"Are you mocking me? Or mocking Natsuki-san and Emilia-sama?"

"Neither. I merely pity you. In bringing us here from Lugunica to Vollachia, your family home's contributions played a part as well. On that point, I am grateful to you too, which is why I've chosen to offer this counsel, presumptuous as it is."

"————"

"Overinvesting yourself, differing in opinion with Subaru-kun and Emilia-sama—that is poison to you. I worry that poison will eat away at you and, in time, kill you. You are, after all, a hard-to-replace talent."

Quietly, letting the tone of his voice settle, Roswaal spoke to him head-on like that.
At some point, the clownish quality had drained from his tone, and something sincere had settled into his mismatched eyes as he appealed to Otto Suwen.

At that manner and those words, Otto fell silent for a while—a good while—and then realized.
The aim of the man named Roswaal L. Mathers.

"Margrave, I understand what you're saying. That said, I cannot heed your counsel."

"Hm..."

Narrowing his eyes, Roswaal let out a sigh tinged with what seemed like concern.
To him, seeking to hear the true meaning behind Otto's refusal to accept the counsel, Otto said,

"I understand perfectly well. —I'm in your way, aren't I. I haven't forgiven what you orchestrated, Margrave, and I still suspect you have some further scheme in mind."

"...Oh?"

"So you waited until I'd built up plenty of frustration, and then spoke to me. Perhaps you thought, with some convenient pretext, this was the perfect chance to remove me. But that's a grave mistake."

Certainly, Roswaal's read on him was correct.
The exchange just now could fairly be called the moment of greatest anger Otto had felt since joining the Emilia Camp. Before joining the camp, there was the incident where he'd gone so far as to punch Subaru flying, which would rank alongside it—but this was anger on that same scale.

"But it's a huge mistake if you think that means I'll throw everything away over it."

"Otto-kun..."

"More to the point, what was that nonsense just now supposed to be? Whatever Natsuki-san and Emilia-sama wish for comes to pass? Don't say something so foolish. Precisely because it's not remotely so, I, and Garfiel, and Beatrice-chan, and Ram-san, and Petra-chan, and Frederica-san, and Patrasche-chan too—all of us have been desperately doing everything we could to get this far, haven't we."

That this was utterly off the mark—Otto felt genuine anger toward Roswaal over it.
Roswaal's argument just now amounted to this: since the people around them would somehow make whatever Subaru and Emilia wished for come true regardless, there was no need to wear yourself thin fretting over it uselessly. No matter how much you objected, your opinion would just get silenced, and there'd be no meaning to your own existence.
But—

"That's exactly backwards."

"————"

"I don't know how wide a range the Margrave means by 'everyone rallying around'—but at the very least, if I were to side with every single one of Natsuki-san's opinions, affirm every one of them, and smooth the path so that every one of them comes to pass—I don't think so highly of my own competence that I believe myself capable of such a feat."

He couldn't lend great strength the way Beatrice or Garfiel could.
He couldn't fulfill an indispensable support role the way Ram, Frederica, or Petra could.
Unable even to become the strength that makes every wish come true—what, then, was the meaning of Otto's presence?

"That, I cannot do. That, I cannot accept. That, I cannot forgive. The moment I stop saying it, when Natsuki-san or Emilia-sama wish for something—that will be the moment my reason for existing disappears."

"————"

"Unfortunately for you, Margrave, things will not go the way you wished."

Clearly, firmly meeting Roswaal's gaze, Otto declared this.
Never before had he laid into Roswaal so unilaterally, all in one breath, like this.

Of course, it wasn't as though he'd never spoken it aloud before—but this sort of prickling tension had always been stretched taut between Otto and Roswaal.
Precisely because of that, Roswaal too, seeing an opportunity, would press in on Otto to eliminate obstacles, trying to arrange the board to his own advantage.
But Otto would not yield. At least, not to such a flimsy piece of logic as today's.

"Where I stand is already decided. I can't walk in the light—and that's fine by me."

"————"

"A poor move, Margrave. You'd have been better off not speaking to me at all."

Even so, the conclusion wouldn't have changed—that much he could say.
But at the very least, it would have taken longer to reach that conclusion, and he'd have wavered along the way. Yet by rushing for the result Roswaal wanted, the opposite had happened—the result he didn't want.

And just as Roswaal, wearing a wry, complicated expression, stood there, Otto steadying his breath—

"Ah, Otto-san! Found you!"

Came the sound of small, pattering footsteps, and a high voice calling out made Otto turn around.
Then his eyes met Petra's, running toward him while waving her hand.

"Petra-ojousama... no, Petra-chan."

Almost slipping into the habit from his undercover days, Otto caught himself, put a hand to his mouth, and corrected himself. Petra, running up to stand before him, spoke with her breath a little quickened.

"Otto-san, is your hand okay? I heard you slammed it into the wall as hard as you could..."

"Everyone keeps telling me that. Luckily, Garfiel healed it, so I'm fine. Sorry to worry you."

"No, if you're okay, that's good. ...What's the master doing?"

Wry-smiling, Otto showed Petra, who'd worried over him, his unharmed hand. Looking reassured by that, Petra immediately switched her expression and glared at Roswaal.
At that look, Roswaal shook his head weakly and said, "Well now—"

"I was just savoring the fruits of my daily conduct."

"The master...? Since you never reflect on anything, wouldn't it have no flavor at all?"

"Whoa there."

Otto had thought he'd pushed Roswaal pretty hard himself, but Petra's single remark was on an entirely different level of devastating.
Sure enough, Roswaal's shoulders slumped—and Petra, who'd been watching him with narrowed eyes, suddenly said "Ah," as if remembering something.
Then she turned back to Otto, the ribbon atop her head swaying, and said,

"Oh, that reminds me—it's not just about your hand, I heard. That a little while ago, you all were having a talk about Louis-chan, and so..."

"Ah, ah, right, that. Well, if that's worried you too—"

"So I went and smacked Subaru upside the head in your place, Otto-san!"

"————"

Thrusting out a small hand balled into a fist, Petra declared this firmly.
Looking back and forth between the thrust-out fist and Petra's determined face, Otto blinked.
At Otto's reaction, Petra huffed a little through her nose and said,

"I heard you held back, Otto-san. That you couldn't bring yourself to hit tiny little Subaru right now because it'd be too pitiful. So I went and did it for you."

Opening her closed fist, Petra showed off her palm. Peering up at him from behind that palm, she left Otto silent for a while.
But it was no use.

"Ha, hahaha, ahahaha!"

He'd endured the discomfort of being apologized to by Julius.
He'd endured the helplessness of being pummeled, over and over, by Roswaal's offensive.
But this—Petra's satisfying, cheerful declaration just now—he could not endure.

Laughing didn't settle anything.
The problem remained exactly as it was, and Otto still stood opposed to Subaru's thinking.
Even so, precisely because it was his reason for existing to keep objecting, to keep insisting that the compromise Subaru had found wasn't good enough, even while remaining opposed—he would keep saying it.

As if he'd ever yield.

"Petra-chan."

"Yes?"

"Thank you."

Saying that, Otto pressed his own palm against the small hand the girl held out.
With a light clap, Petra laughed, "You're welcome."
