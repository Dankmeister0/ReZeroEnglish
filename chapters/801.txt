Arc 10 Chapter 41: "What You're Worth"

"—Yes, I withheld it. That was my decision."

Russell showed so little discomposure at the direct accusation that Subaru, who had come to confront him, found himself the one thrown off balance.
After hearing Melty and then Black Dog declare their respective resolves, Subaru had come to confirm with Russell the crucial fact revealed in the course of those conversations—Emilia and the others' escape from the royal castle.

"For the time being, I have the castle waiting above the clouds. Between raising it to its maximum altitude and the effects of 'Perception Inhibition,' we should be able to buy ourselves some time. Meanwhile, we shall determine our next course of action. Agreed?"

That had been Russell's decision as he took his hand from the black sphere used to pilot the castle—the 'Heaven-Control Sphere'—and brought Kugelschreiber Castle to a halt in midair. It should be added that Subaru had advocated calling the sphere the 'Ball Steering Wheel,' but Beatrice had rejected the suggestion because it already had a proper name.

"Betty would like to take Subaru's side, but she cannot ignore the name Mother gave it first, in fact. Betty shall teach you all sorts of things about this castle later, I suppose."

"Promise. Come to think of it, we never did get around to picking up that thing you left here, either."

Before the Beloved Children attacked, they had been on their way to retrieve something Beatrice had left in the castle four hundred years ago. She had said it was a gift from her mother, and Subaru had to make sure it found its way back to her hands.
To that end, too—

"I was hoping we could clear the air properly here and now..."

"I agree. It is difficult to speak openly with one another while harboring ulterior motives. I, too, often wish for a world in which everyone could show each other all their cards."

"Everyone except yourself... is that it, in fact? Always wanting to hold a trump card no one else has—just the sort of thinking one would expect from Roswaal's friend, I suppose."

"Hmm. I understand that he is someone close to you, but I must say it is a bad habit to invoke Roswaal when judging everything I do. 'Librarian,' you may find yourself overlooking the essence of things that way."

"W-w-what an outrageous thing...!"

Confronted with that observation, Beatrice looked back over her own words and actions in dismay.
Subaru had assumed she had been doing it on purpose, but apparently it had been unconscious. As he stroked the head of Beatrice, who had frozen at the realization that she was constantly thinking of the absent Roswaal, he sharpened his voice. "Russell-san."

"—'Silver Fox,' please. Even with no one else present, we must not lower our guard."

"Sorry. But I'm partly doing it on purpose this time. Isn't it about time we spoke frankly? Melty-chan and 'Black Dog' did."

"And so you have come to negotiate directly on their behalf?"

"I've heard even confronting the boss means risking their lives. It's not like they're using me or anything."

"I see. Sensible. —However, both 'White Sheep' and 'Black Dog' accepted their curse marks with full knowledge of the risks. Objecting to that after the fact would be misplaced."

"They might've agreed to being stopped from spilling secrets, but taking away their right to object goes too far. Whether it's a dictator or a boss running the whole show, if the person at the top steers wrong, it's all over... If it's a country at stake, you could stand to be more careful."

"Careful, you say. —I assure you, that is precisely what I believe I am being."

His voice lowering slightly, Russell slowly shook his head.
Something in the complicated shades of emotion in that murmur caught Subaru's attention, and he frowned. Before he could put his finger on it, however, Russell met his gaze.

"Those two... 'White Sheep' and 'Black Dog' are fundamentally inclined toward goodness. It was easy to imagine that, after spending enough time together, they would grow close to you. And that, in turn, you would take their side. I am not surprised."

"So you think you're more of a bad guy, Russell-san, and that's why we can't get along?"

"No, I do not see myself as a villain. —If I had to describe myself, I would say I am nothing."

"...Nothing?"

As Subaru echoed the word despite himself, Russell dipped his chin in silent assent and looked around the room.
They were speaking in the laboratory of the Witch Echidna, the former mistress of Kugelschreiber Castle—a room in which they had returned the documents they had initially intended to remove to their original places, deciding instead to take their time examining and scrutinizing them here.
It was unsettling, but Crusch and Ferris were already using the proper guest room, and they could think of nowhere else suited to a private conversation. That was why they had chosen it.
Surveying the room without emotion, Russell continued without meeting their eyes.

"To begin with, multiple Bird Mail messages were dispatched in case any failed to arrive. The one who informed you of the matter was 'Black Dog,' I presume. His actions came very close to violating the terms of his curse mark... But those two never swore loyalty to the kingdom in the first place. A certain degree of evasion is within expectations."

"Since you've figured it out, I'll tell you: 'Black Dog' said he read a message he stole. ...If it's an important report, doesn't sending a bunch of them multiply the risk?"

"Bird Mail has conditions that must be met before it can be opened. Opening the 'Six Tongues'' Bird Mail requires the recipient to bear the same curse mark. A condition even the 'Beloved Children' cannot satisfy."

"...A curse mark is an Oath binding the soul of the one who bears it, I suppose. If the 'Six Tongues'' curse mark has rules unique to it, imitating it would be all but impossible, in fact. Ideal for security, I suppose."

Beatrice added that explanation to Russell's answer to Subaru's concern.
Hugging her a little tighter now that she seemed to have recovered from her Roswaal-induced shock, Subaru understood how Black Dog had managed to intercept the Bird Mail.
Still, it had probably been as close a call as Russell said.

"Just so I know, what if it hadn't been close? What if he'd crossed the line?"

"His entire body would have been wracked with unbearable agony. If he divulged a secret that must not be revealed, even his life would be forfeit. It is by following these rules... these regulations, that we have contended with the 'Beloved Children.'"

"And because you're so steeped in all that, nothing that happens to anyone rattles you, Russell-san? Is that why you call yourself nothing? But people's hearts aren't that simple."

They were not. A human heart was no convenient tool that stopped developing cracks simply because its owner had decided what it ought to be.
Even the Sin Archbishops and the Witches, whose values lay beyond ordinary people's understanding, could not escape being bound by their own hearts.
Yet—

"When the world one sees is different, what takes shape upon it changes as well. Humans have human reason, spirits have spirit reason, beasts have beast reason. None of those apply to me. Neither spirit nor beast, but an inhuman man... No, a slave to a mechanism."

"A mechanism... a slave to a system?"

"I ask for no sympathy. Nor for understanding. When two courses of action are compared and weighed within me, the one of greater value is chosen. That is an unerring guiding principle within me."

"...That's your excuse for hiding what happened to Emilia and the others?"

"It is a disclosure of my criteria for judgment. Though you are free to consider it an excuse or an attempt to gloss things over."

Subaru felt that a deep, decisive line had been drawn between them.
He did not think Russell was trying to confound them with incomprehensible reasoning divorced from reality. The man was probably dealing with them sincerely, in his own way. It was just that, as he himself acknowledged, his criteria for judgment were vastly different from theirs.

A person's position and circumstances played a large part in shaping who they were. But more than his position as head of the Six Tongues, the greatest factor defining Russell's nature was surely his 'Divine Protection of Evaluation.'

'—It is said that the famed "Hoshin of the Wilderness" possessed the same Divine Protection.'

The subject had come up briefly during an earlier casual conversation with Russell.
Subaru had had rather mixed feelings upon hearing that. He had suspected that Hoshin, the founder of the Kararagi City-States, had been transported from another world just as he had. But if Hoshin had possessed a Divine Protection, that theory became rather doubtful.
Perhaps it was a case like Subaru's, who had acquired Authorities later in life. Or perhaps, if reincarnation in another world was possible alongside being transported to one, that changed things—but those considerations could wait.

The role played by Hoshin of the Wilderness, who had possessed that Divine Protection of Evaluation, had been immense. His achievements were extraordinary enough to endure for centuries.
Someone capable of such feats must, after all, see things and judge their worth differently from other people. The same could be said of Russell. That was why he did not ask to be understood.
Because—

"You've already weighed whether or not to ask for understanding, and reached your answer."

"—Yes. Precisely."

At Subaru's unsettled words, Russell's lips curved and he nodded.
Even that smile, free of malice and giving no cause for resentment, inspired something approaching awe at the harshness of the road Russell had traveled, when Subaru considered that the process that produced it differed from other people's.
It was like living among a crowd of aliens who saw and experienced the world differently from you, all while trying not to let them discover that you were unlike them.
And if he had relied on his Divine Protection even to judge whether he was succeeding—

"Please do not expect me to change. This is who I am. That is unshakable."

"————"

"—Even spirits cannot remain forever unchanged. Your thinking is far too one-sided, in fact."

Having heard Russell's conclusion, the long-lived Beatrice spoke with a bitter expression.
But Beatrice's argument had meaning precisely because she was willing to change. It did not reach Russell, who saw no value in doing so.
Feeling the gulf between them keenly, Subaru let out a long breath.

"I understand why you hid it. And I get that there's no point picking at you over why. Then at least tell us the details. You've got them, right? More detailed information than 'Black Dog' had, and more recent."

"Having withheld information, do you not think I might falsify it next?"

"I do. That's why I'm asking you to give it up already. From here on, it's a matter of trust. And speaking of trust, I'm not going to believe you don't know what happened to Emilia and the others afterward."

"Hmm. May I ask why?"

"The 'Six Tongues' already lost track of my friends once. You're not the kind of person to make the same mistake twice, Russell-san. And you must've been keeping the castle under the strictest watch you possibly could."

If the Six Tongues had been founded to protect the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica, the safety of Emilia and the other Royal Selection candidates had to be among their highest priorities. At the very least, they could hardly have adopted a policy that disregarded the candidates. They must have been keeping them under more than adequate surveillance.
Under Subaru's scrutiny, Russell remained silent a while before beginning to speak.
What he said was—

"—Emilia-sama and Felt-sama, who were under house arrest in the royal castle, escaped with the assistance of Marcos Gildark, captain of the Royal Guard, and are currently at large. For some reason, the new Royal Selection candidate, Filore-sama, is said to be accompanying them."

"—That scary-looking captain helped Emilia and the others? And Filore's with them too... Is that... going to be okay?"

At the unexpected revelation, Subaru's eyes widened in astonishment.
Marcos, who had taken Emilia and the others out of the castle, was captain of the Royal Guard—in other words, Reinhard and Julius's superior, and a man of considerable importance in the kingdom. If he had defied the castle's orders and fled with Royal Selection candidates, upheaval in the capital was inevitable.
And on top of that, Filore was with them—

"Melty-chan seems to think Filore's in the clear, even within the 'Divine Dragon Church,' and I can't imagine that girl plotting anything either, but..."

"It appears she is participating as an individual, rather than in her capacity as the 'Divine Dragon Church''s Saintess. That said, I am told her first knight is accompanying her."

"Filore's first knight..."

"—As you know, Heinkel Astrea."

That, too, had become known during the two months since Filore had become a Royal Selection candidate, and it was among the more shocking pieces of news surrounding the selection.
Subaru had a hard time settling on an opinion of Heinkel as a person. His first impression had undeniably been awful, but between his work in the Vollachian Empire and suspicions that he had sided with Al while Subaru and Beatrice were sealed inside the black sphere, he was an exceedingly difficult man to judge.
Still, Subaru had felt that Heinkel was probably not simply ingratiating himself with Filore to take advantage of her good nature.

"But that's just a gut feeling mixed with wishful thinking. Things would be seriously awkward between that old guy and Emilia-tan or Felt."

"If we are talking about people who make poor conversation, the captain of the Royal Guard would give him a good contest, I suppose. ...Even knowing the details, there is still no end to the worries, in fact."

"I'm sure Marcos-san is strong, but..."

Actually clashing with the Beloved Children had taught him that what made them troublesome was not raw fighting power, but the wickedly underhanded ways they used their unusual abilities. And this was one conclusion Natsuki Subaru had reached during his life in another world: the strongest people here were vulnerable to underhanded tactics.
Because, for them, battles were often won by whoever hit hard first.
That was why Subaru considered his role to be seeing through the enemy's tricks, then deploying his friends in the right positions to hit the enemy hard before they could hit back.

"Put the winning hand where it can win. Like setting up a game of rock-paper-scissors they can't lose. That's all the more reason we need to meet up with Emilia-tan and—"

"—If you intend to defect in order to rejoin them, I shall activate 'White Sheep' and 'Black Dog''s curse marks."

"Huh?"

With worry and impatience vying equally on his face, Subaru could not immediately comprehend Russell's next words.
What had he just said? —Activate Melty and Black Dog's curse marks?

"...That makes no sense, I suppose. Killing those two would gain you nothing, in fact."

"I do not recall saying I would take their lives. Activating their curse marks... I would merely inflict the unbearable agony I described earlier. Pain as though one's soul were being torn apart. Nothing I would ever wish to experience again."

"The way you say that, it sounds as though you have..."

"I have. Do you imagine I have occupied my current position since birth? There was a time when I, too, stood where 'White Sheep' and the others do... on the receiving end, rather than the administering one. That is why I know. I know just how excruciating it is."

"————"

Cold sweat ran down Subaru's cheek as Russell spoke dispassionately, in a tone that left no room to suspect a lie.
It was an unmistakable threat, and plainly no empty one. Russell had enough inhumanity in him to make Subaru certain he would follow through. To borrow Beatrice's words, he was every bit the close friend of Roswaal one might expect.
And—

"...Betty's answer remains the same, I suppose. Whether you kill those two or merely hurt them, it is meaningless to us, in fact."

"—Do you truly believe that, 'Librarian'?"

At that question, Beatrice's shoulders trembled beside Subaru. She glanced at him standing next to her, then bit her lip in frustration.
He had done his utmost to keep it from showing. But it was no use. His face had given him away. —Russell's threat worked all too well on Subaru.

"I believe I told you. It was easy to imagine those two growing close to you."

"Then the reason you didn't make that threat sooner..."

"Because it would not be fully effective until you... to be precise, until 'Ashen Star' had developed an attachment to those two."

"You really are Roswaal's friend, I suppose."

"For now, I shall accept that assessment without protest."

Russell's expression did not change at Beatrice's bitterly spat words.
But the fact was, his plan had almost entirely succeeded. Knowing that his actions would cause Melty and Black Dog to suffer what amounted to torture, Subaru could not make that choice.
And yet, on the other side of the scales stood none other than Emilia.

"Russell-san...!"

"I shall not claim that this was a measure I wished to avoid. If it is necessary, I play the necessary card. My criteria for judgment are exactly as I have explained."

"...A curse mark is bound to the soul. Since inscribing it requires the consent of those involved, undoing it from outside is impossible, in fact."

Russell's answer to Subaru's teeth-clenched stare was cold, and Beatrice anticipated the next course Subaru would want to take.
Removing the curse marks was impossible, and Russell's values could no more be changed than his nature. —But Subaru, too, had things about him that were impossible to change.

It was impossible to turn Natsuki Subaru into someone who could give up what mattered to him.
So—

"————"

Think. Think until his thoughts burned out, until his brain cells boiled.
In the recent battle with the Beloved Children, he had forced Crusch and Ferris, Melty and Black Dog to bear the burden, while all he had done was nearly die. Make up for it here. For lying there unable to do anything, for being too foolish to notice the danger Emilia and the others were in. Make up for it.
That was—

"—Take away the cheap tricks and the dirty tricks, and what've I got left?"

Subaru gripped Beatrice's hand tightly, and with his other hand, clasped the black sphere at his chest. What he held in both hands was resolve and readiness—and with them, he set his soul alight and faced forward.
Natsuki Subaru would not change. If Russell Fellow claimed the same was true of him—

"—Beako, is there anything to write with in here? A brush, a pencil, a crayon, anything."

"—! Betty hardly knows what a crayon is, I suppose. But there is something, in fact!"

Looking at Subaru's face as he turned forward, Beatrice sprang away, her twin tails bouncing. From a small room at the back of the laboratory, she fetched something resembling a black brush.

"Use this, I suppose! It is a brush Mother used, and it never runs out of ink, in fact."

"Not knowing how that works is scary! But thanks. Now... I can use the back of this, right?"

Holding the brush with its mysterious workings, Subaru drew a sheet from the stack of documents on a nearby workbench. It was one Russell had already appraised and judged unnecessary. After checking that the reverse of the old paper was blank, Subaru set the brush moving across it.

"—? 'Librarian,' what has 'Ashen Star' suddenly begun to...?"

"Hmph, Betty does not know either, I suppose. But this much she does know, in fact. —Subaru is about to knock you flat with surprise, I suppose."

"————"

Unable to grasp Subaru's intent, Russell narrowed his eyes at Beatrice's air of certainty. Subaru had to knock him flat with surprise, if only to live up to that trust. —Even if he failed to surprise him, he could only believe that this attempt would work.

—Russell based his judgments above all on the relative value, or changes in value, revealed by his Divine Protection of Evaluation.

He had identified the entrance through which the Beloved Children invaded Kugelschreiber Castle by its plummeting value, and applied the same principle to selecting strategies and courses of action. Its accuracy had to be high. That much was clear from the fact that he had held his own against Capella, perhaps the worst enemy imaginable in a battle of intelligence.
But for that very reason, Russell should be unable to lie to his own Divine Protection.
If so—

"—This should work on you!"

Saying that, Subaru folded the paper over which he had been rapidly running the brush, tore it in two along the crease down its middle, and turned to face Russell with one half in each hand.
Then he held up the two sheets, one in his left hand and one in his right, for Russell to see. What was written on them was—

"A comparison of Natsuki Subaru if Emilia-sama is helped, and if she is not...?"

"That's right! This is me if we help Emilia! Mentally stable, with a lot of trust in Russell-san for sharing his information! More fighting strength, more options at my disposal! And, on the other hand, me if we don't help her..."

"Suspicion and anxiety in abundance, with trust in you, who are willing to resort to threats, down in the negatives. Fighting strength unchanged, mind elsewhere... Subaru with nothing to recommend him, in fact."

"Right, exactly!"

What he had written on the separate sheets in his left and right hands was, put plainly, a pair of options.
Depending on the decision made here, Natsuki Subaru could become either Natsuki Subaru. He had written the differences in a little more detail on the paper, with somewhat exaggerated wording, but there were no lies.
Of course, it was not impossible to pursue a policy of leaving Emilia and the others' safety unknown and expecting results from Subaru's continued desperate efforts to reclaim the companions he could not find.

But Natsuki Subaru was certain of one thing.
He was stronger when he was with the people he cared about than when he was worrying about them—

"Well, Russell-san? You've come this far by trusting your own Divine Protection. Use that 'Divine Protection of Evaluation' to take a good, hard look at which version of me is worth more to you, and then choose your next words."

"————"

"Do not lie... No, you probably cannot, so there is no need to worry, I suppose. In any case, there is someone waiting her turn, in fact."

As she spoke, Beatrice stood in front of Subaru, similarly holding in each hand a sheet on which she had written a different future for herself, positioning them to enter Russell's field of vision together with his.
Of course, neither Subaru nor Beatrice could be certain which of their prepared options would demonstrate greater 'value' in any universal sense. —They simply believed.
And then—

"Come on, Russell-san."
"Well? Give us your answer, in fact."

"—I have lived with these eyes for over thirty years now..."

"————"

"And you are the second. No, including 'Librarian,' that makes three."

Before Subaru and Beatrice, who leaned forward to press him for an answer, Russell abruptly began to speak. Having said that much, he paused and rubbed the space between his eyebrows with a finger, as though uncertain.
It was a gesture that seemed to question the truth of what his own eyes had shown him, to reconsider it.

"————"

Yet after taking ample time, Russell opened his eyes again and gave a short sigh. —Apparently, what he saw had not changed from a moment before.
In other words—

"The second person reckless enough to use the value I see with my own eyes to try to make me accept a decision that diverges greatly from my own thinking."

That answer testified to which of the options Subaru and Beatrice had presented appeared more valuable to his eyes.

—The instant she heard the tremendous crash, Emilia was already leaping from the bed of the covered dragon carriage.

"—Keep going! Don't stop! Run!"

She landed almost rolling on the ground and shouted sharply to those around her. But she did not know whether her voice had properly reached them. The splintering roar that had begun to sound was accompanied by tremors, which were now shaking the entire Milesta Dragonback Bridge violently.
The vibration reaching her through the soles of her shoes began deep and distant, but closed that distance all at once, surging upward until it finally exploded with a boom.

"—Ah! No!"

Her desperate cry was drowned out by the screams and shouts rising at the same moment.
The people attempting to cross the Dragonback Bridge, and the earth dragons carrying them, raised their voices one after another, loudly crying out in confusion at the unthinkable situation that had struck them.
She understood how they felt. But that would not do. Even if they stopped and desperately called for help, everyone around them was in the same predicament.
And then, with a shriek loud enough to swallow all their screams, the Milesta Dragonback Bridge itself cried out.

"No, no, no, no! This is bad, Emilia! I can't explain it, but it's really, really bad!!"

The voice that rose above even the bridge's groaning scream was Filore's, tremendously loud.
Looking over, Emilia saw Filore leaning out of the carriage bed she had just leaped from, pointing behind her and flailing about, her beautiful red eyes wide.
At the same moment, Emilia felt the death throes of the violently shaking Dragonback Bridge reach the spot just behind her—

"—Icicle Line!"

White cold swirled, and pale-blue ice arrested the paving stones as they peeled away and threatened to fall. Spreading with a sound like the air itself cracking, the white frost caught carriage wheels that should have spun into empty space and sealed the holes that would have swallowed the earth dragons along with them.
Emergency repairs to the bridge, in one place, two, three—far too many to count.

"Honestly! Doing something this awful just to find us...!"

With the wind whipping the hem of her robe and sending her long silver hair dancing, Emilia seethed.
Across the Dragonback Bridge in her field of vision, where fractures were still spreading, more than fifty dragon carriages were traveling besides their own. Including those crossing on foot, there might have been anywhere from several hundred to more than a thousand people.
And yet—

"I won't let you! I'm reeeally angry now!"

Emilia braced herself, furious at pursuers who would stop at nothing and showed no hesitation over taking more than a thousand lives.

It was the opening of a battle to defend the Milesta Dragonback Bridge, a structure with more than three hundred years of history—the final day on which its proud form would ever be seen.
