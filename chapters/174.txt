Arc 4 Chapter 8: "The Long-Awaited Reunion"

Despite advancing along an unpaved road, the jolting inside the carriage was minimal in the extreme. However many times he experienced it, even now that he was actually making use of it, Subaru couldn't help but think this "Divine Protection" business had some genuinely mysterious power to it.

If the "Wind Barrier" protection alone accomplished this much, then if you unraveled the full range of the many Divine Protections out there, you'd probably find words other than "mysterious phenomenon" to describe them.

"...Not exactly the time for escapist musings like that."

Craning only his neck forward from the coachman's seat, Subaru squinted past Otto's shoulder at the view ahead — the exterior of the "Sanctuary," their destination now just moments away.

They advanced over dirt that had barely been cleared into something resembling a road, and perhaps a hundred-odd meters ahead, the forest opened into a clearing. Scattered wooden rooftops were visible in the distance, and Subaru guessed these must be the houses of the people living in the "Sanctuary." Seen from this distance, there was an air of a poor, remote village about it, but nothing that particularly caught the eye.

If he had to offer an impression, though—

"There's a real gloomy feeling hanging over the place..."

The entrance to the "Sanctuary" — a badly weathered stone gate — only reinforced that impression, and the village, ringed by a low fence, gave off a distinctly closed-off feel.

Garfiel, catching Subaru's unintentional remark about the atmosphere, slapped his own knee with gusto.

"Tha-a-at's right. Gloomy place, ain't it? And lemme tell ya, the folks inside are even gloomier. Every last one of 'em's got a sour mug on, walkin' around dead while they're still breathin'."

"Man, you really trash-talk your own people. But hearing that makes it feel even further from what I'd call a 'Sanctuary.' It's more like..."

Subaru huffed a breath at Garfiel, who cheerfully affirmed his own cynical description, and thought back to what Garfiel had said earlier. When Subaru and the others had called this place the "Sanctuary," Garfiel had responded without hiding his self-deprecating feelings. That's right—

"What did you mean, the Witch of Greed's graveyard?"

Emilia, it seemed, had arrived at the same question troubling Subaru.

Her eyes as she asked it were resolute, but her fingers, stretched out where she thought no one could see, had curled fondly into the hem of Subaru's clothes. He felt a quiet satisfaction at being relied upon like this, even as, sensing where her unease came from, his feelings grew complicated.

"Witch — basically, when people say 'the Witch,' it's common knowledge that they mean the Witch of Envy. Almost nobody knows about the other witches named for the other Deadly Sins."

"Huh, really? I thought they'd all been famous since four hundred years ago, though?"

"Emilia-sama's got it right. Ah — Subaru's fine, right? It ain't that the Witch of Envy's so famous the others get overshadowed. Records of the other witches the Witch of Envy devoured — there's almost none of 'em left, anywhere. But well, there're exceptions. Kind of."

"And this place is one — is that what you mean?"

Garfiel, picking up Subaru's question, dipped his chin in response to Emilia's. She looked shocked, eyes wide, but for Subaru, who had no deep knowledge of witch-related matters, it was more a matter of "oh, is that how it is" and moving on.

No — a sudden misgiving blew away that carefree feeling. If there were multiple witches, then—

"W-wait, don't tell me there's a Witch Cult for each and every witch? Do you have any idea how much trouble it was taking down just one Archbishop of Sin? Give me a break."

That was exactly the reason for the chill that ran through him — not something he could dismiss.

Judging from the things Petelgeuse had let slip, it was safe to assume he'd been a senior member of a faction devoted to Satella, the Witch of Envy. The other Deadly Sins Subaru would eventually have to defeat — "Gluttony," "Greed" — were surely of the same ilk.

But if, hypothetically, there existed factions devoted to worshipping other witches as well—

"That's a scary thought, but you don't need to worry about that, Natsuki-san."

But it was Otto, still facing forward with the reins in hand, who put to rest the chill running up Subaru's spine. Unlike Emilia, who was ignorant of the Witch Cult to begin with, or Garfiel, whose reliability was somewhat questionable, Otto could be trusted on matters of common knowledge among the populace. What Otto knew was probably a fair reflection of what the general public knew.

"The Witch Cult... I don't really like even saying the words, but the only one they worship is the Witch of Envy. The other witches are even more taboo to mention than she is."

"More than the Witch of Envy...? What do you mean? Like, they're worse-natured than the Witch of Envy?"

"It means nobody knows what a Witch Cultist might do if they hear the name of a witch other than the one they worship — and that's what makes it frightening. Do you know about the incident where an entire city was destroyed in the Vollachian Empire to the south?"

At the topic Otto raised, Subaru recalled something he'd heard before. It was something Wilhelm had told him about, back when discussing the terror of the Witch Cult during the campaign against Petelgeuse. If he remembered right, it was—

"That's the one where the Archbishop of 'Greed' took down a whole city in that empire, all by himself, right? Something about how even a hero there couldn't stop him."

"What he did was so flashy the reason behind it tends to get overlooked, but the reason the Witch Cultist did it in the first place is the scary part. That city was — for a closed-off place like Vollachia — pretty much the only one with thriving trade... and apparently there were rumors that some artifact connected to a witch had been unearthed there."

"An artifact connected to a witch, huh."

"To this day nobody knows what the thing actually was. But there are always eccentric collectors who'll want something like that regardless. If it had been something connected to the Witch of Envy, it probably would've ended as just a matter of bad taste... but as it turned out, an entire city was destroyed."

Presumably "Greed" had moved either to seize that artifact or to erase it. The Vollachian Empire had needlessly provoked the Witch Cult, and as a result found itself on the receiving end of a brutal retaliation.

"Ever since, any involvement with witches besides the Witch of Envy has been forbidden, since it risks provoking them. ...Even so, there's no shortage of shameless people who trade in such things behind closed doors."

"That's some rare venom coming out of you. Something bothering you about it?"

"...It's nothing much. I just had relatives caught up in it, in that city, back then. It was more than fifteen years ago now — I was just a kid, it's got nothing to do with me."

With that, Otto pressed his lips shut, refusing to let the topic go any further. Left with no choice but to give up pursuing it, Subaru scratched his cheek and turned his attention back inside the carriage.

In any case, having reconciled the gap in their understanding, Garfiel — who'd been waiting for Subaru — touched his chin and asked, "You satisfied?"

"The fine details, even I don't know. But that this is the Witch of Greed's graveyard — the old geezers and hags repeat it so much it's practically 'Peromio who festers the moment he hears it,' so there ain't no mistake."

"I've got no shortage of curiosity about what exactly is 'festering,' but I guess even you don't know the details."

"Only thing I'm interested in is that I'm the strongest there is. If ya wanna hear the details, you'd have to grab Roswaal by the collar and shake it outta him. Dunno if that's even possible right now, though."

"—? What do you mean by tha—"

"Excuse me. Looks like we've arrived — should we go on inside as we are?"

Garfiel's suggestive words — but before Subaru could press for clarification, Otto's voice called out from the coachman's seat ahead. At Otto's prompting, Garfiel muttered "guess it can't be helped" and hopped lightly down from the carriage.

"We go bargin' in without gettin' word out first, and they'll think we're outside intruders and swarm us — turn us into somethin' that looks just like a 'crater-riddled Magmarine laughin'.' I'll go talk to 'em, so wait just a bit."

"Yeah, go ahead. Actually, now that I think about it, weren't you supposed to be doing patrol duty or something for the 'Sanctuary'? Given the situation when we ran into you."

Which made it all the odder that he seemed to have so casually abandoned that role — including the fact that he'd been out on patrol alone. Though, for someone of his caliber, maybe acting solo was more efficient.

Garfiel only waved a hand at Subaru's question without answering. Subaru had barely furrowed his brow at the non-answer when, almost simultaneously, Emilia let out a small "ah" beside him.

Turning to look at Emilia, who'd raised her voice in surprise, Subaru saw her lifting a finger, seemingly without thinking, to point ahead. Following her gesture, he understood at once what had startled her. Standing there was—

"—You're back, Garfiel. That was quite fast."

"Didn't need to circle the whole forest anymore. Ain't like you to be away from Roswaal's side. He finally kick the bucket?"

"If that had actually come to pass, this place would already have been burned to the ground by a self-destructive Ram. Be grateful to Roswaal-sama that it hasn't."

"What kinda theory is that, makes no sense!"

The familiar figure of a girl in maid attire stood facing Garfiel, her pink hair swaying. Against Garfiel's cheerful laughter, her expression was cold, her emotions muted. Confirming that after so long, Subaru's shoulders loosened with relief.

"Ohh, so that's the older sister I heard about. I see. She really does look just like the young lady who's sleeping, doesn't she — well, of course."

Otto, seeing her for the first time, let out that murmur of admiration. Standing before his gaze was a figure identical to Rem, whom Otto had also seen — yet utterly different inside.

It was Ram, Roswaal Manor's laziest servant, and Subaru's long-awaited reunion with her.

"—Ram!"

Leaning forward and waving from the carriage, Subaru caught Ram's attention. She narrowed her eyes slightly, regarding him, then gave an unmistakable shrug and shook her head.

"I don't know which Barusu this is, but I'm disappointed by such a late arrival. I expected you'd notice the trouble here and come sooner... ah, but I suppose that was too much to ask of Barusu."

"If you're gonna claim you don't know me, stick with that story, don't go flip-flopping! And another thing — you and Roswaal both, you're impossible to read, what you people actually want from me! I've got a whole list of complaints saved up for him later, by the way!"

Subaru raised a finger in protest at her unchanging attitude. Ram let her shoulders sag theatrically at his response, then turned her gaze to Emilia standing beside him.

Like Subaru, Emilia's cheeks relaxed with relief at confirming Ram's safety. But Subaru caught it — a fleeting, fragile flicker of sorrow that seemed almost about to vanish, crossing Ram's eyes as she looked at Emilia. It was gone in an instant, though, and—

"Emilia-sama, welcome, it's good to see you as well. Roswaal-sama is waiting, so please, head on to the building ahead. Garfiel, take the carriage and the coachman somewhere appropriate."

"Real shabby treatment, huh! There's such a thing as askin' nice to get someone motivated, y'know!"

"If you want to eat Ram's cooking, then apply yourself. Though if you'd rather throw away what little chance you have with your own careless words and actions, Ram has nothing more to say."

"Fine! Fi-i-ine! Impossible woman to get a handle on — but that's what I like about her. Oi, coachman-boy. I'm parkin' the earth dragon and carriage over yonder, so follow me."

"I believe I properly introduced myself, could you please stop with the humiliating nicknames!? And — actually, dealing with this man one-on-one feels like a genuine hazard to my personal safety!"

Otto showed clear resistance at being led off by Garfiel, freshly humbled by Ram, but Subaru just thrust a thumbs-up at him, teeth gleaming.

"I'll make sure your bones get collected!"

"That's a phrase that is definitely never used in a good way, isn't it!? I mean it, if anything happens to me, I'm filing for damages!"

With that parting shot, Otto helped Subaru and Emilia down from the carriage before following after Garfiel. Subaru watched the two head off leading the way toward the village interior, and Patrasche, who nuzzled her nose against him in a brief farewell, before cracking his neck and saying, "Well, then."

"I've got all sorts of things I want to ask and talk about piling up — you up for getting into that stuff with me now?"

"...Ram hasn't been given permission to tell you that herself. You should ask Roswaal-sama directly. Though I don't know how much Garf let slip."

"Garf... oh, Garfiel, you mean. Different impression than I got just hearing his name. That guy. Speaking of, he said something that caught my attention."

"What was that?"

Ram's brow rose sharply, quick to catch it. She seemed to brace herself, imagining he was about to press for information Roswaal hadn't disclosed, but Subaru just crossed his arms and said, "Nah—"

"Does he like you? He said something along those lines."

"...I wonder what on earth you're bringing up."

Ram sighed, making no effort to hide her genuine exasperation. Still, since she didn't deny it either, Subaru couldn't quite suppress a grin, ill-suited to the moment as it was.

"I'm not saying it's weird or anything. You're cute enough, looks-wise. ...Though sticking with liking you for that long, given how you are — that takes some guts, I'd think."

"It's unavoidable that there'd be males drawn in by Ram's combination of talent and beauty. All of Ram already belongs to the one she's meant to devote herself to, so there's no hope for him, but that's beside the point."

Ram responded to Subaru's teasing with a shrug, delivering a blunt verdict on Garfiel's feelings. Then she turned her back and, saying only "I'll show you the way," set off walking without further ado.

It wasn't as though Subaru had wanted to gossip about romance, but her offhand attitude still left him a bit deflated. Though, given that he himself had been too afraid to bring up the topic he really should have, maybe he had no right to say anything.

"Even now, scared to bring up Rem's name, huh. Not that I was expecting anything."

He'd been afraid to confirm it.

Even after already hearing from Emilia and Petra that they had no knowledge of Rem, to ask her own blood sister whether she, too, had forgotten Rem's existence—

Even if the fact that she hadn't confirmed Rem's existence here already told him the answer to the question he couldn't bring himself to ask.

"No use getting depressed over hypotheticals. Anyway, let's follow Ram, Emilia-tan. —What's wrong?"

Subaru remarked on Emilia, who had stayed silent this whole time. Since stepping down from the carriage together, she'd kept her mouth shut, restlessly glancing around, and at his call she gave a small shake of her head.

"I just... feel unsettled, somehow. It's like — I don't know, it feels strange... I can't quite put it into words, and it's frustrating."

"Unsettled, huh. Coming from a homebody shut-in like me, I feel like anyone would feel that way in a new place... but personally, I don't get any bad vibes, particularly."

He looked around, following Emilia's lead, but it still struck him as a thoroughly ordinary, poor village. Compared to Arlam Village, if anything, the houses here seemed somewhat older and less well-maintained, but that was a minor enough difference to write off.

Still, it wasn't as if there was no sense of wrongness at all. What exactly that wrongness was, though, even Subaru couldn't quite say.

"No use staying on guard, Emilia-tan. Ram and Roz are both here anyway, so I figure we can go ahead and assume there's no danger."

"It's not really like being on guard or anything... never mind, it's fine for now. If only I could really talk to Puck about it."

Emilia touched the crystal stone at her chest — the green stone hanging from her neck — and called uneasily to the name of the spirit sealed within. It was plain that the absence of the great spirit, who had always stayed close by her side, was bringing her real anxiety now.

Her literal frailty, and his own hatred at not being able to be someone she could rely on—

"—Subaru?"

"Let's go. If anything happens, just leave it to Meat Shield 2."

Without quite meaning to, Subaru took the hand she had rested on the crystal stone, turning his face away as he said it. He kept pulling her along, walking off before she could get out any word of protest. Naturally, Emilia found herself following along, swept up by his forceful gesture.

Even he thought, unthinking as it was, that it had been an embarrassing enough act to make his face burst into flame. Still, it was a result of following feeling before thought. He just hoped it hadn't come across as strange.

"—Okay."

But despite the speed of Subaru's own heartbeat, she gave a small nod and didn't shake off his hand.

—That house was one of the few structures in this "Sanctuary" that had kept anything close to a proper shape.

The stone-built structure was, by the standards of Subaru's old world, roughly the size of an ordinary single-family home. A single story, divided into simple rooms by a basic floor plan — livable enough, presumably. To Subaru, now thoroughly steeped in a standard of living well above average thanks to Roswaal Manor and the Crusch estate, it felt cramped, but given his fundamentally small-minded nature, he figured given enough time he'd probably come to find it comfortable enough. It was tidy enough a place to make him think that. And there—

"Ye-e-es, well, Emilia-sama, Subaru-kun. It does feel like quite the lo-o-ong-awaited reunion, doesn't it?"

Roswaal greeted them with a slack smile, waving from where he sat.

This was, in fact, the first time Subaru had laid eyes on Roswaal since the royal capital. Considering there'd been no reunion at all during the previous loop, the span amounted to nearly a full month. His accumulated grievances toward the man over that time were considerable, and he'd been thinking that once they were reunited, he'd need to land at least one punch to the man's face before he could feel settled about it.

"We-e-ell, I'm relieved above all that you're safe, Emilia-sama. Ram had told me about the trouble that occurred around the mansion, you see. I was positively sick with worry that something might happen to you."

"If you really felt that way, you'd have made better preparations... no, forget that, what about you? What happened here?"

Roswaal seemed relieved at Emilia's safety, but the two facing him were anything but at ease. All the many things they'd wanted to say seemed to scatter into nothing in front of the current Roswaal.

Lying in bed, his whole body bearing an array of injuries too severe to ignore, his body fixed in place with bandages seeping blood — it was a pitiful sight.

At Subaru's question and Emilia's silent, staring scrutiny, Roswaal, with his left arm — the less injured of the two — traced lightly over the eyepatch covering his closed left eye.

"Oh de-e-ear, you're going to ask about that? I-I'll have you know I'm still a man, you know? Just having my disgraceful state exposed like this wounds my pride enough as it is — I'd rather appreciate it if you could leave well enough alone, though I suppose I understand if that's asking too much."

"I can't just let that go. Seriously, what happened, Roswaal? Getting hurt this badly... and you're saying it like it's nothing?"

Emilia protested against his glib deflection, no way she'd be fooled by it, and half-reached out with trembling fingers, only to hesitate at actually touching Roswaal, injured everywhere as he was. He gave a wry smile at her, turned his right eye toward the ceiling, murmured "Now then," and said:

"Whe-e-ere to begin, I wonder. We-e-ell, as for my injuries, I suppose I'd say it's largely a matter of an honorable wound, or something unavoidable for appearances' sake."

"Stop trying to dodge with these roundabout answers. I'm asking seriously. Answer seriously too, Roswaal."

"...It seems Emilia-sama is in rather poor humor as well. Well, tha-a-at might be unavoidable, given this place."

Subaru felt a flicker of unease at how sharp Emilia's interrogating tone had grown, at the exact same moment Roswaal pointed it out himself. Emilia's brow rose slightly at the observation, but she bit her lip shallowly, acknowledging the truth of it.

"There's this rustling, unsettled feeling in my chest. What is this place, really? You called it the 'Sanctuary,' but I don't feel that way about it at all. If that's the case, then this is..."

"The Witch's graveyard — much easier to accept, isn't i-i-it?"

"—!"

Roswaal dropped his tone and spoke the words. With Garfiel having said something similar and now Roswaal saying it too, the weight of the term suddenly grew heavier.

Emilia's gaze snapped over to Subaru. Seeing the complicated emotions swirling within it, Subaru dipped his chin in solidarity with her confusion, and said—

"Wait, wait, wait, let's organize what we want to ask here first. If we keep going like this, the conversation's just going to spin off in all directions and never come together. We won't land on a single conclusion."

"O-o-oh my? You've gotten quite good at taking charge, haven't you, in the time I haven't seen you. Subaru-kun, did so-o-omething happen to change your outlook?"

"Getting into that would take forever to explain, so I'll save the bragging for after we're done with what I want to ask. Oh, right, one thing first."

Subaru raised a finger at Roswaal's teasing, glaring at him.

"The alliance with Crusch-san is done. You heard from Ram, I bet — you satisfied, leaving me behind like that?"

"—Satisfied indee-e-ed. Just as I thought — you truly were a good find."

Subaru sighed and closed his eyes at Roswaal's mouth curling in satisfaction. He'd expected as much, but it seemed Subaru's actions had gone exactly as Roswaal had intended. There'd been an aspect of using that to his own advantage, sure, but being told outright he'd been the one used wasn't exactly pleasant.

In any case, Subaru reorganized his thoughts once more and pressed on.

"First, the people from Arlam Village. Since Ram's safe, I'm guessing that part's fine, but the people who evacuated here — they're all okay, right?"

"You can rest ea-a-asy on that front. I know I may not have much credibility given this sorry state I'm in, but I am, for all that, a lord. I made sure to put my body on the line to protect my people, at least that much. Everyone's living in this village's cathedral now."

"The cathedral, huh. I'll save the questions about that for later — so next up would be..."

First, confirmation that the evacuees were safe was a relief. Since it had been Subaru who chose to have them flee and where to send them, their safety had been the last outstanding concern from the previous loop — after all, that part of things could no longer be redone.

His shoulders relaxing with relief, Subaru exchanged a glance with Emilia. Taking the cue, she dipped her slender chin and said:

"Tell me about this place. Roswaal called it the 'Sanctuary.' But Garfiel called it 'the Witch of Greed's graveyard.' Which one's true?"

"Both are true, Emilia-sama. This place was once the final resting place of the Witch of Greed — Echidna — and to me, it is a place that deserves to be called a sanctuary."

"—Witch."

"Echidna..."

Subaru and Emilia both caught in their throats at the same moment, hearing the answer to their question.

Roswaal answered quietly — but with a tone that had stripped away every last trace of his usual clowning. Precisely because of that, there was an unmistakable weight of truth in his words now, nothing like a mishearing.

Emilia caught her breath, blinked several times, and then said again:

"The Witch of Greed... the other witch, the one destroyed by the Witch of Envy, right?"

"Ye-e-es, that's right. Nowhere in the world's history now does her name remain. Only, faintly, in the memories of those who once knew her personally."

"Wait, wait, wait, that doesn't add up."

Subaru cut in with a wave of his hand at Roswaal's wistful account. Slightly cowed by the way Roswaal narrowed his one-eyed gaze at him, Subaru pressed on anyway.

"If my memory's right, the Witch of Greed... getting taken down by the Witch of Envy was four hundred years ago, right? I can accept that this place being that witch's final resting place is from four hundred years back... but you talking like you personally knew her — that's a bit much, even for you."

"I wouldn't say that I know her myself, no-o-o. This is something passed down as a kind of oral tradition, handed only to the successive heads of House Mathers — to whoever inherits the name Roswaal."

"An oral tradition... so what, some ancient head of House Mathers way back had some connection to the Witch of Greed?"

"—Echidna."

"Huh?"

Emilia's eyes widened as the name alone was suddenly inserted into the conversation. Roswaal turned his gaze to her, then, as if confirming it, murmured "Echidna" once more.

"Ple-e-ease, use her name when you refer to her. A title like 'the Witch of Greed' carries such a wicked air about it, doesn't it — not good at a-a-all. And it's a mouthful besides."

"Um, okay, got it. So, Echidna's final resting place is this village, and House Mathers has managed this village for generations... is that the gist of it?"

"Ye-e-es, that's about right. Though I wouldn't call it 'managing' exactly — it hasn't taken that much effort. Echidna's influence lingers strongly here, so much that one cannot simply set foot inside without following the proper procedures. That you all were able to come in... I'd guess that was thanks to some cooperation from Frederica, perhaps."

Seeing Roswaal nod in confirmation, satisfaction rising in his eyes, Subaru now stepped forward, seeking to push the conversation further.

"So this is Echidna's graveyard, and it's under your jurisdiction — got it. What I still don't get is what this place's actual purpose is, and why none of you — including the villagers — have come back."

"It might sound odd coming from me to say so, but you're accepting this rather rea-e-eadily, aren't you. This being the Witch's graveyard, something I'd been keeping secret, and all."

"I don't really know what the Witch of Envy did, but I know even less about what this Echidna witch actually did. Frankly, isn't that a part almost nobody besides me knows either? Seems shortsighted to assume someone's evil just from the word 'witch' written on them. Just like — could you imagine, from the words 'half-elf' alone, that Emilia-tan here would turn out this cute?"

"...! D-don't say pointless things like that. Sneak attacks like that are banned, you know."

A casual pickup line slipped in right at the tail end of a serious conversation, and Emilia, face reddening, gave Subaru's side a light pinch. He gave a wry smile at her adorable retaliation, and Roswaal, quick to notice, let out a sly "ahh-ha," saying,

"My, it does seem the two of you have grown quite a bit closer in the time I haven't seen you. I did wonder how things would turn out, given that falling-out in the royal capital, but it seems you've managed things well, haven't you-u-u."

"It's a lovey-dovey thing we finally earned after a whole lot of twists and turns. I'd love to brag about it more, but the topic's wandered off, so let's get back to it and get an actual answer. This place's purpose, and why you're not going back."

"You've lost that amateurish edge and gotten quite reliable, my my. We-e-ell then, as for the reason the villagers and I haven't returned... put simply, I suppose it's that we can't go back even if we wanted to."

"Can't go back?"

Subaru's brow furrowed at the unclear answer, and Roswaal nodded.

Then, before Subaru's questioning look, he laughed breezily and said:

"Right now, all of us — every one of us — are being held under house arrest, so to speak, by the residents of this village. A-a-ah, and the moment the two of you stepped inside, you found yourselves in exactly the same position, I'm afraid."
