Arc 3 Chapter 61: "The Road to the Mathers Domain"

—The cheers spread across the moonlit plain.

The swords the knights raised caught the moonlight, reflecting it in a scene almost beautiful.
The White Whale's massive body lay beneath Flugel's Tree, and a fervor wrapped around the group surrounding it. Everyone was swept up in victory, weeping tears of joy at having fulfilled their long-cherished wish.
As if to throw cold water on their joy—

"——!!"

Two mighty roars rose up, shaking the air of the Lifaus Highway, which had briefly gone quiet.
They came not from the slain White Whale, but from the two White Whales that were mere clones, having lost their main body. Their massive forms writhed upon the ground, ferociously raging even as their shapes began to blur into something hazy and indistinct.
The supply of mana from the main body had been cut off, and they were losing the ability to maintain their forms. Left alone, they would likely vanish within minutes—a pitiful sight. But—

"How uncouth."

With a single word, a swung arm cut short their unsightly display, unleashing an invisible blade of wind.
A slash of wind accompanied by a sudden gale struck one from the head, easily cleaving the writhing Whale's outer hide, which had lost its former resilience—splitting it clean in two, left and right, scattering its existence quite literally into mist.

The remaining one was pulverized by a single blast from the subjugation force's cannon, its scattered mana likewise dissolving into the air, its massive body vanishing completely.
This time, in the truest sense, the battle to subjugate the White Whale had come to an end. But—

"I can't just be swept up in the celebration."

Clenching her fist, aware of the exhilaration rising within her own chest, Crusch nonetheless kept it from showing on her face and shook her head.
Everyone working together to defeat an evil Witchbeast, the story ending happily ever after—that wasn't how it would go. Such a tidy close was permitted only to picture-book tales; in the reality that continued on past "happily ever after," there was no end to what still had to be done.

The surviving wounded had to be cared for, the dead whose remains still existed had to be given a proper burial, and the traces of those whose remains did not had to be sought out.
And as Crusch let out a breath before that cleanup, she noticed something.

A short distance from the White Whale's corpse, there stood one of the heroes of this victory, desperately crying out.

"Rem...! Rem, open your eyes...!"

Cradling the girl in his arms, her body limp and weightless, Subaru desperately called out to her bloodless face.
The Earth Dragon nestled close beside them rubbed its black snout against them worriedly. But the impatience driving Subaru now was so fierce that he couldn't even respond to the dragon's display of affection.

—The plan to have the White Whale chase Subaru's scent and crush it beneath Flugel's Tree had succeeded brilliantly.

It had seemed a plan likely to draw opposition, given the reluctance one might feel toward felling a historic tree, but the pragmatic beastman mercenaries had shown no compunction whatsoever, and even Crusch had shown the breadth of mind to accept it without hesitation, so long as it was necessary.
As a result, the plan—devised by Subaru himself, who bore tremendous risk in proposing it—had been carried out, and it could be said to have brought about the best possible outcome.
But if this was the price for it, then it was simply too much.

"This isn't good... please, Rem... I need you... I can't do this without you..."

Before him, Rem still showed no sign of responding to Subaru's calls.
There was no sense of will passing through her limp limbs, and his tearful voice calling her name passed clean through her eardrums, echoing hollowly into empty space.

They had run for their lives, the trunk of the great tree looming before them, the White Whale in furious pursuit behind.
Its massive body had struck the weight of the tree directly, and a violent tremor and shockwave had blown everything around them apart indiscriminately—and among that everything had been Subaru and the others, running right alongside it.

Amid the violent impact that left him unable to tell up from down, Subaru remembered being protected, wrapped in something warm. And he remembered, too, the instant he became aware of that, a tremendous crash had thundered out, slamming him and that warmth together into the ground.

Emerging from the fog of a hazy consciousness, Subaru shook his head and realized he had been lying collapsed on the ground. He raised his face and realized someone had been holding him—and then he knew that it was her body, the one that had held him tight until the very end.

"...Subaru... kun..."

"Rem—!?"

Her eyelids trembled, just slightly, and beneath them her eyes reflected Subaru with a powerless light.
Seeing his own reflection in those eyes look so terribly weak seemed almost like an unconscious admission of the reality closing in before him.

"Th-thank god... yeah, it's me. You know who I am, right? It's Subaru. Rem, your body—"

"Subaru-kun... I'm so glad... you're safe..."

"...!"

His throat closed up. He couldn't even properly force out words of concern for her condition, choked with tears—because Rem had smiled, relieved, at the sight of him.
Disregarding even the state of her own body, simply happy to have confirmed that Subaru was safe.

"The Witchbeast... what happened... to it..."

"...It went down. We got it. It worked. Everything worked out! I'm fine too, not a scratch on me... all thanks to you..."

"I... see. Then Roswaal-sama, and Emilia-sama... too, must surely be all right..."

"I'll make it work out. Leave it to me. So Rem, you don't have to say anything right now, just rest... no, wait, don't... close your eyes... ah, damn it, what do I do..."

He didn't want her to push herself to keep talking. And yet, if her mouth didn't keep forming words, he couldn't shake the anxiety. There was some unbearable, coercive force of fate that felt as if it were trying to snatch her life right out of his hands.
He didn't know what to do. He didn't know how to help her.
Not knowing, all Subaru could do was hold her hand, and tighten the arm wrapped around her.

"That hurts, Subaru-kun..."

"Sorry. My bad. But if I don't do this, you'll go off somewhere..."

"I'm not going anywhere. ...Rem is... by Subaru-kun's side..."

To Subaru, who was throwing a tantrum like a sobbing child, Rem smiled back with what could only be called a motherly warmth, and then, suddenly, the strength went out of her body.
At the feeling of her body going soft in his arms, Subaru's throat froze with terror. He heard the sound of his own blood draining away all at once, ringing in his ears, and everything else was left behind.

"Rem...? Rem! Please, Rem... open your eyes..."

"I'm just... so terribly sleepy... I'm sorry. ...Just a little sleep, and when I wake up, again... right away, for Subaru-kun's sake..."

"I don't care about any of that! You don't have to do anything. Just being by my side is enough. So please, Rem..."

Even though she was right there in his arms, she was gradually growing distant, and desperate to hold her there, Subaru wrung his voice dry. And yet that voice couldn't seem to reach her, so close before him.

"Can I... be selfish?"

"...! Say it, say anything. I'll listen to anything, do anything..."

"I want you... to say you love me."

In a hoarse, weak voice, Rem, looking up at Subaru, murmured this small request.
Pushing back a vision blurring with rising tears, Subaru shook his head. And then he brought his face close to hers, and—

"I love you."

"——"

"I love you so much. Of course I do... I can't go on without you."

It was words from the heart.
If he were to pour everything he had into this one moment, it would be nothing but the plain, unvarnished truth.
Without her, he wouldn't have made it this far. Without her, he couldn't go on living.

"Ah... I'm so happy..."

At Subaru's confession, tears traced the edge of Rem's closed eyes.
She received the words thrown to her with what seemed like happiness, a flush of red rising quickly to her cheeks. And with that, in what felt like the last of it, the strength drained truly from her body.

"Wait, hold on..."

"I love you, Subaru-kun."

"Don't screw around, stay with me. Are you gonna leave me with nothing but regrets again?!"

He couldn't bear the thought of her absence from the future he'd fought his way toward.
He'd known that for a long time now, and her presence had only grown larger and larger within him since then.
So—

"In the future where I'm laughing and talking, you have to be there... I won't accept anything else."

"In that future, may Rem be there beside you?"

"...Of course. I won't let you go anywhere else."

Closing his eyes, shaking off the tears threatening to well up, Subaru looked straight at Rem.
And then, he declared it.

"You're mine. I won't hand you over to anyone."

"—I'll hold you to that."

"Huh?"

An oddly composed response came back, and Subaru let out a dumbfounded sound.
And then, just like that, Rem slowly opened her eyes, which had lost all their strength, and—of all things—pushed herself up in Subaru's arms.
And before a stunned Subaru, unable to grasp the situation, she tilted her head and smiled,

"The spot next to Subaru-kun is already reserved by Rem. ...There's no taking it back, you know?"

Whatever had become of that near-death appearance, it was nowhere to be seen now; with a teasing, mischievous tone, she closed one eye and gently pressed a finger to Subaru's lips.

His shoulders sagging, all the strength drained out of Subaru, and he slumped down.
He let out an exhausted breath, then looked up at Rem with narrowed eyes,

"You... you, you... you—!"

"Yes, Rem, Subaru-kun's Rem. In name and in fact."

The stock answer sounded shameless now, and Subaru couldn't find the words to continue.
Still, the fact that the girl before him was unharmed took priority in his mind—it should have been a scene where he had every right to show his anger, but he was simply too happy—

"After we both blurt out our real feelings, you go and get way too carried away, don't you..."

"A girl who's grown honest about love is a strong thing, Subaru-kun."

Flustered by Rem, who no longer had any intention of hiding her feelings for him, Subaru felt his face heat with embarrassment among other things, and let out a small breath,

"...If you'd died, I probably would've died too."

"Wow. To be thought of that much—Rem is truly blessed."

"No joke, either."

To Rem's answer, laced with a small laugh, Subaru responded with nothing but honest feeling.
If she had truly been lost, Subaru surely would have redone the world. And even if given no chance to redo it, there was no doubt he would have tried anyway.
That was how much space Rem now occupied in Subaru's heart.

"Then I absolutely cannot go and die, can I?"

"Damn right. Even if you die, I won't let you stay dead."

Bringing his face close, pressing forehead to forehead, they gazed at each other at close range.
Rem gazed back at Subaru's gesture with adoration, and the closeness of a girl near enough to share breath made Subaru's skin prickle. Naturally, his gaze was drawn to her pink lips, and he felt his heartbeat quicken faintly—

"—Aren't you two about done yet, nya?"

Ferris, who had been watching the two of them flirting from a distance with an exasperated look, cut into the crucial moment and interrupted magnificently.
No doubt it was deliberate.

"Calling out so desperately like that, Subaru-kyun is just too cute. 'I can't live without you'—!"

"Shut up, quiet down! Think about your own bad taste, staring like that!"

"Well, if you'd just calm down for a sec, you'd realize. Since Feri-chan, who's been going around treating the wounded, didn't rush right over to her, that means Rem-chan's injuries clearly aren't life-threatening or anything—"

"How am I supposed to calm down in a moment like that?! A girl I l— a girl I love—! is unconscious and hurt, of course I'd panic!"

"There are things a boy's pure heart just can't say outright in every situation, huh~"

Unfazed by Subaru's shouting, Ferris laughed lightly, floating a blue glow in his palm and directing it toward Rem.
Slamming an irritation he couldn't quite contain at that profile, Subaru still couldn't hide his relief at watching Rem's expression gradually ease.

There were plenty of parts of what Ferris said that Subaru couldn't nod along to easily, but the fact that Ferris—treating the severely wounded in order of priority—had left Rem for later must indeed have been true.
As a hero of the White Whale subjugation and part of another camp's forces, his master would never allow Rem or Subaru to be treated carelessly. As Subaru arrived at that conclusion in his mind—

"Are you unharmed, Natsuki Subaru?"

—the very master in question, Crusch, appeared, striding leisurely through the grass.
Stained here and there with blood and mud, Crusch's upright bearing was nonetheless beautiful. Naturally, an undiminished dignity lingered even in the afterglow of battle, and she was truly the embodiment of the phrase "warrior maiden."

"Somehow or other, yeah. Glad to see you're okay too, Crusch."

"I am. But the subjugation force's losses are by no means small. Even having felled the White Whale, those who vanished will not return."

She dipped her chin at Subaru, who raised a hand in reply, but a pained expression crossed her face as she turned her gaze away. Her eyes went to the White Whale's corpse, still lying pinned beneath the great tree, left undisturbed.

In the direction of her gaze, the relatively less-wounded survivors of the subjugation force had gathered and seemed to be attempting, first of all, to move the tree off of the White Whale.

"What are they doing over there?"

"The White Whale's corpse must be carried away. Something must also be done about Flugel's Tree, which was sacrificed for the plan. It's after the battle that there's no time to rest easy."

"Carry it out... you mean that huge corpse?"

Wondering if he'd misheard, Subaru double-checked, but Crusch's manner didn't change. Subaru hastily turned his gaze back to the White Whale, taking in its massive form, stretching perhaps fifty meters in length, and—

"Isn't that kind of impossible?"

"'Cannot be done' won't do. This was a threat that swam the skies of the world for four hundred years. Only with the solid proof of its corpse will the people's hearts find true peace of mind. At worst, it may come down to just the head."

Listening to Crusch speak with evident chagrin, Subaru reconsidered—that judgment was only natural. To begin with, for Crusch, the subjugation of the White Whale carried great significance as an appeal to the merchant faction in the Royal Selection.
Already the frontrunner with strong support from the citizenry, if she'd now won over the goodwill of the merchants as well—a point of concern until now—her standing would be rock solid, and—

"Wait, did I maybe just give her a pretty bad boost here?"

Belatedly, it began to feel undeniable that, in terms of throwing his weight behind another camp, he might have committed an achievement whose consequences were beyond recall.
Naturally, cold sweat ran down Subaru's back at the sense that he'd only added fuel to the disadvantage of his own camp.
But—

"You're wearing quite the gloomy face. That's not the face of the hero who brought down the White Whale."

"Emilia-tan called me a traitor the second she saw me, first thing... wait, what did you just say?"

"The hero who brought down the White Whale. I would not want to be so shameless as to claim your achievement entirely as our house's own doing."

Turning her gaze back from the White Whale, Crusch fixed Subaru with a sharp, sword-like stare. Blinking at a brilliance that seemed to straighten his posture, Subaru faced it in turn.
To Subaru, doing so, Crusch slowly placed a hand to her chest, and—

"I cannot express enough gratitude for your cooperation this time. Without you, the subjugation of the White Whale would not have been achieved, and my path would have crumbled midway."

Saying so, she bowed deeply toward Subaru in a gesture of thanks.
At this sincere gratitude directed at him from such a person of high integrity, Subaru found himself involuntarily stiffening. In his life thus far, he had no memory of ever having such straightforward gratitude offered to him by someone of higher standing.

"N-no, come on, stop it. I didn't do anything that big..."

"You pinpointed the time and place of the White Whale's appearance, ran yourself ragged assembling forces beyond what the subjugation force alone could provide, roused the resolve of knights whose morale was on the verge of breaking, proposed a life-risking strategy for a decisive turnaround at the cost of your own safety, and on top of all that, saw it through to a magnificent success."

To Subaru, replying in halting fragments, Crusch laid out the results of his actions in this battle in careful order.
Hearing the consequences of his own deeds recounted so systematically, it truly was—

"Even I gotta say, that sounds like the work of someone out of their mind..."

"'Fought like a lion' doesn't quite capture it. But there is no doubt that you were the linchpin of this battle. Should your deeds ever be belittled, I would correct it, on my honor, without fail."

Piercing him with earnest eyes, throwing straightforward words at him, Crusch's praise held not a shred of calculation or hesitation. Given that she was a person who seemed the very embodiment of the word "sincerity," there could be no trace of falsehood in the gratitude her mouth wove.
Precisely because of that, Subaru, recalling their relationship up until the eve of departure, gave a wry smile.

"Well, glad to see your opinion of me's improved so much."

"There's no need for modesty. And I must admit that my assessment of days past was greatly mistaken. You have brought a rare stroke of good fortune. Ordinarily, I would wish to bring such an achievement into our house and reward it accordingly."

"I'll pass on that, thanks."

Narrowing her eyes, inviting Subaru in a low voice. But Subaru raised a hand and declined her invitation without a moment's hesitation,

"It's not loyalty, and it's not fealty either, but my trust is already placed where it belongs. You're a good person, and I really do think you'd make a great king..."

Crusch, surely, could become a monarch who led her people with more integrity than anyone.
She had that much capacity, and he'd witnessed her character time and again in this place of death. To Subaru, who had gone on lying to himself and others, it was almost too dazzling to look at directly, and he couldn't stop himself from admiring her with envy.

"—I'm going to make Emilia queen. Not for anyone else's sake. Because that's what I want to do."

"...It was something I already knew, and yet it stings all the same."

At Subaru's answer, Crusch let her lips curve into a faint smile and dipped her chin.
Then she unfolded her crossed arms, curled her pale fingers into a fist, and turned it toward Subaru.

"Very well. I shall repay your achievement in some other form. On the name of Crusch Karsten, I swear that promise shall be fulfilled."

Declaring it solemnly, Crusch opened her clenched fist and looked at her own palm.
Then, dropping her voice slightly,

"Come to think of it, this is the first time I've ever had an invitation refused this satisfyingly. Not even a moment's hesitation shown—it's a rather refreshing sort of defeat."

"...I think you're an amazing person. If it were just me, drifting around alone, I'd definitely be tempted to lean on that hand of yours for support."

Without anywhere to belong, with nothing settled, if someone of Crusch's caliber had extended a hand like that, he surely would have leapt at it without hesitation, clung to it, entrusted everything to it.
But right now, Subaru had someone he wanted to reach out and hold onto, someone whose palm supported his swaying, unsteady back.

"About the alliance—I'm counting on you. Whatever state things end up in when we finally end up on opposite sides, let's get along well until then."

"Natsuki Subaru. Let me correct one thing."

At Subaru's words, Crusch shook her head, pressed her lips together, and set her expression into something stern.
A tense atmosphere emanated from her once more, and Subaru felt a tingling sensation run down his spine, his eyes widening.
Crusch raised a single finger at Subaru like that, thrusting it before his face,

"Even should the moment come to decide the victor between us, I shall remain friendly toward you."

"——"

"Even on the day of our inevitable parting of ways, I will not forget my debt of gratitude to you from this day. Therefore, even when the time comes for us to stand opposed, I will pay you respect to the very end, and remain friendly."

Lowering the arm with its raised finger, Crusch declared it plainly, in a voice clear and resolute.
At her bearing, this time a chill truly ran down Subaru's spine. It wasn't born of any negative feeling, but simply from being overwhelmed by something great.

—This was who Crusch Karsten, Duchess of House Karsten, truly was.

"If the first and second spots in my heart weren't already taken, that would've been pretty dangerous."

"Ha. I have no thoughts of pursuing you as a woman would a man. There have been moments that stirred something in me, I won't deny, but regardless of my own will, I come at rather a high price."

To Subaru's flippant remark, meant to hide his unsettled heart, Crusch replied in kind, with a thin smile. Then she shifted her expression, her eyes turning distinctly composed, and with a "Now then" by way of preface,

"If I could, I would wish to carry the wounded and the White Whale's corpse back to the royal capital just as we are. But it seems you still have some mission left, don't you."

"...Guess someone with a Divine Protection would know, huh."

"One need only look at the eyes of that boy to know as much. No need for the power of a Divine Protection."

Watching Subaru's dark eyes intently, Crusch closed one eye and answered so. Then she looked Subaru up and down,

"You yourself surely aren't unharmed either. And still you push forward with something you must do?"

"Something I gotta do even if I'm badly hurt. In a way, this whale hunt was just a step toward pulling that off. Sorry if that sounds bad."

"Oh? So this White Whale subjugation was incidental."

To words that would surely sound ill to most ears, Crusch showed no sign of anger.
She seemed rather to have taken an interest in whatever this purpose of Subaru's was, murmuring "How intriguing" with a hand to her chin,

"The alliance with our house, too, was surely made with that in mind. If so, I can guess at the role being asked of us. ...Do you need hands?"

"I need them. But... honestly, I didn't think it'd be this rough getting here."

Answering weakly, Subaru surveyed the scene, filled with the wounded, and shrugged.
What awaited Subaru, having finished the subjugation of the White Whale, was a return to the Mathers domain where Emilia waited—which meant confronting that despised group.
In that fight against that hated enemy, it would have been ideal if he could borrow the strength of the Crusch Camp, but—

"With this many people hurt, I can't exactly ask for the impossible. Even Crusch, you've got the opinion of a head of house to consider, not just feelings. On top of all that, asking you to lend me hands..."

"Then let this old bones be worked to the bone, if you would."

The one who suddenly cut into the conversation, approaching with quiet steps, was a tall figure—drenched all over in someone else's blood, the wound on his left arm still visibly painful—the elderly swordsman, Wilhelm van Astrea.
He came toward them with a gait that showed not the slightest sign of the wound's effect, holding out the treasured sword he'd carried in his right hand toward Crusch,

"Crusch-sama, I return what you lent me. And for this occasion, I offer my heartfelt thanks. That my life's long-cherished wish has been fulfilled is thanks to your cooperation, Crusch-sama. ...Thank you. Truly, thank you."

"My aims and your long-cherished wish simply happened to align, that is all. That sword—keep it a while longer. Unarmed, you'll be of little use after this."

"Understood. I am grateful."

Answering Wilhelm's wrung-out words of thanks briefly, Crusch gestured with her chin, and Wilhelm turned to face Subaru.
Up close once more, the scent of blood wafting from him was tremendous, and the bladed intensity radiating from him brought an unintended sense of tension, as if a blade were being held to Subaru's slender nerve. And yet, it was true that the taut, strained atmosphere that had hung over him before the battle was gone now—Wilhelm's manner was clear and unclouded.
He looked straight at Subaru, and then knelt on the spot.
It was the same deepest bow of respect he had shown the night before their departure, offered to convey the utmost esteem for another. And—

"Natsuki Subaru-dono. This day's subjugation of the White Whale was accomplished thanks to your cooperation. That this body has been able to fulfill the meaning of its life up to this very day is thanks to you. My thanks. My thanks. —On everything I am, I offer you my thanks."

"——"

Wilhelm, who had devoted half his life to the sword and spent over ten years pursuing his revenge, had finally accomplished it. Swept up in the immense wave of passion behind the gratitude directed at him from this man, Subaru, afraid his voice might falter, could not bring himself to speak.
He waited a moment, calming himself, until he felt ready to give proper voice to words for the old man before him.
To show anything unsightly in the face of Wilhelm's resolve—that simply could not be allowed.

"What you accomplished was your own strength, Wilhelm-san. You're the one who decided to take down that White Whale, who researched it, trained for it, fought without giving up..."

Surely he had tasted setback again and again, come close to giving up more than once, thinking his obsession would never bear fruit.
Surely there had been moments of temptation to throw everything away, to free himself from that all-consuming fixation.
Because Subaru, more than anyone, knew the weakness of the heart, the experience of losing to oneself, the unreasonableness of being thwarted by fate—he could understand the hardship behind the fruition of Wilhelm's fierce resolve.
So—

"You loved your wife so much you made it all the way to taking down the White Whale. If I got to help with that even a little, then nothing could make me happier. I don't know if it's right to say this, but... congratulations. And—you did good."

"——"

At Subaru's words, Wilhelm raised his face, his eyes within that weathered, wrinkled face opening wide.
The feelings and the emotion Subaru felt were things Subaru had projected onto Wilhelm, empathizing with him on his own terms. He didn't think he could convey all of that in these brief words, and he doubted Wilhelm would take kindly to being spoken to as though understood so easily.
And yet, even so, he couldn't hold back the urge to say it. Words of comfort, offered to a predecessor who had kept the flame of love for his lost wife burning for fourteen years, who had kept running all this way, who had fought against fate itself.

"—My thanks."

Briefly, his voice trembling, Wilhelm answered so.
Then he bowed his head slightly, and after only a few seconds, rose to his feet and straightened his back. Turning his eyes to Crusch, he received her nod, and,

"I have received permission from Crusch-sama. This body, I shall entrust to you, Subaru-dono. Please make full use of me for your purpose."

"Wait, seriously?"

Looking to Crusch to confirm, she dipped her chin in affirmation.
Looking Wilhelm over once more, closely, Subaru felt both reassurance and a chill of awe at the pressure of a sword demon undiminished even with one arm wounded.

—Wilhelm's cooperation was, for Subaru, exactly what he could have wished for.

In the current situation, where any bolstering of forces was desperately needed, the strength of Wilhelm the sword demon was something Subaru wanted so badly it made his throat itch. But to an untrained eye, the old man's condition could hardly be called "light" injuries.
In response to that concern of Subaru's, Crusch shook her head, saying "Not a problem," and,

"Ferris!"

"Yes, yes, Crusch-sama."

At her sharp call, Ferris appeared, gliding smoothly into view.
He fell into step beside Crusch with a bouncing gait, the cat ears atop his head twitching in small movements,

"What is it, Crusch-sama? Feri-chan's in the middle of a whole lot of spinning plates right now, but of course Crusch-sama's request takes top priority, so it's fine, nya~"

"You'd better take responsibility for what you're about to say, halfway through saying it!"

Cutting in at the offhand way Ferris was about to toss aside his role as a healer, Subaru got a sour look from him in response. Crusch, addressing him, asked, "Are there any wounded whose lives are at risk?" and Ferris, pressing a finger to his lips,

"Most of the emergency treatment's done, so there's no one who's gonna just up and die if left alone, nya? A stroke of luck in an unlucky situation, though everyone here's stubbornly hard to kill, too~"

The affected pose she struck while answering grated on him every time, but at that answer, Subaru pressed a hand to his chest in relief. At the very least, it seemed Rem's condition wasn't serious. Given how much she'd teased Subaru in the latter half of their conversation, that worry had probably been mostly unnecessary—but even so, hearing her safety confirmed once more was a comfort.
In any case, Crusch, receiving this, said "Understood" solemnly, then,

"Can the wounded be transported? Then Ferris, that's all for now. After this, you'll accompany Natsuki Subaru and fulfill your role as part of the alliance."

So she commanded Ferris.
At those words, Subaru was startled once more. Removing "Blue" Ferris from this place meant that, once the minimum safety of the wounded had been secured, priority would be given to Subaru's business. It was a decision that put consideration for Subaru ahead of her own standing, and one would naturally expect it to draw the ire of Ferris, who put Crusch above all else—

"Understood. Feri-chan will accompany Subaru-kyun as is. Gotta treat Vil-jii along the way, too."

"My apologies for the trouble."

"In exchange, Vil-jii's gonna have to swing that sword around, so we're even, nya?"

No objection came.
Ferris accepted it as a matter of course, and Wilhelm too behaved as though he'd never doubted the decision. Subaru couldn't hide his surprise at the exchange between the master and her two attendants. Ferris glanced sidelong at Subaru, who wore that expression,

"So anyway, about half of the subjugation force who seem okay—twenty-some people, nya? I'll bring them along to help Subaru-kyun. Take good care of us~"

"'Take good care of us' is too casual! Are you sure about this?!"

"About what?"

"About what, he says... all kinds of stuff. Can you even trust my judgment?"

The more Subaru thought back on it, there was no one besides Ferris who had gone out of his way, in the royal capital, to needlessly twist the knife in Subaru's wounds.
Always wearing that friendly smile, always affecting that dainty manner—but Subaru had somehow understood that Ferris held deep contempt for his weakness. It seemed only natural to Subaru that he'd feel some reluctance about following someone like that, but—

"It's not that I trust Subaru-kyun, it's that I don't doubt Crusch-sama's judgment to trust Subaru-kyun. Don't get that mixed up, 'kay, nya?"

Snorting at Subaru's line of thinking, as if to drive the point home.
Fighting down the urge to let a vein bulge on his forehead at that malicious attitude, Subaru took a few deep breaths and, haltingly, managed to squeeze out an "Appreciate it." Then,

"Oh, forgot to mention—Rem-chan's staying behind, or rather, she's gotta go back to the royal capital with Crusch-sama, so keep that in mind, 'kay?"

"—Why?!"

At Ferris's words, tossed out as if suddenly remembered, a voice rang out sharply in denial. It was Rem, who had been watching this exchange from behind them; pushing herself half-upright, she glared resentfully at Ferris,

"Rem's fine, Rem is fine. Subaru-kun is going somewhere dangerous again from here, so how can Rem not—"

"You say that, but your body's not moving, is it? You went and took on the White Whale practically by yourself, and on top of that used top-tier magic over and over... Rem-chan's body right now is worn down to the point of being basically hollowed out. As a healer, I can't let you push yourself further than that. Understand, nya?"

"But!"

As if unwilling to accept it, Rem tried to rise to her feet to press her case further. But the arm she braced to push herself up gave out, and unable to support her trembling body, she nearly collapsed on the spot. Subaru rushed forward, catching her crumpling body just in time,

"Whoa, careful. —Hey, please, listen to Ferris and don't push yourself too hard."

"But! I don't want this, it's too much, I can't bear it."

Looking back up at Subaru so close before her, Rem's eyes welled with large tears.
More than being left behind, more than anything else, what she truly feared was—

"When Subaru-kun is troubled, Rem wants to be the one who reaches out a hand before anyone else. When Subaru-kun is lost, Rem wants to be the one who pushes his back forward. When Subaru-kun is facing down something, Rem wants to be there beside him, stopping his trembling. That's all Rem wants, that's the only thing Rem wants. So..."

"Then you don't have to worry about any of that."

"Eh?"

At her voice on the verge of tears, at those words heavy with growing affection thrown at him, Subaru felt his lips naturally loosen with something too tender to hide, and,

"Our hands are always linked. You've pushed my back more times than I can count. And as for trembling—just thinking of you is enough to steady me, one way or another. —I've already been saved by you, over and over."

"...Ah."

"It's fine, Rem. I'll go handle all of it myself, every last bit. I'm your hero. I decided to take that step. So there's nothing to worry about."

Her trembling eyes looked up at Subaru, her flushed cheeks warm and red. Subaru turned a smile toward her, grinning ferociously, baring his teeth,

"I even pulled off a whale hunt. Your hero's basically a demon now, isn't he."

"Subaru... ku—"

As if unable to hold back what was welling up inside her, Rem's attempt to call his name broke off partway. She struggled several times to swallow the impulse, holding her breath again and again, and then, unable to contain what overflowed, let it spill in drops from the corners of her eyes,

"—Yes. Rem's hero is the best in the world."

And smiling through her tears, she said so.


※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※


Leaving half the escort behind for Rem and the other wounded, and for Crusch as she returned to the royal capital, Subaru set out with the remaining subjugation force, heading straight for the Mathers domain.

With Wilhelm and Ferris as representatives, the number of subjugation force members accompanying Subaru came to twenty-four. It was quite a bit fewer than he'd originally hoped for, but they remained a vital force all the same.
And it wasn't only them accompanying him, either—

"Ahh, still, man, all the good stuff got snatched up by big bro, huh!"

"Cap'n! Mimi too! Mimi worked hard too! Mimi worked super duper hard!"

Chattering noisily back and forth, two beastfolk rode atop a large dog.
One was Ricardo, who had nearly become "filthy fireworks" shielding Subaru; the other was Mimi, who hadn't dropped her childlike demeanor even in the thick of a life-or-death battlefield.
Along with those two, about ten of the surviving beastman mercenary corps accompanied them as well. The remaining wounded, it seemed, were being led back to the royal capital by the other vice-captain, Hetaro.

"Still, your brother was so out of breath, how come you're so full of energy?"

"Hetaro's just puny, that's why. Not like Mimi, all trained up—he's a weakling! So lame!"

She laughed, cackling, mocking her brother's frailty. But Subaru's judgment was that the sister was probably just a stamina freak.
The type who simply loved fighting too much for her own good—a berserker type. Given her appearance, an adorable kitten-type beastfolk, the contrast made her almost pitiable in a way.

"Well, we didn't do all that much, but don't worry 'bout it. Got a proper request from the boss lady, so I'll cooperate with whatever your goal is, big bro."

"Cooperate with my goal, you say, but do you even know what I'm trying to—"

"You're gonna square off against the Witch Cult, ain'tcha?"

At Ricardo's words, his voice dropping low all of a sudden, Subaru's throat caught.
Naturally, he found himself gripping tight on the reins of the Earth Dragon he was resting his weight on—Patrasche—and heard the dragon give a small whinny, as if worried for its rider.
At Subaru's profile going tense like that, Ricardo bared his teeth, saying "No need to be so shocked," and,

"For merchants, freshness of information's everything, and we're in the boss lady's employ. Means we keep our eyes and ears open to some extent, including on the background of things. Our ears ain't just big for show, y'know."

"That's right! Mimi's ears are huge!"

"Wasn't talkin' about you, squirt."

While Mimi reacted from an entirely different angle to Ricardo's joke, earning a wry laugh from him, Subaru swallowed quietly, realizing just how much he'd underestimated the reach of Anastasia's hand.
Still, since he needed their cooperation for his goal, it wasn't a matter he could keep silent about, and disclosing that information was only natural at this point.
Though, if possible, he wanted to hold off on all of that until he could confirm the results of his insurance—something he'd set in motion before leaving the royal capital.

"Oh, looks like we might be able to join up."

"Huh?"

Subaru noticed Ricardo, looking ahead, gazing off into the distance, and hurriedly followed the direction of his gaze. But what spread out before Subaru's eyes was still the deep darkness of the nighttime plain, and he couldn't tell what it was Ricardo was looking at.
Straining his eyes, still struggling to see anything, Ricardo let out a small chuckle,

"No need to strain yourself like that. Just wait, and you'll see soon enough. Relax."

"I want to eliminate uncertain factors as early as possible. Don't drag it out."

"Fair enough, then I won't drag it out. —It's a bit far off, but what's comin' this way is the other half of our mercenary corps."

"Half?"

Not understanding what Ricardo meant, Subaru tilted his head and asked back.
Half—well, the wounded and such were, as it stood, already accompanying Crusch and the others back to the royal capital.

"By half, I mean exactly that. To begin with, we, the 'Iron Fang,' only sent half our people to the White Whale subjugation. The other half had their own job to do."

"Their own job, meaning?"

"Gotta keep other folks from wanderin' onto the highway and gettin' caught up in the fight, right? So it was their job to seal off the far side of the highway ahead of time. They set out last night, so you never had the chance to cross paths with 'em, big bro."

Listening to Ricardo's explanation, Subaru nodded in understanding.
Still, the point about not committing full force to the White Whale subjugation was hard to swallow easily—but there had, after all, been a real possibility of Crusch's subjugation attempt ending in total failure. In that case, Anastasia's decision to avoid losing all of her valuable forces at once wasn't wrong either. With so few cards in hand, Subaru, who had no choice but to go all-in, wasn't really in a position to judge on a different level than that.
Regardless,

"So, the ones coming now are the rest of your comrades. There's a leader for that group too?"

"Mimi's little brother Tivey's runnin' it! He's a super amazing guy who can do combo moves with Mimi, instead of Hetaro!"

Answering Subaru's question, Mimi puffed out her chest proudly.
Just hearing her answer made his expectations for the rest of the group drop slightly, but—

"Well, but the brother we already met was normal enough. Maybe there's a small chance this other brother's normal too...?"

"Sorry to worry ya, but Tivey's actually pretty sharp, one of the smartest in our whole crew. Handles the money and the negotiatin', and he's good at managing Mimi too. Upgraded version of Hetaro, basically."

"Don't say that, Hetaro's gonna feel sorry for himself..."

Including being left behind as unfit for combat, Hetaro was becoming almost too pitiable.
Setting that pity aside, if this lineup joining them meant their forces would be further strengthened, that was a welcome bonus. Once they joined up, he ought to set up another discussion.
Subaru racked his brain over how to explain the hostility toward the Witch Cult to the Crusch Camp—Wilhelm, Ferris, and the rest. Right before his eyes, exactly as Ricardo had said, the pack of Ligers aiming to rejoin them drew steadily, steadily closer, kicking up clouds of dust, and—

"—Hm?"

Subaru let out a small sound, noticing something off.
Among the multiple Ligers running toward them, he could make out a single silhouette mixed in whose outline was different from the rest.
As the distance closed and its hazy outline gradually sharpened into something clear, the vision coming into focus revealed the form of an Earth Dragon.

And astride that Earth Dragon's back was—

"—Why the hell are you here."

"That's quite the way to greet reinforcements. As ever, aren't you."

The two Earth Dragons came to a stop, facing each other, and Subaru and that figure confronted one another.
A man with purple hair neatly combed back, clad in the dignified regalia of the Royal Guard, a composed smile rising at the corner of his mouth.


—The figure of fate, Julius Juukulius, gazed at Subaru with an air of elegant poise.
