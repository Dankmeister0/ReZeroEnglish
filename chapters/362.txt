Arc 5 Chapter 46: "The Shape of One's Heart"

When Subaru brought Wilhelm back to the meeting hall, everyone had been eagerly awaiting his return. Reinhard looked at Wilhelm, and the grandfather dipped his chin to his grandson in welcome as he rejoined them.
Watching the two of them stand together at the wall's edge out of the corner of his eye, Subaru took a seat at the round table beside Otto.

"Sorry for the wait. How'd things go?"

"We received a full explanation, and the explanation just finished. More importantly, Natsuki-san, how is Crusch-sama doing upstairs?"

"Not great. But I'll say this much—it's not like there's no hope. It'll have to wait until after we've driven off the Witch Cult, but there might be a way."

"I see. Well, that much is good news, at least."

Otto let out a relieved breath, and the others who'd been listening in relaxed as well. Watching their reactions, Subaru's heart was full of an urge to apologize to them.
It wasn't a lie, but it was far from the whole truth. The method that might save Crusch was, for Subaru himself, an extremely risky proposition. If there were someone else who shared Subaru's particular condition, the odds would improve considerably—but as it stood.

"Either way, Crusch-san won't be able to rejoin the front lines during this battle. Ferris won't want to leave her side either, so I think it's best if the medical team just stays put at the city hall. If they're posted anywhere else, there's a chance they won't be able to respond in time."

"The plan for simultaneous attacks on all four sites hasn't changed, so making the city hall—which sits at the center relative to all four control towers—our base makes the most sense. In that case..."

Anastasia clapped her hands and looked around at everyone's faces.

"Well then, shall we finally get down to the real business? The four control towers, and the four Sin Archbishops. Let's talk about how we're going to allocate our forces to take them down."

The Crusch Camp would field the "Sword Devil," Wilhelm.
The Felt Camp would field the "Sword Saint," Reinhard.
The Anastasia Camp would field the "Finest Knight," Julius, and the "Iron Fang's Captain," Ricardo.
The Emilia Camp would field the "Spirit Knight," Subaru, and the "Shield of the Sanctuary," Garfiel.
And the Priscilla Camp—

"Myself and Al, the two of us."

"This might sound harsh, but... are you actually going to fight? You're a Royal Selection candidate."

As each camp's forces were being confirmed, Priscilla proudly put forward her own name. Subaru frowned at that, and she snorted derisively in response.

"It is precisely because I am a candidate for the throne that I must. Do not lump me together with fools who've rendered themselves useless before the crucial hour, or weaklings who lacked the power to fight from the very start. Whether with a blade or upon a stage, I stand above all others—that is what makes me who I am."

"That last remark I cannot let pass unchallenged. This 'fool' you speak of—surely you don't mean my lord?"

"You only bristle because it struck a nerve, does it not? Your phrasing is far too roundabout, old soldier. Withdrawing over a trifle before the crucial hour is hardly the conduct of one who has been chosen."

Right at the outset of the discussion, Wilhelm and Priscilla were already trading sharp-edged words. Normally this would be the sort of thing to let slide, but various circumstances had left Wilhelm without his usual composure. Priscilla, meanwhile, was simply being her usual self, and her sheer insufferability left the others speechless.

"Yeah, yeah, weakling, fool, whatever—let's just move things along. No need to snap at each other."

"Oh, how dull. I am not so easily managed that I'll sit quietly and listen to talk of weaklings, you know."

"Being weak and being unable to win aren't the same thing, are they? If you don't show a little generosity of spirit, people aren't going to follow you. Everyone's on edge right now. Show a bit of patience."

"Hmph."

Whether Anastasia's remark had struck home or not, Priscilla simply snorted and offered no rebuttal. Seeing her sheathe her claws, Wilhelm, who had been radiating fierce killing intent, withdrew it as well.
It was only natural, given they were from different camps, but this was hardly a harmonious group.

"So then, Priscilla-sama's camp consists of Priscilla-sama and Al, the two of you... and I take it we shouldn't expect anything from the boy over there?"

"Do you imagine that frail, feeble child could accomplish anything? I keep him at my side purely for my own amusement, through and through. Naturally, he'll remain here."

"Understood. That means the eight of us will be handling the four sites."

Priscilla's page—the boy Schult—stepped back, and the round table was neatly divided in two: those who could fight, and those who could not. Setting aside the eight combatants, the non-combatants numbered six: Anastasia, Schult, Liliana, Otto, plus Crusch and Ferris upstairs.

"Before we divide up our forces, let's go over what we know about the Sin Archbishops again. Let's see... the only one who's seen all their faces is... Natsuki-kun, right?"

"Yeah, I guess so. I know it's kind of grating to be called the world's foremost authority on the Witch Cult, but I'll go ahead and explain. At least as far as their abilities go, what we know."

Subaru stood, drawing everyone's attention as he began to speak—about the Witch Cult that had assaulted this city, and the worst of the worst, the Sin Archbishops who commanded them.

"First, 'Wrath.' Goes by the name Sirius, wrapped head to toe in bandages. Can't tell from looks alone, but I'm pretty sure it's a woman. Coils chains around her arms and swings them around to attack. Also seems to use fire magic."

"If that were all, she wouldn't sound like much of an opponent. Is she considerably skilled, then?"

"Coming from you, that's a hard question to answer no matter who we're talking about, Reinhard. ...In terms of raw combat power, Wilhelm-san or Julius could hold their own against her just fine. In close combat, she gave Emilia a decent fight. But there's the matter of Wrath's Authority."

"Authority..."

Reinhard put a hand to his chin, his brow furrowing at the word.
Subaru nodded back at him.

"The nastiest part about the Sin Archbishops is that Authority—their unique power. It's not magic, it's not Curse Arts, it's some incomprehensible force, and thinking about the principles behind it is a waste of time. Every one of them is powerful, and figuring out how to counter it is the key to beating each Sin Archbishop."

"Subaru-dono, you defeated 'Sloth' in the past, did you not? Did that one also possess an Authority?"

"It did. The Sin Archbishop of Sloth had two Authorities: 'Unseen Hands' and 'Sloth' itself. One's the power to extend any number of invisible arms with monstrous strength. Get caught by one of those, and even a human body gets torn apart easily."

"I too confirmed that horror, though only through his eyes. Indeed, it possesses enough force to gouge out flesh from a mere graze—it is credible."

Julius backed up Subaru's explanation.
Having lent his eyes to Subaru during their joint battle against Petelgeuse, he had witnessed the Unseen Hands firsthand. He was in the perfect position to corroborate the account.

"The other 'Sloth' power forcibly saps the ability to act from anyone within its range. Whether that one really counts as an Authority is a bit iffy, but we got around it because it doesn't work on people with the aptitude to be a spirit user. That one, too, was possible because it was me and Julius."

"What can be said of both powers is this: however terrifying a strength a Sin Archbishop may wield, if the right person is put in the right place, they are never an unbeatable foe."

"Rare for you to say something worthwhile, Julius. That's exactly right."

At Subaru's backhanded praise, Julius gave him a look that was somewhere between warm and unsettling. Feeling a faint chill at that gaze, Subaru cleared his throat once more.

"Anyway, back to Wrath's Authority. So far, what we know about Wrath's power comes down to sharing and transmitting emotions and sensations."

"Sharing... emotions and sensations?"

Most of the faces around the table tilted in confusion, not quite grasping Subaru's explanation. It was a difficult thing to explain, so Subaru had to choose his words carefully.

"In other words, Wrath can concentrate the emotions of everyone within range into one. Turn one person's anger into everyone's anger, one person's sorrow into everyone's sorrow, that kind of thing."

"What the hell's that supposed to mean? I don't get what the point of that even is."

"By itself, sure, it sounds like just a power to force everyone onto the same emotional wavelength. But that's not all it does. The scary part is that it can concentrate hostility too. Meaning—whatever hostility Wrath feels toward an enemy, it can transmit that same hostility to the people around that enemy."

"Are you saying the city's own people, the ones nearby, would turn hostile toward us?"

"Exactly."

Subaru snapped his fingers at Otto for landing on the right answer.
Everyone's expressions darkened at the implication, but the problem didn't end there.

"Fool. You said just now that Wrath's power shares emotions and sensations, did you not?"

The first to arrive at that understanding was Priscilla, lounging back in her seat.
She pierced Subaru with her crimson eyes, hiding her mouth behind her fan.

"If the sharing of emotion is as you've just explained, then the sharing of sensation is another matter entirely. And if it is what I imagine it to be, it's rather a hideous power, is it not?"

"I don't know if what you're imagining matches, but yeah, it's about as bad as it gets. Wrath's Authority shares wounds too, among everyone within range. And that includes Wrath herself."

"Wait, if even she's not exempt... hold on, bro, you're kidding, right? That's the worst. So basically, if we kill Wrath, everyone else connected to her dies too?"

The worst-case scenario, one that had already been made real once by Reinhard's own hand.
Even if they struck down Sirius, the source of the malice, it would leave behind wounds on everyone else that would never disappear. There was no telling how many people would have to die alongside her in order to kill her.

"—How interesting."

Faced with such hopeless information, everyone fell silent, unable to think of a countermeasure.
And in the midst of that silence, one person alone—Priscilla—twisted her cheek into a delighted smile.

"Very well. That fool called 'Wrath'—I shall wring her neck myself. Rejoice."

"W-wait, wait, wait a second! I don't get why you're so gung-ho, but this isn't as simple as you're making it out to be! Were you even listening!?"

"Of course I was listening. And having listened, I have declared that I shall do it. My, what a base and loathsome opponent. All the more fitting for me to cut down."

Ignoring Subaru's attempt to stop her, Priscilla snapped her fan shut with a crack and swept her gaze across the room.
The sharpness and heat of her stare was enough to overwhelm even this room full of formidable figures.

"If what you have described is the whole of her Authority, then I have already grasped the mechanism behind it. To think she means to command the common rabble while setting me aside—how utterly insolent. All the foolish masses exist for my sake. Any insolent vermin who dares lay a hand on what is mine shall be swiftly disposed of, cast out from my garden."

"P-princess... isn't that laying it on a bit thick, even for you?"

"What nonsense do you speak, Al. I am well aware of your cowardice, but for you to shrink back before some insolent wretch who has dared offend me—what is the meaning of this? With myself, and that songstress over there, there is nothing to fear."

"I'm not saying it 'cause I'm scared, it's just... songstress?"

Al, who had been trying to rein in his mistress's wild talk, froze at the unexpected remark. Nodding magnanimously at his attendant's surprise, Priscilla indicated Liliana, seated at the corner of the round table, with her folded fan.
Suddenly thrust into the spotlight and pointed at with the fan, Liliana's eyes went wide.

"M-me!? You're naming me!? Why on earth would that suddenly—!?"

"You have not forgotten what happened on the road, surely? How many of the common rabble your song stirred the hearts of. Simply do the same again. As for the emotions of the foolish masses—one need only wrest them away."

"I just cheered up some people who were feeling a bit anxious, that's all! That's an awfully generous overestimation of what I did, if you ask me, and besides, I'm a delicate little thing who crumbles under pressure..."

"Oh? Then you admit defeat—the defeat of the song passed down to you by your ancestors."

At Priscilla's snort and thoroughly contemptuous words, Liliana's expression changed.
Her forced smile and the servile deflection she'd been attempting gave way to something in deadly earnest.

"What... what do you mean by that...?"

"Is it not obvious? This song you have so preciously carried forward—does it not, in this hour when people's hearts cry out for salvation, sing of cowering away and making a pathetic spectacle of yourself? Such a loser's howl, is it not nothing but a heap of futility and waste? A dog's bark would be preferable, at least it doesn't proclaim its own selfishness. Well? Care to compose one? A hymn to losers, perhaps?"

"Aaah-aaah! You're really going there!? You're actually saying it!? Fine! Bring it on! I'll do it! I got it, fine, that's just fine, isn't it! Me! The bard Liliana! To be treated like this, to be spoken to like that! If I stay quiet after this, I'll never live it down as a woman! Even poor Kiritaka-san, may he rest in peace, would crawl right out of his grave in mortification!"

Provoked by Priscilla's ferocious taunting, Liliana detonated into an equally ferocious outburst. Her face flushed bright red, she began violently strumming the instrument on her lap.

"I was going to sing a requiem to comfort the soul of poor Kiritaka-san, scattered on the city's waters, but forget it, forget it! A fight over emotions? Bring it on! There's no way the song I've inherited, the song humanity has woven throughout the world, is going to lose to some incomprehensible power like that! The power of song is plenty incomprehensible in its own right, thank you very much! Hmph!"

Thoroughly worked up, Liliana lay sprawled across the round table performing, and Schult and Otto hurriedly pulled her down. Watching from a distance as Liliana retreated to a corner of the room and began playing something suspiciously like rock, Subaru turned his eyes back to Priscilla.

"Never mind how easy she is to rile up—what's your actual plan here? I get that you can handle yourself, but I can't just throw someone into a fight they have no chance of winning..."

"I do not engage in battles I intend to lose. All things in this world unfold as I will them to. Besides, until we arrived at this city hall, it was I who was in that songstress's company the whole while. It is precisely because I recognized her usefulness that I brought her along—and it is I who now decide to bring her further still."

"You're saying Liliana has some power that can counter Sirius?"

"If she did not, then I would have lost. And my losing is not a thing that can exist in this world. Therefore, that songstress possesses it. Must I explain further?"

The explanation was lacking in every particular, and Subaru's patience was nearly at its end. But as if to back up Priscilla's claim, it was Schult who raised his hand instead.
The boy attendant, his lovely eyes trembling, carefully chose his words as he spoke earnestly.

"U-um, um... I do believe it's true that Liliana-sama's song holds a special power. Hearing Liliana-sama sing really did free people from their anxiety and irritation—that's something we saw for ourselves, at the shelters we passed through on the way here..."

"You went around to shelters and had Liliana sing?"

"Did I not just say so?"

"You did not!"

The lack of explanation was staggering.
Holding his head in his hands over Priscilla's attitude, Subaru turned back to Reinhard.

"Hey, Reinhard. You can look at a person and tell what power they've got, or, right—Divine Protection. Can't you see people's Divine Protections or something?"

"A Divine Protection that reveals other people's Divine Protections? I've heard that the possessor of the 'Divine Protection of Judgement' can do that. There's no one with it in Lugunica, but apparently there is in Vollachia. Ah, I see—you want to confirm what Divine Protection Liliana-sama has. That would indeed give us something to go on."

Grasping Subaru's intent from the question, Reinhard fell into thought.
Subaru had only asked on the off chance it would work, and he knew he'd just made an unreasonable request of Reinhard. He shook his head at the red-haired young man lost in thought, telling him not to worry about it.

"I mean, I've heard you're crazy talented at all kinds of stuff, so I got my hopes up for a second, but I guess it's not that convenient. It's fine. Let's just try seeing whether Liliana's singing can cancel out Wrath's Authority for now, and—"

"No need to worry about it, Subaru. —I've just received it."

"Huh?"

Reinhard laughed, clapping Subaru on the shoulder just as he was about to propose the singing experiment. Then he narrowed his blue eyes and gazed at Liliana, still performing in the corner of the room.
And then—

"Remarkable. Liliana-sama possesses the 'Divine Protection of Telepathy.'"

"Forget the Divine Protection, right now I'm more surprised by you. Huh? What did you just say? You 'received' it? Received it how, like some kind of stork delivery?"

"Subaru, this isn't the time to joke around. I've confirmed Liliana-sama's Divine Protection. The 'Divine Protection of Telepathy' is, so to speak, a Divine Protection for conveying one's own thoughts to others. Normally it's only strong enough to send small thoughts to someone with whom one shares a deep bond, but... a song. I'd never considered that."

Reinhard was genuinely impressed by Liliana, but it was Subaru, watching his profile, whose jaw hung open in disbelief.
He'd been calling Reinhard's power cheat-level, superhuman, all this time, but this was just too much—being loved by the heavens to this degree.

For the Divine Protection you wished for to simply arrive in the hands of the very person who wished for it.

"——?"

Following that thought to its conclusion, something snagged in Subaru's mind.
Being able to obtain the Divine Protection you wanted. At the very least, that was the only way to describe what had just happened to Reinhard. And that in itself was an incredibly reassuring, enviable power.
But it felt like it might connect to something wrong, something Subaru couldn't quite put into words.

Wrath assault team—Priscilla. Al. Liliana. Decided.
