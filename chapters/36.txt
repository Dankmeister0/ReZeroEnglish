Arc 2 Chapter 12: "Two People Whose Compatibility Is Both Good and Bad"

Having sent Subaru flying, Beatrice let out a breath and smiled quietly, looking pleased with herself.

Then she turned the hand that had just dealt out violence palm-up and shifted her gaze,

"Serves him right, I suppose. ...Nii-cha, over here."

"Yes, yes. Well then, I'll see Lia later."

Called by the beckoning girl, Puck floated fluffily toward the door. Her palm welcomed his arrival, and this time the door was shut tight for good — turn end.

The opponent's turn of playing cards had ended, and now it was Subaru's turn — but battered and bruised as he already was, his will to play his next card was already fading fast.

Subaru, still pinned underfoot, looked up resentfully at the twins as they carefully straightened the angle of the furnishings.

"Sure, compared to me — a guy who's not worth a single cent — that's higher priority, I get it... but what kind of maid steps on a guest and just leaves him there?"

"My, did you hear that, Sister? He's saying something strange."
"Yes, I heard, Rem. That's a rather sad thing to come out of his head."

"That's harsh! Anyway, if you're both fine now, get off me already! Wait, you two even match your underwear — blue and pink? I'll give you credit for tha—bwonkrshhn."

His words cut off as the twins stomped on his face in tandem. Chanting a flat, deadpan "Kyaa, iyaa~" in perfect sync, the twins moved away. Watching Subaru rise to his feet with footprints stamped on his face, they took each other's hands and leaned their heads together.

"Well, our guest — no, Subaru-kun, is going to be our coworker, isn't he?"
"Well, our guest — no, Barusu, is in the same position as us, a servant, isn't he?"

"Hey, one of you — my name just became a blinding incantation."

It was a classic bit that virtually everyone touches on at least once in elementary or middle school. The pink-haired girl, having unwittingly put on a crossover with Subaru's original world, tilted her head in bewilderment.

Still, there was some truth to what they'd said. Which was to say,

"So that's my position, huh. A servant — basically a manservant kind of role, then."

"As things stand, it'd be best for you to help out with work around the manor under Ram and Rem's direction. Do you have a complaint?"

"If I've got a complaint, it's only with myself a minute ago for mixing up 'hire me' with 'take care of me.' ...Well, but it's all in how you look at it! Who knows, maybe the girl I've got my eye on will get all fluttery over the profile of a hardworking man!"

There was something to the idea that labor, service to society — that's what makes a man worth his salt.

He glanced hopefully at Emilia now and then, and she answered his gaze with an ambiguous smile,

"Um, I'm not really sure what's going on, but... good luck?"

"Right! Leave it to me — think of it like boarding a super luxury cruise ship! Never worked a day in my life, though!"

Zero work experience — and zero times he'd even resolved to get some. He felt almost proud of the remarkable progress made by the freeloader of freeloaders he'd once been.

But it seemed no one around him was willing to share in his sunny mood.

"Sister, Sister. Rem was a fool to think the work might go even a little smoothly."
"Rem, Rem. Ram was a fool to expect she could slack off even a little."

"Sister, maybe learn to wrap your true feelings in a little tact!?"

The twins made no attempt to hide their disappointment. He protested desperately, but even Emilia beside him let out a tired sigh, and his spirit nearly broke.

Perhaps because Subaru's face looked so pitiful, Emilia hastily prefaced her words with "even so,"

"Judging from the look of your hands and fingers, Subaru, maybe it can't be helped. You're stubborn, so I won't ask, but you probably came from a position where you didn't have to work."

"Hearing just that makes my background sound seriously sketchy, doesn't it."

"I'm trying my hardest to smooth things over for you, so don't make fun of it!"

As if dubbing the moment herself with a reproachful "no-no," she poked his forehead — and in the process, Subaru caught her outstretched hand.

Seeing her arm as she gave a surprised "ah," he marveled at how slender and lovely her fingers were. If anything, they didn't look like the fingers of a working woman at all.

"Naka-maaa."

"I really, really hate that pronunciation. And besides, we're not the same. I can properly take care of myself in every way. I may be relying on this manor's hospitality now, but I managed on my own originally."

Shaking her hand free of his grip, Emilia gave a little "hmph" and puffed out her chest lightly. Having household skills probably made her feel like she had the upper hand over Subaru.

That slightly childish side of her was awfully cute, though she herself seemed to be under the impression that the gesture was something like "elegant."

"Anyway, setting that aside, I refuse to be treated as useless! I'll have you know it's not like I can't do any housework at all. I've got my strong subjects too!"

"Oh? That's a bit surprising. What are you good at? Cooking, laundry... cleaning?"

"Sewing!"

"There's another surprising skill!"

Emilia looked like she'd been blindsided from an unexpected angle, while Subaru pouted, jutting out his lips to show his displeasure.

It wasn't a lie or a bluff — being good with a needle was simply the truth. Threading a small needle with fussy, careful precision and mending things with it was one of Subaru's hidden skills.

"I can sew back on buttons that come off, or mend torn little things! And what I'm really good at is... embroidering a little one-point design on clothes or accessories."

"That's a skill so refined it's almost hard to find a use for it..."

Even as she said it, Subaru thought, yeah, that's probably true.

Compared to orthodox housework like cooking, laundry, and cleaning, sewing had to be admitted a step behind in "major" appeal. Especially since, unlike those three daily essentials, it wasn't exactly an essential skill.

"I wonder what your family was thinking, teaching you that..."

"I don't think they were thinking anything at all — and it's less that I was taught, more like I just picked it up on my own."

"Hmm?" Emilia gave him an interested look, but Subaru waved it off with "well, well, never mind, never mind."

Then Subaru turned back to the twins and, puffing out his chest pointlessly, said with an air of self-importance,

"So then. What kind of work will you entrust to a man like me? I'll defer to the opinion of you senior ladies! I'll give it my all — crush my bones to—"

"'—smash my body.'"

"Yeah, that. I'll do that."

The three of them pointed it out together, confirming the word that had briefly slipped his mind.

Watching the servant group already showing quite the teamwork, Roswaal nodded in satisfaction, touching his chin with a "mm-hm."

"How beautiful it is when people get along. There seems to be no lingering ill will between you-u, which as an employer I find most gratifyi-ing. Don't you agree?"

"It's easy to work with them, I guess? There's this weirdly good wavelength between us? I'm sure our chemistry's good — way better than with that loli, anyway!"

"It really must have bothered you, having such good chemistry with Beatrice..."

Emilia's pitying murmur became the final word that closed out the long, noisy exchange at the breakfast table.

Clapping his hands lightly, Roswaal gathered the gazes of the remaining four, then pointed at what looked like a picture frame hanging on the wall.

Drawn along, Subaru looked too, his furrowed brow expressing his confusion.

—Because what was set into that frame wasn't a painting like the ones he'd occasionally glimpsed in the hallways, but a crystal a little larger than a fist.

A faint, dark blue like the bottom of a lake — the crystal wore that eerie glow, dimly receiving the gazes of the five people in the dining hall. Pointing at it, the tall man said,

"Now the-en, the Hour of Water is about half go-o-one already. Time is finite, so let's get a move on, shall we-e? —Ram and Rem, as I said before. Emilia-sama, please stop by my room once. Well then, dismissed."

With those words from Roswaal, the cozy gathering of breakfast came to a complete end.

Rem began briskly clearing the tableware, while Emilia let out a wistful sigh, perhaps thinking of her schedule ahead. Roswaal, meanwhile, approached Subaru with an almost bouncing step and clapped the shoulder of the slightly left-behind young man,

"Well the-en, I'll be looking forward to seeing how you work. Of course, now that I've hired you, you'll be paid too, so don't you worry about that."

"I guess we'll hash out the details of my employment later... but anyway, thanks for looking out for me. Should I call you 'master' or something?"

"Ahaha, so long as it's not in front of guests, 'Roz-icchi' is fine. It's surprisingly refreshing to be called by a nickname like that."

Winking with his yellow eye, Roswaal gave a light wave and left the dining hall. Just before that, he shot a small glance at Ram, and the pink-haired girl, receiving it, flushed and nodded repeatedly as though she'd been given a silent instruction.

The twins were normally sparing with changes in expression or tone, but only when dealing with Roswaal did that flat composure crack. The word *loyal dog* flitted through Subaru's mind.

"Well then, Barusu."

"Ah, so you're fully committed to that one now."

"Yes, that's right, Barusu. Per Roswaal-sama's instructions, I'll show you around the manor first. Don't get separated — follow along."

"I'm not Emilia-tan, I'm not gonna wander off like that."

"Su. Ba. Ru!"

Emilia puffed out her cheeks at the teasing remark. Struck by the novelty of her calling his name syllable by syllable, Subaru broke into a grin and pointed at her,

"It's a stylish little party joke. If you can't roll with light banter like this, you'll never make a lovely queen, Fräulein."

"What's a 'Fräulein'..."

"That, among other things, is something you need to study up on. Life is like climbing an endless mountain path unarmed. Slack off on gathering knowledge — item pickups, if you will — along the way, and you'll drop out around the third station, let alone reach the summit."

Emilia fell silent, her eyes wide, seemingly moved by what he'd just said.

Well, coming from a man who'd dropped out of school life partway through, those words carried real weight. Inwardly wounded by his own self-deprecating joke, Subaru turned to Ram with a "now then,"

"Well then, much as I hate to leave, shall we get going, senpai?"

"Let's do that, Barusu. Well then, Emilia-sama, see you later."

Pinching the edge of her skirt, she bowed, then headed for the door with Subaru following behind, her pink hair swaying. Emilia, last of all, said,

"Subaru. Me too, but... you do your best too, okay?"

"What is that, I'm so happy, I've got motivation overflowing now — are you a goddess?"

Pinching the hem of his tracksuit just a little, he bowed in imitation of Ram, turning Emilia's expression into something quite odd, before leaving the dining hall.

Ram, who had stopped once out in the corridor, watched Subaru's little gesture and let her unmoving expression frown just slightly,

"What's required of a maid and what's required of a servant are different things, you know."

"I know, I know. But since I'm doing housekeeping anyway, I wanted to do it gracefully and elegantly too. Oh right, is there like a uniform or something?"

Starting servant life still dressed in a tracksuit was admittedly a bit flavorless.

Of course, in terms of ease of movement, it was without question an all-purpose material, but—

"I mean, now that I'm actually being put to work at a noble's estate like this, I kind of long for a dignified sort of outfit, you know? I bet formal wear suits me more than you'd think."

"True, clothing matters. There should be — yes, I'm sure there's just the right outfit."

"Right, right, then let's start by putting that on. I'm the type who goes by appearances first, generally! Like with studying for exams — I always start by cleaning my room so I can focus on studying."

And then he never comes back to it.

Subaru gave a thumbs up, teeth gleaming. Ram, in response, raised the fingers of both hands and murmured "hmm" as she looked over his whole body.

With just that, she seemed to have roughly measured his clothing size, and nodding in satisfaction, Ram gestured upward with her hand,

"There's a servants' waiting room on the second floor — change there. Given your size, Barusu, I'm sure Frederica's clothes, the ones she left behind when she quit two months ago, will fit you."

"Oh, how convenient timing that Frederica quit... wait, isn't she a woman?"

"Her build was about the same as yours, Barusu."

"But her gender's different, right?"

Stopping her steps just as she'd started walking, Ram turned back and gave Subaru a cold, dead-eyed look. Then, sighing tiredly, she touched her forehead,

"You said you wanted to look graceful and elegant, so I'm trying to make that happen for you — what exactly is your complaint?"

"If it were Emilia-tan, sure, but who benefits from me wearing a maid outfit?! What if I awaken some weird fetish and it becomes my benefit? There's a chance of it, so I don't want it to awaken!"

Ever since crossing into this other world, all sorts of fetishes had been blooming vividly of late.

If the door to crossdressing opened on top of that, there would be nothing left anywhere of the original prototype that was Natsuki Subaru as a man.

Ram reacted to Subaru's shouting by covering her ears, then, watching him catch his breath with his shoulders heaving, said with a bored look,

"I really can't get into my rhythm without Rem around. Let's just go already."

"More unshakeable than me — seriously, senpai's on another level."

Maybe you need a heart this unshakeable just to get by here, huh.

Before his first-ever job experience, spanning both his original world and this new one, Subaru was already growing gloomy, having realized just how high that wall stood.

—As an aside, though.

"Of the servants' waiting rooms on the second floor... let's see, any of the ones on the west side will do. Pick whichever you like as your own room. I'll leave the uniform there too."

"Roger that. Well then, let's see..."

Guided to the second floor of the manor and given a uniform and temporary lodging, Subaru surveyed the rooms offered as candidates.

Even so, the contents were surely the same, just shifted in position. If that were the case, one closer to the stairs would be more convenient for getting around.

With that thought, he twisted the doorknob of a random door.

"Well then, this room—"

"Nii-cha's wonderful. Fluffiness is the best, I suppose, fluff fluff fluff."

The moment it opened, he found a loli in a library, petting a kitten to her heart's content.

Noticing his presence, she slowly turned her ringleted gaze toward him. Subaru looked at Ram, standing in the hallway, and confirmed she was shaking her head.

Then, scratching his head, he said to Beatrice in front of him,

"Congratulations. I hereby bless the birth of a new fluff-ist!"

"Just shut the door already, I suppose!"

Blasted by magic power, he slammed into the corridor wall once again.

With Subaru seeing stars after hitting the back of his head first, the door was slammed shut with a bang, leaving him behind.

"You... damn...!"

Shaking his head, even Subaru, with his vision still flickering, was seething with anger. He threw open the door that had just been slammed shut, meaning to hurl complaints at the flirting going on inside. But—

"Putting your hands on someone else's fluff-girl — pay up damages, damag—...huh?"

Spraying spit as he stepped inside, what spread before him was a plain room containing nothing but a bed and a closet.

Not a trace of the library from before remained. Probably the effect of "Door Crossing."

"Once Beatrice-sama hides her presence, there's no telling anymore. Unless you try every single door in the manor, she won't come out on her own."

Ram said this flatly, as if telling him to admit defeat outright.

His shoulder was patted from behind as if in consolation, and at that touch, Subaru—

"Man, that really pisses me off. The way she acts like I'm the one in the wrong is what's wrong!"

—did not admit defeat.

Shaking off Ram's hand, Subaru spun around and dashed down the corridor at full speed. Before Ram's widened eyes, he sprinted all the way to the door at the very end of the hallway, and—

"This one!"

"Fluffuh!?"
"Impressive, Subaru."

A girl's shriek, and a grey cat's praise.

Watching Beatrice's face flicker with agitation at having her "Door Crossing" broken through once more, Subaru, determined not to get blasted away this time, immediately tumbled into the library.

A handspring into a headspring. A cartwheel, then a round-off with a twist, a backflip, and finally a gorgeous back tuck to finish — landing right in front of the girl.

"Alright! A perfect ten!"

"You kicked up a huge cloud of dust, I suppose!"

"That's 'cause you don't clean your workplace properly! And anyway, you shouldn't be bringing a cat into a library in the first place! It'll scratch up the thick book covers!"

"My claws are trimmed real short by Lia, so it's fine~"

Beside Subaru and Beatrice as they hollered at each other, Puck murmured leisurely, but it didn't reach the two arguing.

The two of them kept trading shouts loud enough to echo through the whole manor.

Ram, arriving late at the door connecting to the Forbidden Library, watched their quarrel and murmured quietly,

"Whether they get along or not, it does seem to be true that they have good chemistry."

"'—No way that's true!!'"

Their synchronized shout shook Roswaal Manor's morning air to its core.
