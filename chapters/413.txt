Arc 6 Chapter 11: "A Tearful Voice Is Heard"

—All he saw was light, nothing more.

He remembered looking up, craning his neck.
And immediately after, part of the tower ahead flashed white, and he remembered narrowing his eyes against the glare.
But the memory ended there. He remembered nothing after that.

No pain, no impact, no fear—he felt none of it.
To Natsuki Subaru, those were always the inseparable companions of "death" arriving.
No pain like a scream given form, no impact of flesh tearing apart, no terror of everything being lost.

Perhaps that "death" had been gentler than any "death" Subaru had known before.
Though, having died with even his brain vaporized, Subaru had had no time at all to consider such a thing, nor the leisure to look back and dwell on it.

Like the blink of an eye, his vision closed to darkness for just an instant—and immediately after, Natsuki Subaru's lost "life" regenerated, flowed backward, and was cast out into reality once more.
—Cast out.

"—Tsk, tsk, tsk."

"—!"

For an instant, a breathless heaviness pressed down on all five senses, and Subaru's eyes flew open.
The sound of blood rushing through his whole body felt deafeningly loud, his contracting muscles screaming with pain. The reins gripped so tightly his nails dug into his palms, and Beatrice asserting her presence with the heat of her body.

"...Ah?"

In the dim field of vision, he was looking down at the back of Beatrice's head from close range.
The violently sweet scent slipping into his nostrils was different from the one that clung to the girl when he lifted her up. Beatrice's own scent was like sweet-baked pastry, but this one drifting all around was a viscous, poison-like sweetness, cloying and insistent.

He knew this sweetness. Of course he did. He'd been smelling nothing but it, endlessly, just moments ago.
If anything, it was strange that it had been interrupted for even a few seconds.

"Tsk, tsk, tsk, tsk."

Into Subaru's clouded consciousness came a faint clicking sound, tongue against teeth, riding a rhythm.
Ahead, Beatrice had gone rigid, and even Patrasche was holding her breath, watching the Dragon Carriage carrying Emilia and Rem—and at the nose of that carriage stood a hideous Witchbeast.

Its whole body laced with fine, root-like tendrils, a bloodthirsty, ferocious Witchbeast—an Oiran Bear.

In that instant, a vivid sense of reality that no mere déjà vu could describe came flooding back into Subaru.
There was no mistaking this feeling. Return by Death.

Natsuki Subaru had just experienced "death," and had returned to this moment in time.

"—!"

—But of all the moments, to be sent back to this one!?

He cursed not the events of the moment of "death," but the sheer bad timing of the Return by Death.
Clicking her tongue to draw the Witchbeast's interest, trying to peaceably lure it away from the front of the carriage, was Meili. Her gambit succeeded right up to the very last moment—and then failed.
Because Giant, the Earth Dragon pulling the carriage, couldn't bear the pressure of the Witchbeast any longer.

"——"

Even knowing this, Subaru hesitated, unable to decide in the moment.
He couldn't see Giant's condition from behind. But Julius, gripping the reins, had no way to notice the Earth Dragon's distress. Even he had no room to spare attention there.
Everyone aboard the carriage was praying for Meili's contact with the Oiran Bear to succeed.
But unfortunately, that—

"Tsk, tsk, tsk... tch—"

Meili's voice took on a faint edge of urgency, her finger pointing to the right side of the carriage. Drawn by the motion, the Oiran Bear shuffled its feet in that direction.
A sense of relief began to bloom on the carriage, and in Beatrice too. But Giant couldn't withstand the snapping of that thread of tension.

"Juli—"

"——!!"

Before he could make the right call, his cry was cut off by Giant's roar.
As if replaying the earlier scene, Giant let out a growl and stamped the ground, and the cry and the tremor jolted the entire pack of Oiran Bears awake at once.

Eyes already bloodshot bulging further, drooling as they leapt—the pale blue light pierced straight through one bear's head and blasted it apart, the flow of events identical down to the last detail.

"El Huma!!"

Standing atop the carriage's roof, Emilia swept both hands through the air, dancing as she wove a spiraling whirl of ice blades into the sky. Spinning blades of ice launched one after another toward the ground, and the pack of Oiran Bears was swallowed by the circular-saw-like ice weapons, limbs sliced away, death cries rising.

"Ha—run, run, run, run, run, run—!!"

Pulling the reins to spur Patrasche faster, Subaru shouted, and the Dragon Carriage lurched into motion.
Glancing sideways at the driver's seat, he saw Ram leap out to swap places with Julius as driver, and Julius, blade drawn, strike down an approaching Oiran Bear, scattering it.

—Completely identical.
Realizing he'd traced the same course exactly, Subaru clenched his back teeth hard, hard enough to ache.

The fact that his consciousness hadn't caught up with the sudden Return by Death was no excuse.
Setting aside the shock of his own death for later, Subaru's heart filled instead with regret.

To waste a Return by Death this thoroughly—this was the first time it had happened to Subaru since he'd become aware of the ability at all.
There had been times he'd mishandled information gained from a Return by Death and ended up dying again as a result. But never before had he, despite having Returned by Death, traced the same situation with no countermeasure at all—helpless, regardless of his own will to do otherwise, simply carried along the rails of fate.

"—Subaru! There's no time to space out!"

"—!"

Head bowed in frustration, gripping the reins tight, Subaru felt Beatrice's back slam into his chest. At the light impact and her voice he looked up, and saw the ferocious Witchbeasts bearing down.
At the same time, he took the small hand reaching toward him, set Beatrice up on the dragon's back, and the counterattack began.

"Minya! Minya! One more, Minya, I suppose!"

Through her palm, the aimless mana within Subaru's body was drawn out, converted through Beatrice into power. The purple crystals that formed pierced into the bodies of the Witchbeasts blocking their path, crystallizing and shattering them, and Patrasche pressed forward, trampling the shards underfoot.

"My last resort—Sand Earthworm!!"

Meili's near-desperate cry rang out, and sand erupted at the edge of his vision.
From beneath the sand plain covered in Oiran Bears, a Sand Earthworm reared its body up, its massive jaws swallowing several of the beasts whole, its towering bulk crushing the enemies together in one blow.

A giant monster showdown, round two—but Subaru also knew the odds weren't in their favor.

"—Barusu! Drive as if your life depends on it! Because it does, if you don't want to die!"

A voice sharp as a scolding cut into Subaru, burning with a shapeless urgency.
It was Ram, handling the reins from the driver's seat, controlling the excited Giant. Her handling of the reins was no less skilled than Rem's, keeping the Earth Dragon under control—but at this rate, things would go badly.

"We can't just keep going straight! Ram, change course!"

"—! What are you talking about? The watchtower is straight ahead, and all around us is Witchbeast territory!"

"That's true, but it won't work like this!"

Patrasche lowered her head and rammed upward into the torso of an Oiran Bear blocking the way. With the writhing beast balanced on her snout, the jet-black Earth Dragon charged forward using its body as a shield. Claw after claw struck down, tearing the beast's flesh apart, scattering petals and fresh blood across the sand sea.

"Barusu, what did you notice! Explain clearly!"

"If I could explain clearly, I wouldn't be in this mess! Just—change course, now!"

Against Ram's irritated words, Subaru had no choice but to shout back just as roughly. He knew it was an unreasonable way to respond, but he had no other means.
Because right now, Subaru didn't even know the cause of his own death.

There was no doubt he'd Returned by Death—but he couldn't recall the reason for his death.
As for having Returned by Death without noticing it happen, this time felt closest to the sensation of the very first time he'd met "death" at Roswaal Manor.
First, he had to confirm the cause of death, and eliminate that problem.

"There's no time for that, none at all!!"

Had the Witchbeast caught up to him and made him prey for its claws? Had his guts been slurped out, just as the tavern keeper had warned, turning him into some pitiful meal? Or had he simply fallen from Patrasche's back and, out of sheer bad luck, broken his neck? It seemed truly hard to believe, but there was even a chance he'd died of exhaustion from Beatrice drawing too much mana from him.

"Whatever it was, my memory's never cut off this cleanly, this completely, before."

Gutted open, frozen solid, his skull crushed by an iron ball, devoured whole by rabbits, caught in the blast radius of some incomprehensible Authority—Subaru had Returned by Death through all of it.
And whatever the cause of death, he had always been able to trace back the events just before "death," sift through his own hopeless situation, and find some means to break through it.

That thin thread of fate was nowhere to be found this time.
And they had no time to spare searching for a thread that couldn't be found.

—This was, in its quiet way, the worst kind of Return by Death sealing he'd ever faced.

If he died again by the same method, he'd be starting over completely blind.
Before that happened—

"We move first! Just don't get near the tower!"

"So why is that—"

"The tower lights up! That light is probably the dangerous part!"

"Hah—!?"

In such a desperate situation, even Ram had no time to coat her words in venom before flinging them back. Amid her blunt, disbelieving retort, Subaru pulled the reins and conveyed his intent to Patrasche.
Not straight ahead—but to turn, no matter what.

"The tower's right there ahead of us, but...!"

In the middle of the sand sea, with the Oiran Bears advancing en masse, the watchtower straight ahead was unmistakably drawing nearer. But if they got close to it, it really would be a rerun of what had just happened.
To avoid that, what Subaru had to choose was—

"We're reversing! Better to pass through the Sand Time once more! Back to the last dune!"

"What nonsense are you spouting all of a sudden—"

"Ram! Do as Subaru says!"

At a decision that could only look like madness, Emilia stopped Ram before she could voice her objection. Sweeping away the Witchbeasts with a hail of ice shards, she nodded toward Subaru.

"There's no way Subaru would say something this strange with no thought behind it!"

"Barusu lives his life spouting nonsense and idiocy on a daily basis."

"There's no way Subaru would say something this strange with no thought behind it when he's in danger!"

"Thank you so much for the clarification!!"

Whether to lament being treated like the boy who cried wolf, or to celebrate being treated as a man to be relied on in a pinch—he set aside both the self-reflection and the vanity for later, and Patrasche, following Subaru's will, planted her feet in the sand and spun into a sharp turn. Her wind-cutting tail swept an Oiran Bear's body aside, her kicking legs violently struck down another, and she began her reverse charge.

"—! You two, up top and on the side! Make sure you're not thrown off!!"

Watching Subaru and the others make their sharp turn, Ram too used her skillful handling of the reins to wheel Giant around. Naturally, the carriage, subjected to enormous centrifugal force, tilted hard, nearly tipping over—but,

"Emilia-sama! The footing!"

"Eh? Ah, right! Okay!"

Julius, bracing himself by planting his sword into the carriage frame, called out to Emilia for support against the tilting carriage. At his words Emilia's eyes went wide, and instantly her magic surged directly beneath the carriage. A slope of ice formed to support the wheels tilting at an angle, and the carriage swung in a half-circle, narrowly avoiding a rollover.
Without losing momentum, it continued straight into a reverse pursuit, chasing after Patrasche.

—It was right after that.

"Wha—!?"

The instant they began their reverse charge, a sharp *keen* sound rang out, scraping at their eardrums.
Subaru instinctively hunched his shoulders. Behind him, wind suddenly stirred.

"What was that just—wait, oi oi oi oi!?"

"What was—eep!?"

At the mysterious sensation rising up behind them, Subaru and Beatrice both spun around in shock at the same moment.
On the carriage running right behind them, Emilia and Julius too had their eyes bulging wide.

"The Sand Earthworm-chan is...!"

Leaning out from the driver's seat, her throat trembling in shock, it was Meili.
Right before her eyes, behind the carriage, the massive body of the Sand Earthworm had reared up, covering the space behind them. Over ten meters long, its torso thick enough that two grown men linking arms couldn't have circled it. Head thrust up out of the ground, glaring down at its prey, it looked every bit like the king of the sand sea.

And that Sand Earthworm's body had just been blown to splinters by a direct hit from something.
Its slick hide torn apart, its thick, immense torso cleanly split in two. The headless body toppled sideways, and the head, having lost its support, began falling—slowly, slowly—toward them.

"Dodge sideways, nooowww—!!"

The falling head of the Sand Earthworm carried more than enough mass to crush the carriage flat.
With the death cry of a Witchbeast crushed beneath the torso ringing out behind them, Subaru and Ram each relayed instructions to their respective Earth Dragons, forcibly veering off course to escape the head's direct trajectory.

"——"

Letting out a shrill cry, the Sand Earthworm's head slammed down into the sand sea with tremendous force.
Sand billowed up, the bones of Oiran Bears that failed to flee shattered, and limbs went flying, caught in the impact. The head bounced, rolling onward and dragging in more Witchbeasts along its path, before finally being reduced to shredded meat under a barrage of countless claws.

"Ab—ah, that was, that was close!"

"Subaru, we've messed up, I suppose!"

Just as he was breathing a sigh of relief at their narrow escape, Beatrice immediately shot that relief down.
Wondering what was wrong, he looked at the girl in his arms—she was brushing away drifting petals with irritation, scanning behind them, and said,

"With that, we've gotten separated from the other carriage, I suppose! It's just us here, Betty and the rest, I suppose!"

"What!?"

Startled, Subaru followed her gaze and confirmed the carriage was nowhere in sight around them.
But in the distance, he could make out the pale blue glow of mana and the roar of Witchbeasts, as well as a smaller eruption of sand—signs, from afar, of Emilia's continued fight and Meili summoning a new servant.

But to try to rejoin them now, the gap between was filled with countless Oiran Bears. And against Subaru's group, now reduced in strength, the Witchbeasts' onslaught showed no sign of weakening.

"Simply put, our fighting power's halved! And the enemy's numbers are doubled!"

"Four times worse than before, I suppose!"

Claws raised, a lunging Oiran Bear took the crack of a whip straight to the face.
The blow luckily struck true, crushing the beast's eye, and using the one thrashing in agonized pain as a breach, Patrasche charged forward once more. But this direction was different from the one toward Emilia's carriage.

"Patrasche! This isn't the way—"

"This is the Earth Dragon's own judgment for survival, I suppose. Better to trust this one's instincts than Subaru's or Betty's, I suppose!"

Cutting off Subaru's cry, Beatrice scattered an Oiran Bear blocking their path with magic. Patrasche broke through a thin spot in the encirclement, doing everything she could to protect the two on her back.

"Damn it! Again this... this same shape again! What the hell am I even doing!"

Cracking his whip, he knocked aside the face of an approaching beast. But that alone couldn't stop the momentum of a creature lunging at him with all reason gone from its expression.
Deal a fatal blow with the sword, sap its ability to act with magic—the power Subaru had chosen fell far short of either. His cleverness was being crushed flat under overwhelming force.

He'd done what he could, increased what he was capable of, and thought things had gotten a little better.
Was fate simply going to sneer at Natsuki Subaru's petty efforts and grind them underfoot?

"That bastard Regulus could handle this a hell of a lot better than I can...!"

"There's no time to be lost in thought, I suppose! Some way, we need some way..."

"Bea-ko, cast Muraku on Patrasche! Lighten her weight, get her over the pack's heads!"

"—! Muraku!"

At Subaru's instruction, Beatrice cast Muraku—the magic that lessened the effect of gravity—on Patrasche, and her running speed accelerated. Each stride now covered double the distance, and Patrasche, leaping, attempted to break free of the field of writhing beasts serving as her footholds.
But—

"Light—!?"

A flash of white light bloomed at the edge of his vision, and the instant his voice cracked, "it" came streaking across the sky.
White light tore across the ground, the sand sea blasted away with terrific force. As the light charged forward, Witchbeast bodies were torn apart, pillars of blood rose, flew, flew, flew.
And the shockwave of it lashed out toward Patrasche as well—the Earth Dragon's massive body, caught in the wind pressure of the approaching "it," was sent flying, tossed as easily as a leaf.

"Beatrice! Hold on tight!!"

"You'd better be holding onto me properly too, I suppose!!"

"——!"

Patrasche whinnied, and Subaru pulled Beatrice's whole body into his arms, clinging on.
Spinning through the air in a corkscrew, Patrasche's massive body bounded over the heads of the Oiran Bears. And chasing after Patrasche came a continuous rain of white light.
Sand exploded, Witchbeasts caught in the blast exploded, and Patrasche, grazed, let out a cry of pain.

"Uwaaaaaah—!?"

In the midst of the attack, the Divine Protection of Wind Evasion slipped off, and Subaru took the full brunt of the impact.
Gripping the reins and Beatrice, wedging both feet firmly into Patrasche's saddle, he was battered by the force, his body slammed against everything, consciousness nearly torn away.
And yet even in that state, the claws of the Oiran Bears below kept coming, pressing their follow-up attack.

Swallowed by the tremendous impact, he fought desperately not to be thrown off. If he fell now, there would be no surviving it. That he still clung to life at all was only because he refused to give up.

"But at this rate—"

"This is bad, I suppose...!"

"—Patrasche!?"

An especially violent impact struck, and Patrasche's pained cry trailed high into the air.
Looking down, he saw Patrasche's flank—sent flying so easily thanks to Muraku's effect—had finally been torn open by an Oiran Bear's claws. Ugly claw marks had been carved into her jet-black scales, and fresh blood spilled from the deep wound.

"—Gah!?"

Carried by that momentum, tumbling, Patrasche finally collapsed onto the sandy plain. At the same moment, Subaru and Beatrice were thrown to the ground, left stranded amid the feeding ground of surrounding Oiran Bears.
He shook his head, forced his body up. His hand was still linked with Beatrice's. She too brushed off the hem of her dress and stood, eyes sweeping the area without letting her guard down. But there was no denying the disadvantage they faced.

"You bastards! Don't you dare lay a single finger on Patrasche!"

"Minya!"

Magic pierced into the Witchbeasts swarming toward the wounded, groaning Patrasche. Subaru pulled Beatrice by the hand and rushed to Patrasche's side, kneeling to touch her wound.
The claws hadn't reached her internal organs, but if she were forced to strain, the pressure in her belly might rupture her intestines. She couldn't be pushed any further. She needed time to be treated.

"Beatrice! Shamak! Confuse the Witchbeasts around us so they can't perceive us!"

"Now!? That'll only buy us a little time, I suppose! And besides..."

"Right now, that time is what we need! Hurry!"

Bloodshot eyes, bodies leaning forward, the Witchbeasts were watching for their chance to pounce.
If attacked from all sides at once, there'd be no way to cope. Which beast would be the first to break and trigger the rest depended entirely on their patience. And that, most likely, couldn't be counted on.

"—! El Shamak!"

Beatrice pressed her hands together, and something was drawn wholesale out of Subaru from within.
It spiraled in the girl's hands beside him and became a quiet force, sweeping across the sand sea—a black mist enveloping a radius of over a dozen meters, centered on the two of them.

The foundation of shadow magic, the great and righteous Shamak, activated.

Shamak's effect, born of a discrepancy in perception, spread outward, and the Oiran Bears' movements ground completely to a halt. Their thoughts vanished beyond comprehension, even the instinct to hunt down prey forgotten.
Naturally, the effect didn't touch Subaru and Beatrice, its casters. But it wouldn't last long either.

"If anyone from outside the effect's range wanders in, it'll be disrupted eventually, I suppose. Before that happens, we need to get that Earth Dragon and escape, I suppose!"

"I know! Muraku's effect should still be active, right? Then I'll carry Patrasche, and we'll just keep casting Shamak over and over to rejoin the other carriage—"

Judging that escaping the killing field had to take priority, he reached out toward Patrasche.
Normally her massive body weighed hundreds of kilograms, but while Beatrice's Muraku was still in effect, her body was—not quite light as a feather, that would be an exaggeration, but light. Light enough that carrying her while fleeing wouldn't be much trouble.
Her yellow eyes stared fixedly at Subaru. The emotion passing through them was complex, eloquent to a degree that hardly seemed like something an Earth Dragon should be able to express.

Those eyes were telling him something. —Leave me, they said.

"—Subaru!"

The instant his gaze was caught by the light in Patrasche's eyes, Beatrice's voice rang out sharply.
Rushing to his side, she thrust her palm toward Subaru, and a surge of magic power formed a shield—a purple magical wall appearing right beside him—that deflected the white light being fired at him.

"Gah!?"

A sharp sound like clashing steel rang out, and the impact sent Subaru's body flying sideways. He rolled, landing spread-eagled on the sand, and coughing, he tried to raise himself up when he noticed—
Blood dripping from his right side where he'd taken the impact, soaking his right leg through.

"What did I just... take...?"

Pressing a hand to his bleeding side, one knee on the ground, Subaru let out a stunned breath.
An instant later, the shock of his insides being churned rose up as nausea, stomach acid and blood gushing from the corner of his mouth all at once.
Air escaped from his torn belly, and along with a froth of blood came a pathetic sound like a fart.

"Ah, hh..."

"—baru! Subaru!"

The rasping breath and his vision tilting sideways happened at the same moment.
He understood he'd toppled over, unable to move. He understood that much, but nothing further would follow. The wound in his side burned with heat, and there was a pain like his body was melting away from that point.
Desperately, he heard a voice calling his name.

"Subaru! Subaru, don't—I suppose! Don't die... don't, don't die... don't leave me alone... please... stop, please..."

His shoulders were shaken. A tearful voice—he wanted so badly to reach out a hand somehow, but he couldn't move.
As if his brain had been scorched, he found he couldn't remember who the girl in front of him was.
Her face was lovely, but crying—and he thought, she shouldn't be made to cry like that.

"——"

Beyond her, a great, enormous lizard lay fallen.
A black lizard, beautiful in form. Several long, slender white things were driven into its body, and utterly motionless, it was clearly dead.

Probably, he thought, I took a hit from the same thing too.

"Don't leave Betty behind, please...!"

Sobbing, the girl desperately pulled Subaru's body close to her.
For her small frame, Subaru's limp, strengthless body was far too heavy. And still, she held on with all her might.

Tears traced down her cheek. He wanted, at least, to wipe those tears away.
He searched inside his body for something that would move, but couldn't find it. So instead, he reached for something that could move from somewhere else entirely—not from within his body.

"—Hh."

"Subaru?"

An invisible "hand," visible only to himself, wiped the tears from the girl's cheek.
Black fingertips traced the falling drops, and the girl, her face registering that she'd noticed something, looked toward him. He had no strength left even to smile reassuringly at her.

"Suba—"

For an instant, the girl began to say something.
But it was cut off by white light streaking in from far behind her.

"——"

A second impact drove itself into Subaru's chest.
Slowly lowering his gaze, he saw it had pierced clean through the back of the girl embracing him, and gone on to pierce through Subaru's own chest, out the other side.

"Ah."

Leaving behind a rasping breath, the girl embracing Subaru vanished.
As though she had never belonged to this world at all, her form disappeared from sight.

Losing his support, Subaru collapsed where he lay. He couldn't move. There was no reason left to move.

"Kh... hh."

Only his fingers twitched faintly, scraping at the sand—that alone.

Having lost its caster, the black mist that had separated them from the space around them was scattered by the wind.
With the power that had confused perception laid bare, what remained were the corpse of an Earth Dragon and a dying boy. The pack of Witchbeasts licked their lips and closed in on their prey.

"——"

His breath stopped, his vision losing focus.
A sensation unlike death by blood loss. Something fatal, being poured into him.

Everything faded into incomprehension.
Close beside him, he heard the growl of an Oiran Bear raising its claws.

Would that claw come down and shatter his skull?
Or was the cause the faint glint of "that thing," flickering at the edge of his vanishing vision?

He never learned which was the reason—but Natsuki Subaru's life had come to its end.
