Arc 6 Chapter 90: "Hero"

—Holding the small scorpion that had emerged from the dust on his palm, Subaru's shoulders shook quietly.

He'd wanted to save her.
He'd wanted to take her out of this lonely tower of sand.

He'd promised he would help her.
And yet, he hadn't been able to keep that promise.

Natsuki Subaru always made promises he couldn't keep.

"——"

Crouched on the sand, no one could bring themselves to speak to the silent Subaru.
Beatrice at his side, Julius and Meili behind him — none of them could find the words.
Only the scorpion on his hand, as if to comfort Subaru who had cried until even his sobs had left him, crept up his arm, climbed his shoulder, and pressed its body against his neck.

Subaru had no idea what this small scorpion actually was.
The 'Crimson Scorpion's' enormous body had crumbled to dust, and out of that dust this scorpion had emerged — but what relation did it bear to Shaula? Or was this scorpion Shaula herself? He wondered, but—

"—No. It isn't."

There had been words exchanged between Subaru and Shaula, right at the very end.
Whether that exchange had truly passed between them, or whether it had been a daydream Subaru saw at the moment of parting, he couldn't be sure.
But he held the sorrowful conviction that it had, in truth, happened.

Shaula was lost.
Her existence had been bound to the very rules of the Pleiades Watchtower itself — ultimately, like Reid, like the Monolith on the third floor, nothing more than a stage device to advance the 'Trial.'
And so, once the Watchtower's role had come to an end, she too had to make her exit.
To the very last, she had martyred herself to the role of that 'stage device.'

And yet she could not have been merely a 'stage device' — she had left a hole gaping open in Subaru's chest that proved as much.

"——"

That unguarded smile, that irritating clinginess for physical contact, that voice calling him Oshi-sama, Oshi-sama with an affection he'd never earned — all of it, lost.
If Shaula had wailed and screamed that she didn't want to disappear, Subaru would have searched with everything he had for a way to save her, would have thrown his life away again and again to go rescue her.

But she hadn't wanted that.
Smiling, she had left him with only one wish — that they meet again — and vanished.
He didn't know how. He didn't even know if it was truly possible.
He didn't know — but—

"Got it. ...Someday, I swear, I'll meet you again. So—"

He would fulfill the wish of the girl who had told him, with that radiant smile, that she loved him.
For that—

"—So for now, goodbye, Shaula."

The sand-laden wind carried away the remnants of her feelings, turned to red dust.
Watching it go, Subaru let out a breath. Looking down, he saw the scorpion nestled against his neck pinching his ear with its claw, as if telling him to buck up.

"Ow!"

The sharp pain felt like a shove at his back, telling him not to wallow.
Eyes watering from the pain, Subaru nodded, muttering "okay, okay," and grabbed the scorpion at his neck, trying to pry it off his ear.
But—

"Ow! No, I said I got it, you can let go now— ow! Hey, this is — my ear's bleeding — you little— you're actually serious about this...!"

"...Honestly, Onii-san, what are you even doing~"

With the small scorpion clamped onto his ear and refusing to budge, Subaru began to feel genuine alarm.
Just as his ear's soft cartilage was about to be seriously mangled, Meili came to the rescue.
Looking thoroughly exasperated, she deftly took over from Subaru, catching the little crimson scorpion in her small hands.

"Even if it's tiny, it's still a witchbeast, y'know~. If you carelessly get it near your face, don't blame me if it eats your eye or your nose or something~"

"My eye or nose!? But it wouldn't actually—"

"It's a witchbeast, Onii-san. — Not that naked lady, y'know~"

"——"

Saying that, Meili plopped the little crimson scorpion onto her own head.
Unlike the violence it had just inflicted on Subaru, the little scorpion caused no mischief atop Meili's head. Falling under the influence of her Divine Protection of Demon Manipulation, it seemed to have tucked its ferocity away.
Without her needing to say it, that was proof the little crimson scorpion was a witchbeast Meili's Divine Protection could reach — and proof, too, that it had no ego of its own as Shaula.

"Subaru, you need to have your wound treated, I suppose."

Tugging on Subaru's sleeve as he began to hang his head again, Beatrice gently fretted over him.
Biting his lip at her consideration, Subaru nodded deeply. They couldn't stay in the sand sea forever.

"—Subaru! Everyone!"

Far off, Emilia threw open the great doors at the Watchtower's entrance and came running toward them.
Her appearance too showed signs of having been caught up in considerable turmoil — the path had clearly not been an easy one for her either. That, too, was something they'd all need to talk about, together.

There was too much that needed saying, too many farewells that needed making.


△▼△▼△▼△


"...Shaula was a reeeally hard worker, wasn't she."

Hearing the full account of what had happened, that Shaula could no longer be found anywhere, Emilia gazed out over the sand sea where even the scattered dust had blown away, and mourned the loss in her own way.
Gently, her white finger reached out to touch the little crimson scorpion atop Meili's head. Though it had been ordered by Meili not to act up, the way it accepted Emilia's touch — even without any visible expression or gesture — somehow looked as though it felt good.

"Emilia-sama, how did things go on the first floor? You completed the 'Trial' safely, I take it?"

Having finished the explanation about Shaula, Emilia turned a worried gaze on Subaru's downcast face.
But it was Julius who deliberately chose to move the conversation forward. At his prompting, Emilia nodded, "Yes," and said,

"It was really confusing and hard, but somehow I managed to finish it. ...And, Julius, do you properly remember me?"

"—. Yes. I do. I can recall you clearly indeed."

At Emilia's hesitant question, Julius answered with a startled expression.
Placing a hand to his own mouth, Julius repeated "Emilia-sama" to himself once more, confirming that the memory of her presence — which should have vanished within him — was there, and nodded.

"Beatrice! Do you remember me? What about Meili?"

"...You needn't worry, we've remembered, I suppose. If anything, I'd half-forgotten I'd forgotten until you mentioned it. It's a rather eerie feeling, I suppose."

"I remember you juuust fine too~. What about you, Onee-san — do you properly remember me, and the promise we made~?"

"Of course. I'd never forget something like that. I'm so glad. Ram and Patrasche-chan remembered too, so I figured it was probably going to be okay, but..."

Hearing Beatrice's and Meili's answers, Emilia let out a relieved breath, pressing a hand to her chest.
Seeing their reactions, Subaru called out, "Wait."
Drawing everyone's attention to himself, he cleared his throat—

"That's an incredible piece of good news, but I want to get this straight. So — you're saying everyone properly remembers Emilia-tan? That means, um..."

"—It means Ram-joshi struck down Lye Batenkaitos."

"——"

Subaru's eyes went wide at the conclusion Julius offered, stated so plainly.
Lye Batenkaitos — one of the three Sin Archbishops of 'Gluttony,' and, for Subaru and Ram, perhaps the most bitterly personal enemy of all.
In this tower too, he had devoured Emilia's 'Name,' and had gone on his own rampage besides, forcing them all to swallow bitter defeat again and again.
Hearing that he had been felled, Subaru's throat grew painfully dry.
Because if Lye Batenkaitos had fallen, that meant—

"...The stuff that guy stole — it's coming back? If that's true, then—"

If Emilia's 'Name' had returned, then the other effects of 'Gluttony's' Authority should be unraveling too.
Playing at being a 'gourmet,' Batenkaitos had gone on stealing far, far too much. If everything he had stolen was being released — then the people of the floodgate city of Priestella, too—

"——"

Thinking that far, Subaru shook his head.
No more deception. No more sealing away his own heart, no more trying to lie to himself.
In this moment, Natsuki Subaru harbored an intensely selfish, egoistic hope.
If Lye Batenkaitos had been struck down, and his Authority's effects were unraveling—

"—Did she remember Rem? Did you all remember Rem?"

The girl forgotten by everyone, whose sheer absence had blown a great hole through Subaru's chest.
This journey had been a journey to reclaim what had been stolen — and for Subaru, it had been, in its entirety, a journey to save Rem.
That the search for the knowledge to do so had brought the ironic twist of 'Gluttony' descending upon the Watchtower didn't matter, if the outcome it delivered turned out to be the same.

Driven by that impatient hope, Subaru swept his gaze across everyone's faces.
But to Subaru's question—

"...I'm sorry, Subaru. I still haven't remembered Rem."

"—! Why not!"

"Betty hasn't either, I suppose. Ram's little sister — I still can't remember her. And besides..."

"Besides what? Besides — what? What else is there?"

Denied by Emilia, and then by Beatrice as well, Subaru's eyes went wide.
His newly-sprouted hope rejected, Subaru pressed in on Beatrice as she trailed off. At his desperate look, Beatrice gestured with her chin toward the rear — toward Julius.

"I still haven't remembered Julius either, I suppose. It seems not all of the damage from 'Gluttony' has been undone."

"Julius's..."

At Beatrice's words, Emilia nodded along in the same way.
Meili, who hadn't known Julius from before his 'Name' was stolen to begin with, merely shrugged, but there was no reason for Emilia and the others to lie.
So then — neither Rem's nor Julius's 'Name' had returned—

"On that point, I have some idea as to why my own 'Name' hasn't returned."

Amid Subaru and the others' confusion, it was Julius himself who spoke up.
His narrow eyes met theirs, and he continued.
It was this—

"—The Sin Archbishop of 'Gluttony,' Roy Alphard, has been taken alive. Strictly speaking, my 'Name' is something that was stolen by him. That, I believe, is why it hasn't returned yet."


△▼△▼△▼△


Passing through the great front gate and back into the interior of the Pleiades Watchtower, they found the dragon carriage that had carried them to the sand tower parked on the fifth floor, and beside it, a waving figure came to greet them.

"Emilia-san, and Natsuki-kun too — my, it's been quite a while, hasn't it."

"...Don't tell me — Anastasia-san?"

Seeing the person smiling gently and waving with an easy motion, Subaru's eyes went rigid and wide.
He confirmed it in her gestures, her bearing, right down to her expression — all of it natural, unforced. This wasn't Echidna, the artificial spirit, wearing her form and unable to fully trace her humanity — no, this wasn't a reproduction at all.
It was the original person's own nature. You wouldn't call that a reproduction.
In other words—

"Anastasia-san! You woke up?"

"Yep. Seems I nodded off for quite a long while there — sorry to worry you all. As for what's happened these past few months, I had Echidna fill me in."

"Echidna's okay too, then."

"More or less. Aside from self-reproach, it seems there's nothing much that's going to kill me at the moment."

As Anastasia answered Emilia's question, the fur stole around her neck moved — a white fox drooping its head apologetically.
Stroking Echidna's head, Anastasia scolded her with a "now, now," and said,

"There you go blaming yourself again. I told you, didn't I? This was my own choice, so there's no need for Echidna to mope over it. You think so too, don't you, Julius?"

"Me? ...Well. Honestly, Anastasia-sama's decision had me thoroughly on edge, so it's difficult for me to simply agree that you're right."

"Difficult, but what?"

"That I could hear the reasons you shut yourself away — that alone makes me feel a knight's calling was worth it."

Lips curving, Julius answered with elegant poise. Hearing it, Anastasia laughed, hand to her mouth, "My, listen to you."
It was a remarkably well-matched exchange. Master and retainer through and through, one might say, but—

"Anastasia-san, you really don't remember Julius, right? Because for someone who doesn't, you two seem awfully in sync..."

"Forgetting what kind of relationship we originally had — that alone is enough to have me churning in my gut with frustration I can barely stand...! But I can bear it, but...!"

"She grits her teeth and bears it — that's the stance we've settled on. Fortunately, I can speak for the Julius I've come to know these past two months myself. Seems that's what I was born for, if anything."

"...You've swung off in an odd direction yourself, it seems."

Watching Anastasia's lips trembling, Beatrice offered a gently teasing word to Echidna, who had defined herself at Anastasia's throat.
Hearing that, Echidna gave a small fox-snort and nodded, "Ah."

Echidna, the artificial spirit without a past, who had come to the Watchtower in Anastasia's stead — she too seemed to have reached some manner of forward-looking conclusion after much reflection.
That Anastasia had safely reclaimed her own body was, no doubt, part and parcel of that.

The regret over having whittled away Anastasia's Od to keep acting, the problem she'd carried since birth and all that entailed — none of that had been resolved. But—

"That's not something for us to go poking our noses into, huh."

The matter between Anastasia and Echidna was theirs to talk through.
Of course, if asked for help, he'd worry alongside them, lend a hand toward solving it. That much of a bond, he could believe these two months of the sand-crossing journey had nurtured.

"—Say, Anastasia-san. I'm reeeally glad you came back safe, and there's a lot I want to talk about, but..."

"I know, I know. You mean the Sin Archbishop Julius took down, right? Julius did whatever needed doing and stuffed him in the dragon carriage, that's where he's been left."

"Julius did whatever needed doing..."

Shrugging, Anastasia indicated the dragon carriage behind her.
Beside Emilia, who echoed the words back to herself, Subaru held his breath and looked at the carriage. Inside it, the Sin Archbishop of 'Gluttony,' Roy Alphard, was held captive.

"——"

Before circling behind the carriage, Subaru stroked the neck of the earth dragon tethered there — Jaian.
The Agaress-breed earth dragon with its four thick legs had been, in the final battle against Shaula, the unlikely hero who'd staged Subaru and the others' victory.
The best possible outcome hadn't been achieved. But that didn't make Jaian's consideration meaningless. If anything, Meili's survival was the fruit of Jaian's resolve.

"You saved us. ...If anything happens after this, help us out again."

"——"

Having his thick neck stroked, Jaian let out a breath through his nose as if to say the burden was too heavy.
Wryly smiling at that just slightly, Subaru quickly firmed his expression again and moved toward the carriage's cabin. Then, exchanging a nod with Emilia and the others, he peered inside.
There, the Sin Archbishop of 'Gluttony'—

"—Huh, this is..."

Peering into the carriage with tension coursing through him, Subaru found himself let down by what he saw inside.
Inside the carriage, Alphard was indeed there. However, the manner of his restraint was quite different from what Subaru had imagined.
Roy Alphard's entire body was encased in something like black crystal, his eyes rolled white — restrained, no, sealed.

"The opponent is a Sin Archbishop... simply immobilizing him wouldn't be enough. So I hardened him down as thoroughly as possible."

"Hardened... what's the principle behind this, exactly?"

"In principle, it's an application of Shadow Magic, I suppose. Separate the target's consciousness with Shamak, and harden them in place, I suppose. ...This is a rather nasty method, I suppose."

"So this black stuff is basically a lump of Shamak..."

Hearing Beatrice's explanation, Subaru looked again at the seal, startled anew.
Shamak was, before Subaru had contracted with Beatrice, a magic he'd relied on more than almost anything else in this world, no exaggeration — but the effect it produced, and the way Julius had applied that effect to devise this seal, left him with nothing to do but marvel.
Seeing Subaru's reaction, Julius prefaced with "I'd rather you not misunderstand," and said,

"This is not some technique I invented myself. The most famous seal in the world employs this same formula. Though the scale is, of course, incomparable."

"The most famous in the world... don't tell me you mean—"

"—The 'Witch of Envy,' isn't it."

Emilia, who had been observing the hardened Alphard, spoke, and Julius nodded deeply, "Yes," in confirmation.
Alphard's seal — the same method used on the four-hundred-year slumber of the 'Witch of Envy,' sealed far to the east of this Watchtower, in that land.
In other words, a method with a proven track record of trust — and with Julius the one who had applied the seal, its reliability was all the higher for it. But that wasn't where the problem lay.

"The problem is why we're keeping this guy alive at all."

"Subaru..."

Gazing at the sealed Alphard, Subaru said this, and Emilia's brow drooped with concern.
His chest aching at her gaze, Subaru turned back with, "Well, isn't it obvious?"

"'Gluttony's' other half... Batenkaitos died, and everyone remembered Emilia-tan. So if the rest of the 'memories' just need this guy to die too—"

"There's no guarantee they'll come back. That was the single biggest reason I didn't put him to death myself."

"——"

"There's no doubt Ram-joshi struck Batenkaitos down. But is it truly certain that Emilia-sama's 'Name' was freed simply, purely, by killing him? The possibility that something else — some active act needed to force it back from him — that can't be ruled out. Act too hastily, and we risk losing everything."

Julius's words, offered as if to check Subaru's impatience, were correct point by point.
Correct enough that Subaru had nothing to say against them.
But—

"Then... then what about the 'Book of the Dead'?"

The alternative Subaru put forward was a method that existed only within this Watchtower.
As a means of exposing another's thoughts, surely nothing was less concealing than the 'Book of the Dead.' After all, the thing let you relive the entirety of a person's life.

"Even if we interrogated him, there's no guarantee he'd tell the truth. So if we used the 'Book of the Dead' to lay his true heart bare instead..."

"Subaru, that's... I don't think that's good. That method..."

"But it'd be certain. This way..."

"Hold on — mind if I chip in?"

At Subaru's proposal to use the 'Book of the Dead,' Emilia showed reluctance. Just as Subaru was about to press the point, Anastasia raised a hand.
Looking at Subaru's sour expression, she pressed her white hands together before her chest.

"This is stuff I heard from Echidna too, so there might be gaps here and there, but... that 'Book of the Dead,' was it? Isn't it dangerous to put too much faith in it?"

"Dangerous how?"

"How, she asks — wasn't that something Natsuki-kun himself experienced firsthand, more than anyone? While I was asleep, I heard Natsuki-kun himself nearly lost who he was, didn't he?"

"——"

In however short a time she'd managed to talk with Echidna, Anastasia struck right at a sore spot.
However, Subaru's memory loss and the danger of the 'Book of the Dead' weren't, strictly speaking, the same thing. Subaru's memories hadn't vanished because of the 'Book of the Dead's' influence.
But that didn't mean the 'Book of the Dead's' danger vanished entirely either.

In fact, Subaru had, through reading Meili's 'Book of the Dead,' come close to having part of his thoughts and feelings painted over by her personality.
Subaru didn't think of himself as having a particularly strong mind, or a particularly strong sense of self, but even so, he couldn't say what would happen to someone more easily influenced.
And there was no guarantee that the result wouldn't be a new Roy Alphard born from inheriting the mind of the old Roy Alphard.

"Then... then are you saying it's right to keep this guy alive too, Anastasia-san? After everything this guy's done!"

"If we're talking about right and wrong, I don't think it's right to keep a Sin Archbishop alive either. If killing this man brought back Julius's 'memory' inside me, I'd think that was fine too. But I've got my own principle on this."

"Your own principle...?"

"Taking a life, fighting over who lives and who dies — that's the last resort. A person who lets people die easily doesn't come to a decent end. That's not something out of Hoshin's sayings — that's my own words."

At Anastasia's words, Subaru's eyes went wide.
A pushback rose in him, in this fantasy world of swords and magic, at what naive nonsense that was. But at the same time, the ethics within Subaru sensed that it was right, too.

Subaru too believed, of course, that fewer deaths were better.
For allies, of course, but for enemies too — fewer deaths were better.
But that logic only held so long as the other side deserved that mercy.

"He's made countless people miserable, and he's tormenting people right now, even as we speak. ...Even a guy like that, you're saying not to kill him?"

"When it's necessary to kill, I'll kill. I'll make that call, and if it comes down to it, I'll get my hands dirty too. But letting yourself be ruled by impulse — that's different. I think Natsuki-kun belongs on this side too."

"That's not..."

"That's why you can still cry tears for someone who's gone. ...I'd rather have a long relationship with that Natsuki-kun than with a cold, merciless one with no blood and no tears."

Anastasia traced a finger along her own cheek, pointing out the trace of tears on Subaru's face.
In that instant, the wound of losing Shaula throbbed, and Subaru quietly hung his head. Anastasia's way of putting it was underhanded. But undeniably effective.

"...Subaru, my opinion's the same as Anastasia-san's. Thinking about Julius, and Rem still sleeping, I want to solve this fast too, but..."

"At the very least, please don't concern yourself over me. Given where things stand, we should prioritize certainty over haste. ...My brother's fate hangs on this too."

Joshua, left behind in Priestella, whose memory teetered on the brink of returning — Julius's opinion was the very picture of caution — but as noted, it was the correct one.
Wanting to fill the pain of loss, demanding results too hastily — that was, no doubt, exactly the state of Subaru's heart right now.

"Let's sum up. Ana and Julius's position is: transport Roy Alphard — the Sin Archbishop of 'Gluttony' — to the royal capital, and draw out from him a way to save the victims of his Authority. After that, there'll be no clemency. Execution is inevitable."

"There's a limit to how much leniency his age can buy him. In the end, that's how it'll go, I imagine."

Watching Subaru relax the fists he'd clenched, Echidna and Anastasia laid the matter out plainly.
Emilia had no objection to Alphard's fate either. His body would be transported to the royal capital in its sealed state, in a sense placing him in the same position as Sirius, taken ahead of them.

"Emilia-san and the others, that all right with you too?"

"Yes. I want to properly remember the people I've forgotten too."

At Anastasia's confirmation, Emilia lifted her face and answered without hesitation.
Her words, allowing no argument, left Subaru feeling both reassured and something bitter at once, and he turned his eyes away from the Sin Archbishop in the carriage — and there, unexpectedly, dropped to one knee.

"Huh..."

"Subaru! Oh, honestly, I knew you were pushing yourself too hard, I suppose! Carrying around something that horrible for so long — of course this would happen, I suppose!"

His head grown heavy, his vision swimming, Subaru's shoulder was caught by Beatrice as she shouted at him.
Her adorable voice rang, yip-yip, in his skull, and Subaru realized he'd been more worn down than he'd thought.

Understandable, if you thought about it.
Losing his memories, dying over and over in that span, then dying over and over again after that too, confronting himself to reclaim his memory, waking to smash headlong into the five obstacles guarding the tower, fighting while shouldering his companions' burdens through his Authority, and finally losing Shaula—

His mind and body alike had suffered a drastic toll.
It was only a matter of days, and yet it felt as if he'd spent five years or more in this tower.

"—Ah, I—"

"Subaru, it's okay, it's fine. Just rest a little for now, okay? We'll talk properly once you wake up again. I have a lot I want to talk about too."

Going limp, sinking down where he knelt, Subaru was caught full in the front by Emilia. That soft warmth, that sweet scent — normally something that would make him stiffen up — struck him now like a powerful drug, and his consciousness slipped gently into the dark.

If, like this, he lost his life somewhere in the dark, would he return again to the place surrounding this tower and be able to search for a way to save Shaula there?
He half understood there was no such thing, and yet he couldn't help wishing for it anyway.
Slowly, Subaru's consciousness was swallowed by the darkness.


△▼△▼△▼△


"There we go."

Lifting the unconscious Subaru's body into her arms, Emilia let out a breath.
His eyes closed limply, his breathing deep enough to seem almost like death — it was proof of just how hard he'd fought to solve this Watchtower's problems.

How desperately Subaru had struggled to save Emilia and the others.
Even while he himself had been struggling with his own lost memory, he had rushed to her side after Batenkaitos stole her 'Name' and everyone had forgotten her — how happy that had made her.
She wanted to properly convey that to him.
So that Subaru, always working so hard, wouldn't go on blaming himself too much.

Shaula's fate wasn't Subaru's responsibility.
It belonged to all of them — to Emilia and everyone. Or, more precisely—

"—I think Oshi-sama's Flugel is a bad person."

The one who had shouldered the achievements of being the 'Sage' onto Shaula, and then, along with the renewal of the Pleiades Watchtower's role, had been the very one to put her to sleep.
Even if he had been one of the Three Great Heroes who saved the world, the moment he'd left Shaula lonely and made Subaru cry, he'd joined the ranks of the truly wicked in Emilia's book.
Of course, there was no chance to meet him and talk it out, so this frustration would go unresolved by anyone.

"From what I can see, the exhaustion is severe, but... nothing serious. He should recover with rest. Shall we take him to that 'Green Room,' as before?"

"Yes. Ram and the others are there too... While you were all rushing to help me, Anastasia-san was watching over Rem, wasn't she?"

"It's not really that grand a thing? I only saw off Julius, going to help Natsuki-kun, and Ram-san, going to help you all, after I got myself back. ...Sadly, there was no change on Rem-san's part."

"I see..."

Thinking of Ram and Rem, and Patrasche too, waiting in the 'Green Room,' Emilia's finely shaped brows knit together.
Batenkaitos's defeat could, in a sense, be called avenging Rem, who had lost both her 'memory' and her 'Name.' But what mattered was that she wake up, and until that was accomplished, whether the revenge had succeeded or not was a trivial matter.
At the very least, Ram would say exactly that, without hesitation. That what mattered was Rem's return.

"Let's hope for what we can draw out of Roy Alphard on that front too. There's still far too much we don't understand about this Watchtower — no, about the Great Library Pleiades."

"A great library that knows everything... that's what Shaula claimed. She was a scattered sort of girl, but that's exactly why I don't think she'd twist the words she was told. I'm sure this place really was told to her as a great library."

Whether that function as a great library referred to the 'Book of the Dead,' or whether there were other possibilities besides, they'd need to confirm.
On top of that, there was something Emilia needed to tell Beatrice and the others.

"Listen, after we let Subaru rest in the 'Green Room' — I want everyone to come with me somewhere. ...There's someone I want you all to meet."

"...Could it be that has something to do with the first floor?"

Emilia had ascended to the first floor and broken through the final 'Trial.'
That much, Beatrice and the others already knew. The problem was what came after, which hadn't been told yet.
Specifically, what was on the first floor, who Emilia had met there, what she had done.
It was such a bizarre, transcendent event that it was difficult to put into words at all—

"It's not that long a story, but it's complicated, so — will you come see for yourselves?"

And, pointing up above her own head, toward the tower's peak, far above, Emilia said this.


△▼△▼△▼△


『—You who have reached the tower's summit. You who tread the first floor, omnipotent petitioner』

"——"

Guided by Emilia, the group that reached the topmost floor of the Pleiades Watchtower — the first floor — was overwhelmed into silence by the welcome of a colossal dragon.
Seeing everyone's reaction, Emilia tilted her head, saying, "Surprising, right?"

"When I came up to the first floor, Volcanica was waiting. I was reeeally surprised too..."

"H-hold, hold on, I suppose. Su-surprised is not a word that will do here, I suppose!?"

Before the 'Divine Dragon' clad in blue scales, Beatrice's voice cracked in panic.
Her hands flapped up and down restlessly. But she wasn't the only one thrown off balance.

"This... is..."

"Whew — even I didn't see this one coming, I'll admit. What's this about? Wasn't the 'Divine Dragon' supposed to be somewhere beyond the Great Waterfall?"

"That was my understanding as well. According to the prophecy of the 'Dragon History Stone,' it was said he would appear the year the Royal Selection was decided, to renew the covenant with whoever became Lugunica's king anew."

Beside the speechless Julius, even the normally composed Anastasia couldn't hide a cold sweat. Echidna, at her throat, spoke with a voice that wavered slightly too.
But, a beat behind the women's reactions, Julius straightened, saying "Forgive my discourtesy,"

"Great dragon, protector of our kingdom — 'Divine Dragon.' Long have you upheld the covenant, and granted us much as the bearer of salvation. Forgive my disrespect, Volcanica-sama."
