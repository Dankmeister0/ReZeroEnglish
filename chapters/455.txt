Arc 6 Chapter 50: "――The Failure Is You"

At the cold voice calling out to him, Subaru quite literally felt his heart freeze solid.

"——"

Standing in the doorway of the room he'd spun around to face, Ram regarded him in silence, a frozen flame lodged in her pale pink eyes.

There was a heat in her that easily betrayed the impression Subaru had formed of her in their short time together — that of an iron mask who rarely let emotion show on her face.

"N-nuh..."

"Why are you so flustered? Ram asked a question. Answering it is Barusu's job... no, yours."

"I—I was just..."

"Just?"

Subaru's mind reflexively began racing, telling him he had to come up with an excuse. He had to get his tongue moving somehow, whip his sluggish brain into gear, and talk his way through this with some divinely inspired eloquence.

But on top of being thrown into a situation he'd never anticipated, Subaru hadn't even finished processing his shock at what had just happened — the disappearance of Meili's body. He had no excuse prepared, nor any composure with which to calmly face what was happening in front of him.

The best Subaru could manage was to open and close his mouth uselessly, and try not to say anything that would incriminate him further. Even if that very attitude spoke louder than words in confirming just how suspicious his behavior was.

"——"

At Subaru's silence, Ram's eyes narrowed. The chill in her gaze, already what he'd have called absolute zero, dropped even further, and Subaru was struck by the illusion of standing exposed to a razor-edged blizzard.

Desperately fighting to still his trembling knees, Subaru quietly arrived at an understanding that had come far too late.

*——Had he been set up?*

The entire discussion at the dinner table, about how Meili's body hadn't been found — it had been a bluff. Reporting that the body hadn't turned up had let Subaru dodge the moment of accusation, and he'd felt relief. Despite the desperate search coming up empty — despite the undeniable strangeness of a girl's body, hidden away so carelessly, still not being found — Subaru had simply accepted the convenient truth that his bad luck had won out, unquestioningly, and put off the seeds of his unease.

And the result was this thoroughly pathetic scene.

Hadn't Subaru seen it countless times, in any number of TV shows? A murderer who'd crafted the perfect plan, only to slip up at the crucial moment by returning to a scene watched over by a detective and the police, exposing themselves and handing over the damning evidence with their own hands — that almost comedic instant when a killer gives themselves away.

Somewhere in his heart, Subaru had always believed he'd never make such a fool of himself. It was practically an aesthetic convention of crime fiction — most people watching a mystery drama would find the culprit's foolishness, botching things at the very last step, laughably absurd.

The shock of realizing he had done exactly the same thing — no, played the part with even more pathetic clumsiness — was devastating.

"——A fake."

Ram's quiet murmur struck Subaru as he sat crushed beneath the weight of his own imperfect performance. A single word, and the fragile walls around Subaru's heart were stripped away as if by mortar fire. His color drained, his lips trembling, and Ram let out a sigh at the sight of him.

"No rebuttal to that, I see. Proof enough, I suppose, that you were aware of your own poor performance. You didn't research the person you're impersonating nearly well enough. There's only so much ignorance one can get away with."

Making no effort to hide her contempt, Ram laid out the grounds for her suspicion of Subaru. Called a fake, told his acting was clumsy, accused of not understanding 'Natsuki Subaru' at all — Subaru had no response, and more than that, felt his already-cracked heart shatter further, a pain sharp enough he could almost feel it bleed.

In fact, Subaru had bitten through the corner of his own lip, and it stung and bled.

Fake, fake, fake, fake, fake, fake, fake, fake, fake, fake, fake——.

An inferior, ill-made fake of 'Natsuki Subaru'——.

The thought pooled at the bottom of Subaru's gut like thick, pitch-black sludge. The ghastly mire filled his entire body, and under its weight, the trembling in his knees stopped. In its place, a searing heat rose behind his eyes, and a fuse was lit on something dark.

People might call it hostility, malice, ill will — or perhaps, murderous intent.

"No one but someone utterly desperate would fail to notice how unnatural you are. Unless it were, say, Julius, or Lady Anastasia... or Echidna, perhaps."

"...Keep quiet and listen, and you'll say whatever you please, huh."

"——"

"Fake, huh. There's a limit to how far you can stretch an accusation. Who do you think you are?"

"An accusation, is it."

Against the unrelenting edge of Ram's tongue, Subaru attempted a rebuttal even as it pained him. To be sure, everything she said was entirely correct. Even if he couldn't nod along to that harsh word "failure," there really was an unfillable gulf between Subaru and 'Natsuki Subaru.'

But he couldn't admit it. If he did, there'd be no escaping through excuses anymore. He couldn't stop his feet now. Not here, halfway down this road——

"That's an accusation if I ever heard one. My ears are itching just listening to it."

Shrugging his shoulders, twisting his face into a scowl, glaring at Ram, Subaru spat the words out. He dressed up a lie he himself found transparent, playing it up as though it were the truth of the world.

"Besides, so what if I was out walking around at night? I just wanted to think some things over, so I took a walk when nobody else was around. Somewhere I couldn't hear your little sister... Rem's breathing, or feel Patrasche nearby, so I could really think it through..."

"——Did you really think no one saw anything?"

To Subaru rattling off his words in a rush, Ram posed a quiet question. Her words were brief, but sharp enough to carve straight into a vital point.

"——"

At the question, Subaru held his breath, racking his brain to fathom what she truly meant.

To see. To be seen. Not being seen. Must not be seen.

*——What was Ram's "saw" referring to? What did it mean?*

Meili's corpse? Or Meili before she became a corpse? The dishonorable concealment Subaru had carried out on her body? Or——

"Even if you thought nothing and no one saw you, the earth dragon saw your suspicious behavior quite clearly. And Ram, in the same way, 'saw' it too."

Ram pressed a finger to her lips, showing Subaru a gesture entirely out of place for the moment.

"...What the hell are you talking about?"

Unable to grasp the meaning behind her action, all Subaru could do was answer in bewilderment. Still, he recognized the gesture itself from recently — it was the very one Subaru himself had made toward Patrasche, right before leaving the spirit's room. Why it now overlapped with Ram, here of all places, he had no idea——

"Your ignorance has reached its absolute limit, 'failure'——"

Subaru understood that this word from Ram, spoken with undisguised disappointment, was an ultimatum.

"——"

Up to now, Ram had remained cold but had never lost her willingness to exchange words with him — but even that intent vanished now. The instant he saw the color in her eyes, Subaru concluded that excuses were entirely meaningless now.

No matter what excuse Subaru offered, Ram would no longer lend it an ear. Which meant Subaru had no choice left but to force his way through this situation by sheer strength.

*——In that instant, however carelessly, Subaru's mind began weighing ways to kill Ram.*

"——"

To think of killing. He no longer felt any resistance to the thought. He'd already killed one person. Whether it was one or two hardly made much difference. Before that, even, 'I' had experience taking countless lives.

There'd be no problem in carrying the 'Book of the Dead' plan out from here.

"——About that lady, though~"

Just as the countless choices within Subaru crumbled away with a clatter, leaving nothing but dangerous options behind, the illusory girl who had kept silent until now cut in.

An invisible phantom, yet one that felt so close he could sense her very breath. She wrapped her arms around Subaru's body from behind, whispering softly into his ear, tempting him.

And the saccharine temptation of her words was——

"——It's probably her condition, you know~ Her weight's off on the left, her balance is bad~"

What the sweet whisper delivered was the weak point of a fierce, dignified girl — in short, instructions on how to kill her.

Inferior in build, inferior in physical strength, inferior in experience with killing. But with the knowledge of 'I,' who had spent so long alongside the jet-black slaughterer, killing a mere woman would be nothing at all.

"Step in from her left side and slam her head into the wall. That's all it takes~"

Taking the difference in physical strength between them into account, 'I' offered up fitting advice. Simply following it would crack the girl's skull open, blooming a crimson flower across the stone wall.

A crimson blossom, fitting for a lovely girl of pale pink——.

"——!"

With no signal, no trigger, Subaru lunged forward in time with his opponent's blink. With the phantom's cheers ringing at his back, Subaru pressed forward without hesitation toward Ram, who tensed ever so slightly — toward her "right" side. He meant to shove her body straight into the wall——

"When words fail, resort to violence. A savage, tedious conclusion."

In that instant of clash, just before Subaru's arm could reach her, Ram's lips spun out those words. Their eyes met, and Subaru caught his breath at the chill in Ram's gaze.

"——Did you really think Ram, frail as she is, would come challenge a savage man like you all alone?"

More pitying than mocking, Ram's words overlapped with the sound of the air itself cracking. And in fact, it wasn't a trick of the ear — it was the death cry of the air itself, as moisture rapidly condensed and was forcibly wrenched from gas into solid.

"Wha—!"

The sound erupted from beneath Subaru's planted foot as a white, frost-bitten spike of ice thrust upward, catching his forward momentum violently and halting it in a burst of pain.

"I—ice⁉"

Thrown back by the cold, hard impact, Subaru doubted his own eyes. But the transformation didn't end there. The ice spike wasn't limited to the one beneath his foot — more and more rose up one after another, until they had transformed into a cage of ice, enclosing Subaru completely.

"No way... This is—don't tell me this is magic⁉"

In the span of a single blink, barely a second, the ice cage was complete, and Subaru's movement was sealed away. Grabbing the icy bars with both hands and shaking them in an attempt to break free, Subaru was stunned by their sheer strength. They didn't budge an inch. No matter how hard he might kick or punch, he couldn't muster any confidence that he'd manage to break them.

And Subaru, now trapped within that cage of ice——

"——I really wish this was all just Ram's misunderstanding."

—was watched over by Emilia, who had appeared from behind Ram, her violet eyes sorrowful.


* * *


At a turn of events that was, if anything, only natural, Subaru sat stunned in his cage like a captured monkey.

"——"

Ram and Emilia joining forces — of course they would. Unlike Subaru, they had the option of cooperating with one another. Their circumstances were entirely different from his own, forced as he was to fight a lone battle. It probably hadn't even crossed their minds, from the start, to face a dangerous individual alone.

In the end, Subaru had been dancing in the palm of their hands the entire time, in every single scene.

"Not even able to feign glibness in a moment like this, nor talk big without any grounds. As a performer, you're not even second-rate — third-rate, at best, if that."

Fixing her gaze on Subaru in the cage, arms folded across her chest, Ram spat the words out. Looking closer, he saw she hadn't moved an inch from her original spot since first appearing. More than that, he hadn't even managed to make her shift her stance.

That alone spoke to how different a caliber of player she was, compared to him.

"Acting like that wouldn't fool anyone but someone truly desperate. Which means it's hardly worth wondering who among us has been the toughest, mentally."

"That's a compliment, right? Thank you."

"...You're welcome."

Beside Ram, who reacted with a faint grimace, Emilia stepped forward, her silver hair dancing. Judging from Ram's attitude and words, this ice cage was Emilia's own handiwork — in other words, a form of magic she was able to use.

Under any other circumstances, Subaru might have been moved to think how well such fantastical ice magic suited Emilia, with her mysterious beauty. But now that he was actually a prisoner within that cage of ice, it was plain at a glance just how powerful it was, and just how overwhelming a gap in strength his current disadvantage stood upon.

The difficulty of carrying out the 'Book of the Dead' plan, and the ranking of the obstacles in his way, had just shifted drastically.

But before that——

"——Subaru, what were you trying to do, out at a time like this? Weren't you supposed to be resting in your room?"

"That's..."

Looking worried, Emilia asked Subaru what he truly meant, even now, at this stage. What dwelled in her eyes was sincere concern, but given the situation, it seemed nothing short of an earnestness bordering on foolishness. She was under the mistaken belief that there was a coherent reason behind everything.

Subaru, too, had things he could say in his defense. There was, in fact, a reason things had come to this. But if he told a story as intangible as grasping at clouds, who on earth would believe him?

"Lady Emilia, there's no point in asking. I don't think that thing will give a proper answer to our questions. And frankly, I question whether we should even go on treating it as Barusu."

"But Subaru is Subaru. Surely you can see that too, Ram?"

"At best, it's a shoddy imitation that merely shares the same appearance... That's Ram's judgment."

Seemingly leaning, if anything, toward believing this was Subaru, Emilia had her inclination reined in by Ram. It was probably Ram who'd first grown suspicious of Subaru, and Ram who'd proposed keeping watch over his nighttime movements. Emilia, no doubt, had simply been standing by in reserve, prepared in case of an emergency.

In other words, in Ram's mind, the question of how to deal with a Subaru who wasn't 'Natsuki Subaru' had long since been settled. All that remained was the painful process of confirming it.

"Thanks to Lady Emilia, we've managed to subdue it without any harm done. Perhaps if it suffers a little pain, it'll be inclined to tell us what we want to know?"

"Hurt me... you mean torture? Everyone knows that takes more than just a sadistic streak — it requires serious expertise."

"If it's necessary, then I will. And while I don't enjoy inflicting pain — I am rather good at it."

Trapped within a cage barely larger than a single tatami mat, Subaru put up a brave front, but Ram showed no mercy. Her fingers were pale and slender, yet her modest claim of being "rather good" at inflicting pain struck Subaru as oddly convincing.

If he could do nothing as things stood, if no means of counterattack came to mind, then his shallow depths would be probed and laid utterly bare, exposing nothing but a meaningless, pathetic display of humiliation.

"——Wait. You can't hurt him. I won't let you."

But the one who stopped Ram from carrying out her extreme resolution was Emilia — the very one who had bound Subaru within the cage of ice. She shielded the cage containing Subaru with her own back, standing to face Ram head-on.

"...I thought Lady Emilia agreed with Ram?"

"I agreed that Subaru was acting strange, and that we should hear him out. And I thought something like this might happen... so I knew I had to be here."

"It's precisely because I didn't want this to happen that I asked Lady Beatrice to handle Julius and the others. And now even you won't listen, Lady Emilia... You're being far too naive."

Making no attempt to hide her irritation at their disagreement, Ram pointed past Emilia at Subaru behind her. Then, with a thorny edge to her voice, she said, "Listen," and continued:

"That thing is clearly missing far too much of what the real Barusu ought to have. I heard about what happened in the Watergate City... Priestella. There was apparently a Sin Archbishop there who could change appearances, wasn't there?"

"...Yes. Part of the reason we came here was to restore the people that Sin Archbishop changed into different forms."

"Then isn't it possible that Sin Archbishop has disguised itself as one of us?"

"——"

"Even if it isn't the Sin Archbishop itself, if something out there can freely alter its appearance, then looks alone mean nothing. And if its usual behavior, and the way it comes across in conversation, differ this much from what we know..."

Against Emilia's appeal to emotion, Ram, holding back her anger, answered with cold logic. Honestly, to Subaru it was nothing but a baseless accusation dressed up as reasoning, but he had absolutely nothing on hand to refute it.

And more than that, Subaru — no, 'I' — felt a wave of revulsion at the topic itself.

"——!"

A flashback. A memory, white.

It wasn't Natsuki Subaru's memory — it was a fragment glimpsed within the 'Book of the Dead,' a memory from back when 'I' was still 'I.'

*——The treatment inflicted upon 'I,' under the name of discipline.*

Countless terrors had been carved into 'I,' but the most horrifying of all was when its own body was remade into countless, uncountable "frogs." Its consciousness resided in only one of them, yet the countless fragmented copies of itself hopped off wherever they pleased and fled.

The terror of never being able to return to what it was, and the sense of forgetting what that "original" even was in the first place — the plummeting value of its very existence as a living thing — made it, the moment it was finally restored, genuinely grateful to "Mother," from the bottom of its heart.

And at the same time, it was carved into its very soul that it must never, ever defy an order again.

"——Ngh."

Reliving that terror directly, as his own experience, Subaru's vision flickered. One's own form and shape connects directly to the root of one's identity. To toy with it at will, however one pleased, was a desecration of that very existence.

One of the most contemptible acts imaginable——.

"That's an extreme thing to say, and it's not like you, Ram! Talking like you're just going to shut him down from above!"

"Can you say for certain that it isn't true? With that man standing right behind you..."

With a nauseating sensation churning his stomach, Subaru writhed alone in agony, left behind as the argument between Emilia and Ram carried on. But Ram's argument was something like proving a negative. One could prove something existed, but no one could ever prove that it didn't.

That this Natsuki Subaru was not the 'Natsuki Subaru' they wanted him to be. In explaining that logic, the power of someone able to change their appearance was useful, easy to understand — and at the same time, it was something utterly unbearable to Subaru right now.

Unable to manage the conflicting emotions warring within himself, Subaru writhed in pain, gasping——

"We should make it talk right now! To find out where the real Barusu and Meili are!"

"——Huh?"

At Ram's sudden outburst, Subaru's attention was seized and pulled into an entirely different track. It was a bolt from the blue, an utterly unexpected remark.

"——"

Lifting his head, Subaru looked at Ram and Emilia, still arguing. He couldn't see Emilia's expression from behind her, but Ram's face was clearly visible. In her eyes, blazing with anger, there was no trace of scheming to trap Subaru with a lie. At the very least, she'd meant what she'd just said, in earnest.

Which meant Meili's body had not, in fact, been found by Ram and the others. What they had been waiting here to accuse Subaru of was, purely and simply, his suspicious behavior — his failure to fully play the part of 'Natsuki Subaru.'

That explained the somewhat disjointed quality of Ram's interrogation. But at the same time, it left him baffled.

If it wasn't Ram or Emilia, then who on earth had moved Meili's body? Some other agenda was at work here — not Subaru's, not Ram's or the others'——.

"You can't just decide he's real or fake like that! Because the Subaru standing right here is..."

"——I've lost my memory!!"

"Huh...?"

Gripping the icy bars, Subaru shouted, cutting into the argument between the two of them.

It was Ram whose eyes went wide, dumbstruck, at the outburst. Struck completely from an angle she hadn't expected at all, her mind genuinely went blank. Had this been some scheme to rattle her mind and exploit the opening, it would have been a total success. But it wasn't. This wasn't that at all — it was Subaru's dead-serious cry.

"——!"

Even Subaru himself, the moment after shouting it, couldn't understand why he'd said it right now, at this exact instant. This loop, Subaru had made every effort to hide the fact that he had no memories, striving to pass himself off as unchanged from the previous 'Natsuki Subaru.' Thanks to that effort, this loop had so far avoided any situation where Subaru's life was taken.

It hadn't — and yet, one after another, situations just as shocking, or even more so, kept toying with and hounding Natsuki Subaru regardless.

To be sure, hiding his amnesia was the reason everything in this loop had gone so badly wrong. But even so, what good would confessing it now do?

"At a time like this, what nonsense are you spouting...!"

Sure enough, the moment her thoughts, once frozen, started moving again, Ram's expression flooded with anger. To her, Subaru's remark was nothing more than a desperate lie — not even worthy of that title, just idle nonsense.

But even if that was so for Ram——

"Ram! Subaru just said it! There really is a reason after all!"

"Are you serious, Lady Emilia⁉ Something like that isn't even worth believing...!"

Spreading her arms wide, standing in Ram's way, Emilia took Subaru's side. If it had simply been a case of her leaping gladly on some far-fetched claim, Subaru might have given up on saying anything more. But in stark contrast to Ram, who dismissed it outright, Emilia stood firm, facing forward,

"It is worth believing! Isn't that what all our time together has been about⁉"

"——!"

At Emilia's appeal, Ram's cheeks stiffened, ever so slightly. For a moment, hesitation surfaced in her pale pink eyes. He could tell that all manner of thoughts were spinning through her sharp mind. Then, at last, she parted her tightly pressed lips,

"Ram, let's listen to what Subaru has to say, together..."

"——What happens to Rem, then?"

"——Ah."

In that instant, Ram closed her glistening eyes, spun her small frame around, and lunged at Emilia. Making an instant decision, Emilia stepped back and braced herself to restrain Ram. But Ram's advance was faster, and Emilia's left hand was caught by Ram's right.

"Don't get in my way!"

"Kyaa!"

As she spoke, with minimal footwork and body movement, twisting wrist and shoulder joints just enough to produce maximum effect, Ram sent Emilia's body flying. Emilia was thrown effortlessly, in something like a sideways somersault. Startled by her spinning field of vision, she nonetheless grasped her own situation instantly, planting her long legs on the floor in an attempt at an emergency landing.

"——!"

Right where Emilia's foot was set to land lay one of Ram's discarded shoes. Stepping on it, Emilia's control over her posture collapsed, and she was forced down onto her hands and knees on the floor.

Having overcome Emilia in close combat through skill and instinct alone, Ram slipped into the gap she'd created. She thrust a slender, small wand through the bars of ice, right up to the tip of the astonished Subaru's nose.

"Say it again — that you forgot."

"T-that's not... what I meant..."

"With that face, that voice. Say you forgot Rem, even one more time, and..."

Grinding her back teeth hard, Ram was visibly gathering something at the trembling tip of her wand. Watching an invisible force, likely what one would call mana, accumulate before his very eyes, Subaru couldn't manage a second word.

No words came to him that might stop Ram — the girl before him, on the verge of tears. Not Natsuki Subaru — would 'Natsuki Subaru' have been able to do it?

"No, Ram! Stop!!"

Having regained her footing, Emilia cried out and moved to stop Ram. But she was too late.

"——"

A white light flashed beyond the icy bars, and the impact swallowed Subaru whole. His body was hurled backward with full force into the ice bars, the back of his head striking hard.

"——!"

His head reeled, and his consciousness clouded over. Without even time enough for an excuse, Natsuki Subaru's consciousness cut out right there.

"——Ngh?"

With a faint, feeble groan, his consciousness was drawn back toward waking. Slowly, slowly, his consciousness rose up out of a darkness like thick sludge. A hazy, indistinct sensation, gradually, gradually gathering speed, taking on the texture of reality, until at last——.

"——Guh, ah⁉ Ow⁉"

The instant his mind drew close to waking, it was hauled up with the forceful yank of something gripping him by the scruff of the neck, and a sharp, searing pain scorched through Subaru's brain. His body jerked, and groaning in pain, Subaru woke up on a hard floor.

"Ow, ow, ow, ow ow, what — what is this...?"

Sitting up through the intermittent pain, Subaru reached a hand toward his left shoulder. The instant he touched it, a fierce pain seared through his brain again. His left arm wouldn't move at all.

"Is this— is my shoulder out of its socket...? Dislocated? I've never had that happen before..."

Everything past his shoulder dangled uselessly, refusing to obey his will. Still, since trying to move it, or even touching it, sent a stab of agony through him, Subaru got to his feet with the utmost care. He looked around. No one was there. And this place was——

"The room where I hid Meili..."

In other words, none other than the very room he'd been conscious in until just moments before. As proof, the ice cage Emilia had made with her magic still remained standing right behind him. What was strange was that Subaru was lying outside that cage. From what he could tell, the cage showed no sign of having been opened, and there should have been no ordinary way to escape it——

"——So that's the shoulder, is it?"

Thinking that far, Subaru realized the connection between his dislocated shoulder and his escape from the ice cage. The gaps between the icy bars of the cage were wide enough that, if it were a child, squeezing through wouldn't have been impossible. Forcing it — say, by dislocating a shoulder — should have made it possible to pass through.

The problem was how an unconscious Natsuki Subaru had managed to do that in the first place.

And——

"——Where did Emilia and Ram go?"

Nowhere in the room could he find any sign of the two of them, who only moments ago had been arguing, on the verge of escalating into something like a fight to the death.

It was a far too unnatural situation.

*——No, more than unnatural, it ought to be called terrifying.*

Subaru's consciousness had been knocked out, his shoulder was dislocated with no memory of how, and Emilia and the others, who should have been there without fail, were nowhere to be seen.

What on earth had happened while he was unconscious — had something strange occurred? Subaru scanned the room — and noticed.

"Natsuki Subaru Has Arrived!"

"——"

Carved into the wall was that same phrase he'd seen once before. Words gouged crudely into the stone wall, scrawled as if across a blank canvas.

Had it been done with the fragment of stone lying near the wall? It seemed, from the look of it, to be what remained of the stone block that had originally sat at the back of the room — the very one used to conceal Meili's body.

Using that stone fragment, the words "Natsuki Subaru Has Arrived!" had been carved into the wall. — On its own, that wouldn't have carried the same impact as the same phrase carved into his arm once had.

A rerun of the same shock shouldn't have amounted to much — he should have been able to laugh it off.

But, however——

A chill ran through Subaru, frozen in place, at the voice from behind him.

It wasn't that he hadn't sensed anyone's presence. To begin with, Subaru's attention had been completely consumed by the writing on the walls. He wouldn't have noticed anyone approaching, whoever it was.

So that wasn't the reason for his shock. What shocked Subaru now was that he recognized that rough, dismissive tone of voice. A memory so infuriating he wanted to forget it as soon as possible——

So he said, and behind Subaru — who couldn't bring himself to turn around — a red-haired man, shark-like, laughed.

A man who shouldn't have been able to be here was, without question, sneering.
