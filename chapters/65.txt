Arc 2 Chapter 41: "Oni"

My throat trembled, and a war cry echoed through the deep, dark forest.

Neither an "ah" nor an "oh," meaningless syllables devoid of sense, an explosion of pure noise.
Even though it issued from his own throat, Subaru himself couldn't find any reason or meaning behind the act.

The red points of light leapt at him all at once.
As they drew closer, they gradually resolved into shape—savage beasts cloaked in jet black, fangs bared, closing in for the kill.

There was no time to register the trembling of his legs, the terror in his heart, the scream of his soul.
All that dominated Subaru's mind in that instant was not any feeling toward the overwhelming embodiment of "death" bearing down on him, but only the safety of the girl lying just behind him.

He widened his stance, spread both arms, and made his body a shield so the beasts' claws and fangs couldn't reach past him to her. It wasn't a conscious decision—just his body moving on its own.

If the enemy had been the kind to simply back off in deference to that resolve, he might have been saved. But the reality bearing down on him was merciless, and in the face of wild beasts brimming with feral hunger, such sentiment meant nothing at all.

—It ends here. After coming this far, just when the whole picture had finally come into view.

Not enough strength, again. Out of reach, again.
No matter how his heart struggled not to yield, sheer lack of power kept stripping away Subaru's options. Was this situation, too, going to be nothing more than that?

"—————!!"

He roared, refusing to accept defeat.
That alone—not losing to any of the lights before him—was the one thing he held onto with sheer force of will. Literally a bluff, papier-mâché bravado—fangs closed in on his throat, and—

—right before his eyes, he saw a witchbeast's head burst apart like a piece of fruit.

Blood sprayed from the point-blank execution, and Subaru, standing directly in front, was drenched in the fresh blood from head to toe. An instant later, the now-headless body, momentum undiminished, slammed into Subaru. He was sent flying backward, tumbling across the ground, and rose to his feet shaking his head against the pain and the sickening feel of blood.
What on earth—

"Sorry for the delay—but I'm glad I made it in time."

Her skirt hem sweeping gracefully, pinching the white apron dress lightly with one hand while the other gripped a vicious iron ball, the blue-haired girl descended into the abyss.
She spun, stepped lightly, the chime of chains ringing out as she moved.

"I believe the plan was for me to focus entirely on buying time?"

"I did my best! I bought a ton of time! I went at it like it'd be fine even if I ended up beating them! And this is the result, thank you so, so much!"

To Rem, whose Morningstar's chain sent a chain of sharp clangs ringing through the air, Subaru offered his gratitude in a literal full prostration.
Watching his reaction out of the corner of her eye, Rem slowly turned to face forward—toward the pack of witchbeasts eyeing the newcomer, licking their chops.

The creatures that had been so belligerent toward Subaru alone had switched to caution the moment one small girl joined him. They clearly weren't taking lightly the fact that one of their pack had been slaughtered in an instant.
As his exchange with that one beast earlier had already shown him, for all their bestial appearance, these things were remarkably shrewd and cunning.

"Rem…"

He called out to her back as she stood her ground, urging her to stay alert.
Rem didn't turn around at the subdued tone in Subaru's voice, only let out a breath.

"I chase down Subaru-kun's terrible body odor, hurry over at the sound of an unearthly shriek he doesn't even care who hears, and this is what I find. Not exactly a dignified state to be in."

"'Terrible body odor' kind of stings. Also, I don't remember it myself, but I'd like a detailed account of exactly what I was screaming earlier."

"If Rem feels like it. And if the ones over there will allow it, I might tell you in detail."

Pointing the iron handle straight ahead, Rem let a dangerous light kindle in her eyes, as if goading them on. While it chilled Subaru to the bone, the witchbeasts on the receiving end of that hostility seemed far from calm about it. In the eyes of the creatures that had been silently glaring back at them, a clear readiness for battle and an eagerness to trample them underfoot now surfaced.

"That doesn't seem to be an option, it appears."

"—Rem!"

Unable to hold it back, Subaru cried out her name at a pitch bordering on a scream.
What suddenly erupted in front of her was a ferocious assault from two witchbeasts splitting off to either side. Closing to within a few strides of her, the two beasts running in parallel broke apart left and right simultaneously.
Had Subaru been the one facing them, he wouldn't have been able to decide which way to turn his attention in time, and their venomous fangs would have torn him apart without effort.
But—

"——hah!"

Her right arm, gripping the iron handle, swung as casually as if swatting away an insect. The chain attached to the handle followed the arc, and the iron ball linked to the chain came trailing after it.
What should have been a sluggish weapon of destruction spun with tremendous force, sweeping away everything in its arc as her arm swung, striking the torso of the witchbeast that happened to be in its path dead-on, blasting the body nearly in two. Bone and viscera scattered, transformed in an instant, unseen by anyone, into fertilizer for the forest.

And then, the remaining beast, its partner slain in an instant, poured all its fury into its fangs and drove a sharp strike at Rem's undefended left side—only for her left fist to crash down on the witchbeast's snout from directly above just before impact. The force of the punch caved in its skull, killing it instantly, its head driven so deep it was half-buried in the ground.

It was a masterstroke of technique. But its brutality was far removed from anything that could be called masterful.
Words of praise fell short, and Subaru, eyes wide at the carnage unfolding before him, could do nothing but stare in stunned silence.

He had thought he understood Rem's destructive power firsthand. Now it became clear, in every sense, that "thought" was all it had ever been.

"S-so strong! !!"

"I'd think twice before saying that to a woman, Subaru-kun."

"With my pathetic vocabulary, that's the only way I can describe the situation right now. Seriously, you're insane."

Faced with a reliability from Rem that exceeded all his expectations, Subaru sprang back up and returned to his original spot.
Crawling back, he checked on the sleeping girl's condition. Even at the very brink of death, and in an ongoing life-threatening situation, the sleeping girl remained oblivious to it all, slumbering on in deep, undisturbed sleep.

"Man, what carefree, oblivious sleeping. Bet she'll grow up healthy and full of stamina!"

Praising her impressively sound sleep, he used his aching arms to lift the small body back up. Readjusting his hold so as not to drop the light weight—

"Rem, could you actually wipe them out solo!?"

"Too many of them, too few of us. It'd be a losing battle of attrition."

"Yeah, figured as much. Then…"

Still carrying the girl, he moved to Rem's side.
With two more of the pack lost, the witchbeasts' movements had grown sluggish. They crouched low, as if gauging their opponents' next move, hostile glances glinting, but with no trigger to spur them into action. Had they simply relied on numbers and pushed a saturation assault, Subaru and Rem, outmatched in raw strength, wouldn't have stood a chance.
But the creatures mistook caution for cowardice, and in doing so, forfeited the optimal choice on their own.

And so, Subaru and Rem, almost simultaneously, fixed their eyes on the very same spot.

"There!"

Rem's strike answered Subaru's cry.
The iron ball's roar tore through the air, a howl cheering on slaughter. It struck true, just short of the point where the witchbeasts were clustered densest, blasting the ground apart—clods of earth and dust billowed up, a cascading deluge of soil and rubble blocking the retreating beasts' sight for several seconds.

"Now—!"

At Rem's next cry, Subaru's body took off running as if kicked into motion.
He headed for the one gap that single blast had torn open in the encirclement—a single point to break through the densely packed line.

As Subaru dove into the gap, a witchbeast that had yielded the path let out a roar. The beast howled and leapt again to block Subaru's way—

"—whoaaa, trauma sound!!"

Past his ear as he ran, the light chime of chains brushed gently by.
Shivers ran up Subaru's spine at the déjà vu of that sound. Right beside him, the iron ball's welcome exploded, and a single flower of blood bloomed in the night forest.

Leaving the fresh blood behind, he planted his feet on uncertain ground, plunging into the forest under a barrage of overwhelming hostility. Leaping over tree roots, branches whipping at his cheeks—

"Rem, I can't tell which way to go!"

"Straight ahead, forward. Once you clear the barrier, this is settled. Until then, don't lose your bearings!"

Told to go "straight ahead," he still couldn't make out what was in front of him.
He hadn't imagined that darkness where he could see only the barest sliver ahead could so thoroughly scramble one's sense of direction. Even trying to feel his way forward, both his hands were occupied with the girl's weight.
Inevitably, while keeping watch for obstacles ahead, Subaru was forced to curl his body to shield the girl from harm as much as possible while running.

His breath ran ragged. Was he still on course? Would they catch up to him? Could they be circling around, closing in from ahead?
His anxieties were endless, and the mounting fear steadily whittled away at Subaru's spirit.

Feeling in his left arm had gone dull, the unceasing bleeding dripping from his jacket's fabric, and the droplets falling into blood gave him the illusion of leaving a trail for his pursuers to follow.

Run, dash, keep fleeing.
The same scenery seemed to repeat endlessly, giving him the sensation that he hadn't advanced a single step. It couldn't possibly be true, and yet his mounting desperation spurred that imagined dread onward.
And so, each time weakness swelled in his chest and his knees threatened to buckle—

—from behind, the rippling sound of chains spurred Subaru onward.

Every time he heard that sound, Subaru gritted his teeth and looked ahead.
The only reason he could keep running now was thanks to Rem. Because she kept eliminating the threats and guarding his back, he was able to go on exposing this shameful display of survival.
Having already exposed this much, he figured he might as well throw away his last shred of dignity too, and struggle on to live, however unsightly it looked.

"Ah, damn it! My side hurts—!"

He spurred himself on with a shout of misplaced frustration and pushed his legs forward.
Forward, forward—!

Then suddenly, the darkness before Subaru's eyes broke open.
His field of vision widened, and squinting instinctively at the sudden change, he saw, far in the distance, artificial light burning.

"Rem! Light! People—we're almost to the barrier!"

At the literal appearance of a light of hope, Subaru turned his head back to share his joy.
But immediately after, he found himself frozen, eyes wide in shock.

Behind him, the sight of Rem, who had been fighting to protect his back, was simply too horrific.

Her crisply tailored, starched maid uniform was slashed in numerous places by claws and fangs alike. Her bare white skin bore wound after wound, none of them shallow, and her once vividly blue hair was disheveled beyond recognition, so drenched in the blood she'd taken to the head that its original color could barely be told.

"Rem—!"

"Keep running! …While Rem—while Rem can still be Rem!"

Subaru's voice, full of concern, was cut short by the force of the words thrown back at him.
He couldn't accept it. But he shook off his hesitation at that cry and ran on toward the barrier.
All sentiment set aside for later—all that mattered now was reaching the barrier, just getting through it—

"What…?"

The momentum of that resolve was instantly crushed by an unexpected sight.
Subaru's stunned gaze fixed on—the glow of the crystal governing the barrier, and right beside the great tree it was embedded in. His line of sight dropped low, nearly level with the ground. And there, a small, tiny, impossibly tiny shadow crouched.

As he drew closer, as its outline grew clearer, Subaru's eyes widened.
It was a small creature. Its body was small enough to be entirely cradled in both arms, its coat as soft as down—nothing comparable to the larger ones he knew.

—It was the very puppy-like witchbeast that had led Subaru to the village.

"So that's where you were hiding… but you know, even I can kick around something your size!"

If it meant to block his path, its weight class was hopelessly insufficient.
If it intended to stand as the final obstacle in this chase for survival, he had to call that a shallow miscalculation.
That very thought of Subaru's—

"No way…"

—was swept away before a torrent of earth and rubble unfurling before his eyes, vast enough to blot out his entire field of vision.
Whoever had made that shallow miscalculation, the sight before him made it painfully clear it had been him.

The world slowed, gradually, gently. Subaru knew from firsthand experience that this was the grace period granted when one's life hung in mortal peril.
In that world where everything crawled forward in slow motion, he belatedly realized something.
Before entering the forest, Rem had told him this:

—Witchbeasts are enemies of humanity that wield mana.

Not that they could use curses. She'd said they possessed mana. Which meant—didn't that also imply they could use magic like this?

"Ohhhhhhhh—!!"

He thrust a leg out to the side, slamming on the brakes against his own momentum. But it was pointless. His body pitched forward, losing its balance, and the torrent of earth was already right in front of him. There was no way to dodge. And if he were swallowed by that flow, the frenzy of sand and stone would tear him apart in an instant.

It was all over.
The instant that thought raced through him, Subaru braced himself to at least fling the girl in his arms to the side—when an impact from directly beside him sent him flying, tearing through trees as he went.

"Ogohhh!?"

Struck near his right side, he screamed at the force threatening to spill his innards out and was sent flying. Amid a sensation defying gravity, up and down and left and right all tangled together, he curled his body purely to shield the girl, and slammed back-first into a thick trunk, the breath knocked out of him as he fell to the ground.

"Gah, hff, ahh… it hurts…!"

Groaning in pain, he lifted his head, rubbing the belly that had taken the blow.
And then he looked directly at Rem, sent flying up into the treetops by the direct hit of the earthen torrent.

Battered by the violent welcome of soil and stone, Rem's small body tumbled through the air as lightly as a leaf. Her figure, blood spraying from her, was a world apart from how she'd looked drenched only in the blood of her enemies before—it was clear even to an untrained eye that she'd taken damage that was seriously bad.

Rem's body then fell, unable even to break her fall, crashing hard onto the ground. The only mercy was that the earth, freshly churned up by the torrent of soil, hadn't cracked the fallen girl's skull.

Even Subaru, slow as he was to catch on, could grasp the situation now.
Frozen before the oncoming torrent, Subaru had been shoved aside by Rem, who sacrificed her own chance to dodge to do it. And so she was the one who took the full brunt of that impact.

Of course, it was a force Subaru himself could never have withstood. But if it meant Rem had to be sacrificed for it, then none of it meant anything at all.

"Rem, you idiot! You— like this, I— this makes no—!"

What was it all for, he began to scream, when Subaru's spine suddenly went cold.
Likely because they'd felt the same thing. The witchbeast that had unleashed the torrent of earth, and the pack of pursuers that had caught up as well—all of them, all at once, froze in place.

There was a premonition. A certainty. Only the thick, heavy presence of "death" hung in the air.

—Slowly, Rem's fallen body began to rise.

Despite having taken such a violent blow, there wasn't a trace of injury in the way she rose to her feet. If anything, the wounds she should have borne were visibly closing before his eyes. Her extraordinary regenerative power gave off intense heat, the blood evaporating off her, producing a red haze.

And then he saw it. Subaru saw it.

Rem's eyes, sweeping in a slow circle to survey her surroundings, had lost all reason.
Her blood-soaked expression twisted into an ecstatic smile, and above all—from her head, now bared of its hair ornament, a single white horn had sprouted.

"Ahh, hahaha—"

Laughter. It was laughter like that of a young child, overflowing with unmasked cruelty.

Her body whirled, riding the wind, and charged straight into the pack. Before the lead witchbeast, which had stopped in its tracks, could even react, its nape was crushed beneath Rem's heel. The witchbeast, its throat crushed from directly above, died instantly; she kicked its body away with full force to keep the others held back at the rear. One after another, starting with whichever beast's movement stilled first, she brought her Morningstar—the chain now stowed within the iron handle, transformed into a mace—down again and again, mass-producing flowers of blood and corpses one after another.

"Witchbeast, witchbeast, witchbeast—Witch!!"

What was most terrifying of all was that Rem, her eyes stripped of reason, hadn't lost her sanity.
Even as she surrendered herself to the overflowing bestiality within, she hadn't lost sight of the situation. With that overwhelming power, she was venting some smoldering grudge that had been burning inside her.

Blood burst forth, skulls caved in, and intestines and brain matter scattered across the forest in horrifying volume.
On his knees, forgetting even his own pain, Subaru could only stare, transfixed, at the sight unfolding before him.

He had no courage now to make a sound. If he did—if he were to somehow intrude on the consciousness of Rem raging like that—he couldn't shake the feeling, irrational as it was, that he might be killed for it.
Or perhaps that wasn't a misconception at all. That was how far beyond normal Rem seemed right now.
That figure—drenched in blood, her deranged laughter echoing through the forest, that otherworldly visage—was, without question,

"—an oni."

No matter how much blood she was drenched in, that white horn alone never seemed to be stained.
Atop her head, it gleamed, its sharp point seeming to proclaim its own existence for all to see.

Subaru was swept up in the situation.
But unlike Subaru, who had frozen in place, the witchbeasts were not the type to simply sit and wait to be slaughtered. Individuals freed from their momentary paralysis began surrounding Rem one after another, and even as the pile of corpses grew with every strike she landed, little by little, their claws and fangs began to find their way to her body.

She had been outnumbered from the start. The number of witchbeasts, already swelled beyond the original pack during their flight through the forest, was beyond counting. Even though something like the original pack's numbers must already have been wiped out, the red points of light floating in the dark night kept welling up without end.

"Even in max-strength mode, there's no way she can hold out forever against an infinite spawn…"

Amid the dizzying shifts in the situation, only one thing remained constant—Subaru and Rem were at a disadvantage.
Having grasped that much objectively, Subaru cut himself off as he sensed mana beginning to gather once more.

The mana was concentrating, once again, at that puppy's location.

The mana in the air was being drawn there wholesale, his vision warping and twisting as if distorted. She must have sensed that vortex of magic. Turning around, Rem readied the hand-blade that had just gouged out a witchbeast's innards, moving to counter the threat, heading for the puppy—but,

—not missing the opening created by her dash, the pack of witchbeasts leapt at her back all at once.

"—!"

A flicker of unsettlement. Even Rem, deep in her berserk state, seemed shaken by a situation that had exceeded even her own comprehension.
Emotion returned to those eyes that had lost all reason, and her blood-soaked, malicious grin crumbled, revealing beneath it the grief-stricken face of a young girl.

—Ah, so she can make a face like that too, Subaru thought, somewhere at the edge of his consciousness, as—

"—gaaaaaaaah!!!"

—the searing agony of his left arm being crushed between fangs tore a scream from Subaru's throat fit to rip it apart.
An instant later, his right leg, his left side, his back—fangs sank in all at once. His vision turned entirely red. The pain no longer even registered as pain. His ankle was crushed. A chunk of flesh was torn clean from his side. Blood, and everything else, leaked out. Spilled away. Such a waste. His flesh and blood.

"Subaru-kun—!!"

A voice like a scream reached him.
He tried to lift his face toward where it came from, but his body no longer obeyed him. His balance gave out. The ankle that hadn't been crushed was now half torn off too—which, he thought, made for a nicely even distribution of wounds. He collapsed to the ground. Right before his eyes, a maw lined with fangs closed in. Fangs closing on his throat. Crushed, right before his eyes, caught between an iron ball and the earth. Blood scattered. Whose blood, his own, or—

His consciousness began to slip. He didn't know what would happen when it faded entirely.
Life was spilling out of him. What a stupid thing he'd done, he thought, even of himself. If he clung to this so hard that it undid the whole point of starting over, then this was putting the cart before the horse.

It hurts, it's agonizing, it's far away, he can't see, he can't hear, his life is wearing thin.
From the hole torn in his side, life spilled out like sand through an hourglass.

Fading, ending, everything—I am—

"Don't die, don't die, don't die—!"

A voice on the verge of tears.
Crying.

I am—.
