Arc 3 Chapter 14: "The Royal Selection Begins"

Subaru's all-out "Tehepero" missed spectacularly — utterly, gloriously, and completely irrespective of era-appropriate etiquette or the culture gap between worlds.

Sound vanished from the world, and even the passage of time grew hazy in the unbearably awkward silence.
And yet the reality remained that time had not actually stopped — Subaru's heart, shamelessly, kept pumping blood, and Emilia's expression in front of him kept tilting, moment by moment, toward worse. It would have been faster if he could simply die of embarrassment, but for a man who'd survived his days in another world purely out of love for his own hide, death by mortification or writhing agony was a distant prospect.

All he could do was endure the excruciating silence and wait for her reaction, still frozen mid-"Tehepero."
This was a contest. Yes, a contest.
Over these days in another world, he'd faced a handful of such do-or-die moments — in the Loot House, in the Witchbeast forest — and now, here in the throne room, in front of Emilia, having missed spectacularly, was just such a moment again.

Subaru couldn't move. He'd resolved in his heart not to so much as twitch until Emilia reacted — an admirably steadfast state of mind, all things considered.
Emilia, for her part, trembled her lips as if searching for words, her eyes wandering as she tried to gather her thoughts.
Then her lips pressed together, and as though she'd reached some conclusion, her violet eyes looked straight into Subaru's black ones. Feeling as though he might be pulled into them, Subaru braced himself to accept whatever her first words might be, no matter what—

"You were staring hard at my maid. Did something happen, halfblood?"

"Ohf—"
"Eh?"

An impact from behind struck Subaru as he waited on Emilia's words.
A terrifyingly soft sensation pressed against his back, and arms wound around his chest and neck in a decidedly sensuous lock. The orange-haired girl had risen on her toes behind him, her chin now resting on his shoulder, her face brought alongside his to face Emilia.

Naturally, from the girl's posture, Subaru realized exactly what that sensation against his back was, and his ears went red — but he immediately discarded the thought and twisted around.

"What do you mean, maid? Emilia-tan's going to get the wrong idea—"

"Oh? Not my maid, is it. Then what, pray tell, is the standing of a man who can stroll so boldly through the royal castle?"

"Ngh—!"

His hasty rebuttal was cut off by a spiteful smile.
There was no denying Subaru's overwhelming disadvantage here. It was an inescapable fact that his position in this castle was guaranteed only by her goodwill, and by clinging to it. Given the girl's capricious nature, if Subaru chose here to act against her wishes, he could all but see himself reported and thrown in a dungeon on the spot. Worst case, he might be dealt with then and there.

And yet, acting familiar with a girl other than Emilia, in front of Emilia, was simply out of the question. It wasn't about what Emilia would think — it was a matter of Subaru's own pride as a man.
So Subaru's situation was at an impasse. If he weren't an utter fool, reason dictated he go along with the girl's wishes and get through this unscathed.
But, just as with the circumstances of his intrusion into the castle, Natsuki Subaru was, in fact, an utter fool.

"There's no way I'm bowing my head to anyone in front of Emilia-tan."

"...Oh? How amusing. Then what do you make of yourself?"

The girl's expression, which had been merely teasing him, shifted completely, and she pressed the question in a tone that carried a chilling edge.
His answer here would translate directly into the value of Subaru's own neck. He couldn't see Emilia's face behind him, but Al, standing behind the girl, shook his helmeted head frantically, silently signaling *this kid's not in a joking mood right now!*
Sorry as he was to trample on Al's well-meaning gesture, Subaru drew a quiet breath and resolved to state his position plainly.

"I'm here in this castle to—"

"Well, we-e-ell, if it isn't Priscilla-sama. My deepest apologies for whatever inconvenience our household's employee has caused. That he'd go and get himself lost within the castle — how truly regrettable. Our sincerest apologies."

What interrupted this particular pointless flare of masculine pride was a familiar, honeyed voice.
Beside Subaru — who'd been standing there far too worked up — indigo hair now fell into place on his right. It was Roswaal, dressed in the formal attire of a court mage, wearing the same easy, breezy smile as ever.

Gesturing at Subaru with an air of theatrical, only-surface-deep reverence, he continued,

"I'll repay this favor in due time. Once again, our household's man has been terribly rude."

"So the head charlatan has come to intervene, has he. Well, no matter. Thanks to that jester and the halfblood there, I've been entertained well enough. —And it was a request from my retainer, besides."

At Roswaal's obsequious insolence, the girl gave an magnanimous nod, as if to display her generosity. Behind her, Al expressed his relief with a visible shudder.
Having found his countryman after eighteen years, only for the reunion to nearly turn into a "dead or alive" situation so soon, he seemed to have been thoroughly rattled, and said,

"C'mon, cut me some slack, Princess. My candle of life just melted like a popsicle in July..."

"Your metaphors are, as ever, mostly incomprehensible. In any case, the both of you ought to be grateful for the breadth of my generosity. Revere me, revere me."

"Yeah yeah, amazing, amazing, so very great."

He gave the girl's head a perfunctory one-armed pat, her neck wobbling back and forth in a display of affection unsettling enough that one almost wanted to intervene. And yet, roughly handled as she was, the girl's mood didn't seem to sour in the least. It really did seem to be, in all sincerity, her idea of affection.

In any case—

"Seriously, that was a real brush with death. You saved my ass, Roz-icchi."

"Well, I-I suppose I bear some of the responsibility myself. Tha-a-at said..."

Roswaal looked down at Subaru, who was letting out a sigh of relief, with a thoughtful expression in only his yellow eyes. Feeling uneasy under that gaze, Subaru asked, "What?"

"I honestly didn't think you'd actually be reckless enough to march into the castle for real. I wonder just how far you'll go on exceeding my expectations... well, no, I suppose that's a moot point to ponder."

Murmuring this, Roswaal gave a nod that seemed to satisfy only himself.
Subaru meant to press further at this cryptic attitude, but before he could, he noticed, out of the corner of his eye, someone else he needed to make amends to first.

"Ah, sorry, leaving you hanging like that. I've got a lot I want to say, but..."

"Why..."

"Hm?"

As Subaru tried to sort through his string of excuses and decide which order to deliver them in, Emilia made a murmur so quiet it nearly disappeared in her own mouth.
When Subaru asked her to repeat it, she shook her head once, then looked back at him properly and said,

"Not how... but why. Why are you here, Subaru?"

"Well, if I try to explain that, there's this reason that's kind of deep but also shallow, and kind of heavy but also super fluffy, that may or may not exist—"

"Don't joke around. Subaru, I told you, didn't I? Don't you remember?"

At her repeated, probing question, Subaru's throat made a small choked sound.
What Emilia meant, of course, had to be the promise they'd made back at the inn. Barely minutes after promising to wait, he'd broken it and come here — there were simply no words for how flimsy his resolve to keep his promise had proven to be.

The guilt of having broken that promise was inescapable. But at the same time, the fact that he'd come rushing here out of worry for her safety was no lie either.
How to blend those two things well, and spin it in a way that leaned heavily in his own favor — Subaru's mind raced, running through possibilities at full tilt.
And then—

"—Everyone is now assembled. The members of the Council of Wise Men will now enter."

As if cutting off the conversation between Subaru and Emilia, the doors of the throne room opened.
Subaru turned at the sound. Facing forward, the first to come through the great doors was the knight who'd exchanged words with the imperious girl before they entered — Marcos, he thought his name was.

Hidden beneath a helmet that had been fitted back over a face radiating sturdiness, he advanced with a dignified bearing. His figure was the very image of a knight straight out of a fairy tale, carrying a presence commanding enough that even Subaru found himself instinctively straightening his back.

Following behind him as he walked came a group of several elderly men.
All of them were dressed in attire befitting the occasion and their station, and their bearing and manner made it clear they were people of considerable rank. Each face bore deep wrinkles carved by long, heavy experience, and a dignified presence radiated from every gesture.

Among that group, what caught the eye most was the white-haired old man walking at its center.
His back showed no sign of a stoop, but his height was a head shorter than Subaru's. His hair, bleached pure white, had been grown long, and his beard, as if competing with it in length, had likewise been groomed to an impressive length. Given his short stature, there was a precarious sense that it might drag on the ground — but any such impression was cut short by the sheer, blade-like sharpness of his eyes.

"That there's the representative of the Council of Wise Men — in other words, with no king currently on the throne, the person with the greatest voice in all of Lugunica right now. Mikl-o-otov-sama, that i-i-is."

Roswaal offered this commentary in a hushed voice beside Subaru, who'd momentarily lost his voice at the sight.
*So that's it,* Subaru thought, convinced by the explanation. The man who so obviously wasn't ordinary at a glance was, indeed, no ordinary man in truth.
He recalled having heard, too, both of the representative of a kingless Lugunica, and of this body called the Council of Wise Men.

"If I remember right, that's the group running the country in place of a king or something?"

"Nominally, it's meant to be an advisory bo-o-ody, but currently, actual governance rests on the Council as well... though, to be fair, that hasn't really changed much even from back when the royal family was still alive."

Roswaal shrugged in response to the murmur. In short, it seemed the Council had effectively been left running the country almost entirely, dating back to the era of a previous king who'd lacked the capacity to govern.
That might well be true, but the fact that he'd casually let this slip to Subaru — practically an outsider — let alone doing so right here in the throne room, could only be called audacious.

Subaru had more or less suspected as much already, but it seemed his employer's loyalty to the crown had some rather soft spots in it. For someone involved at the very center of the nation, that seemed like it might be a rather troubling quality, but—

"If we start going down that road, we're gonna end up asking what my position even is in the first place. You sure this is fine? I can only play defensive midfielder, y'know?"

"If you could play defensive midfielder, I don't think we'd have ended up in that little situation just now. More importantly, we're over there, brother."

Al, who'd apparently caught the muttering with sharp ears, cut in, and Subaru turned his gaze where indicated. There, along the wall of the throne room, knights had begun forming ranks, and three figures giving off a distinctly different air from the uniformly armored knights had also appeared over there.

"Oh, isn't that..."

Naturally, his eyes were drawn to the three standing out from the rest — and, remarkably, all three turned out to be faces Subaru had seen before.
Al clapped Subaru on the shoulder, guiding him to head their way.
Seeking approval, Subaru glanced sideways at Roswaal beside him.

"So, that's how it is — is it cool if I go over there?"

"The corre-e-ect procedure would technically be to quietly usher you straight back outside right about now... but, it's amusing, so go on, follow the man in the helmet."

"Hold on, Roswaal."

At Roswaal, unchanged from his manner back at the manor, Emilia — likewise unchanged in her irritation with him from back at the manor — pressed forward to object to his glib words.

"Regrettably, this isn't the time to indulge Emilia-sama's very reasonable objections. To state the situation plainly: Subaru-kun will be taking his leave here... in the long, lo-o-ong-term sense, that is."

"Even so, if you let him stay in a place like this with me—Subaru will..."

"That debate will also have to wait, I'm afraid. Emilia-sama, the other candidates have gathered. Please, this way."

Where Roswaal's arm pointed, at the far side of the throne room, that "distinct air" had gathered.

The throne itself sat empty, its throne room seats surrounding it filled by the elderly men of the Council of Wise Men. Beneath the great dragon wings painted upon the wall, their descending forms were the very picture of loyal retainers attending a king who bore a dragon on his back.
And arrayed in orderly rows before those elders of the Council were beings who radiated a different kind of presence entirely — not the crafty gravity earned through years of experience, but a brilliance innate to them, one that resonated in others from birth.

Led by the orange-haired girl, three young women stood there in bold formation.

Standing at the center was a girl with her hand on her hip, chest thrust out arrogantly, wearing a sun-colored dress. Priscilla, he thought she'd been called before entering. Even under the weight of gazes from the very apex figures who ran the nation, there was no trace of wavering in her core of unshakable arrogance.

Standing to her right was a woman with long, deep green hair. The richly saturated color was near-black, but on closer look, it proved to be a glossy, lustrous green. She was tall for a woman, her eyeline only slightly lower than Subaru's — and yet the difference in leg length between them was considerable.
Her hair, gathered into one bunch, reached nearly to her waist, and her figure, rich with feminine curves, was wrapped in a dark navy uniform. It resembled the sentries' attire he'd seen around the royal capital, but the gold trim and design were different. Dressed in trousers, she was a beauty whose fine, dashing features suited the phrase "a lady in men's clothing."

Standing to Priscilla's left was a woman with purple hair, giving off an impression somehow more cheerful than the sharp, blade-like air of the woman across the way.
Her hair, too, had been grown to waist length, but whether naturally or not, it held a wave, and being fine in texture, it gave the impression of cotton-candy softness. Compared to the two beside her, this woman was somewhat petite, her comparatively modest figure wrapped in a white dress.
But what caught Subaru's eye most about her outfit was the stole draped around her neck, made from the fluffy white fur of some animal. It was a piece that struck straight at the core of Subaru's identity as a devoted "fluff enthusiast" — its sheer fluff-factor left him practically salivating.

Contrary to Subaru's sidetracked musing, Emilia bit her lip in frustration and said,

"We're absolutely going to talk properly later, okay?"

—telling Subaru this as if to press the point home, before running off to join the three other young women.
As Emilia, her silver hair dancing, took her place alongside them, Subaru — with more than a little bias — judged that while she conceded a step in terms of attire, in inner charm, she stood head and shoulders above the rest.

In any case—

"So basically, those are the Royal Selection candidates — the future queen candidates, huh."

Judging from Emilia joining their ranks, and from the exchange up to this point, Subaru concluded that must be the case. Honestly, he was surprised the royal candidates turned out to be entirely women — though he supposed if there'd been a man among them, that person would probably have been decided on outright, so in a way, he should have expected as much the moment he learned Emilia was a candidate.

Letting out an inwardly thoughtful breath, Subaru noticed the people around him beginning to move and hurried to join them. Roswaal joined a group in the direction opposite the assembled knights, alongside several soft-spoken men who looked every bit the part of civil officials. Subaru followed close behind Al, who strode along without a hint of hesitation, to a position slightly closer to the throne than the armored knights. And there—

"—So you came after all, Subaru."

A handsome redhead raised one hand, greeting Subaru's arrival with a smile.
Even after two weeks apart, there wasn't a trace of dulling to his radiance — the fine figure of a man that was Reinhard. His burning red hair swayed as ever, though today, unlike the light attire Subaru had seen him in down in the lower district, he wore a knight's uniform. What hadn't changed was the divine craftsmanship of what lay beneath, and the sword at his hip, etched with a dragon's claw mark.

"When I heard Emilia-sama would be attending, I thought you'd likely be the first one to show up."

"Long time no see, and also — what's with that absurdly high opinion you have of me? Last time you saw me, I was calling for help in a falsetto and pathetically committing seppuku, so I don't think I left much of a good impression."

"Not only did you protect Emilia-sama from a murderous blade, but in every other respect too, you kept choosing the best course of action. I'd say excessive modesty is a virtue in itself, though."

Said with a face utterly devoid of malice, there was nothing left for Subaru to say in response.
This was the trouble with genuinely good-looking men. Men favored by their looks rarely find themselves in the kind of hardship that twists a person's character, so as a result, they tend to end up with good personalities too. The "no truly handsome man is ever pointlessly evil" theory.
A handsome man with a twisted personality was, relative to the total population of handsome men, an exceedingly rare exception.
And of them all, Reinhard's air of good breeding stood head and shoulders above the rest — meaning he was a handsome man among handsome men. An elite among the handsome, one might say.

"If I pray to you, will some of that luck rub off on me? Namu amida butsu, namu amida butsu."

"I've never heard that incantation before, but nothing you do will work. I was simply born this way."

"Nah, this one's purely in the realm of prayer, don't worry about it. Oh, right, right. There's something I need to say before anything else."

As Subaru straightened his posture, Reinhard blinked his blue eyes in mild surprise. Subaru then bowed his head slowly toward him and said,

"Seriously, you really saved me back there. Or well, I'm sorry I never really got the chance to thank you, or figure out how to reach you afterward. I wish I could've said something sooner."

"I understand the circumstances on your end regarding that. It couldn't be helped. You were honorably wounded, and I've been rather busy myself lately."

Giving a small shrug, Reinhard nonetheless accepted Subaru's words of gratitude with a pleased curve of his lips. Even such a modest gesture managed to look picturesque — there wasn't even room for jealousy toward a truly handsome man like him.
And then, into the exchange between the two old acquaintances rekindling their friendship—

"Reinhard and Subaru-kyun knew each other? Su-u-urprising."

—broke in a voice, light as a feather, from a person with naturally catlike ears.
Today, dressed in a uniform matching Reinhard's, black-based with a dragon motif, was the girl who'd decorated her flaxen hair with the same white ribbon as before.
She was the one who'd visited Roswaal Manor a few days prior and delivered the message that had triggered this whole trip to the royal capital.
She raised a hand shaped like a knife-hand to her forehead in a salute-like gesture, and winked.

"It's only been like two days, huh? How've you been? Everyone's Feri-chan's been doing great."

"Didn't ask, but — anyway, good to see you again. Honored to meet you once more."

"Aw, wasn't the second half kinda stiff? Loosen up those shoulders a bit more, c'mon."

The girl reached out and touched Subaru's shoulder familiarly, urging him to relax. Reinhard watched the exchange between her and Subaru with a slightly surprised look, and said,

"Subaru. Are you and Ferris acquainted?"

"She's the one who came to confirm attendance for today, at Roswaal's place, where I'm staying. I figured she was just some low-ranking maid, but..."

Standing here now, dressed identically to Reinhard, she too must be someone whose station befit standing alongside the "Sword Saint." In fact, Subaru had already been told back at the manor that she was "the finest practitioner of water magic in the royal capital, without peer."
Piecing together bits and pieces, he was getting a strong sense that she held a fairly considerable rank.

"I see, so it was Ferris who went to Emilia-sama's. If my hands had been free, I would have loved to go myself."

"Like Captain Marcos would ever let the Sword Saint leave the capital for something like that. It'd be cruel to pile any more worries onto the captain — he's already got that old-man face even though he's not even thirty yet. Keep it up and it might start creeping from his face into his head too."

At Ferris's joking remark, Reinhard gave a faint, wry smile. Given the content, he could hardly laugh openly about it. Subaru, too, recalled Marcos's rock-solid face and privately thought, *dude's way too dignified for not even thirty.*
And then, further still—

"Would you mind introducing them to me as well, at some point?"

—called out the last figure present who gave off an air distinct from the knights.
Subaru recognized this person too, though honestly, it wasn't someone he had particularly good feelings toward. After all—

"That's the guy who kissed Emilia-tan's hand at the checkpoint."

A smug hand-kiss bestowed upon the back of Emilia's hand — a shameless act of poaching. Silently branding him "Pretty Boy" in his head, Subaru put on a smile on the surface and said,

"Ah, sorry my introduction's a bit late — I'm Natsuki Subaru, a small-time nobody. Please, feel free to file me away in some dusty corner of your mind, heh heh."

"That's an oddly self-deprecating tone all of a sudden, Subaru."

Reinhard gently chided him, then turned to face Pretty Boy.

"Please don't take offense, Julius. Subaru has a habit of testing people by acting like he's beneath them, in a way."

"Oi oi, could you not go pinning some crafty backstory on me like that? There's no hidden intent, I'm just doing my own kind of air-reading and this is how it comes out."

Reinhard's overestimation of Subaru had reached fairly considerable proportions. In his mind, it seemed Subaru's actions on that first day carried an outsized weight of merit. Being praised so much was ticklish, or rather — since he wasn't used to it — it made him suspicious there was some catch.
That said—

"And, well, that's how it is. Julius, elegantly, if you would."

There wasn't the slightest hidden motive behind Reinhard's attitude. Faced with a profile built entirely out of pure good will, Subaru sensed there was no point pressing the matter further.

"That happens to be my specialty above all else, Reinhard. Julius Juukulius, of the Order of Royal Knights. A pleasure to make your acquaintance. Yours as well, sir knight."

At the redheaded youth's prompting, the corner of his mouth easing into a smile, Pretty Boy — Julius — introduced himself thus to Subaru. He then turned his attention to Al, standing beside Subaru.
Al, addressed with the honorific title "sir knight," rolled his neck as if it itched, and said,

"Ah, nah, I ain't nothin' that grand, so cut the 'sir knight' stuff. I'm just, y'know, a lone wandering ronin type. Not like you fine upstanding folk."

His voice, muffled and low in energy, carried a clear intent to keep the other at arm's length — quite unlike his manner toward Subaru.
For Subaru, who'd gotten the impression Al was a fairly familiar, easygoing sort of person, this attitude of his came as a genuine surprise. Though, unfortunately, there was no time left to press the matter.
That was because, just as the round of introductions wrapped up, the man addressed as the Knight Captain, Marcos, raised his voice at almost the same moment.

"—Members of the Council of Wise Men. Candidates, all of you. Everyone is now assembled. If I may be so bold, I, Captain of the Order of Royal Knights, shall preside over the proceedings."

"Hmm... very well, please proceed."

Seated, hands clasped, giving a faint nod with his chin drawn in, was Miklotov, representative of the Council of Wise Men. Marcos gave a respectful bow to the old man's response, then furrowed his rock-hewn brow and continued,

"This summons today concerns a matter of grave importance for all those involved in the selection of the next sovereign — the Royal Selection. It is for this reason that you have been asked to journey to the royal castle, and that the members of the Council have likewise been gathered here."

His voice, resonant and clear despite not being especially loud, reached every ear in the throne room equally. Sensing a quality innate from birth, meant to be heard by others, present even in his voice, Subaru found himself listening intently without so much as a stray quip.

"The matter began roughly half a year ago — stemming from the fact that, one after another, members of the royal family, beginning with the former king, passed away. A kingless state is, for any kingdom, a crisis above all others — and for the Dragon-blessed Kingdom of Lugunica in particular, it bears deep implications for the 'Covenant.'"

The Covenant — presumably referring to some kind of pact the kingdom was said to have made with the Dragon. Given it was something he'd been told almost without fail from the very start of his days at the manor, his ears had heard quite enough of it by now. Still, what Subaru knew was only surface-level information, and the same held true regarding the details of the Royal Selection. This lecture, in that sense, was — if he had to say — a welcome one.

"Hmm. Maintaining the Covenant bears heavily upon the kingdom's continued survival. It is all the more a source of deep regret, then, that the royal bloodline was struck all at once by illness. We had no choice but to find, with all possible haste, a new 'Maiden' capable of communing with the Dragon."

"To that end, we of the Order of Royal Knights, under the command of the members of the Council of Wise Men, have taken up the task of seeking out maidens chosen by the light of the Dragon's Gem."

What Marcos drew from within his coat, resting in his palm, was a small emblem Subaru had seen several times before — the jewel-embedded proof of qualification for a Royal Selection candidate.

Marcos walked toward the row of assembled candidates, bowed to them, and said,

"If you would all, please present your Dragon's Gems—"

In answer to the call, the young women each raised their own emblems before them.
Every gem shone with a dazzling brilliance in their hands, each casting its own distinct hue of light scattering across the throne room.
A murmur of admiration spread among the watching knights, and even the elderly members of the Council let expressions of relief and something like joy surface among the deep wrinkles of their faces.

"In this way, all of the candidates present hold the qualifications of a Dragon Maiden. Having borne witness to this, we shall now, in accordance with the Dragon History Stone—"

"'Scuse me?"

Marcos, solemnly proceeding with the agenda, had been about to continue in that same weighty tone when a mild, unhurried voice suddenly cut in with a "hold on."
Turning around, Marcos found the one who'd called out to be the girl whose Dragon's Gem shimmered with blue light — the one in purple hair and a white dress.
Still holding her gem aloft, she tilted her head slightly, and said in a gentle drawl,

"I get that the captain wants to move things along nice and proper-like, but Ah'm a busy woman too, y'know? In Kararagi, we've got a saying — 'time is money'?"

For all her mild tone and easygoing look, the girl's demand landed with startling directness. Tucking her gem away, she clapped her hands together with a soft sound and continued,

"Rather than sit through stuff we already know, honestly, what Ah'd rather hear is the actual heart of the matter — why we were all called here."

Weaving her words in that distinctive lilt, she capped off her demand with a smile.
Marcos looked a bit taken aback by her manner, but it was Subaru who felt an even greater shock than that.
Amid this scene brimming with tension, even Subaru — of all people — had been keeping his mouth firmly zipped, determined not to say anything that read the room wrong. And yet—

"Whoa, hold up, is that seriously a Kansai accent?"

"Oh, brother's spooked too, huh. Apparently over in the western nation of Kararagi, that's just how they talk, or so I hear. Never seen it myself, but... guess the translation system of this other world just picked that as the language option or somethin'."

At Subaru's muttered comment, Al beside him echoed a similarly quiet observation.
As one might expect from a veteran of eighteen years since his own summoning, he'd apparently gotten over the impact of that particular culture shock long ago — her remark didn't rattle him nearly as much as it did Subaru.

Meanwhile, the throne room stirred with astonishment for reasons entirely unrelated to Subaru's. Amid the faint murmur threatening to spread through the hall, it was another woman's cool, composed voice that stopped it in its tracks.

"A reasonable point."

"—Crusch-sama."

The green-haired woman, arms crossed, gave a nod of agreement, chin drawn in. At her reaction, Marcos spoke her name, his brow furrowing slightly in puzzlement.

"For the head of House Karsten to say such a thing..."

"I understand the impulse to value formality, but it would defeat the purpose if doing so meant neglecting the matter at hand. We ought to address the reason we've been gathered here without further delay. Though, admittedly—"

The woman closed one eye, her gaze sweeping past Marcos to the elder statesmen of the Council beyond him, and continued,

"I have a fairly good idea what it is already."

"Ho, I see. As one would expect from the head of House Karsten. So you had already grasped the meaning of this summons."

Nodding at Miklotov's admiring words, the woman lifted her fine-featured face and addressed the true purpose behind this gathering, held before the throne. Which was—

"Indeed. —A banquet, is it not? We may be destined to compete against one another eventually, but for now, there is still far too much we don't know about each other. If we were to sit at one table, raise cups together, and speak candidly, our characters would naturally come to be known to one another, and—"

"No, that's not it."

Unable to help himself, the old man interrupted the woman as she solemnly laid out plans for what amounted to a drinking party. She made a face of surprise at this, then slowly turned to look toward Subaru's group.

"Ferris. This isn't what I was told."

"Oh no, come on now. Feri-chan just said there was a lot of food and drink being brought into the castle, so maybe they're throwing a banquet or something — that's all Feri-chan said, geez!"

"I see. My mistake, then. I forgive you."

It was an act of admirable open-mindedness, but the content of the exchange was clearly off.
Turning back to face forward, and taking the exchange just now into account, the woman let out a light sigh and said,

"In that case, please disregard everything I said a moment ago. It's rather embarrassing."

"Aw, Crusch-sama, you're just too manly!"

Ferris squirmed, hand pressed to her cheek. Reddening and wriggling her hips, she didn't seem the least bit bothered by having spread misinformation to her lady in the first place — if anything, watching this, it seemed almost deliberate.
It was difficult to judge exactly where the line for acceptable mischief lay, Subaru thought, keeping his composure even as he was swept up in the atmosphere of the hall. His train of thought was cut off by the sound of clapping hands once more.

"Even if Crusch-san backs off, my opinion don't change none. There ain't no point goin' over the surface-level stuff of the Royal Selection at this point — everyone here already knows it. Ain't that right?"

Leaning forward, the girl posed the question to the three standing beside her, as if seeking agreement.
At her question, the woman called Crusch nodded, Priscilla gave a small, bored snort, and Emilia trembled her tense lips and said,

"I—I think we should properly hear them out, though..."

"Sorry, but Ah wasn't askin' for your opinion."

But to Emilia — the only one who'd responded — the girl's attitude was outright dismissive.
At that flatly delivered pronouncement, a look of resignation surfaced on Emilia's face. Watching her downturned profile as she was pushed aside, something sparked in Subaru's head, and—

"Hey, you, what's with that attitude—" "Uwahaaay! I don't know jack about this Royal Selection stuff so I kinda just wanna hear what's next!!"

Just as Subaru stepped forward, about to unleash a shout of anger, a sturdy arm cut across him and held him back. The curse that had been about to burst from his mouth was drowned out and overwritten across the hall by a voice cutting over his.

It was Al who'd blocked Subaru's rashness. Drawing every eye in the hall onto himself, he fiddled with the metal fittings of his jet-black helmet with his one arm, tapping out a rhythmic clang of metal.

"Don't stare like that, it's embarrassing. I know I'm outta place here, but keep treatin' me like an outsider and I'll start bawlin' and makin' a big scene — grown adult and all."

As if mocking Subaru's own careful probing of the boundary for acceptable mischief, Al tap-danced his way lightly across the minefield with breezy nonchalance.
His behavior — less bold than simply having dropped all sense of tact somewhere along the way — left even Reinhard and the others visibly taken aback, but—

"Priscilla-sama. I was informed he is your knight... might I ask for an explanation?"

"Must I explain, when the lot of you love to talk so much yourselves? I merely spared myself the trouble. Repeated grumbling is no different from talking in one's sleep. And one oughtn't talk in one's sleep even while awake."

While only Marcos somehow managed to keep his composure, Priscilla stoked the fire with her characteristically imperious tone.
Every last one of them, without exception, seemed to be some manner of broken personality — it was almost troubling how, by comparison, Emilia's decency stood out so starkly. Though, as the exchange just now had made plain, it seemed she herself wasn't being treated all that decently either.
In any case—

"—That's one favor you owe me. Or, wait, is it two now?"

Al held up two fingers, turning his head toward Subaru, and inwardly Subaru felt a wash of gratitude toward him.
Had Subaru flown into a rage and raised his voice in that moment, he didn't think he'd have been able to handle the situation nearly as deftly as Al just had. Subaru didn't rate himself that highly.
Stepping forward and taking on the brunt of the criticism himself, Al had shouldered the burden on Subaru's behalf.

Despite being in a position much like Subaru's own, the fact that Al could carry himself with such confidence was likely thanks to having spent eighteen years building a solid foundation for himself. In another world, Subaru — who had yet to find anything solid to stand on — was simply built differently, mentally, from the start.

"Aaand there she goes again with the princess's selfishness. Give it a rest already, would ya."

"That I make lesser folk bend to my will is heaven's own decree. Rejoice, and dance upon my palm. Continue, Marcos. Explain to my knight how I am to become queen."

"For someone dumping the job on someone else, you sure got a lot to say about it. Ah won't say nothin' more."

The girl shrugged, throwing up her hands at Priscilla's attitude.
Seeing that the two of them had reached a shared conclusion, Marcos gave a small cough and confirmed with both Emilia and Crusch, "Is this acceptable?" Once he saw both nod, he continued,

"Well then, we've strayed a bit, but let us return to the matter at hand. —The reason all of you, bearers of the qualification of Dragon Maiden, have been gathered here in this way, stems from a prophecy newly inscribed upon the Dragon History Stone."

*Another term I don't know,* Subaru thought inwardly, a question mark forming in his head. Judging from the context, it was probably some kind of prophecy tablet, but the details remained unclear to him.

"The prophecy inscribed upon the stone tablet reads as follows: 'When Lugunica's Covenant is severed, a new bearer of the Dragon shall arise to uphold the Covenant and guide the nation.'"

"Hmm. What the tablet has shown is nothing less than heaven's own will. The Dragon History Stone, which has accumulated the same span of history as the kingdom itself since its founding, inscribes characters in response to events that determine the kingdom's fate. Given that its contents have shaped the course of history that followed, it is our duty to heed it."

At Miklotov's reflection, the other elders of the Council nodded solemnly as well.
It could, in essence, be interpreted as rather boldly punting the whole future course of the nation onto a prophecy tablet — but in a world where magic existed, a prophecy of this sort probably carried more than enough real power that Subaru felt he had no room to object.
In any case, following that prophecy, it seemed Emilia and the others had been gathered not exactly as candidates for king so much as for their qualifications as maidens capable of communing with the Dragon.

And then, having thought that far, a question suddenly surfaced in Subaru's mind.
Namely—

"If it's all about talking with the Dragon about the Covenant and stuff, then there's no real need to get wrapped up in this whole king business, is there? You'd just need something like a clan whose role is to be Dragon Maidens."

He voiced the question quietly, not to Al, but to Reinhard on his other side. Reinhard glanced down at him and, a wry smile rising to the corner of his mouth, said,

"What you say is reasonable, I think as well. And yet, it isn't so simple."

"Mind telling me whyyy?"

"The answer is that the Covenant for the kingdom's prosperity was made between the Dragon and a king. The Dragon didn't form a pact with just anyone capable of communing with it. It was because that person bore the kingdom as its king that the Covenant came to be formed."

A fragment of "the Dragon's Tale," a story Subaru remembered reading as a picture book, resurfaced in his memory.
Just as Reinhard's explanation indicated, that was a story of a king and a dragon. The weight of history handed down, unbroken, through the ages. Upholding that tradition, even to whatever degree possible, was presumably the policy shared by the Council of Wise Men and the Order of Royal Knights that had gathered Emilia and the others here. But—

"Even more reason, though, isn't it — wouldn't a hastily assembled maiden-turned-queen practically be poking the Dragon right where it hurts? It's not like some Ikkyu-san riddle contest — you can't just go 'oh, the royal family's gone, so we'll make a maiden into a queen instead' and expect that to fly, can you?"

"I've heard opinions on that matter clashed quite considerably. But in practice, the prophecy tablet handed down within the kingdom, the Dragon History Stone, showed that this tightrope-walk of a solution was heaven's will. And the members of the Council of Wise Men accepted that, and gave us our orders accordingly. I'd rather not think it will end badly."

Subaru gave a convinced nod in response to an explanation that, for something that sounded almost like a prayer, came across far too refreshingly composed.
There was no certainty — but there was also no other option. Doing one's best under those conditions was, it seemed, the current state of affairs for the Dragon-blessed Kingdom of Lugunica.
What judgment the Dragon itself, the one that actually mattered, would ultimately pass on the decisions of this kingdom's people — that, no one could know for now. Perhaps only whatever heavenly will it was that so grandly inscribed characters onto that so-called prophecy tablet knew the answer.

"Well, I get the gist of it. So it comes down to picking a queen out of the four of them. Why not just make all four queens and rank them or something, wouldn't that work?"

"The reason that wouldn't work is also inscribed on the Dragon History Stone... the Captain's about to read it now."

Subaru's proposal, offered as if it were a brilliant idea, was dismissed with a single shake of the head. As the true reasoning behind it was about to be revealed, Reinhard's blue eyes turned once more toward the center of the hall.
The rock-hewn knight, drawing every eye in the hall, turned back to face the four assembled candidates and said,

"And the prophecy continues thus: 'Among the five who may become guides to the new kingdom, one maiden shall be chosen, to stand before the Dragon in Covenant.'"

Hearing what Marcos declared resoundingly, Subaru could only give a low groan of understanding as he parsed it. If the prophecy tablet said to choose one, then there was nothing to do but choose one.
And then, having thought that far, one detail suddenly snagged in his mind.

"Five...?"

"That's right. Five."

Reinhard nodded in response to Subaru's question.
Glancing coolly sideways at Subaru, whose face was now full of suspicion, he continued,

"In other words — with only four candidates present, the Royal Selection hasn't even truly begun yet, as things currently stand. On that point, I'm afraid you'll simply have to blame the Order of Royal Knights' shortcomings — we'd been unable to locate the fifth candidate."

"Wasn't the population supposed to be like fifty million or something? Finding five people out of that seems brutal, and if you managed it in about half a year, I'd say you moved crazy fast, honestly."

In truth, he suspected that outside of a country like Japan — where communication infrastructure was established and a national census could be conducted all at once — meeting such a condition would be considerably difficult. Even in Japan, he imagined there'd be plenty of exceptions slipping through such conditions.
He didn't know how large or how capable the Order of Royal Knights was as an organization, but selecting even four candidates in such a short span was, in itself, quite the achievement.

"Well, if it turns out there's actually a whole horde of potential maidens just lying around able to make their Dragon's Gem shine, I'll blame you without mercy, you blind fool—"

"I can't claim we investigated all fifty million citizens, but we covered over eighty percent of them before arriving at these four. I would hope you might give the knights some credit."

He clearly had confidence born of the effort he'd put in. There wasn't the faintest trace of shame in Reinhard's manner. Given that, it was Subaru — who'd been the one getting carried away — who now felt a bit awkward.
But the situation continued to move forward, indifferent to Subaru's inner feelings.

"Because that fifth person could not be found, we have, in this way, known of the rival we must eventually compete against while being unable to do anything about it. A frustrating state of affairs, indeed."

"Truly. We keep gettin' summoned here for one thing or another, interruptin' my business dealin's more times than I can count. Just havin' a connection to House Juukulius alone's startin' to feel like it ain't worth the trouble."

"Anastasia-sama. Your candor is indeed a charming virtue of yours, but perhaps you might tone it down somewhat, in this setting..."

At Crusch's words, the purple-haired girl nodded in agreement. Julius, with a somewhat affected gesture, offered a word of gentle admonishment. The girl — addressed as Anastasia — turned her eyes toward Julius and said,

"What? You sayin' I'd get a better reception if I toned it down?"

"No. I merely meant that doing so would be more elegant."

"That standard of yours is the one thing about you Ah just can't wrap my head around, Julius."

Though hardly a persuasive piece of advice as far as admonishments went, Anastasia let out a sigh, the venom apparently drained out of her. She then turned back to face Marcos and said,

"Anyway, everythin' up to this point is stuff we already knew. Is the princess satisfied now?"

"Who can say. Al, did you follow all that?"

"Yup, got it. Thanks for goin' to the trouble — you too, miss Kararagi-accent over there."

At Al waving his one arm dismissively, Priscilla responded to Anastasia with "so he says." Anastasia closed her eyes at the sheer sloppiness of that master-and-knight pair. Then, looking up once more at the Council, she said,

"If there's more to say, hurry it up, would ya? Ah've got plenty else Ah'd like to be doin' after this too — Ah figure you granddads holdin' the nation's purse strings understand that much, don't ya?"

Her tone, though phrased as a bit of gentle counsel, came across as nothing but mockery, given everyone present understood full well she was addressing her elders. Subaru braced himself, half expecting the mood in the room to sour — but Anastasia, it seemed, was someone who'd been through enough scenes like this to know precisely where the line lay.
Not one of the Council members, despite receiving remarks so lacking in reverence, showed any sign of taking offense at what she'd said. If anything, Miklotov looked almost pleased as he said,

"My apologies to busy Anastasia-sama, but I would be grateful if you could bear with this old man's talk a little longer. For today, you see... is a day that will be etched into the kingdom's history."

At the end of that otherwise casual remark, Miklotov's voice suddenly dropped low.
Hearing it, the hall — which until then had begun to somewhat lose its initial tension — was swept by an atmosphere that left no one able to help but straighten their spine.
Everyone tried to divine the true meaning behind Miklotov's words, and everyone feared being the one to speak first. Amid that silence, it was the girl who carried herself without a hint of timidity who broke it.

"You said history moves, did you not. Meaning, that is precisely what this is — correct?"

At Priscilla's quiet question, Miklotov gave a small nod from the dais. He then turned his heavy-browed gaze toward Marcos, giving a look as the signal.
Receiving it, Marcos placed a hand over his chest, bowed, and said,

"—Knight Reinhard van Astrea! Present!"

"Yes!"

Marcos's voice suddenly rang out across the hall.
Beside Subaru, who flinched involuntarily at the sound, Reinhard responded as though he'd been waiting for the call. He then advanced to the center with a smooth, gliding gait, offering a bow to the four candidates before proceeding to stand before Marcos, the Knight Captain.

"Now then, Reinhard, your report."

"Yes, sir."

Stepping back, Marcos cleared the center of the space before the dais. Stepping forward into it, Reinhard drew every gaze in the hall solely upon himself, and, with an expression free of the slightest strain, faced the Council of Wise Men.

"Honored members of the Council of Wise Men. I, Reinhard van Astrea, Knight of the Royal Order, hereby report the completion of my assigned task."

"Hmm, then, let it be heard by all present."

At Miklotov's instruction, Reinhard bowed, then turned around, drawing himself up straight before the entire hall.

"The Dragon Maiden, candidate for the throne — the last, fifth person, has been found."

A murmur of astonishment spread among the ranks of assembled knights, and the expressions of the candidates each shifted in response to their own strong emotions — joy, boredom, bewilderment.
And Subaru, too, silently mouthed the words "knew it" at Reinhard's declaration. Given the flow of the conversation so far, it had been obvious this was where things were heading.
What he didn't understand, though, was Reinhard's own position in all this. Why had he been the one chosen to make this report? And then, thinking that far, Subaru noticed the common thread among everyone gathered in this spot.
Which was—

"Everyone here is connected to one of the candidates... which means—"

Al to Priscilla. Ferris to Crusch. Julius to Anastasia. And if Subaru himself counted as falling into that same category through his connection to Emilia, then Reinhard's position here, too, became obvious.

"Bring her in."

Though spoken quietly, the voice carried effortlessly over the murmuring of the hall, all the way to the great doors. At the sound, the sentries stationed at the entrance saluted, and the doors began slowly to open.

And from beyond those opened doors, accompanied by several women dressed as attendants, a single figure was ushered into the throne room.
At the sight of that figure, Subaru's brow furrowed in bewilderment.

A dress of pale yellow fabric. White gloves reaching to the elbow, a swaying skirt, and shoes with heels so high they looked plainly difficult to walk in, treading across the carpet. Her small, delicate frame looked thin enough to break if embraced, and her fine, golden hair suited the fragile impression perfectly.
And yet — her semi-long golden hair swaying, her red eyes burning with strong will, the owner of a mischievous set of fangs — Subaru knew her well enough to know she had nothing whatsoever to do with such fragility.

Struck by how transformed her appearance was, Subaru's mouth fell open in blank astonishment.
While everyone else, ignoring Subaru's reaction entirely, fixed their eyes upon the newly arrived girl's figure, Reinhard's voice rang out clear across the hall.

"The one I hold aloft as my sovereign — her name, is Felt-sama."

Frozen in shock, Subaru's eardrums seemed to echo with that voice, over and over, again and again.

—Thus began the Royal Selection, that would decide the future of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica.
