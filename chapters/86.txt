Arc 3 Chapter 10: "Joining Up and Parting Ways"

"At first I thought even a grimy place like this might have some charm in its clutter, but once the eye grows used to it, there's nothing left to catch it. It does nothing to relieve my boredom."

At the end of her upraised arm, the hem of her dress fluttered lightly as the orange-haired girl murmured this, her bored expression never wavering.

"That's quite the verdict for someone who's seen a back alley for all of five minutes. I doubt whoever designed the royal capital ever imagined getting reviewed as 'boring.'"

"Since the world exists for my sake, everything in it ought to exist to entertain me. I cannot fathom the mind of whoever settled on such a tedious cityscape. Royalty, it seems, has surprisingly poor taste. Perhaps that's why they've apparently gone extinct of late."

"Wh— you do realize you're saying that right on the king's doorstep, right...?"

Just listening to her was enough to set Subaru's nerves on edge, and even knowing full well no one else was around, he found himself shrinking down and glancing about.

The girl snorted at Subaru's caution — or rather, his cowardice.

"A dull reaction to a pointless worry. That you'd quail at something so trivial only proves you're just another common vessel, in the end."

"I'm well aware I'm a common, mediocre, run-of-the-mill little commoner, thanks. As someone who's already caught more than enough flak today, I'd rather not get dragged any further into your careless remarks."

"Now that's pointless. You ought to be proud to have played even a bit part on my glorious path. To find fault with that is nothing short of defying reason itself."

"I don't want to waste any more time and trouble my companion! I'm already totally out of time as it is, but the longer this drags on, the worse my standing gets! I'll be out of the picture entirely!"

The thought of Emilia looking at him with cold eyes sent a shiver of dread down his spine. And, strangely, a thrill too. He had no idea what that mysterious sensation actually was, but he had a vague sense that it was the kind of feeling he was better off not investigating.

So, if at all possible, he wanted to avoid keeping company with this girl's unshakeable ego any longer than necessary.

That was the sense of crisis Subaru felt. But the girl only curled the corner of her mouth, as if mocking his plea outright.

"Ridiculous. While in my presence, it's rude to spare a thought for anyone besides me. I too have a companion, as it happens, but I think nothing of having gotten separated from him."

"Maybe spare him a thought there. I can't help but pity whoever's stuck with you."

Being the attendant of a girl who was practically the embodiment of arrogance itself — even in this short time, Subaru could well imagine the hardships of this as-yet-unseen person, and sympathy blazed up within him.

No doubt it was some ill-starred, demure beauty, run ragged by her unreasonable young mistress. As someone with a weakness for the elegant, modest type, Subaru definitely wanted to check this person out.

"Too bad the only way to make contact with her is a package deal with this one, huh."

"Calling me 'this one' — you're getting rather full of yourself. Push my mood too far and you may just lose your life along with it."

"I'm sick to death of that kind of talk! How careful do I have to be to avoid tripping some death flag, dammit. There, there, cheer up now—"

He circled around in front of the girl walking beside him, waving a hand teasingly. At his attitude, she furrowed her brow as though something occurred to her, then let out a short, scornful laugh.

"Well, no matter. A fool only truly becomes a fool once he accepts himself as one. A wretch who can't even settle on his own place in things isn't worth dirtying my hands over."

"Are you dressing up an insult in smart-sounding words to make fun of me?"

"If I confirmed that as foolishness, it would only play right into your hands, would it not — whether you say it knowingly or not."

She said this in a tone of someone utterly enlightened, casting a mocking eye at Subaru walking ahead. Under that unpleasant gaze, Subaru became keenly aware of just how poorly they got along.

"Well, whatever, I guess."

She was a chance encounter to begin with — the two of them didn't even know each other's names, and once they reached the main street, they'd likely turn their backs on each other and never meet again.

He didn't possess the kind of universal goodwill that would have him endure discomfort just to get along with anyone and everyone. Forcing himself to like something he disliked was, to Subaru's mind, about the most detestable thing a person could do.

And yet, even having decided all that, the fact that he still had no intention of parting from the girl before reaching the main street said something about the kind of person Subaru was.

Between these two bickering companionably, the presence that might have smoothed things over — Old Man Rom — was nowhere to be found. He had refused to come out to the main street, firmly declining to accompany Subaru and the others any further.

The old man, whom he'd met again after so long, had kept up his easygoing manner, but Subaru could tell his standing wasn't a good one.

Subaru, whose grasp of both the royal capital and the slums ran shallow, had no way of knowing just how much value the Loot House held for the people of the slums. But from his exchange with the thugs earlier, he could guess that Old Man Rom's position as the Loot House's master had been fairly well known.

And that, having lost the Loot House, his standing had fallen in anything but a bright direction.

"Not being able to do a thing about it — yeah, that's a cold way to put it, I know."

Even sensing his situation, there was nothing Subaru could do for him. To begin with, Subaru was practically one of the parties responsible for the Loot House's destruction.

From Old Man Rom's perspective, Subaru was one of the detestable bunch who'd smashed his workplace to pieces. Even though it would've been understandable for him to lash out with misdirected resentment, the fact that he hadn't done so to Subaru proved he really was a rational man.

Precisely because he found the man's character admirable, Subaru wanted to grant his wish somehow, if he could.

At the very least, he had to confirm Felt's whereabouts — otherwise there was no way to repay him.

Following the route Old Man Rom had described would, he'd said, get them safely to the main street, even if it took a bit longer thanks to the detour. Given his position — flatly refusing any second encounter with the thugs — there was nothing to complain about, save for the fruitless back-and-forth with the girl along the way.

"—And while he was thinking all that, they'd finally reached the exit, huh."

Passing through the winding alley, he could finally see a sunlit street beyond the darkness. Judging by the unbroken stream of figures crossing it, they were about to come out onto Merchant Street or somewhere just as lively.

This agonizing stretch of time was finally coming to an end, Subaru thought, turning back to the girl.

"Once we're out in the open, you and I are complete strangers again. I've got my own adorable, adorable companion to go find, and I want to steer clear of any more trouble from hanging around you. Your own companion's probably out there frantically searching for you too, so quit wandering around and let yourself get found."

Prepared to be called heartless or cold-blooded, Subaru pushed her away rather firmly. Partly it was payback for having his pace thrown off the entire time, but more than that, he'd judged that unless he spelled it out this plainly, nothing would get through to the girl in front of him.

Perhaps because Subaru had stated it so plainly, the girl didn't seem to twist it to her own convenience this time. Even so, she didn't forget to flash a fearless smile.

"That you and I have nothing to do with each other hardly needs saying. You are, at most, one of my countless stepping stones. Bothering over a stone by the roadside is pointless for one such as I, who bears the whole world's favor upon herself alone. —Not that I care in the slightest, mind you."

Crossing her arms, accentuating her ample chest, she cast repeated sidelong glances at Subaru.

Feelings at odds with her words flickered through that gesture, plain enough that even Subaru, no reader of subtlety, caught it clearly.

"You're this arrogant and you're still scared of being disliked!? You're such a pain, you know that!"

"That I am loved by all creation is a law of this world. To defy it would be to stray from reason itself. If anything, I'm the one astonished by your lack of common sense."

"I've got plenty of confidence in my own lack of common sense, but not on your level!"

Whatever hostility had hung between them a moment ago vanished, and Subaru shot back with a loud retort.

If she'd just let him build up simple dislike and part ways, he could've forgotten her without a second thought — but no, she had to act like this. There was no denying she was a beauty to look at, and being shown these subtly endearing cracks made it hard for Subaru to know how to handle her.

For all her unshakeable ego and her habit of saying things with no regard for anyone else's feelings, she was, it seemed, afraid of being disliked.

—It was almost like watching someone else entirely, and it made his chest itch unbearably.

"Yeah, yeah, okay, okay. I don't hate you, I don't hate you."

Somehow all the fight drained out of him, and Subaru answered her with a wry smile. At that, the girl frowned, dissatisfied, and prefaced her reply with, "What is it—"

"Being loved is only natural for someone like me. Don't go stating the obvious."

"Aaah, yes yes, dat's wight~. Since you're a itty bitty baby on de inside, of course everyone wuvs you, just wike a baby~. Ababababa~"

This all-out silly face was a certified hit with every baby in the neighborhood. Ever since, the neighborhood moms burst out laughing every time they spotted him, even when he wasn't actually making the face at the time — which left him feeling weirdly hollow, honestly.

An approach bound up inseparably with that bittersweet memory. He'd been certain it would earn nothing but applause — but as for the girl's actual reaction—

"Hmph. I shall pity you, just a little."

Rather than laugh, she sighed, pressed a finger to her brow, and shook her head with her eyes closed. Without so much as looking at Subaru, frozen mid-silly-face,

"Knowingly or not, behaving that way has soaked right into you, hasn't it. It cannot count among your virtues. It's nothing but a flimsy shell to hide your weakness — unbearable to look at. Much like that face of yours, as it happens."

"You said something almost serious in the first half and then casually trashed my actual face in the second!?"

"If you insist on carrying on like that regardless, it's no concern of mine."

With that, the girl closed the subject, and made no further remark on Subaru's behavior after.

Having lost his chance to drop the silly face, Subaru, expression still twisted, tilted his head to show he didn't follow her point at all.

What exactly she was trying to say never quite got through to Subaru.

As her attitude and behavior made plain, she was a girl whose words, too, lacked any consideration for making herself understood by others.

Pressing further probably wouldn't get him a clear answer anyway. Once he thought that, Subaru gave up on saying anything more to her.

Or perhaps, by writing her off as simply incomprehensible, Subaru was avoiding having to actually face her words there and then.

Though, at least in this particular moment, no answer Subaru could have given would have gotten him any further response anyway.

Because—

"—Finally found you."

The moment they stepped out onto the street, a voice reached them, greeting the two of them.

Unlike the gloom of the back alleys, the main street was blazing under high, unrelenting sunlight. Emerging suddenly from darkness into the sun, Subaru narrowed his eyes as the light seared them.

As if bearing that brightness on her back, a girl wrapped in a white robe stood looking at Subaru.

Her fine brows drew together in something between weariness and worry, the silver hair hidden beneath her hood swaying lightly. Amid her lovely features, her lips curved into a relieved smile, and he could see her violet eyes tremble faintly with some indescribable emotion.

One glance at that careworn beauty was enough to tell him how much she'd worried herself over his whereabouts.

Even as he felt sorry for having made her so anxious, some part of his heart as a man was glad to be worried over. Love, it seems, is a complicated thing regardless of which side of it you're on.

"Ah, Emil—"

The moment they stepped onto the main street, the very person he'd wanted to see had shown herself.

Subaru's face lit up at reuniting faster than he'd expected, and he started to call her name — only for something off to cut him abruptly short.

Beside her, letting out a soft breath as she looked his way, stood another figure.

Judging by the sturdy build, it was unmistakably a man. The word *cuckold* flashed instantly through Subaru's mind, and—

"Hold on, hold on, what is this!? Who is this guy to Emilia-tan!?"

He stomped forward and instantly wedged himself between the man and Emilia. Backing up to shield her behind him, he thrust out a finger, glaring — and Subaru's expression froze as he finally got a look at the hated man's face. Then—

"Whoa, hey now, missy, missy. Your buddy's got a few screws loose, huh — he gonna be okay?"

The man shrugged and spoke to Emilia with easy familiarity — his voice somewhat muffled, a bit hard to make out.

Which was only natural, given his head was entirely enclosed in a jet-black full-face helm.

A helm concealing the whole face — on its own, that wouldn't have been such an eye-catching getup. This was the royal capital, after all, and the people passing along the street came in every variety.

Among them, things like full plate armor or mage's robes were common enough sights, given knights served as guardsmen even down in the city. A single suit of armor wandering around wouldn't have startled Subaru this much on its own.

Even so, what left Subaru dumbstruck by the man's appearance was, of course, that his outfit was even more bizarre for the capital than full plate armor would have been.

"Getting called a homewrecker in a girl's voice right off the bat — that's a first for me. Kinda exciting, honestly."

"You're one to talk, your fashion sense is pretty messed up too!?"

"And you've got a pretty rotten way of talking to your elders, y'know. I'll let it slide 'cause I'm a cool, easygoing old-timer, but with the wrong guy that'd get your head lopped clean off."

Facing a shrieking Subaru, the man on the receiving end of his pointed finger cheerfully patted the back of his own neck.

The strangeness didn't stop at his head — no, if anything, it was strange precisely because it did stop there.

A jet-black full helm covering his head. Below the neck, one would normally expect armor of matching color to continue. But in reality, below his neck was no armor at all — just light clothing made from some cheap, burlap-like fabric.

It didn't even look like it was woven from anything special — genuinely just plain, cheap clothes. Not just the top, either; the bottoms matched, and to top it off, his footwear was a pair of straw sandals.

The combination made absolutely no sense whatsoever.

For sheer outlandishness, Subaru's own tracksuit was plenty alien to this world too, but there was no doubt the man before him ranked pretty high on the obscurity scale as well.

Though he couldn't entirely rule out the possibility that this was standard fashion in this world—

"That's not a thing, right, Emilia-tan!?"

"You can relax, Subaru. I was just as surprised by his outfit as you are."

"Yeah, yeah, she was real surprised, real cute about it too. Though I was the one surprised when she went and offered to help me look for who I'd lost track of afterward."

Cackling, the man let the story of how he'd run into Emilia slip out without a second thought.

Hearing that, Subaru shot Emilia a flat, narrow-eyed look.

"Emilia-tan, your soft heart is a huge virtue, but you really oughta be pickier about who you show it to. You know why poisonous mushrooms have such garish colors? It's them warning everyone, 'I'm poisonous, I'm dangerous, eat me and you die' — heading off the damage before it happens."

"Whoa, hey now, the only way to take that is that you think I'm some kinda dangerous character. That's kinda harsh, don't you think?"

"Somebody who looks as sketchy as you would get reported on sight back where I'm from, where overprotective parenting is basically a national pastime. They'd call an emergency assembly at every elementary school in the area."

It'd probably get to the point where just walking down the street on a weekday afternoon would get a neighborhood notice passed around.

Whether it was Subaru's own bias that made him feel like all that excessive vigilance didn't actually do kids much good in the end, he couldn't quite say.

Arriving at that useless little observation, Subaru turned back to Emilia.

"Anyway, I'm always telling you to watch out for cars and men, right, Emilia-tan? Especially men — they're wolves, so flashing an unguarded smile at one is a bad idea... are you mad?"

"I'm just wondering whether, after telling me all that, you actually remember the things I've told you too. Really, no ulterior motive at all."

Stirred up trouble for himself, Subaru thought, wanting to bury his face in his hands over his own slip of the tongue.

But mercifully, any further lecture was cut off right there — and not by any of the three people currently in the conversation.

"Hmph. Waiting for me right where I'd end up — quite thoughtful of you. More commendable than I expected, Al."

"...Honestly, I can't shake the feeling it was pure dumb luck, but pointing that out and pissing off Her Highness sounds like a hassle, so I'll just go along with it. Yeah, that's exactly right!"

The man — called Al — laughed at the girl who stepped forward and declared this so imperiously. Then he ruffled the orange-haired girl's head roughly with his palm and pulled her over beside him.

"Real strange coincidence, but seems like the person you were looking for, missy, and the one I was looking for turned out to be traveling together. Might be some kinda fate tying us together, huh."

"I choose to believe that was just the red thread of fate between me and Emilia-tan getting reeled in, and that there's no connection whatsoever between our two duos!"

"Never short on backtalk, this one."

Al let out a muffled laugh at Subaru's response and waved the hand that had been petting the girl. Ever since he'd appeared, all his gestures had been carried out with that right hand alone.

Which made perfect sense — the man before them was missing his left arm entirely, where one ought to be.

A one-armed man in a jet-black helm, dressed in clothes far too flippant for such an intimidating appearance.

Judging by his voice and everything about his appearance besides his face, he was probably more than a decade older than Subaru. And yet he didn't give off the impression of being his senior, likely because his attitude was every bit as flippant as his clothes.

Put kindly, he was easy to warm up to. Put unkindly, he was a grown man who couldn't sit still.

"Wait, hold on, you're her companion!? What happened to the standard-issue elegant beauty who's always run ragged and troubled by her!?"

"Life's not that convenient. Settle for an old man, would ya. —Missy?"

To Subaru, who couldn't hide his dissatisfaction at how things had turned out against his expectations, the man brushed it off with easy nonchalance, then turned to Emilia with a puzzled look.

It was the man's manner that finally, belatedly, made Subaru notice something off about Emilia.

She'd stayed silent, not joining the conversation at all, and something about it was strange.

She pinched the edge of her hood as if casually hiding her face, trying to slip behind Subaru's back and block herself from view.

There was only one person present she could possibly be hiding from. He didn't even need to work it out from where everyone was standing — it was obvious at a glance.

"What, staring at me so intently. A beauty you'll come to miss once you're apart from it, I imagine. I am, admittedly, a sinfully perfect work of the gods, but staring silently is quite rude, you know."

"Sorry, but if it's eye candy you're after, my side's stacked pretty well too. ...Looks like we've both found who we were looking for, so I'd say it's about time we split up."

Subaru answered curtly to the girl — the very one Emilia was trying to hide from — before steering the conversation not toward her, but toward Al.

He didn't know why, but Emilia was clearly hesitant to make her presence known here any further. If so, respecting that was the choice Subaru made. And for that, it made more sense to negotiate with whoever seemed capable of understanding, rather than whoever clearly wasn't.

Whether Al picked up on Subaru's inner intentions or not was unclear, but he didn't say anything to particularly contradict him.

"Yeah, that works. I get it — dealing with a princess like her wears a guy out."

"...I feel for you, seriously. No joke."

"Handle her right with a little grown-up patience and it's not so bad. Wouldn't ask that of some kid her own age, though — that'd be cruel."

Al shrugged at Subaru's fairly sincere words and lowered his gaze to the girl. He couldn't see the eyes behind the helm, but the mood was close to that of a father watching over a beloved daughter.

It's probably not a bad relationship between them, Subaru thought, vaguely sensing as much.

"Well, we're headed this way... you two?"

"Then we shall go this way too."

"...Then, we'll go the other way, us."

"Then we shall go the other way as—"

"You're a pain, seriously, did you go and fall for me!? What are you, some pushover love interest!?"

"Obviously a joke, honestly, how tiresome. Boring men meet boring ends."

Declaring this with a bored air right to the very end, the girl walked off with stately dignity, her attendant in tow. There wasn't a shred of hesitation in her back, and, having wanted this himself, Subaru still found it annoying to be cut off so cleanly.

So Subaru packed one last bit of payback into a parting shot at the departing girl.

"Hey, catch, Your Haughtiness."

"Such insolence toward me. I shall order Al to take your head—"

The girl, turning back mid-threat, widened her red eyes slightly.

Arcing through the air toward her, thrown with poor form, were two Appa that Subaru had tossed her way. The two fruits sailed in a gentle arc and landed neatly in her hands.

Once he'd confirmed she'd caught them properly, Subaru grinned.

"There you go, Appa of friendship. Next time some shady grown-up dangles an Appa in front of you as bait, don't go wandering off after them, all right?"

"I was not lurking in the back alleys for some foolish reason like that, I'll have you know."

"You're locked into that role in my head now, permanently. Anyway, just take it."

Pushing the fruit on her with that thoroughly unreasonable logic, Subaru took Emilia's arm and turned his back on the two of them. Just before turning, he found himself regretting not simply putting an arm around her shoulders — she might not have refused him just now. Having regretted it, he settled for convincing himself that holding hands was enough to be satisfied with for the moment.

And so, as the two of them hurried off, one last thing reached them from across the street—

"—Thanks for helping me look, missy!"

A muffled voice, calling that out with real gratitude behind it.

* * *

After parting ways with the haughty girl and her mismatched-looking guardian, Subaru walked along the street a while before coming to a stop.

"Hey, so, Emilia-tan. Those two are gone now — think we can talk?"

Checking their surroundings, Subaru addressed Emilia, who had likewise come to a stop. She'd stayed silent the whole way here, and with her hood pulled down deep again, she wasn't even showing Subaru her expression.

Subaru fretted over whether he'd said something during the exchange with the girl earlier that had put her out of sorts. To that Subaru,

"...Um, sorry, Subaru. I was just, a little spaced out."

Emilia blinked as though suddenly coming back to herself and murmured this.

Subaru breathed a sigh of relief just to hear her say something at all, and quietly tucking that relief away inside his chest, he played the fool.

"You can space out in front of me as much as you like, y'know? Gives me a chance to do all sorts of this-and-that to you that I can't say out loud."

"Can't... say out loud...?"

At Subaru's words, fingers wiggling suggestively, a ripple suddenly rose in Emilia's violet eyes. Seeing those big eyes waver with that ripple, Subaru tilted his head and went on,

"Yeah, the kind of thing words just can't capture. But someday, Emilia-tan, you'll find yourself utterly captivated by the movements of my Golden Fingers, and eventually you'll be turned into a body that can't live without me... huh, is it just me, or does the back of my hair feel like it's freezing!?"

*'I don't think it's just you.'*

A faint chill drifting through the air physically cooled the back of Subaru's neck. At the same moment, echoing in his mind, came that forced telepathy he'd experienced firsthand not even an hour before.

Keenly feeling the quiet anger of the unseen gray kitten — Emilia's guardian — Subaru gestured wildly, breaking into a cold sweat.

"Th-that said, y'know! I'm a red-blooded Japanese man, a valiant warrior through and through, so I'd never do something so awful without consent. First we oughta be in a suite with a proper night view, make sure we've told each other exactly how we feel — that's how it's gotta go, right, right!?"

At Subaru's words, veering into thoughts fit for a maiden, came the sense of an unseen someone sighing.

Looks like he'd managed to dodge punishment somehow, Subaru thought, setting down a different kind of relief from before. To that Subaru, Emilia pressed her lips together slightly,

"Subaru. —That girl just now, how do you..."

"Huh, what, what, Emilia-tan, are you jealous? Is this a whole 'getting a little green-eyed' kind of development? For real? Two whole weeks of struggle — is this the day my efforts finally bear fruit—"

"—Subaru."

Subaru cut Emilia off to voice his own wishful thinking. Cutting Subaru's words off in turn, Emilia called his name in a firm tone.

Her expression was serious, her violet gaze carrying something close to intensity, and even Subaru sensed something was wrong and stopped joking around.

"Wh— what? Emilia-tan, why the serious face, what's..."

"Please, don't joke around right now. Subaru, how do you know that girl—"

Emilia was trying to press him about something. Shaken by this demand from her, he still had to give some sort of satisfying answer.

—That rare, earnest resolve of Subaru's,

"Finally found you! You gave us a real runaround!"

was drowned out by a rough, savage roar that overwrote their whole conversation.

Startled, Subaru looked around and froze in shock.

The street, which had held at least some passersby a moment ago, was now surrounded by a group of men who looked every bit like thugs.

At the head of the pack stood a face Subaru knew all too well — Ton, of the nonsense trio Ton-Chin-Kan. No sign of "Chin" or "Kan" at his side today, but Ton looked down at Subaru and the others, frozen in place, with a smirk.

"You made a real fool outta me back there, huh. Figured I'd pay you back for it, so we went and hunted down you and that little girl."

"...Rounding up your whole crew for payback over one little argument — man, your rotten streak is really disappointing me. I thought you were the kind of man who, no matter how much you got mocked, no matter how frustrated it made you, still cleaned up his own messes. Took responsibility himself. That's what I always believed about you...!"

"Don't go saying stuff that hits me right in the chest! What the hell do you even know about me!?"

Listening to the man spit curses, Subaru quietly took stock of their surroundings.

Blocking both sides of the street stood roughly fifteen or sixteen men. Every one of them held some inconspicuous weapon — short clubs, knives, that sort of thing — and they'd apparently done a decent job clearing the area of bystanders too. There wasn't a single civilian in sight who might get caught in the crossfire.

He figured a report, or guards noticing the disturbance, would bring help eventually, but whether some conveniently-timed hero like Reinhard would show up in time was, honestly, a gamble.

Running was difficult, and waiting around for someone else's help wasn't realistic either.

Accordingly, the most effective way to break through this situation was—

"Pathetic as it is, relying on Emilia-tan and Puck really is the best move here, even I gotta admit...!"

*'It really does sound pathetic, but I don't think it's so bad that you can admit your own helplessness without the slightest hesitation.'*

Puck, having overheard, praised Subaru's refreshingly shameless reliance on others' strength.

Unsure whether it was sarcasm or not, Subaru nodded grandly at the remark, then stepped, almost dancing, in front of Emilia.

"Anyway, that's how it is, so let me give you all a heads-up first. Fair warning: it's you guys who'll regret it if you jump us."

"Can't you count? I get wanting to look cool in front of a girl, but you're the one who's gonna end up in tears when this goes bad, pal."

"Don't jump to conclusions. The one who's gonna wreck you all isn't me — it's the cutie standing behind me. Serves you right!"

"Hey, you! At least try to look cool in front of a girl yourself!?"

Letting Ton's roaring bellow wash pleasantly over him, Subaru turned back to Emilia with an "All right," gesturing with his arm at the men surrounding them.

"Once I decide to rely on someone, I go all in. So, Emilia-tan — go ahead and clean 'em up, nice and quick, would ya? Heck, turn the whole lot of 'em into ice sculptures and we'll throw a Lugunica Snow Festival while we're at it."

*'You're saying some pretty scary stuff when I glance into your head like that. ...But it doesn't look like that'll be necessary.'*

Puck murmured this to Subaru, who'd been fully prepared to conjure a street lined with ice statues and christen it something like "Eternal Force Blizzard Street."

Just as Subaru voiced a puzzled "Hm?" at his words—

"—I followed Subaru-kun's scent here, and what in the world is all this commotion?"

came a line that sounded almost unsettlingly obsessive from somewhere overhead, and a blue-haired maid dropped down at almost the same instant.

Spinning vertically through the air on her way down, Rem held down the hem of her skirt and touched down with a boom that sounded like a sound effect — waving away the dust cloud with one arm, drawing every eye on the street to herself, she said—

"Now then, Subaru-kun. Isn't there something you'd like to say to Rem?"

"Rem, seriously, you're the ultimate maid who always scratches exactly where it itches! I'm falling for you all over again!"

"Praised that much, I actually find myself at a loss."

Amid the thugs thrown into confusion by Rem's sudden entrance, the usual Subaru-and-Rem back-and-forth carried on today just as normal.

Rem flushed and squirmed at Subaru's applause. It was Ton who, veins bulging at their exchange, made the first move.

Rem happened to be standing directly between Subaru and Ton. The tough-looking man grabbed at her shoulder on pure impulse, snarling—

"Hey, Miss Maid, you're way outta place here. Those brats over there got some business with us—"

That threat, delivered with every ounce of menace he could muster, vanished into the realm of dreams before he ever got to finish it.

Ton, seized by the collar by Rem, was slammed headfirst into the ground on the spot. His body, upended against the pavement, slowly toppled sideways, eyes rolled back white, not so much as twitching.

Probably, most likely, surely, he wasn't dead. Probably.

"Those who threaten the safety of Subaru-kun and Lady Emilia — that is Rem's judgment of them."

Rem's voice dropped low, her face settling into the expressionless mask of work mode.

Even so, the fact that she'd put Subaru's name first said something about how her priorities weren't quite sorted out properly.

Sensing all this, Subaru felt a twinge of sympathy for the thugs who had visibly flinched. Pressing his hands together in his mind, he offered them one small mercy—

"Rem."

"Yes."

"Don't kill them, okay?"

"Understood. Three-quarters dead it is."

With a firm seventy-five percent kill rate locked in, Rem began moving, slow and deliberate.

Some lunged at her in desperate abandon. Some turned to flee. Some simply crumpled to the ground without even understanding why.

—Upon every last one of them, her retribution rained down in equal measure.

"Whoa, amazing."

Watching people go sailing effortlessly through the air, Subaru let that slip out, stunned.

His mind was entirely fixed on the resolution of the commotion unfolding before him, holding onto a stillness cut off from the chaos around him — and so, toward the violet eyes gazing at him,

"—Subaru."

he failed to notice even a trace of that murmur, pleading as it was.

* * *

Meanwhile, trouble was spreading just as much for the girl and Al, who had parted ways with Subaru and the others.

After walking a while with their backs to Subaru's group, they were ambushed in a spot where the crowds had thinned out slightly — the method identical down to the letter. Here, the two men Subaru had dubbed "Chin" and "Kan" had shown up, and counting them, this side actually had a slightly larger number.

Compared to Subaru's group, the girl's fine attire had probably drawn more of the thugs' interest. Or perhaps she'd simply collected more of their anger, plain and simple.

"So, that's how it is — you're gonna get hurt a little. Just a little, yeah, just a little."

Laughing in a vulgar tone, Chin ran a lecherous gaze over the girl's figure.

A similarly base gleam lit the eyes of the men surrounding her, and what they intended to do with her once she was caught was obvious without a word needing to be said.

And as for the girl on the receiving end of those lustful stares—

"...Hm. Sweet and tart. So the inside really is Appa, no mistake. That rules out the possibility that fool from earlier just painted the fruit red as a joke. How surprising — so Appa are red, after all."

"Aaah, c'mon, princess, you seeing what's happening here?"

The girl, slicing the Appa in her hand with a dagger and bringing pieces to her mouth, paid the men not the slightest mind. As the thugs, noticing this, grew all the more furious, Al spoke up as if voicing their feelings for them.

Amid the men uniting in a silent "Yeah, tell her," the girl looked up sharply at Al, who'd spoken to her, and said—

"Say plainly whatever it is you're trying to say. I despise being roundabout."

"Fine, I'll say it plain, then. —There's two Appa there, so shouldn't one of 'em be mine?"

"Hah, ridiculous. Listen well — both Appa the fool threw were caught by me. Therefore both belong to me, in full."

"There's two of 'em, meaning split between the two of us — that's just common sense."

Sighing wearily, Al shrugged, as if asking the men around them for agreement.

But of course, there was no way the men would give Al the answer he wanted, given he'd just betrayed their expectations to begin with.

"Ohh, look at you all, getting all murderous. Can't even take one playful little joke, the whole lot of you lined up together? Pretty pathetic."

"I was not joking in the slightest. I am not giving up the Appa. Both are mine."

At the girl's stubbornness, a wry, amused air leaked out from behind Al's helm.

At this exchange, the thugs' patience finally seemed to hit its limit for good.

Weapons in hand, radiating open hostility, they began closing the circle inch by inch. They seemed to have noticed just how strange Al looked too, but factoring in his shabby getup below the neck and his missing arm, they'd apparently judged that, all together, he'd be no match for them.

It was a formation that looked well-practiced at ganging up on a lone target. Praising the maneuver with a sigh laced with irony, Al turned his head toward the girl.

"So, Your Highness. What does the world want done here?"

"My choice is, by definition, the world's choice. Seems you understand that much, Al."

"More or less."

Nodding in satisfaction at Al's words, the girl took another bite of the Appa, her cheeks relaxing at the sweet tartness, an angelic smile carved onto her lovely face.

And then—

"I happen to be in a good mood right now. —So, you may leave them alive."

She said this as a matter of course, with all the innocence of an angel plucking the wings off a gnat.

Hearing this, Al turned back to face the men. His hand swept behind him to the small of his back, gripping the hilt of the broad-bladed greatsword slung horizontally there.

Slowly, with a rasping sound, the blade slid free of its sheath.

And to that as background music—

"—Aye, ma'am."

a grin the color of blood was born, savage and vivid, somewhere behind the jet-black helm.
