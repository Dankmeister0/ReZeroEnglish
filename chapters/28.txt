Arc 2 Chapter 4: "Boot Camp in Another World"

Wiping away his tears, Subaru rejoined Emilia, and this time, after passing through a corridor that actually had an end to it, he found himself led out into the mansion's garden.
Incidentally, the twins apparently had chores to attend to, and had scurried off partway there.

"Man, this place really is huge. The mansion's one thing, but this isn't a garden—it's practically a field."

A rich mansion's garden—the kind of place that shows up again and again in manga and anime, the setting for standing buffet parties and the like. Letting out a breath of admiration at the scene, straight out of one of those, Subaru wasted no time and immediately launched into some knee bends.
Watching Subaru fall into rhythm, Emilia, beside him, gave him a puzzled look.

"That's a strange thing you're doing. What is it?"

"Huh? Don't you people have the concept of warming up? If you don't loosen up your muscles here and there before moving your body, you'll end up with a torn ligament out of nowhere! A ruptured Achilles tendon! Stuff like that."

"Hmm, can't say I've seen much of that. But it's true that moving suddenly without warming up makes you prone to injury."

"So people in this world don't warm up, huh. Well, can't be helped. Guess I'll just have to teach you, won't I! A time-honored warm-up routine passed down in my homeland!"

Perhaps swept up by Subaru's overflowing confidence, Emilia flinched slightly but went along with it. "O-okay. Just a little, then."
Subaru had her line up beside him. Once the two of them stood side by side, backs to the mansion and facing the sun, he took a great big breath, and—

"Radio Calisthenics Number Two—! Da-da, da-da-da-da-da♪"

"Wait, seriously, what?"

"Reach your arms out front, and stre-e-etch upward—! Follow me, follow me!"

Scolding a bewildered Emilia into compliance, Subaru sang the nationally famous Radio Calisthenics a cappella. At first Emilia imitated him with a dubious expression, but gradually she seemed to get into it, throwing herself into the exercise with a serious look on her face. She saw it through all the way to the final deep breath, and—

"And to finish, raise both hands overhead—Victory!"

"V-victory..."

"Alright, that's it. Not bad for a first try. I hereby award Emilia-tan the title of 'Novice Radio-ist.' Keep at it! Fight-o!"

Thus began Subaru's new life in another world as the transmitter of Radio Calisthenics Number Two. Emilia, his commemorative first disciple, let out a sigh with a faintly unimpressed look at his enthusiasm.

"Whatever you might say aside, it's true the exercise itself was legitimate. I can feel the mana circulating cleanly through my body."

"Feel free to spread the word. Just make sure you spread the song and lyrics correctly, though."

It only worked with that music and those calls. That much he wouldn't compromise on.
Overwhelmed by Subaru's unusually serious attitude, Emilia nodded.

"Um... it was da-da, da-da-la-la-la♪, right?"

"Wrong! It's da-da, da-da-da-da-da♪!"

For a while the two of them went back and forth saying "da-da, da-da-la-da" at each other, until it was finally settled by Subaru writing the lyrics down on paper.
Having arrived at that solution, it suddenly occurred to Subaru that it wasn't certain whether his own script would even be legible on this side. For now, at least, it was already confirmed that he couldn't read this world's writing.

"I'll check on that later, I guess. All I can write is Japanese, a little bit of English, and... gyaru-moji, more or less."

Gyaru-moji, a skill acquired at the end of a surprisingly earnest bout of time-killing. The fact that he'd never once had occasion to use it made it, in Subaru's book, a pretty delicious waste of a skill.

"Still, you're pretty fit, aren't you, Subaru."

Emilia murmured that abruptly at Subaru as he rotated his wrists and ankles, working his joints.
Being looked over so intently from head to toe, even though she'd already seen him stark naked for treatment purposes, he found himself blushing all over again at being scrutinized like this.
The ultimate humiliation from ten minutes ago came rushing back too, and Subaru scratched his head.

"Ah, well, somewhat. I'm a shut-in, so I figure I should at least keep in shape."

"That, um, 'shut-in' thing, I don't really get it. Aren't you from a fairly good family, Subaru? Didn't you train in martial arts or something?"

"Nah, I'm seriously from a totally ordinary middle-class family... where'd you get the idea I'm from some noble house? Was I giving off some kind of elegant, aristocratic air?"

"You definitely give off a *peculiar* air, that much is certain."

Well put, Subaru thought, throwing his hands up in a playful gesture.
Whereupon Emilia swiftly seized both of his raised hands. Caught off guard by a girl's fingers touching his with such unexpected force, Subaru's throat froze into a strangled "gah."

"These fingers are part of it, but it's also the look of your skin and hair. These are hands that have lived far too differently from common folk. And the way your muscles are built doesn't look like it came from manual labor, either..."

As she poked and prodded at his palm, Subaru, blushing, found himself convinced.
Being pointed out things about his hair and skin that hadn't come up before made him keenly aware of his own obliviousness.
With the conversation turning to an appearance that couldn't simply be chalked up to being a mere foreigner, Subaru's mind spun dizzyingly. Meanwhile, Emilia continued:

"Black hair, black eyes. That's a common trait among the drifters from the south, but you're here in Lugnica in that state. I didn't see any, but didn't you have attendants or something? Even those clothes of yours were made of some material I've never seen before... So, am I right?"

Emilia smiled triumphantly at Subaru, who had fallen silent.
Feeling something stirred by the bewitching air that matched her beauty, Subaru weighed her words and nodded solemnly.

"If it's a matter of right or wrong, you're completely wrong, but... how do I put that without hurting your feelings?"

"If I'm wrong, just say I'm wrong outright! Otherwise it's just me embarrassing myself!"

"It's fine, fine, fine. I've been rattling off my own ignorance left and right too, so don't worry about it. Let's just embarrass ourselves together, nice and cozy!"

"I don't recall us having deepened our relationship enough that I wouldn't be afraid to embarrass myself together with you. This isn't getting anywhere, so I'll just ask you straight out—what are you, Subaru?"

The question came flying at him, stripped of all shame or pretense, and Subaru let out a "hmm" as he mulled it over.
He could just honestly say, "I'm a freeloader who got summoned to another world, my bad," but in this kind of summoning story, saying that out loud was basically the gateway to getting branded a "guy with something wrong in the head."
In a two-dimensional world stuffed full of convenient plot devices, even a crazy person probably wouldn't get thrown out—but looking at the seriousness on Emilia's face right in front of him, a bad answer here seemed liable to get him turned into an ice sculpture, at the very least.

"In that case, I think Delusion Pattern B is probably my best bet here."

"Muttering to yourself like that is reeeally off-putting. Do you even intend to answer?"

"I do! Totally do! But unfortunately, I don't actually have that answer on hand. ...Because I've got amnesia, see, so I don't know a thing about myself."

"But you introduced yourself as Natsuki Subaru."

"Crap! Sloppy work! Objection raised without even a follow-up question!"

"So, no intention of giving a proper answer, huh. —Well, if you've got your reasons, I won't pry."

Emilia let Subaru off surprisingly easily as he clutched his head over his own shallowness. Leaving behind a single "well then," she drew a green crystal out from within her clothes.

"Oh, that's—"

"It's a spirit crystal—a vessel a spirit's body can dwell in. You already know about Puck, don't you?"

"The gray cat who dozed off at the crucial moment, right? He probably doesn't even know about my heroics afterward, since he was asleep."

It was the vessel of the spirit who'd vanished partway through the battle to intercept Elsa.
This was the first time he'd seen it in this world, but he'd caught a glimpse of it during the first loop.
The green crystal suddenly began to glow, as if reacting to Subaru's insult, and—

"Sorry to disappoint, but I heard all about it from Lia after the whole commotion was properly settled. I wasn't sleeping the whole time, you know, Subaru."

Light spilling out from the crystal gathered together, gradually forming a small outline.
A head took shape, a torso emerged, limbs were fitted on, and then it was covered in fur. In a matter of seconds, a small, bipedal cat had appeared in the palm of Emilia's hand.

"Yo. Morning, Subaru. Nice morning, isn't it?"

"It's been a pretty eventful night into morning for me, actually. An endless corridor, the need to pee, and after overcoming all that, Emilia-tan turned my body into one that can never get married..."

"Don't phrase it in a way that makes it sound so bad."

Reproached like that, Subaru gave a vague "wei-ay" in response. Emilia closed her eyes as if giving up on him, then looked down at Puck in her palm.

"Morning, Puck. Sorry for making you push yourself yesterday."

"Morning, Lia. But I think yesterday was more my fault. I nearly lost you. I couldn't thank Subaru enough for that, really."

Puck looked up at Subaru with his round eyes, tilting his small head.

"I ought to thank you properly. If there's anything you'd like me to do, go ahead and say so. I can manage most things."

"Well then, let me pet you whenever I feel like it."

Against Puck's grand offer, Subaru shot back with an equally instant answer.
It might not just have been the speed of the reply but its content that was surprising. It wasn't only Puck—already round-eyed to begin with—whose eyes went wide, but Emilia's too, as she listened.
She reacted with a hint of flustered urgency.

"Wait, don't you think you should put a little more thought into it? He might look small and weak, but Puck's power is genuinely amazing, you know?"

"That phrasing bugs me a little, but yeah, it's true. I might not look it, but I'm actually a pretty great spirit. So you're welcome to be greedier with your request, you know."

"Hey now, to a first-class fluff-enthusiast like me, the right to pet a fluffy thing whenever I want is, in a sense, a reward worth more than a king's ransom. The Right to Fluff—it guides the human heart to greater heights, and purifies even the most ravaged of souls, fluff fluff fluff fluff fluff fluff—"

Even as he spoke, he exercised his newfound right, petting Puck to his heart's content there on Emilia's palm. The belly, the chin, and finally, the finishing blow—the ears.

"These ears are insane! I'm already head over heels for how fluffy you are!"

"Hmm, the amazing thing about you, Subaru, is that you actually mean it. I can tell because I can read minds, faintly, dimly."

Puck said this cheerfully, even as he was freely toyed with by Subaru's fingers.
Watching the two of them frolic, Emilia let out a clearly exasperated sigh.

"Honestly, trying to understand you is exhausting, Subaru."

"Giving up isn't good, you know. Life, and interpersonal relationships in general, are built on a spirit of mutual understanding. But don't you go thinking you can understand me that easily, either!"

"What is that? That's reeeally irritating."

He crossed his arms, turned his face away, and tried a tsundere move with just one eye. At Emilia's response—an utterly maximal display of displeasure—he firmly resolved to seal that move away for good.
With that, she retrieved Puck's body from Subaru's fingertips. Giving a light wave of her hand, she turned and headed toward the far edge of the garden.

"Well then, I'm going to go finish up my Oath... Subaru, um, could you go quietly pull some weeds over there or something?"

"Alright, I'll get real fired up and pull those weeds— wait, I didn't come all the way down to the garden to do that!?"

Laughing, "Just kidding, just kidding," she walked off, and Subaru watched her silver hair recede before lightly leveling the ground and dropping onto all fours right where he stood.
He'd already finished his warm-up, so all that remained was his daily strength training. Since he'd already skipped a whole day yesterday, it only made sense to do two days' worth.

A hundred push-ups, a hundred sit-ups, a hundred squats.
That was, more or less, the basic strength training Subaru had kept up even while holed up at home. Every now and then he'd add in some wooden sword practice swings on a whim, but for the most part, that was the routine.

He worked through the push-ups, tormented his abs, and broke a sweat with the squats.
After a brief interval, he ran through the whole cycle once more. It was a routine that wrapped up in a mere ten minutes or so, but Subaru had the almost foolish diligence to keep it up every single day.

"It's a little unsatisfying. If I at least had a wooden sword, it'd be a bit more decent..."

"Here you are, honored guest."
"Please use it, honored guest."

The sisters, appearing out of nowhere, briskly pressed a wooden sword into Subaru's hand.
He accepted it with a reflexive, formal "Much obliged," and was left momentarily stunned by the two of them vanishing again just as suddenly as they'd appeared. In any case, in his hand now was exactly the wooden sword he'd wished for.

Its shape was modeled after the two-handed swords common in this world, giving it a slightly different feel from a Japanese bokken. Still, at its core, handling it was much the same.
He gave it a light swing to test it out and judged the weight to be just right.

"Gotta thank those jack-of-all-trades maids. Alright then, time for a little swinging practice, been a while."

He took his stance and swung, took his stance and swung.
Subaru had only been in the kendo club during middle school, but he'd trained enough to earn a dan rank, at least nominally. Since he'd kept up the practice swings on his own afterward, his sharpness probably hadn't changed much from his active days.

"It's a fantasy world of swords and magic, so maybe this'll come in handy after all."

He threw himself into his practice swings, feeling sweat trail down his forehead and drip from his chin onto the lawn.
What came back to him was yesterday's clash at the Loot House. The sight of Reinhard and Elsa exchanging the dull glint of blades.
He pictured himself now, gripping a real two-handed sword, bursting into that scene.

"Take this, Elsa! Your reign of evil ends he—aaand I'm dead!"

He leapt in, traded a single clash of blades, and got his stomach sliced open.
Deciding that wouldn't do, he launched into a second round of mental training, but this time he wandered into the line of Reinhard's slash and got reduced to ash.
Conclusion: no matter how hard an ordinary person tries, they can't beat a cheat.

"Well, what a discouraging thought, all that effort for nothing... anyway."

He shouldered the wooden sword and took a short break. Catching his breath, he found himself curious about how Emilia was doing and glanced in her direction.
She sat on the ground in the green garden with her legs folded to one side, Puck perched on her arm, which rested on the ground, exchanging words about something.
He'd naturally assumed her conversation partner was Puck, but—

"Unless I'm seeing things, something's glowing around Emilia-tan."

Around the seated silver-haired girl, in roughly the space within her reach, he could see the air wrapped in a faint, hazy glow.
Drifting softly within it were lights of various colors, swaying faintly and fragilely, like the glow of fireflies.

It was a mystical sight, somehow fantastical.
A scene that made one instinctively hesitate to touch it with a human hand—a sanctuary that only those permitted by some supernatural presence could exist within. Faced with such a sight, Subaru said—

"Whoa, this is amazing. Don't tell me—are all these fluffy little things spirits?"

"—Eep!"

He'd barged right in, quite bluntly and without a hint of hesitation, and spoken to Emilia.
The startled cry had spilled from her lips, and the eyes that looked up at Subaru were damp with tears that had welled up reflexively from the shock.
And the fragile lights surrounding her caught the same wave of alarm—

"Ohh, they're panicking, they're totally panicking."

The countless lights swayed left and right as if flustered, trying to circle behind Emilia as though to escape Subaru's notice.
It felt, in essence, like scattering a flock of pigeons in a park.

"Honestly, you're something else, Subaru. Spirits are normally the kind of thing that takes a lot more courage to approach."

"Is that your way of implicitly saying 'you're a lucky guy for getting to pet me'? I know that already! I told you, I'm head over heels for you, pet pet pet pet."

On the arm of a still-frozen Emilia, he went at Puck's body again, this time from the back. In the course of his valiant assault, he casually savored the feel of Emilia's silver hair against his fingers, and, tilting his head at her continued silence, said—

"What's wrong? Stay unguarded like that and someone'll steal your badge again, you know?"

"Don't go poking at my sore spot! That's not it—you just startled me, that's all!"

Emilia scooped up her welling tears with a finger, chiding Subaru for his thoughtlessness.
The spirits bobbed up and down as if agreeing with her words. Watching their reaction—huh, they've got a surprisingly good sense of rhythm, he thought—Subaru said,

"Well, this is the first time I've ever seen spirits, so I got a little carried away. And from the looks of it, they didn't seem dangerous or anything."

"It was only fine because they're under my control. If you pulled that stunt on an inexperienced Spiritual Arts User, it could trigger a spirit rampage and... ka-boom, you know."

Emilia lowered her voice, trying to intimidate him, but partway through she apparently ran out of vocabulary, because what came out was "ka-boom." Having been listening earnestly, Subaru felt the anticlimax and looked over at Puck. "That's a bit much."

"Are those soft, fluffy little lights really dangerous?"

"Sure. For instance, I could turn you to dust this very instant, if I wanted."

"I'm so sorry for underestimating you, sir!"

He prostrated himself fully on the ground to express his apology.
Puck laughed, waving a small hand, while Emilia pouted just a little, dissatisfied at the difference in how she was treated compared to Puck.

"Why can't you turn that obedience my way, I wonder... Right, Puck?"

"Guess it's seniority."

Puck answered languidly, fiddling with his whiskers. Perhaps disarmed by his mild manner, Emilia let out a sigh, as if giving up on pressing the matter further.

- Title: 「異世界ぶーときゃんぷ」 is written in a deliberately childish/silly kana mix (ぶーと for "boot," きゃんぷ for "camp"); rendered straightforwardly as "Boot Camp in Another World" since the playful spelling has no clean English equivalent.
- "You definitely give off a *peculiar* air" (好奇な感じ) is a homophone pun on Subaru's 高貴な家柄 ("noble lineage," also read kouki) — Emilia echoes the sound but swaps in 好奇 ("curious/odd"), turning his boast into a jab. Preserved via italics/word choice rather than a literal pun, since no English homophone pair matches.
- ギャル文字 ("gyaru-moji") left transliterated with contextual gloss — it's a real Japanese decorative texting script (associated with gyaru subculture), not directly translatable.
- モフ/モフる ("mofu") rendered as "pet"/"fluff" throughout for readability; the repeated onomatopoeia (モフモフモフ...) is kept as "fluff fluff fluff..." or "pet pet pet..." depending on context.
- ぼかん (Emilia's word for "boom") rendered as "ka-boom" to preserve the deliberately childish, deflating effect described in the text.
</notes>
