Arc 3 Chapter 81: "Pursuit"

Subaru let out a short breath at Petelgeuse's grave marker, then turned his eyes away from the rest of the battle's traces.

Turning around, he was greeted by Julius brushing the dirt from his hem, and Mimi and Tivey, who shook their heads as they climbed down from Patrasche's back.

The mental corruption afflicting the brother and sister seemed to have largely cleared. The fact that its source had lost his life was probably the biggest factor. The mechanism behind the mental corruption remained frustratingly unresolved, but Subaru wanted to believe there would be no lingering ill effects.

"Coming from a guy who's had that bastard inside his own head once already, this doesn't carry much weight. ...But seriously, there'd better be nothing left, that's terrifying."

Petelgeuse had been a Spirit, and had once gone so far as to force a Contract into being.

A being that pushed a contract on you like some kind of high-pressure salesman had nothing going for it, whether in his original world or this one. Much less when that being was a madman who'd taken things as far as mutual slaughter.

"Mutual slaughter... huh. Not a phrase you'd ever use in everyday life."

Shaking his head, Subaru became aware of just how much his own sensibilities had been steeped in this other world. Whether that was a good thing or a bad thing, he couldn't say.

The strange sentimentality creeping over him was probably just the relief of having cleared a major hurdle. Thinking back, this loop that had begun in the royal capital had been one climax after another, the biggest since he'd been summoned to this world.

Having broken through to that final goal, it wasn't strange that some sense of emptiness would follow.

"Never thought I'd get all sentimental after taking down Petelgeuse. ...Snap out of it, me. Calm down, look up, and once you actually think about it, there's a mountain of stuff to do."

After all, the original biggest goal — making up with Emilia — still hadn't been accomplished in this loop.

It had started with their falling-out in the royal capital, followed by the alliance with the Crusch Camp and the subjugation of the White Whale. Then, tied up with the Witch Cult subjugation, there was the matter of having tricked the villagers with false information to get them evacuated. And on top of all that, the aftermath explanations that would follow — in exchange for the physically taxing event being over, there was now a whole slate of emotionally draining ones waiting.

But still—

"As long as nobody gets hurt or dies, that's a whole lot easier to deal with. Guess you really don't appreciate the value of peaceful, ordinary days until you lose them. Me, I was always Team Unchanging Daily Life Forever from the start, though."

In any case, he needed to head back to the village, regroup with the members of the subjugation squad, and discuss what to do next. Rem and the others headed for the Sanctuary and Emilia's group headed for the royal capital would need to be reunited in two separate efforts. If he dawdled, they'd both reach their destinations before he could catch up.

Walking across the rocky ground, Subaru turned his mind to it. Then his steps abruptly stopped, as though he'd noticed something. His gaze fell on a trail of blood — the path leading away from Petelgeuse's grave marker, the trail of his final crawling steps. And at the start of that trail—

"—the Gospel."

Whether it had slipped from the hem of his priestly robes, or been dropped at the very last moment as he reached for it, the Gospel lay abandoned there, its cover fouled with blood and mud.

Subaru walked over, picked it up, and flipped through its pages. As far as he could tell, nothing had changed since he'd examined its contents once before, in an earlier world. The characters forming the text within were still in some script he'd never seen, and the latter half of the pages were still blank, just as before.

Now that Petelgeuse had been defeated, there was no longer a way to question someone so close to the core of things — an Archbishop of Sin — and so:

"Guess this is about the only lead we've got left, huh... For now I'll hang onto it and see what Crusch-san and Roz-chi have to say."

Incidentally, the fact that Roswaal came second in that thought was simply a matter of trust levels. His absence during the crisis had taken its toll, and despite being on the same side, Roswaal's approval rating had dropped across the board. His future performance was very much anticipated. Though honestly, Subaru's real feeling was that it would be better if no situation ever arose that called for Roswaal to shine.

Wiping the grime off the Gospel, Subaru dragged his feet back toward Julius and the others. He'd suffered almost no direct injuries, but the phantom pain from having synchronized senses with Julius still throbbed dully throughout his body. Even if treated, since there was no actual wound to speak of, how long would the pain from this invisible injury take to fade? Just thinking about it was a little frightening — and in the middle of that thought, Subaru suddenly noticed Julius's expression harden.

"I'd like to say we've finally settled things, but we need to return to the village immediately, Subaru."

Before Subaru could even ask what was wrong, Julius raised his face.

His expression drawn tight and serious, in his hand — just as Subaru had one of his own, apparently just picked up — was a dialogue mirror, and if the connection hadn't broken, it should still be maintaining communication with Ferris.

Furrowing his brow at the ominous atmosphere, Subaru silently prompted him to continue. The knight nodded.

"During Ferris's interrogation, one of the Witch Cultists apparently said something concerning. The people we evacuated may be in danger."

And with that, he uttered words that threatened to shake the very foundation of Subaru's plans.


※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※


"Welcome ba~ack. Glad to hear you managed to kill the Archbishop of Sin without a hitch."

The one who greeted the four of them — having ridden their Earth Dragons at full speed back to the village — was Ferris, sounding thoroughly delighted despite the dangerous subject matter.

His carefree attitude made the tension of the moments before suddenly seem to evaporate, and Subaru let out a sigh. Still, this was just how he always was, so there was no point commenting on it now.

Turning his head around, Subaru surveyed the members of the subjugation squad gathered at the center of the village. This attack had been an ambush launched from a position of overwhelming advantage on their side. Even so, the fact that no small number of casualties had occurred hinted at just how unfathomable the Witch Cultists truly were. Still, the fact that there had been no deaths was Subaru's one saving grace.

"Everyone made it back, right?"

"I gave the signal, tora-tora-tora~. Made sure to crush all the other fingers besides the main one, too. Got a few prisoners, and that helped us find some stuff out, y'know~?"

Narrowing his cat-like eyes, Ferris glanced toward the edge of the village. There, some four people in black robes were bound and captured, including one of the earlier-captured merchants.

The prisoners all hung their heads in a state of blank shock, likely already having lost their mental equilibrium at Ferris's hands.

Feeling both reassured and unsettled by that skill in equal measure, Subaru frowned as he silently asked Ferris with his eyes for the details of the summons. Ferris, still with his eyes narrowed, dipped his chin.

"So, from what I got outta the ones we caught, seems like there's a separate unit. Not hidin' out around here — more like keepin' watch over the highway?"

"Whoa, hold on! Don't tell me — that's not another one of Petelgeuse's Fingers, is it!? Forest, cliff, and the merchant caravan makes ten spots! Ten fingers, that's the end of it, right!? Don't tell me you're gonna pull some childish thing about counting toes too."

"Where he himself was ain't a finger, it's the main body, so if you're countin' fingers, we've only crushed nine spots, don'tcha think~?"

Ferris pushed back against Subaru's argument. Subaru's voice caught in his throat with a "guh," recognizing even as he did that the logic, sophistic as it sounded, wasn't something he could simply deny.

Letting his gaze wander, Subaru turned his mind toward what to do if that were the case. If the corpse buried beneath that grave marker hadn't been the last one—

"Wow, that's a stubborn look you've got there."

"—Huh?"

"Feri-chan just told Subaru-kyun something that should've flipped his whole world upside down, y'know~... but instead of givin' up, you went straight to figurin' out what to do about it. Mm-hmm, I like that attitude."

Winking and grinning at Subaru, Ferris wiped away his earlier demeanor entirely. Subaru blinked once at the sudden change, then flushed red.

"Don't tell me you were messing with me!?"

"Nah, that's not it. That's expectin' too much of me. If I told you just now was all Feri-chan bein' a jokester, I get wantin' to cling to that so you don't gotta worry anymore, but I don't think that part of you is a good thing, y'know~."

Stung right where it hurt by the teasing tone, Subaru clamped his mouth shut, the discomfort of being outmaneuvered again rising in him. But swallowing the urge to argue back, he shook his head instead.

"Anyway, what you said before — that's actually true, right?"

"It's true. Ah, but it's not exactly about fingers, y'know~. Seems like the 'Sloth' Witch Cultists are all wrapped up. The ones watching the highway aren't 'Sloth' affiliated."

Frowning at Ferris's words, it took Subaru only a few seconds to grasp what he meant.

If there had been contact with Witch Cultists outside of "Sloth" in this battle, there was only one possibility.

"'Gluttony' Witch Cultists—!"

"If the White Whale's 'Gluttony,' then it bein' out there alone woulda been weird, right~?"

At Ferris's answer, eyes narrowed, Subaru clenched his fist in realization.

This was from an earlier loop. When the White Whale had appeared and he'd broken through the fog, Subaru had had a near-miss encounter with Witch Cultists. At the time, it was Otto, who'd been traveling with him, who'd been attacked, and the fact that the location didn't match up with Petelgeuse's Fingers had nagged at him.

—In other words:

"There's a Witch Cultist hiding somewhere on the Lifaus Highway?"

"Yep. Whatcha gonna do?"

At Ferris's tilted-head question, Subaru put a hand to his mouth.

If the current assumption held true, the situation wasn't fully resolved yet. But he couldn't let panic cloud his judgment over it. Though, more to the point—

"That's a situation we should've already accounted for, to some degree. That's what the insurance was for, wasn't it."

"Ohh, what a naughty smile. Where'd that weak, adorable little Subaru-kyun go~?"

Throwing both hands up in a banzai gesture, Ferris closed his eyes at Subaru's answer.

Ferris's reaction was proof that he felt the same sense of security about that "insurance" as Subaru did. The insurance Subaru had laid was—

"Why do you think I split off our biggest fighting force to Emilia-tan's group, knowing full well it'd make taking down 'Sloth' harder? I put resources aside exactly for a case like this."

As he spoke, Subaru scanned the faces of those who'd returned to the village.

Among them, the single most reliable fighter of the subjugation squad was absent — the Sword Devil, Wilhelm van Astrea.

Before the decisive battle with "Sloth" Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti, what Subaru had asked of his Sword Devil wasn't help as a blade in the fight, but a role as a shield to protect someone important.

From the start, he'd been wary of the possibility that Witch Cultists might be lying in wait along the highway or elsewhere. That was why he'd taken the utmost care to ensure no harm would reach Emilia and the villagers he'd sent fleeing toward the royal capital.

In the battle against Petelgeuse, aside from Julius the Spiritual Arts User, anyone who "couldn't resist mental corruption" was equally at risk of possession. It could be said that those vulnerable to mental corruption were, conversely, outside Petelgeuse's possible targets for possession — while they wouldn't be useful as combat strength, they were also safe allies who couldn't turn against them.

Conversely, people like Wilhelm who had resistance to Petelgeuse's mental corruption risked inviting his possession. That was why Subaru had chosen to keep everyone but himself — anyone who could be a target of possession — away from the battlefield, bringing along only himself, who had a countermeasure against possession, and Julius, Petelgeuse's natural enemy.

"Do we even know how many Witch Cultists are lying in wait on the highway? Just the 'Sloth' affiliates numbered close to a hundred around here. Even Wilhelm-san can't take on that many at once..."

"I said 'watch,' didn't I? The numbers don't sound like much. If it's twenty or thirty, Vil-jiji and his aide should be able to handle it just fine, no problem, I think."

Subaru's worry had been a scenario where a horde of Witch Cultists lay in ambush, but that was denied by Ferris's words. To begin with, the White Whale alone, handling "Gluttony," had more than sufficed as a threat on that foggy highway. It was plausible that whatever subordinates — if you could even call them that — handled this kind of watch role wouldn't number very many.

"—If the matter could be settled with numbers that small, you wouldn't have rushed us back like this. Ferris, you shouldn't be so roundabout about it."

It was probably because Julius hadn't missed the way Subaru's relief had let his guard down. Having been tending to Mimi and Tivey behind them until now, Julius walked over, clapped Subaru on the shoulder, and voiced his complaint to Ferris.

At Julius's words, Subaru frowned in confusion. Ferris gave a small "as expected~" smile and said,

"I trust Vil-jiji's skill, and a handful of Witch Cultist attackers ain't somethin' worth worryin' about that much. In a pinch, even Emilia-sama could fight, so there's no anxiety in terms of raw power. Just, there's one li~ittle thing that's buggin' me, y'know~."

"What is it, this thing that's bugging you?"

Ferris made a small circle with his fingers, expressing the size of that little worry. He nodded at Subaru's prompting to continue, then stepped smartly aside, as though yielding the floor.

"From here on, let's have the person who actually noticed the important part tell you himself. So, without further ado, here's Otto-kun, who brought us this valuable piece of insight~."

As Ferris clapped and hyped him up, Otto emerged from behind him with an uncomfortable look on his face. Subaru's eyes widened in surprise at this unexpected appearance, and Otto bowed to him, saying "I'm sorry."

"Sorry for startling you. First of all, I'm glad you all made it back safely. If you'd all lost, I wouldn't be able to guarantee my own safety either, after all."

"Good to know you're honest with yourself, but... what'd you notice? Frankly, I already have a bad feeling about this given how you started."

Scratching his ear, Subaru felt a rising sense of dread. Otto, trying not to further stoke that anxiety, nodded while keeping his tone measured.

"A little while ago, I noticed that an acquaintance of mine was among the prisoners — Elgleed-san. He was... a Witch Cultist, right?"

"That old guy's name is way too cool. ...And yeah, your question's right on the money. If someone you know says it's true, well, this Ketty person was one too."

"It's about Ketty-san."

Hearing that an acquaintance had been thoroughly steeped in such a vile religion, Subaru felt a pang of sympathy for how heavy that must weigh on Otto. But Otto showed little interest in Subaru's concern, and instead stepped forward.

"I'm surprised — and it's a shame — that Ketty-san was a Witch Cultist, but that's a separate issue. —Ketty-san's Dragon Carriage is being used for the evacuation, isn't it?"

"—? Yeah, we're using it. Whatever the owner was, there's nothing wrong with the property itself, I figured. We're barely scraping by on the number of carriages as it is, so we couldn't afford to leave one idle."

"And the merchants' cargo that was loaded on the carriages was unloaded here in the village, and the villagers were put on board instead for the evacuation... is that correct?"

Subaru responded with an "uh, yeah," a question mark still hanging over his expression at Otto's insistence on details. Otto put a hand to his chin and murmured "I knew it," closing his eyes as though something had been confirmed. A brief silence followed, and just as Subaru began to grow impatient, wondering what was going on—

"Among the cargo that should have been unloaded from the carriage, something that ought to be there is missing."

"Something that ought to be there... meaning?"

Running his eyes over Otto's words, Subaru tried to recall the merchants' cargo that had been crammed into the village storehouse. Most of the merchants had originally planned to deal in ironware in the royal capital, so the bulk of it had consisted of swords, armor, and other weapons and equipment. If there was anything unusual, it would be Otto's own oil, taken into custody last.

"So what about it?"

"I told you I raced ahead of everyone to get here first, right? Which means I heard about the moneymaking opportunity in the Mathers domain at the same place as everyone else. Naturally, I'd also been in contact with Ketty-san as of the day before yesterday. At that time, something Ketty-san should have had loaded in the carriage is nowhere to be found among the cargo."

"Something she should have had loaded...?"

From the way Otto was speaking, Subaru realized the conversation had taken an ominous turn.

To press home the point and drive Subaru toward certainty, Otto gave a small nod and said:

"—A large quantity of fire magic ore. Enough to blow seven or eight Dragon Carriages to bits without a trace. It's gone missing."


※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※


—The Dragon Carriage of the deceased Ketty was being used to transport Emilia's group of evacuees.

That was the conclusion Subaru's mind reached after searching his memory following Otto's report. The carriages that had lost their original drivers had each been assigned a driver chosen from among the subjugation squad.

He didn't remember which carriage exactly, but Wilhelm had also been assigned that role, so there was no mistaking that the Sword Devil was riding in one of them.

"Is it definitely true that the magic ore was loaded? Maybe it was unloaded somewhere along the way..."

"Would a Cultist meant to launch a surprise attack on the villagers and Emilia-sama scheduled to evacuate really do something like that? Subaru, optimism and wishful thinking are not the same thing."

Julius, eyes narrowed, chided Subaru's answer as his lips trembled with anxiety. Running his fingers through his hair, Subaru honestly apologized, then pressed Otto for further details.

"There's no magic ore in the storehouse. I checked myself, that much I'm sure of. Otto, did you see the actual thing inside the carriage before you got here?"

"Unfortunately, quite clearly. They were cheap ones, not even fist-sized, but there were enough to fill a burlap sack. If they were all set off at once, I think it would be more than enough to accomplish the goal."

But no such burlap sack had been found.

It was precisely because Subaru and Julius had confirmed the carriages' safety that they'd felt comfortable reusing such a suspicious tool, one that had belonged to a Witch Cultist.

"I confirmed there was no magic formula planted... but the possibility of simply rigging magic ore directly onto the carriage itself never occurred to me. That was my failure. I'm sorry."

"It's not your fault. I should've been the one to catch it."

Shaking his head at Julius's apology, Subaru bit his lip at his own tremendous oversight.

In a world where using magic was taken for granted, if Julius the Spiritual Arts User was the one to think of magical methods, then it should have been Subaru who suspected physical ones.

Having overlooked that, Subaru couldn't help but acknowledge that a situation he thought he'd already overcome had run aground again.

"A Witch Cultist attack isn't a problem as long as Wilhelm-san's there. There's no way anyone could physically break through him. But a booby trap is a different story. There's nothing to be done about that if nobody notices it."

"Of course, it's possible we're worrying over nothing. It's possible that, upon suspicion alone, the magic ore was actually distributed among the Cultists individually, and nothing was rigged onto the carriage at all..."

Otto's words weren't meant to comfort Subaru's mounting anxiety. Rather, they presented a possibility precisely so that Subaru himself could refute it. Riding along with that intent, Subaru shook his head and said, "No."

"I've body-checked the prisoners and the confirmed corpses. No magic ore, not even a Gospel to speak of. ...The magic ore is rigged onto the carriage."

That degree of spiteful, obsessive nastiness was exactly the kind of thing Petelgeuse would pull off without fail. In fact, Subaru knew for a fact that Petelgeuse had once blown up an entire carriage along with his own body. At the time, he'd assumed the trick was rigged into the man's own body, but—

"It wouldn't be strange for a similar device to be rigged onto a Cultist's carriage...! Ferris! Can we fly the carriages out now and catch up with the evacuation group headed toward the royal capital!?"

"That's gonna be pretty rough, y'know? The royal capital group left about an hour and a half ago... and since their whole goal is to evacuate without bein' found, they probably ain't racin' at top speed, but still."

To begin with, the level of secrecy imposed differed between the "Sanctuary group," who couldn't afford to have their base location discovered, and the "royal capital group," whose advantage lay in the Witch Cultists not knowing that the threat of the fog had been eliminated. The Sanctuary group was giving maximum consideration to staying unseen, while the royal capital group's priority was clearing the highway quickly.

Catching up to them posed considerable problems as well.

"Again... is it happening again? After all this, am I going to..."

Was he once again going to be unable to influence the fate of someone precious to him, powerless to intervene?

Struck once more by the same helplessness he'd felt before, Subaru nearly buckled beneath it. But as he gritted his teeth against the despair bearing down on him again—

"May I say one thing, Natsuki-san?"

Otto raised a hand to interrupt the conversation, a serious light kindling in his usually mild face. Subaru recognized this change in his demeanor.

It wasn't from this world, but from an earlier one — the time he'd truly met Otto for the first time. Drunk on despair-fueled sake, Otto had worn this exact same face in the middle of a conversation with Subaru, right before a negotiation.

Which meant that right now—

"You got something you can bargain with me over, Otto?"

"I don't dislike a perceptive person. —Natsuki-san, I'm honestly at quite the edge of a cliff right now. The cargo I was carrying — I don't know if I can even sell it for pennies, having missed my window. I bet everything on this moneymaking opportunity for one last shot, and out of greed, I ended up missing the crucial moment — a total disaster. They say as long as you're alive you've made a profit, but I'm in no position to laugh about it."

Otto's circumstances were, to hear them, pitiful — more comical than tragic, really — but there wasn't time to dwell on it now. Subaru nodded, silently urging him to continue.

Otto took a small breath, blinked once, then raised his face again.

"Let's make a deal. If you agree to it, I promise to give it my all to get you to your destination — to catch up with the carriage that left ahead of us."

"You can catch up... you're saying? Leaving now, how!?"

"Before I explain that, I'd like you to give me your word first. That you'll agree to the deal. What I have to offer holds a great deal of weight even for me personally. I can't cooperate easily. Even if you tried to threaten me."

"I'm not gonna do anything as crude as strong-arming you. If there's a way, tell me. Whatever condition you set, if I can do it, I'll do it."

Grabbing Otto by both shoulders, Subaru pressed him for whatever condition he had in mind.

Having already repeated this five times, Subaru was confident that he'd achieved better results than ever before this time. Rather than throw all of that away, how much happier would it be to kick "giving up" aside and push for one last effort?

At Subaru's plea, Otto held his breath for a moment. For him too, this was a major moment — the condition he was about to offer could sway the rest of his life.

Complicated emotions could be seen racing through his eyes with tremendous speed in that instant. Overcoming that inner conflict, Otto spoke the condition. It was:

"I'd like you, Natsuki-san, as someone connected to Margrave Mathers, to arrange for me to have an audience with Lord Mathers. If possible, I'd be grateful if, as a reward for this achievement, you'd buy up my cargo of oil. ...At whatever price I name, how about it?"

Narrowing his eyes, Otto put on a merchant's face and spoke as though testing Subaru.

It was the basic groundwork of negotiation — throw out the maximum demand first, then extract concessions from there. Otto himself surely didn't for a moment think Subaru would accept a condition where he could name an outrageous price. Still, he judged that, given how dire the circumstances were, he could extract considerable concessions from Subaru.

There was no need for guilt or a pang of conscience in exploiting the situation. Use whatever situation was usable. For a merchant staring ruin in the face, nothing would be more foolish than letting a once-in-a-lifetime chance slip by.

Having steeled himself with that resolve, Otto waited for Subaru's next words.

And before him, Subaru swallowed, and said:

"That's all you want!? Fine, I'll buy whatever you've got, and if you wanna meet that pervert, I'll get you as many meetings as you want. Deal's done!"

"Eh, what, that's terrifying."

An echo of an earlier world's reflection — through a course of events Otto himself could have no way of knowing — Subaru's negotiation with him once again ended in Otto's favor.

Though whether he'd call this uncontested victory something to be happy about was another matter entirely.


※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※


"I'll have Ia accompany you. If it's true that magic ore has been rigged onto the carriage, Ia should be able to notice it. Make use of her."

So saying, Julius entrusted Subaru with one of the Lesser Spirits contracted to him.

The Spirit, glowing a pale red, circled above Subaru's head at Julius's instruction. Eventually, as though satisfied, it landed atop Subaru's head. It seemed to have decided that was its designated spot, settling in comfortably. The crown of his head grew steadily warmer.

"Hey, this doesn't run the risk of making me go bald, does it? Never going bald and never going fat is the single greatest goal of my entire life."

"Ia won't stay materialized for very long. Once she senses the flow of your mana, she'll blend in and go quiet... see, she's already out of sight."

Laughing off Subaru's concern, Julius pointed, and sure enough, the warmth atop his head was gone. Still, even though he could no longer see her, he could feel her presence nearby. So this was what it felt like to have a Spirit accompanying him — Subaru nodded, as though convinced, and said,

"Still, is this really okay? Lending me a Spirit and all."

"I don't want to regret sending you off without any expertise. I'd rather come along myself, honestly..."

Trailing off, a look of frustration crossed Julius's refined features. Subaru understood the source of that vexation. It was the person beside him, kneeling on the ground against his will, placing a hand glowing blue light against his body.

"Don't be silly, all banged up like this. Actin' tough doesn't fool me. And your mana's basically bone dry too. Used too many big techniques, didn'tcha?"

"I had the support of the buds. I barely used any of my own magical power. Though, given the state I'm in from that alone, it really drives home just how little talent I have."

"That's just you bein' sarcastic. Ugh, fine, whatever."

Sensing the Spirit's presence beside him, Subaru gave Julius, now focused on his treatment, one last confirmation. Julius dipped his chin and gave a short "yes," then said,

"I'm lending my dear child to a friend. I'd like you to show her some consideration, too."

"Could you not say 'friend' straight to my face while stone sober like that? I let it slide before 'cause the situation called for it, but I want to have a proper talk about that later."

Subaru answered gruffly at the awkward address. Julius let it pass with only a smile, and Subaru, unable to bear the awkwardness, looked to Ferris instead to cover for it.

"Leave the treatment of the others to you. Julius, and those kitten siblings too."

"Aye-aye, Subaru-kyun, you take care too, okay~? If Subaru-kyun died, I bet Crusch-sama would be so disappointed too... ne."

The way his tone dropped at the end of that was a little concerning, but then again, Ferris always had that streak of enjoying rattling Subaru's nerves.

Not taking it too seriously, and taking away only the reassurance from what he'd actually said, Subaru waved to the two of them and headed for the village entrance. There, Patrasche and Otto, hitching his own Earth Dragon to a carriage, awaited him.

"Well then, shall we go, Natsuki-san?"

"Yeah. Guide the way and everything else, I'm counting on you, Otto."

Climbing onto the driver's seat and sitting beside Otto, Subaru looked ahead. Beside Otto's Earth Dragon — known for its massive build — stood Patrasche's jet-black frame. Compared side by side, the difference in build was considerable, and it seemed worrying that the two would be able to pull the carriage together, but—

"The 'Divine Protection of Wind Evasion' works on Earth Dragons, so there's no problem with two of such different builds running side by side. Both are female, so from what I 'heard,' they don't seem to get along badly."

Perhaps reading Subaru's doubt from his expression, Otto, gripping the reins beside him, said this.

Subaru tilted his head with a "hmm" at the odd wording of "heard" that Otto had used.

"What's wrong?"

"Nah, just thinking this world's Divine Protections are amazing. I guess I should think of it like a kind of talent, but there's way too much stuff that goes beyond my common sense."

"You talk about this world's Divine Protections like they're someone else's business, Natsuki-san. And having a Divine Protection comes with its own hardships, you know. Especially my 'Word of Spirit' — I couldn't control it at all as a kid."

Responding to Subaru's admiration with a wry smile, Otto spoke of his own Divine Protection.

The "Word of Spirit" Divine Protection that Otto possessed.

Its disclosure and use were the content of the deal by which Otto had offered his cooperation to Subaru. The effect of the "Word of Spirit" Divine Protection was simple and clear: it was a Divine Protection that allowed one to exchange "words" with any being, regardless of who — or what — they were.

"So including Earth Dragons, you can hold conversations with animals. That's how you managed to race here to the Mathers domain by the shortest route, huh."

"I put quite a strain on my own Earth Dragon — Frufoo — too. Asked birds and insects and everything about shortcuts and rough terrain, and went all out with it."

Apparently he'd cut through paths that could barely be called paths at all to pull off arriving first at the Mathers domain. That the result of all that was getting caught by the Witch Cult, with a "congrats on first place," was nothing to laugh about.

In any case, going by his Divine Protection:

"Catching up with Emilia-tan's group ahead of us isn't difficult, then."

"No, it is difficult, you know? It's just that there's a decent chance, that's all. And to begin with, cooperating was the condition of our earlier deal, so whether it actually leads to results, and first of all whether the magic ore that might be rigged even goes off at all, that's all still unknown..."

"Catching up with Emilia-tan's group ahead of us isn't difficult, then—!"

"You can't just declare that with such a great look on your face, it's a lot of pressure, you know!"

Otto cried out at how heavy Subaru's repeated trust weighed on him, but there was truly no time to entertain that. Subaru wiped the emotion from his expression, let out a small, long breath, and said,

"I'm counting on you, Otto. You're the one I'm relying on."

"...That's really a killer line, isn't it. Damn it."

As though struck in the chest by Subaru's solemn words, Otto sighed in resignation. Then he gripped the reins firmly and gave the two Earth Dragons the order to run.

The carriage was pulled forward, and Patrasche and Frufoo kicked powerfully off the ground, and the pursuit of Emilia's group began.

"Argh, fine, I'll do it! I'll do it and hold it over you and squeeze you dry to the bone, just you wait—!"

Otto's desperate, half-crazed shout rang out into a sky where, muffled by the "Wind Evasion" Divine Protection, only Subaru could hear it.

Following the course of events described above, Subaru and the others raced down the highway in pursuit of Emilia's group.

The endless stretch of terrible roads up to this point had been a nightmare in its own right, an environment so relentless that recounting it would fill half a day with nothing but grievances against Otto.

Whether it was the reckless suicidal act of racing down a nearly vertical cliff, or crossing a decrepit, sagging suspension bridge that looked to have been abandoned to neglect years ago (which, in fact, collapsed the moment after Subaru and the others crossed it), through countless death-defying stretches, how many times had he resigned himself to a Return by Death?

Still, thanks to all that hard-won effort, Subaru and the others, now halfway down the Lifaus Highway, had made remarkably good time. It was the kind of miracle that, even if asked to repeat the same mad dash, could probably never happen twice, and yet here they were.

"If I don't convince myself of that, I'm never gonna be able to touch Patrasche again without being scared!"

Otto driving the carriage down such terrible roads was one thing, but it was Patrasche and the other Earth Dragon who, under commands relayed through the reins, had raced down a road of death. If the driver could make this level of performance possible, it was only natural to feel some anxiety about how to handle Patrasche going forward.

In any case—

"Let's cut through the forest on the left! That's the fastest way to the royal capital!"

"'Forest,' more like — there doesn't even look to be an animal trail!? Are you seriously sure this is okay!?"

"Why are you being quiet, huh!?"

Ignoring Subaru's shout, Otto steered the carriage toward the forest.

Left with no choice, Subaru clasped his hands together, praying at the very least for no direct hits from trees, and ducked his head down on the cargo bed in a defensive crouch.

The carriage jolted from the impact of running over tree roots, and the view turning green told him they'd entered the forest. Charging at full speed into a place riddled with obstacles seemed like nothing short of suicide, but for Otto, who could literally communicate with the Earth Dragons, it seemed like a routine feat.

If he was the standard for a merchant, was this a profession that demanded more guts than one would expect?

"—Natsuki-san!!"

As Subaru was lost in such musings, Otto's voice suddenly rang out. The urgency packed into that voice made Subaru sit up and scramble hastily to the driver's seat.

"What's wrong, what happened?"

At Otto's alarmed cry, Subaru held his breath and looked around as well.

But in a forest where the entire field of view was covered by a mass of trees, that vigilance did little good. Reading that thought on Subaru's face, Otto nodded with an "understood," and said,

"I'll swerve and get us out of the forest right away. Natsuki-san, keep watch on the rear—"

Just as Otto was about to change course and give instructions to the Earth Dragons, Subaru held him back with a hand. His face, still turned toward the rear as instructed to keep watch, was fixed on the scenery being left behind the carriage.

Trees were snapping and flying through the air, the forest dying tree by tree under the violent onslaught.

Destruction was erupting, the ground churning up right behind the path Subaru and the others had just torn through, and "it" was charging straight toward them, utterly indifferent to the surrounding devastation.

"Floor it, Otto. —Whatever you do, don't let it catch us!!"

"Natsuki-san!?"

Holding back Otto, who was about to turn around, Subaru dashed to the back of the cargo bed and, facing the thing closing in on them, roared with a face bared in undisguised fury.

"You—! How damn persistent can you be, you bastard!!"

In the direction of Subaru's shouting gaze, a massive black shadow writhed.

Trailing black, hand-like tendrils oozing from a corpse, no longer even recognizable as human — a mass of malevolent obsession, the wreckage of Petelgeuse, was closing in behind them, chasing Subaru down.
