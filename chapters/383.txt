Arc 5 Chapter 66: "Liliana Masquerade's Stage"

"Guooohh! This is bad! This fire's totally blazing! Totally blazing! Seven times hotter than normal fire! If I get burned by this, I'll suffer seven times as much before I die!"

A wall of white flame stood right in front of me! It was some inexplicable fire that didn't burn you even if you touched it—just gave off heat—so, to be blunt about it, if you could just bear the heat, it wasn't actually impossible to push through. Basically a bluff, all show and no bite.

So, to keep the crowd—too scared of it to push forward—from finding out the truth, I did my best to act like someone writhing in agony as the flames caught on me, running around to every single one of the canals on all four sides, desperately proclaiming just how dangerous the fire was!

Well, even with everyone's sanity hanging by a thread, they still seemed to retain the instinctive sense that fire was bad news, so my death-defying pantomime might've been pointless after all—but if I didn't do something, I wouldn't have known how to fill the time!

"Priscilla-sama makes it sound so easy—'sing and dance and captivate them,' she says—"

Honestly, if I started singing with the mindset of "I'm gonna charm the pants off you, uriiii!"—that would be completely corrupt from the start, going against the whole philosophy of song in the first place.

If, as a result of singing with all my heart, something moves the listener's chest, that's the natural order of things. And if they're moved by that natural order and happen to slip me a little something as thanks, well, that's a kind, happy world for both of us!

But if you start out with the tip as the whole point from the get-go, then of course that ulterior motive rides along in the song too. And a song riding on ulterior motives isn't what I want.

So this plan is, in a way, already broken from the very start!

"Thinking back, though my life has been short, I've lived through days of great upheaval. Father, Mother—when I was thrown out at thirteen, told it was 'for my education, to make me independent,' and cast out into the harsh waves of the world, I resented you both. But now I feel nothing but gratitude that you bore such an adorable child. I was a terribly unfilial daughter, but not a day has gone by that I haven't done my daily set of gratitude-and-fury sit-ups toward you both—geh! Gyaaah! Hot, hot, hot, endless summer!!!"

Right in the middle of composing my feelings of gratitude for my parents and whatnot, I sensed someone on the other side of the flame wall about to come this way, so I had to restart my scorching-hot performance—come on, read the room, people!

But but, this gripping performance of mine was going to be found out before too long. And once it was found out, there'd be no way to hold back Priscilla-sama's extremely short fuse.

Yep, this is no good! We're done for!

"My my, do you have the composure to look? That girl you placed your hopes in is wearing a face of utter despair, you know? The sorrow and futility filling her heart—I can sense it as if I could reach out and touch it. Aching, weeping—don't you think it's pitiful?"

"Not in the slightest."

While I was clutching my head and starting a countdown to the end, out in the middle of the plaza surrounded by blazing canals, the clash and conversation between Priscilla-sama and the fiend Sirius continued.

Though "conversation" mostly meant Sirius speaking in that mild, gentle tone while Priscilla-sama refused to listen and cut them down flat—that was the standard pattern! And as for Sirius, whatever the tone of voice, the way those chains whirled from both arms wasn't remotely mild.

Slicing the wind, mowing through the air, the whirling iron chains traced paths that shifted freely, endlessly! Writhing, leaping about, striking at Priscilla-sama from front, back, left, and right—the jaws of iron serpents!

The sound of the chains extending and contracting chained together so noisily, one after another, that to my eyes it looked like Priscilla-sama had been sealed inside a prison of chains—not a single opening in sight.

The paving stones that took a direct hit from the iron chains burst apart, gouged and shredded, mercilessly telling of their power. If it caught bare skin properly, you'd end up looking like you'd been traced over by a serpent's fangs and tongue—a sight too gruesome to look at twice! And for someone with skin as pale and beautiful as Priscilla-sama's, it wouldn't be an exaggeration to say the cruelty of it would stand out all the more!

And yet, and yet, Priscilla-sama, in the face of that ferocious chain assault—!

"A noisy, tasteless racket; a shameless lack of restraint, lashing out wildly at anything and everything; a crude weapon with not a shred of aesthetic sense to it; shallow, insufferable drivel... "Priscilla-sama, you're amaaaazing!!"

Chains came at her wildly from every direction, and Priscilla-sama, still wearing that bored expression, batted them all away with the Sunlight Sword. Front, side, up, down, fine, but how on earth was she deflecting the ones coming from behind that she wasn't even looking at?

On top of that, whether it's some special power the Sunlight Sword has or what, every time it deflected a chain, the point of contact burned white. The heat wasn't half-hearted either—the chains were being scorched clean through in an instant, getting shorter and shorter! Keep that up and the chains are gonna run out of lives for sure!

"Hey hey heeey! Priscilla-sama, keep it up, just keep on giving it to 'em!"

"See there, that's what an honest reaction looks like once it forgets to pretend otherwise. It's not easy for most people to bare themselves that far, but it's far more pleasant than being fed some tedious little theory. Well, I do think one of that sort in the world is quite enough."

Even if someone points out now that it wasn't actually a compliment, too late—I already got happy about it!

Spurred on by my cheering, Priscilla-sama's momentum grew even stronger. She pressed steadily forward, burning through chain after chain. Even the Sin Archbishop of "Wrath," fiend that they were, seemed overwhelmed by that sheer intensity—now all that was left was to wait to be sliced up! A potato on the cutting board!

"Wait, Priscilla-sama, you can't just lop 'em off like that! If you do that, aren't we all gonna get lopped off too—whole and complete!?"

"Won't you let me help you come to understand one another with others? The true you is deeply loving, and loves the world. You wish to be loved. I can see that. I want others besides myself to understand that too. So that you'll never again have to spend a lonely, uncertain night by yourself."

"My, thank you. That title suits my husband far better. The ravings of some guilty wretch, unable to grasp the smallest part of his wonderfulness, spat out in envy and jealousy toward him."

"Not letting me skim even the surface—now that really is the height of stubbornness... isn't it. Well then, how about a change of approach? 'Iris and the King of Thorns.'"

"——"

"Or perhaps 'The Rose Knight of Tieros'? 'Magrizza's Guillotine'?"

I had no idea what any of the things Sirius blurted out meant, nor what the fiend's aim was in saying them.

"—Die."

The instant I thought she'd whispered it, Priscilla-sama's figure vanished from sight.
By the time I went "huh," the distance between Priscilla-sama and Sirius had dropped to zero. Priscilla-sama had drawn back the Sunlight Sword, its arc aimed mercilessly at Sirius's throat. The world went astonishingly slow, and even I could see the path the blade traced.
All it would take was for that path to be followed through, and Sirius's slender neck would be cleanly severed.

If that happened, my head would fly too, and so would the heads of everyone else around. Was that why it looked slow, because some part of me already knew? You know, that thing where when death is bearing right down on you, human focus kicks into overdrive and the whole world seems to slow to a crawl—wasn't this exactly that?

But if that was the case, what could I—or any of the many other people—actually do, when the balance of our own lives had been tipped into someone else's hands?
The Sunlight Sword's red glow flowed on through the air, exactly as its course had been set—

"—Eh?"

My understanding couldn't keep up with the scene in front of me, and all that came out was a dumb little noise.

Instead, Priscilla-sama's body—which should have plunged straight into Sirius—was sent flying back hard.

"Priscilla-sama!?"

As if it had taken on, in her place, the attack Priscilla-sama had just received.

Sirius's composure never so much as cracked, and Priscilla-sama's fury boiled over. The glow of the Sunlight Sword in her hand grew brighter—or was that heat increasing? Even from where I stood, Priscilla-sama's whole figure seemed to shimmer and waver, like the air on a scorching midday.

Watching that, I couldn't imagine for a second that Priscilla-sama might actually lose... but then there was that inexplicable thing that had just happened.

The fiend Sirius had apparently provoked Priscilla-sama with some words I didn't understand at all, and Priscilla-sama had taken the bait, hook line and sinker, and charged in—but that's not to say her movement had been so obvious that she got intercepted head-on because of it.

Because at the very instant she brought the Sunlight Sword down, Priscilla-sama's movement stopped.

There's that phrase people use, "it looked like it stopped," a callback to that whole focus-and-slow-motion thing—but this felt different, felt more clearly, completely stopped than that.

I can't imagine Priscilla-sama would intend to freeze in such an unnatural posture, and I can't imagine a human being able to do that on purpose either. Which means, I'd guess, some kind of who-knows-what power was at work in that stop—what do you think?

"Please, don't be so furious. You always seem to be angry at something, but that must be exhausting, and it just leaves your heart drier and drier, doesn't it? 'Wrath'—it's the most abominable grave of emotion this world has to offer. The human heart begins with feeling... and if so, then that heart ought always to be filled with joy, with delight."

"Oh?"

Wait!? Did I just say that out loud!?
The fiend Sirius turned to stare fixedly at me, catching me fast with those bulging eyes visible through the gaps in the bandages. Uhieee, oh no, I poked the sleeping snake right in the butt!

"Indeed, right now, the hearts of everyone outside are ruled by anxiety and grief. That, too, is a sad thing, but it's the result of people's hearts being filled with compassion and love for others."

"Once someone comes under the influence of my Authority, they open their hearts and come to understand one another through feeling. Then, even the emotions they've kept bottled up, unable to put into words, get shared with each other. People are precious beings capable of empathy, of putting themselves in another's place. Cherish another's heart, see their sorrow, and sorrow is born in your own heart too—and then, in the heart of whoever sees that sorrow, a fresh sorrow buds anew. Repeat that, and drops of sorrow become a pond, become a lake, become a great river."

This person had gone and said something wild.

In other words, the more people gathered together, the more the emotions get amplified to a dangerous degree around this person. So that's the mechanism behind everyone in the whole city going into a fit of rage or breaking down in tears all at once... It's not just about making hearts unstable.

Sirius clapped their hands, voice bright as if to say "brilliant idea."

—I'm scared, it's sad, help me, this hurts, why, why, I don't want this.

I said it, I really said it! Wahyahyaaay!

"—!!!!!"

So that meant this white flame wrapped around the control tower wasn't going to burn my body!
It was hot enough that it felt like burning, painful enough that I thought I might actually die, and I wanted to writhe on the ground right then and there from the pain—but I wasn't burning, wasn't scorching, wasn't actually going to die!

My eyes are melting, my tongue is shriveling, my hair is burning, my skin is blistering, the Lyulyre is on fire, my bones are bursting, my flesh is charring, my mind is blowing apart—all of it an illusion—!
Not hot, hot, hot, hot, not hot, not hot, hot, dying, don't want to die, hot, not hot, hot hot hot hot hot but it iiiiis!

I raced up through the control tower—first floor, second floor, how many floors does this thing even have!? Where's the roof!? How far up has the fire gone, how far up have the flames climbed—look right, look left, white flame everywhere, hot, hot, why am I putting myself through all this pain, hot, desperately hot, me—

"——!"

I wanted to scream out every bit of that heat, to shriek right now until my throat tore open.
Absolutely not, no—if I screamed this agony out loud as it was, my voice would be shot in a single go, no question. This throat is something I can never give up. My fingers, the same. I wanted to claw at myself, to slam my hands against anything at all just to let it out. Can't, no, if my fingers give out I won't be able to play anymore.

My eyes, fine. My skin, fine. My hair, fine—if they melt, so be it.
But my throat, no. My fingers, no. My ears, not allowed either. I need every one of them for what's ahead.

I pounded up the stairs, kicked open a needlessly thick door, and right in front of me was the night sky— wind slammed into me, heat climbing up hard from underfoot, but there were no more walls, and staggering, I ran clear to the edge of the stone floor, where I could look down below.

The wind was howling, and down below, a red figure and a white figure were swinging around something dangerous, and all around the white flame, a huge crowd of people was wailing and crying.

I was hot, so hot, like I might die any second.
Even now I wasn't free of the heat—the soles of my feet burned, and whenever the wind gusted the white flame only flared up stronger, and all at once a wave of sorrow came surging up into my chest, thump, thump, and I nearly broke down sobbing—but—

"Sniffle, sniiiffle... th-this here, ha-haah, is what they call the once-in-a-lifetime big stage, y'know!"

Hot and agonizing enough that I thought I might die—but even after going through all that, I'd made it to where I wanted to be, and from here, I could see everyone, and my voice could reach everyone.

I felt like I was dying, dying for real, but there was something I had to do before I actually died, so, well.

"Come now, come now! Those too far to see, hear it by sound! Those nearby, watch the dance too! Those further still, I'll shout even louder, so listen up!! Liliana Masquerade will sing, and play, and dance for you all! Listen well!! —The Sky Overtaking the Morning Glow!!"

—Let's just spill out every last bit of this burning-hot passion, why don't we!
