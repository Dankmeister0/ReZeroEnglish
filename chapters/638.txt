Arc 8 Chapter 22: "Want to Believe. Won't Forgive."

"—Village Chief-kun, got a moment?"

Called out to from behind, Vincent Vollachia stopped walking.
Even before turning around, he already knew whose voice it was. He never forgot a voice once heard, or a person once seen. So he knew immediately — it was the golden-haired, blue-eyed merchant, Flop O'Connell.

"Unfortunately, the Empire faces a crisis of survival at this very moment. I have no time to spare for your idle chatter."

He understood that much, and understanding it, Vincent still paid the man no mind.
He needed to confer with Berstetz, Serena, and the others about how to handle the oracle just received from the Stargazer, Ubilk.
Groovy had been named as a possible countermeasure against the enemy, but given the strong likelihood that he was already dead, building a plan that relied on him would be the height of folly. On that score, placing hope in Natsuki Subaru — who had gone to persuade the other named individual — was, if anything, the lesser folly.

To begin with, Vincent himself had no prospect of persuading that girl.
Proper communication with her was scarcely possible to begin with. Since none of the cards in Vincent's hand worked on the girl, it stood to reason that one of the two lights would have to be seized by Subaru instead.
And so all that remained for Vincent to do was accumulate considerations of realistic strategy—

"Whoa, whoa, now! Whether it's idle chatter or not isn't something you can know until you've heard it out, is it! Even a village chief as clever as you can't know what seeds of conversation I'm carrying before I've watered them, can he!"

"You—"

So saying, Flop caught Vincent's shoulder just as he tried to resume walking, holding him back. It could only be called an outrage — the height of recklessness.
Were the circumstances different, it was the sort of insolence that would have had Flop's head struck from his shoulders on the spot.

"Let go, this instant. Or you'll forfeit your life."

"Of course, I'd like nothing better than to talk right away too. It's just that this is a matter that deserves a proper amount of time to explain. It won't do to rush through it."

"I did not tell you to speak. I told you to release my—"

"Now, now, don't say that, Abel-chin. Play along with big brother's story, would you?"

"—!?"

Turning only his head to glare at Flop, who still had a hand on his shoulder, Vincent found himself, this time, hoisted bodily off the ground by hands slipped under both his arms, from the front — another outrage.
When he caught his breath, he found the one responsible was Medium O'Connell, grinning brightly — the O'Connell siblings, Flop and Medium, had Vincent sandwiched between them, front and back, both smiling.

"Abel-chin, you've been ignoring big brother this whole time, haven't you? Big brother's all beat up and hurt and has to lie down for as long as possible, so quit being mean and just listen to him, 'kay~?"

"Do not speak such nonsense. To begin with, how much longer do you intend to go on treating me with such familiarity? Circumstances change, and with them, one's standing. This is no longer the settlement of the Shudrak, nor the walled city."

"Well, Abel-chin might be playing emperor now, but I don't think that means it's okay to brush us aside like that! Right, big brother!"

"Right you are, little sister!"

The siblings, deaf to reason, paid not the slightest heed to Vincent's words.
Answering his high-spirited sister's call with equal vigor, Flop opened the door of the guest room right beside them, and Medium hauled Vincent inside before the door was shut.
Having swiftly locked the emperor away in a private room, Medium finally released him.

"Do the two of you understand that this is an outrage severe enough to implicate not just yourselves, but your entire family line?"

"Ha ha ha ha, too bad, Village Chief-kun. Our family is just the two of us, siblings. So your threat doesn't carry any weight at all, I'm afraid."

"Oh, but big brother, what about everyone at the orphanage? Even without blood ties, everyone who ran away with us is family too!"

"Ha ha ha ha, now that you mention it! Village Chief-kun, what would it take for you to forgive us, I wonder!"

"—Release me this instant, and behave yourselves."

Whatever the circumstances, the O'Connell siblings' conduct never changed in the slightest.
Medium, who could stretch and shrink, and Flop, who was supposedly injured — watching the two of them now, both facts seemed doubtful.
Normally, if one's own body stretched and shrank like that, it would place enormous strain on the mind, yet Medium seemed remarkably unaware that she had shrunk and then stretched back out again.
As for Flop, he'd been described as gravely, mortally wounded, yet here he was, hale and hearty. —No, well, there was a trace of pallor at his throat, though it looked as if he were covering his complexion with makeup.

"A merchant has to keep up appearances too, you know."

"—————"

"Though, ordinarily, the ironclad rule of commerce is to buy and sell whatever's wanted — but I can't meet your current request, Village Chief-kun. I don't want to put this off any longer."

"What are you talking—"

"I've been asked to deliver a message. —From the person who was playing emperor in your place."

Expecting more nonsense to follow, Vincent's eyes widened, ever so slightly.
That the O'Connell siblings would go so far as to lock the emperor away in an empty room just to convey this to him — the one who bore that particular crown was one person, and one alone, in this world.
And so—

"You should hear his words, Village Chief-kun. —No, I should say, Your Imperial Majesty, Vincent Vollachia."

At Flop's expression — serious for the first time — Vincent chose not to reach for words to cut him off.

—Rui Arneb, the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony.

The moment he put it into words once more, Subaru heard something inside his own chest creak loudly.
It was a forbidden box, always within arm's reach, whose lid he'd hesitated to open — a Pandora's box he could have opened at any time, the instant he resolved to.

"—————"

Judging this not a conversation to hold in a corridor, they'd changed location; Subaru and the others had taken over a large guest cabin.
It was Abel's consideration — use it if you need it — a rare instance of the man showing thought for other people's feelings. Though perhaps it was only natural he'd extend such consideration, given that however this discussion was settled, it could prove a matter of grave importance for the Empire as well.
Gathered in that cabin were Emilia, Beatrice, Otto, Garfiel, Roswaal, and the rest of the Emilia Camp, along with—

"The Sin Archbishop of Gluttony... my, my, what an unfortunate connection to keep having."

Anastasia murmured that quietly, resting a hand against her cheek with an air of languid ease, and beside her, Julius, keeping his silence — the participation of these two was likewise indispensable.
Not because they'd come all this way, crossing the border to help Subaru and the others, but because they too were parties who'd suffered harm from "Gluttony."

"Lui herself is with Rem and the others right now. Everyone except Anastasia-san and Julius already knows about Lui, but..."

"O' course we do, I suppose. ...Honestly, I was awfully surprised the first time, I suppose."

"That... same here."

Beatrice's word — "surprised" — was probably a considerable understatement.
In truth, when Subaru had first realized that he and Lui had been sent flying here together, driven by the desperate wish to protect Rem, he'd taken a rather forceful stance himself. At first, that was exactly why Rem had regarded him with such suspicion, convinced he simply couldn't be trusted.
He had no idea under what circumstances Emilia and the others had first met Lui, but it wasn't hard to imagine that first contact had been fairly fraught as well.
For that, he wanted to thank them — that no one there had resorted to anything extreme.

"Thanks for holding back. Otto, Garfiel — I figured you two would've blown up right away."

"In fact, the choice Garfiel and I proposed was either eliminate her or restrain her. That she ended up as neither is thanks to Emilia-sama, who talked the rest of us down."

"Emilia-tan did..."

Just as expected, it seemed Otto — apparently the spearhead of the hardliners — had had his position walked back by the moderate Emilia.
At their gazes, she gave a short nod, saying "Yeah," and continued,

"Medium-chan was protecting that girl... Lui, with everything she had. If she's cared for that much by the people around her, I thought it'd be scary to decide everything about her right there on the spot."

"I see, so it was Medium-san..."

Hearing that Medium had shielded Lui, Subaru felt a faint sense of relief.
Lui had originally taken quite a liking to the bright, caring Medium. And yet, once Lui's true identity as a Sin Archbishop had come to light, Medium had grown afraid of her too — that was the last point of contact between the two of them that Subaru had witnessed, back in the demon city of Chaosflame.
If Medium had gone on to shield Lui after that, then somewhere Subaru hadn't seen, her feelings must have changed. That was something to be glad of.

"I've been through it myself. Everyone thinking I was some terribly scary thing, a witch, and nobody around me willing to even talk to me. So..."

"In Emilia-sama's case, that was the unreasonable prejudice against half-elves. Her case, however, is different. It's a clear-cut distinction rooted in nothing but her own actions."

"Otto-kun..."

To Emilia, who — on top of defending Medium — explained her own reason for taking Lui's side, Otto delivered a sharp, severe rebuttal. He didn't hesitate to make plain, in no uncertain terms, that he stood as Emilia's ally while simultaneously standing as Lui's enemy.
That, at most, his going along with them this far was nothing more than a deferral of the final verdict.

"Might I say something?"

Into the air that Otto's declared position had left charged with tension, Roswaal raised a hand with a certain easy nonchalance. When Subaru's glance prompted him to go on, Roswaal closed his blue eye and said,

"Ram, myself, and Anastasia-sama's group joined up a bit later, after all, so ultimately, we don't know how this girl called Lui has behaved... though I take it Garfiel's had his eyes, ears, and nose lit up the whole tiiime?"

"Ain't been lightin' up nothin' left and right. But me an' Otto-bro are of the same mind. So I been keepin' watch on her ever since we ran into her, all the way through the big fight, and..."

"And the result came up empty. Then on what grounds are we to conclude the girl is a dangerous Sin Archbishop?"

"That'd be me and Beatrice-chan. We made contact with a girl calling herself Lui Arneb of 'Gluttony' in Priestella. She even gouged out my leg — I'm not likely to forget that."

As the representative of those with the thinnest connection to Lui, Roswaal posed the question, and Garfiel and Otto each stated their own view in response.
As he spoke, Otto stroked his own leg — the injury that had kept him off the front lines for a while.
It was easy to understand why Otto, in particular, was so strongly wary of Lui.

"Forgive me for following the Margrave, but might I add something as well?"

With Roswaal's question resolved, Julius joined the discussion in quick succession.
Given that the topic was "Gluttony," he'd worn a difficult expression from the very start, and now, glancing sidelong at Subaru's even grimmer face, he said,

"First, I'd like to confirm something. Is the girl called Lui riding with us on this dragon carriage truly, without question, the same person as Rui Arneb, the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony?"

"—? What do you mean by that?"

"Emilia-sama and Lady Ram should know this as well. In order to reproduce the abilities of those it devoured, 'Gluttony' would sometimes alter its own appearance into that of the person in question. In other words..."

"You mean the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony and the Lui that Emilia-san and the others have been talking to might not be the same person at all — that they could be in a relationship of eater and eaten?"

At the hypothesis Julius had raised, Emilia and the others made faces of surprise — "Ah."
Indeed, considering the nature of "Gluttony," it wasn't an impossible notion. If it reproduced the form of whomever it devoured, then logically there had to be an original who'd been devoured.
If that were true, then it would be possible to think of that Lui and "Gluttony" as separate existences entirely.
However—

"...No, that's not it. Lui's probably reproducing the abilities of the people she's eaten. That should be, without question, because of the Authority of 'Gluttony.'"

"I see. My apologies for the confusion my words may have caused."

Julius closed his eyes and apologized to Subaru, who'd shaken his head.
It wasn't that Julius was in the wrong. If his guess had been correct, Subaru wouldn't have had to agonize this much in the first place. But an excuse cobbled together on the spot wouldn't do.
What was needed was an answer arrived at only after sharing the truth.

Lui — sent flying to the Vollachia Empire together with Subaru, treated coldly by him again and again, yet time and again fighting desperately to save him, taking wounds, putting her own life on the line.
Making everyone accept that she — one and the same person as Rui Arneb, the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony — was nonetheless someone necessary for the battle to come.

"—. First, let me explain things in order. What Lui and I were like together, in the Empire, before Emilia-tan and the others joined up with her."

"—————"

Everyone's eyes settled on him, fixed and unwavering.
Even the gazes of companions he knew well and trusted — even so, Subaru felt an unmistakable tightness in his chest. As if what was about to begin here were nothing so much as a grading, a checking of answers.
A checking of answers, so that Lui's existence might truly be accepted by his companions.

"Subaru, you don't need to rush."

To Subaru, bracing himself, Emilia offered that gently. Beside him, Beatrice, too, nodded as she gently re-took his hand in hers.
Saved by their consideration, Subaru drew a breath, and began.

"The very first time, we realized it near a great forest, far to the east—"

He'd tried to choose his words plainly, as briefly as possible, so the facts would come across.
And yet, everything he had to tell welled up one thing after another like a spring, and though it had all happened in what was, in truth, a fairly short span of time, he felt keenly just how dense those days had been.
To begin with, far too many outrageous troubles had occurred in far too short a span.
And every single one had been an accident that put Subaru's, Rem's, and their companions' lives in danger, and Lui had naturally been drawn into it each time as well.
Naturally. —Yes, naturally.
Because Lui had stayed with Subaru and the others the whole time, drawing Subaru's animosity even as Rem treated her kindly, and still she'd kept following along after Subaru, undeterred by his coldness toward her.
That's why—

"—Lui and I worked with Yorna-san and managed to win against Olbart-san. Through some against-the-rules tactics, mind you — turning hide-and-seek into a game of tag."

"—————"

"After that, I hear something outrageous happened in Chaosflame. The people living there were miraculously safe, and joined up with Abel's rebel army. I lost consciousness there and got separated... After that, you all know more about Lui than I do, right?"

"—. Yes. Because after that was when we — after we'd been utterly battered in Guaral — welcomed Abel and the others back."

At Subaru's confirmation, Emilia nodded, and having finished her explanation of that stretch, let out a long breath.
He hadn't meant to take so long, but in the end, the telling had gone on for nearly an hour. That his companions had barely interrupted the whole while, devoting themselves to listening, was something to be grateful for.
Though it had been unavoidable to tell it from his own point of view, he'd tried his best to be careful to relate things as objectively as he could.
And on top of that—

"Like I said, Lui never did anything suspicious the whole time she was with me. I think you'd hear the same thing from Rem."

"That much I don't doubt at all. If Ram genuinely suspected her, there's no way she'd let her stay by Rem-san's side."

When it came to family, and Rem in particular, there was no need to even mention how sharp Ram's eyes were.
In fact, that she'd overwhelmed Lye Batenkaitos of "Gluttony" at the Pleiades Watchtower had, above all, been because her relationship with Rem and herself had been meddled with.
This was Ram, who would go that far for a sister who didn't even remember her.
If she'd sensed even the slightest hint of danger from Lui, there was no way she'd have let her stay by the side of Rem, who'd only just finally woken up.

"...Roughly speaking, we get how tough it must've been for you, Natsuki-kun."

Anastasia, who'd been listening in silence until then, rubbed the bridge of her nose with a finger at the sheer hardship of the journey Subaru had just finished recounting.
Even for a woman as sharp as her, it had clearly been a volume of information that took some effort to fully digest. Having managed to swallow it down somehow, Anastasia took her finger from her brow and said,

"I'm curious what happened to you after you lost consciousness in the demon city too, Natsuki-kun, but that's not the main topic right now, so I'll set it aside."

"Yeah, what matters right now is Lui. Like I said, up to this point she hasn't shown us her dangerous side. And then there's Ubilk's prophecy, the 'Stargazer' thing... that too. So for the fight ahead, I want Lui to help us out—"

"—Where exactly are you plannin' on takin' that story, is what I'm wonderin'."

That quiet remark stopped Subaru's words cold, right where he stood.
Having spoken, Anastasia traced her lips lightly with the finger that had been rubbing her brow, and, ensnaring Subaru's heart with those clear, pale-blue eyes of hers, went on.

"I don't dislike that way of thinkin' you've got, Natsuki-kun, tryin' to make use of anythin' and everythin'. Meili-san, who helped us cross the Augria Sand Dunes — she started out as an enemy too, from what I hear, and if we're talkin' about that kind of category, that girl falls under the same one."

"That's... yeah, the same, isn't it? Meili and Lui should be in the same position."

"Mm, no, they're not. —That's what everyone here, 'cept you, Natsuki-kun, probably thinks."

"—!"

At the quiet voice piling on top of the last, Subaru looked around with a start.
He scanned the faces of everyone else besides himself, everyone Anastasia had just named. Hoping that someone, anyone among them, would tell Anastasia she was wrong.
But not a single one of them answered his hope.

"Guys...!"

"Since nobody else wants to say it, I will."

Amid the group, silent with difficult expressions, Otto raised his hand, his own expression scarcely changing.
Though "no change in expression" here meant no change from the composed look he'd worn since the meeting began.
Neither his gaze nor his voice were, by any means, unconditionally on Subaru's side.

"I agree with Anastasia-sama. The circumstances with her and Meili-chan are different. They're not in the same position."

"Otto!"

"I'll take this one too, since nobody wants to say it. —It's because she's a Sin Archbishop."

To Subaru, whose voice had risen unconsciously, Otto answered in a flat, even tone.
His answer, preempting the "why" that was sure to come, was about as fitting an explanation as could be for why no one in the room had been able to take Subaru's side.

"Subaru, Betty wants to take your side, I suppose. Whether we can believe that girl... Lui, has no malice now — that, too, I suppose. But..."

"As a matter of fact, we too have suffered harm from 'Gluttony.' And considering how difficult that harm is to make visible, one wonders just how many victims there might be, lurking unseen."

"There's no way to confirm it, but... those who lost themselves, were forgotten by others, and — left without anywhere to belong — never managed to recover, their grief is more than I can fully imagine."

Beatrice, who'd been mindful of Subaru's feelings, spoke first, and Roswaal and Julius followed with words that were, respectively, the honest opinion of someone connected to the harm caused by "Gluttony's" Authority, and of someone who'd been a direct victim of it.

"—————"

Just as Roswaal and Julius said, those who at least knew they'd been harmed were the fortunate ones.
What called for true despair was the thought that there must be many, many victims who'd had their "names," their "memories" stolen from them, left with no way home, never saved.
And then, the one who had produced so many such victims — the Sin Archbishop of "Gluttony"—

"I don't think it's that everyone can't believe you, exactly."

"Emilia..."

"It's more that... I think they can't forgive her. That's it, I think."

It wasn't that Subaru's explanation had been lacking, or that his feelings hadn't gotten through.
The focus of the problem lay elsewhere entirely — Emilia's words, spoken with the corners of her brows drawn down, shattered something in Subaru's chest.
Emilia and the others, who knew what had happened while Lui was with Subaru, and what had happened after Subaru had gone missing, understood perfectly well that the Lui of "now" wasn't someone who would do them harm.
Understanding that, what remained a problem was what Lui of the "past" had done.

"—Whatever wrong you've done, it never, ever just disappears."

"—!"

Anastasia murmured that quietly.
It was the same despairing warning that had once frozen Natsuki Subaru solid, shattered him to pieces.
The fact that those words had once been hurled at him now existed only within Subaru himself. And now, on a different occasion, they had once again reached Subaru's ears from her mouth.

"No matter how much you insist that girl's harmless, Natsuki-kun, it doesn't erase what happened when she wasn't. The fact remains, the victims are still victims... this isn't a problem shallow enough to paper over with nice-soundin' words."

"—Guh"

"First off, shouldn't it start with returnin' all the 'names' and 'memories' she's eaten up till now? If you try to move forward without that, even you wouldn't really be satisfied, would you, Natsuki-kun?"

Anastasia's words cut Subaru, stiffened where he stood, one blade after another.
Each one was undeniably correct, and among them, the one that struck with the sharpest pain was the very last point — that without returning the "memories" and "names" she'd stolen, they hadn't even reached the point of standing on solid ground to decide Lui's treatment at all.
That was precisely the premise underlying what Emilia had called "can't forgive."

"Mind you, His Imperial Majesty already knows about this prophecy Subaru-kun heard tell of. Not havin' that girl called Lui cooperate in the fight isn't really a realistic option, I'd saaay."

"Ahh? What're you gettin' at. Spit it out already."

"I'd rather you not vent your irritation at me... but regardless of how our discussion here resolves, the policy of having the girl cooperate won't change. What remains is only how she's treated."

"Treated...?"

Told to spit it out already, yet Roswaal continued to speak in his roundabout way, fraying at Subaru's patience. At Subaru's earnest, pressing look, Roswaal shrugged and said,

"It's a question of what and how we tell her, to get her to cooperate. For instaaance — promise her a pardon, once we've overcome this crisis threatening the Empire's survival. And once it's all over, quietly carry out whatever sentence fits her crimes. This would be the cleanest method, with the least left to fester afterward."

"—! The hell're you sayin'!"

At the vicious policy Roswaal had laid out, Garfiel snarled, baring his fangs. He turned a sharp glare on Roswaal and said,

"Just 'cause the other side's a monster don't mean we gotta become monsters too! Danglin' bait that don't even exist to get her to help — I ain't lettin' that slide!"

"Oh my, is that so? I rather think Otto-kun's had the same thought, though..."

"Don't you go lumpin' Otto-bro in with the likes of you!"

"—. What the Margrave says is likely what most people would say, in his stead. A Sin Archbishop shouldn't be granted a pardon. Or is it—"

There, Otto cut himself off once, and fixed Subaru with a steady stare.
To Subaru, who caught his breath at that gaze, he continued, without mercy.

"Are you trying to set a precedent, Natsuki-san, where a Sin Archbishop gets forgiven?"

"—! That's not what I— There are villains in this world who shouldn't be forgiven. The Sin Archbishops of the Witch Cult are exactly that kind."

Petelgeuse, Regulus, the brothers Lye and Roy, Sirius, Capella — every Sin Archbishop Subaru had met so far had, without exception, been something twisted beyond redemption.
Beings who would sacrifice others without the slightest hesitation, so long as it satisfied their own desires.
And yet—

"—Are you saying she — Rui Arneb alone — is different?"

"Th-that's..."

Otto's question tried to pry open the depths of Subaru's heart.
A Sin Archbishop must never be forgiven. That line — one that not just Otto, but everyone here had drawn, unwilling to give ground — Subaru understood well enough himself.
And yet he wanted to set Lui apart, in a category all her own — just as Otto said, that was strange.

"—————"

Bathed in everyone's gaze at once, Subaru asked the question of his own heart once more.
What did Subaru want to do with Lui? In the Corridor of Memories, where the two of them had so thoroughly hated each other, Subaru had chosen not to save her, even as she suffered the aftereffects of Return by Death. He didn't think that choice had been wrong. Even faced with that same scene again, he'd make the same decision.

But at the same time, he knew the Lui who had shared hardship with him in the Vollachia Empire.
The fact that she had risked her life, again and again, to save him — that he had witnessed her actually lose her life, more than once — that, too, was carved firmly into his soul.
Somewhere along the way, faced with such earnest desperation from Lui, Subaru's guard had melted away—

"Why?"

"—————"

Suddenly, that quiet question was tossed at the side of Subaru's face as he sat there thinking.
When he opened his closed eyes, he found himself meeting, head-on, the violet-indigo eyes of Emilia, who'd asked it. She narrowed those eyes, framed by long lashes, and repeated the same question once more.
It was—

"Why is it that you came to feel that way, Subaru?"

"Why, is..."

"You hated the Sin Archbishop of 'Gluttony' so much too, didn't you? You thought you couldn't forgive it. So why is it different now?"

"That's — I already told you. She... Lui, in this Empire, with us—"

Having gone through so many hardships together, Lui had tried valiantly to protect Subaru and Rem.
What had happened in the Empire had left just as strong an impression on Subaru — if not stronger — than what had made him regard her as an unforgivable enemy in the Corridor of Memories. That was why.
That was why Natsuki Subaru's feelings toward Lui had changed.

"Subaru, let me tell you a cruel fact, once again."

To Subaru, who'd answered Emilia's question haltingly, Julius said this in a low voice.
The fact that he'd deliberately prefaced it as "cruel" put Subaru on guard. To that guarded Subaru, Julius traced the scar beneath his left eye with a finger, and said,

"The people of this world will never forgive the Sin Archbishop of 'Gluttony,' the girl named Rui Arneb. Kingdom or Empire, it makes no difference — that, I think, can be called the consensus of the world."

"—————"

"No matter where in the world she might flee, there is no place that will forgive her. Commit a crime, and you are punished for it. And a criminal who can only atone with their life — that is what a Sin Archbishop is."

That it was a cruel fact — that preface, at least, had held no falsehood.
Julius had stated it plainly, in a firm tone, in terms too definite to be mistaken.
That in this world, there was no place where Rui Arneb could live and be forgiven.
At those heavy words, Subaru found himself with nothing to say in return.
But—

"I, too, wish nothing for a Sin Archbishop but death. Nothing but the 'death' of a being we consider an enemy of the world. That's the very most I can say."

"...Eh?"

"Julius Juukulius!!"

It was Otto who raised his voice sharply and glared at Julius like that.
Unable to grasp the meaning behind the words just added, Subaru, eyes wide, was left out of it as Otto and Julius pierced each other with their gazes.
Julius's, full of pain; Otto's, full of bitterness — each look wounding the other.

"For an outsider like you to say that is nothing short of overstepping your bounds...!"

"Forgive me, but let me correct that perception. I too am one of the parties concerned. I intend to make use of that right — the right to voice an opinion."

"—!"

Grinding his teeth, Otto glared at Julius with even greater intensity.
Though he knew he could never match him in raw strength, he didn't yield an inch in the battle of wills. Julius, too, closed one eye at the sheer strength of Otto's resolve, and let out a small breath.

"Nothing but the Sin Archbishop's 'death'..."

Alongside that exchange between the two of them, Subaru murmured that, in the sensation of some part of his brain going numb.
What Julius had been trying to say, and the way the perceptive Otto had bitten back at him so hard for it — there was something hidden there, beyond the mere words, that Subaru hadn't grasped.
Nothing is wished for a Sin Archbishop but "death." The world will not forgive Rui Arneb.
The Sin Archbishop. Rui Arneb. The world—

"—Ah"

"Subaru, it's Betty's turn to talk, I suppose."

Just as he felt a faint breeze blow through the maze of his thoughts, Beatrice spoke.
She looked up at Subaru with those round eyes marked by their distinctive pattern, and said,

"Betty ran into the Sin Archbishop Lui in Priestella, I suppose. And then, this time, running into her again in the Empire, Betty thought this about how that girl seemed — like she might be a different person entirely, I suppose."

"—————"

"That girl is different from the Sin Archbishop Betty knows, I suppose. What did it look like from where Subaru was standing? From Subaru, who traded words with the Sin Archbishop up close?"

Beatrice's words pressed him — gentle, yet allowing no escape.
He understood what she was getting at. It was something Subaru himself hadn't wanted to admit, and so hadn't.

The "Lui" of now, and the Rui Arneb he'd met in the Corridor of Memories, seemed like different people entirely.
And yet, as he'd countered Julius's question, "Lui" did use the Authority. Just as "Gluttony" had, she freely wielded the abilities of those she'd devoured.
"Lui" possessed the Authority of "Gluttony," while only her spirit, it seemed, had been reborn anew.
For instance, it was like—

"...Like me, when I lost my own memories."

If that were so, had "Lui" been suffering this whole time?
Just as Subaru, back then, had chased after the illusion of "Natsuki Subaru," unable to trust anyone around him, agonizing over it — had "Lui," too, been crying out for help?
And even in such a state, "Lui" had gone on saving Subaru and Rem, all the way up to this very day.
And then—

"—I've been thinking about this the whole time. If you do something bad, is there really no taking it back?"

"Emilia..."

"If you apologize, and you make up for it, and even then people still say it's no good — there could be times you'd get sick of even trying to apologize or make up for it, right? So I've been thinking of a way to keep that from happening... but I couldn't easily find some amazing method like that. But—"

"But?"

"There's just one way — maybe. A way Subaru taught me."

Emilia placed a hand over her own chest and chose her words, putting everything she had into them.
At what she'd said, Subaru searched within his own heart. But nothing came to mind that he'd ever taught Emilia that applied to this very moment.
To Subaru, who had no memory of any such thing, Emilia looked at him with gentle eyes and said,

"For people around that person to want them to be happy, even more than they want to hold it against them, that they can't forgive them."

"—————"

"For a great many people around them to want that person to be happy. I think that's what we need, for us to be able to forgive that girl — to be made to feel that way."

Emilia's words seeped slowly, quietly, into Subaru's chest.
A way to forgive a wrongdoer. A way to forgive a wrongful act. A way toward whatever salvation might lie beyond apologizing and making amends — the answer she'd arrived at, after thinking as hard as she could, was this, Emilia said.
What exactly Subaru had taught her to arrive at this, or when he'd ever taught her such a thing — he had no idea at all.
And yet, somehow, the words seemed to settle firmly, with a soft thud, into Subaru's chest.

Why was it that Subaru went to such lengths, agonized so much, for the sake of a Sin Archbishop — for the sake of "Lui"?
It was because "Lui" had done more for Subaru than had ever been done to Lui.
That's why—

"Let me have that same conversation with you again."

Hearing Emilia's words, and the faint breath Subaru let out, Anastasia said that.
Stroking the Eridna at her collar, she looked back and forth between Subaru and Emilia, and said,

"—Where exactly are you plannin' on takin' that story?"


△▼△▼△▼△


"Ah-woo?"

Right in front of her, cupping both hands around the cheeks of Lui, who was peering curiously into her face, Rem let out a long, deep sigh.
At Rem's reaction, Lui, cheeks still held between her hands, only looked more puzzled than ever.
Seeing the girl like that, Rem reflected that she must be causing her worry.
She reflected on it, but—

"Haah..."

"That's quite the melancholy sigh, Rem. Is something wrong?"

"Sister..."

Just as she'd let out that big sigh, a gentle voice called to her from the side.
It belonged to Ram, who'd returned to the guest cabin they'd been given, having arranged for tea. She'd brought with her the girl with the deer antlers growing from the back of her head — Tanza, if she remembered the name right.
Noticing Rem's gaze, Ram said "Ah," nodding, and,

"She looked like she had nothing to do, so I brought her along."

"It's not that I had nothing to do..."

"Really? Your face was practically leaking discontent at being kept away from the other noisy lot. So even the Empire has this idea of protecting women and children while they fight. I'd thought them even more foolish than that."

"Um, Sister, isn't that a bit much..."

At her sister's rather blunt way of putting it, Rem's cheek twitched, just slightly.
It would still take time before things were fully settled, clear and resolved, between her and her sister — even so, the plea from the roots of her soul was honest, and she could feel her heart shaken by Ram's every little gesture. That aside, Tanza looked visibly displeased at Ram's remark.

"It's true that many in the war band, starting with Cecilus-sama, tend to think of things simply, but there are also those with sharp minds, like the Governor-sama."

"I see. So you're on standby duty here because that sharp-minded governor of yours told you to be."

"—. No, it was Schult-sama who told me to rest."

At Ram's riposte, Tanza answered that with the corner of her mouth stiffening, just slightly. Just then, Lui, whose cheeks were still held between Rem's hands, let out a groan as the grip of those hands tightened.
Oh no — Rem said "I'm sorry" to Lui at once.

"I wasn't paying attention. Are you all right, Lui-chan?"

"Ah-woo... uh-wow?"

"...No, that's not related to it at all."

"Woo—"

At Rem's answer, still holding her cheeks, Lui gave her a suspicious look.
Averting her eyes from Lui, Rem happened to meet Ram's gaze as she finished serving the tea. Ram set a cup of warm, fragrant tea down in front of Rem and said,

"Even a secret imperial dragon carriage doesn't seem to be stocked with much in the way of decent tea leaves. It's nothing special, but it should help warm you up."

"Th-thank you very much. I'll have some."

"So? Did Barusu do something rude to you?"

"Pff—"

The words hit right as she'd finally released Lui's cheeks and taken a sip of tea, and she spat it right back out. As Rem hurriedly set down her cup, Lui tried to wipe her face with her own sleeve for her.

"I'm fine, really, I'm fine, Lui-chan. ...Um, what do you mean, Sister?"

"What do I mean, nothing more than that. Barusu's actions are, more often than not, rude and thoughtless. Even a wild guess has decent odds of landing on the reason your face clouded over."

"Even so, that's a bit much to say, isn't it..."

So it really had just been a wild, off-the-mark guess, Rem thought, letting out a breath of relief as Lui wiped her face for her. But even that reaction alone seemed enough for the perceptive Ram to guess the true cause of her sigh.
Sitting down directly across from her, legs crossed, Ram tilted her cup and simply stared at Rem.
Unable to bear that silent pressure for long, Rem soon confessed.

"Um, it's about Emilia-san."

"About Emilia-sama? I see, so Emilia-sama has her own ignorant, thoughtless side too. That's the reason your face clouded over, is it."

"N-no, that's not it! It's... um, that person, and this person named Natsuki Subaru, and Emilia-san — I was wondering what kind of relationship they have..."

Rem managed to lower the pitch of her voice and ask the question while keeping herself as composed as possible.
But she failed to notice, at this point, that the very choice of topic alone was more than enough to make Ram's pale pink eyes narrow. Her clever-looking sister only murmured "I see" softly, and said,

"Right, Barusu ought to be torn limb from limb. Let's do it together later."

"Sister!?"

"Heh, a joint effort between sisters. Even Barusu can be useful once in a while."

Her sister, smiling faintly, was beautiful enough to make one stare, but her remark was dangerous in the extreme. And hearing Ram's remark, it was Tanza who, quite literally, bristled with indignation.
She'd taken a sip of the tea poured for her too, giving a little "fwah" of surprise at the taste, but then,

"Isn't that remark a touch rash? Barusu... I take it you mean Schult-sama — should you lay a hand on Schult-sama, neither I nor the rest of the war band will show any mercy."

"What an earnest answer. ...Tanza, how old are you?"

"—? I'll be turning twelve this year."

"I see. That explains it. I understand now."

"I don't understand at all just because you say you understand on your own."

Rem found herself in agreement with Tanza's discontented objection.
Why had Ram, upon hearing Tanza's age, said she understood? To begin with, Ram still hadn't answered the question Rem had asked in the first place.
About what kind of relationship that woman, Emilia, had with Subaru.

"...They seemed awfully close, is all."

Asked what of it, Rem herself would have nothing to say but "Huh?" in response, but regardless, there was quite a bit weighing on her mind about the distance between the two of them.
So she wanted to settle the matter, whatever it was, as quickly as possible, and get back to focusing on Rem's own problems.
Rem's problems — which was to say, in any case, they were Rem's own problems.

"That's right. It's a trivial matter I want to settle quickly so I can face the bigger problem. Nothing more, nothing less."

"—Rem, might I have a moment?"

"Huh!?"

With a loud clatter, Rem found herself on her feet before she knew it. In the process, she'd unwittingly scooped up Lui, who'd been sitting on her lap.
Apologizing yet again to Lui, who let out a startled "Ah-woo!?" at being hoisted up, Rem looked toward the door of the cabin the voice had come from and said,

"W-who is it?"

"Who else but Torn-Limb-from-Limb Barusu. —I suppose the meeting's over, then."

"Ah..."

At Ram's remark, savoring her tea with elegance, Rem let out a small breath and hugged Lui close. Burying her nose in Lui's golden hair as she tilted her head and said "Woo?", Rem closed her eyes.
She'd been present, just before, for the incident when the Coupled Dragon Carriages had nearly come under attack, but she hadn't joined the discussion afterward, the one Abel had led Subaru off to with such a grave look on his face.
Of course, part of it was that she wouldn't have been of any use even if she'd joined in on something so important, and part of it was that the small, trivial matter she'd just confirmed for herself had been nagging at her.
But the biggest reason was—

"U?"

—because of an uneasy, unpleasant feeling in her chest, concerning this very Lui in her arms.
She had no idea why, but Rem's heart had intuited that she ought to stay close to Lui. And so, while she'd been holed up in the cabin like this—

"—Schult-sama, if you please."

"Ah, thanks. ...Wait, Tanza's here too?"

"Yes. Because Schult-sama conspired with everyone to saddle me with standby duty."

"Somehow, that phrasing makes me feel weirdly guilty..."

While Rem was still flustered, Tanza had already welcomed the visitor into the room in her stead.
As Tanza aimed that resentful remark at him, the one who'd entered scratched his head, looking troubled. Even with his height shrunk down, the mood of that troubled profile hadn't changed much at all.
No doubt it was because, however much his height might grow, his face could never quite shake off its childishness.

"Such shifty eyes, yet such a strange man..."

"Wait!? Did someone just badmouth my eyes just now? I heard that!"

"Quite impressive, that the complaints of the citizens of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica could be heard clear across the border."

"There's no way the whole kingdom's sitting around talking about my eyes! I bet they're all having nice, happy conversations about what's for dinner tonight or their plans for tomorrow!"

Reacting with great exaggeration to Ram's little jab, Subaru came stomping into the room.
Somehow unable to look toward Subaru, Rem hid her face behind the Lui she was holding, and didn't face him directly.
In her stead, Lui greeted him with an "uh-wow."

"Hey, Lui, I had business with you too, actually. You don't happen to know where Rem is, do you?"

"Ah-woo-woo"

"I see, you don't know either, huh. Man, that's rough. I had something important to talk about, too..."

"—. If you have something important to discuss, please stop fooling around and get to the point."

"Whoa, Rem! You were behind Lui the whole time! I didn't even notice!"

At Rem's interjection, Subaru reacted with exaggerated, deliberate theatrics. Though it seemed even that exaggeration was lost on Lui, who slumped her shoulders in genuine disappointment at having failed to hide Rem after all.
Setting Lui down on the floor, stroking her head to comfort her, Rem narrowed her eyes.

"...Are you, by any chance, pushing yourself too hard?"

"—————"

"I believe I've told you this before. Please stop, this forcing yourself, taking on more than you can bear like that. You're not some hero who can do absolutely everything."

Subaru's manner just now had struck her as somehow a bit too much like putting on a brave front, and Rem quoted back to him the very same words she'd told him once before.
At those words, Subaru's eyes went wide, but he quickly gave a rueful smile and said,

"Nah, surprisingly, objectively speaking, I'm actually Superman, so I'll gratefully take your opinion there, Rem, as just... a cute thing to say."

"Please don't joke around."

"I'm not joking. —Rem, I have something important to talk about, regarding Lui."

Straightening up, his wry smile gone, Subaru said that, looking straight at her.
Hearing it, Rem caught her breath, and in her place, beside the two of them, Ram let out a sigh and said,

"Barusu, should Ram and the others clear out?"

"Nah, Sister and Tanza can stay too. Sister, represent the older-sister department; Tanza, represent the loli department, and both of you sit in on this."

"Meaning I outrank Frederica as an older sister. Naturally."

"Um, what exactly does this 'loli' department mean...?"

Asked to sit in, Ram lifted her chin with pride, while Tanza tilted her head in confusion.
With that, at Subaru's gaze turning back toward her once more, Rem gently drew Lui closer to herself. Holding the back of Lui's head against her own chest just like that, Rem looked steadily at Subaru.
And then—

"—Let's settle this. This odd, precious relationship of ours, the one neither of us really understands."
