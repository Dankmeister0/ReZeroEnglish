Arc 6 Chapter 43: "Tower of the Living"

—Think.

Think, think, think, think, I have to think.

"――――"

Clinging with his whole body to the sensation of hard, rough scales, Natsuki Subaru turns his mind to thought.
What's happening to him, what is actually going on inside the tower, who among the people here means to kill him, who means to kill someone other than him, the line between enemy and ally—

"Subaru, that's enough now, isn't it? Have you calmed down yet, I wonder?"

"――――"

Suddenly addressed from behind his tangled thoughts, Subaru turns slowly around. There, he finds, sitting on the vine-woven bed, is Beatrice, the one who spoke.
The girl, wearing a sullen expression, sits beside Emilia holding her hand, swinging her legs and glaring in Subaru's direction. At that gaze, his cheek stiffens faintly.

Even the barest hint of negative feeling directed at him leaves him ill at ease—

"Now, now, Beatrice. Don't talk that way. I bet Subaru just woke up startled, so clinging to Patrasche can't be helped, I think."

But Emilia, smiling, dismisses Subaru's misguided worry. At her point, Beatrice turns her face away with a huff.

"It's not that Betty's angry, exactly. Just not satisfied, I suppose. Betty and Emilia were worried about Subaru too—it's not like that Earth Dragon is anything special, I suppose."

"Fufu, that's true. You really did worry us."

Stroking the head of the turned-away Beatrice, Emilia looks toward Subaru, her brows drooping. Seeing the light in her violet-indigo eyes, Subaru holds his breath.
A deep, affectionate gaze that made his chest ache. —And at the same time, a strange restlessness welled up in him.

He'd remembered. That here, he was wanted as "Natsuki Subaru."
And that he'd been driven to anxiety, unable to bear that weight.
But, even so—

"—Um, sorry for worrying you. My bad, seriously sorry. Waking up and hugging a girl in a daze isn't really my character, so let's just call it me covering my embarrassment."

Softening his stiffened cheek, Subaru answers with a relaxed smile. At his reply, Emilia and Beatrice exchange a glance, and—

"But Patrasche is a girl too, isn't she?"

"Wha—!? No, look, Patrasche's in a special category, like, a bond separate from that kind of thing, like a seeded bye straight into round two."

"Mm, that's not something Betty can let pass, I suppose. Betty can't accept that Earth Dragon getting special treatment like that. Betty demands an explanation, I suppose."

"Don't try to put her in the same ring as you! With, uh, the Earth Dragon...and stuff!"

To an increasingly displeased-looking Beatrice, Subaru answers while carefully watching the two girls' reactions. Neither of them seems to sense anything off about his replies.
Relieved at that, Subaru turns back to the lizard—no, to Patrasche, whose neck he'd been stroking all along.

"...You really are the only one in a special category, huh, Patrasche."

"――――"

The black Earth Dragon closes its eyes and gives a small rumble in its throat; Subaru presses his forehead to hers.


※※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※


"So, Subaru, are you really all right?"

"Yeah, totally fine, no prob. Sorry for worrying you. I think I was just worn out. Falling asleep like that happens sometimes, you know."

"I see. ...But if you're tired, tell me, okay? No pushing yourself."

Rolling his arms and legs lightly, Subaru answers breezily while Emilia presses the point. Answering "Roger, Captain" with a joking tone, Subaru's mind was racing at full speed.

Right now, Subaru is walking through the tower's fourth floor with Emilia and Beatrice in tow.
Their destination is the fourth floor's hall, their purpose, breakfast—in other words, the three of them are walking along as if there were no urgent matter at hand.

This time, Subaru is deliberately trying to act differently than he had in prior loops.
Namely—he hasn't told Emilia and the others that he's lost his memory.

"――――"

Put another way, that's a situation equivalent to impersonating someone else.
Wetting his dry, tension-tightened lips with his tongue, Subaru closely observes Emilia and the others.
He still hasn't fully grasped what kind of person "Natsuki Subaru" is to them. Under such conditions, tracing another person ought to be impossible—but if it's "impersonating oneself," a condition that normally wouldn't even apply, then it's a different story.

—However far you go, "Natsuki Subaru" is nothing but Natsuki Subaru.

Even after spending a year in another world, the core of a person's nature doesn't change. If so, all he needs to watch is the relationships involved, and he should be able to trace the same persona well enough.
And then, while tracing "Natsuki Subaru," he'd work out, conversely, what was going to happen.

What would happen in this tower, who would kill Subaru, who would be killed by whom.

For the moment, by concealing the fact that he'd lost his memory, Subaru means to test one possibility for what triggers his own death. Namely: does whoever it is kill Subaru because he's lost his memory, or is that irrelevant?
That said, honestly, he doesn't expect much from this. It's hard to imagine a situation where the presence of Subaru's memories would be a problem, but their absence wouldn't. Though there are some exceptions.

"Like if the culprit is someone who'd be in trouble if my memory came back."

He recalled having seen something like this in a movie once.
A witness to a murder loses their memory from the shock. The culprit, having been seen at the scene, can't afford to have that memory return. So they try to kill the witness—that kind of movie.
It's entirely conceivable that a similar situation is happening to Subaru right now. To begin with, the circumstances of how he lost his memory are hazy—

"Emilia, I was found collapsed in the third-floor library, right?"

"Yeah, that's right. You'd collapsed on the library floor... Beatrice and I rushed you to the green room."

"Well, Emilia just scooped Subaru right up, so Betty was only cheering from behind, I suppose."

At Beatrice's sighing addition, Subaru glances at Emilia's slender arms.
Thinking calmly about it, how these two had carried Subaru from that library to the green room was a mystery. There wasn't the faintest sign Beatrice would be any use at heavy lifting, and taking that remark at face value—that Emilia had lightly carried Subaru—was hard to swallow too.

"—? Something wrong?"

"No, nothing. Anyway, thanks. I know I've said it a bunch of times already, but sorry."

"Not sorry—I'd be happier if you said thank you instead."

When he bows his head in apology, Emilia presses her palm to Subaru's forehead to stop him from bowing further. At the smooth touch of her fingers, Subaru's eyes widen, and he manages to reply, "Guess so."

He'd thought this before, but Emilia's sense of personal distance is a bit much for Subaru. She approaches and touches him fairly without hesitation.
Come to think of it, the moment he'd woken up, he'd put both hands around her neck. And even then, she'd just stared at him with a blank, unbothered expression.

"Just what kind of life have I been living here, to end up this close with this girl..."

Or maybe, despite her frighteningly perfect looks, Emilia is simply abnormally familiar with people. No doubt she's kind to everyone, and having been raised loved, she doesn't feel any walls.
An untroubled, sheltered girl—with that impression, this closeness makes sense.
If you've never suffered or carried a single regret, you can go on believing only in others' good hearts, keeping those clear eyes. —Or is even that just a pose?

"――――"

Watching Emilia and Beatrice side by side, Subaru sinks into deep thought.
Put simply—very simply—if he thinks about it that way, the suspects most worth being wary of, regarding the previous massacre in the tower and Subaru's own death, are these two: Emilia and Beatrice.

He'd seen Shaula's corpse, Echidna's corpse, the corpses of Ram, Julius, and Meili.
Carried out by Patrasche, brought outside the tilting tower, Subaru had had his neck cut from behind by someone standing there, and lost his life. —He never saw the deaths of these two.

Of course, whether the two of them, even in collusion, could have managed to kill the remaining five is unclear. Still, Subaru's guess is that it would be difficult.
But suppose there were one more here—a third person added in. Wouldn't that change things?

"—Next, try and guess, hero."

He remembers an unfamiliar voice saying that to him, at the very end.
A voice heard amid the upheaval, in the whirlpool of confusion, in the despair of wanting it all to just be over.
Wasn't that the third party cooperating with Emilia and Beatrice—the unseen third person's true identity, who had carried Subaru from the library to the green room?
So the two of them told that outrageous lie—

"Ah, Subaru, you've gone too far."

"Huh, wha?"

He'd apparently walked right past their destination while lost in thought. Emilia, stopping in front of the room, grabs the shoulder of Subaru walking ahead to hold him back.
In that instant, Subaru feels a strange sense of unease and looks at Emilia's hand gripping his shoulder. Then he takes her hand and shakes it.

"Subaru? Whatever are you doing?"

"No, just a little experiment... Emilia, want to have an arm-wrestling match?"

"Eh, I think that sounds dangerous, we'd better not..."

The handshake itself provoked no resistance at all, but Emilia shows reluctance at the idea of a contest of strength. Seeing that reaction, Subaru grows all the more convinced, and says "Please" firmly.
If Emilia's slender arms lack the strength to carry Subaru, his earlier reasoning would be strongly supported. And right now, Emilia surely has no idea she's under suspicion.
To make use of this advantage, this moment is the only chance he'll get.

"Please, it's important."

"...If Subaru's this serious, it must really matter."

At Subaru's plea, Emilia, who had been hesitating, presses her lips together and nods with a determined expression.
Seeing that, Subaru inwardly clenches a fist and thinks, "Got her." All that's left is to prove her lack of strength, and his theory will make major progress.

"Then let's make it an arm-pull so it's not dangerous, I suppose. Is that all right?"

"Yeah, OK."
"Mm, got it."

Following Beatrice's instructions, Subaru and Emilia clasp hands and step apart. Gripping each other's right arms, the two look straight into each other's eyes.
And then—

"Ready, go, I suppose!"

At Beatrice's call, Subaru pulls at Emilia's arm with all his strength.

"Uwaaaaaaah—!!"

The grip strength that crushes apples, the grip strength built from swinging a bamboo sword aimlessly, the arm strength somehow maintained from never skipping push-ups, the physical difference between men and women—he poured all of it into the fight.


—Emilia's arm didn't budge an inch.


※※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※


Subaru's theory suspecting Emilia and Beatrice was back to square one.

Though it only confirmed that Emilia and the others could have carried Subaru to the green room without borrowing a third party's strength—it didn't mean their suspicion had been cleared. Subaru still hadn't seen their corpses.
It's still not settled that the culprit behind that massacre in the tower was someone else entirely.

"In the end, is it just, purely, that the one who showed up at the very end was the culprit?"

That one had, without doubt, killed Subaru.
But was that same one responsible for the shadow that swallowed the tower, and for the massacre of everyone before it? Everyone who'd been killed had died in a different way. What did that explanation account for?
The easiest deaths to make sense of at a glance were Echidna, cut down cleanly by something sharp; Julius, whose wounds had overlapped; and Meili, who'd bled to death.
There's no doubting that everyone had died fighting someone—or so it should be.

"So, what on earth was that little prank about?"

An exasperated voice is directed at Subaru, lost in thought.
It's Ram, preparing breakfast. Having rejoined them, she's setting out preserved food and water, and demands an explanation of the experiment Subaru had just been running—the arm-wrestling match with Emilia.

The contest meant to prove his theory ended with Subaru narrowly losing to Emilia. It was Ram, joining them in front of the hall, who provided the cue to end it.
Seeing Subaru's reddening struggle, she'd finished it off with a single "Aren't you an idiot?"

"That was because Subaru asked me to. I don't really understand it, but it must have had some really important meaning. Right, Subaru?"

"Is that so. To Ram, it just looked like you found an excuse to hold Lady Emilia's hand."

"That's way too cynical a read! What, you want to try arm-wrestling me next!?"

"How lewd."

"Why is that lewd!?"

To Ram's remark, dripping with vulgar implication, aimed at Subaru who'd tried to protect Emilia—pushing back against it only made his position worse. A true vicious cycle.

"Hah!"

With that derisive snort, Ram turns her attention back to serving the food. Watching her back, Subaru quietly breathes a sigh of relief that his current behavior doesn't seem to be raising suspicion.
It seems he still hasn't diverged from the "Natsuki Subaru" they know. He's relieved at that, but exasperated too.
He needs to keep his mind angled elsewhere like this, at least a little—

"Ugp."

For an instant, he suppresses a wave of nausea and puts a hand to his mouth.
The cause is Ram, working diligently with her back to him. —Her back overlaps, every time he blinks, with the way she died, burned into his eyelids.

Ram, struck from behind by a blow that had blown one side of her stomach clean apart, dead.
A death mask coated in fury and regret, her lovely face twisted by a curse, carving into her soul a wail that would tear to shreds everything her eyes had seen.
And here, right in front of him, the one who wore that death mask was moving, breathing, alive and well.

Honestly, the first moment he'd encountered her in the hall, he'd been frantic just to reconcile the wrongness with the reality in front of him.
He'd been grateful, at the time, that his straining from the arm-wrestling match with Emilia had given him an excuse for the dizzy spell that made him sink to the ground.

But he hadn't managed to watch, as he'd needed to, Ram's expression.

"――――"

Gritting his back teeth, Subaru forces strict self-restraint upon himself.
From here on, more and more people connected to the tower would gather in the hall. He mustn't miss a single person's expression. —Because that, if anything, might be the key to breaking this whole situation open.

"Uhyaa, that smells great-ssu! A luxurious meal on a refreshing morning! I really feel like I'm living for this, ssu~!"

Chattering noisily like that, the first of them appears in the hall.
A beautiful woman with long black braids swaying, her ample body shown off unashamedly—her head, still very much attached to her body, that's Shaula, appearing in the hall.

She gazes at the meal Ram is preparing, but the moment she notices Subaru at the back of the room—

"Master! Morning-ssu! Did you sleep well last night-ssu?"

Her expression brightens at once, and she comes bounding over with the energy of a puppy. Then, taking Subaru's arm, she pulls it tight against her chest.

"Uh..."

"By the way, I slept super well-ssu! Had an old dream for the first time in a while-ssu. Me and Master and Mama and then and then—"

"Ah, got it, hold on. I'll listen to the whole dream properly sometime. ...Hey, you—looking at me this morning, anything seem off?"

"Eh, that's a real vague question-ssu. So very Master-like!"

Prying his captured arm free somehow, Subaru edges away from Shaula while asking his question. In response, Shaula grins with an expression free of any malice.

"Honestly, looking at Master, nothing's different at all-ssu! Same as ever, same as always, in Shaula's book, the coolest guy around-ssu! Four hundred years and counting on that proposal-ssu!"

"That's a patient kind of wait. ...Jokes aside, if nothing's off, that's good."

"—? Is that so-ssu? Then Shaula won't worry about it either-ssu~."

Not getting the reaction he'd hoped for, Subaru feels some disappointment—and more relief than that—and withdraws his suspicion of Shaula.
As he does, the next suspect arrives in the room: Meili.

"Fwaa... Morning. Still sleepy this morning, huh..."

The girl passing through the entrance covers her mouth with a hand, yawning adorably. And yet, she's still managed to properly finish all her troublesome grooming—Subaru can only admire her sense of style.
Quite the contrast to Subaru, who just needs to wash his face and let his hair stay a mess.
Anyway—

"Morning, Meili. You slept in a bit this morning, huh."

"Thinking calmly about it, I don't really feel like there's a reason for me to wake up and get moving in step with you sisters. It's not like this tower's got anything to do with me anyway."

"That's an ill-natured thing to say, I suppose. Besides, if Betty and the others don't finish this tower's trial, you can't leave either, I suppose."

"Well, that's true, buuut... ah."

Caught making excuses for her yawning, Meili pouts at Beatrice's remark. Midway through, though, she notices Subaru and Shaula against the wall, and waves, smiling.
At that, Subaru raises an eyebrow, then waves back.

"Even suspecting a kid who was already dead—am I overthinking this...?"

"Master, when in trouble, go back to basics, ssu. Start with how to throw a jab-ssu."

"...What kind of teaching is that?"

"Master's teaching-ssu."

"No wonder it's useless."

Overheard muttering to himself, Subaru dodges Shaula's quip while inwardly on edge.
In any case, he'd gotten no expected reaction from Meili either. Which left only two people to test whether this approach would bear fruit—

"—Morning."

At that, the last two of the group arrive in the hall together.
Echidna and Julius. Echidna, wearing her white fox-fur stole, strides confidently into the hall with Julius in tow.
In turns, Emilia, Ram, and the others greet her, exchanging morning pleasantries.
And amid that—

"――――"

Julius, following behind Echidna, sees Subaru standing at the back of the room and his cheek visibly tightens. Then he averts his eyes and pushes Subaru out of his awareness.

"—You, huh."

That reaction is fairly close to what Subaru had been hoping for.
And given that not a single other person had shown such a reaction, the highest probability, he has to conclude, points to him—the one who'd shown such an unmistakable response.

"...Shaula, I need a favor."

Subaru mutters that, faintly, like something oozing out.
There, unquenched flames of anger and the lingering trace of something like simmered hatred had lived.


※※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※


—Breakfast in the hall was entirely taken up by an explanation session about Echidna=Anastasia.

Watching Emilia and the others' surprised reactions out of the corner of his eye while wearing an expression of "first time hearing this," Subaru is struck by how the world seems to move along like this when his own memory-loss issue isn't in the picture.
Up to this point, with so much unreality and misapprehension of facts piling up, Subaru still hasn't been able to grasp much about his own "Return by Death" trait. Still, much like the time-leaps common in stories, he understands: matters Subaru isn't involved in proceed the same way, while matters he does get involved in change.

In other words, because in prior loops he'd been at the very core of things as an "amnesiac," he'd hardly been able to get into the topic that ran deep—the fact that a being called Echidna dwelt inside the body of a girl named Anastasia.
This time, too, nothing happens that reveals a way to solve that problem, or brings a new fact to light. Change, it seems, is only generated by Subaru's own actions.
If he wants to dramatically move things forward, there's no way but for Subaru to actively act and get involved himself.
So—

"—Julius, I need to talk to you a moment."

As breakfast ends and everyone begins to leave the hall, Subaru calls Julius to a stop. Julius, halting at the call, turns back toward Subaru.
Since he's somewhat taller, standing face to face up close puts Subaru in the position of looking up at him. But not to be cowed by that pressure, Subaru squares his shoulders defiantly and locks eyes with Julius.
Sensing that spirit, Julius lets out a weary sigh through his thin lips.

"What is it? Wasn't the plan, at Lady Emilia's suggestion, to rest until noon?"

Julius's words respond to a suggestion Emilia had made at breakfast.
It seems Emilia, taking unusually seriously the notion that Subaru had exhausted himself and fallen asleep in the library, has been showing concern for everyone's accumulated, unseen fatigue.

In truth, though Subaru has no memory of it, this group had only just crossed the desert a few days ago.
Though Subaru himself had given up after only a few steps of it in his own experience, accomplishing that crossing must have taken considerable hardship. No one raised any objection to Emilia's suggestion.
This too could be called a change triggered by Subaru's own words. A careless remark can throw off the whole day's schedule. He needs to watch what he says and does.

Bearing that lesson in mind, Subaru squares off directly with Julius. Then, dipping his chin at Julius's words, he says:

"I want to talk about what happened last night."

The effect of that one line was dramatic.

"――――"

At Subaru's words, a complex, overwhelming welter of emotion passes through Julius's yellow eyes.
Seeing that immediate, telling reaction, Subaru's confidence in his own theory deepens.

"Come on. Let's change locations."

At Subaru's beckoning invitation, Julius follows along, looking as though he's resigned himself to it.
Subaru leads Julius on, choosing some appropriate room on the fourth floor as the site for their talk. Naturally, it's a private conversation, with no one else allowed to interrupt.

"Now, where to start..."

In the not-especially-spacious room, Subaru turns to face Julius.
There's a faint tension, but he doesn't want to make some blunder that shows it on his face. The thought that he presumably has the psychological upper hand to some degree stirs his courage.
Julius's expression, meanwhile, is complicated, hard to read. Still, it's clear that an uneasy state of mind has continued unbroken since the breakfast table.

"For now—about last night."

With a preamble like "for now," Subaru cuts straight to the heart of the matter.
As things stand, he holds the psychological advantage, but the informational advantage overwhelmingly belongs to the other side. To begin with, there's far too much about last night's events that remains unknown to Subaru.
Still, presumably, something happened between Subaru and Julius in the library last night.
As a result of that "something," Subaru had lost his memory—or so he suspects.

"Last night, huh."

Repeating Subaru's words, Julius closes his eyes, framed by long lashes. Facing the handsome man with his eyes shut, Subaru consciously repeats slow, deep breaths.
He mustn't be swallowed up. At the very least, he can't lose this on feeling alone.

"I, at least, believed that conversation ended where it was, in that place. Do you feel differently?"

"—. Yeah, I do. Not even close—I haven't accepted it, not even a little."

Julius plays along with the topic, and Subaru matches the conversation to it.
Julius's voice suppresses its inflection, careful, so to speak, not to show more emotion than necessary. Naturally, receiving that, Subaru chooses to bite back in response.

"Accepted, hm. I see, that sounds like you. In other words, you'd set aside my feelings and want to solve your own problem first? Isn't that being a bit too self-serving?"

"Something's slightly off there—that's not what I'm talking about. Sure, I haven't accepted it, but do you look like a man who has? Both of us have got something churning in our gut, and you want me to keep a calm face and act like nothing's wrong? Like hell I can."

"—Then, what other option is there?"

Julius, quietly suppressing his emotion. But little by little, feeling begins to spill into his voice.
Subaru too, still without knowing the details, senses in Julius's expression and tone the unmistakable overflow of some fierce passion directed at him, and it stokes a fire in the pit of his stomach.

What option—that's exactly what Subaru wants to hear.
Carrying that passion, what option had Julius taken toward Subaru? And how had Subaru made him choose it?

"My feelings were told to you last night, as they were. And I don't intend to say more than that. I never noticed—not in the slightest—your secret affair with Lady Anastasia... with Echidna."

"Me, and Echidna's... affair...?"

An unexpected fact leaps out, and this time it's Subaru caught off guard.
As far as he knows so far, Subaru is, after all, a member of a group with Emilia at its head. Julius and Echidna—in this case, meaning Anastasia, the original owner of Echidna's body—are master and retainer, and from a broad perspective, he'd heard they were in an adversarial relationship with Emilia's group.
But that Subaru, of all people, had been having an affair with that very Echidna—

"I understand you meant no malice. I've exchanged words with Echidna too, not that it was enough. She's someone I can trust... no, trusting her is the only path left."

"――――"

"To save Lady Anastasia, that possibility is the only hope left. ...Even if, once I've won her back, she doesn't remember me."

There's something terribly lonely in Julius's tone as he speaks those words.

Subaru has heard about Julius's own circumstances.
Like Ram's sleeping sister, like so many others in that other town, Julius too has met with something like a cursed fate of being forgotten by others.
Even if Echidna were to return control of the body to its rightful owner, that Anastasia would likely not remember Julius, her own knight.
And yet—

"—What I must do doesn't change. I don't know just what you're trying to drag out of me, but let me tell you this much in advance."

"...What."

"I beg you. ...Please, don't humiliate me any further in front of you."

At that weak voice, Subaru couldn't find words in the moment.

"――――"

There's only a deep unease churning in his chest, and Subaru falls silent.
Seeing him like that, Julius loosens his lips as if letting out a faint breath.

"It seems, it appears, this can only lead to a fruitless exchange from here."

With that, Julius turns his back on Subaru and starts to leave the room. Then, just before exiting, he stops once, just for a moment.
And, without turning to face him—

"Being asked about last night, I felt a little afraid. —Wondering what I ought to do if you spoke words of apology."

"If I... apologized..."

"What would I have answered you, I wonder. That too, I suppose, is something I no longer know."

Leaving behind a murmur tinged, somehow, with self-mockery, Julius exits the room.
Watching his back disappear, Subaru lets out a long breath. A weariness settles over him like a weight dropped on his shoulders, and sweat breaks out.
He feels as though he's become a thoroughly unpleasant person.

"—Master, was that all right, ssu?"

At that, crossing paths with the departing Julius, Shaula pokes her head into the room. At her carefree attitude, Subaru lets out a small "ha" of a sound.
Then, putting a hand to his forehead, he shakes his head. "It's fine."

"From the look of things, Julius probably has nothing to do with the library. ...Though it's made me a little more distrustful of myself again."

"Not sure I really get it, ssu, but was Shaula of use to Master?"

"—. Yeah, you were. Thanks to you, I could face Julius with some peace of mind."

"Ehehehe~, that's great, ssu~. So, so, so, Master, Master..."

Cheeks flushed red, body wriggling with delight, Shaula approaches Subaru. Then she timidly spreads both arms wide and—

"As a reward, Shaula wants Master to give her a big hug, ssu."

"That's a no."

"Ehhh!? So mean, ssu! Master, you said you'd listen to Shaula as long as she didn't go past reward-level stuff, ssu!"

"That request exceeds my innocence..."

Subaru coolly turns down Shaula's overly affectionate wish for physical contact.
Even so, it's true that having Shaula standing by in reserve had given him room in his heart for the confrontation with Julius.

Before calling Julius aside, Subaru had stationed Shaula in the next room as backup.
His reasons for choosing her: she seemed to have the highest physical ability among the other women in the group, her fondness for Subaru made it likely she'd do a favor for him, and she seemed like the type who'd help out without needing a detailed explanation.
There was also the calculation that, as the one who had first found the bodies, she was probably the least dangerous among the suspects.

Anyway—

"So the idea that Julius was the one who stole my memory in the library was off the mark, huh..."

From his reaction on seeing Subaru this morning, he'd been almost certain—but that guess had been wrong.
Whatever had happened in the library, however much of an event it must have been to make someone lose their memory, Julius hadn't touched on the matter at all just now—and his emotion had been far too natural for an act.

The affair with Echidna—whatever scheme "Natsuki Subaru" had been pursuing with that, he doesn't know, but at the very least, it's plenty of reason for the current Subaru to feel distrust toward his past self of last night.
That, on top of the fact that, apparently, before losing his memory, there had already been some unsettling exchange with Julius and Echidna.

"Just what were you up to, and who's after you, 'Natsuki Subaru'..."

"Master, if that's the case, how about jumping into Shaula's chest instead, ssu? Shaula will hug Master with this bouncy body, ssu."

"That request also exceeds my innocence."

"Master, so mean-ssu~!"

Speaking of not knowing, Shaula's abnormal fondness for Subaru is another thing he doesn't understand.
Why she likes and adores Subaru so much—apparently even Emilia and the others don't really know either, and had said they were just making convenient use of it. But is that really all there is to it?

The affair with Echidna, the murkiness of his actions last night, and even his relationships with them in general—including the fact that he can't talk to them right now—the most unknown existence to Subaru, right now, is "Natsuki Subaru" himself.
What kind of scheme had he been carrying, facing off with all of them?

—Had "Natsuki Subaru" really been their ally at all?

"This is bad, my thoughts are getting jammed up. ...Guess I'll go see Patrasche for a bit."

For now, he wants to go somewhere he can think calmly. When he considers that, the greatest ally rising up in his mind right now is that pitch-black Earth Dragon.
Having been protected at the cost of her life like that, memory or not, there's nothing to do but trust her.

"Ehh, off to see that Earth Dragon, ssu? Shaula's not too fond of that dragon—it always glares at her, ssu."

"I won't allow trash-talk about Patrasche. No matter what kind of world this becomes, where any other insult in the world might be allowed, I will never allow anyone to badmouth Patrasche."

"Master, how thoroughly whipped are you by that dragon!?"

Shaula raises what's practically a scream, but Subaru pays it no mind whatsoever.
Anyway, right now he wants to bask in peace and contemplation beside Patrasche. Either way, after lunch, the tower's true objective—clearing it—will begin.
As long as he's hiding the fact that he's lost his memory, there's no way to stop that from happening.

"So anyway, this is where you and I split up. See you later."

"Uweh, my reward's been brushed off entirely, ssu. But even so, Shaula won't be discouraged. A convenient woman for Master—that's Shaula's reason for existing, ssu."

"――――"

Just as they're about to part ways in front of the room, Shaula says that and snaps off a crisp salute. At her remark, Subaru holds his breath.
Turning his back to her, Subaru bows his head and closes his eyes.

"...Where's the value in going that far, in anything that is Natsuki Subaru?"

"—? Master?"

"—! Ah, damn it!"

Unable to hold back the irritation welling up, Subaru scratches roughly at his head and turns back toward Shaula, who's tilting her head in confusion.
Then, stepping up to her where she stands stock-still, he spreads both arms and pulls her slender body into a firm embrace.

"—!"

"Don't think about being a convenient woman or any of that. It's on me."

Meaning only to use her for his own one-sided ends, Subaru was on the verge of becoming just like "Natsuki Subaru."
At Subaru's action rejecting that, Shaula catches her breath, her body going stiff.

Shaula's height is tall; she's not that much shorter than Subaru.
Even so, the body he's wrapped his arms around is slender, its suppleness and softness worlds apart from his own. Though he had no room in his heart to be conscious of such a thing right then.

"Ma... ster..."

"I'm glad you were here. ...That's all."

"――――"

He probably held Shaula for a good thirty seconds or so.
Judging it's about time, Subaru lets her go. Honestly, he's a bit embarrassed, but the sense of accomplishment outweighs it. Better this than becoming someone who just takes gifts handed to him without a thought.
Better this than becoming the same as "Natsuki Subaru"—

"Master..."

Watching Subaru thinking that, Shaula, cheeks flushed, wobbles closer. Her eyes are moist, her breath somehow heated, her gaze fixed on Subaru's lips, and—

"Master, has Shaula finally won your love enough that..."

"Past my innocence!"

"Gueh!"

Pushing up the approaching chin with his palm, Subaru snaps Shaula back to her senses.

"Give her a reward and she gets carried away with it... a tough one to gauge, that girl..."

Somehow managing to calm the excited Shaula and extract himself from the situation, Subaru lets out that sigh.
In the end, Shaula had stayed in a dreamy daze even after all that, but had backed off with, "Knowing when to pull back is also a mark of a good woman, ssu. Secret makes woman woman, ssu."

Honestly, that's not remotely what the phrase means, but pointing it out felt like too much trouble.

"Well, there's such a thing as need-to-know in this world, after all."

Anyway, in contrast to that carefree little scene, Subaru's situation isn't looking particularly bright.
He'd at least hoped that prodding Julius might turn up something about what happened in the library, but now that too has come up empty, and he's essentially back to square one again.
Or perhaps, as originally assumed, no one else was involved in Subaru losing his memory. The only one whose expression he hadn't been able to confirm was Ram—

"If it turns out that near-crying look of hers was fake crying, I really won't know anything anymore..."

Ram was the one who'd stubbornly refused to believe the story about Subaru losing his memory.
Of course, there's a chance that was an act, or that she simply couldn't bring herself to admit some involvement of her own—but if he starts down that road, there's no end to it.
The reality is, if he doesn't trust something, he can't even begin to doubt anything else.
And for now, the foundation of that trust, for Subaru—

"—Right now, that's Patrasche, I guess."

Having said that outright, he's exasperated with himself—that's the conclusion of a man who's fallen into complete distrust of humanity.
But in truth, that's very much the case. Trusting other people is one thing, but now that he can't even fully trust himself, "distrust of people" is, there's no other way to put it, deep-rooted.

"Might honestly be happier living in some paradise with just Patrasche and Earth Dragons and nobody else..."

"—Ah, caught that, did I?"

"Wehh!?"

The instant he mutters that to himself, a girl comes bounding out from around a bend in the corridor. Blue braids bouncing, it's Meili, giggling with a hand over her mouth.
Realizing his pointless muttering had been overheard, Subaru's shoulders slump with dismay at the sight of her.

"It's a joke, just idle nonsense. You're not actually taking it seriously, are you?"

"Oh, of course not. But even if that was really your true feelings, big brother, I wouldn't laugh about it. If there were a place where I could live with only the bad animal-kins, I wouldn't mind living there myself, either."

"...Hmm."

To Subaru's awkward excuse, Meili, pinching the hem of her skirt, says that with a gesture that could be either refined or free-spirited—hard to tell which.
Even receiving that reply, Subaru has no idea how to respond. But it seems his half-hearted answer doesn't sit well with her.

"Ah, giving a half-hearted answer, are you? After I went to the trouble of talking seriously, not listening properly is proof of a bad boy, I think."

"My bad on that. ...Are you alone? Not with Beatrice?"

"Ohh come on, why would I be with Beatrice-chan? If anything, that's the question I'd ask you, big brother."

He'd simply thought that, being girls of similar age, they might hang around together, but apparently that's not the case.
It's true that Beatrice is often acting together with Emilia. —And so, in terms of suspicion, the two of them come as a set, hard to separate and consider individually.

"Well, that's fine if that's how it is. Anyway, I don't have time to play around with you. Sorry, but if you want someone to talk to, go find someone else. Shaula's over that way."

"Talking with the half-naked big sister sounds nice too, but right now I've got business with you, big brother."

"With me?"

Not wanting his rendezvous with Patrasche—or rather, his time to think—interrupted, Subaru nonetheless widens his eyes at Meili's unexpected invitation.
Come to think of it, like Shaula, Meili is another girl who, in Subaru's mind, somehow occupies a position that's easier to be around compared to Emilia and the others. Even in the previous loop's ending, her remains hadn't been destroyed to a point unbearable to look at.
She'd died as if sleeping, so even facing her directly like this, he doesn't have to contend with too much overlap of that shocking image. His heartbeat, too, stays calm.

"So what's your business with me? Something quick?"

"Yeah, I won't take up much of your time."

With that sense of reassurance, Subaru feels inclined to listen to what Meili has to say. And as Subaru settles into a listening posture, Meili touches a finger to her own lips, then licks the tip of that finger with her tongue.
A gesture, somehow sultry, that doesn't match her childlike appearance—Subaru frowns at it, and then—

"—About last night. Just how seriously should I be taking that, I wonder?"

Still wearing that alluring smile, the girl says that to Subaru.

・


"—Nn, ah?"

Suddenly released from a strange, suffocating sense of enclosure in his mind, Subaru presses a hand to his own throat.
There's a breathlessness, something scratchy in his throat, and he coughs.

"Koff, kofff..."

Touching his throat, tasting something like a painful dryness, Subaru coughs again and again.
And then, all at once, he notices.

"Huh, was I..."

—What had he been doing?

Right, up until just before this, he'd been talking with Julius, then there'd been that farce with Shaula, then he'd been heading toward Patrasche, and partway there, Meili had—

"Ow..."

Rubbing his own arm while thinking, Subaru clicks his tongue at a sharp pain.
Looking down, he sees his hands—both of them. On both his right and left wrists, just above them, something like scratches has been carved into his skin, painful blood seeping out.

"Guh, what the—this is..."

The wound is fairly deep, but the wound itself has an oddly warped look to it.
"Scratch" isn't quite wrong, but it's not far off either—it's certainly not the wound of a sharp blade. Deep, driven-in nails, a wound made with force enough to gouge into flesh.
A wound like this—where on earth had he taken it—

"—Ah?"

Grimacing at the pain, Subaru looks around for something to press against it. And then he realizes he's in one of the many similar stone-built rooms on the fourth floor—and then, he realizes something else.

"――――"

On the floor, a pair of white legs lies sprawled out.
The legs aren't moving, limp, all strength drained from them. Following the line of sight slowly upward from the feet, there's immediately a skirt, then an upper body comes into view, and then.

And then—

"—Huh?"

And then, there, a girl who's gone utterly still, not so much as twitching, lies collapsed.


—Meili lay collapsed before Subaru's eyes, breath gone from her body.
