Arc 7 Chapter 88: "Vent"

The siege of the imperial capital Lupugana — that battle began as a fierce clash in which neither side held anything back.

For the rebels dissatisfied with the current state of Vollachia, and for the regular army seeking to protect this reign, this could be called a total war between the two. But there was, properly speaking, no necessity for Emilia and her companions to take part in it.

To begin with, the reason Emilia's group had entered the empire was to search for the people they were looking for.
Of course, having made their way illegally into Vollachia, they had come to feel both affection and obligation toward the people they'd met along their travels. There were people they wanted to see find happiness, national borders be damned — kingdom or empire alike — and people they wished to lend their strength to. Everyone in the group agreed on that much.

But even a group of undeniable softies like Emilia's had priorities. Neglecting their original purpose would be putting the cart before the horse, and the group possessed the judgment (Otto, Petra) to restrain the conscience that reflexively wanted to help people (Emilia, Garfiel).
Even their participation in the battle at the walled city — thrown into chaos and screams by the flying dragon's attack — had been, at bottom, because they were following information suggesting their sought-after companion was there.
Now that lead had come up empty, logically speaking there was no reason left for Emilia and the others to join the rebel army's assault on the imperial capital.

In fact, even as word of the empire's steadily worsening, scorched-earth situation reached their ears day by day, Emilia's group's resolve held firm, and their sense of purpose had only been shaken to a minimal degree.

That should have been the case — and yet—

"—Given that it was the 'Dragoon General' who carried that girl off, her whereabouts will be either the dwelling of the Dragonkin Madelyn Eschart, or the residence of her keeper."

In a room on the top floor of the walled city's municipal hall, Abel stood before a map spread out on a table, touching the demon mask that covered his face as he answered Emilia's question.
Hearing Abel's answer, Emilia murmured, "Madelyn's keeper…"

—The time was several days before the siege of the imperial capital Lupugana.
Having headed for the Mad City only to miss their chance to rejoin Subaru — who was supposed to have returned to Guaral — and having likewise missed the now-awakened Rem, Emilia and the others were at a loss over what to do from here.
Since the fall of the Mad City of Chaosflame, Subaru's whereabouts were completely unknown, while for Rem, at least the identity of the one who'd taken her was known. Which search should take priority?
Wanting material to help her decide, Emilia had been given those opening words by Abel.

"That short-tempered Dragonkin wouldn't take kindly to hearing this, but the man is a general recommended by Chancellor Berstetz Fondalfon. Should anything happen to him, it would fall under the man's purview to return to."

"――――"

"What? Have you some doubt?"

"No, that's not it… it's just, the way you said 'keeper' — I think that's really, really not a good way to put it."

Emilia had exchanged few words with Madelyn — mostly they'd fought each other fiercely instead — but she thought Abel's phrasing just now was the kind of thing that would make Madelyn furious if she heard it.
At Emilia's pointing this out, Abel fell silent, narrowing his eyes behind the mask. She hoped he was reflecting on his own words and realizing they hadn't been good.

In any case, Abel's knowledge of the empire's internal affairs — things Emilia and the others didn't know — was a great help. With rebellion stirring the air in every corner of the empire, and even the sky and the dryness of the air seeming to worsen, Emilia, who liked to think things through carefully, felt no good omen from it. They kept missing every chance to meet the people they were searching for, and the timing in the empire was simply terrible.

"But if Madelyn went back to that person Berstetz's place, then maybe Rem too…"

"If Rem-chan's there, then big bro too!"

Following the thread of the conversation, Medium — clinging to the war table — chimed in. Catching the eye of Medium, who was stretching her small frame as far as it would go to make her case, Emilia's lips loosened. Medium's precious older brother was also among those taken away by Madelyn, along with Rem.
She must be terribly anxious and worried, and yet Medium never once showed a weakened side.
That strength of spirit — Emilia felt she ought to learn from it too.

"The chancellor… Lord Berstetz Fondalfon, isn't he the one they call extremely capable and talented, a linchpin of the empire?"

So interjected Petra, beside Emilia who was moved by Medium's gallant spirit.
Petra, who had insisted on coming along, unwilling to let Emilia go alone. At her murmur, finger to her lips, Emilia tilted her head. "Is that so?"

"A linchpin of the empire — that's a really splendid evaluation, but isn't that supposed to be the emperor?"

"Of course, I think it's the Emperor's own power that's kept the empire at peace all this time. But doesn't the Emperor's power also include making good use of capable subordinates?"

"Ah, that's true, isn't it. Even if the Emperor is really, really strong and really, really smart, it doesn't mean he can do absolutely everything by himself."

Emilia, who was diligently working at self-improvement, didn't think herself on the same level as the Vollachian Emperor. But she would have to reach that level eventually, and she ought to strive toward it. Yet no matter how hard Emilia tried, she would surely always be borrowing everyone's strength. That must be true for the Emperor of Vollachia as well.
However, alongside the admiring Emilia and Medium, Abel apparently held an entirely different impression. He sharpened the gaze behind his demon mask and pierced Petra with it.

"What do you hope to accomplish by going out of your way to recite such self-evident logic? Do you imagine there is room to indulge a child's prattle, with time this precious slipping away?"

"Sorry, I was being roundabout. I just wanted to ask this — if he's the Emperor's important right-hand man, then the chancellor's residence would be in the imperial capital, right?"

"――――"

Petra, unafraid of the sharp gaze, and Abel falling silent at her question.
Abel's fearsome eyes ought to feel like having a blade pointed at you, yet Petra didn't waver in the slightest. That was how strong Petra's heart was.
Strong enough that Emilia, her found family, wanted to puff out her chest with pride.

"The answer to your question is yes. Necessarily, then, the two Madelyn Eschart carried off — Rem and Flop O'Connell — will likewise be under that Berstetz's charge, in the imperial capital."

As though worn down by Petra's gaze, Abel answered in a low voice. At that answer, Emilia moved to praise Petra, as expected of her.
But Petra's own expression was far from pleased — if anything, she let out a visible sigh, and said,

"…It feels like we're being maneuvered. I don't like it."

"Eh? What do you mean by that?"

Not understanding the meaning of Petra's reaction, Emilia blinked. In response to Emilia's words, Petra, still wearing a sour expression, lowered her voice.

"We have nothing to do with the problems happening in this empire. I understand it's going to become a really huge battle, and I'm worried about Medium-chan and Utakata-chan, but…"

"—. Yeah, it's okay. I understand what you're trying to say. We came here to look for Subaru and Rem, the two of them."

Getting ahead of Petra's reluctant words, Emilia chose her words gently.
She understood Petra's anxiety and concerns. If nothing else were at stake, even Emilia would want to keep going along with Abel and the others like this, lending her strength for the sake of everyone they'd met. But if they kept chasing only the problems right in front of them, they'd never truly reach Subaru and the others, which was their real purpose—

"Huh?"

Having thought that far, Emilia felt something catch in her mind.
Just as she'd told Petra, Emilia and the others' purpose was to bring Subaru and Rem home safely. So however painful it was, they had to pass by the fighting happening in Vollachia without getting involved.
That's what she'd been thinking, but—

"But right now, Rem might be in the imperial capital. In that case…"

"—We have to go to the imperial capital too."

The doubt Emilia had been holding dissolved like ice, and across that melted ice, her eyes met Petra's.
Petra had noticed that fact early on — that was why she'd been wearing such a bitter expression. As if to confirm Emilia's surprise, Petra glared at Abel.
And then—

"That's cowardly. Staying silent until we had to be the ones to bring it up."

"It is logic that connects into a single thread of its own accord, if one reasons it through. To berate me as base, while conveniently overlooking your own poor circulation of blood, is nothing short of arrogance."

"Then explain it to me. You said there were two places the 'Dragoon General' Madelyn might return to — the chancellor's in the capital, and one other. But you kept talking only about the capital, so that Emilia wouldn't notice the other one."

"――――"

"Taking advantage of someone's ignorance and exploiting it — where I come from, we call that shameless."

Sharp as static, Petra glared as if reproaching Abel. At Petra's accusation, Emilia — who had been thoroughly and neatly deceived by the omission of information — drooped the corners of her brows.
On Abel's side too, Medium, who had been peering at the map beside him, looked up at him as if to caution him about his methods, murmuring "Abel-chin…"

Emilia felt properly chastened. At the same time, she understood well enough why Petra was angry.
Setting aside the concealment itself, the preceding matter — as Abel said — was something Emilia could have realized herself if she'd thought it through carefully enough, but he could have told her all the same.
Of course, Emilia didn't intend to rely on others for every problem either.

"Abel, you were the one saying we don't have time. That goes both ways, so don't be mean about it."

"That too could be called a statement born of arrogance. Why should I need to painstakingly and courteously explain the logic of things to you?"

"—? If you want to borrow our strength, you have to say please, don't you think?"

"—. What a woman who fails to meet me halfway."

Crossing his thin arms, Abel let out a long, heavy sigh.
But at his response, Emilia held an entirely different opinion: "Is that so?" It wasn't that they failed to meet halfway — it was that Abel disliked meeting halfway.

"Right? Because if you're about to fight the biggest battle in Vollachia, you should want to borrow our hands too. We're really, really strong, you know."

"Emilia, your phrasing might be a little concerning."

"We're really, really reliable, I mean!"

"You're reliable. Especially Emilia and Garf-san."

Beside the rephrased Emilia, Petra puffed out her chest and said so too.
Emilia's group — Garfiel was unquestionably strong in battle, and Emilia had confidence in her own fighting too. Otto and Petra were very clever, and Frederica was always a master of consideration. Until they were reunited with Subaru, Beatrice, who couldn't be overworked, was resting, but the thought of needing to protect her made strength well up abundantly.
And so—

"With us there, wouldn't your goal absolutely be easier to accomplish? And yet all you can manage is this kind of talk — is it because you don't want us to think you asked us for a favor?"

"――――"

"Roswaal's bad at saying please too, but…"

Abel was probably, like Roswaal, the type who didn't want to show weakness to others.
Because of that, he tried to make the other party say things first, rather than saying them himself. Maybe that had worked out for him all this time.

"I think you should stop before someday you get a really, really big comeuppance for it. Before you end up like Roswaal, getting hit by Medium-chan or Yorna or Zikr-san."

When Roswaal schemed something that involved the mansion and the "Sanctuary," he'd tasted the comeuppance of getting slapped across the face by everyone, Emilia included.
Not that Emilia, who had participated, was in a position to talk, but that had really hurt — and Roswaal, being well-trained, got off with just a swollen face, but there was no telling what would happen to Abel.
That was setting aside her worry for Abel's body in that regard, too.

"Is what I'm saying completely off the mark? If so, that would be embarrassing, but tell me so. Then I'll apologize, and think of something else."

"Something else?"

"I don't think you're someone who can't think of anything, someone who can't consider what's ahead. Continuing to wonder what someone's thinking is an important thing to do."

The old Emilia had been quick to give up on things she didn't understand.
Quick to blame something else and give up, spoiling herself with the comfortable idea that doing whatever Puck said was the right answer. Lately she'd often borrowed the strength of people good at thinking, like Ram and Otto. But it wouldn't do for that to simply become a matter of switching who she relied on.

Petra had said the Vollachian Emperor was very good at making use of people. But even an emperor skilled at using people must have thought for himself about what to use them for.
And someone in an exalted position, someone at the center of everyone, had to be able to do that.
Aiming for that was what Emilia had chosen to do.

"—What a heartening thought. I'm glad, Emilia."

Sharply, a voice from behind praised Emilia as she faced off with Abel. A voice familiar enough that she didn't need to turn around — its owner was the reliable Otto.
He'd been off on his own, saying he had something he wanted to look into — but it must have been Abel, not this municipal hall, he'd had business with.

"Otto-kun, you had business with Abel too? Were you okay alone?"

"Thank you for your concern. But there's no need to worry so much…"

"Really? But Otto-kun, there was that time you got kidnapped while everyone had their eyes off you, so…"

"Not a word to say in my defense! …Well, I did take some precautions this time, at least."

Otto, his voice rising at Emilia's concern, and right behind him, Garfiel appeared. He seemed to have accompanied Otto on his investigation.
With relief, Emilia's chest settled, and she said,

"Thanks, Garfiel."

"Hah, ain't nothin' worth thankin' me for. More importantly, what the hell were you two — Emilia and Miss Petra — talkin' about?"

"Um, lots of things, but… thanks to Petra, I didn't get talked into anything. I didn't get talked into anything, right?"

"Yeah, it's okay, Emilia. You were really reliable — it surprised me."

"Fufu, right?"

In front of Otto and the others, Emilia and Petra, having joined forces, high-fived. Emilia, like Garfiel, had worked hard with all her might to protect Petra, and it had paid off.
Otto, watching the two of them with softened eyes — but then he turned to face the demon-masked man across the war table, saying "Abel-san,"

"I can roughly guess what you were discussing with Emilia and the others. If Rem-san was carried off by one of the 'Nine Divine Generals,' and her destination is the imperial capital Lupugana, then we've already become enemies of the empire — is that the gist of it?"

"—! Amazing! Otto-kun, were you eavesdropping?"
"Eavesdropping, you say?"

"Emilia, maybe, but even Petra-ojousama too… I wasn't eavesdropping at all!"

"I see. Then it really is amazing."
"…Were you seriously not eavesdropping, Garf-san?"

"Emilia, maybe, but Petra-ojousama is…"

Emilia, impressed, and Petra, cross-checking the facts with Garfiel. Otto, wry-smiling at the two opposite reactions, gave a small cough after correctly guessing the just-prior conversation.
Then, turning eyes emptied of playfulness toward Abel,

"I've just come from hearing accounts from people who arrived in the city from outside. Voices of rebellion are rising in various places, that's true, but they're also gathering here in growing numbers, one after another."

"Given the origins of this affair, and the incident in which the 'Dragoon General' was repelled, that much is only natural. As is the fact that Yorna Mishigure has brought the Mad City's residents and made this place her base."

"Yes, that would be so. —Masterfully done."

Nodding along, Abel responded flatly to Otto.
The content of that exchange itself was something Emilia already understood. Over these past few days, many people had gathered in Guaral, and Emilia herself had been lending a hand repairing damaged homes. Though, since Frederica and Petra had warned her not to do too much heavy labor, her role was mostly freezing things like pillar repairs with magic to help out.

In any case, there was nothing new in the content. And yet Emilia felt a faint sense of unease — probably because of Otto's demeanor.
As always, quiet, clever, dependable Otto — but what Emilia sensed in his soft profile felt like anger he couldn't quite hide, no matter how he tried.

"How far did your groundwork go?"

"――――"

That was the one line that pushed Emilia's vague suspicion into certainty.
At Otto's question, Abel fell silent. But Emilia took it not as him considering his answer, but as a pause meant to make his opponent stew. Because Abel's gaze never wavered, his expression never changed. He'd already decided his answer — he was only being coy about voicing it.

"How much of this is going according to your design, I wonder."

Otto repeated the same question, rephrasing it.
Being made to stew, she didn't think of it as losing patience. She wasn't fond of talk of winning and losing, but in this scene, the winner and loser had already been decided. Otto's manner was that of someone asking the reason for his defeat — and his defeat was not something unrelated to Emilia and the others either; it was the same problem.

"—Your existence only became known to me upon returning to this city. To have factored that much into a scheme would require something akin to reading the stars themselves."

"So you're saying it was mere coincidence?"

"I have no intention of dismissing it as mere coincidence. Fortune, after all, is but the last of many factors."

Shaking his head once, side to side, Abel decisively denied Otto's words.
Seeing Otto's cheek stiffen at those words, Emilia couldn't help but tug at his sleeve. And put an end to letting him bear his sense of defeat alone.

"Otto-kun, what does that mean?"

"…There was already a report on the situation Natsuki-san currently finds himself in. I thought it was quite an absurd turn of events."

"He's small now, just like Medium-chan, right?"

Hearing Otto's account, Petra glanced at Medium beside Abel.
Medium had told them how she'd been attacked by an enemy in the Mad City and turned from an adult into a child. Not just her — Subaru had apparently been placed in a similar state. And in order to protect Subaru in that vulnerable condition,

"Abel spread a rumor that a black-haired, black-eyed boy is the Emperor's child, right? That way, even if people who might think ill of Subaru found him…"

"To verify the truth of the matter, his life would not be taken. I too thought it a fine plan at the time. Whatever the case, it would lower Natsuki-san's degree of danger, or so I thought. But—"

"Somehow, it don't seem to be just about the big guy bein' safe anymore, apparently."

Picking up from Otto's remark, Garfiel spoke with his arms crossed. "Eh," Emilia turned, and Garfiel gnashed his sharp fangs.

"All the folks pourin' into the city, every damn one of 'em's here to fight the Emperor. I ain't got nothin' to say about that part. But if it means the big guy's in danger, that's a different story."

"Subaru's in danger…"

"…I see, so that's how it is."

Beside the frowning Emilia, Petra murmured as though she'd realized something. Then she sharpened her round eyes and, like Otto, glared at Abel.

"A black-haired, black-eyed boy… I don't know if he's really the Emperor's secret hidden child or not, but the soldiers can't carelessly lay a hand on him."

"Eh, yeah, that's right. So Subaru is safe and…"

"But he's only safe until they confirm whether that boy is the real one or not. So where do they take that boy to confirm it?"

"Where, huh… ah."

Following Petra's reasoning as she organized her thoughts, Emilia finally caught up to the point.
Because of the rumor Abel had spread, Subaru — turned into a child, a black-haired, black-eyed boy — would be sought out, and whichever child was found would be taken away to confirm whether he was the real one.
And that place was, of course—

"—The imperial capital, where the Emperor is?"

"Unwittingly, both the things you seek lie in the imperial capital. That saves some trouble, does it not."

"How shameless…!"

As Emilia arrived at the conclusion, Abel nodded deeply, as though he'd been waiting for it. At once, Petra raised her voice at Abel, sharply condemning his attitude.
But Abel merely shrugged at Petra's anger, and said,

"I did say as much — that your existence was, to me, beyond expectation. It is merely that the rumor I spread to stoke the rebels' existence happened to have that effect as well."

"—!"

"Or would you rather claim this: that I deliberately shrank Natsuki Subaru and made him disappear, spread a rumor to confound the entire empire, and, while I was at it, had one of the 'Nine Divine Generals' abduct the healer-skilled oni girl — that all of it was my own contrivance? I am a busy man indeed."

Spoken as though mocking her, Petra's expression stiffened.
Naturally, neither Otto nor Garfiel found it amusing to see Petra treated that way. So it was Emilia who moved first — and Abel ought to be relieved of that.
Emilia stepped forward as if to shield Petra, staring fixedly at the arms-crossed Abel.

"I don't think of you as someone who knows everything about everything. Don't tease Petra-ojousama with weird phrasing like that. If you do the same thing again…"

"If I do the same thing again, what?"

"That comeuppance I talked about earlier — I'll be the very first one to give it to you!"

Thrusting her clenched fist forward, Emilia declared it firmly.
Emilia thought that ideally, everything should be resolved through discussion, but for someone who simply wouldn't listen, who couldn't be reasoned with, a raised fist was sometimes unavoidable. All the more so when the one spoken ill of wasn't Emilia herself, but her precious found family.

"—. Not a shred of subtlety. Is your foremost representative always like this?"

"Yes, it's often quite a struggle when she doesn't choose her words carefully. But—"

"That's exactly why it's worth the trouble. Way better'n some bastard who reasons everythin' out, blocks every road, and thinks that'll force people to walk the same way."

At Abel's narrow-eyed remark, Otto and Garfiel each responded so.
Somehow it felt like they were talking about her rather than Petra, the supposed provisional representative, but Emilia didn't dig into that too deeply.
Right now, what everyone needed to shield and discuss on Petra's behalf was—

"Point is, the big guy who ain't here and Rem too, both of 'em might be in the imperial capital."

"And given that the site of the decisive battle between the regular army and the rebel army is highly likely to be the imperial capital, we have no option of simply passing it by."

"Shameless…"

Subaru and Rem — both might be in the imperial capital Lupugana.
Maybe Rem was somewhere else, and maybe Subaru, too, was just being gathered into the capital along with the other black-haired boys, and this was all getting their hopes up for nothing.
Even so, if this was the most likely possibility, then it was as everyone said.

"I've decided. Let's all go to the imperial capital together. We have to find Subaru and Rem."

"Emilia, the decision belongs to Petra-ojousama."

"Ah, that's right! Um, what do you think, Petra-ojousama? Is it okay to go with what I decided?"

"—. Yeah, it's okay, Emilia. Honestly, I don't like that it feels like everything's going exactly the way that person wanted, but…"

Fixedly, Petra glared toward Abel, but then quickly softened her expression.
She'd swallowed her frustration at being talked circles around by Abel, for the sake of Subaru and the others. Accepting Petra's consideration gratefully, Emilia turned back to Abel, saying "That's right."

"Hey, Abel, Petra-ojousama said everything's going exactly the way you wanted, but…"

"I did say as much — that it is impossible for me to have contrived and guided everything. Or do you, too, regard me as some being beyond the ordinary, same as that girl?"

"No, I don't think that at all. Abel is smart, and someone who wears a weird mask, and… I think you're also, after all, someone who's just bad with people."

Finger to her lips, Emilia answered, tilting her head so.
When the topic of the mask came up, Abel's hand lightly touched his demon mask, and right behind her, Emilia heard small laughs from Otto, Garfiel, and Petra.
Thinking it was good to see those three react that way, Emilia was asked by Abel, "What sort of evaluation is that."

"An evaluation I've never heard before. Why would you assess me as such?"

"Bad with people? Um, the reason I thought that is… oh, right! It's about Subaru."

"About Subaru? What do you mean?"

For a moment, Petra's eyes widened as Emilia searched for the path her own thoughts had taken.
To Petra, who saw Abel as a master schemer, Emilia's meaning probably didn't quite make sense. Emilia herself thought Abel was a master schemer too, but—

"Abel said it himself, didn't he? That our being here was, for him, a pleasant surprise."

"Do not dress up my words as you please."

"But it's true that it helps you to have us here, isn't it? You haven't once said that's wrong. So, let's go back to the topic, okay?"

"――――"

"Us being here was unplanned, but Abel spreading the rumor about the black-haired boy for Subaru's sake was planned… and if that raised the chances that Subaru is in the imperial capital, then that's exactly what you planned, isn't it?"

Getting a little tangled up saying "planned" and "unplanned" so many times, Emilia carefully chose her words, organizing them to properly sort out Abel's thinking.
Even without Emilia and the others, he'd told the lie that the Emperor's child was cooperating with the rebel army. By doing so, the moments of danger for the now-child Subaru would be reduced, and even if someone caught him, he'd be brought to the imperial capital to verify whether the lie was true—

"The lie Abel told — isn't it a lie meant to raise, even just a little, the chance that Subaru will be in that place when everyone fights in the imperial capital?"

"—Guh, that might be so, but why the hell would this guy do that for the big man—"

"Isn't it obvious? —Because Subaru is really, really reliable!"

Which scheme was for what purpose, whose aims tangled with whose — untangling all of it alone was difficult for Emilia. But the answer to the question Garfiel voiced was plain.
The many schemes Abel had woven were a plan to draw Subaru to the site of the decisive battle.
If he'd prepared it so it wouldn't be found out, it must be quite embarrassing for him now.
Even so—

"If you'd just properly asked for help, I'm sure Subaru would have listened."

Perhaps there were times when saying "please" was harder than trying to manipulate someone. Emilia had never tried to manipulate anyone, so she didn't really understand it, but there were times when one couldn't bring oneself to say please.
And how much could that situation — being unable to say please — be reduced. How much was it all right to rely on someone by saying please.
Being able to draw that line properly for herself was Emilia's current goal.

"――――"

Having said that, Emilia fixed her gaze on Abel, waiting for his answer.
Across the demon mask, black pupils and violet-and-indigo eyes clashing, Emilia suddenly noticed something. —Abel never blinked both eyes together. He always kept one eye open at all times. Didn't his eyes dry out?

—Pushing himself that hard, she wondered, didn't his heart dry out too?

"What is it. What are you waiting for."

"Eh?"

"What reason is there for me to entertain your rambling words, responding this way and that? Do not presume this whimsy will continue indefinitely."

"Abel-chin!"

At the end of his continued silence, Abel moved to end the conversation. At his way of putting it, Emilia's eyes widened, but it was Medium who raised her voice instead.
Having quietly watched the conversation unfold, she tugged at Abel's sleeve beside her and said,

"That was super uncool just now! Even I can tell, Abel-chin, you lost!"

"I have no recollection of making this a contest of winning and losing. Stop pulling my sleeve. I have no spare."

"If you run out of clothes, just dress like a girl again! It suits you, Abel-chin!"

Roughly, tugging his sleeve hard once more, Medium then stuck her tongue out at Abel. She scurried around the war table and came before Emilia and the others, saying,

"I don't get difficult things either. But if Rem-chan's in the imperial capital, then big bro's probably there too… I want to see the two of them again. And Subaru-chin too! So, so…"

Fumbling but earnest, Medium suddenly bowed her head deeply. Her long golden hair flew over her head as if flung, falling at Emilia and the others' feet.
But Medium, not even noticing that, said,

"Please! Abel-chin's like that, but please, Emilia-chan and everyone, help too!"

With all her might, Medium's voice trembled as she said so.
At her request, Emilia couldn't help but soften her eyes. Then, past the bowing Medium, she looked at Abel standing beyond her.

"Abel, I think this is what it means."

So told by Emilia, Abel looked at Medium's bowed back. Then, whatever he thought behind his demon mask, without letting it show in those black pupils, he said,

"I take this as your declaration of intent — that you too will join the assault on the imperial capital."

"Shameless!"
"Abel-chin—!"

And the two young girls' angry voices scolded Abel, who couldn't say please.

—With that interlude behind them, Emilia's group resolved to join the decisive battle for the imperial capital.

Honestly, there was no telling how much Abel had calculated in setting up the board.
Perhaps, as he'd told Petra, everything concerning both Subaru and Rem was exactly according to Abel's calculations, or perhaps it really was all mere coincidence.
If there was one thing that could be said, it was this—

"Let's not get too caught up in what Abel-san says or does, and simply accomplish our own purpose."

"Yeah, I think what Otto-san says is right too. …What about Dudley?"

"For now, it seems he's managed to link up with the acquaintance who was his own minor objective — Count Dracroy. And apparently the count in question is quite eager for rebellion."

"So then that count and Dudley… Roswaal too, are headed to the imperial capital?"

"It seems so. —All the more reason we can't let our guard down."

Roswaal, who had split off from Emilia's group to visit an acquaintance in the Vollachian Empire, seemed to be caught between results and problems over there as well.
Still, it appeared both Roswaal and Ram would end up heading to the imperial capital Lupugana.
If Subaru and Rem really were in the capital, then everyone would end up gathering there at once.

"However, what awaits us there is the center of a great upheaval engulfing the entire empire… frankly, the circumstances have changed completely from what we originally anticipated."

"Well, sis, don't tell me you're gettin' cold feet now, of all times."

"I'm not faint of heart, but I do have no small amount of anxiety. Interfering in another country's strife is a problem in itself, and if it becomes a battlefield…"

Eyes downcast, Frederica hardened her expression and hesitated over what came next.
What she was reluctant to voice was something everyone around her could imagine — if their destination was the decisive battle for the imperial capital, both sides would surely suffer tremendous casualties.
An unavoidable great upheaval, one that should properly have been a neighboring country's affair.

"Frederica-neesama, I'm happy you're worried about me. But…"

"Of course I'm worried about you too, Petra. But I myself am afraid. I'm sorry — because I'm such a coward."

"Neesama…"

Frederica, shaking her head resolutely even so, and Petra, lowering her eyes at the consideration filling her voice.
Many people would be hurt, or lose their lives, in that battle — Frederica's anxiety about taking Petra to such a place was understandable.
That unease, that resistance, wasn't only Frederica's; everyone shared it.
And so—

"If we weren't in the empire, we might have heard about this as something happening in a neighboring country, just furrowed our brows and let it pass. But."

"But?"

"We're here, and we've come to know people who are going to fight in this battle, so even if we could run away from here now, just furrowing our brows wouldn't be enough anymore."

Even if the same number of lives were lost either way, it feels different depending on whether you know the person or not.
Life is very precious, not something to be compared against anything else. So if lives must be compared, it can only be against other lives in the same way. —And that, too, is surely a very self-righteous way of thinking.
But the resolve to carry through with her own selfish thinking, Emilia had already made up her mind about, long ago.

"If we take part, I think we can reduce the number of people who die."

"Emilia — no, Emilia-sama. That's quite the thorny path."

At Emilia's words, hand on her chest, Otto responded, correcting how he addressed her.
Even in places with no one but their inner circle present, Otto had stubbornly kept up the false name to avoid any slip. That alone made his seriousness here plain to see.
Not responding to that seriousness in kind would be discourteous, so Emilia nodded seriously as well.

"Mm, I understand. No, maybe Otto-kun has realized something far more difficult than what I'm imagining, but I'll manage that too somehow."

"Our goal is to bring Natsuki-san and Rem-san home. Even if only two people die in this decisive battle, if those two are Natsuki-san and Rem-san, it's a failure. Conversely, even if every last person from the empire dies, if those two are safe, we've won."

"Otto-anii, no matter how you slice it, that's—"

"I'm speaking with Emilia-sama right now."

To Otto stringing his words together in a quiet voice, Garfiel couldn't help but try to cut in. But Otto brushed it aside, seeking an answer only from Emilia.
Meeting Otto's eyes, Emilia's violet-and-indigo gaze trembled faintly.
And then—

"I'm sorry, Otto-kun. Ever since we came to the empire… no, ever since before we came to the empire, since we decided we had to go save Subaru and the others, you've been working so hard the whole time."

"…That's my role, after all. So there's no need to factor in how I—"

"Yes, I understand. —What we need is our full strength. And Otto-kun is necessary for our full strength, so I'll have you keep working hard until we can meet Subaru and the others. Even if Otto-kun collapses, I'll carry you on my back by force. So."

"――――"

"So, saving Subaru and the others, and making sure as few people from the empire die as possible — let's do both, working really, really hard together!"

She was aware she was saying something outrageously unreasonable, and she knew full well it was a very selfish request. Even so, Emilia had stopped compromising before she'd even begun doing what she wanted to do.
And so—


△▼△▼△▼△


"—Let's open a vent in this battle."

A fiercely cold wind raged, white air burying Vollachia's green grasslands.
Buffeted by the falling temperature and the chilling air, many combatants ground to a halt.

All around, the ones stopping in their tracks, breathing white breath, were people with a striking feature to part of their bodies. Men and women alike, regardless of age, shared one thing in common — weapons growing from their bodies.
Some had arms that were blades; others had legs entirely made of iron. Some had deformed heads shaped like hammers, and others had backs that were themselves shields.

She'd heard that Vollachia was home to many kinds of Demi-Humans, but true to an empire that had drawn people together from every corner, it was a battlefield of endless surprises.
Still, even with bodies turned to weapons, that didn't mean they could smash through every problem before them, or protect against everything — as proven by the wounded, fallen bodies before her.
And so, taking a deep breath,

"Everyone! Get away from here! I'll do my really, really best from here!"

Calling out to the fallen — Weaponkin — Emilia strode forward in a hurry with long steps.
She meant to draw the enemy's attention to herself, so they couldn't be struck again. But that thought of Emilia's proved unnecessary.

It wasn't that it hadn't worked. The enemy's eyes were already more than sufficiently drawn to her.
Because waiting atop the castle wall Emilia had headed for was—

"—You again, is it."

Even the impression made by seeing Weaponkin for the first time, which had so impressed Emilia, felt shallow beside this — a presence rare even in Vollachia, that melting pot of countless races.
Eyes shining gold, two dull-gleaming black horns on her head. Catching the thrown Flying Winged Blade in her small hand as it returned, a girl clad in an intimidation that belied her small stature.
Looking up at the one standing there, Emilia sharply pointed a finger and said,

"Yep! It's me again, Madelyn! Passing-through Spiritual Arts user, Emilia!"

"An infuriating girl, you are. In our last battle, that you cannot best a dragon—"

Gritting her teeth hard, Madelyn's expression twisted into something fierce and menacing.
Marking Emilia as an enemy, meaning to avenge the humiliation of their previous battle in Guaral, Madelyn raised her Flying Winged Blade against Emilia below—

"—There!"

In the next instant, just as before, a mass of ice larger than the municipal hall came crashing down from the sky onto Madelyn, and with a thunderous roar, the battle between Emilia and Madelyn began.
