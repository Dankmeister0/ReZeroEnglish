Arc 9 Chapter 26: "Sword Devil VS Crimson Cherry Blossom"

A ferocious sword-intent hung thick in the room, and without exaggeration, it raised gooseflesh on the skin.
She had trained her heart not to be shaken by most situations, but even amid a string of the unexpected and the unforeseen, a moment like this had to be called an exception — an aberration.

"—Wilhelm Trias."

Yae Tenzen rolled the given name around in her mouth, quietly deepening her guard.
They had only crossed blows once, but on that first exchange she'd taken a kick straight on, and Yae was the one at a disadvantage.
Even after leaping back to absorb the impact, the damage from that kick sat heavy in Yae's slender frame. Had she not retreated, the force might have punched a boot-shaped hole clean through her stomach. To land a kick like that on a young woman without holding back — well, just as the man himself had said, it was hardly the conduct of a gentleman.
A name fit for this old swordsman had been on hand in this kingdom for more than forty years now.
Namely—

"—Fancy that — the 'Sword Devil' himself makes an appearance. I'm just thrilled to meet a celebrity~. The tale of avenging your late wife — now that really is the pride of the Kingdom's swordsmanship, isn't it~?"

"—"

"Oh my? But if I recall correctly, I heard there was an unexpected reunion with your wife after that! What happened in the Watergate City must have been terribly painful for you, hm? My condolences, truly — boo hoo hoo."

"—"

Yae dabbed at the corners of her eyes, feigning tears, all while watching the man's every move.
Under Yae's crimson gaze, the old swordsman — Wilhelm — stood without so much as a flicker of wavering, dignified, sending a keen, clarified sword-intent through the twin blades in his hands, his eyes fixed steadily on her.
In the quiet light of those blue eyes, Yae understood that her deliberately tactless attempt to trample his heart and shake him had been a dud — she'd only succeeded in playing the part of a nasty woman.

"...From what I'd heard, I figured he'd be more of a hothead~."

It seemed that intel, too, couldn't be relied on.
The source of that information had been none other than Heinkel, so if even his account of the man who should have been his own father was unreliable, what on earth was she supposed to expect from him? She might as well have put him in charge of babysitting Felt instead of the 'Dragon.'
And if Heinkel had then let Felt slip away, that would have given her both a pretext to dispose of the girl and a pretext to cut Heinkel loose, all in one stroke.
The reason Yae was even entertaining such thoughts was—

"Aside from the old man who heads our village, there really shouldn't be many people who could notice me in hiding — Felt-sama, did you happen to do something naughty, by any chance?"

"That's a hell of an accusation, even for you. You're the one who knows best that I can't lift a finger."

"—"

"I haven't done a damn thing besides talk to you. That's about all the effort I've got in me right now."

Felt, pulled back into a corner of the room, answered with a note of self-directed anger in her voice.
Faking anger wasn't the sort of clever trick her personality was suited to. If she possessed acting skill precise enough to even adjust the tremor in her voice, then bravo — what masterful deception.
So Yae gave the steel thread looped around Felt's neck a light tug, just enough to make her aware of it, and said—

"Now, don't go thinking of running off, all right? I'd hate to make Felt-sama's cute little neck part ways with her torso in tears."

"That's one more kick to the ass added to the tab. Also — not that I've got much room to talk, but—"

"What is it?"

"You don't exactly have time to be looking away right now, do you?"

Felt stuck out her tongue, and the very lack of cuteness in the gesture was, somehow, cute. But she needn't have said it — Yae had never once let her guard down from Wilhelm. Wilhelm, still standing exactly where he had been, had yet to settle on how to answer Yae's unfamiliar Steel Thread Technique. And that was only natural.

—The great majority of Shinobi Techniques are specialized for striking at an opponent's blind spots.
That is precisely why, unlike a warrior's combat arts — skills for fighting — a Shinobi's skills, meant for killing, are called Techniques. Yae's Steel Thread Technique was foremost among them.
The more battle-hardened a warrior, the more caution he exercises in how he fights. And in that interval, laying trap after trap, ten-fold, twenty-fold, like a spider's web — that is the true domain of the Shinobi—

"—Fourth move."

"—? What's that?"

Yae frowned, unable to parse the meaning of the word that had suddenly spun off Wilhelm's lips.
To the question in Yae's mind, Wilhelm gave no reply. —And yet, a response came all the same. His eyes narrowed just slightly, and the sharp sword-intent he unleashed answered by slicing through her entire body.

"—!"

The instant she thought *it's coming*, Yae mobilized every finger on both hands without hesitation and unleashed on the 'Sword Devil' a lashing strike of steel thread meant not to subdue but to slaughter. Al's order not to kill only had to be honored in the end result. If she attacked to kill and the man survived anyway, all the better — but an attack thrown with the intent not to kill? Against this opponent, such a thing would never work.
So, from all eight directions, and those subdivided further into sixteen, then thirty-two, an ever-more finely divided lattice of steel thread crushed in with no room for escape. Against it, Wilhelm flashed the blades in both hands—

"OOOOHHH—!!"

The gale whipped up by his swinging twin blades literally kicked away, in a single stroke, the surging steel wires of slaughter.
His swordsmanship was so fast that the clashes of blade against steel thread all sounded at once, and the sparks thrown off gave Yae the illusion that her eyes were being seared. Her first strike had been blocked by his overwhelming sword-pressure — but the Steel Thread Technique did not stop there.
Giving the steel thread produced from her rings some slack, she prepared her next attack, divided finer still — in that instant, right before Yae's eyes, the room's "floor" was slashed and made to rise up.

—What rose was a section of the floor, cut through and stepped on at one edge so its face lifted up. A slab of "floor" as large as an extravagant bed hid Wilhelm from Yae's sight.

"—"

With a heavy *thud* of a violent step forward, that "floor" was booted flying straight at Yae.
In the confines of a room with limited room to run, there was no gap to escape through, so Yae changed the angle of the steel thread controlled by her left hand to deal with the charging "floor" — not cutting it, but binding it, bouncing it, sending it rebounding back.
The "floor," carrying the full recoil of the kick that had launched it, made its return trip with even greater speed and danger than its outbound one, bearing down to blast the 'Sword Devil' standing in its path clean away.
And that was—

"Huh?"

A sharp piercing sound rang out, and Yae's eyes went wide at the sight of the "floor," shot down and pinned in midair.
Its momentum had accelerated, its force doubled by its own weight. Against that treacherous "floor," the 'Sword Devil' had aligned his blade-tip with its dead center, without a hair's breadth of error, and canceled its full force outright, in midair.
It was, quite simply, a feat of divine skill beyond what any human hand should be able to achieve.
Yae herself, having spent much of her life being called a prodigy beyond the realm of ordinary people, could recognize it for what it was. —That even in old age, the 'Sword Devil's' swordsmanship still stood firm within the domain of divine skill.

"Third move."

In the field of vision of Yae, who had gone breathless, Wilhelm murmured from beyond the "floor" suspended in midair.
Hidden behind the "floor," modest in width but generous in length, Wilhelm's form couldn't be seen by Yae. But the advantage of steel thread lay precisely in its ability to deliver an attack even to a position unseen, one an ordinary strike couldn't reach.
She had the thread route around the "floor," driving the 'Sword Devil' beyond it out to one side or the other.
And thread was strung along both the left and the right, wherever he might break free to—

—The very next instant, piercing straight through the airborne "floor," a blade-tip closed in on the tip of Yae's nose.

There came the sound of steel striking steel, and a sword shot forward at a speed that put a mighty bow to shame. The sword that had been driven into the "floor" and stopped there — he had struck its pommel, and driven it clean through the "floor."
Faced with an arrow — no, less unexpected than simply off the scale — Yae gave up on snaring the sword with thread, instead deflecting its trajectory just slightly, tilting her slender neck to avoid a fatal wound. The blade-tip passed right beside her neck, and the sword struck home with a *clang* beside her, against the wall at her back.

She'd dodged it. And even in that instant, Yae had not taken her eyes off the 'Sword Devil.'
Which was why she realized, immediately, that she had made a mistake.

The sword had buried itself deep, right beside Yae's neck on the right. —Her escape had been confined to the left.
And into that—

"RRRAAAAHHH—!!"

Springing off the "floor" as a foothold, kicking off the ceiling to gain speed, the 'Sword Devil's' strike came crashing down.
A blade swung in a straight vertical split aimed dead at her life, cutting deep into Yae's left shoulder.

Wilhelm van Astrea. —No, Wilhelm Trias had been staying in the royal capital in search of some means to heal his liege, Crusch Karsten.

In the battle against the Witch Cult over the Watergate City of Priestella, the Sin Archbishop of "Lust" had poured a vile poison into Crusch's body, one that gnawed at mind and body alike, and she had gone on gasping under a curse-like agony ever since.
Even Ferris, who held the title of "Blue" as the Kingdom's foremost healer, had been unable to ease that suffering — but a ray of hope had finally broken through this hardship, just recently.
It wasn't that Crusch's condition had dramatically improved. It was only that word had come that a path had opened toward a means that might unlock such a possibility — a road to the Pleiades Watchtower.

If they could borrow the power of Shaula, the 'Sage' — one of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica's Three Great Heroes, said to be nothing short of omniscient and omnipotent — they might learn a way to remove the vile poison tormenting Crusch.

"If it's to help Crusch-sama, well, there's nothing for it but to bite down on a rock and hang on, is there."

Wilhelm had no words to caution Ferris, who had leapt at that news as if clinging to it for dear life.
The previous year, the Witch Cult had stolen Crusch's "memories," leaving her greatly changed from before, and it had been Ferris who devotedly supported and assisted her through it all. For Ferris personally, the process of rebuilding a relationship with Crusch from scratch must have been full of anguish and confusion.
That anguish had continued for nearly a year, and only now had a bond of trust finally formed between the two of them — different from before, perhaps, but unshakable all the same.

And that was exactly what the Sin Archbishop of "Lust," Capella Emerada Lugunica, had ruined.

Crusch's subsequent suffering has already been described, but the sight of Ferris standing by her side through it, aching, was likewise almost too painful to watch.
So when word came that Subaru's party, having set out for the Pleiades Watchtower, had achieved the feat of actually reaching it, a hope more searing than mere admiration burned through Wilhelm and the others.

There was something odd about the fact that Subaru's group hadn't brought the news back themselves, but there was no way to pry too deeply into the affairs of another camp.
Still, they had heard that a trial run had been made to confirm the tower could be reached and left reliably. Unable to simply sit still, Wilhelm and Ferris had come to the royal capital to learn whether it had succeeded and, if fortune allowed, to plead to be included in the next party bound for the tower.
And so, in a room of the royal mansion set aside for visiting dignitaries—

"—There's something I'd like to bring to your attention, and something I'd like your help with."

And so it was that from this young man, the Emilia Camp's minister of domestic affairs, Wilhelm learned the outline of a calamity that might well shake the very foundations of the Kingdom, along with the two names that would give him reason to take up his sword.



"RRRAAAAHHH—!!"

He kicked off the ceiling and drove a vertical split strike down onto the red-haired girl — the formidable Shinobi.
Her ultra-fine thread-shaped weapon scattered its threat across erratic trajectories in far too many variations. Judging that the odds of a slip only grew the longer this fight dragged on, he had committed to settling it quickly.
The strike that connected bit into her from the left shoulder, and should have carried straight through in a diagonal cut to exit at her right side, cleaving her life in two.

"—!"

In that instant, the girl, gritting her teeth hard, sank into the floor — no, she hadn't sunk. The floor itself had been cut apart by thread and come loose. Her body fell first, straight down into the basement below, and Wilhelm found himself chasing after her.

With a hand thrust out on reflex, he yanked from the wall the sword that had sealed the girl's movement, securing his twin blades once more.
He hadn't managed to lop off the girl's left shoulder entirely, but it was a deep wound regardless. The moment she vanished from sight, she became five, even ten times more troublesome than when she'd been visible — that was the formidable nature of a Shinobi.
In other words—

"—I'll finish this here."

Not by any arrangement between them, Wilhelm's voice and the girl's overlapped, saying the very same thing.
Falling through a pitch-black hole gaping open into the depths below, Wilhelm caught, for just an instant, the glinting reflection of light seeping into the darkness — the presence of thread. —It was the outrageous violence of lethal thread, strung throughout that hole leading underground like a spider's web.

Falling back-first toward the depths below, Yae bound her own left shoulder with steel thread to stop the bleeding.
She'd let a blade sink rather deep into a maiden's tender skin, but since the purity of her life hadn't been scattered, she told herself, in sour consolation, that it counted as safe — while at the same time marveling at the sheer terror of the 'Sword Devil's' swordsmanship.

The first move that blocked Yae's steel thread; the second move that kicked the slashed-up "floor" flying at her; the third move that pinned down the "floor" Yae had knocked back; the fourth move that used the sword driven through it as a decoy for the finishing cut — exactly as he'd declared at the start, she had come within a hair of being killed in four moves.

"Nothing but disagreeable old folks around here, isn't there...!"

The "Vile Old Man," the worst-tempered elder she knew best of all; the aged Giant on the plains who'd pushed Al to within a single step of defeat; and now this 'Sword Devil,' who at his age had no business standing at the pinnacle of swordsmanship — every old-timer Yae could bring to mind off the top of her head was, without exception, someone who had never learned to mellow with age.

To begin with, it was simply the way of the world that the flame of one's life might go out at any moment, without warning. And yet, by the very fact of having lived long in such a world, the old were those who had already won out in life's selection process.

"'—That's why old geezers and hags are the scary ones, see. Go around thinking the old are weak or frail, and you'll find plenty who end up under the ground before they ever get old themselves. Well, coming from an exception among exceptions — a Shinobi who's actually lived this long — maybe that's a bit rich, eh?'"

Remembering the booming laugh of the village elder, who had survived a road especially harsh, made her cheek twitch.
To steal past an opponent's eyes, confound their heart, bind their feet, and take their life — that was the true essence of the Shinobi. And though Yae had been a star pupil at the art of confounding hearts for that purpose, she knew perfectly well she still lost out to the elder's years of experience.
Yae probably wasn't twisted enough in character to match him, nor did she think she'd live long enough to grow that twisted. In fact, she'd already come within a breath of being killed by Al once.
She didn't imagine that stroke of luck would repeat itself many more times. —A kind-hearted monster's passing whim was, after all, nothing short of a miracle.

"—"

Chasing after the falling Yae, Wilhelm, having pulled his sword from the wall, likewise fell into the hole.
The confident movements of the 'Sword Devil,' who clearly had real combat experience against Shinobi too, no doubt owed something to the Vollachian Empire, which had dispatched Shinobi in the past — and Yae found herself with an endless grudge against whichever predecessor Shinobi had failed to kill Wilhelm back then when they'd had the chance.
Compressing all of that into a single breath and exhaling it out, something clicked and switched inside Yae — and within the darkness of that underground space, her field of vision expanded, letting her see almost as brightly as if it were daytime.

The fruit of training to adapt to night's darkness — a form of bodily manipulation that controlled blood flow around the eyes to alter how she perceived things.
At the white shape of the 'Sword Devil' rising into a field of vision now shifted from black to gray, Yae spread her hands wide, thinking at last that she'd drawn him into her own field—

"—I'll finish this here."

That improbable phrase came from both of them at once again, yet neither so much as smiled nor showed surprise.
Each had decided to kill the other. Now that mental attacks had proven pointless, there was nothing left but to set aside every technique of confounding the heart and give one's utmost, wholehearted effort to landing a blow on the other's life.
This place was the killing field of the "Crimson Cherry Blossom," prepared by Yae for exactly this purpose.

—The hollow space beneath the Barielle villa hadn't been Priscilla's doing when she'd had the building remodeled; it was a parting gift left behind by its original owner, Leip Barielle.
Priscilla's late husband, said to have been afflicted with a pathological distrust of others and a warped suspicion, had prepared contingencies not only in the villa he'd built in the royal capital, but in the manor and hideouts on his own domain as well.
This hole leading underground had been one such contingency, meant originally to function as an escape route for slipping quietly outside — but judging it could just as easily become an infiltration route for the enemy, Yae had sealed it off in advance, while keeping the deep, long shaft itself intact since it seemed a waste not to.
Yae had never met the man herself, and felt nothing but appalled distaste for a Leip who, by all accounts, had forced a young and beautiful bride like Priscilla into marriage only to try and seize control of the nation's politics through her as a puppet — but she did sympathize with the impulse to prepare against every possible disaster, and gratefully rode along on his coattails.

With steel thread strung throughout it, it made an execution platform for slaughter, one only Yae herself could move freely around.
The reason she'd infiltrated the royal capital so as to be able to help Al at a moment's notice, and had placed Felt — whom she kept around under watch — in that room, was so this contingency could always be put to use.
Which meant—

"—From here on, no holding back with my feet, either."

So saying, Yae folded her knees, slipped her shoes smoothly off her feet, and stretched out toes wrapped in black undergarments — spreading the five toes of each foot wide, she sent steel thread flying from the rings fitted onto them.
Quite literally, no holding back — with hand or foot alike — she skillfully worked all twenty fingers and toes together, and Yae Tenzen's consummate art of slaughter ran rampant through the darkness—

"—"

Both of them, equally, wanted this settled quickly.
Still falling, Yae hooked her knee on one of the threads she'd strung, breaking off the momentum of her fall and flipping around, and inside the cylindrical shaft — like some great well — she sprang off the thread's tension and launched her attack.

Employing an acrobat's lightness of body, Yae danced and whirled like a petal caught up in the wind.
Accompanying Yae's graceful dance were silver threads that lured anyone captivated by it toward death, and around her as she danced, flowers of blood bloomed from whoever had been treated to her performance — hence, the "Crimson Cherry Blossom."
A beautiful, cruel crimson cherry blossom, blooming in full glory as it drank the blood flowing from the corpses fallen at its roots.

"—!"

A ferocity fit to have earned that moniker was unleashed without mercy, slashing across the entire body of Wilhelm as he gave himself over to free fall, blood and flesh scattering into the air beneath his well-tailored clothes.
Loose strands of hair stirred by the wind of his twin blades even as he fell, Yae could only marvel, half in disbelief, at the 'Sword Devil's' sheer force of eye.
Unlike steel thread, a sword could only reach as far as the arm swinging it. And so, on this execution platform she'd prepared, Wilhelm had no choice but to stay purely on the defensive.

Naturally enough, Yae — who could move through the darkness freely, at will, relying on her threads — never once came close to Wilhelm, bound and hampered as he was by gravity.
She kept, without fail, a mid-range distance his sword could never reach, landing one-sided attacks of steel thread — all before finishing a single dance, before the 'Sword Devil' could reach the ground and reclaim his freedom.

Yae's arm rose, unhurried. —Blood sprayed.
Yae's slim, slender leg traced along the wall, through empty air. —Blood sprayed.
Yae's slight frame twisted, arching her body to its very limit. —Blood sprayed.

Each time Yae's dance drew nearer to completion, the 'Sword Devil,' unable to fully guard against her thread strikes, was dyed further in blood.
On his leg, his torso, his shoulder, his neck, his cheek, his brow — sharp steel thread licked wound after wound into him, and as he bled, the 'Sword Devil' was drawn ever closer toward death.
And yet—

"—What an unpleasant look in those eyes."

The gleam in his eyes, holding a frighteningly clear sword-intent, hadn't clouded with even a trace of dimmed fighting spirit.

"—"

On top of the steel thread Yae swung with both hands and both feet, there was also the thread already strung throughout the underground space.
Needless to say, if he devoted all his attention to dealing with Yae and carelessly let a hand or foot brush against one of the taut-strung threads, he'd lose the limb; if it caught his neck or torso, that would be the end of his life.
For Wilhelm now, this great pit was as good as the jaws of a Witchbeast he'd leapt into without a second thought — flesh shaved off by fangs, blood lapped up by tongue, and finally, nothing left but to drop into its stomach and have his life dissolved away. —But his eyes held none of that pointless struggle against despair.

"But there isn't much you can actually do, is there?"

Even setting that gleam in his eyes aside, Yae wasn't so foolish as to rate the 'Sword Devil' cheaply enough to think this would end here. Having long since cast aside Heinkel's assessment of Wilhelm as unreliable, she instead judged him by what she'd confirmed with her own eyes, here in the midst of a life-or-death struggle.

So, against the 'Sword Devil' falling helplessly with no recourse, she remained on guard against two possible attacks.
One was that he'd kick off the wall of the shaft and unleash a sword strike equal to the one that had knocked Yae down here in the first place. The other was that, just as he'd shown up above, he'd hurl his sword straight at Yae's life.
And which of those two it would be would arrive an instant later, too soon to spare even the blink of an eye.

"—Hah!"

The instant her outstretched black-clad toes traced a circle and opened three lines of cuts across Wilhelm's cheek, the 'Sword Devil's' powerful leg — having swung both blades straight down to flick away the steel thread — surged, planting the sole of his boot flat against the wall.
With his knee bent just slightly, enough to store up sufficient leg strength, the 'Sword Devil' shot forward through midair — but an instant before that, from arms raised high, two swords were hurled, streaking straight toward Yae's life.

Two lines of silver light raced through the air, bearing down on her, and Yae evaded them as part of the seamless flow of her dance.
Using the strung thread as a foothold, she flipped through the air, letting the blades pass by within a hair's breadth of the space between her legs and her arched-back spine, and came through unscathed. Then, at the 'Sword Devil' flying in just behind his own swords, she drove home the steel thread from both her feet.
Having let go of his swords, Wilhelm — with no way left to parry steel thread — twisted his body, shielding his head, and slipped through the mesh of thread with minimal damage. But by then, Yae was no longer anywhere along his headlong path; having sprung away on her thread to safety, she was well beyond the reach of the now weaponless 'Sword Devil's' hands.

That made two moves she'd guarded against, and both had been successfully blocked — but it was still too soon to breathe easy.

"—OOOOHHH!"

Kicking off the wall, Wilhelm — having missed Yae — shot toward the opposite wall of the cylindrical shaft. There, the twin blades he'd thrown earlier stood embedded; grabbing them, he kicked off the wall once more.
Chasing after Yae, who had fled diagonally upward, Wilhelm charted a course upward at bullet speed. —Just like that, having leapt, he kicked off the opposite wall, then leapt to the opposite wall again. Repeating it over and over, he closed the gap all at once on Yae as she danced her way upward.

"You've got to be kidding...!"

At Wilhelm chasing her down with nothing but sheer brute force, Yae felt the back of her throat tremble with astonishment.
Kicking off walls, ascending in the manner of a triangle jump — that itself was something even Yae could do. Nothing to be astonished by there. What was astonishing was that this place, where Wilhelm kept repeating the move, had countless strands of steel thread strung throughout it by Yae's own hand.
Naturally, jumping through it without the leisure to scrutinize every strand risked hitting a thread and being torn to pieces.
But Wilhelm showed no fear of that. Why? —Because this was space he had already fallen through once, and he had grasped exactly where the strung thread lay, leaping in a way that avoided it entirely.

It was a terrifying fighter, one in whom an abnormally exceptional combat sense and eye for observation coexisted with the resolve to stake his life without hesitation on what he'd confirmed with his own eyes.
Dancing lightly on, Yae faced the 'Sword Devil' as he boldly threw himself into the storm of silver thread, and, resigning herself that there was no other way, reached for her trump card—

"—Hyup, hah."

Using the thread's spring to launch herself into a great leap, Yae extended all four limbs upside down, and tangled the steel thread flying from all twenty of her fingers and toes together with the thread already strung throughout the shaft, drawing her limbs in tight.
Yae's masterful Steel Thread Technique, by exploiting how the threads tangled together with the surrounding objects, was capable of a feat like binding over a hundred ruffians at once in a forest skirmish. And now even Yae herself poured in enough force that she thought the bones and muscles of her whole body might be torn apart, wrenching the steel thread taut all at once.

In that instant, inside the cylindrical shaft leading underground, the snapped steel thread thrashed and raged wildly, without pattern.
A device like cutting sausage with a string, harnessing the tension released when taut steel thread snaps — a slashing thread-strike that tore through the entire space, its trajectory and number of strands impossible even for Yae to fully read.
And on top of that—

"—A capable maid always adds that one extra touch!"

With the space below her crushed under countless thread-strikes, Yae drew the rings on both hands up to her lips and kissed them. —The very next moment, flame raced along the thread trailing from the rings, and a beat later, an explosion of fire burst forth.
On top of the raging thread-strikes, a scorching, all-consuming inferno — Yae's merciless Shinobi Technique, two trump cards played at once, wrapped the 'Sword Devil's' form in crimson light and seared him black.

"I guess, after all—"

—she hadn't managed to see it through without killing him, Yae thought, chagrined at the outcome of the fight.
But that was something she'd already realized the instant this began. He wasn't an opponent she could beat while aiming not to kill. Only by going in intending to kill could she even hope to have a choice in whether he lived or died.
So—

"Let's keep this a secret from Heinkel-sama—"

"—No need. He, too, should know I've come."

In that instant, a flash of the 'Sword Devil's' blade burst out through the flames and grazed Yae, suspended in midair.

"—Ngh."

Caught across the torso by a razor-honed flash of the blade, unable to redirect its force, Yae spun trailing blood in a shape quite unlike her dance, eyes wide, groaning at what had just happened.
What had happened was plain enough. Her enemy had survived both that storm of steel thread and the added blast of flame. The question was how he'd managed it.
Wilhelm's final leap had gone straight up, its trajectory different from the diagonal leaps off the walls he'd made until then. And to achieve that kind of leap inside this shaft, there was only one way to do it — the same method as Yae's own: springing off a foothold of steel thread.

"How did you... my thread..."

"You showed me, many times over. —Your thread is a frighteningly sharp weapon, but at certain angles and with the right amount of force applied, it can also bind rather than cut."

Wilhelm's answer was a sound one, one that had genuinely seen through the nature of Yae's steel thread.
What was uncanny was that he'd seen through it in so short a time, in the middle of a fight, and that he had, once again, decided without hesitation to stake his life on something with no certainty behind it.
And on top of that, what was baffling—

"Then what about the fire?"

"I cut it."

The answer was terribly blunt, and hopelessly so, and Yae let out a sigh of disbelief.
It probably wasn't a lie. That was how confidently he'd answered. And just as she accepted it, a certain phrase suddenly resurfaced in a corner of Yae's mind.
It was something she had once tossed far away as useless—

"'—My old man's strong. Ridiculously so. You and that Aldebaran guy are plenty strong yourselves, I'd wager, but even so, it's my old man who scares me more. That's just what the "Sword Devil" is.'"

Full of complicated resentment as it was, Heinkel's assessment had been correct.
She couldn't go so far as to admit he was stronger or scarier than Al, but at the very least, he was more terrifying than Yae herself.

—But in admitting that much, just then, Yae Tenzen had made a miscalculation.

It was this: she had rated the reliability of the information as roughly half-true simply because it had come from Heinkel. And more than anything, it was that Wilhelm's skill had grown more refined than it had been during the battle with the White Whale, the campaign against "Sloth," or the defense of the Watergate City — all fights Yae had looked into. That, of all things, was something Yae had no way of knowing: he was drawing ever closer to the peak of his active years — the era in which he had defeated the "Sword Saint."

And so, the battle between the "Crimson Cherry Blossom" and the "Sword Devil" that had begun at the Barielle villa—

"—RAAAAHHH!!"

—ended with the flash of a blade halting the dance before its completion, staining the crimson cherry blossom's own body with blood, and the victory went to the 'Sword Devil.'
