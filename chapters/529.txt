Arc 7 Chapter 31: "A Conversation Between Countrymen"

Grabbed by the shoulder by a sturdy arm, Subaru turned and his eyes went wide.
That was how unexpected the attitude of the iron helmet staring back at him — Al's attitude — was.

"――――"

The arm gripping his shoulder was strong, impossible to ignore. From it, Subaru could feel a will absolutely determined not to let him continue slamming his forehead against the wall, that act of self-harm.

"Cut that out."

"...ah."

Their eyes met, and at the repeated words telling him to stop, a breath leaked out of Subaru.
In that instant, a wretched shame welled up from the pit of his stomach at having his recent bout of madness caught. Like some game character made to run straight into a wall.
Including the fact that he hadn't been thinking about anything at all — wasn't that exactly what he'd been?

"Keep that up and you'll turn into an idiot, I don't care. No, wait, I did care, that's why I stepped in to stop you."

"...Sorry for the trouble."

"Why're you talking all polite? Also, your voice is way too scratchy, it's freaking me out."

Pointed out by Al for how hoarse his own voice sounded — surprising even himself — Subaru sneered at himself once more.
At himself, so thoroughly affected by his crumbling mental state, and at the pathetic sight of himself, driven to a mental state this wrecked.
Of course he was. After all, Subaru had been told that, by none other than Rem herself—

"—Was what that girl said to you really that rough?"

"—!"

"Whoa, scary! Don't look at me like that. Dressed like this and you're making me flinch."

At Subaru, who'd snapped his head up as if struck, Al gave an exaggerated shrug.
Glared at, Al stepped back — but Subaru closed the distance Al had retreated, and bit straight into Al's words head-on.

"You... were listening?!"

"It's not like I was trying to eavesdrop, y'know? I just happened to go call for my brother-in-arms and walked right into the middle of a serious conversation. So I figured barging in would've been rude."

"――――"

"Don't give me that suspicious look, I ain't lying. Well, after that, my brother-in-arms went walking off with a face like the end of the world, so I got worried and followed. And sure enough..."

As he spoke, Al pointed to the forehead of his own helmet.
He probably meant that his worry had, sure enough, hit the mark. More than Al having watched closely, it was probably that Subaru had been so plainly in despair that it gave that impression.
At the spot Al pointed to, the pain from where he'd been slamming his forehead came rushing back, and Subaru looked down in embarrassment.

"...Rem, is..."

"Hm?"

"What happened to Rem? After... after I talked with her..."

"Whoa there, don't tell me what that girl said shocked you so bad your memory got wiped? Give me a break. If my brother-in-arms loses his memory, I lose my one and only comrade."

Fiddling with the metal fittings on his helmet with the hand he'd raised to his head, Al answered Subaru's question.
Unfortunately, whatever situation Al was worried about had already happened. Still, for Subaru it was a matter serious enough to rival memory loss, so it was no wonder his mental balance was shaken.

Perhaps reading that state of Subaru's heart in his expression, Al prefaced with "No need to worry," and continued,

"Even after your face went pale, you kept answering her properly. So I don't think that girl thought anything was off about you, y'know?"

"I see..."

Given Al's assurance, Subaru felt the faintest, the very faintest relief.
It seemed the pathetic display Subaru had shown had started only after he'd left Rem's presence. If he'd barely managed to keep up appearances, then at least he hadn't burdened Rem with worry.
In any case—

"Al, what you said earlier... what did you mean by that?"

"Earlier?"

"...About how you understand wanting to die, or whatever it was."

Pressing a hand to his aching forehead, Subaru asked after the true meaning behind the words Al had thrown at him.

*"I get wanting to die. But no matter how many times you do it, it don't end, that kind of thing."*

Wanting to die, and no matter how many times you do it, it doesn't end.
Honestly, Subaru thought it wasn't that snagging a turn of phrase. But at the same time, it was also words far too meaningful to say to Subaru in that situation.
Or rather, it made him wonder if Al knew about Subaru's peculiarity, about his Authority—

"—Ah, that."

"Right. That was pretty... pretty meaningful, wasn't it. You..."

—You know something, don't you?

That was the suspicion Subaru's gaze carried as he sent it at Al. He strained to make out the emotion hidden behind that cold iron helmet, but he could see neither expression nor eyes.
Still unable to see either expression or eyes, Al gave a small snort through his nose, and,

"I don't like saying it, but I'm a middle-aged guy who's had my fair share of years too, y'know? A messed-up scene like this, my brother-in-arms — I've got experience with that myself. Including doing something uncool in front of a pretty girl."

"...Huh?"

"Don't 'huh' me. Wanting to die from sheer embarrassment — every man goes through that at some point. Hell, even at my age I still pull that kind of thing fairly often. And when I do, the way my princess looks at me — like she's looking at a bug, I swear."

Grinning easily, Al slapped Subaru's shoulder and delivered what was supposed to pass as an elder's wisdom.
Taking in both the content and Al's attitude, Subaru stared hard at him, trying to determine whether it was a dodge or the truth.

"Hm? What's up, brother?"

"...No, nothing."

But no matter how he tried to probe Al's true feelings, the black iron blocked him.
Until now, Subaru had brushed off Al's getup as simply eccentric — but now, confronting him trying to read his true intentions, he realized it was a far sturdier defense than he'd thought.
At the same time, though, he reflected that he was being overly nervous.

"There's no way he could know, huh..."

It was just that Al's phrasing happened to snag on Subaru's mind.
If Al had known about "Return by Death," he would surely try to reveal it to Subaru in some clearer form. The Authority of "Return by Death" — its very existence was a dangerous thing.
As had been the case with Roswaal, if someone knew, they couldn't help but bring it up.
At the same time—

"—Not everyone summoned to another world gets handed some kind of power."

He didn't know the details of Al's circumstances, but it seemed safe to think that way.
Because, if Al had been granted some kind of special power, then surely his lost left arm — its loss — could have been prevented.
For instance, if Subaru were ever to end up in a situation where he lost an arm the same way, then at that point, using his own Authority, before losing the arm—

"...I'd go back?"

Having thought that far, Subaru stared intently at his own left arm.
It was a hypothetical, but a thoroughly plausible one. To begin with, the only reason Subaru had managed to survive with all four limbs intact until now was that he'd made full use of "Return by Death" and it had gone well.

In the loops where he'd lost his life, he'd suffered the loss of arms and legs more times than he could count.
If he'd survived as-is in those moments and days had continued without him using "Return by Death," it was entirely plausible he'd be living now missing part of his body.

—Subaru resolved in his heart never to rely on "Return by Death" completely.

But then, how far could he rely on it?
Arms, legs, fingers, eyes — how much would he have to lose before he could decide to "rely on it"? And more than that, what if it wasn't his own limbs, but Emilia's or Rem's?

In the water gate city of Priestella, Subaru had not used "Return by Death" for the sake of Ricardo's lost arm. Even now, today, he was not using "Return by Death" for the sake of Mizelda, who had lost her leg.
Even knowing that using his Authority might draw a better outcome.

Even knowing he could reduce the number of people who lost an arm or a leg, who had the path they'd walked cut short.

"I'm..."

"――――"

"I'm a hypocrite."

Knowing the Authority granted to him was a formidable thing, and still unable to take that decisive step.
Natsuki Subaru was far, far too powerless, and far too selfish.
So—

"Even to Rem, I—guh?!"

"Bad loop, brother."

Even to Rem, he'd let her say that decisive line.
As Subaru began to sink into that thought, his forehead was snapped back by a sharp flick to the head. Eyes watering from the loud crack of the sound, Subaru stared at Al, going "Wh-what—"

"Ooh, that made a real nice sound. Well, guess I piled it on top of that headbutt from earlier. Wasn't trying to do that, my bad, my bad."

"That's not — no, not fine — what was that just now?!"

"A flick to the forehead. And now the right to ask questions has passed to me, so I get to ask you something too."

With surprise outrunning the pain that was only now catching up, Al jabbed the finger he'd flicked with right at Subaru. With a finger thrust practically up his nose, Subaru instinctively reeled back and shut his mouth.
Into that, Al continued with a "Listen up,"

"How shaken up are you gonna get over one line from that girl? Getting this pathetic over being told off a little — there's nothing like it. You don't think so yourself?"

"That's..."

"I can't have you falling apart over something that small. It's embarrassing to even say this, but — I've got expectations for you, brother, y'know?"

"—Expectations?"

Hit with words he hadn't expected at all, Subaru was simply stunned.
Seeing that reaction from Subaru, Al gave a deep nod and said, "That's right."

"You remember the speech you gave in Priestella, right?"

"Ah, yeah, I did, but..."

"At the time, I told you, didn't I. That by broadcasting there, you were taking on the 'Hero's Fantasy.'"

In that tense situation, with the city under attack by an Archbishop of Sin, countless people in dire straits.
Amid that, called upon to play a role, unable to take that last step forward — the words Al had given Subaru then, Subaru remembered them as being about the "Hero's Fantasy."

Someone who carries the expectations and hopes of the many upon their own shoulders alone, someone not permitted to lose.
Carrying the "Fantasy" called "Hero," the thing everyone wishes someone could be.
And at that time, Subaru had answered all too lightly.

—That it was no different from usual.

"It's the same here too. So what if one person denies you? Does that overturn everything you've done, brother? Does it flip your resolve inside out?"

"――――"

"Don't break, brother. Show 'em, brother. —Live up to those expectations, brother."

Once more, Al, who knew of Subaru's achievements in the water gate city, slapped his back.
Rem, who'd forgotten everything, Louis, regressed to a child's state, and of course Abel, Flop, Medium, the People of Shudrak too — none of them knew of Subaru's efforts there.
Priscilla might know, but whether she'd bothered to remember was questionable.

"I haven't forgotten. And, sorry, but I'm not letting you run away now, brother."

"Not letting me run away, what do you..."

"You already started this. Once you've raised the banner, you can't take it down till you die, that's the story."

"――――"

At Al's critical words, Subaru once again caught his breath.
He realized that broadcast in the water gate city had rippled out not only to the people there who'd nearly been swallowed by fear and anxiety, but to someone else besides.

Once you raise the banner, you can't take it down until you die.
And unfortunately, "death" would never come for Subaru.
Which meant there was nothing to do but keep fighting—

"...All this time."

At Al's words, Subaru covered his face with both hands, shutting out his vision, and a voice leaked from his mouth.
Echoing endlessly in his head was the fatal line Rem had given him. Words of certain death, tearing Subaru's heart to shreds and trying to drown him in the blood that flowed from it.


*"—You're not a hero, after all."*

"All this time, I've been getting by relying on that one line from Rem."

*"—You're not a hero, after all."*

"Because Rem believed in me, I could keep going without dropping to my knees. In the Sanctuary, in Priestella, at the Pleiades Watchtower too, it was the same, and..."

*"—You're not a hero, after all."*

"Rem woke up, and her memories were gone, and even so I was so happy... just one more step and I could get it all back, now's the moment I need to hold my ground more than ever, and yet—"

*"—You're not a hero, after all."*

"It was the magic words that kept me fighting."


Rem had called him a hero. She'd said so.
She'd caught the feet of Subaru as he tried to throw everything away and run, kept his knees from buckling before her as she slept, sent him to face the impossible problems of the closed-off Sanctuary and the mansion, played a part in his desperate scramble to save the water gate city that had fallen into enemy hands, and shaken the soul of Natsuki Subaru even after he'd lost his "memories," driving him to conquer the Pleiades Watchtower.
That was—

*"—Because, Subaru-kun is Rem's hero."*

Because he'd had that one line, that trust, that support, he'd been able to make it this far.
And with that taken from him, Natsuki Subaru—

"Then just take it back."

"—Eh?"

Face still covered, drowning in a darkness too deep to simply be the backs of his eyelids — Subaru caught his breath at that one line from Al, and lifted his face.
Al's face was right there in front of him, and Subaru instinctively stepped back. But just like the earlier exchange reversed, this time Al stepped forward by exactly the distance Subaru retreated.
His back immediately hit the wall, leaving him nowhere further to go. And as if cornering Subaru, who could retreat no more, Al stretched out that single arm of his, blocking off any escape.
And then—

"You take it back. That girl's expectations, and your own confidence, brother."

"Expectations, and confidence..."

"You can't take the banner down. There's nothing to do but fight. And if you can't just keep losing, then there's nothing left but to keep winning, just like you want. Keep winning like that, and take it back."

"――――"

"Lost expectations and lost trust — the only way to win those back is with results that surpass them. You've experienced that yourself, more than once, haven't you, brother."

Al pressed his face in close, until the cold iron of his helmet touched Subaru's forehead.
Yet Al appealed to him so intensely, so earnestly, that neither of them seemed to notice their foreheads were touching. The shock that fact delivered to Subaru was immense. More than anything, Al's words seemed to hold more than a grain of truth.

Lost expectations and trust — for Subaru, the bitterest memory was, of course, his disgrace at the royal castle.
At that time, Subaru had utterly squandered Emilia's trust and expectations, and the regard of the other Royal Selection candidates besides. Afterward, he'd been able to restore their trust because he acted.
Certainly not because he'd been crushed and slamming his forehead against a wall.

"...Am I an idiot? No, I am an idiot."

That was exactly the same as what he'd done back at the royal castle, wasn't it.
Back then too, Subaru had tried to avert his eyes from the gaping hole before him by asking Wilhelm for sparring lessons as an excuse, fleeing from the great pain right in front of him.
But now, Subaru had no room to be that weak-kneed.

He had to protect Rem and bring her home. And unlike back then, there was no one else he could rely on for that. —For Rem, there was only Subaru.

And to give even the slightest reassurance to Rem, memoryless and surrounded by anxiety, he couldn't afford to show her a pathetic sight anymore.

Even if Rem, who'd forgotten everything, said he wasn't a hero.
Even if the words that had kept propping up Subaru's back with such strength were retracted by that same face, that same voice.

"—I am Rem's hero."

Wasn't continuing to hold his head high like that the true measure of Natsuki Subaru?

"...Got a bit of your spirit back?"

Hearing Subaru's resolve, the edge left Al's voice too. Answering "Yeah" to that, Subaru looked up at Al from close range,

"Much better now. But get off, back up. Who's supposed to enjoy this wall-slam, huh?"

"Right?! You're crossdressing and I'm a one-armed guy pushing forty!"

Laughing as if thoroughly entertained, Al pulled his hand off the wall and stepped back.
And as his field of view opened up, Subaru felt as if it wasn't just what was right in front of him — his whole perspective had broadened.

—Honestly, Al's words were first aid; they hadn't completely closed the fatal wound.

Surely, if he stood before Rem, his legs would tremble, and he'd end up anxiously watching her expression, dreading being told the same thing again.
He still hadn't found an answer for what to use the Authority of "Return by Death" for, or how far.
Probably, no matter how much he thought it over in his head, he wouldn't reach a conclusion until the moment itself came.

But one thing he could say clearly.
This arrogant Authority that Natsuki Subaru possessed was indispensable if he was to make the "Hero's Fantasy" he had to bear on his back into something real.
So, going forward too, he would likely keep agonizing over how to deal with this Authority.

"Ah, by the way, brother, there's something I wanna ask."

Looking down at his own two hands, renewing his resolve, Subaru was watched by Al, who suddenly broached something as though it were hard to bring up.
Subaru found himself suspicious of Al's attitude, given that up to now Al had shown no hint of hesitation at all.

"A bit late for that now. If there's something you want to ask, just ask."

"Then, no holding back... that Rem girl, what's her position, exactly?"

Tilting his head, Al voiced the question. It was, if anything, a question so overdue it was almost absurd.
Still, hearing it asked aloud by Al, Subaru realized for the first time that he'd never explained anything about her.

"She's someone you're dragging all the way out to the Empire with you. Not a Half-Elf girl, not the little Contract loli either, apparently. And on top of that, you treat her like your wife, don't you?"

"That's just for convenience, and she hates it too."

"But one line from that girl left you a total wreck. —What's the deal with that?"

His voice dropping slightly, Al asked with an oddly serious tone.
At his words, Subaru frowned and searched his memory, confirming that Al and Rem had no acquaintance with each other — at least, not in this timeline.
To begin with, Subaru had never even brought up Rem in front of Al. It was only natural that Al wouldn't know about her. And yet, something about it nagged at him faintly.
It was probably Al's attitude in asking that was the cause.

"――――"

He couldn't see the expression. But he could feel the heat carried in that gaze.
Subaru sensed it as a sign of seriousness, of urgency. Including the earlier exchange, he was bewildered by this side of Al he didn't know, shown to him in such a short span of time.

The one and only fellow countryman from another world, always breezy, easygoing, surviving with an unserious attitude.
Feeling his impression of Al shifting within himself, he said,

"Rem is my... our family. But she was a victim of the Archbishop of Sin of 'Gluttony.' Because of that, she vanished from everyone's memory, and doesn't even remember herself."

"—I see. That's how it is. Ahh, that makes sense, that clears things up."

"Clears up what?"

At Subaru's plain statement of fact, Al put a hand to his chin and nodded repeatedly.
Puzzled by the words that slipped from his mouth, Subaru tilted his head, and Al gave an "Ah" in response,

"There were a bunch of things bugging me. ...She was supposed to be a girl I didn't know, but she looked like a girl I do know. That's what was nagging at me like a fish bone caught in my throat."

"Don't know but do know... you mean Ram?"

"Ohh, yeah, exactly."

At the unexpected connection that came flying out, Subaru stared in surprise as Al's voice brightened.
Still, that also cleared up Subaru's confusion over Al's attitude.

Ram and Rem — the reaction to one of the two losing her memory, that was something Subaru had been made painfully aware of, thanks to none other than everyone in the Emilia Camp.
Even Roswaal and Frederica, who'd known her a long time, had completely forgotten Rem's existence until they saw her in the flesh, and once they'd met her face to face, they hadn't doubted for a moment that the two were twin sisters.
Setting aside personality and temperament, the twins Ram and Rem really did look remarkably alike. —No, come to think of it lately, perhaps even their temperaments were similar in a sisterly way, more than one might expect.

In any case, he now understood the source of the discomfort Al had been feeling.
Knowing Ram, and then seeing Rem, who was the spitting image of her — of course that would cause confusion.

"But this is the first I'm hearing that you and Ram know each other."

"Not exactly 'know each other.' More like we had a bit of a connection. But that pretty much clears things up. —Twin sisters, huh? And you want to reunite the two of them?"

"...Yeah, that's right."

Reuniting Rem with Ram — that was the top priority.
On top of that, having everyone in the Emilia Camp welcome Rem back, bringing her home to where she belonged — that was Subaru's great goal. For that too, he couldn't afford to remain having lost Rem's trust.
No matter what, he had to win back her trust, and have her hold his hand again.

"—Alright, got it. I'll help out, brother."

Fixing his gaze anew on his great goal, Subaru clenched his fist. And before him, Al, having chewed over the circumstances he'd been told, nodded and said just that.
Unthinkingly, Subaru let out a dumb "Huh?" and,

"Brother, you just made a real dumb noise there, huh?"

"Shut up! I mean, no — what did you say? You'll help? Who's helping who?"

"Me, helping you, brother. Well, the princess'll probably have all kinds of things to say about it, but that's something I can work around one way or another. Anyway, I've decided — I'm backing you."

"――――"

"Though the only shoulder I've got to lend is worth half an arm."

"That's not funny."

Said with an almost obnoxious amount of enthusiasm, Subaru reflexively shot back even as he was left bewildered.
And no wonder — he had no idea at all what had struck a chord in Al.

"...Is it because you know Ram that you're lending a hand?"

"Not really that either. Who I'm backing is you, brother. —Either way, if you're gonna play hero, that girl's part of the deal too. That's what I'm saying I'll help with."

"I'm not exactly trying to become a hero—"

"—You will be. Natsuki Subaru is going to become a hero."

It was said in a way that allowed no denial.
That one line, delivered with such quiet intensity, carried a heat that seemed as if it would sear Subaru's heart.

"...Just kidding."

But that fierce heat scattered instantly at Al's next, joking remark.
Thrown off by the abrupt shift, Subaru was met with Al waving a hand and saying "My bad, my bad,"

"But let's go with that kind of spirit, brother. Bluffing a little, cutting off your own retreat — that's the kind of thing that keeps lazy guys like me and you sharp."

Saying that, Al turned on his heel and started walking off with an easy, unhurried gait.
Left a step behind by that confident stride, Subaru called out, "Al—"

"What was that just—"

"Whoops, let's cut the extra chatter here. 'Cause, I just remembered, I came to call you to the meeting hall, brother. You're already getting a huge earful from the princess, guaranteed."

"――――"

"C'mon, let's hurry. Don't worry, there'll be plenty of chances to talk later."

Saying so, Al glanced back over his shoulder, shrugged, and made a gesture of breaking into a light jog. Hurried along by that motion, Subaru reluctantly set aside what was on his mind.

He didn't know how much of what Al had just said to take at face value.
Even so, being told outright, straight to his face, "I'll help" — that gave Subaru at least some strength to turn forward.

Even if by force, Subaru had to face forward.
And so, holding his chest high, straightening his back, walking with long, sure strides—

"—Rem's..."

—Because otherwise, the anchor by which he could affirm himself as a hero would be lost.


△▼△▼△▼△


"—'Hero's Fantasy,' huh."

As he jogged along, a murmur echoed only within the iron helmet.
A voice that reached no one outside, meant only for himself to hear, as he closed his eyes.

And so, with his eyes shut, as if to make it echo in the darkness behind his eyelids—


"I'm gonna make you into a hero, brother. —No, Natsuki Subaru."
