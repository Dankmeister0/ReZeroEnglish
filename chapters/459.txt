Toward Subaru, who had collapsed and could no longer move, the shadow stretched out countless hands.
Spiraling, swirling, jet-black hands of malice reaching to swallow Natsuki Subaru, soul and all.
From every point they touched, he could feel his existence turning hollow — melting away, crumbling, unraveling.

But strangely, he felt not the slightest revulsion at it.

"—————"

Within his field of vision his body crumbled, his existence was overwritten, his soul was churned and stirred. Even as this — perhaps the ultimate blasphemy that could be committed against a living thing — was inflicted upon him, Natsuki Subaru's heart was quiet enough to be called, of all things, tranquil.

Much of that was the lingering effect of the despair he'd felt to the depths of his being, just moments before.
But that wasn't all of it. It was that the shadow — this hand of malice, and only this — had been honest with him.

Of Natsuki Subaru's wish to vanish here and now, this shadow alone was considerate enough to take heed.

*I want to die. I want to disappear. I want to be crushed, trampled, reduced to ash without a trace left behind.

Cutting through that overwhelming flood of love that seemed to paint over even the world, along with Natsuki Subaru's very existence, a voice like a silver bell flashed once, straight and true, and reached him.

Light burst forth.
It pierced into the jet-black hand of malice that had been about to swallow Subaru whole. A shockwave erupted; the hand that took the direct hit shattered — but that was merely one of countless writhing hands. Shaving one from a thousand hardly balanced against the hostility such a vast, overwhelming shadow would return for it. Calling the name of the collapsed Subaru, the owner of that silver-bell voice seized his limp hand tightly. His body was hauled upward at once, dragged bodily out of that place.
To stop her, the shadow stretched its arms to block both front and back, trying to seal off any path forward or back.
But even faced with that obstruction, the feet of the girl running ahead did not stop.

"—Hyah!!"

She thrust out the hand not gripping Subaru's, and a dazzling light burst forth there.
An instant later, what arose was a beautifully glowing crystal of ice — an ice blast, sharing its origin with the very ice cage that had once confined Subaru, magnificent and fierce.

It shattered the wall of jet-black hands arrayed before them, forcing a path open by main force.
Subaru, who had thought of that eerie shadow as something that would swallow absolutely everything, felt the heart he'd thought would never move again tremble faintly at the sight of it scattered in an instant.

And once that hardening heart stirred even slightly, his eyes were drawn to the beautiful face right beside him.
Gripping Subaru's hand, gazing single-mindedly ahead, her long, shimmering moonlight-silver hair streaming behind her — the girl, Emilia, ran on, taking Subaru with her.

Her survival was confirmed — and still his heart did not soar.
If anything, Subaru felt, in some illusion at the back of his skull, the sound of something cracking.

He couldn't rejoice at her survival. Of course he couldn't.
The others whose survival he'd confirmed — Julius, Beatrice, Echidna — he had let die before his eyes.
He hadn't witnessed the decisive moment, but there was no imagining a good outcome for Julius, left behind under the sheer weight of Reid and the Witchbeasts. Beatrice had vanished shielding Subaru; he hadn't even been able to ease Echidna's suffering as she lay dying in agony.

Natsuki Subaru was a god of misfortune. Death clung to his existence, thick and unmistakable.
As if in exchange for undoing his own "death," he pushed that misfortune onto everyone around him — he was enough of a blackly fated child of ruin that such an absurd hypothesis could even occur to him.

"—That's enough. I've had enough."

"Eh?"

"I said there's no point struggling any more than this."

Resisting Emilia's insistent pull on his arm, Subaru stopped his feet. Emilia tried once more to drag him along, but this time Subaru refused to follow her, with resolute will.
The difference in raw strength between them was obvious, but Subaru's will was equally unyielding. Perhaps sensing that dark impulse in his black eyes, Emilia caught her breath and stopped pulling by force.

"—————"

The two of them, Subaru and Emilia, faced each other head-on and stared into one another's eyes.
He realized only then that, somewhere along the way, the shadow that had been trying to swallow him whole was nowhere to be seen. Had they simply outrun it while fleeing together with Emilia? If so, would they run into it again if they turned back?

To erase Natsuki Subaru, existence and all — that shadow had seemed the most fitting thing for it.

"Why did you come to save me? That's insane. You too — you thought I was a fake, didn't you? ...That's why. That's why you shut me in that ice cage and tried to kill me."

Words of deceit — twisting the facts deliberately, spinning them cruelly, to wound.
So that Emilia would never again think, even by mistake, to take this hand of his.

But such base scheming meant nothing to Emilia, who looked the facts straight in the eye.
Her narrow eyes flared sharp with anger, and she raised her voice at Subaru.

"I wasn't trying to kill you! I just wanted to hear you out, because something about you seemed so wrong. And then you told me! That you didn't have your memories. So..."

"That could just be — a convenient excuse! And you just believed it, easily? That's insane. Something's wrong with you! With you, and Julius, and Beatrice!"

Trapped in that ice cage, cornered, he'd confessed the fact of his lost memory as a last, desperate measure.
But no one with a sound mind would believe such a story. Ram's reaction, Echidna's reaction — those were the correct ones. And yet Emilia and the rest, more than half of them, had been fools.

"No — that's wrong... everyone's a fool! Right up to the end... in that state, right to the very end, even Echidna apologized to me... it doesn't — it doesn't make any sense."

"Echidna, at the end...? Subaru, what happened? Echidna and the others—"

Covering his face with his palm, Subaru muttered as if groaning, and Emilia asked. Even that troubled expression of hers was beautiful, and he wanted to dig his claws into her heart for it.
For that purpose, he'd bare the glass-fragile wounds of his own heart, as much as it took.

No more blood to shed, her expression faint and weak, having only granted herself the pitiful, bleak wish that at least she wouldn't have to make someone precious suffer — the vision of that girl, her life snuffed out, rose again in his mind, and Subaru, as if tearing the wound in his own chest open wider by his own hand, shouted.

"She died! Echidna died! Both her legs were blown off, and between the pain and the blood loss... anyway, she suffered and died! Beatrice too!"

"—!"

"That girl too — she shielded me and... self-severance, what a ridiculous system... if only I'd noticed in time. I couldn't, and she died. Said she wouldn't forget me, said..."

Even if Subaru forgot, Beatrice would not forget.
She'd declared, with stubborn resolve, that she would absolutely restore Subaru's memories.
But she had been lost. Right after saying it.

That was exactly what "all talk" meant. Grand ambitions spoken with nothing but her mouth, put into words, and gone the very next moment.
She'd kept Subaru far from death, and vanished from this world with a face that looked almost relieved.

"She should've just stopped, instead of disappearing like that. Carried her out? Did she carry me out? Doesn't matter either way. Doesn't matter. Anyway — if she'd been pulled out of somewhere, from somewhere that wasn't here, she should've just stopped. Then..."

Then she wouldn't have had to meet an end like that, vanishing with that face.

"Julius too. By now, surely he's already... in a place crawling with those terrifying Witchbeasts, with Reid getting in his way on top of it, and he — 'go on, I'm begging you' or whatever... idiot."

Everyone was an idiot. What on earth were they expecting?
Begging, restoring, sorry for doubting — what were they even saying?

What good does begging do. What meaning is there in restoring anything. Isn't it only natural to be doubted?
Natsuki Subaru was here, precisely because he had betrayed everything entrusted to him.
Because he alone survived safe and sound, and that was unbearable, he wanted to disappear, to vanish—

The stupidest, the most foolish, the most hopeless, the most beyond saving — that was—
That, if not Natsuki Subaru, then what was it—

"—The first time Subaru and I met was in the royal capital, at a place called Loot House."

"—————"

Sunk in self-recrimination and self-doubt, in a bottomless swamp with no way out, unable to move on his own — that was Subaru.
What made his eardrums tremble was Emilia's sudden confession — a nostalgic, fond reminiscence, told in a tone that seemed to call up cherished memories.

"...Huh."

At those abrupt, contextless words, Subaru could only exhale, dumbfounded.
There was no scorn or mockery in it — What are you even saying — his mind hadn't even caught up that far yet. He was, in all honesty, simply left speechless. Paying no mind to Subaru's reaction, Emilia counted off on her fingers, calling the memory back to life.

"Back then, Felt-chan had stolen a really, really important insignia from me. Puck and I were in a huge panic trying to get it back. ...And then, chasing after it, I ended up fighting Meili's older sister, and it was dangerous, but Reinhard helped me. And right when I'd finally relaxed, Meili's sister came after me again... and that's when Subaru saved me."

"—————"

"That was the first time Subaru and I ever met. ...Do you remember?"

At the question, Subaru shook his head.
It was a memory told in vivid detail, but he hadn't the faintest recollection of any of it.
Of course not. That was a memory belonging to Emilia and "Natsuki Subaru." A single fragment spun by the "Natsuki Subaru" who kept repeating actions Subaru himself could never believe he'd taken.

"But Subaru got badly hurt protecting me, so we brought you back to Roswaal's mansion together. Beatrice healed you there, grumbling the whole time, and Ram, and... I'm sure Rem too — Subaru got close to them."

"—————"

"After that, this time it wasn't the sister but Meili who set Witchbeasts loose to cause trouble. Subaru and Ram held them off, and Roswaal finished off the Witchbeasts, and I stayed home and waited... that's also when we made our promise for a 'date.' ...Do you remember?"

"—————"

He shook his head.
He didn't remember. He hadn't done any of that. — *I* hadn't done any of that.

"So many things happened at the mansion. We made mayonnaise, we all drank together, Puck made it snow, we played the 'King's Game'... and then, we were called to the royal capital for the Royal Selection."

"—————"

"That's also when Subaru and I had our first real fight. I didn't want Subaru to keep pushing himself recklessly and getting hurt anymore, and I didn't understand why you were so kind to me, and it scared me. So I thought everything would end, once we fought... but instead..."

A faint tremor crept into Emilia's voice as she told her memories.
It was a mixture of so many conflicting feelings — joy and sorrow, anxiety and hope — and Subaru felt an intense, parching thirst seize his throat.

He yearned, he yearned, he was yearning. His chest burned with a searing longing.
He yearned for everything that could put such an expression on Emilia's face — no, for the one single cause of it all.

"When I didn't know what was happening, when I was just being swept along by a situation I was so afraid of, when my heart was the most tangled and troubled it had ever been — Subaru came running to me, and then..."

Why is it you, "Natsuki Subaru"?

Emilia, everyone — they were all thinking it.
Give us back the real "Natsuki Subaru." As for you — as for a Natsuki Subaru like you, just die.
If it were you here instead, how much better—

As if to erase away all the sorrow, all the anxiety, joy and hope settled into her smile.
Witnessing that smile of Emilia's, Subaru's lips trembled.

There was nothing. Nowhere was there anything.
Nothing he had said, nothing he had done for her, nothing he had promised to do.
Inside this body, inside this head, in the depths of this heart, at the farthest reach of this soul — there was nothing at all.

"—————"

Emilia's violet eyes widened at that roar.

"Someone who'd stake his life for another! Someone who could move on instinct for someone else! Someone who could run himself ragged trying for someone else! Told that she remembered, and answering that he couldn't recall.
Unable to answer the words Beatrice had given him before she vanished, unable to wipe away the regret of it, and now Emilia, gently, as if trying to make him understand, telling him these memories.

Julius entrusted to him. Beatrice believed in him. Echidna forgave him. Emilia wished for him.

Why entrust anything to a man who could do nothing? Why believe in him? Why forgive him? Why wish for him?
There had to be a better way to do this. There had to have been someone else, someone who could do it properly.

Even if that was the "Natsuki Subaru" everyone wanted, he was nowhere to be found anymore.

Everyone has their place, their proper station. He wanted them to understand that. Subaru had no right to walk beside Emilia and the others. No right to be wished for by them.
He wasn't strong, wasn't clever. There was no need to wish for a Subaru like that.

"—My name is Emilia. Just Emilia."

"—Ah?"

Purging himself of that helplessness, and yet with his heart still ruled entirely by what he'd just poured out, Subaru — who'd meant to feel empty — let out a strangled sound at that sudden, bell-like voice.

"—————"

He didn't understand the meaning of the words. — No, not the meaning. He didn't understand the intent.
Raising his face, Subaru looked straight ahead at Emilia, who had just given her own name. She pressed a hand to her full chest, her round, wide violet eyes reflecting Subaru's form.
He caught his breath at those shining eyes. Before that Subaru, Emilia went on.

"There's so much we need to talk about, so much I need you to hear. So, so much. But right now, just let me ask you one thing."

"—————"

"Julius, Beatrice, Echidna. And now, me — holding your hand, running beside you, wanting so badly to protect you, not wanting to let you die, doing all of that because..."

Emilia closed her eyes, her heart brimming with feeling.
For several seconds, she paused her words. In that brief silence, he could tell that a whole swirl of feelings had passed through her chest. He could feel, too, her concern for the companions not here with them now.
Holding all those feelings inside her, Emilia's cherry-blossom-pink lips trembled, and,

"The person who made all of us feel that way — who are you?"

"—————"

"Please. — Tell me your name."

At Emilia's question, something trembled deep within his chest.
It was not, he realized, an expression of intent to deny the Natsuki Subaru standing before her, to reclaim the Subaru of the past.

—It was an affirmation. Of Natsuki Subaru.

"—————"

It would have been so much easier if she'd said the one in front of her was a fake, if she'd wished for the real Natsuki Subaru back, if she'd hurled abuse at him instead. Being told that, wished for that, cursed for that — all of it would have been kinder.
Because that was exactly what Subaru himself, more than anyone, had wanted.

That he couldn't become the "Natsuki Subaru" they all wanted — that was why he'd wanted to be denied, wiped away, made as if he'd never existed. That was his own wish.

But Emilia — no, not only her.
Everyone who had spoken to Natsuki Subaru, up to this very moment, had wished for the same thing.

Strong or weak, it made no difference.
Even like this, having forgotten everything, having shown them such an unbearable, wretched display — nothing had changed.
They needed Natsuki Subaru — and they'd shown it, in their bearing, in their words, with their very lives—

"...Why?"

"—————"

"Why does it have to be Natsuki Subaru, here of all places? What can that guy do? What are you even expecting from him..."

He didn't understand.
In the face of this — this overwhelming despair, this hopeless, crushing disadvantage — what could possibly change, what could possibly turn around, what could possibly be broken open, just because Natsuki Subaru was there?

"That weak, stupid, pathetic, spineless excuse of a man — what could he possibly—"

"—Maybe you're right."

Shaking his head over and over, not in denial but in pleading, Subaru — and Emilia lowered her eyes.
Her eyes framed by long lashes, her bell-like voice that seemed to tickle the heart directly, everything about the existence called Emilia — it was like a linchpin pinning Natsuki Subaru to this world.
Toward that Subaru, whose heart, however out of place the sentiment felt, was undeniably bound by it, Emilia went on.

"There are people stronger than Subaru, and I'm sure there are plenty of people smarter, too. But no matter what happens, I want the one beside me to be Subaru. I believe you'll come through, and I hope for it. Because..."

"—————"

"Because — if it's someone helping just because they could, because they happened to be there, then having someone I love do it for me instead — that makes me so, so much happier."

That was what Emilia said, smiling as she said it.
Smiling, and her cheeks coloring just the faintest bit, as she said it.

"—————"

Subaru breathed out, as if exhaling everything at once.
At Emilia's words, for one instant, every last moment inside him had, without question, stopped.

He could feel his heart pounding, throbbing.
And at the same time, welling up from somewhere inside, came mockery — mockery aimed at "Natsuki Subaru."

"—Ha."

So that's how it was, "Natsuki Subaru." You'd fallen for a girl that beautiful, had you.
Talk about aiming above your station. As if a girl like that would ever look your way.

That cool, dashing knight. That clever-looking woman. That adorable girl.
And now, the beautiful girl standing right in front of him.

They'd entrusted things to you, believed in you, forgiven you, wished for you to be the one.
Not asking you to be their salvation, not clinging and begging you to save them — but when it came to facing down the wall in front of them together, wanting not just anyone capable of it, but you.

"—My name is Emilia. Just Emilia."

To the silent Subaru, Emilia gave her name once more.
Her violet eyes looked his way. Subaru's black eyes met them, straight on.
And then—

"—Please. Tell me your name."

"I..."

At Emilia's question, asked once more, he hesitated over his words.

Again and again, he had denied it.
That he couldn't be that. That he couldn't become that. That he wasn't that — denial upon denial, over and over.
So this — surely this was nothing more than a convenient bit of wordplay.

—Entrusted to. Believed in. Forgiven. Wished for.

If there was anyone with the right to receive all that from Emilia and the others, here in this tower in the desert.
If there was anyone who could save Emilia and the others, here in this tower in the desert.

If that was "Natsuki Subaru" — and if that "Natsuki Subaru" existed nowhere else—


"—My name is Natsuki Subaru."

"—————"

"If the name of that man — entrusted by Julius, believed in by Beatrice, forgiven by Echidna, wished for by Emilia — by you — is Natsuki Subaru, then—"

Meeting the violet eyes with his black ones, the black-haired boy answered the girl with the shimmering silver hair.
To the question asked with trembling cherry-blossom lips, an answer came from lips stained blackish-red with blood.

"—I'm Natsuki Subaru."

Now, weak, powerless, his body and heart eaten away by despair — even so, let him declare it.
That he wanted, that he wished for — Emilia's safety, and peace. Emilia's and the others'. Yours, and all of yours.

"—————"

Subaru said it, firm and unwavering.
And still, deep within him, the distrust toward "Natsuki Subaru" hadn't been wiped away, not even a fragment of it.

Even now, seared into Subaru's mind and refusing to leave, was the voice and face of that wicked man who had let "watashi" — no, Meili — die. Perhaps that would never be erased.

But that was fine. Even so, that was fine.
He didn't want to be saved. He wasn't clinging, begging to be saved.

*I want you to be saved* — that was what he thought.
*I want to help you* — that was what he wished.

—If "Natsuki Subaru" could do that, then he would be the one to do it.

Let it be — please, let it be — that the reason he'd first started running, and the destination he'd run toward, were one and the same.
If the path he pictured led to the same place, then so be it, strange bedfellows and all; he had no complaints, even if you hated him for it.

—Let me, Natsuki Subaru, save Emilia and the rest of you.

"Thank you, Emilia. — For letting me think that."

"—Subaru, I—"

At Subaru's answer, ripples spread through Emilia's violet eyes.
Her lips trembling, Emilia seemed about to weave words toward the Subaru who had just defined himself, on the verge of saying something.
It was right at that moment.

"—!"

Until then, the world had seemed to hold its breath entirely, quiet as if taking care not to interrupt the two of them's exchange — and in an instant, that stillness shattered.

"—Emilia!"

All around them, the passage the two had been standing in was, in the blink of an eye, crushed and pulverized by the shadow.
The passage lost its shape; Emilia, with no footing left, lost her balance badly. Toward her, Subaru — with just barely enough floor still remaining beneath him — kicked off hard.

In that instant, the tower lost its form entirely, crumbling into shards of stone that carried the scent of sand, ancient and disintegrating.
Through the midst of it, toward the falling Emilia, Subaru threw himself with all his momentum. The distance closed; he caught up to her streaming silver hair, and at last wrapped his arms around her slender body.

"Subaru...!"

Pulling her slim, soft, warm body close, Emilia, calling his name, shifted her weight. She was probably trying to reposition herself in his arms, trying to end up underneath.
That would put helper and helped backward.
Emilia, and everyone else, really — something was wrong with all of them. But sadly, in this situation, even Emilia's effort was of no use.

With her back to the ground, unable to see where she was falling, Emilia couldn't have known.
What awaited Subaru and her was not the tower's hard floor, nor the desert dunes outside after being flung clear — but the jet-black shadow enveloping the whole tower, leading everything to its end.

And so the two of them would, just like this, be swallowed by the shadow in each other's arms, and it would be over.

—No. The opposite of that.

This, surely, would become a beginning.
What had once begun, would begin again, here, anew — starting from an ending.

Let them make a promise, for the sake of that.
Here, in this world, in this place, let the words exchanged with Emilia become real.

Having been saved, having wished to save.
Carrying all of it with him, let him begin from the ending. The smoldering time was over now.

A curse-like attachment — fine. Let it be. That's exactly what he wanted.

Whether Natsuki Subaru had the right to be loved, he didn't know.
But Emilia — Emilia and the others — did have the right to be loved.

In this ending world, in this beginning world, even if you don't remember the words you gave me, even if you've forgotten them—

In this ending world, in this beginning world, even if you don't remember the words I hurled at you, even if you've forgotten them—

I remember. All of it. I remember all of it. This time, even if I have to cling to it with everything I have, I won't forget.



"Even if you forget — I won't forget you."


—Remember it forever, Natsuki Subaru.

The shadow closed in, jet-black, swallowing Subaru and Emilia both.
And so Natsuki Subaru, and Emilia, sank into the shadow, deeper and deeper, without end.


Everything shifted, everything was lost, everything became zero, and just as intended, the end arrived.
And then, in the place where everything had become zero, it would begin.
