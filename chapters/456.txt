Arc 6 Chapter 51: "Tower of the Living, Part 2"

At the familiar man's voice reaching him from behind, Subaru shuddered so violently he forgot to breathe.

"――――"

For that instant alone, he even forgot the pain his dislocated left shoulder was screaming at him.
What dominated his mind was a parade of negative emotions born of fear and cowardice, and a storm of despairing sensation, as if the single word "why" were painting over all his thoughts.

Why. The questions never ran dry.
Why was his left shoulder dislocated. Why was "Natsuki Subaru was here" carved into the room's walls so many times over. Why weren't Emilia and Ram, who should have captured Subaru, anywhere in this place. Why was Meili's corpse, which should have been hidden, nowhere to be found. Why had Natsuki Subaru's memories been lost. Why had Natsuki Subaru been summoned to another world. Why had he been unable to tell his father, his mother, the truth. Why——

"The hell're you shrinkin' for, huh. Don't just clam up on me, that's a real unpleasant thing to do, y'know."

——Why was a man who shouldn't have been able to come down to this floor standing right here.

"――ッ"

A note of irritation crept into the voice, and Subaru whirled around in a panic.
There was fear in the act of looking directly at the one standing behind him. In most cases, the source of that particular kind of dread lies in the unpredictability of the thing's actions.
But the fear Subaru felt toward this person was different — the anxiety of *not* laying eyes on the real thing outweighed the risk of looking directly at him.

With this man, the point of his actions was never to stoke Subaru's fear.
Put more bluntly, Subaru suspected the point was simply to hurt him, or to take his life.

"Hah. The hell's with that face, huh. You scared? Look like you're about to cry. Real unpleasant guy, in this creepy-ass room and all, real unpleasant."

Mocking Subaru for having steeled himself, the man in the loose kimono bared his white teeth without a trace of shame.
Long red hair, an eyepatch over his right eye, a bare torso wrapped in white sarashi bindings — his powerfully honed, steel-hard body looked down on the pitiful Subaru.

The watchman of the second floor of the Pleiades Watchtower — the man known as Reid.

"――――"

"...Oi, you lose your tongue or somethin'? Can't hear me? Or you tryin' to show me with that attitude that you got no intention of talkin' to me? Fine if that's the case, but if it is, stand up a little straighter about it, huh."

He said it with a mocking air, glaring at Subaru with a snarling expression. Cowed by the sheer pressure of it, Subaru worked his mouth, mumbling,

"R-right eye..."

"Ahn?"

"The eyepatch. You had it on your left eye. Why's it... on the right now?"

A question absolutely not worth asking right now.
At Subaru voicing it, Reid's blue eyes flickered faintly. Then the man clicked his teeth — "khah" — and said,

"Mood, that's all! Mood! Don't matter to me either way, the eyepatch. This thing ain't no different from wearin' it just for the hell of it. Without it on, I see too damn much. That's all it is."

Saying so, Reid flipped up the eyepatch over his right eye with a finger. Beneath it was an eye with no injury at all, sound and healthy exactly as he'd said.
The bare left eye, too, was of course unharmed. In other words, there was no lie at all in his words — the eyepatch really was just something he wore "because he felt like it."
And even that, for him, was probably just some idle game to make up the gap in strength between himself and others——

"――The hell, you. Your shoulder's outta joint, ain't it. Figured somethin' looked off."

"――ッ！？"

The instant that thought crossed his mind, Subaru's brain was scorched white-hot from behind by a sudden shock.

"Guh... gah...!"

His throat seized with agony, and his vision flickered from the piercing pain.
Looking down, he saw Reid's right hand gripping his dislocated left shoulder without the slightest hesitation or care. Then, with careless ease, he snapped the joint back into place using nothing but a twist of his wrist against the dislocated bone.
The displaced bone was corrected, a dull, visceral sound rang out, and freedom returned to Subaru's left shoulder.
But the pain he'd once managed to ignore came roaring back, a pain that made him want to curse the world, and tears traced down his cheeks.

"Don't make such a big deal outta it. Makes it look like I'm bullyin' ya. Truth is, the one who bullied you ain't me — it's that stunner over there, ain't it."

"Stun...ner...?"

"That silver-haired stunner, obviously, who else. Look at the ice cage back there and your dislocated shoulder, and I can figure out the gist of it. You two have a lovers' spat or somethin'? Funny."

Holding his left shoulder, Reid snorted through his nose at Subaru's pained groans. Hearing his words, Subaru understood that "stunner" referred to Emilia.
At the same time, he felt a kind of dread at Reid's powers of observation — that from just the state of the room, he'd grasped that a rift had formed between Subaru and Emilia's group.

"How could you tell something like that...?"

"You're stuck in a shabby-ass tower like this, and whatever's goin' on between a man and a woman, it's either they get way too friendly, or way too unfriendly, one or the other. Figured you're the type more likely to piss someone off than not. Ain't no big deal."

A conclusion too crude to even call reasonable. Unable to argue back, Subaru watched Reid turn his gaze away, looking around the room at the ice cage Emilia had left behind.

"――――"

Tilting his head, Reid drifted past Subaru with an air of boredom. He looked over the ice cage carefully, planted a foot against its icy bars, and pressed down with force.
At once there came the sound of the ice bars cracking, and in the next instant destruction spread through the entire sturdy cage all at once, the icy lattice shattering into powder.

The ice bars that hadn't so much as budged under Subaru's full strength — let alone the whole cage — were reduced to that.
In magic, in physical ability, faced with such an overwhelming gap, Subaru fell silent. Paying no mind to Subaru's reaction, Reid lightly flexed his own limbs and muttered,

"――Well, seems like it moves decent enough. Good, good."

Murmuring as if confirming some sensation, he walked slowly out of the room.
His manner suggested Subaru, still standing there dumbstruck, wasn't even worth noticing, and it startled him into motion. Confusion still hadn't settled, and he had no real grasp of what had happened, but he understood at least this much: letting Reid out of his sight now would lead to nothing good.
Besides, there was something more pressing.

"Wait! Weren't you... weren't you the one who couldn't come down from the upper floors? So why are you wandering around this floor like it's nothing!?"

Chasing after Reid as he walked, unhurried, down the fourth floor's corridor, Subaru burst out of the room.
Glaring at the back of the man treading the floor boldly, without a shred of hesitation, Subaru flung out one of the questions that had first filled his mind.
In response, Reid, without even turning around, waved a hand dismissively.

"When'd I ever say I couldn't get around outside the second floor? ...Well, sayin' that's a little sly of me. Relax. The premise that I can't get around ain't wrong. Just so happens, right now, I can."

"Just so happens... that's — I'm asking you why that premise broke down, aren't I?"

"Ain't plannin' on spellin' it out for you all nice and polite like that. I can get around. You're scared shitless and about to piss yourself. That's it, end of story. ――No, not quite the end."

As Subaru scrambled to catch up and fell in alongside him, Reid's tone shifted, and he narrowed his eyes. He cut Subaru down with a sharp glance, his cheek twisting as Subaru flinched and shrank back.
Empty-handed, no weapon on him — a red-haired swordsman whose glare alone could probably cut a man down. Under that gaze, Subaru swallowed hard, deep in his throat.

"Wh-what..."

"It's fine, keep puttin' up that bluff, you. Man's finished the day he can't even manage that anymore. So, lemme ask you, you on the verge of failin' as a man — I'm plannin' on gettin' outta this shabby-ass tower, but where'd your buddies run off to, the ones you had your little spat with?"

"Huh?"

Heart clenched under the pressure of proximity, Subaru's eyes went wide, certain he'd misheard something startling. At his slow reaction, Reid said, "You," as if it were obvious,

"I'm plannin' on gettin' outta here, but I need food, I need water, I need booze too. Wouldn't say no to a woman on top of that, either. Outta your buddies, the stunner and the woman in the racy getup are the candidates. Feel kinda guilty tryin' to woo the stunner, so the racy woman's got the edge."

"Get out... of this tower? But then — wait, what about the 'Trial,' or — there's more to it than that, isn't there? What about the situation, all of it, how are you gonna deal with all of it!?"

"Nah, don't know nothin' about your 'all of it.' Clean up your own family's mess yourself. Ain't got nothin' to do with me. Ah, wait. There's one nasty bit of leftover business."

"Nasty... leftover business...? — Guh!?"

Reid's expression shifted, and at that one line, Subaru's furrowed brow met a flicked finger. The force of the flick sent his whole body slamming back-first into the corridor wall. Looking down at Subaru as he crumpled to the floor in pain, Reid snorted, "Hah,"

"Told ya already. Don't go thinkin' you get an answer for everything, you baby bird. Whether you're a fry or a chick, plant your damn feet, you."

"That's — that's just a matter of how you define it..."

"Don't go usin' me, showin' up all convenient like this, as some hidin' spot for your anxieties and doubts and regrets. Sort your own shit out yourself. Don't use me to comfort yourself."

"――――"

It wasn't a tone you could call irritated.
Irritation requires feeling, and Reid didn't give Subaru's existence a second thought. Feeling doesn't stir for someone you pay no mind to. So there wasn't even irritation in his voice.

But the words, tossed off so carelessly, drove a blade deep into Subaru's heart all the same.
Groaning at the pain, the questions that had been left behind in Subaru's chest began once more to fill him up. But just before that—

"――Ah, here it comes."

Turning his head toward the far end of the corridor, Reid murmured as if something had clicked into place.
Lifting his face, Subaru followed his gaze in the same direction, but he saw no change there. Only belatedly did he notice.
He noticed because he was crouched down, his backside on the floor.
A faint tremor. The tower was shaking. Faintly, less than an earthquake, but he could feel the ground rumbling.

"Khah."

Clicking his teeth in a laugh, eyes lighting up, Reid set his sandaled feet firmly against the floor and started walking. Catching that unhesitating stride in the corner of his eye, Subaru scrambled up to chase after his back.

Reid had told him not to use himself as a hiding place for his own anguish.
That was, without question, a line that had cut straight into Subaru's weak heart. Subaru's problems wouldn't be solved by appealing to Reid.
But that aside, right now, Subaru had an obligation to determine the reason Reid was wandering the fourth floor, and what exactly was happening to the tower as a whole.

"Reid!"

"――――"

"Hey, wait! Wait up! At least tell me what you're planning to do..."

Striding forward with long, hurried steps, with no reason to stop, the back before him gave no answer.
With pain in his shoulder, doubt in his heart, pushed not by any active resolve but by a reluctant, passive sense of obligation, Subaru had no choice but to chase desperately after the back ahead of him.
And then the back, swaying its long red hair as it went, suddenly came to a stop.

"――――"

Where Reid stopped was the spiral staircase overlooking the atrium beyond the corridor — the place where Natsuki Subaru, having lost his memories, had twice been thrown to his death.

So desperate to keep up with Reid that he'd been slow to notice where he stood, Subaru caught his breath.
But the desire to break through the current sense of suffocation overrode his revulsion toward the place that had brought him "death," and gave Subaru the strength to move forward.

——The place hadn't killed Subaru. Malice had killed Subaru. What deserved his revulsion wasn't the place, but the cause that had led to it, and the one responsible.

"――――"

His heartbeat, the sound of blood rushing through him, felt unbearably loud. Stamping that down, Subaru wiped away his clammy sweat and pushed forward to stand beside the halted Reid.

Only then did he finally notice that Reid was standing at the edge of the high drop, looking down at the sight below. He's a man who looks the part no matter where he stands, looking down on things, Subaru thought idly, uselessly, as he followed the man's gaze.

Following it, he finally noticed.
Buried beneath the loudness of his own heartbeat, there was a sound he hadn't been hearing.

"――――"

——Below, the fifth floor, the terminus of the great staircase, was buried under a swarm of ghastly flame witchbeasts that had welled up from somewhere, the scene taking on the aspect of hell itself.


※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※


At the sight below him — something he hadn't even conceived of as a possibility — Subaru's mind went completely blank.

"――Huh?"

Writhing red flame, and countless shrieking wails echoing through the tower like crying infants.
The two phenomena were connected, tying together into a single answer — namely, a violent flood of grotesquely shaped witchbeasts wreathed in manes of fire, swarming in a horde.

Four legs and a body like a horse, with human-like arms and an upper torso growing from it. In that human upper body was a mouth lined with fangs, and terrifying flames of tremendous heat were burning away the oxygen inside the tower.
Subaru could see roughly twenty or more of the witchbeasts flying about on the fifth floor below.
Tremendous heat reached Subaru and the others dozens of meters above, and the wave of it was fierce enough that it felt like it could dry out his eyes in an instant just from looking down, forcing him to recoil.

What — what on earth was happening.

"Creepy-lookin' witchbeasts, these are. You, you know what the hell those are?"

"...No idea. Never seen a witchbeast besides that huge worm before, either... ah!?"

Beside Subaru, who couldn't tear his eyes from the scene below, Reid, gazing at the same sight, posed the question. Subaru shook his head at it, only for his throat to catch involuntarily at a change in his field of view.

The witchbeasts, wreathed in flame and flying about the tower — with the appearance of horse and man fused as one, called centaurs here for convenience — one of the centaurs let out a deafening roar.
What rose from the misshapen mouth spanning chest to belly was a fierce cry born of raw emotion — something between a scream and a furious howl.
And the reason the centaur was screaming like that lay in the presence of a single knight, darting between the swarming witchbeasts, striking back with a slender sword.

"――Hah!"

Holding his breath, a graceful stroke of the blade slipped through the gap in the flame-wielding horde of beasts.
Blood scattered, a witchbeast's arm or leg severed, and a beat later a scream thundered through the surroundings. Hearing it at his back, the knight — pressed by overwhelming numbers — twisted his body and leaped at the next enemy.

And that was——

"――Julius!"

Subaru's lips called out the name unthinkingly, moved by the joy of finding a survivor.
Whether that joy carried more of gladness or grief in it, even he couldn't tell.
Only, right after his consciousness had blanked out and he'd done things he couldn't account for himself, there had been a moment when he'd come face to face with Meili's corpse.
There was no guarantee that his next reunion with someone wouldn't also be with that person's corpse. That anxiety had never left him. So having that anxiety proven wrong was, at least, good news.

"――――"

Oblivious to that startlement above, Julius below kept swinging his sword, continuing to fight the witchbeasts.
Outnumbered, but individually, none of them seemed to match Julius's skill. Still, with a horde of beasts freely conjuring flame, a man armed with nothing but a sword would eventually meet his limit.

——And if he were to burn to death there, his "Book of the Dead" would be added to the archive.

"Wonder where the rest of 'em went off to. ――Well, convenient, I guess, in its own way."

"――ッ"

The instant a dark thought — wishing for the "death" of an acquaintance — reared its head in Subaru's chest, Reid's murmur, gazing at the same sight, yanked his awareness back to reality.
But Reid himself, as if utterly unaware of Subaru's inner conflict, bared his teeth and took one more step forward, right at the very edge of the drop he was already teetering on.

"H-hey, what are you planning to do?"

"You, nothin' but questions outta you. Instead of just askin' me all the time, how about you try somethin' unexpected for once, huh. Ain't no fun talkin' to you. Ain't fun to look at, ain't fun to talk to. What're you even talkin' to me for?"

"――――"

"Way I see it, question's what *you* wanna do. Your own people down there, surrounded by creepy-ass witchbeasts, and you're just standin' there like a post? Weak ones got fewer options. Just get better at makin' excuses."

Like a carnivore toying with prey, Reid's words were built entirely on the logic of the strong.
That Subaru couldn't refute it was precisely because Reid's reasoning didn't apply to him at all — a true, unbridgeable gap between the strong and the weak sat right there between them.

"Hah."

Without so much as glancing at Subaru, who offered no retort, Reid finally leaned his body forward.
There was no time to stop him. Just like that, Reid's body slowly tipped downward, thrown out into empty air with nothing to support it, tracing through the sky the same path Subaru had followed to his death.

Straight down, straight down, straight down, Reid's body fell.
For Natsuki Subaru, consciousness had faded before he hit the ground, so that he hadn't even been able to look his own "death" in the eye when the moment came.

That same fall — the red-haired swordsman, Reid, fell it the same way, falling, falling, falling——

"――ッッ！！"

From directly above, from an immense height, the blow of a sandaled foot struck a centaur's horse-body from behind, crushing and snapping it. Unable to absorb the impact, its limbs shattered, and the massive body was crushed as if it were nothing, spraying dark blood as it turned into a stain on the fifth floor.

The one who had accomplished this — leaping from a height that would have killed Subaru, crushing a witchbeast flat beneath the sole of his sandal, and standing there perfectly unharmed — was the outrageous act of Reid Astrea.

"――――"

Given their appearance, the witchbeasts — likely lacking intelligence — froze in their tracks, and at the sudden intrusion of the red-haired man's presence, the infant-like wailing all ceased at once.
Looking closely, Subaru guessed that the headless witchbeasts were confirming the presence of their opponent not through sight, but through some other sense entirely.

Even lacking sight, through whatever senses remained to them, Reid's abnormality communicated itself more than sufficiently.
And that held equally true for the knight who had been putting on a grand display against the witchbeasts — Julius, whose five senses were all perfectly intact.

"You... why are you here?"

"Why, why, why. Same damn question outta both of you. Ain't there somethin' else you could ask? Like the secret to bein' popular with women, or what's a good brand of booze, or how come you're so damn strong."

Dumbstruck, eyes wide, in front of Julius, Reid flicked a scrap of flesh off the sole of his sandal with a finger. Then he pointed a hand toward another centaur standing nearby.
In that hand, by whatever joke of fate, was gripped a pair of thin wooden sticks — chopsticks.

"Secret to bein' popular with women's your face. Good booze is the liquor 'Granhiert.' ――As for why I'm the strongest in the world, that's 'cause I'm me."

Pausing between words, Reid gave the chopsticks a little flick with his fingertips.
In the next instant, cracks raced across the entire body of the centaur that had been frozen in place, and blood burst out. The witchbeast, realizing its own body's collapse only belatedly, screamed in the agony that brings "death."

That it sounded like the wail of an infant clinging to this world was, in the worst possible way, a matter of terrible taste.
But the man who had brought this about kept his grin unbroken, and turned that weapon — not a drop of blood on it — the chopsticks, away from the witchbeast and toward Julius instead.
And as if sensing something more terrifying in those chopsticks than their appearance suggested, Julius's yellow eyes went wide, and watching that, Reid bared his teeth and said —



"――There, now. Back to the 'Trial.' Go on and wrest it from me, before I get bored, you."
