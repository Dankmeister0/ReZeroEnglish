Arc 8 Chapter 2: "Old Grudges"

—Beatrice was at a loss over how to treat the girl called Rui.

They'd been partners of a sort, working together to send the magic crystal cannon fired from the Crystal Palace across the intervening distance to that far-off place.
Without Rui's inscrutable power of transference, they never could have reached that spot. And if that had failed, the battlefield would have claimed a great many lives, Zikr's among them.
That one act didn't wash away the sin Rui bore as the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony.
And yet—

"Ah-woo!"

"Am I to believe you bear no ill will toward Betty, or Subaru, or the others?"

Astride the swaying saddle, perched awkwardly with her weight on her seat, the red Galewind Horse ran on — child-sized though it was, carrying not just its rider but three extra passengers besides.
On that Galewind Horse's back sat Beatrice and Rui, with Subaru sandwiched between them. Unlike Beatrice, cradled in Subaru's arms, Rui — seated behind him — had nimbly risen to her feet atop the horse, and was now slapping Subaru's shoulder while pointing single-mindedly in one direction.

—Even to Beatrice's eyes, the state of the imperial capital of Lupugana was a cauldron of chaos.

It began with the clash between the regular army and the rebel forces; unaligned Flying Dragons and a certain Flying Dragon fought over the sky; look anywhere and a world of white and a world of red were despoiling the ordinary world; Giants and a Dragon rampaged; and now, one after another, the dead were rising to tear the city apart from within.
Exclusive and uninvolved as she generally was, Beatrice had directly touched few such cases — but this absurd, incoherent state of the world called to mind the turmoil of four hundred years ago.

"—'Witch'."

Looking back on that old age, the word slipped naturally from Beatrice's lips.
If anything symbolized that era four hundred years past, it was, without question, the 'Witch.'

Echidna, Beatrice's creator and the one she looked up to as a mother, was one of those called 'Witch.'
Beyond her, there had been several more 'Witches' bearing the name of a Sin — that era, when everyone had fought desperately just to live, had been a dark age as if a child's nightmare had come to life.
The present imperial capital was its return. —Just what could have summoned such a thing? Her chest tightened with unease.
While Beatrice held that anxiety close to her heart—

"—Hang on, Rem!"

Right behind her, she heard Subaru's voice, breath ragged with eagerness.
Rui gave a low growl and pointed, and Subaru, urging the Galewind Horse onward in the direction she indicated, showed no doubt at all that the one he awaited, the girl he sought, lay ahead.
Beatrice couldn't help but feel a twinge of unease wondering just what kind of time Subaru and Rui had spent together while she'd been separated from them — but she understood, at least, where that trust came from.

Beatrice, too, had no doubt that Rem lay in the direction Rui was pointing.
Now that she thought of it, Rui had used a vast amount of Mana to erase the magic crystal cannon, then made straight for Subaru to save Beatrice as she was fading away. The girl had some ability to pinpoint the location of whoever she was looking for.
Whether that worked for just anyone, or only for people she knew, remained unclear.

"Big house!"

"What do we do, Schult! The wall's high! Should we swing around to the front—"

"No, we don't have time for that! Tanza!"

This exchange took place as they crossed street after street, drawing closer to their destination.
The building, hidden until now by the rows of others, turned out to be a mansion occupying a broad, imposing plot in the northern part of the imperial capital — its surroundings alone marked it as the residence of someone of high standing in the city.
Naturally, a wall tall enough to guarantee proper security encircled the estate.
But—

"I'd like it understood, if you don't mind,"

—said the Deer Human girl, her voice as devoid of feeling as her expressionless face, only the faintest trace of displeasure leaking into her sidelong glance and murmur, as she kicked off the ground.
Tanza, hurtling straight for the wall ahead of her, continued.

"I am not Schult-sama's tool for breaking down walls."

The instant she finished declaring this, the thick sole of Tanza's boot struck the mansion wall dead-on — a wall very likely inscribed with a Divine Protection of Earth Attributes to reinforce its defenses — and caved it in, punched through it, brought it crashing down.
Amid a trailing roar and blast of wind, the girl's small silhouette shot through to the far side of the wall. As if diving headfirst into the resulting cloud of dust, the Galewind Horse carrying Beatrice and the others leapt into the grounds.
And then—

"Uh-woo!"
"—Subaru, over there, I suppose!"

Within the mansion's broad grounds, several buildings stood arrayed — a main house and a number of outbuildings — and for a moment they nearly lost their bearings on where to head. But just as Subaru swept his gaze around, Beatrice, her round eyes wide as she searched for their target, spotted the gathering of people.
At that same instant, Rui's gaze fixed on the same spot as Beatrice's, and her cry lent further certainty to the sighting.

Ahead, at the entrance of what looked to be an outbuilding, stood several figures.
Before a firmly shut door, there was a back clad in the tattered gear of an imperial soldier, and facing it, two more figures — one of them unfamiliar to Beatrice.

—But the other was a girl Beatrice recognized.

"—Ere!"
"Meownya!!"

A dull gray blade was raised, aimed at the girl and her companion.
The instant it caught their eyes, Beatrice and Subaru, hands joined, thrust out their free hands and chanted their spell — the resulting arrows of deep violet struck the backs of the pale-faced men, turning them to crystal.

"Gh—"

A stifled groan escaped them, but they were allowed no further action.
With a high, cracking sound like breaking ice, the men shattered and crumbled away.
And then, with their lives saved by a hair's breadth, the Galewind Horse reached the girl, who stood there wide-eyed with shock. Pulled by the hand, Beatrice climbed down to the ground together with Subaru.
There—

"—Sorry to keep you waiting, Rem! The main act has arrived!"

Subaru declared this with a triumphant flourish, winking one eye and flashing his teeth.
Even in this childlike form, his unwavering valor — always running headlong to help someone — filled Beatrice with pride, and she too, from within his arms, looked at the person before them.

Under the combined gaze of Beatrice and Subaru, the girl whose life had just been saved widened her eyes.
Her throat seemed to tremble, as if with emotion and gratitude—

"—Who are you?"

—was, unfortunately, not quite the atmosphere of what she said next; her tone was, instead, one of puzzled wariness.


△▼△▼△▼△


"C'mon, it's me, me! Sure, I shrunk a little, but it's still me! You can tell, right?"

"...I'm grateful you saved us, but I don't recall you."

"No way!"

At this unexpectedly frosty response from Rem, Subaru flailed his short arms in despair.
He'd been fully expecting a moving reunion — after all, she'd just endured a helpless, life-threatening ordeal and been rescued from it — so this reaction was a letdown.
Of course, Subaru himself had shrunk without Rem's knowledge, so demanding unreserved joy from her was probably asking too much.

"In that sense, I get why Rui — who already knew me in this state — accepted it right away, but Beatrice, you took it in stride awfully fast too..."

"It's not as though it means nothing to Betty, I suppose. It's just that, shrunk or not, you're still Betty's partner, that's all. More importantly—"

"More importantly?"

Lowered from his arms to the ground, Beatrice looked back and forth between Subaru and Rem. Then, narrowing her eyes with their distinctive markings, she said,

"That attitude — it's nothing like what Betty heard from Subaru. That girl... Rem, wasn't she supposed to be kind to Subaru? Was that a lie, I suppose?"

"No, it's not a lie! It's just that right now she's forgotten a bunch of stuff!"

"Forgotten..."

Rem's frosty response had, by extension, planted an unwanted suspicion in Beatrice's mind.
Probably the suspicion that Subaru had been feeding everyone false information about the 'Sleeping Beauty'-state Rem that they didn't remember.
It was a preposterous misunderstanding, and besides — misrepresenting the personality of the sleeping Rem as fact would be nothing short of sacrilege, not to mention devastating to Subaru.
In the face of this protest, Beatrice fell silent and turned back to Rem.

"You — you don't remember Betty, I suppose?"

"...No, I believe this is our first meeting."

"—. I see. That explains it, I suppose."

"You seem satisfied, but in the end, who are you people? I am grateful for the rescue, but..."

From the way Rem answered, Beatrice's expression showed she'd accepted that Subaru wasn't just making things up. Rem's own confusion, however, remained unresolved.
No matter how satisfied Subaru and Beatrice were on their own, Rem's questions only kept piling up.
Within himself, the strangeness of the situation had mostly faded by now, but seeing this side of Rem's reaction, it came through all too clearly — big Subaru and small Subaru were simply different people to her.

"Anyway, I'm still me! Rem, listen, just—"

"Wha—?! I was wondering who in the world had come to help, but Mister Husband! It's Mister Husband, isn't it! How splendid of you to come rushing to the rescue!"

"Oh, hey, see, Flop-san gets it—wait, huh?! Flop-san?!"

Just as Subaru was struggling to untangle Rem's confusion, a blond-haired young man popped up from behind her, and Subaru was stunned to see Flop standing there.
He'd come to rescue Rem — he'd never imagined Flop would be here with her too.

"Why are you here? Don't tell me you got kidnapped along with Rem?"

"Well now, coming to rescue your wife without knowing a thing — how blind of you! But yes, that's more or less the right idea. To be more precise, it's because I suffered a mortal wound that my wife accompanied me for treatment — that's the accurate version!"

"Mortal wound — whoa, seriously?! That's a nasty injury!"

Flop seemed as cheerful as ever, but despite that ridiculously bright disposition, the skin visible through the gaps in his clothes was wrapped round and round in bandages, and his complexion looked somewhat drained of color.
Going by experience from the Sword Slave Island, this was the kind of severe wound where just standing upright was an achievement.
"Suffered a mortal wound" didn't sound like an exaggeration after all.

"But then Rem came along with you... man, you've got some serious guts."

"Wait, please. You're trying to move right along, but... Flop-san, the way you're addressing this boy—"

"Ah, maybe these things are easier to notice from a third party's perspective? Just as my wife suspects, this is indeed Mister Husband. Though his baby face has gotten a touch more pronounced!"

"—————"

Prompted by Flop, Rem looked Subaru over again, intently this time.
In response to the doubt swimming in her pale blue eyes, Subaru tilted his face to give her a better look and flashed his teeth. The color of her doubt shifted to exasperation, then quickly to astonishment.

"I already thought you were a strange person, a strange person... but can you not only cross-dress, but transform into a child as well?"

"No! This state is beyond my control! Right, Beatrice?"

"Subaru, were you cross-dressing again somewhere Betty and the others didn't know about, I suppose...?"

"Don't latch onto that part right now!"

Caught under suspicious looks from both Rem and Beatrice, Subaru floundered. Amid this exchange, Flop raised a finger to his own well-formed face and added,

"Well, it wasn't just Mister Husband who dressed as a woman — the village chief and I did too, you know. We all suited it remarkably well, if I do say so, and quite cutely too."

"That was me in my all-in-on-charm era. Anyway! I'm glad we managed to reunite."

Letting out a breath of relief, Subaru smiled at Rem and Flop. Flop nodded back without reservation at that smile, while Rem fell silent for a moment in thought before saying,

"...You came all this way looking for me?"

"Of course. Well, I didn't know you'd been kidnapped until partway through, but the moment I found out, personal feelings just came pouring out. What made me sure you were here was—"

"Uu-!"

Just as Subaru answered Rem's question, the very reason for that certainty came flying in.
Cutting in between Subaru and Rem, Rui leapt onto Rem, her golden hair bouncing as she threw her arms around her. Rem gave a surprised "Wah" but caught the girl anyway.
Seeing her face up close, relief bloomed in Rem's eyes.

"Rui-chan, I'm glad you're safe. You went off with that person on your own, and I was worried you might have been treated coldly."

"Ah-woo!"

"I don't think I was ever cold enough toward you to warrant that much worry... was I? I was. Maybe I was. Well, I'm feeling sorry about it now, at least."

Petting the head of the clinging Rui, Subaru pursed his lips at Rem's comment.
In truth, if the last parting between Rem and Rui had been when Subaru and the others headed for Chaosflame, then the relationship between Subaru and Rui at that point had been about as bad as it could get.
There was no telling whether, somewhere along the way, Subaru might have decided this was a fine opportunity and abandoned Rui in some field. Rem's worry was hardly unreasonable.
Still—

"I'm not thinking of doing anything to Rui now. We made up."

"...Really? Rui-chan?"

"U!"

Rui nodded with a beaming smile, and at that reaction, the question marks hanging over Rem finally dissolved.
What might have been the biggest crisis since his body had shrunk had resolved peacefully, and Subaru breathed a sigh of relief. Just then, gripping Subaru's hand, Beatrice murmured,

"Even meaning to accept it, it's still a strange sight to take in, I suppose. That girl and Rem, getting along so peacefully like that — it's an odd feeling, I suppose."

At Beatrice's mutter, Subaru gave an inward wry smile.
Thinking back to when they'd first been sent flying into the Vollachian Empire, when the newly-awakened Rem had regarded Rui — whose intentions were utterly unclear at the time — as an enemy, Subaru wholeheartedly agreed with her.
By some twist of fate, the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony, the very culprit who'd turned Rem into the 'Sleeping Beauty,' was now spending time here in friendly fashion in this very same place.

"—. As for that, I still haven't got a real answer."

Rem's memories hadn't come back yet. Rui's true nature remained unknown, too.
But even so, he'd managed to be reunited with Rem, alive, and Rui was showing friendly behavior toward them within arm's reach. If that was the case, then surely a solution could be found.
And so—

"—Schult-sama, I don't think we have much time to spare on peaceful reunions."

Into that atmosphere of reunion stepped a girl whose interruption, however unwelcome in tone, came at just the right moment.
Subaru turned back at the intervention of the perceptive little kimono-clad girl, Tanza, with a "Right," while also keeping an eye on Idra, who was keeping watch on their surroundings from atop the horse.

"Found who we were looking for. By the way — there's no one else who's been carried off, right?"

"Not as far as Betty's heard, I suppose."

"I haven't seen anyone either. Flop-san, do you know of..."

"I can't think of any other acquaintances who might've been brought here either. Unless, heaven forbid, Medium's been carried off too! What do you say, Mister Husband!"

"She wasn't kidnapped when I last saw her. ...Though she had shrunk."

Worried about his little sister's safety, Flop pressed, and Subaru kept quiet about the fact that Medium had shrunk too.
Aside from her shrunken appearance, she'd been perfectly healthy, and surely by now Medium had made it safely out of Chaosflame and rejoined the Guaral group.
There was no time to waste hashing out every little detail here and going back and forth about it.
It was precisely to secure time for that kind of noisy, chaotic back-and-forth later that they needed to move now.

"So, anyway, including Rem and Flop-san, I want to take as many people with us as possible when we escape. Anyone else here?"

"There's a woman named Katya-san I got to know here. And then, in this outbuilding..."

"Here?"

Rem nodded and glanced toward the firmly shut door right beside them.
They'd had to deal with the enemy in a hurry, so Subaru hadn't checked it properly, but apparently Rem and the others had been trying to open this building up.

"What's inside, apparently, are 'Black-Haired Crown Princes' gathered from all over."

"'Black-Haired Crown Princes'... the fakes posing as Schultz, huh?"

Flop answered the question in Subaru's mind, and Idra grimaced at the content of it.
The notion Flop had voiced — that these were fakes of "Schultz" — was, broadly speaking, shared by every member of the Pleiades Battalion; it was one of the great lies Subaru had been maintaining with them.
The 'Black-Haired Crown Prince' talked about throughout the empire was said to be a hidden child of Emperor Vincent Vollachia, and every such figure who'd made a name for himself in various regions was, without exception, a fake.
The real 'Black-Haired Crown Prince' was none other than Natsuki Schultz himself — or so the story went.

"People propped up here and there as a figurehead for their rebellions, huh. Frankly, I don't much care for that kind of shameless approach."

"Betty agrees with the bearded man on that, I suppose. Don't like the one who thought it up, either."

"B-bearded man..."

At the sight of the presumably imprisoned fake princes inside the outbuilding, Idra voiced his displeasure without hesitation, and Beatrice nodded along in agreement.
Frankly, Idra's words stung Subaru's ears too. The remark Beatrice tacked on also seemed to back up Subaru's own suspicion about just whose scheme this had been.
Honestly, it was hard to tell whether the fake princes were good people or bad ones, but—

"Schult-sama, what will you do?"

"I mean, what else is there to do? If we leave them stuck here, sooner or later they're absolutely going to get killed by the zombies, so there's no option except to help them."

"—————"

Keeping her voice low, Tanza asked for Subaru's judgment, and he answered as above.
Tanza's eyes widened at that answer — because she, and she alone, had known since their time on the Sword Slave Island that the 'Black-Haired Crown Prince,' the identity of the emperor's heir that Subaru claimed for himself, was a lie spread to sow confusion.
Part of why the comrades of the 'Pleiades Battalion' trusted Subaru, and were willing to let him openly join this Battle for the Imperial Capital, was largely because they believed Subaru truly was the 'Black-Haired Crown Prince.'
If they let the captive fake princes out, the chances of that lie being exposed would rise considerably.

"But that's one thing, and this is another."

Well aware of Tanza's concerns, Subaru wasn't about to sacrifice people's lives just to protect a lie.
Of course, he could only hope this consideration would get through to the fake princes, and that they'd hold quiet, grateful feelings toward Subaru and the others for it.

"That's just like Betty's Subaru, I suppose."

Beatrice squeezed Subaru's hand and looked at Tanza with evident pride. For a moment, something sharp seemed to crackle between the two of them again, but Subaru set that aside.
With that settled, he turned to face the outbuilding's door.

"Rem, you two got a key for this door?"

"N-no, we didn't know where to find the key, so we were thinking of forcing it open."

"Counting on my wife's arm strength. As you can see, my own arms are exactly as feeble as they look."

Rem, with Rui still attached to her waist, and Flop, flexing a pitifully weak bicep. At their answers Subaru scratched his cheek and, rather sheepishly, turned to Tanza.

"Could you do your master-key thing again?"

"...I don't know what a 'master key' is, but I understand what you're asking. Schult-sama, allow me to say this again, for the record—"

"Tanza's not a tool for breaking open walls, right. Sorry."

Subaru bowed his head solemnly, and Tanza let out a long, long sigh.
But sighing wasn't all she did — ever considerate, Tanza said "please move aside" to get Subaru and Rem away from the door, then took hold of the large padlock on it with both hands.
With that, she put her strength into her slender arms, and the lock twisted apart and snapped.

It was a fairly shocking sight, but Subaru and Idra were used to it by now.
While Rem and Flop stared wide-eyed at Tanza's unbelievable strength, Subaru caught his breath for a moment, then slowly pushed the outbuilding's door wide open.
And then—

"I know everyone here's got a lot going on, but for now, let's set aside our various grudges and work together to escape from here, all right?"

So Subaru said, taking care not to put the people inside on guard, calling out into the outbuilding — to the fake princes held captive in the narrow cells lined up within.

"—————"

At Subaru's call, the faces that turned toward him belonged to some fourteen or fifteen boys.
Given that they were all called 'Black-Haired Crown Princes,' it stood to reason, working backward from the age of Emperor Vincent Abel, that they'd cluster around Subaru's current age.
Even so, the sight of children isolated from view and locked in cells turned his stomach.

"That said, the spacing between the individual cells is generous, and they appear well kept, so I wouldn't call the conditions here truly dire."

Tanza, peering into the outbuilding from beside him, added her own opinion to Subaru's impression.
As she said, the space had at least a minimum standard of cleanliness. The fake princes seemed to have been properly fed as well; none of them looked extremely weakened.

"I suppose they were prepared, just in case, for the possibility that the real prince was among them."

"Might just mean the owner had no particular taste for tormenting children, I suppose."

"...Given the master of this house's temperament, either explanation seems plausible."

The impression of the outbuilding's interior passed from Tanza to Beatrice and then to Rem, and from that explanation Subaru found himself picturing the owner of this vast estate — presumably some powerful figure in the imperial capital.
Given the circumstances, rounding up this many candidates for 'Black-Haired Crown Prince' was itself, most likely, an expression of defiance toward the false emperor — one who surely knew perfectly well there was no such thing as a real fake prince.

"The son himself would know better than anyone that he's a fake... wait, hold on. Is there a chance the fake emperor himself doesn't know? If Abel's the type to be careless with women..."

"Not that it's my place to say, but I have a hard time picturing that side of Abel-san."

"Yeah, me too. That guy probably thinks he's the most beautiful woman around when he's cross-dressing."

Subaru said this, recalling the time he'd gone by Natsumi Schultz, Abel had gone by Bianca, and Flop, for that matter, had been Flora.
In any case—

"I'm going to open the cells now. Outside's gotten pretty lively, and you can probably tell from the noise and the shaking. Let go of your grudges — let's all get out of here together!"

"...Get out, you say? But the cells are locked."

Among the fake princes inside — of varying races and skin tones — one voice responded to Subaru's call, and Subaru caught it with an "Oh."
All black-haired, black-eyed — a physical trait not so common in this other world, shared with Subaru himself — and he felt an unbidden sense of kinship with them as he said,

"Don't worry about the lock. Like so."

"Like so."

Subaru gestured with his thumb at Tanza beside him, and she gave a bow, holding up the twisted, broken padlock in her hand for the fake princes to see.
Every face turned to a look of shock, the half-doubting expressions vanishing. In their place, awe of varying degrees was directed at Tanza, but—

"Doesn't look like anyone's going to get in our way. Tanza can handle getting these guys out — meanwhile, Rem!"

"Y-yes?"

"That friend you mentioned earlier, the one you got to know here — where is she?"

"—, If you mean Katya-san..."

Rem, who had been resting a hand on Rui's head, tightened her expression and turned back toward the outside of the outbuilding. Judging by her reaction, this Katya person was probably in the main house.
Within the same grounds, they'd succeeded in defeating the three zombies in front of the outbuilding, but there were likely others who'd made their way in elsewhere. They needed to get her out somehow before those things found her.

"This Katya person — what does she look like?"

"She has brown, curly hair, and deep-set eyes. And..."

"And?"

"Miss Katya has trouble with her legs. So while my wife and I headed for the outbuilding, we had her hide in the other building over there."

"Trouble with her legs..."

At the details Flop supplied in Rem's place, Subaru put a hand to his chin.
Fortunately, they had the Galewind Horse they'd ridden in on, which made for a convenient way to carry someone with leg trouble. With the fake princes to bring along too, their overall mobility would drop considerably, but that probably wouldn't be an issue given the horse.

"What worries me is that Tanza's the only real fighter we've got."

"With Betty and Subaru as they are now, I doubt we'd fall behind that Deer Human girl, I suppose."

"I believe I can hold my own in a fight too, though not on the level of Weitz or Lord Gustav."

To Subaru's concern about their combat strength, Beatrice and Idra offered reassuring answers.
Of course, Idra would need to keep hold of the Galewind Horse's reins, so it wasn't something to over-rely on, but making the best use of the cards in hand was exactly what Subaru was good at.

"If they've been forced into playing fake prince, there's probably at least one who can ride a horse..."

"Um, I'm worried about Katya-san, so if possible..."

"—, Sorry, right, of course."

As Subaru tried to work through the logistics, Rem, her face impatient, pressed her point.
He needed to remove the source of her anxiety as quickly as possible, and Subaru nodded back in agreement. For the moment, with himself and Beatrice on hand, they had ways of dealing with zombies.

"Just in case, Tanza, come along too. And if Rem or Flop-san could come to help put this Katya person at ease, that would—"

That way, they could avoid causing needless confusion — Subaru was in the middle of saying just that when—

"—No need to worry about that. Katya's already been safely rescued."

"—————"

—A voice spoke from outside the outbuilding, and it cut Subaru's words short. No — it wasn't simply cut short because someone had interrupted.
It was because the voice that reached his ears drove something ice-cold straight through Subaru's heart.

"—Subaru?"

Beatrice, gripping Subaru's hand, felt his whole body suddenly tense and noticed the change.
But Subaru couldn't respond to Beatrice's words — he moved swiftly toward the outbuilding's entrance, stepping out ahead of Rem and Flop, who stood there, before they could even turn around.
To confirm who it was. —And to keep them from getting anywhere near Rem.

There, standing as the source of that voice, was—

"Even so, strange coincidences do happen. Though, well — given the circumstances, is it really so strange?"

"—————"

"We've each got plenty going on, I'm sure... but for now, why don't we set aside our troublesome grudges and work together to escape from here?"

So the man said, mockingly echoing what Subaru had just said moments before, and shrugged.

—The most troublesome enemy Subaru had made in this empire, the man born of the deepest grudge: Todd Fang.
