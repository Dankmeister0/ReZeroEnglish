Arc 3 Chapter 64: "The Full Story of the Witch Cult Hunt"

"Here's the witch cult hunt plan, simple enough for a monkey to follow—it's basically the same as the White Whale. I use my scent to lure the rest out, then we take down whatever bites. Easy, right?"

At the sheer flippancy of Subaru's proposed plan, the group seated in a circle all frowned in unison. Some out of bewilderment, some out of concern, some out of anxiety, and at least one because the person next to them was making a strange face and it seemed worth imitating—but for all their differences, the frowns all stemmed, at bottom, from the same judgment. Namely:

"Isn't it dangerous to treat them the same as a mindless beast? Whatever else they are, they're surely not simple enough to be lured in with the same trick that works on witchbeasts."

"That's the thing, though—they are simple. At least, if I've got someone skilled enough to erase their presence tailing me from behind, the odds are pretty good it'll work."

Subaru shook his head at Julius's point, casting his mind back to an earlier memory.

In the forest where the Witch Cult had cast its net, they'd apparently been able to pinpoint Subaru's presence as if it were written on a map. No matter what route he took, they always appeared in front of him. That, in turn, was surely proof that the Witch's Lingering Scent they could smell was wafting off Subaru's body into the surroundings.
But in truth, there was reason to suspect that while the Witch Cultists could sense Subaru's presence, their ability to detect anyone else wasn't especially sharp.

"When I tangled with the Witch Cult before, they were so fixated on me that they never noticed someone else coming at them from behind."

Subaru still had a faint memory, hazy as it was, of the moment a Witch Cultist reaching out to seize him had his head crushed by an iron ball.
—It was the memory of Rem, desperately trying to protect Subaru, thrown from the dragon carriage and groaning in pain, covered in wounds, from the Witch Cultists.

Back then, they hadn't noticed Rem at all until they took her first strike.
Partly that was because they were too fixated on Subaru, and no doubt it also owed a great deal to the fact that Rem was skilled at erasing her presence in the mountains.
In other words—

"If I dangle myself as bait, anyone else stays out of their field of view. So we use that to lure out whoever's scattered around the forest, and find their hideouts."

"And then raid the hideouts we find, is that it? But word of the disturbance will spread quickly. Once it does, it's obvious the believers in the other hideouts will move."

"That's why we need numbers. We watch the hideouts we find, and once the signal goes up, we hit them all at once and sweep the lot. Well?"

Subaru held up a finger to punctuate his proposal, and Julius touched his lips, thoughtful. Beside him, Ferris nodded, sounding impressed, with a "Nyah, I see"—while the mercenary band, Ricardo and Mimi among them, murmured that it was a plan worth considering. Amid all this—

"There's just one thing that concerns me, if I may."

So said the small demihuman who raised a hand—a boy about Mimi's height, with a face much like hers, an adorable youth with a monocle over one eye: Tivey.

Introduced as the youngest of the siblings alongside Mimi and Hetaro, he fixed Subaru with eyes that shone with even sharper intellect than his brother or sister's, and said—

"Our employer seems awfully well-informed about the Witch Cult's affairs. Is that information we can actually trust? We're talking about the Witch Cult here—the great unknown, all vague and murky."

Tivey turned a suspicious eye on Subaru for knowing so much, voicing his misgivings aloud. Subaru clicked his dry tongue softly at the entirely reasonable remark.

"There's history between me and them. Honestly, I figured someone would try to sound me out like this. I figured it, but... you're just going to have to trust me."

He couldn't explain it.
If he spoke of Return by Death, his heart—whether it was his own heart or someone else's, he couldn't say—would suffer agony like the end of the world.
So that was the absolute limit of what Subaru could offer. If more than that were demanded of him, he had no way to answer.

At Subaru's answer, Tivey narrowed his eyes further still and opened his mouth to press further. But—

"Honestly, being told to just believe you with no grounds at all is..."

"I dunno what you're so worried about. C'mon, be a bit more big about it, you're a boy, aren't you, Tivey?"

So said Tivey's sister, laughing boisterously as she smacked the back of his head as hard as she could.

There came a tremendous cracking sound, and Tivey's head snapped forward so hard it looked ready to pop off. Mimi caught the back of his neck as he pitched forward, turning her wide-eyed, blinking brother to face her.

"This mister here, he's, like, that guy—he worked super hard beating that huge fish, y'know? No way someone who tried that hard is thinking of doing something awful to us. You don't even gotta think about it, silly."

"S-sis, that's—!"

Shaking his head, Tivey tried to bring his eyes into focus and push back, but Mimi cut him off again with a delighted laugh. She lined up her forehead with his as he leaned forward—crack—and sparks flew before Tivey's eyes.

"Oww."

"See, that's why you're always so ko-kazashii... huh? Ko-kaza? Koza-ka? Koza-kozashii...?"

"D-do you mean 'cheeky'?"

"Yeah, that one! Keep on doing that and you'll never get any bigger."

Having headbutted her brother, the sister poked a finger at the bump now swelling on his forehead, bringing tears to his eyes all over again, before glancing back at Subaru.

"Tivey didn't fight that fish earlier, so maybe that's why he can't trust mister here. But if that's the problem, he can just trust me instead."

"..."

"I trust mister, so Tivey should trust me, and trust mister along with it. And if anything happens, I'll protect Tivey no matter what, 'kay?"

Mimi laughed and slapped her brother's shoulder. At her words, Tivey's eyes widened in surprise for just a moment, but he quickly let out a breath, the fight gone out of him.

At Tivey's change of heart, the people around them who'd been watching the exchange broke into smiles too. Laughter spilled out unbidden, and Mimi tilted her head, puzzled.

"What's wrong?"

"Don't worry about it. Actually, you're just fine the way you are. Well said."

A large palm came down from above, engulfing Mimi's small head entirely and ruffling it roughly.

Ricardo rubbed away at Mimi with obvious delight as her head was twisted this way and that, and she squeezed her eyes shut as though it tickled.

"Sure, there's things that bug me here and there, but doubtin' the bro at this point ain't it. We're well past that stage already, is what I'm sayin'."

Ricardo winked at Subaru, who was left dumbstruck, utterly lost in how the conversation had gotten here. Following his lead, Wilhelm nodded, "Indeed."

"That my long-cherished wish was fulfilled was only thanks to Master Subaru's help. That being so, it is only natural that I should answer Master Subaru's plea. There is no need to harbor suspicion, nor to give rise to doubt."

"I... I'm..."

At the trust Wilhelm's eyes entrusted to him, Subaru's lips trembled.

No excuse came to him. He couldn't tell them everything. And yet Wilhelm, Ricardo, Mimi—all of them said they'd trust him anyway. That was—

"That trust arises from the results Master Subaru has already accomplished."

A track record substantial enough for them to trust Subaru, even though he couldn't explain the circumstances.

Just as Rem had trusted Subaru no matter what, so too did the people here now—bewildered though they might be by the truth of his words, they harbored no doubt about his sincerity.

Repeating Return by Death over and over, Subaru had finally, at the end of it all, found one way to convey information about the future to them—and here, now, before his very eyes, that thread had connected.


* * *


'I-it's not like I'm happy just because you believe me or anything. These aren't tears of joy, they're just tears that came out because I remembered the time I failed to record the finale of my favorite anime, so don't go misunderstanding that I'm happy about this!'

—That was Subaru's flustered attempt to hide his embarrassment at the end of the exchange, but that's a separate matter. From there, the Witch Cult hunt proceeded swiftly, according to plan.

They entered the Mathers domain, and Subaru went alone into the forest where the Witch Cult was presumably hiding. Behind him, at a careful distance, followed those skilled in stealth—Mimi, Tivey, Wilhelm, and others.

Whenever a Witch Cultist showed themselves, drawn by Subaru's scent, they'd let the cultist lead them to the hideout, station part of the strike force in hiding nearby, and repeat the process.

"If I take what Pete-boy said at face value... there ought to be about ten hideouts."

If Subaru remembered right, Petelgeuse had called his own subordinates his 'Fingers.'

He also remembered that the group Rem had fought and annihilated to reclaim Subaru had been called the 'Ring Finger.' If that held true, then this time's Witch Cult, including Petelgeuse's own hideout, ought to be divided into ten small groups.

And indeed, thanks to Subaru's wandering the forest and scattering his scent everywhere, they managed to locate seven hideouts.

But by the time only two remained, the sun had already climbed quite high, and spending any more time risked exposing the ambush parties they'd left behind.

Under that judgment, with some unease still lingering, Subaru pressed on through woods he recognized, toward the cliff where Petelgeuse waited. There, he came face to face with the Sin Archbishop of Sloth—

"Did we get him!?"

Wilhelm's blade flashed in from nowhere, and Petelgeuse's slender frame was cleaved diagonally from behind. Sliced clean from shoulder to hip, Petelgeuse's face twisted in shock as his bloodshot eyes glared at Subaru—

"Impos—"

What the madman had meant to say next would remain forever unknown.

A flash of silver traced an arc, shedding blood as it tore through the wind in a single horizontal sweep, and the madman's head went flying lightly into the air amid a fountain of blood.

Subaru was struck speechless by the splatter-movie sight of a head flying off right in front of him. An instant later, a massive frame dropped in from directly above, swinging down a long machete gripped in both hands, hacking and crushing through the headless torso as it collapsed with a brutally messy cut.

A tremendous sound rang out as flesh was crushed between earth and steel, and at the wet noise that followed, nausea surged up Subaru's throat. Through sheer willpower he managed not to spit out the burning liquid pooling in his mouth, and crouching where he stood, he looked up with teary eyes.

"I-is it... over?"

"If this ain't the end of him, guess I'll start believin' in this Witch's Blessing thing myself."

Ricardo answered Subaru's timid question, machete slung over one shoulder. Subaru looked away from the sight of him scraping bits of flesh off the blade's edge with a finger, and looked down at his feet—where lay what was left of Petelgeuse, no longer recognizable as having any original form.

With a human body destroyed this thoroughly, it almost looked fake, lacking any sense of reality. Subaru felt a strange relief as the nausea receded because of it.

"Wonder if everyone else pulled it off okay."

"Even though the Sin Archbishop was our primary target, our main strength gathered here. Still, with Ferris and Master Julius in command, there should be no trouble."

Wilhelm, having gone to confirm the severed head, returned and gave a small shrug.

The old swordsman vouched for it, but Subaru's worries were far from over. They had the numbers, and it was a surprise attack on hideouts they'd already located. He'd told everyone to go all out, and the basic plan was to prioritize burying the enemy alive, so problems shouldn't arise easily, but—

"Witch Cultists are pretty solidly combat types, though. Enough to give even Rem trouble."

"Ferris aside, Julius-boy oughta be fine. Among us lot, only Wil here and I could really go toe-to-toe with him."

"...Huh. He's that strong, huh."

Ricardo's answer left Subaru feeling somewhat conflicted.

As a reliable ally, Julius being strong was more than welcome—but the persistent sense of inferiority he still felt around the man made it oddly hard to fully accept.
In any case—

"Our hole here's plugged, so no reinforcements coming, right?"

"With mine and Sis's resonance waves, the cave under the cliff is completely collapsed. If there was anyone still inside, my condolences."

The demihuman siblings, who'd gone to confirm the collapsed and buried cave, returned. At Tivey's report, Subaru felt fresh relief that the danger here had been eliminated.

Then he closed one eye and turned his gaze to what remained of what had once been Petelgeuse.

"Finish him off in one go with an ambush from an angle he'd never expect. Honestly, I had my doubts about the method—but don't take it personal. You're the far bigger piece of trash here."

In this world, it had ended in an attempt only, but Petelgeuse's actions were still unforgivable to Subaru. From what he'd heard, this man calling himself Sloth had caused similar damage all over the world, again and again. There was no room for leniency.

The Witch Cultists buried under the collapse could be said to share that same guilt.

Looking back, Subaru himself had no memory of being harmed by them directly—but remembering how badly Rem had been hurt, a simmering anger welled up in him too.

"Witch Cultists, huh..."

"Hm?"

"What is it these guys even want, doing all this? The Witch is some incomprehensible thing hated by the whole world, and yet..."

At the question Subaru murmured quietly, everyone tilted their heads.

Only these four had accompanied Subaru to bring down Petelgeuse. Wilhelm knit his brow, troubled; Ricardo wrinkled his muzzle, looking uninterested; and Mimi was busy kicking dirt over Petelgeuse's decaying remains. That left just one to speak up.

"Perhaps they harbor some death wish? Or if not that, decadent thinking—or they've convinced themselves they're the unhappiest people in the world and want company on their way down?"

"Man, that's dark. 'I don't understand it one bit'... is somethin' I can't say even if you tore my mouth open, though."

A death wish—the urge to drag everything down with you and let it all dissolve into nothing—was something anyone driven into a hopeless enough situation could probably recognize in themselves.
Subaru in particular had leaned that way strongly enough that he understood it all too well.

At Subaru voicing such sentimental words, Ricardo waved a hand—"hey, hey"—and pierced him with sharp eyes, baring his fangs.

"No good, sympathizin' with the likes of these guys. Not even a sliver of feeling, not a single hair's worth of understandin' should pass between you and them. Otherwise you'll get pulled in, thinkin' they're one of us."

"I'm not talking about anything that grand. It's just scary to think there are people out there whose thinking I can't understand at all, not even one bit, existing among the same humanity as me."

"That's exactly the mistake right there."

Ricardo poked a finger at Subaru's forehead as he protested, then used that same finger to point at the cave, and at Petelgeuse's corpse being buried beneath the earth.

"You shouldn't think of 'em as the same humanity. We and these guys are different creatures. That's why we can't understand each other, and words don't get through. Go lookin' for understanding, and you might just find a 'Gospel' delivered to your door, and I won't be responsible."

"Gospel... You know something about that?"

That word was one Petelgeuse had uttered again and again.

'Gospel'—Subaru had simply assumed it referred to the black-bound book Petelgeuse always carried, but—

"And now I'm wondering, you were pretty eager for this Witch Cult hunt too—you got some history with them as well?"

"...Look, bro. Since you don't seem to know, I'll say it plain: not gettin' mixed up with the Witch Cult, and not tryin' to understand 'em, that's just common sense. Same as the Witch bein' hated by everybody—plain, ordinary common sense. The Gospel ain't some complicated story either."

Shaking his head, Ricardo brushed off Subaru's question as though it were a nuisance. Subaru opened his mouth to press the matter, but Wilhelm spoke first.

"The Gospel is something every one of the Witch Cult's believers comes to possess... yes, one might call it something like a scripture, perhaps."

"A scripture...?"

"Way I hear it, it just gets sent to whoever's likely to join the Witch Cult. And once they've got their hands on it, that's the end of 'em—lo and behold, out pops a fresh Witch worshipper."

"Wha—!?"

Subaru was struck speechless at Ricardo's telling of it, delivered with such a jaunty air.

Reading deeper into those words, wasn't it possible that people sent this book called the 'Gospel' were being brainwashed by it, forced into joining the Witch Cult against their own will?

"If that's true, then the guys who got buried alive were just caught up in it..."

"Master Subaru, that is not the case."

Wilhelm quickly soothed Subaru, whose face had gone pale with alarm. He gave a deep nod, as though to reassure Subaru's darting eyes.

"The moment the 'Gospel' arrives, that person can already no longer turn back. And the moment they open it, their own will is made plain. There is no such mercy for them as being 'brainwashed.' Did the Sin Archbishop you faced seem sane to you?"

"N-no, he sure didn't... but I figured he might've been an exception."

Petelgeuse's deranged behavior raised worries about the Witch Cult's corruption in a sense apart from brainwashing altogether. Honestly, it was near impossible to derive real reassurance from that alone, but Subaru couldn't imagine Wilhelm offering empty comfort either, so he swallowed the concern that had surfaced and let it go.

"Hey, hey, isn't this enough already? No point standing around here yappin', let's head back to everyone else already."

So said Mimi, who hadn't taken part in the conversation until now, growing impatient.

Wagging the tail poking out from the seat of her underclothes, she finished burying Petelgeuse's body in a simple makeshift grave, then bounced up and down on the spot.

"We already killed the enemy dead, so we gotta go check if everyone else managed to kill their guys dead too, right? C'mon, let's do that!"

"You talk with this slow, drawn-out drawl but the actual content is pretty violent when you sit and listen to it. Combined with how cute you look, I'm getting a real culture shock over here."

"Ehehe, callin' me cute, how embarrassin'~"

Subaru let out a breath at Mimi, blushing after picking out only the convenient part to hear, and turned around.

The cave lay in complete silence, and there was no shocking sign, at least for now, of Petelgeuse's remains beginning to regenerate. It was a rather anticlimactic state of affairs, but just as Mimi said—everything that could be done here, and at least today's confrontation with the Witch Cult, was over.

And it had ended in the best possible form: total victory for Subaru's side.

"No, but, seriously—do you really think it'd go this smooth? This is me we're talking about. You have any idea how many times I've poured my whole heart into something only to get betrayed? A deal this good... there's gotta be a trap somewhere, some pitfall..."

"What're you gettin' all paranoid for. C'mon, let's get movin'. We'll leave 'em waitin' forever otherwise."

Ricardo said this with an exasperated look at Subaru, who kept muttering, unable to fully believe in his own victory.

At his call, Subaru answered with a somewhat unconvinced "R-right," and followed the others, who had already started walking, away from the spot.

They had to circle the forest and rejoin the companions they'd stationed at each point. Only once their safety was confirmed could this battle truly be called a victory.
And then—

"—wait, isn't this exactly the kind of flag where he looks gone but he's actually revived!?"

Subaru came running back and slid to a stop, kicking up a cloud of dust.

Straight ahead as he raised his head, Petelgeuse's crude grave remained undisturbed, with no sign of anything crawling out from beneath it. No resurrection flag in sight, he judged.

"Phew, yeah, no way something like a third-rate horror flick actually happens."

"What the hell've you been doin' this whole time!? Get back here already or I'm really gonna get mad!!"

Ricardo punched Subaru, still unable to shake his paranoia, and roughly hoisted his body over one shoulder.

Carried by the tall demihuman, Subaru left the place, for real this time.
For real this time. And then—

"Mister was bein' so noisy about it, so just in case—"

—Mimi's tossed magic ore stone blew Petelgeuse's grave to pieces.

This time, with no lingering worry left at all, Petelgeuse, the Witch Cult's Sin Archbishop of Sloth, was blown to smithereens.


* * *


"From how you look, it seems your side wrapped up safely as well."

Julius carved a faint smile at the corner of his mouth as he looked up at Subaru, who had retrieved Patrasche—left waiting partway through the forest—and now sat astride her back.

Julius let out a light breath, his bangs slightly disheveled, his formal Royal Guard uniform speckled here and there with someone else's blood. Judging by the way he was wiping down the blade of the slender sword in his hand, things on this end apparently hadn't gone as an easy overwhelming victory from a first strike.

"At the moment your signal went up in the sky, as ill luck would have it, about four of them had just stepped outside. I had no choice but to handle destroying the hideout and dealing with the believers at the same time. I suppose you should count yourself fortunate it was me here—it would have been a bit troublesome for Ferris."

As he spoke, Julius sheathed his now-cleaned sword. Where his gaze turned, as already mentioned, lay the bodies of four figures in black robes.

They were bodies dispatched neatly, each with a single blow, in a manner that didn't compromise his elegant bearing in the slightest. Each had been run through at some point in the neck or torso, and lay collapsed, blood pooling from the wound. As Subaru recalled, these had been reasonably skilled fighters, so it served as quiet proof of just how formidable Julius's ability really was.

"So, what about the Sin Archbishop?"

"Wilhelm-san took his head off, and Ricardo smashed the body to pulp. Then the little cat's sister blew even the corpse to bits for good measure, as a joke, so it's certain."

Even Subaru had been startled that she'd blown the grave itself to bits, but once it was flatly declared she'd done it out of concern for him, it was hard to complain. He complained anyway.

Incidentally, the sister, having been scolded mercilessly by everyone, had fully sulked, and now sat curled up atop her brother's back, lips pursed. Her brother, saddled with carrying her, was entirely an innocent bystander.

As Subaru nodded, feeling the weight of it, Julius shifted his gaze past him—toward Wilhelm. At the meaningful look, Wilhelm answered quietly, "There's no mistake," and only then did Julius's expression finally ease.

"Hm, that's a relief. If Sir Wilhelm says so, it must be true. It seems we've at least managed to bring down the Witch Cult's commander here."

"You didn't trust my answer at all, did you!? I wasn't messing around either, I checked with my eyes peeled wide as saucers! I looked two, three times even!"

"My apologies, I'll offer an apology for that. Now then, having apologized, let's move on."

"Don't you get to just move on! That's exactly the kind of thing about you I can't stand!"

Subaru stamped his feet, openly furious at Julius's detached attitude. Julius glanced at him sidelong, the way one might regard something faintly amusing, then swept his eyes over their surroundings.

"I don't see Ricardo. Where is he?"

"That fake kobold's off welcoming Ferris back. Wilhelm-san said there's currently no sign of fighting anywhere else in the forest, so I figured splitting up to go meet everyone would be faster."

In the worst case, of course, "no sign of fighting" could simply mean the fighting was already over.

In which case, it could mean the group including the not-very-combat-capable Ferris had been wiped out in an instant—an absurd scenario, but a possible one.

"Well, to keep that from happening, I made sure some real fighters were gathered around Ferris. So even with him being a healing specialist who's basically hopeless on offense, it should work out somehow..."

"Aw, thanks so much for worryin' about lil' ol' me~♪"

A breath was blown softly against his ear from behind, and Subaru's limbs snapped rigid with shock. Glancing back with just his eyes, he found a small, adorable face gazing at him from up close—and knowing full well it belonged to a man, his face went pale rather than red.

"Seriously, who is this even supposed to please!? Stop it, I mean it, stop! This isn't me playing hard to get—do it again and I'm putting a killer punch right into that cute face of yours. Big Bang Impact incoming."

"Aw, all embarrassed now, how cute~. No need to worry, though—Subaru-kyun's Feri-chan came back safe and sound just for you, 'kay?"

Subaru, worn out by Ferris offering both a wink and a blown kiss together, let his shoulders slump but checked the man over anyway. At a glance, there seemed to be no difference from how he'd looked when they parted. Unlike Julius's side, it seemed everything there had gone off without a hitch.

Ricardo, who'd come back together with him, was followed in turn by the other members who'd accompanied Ferris. With this, it seemed—

"Everyone's regrouped safely... nowhere failed, right?"

"Including this spot, it seems fighting broke out in two locations, but there were no problems. As expected of those trained under Sir Wilhelm's banner—their base strength is on a different level."

He'd heard that the members who accompanied Ferris in particular were the pick of the litter. Given the rarity of Ferris's skill—the man who bore the epithet of "Blue"—that treatment was only natural, but even so, Subaru couldn't quite grasp what Crusch was thinking, sending him off to the front lines without hesitation.

"That's just Lady Crusch's sincerity for you. Don't make me spell it out, idiot."

"Mm..."

It seemed Subaru's thoughts had shown completely on his face, and Ferris, reading his mind perfectly, hurled that insult at him.

In any case—

"So the bulk of the Witch Cult's wiped out... but the problem is, there's still two hideouts we haven't found. We took out the head, so optimistically speaking they might just pull back, but..."

"We're talkin' about the Witch Cult here. Best not to count on 'em thinkin' normal, I'd say."

Ricardo said this, arms crossed, as if affirming Subaru's worry. It seemed the general opinion agreed with him too, judging by how many faces hardened and nodded gravely.

In truth, Subaru had already decided that leaving those uncertain elements unresolved was out of the question. So he chose a solid response for them too.

"Let's search the forest and hunt out the rest of the Witch Cult too. I'm not saying we should just kill them all, that's too rough... but at least, I think it's worth trying to capture them."

"Though I suspect the odds are high they'll take their own lives first. ...It's always been that way, up to now."

Julius said this quietly, as though correcting Subaru's overly naive assumption. Subaru caught his breath at that, but Julius too wore a pained expression on his profile.

"If we can manage without killing them, that's certainly for the best. I agree we should prioritize capture and search out the remaining believers. But I also think it may be time to go report the state of things to Lord Roswaal. What do you say?"

"...Oh, right. Yeah, that's true."

At the suggestion, Subaru was reminded of a reality that had completely slipped his mind.

Subaru had deliberately chosen not to rush to the mansion first to keep Emilia away from danger, and instead chosen the swift elimination of the Witch Cult. A large part of that was to avoid shifting things too far from the future he already knew, in terms of whether or not Petelgeuse's attack would occur.

And now, having splendidly defeated Petelgeuse himself—

"Am I... allowed to go back to the mansion now..."

The instant he murmured that quietly, a sudden sense of reality struck Subaru.

Blood began rushing to his face, and a feeling he hadn't been conscious of in a long while set his whole body burning.

Now, he could return to the mansion in triumph, chest held high.

He could show his face to Emilia with something to be proud of, and apologize for the pathetic mess and the string of behavior that had betrayed her trust just days before. Back then, he'd thought of nothing but himself, and hurt her badly, thoughtlessly—now he could offer her a heartfelt apology.

And only once they'd made up could Subaru truly begin his relationship with Emilia anew.

"Whoa, I'm getting fired up. That settles it, I want to get back to the mansion as fast as possible. Go home! Yankee, go home!"

"Gettin' all worked up's fine and dandy, but let's finish up here first, kay? So, what're we actually gonna do about it—the Witch Cult, I mean."

Ferris narrowed his eyes with a teasing look, chiding Subaru, who was thrashing about, unable to contain his impatience. Subaru choked out a "ngh" and fell silent, and it was Julius who shook his head, resigned. He held up a single finger, drawing everyone's attention to himself.

"Let's do this. We'll split into two groups: one heading for Lord Roswaal's mansion, and the other continuing to search the forest for the Witch Cult. Subaru should be the one to go to the mansion, as both messenger and, more importantly, the party most concerned. There's no guarantee he won't be attacked by the Witch Cult on the way, so Sir Wilhelm along with a few others as escort should suffice. The rest will search the forest, with myself and Ricardo taking the lead. How does that sound?"

Having wrapped things up neatly and quickly, Julius looked to Subaru last, as if for confirmation.

His expression practically said "I saved you the trouble," which stirred up an unnecessary streak of defiance in Subaru, but he swallowed it down with a deep breath.

"Let's go with that. Everyone, please follow Julius's plan."

"Understood."

At Subaru's request, the old swordsman nodded in reply.

That set off a chain of agreement among everyone present, and the matter was settled.

With everyone's will thus unified and the group gathered into one, Julius suddenly raised a hand and, prefacing it with "if I may,"

"I'm not Julius—I'm Yuri, a wandering mercenary. Just don't get that part wrong, please."

"Nobody remembers that setup anymore at this point!!"

—so went the shout, unified in everyone's sentiment, echoing across the forest sky.


* * *


"Oh man. My heart's pounding so hard I think I might throw up."

Patrasche gave a small whinny at Subaru, who leaned against her, muttering weakly. Her attitude seemed faintly displeased, probably because, clever as she was, she could tell that Subaru on her back was genuinely feeling nauseous. Still, the fact that she didn't throw him off was at least some measure of the trust the two of them had built.

"Perhaps the fatigue of riding an Earth Dragon you're not used to has caught up with you all at once, now that you can relax?"

"Maybe that's part of it, but I think it's probably something else. ...Honestly, thinking about facing Emilia-tan head-on, the fear's just exploded out of nowhere."

After all, looking back, they'd parted on the worst possible terms.

For Subaru, that was already more than two weeks in the past by now, and the intense emotions of that moment had largely been processed. But for Emilia, it had only been four days. Unlike Subaru, there was no reason to think she'd undergone any dramatic change of heart since then.

That much he'd already confirmed, in the brief time he'd spent with her during the previous loop. The same went for the terrible mistake Subaru had made back then.

"I don't know the details of what happened, but I believe you parted ways after a quarrel, was it?"

"That's exactly it, and man, it's rough. That's the thing. That's exactly why facing her is so awkward. Ugh... my stomach, my stomach hurts. And my chest hurts too, from lovesickness."

"You seem more pained than when you faced the White Whale. I understand the feeling well enough."

Wilhelm smiled at Subaru, who was trying to distract himself somehow with idle jokes. Subaru, catching something unexpected in his gentle manner, let out an impressed "heh."

"Did you feel that heavy too, Wilhelm-san, whenever you fought with your wife?"

"Of course. In my case, once I angered my wife, I couldn't win physically. I was often forcibly cut down."

"The Sword Saint really is on another level!?"

"After that, I would take her forcibly into my arms and hold her tight until her anger melted away."

"Man, your wife stories really go to some intense places!?"

Warmed up by talk of his revived wife, Wilhelm wore a somehow bright, refreshed expression.

Subaru felt a pang of envy at that unrestrained openness, and slapped his own cheeks hard. Here was his senior, going out of his way to loosen up Subaru's tense heart. To let that consideration go to waste would surely disqualify him as a man.

"You're not seriously, genuinely going to keep pushing wife stories on me going forward, are you?"

"I cannot say I understand what you mean. ...Oh?"

Wilhelm answered Subaru's timid question with an innocent look. Then, quite suddenly, his eyes narrowed as he looked ahead.

Following his gaze, Subaru realized that at some point the road had widened, and they'd entered the proper highway—the road connecting the mansion and the village—now at a point where the village was just coming into view.

He also noticed that, at the end of Wilhelm's gaze, the entrance to that very village could just be glimpsed.

"That's the village closest to the mansion. Well, I guess you'd already know that, Wilhelm-san."

"Yes. I once visited the mansion together with Ferris, and passed through the village as well. ...Something's strange, though."

Wilhelm, still narrow-eyed, had begun to give off a faint tension from his entire body. Stirred to unease by his ominous manner, Subaru felt a bad feeling rising up from deep in his gut.

"W-what's wrong, is something off..."

"There are an unusually large number of dragon carriages stopped in the village. —Master Subaru, please wait here. I'll go take a look."

Having said his piece, Wilhelm gave the same instruction to the subordinates accompanying them.

Then, with a light kick off the ground, his body crossed the road at a speed as if riding the wind, and he slipped into the village in an instant.

And so, about three minutes after Wilhelm had rushed inside—

Subaru's teeth chattered with inward anxiety, wondering if the very disaster he thought he'd avoided had struck again. What flashed through his mind was the gruesome deaths of the villagers he'd witnessed twice already, and the face of the girl reflecting nothing at all—Petra—burned clearly into his memory.

He never wanted to go through that again. And yet, even though he'd worked so hard to avoid it, why on earth—

"Master Subaru."

One of the knights suddenly called out to Subaru, who had shut his eyes and was on the verge of tears. He turned his face that way reflexively, and the knight pointed toward the village, indicating something. Following the line of his finger, Subaru saw Wilhelm waving from the village entrance.

There wasn't a trace of the earlier tension in his figure, and when Subaru hurried over to him, he finally understood why.

Inside the village, a great number of dragon carriages—more than twenty—stood lined up. Naturally, the Earth Dragons pulling them and their drivers had entered the village along with them, all as a set.

As for where this unfamiliar swarm of dragon carriages had come from—

"Oh, right. This is my insurance policy, isn't it."

"So it would seem. You arranged this back when you left the royal capital?"

"Yeah. Asked Anastasia and Russell-san for a bit of a favor."

Subaru gave a wry smile at the sheer spectacle of the assembled carriages, and looked toward the center of the village. There, spread out before him, was what looked like a representative of the carriage group and a representative of the village arguing with each other.

Probably, with no word having reached either side, opinions had split between the two. If the carriage group's arrival matched what Subaru had planned, then getting things to run smoothly would need a bit of time—meaning this was precisely the moment mediation was called for.

"Time out, time out! Okay, eyes over here! I'm sure you've both got plenty to say to each other, but first, hear me out!"

Subaru got down off Patrasche and dashed over, cutting in between the two parties, whose atmosphere was threatening to turn hostile.

The two arguing were a man and a woman, and for the moment he looked up at the man, whose temper had seemed the stronger of the two. Seeing him, Subaru let out a small, involuntary "Ah."

It was a face he recognized. If he remembered right, this was the person who had introduced Otto to Subaru, back during the very first loop.

"Your name is, uh, I forget... but you're the traveling merchant who came here because of the notice from the royal capital's guild, right?"

"That's right... but who are you? You don't look like a merchant."

"I'm more on the employer's side of things, actually. There's been a bit of a mix-up and word hasn't gotten through properly. I'll get it sorted out with both the higher-ups and the folks here."

If the letter he'd requested be sent had reached the mansion, things shouldn't have gotten this confused—but it likely hadn't yet made its way to Emilia.

If she'd known the situation, she'd surely have made the best possible call.

"Well, anyway, stop, please."

Subaru held back the man, who looked ready to keep arguing, and cast a casual glance around at the surroundings. He wondered if perhaps Otto, the merchant he'd had so much contact with, might be mixed in among them somewhere, but a quick sweep of the area turned up no sign of him, leaving Subaru with a small pang of disappointment.

Then he turned around, and this time faced the woman. She, apparently standing as the village's representative, was a person with softly flowing reddish-brown hair and somewhat upturned eyes—

"Anyway, hear me out. For the sake of this unforgettable, endearingly sanpaku-eyed guy standing before you—wait, who are you?"

At Subaru's words, the woman before him knit her brow as if she had no idea what he meant.

But it was her reaction that Subaru couldn't understand. Because—

"I know the face of every single person in this village, and I'm confident they all know mine too. And yet, I don't recognize your face at all... so who exactly are you, standing there acting like the representative?"

Driven by his own impatience, Subaru pressed the question out quickly.

And that was, for Subaru—who had kept himself braced and alert right up until this very moment today—the first and most bitterly regretted slip of the tongue.

—As if suddenly exploding, the shadow of the woman before him swelled up violently.

"—Wha!?"

Subaru was struck speechless at the horribly familiar sight, and by the time he realized it, it was already too late.

The swollen shadow rose into the air, began spreading in a swirling vortex, and unraveled like unwinding threads into countless black demonic hands—which attacked every single person in sight.

The gathered traveling merchants, the villagers watching from a distance, Wilhelm and his subordinates who'd sensed the disturbance around Subaru first and moved to rush over, and even Subaru himself—the only one who'd noticed what was happening right before it happened, yet who froze and let the chance slip by.

Black arms coiled around every single throat, sealing their movements completely.

"This can't be... because, this is..."

Gasping against the crushing force squeezing his throat, Subaru's whole body was pierced through with shock, sweat pouring down his forehead.

This phenomenon, this occurrence, was unmistakably—the very thing he was certain he'd already finished off—

"Ahh... my brain, is, trembling."

Subaru heard a woman before him murmur that quietly.

Eyes wide with shock, Subaru stared at the figure before him.

At a distance close enough that he could have reached out and touched her, the woman, her face lowered, raised a finger to her mouth and bit down brutally, crushing the tip. Blood dripped down, and amid the eerie silence of a world where everyone was held frozen, breath caught, by the sheer act of it, the woman raised her face.

—her mouth stained red with blood, decorated with a ghastly, insane grin.

"I am the Witch Cult's Sin Archbishop..."

Kehkeh, kehkeh.

"—in charge of 'Sloth'—"

Mad laughter, raucous laughter, mocking laughter, echoed and rang out—


"—Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti, that's ME!!"


Subaru heard the woman proudly declare the name of the man he was certain he'd killed.

Kehkehkehkeh, the mad laughter kept ringing out.

Kehkeh, kehkeh.


—Kehkeh, kehkeh, kehkehkehkehkehkeh.
