Arc 6 Chapter 26: "The Stick-Swinging Man"

—A woman. There once was a woman.

The woman had lost her mind. The woman stood at the brink of madness. The woman stood at hunger's utmost extreme.
In a solitary castle she let out groans, gnawed at an empty throne, chipped her teeth, split her mouth, and starved.

The woman who had been born human was, by this time, human no longer.

It all began several years earlier, when the man who ruled this land contracted an incurable illness.
Weakening day by day, fearing the death closing in on him, the man reached for every means he could to prolong his life.
Eventually that reach extended to forbidden arts, and many lives were gathered for the atrocities committed merely to keep him alive a little longer.

Many lives were sacrificed, many lives wasted for nothing, and the woman was one of those sacrifices.
Who she was, what her name had been, where she was born, who her family were—all such memories had burned away to nothing.
The woman was nothing but a tool. A seedbed for the life of a man being eaten away by disease, a man who could do nothing but wait for death.
A life meant only to be spent and wasted—and yet the woman survived.

A body that would not perish, a power that welled up without end, a flame of life left behind by the thing called time.

The wish of the man ravaged by illness, his desperate prayer, was realized in the woman's flesh.
The man rejoiced at the result, and putting off any mourning for his victims, forced the woman's bindings loose.

—When she came to herself, the woman was alone, writhing in hunger within an empty, deserted castle.

Thought was scoured away by a cruelty of hunger, and memory was gnawed white by a violent thirst.
She remembered nothing. She understood nothing. Only that her whole body was bound, and she had no freedom.

She devoured every scrap of food in the castle, vomited up the contents of her stomach, and chewed it all over again, again and again.
In the end, driven by hunger, she gnawed at the stone walls, ground the throne to splinters between her teeth, and tore the carpets to shreds.

At this rate, she would starve to death. In a body that could not die, she would starve to death.

"Grrrl, grrrl. Grrrl, grrrl..."

In hunger so extreme it swept away all sense of self, the woman finally began to see visions.

Within the solitary castle, running about at will, was a pack of dogs cloaked in beast fur.
An hallucination would do. If she could not be filled physically, she wanted at least her heart to be filled.
With that single thought, the woman crawled and sank her teeth into the beasts. She was bitten in turn. She devoured them whole.

She was filled. An instant later, she vomited. She licked up what she'd vomited. Chewed it. Vomited, devoured.

The illusion, the misperception, the delusion, the wish—it had a taste. It had a shape. It rolled upon her tongue.
Before she knew it, the castle had become a den teeming with ghastly beasts.

"Ahaha, ahahaha, ahahahahahahaha—"

She rejoiced. Buried in days of devouring and being devoured, she ate to fill herself, and filled herself only to vomit.
Caught between starvation and refusal to eat, the woman devoured and vomited, sipped and spilled, over and over.

Birthed from the empty void—no, birthed again and again—were beasts with a ghastly presence.

At some point, the woman's bindings, unable to withstand the mutual devouring with the beasts, came loose, and she was free.
Having gained her freedom, the woman crawled out of the castle.
The beasts, too, followed the woman out of the castle, scattering across the land, here and there.

Her hunger, her starvation, never ceasing, the woman left behind the castle she had ravaged and grown weary of devouring.
That someday, a day might come when this hunger and thirst would at last be sated.

—The Witch of Gluttony birthed a horde of Witchbeasts, and continued to starve in an inexhaustible hunger.

—The 'Trial' at the Pleiades Watchtower's second layer, 'Electra,' begins.

The setting: the watchtower's second layer, a domain of chalk-white.
The examiner is a red-haired man standing languidly at the back of the room, grinning like a shark.
A man who called himself the 'Stick-Swinger,' the sword-intent radiating from his entire body was nothing ordinary.
The circumstances of his appearance were themselves telling. Shaula, who was nominally the caretaker of this watchtower, had reportedly fainted dead away at the mere sight of him. He was clearly no ordinary man.
And so—

"Allow me to go all-out from the very first move—!"

"—"

With that declaration, Julius leaned forward and closed the distance a single step.
From his arm, gently tossed toward the man, was the Sword of Selection that had originally stood planted in the floor of this 'Electra.' Spinning end over end, the blade traced an arc and struck the floor at the man's feet, unerring in its aim.
As though inviting the man to simply reach out and pull it free.

"What's this, you? Tossin' a sword at me—what's the big idea, you? You lookin' to die?"

"Unfortunately, striking down an unarmed opponent would be crude beyond measure—a disgrace for any knight!"

"Hah! Don't make me laugh. —I ain't unarmed. Look closer, you."

As Julius closed in, the man bared his fangs in a vicious grin. Without ceremony, he swung up his leg and kicked the sword away with brutal force. The Sword of Selection went flying with a loud clang.
It sailed past, provocatively, right beside Julius as he lunged in—

"—! You'll regret those words!"

His fair and honorable consideration thrown back in his face, Julius's jaw tightened as he drew his knight's sword.
The slender blade shot forward in a straight line, an iron hammer aimed at the insolent man defiling this serious contest.
That thrust, like a bolt of lightning—

"Don't go yappin' so cute, idiot. You got a pretty face on you. Wouldn't want to make you cry and get all excited about it, now would we?"

"Wha—!"

The thrust meant to pierce clean through the man's torso stopped dead, with a sound exactly like a thunderclap.
Naturally, it was not that Julius had eased off. He was, always, giving his full effort within the bounds of what he could do. So it was not he who had stopped the blade—it was the shark-toothed grin facing him.

"Impossible."

"Believe what you see, exactly as you see it. That's the first step, you—the first step."

Laughing cheerfully, the 'Stick-Swinger' scratched at his own chest with his right hand, scritch-scratch. His manner showed no change at all from a moment before—the same attitude as when he'd been spouting nonsense.
And yet his left hand had, with terrifying precision, pinched Julius's thrust to a halt.
And with—

"—A tree branch?"

"Nah, look closer. Your eyes bad, you? Chopsticks. Chopsticks. Good for pickin' at snacks, ain't they, chopsticks. That's why I carry 'em around."

A wooden stick, warped even at a glance—the 'Stick-Swinger' handled it deftly with his left hand, wielding it in exactly the way that made Subaru groan under his breath in recognition. That this world had chopsticks too, Subaru had learned firsthand during their stay in Priestella, but he had never before seen a person use them with such flawless mastery.

—No, no matter how flawlessly mastered, stopping first-rate swordsmanship with a pair of chopsticks was hardly something a human could do.

"Don't make me laugh, you. At the best angle, at the best speed, with just the best feel, swung around in just the best way—there ain't nothin' you can't cut, even with chopsticks."

"Ngh...!"

Faced with a sight that should have been astonishing, the 'Stick-Swinger' pronounced this with a look that seemed one yawn away from boredom. Everyone alike had been struck speechless by the sight—but Julius, the one actually involved, could not afford that luxury.
He put all his strength into his arm, trying to wrench back the knight's sword pinned at its tip between the two chopsticks. But however much force was packed into those slender sticks, the sword would not budge so much as a hair.

"Just so you know, these chopsticks ain't made of anything weird. Bad materials are bad for your skin, after all. Pure and simple, just some wood from around here somewhere."

"Gh...!"

"Don't strain yourself, don't strain yourself... Smile, you. You're prettier when you smile. Not that it matters much on a guy."

Suddenly the sword's restraint loosened, and Julius, his own applied force now with nowhere to go, faltered for the briefest instant. In that fleeting opening, the man spun his body and his bare foot snapped up. It struck with tremendous force, right above Julius's slender waist, and sent him flying.

"Julius—!"

Subaru couldn't tell who had cried out like a scream. He could only stand there, his own careless movement sealed away, and watch as Julius's slender frame was sent flying, light as nothing.
And then, chasing after him as he struggled to control his posture in midair—

"Hah!"

The 'Stick-Swinger's' tall frame leapt, closing on Julius at bullet-like speed. With an impossible feat of physical ability, he caught up directly above Julius, a single chopstick readied in each hand, and unleashed a storm of slashes upon him—if they could even be called slashes—a barrage of violence delivered by chopsticks.

His swinging arms moved with such speed they seemed to vanish from sight. Everyone saw the same thing, and Julius, already helplessly constrained in midair, had no means of evasion.
Relying solely on the knight's sword in his hands, Julius desperately matched his defense to the man's chopstick strikes. But the man's chopsticks slipped through as if mocking him, and struck into Julius, struck, struck, struck, struck, struck—

"Jiwarudo—!!"

A beam of heat shot out, aimed at the two locked in combat in midair.
The white light, true to its simple nature, scoured away the world with terrifying directness. That is to say, it was a blade of heat that incinerated, burned through, and severed whatever lay along its path.
Its straight-line trajectory seemed a weakness, easy to dodge—but the beam crushed that weakness by main force, moving at a speed that put light itself to shame, and struck straight at its prey.
Even the 'Stick-Swinger' would surely have no way to resist a beam of light launched by a third party—

"—My blade cuts light too, you."

Before the boastful voice could even finish, the unleashed chopstick strike split the beam of heat clean down the middle.
It was a sight beyond imagining, an impossible illusion that made everyone's eyes bulge. Only the man himself scoffed as though it were the most natural thing, and continued to toy with Julius using his one remaining free hand.
As if mocking him—no, in truth, mocking him quite magnificently.

"—! Jiwarudooooo—!!"

Eyes bloodshot at that fact, an incantation layered atop another—

It was Anastasia, her lovely face colored with a look of do-or-die resolve, spreading both arms wide as she chanted a spell to unleash beams of heat. From the tips of her spread fingers—ten in all, both hands together—she fired heat beams simultaneously, ten lines of death dancing all at once toward the 'Stick-Swinger.'

—And the 'Stick-Swinger' avoided them by an astonishing method.

"Hah!"

The 'Stick-Swinger' cut away the dancing light with his chopsticks just as before, then in the next instant kicked off against empty air where there should have been nothing to kick off from, and plunged straight downward in a single dive—landing on the white floor with Julius still pinned beneath his body, he set the tip of a chopstick against Julius's solar plexus, and took off running, dragging Julius along the ground as he went.

"Ha-ha-ha-ha-ha! Aim better, you. Flies'll land on you if you keep that up, you. Won't get your pretty boy back like that, you. Ha-ha-ha-ha-ha!"

"Jiwarudo! Jiwarudo! Jiwarudooo—!"

Anastasia kept up her assault against the man as he sprinted along, cackling. But however great the power of her magic, it meant nothing if it never landed.
Beams of heat flew in every direction, but the man evaded them with ease—now cutting them away, now ducking low, now spinning atop Julius's body.

His overwhelming skill and combat prowess threw into stark relief, at the same time, the deficiency in Anastasia's technique.
Though in this case, perhaps it should be called the deficiency not of Anastasia, but of Echidna, who was presently occupying Anastasia's body. Echidna wrung every last drop of mana from herself for Julius's sake in his desperate strait, but for all her determination it came entirely to nothing—not a single attack so much as grazed him.
Before long—

"—Ah, ngh."

"Hah?"

Her time simply ran out first.
The 'Stick-Swinger,' who had been dodging the heat beams with obvious enjoyment, raised an eyebrow at the sudden cessation of the assault. At the end of his gaze, Anastasia's slight frame crumpled and fell.
Blood trickling from her nose was proof of the tremendous strain placed on her body. She herself had once said as much: this trump card couldn't be overused, not without carving away at herself in the process—

"Anastasia-sama—!"

"Whoa!?"

At the sight of his fallen mistress, the knight who had been purely on the defensive found sudden resolve. Julius, who had been dragged along the ground on his back while taking a torrent of chopstick blows across his entire body, finally twisted free of the 'Stick-Swinger's' onslaught.
He'd undone the buttons at his chest and shed the cloak of the Royal Guard, changing the friction against his back just enough to slip free of the man's exquisitely calibrated grip.

Without pause, Julius swung his absurdly long, slender legs around, launching a kick at the man's temple from a prone position on the ground. The man dodged it with the barest tilt of his jaw, but in that opening Julius, in a move like breakdancing, followed up with two more kicks—both dodged as well—all while regaining control of his posture and rising to his feet.

"That there, you—that's just my type. Gets me goin', you."

"I've no time for your nonsense! Step aside—!"

Understanding full well the overwhelming gap in ability between them, Julius roared and charged the man regardless. The knight's sword, never once released despite that hail of blows, sang through the air and struck like a serpent.
Driven forward by righteous fury and a sense of duty, his swordplay was fluid and elegant—perhaps the very pinnacle of swordsmanship attainable by a knight.
So much so that one could scarcely guess how many months and years, how much training, how many days of blood-soaked effort had gone into achieving it.
And yet—

"You playin', you? You just messin' around, you? Don't you dare hold back on me, you."

"—!"

"The hell is this. Don't make me laugh, you. Get serious, you. You doin' this for real, you? If this is you goin' for real... then what a damn letdown, you."

Thrusts halted. Slashes deflected. Combinations shot down. Killing blows turned aside.
Every bit of swordsmanship Julius had built up, everything he had mastered as a knight, was met by nothing more than a bored-sounding sigh from the man calling himself the 'Stick-Swinger,' and by two chopsticks wielded with a terrible, beautiful, vicious grace.
By nothing more than two little sticks, the whole of Julius's 'life so far' was trampled underfoot—

"This ain't it, is it, you. What, you fightin' all alone, you? This ain't your way of fightin'. —That's why it's so damn boring, you."

"I..."

"You wanna run back to your woman, I'll let you go. Borrow her soft lap and cry your eyes out like a spoiled brat. You half-baked, ugly excuse for a swordsman."

Whatever it was that flickered for an instant across Julius's profile—anger, pain, grief, or despair—
whichever it was, no one else could truly know what lay in his heart.

"—"

Julius's blade flashed, the slender knight's sword tracing a silver arc it had repeated countless hundreds of millions of times before.
And yet it was, plain to every onlooker's eye, a strike laced with hesitation.

A chopstick cut in from the side, into that flash of steel.
In the next instant, the whirling stick sliced clean through the middle of the steel blade with ease—with nothing but a light, airy sound, Julius's knight's sword snapped clean in two.
Julius's yellow eyes followed, blank with shock, the tip of his beloved sword as it flew off. What history that sword held, how long he had wielded it—none of that is known.
But surely, what broke in that moment was, for Julius, not the sword alone.

"Sleep."

With that single spat-out word, a terrifying fist buried itself in the side of Julius's face.
It was violence with no connection whatsoever to refinement, the most primitive kind this world has to offer—a blow struck with that most primal of weapons, one's own flesh, dating back to an age before humanity ever took up tools.

"—"

The merciless blow smashed through, distorting Julius's fine-featured profile. Its crushing force reaped away his consciousness in an instant, and his body, like a marionette with its strings cut, was sent flying by pure inertia, tumbled, slid violently across the floor—and came to rest right beside Anastasia.
Mistress and retainer lay side by side, unconscious—as though it were some wildly out-of-place consideration from the beast of a man.

"Now then, next up..."

Cracking his neck as though he had just finished warming up, the man turned to face this way.
In truth, it really had been something like a warm-up. From the moment Julius charged onto the battlefield, through his one-sided mauling, Anastasia's intervening support, and the two of them falling—it had all taken a mere few dozen seconds. Throughout it, Subaru had found not even a sliver of an opening to intervene, and could do nothing but stand there, frozen stiff.
And it was the same for everyone besides Subaru—the rest of the group, Emilia included—

"—Ice Brand Arts, Icicle Line."

"—"

—No. Absolutely not.

As if to prove it, light danced through the white expanse.
It was a wild, pale-blue-gleaming dance of light, particles of ice barely visible to the eye—the ice-and-snow barrier field, "Icicle Line," born of Emilia's absolute mana.

"There's just one thing I want to ask."

Emilia, having spread out mana connected to her own magic power within a limited range to form a kind of barrier, called out to the man standing at its center as he scratched his left eye over his eyepatch.

"Hah? Go on, ask away, hot stuff."

"I'm Emilia. Just Emilia. —Wasn't the idea that we just had to make you take one step? Because you were running around an awful lot."

Introducing herself, Emilia then voiced the obvious question.
Before the fight had begun, the 'Stick-Swinger' had indeed said so, laughing: "Try and make me take even one step." Going by that condition, he had clearly broken it himself.
His fight with Julius had gone well beyond simply darting all over the room.
But at that observation, the man shrugged with a "hey now" and said—

"Don't take it so serious, you. I just said it 'cause it sounded good, you. Happens sometimes, y'know—sayin' somethin' cool without really meanin' nothin' by it. That's what that was, you. You get it? Guess not, huh, bein' a woman and all. Bein' hot stuff and all. C'mon, go out with me tonight, you."

"Sorry, I don't really get what you're saying. Besides, I don't think I could beat you even if we fought."

"E-Emilia...?"

Even as she poured out her mana with all her might, readying herself for battle, Emilia said this with perfect composure. At her words the 'Stick-Swinger's' eyes went wide, and Subaru, frozen stiff until then, forced a sound out of his throat.
At that, Emilia said a quick "sorry" to Subaru, and continued—

"You seem incredibly strong. I could tell just from watching. But we have to get through this 'Trial.' So please, give us a way to win."

"..."

"If we can make you take even one step, we win. Let's settle it that way. ...Is that no good?"

Emilia made her proposal to the man, whose brows had drawn down as he fell silent. At the end, she added that last bit with just a touch of uncertainty, and Subaru could only gape, dumbfounded, at her words.
It was, in every possible way, an offer so absurdly, thoroughly shameless it beggared belief.
At that shameless proposal, the 'Stick-Swinger' fell silent for a moment, and then—

"Hah!"

he let out a short laugh, teeth clicking together, and opened his blue eyes wide to look at Emilia. Then, slowly, he ran his gaze over her entire body as if licking her with his eyes, and said—

"—Not bad. I don't hate it, you. Takes guts to say that much to my face. Biggest fool I've seen since Trishya, you. I like it."

"So, do we pass the 'Trial'?"

"I ain't handin' out that much for free, you! But fine. I already went and acted cool in front of a fine woman. And once you've gone and acted cool, you gotta see it through to the end, or you can't even die properly. —I'll do it your way, you."

"We pass..."

"Make me take even one step and it's your win!"

At Emilia's persistence, the 'Stick-Swinger' raised his voice with a look somewhere between exasperated and disarmed. At that, Emilia nodded, and turned her eyes toward Subaru—

"Please take care of Anastasia and Julius. Get them both some treatment."

"W-wait a second! You saw what just happened, right!? Going in with no plan..."

"It's fine. He doesn't seem to be trying to kill anyone... and I'm going to give it everything I've got, too."

Fully resolved, Emilia shook off Subaru's attempt to stop her and stepped forward. Her profile still composed and dignified, she turned both arms toward the 'Stick-Swinger.'
With the distance kept open, from far away, her magic ought to let her snipe him one-sidedly.

"Got a hot face on you, and shrewd to boot, you."

"Doing everything I possibly can—that's the way my knight taught me!"

Arms crossed thick over his chest, the man simply grinned ferociously, despite standing in an overwhelmingly disadvantageous position.
Aimed straight at that grin, Emilia put strength into her voice—and an instant later, across the field where pale-blue light danced in a wild swirl, the sound of the air cracking chained on and on, and one after another, weapons of ice took shape.

Swords, spears, axes, halberds, arrows—countless weapons, all around.

Ice Brand Arts, Icicle Line—a limited zone of absolute destruction powered by Emilia's vast reserves of mana, the ultimate technique Subaru himself had devised, now activated.

"Hyah, ya!!"

It was a battle cry that sounded almost lackadaisical, but what followed was anything but half-hearted.
The instant Emilia's voice rang out, every weapon with its sharp point already trained on the man lunged at once, from every direction, toward the 'Stick-Swinger.'

A simultaneous assault striking even from blind spots—truly an all-encompassing attack with no way to guard against it.
Against it, the man once more took up a chopstick in each hand, and a rampage of violence—not by countless flashing blades, but by chopsticks—raged wild.

The sight of ice being cut apart, beautifully, unfolded like a work of art.

"—"

Following Emilia's instructions, Subaru dashed over to Julius and Anastasia. Checking on their condition, he was relieved to find they had merely lost consciousness.
Anastasia's state was, most likely, exactly the result of the self-carving Echidna had feared. The nosebleed had already stopped, and while it was hardly a relief, she was simply unconscious. As for Julius, mauled so one-sidedly, the chopstick strikes had certainly battered his body, but the injuries went no further than blunt bruising.
The fact that he had been struck a hundred times over would, with a real blade, have proven he'd been killed a hundred times over—but he had been shown mercy, and had survived.

"Are they both all right? But..."

"I know."

Subaru cut off Beatrice beside him, who had likewise checked on the two of them.
Their safety had been confirmed. But the beast of a man who had done this to them, and Emilia, were at that very moment locked head-on in combat—

"Hah!"

Scattering ice—the man popped a shard into his mouth, crunched it between his teeth, and swung his chopsticks with obvious relish. That single swing sliced clean through both an ice sword and axe that had struck at the same time.
The destroyed ice weapons dissolved instantly into faint light, scattering light around the beautiful savage rampaging so crudely and wildly—an almost fantastical scene, playing out without end.

And yet, even as all this went on—

"—He's not moving."

"Once a man's gone and acted cool, let him see it through to the end, that's just manners, you. If you can't stand your ground on the brink of death, where the hell can you, you?"

Catching Subaru's groaning voice, the 'Stick-Swinger' intercepted the magic while humming a tune. All the while, unlike his upper body, which danced wildly as though in some frenzied performance, his two legs stayed planted heavily on the floor, immovable as a mountain.
In that state, no matter how relentlessly she rained down her magic, nothing would come of it. That was a frustration felt not only by Subaru, watching the battlefield from outside, but by Emilia herself, in the thick of it.
And so, to break through the stalemate, Emilia stepped in—

"—Hyaah, yah!!"

Slender, supple body dancing forward, silver hair streaming behind her, Emilia dove straight in. As she swung both arms up, a great two-handed battle-axe formed in her grip.
Spinning her whole body around in a bold vertical arc, she brought it crashing down at the man from directly overhead.

"Ha-ha!"

Against that blow of the ice battle-axe, the man calmly flicked his chopstick up, interposing it in its path. The axe's straight-down descent was deflected only the barest fraction by the chopstick, but that tiny shift grew larger and larger as it fell toward him, until at last it veered off, grazing the sleeve of his loose kimono, and buried itself in the floor.
A blast wind roared out with the violent impact, and the ice battle-axe, unable to withstand its own force, shattered to splinters from the base up. But in an instant, Emilia let go of the axe, snatched up a halberd launched in from behind as though seizing it, and spun her body to begin her next attack.

"Hyah! Ya! Toryah! Uryah! Uryah-uryah! Uyaah!"

A blow from the halberd, a flash from twin blades, a slash from a longsword, an iaido draw from a katana, the sonic snap of a whip, the crushing power of an axe—every conceivable attack hammered down was, before the man, blocked with ease.

Of course, it wasn't that Emilia's skill was lacking.
Emilia's physical ability was high, and Ice Brand Arts, which combined her enormous mana reserves with her combat prowess, was—if Subaru, its inventor, could say so himself—a fighting style that could confidently be called a full realization of Emilia's capabilities.

"—Subaru."

His hand gripped tight, Beatrice let out a voice full of concern. Regaining a measure of composure from it, Subaru strained his eyes desperately at the battlefield.
He could find no opening to intervene. That was how seamlessly Emilia swapped weapons, drawing on her own strengths, keeping up her own attacks alongside a storm of magical assault all at once. It was, precisely, ground zero for a carpet bombing carried out with every weapon imaginable.
The abnormal thing was that man, standing dead center of that ground zero, handling it all without moving so much as a single step—a monster who called himself nothing more than a 'Stick-Swinger.'

If he carelessly interjected, he risked becoming nothing but a distraction to Emilia.
That worry kept Subaru rooted in place. Beatrice, too, holding his hand and staying silent, had no choice but to endure her own frustration and watch the battlefield unfold.
Was there truly no way to move the situation forward, short of Emilia's stamina running dry?

Into that stalemate came an unexpected change. And it came all of a sudden.

"Hmph! Hyah! Teryah!"

Emilia's two arms seized a pair of blades and struck out from left and right, aiming to catch the man's neck between them. He dodged with ease, simply lowering his head, and the twin blades slipped clean out of Emilia's grasp. But she instantly conjured another pair into her hands and followed up in the manner of opening her crossed arms—this time the man dodged it boldly, leaning back until his body was nearly parallel with the floor.

"Hah!"
"Wha—"

With his knees bent, in a posture nearly lying flat against the floor, the man supported his entire body on the strength of his ankles alone. Emilia, meanwhile, having swung and missed, was thrown off balance by her own momentum and stumbled back a step, arms still spread wide.
It was the first fatally dangerous opening Emilia had shown in this entire fight—though the man had surely had countless chances before, this was, among them, the single worst possible instant.
Into that instant, the man straightened his bent knees and sprang his body upright, restoring his posture. Emilia, still wide open, gritted her back teeth at the sight of him bouncing back.
In that moment, the man bared his teeth more ferociously than he had all fight, leaning forward still wearing his shark's grin, and—

"Opening."

With the chopstick in his hand, he swept it upward from below, stroking Emilia's chest as though scooping it up.

"Wha—"

Emilia wore her usual white outfit, the kind that showed her smooth white skin. He stroked the chopstick against her chest, warping the rich curve there in a lewd way, and the man's crude grin deepened.
Pleased with his obscene act, the man let out a satisfied "Hah!" from his throat, and—

"Perk of the job, perk of the job. Don't go gettin' mad over somethin' this small..."

"Toryah!"

"Guohh—!?"

Emilia clasped both hands together overhead, an ice gauntlet forming around them, and brought it down in a direct hit atop the head of the man wearing his vulgar grin.
The blow carried enough force to shatter the ice itself, a hard impact ringing out, and the man let out a scream of "Guooooh!" clutching his head as he rolled around on the spot.

"Owwwww! Wh-what were you thinkin', you!? Normally a woman'd freeze up if a guy did somethin' like that to her! You didn't hesitate for a second, you!?"

"—? You just touched my body, didn't you? You left yourself wide open."

"The hell!? What kind of upbringin' did you have!? What were your parents even doin'!?"

Rubbing his beaten head, the 'Stick-Swinger,' now sitting cross-legged on the ground, protested loudly. At his cry, Emilia blinked and touched her own chest, where the chopstick had stroked her, saying—

"...Did I say something strange?"

"Hey! Do somethin' about this hot stuff, you! Don't let her walk around outside like this! Small fry! You're her attendant, ain't you!? Get it together, you, straighten up, you, damn that hurts, you, dammit...!"

"I—I don't have to take orders from you about Emilia, of all things. More importantly..."

"Hah!?"

Overwhelmed as he was, Subaru managed to talk back to the raging man's words. Setting aside, for the moment, his anger at the fact that the man had laid a lewd hand on Emilia, he pointed at him.
Then he declared—

"You've moved a lot more than one step."

"—"

"Ah! You're right! Yay, I win!"

At Subaru's observation the man fell silent, and Emilia clapped her hands together and jumped for joy instead.
Around her, in answer to her emotion, icy mana bloomed one after another into flowers, blossoming as if to celebrate Emilia's own victory on her behalf.

The condition the man himself had set—make him take even one step—had been fulfilled.
It was plain to anyone's eyes, and so long as the man didn't try to weasel out of it, it ought to hold.

"So, what'll you do?"

Sorry as he felt toward Emilia, who no doubt was overjoyed at her own victory and the clearing of the 'Trial,' Subaru didn't trust the man in front of him to accept his loss gracefully. Given everything that had happened so far, it was self-evident that expecting good sportsmanship from him was a stretch.
It wouldn't have been the least bit surprising if he flew into a rage over this defeat and, casting off his own restrictions, attacked them all, wiping away the entire course of the 'Trial' as though it had never happened.

It was as that wary thought ran through Subaru's mind, a bead of sweat rolling down his forehead and along his cheek, that—

"Ahh, no helpin' it. What's said is said. Gettin' my legs swept out from under me by my own perverted little urge is nothin' but a joke, but there's no helpin' it, is there."

"Y-you're admitting it...!?"

"What d'you think I am, you? If I dug in my heels here, my stock'd take a hit after I already went and acted cool, wouldn't it, you. Couldn't take that back, could I, you. And if that happened, who's gonna take responsibility, you? No woman'd ever come near me again, you."

"At this point your stock's already lower than it could possibly go, given how badly you just lost..."

"Shut it, small fry! Small fry, hell—more like a damn fry that hasn't even hatched! Don't go squawkin' at me, you. Anyway, that hot stuff there wins. I'll let you through. That was the deal. Can't be helped."

Scratching roughly at his head, the 'Stick-Swinger' openly, unabashedly admitted his own defeat.
Whether that counted as good grace or not was anyone's guess, given how nonsensical his speech was. But once that declaration had been made, Subaru had no intention of pressing the point any further.

Julius and Anastasia had certainly been left injured by their clash with the man, but it was likely within a range that could be sufficiently healed if carried to the green room.
As a second 'Trial,' it hardly seemed to have offered much of a challenge, all things considered—

"—So, is it that fry's turn next? Or one of these two runts?"

"Huh?"

They could head up to the next layer.
Subaru, whose thoughts had just begun to race with that realization, went wide-eyed at the man's next words. Seeing his reaction, the man rose slowly to his feet, and slipped his left shoulder free of his robe, just as his right shoulder already was bare, turning to face them squarely.

—The air itself smelled as though it were burning.

That was the sword-intent surging from the man, on a completely different order of magnitude—a change so dramatic that everything before now felt like mere play by comparison—and it was only belatedly that Subaru understood this was instinct itself sounding the alarm.

"Seven of you in the tower, all told... and the first one through is your woman there."

"—"

"So who's next gonna get past me? —Well, you?"
