Arc 10 Chapter 22: "Groundwork and Rejection"


—This was at the exact same moment Natsuki Subaru was being informed at the "Six Tongues" hideout of the catastrophic peril plaguing the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica—a scene unfolding in the commercial district of the Royal Capital, Lugunica.


"—By whatever means, the enemy has already secured the Dragon History Stone and seized total control. That is the situation currently facing the royal castle. While I have filled in the gaps in information with my own conjectures, I doubt I am far off the mark."

"...Indeed. That would explain why we are being hunted down right now. At the very least, we must assume the royal castle's chain of command has been completely compromised."

"――――"

"Garfiel, are you alright?"

"Huh? Ah, y-yeah, I'm fine... Naah, honestly, it's just a scale I ain't even considered before, y'know...? I don't even know what the hell to say."

Shaking his head side to side, Garfiel confessed his brutally honest feelings. However, Otto sympathized with his younger sworn brother, knowing that such a reaction was only natural.

"...Given the situation, after all."

To be frank, Otto was just as tormented by chaos and confusion. The only difference was that, given his role in their camp, he simply could not afford to let his mind freeze up.
In their current cornered state, Garfiel had fulfilled his duty admirably. —No, not just Garfiel. It was the result of everyone coming together and giving their absolute all.
The only one who had failed to deliver was himself, having pathetically been stripped of his own weapon.

"—Tch."

Leaning his back against the wall of the room, sitting with one knee pulled up, a groan of pain leaked from Garfiel's throat.
The fact that intense exhaustion and pain refused to fade from his face was something unheard of for Garfiel, with his absurd toughness and recovery speed. That alone was proof of how off-the-charts and absurd the strength of the opponent who dealt Garfiel that blow—Reinhard—really was.

—It had been some time since Reinhard stood in Otto and the others' path, and they barely managed to break through by expending every single card in their hand.

Though the battle lasted less than a single minute, by the end of it, everyone was battered and bruised from head to toe. Garfiel in particular, who had engaged Reinhard head-on, was in a terrible state; even though he had intentionally baited the enemy into letting down his guard, he felt wretched that merely having his chin grazed was enough to render him immobile.
The victory they managed to scrape together was only thanks to unexpected reinforcements, and including the fact that they were backed into such a corner in the first place, Otto had far too many mistakes to reflect upon.
Yet, the only reason they were able to hold this makeshift debriefing at all was because the reinforcements who joined them had brought them here, securing a moment of sanctuary.

"—Just shaking that fellow off is an impressive feat. Even if it was a role forced upon him, a hundred ordinary soldiers wouldn't stand a ghost of a chance."

"Even so, to achieve that, we forced someone to play a card they never should have had to play. What she surrendered from her life was immense. I cannot forgive that man."

"...Your indignation is entirely justified, I'm sure. And he is likely prepared to be hated. That is what it means to be the 'Sword Saint.' Even I have never lost my anger toward the 'Sword Saint.'"

"That is..."

Otto narrowed his eyes softly at the figure resting a large palm against his own bald head.
Whether what he intended to follow up with was a question or a guess, he fell silent because he realized he had already voiced something he shouldn't have.
However—

"—You speak not of anger toward Reinhard, but toward my wife, I presume."

Taking the words right out of Otto's mouth as he lapsed into silence, the elderly swordsman who opened the door to the entrance—Wilhelm—said so in a tone filled with certainty.
Entering the room, Wilhelm brought along Rem and a short-haired elderly man by his side. After ushering Rem inside first, he turned back to the man and said,

"Conwood, I am sorry to burden you. You have my gratitude for harboring us."

"Ha, to be thanked so meekly by none other than Wilhelm Trias—the very incarnation of arrogance and selfishness—it might just rain tomorrow."

"You..."

"It's a joke. —Besides, it's only natural. It's only natural, Wilhelm. For me to lend you a hand is something so obvious it doesn't need any thanks."

"――――"

Following his lighthearted tone, the man's voice trembled slightly, causing Wilhelm's cheeks to stiffen.
Amused by his old acquaintance's reaction, the man softened his expression, then looked over at Otto and Garfiel inside the room.

"I owe your companion, Sir Natsuki Subaru, a debt from the Subjugation of the White Whale. A debt so huge it'll take me a lifetime to repay. No one gets through here, so rest easy."

"...Master of the house, you have my thanks."

"I told you, my gratitude is far greater. ...No one believes Sir Natsuki Subaru is Witch Cult. The same goes for Lady Crusch."

With those words, the man patted Wilhelm on the shoulder, then closed the door behind him as he stepped out. Watching until his back was hidden behind the door, Otto spoke to Rem: "Rem-san."
The place they had reached under Wilhelm's guidance was a private room in a tavern offered as a shelter. While Otto and the others were laying out the situation in this room, Rem had been in an adjoining room.
The reason being—

"Are Petra-chan and the others alright?"

"...For now, they are resting. Petra-chan was beside herself, so Meili-chan was the one calming her down... but they've both fallen asleep."

"I see... Understood. Thank you very much."

"No, Rem did nothing. More importantly, how is the discussion?"

"—We have just finished reviewing the current situation, I suppose."

Otto answered Rem, whose face was tense, while directing his light blue eyes toward the back of the room.
Following her gaze, standing—or rather, seated in the private room's booth—was an imposing figure whose head was still positioned higher than Otto or Wilhelm even while sitting.
A bald, aged giant with sun-baked skin—his name was—

"—Barga Cromwell. One of the primary culprits behind the escalation of the kingdom's past civil war."

"...Unfortunate, but I haven't the foggiest who you're talking about. I'm just Old Man Rom, nothing more than a giant old codger who used to live in the slums."

"How outrageously feigned. What of the wound I gouged into your chest? Are you hiding it beneath that grand tuft of white chest hair?"

"I understand you even less now. Not that it's my business, but one truly shouldn't grow old. To think even the famous 'Sword Demon' cannot resist the toll of old age on his mind."

"You—"

"...Please stop fighting, both of you. Petra-chan and the others are resting upstairs. Surely we don't have the time to be squabbling among ourselves, do we?"

Stepping forward between the two bickering men, Rem glared at each of them in turn. Under her stern gaze, Wilhelm bowed his head, and the giant figure—the old giant who called himself Old Man Rom—also cleared his throat and showed a posture of compliance toward Rem.
While grateful for Rem's judgment in stepping in to mediate, Otto could not help but feel a sense of understanding regarding the fierce exchange between the two.

—Otto was also a citizen of this Dragon-Blessed Kingdom of Lugunica.
He knew the fact that Wilhelm van Astrea, the "Sword Demon," was a hero of the Demi-Human War, and that Barga Cromwell was one of the figures who commanded the Demi-Human Alliance during that civil war.
He had never imagined that Barga Cromwell was actually alive.
Of course, Old Man Rom himself denied any connection to Barga and would surely never admit it.
At any rate—

"I will call you Rom-san, then... but I am grateful that you sent Wilhelm-san as reinforcements. Without that, we would have been completely wiped out by now."

"Can't say that's much of an exaggeration. If taken into custody by the castle in our current state, anyone who isn't a Royal Selection candidate or a knight could easily be made to vanish, with whatever excuse they care to make up."

"Easily made to vanish... how could..."

"We must assume the worst possible outcome. Even if we shrink from it and escape into optimism, there's no reason the enemy would go easy on us. ...Especially if Miklotov is dead."

Rem fell speechless at Old Man Rom's grave assessment of the situation, but Otto shared his opinion.
Always assume the worst possibility. In the end, that was what led to finding a way to survive in the jaws of death—though even he considered himself a pessimist.

"Hey, Wilhelm-san. Thanks for earlier."

At that moment, Garfiel, sitting by the wall, raised a hand slightly toward Wilhelm. At the call, Wilhelm turned around with a "Garfiel-dono,"

"I apologize that my skill was lacking in that moment. Unless I caught him off guard by cutting through you as well, I would not have been able to reach Reinhard with my blade."

"Aah, I braced meself and took that hit, so don't sweat it. The cut from your side's pretty much closed up already. ...Though the chop from the 'Sword Saint' is nowhere near done hurtin', damn it."

"――――"

"More importantly, what were you up to, Wilhelm-san? Sounds like our Captain and your Duchess are runnin' off together..."

"Indeed. Allow me to explain what transpired on my end."

So saying, Wilhelm quietly began to relate what had happened to him and his master, Crusch, bringing them up to speed on the situation thus far.

"After Sir Subaru and Lady Emilia visited the manor and saw everyone off, Lady Crusch confined herself to her room. She permitted neither myself nor any of the household staff to approach her, barely even touching her meals."

"That must have been... deeply concerning."

"Yes. Everyone knew this could not continue. Therefore, I left the manor to prepare to ask for assistance from Lady Crusch's father, the former Duke, Lord Meckart Karsten. However, in that brief interval—"

"—Lady Crusch left the mansion alone and headed for Lord Miklotov McMahon's estate."

"Precisely. By the time the news reached my ears, matters were already beyond repair."

With an expression that hid no regret, Wilhelm lamented his own inadequacy.
However, if Crusch had deliberately chosen a moment when Wilhelm was absent, it was merely a matter of sooner or later; there was no reason to lay the blame on him.
Still, given that this was the result, the "Sword Demon's" self-reproach was understandable.

"Naturally, it was unthinkable that Lady Crusch would commit such a reckless act. I immediately went up to the royal castle and appealed to Lord Bordeaux Zergev, an old acquaintance. However, far from listening to me, I ended up being pursued by soldiers on my way back. And there—"

"—I called out to him. Truth be told, it was a bitter choice."

"...Nor was it my intention to cut through the kingdom's soldiers by force to break out. When I saw this man in the light of day, I could scarcely believe my eyes."

"Both of you, please."

An indelible anger and history inevitably poisoned their words.
Leaving the men who were lightly chided by Rem to adjust, Otto understood how they had joined forces—and the circumstances leading up to Wilhelm's arrival.

"In that case, what about you, Rom-san? Your judgment seems to have been remarkably fast and well-prepared."

"I can't blame you for doubting me, but I was only able to move at the last second myself—not much different from you lot. I only noticed because my nose was sharp... Ever since the old days, I've had an instinct for approaching danger. It was rusty for a while, but all the recent chaos seems to have knocked the rust off."

"I see, is that so? —Understood."

As Old Man Rom rolled his massive shoulders and gave his nonchalant answer, Otto did not press any further.
At the end of the day, they were merely facing the same direction for the moment, belonging to opposing camps by position—it was only natural to keep one's cards hidden, and questioning that would be petty.
At the very least, Old Man Rom's actions had saved Otto and the others from a dire crisis. For now, valuing that deed above suspecting his true motives was the right call.

"You're a frightening young lad. Your eyes aren't smiling at all."

"Forgive me. The basics for a merchant are to smile on the outside and smirk on the inside, but lately I haven't been able to act much like a merchant. I'm sick of it myself. —Though, if a good-natured merchant ends up letting his own family die, such a person isn't needed right now anyway."

"――――"

"...Forgive me, my tongue ran away with me."

Shrugging after replying, Otto admonished himself, recognizing that those words were unnecessary.
Thinking it was one thing, but voicing it turned it into a weapon. If you suddenly pulled a weapon while standing face-to-face, you couldn't complain if you were stabbed to pieces in return.
As expected, having his Divine Protection of Soul Language bypassed was lingering far deeper in his mind than he realized. Even though he had constantly drilled into himself—almost like a curse—that relying on his Divine Protection for his identity was dangerous, he had depended on it too heavily recently. This was the bill coming due.

"—In any case, everyone here stands in the same position. Our masters have been unfairly slandered, and the royal castle condones it... no, rather, they are spearheading it. Their goal is to eliminate the Royal Selection candidates, destroy the very rules of the selection itself, and bring the kingdom under their rule."

"And for that purpose, Emilia-san and that person are being spoken ill of... as well as the candidates Wilhelm-san and Rom-san support, right?"

"Lady Emilia, Lady Felt, and the Duchess... then that only leaves Lady Anastasia and the Church's new candidate, Lady Filore!"

"Who knows? If the Council of Wise Men or the participants at the semi-round table hold nothing back, there are countless ways to slander Lady Anastasia as well. Since Lady Anastasia originates from Kararagi, they could claim she is orchestrating a takeover of the Kingdom by the City-States... or perhaps that she conspired with Lady Emilia in the Vollachian Empire to murder Lady Priscilla—"

"—Please do not joke about that!"

Rem raised her voice harshly at Otto as he laid out the vile scenarios he could conjure up.
Her light blue eyes moist and her breathing ragged, she clenched her fists tightly, openly expressing her rage at the hypothetical desecration of Priscilla's death that Otto had posited.

"Desecrating Priscilla-san's death and using it like that—Rem will never allow it! If anyone seriously tries to claim such a thing, Rem will never forgive them...! If that reaches Abel-san's ears, it will mean war with Vollachia!"

"In truth, it isn't much of a joke. Humiliating Lady Emilia—who returned with the monumental achievement of securing a peace treaty with the Empire—is equivalent to smearing mud on the face of the Empire that recognized her. It may sound like a foolish pretext for war, but history is full of far foolish reasons to ignite conflict. Furthermore..."

"—Even if it comes to war, they have Reinhard. Controlling the royal castle means being able to command the 'Sword Saint.' Until now, the authority to issue orders to the 'Sword Saint' was strictly regulated to prevent such an outcome... but the enemy has seized that power."

"Such a thing..."

Though it was merely the absolute worst-case scenario, Old Man Rom did not dismiss Otto's reasoning; rather, he filled in the blanks, paving the road to a living nightmare.
At the sheer terror of it all, Rem and Garfiel—who had just barely survived an exchange with Reinhard at the risk of their lives—were struck speechless.
Only one person—

"—Is it the 'Divine Protection of the Sword Saint' after all?"

The "Sword Demon" merely murmured in a voice nearly imperceptible to anyone else.

"...Judging by the situation, it is glaringly obvious that the enemy intends to attack the Royal Selection itself. In that case, we can safely regard Lady Filore, who recently stepped onto the stage, and the Divine Dragon Church supporting her as hostile forces. Allow me to verify—regarding the princess who disappeared fifteen years ago..."

"—Felt is Felt. There is no other answer."

Disliking the stagnant silence lingering over the discussion, Otto sought to organize the facts, only for Old Man Rom's firm, clear voice to cut him off.
Perhaps thinking that ending on such a blunt denial overlapping the question was overly unkind, Old Man Rom met the surrounding gazes and added, "However,"

"The idea that the Divine Dragon Church was harboring the missing princess strikes me as highly dubious. Even if they kept their distance from the royal castle, keeping such a secret hidden for over a year since the Royal Selection began—especially during a crisis threatening the Kingdom's very existence—is utterly absurd."

Holding a definitive stance alongside an elusive demeanor, Old Man Rom was clearly keeping secrets.
Naturally, the "Sword Demon's" glare toward the old giant grew ever sharper.

"――――"

Back then, fifteen years ago, Wilhelm had been commander of the Royal Guard and was said to have led the search for the missing princess. His desire to learn the truth behind the princess rumors surrounding Felt and Filore was surely stronger than anyone else's.
However, the nerve of a man suspected to be one of the Big Three of the Demi-Human Alliance was hardly fragile enough to spill his guts under the force of that lingering glare.
At any rate—

"...I understand how dire the situation is."

Letting out a soft sigh, Rem straightened her back and forcibly reset her mindset. Looking around at Otto and everyone else in the room in turn, she asked,

"To be honest, Rem is deeply worried about that person who isn't here with us, but Emilia-san and Sister at the castle are in danger, aren't they? How on earth are we going to... no, Felt-san as well. How do we get them out?"

"With Reinhard and the Knight Commander over there, a frontal assault is askin' for too damn much no matter how you look at it. It'd end up like 'Gaddgee and Guaddzeadd's Mountain Seclusion.' We need a clever trick, Otto-bro."

Garfiel, who bore a heavy physical toll from his fight against Reinhard, agreed with Rem's assessment and turned an expectant gaze toward Otto for their next strategy.
For the two of them, worrying about Emilia detained in the castle was natural, but even more agonizing was their concern for Ram, who had accompanied her as an attendant.
To tell the two of them this was truly painful, but—

"—No. From here on, our top priority will be evacuating the Royal Capital. We do not have the luxury to extract Lady Emilia, Ram-san, or Lady Felt from the castle."

""Wha—!?""

Their eyes wide with shock, Garfiel and Rem shouted in unison. Pushing off the wall, Garfiel stood up and roared, "Otto-bro!"

"Escapin'...? What d'you mean, we ain't gonna go save 'em?!"

"It is exactly as I said. Right now, we possess neither the preparation nor the military strength to rescue Lady Emilia and the others held in the castle. Half-baked schemes will simply be crushed by brute force, leaving all of us to fall into enemy hands. We cannot engage in a battle we are destined to lose."

"A battle we're destined to lose...? But Sister and Emilia-san are in there!"

"I know. But even if the enemy has already seized control of the castle, they cannot easily lay hands on a Royal Selection candidate. Even Reinhard-san, obedient as he is, would not stand idly by if such a move were made. Their freedom will be restricted, but as long as they remain inside the castle, there is a high probability Lady Emilia and the others will not be harmed. That is my assessment."

"B-But, still...!"

Otto spoke as calmly as possible, taking care not to let emotion seep into his voice, but Garfiel and Rem, their raw emotions bared, were deeply shaken. However, amidst their back-and-forth, Old Man Rom raised a massive hand and interjected, "May I?"

"I'm of the same mind as the young lad. Even if they stay in the castle, Felt and the others can manage somehow. Frankly, I'm far more terrified of our movements giving those bastards a pretext. The tide's already turned against us over that witchbeast incident. We want to avoid piling any more on top of that."

"Even Old Man Rom agrees with Otto-san... What about you, Wilhelm-san?"

"—I understand Rem-dono's feelings, worrying for her master and her sister. However, recklessly challenging an enemy in full preparation despite knowing the odds are against us is folly... Infuriating as it is, Barga has a point."

Wilhelm slowly shook his head as he answered, causing Rem to bite her lip.
Her frustration was something Otto understood all too painfully. Furthermore, to avoid inviting even greater pushback from her and Garfiel, there was something Otto intentionally chose not to voice.
Even if the Council of Wise Men had fallen into enemy hands, they could not easily harm a Royal Selection candidate. —However, the safety of their attendants and servants was guaranteed by no such thing.
And Ram, who had volunteered to serve as an attendant, had taken that spot precisely because she was prepared for that outcome from the very beginning, refusing to yield the position to either Rem or Petra.

"...Otto-bro, if we're clearin' out of the capital, what about the Captain and the others?"

"—Joining up with them will be difficult. My Divine Protection can't be relied upon right now either. If I carelessly choose the wrong creature to speak with, our internal situation will leak straight to the enemy. We cannot take such a risk."

If at least his Divine Protection of Soul Language were fully functional, he could have utilized overwhelming numbers to search for Subaru and the others, deliver messages, or signal a rendezvous point.
With that option stripped away, searching for Subaru and the rest without a single clue was impossible.

"Barga, I will not press you deeply. But surely someone like you could..."

"You seem to rate a mere old codger from the slums far too highly, but unfortunately I can't live up to those expectations. I'm not puttin' on airs or holding back. The frail thread has already frayed. I've got no prospect of piecing it back together."

"Useless when it matters most... no, I suppose I am no different."

Wilhelm started to hurl a hateful retort out of reflex, but stopped himself in self-reflection. In Old Man Rom's eyes as he glanced back at him, there was a tinge of frustration that suggested his words were no lie.
Indeed, there were no moves left to play. Facing that reality, Rem gripped the hem of her skirt tightly.

"Rem trusts Sister and the others in the castle. Rem believes that if we combine our strength here, we can manage. But that person... he will definitely do something reckless. Rem knows it."

"Rem..."

"No, not just Rem. Garf and Otto-san know it too, don't you? At times like this, he always tries to push far beyond his limits. And because of that, he will surely get hurt terribly again. Even though that's bound to happen..."

To not be by his side. Facing Rem's heartfelt frustration, no one could utter a word.
That was surely because everyone present had interacted with Subaru in some way, had been saved by the very actions Rem described, and had personally witnessed him suffering wounds.

"...We are placing a heavy burden on Beatrice-chan."

Now, Beatrice—the only one likely staying by Subaru's side—was the camp's sole hope to act as a safety net for him. Just as Otto and the others were thinking this, Wilhelm stepped forward with a firm "No."
Standing before the downcast Rem, he gently placed a hand on her shoulder and said,

"It is only natural to worry. However, Sir Subaru is not accompanied by Beatrice-dono alone. Lady Crusch is with him."

"—Though widespread rumors claim the Duchess has lost her mind. Indeed, did she not slip out of the manor behind your back?"

"Silence, Barga. What could someone like you possibly understand about that lady?"

"――――"

"As you say, I may have been fooled. However, Lady Crusch must have acted with some purpose in mind. Until I hear it directly from her own lips, I will pass no judgment."

Not merely spouting angry retorts, Wilhelm infused his voice with unwavering conviction, his blue eyes harboring deep loyalty toward his absent master as he looked around at Rem and everyone in the room.

"Even if her memories were taken, the righteousness of her spirit remains undiminished. It is not merely because I owe her a debt regarding my wife. —I have resolved with my whole soul to place that lady upon the throne."

"Wilhelm-san..."

"Lady Crusch is by his side. Sir Subaru is a man of remarkable caliber. And while Beatrice-dono appears as an innocent child when with Sir Subaru, I understand she is a Great Spirit who has lived for countless years. They will surely shatter our concerns."

Spoken slowly, those words were not meant merely to persuade Rem, but felt like a forceful inscription of his own desire onto the very fabric of the world, demanding it be so.
Though it carried a hint of arrogance, when uttered by the "Sword Demon"—who had once defeated the "Sword Saint" with his own hands and snatched his bride from the jaws of fate—it possessed persuasive force that compelled belief.
An inexplicable power that made one feel that things would indeed turn out that way.
And that was—

"...Sounds like somethin' the Captain would say."

"If so, that is a deeply flattering assessment."

When Garfiel voiced the very sentiment Otto was feeling, Wilhelm nodded deeply. Basking in the "Sword Demon's" passionate appeal up close, Rem softly relaxed her shoulders.
Though anxiety still lingered in her eyes and expression, Rem nonetheless raised her face bravely.

"Forgive me, Rem lost her composure. You are right. If Beatrice-chan and Karsten-san can be relied upon, then one or two of that person's reckless stunts... though Rem wonders if it will really stop at just one or two."

"Just when you seemed ready to agree, getting stuck right there isn't great, you know..."

"Whether it's three or four, Beatrice'll manage it somehow! ...Right now, trustin' that is the best we can do, ain't it?"

Even though his own anxiety had not fully dissipated, Garfiel hid it and ground his fangs together as he spoke, prompting a subtle smile in return from Rem.
Otto felt the same way. No one could move forward while completely wiping away their anxiety in a situation like this. If a disaster rolled around later, they might regret today's choice.
Even so, in order to step over the hesitation of this moment, they had to make their choice.

"Then, once more—we shall evacuate the Royal Capital."

Clearing his throat, Otto looked around at everyone in the room and re-declared their course of action.
The royal castle had already fallen into enemy hands, and they had become wanted fugitives hunted by the capital's troops. A careless move would bring the "Sword Saint" upon them, and capture meant mortal peril—a complete dead end.
Leaving the capital under such conditions would naturally make anyone think they were running away with their tails between their legs.
However, he dared to declare:

"This is by no means a defeat. —It is the groundwork for victory, so that we can strike back next time and win!"

"Heh! Now that's a bloodthirsty threat fit for Otto-bro!"

"I am not satisfied with that evaluation in the slightest, you know!"

Among the nodding faces, Otto responded in a loud voice to Garfiel, who was rubbing his nose with a finger, then nodded toward Rem before turning his mind beyond the wall—toward the Royal Capital.
He did not know where they were. Yet he thought of Subaru and Beatrice, who were surely squirming and struggling in a dead end in this capital, just like Otto and his companions.
Anxiety was endless. As Rem said, Subaru would undoubtedly do something reckless.

—*I don't want him to.* That was Otto's true feeling.

"...Even so, you're going to do something absurd anyway, aren't you?"

Letting his unreachably faint murmur absorb into the wall, Otto pulled his hat back down deep over his eyes.
Otto knew all too painfully that a person like Natsuki Subaru would casually place his own life and mind on the scales if it meant fulfilling someone's hopes or prayers.
That was why, in truth, he did not want to rely on him, nor should he.
Because counting Natsuki Subaru as "the person who will fix everything," just like everyone else did, felt no different than preying upon his kindness and weakness.
So, at the very least.
So that such recklessness would not consume Natsuki Subaru entirely and leave him crushed.
Even from far away, to resist this unreasonable absurdity, they would do what they ought to do—Otto thought with a bitter feeling in his heart.

"Achoo!"

"Did your body catch a chill, I suppose? Are you alright... I suppose?"

"Nah, my nose just started tickling out of nowhere. ...Though my blood definitely ran cold, to be fair."

Sneezing from a sudden itch deep in his nose, Subaru relaxed his mouth toward the worried Beatrice, but unable to even maintain a dry smile, he sighed.
That was how shocking Russell's story had been—the secret machinations of Capella of 'Lust', and the terrible situation the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica was placed in.

"Shape-shifters being a pain in the ass is a trope as old as time, but... this much? No wonder she's been harassing you guys for thirty years, Russell-san."

"Strictly speaking, it was the Kingdom that was being harassed, and we were attempting to stop it. Though given our current predicament, I can hardly speak with any pride."

"I understand that you were holding the line at the water's edge, I suppose. But it doesn't add up, I suppose. Why did that thirty-year tug-of-war suddenly tip so violently, I suppose?"

"That's... probably because of us, I think."

Lowering the corners of her eyes, 'White Sheep' made that self-report while drooping her head.
In front of the dubious Subaru and Beatrice, with guilt and self-reproach dwelling in her eyes, she continued,

"When we left the 'Beloved Children' and joined up with the 'Silver Fox' and the others, there were many occasions where we tripped up their plans. Because of that, I think Mama realized... that the 'Six Tongues' had caught onto her tail."

"Well, if traitors pop up in your own ranks, anyone would figure it out... Or is it like, once someone leaves Capella's sight, she forgets all about them and never thinks of them again?"

"No! Rather, she's the type of person who remembers that forever."

"Thought so! Totally matches my impression of her. Man, she really is a piece of work..."

Having his grasp of Capella's twisted personality reinforced once more, Subaru spat out those words. To supplement the exchange with 'White Sheep,' Russell raised a finger and added,

"As she says, the enemy likely realized that our hands had become well-stocked. When the Witch Cult launched their simultaneous attack on Priestella, 'Lust'—who had previously stayed out of sight—boldly revealed herself, likely aiming to make us aware of her existence."

"Make you aware of her existence? What point is there in doing that..."

"—The 'Six Tongues' have already disposed of seven Capella Emerada Lugunicas."

"Huh?"

"It is a fact. When witness reports matching the physical traits of 'Lust' confirmed in Priestella surfaced, we were compelled to investigate. Naturally, while we were occupied, other schemes utilizing the 'Beloved Children' were put in motion. This situation is my failure for having too much of my attention consumed by dealing with those."

"...I suppose there is no need to ask, but those seven were fakes, I suppose?"

"Yes. Needlessly to say."

Russell explained with flat emotionlessness that they had been outmaneuvered, but Subaru could hardly imagine remaining so calm without flying into a rage if he had been in Russell's shoes.
To think that even that outrage in Priestella, which caused so many tragedies, was nothing more than groundwork for Capella to toy with the Six Tongues and seize control of the kingdom later on.
After breaking and twisting the lives of so many people.

"――っ"

"...Subaru, I understand how you feel, but calm down, I suppose. You are only hurting yourself... I suppose."

"Eh? Ah, sorry. I just..."

Gnashes his teeth, Subaru lowered his head, and Beatrice, sitting in his cross-legged lap, gently tapped her back against his chest. Her hand reached out to softly touch his right arm, where Subaru had unconsciously been digging his nails in.
Looking down, the handkerchief wrapped around the burn he suffered at the McMahon mansion had been removed and replaced with clean bandages. He had almost dug his left nails in over top of those bandages. As he started to apologize for his unconscious movement, he suddenly realized—the pain from the burn was completely gone.

"Did Russell-san do this... wait, was it you, Hakuyo-chan?"

"Eh? Ah, no. That was 'Blue Snake'-san..."

"'Blue Snake'?"

"—One of my subordinates. A highly capable healer. Pardon my presumption, but I had him perform a full treatment while you were asleep. Provided there are no lingering discomforts..."

"Ah, yeah, none at all. It feels so fine it almost feels weird."

Flexing his wrist lightly and shaking his arm, it felt like he was in absolute top shape.
Even considering Beatrice's mana shortage had limited her to basic first aid, to be able to heal a burn that severe meant Russell's subordinates were quite a talented bunch.
If there were injuries, Crusch's and Rachins's must have been healed as well.
Though—

"—Old man."

With a hand over his mouth, Rachins was lost in deep thought, in no state to care about that.
If it was true that Capella's demonic reach had extended to the royal castle, replacing the high nobility starting with the Council of Wise Men, then Rachins's father was likely among them.
Although Rachins voiced defiance toward his father, the unexpected intelligence glimpsed from him here and there clearly possessed a backbone rooted in his noble origin.
And that backbone was surely not unrelated to the father in question.

"Rachins, you good?"

"...I just got stripped down to my waist. Like I give a damn."

"—I see. Got it."

Knowing full well the intent behind the question, Rachins gave that answer, and Subaru respected it.
Feeling anxious was only natural. And Subaru currently had no means to resolve those fears, nor did Rachins wish to spill his thoughts.
Worrying about family was a heavy burden. From experience, talking about it didn't guarantee relief, so he couldn't force it out of him. Of course, if Rachins wanted to speak, Subaru was ready to listen—

"—Hey, hey! Are you done with these gloomy, pain-in-the-ass talks yet?"

As if shattering the delicate atmosphere, the infirmary door slammed open violently.
Stamping inside with brazen footsteps was the dangerous figure clad in black with a jester mask—'Black Dog', holding a heavy bag in one hand.
Since Subaru and the others were safe, Subaru assumed he'd be fine too, but—

"You really love interrupting, don't you...? We're right in the middle of something important."

"Well, well, aren't you living a busy life? Good for you! Though since you're desperately clinging to life anyway, you'd probably claim there isn't a single instant or second where you aren't talking about something important!"

"'Black Dog', stop it! Natsuki Subaru-san is our benefactor!"

"Oh, look at you playing the good girl! A wicked woman whose specialty is making others wag their tails for her, yet you can't wag yours enough—my head isn't bowing to you, my ass is! Kuhahaha!"

"～～っ"

Reprimanding 'Black Dog' for his rude attitude, 'White Sheep' flushed red at his counterattack.
It seemed that despite both having escaped from the 'Beloved Children,' these two didn't possess a tight-knit relationship of mutual trust like Subaru and Beatrice.
Naturally, Subaru felt inclined to side with 'White Sheep', but—

"You chatter far too much, 'Black Dog.' Keep your business brief."

"Tch. The preparations for moving are almost ready, so you lot better get yourselves set too!"

"Wha—I suppose!"
"Whoa, what the hell!?"

Speaking roughly, 'Black Dog' suddenly hurled the large bag he held straight at Subaru and Beatrice. Pulling back in reflex, the bag landed on the bed right at their feet.
Eagerly untying the bag, 'White Sheep' revealed its contents—

"—Robes infused with a 'perception-blocking' effect. I obtained them through Roswaal. There are sets for Sir Natsuki, Beatrice-dono, and Lady Crusch. Ah, and a spare for Rachins-dono as well."

"Thanks for treating me like an afterthought!"

While Rachins spat out his complaint at the casual treatment, Subaru raised an eyebrow at the familiar term "perception-blocking" for the first time in a while, spreading the retrieved robe across Beatrice's lap.
It was a white fabric. Simply touching it yielded nothing to Subaru, but Beatrice gave a nod with an "Mm,"

"Indeed, there is no doubt Roswaal's hand was involved in making this, I suppose. Now the story about that bearded fellow being Roswaal's friend carries far more weight, I suppose."

"You say that like it's bad news... Still, these robes are a lifesaver for wanted fugitives like us, but did you say we're moving? Isn't this place a Six Tongues base?"

"No, this is merely one of several small holding areas. It lacks the capability to counter the current strength of the 'Beloved Children'... We shall vacate promptly."

"But that's... no, you guys understand how terrifying the enemy is way better than we do. We're just along for the ride anyway, so my bad. We'll follow your lead."

Just when he thought they had finally settled down, being told they were moving again made him reflexively want to push back, but Subaru quickly pulled his objection.
The terror and meticulousness of the enemy had been explained more than thoroughly enough. If they hadn't joined up with Russell and his group, they would undoubtedly still be running from an invisible foe right now.
So Subaru decided to defer to Russell's judgment for now.
However—

"So, are we moving right away? I mean, is the next spot far from here?"

"Indeed. It will take some time. —After all, we will be leaving the Royal Capital."

"...Eh?"

Unable to properly digest the explanation striking his eardrums, Subaru's eyes went wide.
Hoping he had misheard, Subaru waited for Russell to utter different words, but in vain—Russell's expression remained entirely unchanged.
Naturally, Subaru had no choice but to take time swallowing the reality of those words—

"—I cannot easily agree to that, I suppose. From the sound of your words, Emilia in the castle is being left behind, I suppose?"

"Yes. At the risk of being misunderstood, we cannot spare resources to rendezvous with the other members of your camp fleeing through the capital either. We are already inside the palm of our enemy's hand. Unless we move before that hand clenches completely shut, the situation will become beyond repair."

"That's... but..."

"Allow me to restate: we will abandon this base and evacuate the Royal Capital. As of now, this city is a devil's den ruled by the 'Beloved Children.' —We cannot afford defeat."

—Exuding fiery resolve and pride, Russell rejected defeat, leaving Subaru utterly speechless.

"――――"

Subaru's understanding was naive. Shallow. Insufficient.
The situation was far worse than Subaru had imagined; they were on the brink of defeat—placed on an execution platform without realizing it, with the moment of beheading drawing near. That was what this meant.
And yet—

"Subaru, Subaru, look at Betty, I suppose. Betty is right here, I suppose."

Turning around, Beatrice embraced Subaru, who was trembling uncontrollably, from the front. Feeling her small palm stroking his back, Subaru remembered how to breathe, an action he had forgotten.

"I get what Russell-san is saying. I get it, okay? But leaving Emilia..."

Leaving the Royal Capital fallen into enemy hands, rejecting defeat, and creating an opportunity to rise again.
He understood the intent. He understood the logic. The capital right now held nothing but danger, and unless they made a drastic move, they would fall right into the enemy's trap. —Yet, were they going to leave Emilia behind in a place like that? Give up on regrouping with their separated friends and run away?
A choice like that... Natsuki Subaru—

"—'White Sheep.'"

"...N-Yes."

Before Subaru, who remained locked in a hug with Beatrice and lost in thought, Russell suddenly called out to 'White Sheep.' At the call, 'White Sheep' choked back her breath slightly and nodded.
She then stepped up to the side of the bed and softly took Subaru's hands in both of hers.

"'White Sheep'-chan...?"

"I understand how you feel so much it hurts. The decision to part ways with someone precious to you is painfully tearing yourself apart. But... that doesn't mean we should just die together, right?"

"――――"

"Please trust us. With Duke Karsten's strength and Natsuki Subaru-san with us, we won't lose. Please...!"

Desperately, her eyes trembling as if she were on the verge of tears, 'White Sheep' tried to persuade Subaru.
It was an ideal, a platitude—nothing more than a convenient conclusion Subaru had repeated to convince himself ten, a hundred times over. —Yet somehow, hearing it from 'White Sheep' moved his heart.

"—Russell-san, what about Emilia and the others at the castle?"

"The other side will likely refrain from laying a finger on a Royal Selection candidate as well. Though their freedom will be restricted, their physical safety will not be compromised. I have collaborators keeping watch."

Inhaling deeply and exhaling, Subaru asked, and Russell answered.
In that response, which felt infused with a desire to dispel Subaru's anxiety as much as possible, lay perhaps the maximum consideration and concession Russell could offer.
Emilia's safety left to the enemy's good judgment, their friends' safety left to their own strength—given who they were up against, he couldn't deny it felt like a bet with terrible odds.
Even so—

"—I will definitely save Emilia. All of them, too. Remember that."

Even as he felt his whole body being scorched by the black flame boiling deep within his chest, he forged his resolve.
At Subaru's answer, Beatrice stiffened her posture, while 'White Sheep', who was still holding his hands, let out a faint sigh. Watching in silence, Rachins caught Russell's gaze and stuck out his tongue, clearly discontent but indicating he wouldn't object.
Only 'Black Dog', resting his back against the wall with his hand on his worn mask—

"—Kuh, drop dead and croak, you piece of shit."

Spitting out that bitter murmur to no one in particular, it fell onto the medicine-scented floor of the room and vanished.
