Arc 5 Chapter 58: "――I Believe"

*Due to a comment in the feedback section, what was originally a single chapter has been split into two.
Arc 5 Chapters 57 and 58 were originally the same chapter — sorry for the inconvenience.*

"Pfft—"

It came right after Emilia pressed a hand to her chest and stated it flatly.
A laugh burst out, one he couldn't hold back. At first it was faint, like breath being let out, but it steadily grew, swelling until it could no longer be contained, and finally broke into full-throated laughter.

"Hahaha! Ah-hahaha! Pfft, ahahahahaha!"

Arching his back, Regulus roared with laughter as if he'd just heard the most delightful joke imaginable. He plunged his hands into his white hair and clawed at it, the madman writhing with a laughing fit that no one else could share in.
In that blatant, performative display, Subaru saw the confirmation of Emilia's guess.

"You bastard, what the hell's so funny!?"

"Of course it's funny!? If anything, you two are the ones who should be laughing — laughing in resignation at just how truly cornered you are! Hey, do you even get it? You've been strangling your own necks with your own hands, round and round!"

"Guh—"

Subaru's throat seized up.
Just this once, Regulus's counterargument was so flawlessly reasonable there was no room to talk back.

Subaru turned to Emilia, silently asking whether her guess had been right.
But to his pleading look, Emilia only shook her head.

"There's no mistake. I had the minor spirits check, and I can feel it myself too. There's something inside me that isn't me — something extra. It feels really, really awful."

Emilia's declaration was also, in effect, the presentation of a hopeless truth.
The effect of the "Lion's Heart" had transferred to Emilia. Which meant that to sever its power, there was no way left but to stop the beating of Emilia's own heart.

"In the first place, why Emilia's heart...? Did I misread how the 'Small King's' power works? That his heart can be entrusted to anyone he likes, so long as..."

If that really was the nature of the ability, then there would be no gap in Regulus's Authority at all. If Regulus could entrust his heart even to a total stranger, then as long as a human being was alive, there would be no way to kill him.
Or — if it could be transferred to any living thing that possessed a heart—

"You have no shame."

"Isn't the howl of a loser just delightful. Ha-ha, say whatever you like. Spouting sour grapes to your heart's content — that's the loser's privilege. And savoring it with a sense of superiority is the winner's privilege — mine. Ahh, not bad at all! Not bad at all!"

"You were the one who said I wasn't fit to be your wife."

"So noisy. Going on and on, so full of yourself about your 'privileges.' More importantly, how are you going to take responsibility for killing my wives? My ideal brides... do you have any idea how many years it took me to gather them all? A man of my age with not a single wife, not a single lover — are you trying to turn me into some pathetic widower? You have an obligation to serve as my stopgap until I find a new wife!"

To Emilia's sharp, contemptuous words, Regulus brandished logic that was pure garbage in return.
The lunatic's belief in his own outrageous argument seemed to affirm his own presence squatting inside Emilia's heart. If that were so, then the possibility of it moving anywhere but Emilia's heart—

"Want to try it and see? Whether there's anywhere else the heart can move to?"

"—"

"It's simple to test. Just kill the girl standing in front of you right now. Snuff out her breath, and you'll naturally find out whether my Authority hits a dead end. So very, very simple, and thoroughly rational... ahaha! But you can't do it, can you? If you did that, you'd lose the whole point of challenging me in the first place, the whole significance, every last bit of self-serving justification for it!"

Infuriating as it was, Regulus was right.
Subaru had no courage to sacrifice Emilia. Call it ego, call it selfishness, he had no choice but to accept the accusation — but he simply could not do it.

To bring Regulus down, his brides had thrown away their own lives.
Subaru could steel himself to accept those sacrifices as unavoidable, but he could not go so far as to wager Emilia's life, or the lives of his other companions.

Natsuki Subaru's choices were always, sickeningly, self-centered.

"See? Look at that. He can't do it at all. Then why don't you do it yourself instead? It's easy. Just do exactly what you did to my other brides. Or what, can't do that either? You can steal other people's lives without a second thought, but your own precious life you just can't bring yourself to sacrifice? Amazing. Makes you sick, doesn't it?"

"—Subaru."

"Wait, no. Don't. Seriously, not that. Anything but that."

At Regulus's provocation, Emilia called Subaru's name in a voice that carried a strange note of resolve. The sheer mercilessness in that tone frightened him, and he stopped her.
It wasn't that she'd taken the bait, nor that she'd given up in desperation.

But if there truly were no other way to win, Emilia was prepared, at the very worst, to choose that option.
And Subaru's will was set on nothing but refusing to let her make that choice. To let her make it would be to lose.
He called Emilia's name, made her stop — and then had nothing more to say.

"Well then, shall we wrap this up? Dragging around a distasteful woman like you isn't really my taste, but I'll compromise for now. Consider it a stopgap until I find my next wife. Though I'll be killing that boy over there. He's trampled all over my rights this much... ah, right. Speaking of which, isn't this funny?"

Before Subaru, teeth grinding, Regulus's lips curled with amusement.
Mana swirled around Emilia, her resolve on the verge of being carried out. In the midst of that, Regulus, showing no concern at all, sneered.

"You, that's right, aren't you? The one who went around shouting all sorts of things across the whole city before the wedding? Something about killing a Sin Archbishop... what a joke, right? If you actually thought killing that defective reject meant you could beat me, well, my condolences. That guy couldn't accomplish a single thing properly, not before he became a Sin Archbishop, not after — he was a useless waste of space the whole time."

Regulus roared with laughter. There was no mistaking who his words pointed to — even for Subaru, the loathsome madman Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti.
Petelgeuse was the lowest kind of fanatic, beyond any defense. There was no way to feel any goodwill toward that madman; Subaru's grudge against him ran bone-deep, and he was a monstrosity who deserved to die.

And yet, even so, hearing Petelgeuse — who was, in some twisted sense, a fellow Sin Archbishop — being mocked and belittled brought a deeply primal displeasure welling up in Subaru's heart.
All the more so given the extremity of the moment, with Emilia's very life hanging on the possibility of defeating Regulus.
And in the first place, Petelgeuse was—

"—Ah."

The hateful madman, that blood-soaked cackle rising up in his mind and echoing there — the instant it did, Subaru lifted his head. He clutched at his own chest as if clawing at it, and caught his breath.
Could it be. Could such a thing... be possible?

"Can I... do it?"

He didn't know.
Strictly speaking, the possibility that had flashed through Subaru's mind was guaranteed by no one — an armchair theory, no, closer to a product of Subaru's own delusion. A realization that belonged to him and him alone.
But precisely because of that. Precisely because of that, Subaru was the only one who could even guess at the possibility.

The idea had come to him just now; the basis for it was pure intuition; whether it would even work, not even the gods could say — but even so,

"Emilia."

"—"

Feeling the effect of the mana swelling to its utmost against his skin, Subaru called her name.
Emilia said nothing, her expression carrying a resolve that looked almost tragic. But deep in her eyes, a sudden emotion flickered past. It was expectation, and trust, directed at Subaru looking back at her.
As if pushed forward by that feeling, Subaru asked.

"Emilia."

"Mm."

"—Trust me. Leave everything to me. Can you do that?"

"Mm."

To the question wrung out of him, the answer came simple and without hesitation.
Emilia pressed a hand to her own chest, and for the first time since she'd returned, her cheek moved — she smiled.

"I believe you'll pull it off, Subaru. I believe in you too."

Ah, damn it. What an unfair thing to say.

To be handed that much unreserved trust by the girl he loved — how could he possibly fail?
Whether he had to claw at it or sink his teeth in and hang on, he had to make it succeed.

Subaru drew a deep breath, and let it out.
Then he glanced sideways at Regulus, who was watching in silence. Regulus made no move to interrupt their exchange, standing with arms crossed, waiting with an air of leisure.

"Confident, huh?"

"Confident, sure."

There wasn't a shred of a chance he could lose.
Regulus had laid bare the entire trick behind his ability and had them completely sealed off. In truth, the Authority of his "Lion's Heart" was flawless. Even after unraveling the mechanism, Subaru hadn't expected victory to be placed this far out of reach.

But it was precisely because he was certain of his imminent victory that Regulus could watch with such detached amusement, wondering what futile struggle Subaru and Emilia might try.
He had no idea what was about to happen. And in that, Subaru was exactly the same.

"—"

If Beatrice were here, maybe there would have been some other way. If that clever girl were at his side, would she have offered up some plan with far better odds?
Deep in his chest, there was still a connection to the girl who was his partner. No doubt he'd get a magnificent scolding once everything was settled — and he'd have to accept it, deserve it even.

So for now, alone, he called to mind what it had been like when he really had been alone — summoning up the memory that lived in his chest, one that was never something to be glad of, the primal landscape of fear and agony.

"Subaru."

"—"

"Go for it."

Emilia's call gave his resolve the final push it needed.
Subaru gripped roughly at his own chest, and focused his mind on the pitch-black power swirling inside him — a power that hardly felt like it belonged to him at all — and released it.

Just this once, right now, let him properly name it.
So that even the madman who'd hurled insults at another madman would understand what was about to happen — just this once.
This power was something he'd inherited from that hateful lunatic.

"Come on... Unseen Hand—!!"

—Invisible Providence. Or, "the Unseen Hand."

Subaru defined this power swirling inside him as a witch's power stemming from the Witch Factor. As Echidna had told him in the dream world, it was the Witch Factor he'd been made to inherit from the madman upon killing Petelgeuse — what drawbacks it carried, he had no idea. But the power that granted Subaru his invisible magic hand undoubtedly traced back to that source.

Which was why Subaru had never once, until now, sought the origin of his power anywhere but the Witch Factor.

The reason the ability took on a nature so similar to that madman's was because the "Sloth" Witch Factor he'd inherited simply had that kind of trait to begin with. That was surely how it was.
The possibility that Petelgeuse might still be drawing breath somewhere inside him — that was something he didn't even want to consider.

—But if that were so, then what was this sensation?

Swirling, stirring with color, a voiceless cheer rose up inside Subaru.
A cheer for having been called forth. A cheer for having gained power once again. A cheer for being needed, and for being able to answer that need. And beyond all that, an inexplicable elation that couldn't be accounted for by any of it.

This overwhelming sense of bliss, of exhilaration, of gratitude, that came with the power's release.
This baffling storm of emotion was, without a doubt, not something that could be chalked up to Subaru alone—


"Hah!?"

At Subaru's ringing shout, Regulus let out a strangled cry of shock.

There was no way he should be able to see it. This was a magic hand that could not be seen.
An invisible poison hand capable of certain, silent death — nothing more than the power of a defective reject, one that Regulus had scorned as a useless waste, diluted down into an even more defective reject.

One in number, extremely short in reach, its potential entirely unknown.
As a key to breaking open this deadlock, it was woefully underqualified for the role.

"—"

The first stage — the activation of the magic hand — had been cleared. From here, he had to push forward into the unknown second stage, and the final, third stage.
Subaru moved his fingers according to his own intent, pouring his wish into the magic hand woven, it seemed, out of shadow itself.

"Emilia!"

Once more, he asked whether her resolve still held. Seeking, from her, the push he needed to go on.
At that voice, Emilia closed her eyes, then nodded as if she understood.

"Oh. So that's where you were, Juice."

Understanding and affection filled her eyes as Emilia spread her arms.
As if she'd grasped Subaru's intent and seen through to what was about to happen, she laid open the shortest path to her own heart. Subaru, without hesitation, sent the magic hand through it.

"—"

The unseen magic hand slid into the center of Emilia's chest. The instant its fingers slipped through her pale skin, Emilia's shoulders gave a faint jolt, as though she'd felt something.
But the hand did not stop. It passed through her breastbone, threaded between her lungs, and reached the very source of her heartbeat.

—The magic hand had arrived at Emilia's heart.

The second stage was complete.
When Subaru touched upon forbidden territory, the witch's hand would pass through his own body to wound his heart in retaliation — this was an application of that same effect. Whether the Unseen Hand and the witch's hand were of the same nature had been nothing but a gamble.
But the gamble had held, this far. The problem now was the last stage — a power with no basis to rely on at all.

If all he wanted was to simply crush Emilia's heart, he could do that this very instant. But there would be no meaning in that at all. This power wasn't meant for something like that.
Then what was it meant for? — Right now, in this moment, it was a power meant to save.

"—"

Was it even possible — in that fraction-of-a-second world, Subaru held his breath.
Was the Unseen Hand a hand capable of saving someone? Under the madman Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti, how many lives had been stolen by this very power?
They say power is only as good as how you use it, but there were plenty of powers whose use was, in practice, fixed and limited. Wasn't the Unseen Hand, too, nothing more than a power built for destruction?

That this power had been born for the sake of keeping someone alive — that seemed altogether—

"Subaru."

In that instant of hesitation and wavering, he heard Emilia's voice, a voice he shouldn't have been able to hear.

"It's okay. — I believe in the two of you."

Who, and who else, did she mean?
Emilia's trust was directed at Subaru, and at some other person Subaru didn't know.
And yet, he found himself believing it, astonishingly easily.

—That this hand would surely never bring harm to Emilia.


"Ohhhh! Roar, my third hand!!"

The doubt he could not fully believe, about the power within him, cleared away.
Where this power's origin lay no longer mattered. What mattered was that this power was here now, in Subaru's grasp; that Subaru had no intention of hurting Emilia; and that maybe — just maybe — there was something more to the power itself as well.

Inside Emilia's chest, the magic hand woven of shadow closed its fingers.
As the fingertips caught upon Emilia's beating heart, a sensation like a sweet scratch across its surface made her gasp softly. A touch where ticklishness came before pain.
In the chest of a blushing Emilia, the magic hand, fingers closed, took hold of something for certain.

Something entirely apart from the pulse that kept Emilia alive — the far too "Small Lion's Heart."

"Got you—!"

There was no room to spare for drawing it out.
The heart that had gone on brazenly beating inside Emilia — Subaru's magic hand crushed it.

Without leaving so much as a scratch on Emilia's own heart, it crushed the parasitic, harmful organ that had lodged there singing of love.
In a third hand that did not exist, Subaru felt the certain sensation of it. And then—

"Guhah!"

An unprecedented degree of concentration, and the penalty of using a power that wasn't truly his own.
A pain like his innards being wrenched and torn, and a sense of loss like he himself were being defiled, tore through him, and Subaru dropped to his knees on the spot. He coughed violently, coughing up blood.

"Subaru!"

Kneeling on the water-soaked ground, blood trickling from the corner of his mouth, Subaru reached for the hand Emilia extended toward him. He caught the outstretched hand and pressed it to his own cheek.

"Ah..."

"You're alive, right?"

"...Yeah, I'm fine. My heart's still beating away inside me, right where it should be."

As Subaru confirmed the warmth of blood in the hand he held, Emilia used her free hand to check her own pulse. It beat on, without a doubt — a heartbeat that seemed to celebrate the simple fact of being here, now.
And gazing at the two of them, only Regulus wore an expression that had gone entirely past comprehension.

"Huh? What, what is this? You two just went and understood each other and left everyone else behind, is that it? What kind of cheap melodrama is this? What happened, what exactly did you two—"

"...You still haven't noticed?"

"Hah? What are you even talking about. Noticed, not noticed, nothing's changed one bit—"

"Look at your feet. You're soaked."

"—?"

To Regulus, who seemed ready to give himself over to a fit of temper, Subaru pointed and told him. Regulus, looking down at his own feet with suspicion, fell silent for a moment, then his eyes went wide.
He'd noticed — his white tuxedo, its white shoes and hem, utterly soaked through by the water pooling at his feet.

"You bastards—!?"

Realizing the change far, far too late, Regulus bared his teeth and raised an arm to strike. But before he could, a long, pale leg swept up and delivered a magnificent kick straight to the side of his face.
Caught completely off guard, Regulus took the blow directly and was slammed down into the water-soaked ground with a groan of pain. Half his body soaked further in the water, a clear shoeprint left on the cheek that had taken the kick.

"Guh, buh... th-this, this can't—"

As if unable to believe it, Regulus lifted his face in a daze. Looking down at him, Emilia, having just landed that beautifully executed kick, tilted her head slightly.

"There. Finally connected."

"You, you—!"

At Emilia's brief exclamation of accomplishment, Regulus's face reddened with fury. Scooping up water as he surged to his feet, his hand hurled a scattershot blast of water at Emilia.
But whether it was the pain of the kick throwing off his stance, the water bolts flew wide of their mark, and instead struck his own wide-open torso, which had left itself completely defenseless—

"Ice Brand Arts!"

"Gobh!"

The ice mallet that took shape in Emilia's hands drove straight into the center of Regulus's body.
Taking a full swing that seemed like it would crack bone, the madman's body tumbled through the water. Coughing, slamming a fist against the water again and again, Regulus glared at Subaru and Emilia with bloodshot eyes.

"Why! Why why why why! How, how could you two, you two of all people, how, what, why, the Authority of 'Greed'! My rights—!?"

"If you watched all that and still don't get the answer, explaining it to you would be a complete waste of time. Well, whatever. Simple version, then."

Watching the ranting, screeching Regulus with something like pity, Subaru bit back the screaming of his own insides and grinned.
A grin every bit as vicious as Petelgeuse's had ever been.

"You got blindsided by your own enemy while you were out here sandbagging."

"—!"

Even if the exact meaning of the words was lost on him, the intent to mock came through loud and clear.
Regulus let out a wordless scream and, ignoring Emilia, braced to unleash an attack on Subaru. But Emilia beat him to it.

"The first attack — from the brides — seems like it was a dud, so make sure this one actually connects."

"Don't screw with me—!"

An overwhelming number of icicles formed above Regulus's head.
Each varied in size, but if even a fraction struck home, death was all but certain. Emilia's loathing for Regulus had reached a point that even her gentle nature could no longer forgive.

Leaping up, Regulus flung sprays of water against the falling icicles. Shattered icicles rained down, but the shards, broken small and fine, weren't finished serving their purpose even then.
Wave after wave of ice bullets fired out in a storm, and Regulus, drenched head to toe, ran through the water screaming curses too foul to bear listening to.

White ice crystals gave rise to mist, and the flooded cityscape froze solid. Even Subaru, kneeling in a puddle, found a film of ice forming around him, and hastily peeled his hands off the water.
Even with Emilia holding back for Subaru's sake, this was the collateral damage around him. Naturally, the damage inflicted on Regulus, the actual target, must have been on an entirely different scale.
And yet—

"...Unharmed?"

In the frozen scene left behind once the barrage of ice ceased, Regulus stood there, still very much alive.
Hands on his knees, breathing raggedly, soaked from head to toe — and yet he had avoided the end that should have come from being skewered through.

"Wheeze, wheeze, ah, haah...!"

Clawing at his chest, Regulus looked on the verge of collapse.
Seeing that, Subaru understood. The invincibility effect granted by the "Lion's Heart" remained usable even with the heart lodged inside his own body. Except—

"To make yourself invincible, you have to stop your own time — which means stopping the heart inside you too. Guess that means your invincibility comes with a hard time limit."

"Guh...!"

Whether the remark had struck a nerve, Regulus's face twisted with fury as he fought back the pain in his chest. If there was a time limit, then given enough sheer volume of attacks, Emilia would eventually get through.
And once that happened, Regulus would be nothing more than a mook who'd dumped every point into offense.

"H-hey, um... don't you think that's cowardly!?"

At Subaru, coolly analyzing the balance of power between them, Regulus jabbed a finger. He then pointed at Emilia too, glaring back and forth between the two of them.

"Doesn't it bother you at all, the two of you ganging up to torment one person? Isn't something important in you as a human being seriously messed up? Don't you have any doubts about yourselves? You should, obviously!?"

"...You really are something else."

With the very mouth that, moments ago while holding the advantage from the "Lion's Heart," had said whatever he pleased, Regulus now, having lost that advantage, demanded fairness from his opponent on the grounds of his own disadvantage.
Subaru was well past exasperation, and had arrived somewhere close to respect. A being this utterly devoid of any human appeal — surely none like it had existed before, nor would exist again.

"So basically, that's your thing? Two against one is unfair, so let's fight one-on-one, fair and square. That's the way a fight is supposed to be, is that what you're saying?"

"Exactly! I'm just asking for the obvious to be done in the obvious way, aren't I? Do you even know who I— who I am! I am the Witch Cult's Sin Archbishop, in charge of 'Greed' — Regulus Corneas!? The most fulfilled, most unshakable being in this entire world, and—"

Voice trembling, Regulus looked down at his own two hands.
Subaru had no words left for him. So in his place, Emilia spoke.

"Everything you say changes in an instant, and there's nothing behind any of it. I think you're the most pathetic person in the whole world."

"—! Don't mess with me! I'll make you regret ever making a mockery of me... of 'Greed'!"

Even his fury at being scorned rang shallow, and Regulus kept spitting the same abuse over and over.
Watching his hopeless display, Subaru felt something like relief settle over him. Regulus truly knew no way to win except from a position of overwhelming advantage.

If he could still use the "Lion's Heart," even briefly, there were any number of paths to victory left open to him.
And yet the moment things got the least bit difficult, he threw in the towel without even bothering to look over the whole board.

"Coast through life without ever really trying, and you're bound to trip up somewhere you never expected."

"Hah...?"

"Nothing, just talking to myself. Anyway — I'll take you up on that one-on-one, if you want it."

"—! That's right. That's how it should be. Of course, your knight isn't going to go and put his own master out in front first, is he?"

Latching onto the convenient offer, Regulus tried to wring out even more favorable terms.
Between Subaru and Emilia, there was no comparison as to who had the higher combat ability. Kill Subaru first, throw Emilia off balance, and maybe there'd be a path to victory after all. Twisting his empty head for an unnecessary bit of cunning — as far as results went, it was at least rational.
But if he thought he could beat Subaru with such petty, small-minded scheming, he was a hundred thousand years too early.

Because finding a way to win on a board that looked like a dead loss — that was exactly Subaru's way of fighting.
In their very approach to a contest, Regulus and Subaru stood at opposite poles.

"Sure. It's only right that a knight does the fighting."

"Then—"

"So — sorry to make you wait again, but I'll leave the last part to you."

Standing with his feet still soaking in the puddle, Subaru said it as easily as an exhaled breath.
At those words, Regulus cocked his head. "Huh?" But Subaru's words hadn't been meant for him at all. They were meant for *him*.

"Ah, understood. — A one-on-one duel has been offered; as a knight, I shall accept it."

The one who answered was flame itself.
Across the flooded streets, without so much as a ripple disturbing the water, the young man came walking. Not the counterfeit mystique of Regulus, but the power of a Divine Protection granted to one beloved by the heavens.

"Of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica's Royal Guard, of the lineage of the 'Sword Saint' — Reinhard van Astrea."

Stepping out in front of Subaru and Emilia, the knight who gave his name pointed his still-sheathed sword at Regulus. A posture that offered a fair and honest duel, name given for name in return.
The kind of righteous, universally recognized challenge that even that "Bowel Hunter," Elsa, had once accepted.
In response, Regulus scrambled to his feet, thrusting both hands out before him—

"W-wait! This, this is, this is wrong, isn't it!?"

For the one who defiled a sacred duel, who denied the very nature of what it meant to be a warrior, the "Sword Saint" showed no mercy.

An upward slash entered from beneath Regulus's legs, splitting his body vertically in a single flash — and Regulus, without even the chance to scream, was sent hurtling far, far up into the sky above.

"—————!!"

Up into the high sky, high enough to look down upon the whole panorama of the water-drowned capital he himself had destroyed.
A cry, neither scream nor curse, echoed on and on, fading into the distance.
