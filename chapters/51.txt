Arc 2 Chapter 27: "The Fang That Struggles to Live"

His throat trembles. His soul screams.
Flesh is torn open, nerves are ravaged, and a sharp, maddening agony pounds against his brain.

"GYAAAAAAAAAH——!!"

Everything in front of him goes bright red; he can't grasp reality at all.
A black beast has sunk its teeth into his right thigh. The thought of tearing it off doesn't come before the reflexive scream of pain does.

——It hurts, it hurts, it hurts, it hurts, it hurts, it hurts.

His mind is filled entirely with that, leaving no room to even consider fighting back.
His sense of balance abruptly collapses. The beast, fangs still buried in his flesh, wrenches its head, and with a body weight easily double Subaru's, drags him down to the ground.

"Ugoah——"

Carried by the momentum, he half-spins and is slammed face-down into the earth. Failing even to break his fall, his face crashes into the dirt — the crunching sensation of his nose flattening, the taste of soil in his mouth.
Pain asserts itself from above and below, and as he spits out dirt, a low groan keeps rising from Subaru's throat. Then, only now, he notices the fangs have slipped free of his right leg, and he frantically flips himself over.

"This is bad……!"

He rolls onto his back and forces his upper body up.
His right leg is bleeding profusely, the flesh beneath the torn cloth peeled back and raw. The pain of a wound like it was gouged out with a knife surges back, but more urgent than that is getting to his feet — except there's no time.

The jet-black shadow, having pinned its prey's movement, is already moving in for the follow-up, too fast to track.
Bounding left and right across the ground as it closes in, all Subaru can manage is to shield his upper body with both arms.

A single flash of raised, razor-sharp claws rips his right arm open in one stroke, from the back of the hand all the way to the shoulder. Blood sprays out; pain cheers at the fresh wound, bringing Subaru's brain to a boil with a chorus of agony.

Before tearing out his throat, it means to crush his limbs first and strip him of any power to resist.
Experiencing firsthand the cruelty of a wild hunt, Subaru shudders and screams. The wordless noise does nothing to affect the horned beast's movements. As if going through familiar, practiced work, it sinks its teeth into the left wrist of the still-screaming Subaru, stealing the third of his four limbs from him.

The sensation of fangs punching straight through his left wrist.
Beyond pain now — nothing but a trigger for more screaming. His vision flickers, thought itself barely functions, and yet what still drives Subaru forward is nothing but a reflexive survival instinct.

"AAAAAAAH——!! GODDAMN IT ALLLLL!!"

Screaming, with everything he has, he slams the beast — along with his own bitten left wrist — down against the ground. Its jaws aren't weak enough for that to shake the fangs loose. Even as it stains its black fur with blood and drool, the horned beast presses its fangs in deeper still, as if mocking Subaru's resistance.

Into the side of its face —

"Don't get — cocky!!"

The quill pen he'd never let go of drives itself deep into the beast's left eye.

Its eyeball crushed, its insides churned — now it's the horned beast's turn to howl.
Unexpected retaliation from what it had marked as helpless prey sends the creature thrashing wildly, its wild instincts fighting back. But Subaru has judged that if he lets this chance pass, there won't be another.
Right arm still driving the quill in, left arm still clamped in its jaws — he throws his whole body over it, refusing to let it escape.

"It hurts it hurts it hurts it hurts it hurts——"

His right leg, its nerves ruined, can't take his weight; his left wrist dangles by a single strip of skin; and deep inside the peeled-open wound on his right arm, he can see bone.
Blood seems to be pouring endlessly from his battered nose, and if he's unlucky, he thinks he might be missing two or three front teeth as well.

Even in that state, he pours everything he has into escaping the crisis before him.
There is pain. There is suffering. There is fear. He is alive.
Both he and this beast — both of them still draw breath.

"Agah——"

A claw swung blindly grazes his forehead, and the jolt to his brain loosens his grip. In that instant, the horned beast shoves Subaru off and puts distance between them, the quill pen still lodged in its left eye.

A single leap carries it several meters away, and the jet-black shadow melts into the forest and vanishes.
Slumped on the ground, Subaru forgets even his pain as he searches for the shape that disappeared.

He can't afford the optimism of thinking it might retreat over its injury.
A wounded beast is dangerous — that much he's heard often enough. And to the beast, Subaru is just as much a wounded opponent.
For both of them, the other is something that must be finished off, here and now.

"——Damn it."

He hauls himself up, his unsteady right leg bent at the knee, relying only on his left leg to rise. Both arms are a wreck — especially the left, which he can't even bring himself to look at without feeling his resolve crack.
A pounding sensation hammers at the side of his head as if being struck again and again. He forces the ringing alarm bells of pain temporarily out of conscious thought, and Subaru's gaze glares into the forest where the beast vanished.

It's coming. Definitely.
That baseless certainty was perhaps the fruit of one of the few things Subaru had actually gained in this world — the experience, not inconsiderable, of having faced down murderous intent before.

"————!!"

Whose was that breath-held scream — Subaru's, or his opponent's?
All he knows for certain is this: right before his braced, waiting eyes, a mouth lined with row upon row of sharp fangs opens wide, and the horned beast comes hurtling at him like a bullet.

Subaru's whole body feels the force of a creature over thirty kilograms charging at full speed with no restraint whatsoever. And this time, with the added condition that he can't rely on the divine protection of having both legs to brace himself.

On sheer reflex his right arm comes up, and the horned beast's bite is knocked aside, deflected from directly beneath its jaw. But there's no way to fully kill the destructive force of the charge, and Subaru's body goes rolling across the ground, tangled together with the beast from the impact.

"OOOOOOAAAAAH——"

Flung backward, his whole body slamming hard again and again, Subaru flails his arms in a desperate frenzy. At some point he feels coarse fur under his hand, grips it with all his strength, and clings on without letting go, not even knowing what part of the creature it is.

Rolling across the ground, still tangled together, he and the beast plunge onto a slope. Far from stopping, they gain speed as they head down the incline, and then, at last —

"——No way."

Suddenly, the hard ground that had been battering his whole body is gone.
An instant later, a floating sensation rushes in, and Subaru realizes: he's just gone off a cliff.

"————!!"

Whose wordless scream was it this time — Subaru's, or the beast's?
As it turned out, the scream didn't last very long.

The floating sensation cuts off after only about two seconds, and once again a shock slams into his whole body, his bones creaking with the impact. A dull crack sounds, and he thinks something has actually broken. But his body's priorities are elsewhere.
He extends his left arm — numb past the wrist — and claws at the ground to try to kill the momentum of the tumble. The nails of his five splayed fingers are torn away against the rock face, spraying fresh blood in a pattern like a spray-painted stain across the cliff wall.

Only by doing that much does the momentum finally slacken, and Subaru's body barely stops again at the edge of a cliff. Stopped with half his body hanging out over open air, he hurriedly rolls himself toward solid ground, and —

"Bwuhah…… haah…… ah, ah, ah, damn it!"

He pinches his nose and forcefully expels the blood still flowing, not yet spent.
Unable to even tell whether the liquid pooling in his mouth is drool, blood, or stomach acid, he spits it all out onto the ground along with a curse.

Breathing hard, his face twisted in pain from the lacerations and bruises screaming from every part of his body, Subaru then notices that the beast that fell with him is nowhere to be seen.

"Hey, where did……!?"

This isn't the moment to feel lucky about surviving the fall.
If it attacks him again while he's still at the cliff's edge like this, his fate this time will be sealed for good. Bracing for the sight of the horned beast leaping nimbly back to threaten him, Subaru shifts his stance and scans his surroundings.
And then, he catches sight of that black shape almost immediately.

"……I see. So you couldn't handle it either."

The horned beast lies below him, at the base of the cliff.
Unlike Subaru, it hadn't been able to avoid a second fall. Unable to withstand a drop from more than double the height of the first, the beast — which seemed to have landed head-first — lies sprawled on its side atop a pool of blood, not twitching in the slightest.

——It seems, somehow, Subaru has once again managed to scrape through with his life.

"Damn it all…… what a stupid…… mess……"

Perhaps because he watched that life come to its end, and relief slipped into the crack it left in his heart.
Subaru, too, bleeding more than he should be able to bear, collapses onto the ground and, unable to resist the wave of sleepiness bearing down on him, closes his eyes.

And just like that, without another thought, his consciousness fades — and goes out.
