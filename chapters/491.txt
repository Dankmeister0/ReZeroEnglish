Arc 6 Chapter 84: "Heave-Ho! Heave-Ho!"

—To shape ice exactly as one intends, what matters most is solidifying the 'image.'

When Subaru had first proposed 'Ice Blade Arts' to her, Emilia had thought it sounded very useful, but she'd been anxious about whether she could actually pull it off.

"It's fine! No need to worry! Emilia-tan can definitely do it!"

She remembers how Subaru, flashing a smile, gave her a thumbs-up when she was worried like that.
Thinking back on it now, that encouragement had been groundless — no, in Subaru's case, it was probably backed by the reason "because I like her," and that was what had pushed Emilia forward.

In the process of learning to create weapons through 'imaging,' Emilia had studied drawing a great deal.
Unlike singing, drawing wasn't something she was especially good at, but even so, after drawing picture after picture together with Subaru, she'd made real progress.

Alongside her studies, watching Emilia draw with Subaru and Beatrice, Ram had looked exasperated, Otto had given a wry smile, Frederica and Petra had joined in from time to time, and Garfiel had offered advice on how to do it better. Roswaal, too, had occasionally watched from a distance as Emilia and Beatrice sat side by side, drawing.

A memory Emilia held to be very, very precious — one too dear to let go of.
A memory that might have vanished from everyone else, but one Subaru still remembered. Just thinking of it made a warm, glowing feeling well up in the depths of her chest.

"I'll turn that into courage — with a great heave — !"

Fueled by the feelings welling up inside her, Emilia challenged the 'Divine Dragon' Volcanica head-on, wielding the ice weapon she'd created and accompanied by seven ice soldiers who advanced alongside her.

As explained before, 'imaging' is essential to shaping ice.
That held true not only for weapons but for the ice soldiers she created as well. In the end, the ice soldiers had taken shape in a form Emilia found easiest to 'image.'
In short, the ones fighting alongside Emilia were seven Natsuki Subarus.

"But they're stronger and quicker than the real Subaru!"

Subaru himself was no slouch when it came to mischief and energy, but the ice soldiers were fundamentally different in construction.
The soldiers' strength depended on the density of mana Emilia poured into them, putting them in a different league from simple ice statues. Like her ice weapons, they likely wouldn't lose out even to steel.

"Go!"

Following Emilia's command, the ice soldier at the head of the group entered Volcanica's range.
Volcanica remained motionless, leaning against the great central pillar of the Watchtower's first floor. Judging from the tail strike from before, its reach was somewhere around six or seven meters —

In that instant, as though sensing the intent to reach the pillar, the dragon's blue tail moved at tremendous speed.
A bizarre sound rang out, one that could only be described as a hole punched through the wind, and an instant later the lead ice soldier's upper body shattered into countless fragments. The blow struck around the chest, splitting the soldier's torso clean in two, and its head — with its Subaru-like, ill-tempered eyes — went flying off.

"Sorry! But that means it'll go for anyone, not just me."

She felt bad for the ice soldier that had been made into a touchstone, but this made Volcanica's targeting clear.
The 'Divine Dragon,' senile as it was and having even forgotten about the 'Trial,' nonetheless still retained a clear will to intercept anyone who approached the pillar.
And that will made no distinction for whether the target was alive or not. If so, there was a way to work with this.

"Soldiers, if you please!"

In response to Emilia's voice, the ice soldiers surged forward, overtaking the silver-haired girl.
With the first one destroyed, the six remaining ice soldiers scattered in different directions, closing in on the central pillar with slight gaps in timing between them.

'—Thou who hast come to reach the tower's summit. One who treads the first floor, all-powerful petitioner.'

Sensing the approach of the ice soldiers, the 'Divine Dragon' again repeated its proclamation.
Watching it recite what had become nothing more than a meaningless string of words at puppets that couldn't hear a thing, Emilia felt a deep loneliness, an anguish that clawed at her chest.
Even reduced to such a state, Volcanica was still trying to protect *something*.
She had no idea with whom, or what, or for what purpose that promise had been made, but—

'—I, Volcanica. By the ancient covenant, I shall question the resolve of the one who would reach the summit.'

The tail lashed out, and two ice soldiers who had readied their weapons were shattered in an instant.
Both had taken defensive stances by reflex, but one lost everything below the waist, and the other had its right half blown away, collapsing without being able to do anything more.

So instead, the remaining four moved to pin down the tail as it tried to return from its attack to its resting position.
At present, Volcanica's only means of interception seemed limited to its tail strike. Perhaps it wasn't just senile — maybe its legs had grown feeble with age too.
If getting up was too much trouble for it, then all she needed to do was stop that tail.

"Hold steady!"

Aiming to halt the tail's movement, three ice soldiers lined up side by side, arms linked at the shoulder.
Aiming to sweep them all away at once, the tip of the 'Divine Dragon's' tail once again outran sound itself. In principle it worked something like the whip Subaru wielded, but its speed and power were on a completely different order.
Emilia could catch Subaru's whip with her bare hand, but a dragon's tail strike was another matter entirely.

The 'Divine Dragon' unleashed power equal to most creatures' all-out effort with the casual ease of swatting away a bug.
That sweeping tail struck the three ice soldiers braced side by side all at once. And yet the violent crash of destruction that should have followed never came.
That was because a wall of ice had risen up behind the three braced soldiers, supporting them.

If you know it's coming, there's a way to deal with it.
Even as cracks spread across its whole body, the corner of the ill-eyed, boy-like ice soldier's mouth curled upward. And from behind the three who had absorbed the blow, the last soldier — assigned a separate task — leapt into action.

What the last ice soldier brandished was a weapon known as a *sasumata*.
At the tip of its long, spear-like shaft was a curved metal fitting meant not to stab an opponent but to pin them down — apparently it's classified as a restraining tool.

With that sasumata, it drove down and pinned the 'Divine Dragon's' unruly tail to the floor.
Even at its thinnest tip, the now-stilled dragon tail was as thick as a log — impossible to hold down with a single sasumata — so the three cracked soldiers joined in one after another, each striking with sasumata of their own.
And with a total of four points pinned by sasumata, even the mighty 'Divine Dragon' had its tail sealed, or so it seemed—

'—Thou who hast come to reach the tower's summit. One who treads the first floor, all-powerful petitioner.'

An instant later, a sound like the world itself being scraped away rang out, and the four ice soldiers, still slapping each other's hands in a high-five, were gouged away wholesale.
Everything from the waist up vanished cleanly, leaving only the lower halves of the ice soldiers to collapse. What had done this was not a serious counterattack from the tail whose movement had been sealed — but claws.

Volcanica, lying low against the floor, had swung its left foreleg instead of its tail.
With just that, Emilia's ice soldiers had been forced into instant annihilation.
She'd nearly been fooled into thinking it was feeble.

"If its tail and legs are all fine and dandy... then honestly! Why is it just its head that's gone all blank!"

'—I, Volcanica. By the ancient covenant, I shall question the resolve of the one who would reach the summit.'

"I already got that, so— !"

A repeated refrain that might have driven anyone but Emilia into a fury.
But even hearing it over and over, Emilia's bold fighting spirit didn't break. What had been broken and shattered were only the ice soldiers — though of course, her heart ached a little.

"It's fine! It's not like I learned nothing from that!"

As if to insist that the fallen ice soldiers hadn't died in vain, Emilia herself advanced into the tail's range.
The ice sasumata pinning down the tail, however, were still intact — the sacrifice of those four ice soldiers had been worth it. Naturally, then, the foreleg would come after Emilia in place of the tail—

"The reach of your forelegs is shorter than your tail, isn't it? And another thing—"

Before those claws could catch Emilia, ice spears shot past her, closing in on the 'Divine Dragon.'
They came from behind Emilia — ice soldiers freshly recreated at their starting positions. Emilia could make at most seven ice soldiers at once, but she could remake any of them each time they were destroyed.
In other words, the ice soldiers could rise again and again, as many times as it took, for as long as Emilia's strength held out.
Just like the real Natsuki Subaru would.

'—Thou who hast come to reach the tower's summit. One who treads the first floor, all-powerful petitioner.'

"Kyaa!?"

Against the shower of ice spears raining down from outside its range, Volcanica slammed its foreleg down straight ahead.
The blow tore through space itself, as if leaving claw marks that would remain forever, generating a shockwave that threatened to swallow Emilia, the ice soldiers, and the whole first floor in a raging gale.

Her forward momentum halted by the shockwave, Emilia narrowed her violet eyes and stared hard.
The central pillar Volcanica guarded looked sturdy and hard, and she still hadn't been able to examine it closely. From a distance it looked no different from the other six pillars, but that judgment was premature too.
Still, what Emilia was really after here wasn't the central pillar itself, but—

"—what's above it! The very top of the tower!!"

Refusing to lose to the wind, Emilia pressed forward and kicked hard off the floor.
She was still far too distant to take any real action against either Volcanica or the pillar. But launching off right here was the correct move.
Because the ice soldier regenerating from its first destruction had crouched low, both hands readied.
And it caught Emilia's advancing foot in both hands, boosting her into a single explosive leap straight upward.

Pin the tail, draw its attention to the pillar, and in that opening, seize the true target: the topmost floor.
It was a roundabout tactic Emilia rarely resorted to, but her instincts, now that she'd reached this place, insisted that the very top of the tower was where she had to go.

With the great leap aided by the ice soldier, she cleared Volcanica's head in one bound and caught hold of the upper part of the pillar. From there, if she could climb straight up to the topmost floor—

"—Eh?"

An instant later, as Emilia reached to get her fingers on the pillar, a silent wave of heat grazed past just beneath her feet.
—No, it hadn't been silent. The sheer force and heat had been so overwhelming that sound itself had died. If the very concept of sound had been killed, it was no wonder the phenomenon played out in silence.

Emilia's senses caught the disappearance of the ice soldiers deployed across the first floor.
The one that had boosted her leap, the four that had been throwing ice spears in support, the two that had been running to add more sasumata — all of them vanished at once.
And what had done this was neither Volcanica's tail nor its foreleg.

'—I, Volcanica. By the ancient covenant, I shall question the resolve of the one who would reach the summit.'

From that mouth, which never once lost its solemn dignity, came the dying scream of scorched air.
The repeated refrain reaching her ears again was what told Emilia that the murdered concept of sound had come back to life. At the same moment, her fingers finally hooked onto the upper pillar.
Somehow managing to fix her body in place through sheer desperation, Emilia looked down below.

The first floor spread out beneath her, scorched pure white.
White smoke smoldered up from spots all across it, and the ice soldiers that should have been there hadn't even left wreckage behind. Such was the heat, such was the power, such was the annihilating force—

—The 'Divine Dragon' Volcanica's breath had burned everything away.

"Your tail's fine, your legs are fine, and you can even breathe fire!"

'—Thou who hast come to reach the tower's summit. One who treads the first floor, all-powerful petitioner.'

"I get it! I understand! I get it, so... ah."

Her reply came out far less desperate than what had just happened warranted, but partway through, Emilia's eyes went wide.
Slowly, the 'Divine Dragon's' eyes turned upward, its golden gaze reflecting Emilia.
And then—

'—I, Volcanica. By the ancient covenant, I shall question the resolve of the one who would reach the summit.'

Saying so, Volcanica spread its two blue wings and rose to its feet right where it stood.

"This is bad— !"

Grasping the danger of what was happening below, Emilia scrambled to reach for the upper part of the tower in a panic.
Even as she did, the rising Volcanica yanked its sasumata-pinned tail free and, unhurried, began to beat its wings.

It meant to take flight.
Being the 'Divine Dragon' Volcanica, of course it could fly — and yet, Emilia had never seen a dragon fly before, so it hardly felt real.
To begin with, could a creature that enormous really fly through the sky?

"I've only ever seen Puck or Roswaal flying, so..."

Puck being a Spirit, and Roswaal being a mage eccentric enough to rank among the greats, their ability to fly made perfect sense.
From what she'd heard, the Vollachian Empire to the south had flying dragons called wyverns, alongside things like Earth Dragons and Water Dragons — maybe Volcanica belonged in that same category as those wyverns.
Or perhaps it was simply a mistake to lump together dragons and 'Dragons' as the same thing—

"Hup! Heave! Hyah! Ta!"

Calling out with each effort, Emilia scrambled up the tower at the greatest speed she could manage.
It was a speed that would have left any onlooker gaping in disbelief, but even with Emilia's absurd physical prowess, it wasn't quite enough to pull ahead and escape clean.

'—Thou who hast come to reach the tower's summit. One who treads the first floor, all-powerful petitioner.'

It wasn't her imagination that the voice sounded closer than before.
After all, the pronouncement no longer came from far below, left behind — it came from the same height she now occupied.

—Its two blue wings spread wide, that unbelievably massive body danced through the air.
The 'Divine Dragon' Volcanica floated above the desert sky, majestic, its undiminished presence on full display.

The pressure it radiated, the piercing glare that could freeze one in place, its tail, its foreleg, right down to its breath — everything about it lived up to its status as the legendary, top-class Dragon of myth.
The one thing that betrayed that image, however, was—

"Me! I'm planning to go above the first floor, so maybe I'm not even your enemy!"

'—I, Volcanica. By the ancient covenant, I shall question the resolve of the one who would reach the summit.'

As if refusing to listen at all, its voice talked right over her, and Volcanica's fury bore down on Emilia as she clung to the pillar.
Catching her breath, Emilia clenched her back teeth hard and, borrowing the power of 'the foothold that never existed,' leapt clean over the slamming tail.

"Nyauh!!"

An application of 'Ice Blade Arts' — rather than clinging to the pillar and climbing hand over hand, attaching footholds to its side and running straight up let her move faster.

"That frees up my hands and feet too... kyan!"

The twin ice swords she'd drawn on reflex knocked the slamming tail aside with a tremendous jolt.
That one swing, thrown on a wild guess, left both of Emilia's arms numb from wrist to elbow — it would be a few seconds before she could grip a new weapon again.

Yet unlike the damage Emilia had taken, Volcanica had merely swung its tail.
Mercilessly, the 'Divine Dragon' kept pace at the same altitude as its challenger and swung its tail again, moving to knock down the reckless opponent trying to conquer the pillar — no, to crush her outright.

"—!"

Emilia's breath caught at the sight of the tail bearing down on her.
If she got knocked away once more, she didn't think she'd have the strength left to grab the pillar again. If that jagged-scaled tail hit her dead on, she'd surely end up a mess all over.
She couldn't afford to take that tail's attack.

"I've still got... so much more in me!!"

Her hands were useless. But her legs could still move. She could still aim for the top.
She'd made a foothold to aim upward, but that alone wasn't enough. There was a limit to how much ice she could craft at once. She couldn't afford to waste that process.
Something that would serve as a foothold, and more besides—

"—Got it!"

'—Thou who hast come to reach the tower's summit. One who treads the first floor, all-powerful petitioner.'

Toward Emilia as the idea struck her, the tail bore down once again.
She dodged it by borrowing the power of an ordinary foothold. Then she brought the new idea to life on the pillar, and with her outstretched hand, gripped tight onto that 'hand.'

"Please, soldier!"

Answering Emilia's voice, the one creaking its ice-made arm was a Subaru-lookalike ice soldier — burned away and then remade — though only its upper body.
Not merely a foothold, but something functioning both as her footing and as a dependable ally — that was the 'road of ice soldiers,' upper bodies sprouting one after another from the pillar.

Since it only meant sprouting upper bodies from the pillar, she didn't need to make legs, letting her exceed the usual limit of seven.
She sprouted about ten of them onto the pillar all at once, and with their hands to help her, Emilia climbed up, and up.

"Hup! Ta! Yah! Heave!"

Literally lending her their hands, the icy upper bodies sprouting from the pillar helped Emilia climb toward its top.
All the while, Volcanica tried to interfere with her, but the men of the pillar got in its way from the side.

Bound by its instinct to guard the pillar, Volcanica couldn't leave the ice soldiers on it alone.
The ice soldiers being born hurled ice swords and axes up at Volcanica in the air, and the 'Divine Dragon,' batting them away, hunted down the pillar's ice soldiers one by one with its tail and forelegs.
But all the while, Emilia kept climbing the pillar, up, up, and up—

"This part's a bit rough, but!"

It was rather too dramatic a sight to be called "walking on thin ice," but built on the strength and sacrifice of her ice soldiers, Emilia's hand finally reached the very top of the pillar.
Just ten more meters or so and she'd reach the topmost floor of the tower, but the upper part of the pillar curved outward like a rat-guard, making it a nearly impossible feat to climb from here.
It was a situation that would give even a skilled mountaineer trouble, but fortunately, Emilia had always been good at climbing trees. Back in the day she'd thrown herself into it so much that Fortuna and Archi used to scold her for it.

"The numbness in my fingers is fine now, so this should...!"

Feeling returning to her once-numb fingers, Emilia took on the spitefully curved pillar.
She grabbed the hands of ice soldiers sprouting upside-down along the curve, her feet lifting off into the unstable open air. But thanks to the soldiers' help, the climb held steady.
All that remained was Volcanica's interference—

"—!"

In that instant, a single moment's slackened guard, and fate's cruel little trick slipped right in.

'—I, Volcanica. By the ancient covenant, I shall question the resolve of the one who would reach the summit.'

The violent tremor that followed came from Volcanica's tail, still reciting those words, slamming hard into the pillar. The impact traveled through the entire pillar, cracking every upper-body ice soldier sprouting from it down to the root and shattering them all at once.
The curved ice soldier whose hand Emilia had been relying on was no exception.

"—Ah."

The hand she'd grabbed snapped off at the shoulder, and losing her support, Emilia's body began to fall. The ill-eyed ice soldier desperately tried to stop her, but its other hand couldn't reach.
A sense of weightlessness immediately enveloped Emilia's whole body, threatening to undo every bit of height she'd desperately climbed and send her plunging back to the first floor as if none of it had happened — but it didn't.

"Kyaa!"

Emilia should have plummeted dozens of meters, the whole height of the pillar she'd been climbing. But before she could even finish gasping at the thought, something caught her from below, far sooner than she'd expected — right under her seat.
It wasn't an ice soldier. By feel alone, she could tell that every ice soldier she'd sprouted on the pillar had already been shattered.
And besides, the hand she'd instinctively put out was touching something extremely rough and hard—

"Don't tell me..."

'—Thou who hast come to reach the tower's summit. One who treads the first floor, all-powerful petitioner.'

The voice reached her from point-blank range, just as she was confirming the wind at that great height and the sensation beneath her.
From how close the voice sounded, Emilia belatedly understood — she had fallen onto the back of the 'Divine Dragon' Volcanica.

"—! No time to space out! From here, if I—"

She could use Volcanica as a springboard once more and get back onto the pillar.
That was Emilia's plan, but a new difficulty came crashing down, one that wouldn't let her carry it out so easily.

Beating its wings, Volcanica climbed higher, twisting its body, coming to shake her off.

"—Ngh, nngghhh!"

Enduring the wind that battered her whole body with tremendous force, Emilia clung desperately to the dragon's back.
It was a physical wind unlike anything Emilia had ever felt before — she was clinging to scales that were each large and tough, like slabs of rock, but at this rate she wouldn't last long.
And on top of that, Volcanica was simply flying freely through the sky.

She wasn't being subjected to a tail attack, a strike from the foreleg, much less its breath.
It was simply flying with force, trying to shake off the Emilia clinging to it.
To lose to something like this would be far too—

"—Then I won't be able to go see Subaru and the others."

If she carelessly opened her mouth, the wind rushing in might well burst her lungs.
Head bowed, teeth clenched, Emilia pictured the people precious to her behind her closed eyelids.
It was the kind of mental preparation one might make as if about to die, but that wasn't it at all.

Emilia thought of her precious people in order to face forward.
It was to have her back pushed, to be given the courage to lift her head.

"—————"

Even as she was swallowed by the raging wind, Emilia forced her eyelids open with all her strength.
It was a state where she couldn't even open her eyes without pouring in everything she had. Even so, without letting the strength drain from her violet eyes, Emilia deliberately opened them to find a way through.

When people feel about to be crushed by fear or anxiety, they tend to shut their eyes.
But the precious people Emilia had pictured behind her eyelids were the sort who faced forward precisely at times like this. The sort who never closed their eyes precisely at times like this.

Her eyes had to stay open.
To reach something, to take hold of someone's hand, and—

"—That weird thing—"

The whole scene, above and below, left and right, everywhere, was dominated by blue.
Partly that was because Emilia was up in the sky higher than the clouds, and partly because the 'Divine Dragon' she clung to wore brilliant blue scales.
Everything else went by at such incredible speed that even Emilia's dynamic vision — sharp enough to make out the stitching on a thrown ball — couldn't fully take in the world around her.
So what Emilia's awareness caught hold of wasn't the world outside that blue.

'—I, Volcanica. By the ancient covenant, I shall question the resolve of the one who would reach the summit.'

Volcanica flew while twisting and writhing its body.
It seemed Emilia was clinging on somewhere around the base of Volcanica's wings. Strangely, the dragon wasn't beating those wings all that frequently. Birds and insects needed to flap their wings to fly, but the principle behind a Dragon's flight seemed to be something else entirely.
It was probably closer to whatever let Puck or Roswaal fly.
If that was the case—

"Roswaal uses magic... and Puck's power is strange too, so—"

Unfortunately, she'd never tried this on Roswaal, and she doubted she ever would.
But Emilia had, for a very long time, spent all her time with the one family member she'd once believed was the only one she had.
This past year, apart from him, there had been so much loneliness. There were nights she'd nearly cried, but her memories had been there to hold her up.
And the call of that memory showed Emilia hope even now, in a moment like this.
It was—

"This is just a guess, but... aren't you also weak to having your neck tickled?"

Her forced-open violet eyes fixed on the writhing 'Divine Dragon's' long neck. There, beneath the great Dragon's jaw, amid the dense array of blue scales, was a single white scale.

—Days spent playing around with Puck rose up in Emilia's mind.

'That's no good, Lia. If you do something that ticklish to me, it'll throw off my concentration, won't it?'

"—Right, Puck."

With that answering voice, Emilia focused her mind.
Even in that still-fierce wind, Emilia had no way to reach that white scale directly by herself. But what she'd learned at the pillar could still apply, even if her target had changed from something inanimate to something living.

"—Soldier."

At the focus of Emilia's mind, ice soldiers appeared once more around the white scale.
The upper-body-only ice soldiers braced firmly against each other, and the one born at their center slowly reached its hand toward the white scale—

'——!!?'

—For the first time, Emilia heard a voice from the 'Divine Dragon' that wasn't its endless refrain.

—There is a word: the "reverse scale."

Originating in an old Chinese legend, the word refers to the single scale on the throat of the mythical Dragon that must never be touched — that scale is called the "reverse scale."
A Dragon whose reverse scale is touched flies into a rage, and is said to invariably kill whoever touched it.
From that legend came the expression "to touch the reverse scale," used to describe touching upon some part of another person that must never be touched.

Of course, Emilia had no way of knowing the origins of an idiom from some entirely different world.
Her attempt to touch the white scale on the dragon's neck had simply been meant to disrupt the concentration of the creature flying about so forcefully to shake her off, and create an opening.
However—

"Kyaaaaaa!!"

Screaming, Emilia was flung out into the air, up and down spinning wildly around her.
But this time, the feeling of falling ended even more quickly than when she'd landed on the dragon's back before. Caught by something hard, Emilia instinctively broke her fall with her whole body and rolled.
Then she scrambled quickly to her feet and looked warily around her.

"—————"

Breathing hard, Emilia swept her gaze around her surroundings.
Fortunately, there was no sign of any sudden attack coming at her. That was only natural. Volcanica, currently her greatest threat, was still far, far above.

'——!!'

Volcanica, climbing higher into the sky, writhed as if it had truly hated having that white scale touched.
A scream that seemed fit to bite through the very sky echoed out, and Emilia couldn't help but widen her eyes.

"But Puck always seemed to enjoy it..."

Still, whether one enjoyed it or not was a matter of individual reaction.
Cautioning herself not to be pushy about it, Emilia checked the feeling in her hands and feet.

Having been thrown about so violently on Volcanica's back, the blood flow through her body had gone a little strange.
Depending on circumstances, that could have caused her blood flow to stall, cutting off blood to her brain and costing her her eyesight, but Emilia had barely managed to hold on.
And having confirmed as much, Emilia noticed something.

"—Ah! Wait, this is..."

Looking around, Emilia realized her vantage point was a full level higher than when she'd first arrived on the first floor.
The absence of the six pillars that should have surrounded her was proof that Emilia had risen above the very tips of those pillars — meaning this place was none other than the topmost floor.

She had arrived here by being thrown clear off Volcanica's back.
To the topmost floor of the Pleiades Watchtower, a place no one had ever set foot before — at last—

"Wow, I did it! All that hard work paid off!"

With a reaction rather modest given everything that had just happened, Emilia clapped her hands together in front of her chest, then immediately rushed toward the center of the topmost floor.
If she dawdled, the writhing Volcanica would come back.
Before that happened, she had to find the 'Trial' that would help save Subaru and the others.

"Please, please let it be a question even I can figure out..."

Volcanica having forgotten the 'Trial' was a huge problem in itself, but for Emilia, there was also the separate matter of whether she could even solve the first floor's 'Trial' to begin with.
Fearing that even as she went, Emilia reached the center of the topmost floor. There, she arrived at the base of the pillar stretching up toward the heavens, and her eyes went wide. "Ah."

There it was. A feature that the six pillars one level below hadn't had.
A strange feature found only on this topmost floor's central pillar, and it was certainly there.
It was—

"—Someone's handprint?"

At the base of the central pillar on the topmost floor of the Pleiades Watchtower stood a black monolith.
And pressed into that black monolith were handprints.


—Six of them, each a different man's or woman's handprint, pressed there.
