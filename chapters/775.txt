Arc 10 Chapter 16: "Sneak Attack"


"Subaru is..."
"—a Sin Archbishop?"

It was a suspicion that made absolutely no sense.
Even hearing it spoken aloud again as if to confirm it, it still made no sense at all. It was so incomprehensible that if their goal was to get him to lower his guard, he would have burst out laughing at how hugely successful they were.

However, for Garfiel, who held the position of top military officer of their camp, the grim expression on the large man standing before him—the man called Marcos—was far too stern to laugh off this nonsensical suspicion as a joke.
At the very least, relaxing his vigilance in front of this man wasn't an option for him in the slightest.

"..."

With a quiet snort, Garfiel quietly caught the scent of the heavy tension permeating the royal residence. What he sensed was suppressed hostility, anxious doubt and distrust stirring inside their hearts, and behind all that, the sharp, pungent stench of fear.
Having caught whiff of this exact same air multiple times recently in the Vollachian Empire, Garfiel knew this was the atmosphere of a fire magic stone warehouse on the brink of exploding.
Which meant—

"You tryin' to pick a fight with yours truly 'n' the others or what?!"

"—Does that mean you refuse our request?"

"Hah?! After spittin' in our faces first, don't go puttin' on a face like *you're* the one being picked on!"

"Wait. Don't snap at him, Garfiel. Please."

Emilia's voice called out from behind, stopping Garfiel just as he clicked his fangs in irritation and glared up at the much taller Marcos.
When she went so far as to add "Please," Garfiel had no choice but to pull back his horns. Appreciating his compliance with an added "Thank you," Emilia then looked up resolutely.

"I'm sorry about my boy. He was so hugely surprised that he couldn't find the words, so he just got angry on my behalf."

"Then, Lady Emilia, are you angry as well, just like him?"

"That... yes, that's right. After the shock of hearing such a strange thing, I feel sooo frustrated. I feel like I could burst out in anger at any moment. But—"

Emilia paused there and placed a hand on her chest. Toying with the green magic crystal hanging there with her fingers, as if organizing her thoughts with its hard texture,

"Throwing a tantrum here won't make Subaru's situation any better. That would just be me getting angry because I want to be angry. And I don't think that's right."

"—You look me in the eye and say it so clearly."

"...It's been a while since we last spoke properly, hasn't it, Marcos-san? I'm like this now—a tiny bit stronger than I used to be. And that's thanks to Subaru, too."

"..."

Neither swayed by emotion nor backed into a corner, Emilia gave her answer, causing Marcos to narrow his eyes with a thoughtful expression.
Meanwhile, listening to Emilia speak, Garfiel felt the blood that had rushed to his head slowly cool down. Though that didn't mean his body relaxed its tension, nor would he allow it to.

"In the first place, this so-called suspicion strikes me as nothing short of absurd. Natsuki-san having ties to the Witch Cult? And at the level of a Sin Archbishop, no less? It's not even funny as a joke."

"That's right! Besides, Subaru-sama has resolved so many problems caused by the Witch Cult up until now! He even defeated Sin Archbishops, and saved me and everyone in the village... Please don't say such strange things!"

With Otto and Petra offering covering fire, Garfiel nodded along with a "Yeah, yeah!"
'Sloth', 'Greed', and 'Gluttony'—the Sin Archbishops of those sins had been slain or captured in battles led by Subaru, dealing massive blows to the Witch Cult. On top of that, Subaru had contributed hugely to the subjugation of two of the Three Great Witchbeasts, the 'White Whale' and the 'Great Rabbit'.
Claiming that Subaru was part of the Witch Cult after all that was beyond ridiculous.

"You ain't sayin' they had a huge fight among allies and ended up like this, are ya? Even if yours truly had a fight with Otto-bro, I wouldn't beat him to a pulp! Not that we'd get into a fight in the first place, though."

"While Rem doubts whether Garf and Otto-san's relationship is an apt comparison, Rem believes that question is entirely reasonable. ...Rem cannot believe that man is capable of harboring such malice."

"That's right~. Sure, it seems like big brother loves taking on tons of trouble, but I think the biggest reason he does it is just because he doesn't think things through~."

Despite that sharp jab from Rem, with Meili following up, the opinion of their camp aligned with Garfiel's; it was their collective consensus centered around Emilia.
Receiving this, Marcos did not let his crag-like expression twitch in the slightest as he spoke.

"I understand that everyone in your camp places tremendous trust in Knight Natsuki Subaru. That being said, I must once again request that Lady Emilia come to the castle."

"Should I just say what we just talked about to the people at the castle, too?"

"In addition to that, simply answer our questioning truthfully. If I may offer a word of advice, extending your utmost cooperation to resolve these suspicions is the best course of action to clear his name."

"..."

"—Excuse me, may I ask a question?"

Just as Emilia lowered her dark purple eyes thoughtfully in response to Marcos repeating his original request, Rem interjected.
Like Garfiel, she asked while keeping a sharp, watchful eye on the surrounding Kingdom soldiers.
Her question was—

"Does that man, the central figure... Natsuki Subaru, not need to come along?"

—In an instant, the atmosphere among the Kingdom soldiers shifted entirely.

"..."

The surrounding emotions directed at them transformed into something razor-sharp, making the fine hairs on Garfiel's skin stand on end.
Though no blades were physically drawn, an intense gaze equivalent to having swords pointed right at them dominated the reception room of the royal residence. It was a ripple of emotion, like a barely tranquil water surface trampled upon, creating ripples that would not fade.
It was a powder keg, where even a single popping bubble or stray splash could ignite an explosion—

"—Quiet."

A single word—no, a fierce wave of sword intent accompanying that single word—swept away the surging emotions of the crowd in a single blow, quelling the waves by brute force.

"—gh!"

Garfiel felt the heavy illusion of being struck by a mountain-sized palm. The momentary impact caused all his pores to open, and he had to use every ounce of his reason to restrain his body from instinctively transforming into a beast.
His instincts flared in terror at the extraordinary power possessed by the source of that sword intent—an intent so massive that what the Kingdom's soldiers had directed at them just moments ago paled in comparison.

"My soldiers have been exceedingly rude. —To your camp's military officer, I offer a sincere apology for the offense."

"—Hah? Just yours truly?"

"I intended to target only my soldiers, but it seems you were caught in the crossfire. You must be exceptionally well-trained."

As Marcos bowed his head slightly, Garfiel noticed that the rest of his companions in the camp were frowning with puzzled looks, clearly having felt nothing of that sword intent.
It seemed that, just as Marcos said, the only ones who actually felt it were Garfiel and the Kingdom soldiers who were the original targets. —Once again, Garfiel was forcefully reminded.

Marcos Gildark—though overshadowed by the nonsensical absurdity of the 'Sword Saint' Reinhard van Astrea, he stood at the apex of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica Knights, and his strength was no hollow show.

"...Your reaction just now felt sooo strange. Could you answer Rem's question? What's going on with Subaru and Beatrice?"

Setting aside Garfiel, whose emerald eyes held both tremor and vigilance, Marcos deepened the shadows on his face in response to Emilia's thoughtful inquiry.

"—Lady Emilia, do you know of Knight Natsuki Subaru's whereabouts?"

"Today, he went out saying he had something to discuss with Miklotov-san. By himself... well, Beatrice is with him so the two of them, but he said he had an appointment to speak with him—"

"—Lord Miklotov McMahon has passed away."

"Eh?"

The report injected to cut off Emilia's words struck the air of the reception room with an impact far greater than any reprimand for rudeness.

Garfiel, of course, knew the name Miklotov McMahon.
Not only was he the person Subaru and Beatrice had gone to see, but he was also a major figure involved in the Royal Selection. As a core member of the Council of Wise Men, hearing of his death was bound to be shocking.
However, the true shock lay in the words that followed.

As Garfiel and the others were still reeling in confusion, Marcos pressed on relentlessly.
That was—

"Currently, the Kingdom is pursuing the lead that Knight Natsuki Subaru and Duchess Crusch Karsten, a candidate in the Royal Selection, conspired together and were involved in Lord McMahon's death."

"Wait! What do you mean? Subaru and Crusch-san? Involved in Miklotov-san's death...?"

"The sword wound on Lord McMahon's body recovered from the scene matches a knight's sword bearing the crest of the Karsten Duchy. There are also reports that Lord McMahon fell by Duchess Karsten's hand, and that Knight Natsuki Subaru aided in her escape."

"Wha...!"

At the barrage of neatly laid-out information, Garfiel felt dizzy for a moment.
Not only was an important figure like Miklotov murdered, but the one who did it was Crusch, and Subaru helped her—it sounded like nothing more than an utterly absurd accusation.

"That duchess...!"

Even setting aside the ridiculous suspicions against Subaru, Garfiel couldn't believe that Crusch was suspected of committing such an outrage either.
Garfiel's connection with Crusch was limited to a mere day and a half in the Watergate City. But in that battle, they fought shoulder to shoulder, comrades who fought the Witch Cult together to save the city.
He knew she was someone who could truly swing a sword for the sake of others, and he had sincerely wished to do something about the price she had paid for it.
Though Garfiel hadn't seen it directly, when he heard that Crusch's body had been healed by the power of the Divine Dragon Church's miracle, he had set aside all the messy circumstances surrounding the Royal Selection and felt genuinely relieved.

—For that Crusch to commit a murder, and Subaru to aid her... what nonsense!

"—Allow me to confirm. Since you say you are pursuing their whereabouts, that means Natsuki-san and Lady Crusch are currently on the run, correct?"

Suddenly, a voice devoid of emotion cut through to properly assess the situation. The one raising the question was, naturally, the cerebral core of their camp, Otto.
Emilia turned her dark purple eyes toward Otto, trying to compose her agitation.

"Otto-kun..."

"This is important. The fact that the Commander of Knights himself came all the way here isn't so much a show of respect toward Lady Emilia as it is out of caution."

"Caution? You mean he thinks Emilia-san or the rest of us will go wild?"

"While that may not be out of the question, their primary concern is likely a rendezvous. This deployment is a precaution against Natsuki-san and Lady Crusch joining up with us to be harbored. Am I wrong?"

"—Explaining the intent behind our unit placement touches upon military secrecy, so I cannot answer."

"I see. A lack of denial is answer enough."

As Otto nodded at Marcos's non-answer, Garfiel, struggling to knead the rising fury and agitation within himself into something harmless, felt nothing but admiration for him.

In moments like this, he truly felt grateful to have Otto around.
Even on a complex board where countless motives intertwined, because he could trust Otto to sort things out neatly and provide the necessary information, Garfiel could focus entirely on his role as the shield.
Here, too, Garfiel resolved not to be led astray by stray emotions and to stick to his role.

"The death of Lord McMahon and the suspicion that that man might be a member of the Witch Cult... they seem connected, yet at the same time they feel entirely separate. Using that to demand questioning from Emilia-san is, no matter how you look at it—"

"I have already stated that the best way to clear these suspicions is to respond to questioning at the Royal Castle. Should you refuse, we will have no choice but to interpret it as Lady Emilia and her camp abandoning their responsibility to explain the allegations against Knight Natsuki Subaru."

"That's just forcing her, completely ignoring Emilia-nee-sama's own will!"

"It is not forced. However, if she does not comply, the suspicion will remain as it is, and the Kingdom will simply decide its stance toward Knight Natsuki Subaru accordingly."

"..."

Without letting any emotion creep into his low voice, Marcos persisted with the same request.
Whether pushed or pulled, he stood firm as a mountain, unmoving.
Just as an atmosphere of stifling frustration threatened to spread across both sides—

"—I understand. I will go to the castle. Let me speak about Subaru there."

"A wise decision."

"...In that case, I will prepare at once."

Refusing to let the situation stall or implode, Emilia made up her mind to comply with the demand, earning a nod from Marcos. Bearing resentment toward his attitude in her eyes, Petra moved to prepare for going to the castle.
However, Otto stopped Petra with a "No." Narrowing his eyes, he nodded to the wide-eyed Petra before turning back to Marcos.

"We will carefully select who accompanies her to the castle. ...Correct?"

"Discerning as expected. You are quick to catch on."

"...What do you mean?"

Emilia knitted her well-shaped eyebrows as Otto and Marcos exchanged glances. To answer Emilia's question, Marcos stood tall and straight as he explained:

"Regarding Lady Emilia's entrance to the castle, taking the rest of your camp along cannot be permitted. Currently, the suspicions surrounding Knight Natsuki Subaru could potentially extend to Lady Emilia's entire camp. To preserve the independence of testimonies, we must limit her escort to a single person responsible for her personal care."

"An attendant... only one?!"

"We apologize for the imposition, but we ask for your cooperation."

Having likely anticipated their shock, Marcos's stance remained unwavering.
Garfiel glanced at Otto, and from his big-brother figure's slightly grimacing face, he deduced that Otto had also anticipated this response. The problem was that even if anticipated, it was unquestionably a heavy blow to their camp.

"At least let yours truly go along..."

"I do not recommend that. While I understand your desire to serve as a guard in your capacity as a military officer, you are more than capable as an immediate combatant. If Lady Emilia, who is at the center of suspicion, enters the castle accompanied by military force, it will be viewed not as a plea for explanation, but as a show of force."

"That's just a crazy excuse..."

"I am saying that there are those who would interpret it that way."

Piling argument upon argument, the opponent thoroughly squashed their intentions. They wanted to duck out of it somehow, but as long as even Otto didn't interject, it meant the other party's logic was sound.
However—

"If it comes down to an attendant..."

The only options coming up in the camp's current state were Petra and Rem. If Frederica were here, leaving it to his big sister would have been best, but wishing for what wasn't there was pointless.
Entrusting Emilia to Rem, who hadn't regained her memories, was difficult, and sharp-witted as Petra was, leaving a situation of this magnitude in her hands caused more anxiety than assurance.
What on earth was the best choice—just as he thought that.

"—Then it's settled. Ram will go with Emilia-sama."

"—! Ram?!"

As Ram appeared at the entrance of the reception room while speaking, Garfiel's eyes popped wide.
Dressed in her familiar maid outfit and wearing her usual resolute expression, Ram's appearance left not only Garfiel, but Emilia and the others utterly unable to hide their shock.

After all, Ram was supposed to have been resting in a state akin to bedridden due to the heavy strain that started with the journey to the Pleiades Watchtower. She was supposed to wait in the Royal Capital for Garfiel and the others to return, without accompanying them on the trip to Priestella planned for later today.
Yet she stood up, and of all things, took her place grandly beside Emilia. Then, before their dumbfounded eyes, she pinched the hem of her skirt and curtsied.

"My apologies for being late. Because Ram was responsibly checking the softness of the bed, she was slow to notice our guests' arrival."

"...Is the bed's feel alright now?"

"Indeed. One might say it passes muster."

Shrugging slightly, Ram gave her answer without a trace of exhaustion on her face. However, it was clear that she was merely skilled at hiding her burden as usual, not that she had actually recovered.
Her exchange with Emilia just now was simply rephrasing her physical condition as the state of the bed, leaving cause for lingering anxiety without a doubt.
However—

"Nee-sama..."

"Rem, Ram leaves taking care of the leftover group to you. After all, everyone here would fall apart without the help of the cute and competent Ram and Rem sisters. Only Petra is somewhat better."

"—Yes, Rem understands."

Naturally accepting Ram's decision to step into that role, Rem swallowed her worry and anxiety for her sister, nodding while holding back tears from her light blue eyes.
Beside that exchange, Garfiel looked over at Otto. Though it could be seen as a sort of arbitrary action on Ram's part, Otto's judgment was—

"I agree, I think Ram-san is the right person. Can we entrust Lady Emilia to you?"

"There is no need to even ask. Otto, you had better pull yourself together. Garf, try your best to let yourself be put to good use."

"...I don't need ya to tell me that."

Even as Emilia, Rem, and Otto respected Ram's decision and responded resolutely as those entrusted with her words, Garfiel's voice was tinged with weakness.
Garfiel felt ashamed of it, but Ram slightly softened her light pink eyes and,

"Foolish boy."

...brushed aside Garfiel's weakness with sweet sternness.

"—Everyone, I'm off. I'll properly clear up the misunderstanding about Subaru and talk to them so everyone understands."

With Ram's accompaniment as an attendant unanimously agreed upon by the camp, Emilia, having firmed her resolve to enter the castle, made her declaration and looked around at the faces of Garfiel and everyone else.

To be completely honest, the situation was absurd beyond measure.
In an atmosphere like this, taking a beating completely passively was a bad sign. If this were a fistfight, letting the opponent do whatever they wanted would lead to immediate defeat.
To avoid losing, he had to see through what the opponent wanted to do, stop it, and hit back with what *they* wanted to do instead.

"Like always, please lend me your strength. Please."

Saying so, Emilia bowed deeply, making her plea to her companions in the camp.
Even though she was the top of the camp, a Royal Selection candidate, and possessed a grand title, she still lowered her head without hesitation to rely on someone else's strength.
That was precisely why Garfiel—no, why everyone in the camp felt they wanted to devote their all for Emilia and Subaru.
Therefore—

"Ooh! 'Course! Don't worry 'bout a thing, Emilia-sama!"

Hoping to blow away any anxiety for Emilia and Ram as they headed to the castle, Garfiel raised his fist with a voice pumped extra full of spirit.


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△


—And so, after watching Emilia taken away to the Royal Castle by Marcos and his men, accompanied only by Ram as her attendant...

"Everyone, pack your belongings, minimal baggage is fine. —We leave in five minutes."

Thus spoke Otto to the remaining group at the royal residence almost immediately.
Having just shooed away the soldiers left to guard the manor under the guise of wanting to discuss things among themselves, Garfiel involuntarily widened his eyes at Otto's prompt announcement right after they were left alone in the reception room.

"Five minutes?! You ain't waitin' for Emilia-sama?!"

"Indeed. The situation allows not a moment's delay. Between the suspicions cast on Natsuki-san and their coercive attitude in demanding Lady Emilia's presence at the castle... their objective is obvious."

"—That means... eliminating her from the Royal Selection, right?"

"Almost certainly."

Responding to both Garfiel's question and Petra's words, Otto, his usually soft expression replaced by sharp intelligence, raised a finger and said, "Listen closely."

"The timing aligns far too perfectly. On the very day Lord McMahon summoned him, Natsuki-san gets dragged into a bloody altercation... and for the perpetrator to be Duchess Crusch, a fellow Royal Selection candidate? There is a powerful force at work here."

"To remove Emilia-san and another candidate from the board at the same time... that man was used for that purpose, wasn't he?"

"Based on the information we have right now, that is the only logical conclusion. As for Lady Crusch, continuing in the Royal Selection should have been difficult for her the moment she relied on the Divine Dragon Church, but..."

"He~y, I get that you could talk forever, but I don't think we have that much time~. It'd be bad if that rock-head came back, wouldn't it~?"

Meili cut in, putting a hold on the lingering discussion.
Fiddling with her braid while making the tiny red scorpion's pincers snap on top of her head, her words—"that rock-head"—likely referred to Marcos.
Indeed, Marcos was currently accompanying Emilia to the Royal Castle and was absent from this place. As it stood, the royal residence was surrounded by Kingdom soldiers ordered to stand by, placing the remaining Garfiel and company under de facto house arrest, but—

"If we follow orders submissively, we will run out of options. We move before Marcos-san returns."

"...We are going to search for that man, then."

"Natsuki-san and Lady Crusch. Unless we learn what happened to those two and what they saw at Lord McMahon's residence, we can't even begin to clear this up."

"Will it be alright? Relying on that man..."

"Even if Natsuki-san is unreliable, Beatrice-chan is with him. The little girl attached to him is far more dependable than the main unit, after all."

Showing a faint smile, Otto offered a reassuring grin to Rem. Lowering the corners of her eyebrows, Rem accepted his consideration, even if it didn't completely dispel her anxiety.
In that flow of everyone working together to shatter the lingering uncertainty, Garfiel slapped his cheeks with both hands and said,

"Got it. Yours truly is girdin' his loins, too! Otto-bro, even if we scram, as for Emilia-sama 'n' Ram..."

"There should be no danger to them. Skipping various procedures to harm Lady Emilia, a Royal Selection candidate, would not be permitted. They will surely abide by those rules. Either way, the moment they demanded her presence at the castle, the opponent's objective was exclusively her detention."

"Tch, fine! Then yours truly will beat up all the guards, 'n' in the meantime—"

"—You must not."

"Hah?"

Otto shook his head in front of Garfiel, who was cracking his knuckles and working up his fight spirit.
Having his fire doused, Garfiel widened his eyes, and Otto stared at him with a dead-serious expression.

"The guard soldiers are merely following orders; they are not our enemies. We must avoid inflicting injuries. Ideally, we shouldn't even let them draw their swords. What we need to accomplish is an escape to prove our innocence, not a war."

"If we cause casualties, it will worsen the standing of Emilia-sama, Nee-sama... and that man as well."

"Precisely."

Garfiel gnashed his molars as Otto nodded while adjusting his hat.
He got it. He understood the logic. He understood the logic, but Garfiel couldn't think of how to proceed from there. Of course, if Marcos returned, there would be zero guarantee they could safely escape the royal residence.
However, right now, at least, they could escape by force. If that option was sealed off, what on earth could they do—

"I can inquire with the bugs and birds about where the guards are sparse. We could also ask them to draw their attention. Even so, it's not foolproof, but..."

"—In that case, I have an idea!"

"Is that true, Petra-san?"

As Otto suggested his usual method utilizing his Divine Protection, Petra suddenly raised her hand, prompting Rem to ask with widened eyes.
In response, Petra tightly clenched her small hands into fists with a "Yes!" and declared,

"I am super angry right now! About Subaru being suspected, about Emilia-nee-sama being glared at, and about Ram-nee-sama having to push herself! So there's no way we're just going to do what they say!"

"That's pretty scary 'n' reliable, but... what're you gonna do?"

"Of course, I have a special tactic. Otto-san's specialty!"

"My... specialty...?"

Pointed at sharply by name, Otto tilted his head with a completely bewildered look. On the other hand, with cheeks flushed with excitement, Petra seemed full of confidence in her so-called secret plan.

"Petra-chan, what are you planning to do~?"

"It's something Meili-chan and Garf-san both know about. You see—"

Saying so, Petra beckoned everyone closer. As they leaned in, she proudly explained the details of her special plan.
Which was—


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△


"Fire—!!"

A Kingdom soldier's panicked shout echoed, and panic spread like wildfire through the royal residence.
What they witnessed was thick smoke pouring out of the kitchen with tremendous force. Faced with the massive outbreak of smoke accompanied by heat and a burning smell, many of the brave soldiers immediately moved into action.

They rushed into the kitchen to check the source of the fire, hurrying to identify the cause of the dense smoke. Their aim was to extinguish the fire if necessary, quickly subduing both flames and panic.
However—

"The source of the fire is under the floorboards, and the cause of the smoke is a blocked chimney... Putting it out won't be easy!"

Saying so, Petra—who had proposed the fire alarm tactic and planned the detailed method—deepened her smile as she listened to the chaos unfolding just as intended.
It should have been what anyone would call a cute smile, but seeing it up close, Garfiel couldn't help feeling an odd chill run down his spine.
At any rate—

"I really can't accept burning down buildings being treated as my specialty, though!!"

Ignoring Otto's protest, the fire alarm tactic proved a resplendent success.
Unable to endanger the important building that was the royal residence and the involved personnel under house arrest inside, the Kingdom soldiers became occupied with dealing with the smoke, their attention diverted completely from Garfiel and company.
In the meantime—

"Sorry, but sleep for a bit, will ya!"

"I am sorry. Rem holds no grudge... other than on Nee-sama's behalf."

"—gh!"

Garfiel and Rem each took care of the two soldiers who hadn't left their posts at the back door, choking them unconscious to clear a path. Without letting them draw their weapons. Satisfying the condition set by Otto in advance, they exchanged glances and nodded to each other.
Then, together with Otto, Petra, and Meili, they slipped outside the building.

"If you wanted to cause a ruckus, I could have used my cute witchbeasts to handle it easily~."

"That's strictly off-limits! If Meili-chan called witchbeasts to rampage around, it would just lend weight to the suspicions against us! They'd say Subaru is a Witch Cult Sin Archbishop, Emilia-sama is connected to 'The Witch of Envy', Meili-chan goes wild with witchbeasts, and Otto-san burns down buildings right and left..."

"Regarding that last suspicion, just like back at the mansion, I have the counter-evidence that Petra-chan was present too, not just me!"

"Setting aside big brother Otto's true nature, I understand~."

Swishing the hem of her skirt as she pitter-patter ran, Meili shrugged.
That said, detached as she acted, Garfiel felt her recent remark was likely born from her own sense of frustration.
Meili possessed power. However, it was a double-edged sword that, if brandished, would put even those she wanted to protect in danger—the existence of a blade she could not wield filled her with frustration.

"Don't worry 'bout it. Whatever you're lackin', yours truly will pull an extra shift to cover for ya."

Garfiel patted Meili's head as he ran alongside her. Instantly met with a disgusted look and a dry stare from Meili, Garfiel clicked his fangs.

"With Otto-bro's ears 'n' my nose, we'll track down the Boss somehow. Until then, well... cheer for us, cheer! Just cheer us on!"

"Cheering... like, 'Do your best~'?"

"Yeah! In fact, bein' cheered on really gives ya strength."

Meili looked doubtful, but it was a feeling Garfiel knew well.
After all, at Priestella where they were originally supposed to go, the reason Garfiel was able to contend with the corpse soldier 'Eight-Arms' Kurgan was thanks to his younger siblings who were there—and the cheering of citizens inspired with hope after being pushed forward by Subaru's speech.

Yes, exactly. Garfiel's victory back then was through the power of cheering. And the energy to cheer existed because none other than Subaru had stirred the people's hearts.
It was the result of Natsuki Subaru painting over the despair prepared by the Witch Cult with hope.

"There's no damn way that Boss of ours is a Sin Archbishop...!"

At a story so absurd that even doubting it was laughable, Garfiel felt as though the back he so respected was being smeared with filth, making him truly, truly infuriated.
It wasn't just Priestella. The 'Sanctuary' where Garfiel lived with his grandmothers, Petra's Arlam Village which was saved from the Witch Cult, and those who fought alongside Subaru in the Subjugation of the White Whale—there were countless people who knew the goodness of Natsuki Subaru.

—And among them all, the one who believed in Subaru most strongly was none other than Emilia.

According to Otto, the demand for Emilia to come to the castle was aimed exclusively at her detention from the start, and the questioning to clear Subaru's name was merely an excuse.
And yet, if the opponent had any genuine intention of clearing Subaru's name, there was no one other than Emilia who could weave words for that purpose with her whole soul.

"Dammit all."

It was frustrating. Frustrating. So unbelievably frustrating. Frustrating, frustrating, he couldn't stop feeling frustrated.
Subaru had been working so hard, hadn't he? Wanting to be of strength to Emilia whom he fell for, wanting to protect the hearts of everyone around him, he had bust his butt working recklessly hard no matter what!

So why wouldn't they listen to the voices of those who knew Subaru's hard work?
If they would just give them that one opportunity, they ought to understand.

—That Natsuki Subaru was a ridiculously cool big-brother figure Garfiel was proud of, struggling with all his might to fulfill an unfathomably grand ideal.

"Garf-oniisan..."

"—! It's nothin'! 'Kurgan finished off his enemy even after losin' his arms!' Yours truly can't be moping around, either!"

Intending to encourage Meili, he had ended up becoming emotional himself instead; driving it away with a spirited voice and a click of his teeth, Garfiel faced forward.
At his demeanor, Meili slightly narrowed her eyes and,

"Everyone really is big brother Subaru and big sister Emilia's companions, aren't they~?"

...muttered what to Garfiel could only be considered the highest compliment.

"—Otto-san, what do we do from here?"

With Garfiel and the others' exchange behind her, Rem pulled up alongside Otto, who was running ahead, and posed her question.
In response, Otto guided Garfiel and company while saying, "This way," and continued:

"For now, I'd like us to lay low so our trail can't be followed. It won't take long before our escape is discovered. I want to leave the Noble District immediately. To that end, I am borrowing their help as we speak."

"...I see."

Choosing shadows and narrow alleys, Otto led the route away after bursting out of the royal residence. In the direction of Otto's gaze, those familiar insects were stealthily squirming about.
At his explanation of borrowing their strength, Rem refrained from saying more than her quiet acceptance. Garfiel had also suffered at their hands in the past, so Otto negotiating with insects—mainly Zodda Bugs—brought back rather unpleasant memories.
Though, as allies, there was no human—or insect—more reliable than them.

"If we get caught at a checkpoint, it's over. So—Garfiel!"

"Got it! You two, hold on tight!"

"Kyaaa—!"

At Otto's call, Garfiel scooped up Meili and Petra beside him, holding them under each arm as he kicked off the wall in front of them—the sturdy defense wall connecting the Royal Capital's Noble District and Commoner District—and leaped right over to the other side.
A wall that was supposed to serve a crucial security role in the capital was child's play for Garfiel's leg strength.

"Wait here a bit. Gotta go fetch Otto-bro 'n' Rem, too."

"I understand that, but... Garf-san, you shouldn't suddenly scoop girls up like that. I was super surprised just now!"

"Hah? What's with worrying 'bout such boring—don't give me that scary look! Fine, I get it!"

Evidently serious about it, Garfiel felt a chill from Petra's glare and apologized. Then, taking a running start, he glared upward to leap over the wall again—

"—My apologies. Rem was able to cross even without Garf."

Landing right before his eyes as she spoke, Garfiel widened his eyes at Rem. That she could jump over this wall was surprising enough, but what was even more shocking was—

"Big brother Otto managed not to scream, didn't he~?"

"It was a close call, though... Rem-san, please put me down. Otherwise I'll be killed by Natsuki-san."

"Huh? Why does that man's name come up? Rem has no idea what you mean."

"That phrasing is definitely the phrasing of someone who knows exactly what I mean!!"

Glared at up close by Rem, who was carrying him in her arms, Otto squeaked out his protest. As Otto finally had his feet planted back on the ground and was teased by Petra and the others, Garfiel let out a deep breath, suppressing the emotions that had surged inside him until just now.

Without a doubt, the situation was grim. Perhaps even worse than when they were caught up in the civil war in the Empire.
However, Subaru was surely safe alongside Beatrice. Emilia, too, with Ram by her side, would do her best without being reckless. And everyone here—Otto, Rem, Petra, and Meili—were all dependable. —And he would support them.

The situation was bad. But he could still smile. If he could smile, they weren't cornered yet.

"Otto-bro, next time yours truly will carry ya 'n' fly over!"

"Yes, I'll rely on you then."

"You got it. So, what's next? Ya said somethin' 'bout layin' low for a bit."

"Indeed. For now, I'll guide us to somewhere deserted—"

Slowly shaking his head, Otto looked around before cutting off his own words. Blinking rapidly, he frantically scanned the buildings, shadows, and various spots near the defense wall.
Catching the scent of impatience he couldn't hide, Garfiel asked:

"Otto-bro?"

"—They're gone."

"Gone? What d'ya mean gone...?"

"The Zodda Bugs. No, not just the Zodda Bugs. Every single insect I entrusted with guiding us here has completely vanished... Don't tell me!"

Eyes wide, Otto turned around to face Garfiel and everyone else rather than the shadows.
His expression stiffened, his voice strained with panic as he grabbed Garfiel's arm.
And then—

"We messed up! Somehow... the insects tricked me!"

"Insects tricking... Otto-san? Such a thing..."

"It happens! We were led here! At this rate, we have no way out! We need to leave right—"

Unwittingly, the zone they thought was safe had been violated; even Otto himself seemed unable to believe it, yet he pressed the worst-case scenario onto his allies.
Hearing that, Garfiel shook off his stunned disbelief, intending to move immediately according to Otto's warning—







"—That's far enough."


—That was the exact moment when perhaps the worst pursuer imaginable caught up to them.
