Arc 3 Chapter 73: "Malicious Sloth"

A black ruin swallowing the sky, with a force and mass so overwhelming it could be mistaken for a nightmare.

Beneath the feet of the woman wearing a twisted smile, the swollen shadow spewed forth an endless stream of Unseen Hands — their number showing no sign of a limit.
Stretching into the sky, their palms turned this way — their number needed no counting; it was closing in on a hundred.

"—Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti!!"

"Oh myyy? To be called by name before I've introduced myself — how strange, DESU! Though the fingers I'm moving now should be different from the ones you know, DESU?"

Subaru shouted in fury amid the still-swirling dust, and hearing it, Petelgeuse — puzzled by the content of the words yet looking all the more delighted for it — stamped a foot on the ground,

"It seems that most of the Fingers I had hidden in the forest have been exterminated, annihilated, wiped out entirely, DESU — but that too cannot be helped! The lives of my pious followers, precious and dear, lives that could never be allowed to go to waste — I now know they existed for this moment, this instant, this very now, DESU! Ahh! Ahhhh! O love, o favor! O beloved Witch!"

In his excitement, he thrust the fingers of his right hand into his mouth and crunched them one after another, decorating the battlefield with a blood-soaked mad grin, and swung the arm dripping with blood,

"Now, let the end begin, and let the end of the end be pronounced, DESU! And and and and andddd! Before my love, before my diligent service! Offer up your bodies, your lives, your souls — DESU!!"

The instant he declared it, the arms that had blanketed the sky rained down upon them — and the Witch Cultists surrounding the raging Petelgeuse likewise broke into a run, cross-shaped daggers in hand.

In response, their own preparations lagged a step behind, their momentum having been cut off at the outset.

All around, the subjugation force still bore the thick confusion of the explosion from behind; smoke billowed up from the blast site, and there was concern for the safety of the two main fighters who had been there. But—

"There ain't no time to stand around gawkin'! It's kill or be killed — ain't that right!"

Swinging his great cleaver, Ricardo bellowed this, his sharp canines gleaming. That alone was enough to settle the confusion among the beastfolk mercenary band, and every one of them, led by their captain, sent their Ligers running.

At the same time, Julius raised his knight's sword high, drawing the eyes of the knights,

"Do not let their cowardly deeds shake your hearts! Nothing has changed — behind us are the people we must protect. — All units, charge!!"

With the sword's downswing, Julius's Earth Dragon was the first to bolt forward. Following him, the knights too raised their own weapons high and fell in alongside the path of the finest knight.

And so the combined force of mercenaries and knights charged forth — at its very front ran Natsuki Subaru, clinging awkwardly astride an elegant, black-coated Earth Dragon.

Even before the two leaders had finished calming their comrades' confusion, Subaru had already commanded Patrasche to plunge headlong into the threat of the attacking Witch Cult.

It wasn't as if he had any chance of winning.

If anything, since nothing had changed about Subaru's own combat strength, he was helpless enough to be killed even going up against a single dagger-wielding cultist.

But even so, Subaru had to be the one running at the front. Because—

"Hey, Pete-boy! Target me! —'Cause I can see every last one of your many little hands, real reeeeeal clearly!!"

"—!?"

Choking down his fear, Subaru flashed a boldly provoking grin and raised his middle finger, freezing the mad grin on Petelgeuse's face solid.

As if to prove his words, Subaru worked the reins and had Patrasche dodge the writhing Unseen Hands that came lashing at them.

He leaned right to slip past a reaching arm, dropped low against a palm thrust straight at him. A sharp turn let him slide past the cluster of arms blocking the front, and a burst of speed left the demonic hands closing in from all sides behind.

It wasn't that Subaru's own skill was so great — the real reason the evasions succeeded was that Patrasche read his intent through the reins instantly.

And at Subaru, who by the narrowest of margins had proven his own words true, Petelgeuse — that face so innocent-looking for a woman — twisted grandly with fury and humiliation,

"What foolishness... that cannot be! Why, why why why whywhywhy... My own, the favor granted to me, to me alone! You dare set your eyes eyes eyes eyes eeeeeeeyes on it!?"

"Maybe the Witch's just a two-timer? Whoa whoa whoa whoa, did she play you for a fool? Aww, poor thing!!"

"What an insult! What a humiliation! What a disgrace! To speak such words not only of me but of the very guide of my love... it cannot be forgiven! It must not be forgiven! Such a thing must never be allowed to be forgiven, DESU!!"

Prying his bloodshot eyes wide, foam gathering at the corners of his mouth, Petelgeuse pressed the pursuit. Bundled black demonic hands merged into one massive palm, closing in to crush him, but Subaru broke through by sending Patrasche in a sharp swerve left — the aftershock tore up the earth, gouging a deep rift into the grassland as if carved by a giant's palm.

"Whoa, that was close! Seriously, it's gonna be nothing but close calls from here on... but still, with this!"

Petelgeuse, hatred blazing in both eyes, had completely lost himself and fixed his aim solely on Subaru.

Even as he felt a skin-crawling chill at having the madman's full hostility turned on him, Subaru clenched his fist at seeing events unfold exactly as he'd intended.

Going by what had happened in the previous loop, he'd been able to predict that the number of Unseen Hands Petelgeuse commanded would increase once he achieved his Possession-revival.

And that if those lethal demonic hands — invisible to ordinary eyes — were unleashed in countless numbers, that alone would be enough to drive even the numerically superior subjugation force to ruin.

That was why Subaru chose to stoke Petelgeuse's mad obsession with the Witch to the utmost, drawing his focus onto himself alone.

Meanwhile, behind them, the clash between the Witch Cultists and the subjugation force had begun.

The opening clash exploded in the subjugation force's favor, and Subaru could see the Cult's front ranks thrown into the air in sprays of blood. But he could also tell that the knights, unaccustomed to the Cultists' eerie, gliding movements from that point on, were being forced into a hard fight.

To keep holding out and expect reinforcements to come rushing to the rescue immediately was far too convenient a hope.

If only Wilhelm, whose skill in man-to-man combat had no equal, were still standing, Subaru thought with regret. But—

"My mistake was thinking too shallow... If he's got the power to possess someone by dying, I should've predicted he'd have some means of suicide ready!"

The explosion of the Dragon Carriage carrying the old Petelgeuse had surely been caused by exactly that.

Petelgeuse had likely planted something like a bomb in a follower's body beforehand, giving instructions to detonate it depending on the situation.

As a result, the old Petelgeuse's body had, quite neatly, taken the Dragon Carriage and their nearby main fighters down with it, and successfully transferred into a new vessel.

Thick smoke still rose from the blast site, and neither Wilhelm nor Ferris were anywhere in sight. — Though he couldn't believe they'd simply been blown away like that.

"I can't be optimistic enough to think they came out unscathed. — Just stick to the plan, I'm counting on you!"

"DESUDESUDESUDESUDESUDESUDESUDESU DEEEEEEEEESU——!!"

Shaking his head in a frenzy, Petelgeuse deployed the Unseen Hands to block the way. But Subaru cut through it with an instant counter-turn, closing the distance, and at the startled Petelgeuse's face—

"Ka-BLAM!!"

"Bwuh!?"

Patrasche's pivoting leg dug into the ground as she drifted, and the scattering clods of dirt struck Petelgeuse's face dead-on. Subaru raised his middle finger at that dirt-caked face and circled around, inviting him on,

"Hey hey, doesn't matter how many hands you got if the head running 'em is useless. Take a lesson from me and Patrasche — eye to eye, hand to hand, heart to heart, we're completely in sync. What's the matter, you Sloth-ful or something!?"

"—!!!"

Having lost even the words for his fury, Petelgeuse silently sent forth a palm of death.

But Petelgeuse, worked up by Subaru's outstanding gift for the skill of "provocation," had noise running through his thoughts, and his attacks lacked their usual edge.

Which is to say, for all the advantage of his countless palms,

"Your brain can't keep up with the processing load. It's a joke if the hardware's a rust-bucket next to the software's spec requirements. Didn't shake your brain around too much, did ya!?"

In a situation where one wrong step meant instant death, Subaru couldn't quite suppress his laughter at his own state of mind, still able to crack wise like this. A breathy, snorting grin leaked out of him, and it only added to the effect of his provocation against Petelgeuse.

What should have been razor's-edge clashes had, in his evasive movements, begun to feel almost like something he was used to.

With Patrasche, whom just a few hours ago he'd barely managed to sync his breathing with, now not just their breath but their very heartbeats meshed, and their intentions passed clearly to one another through the reins.

This was what Julius had spoken of — "entrust your faith, and leave the wind to her."

— And now, not through words but through his heart, Subaru finally understood what those words meant!

"—ru!!"

Bursting through the rising dust, his innards churned by the centrifugal force of a sharp turn, Subaru's mind burned hotter with exhilaration than nausea — and then his eardrums suddenly caught it.

From far off in the distance came a voice calling his name, and the owner of that voice — one he could never mistake — was,

"Subaru——!!"

He saw her at a distance, leaning out the window of a Dragon Carriage that was rapidly picking up speed as it tried to break away from the combat zone, her long silver hair tossed and tangled by the wind — Emilia.

Evacuating from the combat zone — it wasn't only the Dragon Carriage she rode in doing so. Around them, the merchants' Dragon Carriages carrying the villagers had likewise begun to flee, a handful of knights assigned to escort them. All of it was exactly according to the plan arranged beforehand.

— The Witch Cult's last desperate struggle: that had been a foreseen contingency.

If it came to a fight, they couldn't afford to have a crowd of non-combatants on hand while facing an enemy who fought without regard for anything. That was why the merchants had been instructed in advance to evacuate the moment fighting broke out. To keep the confusion from spreading among the villagers, only those composed enough to listen calmly had been told beforehand, and they'd been split among the carriages to prepare for the worst.

And of course, this had also been conveyed to Ram, who needed to navigate a separate route to the Sanctuary — but—

"Why...!?"

Subaru had not told Emilia any of it.

From within the receding carriage, he could see Emilia's face twisted in anguish.

Seeing the shock of an unbearable betrayal etched into it struck a deep, tearing pain into Subaru's chest.

Even so, Subaru hid that inner shock away, and despite the jolting and the roaring wind, took one hand off the unfamiliar reins to give a thumbs-up.

"Leave it to me, easy as pie, I'm Natsuki Subaru! Go on ahead, Emilia-tan! —I'll catch up in no time, and give you a heartfelt hug!"

"—!!"

He couldn't tell if she was close enough to see his teeth flash in a grin, but from the carriage now racing off down the distant forest road, only a wordless echo of a voice still reached him.

Once he'd confirmed that her group had safely broken free of the combat zone, Subaru let out a deep breath and turned back to face Petelgeuse once more,

"Didn't expect you to wait around so patiently. Turns out you're the sentimental type?"

"Silence, silence, silence, be silent, DESU! I never stopped my actions — you were simply the one dodging everything, DESU! Insolence! Blasphemy! To refuse the Witch's love! Her embrace! Oh, the foolishness of a body that will not accept it! Why must you go so far to...!"

"Oh, you were attacking me this whole time? Your palms are so slow once I can actually see 'em, I figured a fly could land on one, so I thought you'd just stopped, stopped. Feel free to keep it up nice and slow, I'll just — oh."

Subaru, keeping up the provocation as if this were his true calling, raised an eyebrow at a sudden shift he sensed on the battlefield. Behind the one-on-one standoff between Petelgeuse and Subaru, a major change had come over the clash between the Witch Cult and the subjugation force.

— Several Witch Cultists' bodies were sent up into the air all together, trailing a mist of blood.

"Nguraaaaahh!!"

A gleaming sacred sword whirled back, scattering blood and sunlight, its blade raging at a terrific speed. Arms, legs, necks, and torsos in its path were sheared clean through by that edge, and the knights finished off, one after another, the enemies sent flying by the follow-up strikes.

Drenched head to toe in blood, a demon of the blade tearing across the battlefield — Wilhelm had joined the fight.

"—Wilhelm-san!"

Subaru cheered at seeing him alive and well, while Petelgeuse bit down at the corner of his mouth in irritation.

As if answering Subaru's cheer, Wilhelm, moving low as if crawling across the ground, severed two more men's legs below the knee. He weaved his body to dodge an incoming fireball, and, accelerating with the blast of flame at his back, mass-produced death.

With Wilhelm's entry into the fight, the momentum tilted all at once in their favor.

Ricardo and Julius, as if determined to keep pace with the sword demon, launched into a fierce assault of their own — with every swing of the great cleaver came the sound of flesh bursting, and with every flash of a knight's sword, sparks of glowing Mana scattered.

Further beyond, at the site where the Dragon Carriage had been blown apart — as the smoke cleared, a figure was crawling out, a scorched canopy cloth draped over one shoulder to hide a bare body.

It was Ferris. Having apparently taken the blast at close range, he too was alive and well — and moreover, neither his beauty striking enough to be mistaken for a woman's, nor his carefully tended hair, showed the slightest effect. The only casualty of the damage seemed to be his clothes,

"Ohh, geez — I basically died once already, didn't I, nya! Vil-jii! Don't go easy on 'em anymore, tear 'em to pieces!"

Thrusting a small fist into the air, the half-naked boy who looked every bit a girl threw his high-pitched cheer at the sword demon.

Though Subaru felt something less than reassuring about both his state and his shout, he turned a triumphant grin toward Petelgeuse,

"Your do-or-die self-destruct got zero casualties... and the tide's already turned! Give it up already!"

"Give up? Do you think such a thing, such a foolish thing, such an idiotic thing could possibly be allowed, DESU!? Do you think that I, I who embody 'Sloth,' would sink into such a slothful choice — abandoning both thought and will — DESU!? What supreme insult! What humiliation! To repay love with every ounce of diligence I possess...! If that alone is the reason to wash away this disgrace——!!"

Spreading both arms wide, the Unseen Hands that had been gushing from every part of Petelgeuse's body suddenly vanished.

Subaru felt a bad premonition at that stance of his. But the distance he'd put between them for evasive maneuvering now worked against him — he couldn't close that gap in time.

As a result,

"O Witch! O guide of my love! I offer, allow me to offer! Now, you traitors who foolishly turn your backs on her favor! You parched, vulgar creatures who know nothing of love!"

"Wait, Petelgeuse — you bastard!!"

"Now — be Sloth!!"

In the next instant, a black wave spread out to swallow the battlefield whole — and the front line collapsed.

* * *

The Witch Cult's Sin Archbishop in charge of "Sloth," Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti.

For Subaru, this already marked the fourth time facing this man, but he could say with certainty that, ever since being summoned to this other world, no formidable enemy had tormented him as much as this man had.

Across his repetitions, Subaru had already lost his life in this world eleven times. In all that time, never once dying the same way twice, every one of those deaths was carved into his Soul with an unforgettable weight of reality.

Before an overwhelming gap in strength, before an unavoidable unfairness, sometimes through a lapse of attention born of carelessness, and, at the end of a mutual failure to understand one another's intentions, Natsuki Subaru had fumbled away his life again and again in this other world.

There had been horrifying enemies. There had been confrontations that threatened to shatter his heart. There had been overwhelmingly powerful beings. Against them, helpless Natsuki Subaru had had his spirit broken time and again.

And yet Subaru had always risen back up regardless. But this man's malice alone stood apart from all of it, unlike anything that had come before, by a wide margin.

Reaching out unseen arms to tear apart absolutely anything: the Unseen Hand.

Discarding a dead body and moving his Soul into a subordinate's flesh to keep his life going: Possession.

And the Authority that could be declared the very worst in any battle involving multiple combatants: Sloth.

That ferocity, which he had nearly let himself forget, now swallowed the battlefield whole, and the fighting spirit of the subjugation force — which until then had been pressing the battle forward in a single relentless push — shattered to pieces. — No, it wasn't their fighting spirit that had been shattered. It was their sanity.

"—!!"
"—Ahh!?"

Screams and shrieks echoed across the battlefield, and many dropped their weapons. And, above all, the Witch Cultists were entirely unaffected by Petelgeuse's Sloth.

"Stop...!"

He shouted, trying to halt what was happening, but words held no meaning at all in this moment.

Before Subaru's voice could fully reach anyone, the swung cross-daggers had already drunk blood. The gushing blood summoned anguished screams, and the front line was broken apart all at once.

Subaru himself was unaffected by Sloth, and he had no idea exactly what symptoms the mental contamination it caused inflicted on those afflicted. But everyone under its influence was driven to the brink of madness by hallucinations, phantom sounds, and phantom pain that didn't exist, foaming at the mouth, eyes rolled back white.

Of the roughly fifty men in the subjugation force, only about fifteen managed to barely resist the mental contamination. The rest collapsed where they stood, or were cut down and left bleeding before the Witch Cult's blades. — The one small mercy amid the misfortune was that nearly all of the main fighters had held out.

"How uncanny—! This is just like the White Whale's mist..."

Wilhelm deflected the blades of three attackers head-on with a single stroke, then fell back, dragging the body of a fallen comrade. In the same way, Ricardo carried the cat siblings under each arm, while Julius chanted wind Magic to raise dust and cover their retreat with a smokescreen.

"Ohh, more of you remained than I expected, DESU! How splendid, DESU, what a joyous thing, DESU! If this many with the right aptitude remain, then the Cult's future is secure, DESU!"

"Aptitude...? What are you on about!? No, more importantly, this situation is..."

Bad.

With just a single move, the situation had been overturned all at once.

In terms of numbers, combined with the losses inflicted before the mental contamination spread, the two sides were now roughly even. But since their own side had more wounded than dead, the hands of those still standing were tied up tending to them.

The Witch Cultists, meanwhile, cared nothing at all for their own wounded. They trampled without hesitation over the corpses of comrades who could have been saved with treatment, charging in prepared to trade blows even while sustaining fatal wounds.

And above all——

"You — it seems your goal all along was a diversion to hold my attention, DESU?"

"—!"

Subaru's aim had been seen through by Petelgeuse, and his shock showed plainly on his face.

Reading that, Petelgeuse clapped both hands together, cheeks twisting in genuine delight,

"I seeee! Truly, truly truly truly truly truly, a diligent thought, DESU! I don't know by what means you learned of my Unseen Hands, or of my Fingers — but if you knew, then your actions just now, until now, all along — they make sense, DESU! And... once I understand that much, there's no longer any reason for me to bother with you, is there, DESU?"

With that, Petelgeuse spun on his heel. His gaze turned toward the battlefield where knights and Witch Cultists clashed, and if the Fingers stretching from his shadow reached that way, it would be impossible to avoid being forced further into a losing position.

At that malicious move, Subaru caught his breath and commanded Patrasche to charge straight at the madman's back. If he could at least deal enough damage with a body-check to keep from killing him——

"—You're being slothful, aren't you, DESU?"

"—Ngah!?"

But that momentum, driven purely by panic, was nothing more than an event already within Petelgeuse's grasp.

Stretching his shadow, an Unseen Hand approaching straight from behind — outside Subaru's field of view — seized the tail of Patrasche, who had been riding the wind at full speed, and hurled the Earth Dragon's massive body effortlessly into the air.

After a sensation of weightlessness in which his whole body was battered by tremendous force and wind, Subaru fell while being tumbled through the air so violently he lost all sense of up, down, front, or back — and slammed back-first into the grass.

"Gh— hah."

His whole body creaked audibly, sparks scattered before his eyes, and his vision went pitch black.

The impact ran from his slammed back straight into his innards, his convulsing lungs refused oxygen, and vomit rising up his throat burned hot on the way.

And a beat behind all that, a ringing in his head and a pain like being struck by lightning tore through his limbs, and a groan of agony spilled from the throat of the writhing Subaru.

— It hurts, this is bad, this is awful, it hurts, it hurts, bad, bad!

With his numbed limbs and convulsing organs, his consciousness dominated by the pain of it all, Subaru's sluggish mind kept listing off those danger signals like a curse.

He needed to make some snap decision and act, and yet it wouldn't take shape within him as any certain meaning, any coherent form.

Pushing aside reason, cast into neglect, only instinct alone kept insisting.

Move or die. I don't want to die. I have to do something to live.

But even wanting to obey instinct, his body refused to move, and all Subaru could do was repeat ragged breaths, barely keeping himself alive. And then——

"—O-oh?"

"What diligence, DESU..."

Subaru let out a groan of incomprehension at the sudden sensation, while Petelgeuse, watching the result unfold, let a look of respect cross his face as he murmured in admiration.

Wondering just what had happened, his whole body tossed about by a sensation of floating and shaking, Subaru turned his head and realized—

— Patrasche had clamped her jaws around his waist and was trying to pull them both free of Petelgeuse's demonic hands.

Not even sparing the time to set him back on her saddle, the jet-black Earth Dragon worried only for the safety of her immobilized master. Saved by that unwavering loyalty, Subaru succeeded in fleeing from his confrontation with Petelgeuse.

Petelgeuse, too, seemed genuinely moved by Patrasche's judgment in protecting her rider without any command, and tears began streaming from eyes he covered with both palms.

"An Earth Dragon... oh, an Earth Dragon! How splendid, DESU! Love, love, loveloveloveloveloveloveloveloveloveloveloveloveloveloveloveloveaaaaaahhhh iiiiiiiiii!!"

Weeping torrents of tears, Petelgeuse stamped his feet in a frenzy, spinning in place. He turned his palm toward the back of the retreating Earth Dragon — yet not to attack, his fingers pointed upward, as if reaching out toward something beloved slipping away,

"Love! Love supreme! Love unconditional! Love unstained! How splendid a thing, DESU! How beautiful a thing! Worrying only for her master, running with single-minded devotion — what a remarkable will, what a remarkable resolve, what a remarkable way of being, what a remarkable way of loving, DESU! Ohh, ohh, ohhh! Diligence, DESU! You truly are a diligent devotee of love! Ahh, ahh, magnificent!!"

The madman, moved to ecstasy, was lost entirely in his own solo performance, with no interest whatsoever in the escape itself.

And so Subaru's body was carried on, racing across the grassland, circling around the battlefield to the rear. There, Ferris remained, tending to the wounded,

"Subaru-kyun!? Did they get you!? Are you dead?"

"Barely... I'm alive. Everything hurts like hell, but thanks to Patrasche... wait, oof."

Subaru answered the harsh greeting with a wry smile. The moment he reached Ferris's side, his body was flung roughly onto the grass.

Face-planting into the ground, the taste of leaves and dirt mixing in his mouth, Subaru was left dumbfounded. Then, meaning to voice at least one complaint, he turned back toward Patrasche——

"Hey, if you're gonna carry me all the way here, you could at least be a little more—"

Faced with the wretched sight of the Earth Dragon, his throat froze solid.

When she had been hurled by the Unseen Hand, Patrasche had been seized by the tail. The portion corresponding to her tail had been torn off at the root, and a tremendous amount of blood had poured out, still staining the grassland red even now. Beyond that, her flank had been gouged open in two places, and — perhaps from when she'd been thrown and slammed into the ground — her left foreleg was crushed to a pulp from the tip inward.

No question about it — she was, quite literally, wounded from head to toe.

Whether it was carrying Subaru all the way back that used up her last reserves, Patrasche let her huge body sink down onto the grassland with a rumble that shook the ground. She simply lay there, repeating shallow, faint breaths as though already dead.

Without thinking, Subaru clung to her body,

"Hey! No way... Patrasche! You, you... this is because of my— my bad call, this happened because... hey!"

Even as he called to her and touched her with his hand, there was no response from Patrasche. If anything, her breathing grew shallower by the moment, her life force fading further and further.

"F-Ferris! Patrasche is... please, save her..."

"This girl carried out her duty to protect you, admirably. Just be proud of that much for her."

To Subaru, who turned and pleaded in a trembling voice, Ferris said this in a sharp, decisive tone.

For a moment, Subaru couldn't grasp the meaning of that answer. It wasn't the answer he wanted. It sounded, for all the world, like he was being told to give up on Patrasche.

"That Earth Dragon lived her life out fully. Neither you nor I can save everything. You understand that, don't you?"

Ferris cut the words off flatly, so bluntly that Subaru, in that moment, felt it as outright cruelty. He started to protest that attitude, but he didn't know what he should even say.

All he had, in his hands, was a sense of loss — as if Patrasche, whom he was still touching, were somehow growing distant and blurred——

"Besides, now that you've pulled out... the Sin Archbishop is on the move!"

"—!"

Turning his face toward that voice, Subaru saw an Unseen Hand stretching out toward the battlefield.

In the path of that palm were two knights, uncontaminated and still fighting hard — an old knight and a young one — but,

"You two, look out——!"

Before his voice could reach them, the palm had already torn the heads off the two knights at the neck.

Blood sprayed, the heads seized in that black palm hung suspended in midair, their expressions frozen in shock — and in the next instant, they were crushed like fruit. A beat later, their bodies collapsed, convulsing, to a peal of raucous laughter.

"Now! Now! Now now now now now now now!! This is the beginning of the end, DESU! It shall end, DESU! Let the end end the end, and offer up everything, DESU! I am right now deeply moved, moved to tears, moved to rapture, DESU! And so, with my own hands, filled with love, I wish to grant each of you a peaceful end, DESU!"

Petelgeuse's raucous laughter and self-serving proclamations wouldn't stop.

Subaru could see countless Unseen Hands stretching from the shadow, selecting their prey as if licking their lips over it.

Wilhelm and the others braced themselves too, wary of the invisible threat, but against unseen demonic hands whose very touch meant death — there could be no means of defense.

"What... do I do..."

Desperately, Subaru turned his mind over and over.

In this situation, Subaru was the only one who grasped what was happening. If anyone could pull off some scheme to turn things around, it had to be him.

And yet, no matter how hard he racked his brain until it felt like it was boiling, no answer came — worse, half of his awareness was still being pulled toward Patrasche, whose life was even now slipping away.

— What do I do, what do I do, what do I do, whatdoIdowhatdoIdowhatdoIdowhatdoIdowhatdoIdo doooooooo.

His mind spun so fast it felt like it was scorching, and yet nothing but a rasping breath came out of him.

In a situation on the verge of making him despair at himself, without waiting for any decision on their part, Petelgeuse raised both hands as if declaring time up,

"—Hm!?"

On instinct, the small body leapt backward, and an instant later Mana converged on the spot Petelgeuse had just occupied — a spike of ice burst upward from directly below, narrowly failing to finish off the madman.

"Just who is that, DESU!?"

Raising his voice, Petelgeuse spun around and looked up toward the hilltop.

Drawn along, everyone present followed his gaze and noticed the figure there.

The wind blew, silver hair streaming in it, and a beautiful girl stood with the sunlight at her back.

Out of breath, sweat beading on her brow, and yet her violet eyes had lost none of their strength,

"—That's far enough, villain."

Her voice, clear as a silver bell, carried a sharp, resolute ring on the grassland wind.

At the sight of her, Petelgeuse's eyes widened in delight, and Subaru's eyes widened in dismay.

At his own bitter regret for letting the girl who must never meet the madman come face to face with him after all, Subaru's throat filled with despair.

— The endgame drew near. With no glimmer of hope in sight, the end was closing in.

- 男の娘 (describing Ferris) is an idiom for a male character with a girlish appearance; rendered as "the half-naked boy who looked every bit a girl" rather than transliterated, to keep it natural in English.
