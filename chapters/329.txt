Arc 5 Chapter 13: "A Peaceful Dinner"

The garden on the grounds of the Water's Feathered Robe Inn was, in keeping with the ryokan atmosphere, laid out in the style of a Japanese garden, its ground carpeted with gravel.
It couldn't quite boast a pond or a shishi-odoshi water clacker, but the arrangement of stepping stones and the bamboo-like plants growing among them were more than enough to earn some appreciation.

"Guess Wilhelm-san still wouldn't come down after all, huh."

Sitting on the veranda overlooking the garden, Subaru toyed with the gravel with the tip of his toe.
What idly came to mind as he murmured this was the apologetic face of the white-haired old man. Having declined Subaru's invitation, what was Wilhelm doing in his room right about now?
He'd be bored all alone until dinner, surely.

"Then again, he's not the type to come watch something just to kill time, no matter how you slice it."

"Said that way, it makes those of us who did come at your invitation sound like we're just eccentrics."

"I wouldn't call it good taste, no argument there. Myself included."

Subaru aimed the jab sideways at Julius, who sat elegantly cross-legged beside him on the same veranda. Julius laughed and nodded, "True enough," at Subaru's words, but the one who couldn't so readily agree was seated on Subaru's other side, opposite Julius.

"Aah, that's so mea-ea-ean. Feri-chan didn't come because she wa-anted to, y'know? Subaru-kyun just insisted, said it'd be a problem if something happened, so she got called along, that's a-all."

"Well, I did feel a little bad about it. But just in case, having you around means we could get away with more reckless stuff. Not that it turned out to be necessary, apparently."

Subaru answered Ferris's grumbling, the cat ears twitching, with one eye closed. The eye he kept open was fixed straight ahead, on the high-speed bout unfolding in the garden.
Frankly, it was a level of skill Subaru's eyes could barely follow at all—but even so, there was one thing he could say for certain.

"Reinhardt really is a monster, that guy."

"It's hard to deny, which is precisely the difficulty—though it's not the sort of adjective I'd care to have a friend use about me."

"Can't deny it though, that's the sad pa-art."

Before the eyes of the three, sharing this common impression, unfolded a sight that only confirmed the verdict.

Facing off on the gravel-strewn garden, trading blows, were a golden-haired boy baring his fangs and snarling, and a red-haired hero deftly parrying his ferocious animal pressure.
The challenger, Garfiel, hurled himself at Reinhardt with every spring in his body, moving with truly free, unrestrained mobility in all directions. But Reinhardt read every claw, every fang, every kick, every elbow and knee bearing down on him, and continued to dodge them all with unhurried ease.
And what's more—

"That guy hasn't moved a single step from that spot this whole time, has he?"

"Just as the original terms stipulated. There's no chance Reinhardt would break them. Though I imagine it's precisely his inability to make Reinhardt break them that galls Garfiel most."

Garfiel struck high, then low, trying to break through some gap in Reinhardt's guard—but every attack found no gap to exploit, because none existed, and worse, each was effortlessly deflected, throwing Garfiel's own stance into disarray.

Reinhardt still hadn't moved from the spot where this reckless challenge had begun.
He kept both feet planted right there, parrying every one of Garfiel's attacks without once shifting his stance.

—When Garfiel had first come to the guest room and declared his challenge to Reinhardt, Subaru had judged it reckless from any number of angles.

To begin with, he hadn't thought Reinhardt would even rise to Garfiel's provocation.
Frankly, it was Garfiel being selfish, and there was nothing in it for Reinhardt to accept. Reinhardt didn't seem the type to care about something as manly yet childish as who was stronger between the two of them—that was part of the reasoning too.
Given the complicated relationship between the two as knights of rival factions, seen with a cynical eye, Garfiel's challenge could even become unnecessary kindling. Of course, Subaru trusted Reinhardt wasn't the sort to play such petty games, but the list of reasons against the challenge going forward was long.
On those grounds, Subaru had more or less resigned himself to the idea that this match would never actually happen.

Still, if he was honest with himself, there was a part of him that wanted to see it happen.

Garfiel Tinzel was, without question, the single greatest fighting force the Emilia Camp possessed. Of course, victory or defeat in combat depends on countless conditions and matchups, and an unbroken record of victory isn't easily built. Garfiel had plenty of weaknesses too.
Even so, within the group the Emilia Camp had built up over this past year, Garfiel himself was the backbone of its military strength.
Everyone in the camp acknowledged it, and Garfiel himself surely took pride in it. He'd earned that much through the results and achievements he'd kept producing.

And yet, alongside that thought, there was a complicated issue with Garfiel as well.
Namely, that since leaving the Sanctuary, he'd only once faced an opponent who matched him in strength.
The only time Garfiel had fought a true head-on, all-out battle was against the murderer Elsa, when she'd attacked the old Roswaal Manor. And having ultimately overwhelmed even her, Garfiel hadn't since faced anything that could really be called a hard-fought struggle in any chance he'd had to use his strength.

Certainly, Subaru and the others had pooled their full strength to defeat Garfiel once, but that had only worked because they'd resorted to underhanded tactics to scrape together the conditions for a win.
If anything, restricted to the condition of a straight, head-on fight, Garfiel Tinzel was a man who had never once known defeat since the day he was born.

—And so, knowing full well how cruel it was, Subaru had desperately wanted this fight between Reinhardt and Garfiel to happen.

If Garfiel could reach the summit without ever knowing defeat, that would be fine too.
But it was far too precarious for him to remain the greatest fighting force there was, never knowing what the summit even looked like, resting only on the luck of having fought nothing but opponents weaker than himself.

So Subaru had decided to place his trust in the strength of the hero whose fighting he'd witnessed only once.
The strength of Reinhardt van Astrea.

"And I did trust it... but I never expected the gap to be this big."

Things had gone exactly as he'd hoped, beyond his wildest expectations—and yet, faced with the result, all Subaru could manage was less astonishment than sheer disbelief.

When the fired-up Garfiel had dragged him along to visit Reinhardt's room, Reinhardt had cheerfully agreed to their rude request. He'd agreed so readily it was almost anticlimactic.
And then, when Garfiel tried to suggest they take it outside the city to avoid any damage, Reinhardt had said with a smile, "The garden should be plenty of space. I'll have a word with the inn staff so we don't tear up the grounds too badly."

From Reinhardt's side, it had surely been an innocent remark—but as a provocation aimed at Garfiel, it ranked among the finest.
Indeed, upon hearing Reinhardt's proposal, Garfiel had radiated a killing intent so fierce that even Subaru, standing beside him, grew uneasy, his surging fury aimed squarely at Reinhardt like a blade.

They'd headed down to the ryokan's garden, set a few rules—no weapons, no bout that left injuries, no use of dangerous Divine Protections—and the match had gone ahead.
In the meantime, Subaru had called Ferris in case either of them got hurt, and asked Julius and Wilhelm along as well, to commentate on the fight itself. Unfortunately, Wilhelm had declined the moment he'd heard the gist of it, but Julius and Ferris were both here now, watching over the match. Otto, incidentally, still hadn't come back.

"Didn't invite the ladies, or Mimi's brothers, though."

"A wise choice, I think. Anastasia-sama shows no mercy where this sort of thing is concerned. If she'd learned we were starting this, she'd have turned it into a spectacle for profit in no time. And if word reached Hetaro and the others, there's no doubt Mimi would have made a fuss about it."

Eyes still on the fight, Julius agreed with Subaru's words. Even so, his attention was clearly fixed on the battle before him, and he looked, if anything, like a man whose blood was up.

And so, with this small handful of spectators gathered, the fight had begun.
At the start, Garfiel had probably been thinking something along these lines:

—Make him regret running his mouth like that, and drag him somewhere he'd have to go all out.

However spacious the ryokan's garden might be, that was only true in the general sense of a garden meant for viewing.
Trying to cut loose and fight there, it obviously wasn't nearly enough space. And on top of that, Reinhardt had added the condition of not tearing up the grounds.
What was so strange about taking that as overconfidence?

So of course, as a boy, it was only natural to think he'd make Reinhardt regret it.
And yet, now that the lid had come off, what was the result?

"Hey, Julius. Mind if I ask you just one thing?"

"Not limited to just one—ask whatever you like. Whether I answer is another matter."

"Don't be a smartass about it. That's exactly why I don't like you."

Trading their usual banter, Subaru propped his chin on his hand, eyes still forward.
Then, after searching for the right words for a moment, he said:

"From where you're standing, what do you make of Garfiel?"

"—He's strong. I'd heard the rumors, but the name of the twin pillars guarding Emilia-sama isn't for show. Since I'd heard one of the pair was you, I'll admit I'd set my psychological expectations rather low."

"I'll deck you."

"He's strong. His skill is the real thing. If it came down to blade against blade alone, even I couldn't say for certain I'd win. And he still has plenty of room left to grow. Without question, if he breaks through his own shell, he's the kind of talent who could stake a claim among the very best in the martial world."

There was a quiet excitement in Julius's forceful declaration, born of having glimpsed such potential. Even in his eyes, the talent slumbering inside Garfiel was the real thing.
And along with it, he carried an unbearable envy at seeing it in someone else.
Naturally so. Julius, too, walked the path of the blade, and was, above all, a man.

"Bu-ut, no matter how bright that future waiting for Gar-nyan might be, sadly for now all he can do is get played with like that, meow."

So Ferris said, giving voice to the bitter reality.
But no one spoke up to deny it. Everyone watching understood it completely. And none understood it better than Garfiel himself.

Garfiel, who might someday reach for a place among the very strongest.
But for now, faced with the current strongest in the world, all he could do was flail without laying a hand on him, toyed with until the end.

"——!"

"So close. You rushed that."

Garfiel overextended on a big swing, and the Sword Saint slipped inside his guard.
His body, seized by the arm, was flipped high into the air and thrown down mercilessly onto the gravel, back first.

Dust kicked up, and Garfiel, gasping at the impact, lay sprawled on his back on the ground.
When Reinhardt's hand came to rest on his forehead, Garfiel went still, as if his entire body had been sealed in place, and let out one long, long breath.

"I lose."

Before anyone else could say it and make it humiliating, he'd been able to admit it himself.
Just being able to do that much, Garfiel had managed to preserve his pride, at least in part.

Subaru could only hope, at the very least, that it offered some small comfort.

—Garfiel did not show his face at the dinner table.

"Why'd you all go and do somethin' that fun-lookin' without tellin' me?"

Glancing over the men gathered in the tatami room, Anastasia let out a resentful complaint.
She wasn't in her familiar white fur-trimmed dress, but in a yukata, her damp, glossy purple hair swept back over her shoulders. Her fair skin held a faint flush, and there was a subtle allure about her that didn't quite match her otherwise youthful appearance.

"I thought you might say something like that, which is precisely why I avoided inviting you this time. It seemed as though you and everyone else were busy deepening your friendships."

Julius, wincing with a wry smile, was the one who sat down beside her the moment they returned. The men had just finished tidying up after the match in the garden and come back.
At his words, Anastasia's pretty face took on a grin like a miser eyeing a coin.

"Heh. My knight's as smooth-talkin' as ever, I see. It's not like I try'n turn everything into money, y'know? It's just that Kararagi spirit of mine, wantin' to make a big fuss with everyone over somethin' that looks fun."

"Please don't, our strongest shield's heart is wounded enough as it is. Ah, if he sulks with his head in his hands for a night, I figure he'll bounce back fine, so just treat him normal when that happens."

Subaru made this request on behalf of his heartbroken little brother figure, in response to Anastasia's words. Everyone present nodded along, seemingly in agreement. However—

"Let me guess. This useless knight here went easy on nobody again and bullied the poor kid, right? Tough break, bro."

Felt, having heard the gist of the story, grinned at Subaru, flashing her canine tooth. Her hand smacked away at the shoulder of Reinhardt seated beside her, and the red-haired knight gave a wry smile.

"Felt-sama. Phrased that way, it invites misunderstanding. And the match earlier wasn't at all a case of me one-sidedly tormenting him. There were several moments where I myself broke into a cold sweat—it was a fulfilling bout, truly."

"Not real convincing when I've seen how you drill Gaston and the others day to day. You never let up on 'em just 'cause you say they got potential."

"I couldn't do something so disrespectful as hold back against anyone. And I don't think so highly of my own abilities that I'd assume I could hold back and still produce results."

Felt, faced with Reinhardt's resolute answer, snorted and lost interest. Honestly, it was an exchange that left Subaru with plenty of doubts about whether this master-servant relationship was really working, but more than that, it was Reinhardt's words that struck deep in Subaru's chest.

Having actually watched the fight in the garden, it was obvious that Reinhardt's words just now had been said out of consideration for Garfiel. And yet, there was nothing in the ring of Reinhardt's words that suggested mere hollow, hypocritical flattery.
No doubt, Reinhardt had simply spoken his true feelings. Despite saying something that could easily be taken as sarcasm, there was a genuine sincerity to it that made you believe otherwise.
Perhaps that, of all things, was the most unsettling thing about him.

"By the way, Felt-sama, about your attire—"

"What, you got a complaint? We all took a big bath together with everyone else just now. And everybody's changed into these, so I did too. Don't you dare say somethin' lame like it's unladylike."

"No, not at all. I only meant to say it suits you very well."

"Ugh, annoying."

The praise offered by the Knight among Knights, who commanded the respect and trust of so much of the kingdom's people—the kind of bouquet any woman would envy—Felt swatted away with a face of genuine displeasure. Between that and the way she'd thrown her yukata on all sloppily, she gave off a serious air of having given up on being ladylike altogether.

—And indeed, just as Felt's words implied, it seemed the women had been washing off their sweat in the large communal bath while Reinhardt and Garfiel's match was underway.

As a result, every woman who'd shown her face at the tatami-room dinner was in a yukata.
Not just Anastasia and Felt, but Mimi and Crusch. And Emilia, and for some reason Beatrice too, all in yukata.

"Bea-ko, when'd you get around to a bath..."

"After Subaru left Betty behind and went to the garden, Betty was walking the inn alone and got caught by Emilia. Betty said she didn't need to, but she made her anyway, I suppose."

Beatrice, looking away, was dressed in a pale blue yukata.
On Beatrice, so thoroughly Western in her beauty, the functional elegance of the yukata suited her strangely well. Strange, too, was the fact that although her hair hadn't fully dried, it somehow still kept its usual vertical curls. Even grabbing a lock and tugging on it, it bounced back with a slightly heavier air than usual.

"So says Beatrice—but what actually happened?"

"Eh? Subaru came to me looking all lonely, saying Beatrice was being cold to him, so I invited her to come join me in the bath I'd already been invited to. I think Beatrice had fun too, though..."

"Th-that's fabricated, I suppose! That's a fabrication, I suppose! Which of us is Subaru even planning to believe, Betty or Emilia!"

"That's pretty much a confession right there, you know."

Weighing Beatrice's stubborn denial against Emilia's all-too-honest testimony, the verdict came out plain and simple. Beatrice hung her head in frustration, and Emilia, watching her, laughed happily. She too was in a yukata, her long silver hair, still damp from the bath, tied back in one bundle behind her.
The glimpse of her pale white nape it offered up was, he had to admit, magnificent.

"Subaru, you're breathing kinda hard—you feverish or somethin'?"

"Bit of a fever of the heart, from love. Emilia-tan, can I braid your hair?"

"Sure, but I think dinner's gonna start any second. Maybe do it after?"

Subaru had reached out to touch the end of her bundled hair, and Emilia gestured at the table, suggesting he wait. Reluctantly, Subaru withdrew his hand, only to notice everyone around them giving him odd looks.
Subaru cocked his head and, for lack of anything better, called out to Felt sitting across from him, "What?"

"Somethin' weird about that?"

"You and sis got some real confusin' distance thing goin' on. For all that, there's no romantic mood between you two, and last I saw, you two were on pretty rough terms besides."

"What's this about it being weird for a man and woman our age to be all lovey-dovey without romantic mood? Also, please don't go digging up what happened at the royal capital, it hurts my heart."

Even as he replied, Subaru couldn't entirely disagree with what Felt was getting at.
He was well aware that over this past year, he'd served as Emilia's knight in extremely close proximity to her—but he didn't actually feel that the distance between them as a man and woman had shrunk because of it.
If anything, he almost felt his awareness of her as a woman, and himself as a man, had actually diminished compared to before their master-servant relationship had been established.

That was because Emilia's mental age had become clear to him.
Emilia's emotional maturity hadn't yet grown to the point of being able to properly accept Subaru's feelings as those between a man and a woman. Because of that, Subaru's way of interacting with her had naturally adjusted to match.
His affection hadn't faded—it was more that the way he approached the one receiving it had changed. He figured this would stay the same until Emilia's own awareness changed. At the very least, he understood that no amount of getting ahead of himself on Subaru's part would fix anything.

"If I had to compare it to something, I guess it's closer to the feeling you've got with Crusch-san."

"The relationship between the two of us, you mean?"

Crusch, hand at her chin, tilted her head in puzzlement at Subaru's idly muttered comment.
Naturally, she too had joined the women's bath, and was now in a yukata. The feminine curves that hadn't been visible in men's dress now clearly asserted their presence through the thin fabric of the yukata.
Her beauty unchanged, but stripped of her usual dignified sharpness, Crusch now had a noticeably more vulnerable, ladylike air about her, and even in a yukata properly fitted with Ferris's guidance, there was something alluring about her.

Meeting the eyes of a Crusch grown more innocent, Subaru rubbed under his nose with a finger.

"Yeah. Ferris is crazy attached to you too, Crusch-san, but when it comes to that man-woman kind of feeling, he doesn't push it on you regardless of how you feel about it. My situation started with an ulterior motive so the conditions are a little different maybe, but the way you treat someone you like, I figure there's something similar there."

"Put that way, it's rather embarrassing to hear. Fufu. Isn't it, Ferris?"

"Feri-chan's got a full-blown ulterior motive for Crusch-sama though, meow."

"——"

For a moment, the air in the tatami room froze at Ferris's remark.

Crusch's expression stiffened, still holding the smile from a moment before, while Ferris gazed at her, beaming from ear to ear. Incidentally, Ferris too, at some point, had changed into a yukata, and looked every bit as good in it as the other women there—which was somehow irritating.
In any case, that was beside the point right now.

"Sorry for digging up a secret nobody needed dug up. Right, let's get some food going, shall we."

"Don't dig up a bomb and then run away from it like that!"

Crusch, eyes welling up, clung pleadingly at Subaru as he tried to get the meal brought in.
It might have been a bolt from the blue for her, but Subaru hadn't wanted to find an unexploded bomb either. As Subaru's eyes wandered, wondering what to do—

"Ferris. You shouldn't startle Crusch-sama like that. I've noticed you've been showing more of a mean streak than usual of late."

So spoke Wilhelm, breaking his silence and completely changing the mood of the room.
The old man was, incidentally, the only man in the room dressed in a yukata. It seemed he'd finished his bath at the same time as the women, and the overwhelming aura of Japanese refinement he'd carried even while sitting seiza extended just as well to his yukata.
All he'd have needed was a single sword at his side to complete the picture.

"Aw, even Gramps Wil's gonna say it like tha-at?"

"Reverence, affection, romantic love. Among a master and servant sharing this room, it's only natural to hold no small fondness for one another. To needlessly conceal your true feelings for the sake of stirring up confusion isn't childlike innocence, it's simply unbecoming. If you show no sign of reflecting on it, I may have to speak more sternly."

"Fnyaa-a. So strict, honestly."

Under the weight of Wilhelm's scolding, Ferris pursed her lips and gave a sign of surrender.
Leaning against the shoulder of a troubled-looking Crusch, she said:

"You don't need to be all wary like that, it was obviously a joke, meow. If Feri-chan actually had a full-blown ulterior motive for Crusch-sama lookin' like this, all sorts of problems would crop up, and that'd be a real hassle, meow."

"O-of course. Whew, that startled me. I thought—since I still can't quite make full use of my Divine Protection right now, maybe I'd been misreading Ferris's various feelings all along."

"—That's not true at all, you know."

Crusch let out a sigh of relief, but the emotion that flickered for just an instant in Ferris's eyes as she looked at her caught Subaru's attention.
That complicated emotion was, perhaps, a rare glimpse of hesitation that Ferris didn't normally show.

Over this past year, Ferris, too, had failed to bring about her master's recovery, same as Subaru.
In that time, she'd surely struggled just as Subaru had, tasted the same frustration, and still had to go on supporting a mistress who had lost her memories as any anchor to hold onto.

"The meal is ready, ma'am. Might we bring it in?"

Just as the conversation reached a lull, one of the ryokan staff called out. Joshua, who'd been shrinking into the corner all this while, gave the go-ahead, and one after another, the inn's staff began carrying in the food.
Everyone present looked with equal astonishment at the dishes lined up one after another across the long table.

Though, no doubt, the direction of that astonishment differed between everyone else and Subaru.
Emilia and the others were astonished by the array of unfamiliar dishes and cooking methods, while Subaru's astonishment came from seeing familiar things turn up in a place he never expected.

Since this world had no ocean, there was no surprise like a live-caught fish served whole—but even so, the shock of an assortment of raw sashimi didn't let Subaru off easy.

"Wait, you can just eat this as is?"

"Not somethin' you're used to, I take it? This kind of dish, you really can only get near water like this. The Water's Feathered Robe Inn even bills it as a signature dish."

The unconventional dishes weren't limited to sashimi alone—there were plenty of other dishes that looked like similar Japanese cuisine lined up as well. Amid everyone else's hesitation, Anastasia moved to take the first bite—but before she could, Subaru beat her to it.

He dipped a slice of some mystery fish's sashimi into a seasoning that resembled soy sauce and popped it into his mouth.
Beside him, Emilia and Beatrice both cried "Ah" in unison, but it was nothing to worry about. The concern about parasites only crossed his mind after he'd already eaten it, but given today's clientele and the host's evident consideration, it seemed unthinkable that a ryokan of this caliber wouldn't have accounted for that.
Which meant all that was left for Subaru to do was simply savor the taste.

"Mm, so good! Ah, nostalgic sashimi! Amazing! What a treat!"

"I-is it good?"

"Good doesn't even cover it, it's exquisite. Might be because it's so fresh, but even measured against my own standards, this ranks pretty high up there. If we had sushi vinegar and rice here, I could've shown off the Edomae nigiri skills I used to practice, y'know."

"Sorry. I don't really get what you just said. But okay, so it's good."

Letting most of Subaru's rapid-fire rambling wash right over her, Emilia followed his lead and tried a piece of sashimi with soy sauce herself. In an instant, her violet eyes went wide, and she pumped a clenched fist happily, going "Mmm!"

Seeing that honest reaction from master and knight both, everyone else, one by one, began digging into the meal as well.
Anastasia alone looked a little put out at having had her thunder stolen, but Subaru and Emilia's reactions had been so ideally genuine that her expression naturally softened, and she muttered "Well, can't be helped," before starting on her own meal.

Despite carrying a few worries, and one empty seat, into this dinner, in the end, every single person there was able to enjoy it together, warm and at ease.


—Tonight alone, it was as peaceful a time as if the moon, as if the whole world, had granted them a reprieve.
