Arc 4 Chapter 41: "Tiger"

An unguarded stance. Garfiel stood in a natural posture, both arms hanging loose at his sides.
Seeing him standing there, blocking their path, Subaru instinctively cast a wary glance around the surroundings. He was worried that someone besides Garfiel might be lying in ambush.
Still, the moment he confirmed the opponent was Garfiel, he half-judged that wariness to be unnecessary — because if Garfiel was coming, he would surely come alone.

Sure enough, within the range Subaru could sense, there was no one else around him. Having confirmed that his worry was nothing more than that, Subaru let out a breath, and while stroking the back of Patrasche's neck — she still hadn't dropped her guard — he said,

"Here I was thinking you'd show up without any foreshadowing, and now I'd appreciate it if you didn't go hitting on my partner so soon."

"I ain't got it in me to lie or flatter. Whatever I think, it just comes right outta my mouth. That's why the old hag and Ram are always naggin' at me for it."

At the light jab meant to feel him out, Garfiel clacked his fangs, then let the trace of a smile drop from his face as he looked up at Subaru. Facing that gaze with his one remaining eye, Subaru held up a single finger.

"I have to say, you being here is unnatural as hell. Mind explaining?"

"Ain't no big deal. I'm the fang of this Sanctuary, and you lot couldn't escape the Sanctuary's eye. That's the whole story. Well, guess you could call it a consolation prize."

Garfiel said this with a light wave of his hand, and Subaru's brow furrowed.
If what he called the "eye" meant the same kind of code-word as "grass" that Subaru imagined, then "eye" would mean something like a watcher. But —

"I haven't even heard from Ram that something like that exists in the Sanctuary..."

"You think the folks outside the Sanctuary know every damn thing about it? There's a mountain of stuff even that bastard Roswaal doesn't know. This is just one more thing on the pile. ...Dunno about you, though."

Garfiel's words were merciless toward Subaru, who trailed off in frustration. He snorted at Subaru's silence, then turned his gaze to the line of dragon carriages behind him.

"...Everyone back there — that all of you evacuatin'?"

"Ah, yeah, that's right. Hey, Garfiel. I know it's bad of us to sneak out without a word, but couldn't you just let this go? It's not a bad deal for you either, is it?"

"Ah?"

He was glared at with sharp eyes, but Subaru didn't let it stop him, gesturing behind himself with a hand.

"Right now, getting these people who were basically hostages out of the Sanctuary is to avoid the chance of more conflict happening inside. I've heard there've already been a few skirmishes here and there — the point is to act before it gets serious."

"――――"

"Your position is the same as Ryuzu-san's, right? You want the Sanctuary freed. Leaving a spark like this inside can't be convenient for you. There's more benefit in letting us go."

Keeping his voice quiet, Subaru tried his best to persuade Garfiel. To begin with, what Subaru was saying wasn't just a stopgap excuse. In truth, from Garfiel's standpoint, this evacuation should be something largely in his favor.
Setting aside the part about doing it quietly, in secret—

"Whether or not your personality lets you tolerate that is a separate matter, but just this once, swallow it and—"

"Oi, ain't you got somethin' mixed up?"

"Mixed up?"

"You've gone and assumed from the start that I came here to get in your way. Like you said, I got no reason to stop this evacuation. So all them excuses and sweet talk — total waste, I'm tellin' you."

Cutting off Subaru as he tried to add more, Garfiel snorted as if mocking his misapprehension. Subaru fell silent at the response. He'd been proceeding on the assumption that Garfiel's appearance meant bad news, but logically speaking, what Garfiel said was correct. Still—

"If that's the case, then what exactly did you come here to—"

"Somethin' like a send-off, I guess. You lot are free to leave, but if nobody from the Sanctuary properly sees you off, it ain't no different from sneakin' off in the night. If I can say I watched you all leave, I can shut the others up too."

"...You think about a lot more than I expected."

Half-surprised that he possessed the reasoning and intellect to logically win him over, Subaru accepted Garfiel's explanation. Garfiel folded his arms and nodded at Subaru's slightly rude remark.

"'Course I do. I ain't just strong — I can be considerate and thoughtful too, on top of everythin' else... Man, I'm somethin' else."

"Ah, that response actually put me at ease a bit. And since I'm relieved, here's something for you — I'll refrain from specially mentioning this right eye."

"Ah? Ah, right. You noticed 'cause your blindfold came off. Somebody out there doin' unnecessary things. Oi, you hear that."

Subaru pointed to his own right eye at Garfiel's self-praise. Garfiel, while referencing Subaru's lost eye, directed his attention toward Otto, who had helped rescue Subaru. Sitting in the driver's seat of the dragon carriage behind them, Otto shrank his neck, trying to hide even a little from that sharp gaze.
Cracking his neck at that timid posture, Garfiel continued with, "Still, though—"

"You lost an eye, and yet you're actin' awful calm about it. Honestly, I figured you for grudges and payback."

"If I started with the grudges, I'd have enough to talk till dawn, but I don't have the time, and even if I wanted payback, if I threw a punch you'd probably just crush my other eye too. No thanks on that déjà vu."

"The hell's that mean? — I don't like it, oi."

Garfiel looked displeased with Subaru's response. But Subaru judged there was no need for further exchange, gently stroking his right eye with his palm.

"So it's fine if I just leave you be and send everyone back to the village?"

"Just wanted to fix the bad look of you sneakin' off without a word, that's all. Do what you want."

"Then, if you'll allow me..."

"—However."

Cutting into the voice that was about to set the line of dragon carriages moving, Garfiel leaned his upper body forward slightly. Then, from below at an angle, he glared up with sharp eyes at Subaru astride Patrasche.

"You ain't goin'. Stay behind. The hostages, I'll let through. The noisy older brother too. Ram... well, I'll let her through too. But you ain't goin'."

"...And the reason for that is?"

"Partly's got to do with Emilia-sama's motivation for the Trial, but the biggest problem is your existence. A guy reekin' this much of witch — no way I'm just lettin' you waltz outside."

"That again, huh..."

Flicking his finger against his nose, the blond young man intimidated Subaru. Growing weary of his by-now-familiar complaint, Subaru nodded, figuring if that was the condition, so be it.

"The condition is I stay in the Sanctuary. No objections, right?"

"Bein' honest and understandin' — that's a good thing. If you go draggin' this out any longer, my head can't keep up with rememberin' it all."

"Man, you're refreshingly blunt about it... Fine. I'll go tell everyone in back, so wait here."

Once they cut to the heart of the matter, the negotiation ended quickly. Though it wasn't much of a negotiation to begin with. Subaru swallowed his words as they were, then turned back to inform the main group — the drivers, Otto, and Ram.

"So basically, if I stay behind, we get to pass through safely. Arguing and dragging this out would be pointless, so I figured we should just accept the offer, but..."

"If we can pass through unhindered, that'd be a wonderful condition, I'd like to say — but will the people in back accept that? To begin with, these are people who were being so stubborn they said they'd refuse to evacuate without you, Natsuki-san."

"Ah, yeah, that persuasion might be tough. ...No, but wait, now that we've started moving, everyone's hoping we can make it back to the village like this. If it's just an accident like me stepping out midway, I think their hope will win out and they'll swallow it."

In response to Otto's concern, Subaru mulled it over with a hand on his chin.
In truth, the evacuees' desire to return to the village was surely stronger than anything else. It was gratifying that they'd weigh that feeling against Subaru's safety, but now that things had come this far, he thought it would be difficult for the scales to balance.

"Natsuki-san..."

"Well, since I've got that kind of thinking behind it, I don't think persuading them will be too hard. But this is something I really ought to tell them myself. I'll go over real quick, so everyone get ready to move the carriages right away..."

"I don't like it."

Ram suddenly cut in on Subaru as he crisply began giving instructions.
Combined with Otto's worried glance, Subaru felt somewhat deflated as he turned his gaze to her, scratching his head with a "come on now."

"Garfiel just said something similar to me, but if you keep saying things that sap a person's will like that—"

"Barusu, haven't you noticed yourself? What you're saying right now."

"What am I saying?"

Subaru tilted his head at Ram's words, but no matter how much he pondered, nothing came to mind. Not understanding what she was getting at, confusion showed on his furrowed brow.
Seeing that, Ram let out a sigh as if disappointed.

"If you don't understand, never mind. I suppose I understand what Roswaal-sama meant, then. If it comes to that, everything Ram has done will have been for nothing, I wonder?"

"Wait, what are you even talking about, all of a sudden. Do you know something too? You know something, and that's why you're standing here like this—"

"None of it means anything to Barusu right now. It would be a waste of time."

"You—!"

Subaru ground his teeth at Ram's knowing, condescending tone. Just as the two of them threatened to fall into a tense standoff, Otto cut in with a "Now now now!"

"Let's not have the two of you argue. Just as Ram-san said, it's truly a waste of time. A waste of time means a waste of a chance to earn money. Let's settle this for my sake, just this once. Let's do that, yes, settled!"

"Tch. In any case, I'm going to explain things."

"Tch. Ram has nothing more to say."

"Could you two please stop clicking your tongues at me while you say that?!"

Otto grumbled at his usual treatment, but that was simply his role, so it couldn't be helped. Thanks to Otto, the group managed to avoid splitting apart, and Subaru went around showing his face at each dragon carriage, explaining the exchange as it had happened.
Everyone from Arlam Village showed a sour expression at the condition that Subaru remain behind, but when he brought up that he himself had accepted it, and that returning would only prolong their life as evacuees, they reluctantly, if unwillingly, agreed.

Grateful for their reluctance to let him go, Subaru finished persuading everyone. Returning to the earlier group to report this, he then faced Garfiel astride Patrasche once more.

"Talks over here are done. Your condition stands. Everyone gets to pass through."

"'Cept you, yeah. Get 'em movin' already. That Earth Dragon — stayin' behind too?"

"Walking back would be too tough physically. Sorry, Patrasche, I guess this means you'll have to put up with being cramped a little longer."

Placing a hand on the back of his partner, who was going to be dragged along with him, the trembling Patrasche turned her neck away as if to say, "You don't need to worry about that, geez."
Interpreting his partner's motion that way, Subaru stood alongside Garfiel, watching off the procession of dragon carriages one after another as they made their way down the road out of the Sanctuary. Waving with a wry smile at the villagers looking down at him from the carriage windows, their faces full of things unsaid, he said,

"Otto, when you get back to the village, don't show your face at the manor. If possible, just come straight back here."

"...? That's a confusing instruction, but why? I think it's essential to report to Frederica-san about all this."

"Just do it, no reason needed. Getting back to the village will probably be tomorrow morning at the earliest... but just to be extra safe, don't show your face there until at least past noon at the latest."

Otto's face showed incomprehension at Subaru's instruction. Without being able to clearly voice an answer to his doubts, Subaru jerked his chin, saying, "Just go."
Tonight was the fifth night that would decide fate — in other words, the final deadline for Elsa to take action against the manor. If it followed the pattern of the first loop, something had almost certainly already happened by now, timing-wise.
Even accounting for Elsa's recklessness, she probably wouldn't go so far as to come all the way down to the village to slaughter villagers. As long as they didn't step foot in the manor, Otto and the others should be safe.

Of course, that was nothing other than abandoning the safety of Frederica and Petra at the manor. And Beatrice, and Rem.

"...This time, I'm putting everything into figuring out what happens in the Sanctuary. Yeah, that's what I decided. If I get greedy and end up with nothing, there'd be no point in looking away in the first place."

The guilt rising in his chest at overlooking the worst-case scenario — Subaru forced it down with duty and obligation, and chastised his own cruel state of mind.
Steel. He had to become steel-hearted. To grasp the best possible future, he'd use every method available. Accepting and enduring sacrifices along the way, no matter how much it wore down his heart.

"If I can laugh at the end, that's my win... our win."

So before the sacrifice, he should just crush down his wavering heart.
For the sake of everything he could eventually reclaim, he mustn't hesitate to lay this groundwork. There was nothing left worth cherishing right now — or so he told himself.

"――――"

Watching all the dragon carriages pass by and vanish into the depths of the forest, Subaru let out a breath.
With this, he'd managed to create a state where only the Sanctuary's original residents and those connected to Roswaal Manor remained. All that was left was to wait for tomorrow morning like this, and confirm what would happen in the Sanctuary — that should accomplish the purpose of this loop.

"Staying here forever's just gonna get me bitten up worse by bugs, so let's head back. Being watched by you isn't exactly a great feeling either."

"Don't go orderin' me around. ...C'mon, speakin' of which, you ain't asked me nothin' about Emilia-sama's Trial tonight."

"I figured you being here was answer enough. And honestly, it wouldn't be a lie if I said I had a feeling this time might be tough."

At the very least, like with the hostages, Emilia mustn't rely on Subaru's safety as her motivation. For Emilia to overcome the Trial, a more fundamental change was needed. Or perhaps, given the current push to resolve the Sanctuary's problems quickly, that might not be something that could happen at all.

"That's also to figure out — whether I can wait for Emilia to break through the Trial or not, I can't even consider it without confirming what's going to happen first."

Trial and error looking ahead to the next attempt — in this third world, Subaru thought about the possibilities left to him.
Four times, at most — that was how many times Subaru had died within a single loop. If he intended to aim for a breakthrough in the fifth world, he could only afford to die once more.

"There's a mountain of things I want to confirm, though..."

—He'd already come to think of even his own "death" as nothing more than a foothold toward that breakthrough.
Not noticing the distortion in his own words, Subaru gave Patrasche the order to head back to the Sanctuary. Looking up from behind him, Garfiel—

"...Talkin' like you understand everything. How much do you actually know, huh?"

"Garfiel?"

Seeking the source of the muttering he hadn't quite caught, Subaru turned around atop the dragon. Right before Subaru's eyes, it happened, suddenly.

Garfiel leapt, aiming a horizontal knife-hand strike at Subaru's neck atop Patrasche, meaning to take his head off.

Burning into his mind the sight of fingertips closing in as they cut through the air, Subaru silently gasped in astonishment that death, without any sense of reality to it, had suddenly closed in before him.
It wasn't that he'd forgotten the possibility Garfiel might attack — but he never imagined he would actually inflict direct harm here, and—

"――――っ!"

There came a sharp sound like a blade swinging, and pain tore through Subaru's flesh, accompanied by bleeding. When he reflexively brought a hand to his throat as a groan escaped him, he realized his fingertip had shallowly gouged the area just below his Adam's apple.
Feeling blood drip from the gap between his pressed palm, Subaru instinctively worked the reins to give Patrasche instructions while—

"Garfiel! What are you—!"

"You gettin' in my way? What the hell's the meanin' of that, huh?"

Subaru cried out at the sudden violence atop the dragon, but Garfiel, having landed, shook his blood-wet fingertips and shouted in a different direction entirely.
Grimacing at the pain, Subaru looked that way and saw standing there a girl holding a small wand — pink hair swaying, sharp eyes glaring at Garfiel. Ram.

"Ram!?"

"Had a bad feeling, so I stayed behind to check, and things turned out exactly as expected. Barusu should be thanking Ram right now that his head is still attached to his body."

"If the wind hadn't thrown off my aim, his head woulda popped clean off."

Shaking his head, Garfiel affirmed Ram's presumptuous statement. Nearly losing his voice at the exchange, Subaru's mind burned hot with the pain of his wound.

"What're you — what were you trying to do, Garfiel! Were you trying to kill me just now?!"

"Botched it, though. If you're askin' what I was tryin' to do, ain't no answer but that."

Garfiel affirmed his own murderous intent with startling ease. Subaru's remaining eye went wide at those words, his lips trembling at an act he couldn't comprehend. Because—

"If that's the case, you could have killed me anytime, couldn't you?! While I was locked up, or even before that — if you'd just left me without treatment, I'd have died!"

"If I'd done that, the hostages inside might've blown up on me. Only after they were gone did the order line up right for me to go and kill you."

"That's just—!"

Feeling his thoughts dye themselves crimson, Subaru was crushed by Garfiel's words.
If Garfiel had been watching, biding his time like a tiger, searching for a moment when finishing off Subaru would cause no problems, then it would explain this send-off too, consistently. But even so, there was something else that didn't add up. And that was—

"If you kill me here, what happens to Emilia's Trial? Since I'm the core of that girl's motivation, if I die, without any conceit intended, the Trial won't end anymore."

That should be an utterly inconvenient outcome for Ryuzu's faction, which wanted the Sanctuary freed.
Even if Garfiel harbored some suspicion originating from the witch's scent on Subaru, this was too big a reason to just close his eyes to.
If he said he'd lost himself to rage, that would feel fitting for him — but looking at him now, capable of holding a conversation, it was self-evident that he hadn't lost his composure to the point of losing himself.

Which meant Garfiel had just attacked Subaru now on the basis of cold, calculated reasoning. What could that possibly mean—

"I'm—"

"There's no point listening to your excuses, and no point trying to persuade you either, Barusu."

But cutting off Garfiel's words as he tried to say something, Ram cut sharply between him and Subaru. Pointing the tip of her wand at Garfiel, who clicked his tongue, she said,

"Stop hiding your true feelings behind plausible-sounding logic, Garf. It doesn't suit you."

"O-oi, Ram."

"Step back, Ram will talk. — In any case, it seems Garf intends to kill Barusu."

At Ram affirming someone else's murderous intent, Subaru had no choice but to hold his tongue.
Keeping her wary eyes fixed on Garfiel, she walked over toward Subaru, and reaching out a hand, gently traced a finger under Patrasche's neck.

"Good girl. Do what needs to be done. The master sitting on your back seems to be terribly dense, both about himself and about others."

"――――"

At Ram's gentle, somehow tender words, Patrasche responded without a word. Answering only by licking the offered fingertip with her tongue, Patrasche lowered her head and, slowly, without waiting for Subaru's instruction, turned her feet toward the trees.

"Wh— wait. You two, what are you both—"

"Just don't let go of the reins. As long as you hold onto that much, that Earth Dragon will do everything in her power to protect Barusu. A story any man would be envious of."

"Listen to me! No, wait — let me hear it! What do you two know, that you're doing something like this!"

"There's no time to talk, and no point talking even if there were. It's as I said, Barusu. — Ram will buy you one minute, so in that gap, run as far as you can. That's the only defiance Ram can manage to give you."

Ram's reply, elusive to the very end. But this time, there really was no time to press her on it.
Before Subaru could hear the last of Ram's words, Patrasche let out a small whinny and broke into a run, faster still. Rocked by the momentum atop the dragon, Subaru felt the Wind Barrier Divine Protection deploy around him and clung to the back plunging into the cluster of trees.

"Ram――!"

He shouted. But there was no answer.
His view blocked by the crowd of trees, Subaru bit his lip at the situation carrying him away, left with no choice but to be tossed about.

With Patrasche carrying Subaru forward through the forest, in the now-empty space left behind, the two of them faced each other.

Watching Ram, who hadn't so much as flinched with her wand still raised, Garfiel pointed toward the forest where Subaru had disappeared.

"Sayin' unnecessary shit. Now chasin' after him's a real hassle."

"You think I'll let you go?"

"You think you can stop me? If you're thinkin' the balance of power between me and you ain't changed since back then, that's a huge mistake. Bein' in love with someone and not bein' willin' to force 'em down ain't the same thing."

Cracking his knuckles, he threatened her again. Still, Garfiel knew she wasn't the kind of girl to be intimidated by that. Indeed, Ram's expression didn't change in the slightest. Garfiel roughly scratched at his short blond hair.

"Ram, what the hell are you thinkin'? What's this supposed to accomplish? I ain't heard nothin' about this. Roswaal order you to do this?"

"...Sorry to disappoint, Garf, but Ram is here of her own will — Roswaal-sama's orders have nothing to do with it. At least, right now, there's no need to seek his instructions."

Ram's tone was resolute. And Garfiel's question stemmed from the same root of doubt as the one Subaru had asked. His brow furrowed at her answer, showing the same incomprehension.
Garfiel's face grew even more bitter.

"I don't get it, Ram. If you ain't followin' Roswaal's orders, then I get it even less — I can't think of a single reason for you to pull somethin' like this."

"Really?"

"Ah—?"

"Really — do you not understand why Ram is doing this, Garf?"

The question was quiet, and Ram's expression and tone, as she posed it, were no different than usual. But hearing it, receiving that gaze, Garfiel's expression changed.
Bewilderment. Suspicion. Astonishment. And then, fury.

"You—"

Garfiel took a step forward, his foot stamping the ground violently. Expressing his unbearable anger by gnashing his fangs, he glared at Ram with narrowed pupils.

"No way, but... you're sayin'... doin' somethin' like this is..."

"—For Frederica's sake. And for Garf's."

"Don't you dare speak that traitor's name outta your mouth!!"

Roaring, he brought his foot down, and the ground caved in, crushed with a thunderous roar.
Smoke and dust rose, the force enough to tilt nearby trees. Fury enough that the forest cowered, that even the air seemed to lose sound for fear of provoking him further.
But Ram, faced head-on with that very fury, remained cool-faced.

"You think acting so stubborn, showing your strength through a childish tantrum like this, will make everyone follow you? Garf, how much longer are you going to keep running around this cramped forest?"

"Don't you talk like you know somethin'! What the hell do you — you and Frederica, and the rest of you who abandoned the Sanctuary — what the hell do any of you understand, huh!?"

Ram's words, spoken as if reasoning with a small child, didn't reach the enraged Garfiel. Unlike the stomp before, he ground his foot into the dirt without force this time.

"For my sake? For my sake, huh? You — that's exactly what I got no room to believe. What face are you showin' up with now, sayin' that to me...?"

"Garf..."

"Pity, sympathy — when did I ever ask you for that? How much are you lookin' down on me from up there? Me and the old hag and the rest — none of us want to be pitied."

Covering his face with his palm, Garfiel wrung the words out through ragged breath.
That somehow tragic figure of his made him look smaller to those watching than his actual stature would suggest.

Taking repeated deep breaths, Garfiel finally removed his hand from his face.

"That's enough. I don't wanna hear no more. Turn around right now and get back to the Sanctuary. Do that, and I'll let this go this time. I'm goin' after that bastard."

"I refuse, Garf. If anyone should yield here, it's not Ram, but you. Even if you go back, you can't avoid the collapse drawing near. You know that, don't you?"

"Just get back. I ain't sayin' it twice. Go back, and wait till the Trial's settled."

"No, I won't go back, and I won't wait. Standing still gains nothing. All that remains in your palm from staying stagnant in place is the residue of what you thought you'd gained. Why would you cling to something so feeble and vague—"

"Even so! That's still way better than havin' nothin' left at all!"

Cutting off Ram as she tried to continue, Garfiel roared, lifting his face. Carved into that expression were anger, and longing, and grief, and—

"Collapse? So what? I'll deal with all of it, every last bit. This time, I swear it — I'll handle it all, every damn bit—"

"Garf, I've told you before, haven't I. — What you're doing is a substitute act."

Against Garfiel's exploding emotions, Ram responded with nothing but unwavering composure.
Their two positions ran parallel, each concession clashing head-on against the other's stance. And no moment of mutual yielding was coming, either.
Perhaps sensing that, Garfiel lowered his eyes, and closed his eyelids.

"Go back, Ram. My last request. With every feeling I've ever told you, over and over — I'm beggin' you. So..."

"Then, Garf. — Would you give up everything else, except Ram?"

"――――"

To Garfiel's anguished final plea, the reply was terribly brief, and yet it brought a crushing, unbearable pressure to the one who heard it.

Facing her, Garfiel's expression stiffened, his lips trembling.
Watching him like that, Ram lowered her eyes slightly.

"Out of everything that exists in this world, choose Ram. Look only at Ram. Love only Ram. Devote yourself only to Ram. Be loved only by Ram. Be forgiven only by Ram. Offer everything only to Ram — can you do that?"

"I-I..."

"Ram can."

Placing a hand over her own chest, Ram declared it clearly to Garfiel, who faltered.
Quiet, absolutely unshakable resolve. Putting nothing but that into her words, she lifted her face.

"—Ram can."

And that was, for Ram, her final ultimatum to Garfiel.

Perhaps he understood that. The vigor drained from Garfiel's expression for just an instant. What kind of expression surfaced in that moment — only Ram, who witnessed it, would ever know.
He immediately shook his head, hiding the glimpse of weakness he'd shown, and bared his fangs.

"Known it since way back — you're one hell of a stubborn woman."

"The feeling's mutual. — If you truly won't make Ram the one important thing within yourself, Ram won't lean toward Garf. Ram won't belong to anyone."

"That so."

Facing each other, their gazes crossed.
Having thrown their conclusions at one another, having confirmed neither would yield, the two of them, in quiet voices—

"Goodbye, Garf."

"See ya, Ram."

Those were, for the two of them, the last words exchanged, and the last affection given.


—The forest trembles.
And then, a roar echoed through it.

"Patrasche! Stop! I said stop, didn't I?!"

Desperately pulling the reins atop the dragon, Subaru shouted at Patrasche with all his might as she raced through the forest.
But the Earth Dragon showed no sign of heeding her rider's words, single-mindedly plowing through the rough path, putting more and more distance between them and the battlefield.

He'd heard stories of horses panicking from shock or fear and no longer heeding their rider, but Patrasche's current state didn't match that.
His Earth Dragon's demeanor was unchanged from usual — she remained rational, and was consciously, deliberately ignoring Subaru's instructions.
Which meant this was proof that she didn't recognize Subaru as someone worth seeking instructions from in the first place, and—

"So this whole time, you listening to me was just your good will, huh."

"――――"

Not obeying, and yet not throwing off a master she disliked either. Patrasche's every behavior, from beginning to end, was overflowing with consideration for Subaru, and being merely jostled about on her back, Subaru nearly wept from a mixture of gratitude and self-pity.
It wasn't some cute little story about not being able to control an Earth Dragon. He was disgusted with his own foolishness — being cared for even by an Earth Dragon, and not even noticing it.
And what Subaru was disappointed in himself for, right now, in progress, included all of that, but above all—

"Ram's in danger! I don't want to think Garfiel would do something violent to Ram, but... right now!"

Garfiel's thinking had reached a point where he'd concluded killing Subaru was necessary. That Ram had stood in his way there was surely a miscalculation on his part — but what solution he'd find for that miscalculation was terrifying just to imagine.

Subaru had half-accepted the sacrifice of those at the manor as something unavoidable in this loop. But Ram getting involved was a different story. If it strayed outside the range he'd decided he could accept, Subaru's insufficiently-resolved heart would crack and shatter, exposing its disgraceful state.

"Me getting hurt is... something I hate, but it can be undone. So...!"

A pleading voice, almost like a whine. But Patrasche gave him nothing to grab onto.
The speed of the Earth Dragon, become the wind, didn't slacken, showing no sign of heeding Subaru's entreaties. Ram and Garfiel, growing farther away. A tragedy unfolding somewhere beyond his reach.
Thinking of it, Subaru's heart was crushed once more. Why was his heart so soft, why could he never manage to be strong, no matter how much time passed?

—And so, having repeated his mistakes by turning his eyes only inward, Subaru repeated that same failure once again.

"—Eh?"

His field of view opened up abruptly because Patrasche had cleared the gaps between the densely packed trees. And Subaru, who had cleared the obstacle-filled stretch by clinging to her, gaped at the sight that filled his suddenly open view.

"Wh-what's wrong, Natsuki-san? Rushing like that."

Speaking that way, showing the same surprise on his face as Subaru, was Otto.
The evacuee group, who should have gone on ahead — Subaru had caught up to their line from the side. For Subaru, who'd assumed they'd entered the forest and been running without any fixed destination, this action of Patrasche's was, in a word, unexpected.

"Weren't you not supposed to be able to pass? What happened to Garfiel?"

"I-I don't really get it myself either, but... Ram and Patrasche..."

Trying to answer Otto while catching his ragged breath, Subaru wiped the sweat from his forehead with the back of his hand.
—It was the very next moment that a tremendous roar shook the entire forest.

"Wha—!?"
"Hyaa!?"

Eyes going wide with a throat-catching shock, Subaru and Otto both turned toward the source of the sound at the same time.
The thunderous roar shook the air, and people's hearts, and was overwhelming enough to make even the Earth Dragons show signs of panic.
If there was anyone present who hadn't been shaken by it, it would only have been Patrasche, carrying Subaru on her back.

Which was why she, more than anyone else present, was also the first to make a decision.

"Ah, Natsuki-san!?"

"Hey, Patrasche!"

Turning her neck immediately toward the front of the carriage line, Patrasche kicked off the ground and broke into a run. Where she was headed wasn't the lead carriage — not even that, but beyond it. In other words, further down the road, cutting without hesitation straight toward the Sanctuary's exit.
Leaving Otto's call behind, Subaru, atop Patrasche as she took off running, received the Divine Protection once again. He didn't understand the reason for her action. Just as he tried to raise his voice further to somehow stop her—

"――――ッ!!"

An impact shook the ground, and Subaru heard a scream reach him from behind.
Gasping involuntarily, he tilted his head back, straining his eyes toward the direction where Otto and the others had been.

In the field of view now reduced to only his left side, the dim forest floated hazily into view, and Subaru saw it.

A dragon carriage bursting apart. Swallowed, Earth Dragon and all, by a violent impact, it scattered the evacuees it had been carrying up into the sky, screams and blood staining the forest sky a tragic, painful red.

"—Ah"

Watching that scene of devastation, Subaru saw, beneath the wreckage of the dragon carriage floating in the air, a single beast.

—Its whole body covered in golden fur, it looked to Subaru like a gigantic tiger.
