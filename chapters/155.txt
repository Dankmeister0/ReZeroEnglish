Arc 3 Chapter 78: "The Madman and the Master of Ceremonies"

After Subaru's laughter had thoroughly finished ringing out, Otto's identity was at last confirmed, and he was freed from his bonds.

Rubbing at his bound wrists and the corners of his mouth where the gag had bitten in, Otto glared at Subaru, his face still flushed red with resentment.

"'Thank you for rescuing me...' is what I'd say, except right now I really don't feel like saying it honestly."

"Hey now, is that any way to talk to the people who saved your life? Get a reputation as an ingrate and it'll only strangle your business dealings going forward, you know?"

"Ahh! So you already know everything about my situation, huh! Thank you so much! Thanks to you I get to keep living! I tell you, I was scared out of my wits!"

In response to Subaru's teasing tone—one that showed he knew a fair bit about Otto's circumstances—Otto flung back a loud, half-desperate apology of his own.

The communication mirror confiscated from the informant connected to two hideouts—one had been identified as the cave where Petelgeuse lay hidden, and the other was believed to be the location of the last Finger, the one Subaru had never managed to learn of.
So, in parallel with guiding Emilia and the others to safety, orders had been given to the beastman mercenary company to launch a surprise strike and eliminate that danger beforehand.

As a result, the Witch Cultists—fed false reports through Ferris's suggestion—had noticed nothing of their movements, and were caught defenseless and wiped out by the ambush. Several prisoners were taken, and, for whatever reason, they'd also succeeded in rescuing Otto, who had been captured by the cultists.
On top of that, there was more good news in the faces among the prisoners Ricardo and the others had brought back—or more precisely, that one of them was a woman Subaru recognized.

"She's the woman Petelgeuse has possessed twice already. So far, that's a hundred percent possession rate."

Whether or not it was a matter of preference, the person most likely to be Petelgeuse's next host, should he lose his current body, had been among the prisoners.
With her rendered unconscious and restrained, it could be said the odds had dropped that Petelgeuse—should he lose his current host—would choose to move into her next.
And with the location of the other stronghold likely to hold his backup possession target already known to them, they could say they'd moved considerably closer to victory.

The blank letter of introduction, the last Finger—as several scattered scraps began to connect into a shape, Subaru felt he was managing to piece together the fragment of paper called the future, correctly this time.
Managing to rescue Otto this time around could be counted as part of that same success.

Out of the forest came the mercenary company's pack of Ligers, along with Otto's Earth Dragon—one Subaru recognized—still hitched to its Dragon Carriage. Having had his entire fortune confiscated on top of his very life being held hostage, it was no wonder Otto hadn't felt alive.
In fact, it wasn't hard to imagine what fate would have befallen Otto if events had simply run their usual course. Considering that, if he'd cleared the previous loop the same way as before, he'd never have had this chance to meet Otto again—it almost made his earlier mistake worthwhile.

"Well, I'll let it slide at that. Ptoo."

"What was that reaction!? Did I do something to you?!"

Otto yelped at the sight of Subaru spitting to cover his embarrassment and frustration, but Subaru brushed it off with a vague "that's that" and changed the subject.

"So, what's the story behind you getting caught? Just you alone doesn't add up."

"That's... well, there are, um, circumstances that are extremely difficult to put into words."

"See, most people setting foot in this territory right now are peddlers who flocked here chasing a bit of coin, thanks to that proclamation from the royal capital about Margrave Roswaal's mansion—"

"You already basically know the answer, don't you!? Fine, yes! I jumped at the chance for a nice bit of profit, trying to beat everyone else there, tearing down bad roads to be first, and out of pure bad luck I ran straight into the cultists' den set up in the forest and got caught! Go on, laugh if you're going to laugh!"

"No, hearing a story that greedy and being told to laugh kind of makes me want to back away, honestly..."

"That is NOT a satisfying reaction!!"

Subaru lightly deflated the forced cheer with which Otto had tried to laugh it off, and sneered anew at the unreasonable, undignified way he was shouting.
It seemed his merchant's instincts and his surprisingly reckless streak hadn't changed a bit, and that was a relief in itself. Enjoying the exchange after so long, Subaru gave him a quick once-over.

—checking whether there was anything suspicious in his manner, any strange trace of hostility.

Ricardo, watching the two of them from a distance, was keeping the same kind of watch.
Given the very circumstances of how he'd been pulled out of the hideout, one could say the suspicion was already mostly cleared at this point—but caution upon caution never hurt.
After all, considering that Ketty, the leader-type figure among the peddlers, had turned out to be the informant, even someone Subaru had built a reasonably close relationship with couldn't be trusted carelessly.

Every time he had to repeat that kind of suspicion, Subaru felt disgust at how it was wearing his heart raw.
No matter how many times he repeated the world, Subaru couldn't know the true essence of another person's intentions, their heart, their past. And so all he could do was go on suspecting—struggling to earn their trust in him, all while, deep down, unable to trust them in return.

"Guess I've turned into a pretty unpleasant guy myself, huh."

"What was that?"

"I said it's good you're safe. You'll be out of danger for now as long as you're in this village, so just sit tight. As for the profit business, all I can say is my condolences."

Subaru covered his self-mockery with a flippant remark, and at his words Otto looked around in surprise. Belatedly, his face showed he'd realized the sight he'd been hoping for was nowhere among the village.

"D-don't tell me... could it be, is it possible, has it already...?"

"It means you dropped your chance to cash in on that profit. Well, you'll just have to console yourself that being alive at all is profit enough. Sell that oil of yours well, huh."

Subaru gave the shoulder of the now pale-faced Otto a sympathetic pat, and leaving him looking ready to crumple, turned to survey the others busy with preparations.
His eyes met Ricardo's, who had been watching them; Ricardo gave a little twitch of his nose, as if to say his read on Otto was already settled, and went to rejoin Mimi and the others.
Seemed he'd earned the seal of approval from a battle-hardened mercenary too—Subaru let out a breath of relief, and, wanting to avoid Otto doing anything further reckless here, gave him a rough rundown of the situation.
Though Otto seemed a little distracted, still throwing in the occasional interjection, Subaru finished explaining things with plenty of gestures thrown in, and said,

"So basically—forest bandits, though you probably already know—the folks here are a strike force to bring down the Witch Cult. Meanwhile, since this place might turn into a battlefield, we've been gathering peddlers to help evacuate the villagers. Sorry you got completely caught up in the crossfire, but there's no sending you out of the village now, so you're stuck waiting here until things are settled. Okay?"

"O-okay...?"

"Glad you're on board. Some people are staying behind, so make sure you don't wander off from them. Well then, once everything's settled, let's talk again."

Leaving the dumbstruck Otto with just that, Subaru set him aside for the time being.
Instead, he headed over to the strike force, which seemed to have finished forming up and organizing into squads, and called out to Julius and Ricardo, who were each directing them.
Julius turned, glanced at Subaru and then, from a distance, at Otto, and said,

"That was a reunion with an acquaintance, wasn't it? Have you had enough time to warm up an old friendship?"

"We had a good stupid laugh, swapped some stupid stories, and the whole time I was peering underneath to make sure nothing was hidden. Even I think I've gotten too rotten a personality for my own good."

"Well, it's good that you seem satisfied at least. If you no longer need to suspect someone you'd rather not suspect, that's enough in itself, I'd say. —What will you do with him?"

Gently correcting Subaru's self-deprecating remark, Julius asked what would become of the now-forlorn Otto. In response, Subaru drew his chin in and spread his arms as if to indicate the village.

"I'll leave him in the village. There's going to be a standby squad here either way, so they can look after him. I'd rather not send him chasing after the folks who already evacuated and pile on more trouble at this point."

"That's probably wise. Well then, dawdling any longer will only create needless risk. Shall we get moving, then?"

Nodding at Subaru's answer, Julius narrowed his eyes and posed the question with fighting spirit veiled beneath it. At his call, Subaru could see the assembled knights, and Ricardo too, tighten their expressions.
Fighting spirit rose, and the air of the battlefield began to thicken around them.

Lifting his chin and turning his gaze skyward, Subaru let his thoughts drift for a moment.
There were still some worrisome factors, but he was confident he'd crushed every risk he could crush. He'd done everything humanly possible to the fullest—all that remained was to wait for fate to smile on them.
If that was so, then spending any more time here would be nothing but a foolish act that would only dull their fighting spirit.

"—Let's do this. Stick to the plan, everyone."

Lowering his gaze and sweeping it across everyone present, Subaru quietly gave the call to arms.
In response, the knights nodded in turn and began mounting the Earth Dragons and Ligers they'd each kept standing by.

At Subaru's side, Patrasche pressed close, spirited and high as if she'd been eagerly awaiting her turn; he stroked her hard hide with his palm before climbing onto her back.

Beside him, Julius and Ricardo lined up flanking Subaru on either side, and with their gazes at his back, Subaru drew a small breath, and—

"All right, then—let's do this, everyone. I'm counting on you, so lend me your strength—!"


* * *


Drawing on his experience from the previous battle, Subaru had worked out a theory concerning Petelgeuse's "Possession."

First, Petelgeuse's "Possession" functioned by transferring his consciousness into another body the moment his current body ceased functioning.
Second, the target of that transfer wasn't just anyone—it was thought to be limited to the bodies of the subordinates Petelgeuse called his "Fingers."
Third, given the term "Fingers," the spare bodies likely numbered ten in total. One was stationed at each hideout, arranged so he could switch into them in an emergency.
Fourth, even if Petelgeuse were rendered unconscious without being killed outright, should another Finger sense it, a system was in place to force his current body into suicide, triggering the possession phenomenon and carrying out the transfer regardless.
Fifth, should he lose every one of his transferable "Fingers," Petelgeuse's last resort would be to possess Subaru's own body. That possession was overwhelming—no matter how hard one tried to hold onto consciousness, it wasn't something one could resist. —That summed it up.

"Laying it all out point by point like that, this isn't even on the level of a 'kills you on the first try' trap anymore. And on top of all that, the guy's got the Authorities of 'Unseen Hand' and 'Sloth'? That's too vicious to even talk about."

The jet-black demonic hand that dealt invisible, fatal wounds: the "Unseen Hand."
The mental contamination that crushed the will to fight and stole away sanity from anyone without resistance to it: "Sloth."
And even if one overcame both of those and took his life, there was "Possession"—meaning he couldn't be killed in just one try.

If this were a single life with a single shot, it was a lineup that would make anyone throw up their hands and give up, and rightly so.
If he'd challenged this without knowing a thing, Subaru was confident he'd have died a hundred times out of a hundred tries. Only by repeating it over and over with his memories intact, endlessly working out countermeasures, did even a glimmer of light finally start to show—which made it easy enough to understand why the Witch Cult had thrown its weight around for four hundred years.

That was how specialized in first-encounter kills Petelgeuse's power was.
Thinking of it as a killing pattern that would work against just about anyone, it made sense that he'd be renowned even among the Witch Cult, valued highly enough for his name to stand out.
Which was exactly why Subaru was indispensable when it came to facing Petelgeuse.

Against someone who dealt out first-encounter kills with ease, Subaru was the sole being who could challenge him without it being a first encounter at all.
It wouldn't be an exaggeration to say that Natsuki Subaru was Petelgeuse's natural predator.

—Passing through the dense, overgrown forest, Subaru made for the cave he'd visited so many times before.

In a world where green covered every inch of sight and even his sense of direction threatened to slip away, his footsteps forward carried no hesitation. It was a path he'd already walked many times, for the same purpose. His senses, his feet, guided him there as if tracing over the experience carved into his memory.

Not once had Subaru ever headed toward that place carrying a will to fight.
The time before, and the time before that, Subaru had resigned himself to his role as a delaying tactic—more precisely, a decoy piece—and had faced that place with nothing on his mind but seeing that role through.
Before that, he'd been drowning in nothing but murderous intent, a self-destructive sense of doom, and those negative feelings alone.

But this time was different. This time, and only this time, it wasn't like that.
Subaru had come here this time with his own resolve to fight firmly set.

To carve out, with his own two hands, the conclusion to a long, long-standing grudge and a battle he'd fought over and over again.

"—Welcome, faithful disciple of favor."

The forest opened up and the view widened; Subaru felt sunlight on his eyelids and hard rock underfoot. Narrowing his eyes and looking ahead, he saw a sheer cliff towering up to block the view, and before it, a gaunt figure stood with both arms spread wide in welcome.

This must be the fifth time now that they'd come face to face like this.
Meet someone's face enough times, and you'd think you'd come to hold some sort of favorable impression of them, whoever they were—but with this man, that still seemed impossible.

"I am the Witch Cult's Sin Archbishop in charge of 'Sloth.' Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti... IS!"

Pointing all his fingers on both hands toward Subaru, Petelgeuse announced himself with what had already become his set introduction. The hem of his black clerical robe fluttered as the madman bared his yellow-tinged teeth, eyeing Subaru with a bouncing sort of glee.

"A fine day, this IS. A wonderful day, this IS. To think that on this very day of fate, the day I'd resolved to carry out the trial, I would get to welcome a new favored child of love in just this way...! My chest is fit to burst from tears of gratitude, from rapture, from sheer wonder—it IS unbearable!"

He pressed a hand to his own chest and began writhing, twisting his thin body as though embracing himself. Struggling not to let his revulsion at the madman's bizarre display show on his face, Subaru took a small, deep breath.

"It is an honor to meet you for the first time, Sin Archbishop. To join you just before the trial is carried out is a matter of great embarrassment on my part. But if this body, this soul, might be granted the humblest seat among the disciples carrying out this trial, there could be no greater honor."

Bending his body, dropping to his knees, Subaru bowed to Petelgeuse in a show of the utmost respect. Watching this, Petelgeuse once again welled up with tears of apparent rapture, his cheeks streaming wet with them, and said—

"Oh, oh, what a splendid resolve! What a clear, unclouded way of showing love! Never—never IS there a time in memory I have cursed my own sloth this much! You! To think that someone as devout a disciple of love as you! Has not until now been welcomed as one of our own—that is nothing but a vice born of the Witch Cult's sloth, of MY sloth, IS! Ah, forgive me! Forgive the sloth in me that would overlook even a single drop of the guiding light of love you pour into this world—!"

Whether to match Subaru's kneeling or simply to express the depth of his own rapture, Petelgeuse flung all four limbs out onto the ground and began punishing himself by striking his own forehead against the rock—self-harm carried out with such merciless force that his forehead split open and blood flowed freely, with no regard given to it at all.
Between this and the way he bit through his own fingers, Subaru had assumed the man simply had a taste for self-harm—but now that there was a real possibility this body wasn't even Petelgeuse's own, he could no longer just write it off as ordinary self-harm.

In any case, it would be a serious problem if that self-harm went too far and cost him his life right now.
Their scheme, still working below the surface, hadn't fully played out yet. He needed to keep stalling for time and steer the situation toward the outcome they'd planned for.

"Your Grace, please, stop. Even the Witch would take no joy in such an act."

"Ah, but but but but butbutbutbutbutbutbutbutbutbut! For the sloth in my conduct! For its foolishness! For the faithlessness that fails to answer love! There IS no way to earn forgiveness except to punish and discipline myself!"

"That isn't so at all. Rather than seeing a beloved disciple wound himself, the Witch would surely be far more pleased to see that disciple answer her favor by carrying out the trial."

Stepping closer, Subaru caught hold of Petelgeuse before he could harm himself further and said as much. At that, Petelgeuse's violently writhing body went still, and he looked up at Subaru with his eyes opened wide. Feeling a chill of dread well up inside him at that parched stare, Subaru nodded back without a word.
Suddenly, a palm of nothing but skin and bone seized Subaru's arm with unexpected force.

"—Everything, exactly as you say, IS."

"——!"

"Ah, I was wrong, IS, mistaken, IS, unable to be righteous, IS! Yes! The trial—what I ought to be doing now is not self-punishment, not self-harm, not self-destruction, but the trial! To answer the beloved Witch's favor, carrying out the trial is the very mission I've been granted, one to be staked with my body and life! To forget that and steep myself in the pleasure of self-abasement—how foolish! Ah, your words have opened my eyes, IS! Splendid, splendid, spleeeeeeeeendid—!"

Shaking Subaru's captured arm roughly up and down while flinging out his one-sided gratitude, Petelgeuse rose to his feet. He wiped the blood dripping from his forehead roughly with his robe's sleeve, and with the very mouth that had just declared self-abasement foolish, took a finger between his teeth and bit down, crushing it.

"This slothful body of mine holds no value at all. Diligence is the most precious thing in this world, and sloth the most contemptible vice in it. So then I shall, through diligence, keep the karmic sin of sloth forever at bay. Ah! You truly are a diligent one, aren't you! To be able to welcome you as a new disciple is truly, truly, truly, truly, tr-tr-tr-truly, truly, truly a joyous thing, IS!"

There was no longer any consistency to his words or actions; his logic was utterly incoherent.
Even as he spoke of reflecting on his self-harm, he bit through his own finger; no sooner had he wept in shame over his own rash conduct than he burst into cheerful laughter as if he'd forgotten it all.

Subaru had thought he already understood how broken Petelgeuse's words and actions were—but even having thought he understood, he still couldn't keep pace with him.
It was a glimpse of a humanity so manic that even Subaru, trying to keep the conversation going with stalling for time in mind, found himself falling silent despite himself.

"—Let's speak of the trial."

Holding his breath and closing his eyes, Subaru pushed down that welter of incomprehension and forced out the words.
What he'd brought up was the "trial"—the grand purpose behind this attack, which the madman had repeated over and over. As far as Subaru knew, there had never once been a conversation that got Petelgeuse to go into the actual content of that trial.
Petelgeuse called this attack a "trial" and every single time repeated statements that sounded like he was testing Emilia. If so, then what exactly was the true intent behind it?
What would it take, and how, to be said to have overcome the "trial" Petelgeuse had set? Subaru had not the slightest wish to understand this madman, but he couldn't possibly face him without believing there was some rationale, however small, behind his actions.
If nothing else, he needed to believe he could grasp at least that much: what it was they were after, at the end of all this senseless conduct.

"Now that I've joined you, I would very much like to hear about the purpose of this operation—about the trial, Your Grace."

"—The trial."

Petelgeuse murmured it quietly, as if all his prior emotion had suddenly been wiped away.
A chill of dread crept up Subaru's spine at that sudden quiet, but he barely managed to keep it from showing on his face and waited for what would come next.
The last time he'd tried to broach the subject of the trial like this, he'd been asked to present his Gospel and the whole conversation had fallen apart. How would it go this time?

Swallowing down saliva that went unpleasantly rough down his throat, Subaru felt the silence scorching at his stomach.
And then, just as the cold sweat beading on his forehead was about to become impossible to stop from running down his face—

"The trial! Yes, the trial, IS! It IS the trial! The trial must be carried out, IS! I must test, I must be tested—for that is exactly what I came here for, IS!"

Petelgeuse's face lit up in an instant, and he began spinning in place as if bouncing.
Caught off guard by the sudden shrill outburst, Subaru—stiff with tension until then—went blank for just a moment. Before him, Petelgeuse went on still in that shrill falsetto.

"I must test her, IS! Test whether the body of this half-demon, whose existence has newly come to light, is worthy of bearing down the Witch, IS! If her body is worthy to be the Vessel, she is to be secured; if not, she is to pay the price for her irreverent birth, IS!"

"Bearing down the Witch? A Vessel? ...A price?"

"Solitude! Negative feeling! It is that very repressed, festering thing that raises the state of the Vessel to be fit for the Witch's own, IS! And so, with this slothful body of mine, this will of mine, these Fingers of mine, I must see it carried out to perfection! Ah! To love! To love! I must answer it!"

At the madman's gesture—fingers stretched overhead as if trying to grasp the very heavens—and at the content of his words, Subaru saw a kind of answer take shape, and his spine froze.
Vessel, bearing down—if those words meant exactly what he took them to mean, then—

"...if the trial reveals that half-demon's body is fit to be the Vessel... then the Witch would be brought down into that Vessel..."

"The Witch of Envy, Satella, shall manifest once more in this world—IS! That day, that hour, that very moment—how much, to what degree, this much do I long for it, IS! And if I might become a help, an aid, a cornerstone toward that end...there could be no greater joy than that, IS!!"

Petelgeuse's voice, brimming with rapture, streamed with tears of joy even as he spoke.
Listening to this, Subaru felt a nausea rise in him at the fact that the Witch Cult's purpose was even more vile than he'd imagined. In other words, after acting so brutally, after carrying out such a massacre, after driving Subaru so far to the brink—

"They see no value in Emilia herself at all?"

To them, Emilia's existence was nothing more than a Vessel to house the Witch's Soul.
Before the Witch's Soul entered it, how noble the will of that body's owner might be, how hard she strove, how deeply she was loved by others—none of that mattered in the slightest, not even a fragment.
That amounted to a boundless insult against the very existence of Emilia—and for Natsuki Subaru, whose heart had been so moved by who she was, it was an unbearable humiliation as well.

Burning an entire village to scorched earth along the way probably didn't even register as something to feel guilty over—let alone actual remorse. He could unconsciously treat with contempt the fact that every single life he'd taken had had a story of its own, had had dreams, had had a tomorrow.
How else could anyone carry out such deeds so calmly—not even with a laugh, just flatly, indifferently?

"—Monster."

Petelgeuse didn't notice the hostility that leaked out of Subaru for just that one instant, that single word.
A monster of incomprehensible thought, of notions no one could ever possibly share, one who could never, ever be understood—that was Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti.

"Your Grace, I have heard your esteemed teaching. The Witch Cult's creed—your words surpassed everything I had heard of it, and I am deeply moved. The trial shall surely be accomplished."

"Ohh! I knew it, you are splendid! Yes, IS, that's right, IS, it must be so, IS! Unless we unite as one, single-minded and undivided, throwing our very bodies into our true purpose, we lose all meaning as ourselves! My fingers, my feet, my eyes, my nose, my mouth, my tongue, my blood, my bones, my flesh, my Soul—every one of them IS meant to be offered up to the Witch! From the moment I received her favor, from the moment I knew myself beloved, I, this existence called Petelgeuse, became mere dust and ash, meant only to answer that love with the whole of my being... Ah! What sloth, that I should ever forget it!!"

To Subaru, mouthing flattery so sickly sweet it felt like his tongue might rot, Petelgeuse—taking in only the surface of his words—didn't seem to harbor a shred of doubt.
He spoke proudly of the self-serving faith overflowing from within him, and reaching into his robe, drew out a book bound in black—the Gospel—and began turning its pages.

"The words engraved in the Gospel, the ones that tell of love! They are what steel my resolve to act, IS! To carry love through to the end, to see my deeds through to completion, obstacles are essential! Obstacles are only right to exist! Obstacles are a matter of course...! And that is exactly why my daily diligence, my faith that resists sloth, is tested, tested—the hour of the trial granted unto me—!"

Tracing the characters written on the page with a finger, foam gathering at the corners of his mouth, Petelgeuse ran his bloodshot eyes over it.
To Subaru, who had once succeeded in wresting the Gospel from his hands, the book in Petelgeuse's grasp was nothing but a foreign tome, its lines of characters utterly indecipherable.

But to Petelgeuse, whatever was written there was surely comprehensible.
With every character his eyes traced, his cheeks flushed redder, colored with excitement, and his eyes visibly filled more and more with rapture and delusion.

And then—

"—Present your Gospel."

Tilting his head a full ninety degrees, canting his hips, Petelgeuse spun his whole body around and looked at Subaru.
At those words, Subaru went still for a moment and closed his eyes, thinking, here it comes at last. From past experience, no matter how tenuously a conversation had been holding together, this was the question of Petelgeuse's that always cut it dead.

Every single Witch Cultist, without exception, was said to be delivered a Gospel—something like a scripture of their own.
By what selection, and from whom it was sent—that background remained one of the dark corners of the Witch Cult that even Wilhelm and the others hadn't managed to learn.
But setting aside those unknowns, possession of a Gospel itself was proof of one's status as a Witch Cultist, essential and non-negotiable—

"Please, present your Gospel. I beg you, tell me just what it is the Witch has bestowed upon you—please, please, please, please, please, plea-plea-plea-plea-plea-please, please, please, please, IS."

With every repetition of please, he tilted his head side to side, in a motion dizzying enough to make anyone watching feel sick, all while staring fixedly at Subaru.
From his posture and manner, Subaru judged that at least the signal he was waiting for hadn't come yet. His role of stalling for time was still in effect—so, to keep the scene going, he first drew a breath, and—

"About the Gospel..."

"Yes, the Gospel, IS. If you have been recognized as a disciple, it should already be in your hands..."

"The truth is, whether by some mistake or other, no Gospel has ever been delivered into my hands."

Cutting off Petelgeuse's assenting words, Subaru revealed he didn't have a Gospel to his name. At that, Petelgeuse's eyes went wide, but before he could get out a word of his own, Subaru thrust out a palm to head him off with a "Now, now, hold on just a moment!" And then—

"It is true, I hold no Gospel! I do not have one—but even so, my love for the Witch is truly here, in this chest, within me, overflowing without measure! Even without a tangible Gospel in hand, if this formless devotion is not the very cry of my Soul striving to answer the love bestowed upon me, then what, pray, could it possibly be!!"

"—You..."

"The form may differ, but I too am nothing more than one leaf devoted heart and soul to the Witch, just as you are, Your Grace! I shall aid in this trial with my whole being. I offer up everything, and think only of the Witch. No—for even down to the last shred of my Soul, my very existence has already been surrendered wholly to her!"

Pressing forward word after word, hurling them out one after another, Subaru gave Petelgeuse no chance to object. Raising a fist, stamping his foot against the ground, he screwed up his face as if wringing every last drop of feeling from himself, and—

"Or will you, Your Grace! Will you, Petelgeuse-sama, deny this cry of my Soul! Will you laugh at me, clinging to a formless love, and call me pitiful and foolish! Very well, so be it—I can even accept that as unavoidable! But! Even so! This faith of mine cannot be broken by anyone. For love—love alone—is everything!"

Thrusting his fist skyward, Subaru had said every outrageous thing he'd wanted to say and then some.
As for Petelgeuse, he had listened quietly to Subaru's ragged, breathless words, and at the very end of that outcry, let out a soft "Hoh" of a breath, and said—

"What a fresh, vivid whisper of love, IS..."

As though struck by lightning, whatever sharpness had been in Petelgeuse's expression drained clean away.
The madman, moved by Subaru's words, wept with a face as if his very heart had been washed clean, and said—

"Please, forgive my sloth for having doubted you. You are splendid, without exaggeration, splendid. Utterly unlike me, who cannot even summon the confidence to declare aloud that I am loved without something tangible, without a Gospel in hand. A dense, favored love that can be understood without ever being spoken—you are truly, IS, a being worthy of exactly that."

With an attitude of utter, wholehearted admiration, Petelgeuse dropped to his knees, pressed a hand to his chest, and paid respect to the depth of Subaru's faith. Cut out just that moment, and Petelgeuse's bearing could almost, almost pass for that of a devout man of religion.
Watching that attitude, that figure, Subaru couldn't help but feel a certain kind of sentiment. And that was—

"This guy's a total pushover."

"Did you say something, IS?"

"Please, raise your head, Your Grace. I am no such grand existence as to deserve being treated this way. Above all, are we not, both of us, equally showered with the Witch's love, standing side by side to answer that love together?"

Putting on a smile of pure, unwavering sincerity to cover himself, Subaru held out a hand to Petelgeuse. Petelgeuse blinked, said, "Just as you say, IS," and took Subaru's hand to rise to his feet, then snapped the Gospel shut with a click and said—

"Even bearing the name of Sin Archbishop, it seems my view was far too narrow, IS. I am ashamed of myself. And beyond that, my thanks for your consideration. And I vow to answer, with the utmost diligence, the Witch's love that brought about this very meeting!"

"Yes! Exactly right! Let us continue to hone and better ourselves together from here on!"

"Ohh, what joyous words, IS. That resolve, splendid indeed, even without a Gospel—such strong favor... could it be that you are, perhaps, 'Pride'? If so, then even without a Gospel, I would think you'd have some awareness of having taken in a Witch Factor, IS."

"A Witch Factor...?"

Once again, at a term he'd heard before but couldn't make sense of, Subaru tilted his head.
At his questioning tone, Petelgeuse nodded and said, "Yes,"

"To the disciples, a Gospel; and to one such as myself, a Sin Archbishop, both a Gospel and a Witch Factor are delivered together, IS. Without a Witch Factor, to speak of the trial is..."

Having said that much, Petelgeuse abruptly let his words trail off. As Subaru knit his brow at that, Petelgeuse tucked the Gospel back into his robe and, instead, drew out something else from within.
In his outstretched palm was clasped a hand mirror, faintly glowing with light—

"—!"

"My apologies, IS. Word from one of my Fingers... it seems there has been some change in the situation, IS. Given that we've just joined forces, I do hope it's good news."

Dropping his gaze to the communication mirror, Petelgeuse didn't notice the faint shift in Subaru's expression. Still not noticing, he opened the hand mirror, reflecting himself in its surface, and said—

"It is I, IS. Has something gone wrong—"

"Um, what was it, nya? Oh, right, right! Toraaa, Toraaa, Toraaaa!"

"...Huh?"

That expression froze at the sight and the words of the one reflected in the mirror.
Watching Petelgeuse's stunned reaction right before his eyes, Subaru could vaguely guess what must be showing in that communication mirror he couldn't peer into himself.
Even the sight of the person reflected in it, her cat ears swaying, waving a hand and sticking out her tongue as she spoke the very passphrase they'd agreed on.

"Just who are you...? Why has my Finger—"

"Tora, Tora, Tora."

"Huh?"

In front of Petelgeuse's bewilderment, Subaru repeated it quietly himself.
Petelgeuse tilted his head at catching that murmur, his whole manner showing he had no idea what it meant. Subaru turned a broad, beaming smile on him.
Nothing at all like the fake smile he'd been wearing until just now—this was his own, genuine one, the corner of his mouth twisted, teeth bared like some delinquent kid.

"It means, 'I have succeeded in my surprise attack.'"

"What... do you mean? I do not follow what you are saying, IS..."

"You don't get it, huh? Ah, don't worry about it."

Having painstakingly, courteously explained the meaning of the passphrase, Subaru nodded along in mock understanding at Petelgeuse's continued incomprehension. And then—

"—Because everything you've been saying has been just as much gibberish to me this whole time!!"

A right straight thrown with everything he had slammed straight into the madman's face—all the frustration Subaru had bottled up, patience piled on patience, finally exploded, sending that gaunt frame flying.

Petelgeuse let out a groan of pain as he tumbled across the ground, eyes rolling wildly, unable to hide his shock at the sudden violence that had struck him.
Looking down at the pitiful man, Subaru unclenched the fist he'd just driven home and pointed a finger at him.
And then, he declared—

"I've put up with a lot, but I'm past my limit now. All right—let's end this, Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti!!"
