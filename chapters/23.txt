Arc 1 Chapter 22: "Re:Zero -Starting Life in Another World-"

"Huh?"

The air within his field of view warped, and somehow the room's brightness seemed to drop a notch.

More than that, the temperature—already lowered by the barrage of ice magic—dropped further still, and Subaru hugged his own shoulders against the shiver that ran through him.

And then—

"Eh, whoa, hey—"

"Sorry... just, let me lean on your shoulder for a second."

Subaru fumbled, flustered, to support the silver-haired girl as she leaned against him.

Her slight frame was terribly hot, and Subaru became aware of his own heartbeat quickening for reasons entirely different from before. But the moment he saw her expression, that feeling was blown away in an instant.

Breathing in shallow, rapid gasps, she looked for all the world like someone in the grip of a raging fever.

"What's wrong, did you suddenly feel sick, or—"

"No. It's the mana... you can tell, can't you?"

*—Not even a little.*

He would have loved to fold his arms and declare as much, but this was hardly the moment for it.

More than anything, what sealed Subaru's words wasn't the weight against his shoulder, but the transformed atmosphere of the room—and the being at its source.

In the center of the room, Reinhard held his greatsword low in a battle stance.

No—that stance was, in truth, the same one he'd held since the fight began. And yet Subaru felt in his very skin that this sight deserved to be called *the first time he had ever truly readied his blade.*

—Yes. The Sword Saint, Reinhard, was now, for the first time, truly taking up his sword.

"Elsa Granhiert, the 'Bowel Hunter.'"

"—Reinhard van Astrea, of the line of the Sword Saint."

A tremendous killing intent pressed through the room, and the two combatants' fighting spirit made the very air shudder.

Elsa licked her lips as she declared her name, and Reinhard answered her in kind with a solemn nod.

In this place that had become little more than ruins, a black-clad murderer and a lightly armed hero faced one another, the steel between them a blood-soaked dagger and a rust-flecked old greatsword—and yet, Subaru found himself holding his breath.

The two of them, each having declared their name, now stood ready to face each other in single combat.

The sight of it seemed almost radiant, like a passage torn from some heroic epic.

"—————!"

A breath-choked cry—whether it belonged to Elsa, to Reinhard, or even to Subaru himself, none could say for certain.

But the result of it, all three of them witnessed alike.

A blade of pure light tore through the roofless Loot House, cleaving even the space itself clean in two.

It was a sight that could only be described as the world itself shifting out of place—the light, released in an instant, painted the room white, but the moment it cleared, the world had utterly changed.

The displaced space began to converge back into itself, and the aftershock—powerful enough to warp the very air—raged through the room as a violent storm.

The swirling wind whipped up loot, furniture, and debris alike into a frenzy, and Subaru desperately shielded the fake Satella and old man Rom's huge frame from the secondary disaster.

"NUWAAAAAAAH!! WHAT THE HELL IS THIS?!"

He had no idea what this storm of destruction meant. But he understood the cause well enough.

Just one swing. One full-powered swing of a sword—and this was the result.

He endured, crying out against the pain and the wind. Eventually the raging force weakened, and the sound of scattered debris and stolen goods clattering to the floor, along with the dull groan of the building itself, chained together to signal the end.

Subaru flung away a scrap of debris that looked like a hanging scroll that had fallen on his head, and confirmed that the two he'd thrown his body over to protect were unharmed. It seemed his coverage hadn't quite been enough, unconsciously—old man Rom looked a bit dirtied with milk and whatnot—but he'd have to beg forgiveness on that count.

"What do you mean monster hunting is your territory—you're plenty enough of a monster yourself!"

"Hearing you say that actually does wound me a little, Subaru."

Reinhard turned around, wry smile on his face, the cause of all this destruction.

His hair, red as flame amid the storm, was disheveled, and even his usual cool composure showed a sheen of sweat. And in his hands, the greatsword—

"I made you push yourself too hard. Rest easy now."

The greatsword crumbled apart in his grip—its shoddy make had never been able to withstand a single blow of Reinhard's.

A single strike powerful enough to make steel itself rot away—and the one who had taken it head-on, Elsa, was—

"Not even a scrap of flesh left... I guess losing the splatter factor is actually a plus."

After a slash that cleaved through the world itself, there truly was nothing left, in the most literal sense.

The destruction had blown away the counter seating near the entrance of the Loot House along with everything else, and its aftershock had reached even the plaza in front of the building. The raging wind had collapsed nearly every piece of the structure's framework; the building looked ready to come down at any moment.

The spot where Elsa had been standing was, naturally, well within the range of that slash. The tall, black-clad figure was nowhere to be found within it, and Reinhard's sheer overwhelming power left no room for comment.

"But with this..."

Subaru stretched out his body, stiff with tension, and let out a long breath.

Then, as if to confirm a fact he still couldn't quite believe, he turned his thoughts to the presence beside him.

The silver-haired girl leaning her shoulder against his—still breathing a little raggedly—noticed his gaze and turned her violet eyes to meet his.

"Is it... really over?"

"Yeah, in the truest sense, somehow."

Answering her faint question, Subaru supported her as she tried to stand. In the process, old man Rom's head, which had been resting on her lap, dropped—but that was a trivial matter.

Once standing, she combed her fingers through her silver hair and, on legs still a bit unsteady, stepped away from Subaru's support.

Watching the fake Satella move out of his grasp, Subaru studied her intently, and—

"Why are you staring? That's *really* rude, you know."

"Just making sure you've still got your arms and legs, and your head's still attached properly."

"...Isn't that obvious? Please don't say scary things like that."

His comment apparently made no sense to her.

She glared at him with narrowed, suspicious eyes, and Subaru raised a thumb and flashed his teeth.

"Right, of course it's obvious. Naturally, I've still got my arms and legs too, no knife sprouting out of my back, and no giant hole blown through my stomach!"

"The way you say that makes it sound like there was a time when you *did* have those things."

There had been. He truly, deeply apologized for the inconvenience caused during that period.

Thanks to Subaru's uselessness, the fake Satella had lost her life too, old man Rom had had his arm and head torn off, and Felt had been cut down mercilessly—it had been a thorough disaster all around.

"Oh, right, Reinhard. I still haven't thanked you. Seriously, you saved us. And earlier, back in the alley too—did you hear my heart's cry, my friend?"

"If I could do that, I'd be rather proud of myself, my friend."

Reinhard shrugged, an apologetic look on his face, and indicated something with a tilt of his chin.

Following his gesture, Subaru looked over and—

"Oh."

Seeing the figure standing there, Subaru felt his own mouth break into an unexpected smile.

At the Loot House's entrance—now nothing more than a gap where an entrance had once been—there, barely hidden in the shadow of a remaining pillar, peeking timidly at them, was the blonde girl with the prominent canine tooth.

"She ran desperately through the alleys and came to find me. I only made it here because of her. After that, I merely fulfilled my duty as a knight."

"Your duty as a knight—flattening a decrepit ruin, you mean?"

"Isn't that a bit harsh, Subaru?"

Reinhard pressed a hand to his chest, as if the remark had struck a nerve.

Having caused this much devastation, and yet retaining that same unchanging, easygoing charm—it was almost frightening. In short: handsome, seriously handsome.

"That girl..."

The fake Satella, too, on her still-unsteady feet, noticed Felt's presence.

Subaru moved to shield Felt from her line of sight, circling around—

"Whoa, whoa, hold up. If she hadn't called Reinhard here, we'd all have been wiped out, you know? Show some mercy on my behalf—spare her the ice statue treatment, would you?"

"I wouldn't do something that violent. Besides, on your behalf...?"

The fake Satella rubbed her brow wearily.

Even a small gesture like that felt, somehow, gratifying to Subaru.

That the two of them could stand here, alive, on decent enough terms to trade this kind of banter—it was, in a rather roundabout way of putting it, exactly the outcome Subaru had wanted.

"The rest is up to my negotiating skills, huh... that's the part I trust least of all!"

"What's with you? You've been all fidgety—it's *really* unbecoming."

The remark landed like a dagger, and Subaru clutched his chest in the same reaction as Reinhard had a moment ago. Though where Reinhard had dignity, all Subaru had was clownishness—not a shred of that composed elegance.

Watching this exchange between the two of them, Reinhard let out a small, guileless laugh. Then, silently, he raised a hand and went to greet Felt, who was still watching from a distance.

His retreating back was so dashing that Subaru couldn't even muster jealousy—all he could do was slump his shoulders. So this was the difference between those who had it and those who didn't.

Wary though she was, perhaps feeling some obligation for his having answered her call for help, Felt didn't try to flee as Reinhard approached.

Subaru watched the two of them with a faintly warm feeling, and—

"—Subaru!"

Reinhard's sudden shout, turning back toward him, made Subaru realize the crisis wasn't over yet.

"—————!!"

Debris flew upward, and a black shadow burst out from beneath it.

The shadow's black hair whipped through the air as it planted its feet with force despite the blood dripping from it, gaining speed.

Gripping a bent, mangled kukri knife, sprinting in silence—it was Elsa, drenched in blood.

"You—!"

The murderer, having somehow survived that ferocious slash and clawed her way back to life, had a pitch-black look in her eyes.

It was, of all the confrontations so far, the killing intent that sent the coldest blade of ice down Subaru's spine.

In the scant seconds before contact, Subaru's thoughts spun at a dizzying speed.

*The bent knife. A momentary encounter. She's probably betting everything on a single strike. Reinhard's rushing over but he won't make it in time. If I can just weather one blow, Reinhard will handle the rest. The fake Satella has no room to even turn around. Which one is she aiming for? This is the third time. I protect her.*

*Protectprotectprotectprotectprotectprotectprotectprotectprotectprotectprotectprotectproteeeeeeeect!!*

"AIM FOR THE GUT AIM FOR THE GUT AIM FOR THE GUUUUUUUUT!!"

He shoved the fake Satella out of the way as if throwing himself over her, hauled up the club still gripped in his arm, and instinctively guarded his stomach—*impact.*

The force of that horizontal sweep felt less like a slash and more like a blow from some heavy, blunt weapon.

The shock to his gut lifted his feet clean off the ground, and as blood spilled from his mouth, Subaru experienced the sensation of the world spinning a full hundred and eighty degrees.

His vision whirled and whirled. His body, sent flying, was actually spinning through the air.

Without any sense of how far he'd been thrown, and without being able to brace himself, he slammed into a wall.

"This brat, getting in my way again—"

Elsa clicked her tongue in frustration, watching Subaru go flying.

Then she turned her eyes toward the fake Satella, still standing frozen in place, but—

"That's far enough, Elsa!"

Facing Reinhard rushing back toward her, she recognized the futility of continuing the fight.

Elsa hurled the kukri knife—now completely warped from that final blow against Subaru—at Reinhard. His Divine Protection of Arrow Avoidance kept it from striking him, but—

"Someday, I'll cut open every belly in this room. Until then, do take good care of your intestines."

That bought her just enough time to leap off the debris underfoot.

Chasing after her slender form as she vaulted lightly over the roof and across the building was no small task. Reinhard, unwilling to prolong the fight any further here, deliberately chose not to pursue her.

Watching her retreating back disappear, Reinhard rushed to the silver-haired girl's side.

"Are you unharmed—"

"Never mind me!? More importantly..."

Reinhard's refined face flickered with urgency, but the fake Satella brushed past his words, scolding her own unsteady legs into motion as she made for the wall—toward Subaru, who lay collapsed upside down.

"Are you okay!? You went way too far!"

"O-owww... piece of cake, piece of cake. That was totally a moment that called for going too far, right? I was the only one who could still move, and besides, I had a sneaking suspicion where she'd aim."

Subaru raised a hand toward the fake Satella, who leaned in with a worried look, and gently prodded the stomach that had taken the hit. Beneath the pulled-up hem of his shirt, an extraordinary bruise had turned the area a deep purple.

Sticking out his tongue with an "uggh" at the gruesome sight, he then used the momentum of flipping himself right-side up to push himself to his feet.

"Now she's really, truly gone this time, right?"

"I'm sorry, Subaru. That was my carelessness just now. If you hadn't been there, it would have been dangerous. If she had been hurt because of me, I would have—"

"Whoa whoa whoa whoa, hold up! No need to finish that sentence. I went through way too much dramatic buildup for you to hand off that part to somebody else—I wouldn't be rewarded for my efforts."

Subaru cut off Reinhard just as he was about to voice an apology, offering a smile to the now-silent knight. Then he turned, slowly, and met the gaze of the silver-haired girl looking up at him.

She shifted, then rose to her feet. The distance between them was two steps—close enough that a reaching hand would find hers. It struck him, with no small feeling, just how long and roundabout a road it had been to get here.

The girl, seeing Subaru suddenly close his eyes and fall silent, looked as though she had something to say.

But before she could open her mouth, Subaru thrust a finger toward the sky, faster still.

Left hand on his hip, right hand stretched skyward, completely disregarding the astonished glances around him, Subaru raised his voice grandly.

"My name is Natsuki Subaru! I know you've got a mountain of things you want to say and ask, but let's set all that aside for now and answer this first!"

"Wh-what is it..."

"I am, right this very moment, the man who has just saved your life from a murderous blade! We good so far!?"

"...Good so far?"

"It means 'is that all right with you.' So—we good!?"

Subaru mimed the letters O and K with the movement of his upper body, and the silver-haired girl, though her expression twitched, responded with a strained "O-okay."

Nodding vigorously at her reaction, Subaru pressed on, building momentum.

"Life-saving hero, that's me. And you, the heroine he saved—don't you think an appropriate reward is in order? Don't you!?"

"...I understand. Though it comes with the condition of 'if it's something I can do.'"

"Well then, my wish is one and only, just a single thing."

He held up a single finger, jabbing it toward her, emphasizing it to the point of excess. Then he added a little wiggling motion with the finger, stoking her unease, and as she swallowed nervously, nodding with a look of grim resignation, Subaru turned a lecherous grin on her.

"Yes, my wish is—"

"Mm."

Flashing his teeth, snapping his fingers, striking a pose with a raised thumb and a triumphant expression—

"Tell me your name."

The girl's violet eyes went wide, her expression one of pure astonishment.

A brief silence fell over everyone, and Subaru, maintaining his triumphant pose, trembled faintly within that quiet.

An overwhelming embarrassment at how badly he'd missed the mark welled up in him, but backing down in a situation like this, before anyone around him had a chance to react, was the worst possible move.

So he stayed silent, waiting for her response.

*Block of ice, icicle, freeze ray, bring it on. Actually, please, not the freeze ray.*

"Heh heh."

Subaru's frantic, high-speed thoughts, pushed to their absolute limit, were swept away by the faint sound of laughter that reached him.

Hand at her lips, pale cheeks flushed pink, silver hair swaying, the girl was laughing.

It wasn't a resigned smile, nor a fleeting, fragile one, nor a grim smile of resolve. It was, simply and purely, the smile of someone laughing because something was fun. Nothing more, nothing less.

"—Emilia."

"Eh..."

At the word that followed her laughter, Subaru let out only a small breath.

Seeing his reaction, she straightened her posture, pressed a finger to her lips, and smiled mischievously.

"My name is Emilia. Just Emilia. Thank you, Subaru."

"For saving me," she added, and held out her hand.

He looked down at the pale hand extended toward him, and hesitantly reached out to touch it. Slender fingers, a small palm, delicate and so warm—the hand of a living, breathing girl.

*—Thank you for saving me.*

Those weren't only words she should be saying to him—Subaru was the one who should have said them to her. It was Subaru, after all, who had been saved by her first. So this—this was merely him finally being able to return the favor.

Three times now, in total, he had died by the blade and clawed his way back to this ending.

Wounded that much, grieved that much, suffered that much pain, fought that hard with his life on the line—and the reward for it all was her name and a single smile.

Ah, what a—

"Ah, man, seriously, what a rip-off deal."

Saying so, Subaru laughed too, and gripped the girl's—Emilia's—hand tightly in return.

—And if the story could have ended right there, that would have been a fine place to stop it. But it didn't.

"Still, Subaru, it's amazing you came out of that unharmed."

He had probably been waiting for a lull, once the exchange with Emilia had run its course.

Reinhard, who had been forcibly holding his tongue, voiced his quiet astonishment at Subaru's safety. If even a swordsman of his caliber found it remarkable, then Elsa's final blow must have carried that much force.

Subaru pressed lightly on his aching stomach, then pointed to the club lying on the floor.

The spiked club, true to its thick, sturdy look, had served admirably as a shield. Not its intended use, granted, but he'd have to ask for understanding on that point.

"Guarded with that thing on reflex. If I hadn't had it, I'd be sliced clean in half by now."

"You're right. Without this—"

*He couldn't have avoided BAD END 4*, Subaru laughed breezily. Laughing along in turn, Reinhard casually picked up the fallen club, and—

"Huh."

In his hand, the club fell apart along a smooth, clean-cut surface, landing with a dull thud.

Cleaved clean in two, right down the middle—its purpose fully and completely spent.

Slowly, Reinhard turned toward Subaru, a pained look in his eyes.

Following that gaze, sensing a bad feeling, Subaru pulled up the hem of his tracksuit. His torso was, as before, a hideous shade of deep purple from the bruising—but now something new had appeared.

—Suddenly, a single red line had been drawn straight across it, horizontally.

"Ah, crap, this—even I can see where this is going."

Sharp pain arrived first, as the vanguard.

And in the next instant—Subaru's stomach split open in a horizontal line, and a great gush of fresh blood burst forth.

"—Wai—Subaru!?"

Nearby, Emilia's voice rang out, frantic with urgency.

*Ah, and just when I'd finally learned her real name—looks like it might be the end again, if things go badly.*

*—But even if it is, I'll come back here again.*

His vision tilted sharply. Perhaps his body was collapsing. Reinhard's face showed alarm, and Emilia, peering close by his side, wore an expression of anguish on her lovely features.

*—Ah, she's seriously cute even when she's panicking. This other world fantasy, man.*

With that same thought as always as his last, agony and shock washed over Subaru's consciousness like a crashing wave, sweeping it away.
