Arc 10 Chapter 5: "The Consequences of the Detour"

—When it came to travel by way of Reinhard, the mental preparation Subaru had done proved useless, in two senses of the word.

"It might feel a little strange at first, but you should get used to it soon enough."

With that preface, what did Reinhard go on to do? He hoisted up the dragon carriage Subaru and the others were riding in — Patrasche included — without so much as a grunt, and went tearing across the sky at a furious pace. No — "tearing" wasn't the word. He *bolted*.

"Ababababababa...!"

Despite the scenery blurring past the windows at breakneck speed, almost none of that motion translated into any shaking inside the carriage — and it was that combination, that sheer visual glitch of it, that left Subaru clutching Beatrice tight, both of them trembling violently.

It wasn't as though he couldn't grasp the principle. In essence, what was happening here was the same thing as an Earth Dragon's Divine Protection of Wind Guard. The only problem was that the one producing the effect was a human being, running at a speed so absurd it made questioning common sense feel pointless.

"I got put through this once before, y'know~ Now it's Subaru-oniisan and everyone's turn to feel it too~"

"E-even so, is this something a person can actually get used to?"

Meili sat placidly on the seat, propping her chin on her knee. At the question from Petra, who clung nervously to the hem of her cloak, playing the seasoned veteran with an air of composure, Meili gave a little "hmph" of a laugh.

"So there are things that scare even Petra-chan~ Who'd have guessed."

"It's not so much scary as — when something totally outside what you imagined happens, of course you'd be startled, is all. Though it seems the Sword Saint-sama is a lot more outrageous than I'd heard."

"...Even I've never heard tell of any past Sword Saint being this far beyond common sense. I'd wager Reinhard here is simply a case unto himself."

Seeing that Petra had turned the conversation his way in the latter half of her exchange with Meili, Rom-jii answered so, patting his own head.

Rom-jii, who had wedged his massive frame uncomfortably into the carriage seat, likewise seemed unaccustomed to this Reinhard-style locomotion — one hand pressed to the wall for the sake of his own peace of mind.

It seemed this wasn't a mode of travel the Felt Camp employed as a matter of course. Which was a shame, in its own way — it struck Subaru as an incredible advantage to have.

"If we relied on a method like this outside of emergencies, folk would say we'd been working the Sword Saint unfairly, and our reputation would suffer for it. And it's the kingdom that suffers just as much if the Sword Saint's authority gets diminished... Handle it carelessly, and it's not just leaving the country that'd get banned — this whole method of travel might be outlawed too."

"Ah, that law that sounds like a joke, the Reinhard Law..."

To Rom-jii, whose lips twisted wryly, Subaru named the absurd, otherworld-specific piece of international law.

The Reinhard Law, true to its name, applied to one person and one person only — the Sword Saint, Reinhard van Astrea — and existed for the sole purpose of keeping him from ever leaving the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica. It sounded like a joke, but once you'd witnessed firsthand just how far outside the norm the man was, it became hard to call it overkill.

That said—

"I mean, sure, Reinhard's strength is ridiculous — basically ridiculous, full stop — but I'd like people to at least give him credit for not being the type to go cause trouble in some other country."

"Hoh. And here you are, from a rival camp, singing Reinhard's praises like that."

"Well, he's my friend, and it'd be sad if he had to stay home alone while the rest of us went on a trip abroad. Like a trip to Vollachia... nah, there's nothing fun in Vollachia. Not unless Flop-san and Medium-san pull some strings and build us an amusement park or something."

If only they could somehow slip it past Abel — or talk him into it — and squander the Empire's treasury on entertainment long enough to put together one grand amusement park.

"Garfiel, what is it you're staring at, I wonder."

"Nah, I figured if I could do somethin' like that too, might make things easier on Boss Man and the rest, y'know? But I guess it's like they say — 'to each his own knee-vela' or however that goes — ain't no way I could pull the same trick as him."

While Subaru was off picturing his grand plans for Vollachia Land, Garfiel — addressed by Beatrice — gnashed his fangs and lamented as much.

Certainly, in terms of raw strength Garfiel probably could do the same thing as Reinhard, but without the Divine Protection of Wind Guard to go with it, the ride would turn into something like hell. And besides, working his little brother like a plow horse felt wrong to Subaru's conscience, emergency or not.

"Ah, so that's why the Reinhard method never got put into practical use. Yeah, I can see why that'd weigh on you."

*"I'm glad you understand. I feel just as reluctant to do this outside of emergencies myself."*

"You just waltzed right into the conversation like it was nothing..."

Reinhard, sending his voice as though it were the most natural thing, was making use of his Divine Protection of Telepathy — something Subaru had experienced firsthand once before, back in Priestella.

This time, it seemed, he'd sent his voice to more than just Subaru — Petra and the others, with Reinhard's voice suddenly ringing in their heads, wore expressions that couldn't quite hide their surprise. Meili, for her part, apparently had some resistance to the Divine Protection of Telepathy as well, and kept right on wearing her smug look.

In any case—

"Anyway, the shock of the Reinhard method has more or less settled down for now, but there's still another bombshell I haven't finished processing. Is it really true? — About this Filore girl showing up."

*"—Yes. It's an undeniable fact."*

He couldn't see it, but Subaru could tell that Reinhard, confirming it, had nodded — and he swallowed down, once again, the shock of the fact he'd just been told, as a heavy breath.

It was the astonishing new development that had unfolded in the royal capital while Subaru and the others were at the Pleiades Watchtower — contact from the Divine Dragon Church, and the appearance of a person named Filore who had made her emblem glow.

That an organization which had, until now, kept quietly out of sight would suddenly emerge bearing a fact impossible to ignore where the Royal Selection was concerned — that, naturally, shook even Subaru, as knight to the royal candidate Emilia.

And yet—

"—Crusch-san pulling through is the best news of all."

The tremor that had run through the Royal Selection was certainly a development he couldn't ignore, but of everything brought up, the topic that carried the greatest weight for Subaru was Crusch's full recovery.

In Priestella, Crusch had fallen prey to the Sin Archbishop of Lust and been left bearing a curse that inflicted unending suffering on her body. Having stood on the same battlefield as her and yet failed to protect her, the guilt had lingered inside Subaru — long, deep, like a thorn that would not come out.

"If it meant really ripping that thing off of Crusch-san's body, I'd have been fine with every last inch of mine turning pitch black."

Crusch, drenched in the blood of Lust, had suffered damage too horrible to look at. Subaru too had received that same baptism of blood, and yet the harm it did him was vastly different from what it did to her.

Of course, the effects had shown up all over his body, but unlike Crusch, who went on suffering, at worst it was like getting a hideous, botched tattoo on his hands and feet. And even that had been wiped clean off his arm entirely when it got blown away, leaving the marks on his legs as about the only reminder — the kind of thing that startled him when he happened to notice it in the bath or the bathroom.

For whatever reason, Subaru's body had been able to take on Crusch's affliction in exchange for pain of his own. Saving her that way had been an option on the table — but—

"Crusch-san never gave her okay to it."

After things were settled in Priestella, she'd refused his offer to take on the curse, saying she couldn't let that burden fall on someone headed to the Pleiades Watchtower — and ever since, her condition had weighed on his mind.

That it had healed was, without question, wonderful news to Subaru.

Though, mind you—

"What's hardest to bear is that the very people it happened to can't simply celebrate the recovery outright."

Just as Rom-jii's weighty words implied, Crusch's recovery — the fact that it had been brought about by the hands of an organization called the Divine Dragon Church — meant something bitterly painful for her and her people.

That much, Subaru understood too. And because he understood, he couldn't say anything careless — couldn't glibly claim that being alive was worth it, even if it left her badly disadvantaged in the Royal Selection.

"But this Filore person showing up, and on top of that making the emblem that proves she's a shrine maiden glow — that's not good, is it?"

"Yeah, that's right~ Just when a spot opened up 'cause one Royal Selection candidate dropped out, that spot's gonna get filled right back up, and we're back to square one~"

"...Meili-chan, if you don't choose your words more carefully I'm going to get mad too, you know. We went all the way to that tower because of that."

"Which the guy in the helmet went and ruined anyway, though~... Ohh, don't be mad at me. Okaay, okaay, my bad, my bad~"

As if remembering her role as a little devil, Meili — having mocked all manner of things that way — apologized half-heartedly to Petra, whose gaze had turned severe. After a small sigh at Meili's attitude, Petra pressed on with an "anyway,"

"Never mind Meili-chan's bad mouth... what's not good isn't about the whole open-spot business. Because that Filore person — she's blonde with red eyes, right?"

"So I heard. That would mean..."

"—That's the trait of the Lugunica royal family, isn't it? And, if I remember, the name of the princess who was taken away fifteen years ago was..."

"—Filore."

Petra pressed a finger to her lips as she cast back through her own knowledge like that. At the end of her words, just as a pause built before the crucial name, someone else picked it up. — Rom-jii.

Rom-jii, drawing every eye in the carriage onto himself, kept his own gaze dropped between his knees as he spoke.

"Fifteen years ago, the name of the younger prince's daughter who vanished from the royal castle was Filore. Precisely the same name, it seems, as this nun of the Divine Dragon Church who's just marched into the castle."

"And on top of that, they say this nun made her emblem glow in front of a great many people at the castle, I suppose. If that story's true, it makes sense that trouble enough to shake the Royal Selection has broken out, I suppose."

Having finished sharing the facts that had shaken Royal Lugunica Castle to its foundations, along with the circumstances surrounding them, a grave and solemn air spread through the carriage.

Yet into that, Subaru — resolved to deliberately not read the room — spoke up with a "hey, so."

"I'm not entirely convinced by that."

*"—Not convinced, in what sense?"*

"I'm starting to get used to holding conversations with a voice in my head, which is its own thing, but — what everyone's worried about is this, right? That the Divine Dragon Church's Filore might be the member of the Lugunica royal family who disappeared fifteen years ago. And that the emblem glowing is basically proof of royal blood."

"Yes, that's right. What about that don't you buy?"

"Well, I mean, that's the exact same condition Felt was under too, isn't it?"

Unfortunately, no matter how far back Subaru traced his memory of that accursed day the Royal Selection began, he'd never actually heard Felt's declaration of candidacy. He'd been thrown out of the throne room after a massive blunder before it got that far.

But even though he hadn't been present, he'd been properly filled in afterward on what had happened in that room, and what Felt had said, once he'd been removed.

Even there, the matter of the royal family from fifteen years ago should already have come up as an issue.

"So basically, the situation's the same as it was with Felt. Well, personally I think a princess secretly smuggled out of the castle who turns out to have been living as a commoner in town is a pretty exciting story, so I'm partial to the version where Felt's the royal."

"Hmm, I see. — But if, as you imagine, Felt really is the princess of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica, then the Royal Selection is decided — Felt wins outright. Are you fine with that?"

"Eh?"

At Rom-jii's remark, delivered with one eye shut, Subaru's eyes went wide.

He wondered for a moment if it was just a joke in poor taste, but no correction came. What's more, everyone besides Subaru seemed to agree with Rom-jii's reasoning.

Finding himself unexpectedly in the minority — no, a party of one — left Subaru shaken.

"No, no, no, wait, why?"

"Why, ya ask? 'Cause that's just how it is, ain't it. In the first place, the whole reason we're runnin' a Royal Selection is 'cause the royal family up and vanished, right? But if it turns out that ain't so, then it only makes sense the royal who's still kickin' takes the throne, don't it?"

"Y-you mean hereditary succession?"

*"I'd appreciate a gentler way of putting it, but yes, that's the gist of it."*

Chided by both Garfiel and Reinhard — the two men of action — Subaru became aware of the gap between his own assumptions and those of someone raised in this world, and let out an "aah."

For Natsuki Subaru, born and raised in Japan, it was only natural for a nation's leader to be chosen by some factor other than bloodline. The very fact that the Royal Selection resembled the election systems he was familiar with also played its part.

But for someone from this world, it was only natural, on the contrary, that the next king after the current one would be his prince, and once that prince became king, his own child would be the next prince.

Therefore, if it were confirmed that Felt was of royal blood, then in the natural course of things, Felt would ascend to the next throne, and the Royal Selection would lose its very function—

"Which means, if it'd been confirmed that Felt was royalty, that would've been really bad!"

"It's precisely because that stayed at the stage of mere suspicion that the Royal Selection was able to proceed at all. Are you sure you're actually fit to be that girl's knight, carrying on like this?"

"Cut it out! I just happen to be a little slow on common sense, that's all! My side's got Beako, Petra, and Garfiel — smart, young folks who've been raised right!"

"Allow me to correct you — Betty, who is pretty and charming, is the most mature one here, I suppose."

Held tight in his arms and set on his lap, Beatrice puffed out her chest at that. Watching Subaru and the others fuss over her head, patting it this way and that, Rom-jii patted his own head with an exasperated look.

True enough, he'd made something of a fool of himself over a small gap in understanding. But even so, Subaru's round of questions wasn't over yet.

"I tripped up a little there, but my question's not settled. I get that if royalty gets confirmed, the whole Royal Selection loses its point, but at the end of the day, the doubt over whether this Filore-chan-san is the real deal is exactly the same as it was for Felt. So then..."

*"—Then it must be determined which of the two is the true royal."*

"—————"

It might have been words chosen, as much as possible, to keep emotion out of them. Precisely because of that, that voiceless statement of Reinhard's felt as if it carried an edge sharp as a blade.

Subaru held his breath, letting the meaning of those words sink into his mind. — Determining which of Felt and Filore was the genuine article, meant—

"It's been unconfirmed suspicion that's kept the Royal Selection going up 'til now. But once there are two candidates who might be the vanished royal, it's not something we can leave be, not anymore. We'll have to worry, too, about the chance that someone with ill intent has slipped into the Royal Selection."

"Ill intent... whichever one turns out to be the royal, that doesn't mean the other one's automatically confirmed evil, does it? Maybe it's just a coincidence that they're both blond and red-eyed..."

"It's precisely because such coincidences don't happen that it's considered a trait unique to the royal family, I wonder."

"But! The emblem lit up for both of them, didn't it!?"

Given a logical explanation from both Rom-jii and Beatrice, Subaru grew emotional. He was in a state where the only way he could argue back was emotionally.

To begin with, even Subaru himself couldn't explain why he was getting so worked up about this.

But if part of the reason he liked Reinhard and Rom-jii was having met them right when he was first summoned, the same was true of Felt. He didn't want Felt's way of being, her efforts, denied for no better reason than "she might not be royalty after all."

Still less did he want to paint Filore, whom he hadn't even met yet, as some villain and one-sidedly accuse her of scheming something.

More than anything—

"If you people wanted the succession to go to royalty no matter what, then when Felt turned up, you should've dropped the whole Royal Selection right then and done a DNA test or whatever to confirm it one way or the other. Instead you started the Royal Selection without doing that, and Emilia, and everyone, how much did they—"

"Subaru..."

Beatrice, on his lap, grew worried for Subaru as he bit down hard on his lip and choked back his voice. At the feel of her hand gently taking his, Subaru closed his eyes and took one long, deep breath. Even so, his heart, pounding hard with violent emotion, refused to settle down easily.

Emilia, Felt, Crusch, Anastasia, Priscilla — the Royal Selection was something each of them had entered staking their own convictions, even their very lives, on it.

That an unexpected interference had barged in was no reason, to Subaru's mind, to accept the whole premise being twisted out of shape.

"If it comes to this..."

"If it comes to this, what exactly do you intend to do, I wonder."

"Like, me and Reinhard staging some kind of protest demanding the Royal Selection continue..."

*"—I see."*

"Don't 'I see' this! Don't go spouting off something so terrifying! And you — what do you think you're doing, recklessly egging the Sword Saint on? What'll you do if he actually goes through with it!"

"If it comes to that, count me in too!"

"Garf-san, that's just gonna make things more complicated right now, so could you please be quiet? Please?"

At Subaru's bitter, cornered words, Rom-jii and Petra both objected fiercely. A rebuke both loud and quiet followed, and Garfiel, glared down by Petra, wilted with a small "gao..."

But it wasn't as if the whole thing had been a joke, not entirely. — No, if anything, he'd been fairly serious. He had to show that much resolve, if he meant to demonstrate the spirit of a Royal Selection candidate's knight.

"There's so much to do once we reach the royal capital...! Feels like I've been running nonstop without a single day off for like half a year straight..."

"That's still just the beginning, I suppose. Why, Betty feels as though she's been going nonstop for something like eleven and a half years now, I suppose."

"Don't say something that scary, my head's gonna get all scrambled up..."

To Subaru's wisecrack, Beatrice topped it with a wisecrack carrying even more force than his own. Trembling at her words, Subaru sighed at the sheer size and number of troubles waiting for him in the royal capital.

He was worried about Emilia, having witnessed all this firsthand, and he wanted to go visit Crusch, whose body had apparently healed. He also had to at least lay eyes on this Filore person, and he needed to press the higher-ups, who no doubt had a trauma-inducing amount to say, directly about what would happen to the Royal Selection. He wanted to look into the matter of the Al-orb hanging from his neck too, and every factor that had driven that outrage to happen.

But for the moment, the thing he needed to deal with right now was—

*"—Subaru, we're arriving."*

"Seriously? That's amazing. Sorry for asking you for a detour."

Just as he shifted his thoughts to that, Reinhard's report came at the exact same moment.

He'd already guessed as much from the scenery blurring past at high speed, but the travel speed really had been extraordinary. For speed alone, Cecilus might well move even faster, but unfortunately the man probably lacked the raw arm strength to shoulder a dragon carriage while doing it, so a "Cecilus method" didn't seem likely to work out.

Carrying one person on his back, maybe he could pull off something similar — or so Subaru was thinking, when the scenery outside the window abruptly regained its sense of reality.

The high-speed blur came to a stop, and the carriage was slowly lowered to the ground. In other words, they had arrived at their destination. And as if to prove it—

"—Just what in the world is going on here!?"

"—————"

At the very first voice he heard, Subaru sprang to his feet, and — still holding Beatrice, who let out a startled "nkya!" — bolted out of the carriage.

There stood Reinhard, not a single drop of sweat on him despite having run the whole way shouldering a dragon carriage, and Patrasche, who'd apparently been carried along in a state of considerable tension.

And, staring wide-eyed at the carriage's absurd arrival, standing before the gates of the dearly familiar Roswaal Manor, was someone with blue hair—

"Guh!"

"Subaru!? Wh-wh-what's wrong, I wonder!?"

Beatrice, dragged out along with him, blinked in bewilderment at Subaru, who clutched his chest and dropped to his knees right there without meaning to. Flustered and fussing, patting at his head and shoulders, Beatrice fretted over him, while Subaru — letting her do as she pleased — felt his chest ache at the overwhelming sight before him.

Because standing there in front of Roswaal Manor to welcome them was—

"I've come to realize just how precious that familiar sight of you really is..."

"...And you, meanwhile, are exactly the same no matter when or where we run into each other, aren't you."

So saying, with an expression that was somewhere between exasperated and relieved — a wonderfully complex look all its own — she in her maid uniform, yes, Rem in her maid uniform, let out a sigh.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼△

Taking shameless advantage of the Reinhard method's speed, Subaru had begged, on the way to the royal capital, to be allowed a stop at Roswaal Manor.

The purpose, needless to say, was so that Rem could be present for the moment they retrieved her memories from Roy Alphard, the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony — itself one of the reasons for heading to the royal capital in the first place.

"I don't think a conversation with 'Gluttony' is gonna go smoothly, but Al managed to drag it out of him somehow, one way or another. So there's no reason I can't do it either. Besides, I already missed the moment Ram and Rem got reunited. If I miss the moment Rem's memories come back too, at this point, I won't be able to recover...!"

To Subaru's impassioned, near-bloody-tears plea like that, Reinhard — who surely wanted to get back to Felt's side even a single second sooner — nonetheless agreed without so much as a hint of displeasure.

There was really no other way to put it than that he was a genuinely great friend. Subaru wanted to take on any request Reinhard made of him too, but if he were asked to shoulder a dragon carriage and take a detour of several hundred kilometers, whether he'd agree so readily was doubtful.

But Reinhard had actually gone and done it.

And what's more, that wasn't all Reinhard had taken on—

"—Sorry, for having you come along all the way out here."

"Not at all, I don't mind. Besides, though I have no awareness of it myself, I too have suffered from the Authority of the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony. It seems I've been made to forget at least one friend."

"Yeah... Come to think of it, apparently that guy turned it around and said getting forgotten was a good chance to challenge you, Reinhard. Something about how you don't know his tricks now, so this is his shot at winning."

"That's... honestly, something to look forward to."

Watching Reinhard out of the corner of his eye — eyebrows lifting slightly, then breaking into a genuinely delighted smile — Subaru privately apologized to Julius, thinking he might have said something unnecessary and lit a fire he shouldn't have.

He might have just crushed whatever slim chance of victory Julius could have had. Then again, knowing Julius, he might well decide it wasn't fair for him alone to know his own tricks, and go out of his way to reveal his hand instead. So Subaru held off on apologizing anywhere but in his own head.

—That he found himself chasing such useless thoughts was itself a sign of just how heavy the air was in this place.

"...Guess that's what you get from a place called the Prison Tower."

A strangely cold sweat trickled down the forehead of Subaru as he muttered that.

He wasn't particularly panicking, and he hadn't been running. And yet the cold sweat that had welled up and run down anyway was his whole body's rejection response to this space.

Built near the royal castle, at the topmost level of the royal capital of Lugunica, the stone spire served as a prison housing some of the kingdom's most hardened felons. Naturally, so that none of the prisoners could easily be encountered face to face, every cell's iron door was sealed tight.

It wasn't the sort of jail you often see in manga, with prisoners visible behind iron bars, and stepping inside, Subaru felt something like relief mixed with letdown, at about a seven-to-three ratio.

"The folks on Sword Slave Island — some of them were criminals through and through, sure, but they were a bit more of the easygoing sort of villain..."

Chalking it up to national character felt a bit odd, but the crimes committed by Hiain, Weitz, Idra, and the others who'd been his companions on Sword Slave Island Ginunhive were basically the rowdy, brawling kind, without any real malice behind them. Sometimes, as with Hiain, someone got thrown in with the merciless brand of "fallen into slavery," but even what counted as a so-called serious felony there was, at most, the difference between a small ruckus and a big one.

By contrast, the atmosphere in this Prison Tower, where Lugunica's serious felons were held, carried a stench of blood entirely different from that island, at least by Subaru's own gut sense, having known the place.

"Is the rate of Witch Cult members locked up in here pretty high, then?"

"Yes. Considering that they're mostly active here in Lugunica, that assumption is correct. In fact, 'Wrath' is imprisoned in the basement here as well."

"The basement... so they're keeping some distance from 'Gluttony,' huh."

"Better safe than sorry, that's all. There are differences in how each of them is being held, but we can't carelessly let anyone near 'Wrath.' Even the other prisoners are kept away, for that matter."

The Authority of the Sin Archbishop of Wrath, Sirius Romanée-Conti, was something like a mental contamination that spread through the air, swallowing anyone caught up in her words and atmosphere. Given how far-reaching that influence was, it was hard to say the danger had been eliminated just because her movement was sealed.

In fact, it even seemed possible that some part of the unpleasant presence hanging over this Prison Tower came from the Sin Archbishop of Wrath herself.

"I've got a gut-level aversion to it, but I guess I'll have to talk to her too, eventually."

"Call on me when that time comes. I'll be there with you, just as I am this time."

"That's reassuring. I mean it, I'm counting on you."

How reassuring it was, Reinhard thumping his own chest to take it on. And yet Sirius's Authority was the kind of thing where Reinhard's own strength could backfire. It would need careful handling.

In any case, the reason Subaru and the others had come to the Prison Tower today wasn't her. Sorry, but Sirius would just have to keep enjoying the cold basement air a while longer.

Instead—

"—This way, please."

So said the Prison Tower guard leading the way, gesturing to a single door. Subaru nodded to the man, whose face carried equal parts tension and professional bearing, and looked to the door.

It was a door that made your spirits sink just looking at it, radiating a cold, oppressive heaviness. — Beyond it, the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony was imprisoned.

"Though, given he's probably in the same kind of state as the Al-orb, whether he's even conscious in there is anyone's guess."

Measured against his own experience, being sealed inside the black orb had felt like being submerged in lukewarm water, unable to move — some indescribable sensation.

In an environment where you couldn't even keep your consciousness clear, Beatrice had worked out the mechanism of the Forbidden Arts for Subaru's sake — her hard-working, plucky spirit made him want to hand her the Subaru's Person of the Year Award, but the list of nominees alone was already filling up both hands, so he'd need to deliberate at length before deciding.

"Subaru? Why are you kneading Betty's cheek, I wonder."

"Nah, since I can't hand out the Person of the Year Award right away, I figured I'd show my affection some other way instead..."

"Once we're done here you can do as you please, so focus on what's in front of you right now, I suppose."

Whether she'd seen through his need for physical contact born of nervousness or not, Subaru pouted a little at Beatrice's earnest scolding, then let out a breath and said, "Yeah, you're right."

Beatrice at the front gate, Reinhard at the rear — this was the best formation Subaru had assembled for visiting "Gluttony" in the Prison Tower. What he really wanted was to have Rem, the person most concerned, present as well, but either way, he didn't expect the first round of talks to go smoothly.

Persuading — or threatening — "Gluttony" was expected to require patient, repeated attempts.

"Either way, he's sealed up with Shadow Magic, so we need Beako to undo it."

"Leave it to me, I suppose. And if he starts thrashing about the moment I undo it, then, Sword Saint, I'll be counting on you to beat him down, I suppose."

"Yes, understood. I won't let him lay so much as a finger on either you or Subaru. Please rest easy."

"Honestly, that's a huge relief. All right then..."

Counting on the strength of allies almost too reliable, Subaru gave the guard a nod, and had him unlock the door. Then, as the guard slowly pushed the door open, he clenched his fists tight.

—Deep within the dimly lit tower, in a cell that felt especially dark, Roy Alphard was imprisoned, bound by Shadow Magic into something like a monolith. They would wake this Roy, and force him to disgorge Rem's memories — the names and memories of every last victim.

Exchanging words with a Sin Archbishop was bound to be a battle that chipped away at one's own sanity, but if there was a certain reward waiting on the other side of it, Subaru was resolved to see it through, stubbornly, no matter—

"—Ah?"

Subaru, who had been about to hold his breath for the sake of that resolve, let it slip out instead, unthinking.

As his eyes began adjusting to the dim gloom, the sight the cell revealed was not what Subaru had expected. What should have been waiting for him was Roy Alphard, embedded in a black, slab-like monolith, reduced to something like a specimen.

But what was there instead was a sight entirely beyond anything he'd anticipated.

"—————"

Unable to make sense of it, Subaru froze in place — but Reinhard gently pushed his shoulder aside and stepped into the cell in his stead. Right beside him, Beatrice pressed close, leaning her whole body against Subaru — no, she was holding him up.

Because the strength had drained from Subaru's legs, and he was on the verge of collapsing.

With the near-collapsing Subaru and the Beatrice supporting him behind him, Reinhard advanced into the depths of the cell, looked at what lay scattered across the cold prison floor, and shook his head.

Then he turned back and said:

"—I'm sorry to say, he's dead."

There, at the feet of Reinhard as he delivered those words, lay the gruesome scene: the pitiful corpse of the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony — Roy Alphard, the "Foul Eater" — torn limb from limb, his body ripped apart and scattered, strewn across the floor.
