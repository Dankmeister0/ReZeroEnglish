Arc 9 Chapter 41: "Declare to the Usurper"

—Gods, Buddhas, Od Lagna-sama. I swear I will never drink alcohol again as long as I live.

△▼△▼△▼△

"—This is all-out war. You're welcome to go down quietly, beaten to a pulp."

The moment Petra declared this, there in the mountains where Al had vanished, the tension swelled all at once and burst.

In that instant, the remaining members of Al's group, who had been on guard at Petra and the others' appearance—the 'Divine Dragon' Volcanica, the 'Gluttony' Roy Alphard, and Heinkel—all moved at once.

However, the three of them acted with absolutely no coordination between them, each going his—or her—own separate way.

"—"

Seeing them spring into motion, Petra— no, her actual first move had been the Flash Remenade, which had stolen Al's attention with Rem's presence, so this was the second arrow in her quiver.

It was a plan to fracture Al's group—each of whom was a formidable enemy not to be underestimated—into a disorganized rabble, tearing it apart in midair, so to speak.

"First we cut Al-san out of the loop, and give everyone left behind a huge scare. And then—"

'On your marks, go, and everyone starts doing their own thing!'

Before their eyes, as Petra's voice and Imaginary Subaru's voice ran together, the 'Dragon,' the Sin Archbishop, and Heinkel all acted on their own individual judgment.

It was a full mobilization of force from the very first move, meant to scatter their choices as widely as possible.

Of course, this plan also carried a real risk of the 'Divine Dragon' scooping them all up in one net—

"—I bet the way Al-san won the 'Divine Dragon' over is the same as what happened with me."

That was Petra's guess as to why the famed 'Divine Dragon' Volcanica had betrayed its side.

Petra had heard from Emilia about the state Volcanica had been in atop the Watchtower, and understood that the long years had left the 'Dragon' senile. —And as for the method of forcibly stuffing 'something' into that blank space in its consciousness, there was no one who understood it better, from firsthand experience, than Petra herself.

Just as Petra had used the Book of the Dead to house Natsuki Subaru as part of her own consciousness, it was highly likely that Volcanica, too, was housing some presence sympathetic to Al. And that presence shared Al's goal quite firmly, having resolved to turn the whole world into their enemy together.

Petra was betting on that—on Al and Volcanica sharing a vow of 'no killing.'

'—!!'

The first to make a move, springing into swift action despite its massive bulk, was the 'Divine Dragon.'

The 'Dragon's' mighty jaws opened, and the breath it unleashed was not the light of annihilation that reduces everything to nothing—but a mist of pure fresh water, conjured using the dragon's vast reserves of mana. It swallowed up Petra and the rest of the Royal Selection joint army, the Aldebusters, all at once, drenching every one of them to the skin.

For a moment confusion arose, no one grasping the 'Divine Dragon's' intent, but that uncertainty was resolved at once.

"—"

A creaking sound in the air struck their eardrums, hindering any attempt to instinctively brace themselves. Looking down, a faint frost had settled over their soaked bodies, and in a blink Petra and the others were being frozen solid—

"—Ul Goa."

In that instant, red flames swept back over the forest that had begun to blur white with mist.

It forced down, by sheer power, the freezing effect the 'Divine Dragon' had aimed for through its thick fog, with an expansion of flame that blanketed the entire field of view, instantly melting the ice and drying even their soaked bodies in a heartbeat.

The one who did this, standing at the center of the group with a finger raised, blocking the 'Divine Dragon's' intent with a single incantation, was a mage of rare talent—the finest in the kingdom in skill, though the very worst in personality—Roswaal L. Mathers.

Needless to say, the higher-tier a spell is, the more delicate control it demands.

To respond instantly with an Ul-tier spell, which is far from something anyone can simply cast, and moreover to undo everyone's freezing on the spot, dry even their clothes, and do it all without inflicting a single burn—that was nothing short of divine skill.

Even the 'Divine Dragon,' which had lived since ancient times, was astonished that Roswaal had pulled it off in one breath.

And into that moment—

"What you should be paying attention to is over here. Attend."

That quiet, properly measured voice came paired with a blow beyond imagining.

Clind, leaping up with a light spring, drove one long leg in a mighty kick into the side of the momentarily still 'Dragon's' face. The force of that kick was cannonball-like, rivaling even Garfiel's, and the 'Dragon,' its stance broken, put a hand to the ground as a cloud of dust rose.

The 'Divine Dragon's' opening move had been met and pinned down head-on by the Roswaal-and-Clind combo.

Meanwhile, bursting out through that cloud of dust, fangs bared, came the Sin Archbishop of 'Gluttony'—

"Ha-ha! Lining up all these delicious-looking mugs—this is basically love, isn't it!"

Roy, drooling with fervor as he leapt into the battle line, was unlike the 'Divine Dragon,' which had tried to stop the entire Aldebusters—he leapt straight at a single, clearly chosen target.

Contrary to the wild motive of appetite, Roy closed in on his prey with an oddly refined agility. The one at the point of 'Gluttony's' foul tongue was the peach-haired maid standing amid the group—

"—Ram."

The hand he extended readied his meal by touching something invisible to the eye.

Roy, calling out her name with a leering grin, and Ram, her expression frozen at being named, locked eyes for a moment—and the next instant, the Sin Archbishop licked the palm that had touched her, showing it off deliberately.

The Authority of 'Gluttony' activated, and a new tragedy began to bud there—except it didn't.

"Ugh."

Roy's eyes went wide, and he clapped a hand over his mouth as if nauseated by the backlash of a failed 'Corrosion.' Before the faltering Roy, Ram spun her body around gracefully in one smooth turn—

"Idiot."

Ram's leaping knee slammed brilliantly up into the Sin Archbishop's jaw.

"Damn it, damn it, damn it! After coming this far...!"

While the 'Divine Dragon' and 'Gluttony,' though aiming at different targets, had each chosen to attack, the last one remaining—Heinkel—made a different choice.

Choosing something different from both the 'Dragon's' cold breath and the Sin Archbishop's gluttonous appetite, Heinkel, keeping the front at bay with his drawn sword, leapt backward rather than forward. He likely meant to put distance between himself and the fight, hoping someone other than himself would turn the tide.

But—

"Things won't go so conveniently for you, milord."

"Milord, honestly, I've wanted to do this for a long time."

Down upon him dropped the twin sisters, who had been lying in wait up in the trees ever since the moment of 'Compression.'

Flam and Grassis—the twins who served House Astrea—drove kicks in from front and back, sandwiching his torso and spine between them; and, ironically, the one they struck was the very man who was head of the house they served.

"Guh...!"

The kicks of the sisters, trained in the Flow Method, produced a boom that belied the fact that they came from such small girls.

Under an impact that felt like his organs were being wrung out, Heinkel's eyes rolled white and he let out an agonized groan as if he were dying. —The three simultaneous clashes had gone almost exactly as intended, each opening with the upper hand secured.

And on top of that—

'—Game change.'

The snap of Imaginary Subaru's fingers—audible only to Petra—rang out loud and clear. At that signal, the tide of battle shifted.

—A further division wrought by 'Compression' tore Al's group apart yet again.

△▼△▼△▼△

—Petra Leyte had come to recognize just how terrifying Al's group truly was, more strongly than any mere result could show.

Ever since his betrayal atop the Pleiades Watchtower, Al had brought the 'Divine Dragon' over to his side, evaded Reinhard and the Felt Camp, and successfully broken the Sin Archbishop of 'Gluttony' out of prison in the royal capital.

In everything that had happened so far, the accomplices acting alongside Al had each surely played a significant role—but even so, the greatest threat remained none other than Al himself.

Having been told the truth behind Al's abnormal power over fate—the power to draw in whatever result he sought—Petra had found herself convinced beyond any doubt.

That, of course, was because of the truth about Natsuki Subaru that she had learned from reading the Book of the Dead.

'So Al's got the power to Leap too... then I understand better than anyone how dangerous that is. And unlike me, whose own combat ability is pathetic, Al's the type who can fight on his own—so simply put, I'm up against an upgraded version of myself.'

"...Subaru's plenty reliable too, when he's together with Beatrice-chan. And when it comes to little tricks and cunning schemes, you're not even second to Otto-san."

'But the original me is currently caught along with Beatrice, and "little tricks and cunning schemes" isn't exactly the kind of skill set a strong guy proudly shows off...'

Imaginary Subaru said things like that, sounding unsure of himself, but setting aside romantic feelings or sympathy for the underdog, Petra didn't think Subaru was inferior to Al—much less some downgraded version of him.

On that point, she had also heard an interesting theory from old Rom, who had actually been the one to work out that Al's Authority might be time leaping.

—The idea being that Al's time leap is, after all, likely limited to redoing only a short span of time.

"If it really were the exact same power as Subaru's, then to begin with, he should've been able to just dodge the clash with Felt-sama and Rom-ojiisan and the others before it even happened, right?"

'But you shouldn't put too much faith in that either. There's a good chance it just happened that his restart point landed at a moment where dodging Felt and the others wasn't an option. Even I wasn't able to completely stop Petelgeuse from attacking Emilia-tan and the others.'

"I think it was pretty much a total shutout, though—"

She started to say more, but out of consideration for 'Subaru's' feelings, Petra fell silent.

Just as Petra said, if 'Subaru' had perfectly contained the Sin Archbishop of 'Sloth' who attacked Roswaal Manor and Arlam Village, the tragedies that followed for Rem and Crusch would never have happened.

Subaru had done everything he possibly could. That was Petra's assessment—but Subaru himself saw it differently.

That difference tied, sadly enough, into just how low Natsuki Subaru's self-evaluation ran, and to Petra that was enormously frustrating—but even so—

"Let's set that aside for now... because it's not just that outrageous Al-san we're up against, but also the 'Divine Dragon' cooperating with him, and the shinobi maid, and the Sin Archbishop, and Heinkel-san too."

'What an outrageous lineup, now that I think about it again. A "Dragon," a shinobi, a Sin Archbishop, and even Reinhard's old man—being the father of that Reinhard, he's probably no easy nut to crack either.'

"No comment on Heinkel-san."

To 'Subaru,' who wore a troubled expression, Petra pressed a finger in an X shape against her lips, refusing to answer.

She had traveled alongside Heinkel only for a brief moment in the Vollachian Empire, and since he had deliberately put on an attitude meant to push people away, she'd had next to no real connection with him.

The only exception was Garfiel, who had bothered with him to some degree—but if Garfiel learned about Heinkel's current behavior, he would surely be devastated. Just imagining a crestfallen Garfiel stirred up a simmering anger toward Heinkel inside Petra as well.

The Emilia Camp was the sort of group where, when someone hurts one of their own, everyone gets angry together as one.

"Whew... I need to calm down. It won't mean anything unless Garf-san works through his own feelings himself, but we have to lay the path for him to do that."

'Easy to say "path," but I don't know where to even start...'

"Yeah, that's true. It's hard to know. But—"

Seeing 'Subaru' at a loss, Petra smiled mischievously and turned to look back. Drawn by her movement, 'Subaru' followed her gaze in the same direction.

There, she saw everyone who was here with them now—people that a Natsuki Subaru prone to bottling things up had, through repeated stumbles and falls and failures, nonetheless managed to bring results for, and to gather here.

'—'

"It's true, I think Al-san and the others are incredibly tough, and if we went about it normally, I think we wouldn't be able to lay a finger on them either. —But you know, we're also pretty far from normal ourselves, aren't we?"

Closing one eye in a wink, Petra put on what might have been one of the cutest faces of her life, smiling at the profile of a wide-eyed Imaginary Subaru as she said this.

And so, together with her wonderfully un-normal, dependable companions, she worked out their strategy—

—and, triggered by Otto Suwen's signal, the battle began.

—'Compression,' the Authority granted by the Witch Factor of 'Melancholy,' was by no means all-powerful.

The phenomenon 'Compression' brings about is, in the end, a compression of events that are already achievable—the representative case being something like shortening the time it takes to travel to a place that could be traveled to anyway.

Put the other way around, this also means that 'Compression' cannot make possible an event that would remain impossible no matter how much time were spent on it.

Hence, even splitting up Aldebaran's group was not an easy matter—after first hurling Aldebaran himself out of the battlefield, further dividing the group that remained behind was no simple task.

If the opponent grew wary of 'Compression,' the difficulty of applying the Authority's effect rose dramatically. And when the opponent happened to be the 'Divine Dragon' or a Sin Archbishop, merely carrying them off was itself an extremely difficult feat.

The weakness of 'Compression' was that, apart from activating it on a target who consented to its effect or one who was caught off guard, its applicable range narrowed drastically.

That weakness was what the Royal Selection joint army, the Aldebusters, overcame with a barrage of unconventional tactics.

They used sheer numbers to shake Heinkel, capitalized on Volcanica's psychological attachment to its 'no killing' principle, and turned away at the door Roy Alphard's 'Gluttony,' so absorbed as it was in its feast.

Every one of these was a gambit with no guarantee of success until it was actually carried out. —But then, no plan in this world ever comes with a hundred percent certainty.

All that exists is the effort to push closer to a hundred percent, and the conviction that one has done everything possible to that end.

And as for the resolve of those able to water that sprout of possibility—

"Ah, how delightful this is. Marvel."

Nodding thus, Clind, having landed upon the earth, fixed his gaze straight ahead on the 'Divine Dragon' Volcanica.

Volcanica, propping up its off-balance body with one hand, met Clind's gaze head-on. In the 'Dragon,' its golden eyes narrowing, could be felt the clear sprouting of a will of its own.

Just as Petra had guessed, another consciousness dwelt within the 'Divine Dragon's' Dragonhusk.

'—'

Volcanica, glaring down at them, had naturally been barely affected by Clind's attack a moment before. It might have succeeded in startling it, but the blow was far from enough to drive the creature into a corner.

Even so, the objective of that first ambush had been achieved. —In the field of battle facing the 'Divine Dragon's' Dragonhusk, only two of the Aldebusters remained to confront the usurper.

It had come down to just the two of them: Clind and Roswaal L. Mathers.

'...Same method as the one that blew away Origin, huh.'

Pushing itself up on the hand planted against the ground, the 'Divine Dragon' murmured this in a low, weighty voice.

The 'Dragon' swept its golden eyes over its surroundings and took note that they had been moved to a plain, set far apart from the mountains where they had just been.

It was proof that movement via 'Compression' had triggered, successfully cutting off a troublesome opponent.

But—

'Clind, was it? I figured it couldn't be, but... seems it's no mistake that you're the holder of the "Melancholy" Witch Factor.'

"—. Where did you get that information? Question."

'Learned it from a know-it-all deity acquaintance of mine. Though it was buried in the middle of a bunch of tall tales, so even I hadn't managed to recall it until just now.'

The 'Divine Dragon' shrugged its shoulders with a gesture uncannily human, and Clind pondered both the fact that the word 'Melancholy' had come out of its mouth, and the fact that he had been named as the Witch Factor's holder.

If the usurper could draw out even the Dragonhusk's knowledge, there would be nothing strange about it knowing that. But if that were the case, its behavior up until now didn't quite add up.

If one tried to make sense of both facts, the conclusion would be that the usurper had not fully mastered the Dragonhusk, and yet had been told of Clind's circumstances by someone—

"When you two go and understand each other all on your own like that, it makes me feel rather left ou-out, you know."

"My lord..."

"You're a man capable of fine, meticulous work, but taken too far, that's the sort of thing people call 'nervous.' How our opponent came by its knowledge isn't the poi-int. After all, the plan is working just fine, isn't it."

'A plan, huh.'

As Roswaal admonished Clind, the usurper spread wide the wings on its back. That alone whipped up a sudden gust, and the wind that buffeted them sent Clind and the others' hair streaming.

'Not that it's my place to say, but—leaving aside Roy and the old man and the rest of my own allies, wasn't it a blunder to get separated from your bunch over there?'

"Oh-oh? Now that's an interesting opinion, isn't i-it? May I hear more?"

'You already know, don't you. It's just that with a big crowd in range, there's a good chance it'd be easier for me to hold back and shrink down. With this frame and all this overflowing pow-wer, going easy isn't exactly simple, you know.'

"I see, that makes sense. If your aim were to create a situation where you couldn't perform at full capacity, then yes, that's exactly what you should have do-one... but..."

"—Unfortunately, that same condition applies to us as well. Parity."

'Say what?'

The Dragonhusk's usurper, having implied without saying so directly that they shouldn't set the stage for it to rampage in earnest, narrowed its eyes at Clind's single remark—and then, immediately after, found itself widening them.

Encircling the 'Divine Dragon' on every side, lights of every color had risen up, packed without a single gap.

At a glance, they looked as pale and lovely as a gathering of minor spirits materializing in a mana pool, but anyone with some grounding in magic would sense that each faint light was in fact a colossal mass of mana—the twinkling glimmer of a great spell of terrifying power.

Needless to say, the source of it all was, without question, the strongest mage the history of the world had ever seen—

"Now, don't go praising me like that, will you? For someone who knew my teacher to speak of me that way is truly, truly a heart-rending mistake, you know-ow."

Roswaal spoke as though he had glimpsed straight into their thoughts; if he meant to unleash his true ability at maximum firepower, allies standing nearby would only get in the way.

And that was—

"—The same is true for me. Affirm."

As he spoke, Clind raised a hand and removed the monocle he wore over his left eye.

In that instant, the air trembled faintly, and what had been faint gradually grew, little by little, until, with a sound like space itself cracking, the earth began to rumble.

It was not that the monocle itself was anything special.

What was needed was a shift of mindset—a single step of resolve to return to the self he had sealed away in the past. —By refusing to take that step, Clind had gone on forcing himself to remain a mere bystander.

When a girl with a shining soul had pointed out that melancholy and gloom of his, Clind had felt ashamed of himself.

Having received that final push, he could no longer go on standing still.

And so—

'...Oh, come now.'

With a creak of bone, two black horns thrust up from Clind's head, beneath his blue hair. These were neither the horns that marked the Oni Tribe nor the horns borne by Witchbeasts. —Neither one nor the other; there was only a single race in this world that grew such deep-black horns.

A race so diminished in number that it was thought all but extinct—the Dragonkin.

A new race, the next generation born of the mighty 'Dragons,' who chose to cast off their Dragonhusks and take on human form. Clind, too, was one of these Dragonkin, an existence of special significance to the world.

Because—

'...So I finally figured out what that strange feeling was. —You're the one, of this body's—'

"Any further words would be tasteless. Unnecessary."

The usurper still tried to press on with words, but Clind cut it off head-on.

No one else, whoever they might be, had more right than Clind to silence the usurper. No one was more fitting to do so than the true original owner of the Dragonhusk the usurper had stolen.

"—Disciple of the 'Witch,' Roswaal L. Mathers."

'—The "Divine Dragon," Volcanica.'

Holding his great spell aloft, Roswaal, planting his feet firmly on the ground, declared his name. In answer, the usurper, with equal shamelessness, called itself by that same name.

Following that same custom, how long had it been since he had last placed himself upon the stage? —Thinking so, Clind too, wrapping his body in violet lightning, declared his own name.

"—Dragonkin of the 'Divine Dragon,' Clind."

In that instant, the two bound to the 'Divine Dragon' sprang into motion, and the deployed spell unleashed its power.

Triggered by a shock and devastation like some natural catastrophe, the battle between those who had known four hundred years began.
