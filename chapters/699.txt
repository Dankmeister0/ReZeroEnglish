Arc 9 Chapter 5: "931 Blows"

—On the rooftop of the great fortress of the walled city of Garkla, a stronghold that still stood out even amid a city thick with the lingering aftermath of its fierce battle against the "Great Disaster," an unusual atmosphere hung heavy.

The rooftop had been designed to hold a considerable number of soldiers for loosing arrows in times of crisis, but a crowd this large—over a hundred people—was surely beyond what anyone had anticipated.

The sight of brawny, powerfully built men crowded shoulder to shoulder might have suggested they were gathered for some manner of punishment, an image liable to press a gloomy mood on all who saw it. And yet there was no such grim despair on their actual faces—only a serious, earnest light in their eyes.

And at the far end of the more than a hundred pairs of eyes fixed there lay an open, empty space.

There—

"—Ptoo."

With a rasping, wet sound, a black-haired boy spat out a mouthful of blood onto the floor.

His face was smeared with blood from his nose, his eyelids swollen shut, his head swaying unsteadily back and forth—battered to the point that anyone could see at a glance he was covered in wounds from head to toe. In a state where simply remaining upright was a small miracle, the boy planted his hands on his knees, stamped one foot hard against the ground, and lifted his face.

—Straight into that face came a blow that crushed his nose head-on, and the boy went flying backward more spectacularly than ever before.

He flew, and flew, and rolled, and landed spread-eagled on his back.

Limbs flung out limp, given the sheer accumulation of damage he'd taken up to this point, it seemed impossible he could rise again—

"Not yet..."

It was none other than the boy himself, sprawled there on his back, who overturned that assumption.

Slowly he raised his upper body, fresh blood from his nose further staining his mouth, and still the boy pushed himself up right there, letting out a deep breath.

And then—

"—!"

His opponent's raised fist came down, and once again the boy was struck with brutal force.

△▼△▼△▼△

"—This is my atonement. So please, whatever happens, don't step in."

Had she not been told that beforehand, she would have wanted to leap in at once.

But she swallowed that urge, biting her lip, and respected his wishes instead. That was, right now, the only thing Beatrice could do for her partner, Natsuki Subaru.

"—!"

A hard, cracking impact rang out, and Subaru's face snapped sideways as a groan escaped him. His body threatened to topple, but he held his footing, and, teeth clenched, turned his face forward once more.

The next fist slammed into his nose, and he reeled back violently. He reeled—but—

"N-not... not yet...!"

Blood dripped steadily from his nose as Subaru squeezed the words out through eyes bloodshot red.

At that voice, the man with the skull tattoo who had already struck Subaru more than twenty times—Weitz—twisted his hard-set face and raised his fist once more.

And so, struck again, Subaru's blood scattered, staining the rooftop floor of the great fortress further.

It had repeated like this many times over these past few days—today alone, for more than an hour already.

"Subaru..."

At a glance, it looked like a duel between Subaru and Weitz, but it was hard to call something a duel when one side simply stood there taking every blow. Subaru stood stock-still, receiving Weitz's fists head-on, over and over.

And watching that was Beatrice—no, not only Beatrice.

"—————"

All around, more than a hundred people crowded the rooftop of the great fortress.

Every one of them belonged to the Pleiades Battalion, the allies Natsuki Subaru had won over in this empire—and, at the same time, they were witnesses to this duel.

Yes, this ritual in which Subaru let himself be struck again and again—

"—'Sparka.'"

Beside Beatrice, who clutched at the hem of her skirt, the deer-human girl Tanza murmured.

Watching the same scene, Beatrice felt something raw and frayed rise up at the sight of Tanza's profile, her dark, wide eyes betraying no readable emotion.

Because it felt, however unintentionally, as if Tanza's calling it "Sparka" implied she understood the feelings behind Subaru's challenge in this ritual better than Beatrice herself did.

After all, wasn't that only natural?

"...Betty doesn't understand why Subaru has to do something like this, I suppose."

Subaru, of his own free will, had imposed upon himself this ritual of being struck one-sidedly.

Asked to bear witness to it because it was his atonement, Beatrice, enduring the ache in her chest, could do nothing but keep standing here, if only to grant him that much of a wish.

—Priscilla Barielle's death had become a wound in the many people who had known her.

Those for whom the wound had stayed shallow suffered precisely because of that shallowness, while those whose wounds had been gouged deep and painful were left scrambling to tend to wounds that bled and cried out with pain.

And, sadly, Beatrice belonged to the former, while Subaru belonged, without question, to the latter.

"—————"

When he had begun this "Sparka," Subaru had called it his atonement.

Having met the sword slaves while shrunk down by "infantilization," Subaru had, through the twists of circumstance and his own calculated need to gather allies, disguised his true identity, deceived them, and dragged them into the very center of this war.

Given the ferocity of that battle and the great role the Battalion had played within it, without them the losses would surely have been far greater—or the fortress city might have fallen entirely.

So Subaru's judgment, his choices—they had been the right answer for saving the empire.

It was only that Subaru himself, of all people, couldn't forgive it, and so sought out this punishment—

"Eh-ao..."

A small hand came to rest atop Beatrice's own, which gripped her skirt tight.

Glancing over, she found it was Spica who had reached out, peering up at Beatrice with worry. Her large eyes were full of concern as she fretted over Beatrice's hand, clenched so painfully tight.

And the fact that she was making even Spica worry about her was, to Beatrice, pathetic.

Spica was, along with Beatrice, the only one here with no connection to the Pleiades Battalion. And Beatrice desperately wished that all of that girl's worry and concern could be spent on Subaru instead.

"Sorry, I suppose. Spica, your—"

"—Oh my. I thought there seemed to be quite a crowd up on the roof, so I dropped by, and here it is—the Boss's 'Sparka.' Taking a beating again today, are we? For all appearances, you really are conscientious about this."

"—————"

Just as Beatrice returned Spica's grip, a figure with an airy voice and manner hopped up, planting a foot on the rooftop fence, and showed his face at the site of the "Sparka."

He did it with the ease of a man skipping a stair or two, though hopping up to the roof of a fortress nearly a hundred meters high was surely no simple matter. Then again, common sense of that sort apparently didn't apply to this kimono-clad swordsman—the "Blue Lightning," Cecilus Segmunt.

"Cecilus-sama."

Balancing on one foot atop the fence, shading his brow with a hand as he surveyed the "Sparka," Cecilus broke into a loose, easy grin at Tanza's greeting.

"Hey there, hey there, Tanza-san. It's an oddly fresh and peculiar feeling, being at a different eye-level from you like this. This is my true, standard state, and the child-me of these past while was really just a temporary form, but you've spent longer with that version of me, so doesn't it feel a bit strange to you?"

"I suppose so. Though even if the height of your eyes has changed, the height of your heart hasn't changed at all, so honestly, my impression of you hasn't changed much."

"Hahahaha, nice one!"

He clapped his hands and laughed at Tanza's curt reply, then his gaze swept around toward Beatrice. At that blue-eyed look, Beatrice frowned.

"The Boss's partner looks awfully dissatisfied."

"...Of course I am, I suppose. There's no need for Subaru to do something like that, is there."

"Let's not get into talk of need or no need. Twist it into extremes and you end up talking about whether a life has value or not, which isn't remotely stylish. Besides, even without a need, there's still a reason. It might not exist in you, but it's there in the Boss, properly."

"—————"

"Well, honestly, I do think he's too earnest for his own good. Deceiving everyone in the Battalion who's been with him ever since the Sword Slave Island, and now sincerely apologizing his heart out until he's earned every last person's forgiveness. Though, well, the Battalion folks are all hot-blooded sword slaves, so this is naturally how the atoning ends up looking."

Speaking fluently and rapid-fire, on and on, Cecilus finished by pointing at the battered Subaru.

Infuriating as it was, nothing Cecilus said was wrong. —Just as he said, this atonement of Subaru's, taking blow after blow from Weitz, was penance to the Pleiades Battalion.

Nine hundred thirty-one members of the Pleiades Battalion—which meant, in total, nine hundred thirty-one purifying blows.

Taking every one of those blows, delivered by Weitz on behalf of the Battalion, was the ritual Subaru had imposed on himself and used to win the Battalion's acceptance—the "Sparka."

"Even split across several days, that's nine hundred blows, right? Get hit that many times and even I'd die, surely. Though, honestly, I've never even considered a situation where I just stand there and get hit defenseless, so the whole premise feels shaky to begin with... Where are we at right now?"

"Two hundred fifty-six blows, total across all days."

"I see, I see... makes my head spin just thinking about it!"

Told the count by Tanza, Cecilus said it without a hint of malice.

His flippancy made her want to say something, but with a man like this, it would be pointless. Beatrice gave up on it, squeezed Spica's hand back, and turned her attention to worrying over Subaru.

For the duration of this "Sparka," Subaru had forbidden Beatrice from using healing magic on him.

So the moment today's "Sparka" ended, she meant to rush to his side as fast as she could, and was building up her mana now in order to heal his wounds.

"Cecilus."

In place of Beatrice, still lost in that thought, a heavy, stern voice called Cecilus's name.

The burly man with four powerful arms crossed and a fearsome face—Gustav—turned his eyes to Cecilus standing on the fence, and to the scenery behind him.

"This official doesn't know if he has the right to ask, but—General Arakiya?"

"There's nothing between you and me worth hiding, Gustav-san. Going forward, you'll hand off the governorship of Sword Slave Island to someone else and take up an important post in the capital, right? Then you and I, at least while I'm living in the capital, ought to be crossing paths fairly often. Well, the capital being in the state it's in right now, where anyone will actually be living is another question..."

"—————"

"Oh, my apologies, you meant Anya. —She's still having a hard time balancing her heart and body. Even without that, she took in a spirit big enough to nearly burst that slender frame of hers. Bringing that under control requires stability of the heart above all else, and yet the little princess she'd leaned on for support has passed away. A real problem, that."

"Her pain and hardship are more than I can well imagine. But the role only you can fulfill concerns the very survival of the empire. Not just this official—every citizen of the empire is placing their hopes in you."

"What are you even saying, honestly, Gustav-san."

"Hm..."

"I'm the star performer of this world, you know? Carrying someone's expectations—let alone the whole empire's—is an everyday affair for me. —And with Anya in the picture too, I won't be cutting corners."

Cecilus answered without a trace of strain, but just how difficult a thing he'd spoken of, even with that seemingly careless air, was proven in a visible form.

—Behind Cecilus, past the western wall visible from the fortress rooftop, lay a broad wasteland scarred all over with the freshly carved marks of what looked like natural cataclysms.

It was proof that Cecilus, who had come here with such an unruffled face, had been keeping company with Arakiya to help vent her power and prevent an outburst from the extraordinary force she had taken in—that of a great spirit ranked among the Four Great Elements.

Left unchecked, that vast power could well become a second "Great Disaster." And yet, no half-measure of a person could even manage to stay near Arakiya as she was now.

Which was precisely why, looking across the whole breadth of the empire, only Cecilus could shoulder that great task.

"Whether they wish it or not, people can't stop their steps forward. Only the dead are allowed to stop; the living have no choice but to keep moving on. Anya's suffering and the Boss's struggling, floundering purification—at their roots, they're the same thing. Painful as it is, I'd say it's something to welcome."

"Talking like it's someone else's business, saying whatever you please, I suppose."

"'Someone else's business'—what an unrefined way to put it. This is a story about the Boss's place, the Boss's showcase moment. That said... I think that's it for today."

Closing one eye, Cecilus murmured that in reply to Beatrice.

At his words, Beatrice gasped, "Eh," and then, as if snapped by a spring, spun around—at the very same moment that Weitz's fist drove Subaru down onto the floor.

"Hah... hah... that's it... that's the end...!"

Breathing hard, Weitz lowered the fist he'd used to beat Subaru, now smeared with blood. There was no reply from Subaru, who had lost consciousness.

"Subaru!" Rushing to his side, Beatrice hurriedly cast her healing magic. Having seen that through, Weitz turned sharply to leave, but—

"Wait, I suppose. Let me heal your hand too, I suppose."

"Don't need it... Put Schubert before me..."

"That won't do, I suppose. Spica."

At the call, Spica scurried around in front of Weitz and spread both arms wide with a "Uh."

Weitz, unable to shove past Spica as she stood blocking his way, found Beatrice instantly casting her healing magic on him; at the gradual sensation of his wounds mending, he turned and glared her way.

But even a man whose skull tattoo lent him such a menacing air couldn't hope to intimidate the great spirit Beatrice with a face twisted up in pain.

"H-hey, Tanza, how many's that make now?"

"Two hundred fifty-eight blows, Hiain-sama."

"Six hundred seventy-three left, then... ...long way to go still."

Beside Beatrice and the others, it was the lizard-man Hiain who had asked Tanza in a trembling voice. Tanza gave him the count in answer, and the bearded Idra shook his head, looking downcast.

At that, Hiain said, "Look," glancing around at everyone.

"Ain't it fine already? He's done this much—seems like everybody's gotten the message plenty by now."

"Talk in your sleep if you're gonna talk nonsense, lizard bastard...! If we're stopping halfway, what do you think I volunteered for this job for...!"

"What for? 'Cause you're the one who's most pissed off, ain't you! Me, I—I'm not that far gone!"

A vein bulged on Weitz's forehead at Hiain, whose voice cracked as his long tongue darted out. But as he moved to close in on Hiain, Tanza held him back with, "You're still being treated."

Instead, Idra let out a sigh and turned to face Hiain.

"To be blunt, I agree with your point too. It's true I was shocked by Schubert's true identity, and there was a sense of having been deceived, but..."

"Schubert got us out of that island...! What more is there...!"

"T-then nobody actually wants this, do they?! So why..."

"That's something Natsuki Schubert—no, Natsuki Subaru himself needs. As long as Tanza and his contracted spirit Beatrice aren't stopping it, this official approves of this act."

Gustav cut into the exchange between Idra, Weitz, and Hiain, and at his heavy, stern words, the sword slaves around them showed, in their various gestures and words, reactions of roughly similar temperature.

No doubt there were some among them still seething, unable to forgive Subaru's conduct—

"Uwah..."

Beatrice holding her hand out over him, and, crouched on the other side, Spica gazing worriedly at Subaru, his face swollen, sprawled limp.

Anyone who could look at those girls, and at the sight of Subaru so painfully throwing himself into his atonement, and feel nothing at all, surely wouldn't have belonged to the Pleiades Battalion in the first place.

"Right now, this must be something Schubert-sama needs. Proof that he himself hasn't stopped moving forward. Even if that proof is pain."

That one line from Tanza brought the question Hiain had raised to its conclusion.

Taking that in, Weitz clenched the fist Beatrice's healing magic had mended.

"I'll keep going... Even if my knuckles split, I'll see him through six hundred more...!"

"Good, very good. That's how you use a showcase moment, Weitz-san."

"—————"

"Huh? What's with this strange atmosphere?"

For a moment, everyone present stared, startled that Cecilus had spoken up. Weitz especially, looking caught off guard, fixed his eyes on Cecilus.

"Never figured you'd actually remember my name..."

"It's true I'm forgetful, sure! But I keep the names of performers worth remembering properly in mind."

"Hmph..."

Weitz snorted at Cecilus's shrug, then looked down once more, hard, at the fallen Subaru—a face that likely overlapped in impression with the young Subaru burned into his memory, but that would still need time before the two images could line up clearly.

And then—

"The rest, I leave to you..."

With those parting words, Weitz strode off the fortress rooftop in long steps. The other men followed after his back, as did Idra, leading out a reluctant Hiain.

Last, Gustav nodded toward Beatrice and the others still remaining.

"This official will take his leave as well. Natsuki Subaru... no, Schubert still fits better, somehow. I'll leave him in your hands."

"No need to tell me, I suppose. Though, do remember one thing, I suppose."

"And what would that be?"

"If Subaru is Schubert to you, he won't mind if you keep calling him that, I suppose. There's no need to throw away a name that's special between you all just out of needless consideration, I suppose."

"—I'm grateful."

With a deep nod, Gustav too slowly took his leave.

And once the sword slaves who had served as witnesses to the "Sparka" left all at once, Beatrice let out a sigh in the rooftop that had suddenly gone hollow and empty. Then she glanced up at the girl in the kimono beside her.

"You could have gone along with that lot too, you know, I suppose."

"There's no need to worry on my account. Unlike everyone else, there's nothing strange about me staying here."

"—. You must be worried about your mistress too, I suppose. She's suffering as well, I suppose."

"—————"

That remark hadn't been meant as sarcasm or spite of any kind, but Beatrice regretted for an instant that it had ended up sounding that way regardless.

The remark that had made Tanza's cheek stiffen ever so slightly touched on her mistress, Yorna Mishigure—herself one of those who bore a great wound from Priscilla's death. By some irrational binding upon her soul, among the many past lives through which she had been reborn, one of those past selves had been none other than the mother of the now-dead Priscilla.

Which meant that Priscilla and Yorna, mother and daughter, had each tasted their own complicated form of bereavement.

"Thank you for your concern, Beatrice-sama. ...But Yorna-sama has said she's fine on her own, just so."

"—————"

"That is what she has told me, so—"

At Tanza's answer, eyes lowered, Beatrice realized she had misread her.

Tanza wasn't convinced by Yorna's words. And yet, just as Beatrice had decided to bear witness to Subaru's atonement, Tanza had decided to respect Yorna's wishes.

It was difficult for anyone else to gauge just what Yorna, insisting she was fine alone, was truly feeling right now. —And yet Beatrice felt she could grasp some part of it.

It wasn't the same as reincarnation, but over her own long life, Beatrice too had seen off many lives. Before Subaru brought her out of the Forbidden Archive, it was something she had consciously avoided.

She could now recognize, and regret, what an insincere attitude that had been toward those who had tried to face her honestly. Unlike Beatrice, who couldn't learn it without being taught, Yorna might have come to know and learn it through her own experience.

Even so—

"...Mm."

"Uwah!"

"Spi... ka...? Ah, wha— me..."

Subaru's eyelids trembled, then slowly, his dark eyes opened. At that, Spica's eyes lit up and she leapt at him, and Subaru blinked over and over where he lay.

Seeing that, Beatrice sighed, realizing his memory of before he'd passed out was gone.

"They're all gone now, I suppose. I was in the middle of casting healing magic on you, I suppose."

"Ah, ahh, right! Right, right, sorry, sorry! Man, as always I'm just giving Bea-ko nothing but worry, my bad! I'd really be hopeless without you!"

As Beatrice's explanation caught up with him, Subaru slapped both his hands hard against his face. With that dry crack of a sound, he sprang up from where he lay with a "Sh!"

The force of it was so strong he nearly pitched forward—but—

"Uu-uu!"

"Whoa, sorry, sorry, Spica... Actually, thanks for watching over things. You held back real well without jumping in. Thinking back on it now, it feels like it was a pretty close call whether you'd accidentally deck Weitz one, WHAM!"

"Auu-uu, uwah."

"Tanza was way more likely, huh? Yeah, now that you mention it, true! I mean, Tanza cares about me a whole lot. Weitz was in danger from multiple directions, maybe."

Saying that, Subaru took the hand of Spica, who had steadied him when he'd nearly fallen, and began spinning around with her right there.

The swelling on his face had gone down considerably thanks to the healing magic, but his lips were still split, and his eyes still a touch puffy in a way that bothered her, so, honestly, Beatrice wanted to keep treating him a little longer.

"That concerns my honor, so please don't say such things without permission, Natsuki Subaru-sama."

Tanza let out a sigh and needled Subaru, still dancing with Spica, with that remark. At which Subaru made a sour face and said, "Hey, hey."

"Don't you think there's a thorn in your words ever since I got back to my normal size? Also, being called something other than 'Schubert-sama' by Tanza hits surprisingly hard, so please stop."

"My apologies, Natsuki Subaru-sama. As I understand Natsuki Subaru-sama to be an honored guest from the Kingdom, I could never presume to be so discourteous, Natsuki Subaru-sama."

"Tanzaaa~"

At Tanza's answer, with all that emotional distance in it, Subaru, cradling Spica in his arms, looked crestfallen. —That act of his, putting on a foolishly bright, everyday composure, was more than Beatrice could bear to watch.

Demanding his own atonement before the Pleiades Battalion, and then acting cheerful in front of Beatrice and those close to him. All of it, surely, so as not to worry the people around him—but—

"...It's having the opposite effect, I suppose."

Acting cheerful when he wanted to cry, trying to make everyone laugh when things were hard.

Beatrice loved that quality in Subaru, but that only held true when the urge to cry and the pain hadn't yet exceeded what he could bear.

No matter who you were, you shouldn't clamp a lid down on your own urge to cry, your own pain, like this.

"Bea-ko? What's with that cute face all of a sudden?"

"—. Betty being adorable is just how things always are, I suppose. More importantly, treatment's still not finished, so stay still, I suppose."

"I'd like to say I can't keep making you go to all this trouble... but I've gotta be in top shape, 'cause this mountain's not even half-climbed yet. Alright, bring it on."

Nodding, Subaru sat down right there, set Spica on his crossed legs, and put on a sharp, resolute face.

Beatrice resumed her healing magic on him, glancing briefly toward Tanza. But Tanza only shook her head slowly, wordlessly, side to side.

She too had no idea what the right thing to say to Subaru right now would be.

And then—

"Boss, Boss, got a minute?"

With a face utterly oblivious to the tension of Beatrice and the others fussing over Subaru, Cecilus, who had been watching how things played out, called out to Subaru.

At the call, Subaru said "Oh," spotting Cecilus atop the fence.

"Thought it was someone else, but it's big Sesshi. Seriously, you just scaled straight up and it's not weird at all, huh."

"I'd like to say the same about you, Boss, but once you properly grow up, that nasty look in your eyes really stands out, doesn't it. On your kid-self it was charming, but on the current you it's simply a lethal weapon."

"Say whatever you want, huh... You alone? Isn't she with you?"

"If you mean Anya, I've got her lying down in a crater. With the power of the 'Rock Chunk' making her outrageously tough, I had to kill her about a hundred times. I don't think she'll be getting up for a while. But more importantly—"

Saying so, Cecilus hopped down off the fence, landing right in front of Subaru. Meeting him there, Subaru and Spica tilted their heads at the exact same angle.

At the two of them reacting the same way, Cecilus grinned—

"You and the others will be heading back to the Kingdom before too long, right, Boss? Before that, how about showing me you sock His Excellency good and proper across the face?"

△▼△▼△▼△

"And that's why I've brought the Boss along to see His Excellency! Ever since Sword Slave Island, the Boss has been going on and on about socking His Excellency, so I really wanted to witness that moment!"

"Be gone, at once."

Vincent, apparently buried in his official duties, cut Cecilus off flatly without even lifting his eyes from his paperwork, even as Cecilus barged in with a sharp raise of his hand and a broad grin.

It was only natural, given how busy the Emperor was, but even faced with such a natural response, Cecilus—likely the most stubbornly self-willed person in the whole empire—wasn't about to back down.

He pouted his lips in discontent with an "Ehh~," and, bold as brass, planted his rear right on the working Emperor's desk.

"Isn't this overworking yourself even by any reasonable standard? Sure, I get that a soldier's job is only during a crisis, while a bureaucrat's job never stops, crisis or no crisis!"

"And you think that constitutes persuading me? Suppose I did indulge your honeyed words and set my work aside—what exactly do you intend to have me do instead?"

"Get punched by the Boss!"

"Vanish, at once."

"Boo! His Excellency the workaholic! Don't you think so too, Empress-sama?"

"Me?"

At Vincent's merciless answer, Cecilus, still perched on the desk, leaned to one side and called for reinforcements. The one who responded to his voice was Medium, who had been playing with Spica on the sofa in the office.

Combing through Spica's long golden hair, she gave a low hum of "Mm~."

"I do think Abel-chin works too much too, but I get that what Abel-chin's doing is important, so I can't really push him on it, y'know? Plus I don't wanna see Abel-chin and Subaru-chin fight, either."

"Not a fight, but His Excellency getting punched one-sidedly... in a sense, that would be a 'Sparka,' wouldn't it!?"

"Move your backside, at once."

Cecilus, pumped up, clenched a fist tight. Vincent, trying to pull out the papers pinned beneath that backside, closed one eye and sighed.

Then he turned his dark eyes toward the entrance of the room.

"How long do you intend to stand there. You're an eyesore."

"Ngh... that's, um..."

"Subaru-chin, go ahead and take a seat up front with Beatrice-chan!"

Drawn in by Vincent's cold voice and, in contrast, Medium's warm one, Subaru, who'd been wearing an awkward look, sat down in the middle of the room, on the sofa across from Medium. Beatrice, hand in hand with Subaru, took her seat alongside him, and Medium gently set out tea for them.

"Here you go, it's nothing special, but please."

"Temporary or not, this is my office. Are you serving the Emperor lowly tea?"

"Big bro taught me that's proper manners! Even I don't really get manners, so I'm doing my best~"

As she spoke, Medium poured a refill of the tea that had also been set on Vincent's desk. Vincent voiced no particular complaint even at the rising steam, and Beatrice, too, could confirm with her own tongue that there was nothing wrong with the tea that had been brewed.

Beside Beatrice as she did so, Subaru frowned.

"I mean, man, Abel and Medium-san are actually gonna become a married couple, huh."

"Umyuu!"

"What was that noise just now."

"Uh, uhh~, that word still makes me kinda embarrassed."

Face reddening, Medium let out a small whinny of a sound, and though she seemed flustered by the situation she found herself in, to Beatrice she didn't look as though she were rejecting it from the heart.

Honestly, Beatrice didn't know much about the kind of person Vincent was.

Still, given how thoroughly Subaru had trashed his character, she suspected, paradoxically, that he was probably a man with plenty of redeeming points, much like Otto or Julius.

"Otherwise, I wouldn't feel at all easy leaving Medium in his hands, I suppose."

"That's fair. Not like Flop-san to get his head turned by money and status, though."

"Oh dear, you're being told off, Your Excellency. From the Boss's phrasing, it sounds like the only good things going for you are your status, honor, and fortune as the Emperor of Vollachia."

"Is it the plan of every one of you to anger me into setting my work aside and ruin the empire?"

If that was the aim, one had to admit the attempt was, at present, going rather well.

Talked at this much, and with his paperwork physically pinned down, Vincent's work had, naturally, no choice but to grind to a halt.

With that, Vincent gave up trying to pull his papers out from under Cecilus's backside, and instead pierced Subaru with his dark eyes.

"I hear you've been calling it 'atonement' and repeating this strange behavior with escaped convicts from Sword Slave Island."

"...Figured you'd tell me it's pointless. More to the point, I can't just let 'escaped convicts' slide. Everyone on that island..."

"They were sent to Ginunhive for reasons in the first place. Escaped convicts, precisely put. And they broke out amid the empire's chaos, wielding their martial prowess to rampage however they pleased."

"Then what do you plan to do with them all."

His cheek stiffening at the dangerous edge in that phrasing, Subaru glared back at Vincent. For a moment, a sharp tension crackled between the two of them.

But Medium sliced through their locked gazes with a chop of her hand, saying, "No fair."

"Abel-chin, if you say it in such a mean way, Subaru-chin's gonna get the wrong idea."

"—. The island's escaped convicts will be granted amnesty for their service in this recent affair. That much was also proposed by Gustav Morello, who served as governor of Sword Slave Island."

Prompted by Medium, Vincent announced the fate of the Pleiades Battalion in a quiet voice.

A crisis for the Battalion that had seemingly come out of nowhere—one that, to begin with, had been raised by Vincent himself, only to be resolved by Vincent himself just as abruptly; the emotional swing was severe.

In fact, Subaru glared at Vincent, trembling.

"D-do you get some kind of kick out of toying with people over a problem that's already solved...!"

"Ahh, I get it, I get it! His Excellency does have that side to him, doesn't he. Now that I think about it, I recall Chisha pulling similarly mean tricks on me fairly often, so is this some kind of common trait among smart people?"

"Speaking of matters already dealt with—if it's the spite against the Emperor that Cecilus keeps talking about, Subaru's already settled that on his own, ages ago, I suppose."

"Whaaat!?"

At Cecilus's voice cracking, Beatrice stuck out her tongue at the reaction. It felt satisfying, finally throwing off this reckless man's pace.

Still, taking it in stride, Cecilus said, "Hmm," and went on.

"I'll heal the shock of missing such an interesting showcase moment by getting the details later, but here's a question. If that's so, why did the Boss let me bring him to see His Excellency? Surely you could have shaken me off coldly, the way Tanza-san did?"

"That's..."

At Cecilus's question, Subaru found himself at a loss for an answer, glancing worriedly toward Vincent.

Even without watching that reaction, Beatrice could see straight through to Subaru's true feelings. These past few days, Subaru had been actively reaching out to Beatrice and the others and to the Pleiades Battalion, but there were a few people he simply couldn't bring himself to face.

—Those were Vincent and Yorna, and the people of Priscilla's camp.

Given Subaru's excessive sense of responsibility, it was only natural that those relationships would end up this way. Even so, Subaru didn't think it was all right to leave things as they were.

So being dragged along by Cecilus, under the pretense of force, suited him just fine.

That way—

"So you've finally shown your face before me... before I."

"Abel..."

The dilemma Subaru carried within him, his hesitation, all those tangled feelings.

It seemed that Vincent Vollachia, called the "Wise Emperor," had seen right through them all along. At that sharp gaze, Subaru was, without question, cowed.

But the hand joined with Beatrice's tightened, and Subaru, resolving to meet that pressure head-on, opened his mouth.

"Abel, sorry—"

"—A moment ago, you criticized me over some matter of a problem already solved."

"Eh..."

"But, if you ask me, it's you who keeps chafing at a problem already solved."

Cut off before he could even get going, and told that on top of it, Subaru blinked.

For a beat, his face showed he couldn't quite catch up with what that meant. But the instant he understood its meaning, Subaru's expression changed utterly.

It was a fury tangled together with grief—rage in its truest form.

"A problem already solved... what, what are you even talking about!"

"Most of the problems you're currently entangling yourself in. Your atonement to the sword slaves is one, your manner toward myself and Yorna Mishigure is another. And above all—"

"Stop it!!"

As Vincent tried to continue, Subaru, shooting up from the sofa, lunged forward furiously to try to choke off his words.

But a third party cut in front of Subaru. —Cecilus.

Sliding his backside off the desk, Cecilus stepped between Subaru and the Vollachia Emperor, barring Subaru's advance.

"Get out of the way, Sesshi! You were the one who wanted to watch me deck Abel!"

"Yes, that's true, that's right. But that was about wanting to see a 'Sparka' between you and His Excellency—not about wanting to see some impulsive, boring card played out."

"—!"

Both now back to their original heights, the two of them faced off, glaring, as men and not as children.

But in this standoff between Subaru and Cecilus, Beatrice found no urge welling up in her, as usual, to take Subaru's side. If anything, she found herself siding with Cecilus—no, with Vincent, who threw his words at Subaru head-on.

His manner of putting it was nothing but cruel, and surely any number of gentler phrasings existed, and yet the way he'd deliberately chosen words drenched in blood and pain—that much she loathed—but—

"—Priscilla's death, too, is already a thing of the past."

"Abel—!!"

At that declaration, Subaru's voice flared with fierce, fierce anger as he glared at Vincent. But under that gaze, cast past Cecilus, Vincent didn't so much as flinch.

It was Beatrice and Spica, and Medium too—the women present, every one—whose expressions tightened at how painful it was to watch Subaru.

"Priscilla was your... your sister, wasn't she. Even so, you..."

"That's right. She was my sister. Clever and impudent from her earliest years, and she lost her life without her nature ever once being corrected. ...Just as with Chisha, losing Priscilla did, without question, leave a wound in me. I won't hide that away."

Shaking his head slowly, side to side, Vincent laid a hand on his own chest and let the words spill out.

In it, one could plainly sense the anguish over his sister and the grief of losing her, hidden, in his own way, behind the pride Vincent bore as Emperor.

And that Subaru regretted having caused that in Vincent, and that precisely because of it, he'd been unable to show his face, not knowing what to say.

But—

"—This is mine. There's no room in it for you."

"—————"

"This is a matter between Priscilla and myself. Do you truly wish for the right to lay even a finger on it?"

"N-no... that's not it, that's not it. That's not what I mean. That's not it, but..."

At the edge of Vincent's tongue, Subaru had cold water thrown over the anger that had blazed in him only a moment before, his gaze wandering, his face unable to find the words he needed to say.

And still, to Subaru caught like that, Vincent didn't stop speaking.

Perhaps it was a line of pursuit that could only exist because of time spent together in the empire, between Subaru and Vincent, time Beatrice and the others knew nothing of.

Vincent went on quietly, like this. It was—

"Natsuki Subaru. —What were the last words Priscilla spoke to you?"

"—————"

"If they were words of a curse, then I will take on whatever they left within you. But if they were not... then that, whatever it was, is the fruit of what passed between you and Priscilla."

"—————"

"Take my sister's parting gift and make it your own flesh and blood. —That is the solution to that problem."

What words could he have offered. What could he do to atone.

Those countless questions and doubts, the impatience of a mind that couldn't allow itself to stop moving, the self-punishing conviction that he had to suffer—those had been the root of Subaru's actions these past few days.

And Vincent declared, flatly, that the very starting point of that root was mismatched to begin with.

"Priscilla... said..."

"Said what?"

"...She said I was... a true knight."

"Then that is the whole of what Priscilla gave you. —Well done, Natsuki Subaru."

"—————"

At that one line, Subaru's expression broke.

The anger and grief, the raging passion that had governed him until now, was swept away, and what came surging up from behind it was unending—an emotion with all the force of boiling water overflowing from a pot at a rolling boil.

Something he had, perhaps deliberately, refused to look at directly ever since that morning he had watched Priscilla die.

"Subaru..."

"Bea... trice... I, I..."

"It's all right, I suppose. Properly, Betty... Betty and Emilia too, everyone understands properly, I suppose."

Drawing close beside Subaru as he stood frozen there, Beatrice took his hand and nodded to him. At Beatrice's words, Subaru's face twisted, unable to hold back the tears spilling over.

And so, finally, for the first time since that morning, Beatrice was made certain that her voice had at last reached Subaru.

"Wa— oof, I suppose."

It was just after she'd registered, all at once, that Subaru had dropped to his knees.

Kneeling, Subaru wrapped his arms around Beatrice from the front. At the frail, clinging strength of that embrace, Beatrice, gently, held him back in turn, of her own accord.

"—Well, this isn't quite His Excellency getting punched, but I got to see a fine showcase moment all the same."

"Some things are better left unsaid, I suppose."

"My apologies."

With a gesture of pinching shut the mouth that had said something unnecessary, Cecilus winked. Behind them, Medium, hugging Spica close, was crying too, pulled along by Subaru's tears.

And Subaru, burying his face in Beatrice's chest and sobbing, couldn't see what Beatrice could see.

"—————"

The sight of the Vollachia Emperor, the one who had broken Subaru open, softening the corners of his eyes by just the faintest degree.

Even Beatrice, who had known him only briefly, could tell. —It was, most likely, an expression that Vincent Vollachia, called this "Wise Emperor," rarely showed—perhaps had never shown to anyone at all.

An expression, it seemed, of honoring the one who wept in his place for his beloved sister's final moments.
