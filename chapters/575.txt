Arc 7 Chapter 70: "One"

The most important thing when asking Old Man Null for "medicine" was certainty.

Old Man Null had never been an eager student of medicine to begin with.
He was simply an amateur who'd ended up permitted into the healing room after tending to his fellow sword slaves, and had somehow just settled in there.
Even the island's management wouldn't hand something as potent as a drug that could serve as both poison and medicine to Old Man Null, a mere sword slave.
So compounding an actual medicine — a fast-acting "toxic drug" — was beyond him.

All Old Man Null could make was a poison, mixed from whatever medicines he could get his hands on, that killed its target by throwing their whole bloodstream into chaos.
Drink it, and after tremendous suffering, death was guaranteed — and yet, depending on how you looked at it, that fact was convenient for Subaru.

"In the end, at my root, I'm just a lazy, oversized idiot."

Given how soft Subaru's true nature was, a poison that killed without suffering was out of the question.
He'd surely start thinking about redoing things over the smallest setback, taking action banking on the poison's effect. Doing that would turn Natsuki Subaru into a monster.
A monster estranged from humanity, unable to understand anyone's pain or suffering.

"That's an absolute no."

Already, the memory and sensation of the time his limbs had been stretched to their limit was fading bit by bit. Even so, Subaru's experience and beliefs must never be allowed to warp so badly.
That change in character would be a betrayal of everyone who had accepted this Subaru.

He could betray himself, but he couldn't betray everyone — his family.
Natsuki Subaru could be set aside, but the bonds of Natsuki Subaru could not.
And so—

"...Sorry to Old Man Null, who I lied to about using it on 'Sparka's' enemy."

Even if it meant tasting the agony of hell, Natsuki Subaru would not withdraw the hand he'd extended.
That was the one unbending core that held true even here, on this island of exiled sword slaves cut off by water.

△▼△▼△▼△

So he tried to put on a brave face — but—

"Guh, gk, gghh...!"

Gritting his teeth to the point of cracking, Subaru endures the agony that shreds his heart to pieces. No — he isn't enduring it. The pain that requires enduring, he's already left behind.
He died, came back, so the suffering that caused his death exists nowhere anymore.
But his soul is still dragging it along. Because of that, tears and snot pour down without stopping.

"Hey, Schult... What's wrong, all of a sudden...!"

"I-I'm fine..."

"There's no way you're fine... looking like such a wreck...!"

Crouched on the spot, his face smeared with all manner of fluids, Weitz's voice trembles at the sight of him.
Rushing over, he pulls Subaru up and wipes his snot with a bare hand, without hesitation. Every single time, Subaru is struck with admiration for that attitude of Weitz's.
Being able to act without treating something dirty as dirty was genuinely admirable.

"Aah, uh..."

Roughly wiped of snot and tears, Subaru brings a hand to his dazed head.
Bit by bit, the shock of the past death fades, and he accepts the sluggish, living present. This leisurely switch of consciousness is more or less what happens every time Subaru dies by "medicine."

The "poison" tucked behind his back molar as a surefire trump card for certain death — surprisingly, it doesn't come into play all that often among the causes of Subaru's deaths on the island of exiled sword slaves.
Deaths during "Sparka" are overwhelmingly caused by the claws and fangs of sword beasts.

Still, when he fails to die cleanly, or when he somehow manages while leaving casualties behind, this "medicine" is the only thing left to do the job.
That was a torment Natsuki Subaru had to bear.
Because—

"...It's because I screwed up."

If the one person who should be most able to fix things fails, what are the others supposed to do?
Subaru bears responsibility. The responsibility of being the one most likely able to do something about it.

Even if it isn't power he wanted or obtained by choice, surely he can wield it — because he's Natsuki Subaru.
Because he's that man's son — Natsuki Kenichi's son.

"—Hng, what...!?"

"The drawbridge...!"

Pressing his fist to the filthy floor, just as he tries to sit up, the whole island shudders like it's groaning.
From Weitz in front of him and the dim, cold air, Subaru realizes where he died and returned to — the lower level of the island of exiled sword slaves, right before the drawbridge rises.
The one bridge connecting the isolated island to the outside world, being lowered.
Which means—

"—They're coming."

That hellscape of a scene, and the worst enemy who created it. A rematch with Todd — the man Subaru had prayed he'd never have to see again.

"——"

A chill wraps around Subaru, and he hugs his own shoulders.
The instant he closes his eyes, what revives behind his eyelids is that carnage littered with corpses. The worst memory — familiar faces bleeding from eyes and nose, twitching and rolling on the ground.
That included Weitz right in front of him, the one who'd just wiped Subaru's snot.

"Never again, that..."

A sense of mission burns hot in his chest — *I mustn't let it happen.*
But even fired up like that, when it comes to figuring out what to actually do, his thoughts stall. Just how had that desperate situation come about in the first place?

Todd and Arakiya, climbing up onto the island of exiled sword slaves under the guise of imperial messengers.
It's headache enough that one of the "Nine Divine Generals," someone he's fought before, stands in the way — and having Todd, a devilkin, added on top is the worst nightmare imaginable.
To begin with, why were Todd and Arakiya together at all?

"...Idiot, remember, me. It was him who let the captured Arakiya escape."

Maybe it was shocking enough that his Todd-related memories are unusually clear.
The last of Subaru's history with Todd was in the walled city — Guaral — in the middle of the operation to seize the city hall. Their last contact was when Subaru, in disguise as a woman, met him.
After that, the rampaging Arakiya was taken down by red Priscilla, and the Arakiya who should have been captured was let loose by someone — and Subaru had suspected it was Todd.
If they're together now, that suspicion wasn't wrong, it seems.

"Damn it, you kept saying you wanted to go home to your fiancée, so why the hell are you here? If you'd just quietly holed up back home... you damn liar...!"

Growling that a promise had been broken didn't make Todd vanish. Even knowing that, he had to vent the surging anger once.
Having done that, he took a deep breath in, out, in, out.
And then—

"The next sacrifice for 'Sparka,' huh..."

"Not necessarily. Weitz, mind lending me a hand for a bit?"

"—. Did you already forget what I just said...?"

Feeling the tremor through the soles of his feet, the tattoo on Weitz's face twisted at Subaru's question.
At this timing, the words Weitz had spoken. His head had been in a panic from the aftereffects of the "medicine," but the impact of those words meant he remembered them.

"Just lend a hand, right? So lend it."

"Fine, as long as you understand... So, what do you want me to do?"

Naturally, he'd assumed he'd be asked to go see the next sword-slave candidate coming across the drawbridge.
Even though that's surely what he expected to be proposed, Weitz, arms crossed, switches gears fast. His loyalty, and the stubbornness with which he keeps to a rule he's decided on once, are genuinely a huge help.
Deeply grateful for Weitz's character—

"Just a little will do. Right now, I want you to hold off the group that's just arrived on the island."

△▼△▼△▼△

—Natsuki Subaru's sole weapon: the Authority of "Return by Death."

Since coming to the Vollachian Empire, in the midst of overcoming various obstacles, it had clearly begun to malfunction.
The greatest suspected cause is the "Infantilization" currently assaulting Subaru, ongoing even now.
Ever since his body shrank like this, "Return by Death" has been thoroughly abnormal.

The most extreme instance of this was those worst ten seconds at the Crimson Lapis Castle in Chaosflame.
An infinite ten seconds seemingly given not to save Subaru, but to drive him to despair — and even after that passed, the situation stayed just as bad on the island of exiled sword slaves where he was flung next.

Not as bad as the ten seconds at the castle keep, but the grace period given him is abnormally short.
This time too, he could only come back roughly thirty minutes — and if you consider the timing when everyone on the island began to die as the limit, that window is even shorter.
In other words, Subaru has to rewrite the situation within this span of under thirty minutes.
For that—

"First, I need time to think. —Remember, remember, remember, what happened."

Relying on Weitz, who'd firmly agreed to take on the request, Subaru had asked him to delay the visiting messengers from being brought onto the island.
However, he'd also told Weitz not to go so far as to start a fight himself.

The reason was simple: even if Weitz went on a rampage, with Arakiya there he'd be stopped in an instant. And if Todd was there too, Weitz would be killed without mercy.
Both of those were outcomes Subaru wanted to avoid.

"Everyone, bleeding from the face, dead... poison gas? Some kind of magic?"

It had happened right in the middle of confiding that he planned to escape the island of exiled sword slaves.
Hiain and Weitz, Idra, one after another struck down, and Tanza was no exception. Everyone died the same way; probably, there was no difference there either.
If it meant they shared the same cause of death, then the other fallen sword slaves too — no, wait.

"The guards were dead too."

If he properly recalled what had happened, there truly were no exceptions in that hell.
At the time his head hadn't been working, so he hadn't confirmed whether the sword beasts in their cages were alive, but at the very least, every human was dead, without exception — no, that's wrong too. There was an exception.

"Me, and Todd."

Yes, only Subaru and Todd had faced each other on the island without dying.
Though even without dying, the same agonizing near-death suffering, the same phenomenon of blood pouring from the face, had happened to Subaru too. Todd didn't seem to have that.
Since it's hard to imagine that was mere bravado, Todd must have been excluded from that method of death.

"If it's poison gas, maybe Todd drank an antidote before coming? If it's some kind of magic... did Arakiya use it? Now that I think about it, Arakiya wasn't anywhere."

He hadn't meant to, but he'd ended up checking the bodies of everyone he recognized, one by one.
Of course Tanza and the others who'd fallen right in front of him, but also Orson and the others he'd saved in the second "Sparka," Old Man Null who'd spent his time in the healing room, the familiar guard with the harsh, cold attitude.
Every single one of them, bleeding from the face, unmoving.

"But there are three faces I haven't seen."

—Those were Arakiya, Gustav, and the fake Cecilus.

"——"

Of course, Subaru doesn't remember every single sword slave on the island, so he can't say with total certainty that everyone else was dead. But those three, he's certain, were nowhere to be found.
Arakiya is on Todd's side. As an ally of Todd, presumably the mastermind behind that massacre, it's only natural she wasn't among the dead.
But what about the other two?

Gustav, the island's administrator, in the position of warden, and the mysterious, suspicious child, the fake Cecilus.
Those two held special positions even on this island of exiled sword slaves—

"—Ah."

Just as he recalled those two special figures, a bolt of lightning suddenly shot through Subaru's mind.
He'd been suspecting poison gas or magic as the method behind the massacre that occurred on the island, but one more possibility had surfaced, faint as it was.
It was the thing that holds great sway only on this island of exiled sword slaves — the "Curse Clause."

The invisible, restraining bond that bound the sword slaves of the island of exiled sword slaves, Ginunhive, hand and foot.
Defy it, and it's said even your life would be at risk — that Curse Clause rises as a candidate.

"But the Curse Clause is just a threat, it shouldn't actually exist."

On that point, Subaru himself had gained certainty through a life-or-death exchange with Gustav.
He'd stated outright that he intended to escape the island of exiled sword slaves, utterly destroying Gustav's standing, and declared he'd go against the will of the Vollachian Emperor Gustav swore loyalty to.
If the Curse Clause really existed, Gustav should have used it right there. Without making an example of Subaru like that, the order of the island of exiled sword slaves would fall apart.
It would be a crisis if it got out that the Curse Clause didn't exist and the sword slaves started rioting.

"No matter how much Mr. Gustav doesn't want us dying, bending on that wouldn't make sense. So there shouldn't be a Curse Clause... no, wait, I'm overthinking this."

Whether or not Gustav's policy of keeping sword-slave deaths to a minimum applied, there was a much clearer piece of evidence against the Curse Clause. It was scattered everywhere.
Wasn't it not just the sword slaves who'd died, but the guards too?

"Mr. Gustav has no reason to wipe out the guards. ...And if we're going there, he has no reason to suddenly massacre the sword slaves either."

The more he thought about it, the more the massacre and Gustav's will seemed unconnected.
If so, the reasoning circles back to the very start — it seems safe to narrow it down to two options: either Todd, the mastermind, released poison gas, or he cooperated with Arakiya to use some outrageous magic.
However, both of these share the exact same problem.

"Why was the effect only weak on me?"

Setting aside Todd, who'd presumably prepared countermeasures beforehand, Subaru was the only one whose symptoms were mild.
What is different about Subaru, compared to everyone else who died?

Being a child? —No, Tanza had died.
A difference in what he'd been doing right before? —No, Weitz had died.
A difference by race? —No, Hiain and Idra had died together.

Being from another world? Having spent time in the Kingdom? Being in a Contract with an incredibly cute spirit? Continuing to be the victim of the "Witch's" stalking? Being smaller than his original body due to "Infantilization"? What else, what else, what else, what else, what else could there be?

—Something about Subaru alone, different from everyone else, what is it?

"What—"

"—Oh my, Bussu, something troubling you?"

"Waugh!?"

While Subaru was desperately racking his brain, searching for possibilities, a voice came from behind him.
Because it was so sudden, and because it came from someone he'd mentally ruled out as a possible speaker, Subaru let out a shriek, spun around, and his eyes went wide.

Behind Subaru, walking with his hands tucked into his kimono sleeves, is the fake Cecilus.
His attitude, as flippant as ever, makes Subaru blink several times.

"Se-Sessy...?"

"Yes indeed, but what's with that reaction? You look as though my being here were some earth-shattering, absurd development. Certainly I am this world's leading man, so I understand a bit of awe, but I did think Bussu and I have spent our time together within talking and touching distance, you know?"

As if to prove it, the fake Cecilus comes right up, peering up into Subaru's face from close enough that his breath grazes it.
At the utterly unchanged attitude and gestures, Subaru instinctively steps back.
Then the fake Cecilus, from beside the retreating Subaru, quietly looks down at the view below — from beside Subaru, who had climbed up to the high ground, at the already-lowered drawbridge.

"Seems this round's dragon carriage wasn't carrying the next 'Sparka' participants, but some other guests entirely. The whole island's abuzz with the topic."

"...Yeah, apparently they're messengers from the capital. Not sure what they've come for."

"Messengers from the capital brings to mind either news of the outside situation, or perhaps a meeting about the next show. Isn't that welcome news for a Bussu who wants to get outside?"

"—. I won't deny it."

Shading his eyes with a hand as he gazes across the lake's surface, the fake Cecilus gets this brief answer from Subaru.
Honestly, with this unplanned encounter with the fake Cecilus, Subaru couldn't decide how to act toward him.

Subaru doesn't think the fake Cecilus took part in the massacre alongside Todd and the others.
But it's a fact that he, presumably the most feared sword slave on the island of exiled sword slaves, had vanished during that massacre.

"Even whether my ears were working right is questionable, but still..."

In that incredible situation, he can hardly claim to have been mentally stable.
Even so, if anyone on the island had been fighting or clashing, he can say with certainty he wouldn't have missed hearing it.
So, thinking about what must have happened to the fake Cecilus at that time—

"You died somewhere I didn't see, or you weren't on the island?"

But if he wasn't on the island, where, and how?
Even now, in this moment talking with the fake Cecilus, the time limit keeps closing in. The fake Cecilus, too, presumably has no more grace period until that outrageous situation arrives.

"I won't deny it. But that's the face of someone with something else on his mind."

Picking up on the trailing edge of Subaru's vague, hesitant words, purely on instinct, the fake Cecilus. Not by reading Subaru's expression or words, but probably a wild guess. Intuition. That's the kind of guy who pulls out the right answer that way — the fake Cecilus type.

Because it's a world crowded with such a handful of monsters, Subaru's resolve is being tested.
The resolve not to lose, the resolve that he mustn't lose.

"Sessy, something outrageous is about to happen on this island. Probably, the guys from the capital are going to do it. I can't say exactly what, though..."

"From how grave you look, it's something too horrible to even voice aloud... a crisis, or should I say a mortal peril, for that deer-girl and Bussu's 'party' members."

"—Ngh"

"Ahh, Bussu's reactions are so vivid, so pleasing! But I see. Splendid that the omens have finally arrived. I was on the verge of dying of the wait!"

Laughing with his mouth open, the fake Cecilus stamps his foot with vigor.
Stunned at that reaction, like trouble is something to welcome wholeheartedly, Subaru then bares his teeth and closes the distance, leaning in.

"Sessy! This isn't a joke! Everyone's in danger!"

"All the better! If it's not a joke, nothing could be finer. Being told it's all a fabrication, a figment of overthinking, would be far more deflating, so this is most welcome. Crisis, peril, hardship — a great help!"

"—!"

"Or did you think, perhaps, that I would unconditionally side with Bussu?"

Told that he didn't understand the gravity of the situation, the fake Cecilus's voice drops low. As if to say it's Subaru who doesn't understand.
Feeling something blade-like and cold in those narrow blue eyes, Subaru holds his breath.
And to the now-silenced Subaru, the fake Cecilus grins broadly,

"—It's here. Right here."

And with those words thrown straight at Subaru, he spins on the spot and throws both arms wide.
As if turning this halfway point of the island of exiled sword slaves, this high ground overlooking half the island and the lake, into his stage. The fake Cecilus looks up at the sky, opens his mouth wide, and—

"Truly, here is the watershed! I asked whether you could win me over unconditionally — allow me to tell you the condition to meet that, is this very moment!"

"This, right now..."

"Yes, yes, exactly! Bussu shall enchant me with premonition! Bind me with anticipation! Make me laugh with clever maneuvering! And in the climax, prove it!"

"——"

"Come now, wolf run through by a sword! And you Observers who look ever down upon this harsh, cruel earth, bear witness! Who, indeed, shall captivate this Cecilus Segmunt!"

A grand declaration that could only be made by someone utterly convinced of an audience out there beyond the sky.
Utterly, this eccentric behavior of the fake Cecilus deserved to have his sanity questioned — surely something everyone on this island would avert their eyes and ears from — and yet, Subaru felt a strange certainty settle over him.

The fake Cecilus is — no, Cecilus Segmunt is the real thing.

This genuine monster truly believes he can converse with the world, and if the world genuinely had a will, the world would indeed be captivated by him.
That much presence, that much persuasive force, surges within this small monster.

"You..."

"What is it?"

"I'm making you one of mine. I've decided — I'm going to work you to the bone."

Overwhelmed, even though it was hardly the moment for it, Subaru put a hand to his own chest and said it.
Looking his opponent squarely in the eye, he declared it.

"—I don't dislike that recklessness. Rather, I like it."

In a tone tinged with a smile, Cecilus answered Subaru not with his usual carefree grin, but with just the faintest loosening of his lips into a smile.
That might have been proof that Cecilus had rightly acknowledged Subaru as a challenger.

Immediately after—

"—Hm."

Pressing his smiling lips together, Cecilus gently stroked his own chin with a finger.
The chin down which blood dripped, trickling from his well-shaped nose.

"Wha—!"

Seeing the fresh blood spilling from Cecilus's nose, Subaru hastily looks out beyond the railing.
He can't see through into the buildings or underground within the island stretched out below, but even so, in the range he could glimpse here and there, he could see several fallen human silhouettes.

It had begun. The massacre.
While Subaru had spent his time here sorting through the situation and mulling over possibilities.
"Death" was running rampant across the whole island, reaping lives without exception.

Yes, without exception—

"Whoop."

"Sessy!?"

Pulling away from the railing, feeling something sharp prick at the back of his nose, Subaru worries about Cecilus, who'd sat down right beside him.
Cecilus had leaned his back against the railing, plopping down cross-legged on the spot.
An unhurried attitude, but the blood from his nose, which he pressed with a hand, hadn't stopped.

"A-are you, okay?"

"Ahh, looks tough. This is quite the formidable opponent, Bussu. A worthy adversary, wouldn't you say?"

"Is this the time for that!? Your nose is bleeding, and your eyes too!"

"Hm... here I'd been proud of not shedding a single drop of blood."

Even as they spoke, blood began flowing from the seated Cecilus's eyes.
Cecilus, whose condition was worsening to something almost too awful to watch, kept letting blood pour from his nose without his voice changing tone in the slightest — that was the frightening part.
But the scariest thing about Cecilus wasn't even that.

"Aren't you... scared to die?"

"People die, you know."

"——"

Understanding that what loomed before him was "death," Cecilus's answer held no hesitation.
No hesitation, no confusion, no fear, no anxiety, no tension, no regret.

There's presumably no "beyond death" for him the way there is for Subaru.
And yet Cecilus has none of the fear of "death" that a living creature should hold above all else.
And, without ever showing any sign of fearing "death"—

"——"

Lowering the hand pressing his nose, wrapping his blood-stained sleeve around his arm as if to hide it, Cecilus stops moving, his back still resting against the railing.
Head bowed, eyes closed, he doesn't so much as twitch.
Subaru understands clearly what that means.
And then—

"Cough."

The hand held to his coughing mouth stained with blood, "death" was closing in on Subaru too, right nearby.

△▼△▼△▼△

The same, yes, the same devastation had taken hold of the entire island of exiled sword slaves.

"——"

Dragging his feet slowly, Subaru walked around the island, searching for survivors.
But now that even Cecilus was excluded from the candidates, no presence appeared to answer Subaru's tattered hopes.

"Tanza, and everyone... Old Man Null too..."

Everyone had fallen, their lives lost.
Closing the eyes of everyone left with expressions of stunned incomprehension at what had happened, gently wiping their faces — that was the most Subaru could do.

"Sorry, Tanza..."

He'd failed. Gotten the order wrong.
He should have warned Tanza and the others of the danger too, let them take some kind of precaution.
Before climbing up to the high ground, he should have joined up with Tanza and the others, stayed with them at the very least. Then he wouldn't have let a girl, anxious in this unreliable place, die without understanding anything.
Until he found their bodies, such an obvious thing hadn't even occurred to him.

"——"

Making his rounds of the island, he confirmed, sure enough, that the guards too were dead.
The manner of death was the same as the sword slaves' — everyone dead, bleeding from the face. Going next to check the cages where the sword beasts were confined, they too were all dead.
Fallen in their cages, blood flowing from eyes and nose, dying the same way as the humans.

"...Guess I'm just tougher, then."

In an almost-dying state himself, an unfunny joke slips out at the sight of the dead sword beasts.
Breathing is difficult because his nose is completely blocked with blood. His eyes are blurred and misty too, and wiping them with a finger leaves a faint red curtain over everything.

The reaper creeping slowly, only toward Subaru, is nasty.
At that nasty reaper, Subaru raises a trembling middle finger.
And then—

"—What are you doing here, of all places?"

In the middle level of the isolated island, in the place called the courtyard, the reaper cracks his own neck and mutters.
Wearing a black bandana, with the face of a would-be nice young man — the reaper, Todd. Watching Subaru raise a middle finger at him, he slowly walks closer.

"From the looks of it, I figured everyone on the island had died to the last man, but..."

Deftly drawing a large knife, Todd looks at Subaru with a puzzled expression.
He's probably thinking he's fallen into some inexplicable situation. Meeting a living human here is surely unexpected, for Todd.
The sensation of having been lain in wait for must have unsettled Todd too.

But Subaru had known, if he waited here, that Todd would come.
He hadn't wanted to see him, if he could have avoided it, but this was an unavoidable confrontation.
No one else was alive anymore, and until Subaru himself died, the reaper was the only one there was any possibility of exchanging words with.

"——"

His eyes are wary, but there's no hesitation in the footsteps closing the distance. That's not so much contempt for Subaru as an attitude of not treating Subaru as a threat at all.
It's not that he's underestimating him for being a child.
Without joke or metaphor, Subaru really is on the verge of death. If Todd were holding excessive wariness even toward a dying child who can do nothing more, that wouldn't be caution — it would be cowardice.
Todd is cunning and cautious, but not cowardly, is the thing.

—In contrast, Subaru, who through sheer stupidity had let a girl's "death" slip past unnoticed, thinks.

Todd showing up as expected, lain in wait for, was as hoped, but whether the unfolding from here goes as hoped is another matter.
Even if this is a chance, Todd isn't someone who'll easily hand over hints. Even to a pitiful, dying child, he has no kindness that would offer up a parting gift for the road to the afterlife.
So then, how does he bring back any piece of information at all from this cold-blooded reaper?

Probably, just one.
Even if things go incredibly well, the information he can get is just one thing.
That one thing has to be made to count.

"——"

Step by step, the distance between Subaru and Todd closes.
The moment this distance hits zero, he wouldn't be surprised if the man silently stabbed Subaru dead. With a head and body drained of blood, he has to land on the best possible answer.

It's Todd who carried out this massacre on the island. No point asking that.
Who's survived. No use asking that either. Meaningless.
Where is Arakiya. He wants to know, but there's no way he'd answer. Pointless.
What's Gustav up to. Same as with Arakiya, a wasted shot.

Then, what's left is — the method of this massacre.

"——"

Poison gas — no. He erases that option from his mind.
The reason he'd ended up encountering Cecilus on the island's midlevel, on the high ground, was because Subaru had decided to hole up there. —If the answer to the massacre was poison gas, the windy high ground might be safe.
But as it turned out, neither Subaru nor Cecilus had escaped the massacre's toll. Given that even those outside the island buildings had fallen, the possibility of poison gas was quite low.

Then, what's left as a possibility—

"—Ah."

Arakiya's magic. Arakiya, who'd appeared together with Todd, using some kind of massacre magic to wipe out the sword slaves, the guards, the sword beasts of the island.
If he could gain certainty of that, then next, next, he could do so much better.

If certainty could be gained—

*"—It's here, right here."*

In that instant, an auditory hallucination overtook Subaru's mind.
The voice of the star performer, who by now should be nowhere at all, generates heat within Subaru's weakening thoughts.
And then, as if spurred on by that heat, he opens his mouth—

"—The Curse Clause!!"

A possibility once dismissed, but never fully erased, thrown at the reaper in a raised voice.
That bloodshot cry of Subaru's becomes an inescapable strike, catching the reaper. And the instant he heard that scream, the reaper — Todd — moved.

"Die."

All trace of wariness, all trace of that bothered air, vanishes, leaving only killing intent behind.
A thick, oozing killing intent dense enough to make one faint if witnessed with a sound mind. Carrying that in his eyes and in the knife in his hand, Todd closes on Subaru in a single leap.

"Gah."

The cold blade thrust out sinks deep into Subaru's chest.
Without a millimeter of mercy, the knife stirs through everything precious inside his chest and is pulled out with a twist, ensuring there's no possible chance of survival.
The blood that spurts out splashes, staining Todd's expressionless face with fresh blood.

"—Ghob."

Stabbed and withdrawn, the force sends his body toppling onto the hard ground. Blood overflows his throat with a force he's never experienced, and mixed into the blood he coughs up, gob, is the wrapped "medicine."
He'd bitten down, failed to break the wrapping open. But this was a wound that needed no "medicine."

"Sane enough to talk, coming out of your mouth like that?"

Picking up the wrapping, now dyed crimson, between his fingers, Todd mutters, sniffing at it. Whether he'd gathered from the smell alone that the contents were dangerous, or figured it out from it coming out of Subaru's mouth.
Either way, it doesn't matter. It's nothing that reaches Subaru anymore.

Only, only, if there's something he can say, then it's this.

"...O—"

"—What?"

"O-one."

He grabbed one thing.
Changing his expression, prioritizing it above everything else, he'd hurried to kill Subaru.
So, he'd grabbed it.

"Cur—"

Grabbing, grabbing, and then, he died.
In exchange for dying, he'd grabbed it.

—That what kills the island of exiled sword slaves, Ginunhive, is the "Curse Clause" that shouldn't exist.
