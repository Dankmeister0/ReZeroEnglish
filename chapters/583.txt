Arc 7 Chapter 78: "A Reunion on the Brink"

Breath ragged, running as though flying so as not to jostle the head of the girl in her arms.
The instant word reached her that a line of carriages had arrived on the south side of the city, that a great many people had come from the witch-city of Chaosflame, Emilia set her silver hair dancing for the reunion she'd waited so long for.

"We'll get to see Subaru soon, Beatrice…!"

Cradled in her lifted arms, the girl who slept with her eyes closed — Beatrice.
She had been kept sleeping long and deep to hold down her exhaustion, and the one absolute condition for waking her was contact with Subaru, her Contract partner, so she could receive a supply of mana from him.
On Subaru's end too, unless Beatrice took his mana off his hands, things would get bad for his body — truly, the two of them were what you'd call a community of fate.

Normally the two of them holding hands together, looking so pleased with each other, was a heartwarming sight, but in a situation where Subaru had been flung off to the Empire and the two were kept apart, it became something quite serious indeed.
They needed to get Subaru and Beatrice cuddled up together again as soon as possible.
And besides that—

"—Subaru."

Emilia herself wanted, even a single second sooner, to confirm with her own eyes that Subaru was safe.
She hadn't felt this restless since Puck had unilaterally broken their Contract and vanished off on his own — no, it was more than that, even.
Puck might be fine on his own, but Subaru wasn't like that.
Someone who understood Subaru needed to be properly by his side.

"Uu~, sorry!"

Opening eyes she'd squeezed tightly shut, Emilia set about taking a shortcut straight to the city hall ahead of her.
She really ought to go in through the entrance and climb the stairs all the way to the top, but the thought of doing that made her too impatient, so she made a foothold of ice instead. Riding atop an ice pillar that surged up from the ground with a great groaning heave, Emilia's body — with Beatrice held in her arms — shot straight up to the top of the building in one go.
She'd startle the watchmen and everyone in the city, but she couldn't help it.
Now, finally—

"Subaru!"

"Mu, Emilia-ka."

Once the ice pillar reached the height of the top floor, Emilia leaped from it into the building.
She landed and lifted her head to find quite a number of people already gathered in the city hall, and everyone seemed startled by Emilia's sudden entrance.
Amid it all, the one who received Emilia's arrival with composure was a woman with a wooden prosthetic leg and striking, piercing eyes — Mizelda, who led the People of Shudrak.
She tapped the tip of her wooden leg against the floor, gazed at the ice pillar behind Emilia, and said,

"Ya use magic mighty conveniently. Different from the roots up, we are."

"Mm, I was in a super hurry, so… ah, sorry for startling you! Um, I'll get rid of it right away. There!"

At Mizelda's impressed reaction, Emilia hastily broke the ice pillar down into mana.
In the Kingdom, using magic for this and that was fairly common, but in the Empire it seemed there weren't many mages around. It was a strange feeling, given that the Empire was said to be full of people eager to fight.

"Most times, if ya hit a body before they can cast their magic, that settles the talk."

"I think I get that too. But only when they're close, not far away."

"If they're far off, then it's my bow's turn. Though for skill with a bow, Taritta's the better hand, more than me."

Mizelda's lips curved as she said this, and Emilia smiled back, "Is that so."
The name "Taritta," which she'd heard a few times now, should be Mizelda's younger sister's name. Taritta, apparently Mizelda's pride and joy of a sister — she'd been with the group that left Guaral and headed for the witch-city to the southeast—

"—! Oh, right, right. The people who went south came back, didn't they?"

For a moment her mind had wandered elsewhere, but that was, after all, the very reason Emilia had come rushing here in such a hurry, even resorting to magic.
Shifting Beatrice back against her chest, Emilia swept her gaze around the great hall on the top floor.
Here and there she spotted unfamiliar faces in travel-worn clothes — proof, surely, that people who'd made the carriage journey and reached the fortress city had arrived.
Among them—

"Subaru! Are you here!? Everyone's come to meet you! Otto-kun, and Petra-cha… Petra-ojousama too, will be here soon!"

Restlessly, she scanned the crowd for the black-haired boy she'd been waiting for.
When she'd heard at the house where they were staying that the carriages had arrived, Emilia, her heart racing, had scooped up Beatrice and rushed to city hall ahead of Petra and Otto.
Those two were surely dashing to city hall in a hurry to see Subaru as well.
She shared the same feelings as those two, and yet Emilia knew it was unfair of her to have come rushing here, out of breath, ahead of them. Even so, she'd wanted to see Subaru sooner.
It wasn't only about Beatrice — Emilia herself, with Subaru—

"—! Subaru!"

Suddenly, someone tapped her shoulder from behind, and Emilia spun around as if struck.
The turn was so forceful that the person who'd tapped her shoulder blinked, eyes wide with surprise. But it wasn't Subaru, the one she sought, whose eyes had gone wide there.

"Emilia…"

"Frederica… you got here first, huh. Um, well…"

Somehow embarrassed, Emilia didn't know what expression to make.
Of course, Frederica had been worried about Subaru too, so it was no wonder she'd be at city hall ahead of her.

"So, um, did you get to talk to Subaru? See, I want to let him see Beatrice. I want to talk with Subaru too, but first Beatrice needs to—"

"—. There is something I must tell you."

"Eh?"

Fumbling, as though making excuses, Emilia trailed off, and before her Frederica lowered the corners of her lovely emerald eyes, speaking in a subdued voice.
Emilia couldn't understand why Frederica would take such a manner.

"Ah, Garfiel…"

Looking around, she saw that Frederica hadn't been the only one at city hall first — Garfiel was there too. Add Petra and the others arriving later, and all of Emilia's group who'd come to Guaral would be gathered together.
All of them together, they could welcome back Subaru, who'd been separated from them.
That's what Emilia had been hoping for, but—

"I'm afraid — Subaru-sama has gone missing again, in the witch-city Chaosflame. …In the carriage that returned, Subaru-sama's figure was nowhere to be found."

And Frederica, looking as though it pained her even to say it, delivered that sorrowful report.


△▼△▼△▼△


"What do you mean Anchan and Rem-chan got taken away~!?"

Her big, round eyes went wide, and the girl cried out, stamping her feet in place.
A girl with a curious hairstyle, her long golden hair braided and tied into all manner of knots. She looked twelve or thirteen, her face full of bewilderment as she looked around at the adults nearby.

The place was the great hall on the top floor of the city hall in the fortress city of Guaral, where those who'd been given various roles in the upheaval of this city — or rather, of this Empire — had been gathered.
Otto Suwen felt like holding his head in his hands at the fact that they'd somehow gotten swept up in such an upheaval.
Still, this was in front of people flying a national flag depicting a wolf pierced by a sword. Showing such a faint-hearted face was out of the question, so he kept his expression composed.

"We come back and the city's a total wreck, even the walls are gone, this is awful! And on top of that Anchan and everyone's gone too, I'm so shocked my head's spinning~!"

"Aa, uu!"

True to her words, the girl stomped her feet, eyes and head spinning round and round. Beside her, another girl of about the same age, this one also blonde, let out a groan of agreement.
Faced with the two girls' joint appeal, the adults couldn't hide their confusion and bewilderment.
Given the situation, someone needed to take charge, but Otto was torn on whether he should step forward, and besides, he found himself unable to move, gripped by a strange, nagging feeling.

"…Um, that girl who's groaning — I feel like I've seen her somewhere."

Beside the girl speaking up so readily, the other one, who uttered no meaningful words, struck Otto as somehow familiar, though he couldn't say why.
Ordinarily, Otto made a point of never forgetting anyone he'd exchanged words with, but his memory grew hazy after truly heavy drinking. Since starting to work for the Emilia Camp, he'd done his best to avoid getting drunk enough to lose his memory — but perhaps this was a girl he'd encountered in such an inebriated state.

"Hey, I wonder if Anchan and everyone's okay? Anyone who knows…"

"—Before that, allow me to confirm one thing, if I may, young lady."

"Hm? What, what, Zikr-chin?"

While Otto pondered, it was Zikr, the city's representative, who spoke up to the girl.
He was someone Otto had already exchanged words with many times, and someone he regarded as trustworthy. At the very least, simply being a person in the Empire one could actually talk to made him a hard-to-find asset.
Zikr knit his thick brows, looking the girl carefully up and down, and said,

"If my perception is not mistaken, would you happen to be none other than Miss Medium?"

"Eh? Yeah, that's right, what about it? You can tell just by looking, can't you?"

"Uu!"

Hands on her hips, chest thrust out proudly — the girl called Medium nodded, and beside her, the girl Otto couldn't place struck the very same pose.
At her words, Zikr, looking troubled, turned his eyes to the person standing beside Medium.
And then—

"—Abel-dono, this is…"

"This is the work of Olbart Dunkelkenn's technique. That her answers missed the mark is due to her original disposition, not to this form she's taken."

"Is that so. Olbart-ichishou's doing… I am glad you are unharmed."

"If this can be called unharmed, then yes."

Hearing the explanation, Zikr, looking as though it had clicked into place, gently touched a hand to his chin. The person who had prompted this crossed his arms, unruffled, and glanced toward Medium.

"Those left behind are confused. Astonished, that is, at your having shrunk."

"Me? …Ah! Oh yeah! Sorry, sorry, everyone! I'd gotten so used to being small I forgot to mention it, but I got shrunk! Along with Subaru-chin and everyone!"

Raising a small hand with her small body, Medium explained her situation. Hearing this, Otto found nothing clicked into place for him at all, but the Empire's own people seemed to have some sense of understanding.
Their own lack of knowledge on the matter, causing their confusion, was undeniable, but more than that, there was something in what had just been said that Otto and the others couldn't afford to let pass.

"—Subaru"
"Uau…"

A faint murmur, spoken at the same time, presumably carrying the same meaning.
The one whose voice carried a resonance clearly distinct from the groaning before was the girl beside Medium with the downcast face. And the one who uttered a sound of the same import as that girl—

"Are you worried about Subaru too?"

That was Emilia, her brows lowering at the girl's demeanor.
Holding the sleeping Beatrice in her arms, Emilia was one of those who'd been especially downcast to hear that Subaru wasn't among the group that had returned from the witch-city.
Compared to her and Petra, calling Otto's own disappointment by the same name would be presumptuous. It wasn't as if he'd had some premonition that reunion wouldn't go smoothly.
In any case—

"Uu, aau."

"No, I don't think it's your fault. I was super disappointed Subaru wasn't here, but this isn't the time to mope. Subaru's the one having a much harder time."

"Uu…"

"You're worried too? Thank you. You must have had a hard time as well."

The downcast girl and Emilia communicated like this.
The words spilling from the girl's mouth carried no clear meaning, yet Emilia exchanged words with her, looking steadily into her eyes as though she truly understood.
That alone spoke to the resolve of a downhearted Emilia, and it was an exchange that couldn't help but bring Otto some relief. However—

"—To think that Natsuki Subaru's companions would come all this way."

The voice cut through the great hall like a cold, dry wind.
Emilia's gaze rose sharply toward the one who'd spoken — the man in the demon mask who had exchanged words with Zikr and Medium a moment ago. The black-haired man, his face covered by the oni mask; at the glint of his eyes, Emilia narrowed her violet gaze.

"Are you Subaru's friend too?"

"I have no friends. Nor does that one think of me as such. We are merely bound together, hands joined, on the way to our respective goals."

"Then you're an ally. You were called Abel — could you be the person Zikr-san told me about before?"

Though the man's reply was hard and pointed, Emilia, undaunted, turned to address Zikr instead. To that question, Zikr dipped his chin. "Yes."

"This is the man who stands as our leader — Abel-dono. I myself have simply been entrusted with overseeing the city on his instruction."

"To say so, and yet you have let it suffer so much attrition."

"…I have no excuse to offer."

Making a bitter face, Zikr bowed deeply to the man — Abel — over the damage the city had sustained.
What showed in Zikr's grim expression wasn't resentment at unreasonable rebuke, but the color of self-reproach, regret at his own insufficiency.
As someone who'd actually been present on the ground, Otto thought that the city having escaped total annihilation, given the circumstances, was the result of everyone in the city — Zikr included — doing their absolute best, but—

"Abel-chin, don't say such mean things! Everyone did their best!"

"And yet, I thought you were also enraged that your brother was taken?"

"Anchan got taken because he did his best! If Anchan hadn't gotten taken, it would've been way worse! That's just like Anchan! Even getting taken, he's so cool!"

It was Medium who came at Abel's remark with such vigor.
From the flow of the conversation, her brother must be the traveling merchant said to have been taken along with Rem. Mizelda had described him as a gentle, approachable ladies' man.
According to what Kuna and Holly had said, the fact that this man and Rem had been taken was, in truth, the decisive factor that had made the enemy's "Nine Divine Generals" call off the flying dragons.
In other words, Medium's brother-loving outburst wasn't so far off the mark.
Of course, Abel, having just returned from the witch-city, had no way of knowing the circumstances behind this—

"Zikr."

"Yes."

"Disregard my earlier words. To begin with, you faced Madelyn Eschart and a swarm of Flying Dragons. Under circumstances where abandoning the city would have been forgivable, your efforts are worthy of praise."

"—! I am honored."

The words, delivered without hesitation, were surely not the result of being moved by Medium's pleading.
They were words prepared from the start to be given to Zikr. From the very beginning, it seemed, there had been no intention of blaming him over the damage Guaral had suffered.

"…Abel-chin, you're so mean-natured!"
"Uu!"

"Enough."

Medium and the girl assailed Abel, standing there with his arms crossed like a temple guardian, from both sides. Beset by the two of them, his sleeve tugged and his side poked, Abel offered a curt rebuke.
It seemed, more or less, that Otto had gotten a sense of the relationship there — but—

"—So? Just when might this conversation proceed?"

Amid all this, the woman who had quietly watched the proceedings unfold finally moved her lips.
A fox-eared beauty carrying a presence oddly out of place here, an excessively resplendent bearing — in some sense, a figure impossible to ignore simply by existing, much like Emilia with her otherworldly beauty, or Abel whose bizarre demon mask drew the eye.

One could tell without it being explicitly stated.
That the woman with this presence was precisely the reason Abel and Medium had left the fortress city and headed for the witch-city of Chaosflame.

"Yorna Mishigure-ichishou."

Who was it that murmured that, quietly?
Probably one of the assembled faces, the name slipping from someone's lips. But since no one raised a voice to correct it, that alone was proof enough that it was correct.

—Yorna Mishigure.
The ruler of the witch-city Chaosflame, and one of the "Nine Divine Generals," the Empire's mightiest military officers. And one of the absolute conditions determining the outcome of this upheaval.
Bringing her onto their side would be a major linchpin, capable of shaking the very foundations of this vast Empire.
The very journey on which Subaru had gone missing had, at its root, had the purpose of recruiting her.

"Yorna-ichishou, first, allow me to thank you for making the journey. I am Zikr Osman, one who holds the rank of Second General in the Vollachia Empire."

"I've heard your name along the way. Seeing the real thing, you do seem a gentleman of some worth, as Medium and Taritta said."

"Miss Medium and Miss Taritta did?"

Zikr, who had begun speaking solemnly, blinked at Yorna's reply.
This hardly seemed the moment for it, but his gaze turned toward Medium, and toward another tall woman. A dark-skinned woman with a somewhat masculine air — she must be the Taritta in question.
The two of them answered Zikr's gaze with smiles and nods. Given his gentlemanly bearing, which had earned him the reputation of a "lady's man," it might have been ticklish yet pleasing for him.
He gave an "Ahem" to steel his feelings once more, and said,

"Then, does this mean Yorna-ichishou will fight alongside Abel-dono as well?"

"You may think of it that way, if you like. I have my own designs, but as it happens, they align with this one's — Abel's."

"The matter is settled. There is no issue with an accord with Yorna Mishigure."

Clad in a magnificent kimono, a golden pipe in hand, Yorna carried herself with a composure that belied the considerable unease her reputation had inspired beforehand.
Word had already reached Otto and the others that Chaosflame had suffered enormous damage.
That was precisely why the carriages that had reached Guaral formed such a massive line, and why they now found themselves needing to take in so many people at once.
And in the midst of the great disaster the witch-city had suffered—

"—Subaru got sent off somewhere."

"—————"

It was a voice like a silver bell that cut into the conversation — Emilia.
Interrupted from another angle, Abel's gaze, through his demon mask, turned to Emilia. His eyes clearly unwelcoming, yet Emilia didn't so much as flinch.
Partly because she held Beatrice in her arms, and partly because of Petra and Frederica standing quietly at her side, and Garfiel with his stern expression as well. Perhaps Otto had contributed a little to that too.

Certainly, Emilia's remark might be noise to them.
But they had their own circumstances to consider. It wouldn't do to have the conversation carry on while disregarding the people who had come rushing all this way, crossing borders to do so.

"Let's start over properly from the beginning, one more time. —I'm Emilia, and you're Abel, right? Subaru's friend… no, ally."

"—. That is correct."

"Subaru went with you to a different city… um, to Yorna-san's place, right? And something terrible happened there and you got separated. Is that right?"

"That's correct. Though 'something terrible' is putting it rather mildly."

Her fine brows knitting, Emilia confirmed what had happened, and Abel and Yorna both nodded. At their reaction, particularly Yorna's words, Emilia accepted it plainly with an "I see."
For those directly involved, the shallow words of an outsider must be galling. Emilia, too, seemed aware her own words might fall into that category, and chose her next ones carefully.

"After you got separated, you don't know where Subaru got sent to. You searched but couldn't find him, and lots of people lost their homes too. So you came back to this city, and now you're having this discussion here… is that right?"

"There is nothing to correct there. That said, it was quite the disaster. Given its scale, it's hard to imagine that one, having gone missing, is unharmed—"

"—Subaru's fine. That's certain."

Cutting Abel off, Emilia declared it, her voice not especially loud, but strong.
At the conviction packed into that voice, the black eyes behind the demon mask narrowed. A shadow of consideration crossed Abel's eyes, seeking the grounds for Emilia's answer.
Sure enough, after a beat, Abel pressed her: "Why can you say so with such certainty?"

"You lot cannot know the details of what occurred in the witch-city. Under those circumstances, where a single misstep could cost one's life, that one vanished without a trace — why can you claim it survived? —Do you have certainty that it survived, under those circumstances?"

"—————"

All that had reached Otto and the others' ears was word of Chaosflame's devastation, and rumor that it had been brought about by some incredible calamity.
The truth of Abel's words was unknown, and how much he might have embellished the facts was impossible to gauge. Still, Otto felt certain it wasn't meaningless bluster.
At the same time, he sensed a faint something catching in the man's tone.

Abel's manner was cold, but Otto could sense an intent to probe them. —No, more precisely, not so much them, as Subaru, through them.
It seemed Abel believed Subaru was holding something back. If that had to do with Subaru's standing in the Kingdom or the like, they couldn't hand over information carelessly.

"Emilia, this is—"

"My certainty is believing in Subaru. I believe Subaru's doing his best, holding on until we come to get him. No matter how hard it is, wherever he is."

Fearing a leading line of questioning, Otto had tried to head off the conversation, but Emilia answered ahead of him. Still, that was trust born of spirit alone, not something that could be called concrete grounds.
It wasn't what Otto had feared, nor was it what Abel had sought. So naturally, Abel lowered his eyes as though disappointed.

"Wishful thinking with no persuasive power behind it. That this is your grounds is disappointing."

"Really? I think believing in someone is a super good and important thing. If you're believed in, you can feel at ease, and if someone believes in you, you get strength from it. Isn't that so for you?"

"In the end, a state of mind is just a state of mind. It cannot possibly serve as decisive proof beyond that."

Shaking his head, closing one eye, Abel gave his answer, and Emilia's eyes wavered.
Otto had missed his chance to interject, but that alone told him the two of them were like oil and water. To put it plainly, it was the difference between Emilia, who believed in the value of the heart, and Abel, who did not.
This rift was likely a difference in worldview, and not one likely to be easily bridged.
Hence—

"—No one can declare with certainty whether Natsuki Subaru lives or dies. Even if that one has continually skirted the possibility of death by a hair's breadth thus far, there is no guarantee this time will be the same."

That declaration seemed to be Abel's conclusion to this exchange.
And hearing it, Emilia pressed her lips tightly together, and—

"You, could it be that about Subaru you—"

She fixed her eyes on Abel, seeming about to say something.
But before her words could continue, a girl stirred first. The girl who had been sleeping, eyes closed, in Emilia's arms.

"…If it's proof that Subaru's alive you want, Betty's got that, properly, I suppose."

"Beatrice!"
"—sama!"

Opening her eyes, marked with their distinctive pattern, within the arms that held her — Beatrice. Emilia, eyes flying open, called her name, and Frederica hastily added the honorific.
Whether or not that covered for it, Beatrice's words had a certain effect in drawing the attention of Abel and the others who didn't know the circumstances.

"That you hold proof of Natsuki Subaru's survival — why is that?"

"It's a simple matter, I suppose. Betty and Subaru are connected through their Souls."

"—. Ah, is that how it is. If this child and that one are joined at such a deep level, then that reasoning, I can understand."

"Yes, the two of them are in a Contract. So…"

"That would explain the state of this girl, so utterly worn down. It adds up."

Without any clear explanation given, Abel and Yorna grasped the circumstances.
The revelation of Beatrice being a Spirit was inconvenient for Otto and the others, who'd entered the Empire under false pretenses, but at the least, it seemed the two in question had already caught on.
Though it seemed unlikely that Abel and the others, aiming as they were to defy the Emperor in the capital, would do anything to them over a violation of Imperial decree.

"Now that you understand, you'll see. Setting aside Natsuki-san's whereabouts and safety, we do have a means of grasping whether he's alive or dead. If Beatrice-sama says so, then it's certain that Natsuki-san is alive, somewhere… wherever he was sent."

"—. Is that so. Then Tanza, too, may rest a little easier."

At Otto's supplementary words, Yorna lowered her brows faintly and looked down.
The word spoken in a whisper-like tone was likely a person's name. From how she said it, someone who had lost their life. —And that, too, seemed not unrelated to the witch-city's destruction.
Delving deeper would require the resolve to press further into Yorna's affairs.

"Um, can I bring the topic back for a second?"

"Petra-chan"
"—sama!"

Not letting the momentary silence slip by, Petra raised her hand to request the floor. Emilia had again carelessly dropped the honorific, prompting Frederica's immediate reinforcement.
Glancing at this out of the corner of her eye, Petra, gathering the attention of the unfamiliar adults, spoke with composure,

"Earlier, Medium-chan said she got made small. Is that really true?"

"Ah, yeah, it's true! I used to be super tall, y'know! About the same as that blond boy over there and… no, that lady!"

"Whyyy am I the one gettin' left out here!"

"You understand, don't you. Though, I myself am not particularly pleased to be spoken of in terms of height, either."

Pointed at in turn by Medium, Garfiel and Frederica each reacted in their own way.
Still, that Medium's story wasn't a lie was evident from the fact that no one contradicted it. Hard to believe as it was, it seemed some strange phenomenon capable of shrinking a person's body had indeed occurred.
And moreover—

"Did Subaru get made small too?"

Petra asked seriously, her voice slightly stiff.
That, in fact, was a major issue Otto and the others couldn't afford to let pass either. It was hard enough that Subaru, flung off into the Empire, was difficult to rejoin — they hadn't accounted for the possibility of his body being shrunk on top of it.
Otto, praying it was a mishearing, nonetheless felt certain — as he had every reason to by now — that his prayers would go unanswered, now as ever.
Unaware of Otto's feelings, Medium nodded lightly. "Yup, that's right~."

"Subaru-chin's the same as me! Maybe even a little smaller? Anyway, he got made small! But I heard he helped put that scary old geezer in his place along with Yorna-chan, so Subaru-chin's amazing!"

"Yes, Subaru is amazing!"
"Yeah, just like Medium-chan says."
"Naturally, I suppose. That's Betty's partner."

The content itself was hard to welcome, but Medium's unhesitating praise was pleasant to the ear. In fact, hearing Subaru praised put Emilia, Petra, and Beatrice all in good spirits.
Garfiel and Frederica, and Otto too, didn't feel bad about it either. They didn't, but the fact that this praise came attached with information hard to welcome was what concerned Otto more.

"Natsuki-san shrunk too… inferring from this, does that mean he's become a child? Not content with cross-dressing, now it's turning into a child… Infantilization? What a restless man…"

"I suppose it would be unkind to blame Subaru-sama, given the other party involved… but still, I am worried. There's simply no way I could introduce him to Clind like this."

"That in itself would bring other worries, I imagine…"

By what means had such a phenomenon been brought about?
Given how utterly divorced from reality the whole thing was, they would need to worry both about whether he could be restored, and whether there might be lingering aftereffects if he was. In the worst case, Subaru might remain small permanently and return in triumph to the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica as Emilia's knight, still tiny.

"Well, in terms of ability it wouldn't change much, so let's set that aside."

Subaru would surely be indignant to hear it, but if it meant securing his person, it could be counted as damage within an acceptable range to swallow.
So long as he could properly grow from there, it likely wouldn't pose much of a problem for his relationship with the half-elf Emilia, either. She could presumably wait for him to grow up.

"…This won't do. Perhaps I'm not entirely calm myself, either."

The order of priority among the things to worry over felt out of order.
For now, failing to reunite with Subaru was a painful blow, but thanks to Beatrice, at least they had certainty on the matter of his survival.
What remained was to decide whether to continue on a cooperative course with Zikr and the others — no, if Abel was truly their representative, with them — in the search for him.

"—Beatrice-sama? What's with that dumbfounded face?"

"—?"

Hand at his chin, just as he was about to weigh his options, Otto found his attention drawn, almost idly, by Garfiel's words.
Garfiel's gaze was fixed on Beatrice in Emilia's arms. Though she'd awoken, wanting to avoid exhaustion she remained held up in Emilia's arms — but her brief good mood at Subaru's praise moments ago had already given way to wide, round eyes.

What her wavering gaze was fixed on wasn't the Medium in question.
It was another girl beside her, nestled close as if the two were sisters. A girl whose name they hadn't even heard yet, and at whom Beatrice stared, transfixed.

"…Why in the world are you here, I wonder."

"U?"

"Answer me! No — don't you dare do anything unnecessary, I suppose!"

Right after questioning her in a trembling voice, Beatrice, her face draining of color, raised her voice sharply.
At that sound, the girl tilted her head, then jumped in shock, and naturally, Otto and the others too were startled by Beatrice's reaction.

"B-Beatrice… -sama, what's wrong? Suddenly shouting at that girl…"

"Of course I'm shouting! Otto! Did you not notice, I wonder!?"

"Me? Notice what… ah."

Being singled out for rebuke, something in the back of Otto's confused mind asserted itself.
It was something that had been nagging at him from the very first moment he'd laid eyes on that girl, never quite taking clear shape — but with Beatrice's reaction, it began, gradually, to come into focus.
Yes, Otto did recognize that girl. And that was a sense of déjà vu that shouldn't exist here, in the Empire, on what was practically his first visit here—

"Don't tell me, she's—"

A startling possibility rose in his mind, and Otto too stared at the girl, transfixed.
The possibility that had surfaced was something tucked away in the hazy, uncertain corner of Otto's memory. At that time, his leg had been gouged open, his consciousness hazy from the pain and blood loss.
So he hadn't gotten a clear look at the one responsible for it, but—

"Beatrice, Otto-kun, what is it? Both of you, staring at that girl…"

Bewildered by Otto and the others' reaction, no one else could fill in for Emilia, who'd once again dropped Beatrice's honorific. And there was no longer any need to.
Because there was no longer any need to leave the confusion as mere confusion.

"—It's 'Gluttony,' I suppose."

"Eh…"

Quietly, in a hard voice, Beatrice spoke, and Emilia's eyes went wide at it.
But it wasn't only Emilia who froze — everyone whose ears the voice reached did as well, and in that instant, Beatrice's voice drew the attention of everyone present.
And so, everyone came to hear of that girl's true identity.

"That girl is the Sin Archbishop of 'Gluttony'… the girl who called herself Rui Arneb, I suppose."


△▼△▼△▼△


—The moment Beatrice's words were spoken, an atmosphere of imminent violence gripped the room.

"Ya gotta be kiddin' me. Sin Archbishop, what the hell're ya thinkin'."

As Beatrice's words rang out, astonishment, dread, and confusion naturally spread through the room.
It was something a young girl had said. No sane adult with proper judgment would simply accept it at face value. But Otto and the others were not such people.
Otto, who'd had a vague sense of recognition toward the girl — Rui — of course, and the comrades of the Camp who trusted that Beatrice wouldn't lie, immediately took it as fact.

Hence, Garfiel cracked his knuckles, bared his fangs, and stepped forward.
Right there, narrowing the pupils of his emerald eyes, he glared at Rui.

"An' on top've that, of all things, 'Gluttony'? That's one of the bastards monopolizin' the top spot on the list of guys we wanna beat down. Is this how they do things in the Empire!?"

"W-wait! It's not like that! Rui-chan's not a bad kid!"

Blocking Garfiel's line of sight, Medium spread her arms wide as if to shield Rui.
Her resolve to protect the girl behind her was admirable, but it seemed unlikely she could possibly hold back Garfiel's anger. To begin with—

"You say she's not a bad kid, but are you serious? For reference, I had both my legs viciously gouged open by her. The scars remain quite vividly too — shall I show you?"

"That's… th-that might've been a different kid, not Rui-chan…"

"Unfortunately, she introduced herself quite clearly by name as she did it. And the victims aren't limited to me either… in fact, there are countless others. Among our own people, and among our allies' people too."

"B-but, but but…!"

Eyes welling up, Medium desperately searched for words to counter.
But if she truly had nothing more than a child's wisdom to match her appearance — or even if only her outward form had shrunk — Otto intended to shut this argument down.
In the first place, there shouldn't be anyone in this world capable of defending a Sin Archbishop.

A Sin Archbishop, a follower of the Witch's Cult — that was how deeply loathed such a thing was.
Which was precisely why Emilia, whose appearance was the very image of the "Witch of Envy" who'd set it all in motion, could never escape mockery for chasing an impossible dream.

"Maybe that was true of the old Rui-chan… but the Rui-chan now is different!"

"Different from before? What grounds've ya got for sayin' that?"

"Because! She was friends with Anchan, and Subaru-chin, and Rem-chan too! She was with me the whole time and… she never did anything bad, not once! She didn't!"

"—With Natsuki-san?"

Crying out as she desperately searched for words, Medium's frantic, last-ditch struggle nonetheless dropped into their ears something far too large to simply let pass.
That Subaru had been acting together with Rui. —Surely it wasn't that he hadn't known who Rui was. Subaru must have properly sensed Rui's true nature.

"Otto-san, don't tell me Subaru's lost his memories too?"

"For a moment, that worry crossed my mind, but from what I've heard from Mizelda-san and Zikr-san, I think we can say that concern is, just barely, unfounded."

At Petra's anxious words, Otto ruled out the worry from the available evidence.
If the Sin Archbishop of "Gluttony" was here, and she'd been acting alongside Subaru, then indeed the frightening possibility was that Subaru had had his "memories" stolen.
Given that he remembered Subaru's name, there was probably no fear of his "name" having been eaten, but "memories" were a matter of the person's own subjective awareness.
However—

"—The talk of Subaru we've heard in this city is of the Subaru we know. So there's no need to worry about that, Petra-chan. …Petra-sama."

"Emilia, thank you."

At Emilia's words, having reached the same conclusion, Petra finally showed an expression of relief.
But that was only grounds for Subaru's "memories" not having been eaten — it was hardly evidence to decide what should be done about Rui, present here.
Of course, there remained the question of why Subaru hadn't done anything about Rui—

"Knowing Natsuki-san, it's probably something like being unable to bring himself to harm someone with such a young appearance. —Could there be concern over the conditions for releasing her Authority?"

"He mentioned that before. That even if we defeated the Sin Archbishop of 'Gluttony,' there's no certainty that everything lost would be restored."

"Troublesome, but that would explain why he didn't act rashly."

Just as Garfiel had said, defeating the Sin Archbishop of "Gluttony" was an unshakeable, cherished wish not only for Otto and the others, and the Royal Selection candidates, but for the world itself.
However, that wasn't simply out of a desire for revenge — the true objective was the recovery of what "Gluttony" had taken.

If taking Rui's life would restore the victims of her Authority, there would be no issue.
But what if it didn't restore them? Take a hasty action, and the means of restoring the victims might be lost forever — perhaps that was what Subaru had been wary of.

"—I get where Otto-nii's comin' from. But that ain't the same as sayin' we gotta let that runt roam free."

Understanding Otto's reasoning, Garfiel nonetheless spoke with haughty conviction.
And indeed, that was true. As frightening as it was that taking a life might erase a possibility, it was hard to imagine that restraining her would cause any problem.
If anything, the danger of leaving a Sin Archbishop unrestrained was surely far greater.
Hence, at times like this, Garfiel's tendency toward quick decisions was a reassuring thing.

Step by step, Garfiel closed the distance to restrain the dangerous Sin Archbishop. At Garfiel's approach, Rui shrank her small body, letting out a "uu."

"Abel-chin! Say something!"

Shielding Rui behind her, Medium, facing off against Garfiel, called out to Abel.
Abel, who'd been holding his tongue through the proceedings, turned his gaze to Medium's appeal, and said,

"What would you have me say. The handling of that thing is your domain, is it not."

"But I can't convince him well!"

"Do not foist the blame for your own lack of virtue onto me. Besides, it's unnecessary."

At being called "unnecessary," Medium's eyes wavered.
Abel's cold way of putting it might have made her think he'd abandoned Rui. But that wasn't the truth of it.
What Abel had said was that there was no need for him personally to lift a hand.

"Oh yeah, I don't go easy just 'cause my opponent's a woman."

"That is most reassuring. Were it otherwise, I should have to endure the excuse that you held back on account of my beauty."

The Garfiel who had halted his advance found his path blocked by Yorna, who stood there languidly.
With her already considerable height further increased by thick-soled footwear, pipe in hand, Yorna looked down on Garfiel. Meeting her gaze head-on, Garfiel bared his fangs ferociously in turn.

That Yorna would shield Rui was, in itself, a statement of intent.
Given that she'd come to Guaral from the witch-city together with Rui in the first place, it had already been apparent that she had no intention of eliminating the girl — but that in itself was hard to believe.

"I'll say it again — she is a Sin Archbishop. Surely you don't mean to say the horror of the Witch's Cult is unknown here in the Vollachia Empire?"

"The deeds of those miscreants, and the ghastliness of their nature — well known. And naturally, the malice of those wretches called Sin Archbishops as well."

"I hear the fortress city of Garkla still has not fully healed its wounds, even now."

The great Imperial city said to have fallen to the rampage of the Witch's Cult's Sin Archbishop of "Greed."
The perpetrator, "Greed," had been struck down in Priestella, but that the Witch's Cult chose no particular place to run rampant was common knowledge the world over. Naturally, in the Empire as well.
And despite that, to let Rui roam free was—

"You think she can be tamed? That's nothing short of rash thinking."

"If she proves useful, one considers a use for her. You lot, for your part, are sorely mistaken if you think everything will simply side with you."

"—————"

Slipping past Otto's pressing question, Abel gave a light jerk of his chin.
As he said, the reactions of those gathered on the top floor of the city hall varied considerably. There were, of course, those who felt hostility and revulsion at hearing Rui was a Sin Archbishop. But there were also more than a few, like Medium and Yorna, who showed reluctance at the idea of eliminating her.
Especially—

"—I'll say this much: Subaru and Rem, the two of them, are our comrades. And since it was those two who kept Rui with them, it's those two who have the right to decide Rui's fate."

"Mizelda-sama…"

"Naturally, if the chief has a different opinion, I'll follow it."

Piercing them with her sharp, forceful gaze, Mizelda too moved to stand on the side of protecting Rui. At that intense look, Frederica, who'd had many occasions to exchange words with her at city hall, caught her breath.
Mizelda was the representative figure of the Shudrak, and the other Shudrak followed her lead. —No, more precisely, the one they followed was the true chief who had returned to the city.

Addressed by Mizelda's words and gaze, the decision was left to the woman in black ceremonial dress. Taritta, the Shudrak's new chief, asked by her elder sister, "What'll it be," said,

"My opinion's the same as my sister's. But it's not that I decided that way just 'cause my sister said so — it's my own thinkin', to protect Rui. I owe Rui a debt."

"Heh, is that so."

At Taritta's clear answer, Mizelda relaxed her lips and nodded.
Whatever the relationship between these sisters truly was, Mizelda seemed, somehow, to receive Taritta's assertion with a certain gladness.
Though Otto and the others had no room to spare for finding that heartwarming.

"A Sin Archbishop, and 'Gluttony' at that — that's not something to overlook, I suppose. You lot better cool your heads too, I suppose."

In Emilia's arms, Beatrice's words carried force.
She'd come this far holding back her exhaustion, but in a moment of urgent crisis, there would be need to act with her own power too. And once it came to that, not hesitating was a bad habit Beatrice shared closely with Subaru.

"No, everyone calm down! Fighting like this won't—"

Amid the rising fighting spirit of Beatrice and Garfiel, poised for battle, and Yorna and the Shudrak, Emilia, her face pained, tried to stop the conflict.
In truth, if one were to genuinely stop this conflict by force, Emilia was the one best suited to the task.
If she froze all of Guaral solid with her overwhelming mana, Otto could aim to secure Rui in the meantime. Worst case, even if it meant a total break with the people of Guaral.

"Better the risk of that than letting a Sin Archbishop go free—"

So Otto was thinking, watching for an opening, when—

"—What now, do you lot still hunger for more fighting. How bloodthirsty."

"—————"

Suddenly, a new voice, full of power, shook the air of the great hall in city hall.
It belonged to a woman climbing the stairs slowly, her high heels clicking. Otto had earlier called Emilia, Abel, and Yorna presences one couldn't ignore, but the owner of this voice, too, carried an air that matched that impression perfectly.

Swaying the hem of a dress red as blood, a beautiful woman appeared, unhurried.
White skin, orange hair, adorned boldly with a profusion of ornaments — a beauty so innate that even those jewels seemed outshone by her splendor — Priscilla Barielle.

The last one, of those in this fortress city of Guaral who held any real sway, to make her appearance.
Behind her as she walked slowly came the page Schult, whose dainty, quick movements caught the eye, and Al, with his unforgettable, outlandish appearance.
Al was also one of those who had gone with Abel, Medium, and the others to Chaosflame with Subaru. It seemed he'd gone to see Priscilla the moment he returned, and had come up together with her now.
Heinkel, who ought to have been present as well, was nowhere in sight, but that was a minor matter.

More important to Otto was that a presence had just entered that seemed likely to make this already volatile atmosphere even worse.
Capricious as she was, and a formidable power in her own right, Priscilla was someone whose allegiance was utterly impossible to predict.
It would almost be a relief if she could simply be declared an enemy outright.

"I hear Abel and the others have returned, and come to see for myself, only to find this atmosphere already brewing — how impressive. Even with the city ravaged as it is, it seems even starving dogs can't choose where they bark."

"Priscilla! Everyone's super on edge right now! Don't say anything weird."

"Fool. Had I not come, you would likely have made it snow again. I'll have you know, no matter how cold it gets, I do not intend to stop baring my shoulder. But cold is cold, all the same."

"Sorry about that, but…"

Emilia apologized to the entering Priscilla in a rather nonsensical exchange.
But it wasn't out of contrition that Emilia's words trailed off and lost their force.

It was because a sharp, hard sound of something dropping rang through the great hall.

"—Yorna-chan?"

Turning her head at the sound, it was Medium who murmured, looking up at the woman standing right in front of her. At Yorna's feet, under Medium's blue-eyed gaze, lay a single fallen pipe.
The very piece — clearly no cheap trinket — that Yorna had been toying with in her hand.

"Uu"

Groaning so, Rui, the very center of the conversation, picked up the fallen pipe. But even offered the retrieved pipe, Yorna did not take it.
More than that, she wasn't even looking at Rui or Medium. Yorna's gaze was fixed on one single point — the presence that had just now stepped into the great hall, Priscilla.

Staring fixedly at Priscilla, Yorna's eyes were wide open.
Composed, unshakable, a woman who gave the impression that nothing could ever ruffle her — utterly stunned.
Her rouge-painted lips parted, hesitant, and—

"P-Prisca…?"

She called out to Priscilla by that name. —An unfamiliar, different name.
Similar, yet different, and Yorna, her eyes wide with astonished disbelief, called Priscilla by it. Three people in the room reacted to that name.

One was Zikr, another was Abel, and the last, needless to say, was Priscilla herself.
Zikr looked thoughtful, Abel looked pensive, and Priscilla looked displeased as they regarded Yorna.

"What now, you — why do you call me by such—no."

Not hiding her displeasure, Priscilla returned Yorna's gaze with crimson eyes.
It seemed her sharp tongue might come crashing down upon Yorna then and there, but she cut herself off at the last moment. Priscilla, too, stared fixedly at Yorna.

Yorna's blue eyes and Priscilla's blood-colored eyes met, and a moment of silence passed.
Then Priscilla let out an "Ah," like a sigh, and said,

"—And here I wondered who it was. If it isn't Mother."

She said it flatly, a remark that would set off an explosion quite different from the imminent violence that had filled the room a moment before.
