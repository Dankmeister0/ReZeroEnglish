Arc 5 Chapter 4: "On the Road"

In the end, it took three days before Subaru and the others actually set out for Priestella.

"For my part, I respect Lady Emilia's wishes, and there's no pressing duty to attend to at the moment, so it's fi-i-ine by me. Though I'll admit, not being able to see what they're plotting on their end is a bit worrying, if you want to call it that-t."

The Western Frontier Lords' Council—in short, a gathering of Margrave Roswaal and the lords under his banner—was to be held at the mansion of one of those prominent nobles who had declared himself neutral among the leading houses.

For the most part, the lords had come to align themselves with Roswaal's policies, but even so, there were still those who voiced opposition or unease at the idea of rallying behind Emilia as their figurehead.

In most of the territories, Roswaal's policy of treating demi-humans and humans equally—rather than simply favoring demi-humans—was upheld, but the view that "demi-human" didn't necessarily mean "half-elf," that goodwill toward demi-humans in general didn't automatically extend to her, remained stubbornly entrenched no matter where one went.

Over the past year, the achievement had been to nip such seeds of doubt in the bud through dialogue and conditional, outward compliance. This meeting was also a stepping stone toward arranging another opportunity for the lords who still stubbornly refused to nod along, to sit down and talk with Emilia directly.

The reason Roswaal had been away from the mansion was to lay the groundwork for exactly that.

"I'm sorry. Honestly, I wanted to attend that meeting myself, but..."

"Tha-a-at would backfire. This meeting exists solely to create that opportunity—bringing Lady Emilia out here would just turn it into an ambush... Of cou-ourse, if Lady Emilia could calm the ensuing chaos and deliver a speech masterful enough to silence the opposing lords, then perhaps such a surprise attack would be worth considering-g."

"...I don't think I could manage that yet. All right. I'll behave myself."

Roswaal nodded, satisfied, as Emilia pressed her lips together and lowered her eyes in frustration.

There was a part of him that wanted to say something about that sarcastic tone, but he also couldn't deny that Roswaal was facing Emilia more directly than before.

*It's so much better now than a year ago, when I was so obviously kept away from political matters and just felt like a decoration*—that was the honest feeling Emilia had confided to Subaru when he'd grumbled about it.

Roswaal, for his part, was moving more energetically than before too, making him a far more dependable backer than he used to be. Though given how dangerous his true intentions could be, in the end it probably balanced out to about even.

"So Petra's confirmed to accompany you to the meeting... who's staying behind at the mansion?"

"Anneroze's side will be joining this time too, you know-w. With Clind, who dotes on Petra, along, most things should be fi-i-ine. Frederica, on the other hand, doesn't get along with Clind, so I was thinking of leaving her behind... What about you, Ram?"

"Ram will go wherever suits Roswaal-sama's wishes."

"Saying 'wherever suits your wishes' while shoving your own opinion through anyway..."

Ram remained a devout Roswaal supremacist through and through, but she'd grown less hesitant about pushing her own selfishness forward these days. Watching how naturally Roswaal accepted that from her, it seemed different from before, when the dependence had run only one way, from Ram's side.

The syrupy air of dependency had faded, replaced by something more like mutual understanding between the two of them. Whether having someone like that in his life was a good thing for Roswaal, though, was hard to say.

"What are you staring at. If you're going to get worked up over just anyone, I'll gouge your eyes out, Barusu."

"Just how much of an indiscriminate horndog do you think I am, sis?"

"............"

At Subaru's question, Ram's expression turned thoroughly complicated.

It wasn't that he'd asked a hard question to answer. It was just that Ram always made this face whenever she heard Subaru call her "sis."

It wasn't that she disliked it—the word simply didn't feel real to her. For someone whose memories of being sisters with Rem had yet to return, the days when Rem had looked up to her as an older sister existed only somewhere beyond a blank void.

"Honestly, if it were just Lady Emilia and Subaru-kun, I'd still have some worries, but with Garfiel and Otto-kun going along, it should be fi-i-ine. Otto-kun's not the type to bungle a negotiation, and even in the worst case, if Garfiel smashes everything to pieces and they just run for it, that'll do too."

"Isn't that its own kind of huge problem...? I'll do my best to make sure it doesn't come to that, though."

"Stirring things up is my job, Emilia-tan. Doesn't matter if it's Anastasia-san or Julius, bring it on. When it comes to derailing an important negotiation, I've got the seal of approval from a certain talkative Witch."

"Is that really something to brag about...?"

Subaru gave Emilia, who smiled wryly, a thumbs-up with his teeth gleaming. Of course, Emilia understood that Subaru's glib words were meant to put her at ease.

That much trust between them, at least, they'd surely built up plenty over the past year.

"We-e-ell then, Beatrice. I'm counting on you to look after these four proper-r-rly, all right?"

"There's no need to even say it, I suppose. Without Betty around, every last one of them is such a troublesome bunch that nothing could be entrusted to them with any peace of mind, I suppose."

The girl entrusted with watching over them all puffed out her chest, her ringlets swaying.

Her attitude, unable to shake off a certain endearing quality no matter what, brought smiles to the lips of everyone present. Though the girl who'd caused those smiles seemed less than pleased with the reaction.

—In any case, with everyone informed, the trip to Priestella was settled.

"Well then, once you arrive, please ask for the 'Water Feather Inn.' We'll be waiting there along with Lady Anastasia."

"Well then, we'll be waiting for you-u!"

So said the two envoys from Anastasia as they left Roswaal Manor.

They were to return to Priestella ahead of the others, bringing back a reply to this invitation.

"Yo, take care now, ya hear."

"Garf, you too! We'll be waiting in Puri-Puri, so make sure you really, definitely come, okay-o!"

"The heck's 'Puri-Puri' supposed to be. I got it, I got it. Yer all a buncha worrywarts who can't do nothin' without me around. I'll settle things on my end. Wash yer neck and wait for it."

"Oh? Okay, I'll wash it and wait-o!"

At the parting, the heartwarming way Garfiel and Mimi bantered stood out. It seemed Garfiel had genuinely kept a close watch on the two of them during the envoys' stay, but judging by Mimi's attitude, it was hard to deny that his vigilance had been needless worry after all.

If anything, Mimi had grown rather friendly with him. She'd started calling him "Garf" at some point without anyone noticing, and it seemed there'd never been any hostile intent on her side to begin with.

"Mimi may have been won over, but I... I won't be going easy on anyone."

And so Joshua tried to strike a serious tone, dragging along his overexcited bodyguard.

Honestly, the sight of him keeping a straight, serious face while tilting sideways, one arm caught in Mimi's flailing grip, was more than a little comical.

Though even Subaru had, over the course of a year, developed enough kindness not to say so to his face.

"Joshua-kun, are you all right? Your clothes are so nice, and your sleeve looks like it's about to come off..."

"I—I'm fine. Please don't worry about it!"

But while Subaru understood the boy's pride, the angel standing beside him to see them off couldn't grasp it in the same way. Faced with Emilia's zero-sarcasm concern, Joshua couldn't very well raise his voice at her either, so he tried to peel Mimi off with a pitiful expression instead. As you might expect, though, Mimi's grip strength outmatched his, and it came to nothing.

"My brother calls you a friend, but from where I stand, he's far too kind. That may be one of his virtues, but I believe it's my role as his younger brother to make up for it—so please don't expect any mercy from House Juukulius."

"Wait, already dropped the act of swapping 'I' for 'myself'?"

"P-please listen properly when I'm talking! Don't make fun of me...! You're a horrible person!"

"I'm more worried about you forgetting you're supposed to be acting as an envoy right now. If you went off on someone like this in an official setting, wouldn't that be a mark against House Juukulius?"

"——!"

Joshua's face went pale, but Subaru had only been needling him for fun—he hadn't meant it seriously at all. If it came down to calling out verbal missteps in official settings, Subaru himself had thrown far more dangerous pitches than that.

Of course, Joshua had no way of knowing that, and the fact that Subaru wasn't about to enlighten him was exactly what made Natsuki Subaru Natsuki Subaru.

"Subaru, don't tease kids younger than you. Joshua-kun, I'm sorry. Subaru's just... kind of like this, sometimes."

"—! N-no... it's I who was terribly rude. Please accept my apology."

"Me? Ow, ow, that hurts, Emilia-tan!"

Emilia's fingers seized Subaru's ear without mercy just as he was about to needle Joshua again. Seeing Subaru reduced to teary eyes seemed to settle something in Joshua, and he apparently decided to let the matter rest there for now.

Taking a deep breath, Joshua climbed back into the dragon carriage they'd arrived in—though since it was a liger, not a dragon, pulling the carriage, it probably ought to be called a "dog carriage" rather than a dragon carriage.

Made him wonder if maybe there was something like dogsled racing here too.

"Might be worth trying to popularize something like horse racing, as a spectator sport."

Frankly, his modern-knowledge cheat mode had been stuck at the conceptual stage so far, but maybe it was worth taking a little more seriously trying to bring to life the kinds of things his knowledge could actually reproduce.

Though first, he'd need to seriously weigh the pros and cons of introducing horse racing in the first place, of course—

"What's wrong, Subaru? You've got such a scheming look on your face."

"Just had a modern-knowledge cheat idea bubble up after a while. Full-on Subaru-ism eruption, you could say."

"Oh, another new seasoning? I like mayonnaise, but I also love the tartar sauce you made after that."

"This time I'm thinking of going a bit further outside the common-folk sensibility with my cheating."

For what it's worth, the tartar sauce, like the mayonnaise, had ended up being stockpiled at Roswaal Manor. It was generally well-received by pretty much everyone, which left Subaru a little dissatisfied, in his own way.

In any case, even as this idle chatter went on, preparations for the return trip proceeded, and the dog carriage slowly began to move.

The one steering wasn't on a driver's seat but Mimi, riding directly astride one of the two ligers pulling the carriage. She flipped the hem of her white robe and laughed cheerfully as she gripped its mane.

"Well then, see ya later, mister, miss, and Garf too-o!"

They saw Mimi off cheerfully, and waved to Joshua too—still at maximum wariness—through the carriage window.

And so, having sent the envoys off, it was two days later that Subaru and the others set out to follow them.

* * *

"Even pushing the dragon carriage at full speed, it's a journey of over ten days. There's no particular reason to rush, so let's take plenty of time and go at a pace that won't wear us out."

Everyone deferred without complaint to Otto, who'd settled the itinerary with those words.

He was, after all, the most travel-seasoned of the group heading to Priestella, and if you averaged out physical endurance among them, the result would probably land somewhere close to Otto as well. Choosing the pace and route that he himself judged wouldn't be a strain would surely make for the safest journey for everyone.

"To ease the load, I'll have my Frufoo and Patrasche-chan pull the carriage together. I've arranged the schedule so we won't need to camp out, so the bare minimum of emergency gear should do."

"If I'm stuck bein' jostled around on this thing the whole way, my body's gonna go soft on me, Otto-nii."

"In that case, feel free to hop off and run alongside every now and then, Garfiel."

"A'ight, I'll do that then."

"You're actually going to do that?"

With little scenes like Emilia's surprise at Otto and Garfiel's usual back-and-forth thrown in here and there, the curtain rose on the journey to Priestella.

That said, one way or another, the journey itself passed smoothly enough.

There were minor bits of trouble here and there—leaving the Mathers frontier territory, entering other domains, passing through mountain checkpoints—but the Mathers family crest and Emilia's presence mostly smoothed things over without incident. By now, word of the Royal Selection and its candidates had spread to nearly every corner of the land.

Of course, that recognition could just as easily invite new sparks of trouble, but on this particular journey, they didn't cross paths with anyone scheming anything untoward.

On the occasions when wild dogs or witchbeasts recklessly targeted the carriage along the way,

"Perfect timin'. Was just thinkin' I wanted to punch somethin' livin' down for once, first time in a while."

Garfiel would shatter the pack apart with overwhelming violence, driving off their disciplined wild instincts through sheer terror. As a bodyguard, his work left nothing to be desired—if anything, it was the stray witchbeasts who ended up seeming pitiable.

Yet for all that fearsome side of him, Garfiel would spend his idle moments gnawing on a knife he'd brought along to pass the time, and there was something of a gap between that and his otherwise boyish demeanor.

Incidentally, the sight of sturdy steel being crunched apart between his teeth was quite the spectacle, no matter how many times you saw it.

"Mm. This earth dragon's got some real promise, I suppose. Betty supposes she'll give it her approval."

So said Beatrice, seated beside Subaru as he held the reins.

It might come as a surprise, but holding the reins along the way wasn't Otto's role alone. Thanks to a year's worth of effort, Subaru had become capable of handling a dragon carriage himself—provided it was an earth dragon he knew well enough to have a feel for its quirks, at least.

That said, that only really applied to Patrasche, whom he knew well, and Otto's earth dragon Frufoo. Aside from those two, there were only two more he could manage—Rascal and Peter, kept at Roswaal Manor. Needless to say who'd been responsible for naming them.

"Instead of just talking big all the time, wanna try holding the reins yourself for a bit? Patrasche's overflowing with motherly love, so I bet she'd be gentle with you too, Bea-ko."

"Betty will pass on that, I suppose. Besides, that earth dragon's eyes are unmistakably hostile toward Betty, I suppose. That's not the look you give someone on your side. Motherly love, my foot, I suppose."

"Hey, hey, I won't forgive you for badmouthing Patrasche. I don't let anyone talk trash about Emilia-tan, Rem, Bea-ko, or Patrasche—no exceptions."

"Betty's included in that list as a matter of course, and yet she's not allowed to say a word, I suppose?"

"If you're one of the people on that list, then whoever says it is the one in the wrong."

Beatrice tried to flee, but there was no escaping on a cramped driver's bench. He reached out, caught her by the neck, and pulled her in; she ended up plopped onto his lap, thrashing all the while. He was about to sentence her to a bout of tickling right then and there, but Beatrice's bouncing hair brushed past his nose, and Subaru let out a tremendous sneeze—the carriage lurched.

"Hey, Natsuki-san! Don't make it jerk around like that! If we accidentally slip outside the Divine Protection of Wind Evasion, we're all going to end up horribly carriage-sick together!"

"My bad, my bad! Bea-ko was just getting a little too excited, so I—"

"Stop blaming it on Betty, I suppose! Subaru's the one who just went and... started tickling her, I suppose! Stop— pfft, hee hee!"

From inside the carriage, Otto let out an enormous sigh at the two of them fooling around on the driver's bench. Seeing that, Emilia laughed softly,

"Those two really are the best of friends, aren't they. It's hard to believe Subaru and Beatrice would end up so close like that—not long ago, I couldn't have even imagined it."

"If anything, I find it harder to believe there was ever a time those two weren't together. Between how much Beatrice-chan clings and how much Natsuki-san spoils her, it's enough to give me indigestion."

"That might be true. But I think it's good this way. Everyone's always thought that smile suits Beatrice so well, that laughing face of hers."

Even Emilia's wistful smile, to Otto's eyes, seemed to carry something of an elder sister's or a mother's tenderness. Though Otto wasn't so tactless as to point that out aloud, nor did he have any intention of letting it go to Subaru's head.

"Well, let's leave those two to their fun over there and get to our own important business. As I've said a number of times, this is about what the Anastasia Camp is aiming for this time, and how we should respond to it."

"So it's not just about creating some simple favor to be owed, is what you mean?"

"With the Royal Selection set to be decided in three years, a full year has already passed, so everyone should be closing in on the final stretch of laying their groundwork. For us, once this current move is in place, we'll consolidate support all at once at the next Western Frontier Lords' Council. Setting aside the camps that were already unified from the start, the other camps probably aren't that far off from us in terms of progress."

"What about Anastasia's camp itself?"

For a while now, Emilia had been kept at a distance from the details of the other camps' movements.

That was because, rather than feeding her extraneous information that would only stoke her anxiety, it had taken time just to teach her, from the ground up, the mindset needed as a ruler, and to have her master the necessary skills first.

Turning her attention outward could come after that—that had been the shared view of the Roswaal-and-Otto domestic-affairs duo, and they'd likewise agreed that this trip might be a good opportunity to ease that restriction a little.

So Otto gave a small nod at Emilia's question and began organizing the information in his head.

"First, let me talk about the current approval ratings among the Royal Selection candidates. At the start, this Royal Selection was largely understood in terms of two camps—Duchess Crusch Karsten and Anastasia Hoshin—as the frontrunner and her rival. The other three, Lady Emilia included... if I may speak bluntly, were regarded as little more than numbers to round out the field."

"...Yeah, I don't think I can deny that. But the way you're phrasing it means—"

"Yes. At the very least, it's a fact that public perception has shifted over the past year. The cause is that candidates beyond the two presumed frontrunners—starting with you, Lady Emilia—have begun racking up achievements."

The Emilia Camp's most conspicuous achievements were, without question, the Subjugation of the White Whale and the defeat of Sloth. The Crusch Camp had taken the lead in the White Whale subjugation, but as for the scale of Sir Natsuki Subaru's contribution to that effort, Crusch herself had publicly declared it, of all people.

As for the subsequent defeat of Sloth, though they'd borrowed strength from two other camps, it had been led by Subaru. Both of these achievements together served to spread Emilia's name all at once—a name that had drawn no interest whatsoever from the common people before, neither for who she was nor that she existed at all.

At the same time, that also meant her lineage stood out in an unflattering way, and for better or worse, Emilia's name became widely known as the subject of gossip and rumor.

With that, attention gathered on the other candidates too, and the ones who benefited from it in turn were the equally unknown Felt and Priscilla.

Priscilla Barielle's achievements in particular were remarkable. Having inherited outright the domain of her late husband, Leip Barielle, she turned the unfavorable condition of its location along the border with the Vollachia Empire—with which the Kingdom had long traded skirmishes—to her own advantage, and won over the surrounding lords, unsettled by the volatile situation, all at once.

Using methods that seemed almost like magic, she calmed Vollachia's aggression and brought the lords to her side; beyond that, Priscilla threw herself into restoring lands worn down by repeated warfare, and in a short span of time had already shown clear signs of genuine improvement.

Combined with her temperament—she'd dance as much as she was flattered—and her beauty, Priscilla's support in the southern reaches of the Kingdom was said to be growing stronger by the day.

Felt, on the other hand, had been forced to start from the domain belonging to House Astrea of the knight Reinhard van Astrea—a position that, compared to the other candidates, was rather threadbare next to the renown of the Sword Saint's name.

Even the name "Sword Saint," which commanded overwhelming fame and support among knights and commoners alike, wasn't especially compelling as a qualification for choosing someone to look up to as king. Starting with the Astrea territory that served as her home base, the attitudes of the surrounding lords leaned less toward caution than outright distrust.

But the girl named Felt broke through this dire situation in an unexpected way.

Rather than relying from the outset on those in a position to withhold judgment on a candidate—that is, powerful nobles—she gathered people instead from those who'd fallen from grace for one reason or another, or from among the common folk.

Felt, it turned out, had an uncanny knack for spotting hidden ambition and talent that had never been given a fair chance. Setting aside the rumored tall tale of being "royal blood," she possessed what was, in a sense, the most important quality a leader could have: the ability to recognize others' abilities and entrust them with work.

And so, sparked by that small ember, House Astrea and its surroundings grew visibly livelier by the day, and even among the lords who'd been watching from the sidelines, gradual changes had begun to appear.

It was still a small flame, but undeniably a footprint being carved into history. There wasn't a single notable figure in the Kingdom who could afford to ignore that tide.

"So, that's the kind of movement we're seeing on various fronts. In terms of major achievements, our own camp is doing quite well, but as far as the sense of steadily solidifying a foothold goes, those other two are faring better. If only the Great Rabbit subjugation had been recognized too, I think we'd still have come out ahead."

"Even you're saying the same thing as Subaru now, Otto-kun. But if that's the case, is it all right for me to indulge a little pride, thinking we're roughly neck and neck with them right now?"

"For the moment, yes. Ah, though... there's been a change over at Duchess Karsten's place too. Though for us, it works out as a tailwind."

"A tailwind for us?"

"Yes. The candidate, Duchess Crusch Karsten—apparently, over this past year, she's lost her spark, almost like she's become a different person. She used to be strict but energetic, in both public and private matters, to the point that even those who'd supported the previous duke had to grudgingly acknowledge her, or so the story goes."

Both her policymaking and her governance were apparently of an entirely different quality than before.

The decisive judgment she'd once shown had gone quiet, replaced by decisions that stood out for a certain softness. She'd been carrying the great role of duchess despite being a woman, and the general rumor now was that she'd finally shown her true colors.

They'd apparently even had to drag the retired previous duke, who'd settled into the role of guardian, back out to help handle the grievances of the citizenry and the lords.

"When the Royal Selection began, thanks in part to the White Whale subjugation, people were saying the throne was practically hers for certain... but there's no telling what might trigger a fall like this. Please be sure to keep your own guard up as well, Lady Emilia."

"—I see."

At Otto's explanation, Emilia lowered her eyes, her violet eyes filling with concern.

She seemed unable to hold back sympathy even for a political rival, but Otto considered that a dangerous kind of fragility. They were, after all, bound to end up kicking each other down eventually. Overinvesting emotionally in that would only invite instability.

That much held true in business as much as in politics—this past year's experience had taught Otto as much.

"Please don't dwell on it too much. There will surely be talk like this again, going forward."

"Right. Thank you. I understand you're looking out for me, Otto-kun."

"That's a relief to hear, then. Let's finally get to the Anastasia Camp. That camp doesn't have a base to serve as ground footing within the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica, but in exchange, they have a backer nothing else can match: a great merchant house in Kararagi. Given that they'd apparently already been considering expansion, shops affiliated with the Hoshin Company have been opening one after another across Lugunica."

"So what happens then? Even if there are more shops, that doesn't mean more people are on their side... oh, but more people would know who they are, so their name recognition goes up?"

"That would be a secondary effect, sure, but there's a simpler aim at work. —Put simply, it means they get to use a lot more money. And precisely because it's that simple, this power called wealth works on absolutely anyone. There's no social creature out there who can take an economic beatdown and not feel it."

Winning merchants over to your side means increasing your allies in the world of commerce. And so long as an economy sustains a society, holding power within it is synonymous with holding both offensive and defensive strength against that society.

So long as they went on indiscriminately gathering allies, standing against an opponent who'd deliberately clothed themselves in the power of wealth would become an extremely difficult task.

"That's why, as things stand, I consider the Anastasia Camp the candidate we most need to be wary of. An invitation from that camp... one that already looks set to leave us owing them a favor from the very start—can you understand how much this makes me want to clutch my head?"

"...I finally understand, really deeply. I'm sorry for acting on my own like that."

"As long as you understand, that's fine for now. I'm sure, I'm sure that from here on, you won't act carelessly again... because I trust you've understood that much...!"

Otto shook his head, saying it was too much for him to accept, and let out a long breath as Emilia bowed her head.

Otto's explanation, broken down into easily digestible terms, sank in, and Emilia nodded again and again.

Indeed, the world of politics was, in all sorts of ways, difficult and complicated.

She'd long since known that a heartfelt "I'll do my best" or "Let's do our best together" wasn't going to be enough, but keeping track of every other camp's movements on top of that made her head spin.

She was glad to finally learn the parts that had been kept from her until now, but her anxiety was growing in return.

"There's no need to shoulder your worries all on your own."

Otto spoke up, perhaps having read Emilia's inner thoughts from the look on her face.

As Emilia lifted her head, Otto, fiddling with his own gray hair, said,

"You're the central figure, Lady Emilia, but that's not the same as saying you have to do everything yourself. It's just like this carriage."

"Just like this carriage?"

"Right now, it's Natsuki-san holding the reins. It's Beatrice-chan keeping watch to make sure he doesn't slack off. It's Garfiel keeping an eye on our surroundings from up on the carriage roof, and I'm the one who worked out the travel itinerary. And you, Lady Emilia, are the one calling out 'thank you for your hard work' to all of us, as we all trudge along, one way or another, toward Priestella together."

Understanding what Otto meant, Emilia's eyes went wide.

And at the same time, she found his roundabout way of putting it so much like a certain someone's that it struck her as funny.

"Otto-kun. That way of phrasing things sounds an awful lot like Subaru, somehow."

"Ugh!? Really? Oh no... don't tell me it's rubbed off on me the longer I've spent around him... n-no, please, stop with the scary imagination there!"

"Hey, Otto! What are you two having such a fun chat about? Emilia-tan's cute smile is my staple food, so don't go stealing it from me!"

The voice of the man in question cut in, and Otto's shoulders jumped despite himself. Seeing that, Emilia burst into a smile, and Otto laughed too, with a pitiful look on his face.

"Hey! What are you two getting all excited about!? Come on, that's not fair! Beatrice, hold the reins a second. I'm butting in."

"No! Betty can't, I suppose! Stop it, I suppose! It won't work with Betty... ah, it's going to tip over! It's definitely going to tip over, I suppose! Look, this thing's got a look in its eye like it wants to flip us, I suppose!"

Hearing overlapping shrieks from the direction of the driver's bench, Otto rose to his feet, resigned.

It was probably about time the ever-impatient knight reached his limit. He decided it would be best to obediently swap places here and take over placating the earth dragon himself.

"Otto-kun."

Emilia called out from behind to Otto as he prepared to move from the carriage cabin to the driver's bench. He turned at her voice, and found himself holding his breath despite himself.

Emilia's smile, full of trust, struck straight at his chest.

"I know I cause all sorts of trouble, but I'll do my best too. I'm counting on you."

"—Yes, please do. And I, for my part, will be looking forward to whatever scraps come my way from it."

"That answer sounded kind of like Subaru too, somehow."

With a wry smile, Otto stepped off toward the driver's bench.

Subaru and Emilia alike—this master and man were, the both of them, always trying to lead people astray. For Otto, afflicted as he was with the sickness of wanting to live up to whatever was expected of him, this master and follower were nothing short of lethal.

Passing through exchanges like these, twelve days after departing Roswaal Manor—

Emilia's party arrived safely at the water-gate city of Priestella.
