One Day I Part 2: "The First Mayonnaise Incident"

"The things you need to make mayonnaise! First and foremost, love!"

"Yes, leave it to me."
"You can skip that part."

Caught between his little sister's and his big sister's polar-opposite reactions, Subaru clenched his fists and argued his point with real conviction.

They'd relocated to the kitchen for the occasion, and Subaru had thrown a simple apron on over his butler uniform. Working alongside a Rem whose face was brimming with enthusiasm (her horn, of course, had retreated) and a Ram who made no effort to hide her bored gaze (currently washing dishes), it was finally time to attempt mayonnaise-making.

"I'm going full hazy-memory mode here, but I don't think there were that many ingredients."

"If it can be made just by mixing existing things together, the difficulty shouldn't be too high. If this required aging or fermenting, though, we'd never have caught up in time."

"It wasn't some complicated process like that, I'm pretty sure. Man, I wish I'd paid a bit more attention when Mom was making her homemade mayonnaise."

Thinking back on it now was, as they say, too little too late.

When Subaru's mother had gotten fired up with a cry of "Further advancement in mayonnaise-making!", he didn't recall her gathering anything particularly special in the way of ingredients. In the end, the homemade mayonnaise had been a failure, and his easily-bored mother had immediately given up on it and gone back to smooching store-bought mayo.

"Actually, in our house it was my dad who was the meticulous one. In the end he was the one who finished the homemade mayonnaise — though it never made it to the table because it was worse than the store stuff."

His memory was that his father had taken full responsibility for disposing of the finished product.

Thinking about it now, as a self-respecting mayo-lover he really should have actively tasted the difference between homemade and store-bought with his own tongue. But that too was an after-the-festival regret.

And yet, that after all this time Subaru would get another chance to take on homemade mayonnaise — perhaps you really can't fight blood.

"Well, enough reminiscing. Let's move on to ingredients besides love. Er, first off, of course, eggs."

Mayonnaise meant eggs. For Subaru, a man who loved egg dishes through and through, eggs were an inseparable staple ingredient. That said—

"Since you mentioned bird eggs would be good, Subaru-kun, I had those prepared. They're the same kind we normally use in cooking."

"That'll do. Going for something exotic is step one toward a terrible meal. Especially since there's bound to be some difference between this world and my hometown, so we've got to be extra careful there."

Given the differences in ecosystems, it wasn't as if the ingredients here were a perfect match for the ones back in his original world. Culinary matters were fairly similar, but it was an element he mustn't forget that they weren't identical. What was meant to be a small adventure could easily turn into an unexpected big one.

"After all, the logic of cooking is the logic of science, logically speaking. Adventuring is the last thing you want when you should be approaching this with the mindset of a scientific experiment."

"If it's what you wanted, Subaru-kun, I could have gathered even more expensive ingredients."

"You'll go bankrupt spending big money on something that might succeed or might fail, you know? By the way, what's a high-class egg like? Some kind of brand-name chicken thing?"

"Flying Dragon eggs, for instance, are an ultra-premium item that very rarely makes it onto the market."

"Using dragon eggs for mayonnaise, isn't that a bit much!? And wait, wasn't this country supposed to be some kind of Dragon-worshipping kingdom or something!?"

In a country meant to revere dragons, was there really a custom of eating dragon eggs?

At Subaru's doubt, Ram let out an exasperated sigh.

"You get confused like that because you're lumping 'lesser dragons' and 'Dragons' together. There's an uncrossable wall between the noble Dragon and the lesser dragon species. Understand?"

"When you just say it out loud the nuance doesn't really come across... So basically, lesser dragons and Dragons are treated differently?"

*Obviously*, her shrug seemed to say, and Subaru pouted with dissatisfaction. Then Rem, tugging at his sleeve, looked up at him.

"So there's no need to worry. If you like, I could go procure a Flying Dragon egg right now, even..."

"The word 'procure' makes it sound less like buying and more like hunting, which is a mysterious thing! Well, it's fine to start with regular chicken eggs for now. Ask again if my palate isn't satisfied."

Since he was, by his own admission and everyone else's, a cheap-tastes kind of guy, the odds of him craving luxury were low. But if he brushed off her suggestion outright, he could already picture Rem deflating. So he accepted the proposal in a "we'll hold that in reserve" sort of way, letting Rem save face, and then—

"All right, and next up, ingredient number two, abracatabra oil!"

"The oil we normally use here at the manor is already top-grade, so no issue there. It's stockpiled in jars, so it's fine no matter how much is used."

"But now that I think about it, is it really okay to be using the manor's stuff for such a completely personal project? Won't we get scolded for that?"

Roswaal, who had been present at breakfast, hadn't said anything on the matter, but there was no doubt this whole endeavor leaned pretty heavily toward personal indulgence.

Getting carried away and causing friction with his employer was something Subaru wanted to avoid, but—

"Don't worry. I'll properly reimburse whatever's used for personal matters out of my own pocket later. There's no need for you to worry about anything, Subaru-kun."

"I'm almost fooled by how kind that sounds, but doesn't relying on that make me a total scumbag!?"

At Rem's words — she who wouldn't hesitate to dip into her own savings — Subaru struck a compromise by promising to split the cost once his wages came in. Since Rem flatly refused to let him cover it entirely, that was the best middle ground he could manage.

That Rem had made self-sacrificing devotion her purpose in life still didn't sit right with Subaru. As he watched the exchange between the siblings out of the corner of her eye, Ram's gaze showed no sign of warming from its absolute-zero state.

With Rem, in her precarious state of dependence, still by his side, Subaru swallowed a sigh in his chest and moved on with the mayonnaise-making.

"Okay, that's the main ingredients. The rest is, let's see, first, salt."

"Solt, you mean."

"And then pepper?"

"Peppa is right here too."

"And sugar... I don't think we needed it, but just in case."

"Sugar's ready as well."

"Man, why's sugar the only one with no twist to it..."

Facing the array of lined-up seasonings, Subaru put a hand to his chin with a "now then."

Eggs, oil, and a few seasonings — that, as far as he remembered, was about the extent of the main ingredients. The problem was in what order, and in what proportions, to combine them.

"Well, a journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step. Let's factor in failure and go steadily forward."

"Yes, that's right. So, even if we fail, please don't abandon Rem..."

"Whoa, hey! What did I say that struck a nerve!?"

Rem pinched Subaru's sleeve, trembling faintly.

For someone in her state of hypersensitivity to evaluation, a strategy premised on failure seemed to be quite mentally taxing. In other words, trial and error was an extremely poor fit for her.

Somehow soothing the faintly-shivering Rem, Subaru nodded reassuringly.

"Right, yeah, exactly. Going in with an 'I'm prepared to fail from the start' underdog mindset is no good. We've gotta go into everything with the resolve to nail it in one decisive strike! Yeah, I've always been the type to blurt out the right answer without reading the room anyway, right? Easy, easy."

Puffing out his chest with a show of nonchalance, Subaru turned back to face the ingredients while inwardly breaking into a cold sweat.

With Rem watching him with such worried eyes beside him, the strategy of trying random combinations with failure baked in was no longer an option.

The tension he'd felt only a few days ago — it came rushing back now, and Subaru's heart began pumping blood at a painfully fast rate.

"For real, the kitchen's a battlefield... All right, let's do this. Rem, give me an egg."

"Yes. Would around two hundred be enough?"

"Are you trying to make me make enough mayonnaise to swim in!? One's fine!"

The basket Rem lifted was piled high with eggs, conveying just how fired up she was. Recoiling somewhat against that momentum, Subaru retrieved just a single egg and, for the time being, cracked it into a nearby bowl to begin work.

Staring down the yolk that had dropped into the bowl, he tossed in salt and pepper with fairly reckless abandon. He mustn't show any hesitation. Thinking of Rem's trust-filled gaze standing beside him, faltering here and clouding her eyes was out of the question.

Pushed forward by a strange sense of mission, Subaru put his faith in himself and in his parents inside his head. He whisked the egg and seasonings together with a whisk, and once it seemed to be coming together, he poured in the oil. He remembered now. Right, no matter what, mixing was the important thing — that's what his parents had seemed to say.

Oil, more oil. He poured the oil in with single-minded devotion, as if his passion alone could set the mayonnaise in the bowl ablaze, running the whisk as though he'd come face to face with his parents' killer.

His breath grew ragged, sweat traced down his forehead and off his chin, and his vision gradually began to cloud white. And still, as though trying to reach some unreachable domain, he mixed and mixed with all his heart poured in.

Sound grew distant, color drained from the world. But in exchange, his senses sharpened. In a hyper-sensory world where he could even feel the movement of air against his skin, Subaru kept staring into the mayonnaise in the bowl. And then, he was certain.

"It separated."

Sheer force of will alone just wasn't enough, after all.

—After that, the mayonnaise-making raged fiercely.

A seesaw battle, if you wanted to put it nicely, but frankly, the record was a total shutout.

Countless eggs sacrificed, and what piled up was a mountain of accumulated failures. No, not a mountain — more like an ocean of incomprehensible liquid, yellowish in places, cloudy white in others, the sort of thing you didn't even want to look at.

By the time he'd sacrificed several dozen of the mountain of eggs, Subaru could no longer bear the waste of life.

"Damn it! Still not there! Why!? My method should be right... what am I missing!? What do I need to do. Don't tell me the world is actually interfering to stop mayonnaise from being brought into another world—!?"

In isekai-summoning stories, bringing modern civilization along and triggering a cultural explosion was practically a genre staple. To Subaru, who had no particular expert knowledge, it had always seemed like a distant dream, but he'd never imagined a pitfall like this could exist.

"Damn it, how terrifying, this wobble in the fabric of the universe. If I'm stumbling this badly over mayonnaise, bringing in any larger-scale knowledge might just be flat-out impossible..."

His heart felt close to breaking from disappointment.

Had Subaru's heart ever been crushed by this much despair before? A domain that, no matter how far he reached, remained out of his grasp.

To be made to realize that in the middle of such an ordinary little scene—

"...Actually, come to think of it, I've been pretty despairing lately, huh, me."

He was self-aware enough to know his experience points in the "heart getting crushed" category had been piling up quite a bit.

Unfortunately, no matter how much of that particular kind of experience he stockpiled, it did nothing for his resistance, nor did it lead to leveling up — a dump stat, through and through.

In any case, he'd racked up enough failures to prompt that kind of sentimentality, and surely it must have brought Ram and Rem to similar disappointment — or so he thought, drooping his shoulders to glance beside him. And—

"I feel like it's taking on a decent shape, but the flavor's totally off. How is it on your end, sister?"

"Adding the oil little by little and adjusting the flavor with Solt and Peppa. Stirring seems like it requires patience, which isn't really suited to Ram."

She was chatting away while working, whisk stirring inside her bowl. And when Subaru sneaked a glance over—

"Wait wait wait what? You're supposed to know even less than me, so why does it look like something mayo-shaped is actually taking form over there, whyyyy!?"

In both Ram's and Rem's bowls, a faint but distinctly viscous substance had begun to form. Ram's homemade version seemed to be somewhat closer to the ideal form, if anything, but—

"W-why...?"

"This is the difference between someone who cooks and someone who doesn't. A trivial gap in knowledge doesn't even register as an issue. Learn that a hastily-acquired veneer of skill is worth nothing."

"You call that cooking, but the only thing of yours I've ever seen you cook is steamed potatoes!?"

His hasty rebuttal was nothing but the sourest of sour grapes, and against Ram's familiar "hmph" as she scoffed, he had nothing left to say.

Meanwhile, Rem, seeming to comfort a Subaru dejected at the gap between them, smiled.

"It's all right. Please just do things your own way, Subaru-kun. From the perspective of Rem, who actually cooks, it's irrational, illogical, and honestly, I can't help feeling a bit sorry for the eggs, but that's your way of living, Subaru-kun."

"That's the first roundabout poison dart in a while! Stop bringing my whole way of living into this!? I don't want to be judged that hard over mayonnaise-making!"

Shouting back at Rem's words — which sounded supportive but weren't at all — Subaru peered at the two women's work and compared the differences.

As Subaru watched with eyes wide as saucers, the two of them added oil little by little to the viscous not-quite-mayonnaise.

"Ohh, I see. You don't add it all at once."

"Pouring in a large amount all at once hastens separation. After that, it's about patient, persistent stirring. Rashness and patience — it's a bit of a sore point, isn't it."

"Wow! Amazing! Rem, you're the best! Great catch! Nicely spotted! Well done, well done, wonderful!"

He stopped Rem, who looked liable to sink under the weight of her own words, with some light praise. At his words, Rem's expression brightened instantly, and she gave an embarrassed smile. "I-is that so."

She was becoming a touch troublesome, and also a touch too easy to sway.

"That's exactly the kind of thing this whole mayonnaise project was supposed to make you realize, though, huh."

The reason she constantly sought outside validation for herself was rooted in an excessive self-deprecation.

Which meant that, if only she could gain confidence, she'd surely be able to break free from her current "clingy puppy" state. That, at least, was Subaru's theory, informed by his many manga and games.

In truth, most of the work at the manor wouldn't run without Rem, and yet that had become so normalized that her contributions went praised far too rarely. Even that was probably part of what fed her conviction that she was "surely still not doing enough."

Which was precisely why he'd proposed this mayonnaise-making — as a way to have her take on the unknown.

By accomplishing something that couldn't have been finished without her, he hoped it might, even a little, become a source of confidence for Rem.

That was the thought behind the proposal. So it was by no means driven entirely by self-interest.

"And yet, why's it you who's doing better than me right now. Seriously, you never read the room, do you."

"Quite the insult, aimed at Ram, who happens to excel at reading the wind. Can't you see what's in Ram's hands?"

At his mutter of complaint with a sidelong glare, Ram snorted and gestured with her eyes toward her own hands. Inside the bowl she held, a small vortex of wind had formed. It gripped the tip of the whisk, enabling a rate of agitation no human hand could ever achieve.

Watching, within that ferociously spinning spiral, the not-quite-mayonnaise steadily shift form into actual mayonnaise, Subaru nodded with a wry "yeah, yeah" and a smile.

"That efficiency of yours is what I hate right now."

"Setting aside the physical aspect, is it really my fault my head just happens to work sharply? And besides, whatever Barusu's intentions might be..."

After giving him a sidelong glance, she turned a gaze toward Rem, as though there was something on her mind. Rem, unaware of that gaze, was fully absorbed in turning her whisk.

At her little sister's profile, Ram's mouth loosened just slightly.

"When someone relies on you, you end up wanting to show them your best side. Ram's own petty pride as an older sister — does Barusu really have any right to interfere with that?"

Even while acknowledging she fell short of her sister in ability, Ram still identified as the older sister.

Whatever Subaru's intentions might be, they probably weren't a negative for her either, but Ram had her own convictions, in her own way, about how to relate to Rem. If that was so, commenting further on it would only be tactless of him.

Even so—

"In the end, if you finish first, it's not gonna do anything for Rem's confidence, is it. What're you gonna do if she spirals into 'as expected, sister is amazing, and compared to that, Rem is...'? You're not exactly thrilled either about her getting even more attached to me than she already is, right?"

Having Rem's dependence on him grow even further was something Subaru wanted to avoid. If her demands for rewards grew any more excessive, she might, in a worst-case scenario, offer to follow him into the bathroom and even help wipe. Terrifying.

Oblivious to Subaru's inner shudder, Ram let out a breath and murmured, "That won't happen," and continued.

"Ram should be holding back on using Mana around now anyway, so this won't get finished. If I just show her the path, Rem will walk it on her own from there. Since there's the fact that I laid the first steps, Ram's pride won't be hurt either. Satisfied with that compromise? Barusu."

"Man, you're seriously unlovable, sister. — Did I push you too hard, by any chance?"

He thought back to the Witchbeast forest, when she'd overused wind magic and been left unable to move.

If this time around had placed a burden on her again, that was on Subaru too. But she shook her head at his question.

"It's simply that there's no benefit for Ram, purely speaking, so I'm stopping. Even if I finish this, the only one who'd be pleased is Barusu. Ah, how utterly uninspiring. I'm done."

Declaring so, Ram unceremoniously tossed her bowl toward the sink.

It was a cutoff decisive enough to be almost refreshing, and whether what she'd just said was the truth or a pretext to conceal it, it carried more than enough force to irritate Subaru.

"Sister?"

"Rem. Ram's tired, so I'm stepping out. I'll be having tea in the dining hall, so keep Barusu company appropriately until lunch."

Rem, too absorbed in stirring to have taken part in the conversation, responded promptly to the call. Ram poked the sulking Subaru's shoulder and left the kitchen.

Left speechless by her domineering behavior, Subaru sighed and retrieved the bowl Ram had thrown aside.

"The hell, it was looking pretty good and she just chucks it. How many chicks' lives did I sacrifice without ever getting this far..."

His own losing streak was becoming truly pathetic. Muttering that complaint, Subaru dipped a finger into the bowl and brought the slightly yellowish not-quite-mayonnaise to his mouth. But—

"The texture's pretty close, I think... ugh, no, still something's off."

The rich oiliness, the appearance — both were fairly close to the mayonnaise he was after. But the crucial flavor was something else entirely.

Something felt missing, but it didn't feel like it was about how much salt or pepper there was.

Salt and pepper were purely part of the seasoning — what was different, beyond those? Something more essential still seemed to be missing.

"Something's missing from the ingredients, man. What is it, I don't get it... it's not like sweetness, or spiciness, or saltiness..."

Licking the contents of the bowl over and over, Subaru cocked his head.

Purely as a matter of taste, the unfinished not-quite-mayonnaise, no matter how many times he tasted it, only left the oiliness at the forefront, and with each repeat his queasiness only grew.

Beside him, Rem, tasting her own bowl in the same way, closed her eyes, and as she savored the oiliness spreading in her mouth, she murmured "hmm," seeming to hesitate over something.

Just by appearance, it was close to the finished form. The problem lay in that one last step, something missing. But Subaru's train of thought was suddenly cut off by her words as she abruptly opened her eyes.

"—Acidity."

"Huh?"

"Given the general flavor here, I feel like it would taste good if a bit of sourness were added."

Blinking at Rem's suggestion, Subaru brought the not-quite-mayonnaise to his mouth again to check.

Amid the rich oiliness, a pure queasiness spread. But what if a tongue-tingling sourness were added to that? — In the shape of his imagination, wings sprouted, and the not-quite-mayonnaise soared triumphantly into the blue sky. Now, he could see the answer.

"Acidity! That's it, acidity! It's missing sourness! What, what should I add!? Something sour, something sour... oranges, maybe!?"

"I think Lemom fruit or Vinegee would work best."

"Lemon juice or vinegar! I agree! If that's the answer, the possibilities are endless!"

Following Rem's suggestion, Subaru pulled seasonings out from the back of the kitchen. Crushing a lemon-like fruit and squeezing out its juice, and pouring out a vinegar-like liquid whose intensely sour smell was strong enough to make the back of his throat want to scream, into the sink.

With the two sour elements prepared, Subaru nodded to Rem, then added lemon juice to the bowl Ram had discarded, and poured vinegar into the bowl Rem held.

"Now it's just single-minded, all-out stirring! Until our Souls are worn thin...!"

Learning from his earlier mistake, he kept the amount he added modest, despite the intensity of his tone.

Adding it little by little to the not-quite-mayonnaise, he stirred and refined the form while suppressing his eagerness.

Taking his time, pouring in his affection, and before he knew it, right in front of Subaru, the not-quite-mayonnaise had evolved into a single, unified form of mayonnaise.

He held his breath, feeling an ache even in his parched, dried-out throat. Even in that state, his heart craved not water, but the highly viscous substance before his eyes, and nothing else would do.

He turned around. Rem was there. She nodded, backing up his resolve.

Nodding back to her, Subaru turned back to face the bowl. The oily aroma drifting up, and the faint tang tickling his nose — no mistake, the mayo-lover spirit within Subaru cried out.

Now, in another world, Subaru had blazed a new path—.

He plunged a finger in and scooped some up.

Gazing lovingly at the finger now richly coated white, Subaru brought it to his mouth. He sucked his finger in a manner sensuous, even faintly erotic. Licking it clean to every corner, he pulled his finger from his lips with a wet pop.

An expression of rapture. And then, Subaru turned back to Rem.

"—This is awful!! It's a failure!! This isn't it! Blegh!!"

"A-are you all right!? Was it not a matter of amount?"

"No, no, this is wrong from the ground up! Like how wasabi and mustard and habanero all have completely different kinds of spiciness, this is a category error in sourness!"

The lemon-juice souring had ended in resounding failure.

Tearfully pouring out the not-quite-mayonnaise-turned-not-even-mayonnaise from his mouth, Subaru turned clouded eyes toward Rem.

Because hope had been at the forefront, the weight of his current anxiety was all the greater.

If Rem's had failed too, he genuinely wasn't sure he could get back up. And yet, not confirming it was equally unbearable.

At the gaze Subaru turned toward her, mixed with both hope and negative emotion, Rem seemed to understand that all of his hope now rested in her hands.

Naturally, some resolve toward that fact seemed to rise within her as well.

She held her breath, and when she raised her face, the hesitation had vanished from her expression. And there, the weak sentiment of seeking evaluation for herself was also gone, leaving behind only the bearing of someone striving toward a purpose, like a seeker of the way.

"—Rem."

At Subaru's plea, Rem gave a small nod.

No words were needed between them any longer.

To answer Subaru's trust, Rem gently dipped a finger into the bowl.

Staring intently at the cloudy white liquid clinging to her fingertip, she paused, still, for only a few seconds. What she was thinking during that few seconds of stillness — no one could say.

But the instant that stillness broke, Rem, without hesitation, brought it to her mouth.

Her tongue moved as if to confirm, the red of it tracing sensuously across the white of her finger. A faint pink breath lingered on her fingertip, and Rem, eyes closed, savored what had slipped into her mouth.

A brief span of time, that single encounter — and yet it felt as though an eternity of stillness had passed there. And when her eyes opened, Subaru simply spoke the blank space and asked her.

The answer to that was—

"The flavor born here today — let's call it mayonnaise."

Those were the words that marked the first step in spreading the mayo-lover way to another world.

The moment the mayonnaise-making had triumphantly reached completion was already past the time they'd normally have needed to start making lunch.

Their joy was short-lived — cold water threatened to be thrown on the two of them at the reality that they might not be able to complete their scheduled routine work. But the one who saved them was none other than—

"I figured something like this would happen, so I already got things ready."

—Ram, who had finished preparing her signature dish, "steamed potatoes."

Frankly, while the quality of her steamed potatoes was guaranteed, it did seem like something of an issue to serve it as a noble household's lunch menu.

But what helped solve that very problem was, of all things, the mayonnaise that had started the whole issue in the first place.

"—And so, this is the ultimate seasoning handed down from my hometown, mayonnaise. Applying it to plain steamed potato really brings out the flavor. Now, dig in!"

Subaru carried on as though there were a proper reason for not having prepared a proper lunch menu.

On the luxurious table, atop the gleaming tableware, sat a simple offering of warm, steaming steamed potatoes topped with nothing but mayonnaise.

"Well now, this is quite the bold meal, isn't it?"

"I wanted everyone to understand the wonder of mayonnaise. I made Ram and Rem strip away everything unnecessary, against their better judgment. If anyone's got complaints, it's all on me, so speak up. And if you do—"

"And if we do?"

"I'll wipe out this lunch's rudeness with the credit I earned from the whole Witchbeast incident...!"

"Same as with when you saved Emilia-sama's life, Subaru-kun, you really are-e-e terrible at using your rewards, aren't you-u-u."

Roswaal chuckled wryly and let Subaru's impudence slide.

From his perspective, he'd surely seen through Subaru's flimsy excuses in an instant. Especially with Rem standing behind him, looking apologetic. All the more so, given that.

Meanwhile, Ram, who'd had not the slightest hesitation about serving steamed potato, stood proudly beside her identical twin, chest puffed out. Considerable nerve, that.

Amid all this back-and-forth, Roswaal wasn't the only one showing resistance to the menu in front of them. Looking over, Emilia — the person whose reaction mattered most to Subaru — was also studying the mayonnaise-topped steamed potato with a careful eye.

"So, how about it, Emilia-tan? Think you can eat it?"

"Since it's my first time seeing it, honestly, it's a little scary. But Rem's vouching for it, and you're saying so much about it, Subaru, so I want to believe it's good."

Its appearance wasn't exactly gentle enough to be eaten without hesitation at first sight.

Deciding to have someone try it first who wouldn't incur any guilt if forced, Subaru scanned the table, but—

"Huh, where's Beako?"

"If you mean Beatrice-sama, the moment she stepped into the dining hall and saw the menu, she turned right around and left."

"That little lolita, she'll remember this later..."

*No leftovers allowed*, Subaru silently vowed, secretly resolving to bring some mayo-potato to the library later.

In the end, with no one willing to take the plunge first, Subaru sighed.

"Fine, I get it. Then, next up, Puck, if you would."

"Eh, me?"

Shaking his head as though making a painful decision, Subaru handed the honor of going first to the little cat.

Puck sat, small and neat, across from a small steamed potato that had been placed on a plate beside Emilia's seat.

He seemed to be playing at being outside the conversation, washing his face with his paw and grooming his tail, but that wasn't going to fly.

Under Subaru's intense stare, Puck looked up at Emilia with a troubled expression. But even Emilia herself, looking apologetically at Puck, clouded her expression.

"Puck, please."

"...I'm weak when Lia asks me. Well, no helping it."

Puck flicked his head with apparent reluctance and slowly turned to face the steamed potato.

Then he looked up at Subaru with his black eyes, trembling his pink nose, and murmured quietly.

"By the way, just so you know, if I ever die, I've got a spell set up that freezes everything in the surrounding area solid, taking everyone down with me except Lia... just keep that in mind, would you."

"Don't casually confess something that terrifying! You don't have to worry about that!"

Cutting in on Puck's near-farewell-sounding declaration, Puck shook his small body and got to work on the steamed potato. Then he opened his little mouth and bit into the mass, and—

"Mmph, mmph, it's good."

"Make sure you actually try the mayonnaise, not just the potato part."

"Ah, caught me. Then, ahh."

Called out on trying to fudge it with just the potato, Puck gave an embarrassed laugh and licked the mayonnaise. Chewing the potato and mayonnaise in his mouth, Puck came under everyone's watchful eye.

Even though Subaru himself had already confirmed the finished product with his own tongue, how it would be evaluated by an unbiased party was still unknown territory. Naturally, he waited, tense, for Puck's reaction. And then, the gray kitten slowly looked up and around at everyone present.

"—Yeah, I think it's pretty good."

And with that, he gave the steamed potato a light pat with his paw, stamping it with his seal of approval.


With Puck's endorsement secured, the lunch proper got underway.

At first Emilia hadn't shaken off her wariness, but once she resigned herself and popped a steamed potato into her mouth, she was struck by the depth of the mayonnaise's flavor.

"Ah, it's good. Oh no, I can't stop."

And, in a manner that seemed reluctant despite itself, she ate several more pieces of steamed potato, looking thoroughly pleased.

Roswaal, too, said "This is delicious, isn't it," seemingly satisfied even with his noble-grade palate, and Subaru, as mayonnaise's evangelist, breathed a sigh of relief at the result.

"All right, total success. Rem, you did great."

Beaming at the unexpectedly high praise, Subaru patted the shoulder of Rem, who was still frozen behind him.

At the address, Rem lowered her head as if it were too much for her.

"No, no, what Rem did wasn't much at all. This is all thanks to your actions, Subaru-kun. Rem got so absorbed she even forgot to prepare lunch... if it weren't for you and sister, who knows what kind of scolding I would have received from Roswaal-sama."

"Why do you always spin it negatively like that. If you can give me and Ram credit for our schemes, can't you look at your own actions a bit more positively too?"

Faced with Rem's self-deprecation, Subaru could only shrug.

In truth, Ram and Subaru's actions had been a lump of scheming through and through, and there was nothing for Rem — who'd been dragged into being their accomplice — to particularly credit herself for.

If anything, it was Subaru who should feel apologetic, having made her do things outside her job and, as a result, left her with a guilty conscience.

"Thanks to Rem, the mayonnaise got finished. Now I can make flowers bloom at the dinner table over here too. I'm grateful, y'know."

But bringing that back up here would only lead into an apology contest.

So, Subaru chose to just push forward on momentum.

"—Thanks to Rem?"

Riding perfectly along with Subaru's intentions, Rem's face brightened at once.

Chuckling wryly to himself at how easily swayed she was, but figuring that was part of her charm too, Subaru nodded back.

"Oh, of course. Without Rem, today's success wouldn't have happened. You should be proud."

"Was I of use?"

"Big time. Thanks to you, my life as a mayo-addict has been saved. It really was in the danger zone, seriously."

"The danger zone..."

"Like, I might've actually died if I didn't get to soak myself in mayonnaise. Thanks to you, I lived to see another day. God, Buddha, Rem-sama, and Emilia-tan, that kind of situation."

Subaru's carelessness knew no bounds at this point.

At his words, Rem nodded along earnestly, seriously.

"Understood. Leave it to me."

"—Hm? Got it. Totally leave it to you!"

He didn't quite follow, but if it fed Rem's confidence, that was fine by him.

Giving her a thumbs up in response, Subaru headed toward the seat beside Emilia to partake in the wildly-praised lunch himself.

Behind him, unnoticed, Rem quietly clenched her fist in a gesture of resolve.


※※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※


—A night after the mayonnaise-making, early the next morning.

A servant's morning began early, and Subaru too got out of bed promptly and walked the manor's hallway, stifling a yawn.

Yesterday had been quite a to-do in various ways, but that mayonnaise had become something edible in this world was a rare stroke of luck.

Going forward, he'd have to worry less about differences in diet. All thanks to Rem.

And, just as he was thinking that—

"Speak of the devil, right on cue. Morning, Remrin."

"—Ah, Subaru-kun. Good morning!"

Rem, who seemed to have woken up before him, came hurrying toward him from down the hallway, breath quickened.

She brightened instantly, waiting, it seemed, for his reaction. Subaru gave a wry smile. Then, with an "ah" as a lead-in, he said—

"Thanks a lot for yesterday. Made me happy to remember the taste of home, thanks to you."

"No, not at all. If what Rem did was of use to you, Subaru-kun, there's nothing that makes me happier than that. —You could praise me some more, you know?"

Rem's invisible tail seemed to wag. Subaru placed a palm on her blue hair and gave her head a leisurely pat. Rem hummed and closed her eyes, looking pleased.

Feeling somehow as though he were doing something he shouldn't, Subaru half-began to ask himself what he was doing first thing in the morning. And then—

"Oh right, Subaru-kun. Did you happen to sweat a bit while sleeping?"

"Eh, wait, seriously, do I smell? I guess it was a bit muggy yesterday, come to think of it."

It was true he'd found it a bit uncomfortably warm for sleep, and Subaru, startled at being called out on it, raised his arm. He tried sniffing himself, but a smell you're thoroughly used to is a hard thing to judge.

Subaru tilted his head. But if Rem was pointing it out, it must be fairly noticeable.

"Embarrassing if Emilia-tan notices too. Guess I'll go wipe myself down with some water."

"In that case, please use the bathhouse. The smaller bath should be fine to use even this early in the morning. —If you'd like, I could scrub your back."

"You don't have to scrub it for me. Girls oughta have a bit more modesty, y'know?"

"How unfortunate."

Rem, with a half-joking air, pulled her hand back. At her remark, though, Subaru decided to take her up on the offer after all. After all, if she was going out of her way to recommend the bath, whatever smell was currently emanating from his body might not be an ordinary one.

That it was Rem — favorably disposed toward Subaru — who'd encountered it meant it had only gone this far. If it had been Ram or Beatrice who'd run into him, the thought alone was enough to crush his spirit.

"Well then, guess I'll take you up on it a bit."

"Yes. —Enjoy."

There was something faintly suggestive in Rem's words. But Subaru, having decided he needed to wash off the sweat as soon as possible, didn't notice.

Raising a hand in farewell to Rem, he headed off toward the bathhouse at a slightly hurried pace.


The experience of bathing first thing in the morning was a fresh sensation in a world without showers.

Back in his shut-in days, his life had been such a day-night reversal that he'd often head to the bath whenever the mood struck and finish up with just a shower.

In this world, that kind of quick bathing wasn't an option, and besides, skimping on grooming right in the vicinity of the girl he had feelings for was out of the question.

So, naturally, Subaru had increasingly come to take his time bathing. But—

"Getting to have the whole bathhouse to myself like this, it's weirdly exciting, huh."

Surveying the spacious bathhouse, Subaru, stark naked, puffed out his chest pointlessly and muttered to himself.

The large communal bath, rivaling even a public bathhouse, had its water drained at the moment, its marble-like tub waiting to be cleaned. But Subaru's target wasn't that one — it was the small bathtub set in a corner of the bathhouse.

It was a tub meant to save on the trouble of needlessly heating a full bath when the master, Roswaal, was away. It was plenty large enough for one person, and came fully equipped with a mana-stone-powered water heater, making it genuinely convenient.

And then, glancing casually at the tub—

"Steam rising leisurely... don't tell me Rem thought ahead and got it ready?"

Subaru had come to the bathhouse after going back to his room to fetch a change of clothes.

Given the time lost in that detour, it wasn't at all implausible that Rem might have quietly gone and heated the water in the meantime, considering how overly considerate she'd been lately.

Either way, if the water was already heated, all the better.

Being alone, naked, in the bathhouse in the early morning — the synergy of it all had an unexpectedly strong effect on Subaru's mental state.

As a result—

"Number one, Natsuki Subaru — coming through for a bath!"

Dashing through the bathhouse in a strange fit of high spirits, Subaru executed a hop-step-jump. Without meeting the disaster of slipping on the wet floor, he flung himself forward, his manhood swaying as he leapt — his body spun through the air and shot straight toward the tub.

And then—

"—Guhh!?"

With a wet, viscous sound unlike any he'd ever heard, his body sank right in.

Startled by the unexpected sensation and warmth, Subaru struggled to bring his body up through the slick, gooey liquid, somehow managing to get his face above the surface — but his eyes wouldn't open.

"What, what!? What is this feeling, what is this situation!? It's insane, my whole body's slimy! This is bad! And it's stinging in a cut somewhere! Bad, why!? I might actually die!"

Leaping out of the tub, Subaru rolled dramatically across the bathhouse floor, screaming.

His whole body was coated in a viscous liquid, and every time he tried to stand up, he slipped, and slipped again. Even when he tried to plant his hands on the ground, his palms slipped, slipped, slipped.

It made no sense. A completely unexpected turn of events.

With his eyes still unable to open, in a world shrouded in darkness, alarm bells rang in Subaru's mind. The sensation of his life being threatened, one he'd just tasted a few days ago, returned at close range, and Subaru silently berated his own carelessness.

He was such an idiot. What had he been so complacent about?

How could he have forgotten the malice of the god presiding over the fates of this other world? Thought you were safe? Sorry, BAD END! How had he not been prepared for that kind of betrayal?

Amid the agony of struggling to breathe, Subaru gasped and turned his face toward the ceiling.

Spread-eagled on the bathhouse floor, coated head to toe in the viscous liquid, his five senses stubbornly refusing to cooperate, Subaru desperately raced through his thoughts.

He could still speak. He could call for help. But what if this was a trap too? Even if he called for help, wouldn't he just be dragging whoever answered into it as well? But then again, if he stayed silent and did nothing, whoever had orchestrated this would simply finish him off anyway. Then what was the point of staying quiet — his thoughts raced dizzyingly in that single instant.

That was suddenly interrupted by a strange sensation that had arisen in his mouth.

"If I die, keep my death secret for three days... in that time, work behind the scenes to... hm?"

Amid the chaos, Subaru, who'd been muttering something like a will, frowned and carefully savored, with his tongue, the sensation that had arisen in his mouth.

Viscosity, oiliness, and a supreme sourness — the conclusion to that was—

"—Rem! Rem! Get in here!"

"Yes! Did you call, Subaru-kun!"

At the summons, Rem's voice answered as though she'd been waiting right outside the bathhouse the whole time.

She swiftly hurried over to Subaru, and with a hand towel she'd brought, gently wiped his mayonnaise-covered face.

"How is it, being bathed in mayonnaise? In answer to your request, Subaru-kun, Rem did her very best too. —You could praise me for it, you know?"

Her smile implicitly demanded, *praise me, praise me*.

Sitting up, wiping his mayonnaise-drenched body down with the towel, Subaru returned a smile to Rem's innocent face and said—

"You're an idiot!? Seriously!?"

An unprecedented roar of anger from Subaru exploded through the early-morning bathhouse.

Combined with the fact that his soul's beloved companion, mayonnaise, had been wasted, the extent of Subaru's fury was truly something else, and it was said that his lecture, with Rem forced to sit in seiza, continued long, loud, and fierce, right up until an oversleeping Ram finally showed up at the bathhouse.


—As a side note, at Roswaal Manor, mayonnaise secured its place for good on the strength of Emilia's opinion that "it's tasty, so I'd be happy if we always had some," and it would go on being stocked, strictly adhering to the two words "in moderation."

It should also be added that although the mayonnaise-making had begun as a way to build Rem's confidence, she ended up, as a result of this whole incident, developing a strong fear of mayonnaise — and that, factoring in Ram's evident aptitude for mayonnaise-making, it became her job instead.


The First Mayonnaise Incident, concluded — to be continued in the Second Mayonnaise Incident.
