Arc 4 Chapter 59: "Sweet Baked Treats and an Unsweet Talk"

"...My, you're back awfully soon, aren't you."

The tall maid who opened the mansion's front gate to welcome the returning Subaru murmured this while rounding her sharp eyes in surprise.

"Ended up in a situation where I had no choice but to make a quick U-turn. Two days... no, three, since I saw you? Sudden reunion with no warning, but bear with me, would you?"

Subaru answered from atop Patrasche's back, the dragon breathing hard beneath him, his body carrying the fatigue of half a day's travel. Looking up at Subaru astride the dragon, the maid — Frederica — cracked a light joke while covering her mouth, where a faint glimpse of fang showed, her expression betraying concern at his poor complexion.

"It's no trouble at all. If I couldn't offer flawless hospitality no matter when or what guest arrived, it would be a disgrace to my standing as my lord's attendant. I'll return the Earth Dragon to the stable, so please, Subaru-sama, go on into the mansion. I'll have Petra attend to you."

"You don't have to go to all that trouble... ah, no, actually — please do."

As he dismounted from Patrasche and answered, his knees nearly buckled on landing. He caught himself on the reins just in time, but it seemed his body had taken more strain than he'd realized.
Small wonder — he'd flown here from the Sanctuary without sleep or rest, and his body had already been running on a sleepless night even before he'd left the Sanctuary in the first place.
Even with the Divine Protection of Wind Evasion, and Patrasche's quiet consideration in trying not to burden her rider, the more than six hours of travel had taken a heavy toll on Subaru's body.

Realizing his body was more worn down than he'd thought, Subaru obediently accepted Frederica's offer. He handed the reins to Frederica and stroked the neck of the visibly worried Patrasche.

"I'm fine, I'm fine, really. You're the one who put up with my selfishness — thank you. When I get to the stable later, I'll give you a good brushing to make it up to you."

Unlike horses, Earth Dragons don't have thick fur, but they love being brushed all the same, the bristles scraping against their hard skin. Patrasche was no exception, and at Subaru's promise she pressed her muzzle forward eagerly, catching him with a full frontal nose-attack that sent him reeling back with an "Oof!"

"My my, aren't you lively. Well then, off we go, Patrasche-chan. I'll lay down fresh straw and get your bed all set."

"Yeah, thanks. — Oh, right, Frederica."

"Yes?"

Frederica had already taken Patrasche's reins and started leading the dragon toward the stable. At Subaru's call, she stopped and turned back, just her head twisting around.
Her long blonde hair swayed — a maid with a fearsome face and a gentle heart. Subaru cracked his neck before speaking.

"—You don't have any plans to go to the cabin today, do you?"

"...? No, I don't. Why do you ask?"

Frederica answered Subaru's low-voiced question with a puzzled tone. Subaru watched carefully — her answer, her expression, the direction of her gaze — then shook his head and said, "Never mind."

"If you're not going, that's fine. Once you're done with Patrasche, sorry, but could you come straight back to the mansion? I want to talk about something — including what happened at the Sanctuary."

"Understood. I'll be right back."

With a polite bow, Frederica finally departed with Patrasche.
Once she'd vanished from view past the front garden, Subaru stretched where he stood and looked up at the mansion. — Its imposing form unchanged, the mansion of the shortest loop yet, returned to the evening of the second day.

At the latest, the sixth night; if it came sooner, the fourth.
Either way, the end would arrive wearing the shape of a blade.
Before that happens—

"I need to find a way to end the ending, before the ending comes."

The sacrifices made to get here this time were too many.
For the sorrow of leaving people behind in a world about to vanish, Subaru needed to find something to repay it with.

Garfiel's anguished cry. Emilia's unseen sorrow.
These were what would give Natsuki Subaru the resolve to keep fighting, eyes averted from the pain.

"Wow! You're back so soon!"

Those were the first words Petra spoke, face lighting up, positively brimming with adorableness, as she welcomed Subaru back to the mansion.
Despite the content and phrasing being almost identical to Frederica's, the affection packed into them differed by exactly the weight of the bond they'd built — a textbook example, easy to grasp.

"You look exhausted, are you okay? No wait, are you all right? I can have the bathhouse ready right away, so if you'd like to bathe first... what's wrong?"

"Nah, I was just seriously thinking how healing it is to look at you, Petra. Thinking back on this whole thing, you're the only one I can be with without thinking about fronts or backs or complicated stuff at all."

Slipping between polite speech and her usual way of talking, energetic Petra bounced busily around Subaru.
When he reached out and ruffled her chestnut hair, she leaned into his hand with a happy little purr, utterly adorable. Just as he'd said — healing.
At the same time, what crossed Subaru's mind was what had happened at the mansion last time, and the too-cruel end Petra had met in the course of it.

"Petra, this is sudden, but... would you be willing to do something for me?"

"...? Yeah. I mean, yes. Whatever Subaru-sama needs, I'll do anything."

"Heh, 'anything' is reassuring to hear. Okay. It's kind of an important talk. Frederica should be back soon too, so I want to talk in the drawing room. Could you just bring some tea?"

"Frederica-neesama too?"

"Yeah. It's the kind of talk that might affect what happens from here on, and Petra, you're not unrelated to it — you're practically a party to it. I want you there."

"Not... unrelated..."

Petra pressed a hand to her mouth, lost in thought. Then, as if something had occurred to her, she snapped her head up, cheeks coloring slightly.

"Is this an important talk about me and Subaru-sama?"

"Hmm, I guess you could put it that way? It's definitely important, for both you and me. After all, it's not something that can be settled between just the two of us."

"But for something like that, what the people involved feel matters, doesn't it?"

"Feel? Feelings, huh? Feelings are... well, important, I guess? It is true I don't want to do anything against your wishes, so that's not... wrong, I suppose?"

At Petra's insistence, Subaru tilted his head but answered in the affirmative, and the girl's face lit up brilliantly as she spun in a full circle on the spot. With dancing steps she dashed off into the mansion.

"Right away! I'll be right back! No running away, okay?!"

"I'm not running anywhere, but you'd better not trip rushing off like that."

Petra, riding high, looked ready to fly straight up to the second-floor servants' room. Suddenly remembering something, Subaru called after her retreating back, "Petra!"

"Petra, thanks for the handkerchief. It ended up being used a little differently than you probably intended, but — it saved me."

"Really? I actually helped Subaru?"

"Yeah. Saved my life... well, not exactly, but something like that."

The white handkerchief he pulled from his breast pocket, embroidered by Petra, was her gift to him.
And last time, in his encounter with the Witch of Envy, it had also been the weapon he'd used to take his own life just before being consumed by the witch. Though admittedly, Echidna's intent had likely been at work there as well — but its origin lay in Petra's feelings.

Thinking about it, as long as Echidna's will was involved, did this handkerchief still function as a weapon even now? Going by how it had worked last time, the trigger seemed to be either Subaru's life being in mortal danger, or pouring mana into it the way one would cast a spell. In the latter case, though, the difficulty was far too high for Subaru to manage.

"Anyway, it's thanks to you, Petra. I owe you for this, somehow, sometime."

"Then — a date! One date!"

"Did you hear that from Emilia?"

The reward for the Witchbeast incident — Subaru's first date with Emilia — had taken place in Arlam Village.
The considerate villagers and children had left Subaru and Emilia alone together, and it seemed Petra had remembered that.

"All right. I'll be your escort. Petra's first date being with me is, well, a pretty luxurious and honorable thing, if I do say so myself."

"It's a promise!"

"Yep yep."

Raising his hand in a casual wave, Subaru watched Petra, smiling like a flower, finally disappear down the far end of the corridor.
Seeing off her small retreating back, Subaru let his thoughts drift to Petra's future. Even now, she was a girl whose future was something to look forward to, with her lovely appearance. In another five years — no, even three — her limbs would fill out and she'd grow into a young woman beautiful enough to make him do a double take.
By the time that happened, whatever feelings of affection she held for him now would probably have faded. But the fact that the girl she'd become had chosen Subaru as the partner for her very first date — that, surely, would bring Subaru a sinfully satisfying feeling. And so—

"Let's make that promise again for sure, Petra."

A promise made in a world that would surely vanish wouldn't remain within her.
But Subaru would go on remembering that the promise had been made.

When the time came that he could choose the right future, he'd make that same promise again.
Picturing Petra's smile, Subaru thought this to himself as he stepped into the drawing room.

Sitting face to face on the sofas in the drawing room, Subaru watched with a wry smile as Petra, seated across from him, puffed out her red cheeks in displeasure.

Swinging her legs from the knee down on the sofa, Petra made no effort to hide her dissatisfaction now that she'd heard the whole story. Subaru found the girl's childishness rather endearing, but it apparently didn't sit well with the senior maid seated beside her.

"What is that face, Petra? It's disrespectful in front of Subaru-sama."

"But, but, Frederica-neesama..."

"There is no 'but.' Even with those you're close to and familiar with, your conduct must remain proper. If you don't keep that in mind day to day, how do you expect to manage it when it truly matters? You're a quick learner, Petra, but this is one area where you still have far to go."

"Uuu~"

Petra bit her lip in frustration and hung her head.
Feeling sorry for the girl being talked down so thoroughly with pure logic, Subaru tried to smooth things over with a "Now now, that's enough..." only to be silenced by a sharp glare in return.
Having reconvened with Frederica, back from the stable, and Petra, who'd finished preparing the tea, the three of them had settled in the drawing room, wet their throats with tea, and gotten down to the real matter at hand.
Right now was the point where the first topic Subaru had raised had left Petra sulking. And that topic was—

"Why do I have to leave the mansion? I've only just started working here — it's barely been a week and a bit..."

Petra clung to him with a face on the verge of tears, and Subaru felt guilt gnawing at him. But considering what was to come at the mansion, giving in to guilt and coddling her wouldn't do.
Steeling himself, Subaru shook his head.

"It's not like I'm saying stay away forever. It's not a dismissal or anything — I just want you to go back to Arlam Village... well, back home, for about a week."

"You can't tell me the reason, right?"

"...Not the details, no. But it's true that danger is closing in on this mansion. I told Frederica about the Witch Cult targeting us recently, right?"

At the words "Witch Cult," Frederica's expression shifted.
Though she'd been away at the time, it had been roughly two weeks ago that Witch Cultists led by Petelgeuse had attacked the mansion and Arlam Village.
Having grown up in the Sanctuary, Frederica — with Emilia's heritage tangled up in it too — surely understood keenly what being branded a heretic could bring about.

Just as Subaru had hoped, Frederica set her jaw despite her troubled expression.

"If that's true, then I believe Subaru-sama's judgment is sound. Petra has no means of defending herself yet."

"It's fine! Subaru will protect me!"

"I'd love to tell you to leave it to me like a real man, but I'm all too aware of my own lack of strength — or ability, or whatever you want to call it — so I can't go making promises like that."

Petra, having risen to argue with Frederica, was cut short by Subaru's own admission of inadequacy. She slumped her shoulders at his answer, but Frederica stroked the girl's head as if to console her.

"Petra, don't be discouraged. You're not so foolish as to fail to understand how much it must pain Subaru-sama to speak of his own lack of ability, are you?"

"...No, Frederica-neesama."

"Lamenting a lack of strength is the same for anyone — Subaru-sama, and you as well. Subaru-sama acknowledges it and still searches for what he can do. Petra, what will you do?"

"Uuu~"

Once again on the verge of tears, biting back her frustration, Petra looked up at Subaru with a sharp, watery glare, then, peering up at him—

"W-what I can do... isn't there anything at the mansion?"

"...No, sorry. This time, there's nothing here for you to do. It's my own lack of ability — I don't have the room to look after you too. I'm sorry for that."

As Subaru bowed his head, Petra squeezed her eyes shut and rubbed her eyelids with her sleeve.
When she raised her face again, no trace of tears remained. Only her reddened eyes were set firm as she carefully pinched the hem of her skirt and curtsied.

"Understood, Subaru-sama. Petra will take her leave starting tonight, for a while. Once the problem is safely resolved, please call for me right away."

"Yeah, I will. Once this is over..."

If, in that moment, all the mansion's members — and if possible, the key people from the Sanctuary too — could gather in one place overflowing with smiles, that would be the best outcome imaginable.
With Petra accepting Subaru's proposal, the first matter had reached its conclusion.

Once Petra, given the task of clearing away the finished tea and lightly packing her things, left the drawing room, only Subaru and Frederica remained in the room.
Watching the door click shut, and feeling Petra's footsteps fade down the hallway, Subaru reached for one of the sweet baked treats served with the tea, savoring its sweetness on his tongue.

"Can I ask you something, Frederica?"

"That depends on what it is, Subaru-sama."

Subaru gave a wry smile at the response — predictable, as expected. Frederica's expression remained unruffled, waiting for him to open his mouth.
He took a breath, weighing how best to phrase his first question. But of everything he wanted to ask her, there was really only one thing that came first.

"What do you think Garfiel wants for the Sanctuary?"

"—Has something happened with my unworthy brother?"

"We've butted heads plenty of times, on plenty of things. And I want to figure out whether that clashing is the kind that can never be resolved, or the kind where if we just talk it out, we can understand each other."

That would determine how Subaru moved forward from here.
Whether Garfiel was an enemy to be defeated, or an ally worth winning over.

"The fact that you're not surprised means you must have heard about my relationship with my brother from him."

"Ryuzu-san didn't deny it either. You know Ryuzu-san, right?"

"Of course. In the Sanctuary, she was the one who raised my brother and me, both of us without any real family... though given the years she's lived, 'grandmother' might be more fitting than 'mother.'"

"Garfiel kept calling her 'old hag' too."

The anguished shout of "Granny," at their parting, wouldn't leave Subaru's mind.
Perhaps Garfiel used to call Ryuzu that, once, long ago. The rough talk now might just be an act — and if that had been the real him back then, then—

"Was Garfiel maybe, like, a real grandma's boy?"

"If you mean toward my grandmother... yes, that would be true. My brother has a deeply affectionate nature beneath it all, so his fondness for her runs quite deep. Though he probably thinks he's hiding it."

Even from his own sister's perspective, Garfiel's family devotion toward Ryuzu apparently stood out remarkably.
Just where along the line that turned into protecting the Sanctuary — and led to that atrocity — Subaru still couldn't say.

"Whether I can forgive what he did, and how he actually behaves, are two separate things."

"Subaru-sama?"

"Nothing. Just remembered why I'm mad at him, that's all. I keep telling myself I need to judge things without prejudice, but..."

Subaru still hadn't reached an understanding of the root of what had driven Garfiel to the massacre of the villagers.
But it was certain that Garfiel held command over Ryuzu Meyer's clones — a byproduct of the immortality experiments once carried out in the Sanctuary — and that he'd been the one managing the facility.
Why would he be the one to destroy it? Why did he hold that authority in the first place? Too much remained unclear, but—

"Frederica. I know you used to live in the Sanctuary. And that as a quarter-blood, you weren't caught by the barrier — you could leave."

"You already know that much..."

"And that Garfiel, in the same position, could have left too, but chose to stay inside. Tell me, Frederica — do you know what purpose the Sanctuary was built for?"

Only four days had passed in this timeline since Subaru had first headed to the Sanctuary. Frederica seemed startled by the sheer volume of information he'd gathered in so short a time, but his next question widened her eyes even further. Then—

"No, I don't know the details. I understand that the Sanctuary was built once for the purposes of the Witch of Greed's experiments, but..."

"You don't know? Really? Your answer won't change?"

"I don't know what you suspect, but my answer remains the same. The Sanctuary was the witch's testing ground; now, only the barrier she set remains, and it won't be undone until the Trials of the Tomb are overcome. That's all I know."

Frederica shook her head slowly, closing the matter. There was no way to verify the truth of her words, but to Subaru, she didn't look like she was lying.
Which meant Frederica didn't know the reality of the immortality experiments. And that, in turn, meant her understanding of what those experiments had produced was equally thin.

"Wait a second. So then, Frederica — you don't know anything about who commands the clones?"

"Clones...? No, I've never heard of such a thing."

Her denial carried the same tone of sincerity as before.
Subaru sat stunned at her answer, then let his weight sink back into the sofa, drained.

"I'm sorry. It seems I've given you an answer that disappoints you."

"No, it's fine. It's not your fault. ...How many years ago was it that you left the Sanctuary again? Did I ever ask you that?"

"It's been about seven years since I left the Sanctuary and came into the mansion's care. Ram arrived a little after that, so I suppose I'm the most senior member of the staff."

Subaru sighed — both at the fact that Rem's name hadn't come up as Frederica recalled the past, and at the fact that the amount of information Frederica held about the Sanctuary hadn't changed from the previous loop.
This all but ruled out the possibility that Frederica was deliberately withholding information from him for some reason. Approaching from different angles and getting the same answers each time meant it was simply the truth.
Frederica knew nothing of the immortality experiments, nor, presumably, of Ryuzu's clones. Either Ryuzu had managed her daily rotations remarkably smoothly, or perhaps even Frederica — composed as she was now — had been a more scatterbrained girl in her youth.

"But then, that leaves Garfiel as the mystery. Just when did that guy learn the truth about the Sanctuary...?"

Holding command over the clones meant Garfiel had known about that facility. And more than that, it was likely he who would eventually destroy it.
Garfiel knew about the experiments involving Ryuzu Meyer. Had he learned the truth his sister didn't know after she'd left the Sanctuary? Or was it precisely because he knew that he'd stayed behind?

"—Ah."

And then, thinking it over, Subaru suddenly realized he'd been overlooking something all too crucial. The moment he noticed it, he was genuinely appalled at his own stupidity.

"If he held command over the clones, that means he cleared the same condition I did. Which means he's got that ridiculous Apostle of Greed status too, or whatever it's called..."

Which was to say, it was proof that Garfiel, too, had met the Witch of Greed — Echidna.
Why hadn't he noticed sooner, Subaru wondered, holding his head. If Garfiel had met Echidna, it would explain his holding command over the clones. It would explain the almost prejudiced way he viewed the Trials. It would explain his sympathy toward Emilia, who kept challenging the Trials and being broken by them.

"Frederica. — Garfiel's taken on the Trials before, hasn't he?"

"—! How do you know that?"

"Put a bunch of different pieces together and it led there. I'm guessing he failed, but... what actually happened?"

At Frederica's confirmation, Subaru clenched his fist, feeling he'd grasped part of the core of the matter.
Frederica let out a breath at his eagerness, then closed her eyes as if reaching back into the past.

"...I wasn't the only one who wished for the Sanctuary's liberation. There was a time when my brother, too, was desperate to show the outside world to Grandmother and the others. Still young, he snuck into the tomb and challenged the Trials. I remember envying his recklessness."

"You never went in yourself?"

"I didn't have the courage. Even knowing that overcoming the Trials within would free the Sanctuary, I couldn't. It was a place I'd always been told never to enter. I envied my brother, who could dive straight in without hesitation."

It was an easy scene to picture.
Take his current personality and make it even more reckless, and there was young Garfiel, charging headlong into the tomb's Trials, driven by nothing but the wish to show the outside world to the family he loved.
But then—

"My brother went into the tomb and didn't come out, and I regretted not stopping him, and I called for Grandmother... She hesitated too, but she quickly made up her mind and went in after him. Some time later, she came back to me, praying, with my brother in tow. But—"

—Never go into the tomb again. And forget what happened today. Don't tell anyone.

That, apparently, was what Ryuzu had firmly told Frederica.
Hearing this whole account, Subaru recalled a contradiction in what Ryuzu had once said — one Ryuzu claiming to have entered the tomb, and another flatly denying ever having done so.
It made sense now, knowing there were multiple Ryuzus bound by an inability to lie — the contradiction between an experienced Ryuzu and an inexperienced one, resolved.
And what this story revealed was—

"Garfiel took the Trials once. And there, he must have come face to face with the Witch of Greed too. That finally makes all the pieces fit."

The feelings Garfiel held toward the Trials. The reason he held command over the clones.
The question was what he'd seen in that "past." Why he opposed Subaru's efforts to free the Sanctuary. And why Echidna had kept quiet to Subaru about Garfiel at all. The answers all lay within the tomb.

"I absolutely need to see Echidna at least once more."

To wrest out everything she was hiding, from the witch who knew it all.
Before him, as Subaru quietly steeled his resolve, Frederica silently looked up at him.
Noticing her gaze, Subaru scratched his cheek and murmured, "Sorry."

"For a lot of things. I'm sure some of what I asked wasn't stuff you wanted to be asked about."

"No, it was necessary. I've also been instructed to by my lord. If what we've discussed here helps Emilia-sama — and by extension, helps free the Sanctuary — then I have no complaints whatsoever."

"I will free the Sanctuary. No matter what. There's a reason I have to, so I'll free it by whatever means necessary. But honestly, how much I can factor in Garfiel's own wishes along the way — that's a secondary concern."

"............."

"I have absolutely no idea what's going through Garfiel's head. And worst case, whatever he's thinking, if it doesn't line up with what I intend to do, I'll push my own judgment through even if it means crushing him. I'll choose the greatest happiness for the greatest number. Sorry, but that's how it is."

Calamity was coming for both the mansion and the Sanctuary. If Garfiel stood in the way of Subaru's efforts to avert it, he'd have to be prepared to be knocked aside.
At Subaru's answer, Frederica closed her eyes tightly once, then—

"Please, take care of my unworthy brother."

—And with that, bowing her head, she gave her answer.
