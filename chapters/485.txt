Arc 6 Chapter 79: "READY STEADY GO"

The chaotic battle kicked off, and it was the Great Scorpion that fired the first shot.

"——"

Its red compound eyes blazed with a fierce glint, and an instant later, white light burst from its tail as it lashed out.

The terrifying part was that the blow meant to deliver a fatal strike came without so much as a shout to announce it. The Great Scorpion never roared — it simply dispensed death, flatly, methodically.

That white light carried a lot of unpleasant memories. To be specific, of the more than fifteen rounds of trial and error inside the tower, roughly half of his deaths had come from this very attack.

But die enough times to it, and you learn things whether you want to or not.

For instance—

"Its initial speed, and the telltale sign before it attacks...!"

Both the compound eyes and the stinger gave off a faint extra glimmer the instant before they struck. It was a sign so slight it could be mistaken for a trick of the eye — but it was the thread of survival Subaru had seized through life-or-death trial and error.

To make the most of it, Subaru ran with Beatrice held in his arms, deliberately closing the distance to the Great Scorpion. Because—

"The initial speed is the slowest part...!"

Even as he said it, he twisted his body to dodge the surging white light.

Slow as the white light might be, it was still far faster than a mediocre high school pitcher's fastball. Fortunately, he had confidence in his eye for a pitch. After all, without it, he wouldn't have survived this long.

Life is trial and error, some great person had said once. But surely that great person hadn't been picturing a life-or-death situation when they said it.

Still, the only way to make up for what you lack is to fail again and again. That much was true. And as it happened, Subaru had — "luckily" — the ability to do exactly that: trial and error, in a life-or-death situation.

Making full use of it, he searched out the odds of survival. This — this was Natsuki Subaru's home turf.

"Sorry I couldn't live up to your expectations, me!"

To the other self who wanted an all-around, capable Natsuki Subaru, he apologized from the bottom of his heart for being able to fight only in this scrappy, unrefined way. But in his arms, held tight as she danced this near-death waltz right alongside him, Beatrice let out a "hmph" and said,

"What are you talking about. There's never once been a time Subaru failed to live up to expectations, I suppose."

Beatrice, saying such a heartening thing. She swiftly raised her hand, and from her thin lips came the incantation for the Yin magic that warps the law of gravity — Murak.

The Great Scorpion had already been struck by that spell once, sent plummeting far below. Now it tensed its jet-black carapace, planting itself firmly so as not to suffer the same fate twice.

However—

"Once you stop moving, you're all ours~!"

The Witchbeasts' concentrated assault fell upon the Great Scorpion the instant it planted itself and stopped.

The all-out attack Meili ordered came from the grotesque Witchbeasts called "Wing Drakes," whose wings and horns seemed fused into one. They closed in on the Great Scorpion like bullets that feared no death, slamming their horns without hesitation into its hard shell, one after another, in a chain of impacts and shattering sounds.

The Great Scorpion writhed under that chain of impacts, and when it raised its great pincers to deal with them—

"——!!"

Then, taking their place, delivering a fierce blow of its own, was a monstrosity among monstrosities — a being that seemed like a nightmare given form, a centaur of horse and man fused as one: the Starved Horse King.

A baby's tremendous wailing cry swept across the watchtower's terrace, and a grating roar that made one want to clap their hands over their ears, along with an explosive blaze, violently blanketed the space so close to the sky.

"——!!"

Into the heart of that flame, the Starved Horse King thrust the fiery lance in its hand without hesitation. The white-hot point was a killing blow capable of burning through and piercing even an iron-hard shell.

In sheer combat strength, the Great Scorpion overwhelmingly dominated, but that didn't mean attacks had no effect on it whatsoever. If it landed, it hurt. Naturally so, for any living creature.

If it landed, it hurt. If it landed.

"——"

And so the Great Scorpion embodied the principle that, if a blow never lands, it doesn't matter at all.

It caught the thrusting fiery lance with its great pincer and, in the same sweeping motion, severed the Starved Horse King's arm. The Starved Horse King tried to regenerate from the wound, but the cut was mercilessly seared shut by its own fire lance. The bubbling, roiling motion of the wound trying to knit itself back together came to a stop.

Burn the wound and it can't regenerate — a piece of insight nobody needed, gained right there. And then, into the body of the now-motionless Starved Horse King, the Great Scorpion's stinger detonated, and the huge frame burst from the inside.

"——"

Flesh and blood scattered, and the ferocious Starved Horse King's life was extinguished.

The Wing Drakes too, once their heads — fused with their horns — were crushed against that shell, had no life left in them. Fighting with a strategy that spends lives so mercilessly, so disposably, was conduct that rightly deserved to be condemned as heinous and inhuman.

To close one's eyes to it just because they're Witchbeasts would be wrong. But Subaru had no intention of mouthing pretty platitudes like "all lives are equal."

Life has value. And unmistakably, that value differs from life to life.

Someone precious to him and someone who isn't clearly feel different in the weight of their life to him. He had no intention of hiding that behind some comfortable lie. So he would use the lives of Witchbeasts to win.

And then—

"Overlooking me might be the smart move, but it's the wrong one!"

The Witchbeasts' vanguard was blown away, and he planted his feet hard on the red-paved floor. With Beatrice held in his left arm, gripped in his right hand — drawn back for a mighty swing — was his unsheathed whip.

The Guiltywhip, fashioned in blasphemous fashion from the corpse of a once-formidable foe — Natsuki Subaru's fastest strike, one that tore through sound itself, slammed directly into part of the Great Scorpion's shell.

A sound like a thunderclap rang out, and the red compound eyes glared at Subaru. There was no sign the Great Scorpion had taken any damage, not the faintest hint of pain.

But that was fine. The aim wasn't to deal damage. That was precisely why the Great Scorpion had left dealing with Subaru for last, after all the other Witchbeasts.

That judgment was correct. And it was wrong.

"Hraaaaah——!!"

"Reeling you in, I suppose!!"

When he put his strength into hauling up on the whip wrapped around its shell, the Great Scorpion's massive body rose up from the recoil — proof that Beatrice's Yin magic was still in effect, its weight drastically reduced.

Caught off guard, the Great Scorpion tried to brace itself, but the ground beneath it was fouled with blood and entrails — the worst possible footing to plant itself on. As its sharp claws tried to anchor its body to the floor, Subaru hauled it up in one great, sweeping motion.

To an outside observer, it would look like Subaru's monstrous strength alone was swinging the giant beast around. The sheer, spectacular sight — made possible only through Beatrice's cooperation — saw the Great Scorpion's huge body whipped around and around and around to its limit, and then hurled away.

"Get, the hell, OUT of here——!!"

With the force of some super giant swing, the Great Scorpion's body spun round and round through the air.

Even while spinning wildly, the Great Scorpion still tried to fire off precisely aimed bursts of white light at Subaru. But every single one was swatted away by Beatrice's raised palm.

She formed a purple, glowing crystal in midair, shooting the white light down head-on. While that teamwork was still holding, Subaru released the Great Scorpion from the whip, and once more it went flying off into the sky.

"That should buy us more time—"

—Subaru thought, finding a narrow path forward through a razor's-edge gambit, one that let flesh be cut to sever bone. But before he could finish the thought, his eyes went wide.

Right before him, the Great Scorpion, still hurtling through the air, pulled off an astonishing acrobatic feat.

"——"

A sweeping blow of its own great pincer hacked its own tail clean off at the root. The severed tail went flying, scattering dark red bodily fluid — and from it, one very last burst of white light was fired.

That white light struck the now-tailless Great Scorpion and sent it flying back toward the watchtower balcony with tremendous force — a reckless trick that took advantage of its own lost weight. The Great Scorpion returned to the terrace amid a shockwave, jamming its great pincers into the floor to brake itself, and glared back at them.

"Wh-what a ridiculous stunt! But if that means its tail's gone now, that's actually a relief for us..."

"——"

"—hey, hey, hey, hey!"

From the tail-stump of the Great Scorpion, crouched low after landing, more dark red blood welled up. But that wasn't the wound deepening. Just the opposite — from the wound, the severed tail was regenerating.

Not only that — the cracks in its shell, caused by the very white light it had used on itself to go flying, were visibly bubbling and filling back in the same way.

"Tough, regenerates, and smart — quit stacking up traits that don't sound anything like Shaula!!"

Regeneration aside, Shaula's body had been soft, and she hadn't been terribly bright. But casting off all those Shaula traits, the Great Scorpion was coming at them with overwhelming, annihilating force.

Swinging its regenerated tail around, it unleashed its stinger not with careful aim but in a wide, indiscriminate spray. It was an area attack unrelated to the telltale warning sign, and for Subaru and the others, outmatched in raw power, it had to be called the worst possible means of attack.

"Whoaaaaa——?!"

"Ngh... Subaru, we've got no choice but to jump, I suppose!!"

There was no way to escape the barrage of white light while on the ground. That meant the only path forward was up. Beatrice instantly released her spell from the Great Scorpion and recast it on Subaru at once.

Trusting his partner on whether the spell would take effect, Subaru kicked off the ground and leapt with all his might into the air. Grazing past his feet, the white light shattered the terrace below.

"Just barely made it...!"

"Not quite, I suppose...!"

He'd literally leapt clear of a lethal possibility, but there was no time to feel relieved.

It was the same thing Subaru and the others had just done to the Great Scorpion. Flee into the air, and you lose both the footing to brace yourself and any avenue of escape. All that's left, then, is to be smashed by an attack.

"Beako! Original Spell, round two——!"

It was a trump card he could only play three times a day, but he couldn't afford to fumble the timing and die. When Subaru pulled her tight against his shoulder, Beatrice immediately came to the same conclusion.

In that instant, the special incantation began preparing to deploy, and a fixed window of invincibility for Subaru's body was about to—

"——!!"

"Whoa!?"

"Wapu!?"

Right before the incantation activated, Subaru and Beatrice's bodies were snatched away from the side. At the same moment, a burst of white light passed through the space where they'd just been — they had come within a hair of being vaporized.

The one who saved them from that crisis, and who continued to protect the two of them from the Great Scorpion's pursuit, was—

"Th-thanks for the save. What just happ— ugh!?"

"I suppose so!?"

Clinging to a rubbery texture, Subaru checked what exactly had happened to him and let out a startled tremor from his throat. Likewise, tucked against him, Beatrice was scowling hard as well.

Because the one who had saved them from the Great Scorpion's attack, and who was still desperately shielding them now, was a Witchbeast with a bluish-black hide and a menacing appearance — the Starved Horse King.

"When I first laid eyes on you down in the depths, I never would've guessed we'd end up fighting side by side at the climax!"

"——!!"

To Subaru's bluff of bravado, the Starved Horse King answered with a cry so grating it was miserable to hear. As Subaru's cheek twitched at that, another individual Starved Horse King drew up alongside them. And clinging with her small body to that one's back was Meili.

"You almost busted out your trump card there, didn't you~? Be more careful, okay~? Onii-san and Beatrice-chan's trump card is my lifeline too, you know~!"

"Nice save! Nice backup! You did great, Meili! Can you change the battlefield for us, just like this!?"

"So basically, you want to move somewhere else~? I can do that, buuut..."

"Then please! For now, there's way too few escape routes up here!"

Looking around, Subaru decided to abandon the fight on the balcony, with its scant footing. Already the corpses of many Witchbeasts were scattered about, and it was a situation ripe for some irregular accident to occur.

Weighing the efficiency of that judgment, Meili nodded, slapped the Starved Horse King's back, and ordered, "Please." At that, the two Starved Horse Kings surged in speed — leaping out beyond the balcony and, in one tremendous burst of motion, beginning to race straight down the watchtower's outer wall.

"Whoa, whoaaaaaaa——?!"

"Wabababababababababa, I suppose!?"

At a stunt more reckless than they'd expected, Subaru and Beatrice screamed while clinging to each other. But at the same time, there was nothing to do but tip his hat to Meili's ability to command even the Starved Horse King and fold it into her own forces.

"——"

With the high-offense Starved Horse King at the forefront, Wing Drakes flying through midair, and on the ground giant Sand Earthworms, Oiran Bears, and many other Witchbeasts all as allies, their strategic options widened considerably.

For Subaru, who wanted to stack up delaying tactics and buy time, she could be called the best possible partner.

"Such a rich variety of attack methods! Meili! You and I might just have better chemistry than I thought!"

"Cut it out, Onii-san~! I absolutely do not want Petra-chan or Beatrice-chan glaring at me, okay~!"

Racing straight down the watchtower's vertical face, he shouted words back and forth with Meili, clinging to the back of the Starved Horse King running alongside. To Subaru's joke, Meili made a face of genuine, sincere displeasure. That reaction wounded his subtly delicate male pride a little, but now was the time to close his eyes to such minor injuries.

"Once we hit the sand sea, round two starts. That thing's going to come after me! For now, at least, it shouldn't be able to touch anyone still inside..."

"But~, we still don't have a finishing blow, right~? Running away forever isn't gonna get us a win, is it~?"

This was the Augria Sand Dunes, where Witchbeasts sprang up inexhaustibly. Their reserve of "ammunition" wouldn't run out — if anything it kept growing — but all that guaranteed was staying power, not a finishing blow.

"——"

Glancing over, Subaru saw Meili wipe sweat from her brow, her breath faintly ragged.

Part of it was surely the tension of being thrust into the thick of a life-or-death fight, but he couldn't help feeling that no small part of it was feedback from controlling the Witchbeasts. It wasn't something Subaru, who had no Divine Protection, could really understand, but there was every possibility that even a Divine Protection, overused, could turn to poison.

"Back when I couldn't control my Divine Protection, the world felt like hell, Otto had joked once..."

That offhand line, dropped by a dead-drunk Otto, might not have been something to laugh off after all.

If Otto was right, and a Divine Protection could become a burden, then there was a limit to how long Meili could keep fighting too. If Meili collapsed in the middle of the battle, Subaru and the others' defeat would be sealed instantly.

In other words, this fight was—

"—decided by just how much we can dote on Meili like she's a delicate little flower——!"

"That is, like, the single most unacceptable battle plan I've ever heard, I suppose!"

The instant after Beatrice arched her brow at Subaru's declaration, an impact tore through both their bodies. It was proof that the Starved Horse King, racing down the tower at furious speed, had reached its final stop.

In other words, it was proof they had broken past the watchtower's vertical wall and returned to the sand sea.

"Whatever else, it's been right beside us this whole time, but... coming down into it like this, it feels pretty different somehow."

"What kind of feeling, I suppose?"

"The worst kind of feeling. Deserts are covered in sand and I hate it. It's reminding me that I never liked those sandy stages games always have, either."

Depending on the game's system, sometimes your character's HP just drains from walking around in the scorching heat. This sand sea had nothing to do with hot sand, but it still didn't leave him with any good memories.

And on top of that, being surrounded on all sides by countless swarms of Witchbeasts made it all the worse.

"Even knowing they won't attack..."

With a nose-bending, cloying sweetness in the air, and an even more overpowering ferocious animal stench on top of it, it was safe to say the air quality around the sand sea ranked among the worst in the world. An environment stocked with all manner of Witchbeasts, from the familiar to the utterly unknown — perhaps a Witchbeast researcher would find it mouthwatering.

Unfortunately, for Subaru and the others it was purely, simply uncomfortable.

"So~, what's the answer to my question~?"

Meili, astride the Starved Horse King, pulled up alongside Subaru and the others as they shivered. To her, wanting an answer to her question, Subaru glanced up at the tower and said,

"Like you said, there's nothing we can do about Shaula... about that Great Scorpion. So the whole key to this is in Emilia-tan's hands."

"...You mean that silver-haired lady from before?"

"That's right. Emilia-tan, and the fifth rule — that's the whole key."

Nodding at Meili's puzzled look, Subaru held out his open palm toward her. Five fingers raised — those were the rules imposed on Subaru and the others, as challengers, within the Pleiades Watchtower.

"You can't leave without finishing the 'Trial.' You can't break the 'Trial's' rules. Disrespect toward the archive isn't permitted. The tower must not be destroyed. And..."

"And?"

Meili should have already heard up through the fourth rule.

But the fifth rule, even she didn't know. Because Shaula had tried to keep it hidden, the only ones who could come to know that rule were Shaula herself, and Subaru, through Return by Death.

The fifth rule, the one Shaula had kept her mouth shut about, refusing to reveal.

It was—

"—'Destroying the Trial is not forbidden. The rules of this tower can be broken.'"

The "Trial" bound the actions of the challengers — Subaru and the others. But at the same time, it was also a rule that bound the actions of Shaula, the examiner.

Shaula was bound by the tower's rules. That was why, even if she didn't want to kill Subaru and the others, she couldn't escape the compulsion to become the Great Scorpion and try to kill them.

If that was the wedge that had bound her for more than four hundred years—

"—! It's coming, I suppose!"

Right after Beatrice's warning cry, a tremendous plume of dust and smoke erupted right before Subaru and the others.

It was the shockwave produced by a massive shadow that hadn't raced straight down the tower, but had instead leapt without hesitation from the balcony down to the ground.

From within the swirling dust came the dull, heavy grinding sound of great pincers scraping together. As the Great Scorpion's form slowly emerged, its compound eyes were turned not toward the countless enemies surrounding it, but fixed single-mindedly on Subaru.

"Beako! Meili! We're buying time! Our win condition is Emilia-tan's victory!"

"Got it, I suppose!"

"And how much time is that, exactly, I wonder~?"

"As fast as Emilia-tan can possibly go."

Emilia was always serious, always giving it everything she had.

So there was no way she'd compromise on the problem in front of her, or hold anything back. Whatever result she produced would always be the maximum, the highest output she was capable of.

Because he believed in that, loved that, treasured that about her, he could plant his feet and hold the line here.

"All right, let's do this — smash fate itself, no, smash the tower's whole system!"

"I see, so that's why you—"

"Yes! That's right! Subaru told me — if I get all the way to the top of the tower, I'm sure to find a way to rewrite this whole situation!"

Answering briskly, Emilia put more strength into her running legs. Held in her arms as she ran was Echidna, her body shrunk small.

At first she'd been running along being pulled by the hand, but partway through, Emilia had found it too slow and simply scooped her up, and since then she'd just let herself be carried.

In truth, this was much faster and spared her body the strain, which was a relief, but—

"Shouldn't you be conserving your own stamina too? You have no idea at all what's waiting up there, do you?"

"Huh? Oh, don't worry, it's fine! Anastasia-san's body is really, really light, so it doesn't change a thing even carrying you inside it. It's a breeze!"

"Whether I'm in here or not has nothing to do with Ana's weight, you know... no, wait, that's not the point."

At the subtly mismatched exchange, Echidna, feeling a certain awkwardness, looked at Emilia — at the unfamiliar beauty of this Half-Elf.

One of the companions who'd had her very "Name" stolen by the Authority of Gluttony. Her circumstances should be exactly the same as Julius's, and yet her bearing was quite different from his. Was it a difference in their innate temperament? Or was it a difference in who supported them?

"Aren't you afraid, of having been forgotten?"

"I'm really, really scared, and lonely. But there's no time to sit around hugging my knees. Right?"

To the question that slipped out unbidden, Emilia gave an earnest, faithful answer. That strength to switch gears — was it proof of her mental toughness, or did it seem to stem from some other factor?

Emilia had said it herself — that because Subaru remembered her, she wasn't afraid.

It was a terribly simple, dreamy sort of line, and yet at the same time it seemed to hold a kind of truth.

"——"

Why Natsuki Subaru was immune to the effects of the Authority of Gluttony remained unknown. Strictly speaking, it wasn't as though he was entirely unaffected. In fact, hadn't the loss of his own "Memory" come about precisely because of an unexpected encounter with Gluttony?

So it couldn't be said that everything that had happened was entirely down to some special quality of Subaru's. Which meant — wasn't there a possibility, after all?

That, through some method or another, both "Memory" and "Name" could have been protected.

If that had been possible, then Anastasia, too, and Julius, too—

"——"

Considering the feelings of Julius, forgotten by everyone, who could blame him for wavering? And yet, seeing the strength of Emilia, placed in that exact same situation, Echidna couldn't help but wonder what accounted for the difference.

That difference was a difference in the people at their side. Wasn't it a difference in who stood beside them, trying to support them?

Could Julius, too, have remained unbroken, if he'd had support? And shouldn't there have been someone for him — someone like Natsuki Subaru was for Emilia?

"I..."

What she ought to do — Echidna couldn't produce an answer to that within herself.

So much so that it made her think this might be the first time, in her whole life as a hollow Artificial Spirit, that she'd agonized over anything this much.

"Echidna?"

"—It's nothing. More importantly, is it true? That you already broke through the second-floor 'Trial'... through Reid Astrea's violence, once before?"

"Mm, it's true. Geez, being forgotten to the point that even that's gone means I have to explain everything all over again. It's really, really a lot."

Emilia puffed out her cheeks in an adorable pout, but Echidna was struck with awe at the fact that this very woman had broken through the "Trial" of a man who was violence itself, given legs and clothed.

It seemed unlikely she'd lie in a situation like this, and even in the short time Echidna had known her, it was clear her personality simply wasn't suited to lying, so it was probably all true. Judging from the way she'd handled herself against the Great Scorpion on the balcony, if only for a moment, there was no doubt she was a considerable talent.

So the remaining question was—

"Reaching the very top of the tower will break the deadlock — what's the basis for that?"

"Apparently Subaru heard the fifth rule from Shaula. And this is something Subaru thought through carefully and came up with, so I'm sure it's the right answer."

"That strikes me as a rather blindly devoted conclusion, but..."

"If doubting would get us somewhere, I would, but I don't think this is that kind of time... And Echidna, you came with me because you believe in Subaru too, didn't you?"

Faced with eyes that held not a shadow of a doubt, Echidna found herself unable to muster even a mumble in reply. Seeing that reaction, Emilia, quite inappropriately for the moment, broke into a happy smile.

"See, my knight really, really works hard for me."

To Emilia, whose whole manner showed how proud she was that his hard work had been recognized, Echidna too felt a sentiment out of place for the moment, and clutching at Anastasia's slender chest, let out a breath.

"——"

This feeling is dangerous, she told herself.

It was terribly irrational, out of place. At the very least, it wasn't something to be holding onto this very moment. If possible, she wanted to forget it forever, and if not that, then at least for the duration of this situation.

"—Because that Subaru relied on me!"

Envy — toward this girl who believed so fiercely, so fiercely, in herself and in the one standing beside her.

For this moment, at least, she should forget it and focus on breaking the deadlock.

"——"

Emilia's long legs turned gracefully, and she flew up the stairs at breathtaking speed. And beyond what should have been a long climb, a tremendous burst of silver light scattered sparks.

Right before them, filling the whole width of their opened field of view, sweeping right and left in a great melee, was a knight with pale violet hair streaming behind him and a white uniform stained with blood — Julius Juukulius.

And facing him—

"Ngh, gh——!"

"Come on, come on, come on, come ON! You think somethin' like that's gonna do anything to me? Don't get cocky, you. You here to play around or somethin'? If you want me to play with ya, go put your makeup on first. Then I'll stomp on ya, rough ya up, and give ya some love, ya hear me!"

Alongside his foul mouth, the embodiment of violence himself, Reid Astrea, unleashed nightmarish slashes with the two chopsticks gripped in his hand, dancing his dance of death across the stage of the second floor.

That this was a clash of blows beyond human comprehension, and that Julius was nonetheless caught at a disadvantage, was — sadly enough — a difference so stark even an amateur's eye could see it at a glance.

"—"

Parrying countless slashes and kicks all at once, Julius spun his knight's sword, countering by driving a thrust into the gaps.

A razor-sharp thrust of such superhuman technique it looked like nothing so much as a flash of light — and yet Reid, even with one eye sealed shut by his eyepatch, dodged it easily, half-yawning as he did.

"Hyah!!"

An instant later, into the torso of Julius, still wide-eyed at having his thrust dodged, a carelessly raised foot slammed home. The sole of a sandal gouged into his abdomen, and Julius's body reeled backward with a groan of pain.

And into that opening, a chopstick came down from overhead in a straight vertical split—

"Scatter, why don't ya."

A single vertical flash, the slash carrying a shockwave that cut through the air, cut through space itself, cut through concept itself.

It was a swordstroke so overwhelming that words almost failed to describe it — despite the weapon being mere chopsticks, it possessed a beauty that would steal the breath of even a total amateur watching — the pinnacle of swordsmanship, its very extremity, made real.

"——"

A slash beautiful enough that simply being entranced by it could well be listed as your cause of death.

Julius dodged it with a leap to the side, though the tip of his cape, too slow to escape, was swallowed by the blade and vaporized. Into the second floor of the watchtower, whose very material remained unknown, a vertical cut was carved as if it were a joke.

On top of that—

"Oi, oi, you think dodgin' that means you're in the clear? Naive."

"—!"

At the same moment as his mocking grin, the sliced air bent, warped, and a wind was born.

In an instant, Julius, who should have already leapt clear, held his breath as his feet were dragged as if pulled into the sliced space, hauled back into range of Reid's next blow.

Unbelievably, it was the pull of the cut space trying to restore itself. That force dragged back Julius, who had once escaped death, and neatly tossed him right back into range of the next strike.

Toward Julius, unable to move in the sudden crisis, Reid's fist shot straight out—

"—That's far enough."

In that instant, what cut in between the two swordsmen was a clear, dignified voice like a silver bell.

But contrary to the beautiful ring of that voice, the manner of joining the fight was bold to the point of recklessness. At the sheer sight of it, Echidna lost her voice for a moment.

"Ahn?"

"—"

With that, Reid tilted his head up with a suspicious grunt, while Julius's eyes, wordless, went wide.

The foreign object that had appeared above both their heads — it was a massive block of ice, big enough to fill the ceiling.

A mass of destruction capable of smashing even a Dragon Carriage to bits in an instant came raining down upon the swordsmen.

In that instant, Reid and Julius's reactions split in contrasting directions. That is, Julius leapt backward to dodge the incoming block of ice, while Reid twisted his cheek into a grin.

And then—

"Hah!!"

Wearing a ferocious grin, Reid thrust one of his chopsticks straight up at the falling block of ice. The point of impact and the raised chopstick collided, and miraculously, the force balanced perfectly point against point — the chopstick bent.

On top of that, when Reid stomped down hard on the floor with his sandal, the force of that step traveled up through the chopstick, flowing straight into the ice block supported at its tip. The chopstick snapped in half, and a crack ran through the ice.

"...Not bad, huh."

Reid muttered it like he was spitting the words out, and right after, the ice block shattered in an instant, centered on that crack.

Amid the pattering scatter of ice fragments, Reid slowly turned around. Pierced straight through by that single blue eye, Emilia, her palm still raised toward him, tensed her cheeks.

Crossing gazes with Emilia, Reid's eyes widened in surprise, and—

"Pretty damn combative there, huh. Ain't like I hate a woman like that, but... ah!? What the hell, you're way too fine, ain'tcha!? Straight-up smokin'! What's a knockout like you doin' in the middle of a sand sea like this! You fight me instead, ya hear!"

"I'm sorry. For butting in like this, without asking. But Julius has to beat you, so..."

"Whaaat?"

Paying no mind to Reid's self-centered griping, Emilia went on, her brows drooping. At that one line Reid's cheek twisted, but even more bewildered than him was Julius, who'd effectively just been saved.

Having escaped the range of the ice block Emilia had thrown, and keeping a certain distance from Reid, he couldn't hide his suspicion at Emilia's abrupt appearance. Turning his yellow eyes toward Echidna, standing at Emilia's side, he said,

"I understand I've effectively been helped, but... Echidna, who is she? Just who is she?"

"I'm in something of an awkward position to explain her identity myself. But if you'll let me put it simply, she's someone in the same position as you, Julius."

"What...?"

At that, Julius looked at Emilia again, closely this time. His eyes widened slightly a moment later — perhaps because he'd found a match between Emilia's features and someone within his own knowledge.

"Silver hair, and eyes of violet-blue, an Elf... no, it's hard to imagine such a distinctive being suddenly appearing within the tower. Then could it be that she—"

From the strangeness of her distinct appearance and the fact that no impression of her remained within himself, and taking the tower's circumstances into account, Julius drew out the sense of wrongness within himself on his own.

To Julius's surprised eyes, having reached the answer through his own effort, Emilia gave a deep nod and said,

"Julius, right now, I understand your feelings really, really well."

"Then, you too, after all..."

As two people who'd both had their "Name" stolen by Gluttony, they grasped each other's situation instantly. And once that was settled, a conviction seemed to take root within Julius that Emilia was an ally.

He shielded Emilia and Echidna behind him and leveled his sword at Reid once more, saying,

"My thanks for the assistance just now. But given the state the tower's in, I can't quite grasp why the two of you came rushing here. What's the situation outside, and with Miss Rem, and the 'Taygeta' archive?"

"An attempt is currently underway to wrap all of that up together and settle it all at once, somehow. The idea came from young Natsuki, and the ones cooperating are everyone who's come to the tower."

"Subaru? But he's—"

At Subaru's name breaking into the explanation midway, Julius knit his brow in suspicion.

As far as he understood it, Subaru had challenged Reid's "Book of the Dead" within the "Taygeta" archive, and had remained in a state where his consciousness had not clearly returned. Told by Beatrice that recovering from that would be difficult, Julius, in his own way, had continued fighting Reid in order to break the tower's chaos.

To be told now that it was an idea from a now-awakened Subaru — it was only natural his understanding wouldn't connect the dots easily. But—

"Hate to break up your nice little chat, but don't go yappin' away ignorin' me!"

With a boom, the floor exploded, and Julius took the blow of Reid's charging chopstick strike. He'd lost one chopstick, but he still had the other remaining. In the first place, without a chopstick he'd simply use a knife-hand strike instead — it wasn't as though it diminished Reid's fighting strength at all.

"Ngh!"

Taking the sharp attacks, Julius reestablished his footing and kept up his desperate defense. Watching that, Reid, while literally landing blows one-handed that looked like they could fell even a Dragon, pointed the fingers of his free hand at Emilia, who was watching for an opening.

"Dunno what you've been aimin' for this whole time, but don't think I'm gonna let you do whatever you want, you. I'll deal with you after I finish off this half-baked amateur here... oh?"

Even as he spoke, Reid suddenly tilted his head.

Still with his head tilted, he touched the eyepatch over his left eye with the finger he'd been pointing, and spat out, "Oi, oi," with irritation.

"What the hell, you. For some reason my hand won't move to stop you. Unless I just fell for you outta nowhere, this is... don't tell me, you've already cleared the 'Trial'!"

"Yes, that's right! You lost by poking my chest with your chopsticks!"

"Hah! What a way to lose, exactly how I'd have wanted it. Damn shame I don't remember it, smokin' hot!"

Reid's remark, punctuated with a click of his tongue, showed that whatever wedge bound him was unable to catch hold of Emilia. Which meant that whatever obstacle stood in the way of her fulfilling her role had vanished.

In other words—

"Julius, I—"

"You should go. You, fair one, who — like me — no longer knows your own name."

Turning only his head, he cut off Emilia as she started to say something. At his words, resetting his stance with his knight's sword, Emilia fell silent.

To her startled face, Julius smiled bravely and said,

"You have your own role to play. I understand it isn't to lend me your strength. And that's fine as it is. — Please, go with everything you have."

"—. Yes, you too!"

Receiving Julius's encouragement, Emilia nodded and made to dash off. Reid made no move to stop her flight. Letting her pass by his side, the one-eyed red lion saw her off.

And when Emilia reached the innermost depths of the second floor, in front of the stairs leading further up, she stopped there and turned back, saying,

"Emilia."

"——"

"My name is Emilia. Just Emilia. — Let's meet again later, for sure!"

Leaving her own name behind in that place, Emilia dashed briskly up the stairs. Watching her back until it vanished from sight, Echidna let out a very long breath.

From here on, heading upward was Emilia's role. And Echidna's role was—

"You mean to go nowhere, and simply watch my fight through to the end?"

"If you'll allow me to. ...No, that's not it. I judged, myself, that this is what I ought to do."

"——"

Glancing over at Echidna standing by the wall, Julius pressed his lips firmly together. Watching various emotions swirl across that profile, Echidna drew a small breath.

And then—

"It's not as though there's anything I can do, but if Ana were here, she'd have done this. Wherever I am within this tower, my position is unstable regardless. So then, of my own will, I'll stand at your back. Because..."

"——"

"—Because you're Anastasia Hoshin's knight. Aren't you?"

Believing in something you have no real feel for is a thing that takes tremendous courage.

Compared to believing in something with tangible form, there's no telling just how much strength is needed to believe in something with no ground to confirm it by, before you could ever feel secure.

But believing in that uncertain thing all the same, Echidna spoke to the back before her.

"——"

Receiving that, Julius lowered his eyes, framed by long lashes. And then, quietly, deeply, at length, he let out a breath—

"It's surprising, how much strength it gives you. That someone, having mustered their courage, believes in me and holds out hope for me — even though I may no longer be anyone at all."

"Julius..."

For Julius, the footing he'd lost was uncertain.

For Echidna, the bond she should have known was vague.

Relying on such unreliable things, the two of them had to forge a relationship different from the one that should originally have connected them — and yet, in this moment at least, they were surely looking at the very same thing.

And so—

"—Julius, I have a message for you."

"A message?"

"Yes. Everyone's fighting all across the tower right now. So — hurry up and finish this off, then go help out someone else, apparently."

"——"

Knowing it was his own way of cheering him on, Echidna relayed it to Julius just as she'd heard it. Hearing that message passed on to him, Julius's slender shoulders stiffened faintly.

And within Julius, the content of that relayed message was chewed over, and swallowed. His reaction afterward was clear, and dramatic.

"Hah."

Short, it seemed at first like a sharp intake of breath. But it wasn't that.

It was a laugh. From deep in his gut, Julius let out a single breath meant for laughing. And that alone would have been a shocking thing, had anyone who knew Julius been present to see it.

—That Julius Juukulius would wear a smile in the middle of a battle.

"—If he's broken out of his shell, then I can't afford to lose either."

It was a quiet declaration of resolve, but one with a burning feeling hidden within it.

Raising his knight's sword before him, reflecting himself in the blade, Julius faced the enemy standing opposite him. Reid had been wearing a long, bored expression, but now — he grinned like a shark.

"You finally fired up, huh."

"With respect, I've always approached every battle with all seriousness."

"Nah, nah, that ain't it. Even if I don't say it, you know what I mean, don'tcha?"

Laughing, Reid raised his left hand and flipped up the eyepatch covering his eye. And with both of his blue eyes now intact and open, the pinnacle of swordsmen turned a gleefully cheerful killing intent upon his challenger.

It was a sword-aura so overwhelming that a faint heart might well die from the glare alone. But Julius took that razor-sharp gaze head-on, and behind him, Echidna too stiffened her body and endured it.

Seeing Julius and the others endure that storm of sword-aura, Reid clacked his fangs loudly and said,

"Name's Reid, the 'Stick-Swinger.' Remember just that much, and then go scatter."

"——"

In battle, exchanging names before crossing swords is proof that one recognizes the other as an equal, warrior to warrior. Just how much Reid himself valued that custom was unclear, but whatever Reid's own thinking, the thinking of the one on the receiving end changed dramatically.

Breathing out, steadying himself, Julius reined in his surging heart, and said,

"Allow me to give my name properly. I am Julius Juukulius. First knight to Anastasia Hoshin-sama, candidate in the Royal Selection of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica. — I'm done playing the nameless knight."

As if carving it into himself, and into the world itself, no less, he declared his own "Name" proudly, without reservation.


△▼△▼△▼△


Leaving Julius and Echidna behind on the floor below, Emilia raced up the stairs.

Working her long legs desperately, flying up the stairs two, three steps at a time, Emilia's speed was far from ordinary — but inside her own heart, it was nothing but faster, faster still, over and over.

"—"

Biting down hard on her back teeth, Emilia's beautiful profile took on a look of desperate intensity.

She was worried about the two she'd left behind on the second floor below. Reid was terribly strong, a violent brute, and his mouth was awful. The two facing him might get hurt, in body and in spirit both.

Of course, Emilia's worries weren't limited to just Julius and Echidna. Could Subaru and Beatrice, together with Meili, properly hold Shaula back? Could Ram fulfill her role too? Would Patrasche protect Rem? Because her "Name" had been eaten, would she have to build her relationships with everyone all over again from scratch? The seeds of anxiety were endless, and if she stopped moving, she felt like she might cry.

But she wouldn't stop her feet. She wouldn't shed tears either. She would hold back that sharp sting at the back of her nose.

"Because nothing — nothing at all — is over yet...!"

Being able to believe, and being believed in — that was what supported the whole of Emilia right now.

There was plenty of worry, plenty of anxiety, but there was even more "I believe" than that, filling her to the brim.

"—! Light!"

Ahead of her single-minded, driving gaze as she raced upward, Emilia's violet eyes caught sight of white light. Emilia understood it to be the end of this long, long staircase — the light leading on to the unknown first floor.

The instant she thought that, Emilia kicked hard off the floor and accelerated even further.

And then—

"—Made it!"

As if bursting through the light, the stairs came to an end before Emilia. In that instant, what spread out before her eyes was not the space that should be called the first floor.

"Eh...?"

Stopping in a daze, Emilia let out a small sound at the sight before her. And no wonder — what her violet eyes reflected was not a continuation of the familiar watchtower.

There were no walls, no ceiling — what spread out was a tremendously blue sky. Emilia was standing not inside a building, but outside. As a result of racing up the stairs from the second floor, Emilia had come out directly onto the roof, atop the tower.

"The first floor is... outside? This is... higher than the clouds..."

Atop the tower, the very peak had a round, spacious floor, with no partitions and nothing like a railing at the tower's edge. So if she went to the edge of the floor, she could easily look straight down below.

The tower was in contact with clouds that should have been far up in the sky, and Emilia, realizing she'd come as far as inside the clouds, or perhaps even above them, held her breath.

Even for Emilia, this was the first time she'd ever come somewhere this high. But that astonishment of hers was quickly stolen away by an entirely different feeling.

"—"

—It was a presence so quiet, so overwhelmingly majestic, that she hadn't even noticed it.

"—Ah."

Emilia, who had been focused on her own footing, the height of the clouds, and the state of the first floor, belatedly noticed a presence brushing at the edge of her vision, and then slowly turned around, letting out a breath.

Even having had her own "Name" stolen, even on the brink of despair at having been forgotten by the whole world, Emilia's heart had still held firm in the end. — But even that same Emilia was struck speechless.

That was how far beyond imagining the "presence" that had appeared before her eyes was.

Because it was—

"You are..."

"—Thou who hast reached the tower's summit. Petitioner of omnipotence, who sets foot upon the first floor."

A grave voice rang out, seeming to thunder directly into her very soul, and Emilia became aware of the tremor in her own voice.

Who could possibly belittle Emilia for that, call her weak of heart? No one could do such a thing. It was impossible. Because every single being had no choice but to prostrate itself before this one presence.

The name of that presence was—

"—I am Volcanica. By the ancient Covenant, I shall question the resolve of the one who has reached the summit."

Its massive body covered in scales that shone a brilliant blue — the "Divine Dragon" Volcanica — looked down upon Emilia and declared this with a presence overwhelming enough to seem capable of snuffing out even a soul.


△▼△▼△▼△


Outside the tower, the battle between Shaula, transformed into the Great Scorpion, and Natsuki Subaru and his companions, had begun.

On the tower's second floor, the swordplay between the laughing engine of violence, Reid Astrea, and Julius, resumed.

On the tower's first floor, Emilia had an unexpected encounter with the mighty presence waiting there.

And on the spiral staircase connecting the tower's fourth floor up through the sixth—

"Argh, seriously! Even though I went to the trouble of stealing it, it's a sense we can barely even make proper use of — honestly, what are we even doing!"

Spitting the words out irritably, the boy clicked his tongue while raking his dark brown hair. When he swung down the hand that had been raking through his hair, what appeared there was a beautifully ornamented sword shaped of ice.

It was a special power reproduced based on a stolen "Name," but contrary to its appearance, adjusting the mana was exquisitely difficult, and combining it with other "Memories" was quite the challenge. To begin with, combining the skills of separate individuals required a fitting sense for it, and Roy and Rui weren't good at that part.

"Well, that's the part we're good at, so we can pull it off, but still."

Even among the three siblings who held the Authority of Gluttony, the way each of them used their Authority differed subtly. In that light, as someone who took pride in being a "gourmet," there was no shortage of things he wanted to say about his younger siblings' approach. — Strength led to a sense of superiority. Not that giving advice was really his style.

"Can't get through to cute little Rui, or that guy Roy, either... Ahh, no helping it! If those two say they can't do anything, then there's no helping it! That means all the delicious feasts inside this tower are ours to lick clean, right down to the bone! Good, that's good, sure, fine, why not, go ahead, all right, all right then, all right, so! Overdrink! Overeat!"

Crunching down on the ice sword in his hand, baring sharp teeth, Gluttony — Lye Batenkaitos — resolved to line up every single target within the tower onto his own plate, without leaving a single one out.

Fortunately, he had a grasp on the circumstances. All that remained was the order, and what to choose as the main course—

"—You think you have the right to choose that? How carefree of you."

"——"

At the voice raining down from above on the spiral staircase, Lye stopped chewing on the ice and lifted his head. Above him, on the stairs between the fourth and fifth floors, Lye stood — and there was a pair of pale pink eyes, standing on the fourth floor, looking at him.

Eyes the same color as blood and flame, yet holding a terribly cold heat within them, looked down as if pitying Lye's ferocious hunger.

And then—


"From what I hear, you're the one who tore apart the sisterly bond between Ram and Rem. — Die squealing like the pig you are."
