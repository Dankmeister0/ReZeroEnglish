Arc 2 Chapter 35: "The Shopping Promise"

Subaru had been left behind in the darkness.

Darkness covered his entire field of vision. No sound reached him, no one's form could be seen. Even reaching out and touching something, he couldn't tell whether it was ground or wall — a forced incomprehension had spread over everything there.

Without realizing it, his throat had trembled and let out a scream, or so it felt.
Far, far away, hoping it would reach to the very ends of nothingness, he'd done nothing but raise his voice.

"...ru"

Suddenly, a voice reached him from beyond the darkness.
A change born within the "nothing" that held nothing at all — he clung to it as if leaping for it.
Clawing with his hands, turning his legs, toward the voice.

"Subaru—"

Forward, forward — !!

"To cut to the conclusion, Subaru's Gate is the type that can't be controlled, so you'd better not push yourself," Puck said.

"That's the first thing you say looking at me like this?"

"Tee-hee."

"That's not cute, damn it!?"

Subaru cursed at Puck, who floated with arms crossed as he delivered his overall assessment, and savored the sensation of the hard, cold grass with his entire body.

Lying face-down on the grass, Subaru was gasping for breath, his whole body abnormally sluggish. A lethargy like a high fever had spread through every part of him, and he couldn't feel any fighting spirit reaching his hands or feet.

It was a sensation he'd felt something similar to before.
On his first day of summoning, when Puck had drained his mana, or the times Beatrice collected mana from him on one occasion or another.
In short, Subaru was currently in a state of complete empty-tank exhaustion.

"For better or worse, Subaru's Gate has coarse mesh. So ignoring the intent of whoever's using it, the contents just gushed right on out."

"Don't... talk about a person... like they're a screen door..."

He was out of breath. Just to get out that one bit of sore-loser talk, how much stamina was he wasting?

He tried somehow to stand, but he couldn't get strength into any part of his body, and the best he could manage was lying there groaning.

Emilia lowered herself to Subaru's level in that state and met his eyes.

"You mustn't move. You used up all the mana in your body, so stay still. ...You might not even be able to work today."

"—That's a problem!"

At Emilia's attitude, like a mother scolding a mischievous son, Subaru found himself blurting that out in a sharp voice.
Beside a startled, blinking Emilia, Subaru was genuinely, seriously regretting his own carelessness.

If things really did end with him wasting this entire day, that would mean throwing away, of his own accord, the one day's grace he'd earned against the loop.
That was too stupid. It was fatal.
Tripping over a wound of his own making — that could not be allowed.

"Nnngh...!"

He turned his self-directed anger into strength and struggled to stand.
If he could at least raise his upper body, that ought to help a little. From there he could gradually regain control and recover.

"Hey now, you mustn't push yourself."

"Now's exactly the time to push myself. If I don't do it now, I'll seriously never forgive myself..."

It wasn't unusual for him to reap what he sowed through his own actions, but right now the timing and circumstances were the worst possible.

Struggling, sweat beading on his forehead, at the desperate sight of him Emilia gave a small shrug of her shoulders.

"Honestly, there's no helping you, is there."

She crouched down again, saying that.
Not understanding what she meant by it, Subaru looked up at her with just his eyes.

"—? Emilia-tan, what are you dmmf—"

Her hand, meeting that gaze, was suddenly pressed to his upturned face. And then he felt something being pushed into his mouth.
Something round, with a soft texture against his tongue.
When Subaru looked at her in confusion, Emilia nodded while still holding her palm over his mouth.

"Bite."

"—?"

"Bite it, and swallow. Three, go."

Overwhelmed by her no-nonsense attitude, Subaru resolved to bite down hard on the thing in his mouth.
A faintly sweet-and-sour taste spread through his mouth. He guessed from the texture that it was probably some kind of fruit, and immediately after — it arrived.

"FWOOOOOOOOOOOOH—!?"

Heat surged through his entire body as if he'd been set on fire, and Subaru leapt to his feet on the spot.
It felt as if his blood were boiling and clamoring inside him, as if his hands and feet, right down to his fingertips and under his nails, were about to be scorched away by passion. His breath grew ragged, as if he wanted to expel this heat he didn't know what to do with, and his legs, unable to contain the impulse, began doing high knees entirely of their own accord.

But it was only after he'd gone that far that Subaru finally noticed he'd regained the will and strength to stand.
He looked at his own two hands with an expression of disbelief, opening and closing his fists to check the sensation.

The heat in various parts of his body still hadn't fully subsided, and his joints were still dully complaining of fatigue. But it wasn't bad enough that he couldn't move.

"W-was that just now...?"

"It's a fruit called a Bokko Fruit. Eating it activates the mana inside your body — it's just a stopgap, but it lets your Gate regain some strength."

The fruit sounded familiar; its identity matched the one Subaru had received from an old woman living in the slums on his very first loop, on day one.
He recalled that back then too, he'd eaten it and suffered something similar — though that time, he'd been saved from the worst of it by Puck's subsequent mana-drain. This time, it seemed, the order of events was exactly reversed.

Either way,

"Whew, what a relief. If I'd gone straight into collecting a BAD route ending like that, I'd have found it a bit hard to forgive myself. Thanks, Emilia-tan."

"There aren't very many of them, and they're not good for you either, so I didn't want to use one. ...That wasn't just bluster just now, was it?"

For her to go so far as to use a precious item like that, Subaru's desperation a moment ago must have seemed all too genuine.
At her probing tone, Subaru raised his face.

"Of course not," he declared flatly.

And then, adding the gesture of wiping his brow,

"But man, I was an idiot... If it had ended there, I'd have died of self-directed rage worse than last time."

He rather prided himself on the fact that, in the sense of experiencing all sorts of deaths, he'd racked up quite a number of experiences most people couldn't claim, but—

"Dying of humiliation, though — honestly, that one I could really do without."

To begin with, if at all possible, he'd like the last time he'd flung himself from a height to be his last death, period. That one especially had been agonizing precisely because it hadn't been instant.
One death was plenty. And if he could choose, he wanted to meet it having lived out his natural span, dying peacefully in bed. Or, best of all, having fulfilled some spectacularly cool flag, watched over in Emilia's arms as he passed—

"...Nah, the fact that I can't even convince myself of that is exactly why I'm such a small man."

Even in jest, he found he could no longer speak of "death" lightly.
That this could be laughed off as cowardice — only someone who had truly shared Subaru's exact experience could say so.

He turned his body, checking his condition once more.
Far from being in peak form — if anything, the result left plenty of cause for worry in various places.
But Subaru had no room to let that be an excuse to skip his plans.

"Are you okay? Do you think you can really work?"

"I'll work, and I'll get everything else done too. Emilia-tan, just think of yourself as riding aboard the unsinkable battleship Natsuki Subaru Kai, and sit back with confidence."

"But it's 'unsinkable' and it's already been refitted once..."

If she noticed that, the reassurance factor would vanish entirely.
Honestly, it wasn't once but more like seven times he'd gone under, but there was no need to point that out and sour the morning air. Subaru had learned his lesson.

Pretending not to have heard Emilia's remark, Subaru did some knee bends on the spot to steady himself, and rallied his spirits.

"Well then, shall we face my senpais with renewed feeling!"

"Right. I don't think you've said anything to either of them since yesterday..."

"—Ah."

Said that mid-stretch, his back gave a dull crack in protest.

"Sister, sister. The heartless man named Subaru-kun has arrived, I see."
"Rem, Rem. The wage thief called by the name Barusu has shown up."

"I'm truly sorry about yesterday! So please forgive me, I'm begging you."

He bowed his head, groveling in apology, begging forgiveness.
Somehow it felt like all he'd done for the past half-day was bow his head. Everyone in the mansion except Roswaal — and among the women, it was a clean sweep.

"Route where I get looked down on by a girl, complete — the sin runs deep in me."

"Sister, sister. Subaru-kun really is quite the pervert."
"Rem, Rem. Barusu is a lowly masochist, that's what he is."

"That's way too far, Sister especially!"

Still displaying the Japanese man's ultimate technique, the DOGEZA, Subaru shouted from that posture, shifted his weight to his arms into a handstand, spun his body, and rose fluidly to his feet.

"Anyway, sorry about being pathetic yesterday and being annoying the day before. ...Well, there was a lot going on and I was at my wit's end, but I'm turning over a new leaf, and starting today it's New Subaru."

"Lap pillow, was it."
"A lap pillow, indeed."

"Wait, does everyone know about that, how embarrassing!?"

He covered his face in a gesture of mortification. As Subaru crumpled, the twin maids exchanged glances.

"Shall we get started on the morning's work, Sister?"
"Guess it's time to start the morning duties, Rem."

"Getting no comment on that is depressing in its own way!"

The two of them cut Subaru's protest clean in half with a wave of the hand.
While Subaru stuck out his tongue and reacted with an "owww," the two of them promptly began to scatter off toward their work. At that—

"Wait, wait. Before you head off to the morning's work, I've got a favor to ask."

At Subaru's call, the two stopped and turned back, tilting their heads in unison.

"A favor?"
"A hassle?"

"It's been a while since I've dealt with you two on good terms, so why is this feeling bubbling up in my chest... humans are strange creatures."

While he was glad they could exchange banter like this, unlike her sister, Rem's frown was fairly straightforward, and it made his wry smile twitch a little.
He let out a breath, brushing off the irrational irritation that had welled up, and said,

"Actually, I wanted to go to the village. It's nearby, right? You don't happen to have a supply run planned or anything?"

To confirm the guess he'd made last night in the forbidden archive, he wanted to make it to the village sometime today. In response to Subaru's intent, Rem put a hand to her chin.

"We're running a bit low on spices, so I was thinking of going to the village tomorrow, but..."

"Then how about pushing that up to today? If you're running low, sooner's better than later, and it's not exactly the kind of place where you can just pop over to the neighbor's for a cup of miso when you're out of spices, right?"

Even if they wanted to be neighborly, there wasn't another mansion standing nearby to begin with.
Rem seemed to hesitate a little at Subaru's proposal.
But,

"I don't see why not."

"Sister?"

The one who answered breezily on behalf of her hesitating sister, lightly touching her peach-colored hair with one eye closed, was Ram.
She turned her gaze toward her sister's questioning voice.

"We do need to go shopping, and there's no urgent business. And we have Barusu here as a porter — might as well work him for it while we have the chance."

"Do keep in mind that I actually had my stomach sliced open and I'm only three days out of recovery!"

Subaru shot back at Ram's rather blunt reasoning, but inwardly, he couldn't help but tilt his head at this covering fire she'd provided.
Because, when it came down to it, it seemed the twin maids' opinions weren't aligned.

Judging from the situation in the previous loop and the one before that, the twin maids were, under the right conditions, both supposed to be in a position to bear the role of disposing of Subaru.
For their opinions to differ like this was, to Subaru — who had actual experience of being killed by them — a discomfort he couldn't simply overlook.

Regardless, he was honestly grateful for the support fire.
He'd been fully prepared to genuinely rack his brains over how to persuade the two of them, after all.

"...If Sister says so, then."

After a moment's silent consideration, Rem too voiced her agreement.
Rem tended to take the lead in most matters, but from their interactions so far, it was clear she basically deferred to her sister's opinion.
The moment Ram had been won over, even if by chance, the outcome of this negotiation had essentially been decided.

While Subaru secretly pumped his fist, Rem's expression, which had been thoughtful until then, shifted into a composed look as she said,

"That said, going to the village will be after lunch either way. Two in the afternoon at the earliest — until then, let's at least finish our usual work."

"Don't worry. Barusu, who brought this up, will work himself to the bone. Isn't that right?"

"I'd appreciate it if you, who agreed to this, would pitch in too, Sister. ...Well, I'll work my fingers to the bone and give it my all."

"" 'Give it your all,' not 'work your fingers to the bone.' ""

Corrected in stereo on his mangled expression, Subaru scratched his head and went along with it. And so, the negotiation had borne fruit splendidly.
With the shopping promise sealed, servant time could finally begin. Rem, apparently reorganizing her mental schedule for the day now that the shopping trip had been added, set off at a brisk, businesslike pace.

Seeing off the blue-haired girl, Subaru looked at the peach-haired girl beside him. Having again been appointed as Subaru's chaperone today, she'd be accompanying him for his apprenticeship in the unfamiliar life of a servant.
Yesterday — well, technically the day before yesterday now — but in any case, it was probably wise to avoid keeping up that strange high-tension routine from that day. Or rather, now that he'd come full circle and had the composure to reflect on himself, it was just too painful to imitate again.

"Scary how fast I can recognize my own dark past. That's a pseudo-Sage moment right there for me. ...Well, no use dwelling on it — let's get to work, senpai."

"Before that."

Just as he tried to walk off, this time it was Ram's turn to return the favor and stop him in his tracks. He'd taken a step forward, but pulled it back and turned to face her, standing bowlegged.
Ram crossed her arms and looked up at Subaru from below.

"About that magic in the garden earlier..."

"Ah, yeah, sorry about that mess. Totally useless. I'm sealing that away for a while. Apparently it takes like twenty years to master it properly, from what I hear."

"It's not just that it was pathetic. Try not to provoke Rem too much."

"—?"

Not understanding the meaning of Ram's remark, a question mark floated up in Subaru's head.
At his blank expression, Ram gave her occasional derisive little "hah" through her nose.

"In a corner of the garden, disrupting the area including Emilia-sama with Shamak — Barusu ought to be dancing in gratitude toward Ram, who stopped Rem."

"Ah... ah-ha, ah, yeah, that's... that's true, isn't it."

He'd been too shocked by his own magical failure to notice, but seen from the outside, that situation might well have looked like 'that kind of situation.'
He was genuinely grateful, in the truest sense, to Ram for not letting things get out of hand there. Conversely, Rem's tendency toward snap decisions when it came to jumping to conclusions like that sent a chill down his spine.

"Seriously, I can't afford to be careless... four continues in and this is where I'm at. Doesn't bode well for me."

"What are you muttering about... hurry up and get to work, or you'll miss both breakfast and lunch."

"Yeah, yeah. Oh, but hey, about that shopping trip this afternoon — I'm curious which of you two is actually coming along with me."

Thinking about how emotionally exhausted he already was, having Rem along felt like a bit of a burden.
Still, considering his actual purpose in the village, Rem was really the more fitting companion. He had no idea what conditions determined which of Ram or Rem would come along on this particular village trip, so the question was partly meant to feel that out too. But—

"What foolish thing are you saying?"

"Huh?"

At Subaru's tilted head, Ram, rare for her, turned her usually blank expression into a smile.
An extremely, wickedly mean smile, one that chilled him to the bone.

"Both Ram and Rem are coming. A flower in each hand, as they say, Barusu."

—Let's hope those flowers aren't poisonous.

Never having considered what might happen if the negotiation went a little too well, Subaru covered his face with his palm, looked up at the ceiling, and simply murmured that to himself, deep in his heart.
