Arc 4 Chapter 70: "Beyond Hell"

—Just what was he looking at right now?

'————'

Emilia let out a shriek, wailing Subaru's name, sobbing.
Subaru's body, slumped face-down against the bed, had no strength left in it, and the eyes staring wide open had long since lost their light of life.
Of course they had. No one could go on living after having their throat torn open by a dagger and losing that much blood.

To look down upon one's own corpse, one's own manner of death—it was a rare experience indeed.
There was a warped sensation to it, as though his soul had slipped free of his dead body as a ghost and was being forced to watch the scene left behind after his death.
And though most of that sensation was mistaken, the essential part of it was not.

—What Subaru was being shown was, without question, the aftermath of Subaru's own death.

'————'

The furnishings of the room, the faces gathered there, and the pathetic sight of his own dead self.
Putting it all together, Subaru realized just what scene he was being made to witness.

This was the result of Subaru's own rash act—the one he'd committed after defeating the Sin Archbishop Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti, saving Emilia, and then, for the first time, learning of Rem's loss.

Having brought down the White Whale, driven off Sloth, and saved Emilia and Arlam Village, Subaru had rejoiced—only to be struck down into the depths of the Abyss the very next moment upon learning that Rem had been lost.
After that, he'd raced a Dragon Carriage to the royal capital, and at Crusch Karsten's second residence, he had come face to face with the sleeping Rem. The instant he confirmed that her consciousness was gone, that her memory remained in no one's heart, Subaru had driven a knife into his own throat and taken his own life.

It had been an impulsive act, not one born of any deep deliberation.
It was simply to deny the sight before his eyes. To take back with his own two hands what had been lost, he had clung to Return by Death, meaning to redo the past.

—But that rash act bore no fruit. The Subaru who killed himself was flung back not to some distant past, but to the very moment just before he'd driven the knife into his throat—the reunion with the sleeping Rem.

The save point of Return by Death had updated.
That merciless arrival had stripped Subaru of any means of getting Rem back, and cast him once more into the depths of despair and disillusionment.
After that, he had resolved himself to reclaim Rem, sworn to rise again, and was somehow managing to stand on his own two feet now—but.

'I don't—I don't know this. ...I don't know this sight. I don't know it... there's no way I could know it!'

It was a sight he had never seen.
Of course he hadn't. Because in this world, Subaru was already dead.
Even Subaru, who possessed the means to fall dead and then return to the world, had no way of grasping what became of the world after his own death. No—he *couldn't* grasp it.

And yet, until now, he had never once given that fact any thought.
For a Subaru who had avoided a world grinding to a standstill by dying, returning, and walking a different span of time, a world in which he'd died carried no information forward beyond "what caused the death"—it was nothing more than a waypoint passed through.
Having decided to use Return by Death as a checkpoint toward the future he ultimately sought, even this very moment of the world was, to him, nothing more than a passage recognized as a waypoint.
And that—was now crumbling apart.

'Stop. Stop, stop, stop, stop, stop, stop, stop, stop it, please!'

Unable to accept the sight before him, Subaru let out a soundless scream.
But no voice came from a body with no throat, no face without eyes could turn away, no head without ears could shut out the sound, and the world carved its outcome into Subaru, who was nothing but consciousness now.
—The punishment for the rash act Subaru had committed.

"Emilia-sama, what is—!"

Drawn by the sound of Emilia's wailing voice, a new figure stepped into the scene of horror.
An old man, his trained body wrapped in a brand-new butler's uniform, moving with a stride that betrayed no hint of injury—Wilhelm.
The old warrior's steps slid across the floor as he entered the room, and before that sight, he lost his words despite himself.

—Even the Sword Devil Wilhelm could wear a face of such utter shock?

Subaru, looking straight at Wilhelm's face, had that oddly out-of-place thought.
That was how far Wilhelm's expression, looking down at Subaru's corpse, had strayed from its usual composure—how unable he was to hide his agitation.

"Just what has—no, right now—Subaru-dono!"

But Wilhelm's confusion lasted only an instant.
He shook his head, quelled the turmoil of his mind at once, and rushed to the collapsed Subaru. Emilia, clinging to the lifeless body, remained unaware of Wilhelm's approach.

"Subaru... Subaru... liar... you said we'd... go together, you said..."

"Emilia-sama, forgive me—!"

Wilhelm pushed aside Emilia, who was condemning Subaru's betrayal as though casting a curse, and moved her away from the corpse. Emilia, having lost the strength to hold herself up, toppled sideways onto the floor, but Wilhelm's attention returned to Subaru after only a fleeting moment spent on her, and he set about trying to revive the boy soaked in a horrific amount of fresh blood.

'————'

With a grim expression, Wilhelm stripped off his coat, pressed the cloth to Subaru's throat, and without hesitation pulled free the dagger buried there. For an instant, the spurting blood spattered Wilhelm's already fearsome features, but he sealed the wound without so much as a blink.
Having staunched the bleeding, he struck down on Subaru's chest, where the heartbeat had stopped, trying to jolt it back to life.

"Ferris! Felix! Get here now!! It's an emergency! Hurry!!"

He roared out through the doorway, and continued the resuscitation while holding down Subaru's wound. But the amount of blood already lost was far too great. Subaru's limbs and face had lost all color, and it was plain to anyone's eyes that the soul called Natsuki Subaru no longer remained in that place.
Even so, Wilhelm would not stop his hands.

"Vil-jii, what are you shouting so—!"

"Felix, hurry! He's stabbed himself in the throat with a blade! There isn't a moment to lose!"

'————!'

Ferris, who came running at the call, nodded at once to Wilhelm's instruction, wrapped his palms in a blue glow, and sent healing magic pouring into Subaru's fallen body.
Subaru, looking down at what remained of himself—an empty shell of a soul—watched an unprecedented seriousness settle over the profile of Ferris, who never usually let his flippant manner slip.

'Stop it already... it's useless. It's useless. He's not coming back...'

No matter what they did, it was futile.
In Subaru's memory, there was no memory of being saved after attempting to take his own life.
Natsuki Subaru had, in an attempt to deny reality, impulsively driven a dagger into his own throat, left wounds in the hearts of many that would never heal, and vanished without feeling a shred of guilt over it.
That was the fact of it. That was the ending these two people's devotion could not reach.

"I will not let you go! I will not let you die, not ever! If I am to lose my benefactor in such a manner, I could not go on living with any shred of honor...!"

"Why would you do something this stupid at a time like this...!"

Wilhelm, pressing down on the wound, cried out with desperate conviction, and Ferris, spitting out his words in irritation, continued to wield the gentlest magic in this world.
At that scene, at the ripples of those two people's emotions, Subaru's heart kept being struck again and again by the shock of it.
But even their desperate efforts—

'————'

"Felix! Why? Why have you stopped the treatment! At this rate—"

"It's over already, Vil-jii. ...His soul isn't left anywhere anymore."

Pushing away Wilhelm, who pressed closer, Ferris removed the coat the Sword Devil had been holding against the wound and wiped it clean with a handkerchief pulled from his pocket. The wound had closed cleanly, restoring Subaru's body to a state that looked as though he'd been perfectly fine mere minutes ago, hardly like someone who'd suffered a fatal injury.

Only the enormous quantity of blood already spilled, and the soul that had already slipped away, were not there to be restored.

Wiping away the blood, looking down at Subaru's pale, death-marked face, Wilhelm shook his head as he spoke,

"Why... why is this so! Why could this happen so easily... Subaru-dono, you...!"

The floor beneath his slammed fist burst apart with a hard crack.
Blood mixed in with the shattered fragments of floor, because Wilhelm's own fist had broken along with it. Blood dripping from his hand, Wilhelm had bitten through his own lip in the depth of his mortification.

Facing Wilhelm, who bared his violent emotion openly, Ferris too looked down at Subaru with a face that could find no peace. His own cat ears drooping, he stared at Subaru's less-than-peaceful death mask and said,

"...You coward. You weakling. Leaving behind everyone you love, just like that-nya. ...Dumping all the pain, all the suffering, onto everyone else... and that satisfies you-nya?"

It was too fierce to be sarcasm, too merciful to be condemnation.
The complexity of feeling hidden in Ferris's voice was beyond the reach of Subaru's frozen mind to understand right now.
But one thing was made clear by Wilhelm and Ferris's manner.

—That Subaru had inflicted an irrevocable act upon the two of them.

'————'

His thoughts had come to a complete halt.
What was he being shown, right now?

He knew. What he'd been shown—he'd known long ago.
He was being shown his sin.

"—Emilia-sama?"

Wilhelm, who had been looking down, suddenly called out her name.
There was a note of unease mixed into his voice, likely because Emilia's sobbing had cut off, and the trembling of her collapsed body had stopped entirely.
Sensing the change, a pained emotion crossed Wilhelm's face. Having only just tasted a sense of loss himself, he could only imagine how much greater a shock this must be for Emilia, who had been so much closer to Subaru than he.

The old man closed his eyes tightly once, then rose to his feet.
He walked to Emilia's side where she still lay collapsed, and reached out a hand to help her up.

"I apologize for earlier, Emilia-sama. But remaining like that will harm your body. Please, take care of yourself..."

"—You said it."

"Emilia-sama?"

"You told me you loved me...!"

Still curled on her side, hugging her knees, Emilia balled herself up small and wailed.
There was no one present who could scold that figure for looking so childlike. Wilhelm knit his brow as though enduring pain, and even Ferris turned his face away, unable to bear the sight of Emilia's broken heart.

And then—

"Eh?"

Ferris let out a blank, dumbfounded sound, his eyes and mouth falling open.
As though drawn by that voice, Wilhelm too followed Ferris's line of sight, and was struck with shock.

'————'

—Before the eyes of both of them, Subaru, who should have been utterly, completely dead, was pulling himself upright.

'————!?'

At that sight beyond comprehension, Subaru's disembodied consciousness was likewise struck with astonishment.
The risen body, moving with the stiff, awkward motion of a wind-up doll, stretched out its limbs and stood, its head tilted a full ninety degrees as its eyes slowly opened.
Unfocused pupils, eyes that had lost their light, swept a glare across the room.

"Feri..."

"That's impossible! That body was undeniably dead! The revival—it failed!"

Wilhelm, as though clinging to some faint hope, tried to call out to Ferris, but midway through, Ferris sensed his intent and shouted out his own assessment first.
Hearing that, Wilhelm immediately resolved on his own course of action.
Namely—

"Subaru-dono, forgive me—!"

Even unarmed, the keenness of the Sword Devil's technique showed not the slightest dulling.
Wilhelm scooped up, in a crouching motion, the coat discarded on the floor, twisted the fabric damp with Subaru's fresh blood, and launched it like a spear with the full motion of his body.
Riding on speed, weighted with blood, twisting at its tip as it bored through the air—it was a spear of cloth. With a technique one might call the art of the cloth spear, he struck a preemptive blow against the rising Subaru.
True to its aim, the tip of the coat seemed to be sucked straight into Subaru's face—

"—Hm."

A waterfall of shadow surged up from beneath Subaru's feet, swallowing the cloth whole and erasing Wilhelm's strike.
Seeing that motion with no telltale wind-up, Wilhelm instinctively yanked his arm back—but he could not escape the damage entirely. Three fingers of his right hand, along with the cloth, were torn away at the first knuckle.

Leaping back, blood dripping, Wilhelm clicked his tongue, putting distance between himself and the standing Subaru, and said,

"Felix! Take Emilia-sama and get away at once! I will hold him here!"

"Not even one sword... all I've got is this dagger-nya!"

Rolling to the corner of the room, Ferris tossed the short blade at his waist to Wilhelm. Catching it in his left hand, drawing it from its sheath with a twist of his wrist, Wilhelm muttered, "A short weapon feels different," and said,

"Escape the mansion, get word to Crusch-sama—no, that won't do right now. Felix, use your own judgment. Go bring the knights."

"Is the mood too heavy for Vil-jii alone-nya?"

"On par with the White Whale, or perhaps... Subaru-dono, what have you been keeping inside yourself?"

Gauging his opponent's strength, Wilhelm drew a breath, sweat of anxiety beading on his brow.
Before the wary Sword Devil, Subaru stood with both arms hanging limp, his gaze wandering without settling on anything, his upper body swaying unsteadily side to side.

There was no reason left in him. Likely, his consciousness too was fragmentary at best.
The trouble was that, in such a state, some sense of self-defense still seemed to remain.

Bit by bit, Wilhelm continued his standoff with the thing Subaru had become, an entity beyond comprehension.
Meanwhile, watching this scene from above, Subaru's own consciousness was tossed about in a storm of question marks.

The wind had clearly shifted from what it had been before.
Having been shown his own sin and having his heart broken by it, Subaru was now further being shown some incomprehensible vision of the world after death.

What in the world was this scene?
Was it something that had actually happened? If not, then what meaning could this sight possibly hold? Why was his consciousness even here, in a place like this?

He understood nothing. Nothing made sense at all—but.

"Felix! Get Emilia-sama—!"

"I know, I know! Emilia-sama, over here...!?"

Responding to Wilhelm's urging, Ferris crossed the room and roughly hauled up the collapsed Emilia. But in the very next instant, a violent tremor ran across Ferris's expression.
The reason for it—

"—You made Lia cry. Unforgivable."

Generating a white mist in the air, a small shadow descended into the center of the room.
Gray fur, a tail as long as its own body, small enough to perch on a palm—and yet the pressure it gave off was enough to make one mistake the encounter for that of some towering, ferocious beast.

The small great spirit, whose form had not been seen in a long while, floated in the middle of the room, looking down at Subaru. There was a sharpness on his face, hard to read, and hatred in the words he murmured.

"Along with the atrocities that body's owner committed, you deserve to die ten thousand deaths—witch."

Cold killing intent spread through the cramped room, and Wilhelm, breathing out a white breath, hardened his expression at the sight of Puck turning that killing intent into a blade of ice.

"A spirit... Emilia-sama, could it be that..."

"Lia's unconscious right now. Based on the Contract, I act on my own judgment. I won't forgive the Witch. I'll protect Lia. —And I won't forgive that man, either, for making Lia cry."

"Not now! If we fight here, the damage—"

"The vow was broken, and my Lia's heart froze over. —It ends here."

Not heeding Wilhelm's appeal, Puck's cold killing intent only sharpened further.
White air filled the room, began to freeze, and everything within it began to die. In a world where even one's breath might freeze, Subaru stood there taking the full brunt of Puck's murderous intent.

That Subaru raised his head, and for the first time, looked at Puck.
Eyes reflecting nothing gazed at Puck floating in the air, and then, abruptly, the eyelids moved.
And then—

'————'

He laughed.

Subaru's corpse twisted its cheeks and laughed, looking at Puck.
A malevolent, warped, twisted expression, full of mockery.

'—————Stop.'

Having witnessed that much, Subaru's consciousness called out to halt the decisive catastrophe about to unfold.
But it did not reach.

Puck raised his small hand and brought it down, birthing a small-scale glacier within the room, absolute zero seeking to swallow Subaru's corpse whole. From directly below, a surge of shadow burst up and struck it back, a torrent of Mana raging wildly through the cramped room, engulfing even Wilhelm and Ferris caught in its midst, and exploding—screams and shrieks, freezing, the sound of shattering breaking apart in a chain reaction, and before Subaru's eyes, white annihilation and black despair mingled together and swallowed everything whole.

'————!!'

With a click, as though a switch had been flipped, the color drained out of the world.

"————guh."

The pain of his face slamming into the ground pulled Subaru's consciousness back to wakefulness.

Chin striking against the damp floor, tears welling from the sharp pain, Subaru shook his head.
At once, he raised his face and swept his gaze quickly around him. —No abnormalities.

"The, the tomb... inside the tomb..."

The cold air, the dark space, the sensation of a damp floor, and the sour smell—it was unmistakably the inside of the tomb.
Confirming that, Subaru next opened and closed both hands, checking that there was no strangeness in his limbs. His ragged breathing gradually began to settle, and by forcibly emptying the contents of his lungs, he willed himself back to some semblance of calm.
But he could not shake off the sensation of his organs trembling down to their roots.

"A daydream... there's no way it's something that convenient. But that was..."

Just what had that been?
Having been forced to witness a sight against his will, Subaru retraced just what situation he'd been placed in.
Almost without a doubt, that vision had been "the world after Subaru's death."

Emilia's shrieks upon witnessing Subaru's death, Wilhelm and Ferris's devotion that never reached him—and then, at the end, that nightmarish clash of powers.
The former had carved a scar into Subaru's heart, and the latter had bred a suspicion in it; together, incomprehension and loss of control had been hammered straight into his soul.

"Ugh, guh—"

The moment he remembered, Subaru doubled over as though his innards were being wrung out, and emptied the contents of his stomach onto the floor.
Even so, he hadn't managed to get so much as a proper dinner into him. What came up was only a little water from the tea he'd drunk an hour or so ago, and some yellowish stomach acid.
By repeating the motion of retching violently, he made his stomach contract, pretending to answer his body's demands.

And as he repeated the retching over and over, Subaru noticed he had some idea both of the change in his situation and its cause.

If, inside the tomb, he had not been invited into Echidna's dream castle, then there was only one place his consciousness could have been called to upon losing awareness.

"Don't tell me—the 'Trials'... not the past, but the second one...!?"

The possibility that a second Trial, different from the first one that had confronted him with the past, had begun.
Realizing that, Subaru was struck with dismay.

Certainly, for Subaru, clearing the first Trial had already happened days ago. But that was only true in terms of his soul—in terms of his body, it had only been a matter of hours ago in this world. In other words, he shouldn't have met the conditions to advance to the next stage of the Trials.
And yet, if the Trial had begun regardless, there was no way to describe it other than as irregular. Above all, according to Echidna,

"She said it wouldn't be a Trial as painful as facing the past, again..."

—If, hypothetically, the sight Subaru had just witnessed was part of a Trial, then even having only traced its surface, he could sense that this was the worst possible development.

That sight was, for Subaru, something beyond hell.
Hell, he had seen many times over. He was well aware of that.
And in order to seize the best possible future, he had resolved himself to see that hell however many times it took.

—But to be resolved to face a world beneath hell, a world lower than hell itself—that was another matter entirely.

'Behold the present that should not be.'

"————wha!?"

Faced with that terrifying experience, as Subaru wavered between retreating and holding his ground, someone's whisper suddenly brushed past his ear.
Startled by it, his body stiffened—and in that instant, his consciousness slipped away from his body.

His arms gave out, and unable to hold himself up, he collapsed once more to the floor, shoulder-first.
He desperately tried to raise his face, to hold onto consciousness, but his eyelids and neck could not resist that unseen force, and he was pulled all at once down into the depths of the Abyss.

—The Trial, the very floor of hell, opened once more to receive Subaru.


'————'

When he opened his eyes, Subaru found himself in a grassy field, come upon the scene of his own death—his throat slashed open by Julius's blade—and was made, once again, to face his own sin.
