Arc 8 Chapter 45: "How to Bring Down a Star"

And so, once more, time returns to that same instant on the battlefield where dragon and tiger clash.

Garfiel, having broken through from the first vertex of the star-shaped fortress at the Imperial Capital's Lupugana south gate, collides head-on with the ruler of the heavens, the "Cloud Dragon" Mezoreia.
Far above, the sky where abnormally thick clouds have gathered—is it proof of the "Cloud Dragon" rampaging, or preparation to unleash its greatest performance? Either way, the die is cast, and the battle to make it land the desired number has already begun.

Garfiel was entrusted with the role of leading the charge, tasked first with pinning down Mezoreia's head, because the "Cloud Dragon's" mobility and offensive power posed the single greatest obstacle to capturing the Imperial Capital. A "Dragon" able to flee to high altitude and strike from ultra-long range with its breath is something no one but a handful with truly exceptional combat strength could survive even a single hit from. Getting picked apart by that, unable to lift a finger in response—that would be the worst possible outcome for the "Vollachia Empire Salvation Squad." So Garfiel was given the job of gouging out its eyes.

That said, pinning down Mezoreia doesn't mean everything after goes smoothly. There are other "formidable foes" who could inflict catastrophic damage with a single wrong move in response. And so—

"Ooooooraaaaa!!"

Contrary to the crude battle cry, the twin blades swung through a refined arc. Swift footwork and a sword technique that skillfully shifted the body forward and back knocked aside the tip of the opponent's thrusting spear with force, throwing his stance badly off-balance. In that instant, the flashing white blade turned to sever the man's neck, cutting the air with lethal intent—

"Idiot, don't kill him!"
"Whoaaaaa!?"

At the sudden shout of restraint, the trajectory of the white blade jerked aside on reflex. The blade that should have taken off the head instead merely severed the left arm at the shoulder; the enemy, struck by the blow, leapt backward, wielding his spear one-armed as he tried to mount another attack. But that spear would never reach any ally.

"—'White Cloud Duke' Gaoran Peitiet."

"————"

A quiet but clearly audible male voice spoke that epithet and name. At once, the movement of the towering Undead with the long white beard—the thrust he'd been about to unleash—stopped cold. Not missing that instant, three small, connected shadows slipped into the Undead's guard.

In that split second, the Undead, glancing down at his own chest, met their eyes—eyes utterly unlike a living being's, the whites dyed black, golden pupils floating within. And then—

"Gaoran Peitiet!!"
"Iaaiau"

The star named Spica devours the role forced upon this once-living being, stripping it away as if to confirm the proof of the life he'd once lived, the role of being worked mercilessly as an Undead. Passing by at a speed that seemed to shave the very street, behind Spica—who had merely waved a small hand—the body of the Undead whose role had been devoured, no, of Gaoran Peitiet, crumbles into dust.

"————"

But in the moment of his scattering, he drove his spear into the ground and left a bow toward the man who had called his name. What feeling in the heart of a vanishing dead man had prompted that, no one could say.

"Atrem Nevi, Difon Trevora, Gaion Talfo, Lescar Blaine, Niorf Trad, Yaren Swalker, Beram Joit—"

The same voice that had called Gaoran's name went on to read off name after name in succession. It had the pace of someone simply rattling off whatever names came to mind, and yet it was not so—these were certain names, names of people who had each lived their own lives.

The names of the Undead leaping out one after another to block the "Salvation Squad's" path through the Imperial Capital—

The black-haired Emperor, Vincent Vollachia, correctly named every single one of them without a single mistake, though their faces and forms had been changed utterly from what they'd been in life, and thereby marked the path for the "Star Devourer" to consume. And what was maddening about it—

"Damn, that's kind of cool, dammit!"
"Then Beatrice's Subaru had better not lose either, I suppose!"

Beatrice pulls at Subaru's hand, his face twisting at the sight of Abel calmly fulfilling his role. Amid the Imperial Capital where Undead now stalk—no, amid Lupugana turned into a City of the Dead—Beatrice, guiding Subaru with unchanging form and unchanging love, the hem of her ornate dress flaring, is a reassuring sight. With the hand not clasped tightly with Subaru's, she turns toward the street and weaves magic with sweet lips and a beloved voice at the dead who stand in their way.

"El Miña!!"

At the incantation, violet-indigo crystal erupts from Beatrice's palm and races through the air, stripping the Undead of hands, feet, and weapons, whittling down their combat strength. Beatrice's negative magic is tailored to strike Undead, but shattering them outright runs counter to the mission's objective. So this is the deliberate, fine adjustment—borrowing the strength of her lovely partner, Subaru too leaps forward. And, with his left hand—the opposite of the right hand clasped with Beatrice's—joined to Spica's, he hauls her up with all his strength.

"Here we go! Atrem Nevi, Difon Trevora, Gaion Talfo, Lescar Blaine, Niorf Trad, Yaren Swalker—"
"Au! U! U-u! Aau! Uau! U'u-!"

The names Abel had read out, matched precisely and in order to the faces of each hostile Undead, are brought one by one, without exception, into contact with Spica. Spica too understands her role, and against the encroaching Undead, her outstretched hand traces, sometimes pierces, sometimes strikes down, sometimes flings away—activating the Authority of the "Star Devourer."

"Last! Beram Joit!!"
"Iaaiau!"

Face to face with the Undead swordsman bearing down to split Subaru's forehead in front of him—Beram Joit's pale, deeply wrinkled face—Subaru clenches his back teeth. Just short of reaching his forehead, the man's blade stopped, because his right arm, from the shoulder joint down to the waist, had crystallized, no longer obeying its owner's will. Grateful for that covering fire from Beatrice, Subaru urges Spica's hand forward, and it touches Beram's chest. Slowly, the Undead's face twisting in what looks like satisfaction, his body turns to dust.

"Bwah, haah…!"

Watching the Undead around him, Beram included, rendered harmless, Subaru lets out a deep breath. The close-quarters melee, something like fighting on a bridge crossing, brought back memories of the brutal days on the Sword Slave Island. Subaru, panting hard, feels Spica peer up at his back with an "Aa-u?" and pat it, and finally he can breathe easy. On Spica's other side, Beatrice plants her hands on her hips and glares at Subaru.

"You charge in far too recklessly, I suppose! Beatrice said she wanted to see a cool performance. Even a cool way to die isn't something Beatrice wants to see, I suppose."

"I know. That last move only worked because you had my back. I'm reflecting on it myself."

"…If you understand, then that's fine, I suppose."

At Subaru's honest show of reflection, Beatrice, still in scolding mode, purses her lips. Even though there's no time for a long debriefing on the battlefield, her expression looks almost let down. But it's not that he was just brushing her off—Beatrice's scolding is something to be properly taken to heart. Just now, he'd very nearly had his skull split open and died. One more step, one inch deeper, and it would have been over—a chill runs through him at the thought. That would have been a meaningless death.

"You very nearly died from overeagerness right at the start of the battle. Restrain yourself."

"You—"

Scolded by Beatrice and comforted by Spica, Subaru scowls and turns at that utterly unsympathetic remark from behind him. There stood Abel, arms crossed, who hadn't even made a gesture to help in Subaru and the others' desperate fight. Subaru can't help but admire, in a backhanded way, an Emperor so refreshingly willing to leave things to others.

"We've been through a lot, but I've never thought you looked more like an Emperor than right now."

"I have just finished a piece of work. In deference to the two at your side, I'll overlook that insolence."

"You know, you really ought to realize that from the outside, you're playing the worst kind of noble-lounging-with-arms-crossed-while-children-fight role."

An Emperor standing by, arms folded, watching children and Undead fight—it's like some grotesque entertainment out of a post-apocalyptic zombie-pandemic world. There are limits to how bad that looks. Still, given Abel's position, role, and combat ability, it's actually a relief for those traveling with him that he doesn't carelessly step to the front—so this too is a mixed blessing. And then—

"Tch, we cleared out the sentries right after entering the Imperial Capital, but… at this rate, this is going to eat up way too much time."

Saying so, spitting on the ground with irritation, is Jamal. Having taken on a swarm of Undead and contributed to their opening victory, he adjusts the position of his eyepatch with a hand still gripping his twin blades, looking around.

"Wouldn't it be faster to just hack apart every Undead that shows its face? Why not do that?"

"…He's a man who just won't listen no matter what, I suppose. If we do that, this Spica's Authority won't be able to give the Undead 'Repose,' I suppose."

"Ah? What the hell is this 'repose' thing?"

"It's the means to make sure that no matter how many times you knock an Undead down, it won't get back up, I suppose! I've explained this over and over by now, haven't I, I suppose!"

Beatrice stamps her small feet on the ground, but her protest doesn't seem to land with Jamal. His blank reaction only reddens Beatrice's face further, but unfortunately, no amount of explanation seems to be worth the effort against Jamal's resistance to anything remotely complicated. And so—

"Jamal Aurélie. Obey them. I have judged this to be the best course."

"If Your Excellency says so, I'll do whatever's needed!"

"Grr, grrrr, I suppose…!"

In the end, Abel, cutting in from the side, gets to claim all the credit, and Beatrice grits her teeth in frustration at Jamal's mercenary about-face. Adorable. Watching this exchange, Subaru relaxes just a little, and Spica, having stopped patting his back, peers up at him.

"Au?"

"Yeah, I'm fine. More importantly, Spica, is anything feeling off with you?"

"U! Aau!"

Asked, Spica makes a determinedly resolute face, puffing out her chest to reassure Subaru that she's in fine shape. Seeing no sign of a lie in that, Subaru clenches his fist, relieved that the "Star Devourer" isn't doing her any harm and that the operation is progressing. At Subaru's profile, Abel narrows his dark eyes.

"Galling as it is, it seems your read was correct."

"I don't get why it'd be galling, but what read?"

"Obviously. The enemy's main force as an army has been directed at the fortress city, where the bulk of their strength remains. What was left in the Imperial Capital is a portion of the Undead and troops unsuited for war."

"…Yeah. What's left behind are the ones who can't work as a group. Every last one of them is annoyingly strong, but in a twisted way, it's a tailwind for us, Imperial-style."

Subaru nods at Abel's assessment of the situation, having observed the Imperial Capital's state himself. As Abel said, the Undead's main force as a great army is besieging the fortress city of Garkla. Holed up in that sturdy urban fortress, drawing the enemy's attention—the more the ones left behind there fight hard, the more the numerical disadvantage here in the Imperial Capital is expected to ease. The essence of this operation is a lightning strike to take the head of the Imperial Capital's ringleader while the fortress city holds the enemy's eyes.

However, if the fortress city's enemy prioritizes "numbers," the Imperial Capital's prioritizes "quality." Starting with the "Cloud Dragon" Mezoreia, which broadly covers the capital's skies, every force left to defend the city is a one-in-a-thousand, an outlier possessed of transcendent power. Which is precisely why—

"—A two-stage feint, via simultaneous attacks on each vertex."

At the words from Subaru's mouth, Abel and Beatrice and the others' expressions tighten. Right now, the group is at the northwest of the Imperial Capital—near the fifth vertex. The star-shaped rampart's massive collapse there is, by report, thanks to Garfiel's efforts during the Battle for the Imperial Capital. There's no telling how much more reliable Garfiel might grow in a short span of time, but the point of entry he carved out—far too large a landmark—was where Subaru & Beatrice with Spica, and the mismatched lord-and-vassal pair of Abel and Jamal, made their entrance. In terms of raw combat power, this seems like an unreliable allocation of forces, but it's the optimal arrangement.

With any combination other than this one, the "Salvation Squad's" operation would fail for certain. That said, even having arrived at this formation, they've merely reached the starting line.

"We're the arrow that matters, and there's no doubt the arrow's destination is that huge crystal palace. If anyone but us goes up against Sphinx, all that happens is she gets away, and it means nothing."

Across long, long years, spanning two nations, Kingdom and Empire, she's a being who's wreaked havoc on a grand scale. If Sphinx, the mastermind behind the "Great Disaster," slips away, there's no telling what catastrophe she'll plan next. To defeat the Undead Sphinx, there is no method but Spica's "Star Devourer." But—

"So, before a bunch of extra obstacles come swarming in, we sprint from here straight to the crystal palace and kill the enemy boss! Then, on His Excellency's word, we take back the Imperial Capital!"

"U'u-!!"

"That's the ideal, but it's not going to be that easy."

"Ahh?" "Aauu?"

Jamal, sword tip aimed toward the crystal palace said to be the enemy's stronghold, is fired up, and Spica echoes his enthusiasm. The two of them, their momentum checked, turn to Subaru, who checked it. But Subaru only shakes his head slowly.

"Going straight for the crystal palace isn't possible. More than that, we can't even carelessly approach the castle."

"You little—don't tell me you've gotten cold feet and…"

"It's clearly not a matter of nerve, I suppose. What's waiting there?"

"—A Curse. Because of it, just getting near the castle renders you unable to move."

Cutting off Jamal's grinding teeth, Subaru answers Beatrice's gaze that way, pressing a hand to his own chest as though recalling an inescapable, unbearable pain. As long as that obstruction exists, no one can approach the castle—more than that, no one can even function properly anywhere in this Imperial Capital.

"Why the hell can you talk like you know all this when you haven't even done it? Huh?"

Naturally, there are those who can't simply swallow Subaru's explanation. Jamal chief among them. Unlike Emilia and Beatrice and the others, there's no trust between him and Subaru such that he'd believe Subaru's reasoning without asking where it came from. And the same could be said of Abel. Between Subaru and Abel, too, there's no trust like what exists with Emilia and the rest. Which is why he—

"On the surface, this act may seem reckless, even roundabout, but there is intent behind it, on your part."

"Your Excellency!?"

"Hold your tongue, Jamal Aurélie. There will not be a third time. Obey them—obey him."

Emperor Vincent Vollachia judges not by trust in Subaru, but by linking Subaru's actions and true intentions to his own strategic acumen. At the Emperor's repeated command, Jamal's single eye widens, and he falls silent, then strikes his own forehead with the hilt of his twin blades. He strikes, and strikes, striking until blood seeps from his brow.

"—Understood. I will never make you say it a third time."

With the hesitation gone from his face, he bows deeply. At Jamal's answer, Vincent gives a slight nod, and Beatrice's eyes flash with anger.

"Why would you go and injure yourself, I suppose!? Heal it this instant, I suppose!"

"Ah!? Damn it, don't go pulling extra crap, shrimp! This is my oath—"

"You've only got the one eye—what'll you do if blood gets into it, I suppose!? It's all people who don't think ahead, and it's Beatrice who has to deal with the trouble, I suppose!"

Squabbling loudly, Beatrice makes quick work of treating the wound on Jamal's forehead. Alongside that exchange, Abel says "Now then," and turns his gaze back to Subaru.

"If we cannot climb to the castle as we are, do we simply wait in silence for someone else to move the hour and carve a path? Surely that is not the case?"

"Way to talk like you're just here to watch the show… Our fight here has meaning too, properly."

In answer to that questioning gaze, Subaru sets a hand on the head of Spica beside him. Looked up at with an "U?" by Spica, the key to this operation, Subaru nods back to her. Charging heedlessly at the crystal palace would be suicide; and yet there's no way for Subaru's side to act directly against that "Curse." As things stand, no one can force their way into the castle. And yet, if they want to confront Sphinx within, there's only one way to do it.

"To that end—"

Just as Subaru said this, a sudden crash of destruction shook everyone's eardrums. Multiple buildings collapsed, the street reshaping itself amid billowing smoke and scattering rubble. As Subaru and the others whipped around toward the sound on reflex, Beatrice and Jamal, mid-argument, moved to shield the non-combatant Subaru and Abel, glaring at the smoke. And there, in the sight line of the wary group, emerging from beyond the smoke, was—

"…Guarding His Excellency sure is never easy."

Seeing the thing emerge from the depths of the smoke, Jamal's face twisted as he muttered that. None of them, Subaru included, could raise any objection to Jamal's words. Great or small, the feeling he'd voiced was shared by every one of them.

"—Ooooohhh"

Dragging its body along with a low, deep, eerie sound like wind rushing through a cavern, the misshapen thing crept forward through the Imperial Capital. It might once have had a human shape, but the number and form of its limbs had strayed so far from anything natural that it looked like the innocent, chaotic scrawl a small child might make drawing their parent for the very first time, brush moving however it pleased.

An abnormally long right arm, branching at its joints, thrashed about like a beast's tail, while a large, stubby left arm gripped the ground and hauled the torso forward. Its legs, by contrast, looked feeble—four of them, yet none functioning, left dangling limp, dragged along by the arms. The thickness of its body varied wildly between chest, belly, and shoulders, an utterly disjointed thing—but in what appeared to be its head, there was just the one enormous golden eye—

"—Subaru."

"Yeah, I know."

Called by name by Beatrice, Subaru nods, not needing to hear the rest. This wasn't the first time he and the others had encountered a being so grotesque and misshapen. A reunion with the very thing they'd thought—this time, at last—they'd finished off in that dust explosion—

"—Ooooohhh!!"

Without even time to wipe away the cold sweat that fact drew from him, the failed Undead thing tore open a mouth like a gash and let out a great, great roar.

△▼△▼△▼△

—And at that same hour, when the Subaru-and-Abel party came face to face with that misshapen being.

Not long after the Battle for the Imperial Capital, Lupugana has once more become a battlefield. The fight over the Empire, though the pieces on the board have shifted, remains fundamentally unchanged in nature—a decisive battle aimed at the head of the ruler of that beautiful castle towering at the Imperial Capital's deepest point, the crystal palace. That holds true not only for the victory condition but for the very conduct of the battle—namely, that the fight over the star-shaped fortress ringing the capital, over its five vertices, will decide the outcome. Therefore—

"Still—still! 'No-Chance Ifluese' is only just getting started, nosebleed dragon!"

Forgetting pain in the heat of battle and the thrill of it, determined to answer an unbroken trust, the boy burns through every ounce of his being to challenge myth itself.

"Let the Observers on high bear witness. —To which side the world shall choose."

Posturing as though singing, smiling as though it were a mask, dancing along the line between what suits him and what doesn't, the young-looking figure of lightning cuts in against the swelling second sun with stubborn refusal to give up.

"—At last. At last, I have been granted the chance to see you again."

Voice carrying a joy she cannot fully hide, the deer-horned girl in a kimono arrives, trailing snow in her wake, to the crisis of the precious person she has long awaited reuniting with.

"I'm not letting you go running off wherever you please anymore. Properly, talk to me, Bal-nii."

Setting her sunlit, cheerful expression into resolve, the girl who has steeled herself for a cruel reunion, borrowing a mage's hand, meets the eyes of the Flying Dragon Rider dancing through the heavens.

And then—

"Well now, what a nasty-tempered caster this is. —No love in it at all."

The black kimono-clad, black-furred, ominous beast, before a curse far more ominous than its own existence, opens its slitted golden eyes.

The decisive battle for the Imperial Capital, the struggle for the five great vertices, the fight to bring down a star, has truly begun.
