Arc 7 Chapter 63: "Sparka"

—Time rewinds, just slightly.

"—From here on, I will have all of you demonstrate whether you have what it takes to become sword slaves of this island."

Dragged out of the healing chamber and forced into a room, Gustav crossed his four sturdy arms and swept his gaze slowly around the room.

That mechanical, cold gaze fell upon four people in total, Subaru included.

A gray-scaled Lizard Human wrapped in filthy rags, a gaunt, bare-chested man with a shaved head tattooed everywhere, and a young man with a rugged face and long, rust-colored hair.

These were the ones crammed into the same room as Subaru.

Judging from what had just been said, in Gustav's eyes, Subaru and the men stood on equal footing.

From the perspective of those thrown together, however, there seemed to be some objections to that idea.

"Wait, Governor Gustav! Are you intending to begin the 'Sparka'? With a child added to our number? This is no joke!"

The rust-haired young man shouted this after seeing Subaru, who'd been shoved through the doorway into the room.

His features were reasonably handsome, but the stubble, the exhaustion in his complexion, and the irritation of his complaining expression cut a good deal off that appeal.

But looks had nothing to do with the situation at hand. As proof, the other two, as if caught up in the young man's heat, also snapped at Gustav.

"That's right! What good's a brat gonna do!? Our lives are on the line here!"

"I heard it was the Governor's policy not to turn the sword bouts into a cruel spectacle... ?"

"Just as these two say. I'll have a satisfactory explanation from you. To begin with, even counting this child, we're short a person. You said it would be a group of five, did you not!"

The Lizard Human, Tattoo, and Rust-hair all raised their voices at once, hurling their anger at Gustav.

For Subaru, who hadn't quite grasped either the nature of the "Sparka" or his relationship to these men, it was a hard situation to speak up in. Still, there was something he could vaguely tell.

It seemed Subaru had betrayed the three men's expectations somehow, and their dissatisfaction over it was being dumped on Gustav.

"...Gustav-san, everyone's saying this, but—"

"I hear it, Schwartz. Unlike my arms, my ears number only two, the same as yours, but I intend for them to fulfill their role fully. As does this head of mine, of which I have only one."

"If that's so—"

"—Be silent. To interrupt my words is tantamount to interrupting the will of His Imperial Majesty."

That single line crushed whatever Rust-hair had been about to say next.

Gustav hadn't particularly changed his expression or the tone of his voice. And yet the weight carried in the meaning of his words was, in that moment, of an entirely different order than before.

Not just Rust-hair, but the other two also fell silent. Seeing this, Gustav nodded.

"Good, then I'll continue. As declared, your 'Sparka' will now begin. The audience consists already of the sword slaves who reside on this island; there are no outside visitors present. This also serves as a touchstone to determine whether you possess the ability to be entrusted with entertaining an important guest."

"Audience..."

Feeling an unpleasant premonition, Subaru murmured this quietly.

As things stood, the fake Cecilus—who'd been with Subaru right up until he was shoved into this room—had said as they parted, "Do your best! I'll be watching from up above!"

Subaru hadn't confirmed what that meant, but now he could pretty much guess.

"Don't tell me... you're planning to make us fight..."

"Hah! Seriously, what a clueless brat. If that were the case, we'd be overjoyed to have someone like you mixed in with us!"

"Ugh..."

The moment he voiced the thought, the Lizard Human stuck out his long tongue and mocked him for it.

But there was a point to what he said. If the "Sparka" meant the people gathered here fighting each other, a child joining in would be more than welcome.

Rather, what they were worried about was that Subaru would be useless as a combatant—

"So instead, all of us together are being made to fight something?"

"Smart little brat. I've got a short fuse. Shut up for a bit..."

Instead of a gentle "correct" for his answer, Subaru got a bloodshot glare and a warning from Tattoo.

In any case, it seemed team play was starting off from the worst possible place. Not that it was a situation where he could afford to crack jokes about it.

"Just what do you intend to have us fight?"

"I won't say a trained sword slave. There'd be no contest between you and them. That's all I can tell you. —For your enemy does not always appear beneath the light of day."

"—? A riddle? How tasteless."

Rust-hair replied to Gustav's words, twisting the corner of his mouth.

Then he let out a small breath and continued, "And so—"

"Even if I'm forced into silence, I'll have you answer me this much. If this child is the fourth, what becomes of the fifth? Our unit—"

"Your unit's fifth member remains asleep in the healing chamber. As long as that person does not wake, you will face the 'Sparka' with only these members."

"Are you in your right mind, Governor...!"

"If it is necessary to align with His Imperial Majesty's will, I will make use of sanity or madness alike."

At Gustav's cold answer, Rust-hair looked away, realizing this was going nowhere. Then his gaze turned toward Subaru, and he called out, "You there."

Subaru stiffened his cheeks at the man's fierce manner and the sudden address.

"W-what..."

"From what I can see, you look like nothing more than a scrawny child, but what can you do? Any special skills? Magic? Do you possess some useful strength of any kind?"

"U-useful skills..."

Peppered with questions, Subaru's eyes darted about in a panic.

Unfortunately, since shrinking he didn't feel he could properly handle a whip, and as for magic, without his adorable Beatrice he was useless. As for other small odds and ends of tricks, those too were unusable properly now that he'd shrunk.

Unable to say anything, Subaru fell silent. Seeing this, Rust-hair pressed a hand to his forehead.

"Are you truly just an ordinary child... good grief."

he muttered, in a voice thick with disappointment.

"Hee, hee hee, on top of having a brat, we're short-handed too. It's over, over! O-ver!"

"Don't raise your voice, scale-boy. You want to make me angry and die...?"

"Huh? Can't hear you, your voice is too small!"

The exchange between Subaru and Rust-hair must have thoroughly displeased them, because the Lizard Human and Tattoo now launched into a full-blown fight of their own. Glaring at each other, the two looked ready to start throwing punches any moment. Rust-hair frantically cut in with a "Wait!"

"What good does it do for us to fight each other! I understand feeling driven to despair, but listen! Before coming here, I was a warrior in service to a person of noble standing! If you follow my instructions—"

"Shut up! A warrior, were you? Then why the hell are you here! It's 'cause you lost and got taken prisoner, ain't it! That's bad luck, that is!"

"There's no folly greater than currying favor with others and entrusting them with your life. No one controls my fate."

"Y-you bastards...!"

But the Lizard Human and Tattoo paid no heed whatsoever to Rust-hair's interjecting words.

On top of that, being talked back to by both of them, Rust-hair turned red in the face and began to fume. Subaru had no idea what awaited them from here, but it was certainly not a good situation.

"G-Gustav-san..."

"Schwartz, there is nothing more I can say. My intention has already been conveyed. Even to the last drop, the blood spilled on this island of sword slaves shall be used for the prosperity of the Empire. To the last drop."

"...Even said again, I don't get what that means."

As the three men grappled with each other, Subaru, left out of it, couldn't grasp the meaning of Gustav's words.

Even as he looked down upon the four of them, placed in such a hopeless situation, Gustav made no attempt to say anything more.

And then—

△▼△▼△▼△

—With relations among the four at their absolute worst, the fateful "Sparka" began.

"..."

A circular field entirely enclosed by tall walls—this was the stage for sword slaves known as the arena.

Beyond the walls were tiered spectator seats, scattered figures filling them here and there, and Subaru could sense a quiet fervor hanging over the whole venue.

What was eerie, though, was how quiet they were—these people looking down on the arena, who, according to Gustav's explanation, would be sword slaves themselves.

Subaru had assumed, in his own arbitrary image of things, that an audience at a place like this would be crudely rowdy.

But perhaps that mismatch of expectation couldn't be helped.

After all, it was Gustav who managed and ruled this isolated island. —Subaru couldn't imagine that inflexible-looking giant of a man tolerating any crude behavior from the sword slaves.

"Feels like you'd get severely punished just for running in the hallway."

Subaru said, averting his eyes from reality while putting up as brave a front as he could manage.

If he didn't, he felt he might force his trembling knees into flight right then and there.

—Away from the eyes of the Witchbeast slowly revealing itself beyond the opening iron gate.

"..."

The lion-faced Witchbeast advancing forward—its garish form was one Subaru recognized.

It was the huge Witchbeast that the girl with the braids had once brought to the mansion, the very one whose parts had, memorably, become the material for Subaru's whip. Its name didn't come to him right away, though.

"A l-lion...?"

"—GRAAAHHH!!"

As if answering Subaru's call, the Witchbeast—the arena beast, the lion—roared. The roar echoed through the arena, and Subaru felt the sound reverberate all the way through his own body.

An arena beast bigger even than Gustav—just seeing it from a distance was terrifying enough.

"Unless we bring that thing down, none of us will make it out alive."

Glaring at the lion, the rust-haired man said this in a bitter voice.

Yes, that was it exactly. That was the condition for ending this "Sparka"—the impossible task that Subaru and these three had to accomplish together.

It was a terrifying task with no visible chance of victory anywhere.

"Damn it, damn it, why am I stuck in a mess like this...!"

"Enough with the whining already! Put everything into finding a way to survive! Look at that!"

"Huh?"

The Lizard Human, hands over his face, lamented as though it were the end of the world. Slapping his shoulder, Rust-hair pointed at something visible at the two far ends of the arena—from where Subaru and the others stood diagonally opposite the lion, it was to the left and right of the venue.

There, standing upright in each spot, something could be seen—

"—Are those... swords?"

"T-that's how it looks to me too...!"

When Subaru agreed with Tattoo's words, he was met with a cold, sharp glare. But even without gaining an ally's agreement, what he'd seen wasn't going to vanish like smoke.

What stood planted there were, indeed, two swords.

"They wouldn't leave us without weapons just to make us idle feed for the arena beast. Securing a weapon comes first, above all else. That said, only the right-hand sword is usable."

"...Right."

Tattoo nodded in agreement with Rust-hair's words. Subaru, thinking he'd be glared at if he spoke, said nothing, but he shared the same opinion.

After all, the sword on the right was small, but the one on the left was far too large.

The left-hand sword, in fact, was large enough to make one suspect it was meant for Gustav's use, its blade thick enough that swinging it around seemed utterly impossible.

Even if Subaru hadn't shrunk, it looked too enormous for him to even lift.

Probably neither Rust-hair nor Tattoo could use that oversized sword either.

So the only usable weapon was the smaller sword. If only they'd had a member capable of wielding that huge blade—that would surely have been reassuring.

"Given its sheer size, our only option is to get our hands on a weapon and aim for the arena beast's eye. Scramble the contents of its skull and bring it down. That is our one and only path to victory."

"...Can you use a sword?"

"I told you. I was a warrior. I can use one far better than the likes of you."

Rust-hair answered with confidence, and Tattoo closed one eye, considering. Apparently forcibly taking that silence as agreement, Rust-hair turned to Subaru and the Lizard Human.

"Once the declaration to begin is made, I'll head for that sword. In the meantime, don't let the arena beast get near me while I'm defenseless."

"D-don't let it get near, but how?"

"Make noise, draw its attention. Figure that much out yourself!"

"O-okay, fine..."

Snapped at forcefully by Rust-hair, Subaru nodded obediently.

It was a fairly terrifying and reckless plan, but no other method came to mind. And if Rust-hair really was as skilled with a sword as he claimed, they could defeat the lion.

They could end this terrifying "Sparka" and then think about what to do next.

So—

"—Now then, the 'Sparka' begins!!"

With Gustav's thick, rough declaration, the released lion crouched low, readying itself.

Overwhelmed by its menace, Subaru drew a breath to draw the arena beast's attention, buying time until Rust-hair could secure the weapon—

"Guaaah!?"

"—!?"

Before he could raise his voice, he was struck speechless at the sight of Rust-hair collapsing to the ground right beside him.

Rust-hair, who was supposed to dash off the instant Gustav gave the declaration, was lying on the ground. That alone was shocking, but what shocked Subaru even more was that his fallen face got kicked.

The one who'd done it was Tattoo—supposedly an ally.

He'd tripped Rust-hair as he tried to run, then kicked him down after he fell. Then, after watching Rust-hair sprawl flat on his back, he said—

"I told you. I have no intention of entrusting my life to others..."

Saying this, Tattoo took off running with a burst of speed, heading for the smaller sword.

At this sudden turn of events, Subaru's eyes went wide too, and he floundered, not knowing what to do—

"I-I want no part of this! Dying—I'm not gonna die, damn it!"

"H-hey!?"

The Lizard Human shrieked in a cracked voice and turned his back on the lion, fleeing. He plastered himself against the arena wall and slowly began changing the color of his scales.

The gray scales blended into the color of the wall, his large body becoming shockingly hard to spot.

One had outmaneuvered his companion, one had plastered himself to the wall, one had been done in by his companion, and the last, confused by the whole situation, could only stare wide-eyed in a panic.

—It was, without question, the worst possible start.

"W-what should I..."

Subaru wondered, face pale, looking toward the lion.

What impression had the arena beast formed, seeing this group with zero unity? Or perhaps, the instant it turned around, its fangs might pierce Subaru—he cringed at the thought.

However—

"..."

The start had been declared, and Subaru expected the arena beast would surely come toy with these fools. But the lion itself remained utterly motionless, still in the same forward-leaning stance it had first taken.

As if the declaration to begin had never been made at all.

Thinking this, Subaru realized—that his impression was exactly right.

"D-don't—!"

He reached out a hand, trying instinctively to call out, but the words wouldn't follow.

After all, Subaru didn't know his name. He couldn't call out a name he didn't know, and even if he called out to someone unknown, how much power could it possibly hold?

So there was no strength in Subaru's voice, and he couldn't stop what happened next.

"Got it—with this..."

Having outmaneuvered his companions and sprinted with all his speed, Tattoo reached the sword planted in the arena floor.

He smirked, pulled the sword from the ground, and tested its feel in his hand. How much courage did that cold metal's feel and weight give a man who'd had nothing?

Turning around with a flourish, his face all but declared he had no equal.

"—!!"

That face of his was sent flying without mercy by the lion's swung forepaw.

The sharp claws sent everything above Tattoo's neck flying off, and from the body—too slow to realize it had died—blood gushed out afterward in a spray.

"Hh—"

Subaru's throat froze at the sight of that horrifying scene.

At the same time, he cursed himself from the bottom of his heart for being too slow to realize.

The lion had risen the very instant Tattoo gripped the sword's hilt.

That had been the true starting signal, as far as the lion was concerned. Until that signal came, Subaru and the others had surely still been allowed time to plan.

And they, in their panic and anxiety, had squandered it.

"—!!"

With a high metallic clang the sword fell to the ground, and Tattoo's headless body toppled backward. Indifferent to the death of such prey, the lion let out a great roar and began charging furiously on its four powerful legs—straight toward the arena wall.

"Stop, stop stop stop stop, don't come, don't come, don't come!!"

How the lion had found the location of the Lizard Human camouflaged against the wall, Subaru had no idea. But whatever the case, there was no doubt that the Lizard Human's own scream and flight, unable to bear the approaching lion, sealed his fate.

"—!!"

The lion slammed head-first into the wall, its ferocious headbutt crushing the lower half of the Lizard Human, too slow to escape, and his insides burst out like a frog run over by a car.

"Gueh."

The organs disgorged from his crushed mouth were, properly, red and pink in color, and Subaru thought vaguely that even though the outside had disguised itself in the wall's color, the inside had stayed just as it was.

Even as Subaru thought this, it was clear beyond doubt that the crushed Lizard Human had died.

And then—

"What a... what a foolish thing to do."

Shaking his head, Rust-hair, having witnessed both deaths, let this spill out weakly.

In his own way, he'd surely been desperate to pool their strength and survive this situation. And yet, outmaneuvered by one companion and abandoned by another, that possibility had vanished entirely.

Rust-hair, too, surely didn't think Subaru capable of what he'd expected from Tattoo or the Lizard Human.

And even if he had been expected to, Subaru himself didn't think he could live up to it.

Perhaps that voice in Subaru's heart had gotten through to him, from just looking into his eyes.

"...Stay behind me."

When one realizes a situation is hopeless, how many people can put up a brave front in the face of the end?

Unable to get his hands on a weapon and having lost his companions, all the rust-haired man could do, perhaps, was wish to meet his final moment with at least some dignity.

Shielding the child Subaru behind his back, he glared at the lion as it turned toward them.

Receiving that gaze, the lion gave a low growl, then kicked off the ground with its powerful legs, kicking up dust as it charged toward Subaru and the man.

"Uh, uhh...!"

Everything had happened in the blink of an eye, and Subaru's mind couldn't keep up at all.

If some reliable companion were here, would he have thought of some way to manage?

Rui, Medium, Flop, Taritta, Al, Abel, Mizelda, Holly, Kuna, Utakata, Rem—

If any of them, any companion, were here, could something have been done?

"I..."

Not here, of all places, Subaru tried to wring out in a trembling voice.

But he couldn't. Not because the lion reached them before he could get the words out—

It was because his shoulder was grabbed and he was forcibly shoved forward.

"No, I don't want to die, I don't want to die—!!"

Screaming, the rust-haired man shoved Subaru out in front of the lion.

Eyes wide, there wasn't even time to voice resentment. Subaru simply took the full brunt of the lion's charge and his body went flying, light as a leaf.

"..."

What a fragile body it was.

A single blow from the arena beast, and Subaru's body was reduced to pieces just like that. His limbs weren't torn off, but everything inside was shattered to bits.

Bones and organs alike, everything broke as if he'd been hit by a truck, turning to mush.

"Help me! I was wrong! It was a lie! The whole warrior thing was a complete lie! I was just trying to make myself look good! Let me out! Let me out, please!!"

Spinning through the air, and below him, Rust-hair leapt at the wall, wailing. What he was desperately appealing to was Gustav, up on the wall.

But Gustav, even looking at the man wailing with snot dripping from his nose, didn't so much as twitch an eyebrow, his expression utterly flat.

Perhaps he'd known about Rust-hair's lie all along.

Or perhaps he simply had no interest whatsoever in whether it was a lie or the truth.

Either way, nothing could be done about it.

"Hel—"

Rust-hair, clinging to the wall in appeal, was struck by the lion's forepaw as it charged in from directly behind him, and was crushed against the wall like a tomato thrown at it.

Tattoo, the Lizard Human, Rust-hair—all of them had fallen before the arena beast.

"—Su, Su-bassu, can you hear me?"

Suddenly, amid the noisy ringing in his ears, a voice reached him, and Subaru moved his neck limply.

Only then did he realize that he, sent flying, had at some point fallen to the ground and rolled there. Realizing it now didn't help matters, though.

And the voice had come from atop the wall, where a figure looked down at Subaru rolling on the ground.

A boy with blue hair tied back, wearing an infuriatingly punchable face.

"Sorry, seems I misjudged this one. I thought you could pull it off, but I guess it's just not the same as my father after all. Looks like I don't have an eye for people!"

"Ah, aah..."

"For what it's worth, is there anything you'd like passed along to that girl traveling with you, once she wakes up? I figured that much of a message would be the proper thing to do, so this seemed like the moment for it!"

Watching Subaru groan, the fake Cecilus's attitude didn't change one bit.

Come to think of it, whether when Subaru woke up, or when they talked in the healing chamber, or now like this with Subaru on the very verge of dying, his attitude hadn't changed in the slightest.

That thoroughly abnormal view of life and death—even though the fake Cecilus was supposed to be a boy who cried wolf—felt, oddly, like an element that made him seem a little like the real thing.

If that were so, it was because he was the strongest general in this Vollachian Empire.

"—I..."

"Eh? What was that? Su-bassu, since this is the end, please speak up! I think it'd be a shame beyond regret if I got the message wrong."

"I... hate it..."

"..."

Subaru declared this to the fake Cecilus, who had a hand cupped to his ear, straining to catch Subaru's voice.

At those words the fake Cecilus raised his eyebrows. Subaru pushed a little more sound up from the back of his throat.

"The Empire... I hate it. I really hate it."

"—Yes, yes, indeed. I'll be sure to remember that and pass it along properly, Su-bassu."

Subaru cursed the fake Cecilus, who took in his resentful words yet wore a face as though it were somebody else's problem entirely.

But whether that curse would bring down the Empire was something Subaru had no way to confirm.

Because—

"—!!"

The arena beast, having saved him for last of the four who fought it, came at Subaru, opened its great maw, and swallowed him whole from the head down without mercy.

"—!"

His bones were already all in pieces, and yet the lion's fangs tore evenly through his body regardless.

The one saving grace amid the misfortune was that his body was already mangled well past the point of feeling pain, so being bitten or shredded only registered as vaguely unpleasant.

He tried, at least, to lodge himself stubbornly in its throat, but he couldn't manage even that.

Because the final bite crushed the very head that had been scheming to do it.

△▼△▼△▼△

"Unless we bring that thing down, none of us will make it out alive."

He'd just been jostled about for a while, surrounded by squirming reddish-black flesh.

A voice, sounding like an ambush, pulled Subaru back to reality. Except it was a reality so hopeless that even awake, he wanted to mistake it for a nightmare.

The quiet fervor that felt oppressively loud, and the dry sand-covered ground of the arena.

Right beside him were his companions—no, just people caught in the same circumstance—each scheming in their own way to protect their own life above all else against the worst moment now approaching.

If he didn't think of it that way, he couldn't possibly accept what had just happened.

"Uwah, aah, aaahh...!"

He shouldn't have, but he thought about it anyway.

Because of that, his whole body remembered the arena beast's charge, the terror of being chewed apart, the reckless fury at reality.

Once he remembered, he could barely stand.

"Tch, a child after all, is it... argh, forget it! You two, look at that!"

"Huh?"

Ignoring the crouching Subaru, Tattoo and the Lizard Human followed Rust-hair's instruction and looked at the large and small swords laid out.

Naturally, their attention gathered on the smaller sword. The unusable large one was ignored, and securing the small one and defeating the arena beast were set as their plan.

"...Can you use a sword?"

"I told you. I was a warrior. I can use one far better than the likes of you."

The same story he'd heard before continued flatly, and anxiety began to seethe and burn inside Subaru.

But it was a statement he couldn't let slide, not while trapped in anxiety and fear.

"—That's a lie."

"...What?"

Suddenly, Subaru's voice cut into the men's war council.

The moment he heard it, the rust-haired man's tone changed. He turned and looked down at the crouching Subaru. Still on his knees, Subaru stared fixedly up at the man's face.

And then he repeated it once more.

"That warrior thing—it's a lie. You're telling a complete lie!"

"W-what grounds do you have...! Do you mean to insult me just because you're a child!?"

"Which one of us is doing the insulting! You're the one lying to make yourself look impressive—that's way more of an insult! What's the point of putting on airs in a situation like this!"

"Gh, guh...!"

Perhaps thinking a loud shout would silence the child, Rust-hair instead visibly floundered when Subaru pushed back and bit right into him.

That degree of shaken composure must have been visible to Tattoo, who'd been listening in.

"...Oi, is what that brat's saying true? Are you saying you're not a warrior, and you tried to deceive me?"

"W-wait, that's just a child's nonsense!? At a time when our lives are on the line, over something so trivial..."

"Precisely because our lives are on the line, convince me..."

Tattoo grabbed Rust-hair by the collar, his skull-tattooed face twisting with rage. At that fierce manner, Rust-hair swallowed hard, gasping low and breathless.

Because Subaru had spoken up without thinking, this fight had broken out. Looking over, he saw the Lizard Human, not joining in the fight, edging steadily backward, already preparing to flee.

Even though it was Subaru's interjection that had triggered it, the team was once again falling apart, disintegrating in midair.

And moreover, whatever state Subaru and the others were in—

"—Now then, the 'Sparka' begins!!"

Gustav was not the sort to postpone the declaration that the battle had begun.

"You, do you have some explanation for—"

"Uh, aaaaahh—!!"

The thick voice rang out proclaiming the war's start, and immediately after, a man's cracked voice tore through the arena.

Looking over, the one screaming was Tattoo, who'd been pressing in on Rust-hair. It seemed the hand gripping the collar had been bitten, its fingers torn clean off.

Rust-hair, his mouth stained with blood, shoved the screaming Tattoo's chest away and ran for the sword.

"I-I want no part of this! Dying—I'm not gonna die, damn it!"

And so, opposite to Rust-hair running for the sword, the Lizard Human, just as before, plastered himself against the arena wall and began his camouflage, changing the color of his scales.

Tattoo, having lost two fingers, crouched clutching his right hand, and Subaru, facing a situation even worse than before, felt his knees shaking uncontrollably.

"You did something unnecessary...! If you'd just obediently done as I said!"

Ignoring Subaru and the others, Rust-hair, eyes bloodshot, reached the small sword.

Cursing both the fact his lie had been seen through and the fact Tattoo had grabbed at him, he reached out, pulled the sword from the ground, and readied it with all the air of a warrior.

"—!!"

Right then, having received the "begin" signal, the lion pounced with furious speed.

What happened was almost identical to when Tattoo had taken the sword moments before. Rust-hair had no time even to swing his readied sword before he took the arena beast's forepaw and had both arms blown off.

Still gripping the sword tightly, both his arms were torn off at the elbow and upper arm, and the instant Rust-hair, eyes wide, let out a thin "Ah," the lion's ferocious body-slam sent the now-armless man flying, smashing him headfirst into the wall.

The rust-haired man became a stain on the wall, and the roaring arena beast, ignoring Subaru and Tattoo, charged furiously on toward the wall behind the arena.

"Stop, stop stop stop stop, don't come, don't come, don't come!!"

At the menace of the charging lion, the Lizard Human, who'd gone to the trouble of disguising himself as the wall, screamed.

Having given away his position by screaming, the Lizard Human frantically tried to shift position, but it was too late. The lion's charge crushed his lower body, forcing his organs out in a fatal wound.

And then the lion, having killed two of them in the blink of an eye, slowly turned toward Subaru and Tattoo, letting out a blood-scented growl.

"Hh..."

"Damn it, to keep drawing this bad a hand, all the way to this point..."

His brain, numbed, had its paralyzed thoughts slowly start working again. Because of that, Subaru's body remembered its fear—his teeth chattered, his insides clenched tight.

Just as the teeth-grinding Tattoo said, it had turned into a result so hopeless it was like stacking the worst on the worst and drawing the worst yet again.

Calling Rust-hair a liar had accomplished nothing at all.

Exposing Rust-hair's lie back there had done no good whatsoever, and yet Subaru had done it without thinking. Because of that, everything had turned into a mess.

"...Do you still have the will to live, brat?"

"Eh..."

"If you've got the will to live, pick up the sword."

Tattoo said this to Subaru, whose vision had gone blurry, on the verge of sobbing.

Looking over, Tattoo was pounding the ground with his bleeding right hand, breaking out in a cold sweat as he pushed himself up. He jerked his chin to indicate the fallen sword—the one lying together with Rust-hair's torn-off arm.

"I'll draw that monster's attention... You pick up the sword and throw it to me."

"B-but..."

"Even short two fingers, I can still handle it better than a brat like you..."

Put that plainly, Subaru had nothing he could say back.

It was called the "smaller" sword only in comparison to the other, larger one, but it was still long and heavy enough that handling it with a child's body would be difficult. Just as Tattoo said, even if Subaru swung it with both hands, he'd likely end up thrown around by its weight and length instead.

Still, if asked whether Tattoo holding the sword could actually defeat the lion, Subaru—knowing how he'd died last time—couldn't help but think that was absolutely not the case either.

And yet—

"I don't... want to give up either..."

"...You're better than the fraud and the coward."

Enduring the pain, Tattoo twisted his lips, welcoming Subaru's resolve.

Still like that, Tattoo glared at the lion, which was carefully observing them, and while steadily widening the distance from Subaru, said—

"Weitz..."

"...Eh?"

"That's my name, Schwartz."

Called "Schwartz," Subaru's eyes widened in surprise.

He wondered where he'd heard that name, but the only one who called Subaru "Schwartz" was Gustav. So Tattoo—Weitz—had remembered the name from that first meeting.

Of course, that in itself didn't help matters, and Subaru knew perfectly well that Weitz had, last time, pulled that cowardly move to outmaneuver Rust-hair.

He knew it, but—

"—Go!"

When Weitz shouted this, it became reason enough for Subaru not to hesitate in trusting it and taking off running.

"..."

Holding back the urge to scream with all his might, Subaru ran across the arena floor at full speed.

Naturally, he was slow. A child's body had short limbs, and his organs, shrunken with fear and tension, ached terribly. Even without that, he suffered often enough from lack of strength—this body was just too much.

Even so, he ran desperately. Ran and ran and ran, and reached the sword.

"Uh, ghh...!"

Rust-hair's severed hands were still attached to the sword, and just the sight of it nearly made him retch. But this was no time to be sick.

He pried the stiff fingers off one by one, forcibly detaching Rust-hair's hands from the sword.

Then, breathing hard, Subaru hoisted up the sword.

"There, it's off!"

Letting out a great breath, Subaru raised the sword he'd taken up. Then he turned around, meaning to throw it to Weitz—

"Schwartz!!"

Weitz's desperate voice made every fine hair on Subaru's body stand on end.

As Subaru turned, the lion, jaws wide open, filled his entire field of vision, closing in. In that instant, feeling like a deer about to be eaten, the lion's movements seemed to slow into slow motion.

"..."

The terrifying face of the lion, drawing near, slowly.

It looked like he should be able to dodge it, but because his own movements watching it were also in slow motion, he couldn't escape at all. Still, the motion of throwing the raised sword continued.

At the very least, he wanted to finish throwing the sword before the lion's attack landed.

—Hurry, hurry, hurry hurry hurry hurry hurry!!

Amid the maddening slowness, Subaru's inner voice screamed desperately, pouring his wish into his arm and fingers.

Whether it got through or not, the sword finally left Subaru's hand, spinning vertically end over end as it slipped past the charging lion and flew toward Weitz.

Weitz, reaching out with his hand missing fingers, was shouting something at Subaru, who was about to be attacked by the lion. But Subaru couldn't make out what the shout was. It didn't matter if he couldn't.

As long as Weitz could make good use of that thrown sword, that was all that mattered.

"Uh, waaaaahh—!!"

The instant he felt he'd fulfilled his role, the slowed-down world returned to normal.

The roaring lion lunged in, and Subaru, screaming, fell backward onto his rear, certain he was about to be torn apart by the arena beast's claws or fangs. Tears and snot burst out, streaking his face, and Subaru resigned himself to the fact that his dying expression was going to be a truly pathetic one.

And yet—

"—Eh?"

The lion's attack, which should have killed Subaru, never landed.

Because the lion drove its forepaw into the ground right in front of Subaru's face to kill its momentum, and then, in the same motion, snapped its hind leg upward, driving it into Weitz right behind it.

"Buh."

Weitz, having caught the sword Subaru threw and struck from behind the lion, had his tattoo-covered face shattered to pieces by the lion's hind hoof.

Just like before, everything above his neck was blown off once again, and Weitz died. The raised sword slipped from his grip and rolled uselessly across the distant ground.

"—!!"

And then, slowly, the lion that had killed Weitz turned its attention to Subaru.

There was no softness there that would let him go—only sheer, ferocious murderous intent.

"Su-bassu! Su-bassu! Sorry, but do you think you can turn things around from there?"

From behind, calling out from the arena's spectator seats, was the fake Cecilus, who'd been watching this unsightly battle. Looking down at Subaru sprawled on the ground, he said—

"You know, the kind of thing where a hidden power awakens, or a companion's tragic death unseals some forbidden technique! That sort of thing! Any chance of that? Or not so much?"

"...No, nothing like that."

"Hmm, right, I figured. Sorry, seems I misjudged this one! Anything you'd like passed along to that girl traveling with you? I figured that much would be the proper thing to do!"

Said in the same breezy tone as at his moment of death before, Subaru let out a huff of a breath. What was different from before was that his bones weren't in pieces yet.

Even if he might end up in pieces after this, even if he was scared to death, he still wasn't dead yet.

"Ceci, let me ask you just one thing."

"A question, not a message? A sort of parting gift for the afterlife, so to speak? Whether my answer measures up to Su-bassu's expectations here is truly a test of a leading man's shine—"

"That lion—why didn't it kill me just now?"

"—Hmm."

Pointing at the arena beast in front of him, Subaru asked this of the fake Cecilus up on the wall.

Whether a proper answer worth having would come back from this boy-who-cried-wolf, Subaru couldn't say. He simply consoled himself that, as a last desperate struggle, it wasn't a bad one.

To Subaru's question, the fake Cecilus stroked his own thin chin and said—

"That's simple, Su-bassu. —However ferocious it may be, a beast is a beast. And so, a beast hunts only by instinct. And instinct is, in other words, the will to live."

"...What does that mean?"

"If you were that beast, Su-bassu, who would you target first?"

The riddle-like phrasing wouldn't let Subaru settle into a simple exchange of question and answer.

Irritated, Subaru thought over the meaning of the fake Cecilus's words. If he were that lion, just who would he target first?

And if he did target someone, what reason would there be for it—

"—Ah."

Suddenly, it came to him. So that was it, he thought, everything falling into place.

And then—

"When that girl wakes up, I'll tell her Su-bassu was brave. I'm bad at lying, so I'm glad you actually were brave!"

That thoughtless voice reached him, but there was no time to talk back.

Toward Subaru, eyes wide with understanding, the lion standing before him brought its forepaw down.

A crunching sound—the sound of himself being crushed—Subaru heard properly for the first time.

△▼△▼△▼△

"Unless we bring that thing down, none of us will make it out alive."

His head crushed, his shoulder crushed, his body crushed, his backside crushed—everything crushed.

Slowly, taking in the fact that a human body, struck by force and speed beyond what it could endure, broke apart just like that—Subaru experienced it as though it were happening to someone else.

Of course, it truly couldn't be someone else's business.

If it could have been, the suffering would have been so much less—the pain and fear kept to a bare minimum. And yet, the fact that Subaru could even think this way at all owed a great deal to his experience in Chaosflame.

—Those ten hellish seconds.

Because of that, Subaru understood, down to his very bones, the importance of even a mere few seconds.

Because he understood it down to his bones—

"Damn it, damn it, why am I stuck in a mess like this...!"

"Enough with the whining already! Put everything into finding a way to survive! Look at—"

Right beside him, the Lizard Human and Rust-hair were desperately struggling to accept the situation before them.

Listening to this conversation he already knew by heart, Subaru shouted.

"Weitz! Hold him down!"

"What...?"

The instant Subaru shouted this, Tattoo—Weitz's—tattooed face twisted in bewilderment.

Naturally. Even though he'd never once given his name, Subaru knew it. And even having his name called, he could hardly be expected to simply do as some uncanny child told him.

But neither the bewildered Weitz, nor Rust-hair and the Lizard Human—startled by Subaru's sudden shout—had time to stop what Subaru did next.

And then—

"—Lion! Who are you targeting!?"

Subaru's small hand, shouting this, forcibly seized the sword planted in the ground.

In that instant, without waiting for Gustav's command, the arena beast roared, its legs kicked off the ground, and it charged furiously, furiously at Subaru—

'If you were that beast, Su-bassu, who would you target first?'

The fake Cecilus's riddle-like words were seared into the back of Subaru's mind, along with the pain of it.

Yes, that was it. If Subaru were the lion, he'd target "the dangerous one" first. In this place, for instance, that meant whoever was holding a weapon.

"—!!"

Facing the roaring, charging lion, Subaru clenched his back teeth. There was a fear that made his very organs tremble, but he would not lose, would not flee, would not even cry.

He'd already been killed twice—no matter how many more times he had to face this terror.

"Don't think that just 'cause I'm a kid, I'll cry and give up right away!!"

In cooperation with the cheat, the coward, and the fraud, he would fight this "Sparka" through to the end — with everything he had, at full power, body and soul.

—At the moment of his resolve, the lion's ferocious forepaw came crashing down, violently blasting away the life of Natsuki Subaru's third attempt.
