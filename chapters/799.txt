Arc 10 Chapter 39: "Let's Do This Together"

It had been a while since his last 'Door Crossing,' but the sensation hardly stirred any nostalgia.
To begin with, this spatial transfer through a door scarcely felt like crossing through space at all. Just as when walking into or out of an ordinary room, a single stride carried him across the connection between spaces.
So the breath that escaped Subaru's lips this time was no expression of fondness for 'Door Crossing.' It was his reaction to looking around the room he had entered.

"This is... exactly the kind of weird room a Witch would like."

It was an oddly constructed room, with windows running all the way around its walls. A spacious one, probably at the very top of the castle—the blue sky stretching beyond the windows, and the white clouds drifting below them, gave him a rough idea of where they were. The view outside was not that otherworldly space with its endless fall, still fresh in Subaru's memory. It was the sky of the outside world itself.
In other words, proof that Tiga had not been lying.

"The floating castle, Kugelschreiber, huh... Getting a flying vehicle always makes it feel like you're finally in the last stretch of an RPG. Don't you think?"

"No idea what you're talking about, but it sounds pretty unimportant. Never mind that. Look."

"—Subaru!"

Responding coolly to Subaru's wonder, Ferris pointed straight ahead. Beatrice came pattering toward them from the far end of the room, the hem of her dress fluttering.
With Crusch on his back, Subaru caught her against his chest as she leaped straight at him. He somehow kept his footing, relieved to see that she appeared unharmed.

"Beako, I'm glad you're all right. That's a load off my mind."

"That is Betty's line, I suppose! Getting separated like that, and then running off without listening to Betty—I was terribly worried, in fact!"

"That wasn't my fault. That was the enemy messing with us... Anyway, we all made it through in one piece, somehow. Anything happen on your end...? Well, obviously it did."

"Indeed, I suppose. —We moved the castle, in fact."

With both hands on her hips, Beatrice nodded smugly. Her proud little expression was adorable, but Subaru could not fathom how their earlier predicament had led to moving the castle.
So, much as a flying castle appealed to his sense of adventure, he had plenty of questions about that decision—

"After consulting with 'Librarian,' I made the decision to move the castle. There was a risk that the enemy had taken control of our entrance and was lying in ambush."

Answering Subaru's unspoken question in Beatrice's place was Russell, standing in the center of the room.
There, in the middle of the spacious room, stood a round pedestal that several adults could gather around. A strange black sphere floated above it, and Russell held his hand over it.
It was quite the fantasy contraption. Apparently unable to leave it, he waited as Subaru and the others approached him instead.

"What's carved into this pedestal... is that a world map?"

"Yes, that appears to be the general idea. Though it was carved some time ago, so there are many differences from the world as it is today. It poses no great inconvenience, however."

Peering at the round pedestal, Subaru saw a relief map of the world carved into its surface—one that, just as Russell had said, appeared to depict an earlier age.
Considering the history of Kugelschreiber Castle, the map was probably more than four hundred years old. A growing urge to bring a current map and compare the two welled up inside him, but—

"That can wait... Russell-san, what was that about an ambush?"

"Please call me 'Silver Fox.' —It was a natural inference. A transfer route whose value has fallen considerably even in my estimation, and the 'Beloved Children' who had infiltrated the castle... It would be less reasonable to assume there were only a few of them."

"Probably some bastard got impatient for the credit and tried to jump the gun, huh? There's always someone who can't keep in step with the rest. And from what I've seen, none of those 'Beloved Children' have much team spirit to begin with."

Following up on Russell's answer, Rachins glanced at Melty and 'Black Dog' beside him.
Subaru would have liked to defend them: former 'Beloved Children' or not, that was an unfair assessment. But leaving Melty aside, there was little he could say to excuse 'Black Dog's' lack of team spirit.

"Even so, the bonds we've built over these two months let us drive the enemy off. Right, 'Black Dog'?"

"Bonds? Hey, hey, don't say gross shit that makes me wanna puke! By sheer, miraculous, total coincidence! We just happen to hate the same people, thaaat's aaall!"

"See? As you can tell, we're getting along fine. Lots of the others won't listen to reason at all."

Subaru shrugged at 'Black Dog,' who was giving him a thumbs-down with both hands. Rachins looked as though he had objections to that interpretation, but apparently saw no point in arguing. With a "Whatever," he settled the matter for himself and continued,

"Anyway, we were moving around to avoid an ambush when we met up with 'Librarian.' She wanted to go looking for you lot, but..."

"I explained the situation, and we discussed an alternative. We concluded that if we could disrupt their ambush, they would have no choice but to change their response—"

"And Betty moved Mother's castle, I suppose."

With the reasoning of the group that had been acting separately laid out for him, Subaru finally understood.
That left the question of how they had put their conclusion into practice—how to move the castle itself—

"Does it have something to do with that black ball 'Silver Fox' has been holding his hand over?"

"Yes. I do not know the precise mechanism, but the castle moves in accordance with its operator's will. Though without 'Librarian,' I doubt we would have discovered how to activate it."

"I see. Good girl, good girl. You saved all our lives."

"Naturally, in fact. ...But being unable to stay beside you was nerve-racking, I suppose."

Beatrice pinched the hem of Subaru's clothes, her eyes softening as she spoke. It was an endearing sight. Even if she had accepted the plan as a way out of their predicament, it must have taken considerable courage for her to put off rushing to the side of Subaru, from whom she had been separated.
He was glad that courageous decision of hers had not gone to waste.

"Um, I understand what happened. But is it really all right to fly the castle? Won't it stand out too much and leave us being chased forever...?"

"Of course, we have already discussed that point. 'Librarian,' this castle—"

"Has a 'Perception Inhibition' effect cast upon it, in fact. We shall not spend forever making a gaudy parade across the sky, I suppose. Still, someone with keen instincts, or someone who has been inside the castle before, may be able to see through the effect. We must not rely on it too much, in fact."

"That said, having rumors spread is not entirely to our disadvantage. Our enemies are not the only people whose attention we wish to draw."

"You mean..."

Subaru's eyes widened at Russell's words, and the man gave him a quiet nod. —The people whose attention they wanted to attract with this conspicuous castle were, without doubt, the allies they currently had no means of contacting.
Apart from Emilia and the others, whose whereabouts they knew, there was no way to reach Rem, Otto, and the rest who had disappeared, or Roswaal's group, who had gone into hiding ahead of them. Even while working with the 'Six Tongues,' Subaru's group had no way to get in touch with their separated companions.
But if word of the floating castle spread, for better or worse, it would become easier for people to make a move.

"Roswaal can fly here under his own steam. Maybe he'll just hop aboard."

"To be honest, I cannot deny that I am hoping for that. We are maintaining considerable altitude, but Roswaal should be able to reach us without freezing."

"Besides, a little freezing would do Roswaal good, I suppose."

"Hmm. You're really tearing into him, but he kinda has it coming."

Russell and Beatrice, both closely connected to Roswaal, were saying some dreadful things about him. But since he had undeniably brought much of it upon himself, Subaru offered no defense.
In any case, he now understood how the castle had come to fly, and most of his concerns about that decision had been settled.
All that remained was—

"If we've got a little time now, I'd like to explain what happened, and... Beako, is there a room where Crusch-san can rest?"

"There should be a guest room that has not been ransacked, in fact. I shall show you the way, I suppose."

"Roger that. Ferris will want to come with us, so can I leave the report to Mel... 'White Sheep'-chan and 'Black Dog'? Once that's done—"

"Yes, leave the report to us. —After that, let's talk."

Putting a hand to her chest, Melty gave a deep nod, her expression quiet but resolute.
In contrast to her sweet, delicate features, the resolve in those round eyes was so very strong and sharp, as unyielding as a diamond—

"—About what I'm fighting for."

"—There is a place called Faradar. It's where I was born... a remote land where the Evil Eye Tribe had lived for generations."

In a deliberately matter-of-fact voice, Melty began to tell them about her life.
This was after they had made the castle's least shabby guest room fit for use and settled Crusch there to rest. Leaving the sleeping woman in Ferris's care, Subaru and the others had taken the second-least shabby room as a place to talk. There, they were hearing the story Melty had promised them.
The important secret connecting her and 'Black Dog'—and Tiga and Pooka.

"The Evil Eye Tribe... I heard there were a few of them scattered around Vollachia. A race of demi-humans who can use special powers, something like Divine Protections, right? You too, Melty-chan?"

"Yes, that's correct. Faradar was also a settlement on the outskirts of the Vollachian Empire, and I'm a member of the Evil Eye Tribe... I've told you about my Evil Eye power, haven't I, Subaru-san?"

"Yeah. 'People-Charmer,' which makes anyone you touch feel affection for you, right?"

Melty gave a small nod to Subaru's confirmation, her expression serious.
'People-Charmer'—the name alone did not sound like a particularly grave power, but during their work with the 'Six Tongues,' he had seen Russell rely on it time and again. In fact, she had been chosen as one of the 'Beloved Children.' It must have been every bit as effective as that implied.
As if to support that assessment, Beatrice, who was holding Subaru's hand, murmured, "'People-Charmer'..." and continued,

"I have heard of it, in fact. Of all the powers possessed by the Evil Eye Tribe, which was created to reproduce the Authorities of the 'Witches,' it came closest to fulfilling that purpose. The 'Evil Eyes of Calamity'... if I recall correctly, I suppose."

"You spend all your time looking pretty beside me, and then every now and then you casually drop a bombshell. What's this about reproducing the Authorities of the 'Witches'?"

"This concerns the age of the 'Witch War.' Betty has no firsthand experience of it either, in fact. But there were 'Witches' springing up all over the land in those days, and that was one of the measures taken to oppose them, I suppose. If you wish to know, I shall tell you another time. For now, attend to 'White Sheep'... to Melty's story, in fact."

"Damn it, I'm holding you to that...! Sorry for interrupting, Melty-chan. Go on."

"Yes. But it's just as 'Librarian'-san said. That's what my power was called. They said it was an ability that hadn't appeared in our tribe for hundreds of years, the fulfillment of their dearest wish. ...I do understand that my power is that frightening. Though that understanding wasn't enough."

Before Subaru and Beatrice, Melty laid a hand on her chest, her brows drooping.
It had not been enough. With that fierce self-reproach and regret weighing on her, she let out a small breath.

"When I was little, I was careless about my power. I caused quarrels and all sorts of trouble. I regretted that deeply... But my real regret came soon afterward."

"Regret... You don't mean Capella taking an interest in you?"

"That too. ...But that isn't my greatest regret."

"————"

"...It's Tiga."

After a moment's hesitation, Melty came to the heart of the matter.
Tiga Rauleon—a member of the 'Divine Dragon Church,' Subaru's like-minded, easy-to-talk-to friend, and a man said to be an 'Executor' working with the enemy 'Beloved Children.'
As for the extraordinary connection between Tiga and Melty—

"It was when we were both children. A troupe of traveling performers came to Faradar. Tiga was part of that troupe... Unable to control 'People-Charmer' properly, I was kept away from other people. Then I happened to meet Tiga. And..."

"...And?"

"—With the power of 'People-Charmer,' I wished for him to stay by my side. I let myself wish for that."

Clutching the front of her clothes, Melty spoke as though confessing a sin.
Again, she said she had been a little child. Born with an immense power, ignorant of how to restrain it or use it properly. A mistake brought about by those circumstances—Melty had not been able to be selfish enough to excuse herself that way.

"Tiga left the troupe that had been his family and stayed in Faradar. I was so innocently happy about it... and I dragged Tiga into this hell."

"Hell, I suppose. You mean the 'Beloved Children' of the Sin Archbishop of 'Lust,' in fact."

"—Yes. So he could stay with me when Mama found me and took me away... so he could grant my 'request,' I don't know what terms Tiga offered her. But to win the favor of someone like Mama, how much of his own life he must have sold off, piece by piece...!"

Melty's already fair skin turned deathly pale, and her voice trembled weakly at the end.
Subaru could feel the regret and shame swirling inside her with painful clarity. He, too, would regret dragging someone dear to him—Emilia, Rem, Garfiel, Petra, or any of the others—into an inescapable hell because of his own mistake.

"Betty and Otto were absent from that list, I suppose."

"Well, you and Otto have already given me permission to drag you into hell..."

"I am glad you understand, in fact. Still, I shall have a word with Otto about being placed on the same level as him when we next meet, I suppose."

Apparently, Beatrice had reached some sort of resolution. After they reunited, Subaru would probably get an earful from Otto once Beatrice had given him hers, but, well, that was fine.
At any rate, he understood Melty's guilt toward Tiga, and the connection between them. He understood, but there was something he still could not make sense of.

"I get how deep Tiga's connection to you runs, Melty-chan. But the way he acted when we ran into him in the castle's basement was the exact opposite of what you've told me. Was that..."

"—He doesn't remember. Doesn't remember me."

"————"

"Tiga has forgotten me. ...No, it isn't just Tiga. Mama, the 'Beloved Children,' everyone in the world has forgotten me. —That's why I've been able to leave the 'Beloved Children' like this."

At Melty's answer, Beatrice's grip on Subaru's hand tightened slightly. Understanding what she meant by it, Subaru nodded to show that he knew.
To be honest, he had suspected as much even before hearing this story.
Given the relationship with Tiga that Melty had described—that long and deep connection—he had wondered whether there could be any other explanation for the way they treated each other now.
But if that was true—

"Those 'Gluttony' bastards didn't just turn the relationship between me and my Rem into a compound fracture. They had to go and foul up Melty-chan's pure love too...!"

"It is hardly limited to you and Rem, in fact. Julius, the people of Priestella... There would be no end to it if we began counting everyone affected, I suppose."

"We still don't even know who killed Roy while he was locked up. All they do is scatter mysteries everywhere. Letting Melty-chan get out of the 'Beloved Children' doesn't come close to paying that debt."

In the first place, Capella, who had tormented Melty, was a Sin Archbishop too. It was rather like one Sin Archbishop setting a fire and another putting it out. There was no credit to be claimed for it.
But while Subaru fumed over that, Melty's cheeks flushed faintly.

"U-um, 'pure love' is a little..."

"Huh? It's not? But the way you were with Tiga just now was totally..."

He thought back to Melty and Tiga's encounter in the basement, to their exchange.
The way she had gazed at Tiga when he appeared, stood in his way when he tried to flee, and called after him, her voice falling just short of his retreating back—every bit of it had been driven by deep affection.
In response to Subaru's question, born of that impression, Melty floundered with an "A-aww..." for a while before saying,

"N-no comment, please... Besides, I don't have..."

"—Hey, this might be me fussing over you, but I think I should say it. If you're thinking you don't have the right to love someone, or that you've got some nerve saying Tiga matters to you, you're overthinking it. You don't need to do that."

"H-how did you know!? Subaru-san, are you psychic...!?"

"If I do say so myself, Subaru-san is pretty perceptive when it's someone else's problem."

"You really ought not to say so yourself, in fact..."

Letting Beatrice's teasing pass, Subaru cleared his throat with an "Ahem" in front of the bewildered Melty.
There was not much advice Subaru could offer other people about relationships between men and women. Even so, when a plainly fruitless inner struggle was right there before him, he had to cut it off at the root.

"I've got plenty of insight into girls who think they don't deserve to be loved. Emilia-tan, Rem, and Beako were all like that, after all. ...Though Beako might be a slightly different case? I'd better say it just in case. Beako, I love you."

"Betty loves you too, I suppose. You need not say it just now, in fact."

"Taking courage from how understanding you are, Beako, I'll go on... There's no point worrying about whether you deserve to be loved. Everyone deserves love. The only exceptions are the Sin Archbishops."

"Subaru-san..."

"Like I said before, you're not going to charm me into anything, Melty-chan. So when I think you're good-looking, or that you've got a cute voice, that has nothing to do with 'People-Charmer.'"

"R-really? You're sure you're not being charmed!?"

"Not charmed, not charmed. I even think that about Ferris—how pretty he is, how I could look at him forever. I don't say so because he'd probably give me a really nasty look. Beako, you're ridiculously cute too. I could look at you for all eternity."

"That is simply Betty's charm at work, I suppose. I have no 'People-Charmer' power, in fact."

Looking back and forth between the nodding Subaru and Beatrice, who puffed out her chest adorably, Melty blinked several times, then let out a long breath.
It seemed to release something heavy that had been weighing on her heart until a moment before.

"You two are amazing..."

"Well, we're partners bound by fate who've made it through some pretty tough trials together."

"Indeed, I suppose. Seasoned veterans, in fact."

The number and height of the hurdles they had overcome were on another level. Subaru and Beatrice were quite unabashed about it.
Of course, that did nothing to ease the hardship and anguish Melty had endured, nor did it diminish them. It might not even offer any comfort.
Even so, a smile settled on Melty's lips.

"I'll be sure to remember. —That maybe I deserve to be loved too."

Her answer still carried that slight lack of confidence so characteristic of a young girl.
But surely, giving her true confidence was not Subaru's job. The man who needed to fill that role was in the enemy camp right now.
Still, though he now understood that Melty and Tiga shared a complicated yet unshakable past, one that left no room for anyone else to come between them—

"In that case, what's the relationship between you and 'Black Dog,' Melty-chan? 'Black Dog' seemed to have a connection to that spirit Pooka too. Mind if I dig a little deeper while we're at it?"

With Melty's account of 'People-Charmer' finished, Subaru took the opportunity to venture into a subject he had been vaguely curious about, but had refrained from asking about out of consideration for the mood.

—'Black Dog,' who had left the 'Beloved Children' with Melty and joined the 'Six Tongues.'
For all the nasty things he said, it was obvious that he cared about Melty. 'Black Dog' was foulmouthed with everyone to begin with, so the glimpses of special treatment he gave Melty in other ways were unmistakable.
But once Tiga entered the picture, things became quite complicated.

"I don't know whose side I should take, my friend Tiga's or my current comrade 'Black Dog's.' Are you guilty of breaking hearts, Melty-chan?"

"G-guilty! I knew it, I don't deserve to be loved...!"

"It was a joke, I suppose. Do not take it to heart, in fact. And Subaru, do not trouble Melty, I suppose. Bad boy, in fact."

His unnecessary remark earned him a stern scolding from Beatrice. But after taking a deep breath, Melty lowered her eyes and began with an "Um..."

"...The truth is, I really don't know what my relationship with 'Black Dog' is."

"You mean... you've forgotten 'Black Dog,' Melty-chan?"

"That's part of it, but... 'Black Dog' himself doesn't seem to know who he is either. As for his relationship with me, um, all he knows is that we've been together from the start."

"Huh... So 'Black Dog' isn't keeping his relationship with you a secret?"

"I don't... think so. I don't think he's clever enough to manage that..."

There was a good deal more wanting to believe than certainty in that answer. But Subaru agreed with Melty's assessment. 'Black Dog's' personality did not lend itself to subterfuge.
Yet accepting those premises brought an unnatural situation to light.

"Everyone around him has forgotten him, and he's forgotten himself too... That would mean..."

"It matches the result of having both one's 'Name' and 'Memories' eaten by the Authority of 'Gluttony,' I suppose. But if both had been eaten, this would be impossible, in fact."

"Yeah. ...He should've ended up like Rem when she was a 'Sleeping Beauty.'"

If they believed Melty's account, 'Black Dog' should have been in the same position as Rem after 'Gluttony's' attack, asleep for so long—the same as the people in Priestella who had yet to awaken even now. And yet 'Black Dog' was not in a coma. He was walking around of his own free will.
Could they really be discovering a new kind of victim of 'Gluttony's' Authority at this point?

"But that would make his relationship with Pooka add up too. In other words, they originally had a Contract, but neither of them remembers it. That's why they're at cross-purposes... The same situation as Julius's Contract with Ia and the others staying intact, right?"

"That would make the most sense, I suppose. ...But if so, I pity her, in fact."

"Pity her..."

"For a Spirit who has chosen someone, there is nothing so painful as being forgotten by one's contractor, I suppose."

"Gwooooh! That sharp pain took its sweet time hitting me...! Beako, I'm sorry!!"

Beatrice slowly shook her head. Subaru scooped her up and desperately rubbed his cheek against hers.
It might have felt brief to Beatrice, but there had still been a time when Subaru had forgotten his Contract with her after having his 'Memories' eaten.
But precisely because he understood that pain, Subaru could reconsider things as well.

"How do Tiga and Pooka see things now? Tiga joined the 'Beloved Children' in the first place for Melty-chan, because she was there, right? But now she isn't. His reason's been left hanging."

"...I don't know. But Pooka was looking for her contractor... for 'Black Dog,' I think. If that was true, then perhaps Tiga was too... That's what I'd been thinking, but..."

"Melty-chan..."

With a faint, helpless smile, Melty spoke of a fragile hope that had come to nothing.
At her reunion with Tiga, from whom she had been separated against her wishes, Melty had held on to that one-in-a-million hope. Sadly, it had not come true.
That was the whole story behind their failure to connect in the castle's basement.

"...What do you want to do, Melty-chan?"

Having taken in her circumstances, thought them over, and understood them, Subaru asked her that.
Melty had left the 'Beloved Children,' joined the 'Six Tongues,' and set herself to thwarting Capella's schemes. Of course, she must have wanted to secure her own safety, and felt a sense of duty as someone who knew the plan. But now that he knew her circumstances, Subaru thought there was more to it. —No, he did not merely think so. He wanted there to be more.

Natsuki Subaru already liked Melty Pristis.
And Natsuki Subaru, whose affections were so easily stirred, had a principle: he wanted the people he liked to be as greedy as possible, and to find happiness.
So—

"If there's something you want to do, Melty-chan, I'll help however I can."

"—Are you really sure I haven't charmed you, Subaru-san?"

"I'm sure! I've got plenty of people in first place in my heart...!"

"Subaru always means it, in fact. ...That is what makes him so troublesome, and why you ought not to burden yourself too much, I suppose."

Still held in his arms after he had lifted her to rub their cheeks together, Beatrice murmured that with a sigh. Melty caught her breath at those words and whispered, "'Librarian'-san..."
Beatrice shrugged in response.

"Beatrice will do, rather than 'Librarian,' in fact. Betty shall call you Melty instead of 'White Sheep' as well, I suppose."

"Ah... Somehow, I feel as though I'm the one who's going to be charmed by you two."

"Well, Beako's cuteness is on the same level as an Evil Eye."

"Then Subaru's nasty eyes are on the same level as Evil Eyes too, in fact."

"That's not a compliment, is it!? Aren't you just saying my eyes look demonic!?"

"Aha."

At their mismatched compliments, Melty put a hand to her mouth and laughed despite herself. For a while she gave herself over to the laughter, then finally let out a deep breath.

"—Subaru-san, Beatrice-san."

"Yeah?"
"What is it, I suppose?"

They faced Melty as she clasped her hands firmly in front of her stomach and straightened her back. Keenly aware of the resolve in her round eyes, they waited for her to speak.
Some things could be conveyed without words. Even so, the important things ought to be put into words.

Just as you told someone dear to you that you loved them, when you had resolved to accomplish something no matter what, you had to declare it to the world.
So—

"—There's a life I want to reclaim, no matter what. And in that life, there's someone I want by my side. Please lend me your strength so I can bring him back."

"Yeah, you got it. Let's do this together. Fate—"
"—Bring it on, I suppose."

Melty's eyes widened as Subaru and Beatrice thrust out their fists. Then, with renewed determination on her face, she nodded and gently touched each of her hands to one of their fists.

—It was a modest yet unwavering declaration of Melty Pristis's resolve.
