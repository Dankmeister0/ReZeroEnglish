Arc 10 Chapter 42: "The Death Throes of the Milesta Dragonback Bridge"

—An escape from Royal Lugunica Castle, where they had spent two whole months under house arrest.

In a sense, the breakout had bordered on the height of recklessness, but thanks to unexpected assistance from outside, it had gone better than they could have hoped. Granted, the possibility that they had picked up some extra baggage, along with a fight that never needed to happen, meant the good and bad more or less balanced out.
At any rate—

"I just tagged along for the ride. Ain't my place to keep griping about it."

That was how Felt currently saw herself: one of the people who had escaped the royal castle, and the one who believed she had done the least to make it happen.
The ones who had done the heavy lifting in getting them out were Ram, who had secretly coordinated with Marcos, captain of the Royal Guard; Filore, who had come to rescue them in an utterly unexpected fit of recklessness; and Heinkel, her minder.
Scattered across a complicated playing field, kept apart by their positions and duties, Felt's camp had been unable to take any part in the breakout. That went for Felt herself, who had merely been held prisoner, but also for her knight Reinhard, her adviser Old Man Rom, and even Rachins and the others.
Felt could hardly help resenting how powerless her entire camp had been, but—

"But Ram said the people at the castle had let their 'guard' down partly because we Royal Selection candidates had been doing as we were told. Without that, I don't think Heinkel-san would have been the only one who got hurt."

"Yes, quite right! I agree! So stop looking so glum and downhearted, Felt, and hold your head high! Think of Emilia and me as your big sisters and lean on us. ...Yes! Your big sisters!"

With those two, whose situations were much the same as hers, carrying on like that, even her dejection blew away.
Setting aside the nonsense in Filore's remarks, brooding over having been no help when everything had worked out was far too luxurious a regret.
Luxury was the enemy. For better or worse, luxury put unnecessary fat on the mind as well as the body.

—Working through those shifting feelings, Felt and the others had successfully escaped the royal castle, then gone straight on to escape the capital itself. Their swift dash through the castle gate and into the crowds before anyone discovered the Royal Selection candidates were missing had paid off.
Originally, they had planned to get through the castle gate with the help of a gatekeeper Marcos had brought in on the scheme. Instead, they had done so using a cart carrying medical supplies, arranged separately by Heinkel.

"Even if he doesn't know what's going on, if they find out a soldier was involved, they'll probably come down hard on him. Someone from outside the castle can just say they were threatened."

That suggestion had come from Heinkel, battered and half-dead.
He had a point, though it was tempting to think he had simply wanted treatment after being beaten half to death. Since they had managed to leave the capital in the end, however, that was merely Felt taking out her frustration on him—resentment at having had no choice but to leave Reinhard behind in the castle.
Faced with Felt's complicated feelings, Emilia had furrowed her exceptionally well-shaped brows.

"You're worried about Reinhard, aren't you?"

"...Who knows? Maybe it ain't him I'm worried about so much as what the hell he might do. He ain't as easy to knock down as Bro, and he'll figure something out for himself."

"Hehe. Then we're worried about the same thing."

"The same? Me and Sister Emilia?"

"Mm-hm, that's right. I'm sure Subaru's fit as a fiddle too. But no matter how many times I tell him, he never stops pushing himself or doing reckless things. That's what has me sooo worried."

Emilia smiled as though to say, *See? We're the same, aren't we?* Felt twisted her lips.
It rankled that she could not even call that completely off the mark. For all appearances, Emilia actually saw things quite clearly. Felt had trouble with the way she said so with such unvarnished honesty.
If this had been about something other than Reinhard, she could have dodged it a little better, but—

"That idiot. No matter how long it's been, I still can't get a clear idea of him..."

He was the strongest person in the kingdom, hailed as the 'Knight among Knights,' but as far as Felt was concerned, Reinhard was a colossal idiot who understood neither the world nor himself.
Despite all that strength, he had no single, solid core to anchor him. His head kept swaying this way and that, never settling.
That was why Felt had to keep giving him a smack on the back.

"————"

Her thoughts reaching that point, Felt glanced at the door leading to the back room.
Several hours had passed since they left the capital, and they were now in a hideout in the woods, away from the highway. After escaping the city on the cart mentioned earlier, the group had switched to another dragon carriage, reached this hideout, and decided to spend the night.
In the back room, Filore was currently working hard to treat the gravely wounded Heinkel.

"Really, I shouldn't use the Sacred Rite without the Church's permission, but something occurred to me. If I get approval afterward, I can use it as much as I like... no, I mean, perhaps using the Sacred Rite whenever it's needed is also the will of the 'Divine Dragon'...!"

Having clenched her fists and made that resolution, was Filore still within the bounds of what a 'Saintess' was allowed to do?
Still, Felt had never studied the teachings of the 'Divine Dragon Church,' and she had no confidence in her ability to judge anything involving Heinkel fairly, so she had no right to say anything. If anything, there were some things about Reinhard she wanted to ask him once he could talk, but that was about it.
For now, then, she intended to wait and see, whatever answers Filore brought back from beyond the door, where she was shouting things like "Hiyah!" and "Nnngh!"

"For better or worse, Filore-sama and Heinkel's actions served as a diversion. We used their arrangements to get through the castle gate, while this hideout was prepared on our side... With the sources of our assistance mixed together, it should confuse our pursuers."

"That's good. We have Filore and the others to thank for coming. But Marcos-san, about beating Heinkel-san to such a pulp..."

"—. Given the circumstances, I would ask that you consider it unavoidable. Had it been anyone but him, I believe I would have shown more restraint."

"You saying you got carried away 'cause it was someone you don't like?"

"I mean that I knew he was a man who would not stop unless I went that far. In fact, even after all that, he neither died nor lost consciousness. Whatever else he may be, he is still of House Astrea. ...Though I imagine you hardly need to be told that, Felt-sama."

"Tch. Walked right into that one."

With that, Felt clicked her tongue at Marcos, who had returned from checking the area around the hideout.
At the castle he had been clad in silver plate armor, but now that he was on the run, he had shed that conspicuous equipment and changed into ordinary, light clothing like any other townsman—or rather, like someone from the slums. He wore it well enough, but a change of clothes could hardly conceal his natural build or intimidating presence. It was a drop in the bucket.
Still, the effort proved his cooperation was neither a whim nor a passing lapse of judgment.

"Stone-Face, you got a plan for what comes next?"

"Of course. Will you be accompanying me?"

"Wouldn't make sense to say goodbye after coming this far. Besides, we're after the same thing, ain't we? Taking down the bastards trying to wreck the castle... no, the whole kingdom."

"Mm, that's right. Wrecking the country is Felt-chan's campaign promise, after all. I remember what you said at the very beginning perfectly well."

"Hearing you put it that way makes it sound like I'm picking one hell of a lousy fight too."

Apparently believing she had been backing Felt up, Emilia widened her eyes at Felt's sour expression. "Huh?" A small, breathy sound escaped at their exchange.
Looking over, Felt saw Marcos with the back of his hand over his mouth, apparently stifling a laugh. At her instinctively disgruntled look, he added, "My apologies."

"Your exchange amused me, and I could not help myself. For two Royal Selection candidates, you are remarkably friendly with one another."

"Yes, we are! We're competing for the same throne, but we don't mean to spend our time glaring at each other and making things unpleasant. Right?"

"Ain't like we made a pact or anything, but yeah, that's how it is. We don't hate each other's guts, so it'd be stupid to keep snapping at each other, wouldn't it?"

Felt added her own touch of spice to Emilia's peace-loving opinion. Hearing their answers, Marcos folded his thick arms and silently closed his eyes.
Like that, his quiet presence truly could be mistaken for a mass of rock.

"...To be frank, when it became clear the Royal Selection could not be avoided, I braced myself. The candidates' struggle for the throne would shake the country badly. It might even split it apart. I spent my days asking myself what role we of the Royal Guard ought to play in all that."

When the rock finally spoke, his words carried the heartfelt appeal of someone who truly cared for the kingdom.
In fact, it was certain that not only Marcos, but the 'Council of Wise Men' and everyone connected to the royal castle had been extremely anxious. The Royal Selection alone was unprecedented, yet every candidate it had brought together was an eccentric—with Felt and Emilia the two most striking examples.

"You got my sympathy. Hell of a time to be captain of the knights."

"I appreciate your concern. However, I see it differently."

"Differently? You mean..."

"I am glad that you are the Royal Selection candidates. You are good, reasonable, and sincere. That is precisely why I cannot help regretting that I failed to prevent the present situation."

"...Well, I guess being on the verge of having the kingdom overthrown by a Sin Archbishop ain't exactly a healthy state of affairs."

Marcos's words were heavy with self-reproach, but half-hearted consolation would be pointless.
The failure he felt was an undeniable fact. If the knights were the sword that protected the kingdom, neither Marcos nor the order under his command had fulfilled that duty.
But that meant—

"—We can't just keep letting them do as they please. It's sooo frustrating."

"Hah! Finding out I'm thinking the same thing as Sister Emilia is pretty scary."

"Huh? Why!? Knowing you agree with me makes me feel sooo much better, Felt-chan."

Emilia's eyes went wide at the injustice of it, and Felt answered with a perfunctory shrug. Marcos laughed at their exchange once more. This time, though, it was no little chuckle.
He opened his mouth and laughed out loud.

"Ha, hahahaha! You're funny. You two really are something."

"Oh! You startled me."

"So that's the real you, Stone-Face?"

Responding to the two Royal Selection candidates, Marcos still wore a grin, the urge to laugh lingering at his mouth.

"That's right. Standing on ceremony doesn't suit me. By now they've probably suspended my authority as captain... If they've moved quickly, I might already have been dismissed. Oh, but I do respect you both, mind."

"Mm, I can tell. And you might actually look more lively this way, Marcos-san."

"Manners and etiquette are like armor and a helmet. Wear them properly on a battlefield that calls for them, and take them off and set them aside when it doesn't. Provided, of course, that you both permit it."

"You heard Sister Emilia's answer, right? I'm the sort who finds this easier to talk to anyway. All that respect stuff just makes me itch in the first place."

What she wanted from here on was a frank discussion anyway. Having him drop the unnecessary formality would make that easier.
At their answer, Marcos nodded deeply.

"About those plans you asked about earlier. I've got some of the few contacts left in the kingdom we can trust. They're the ones who arranged this hideout and the escape route from the castle."

"Your friends have got a pretty long reach. Fellow patriots worried about the country's future, huh?"

"I won't deny it. You can think of them that way."

"————"

At Marcos's answer, Felt touched a fingertip to her canine tooth and narrowed her eyes.
Fellow patriots worried about the country's future—she had heard vague things from Old Man Rom and Rachins about some organization other than the knights that protected the kingdom. Born and raised in the capital, even if it was in the slums, Felt had doubted that such a thing really existed.

"Sounds like they really are out there, then."

If the knights were its guardians in the open, perhaps that organization could be called its guardians in the shadows. Guardians who never surfaced in peacetime—and things were bad enough that they had to seek their help.
So that was it. This truly was a desperate crisis for the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica itself, reaching far beyond the Royal Selection.

"Are we going to meet up with those people? Do you think they might know where Subaru and the others... where everyone we've lost contact with is?"

"It is quite likely. At the very least, far more so than if we wander aimlessly while our pursuers hound us."

"—Presenting the worst possible outcome to make the choice you want someone to take look better is an objectionable tactic, Marcos-sama."

That cold interruption came from Ram, who had entered through a different door from the one leading to Filore and Heinkel's room.
Fixing Marcos with her pale crimson eyes, she continued.

"Emilia-sama can judge matters correctly without your resorting to such nasty rhetoric. Ram would prefer that the captain of the Royal Guard refrain from stooping to underhanded tricks."

"Harsh. I learned that one from a disreputable old friend... Roswaal himself, in fact."

"Any method can be either admirable or despicable, depending on who uses it."

"Um, Ram, that sounded like an awfully self-serving opinion to me... Oh, but I'm happy you believe in me. Thank you."

Given both the carrot and the stick in a single remark from Emilia, Ram replied, "Not at all," with a slight bow. Leaving the wryly smiling Marcos to one side, she turned to Emilia and Felt.

"For now, the food is ready. Continue this after dinner."

"Really? Thank you. What's on the 'menu'?"

"Steamed potatoes."

"Hehe, of course. They are sooo lucky for us, after all."

Hearing Ram's crisp answer, Emilia accepted it in high spirits.
Apparently, during their two months under house arrest, Emilia had stubbornly insisted, "If I don't get to eat steamed potatoes Ram made at least once a day, there's no telling what I might do!" That had let her keep her personal maid by her side.
Granted, Felt had wanted Grassis to stay with Reinhard, but the idea had never even occurred to her. Hearing about it, she had had to take her hat off to Emilia.
At any rate—

"We've reached a stopping point with the Sacred Rite treatment! I am hungry, hungry, positively famished!"

As though she had timed it perfectly, Filore burst through the door as well, and their first night as fugitives from the capital wore on over dinner.

—The group agreed without objection on their basic course: follow Marcos's contacts and meet up with the 'guardians in the shadows.'

Filore and Heinkel, who had intended to help Felt and the others escape in the first place, had presumably had plans of their own. As it turned out, what they had planned after leaving the castle and the capital was nothing short of audacious.

"I intended to take Emilia and Felt with me and march into the Watergate City of Priestella. Then the three of us Royal Selection candidates could save everyone waiting to be saved, with a great big flourish! If we presented the royal castle with an achievement so great they couldn't say a word against it, I thought that might do the trick...!"

It would surely have been exhilarating if they had pulled it off, but unfortunately their rendezvous with the 'guardians in the shadows' was in Milesta, a town in the kingdom's east, in exactly the opposite direction from Priestella.
Still, though a little shortsighted, the plan to rattle the royal castle with an achievement it could not conceal was surprisingly decent. Once they had met up with the 'guardians in the shadows,' it might be worth trying when the opportunity arose.
If anything could be done for Priestella's victims, Felt wanted to do it too. If there was a strategic reason for it as well, there was no reason not to consider it.
That said—

"We can count those chickens once we've seen all the cards we're holding now."

Fortunately, on the second and third days, no pursuers caught up with them on their flight.
This was thanks to the assistance of the 'guardians in the shadows,' with whom Marcos kept in contact, and probably also to the effectiveness of the 'Perception Inhibition' robes he had supplied.

"I could only get five, including the spare, so we'll have to hide Heinkel among the cargo, since he can't walk on his own. He really is baggage, after all."

That was Marcos, showing no sign of easing up on his scathing treatment of Heinkel.
Between his natural toughness and Filore's tireless treatment with the Sacred Rite, Heinkel had pulled through the critical stage. All he needed now was rest, but that was difficult to hope for while on the run.
Once they reached their destination, Milesta, without incident, the fog hanging over both their worries and Heinkel's condition should have begun to clear.

—Had their pursuers not caught them on the Milesta Dragonback Bridge, with their destination right before their eyes.

Construction of the Milesta Dragonback Bridge was said to have begun roughly three hundred years ago.

Milesta Gorge, which cut through the highway leading to the capital, had once been a troublesome obstacle for travelers. To reach the other side, they had to descend to the bottom of the gorge and take a ferry, depending on the river's mood, or spend days detouring upstream.
To eliminate that inconvenience, the kingdom had undertaken the construction of a great bridge connecting the two sides.

The work had taken twenty years. The completed bridge stretched five hundred meters, more than a hundred meters above the water. Supported by several thick piers built in the river, its broad roadway was solid enough to withstand the passage of dragon carriages without a tremor.
In the morning, when river mist filled the gorge and hid the piers, only the roadway, rising gently toward its center, appeared above the fog. It was said to resemble an enormous dragon lying across the valley, and even before its completion, the people building it had affectionately called it the 'Dragon's Back.'

Thus, this enormous bridge had come to be known as the 'Milesta Dragonback Bridge.' To make that name official, sculptures of a dragon's head and tail were placed at either end, and touching the nose of the dragon facing the capital came to be regarded as a prayer for safety on one's journey.

Two hundred and eighty years after it opened, with its wheel-worn roadway resurfaced time and again and new stones fitted into its chipped parapets, the 'Dragon's Back' had remained a familiar, imposing sight. Long after the people who built it, their children, and their grandchildren had passed from this world, its piled stones had continued fulfilling their duty of carrying later generations to the opposite bank.

—Now, that Milesta Dragonback Bridge was about to fall.

"————"

There were roughly fifty dragon carriages on the bridge.
Between the heavily laden wagons of merchant caravans, the public carriages crammed with passengers, and the people walking past them on foot, more than a thousand people were making the crossing.
All of them had been brought to a standstill by the Dragonback Bridge's sudden death throes.

Without warning, a great fissure had opened in the roadway. In an instant, cracks had spread like lightning, mercilessly swallowing the wheels of the carriages in the line and blocking their progress. When the carriages at the front stopped, those behind could only haul frantically on their reins. Earth dragons trying to avoid collisions pulled their carriages askew, and the impacts made the iron railings lining both sides of the bridge creak.
All the while, the sounds of the Dragonback Bridge breaking apart and its violent shaking battered the hearts of everyone on it. People shoved and jostled between the carriages as the confusion spread.
Just as it seemed things could not go on like this—

"—Icicle Line!"

A high, clear, beautiful voice replaced the bridge's death rattle with another sound.
What rang out now was the fragile crackle of freezing air. One after another, the mouths of the abyss that the shaking had opened in the Dragonback Bridge were sealed, forcibly bound together by a wielder of absolute ice—

"Honestly! Doing something this awful just to find us...!"

As the mist-laden wind met the cold and sent grains of ice tumbling across the bridge, a beautiful girl with long, dancing silver hair—Emilia—narrowed her almond-shaped eyes in open anger.
Then—

"I won't let you! I'm sooo angry right now!"

At her outstretched hand, the great fissure that should have brought down the Milesta Dragonback Bridge closed.
It was a rough method, like freezing a bleeding wound shut, but in this situation, it was the best possible way to protect more than a thousand lives.
How reassuring her unhesitating readiness to act was in a situation that called for instinct like this.

"Hey! If you can move, don't stop! Leave your stuff! Your lives matter more!"

Trusting in that reassuring presence, Felt got moving too. She leaped onto the roof of a tilted dragon carriage and shouted, peering toward the back of the line.
The great fissure running through the middle of the bridge had been sealed with ice, but that did not mean the carriages whose wheels had fallen into it could get back out. Axles snapped under the pressure, and earth dragons whinnied desperately as the sinking carriages dragged them down. No matter how hard the drivers worked the reins, there was no getting out of those pits—

"Shit. In that case, I'll..."

"—Stay here, Felt-sama."

Just as she bent her knees to leap, an immense weight shot forward, leaving that deep voice behind.
Leaving the imprints of his shoes on the bridge with each step, a mass of rock raced forward at incredible speed—or rather, Marcos did. Reaching a carriage being swallowed by a hole, he seized the leather straps connecting it to its earth dragon, twisted them with inhuman strength, and tore them apart.
Suddenly freed of the weight dragging it down, the earth dragon stumbled forward. Marcos reached out and caught the sharply tilting carriage with one hand, hauling its wheels from the hole and returning it to the roadway.
Witnessing that monstrous strength, Felt caught her breath. Leaving that part to Marcos, she was about to look elsewhere when—

"—!?"

The violent tremor that followed slammed Felt down against the carriage roof.
A strangled "Gah" squeezed from her throat. Forcing her watering eyes open, she looked around to see what had happened, and a piercingly cold wind swept over her entire body. The wind carried a spray of white, and only when she realized that the spray was crushed ice did she notice it. —Countless spiderweb cracks had spread through the white ice that had arrested the bridge's collapse and become their new lifeline.

"Wh-what..."

She never finished asking what had happened.
Before she could, a mass of ice embracing the parapet crumbled, and the icy seams sealing the roadway all turned cloudy white at once. The bridge screamed again, a different sound from before, but heralding the same result. The collapse that should have been halted resumed, and the Milesta Dragonback Bridge began to buckle.
As the noise and shaking wrung Felt's guts—

"...I'll break it. I broke it."

Mingled with the bridge's screams came an utterly matter-of-fact voice.
Felt whirled around. Beyond the line of carriages, a slender figure stood on the broken parapet. It was a gloomy-looking woman with wavy green hair and a face as pale as an invalid's.
Standing on the parapet, she wore an unusual gauntlet on her left hand. Even from a distance, Felt could see white grains spilling from her fingertips and scattering into Milesta Gorge. —They looked like grains of ice.
She did not understand what that meant, but of one thing she was certain.

—That woman was the one trying to shatter the Milesta Dragonback Bridge.

"Maid! That woman!!"

Spurred by that certainty, Felt pointed at the green-haired woman and shouted.
Ram, who had been holding the reins on the driver's bench of their dragon carriage, acted immediately. As soon as she saw whom Felt was pointing at, she sought no further explanation.
She simply drew her wand swiftly, aimed its tip at the woman, and—

"El Fula!"

The blade of wind sailed smoothly across the chaos on the bridge, following a masterful trajectory.
In the hands of someone merely second-rate, that assault would have struck numerous bystanders along the way. Instead, it flew unerringly toward its target, slipping past obstacles as it closed in on the woman.
Just as it seemed the wind blade would mercilessly sever her slender neck—like the surface of water struck by a blow, the blade burst, scattered, and vanished.

"I broke it."

The woman murmured those words, leaning to one side atop the parapet and rubbing her raised fingertips together.
It made no sense. But there was no time to demand an explanation. Her attack had been blocked somehow, and Ram readied herself to unleash a second and third volley in succession.
But—

"—Below! Someone, please look below!"

That desperate cry came from Filore, who had tumbled out of the dragon carriage.
Having leaped from the carriage bed, Filore had crouched beside a boy crying on the roadway. She pointed beyond the broken parapet, pleading frantically with those around her. Felt followed her pointing finger and understood what she was shouting about.

Beneath the bridge, on the distant surface of the river, a great white shape was drifting along.

"That's a ship...!"

It was so large that, for a moment, she had thought a mansion or something of the sort was floating down the river.
It was a rather luxurious vessel with two levels of cabins. Its open deck and window-lined passageways appeared packed to capacity, visibly carrying a tightly crowded mass of people.
The river mist kept her from seeing the whole thing, but judging its full shape from what little she could glimpse, it too must have been carrying several hundred passengers.
And that ship was just about to pass directly beneath the collapsing Dragonback Bridge.

"—No!!"

The high-pitched cry came from Emilia, who had seen the same thing as Felt.
She had shouted because the sheet of ice spreading out to support the Dragonback Bridge had cracked and peeled away, taking part of a supporting pier with it, and was now plunging straight toward the ship on the river.
Just before the boulder encased in ice could crush the vessel from above, a furiously hurled section of iron railing pierced it at an angle, blasting it apart in midair.

"Stone-Face...!"

The one responsible was Marcos, who had been rescuing people from an overturned carriage on the bridge.
He had seized a broken section of railing and wrenched it free, then drawn back the iron fence—which must have weighed hundreds of kilograms—like a spear and thrown it with perfect accuracy at the falling boulder.
As a result, the boulder shattered before it struck, sparing the ship from sinking. Still, the falling fragments must have injured people. More importantly, that was not the end of the danger.

The ship below was still moving, and the Milesta Dragonback Bridge was still collapsing.

"—Sister Emilia!"
"I know!"

The bridge's intermittent screams sank a pitch lower, becoming deeper and heavier.
Instinct told her that sound heralded a fatal collapse. Indeed, without any great tremor, Felt felt the footing beneath her sink with a strange slowness, and saw the entire bridge tilt.

"————"

Emilia planted her hands on the ground, binding the crumbling bridge together with white ice spreading from beneath her feet.
The white icy mist born of that enormous surge of mana swelled explosively. From the bottom of the gorge, it might have looked like a gigantic flower of ice bursting into bloom through the dragon's back.
Knowing that everyone on the bridge and on the ship crossing the river depended on that icy flower for their lives, Felt spun around and kicked off into the air from the broken paving stones.
Using the shoulders of the people around her and the edges of stranded carriages as footholds, she vaulted over the line blocking the way, rapidly closing in on the green-haired woman who had caused all this.

The woman's raised hand still held a glittering lump—a shard of ice.
As Felt concentrated, some unusually cold part of her mind urged her to cut the woman's arm off before those fingers could close all the way.
Felt leaped over the final carriage and raised the dagger she held in a reverse grip—just then.

"—Huh?"

Suddenly, Felt's shadow vanished from the roadway. —No, it had not vanished. A larger shadow had fallen over it, leaving hers nowhere to be.
At first, she thought the darkness sweeping beneath her feet came from a cloud. It was that enormous. But as the river mist streamed violently and the canopies of the carriages lining the bridge all flapped in the same direction, Felt saw it: something far more solid than a cloud.

"Wha... huh?"

Looking up, Felt gaped, forgetting even to lower her raised dagger. Even the hostile woman stopped her hand with the ice pinched between her fingers, staring up at the same thing.
Above the gorge, higher than the cliffs on either bank, a castle looked down upon the dragon's back. —There was a castle in the sky.

"—Am I dreaming?"

That was too much. In spite of herself, Felt doubted her own eyes.
But however much she doubted them, there was unmistakably an entire castle up there. Exposing the underside that should have been rooted in the earth, the fortress floated in the air, pushing aside the mist filling the gorge.
As everyone caught their breath at that utterly extraordinary sight—

"—Subaru!!"

Emilia alone was staring at a single point on the castle, rather than the castle itself, as she shouted.
The desperation in her wide violet eyes cleared, replaced by a spreading light of joy. Following her gaze, Felt saw it too. —Two figures stood close together on a terrace jutting from the castle toward them.
They were a girl in a dress and a black-haired boy holding her in his arms—

"Let 'em have it, Beako!!"
"—Ul Minya!!"

A spirited shout overlapped with a solemn incantation, and violet light filled the sky.
Countless arrows of light poured down from the terrace, striking the Dragonback Bridge like rain. Passing over people's heads and between the carriages, they drove one after another into the tilting bridge spans and the white ice barely holding them together—and the bridge pierced by that light turned to violet crystal.

"————"

The tilting stopped, and firm footing returned beneath Felt's soles. It was proof that the bridge spans, which should have sunk beyond all hope of rescue, had held on at the very last moment.
One beat later, the people saw what had happened to the Milesta Dragonback Bridge.
It was—

"Hey, hey. Bro's still as ridiculous as ever."

In spite of herself, Felt could not hold back the words, half exasperation and half admiration.
After all, the Milesta Dragonback Bridge before them had changed beyond recognition. With a flying castle at its back, the ancient Dragonback Bridge, boasting three hundred years of history, had been replaced by an enormous violet crystal, revealing a new form that glittered in the river mist.
