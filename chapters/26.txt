Arc 2 Chapter 2: "The Forbidden Archive's Guardian and the Twin Maids"

That was a room that could only be called a "library."
The wide space was roughly double the size of a twenty-tatami-mat one-room apartment, with bookshelves installed everywhere, starting along the walls. Every shelf was packed tight with books, and it was hard to even imagine how many volumes there were in total.

"Books take up a surprising amount of space and they're heavy, so having a lot of them is a real pain. In my family, everybody loves manga, so we ended up with so many books the second-floor got its floor collapse on us once—"

The floor had dropped out with no warning whatsoever, a two-room-remodeling catastrophe. Fortunately, no one was hurt, and afterward the manga were kept in a storage warehouse, so it wasn't a problem anymore.
Even to Subaru, who had that particular book-related anecdote, the number of volumes in this room outstripped the house whose floor had caved in. Of course, there was no doubt the mansion's construction itself was sturdier than his own home.

"With this many books, surely there's at least one I can actually read... or is that wishing for too much?"

At a quick glance, there wasn't a single spine written in Japanese to be found. No alphabet either — most of what he saw were the pictograph-like characters he'd spotted on signs along Merchant Street. So that, apparently, was this world's official script.
If he was going to live in this world, learning it looked to be mandatory. For Subaru, who'd given up on language class back in middle school, that thought was already enough to induce despair.
Subaru let out a sigh at the ordeal awaiting him. And then—

"Staring rudely at someone else's bookshelves, and topping it off with a sigh. ...Are you perhaps picking a fight with me? Because if so, I'll take you up on it, I suppose."

"I don't have the communication skills to just start talking with someone I've never met, no prep at all. Cut me a little slack for the warm-up, my bad. So, with that — take two."

Raising a hand to the girl glaring daggers at him, Subaru stepped back out of the room.
Then he opened the door, entered again, and looked at the girl still standing unmoved in the center of the room.

"Oh, a person. Nice to meet you, hello there. Mind if we chat a bit?"

"I understand perfectly well that you're mocking Betty. Trespassing into someone's library on top of it — don't you think a little punishment is only fair, I suppose?"

To Subaru's restaged introduction, the girl smiled a smile both lovely and merciless.

This was — how many times now in this other world? — a beautiful girl.
She was younger even than Felt, probably around eleven or twelve. She wore a lavish, frill-heavy indigo dress, and her lovely features carried off all that excessive ornamentation remarkably well.
Her hair color was best described as cream, its pale shimmer grown long and set into rolling ringlets. Quite the drill-curls. A face that looked like the very word "adorable" made flesh — the kind of cuteness that couldn't help but loosen anyone's cheeks into a smile the moment she smiled.
And right now, that face stood primly, coolly composed, before Subaru.

"You know, being all stuck-up ruins that cute face. C'mon, smile, smiiile."

"It's only natural that Betty is cute. More importantly, the question is what to do with you, I suppose."

"'What to do with me' sounds ominous. My bad for ignoring whatever plans you had going. I'm the type who ends up drawing the one correct answer without even meaning to. Though I bet the GM would rather I trip every single prepared event."

The talent for landing on the one right answer, unconsciously, in a situation with four choices and no hint besides brute-forcing them all — people also call that "failing to read the room."
In the past, there had been plenty of events Subaru had unknowingly wrecked this way.
That business with the illusory hallway just now would no doubt be added to the list.

"Trying to relieve yourself in the hallway of someone else's house, and then, without even crossing the hallway lined with all those mana-sensing traps, just showing up here... In a word, the worst, I suppose."

"I'm the type who never takes a single detour, even in RPGs. Which is exactly why my second playthroughs are always so busy mopping up side quests."

Though in dating sims, since he was devoted to just one heroine at a time, collecting all the CGs was relatively easy. It was the detour-heavy types where the CG gallery ended up full of holes.

Setting aside that rambling train of thought for the moment, Subaru said "Now then" to collect himself, and looked at the girl anew.
Judging from what she'd said so far, it seemed she was indeed the culprit who'd rigged the hallway to loop. Apparently the hallway had also been set up with all sorts of surprise events designed to interfere with Subaru's urge to urinate. He'd averted every single one, though.

"Still, I get wanting to show off your own power, but sabotaging a guy's biological functions to do it isn't funny, you know? I nearly died a social death there. Just thinking about it makes me shudder."

"Thinking about it now, I should have just kept you looping in the same spot forever, I suppose. That way, at least Betty's humiliation would've been cut in half, I suppose."

"That's the spirit, Betty. Well, seeing as things got this far, I guess that means I outplayed you, real-life luck included. Seriously, no big deal, none at all."

The tidal wave of urinary conversation rose up again.
As Subaru marched in place to distract himself from it, the girl pressed a finger to the bridge of her brow, looking exhausted.

"W-what an exhausting person, I suppose... Roswaal, letting that girl do as she pleases is how I end up dealing with such a baffling person. I'll give him a piece of my mind later, I suppose."

"Exhausted, at your age? Kids are children of the wind, full of energy! Furrowing your brow like that is no fun! C'mon, buck up, buck up! Live strong!"

Thumbs up.
"Live strong" — the catchphrase that had been thrown at Subaru himself, more than once, back in the slums. He'd thought it was a bit much at first, but somehow, saying it kept making him feel more energetic.
The girl gave the increasingly fond-of-the-phrase Subaru a half-lidded look. Subaru answered that gaze by sticking out his tongue with an embarrassed grin.

"Wait, I suppose. That reaction isn't what I expected. It's disturbing, I suppose."

"Getting called 'disturbing' hurts more than getting called 'gross,' somehow! Anyway, mind if I get to the point? If I go off on too many more tangents, my bladder's gonna be in a real pinch."

"I don't understand the second half of that at all, but it does seem like it'd be better for Betty's sake too if you got to the point, I suppose."

"Okie dokie. So then..."

He almost asked, "Where's the bathroom," but caught himself — it was starting to feel like something that wouldn't stay a joke much longer, so he held back and left it out.
Whatever else was true, the person in front of him was, category-wise, a mage. Given the skill with which she'd looped the hallway, there was a good chance Subaru simply wasn't in her league.
Worse, Subaru was currently running on empty — sick, hungry, and with his combat strength notably reduced. Especially after that all-out sprint earlier, he could keenly feel how shaky his stamina was.
His wound had healed, but he was probably still short on blood.

"Liver, I want liver. Some liver and chives, replenish the blood supply. Tonight it's not 'Kotetsu' — it's me who's thirsting for blood, hyu~."

"Now you're going off on tangents about tangents, this makes no sense at all, this guy..."

"Ah, sorry sorry, back on topic, back on topic. Um, so, first off — where is this place?"

While the other party was cooperating and engaging with him, he ought to gather information. Grateful for the girl's diligence, Subaru went ahead and threw a few questions her way.
First, confirming his own position. Surely he hadn't been caught by Elsa's side at this point — but he couldn't be certain either way it was one of the other two. If he could get some certainty from her words, that would help, he thought, but—

"This is Betty's bedroom, I suppose."

"...Answering at face value like that and thinking it's the right response — that's the kind of by-the-book thinking common in young people these days, I think. Not great, you know?"

"I toss back one little tease and this is what I get!"

The girl, indignant at being answered with a straight face — she called herself Betty, but was that actually her name? It sort of felt like it might be a nickname.
As Subaru mused on that, the girl puffed out her cheeks. She crossed her arms, her glittering dress swaying as she stepped closer, and said—

"Betty's about at her limit, I suppose. Feels like it's about time to teach you a lesson, I suppose."

"Whoa, whoa, abusing your prisoner is so last-era, come on now! I'm just an ordinary guy holding in his pee — if anything, that makes me less dangerous than an ordinary guy, minus however much the pee-holding is worth."

"In the sense that you're about to wet yourself, that makes you more dangerous than ordinary, I suppose."

"Damn it, that's a good comeback. No, wait, I can't think of any way to prove my own harmlessness on the spot! Q-quaking here, I'm not a bad person, I swear!"

Shaking his head rapidly side to side, shrinking his body down as small as it would go, eyes welling up in a desperate last-ditch appeal. But the girl's approach didn't slow down at all — if anything, it sped up.
And then—

"—Don't move, I suppose."

A chill shot down Subaru's spine, like something icy sweeping over him.
Suddenly, the smell of old paper that had filled the library until then vanished entirely, replaced by a sharp pain, as if ice had been driven up deep into his nose.
His skin prickled, and a distant, high-pitched ringing filled his ears. The failure of his sense of smell was because part of it had been shut down so his other senses could concentrate.
In this case, sight and touch — every scrap of awareness was poured entirely into those two.

Right in front of him, the girl had already closed to arm's reach.
The height difference was stark. The girl's head barely reached Subaru's chest, and her pale blue-green eyes looked up at him from close range.
Her thin pink lips curved into a smile, and the girl's sheer, artless innocence alone kept ringing every alarm bell in Subaru's head.

"Something you'd like to say?"

The girl tilted her head, eyes wide, and asked the question of Subaru, whose lips trembled.
Sweat trickled down his forehead and across his cheek, and that sensation loosened his frozen body for the briefest moment — in that single permitted instant, Subaru searched for the best possible thing to say.
Yes, the one line that ought to be spoken here, the line that would break this deadlock. It was—

"D-don't make it hurt, okay?"

"To keep up the wisecracks even this far — I'm impressed, I suppose. Whether it hurts or not, isn't that entirely up to you, I suppose?"

Speaking in a tone of genuine admiration, the girl's hand reached toward Subaru's chest.
Her palm settled against his chest and gently, modestly stroked its surface.
A ticklish sensation, like a chill brushing the back of his neck — and then—

"Bwuh...!"

—in the next instant, Subaru felt the illusion of his entire body being seared by flame.

Something ferocious ran riot through his insides, a sensation of everything from his fingertips to a single strand of hair being burned to ash.
A painful discomfort, as if every inch of his body, inside and out, had been traced by fingers of fire, leaving nothing untouched.

His vision flickered, and before he knew it, Subaru had collapsed to his knees on the spot, crumpling down as tears streamed from his eyes in torrents. His breath came ragged, as if he'd been running for miles, and his whole body ached with the sluggish heaviness of the day after some reckless bout of strength training.
Above all, there was an exhaustion like something mental had been ripped clean out of him.

"Guess he didn't pass out. Tough as they said, I suppose."

"W-what did you do to me, you little drill-loli..."

"I just asked the mana inside your body a little question, I suppose. ...Mediocre, and yet with such an odd-shaped soul, I suppose. And the Gate's stuck shut too, it seems."

Murmuring something self-contradictory, the girl bent her knees in front of the collapsed Subaru.
Poking with a finger at Subaru, who was desperately propping up his own upper body, she said—

"Well, I've confirmed there's no hostility, I suppose. And with that, I suppose I'll forgive all the endless rudeness you've shown Betty so far, in exchange for this little mana collection, I suppose."

Poked past his limit, his trembling arms failed to hold him up, and his upper body dropped to the floor.
Kissing the ground face-down — this made three times now. His lips weren't so innocent anymore. Thinking that far, he realized: in this redone world, though, it was technically still his first kiss.

Amid these pointless thoughts, Subaru scraped together whatever strength remained in his body and, slowly, over some time, managed to shift the angle of his neck on the floor. He looked up — at the girl's face.
"Hm?" the girl said, watching Subaru's effort with amusement curling at the corner of her mouth. Subaru bared his teeth in a grin at that smile and said—

"You're... one of those, huh. Not human. Not in the personality sense, in this case."

"Considering you've already met Nii-cha, you're a bit slow to notice, I suppose."

Her manner of speech was more childish than her appearance suggested, and that only reinforced the impression of a cruel infantility — the kind of child who plucks the wings off insects for fun.
Seeing Subaru drooling and sprawled flat on the floor, and finding it amusing, only pushed that impression further.

"One correction... personality-wise too, you're not human..."

"Don't measure a noble, exalted being by your own yardstick, human."

That was a remark carrying a temperature far too cold for a girl to be uttering.
Somewhere in his chest, Subaru felt something smoldering. But even though he felt it, he had no strength left to act on it.
Mental and physical exhaustion, entirely independent of Subaru's will, was dragging his consciousness down into darkness.

—I only just woke up, and now I'm losing consciousness again?

"I'll go ahead and tell the others about this, I suppose. Wouldn't want you wetting yourself here of all places, I suppose."

*One last farewell fart, and I could really let it all out right here.* ...No, that took more guts than he had.
Unable even to fire off that petty comeback, Subaru sank back down into sleep once more.

"Oh my, he's awake, Sister."
"So he is, awake, Rem."

The sensation of waking from sleep was, ditto, the same as breaking the surface of water with your face.
Time shifted, and this second awakening began with the voices of two girls, identical in tone.

The soft bedding was apparently the same bed as before.
What seared Subaru's sleep-heavy eyelids was the sunlight slipping faintly through the curtains. The intensity of the dazzling light was gentle, and he sensed, instinctively, that it must be morning.

"Can't tell the time when you're out and about. Handy for times like that — the stubble-timer."

Delivering a sales pitch to an invisible audience, he sat up while checking the time.
Touching the stubble on his chin like a clock, by feel it seemed to be about four or five hours since he'd last woken. His body still retained a fair amount of sluggishness.

"It's currently seven bells daylight, honored guest."
"It's currently seven bells daylight, we should say, honored guest."

Perhaps because he'd looked toward the window, performing the action of checking the time, the voices kindly answered his unspoken question.
Seven bells daylight — he didn't quite grasp the meaning, but going by the wording, it was probably seven in the "bright" part of the day. Then again, he didn't even know whether this world ran on a twenty-four-hour clock.
It was hard to simply assume it meant seven in the morning, but if he didn't count that brief handful of minutes he'd been awake just now—

"Pretty much slept a whole day straight, huh. Well, no big deal for a shut-in like me who once slept fifty-two hours straight at his max."

"My, what a good-for-nothing thing to say. Did you hear that, Sister?"
"Yes, what a freeloader's remark. I heard it, Rem."

"And who exactly are you two, addressing me in stereo like that, my esteemed sisters!?"

Subaru flung the covers aside and bolted upright.
At that exaggerated motion, the girls who had been standing on either side of the bed, flanking Subaru, startled. They swept in a wide arc around the bed, met in the center of the room, clasped hands together, and looked at Subaru.

He'd more or less expected it, but the girls looking at him now, fingers intertwined, were identical, down to the last detail — twins.

Their height was somewhere around a hundred and fifty centimeters. Large eyes, pink lips, and shallow, delicate features that lent them both a childlike loveliness.
The two, with their identical faces, also matched each other with short-cut hairstyles, though their hair colors differed — pink and light blue, respectively. And their hair covered one eye each — but which eye differed too: the pink-haired one covered her left eye, the light blue one her right.

But such differences were trivial matters. In this moment, taking all those features into account, what most threw Subaru's heart into disarray was—

"No way... this world actually has maid uniforms!"

Apron dresses in a base of black, with white headdresses perched atop their heads. Two beautiful twin girls, dressed in what could only be called the orthodox, classic maid uniform style.
—This, right here, was the very embodiment of the maid ideal.

"Oh dear. Right now, inside the guest's head, Sister is being subjected to the most indecent humiliation."
"Oh dear. Right now, inside the guest's head, Rem is on the receiving end of the utmost disgrace."

"Don't underestimate my capacity. Both of you, together, fall prey to my delusions, my esteemed sisters."

Crossing both arms in midair, fingers waggling meaninglessly. At that pointless gesture, a flicker of dread crossed both maids' faces; they released their intertwined fingers and pointed at each other.

"Please forgive me, honored guest. Spare only Rem, and defile Sister instead."
"Stop this at once, honored guest. Spare Ram, and go ahead and ravish Rem instead."

"That is not remotely a beautiful display of sisterly love! Selling each other out like that — does that make me the ultimate villain here!?"

Shrieking "eek, scary," the twins joined hands again and fled.
Leaping out of bed, Subaru gave chase.
Around and around the spacious room the three of them ran, playing tag. And then—

"...Couldn't you have woken up a little more quietly?"

There, knocking twice from inside on the door she'd opened, stood a girl looking his way.
Her long silver hair carried a beauty untouched by any shadow, and today it had been let down, flowing naturally down her back instead of being tied up. Her clothing wasn't the robe he'd seen her wear in town, but a slim-fitted outfit dominated by black tones that suited her well. Her skirt was somewhat shorter than knee-length and glossy, though that stretch of skin was hidden by knee-high socks reaching up to her thighs.

"I get it! Whoever picked this — I totally get it, GJ!"

"...I have no idea what you're talking about, but somehow I can tell it's something dumb, which is kind of impressively sad."

Clenching his fist, Subaru let out an involuntary cheer.
The girl — Emilia — watched him from the doorway with a look of exasperation.
