Arc 5 Chapter 27: "A Grating Voice"

Gwahn, gwahn.

From somewhere both near and far, a heavy, hollow droning sound reverberated low.
It felt like the vibration of the sound was pressing right up against him. Sound entering through his eardrums shook his skull, and from there the tremor ran down through bone to the very tips of his hands and feet.

The flow of his blood had grown sluggish, an unpleasant sensation as if sludge had been poured into his veins.
His internal organs had weakened drastically, leaving him feeling like his stomach was packed with clay.

Oxygen wasn't reaching his brain, and what thoughts did surface were sparse and unreliable.
The heavily echoing sound showed no sign of letting up, and it felt as though his eyeballs were rolling around behind his eyelids.

"――――"

With a body sunk in terrible sluggishness and a listlessness that wouldn't let him move, Subaru slowly opened his eyes, dragging along rising nausea and dull thoughts.

His vision, accustomed to the darkness behind his eyelids, resisted the white light forcing its way in.
The thoroughly blurred world flickered left and right, various colors squirming faintly within a field of white — like a child had splattered paint across a white canvas.
Even that impression faded into something distant as the world gradually came into focus.

After a dozen-odd seconds, once his eyes had regained their normal function, what spread out in Subaru's field of vision was a dim room with a stained ceiling. Then, the presence of several people bustling busily about, and fragments of their bodies at the edges of his sight.

"—Oi, the young fella's woken up!"

A voice, suddenly raised, assaulted Subaru even as the ringing in his ears refused to fade.
There was real force behind that needless volume — enough that the people who'd been busy until then stopped moving, their attention converging on the source.

"Cat-ears, get over here! The rest of you keep at your work! My bad, my bad — back to it! C'mon, time's the name of the game here!"

"Ugh, so loud, nya. I've warned you about that so many times and it never sinks in. There are a lot of people trying to rest here, so get back to your own work already!"

The huge man clapped his hands, apologizing for having drawn attention with his own volume. A beast-man with a dog's head and Kansai-accented speech, and approaching him with visible irritation was a girl with cat ears — no, a young man.
Wearing revealing women's clothing, his body stained here and there with blood, Ferris looked down at Subaru from above and let out a sigh of relief.

"Looks like you're awake. Do you understand the situation... actually, can you talk?"

"...Ah. Ferri-su?"

"Yup-yup, it's everyone's favorite Feri-chan. And you're Natsuki Subaru-kyun. Right now, this place is kind of a makeshift field hospital, and you got carried in here with a serious injury. Following me so far?"

Ferris rattled off the information rapid-fire at Subaru, who answered in a hoarse voice.
Subaru worked his sluggish mind, chewing through each word and swallowing it down.

Turning his head, Subaru checked his surroundings. Only then did he notice he was apparently lying on a makeshift bedding of layered clothes and cloth.
And indeed, just as Ferris had said, the surroundings had all the trappings of a field hospital.

Around him, people lay on simple beds like his own, filling the space, gasping in pain and suffering as they received treatment.
The stench of blood and the sound of sobbing crossed paths in every direction — a scene of devastation. Magical healing alone clearly couldn't keep up; at the edge of his vision he caught glimpses of wounds being stitched shut with needle and thread.

"Th-this... what on earth happened—"

"Still seems like you're confused. Take it slow, try to remember what happened before you passed out. Once you remember that, you'll find the answer on your own."

It wasn't meant to sound dismissive.
Ferris's words weren't gentle, but that wasn't a matter of his mood. It was simply that even he had no room to spare. Rolled-up sleeves, and numerous bloodstains marring his white skin and cheeks.
It wasn't hard to imagine how much had been demanded of Ferris, a first-rate healer, amid this carnage. And the cause of this disaster was—

"The Witch Cult...!"

"Those guys really are the worst, aren't they. I knew it, but... I guess I was still too soft about it. Never imagined they'd go this far, nya. I should have imagined it, though."

Biting his lip in frustration, Ferris gave a short nod at Subaru's murmur.
Subaru understood, painfully, the regret trembling through Ferris. He understood, but this wasn't the time for that. There was something he had to confirm.

"E-Emilia! What happened to Emilia!? She's not here, is she!?"

"…………"

"Th-that Greed bastard... the Sin Archbishop took Emilia, and then I..."

His voice trembled at the end because he already knew his fears had been confirmed.
In Ferris's downcast eyes and unbroken silence, Subaru found his clear answer.

At the very least, Emilia hadn't been brought here with him.
And if the scene from before he blacked out held true, she had fallen into Regulus's hands—

"What about Beatrice?"

"...Beatrice-chan."

"Yeah. Beatrice — the girl who was with me, what happened to her? Ringlet curls, wearing a dress, cute face with a bratty look on it... B-Beatrice?"

That Emilia had been taken by Regulus was, in all likelihood, beyond doubt.
Given Regulus's demeanor, the chance that he'd actually harm Emilia — while not certain — was probably low. Unforgivable, but for now, at least that.
But what of Beatrice? At that scene, it hadn't just been Regulus — Sirius had been there too. And Sirius harbored intense hostility toward Beatrice.

If Subaru had made it here safely to a field hospital, then he'd escaped Sirius.
But who had protected him, and how?

"Hey, please. Tell me. Beatrice is..."

"――――"

The lack of an answer stoked his anxiety, and Subaru pleaded desperately with Ferris. Ferris closed his eyes once, then exchanged a look with the massive beast-man standing right beside him — Ricardo, captain of the Iron Fang — before the two of them turned together to look in one direction.
Following their gaze, Subaru's eyes went wide.

"Beatrice...!"

Set apart from the people receiving treatment, alone, was a girl in a dress.
Lying on a makeshift bed, not twitching so much as an inch — spotting Beatrice like that, Subaru flung off the towel blanket covering his stomach and tried to rush to her.
But the instant he tried to sit up, a sudden heaviness bore down on his head, and on top of that his right leg locked up with pain, throwing him off balance.

The weight in his head was from his listlessness, but the trouble with his right leg made no sense to him.
He hastily looked down, and at the sight of his own leg, his throat seized up.

"O-ah—"

"If Beatrice-chan hadn't cast healing magic on you, you'd be missing a leg right about now, Subaru-kyun. You should be thanking her."

Subaru's right leg had lost roughly half the flesh of its thigh, gouged clean away. Compared to his left leg, the right was conspicuously thin, wrapped over and over in layers of bandages as if sealing away something malevolent. On top of that, something like a splint had been fitted to immobilize the leg — surely the reason he couldn't move it freely.
His fingers reached toward it unconsciously, and the instant they made contact, a sensation like being struck by lightning made Subaru recoil.

"I remember...!"

Regulus's final blow, delivered as he left.
Having taken Emilia and spouted his nonsense, before walking away, Regulus had gouged the ground with the casual ease of a dog kicking dirt behind it, flinging earth at Subaru's leg.
In that instant, Subaru's right leg had suffered a wound as though torn open by some beast's claws. And the result was the leg he now had.

"By the time I got there, the kid's leg was hangin' on by nothin' but a strip of skin and a strip of meat. Figured there was no way it'd reattach. That little lady there used up every last drop of her healing magic cryin' the whole time, and somehow made it in time."

"And after that, once you got carried in, Feri-chan piled more treatment on top. Since it's Feri-chan's work, I can guarantee it'll heal back to normal — but for now the bone and nerves are just connected, and you need to stay still until the flesh regenerates. No overdoing it, got it?"

Ferris crossed his arms into an X, sealing off any overexertion from Subaru. But Subaru couldn't just quietly obey and stay away from Beatrice.
Before Ferris, Subaru struggled to shift and move anyway, drawing a heavy sigh. Then a massive hand scooped up Subaru — crawling along like a caterpillar — in its grip.

"I'll carry ya over to that little lady for now. Figure the two of you earned that much, at least."

"Sorry. Thanks."

"Don't sweat it, don't sweat it."

Carried bedding and all by Ricardo, Subaru was brought to the spot beside Beatrice's makeshift bed. Leaning over to check on her, he found Beatrice so still she seemed to have fallen into a sleep too quiet even to hear her breathing.

Beatrice, being a spirit, sleeping like a human was partly to avoid wasting mana through activity, but there was also the flip side that, unlike Puck, she couldn't vanish into an Anchor or the like — so sleeping was a way of reducing the burden on her even slightly.

For that reason, seeing Beatrice's sleeping face wasn't unusual for Subaru.
But seeing her this still, this deathly quiet, was a first.

"Is this... just sleep? 'Cause my anxiety right now is off the charts."

"Calling it 'sleep' might be misleading. Right now she's completely shut down as a spirit, dormant... mm, closer to a state of suspended animation."

"Suspended animation, why would—!?"

He touched Beatrice's forehead, startled by the coldness of the heat there. Tracing her eyelashes, touching her cheek — not a single adorable reaction came back. And on top of that, Ferris's report just now. It was Ricardo, crouching down, who answered Subaru, his face having drained of color.

"Way the cat-eared lady tells it, she used up mana right to the limit. And honestly, that tracks. The plaza where I happened to find you two — there were a whole mess of folks lyin' around with damn near the same wound as you. Every single one of 'em, this little lady here held the line on, all by herself, so none of 'em died."

"――――"

At Ricardo's sighed words, Subaru's breath caught in his throat.
People with wounds like Subaru's — that meant an increase in casualties from the shared sensation between Subaru, whose leg Regulus had torn open, and everyone else. No doubt Sirius's doing, out of spite. It seemed the monstrous man had withdrawn from there afterward, but Beatrice's desperate struggle had only begun from that point.

Treating Subaru, and treating all the people who bore the same wound as Subaru, equally.
Of course she would. Because Subaru was a greedy man who wanted too much. And this girl, who had ended up spending her time with a man like that, could never abandon someone in need.
So Beatrice had wrung out every last drop of the mana she had, and in exchange for saving all those lives, had collapsed.

"Is Beatrice going to be okay...? Even if I tell myself she'll get better with rest—"

"...Honestly, not really nya. No, to be blunt, it's bad. Even if Feri-chan's a top-tier healer, spirits are outside my field. And this kid's not exactly your average spirit, is she? There's almost nothing I can do."

"Wh-what do I do!? I have to save Beatrice... I..."

It had only been a year.
Only a year since he'd taken her with him and promised to make her happy. Beatrice's life couldn't end here.
Because she was a girl who had to be happy — happy, happy, happier than anyone else.

"The problem's that she's runnin' out of mana and about to disappear, ain't it? Then just bring some in from outside, no? If the kid's her contractor, then from him—"

"...You idiot, Subaru-kyun. Her Gate's collapsed, so we can't even funnel mana into her from outside. Sending mana through Subaru-kyun would be the quickest way, but—"

"Right, right. The Bokko Fruit. What about the Bokko Fruit? If I boost what's inside me and squeeze out mana to give to Beatrice...!"

"You idiot!"

Just as a glimmer of hope seemed to appear and Subaru raised his head, Ferris glared at him with a look of fury.
Startled by the unexpected sharpness of that gaze, Subaru flinched, and Ferris, seeming almost ashamed of his own outburst, fidgeted with his bangs as he went on.

"Don't make me say it again, nya. That thing is... it's genuinely, genuinely dangerous. Especially for Subaru-kyun's body, it's nothing but poison at this point. Do something like that and all you'll get is two corpses instead of one... don't you dare try it."

"…………"

Ferris's harsh words carried a tone that was almost pleading.
At that earnestness, Subaru shut his mouth and withdrew his rash suggestion.

Ferris was an expert in healing magic. Of course he'd already run through a thousand, ten thousand ideas for treating whoever had collapsed.
Whatever shallow notion Subaru could come up with, Ferris had surely already considered and discarded.

"I get that you're worried about Beatrice-chan, Subaru-kyun. I get it, but it's not like something's going to happen to her this very instant either. Right now there's more to worry about than just this one kid — there's a lot of other things we need to think about..."

"Besides Beatrice... right, right. Emilia, too! And..."

Ferris's words pulled Subaru back to the larger situation, and he looked around at his surroundings.
In the space that had become a field hospital, there were still numerous figures lying about — but that was the strange part. The people here clearly weren't all just those with right-leg injuries like Subaru's. There were many, many more people brought in for entirely different reasons.

"Where even is this place, and what's the situation... no, what's going on right now? Why is it so full of the wounded?"

"The Sin Archbishops, the Witch Cult — you said it yourself, didn't ya?"

"That's not all, is it? No. What I saw was just two Sin Archbishops. But this scale of damage doesn't look like just those two acting alone. Actually — it was already strange to think the Sin Archbishops would come without bringing Witch Cultists with them."

The two, being such an overwhelmingly powerful threat, had caught his attention first.
So Subaru had simply focused on the two dangerous Sin Archbishops — but, just as with Petelgeuse, it made far more sense that they'd come to the city bringing their Witch Cultist subordinates with them.
That would also explain the scale of this devastation.

"Two Sin Archbishops and their Witch Cultist subordinates. That's the threat attacking the city right now, isn't it?"

"On that point too, there's a lot we need to talk about..."

Ferris, his expression grim, started to respond to Subaru's conclusion.
But his words were cut off midway. Before he could add anything to Subaru's conclusion, an interruption came from a direction neither of them had imagined.
It was—

"Yoo-hoo. Yoo-hooo. Yoo-hoo-hoo."

Echoing through the space was a shrilly off-key voice.
Spun out in a carefree tone, it was clearly, jarringly out of step with the grim atmosphere. Like accidentally flipping to a variety show channel in the middle of a solemn discussion — that's about how absurdly inappropriate it felt.

"Wha— what is this...?"

Subaru looked up at the sound of the voice and hurriedly scanned his surroundings. But no owner of the voice came into view. Subaru quickly realized why.
The way the voice sounded now was close to how it sounded through a megaphone or speaker — an impression Subaru had had just that very morning, of all things.

"A Magic Vessel broadcasting to the city...?"

"Hey there, all you scum-meat out there — you doin' well? Bet you're gettin' all worked up over my gorgeous voice no matter how many times a day you hear it, ain'tcha? Kyahahahahaha!"

Bearing out Subaru's suspicion, the broadcast through the Magic Vessel continued.
At once, what came through was a woman's voice with a certain childish cruelty to it, a way of speaking that spat on and trampled all courtesy.
Her shrill laughter pressed insistently into the depths of his eardrums, stirring up a purely physiological revulsion.

"What is this, this idiotic voice. Hey, is this—"

"Shh, Subaru-kyun, quiet."

Ferris pressed a finger to his lips, ears pricked, silencing Subaru.
Ferris's expression at this disturbance was deadly serious. Looking around, Ricardo wore the same wary look, and the wounded lying nearby covered their ears, choking back sobs.
It seemed this wasn't the first time they'd heard this voice.

"Now then, now then — for all you scum-meat out there who just can't get enough of my lovely-girl voice, I've got a big announcement for ya! We were thinkin' we'd gotten kinda bored and might just head on home. — Just kiddin'! No way in hell! The day and night's only just gettin' started, ain't they? Kyahahahahaha!"

Grating, grating, a grating sound.
Her voice was saturated with the malicious glee of someone who takes a mirror and presses it right up against another's ear, delighting in scratching it with sharp claws.
What is this. What is this thing. Who — what is this voice, this woman?

Cold sweat beaded on his forehead, and Subaru gasped at the change coming over his own body.
Before his mind could catch up, his body was already sensing something was wrong.

"Now, puttin' aside my side-splittin' jokes there, let me continue the announcement. Like I said, this city here — we've gone and occupied it. All of you are birds in a cage now... no, wrong. Bugs in a bug cage, ain'tcha!"

"—!?"

"And bugs oughta act like bugs, havin' your lives hang on the mood of whoever holds the cage — now that's fittin', ain't it. Wings, legs, necks, torn off however we please and played with... kyahahaha, pathetic, pathetic! What sorry little things you are. Be grateful for our kindness, thinnin' out the herd for ya. Kyahahahahaha!"

Repeated, high-pitched laughter smeared entirely in malice.
A wickedness that took the utmost pleasure in looking down on others, trampling them, violating their very existence.
Of all people, Subaru had the strongest inkling of who could be behind such a thing.

"And, and, and. You dim-witted lot probably can't even grasp the real meaning behind my gracious words just now. So bein' the kind soul I am, for you brainless, half-witted, do-nothin' lumps of squishy meat who can't think about anything but gettin' laid when you've got free time — let me chew it up nice and simple, spit right in the face of you masochistic freaks who'll just eat it right up."

"――――"

"Bugs in a bug cage get toyed with at the mood of whoever's holdin' it, which means all you bugs can do is suck up to us, the ones holdin' the reins of that cage. When we're in a bad mood, you'll be tremblin' wonderin' if your wings or legs are gonna get taken next, won'tcha, you helpless lot — but as long as you keep bringin' the honey, why, I've got the kindness of a mother who won't rip your heads off. So — there — ya — go! Kyahahahahaha!"

At the repeated malice, Subaru fell silent and listened along with Ferris and the others.
With every breath, every word, every phrase piled on, he felt something murky and turbid pooling in his chest — but Subaru sealed it away with iron will.

And then, he noticed. Noticed something.
What is this.

"I'll be sendin' over our demands from all of us, to you scum-meat lot, a bit later. All you gotta do is scramble around desperately with your pathetic faces on, screamin' 'don't wanna die, don't wanna die,' squeeze it out somehow, and get it delivered to us. Do that, and I, who they say brought tears to the eyes of the whole continent with my kindness, just might consider lettin' go of the cage. Kya, how simple is that! Kyahaha, kyaha!"

Getting worked up over her own words, clapping her hands, stomping her feet as if seated in a chair.
The content, the dim-witted way of speaking, the grating voice — all of it set Subaru's nerves on edge, but that wasn't the only problem.

There had been a sound audible since a moment ago.
Presumably, the source of the sound was in the same room as the broadcasting Magic Vessel. It slipped in alongside the woman's voice, cutting into the broadcast, faintly reaching even Subaru.

But he couldn't place what that sound was.
It felt like he was one step, just a little further, from an answer — but it wouldn't come.
It wasn't that understanding wasn't reaching him. It was that his instincts were refusing to understand.

Don't notice. Don't notice. You don't need to know.
His heart pounding, his focus so sharp he could hear the blood moving through his veins. Rejection, comprehension, rejection, comprehension.

—That faint sound, to Subaru, sounded like the buzzing of countless insect wings.

Close. Unbearably close to the answer. Close, but — could it really be?
Could the buzzing of insect wings really slip into broadcasting equipment like this? Subaru didn't know the capabilities of Magic Vessels in this world. So it was nothing more than a guess. He was only feeling a sense of wrongness measured against his own sense of common sense.
And that wrongness, along with the buzzing, clung fast to his eardrums and refused to let go.

"Well then, that's all — my gracious little talk here's finished. My condolences, you perverted scum-meat. And you bugs, best of luck, or whatever. Like I said before... the four control towers that operate this city's waterways — we've each got one staked out. Wouldn't recommend gettin' any funny ideas, y'know? The face of a person who's drowned to death — not somethin' you can stand to look at, it's that ugly! Kyahahahahaha—"

The threatening, shrill laughter faded out naturally, and the sound cut off.
The buzzing that had been scratching at Subaru's anxiety fell silent too, and a wave of exhaustion naturally took hold of his whole body. Right after, Subaru looked up at Ferris and Ricardo.

"That broadcast just now — how am I supposed to take that?"

"Not much to take it as, other than exactly what it was. ...It's pathetic to say, but we've been getting outmaneuvered again and again, completely on the back foot. That's Feri-chan's situation right now."

At Ferris's answer, chewing on his nails with a sour expression, Subaru knit his brow.
There had been a lot of details in that broadcast worth worrying over, but at least the goal and affiliation were clear.

"So that just now, too, was the Witch Cult's—"

"There was a first broadcast while you were out, Subaru-kyun, and that one just now was the second. In the first one, she gave her name. The Witch Cult's Sin Archbishop of 'Lust.' Except..."

Ferris cut himself off there, his face troubled over whether to say the rest.
Not understanding the reason for his hesitation, Subaru tilted his head.

The Witch Cult's introductions, infuriatingly, all followed the same template.
Given the pattern, what followed would be a name.

The Witch Cult's Sin Archbishop of "Lust" — impossibly, a third Sin Archbishop.
And that name was—

"It's fishy, so Feri-chan doesn't believe it, nya. But that's definitely what she called herself."

Ferris prefaced it that way, warning Subaru the credibility was low, before saying it.

"Capella Emerada Lugunica. — A member of the royal family who shouldn't even exist."
