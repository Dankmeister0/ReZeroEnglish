Arc 8 Chapter 48: "The Thorn Curse"

—The name of Eugard Vollachia, the Thorned Emperor, is known far and wide — not merely within the Vollachia Empire, but across the entire world.

The reason lies in a tale of tragic love that has endured in imperial history: *Iris and the King of Thorns*.
The story depicts the meeting and parting of a gentle-hearted girl named Iris and the Vollachian emperor feared as the "King of Thorns" — and the tragedy of its final act, no less, moved the hearts of countless readers.

Their fated meeting, and a first parting. Then, betrayed by his own vassals and driven from his throne, the King of Thorns's reunion with Iris, and the beginning of the rebellion the two of them waged hand in hand — across the long, long passage of time, the manner of its telling drifted ever further from the true historical record, and yet the most essential core of the tale, that the two had loved each other deeply, was told again and again unchanged.
So beloved has the unchanging love story of *Iris and the King of Thorns* remained.

And yet, precisely because of that, the tale — told as a tragic romance that kindled warmth in people's hearts — had unwittingly obscured a portion of the true historical facts.

Iris and the King of Thorns did not spend their days peacefully nurturing love.
In an age of upheaval, in an empire where betrayal and anguished cries crossed paths at every turn, the two of them lived each day with their lives on the line, devoting themselves to the fight to seize the future they wished for — and in the midst of it, they fell into a burning love.

And indeed, Iris and the King of Thorns turned the entire Vollachia Empire upside down, and at last achieved the seizure of the throne. — Needless to say, the tragedy that followed need not be retold here.

But before that tragedy occurred, the two of them had truly accomplished it.
Iris, with her compassionate heart and devoted nature, won many to her side. And with Iris at his side, the King of Thorns achieved feats of war worthy of a Vollachian Emperor.
Feats of war worthy of a Vollachian Emperor — something unchanged since ancient times, the embodiment of the Empire's iron-and-blood code: namely, proof of strength.

—Eugard Vollachia, the Thorned Emperor, is the strongest emperor in the Empire's history.

"GODDAMN IIIIT ALLLL!!"

Roaring with a bellow fit to tear his own throat apart, Groovy Gumlet leapt back in a great bound and flung away the kusarigama, severed clean through the middle of its haft.
In the next instant, the kusarigama, freed from his grip, burst into blazing red flame in midair.
Had he hesitated even a heartbeat, that flame would have caught him too — his instincts, grasping the cremation he'd narrowly escaped, made his fur stand on end. But there was no time to breathe easy.

"The pup dances well. But if it's a dance you want, my star's is far more worth watching."

Flat and emotionless, even as he crooned with besotted pride, a crimson trajectory came at him like a riot of blossoming flowers.
Whatever was swung was the worst kind of weapon — one that didn't even permit a clash, since the kusarigama that took its blow had caught fire instantly. As expected of the treasured sword crowned with the Empire's own name — not merely its sharpness, but even its very properties were too troublesome by half. If only he could forge a masterpiece like that with his own two hands.

"If only the goddamn Excellency had shown it to me up close for once!"

Being a treasured sword that only its rightful owner could wield, chances to even see the real thing were vanishingly rare.
Spitting out his grievances over a general's treatment, Groovy made full use of the small stature his opponent mocked, dodging, dodging, and dodging again with swift evasive footwork against the crimson slashes.

If you can't block it, dodge it — against this enemy, that was the primitive but optimal answer. Had it been Goz or Moguro standing here instead, they'd have taken the blow head-on and simply burned to nothing.

Tasting the acrid char of streets scorched by the aftershock of the slashes, Groovy dodged the crimson blade-wind clean through. But he still couldn't catch his breath.
Even dodging the Sun Sword that burned and cleaved the world, the next black flash split the world in two.

"—"

Even his habitual curses wouldn't come out.
Riding the momentum of the Sun Sword's barrage, Eugard twisted his body and loosed a slash of the Wicked Sword that mowed just centimeters over the head of Groovy, crouched low with his fur bristling.
The result of that slash didn't end with "good thing my head didn't fly off."
Along the line the blade had traveled, tens of meters beyond were shaved clean away tracing its arc; the streets of the Imperial Capital tilted at an angle, and the collapse of severed buildings followed one after another behind him.

Sharpness so absurd that even forging a sheath to hold it had been a struggle — that is the power of the single blade called the Wicked Sword, Murasame.
A blade that exerts such impossible cutting power naturally demands a commensurate price of its wielder. For an ordinary person, a single swing alone could cost them their life — a realm beyond reason.

"And you go swinging it around buzz-buzz-buzz, like it's nothing, goddammit...!"

"Do you fret over me even as you face me? Then set that worry aside. Insignificant things such as yourselves need only fret for their own sake. To worry over me — my star alone suffices for that."

"Goddamn it, there's no talking to you!"

The Sun Sword, Vollachia, in his right hand; the Wicked Sword, Murasame, in his left.
As far as Groovy's imagination could stretch, this was the worst possible pairing of blades an enemy could wield, and though the man hadn't even given his name, the fact that he held the Sun Sword and wasn't burned meant he was its rightful, qualified owner.
Add to that the green hair, the crown of thorns, and the circumstance of the dead reviving as Undead — the worst possibility as to the true identity of this worst of dual-wielders surfaced in his mind, and Groovy's curses had no end. And so—

"Eugard Vollachia..."

"Even unspoken, I know who I am. But I commend the discernment that let you recognize me across the span of years."

Carrying instant-death blades in both hands, Groovy called out — his voice wrung out — to the man who'd put distance between them.
In answer, the Undead standing before him, atop the wreckage of a burning building, dipped his chin solemnly at the summons.
At that answer from the Undead — Eugard — Groovy gave a rude snort,

"Even I read books, more than most of us damn generals. And while I'm at it — I'm one of the Nine Divine Generals."

"The Nine Divine Generals... ah, so that post still exists."

"Sounds like it up and disappeared once, under your reign, though."

Whether interested or not, Eugard spoke with no trace of inflection; before him, Groovy reached behind his own waist and drew two hand-axes, readying them.
He still had plenty of Curse Tools left. The problem wasn't how many Curse Tools remained — it was how much of Groovy's life remained.

"——"

Keeping his eyes on Eugard, he glanced down at his own chest from the corner of his vision. As ever, in his chest where his heart lay lodged, translucent thorns writhed, driving their sharp barbs in deeper.

He'd thrown together a hasty countermeasure against the "Thorn Curse" that circulated poison through him and ate away at his heart, but this reckless, life-be-damned plan was never meant for a long fight.
From what he could tell, the "Thorn Curse" was a powerful curse that indiscriminately engulfed a wide area centered on its caster. That was why he'd predicted the Curse Arts User would be the type to focus on casting and sustaining it, staying hidden all the while.
Naive. Far too naive. His guess had been dead wrong — his enemy, far from hiding, was openly wielding a mighty weapon. No — the weapon truly was mighty, but the weapon alone wasn't the reason for this hard fight.
It was because the one wielding that weapon was a man of extraordinary, abnormal skill.

In the present-day Vollachia Empire, the rank of general is synonymous with standing among the Nine Divine Generals.
That is a system revived by the current emperor, Vincent Vollachia — one that, across history, has been revived and lost more than once. But the very first time the system of the Nine Divine Generals was lost was during the reign of none other than Eugard Vollachia himself.
Why did the system of the Nine Divine Generals vanish during Eugard's reign?
The reason was—

"—It couldn't be helped. Those weaker than I sought to take my star's life."

—Because the Thorned Emperor himself had, with his own hands, executed every single one of that era's Nine Divine Generals, without exception.

Of course, Groovy hadn't been among the Nine Divine Generals back then, nor, he supposed, had there been transcendent powerhouses like Cecilus or Arakiya. But even so, he couldn't believe the men of skill who'd borne the name Nine Divine Generals back then had been so weak as to not even reach the feet of Groovy and the others today.
In short—

"—A short, goddamn fight to the finish it is, then!"

"Cease piling up such unclean words. It is disrespectful."

Kicking through the street littered with the marks of slashes, Groovy's body flew at Eugard.
Readying his twin hand-axes, Groovy came on; Eugard, pointing out not the weapons but the language, stood composed, ready to meet him with the Sun Sword. Against that fully-prepared opponent, Groovy bared his fangs ferociously and brought a hand-axe crashing down in a vertical split.

That was, naturally, blocked by the Sun Sword raised as if in salute — but that was exactly what he wanted.

"Hm."

At the instant axe and Sun Sword collided, Eugard's face cracked. Literally.
The fissure that ran across that pale, impassive cheek was proof that Groovy's blow had landed.

"That hurt, didn't it, you bastard!"

Groovy laughed at Eugard's puzzled look, even as the burning hand-axe went flying off to the side.
He'd let go of the axe the instant it struck the Sun Sword, to avoid catching fire himself — but its effect as a Curse Tool had already triggered: not to cut what it struck, but to rupture it from within through pure shock.

Originally, it was a Curse Tool made to kill an opponent clad in thick armor and a steel helm, armor and all, without needing to strip it off first. The hand-axe's blade, vibrating so finely and rapidly it seemed to vanish, would — even through the briefest contact — travel through the enemy's flesh and blood, pulverizing bone and organ alike.
Damage that would be fatal to any living creature — how effective it would prove against an Undead, he couldn't say—

"Interesting. But if there's nothing to follow it up with—"

"Who said there wasn't? I've got a goddamn mountain of these, my Curse Tools!"

If one or two weren't enough, then — Groovy drew more hand-axes still, one readied in each hand. Hearing his answer, Eugard's cracked face raised its brows just slightly.
Hurling the hand-axes in, Eugard, knowing he mustn't take this one either, spun his body and loosed a slash. Groovy leapt to dodge that too, and kept hurling hand-axes one after another.

"Come on, come on, come on, come on, come oooon!"

"——"

Between the roaring Groovy and the silent Eugard, an instant's exchange turned into a raging battle.
As Groovy's Curse Tools flew every which way, Eugard answered in kind with the duet of the Sun Sword and Wicked Sword, without pause.
A fight where a single graze from either side would tip the balance at once looked, on the surface, even — but in truth, Groovy was at a far greater disadvantage.

Remaining stamina, remaining Curse Tools, the lethality of each other's weapons — every factor was against him.
The deadly poison coursing through his blood whittled his life away moment by moment, and the Curse Tools he kept throwing would eventually run dry. His own tools dealt heavy damage on a hit, but the enemy's blade meant certain death on contact.
Even to an amateur's eye, whose advantage it was would be obvious — all the more so to the two actually trading blows.

So Groovy made use of the very fact that he was at a disadvantage.

"I'll show you something worth a damn."

Groovy never thought of himself as standing head and shoulders above the rest of the Nine Divine Generals.
There had been a time he'd boasted of being Vollachia's strongest, but that illusion vanished the moment Vincent summoned him and he laid eyes on the others granted the same rank of General. He wanted to be strong, but he had no wish to be the outlier — nor could he ever manage to be one.

In strength, he fell far short of Cecilus; in explosive power, of Arakiya; in versatility, of Olbart; in intellect, of Chisha; in the bearing befitting a General, of Goz; in survivability, of Moguro; in sheer freakishness, of Yorna; in anti-army performance, of Madelyn; in anti-personnel performance, of Balleroy.

That was Groovy's self-assessment, and the ceiling of what he could reach as a General.
And yet — even so — there was one strength Groovy had that none of the other Generals possessed.

—When it comes to the tenacity to kill an opponent, and the sheer thoroughness of how to go about it, Groovy is the very best.

"Bluh."

Mid-throw, blood gushed from Groovy's mouth in a great gout, his eyes bloodshot.
The poison circulating through him had ruptured the vessels in his blood, and it was now bursting and spilling out everywhere on his body. Watching Groovy cough up blood, Eugard's expression didn't so much as flicker.
He too was a master swordsman of the highest order, and surely understood that Groovy was closing the distance while resisting his own "Thorn Curse" by some means — and that doing so had been placing a strain on his body that it likely couldn't endure much longer.

The blood he coughed up was merely confirmation of that certainty.
Even if the blade never reached him, Groovy would simply run out of strength and die on his own. Yes — Eugard's insight must already have seen through to that.
And so, he failed to fully dodge the mist of blood Groovy sprayed from what he'd pooled in his mouth.

"——"

Frowning faintly, Eugard fell back, evaporating the bloody mist with the Sun Sword's flame.
That blood carried the poison Groovy had taken into himself, but even bathed in it, the damage to his opponent would be negligible. That wasn't the aim — poisoning the enemy.
The aim was to mark him with Groovy's blood. — And that had succeeded, on a sleeve he hadn't managed to whip fully clear.

"A goddamn deathtrap."

The instant Groovy grinned, blood-stained fangs bared, all around Eugard — the countless hand-axes he'd dodged until now — surged toward him as if drawn by threads.
Homing shots guided through Groovy's blood as a medium — the Curse Tool, Blood Hatchet.

"This is..."

Eugard's eyes widened as he dodged the drawn-in Blood Hatchets, only to watch the ones he'd thought he'd evaded curve around and come right back at him.
A storm of Blood Hatchets closing in from every direction — Eugard dodged, and dodged, and kept on dodging with astonishing physical prowess, but unless he struck them down, they'd keep flying at him endlessly, and eventually catch up.
And Groovy wasn't well-mannered enough to wait leisurely for that eventuality.

"You're always wondering when it'll end, aren't you? I'll give you the goddamn answer!"

What Groovy held now wasn't another Blood Hatchet, but the chain weight, severed off the kusarigama that had been burned by the Sun Sword in their first clash.
Specially treated, that chain weight — he spun it in a short arc and hurled it, arrow-fast, at the feet of Eugard, who was desperately dodging the Blood Hatchets.

"—!"

Eugard turned his wrist and caught that blow from the chain weight on the flat of the Sun Sword.
The eye that instantly discerned the difference from the Blood Hatchets and judged this one safe to block was admirable. But there was nothing among the armaments of Groovy Gumlet, the Curse Tools Craftsman, that was safe to touch.
The instant it struck, the chain weight flared red, and a tremendous explosion engulfed Eugard.

"Ohhh—!"

Battered by blast and flame, Eugard finally let out a groan of pain and was blown backward. Even so, he righted his tumbling body and braced at once for the follow-up.
Toward that Eugard, his movement stopped, the Blood Hatchets swarmed in on their target.

One Blood Hatchet shatters bone; two, organs; three or more, a life.
Ten or twenty raining down at once — even Eugard couldn't withstand that.
So Groovy was certain he'd finished him—

"—Be proud. You are stronger than any of the Nine Divine Generals of my own era."

In an instant, Eugard's fierce face loomed right before Groovy's eyes.
A slash from the swung Wicked Sword shaved off the right ear of Groovy, who tilted his head aside on reflex, and the opponent's kick snapped up into his solar plexus, sending his small frame flying backward.
But even as he was blown back, Groovy unhooked his belt and swung the segmented sword strung together from Snake Human fangs, sweeping it at the pursuing Eugard's head.

"Had you stood among the traitors, my star's life might truly have been in danger."

Writhing, lashing out like the long body of a serpent, the segmented sword's blow closed in — and Eugard, mercilessly taking it apart with the Wicked Sword, rated Groovy's fighting strength against the measure of his own experience.
As proof of how much he prized that evaluation from history's strongest emperor, Groovy clapped his feet together, launched a pair of grapple-hooks from the hem of his coat, and moved to shatter both of his opponent's shoulders.

"Impressive still, even in this state."

Both his heels sheared away along with the grapple-hooks, Groovy spat up the blood the kick had forced up his throat, tore the cloth wound about his neck, and spread it wide in the air.
For an instant, that cloth cut off the line of sight between Groovy and Eugard.

"GOD... DAMN... IIIIT——!!"

Through the spread cloth, Groovy flicked the mana crystal embedded in his own throat with a finger, unleashing a roaring shockwave that shook the very air at Eugard.
An attack from an unseen position, all but sound itself — an ambush with no dodging it.
But against that sound, Eugard brought out the true nature of the Wicked Sword.

—There is a saying: to strike the core.
It carries the sense of getting to the very heart of a matter, but every thing has a "core" that phrase is fit to describe. In any object, any phenomenon, even in concepts themselves, there exists a "core" that reveals its essential nature.

The Wicked Sword Murasame is an Enchanted Sword that cleaves in two whatever "core" it catches.
Once, Murasame — despising Groovy, who had melted it down and reforged it — severed the "core" of its own scent, so that his keen nose could never track it down again.
Since then, no one has been able to trail the Wicked Sword Murasame by scent.

And here, Murasame cut the roaring shockwave Groovy had unleashed.
Groovy's eardrums caught the sound of the mana crystal embedded in his own throat shattering, and the instant he registered it, the slash pressed on, set to cleave Groovy himself in two along with it—

"——"

A sudden gust of wind saved the life that should have been severed.

"Whew, that was close. Guess I owe my life to taking a little stroll — same as you, huh."

A tall figure appeared out of nowhere, catching Groovy — every one of his emergency-scattered Curse Tools neutralized one after another, his own life a hair's breadth from being cut short — and spoke in an easygoing tone.
Both Groovy and Eugard alike couldn't hide their shock at the sudden intruder.
Not only had he cut into this fierce battle, he hadn't let either of them sense his approach until this very instant.

"Debee..."

"Ah, don't push yourself. It was the technique that got cut, sure, but your voice caught some stray damage too, didn't it? Force yourself to talk and you won't be talking ever again."

In response to Groovy's hoarse voice — like it was forced through clotted blood — the one who'd sized up exactly what had happened, a black-furred Wolf Human, set him down on the spot.
Then, narrowing his thread-thin eyes, he looked toward Eugard, who glared back at them,

"Huh. Curse Tools that chase you down no matter where you run — handy things to have, and yet you neutralized them with one arm. And what's more."

There, the Wolf Human's gaze caught Eugard's lost right arm regenerating.
Just as the Wolf Human said, at the instant the storm of Blood Hatchets detonated, Eugard had sliced off his own right arm, sleeve and blood-mark together, and dodged the attack that way.
It wasn't that such a means of evasion was beyond consideration — but even so, losing an arm should have dulled his movements accordingly. Even accounting for his being an Undead, that shouldn't have changed.

And yet Eugard showed no difference in his movements before and after losing the arm.
That was beyond what Groovy had anticipated, and it had led to the fatal opening that, without the Wolf Human's interference, would have cost him his life. — But what that meant was—

"Oi, guh— thit."

"...Did you just call me 'shit'?"

"Neh-mine' tha'—!"

"I know, I know. Didn't need you to say it — I already got it."

Dipping his chin at the summons, the Wolf Human bit down on a golden pipe and set flame to its tip.
Then, exhaling smoke, he went on, his tone dropping just slightly.

"Well, well. Seems there was a real nasty piece of work for a caster. — No love in it at all, huh."

Looking at the same thing the Wolf Man was, Groovy found himself sharing the same thought.
Eugard, testing the feel of his regenerated right arm — the ancient, mighty emperor with the Wicked Sword in his left hand — shed his upper garment, singed by the blast, and cast it aside.

—And there, on Eugard's bared chest, the same "Thorn Curse" afflicting Groovy had taken hold.
