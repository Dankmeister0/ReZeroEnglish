There was a premonition of searing pain about to come rushing up through him.

It was an unpleasant sensation, but ever since coming to this world, he'd never lacked for opportunities to suffer life-threatening wounds like this one. And that sensation was telling him — these next few seconds would decide everything.

Petra's scream echoed through the narrow passage as she reached out, as if to pull free the two stakes driven into Subaru's left side. He knew — the instant her hand touched them, it would begin. So before her fingers could reach, Subaru sent his mind spinning at dizzying speed.

Two stakes — not wounds severe enough to be fatal. A few more seconds before the pain hit. Petra was frozen, unable to move. Where had the attack come from? Her hand was still on the open door. A shrill voice echoed around them. And sliding through the middle of it, a voice steeped in murderous intent slipped into his eardrums.

—Elsa.

Right in front of him, he could tell a shadow was writhing in the lightless dark. And that it was Elsa, crouched low, poised to spring at them in a crawling stance.

The strike that had pierced his waist had been thrown from further down the passage. With sickeningly precise control, consistently aimed at his gut. He almost wanted to applaud.

An absurd thought, a naive one. Why was Elsa here, now, of all times? Where had the grace period he was supposed to have gone? Why was she lurking in a hidden passage no one else should know about? How did she know? All of it could wait. Questions could come second — right now, burn every brain cell just to survive this instant——!

"—Shamaaaaaaaak!!"

No weapon, no means to intercept, woefully unprepared and unresolved.

Facing the sudden crisis head-on, there was only one thing Subaru could do — the single act he'd resolved beforehand to take without any regard for appearances, should he ever cross paths with Elsa.

In answer to Subaru's cry, his imperfect Gate converted the Mana within his body into something approximating an incantation. From the tip of his right hand, thrust out before him, black smoke burst forth — darkness swallowed the passage.

A pitch black deeper than even the shadow of light devoured the narrow space, instantly severing the threat that had closed in on them from Subaru and the others. The smoke itself had no power to hinder movement — a flimsy haze that anyone could push through without resistance. But—

"A wall of incomprehension — go on and cross it, if you can — gwaaaaahhh!!"

The instant he tried to spit out his defiant taunt, the delayed onslaught of agony struck him. Starting at his left hip, searing heat coursed through his whole body — his skull and lower abdomen scorched as though rammed through with red-hot iron, and a scream tore out of him. On top of that, the price of the imperfect incantation — the sensation of Mana being wrung from his entire body far beyond what was needed — brought a bone-dry exhaustion and lethargy that nearly buckled his knees.

What held him up as he was on the verge of collapsing was—

"Subaru——!"

A small, soft touch, gripping the hand of Subaru as he began to buckle. Looking down, he saw Petra, her face on the verge of tears, her long eyelashes trembling as she worried over him.

In her eyes was fear at not understanding the situation, and rejection at the unreasonable threat closing in before her. But stronger than either of those was her concern for Subaru's safety.

The instant he registered that, he forgot — if only for a moment — the nerve-grinding agony and the sense of loss like his soul being flayed away. While that effect lasted, he squeezed back the hand gripping his,

"Anyway, we're heading up——!"

Since they couldn't go forward, the only escape route was the single path back the way they'd come. Even Subaru himself didn't know how long Shamak would last. Whether his body had grown somewhat used to it after several uses, the only achievement he had to show was that he hadn't exhausted his Mana to the point of passing out.

Regardless, he couldn't afford to waste the chance the black haze had bought them by blocking the way——

"Gih... agh!?"

The instant he stepped out to break into a run, the pain of something sharp gouging into his flesh struck him once more.

When he looked to the source of the pain, he found a total of four stakes driven into him, from his right shoulder to the back of his neck. Fortunately, they hadn't gone in deep, but the pain of having several things as thick as a pinky finger screwed into him only intensified the moment he laid eyes on them.

"She can see...!?"

For a moment he thought she might be seeing straight through Shamak's black smoke, but he realized instantly that wasn't it. And beyond the smoke, Subaru understood by instinct exactly what Elsa had done.

Judging the black smoke to be some kind of threat and diving into it too dangerous, Elsa had simply hurled her weapons blindly through the haze toward them, without even bothering to aim.

The passage was narrow — barely wide enough for three of Subaru side by side. With enough control to hit dead center of the passage, there was a high chance of striking somewhere on the backs of anyone fleeing.

The moment he realized it, Subaru yanked his arm back and pulled Petra into his chest. Petra let out a shriek of "Hyah!" as, at the exact spot her body had just passed through, an iron stake identical to the ones lodged in Subaru's body whistled past.

Had he not thrown off its aim, it would have been a straight line to Petra's head.

"This is... the worst!"

Spitting out blood-flecked saliva, Subaru shook his head and bolted down the passage toward the mansion. He pulled Petra's arm, forcing her forward whenever she started to lag behind.

His vision flickered with pain. A world blinking red and black. A faint, hazy blue glow floated in the dim passage. Red and black flashed alternately across it, until the world was no longer distinguishable at all.

In just that instant of combat, Subaru had utterly spent both his will and his strength.

Even if he made it back to the mansion like this, no way to break the situation open came readily to mind. Simply clinging to whatever hope lay immediately ahead, Subaru clenched his teeth to the point of grinding them and kept running.

In that instant, a chill shot up the back of his neck — perhaps a sense of imminent death that only a Subaru who had brushed against "death" so many times could pick up on.

"———!"

Led by that unpleasant chill, he tilted his head back, and in his dark eyes Subaru saw the trajectory of death.

To say it "cut through the wind" would be far too gentle a phrase — a blade that killed the wind itself as it closed in. Elsa's greatest and most vicious curved blade, her kukri knife, bore down on their backs at tremendous speed, spinning end over end with the full weight of its mass behind it.

A speed there was no way to react to. A force there was no conceivable way to match.

And yet, in the face of all that, the fact that he managed to instinctively throw out his arm could only be called an unmistakable miracle occurring in that very instant.

His fingers stretched out as if to seize the blade's edge, and he did manage to catch the edge of the curved knife between his right index and middle fingers — but he couldn't kill even a fraction of its speed or force, and the middle finger, ring finger, and pinky of his right hand were blown apart, scattering in pieces.

The knife continued on, splitting Subaru's arm lengthwise, cleaving it clean in two from wrist to elbow. Driven by the momentum, the outer side of his lowered arm slammed into the wall, and the spray of blood mist painted the passage — and Subaru himself — with red speckles. A roar. A scream. A voice so violent it felt like his throat would crush, burn, and tear apart.

His vision went entirely red, and his molars cracked and shattered from clenching too hard. He lifted his torn-open arm. Red. Just red. Something white was visible. It turned red almost at once. It no longer felt like his own — just some superfluous object emitting nothing but pain.

Just tear off already. Just disappear already. He didn't need an organ that did nothing but hurt. Unnecessary. Disappear, fly away, rip off. Drop dead. Die, die, die—— a touch.

The feeling of his hand being held. On the side opposite the organ that was only pain, there was still warmth. The instant he felt it, his scream cut off. His throat had given out. The neural pathways that only registered pain exceeded their capacity and burst. He forgot the pain. But he didn't forget the warmth.

Pulling his arm in, stepping forward, trembling his voiceless throat, Subaru broke into a run, painting the passage with blood as he went. Heavy — his legs. Heavy — his arm. Was he pulling, or being pulled? He couldn't even tell that much. He didn't know. Didn't know. Didn't even want to know.

The passage came to an end. He was back at the stairs. Climb the spiral staircase, and he'd come out into the mansion. And then what? Who — who would — help whom — Emilia, Rem——?

"It can't... be over...!"

It couldn't end. It hadn't ended. There was no way it could end.

He hadn't seen anything yet. Hadn't found it yet. Hadn't grasped it, hadn't seized it. So how could he possibly throw everything away in a place like this?

He looked up. The top of the spiral staircase was far away. His legs tangled. His tongue went numb. Blood dripped from his arm — his life spilling out of him. Wearing away, dwindling, he pulled at the warmth in his left hand. And then,

"——baru-sama!!"

A beast-like cry. Then the sound of something heavy dropping and landing out of midair. Right before Subaru's eyes as he climbed the stairs, a broad, large back came into view. An apron dress trimmed in precious black fluttered amid the drifting smoke. Long golden hair swayed in the cold wind, and the body that had crouched to absorb the landing rose back up.

A stern face turned back toward him — a familiar face, in which concern showed through.

"Fre-de..."

"Don't talk! These wounds... how terrible."

The moment he registered her presence and tried to call her name, Frederica caught sight of Subaru's wounds and her face went pale. She looked with anguish at his right arm, which had half lost its shape as an arm at all, and then her gaze went to the trail of blood staining half his body, before—

"Ah..."

—she drew in a breath, her voice fading almost to nothing. Subaru's wretched state must have been that shocking. As for Subaru himself, under the flood of his brain's own painkillers, he was starting to lose the ability to register pain as pain. His breathing was ragged, drool dripping unceasingly from the corner of his mouth. He spat out the foam of blood overflowing in his mouth and tried to form words for Frederica,

"Ahh—!"

"——Shh!!"

A slash from the kukri knife closed in once more from beyond the darkness.

The blade, spinning with death coiled within it, aimed for Frederica's head. Subaru, catching sight of the flash of steel, cried out on reflex, and Frederica, reacting to it, drew something from her waist in a single flashing motion — sparks scattered through the darkness of the passage, and with a high, ringing clang the curved blade was knocked away. What accomplished that was—

"It seems we have an intruder."

Frederica said, crossing her arms — on the tips of both hands, she wore gauntlets fitted with claws. From the assured way she held her stance, it was clear these were weapons she knew well.

In a way, the rugged weapons suited her almost too perfectly. Slicing the air with both arms to keep whatever lay ahead at bay, Frederica spoke back toward Subaru,

"To the mansion. Signal once you reach the top. I'll withdraw as well."

"Bu... uh..."

"With those wounds, you'd only be a hindrance either way. ——Please, take care of Petra."

What pushed Subaru's back as he tried to stand his ground was that final, almost pleading request from Frederica. Swallowing the words he'd been about to say, Subaru pulled Petra's small body close. Carrying her would be faster now than pulling her along by the arm. Petra let herself be gathered into his arms without resistance, and Subaru stepped backward, setting his foot on the stairs,

"Don' die..."

"Of course. ——After all, I'm only halfway there."

Dragging his feet, torn between staying and leaving, Subaru raced up the stairs toward the upper floor. As he climbed, spiraling upward, the clash of blade against blade rang out from below. In a cramped space, Elsa would lose her mobility too, and a head-on fight would come down to raw ability. There, Frederica would have a fighting chance too — he wanted, at the very least, to cling to that hope.

Spitting out fragments of his shattered molars, Subaru cursed his own legs for their maddening sluggishness. Faster, sharper — for every one second Subaru spent conquering the stairs, Frederica's fate grew one second shorter. Faster, faster, to the upper floor, to the upper floor, to the upper floor——

"Ma... made it...!!"

He reached the top and, gasping for breath, dropped to his knees on the carpet. Still in that collapsed posture, he thrust his head into the escape passage and shouted down toward the floor below.

"Fre... derica! Now—ow—!!"

If Frederica reached the top of the stairs and the escape route closed right behind her, they could cut Elsa off. Realizing this even as he shouted, Subaru practically threw himself toward the statue with the opening-and-closing mechanism. He put his hand on its neck, waiting for Frederica to burst through. But——

"——Eh"

What suddenly struck Subaru's ears as he braced himself was a tremendous impact and the massive crashing roar of a collapse. Falling building materials smashed against each other, billowing dust into the air as a violent tremor shook the mansion.

What's happening — Subaru left the statue's side and went back to the hidden passage. He peered inside — and saw that the spiral staircase he'd just climbed up moments ago had collapsed without a trace.

"This... aah"

This wasn't the kind of collapse where shoddy construction finally caught up. For an unexpected cave-in, it had broken far too cleanly, without damaging the passage itself. From the start, it must have been rigged so that, using some mechanism, the staircase itself could be detached.

Whether it was to prevent pursuit after using it to escape, or to prepare for exactly this kind of situation — being used as an infiltration route — there was no way to know now. All he knew for certain was—

——Like this, there was no way Frederica could ever make it back up.

The possibility of Elsa coming up to the floor above had been crushed too, but by the same token, Frederica's survival was hopeless. Perhaps, if she outmatched Elsa in strength, she might circle back around some other way and return in triumph — but Subaru's own body knew exactly how slim a chance that was.

The instant he thought that far, the wounds he'd forgotten about began to throb, and Subaru coughed up a clot of blood. The stakes lodged in his shoulder, neck, and hip began to eat into his flesh with a dull, festering ache. Even trying to pull them out, his fingers slipped, and terrified of the massive blood loss it would cause, his trembling fingers refused to move once they touched the stakes.

"What... the hell am I doin'... Idiot. I'm such a damn idiot..."

There was no time to stop his feet or stop his thoughts. Whether Frederica's survival was truly hopeless was still, surely, something Subaru's own actions could change.

Enduring the pain of his wounds, he let his knee slide, stood up, and tried to spring into action. And then, all at once, he remembered Petra, who should have still been in his arms. He'd rushed back to the office — where had she gone, when she'd been in his arms until just then——

"Pet... ra...?"

Turning his head around, Subaru spotted Petra on the other side of the room — near the statue. The fallen girl lay on her side; it seemed that, in the chaos just moments ago, he'd let go of her hand.

The limp girl showed no response to his calling out — perhaps unconscious. At the extreme of fear and exhaustion, she might not have been able to hold onto consciousness.

Even as he worried over Frederica's fate, he had to protect Petra through to the end, if only to honor her words. Forcing his trembling knees to bear his weight, Subaru made his way to the fallen Petra. And as he moved to lift the collapsed girl up—

—he saw the curved blade driven in from the back of the fallen girl's neck through to the back of her head.

Blood dripped heavily from the wound, part of her brain spilling out from her cracked skull. Her soft chestnut hair was heavily soaked with fresh blood, and her soft, warm palm no longer moved.

He raised his right hand — a pitiful lump of flesh, missing three fingers and half its arm. The very curved blade he had thrust this arm out to stop had gone on to strike Petra all the same. He had given up this much, and hadn't managed to protect a single thing.

"———OOAAAAHHH!!"

His ruined throat let out a scream of blood.


* * *


With unsteady steps treading the mansion's carpet, Subaru made his way toward the west wing, his face ghostlike. Cradled in his arms as he walked was Petra's corpse, a white sheet draped over her from above, hiding her death mask from anyone's sight.

Her death mask, frozen in an expression of surprise, was proof that it had been instant — and that alone was, at least, some small mercy. To suffer the same agony Subaru had, and lose her life on top of it, would have been far too cruel. She wasn't saved by it. Though there was no salvation to be found anywhere, in truth.

"I..."

Hadn't he come back because he wanted to save everyone in the mansion? Hadn't he resolved, in his heart, to do everything he could to be even the smallest help toward that?

He had dragged Petra into this spiral of death yet again. This made the third time Subaru had seen Petra's dead body — and every single time, it was an ending that should never have happened, if only Subaru had done something differently.

Last time, it had begun with the Witch Cult's presence. But this time, there was a decisively different element.

Subaru could have objected, back when Frederica decided to take her in as a maid-in-training, in order to spare Petra from being caught up in any of this.

He should have known — that by being near Emilia, by being near him, she might end up in danger.

"If only... what if... there's no end to it..."

Once the game was over, there was no end to saying "if only" and "what if." Subaru knew that much himself. And yet, being unable to help thinking it anyway — that was Subaru's weakness.

As his spirit broke, his dragging footsteps grew heavier. The blood that hadn't stopped flowing left dark red splotches on the carpet, and with every step he took, there was a pain like his nerves being filed raw.

One step, then another — the sound, the pain, of body and mind being ground down. Even that felt, right now, like some kind of mercy — as if he were being punished. If the sin was Subaru's, then the punishment ought to fall on Subaru alone. As long as that rule was upheld, he didn't care how much pain was given to him.

So the girl in his arms, the woman who had stayed behind to let him escape, and then——

"Rem..."

Please, let no calamity come near the one still sleeping where he was headed.

He reached the west wing, the floor lined with servants' rooms. He'd taken the shortest route from the top floor where the office had been, but pushing his wounded body this far had felt like it took forever. His destination was at the very end, on the opposite side from the stairs.

What would happen once he got there — he wasn't thinking about that now. Getting there was the only goal, touching the person who should be there was the only goal, and he no longer had the will to live beyond that.

He'd bled too much, and mixed in with the blood that had flowed out, his resolve and resignation had spilled from his body as well. Above all, this time, he had lost far too much. Carrying this sense of loss, there was no way he could raise his head and keep walking on.

So at the very least, if he was going to meet his end, let it be at her side.

At the side of the one person in this world before whom Subaru could bare his own weakness.

Trailing a path of blood, half-leaning against the wall, converting what little willpower he had left into sheer obsession, Subaru's body barely managed to reach his destination — the door to Rem's bedroom.

He leaned Petra, still in his arms, against the wall and pulled back the sheet. A corpse whose eyes he'd closed, whose death mask alone he'd tried to arrange. He touched her cheek, traced her lips with a finger, and bowed his head over her cold, empty vessel,

"Sorry... m'sorry... 'cause I'm a hopeless... damn idiot..."

There must have been some way, but because Subaru was a fool, he hadn't been able to find it. The resulting sacrifice was Petra, and his words of apology could no longer reach her, who was no longer here.

Letting his tears fall onto Petra's knee, Subaru shook his head, pulled the sheet back up, and covered Petra's death mask once more. Then he stood and turned around.

"—Leaving me behind like that, I do think that's rather cruel."

Down the hall, at the top of the stairs Subaru had just come down moments ago, a beautiful black-haired woman stood with one foot on the step, tilting her head toward him. She toyed with the tip of her long braided black hair between her fingers, while in her other hand a blood-soaked kukri knife dangled, swaying gently.

A black bodysuit under a black coat — the same jet-black coordination from head to toe he'd seen back in the royal capital. Though she should have just fought Frederica, there was no trace of it anywhere on her — not a wound, not even fatigue.

The reason she'd appeared here — that alone was vivid proof of what had become of Frederica.

With yet another person added to the list of those his apology could never reach, all Subaru could do was look up at the ceiling and curse his own powerlessness with a feeling like coughing up blood.

"With wounds like that, I'm impressed you managed to walk all the way here."

"Then gimme a tip. Your life'll do just fine..."

"Shall I take that as a proposal — that you want my life for your own?"

"If yer just gonna snuff it out, then hand it over already..."

Sickened by Elsa's answer, wide of the mark as it was, Subaru glared at the murderer and, leaning on the wall, somehow forced himself upright. As his line of sight rose enough to bring Elsa into view straight ahead, she looked him up and down, taking in his battered state from head to toe, and said,

"The scent of blood, the smell of rage, the fragrance of 'death'... ah, every one of them is exquisite on you. All that's left is whether your intestines suit my taste — and then there'd be nothing left to ask for."

"You freak... I don't even know what the hell yer sayin'..."

With an ecstatic expression, hugging herself, Elsa gazed at Subaru with feverish eyes that made no effort to hide her arousal. Even on a beautiful woman, it was the look of a deranged mind — bringing Subaru nothing but a chill of horror and disgust.

Subaru let nothing but revulsion show on his face. To that Subaru, Elsa, her cheeks still flushed with a sensuous color, said,

"Talking with you is nice and all, but... I'd rather not lose sight of my purpose and get scolded for it. That young lady I met in the royal capital — the spirit, the half-devil — is she at home?"

"If ya called ahead 'fore comin', woulda saved us the trouble. Hired ourselves a mercenary company, gave ya a real grand welcome and everything..."

"I don't intend to answer that. In that case, I suppose I'll simply have to ask your intestines."

Her red lips parted, a pink tongue wetting her mouth in a provocative gesture. She then traced the flat of her raised blade with her tongue, licking away a drop of blood, and smiled in ecstasy.

Her posture dropped low, and wringing the blade back, she came crawling at him spider-like, launching herself forward. Fast. There was no conceivable way to intercept her. But—

"Like hell I'm dyin' by your hand...!"

Even as he said it, Subaru shoved open the door to Rem's bedroom.

Elsa knit her brow at the incomprehensible act, but that reaction alone was satisfying.

Subaru had already half given up on breaking through this time. His wounds were deep, his life kept spilling out unstoppably, and his fate in this loop hung by a single thread guttering in the wind. If so, then at the very least, let it be in a way that struck back at Elsa. At the very least, he wanted to repay her simply by refusing to go along with what she wanted.

Going down before her murderous blade — that, he refused even in death. If it came down to dying at her hands, he'd throw his own life away first. But before that, the one thing he wanted to avoid was her being violated.

Calling it a hard choice sounded fine enough, but it was also, in truth, an all too self-serving resolve.

But if it came down to falling to Elsa's hand like Petra had, like Frederica had—

Then she, in this world that was already coming to an end, should at least be by Subaru's own hand——

"I'll follow... soon..."

He would follow right after her, on the very same path. With that resolve, he pushed his way into Rem's bedroom——

"—Eh?"


——A forbidden archive lined with bookshelves welcomed Subaru, who had resigned himself to the end.
