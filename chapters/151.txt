Arc 3 Chapter 74: "Fulfillment of the Contract"

—Natsuki Subaru had an obsessive conviction that this situation must never be allowed to happen.

He didn't know why. There was no basis for it, no evidence—just a vague, formless unease. And yet the certainty that this must not happen sat firm and unshaken in his chest.

Atop the hill, Emilia stood with her silver hair streaming in the wind, palm outstretched toward him, while Petelgeuse spread both arms wide, his face alight with joy as if welcoming the encounter.

Subaru had, without even realizing it, been working all along to avoid the one thing that must never happen: letting these two cross paths.

And yet now, in this dead-end, hopeless, do-or-die situation—cornered with no cards left to play—she had walked right into it.

"Why... why now...!"

Before shock, before regret, anger surged up first.

It was anger at himself, for knowing this situation had to be avoided and yet failing to make the effort. And above all,

"You should have known better than anyone—Puck!!"

The furious roar tore across the grassland, reaching the spirit hovering beside the silver-haired girl. But the spirit, even as it caught the shout, gave no attention to Subaru at all—its gaze remained fixed, single-minded, on Emilia's profile.

It waved its paws, darting circles around Emilia, its face pasted with an urgency Subaru had never seen on it before.

"Lia! No, you have to leave right now! That's a Sin Archbishop of the Witch Cult! You should never have met him! You shouldn't be here at all!"

"It's too late to turn back now, and I have no intention of it anyway. Everyone's fighting so hard they've ended up like that—I wasn't raised to sit back somewhere safe and behave myself."

Emilia answered Puck's desperate plea, her cheeks tense with a certain strain. At her stubborn resolve, Puck pressed a paw to his face, shaking his head, "No, no, that's not it—"

"It's not about who's who or anything like that! It's the Witch Cult! It's the Witch Cult... ahh, I only just remembered now! The Trial! It's a Trial, Lia. The Sin Archbishops impose Trials! And by the Contract, I can't get involved in a Trial! This is as far as I go! So..."

Puck cut himself off, his expression going rigid as though barred from saying anything further. At the sight of the little cat—always so composed, never once letting his own pace be disturbed—even Emilia's eyes widened slightly in surprise.

"Puck... do you know something?"

"I don't know anything! But I know that I know something! That much I can tell. That's why this is bad, Lia. Go back right now. If you stay here, if you meet them, something more terrifying than death is bound to happen!"

Puck spoke an answer that was no answer at all. But for all his floundering, he made no move to physically stop Emilia, nor did he use his tremendous power to force the situation under control.

At this maddeningly frustrating attitude, Subaru felt an anger he couldn't contain rising in him. Without even understanding why his chest burned so hot,

"If Emilia ends up out here now... then what was the point of everything I—! What were you even for!"

"You're the one saying that to me!? Lia pushed past even my words—for whose sake do you think she came all the way out here...!"

His face twisted with mortification, Puck wrung the words out in answer to Subaru's shout.

At his answer, Subaru too grasped a truth he couldn't look away from, and self-reproach crushed down on him.

It was an obvious conclusion, a natural one.

There was only one reason she would have climbed down from the fleeing Dragon Carriage and come back to the battlefield.

Subaru and the others, who had stayed behind in the face of danger without a word, without telling her anything.

She was someone who always chose to lose out for the sake of others, who followed the shape of her own heart honestly no matter who it alienated—of course a girl like that would never accept being merely protected.

It was a misjudgment. Once again, Subaru had piled another one on.

And with the misunderstanding left to compound unresolved, it had come to light at the worst possible moment.

—There was no longer a single moment's grace to spare.

"Puck..."

Still atop the hill, Puck clung to Emilia, pleading with her to reconsider. But there was no way to bend her firm resolve.

Emilia, too, had half given up on asking Puck for help, and closed her eyes as if to rely on her own magical strength alone, beginning to chant.

And the eerie madman who had kept his silence, Petelgeuse—

"What... ahh, what a fine day, yes. Truly a reflection—surely there could be no body more fitting for a Vessel than this..."

Tears spilling, the madman took a step toward the hill, his voice trembling with passion he couldn't contain. His stepping foot wavered, but that gait was nothing less than a countdown to ruin, ticking down for certain.

"—"

Puck was no help to rely on. Driven purely by a sense of duty, Subaru rose to his feet.

The instant his touching hand pulled away, Patrasche's breathing quietly stopped. A single tear traced down Subaru's cheek. He wiped it away roughly with the back of his hand and broke into a run.

"Subaru-kyun!?"

Leaving Ferris's astonished voice behind him, Subaru ran, forgetting even the creaking pain that racked his entire body. Beside him, the battlefield where the Witch Cultists and the subjugation force clashed went unglanced-at; the only thing the running Subaru fixed his eyes on, single-mindedly, was the girl atop the hill.

Right now, he was the only one who could stop her encounter with the madman.

He didn't know how much good this body, this small and insignificant self, could do. He didn't know, but even so, he had to do it. Otherwise, how could he possibly—

"I would ask that you... not interfere—yes!"

Sensing Subaru closing in, Petelgeuse tilted only his head to look back, and his shadow swelled—jet-black magic hands burst forth, surging in a rush to entangle Subaru.

Against the crushing pressure of palms that seemed intent on tearing his limbs clean off, Subaru twisted his body, threading through the gaps without losing speed. Here, now, a change came over a body that should have been heavy with exhaustion and wounds—his concentration honed to a razor's edge.

In other words, the manifestation of hysterical strength born of an emergency.

Movement beyond his limits took hold of Subaru's body, and he kept dodging the encroaching palms by a hair's breadth.

At Subaru's abnormal movements, Petelgeuse clicked his tongue,

"How infuriating, yes, that you can see the Unseen Hand..."

"Don't you dare open that filthy mouth of yours again! I won't let you do a damn thing more!"

The distance between them dropped under twenty meters, and the number of magic hands pouring out to tear Subaru to pieces had swelled enough to blot out his field of vision.

He drove forward, dodging a palm that grazed his cheek. From his left cheek to his ear split open in a gash, and the pain of gushing blood seared his nerves. But driving his consciousness—on the verge of being torn apart by agony—past its limits, Subaru's body pressed on, forward, forward—and his outstretched arm surged toward Petelgeuse's slender shoulder, reaching to seize it,

Petelgeuse, head tilted ninety degrees, spat out his vicious sarcasm as he receded from view.

No—the ground beneath Subaru's feet had heaved upward at a tremendous speed, shoving his body, directly above it, up into the sky.

"—Ngh, ah!?"

"Subaru—!!"

"A mountain... of ice? No, a tower...?"

An iceberg, built up to the same height as the risen ground in order to save Subaru after he'd been launched into the air—that was the true identity of the mass that had saved him from a fatal fall.

His blood-slicked skin stuck to the ice, and wincing at the pain of skin being peeled away, Subaru pushed himself upright, feeling relief at his narrow escape and the weight of an irreversible mistake all at once.

—There was no need to wonder whose hand had built this iceberg.

"—Emilia!!"

But the risen earth blocked his view like a sheer cliff face, and cut off as he now was on the far side of the hill from Emilia and Puck, he couldn't see what was happening with Petelgeuse either.

All he knew was that Emilia's chant had been interrupted. And she couldn't see Petelgeuse's Unseen Hand—!

"This is bad... this is the worst. To get around behind this wall...!"

The skin of his palms stuck to the ice, and the pain of it tearing away seared through his brain. Enduring the sharp pain again and again, tears welling at the corners of his eyes, he descended the iceberg, literally bleeding as he went.

It was fortunate that there happened to be several handholds to grab onto, and combined with his desperation, he made it down the mountain of ice at a startling speed, half-falling the whole way.

But with no time to catch his breath, Subaru looked up at the wall and was at a loss.

"This wall... damn it, that bastard was playing me the whole time...!"

Slamming his blood-soaked hand against the earthen wall, Subaru gritted his teeth, cursing his own shallow thinking yet again.

He had assumed—underestimated the man—that sealing off Petelgeuse's Unseen Hand alone would be enough to neutralize him. As a result, he'd been thoroughly outmaneuvered—

"—Sir Subaru!"

"Only for an instant, but I caught sight of Lady Emilia. —Is the Sin Archbishop beyond here as well?"

At this sudden, fatal upheaval that had struck his body, Subaru couldn't even manage to voice words as words.

'Aaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaa—!?'

Just before his eardrums seemed about to burn away, he heard—saw—Petelgeuse tormented by the same agony as himself. Since they shared one body, the suffering Subaru endured was the same suffering that tormented him.

And that meant that he, too, was tasting this same helpless, hopeless ending.

—A feeling of serves-you-right welled up, and dissolved at once before the pain.

He writhed in agony, rolled, convulsed, and became unable to move at all.

When he came to, everyone who should have been bound was free. Of course they were. With the very person who should be wielding the Unseen Hand in this state, who could bind whom?

"Ferris! Why would you...!"

"It's not what he wanted himself, nya. He protected what he wanted to protect—so it's what he wished for... isn't it?"

Subaru no longer had the strength left even to move a single finger to express his agony. He understood only that something had happened, that it had been Ferris who caused it, and that Wilhelm was rounding on Ferris for it.

Emilia crouched down, shaking the shoulder of the dying Subaru.

"Wait... Subaru, this isn't right, this can't be. Please. Wait... no, no, this is wrong. I never wanted you to..."

Her voice was thick with tears. Who was she crying for, he wondered. She was probably crying for someone. She had always been that kind of hopelessly good person, one who did everything for someone else's sake.

He thought that if that someone were him, it might make him happy.

"Ah, hell, I can't watch this."

Ricardo turned his massive frame away.

And stepping forward from beside him was the Royal Guard knight with his purple hair faintly mussed—Julius, rainbow blade in hand, a trace of desolation lingering on his face.

"...It was my own failing that brought about Ferris's act. In time, I will surely answer for this."

Subaru couldn't bring himself to tell him not to worry over something so trivial.

Worry over it plenty, never forget it. If no one remembered how much he'd suffered like this, he'd never rest easy.

—The tip of the rainbow blade was set against the throat of the prone Subaru.

"No... no, Julius. Subaru's still..."

"His life-force has run out of control... This cannot go on any further."

Emilia said something more to Julius, but the knight shook his head and would not listen.

The sound of their exchange grew distant. Farther, and farther, and vanished somewhere.

'This wasn't supposed to happen... I—I was supposed to have received love, affection! The Blessing of the Gospel...!'

—Shut up. Come along with me to hell.

Far away, far away, he fell toward some unknown place, falling, falling.

Ah, was he going to die again? Falling to the bottom of the Abyss, dropping everything he carried, clutching his own failures close, dying wretchedly once more.

He looked back over the world.

So as never to forget the cause—what exactly had gone wrong.

"—Subaru, I'm sorry."

He heard that tear-choked voice, and with the voice of the girl he most wanted to hear as the very last thing, his consciousness sank away.

And then—ah—he understood. This time's failure, and the reason for it.

"I—"

—Subaru should never have made up with Emilia.
