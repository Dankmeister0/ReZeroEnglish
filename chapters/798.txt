Arc 10 Chapter 38: "Wait"

—The situation surrounding Kugelschreiber Castle had changed completely.

Hearing Tiga announce it, Subaru and the others caught their breath.
The castle had crossed through space—in other words, it had left the hidden, separate dimension and returned to the outside world for the first time in centuries.
Except that meant—

"Looks like this caught you by surprise too."

"—Who knows? Maybe making you think that is all part of our plan. You've got a bad habit of believing everything you see, my friend. Haven't I always told you that?"

"Subaru-sama, the wind says you're lying..."

"Shhh! Crusch-san, shhh! We're in the middle of some high-level mind games!"

"We're really not. I'm not falling for that much. ...Though I'll admit, when you tell me there's something I've forgotten, it does make my heart skip a beat."

Having gauged their reactions, Tiga glanced to Subaru's side—at Melty, who stood there still watching him.
The look Melty gave Tiga was charged with expectation and feeling. The fact that she had called him by name left no doubt that there was some connection between them.

"And it looks like you've got some history too... Black Dog, what's the story with you and that little spirit?"

"Don't remember a thing, not a scrap, not the tiniest biiit! Tough luck for that itty-bitty spirit I don't know, my bad, I'm sorry!"

"...Right. Okay. Don't worry."

Like Melty, 'Black Dog' apparently had some history—some significant connection to the squirrel-like spirit glaring at them from Tiga's shoulder. For all his antics, Subaru judged from two months of experience that he probably wasn't lying.
Frankly, the sheer amount of information being crammed into his head was dizzying. But now that Beralis, the actual cause of his dizziness, had fallen silent, he couldn't use that as an excuse to stop thinking. —Their fighting force, aside from the lively 'Black Dog' and the somewhat lively Crusch, consisted of Subaru, Ferris, and Melty, all half-dead. Even the first two weren't at their best, worn down and wounded as they were. Recklessness was out.
Still, they weren't entirely without a chance. To make that chance pay off—

"Now then. There are five of us, and even counting the little spirit on your shoulder, there are only two of you. We've got more than twice your numbers. An overwhelming advantage. If you're going to surrender, now's the time."

"...Am I hearing things, ya know? He's ignoring what a sorry state they're all in and talking to us like that just because there are more of them."

"You're hearing him right, Pooka. That's the kind of guy my friend is. Though even I'm surprised he's completely ignoring how worn out they are."

"You've got more people with serious injuries on your side. Another point in our favor."

"When you say that so confidently with a hole in your back, I almost start believing you."

Tiga shrugged as he spoke, looking as though he had absolutely no intention of believing him. Apparently, he had no talent whatsoever for acting. For now, learning that the squirrel-like spirit was named Pooka, and that their opponents were willing to talk, counted as gains.
Still, this extended round of small talk hardly qualified as negotiation. Sensing that a fight was inevitable, Crusch and 'Black Dog' gradually let their fighting spirit rise—

"—Hh! What're ya standin' around chattin' fo-or! Do it, dooo it! Get 'em, 'Executor'! That's what yer here fo-or!"

But the one who raised his voice, even more impatient with the situation than Subaru's group, was Reborn.
Propping himself up with his half-shear, Reborn had lost his sight to Crusch's sword strikes and could no longer use 'Silence,' whose power depended on his vision. Even so, following Tiga's presence, he shouted at him, demanding that he fulfill the duty he had rushed here to perform.
Wishing he hadn't said that, Subaru ran his tongue over his dry lips.

"...Honestly, him skipping the talking would be the worst."

Even with the restraints of 'Silence' and 'Ring Finger' gone, and Subaru's mouth and mind working as usual again, that wasn't going to overturn the decisive difference in strength.
The outright cheat of 'Cor Leonis' combined with 'Blue' could help them in combat, but there was no guarantee how long it would last.
So Tiga trying to overpower them here would be the most troublesome thing of all—

"Hurry up and get these—"

"—Tornel, you can hear me. 'Detour' at full power. We're getting out of here."

"Wha...?!"

But Tiga's next words defied both Subaru's fears and Reborn's demands.
After calling out an order to the third 'Beloved Child,' who was nowhere to be seen, Tiga spun on the spot and closed the distance to Reborn, whose bleeding face was turned toward him. Then he struck the motionless Reborn hard on the back of the neck with the pommel of his drawn curved sword.

"Gah."

"I told you, time's up. This is your fault for rushing ahead without waiting for orders in the first place."

The small figure crumpled with a brief groan. Tiga tossed the words out as he hoisted him easily onto his shoulder. Then, looking back at Subaru and the others as they stared in astonishment, he said,

"You heard me. We're taking our spoiled brats and withdrawing. That's all for today."

"Hey, hey, hey, isn't that just a little too convenient for you? You barge right in, drop a few riddles, and say goodbye? Leave your lives and your answers behind!"

"Wh-what a vulgar, barbaric way to talk, ya know...! I can't believe something like that is my contractor! Did you trick me, ya know!?"

"What do I know, what do I care, you idiot! Take that dead weight and burn to a crisp—burning!"

Shouting out the final word in their argument, 'Black Dog' thrust his palm forward and unleashed a raging blaze.
Green flames swept sideways like the breath of a 'Dragon,' cutting off Tiga's escape routes as the indignant spirit rode on his shoulder. Subaru almost blurted out, "You id—!" at the unthinkable recklessness of it, but a louder voice rose from Melty beside him.
It wasn't a scream, though.

"No! Fire won't work on Pooka!"

"Huh!?"

'Black Dog' responded in disbelief to her certain cry. Her words were proved true when the green flames reached Tiga and the others—and were devoured head-on.
The middle of the onrushing green blaze, precisely the part that would have struck them, was neatly bitten away and vanished. The one responsible was Pooka, who licked her lips.
The cute little spirit lightly wiped her mouth with a forepaw and said,

"That girl seems to know her stuff, ya know. —She knows I'm the Great Spirit of Fire."

"If you knew, if you understood, then say so! Is this your chance to show off your bad-girl side? Never miss a chance to promote yourself, do you!"

"You're the one who attacked out of nowhere, Black Dog! More importantly—"

Snapping back at 'Black Dog's' unfair complaint, Melty looked toward Tiga beyond the divided flames. As though that were his cue, Tiga, with Reborn in his arms, sprang lightly backward—and scooped the fallen Beralis off the floor with his free arm.

"You're alive, right? Spread 'Ring Finger.' Don't hold back. —It won't work on me anyway."

"...Oh, my? Does that mean... you trust me, and you love me? I love you too...!"

Just how tenacious was she? Beralis answered Tiga with love, as incorrigible as ever. Then came a sweet scent that excited the mind and robbed people of their sanity.
Beralis's 'Ring Finger' activated, once again filling the air with the substance that made emotions burst their banks.

"Ferris! I'm about to go nuts, so handle it for me, yahoo!"

"Nngh, you already have! Stop moving, stop thrashing! I can't treat you!"

Subaru activated the combination of taking on all his companions' mental abnormalities through 'Cor Leonis' and having Ferris treat them, somehow holding on. But even while they dealt with that, Tiga, carrying Beralis under one arm, was trying to leave the battlefield with both defeated 'Beloved Children.'
Crusch and 'Black Dog' each moved to stop him, but—

"I won't let you interfere, ya know!"

"—Hh!"

Pooka puffed out her cheeks and roared, spitting back the flames she had just devoured from 'Black Dog.'
The green fire rushed toward the two pursuing Tiga. Crusch sliced through it with an upward slash and stepped through the curtain of flames—and collided squarely with 'Black Dog.'
"Kyaah!?" "Whoa!?" Their startled cries overlapped as the unexpected collision sent them both tumbling. This wasn't another bout of infighting under the influence of 'Ring Finger.'

"—Why!? Where'd Tiga go!?"

Beyond the stumbling Crusch and 'Black Dog,' Subaru, too, had lost sight of Tiga.
It had happened just after the flames Pooka spat out briefly blocked their view of him and the others. Subaru doubted his own eyes at losing track of someone who should have been right there. But he wasn't the only one who couldn't see Tiga. Pressed against Subaru with his armless body, Ferris was also looking around in bewilderment, going, "Huh? Huh?"
Subaru could feel Ferris's healing power at work as closely as it was possible to feel it. Which meant this wasn't the effect of Beralis's 'Ring Finger'—

"—'Detour'! It doesn't just make people get lost!?"

'Detour' had split up their team inside the castle and made it difficult to reunite afterward. If that power, whose user had yet to show themselves, didn't merely scramble their sense of direction but also disrupted their perception, then it had made their eyes and awareness take a 'detour,' causing them to lose sight of Tiga.
At this rate, helpless to stop him, they would let Tiga escape as he ran just outside their awareness—

"Wait!"

That instant, Melty raised her slender voice and stood in his way, striking staff in hand.
Subaru caught the barest glimpse of her at the edge of his vision. Forcing his head toward her and fighting against 'Detour,' he saw Melty standing before the door out of the basement, facing the approaching Tiga.
With both arms occupied by the two 'Beloved Children,' Tiga's gaze sharpened.

"Out of the way! You'll get hurt!"

"Then take responsibility if you hurt me!"

"What's that supposed to mean!?"

Tiga's eyes widened in alarm at the answer from Melty blocking his path. The distance between them kept closing, and just when it seemed she would pay the price for her recklessness—

"Tch! Pooka!"
"You'd better hold on tight to those two, ya know!"

At his call, Pooka leapt off Tiga's shoulder and grew all at once, going from twenty centimeters to a gigantic five meters in the blink of an eye. Her rounded tail, enlarged in proportion, swung around, gently caught Tiga in the back, and launched him forward.

"—Tiga!"

Flying in an arc, Tiga sailed over Melty's head as she stared up with wide, round eyes. She reached out at once, but with his added height and momentum, he was beyond her grasp.

"See you."

"Tiga—!!"

Their eyes met for only an instant. Then, from his very first step after landing, Tiga shot away like an arrow, leaving Melty behind as she desperately raised her voice.
He didn't stop. 'Detour' wouldn't let her catch up to his receding back.

"Wait! I said wait, Tiga! Tiga!"

No matter how often she called, her voice wouldn't reach him, and neither would her outstretched hand.
The distance between them kept growing, until even his retreating footsteps faded away. Behind him, Pooka's gigantic form dissolved into particles of mana and vanished, following Tiga as he withdrew.

"Damn it, they got clean..."

Away. —That outcome, left unspoken at the end of his sentence, was one Subaru had to accept as the result of their encounter with the 'Beloved Children' in Kugelschreiber Castle.

* * *

—Tiga Rauleon and Pooka's intrusion had thoroughly muddied the outcome of the fight.

Honestly, there was nothing to say except that Tiga had gotten the better of them. Leaving aside how trustworthy any information might be, if they had secured the two people they had defeated, they could have gotten a little closer to the unknown secrets of the 'Beloved Children.' Even if that proved impossible, they would at least have whittled down a troublesome enemy's forces.
But with the pair rescued, all of that had gone up in smoke.

"That said, if the fight had kept going, we would've been the ones in trouble. I never dreamed Tiga was a Spiritual Arts User. He really had me fooled."

"—No, that's not right. Tiga isn't a Spiritual Arts User. As far as I know, he and Pooka are simply traveling together..."

"Without a contract, you mean? Maybe it's like Anastasia-san and Eridna... In that case, what exactly is the relationship between those two?"

"That's... I'm sorry. I don't know. All I know is that they aren't under contract."

Head lowered, Melty gripped her retracted striking staff and gave a vague answer. But Subaru didn't think it was vague because she was lying or trying to hide something. Most likely, Melty genuinely didn't know much about Tiga and Pooka's relationship.
Her own relationship with Tiga, though, should have been another matter.

"—I'll tell you everything later. I promise. But right now..."

"I know. Checking what's happening with Kugelschreiber Castle and meeting up with Beako and the others come first... Ferris! Your arms! Will they go back on properly!?"

"I'm concentrating, so stop talking to me! I've numbed the pain, so I can't keep my hands steady!"

"Your hands aren't steady? Those hands of yours have come off..."

Answering in a shrill voice, Ferris was in the middle of reattaching his arms with healing magic, having retrieved them from the wall where they had been pinned—both severed below the elbow.
Even Subaru hadn't expected things to go as far as cutting his arms off. If Ferris couldn't get them back, Subaru would have to spend the rest of his life taking responsibility. When he'd said something to that effect, though, Ferris had given him another look of mortal disgust.

"Besides, if anyone should spend the rest of their life making amends, I think it should be me. I'm the one who actually cut them off."

"I feel pretty responsible for making you do that too, you know. How are you holding up, Crusch-san?"

"I have no major wounds. I can still—"

Keep going, she had probably meant to say. But contrary to her wish to give that answer, Crusch's body couldn't keep up with her resolve. —She swayed, her knees buckling. Subaru reached out at once, hastily catching her before she fell.

"Whoa, careful! Are you okay?"

"Huh...? Subaru... sama..."

"It's probably a side effect of the 'Dragon's Eye.' You worked so hard for us, Crusch-san."

"But... there's still..."

"No, you can't push yourself. Rest for now. We'll work hard enough to make up the difference. Mostly Black Dog, though."

Watching Crusch stir weakly, Subaru gave that answer. 'Black Dog' responded with an indignant "Whaaat?" but one glare silenced him.
Then Subaru took the eyepatch from the pocket of Crusch's jacket and gently placed it over her left eye, whose lid was growing heavy.

"Let's talk again when you wake up. About all the hard work you've been doing, for instance."

"—Yes."

Nodding as her consciousness faded, Crusch closed the eye her patch didn't cover as well.
Over the past two months, whenever she had been forced to rely on the 'Dragon's Eye,' she had always seemed stern and tense as exhaustion carried her into sleep. Now, to Subaru, that severity seemed to have eased from her sleeping face, and it brought him a little relief.

"—Subaru-kun."

As Subaru adjusted his hold on the sleeping Crusch, Ferris stood before him.
His lost arms extended from both sleeves of his black suit, and he opened and closed his hands as though to show off their complete recovery. Seeing that they had properly reattached brought another wave of relief.

"Honestly, it was so gruesome I couldn't look straight at it... You can heal even that, huh?"

"Mm-hm. As long as I have the missing part, and the person's Od hasn't forgotten its original state yet, I think I can restore it pretty much perfectly."

"I feel like you just casually said something outrageous, but anyway, thank goodness. ...Is it okay if I carry Crusch-san on my back? I've got the least else to do."

"—Mm. Thanks. I hate you."

"A femboy who goes from sweet to sour... That's a whole new experience."

As expected, the plan that depended so heavily on Ferris seemed to have cost Subaru a considerable number of affection points. Given how low his starting score had probably been, it might have bottomed out in the negatives by now. He was afraid to ask.
Even so, without Ferris—and without Crusch—they couldn't have driven off the 'Beloved Children.' There were all sorts of hard feelings left over, but everyone had done their best and fought to earn this result.

"Even if Tiga did put a blot on it right at the end...!"

"U-um, I'm sorry. I'll apologize on Tiga's behalf..."

"Hah! Playing the good girl, apologizing on his behalf! As if that could ever fill this lonely, hollow feeling. It can't, it won't! So, what do we do now?"

"First, quit giving Melty-chan a hard time. After that, just like I said—we prioritize meeting up with Beako and the others. But we're going to move carefully."

Tiga had left with the injured 'Beloved Children,' but the whereabouts of the unseen third member were still unknown. There was every chance an ambush remained inside the castle. Subaru and the others knew far too little about this place to drop their guard on the assumption that they had driven off all their enemies.
All the more so now, with the castle having burst into the outside world—above the clouds over the Lifaus Highway, drawing everyone's attention.

"After all, this castle was supposedly made by Echidna. Knowing her, I wouldn't put it past her to announce some trial for the challengers who came inside: escape before the castle she sent flying crashes."

"What kind of opinion do you have of her? She's Beatrice-chan's mother, isn't she?"

"...Rotten to the core, and only just barely better than a Sin Archbishop?"

Strictly speaking, she was little different from a Sin Archbishop in sheer nastiness. But she was already dead, and there would be no further damage, so that was his assessment. Then again, there was Sphinx, who had run rampant in the Vollachian Empire. Depending on what sins remained hidden, his opinion could still plummet.
Subaru looked around at his companions, each wearing their own expression of distaste at that answer.

"Anyway, watch both the floors and the walls like a hawk. If we've left that other dimension, I doubt we can keep falling forever until we die, but..."

"—Ah, apparently you don't need to worry that much."

"————"

The unexpected voice made him whip around. He had had more than enough of new intruders by now, but that flash of alarm and anxiety was unnecessary.
The stairwell connecting the basement to the upper floor—a familiar face had appeared from it.

"—Rachins! You're all right!"

"I'd tell you not to go throwing my name around so casually, but... you lot are a goddamn wreck. You look worse than 'Librarian' said."

The one looking over Subaru and the others in the basement as he spoke was Rachins, who had split off from them before their encounter with the 'Beloved Children.'
Unlike Subaru's group, who had been fighting one fierce battle after another, Rachins showed no sign of having been caught up in any trouble. Apparently, he'd been lucky enough to avoid the enemy, but—

"What about Russell-san... 'Silver Fox'? You were together, right?"

"He's fine—or rather, sounds like we avoided getting spotted because of that bastard's judgment. We were about to head back to the entrance with the documents, and he said the entrance's value suddenly dropped or something..."

"Value... His 'Divine Protection of Evaluation'? He can use it like that too...?"

In other words, the value of the castle's entrance had plummeted—Russell, the holder of that Divine Protection, had probably sensed that something useful to him had become something harmful. If that had alerted him to the change, then yes, he might well have been able to avoid the 'Beloved Children.'
Honestly, Subaru would have liked a warning, but they had been operating separately, so it couldn't be helped. He would just be glad nothing irreversible had happened somewhere he and the others weren't around to help.

"I'd love to catch up too, but Long Tongue-kun, isn't there something serious happening outside?"

"Oh, right, yeah. Apparently the castle's burst out of its separate dimension. It might be a 'Witch's' trap to wipe out all the intruders in one go. We need to—"

"That's not it. 'Librarian's' the one flying it, and 'Silver Fox' gave the order."

"—Beako and Russell-san?"

His alarm brought up short, Subaru's eyes went round.
The report that Beatrice and Russell were doing something together was unexpected enough. All the more so when it was something as spectacular and utterly unconcealed as making a castle fly.
From Subaru's experience working as an associate of the 'Six Tongues' these past two months, it should have been obvious that Russell hated drawing that kind of attention more than anything.

"They're both safe, then? What were they thinking, making the castle fly? What were you doing? Didn't you stop them?"

"Quit firing off questions at me, you're so damn loud! If you're that curious, stop standing around here talking and ask them yourself."

"Fair point. Can we use 'Door Crossing'?"

"Yeah. That's how I got here. Seriously, that magic makes my skin crawl."

Offering an opinion that would have made Beatrice puff out her cheeks and stamp her feet if she'd heard it, Rachins started walking to lead the others. Then, after the first few steps, he stopped. "Oh, right, right," he said, turning only his head back.

"I'll answer just one of those questions... Apparently, we're turning this castle into a base."

"Huh?"

"That's the answer to what they were thinking, making the castle fly. We're making it our base. —If we're serious about taking the kingdom back, we can't keep running and hiding from place to place forever."

And so he explained why Russell, who hated drawing attention, had sent the castle flying anyway.
