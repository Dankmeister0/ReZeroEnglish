Arc 6 Chapter 65: "The Second, the Fifth, and Then—"

"—————"

Majestic and imposing, the red-haired great man appeared on the scene — Reid Astrea.
At the unexpected arrival of this man, and at those sharp blue eyes that showed not the slightest consideration for their surprise, at the sheer fact of his presence — everyone, even 'Gluttony,' was struck speechless.

"Oi, oi, what're you all standin' there gawkin' for? That surprised I'm here, huh? Well, o'course you would be, right?"

And then, gazing at the frozen Subaru and the others, Reid tapped a finger against the eyepatch over his closed left eye, tapping the sole of his sandal against the floor with a pat-pat-pat.
It was a gesture with a certain meaning, one indicating the tower as a whole.

"Both outside and in, you're makin' such a racket a man can't even get a decent nap in. Already bored enough without any booze, and now this — there ain't no way I'm puttin' up with it."

"...That's just your problem, isn't it. Can't you see we're in the middle of something here. Don't go making it more complicated."

"Hah! The little fry's sayin' somethin'? Sorry, but your voice's too damn small to hear. Well, even if I could hear it, I'd still say I couldn't."

"You're one hell of a bastard..."

And it wasn't just his personality that was bad — the timing was bad too.
Too feeble to be called an accusation, too empty to be called a protest. Subaru, cowed by the man's rough, merciless words, realized he felt fear toward Reid, and clenched his fists tight.

His soul was tasting defeat at the mere presence of Reid.
That wasn't only because of the awful treatment Reid had put him through back when he was still consumed by suspicion. Even in the previous loop, Subaru had faced this man's appalling, insufferable arrogance head-on.
And back then, Subaru's heart hadn't been this afraid of Reid — not to this degree.

So why, why only in this moment, did it feel so strangely, so strangely severe?
That was—

"...Because my soul's plainly recognized you as an enemy. That's why, isn't it."

"That's the spirit, little fry. I ain't promotin' you outta little-fry status, but I'll give you credit for not mistakin' me for some convenient reinforcement."

"Not like I could honestly just accept being told to be happy you're reinforcements."

"Hah! Listen to you."

Baring his fangs savagely, grinning like a shark, Reid made Subaru hide the cold sensation lodged in his chest.
Reid's declaration just now was, in a sense, a hopeless pronouncement. But Subaru had never once thought of Reid as an ally to begin with.
That had merely been confirmed. He hadn't mistaken anything.
And just as that exchange between Subaru and Reid reached a lull—

"—Say, Reid Astrea."

That was 'Gluttony,' the first among them to snap back to his senses, calling out Reid's name.
Standing in the very center of the frozen corridor — right between Subaru's group and Reid — the mad Archbishop of Sin turned his gaze, glinting with malicious feeling, on Reid, who sniffed with displeasure as he looked the boy's way.

"Oi, what is it, runt. ...A filthy little runt, at that. What is it, you dirty little runt."

"You're the original Sword Saint, aren't you? So why is it you're here again, I wonder? By our memory, you're the proctor of this floor — you shouldn't be able to come down from the floor above, should you?"

Ignoring the humiliating name, Bateleuse threw his question at the provoking Reid.
It was infuriating that the "memory" he spoke of was clearly Subaru's memory, but the substance of the question itself was one that Subaru's group shared as well.

—The Second Floor's proctor, Reid Astrea, reaching the Fourth Floor.

This was something Subaru himself had witnessed in the previous loop, one of the five obstacles he'd considered had to be cleared.
The mechanism behind that phenomenon — Reid freely wandering the tower — remained entirely unknown. However, that phenomenon never occurred except at this juncture. Though if it turned out he'd simply been *able* to wander before but just hadn't bothered, that would be a whole other, more absurd story.

"Some strange mechanism at work, or maybe the tower's own rules changed. Either way, you being like this is outside our calculations, and it means we'll have to rethink the course quite a bit. Appetizer first, then the main dish, then dessert — that's the basic order, right?"

"Quit spoutin' incomprehensible drivel, you filthy little runt bastard."

"—————"

"I can't come down, huh? Dig your damn eyeballs outta your skull and take a good look, you. You can see for yourself what a stupid thing you just said, can't you, you, oi."

As he spoke, Reid leaned forward, displeased, toward 'Gluttony.' Baring white teeth, glaring at his opponent with his one eye — the very picture of a common street thug.
Unfortunately, though, the pressure radiating off the man himself was on a level incomparable to some punk loitering outside a convenience store; the mere aftershock of it was enough to instill the terror of one's life being threatened.
To put it in terms of an analogy, what stood there was a beast that was tiger and bear and lion and dragon all fused into one.
Draped in the aura of every kind of violence imaginable, Reid bared his fangs.

"Don't screw with me, you. I do what I wanna do, however the hell I wanna do it. Ain't takin' orders from nobody, you. Don't screw around. Anyway, what the hell are you, anyway. Whose permission'd you get to go makin' a ruckus in a place like this. Oi, you, hey, you."

"Ahahaha, amazing, just amazing, this is priceless, no point even talking."

Turning the ice holy sword in his hand into shards of ice, 'Gluttony' brushed back his long bangs.
Even 'Gluttony,' it seemed, found an opponent this impossible to converse with a difficult one to handle. There had been signs of struggle even against the no-nonsense Emilia, but Reid was several degrees less reasonable to talk to than she was.
And yet, against such a poorly-matched foe, 'Gluttony' continued with a "but," and—

"As prey, though, he's exquisite. It's got our appetite as gourmands positively throbbing! Sink your teeth in! Gnaw it off! Lick it clean! Savor it to the very end! Gorge! Gluttonize!"

Howling, raging, 'Gluttony' dropped to all fours on the frozen floor and glared at Reid.
Long tongue lolling between white canine teeth, drool dripped to the floor as the deranged 'appetite' — which devoured others' memories in a way no ordinary person could comprehend — issued its commands.
And then—

"—Witch Cult Archbishop of Gluttony, Lye Batenkaitos."

Whether that declaration was pride or arrogance, either way, 'Gluttony' — no, Lye Batenkaitos — announced his name, and in the next instant kicked off the icy floor and shot forward at arrow-like speed.
It was a sprint so feral it could be mistaken for the hunting charge of some savage four-legged beast.

"Ahh, what a pain."

Facing head-on the charging Bateleuse, Reid stuck a finger in his ear and grumbled with reluctance,

"Well then, I'll be the swordsman, Hauroy Rariol."

"Who the hell is that!?"

"Guy who lived next door to me back home."

At the sheer brazenness of the fake name, Subaru couldn't help but shout, and Reid answered as breezily as could be. Then, pulling the finger from his ear, looking down at Bateleuse right before him—

"—Itadakimasu!!"

"If it came from some fine lookin' woman, maybe, but comin' from a filthy little runt like you, it don't do a damn thing for me."

"—guh"

In that instant, Bateleuse's body, lunging with jaws wide open, snapped violently sideways.
That was because Reid's carelessly thrown right-foot kick had caught Bateleuse's torso dead-on from the side, sending him crashing spectacularly into the corridor wall.

"Guh, gah...!"

"Don't go makin' noises like a chicken gettin' its neck wrung, you. I'll tell you now, a chicken's damn good once you wring it and cook it, but I ain't got no plans to eat you. Gluttony or whatever the hell you are."

"Gi, gah!"

"You jumped a grown man. Better be ready to get punished for it, you!"

As he spoke, Reid pinned Bateleuse's body against the wall with his kicking foot, then began running down the icy corridor at a speed unthinkable for someone moving on one leg.
Naturally, being dragged along the wall in contact the whole time — a wall studded here and there with icy protrusions — Bateleuse had no way to resist. The damage was immense, devastating.

"Gi, gaaaaaaaaaah—!"

"Oi oi, don't go screamin' over somethin' this minor. At this rate we ain't got nothin' to talk about. This is nothin', wouldn't even pass for kids' play back in my day. Kids these days ain't just filthy, they've gone soft too, oi, oi, oi, you!"

Saying so with boredom, Reid stopped and spun sharply in place.
The foot pinning Bateleuse to the wall came free, and the Archbishop's body began to slide down. Before it could, Reid's pivot foot swapped, and a left back-roundhouse kick came flying, its direct hit driving spectacularly into the side of Bateleuse's face, sending the small frame flying as easily as a rubber ball.

"—————"

At an extraordinary speed, unable even to break his fall, Bateleuse bounced along the floor. Spinning at high speed, scattering blood, the Archbishop of Sin passed between the frozen-still Emilia and Julius, and finally went tumbling, bouncing past Subaru and Beatrice, deep into the corridor.
Sprawled face-down in a spread-eagle heap, there wasn't a trace left of the vigor he'd shown just moments before.
It was enough to make one wonder if he'd died.

"That guy — wasn't he the powerful enemy Emilia-chan and Julius were struggling against together, just a second ago?"

"...That's not wrong, I suppose. Only, it's just that this one's even more outside the norm, I suppose. So depending on how things go, it could be said the situation's gotten worse, I suppose."

"The matchup just swapped out, huh."

"For an even worse opponent, I suppose."

Squeezing tight, Beatrice — hand joined with Subaru's — kept her wary eyes not on the fallen, motionless Bateleuse behind them, but on Reid ahead. The same was true of Emilia and Julius.
The battle had already shifted from one against 'Gluttony' to one against a new, formidable power.
Even amid all this—

"Archbishop of Sin, thanks so much for beating him up... okay, is that not the way to make up with someone?"

Emilia was, first of all, the one to address Reid in a friendly manner. But at her characteristically peaceable offer, Reid shook his head, shrugged his shoulders, and kicked the floor.
Batting away the offer with a three-part display of body language, Reid scratched roughly at his head.

"Hah, don't go sayin' somethin' that lets a man's guard down, you. ...Or rather, what the hell, you. You're too damn fine lookin', ain't ya, what's goin' on here, you gotta be kiddin' me! Smokin' hot, ain't you! What're you doin' in a place like this, you. What the hell are you doin' in a situation like this. Quit playin' around in this dusty, nonsensical place and come pour me a drink tonight, you."

"Um, this is actually the second time, though..."

"—Unfortunately, she won't be joining you for a nightcap. Because—"

"Oh?"

"—for you, an illusion, no peaceful night shall ever come."

Cutting off the crude remark, a man stepped forward, knight's sword raised — Julius, shielding Emilia, faced off against Reid, his killing intent honed razor-sharp, his gaze fierce.
Reid's expression shifted slightly, meeting those yellow eyes head-on.

"...What's this, you. Got yourself a bit of a better face on now, huh. Somethin' good happen to you, you. A woman. It's a woman, ain't it, you."

"I won't deny there have been events that shaped my resolve in various ways, but let me tell you, it wasn't only affairs of the heart. Sometimes a woman's embrace heals a wounded heart; sometimes a friend's merciless scolding serves the same purpose."

"Still as roundabout a bastard as ever. What the hell are you tryin' to say?"

"Simply that the reason I now hold this sword is thanks to a friend of mine—!"

The instant he answered, Julius surged forward with a sharp lunge, snapping his knight's sword upward, tracing an arc so beautiful it beggared belief, sending a strike flashing toward Reid's throat.

"—————"

There was no need for the tactless observation that the opponent hadn't yet declared hostile intent.
Standing in this place, Reid's intentions were plainer than fire seen at night. A tingling, unrestrained killing intent lightly crackled even against the onlookers' skin.
There was no way the bearer of killing intent this intense would leave his blade sheathed.

—Reid meant to fight. Just as in the previous loop, heedless of the circumstances.

And so, Julius struck first without hesitation.
That choice was the optimal one. Aiming to break the composure of the utterly at-ease Reid — that could be called precisely the right strike for tackling this original 'Sword Saint.'
The problem was—

"—'At ease,' huh. You even know what that means?"

"—————"

"See, it means whatever's thrown at me, I can handle it so easy I got room to spare, so no matter what little trick you pull it don't matter squat. It means you're an idiot."

—That was because Reid caught Julius's first strike, pinching it between the two chopsticks he held.

"Gh...!"

"Well, not bad, though? If it weren't me you were up against, I might've actually taken that hit. —Alright then, here I come."

"Hah!"

Toward Julius, groaning as his thrust was blocked, Reid grinned like a shark.
And then, gripping one chopstick in each hand, Reid swept the blade's tip aside while stepping in at the same time, unleashing his chopstick strike. — The impact struck the knight's sword head-on, and a sharp, ringing clang resounded.

"—————"

Thin, a wooden rod not even worth comparing to a sword in blade-length — and yet, gripped in the hand of a master like Reid, it became a weapon larger than life, unleashing destruction.
The instant the fierce, ringing clash sounded, the resulting shockwave sent Julius's hair and clothes fluttering, and shattered the frozen portions of the entire corridor in one stroke.

—Truly an anomaly beyond category, beyond common sense, a lump of abnormality that could only be called a bug in the world.

Even having witnessed his prowess more than once, seeing it again in the flesh left Subaru speechless.
The very existence of such a monster in the world, and the demonic, rotten-to-the-core spitefulness of whatever designer had built "surpassing this monster" into the tower's clearance conditions, was enough to make him want to retch.
But—

"—Heh, I'm honestly a little impressed."

How much of Reid's full power had gone into that chopstick strike, no one could say.
But it seemed even Reid hadn't expected that single strike to be fully weathered, and he praised the fighting spirit of Julius, who had absorbed the chopstick strike with his whole body.
At that, Julius, a drop of blood spilling from the corner of his mouth, narrowed his eyes.

"After all, if I can't defeat you, my calculations here fall apart."

"You're plannin' on winnin'? Big talk."

"I would think so. But you'll be keeping me company regardless!"

In that instant, Julius's swordstroke flashed, and Reid roughly parried it aside with his two chopsticks.
The sword's momentum was deflected, and Julius's stance faltered — no, it didn't. As if that very parry had already been factored in, Julius's knight's sword pivoted, connecting to the next strike without a moment's lag. And after that, it kept on.

A fluid, flawless, refined swordplay began, like a stream of water without a single wasted motion.

"—————"

Witnessing that swordplay firsthand, countless attacks and parries layered one atop another, Subaru held his breath.
Limited though it was — limited strictly to what Subaru's eyes alone could follow — it was proof of the tenacity and training of Julius Juukulius, reaching toward the summit of swordsmanship known as the 'Sword Saint.'

—If Reid's swordforce was flame, then Julius's swordforce was like flowing water.

By simple compatibility, water should extinguish fire, but if the flame's force is strong enough, then true to the proverb of pouring water on a heated stone, the water simply evaporates, rendered meaningless.
Most swordsmen of flowing water, no doubt, would have been evaporated by a swordforce like Reid's flame.
But at least in this moment, Julius, unafraid of drying up and vanishing, drove unwavering attacks into Reid.
And then—

"Don't think your opponent's just Julius!"

"Hah! Ain't forgot, smokin' hot! Your face is too damn good to forget, you!"

"Thanks for the compliment! But there's really only one person who actually remembers me!"

Into that swordplay between Reid and Julius, Emilia joined, arms laden with a mountain of ice armaments.
With this, Reid pointed one chopstick at Julius, the other at Emilia. Whether or not that counted as a countermeasure — Reid's swordsmanship utterly demolished such mundane notions of value.

Into the swordplay of flowing water and flame, ice broke in, and the battlefield's colors shifted vividly once more.
With one cast member of the swordplay swapped — Bateleuse's role now taken by Reid as the enemy — Emilia and Julius's awkward coordination continued — no, that changed too.

Whether it was because the enemy had grown stronger, or because in that brief span the two had drawn their fighting styles closer together and adjusted, their once-mismatched rhythm somehow fell into sync, their coordination becoming true coordination.

"Julius is matching himself to Emilia, I suppose."

"You can tell?"

"There's a compatibility of temperament involved too, I suppose. Emilia's become bolder in her decisiveness, and that's let Julius move more like himself, I suppose. Probably, Emilia giving up on trying to match him was the correct answer, I suppose."

"That's one hell of a knowing comment."

Still, since it was working out, that judgment must have been the right one.
In the end, it seemed Emilia was better suited to pushing forward at her own pace without holding back at all, while Julius excelled at plainly matching others' movements while adorning himself in the process.

"Hahhaa! Good, good, you two! Even I'm startin' to have fun here!"

"Uya! Seiya! Torya! Uryauryauryaaa!"

Batting aside, with delight, the two's meshing coordination, Reid laughed uproariously. Into that, Emilia kept driving in blows of startlingly high lethality, launched from calls that sounded far too carefree to have produced them — yet none of it landed as a decisive blow.

Water and flame and ice, a beautifully intertwined, fantastical swordplay.
Ringing out was the steel of the knight's sword, a wooden stick made equal to steel by tracing perfect swordstrokes, and ice weapons that shattered and reformed with every blow — each clashing, breaking, locking hilts, in ringing, resonant clangs.
So beautiful it could almost be mistaken for a true dance—

"—Shaaaaaaaa!!"

And so, the presence that broke in as discordant noise burned itself into both eye and ear as a foreign intrusion.

"You bastard...!"

Into the space where the three were locked in combat, the one who rudely barged in was Lye Batenkaitos.
The boy who should have taken Reid's kick and been left rolling half-dead, now stood up and, as if suffering no damage whatsoever, threw himself back into the battle.

Bateleuse wielded the daggers bound to both wrists, and mixing in combat techniques that made deft use of his short limbs, hurled one lethal attack after another at all three.
Emilia, Julius, and Reid each blocked them off with irritation,

"You really don't know when to quit, do you, Archbishop of Sin!"
"Ahahaa! Leaving us out and having fun all by yourself, don't be so mean about it, big brother! Always hogging it all to yourself? So unfair, seriously!"
"Reid! Can't you see this isn't the time for us to be fighting each other! Would you help us — or at least stay still!"
"You're an unreasonable woman, smokin' hot. I'm havin' myself a pretty decent time right now, y'know? Even if the stars came fallin' outta the sky, ain't nothin' gonna change my mind!"

"—————"

All four, exchanging ferocious blows, hurled their wills at one another all the while.
It was a spectacle of battle inseparable from the stench of blood, one that would never easily arrive at any clear, compromising resolution.

Who held the advantage, who was at a disadvantage, who had the upper hand, who was losing — it was hard to judge from the sidelines.
All that could be done was to at least pray for an ally's victory, but—

"—Ngh"

"Subaru!?"

And then, Subaru — who had no choice but to watch the battle unfold in frustrated helplessness — pressed a hand to his chest and dropped to his knees, and Beatrice cried out in shock.
Touching the shoulder of Subaru, who hunched over, breathing painfully, Beatrice peered into his face.

"Subaru, Subaru! What's wrong. What happened, I suppose!"

"...No, this — what is this?"

"Subaru?"

At Beatrice's desperate calling, Subaru clutched at his own chest, blinking again and again.
There was no intent whatsoever to deceive or dodge the question. He simply didn't know himself. A strange abnormality, a sense of wrongness — an unbelievable heat was rising deep in his chest.

His heart was pounding as if it might burst, and it felt as though every single drop of blood coursing through his body were crying out about something — a terrifying, unprocessable sensation ringing a blaring red alarm bell in his brain.

"—————"

He didn't know. What was happening to his body right now.
This was a phenomenon that had never occurred in any of his loops until now. Some chronic condition, or perhaps interference from something magic-like?
Mobilizing even knowledge he didn't actually possess, he searched for the worst possibilities, and shook his head.

No. This, probably, wasn't something bad. The alarm bell was conveying this very anomaly.

"Haa..."

He exhaled deeply.
Up until a moment ago, what had been burning in Subaru's mind wasn't only worry for Emilia and Julius, locked in combat before his eyes against Reid and Bateleuse.

Given the situation, he was powerless, able only to stand and watch.
What burned in Subaru's brain in that state was the likelihood that, even as this played out, the five obstacles — the remaining two of them — were progressing.

—The five obstacles.
One, the great horde of Witchbeasts surrounding the tower. One, the Archbishop of Gluttony assaulting the tower. One, Reid Astrea, now freely walking the tower's interior. — That made three, as things stood.
The remaining two: a giant scorpion prowling the tower's interior as if it owned the place, and a black shadow surging forward with a terrifying momentum threatening to swallow the tower whole.

Unless these two were also dealt with, there was no way to fully handle the problems bearing down on the tower.
Right in front of him, behind the scenes of Emilia's and Julius's struggle, in the very moment he agonized over how to deal with the still-looming possibility of the tower's collapse, this chest of his throbbed with heat and forced him to his knees.

Thump, thump — his heart pounded, fiercely.
Layering his consciousness over that heartbeat, Subaru breathed slowly and closed his eyes. He felt, instinctively, that he should. Following that instinct, he shut his eyes.

"—————"

Seeing Subaru like that, Beatrice stopped calling out to him.
Likely, she hadn't been certain either. But she did it anyway. To have a companion so understanding — Subaru was a fortunate man.
And then, behind Subaru's closed eyelids, a strange sensation welled up.

—It was a faint, gentle light, floating up in a hazy darkness.

"—?"

A faint, warm, hazy light.
There was one right beside Subaru, and two more a little ways off, straight ahead. And, strangely, Subaru could tell there was light in a direction behind him too — even though he hadn't turned to look.
Behind, in that direction, four lights were gathered together all at once. Far off from there, separated by a great distance, was one more. And then, and then, and then—

—he realized one more was closing in right above his head.

"—Beatrice!"
"Hyan!"

For some reason, Subaru trusted that sensation without hesitation, and threw himself, practically leaping onto Beatrice, flinging them both away from that spot.
Pulling the girl's light body into his arms, Subaru rolled without hesitation across the stone floor — and in that instant, he felt searing heat graze the thigh of his right leg, and let out a cry of pain.

"Ga, guohhhh!"

He realized instantly that the searing heat came from a laceration on his leg. Probably, deliberately averting his eyes from the deeply gouged wound on his leg, Subaru turned around, still holding Beatrice.
Then, forcing open his vision, blurred with pain and tears, he saw.

"—————"

"Knew you'd show up, you damn scorpion...!"

Before Subaru, spitting the words out bitterly, had appeared — this too would mark a second encounter — the giant scorpion. Black carapace, eyes like glowing red points, crawling along the wall on its many legs, glaring down imperiously at Subaru's group.

"—Ah"

Before that ghastly, massive frame, Beatrice's eyes went wide. What her gaze fixed on was the scorpion's pincer, the one that had gouged deep into Subaru's leg. The scorpion held, at the tip of that pincer, a hideously torn chunk of Subaru's flesh, dripping copious blood onto the corridor.

—The dreaded intrusion of the giant scorpion, finally here.

The five obstacles that had to be overcome were converging here, all at once.

"—————"

Bad. Bad, bad. Bad, bad, bad. Bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad bad.

Understanding how bad the situation was, Subaru tried to find a way out through thoughts burning red-hot with pain.
But how the situation could possibly be changed — that, Subaru simply couldn't see at all.

Bateleuse was here, Reid was here, and now even the giant scorpion had appeared.
Even though Meili and Shaula were, thankfully, holding back the great horde of Witchbeasts, this left no hands free for anything else. This wasn't going to work.
This method — it wasn't going to work. There had to be some other way, not this—

"—Subaru!" "Subaru!" "Subaru!!"

Through Subaru's gritted teeth, he heard three voices calling out to him.
Beatrice's anguished voice, Julius's urgent voice, Emilia's pleading voice — each calling Subaru's name, he heard, and then.

Bateleuse, Reid, the giant scorpion — each of them moved.
As if to block the path, the way forward, of Natsuki Subaru and the others — but before that could happen—

"—————"

—With tremendous force and impact, the entire tower shook violently, and a deafening roar echoed through it.

"—————"

That, in turn, sent Subaru's body, collapsed on the floor, bouncing; blew away Emilia and the others locked in combat; crushed even the giant scorpion's carapace, and the whole world crumpled and warped.
The small body right beside him clung tight, as if trying to protect Subaru. Holding that soft body in return, Subaru opened his eyes wide amid the shock.


"—I love you."


—There, a darkness holding nothing but blind love sought to swallow Subaru whole.

—In an instant, with a shock and sensation as if the whole world — white and black, light and shadow, man and woman, love and hatred — had turned itself inside out, Natsuki Subaru drifted, transmigrating.

"—Subaru."

"—!"

"Uhyan!"

Called to, and as if reeling in that voice, Subaru pulled its owner into a tight embrace.
At once, the body in his arms, thrashing and flailing, panicked, looking up at Subaru's face from within his chest.
It was—

"Bea...trice..."

"Y-yes, I suppose! You startled me all of a sudden, I suppose. Not that Betty's saying she hates it, I suppose. It's only, having just come back from the book, Betty was worried... but Betty's relieved you called her name first, I suppose."

"—————"

So Beatrice murmured, quietly, in Subaru's arms. Listening to her voice, Subaru glanced around himself, looking this way and that.
What had happened? Just a moment ago, he'd been collapsed in the corridor, his leg wounded, and then swallowed into that black, black darkness—

"...The library?"

"For now, whether you're half-asleep or not — if you could just make that clear, that alone would give us something to work with, Natsuki-kun."

Surrounded by countless bookshelves, Subaru looked around, dazed, and that voice addressed him.
Looking over, it was Echidna, smoothing back her pale lavender hair with a wry smile. Behind her, leaning against a bookshelf, was Meili too, propping her chin on her hand and saying, "Finally woke up, huh."

"—————"

"W-wah! Subaru! Subaru, what's wrong, I suppose! Is your body really acting strange after all, I suppose? What did you see inside the book — can you tell us, I suppose?"

"Ah, no, yeah. That too, I need to do... but—"

Tightly, Subaru embraced Beatrice's slender body, savoring her warmth.
And then, he acknowledged the reality he couldn't deny.


—He had come back. To this very moment.

He had failed to overcome the five obstacles, and Subaru had been sent tumbling back to this moment.
