Arc 3 Chapter 19: "The Silver-Haired Witch's Wish"

Her name was called, and Emilia answered with an expression thick with tension.

Then, the moment Subaru saw her right hand and right foot come forward at the same time as she began walking toward the center of the hall — he thought, *This is a job for me to sort out somehow.*

The stiffness born of nerves was visible from behind even from a few steps away, a rigidity so pronounced that in ordinary circumstances it would have been the kind of scene that prompted a cry of "Emilia-tan, total puppy!" in praise of her adorableness — but as things stood now, there was no way to see it working in her favor.
Just before she managed to reach the center, she seemed to notice the mismatch in her gait, and her limbs settled back into an ordinary human configuration as she stepped forward — advancing to the middle of the hall, into the gaze of the Council of Wise Men.

*—Emilia-tan, are you going to be okay?*

"There's no need to worry, Subaru."

"Don't read my mind. Am I that easy to see through?"

Reinhard spoke up, glancing sideways at Subaru, who couldn't fully hide his inner unease, his hands hovering restlessly.
In a hushed voice he addressed Subaru's anxiety, gesturing with his chin toward Emilia's back as she stepped forward.

"It seems Subaru doesn't know what kind of regard Emilia-sama commands at the royal castle. At the very least, she isn't held in the low esteem you seem to be worried about."

"Even so..."

Her right hand and right foot had come out at the same time. It was practically a textbook case of someone paralyzed with nerves. If this turned into her fumbling her lines, biting her tongue for good measure, blushing and collapsing into a crouch — that chain of events would be unbearable to watch.
If it came to that, Subaru was prepared to make a spectacle of himself in front of this gathering of the nation's dignitaries, drawing every ounce of contempt and scorn onto himself alone.

—That his thoughts did not run toward *smoothly covering for her and salvaging Emilia's honor* was simply the Natsuki Subaru way.

But such wildly misdirected resolve on Subaru's part turned out to be needless worry, undone by an interpretation running in an entirely unexpected direction.

"Did you see that — the way her hand and foot came out together just now..."
"Could it be some kind of harbinger, a special incantation passed down among the Elves?"
"A bewitching countenance. I find I cannot look away, somehow..."

Awed murmurs reached him from the ranks of the Royal Guard knights standing nearby, and Subaru sagged at the content of it. Persecution complex, or rather delusions of grandeur — it hardly got more excessive than this.
Anyone who actually knew Emilia wouldn't need to think twice to realize her gestures were simply the product of plain nervousness.
As for the third one, his impression wasn't all that different from Subaru's own, on the whole.
Still, that even a single stiff, awkward movement could be interpreted this way — if the man wasn't mocking her but genuinely spreading this in earnest — Subaru could tell this was by no means a friendly reception.

When Emilia reached the center, the murmuring naturally died down.
What remained, besides Emilia standing forward, was the sound of a single tall figure's footsteps ringing sharply as he crossed to stand beside her — Roswaal. When that long-limbed figure with the indigo hair took his place at Emilia's side, the stage was set.
Looking over the two standing together, Marcos, presiding over the proceedings, drew his chin down with a heavy expression. Then he turned his gaze toward Subaru's group — glaring in particular at the one who had just been spouting half-witted rumors — until the man straightened his spine, and said,

"Now then, Emilia-sama. And Sir Roswaal L. Mathers. If you please."

"Yeah, sure, sure. Nyaa, though, after such a fine parade of knightly escorts, don't you think I look terribly out of place here, standing beside her-r-re?"

Roswaal answered in his characteristically flippant tone, turning to Emilia beside him with a "Right?" Emilia offered no reaction whatsoever.
Given how tense she had been until just now, expecting her usual reaction was too much to ask. Roswaal's inability to read the room was enough to irritate even Subaru — but any negative feeling about it was instantly cast aside.
Because—

"It is an honor to meet you all, esteemed members of the Council of Wise Men. My name is Emilia. I have no family name. Please simply call me Emilia."

A clear, ringing voice like a silver bell shook every eardrum in the hall, carving that name into every heart present. There was no tremor in her voice, no wavering in the eyes that looked straight ahead.
Wherever the tension of a moment ago had gone, the figure of Emilia — having spoken her own name before the Council of Wise Men — lost nothing in comparison to the candidates who had come before her.

No — in the way she delivered her address, Emilia possessed a charm none of the other candidates could ever hope to match: an otherworldly, almost bewitching allure. Whether that stemmed from the fact that she alone was not purely human, he couldn't say.
But at the very least, Subaru himself was thoroughly captivated by that bewitching quality of hers. All his prior inward resolve, all the discourtesies directed at her from the surrounding crowd — every bit of it vanished cleanly from his mind, so utterly was he entranced by the sight of her standing there.

"And Emilia-sama's sponsor, unworthy though I am, is myself — Roswaal L. Mathers, Margrave. We are grateful to the Council of Wise Men for granting us your time."

"Hmm. So the sponsor is not a Royal Guard knight, but you, the court mage. Might we hear the circumstances behind that?"

Stroking his beard, Miklotov laid out the flow of the conversation in an unhurried manner — and then his eyes narrowed, taking on the sharpness of a blade. With that gaze he quietly pierced through Emilia.

"Candidate Emilia-sama. Including her background, if you please."

"Understood."

Bowing at the waist, Roswaal extended a hand to indicate Emilia, and upon receiving her nod of assent, began recounting his meeting with her in a resonant, ringing voice.
It was content Subaru himself had never heard, and he found himself unconsciously stiffening, straining his ears not to miss a single word.

"First, though I imagine most of you are already aware, allow me to begin with an explanation of Emilia-sama's origins, shall we-e? Beautiful silver hair, translucent white skin, violet-indigo eyes that capture the heart of any who behold them, and a voice like a silver bell that, once heard, cannot be forgotten even in dreams. As you may already know, these bewitching features are proof that Emilia-sama carries Elf blood."

At Roswaal's words, it was clear that several people throughout the hall drew in a breath.
Elf — a fact that, given how Emilia had been treated, must have already been widely known, and in the matter of the Royal Selection specifically, a difference from others so pronounced it would be impossible to conceal.
Earlier, in Anastasia's case, Subaru had used the mental comparison of a Japanese person becoming President of the United States — but just how much deeper, compared to that, was the chasm lying between the Elf race and the human race?

"A girl of Elf blood..."
"A countenance beyond human comprehension, and an otherworldly air about her — there can be no mistake."
"An Elf on the throne is unthinkable. That alone is beyond comparison with Anastasia-sama's case. And that's setting aside the fact that she isn't merely an Elf, either."

The elders of the Council of Wise Men exchanged glances, murmuring critical remarks under their breath. Especially firm in his attitude was the bald elder bearing a large scar — his stance was unyielding.

"Half of her blood is human — meaning, she is a Half-Elf, is that not so?"

He spat this out, a vein bulging visibly on his forehead, and waved his hand roughly. It was a gesture of casually pushing Emilia away, and indeed, he went on,

"It turns my stomach just to look at her. A silver-haired half-witch — why does no one realize it's practically sacrilege to even admit such a thing into the throne room?"

"Bordeaux-dono, you speak out of turn."

"Is it you who fails to understand, Miklotov-dono? Does that silver-haired half-witch not bear the very likeness handed down in legend of that 'Witch of Envy'!"

Raising his voice even at Miklotov's admonishment, the elder called Bordeaux rose to his feet. He then descended the steps and strode purposefully toward the center, right up in front of Emilia.

"A calamity that once drank down half the world, that drove every living creature into a chaos of despair. Do not tell me you are ignorant of that."

"——"

"How many, do you suppose, would tremble merely at your appearance and your lineage? And you would have such a being on the throne, of all things? Unthinkable. Other nations, our own people — the most charitable interpretation would be that we had lost our minds. And this, mind you, is Lugunica, the Kingdom under the Dragon's protection — the very land where the Witch sleeps!"

Bordeaux spread both arms and stamped his foot, bellowing with rough tone and manner. Emilia remained unresponsive to his conduct even still. But in her place, it seemed Subaru was about to reach his limit.

That, however—

"Subaru, the man you're facing is a member of the Council of Wise Men. Rashness will not serve you here."

"Screw that, Reinhard. What is that if not naked prejudice? If the woman herself had actually done something in the past, fine, but he's treating her like that just because she looks similar? Then are all bald old men like that? The bald old man I know is a good deal more decent than this."

"You're beginning to stray from the point, Subaru."

Reinhard restrained Subaru with quick judgment, just as he seemed about to shout himself into the fray in a fit of passion. But Subaru's anger was in no way going to settle down because of that.
Breathing hard through his nose, he glared daggers at Bordeaux's profile as if he might strike him down with a look alone. And then, noticing that hostile gaze directed at him, the elder in question glanced over toward the two quarreling figures, and said,

"If the escort ranks are making such a fuss — Knight Reinhard is escort to the other candidate. Then that black-haired boy is..."

"Bordeaux-sama, might that be enough now?"

Just as Bordeaux's eyes took on a dangerous glint, on the verge of doing something in Subaru's direction, it was Roswaal who threw cold water on that decision.
He stepped forward with his usual air of feigned ignorance, meeting Bordeaux's gaze head-on. For all his advanced age, the elder was robustly built, and stood nearly as tall as the long-limbed Roswaal himself. Trading looks at close range, the old man fixed his eyes on Roswaal's mismatched eyes.

"If you mean, have I said my piece — I have far more left unsaid than that. What you have wrought is that grave a matter. Do you understand that, Chief Court Mage?"

"I understa-a-and it well. I understand the meaning of the outrage I'm committing, and I understand the consideration Bordeaux-sama has shown in representing the sentiments of the Council of Wise Men, and I understand, too, the matter of the feelings of the people who will one day look upon Emilia-sama."

He took the intimidating tone of Bordeaux's words in stride, and on top of that, Roswaal raised a finger.

"Ho-o-owever, are you not forgetting? The matter Bordeaux-sama takes issue with carries no weight whatsoever in the context of the Royal Selection."

"...What do you mean by that?"

"As it happens, was it not Priscilla-sama herself who said it first? That the Royal Selection begins the moment five candidates are gathered, even if only in form. And once the Royal Selection has begun, all that remains is to proceed solemnly according to the articles of the Dragon History Stone. Is that not so-o-o?"

Leaning forward, Roswaal slipped free of Bordeaux's gaze and redirected the flow of conversation toward the Council of Wise Men on the dais. Receiving this, Miklotov closed one narrowed eye.

"Hmm. In other words, you are saying this: that the single point of importance is that Emilia-sama was chosen by the Trial of the Dragon, and whether she is truly qualified to sit the throne is beside the matter?"

"Precisely so-o. I wish, as swiftly as possible, to begin the Royal Selection and see it through to its end, and return the nation to its proper, ordinary state. Otherwise, I won't even have the leisure to enjoy my little indulgences, you see."

Roswaal casually dismissed the very possibility of Emilia taking the throne. There was a shock in that remark surpassing even the earlier abuse, and Subaru could do nothing but stand stunned before anger even had the chance to rise.
Knowing how hard Emilia had striven toward becoming queen, and yet this was what the man who was supposed to be her backer had to say?

Leaving Subaru behind, too dumbstruck to form words, Roswaal continued on cheerfully, waving his raised finger.

"Calling her a 'decoy' might be putting it poorly, but consider it along those lines, if you would. Emilia-sama's appearance is, ye-e-es, quite distinctive indeed. There is scarcely a soul who could look upon her and not think of the 'Witch of Envy.' Which is to say, from our perspective, she becomes an extremely convenient, easy-to-read piece on the board."

"You mean to turn what should be a contest among five candidates into what is, in effect, a contest among four?"

"Do you not think that fewer options reduces the chance of factional splintering? All the more so given the instability of a nation without a monarch, and even the risk of foreign interference in domestic affairs. In that case, should we not be the ones devising a strategy to reduce such risks?"

Bordeaux wore a thoughtful expression at Roswaal's proposal. The other elders of the Council, too, murmured "In that case..." with looks suggesting no small degree of interest in the suggestion.
The scheme was to elevate Emilia to the role of decoy for the other candidates, reducing the Royal Selection to a fixed match dressed up as a contest.

Weighing the merits against the drawbacks — to which side had the balance tipped?
Bordeaux, who had maintained the most stubborn opposition of anyone, nodded, understanding dawning across his stern features.

"Very well, I accept. I withdraw my earlier objection. Let matters proceed as you propose—"
"Don't screw around with me——!!"

An angry shout rang through the hall, and in the wake of its echo, the room fell utterly silent.
The only sound left in the settled quiet was the ragged breathing of the boy who had loosed that furious cry.

Under the scrutiny of everyone present, the boy — Subaru, his face flushed with anger — glared at Roswaal and Bordeaux, the two of them on the verge of reaching an agreement on their scheming plot.
Receiving that glare, Roswaal closed one eye, fixing Subaru with only his yellow eye.

"Oh-ho, my, my. Can you not read the room? This is not a moment where someone of your standing gets to interject. Apologize, and leave the hall."

"I already said my piece, don't screw with me. And here's what comes next: it's you two who ought to be apologizing."

"Now this is truly astonishing. — To think you'd throw your life away like this."

Closing his yellow eye, Roswaal pierced Subaru instead with only his blue eye.
The usual breezy air slipped away from his bearing, replaced by an overwhelming, bone-chilling malevolence that struck fear into anyone who beheld it. The air around Roswaal seemed to warp — an illusion, surely caused by the sheer magnitude of the mana he possessed.

Faced with that overwhelming presence, gritting his teeth, a great blaze flashed across Subaru's mind.
He recalled the sight from the forest of the Witchbeasts, of Roswaal swinging his arm and reducing the Juggernaut — the very thing Subaru had struggled so fiercely against — to a charred husk in an instant. Even entire packs, he'd heard, had been annihilated before him with the ease of twisting a baby's arm.

Given the precision Roswaal had shown in wielding his magic before, evaporating only Subaru from this spot, leaving everyone else untouched, would surely be accomplished with even greater ease than that.
There ought to have been some measure of trust between Subaru and him — but in this particular moment, he could not, and did not, expect to rely on that bond.
It was, after all, none other than Roswaal himself who had already betrayed that trust first.

"Just as things were coming together, this discourtesy. I will grant you one more chance — grovel and beg for forgiveness, and I shall consider it. How about it, Natsuki Subaru?"

Under the swelling torrent of dangerous hostility, Subaru's knees shook violently. A tremor spread to his fingertips, and if he hadn't clenched his jaw to bear it, his teeth would surely have been chattering audibly.
A sensation of terror shaking him to his very core rose up, and he wanted nothing more than to flee this place immediately.
—But,

"I—I said it, didn't I. It's not me who needs to apologize — it's you two!"

His voice was shaking, and cracking too.
But even so, Subaru refused, at the very least, to look down.

Emilia was here, in this place.
And right now, her wish was on the verge of being trampled. Faced with that, right in front of him, there was no way he could back down.
Who, other than Subaru, could stand as her ally now, betrayed at this last critical moment? Who else could serve as a breakwater against those who would speak ill of her based on nothing but her race and her appearance?

He had no strength. His will was weak, too. But he had sheer stubbornness, at least.
Some nameless, inexplicable force alone drove Subaru forward, refusing to let the knees of the two feet planted firmly on the ground bend.

Faced with that powerless, purely stubborn refusal to yield of Subaru's, Roswaal narrowed his mismatched eyes ever so slightly.

"Ve-e-ery well. Without strength, nothing can be accomplished. Learn the meaning of that with your own body. I do hope you can put it to use in your next life."

The instant the final ultimatum was delivered, the torrent of overflowing power took shape and manifested.
What was born was a fireball wreathed in blinding light bright enough to illuminate the entire hall. The mass of flame that formed atop Roswaal's raised palm was about the size of a human head, but its intensity — as if a miniature sun had been born — lightly scorched even the skin of Subaru, standing some distance away.

Before the unmistakable manifestation of malicious intent, before Roswaal now poised for battle, Bordeaux and the other candidates standing relatively nearby braced themselves. Bordeaux twisted his stern face in anger, and the candidates each retreated behind their respective knights.
And Emilia, the only one among them without a knight at her side—

"Wait, Roswaal. That's not what we—"

Her violet-indigo eyes wavered with a mixture of confusion and urgency as she looked back and forth between Roswaal and Subaru, facing off against each other. Confusion was in the eyes she turned on Roswaal, and in the eyes she turned on Subaru, there was—

"Emi..."

"Let me show you the finest fire magic has to offer. — Al Guhr."

Coldly discarding the words, Roswaal turned his palm toward Subaru.
That is, it was the motion of hurling the fireball held in his hand. The fireball closed in slowly, yet certainly, bent on consuming Subaru's body in flame.
Facing it head-on, Subaru's mind instantly resolved to leap sideways to dodge. But his body, the crucial part of the equation, did not follow his thoughts in the slightest. Was it because his legs were shaking? Because fear had frozen his body stiff?

No. Neither.
It wasn't that his will failed to reach his lower body. His will had accelerated too far, and his body could not keep pace with the speed of that transmission.

His vision slowed to an abnormal crawl, and Subaru felt, with his very skin, "Death" gaping open before him, closing in.
That familiar sensation again — the possibility of it being directed at him by Roswaal was a memory he'd wanted to be spared even at the very end of his second loop.

His consciousness left reality behind, and he couldn't even manage to move his eyes.
And so what remained in Subaru's field of vision, aside from the fireball closing in before him, was only Emilia — at the very edge of his sight — reaching a hand out toward him.
Seeing unmistakable urgency in her expression, Subaru felt a wholly out-of-place sense of relief.

In a situation where his own life was under threat, the girl he had feelings for was watching in fear for his sake — how abnormal it was to feel relief in that fact, was more than Subaru, in this moment, had the time to even register.
And then, as a result,

"'Without strength, nothing can be accomplished.' Now that's a fine turn of phrase. Ah, I couldn't agree more, I have to say."

That voice, though one he knew well, struck Subaru's eardrum gently, having lost the familiar ease he knew it by.

At the instant of the fireball's impact, faced with the red-hot death closing in before him, Subaru forgot even to blink.
And so his eyes witnessed, without missing a single detail, everything that happened before him.

At the moment of impact, the fireball stretched out its limbs as if to wrap around Subaru's body, intent on burning him to nothing. In that same instant, a pale blue radiance unfurled ahead of it, enveloping Subaru's entire body — and against the heat that could vaporize a human body in an instant, it went head-to-head in a contest of raw power — and in the end, left nothing behind but white steam, canceling the flame out entirely.

The being that had pulled off that impossible, superhuman feat with such ease drifted in the air directly in front of Subaru, at eye level.

"For a mere HUMAN to say whatever he pleases, right in front of my daughter."

Arms crossed, giving a small huff through his pink nose — the small gray-furred cat, its round, dark eyes frozen with a coldness they had never held before, spoke those words.

At the sudden appearance of that presence, every person in the hall lost the power of speech.

Floating in the air, surveying the surroundings with disdain, was the palm-sized spirit in the form of a small cat — Puck.
The more one knew of his usual air of feigned ignorance, his laid-back way of speaking, his endlessly unhurried personality, the harder it was to hide one's shock at seeing him like this now.

And apart from Subaru's own surprise, he could sense unease spreading among those seeing Puck's true form for the first time.
What they found troubling lay in an entirely different place from what troubled Subaru.
Namely—

"He canceled out Margrave Roswaal's magic head-on...?"
"And in that brief instant, with next to no incantation?"
"Before that even — that condensed mana, what in the world... One must not look directly at what is visible there! You'll be swallowed whole!"

One of the Royal Guard knights called out a warning, supporting a swaying colleague by the shoulder.
Looking around, similar reactions could be seen among several of the officials as well, all of them staring at Puck floating in the air with eyes full of fear.

"—Huh? Eh? What?"

Their reactions were beyond Subaru's comprehension.
What stood before him was Puck. A gray kitten. Certainly, the atmosphere around him felt somehow different from usual, but there was nothing about the change that warranted the knights all igniting the highest level of alarm in unison.
Staring intently, Subaru couldn't find even a shred of anything that might cause the kind of physical distress they were describing.

Subaru, unable to hide his bewilderment. Ignoring that confusion of his, Puck let out a "hmph" with an arrogance unbelievable to anyone who knew him,

"It seems you don't understand, Roswaal L. Mathers. The concession I showed you before was purely because my daughter wished it. Had that not been the case, I wouldn't have minded sinking this whole nation to the bottom of the permafrost."

As he spoke, wind suddenly began swirling around Puck.
It was a wind accompanied by cold sharp enough to sting the skin, and what had at first swirled only around Puck gradually spread to fill the entire hall, showering every person present with that biting chill in full measure. Naturally, as confusion began to fill the venue—

"—Would you kindly calm yourself, Great Spirit-sama?"

A raspy voice was cast from the dais toward Puck, the cause of it all.
The voice belonged to Miklotov. Amid the no small unease spreading even among the members of the Council of Wise Men, he alone, seated in the center, held his posture unchanged, a spark of rational clarity lighting his eyes.

"Such tremendous power, and such dense mana held within — one might guess you to be a Great Spirit of considerable renown."

"Hm. Well, at least there's a young whippersnapper among you who understands a bit of courtesy."

"Hmph. To be treated as a 'young whippersnapper' at my age — now that's a rare experience indeed."

At Puck's gesture, as if raising one eyebrow, Miklotov responded with a small chuckle. Whether this pleased him or not, Puck nodded without dropping his arrogant bearing.

"As for what sort of being I am, unfortunately, you all likely know better than I do. I am merely a spirit that has existed for a long span of time. What you choose to call me is entirely your own business."

"I see. That stands to reason."

Nodding at Puck's answer while stroking his beard, Miklotov then turned his gaze toward Roswaal, who had kept silent. Receiving that look, Roswaal — who had held his tongue ever since his own magic was nullified — shrugged his shoulders.

"Let us return to the earlier account of my meeting with Emilia-sama. I met her far to the east of Lugunica's royal capital — the Great Elior Forest. Commonly known as the 'Forest of Ice.'"

"The Great Elior Forest...!"

At the place name Roswaal spoke, surprise rippled through several people at once.
As if this reaction were exactly as anticipated, Roswaal nodded.

"Yes-s, the Great Elior Forest. Roughly a hundred-odd years ago, it was suddenly enveloped in ice — a frozen barrier said to admit neither entry nor exit. And permafrost, encroaching ever outward."

"If I recall, it was infamous for freezing winds that gradually spread over time, expanding the frozen range year by year, was it not?"

"Frozen earth, plants, air, living creatures — which is to sa-a-ay, a white extinction. Everything lured into eternity, ne-e-ever to return from that endless sleep."

Clapping his hands, Roswaal gestured with an open hand toward Emilia, then toward Puck.

"And living quietly in the depths of that Forest of Ice were these two here — that is the who-o-ole of it."

"Then the permafrost of the Great Elior Forest is..."

"My doing, you mean. Though really, I was only making it comfortable to live in."

Puck answered without any hint of remorse, to Miklotov, who could not hide his astonishment.
It was a story hard for Subaru to picture, having little connection to the land in question, but judging by the reactions around him, it was without doubt becoming a matter of considerable significance.
The fact of Subaru's outburst earlier was already being swept aside, left behind wherever such things go.

At Puck's answer, Miklotov gave a small, troubled groan.

"Hmm. The power required to reduce the Great Elior Forest to such a state — its magnitude yields nothing in comparison to any spirit recorded in legend."

"That's how frightening I am, isn't it?"

Even as he tossed out words that sounded close to praise, what lay in his true heart, one couldn't say. At Puck reading ahead and voicing that very thought aloud, Miklotov closed his eyes.
Then, with a look of understanding settling into his narrowed gaze, he looked down at Roswaal.

"Hmm, I see. I believe I have grasped the gist of it. What was your reason for making contact with the two who lived in the depths of that Great Elior Forest?"

"Part of an investigation, you might say. The Great Elior Forest lies quite near my own domain, and within a few years, it might well have fallen under the effects of the encroaching permafrost. Before that could happen, I went to conduct a preliminary survey."

Failed twice, actually, Roswaal added, raising two fingers with a wry smile at his own blunders. Then he tightened his expression back into seriousness.

"On the third attempt, I made it through the forest, and there I met the two of them. At first, I was attacked without so much as a word of warning... my, my, it was quite the harrowing experience."

"It had been a while since a human had made it through the forest. I suppose the welcome ended up being a little too enthusiastic."

The two of them recalled that moment with an easy familiarity between them, but no one in the hall could laugh along with it.
Which was to say, this meant that a battle had taken place between the two of them that made the magical duel just witnessed a moment ago look like child's play by comparison.

"The welcome that nearly reshaped the very terrain of the forest was chilling enough, but what chilled me even more was what happened afterward. The transcendent spirit who had attacked me without mercy sheathed his blade the instant he heeded the words of a single girl."

Casually dropping the alarming remark about the terrain nearly being reshaped, Roswaal, wearing a solemn expression, gestured toward Emilia with his hand.
Still maintaining her silence, she had bowed her head slightly; whether she was listening to Roswaal's words or not, the light in her violet-indigo eyes was hazy and unfocused, impossible to read.

Subaru felt a faint sense of unease at her demeanor, but naturally no time was afforded to pursue it. As if picking up where Roswaal's words left off, Puck turned his body.

"The plea of my beloved contracted girl. Whatever else may come, I would hear that out above all else. Conversely, I obey nothing besides my own will, save for Lia."

Having said his piece, Puck then added "So" and swept his gaze across the hall. Under the weight of that black-eyed stare, people shrank back as though pinned in place — and at the last, Puck's gaze settled fixedly on Bordeaux. A grin — the kind of grin that word seemed made for — spread across Puck's face.

"You'd best thank Lia that you unpleasant lot aren't all frozen solid where you stand right now. If this child hadn't been holding me back since a moment ago, this place would already be a hall of ice statues."

His words, delivered flatly, carried with them a bone-chilling cold, driving something icy into the hearts of everyone in the hall.
That his words were by no means empty had already been proven in the earlier clash with Roswaal. The fact that every single person in this hall had their life held in the grip of a single spirit — someone's sharp intake of breath sounded unnaturally loud.

It was precisely amid such a moment that—

"—Ho ho ho."

Miklotov let out a small chuckle, and the sight of him laughing felt terribly out of place.
Puck turned quiet eyes on the laughing man, focusing that chilling gaze on the old body before him. But Miklotov met it head-on without flinching.

"I see — it is enough to make one's heart shrink, indeed. — I shall say this much: for that Roswaal, this was quite the amusing bit of staging."

Even under that gaze of absolute zero, Miklotov's composed bearing did not crack. And on top of that, his baffling remark — but hearing it, Roswaal's expression abruptly changed.
A complete turnabout from his serious look of a moment ago, his face returning to its usual air of feigned ignorance,

"Aw-w, you saw through it?"

"I would call it a reasonably well-crafted script. I have no words for the Great Spirit-sama's performance, but perhaps, my lord, you got a bit too carried away with it."

Leaving the bewildered crowd behind, Roswaal struck his own forehead at Miklotov's assessment. Watching this exchange between the two, the floating Puck toyed with his lifted, lengthy tail with one hand.

"See, look, Roswaal. I told you overdoing it wasn't good, didn't I? Never mind me, but in your case, everyone already knows your usual character, so if you're going to act, you need to do it a lot better than that."

"Ouch, that stings, that stings. I was fairly confident about it too, and now I'm hu-u-urt, honestly."

Roswaal puffed out his cheeks to express his displeasure. Puck let it pass with an exhale, and Miklotov, too, closed his eyes wearily and offered no further response.
The one who called a halt to this scene of only these three parties reaching mutual understanding was the bald-headed elder who, at the very end, had been on the receiving end of a chilling glare from Puck — Bordeaux. Creases of unmistakable confusion carved into his stern features, he said,

"W-wait, Miklotov-dono. What in the world is being discussed here?"

"Hmm, to put it simply in a word — this, right here, was Emilia-sama's camp's form of address. Quite different in flavor from the candidates who came before, I'll grant you."

Miklotov closed one eye, seeking Roswaal's agreement, and Roswaal raised both hands as if in surrender, admitting it with a drawn-out "Ye-e-es."
Bordeaux still seemed unable to arrive at understanding in that instant, but Subaru, listening nearby, arrived at the answer. At the same time, he felt his insides genuinely boil with rage at the sheer nastiness of the long-haired, indigo-headed man standing before him.

Which was to say, through this whole exchange just now, Roswaal had —

"Lord Roswaal, the Kingdom's Chief Court Mage. And a Great Spirit capable of contending with him on equal footing or better. By presenting the picture of Emilia-sama commanding such a being, he demonstrated to everyone present that she possesses no small measure of capability. — Have I summarized the outline correctly?"

At Miklotov's meticulous, thorough explanation, understanding finally began to spread through the hall. And once everyone had come to accept that the entire exchange up to now had been a kind of performance, what welled up next was — not praise for Roswaal's cunning scheme, but rather, in large part, something closer to anger at the way he had toyed with people's hearts and minds.
What was seething and burning in Subaru's own chest, too, was something close to that same feeling.

"That was all an act...an act!? Then this whole scene was staged, a farce from start to finish! Roswaal, you scoundrel, what do you take this place for!?"

The one whose face reddened most with fury was, unsurprisingly, Bordeaux, the one who had come closest to being humiliated in this whole affair. The old man, a vein bulging on his forehead, his face flushed a deep red, clearly putting real strain on his heart, bore down on Roswaal as if he might spit in his face.
But what dammed up that momentum was Puck, appearing suddenly right in front of Bordeaux's face. Bordeaux, seeing a ball of fur materialize before his eyes and realizing at once that it was the source of the earlier cold, immediately clamped his mouth shut, opening and closing it soundlessly as if unable to decide what to say.

Looking down at Bordeaux's reaction, Puck laughed with evident amusement.

"Yeah, yeah, it's only natural to be angry. I'll apologize, I really will. Forgive me, I'm sorry, my bad. — But everything I said before was all true, you know."

Even as he voiced his apology, Puck added a remark that set Bordeaux's heart pounding all over again. The kitten rose higher into the air, circling round and round to the side.

"That great forest is my garden, and it's also true that Roswaal is too much for me to handle as an opponent. The reason I'm doing nothing, just existing like this, is thanks to Lia's request, and the reason I do nothing to you lot who badmouth Lia is thanks to that child's kindness."

He formed his words slowly, as if explaining patiently, and then finally, Puck smiled sweetly, adding "I don't want you to misunderstand" as a preface,

"—The reason you all aren't frozen solid right now is Emilia's mercy. Don't forget that."

With those parting words, Puck's form suddenly lost its outline, dissolving into particles of light and vanishing.
The green-tinged particles drifted through the air, glittering, slowly making their way toward Emilia. They headed straight for the folds of her clothing at her chest, and in the next instant, vanished from sight.
Likely, they had returned to Puck's Anchor tucked within Emilia's bosom — the green crystal stone.

The moment Puck returned, Emilia's head swayed faintly, and then the light returned to her eyes. It seemed the cause of her earlier vacant look had been the effect of Puck's manifestation just now.
She blinked several times, her gaze roaming about to confirm her surroundings, and said,

"Roswaal, how are things?"

"Generally, quite good. If I'm honest, the ideal would have been to carry it through to the end with the Great Spirit-sama, but... it seems deceiving Miklotov-sama all the way through proved too difficult after all."

At Roswaal's answer, Emilia merely responded with a small "I see." As she surveyed the hall, her gaze happened to land on Subaru, who was standing just behind her.
The amethyst glow of her eyes settled on Subaru, giving rise to some emotion there, wavering fragilely. Subaru instinctively began to form words, but not knowing what he ought to say, he found himself simply captivated by that glow, and in the end let the chance to exchange words slip past.

Left behind, Subaru watched as Emilia stepped forward and turned back to face the Council of Wise Men.

"First, allow me to apologize for the discourtesy of the deception, esteemed members of the Council of Wise Men."

"No, no — the fault for failing to see through it lies with us. It was merely this old body indulging in a bit of reckless bravado, relying on the Great Spirit-sama's mercy. And besides, this matter concerns the Royal Selection... one must use every means available and make one's case."

At Emilia's apology as she bowed at the waist, Miklotov nodded, displaying the breadth of his character. At his answer, Emilia's lips relaxed somewhat in relief — but into that moment stepped another elder, one who had been having a thoroughly unpleasant time of things since earlier.

"Every means available...? If the endpoint of that is the threat we just witnessed, then 'half-witch' is truly the only fitting word for her, I daresay."

"'Threat' is an ugly way to put it. It was merely a demonstration of the capability she possesses, was it not?"

"What is that, if not an act of intimidation? You heard the spirit's words just now, did you not? The spirit said, 'If you go against my wishes, I'll freeze you solid.' Such a statement, in this very gathering of the nation's dignitaries — how does that differ from the threat of a usurper demanding, 'hand over the nation'?"

Answering back at Roswaal's admonishment, Bordeaux, breathing hard through his nose, voiced his suspicion. But looking at the results, there was a grain of truth in Bordeaux's words, one that couldn't be entirely denied.
The goal of demonstrating power had indeed been achieved. But by the same token, it was nothing less than the result of having made known to others that force could be wielded as one card among many.

That was information that could well prove a disadvantageous starting point for Emilia, standing at the starting line of the Royal Selection, far more so than being judged powerless or harmless would have been.

Facing that disadvantageous situation, Emilia looked up at Bordeaux, now returned to the dais, and opened her mouth,

"—Yes. I am threatening all of you."

Plainly, she affirmed head-on the very suspicion directed at her.
Bordeaux drew in a breath, unable to find a retort. Still looking up at the old man, without the light in her gaze wavering in the slightest, Emilia said,

"Once again, allow me to introduce myself to the honored Council of Wise Men. My name is Emilia. I spent a long span of time in the world of the Great Elior Forest's permafrost, and I command the Great Spirit Puck, who governs fire mana — a silver-haired Half-Elf. Looking upon me, the people of the settlements near the forest call me by this name."

Emilia wove her words in a clear voice, as if singing.
Before the listening audience, standing alone on the stage, she paused for a moment, then closed her eyes for a few seconds as if severing something, before saying,

"One who lives in the freezing forest — the 'Witch of Ice.'"

The instant that word — Witch — left her lips, the very air of the hall shifted.
Everyone had been conscious of it in her appearance all along, yet had deliberately avoided pressing the matter — the worst calamity the world had ever known, and here she stood, the very image of its defining features.

No one could answer her. Bordeaux, who had only spoken with such bluster, of course could not, and neither could the other distinguished members of the Council of Wise Men. Except for the one man whose very mettle was cast from different stock — Miklotov.

"To display one's power, and then state one's demand. That is truly the manner of a witch. — Then, madam Witch of Ice, what is it you intend to demand of us through this threat?"

"My demand is only one thing. — Simply, that you be fair."

"...Fair."

At Emilia's calm response to his question, Miklotov repeated the demand under his breath. Emilia nodded in acknowledgment at that murmur, saying "Yes,"

"That I am a Half-Elf, that my appearance carries the same features as the reviled Witch — none of this can be changed. That everyone therefore looks upon me with prejudiced eyes, for that same reason, is also unchanging. But I absolutely refuse to have every chance at possibility plucked away from me because of it."

"In other words, Emilia-sama — you wish to be treated as an equal, as one candidate among the Royal Selection, is that your desire?"

"Being treated fairly is an extremely precious thing in my life. To demand anything beyond that, in this place, would be nothing less than an insult to the fairness I hold so dear."

Just how much malice must she have been exposed to, across the days she'd lived through?
There must surely have been times when she was subjected to baseless abuse just as Bordeaux had done, times she was persecuted for the single fact of being a Half-Elf.
And so, she wished — with all her being — simply to be treated with a fair eye, here in this very place.

She turned. Her silver hair flowed with the motion, sending a shimmer the color of a pale moon rippling through the hall. As everyone found themselves captivated by that glimmer, Emilia raised her voice,

"So the only thing I ask of you is this one thing: to be treated fairly. I will never resort to something as unfair as using my contracted spirit as a shield to seize the throne by force."

That was a choice Emilia could make, if she wished to choose it.
But she had ruled out that option from the very start, deliberately wishing instead for a situation that might work against her own interests.
Because—

"I am an immature being, lacking in far more ways than the other candidates. There is so much I don't know, so much I still have to learn. And yet, because I know the summit I must aim for, I have no intention of ever neglecting my effort."

Subaru had watched her, striving hard at her studies in the mansion, throwing herself earnestly into every task before her.
And so, more than anyone else present, Subaru alone knew the truth in her words.

He couldn't hide his trembling. Even as his throat went dry, Subaru became aware of the strange fact that his eyes were beginning to well up, and he fought desperately to keep his own pathetic groan from escaping.

"I don't know whether my efforts are worthy of the throne. But the resolve to keep striving so that I might become worthy — that is genuine. In that feeling alone, I will not lose to any other candidate."

"So," she continued, looking straight up at Bordeaux on the dais,

"Look at me with fair eyes. Just Emilia, with no family name. Not the 'Witch of Ice,' nor a silver-haired Half-Elf. Look at me."

That last murmur carried something close to a plea.
And yet the strength of will, the strength of feeling, poured into it did not waver in the slightest.

What was given as a matter of course to the other candidates, Emilia was asking to be given to herself as well.
To stand at the same starting line. To be granted the chance to run from there.

For a while, silence enveloped the hall.
It was not that no one could produce words. Everyone was bracing themselves, waiting for an answer to Emilia's question.
At last, Bordeaux, under the gaze of everyone present, released a long, long breath, and said,

"My opinion will not change in the slightest. Your appearance, evoking as it does the 'Witch of Envy,' will without doubt exert a harmful influence on the people. As regards the Royal Selection, your position remains disadvantaged all the same."

In a low voice, he directly opposed everything Emilia had argued up to now. At that answer, a faint shadow crossed Emilia's violet-indigo eyes.

"But—"

And yet,

"To interfere even with the hearts of the people is a domain permitted to no one. And so, there is nothing I can do about how you are perceived. Even so, I will apologize for my earlier discourtesy. — No, I apologize for my rudeness, Emilia-sama."

Rising from his seat, Bordeaux knelt right there on the spot, offering the deepest bow of respect.
Astonishment rippled outward at this action. In the midst of it, he raised his face and said,

"You could have frozen me solid, for going against your wishes. And yet you did not do so, and instead asked only for fairness. — That is a noble act."

Speaking gently, with a look of rational clarity on his face, Subaru found himself, belatedly, convinced that this old man truly was one of the nation's dignitaries counted among the Council of Wise Men.
The shadow lifted from Emilia's expression, and the joy of being recognized naturally brightened her features. Her lips curved into an arc, a smile blooming like a flower.

Watching from an angle where he could only catch it in profile, Subaru saw Bordeaux — now facing that smile head-on — catch his breath, and inwardly raised a middle finger, muttering *"Goddamn stubborn old geezer!"* Though surely his inward curse hadn't actually reached him, Bordeaux cleared his throat as if to cover his own moment of rigidity, and said,

"I-in any case, true fairness in the full sense will be difficult to achieve. A path of hardship undoubtedly lies ahead. Even so, do you still desire the throne?"

"I've known from the very start that it wouldn't be a smooth road. Even so, I will sit upon that throne, without fail. Because there's a reason I must."

To Bordeaux's question testing her resolve, Emilia answered in a voice now free of any hesitation.
Hearing that answer, Bordeaux nodded with apparent satisfaction and returned to his seat, extending his palm toward Miklotov's gaze as if entrusting everything to him. Receiving this, Miklotov combed his long beard while nodding repeatedly.

"It has grown into something of a stormy affair, but I believe that is sufficient. Emilia-sama, and Margrave Roswaal — have you anything more to say?"

"No."
"In my case, I honestly still have plenty left to say, but in this instance..."

"Then, thank you very much for your time."

Emilia's answer was brief. Meanwhile, Marcos cut off Roswaal's still-rambling words with impeccable timing, bringing them to a forced end.
Emilia gave a light tap on the back of the tall man, who pouted his lips in dissatisfaction, and the two of them made to return to the line of candidates.
But things did not go quite so smoothly.

"Now then, and what of this fellow here — what is his standing in all this?"

Just as Emilia and the others made to step back into line, that question came from Miklotov's mouth.
The old man raised one eyebrow, a mischievous glint lighting his eyes, his gaze turned toward that "fellow" — that is to say, toward Subaru, who had ended up stepping forward with nowhere in particular to stand.

"Uweh."

Such a groan escaping him unbidden could hardly be helped, one hopes to be forgiven for saying.
After all, despite having leapt forward in a fit of passionate fury, Subaru had been left utterly unable to keep up with the exchange that followed, reduced to fretting uselessly up on the stage playing the role of mere spectator.
He had occasionally slipped in a furtive "Ehh!" or "Ohh!" or "EMM!" here and there, but in terms of not affecting the overall proceedings, he might as well have been part of the scenery.

Though he'd hoped to simply drift off along with the scenery and vanish from sight, he had, quite thoroughly, found himself back in the spotlight, courtesy of the old man's curiosity.
And what's more, it seemed—

"It would gall me somewhat to let things proceed entirely according to Margrave Roswaal's design, after all."

He'd surely meant to mutter it under his breath, but for some reason, Subaru now heard Miklotov's remark quite clearly.
Which was to say, the payback for the earlier staged performance had come circling right back around to Subaru. It was a case of pure misfortune, and yet, thinking that it was his own rashness that had invited this turn of events, it stung all the more painfully.

"Ah, um, that, well, this kid is, um, my..."

Turning around, Emilia moved to stand between him and the Council's line of sight, waving her arms and hands as if to hide Subaru from view.
Flustered, all trace of her earlier dignified poise nowhere to be found, there she was — the Emilia Subaru knew so well from everyday life, back once more.

He felt an oddly indescribable sense of relief at that, and his cheeks relaxed at a moment thoroughly unsuited to reading the room. And of all times, it was at that very moment that Emilia happened to glance sideways at his face,

"It's not — I mean, um, this person is, well... wait, why are you grinning like that. This isn't the time for that, behave yourself properly."

Catching the tail end of Emilia's scolding words, "behave yourself properly," Subaru found Emilia turning displeased eyes on him. Even that accusing look was charming, and Subaru let a small smile escape as he reached out and touched her shoulder.

"It's fine, Emilia-tan. — I won't make excuses either."

"Excuses, this isn't the time for that... Subaru, what are you planning to do? Hey, wait a second."

Leaving her calling voice behind him, Subaru strode forward with purpose.
And then, boldly stepping into the very spot where the candidates before him had stood, receiving the full weight of the Council of Wise Men's gaze from the dais, he clenched his teeth, steeled himself, and raised his face.

"Pleased to make your acquaintance, honored members of the Council of Wise Men, may your countenances shine ever bright! I offer my most sincere and humble apologies for the tremendous trouble I have caused on this occasion, if it please you!"

Lowering his hips, right hand behind his back, left hand palm-up extended forward, he adopted a stance modeled on antiquated etiquette — an other-world rendition of the old Japanese gesture of formal introduction, "if you'll pardon my presumption."
Confirming, after a moment of silence, that no one raised any objection, Subaru became convinced that first contact had been successfully established, and moved fluidly on into his next gesture.

Widening his stance, tilting his hips at an angle, left hand resting on the tilted hip, right hand raised high as if pointing dramatically toward the ceiling — striking a flamboyant pose.
And then,

"My name is Natsuki Subaru! Manservant of Roswaal Manor, and first knight to the royal candidate present here — Emilia-sama!"

He shouted, then lowered the raised right hand, snapped his fingers, flashed his teeth in a grin, and threw in a wink,

"Do keep me in your good graces, if you please."

To make his own standing unmistakably clear, Subaru's wildly out-of-place entry into the fray began.
And as he did, he could feel the air of the room growing colder, faster and more chillingly than it had even at the moment of Puck's appearance.
