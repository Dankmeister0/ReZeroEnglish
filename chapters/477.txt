Arc 6 Chapter 71: "Count One"

——The last words were swept away on the wind, and Natsuki Subaru's consciousness cut off.

"――――"

Ironic, if you wanted to call it ironic — this time's "death" had been a "death" factored in beforehand.
Perhaps that was why his heart had been calm in the instant of "death" — no, that wasn't quite it either. Of course there had been anxiety and tension at throwing himself, of his own will, into an act that courted death.
But unlike every time before, because this "death" had been anticipated, he felt he'd been able to face the coming "death" more sincerely than ever before.

Of course, he didn't for a moment believe that choosing to take his own life had truly been a salvation for Shaula.

*I don't want you to die, so order me to die.*
Shaula, who had worried over Subaru that much, had watched that very Subaru throw himself to his death right before her eyes. The shock of it was immeasurable, and it must have shattered, spectacularly, the heart she'd kept waiting for four hundred years.
So that act was nothing more than Subaru's own self-satisfaction. And on top of that, the worst kind of self-satisfaction — one where he wouldn't even see the outcome with his own eyes.

But even so—

"—so what."

If it was going to be called nothing but self-satisfaction, nothing but hypocrisy, then so what.
In the end, the only scale by which the deeds of this world could ultimately be weighed was one's own. A good deed left undone had no meaning, and in the final accounting, there was no such thing as "mere hypocrisy."

——Everyone survives.

Through his dialogue with Shaula, that had become the final goal Subaru held aloft.
No — to be precise, everyone's survival had been the objective from the very start. It was only that the judgment of whether to include Shaula within that "everyone" had now been settled, and Subaru had decided to love them all.

To guide the incident engulfing this sand tower to resolution without losing a single one of them.
To do whatever he could toward that end.
That was—

"The meaning of why I'm me. —Isn't that right, 'Natsuki Subaru'?"

And with that instant of resolve, the long, long awakening from the brink of "death" came—.

"—Subaru, is what you just said for certain?"

"Yeah, don't start doubting me now, not after all this. That's information I dragged back at the cost of a seriously awful time. If it doesn't get used, I won't be able to rest easy!"

Tossing back what amounted to a snarl at the profile of the handsome man running alongside him, Subaru pulled his small hand along and kept running.
Dashing through the passages of the fourth layer, aiming for the second layer, "Electra" — while carrying his impatience with him, Subaru ran on, pulling Beatrice's hand, and Julius's yellow eyes glanced sideways at him.

"Subaru, your color is bad. Could it be that whatever happened inside the book—"

"I never hid that it was rough. Don't tell me you're about to say I should quietly go hole up in the Green Room."

"Were this not the situation it is, I would recommend rest. Unfortunately, we find ourselves run ragged. Right now, we must use whatever is usable, even if it's a grain of sand."

"Way to put it — calling me a grain of sand, of all things..."

"One that could be swept away by the slightest wind. If possible, I would like you to stay somewhere within sight."

At the elegant turn of phrase, Subaru narrowed his eyes, thinking it was ironic to talk of being swept away by the wind.
In fact, by his body's own sense of it, his "death" of only a few minutes ago, felt in real time, had been swept away by wind. Julius comparing Subaru's existence to a grain of sand wasn't entirely off the mark either.

"But even a grain of sand has its pride."

"Besides, that grain of sand has Betty attached to it, I suppose. Meaning, it's a grain of sand with the most adorable companion imaginable."

"Kinda sounds like the sand that gets stuck in your shoe."

"That is not what I meant, I suppose!"

Grinning at Beatrice's puffed-out cheeks, Subaru tightened his arm and pulled her body close. Beatrice gave a little "eep!" as her light body settled against his chest.
Running while pulling her along by the hand had its own charm, but right now speed mattered more. Fortunately, Beatrice's body was as light as an angel's wing, so carrying her while running was no trouble at all.

"Well, I've never actually held an angel's wing, but still."

"Let's be done with idle chatter. —Subaru, I know I'm being persistent, but—"

"Yeah, persistent. I can't say 'don't worry' even if it kills me, but... it's a certainty that Reid and 'Gluttony' are going to come into contact. That's what we're going to stop."

Coming to a halt, Julius fixed his gaze on the great staircase ahead, leading up to the second layer, and put the question to him. Cutting off that question, Subaru declared that he was certain.
There was no possibility whatsoever that this was a lie. After all, this was something he'd heard from Julius himself. And if they could prevent it—

"It'll put one check on how out-of-control Reid gets."

"...In any case, I would rather avoid him bursting in on us in the middle of this chaos, whatever happens. Even simply confirming his movements is worth the trip."

Setting foot on the great staircase, Julius nodded to Subaru. Nodding back, Subaru, still holding Beatrice, took the first step.
And then—

"—gh."

"Subaru, you really don't look well, I suppose. Is it the effect of the book showing?"

Seeing Subaru's head sway for an instant, Beatrice reached anxiously to touch his cheek. Taking comfort in the warmth of that small palm, Subaru shook his head and said, "I'm fine."

"It's probably just a fever from reading too hard. Besides, like I told Julius — now's not the time to be lying down sick. Everyone's got to pull together."

"...If you're truly at your limit, you must absolutely tell Betty before it comes to that, I suppose."

"...Yeah, I know."

Answering Beatrice's insistence, Subaru let out a deep, heavy breath.
His body was sluggish. His head was heavy. Waves of nausea kept pressing in without pause, and there was a stagnant feeling throughout his body, as if coal tar had been poured into his veins in place of blood.
All of this was the result of Subaru taking on Ram's ailment through "Cor Leonis."

"――――"

The one gain was that the existence of "Cor Leonis" had carried over even across a "Return by Death." —Just like the memory of "death," the Authority itself had carried forward.
Thanks to that, he could currently grasp where his companions were. Everyone was following his instructions, and each of them had begun their own fight back.
Among those companions' signals was Shaula, fighting hard against Meili on the balcony. She was there, without doubt, fighting for the sake of Subaru and the others — no, for Subaru's sake.

"I'll save you. No matter what."

Muttering it briefly, Subaru chased after Julius's back as he went on ahead.
Vaulting past one layer of the tower, the great staircase connecting to the layer above was long. But there was no time to spare for weakness, no room to spare for complaints.
Even if his whole body festered, even if his bones cracked and shattered, this was a necessary price to pay to break through the current situation.
And then—

"—Subaru, we're almost there!"

Julius's crisp voice rang out, and Subaru, out of breath, lifted his face. Julius, having reached the top step, turned halfway back and beckoned him on.
Following that, the moment he dashed up the stairs, he was greeted by an open space.

"――――"

Round, a single space punched clear through the entire floor — the polar opposite of the fourth layer's many separate rooms. This was arrival at the second layer, "Electra," whose entire reason for existing had been devoted wholly to the "Trial."
And in "Electra," which the three of them — Subaru, Beatrice, and Julius — had now reached, the first-generation "Sword Saint" Reid and the Deadly Sin Archbishop of "Gluttony" were—

"—Oi, you. Is this all you got, you? No joke, you. Weren't you here to entertain me, you? Not enough, you. Don't make me laugh, you. No, at least make me laugh, you. This ain't the least bit interesting, you."

They came upon the scene of Reid Astrea, the "Sword Saint," rambling on endlessly at the boy whose leg he had seized, slamming him mercilessly into the floor.

"――――"
"This is..."

Staring fixedly at the scene before him, it was Julius who muttered, dumbfounded. But Subaru's own reaction, looking at the same thing, was no different from what Julius had muttered.
As for what kind of scene he'd been expecting, that was a hard question to answer—

"Ahn? What, you lot. Even you showed up, huh — pretty eager, ain't ya. Or is it not me you want, but this guy here?"

"Ka, ka, ka..."

So saying, Reid scratched at his own chest with his left hand while, with the other, he lifted the boy whose leg he held — the Deadly Sin Archbishop of "Gluttony" — upside down, eyeing him with suspicion.
As stated before, it was hard to say what scene he'd expected, but at the very least, this scene blew far past anything Subaru had imagined.

"I heard that Reid and 'Gluttony' were negotiating, but..."

"Negotiating... is this it, I suppose? To Betty, even 'combat' seems too soft a word, I suppose."

"Yes, I agree. It's hard to call this a discussion."

At Subaru's muttered words, Beatrice and Julius reached the same conclusion. But their reaction didn't quite sit right with Subaru.
Because it had been Julius — who should have witnessed the same kind of situation the last time around — who had told him that Reid's contact with "Gluttony" could be described as "negotiation."
In any case—

"This is the scene where 'Gluttony' got the tar beaten out of him, right?"

Not directed at anyone in particular, Subaru simply put what he saw into words.

Indeed, in the wide space of the second layer, "Electra," traces remained scattered everywhere of what had probably been a battle fought between "Gluttony" and Reid.
There were cracks in the trampled floor, fissures in the walls that looked like the paths of slashes, scorch marks from something catching fire, and clumps of earth that shouldn't have been there — surely evidence of a lavish display, as if every conceivable secret technique in "Gluttony's" repertoire had been laid out like a department store showcase, showing off its true form.

——Only for that whole department store of techniques to be smashed to smithereens by the natural disaster known as Reid.

"Earthquake, thunder, fire — all of it was Reid, huh..."

"So, what'll you do, you lot? Wanna take the 'Trial'? You, with the slightly better face on you now, you can have a go if you want. But you, little fry — no good. Yeah, no good."

"Why do you exempt him?"

"Look at him and you'll see. A guy who wasn't even a decent playmate at full strength, and now he's not just incomplete but full of holes. Don't you dare stand in front of me lookin' like that. You want me to kill you with one finger, don't you."

"Gi, giyaaaaah!!"

As he spoke, Reid drove the fingers of his left hand into the torso of "Gluttony," whom he held. Gouged mercilessly in the stomach, the Deadly Sin Archbishop of "Gluttony" let out an ugly scream.
It was unclear how much stock to put in "Gluttony's" apparent age, but even so, it was hard to watch what looked outwardly like a boy of thirteen or fourteen being tormented by an adult.
It seemed Julius felt the same way—

"Reid Astrea, surely even you have pride as a swordsman. If so, what reason could there be to torment a boy like this beyond necessity..."

"Oi, you, don't talk stupid."

"――――"

Whatever else, to Julius, who had been about to propose starting over and talking things through, Reid — without any change in the tone of his voice, only now carrying a bone-chilling edge of killing intent — cut him off.
Reid swung the body of "Gluttony," held by the leg, side to side, and said,

"Pride as a swordsman, huh? If you're gonna go throwin' around somethin' that grand, then there oughta be such a thing as a swordsman's resolve too, ain't there. You start a fight without bein' resolved to die? If that's how cocky an attitude you're bringin', I guess it ain't your fault you don't get it 'less you feel some pain, huh. Ahh?"

"――――"

Once the argument was placed on the footing of a swordsman's resolve and pride, the disadvantage was Julius's. Having raised pride first, Reid's talk of resolve carried a certain persuasive weight.
And besides, however pitiful it looked—

"If it means 'Gluttony' goes down, that's a result we can live with, I suppose..."

Of course, the Deadly Sin Archbishop of "Gluttony" wasn't included in the goal Subaru had raised of everyone surviving.
The ones Subaru wanted to save were, strictly speaking, Emilia, Beatrice, Ram, Rem, Meili, Echidna, Julius, Shaula. And the two of them, Patrasche and Giant.
"Gluttony" and Reid were outside of that. If the two of them destroyed each other — though calling it that would be too generous a term given how one-sided the result was — that would work in Subaru's group's favor.

However—

"Anyway, you. Is this guy a friend of yours? If so, I get why you're all fired up, sorta, but..."

"No, absolutely not. That Deadly Sin Archbishop of 'Gluttony' is a mortal enemy to me as well, one with whom, at worst, I may yet come to take each other's lives in the course of battle."

"Heh? Then why're you so mad? 'Cause your prey got stolen from you?"

"—Because you are trampling on a swordsman's pride."

Straight on, Julius declared this to the "Sword Saint" — the man first given the title bestowed on whoever stood at the peak of the sword.
To Julius's declaration, which bordered on insult, Reid narrowed the blue eye not hidden by his eyepatch and listened in silence.
To such quiet composure from the "Sword Saint," Julius continued, cutting through the air like a blade.

"Swordsmen, warriors, knights — anyone with the resolve to place themselves in battle should understand that death may await them at the end of that fight. But that should only ever be at the very end of the battle, a death that comes after exhausting every last ounce of strength. Never should a victor toy with the life of the vanquished."

"...You're sayin' stuff that makes my ears itch. Where the hell does anyone listen to that kinda talk?"

"Call it a naive ideal, call it empty platitudes — I don't care! But embodying that ideal, that platitude, is the way of the knight I believe in!"

"――――"

Declaring this so boldly, Julius drew his knightsword.
By what he'd heard, it was a knightsword that had once been broken — not the blade he used to favor, one whose fit in his hand might well be uncertain.
But the thing most essential to his very foundation didn't waver.
Surely, deep within himself, in the deepest place, it kept on shining, without end.

That shining something wouldn't let Julius overlook Reid's brutality.
Even if the victim of that brutality was a Deadly Sin Archbishop, one of the wicked, that made no exception.

"—The way of the knight, huh."

That murmur, suddenly, shook the air of "Electra."

It was a quiet voice.
It didn't sound as though it carried any particular emotion.
It sounded like nothing more than something buried in an idle, bored tone.

——But that was the signal for the eruption of the first-generation "Sword Saint," Reid Astrea.

"Oi, you, how long you gonna keep sleepin'. Get up already."

"Gugii!"

With a creak, a grip strong enough to crush the leg it held tightened, and "Gluttony," whose leg was about to be crushed, screamed. Then Reid looked down at the upside-down Deadly Sin Archbishop and said,

"You said somethin' interesting earlier. Said you'd devour me to the last, savor me to the last, lick me clean to the last, didn't ya? —Go ahead. I'll let you do it."

"—. Gihi, kaha, ahahahaha! What is that?! What, what, what's with the sudden change?! Why the sudden change of heart, red-haired mister? You've been jerking us and them around this whole time just because you didn't want that, and now—!"

"Changed my mind. —Ahh, that's right, that's right."

Grimacing in pain, "Gluttony" tried to regain composure, and Reid glared at him. Then, as if suddenly noticing something, Reid turned his head, saying,

"You lot are just gonna get in the way soon enough anyway."

With a "gun," Reid stepped sideways diagonally as he spoke.
The instant he did, a glowing purple crystal shard slammed into the spot where Reid had been standing. With a high-pitched sound like breaking glass, the purple arrow itself and the floor shattered.
But the aim had missed. And having sidestepped the attack, Reid closed the distance in a single bound toward Subaru and the others who had launched it, saying—

"—! Subaru, this is bad, I sup—"

"There ya go."

Beatrice's warning came too late, and "Gluttony" was slammed sideways into Subaru's torso like a club.
Handling a human being effortlessly like a blunt weapon, an unstoppable force sent Subaru, holding Beatrice, flying mercilessly.

"Gahh!"

"Good thing you didn't hesitate, you lot. In terms of a fight, that's a damn sight better than pretty boy over there. Well, ain't got a shred of actual skill to back it up, though."

With no room to spare to answer Reid's running commentary, Subaru could barely manage to wrap himself around Beatrice and tumble. From that single hit, his organs felt turned inside out, and combined with Ram's burden, it felt as though something like the very wellspring of his stamina — his life itself — had been gouged clean out.

"Subaru! Lady Beatrice! Ngh...!"

Worrying over the flying Subaru and Beatrice, Julius, closing the distance to Reid in an instant, thrust his sword. But Reid stomped it down from above with a foot in a zōri sandal, then swapped his pivot foot and drove a kick into Julius's torso.
Julius caught it on the hastily-drawn hilt of his knightsword, but couldn't fully block the shockwave that pierced through. Gutted by that penetrating blow, he was sent staggering back several steps.
And in that interval—

"There, try it. Whether you can devour me whole, or die playin' along with me, either way, if you want it, you gotta move. That's what life is, ain't it, you."

"—Ahh, ah, ahh! I get it, I get it, I get it, I understand, I understand, I want to understand, precisely because I understand! Overeat! Overindulge! I'll do it just how you want, damn it! I can't help myself — I have to devour!"

Still upside down, hoisted right up in front of Reid's face, "Gluttony" howled at him. Then "Gluttony" reached out a hand and grabbed the area of Reid's eyepatch, over his left eye.
Then, opening its mouth wide—

"—Reid Astrea."

With a lick, as if peeling something away, "Gluttony" released its hand from Reid. Then it gazed lovingly at that hand, which should have held nothing, and touched its tongue to it.
As it was, the sound of something invisible being chewed and greedily slurped down rang out.
And then—

"—ah"

The change came, in an instant, all at once.
As though it had been an illusion or something all along, Reid's figure vanished in the blink of an eye. From the space where his tall frame had unmistakably stood, he disappeared, and "Gluttony," whose leg had been held, dropped to the floor. Deftly bracing with its hands, it flipped over and landed lightly, looking at its own palm.
What passed through its eyes was something close to the emptiness, the desolation, the disappointment of a lavish feast vanishing from the plate right in front of you—

"Ahh, that's amazing. What a flavor, what a flavor, I imagined all sorts of flavors it might be, so much, so much, so very much... but it exceeded expectations!"

"You... ate Reid?"

"Ahh, you saw it, didn't you?! I ate him! I ate him up! Amazing, isn't it, the best, this is a flavor beyond imagining! Even for us, called 'ill-eaters' — this guy's rich, ripe flavor, well, I understand where Lye was coming from—"

Arching back, touching its own sharp canine teeth, "Gluttony" praised the taste of Reid. Conceivably the worst food review in human history — and it stopped midway.
No one had interrupted. The cause lay within "Gluttony" itself.

"—Nn? Ah, ah, ahh, ahh, aaaaahh?"

"W-what on earth is it starting now..."

"No, that's..."

Placing a hand to its own throat and stomach, "Gluttony" began movements resembling vocal exercises. Beatrice found the sight unsettling, but Subaru had a guess as to what was happening.
If what had just passed between Reid and "Gluttony" — if that had been the "negotiation" Julius had described—

"W-w-wait, wait, hihe, gihi, gihihi. That's weird, that's weird, isn't it weird?! I mean, this is... you, this is strange, right?"

"――――"

"Ain't nothin' strange about it, you. Eat or be eaten — that's what livin' is, ain't it."

Strangling its own throat, "Gluttony" spoke in a manner resembling a dialogue with itself. Its manner of speech underwent a clear change, and then, as if dragged along by that change in speech, "Gluttony's" very body began to change as well.
It was a change best described, like the waxing and waning of the moon, as happening before you knew it.

Subaru swore he hadn't looked away.
The same was true of Beatrice in his arms, and of course Julius with his sword raised. And yet, even while remaining within the three of their fields of vision, "Gluttony" had erased its own form in an instant.
And in its place, standing where "Gluttony" had been, was—

"—Ahh, a live body really is different, huh. Feels like there's actual blood runnin' through this meat. Even got a proper appetite goin', don't I. Shoulda had a snack 'fore you came, brat."

So saying, the one who had, in the blink of an eye, completed a substitution — retiring from the stage of the Pleiades Watchtower for a mere few dozen seconds and immediately returning to center stage — was Reid.
Reid Astrea had seized the flesh of the Deadly Sin Archbishop of "Gluttony" and achieved his resurrection into this world.

"This is..."

The result of what Julius called the negotiation between Reid and "Gluttony."
Of course, with Subaru and the others intervening this time, the details of the exchange between Reid and "Gluttony" would surely have changed — but broadly speaking, it must have remained the same.
In other words—

"Some negotiation. Making it sound all fancy..."

"Subaru, this isn't the time for that, I suppose. 'Gluttony' vanished, and Reid's..."

Subaru cursed at the Julius-style way of putting it that had described events the last time around. In his arms, Beatrice called for still greater caution, but Subaru gave her head a light pat, then looked over at Reid, saying,

"Strictly speaking, he turned 'Gluttony's' Authority of reproducing whoever it eats back against it, and overwrote its ego with his own... right?"

"Don't say difficult things at me. I don't give a damn about that. And don't you go actin' all high and mighty about somethin' I don't even know, little fry. Little fry? Ahh, hn? You... ahh? You, this — you, that's it, you."

"—?"

Setting aside the fact of mind taking over body, Reid frowned. Grunting to himself, unusually, he reached up and hooked a finger under his own eyepatch. He lifted it, revealing a blue eye that, being perfectly sound, had no need of the patch at all, and looked at Subaru with it.
Looking, fixing his gaze, staring—

"—You, you're creepy."

"――――"

"How the hell d'you not go crazy with somethin' like that in you. Nah, wait, have you gone crazy? Is that why you're like this? Dunno. Never thought about it."

Subaru was genuinely bewildered by what Reid was even talking about.
On top of the bewilderment, the chills and nausea wracking his whole body were reaching a critical point. Naturally, the more Ram's own body ramped up its voltage, the more the backfire that should have hit her instead came slamming into Subaru.

This time around, Subaru hadn't come face to face with Ram directly, so he had no way of knowing what the cause was behind Ram feeling in good condition. Thinking about it that way, it seemed Ram must have shown considerable consideration for Subaru the last time too.
He was only noticing that consideration from a Ram who wasn't even present here, after dying twice—

"I see, I see, I see, is it. Dunno what it is to me, but seems like what these guys are after is you. Well then, that makes this easy."

"Reid! I am your opponent!"

Closing in, Julius unleashed a slash. But Reid tried to catch it with chopsticks pulled from his robes. However—

"Ahn?"

The slash grazed the tip of Reid's chopsticks, and blood sprayed from the right arm that had failed to fully block it. Seeing this, Reid looked at it with suspicion, and Julius raised an eyebrow in slight surprise as well.
And then—

"――――"

Having seen that with his own two eyes, without question — that alone was a gain for Subaru.

"Tch."

Clicking his tongue, Reid spun the chopsticks between his fingers like a pen, then used them to deftly flick aside Julius's knightsword. In a single step, he erased the distance to Julius, and, pressing his palm against the torso of the man who instinctively tried to pull back, said,

"Said I was your opponent, did ya?"

"Gh—"

"You got a princess in your arms behind you — there's no way you can go toe-to-toe with me!"

A palm strike with a twist added to it exploded against Julius's torso, and, spitting blood, his slender, tall frame was blown backward. He planted his long legs on the floor to try to kill the momentum, but couldn't kill it entirely, and his body bounced along the ground before rolling to a stop.

Then, temporarily cutting short his engagement with Julius as the distance between them widened, Reid turned back, and—

"Shh!"

Subaru lashed out with the whip at his lower back, aiming for the side of Reid's face. But no miracle of muscle memory occurred, and the tip of the whip flew off in a wildly wrong direction.
The effects of his lost memories were bleeding not just into his mind but into the repeated motions of his body as well. Feeling that keenly, Subaru let out a heavy breath.
At the same moment—

"You, with this kinda pointless little trick..."

"—That pointless little trick is the final push that's going to bury you, I suppose."

Past exasperation, edging into open fury, Reid — and behind him came the sound of a great clap of hands. Looking, it was Beatrice, who had left Subaru's side, her hands now free.
But even with hands parted, a real connection remained. Through that link with Subaru, Beatrice extracted what scant MP-like reserve remained — and invoked a great spell.

"—Ul Shamak."

The instant Beatrice's incantation completed, a massive black hole formed in the space.
Appearing suddenly in midair, it manifested as an existence carrying a primal terror — one couldn't tell where it led, or how deep it went — and it moved to swallow Reid standing before it.

"That's..."

At a glance, it was clear this was a great spell that acted upon space itself.
It was less a matter of knowledge than of impression. Subaru had heard about the Spirit that Beatrice was contracted with, one far too fine for him, but the actual force of a great spell was on a wholly different scale.
He hadn't even known this existed. This was a gain.
That black hole swallowed Reid — where would it fling his "Sword Saint" self—

"What is this, air? Air's somethin' that's everywhere — you think that'll stop me?"

"――――"

That overwhelming a spell, and Reid broke through it with ease, with a single swing of his chopsticks.
Literally, with the casualness of cutting through air — no, in fact, that was probably exactly what it was to him. He wasn't the type to go out of his way to brag in some roundabout way about what he was capable of.
He'd cut the air, so that's how he explained it. That was all.

"A dimension slash — as far as I know, that's a pretty high-tier ability, isn't it..."

"Oi oi, don't go givin' it some cool name, it'll get everybody's hopes up. And gettin' 'em up just for you to go splat is no fun at all, is it."

With a whoosh, the chopsticks swung at the same angle as they had to cut Beatrice's spell, and in the next instant, Subaru's body burst diagonally, from chest to hip.

"Ga, aaaah!!"

"Subaru!!"
"Subaru—!"

Tasting a burning-hot sensation, Subaru collapsed on the spot, and Beatrice and Julius called out to him. At their two voices, Subaru bit down on his back teeth and glared at Reid with bloodshot eyes.
And to the two of them trying to rush over to him, he raised a palm, stopping their feet.

"I'm... fine..."

Shaking his head, he insisted to the two of them that they needn't worry about him. Of course, there was no way words like that alone could win them over.

The wound on his chest was shallow. —No, it wasn't shallow. It wasn't shallow, but it didn't hurt. Correct that: it wasn't beyond bearing. In any case, it was painful, but he was fine. It wasn't worth putting the two of them through pain, through hardship. Clench your back teeth, hold back the rising blood — or was it nausea? Either way, hold it down. Face forward. Glare at Reid. Study him closely.

"And that's supposed to reach me, you?"

With an exasperated look, Reid watched Subaru, blood trickling from the corner of his mouth.
Toward that Reid, Subaru thrust out a hand, raising one finger.

"—Ah, ah. It'll reach."

Even now, in a place that wasn't even here. It'll reach.
He would save Emilia, Beatrice, Ram, Rem, Meili, Echidna, Julius, Shaula, Patrasche, and Giant.

Without leaving out a single one.
"Gluttony" and Reid were the exceptions. —And so was any Natsuki Subaru who wasn't "Natsuki Subaru."

So, in this very moment—

"—This is count one."

Piling up the "deaths" necessary for his counterattack, he would see "Natsuki Subaru" through to completion.
At the very last, at Subaru's declaration, Reid's blue eyes flashed with a dull gleam.
