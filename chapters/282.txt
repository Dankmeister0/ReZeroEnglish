Arc 4 Chapter 113: "The Stone of Quein Can't Be Lifted Alone"

His eyes wouldn't open all the way; the blood flowed so poorly it felt like his veins were clogged.
And yet the blood dripping from his nose and his wounds showed no sign of slowing — whether he had enough blood left or not, he couldn't say. Maybe losing a little was just what a head running this hot needed.

Shaking a head that lagged a beat behind the world after taking so many hits, that was the thought that crossed Subaru's mind.

It felt like this stretch of time had been spent almost entirely standing there to be hit.
He didn't mean to just stand and take it — he threw punches back, kicked back — but none of his attacks landed. Every time he was struck down, he spat blood, choked down the pain, and stood back up wearing some unreadable grin, as if to say *your hits aren't working on me*. Over and over.

There was no way they weren't working.
Inside and out, his body was already in shambles — he could almost feel his internal organs, every one of them ruptured and churned together. The bones holding him up felt shattered and ground to powder here and there, an illusion so vivid that it was nothing short of a miracle his four limbs still obeyed his will at all.

But none of it — not one part of it — was truly the product of a miracle.
Every time his consciousness threatened to slip away, threatened to fall, a scathing rebuke seized the scruff of his fading awareness and hauled it back.
A sharp, searing pain from inside his coat — a policy far too spartan, like his brain was being scorched.

Even though he'd asked for this himself, the lack of mercy in the method made his cheek twist.
Loss of consciousness could be staved off with a little trick. Even so, Garfiel had any number of ways he could have stopped Subaru outright.
And yet Garfiel had inflicted no damage severe enough to render Subaru — reckless enough to keep standing back up — completely unable to act. That, too, was entirely Garfiel's own choice.

If Subaru took a full, unguarded blow from Garfiel at his best, he wouldn't stand a chance. One hit, and his body would turn into a mess of pulped flesh in an instant.
Of course, part of the reason it hadn't come to that was the fact that Otto and Ram's desperate fighting had already inflicted serious damage on Garfiel. But even wounded, even having forced himself here in that state, Garfiel still had fangs that could kill Subaru in one bite, claws that could tear him open in one swipe.

That it hadn't gone that way was Garfiel's own judgment — that no matter his own condition, he couldn't bring himself to swing a killing blow at another.

In the end, Garfiel was simply too kind. That was the truth of it.

Ryuzu and the others, Frederica, even Ram — all of them described Garfiel that way.
A heart so delicate it was hard to imagine from his usual short temper and rough behavior. For all his violent talk, the strength he'd been raised to wield had always been turned inward, toward protecting.

That was something unforgivable to him — something that might destroy the Sanctuary.
Even faced with an enemy like that, he couldn't bring himself to take the step of ending a life.

"――――"

Subaru was well aware he was exploiting that temperament of Garfiel's, that kindness.
From the very start, using Garfiel's own nature against him was the whole premise of facing him. If his opponent was a Garfiel who couldn't bring himself past his own kindness, Subaru had a certain conviction that he wouldn't be killed.

Still, taking this much punishment from a Garfiel already battered half to death — if he'd faced Garfiel at full strength, this would never have been enough to leave him standing at all. For that, he owed thanks to Otto and Ram, who had fought on beyond anything Subaru had planned for.

*Neither of them's dead… are they?*

Given Garfiel's nature — unable to bring himself to the decision to kill Subaru — it was hard to imagine he'd killed the other two. If it had come to that, Garfiel wasn't a man who could hide it in his manner.
More than that, if he'd killed Ram, there would be no reason for him to come confront Subaru in human form. It would only be natural for him to have given up restraint entirely and be baring his beast form.

When Subaru had said *you couldn't have killed them*, Garfiel hadn't denied it.
Then that was the whole of how the fight between Otto and Ram and Garfiel had ended.

"――ォォォォォォォ"

Before Subaru's eyes, the body of Garfiel holding his own frame began to change.
His arms, his legs, swelled not just one size but two sizes larger and thicker, his torso thickening and expanding into something massive. Claws and fangs sharpened into blades, his face transforming with an audible crack from a man's into that of a great feline beast.
Golden fur swept over his bared skin, and four limbs planted themselves against the ground to brace his weight.

What stood there now was a great golden tiger, its narrowed pupils reflecting Subaru.

He had to kill Subaru to stop him.
After swinging his fists again and again, Garfiel had finally arrived at that conclusion.
And to kill the Subaru he had to kill, Garfiel made his decision.

He woke the blood sleeping within him, the instinct of the beast, and became a great tiger to take Subaru's life.
In the form of a beast stripped of reason, so that he wouldn't have to watch the moment of his own decision.

"But that's where you're wrong, Garfiel."

Being unable to kill your opponent, unable to swing your fist through to the end — that's kindness.
Deciding to protect the place called the Sanctuary, to protect the hearts of yourself and the people around you — that's kindness too.

But fleeing into a place where you don't have to watch your own actions, just so you can kill someone you can't bring yourself to kill — that has nothing to do with kindness. That's Garfiel's weakness.

And Natsuki Subaru didn't hesitate to exploit that weak point.

"C'mon, do me a favor, body. Don't you dare give out on me over something like this!"

Garfiel, transformed into his beast shape, reflected his blazing hostility in Subaru's eyes.
The flex of his four limbs, the sign that the great beast was about to spring forward to tear Subaru apart.
So the moment for a decision could only be here, and Subaru, after clenching his back teeth once, focused his awareness on the very center of his body — the gate connected to his tanden — and raised his voice.

"――Shamak!!"

"――――"

The instant the great tiger opened its jaws, the world answered a cry poured out with everything he had.
Black smoke burst forth between Subaru and the beast, swallowing the towering bulk of the animal whole. Just before being engulfed, an arm reached out as if to sweep the smoke aside — but it touched nothing, and vanished beyond the smoke.

When swallowed by the spreading abyssal dark, the forced-open incomprehension dragged life toward its end.

"Ngh, ah……!"

The instant he confirmed it had landed, an impact like being struck across the head slammed into Subaru's skull.
A sharp pain as if awls were being driven in from both inside and outside his head at once made his vision flash red and scatter with light. Unlike the dull pain of Garfiel's blows, this was an agony so fierce it felt like it was shaving away at Subaru's very soul — and somehow, he swallowed it down and forced himself to chew through it.

Overexerting the Gate, the very thing he'd been told not to do.
Betraying the words of the royal capital's finest healer, who had pressed on him more than once that he might never be able to use magic again, Subaru invoked it once more.
The unseen, scorching sensation of the Gate. The gate at the center of his body, its root, shook violently, and somewhere far from Subaru's own flesh, something tore loose.

Roughly, crudely, a pain like being ripped away.
Accompanied by an irrecoverable sense of loss, it brought understanding to Subaru's heart.

"Thanks."

The line he'd relied on, leaned on so many times before now, was severed.
It didn't matter. A choice that shouldn't have existed to begin with had simply, truly, run out. Even so, the fact that he'd made it this far was thanks to that power, and for that he was grateful.

So — this was goodbye.

"――――"

He looked ahead.
The last spell, wrung out of him with everything he had, hadn't managed to wrap Garfiel's massive frame completely. It covered the most vital part, his head, at the center, but the body it couldn't fully enclose left the right half exposed.

Even with all his strength poured out, that was as far as he could go.
He exhaled through his nose. Partway through, the clot of blood clogging it fell loose. He wiped it away roughly with his sleeve and forced a foot forward, legs threatening to give out.

A hand reached into his coat. He took hold of a hard shape in his grip, and felt a quiet relief that it hadn't broken in all the chaos up to now. If this had been lost, there'd be no chance of victory, none at all.

"――――"

Ahead of him, the black smoke was gradually beginning to thin.
How many seconds had passed since he'd cast the spell? Ten? Five? Maybe even less. He truly hadn't had a shred of talent for magic. But right now, he wanted to be grateful for that.

The tiger's right half was visible. A body caught in a world of incomprehension, unable to move.
The spell being imperfect now robbed Subaru of any need to hesitate over where to aim.

So the foot stepping forward knew exactly where it was headed.
Right foot, left foot. Too slow to call running. But a plodding sprint, driven by momentum alone.
And then, closing the distance to the massive body until they were nearly touching—

"Come down to my ring. — Garfiel."

Into that thick right shoulder, Subaru drove the blue crystal he'd pulled from his coat — thrust it in.

Light spilled out.

The blue crystal, buried in the great tiger's right shoulder, blazed with tremendous light there.

Unpolished, unshaped, its brilliance carrying the look of something *chipped off*, it drove the sharp point of its cut edge into the beast's flesh, and from there drank deeply of what could be called life force itself.

"――――gh!"

Buffeted by the light, Subaru fell backward as if hit by wind pressure. Landing on his backside, backing away, before him the beast still swallowed in black smoke remained unaware of the change happening to its own body.
The crystal stone, small enough to fit in a palm, greedily drank Mana from the creature it was buried in. Just carrying it in his coat had left Subaru with an unbearable sense of enervation.
What would happen if that effect were brought directly to bear on a body — the answer was right in front of him.

"――Ah, aaah!?"

The smoke cleared.
The disruption of vision from the black smoke, and the forced incomprehension from the magic power.
Freed from both at once, the great tiger — Garfiel — had most of his body's beastification stripped away, reverting back toward the form of the young man he had been.

Fur fell away in fluttering waves, and limbs that had looked like bundles of logs shrank back to an ordinary thickness. Fangs and claws shortened, and the skeleton throughout his body reclaimed a human shape with a series of cracking sounds.

The one watching this transformation with the most disbelieving expression of all was Garfiel himself.

He stared, stunned, at his own body having lost its beast form and returned to its original flesh. He raised both hands, his trembling golden eyes fixed on palms that were unmistakably human.

"Th-that can't… what the hell is… happening…"

"I told you, Garfiel. — I don't pick fights I can't win."

At Subaru's words, Garfiel's head snapped up as if struck.
Brushing the ground off his backside, Subaru rose to his feet, his swollen face twisted into a wicked grin. Seeing it, Garfiel grasped the change in his own body, and the fact that a tremendous burden was still being placed on it. Turning his head, he noticed the foreign object embedded in his own right shoulder.

"This… what, is it? No, wait, this is…"

"You recognize that light, don't you. Both you and I ought to know exactly what it is."

"Where the hell did you get this from… you bastard…!"

"Obviously. — Ryuzu Meyer's crystal. One of the crystal stones stockpiled to maintain its function, or whatever."

—The research facility deep in the forest.
Ryuzu Meyer, the cornerstone of the barrier protecting the Sanctuary. The crystal that sealed her within eternal crystal depended on a copy of herself for the maintenance of its function.
Periodically, a Ryuzu clone would swap out the power source at the base of the glowing crystal. Naturally, the crystal stones for that purpose weren't infinite either. Left within the Sanctuary, eventually they'd run dry and the function would fail to be sustained.

"They should've been carried in regularly, along with the food and everything. If that was one of Roswaal's supply lines, there was plenty of chance to swipe just one."

"But that shouldn't… just stabbing me with something like that shouldn't drain my strength like this… what kind of trick did you rig up…"

"Who knows. ...Maybe there's some monster with a monstrous appetite stuffed inside that crystal stone."

Garfiel, out of breath, his words coming in broken fragments.
He reached for his own right shoulder, trying somehow to yank the crystal stone free, but the blue crystal, as if rejecting his very fingertips, was buried into his flesh and wouldn't come loose.

Letting out a deep breath, Subaru let his whole body go slack and turned his head back over his shoulder.
Looking up, there Emilia stood silently watching the two of them fight from above. Right up until a moment ago, Subaru must have looked utterly hopeless in her eyes.
And yet she hadn't tried to stop Subaru's fight. Once, when he'd kept fighting out of some warped stubbornness that wasn't right for either her or himself, she'd tried so hard to stop him.

Subaru understood that what was there now wasn't something he could simply call trust.
Well — whatever it was, it was surely still something that neither of them could put into words or shape, not yet.

The crystal stone that had pinned down Garfiel's movements.
Seeing its glow, had Emilia noticed something? He didn't know. But it was fine to not know. Right now, in this moment.

"Look at me, Garfiel."

"Ahh…?"

"If you want to stop me, stop me with your own hands. Don't go leaving it to some blood inside your body that turns you into something that doesn't even know what's going on anymore. You're the one making a fool of people here."

He stepped forward.
His whole body creaked, blood dripping unceasingly from somewhere.
Life was draining out of him. But he had no intention of stopping, and no intention of being stopped.

"You, trying to stop us — I'm going to stop you. — Emilia's challenging the Tomb. The Sanctuary's going to be freed. We don't have time to stand around."

"Don't go spoutin' whatever the hell you please! Who asked you! Who gave you permission!? This place can stay just like this, unchanged, forever!"

"There's no way things stay unchanged, stuck in place, like this forever. Something like that… someone should've noticed before it stayed this way for hundreds of years."

"Wantin' things to not change! There's people who want that, and keep wantin' it, ain't there!"

"If you keep protecting things so that nothing ever changes like that, and you're able to reign forever as the guardian of this place, sure, fine, that's great. ...But there are things that no matter how hard you push, alone, you'll never reach."

Time, and the age, would eventually leave Garfiel behind.
The strength to keep protecting a place called the Sanctuary that never changed would eventually be lost.

"Just like you're being cornered right now with all of us ganging up on you — there's always going to come a time when one person alone can't manage it. Even now, at this very moment."

Subaru's walking figure arrived right in front of Garfiel.
Gripping the crystal stone in his shoulder, out of breath, Garfiel still glared at Subaru with eyes that hadn't lost their strength. Subaru glared right back, meeting it head-on.

Both of them already knew that words alone weren't enough between them.
Then—

"Get knocked flat, Garfiel. Learn what strength in numbers feels like."

"There's gotta be, a better way, to say that!"

Garfiel roared.
A fist came up from below. But it was slow. With his vitality being drained root and all by the crystal stone, Garfiel had almost nothing left.
With plenty of room to spare, Subaru tilted his head aside — and took a fist to the cheek from a direction he hadn't expected. His vision swam violently.

"You're the one who oughta hurry up and go to sleep! After that I'll crush that Tomb, and you and every last one of the others, I'll keep you penned up in there till you die together!"

"You— you were planning something like that…!?"

In return, Subaru straightened knees that were about to buckle and drove a punch straight upward. It was rammed with full force into Garfiel's face, which he couldn't dodge. It was the first clean hit from Subaru's side since this fight began.

A clumsy punch, thrown from a wobbly stance with a wavering core, his arm not even fully extended.
Naturally, there was no hoping for the kind of power he'd wanted — but for Garfiel as he was now, it was more than enough.

"—Gh, gah."

On top of all the damage already dealt across his whole body, the Mana supporting his flesh was being wrung out at the root. For Garfiel, already standing at the edge of being unable to fight, Subaru's one hit alone could be the finishing blow that pushed him over that edge.

But—

"That don't work on me!"

"Guh!"

Stomping both feet hard into the ground, Garfiel dropped his hips and drove an elbow into Subaru's solar plexus. Crying out in pain, Subaru lowered his head and drove a headbutt into Garfiel's forehead. Both of their skulls rang with the impact, both reeling back at once, and both swinging their fists as their heads came back forward — a mutual strike.

Fists sunk into each other's cheeks, blood spilling from both their noses.
Subaru had reached the limit of physical damage; Garfiel had reached the critical threshold, mental strain included.

On Garfiel's right shoulder, the glow of the crystal stone was gradually, gradually growing quieter.
Proof that Garfiel's own Mana was nearing exhaustion — in other words, proof that the end was close at hand.

"—Guhah!"

"Whaddya lookin' away for!"

The moment his focus slipped, a fist thrown with an open-handed motion sent his face flying.
The blow, riding on that lapse of concentration, blasted his consciousness somewhere else for an instant. Reflexively he clenched his teeth, and the pain of cracking one already-fractured tooth jolted him back awake.

The little trick to keep him from losing consciousness could no longer lend him its aid. In a very different form, it had already contributed more than could ever be enough. From here on, he had no choice but to endure every last bit of the pain by himself.

Carelessness, complacency — ridiculous.
Subaru had always been the weaker one, the challenger; there was never a moment he could stand comfortably in a position of advantage.

"So there's… no way I'm holding back!"

"Gaoh!"

His swung-down left arm caught around Garfiel's neck, and unable to support their combined weight, the two of them crashed to the ground together. Every part of his body slammed down, and Subaru grimaced in pain as he tried to push himself back up. A blazing, piercing agony tore through the left arm he'd planted on the ground.

Looking down, he found Garfiel's fangs sunk into his upper left arm.

"Gh, aah!"

"—Grrrr."

"Ugh! Let go! It hurts, damn it, you idiot!"

He punched the biting face to force the fangs loose. With a wet, tearing sound, his left arm came free, but the arm — pierced through to the bone — wouldn't move. And then his right arm too—

"Got… you!"

Garfiel's palm seized the shoulder of the right arm Subaru had extended to try to reclaim his left. Even with the strength to swing an arm, to throw a kick, gone, his grip strength hadn't left him.
A grip that could gouge into rock crushed Subaru's right shoulder, bone and all.

A dull, grinding crack rang out, and Subaru let out a silent scream.
His left arm torn open at the upper arm, his right shoulder with its bones shattered and rendered useless — with both arms robbed of function, Garfiel kicked down the wide-eyed Subaru.

"That's it! There's nothin' more you can do now! Shoulda just made you into a jumpin' weakling from the start, that's all!"

Seeing Subaru writhing on the ground, Garfiel's face twisted, this time truly, into the pleasure of victory. Rising on unsteady feet, he looked up at the sky and let his throat tremble.
A beast's roar, a victory cry. And then, as he moved to deliver the finishing blow to Subaru, who had nothing left to do but wait to be trampled—

"...How many times do I gotta tell you not to decide when I'm finished!"

A headbutt rising up from below caught Garfiel square in the muzzle, crushing it, and his eyes spun.
Staggering backward, before him stood Subaru, both arms hanging limp. Impossible. Ridiculous. This wasn't some matter of willpower or anything on that level.

"Neither my ending, nor yours… isn't here yet."

"Don't… don't screw around… don't get up. Don't you dare get up… you bastard…"

Garfiel's face twisted in anguish, and he stepped back once from the still-standing Subaru.
As if he were afraid — afraid of Subaru, who couldn't even use his arms anymore and had nothing left but the strength to stand.

"What's the point of pushin' yourself that hard!? Everyone here, every single one of 'em! Ain't nothin' but the dregs, with nowhere left to go! Rejected by the outside, with nowhere else to be! What happens if you push 'em out there!? What's it gonna do!?"

"Go out there and become something. If it's between that and staying here to end slowly, do that instead. Either way, there's no path left where you stay here."

There was a future no amount of strength Garfiel displayed, no amount of desperate fighting, could change.
By his strength alone, he couldn't stop the ferocity of the Great Rabbit that saw the Sanctuary as its feeding ground. Even giving everything he had, things would slip past him, and each time the number grew, his strength would fail further, until eventually even he would be defeated before an appetite that never ended.

Maybe if he told him that future, it could move Garfiel from this place.

But that wouldn't change what he believed.
It would only move his body, temporarily. His heart would keep shutting itself inside this Sanctuary. And once he'd weathered it, he'd come crawling back here, play at paradise again, and sink back into stagnation.
Ignoring every hand pushing his back, every hand reaching out to him, all of it, Garfiel Tinzel would keep pretending to mourn his mother's death, endlessly consoling his own heart.

"Get out there, Garfiel. There's no wall like the one you're so scared of. Not anywhere."

"There is a wall! It's me! I'm the absolute wall that cuts inside from outside! Me, and Granny, and everyone else! We stopped! And that's it, that's the end!"

Once, they had given up. Afraid of connecting to the outside, the people of the Sanctuary had fled into their own paradise and given up on ever touching the world beyond the forest.
So Garfiel tried to protect that closed-off paradise. He declared he'd protect it.
That was the same as sealing off their own existence, completely. Alone, on his own terms.

"Then we'll smash that wall… right here, right now, all of us!"

"That brother of yours! Ram too, they're out cold! You're gettin' put to sleep next! That 'us' you're talkin' about don't exist anywhere anymore! Me, and all of you — it's over!"

"You think giving up is smart or something? Not giving up is obviously the cooler choice! If you give up once and stop, is that it, you never walk again!? Rest a bit and start walking again! The wind for that must've blown your way a long damn time ago!"

The Trial that had left him terrified of the outside world.
Even after learning of that Trial, Ryuzu and Frederica had still loved Garfiel.
Frederica had chosen the path of stepping out into the outside world, so that one day, when the Sanctuary's barrier was lifted, there would be a place for the people living inside it to go.
Frederica must have looked back and reached her hand out to Garfiel.
Before a Garfiel who had stopped walking, Frederica must have reached out a hand urging him to walk.
Of course she had. Because Frederica was Garfiel's older sister.

When a little brother is crying and standing still, reaching out a hand is what a big sister does.

"You said Frederica went out into the world and left you behind. But that's wrong. You're mistaken, Garfiel. You were never bound by the barrier. If you'd wanted to chase after her, you could've started running any time. The reason you didn't — that's on you!"

"...I—"

"You're the one who let go of her hand first, Garfiel! And you keep blaming your sister for it, whining and whining about it! Doesn't that make you pathetic!?"

Something burned deep in his chest. He himself no longer understood what he was even saying.
Right in the center of his stomach, something black was stirring.

The supernatural gate deep in his tanden, the one that had connected Subaru to the outside world, had lost its function.
So what was this, then — this thing that had begun asserting its presence in the depths of his body right now?

Without understanding anything — his own mind, his own body, or the man in front of him — he shouted.

"Any time! No matter when! If you want to do it! If you think you want to change! That moment right there is the starting line!"

Broken, having lost everything, steeped in resignation, stopped in place, hugging your knees and curling up.
Even convinced you're worthless, dyed through to the core with disappointment in yourself, with others' disappointment in you, with the loneliness of being abandoned by the people who matter to you—

"Who has the right to tell someone to give up on lifting their face again and walking the road ahead!?"

Give up. Quit. Curl up and stay down.
Ridiculous. All of it — nonsense not worth a single ear.

If there's someone hugging their knees, and you're going to bother speaking up to them at all, then cheer them on, damn it.
Do your best. Just go for it. Whatever's going on, if you stand up and run, you'll end up somewhere.

—Something burned, deep in his chest.

"Isn't that right, Garfiel...!"

He called out the name of the man in front of him, eyes wavering weakly, looking small.

—Something in his stomach was on fire.

"Isn't that right, Emilia...!"

He called out the name of the woman watching the two of them from behind, caught somewhere between weakness and something else.

—Something welled up from deep within his eyes.

"Hey — isn't that right, Rem!!"

He lifted his face, opened his mouth, opened his eyes wide, and called the name of the person who had given him the reason to stand back up.
Someone who had once told him — when he'd given up and stopped walking — that it couldn't possibly end there.

Because Natsuki Subaru wished that the strength he'd been given in that moment ought to reach everyone.

"――――"

A power that wasn't Subaru's own stirred in the depths of his body and let out its first cry.
As if in celebration of being born, as if in welcome of coming into the world.

Through the medium of the being called Natsuki Subaru, it connected once more to the world, to existence.

Heat spilled out.
The heat that had been surging up as if to pierce through the center of Subaru's body caught fire.

It swirled bloodshot, before the eyes of a Subaru weeping tears of blood, taking shape, reaching out to interfere with the world.

"I—!!"

Shouting, Garfiel's body launched forward.
Claws raised, fangs bared, Garfiel could no longer deny Subaru's claim with words — only with the entirety of his actions.

Unable to string words together, unable to give shape to his feelings, that was the only method he could think of.
He knew no other way. And so Garfiel extended his claws toward Subaru.

He didn't notice the heat gathering before his eyes, spilling out from the bleeding Subaru.
He didn't notice, right in front of himself as he prepared to leap at Subaru, a warped ripple in space, a crack in the world that shouldn't exist.

—He didn't notice the presence of an overwhelming power extending from it.

Of course not. He couldn't see it. No — no one but Subaru could see it.
Because it was something only Subaru could interfere with — the *Unseen Hand*.

"――――"

The world seemed to move in slow motion.
This sensation was one Subaru knew all too well. The moment on the brink of death, the instant before a fatal wound, the scene of punishment for uttering the forbidden — every one of them a painful memory, dispiriting to recall.

But apart from all that, this awakening of consciousness had come now, for Subaru's sake.

He could see Garfiel leaping toward him clearly.
Hostility bared to the full — and yet it looked, somehow, like nothing more than the tail end of a child's tantrum.

Subaru fixed his gaze on the tip of that jaw.
Somehow, he understood. Before he'd even acted, he'd already grasped it.

All he had to do was take aim, and release whatever it was he'd drawn taut.
That alone would be enough. It would, surely, be completed.

—So Subaru did exactly that.

"――!?"

The unleashed power let out a triumphant roar, blasting the defenseless Garfiel's body from directly below.
The surging torrent of power took the shape of a fist, of an arm, and punched clean up through the face of the leaping Garfiel, sending his body flying high into the air.

"—Ah, haah!"

Struck by a blow utterly beyond anything he'd anticipated, Garfiel was slammed into the ground without even being able to break his fall.
Sprawled spread-eagle on the ground, Subaru understood — this time, for certain, he'd landed the decisive blow.

At the same moment, something tremendous, something dragged out wholesale from within his own body, was taken from him as well.

"Ugh, guh… oh."

He fell to his knees, doubled over, and retched with everything he had — but not even a single drop of bloody vomit came up anymore. Not saliva, not a drop of blood; there was nothing wasteful left anywhere in his body.
That was how much he'd exhausted himself, right down to the very last, with that final blow.

The torrent of power dissolved and vanished the instant it struck Garfiel down.
Its source likely still remained somewhere within Subaru, but he had no sense he could pull it out again. At the very least, there was nothing more than that he could offer right now.
To use that hand any more than this, he would need to offer up even more than he already had.

But with this fight over, there was no need for that now—

"Hey, hey... you've gotta be kidding."

"—Ah, don't… underestimate me."

His body was on the verge of collapsing, of consciousness blowing out entirely.
The edges of his vision were whitening, exhaustion so total he couldn't tell which blink would be the last one before reality gave out.

Having exhausted himself to this degree, and still, even so—
Garfiel Tinzel, streaming a torrent of blood from his nose, was still standing.

"You... how are you this damn tough..."

"I don't... break, so it ain't... over, ain't, ain't over yet..."

Garfiel, too, was half unconscious.
His unfocused eyes looked at Subaru without really seeing him. Only sheer stubbornness kept his body upright, refusing the final push.

Almost certainly, all it would take was one more tap from Subaru, and Garfiel would collapse.
But Subaru no longer had even that much strength left to throw. Far from delivering one more blow, in another ten-odd seconds his own consciousness would go out too.

Both of them had given everything, every ounce of strength they had, in this decisive battle.
Garfiel, of course, but Subaru too, without a doubt, had used up every last trick he had.

If Otto and Ram hadn't worn Garfiel down, he never would have made it even this far.
Thinking the hidden card of the blue crystal stone would be enough somehow — that had been naive. Assisting his awakening of consciousness, and Garfiel's ultimate weakening — even piling those together, it probably still wouldn't have been enough.

On Garfiel's right shoulder, the blue crystal blinked.
It seemed almost to be both encouraging and scolding Subaru as he sank toward defeat, and he nearly let out a bitter laugh at that.

Subaru, the crystal stone, Otto, Ram.
Even with that much strength bundled together, a Garfiel they still couldn't finish off was, without question, a strong man.
Let him acknowledge that, truly, from the bottom of his heart. So—

"You're… done for, now…!"

"Don't take it… personally, Garfiel. I told you we'd smash that wall — all of us."

"There's no one left…"

On unsteady feet, Garfiel closed in on Subaru.
A left arm, raised waveringly, tipped with a blood-stained, blunted claw at its end.
If it landed, Subaru was finished.

Every nerve Garfiel had was poured into that one strike.
So he didn't notice — the rumbling, the tremor of the earth, drawing closer.

—The final push that would drive Garfiel into defeat.

"That's the end o—!?"

"――――!!"

A thin, high whinny of an Earth Dragon overrode the shout.
With a rumble that shook the ground, a jet-black Earth Dragon charged in head-first and sent the defenseless, standing Garfiel flying sideways with tremendous force.

"—Guh!?"

Literally struck with an impact that took his entire body with it, his eyes rolled back, and Garfiel was sent flying as lightly as a pebble kicked aside.
Bouncing off the ground twice, three times, kicking up a cloud of dust, the half-beast man's body collapsed, caked in mud, prone on the earth.

This time, his body didn't so much as twitch.

Having confirmed it, the one responsible for that merciless final blow raised her head and let out a triumphant cry.

"How's that, Garfiel..."

Beside Patrasche, crying out her victory loud and proud, Subaru spoke to the Garfiel sprawled on the ground — in a voice so hoarse he wasn't sure it would even reach him.
What had decided this fight — what had it really been?

It was simple.
A strong Garfiel had fought alone, and a weak Subaru hadn't fought alone.
In other words—

"This — this is the power of numbers."

"There's gotta be… a better way… to say that…!"

Unable to move, Garfiel responded to Subaru's words with something like resentment.
At that voice, Subaru let his cheek relax into a faint smile.

"Then, it's a victory of bonds — everyone's feelings bundled together."

"Heh… ahh… guess that's what they mean by 'the stone of Quein can't be lifted alone,' huh…"

With those last words, Garfiel finally fell silent.
Having confirmed it, and this time truly grasping certain victory, Subaru looked up at the sky, and—

"Finally got a fitting saying out of him..."

He murmured, satisfied, before letting his consciousness slip away and toppling over sideways.
