Arc 5 Chapter 44: "Let's Talk Frankly"

Otto's bombshell declaration, hand raised, swallowed everyone in the room in astonishment.
The very existence of the *Book of Wisdom*, whose reality had been doubted, was now confessed to by none other than one of their own. The shock was only natural — and among them, Subaru's shock was the greatest of all.

"Wh-why the hell would you have the *Book of Wisdom*?"

"First, let me clarify so there's no misunderstanding. It's true — the thing I brought into the city that should be called the 'Book of Wisdom' was indeed me, no mistake there. But that doesn't mean I'm its owner. The Witch Cult's demand caught me completely off guard too."

"'The thing that should be called'—that's a phrasing that catches in my ear. What d'ya mean by that?"

Otto had answered the shaken Subaru, and Anastasia caught the edge of his words to press further. In response, Otto looked around at everyone and said,

"Do you all know about the *Book of Wisdom*? To put it simply, it's said to be the original text behind the Gospels that the Witch Cultists possess — those dubious prophecy books that supposedly record the future of their bearer. This one is supposedly its source, with a reputation for being far more accurate."

"The original text behind the Gospel, huh. This might be an odd comparison, but it feels somewhat close to the prophecies of the Dragon History Stone. Though the credibility and the standing of what each one deals with differ greatly."

"I can't say much, since I've never personally witnessed either the Dragon History Stone or a Gospel record the future in the moment it happens... And the effects of the *Book of Wisdom* are unconfirmed too. By the time I obtained it, most of the book had already burned — it was practically just a wreck."

"Burned... a wreck..."

At Otto's words, the fates of two *Books of Wisdom* overlapped in Subaru's mind.
One had belonged to Beatrice, lost to flame along with the burning Forbidden Archive. The other had belonged to Roswaal, burned by Ram, and lost in the Sanctuary.
It was hard to say how far Echidna's claims, as the book's creator, could be trusted, but if her words were to be believed, there were two *Books of Wisdom* — both of which should have burned and been lost.
What Otto had obtained, then, must be the charred remnants of one of those two.

"I see, I see. Now I finally understand why Otto-kun brought it to Priestella. You relied on the restoration artisan, Dartz, didn't you?"

"...That's right."

Otto nodded with few words to Anastasia, who seemed to have reached the conclusion ahead of everyone else. Watching the exchange, Julius and Reinhard too showed signs of having understood, but for Subaru, only an unfamiliar word had come up, leaving the details a mystery.

"Don't go understanding each other without me. What's this 'restoration artisan' thing?"

"Exactly what it sounds like — a specialist in Light Magic focused on restoring objects. And in particular, this Dartz here in the city is famous in that line of work. Even a book more than half burned away could be expected to recover a fair amount, given enough time."

"I contacted this Mr. Dartz and entrusted him with the remains of the *Book of Wisdom*. So right now, the book should be kept at Mr. Dartz's workshop."

With Otto's testimony, the whereabouts of the *Book of Wisdom* were finally revealed.

"Even sooo, when the heeeck did Brother Otto meet a guy like that?"

"Yesterday, after the negotiations at the Muse Company fell through. Once we split up from everyone and I was acting alone, I paid Mr. Dartz a visit. I'd already spoken with him privately, so he took it on quite eagerly..."

The result being, when the *Book of Wisdom*'s name came up in today's chaos, Otto had understandably been unable to sit still.
With that explanation, both the reason the supposedly-destroyed *Book of Wisdom* still existed and the reason it had been brought into the city made sense. What didn't make sense, though, was the true intent behind Otto's actions. Why had he thought to restore the *Book of Wisdom* at all?

Honestly, Subaru held no good impression of the *Book of Wisdom*.
Not only was its creator Echidna, it was a black book with ties to the very Gospels the Witch Cult possessed. It was the reason Beatrice had been bound to the Forbidden Archive for hundreds of years, and it was also the catalyst for Roswaal's reckless scheme involving the Sanctuary.
Hearing that it had burned away, Subaru's honest reaction had been relief.

"I'll leave out the details of how I got it and my purpose in restoring it. I only wanted to make clear that the *Book of Wisdom* exists, and where it currently is. Anything beyond that is a matter for my own camp."

"The Witch Cult's group has raised the *Book of Wisdom* as at least one of its objectives. On this point, where do you think responsibility lies?"

"I don't think anyone but the Witch Cult should be held responsible for the Witch Cult's actions. If you're going to press that line, I'll have to turn a rather unkind rebuttal back on you."

Under Julius's pressing questions, Otto's resolute bearing did not waver. Seeing Otto's gaze shift toward Anastasia, Julius shook his head.

"Forgive me, that was a pointless thing to say. Of course I have no intention of holding you two responsible for that. The crimes they've committed should be atoned for by punishing them."

"Agreed."

Nodding at Julius's firm words, Otto glanced briefly at Subaru. At that searching look, Subaru found himself momentarily unable to say anything.
Otto's true intentions were still unclear. It never even crossed Subaru's mind to doubt him, but what his goal actually was remained a mystery. To that Subaru, Otto mouthed, without a sound,

*"We'll talk later."*

That was the message conveyed.
He'd explain his true intentions later — that much was clear. Then that matter could wait for now.

"The *Book of Wisdom*'s existence is now clear. That being the case, I hesitate to dismiss the matter of the remaining artificial spirit as mere idle talk as well."

Once things had settled, Reinhard cut into a new topic.
It was a natural progression, but given that Otto had just confessed a truth that could put him at a disadvantage, there was little point in Subaru continuing to keep his own secret hidden either.

"Anastasia-san."

"I know, I know. Really, what a troublesome business this is."

At Subaru's prompting for agreement, Anastasia removed the scarf wrapped around her neck. She spread it out on the table, and everyone tilted their heads at her air of resignation.
But with her next action, those tilted heads straightened right up.

"—Enough playing possum, Echidna. You can talk now."

"In my case, isn't 'playing fox' more accurate than 'playing possum,' Ana?"

"—!"

At Anastasia's call, the white fox scarf stretched its limbs with a will of its own. The astonishment spread even to Julius and Ricardo.
It seemed the artificial spirit Echidna had kept her existence hidden even from her own camp's allies.

"Miss, even I didn't know about this. What the heck is this thing?"

"Sorry for hiding it, Ricardo. Same goes for you, Julius. — This girl is the artificial spirit everyone's been talking about. Her name's Echidna, my longtime partner-in-crime."

"Hello there, Ricardo. It's a bit ticklish, greeting you as though we're meeting for the first time when I already know you so one-sidedly well. Feel free to be as familiar and casual with me as usual."

Ricardo looked at her as if she were something unsettling, while Echidna was oddly friendly in return. Ricardo seemed put off by the white fox's attitude, but the one with the more shocked expression by far was Julius.
At the mysterious existence his partner had been hiding, he showed, rare for him, eyes that couldn't hide their unease, and said,

"...Does this mean, Anastasia-sama, that you are a Spirit Arte user as well?"

"Nn-nn, nope. There's no Spirit Arte contract between me and Echidna. Seems I've got no talent for that sort of thing at all. And Echidna's not like a normal spirit either — she's got no combat ability."

"That's right, I'm powerless. Probably the weakest of all spirits, if I had to guess. Weak enough that even your perception as a Sword Knight wouldn't catch me."

"I... see. ...No, if that's the case—"

Both Anastasia and Echidna denied his suspicion. Still, Julius couldn't back down there, and turned his gaze toward Subaru, who had been left out of the loop.
His yellow eyes, somewhat tense, fixed on Subaru.

"What was the meaning of Subaru appearing to already know about this? Why would he know something even I, the First Knight, did not?"

"That's not— it's—"

"That's because my partner, Beatrice, is the same kind of artificial spirit. When the Witch Cult's demand came up, Anastasia-san told me about it in confidence. ...I only found out myself a little while ago, no different from you."

"She's an artificial spirit...? Anastasia-sama, is that true?"

Cutting off Anastasia, Subaru's quick explanation drew a suspicious look from Julius. When Anastasia nodded in confirmation, Julius accepted it with a "I see," closed his eyes as if thinking for a moment, and then let out a deep breath.

"Forgive me for acting so suspicious. And if I've caused Anastasia-sama any displeasure, I apologize. I'm deeply ashamed of myself."

"It's not something you should be blamed for — Ucchi's the one who kept quiet. If anything, I'm the one who should ask your forgiveness, okay?"

Julius bowed his head to Subaru and apologized to Anastasia. As Anastasia in turn apologized back to Julius out of the corner of his eye, Ricardo snatched up Echidna from the table.

"But still, Miss, that's a mean trick to pull! Keeping it hidden even from me, after all our loo-ong years together — that stings a bit! Is that all our bond amounted to?"

"I'd appreciate it if you didn't handle me so roughly. I do take care of my fur, you know. It wouldn't do to spoil Ana's charm, after all."

"Smooth-talkin' thing, ain'tcha. Ah, whatever. I'll let it slide for now."

Apparently satisfied after pulling and rolling her around, Ricardo released the white fox. Landing on the table, the white fox scurried right back to Anastasia's hand and wrapped around her neck, taking on its disguise once more.
Having stayed there for so many years, it was impressive how the sense of it being a living thing vanished in an instant.

"So, that's the story — artificial spirits really do exist too. ...That said, same as what Otto-kun said just now. Just like with the *Book of Wisdom*, we've got no intention of handing this girl over either."

"Sorry for keeping quiet. But I feel the same. Bea-ko's my precious partner. I've got no intention of letting those crazy bastards get their hands on her either."

Both Anastasia's and Subaru's stance of refusal toward the demand was absolute.
Hearing this, Reinhard nodded and said,

"I understand. It's only natural. Not a single one of their demands can be accepted. Though — perhaps the wedding with the bride is something we could leave be."

"No, that's an absolute no too! And you know why? Because that damn bastard's talking about marrying, and the one he's talking about marrying is Emilia."

"Wha—!? Emilia-sama has been abducted!? I thought it strange I hadn't seen her — don't tell me she's just been evacuated somewhere!? Couldn't you have told me sooner!?"

Reinhard's eyes widened, and Otto's head spun at the shocking fact. At their reactions, Subaru gritted his teeth, added an "I'm sorry," and continued,

"I was useless, and she was taken right in front of me. But as long as they're talking about a wedding, Emilia-tan shouldn't come to any harm. So I'll beat him down, and kill him dead, and knock him flying, and get her back. Absolutely, without fail."

"—Yes, let's do that. Given that, it should never be forgiven."

As the image of Regulus rose in his mind and Subaru's anger boiled over, Reinhard's fighting spirit sharpened as if in agreement with that anger.
A presence terrifyingly reliable. As ever, his existence was reassuring.

And, having thought that far, Subaru looked toward the corner of the room — toward the man who, despite being present the whole time, hadn't joined the conversation at all.
A figure crouched with his back against the wall, expression hidden beneath his helmet.

"Hey, Al. You join in the conversation too. You haven't said a word since the speech ended. Because of the guy you two brought along, my lethal weapon got sealed off. If you don't make up for that somehow, that's gonna count against you, y'know."

Walking over, Subaru called out to Al, who remained slumped over. Sighing at Al's sluggish response, Subaru turned his attention to the presence of Reinhard's father — Heinkel.

Heinkel, who had taken Felt hostage and used her to bind Reinhard's movements.
It was an unmistakable act of aiding the enemy, and drawing a blade against a candidate for the throne went beyond mere disrespect — it bordered on treason against the state itself. Under normal circumstances, such an outrage would be unlikely to escape a death sentence, but what judgment would actually be handed down for it?
At the very least, nothing of that could be read from Reinhard's profile.

"—Sorry, but I'm gonna have to bow out here."

"Ah?"

Perhaps because his attention had been on Reinhard, Subaru's reaction to Al standing up came a beat too late. Al stretched his back and tried to walk past the bewildered Subaru. Subaru hurriedly grabbed his shoulder and spun him around.

"W-wait a second! What d'you mean, bow out? Now's exactly the time we need every bit of fighting power we can get — no way I'm letting you go, are you an idiot?"

"Call me an idiot or whatever, but thinking you can count on me for fighting power — now that's the real idiocy. You'd be better off grabbing someone battle-hardened from a shelter somewhere than relying on me or this cat's paw here. So, I'll just be on my way, yeah?"

"No way in hell! Don't go sulking on me like that. What's gotten into you all of a sudden? You're not a kid — if you've got something to say, spit it out."

"—That's the one thing I don't wanna hear from you, brother."

Shaking off Subaru's restraining arm, eyes behind the helmet pierced through Subaru.
Caught up in that unseen glare and a tone of voice unlike his usual, a chill ran up Subaru's spine.

It wasn't hostility, and it wasn't murderous intent — but it was a fierce emotion nonetheless.
Subaru had felt that unfathomable emotion somewhere before. But he couldn't pin down exactly what it was, couldn't connect it to any concrete shape.
Unable to figure it out, the standoff continued, and then—

"I've had a flash of inspiration. Listen to this. — Your gaze sets my heart aflutter."

"Shut up!!"

"Eep!?"

At the carefree voice that suddenly cut in, Subaru reflexively snapped back. Hearing that, the one who'd spoken leapt back and, dramatically taking a spare desk down with her, toppled over.
Rolling around amid a racket and shrieks was a dark-skinned girl—

"You're— Liliana!?"

"Ohh-woah! My elbow! My knee! The pain of every bone in my body shattering into pieces! All six of my ribs have snapped, no doubt about it!"

Writhing energetically on the floor before Subaru's very eyes was the "Songstress," Liliana.
At her unchanged self, Subaru forgot to point out that she had more than six ribs to begin with, and let out a sigh of relief, hand on his chest.

"I was worried since I didn't know what happened after we split up, but I'm glad you seem okay. What a relief."

"Okay!? What are you saying, you blind fool, looking at me on the verge of death right now! Watching a beautiful girl writhe in agony and feeling relieved — is that some kind of morbid fetish!? I've had a flash of inspiration. Listen to this. — Fingers! Ears! Next, the eyes!"

"You're plenty lively, aren't you."

Sitting cross-legged on the floor, strumming her Lyulyre, Liliana was the picture of health. It was so perfectly business-as-usual that it was almost worrying in the other direction, but her safe reunion was still a welcome thing.

"But why come to the city hall? Wandering around outside right now is way too dangerous—"

"Naturally, it is because I am here, is it not, you common fool."

Before Subaru could even press for the reason behind Liliana's safety, the answer itself appeared, insufferably arrogant.
Footsteps clicked sharply as a woman in resplendent red stepped into the conference room. Clad in red from head to toe, she swept her blood-red eyes across the room, and said,

"It seems the cast is all assembled. I'll say this much — it was good sense of you common folk to sit and wait for me, the leading lady. See that you keep that same sense going forward."

Holding an unfolded fan to her lips, the woman laughed, thoroughly pleased with herself — Priscilla.
At her sudden appearance, everyone's jaws, Subaru's included, hung open, unable to close. But the fastest to reboot was, of all people, her own attendant, Al.

"P-Princess! You're okay! I was worried since I couldn't find you anywhere."

"Hmph, Al. You dare frolic about with common folk instead of attending me? Seeing my figure, hearing my voice, smelling my scent, and obeying my command — that is the duty of you and Schult. As for Schult, having me go looking for him myself is disrespect beyond measure."

"M-my humblest apologies, Priscilla-sama..."

Priscilla addressed her worried attendant coldly. From behind her, clutching her dress, a boy attendant timidly peeked his face out. It seemed Priscilla had not only rescued Liliana but her own attendant as well, and had strode brazenly through this Witch-Cult-infested city.

"What the hell kind of guts is that..."

Subaru let out a sigh at recklessness that went beyond mere guts. Hearing this, Priscilla shot Subaru a glare. Snapping her fan shut with a click, she strode straight up to him.

"You there. Do not move."

"—!"

With a whip of wind, the tip of the fan came to rest against Subaru's throat.
As unnervingly fast as ever — a speed his eyes couldn't follow. Still, since Reinhard hadn't moved, Subaru judged there was no ill intent.

"What's up. We're in the middle of some important talk right now, I don't have time to deal with—"

"Enough. — That clumsy broadcast earlier, that was indeed your voice, was it not."

"...So what if it was?"

With Reinhard's stillness as his only basis — a rather pathetic thing to rely on — Subaru still put up a defiant front against the overbearing Priscilla. At his answer, she narrowed her eyes.

"Obviously. I permit no one to draw more attention than me. Therefore, I shall prove the self-evident truth that I stand above the deeds of a common fool such as yourself."

"Ah— ow!?"

Flicking up the fan pressed to his throat, she caught his chin, bringing tears to Subaru's eyes. With that alone, Priscilla stepped away from him and sat herself down, imperious, in one of the seats at the round conference table.

"A cheap chair. It shows the caliber of what's been used here."

Evaluating the comfort of her seat with venomous appraisal, Priscilla swept her gaze over the faces gathered around the round table. Then, splitting her painted red lips sideways, she bore a beautiful, sinister smile.

"Now then, I permit you to tell me the current state of affairs. Serve as my hands and feet, and fulfill your duties to the fullest. As reward, I shall lend you my hand. Be grateful."

"Wait a sec, Princess! If you've reunited with us, there's no need to stick around here, right? Better to hightail it out of a dangerous place like this quick as—"

"Are you telling me to flee, Al? If so, that is a grave misjudgment indeed."

Al chided Priscilla, who had settled squarely into her seat with every intention of joining the meeting. But Priscilla glared right back at him, and the iron helmet flinched.

"Listen well. It was I who decided to stay in this city. And it is I who shall decide when to leave it. I take orders from no one else, absolutely not. And you'd have me turn my back on a bunch of madmen and slink away like a coward? Who do you take me for?"

"............"

"This entire world is arranged for my convenience. That being so, how could I possibly leave behind a reason that so offends me? If you call yourself my servant, know your place, Al. If it is heaven's will that I am who I am, then my every act is itself heaven's will."

Priscilla's resolve did not waver.
Surely everyone present understood that — Al himself, more than anyone. Al let his one-armed shoulder droop helplessly, and the boy attendant — Schult — quietly moved to his side. At the boy's comforting gesture, a bitter smile seemed to cross Al's face. It seemed he too had made up his mind.

"Otto, got a sec?"

"Yes, sure."

At the round table, the explanation of the situation to Priscilla proceeded.
Slipping through that gap, Subaru brought the matter up with Otto. Otto, as if he'd anticipated it, went along without any sign of surprise.

"Garfiel, call us back once the talk's moved along."

Leaving that word, Subaru stepped out of the conference room together with Otto.
Seeing no need to leave the floor entirely, he faced Otto right there in the hallway. Otto's eyes, looking straight back at him, showed no hesitation. What they were about to discuss was already obvious to them both.

"Why'd you think to restore the *Book of Wisdom* in the first place? Actually, when did you even pick up those remains to begin with?"

"A year ago, after things were settled in the Sanctuary. Once the heavy snow Emilia-sama had brought down melted away from the forest, and I was going around looking through the settlement — I found them by chance... no, it wasn't chance, really. I'd heard about it from Ram-san, and I went actively looking, thinking there might be some charred remains left."

"And you found it, which means it was Roswaal's *Book of Wisdom* that survived."

"Yes. That was also the one I wanted to confirm, so for once, I got lucky."

"For once, I got lucky" was probably self-deprecating, referencing his usual bad luck. Otto smiled wryly, but Subaru couldn't bring himself to laugh along.
The knot that remained lodged inside him was still the same — why had he done such a thing?

"Natsuki-san, honestly, what do you think of Margrave Mathers?"

"Roswaal, you mean?"

At the question Otto tossed his way, Subaru — who had been about to fall silent — pondered it a moment, unsure whether it connected to the current topic or not.

"Let's see. I think he's a guy you can't let your guard down around. There's the whole thing from a year ago, too. But his goal itself is clear now, so as long as it doesn't change, I don't think he's a threat. Right now it's more like — we're accomplices, both fully aware of what the other's after."

"I don't trust Margrave Mathers at all."

Flatly, Otto dismissed Subaru's view as though calling it naive.
At the sheer sharpness of those words, Subaru's eyes widened.

"You said there was the matter from a year ago. Yes, that's right. There's the matter of the Sanctuary, from a year ago. And even before that, it seems that man was scheming various things. Natsuki-san and Emilia-sama seem to be letting that slide rather leniently, though."

"...It's not that I'm letting it slide. What he did was nothing but 'are you kidding me' stuff, and I'm still pissed about it. But the fact is, we need his strength. So there's no point being at each other's throats over it, and Emilia feels the same way."

"That's exactly what I'm calling naive. ...I'm not saying that's a bad thing, though."

Otto glared at Subaru with the eyes of someone watching something frustrating. Somehow, Subaru managed to understand that irritated feeling of his.
In other words, Otto was saying there wasn't enough wariness toward what had been done. Of course, Subaru understood that such awareness was necessary. He understood, but—

"It's fine. I think it's fine for Natsuki-san and Emilia-sama to stay that way. The two of you don't need to change right now. I intend to be the one keeping watch, in your place."

"In our place, meaning?"

"Being in a role like an administrator, I have plenty of occasions to interact with Margrave Mathers. Over this past year, I've watched him, and there's been no particular sign of scheming or guilty conduct. But I don't know about the time before this past year. He might have set something in motion with a time delay."

"————"

Subaru fell silent. Otto's vigilance, Otto's concern — it came through clearly.
The distrust Otto held toward Roswaal was justified. It was only natural that one's actions come back around. Whether for good or ill. No — especially if for ill.

"If that man was following what's written in the *Book of Wisdom*, following its record of the future, then looking at the book should surely reveal what he's been setting in motion. It might even let us prepare for whatever critical juncture forces a decision upon us further down the road."

"So then, trying to restore the book was... because you don't trust Roswaal."

"...The opposite. Even I don't want to suspect one of our own. That's exactly why I wanted some peace of mind. That no harm would come to Natsuki-san or Emilia-sama. At the very least, I wanted to confirm there wasn't some certain misfortune waiting to happen. That's why I secured the *Book of Wisdom* and tried to restore it. ...I'm sorry for acting on my own like this."

Voicing his apology, Otto bowed his head.
But Subaru could say nothing back to him. It felt as though he had no right to say anything at all.

The anxiety Otto carried, and the means he'd taken to resolve it.
Both of those were things Subaru and Emilia should have noticed themselves. If anything, that effort had been made for Subaru and Emilia's sake.

It struck him that he was being helped by Otto in ways beyond what met the eye. That he hadn't noticed it himself felt pathetic, and apologetic, and strange all at once.
Why did Otto go this far for them? Was it because they were friends?

"I won't tell you the reason for that. It's a boring one anyway."

An answer that seemed to have read roughly what was in Subaru's mind, just from his expression.
Beaten to the punch by Otto easing the corner of his mouth, Subaru let out a deep breath.

"Man, I really am just getting carried by everyone around me, you included, huh."

"Who knows. I think that being okay with that was pretty much the point of Natsuki-san's broadcast earlier. And personally, I think that's fine too."

As Subaru scratched at his own head, Otto offered him an easygoing word. At that considerate voice, Subaru clicked his tongue in a sulky sort of way and let his shoulders drop.

"Got the story. I'm satisfied about the *Book of Wisdom*. Still, the fact that those guys want it is a problem. What do we do about the actual book?"

"Regardless of whether the restoration succeeds or not, I think it's better to retrieve it. There's a high chance Mr. Dartz will end up a victim otherwise, and that's not something I want either."

"The plan's shaping up to be a simultaneous assault on all four control towers. We can't spare fighting strength for that."

"I'm a non-combatant, but if I take the waterways, I'll be more than enough. Believe it or not, I'm the best of the best when it comes to charming animals, water dragons included."

The gesture of touching his own mouth was probably a show of pride in his Divine Protection of the Soul of Language.
Indeed, if the main focus was running and evading, Otto's Divine Protection was considerable. And the enemy's main force was concentrated at the control towers. With no Witch Cultist hands-and-feet accompanying him, Otto's odds weren't bad at all.

"More than worrying about me, the assault team matters more. Natsuki-san has to get Emilia-sama back, after all. That's a heavy responsibility."

"I know. I'm not handing 'Greed's' head over to anyone."

The white-haired madman flickered across his mind. The abominable Demon who had taken Emilia away.
Being an Archbishop of Sin of the Witch Cult besides, he was an enemy that absolutely had to be struck down.

"Shall we head back? Explanations should be catching up about now."

Seeing Subaru steel himself once more, Otto turned his head toward the conference room. Nodding to him, Subaru moved to head back into the room together, when—

"—Subaru-dono."

A low voice called out from the direction of the stairs, stopping him in his tracks.
There was no mistaking whose voice it was. Looking down from the top of the stairs, narrowed blue eyes met his — Wilhelm.

"Otto, head back ahead of me."

"Understood. I'll get things moving in the meantime."

Seeing Wilhelm, Otto went back into the conference room first. Subaru set foot on the stairs and made his way up to where Wilhelm waited on the upper floor.
Once they stood at the same height, Wilhelm gave a slight bow of the head.

"My apologies for being unable to join the discussion. I've caused nothing but trouble."

"The circumstances are what they are. No one's going to speak ill of you and the others, Wilhelm-san. ...So... how's Crusch-san?"

He'd heard the situation wasn't good. No — beyond not good. Bad. And that, as a matter concerning her as a woman, there was also a reluctance to let others see.
Recalling the wretched state of his own right leg, Subaru imagined what kind of harm Crusch, under the same conditions, might have suffered. Just imagining it was enough to make him want to take back the thought entirely.
At Subaru's question, Wilhelm quietly lowered his eyes.

"That very Crusch-sama is asking for you, Subaru-dono. Would you be willing to trouble yourself to come?"

"Crusch-san wants me? I mean, of course, but... is that really okay?"

"It is her express request. Though Ferris is none too pleased about it."

"...I bet."

Ferris, of all people, was surely the one most wanting to hurl resentment at Subaru.
On the top floor of the city hall, it had been Subaru and Crusch, the two of them, who had faced Capella. It was only Subaru, after all, who could have protected her.

"Ferris may say something rude, but please don't take it to heart. And if you could, please forgive him for it. Even he understands in his heart. He simply has feelings he cannot help, and doesn't know what to do with them."

"I get it, wanting to curse everyone around you when someone precious to you is suffering. I don't want to think that lashing out is all there is to the person doing the cursing."

If venting frustration eased his heart even a little, who was Subaru to blame him for it.
He was prepared, even willing, to take some harsh words on the chin.

"This way, please."

Saying nothing more to Subaru's answer, Wilhelm led the way toward Crusch. Footsteps clicked in a steady rhythm down the hallway.
And partway there—

"Subaru-dono, there is one more report I have for you."

"What is it? If it's not about Crusch-san, then..."

"It concerns the two swordsmen who accompanied the Archbishop of Sin."

Subaru's breath caught, unbidden.
Despite it being something he should have anticipated, it was pathetic that it had slipped his mind entirely. The wound from the Divine Protection of the Death God, granted through Mimi, that would not close.
The identity of that formidable swordsman, deduced from that alone.

"One is Kurgan of the 'Eight Arms.' A man who long held the rank of general in the Vollachian Empire, a wielder of tremendously powerful swordsmanship. A man who should have died over ten years ago."

"Should have died. Um, Wilhelm-san—"

"And the other."

Cutting off Subaru as he tried to press further, Wilhelm declared it outright.
His feet stopped. Subaru halted too, reflexively. With his back still turned, Wilhelm fell silent for a moment. Subaru stepped forward, leaning in to glimpse his face from the side — and regretted it.
He should not have looked.

"—The other is the previous Sword Saint, Theresia van Astrea. My wife, who should have died fifteen years ago, defeated by the White Whale during the Great Conquest."

"————"

His voice, Subaru thought, had stayed composed. One could call it a testament to formidable mental fortitude.
But all of that faded the moment he saw the profile twisted in anguish. Biting his lip, eyes screwed tightly shut with anger and grief tangled together, the wrinkled face crumpling further still, wracked by a maddening surge of emotion — his true feelings were unmistakable.

"Your wife and the Empire's general. Does that mean they were both alive after all...?"

"If that were so... no, that cannot be. Both my wife and Kurgan should be among the dead. That will not be undone. There is something out there defiling the dead as the dead."

"Defiling the dead as the dead... something like necromancy?"

Necromancy and other death-related sorceries, so-called magic that manipulates corpses, was something familiar from the realm of fiction. Of course, if one were to limit it to fiction, magic that resurrects the dead wouldn't even be uncommon. But no such convenient magic actually existed.
That much, Subaru had learned the hard way over this past year and some months.
If so, then the possibility wasn't the latter, but the former.

"A Curse Art, forbidden, that manipulates corpses. There once existed a being who actually wielded it. During the Demi-Human War — the civil war between the human race and the demi-human races that took place in Lugunica decades ago — that being sided with the demi-humans, and is one of three named as enemies of the Kingdom."

"Enemies of the Kingdom...?"

"The demi-human hero, Libre Fermi. The great strategist, Valga Cromwell. And—"

Pausing once, Wilhelm said,

"The Witch, Sphinx. A being of the utmost evil, who mercilessly spilled vast quantities of blood from both humans and demi-humans alike without so much as a change of expression. The only Witch besides Satella to leave her name in the Kingdom's history."
