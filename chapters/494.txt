Arc 6 Chapter 87: "A Distant Gaze"

Her head was being shaken, violently, with tremendous force.

She realized someone had her slung sideways over their hip, running for all they were worth.
This wasn't the first time she'd been carried like this.
It wasn't that she was used to being handled like an object, exactly, but being hauled around this way by her inconsiderate partner was nothing new.

"Would you cut it out already, Elsa..."

No matter how many times she warned him, he never listened — and she was always at a loss for how to describe him. Not "partner" or "comrade," not enough trust for that. Not close enough to call "sister" or "mother" either. Not a "friend," but not distant enough to be a mere "acquaintance."
So she was always stuck. What should she even call him?

—And what did Elsa even think of herself as, anyway?

"I said don't shake me..."

Saying it wouldn't fix anything, but she'd feel stupid giving up without complaining even knowing it wouldn't reach him — so she grumbled anyway. No, tried to.
Before she could, something hot welled up from the back of her throat, painting over her words. A gurgling sound, and for a moment she thought she'd brought up something she'd eaten. She hadn't.
It was blood — deep, red blood, welling up from somewhere far inside her body.

"Damn it! The bleeding won't stop! Bea-ko! What do I do!?"

"Just make her cough it all out, I suppose! Choking would be worse!"

A man and woman shouting at each other by her ear, and then her body was tilted sideways. She coughed, and still the blood kept flowing from her throat and lips.
Something was pressed to her mouth, and the unstoppable blood was sucked out. Her throat cleared just enough, and air flooded into lungs and brain that had been on the verge of suffocating.

"—Ptoo! Alright, breathing's back! Get the healing magic going!"

"I know that much, I suppose! But I can't only focus on that, you know!"

"I know that too! ...It was my mistake sending Meili in too deep. I'll pay that debt myself."

Voices arguing over her head about something, but now that she could breathe, her body was growing heavier.
No — probably her body had been heavy the whole time. It was just that as the most critical part changed, her awareness spread to the rest of it, and the full picture of the damage came into view.

Her arms and legs wouldn't move. Her head spun. She'd probably coughed up about three cups' worth of blood. Her whole body felt fiercely hot, but her back especially so.
Something felt hugely, terribly wrong across her whole back.

That was probably why her body wouldn't move right, and why she was coughing up blood.
She couldn't remember well what had just happened, who the arguing man and woman were, or what exactly had gone wrong.
But in the dim, hazy world where everything was blurred, there was one thing that snagged at her.
It was—

"A... promise..."

Just the feeling that she'd made one, with someone—


△▼△▼△▼△


—The instant the giant scorpion's body color changed, the light that burst forth was the largest yet.

The swelling white light scattered in every direction, and the burning gama-ō that had targeted its crimson-dyed carapace were blasted to splinters; the shockwave spread further, splitting the sea of sand.
Naturally, Subaru and Beatrice weren't spared the damage either, but worse off than either of them was Meili.

Having leaned forward to command the witchbeasts, she took a considerably fierce hit from the giant scorpion — the "Crimson Scorpion" — in its counterattack.
The unfortunate-in-misfortune stroke of luck was that she avoided a direct hit from the stinger, the true source of the light. If the stinger had so much as grazed her, the girl's small body would have evaporated in an instant.
That much, at least, was avoided. But the deflected stinger shattered the sea of sand, and she took the full force of the resulting shockwave. That alone was more than enough to leave her critically, mortally wounded.

"Meili!!"

By the time Subaru and Beatrice rushed to where she'd collapsed in the sand and pulled her up, she was in a terrible state. She must have curled her body reflexively, because most of the shockwave's impact had landed on her back.
Her black cloak was blown away, and beneath the torn clothing, her bare skin showed shredded and raw. It looked like severe lacerations and burns, and for a moment everything went dark in front of Subaru's eyes.
But—

"Am I an idiot? What am I even here for!"

He hauled his fading consciousness back with a mental fist to the face and called out to the power within himself.
Using "Cor Leonis" — the power, always active, of taking on his companions' burdens — he took on the damage close to Meili's fatal injury.
Of course, if he took it all on wholesale and collapsed himself, that would defeat the purpose. He needed to strike a balance that kept Meili alive without knocking himself out too. But—

"It's fine. I can do this. —You can pull this off, can't you, Natsuki Subaru."

Once upon a time, he might have panicked here, fallen apart, made an unsightly mess of himself.
But having walked the path that "Natsuki Subaru" had walked, Subaru had come to look at himself anew. His role, of course — but also the things only he could do.
So—

"—Emilia-tan and Julius are fine. Echidna and Patrasche, and Rem too — saved."

He probed the reactions of his companions inside the tower and sought the optimal solution from each of their conditions.
Emilia's cut-off response worried him, and Julius's fight against Reid weighed on his mind too. Echidna and Patrasche's positions were also a concern — but trust held him back from dwelling on it.
Instead, Subaru's awareness concentrated on Ram, and on Meili right in front of him.

"—Ngh."

At once, the agony Meili was suffering flowed into him, and Subaru groaned at the pain of his own organs being scorched.
Frankly, just shouldering Ram's burden had already worn him down considerably. On top of that, taking on Meili's mortal, critical injury was practically suicide.

"This is bad...!"

Despite the tough talk, taking both to their absolute limits was impossible.
Given that, he had no choice but to prioritize Meili, whose life was directly on the line, and lower the degree of burden flowing from Ram to himself.
Sharp as she was, Ram would probably figure out what had happened just from that alone.

"I'm definitely getting yelled at for this later..."

*Ha! Talking so big, and look at you now. In the end, you're just Barusu.*
He could picture the response with unpleasant clarity, and Subaru swallowed the bitter taste along with the taste of blood.
"Cor Leonis" was only supposed to shoulder the burden, in theory — but actually tasting blood was proof that some kind of feedback was occurring in Subaru's own flesh.

The influence belief could exert on the body was unusually large.
He'd heard once that a person convinced they'd been branded with hot iron could actually develop a burn on their body.
In other words, by taking on the damage Meili had sustained to her back and internal organs, the same reaction was being born in Subaru's own body.
If he wasn't careful, it could end up producing two corpses with the same cause of death.

"That would be a bit of a problem, so—!"

He spat out the blood pooling in his mouth, hefted Meili's body, and stood. Then he backed away from a swing of the gama-ō's flame spear, dodging it, and used Beatrice's purple arrows to keep it in check as he put distance between them.

The gama-ō attacking now was one of the very ones that had been carrying them just moments before.
Not that the profit-minded witchbeast had suddenly betrayed them the instant things went south — no, it was simply that Meili's Divine Protection had run out, and it had reverted to its default, standoffish behavior.
Without Meili's command, the witchbeasts were humanity's natural enemies who saw Subaru and the others as foes.
And such witchbeasts now existed in this sea of sand without number.

"Gehk."

"Meili!"

While keeping distance from the witchbeasts, Meili vomited blood in Subaru's running arms. Seeing her cough and spit up blood like that, his anxiety only mounted.
There wasn't even time to stop and treat her properly. At this rate—

"Damn it! The bleeding won't stop! Bea-ko! What do I do!?"

"Just make her cough it all out, I suppose! Choking would be worse!"

Making up for the difference in their stride with sheer effort, Beatrice used what little mana she had to keep the surrounding witchbeasts at bay. Meanwhile, Subaru shook Meili's body, trying to make her cough up the blood in her throat.
And yet, Meili's color didn't improve, and the bleeding wouldn't stop. Left with no choice, Subaru pressed a makeshift straw made from his fist to the girl's mouth and sucked the unstoppable blood out through it.

"Ehk! Gehk..."

"—Ptoo! Alright, breathing's back! Get the healing magic going!"

"I know that much, I suppose! But I can't only focus on that, you know!"

"I know that too! ...It was my mistake sending Meili in too deep. I'll pay that debt myself."

Strictly speaking, Subaru had already begun paying for that mistake.
But the interest was piling up at a terrifying rate, and even while cutting down how much he took from Ram, Subaru was already gasping for breath himself.
How much longer could he buy time like this—

"No — this is where I dig in. If I don't do it here, I'm not much of a—"

"Furya! I suppose!"
"Whoa!?"

Gritting his teeth, tasting the effects of his Authority through his whole body as he ran carrying Meili — and then, abruptly, Beatrice leapt onto his head.
Unexpectedly finding himself giving her a piggyback of sorts, Subaru startled. Of course, Beatrice was as light as cotton candy, so it wasn't any hindrance to his flight — but—

"Bea—"

"Subaru, stop trying to carry everything alone, I suppose. Betty and Subaru are partners, and Meili is one of our companions too, I suppose. Subaru isn't the only one who promised to help, you know."

"—————"

Wrapping her arms firmly around Subaru's whole head, Beatrice appealed to him in a quiet voice.
Through Subaru, who fell silent hearing this, she began treating Meili's wounds with her gentle healing magic. —A gradual, warm light healing Meili; Subaru could feel that sensation through her too.
And along with that warmth, he could feel just how much Beatrice cared for everyone.

"—————ッッッ"
"—————"

Behind them, the infighting among the witchbeasts continued.
Fortunately, it seemed that even with Meili's Divine Protection gone, no instant peace treaty had been struck between the Crimson Scorpion and the gama-ō. The same held for the other witchbeasts.
The Crimson Scorpion's sweeping pincers and stinger took down half of the witchbeasts closing in on Subaru's group.
Naturally, since the other half were still coming for them, there was no room to dawdle.

"My options are—"

Actually, they weren't nonexistent.
Taking to heart the words Beatrice had offered him, her feelings of caring for Meili, there was in fact a means — if not to overturn their situation outright, then at least to change it.
But whether it would even work, he couldn't know without trying, and the model for that method happened to be the worst possible person for Subaru — so he hesitated to act on it.
And yet—

"Subaru! If your reason for hesitating is for Betty's sake, you don't need it, I suppose! If the reason's anything besides Betty, I'll apologize alongside you later, I suppose!"

"—————"

"I want to suffer together, and rejoice together... don't leave Betty out! That's the condition of Betty and Subaru's Contract, I suppose!"

Seeing something in the profile of the Subaru carrying her, Beatrice shouted, fury in her voice.
Being right behind his head, he couldn't see her face right now. But even furious, she was adorable — his partner, and his pride.
So, drawing courage from that partner's words, Subaru let himself lean on her too.

There was no time to agonize.
And there was no need to agonize, either, since his partner herself had told him not to.
Therefore—

"Love you, Bea-ko."

"Betty loves you more, I suppose."

Exchanging affection with each other, Subaru looked down at the dying Meili in his arms.
And resolving in his heart never to let her life slip away—

"—Cor Leonis, Second Shift."

He gave a new name to the "Lion's Heart" he had named himself, and clearly shifted gears within himself.
By doing so, he expanded the Authority he'd imposed on himself as the "Small King" — the solitary king's way of being, shouldering everyone's feelings alone — and was scolded for it.

Even so, he couldn't become the shameless embodiment of "Greed," pushing his own burden onto someone else.
So what Subaru wished for, at the far end of this Authority, was this: to share his burden with companions who wished to stand alongside him, who wished for him.
In other words—

"Second Shift — burden distribution."

The burden of his companions that Subaru alone had had to bear with his whole body — he would share it with those willing to share it with him.
Here and now, that meant, for the time being—

"—Subaru."

"Yeah."

"...This is unbelievably rough, I suppose!!"

"Yeah, it's unbelievably rough!!"

The second stage of "Cor Leonis" activated, distributing the load Subaru was bearing.
Not half. More like a quarter, but even so Subaru's burden lightened considerably, while Beatrice, who now shared it with him, went pale.
As if to cover for the pain, Beatrice yelled — and Subaru yelled right back at her, just as loud.

It hurt. It was agonizing. Unbearable.
Taking on everyone's burdens all by himself, being a king could go to hell. —And so, the "Small King" who couldn't stand alone was still standing, thanks to everyone who wanted to support him.

"By the way, is there any way I could pass some of this suffering onto you..."

"—————"

"Guess not, huh. Shame."

Sharing the burden, giving Beatrice — perched on his shoulders, still treating Meili — something to do with her hands while her mind worked, Subaru turned his awareness toward the Crimson Scorpion.
Its true identity was Shaula, and he could still feel their existence connected even now, through "Cor Leonis." A faint, but great light there.
But unfortunately, it seemed there was no way to hand off the burden he and Beatrice were carrying to that Crimson Scorpion. —Because she, right now, had no will to receive it.

Only those who wished to support the "Small King" could actually support him.
A remarkably straightforward, inflexible power. And precisely because of that, he could avoid becoming arrogant.
Because he could never forget that he, too, was being supported by someone.

"Bea-ko! Do something different with your head and your body!"

"—Ugh, that's a tall order, I suppose!!"

He asked her body to keep up Meili's treatment, and her mind to think together with him about a way out of the next crisis. Because the Crimson Scorpion's glowing tail was aimed straight at them—

"—E.M.M.!!"

The first trump card, its absolute invincible magic, was unleashed, and the shockwave swallowed all three of them whole.

—What happened beneath the clouds never reached Emilia above them.
But just like her companions fighting their life-or-death battles, Emilia too had passed through a fierce struggle of her own before finally arriving at that place.

"—————"

The topmost level of the Pleiades Watchtower — higher even than the first level, the true summit in every sense.
Having sacrificed a great many Ice Soldiers to reach it, what Emilia found there was, at the base of the central pillar, a black monolith set in place.

The monolith itself looked identical to the one she'd seen during the third level's "Trial."
The difference was that this one wasn't floating, and its surface — which should have been perfectly flat — bore a striking feature.
It was—

"...Someone's handprints?"

Emilia's violet eyes widened; in her view were several handprints pressed into the monolith's surface — six in all, belonging to different men and women.
From the size of the palms and the thickness of the fingers, she could tell they belonged to six entirely different people.
Still, the fact that they'd been deliberately arranged and preserved like this told her the people in question had been close to one another, and were surely connected to this tower somehow—

"...Could it be Shaula's Master, or Reid?"

Thinking of who might be connected to the tower, those were the only names that came to Emilia's mind.
There was also Volcanica, technically, but every handprint here was human, with no trace left behind by the dragon.
So of the six handprints, Emilia could only account for two of the people.

"—? Wait, this is—"

Having thought that far, Emilia noticed something odd about one of the handprints.
It was the one pressed unassumingly at the very edge of the six — beside it was another of roughly the same size, but the two at that end were noticeably smaller than the rest.
Probably because those two alone belonged to women.
And what caught Emilia's attention was one of those two handprints, because—

"This handprint... is it mine?"

Emilia murmured, frowning as she looked down at her own right hand.
It was a strange thing to think, but she couldn't shake the feeling. One of the handprints pressed into that monolith felt like it was hers.

"—————"

Holding her breath, Emilia faced the monolith. Then, to put her doubts to rest, she reached her right hand out toward that handprint—

*"—Thou, who hast reached the tower's peak. Treader of the first level. Omnipotent supplicant."*

"—! It's back!"

Just before her hand touched the monolith, a stern voice fell from above, and Emilia spun around.
And there, wings beating as it descended onto the top level, was Volcanica — the very dragon who, moments ago, had been writhing violently after her Ice Soldiers touched the white scales at its throat.

*"—————"*

With the monolith at her back, Emilia once again faced the "Divine Dragon."
The battle of climbing the pillar had already been brutal enough, but if this was to be a continuation of that, she'd be forced into an even harsher fight. There was some breadth to the space, but the top level was narrower than the first, making it difficult to maneuver.

"Besides, it'd be really, really bad if the monolith got destroyed..."

She had no idea how sturdy the monolith actually was.
When she'd encountered one on the third level too, Julius and Ram and the others had stopped her before she could try striking it, so its durability remained unknown.
But no matter how tough it was built, Emilia couldn't imagine it surviving a full-force blow from the "Divine Dragon" unscathed.

"If you hit it, it'll probably get destroyed. So—!"

Determined not to let that happen, Emilia summoned seven Ice Soldiers around herself once more.
They had been shattered again and again just to reach the top level, but like Emilia, their expressions were still brimming with resolve, and that was reassuring.
As they readied their ice weapons, Emilia took up her twin ice swords, one in each hand.
Then, uniting with her seven Ice Soldiers, she charged at Volcanica.

"Let's go, everyone! Volcanica's weak spot is the white part of its neck!"

Given the violent reaction she'd provoked earlier, that white scale had to be Volcanica's weak point.
She didn't want to actually hurt it, but if even one of them could just touch it, she should be able to buy time to examine the monolith—

"—Eh?"

In the next instant, Emilia let out a sound as her vision spun, flipping upside down.

"—————"

What had happened, she couldn't tell — it was too sudden.
All she felt was something brushing swiftly against the foot she'd planted to step forward. So faint that she only realized she'd been touched after she was already upside-down.
And at the same moment—

"Ah."

Around the world-turned-upside-down Emilia, all seven Ice Soldiers shattered at once.
Every one of them had had only their heads deftly crushed, and helplessly turned to scattering mana dust. Feeling it against her skin, Emilia realized with a chill of horror.

That had been Volcanica's tail.
Its tail had swept Emilia's feet out from under her at blinding speed, and with that same tail, crushed the Ice Soldiers' heads.
Realizing it had all been done with a single, high-speed flick of the tail, her throat froze over.

It was leagues faster than the very first surprise attack she'd just barely managed to block.
If Subaru's whip was a one, Volcanica's tail strike was a thousand, or ten thousand — that great a difference.
Against an attack of that ferocity, even Emilia would surely have lost without mercy.
So it was a question.

"Why... were you gentle with me?"

It had swept Emilia's feet, but crushed the Ice Soldiers' heads.
What had made the difference here? Surely it wasn't retaliating because she had the same face as the one who'd touched its white scale earlier — that seemed unlikely.
Just as she'd thought that far, Emilia remembered she still hadn't gotten out of her upside-down predicament.

"Oh no—"

She was mere moments from being smashed head-first into the floor.
Then again, a soft impact struck Emilia's airborne, inverted feet from the side—

"Wa, whoa..."

Spun into a half-turn from her flipped state, Emilia landed precariously.
And just as she raised her head, relieved at the close call—

*"...What art thou doing?"*

—there was the "Divine Dragon," thrusting its face right up in front of Emilia's, saying that.

"Um...?"

It was, quite literally, right under her nose.
So close that if Emilia leaned her head forward even slightly, her face would brush against that rock-hard, sharply rigid hide and snout.
That the dragon's face was so close was startling enough in itself.
But what confused Emilia even more was—

"You're saying something different from what you kept repeating before!"

*"—————"*

"Did you come back to your senses? If so, can we talk about a lot of things? Like the 'Trial,' and wanting to change the tower's rules — I have so much I want to discuss..."

Seeing hope in Volcanica's manner, Emilia spoke up rapid-fire.
If the "Divine Dragon," who she'd thought had gone senile, had come back, they could talk about the "Trial" properly again. Then there'd be no need to resort to forceful measures.

"Please, listen! I really need to talk to you..."

*"To leap about so recklessly — what wouldst thou do, shouldst thou fall? Should misfortune befall thee, it is I who shall bear the reproach. ...For none among them can raise their head to thee."*

"Volcanica...?"

Worried about the state of everyone else in the tower, Emilia pleaded desperately, and once again Volcanica spoke words that weren't mere repetition.
But the content didn't seem to answer what she'd said at all, and her confusion only deepened.

Still, in the golden eyes with which Volcanica regarded Emilia, there was a gentle color.
A light of emotion, different from the vague, hazy blankness of moments before.
It gazed at her tenderly, gently, as if cherishing her—

*"Whither have Flugel and Reid gone? To part without a word — Shaula shall grieve at that. And Farsale too shall raise a clamor."*

"—————"

Still gazing at Emilia with gentle eyes, Volcanica continued speaking.
With a look as if gazing at something far away, the dragon spoke the names of Flugel and Reid, and Shaula, and one more—

She couldn't be certain who that was without hearing a family name too, but the sound of it struck a chord in Emilia's memory. Farsale — if that was what she thought it was.

"Farsale, as in Farsale Lugunica? The king from four hundred years ago?"

That was a name that had come up again and again in her studies for the Royal Selection.
Farsale Lugunica — the thirty-fifth king of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica, the great figure who governed the kingdom in the era of the "Witch" four hundred years ago.
And none other than the one who forged a Covenant with the "Divine Dragon" Volcanica himself, and laid the first step toward the Dragon Kingdom's long prosperity — the "Last Lion King."

"—————"

Emilia glanced back at the monolith behind her.
Six handprints — if Volcanica's words weren't unrelated, then three of them might belong to Flugel, Reid, and Farsale. The remaining three were unknown, but one — though unconfirmed — seemed to match Emilia's own handprint.
And the rest were probably one man and one woman.

"Then, does the woman's handprint belong to Shaula...?"

That seemed the most likely possibility.
The identity of the last man's handprint remained unknown, but for now, that was probably where things stood. Still, the biggest problem remained the fact that one handprint was hers, and she had no memory of it—

"Don't tell me... I've forgotten something else, on top of everything about my mothers in the forest!?"

Once she started doubting, Emilia knew she had a past where she herself had sealed off her own memories.
Even setting that aside, with the "Gluttony" Sin Archbishop's existence in play, she knew her "memories" and "name" could get thoroughly scrambled.
Maybe Emilia had wandered in here before, pressed her handprint into the stone, and simply, carelessly forgotten all about it.

"...No, no, that can't possibly be it. If only Puck were here, he'd know whether I've ever been here before."

*"—What ails thee? Some trouble?"*

"Oh, um, it's fine. Thanks for worrying. I appreciate it, but..."

In the end, whether the conversation with Volcanica was even coherent felt dubious, and Emilia was troubled.
It was far better than simply being struck by tail or forefoot without question, but the fact remained that she was stuck regarding the "Trial."
Wondering what to do, Emilia pondered—

*"Speak, should aught trouble thee. Whatever grieves thee, I shall remove it — Satella."*

—Called by that name, she caught her breath.

"—————"

This wasn't the first time she'd been called Satella by that name.
Silver hair, violet eyes, and Half-Elf — anyone in this world who saw Emilia bearing those features would think of that same being.
The only difference was what they chose to call it.

Some called her Satella. Some called her the ultimate calamity. Some called her the "Witch of Envy."
So it wasn't strange that someone would call Emilia by that name too.

But it was strange that Volcanica would speak Satella's name with such affection.
Because Volcanica, Reid, and Flugel — who had pushed his own achievement off onto the others — were the very three who had sealed away Satella, the one called the "Witch of Envy."
So why would it speak of her like—

"Why do you speak so kindly to the 'Witch of Envy'?"

It was, in its purity, a somewhat careless question to ask.
Expecting Emilia to realize that in this moment was asking too much of her — but at least, looking at the situation objectively afterward, that single remark became the turning point.

Perhaps Satella, or "Witch," would have done just as well.
Some called her Satella. Some called her the ultimate calamity. Some called her "Witch."
And Emilia called her the "Witch of Envy."
That—

*"—The 'Witch of Envy.'"*

—was what brought about the change in Volcanica's gaze, turning it distant.

The change that arose in Volcanica's golden eyes was dramatic.
The dragon's head, still gazing at Emilia, remained right under her nose — and precisely because of that, catching the degree of that change up close, every hair on Emilia's body stood on end.

She sensed instinctively: this was not a good change.
Raised in the forest, Emilia had the experience of growing up amid the wild. In such circumstances, she'd seen animals, witchbeasts, turn savage in an instant, more than once.
Following that instinct, Emilia flung herself backward as if launched.
It was the right call.

"—!"

In that instant, the air in front of Emilia's face exploded.
That was no exaggeration whatsoever — quite literally, space itself compressed, then swelled, and burst.
The phenomenon, its principle unknown, was a strange thing, as if space itself had been wrung and twisted; anyone positioned there would have been wrung apart regardless of how hard or soft their defenses were.
And it had occurred exactly where Emilia's head had been.

Had she been even a split second slower in ducking, Emilia would have died.
Between the tail strike and this spatial distortion, ever since climbing to the top of the tower, Emilia had faced situations that could have killed her, again and again.
This might be the most life-threatening situation she'd ever experienced.

"But if everything's worked out so far, maybe I'm reeeally lucky."

If luck being on her side was what was keeping Emilia alive, then the fact she was still breathing at all was itself a gift of fortune — that was how Emilia chose to interpret her situation, positively.
If she didn't, she felt she might buckle under the continuing turns of events.
Because—

*"—Satella."*

—Volcanica, spreading its wings once more, was now turning unmistakable hostility toward her.
Seeing that, Emilia held back the urge to stamp her foot on the ground. Just when it had seemed like Volcanica had come back to its senses, it had reverted right back again.
Worse still, unlike the half-asleep state from before, it now seemed to be gone senile with even more vigor and drive than before.

"—Icicle Line."

So Emilia, too, unleashed her own magic power with no need to hold back, with unwavering resolve.
The air cracked with cold, and a white mist slowly began to drift. Even here, at the top level of the watchtower above the clouds, it made no difference — the world froze white all the same.

With a sound, ice weapons slowly formed — pulling a spear from among those planted in the floor, Emilia spun it and pointed it forward.
Once more, she readied herself for the battle against Volcanica.

But if that one blow that had swept her feet was Volcanica's serious idea of play, she had no idea whether she could handle whatever came next.
A strength on an entirely different order from when it had been senile — no, it still seemed to be senile even now.

"But..."

Gazing straight into the dragon's golden eyes, Emilia didn't want to simply dismiss Volcanica's behavior as mere senility.
Because in Volcanica's eyes, there was sorrow — grief, brimming and overflowing.

*"Satella, yes, Satella. We must stop thee, who hast become the 'Witch of Envy.'"*

"...Were you close?"

*"Would that I had not hesitated, that day. Would that I had not hesitated, that day — then none would have—"*

There was no answer to her question.
But Volcanica's trembling voice felt like an answer in itself.

The grieving dragon drew a great breath, and once again came the breath that would scorch the world white.
Before that happened, Emilia had to close the distance and strike that white scale. If she couldn't, then neither she nor, of course, everyone else, would be saved.

"—Subaru, Beatrice, Ram, Rem, Meili, Patrasche-chan, Echidna, Julius, Anastasia-san."

She thought of everyone in this tower, going through such terrible ordeals.
Everyone she had to save. Everyone raising their heads toward the same goal.
And doing so, from somewhere deep in Emilia's chest, an unfamiliar strength came welling up.

*"—'Witch of Envy,' Satella!!"*

"—No, that's not right. I'm the 'Witch of the Frost' of Elior Forest, Emilia."

To the "Divine Dragon," mistaking Emilia for someone she apparently resembled, Emilia answered in a loud, clear voice, letting the welling strength flow through her entire body.
An enemy, a "Divine Dragon" — so what. Emilia had everyone with her.
So—

"—At least remember my name properly!"
