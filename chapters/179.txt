Arc 4 Chapter 13: "Roswaal's Intentions"

The first thing Subaru felt upon waking was a ticklish sensation from someone's fingertips touching his forehead.

"This slenderness, this softness, this hesitant touch... Emilia-tan?"

"—You're right, but is it just me, or was that a really creepy way to guess?"

Blocked by the palm touching him, Subaru's vision was shut off even as he opened his eyes. But from the glimpse of beauty visible through the gaps between the fingers, and from the answer, Subaru's mouth curved into a small smile.

"Nah. I just figured there's no way anyone but Emilia-tan would be feeling me up at a time like this, so it was just a guess. I mean, come on, I can't actually tell who someone is just by the feel of their fingers."

"Oh, really? That's a bit of a relief. ...Can you sit up?"

"Somehow... yeah, I think I'm okay."

Trading waking-up banter back and forth, Subaru pushed himself upright on the bed he'd been laid in. Turning his neck lightly to look around, he found himself inside an unfamiliar building.

The bed he'd been resting on was crudely made, nothing to compare with the bedding at Roswaal Manor he was used to sleeping in. In any case, he cast his mind back to what had happened before he lost consciousness.

"How much of that was some kind of vision, and where did reality start again..."

The mausoleum—stepping inside it, and then immediately feeling himself caught up in a collapse, was Subaru's last memory in the real world. After that, his encounter inside the mausoleum with the odd woman—the "Witch of Greed"—had, if her own words were to be believed, taken place within her dream.

Troubled by memories that refused to come into clear focus, Subaru held a hand to his forehead and looked at Emilia. Seated in a chair beside the bed, she seemed to be quietly waiting for him to gather his thoughts. Subaru began with an "um..." and continued,

"There's a bunch of stuff I want to ask, stuff I want to talk about... but before that, there's something I need to say."

"Okay, what is it?"

Emilia asked, tilting her head cutely. —But her eyes weren't smiling.

Pierced by the clear, penetrating gleam in her violet eyes, Subaru shrank his shoulders slightly.

"I'm sorry for worrying you. I got a little carried away with myself."

He'd been conceited enough to think he could at least clear the path ahead for Emilia.

The result was stumbling at the very first step—which was where things currently stood.

Receiving Subaru's apology, Emilia let out a small breath from her lips.

"Honestly. I was really worried, you know. You went and screamed and fainted the moment we went inside."

"Never mind the screaming—I actually fainted?"

"Your eyes rolled back and everything. You even started convulsing, and I really didn't know what to do. You didn't look injured, and it didn't look like you were under some strange spell, either..."

At Emilia's hedging words, Subaru became freshly aware of his own unsightly display.

I see. It seemed safe to conclude that from the moment of that collapse he'd encountered right after stepping into the mausoleum—that sensation of falling—he had already been invited into the "Witch of Greed"'s dream.

In reality, Subaru had passed out cold right at the entrance, forcing an unnecessary burden onto Emilia—a hopeless situation, it seemed.

I'll go check if there's any danger, for Emilia's sake—that resolve of his was, at this point, nothing but a bad joke.

As Subaru grimaced at his own self-loathing,

"—Oh? Well look at that, finally awake, huh. Must be nice, livin' the good life."

So he said, pushing open a creaking door and stepping in—the blond-haired young man, Garfiel.

He gave Subaru's condition on the bed a quick once-over, then turned his eyes to Emilia.

"Told ya, didn't I? Nothin' wrong with him. See for yourself."

"...I was still bound to worry, wasn't I? When someone collapses that suddenly for no clear reason. You might be used to it, but that's not something you should be used to."

"Ha, don't act so tough. You saw this guy collapse and went into a panic lookin' like you were about to cry. 'Pale as a bruise gone blue'—that was exactly it."

"Wha—!?"

Emilia tried to object to Garfiel's endlessly rough manner, but his reply made her—lips already pursed—flush bright red.

At that remark, Emilia shot up from her chair with a clatter.

"I—I wasn't crying! I was flustered because I was worried, sure, but I wasn't..."

"Ah, yeah yeah. Supposed to be a secret, my bad, my bad. But ain't no big deal, is it? Nothin' worth hidin' anyway."

"That's not the point. If he hears that I was worried and, um... almost crying..."

Her objection trailed off partway through, and Emilia glanced sidelong at Subaru.

Subaru, who had been silently watching their exchange up until then. What was the silent Subaru doing, there at the end of her gaze—?

"Hm? Oh, go on, keep going. Please, please, heh-heh. Is that so, huh, is that so. Emilia-tan was crying while worrying about me. Is that so, heh-heh."

"Somehow, I had a feeling this is exactly how you'd react."

Emilia's shoulders sagged listlessly. Before her, Subaru still had his nostrils flared wide. Being genuinely worried over by the girl he was interested in, he couldn't hide his joy, improper as it was.

Seeing the shamelessly transactional reactions of Subaru and Emilia, even Garfiel muttered "I see, huh," with some deep feeling,

"That one's on me, I guess. Nah, this is rare. Me admittin' I was in the wrong, straight up."

—and with that content, hardly anything to boast about, he expressed his self-reflection.

"If properly trained, even a dog can hold off eating until given permission."

It was a cold voice, imbued with a sharpness that felt like it would cut you if you touched it.

The resonating voice slowly, deliberately broke its words apart, yet between them was filled with an intensity that allowed no rebuttal.

"In other words, following instructions is a bare minimum promise that even a dog can keep."

Click, click—the sharp sound of high heels struck the wooden floor with a hard tone.

The rhythm of the footsteps was steady, pacing back and forth in front of him again and again.

The regular intervals only underscored the calm composure of the person behind those footsteps, and Subaru could tell it was mercilessly grinding down whatever mental composure he had left.

"Now then—"

The voice, the footsteps, and eyes holding no emotion whatsoever pierced through him—through Subaru—

"So then, what should we call a creature that can't keep a promise even a dog could keep? Barusu, do you know?"

"I'm sorry for not listening to your advice——!"

Before the small girl, Subaru dropped to his knees right where he stood and bowed deeply, shouting his apology.

But the one at whom Subaru's whole-hearted cry was directed merely tilted her head slightly,

"Did it sound like I was asking for an apology, with that 'I'm sorry'? Not only did you not hear the question, it seems you didn't even hear the context before it. Though I suppose it's not so surprising, if Ram's advice never made it into your ears in the first place."

"Could you please cut it out with the prickly, roundabout sarcasm!? I'm reflecting on it, I genuinely feel bad, and this is crushing my soul! I'd honestly rather you just chew me out straight!"

"You should have just died."

"That's TOO straight!!"

Receiving the merciless scolding from the girl—Ram—Subaru clutched his head on the floor. But thinking of what he'd actually done, it was abuse he had no choice but to accept meekly.

After all, he had betrayed, head-on, the advice she'd gone out of her way to give him, and on top of that had caused everyone around him a great deal of trouble.

"Ye-e-es, yes. Why don't you forgive him around there, Ram? He's already had the same lecture from Lady Emilia, hasn't he? There's no point repeating it—it'll only please our masochistic Subaru-kun."

"I've got no masochistic streak. Going and stepping on landmines is just my nature!"

Which is another way of saying he simply couldn't read the room.

At Subaru puffing his chest out over something hardly worth bragging about, Ram let out a long, deep sigh as if utterly exasperated to her core, then silently turned her back and went toward Roswaal.

She quietly took her place beside him, still lying in bed, and Roswaal, now facing them, continued with a "Now the-e-en,"

"Fi-i-irst of all, I'm glad you all made it back safely. If you'd stumbled before the 'Trial,' it would have thrown all sorts of plans out of order, wouldn't it? Though what happened to Subaru-kun was purely an accident, of course."

Struck by that suggestive, knowing chuckle, Subaru crossed his arms and gave a small snort. As if scolding him for that attitude, Emilia, standing beside him, pinched his side.

"Ow. That hurts, Emilia-tan."

"Normally I'd want to take your side, but today you're the one in the wrong. ...If I'd known Ram had told you something like that, I would have..."

—wouldn't have let Subaru go ahead of her, she seemed about to add, but her words trailed off there. Directing a wry smile at her, Subaru murmured inwardly, *That's exactly why I couldn't tell you.* If she'd known he'd been warned beforehand, Emilia would have flatly refused to send Subaru into that dangerous role.

Conversely, without Ram's warning, it was doubtful Subaru would have resolved to clear the path ahead of Emilia to that extent. Which meant, in short,

"Your advice didn't make anyone happy, Ram."

"The dog is speaking as though the origin of all this lay with Ram. ...No, my mistake. I should correct that to sub-canine—it would be rude to actual dogs otherwise."

Ram met Subaru's attempt to shift the blame with contempt of the highest possible order. He almost had to admire the maid's undiminished venom. Though he suspected she held a similarly unflattering opinion of him.

Setting aside that exchange between the two of them, unrelated to the matter at hand, Roswaal leisurely recrossed his legs atop the bed.

"By the way, Lady Emilia... how was the mausoleum?"

"...With what happened to Subaru, I wasn't able to look around very much. But I remember the unpleasant smell in the air, and that uncomfortable, stinging feeling against my skin."

Emilia spoke of her impression of the mausoleum, her brow furrowing faintly.

For someone who generally tended to soften her words when giving a negative assessment, that was quite the verdict. Hearing such an unreserved assessment, Roswaal gave a small laugh and said, "Is that so."

Then his mismatched eyes turned toward the corner of the room—toward Garfiel, who stood with his back against the wall, watching the discussion.

"Garfiel. Were you able to confirm the 'Qualification'?"

At the word "Qualification," Subaru raised an eyebrow and looked toward Garfiel.

The blond young man roughly scratched at his short hair, baring his sharp canine teeth.

"I could only get as far as just outside the entrance, but... the mausoleum's lights were lit up proper. No mistakin' it—Lady Emilia's got the qualification to take on the 'Trial.'"

"The mausoleum's lights...?"

Subaru cocked his head at the unfamiliar phrase, and Garfiel waved a hand irritably.

"There's a bunch of things like candles set up all through the mausoleum. Thing's rigged so that when someone with the qualification goes in while the sun's still up, they light up like they've been lit on fire. Anybody who gets that welcome without a hitch has got the right to take on the nighttime 'Trial.'"

"And conversely, if someone without the qualification forces their way in, they end up like Subaru-kun or myse-e-elf."

Taking over from Garfiel and delivering the final word, Roswaal spread both hands wide. It was a gesture indicating his own body, still painfully wrapped from head to toe in blood-stained bandages. —If that was the punishment meted out to those who forced their way into the mausoleum, then...

"There's a pretty huge gap in the penalty between you and me. Unlike me, who just walked in, seems like you must've done something bad."

"Something bad, like wha-a-at, for example?"

"Like maybe you got the urge and took a leak right by the entrance. That'd get the mausoleum caretaker mad, no question."

"If that were tru-u-ue, then the spot where Subaru-kun collapsed would be right where a little of my pee splashed, wouldn't it?"

Getting a joke returned for his joke, Subaru made a face of disgust and gave his whole body a light brushing-off gesture. Loosening his cheeks at Subaru's antics, Roswaal shook his head and said, "But-t-t,"

"The rejection was the same, but the scale of the damage is different... you-u noticed well. In truth, there's a big difference between the injuries Subaru-kun and I received. But the reason is simple."

"...A mana... a Gate runaway."

The answer slipped in quietly, midway through Roswaal's sentence.

Turning his eyes toward the voice, he found it was Emilia, a finger held to her lips, who had spoken. She lowered her eyes thoughtfully, fidgeting with the ends of her silver hair in an unsettled manner.

"When I went into the mausoleum, I felt really awful. I think it was because my Gate was being unilaterally interfered with. I didn't trigger the condition, so it seems I was let off... but for people who don't meet the condition, that interference bares its fangs."

As she put it into words, it seemed to solidify into conviction, and her tone gradually gained strength. Emilia raised her face, taking in Roswaal's pitiful, wounded state with her violet eyes.

"The interference attacks its target through the Gate. ...If that's true, then the more Gates a person has, and the larger they are, the greater the interference they receive."

"Correct answer. In my case... well, it's a miracle I didn't just burst, I suppose."

Tossing off something terrifying with breezy ease, Roswaal closed one eye and shot Subaru a sarcastic "Lucky you, having no talent."

"So basically, the more of a talented mage someone is, the closer to death they end up. Guess it's a good thing I've got such a small Vessel as a mage, seeing as I got off with just passing out."

"T-that's true, but... doesn't that self-assessment hurt?"

"I'm pretty used to being reminded of things I can't do or can't reach. Don't worry, I'll show my love for Emilia-tan through things only I can do. For starters, can I begin by relentlessly, unglamorously whispering sweet nothings at you?"

"I'll think about it once the Royal Selection is over and things have settled down."

"That's three years away at the earliest!?"

And even then, there was no guarantee she'd actually hear him out.

Subaru shrugged at Emilia's coolness. Then he continued, "Still, though,"

"Setting aside the qualification stuff, that place is a straight-up mage-killer, huh. Dunno who rigged it up, but I can't call it anything but nasty."

"Given that its management has been passed down through House Mathers for generations, it follows that whoever wove the spell formula must have been one of my ancestors, I'd say."

"Ah, well, that's a bad— actually, no, it isn't. Come on. Your ancestors fit the image way too well. It's like your family just keeps reincarnating, Roz-cchi."

A theory that when the previous head died, their will took up residence in the next head—some kind of puppet-lineage family.

Subaru shook his head to deny something that was frightening just to imagine. At Subaru's remark, Roswaal laughed as if he'd just heard an especially good joke.

"There was indeed a house that once researched that sort of magic, but it should have been dissolved quite a long time ago now. ...And as for that mausoleum environment you called a mage-killing space, there's a more accurate name for it."

"Which is?"

"It's a simple matter. —That place is filled with the Witch's Miasma. A nightmarish environment that drives its target mad through the Gate, via mana. That's what we call Miasma."

At the appearance of the word "Miasma," Subaru furrowed his brow, searching his memory for where he'd heard the term before. He was fairly sure he'd heard that word before, back—

"It was about the Witch of Envy. If I remember right, the place where the Witch is sealed is also supposed to be full of that Miasma, or something like that..."

"My, you know it well. Well, it is a famous story, after all. Even now, the Evil Sealing Stone Shrine where the Witch of Envy sleeps is filled with Miasma so dense it's said to blot out your very sight. Where the mausoleum's Miasma rejects those who don't meet its conditions, that Miasma is a genuine phenomenon of malice that invades the mind, destroys the body, and violates the Soul of anyone at all, regardless of who they are. Rumor has it even the faithful of the Witch Cult, who champion the Witch's revival, can't so much as approach it."

"So even the Witch Cult can't get in... well, I guess that makes sense. If getting in there could actually undo the seal, then all they'd have to do is sneak in unnoticed and rush the shrine, and they'd win."

He recalled Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti, who had cried out the Witch's revival as his supreme purpose.

Even that madman, who had done nothing but shout his self-absorbed love, hadn't taken the direct path of trying to rescue the Witch. Though considering that his true nature was that of a spirit, it was probably best to conclude that he too would have been powerless before the Miasma.

"A-anyway, that's how it is—the Witch's seal cannot be undone because the Witch's own Miasma keeps everyone away. And beyond that, anyone trying to approach the shrine would have to slip past the eyes of the Sage Shaula, who watches over the ground from her watchtower."

"Feels like I've heard that before, the Sage Shaula. That makes two sages I know of now. Flugel, and this Shaula."

Subaru wondered whether it was really okay for two people to share the same title, but did no one else find that odd? At Subaru's question, Roswaal gave a small laugh.

"Flugel, would that be the Flugel of the great tree, I wonder? Ce-e-ertainly he too is called a Sage, but comparing him to the Sage Shaula might be a bit of a stre-e-etch."

"Why's that? They're both sages, don't go playing favorites. I owe Mr. Flugel a bit of a debt, so if you insult him in front of me, I won't let it slide."

After all, that particular sage had been of tremendous help during the Subjugation of the White Whale.

Mr. Flugel surely never imagined that, four hundred years later, the tree he'd planted would come in handy for exterminating a monster. Whether he'd actually be pleased about it was another matter.

"It was such a huge tree, so I'm sure the broken part could be put to all sorts of good use—actually, wait, is there a chance it got blown up in that time bomb business?"

"It seems there's still a good deal more we-e need to compare notes on. In any case... Lady Emilia."

Roswaal shifted his gaze from Subaru, hand on his chin, to Emilia, calling her name in a low voice. Emilia raised her face at this and answered, "Yes?"

"To return to the subject, it's a relief above all else that you have the qualification. This means you're now able to undertake the mausoleum's 'Trial.' Which brings me to something I must ask you."

In Roswaal's solemn, low voice, all trace of playfulness had vanished. Emilia, receiving this, urged him on with a serious gaze of her own. Seeing this,

"It's a simple matter. —Will you undertake the 'Trial'?"

The short question dropped into the room, and Emilia pressed her lips together, letting silence settle.

It was a natural enough progression of the conversation, if you thought about it. They had gone to confirm whether she had the qualification to undertake the "Trial," and had returned having confirmed that she did. The question followed naturally. But—

"Before you answer, I want to ask something. This 'Trial'—is it something she absolutely has to take on?"

Before Emilia could voice her reply, Subaru stepped forward in front of her and raised his hand. At the question, Ram's eyes beside Roswaal took on a hostile cast, but Roswaal held up a hand to restrain his maid.

"It's a very you sort of question, I must say. If she doesn't undertake the 'Trial,' anyone with the 'Qualification' cannot leave the Sanctuary. Haven't you heard as much from Garfiel, by any chance?"

"I've heard that. But that's not a reason it has to be Emilia-tan who does it. The 'Witch of Greed's mausoleum is a shady place, and there's no telling what kind of danger could happen there. Sending Emilia-tan—a Royal Selection candidate with everything at stake—into a place like that, is that really okay?"

"Hmm-m. Well, a fair point is a fair point, I suppose. If it's simply a matter of undertaking the 'Trial,' then any other qualified person would do—Garfiel there, for instance."

"Ah? Me? Fine by me, I don't mind. I could just take on the 'Trial,' blow right through it, and call it 'Balbalmoa's right-right-left' and be done with it."

Given the opening, Garfiel pointed a thumb at himself and grinned, baring his teeth. The phrase he tossed out partway through sounded, in the end, like nothing more than "turned right," but Subaru ignored that and focused solely on the reliable part of what he'd said.

In fact, if the "Trial" only needed to be cleared by any single person, there was no need for it to be Emilia. Someone qualified with a much surer chance ought to take it on instead.

—At worst, even if it meant Subaru, who'd been granted the "Qualification," taking on the Trial himself.

"—Nah, that'd be a bit of a problem, wouldn't it."

That was a voice that suddenly reached them from the entrance of the room, belonging to no one they recognized.

Subaru, who had his back to the door, turned around in surprise at the unfamiliar voice. Garfiel stood there, leaning back against the wall beside the door, and at Subaru's glance he gave a small wave of his hand in front of his face.

"Wasn't me. It's this old hag here."

As he spoke, he used the same waving hand to gesture off to the side. Dropping his gaze there, Subaru found, standing beside the already small Garfiel, an even smaller shadow.

"Who're you calling an old hag. Grew up into a mouthy, rotten brat, didn't you."

Said a tiny, tiny girl with long, pale red hair hanging down, in an oddly mature manner.

She was a girl with well-formed features and a lovable face. Her age looked to be about the same as Petra's—perhaps eleven or twelve. Her pale red hair had a natural wave to it, and combined with its fine texture, it looked positively fluffy. She wore a white robe so oversized its hem dragged on the ground, and the way her hands didn't fully emerge from the sleeves was, frankly, shamelessly cute.

Above all, from her manner of speech and the way Garfiel addressed her,

"Figured you'd show up eventually, but here of all places, huh, Loli-hag...!"

"What's this. Am I imagining it, or am I being called something rather unwelcome?"

"Um, that 'loli' thing—I think you use that a lot for Beatrice... does it mean 'small'?"

At Subaru's astonishment, the girl looked up at him with a displeased face. As Emilia, catching his muttering, demonstrated her high level of experience dealing with Subaru through her modern-world knowledge, Subaru held up a single finger.

"That's right, Emilia-tan, correct! To put it more precisely, it means young to a degree outside my strike zone. And when this word 'loli' combines with 'hag,' it completes a miraculous collaboration where the appearance is young but the contents are an old lady! Personally I'm not one for loli characters to begin with, so, well, but I can appreciate the subtle charm of a gap moe!"

"Gap moe?"

"Like, a girl who normally acts all dignified and grown-up, but then turns out to be childish in little moments, or oblivious to common sense, or way too easily fooled because she's so honest deep down—that kind of thing also counts as gap moe!"

At Subaru's rapid-fire, long-winded explanation, Emilia held a finger to her lips with a look of understanding, saying "So there really are girls like that..." The fact that she hadn't noticed the traits described applied directly to herself was utterly endearing, but the person who'd been made into the topic of conversation looked displeased.

"So? I don't get this 'loli' business, but that's me you keep calling 'hag, hag,' isn't it? Well, well, and on our first meeting too—here's someone even ruder than Roz-boy."

"Oops, my apologies, mademoiselle. The name's Natsuki Subaru! The talk of the town Witchbeast hunter. Well, I wasn't actually the one who landed the finishing blow either time, but."

He gave a thumbs up and introduced himself with great vigor, only for the introduction to fizzle out at the end. Then Subaru turned his palm toward her and, prompting the girl now wearing a puzzled expression, said "and, go!"

"I introduced myself, so now it's your turn for self-introduction time, please. A quick profile, hobbies and special skills. Bonus points if you add your own charm point."

"...Ryuzu Meyer. I'm the nominal representative of this Sanctuary, I suppose you could say."

Letting Subaru's nonsense pass right by her, the loli-hag—the person who'd given her name as Ryuzu—poked just her fingertips out of her oversized sleeve and scratched her forehead.

"Didn't notice it while you were asleep, but you're a boy who's gone past rude straight into pitiful. Feels like I lent you a bed for nothing."

"Speaking of a bed, don't tell me the one I was sleeping in until just now was..."

"Yes. Ryuzu-san's house. It was close to the mausoleum, so we had Garfiel carry you over... and thank you again, really, for earlier."

At Emilia's bow, Ryuzu shook her head slowly. Judging by the exchange, it seemed the two of them had already gotten acquainted while Subaru was unconscious.

In any case, since it was a fact that he'd been taken care of,

"Not knowing that, I'm sorry for the rude things I said. Thanks for lending me a bed, that really helped. And sorry again for being late with my thanks."

"...Well now, I'm surprised. Unlike Gar-boy, you can apologize honestly. Natsuki... Subaru, was it. Su-boy, then."

"That sounds like the name of some weather-forecasting sibling duo, but sure, that works. I'll call you Ryuzu-san, then."

With his gratitude expressed, Ryuzu's displeasure was, for the moment, set aside. Now that they'd peacefully settled on names for each other, Subaru continued with "By the way,"

"Earlier you said that would be a problem, Ryuzu-san—why's that? Is there some issue if Garfiel clears the 'Trial'?"

"Quick to switch gears, aren't you, boy. Ah, yes. It's a problem. A big problem. After all, if someone born of the Sanctuary takes on the 'Trial,' it violates the Contract."

"A Contract again..."

At the constant, tangled web of Contracts and Covenants popping up again and again, Subaru made a sour face and looked at Roswaal. Receiving that glance, Roswaal shrugged.

"Unfortuna-a-ately, House Mathers isn't unrelated to that Contract... well, not so unrelated that I could say we're entirely uninvolved, but we're not the primary party either. My family's role is, to the ve-e-ery end, merely a supporting one."

"Skip the self-preserving disclaimers and just explain already. Contract details, three lines or less."

"Ha-a-arsh. Well, simply put, the condition for freeing the Sanctuary's residents is clearing the 'Trial,' but the one taking on that Trial has to be an outsider with the Qualification. Which means, as things sta-a-and now..."

"The only candidate is me. That's what you're saying, isn't it."

Picking up the tail end of Roswaal's explanation, Emilia concluded in a quiet tone. Roswaal affirmed it with a nod, and glancing briefly toward Ryuzu, said,

"A-a-and, everyone in the 'Sanctuary' has already accepted as much. They're hoping Lady Emilia will take on the 'Trial' and clear it."

"I don't want you to think I'm getting cold feet just from hearing this, but... hypothetically, what would happen if someone other than me took on the 'Trial'?"

Emilia's violet eyes turned toward Garfiel as she pushed the conversation forward with a hypothetical. It was Ryuzu who answered, prefacing with "Well, about that,"

"Until now—at least in all the time since I was born—not a single soul has taken on the 'Trial.' So I can't answer a hypothetical like that. No one's ever attempted it, whether resident or outsider."

"Not one person, ever? Kind of scary to ask, but... how old are you, Ryuzu-san?"

Given the universal premise behind the existence of a "loli-hag," the answer to Subaru's question now was the kind of thing that was genuinely frightening to hear. Sure enough, Ryuzu, with a look of gazing off into the distance, said, "Let's see now,"

"I don't actually remember the time when this place was first made, of course. But I'd guess somewhere around a hundred and ten-odd years, at most."

"More than enough! At the very least, you're the oldest person I've met so far."

Excluding spirits, and the Witch who'd become a spiritual being, that was.

Swallowing that caveat, Subaru shot Emilia a glance tinged with worry. But even under that gaze, no shadow of darkness fell across Emilia's expression.

"In any case, I understand the situation. Either way, I can't leave this Sanctuary unless I overcome the 'Trial,' so I'll take it on."

"Emilia-tan looking all resolved and brave is a sight to behold, but shouldn't we look for a more cautious approach first? Find a back way, a loophole, anything—I don't think it'd be too late to take it on after that."

"I think it's really not okay to throw cold water on someone right when they've worked up their resolve."

But at Subaru's insistence—still just wanting to keep any dangerous possibility at bay—Emilia pursed her lips into a displeased pout. Even as he took in her accusing gaze,

"It's like, I dunno, suspicious, or like we're being played, that kind of unease I just can't shake, honestly. It's like the situation's arranged just a little too neatly, like the road's been paved specifically for us to walk down it, complete with traffic control and everything."

"I have no idea what you're talking about. Sometimes, Subaru, you say the most incomprehensible things."

"Nobody says 'incomprehensible things' like that these days..."

As their usual back-and-forth sharpened Emilia's gaze, Subaru hurriedly waved his hands, saying "No, no,"

"That's not it, I mean this whole situation reeks of being engineered. We got called into a place where half-elves can't leave, and now the only way out is for Emilia-tan to go and pull off something huge. And on top of that, everyone around us has already been briefed and already agreed to it."

"Engineered by whom?"

"Whom—come on, there's only one person it could be."

Answering Emilia's question that way, Subaru spun around in place. Then, at the end of the turn, he snapped his finger out and pointed sharply,

"Right? Isn't that right?"

"Ah? Me?"

"Ah, wrong, sorry, spun too far. This way, this way—right? Isn't that right, Roswaal."

"Couldn't have picked a less dignified way to end that, could you-u."

Roswaal offered that wry assessment of Subaru's antics with a bitter smile. But right after, he closed one eye, reflecting Subaru in his yellow one alone.

"Sti-i-ill, sharp as ever, aren't you. In truth, I did want to create this situation, and did. Of cou-ou-ourse, I haven't gone so far as to tamper with the stage itself."

"I think I'm starting to get it, somehow."

Raising an eyebrow at Roswaal's phrasing, Subaru grasped a piece of his scheme. Emilia, unable to follow the conversation, wore a puzzled expression, but Subaru went on to lay the hidden side of things bare for her.

"First, the thing that struck me as off was Roswaal's injury. To begin with, Roswaal should have known he had no Qualification to take on the 'Trial.' Given this land's originally managed by House Mathers, it'd make sense for him to already be acquainted with Garfiel and the rest."

"That... that's true. Yes, it is."

"So Roswaal must have known that going into the mausoleum without qualification would get him rejected. And yet, why did he go in anyway? Some pointless rebellion against the world? Or did his masochistic streak finally hit its breaking point? Both are strong candidates, but I don't think it's either."

"He-e-ello, hello. Is that really the image you have of me, Subaru-kun?"

Deliberately ignoring Roswaal's dissatisfied-sounding interjection, Subaru held up a finger and said, "In other words,"

"This injury you've got is exactly according to your own plan, and it means something. And what it means, I'd bet, ties into the Royal Selection too."

"..........."

"By the way, there's something I want to ask. The people of Arlam Village are being gathered up right now in some place called the Great Cathedral, right?"

The sudden shift in topic from Subaru was directed at Ram, standing beside Roswaal. She, who had kept her silence, gave a small tilt of her chin at Subaru's question.

"Yes, that's right. The villagers have all been gathered at the Great Cathedral... where they're being held under a form of house arrest by the Sanctuary's residents."

"That's it, right there—house arrest. Earlier the conversation got cut off because we went to look at the mausoleum, but... what exactly does 'house arrest' mean here? Why would the Sanctuary's people need to hold the village folk, including Roswaal, under house arrest?"

Subaru next turned his face toward Garfiel, standing by the wall. At Subaru's question, he narrowed his already-sharp eyes further and continued, "Ain't it obvious,"

"Lemme tell you somethin'—this ain't some escape hatch for when things get tough. No matter how much you're the lord and his people, when you've got a problem you've been sittin' on and lettin' fester, and then you go and get used to make things convenient for yourself, that don't feel too good, does it?"

"The problem you've been sitting on, of course, would be..."

"Not being able to leave for the world outside this Sanctuary, that's what."

It was Ryuzu who picked up where Subaru's words left off. A shadow of hardship, ill-suited to her childlike features, fell across her face, and lowering her eyes, she spoke in a thin voice,

"As I said earlier, it's already been over a hundred years since I was born. And yet I've never once set foot outside this Sanctuary. After all, ever since I was born, the Contract has bound me to this land. That's exactly why I've made a kind of peace with it, in half-resignation... but I can't let go of hope."

"The old hag here wants to see the outside world too. So does everyone else. If there's even a chance of gettin' that chance, we'll bite down and drag it in by force if we have to. Gettin' our hands on a weakened lord and villagers we could hold hostage was damn convenient."

At Ryuzu and Garfiel's words, the air in the room shifted abruptly.

In other words, they were, right now, confessing before Subaru and the others the reason for the house arrest—which was to say, the motive behind their actions. It was a part he hadn't consciously registered until now, but the relationship between Subaru and them was that of captor and captive. It was, in other words, also a relationship between perpetrator and victim.

"So that means, huh. You all are holding the villagers hostage... to demand your own release from the 'Sanctuary'?"

"You can take it that way, no complaints from me. And the one who can meet that condition is..."

With that, Ryuzu let her words trail off and glanced toward Emilia.

Taking in the meaning of that glance, Emilia, as if newly aware of her own position, said,

"Me. —So that's how it is."

Accepting the turn of events, Emilia closed her eyes just once. When she opened them again a few seconds later, no trace of hesitation remained. She had already made up her mind.

"You haven't done anything terrible to the villagers, have you?"

"Course not. If we treated 'em like garbage on top of all this, that'd be no different from just takin' it out on 'em outta spite. I'd rather die than pull somethin' that pathetic."

And so, once Emilia had decided to act, the extent to which she could set her own concerns aside was immeasurable. Even as she resolved to take on a Trial that might be dangerous, her mind was already turning to worry over the villagers—over other people. It was clear proof of it.

And it was precisely that strength and that weakness of hers that never failed to draw Subaru in.

"You're making quite the dissatisfied face, aren't you-u."

"...Of course I am. In the end, nothing changes the fact that we're being played along by your scheme. It's just gone from not noticing we're being played, to noticing it and having no choice but to go along with it anyway."

Gritting his teeth against his own frustration, Subaru clicked his tongue at Roswaal, who was watching him with a smile in his eyes. Then, as if quietly remembering something, he turned back to him.

"I forgot to mention it, but, about the cause of your injury,"

"Yes, yes, go ahead and say it. I'll grade your answer, shall I."

"A performance. No—more like laying the groundwork."

At Subaru's words, delivered while scratching the back of his neck, Roswaal's expression stiffened faintly. Watching his reaction, Subaru closed one eye and continued,

"I doubt the people of Arlam just accepted being placed under house arrest without a fight. There must've been pushback, obviously. And to settle that down, you needed to show some kind of action. If you, the lord, could've just gone on a rampage and driven off Garfiel and the rest, that would've been fine and simple—but the Sanctuary's people are your subjects too. You can't pull something like that."

"Hmm. And so, what happens then, I wonder?"

"You have no choice but to accept the conditions Garfiel and the others set. In other words, freeing the half-elves from the 'Sanctuary.' But that absolutely requires Emilia-tan's cooperation. Except that alone wouldn't satisfy either the villagers or the residents. So then, it's simple. —All you had to do was show, by taking on the 'Trial' yourself, that you had both the will to accept their demands and the will to free everyone from house arrest."

"————"

"Dunno if you'd even calculated exactly how much damage you'd take from the Miasma, but if you'd worked out you wouldn't die, it's not a gamble you couldn't take. And the bigger the damage you ended up taking, the more it conveyed how serious you were, and the more sympathy you'd earn. It'd also raise expectations for the real deal coming after you—that's the whole calculation."

In the end, every bit of Roswaal's injury had been staged exactly according to his own design.

The high caliber of Roswaal's abilities as their lord was well known even to the villagers of Arlam, his subjects. A "Trial" severe enough to inflict this much injury on someone like him—if a savior then appeared who could overcome it and rescue them, how would that be received?

"So, putting together malice, bias, and pure convenience into one big guess—how'd I do on the answer sheet?"

"—We-e-ell now, I'm astonished. Truly, genuinely, tru-u-uly astonished. Whatever could have happened to yo-o-ou in just a few short days, I wonder."

At Subaru's words, Roswaal laughed low in his throat, offering him praise.

Clapping his hands, he pasted onto his face a smile bright enough to be radiant.

"Ma-a-agnificent. I'd say that's very nearly a perfect answer. I never imagined you'd read this far into it. I kne-e-ew it, you really were a lucky find."

"Well, thanks a bunch. Makes me want to throw up."

Roswaal remained endlessly moved, but Subaru, on the receiving end of having his guess confirmed, couldn't hide his disgust and looked away.

Roswaal's scheme, his own having read it through completely, the fact that the scheme was precisely an act done for Emilia's sake, and even his own inward approval of it—all of it turned his stomach.

Unaware of the scheming between Subaru and Roswaal, Emilia was still occupied with Ryuzu and Garfiel, discussing matters including the "Trial."

Watching her back, Subaru resolved firmly, never to let her hear what had just been discussed.

*She only needed to keep looking straight ahead.*

*He didn't want her—someone he wished to remain noble and high-minded—to learn of a scheme this shady.*

*If Subaru could take on whatever mud there was to be smeared with, and she could receive whatever praise was showered down, that would be enough.*

In the Royal Selection, Emilia had yet to establish a single achievement, her footing still unsteady.

If the true start of her Royal Selection was to begin here, in this "Sanctuary," then all he had to do was support her with everything he had.

Renewing his resolve, hardening his determination, Subaru made up his mind.

And then, behind Subaru as he clenched his fists, Roswaal, leaning his weight back against the bed,

"...Very nearly correct. Though that isn't the only reason I went into the mausoleum."

—murmured in a voice so small it reached only the ears of the peach-haired maid stationed beside the bed, and even she, hearing it, could only lower her eyes with a pained expression in response.
