Arc 4 Chapter 24: "Hold On"

The first thing to intrude on Subaru's consciousness was discomfort.

"Wuh! Ugh! Blegh—"

A foreign, gravelly sensation in his mouth, the earthy taste dancing on his tongue mixed with bitterness — Subaru coughed it out as he opened his eyes.

His vision had fallen into darkness, and a chill touched his entire body. His awareness drifted to the hardness beneath him and the pull of gravity, and he recognized the fact that he was lying on his side. Then, slowly turning his gaze around, his eyes gradually adjusting to the dark — he understood that he was inside the same old ruins he'd seen several times before.

"Inside the tomb, huh...?"

He said it aloud, as if to confirm it, and spat out the grit still in his mouth — the taste of it fresh in his memory. He'd had this exact experience before, waking with a mouthful of this gravel. Which meant, right now—

"So this is right after the first 'Trial'... back this far... no, more importantly—"

If this really did mean a return from the 'Trial' — that is, a parting from the past — then he wasn't the only one lying here.

"—Emilia!"

He spotted her right beside him — a silver-haired girl collapsed in the darkness.

He walked over, and laying her face upright, he saw it colored with anguish. For just a moment, he hesitated to reach his fingers toward her sleeping face.

He already knew, from having accompanied Emilia through the 'Trial' several times now — if anyone touched her while she was in this sleeping state, the 'Trial' would be interrupted no matter what stage it was in, and she'd be pulled back out.

That was exactly why there was a chance a third party's touch could cause a failure right on the verge of completing the 'Trial.' Touching Emilia inside the tomb required the utmost care. But—

"I already know this time's no good..."

Shaking his head to kill the hesitation, Subaru burned her sleeping face into his memory as he gently lifted her body and gathered it into his arms. Instantly, the pained expression on Emilia's face receded rapidly, and her consciousness was guided toward waking—

"S-Subaru...?"

"Yeah, that's right. It's me, Emilia-tan. You okay?"

Emilia's groggy call, consciousness still lagging behind wakefulness. Smiling to reassure her, Subaru answered while waiting for her to grasp her current situation.

He waited for her to remember the circumstances she'd been placed in, to accept the result of the 'Trial,' and then begin sobbing like a child.

Knowing she would collapse into tears, and hating his own weakness at being able to do nothing about it.

And her nobility, in gathering herself and standing up anyway, no matter how bravely she tried to act.

Holding her gently, so at least she wouldn't break, Subaru kept his arms wrapped tightly around her without letting go, until Emilia had calmed down.


※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※


It was only after carrying the sobbing, broken-down Emilia to Ryuzu's house and confirming that Ram had gotten her to sleep that Subaru was finally able to calm down and start sorting through the situation.

I did a Return by Death.

The others around him — Ram, Otto, and the rest — wanted to hear what had happened inside, but he'd put them off with "First, let's wait until Emilia wakes up," and asked to be left alone for now.

Leaving their side, though they clearly had more to say, Subaru stood outside Ryuzu's house gazing up at the night sky, the wind against his skin, his mind a tangle of conflicting thoughts.

"Even so... again, huh."

He touched his left side, fingers checking the wound that should have been there but wasn't.

A wound gouged deep by a blade, the kind that would spill his organs out into the open. He supposed the chance to see one's own innards was rare in most people's lives, but on that front, Subaru's tally of experience had probably already far outstripped the combined experience of most of the world's population.

"Not that I'm thrilled about it or anything. I'm not so twisted that looking at my own guts gives me the munchies or some kind of rapture... the only one who'd get off on that is..."

He trailed off there, a voice surfacing in his mind.

*"—I told you, didn't I? We made a promise, didn't we?"*

A voice he remembered as sultry, a woman's voice. A voice that carried a deeply immoral allure from being soaked in blood, the voice of someone deranged enough to reach ecstasy from watching a life slip away.

And Subaru had far, far too much familiarity with that voice, that promise, that derangement.

More than that — this wasn't even the first time his stomach had been slashed open.

"So the 'Bowel Hunter' makes a reappearance here, huh... give me a break..."

Subaru pressed a hand to his forehead and looked up at the sky as if lamenting.

Rising behind his closed eyelids was a jet-black beauty with hair as dark as Subaru's own, grown long. A murderer who had wielded a vicious blade and taken Subaru's life twice over. The threat that had cornered Subaru the most during the first loop, driven off by Reinhard's hand — Elsa Granhiert.

"Didn't get a look at her, but it's gotta be Elsa. Or rather, I don't even wanna think there's another one of those things running around. Elsa it is."

By his subjective sense of elapsed time, more than two months should have passed, but in real time, barely a month and a bit had gone by since his encounter with her. The wound she'd taken back then hadn't been shallow, either, but apparently the aftereffects had posed no obstacle whatsoever to her killing Subaru.

Was the difficulty just too low, or had she already made a full recovery?

"Safer to assume there's no wound at all. Considering how healing magic works in this world, it seems like anything short of dying can be patched up. Not that I have room to talk — by that logic I've died plenty of times myself... no wait, I actually have died."

There were too many cases of nearly dying and surviving, and too many cases of nearly dying and just dying, for him to get a solid grip on where exactly he stood.

In any case, Subaru had settled in his own mind that the threat that had attacked the mansion was Elsa. Which meant the real problem now was—

"Why Elsa was at the mansion, and what happened to Frederica and the others."

When Subaru had arrived at the mansion, the night mansion still carried faint traces of life going on as usual — the light in Petra's room, the lights in the entrance hall. He'd exclude the study, since that had been used for the escape route, but thinking about those two other lit rooms—

"At least up until that night, nothing had happened... is that the read?"

He considered whether that conclusion might be premature.

It was possible that the lights in Petra's room, the entrance hall, the study, had simply been left on all day, and that Subaru was mistaking that for evidence everything had been fine right up until that night. But there was one factor that argued against that assumption: how long the light lasted.

"Unlike Lagmite Ore, a Crystal Lamp needs to be recharged by the mana in the air during the daytime. If you leave it on, it won't last even half a day — I know that from experience."

Once, during a lesson on learning letters at the mansion, a Crystal Lamp someone had forgotten to switch off had suddenly gone dark at night. Subaru had made a huge fuss suspecting some kind of "lamp phenomenon," and Ram had sent him flying, Rem had gently caught him, and Emilia had shrunk into a corner of the room trembling — the memory loosened his cheeks into a smile. But now wasn't the time to dwell on that memory; he pulled out only the important part.

"If they'd been on all day too, it'd be weird for the light to still be on at night. So I can probably rule out the mansion being empty since before that. Which puts the limit at... the night of day six. Right now it's the night of day two, so four days from now. No — three and a half days."

In terms of hours, that left Subaru roughly eighty-four hours as his time limit.

The role he'd been tasked with this time, to be carried out within that limited window, was—

"Defending the mansion from that 'Bowel Hunter's' attack, or securing the safety of the people there."

Facing that snake-like, spider-like murderer, he had no confidence the others had made it out safely. He didn't know where that escape route led, but if Frederica and the others had fled the mansion, their destination should have been the 'Sanctuary,' to reunite with their master Roswaal.

And yet Subaru and the others hadn't crossed paths with Frederica's group anywhere along the route to the 'Sanctuary.'

"Either that escape passage was some absurdly long tunnel leading who-knows-where, and they made it out somewhere far off. Or..."

He didn't want to think it, but — the case where they hadn't been able to escape at all.

Weighing the difference in strength between the two sides, Subaru's brow furrowed at how likely that latter, unwanted possibility seemed.

But in reality, the strength Subaru had witnessed in Elsa was on that level. Even having gained no small amount of experience in this other world, having laid eyes on more than a few formidable fighters, he still felt that way.

If he weighed that murderer purely on combat ability alone—

"Above Julius, below Wilhelm-san... or somewhere around there. There's no way in hell I could put up a fight against her even if I really tried."

Subaru's base stats were low to begin with. Compared to her, the gap in power would be the difference between a cat and a tiger, without exaggeration. His odds of winning solo were as good as zero.

In their previous clash, too, a large part of what got him through in the end had come down to sheer luck.

"It'd be great if invincible Reinhard-san happened to wander by again this time too... but no matter how much that guy's built like some protagonist-privileged pretty boy, expecting him to show up at exactly this moment is asking too much."

If this were a story, that kind of development would be far too convenient, and Subaru himself would throw the book down in disgust. Though, faced with real hardship, it was exactly the kind of convenient contrivance he'd welcome with open arms.

Not that he could afford to keep escaping into fantasy — Subaru exhaled once, chasing away the weak thought, and—

"The reason Elsa came to the mansion... is probably, like last time, to obstruct the Royal Selection. In the end, who the hell hired her to get in Emilia's way?"

In the royal capital, Felt had stolen Emilia's insignia. The one behind that request was Elsa, and behind her, there was some mastermind who'd hired her. Given the sequence of events — trying to steal the insignia that served as qualification for the Royal Selection — Subaru had figured it had to be a rival camp.

"After seeing the other candidates, though... I'm not so sure I can say that anymore."

First, there was the matter of hiring an assassin to steal an insignia.

That alone ruled out Crusch as a candidate. Having actually laid eyes on that remarkable woman, Subaru could declare with confidence — she was absolutely not the kind of person who would do something like that.

And given the circumstances, Felt was naturally out of the running too. That left only two: Priscilla and Anastasia.

"Priscilla... would that arrogant, high-handed lady really enjoy pulling off something this shadowy? Unless I'm badly misreading her, she's the type who genuinely believes she's the center of the world. I can't picture her actively resorting to something like this. Which leaves Anastasia-san, but..."

The purple-haired merchant woman came to mind.

Behind her gentle features lurked the gleam of a hunter with sharp instincts, someone who could take flawless action while keeping her own positioning firmly in mind — a quality Subaru himself had once made use of.

If it were her, she might well choose a rational method of knocking down her rivals. She'd probably be more than capable of the kind of outlandish thinking that would gleefully employ hiring an outsider — a sort of forbidden move — with relish. The one thing that argued against that theory—

"Can't picture that guy Julius letting it slide, huh. No, it's not like I think highly of him or anything. Just... yeah, well, it's just a feeling I get."

It might be something done behind the back of that 'Sword Saint' of hers, but the doubt remained: would she really go that far, risking an irreparable crack in an otherwise harmonious lord-and-retainer relationship? Ultimately, involvement from the Anastasia Camp fell out of favor too, and—

"None of the candidates are left as candidates. Except... there's still plenty of room to think about it. Considering how Emilia gets treated."

If it wasn't one of the Royal Selection candidates who'd made the request, then it could well be someone from a faction that simply wanted Emilia purely removed from the Royal Selection. If someone who despised her for being a half-elf had chosen this shortsighted method, that too would be a consistent explanation.

The very thought of "would they really go that far" was probably itself Subaru being naive. The resentment toward her half-elf bloodline ran that deep.

"But if that's the case, it's basically impossible to expose the identity of whoever hired her. Not unless Elsa herself spits it out."

And he lacked the strength to make her spit it out — a circular dead end.

In the end, when it came to who could realistically deal with Elsa's assault—

"Makes you pity how weak this camp's lineup is. I'm a flat no. Otto's not even worth counting. Emilia could put up a decent fight if Puck's around and well, Ram's stamina is a worry if it turns into a long fight. Roswaal's an injured man and a reliable non-factor as usual. No idea how much Frederica can pull off, and Petra having some hidden trump-card power is... too much of a stretch. Which leaves..."

Two breakthrough strategies occurred to Subaru.

One was to head back to the mansion, pull out Frederica, Petra, Rem, and Beatrice — four people — and flee with them into the 'Sanctuary,' avoiding Elsa's attack altogether.

And the other was—

"—Now jus' what the hell d'you think yer doin', sittin' there mopin' about?"

Garfiel, who'd come outside, was looking down at Subaru, seated on the ground with his back against the wall. It was rare to be looked down on by someone shorter than himself, and savoring a bit of novelty at that, Subaru shook his head and said "Nah,"

"Just had some stuff to sort out, was thinking things over. Where's Emilia?"

"Princess is still snorin' away, dead to the world. Just hope it ain't gonna turn into one'a them 'Moroloku's cat-naps last a full day and night' situations."

"No idea what that means, but sounds like Moroloku naps way too much."

Poking fun, as had become customary, at an idiom that made no sense to him, Subaru stood up and turned to face Garfiel.

A height half a head shorter than Subaru, short blond hair. Sharp eyes, a white scar on the forehead. Overly pointed canines and a ferocious, beast-like aura radiating from his entire body — the confidence in oneself that only the strong possess.

Of the two options that had occurred to Subaru for dealing with Elsa, this young man was the other one.

If they cleared the 'Trial' and freed the 'Sanctuary,' Garfiel could be brought out of this place. And if his strength lived up to his boasting, Subaru could reasonably hope for him as a fighting force against Elsa. Given that fleeing was only a stopgap measure, driving her off or defeating her outright would be preferable.

"Hey, Garfiel."

"Wha' is it."

"You're the strongest, right? You're confident no one can beat you, right?"

"Ah? Course I am. Don't matter who it is, I'll crush 'em, blast 'em, kill 'em dead, and stand there gloatin' over their corpse."

Somewhat sullenly, but without a shred of wavering, Garfiel answered Subaru's question. Feeling reassured by his response, Subaru nodded and said—

"Once we get outside this 'Sanctuary,' I think there's gonna be a time real soon where we need that strength of yours. When that happens, I think we're gonna have to rely on you being the strongest."

"Wha'?"

"Prove those words for me, alright? That's what'll be the most reliable thing right about now."

Clapping the bewildered-looking Garfiel on the shoulder, Subaru then headed into Ryuzu's house. The three people inside — Ram, Ryuzu, and Otto — all turned their eyes toward Subaru as he opened the door and stepped in, but bearing their attention, Subaru headed for the bedroom where Emilia lay sleeping.

"Barusu, is Emilia-sama still—"

"Nah, she should be waking up right about now. —Emilia-tan, I know it's probably hard to show your face, but let's talk. Everyone's waiting on you too."

He called through the door, and a faint breath reached him from the other side.

A moment's hesitation. Then, a few seconds later, the doorknob turned timidly, and Emilia appeared from the bedroom, her head slightly bowed.

She looked up at Subaru standing before the door, her lips trembling, and—

"That... I'm sorry, for causing so much trouble. Even inside the tomb, and even now..."

"Trouble caused by Emilia-tan isn't trouble, it's just stuff I want to do, so it's fine. More importantly, is anywhere heavy or hurting? If there's somewhere that feels weird, I could gently pet and rub and heal it for you."

"Mm. I think I hit my hip a little when I fell, so it's kind of throbbing..."

"Alright, got it. Let me get right to work thoroughly on— Ram-san? Ram-san?! The tip of your staff is stabbing into the vicinity of my liver?!"

Ram, who had crept up behind him, jabbed the staff she'd drawn into Subaru just as he was about to crack a lecherous joke. When called out on it, she wordlessly pressed the tip in further, thoroughly tormenting him until he yelped like a puppy and leapt away, and then—

"Emilia-sama, are you feeling alright? Please forget Barusu's rude remarks and tell Ram plainly how you're feeling."

"The way you're just powering through all this without a single comment is amazing! Look, there's actually blood coming through a little for real. How much force do you have to put in to pierce through this fairly thick outfit?"

Rubbing his hip, where a sharp pain still lingered, Subaru protested, and Ram shot him a sidelong glance as if looking at some kind of insect and gave a short "Hah" through her nose. Then she turned back to Emilia,

"So then, are you alright? If there's no issue with your condition..."

"Y-yes, I'm fine. We need to talk about... what happened inside, the 'Trial,' right."

Ahead of Ram's words, Emilia nodded and stepped forward into the center of the room. Before long, Garfiel too came into the house, and the assembled faces formed a circle around Emilia.

And so, under everyone's watchful eyes, Emilia went through the same routine as before — stumbling through an explanation of the 'Trial' and its result. If there was one thing different from last time, it was—

"So then, why exactly was it that Natsuki-san, who went in there, came out fine?"

That, raised in a small voice, was Otto's question, speaking for everyone.

Subaru hadn't inserted his own information into the conversation so far — enough that the question had come up. That was partly to gauge Emilia's thinking regarding the 'Trial,' and—

"I told you, right? I went in because I got 'qualified.' As for where I got it — probably the tomb, this afternoon. And as for what happened once I went in... I went through the same thing they call the 'Trial,' same as Emilia-tan. Seems like I passed, though."

Subaru's statement sent a ripple of unease through the room.

Above all, Emilia — who had also challenged the 'Trial' and failed — showed the greatest shock. Her violet-blue eyes clouded with confusion as she silently stared at Subaru.

Nodding toward her, Subaru continued,

"Just so we're clear up front — it's not like I got through the 'Trial' because I'm some kind of better person. The 'Trial' was about facing your past. For me, since I'd more or less already made my peace with mine, it was basically a bonus stage, that's all."

"I don't know what this 'bonus' thing is, but if young Su cleared the 'Trial'... hmph, that is something worth being surprised at."

"But from what Emilia-sama just said, the 'Trial' isn't over with just one, is it? Since the word 'first' implies there's more to come."

Ryuzu's acceptance, and Ram's remark. Nodding along to both, Subaru stole a glance at Emilia's state. She still kept her silence, only a complicated wave of emotion surfacing in her eyes.

Imagining the thoughts churning in her chest, Subaru nonetheless shook off any softness.

The time limit he'd set, and the difficulty of clearing Emilia's 'Trial,' rooted as it was in her 'past' — weighing those on the scale, there weren't many options he could take. And so—

"When I cleared the 'Trial,' I heard something... apparently, if two challengers enter the 'Trial' at the same time, you can't move on to the next 'Trial.' You have to come back another day to go in."

"...Hmph. Meaning?"

"If me and Emilia-tan go into the tomb together, only Emilia-tan's 'Trial' will start, and I won't be able to take the 'Trial' — the second one, that is."

"Wait, wait, hold on a second, Natsuki-san."

Otto cut in quickly, interrupting Subaru's words. Not noticing the suspicious look Subaru was giving him, he ran a hand through his gray-tinged hair,

"From the way this conversation is going, does that mean you're planning to take on the 'Trial' too, Natsuki-san? Wasn't this supposed to be something that's Emilia-sama's achievement to begin with—"

"Idiot, Otto."

Subaru called out his name to stop the words spilling somewhat hastily off Otto's tongue, but too late. Otto, hearing his own name called, replayed what he'd just said and realized it had included something he shouldn't have said, hurriedly clapping a hand over his mouth. But the content had already reached everyone's ears — including Emilia's.

An awkward-looking Otto, and Subaru shooting him a look of contempt. And Emilia, watching the two of them from a distance, said—

"What did that mean, just now?"

"Emilia-tan, let's calm down. That was just, um..."

"Don't dodge it, tell me properly. —Please, Subaru."

Emilia looked at him with pleading eyes, imploring him.

No boy could refuse a beautiful girl's tearful plea, and if it was Emilia making it, then Natsuki Subaru certainly couldn't refuse either. It would have been better if he could have brushed it off with iron willpower, but Subaru let his shoulders drop, discarding that lingering resistance,

"If Emilia-tan clears the 'Trial,' the people of Arlam Village get freed from being hostages, and the people of the 'Sanctuary' get to leave behind a life bound to this land. If the 'Trial' gets cleared, we should be able to win the support of both those factions... that's the real scheme behind all this."

"...That's... Subaru, did you know?"

"Nah, didn't have the faintest clue until it got said just now."

In front of the visibly shaken Emilia, he brazenly told the lie head-on, chest out. At Subaru's sheer nerve, Otto and Ram's expressions soured as if they'd bitten into something bitter, but a quick glance from him was enough to seal their words.

Then Subaru turned back to face Emilia properly,

"It's all Roswaal's scheme. Honestly, I even suspect his injury might be part of the performance for this."

"Even for Roswaal, that's... I can't say for sure it isn't. Thinking about the situation now, he's capable of at least that much."

"Being irritated at playing into his scheme... isn't really the main reason, that'd be a joke if I said it was, but it is part of it. But more than that..."

In front of a visibly flustered Emilia, Subaru bent at the waist, gazing up at her fine features from below. Watching her long lashes tremble in surprise,

"I want to be your strength. I don't know what you saw, facing your 'past.' But if facing it means suffering that much, sobbing that hard, feeling like your heart's about to shatter... then I want to reach out and help."

"...Subaru."

"If it's just about taking the 'Trial' and freeing the 'Sanctuary,' there shouldn't be a problem with me doing it. If you need the credit for it, I'll give you everything that's mine. My achievement is your achievement. Everyone's carrying some 'past'... but deciding that all of it has to be something you make peace with — that's not right either."

Those were the words Garfiel had told him, in the previous world, before he'd died.

In front of Subaru and the others, who kept pushing Emilia toward the 'Trial' even as she suffered facing her past, Garfiel had argued against the necessity of overcoming one's past.

For Subaru, it had been a way of thinking that struck like a bolt from the blue.

Emilia's eyes widened in surprise, her lips pressed together in thought.

Subaru understood the reason for her conflict. In her heart of hearts, she surely felt the urge to shrink back from a 'past' she didn't want to face either. Precisely because she was strong and noble, because it would never even occur to her to push a painful burden onto someone else, that conflict weighed all the heavier on her.

The doubt of whether she could say for certain that the remaining 'Trials' wouldn't wound Subaru's heart the way her own 'past' assailed her.

"If you're troubled, that's fine too. I know this isn't something you can decide right away. —So even if that's how it is, at least let me have tomorrow, just the one day."

"Tomorrow, one day...?"

"Either way, I'm not gonna be the drill sergeant who drags Emilia-tan, worn out as she is, back to the tomb tomorrow and says 'Now go take the Trial!' If that's the case, then it makes sense for me to go challenge it first — I've still got some slack left, and it doubles as scouting out the second 'Trial' in advance. If it turns out I can actually clear the 'Trial' as a result, that's just a bonus."

Buying one day like that, and somehow buying another day on top of it, cutting straight through the shortest path — at best, the 'Sanctuary' could be freed as early as the day after tomorrow.

The ideal shape of things: no burden on Emilia, the goal achieved, and still in time to help the mansion.

Subaru could see Emilia wavering at his proposal.

It felt underhanded, deceiving her when she was this worn down, but it was a negotiation technique — present a large demand first, then follow up with the smaller, real request. The second, scaled-down concession, offered after the first demand had been rejected — she, in her current emotionally unstable state, didn't have the mental clarity to see through it and refuse.

Tomorrow, and the day after — Subaru would take on the 'Trial' and get through it. The chance for Emilia to stand as Emilia, in her own time, could surely be arranged again later.

The timing was bad this time. There was no time to spare. If they dawdled, that unreasonable fate would strike again—

"Been sittin' here quiet listenin', an' here you go, spinnin' this however the hell you please."

Right before Subaru's underhanded scheme could come to fruition, that voice calling "hold on" came from behind him.

The intruder, golden-haired, jade-colored eyes narrowed with ferocity, gnashing his overly sharp canines, stepped forward—

"I'm against it — anyone but that princess, Emilia-sama, takin' the 'Trial.' Least of all you. I sure as hell don't want you, of all people, bein' the one to free this place."

"Wha—?!"

Unexpected words, an utterly unforeseen declaration.

Subaru swallowed the meaning of the words thrown at him, but the speaker and the content simply refused to connect in his mind, and all he could manage was a bewildered sound.

And before Subaru, eyes wide with shock, the figure leaned in further still, as if driving home this incomprehensible reality,

"Got it? I'll say it again. I don't approve of anyone but Emilia-sama takin' the 'Trial.' Take it as my condition — one even the old hag ain't gonna bend."

So spat Garfiel, wrinkling his nose, fully unleashing his displeasure.
