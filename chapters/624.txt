Arc 8 Chapter 8: "A Vision of the Future"

――When the *Wandering Cloud Dragon* left Madelyn behind and dove into the capital, Emilia, falling behind Cecilus as he chased after it, decided to hide herself within the city as well.

"You there! Take this girl and fall back! I've frozen her arms and legs so she won't thrash, so please carry her carefully!"

As she did, she didn't forget to entrust the half-frozen-white Madelyn to one of the rebels who'd been watching the situation unfold.
The ice-encased Madelyn showed no sign of waking, and since Emilia had frozen her limbs good and hard just in case, there was little worry of her rampaging at headquarters.

"...Somehow, there's a really unpleasant feeling in the air."

After handing off Madelyn, Emilia built a staircase of ice to climb over the city walls, and felt a bad premonition at the atmosphere pervading the capital.
It wasn't as though the air itself changed between inside and outside the walls, but there was certainly a sharp, prickling sensation against her skin, one that tried to make her heart flinch.
Still, she couldn't afford to let that prickling feeling win and make her retreat.

"Both Mezoreia and Cecilus went off toward the castle."

The battle against the *Wandering Cloud Dragon* Mezoreia would have ended in Emilia's total defeat if Cecilus hadn't stepped in between them. Thanks to Cecilus, Emilia still had her strength left, but she couldn't stop reflecting on how close she'd come to being beaten.

"And here everyone went out of their way to bring me all the way to Vollachia..."

Of course, everyone had been equally worried about Subaru and Rem being sent flying off. If she said something like that out loud, no one would say the fault lay with Emilia alone.
Even so, Emilia bore a responsibility as the highest-ranking person in her camp.
She had to be conscious of the fact that Roswaal's greatness as her backer was a different kind of greatness from her own — the greatness of everyone working hard to make her wishes come true.
That was precisely why—

"I have to work really, really hard at the thing I said I could do."

Climbing over the tall, tall city walls and entering the city, the streets of the imperial capital finally came into view.
The atmosphere was quite different from the royal capital of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica, but even so, being the capital of a great nation, Emilia could feel in the orderly rows of beautiful buildings and the thoughtfully functional layout the shape of a city built with real care.
Surely the Emperor of the Empire had envisioned a firm blueprint for the future, and had worked hard to make this city and country better.

"Even after working so hard, it still ends up like this..."

And yet the impression given off by the capital's streets and the actual state of things were far too much at odds.
Whatever feelings the Emperor held about running the Empire, the answer some portion of its people had arrived at was this rebellion — and Emilia stood on the side pushing that rebellion forward.
One could call that drifting along with circumstance, but Emilia hadn't thrown in with this side thoughtlessly; she'd listened, discussed it with her companions, and decided.

Of course, rather than taking the Emperor's life as many of the rebels wished, she thought it would be best to secure his person and have him enter negotiations with Abel.
But that hope and wish of Emilia's would be betrayed in a way she never could have imagined — by the strange occurrence now unfolding in the capital.

"...What?"

Far off, near the dazzling castle at the very heart of the capital — the building called the Crystal Palace — she could see an unbelievably huge silhouette collide with the *Wandering Cloud Dragon* that had taken flight from the battlefield.
The clash of two enormous things raised the danger of the battle for the capital by a full stage or two, but what shocked her even more was something else entirely.

――At the foot of the Crystal Palace, where the fierce battle raged, pale-faced things had begun swarming out all over the streets of the capital, attacking the fleeing people.

"No... !"

The instant she laid eyes on those things, a chill ran up Emilia's spine.
It wasn't merely a physiological revulsion — more likely, it was Emilia's nature as a Spiritual Arts User rejecting them outright.

Spirits were natural things, existences that had arisen because the world wished for them.
By contrast, Emilia intuited that whatever was causing this chill was unnatural — impure things the world itself refused to acknowledge.

At the same time, she sensed that the Empire's civil war — which, however harsh or sorrowful, had until now been a fight between people — had at this moment transformed into a fierce struggle for survival between humans and something not human at all.

"――"

The instant that intuition struck her, the meaning of the battlefield changed within Emilia.
To save as many lives as possible, Emilia raised both arms to do what needed doing, and indiscriminately built ice staircases everywhere within reach along the star-shaped wall surrounding the capital.
By turning a situation where one could only enter or exit through the gates into one where escape was possible from anywhere, she increased the number of ways out.

"Everyone! Run toward the wall! You don't have to make it to the gate — you can escape from right there!"

Calling out loudly, Emilia dropped down from the wall into the city streets.
At once, a pale-faced being rose up from the ground as though sprouting there, reaching a hand toward Emilia. She struck it down mercilessly with a slash of ice.

"I'm sure you all have your reasons too, but..."

Faced with something that attacked others without question or hesitation, even Emilia couldn't persuade it to sit down calmly for a conversation.
Blocking her path were beings all dressed in imperial soldier uniforms; when they took a hit from Emilia as she raced past, their bodies shattered like porcelain and they fell. But the broken bodies immediately began knitting back together, returning to their original state — she couldn't finish them off.

"In that case—"

Not giving up, even when the odds were against her, was Emilia's boldness.
Against opponents that got back up no matter how much she shattered them, Emilia shifted tactics — rather than smashing them apart with her blade strikes, she began encasing the struck areas in ice.

"There! Hah! Hiyaaah!"

Against the 'enemies' welling up one, two, three, four at a time, Emilia kicked off walls, kicked off the ground, using the capital's streets as footholds every which way as she leapt about, landing attack after attack.
As expected, the 'enemies' whose broken parts were frozen by Emilia's attacks couldn't repair the damage back to normal. However, some of the 'enemies' began deliberately destroying the frozen parts themselves and overwriting them through regeneration.

"Then I'll just have to make you stop doing that!"

If her opponents adapted, Emilia would simply outdo their adaptation.
If freezing part of the body wasn't enough, she'd attack the 'enemy' by freezing its entire body instead. Of course, that meant using much more power per enemy, but that couldn't be helped.
She made up for any shortfall in effort with sheer spirit and willpower as Emilia cleared out the street, block by block.

"Go, hurry! Nothing's going to get in your way right now, so run, run!"

Clearly marking the streets she'd secured with ice, Emilia guided the fleeing people of the capital who'd fallen behind toward an escape route. Meanwhile, she ran against the flow of people heading for the walls, pushing further and further toward the heart of the city.

"Someone's doing this on purpose...!"

The 'enemies' appearing one after another were being brought into being in defiance of natural phenomena.
In other words, this was happening through someone's deliberate will, like magic or the Curse Arts. If there was a caster behind it, stopping them was the only real solution.

There was a limit to how much Emilia could freeze.
Fortunately, Emilia's mana reserves were exceptionally large for a person, so she could still keep moving, but between the fight with Madelyn, Mezoreia joining the battle, and now building staircases in and out of the wall and fighting off these endlessly appearing 'enemies,' she was starting to feel genuinely worn out.

"Being a little more energetic than most people is supposed to be my strong point...!"

Feeling frustrated at her own lack of power, Emilia dashed on through the streets of the capital.
Heading straight for the Crystal Palace would let her move faster, but along the way she had to protect people being attacked by the 'enemies.' With that added in, this was as fast as she could go.
Even as she thought this through, she spotted another group struggling desperately not to be noticed by the 'enemies' in the street—

"I'll clear a path right now!"

Four 'enemies' that looked like they'd become obstacles for the group trying to get out into the street — the moment their backs were turned, she leapt down from a rooftop, and before they could notice her landing and turn around, a flash of her ice blade――.

"――"

The pale blue-glowing sword of ice swept through the air, and the four 'enemies' caught by the strike turned into statues of ice.
Watching the 'enemies,' frozen mid-expression of shock, freeze solid and confirming they were properly frozen through, Emilia returned the ice sword in her hand back to mana and turned toward the shadows of the street.

"It's safe now! From here on, just follow the ice as a marker and you can get outside!"

"Ah, th-thank you. You saved us."

At Emilia's call, a reply came from beyond the shadows.
Emilia raised a hand at the words of thanks, thinking that once the people emerging from hiding had safely crossed the street, she'd resume heading toward the center.
Or so she thought, but then—

"Wow, so many children..."

In Emilia's wide-eyed view, nearly twenty people emerged from the shadow of the street. Moreover, most of them were children who looked to be around ten years old, and all of them had black hair.
Since black hair itself was rare, Emilia blinked in surprise at what a remarkably unusual group this was.
Since many of them didn't resemble each other enough to be family, she got the impression that they probably weren't related.
Then, one of the few adults in the group waved a hand at Emilia.

"Well, well, you really saved us there! You see, we've had our hands full just fleeing and hiding. We were just about at the point of deciding whether I ought to play decoy."

"Really? Then I'm glad it didn't come to that. It should be safe from here too, so don't panic, and everyone work together, okay?"

"Understood. My heartfelt thanks, young miss! Now then, my dear! Miss Katya!"

"D-don't call out so loud... What if those things come back, huh...!"

The cheerfully laughing blond young man's call was answered by a woman at the very back of the group. Emilia was mildly surprised to see the wheelchair she sat in, her line of sight lower than the others.

"I've only ever seen one that Subaru made, until now."

A wheelchair was a convenient tool for transporting someone with impaired legs, but that sort of device wasn't something one saw often. The only one Emilia knew of was the actual one Subaru had gone out of his way to design and build for a certain sleeping girl.
That very wheelchair, now that Subaru and Rem had been sent flying off, currently sat unused back at Roswaal Manor, but—

"Katya-san, please don't be so angry. ...I understand feeling anxious, being separated from your fiancé."

"D-don't say unnecessary things! You're one to talk, you've been fretting over those little brats this whole time too...! Don't pin it all on me."

"I don't mean to do that."

While Emilia's attention had been captured by the woman in the wheelchair, she was slow to notice the figure standing behind the woman, pushing the wheelchair.
Holding the handles at the back of the wheelchair, pushing the chair, was a blue-haired girl—

"――Rem?"

"――"

The name slipped from Emilia's lips almost unconsciously, and the girl's head snapped up, her eyes meeting Emilia's.
Emilia stared intently at the face of the wide-eyed girl looking back at her. Seeing this girl with her eyes open for the first time, she nevertheless bore a face identical to that of a girl Emilia knew well.
And no wonder — she'd heard that this girl and that other girl were twins. From none other than Emilia's own dependable knight.

"Do you know me?"

The girl — Rem — frowned, looking at Emilia with suspicion.
At the girl's question, one hand pressed to her own chest, Emilia caught her breath. Caught between the two of them, the woman in the wheelchair looked back and forth between Emilia and Rem's faces.
And then,

"A-another acquaintance of yours? Just how many people are looking for you—- mmph!"

"Rem!"

Leaping past the head of the sour-faced woman muttering something under her breath, Emilia closed the distance in long strides and took hold of Rem's hand.
Rem's eyes went wide at Emilia's sudden movement, but with no room to spare for that surprise, Emilia clutched Rem's hand tight, her eyes welling up at the sight of the girl standing right in front of her.

"You're awake... Rem's awake! Amazing! This is huge! I have to tell Ram and Subaru right away!"

"W-wait, please, just who exactly are you..."

"Um, Ram sent word that she'd meet up outside, so she should already be out there, right? Ugh! Subaru always goes and gets lost at times like this... um, um..."

"Please, listen to me!"

Emilia's mind was in such a scramble from the shock of it all that it was none other than Rem who put a stop to her train of thought.
Still gripped by the hand, Rem stared at Emilia with something close to anger,

"Are you also... someone who knows me as Rem, the previous me?"

"Ah, um, no, that's a bit difficult to explain. I don't remember the time when you were awake either. So it's strange, but it's kind of like a first meeting."

"I... I don't understand what that means..."

"Um, I'm not very good at explaining things either, so I'm worried I won't be able to say this properly, but..."

Faced with Rem's bewildered expression, Emilia searched apologetically for what to say.
Being able to talk like this with a Rem who was awake was an incredibly happy thing, but to Emilia, this girl was Ram's little sister, a girl who had slept continuously for over a year. And on top of that, a girl who'd had her 'memories' from that awakened time stolen from her by the power of the Sin Archbishop of Gluttony.
As for Emilia herself, she had no real sense of it beyond believing — from what Subaru had told her, and from how closely this girl resembled Ram — that it must be true that her own 'memories' with Rem had been stolen too.
Still, there was something Emilia understood from what Rem had said, and something Emilia herself understood.
And that was—

"Rem, do you maybe not remember anything from before you woke up?"

"...Well, I don't really have a sense of what 'before' and 'after' waking up even means, but yes, that's right."

"...I see. So that's how it is, after all."

There had been a small hope that perhaps the awakened Rem properly retained 'memories' of time spent with Emilia and the others, and that she might be able to tell them what their relationship had been like before.
That hope, unfortunately, was not to be granted, but—

"But there's nothing at all to worry about. It might feel really, really scary not knowing all sorts of things, but I'll help too, and Ram and Subaru are here for you!"

"――. What are you to me, exactly?"

"If I had to describe our relationship, I guess it would be like a guest and a maid. But I don't think that's all Ram and I are to each other, so I want to be more than just that with Rem too."

"――"

"Lending a hand when you're in trouble, thinking things through together when you're worried, standing beside you and facing hard things together... would it be no good to explain it as that kind of relationship?"

If asked what she was to Rem, even Emilia herself didn't really know.
Their relationship had been smashed down to zero, and had to be rebuilt from there. So all she could convey was her vision — what kind of relationship she wanted to rebuild.

"I want to get along with Rem. Like, let's work really hard together!"

That was Emilia's honest, unvarnished thought — her vision for the future.

"――"

Hearing Emilia's answer, Rem stayed wide-eyed, opening and closing her lips several times.
But her thoughts wouldn't quite turn into words, and her lips kept opening and closing. As an awkward stretch of time threatened to pass by—

"My dear, I think she's on your side."

"Flop-san..."

"I've met all sorts of people in my time as a merchant, and someone who can speak this straightforwardly is a rare find indeed. I'm sure you can trust her."

It was the blond young man called Flop who said this to the hesitating Rem.
At his cheerful, easygoing affirmation, Rem furrowed her brow, then looked at Emilia once more. Emilia met that gaze squarely, chin up, refusing to lose to it.
At Emilia's attitude, Rem let out a small breath, and,

"...I think I can tell that you're someone who knows me, and that you mean no harm."

Hearing her say so, hesitant as it was, Emilia's chest tightened with emotion. As Emilia's eyes widened at this, Rem narrowed her pale blue eyes slightly, and,

"Um, you mentioned the name Subaru just now..."

"Huh? Yes, I did. Subaru is someone who's been really, really worried about you, and has always treasured you so much..."

"――. If that's true..."

Lowering her eyes at Emilia's answer, Rem's gaze flicked toward the depths of the alley — the direction her group had just come from. Strictly speaking, it wasn't so much looking into the alley as looking back along the path they'd traveled.
Thinking about what that meant, Emilia leaned forward. "Could it be—"

"Was Subaru with you? Is Subaru okay? He's not doing anything reckless, is he?"

"You have that same impression of him too. That he's someone who often does reckless things."

"Mm, that's right. He's such a handful... ah! Oh, that reminds me."

"What is it?"

"My name is Emilia, just Emilia. If you've forgotten, then I should properly introduce myself first."

Emilia might remember Rem's name, but Rem didn't know Emilia's — and if she didn't tell her, she'd never be able to call her by name.
Hearing Emilia introduce herself, Rem's eyes widened slightly, and then,

"Emilia-san..."

"Yes, that's right. So, Rem, about Subaru — is he over there?"

"He is over there, that much is certain, but..."

At that, Rem's words trailed off, and her expression clouded slightly. Seeing this, Emilia's brow rose with a bad feeling.
Given the state of the capital, and the fact that Rem, Flop, the girl in the wheelchair, and the children were all traveling together without him, Subaru acting separately from the group didn't seem very Subaru-like at all.
And yet, if Subaru was acting separately from Rem and the others—

"He's gone and done something reckless again... I have to go help him, quick!"

"The way you jumped to that conclusion right away just confirms it, doesn't it — that he really is that kind of person."

"That's right. Subaru has this habit of always trying to look cool, so I'm always worried about him."

"He really is that kind of person, isn't he..."

Rem nodded, seemingly convinced, at each of Emilia's assessments of Subaru.
Being able to exchange words like this with Rem was a happy thing for Emilia too, but the urgency of the situation didn't allow for a long, leisurely chat.
And something happened just then that made that urgency even more pressing—

"—! Wh-what was that!? What's going on!?"

Suddenly, a tremendous explosion echoed from somewhere far off, and the woman in the wheelchair, startled into flinching, looked around anxiously.
It was hard to see from her angle, but from where Emilia stood, she could see a pillar of red flame rise up further into the depths of the capital, followed by trailing black smoke.

It was an enormous explosion.
The air itself wasn't disturbed in a way that suggested magic had caused it. Perhaps a fire crystal or something similar had ignited.

"That direction is..."

"Could that be where Subaru is?"

"――. Yes."

Looking toward the direction of the explosion, Rem, her voice catching, nodded at Emilia's words.
At once, Emilia couldn't help but feel certain that the explosion had something to do with Subaru. She wanted to rush over there immediately and reunite with him.
But leaving Rem here was also incredibly difficult to do, considering how Ram and Subaru would feel about it, and Emilia found herself torn between wanting to do this and wanting to do that, on the verge of panic.

"...H-hey, are you going toward that explosion? If so, then, then take this girl with you too. She'll be useful."

"Huh?"

"Katya-san?"

The one who put a stop to Emilia's confusion was Katya, the woman in the wheelchair, who had been thoroughly startled by the sound of the explosion.
Fidgeting, glancing at Emilia and looking away again and again, she said,

"Look, you can use healing magic, can't you? With that, it should be fine even if things over there get a little reckless. Besides... you've been glancing over there the whole time anyway."

"...I think it's you who's been glancing over there the whole time, Katya-san."

"Never mind about me! T-Todd, that guy, look, he's the type who clings to life no matter what. He's bound to come strolling back with a perfectly calm face no matter what happens. But your acquaintance, the child — he might not be as tough as Todd."

"But..."

Katya's clumsy way of putting it, gruff as it was, was full of consideration for Rem's feelings. Perhaps that was exactly why it came through so clearly.
Rem couldn't bring herself to nod obediently at Katya's words, but seeing Rem's indecision, Katya raised her voice, saying "Just go already!" and continued,

"From here on, this person has already... look, she's taken care of things, hasn't she. This... what, you, silver hair and long ears — how ominous."

"Ah, if we talk about that it might trouble you, so please don't look right now."

Realizing that if people found out she was a Half-Elf it might frighten them here in the Empire too, Emilia covered both her ears with her hands, hoping they'd forget about it.
Eyeing Emilia's reaction suspiciously, Katya turned back to look at Rem once more,

"As for me, you're worrying too much. That worn-out pretty boy over there will take me the rest of the way. You go ahead and do what you need to do, properly..."

"――"

"A-and if it's not too much trouble, keep an eye out to see if that Todd fellow's messed something up. That's all from me! Now, hurry up and stop dawdling..."

As she spoke, Katya turned the wheels of her own wheelchair and moved away from Rem. At Katya's decision to pull away from her own hands, Rem let out a small "ah" and lowered her eyes.
But after squeezing her eyes shut once, tightly,

"Flop-san, could I leave Katya-san in your care?"

"Ah, consider it firmly entrusted! Now then, what's one more final push, when we've made it this far together with our impostor crown prince and the rest?"

"Yes. ――Katya-san, thank you."

At Flop's confident assurance, thumping his chest, Rem bowed her head deeply. And at one last word of thanks, Katya turned away with a "hmph," her cheeks reddening.
At the heartwarming exchange between Rem and Katya, Emilia's eyes softened too.

"Once we get through this, tell me what you've been up to until today, okay? I really, really want to hear it, and so do Ram and Subaru and everyone else."

"I don't think it'll make for an interesting story, but... understood."

When Emilia held out her hand, Rem hesitated for just a moment before taking it and shaking it back.
Smiling at the feel of that hand, Emilia then gently pulled the clasped hand toward herself. Startled, wide-eyed, Rem stumbled forward and fell into Emilia's chest.
Just like that, Emilia scooped Rem up into her arms with a "there we go!" and,

"Sorry. There's no time to take it slow, so let me carry you a bit quickly!"

"Y-you're strong, aren't you."

"Yep, that's right. ――Everyone, take care! Let's meet again later!"

Carrying Rem princess-style, Emilia called out to Katya, Flop, and the others around her, then leapt from the spot with a "hup!" up onto the roof of a building.

"My dear! Emilia-jō! Take care yourselves as well!"

"Y-you'd better come back properly...!"

Receiving their cheers of support, Emilia looked down at Rem in her arms,

"Hold on tight, okay? I'm going to run pretty fast!"

"――. What kind of relationship did you and I have before, I wonder."

Clinging tightly, Rem murmured as though squeezing the words out. Emilia had no clear answer to give her for that, but,

"That's the same thing I want to know too!"

Declaring this firmly, she took off running once more, carrying Rem, deeper into the capital.
Holding tight to the feel of Rem's arms clinging to her, Emilia ran, and ran, and kept on running, and then――.


"――That's far enough."


――Reaching that very moment, the reunion was at last fulfilled.
