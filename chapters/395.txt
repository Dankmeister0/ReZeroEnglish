"So this 'nameless' thing, that's what you call the folks that got hit by that Great Sin Archbishop, right? The ones who suddenly seemed to just spring up out of nowhere, looking beaten down."

"Given the circumstances, that's the reasonable conclusion. Matching embroidery was sewn into their unit uniforms. ...That we have no idea whatsoever of their identities is nothing but galling."

"And there's about thirty more folks in a similar position... that's a tough one. Not even knowing if they were there or not, that's a hard thing to know how to handle."

The treatment of "Gluttony's" victims — those whose "names" had been devoured — was, in a way, harder to deal with than "Lust's" victims. After all, who the victims even were had vanished, across the board, from the memories of everyone concerned. And with the victims themselves unconscious besides, there was next to no clue by which to trace their identities.
The members of the "White Dragon's Scales" might be somewhat better off, in that at least their affiliation was known, but beyond that, no one knew anything. Was that the extent of the help anyone could offer — knowing who to look after?

"All the 'nameless' found so far, they're unconscious, right? What's the healer over there — sis, no wait, bro — diagnosing about that?"

"...This too is the same as with 'Lust.' I don't know the reason for the lack of consciousness. My diagnosis is just that they're simply sleeping. But even that isn't certain. Someone who's just sleeping should be growing weaker, but there's the example of Rem-chan."

At Felt's words, Ferris said this, looking toward Subaru.
A year ago, Rem, who had likewise suffered "Gluttony's" harm, had received the same diagnosis from Ferris as the "nameless" people. The diagnosis was exactly identical. And to this day, Rem had never once woken, yet neither had her health deteriorated.
As if she were sleeping in appearance, yet her body demanded none of the workings needed to sustain life. Her hair didn't grow, there was no waste elimination. It was a mysterious state.

"Regardless of whether we know who they are or not, protecting those people is simple. They'll be far more bedridden than any ordinary bedridden person, so all we need to do is keep them lying in bed. ...Though really, I wish someone who knew who they were could be at their side."

"That's exactly the part that can't be done, that's the whole problem, ain't it? Tough thing, that."

The talking-to was something Subaru did for Rem, half-knowing it was meaningless. What would truly be of use to Rem, in the true sense, was nothing but defeating "Gluttony."
Even knowing that, offering that small resistance was Subaru's own self-satisfaction.

Ricardo, raising his voice, denied Subaru's gloom with a face free of malice. Glancing that way, Ricardo was scrunching his huge frame awkwardly, sitting across two chairs pushed together to join the meeting.
He still looked much the same bold, hearty man as ever, but there was one part that was different. That was the extravagantly wrapped bandage, and the presence of a right arm missing from the elbow down.

"Don't make such a pathetic face, bro. Sure, I screwed up, that's a fact, but I kept my life. Considering everything this time, that's a way better outcome."

Noticing Subaru's gaze, Ricardo raised his severed right arm and bared his fangs. In the fierce fighting at the control tower, it seemed Ricardo had lost his right arm to a blow from "Gluttony."
Subaru had heard this from Julius, who had been fighting alongside him. That Ricardo had lost the arm shielding Julius, and that Ricardo himself had no memory of it.
As if to prove that, Ricardo shifted the direction of his gaze to beside Subaru,

"Still, that pretty boy over there was an acquaintance of yours, bro? Glad you got to meet up with the guy you were looking for. Helped me out earlier, he really did."

"——"

Calling Julius a pretty boy, Ricardo took the stance of addressing a total stranger. From the flow of the conversation, Julius had likely deflected Ricardo's questioning with the same words he'd first used to try to throw Subaru off.
Since it must have been Julius who had carried the wounded Ricardo back to the shelter, Ricardo's final expression of thanks could be inferred to be directed at that part.

Even so, leaving this mismatch as it was, was far too painful to bear.
And besides, the topic had now reached "Gluttony." It was time to bring it up.

"There's something I want to tell everyone. Something important, related to how to handle the 'nameless' too."

Standing up, Subaru gathered the eyes of the assembly onto himself.
At present, the one with the most information regarding how to deal with the "nameless" was Subaru. He naturally felt the hope for a breakthrough take root in everyone's gaze, but Subaru shook his head.

"Sorry to disappoint your hopes, but honestly, this isn't the kind of thing that turns into hope right away. But it's something that needs to be said."

"What, that's an unsettling way to preface it. What are you gonna say?"

As if trying to ease the tension in the room even a little, Anastasia spoke words teasing Subaru in her soft, easy manner. But she herself was the one who most needed to brace herself.
That fact might well overturn her current state of mind from the very foundation.

Subaru drew a small breath, and swept his gaze over everyone's faces. Then, finally turning his eyes toward Julius, the latter, cheek stiffening slightly, still nodded.
Confirming that, Subaru gestured toward Julius beside him and posed the question.

"Does anyone know the name of the man standing here like this?"

"——"

At Subaru's question, silence dominated the assembly hall.
That silent span of time, however, did not arise from a failure to understand. Everyone had sensed the intent behind Subaru's question and turned their thoughts to Julius's position.
And that, on top of that, no one uttered a single word meant that there was no one who had any idea of Julius's identity, and—

"Al! What about you? No recollection of this guy's face?"

"Huh? What's this, bro? Why me all of a sudden?"

Called on so abruptly, Al raised a voice of genuine surprise. That reaction alone was proof enough of whether Julius existed in his memory or not, but even so, Subaru couldn't help but make sure.
Subaru put his hands on the round table and leaned toward Al, questioning.

"You really don't know the reason? It's what you and I have in common. Doesn't that ring a bell about this guy? How about it? Answer me."

"...Ah, that's what this is about. Sorry, bro. I get what you're trying to say, but I don't think I can help. There's no place for that guy in my head."

"Really, truly? Just think a little more seriously..."

"That's enough. —That's plenty, Subaru."

Al sensed the true intent behind the interrogation — grounded in the idea of "summoning from another world" — but he never nodded in agreement. Even so, it was Julius himself who stopped Subaru from giving up.
Julius, wearing a lonely smile, patted Subaru's shoulder and bowed to Al.

"I'm sorry for placing excessive expectations on you. Allow me to apologize for my rudeness."

"No reason to apologize. Whatever grounds there might've been... dunno if there were or weren't, but there's no need to say sorry to me."

Waving his one arm, Al turned his face away from Julius.
There was no hint of falsehood in that reaction. That Al didn't remember Julius was, in all likelihood, an undeniable fact. If so, then one half of Subaru's speculation was incorrect.

The world one originated from had nothing to do with the influence of "Gluttony's" Authority.
If that was the case, then after all, the most likely explanation remained the Witch Factor within Subaru's body. And, narrowing it down further, Beatrice's "Door Crossing," and the isolation from the outside world that came with it.

"From what I gather of the conversation, Subaru-dono — might we consider that young man one of our own concerned parties... and moreover, someone who held quite an important position?"

Watching the exchange among the three of them, Wilhelm was the one to voice that inference. The old swordsman's conclusion was likely one most of the others had roughly arrived at as well.
Subaru nodded to Wilhelm, then turned back toward Julius,

"This is Julius. Julius Juukulius. As you've all probably guessed, he's one of those whose 'name' was devoured by 'Gluttony,' becoming 'nameless.' But his case is different from the others who lost consciousness. —His own consciousness remains."

"There are cases like that? Forgotten by everyone around, but he remembers himself...? Then, is this person a colleague of someone among us?"

With a look of disbelief, Ferris's gaze went back and forth between Subaru's and Julius's faces over and over. To Ferris's trembling words, Reinhard dipped his chin, saying "It would seem so."
The "Sword Saint" gazed at Julius with quiet eyes,

"Before the meeting, I was asked the same question by Subaru and the others. He... Julius, was surely someone acquainted with both myself and Ferris. Or perhaps our relationship went beyond mere acquaintance. A friend, perhaps."

"...At the very least, I considered you both friends. If you two held the same measure of friendship toward me, there could be no greater honor than that."

"Friends... then, is Julius a knight too? One of the Order of Pegasus Knights?"

Both being called a friend by a stranger, Reinhard and Ferris were left in confusion. Taking that reaction as unavoidable, Julius corrected them, half resigned.
Looking down on the exchange between the three of them from above, Subaru's insides burned with anger.

How twisted, how sickening a sight this was.

Subaru didn't know in detail how the three of them had met, through what circumstances they had built their friendship, or what kind of camaraderie they had shared.
Even so, the three of them, as colleagues, as friends, had surely treated one another with an easy familiarity and closeness as a matter of course. That solid bond now showed no trace of its former self.

When Rem's "name" had been devoured, and everyone forgot her existence, Subaru had once thought that perhaps no greater sorrow than that could exist in this world.
But what of Julius's state now? This one-sided sense of loss, left behind by everything in the entire world — if this was not sorrow, then what was it?

Sorrow is not something to be compared. But even so, this was simply too cruel.

"...It's not just a story of him being one ordinary Pegasus Knight, I'd wager."

And then, Anastasia's voice suddenly cut across the painful first meeting between those friends.
With thoughtfulness settling over her soft features, she touched her chin and licked her lips. Then, pointing at Ricardo, and then pointing at Julius,

"It was Julius-san who carried the badly wounded Ricardo back here. Treated Ricardo, talked a little, and then went straight out to look for someone... that reaction just now, that's what it was about, huh."

"Anastasia-sama..."

A second first meeting between lord and vassal, one that should never have had to happen. Recalling the bitter memory of that moment, Julius called his lady's name with a pained expression.
But Anastasia had not noticed the trembling loyalty woven into those words. She thought for a moment, then held up a single finger.

"Julius-san's case is a pretty rare kind of 'nameless.' Dunno how many people in the city are in the same state, but it's possible more like him could turn up too, same as the unconscious 'nameless.' This is a grave matter as well, no question. Right?"

Turning her gaze away from Julius, Anastasia steered the topic back toward the broader discussion. Julius's identity had been set aside, in this form, as one more problem that couldn't be dealt with for now.
It was a rather cruel treatment for Julius as things stood, but in this room, Subaru was the only one who could even feel indignation over that fact.

"Given all these problems... there's one proposal I have. Mind if I bring it up?"

"A proposal, meaning?"

Leaving Subaru's inner feelings behind, the meeting's attention turned once more to Anastasia.
At the center of that attention, Anastasia swept her gaze around the room, and finally, looking toward Julius and Subaru,

"The Witch Cult's victims... 'Lust's' victims, and the 'nameless' from 'Gluttony.' For both, drawing a solution out of the Great Sin Archbishops themselves is hopeless — that much we agree on, right?"

"In the sense that getting them to talk is difficult, that's correct. But an overly pessimistic view could cloud our judgment in turn."

"I'm not thinking about it that badly either, don't worry. I just wanna say, maybe there's more than one way to drag out an answer."

"A different way to reach an answer?"

Unable to grasp the meaning of Anastasia's words, Subaru found himself echoing her.
Besides the perpetrators — the Great Sin Archbishops themselves — was there some other way forward, if such a method existed, it might be something close to a bargain with a witch.

For an instant, the thought that the "Witch of Greed" might know the answer flickered through Subaru's mind — a choice he shouldn't make.

"So, what do you mean? Please, just tell us plainly."

But instead of Subaru, who shook his head to deny the idea, it was Emilia who pressed Anastasia for her true meaning. Anastasia tapped her own head with a finger,

"If we can't get it out of the ill-tempered Great Sin Archbishop, we oughta just ask someone else who's likely to know. —There is such a person, isn't there. Someone in this country likely to know."

"Surely not..."

Sensing the meaning of Anastasia's words, someone spoke with a hoarse breath.
But unlike everyone else around him, who had realized it, Subaru had no idea what this statement referred to.

"I don't get it. Quit stalling, just say it plainly."

At the same level of understanding as Subaru, Felt demanded of Anastasia with irritation. At Felt's words, Anastasia gave a wry smile, said "Sorry, sorry," and then—

"—The Sage, Shaula."

"Huh?"

"The Pleiades Watchtower — the 'Sage' who should be there... if it's someone said to be able to see through everything in this world, wouldn't it make sense for her to know?"

And with that, she revealed the true intent behind her proposal.
