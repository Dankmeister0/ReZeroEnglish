Arc 5 Chapter 35: "Ambush and Surprise Attack"

The first strike came in without mercy, and it was stopped dead by teeth.

"――――"

Roy Alphard, 'Gluttony,' bit down on the tip of the whip and waved both empty hands as if to make a show of it. Alphard — Subaru had heard that name's ring before, too.

"That guy's got a star's name too——!"

"Subaru-sama, that discussion is over! I'm going in!"

As Subaru wrenched roughly at the whip, Alphard let go of his prize without much apparent attachment. Aiming at that same Alphard, Crusch launched a Hundred-Man Slash from the side.

A raging blade of wind swept across the lobby floor of the city hall, mowing down the lined-up chairs and everything atop the reception counter, sending it all flying in spectacular fashion.
Naturally, the slash showed no mercy to Alphard either, who stood within its range——

"Whoa, that's amazing! But as a party trick, it's kind of fun, y'know?"

"——?!"

The boy dodged the supposedly invisible blade of wind by throwing his body sharply backward. He touched his head to the floor with the momentum of a bridge, then rolled once, backward.
From a crouch, he took up a crouching-start stance, lifted his face, and grinned.

"As an attack, though, it'd catch your own allies too, and it's way too obvious — third-rate. Doesn't look all that appetizing to us, maybe!"

The instant he finished speaking, Alphard's body kicked off the floor and shot forward like a bullet.
His small frame leapt, baring sharp canines with his mouth wide open as he closed the distance — the sight gave the illusion of some ferocious stray dog. But the danger he posed was nothing like that of a stray dog.
Crusch held her sword vertically and swung it down to meet his face. But—

"Good form, but not enough polish! To us, that's less than an appetizer!"

"——Ah!"

Alphard's right hand swept up against the slash, and Crusch's sword was knocked aside with a high, ringing sound. Looking closer, one could see cloth wrapped around both of his hands, with dagger blades jutting out near the wrists.
A fighting style built around a dagger equipped on each hand, exploiting the speed and agility of his small frame.

The left-hand dagger, opposite the one that had parried her sword, aimed for Crusch's throat. She twisted her body to dodge it on reflex, but caught a kick from Alphard as he spun in midair, and her slender body was sent flying sideways.

"Ah, gh!"

"Crusch-san!"

"You, over there — this isn't the time to be looking away. If anything, you're the easiest target here, y'know!"

With Subaru's attention drawn to Crusch skidding across the floor and his gaze diverted, Alphard kicked off the ground again, aiming for him. In the dim light, the form of 'Gluttony,' clad only in rags, melted into the darkness, and Subaru momentarily lost sight of him——

"Sorry, but..."

"That he makes for an ideal decoy is well proven by his performance against 'Sloth.'"

"Hey?!"

By targeting the utterly defenseless Subaru, Alphard ended up exposing an opening of his own.
Subaru, painfully aware of his own helplessness, had sworn to himself not to act rashly even with blood rushing to his head. He bit down on his lip, using the pain to rein in his own consciousness — all so that the most capable person here could land the decisive blow.

A silent thrust from Julius pierced the small body suspended in midair.
Alphard twisted in the air on reflex, but the Sword Saint's slash cut cleanly across his torso, and 'Gluttony's' body rolled across the floor, scattering fresh blood.

"Whoo-ee! Now that's a surprise——"

"Then allow me to prolong that surprise. Bloom, my buds!"

Julius didn't let him finish his line, closing in along with a follow-up strike from the minor spirits as Alphard slapped the floor and sprang back to his feet.
The dimmed lobby lit up in rainbow colors, and from behind the advancing Julius, brilliant light surged toward 'Gluttony.'

"A spirit user!"

"I hope it suits your palate, gourmet that you are. Every one of these buds is a child that blooms into a magnificent flower, if I do say so myself."

"Man, I don't like that kind of pompous talk — neither us nor them, y'know!"

Seeing the brilliant light scorch the world before him, Alphard cried out and leapt backward in a great bound. Julius's slender sword pursued, its blade lashing out in every direction, driving Alphard — who tried to dodge sideways out of range — further and further back.

"——'Girl-chaser'!"

"Don't call me that, 'Yuuri'!"

"Take the 'Sword Princess' up to the top floor! Stop that broadcast!"

Concealing each other's real names as they called out, Julius declared he would take on Alphard.
As Subaru helped the coughing Crusch to her feet, he understood this was the most rational decision — and yet he couldn't bring himself to nod immediately.

The boy leaping around, spewing insults, was 'Gluttony.'
The sworn enemy he had pursued for over a year, always, endlessly. Defeating him had to be Subaru's greatest, most urgent goal, without question.
And to let that slip away, right in front of him——

"Un... derstood. Yuuri-sama, good fortune to you."

"——?!"

But before Subaru's inner conflict could resolve itself, Crusch, rising to her feet, answered Julius with those words first. Subaru looked up with a start, and saw a look of frustration flicker across the profile of Crusch's face right in front of him.

Crusch, too, was a victim who'd had her memories stolen by 'Gluttony.'
Of course she must have thought endlessly about avenging a humiliation she couldn't even remember. And still, she prioritized her duty and entrusted the fight against 'Gluttony' to someone else.
Setting emotion aside, it was obvious her strength wasn't sufficient for the task. Subaru's position and Crusch's, their thinking, were being asked to arrive at the very same choice, at this very same point.

"Well now, well now, what'll it be, I wonder? We don't mind if it's together, y'know? An empty-headed woman and a hollowed-out man — put 'em together and it might make a decent little snack before the main course. And that 'Yuuri' fellow over there — eating, gnawing, nibbling, licking, lapping, devouring, biting, tearing apart with your teeth, crushing between your jaws, overindulging! Gluttonizing! — sounds like it'd work out fine, y'know?"

"Don't waste your breath on idle chatter. This Yuuri has no intention of simply being eaten, you see!"

Even as the brilliant light whittled away his limited space, Alphard kept laughing it off with an air of composure. Julius pursued him, and their clashing blades rang out in a wild dance of steel.
In that moment, Julius and Subaru's eyes met for just an instant.
That gaze told Subaru nothing in words——

"Ah, damn it! Fine! I got it! Don't you dare lose, you hear me!"

"That's my line. You needn't win, but don't die. No — I won't let you die."

"Let's go, Lady 'Girl-chaser'!"

Clawing at his own head, Subaru abandoned his overwhelming frustration and broke into a run.
He'd have liked, at the very least, to take the lead ahead of Crusch, but the pathetic truth was that she was the one capable of reacting to a sudden ambush.
Chasing after Crusch's back as she pressed forward despite her wounds, Subaru and the others made for the stairs. Before dashing up, he took one last look back at Julius and Alphard fighting it out in the lobby.
The battle looked to be going Julius's way. But he couldn't let his guard down.

"Go on!"

"——Damn it!"

Julius, noticing Subaru's gaze, refused to let him worry about his safety even to the very end.
Cursing him inwardly as an unpleasant fellow, Subaru told himself he'd be in trouble if Julius went down before he could complain to his face directly. Then Subaru chased after Crusch, taking the stairs up at a run.
Wary of an ambush, the two passed through the landing and pressed on toward the top floor.
Along the way—

"Subaru-sama, I'm terribly sorry. If things were as they should be, you ought to have been the one to face 'Gluttony'—"

"Stop it, Crusch-san. Nobody thinks this is your fault."

Watching the state of the floor above, Crusch quietly voiced that apology. But the frustration was the same for her, too. Apologizing wouldn't heal either of their wounds.
And what he hated was that being apologized to made some part of him want to lash out and blame her.

"...Sorry, Rem. Wait just a little longer for me."

Calling the name of the girl still sleeping back at the mansion, Subaru sent out a heartfelt apology.
If he could, he wanted to race back this instant and tear that vile, sneering Archbishop of Sin limb from limb. If doing so would bring her back, what could possibly be wrong with it?

But the chaos it would unleash, the countless lives it would put at risk—
Being the kind of simple-minded person who never thought ahead about such things would surely have been far easier.
Even knowing that if he did such a thing, she would surely be furious with him when she woke.

"――――"

Holding his breath, crushing down the emotion welling up inside him, Subaru said nothing, and Crusch said nothing either. She merely closed her eyes as if regretting her apology, and resumed the march with herself in the lead.

The city hall was a five-story building, and Subaru and Crusch's group had already reached the fourth floor. The floors along the way held meeting rooms and paperwork offices, and from a quick check of the landing directory, it seemed safe to assume the broadcast room was on the top floor.
Which meant——

"That means 'Lust' is up there too, huh..."

"Yes, that's right. But given how wide this corridor is, could it really...?"

Peering down the fourth-floor corridor, Crusch furrowed her brow doubtfully.
Her doubt was only natural — Subaru harbored the same question himself.

The width of the fourth-floor passage was, at most, enough for four people to walk abreast.
The black dragon's build, seen from the plaza below, had looked as large as an elephant even from a distance. There was simply no way it could fit within the corridors of this building.
Of course, there was the possibility it hadn't used the corridors at all, instead smashing through walls and forcing its body into a room——

"What do you think?"

"At the very least, I think we can assume there's no ambush in the corridor. You'd agree with that too, right, Crusch-san? The problem is an ambush in the broadcast room... it's been this long since it went inside. No doubt it's preparing something."

"...Yes, I think so too. Do you suppose it's set a trap there, in the broadcast room?"

"If we think of it as the one place we're guaranteed to go, then yeah, no doubt about it. But the people who should've been in this city hall still haven't turned up. In the worst case, there could be a hostage situation, too..."

The more he thought about it, the more bad possibilities kept surfacing.
This wasn't the kind of problem that could simply be solved with brute force. Crusch's combat ability topped out at middling, and she'd admitted herself that magic wasn't something to be counted on. On Subaru's side, losing Beatrice from the front line stung badly. And looking closely, his right leg was seeping quite a bit of blood.

"'Nothing ventured, nothing gained,' they say, but I can't quite bring myself to charge in that recklessly. If the fight outside wraps up, reinforcements from below could turn the whole situation around in an instant, but..."

"If that happens, 'Lust' will have no reason to keep delaying the broadcast any longer. In the end, there's no choice but for you and me to handle this ourselves, Subaru-sama."

"The optimal method against an ambush..."

Crusch spoke firmly, staring intently at Subaru.
Feeling somewhat overwhelmed by the intensity in her gaze, Subaru swallowed hard.

"Um, Crusch-san?"

"Wilhelm told me. That in a situation exactly like this, you are the sort of person who can draw out the optimal answer. I believe that too."

"That's a heavy amount of trust to carry!"

Wilhelm's overestimation of him, combined with Crusch's expectations — she who had forgotten the days when Subaru's reputation had been far lower — piled on top of each other.
Nearly crushed under the weight of the expectations placed on him, Subaru thought as hard as he could, using up what time remained — not clearly delimited, but surely running short all the same.
And then, he decided.

"What can we do, against an ambush..."

"Yes."

"If they're waiting for us as a given... then let's break that given."

——There are several key elements to an ambush.

First, the location of the ambush. Since this tactic is built on the idea of waiting and unilaterally attacking the enemy from an advantageous position, this is the first essential element.
Second, the certainty that the target will actually appear at the ambush point. If you go through all the trouble of setting an ambush and the crucial target never shows up, there's no point.
And third, an estimate of when the target will arrive at the ambush site. If your focus lapses from waiting too long, you can't deliver the ambush's full effect.

Therefore, assuming 'Lust' had indeed set an ambush, this time all three of those elements were satisfied.
No matter what, Subaru and the others had to storm the 'broadcast room' within a 'limited amount of time,' 'without fail.' For the one setting the ambush, there could hardly be an easier prey to hunt.

"So we need to break that situation."

"I understand that. ...No, I am a noble too. Once I decided to trust you, Subaru-sama, I resolved not to keep saying so forever. I leave it to you."

One floor above the top floor where the broadcast room was located.
In other words, Subaru and Crusch had made their way up onto the rooftop, above the top floor itself, and there they prepared to carry out a plan to disrupt their opponent's strategy.
Crusch, who had seemed somewhat bewildered at first by Subaru's proposal, seemed to have steeled her resolve by now. This kind of decisiveness was a virtue that hadn't changed in her, before or after losing her memories.

"Honestly, I'd like to see how the fight in the plaza is going."

"If they sense us from below, our actions here will lose their meaning."

From the direction of the plaza, the faint clash of steel and Garfiel's cursing carried up even to this height. Meaning the battle down there was still ongoing. They couldn't count on help from that quarter.

"Even so..."

Looking all around the rooftop, Subaru gave a nod of understanding.
The claw marks scattered here and there across the ground were surely traces of the black dragon rampaging about unchecked. The fence and iron railing on the plaza side, blown clean away, were the damage from the magic Julius had unleashed.

Thinking to himself what a terrifyingly fearsome amount of power that must have taken, Subaru made a wide circuit of the rooftop, heading for the side opposite the plaza. Based on the floor plan they'd checked, the broadcast room should be in that section.
Naturally, Capella was surely waiting on that side for Subaru and the others to come charging up—

"Subaru-sama."

"Yeah? Give me a sec, I'm almost done setting up."

"It's a bit late to say this, but I've just realized something."

"...?"

Behind Subaru, who was rigging something to the iron railing, Crusch's voice dropped slightly in tone. When Subaru turned to look at her, puzzled, he found her looking at him with an oddly stiff expression.

"It seems I'm not very good with heights. Let's get this over with quickly."

"Huh, an unexpected weakness... Got it. All set, ready to go."

After confirming everything was firmly secured, Subaru gave Crusch a nod. She nodded back, her expression still strangely rigid, then quietly threw herself into Subaru's open arms.

"——Please don't let go of me."

"Crusch-san, saying stuff like that is gonna give a guy the wrong idea, you should probably dial it back."

"——?"

Subaru let out a wry chuckle at Crusch's puzzled tilt of the head, then blew out a breath. Then he hoisted up Crusch's body as she pressed close to his chest, and with that momentum, leaned his whole body out past the iron railing.

Naturally, gravity took hold of the two of them, and they plunged straight down along the wall of the building — and partway through that fall, the length of whip wound around Subaru's arm reached its limit.

"——!"

Bearing the weight of two people, Subaru gritted his teeth against the strain threatening to pop his shoulder from its socket. The momentum of the fall bent at an angle, and the two of them arced in a wide swing toward the wall of the city hall. There, at what he'd estimated to be the window of the broadcast room, Subaru stretched out both legs and smashed straight through——

"Haaa——!"

"Wha—?!"

Scattering shards of window glass, Subaru and Crusch's bodies tumbled into the broadcast room. For a moment, Subaru thought he heard Crusch let out a small shriek, but he pretended not to have heard it and released her from his arms.
The two of them planted their hands on the floor, immediately scanning the room — and then they found it.

"――――"

The black dragon, frozen stiff, eyes wide, staring in blank astonishment at the two who'd come crashing in.
Its absurdly huge frame, just as they'd seen from the rooftop, had been crammed into the room, wings folded, head raised — and it had apparently been aiming its cannon-mouth at the door leading in from the corridor.
No doubt its plan had been to burn to death whoever properly entered through that door — but that aim had missed its mark entirely.

If anything, its oversized body was a hindrance, putting it at a disadvantage in a room with no room to maneuver.
The black dragon spread its wings to fight back against the braced Subaru and Crusch, but—

"Crusch-san!"

"Yes!"

Freed from her fear of heights, Crusch answered boldly and let loose a slash.
A blade of wind cut clean through the black dragon's spread wing at its root, and then, leaping in, Crusch delivered a direct blow that severed one of its forelegs. 'Lust' let out a scream, and thick, blackish blood sprayed out.

"OROOOGAAAAAH!!"

"Watch out! Get down—— oh?!"

Screaming in pain, Capella thrashed its wings and head, destroying the room around it.
Though the room was somewhat larger than a standard one, there was no way its structure could hold up against a creature the size of an elephant thrashing about. Trying to avoid getting caught up in the destruction, Subaru covered his head and tried to flee from it — and then he noticed.

——At the black dragon's feet, a single bound girl was desperately writhing.

"——!"

His eyes locked instantly with the tear-filled eyes of that girl.
In that instant, he realized: when 'Lust's' initial ambush attack had failed, it had already prepared an effective hostage against them. Fury ignited within him.

His focus sharpened to a razor's edge, and Subaru's body chose to press forward rather than flee.
Somehow slipping past the tail sweeping overhead, he slid in beneath the black dragon toward the girl at its feet. He scooped up her small, trembling body, and while at it, swung the whip he'd reeled back into his hand with all his strength against the dragon's back. No visible damage. It just made him feel a little better.
But Crusch's blow was another matter entirely.

"Wait! Wait! This isn't— gh!"

"No arguing! Face the consequences for the chaos and calamity you've brought upon this city!"

Crusch's blade showed no mercy toward the black dragon, which curled its head in with an oddly human-like motion.
Once forced onto the defensive, Capella exposed such astonishing fragility and helplessness against a blade of steel that it was almost anticlimactic, given all the fuss about being immortal.

The other wing, curled protectively over its head, was half severed, and as the massive body let out a scream, Crusch's slender, long leg drove a kick into it. However great the difference from Subaru's own leg strength, the enormous body was staggered by the force and driven backward — toward the window opposite the one Subaru and Crusch had smashed through.

Neither of the black dragon's wings had begun to regenerate yet.
For all its boasting of immortality, its regenerative power wasn't operating at a speed one could call threatening.

"This ends it——!"

"Wai——"

Deaf to any pleading, Crusch's flurry of slashes tore through the black dragon's torso, neck, and wings one after another, slamming its massive body into the wall and, together with the window frame, sending it collapsing and flying out into the open air.
The black dragon, blasted through the shattered wall, spread its wings on reflex, but one was torn off at the root and the other slashed vertically like a hook-tear — neither in any condition to bear flight.

"――――っ"

Unable to regenerate in time, the black dragon plummeted straight down without even a sound.
Just a few seconds later, the sound of 'Lust' slamming into the ground below reached them. A sound like flesh striking a wall, like a wet rag dropped onto a floor.

"I'll confirm and stay on guard. Subaru-sama, please see to that girl."

"Ah, yeah, got it."

Crusch walked toward the broken window frame the black dragon had fallen through, never letting her guard down. Feeling a sense of reliability from her back, Subaru gently set free the girl he'd rescued amid the chaos.
The girl blinked rapidly, looking up at Subaru with a frightened expression, as if she didn't understand what state she was in.
No wonder. Anyone would be terrified after an experience like being glared at by a dragon.

"It's okay. That bad dragon just now got taken down by a strong big sister. I can't take too long here, but... where are the others?"

"Ah, um..."

"Might be hard to believe, but I'm on your side. I came to help you all. There's a lot to sort out before the scary people come back. Can you help me out?"

Kneeling down, matching his eye level to the girl's, Subaru spoke gently.
It was an unconscious bit of mirroring on Subaru's part, one that oddly always won over small children. Whether it was that attitude of his that helped, the girl seemed to regain some composure, and after a few gasping breaths, she said,

"O-over there, there's a room... everyone's, in there."

"They're locked in? That...?"

The room the girl pointed to was a smaller room even further inside, past the broadcast room.
Or rather — this room probably wasn't the broadcast room at all. It was a large room, but there was nothing resembling broadcast equipment anywhere in it. Even granting that it might be some kind of Magic Vessel, there was nothing here that even looked the part. This was probably the broadcast prep room, and the room the girl pointed to was the actual, official broadcast room.

Glancing toward it, Subaru hesitated to voice the rest of what he'd meant to ask the girl. What he'd been about to ask next was whether the people locked in there were alive or dead.
But asking a child something like that was far too cruel, and thoughtless besides.
Subaru stroked the head of the still-trembling girl, then slowly made his way toward the room.

"――――"

His heart pounded loud and fast, and Subaru noticed his back was damp with a cold sweat.
It was strange — his self-initiated Tarzan jump hadn't made him this tense, yet now his throat had gone suddenly dry. A bad premonition, a dreadful premonition, that wouldn't go away.

"Subaru-sama?"

"I'm fine. Checking now. What about 'Lust'?"

"...This is fine too. For some reason, it's not moving, still in the position it fell in."

Crusch answered while keeping watch on 'Lust' below. Hearing her answer, Subaru took a deep breath and approached the door, reaching his hand toward the doorknob.
There was still a possibility that Witch Cultists lurked inside the broadcast room. Given that, Subaru checking the room like this wasn't really the better option.
And yet, for some reason, there was a conviction in his chest that he had nothing to worry about there. And in fact, that thought wasn't wrong.

Because, in fact, there were no Witch Cultists inside — what was there instead was——

"――――"

A great mass of silent gazes stared at Subaru, struck speechless.
Or rather — he merely thought he felt himself being watched. He had no idea how they were observing the world, whatever way that was. He didn't want to know, either.
He simply stood there, speechless. No voice would come. This was what it meant to lose the power of words. His mind froze solid, and he could think of nothing at all. And yet, there was one thing he understood.

——The true identity of the sound he'd heard beneath that grating voice, during the broadcast heard back at the shelter.

"...this... is..."

As if in answer to Subaru's cracked voice, that same sound reverberated through the room.
It could have sounded like it was welcoming him, or fearing him, or rejecting him, or delighting in him, or feeling nothing at all——

Countless wingbeats filled the broadcast room.
In the dim room, a great mass of red-glowing compound eyes seemed to writhe, staring, rolling, at Subaru.

Filling the broadcast room to bursting, there were flies. An enormous swarm of them.
And each one was as large as a human being, body after body after body——

"——AAAAH!!"

"――――ッ!"

Behind Subaru, whose mind had gone utterly blank, a scream suddenly rang out.
Flinching, Subaru spun around to look, even as the flies — reacting to the scream by flapping their oversized wings within the confined room — filled his view.

"Ehehahaha! Idiot, idiot! You dumb-fuck lumps of meat, twisting your empty heads trying to outsmart me — is that sugar you got stuffed in there instead of a brain, huh?! Kyahahahaha!"

Cackling in that grating, high-pitched laugh, a girl stood with the writhing, agonized Crusch pinned beneath her feet.
That familiar, vicious voice unmistakably belonged to——

"It's Capella-chan, dess! Kyahahahaha!"

Sticking out her tongue, striking a pose with a wink, Capella stood there — and at her feet, Crusch was coughing up a great gush of fresh blood, her eyes rolled back to white.
