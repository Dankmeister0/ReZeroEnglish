Arc 2 Chapter 7: "A Gathering at Roswaal Manor"

In the dining hall he'd been dragged to — still locked in a joint-lock hold, all the way to "the place for breakfast" — a curly-haired girl already seated there said this by way of greeting.

"From what I saw watching from above, that's about the size of it, I suppose. ...You must be quite seriously touched in the head."

Subaru rotated his still-aching elbow, screwing up his face into an exaggerated scowl.

"The hell kind of thing is that to say the second we meet, you loli."

"What is that word supposed to mean? I've never heard it before, yet somehow it feels offensive, I suppose."

"It means too young to be a romance option. I'm not really into the younger-girl thing."

"...Being able to talk this rudely to Betty is almost pitiable, in its own way, I suppose."

She said it with a look of pity, leaning back in her chair and sighing. Then she picked up the glass on the table and let the amber liquid slide smoothly down her throat.

The glass's shape was close to that of a wine glass. Surely the contents weren't alcohol... were they?

Catching Subaru's suspicious stare, the girl smiled meaningfully and tilted the glass toward him.

"What, could it be you want a taste?"

"Huh, but that'd be an indirect kiss. Feels like the story's moving a little fast."

"I try to tease you out of spite and you go all bashful on me?! Now I'm the one embarrassed, I suppose!"

The girl snapped angrily at Subaru, who pressed his fingertips together in mock shyness.

With just the two of them in the dining hall for now, there was nothing stopping them from doing as they pleased. The spacious hall held a large table draped in a white cloth, with nearly ten chairs arranged from the seat of honor at the far end down to the lowest seat near the door.

A few of the seats already had place settings, so Subaru's seat was presumably one of those. Simple logic said it would be one of the lower seats, but—

"Here's a thought — I'll go ahead and dare to sit in one of the seats of honor."

"What an outrageous choice, I suppose. I already know it, but you're wrong, I suppose."

"Heh heh heh, this must be the seat Emilia-tan normally sits in, right? Thinking that my butt and her butt are having an indirect sit-down right now gives me a faint little thrill..."

"You're an advanced-level pervert, I suppose! It's not just creepy, it's flat-out disgusting, I suppose!"

There was plenty he wanted to say, but the girl's fundamentally straight-man attitude made her genuinely fun to tease — so much so that he couldn't help wanting to provoke reactions out of her with perverted behavior he didn't even mean.

"So you see, none of this is actually what I want, heh-heh-heh."

"That laugh at the end has zero persuasive power, I suppose. ...There's a limit to how little progress this conversation can make, I suppose!"

As the girl pressed a hand to her forehead and shook her head, Subaru spun in his chair, pivoting on his backside and generating friction heat, and said—

"I think just not paying you back in kind makes me pretty conscientious, y'know. That hurt quite a bit."

Recalling how he'd been knocked out the first time they met, Subaru rotated his limbs to protest. Remembering the exchange in that mysterious library that had ended in his total defeat, Subaru nodded to himself.

"Come to think of it, the number of times I've blacked out in the last two days has reached a scary level. If anything I've spent less time awake than unconscious... wait, that's not so different from my shut-in days."

If anything, there'd been stretches back then where he lazed around even more.

Watching Subaru tilt his head in thought, the girl wore an expression like she was stifling a yawn.

"When your Gate got pried open, mana came gushing out, I suppose. It seemed a waste to let it spill, so I drank it all up for you, I suppose. You may thank me now."

"The contents leaked because of you in the first place. You poke a hole in my Vessel and then tell me to be grateful? That's such nerve my navel could boil tea and heat a whole bathtub while it's at it — I'm in paradise over here."

The girl brushed off Subaru's complaint like it was noise and tipped her glass again. When someone drank that brazenly, it sapped the will to call them out on it.

A little girl drinking alcohol like it was nothing — what a deplorable world.

"And so the girl learns the ways of the night, her spending gets flashy, and in the end it's a shotgun wedding in her teens... Oh, big brother's so ashamed he could cry!"

"Stop casting Betty as the heroine of some grand tragedy without permission, I suppose."

"From where I'm standing you're the airheaded protagonist of some badly-written cell phone novel."

He'd binged on those to kill time once, back when the gap between the good ones and the bad ones was famously huge. He'd also picked up gyaru-moji texting around the same time.

Unable to parse the meaning behind Subaru's remark, the girl scrunched her face up in displeasure and rapped the table lightly with a finger.

"Well, never mind, I suppose. More importantly, don't you have some words of thanks for Betty?"

"Thanks for what? 'Thank you so much for the precious verbal abuse from an actual real-life loli'? That's not exactly a reward in my line of work. Not just anyone will do, you know!"

"Why are you the one getting angry?! Betty's the one who wants to be angry, I suppose! Who was it that saved you when you were half-dead—"

The girl's voice trailed off toward the end, and Subaru shot back a puzzled "Huh?" But before she could give him a clear answer, the dining hall doors opened.

"Pardon the intrusion, honored guest. We shall now serve the meal."
"Pardon the intrusion, honored guest. I'll finish setting out the dishes and tea."

The ones who wheeled in carts and entered the dining hall were the twin maids.

The blue-haired one pushed a cart bearing an orthodox breakfast spread — salad, bread, and the like — while the pink-haired one pushed a cart loaded with plates, forks, and other tableware.

The two of them split to either side of the table and briskly began laying everything out. With flawless teamwork the table was set, and the warm aromas made Subaru's stomach growl before he could stop it.

"Ohh, nice, nice. Now this looks properly aristocratic. ...Here I was worried the table might be lined with a bunch of bizarre other-world delicacies."

Being another world, he'd considered the possibility that the food on the table would look wildly different. If he was unlucky, this world might even have insects as a staple food.

If that had turned out to be the case, Subaru would have had no choice left but to achieve enlightenment and sever all ties with the mortal world.

"Seriously, bugs are the one thing I genuinely cannot deal with. Why do they even exist? I don't get it, I don't get it at all — that shape, that way of living. Isn't the entire reason for their existence just so little kids can murder a bunch of them and learn the value of life?"

"If you oppress the weak, then when you yourself become weak, you'll be oppressed the same way by the strong. There's meaning in learning that. Be quiet now, weakling, I suppose."

She seemed to have decided to play the part of a little girl elegantly sipping her drink, holding him at bay with a theatrical reply.

Something about her elegant airs rubbed him the wrong way, and combined with his hunger, his irritation hit its peak.

"I. Am. At. My. Wits'. End!"

"You lack all elegance, I suppose. Can't you wait more gracefully, more refined, I suppose?"

"Not something I want to hear from a little girl drinking booze! C'mon, food! Food!"

At Subaru's ill-mannered clanging of the knife and fork he'd picked up, even the girl finally lost her patience. The hand she set the glass down with warped the space around it, gathering some kind of supernatural energy as she moved to point at Subaru.

But before that devilish hand could reach him—

"Ahaha, my, aren't you lively. That's a go-o-od thing, a go-o-od thing."

The pervert had shown up, looking pleased.

He'd changed out of his outdoor clothes, having swapped his earlier formalwear-like outfit for a tastelessly loud one with an absurdly large, colorful collar. His appearance was every bit the clown's, matched by his unchanged eccentric manner.

—Yes, indeed, an unmistakable pervert.

Roswaal watched Subaru drumming his cutlery with amusement, then his gaze drifted to the girl quietly tilting her glass, and he raised an eyebrow.

"Oh myyy, how rare to see Beatrice here. Have you finally felt like joining me at the table again after all this time, hm-m-m?"

"One head-in-the-clouds fool at this table is quite enough, I suppose. Betty only came to eat with Nii-cha, I suppose."

Coldly cutting off a doting Roswaal, the girl — Beatrice — turned her gaze past him. There, following the tall man into the room, was Emilia, and nestled within her silver hair—

"Nii-cha!"

The girl sprang up from her seat and ran, her long skirt flapping as she went. A smile like a blooming flower spread across her face, brimming with a charm that made him forget everything cheeky about her up to that point.

At last behaving in a manner that matched her looks, the girl went trotting over, and what reacted to her little dash was the gray cat nestled in the silver hair. Poking his face out, he softened his expression.

"Yo. Betty, it's been two days. Been keeping healthy and ladylike?"

"I've been so looking forward to seeing you, Nii-cha, I suppose. You have no plans to go anywhere today, I suppose?"

"Yep, I'm free. Let's take it easy together today, it's been a while."

"Yaaay, I suppose!"

Puck slipped free of the silver hair and fluttered down onto Beatrice's palm; catching him, Beatrice spun around and around, cradling the little cat.

As Subaru marveled at the cozy, harmonious scene between the two of them, Emilia came up beside him with a wry smile.

"Surprised, huh? Beatrice is totally glued to Puck."

"Surprised isn't the word — what's with that loli's attitude? Playing innocent in front of a cat is a bit too on the nose, don't you think?"

"Sorry. I have no idea what you just said."

She cut him off flat, then tilted her head with a "Hmm?" and, looking puzzled, pointed at the seat of honor where Subaru sat.

"That seat, isn't it..."

"Ah! Right, y'know how sometimes a cold chair makes even your heart go cold? Well, I'm running a special campaign to soothe that drafty heart of yours by becoming your blanket. So I went ahead and warmed the seat myself! It's not like I was aiming for an indirect sit-down or anything!"

"Sorry, I have no idea what you're saying... but that's Roswaal's seat, you know?"

His excuse breezed right past her, and on top of that came the announcement of his despair, buckling his knees.

Collapsing, Subaru realized his blunder and gritted his teeth.

Now that he thought about it, of course the master of the house would sit in the very highest seat. Blinded by an immediate payoff, only to misjudge in the end — what amateur hour.

Greenhorn of the Year, right here.

"But I'm a man who never falls without grabbing something on the way down. In that case... yes, in that case, I'll bet it all on tomorrow!!"

"Also, you really don't need to warm up my seat either. That'd be a little much."

"God is dead——!"

In the end, Subaru began pounding the floor, tears streaming down.

There was no hope left in this world, no future, he thought, utterly crushed.

Then, someone suddenly patted his shoulder, gently. Warmth passed through that palm, granting Subaru relief and comfort, and he lifted his face. There, shining, was hope—

"I'll make su-u-ure to savor your warmth thoroughly."

Hearing the pretty-boy say that, Subaru spat on the chair without a moment's hesitation.

"If my butt's residual warmth is gonna get defiled anyway, I'll do this instead!"

"Oh myyy, such a swift, decisive move — how unexpected, how splendid. But, there."

Roswaal laughed, thoroughly delighted, then lightly raised a finger toward the chair. His eyes went to the spat-upon spot, and after confirming it, he snapped the raised finger—

—and an instant later, right before his eyes, Subaru watched the spit vanish.

"Whoa!?"

Unable to believe it had disappeared, Subaru reflexively reached his hand out to the seat.

Where he touched, he couldn't feel even a trace of liquid residue — only a chair left slightly hot to the touch.

"It... evaporated?"

Given the heat of the chair and the disappearance of the spit, that was the only conclusion he could draw.

At Subaru's conclusion, Roswaal let out an admiring whistle.

"Ahaha, well figured out. Just now I interfered with an extremely tiny amount of fire mana and spiked the temperature in just that one spot, instantaneously."

"The hell, you make some crazy stunt like that sound so easy... could it be you ain't no ordinary pervert, sir?"

"Believe it or not, he's the Head Court Mage of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica."

It was Emilia beside him who answered, in response to Subaru slipping into a strange accent.

Unable to fully process what she'd said, Subaru muttered it back in a flat, mechanical drawl — "THE. HEAD. COURT. MAGE." — while staring at Roswaal.

Roswaal answered that stare with a pose, spinning around and around though nobody had asked him to. Subaru tried to reconcile that behavior with the word he'd just heard — total mismatch between claim and conduct.

"...That's a face that doesn't believe me."

"No-o-o wayyy, I totally believe you. There! Believed it just now! I believe you!"

"It's not really my place to push the issue, so it's fine either way, I guess."

Emilia gave up on persuading him remarkably fast. In their short time together, she seemed to have already learned that the way to defuse Subaru's incomprehensible momentum was to simply not engage.

Being ignored was awakening some new pleasure in Subaru, who watched Emilia take a seat in a chair apart from the head of the table. Presumably that was her regular spot.

Judging that no more people would be joining them in the dining hall, Subaru guessed that his seat must be whatever was left over, once the head seat, Emilia's seat, and — presumably — Beatrice's seat were excluded. That left an awkward, halfway position, far from the head of the table and subtly distant from Emilia too.

Making full use of such a huge table probably meant this kind of lopsided arrangement was unavoidable, but—

"Man, this really doesn't sit right with me."

Muttering, Subaru gathered up the tableware set out at his assigned seat. With a clatter of porcelain knocking together, he set off to move — to, naturally—

"Um, hold on?"

"It's fine, it's fine, just trust Terry."

"Who's that?!"

Emilia turned a bewildered face on Subaru as he relocated himself right next to her.

Unfazed by her reaction, Subaru went on to move his salad and bread over too. Having neatly claimed the seat beside her, he said—

"C'mon, it's fine, isn't it? Better to eat side by side like buddies than sitting far apart. Heck, go ahead and dump any veggies you don't like straight onto my plate."

"Well then, the peppir— wait, that's not the point, manners—"

"Doesn't it feel backwards to worry about that so much you can't enjoy the meal? Besides, food definitely tastes better sitting next to a girl — I won't budge on this. What d'you say, Roz-cchi?"

He said it with a grin to Emilia, who'd apparently pushed the bright-green vegetable to the edge of her plate because she disliked it, then tossed the topic over to Roswaal, who'd been watching Subaru with interest.

In response to being addressed, Roswaal pointed at himself.

"Could it be you mean me, by chance?"

"Who else would it be. Roz-cchi. C'mon, it's fine, seems like it'd tidy a lot of things up."

At the nod of confirmation, Roswaal laughed with pleasure, then turned that same face toward a sullen-looking Emilia.

"It's fi-i-ine, isn't it, Emilia-sama? Courtesy matters, but fretting too much about it among only family keeps you from enjoying your meal. Ah, he's quite right."

She must have taken that remark as the master of the house's final word on the matter.

Emilia let out a sigh, and Ram and Rem, who had been standing by, began carrying in dish after dish. Steaming food was laid out across the table one after another, and before long the breakfast spread was complete.

"Well then, let us eat. — O tree, o wind, o stars, o mother earth."

Clasping his hands and closing his eyes, Roswaal began murmuring something. Emilia and the twins followed suit. Beatrice, back at her seat, merely closed her eyes, but once Subaru realized this was a pre-meal prayer, he hastily copied the gesture.

He found himself genuinely impressed that a Christian-style prayer carried over even across worlds.

Judging by how earnestly they offered the prayer, Roswaal and Emilia might unexpectedly be quite devout. The twins, too, seemed practiced, their gestures well-mannered. Beatrice, on the other hand, did it far too carelessly, far too perfunctorily.

"Now then, Subaru-kun. Do try it. Believe it or not, Rem's cooking is really something."

Urged on by Roswaal, Subaru realized with a start that the prayer had ended at some point and hurried to join the meal. The menu appeared to be a salad, plus something bread-like topped with something ham-like, toast-style. It was a rather vague way to describe it, but it had the general air of a standard Western-style breakfast.

Even up close nothing seemed off, and Subaru felt relieved, for now. Grateful that no food beyond human comprehension had appeared, he took a bite of the toast-like thing and said—

"Mm... this is better than just good."

He lifted his face to give his verdict, and the blue-haired maid standing by the table made a fox shape with her fingers. He had no idea what it meant, but perhaps it was this world's equivalent of a V-sign. Subaru answered in kind by forming a frog with both hands.

"This dish was made by the blue-haired one — uh, Rem-chan, right? — you made this?"

"Yes, that's correct, honored guest. The kitchen of this house is basically entrusted to Rem. Sister isn't very good at it."

"Ah-hah, the classic 'twins with different specialties' pattern. So Pinky's bad at cooking but good at cleaning-type stuff?"

"Yes, that's right. Among household chores, sister is best at cleaning and laundry."

"Then Rem-rin's good at cooking-type stuff but bad at cleaning and laundry?"

"No, Rem is basically good at housework across the board. Cleaning and laundry too. Better than sister."

"Pinky's entire reason for existing just vanished!?"

One twin good at housework across the board, and the other good at only cleaning-type things — and even at that, worse than her twin. If anything, that was a new pattern for a pair of twins.

It was a harsh thing to say, harsh as could be, but Ram, standing beside Rem, showed no sign of minding. The lack of any correction suggested it was simply true, but even so, what was with her unshaken composure about it?

"Or maybe her field's just different. Ram-cchi's more of a combat class... no wait, maybe it's the nighttime-service type!? Damn, tonight I'm starving for all sorts of things!"

"Sister, Sister. In the guest's mind, you're being cast under some baseless suspicion."
"Rem, Rem. In the guest's mind, you're being put into some indecent state."

At Subaru's vividly pink imagination, the twins clasped hands and struck tragic poses. Watching the exchange, Roswaal chuckled softly.

"You're go-o-od, you. Ram and Rem both have such strong personalities that guests meeting them for the first time tend to keep their distance, one way or another."

"If the master's this riddled with flaws, nobody's gonna sweat the servants' flaws. Besides, it's a fantasy world, so I'll forgive just about anything right now."

"There's no doubting they're both capable. It's just that a lot of people don't bother looking past the rest."

The one who cut in with that was Emilia. Carrying her soup to her mouth in small sips, she went on in a slightly displeased tone.

"Honestly, the two of them are amazing. They're the ones keeping this huge manor running, practically by themselves. Whatever their race is — that's such a stupid thing to care about."

"Man, sounds like there's a lot going on there for you."

He said only that much to Emilia, whose eyes burned with righteous indignation, then gathered up the loose threads of what she'd said to raise a question.

"So just now, Emilia-tan said something like the manor only has two servants, right?"

"Ahaha, that's the current state of things, yeah. It ended up being just Ram and Rem."

"Managing a mansion this ridiculously huge with just two people, isn't that insane? Forget quality, they're gonna work themselves to death. ...Or is it more like there's some restriction going on, some 'can't hire servants' kind of situation?"

At Subaru's question, Roswaal fell silent for a moment, folding his hands on the table. A smile stayed fixed on his face, but the emotion in the eyes looking back at Subaru had clearly, unmistakably shifted.

*Maybe I asked something I shouldn't have* — sensing the sound of a flag being raised, Subaru bit into the long-awaited Appa that had appeared for dessert.

With a crisp crunch, the sweet fruit filled his mouth, spreading a gentle, tingling sweetness.
