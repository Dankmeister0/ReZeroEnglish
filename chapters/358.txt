Arc 5 Chapter 42: "The Newest Hero and the Oldest Hero"

——A heavy, sorrowful silence had settled over the shelter.

The faint sound of breath that might have been someone stifling a sob, and the restless tapping of a finger against the floor.
Listening to those sounds echo with an especially uncomfortable weight through the quiet space, the girl who sat hugging her knees felt the cold of the wall against her back.

She was a small girl with golden hair.
Resting her chin on her small white knees, the crouching girl gently drew the weight beside her closer.
Leaning against her left shoulder, his head tucked between her knees and chest, was a boy still young — the girl's little brother. He had cried and wailed endlessly, but now, worn out from crying, he had fallen asleep.
Tear tracks still marked his cheeks, and his eyes, swollen from crying, were red raw. She wanted to gently stroke his head, but the thought that doing so might wake him made her hesitate.

Surely, if he could stay asleep, it was far better that he remain so for now.
Listening to her brother's peaceful breathing, she prayed that at least within his dreams there might be some measure of rest. The reality outside those dreams was, she thought, too harsh for a boy still so young.
And that was just as true for the young sister who worried over him.

——It had been several hours since the announcement that the control tower of the city of Priestella's great floodgate had been seized.

On the morning the broadcast came, the girl had been out with her brother and heard it in the city square. Words too outrageous to believe, malice sliding past the ear in a torrent of abuse. Unable to accept the pronouncement, worried for her parents, she had taken her frightened brother's hand and fled with the surrounding adults into a shelter.

——When the unexpected happens, run for a shelter.
That was the emergency response drilled into everyone by the city hall's regular morning broadcasts.
Truth be told, the girl had no memory of listening seriously to the morning broadcasts except for the Songstress's singing, but even so, the warning had stuck with her enough to recall in an instant.
Still, as for what to do once inside the shelter — neither she nor the adults around her seemed to have any clear answer.

——Witch Cult. Control tower. Great floodgate. Demands.

A wicked woman's shrill, delighted voice hurled abuse as if clawing at the hearts of people already trembling with unease.
Amid the unbearable stream of vitriol, the ominous words scattered through it carried more than enough power to bind the hearts of the girl and the adults alike in fear.

Shut away in the dim shelter, with no clear sense of what was happening outside, and no sign of things turning for the better as time dragged on — it was only natural that mounting anxiety would rear its head.
The voices that had at first encouraged one another grew faint, and little by little, unease and irritation began to creep into the silence. Before anyone noticed, some had begun to show open displeasure, and that mood spread like a contagion, until nowhere-to-go discontent and dissatisfaction became thorns in glances and postures.

Once it reached that point, there was no stopping it.
Glares exchanged, curses hurled. At worst, hands raised, bodies grappling.
That same air had crept through this shelter too, until it teetered right on the edge of eruption.

"Ahh——"

The girl's wailing brother shattered that dangerous, blood-threatening tension into pieces.
Shaking his short blond hair wildly, crying and clinging — apparently the nearly boiling adults still retained enough dignity and sense not to roughly shove aside a weeping child.
For all that, a child's crying does hold a certain power.

Her brother's crying, which she had always thought was nothing but noisy, had actually proven useful — realizing that, the girl embraced the brother who had stopped the fight, holding him from behind, and cried a little herself.

Since then, no more quarrels had broken out in this shelter.
But everyone understood that this, too, was only a fragile peace balanced on a precarious equilibrium.
Next time, a child's crying would surely not be enough to stop it.

Knowing that, the people of the shelter — who ought to have shared a common fate — kept their distance from one another, stubbornly guarding a relationship in which not even a glance or the sound of breathing could reach, let alone words.
They refused to intrude on anyone else's awareness, rejecting the outside world entirely. No one could say what might catch another's attention, provoke their anger, or pull some invisible trigger.
So, for their own sake and for each other's, they made themselves small, held their breath, and waited with grave faces for time to pass. Waiting, in the fragile hope that something might change if only they waited long enough.

"——Ah."

Suddenly, the girl let out a hoarse sound and lifted her face.
Amid her quiet, longing wait for change, a faint shift in the air had caught at her senses.
Reacting to the same thing, the heads of the people around her stirred slightly for the first time in hours. It was a faint tremor in the air that anyone who lived in this city would recognize — the sign that a broadcast was about to begin.

In the world of silence, a sound like a breath brushing past her ear could be heard. That she could now sense the coming of a broadcast at all felt, if anything, hateful.
The change she longed for was, without question, a turn for the better. But broadcasts had only ever brought word of the Witch Cult's malice.

What impossible demand would that shrill voice force on them next?
But the girl's pessimistic guess turned out to be wrong.

『——Uh, um, is this actually reaching everyone properly? Mic check, mic check, one two, one two.』

Because what came through was, somehow, the awkward voice of a boy.
A complete reversal from every broadcast before it — an unconfident boy's voice. Not the familiar voice of some important man they heard every morning, either. A young voice, one she had never heard before.

The girl's eyes went wide. The adults around her exchanged glances, wondering what on earth was happening.
Naturally, none of that reaction on this side reached across the Magic Vessel. The boy tested his voice a few more times to confirm the broadcast was working, then cleared his throat. And then—

『Glad it seems to be getting through. So, first off — sorry for a broadcast like this out of nowhere. I bet it startled you. I'm sure a lot of you are anxious, wondering what's going to be demanded of you this time. But I want you to be at ease. The person talking to you right now isn't from the Witch Cult. I need you to understand that first.』

"...Not... the Witch Cult?"

Unused to handling the Magic Vessel, the boy's volume rose and fell slightly.
But the shock of what he'd said outweighed that, and no one so much as remarked on it. Faces that had stayed dark for hours looked up toward the source of the voice, expressions shifting. It was the change that might have just taken root — the hope of expectation.

Someone murmured, quietly.

"Th-then... are we... saved?"

The hope carried in those words reached everyone in the shelter at once.
Of course. Of course it must be so. If someone other than the Witch Cult was operating the Magic Vessel, that could only mean someone had retaken the city hall. And if someone had driven the Witch Cult out of city hall, then perhaps from the control tower, from the city itself, too—

"Drive those Witch Cult bastards out of here, out of everywhere...!"

『And I hate to get your hopes up only to say this, but the Witch Cult's threat isn't gone yet. We got city hall back, but they're still holed up in the control tower. The danger of the city sinking underwater, and their demands to make that happen — all of that's still live. I need you to understand that too.』

"——"

But that fragile hope was, just as easily, crushed by none other than the boy on the broadcast himself.
The way he spoke seemed almost as if he'd read the hearts of everyone in the shelter. To snuff out a hope only just beginning to bud — wasn't that a cruelty beyond measure?

Someone who had, without thinking, begun to rise, hope kindled in their eyes, sank back down.
Told that the sign of relief from their fear had been a mistake, no one could blame the person now slumping in disappointment. If anything, the anger that welled up turned toward the very boy on the broadcast.

『Sorry about that.』

But the boy had read even that resentment, that misdirected anger from the crowd, before it came.

『Right now, where is everyone listening to this broadcast from? I'm guessing there are people in shelters, and maybe some who haven't been able to reach a shelter at all. I bet everyone's full of anxiety. I get the feeling of being scared enough to want to curl up and hug your knees. And yet here I am, going and stirring up everyone's hopes like that — you must be wondering just who the hell I think I am.』

"——"

『I'm not anybody. Not somebody special, not anybody at all. Same as everyone else — jerked around by the situation, feeling like I'm about to get crushed by something unfair, scared, legs shaking. That's about the level of guy I am. Even taking on this job of talking to everyone over the broadcast — there was a whole mess before I agreed to it. I still think it's too much for me, even now. There really should be someone else more suited to talking to everyone like this. There must be.』

The boy's voice trembled as he spoke, as if voicing on their behalf the fear and dread sunk deep in the hearts of the people listening.
What followed was the boy's own faint-hearted confession, doubting his own worth.

By now, the audience — the girl included — had moved past confusion and disappointment into pure suspicion.
Everyone wanted hope right now. So why was a boy like this the one standing in front of the Magic Vessel?
There should be someone more fitting, the broadcasting boy himself had just said as much.
And yet, why him?

『But right now, here I am, talking. People way more amazing and important than me told me I should be the one to do this. That there's meaning in doing it this way. Is my voice shaking? Standing up in front of people — that's just not who I am. I can't say anything impressive, and I don't have the charisma to lead everyone forward. I'm weak, hopeless, and even now, in this huge moment, all I want is to run away...』

The tone of his voice gradually sank, and the hearts of those listening felt themselves dragged down toward the Abyss along with it.
At that weak, hoarse voice, chests already shrunk with anxiety ached, stomachs clenched. If the boy behind this voice were within reach, within arm's length, she wanted to clap a hand over his mouth.

"Sis..."

At some point, her sleeping brother had woken.
At the sound of his voice calling for her, the girl held him close, desperately trying to keep this coward's voice from creeping into his ears, trying to keep him from being swallowed by this crushing weakness.
But even as she protected her brother, the voice shook her own eardrums, dragging her along into that weakness as its companion.
The boy's voice went on.

『I don't even know what I can do — I want to cover my ears and hold my head and just seriously hope that everything gets solved while I curl up somewhere, leaving it all to someone else...』

"—Nn."

Eyes screwed shut, the girl shook her head, refusing despair and grief.
She knew. Even without being told, she knew.
The boy's words were nothing less than a voicing of the very heart of everyone in the shelter, of everyone in this city trembling before the Witch Cult's threat.

It was the weakness that lived inside the girl.
It was the faint-heartedness rooted deep in the adults' hearts.
It was the unbearable terror gnawing at her still-small brother's spirit.

It was, surely, something no one could do anything about.
But that didn't mean she could bear to look that insurmountable reality in the face——

『——But even so, I can't run, so I'll fight. That's all I am.』

When he said that, the tremor in the boy's voice was still, unmistakably, there.

"...Eh?"

Wondering if she'd misheard, the girl opened her closed eyes and looked up.
There was no speaker to be seen there. But everyone around her wore the same dumbstruck expression.

There was a pause — a moment spent choosing words, steadying his voice.
And then,

『Let me ask you again. Whoever's listening to this voice — where are you right now? Did you make it to a shelter? Are you hiding in your own home? Are you shivering there alone? Are you with someone? Is the person with you someone precious to you? Even a stranger's face — has it become, over these past few hours, at least a familiar one?』

"——"

『I know it's a selfish thing to ask, and it might be hard, but please — don't be alone. When you're alone, nothing but useless thoughts come to mind. That's from experience. I get it. So don't be alone. Stay with someone. And then——』

He drew a breath, a faint hesitation resting on his tongue before he went on,

『And if you can, look at the face of the person beside you.』

"——"

As if led by his words, the girl slowly let her gaze drop into her own arms.
Her brother was looking up at her. Her eyes met his, unsteadily wavering, emerald-green.

『Whose face did you just look at? Someone precious to you, or maybe a stranger you've spent these past few hours with. Could be a friend, too. ...Probably, they look pretty rough right now. Crying, or in pain — I doubt there are many smiles. Actually, maybe some of you have someone putting on a brave, fake smile so as not to worry you. If so, that person's amazing. If someone precious to you is smiling like that, you should be proud of them. Keeping that in mind, compare it to the smile you know.』

Her brother's face was a crying face.
Crumpled, and looking as if he might start crying again at any moment.
And reflected in his eyes, she herself looked hollow, as though she'd lost all expression.

『——Can you stand for that?』

"...No, I can't."

A small, thin voice slipped out of the girl's mouth.
Weak and hoarse, a sound she could barely hear even herself.
And yet,

『I can't stand it. I don't want to accept it.』

The boy's voice that followed rang out with a strength, as if he'd heard her.

『I have people precious to me too. Important friends. And I can't stand the ones making my precious people wear faces like that — faces of pain, of sorrow. I won't forgive them for forcing fake smiles either. Don't screw with us. Don't you dare mock us. I want to shout it loud — the smile I know on this kid's face is way cuter than that, damn it!』

"S-Sis..."

『I can't just keep losing. I can't just keep throwing my hands up, it's not cool. I can't just keep taking it lying down. They're the ones in the wrong. Even if I don't have the strength to beat the ones in the wrong, to do the right thing — I should still know what's right. When you know you're standing on the right side, and losing to the ones in the wrong — I can't stand that. At least, I don't want to admit defeat to people like that.』

"Fred..."

She gently drew her weakly calling brother closer and pressed her forehead to his.
There was warmth passing between them. Hot, hot, living warmth.

Whether it was her brother's or her own, she couldn't tell — but the warmth was there.

『I want to run, but I can't run. I want to cry, but I can't stay crying. The enemy's terrifying, but I don't want to lose. So I'll fight. I know I'm weak, I know I'm stupid, I know all of it, but I'll fight anyway. They're the ones in the wrong. The ones making people I love wear faces like they're about to cry — they're wrong. So I'll fight. I'm going to fight. ——And I want everyone else to fight too.』

"——!"

Her breath caught. Her throat closed on reflex, and she hated her own weakness for it.
Precisely because the tremor from before had vanished, and what she heard now was a boy's voice pointing the way forward with strength.

She understood the feeling. The meaning of the boy's words came through painfully clear.
The girl's own true feelings were the same as his resolve. She wanted to fight. If she could drive out the wicked ones attacking the city, she wanted to. But she and her brother were both small, young, and their hands couldn't reach that far.
Powerless, ignorant, faint-hearted, cowardly, and so——

『Don't get the wrong idea. When I say I want you to fight, I don't mean grab a stick and go swinging at them. If anything, please avoid something that reckless. I'm not asking you to band together and go at the Witch Cult with bloodshot eyes. What I'm asking of everyone, the fight I want from you, is just this — don't look down.』

"...Don't look down..."

『Staring hard at your own feet won't change anything. Your gaze won't burn a hole through the floor, and even if it did, that wouldn't lead to any breakthrough... So lift your face. Look forward.』

She raised her eyes. Not at her own knees, not at her brother's blond hair — the shelter came into view.
And she realized that, just like her, the people around her had lifted their faces too.
Eyes met, and widened in surprise.
Everyone, unconsciously, following the boy's voice, had lifted their faces just as she had.

『If you look around, I bet you'll catch someone's eye. Someone carrying the same anxiety, the same urge to run away as you — but also, someone carrying the same feeling of not wanting to lose. The precious someone beside you, and now, someone whose eyes you just met. Add yourself to that, and that's already three people. Depending on where you are, there might be a lot more.』

Just as the boy said, the girl's eyes met many faces around her.
The light held in those eyes was complicated, and surely her own was the same. But somehow, it no longer seemed to be only the light of trembling fear.

『I hope it helps you feel that you're not alone. Knowing you're not alone — that's actually a pretty decent source of strength on its own, isn't it? Not wanting to see someone precious to you looking sad. Not wanting to look pathetic in front of someone whose eyes you just met. Surely I'm not the only shallow, weak, stubborn one who feels that way?』

"——"

The pleading voice, the calling voice, was trying to stir courage in the hearts of the people.
And yet to the girl, it sounded as if the boy himself was the one asking for help, reaching for something to hold onto.

And only now did she realize.
The boy's state of mind had not changed once, not for a moment, since this broadcast began.

Regretting his own weakness, resenting his own inadequacy — and yet never giving up.
He spoke of that alone as his weapon, and told everyone that this much, at least, they shared.

『Let me believe it. Weak, hopeless me — I still haven't given up. That I'm not the only stubborn coward who won't quit... let me believe that.』

It was a cowardly voice. A shameless plea.
Because this voice — none other than this — in a moment when everyone was crying out for help, was, before anyone else, shamelessly crying out, "Support me"——

『Or is it just me?』

The voice lost its confidence. No — from the very start, there had been no confidence in the boy's voice.
A rising urgency welled up in her. Stop him. Even if she didn't know what to shout.

"...No, it's not."

A voice like a mosquito's whine, too faint to even take shape, spilled from her throat.
A voice like that wouldn't reach him. She had to answer louder, bigger.
To this cowardly voice, afraid of being alone——

『Is it only me who still thinks we can do this... who still thinks we can fight?』

"——It's not!"

Opening her mouth, the girl found herself shouting, almost roaring.
A voice that rang through the shelter. And it wasn't hers alone.
The girl, and others around her who had likewise lifted their faces, were raising their voices too.

It was a voice defying grief, defying weakness, defying fear.

If this was the boy's design all along, then she had been thoroughly played.
Calculated or not, so what. If that weak, trembling voice, that unreliable scolding, that pitiful encouragement, that clinging trust could all be dismissed as nothing but a false performance——
If it had been such a clever incitement, then being swept up in it couldn't be helped.
But if this was instead the clumsy, honest truth of a coward — then how could she leave him alone?

『Isn't that right?』

"It's not!"

『Everyone's still fighting too, right? Not letting yourselves be swallowed by weakness, right?』

"We won't lose... we don't want to lose!"

Deep in her chest, heat burned. Her teeth chattered, boiling with a passion unlike anger.
That feeling wasn't hers alone. It swallowed up the feelings of everyone around her too, rising up as a single blaze — a passion that had, until just a moment ago, been the shared anxiety of everyone here, now transformed into something else, a feeling burning with far greater heat, becoming one.

『If the person beside you is someone precious, take their hand and believe in them. If the one next to you is a stranger, nod and tell them let's do our best together. That you and they can both fight without losing, without breaking. If everyone can stay unbroken, then I won't give up either — I'll fight. Fight — and win.』

"——"

In the end, this was just one shelter, far from city hall.
No matter how loud they shouted here, no matter how they cried out that they felt the same, it wouldn't reach the boy.
And yet the boy's voice, as though relieved, as though it had received the girl's voice and all the others, took in that surge of feeling and let it settle into the tremor of his own voice as he declared it, firmly.

——Fight, and win.

Whether he could do it or not — there was no room to doubt.
He would surely do it. That was what she believed.

Just as the boy's voice believed that the girl, that the people of the city, would not lose to despair.
So too did the girl, and everyone else, believe that this voice — this boy — would win the most dangerous fight of all.
Why could she believe that? It was because this voice, surely——

『——My name is Natsuki Subaru. The Spirit Arte user who defeated the Witch Cult's Sin Archbishop of Sloth.』

"——!"

At the name finally revealed, the identity kept hidden until now, a stir rippled through the shelter.
It was a declaration whose meaning the girl herself didn't grasp. But for the people around her, it was not so. The shock it delivered was immense — and yet, never once a negative one.
First astonishment, then understanding — and then hope and trust spread in an explosive wave, sweeping up even the girl's own heart in its current.

『The Witch Cult in the city — my friends and I will deal with them! So believe in us, and fight. Take the hand of someone precious to you, and knock away the weak heart that's about to give in. And then——』

"——"

『——Leave the rest, all of it, to me!』

A roar of voices spread outward, and heat swept over everyone in the shelter.
Expectation became hope, one hope multiplied into countless hopes, expanding all at once.

The girl looked down at her brother in her arms and saw, in his emerald eyes, a clear light take hold.
Confirming it, she embraced him tighter still. Hesitantly, his hands returned the embrace, holding her back, and feeling the warmth of that embrace, the girl looked up at the ceiling.

Unable to hide his own fear, his own anxiety, none of it — and yet still shouldering the expectations and hopes of countless people across the city, still declaring that he would fight. That boy.
To this hero whose face she couldn't picture, who existed only in her heart, she closed her eyes as if in prayer, wishing every conceivable stroke of fortune upon him.

——Because surely that boy, too, was just an ordinary boy, the kind found anywhere, doing nothing more than defying the unreasonable for the sake of someone precious to him.

Stepping back from the Magic Vessel shaped like a phonograph, Subaru retreated slowly.
The sweat running down his forehead was born of anxiety and tension. He leaned against the nearest workbench and wiped it away roughly.

He drew a series of deep breaths, worrying whether the sound would be picked up by the Magic Vessel.
But glancing at Anastasia beside him, operating the device, it seemed the power — or whatever the equivalent was — had been safely cut. A small relief.

"...Phew, that was rough."

Muttering that with a sigh, Subaru rolled his neck against a fatigue heavier than he'd expected.
Honestly, while he'd been talking, he'd been utterly lost in the moment, and he couldn't recall the details of what exactly had come out of his mouth. It wasn't all a blank, but parts of it were hazy in his memory.
And that despite the fact that Anastasia had handed him a written draft to speak from properly.

"——"

Wiping the sweat that had dripped down to his chin with his sleeve, Subaru noticed how strangely quiet the room had become.
The group who had watched over the broadcast were all silent. Present with him were Anastasia, and both Garfiel and Al. Julius and Ricardo, who'd joined them, were also there in a corner of the room, and the silence from a group normally so talkative felt nothing but uncomfortable.
Had he, perhaps, botched the broadcast that badly — hopelessly clumsy and incoherent?

"Natsuki-kun."

"Whoa?! Sorry! I'll do better next time!"

"Why are you apologizing? What a weird kid."

Subaru's heart had already been aching with anxiety, and at being addressed, he apologized on reflex. Anastasia laughed at the reaction, and, still smiling, tilted her head.

"On top of being a weird kid, this might be an odd thing to ask, but Natsuki-kun, were you maybe—"

"Hm?"

"—some kind of con artist before, or something like that?"

"Of all the things to say! Look at me, I'm nothing special, just your average, run-of-the-mill student... well, in some ways, less than a student, even!"

"Ah, no, that's not it. It's not an insult. It's just, the way you were talking earlier was really polished, y'know? The way you'd knock people down and then lift them back up — your rhetoric was pitch-perfect."

Gesturing at Subaru's response, Anastasia gave a weak "ta-ha" of a laugh.
At her answer, it was Subaru's turn to tilt his head.

"There was no rhetoric, none of that. My head went totally blank, I was talking so fast I don't even know what came out. The notes went blurry before my eyes, and the last thing I remember is giving up on reading them."

"So you completely ignored my draft, huh. You went off and started talking about something totally different from what we'd planned, and you have no idea how much I was sweating over here beside you... though I guess my worrying turned out to be needless."

"I'm sorry about that, really! But wasn't it mostly in line with what the notes said? If it went that badly off track, you would've stopped me, right?"

The notes in his hand — the cheat sheet he'd completely forgotten existed during the big moment — were lined with flowery, carefully chosen words meant to dispel the anxiety of the city's people.
It was a confident piece of work, drawing on Anastasia's negotiating skill, Garfiel's knowledge of idioms, and even Subaru's knowledge of the modern world, all woven into something clever and witty.
He hadn't managed to read from it during the crucial moment, but he figured that if even some of what he'd memorized had stuck with him, the speech would have followed roughly along those lines.

"Not to put too fine a point on it, but Natsuki-kun's little speech just now didn't touch on the contents of that draft at all. Not even a little."

"——Eh?"

Anastasia's words flatly denied Subaru's assumption.
Frozen for a moment, Subaru looked around at the others' faces to confirm whether this was true. But the other four all wore equally solemn expressions, each reacting to his gaze in their own way.
From among them, Julius stepped forward. Toying with his own forelock with a finger, he said,

"Anastasia-sama is correct, Subaru. You broadcast content that wasn't in our earlier arrangement at all. In particular, the fact that you delayed mentioning your feat of defeating the Sin Archbishop of Sloth — something you were supposed to reveal early — until the latter half, made me want to ask just what you were thinking."

"Seriously? So without that, I was just some random dude nobody could make sense of! If it was going that badly, you should've stopped me! When I think it's going sideways, deciding it's better to just start over is a call I have to make in the moment, no other option!"

"Start over? Now that really would have been an absurd thing to do."

At Subaru's judgment of his own performance — that it had been such a failure it undermined the whole point of the address — Julius shook his head, his expression deadly serious.
That expression seemed, to Subaru, to hold a kind of respect.

"——It was a magnificent speech."

"...Huh?"

"Forgetting the contents of the draft is hardly a problem worth mentioning. You accomplished more than what was expected of you, through your own ability alone. That achievement deserves nothing but praise. I find myself feeling, toward you right now, the same thing I felt back then — with the White Whale, and with 'Sloth.'"

To a bewildered Subaru, Julius piled on words with an almost exaggerated weight.
In that uncharacteristic manner, Subaru caught a glimpse of the excitement of the Sword Saint's finest knight. And the instant he sensed it, he thought, what nonsense is this.
Why on earth would this knight, of all people, change his attitude so dramatically over something Subaru had done?

"Quit messing with me. I've thought this before, but your jokes really aren't that funny."

"That you'd take it for a joke is only because you rate yourself too low. But then, it's also true that this speech was one only you could have given, precisely because of that. No one but you could have delivered those words."

"You really are messing with me, aren't you?"

The urgency of the situation only fed Subaru's irritation at Julius's praise.
He was used to Julius's sarcasm by now, but this wasn't the time to be caught up in some fruitless exchange. If the speech couldn't be expected to have any effect, they needed an alternate plan, and fast.

"If it was supposed to help everyone and instead just planted some weird distrust, it's pointless. Guess we'll need someone else next—"

"Natsuki-kun, you really need to quit puttin' yourself down like that, y'know? It doesn't feel good, listenin' to it."

At Subaru's worry, Anastasia cut in from the side.
Her lovely face carried a note of displeasure as she glared at Subaru.

"If the speech went right past you and you've got no sense of how it landed, I'll just say it plain. Natsuki-kun's speech was more perfect than anyone could've imagined. You've got a talent for stirring people up, you know that?"

"I agree with the young lady on this one! Nah, I'm impressed, for real! What was that turn of phrase! You really are a sly one, kid! Getting Emilia-sama, Crusch-sama, a little girl, and an Earth Dragon all wrapped 'round your finger — that's a real testament to your reputation, ain't it!"

"Neither of those is something I can just let slide! I haven't wrapped anyone around my finger, and 'stirring people up' — come on!"

Subaru raised his voice at the far-too-scandalous assessment.
But Anastasia and Ricardo just exchanged looks and shrugged in unison, without a trace of malice. Their perfectly synced reaction suggested it wasn't entirely a joke, either.
That much was clear too from the face of Garfiel, who crouched down to look at Subaru.

"Boss..."

"Garfiel. What did you think?"

"Boss, you really were the boss. Me leaving 'Sanctuary' to follow you — I wasn't wrong to do it. ...That's what I felt."

"...Your expectations are always a little too heavy a load for me, you know."

"But even that's a result of what you did, boss."

Standing up, Garfiel approached, baring his fangs in a grin. At the sight, Subaru let out a deep breath through his nose, and,

"Guess I'll give up on running from responsibility, then. Feels like I said something along those lines in the broadcast just now."

"Ya did say that."

Scratching his head, Subaru accepted it with resignation, and Anastasia laughed at him. She puffed out her slender chest at the unexpected result, smoothing her scarf with one hand.

"Honestly, I'm more worried the morale boost was so strong that everyone else might go and do somethin' reckless. Even us here — the effect of 'Wrath' kinda just made us all fired up."

"Now that's starting to sound suspicious... seriously, what kind of talent do I actually have as a producer, or, I dunno, an admiral or something?"

Being showered with nothing but praise like this, without any sense of it landing himself, made it hard to accept.
Shaking off the swirl of overly warm gazes, Subaru moved to get clear of the Magic Vessel, and,

"Anyway, if it managed to say something useful, that's the main thing. If this keeps riots from breaking out in the shelters, great... Anything else we can think of doing?"

"As for the city's residents, there's not much more to do beyond removing the root cause itself. With Natsuki-kun's speech just now, the Witch Cult should've figured out our intentions, but..."

"How they respond to that is, as we discussed, something we can only hope their irrational nature works in our favor for. At the same time, we need to bring this to a swift conclusion on our end too."

Regardless of how well or poorly Subaru's speech had gone, the situation remained unchanged — a group of lunatics still had their hands on the means to destroy the entire city. Even under the most optimistic outlook, once the date changed, the great floodgate would be released, and the city would sink beneath the flood.
They had to reach a resolution before that happened.

"For that, we need to take all four locations simultaneously... huh."

"Four Sin Archbishops, plus two skilled unknowns. We'll need to talk it over, weighing that against our own strength, and figure out how to pull it off."

Simultaneous capture of the four control towers was the essential condition for saving the city.
Unlike with city hall, they probably couldn't concentrate their forces all in one place. Doing that to take one control tower would risk one of the other three opening the great floodgate in the meantime.
He had no confidence they could pull off that gamble four times over and win each one.

Against six primary enemies, their own available strength was——

"Our hand's thin. We could end up repeating what happened at city hall. If we just had one more card... one more, that'd help."

"——In that case, how about adding one more trump card to the hand?"

The voice cut in abruptly on Subaru, who had been counting their available forces on his fingers.
He turned, startled, to find a figure standing in the doorway. It was—

"You've been gone a while, and somewhere along the way you started rating yourself pretty highly, huh?"

"Not as highly as someone entrusted with giving a public address to the masses, Natsuki-san. ...I didn't think I had a hero among my friends. Guess I misjudged."

"I don't think it suits me either."

At the sight of the other man's mischievous grin, Subaru shrugged and grinned back. Then he turned toward the doorway and exchanged a high five with the still-grinning figure.
Seeing that greeting, Garfiel's face lit up.

"Otto bro! You made it out okay!"

It was Otto — whose whereabouts had been unknown — who nodded at Garfiel's delighted voice.
Somewhat filthy, but apparently unhurt, Otto had rejoined them. As Garfiel came closer, he exchanged a high five with him too, and,

"Barely made it out alive. It was touch and go, but somehow, I managed to keep breathing. Glad to see you two are safe as well. Well, I wasn't too worried about you — you're both far too good at clinging to life to die easily."

"That so. Funny thing, I wasn't too worried about you either. Wonder why."

"Beats me. Maybe it's just Otto bro's good karma or somethin'?"

"Could you two spare at least a little worry for me!? In an emergency like this, going off alone was basically a straight shot toward danger!"

But given that he'd actually made it back to rejoin them like this, the complaint didn't carry much weight.
In any case, as Subaru and the others rejoiced over the reunion, Anastasia clapped her hands and cut in.

"Alright, alright, settle down now. First off, I'm glad Otto-kun's alive and well. There's plenty I want to ask about what you've been up to till now, believe me, but—"

She cut herself off there, piercing Otto with her pale blue-green eyes.

"That meaningful little comment just now... mind telling me what it meant?"

"The trump card, you mean. It's simple, really. If I'd brought them in earlier, my own safe return would've been swept under the rug, so I pulled a little trick — but I brought someone with me."

At Anastasia's pointed question, Otto gave a sheepish look and stepped away from the door.
That, apparently, was the signal to whoever had been waiting on the other side. Footsteps sounded, and a new figure was ushered into the room. And it was——

"——Sorry for the delay."

Just those few words, and yet it carried a strength as if a thousand reinforcements had arrived.
The illusion of a raging wind, mingling with the illusion of flame glimpsed just ahead, stirred the heart.
But in truth, this reunion held exactly that much power.

The arrival of the strength they had wanted, desperately wanted.

"Reinhard van Astrea——belatedly, I've joined you."

So saying, the red flame — the Sword Saint — declared his intent to join the fight.
