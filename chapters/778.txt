Arc 10 Chapter 19: "An Enemy Too Great"


—Just how much time had passed since they started running?

Between an environment where outside light couldn't properly reach them and an unknown period spent unconscious, Subaru felt in his bones that anywhere from a few hours to half a day had passed since everything went wrong.

"Miraculously, it seems we managed to throw off our pursuers, but..."

Catching his ragged breath and wiping the sweat dripping from his chin, Subaru turned around amidst the lukewarm stench, checking for any silent footsteps from invisible pursuers.
Until moments ago, they had been relentlessly hunted by a swarm of Kingdom soldiers, without a single moment to catch their breath.
With their path blocked and escape routes lost, the choices of being captured or forcing their way through had flashed through his mind time and again. It had been a hair-raising crisis for both sides, but—

"Thanks to that ridiculously huge earthquake, we somehow made it... Come to think of it, I don't remember ever running into an earthquake since coming to this world. What was that about?"

"Betty was surprised by that rumbling as well, but... now is our chance, I suppose. Subaru, show me your arm. I shall cast healing magic on your burns, I suppose."

"Oh, thanks. That's a lifesaver."

Saying that, Subaru set Beatrice down onto the ground, whom he had been carrying in his arms this whole time.
Beatrice opened and closed her palms as if checking her state, and then enveloped Subaru's right arm—wrapped with a handkerchief serving as a bandage—in a faint light of healing magic.
Feeling the dull, throbbing pain that had been lingering finally begin to ease, he let out an involuntary breath.

"You were putting on a tough front after all, I suppose. If it was painful, I wished you had said so sooner, I suppose."

"No, really, while I was panicking and forgetting about it, it completely slipped my mind. The moment I calmed down, the *ow-ow-ow* pain came flooding back! H-Hurry... save meee...!"

"Yes, yes, what a helpless child you are, I suppose. It will be over soon, so do not start blubbering, I suppose."

"Thank you, Mama Beako! ...Is your mana getting pretty tight?"

"—To be frank, there is no margin to spare, I suppose."

"...Well, yeah, figures."

At the answer from Beatrice, who was treating his burns, Subaru scratched his cheek with his other hand.
After all, they had been quite lavish back at Miklotov's mansion, so it couldn't be helped. Having spammed Yin magic Minya and Shamak continuously, on top of using E・M・T, Subaru—who was already unreliable as Beatrice's external mana tank—was probably right on the verge of running empty.
He felt thoroughly inclined to lament his low aptitude for this parallel world.

"In the Empire, I was doing ridiculously well though... Maybe when my body was shrunk, it could hold more dreams or something?"

"That was an utterly unnatural state where your Od was distorted, so Betty does not have a very fond impression of it, I suppose. Who knows how many human experiments were conducted to achieve that, I suppose?"

"Even after he's no longer an enemy and we've already left the Empire, Olbart-san's—or rather, the Shinobi's dark side—just keeps getting deeper..."

Indeed, kneading Od—the soul itself—into whatever shape one wished didn't seem like something one could master without practice. Which meant Olbart, or the Shinobi Village, had been practicing kneading souls all along; the title "Vicious Old Man" was certainly no exaggeration.

"—It seems the soldiers are occupied with dealing with that rumbling from earlier. The alertness mingling in the wind has receded, and the tension has thinned out. It is safe to assume we have shaken them off for now."

And entering the conversation there was Crusch, who had been scanning their surroundings with a sharp gaze.
Illuminating the sewer with a Lagmite ore lamp dimmed to conserve light, she narrowed her amber eye—the one uncovered by her eyepatch—and fell silent as she stared at Subaru getting his arm treated.
For a moment, Subaru found her reaction strange, but he quickly realized that it was the healing light—something familiar and unforgettable to Crusch—that had brought something to her mind.

"...It's not really a conversation we can have right now, is it."

Honestly, the situation was moving at a breakneck pace.
From Subaru's perspective, ever since that scene with Al at the Pleiades Watchtower, things had been happening continuously non-stop without a single moment for his nerves to rest. The rise of the Divine Dragon Church, Crusch's recovery, the subsequent uproar at Miklotov's mansion, turning Natsuki Subaru into a fugitive—it was disastrous.

*Truth be told, if he got to meet Crusch, he had wanted to talk about Ferris's dismissal.*

He had wanted to confirm with Crusch her reasons and true intentions behind dismissing Ferris, somehow negotiate a middle ground, and help repair the damaged relationship between the two.
Ferris would surely call it arrogant meddling, but he wouldn't have cared.
An intolerably selfish, arrogant man in the eyes of others—that was Natsuki Subaru.

"—To you all..."

"...Hm?"

"—To you all, I am grateful. For following after me in a situation like that."

"――――"

Subaru fell silent as Crusch suddenly lowered her voice to convey those words.
Feeling that this single phrase of gratitude rewarded the last half-day spent fleeing on foot felt embarrassingly cheap of him. But at the very least, if he hadn't chosen to chase after her back then, he never would have drawn those words from her.

"Still, on my end, I can't just casually say 'you're welcome.' The burn hurts, and while you shielded me from Tiga, the fight started because of you in the first place, Crusch-san. Plus, I'm definitely causing Emilia-tan and the others endless worry and trouble, and admirable Beako here is resolving herself to spend four hundred years of hell with me..."

"Subaru, Subaru, just because your thoughts aren't organized, do not recklessly rub and stroke Betty's head, I suppose. It ruins Betty's cuteness, I suppose."

"Ah, sorry... It really is! Ruined like this... I can hardly bear to look at it!"

"That is impossible, I suppose! Even if my hair is disheveled, far from dimming Betty's cuteness, it merely unearths a brand-new charm point, I suppose!"

Her hair disheveled by Subaru's restless hands, Beatrice shouted back while being completely helpless, unable to interrupt the treatment.
Their usual back-and-forth eased the tension that had stiffened him straight down to his organs, saving him just a bit. —Right when Subaru let out a subtle sigh of relief.

"Heh."

"—! Did you just laugh, Crusch-san?"

At the sound lightly vibrating the air, Subaru couldn't help but widen his eyes.
Looking over, Crusch, standing against the sewer's filthy wall, had a hand placed over her mouth. Meeting Subaru's gaze, she offered a brief "My apologies," and then—

"Watching that exchange so thoroughly lacking in tension, I could not contain myself. How immature of me."

"I've got no reply regarding the tension thing, but this is way better than staying strained forever. Up until now, all you've shown me is a scary face."

"A terrifying face, is it? Given this eye of mine, perhaps so."

"Nah, even with one eye like that, you're still a beauty, Crusch-san."

"――――"
"Subaru..."

"Huh?! Did the mood just sour again?!"

Crusch narrowed her eye as she pressed a hand to her eyepatch, while Beatrice looked at Subaru in exasperation, causing him to panic. However, Crusch let out a faint sigh, slowly shook her head, and—

"You are... no, you all are strong."

"If you're talking about me and Beako, you were right to make it plural. Without Beako, I'm just part of the background crowd. But you know..."

"But?"

"...That kind of strength is something you used to have too, Crusch-san."

At Subaru's reply, Crusch's lips pressed into a tight line.
Seeing that, Subaru reflected on his words, wondering if he had stepped too far. The expression that had momentarily relaxed was now hardened once more.
As Subaru hesitated on what to say next, Beatrice patted his arm.

"There, all done, I suppose. For now, it should be nothing serious, I suppose."

"Mm, I see, thanks. ...Yeah, it feels a bit stiff, but not bad."

"Leave the handkerchief as it is, I suppose. It might get itchy, but the skin might be raw, so be sure not to accidentally scratch it, I suppose."

"That sounds terrifying, so I got it loud and clear. —Now then."

Offering double thanks to Beatrice for giving him a breath of air at just the right moment, Subaru turned back to Crusch. The wound was treated, and they had shaken off their pursuers for the moment. Which meant there was a mountain of things he wanted to talk to her about properly.
First, what he wanted to confirm right away was—

"Crusch-san, do you have any leads or someone to turn to in a situation like this?"

"Someone to turn to... Before things ended up like this, I did. However, I cut him down with my own hands."

"...The way you put that, could it be that the person you were counting on was..."

"Indeed. It was Miklotov McMahon, before his evil scheme came to light."

"Oh my god..."

With her matter-of-fact nod, Subaru felt his vision darken as he looked up, his gaze rebounding off the sewer's filthy, low ceiling.
Subaru himself had felt the same way, so he couldn't criticize her, but there were simply too many entry points that made one want to rely on Miklotov regarding information in the Kingdom. With the matter of the Divine Dragon Church, even Crusch, placed in a dire position as a Royal Selection candidate, had thought so—which spoke volumes of Miklotov's personal virtue.
However—

"That Miklotov-san was harboring a nation-overthrowing level of evil scheme in his heart...?"

"Indeed. —Lord McMahon welcomed my unannounced visit warmly. However, stating that he had an unscheduled guest arriving, he had me wait in a separate room..."

"In a separate room. And what about it?"

"From where I was placed in the guest room, I saw suspicious wind regarding the guest he was about to receive. Therefore, when I tried to question his intentions, the residents of the mansion stood in my way—and the rest is as you know."

"What we saw..."

Miklotov cut down, and the servants and guards fallen all across the mansion. Even having the sequence of events leading up to her slashing them explained again, it was an answer he couldn't easily nod along to.
In the end, the problem was that the sole basis for Crusch suspecting Miklotov and the others was a suspicious wind—that is, stemming from her Divine Protection of Wind Indication.

"――――"

Naturally, Subaru could not share the Divine Protection of Wind Indication in which she placed absolute trust. Thus, outside of his desire to believe Crusch, he couldn't hold any certainty.
And that was—

*—Just like me back then.*

Talking with Crusch, Subaru experienced a tight, painful feeling in his chest.
It was the exact inverted setup of the exchange Subaru once had with Crusch—back when the Royal Selection had just begun, when Subaru's eyes were clouded with hatred for Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti, the Sin Archbishop of Sloth, who had caused catastrophic damage to Roswaal Manor and Arlam Village.

"Back then, blood rushed to my head, my vision was completely clouded because nobody would understand, and I couldn't listen to a single word from anyone around me..."

Crusch had seen right through that distortion clouded by hatred and rage, mercilessly cutting down his foolishly defiant attitude. —This was a reenactment of those roles reversed.
Crusch's current speech and behavior were just like Subaru's when he threw around information obtained through Return by Death—a method ungraspable to others—and threw a tantrum when nobody understood.
Of course, Crusch appeared to behave far more rationally than the foolish Subaru back then.
However, unlike Subaru who was powerless, Crusch possessed both power and position.

"—Yeah, I can't do it."

"...Subaru?"

"Now that I realize she's just like I was, leaving Crusch-san behind here isn't an option for me anymore. Whether it's helping her or stopping her."

As Subaru bit his lip and clenched his fists, Beatrice quietly watched him from his side.
Back then, Crusch had been facing the major event of the White Whale subjugation. Even so, she had patiently continued to engage with Subaru—ignorant, stubborn, and rude as he was. And in the end, she had lent an ear to Subaru's words and provided the help needed to fulfill his grand wish.
This time, it was Subaru's turn to do that. —If she was right, he would stand beside her; if she was wrong, he would stand in her way to stop her. For that purpose.

"Crusch-san, I—"

"—Wait, Natsuki Subaru."

Just as he was about to voice his newly clicked-in-place resolve, Crusch held him back.
However, to think his momentum had been ruined, Crusch's expression was far too serious. Closing the distance between herself and Subaru's group, she shielded them behind her back and narrowed her eyes into the gloom of the sewer.
Even looking in the same direction of her alert stance, nothing was reflected in Subaru's eyes, but—

"—Someone is there. Even if you hold your breath, your emotions ride upon the wind. You cannot escape my eyes."

"—! We should have thrown off the pursuing soldiers..."

"They are likely a bunch with motives separate from those ones. I shall inform you: maintaining silence will bring you no benefit. Or perhaps if I assume hostile intent—"

"—Wait, wait, wait! Don't act rashly! Okay! I get it already!"

It was just as Crusch, dagger drawn without anyone realizing, prepared her posture. Prompted by the rising tension and her vigilant voice, a flustered voice called back from the darkness further down the channel.
After the surprise that Crusch's eyes had been correct, Subaru frowned at the voice itself, thinking *'Hm?'* —He recognized that voice.

"That voice just now..."

"Damn it! I'm coming over now! Got it? Don't attack, alright?! I've got zero intention of picking a fight with you bastards!"

"That depends on your move. Allow me to state that I cannot make light promises. Though, you are one and we are three; your numerical disadvantage is—"

"—Are you Chin by any chance?"

"What?"

Crusch, refusing to lower her guard against the shouting man, raised her eyebrow at Subaru's question. Beatrice, drawing closer to Subaru's side, also murmured softly, "Chin..."

"If I remember correctly, that was a man we saw in Priestella, I suppose."

"Yeah, that's right. He's being taken care of at Felt's place right now..."

"—I came here 'cause that Felt told me to! Tch, how embarrassing."

Tch-ing in displeasure, a man with both hands raised slowly emerged from the darkness.
And just as expected, it was a man with sanpaku eyes even nastier than Subaru's—Rachins, one of the alleyway trio Ton-Chin-Kan who had somehow joined Felt's camp.

"Oh, Chin...! Every time you show up, you give me a fresh surprise."

"Shut it! I told you before, I'm not Chin! It's Rachins!"

"Chin, I suppose."

"I said I'm not Chin, squirt!"

"A reckless Chin, I suppose."

Leaving Subaru deeply moved to see a familiar face after so long, Rachins and Beatrice engaged in a fruitless argument.
In truth, it had been since Priestella since he last saw Rachins—specifically, back during Sirius's outrage that triggered the Witch Cult's terrible gathering. Of course, he had confirmed his safety afterwards to an extent, but it was a relief to see him alive and well like this.

"Man, but really, it's great to meet a familiar face. There's stuff I want to ask you—"

"Halt right there, this 'Chin'. Unlike Natsuki Subaru, I do not know you."

"Crusch-san?!"

In contrast to Subaru, who reflexively rejoiced at meeting an acquaintance, Crusch's guard—dagger in hand with a sharp glare directed at Rachins—had not loosened in the slightest.
Come to think of it, Rachins hadn't stayed at the Water Plumage Pavilion, and after his contact with Sirius he had withdrawn from the front line, meaning there had been no occasion for him to meet Crusch face to face.
Crusch's caution was only natural, or so it seemed, but—

"Hah? You don't know me? Duchess Karsten sure is heartless, ain't she?"

"...What?"

"Well, I ain't in a position to talk casually with high-and-mighty nobles like a duchess, but you're one of the people who learned swordplay from my old man, ain't ya? If you're gonna say you don't know me at all, then my old man must not've done much of a job."

"――――"

Spitting those words out while sticking out his long tongue, Rachins left Crusch with a bewildered look. Meanwhile, Subaru on his end could only be floored by Rachins' brazen nerve.

"Hey, no matter how you look at it, you pulled that lie out of nowhere..."

"It ain't a lie! ...Believe it or not, I come from a decent family line. Countable on one hand maybe, but I've even come face-to-face with the Duchess's house before."

"Seriously? 'Cause if it's not true, your imagination is terrifyingly out of control..."

Still, hearing him speak with such confidence made it seem like the truth, proving that neither human minds nor words were things you could blindly rely on.
Because of that, Subaru timidly peeked at Crusch's expression, but—

"—. ―――. ―――――――. Could you be... the son of Lord Rickert Hoffman, who served as the Royal Instructor?"

"Ha! You hear that? Looks like I rang a bell in Duchess Karsten's memory!"

"For real?!"
"It appears to be for real, I suppose."

Taking a considerable amount of time, Crusch squeezed out a name. At Rachins' attitude confirming it, Subaru and Beatrice involuntarily exchanged glances.
To think that Rachins was truly from Lugunica nobility.

"Then how'd you end up as an alleyway thug picking on the weak?"

"Shut up! People got their own circumstances, don't go prying!"

"If it is circumstances, Betty and Subaru have plenty as well, I suppose. And it seems your coming here is not unrelated to those circumstances either, I suppose."

"Ngh...!"

"Yeah. Just like Beako says. ...Crusch-san, put that down. Chin's probably safe. I guarantee it."

"—. If there is a suspicious gesture, or a wind blows, I will strike."

"I get gestures, but how the hell am I supposed to take responsibility for the wind...?!"

Faced with Rachins trembling violently as he said that, Crusch slowly lowered her dagger and relaxed her combat posture. That said, she didn't sheath the dagger, keeping her single eye fixed firmly on Rachins' movements.
At any rate—

"Again, I'm really glad you came. How did you find us?"

"Nothin' fancy. I wasn't the only one searchin' for you. Gaston and Camberley are wanderin' around checkin' other places too."

"Then you simply lucked out on the winning ticket, I suppose? It really was nothing impressive, I suppose."

"Shut up, squirt. We found you, so who cares?! Ah—so? What the hell happened with you guys?"

"Regarding that, it's a long story and I'm not confident you'd believe me anyway. So let me hear your side first. —How are Emilia and the others?"

Aware that it was impolite, Subaru brushed aside Rachins' question and asked.
After all, ever since escaping Miklotov's mansion, there hadn't been a single moment to get reliable information from the outside. Anxiety, concern, and impatience had turned his mind completely to mush.

"The fact that we're with Crusch-san must have reached Emilia and the others by now. Which means I'm causing them a dead-serious amount of worry and trouble..."

Double the burden: emotional stress from worrying, and physical strain from being questioned by the Kingdom. On top of that, the camp must be in total disarray. —However, Subaru's expectations were betrayed.
And in a way he couldn't have imagined in his wildest dreams.

"Ah..."

"—?"

When asked, Rachins widened his sanpaku eyes slightly, then wore a face that was uncharacteristically considerate of Subaru, and spoke.
It was—

"Your half-elf was detained at the castle. And the rest of your camp ran off and went into hiding. —Just like you, they're all wanted fugitives."


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　△▼△▼△▼


—Emilia's detention at the Royal Castle, and Rem, Otto, and the others fleeing the Royal Villa.

It was bad news so shocking it felt like heaven and earth had flipped upside down.
He chewed over the words Rachins told him over and over, ruminating again and again so his brain could swallow every bit of it. In that manner, he piled effort upon effort to somehow comprehend it.
He did, but—

"W-Why did something like that happen...?"

"...Even Betty did not see this coming, I suppose."

Spilling those words with a pale, stunned face, Subaru realized his teeth were chattering uncontrollably.
Apparently, events that far exceeded comprehension could strip even warmth from a human being. The cold blood circulating through his body gave him the illusion that his fingers and toes were about to die from frostbite.

Holding hands with Subaru, Beatrice gently shared her warmth with him. Even so, his body's trembling wouldn't stop. —The situation was bad, far beyond Subaru's shallow imagination.

"Because of me... because of me, Emilia and everyone..."

"Tch, I get you wanna whine about stupid stuff like that, but stop actin' like it's all your fault. In a situation like this, no matter how you look at it, you've been framed."

"Huh?"

With a high-pitched ringing in his ears, Subaru blinked as he staggered from dizziness. At Subaru's reaction, Rachins crossed his arms and clicked his tongue in irritation.
Just now, Rachins spoke as if it were obvious that Subaru and his friends were "framed".

"Framed... What do you mean..."

"It means what it means! Don't you think it's weird? There's way too many convenient bad things happenin' all at once left and right."

At the dazed Subaru, Rachins shrugged with a sigh.
At his phrasing, in place of Subaru whose brain felt partly numb and dull, Beatrice responded with:

"...That Betty and Subaru being chased, and Emilia being confined in the castle... are part of it, I suppose?"

"—. It ain't just that. The reason your camp's folks bolted without asking questions is probably 'cause they realized the enemy was playing for keeps."

"Playing for keeps...?!"

"It's a figure of speech, a figure of speech! In reality, they can't just up and murder a Royal Selection candidate all of a sudden. ...That's why that rebellious tomboy was able to send us out in the first place."

As a dangerous word popped up, Subaru's eyes went wide, prompting Rachins to kick the dirty wall.
However, contrary to his blunt attitude, thanks to Rachins patiently explaining things, Subaru's thinking ability finally began to recover bit by bit.
In short—

"Someone is pulling strings behind the scenes to drop Emilia out of the Royal Selection. And my actions were conveniently used for that, getting Emilia caught in the castle. Otto and the others realized what the mastermind was planning and fled before they could be immobilized... is that it?"

"There is one thing Betty would like to add to that, I suppose. —There is a possibility that Emilia was not the only target of this villain, I suppose."

"Not just Emilia?"

"――――"

When Subaru raised an eyebrow at her point, Beatrice nodded and gestured with her chin. Following her movement, he saw Rachins poking the inside of his cheek with his tongue, wearing a grim face.
It was visibly the face of someone whose sensitive spot had been prodded—

"...Could it be that the reason Chin came here wasn't just because Emilia and Felt became friends, I suppose?"

"Damn it, this squirt notices too much..."

"Filling in where Subaru falls short is Betty's job, I suppose. Besides, calling me squirt over and over... do you wish to be taught a lesson, human?"

"Then teach your master how to call people by their proper names first, damn it!"

Raising his voice at Beatrice, with whom he seemed to share terrible chemistry, Rachins let out a deep sigh. Then, after stammering for a moment—

"...The one dragged into the castle wasn't just your half-elf. Felt was too. They slapped false charges on 'em, and that bastard Reinhard has had his hands tied too."

"Reinhard?! And Felt got captured too... If that's the case, then the target isn't just Emilia, but the Royal Selection itself?!"

"You're late to the party. That's what Felt was suspicious about, so before she got dragged to the castle, she ordered us, 'Find Big Bro and his crew. If you find them, don't leave them alone.' Thanks to that, we ended up runnin' all over this stinkin' drain."

It was understandable why Rachins had hesitated to speak regarding their camp's disadvantage, but having this information versus not having it completely changed the entire premise.
Subaru had thought his actions had merely been exploited for a personal attack against Emilia, but apparently the enemy's objective was far bigger than that.

If hands were reaching out for Emilia and Felt, and by extension Subaru and Reinhard, and even their respective camps, there was no way it was a coincidence.
A secret plot targeting the Royal Selection that would shake the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica to its core. And Subaru just happened to have heard of a scale this grand before.

"Crusch-san, did you hear that?! What you were saying..."

Lifting his face enthusiastically, Subaru addressed Crusch, who had been watching their conversation.
Until Rachins joined them, Subaru hadn't been able to fully believe the validity of Crusch's statements based on her Divine Protection of Wind Indication, but the torrent of overwhelming information possessed enough persuasive power to lend credence to those doubts.
Perhaps Miklotov's scheme, which had driven Crusch to draw her sword, was also tied to this malice attempting to ruin the Royal Selection.
However—

"...Wait, Crusch-san?"

No response. Finding that suspicious, Subaru looked at Crusch and doubted his eyes. —Crusch, standing against the wall, was nodding off and dozing away.
To fall asleep in the middle of a conversation, while maintaining vigilance no less, was a completely out-of-character, out-of-place attitude for Crusch.
Calling out as he noticed it, Crusch looked back at Subaru with noticeably vacant eyes, and—

"...My apologies, a sudden drowsiness... Likely... the strain from this left eye..."

"Strain...? Wait, since when did you have that eye in the first place—"

"I leave... the rest to you... Subaru-sama..."

"Hey!"

Perhaps reaching her limit right there, her upper body swayed unsteadily before Crusch suddenly collapsed forward. Hurriedly catching her body, the dagger slipped from Crusch's drained hands, clattering sharply against the ground of the channel.
Yet, the person in question paid no mind to the sound, already letting out quiet breaths of sleep.

"You've got to be kidding! In the middle of such an important conversation... she fell asleep!"

"Hey, what's up with the Duchess? People were sayin' all kinds of wild stuff, like she lost her memories, her body broke down, and now she's lost her mind."

"Huh?! The hell are you saying about Crusch-san! I'll knock you into next week, you bastard!"

"I wasn't the one sayin' it! What's your problem?!"

"Goodness, I suppose! Do not fight over every little thing, I suppose! —By the looks of it, she is merely sleeping, I suppose. Just as she declared herself, it seems her fatigue has exceeded its limits, I suppose."

Stepping between Subaru and Rachins, Beatrice passed judgment as she looked down at Crusch's sleeping face. In Subaru's eyes as well, the unconscious Crusch didn't seem to be in any particular pain, appearing simply as though her exhausted body was demanding a mud-like slumber.
However, as Beatrice said, if this was the side effect of the 'Dragon's Eye' Crusch mentioned, then that unnatural thing was certainly not something that could be left alone.
Specifically—

"...The miracle of the Divine Dragon Church, and Tiga's testimony. There's no way this is unrelated to the situation where Royal Selection candidates are being targeted."

Frankly speaking, it was impossible to think these things weren't connected. However, paradoxically, that led directly to suspecting the organization known as the Divine Dragon Church.
The Divine Dragon Church was a religion deeply and strongly rooted throughout the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica. Even if they recognized it as a potential enemy, just how far did the enemies extend?
Given the situation, Tiga had to be suspected, but what about Sakura? —What about Filore?

"If my worst imagination hits the mark, I'll be down, but Emilia will be heartbroken..."

Even without that, Emilia was currently detained at the castle, unable to know the safety of Otto and the others, let alone Subaru and Beatrice.
To relieve even a bit of that mental burden from her, he had to make a move. The best possible move.
Having made up his mind, Subaru hoisted the sleeping Crusch onto his back and held her securely. Meanwhile, Beatrice picked up the dropped dagger, sheathed it, and nodded at him.

"Rachins, we're not getting anywhere here. Since you went through the trouble of coming, you must have somewhere easier to talk in mind, right? Let's move."

"I don't like takin' orders, but I won't pick a fight over it. ...Though, I can't guarantee how well we can slip past eyes in the Royal Capital as it is now."

"...Man, it really sinks into my bones how bad of a situation this is."

The enemy was utilizing a vast, truly vast power, scheming to ensnare Subaru and his companions.
Perhaps meeting Rachins here and hearing his story was a miracle brought by sheer coincidence. Or maybe calling it a miracle would be an insult to Rachins, who had tracked them down on Felt's orders.
In any case—

"I want Crusch-san to rest somewhere quiet too. Once her body and mind get a bit of rest, we should be able to talk properly. So—"

"—Well, isn't that splendid! Right at this very instant, this very split second, I was just dying to invite the fugitives who've thrown the entire Royal Capital into chaos, yes!"

"—Wha!?"

Instantly, at the wildly out-of-place voice echoing through the sewer, Subaru—carrying Crusch—along with Beatrice and Rachins, turned around with wide, frozen eyes.
The voice came from beyond the darkness of the channel, where the faint light of the Lagmite ore couldn't reach. However, Rachins clicked his tongue with a "Tch" and threw something into the dark—

"Oho, let there be light! Kuhahaha, how remarkably thoughtful, how very attentive!"

A hard clatter struck the sewer floor, followed by a dazzling white light illuminating the gloom. What he threw was a piece of Lagmite ore itself, which glowed upon impact.
Just as Rachins had intended with his quick judgment, the light exposed the figure hiding in the dark to Subaru and the others as they turned.
It was—

"...A mask?"

"Hey, hey, hey! The very first impression you have upon seeing me is 'a mask'? Now that's far too literal, far too plain! Exercise a little more creativity! Choose your words passionately, fervently, as if wooing the woman of your dreams! Kuhahahahaha!"

Saying that, the person shaking with laughter was a man—or at least, all they could tell was that he was a tall man. Because he was clad in black garb that completely covered his entire body, with a pierrot-like mask adorning his face.

In the darkness, the faint white clown mask emphasized his sheer abnormality.
His speech, attitude, and the vicious glare directed from behind the mask made it instantly clear after a mere five-second encounter. —This clown mask guy is bad news.
As if to prove it—

"You lot may go ahead and die. Because the only one I have business with is that sleeping woman over there!"

In an instant, a massive flame—far beyond comparison to the light of the Lagmite ore—illuminated the dark sewer, sending an enormous wave of heat surging toward Subaru and his companions.
