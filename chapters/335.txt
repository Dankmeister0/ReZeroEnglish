Arc 5 Chapter 19: "Theatrical Malice"

A word tossed out, half familiar, half formal.
At that bandaged figure's little speech, everyone in the crowd looking up found their voices sealed shut.

Part of it was the sheer bizarreness of the figure standing overhead, eye-catching in the worst way. Part of it was that the words that reached their ears were not the kind you could let slide. But those were, in the end, nothing more than secondary effects.

The reason no one could tear their eyes, their attention, away from that figure was simple and plain. It could be called the most primitive of biological instincts.

—No fool, faced with a threat to their life, would ever look away from it.

"Eh, ah?"
"Just now, what did that person say?"
"You're kidding, right? The Witch Cult, here—?"

Behind the initial wave of unease, understanding spread slowly through the crowd. But no one could move to act, not right there and then. Everyone doubted their own ears, and could only trade glances with the people around them, sharing their mutual incomprehension.

"What the hell did that bastard just say? Did you hear that?!"

That went for Rachins too, who'd spotted Subaru and was pushing his way over.
He cut through the crowd, glancing up warily as he closed in on Subaru, but Subaru himself had put one step of distance between himself and the crowd, fixing his gaze upward.

If he looked away now, even for a moment, it would be irreversible.
There was no need to doubt what manner of thing stood up there.

—That thing was the same breed of creature as Petelgeuse.

"And on top of that, Romanée-Conti...?"

The bandaged figure's self-introduction—Sirius Romanée-Conti.
Absurd as it sounded, that family name, Romanée-Conti, was the same as Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti's. Not that there could be any true blood relation to Petelgeuse, who had been a corrupted Spirit.

"Don't tell me all the Sin Archbishops go around sharing a family name."

If there really were a Romanée-Conti family out there, that would be nightmare enough on its own. A prestigious Witch Cult lineage that produced Sin Archbishops generation after generation—the Romanée-Conti family. The setting was so sloppy, so warped, it practically reeked bad enough to make your nose wrinkle.

Rising up alongside that thought was the bottomless anger toward the Witch Cult that lived inside Subaru. It wasn't 'Gluttony,' the one he was endlessly chasing after—but if a Sin Archbishop stood there regardless—

"—I'll catch him, and beat every last thing out of him."

Force open, by whatever means necessary, the path leading to 'Gluttony.'
Having decided, Subaru immediately calmed the surface of his blazing heart and turned his awareness to the connection with Beatrice inside his chest. If he called, Beatrice would appear at his side. That was the effect of the Contract Pass linking a contractor to their Contracted Spirit. Deep within his body, that hot connection to another—he reached to grasp it, to pull it close—

"—All right! That's enough!"

"—!?"

Just as he was about to summon Beatrice, a dry, cracking report and a booming voice thundered from above.
It was the sound of hands clapped together so loudly that Subaru almost mistook it for something that had echoed across the whole city. Catching his breath, eyes wide, Subaru looked up to see the bandaged figure roll its bulging eyes across the crowd.

"It took all of twenty-two seconds for everyone to quiet down. But thank you for quieting down. I am very pleased. And also..."

Offering thanks laced with sarcasm, the bandaged— Sirius swayed left and right, hands still clasped together. It looked positively delighted, but chains still dangled from both arms, and the sound of them scraping against the tower's wall grated horribly on the nerves.

"You there, and you, and you gentlemen over there, and you right in front. I'm sorry, please don't be so angry. I feel very bad about taking up everyone's precious time. I'm sorry. So, thank you."

"Ngh...!"

Writhing and twisting, Sirius spoke as though pleading in earnest.
The reason Subaru couldn't instantly crush that with a "cut the crap" was simple: the people Sirius had just pointed at and told not to be angry were, unmistakably, Subaru and the others standing with him.

Looking around, the other four Sirius had singled out—probably people with some measure of skill in a fight—were a beast-man with a sword at his hip, a woman with an eyepatch, and Rachins, whose face had gone pale.
What had just happened was unmistakable: Sirius had preemptively called out the ones poised to seize an opening and make a move, warning them that their intentions were already seen through.

"—————"

Feeling sweat trickle down his forehead, Subaru held off on summoning Beatrice.
Even knowing full well how terrifying it would be to let the Witch Cult strike first, he had no choice but to hold back. In the plaza around Subaru, more than thirty people had been packed together in a clump. If the rule was "don't let them get the first move," then this situation had already gone wrong from the start.

Subaru exchanged glances, trying to communicate with the other three Sirius had singled out.
Picking up on Subaru's intent, the beast-woman with the air of an adventurer and the rough-faced townsman gave a small nod. Only Rachins couldn't quite hide the hesitation on his face, even under Subaru's gaze, and merely looked away, uncertain.

Rachins's trump card, no doubt, was surely his ability to summon Reinhard—his strongest hidden hand.
Yesterday, Reinhard had ordered Rachins to send up a signal if anything happened. There was an agreed-upon signal between them, and carrying it out would summon Reinhard to this very spot. And if Reinhard made it in time, then Sirius—Sin Archbishop or not—would be no match for him. He could be struck down.

But what of the sacrifice that summoning him might cost? That, surely, was what made Rachins hesitate.
If they were willing to accept sacrifice, they had a means to eliminate Sirius on hand. But was this the moment to play that card right now? Could it be written off as a necessary cost?

"Yes, thank you. It seems everyone has calmed down a little. I understand the anxiety. The very sound of 'Witch Cult' doesn't leave a good impression on anyone. So I don't intend to do anything special either. It's just that today, there's something I absolutely need to confirm, and that's why I'm borrowing everyone's time like this."

"Something you need to... confirm?"

"I'm sorry, please don't get restless. If everyone speaks to me all at once, I'm not very smart, so it troubles me. It makes me sad. That wouldn't be good, would it? If there's something you want to say, talk to me. I feel indebted for taking up everyone's time, so I'll answer pretty much anything, you know?"

The posture of feigned solicitude, combined with a strangely rational manner of speaking, only stirred up more unease. Even baring rows of teeth as though lacking lips, with only the eyes exposed on that bandaged getup, playing at being a reasonable person only produced the natural revulsion you'd expect.

Everyone there was surely thinking the same thing. Everyone kept their mouths shut in response to Sirius's proposal, only trading glances with those nearby. If that was how it stood, then—

"Mind if I take you up on that and ask something?"

If no one else would raise a hand first, then Natsuki Subaru was the one to raise it.
Feeling a ripple of surprise spread outward from himself, Subaru kept his eyes fixed on Sirius up above. Sirius looked down at Subaru, who had raised both hand and voice.

"Oh, please, go right ahead. Thank you. You're the hot-tempered gentleman from before, aren't you. I'm delighted that you're willing to talk with me. What would you like to ask?"

"I don't know what business you've got here, but I've got girls waiting on me. Four of them, in fact. So it'd help a lot if you could wrap this up quick and let us go."

"Oh my! That's terrible, I'm sorry. But my, aren't you the sly one, sir. Keeping four girls waiting on you—isn't that every man's dream? What a naughty man. Are you troubling them, making them cry, making them sad, making them suffer? That's bad, that's not good, that must not be, that cannot be forgiven, not good, absolutely not."

"H-hey?"

The bouncing lilt in Sirius's voice shrank smaller as he spoke, and with his head bowed he began muttering rapid-fire. But hearing Subaru's bewildered voice, Sirius snapped his head back up.

"Oh no, oh no, I was about to get emotional there. I'm sorry. I try to be careful, but if I'm not paying attention I get carried away before I know it. Thank you for worrying about me. So, um... releasing everyone, was it?"

"...Yeah, that's right. If you could handle this peacefully, I'd appreciate it."

"I'm truly sorry for making you go to the trouble of asking. But it's all right. I'm actually rather famous, even among the Witch Cult, for being one of the quiet ones who doesn't cause trouble for people. It's the others who tend to cause a bit more trouble, which I find rather disappointing in myself."

The conversation was flowing more sensibly than Subaru had expected, and he found himself growing suspicious of the person he was talking to.
The soft manner, the attitude that never broke from a stance of dialogue—and thinking about it as they talked, it occurred to Subaru: could Sirius perhaps be a woman?
He couldn't tell from the face, hidden by bandages, nor from the body, wrapped in an ill-fitting coat. The voice was high, in a sense, but it sounded less feminine than mechanical—something other than a natural human voice—so it wasn't much use as a basis for judgment.
Still, Subaru found himself vaguely thinking that Sirius might be a woman.

In fact, nothing in Sirius's demeanor or words so far suggested a particularly high degree of danger. The initial appearance, the affiliation, the look of the thing had all put Subaru on guard to an abnormal degree, but setting that aside, it wasn't an exaggeration to say the conversation was going far more smoothly than it ever had with Priscilla.
Among the surrounding crowd too, the tension that had held tight and unbroken through the first minute had loosened somewhat, and many were simply, purely waiting to see where the conversation would go.
Subaru, too, felt something like tension draining out of him with an audible sigh.

"Thank you. And I'm sorry. It seems I startled everyone. But seeing you willing to listen like this makes me very glad too."

"Doesn't mean I've accepted or forgiven anything. But go on, tell us your business. Start there."

"Of course, thank you for asking. Then let's get to the main topic. The reason I appeared before everyone like this in the first place was because there was a little something I wanted to confirm."

Swaying its body, Sirius rubbed the chains on both arms together, ringing out a high-pitched sound.
Looking closely, that figure seemed less eerie now and more, somehow, comical. If you thought of it as some kind of clown or performer, it wasn't so far removed from the ordinary world after all, was it.

Subaru's expression relaxed, and he let go of the tension he'd been coiling up inside. He no longer felt any particular need to summon Beatrice. Let's just hear Sirius out and get out of here as quickly as possible.

"So, what did you want to ask?"
"Yeah, come on, hurry up and tell us!"
"That's right, that's right. Hurry up or I'll be late for work."

At Subaru's prompting, voices rose from the crowd egging Sirius on too.
When the last man pointed up at the Sorcery Crystal above Sirius and shouted that, a wave of laughter burst out.
Caught up in the spreading whirl of laughter, Subaru's mouth loosened into a grin despite himself. Sirius, too, seemed to have been won over by the mood, placing a hand on its own head with a gesture of embarrassment.

"I'm sorry, I'm sorry. Truly, I'm sorry. I know everyone's busy. I'll finish talking right away, so please bear with me just a little longer."

"So hurry it up already!"

"Yes! Then let's do that. You see, what I want to confirm is a simple thing. Quite simply, it's something about 'love' I want to confirm. Ohh, how embarrassing."

Whether or not the bandages were hiding a blush, Sirius pressed a hand to its face as if trying to hide embarrassment over its own statement. Sirius, grinning despite itself under the pressure of everyone's amusement, seemed to have nowhere left to hide.

"I thought I'd be laughed at, but having it turn out exactly as I expected is rather troubling too. But thank you for listening. And while I'm at it with the thank-yous, I have a favor to ask."

"A favor?"

"I was hoping everyone might indulge me a little in confirming my 'love.' I'm sorry, I know I'm being awfully selfish."

Fidgeting, rubbing the chains on both hands together bashfully, Sirius made the request.
At the endearing sight, the crowd's reaction was something like, "oh, is that all?" Subaru too crossed his arms and gave a small nod, feeling he'd witnessed something rather sweet.
At that, Sirius's eyes lit up brightly, and it clapped its hands.

"Really?! Thank you, thank you, I'm sorry. The world really is kind after all. Full to the brim with kindness and love. Every time I realize that, I want to give thanks. Being able to forgive each other, to give way to each other. Perhaps that's exactly why I keep saying 'thank you' and 'I'm sorry'—because I want to confirm that."

"Yeah yeah, we get it! Sirius! So what are you gonna do?"

"Oh, sorry!"

The eyepatched female adventurer egged on the visibly moved Sirius. At the easy familiarity of her voice, like that of a ten-year friend or a schoolmate, Sirius and the woman met eyes and laughed together.
Then, as if finally remembering, Sirius walked over to the window of the Time Tower it had been leaning out of and reached an arm inside. And from within—

"Sorry to keep you waiting so long. Come on, come here."

"—————!"

Speaking in a gentle voice, Sirius pulled something out from beyond the window.
It thrashed within Sirius's arms, twisting its body and groaning—a small human figure, a still-young boy, bound head to toe in chains.

The boy, perhaps around ten years old, had chains wound tightly from his feet up to his shoulders, and even his mouth was bound with a loop of chain, blood dripping from the split corners of his lips. Only his head, the one part left free, moved desperately as he wept, pleading for something.

"I'm sorry it's so cramped. But you're a boy, so you shouldn't cry so much. And I'd like to keep this a secret for you, but it looks like you've even wet yourself. That's embarrassing, and it'd be sad if everyone found out."

"Mmm! Mnnh!!"

"That's right! That's embarrassing!"
"He's a boy, don't cry, don't cry!"
"A man's only allowed to cry three times in his whole life, you know! Hahaha!"

Riding on Sirius's coddling of the sobbing boy, voices rose from the crowd below too, teasing the crybaby boy's condition.
Everyone had gotten past crying and being scared over little things like that at some point. There was probably no malice in it, but voice after voice flew out, more than a little lacking in delicacy.

"Now now, everyone, you mustn't say things like that! It's true he's looking a little small right now, but this boy is a very brave one. Isn't that right, Lusbel-kun?"

"————!"

The boy, bound in chains from head to toe, must have weighed quite a bit, but Sirius lifted him up easily with one arm, gently stroking his head while chiding the jeering crowd.
The boy called Lusbel desperately twisted his neck, as if trying to put even a little distance between his head and Sirius's face nearby.
Even that looked somehow comical and humorous, and though it felt cruel to laugh, people found themselves nearly laughing anyway.

"All right! Now, everyone, pay attention, please. I'm sorry. His name is Lusbel-kun, a nine-year-old boy living here in Priestella. His family name is Kallard—Lusbel Kallard is his real name."

"Mmm! Mmmm!!"

"His father is Musuran Kallard-san. Musuran-san is an observer who keeps the flow of the city's waterways stable, and his mother, Ina Kallard-san, is pregnant. Her belly's just starting to show now, and Lusbel-kun is looking forward to the birth of his little brother or sister—we don't know which yet. The Kallard family's house is in the Third District, and it's Lusbel-kun's daily routine to go play at the city park with his good friend Tina-chan. He and Tina-chan are childhood friends, and the two of them think none too little of each other—Tina-chan is in the position of cheering on Lusbel-kun's dream. Tina-chan, by the way, is a girl with pale blonde curls, a bit of a beauty whose future looks very promising. And the dream that even that Tina-chan cheers him on for is to become a famous adventurer, just like that famous Draffin from the song 'Draffin Betrayed by the Sunset,' and to make lots of friends along the way. It's an earnest, endearing dream, just the kind a boy his age would have. Some people might laugh and call it childish, but I don't think that way at all. Who could laugh at the sincere feelings of a boy? I'm sure Tina-chan felt the same way, which is why she cheers him on from the bottom of her heart. Oh, and speaking of Lusbel-kun aiming to be an adventurer—he's actually very much looking forward to his little sister or brother in his mother's belly. Really, he'd love to set off on his adventure this very instant, but he's holding back for the sake of the sibling about to be born. With such an age gap between them, he'll surely dote on the child terribly. Lusbel-kun is a good boy who can think of others, so I think he'll make a wonderful big brother. I would be so pleased if everyone could cheer on Lusbel-kun's feelings like that too. Oh, that's right, I mustn't forget about Tina-chan. Actually, at first, I was thinking of bringing Tina-chan here instead of Lusbel-kun. I thought a girl might bring me closer to the 'love' I want to confirm than a boy would. But I was so moved by the sight of Lusbel-kun begging me so desperately, with all his heart. I'm sorry. I'm not a very strong-willed person. So my heart changes this way and that... ah, but even calling myself fickle, that's only about my usual attitude—when it comes to feelings for someone, I think I'm quite devoted. Oh dear, how embarrassing. Well, never mind about me. More importantly, about Lusbel-kun and Tina-chan. The two of them are already so head-over-heels for each other now, it makes you wonder what on earth they'll be like as they get older, and it was very, very painful for me to have to tear them apart. So, at the very least, I decided to respect Lusbel-kun's wishes and have him cooperate with me. So even though Lusbel-kun is looking a bit broken down and crying right now, he really is a very brave boy. Thank you, and I'm sorry. I made sure to explain properly so everyone would understand."

"Mmm! Mmmmh! Mmgh!"

Hearing the life story of the chain-bound boy—Lusbel—everyone in the crowd nodded in understanding.
Indeed, however unseemly he looked right now, Lusbel's courage deserved to be praised. People felt they wanted to punch their own selves from a minute ago, who had thought his state odd.
But now was no time to be blaming themselves like that. What should be directed at a brave man was not self-reproach, but something of far greater worth.
And so—

"Lusbel, don't cry! You're the best!"

Subaru raised his voice, praising the courage of the weeping boy.
Once you knew the genuine feeling behind those tears, how could anyone laugh and call it unseemly? Beside Subaru, who had spoken up first, Rachins opened his mouth too.

"That's right, don't you dare cry! You showed us you're a man! So keep showing us how cool you can be, kid!"
"That's it, way to go, Lusbel! You're Priestella's pride!"
"Lusbel! You're wonderful! You're gonna grow up to be a fine man, I just know it!"

Cheers rose up, and everyone there applauded.
It was a beautiful scene, celebrating one boy's devotion and courage, encouraging the goodness in the human heart.
No matter how wretched he might look, no matter how pathetic a state he might be reduced to, the will to protect something precious was itself a shining sword, and people were captivated by that brilliance, wishing that this was how things ought to be.

"Ah, ah... thank you, thank you, thank you! Ah, it really is wonderful! I believed everyone would understand. I believed everyone would celebrate Lusbel-kun's courage just like this! Because there is 'love' in the resolve he showed! I believed that if you knew him, you would love him! Because understanding each other, knowing each other deeply, uniting your feelings as one—that is 'love'!"

"Sirius! Thank you! Thank you!"
"Lusbel-kun!!"

Holding Lusbel aloft with both hands, Sirius wept in streams. Watching the eye-holes of the bandages grow wet, Subaru too felt something hot welling up behind his own eyes.

His shoulder was nudged. Beside him, Rachins was laughing at Subaru looking on the verge of tears. But Subaru didn't miss the tears welling in Rachins's own eyes as he did it.

Looking around, the crowd too was linking shoulders and arms one after another, sharing their feelings with each other. Subaru found himself thinking of watching a World Cup match. When facing off against the world, people bind their hearts to strangers whose faces they've never even seen, wanting to share that joy together.

Right now, exactly that kind of spirit of unity was spreading. There was a genuine bond there.

"Rifts form because we don't know each other well. Conflicts arise precisely because we can't share our feelings with each other. Bonds go unformed because we give up, assuming we're all just different. That's sad. That's nothing but tragedy. Are you sad right now? Is everyone sad right now?"

"No way! None of us are sad, not one of us!"

"Thank you! Then, are you enjoying yourselves? Are you glad?"

"Of course we are! It's been a long time since I felt this good! Thank you, Sirius! Well done, Lusbel!"

A wave of applause spread out, and a circle of gratitude formed around the raised-up Lusbel. Right now, the hearts in that place were one—and what filled it was gratitude toward the two figures standing atop the Time Tower.

Twisting his body, tears streaming, and finally, heedless of the corners of his mouth tearing further, Lusbel bit down on the chain gag and screamed through his chipping teeth.

"Gh, gk! Aaghl! S-stodg! Stodg, geddoud... hedb...!"

"I honor your courage, your love, Lusbel-kun! Look down below. Look at how many people, so very many people, affirming your action! Ah, thank you! I'm sorry, Lusbel-kun. This might not have been what you wanted, but this is what I needed to confirm. Ah, ah, the world really is kind after all!"

Pulling the raised Lusbel back into its arms, Sirius embraced the boy.
Cheers rose at that beautiful sight, and Subaru stuck fingers in his mouth to whistle. Amid the raucous cheering, he saw Lusbel's head drop, stunned.
It was the figure of a man who had fought with everything he had. Even if he'd finally lost the will even to cry, no one there would laugh at him for it.

"So it really was there. 'Love' was there. It existed. Everyone's hearts became one, and one in joy, no less. There's no need for tragedy. I'm sick of a world where someone has to cry. Nobody wants that. Even if hearts must become one, it ought to be through the sharing of joy and delight. No tragedy! No 'Wrath,' either! None of it is necessary!"

"That's right! Who needs tragedy!"

"Ah, the detestable 'Wrath' that shakes the heart! Anger—that is, violent emotion! If intense passion is the sin rooted in the human heart, if it's a karmic burden that can never be cut away, then the heart should be filled with joy instead! Just as everyone's hearts are one, right now, at this very moment!"

Sirius raised its voice high, lifting Lusbel skyward once more.
But this time, the motion didn't stop there. Bathing in everyone's admiring gazes, Sirius threw the raised Lusbel out into the air.

"A thunderous—round of applause!"

"—————!"

The stage Sirius offered up, at its finest, for the figure of Lusbel being hurled through the air.
At the sight of the boy soaring high through the sky like a diving sun, Subaru led the way, and everyone, all at once, clapped their hands together.
Applause roaring like thunder blessed Lusbel flying through the air.

That small body spun round and round, but upon reaching the peak of its arc, it fell in a straight line back down. Lusbel came falling headfirst toward the ground.
The crowd hurriedly scattered, clearing space at the point of impact.

It was a valiant's triumphant return.
The unceasing applause honored the falling boy.

"Mmmmmmh—!!"

Craning his neck, staring straight down at the ground, Lusbel raised his voice.
He twisted his surely exhausted body with all his remaining strength, resisting to the very end, refusing to give up trying to dodge the hard stone ground rushing up to meet him.
Everyone there saw the strength of a human being in that, and wept.

And then—

"—Ah, what a kind world!"

Sirius cried out, an instant before the impact.
Hearing that voice, the crowd's applause, unified as one, rose higher still—

"—————"

There came the sound of something hard and brittle crushing, like an egg dropped on a floor, and the world before their eyes turned bright red.
Smashed from head to toe against the unyielding ground, what had been Lusbel transformed into a mass of red flesh, fragments scattering across the plaza—the valiant, blown to pieces.

—And the instant that sight registered—

"Bleh..."

The sound of the crushed egg rang on like unceasing applause.
The plaza became a pool of bright red blood.
That was the last of it.

"For the get-together after the song, don't you think it'd be nice if Natsuki-sama prepared some snacks? I'm sure if you got some sweets ready, hearts would flutter and everyone would grow that much closer, don't you think? Don't you think?"

The instant he thought he'd blinked, a dark-skinned girl stood before him.
A girl striking a fawning pose, sticking out her tongue with a clumsy wink.

Turning his head unsteadily, he found a smiling silver-haired girl right beside him, an insolent-looking red-clad woman standing before him, and a little girl holding his hand—

"...Um, what's wrong? Ignoring me? Are you ignoring me?! S-stop it, please, that's so mean of you. Ah, ah, stop, stop... d-don't sigh after listening to my song... don't make that disappointed face, forgive me...!"

At the sight of the silent Subaru, the girl before him—Liliana—began trembling, her face that of someone having an unwanted memory dredged back up.
Watching this unfold, Subaru opened his mouth.

"...Gross."

"Wha—?! Oh my, how could you! Looking at a girl's face, gazing at her from this close, and the words that come out are 'gross'! I, Liliana, am mortified on behalf of Natsuki-sama's own mother! Mortifiiied!"

Putting on a tearful act, Liliana turned her back on him right there. She kept sneaking glances back at Subaru all the while, but he had no room to spare for her irritating theatrics.
Subaru swayed unsteadily where he stood, and before he knew it, he'd sunk down into a crouch.

"Subaru? What's wrong?"

"Wh— what's the matter with you? Subaru, Subaru?"

Beatrice, holding his hand, and Emilia beside her, anxiously peered into the face of the crouching Subaru. And, his face turning pale enough to make the two of them gasp, Subaru said—

"—Gross."

More than the loop of 'death' after a year's absence, it was the events right before that 'death' that he could not accept, and his knees kept trembling with a surging wave of nausea.


And so, once again, the spiral of 'death' begins.
The loop to overcome the worst day, set upon the stage of the city of Priestella.

—Once again, it begins.
