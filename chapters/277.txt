Arc 4 Chapter 108: "A Man Who's Just Good with Timing"

Mana gathered at the tip of the wand, and by the chant being woven, the world was rewritten—.

The sweeping wind became blades, and the blades converged upon the being standing at the center of the whirlwind, exploding to scatter destruction in every direction.

"——!"

At the center of the vortex—in the path the swirling blades sought—stood Garfiel, roaring up at the sky.
The crashing blades struck Garfiel's torso, their sharp edge shaving diagonally across his rock-hard abdominal muscles. Blood spilled, but he wasn't cut in two, because he leapt backward in a single bound.

Between intercepting and evading, the decision to withdraw was made instantly. Though a faint hesitation showed at the corners of his eyes, Garfiel covered his head with his arms to avoid a fatal blow and slid along the ground, escaping the wind blades.

But these were blades unleashed in a surprise attack meant to decide the battle.
The wind did not easily allow Garfiel's escape; the cutting force pursued him as he retreated. Kicking off the ground, Garfiel clicked his tongue at the invisible slashes bearing down on him.

"Shara, you're pissin' me off——!"

The instant he'd fallen back a great distance, Garfiel shouted and met the wind blades head-on.
The moment his planted heel sank into the earth, the ground itself—as if cut into a square starting from the sole of Garfiel's foot—tilted diagonally and sprang upward. The wind blade was struck by the ground surging up from directly below, shaving away the surface of the soil before vanishing.

The lifted earth fell back under gravity, and a tremor and billowing cloud of dust rose up.
Before such a cataclysmic, overwhelming sight, an ordinary person would surely lose their voice and freeze in place.
But the one who had launched the surprise attack was no ordinary person, nor was this the first time she had witnessed this feat of Garfiel's.

"Hah!"

"Tch!"

Bursting through the cloud of dust leapt a girl, her short skirt flaring.
Beneath her neatly trimmed pink hair, her pale pink eyes shone with strong will. From the tip of the wand in her hand, mana was being released with enough force to warp the very air, and from it an invisible blade of wind extended once more.

It was a precise application, textbook-perfect for a wind user, extending its reach far beyond what appeared possible at a glance.
For all its seeming flimsiness, the wind blade displayed a cutting edge surpassing that of any half-baked sword. Garfiel had readied his claws at his hip to intercept, but even he was at a disadvantage against a blade with claws alone.

Still poised, Garfiel dropped his stance as if to leap backward and escape. But that was blocked by Ram's left hand—the one not holding the wand.

"Fura!"

"Guoh!?"

A short incantation. The lowest tier of wind attack. Struck in the back by the surprise strike from behind, Garfiel had his retreating step preemptively sealed off. No real damage. But he'd been stopped in his tracks.
When he raised his head, Ram had already drawn back her blade for another strike, and—

"——"

Before he could think about what would be best to do, Garfiel's body was already moving.
Claws and fangs—the weapons he himself possessed—could not intercept a wind blade. And evasion, once he'd been caught flat-footed, was equally difficult. That left only one path.

"Ruuuruuraaaaahhh!!"

Roaring, Garfiel sprang up and struck at the invisible tip of the swinging blade from both sides with his hands—a motion resembling catching a blade bare-handed, but against an intangible, formless blade, such an action could not truly serve as a defense.
Still, wind blade or not, the fact remained that it "existed" there. The blade's edge, struck by Garfiel's palm strikes, had its focus disrupted, and instead of cutting clean through, it only managed to slice diagonally across the skin of the body it should have severed.

By the time Ram judged that his attack had been neutralized, it was already too late.

"Don't screw with me!"

A launched front kick aimed at Ram's torso the instant her feet touched the ground.
The sole of his boot, capable of piercing the air and seemingly able to shatter even rock, failed to reach Ram, who spun her body into an even lower stance. She contorted her posture until it seemed as though she were lying flat on the ground, and with a newly generated wind blade, went to sweep Garfiel's feet out from under him.

"——Tch!"

On one leg, in danger of having his ankle taken clean off, Garfiel leapt. He succeeded in evading Ram's blade, but—

"Your feet left the ground."

The whisper brushed his eardrum an instant before Garfiel's body was struck down by a heel descending from directly above.
This outlandish blow came from Ram, who had thrown her body into a spinning vertical kick immediately after unleashing her wind blade.
Garfiel, suspended in midair, had no way to kill the impact, and was sent flying, the bones of the arm he'd hastily used to block creaking, before he bounced off the ground and slammed to a stop against the trunk of a tree behind him.

Breath escaping him, Garfiel glared at Ram with golden eyes burning with fury.
But—

"Wulfura."

There, a violent gale that seemed as though it would crush the entire forest converged—and, engulfing Garfiel at its center, the invisible blow trampled the woodland.

"Guh—h!"

The raging storm battered Garfiel's body, slashing him, hurling him about, mixing him together with the churning trees in one great tumult.
Tossed about until he could no longer tell up from down or left from right, and moreover having been "separated from the ground," Garfiel had no means of defense and could do nothing but be beaten down before Ram's magic.

When the wind ceased and the violent force had passed, there remained the barely-standing figure of Garfiel.
Drenched in blood, gazing up at the sky, Garfiel sank to his knees where he stood, his consciousness half-blown away.

He had leapt into a trap laid with flawless preparation and taken two blasts of super-tier magic directly.
That he still had life left in him at all was astonishing, a testament to his sheer vitality—though the mercilessness shown was, in a sense, precisely because that vitality had been anticipated.

Confirming the damage done to Garfiel, Ram let out a small breath. Then she turned her gaze behind her, toward Otto, who had merely watched over the ambush.

"I knew it would be, but that's an unsightly, pathetic figure not fit to look at."

"For someone who fought with everything he had, body and soul, isn't that an awfully harsh evaluation...?"

"Everything in this world is judged by results alone. Whether it worked or didn't. The process leading up to it is secondary to the result... which is why I'll say it again. An unsightly, pathetic figure not fit to look at."

"Wow, truly merciless. ...This really is the person Natsuki-san described."

Without so much as a word of thanks for Otto's efforts, Ram gave a small snort at his wry smile.
As a result of his hard-fought struggle, the damage Otto had managed to deal to Garfiel was considerable. Setting aside the cumulative provocation effect of the various small traps along the way, that final blow had carried enough force that it wouldn't have been strange for it to have defeated Garfiel outright.
However, if there was one miscalculation on Otto's part—

"It seems it couldn't get past Garf's 'Divine Protection of the Earth Spirits.'"

"His what protection of the what now?"

To Otto's puzzled question, Ram let out a small sigh. Shaking her head as if exasperated, she looked down at the bewildered Otto with eyes full of thoroughgoing contempt, and sighed once more.

"Just how much disappointment are you going to show me? It actually hurts my feelings, you know!?"

"Garf's 'Divine Protection of the Earth Spirits' is, literally, a blessing that grants him the earth's bounty. As long as his feet are on the ground, his body carries the protection of formidable earthen defenses. —Even without that, earth-attribute magic would already be the worst possible match for him. Out of all of Al's trump cards, of all things, it had to be Dona..."

Pressing a hand to her forehead, Ram closed her eyes and lowered her head.

"You're so unlucky it doesn't even inspire pity."

"Ugh, so my rotten luck finally exploded even in a moment like this—how terrifying! Anyway, if you knew about that, Ram-san, I really think you could've told me and I could've contributed in all sorts of other, more useful ways, you know!?"

"That's Ram-sama, isn't it?"

"Why does everyone try to kick me down to the very bottom rung!?"

Ignoring the ranting Otto, Ram swayed the tip of her wand and stepped forward to deal with Garfiel.
He should be unconscious by now, but Garfiel's bottomless stamina was something to marvel at. In the liberation of the "Sanctuary," he was without question the greatest obstacle. He needed to be restrained immediately and kept under strict watch until the matter was settled—.

"..........."

Ram's approaching foot stopped, and a faint furrow crossed her brow.
Sensing the air growing dry against her pressed lips, she let the red tip of her tongue peek out briefly. And then—

"Garf."

"...Damn, you really are one merciless woman, through and through."

Responding to Ram's call, Garfiel—who had let his head hang limply—lifted his face.
In his sharply opened eyes, fury and hostility blazed brilliantly, and his bared canines clicked together with a small sound, declaring that his will to fight had not diminished in the least.

The ambush should have gone perfectly. It had felt, more than anything, like a job well done.
And yet, the monster known as Garfiel Tinzel could not be brought down.

Garfiel, drenched in blood, showed no notable exhaustion or damage in the ease with which he rose. What the wind blades and blows had dealt him was, in the end, only superficial.
He had suffered thin cuts to his skin and countless blows that missed vital points, but not a single one had inflicted damage decisive enough to hinder his ability to fight.

"When you cast that spell on me pinned against the tree, I thought I was done for, for real. I racked my brain in that split second, tryin' to figure out what the hell to do. But nothin' came to me... so I quit thinkin'."

He'd given up trying to work it out with his head and entrusted himself entirely to his body's movements, letting it evade on its own.
As a result, his instincts had greedily chosen survival, and had masterfully steered him through the inescapable violence to minimize the damage he took.
It was the fruit of racial instinct, honed as a creature built for battle.

Even behind her expressionless face, it was a battle sense sharp enough to make even Ram catch her breath, faintly.
For Ram, who prided herself on having a talent for choosing the best course of action in any situation unmatched by anyone—even while falling short in raw stats—it was a rare, hard-won kind of experience to encounter someone who could stand shoulder to shoulder with her in that regard.
Even as she thought, why here, of all places, the fact that her opponent was Garfiel became one more reason for a feeling she couldn't quite put into words to take root within her.

"Hey, Ram. Why are you sidin' with them? How the hell'd it come to this?"

"——"

"You get it, don'tcha? Sidin' with them means agreein' to this scheme to liberate the 'Sanctuary.' Ain't that against what Roswaal wants? That bastard... at least right now, he sure as hell doesn't want the 'Sanctuary' liberated."

"You would speak as though you understand Roswaal-sama, in front of Ram, Garf? We've known each other long enough that you should understand—there is absolutely no way Ram would lend an ear to that kind of nonsense."

"I know damn well how stubborn you are. That's exactly what I'm sweet on, y'know. That's precisely why I don't get it. If you ain't givin' up your Roswaal-worship, why the hell are you on their side? I can't figure out what the hell talked you into it."

At Garfiel's words, Ram closed her eyes just once.
Her lips trembled, and her expression—as if holding back some emotion—was a rare sight. Garfiel's eyes widened at this, but in an instant, that emotion too vanished like dew.

"Ram... will do whatever action seems most meaningful toward Ram's own wish. That's all."

"Your wish... bein'?"

"The fulfillment of Roswaal-sama's cherished desire, of course. —Nothing else."

At Ram's flat declaration, Garfiel let out a deep breath.
Ram no longer intended to debate with Garfiel the contradiction between her actions and her convictions. No one could possibly understand what was in Ram's heart. No one—except the one man who had noticed the truth of Ram's heart and had, in this way, extended his invitation to her.

"Truly, you're an infuriating man, Barusu. ...I don't even know why, myself."

Ram harbored an inexpressible irritation toward Subaru.
It was, in no small part, made up of things like physiological revulsion, or the resentment cultivated over the time they'd spent together, but more than that, it felt like something rooted deeper still.
As if he were some hated enemy who had stolen something precious from her—that was the kind of inexplicable feeling Ram held toward Subaru.

And yet, the reason Ram had gone along with Subaru's invitation was that the proposal he'd brought to her had shaken something at the very core of her heart, that deeply.

"You should be recovered enough to stand by now, shouldn't you?"

"Y-you really work people hard, don't you... I keep thinking it wouldn't kill you to cast even one healing spell on me..."

"Rest assured. Ram can't use healing magic at all. There was never any need to learn it."

"This is the first maid I've ever met who has absolutely nothing to do with the concept of healing!"

Letting out a cry, Otto forced his trembling legs to work and hauled himself upright.
His body swayed unsteadily, his nosebleed only just having stopped. Even standing, he was, of course, in no state to be counted as a fighting force.
And yet, at his attitude—fighting spirit still not diminished even now—Garfiel clicked his tongue in irritation.

"You... I figured our little exchange just now taught you plenty well enough that there's nothin' more you can do. Usin' up your trump card and all, and you still can't see that I'm standin' here just fine? Bein' this bad at givin' up just makes a good man look worse, y'know."

"Unfortunately, I've not yet lost so much of my will to survive that I could gracefully accept giving up. Even if I end up penniless, as long as I've got a body left, I can still run. My friend, at the very least, seems the sort who'd say exactly that and take off running."

"...Him again, huh."

At the word "friend" that came out of Otto's mouth, Garfiel clicked his tongue.

"What the hell's so trustworthy about that mouthy bastard. No real strength. No talent. All he's got is that tongue of his spinnin' round and round, and that's it. Is a guy like you really worth lendin' a hand to, huh?"

"Whether I have worth—who's to say. To Natsuki-san as he is now, maybe I don't have that worth."

"...Huh?"

"But the future is a different story."

At the unexpected answer, Garfiel tilted his head, and Otto deepened his smile as he spoke.
Even now, with his stamina utterly drained and most of their strategies crushed by brute force, with hardly any hope left, Otto's face showed not the faintest trace of anxiety.

"I am a merchant, after all. Investing in someone who seems likely to bring me profit down the line isn't such a bad idea, I think. Natsuki-san... I have a feeling that, against all odds, he might just pull off something big someday."

"——"

"Though that's on the condition that things don't fall apart right here, of course. So, for now, Natsuki-san is nothing more than a sprout, and my role, I think, is to see what kind of flower he blooms into and how much value that turns out to have... to clear away the insects, to prune him into shape."

Scratching his head and muttering that Subaru was a high-maintenance man, Otto gave a wry laugh, and listening to this, Ram let out a bored sigh.

"Honestly, Ram has no idea what there is about Barusu that deserves such admiration. Ram agrees with Garf that Barusu is weak, useless, and can't even properly brew a cup of tea."

"That's an exaggeration... though maybe it isn't."

"Still, Barusu is, at the moments that matter most, a strangely well-timed man."

Ignoring Otto's timid attempt to add a follow-up, Ram declared this outright.
At her words, the two men both tilted their heads and asked, "Timing?" and Ram gave a small nod.

"Timing. A man who's just good with timing—that's Barusu."

Useless most of the time, and it was never clear what good he was for, and yet, the man named Natsuki Subaru was, strangely, a man who was where you wanted him to be, exactly when you wanted him there.

When Emilia had gotten separated from Ram in the royal capital, Subaru had protected Emilia in Ram's place.
He'd been carried to the mansion wounded, and during the several days that followed, there had been the uproar caused by the witchbeasts. In bringing that to a close as well, Subaru had saved the village children and played a part in the witchbeasts' extermination. He hadn't made the greatest contribution, but it was a fact that his presence had been a help.
And when the mansion was entrusted to Emilia upon her return from the royal capital, and an air of unease spread through the surrounding area including the village, Subaru had returned bringing reinforcements and had splendidly gotten them through the crisis.

Natsuki Subaru was, abnormally, a man with excellent timing.
There was not the faintest element about him that drew her in, nor did she feel anything resembling attraction to him as a member of the opposite sex. Ram didn't understand what was supposedly good about him, and there had been times it left her feeling frustrated—though she could never quite recall what exactly had been so frustrating, or what had bothered her so.
In any case, Natsuki Subaru was, simply, that kind of man.

Which was precisely why, regarding this matter too, Ram—

"Barusu's sense of timing, at least, is worth trusting. —If Barusu saw his moment and decided to act on it, then that's the only chance we have to snatch victory."

"Somehow or other, you do have at least some trust in Natsuki-san after all, don't you, Ram-san."

"That's Ram-sama."

"Is that really the part you want to press right now!?"

Displeased at the smirking man standing beside her, Ram silenced him with a sharp glare.
And so the two of them were in agreement in affirming Subaru's aim. Having become allies in this fight, and keeping quiet from Subaru about the fact that they were buying time like this—both were things the two of them had consented to, mutually.

She already knew full well that they'd bought more than enough time by now—.

"I honestly find myself wondering why the will to keep going hasn't faded yet."

"To have the trap and the ambush land this cleanly, and still not finish him off—that's humiliating. Awfully cocky for a Garf. —I'll make him regret it."

"Whoa, scary. This lady is scary. Now I'm starting to wonder if that sleeping girl is really as gentle as she seems, or if Natsuki-san's been lying to me."

Otto muttered this needless commentary under his breath.
Checking the grip of her wand, Ram once again began raising her concentration of mana.

Against the two of them, who showed no sign of dropping their battle stances, Garfiel maintained his silence for a while. He looked down, listening to their voices, and then at last slowly stepped forward.

"——"

Sensing that the opening of hostilities was imminent once more, Otto and Ram both stiffened with tension.
But the resolve of the man and woman was met with—

"—Enough of this."

A hoarse voice, muttered under his breath.
Both Otto and Ram furrowed their brows at the same moment.

"Thinkin's too much of a pain in the ass——"

Garfiel murmured this in a voice utterly worn out.
And then—

"———————!!"

A beast's roar rang out through the air, shaking the entire forest of the "Sanctuary" to its foundations.
Every living creature in the forest trembled, an oppressive pressure heavy enough to bow every head to the ground.

—The Super Beast had appeared.
