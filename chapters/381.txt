Arc 5 Chapter 64: "Liliana Masquerade's Melancholy"

"—————"

I slide my fingers along Lyulyre's strings, plucking the taut lines with movements long since second nature to me. Fingerwork I've done for years, tens of thousands of times over—started, in fact, before I can even remember starting.

For me, everything to do with singing is as natural as breathing—so natural that trying to recall its beginning is difficult enough to make me laugh through my nose.

I open my throat, tighten my stomach, and lay my voice atop the music I've begun to play.

What I sing are the words, the emotions, that rise up in my mind in this very instant.

And all of it rides, just the same, atop music pulled out of the very same instant.

"—————"

When a new song comes together, I like to say it "struck" me—but truthfully, calling it a "strike of inspiration" is a bit presumptuous of me.

Put more plainly, it's less that I was struck by it and more that I found it. The melody, the lyrics, everything that rises up in my head in that instant—all of it was already buried somewhere in this world to begin with.

Something triggers it, and the music buried in the world gets dug up.

There's a trigger for the finding, and it just so happens that I'm the one who picks it up—a gift that seems to fall from nowhere. That's more or less how I think of the music of inspiration.

So that's why I told Priscilla-sama that you don't need learning to make music. Toss aside all the wisdom and knowledge and complicated business, and there's still something you can do.

Because songs get sung by things that aren't even human.

Haven't you heard a little bird sing? Have you never lent an ear to the chorus of insects? Have you never felt peace played for you by a gust of wind, by the babble of a stream?

Do they have what humans call learning? Maybe they do, but I think we ought to assume they don't. Or rather, they just don't—at least not as far as I'm concerned! And they don't need to!

Have you never felt music in the sunlight, in the waxing and waning of the moon, in the smell of earth, in the crackle of firewood popping in a bonfire? I have! That's proof that music fills this world.

Proof that this world is made of music, that this world is filled with music, that this world is connected by music!

"—————"

We troubadours are just borrowing sound from a world already filled with music. Taking what was always there anyway and making it a little easier to notice, then going around telling everyone about it—nothing but self-important meddling.

Shameless, sparing nothing, going around declaring good things good—all for our own self-satisfaction.

It's fine if people think of us that way. It's fine no matter how people think of us.

But this music—it's good, isn't it?

There's joy in sharing what's interesting as interesting, what's fun as fun, with total strangers.

There's joy in raising your voice—saying "this is interesting" when it's interesting, "this is fun" when it's fun.

Music has that. With music, it's allowed.

Because the whole world is singing, so who could ever blame you for singing along?

Now, lose yourself in it, lose yourself, lose yourself.

Now, go mad for it, go mad, go mad.

Soak yourself in pleasure to the last drop, let your heart be filled with joy, become a captive of delight!

Not just with your ears—use your eyes, your nose, your skin, your heart, your soul, all of it, to take pleasure in "sound," to make it "music"!

Fervor swallows the crowd whole, sweeping away the swell of "Wrath" all at once.

They use their bodies to stoke the fire, raise their voices, become part of the performance. When their eyes meet a stranger's beside them, they understand—their hearts are feeling the very same thing now.

Of course they do. Music is always right there beside you—a friend that never leaves your side, from your birth to your death.

You can see it, can't you. You can hear it, can't you. You can feel it, can't you.

The presence of music, calling out that we are always right here—!

"Argh, dammit! It wasn't quiet at all, but thank you all so much for lending me your ears—aaah!!"

I sang the whole thing through, oh yeah!!

Er, well, how should I put this—sorry, I got a little carried away there.

Now that the big dramatic performance is over and done with, remembering just how worked up I'd gotten a moment ago makes my face go hot-hot-hot, if you know what I mean.

"Um, big sister, that was amazing, sir! I was very moved, sir!"

Guh! Schult-kun's utterly guileless, pure gaze hurts! It's a bittersweet ache! Those clear red eyes, innocent of any grime, stab straight into my heart!

No, no, I know full well there's no malice behind it, and it's not sarcasm or anything—I know it's a thoroughly childlike, sincere admiration, honestly given. I know that, but the fact that I can't take it graciously is entirely my own selfishness!

Sorry, Schult-kun... growing up means getting a little dirty, you know.

"—? I don't really understand, sir. Sorry for my lack of learning, sir."

Uhyaa! That downcast, dejected little face is unfairly adorable!

This kid gives off this tremendous aura that makes you want to tease him something fierce. J-just a little, just the teensiest bit, if I just poked him a little... heh heh...

"Liliana. That performance just now wasn't bad. I shall grant you my praise."

"Gyobii!"

"What was that unsightly noise. That is not a sound fit to come out of a girl's throat—much less yours."

Just as the fangs of a wicked adult were about to sink into Schult-kun, Priscilla-sama appeared, timing her interruption as if she'd been watching for the perfect moment to play spoilsport. Not that I was plotting anything wicked at all, mind you. No lapse in judgment whatsoever, truly, none.

For the record, the instant Priscilla-sama showed her face, Schult-kun's expression lit right up and he stuck close to her side. Though "stuck close" doesn't mean hugging her—more like just barely pinching a bit of the hem of her red dress. ...That modesty makes him all the more angelic.

Now then, having come to fetch Schult-kun like this, I'd gone and turned what had been the scene of an absolute catastrophe—the assembly hall—into a banquet hall instead...

"To think it would be this effective... Without even realizing it, I may have reached the very summit of music. Yes, the peak of the goddess of song herself!"

"Fool. Understanding the gap between the common lot and your own vessel is essential, but overestimating yourself too far is graceless. Your song has promise, but it is far too soon for you to call yourself the supreme. In this case, it was merely fortunate that those wretches happened to be in a state easily carried along."

"Easily carried along?"

What in the world does that mean, exactly?

Looking bored, Priscilla-sama fanned herself and swept her gaze across the assembly hall. When I timidly followed her line of sight, what came into view was a great many people.

Yes, freed from that tense, hostile air of shouting insults, throwing punches, grabbing at each other, and eyeing each other's belongings, the people all looked at least a little settled for the time being.

They were exchanging few words with each other now, but some were tending to those hurt in the scuffle, others apologizing back and forth—yes, yes, that's the important stuff.

Wow, but really, my singing is something else! People who'd been at each other's throats like that, and now look how peaceable they've become. Truly the hand of a master craftswoman!

"Do not get carried away. The influence on their stability-blind, puppet-like hearts still lingers. All your song did was draw the poison from what had, until now, been bound by suspicion and fear. Either way, unless the root cause is severed, sooner or later they will revert."

"Bweh!? Ah, no, but, look, in that case, every time hearts start turning stormy, couldn't I just sweep it all away with my fierce oom-pah-pah again...?"

"In theory, that would work well enough. But as a stopgap treatment, it ranks lowest of the low. And besides, this frenzied uproar is not confined to this assembly hall alone."

"Wh-wh-what did you say?"

Ugh, that's the first I'm hearing of it. Though, come to think of it, on the way here Priscilla-sama did mention dodging what seemed like similar sorts of fighting, and there was that explanation about it covering the whole city too—so isn't this a genuinely, seriously bad situation?

"S-someone's gotta do somethin' about Priestella...?"

"Well, that's about the size of it. Honestly speaking, I have no particular obligation to go about saving this city..."

"Priscilla-sama..."

Once again Priscilla-sama was on the verge of some heartless, cold-blooded, iron-masked remark, and Schult-kun looked up at her with eyes trembling something fierce. You know, watching this, I can tell—this Schult-kun, despite being right beside Priscilla-sama, is remarkably normal. A perfectly, perfectly wholesome disposition.

Ahh, geez, he completely fulfills every condition needed to make you go "oh, honestly, this kid."

To Schult-kun's silent plea—the kind that would've had me instantly weak-kneed and melting into a puddle of doting agreement—I can't say for certain whether Priscilla-sama caved for the same reason I would have, but she gave a resigned little shrug all the same. My heart leaps. I stare hard at my hands.

"Priscilla-sama, how old are you, if I may ask?"

"Nineteen."

"Oh-ho, I see. For the record, I'm twenty-two, you know."

"I didn't ask."

I just felt like saying it. What is it, I wonder—difference in diet? Is this the professional downside of being a wandering troubadour showing itself here? Dang it.

"I won't go so far as to say Schult moved me, but I possess no cowardice masquerading as generosity, of the sort that would let me overlook the insolence of those who commit rudeness during my stay. Every last member of the Witch Cult involved will need to have their heads struck off and lined up in a row."

While I sat there clenching my fists and trembling, Priscilla-sama's course of action seemed to have already firmed up.

And even though she keeps making all sorts of excuses with her mouth, I can see straight through to the fact that deep down, she's thinking she really ought to grant Schult-kun's request.

"Honestly, Priscilla-sama, you're surprisingly a big pro-tec-tor♪"

"—————"

"Gyaaa!? It's burning, burning, scorched, scorched!?"

It burned!? It actually burned!?

The instant I nudged Priscilla-sama's side with my elbow, flame erupted right at the top of my head! The tied-up ends of my hair got scorched and curled up!? This time there wasn't even a "hoh" first!

A sudden act of violence, out of nowhere! This is true panic! This dreadful news I shall never forget!

"B-big sister, are you all right, sir!?"

As I rolled around with my head smoking, Schult-kun came dashing over, his face drained of color. Perhaps thinking he had to put the fire out right away, he pulled a bottle out of a bundle he was carrying and struggled desperately to douse me with its contents. And all the while, my head remained wreathed in hellfire—

"Stop, Schult."

"B-but, Priscilla-sama...!"

"That's my liquor for my evening drink, is it not. It belongs to me besides, and if you douse her with it, all that will happen is our singer turning into a fireball. Amusing, but that's all it would be."

"Oyoyoyoyoyoyo—"

Before I could be turned into a fireball, I rolled and rolled and rolled across the cold floor. Schult-kun tilted his little head and let slip, without thinking, "Does liquor burn, sir?"

You wretches, master and servant alike conspiring to burn me to death by flame... But even so! Even if I were to die here and now, a troubadour's soul does not perish—every night my song shall echo at your bedside... because song exists everywhere in this world, after all!

"If that's fine with you, then I suppose it's fine. In any case, you're making an awful fuss over having merely a few hair tips singed. Get up already."

"Eh, wait? What about my vengeful hellfire ghost? I was supposed to be wrapped in scorching flame and reduced to ashes!?"

Ah, it's true, I'm not burnt at all. Huh, all that shock for nothing.

Laughing sheepishly, I brushed off my clothes, feeling somehow like everyone around was staring at me, and made my way back to Priscilla-sama's side. And then, straight to the negotiating table.

"Well then, Priscilla-sama! Here's the thing—to save Priestella, why not go and land one big, flashy blow on the Archbishop of Sin! I'll be cheering you on, for all the little good it'll do!"

"Don't speak as though this were someone else's affair. Obviously I'm taking you along as well."

"Wheeeeeeh!?"

Heaven and earth astonished! The world turned upside down! Beauty's fate is short-lived! Why on earth am I the one being singled out!?

"Someone like me, I'm just a lowly troubadour whose only merits are being cute, being able to sing, and also being cute... Bringing me along, I'd only be good for pleasing your eyes and ears, you know?"

"I don't dislike that frankness. And I believe I already told you—I have taken a liking to you. It would be a waste to lose that voice of yours. All the more so if you were left behind here lumped in with the foolish masses, and something were to happen to you. Stay within reach of my 'Sunlight.'"

"So, in other words... because I'm cute enough that you want to protect me?"

"—Hoh."

"Uhyaa! Priscilla-sama, giving me a beat's pause before getting angry—how kind of you!"

Given the shock of having my hair set alight without warning earlier, just being given a warning now makes Priscilla-sama seem somehow kind. Huh, my heart's pounding for some reason. What is this feeling... my pulse quickening, my palms sweating without end, my breath growing short, the color draining from my face...

"There is yet another reason I am bringing you along. —That tiresome declaration from those fools earlier—the Magic Vessel used for it is housed in the city hall, is it not?"

"Eh? Ah, yes, it's in the city hall. Every morning I get up early and, rubbing my sleepy eyes, fulfill my duty... Ah! Not that I ever sing halfheartedly because of it, you know! It's true that right up until the moment I sing I'm sleepy, or rather half-asleep, or rather more than half-asleep, but the instant it's time to sing, my eyes snap wide open! Wide open, I tell you!"

"Knowing the location is enough. What's needed is that Magic Vessel, and you."

"You want me..."

"Your throat."

I stand corrected. Teheriko.

But, but, thanks to that, I finally started to understand what Priscilla-sama was trying to say. In other words, this is what she wanted to say:

"You want the same thing that happened in this assembly hall to happen across all of Priestella, through the Magic Vessel...!"

"—————"

"Eh, wait, Priscilla-sama? What's wrong?"

"Committing outrages right before my eyes, are we. You there—what have you done with the real Liliana. There's no way the genuine article would be this quick on the uptake."

"So the clever, cute me is being treated as a mere illusion!!"

Honestly, I'm crestfallen wondering just what kind of image of me has taken root.

Still, I understood Priscilla-sama's thinking. If riots like the one in this assembly hall really are breaking out all across the city, then that's exactly when my singing has its role to play.

Touring around and holding concerts in every district has its charms, but going on tour like that all over the place wouldn't make it in time this go-around! So the quickest way is to bank it all on one shot!

"Y-yes, understood! I see, that certainly makes sense. In that case, of course it's only natural that Priscilla-sama would want to bring me along! And what's more, the city hall is where the city's finest minds gather! I'm sure Kiritaka-san will be there too—well, he's the sort who's quite dependable in an emergency like this, I'd think!"

"Mind you, the city hall is certainly where the Archbishop of Sin should be. There's no avoiding a bit of pest extermination. Just be sure to avoid getting caught up in it yourself, at the very least."

"I completely forgot!!"

Right, that's true. Right now, "Lust" should be there in the city hall. Or rather—if the hall's still being used as their base outright, this whole plan is stumbling right out of the gate.

"N-n-n-no, wait, but, but, but, but! By now that Archbishop of Sin might've just tossed the hall aside and wandered off somewhere else! That place has all these rooms you're not allowed into because of some confidentiality thing or other, so it's surprisingly useless for killing time. I bet 'Lust' got bored of waiting around and cleared out too, don't you think?"

Fufun, a burst of brilliant deduction. An idea only I could've come up with, given how I visit that place every morning for my duties.

Really, everyone there is scurrying about working and won't so much as give you the time of day, and a girl who can't do her numbers gets shooed right out with a "sorry, not you"—it's that kind of place, you know!

So surely, by now the city hall must be all empty—or so I thought—

"Yoohoo. Yoohoo-oo. Yoohoo-hoo-oo."

The second broadcast came, of all times, right at that very moment.


※※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※　※


After hearing the second broadcast, we left the assembly hall in low spirits (though Priscilla-sama and Schult-kun looked no different on the outside).

It took a little while to leave the hall because, upon hearing the second broadcast, the people inside grew unstable all over again, and I had to counteract it with my singing.

Honestly, I can only call it a performance given against my will.

Now, now, no matter what the song, I would never be so disloyal as to hold back or go easy on my voice—but by rights, a song shouldn't have any excess impurities mixed into it.

Singing to captivate, singing to draw people in—that's the spirit I want to sing in, at least as I see it. I don't want to think of a song as a means of countering some mental attack that stirs up unease. Even if that's what it ends up doing as a result, if I—the one singing—start thinking of a song that way, if I stop being sincere toward the song, then I can't believe such a song would be able to reach anyone's heart at all.

"Seizing the control towers, the great floodgates. And then their demands in the next broadcast..."

Having left the assembly hall, I could only trail unsteadily after Priscilla-sama, who strode on ahead as ever without a shred of hesitation. Somehow I felt a loss of confidence—or rather, more a loss of purpose?

I myself have no doubt that I exist to sing songs, but is it truly me that this situation calls for—or the song, or the result?

All three surely originate from me, and yet the three don't line up.

I can't help but feel that way.

"Then reaching the Magic Vessel at the city hall will have to wait until after the third demand."

"Why is that, sir?"

"The Witch Cult needs the Magic Vessel only once more, to announce their demand. After that, all they need do is hold the four control towers they've seized to the last man. There'd be no more point in stationing forces at the hall as a key position, so they'll withdraw from it. Though, one could imagine the possibility they keep the Magic Vessel secured for some tasteless amusement..."

"Won't they do that, sir?"

"If it's 'Lust'—the one operating the Magic Vessel—running the show, then no, I doubt it. That one wears the skin of malice, but beneath it lies a shrewdness and cunning to match. A prime example of what happens when a clever fool is handed an opportunity."

Schult-kun kept asking Priscilla-sama the very questions I had in mind, beating me to it every time. He seems to understand the exquisite timing needed to ask without provoking Priscilla-sama's anger (probably unconsciously, on his part!), and since Priscilla-sama is soft on Schult-kun, she answers whatever he asks quite courteously.

Since it's explained in terms even a child can follow, it was easy for me to understand too.

In other words, does this mean the situation is proceeding remarkably exactly as the Witch Cult planned?

"Hence, after the third broadcast, once the demand has been made. Once the Magic Vessel is freed, your song will finally be of use. While unrest fills the city, I cannot leisurely gather my forces—there's no telling where a worm within the lion's body might sprout up."

"Before things get all complicated like that, couldn't Priscilla-sama just use that brilliant sword of yours to go slice-and-dice the Witch Cult and take back the control towers?"

"If even one of the four control towers has its great floodgate opened, the city drowns. Even I have but a single body, however capable. There aren't enough hands for a counterattack. I'm aware there are a few in this city who could serve as hands and feet... but even gathering them requires the Magic Vessel."

The "hands and feet" Priscilla-sama spoke of meant forces capable of standing against the Witch Cult.

And as far as I know, there are people currently in the city who've achieved no small feat against the Witch Cult. Yes—the "Little Girl Tamer" Natsuki Subaru-sama, and the "Silver-Haired Witch" Emilia-sama, who has that very Subaru-sama at her command!

"I-I understand! So we go to the city hall after the third broadcast, right!"

Clenching my fists, my breath comes out ragged before I know it.

If I'm honest, I can't deny there's a part of me that's a little hesitant. But, but, I won't know whether Kiritaka-san is safe until I actually go to the hall—though, well, even knowing he's safe wouldn't make him useful in any combat sense.

Still, somehow I feel like I could sing without anything catching in my chest, so long as even that man is there.

"Then, until that time...!"

"We shall take Liliana-sama around to the shelters, sir!"

"Y-yes, take me around the shelters— waaait, huh!?"

Schult-kun answered triumphantly, but what does that even mean!?

Priscilla-sama nodded, looking satisfied, and seeing that, Schult-kun blushed happily—and even though this concerns me, I'm the only one left in the dark.

Even standing this close, I feel completely alone, you know.

"Now that we know your song is effective against this unpleasant wave, I need you to sing whether your heart is in it or not, for the time being. You've seemed troubled about something since a while ago, but you have no time to fret over such things right now."

"W-what does that have to do with going around the shelters!?"

"Sweeping away the curse all at once via the Magic Vessel is indeed your role as well, but there will be time before that can be carried out. Which means, until then, the unsettled hearts of the common lot are left unattended."

"Ah..."

"There's no guarantee your audience won't all be finished off before you ever stand before the Magic Vessel. To prevent that from happening, going around as a preventive measure suits your own wishes as well, does it not."

At last I understood, smoothly and simply, what Priscilla-sama's proposal meant.

Before my song can reach them, people hurting each other like in that assembly hall's brawl will appear. When that happens, delivering the crucial song of salvation might already be too late.

Surely I can't save everyone. But reaching out toward the places I can save isn't wasted effort.

"From what I've seen, your stage presence is rather considerable. But the way you're wavering now is a touch dangerous. You might well fumble at the crucial moment. So, get some more experience under your belt."

"Experience... you mean?"

If it's experience singing, I've done that more times than I can count. I don't recall ever using a phrase like "stage presence," but I've never once been ashamed to stand on a stage.

Just what could Priscilla-sama possibly mean by—

"I know nothing of your troubles. But what's needed is not a song sung for your own sake. It's a song sung for the sake of others. Come to terms with the self that sings a song for others. Get that experience under your belt."

"—————"

"Consider the presumption of making me walk about like this atoned for by the result you produce."

So saying, Priscilla-sama crossed her arms again, as was her habit. My heart leaps. I press a hand flat to my chest. I stare hard at my hand, then clench it tight.

Putting something other than myself into my song. That way of singing—

"Priscilla-sama really is kind, after all, sir. I understand that properly, sir!"

"Quiet, Schult."

Somehow it feels like a heartwarming little exchange is happening between the two of them, but for my own part, let me summon a fresh resolve and take on this round of the shelters.

Holding onto the meaning of Priscilla-sama's words—both the part that rings true and the part that makes me want to tilt my head in confusion, all bundled together—for the future of Liliana-chan, the troubadour who sings, plays, and dances!


—.
——.
———.


Well, and so, having fired myself up like that, I went around the shelters comforting people who were either rampaging or sunk in despair, and while I was at it, that third broadcast came—

"—Hmph. Beaten to it. How infuriating."

Priscilla-sama looked up at the darkened sky and murmured something like that.

I, too, having listened to the very same thing, understood what that sentiment was directed at.

There had been a broadcast from Natsuki Subaru-sama, using the Magic Vessel.

His words were clumsy, and by no means could be called powerful, but I think they probably managed to carve something other than unease and fear into the hearts of the people throughout the city.

The very same thing we had been aiming to do through song.

And if so, then that's surely something anyone ought to be allowed to do.

"We may have been beaten to it, but just as predicted, the city hall seems to have emptied out. For now, let's join up with everyone and get started on the fight to reclaim the city! I'll be sure to sing properly, I will!"

"Don't go making such a visibly relieved face."

"Oh, no, no, not relief, nothing of the sort..."

It's a little bit of relief and mostly a feeling of disappointment, if I'm honest.

It's a bit like having my thunder stolen—like missing my chance to become "the songstress."

Though there's also a certain satisfaction in having witnessed the birth of a "hero" instead.


—Though that satisfaction, too, stopped mattering the instant the hero's reckless demand got me signed up for a suicide charge alongside Priscilla-sama!

Yay! Turns out there's still a role for singing after all! Dammit!
