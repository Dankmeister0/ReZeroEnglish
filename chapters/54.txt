Arc 2 Chapter 30: "The Clownish Ways of Natsuki Subaru"

Broadly speaking, there are two gates Subaru must clear if he's to survive a full week at Roswaal Manor.

The first is winning the trust of the manor's residents. This isn't limited to Ram and Rem alone — it includes Roswaal, their master, as well.
If Subaru fails to meet their standards, there's a very high chance his life will be snuffed out under the pretext of silencing him.
This was the conclusion he'd reached after mulling it over, again and again, with time to spare during the previous loop.
Likewise, going outside without their permission would fall under the same category. Suspicious behavior leads straight to DEAD END. It's one of the points demanding the utmost caution.

And the second gate — for this one, he hadn't even found a thread to pull yet.
Put simply: the identity of the Curse User attacking Roswaal Manor.
The wielder of Curse Arts who took Subaru's life the first time, robbed him of body and mind the second time, and let Rem die in the fourth world.
Now, in this fifth world, searching out and defeating an opponent whose identity he hasn't even glimpsed a fragment of — that's this run's greatest objective.

To sum up: if he can somehow win a measure of trust from Ram and Rem, and on top of that defeat the wicked mage, he might just be able to break the loop.

That he'd only arrived at a visible victory condition after dying four times was a sorry state of affairs.
What's more, when it came to the crucial part — actually achieving that condition — there was still far too much unknown, and he couldn't deny the feeling that he'd come back with no plan at all.

It was enough to make him sigh anew at the hardship laid out before him, but Subaru shook off the urge to clutch his head and looked forward instead.
No matter how high the wall standing in his way, he had no choice but to challenge it.
He'd come back here even by taking his own life as a means to that end. He'd staked everything on a condition he wasn't even sure would let him return, and still Subaru had come back.

He'd died by his own hand once already.
So then, he ought to face this challenge as if he were already dead.
— That was what Subaru had decided.

"So-o-o then, Ram? From what you've seen, how would you rate him?"

At the hour when the moon hung in the night sky, that secret conference was taking place in the study on the top floor of Roswaal Manor.
There were two participants: Roswaal, master of the manor and of the room, and across from him, the pink-haired maid — Ram.

Roswaal laced his fingers together on the desk, lips curling, as he posed the question. Ram tilted her head slightly, mulling it over.
Roswaal raised an eyebrow at her hesitant manner, as though he'd seen something rare.

"Hmm, for Ram — who tends to make snap judgments about almost everything — to be troubled like this is rather unusual, isn't it? Couldn't take his measure in a single day, hm-m-m?"

"That's not quite it, no."

The denial came quickly enough, but its content still lacked clarity somehow. She touched a finger to her lips, a trace of confusion lingering.

"He — Barusu — is, well, hopeless in terms of ability. His work as a servant is barely a notch above an amateur's. Before the question of whether he's suited to it or not even comes up, he's not even worth evaluating."

"We-e-ell, that he asked to be put to work himself, and yet turns out like this, is also rather strange, isn't it?"

Recalling the breakfast table, Roswaal gave a low chuckle.
He remembered inviting the awakened guest to the table, discussing his accomplishments and what reward they merited.

Roswaal's impression of him had settled into something like: *a boy who's received a decent education, whose head turns well enough, and who's reasonably good at looking out for himself.* Not a bad assessment — though in another sense, that was precisely what made him worth being wary of.

Which was why he'd assigned Ram to him as tutor with surveillance as her covert task, and why he'd arranged this reporting session in the first place.
He hadn't expected results on the very first day — but conversely, for the man to be the sort who could produce this kind of unease in Ram's demeanor, that made him a matter of grave concern.

"It's strange, though..."

"Mm, go on, let's hear it. Say whatever comes, don't hold back."

"In terms of ability, he's utterly lacking, and yet, somehow — Barusu is... how should I put it, disturbingly upbeat?"

Roswaal's brow furrowed at Ram's manner of speech, which seemed less like choosing her words than searching for them.
Even Ram herself likely realized she wasn't getting to the point. Her expression carried the frustration of someone unable to land on the right words, and she went on,

"He just carries on chattering away like that, and even when he fails, his smile never falters — if anything, he ends up behaving with an almost sickening devotion toward us..."

"...And what do yo-o-ou make of it?"

Ram answered the low-voiced question with just that much.
Roswaal, whose contact with the man had been brief, couldn't quite grasp the doubt she held. But this was the opinion of a sister who'd accumulated no small experience in this household. Given his trust in them, her counsel was something well worth heeding.

"Still, it's only the first day — it's no wonder his character isn't yet clear. He has the debt of gratitude owed for saving Lady Emilia. — Let's take a bi-i-it more time and watch how things develop, shall we?"

"...Should the worst happen—"

It came through unmistakably that she didn't want to hear what came next.
Her expression hadn't changed, and yet he could sense it all the same — perhaps a mark of how long they'd known each other. Roswaal regarded that weakness of Ram's with his yellow eye, gave a small shake of his head, and said,

"Then that's a matter for when it happens. I'd like to see it settled as peacefully as possible. — Do be sure Rem doesn't get ahead of herself, whatever you do."

That blue-haired girl had a tendency, at times, to grasp his intentions and then act on her own initiative. For the most part her judgment didn't diverge from Roswaal's own policies, but there were occasions where her snap decisions, nipping possibilities in the bud, proved troublesome to manage.
This time in particular, there was a chance that snap judgment could push things in an unfortunate direction.

*We eliminated the danger preemptively, but our relations with Emilia have soured* — that was no laughing matter. Deeply inconvenient, in fact.
To Roswaal, all three of them were pieces he absolutely needed.

"By the way, Ram. Is that the whole of your report?"

"...Yes. I'm sorry I couldn't bring you anything more substantial."

"I'd never blame you for so-o-omething like that. Now then, shall we see to tonight's duties? — Two nights left unattended. You must be aching rather badly by now, hm?"

At the beckoning motion of his fingers, Ram followed with an expression somewhat entranced. Rising from where she'd stood before the desk, she approached him with an unsteady gait, and settled herself hesitantly onto the lap of the seated man.

"Tonight as well... please excuse me."

"It's simply the exercise of a rightful claim. As always, nothing to be embarrassed about. It's an important body — not one that belongs to you alone."

He traced her cheek with one hand and tilted her face upward as her eyes fell softly closed. Combing his other hand through her pink hair, Roswaal closed one eye, gazing down at Ram with the blue one remaining,

"No-o-ow then, let's see just what sort of existence you are to us. I'd very much like this to proceed amicably, wouldn't you say?"

Murmuring the words only to himself, Roswaal shifted his awareness entirely.
Fixing his gaze solely on the Ram before him, sinking his consciousness into her alone.

The first night draws to its close.
— Closed out by the eerie secret conference between the manor's master and his maid.

"Good morning! Clear skies again today, perfect for laundry! Good for me, good for you, good for everybody! Good smile!"

Greeting the second morning with a triple banzai for the rising sun, Subaru raised his voice in ringing exultation.

Standing in the middle of the garden, eyelids scorched by the sunlight, he shook off his sleep-fogged eyes by throwing his whole body into motion.
The morning's first round of radio calisthenics — blood circulating properly through arms and legs, distributing the energy stored up by sleep throughout his whole body.

Finally, stretching both hands to the heavens and shouting "Victory!" brought the beginning of the beginning of the day to its close.
With a light sheen of sweat on his brow, Subaru wiped it away breezily and turned around.
And then—

"You really are full of energy this early in the morning..."

"Energetic doesn't even begin to cover it, I'm super energetic! My energy is everyone's courage, I love you, love we, love all! Let's fire it up!"

At the edge of the garden, in the shade of trees ringing a small garden plot, Emilia stood mid-way through her own morning routine, giving a wry smile. Floating near her hand was a ball of gray fluff — or rather, Puck — rubbing his black eyes with tiny paws.

"Seeing you wash your face like that, you're totally a cat. Anyway, do Spirits get sleepy too? Ever oversleep?"

"You all get sleepy when fatigue builds up too, right? It's much the same for Spirits when our reserves of Mana, the source of our vitality, run low. When there's not enough Mana stored up, the drowsiness just keeps lingering like this... fnyaa."

Following Puck's yawn, Emilia gave a small yawn of her own, hand raised to her lips. Subaru shrugged theatrically at the sight of the two of them,

"You two staying up late together, not cool. Bet I know what it was — up till all hours gossiping about your crushes, right? Nice, nice, let me in on it! My crush? Um, well, it's kinda embarrassing, but—"

Clasping his hands and lowering his eyes, Subaru snuck glances at Emilia. She gave a perfunctory wave of "yeah, yeah" at his antics,

"The one I love is Puck, and the one Puck loves is me. End of story."

"Mutual love!? Where's the room for me to squeeze in!? Room!?"

"Nyone. Ria's completely smitten with my charms. Subaru, you might not be such a bad guy yourself, but next to me you don't stand a chance. Just accept it and give this girl up... nyow nyow!"

Puck brushed aside the pressing Subaru with a condescending air.
Producing a handkerchief from who-knows-where, Subaru bit down on it, wailing "kiiii—" while Emilia, without a shred of mercy, pinched the ear of the crying man and the ear of the smugly whisker-preening Puck alike,

"You two, quit fooling around! Keep saying stuff like that and I'll get mad, you know."

"Ow-ow-owww, she's mad, she's mad,"

The two of them, man and creature, chorused in unison, cowering under Emilia's punishment.
As both rubbed their freed ears, Emilia let out a small sigh,

"It's fine that you two get along, but don't use me as your material for fooling around. Say you understand. Go on."

"Ye-e-es,"

Prompted by her outstretched hand, he found himself nodding along with the momentum of it before he could help himself.
He couldn't shake the sense of being played like a puppet, but watching her smile with satisfaction made such trifles stop mattering — strange, that.

A smile quietly surfaced from a sense of relief. Feeling his lips loosen with it, Subaru inwardly scrambled a little to keep his grin in place,

"What's with this fluttery feeling. Being the big-sister type with me, someone who's got an older-woman kink — Emilia-tan's appeal offensive is way too intense, I'm both troubled and thrilled, it's a happy scream."

"A happy scream... what does that even sound like?"

"Kyahiiiin! — something like that, maybe?"

He brought both fists up near his chin, gave a small hop while bending one leg. It was the kind of gesture an old manga's fake-cute character might pull, and he surprised even himself with how well he'd nailed it.

"Huh, I don't know why, but I feel really let down."

"Unexpectedly unpopular!! Why!? Is this where the world and I just fail to connect, WH-Y!?"

Subaru's all-out reaction to Emilia's shoulder-slump gesture. The classic manga pose of yesteryear is known as the "Shei," but her response to it amounted to nothing in particular.
Having flopped entirely, the strength drained from his body and he crumpled to the ground,

"Man, my physical bits just keep flopping...! I get it that stuff like 'life' or 'flames' don't translate without context, that's fair, but even a bewitching pose that charmed everyone from kids to adults gets no love? Just how strict is comedy around here...!?"

"I don't know what wall you just crashed into, but good timing, actually. Here, Subaru. Come here, sit down."

To Subaru, hands on the ground looking about to cry, Emilia — seated sideways on the grass — patted the spot beside her lightly.
The instant Subaru grasped the meaning of *come here*, he moved fast. Practically leaping through the air, he slid in right next to her,

"Called and I came flying, slid right on in, present and accounted for. What, what? Perfect timing for what? The man who reaches the itchy spot you can't quite scratch, Natsuki Subaru, will scratch that one spot on Emilia-tan's back that's itchy but out of reach, exactly as commanded! Nothing more, I promise!"

"I only invited you to sit next to me, but somehow that got a way bigger reaction than expected, what do I do..."

"..............."

Even Emilia gave a wry smile at the sheer force of his momentum. On her palm, Puck touched his own whiskers and stayed conspicuously silent.
Perhaps sensing that Puck was of no help in that posture, Emilia said "well, um" as a preface and turned back to face Subaru,

"So, how was yesterday? Did you manage to work properly?"

"Ah, eighty percent disaster!"

"I see. So confident and... eh? It was bad? Eighty percent!?"

"No wait, sorry, eighty percent's an exaggeration even for me. Probably more like... generously speaking, six— no, seventy, seventy-five percent or so..."

"That's a pretty fine line..."

Seeing Emilia deflate for some reason, Subaru felt an inexplicable pang of guilt and found his own shoulders shrinking too. Noticing herself being pulled along by his mood, Emilia waved her hands in a fluster,

"Ah, but, look, it was your first time working and twenty percent went well, right? Then it's fine, I'm sure you'll manage. Come on, have some confidence."

"You're so right! Twenty percent on my first try, that's basically slugger territory, right? I might get carried away with the tailwind and start acting all high and mighty!"

"Don't get that conceited, make sure you actually reflect on it too."

"That's a harsh set of demands!? Ah, no, nothing, never mind, sorry."

Pressed by the intensity of Emilia's narrowed-eye glare, he curled his half-raised shoulders back down small and hung his head.
Still, all that aside—

"Well, honestly, I'm managing somehow with Ram and Rem backing me up. Giving it my all and landing at twenty percent, well, can't be helped, right? So let's chalk up that disappointment to future improvement, looking forward to tomorrow."

"If you're not even bothered about it yourself, then there's nothing for me to say, but..."

At Subaru's determinedly upbeat remark, Emilia pursed her lips in something like a sulk. That childish gesture was abnormally adorable, and Subaru's body heated with a sensation like his chest being squeezed.
But he mobilized every ounce of self-restraint to kill the impulse.
Striking a comical pose, Subaru pointed both index fingers at her,

"Well well well, and so, for such and such reasons, today too I'll be diligently pursuing my servant life, getting bossed around and disciplined by the maid sisters. When I get worn out from that life, I'm coming to dive into Emilia-tan's lap, so make sure to keep it free."

"...If I only take half of what you say seriously, it might actually come out about right."

"Such a harsh review from such a cute face! But but, if you're taking half of it seriously, that means one lap-half is a go, right! Then tonight's Emilia-tan half-lap is reserved for me... don't you dare use it without permission, Puck."

Redirecting his pointed finger at his new target, Subaru issued his declaration of war. Puck, on the receiving end, didn't lose an ounce of his composure,

"Heh, whatever you say, you came along later — Ria has already given body and soul over to me by Contract. It's a bit late to try and nyan-nyan-nyan your way into this relationship."

"Don't go changing the terms of the Contract on your own without my knowledge."

Emilia pinched the incorrigible Puck's ears again, lifting him into the air and swinging him.
With his entire body weight bearing down on his ears, it would be an unimaginable torment for a human — but since Puck's weight to begin with was about that of a cotton puff, he didn't seem to take any damage. He hung there swaying, his expression as leisurely as ever, letting himself be carried along.

"Well then, now that I've refueled on morning vitality, guess I'll get to work."

"Refueled on vitality — were you doing something?"

"Obviously, hanging out all lovey-dovey with Emilia-tan charged up my love-power gauge. Got two full bars stocked up, so by my math that's two ultimate moves ready to fire. This is basically decided already..."

"There you go again, talking yourself up. Keep teasing people like that and when you finally say something真面目, nobody's going to believe you. Haven't you ever read a fairy tale?"

"I know the similar story, sure! Though honestly, I think that guy brought it entirely on himself, no room for sympathy there, if you ask me."

"You're the one saying that...?"

At Emilia's look — the sort that says *look at others and correct yourself* — Subaru stuck out his tongue with an embarrassed grin and got to his feet.
Brushing the grass off his backside, he rotated his hips and stretched,

"Better get going or I'll really be in trouble. This morning I'm starting with breakfast prep. Emilia-tan, you don't like Peppir, right? I'll leave it out for you."

"Even if I don't like it, I still have to eat it, so— wait, did I ever tell Subaru that?"

At Emilia's puzzled tilt of the head, Subaru left behind a small smile and waved his hand.
Just a little — but it made him glad, to have caught her off guard like that.

Swaying needlessly side to side, he kept clowning around all the way until he was out of her sight.
Deliberately, deliberately, keeping the smile from fading, all the while conscious of it.

Watching Subaru wobble off out of sight, Emilia let out a small breath and looked down at her hand.
Following her gaze, the small gray cat sent his own look toward Subaru's retreating back. Noticing Emilia looking down at him, the cat lifted his head,

"What's up, why the long face?"

"It's just, kind of, murky inside. I can't quite put it into words."

Emilia fumbled, struggling to put the confusion inside her into words. But it wouldn't come together properly, and in the end it only dissolved into a wordless sigh, vanishing into the air.
Watching her struggle, Puck touched his pink nose and narrowed his eyes,

"Worried about Subaru, huh? That's rare. Didn't think Ria cared this much about other people."

"Don't just decide on your own that I'm bad with people. It's not that I'm bad at dealing with others, I just haven't had many chances to."

Cheeks puffed out, she grumbled that much to the gray cat she'd known for so long.
It was the kind of childish attitude she couldn't manage in front of others — a form of indulgence, her own particular way of it, that surfaced only in front of Puck, with whom she'd shared so many years.

Puck's own expression, meanwhile, remained gentle.
Like a parent delighting in a daughter's growth — which, from where his feelings stood, was precisely accurate — Puck nodded with that same expression,

"Well, it's no wonder Ria's confused. Something's gotten a little troublesome, you see."

"Troublesome... how?"

Laid-back as he was, Emilia sensed this wasn't something to let slide, and her expression tightened.
As a rule, Puck's manner stayed consistent no matter the situation. Whether that was some trait particular to Spirits or simply a quirk of Puck's own personality, she couldn't say — but it often meant the weight of what he said was left for the listener to judge. Which, in the end, meant it was up to Emilia to decide.

Even faced with her earnest expression, Puck's manner didn't shift.
Toying with the tip of his short whiskers with one paw, he said,

"I gave it a vague little touch. Subaru's heart is preee-tty jumbled up in there. All mixed up, outside and in. Left like that, I wouldn't be surprised if it wears clean through before too long."

He said it in that endlessly unhurried tone of his, through and through.
