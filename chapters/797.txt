Arc 10 Chapter 37: "The Executor"

"—Guh, ggaaah!"

The instant he pulled the knife from his side, lightning shot through his brain.
His vision went red. He forgot how to breathe in or out, and his organs writhed inside him—but only for a few seconds. The moment his mind came back online, he drew on it to let out a long, long breath.
By the narrowest margin, he had survived—

"Okay. Just like we planned...!"

"I won't let you die, but could you?"

Ferris's curse came from a whisper's distance beside Subaru's ear. Then again, Subaru knew perfectly well he'd done something reckless enough to deserve it. Ferris's anger was fair.

Subaru's brush with death had been awful, but Ferris was in a terrible state too. Both arms were gone below the elbows, yet he had put Subaru's treatment first. His severed arms, which had kept him from treating anyone, were still pinned to the wall by the halves of a tailor's shears.
Honestly, Subaru had hoped for a cleaner rescue, but—

"Ferris's arms getting pinned down and another enemy showing up weren't in the forecast... For me, this was supposed to be a safe bet with zero risk."

"Where was the zero risk? Both Subaru-kun and 'White Sheep'-chan nearly died!"

"But you were here, right? That's why I figured we'd pull through. A hundred percent."

"...I'm not Reinhard."

"—Huh? Neither am I. That's why we've both got it rough."

Calling them equals was reaching pretty far when Ferris had the ultimate trump card of healing magic. Still, wishing for what he didn't have would get Subaru nowhere. He could only play the cards in his own hand.
Starting with—

"—Crusch-san, get Melty-chan over here!"

"Please!"

Like Subaru, Melty had been stabbed and needed urgent care.
Subaru shouted despite the pull in his newly healing side. Crusch answered by raising a wind with her magic and gently tossing the fallen Melty toward him.
With Ferris on his back, Subaru reached for her as she arced through the air—

"—Invisible Providence."

Using the third arm that sprang into being, he managed to catch Melty. The wound in her stomach looked painful, but when she looked up at him from his arms, she said,

"...The plan worked."

"Yeah. Best Actress is yours. —Ferris!"

"You don't have to tell me, you pair of idiots!"

Subaru gave a thumbs-up to Melty's strained but defiant smile.
Ferris began treating her wound over Subaru's shoulder. Watching him work, Subaru laid out another card he could play in this situation.

"—Cor Leonis, back on and full blast!"

His Authority, interrupted while he had been ninety-five percent dead, sprang back to life.
The "Little King" resumed taking on his companions' burdens. Subaru alone shouldered the uncontrollable flood of emotion known as "Breach"—the ability Beralis called "Ring Finger." No, Ferris was here. That wasn't the only burden Subaru could take.

"—Guh, ah..."

"—What?! Hey, what did you do?! You nearly died again just now!"

"J-just took on damage for two people... that's all. Yaaay...!"

It hurt. It was agony. But the plan had worked, so he was *ultra happy*.
The negative and positive collided inside Subaru, filling him with an urge to leap for joy even while his grave wounds were being treated. Maybe he should go ahead and jump. Melty in his arms and Ferris on his back had both lost enough blood to weigh less than usual. He could probably hop around carrying them.

"...Don't tell me you're taking on what's wrong with Crusch-sama and the others? I don't know how, but... is this some crackpot magic you made with Beatrice-chan?"

"Crackpot's... a bit harsh... Beako's imagination is always pretty-tastic..."

"Shut up, be quiet! —I'll fix that too."

Buffeted by mixed emotions, Subaru felt Ferris rest his chin on his shoulder from behind. Eyes closed, Ferris began a serious examination, presumably feeling through his mana.
Treating both Subaru's and Melty's severe wounds while finding the cause of an entirely different affliction seemed an outrageous feat. But Subaru could only believe Ferris would manage it.
And leaving him to deal with the "Ring Finger" effect Subaru had taken on twice over—

"—Crusch-san! Fire!"

He cheered on Crusch, who kept moving nimbly to hold their enemy away.
Crusch seemed to have joined them through a hole in the underground ceiling. She was locked in a fierce swordfight with the man swinging half a pair of tailor's shears—the enemy called Reborn, if Subaru remembered right.
Until just moments ago, that surge of emotion should have been eating away at her too. Yet—

"—Yes, Subaru-sama!"

There was an ease to Crusch as she answered and swung her sword, as though a taut string inside her had slackened.
The change caught him off guard. Concern and plain nosiness both urged him to ask what had happened, but he forced them down.
Whatever had changed within Crusch was giving her strength.
The proof was in the way she fought—

"—Hah! Hyah!"

Crusch lunged with a sharp step. Reborn caught her strike on his shears, but the impact hurled his small body backward. He spun through the air, planted his feet against the wall where he landed, and stopped himself—only for blades of wind to rush at him in succession.
With precise aim and a relentless rate of fire, those outrageously powerful blades battered him without pause.

Her swordsmanship alone was formidable. With the efficiency granted by the Divine Protection of Wind Reading, Crusch's command of a battlefield this size was exceptional.
Add the brief boost from the Dragon's Eye, and she would rank well among the strongest fighters Subaru knew.
Under an onslaught that was almost an inescapable combo, even this opponent looked ready to lose the advantage—

"Like heeell I'll let you!"

"—!"

With his roar came a silver disc spinning furiously through the wind blades—or rather, the half of a tailor's shears he'd thrown.
Crusch met the brutal attack head-on and knocked it upward with her sword.
But Reborn slipped into the gap left by the halted wind blades, kicked off the wall, caught his deflected weapon in midair, and tore the hole in the ceiling even wider. A rain of rubble poured down. Soundlessly.

"Oh, cra—!"

A collapse without a single sound was like a nightmare someone had delivered before remembering to put in the sound.
An absurd mass was falling on them, like a building brought down by an earthquake. Yet with nothing to convey the weight of it, the whole thing felt almost as if it were mocking them.

"————"

Wind blades could not stop such a massive stretch of ceiling.
Crusch darted between the falling pieces, but once she disappeared behind the billowing dust, Subaru could think of nothing but whether she was safe. The swelling emotion, doubled by what he had taken from the others, threatened to make him burst into tears right there—

"That won't do anything to Crusch-sama! Don't insult her! —And Subaru-kun, you too!"

"—Oh."

At Ferris's words, the flood of tears that had risen almost behind Subaru's eyeballs hurriedly retreated. The wave of emotion he had thought unbearable ebbed away, leaving a short breath in his throat.
Ferris had freed him from Beralis's "poison."
And just as Ferris's triumph over "Ring Finger" promised, his liege, who had vanished into the dust, was far too tough to be crushed by falling debris.

"————"

A silent leap broke through the smoke. Crusch bounded from one falling chunk of rubble to another, kicking her way upward toward the ceiling as though someone were playing the collapse in reverse.

Reborn clung upside down to the ceiling like a gecko, his shears buried in it. Crusch used the silence to approach from his blind spot.
With one last kick powerful enough to send a massive fragment flying, she sprang toward him—and the sound of that kick rang out through the silence.

Of course, it warned Reborn of her approach—

"—Beralis! Burn her!"

At his command, a voice rose from deep in the dust: Beralis, lost among the falling debris, the "Ring Finger" who might even have been crushed.
Her warm, sultry voice sang of delight and affection—

"When two things that should never touch come together, it's dangerous, isn't it? —That's what makes it wonderful!"

An instant later, Crusch swung her sword well short of Reborn.
She wasn't sending a blade of wind at him. She was trying to cut through an enemy attack before it could take shape.
The Dragon's Eye made that miraculous technique possible: severing the signs of magic before a spell could be cast.
Her strike sought to prevent whatever was forming between her and Reborn. But the threat it should have cut off remained, and colorful fire blossomed above Subaru and the others. Soundlessly.

"Wha—"

To Subaru's eyes, Crusch's strike had been flawless.
Her blade had completely stopped Black Dog's magic, yet it couldn't stop Beralis's oddly multicolored flames. Not just red, but blue, green, purple, and gold, like fireworks.

"Thiiis is the eeend for yooou!"

The blast hurled Crusch downward. Reborn used that same wind to add force to his leap and close in on her.
He raised his shears to cut through Crusch's slender waist. Everything but Reborn's shout looked like a fight scene on mute. Faced with that unreal sight, Subaru's mind grew white-hot and raced.

"————"

He should have all the pieces now. Remembering what Melty had told him: each of the Beloved Children had one ability. "Silence" and "Detour" were straightforward, but Beralis's "Ring Finger" had displayed a different kind of power altogether.
Simply removing the restraints on a person's emotions could not explain it. Her ability was unlike even the Authority of the Sin Archbishop of Wrath. Yet whatever had just caused that explosion had to follow the same rule.

The explosion wasn't magic. There had been no time spent gathering power. If anything, it had been like triggering a trap, and Reborn had seemed to know what would happen.

There had to be a principle behind it. There had to be an answer.
Think think think apply apply apply pursue pursue pursue see through see through see through—

"—!"

His eyes went bloodshot and his mouth dried out. At the same moment, a smell brushed past his nose. A faint stench like rotten eggs. Subaru recognized it because, back in compulsory school, he'd been a kid who loved experiments.

"Crusch-san! Don't cut—make wind!"

How long had he spent thinking before he shouted that?
By the time he looked up, Reborn had almost caught Crusch in midair. His blade came within a hair of her—and then a wind blew.

It must have been a reflex. Crusch probably hadn't thought it through.
Wind burst from her free hand and scattered without any aim. By chance, it sent air toward the onrushing Reborn—and a yellow-orange explosion erupted there.

"Gwaaah—!"

Reborn took the unexpected blast full on and tumbled across the floor.
Watching him fall, Subaru clenched his fist. He'd been right.

"Amazing... Subaru-san, how did you—"

"'Ring Finger'—the medicine finger! Talk about a fitting name!"

The many bursts of flame had carried that rotten-egg smell with them. Sulfur.
The scent he hadn't thought about in ages, the fact that the flames had come in colors other than red, and Crusch's failed attempt to cut the attack all pointed Subaru to what "Ring Finger" did.
If it had been magic, Crusch would have severed it. So it wasn't magic—

"It's chemistry! She scatters substances into the air that shouldn't be mixed!"

Certain chemicals amplify the action of the substances in the brain that put mind and body in fighting mode when a person faces danger. Beralis's ability had a similar effect, forcing people into a state of heightened arousal and releasing the restraints on their emotions.
Negative feelings grew darker; positive ones gained momentum and heat. That was the truth behind "Breach," and the explosions worked on the same principle.

Beralis could create chemical substances and make them react to produce the effect she wanted—

"An elemental shipper! So for all that, she's a science girl!"

"Science Girl? Is that your pet name for me? If you're giving me a pet name, that means you love me...? I love you too...!"

"Subaru-kun, you're the worst. What a cheat."

"Now that's a hot false accusation!"

The woman hidden by the smoke had misunderstood him, and Ferris blamed him for it.
The situation was stacked against Subaru, and his bad luck wasn't finished—

"You're the one who figured out what you shouldn't have, huh...?"

After taking friendly fire from his own side, Reborn turned his soot-stained face toward Subaru.
He had worked out that Subaru had exposed the truth of "Ring Finger" and held their formation together. In an instant, Reborn kicked off the floor and aimed his killing intent straight at them.
Subaru opened his mouth to stop him, but—

"—Shuuut uuup!"

"————"

That one command sealed away their voices. Subaru, Ferris, and Melty could no longer make a sound.
In one respect, "Silence" might have been Subaru's worst match. Confusing an opponent with words was one of his favorite tricks.
Without that, he might as well be helpless.
Except—

*—Invisible Providence.*

This time, Reborn might have been better off listening to Subaru.
The black hand extending straight from Subaru's chest didn't reach for Reborn charging in front of them. It shot behind the three of them, toward the stairs between the cellar and the floor above.
That was where Subaru had entered the cellar and promptly been stabbed by Melty. A door separated the stairwell from the room, and Melty had shut it behind them.

And this was Kugelschreiber Castle—Beatrice's old home, where she had once lived with the Witch of Greed.
Which meant—

"—Huh?"

The unseen hand opened the door. Reborn's red eyes widened at what stood beyond it.
From elsewhere in the castle, transported here by "Door Crossing" using their link through Cor Leonis, came a masked man—

"'—Love you, Bea—' 'Like heeell I'll let you say it!!'"

Rudely cutting off Subaru's declaration of love to the partner who had come through for him even now, Reborn roared out a ban on every sound in the room.
"Silence" spread over the cellar. Reborn's rushing footsteps, the breathing and cries of Subaru's group, even the newcomer's first words—all faded into emptiness. Soundlessly.
But amid that "Silence," Subaru thrust a hand high into the air—

"————"

Without a word, he pointed at Reborn, then drew his raised thumb across his own throat. The next instant, green flames erupted without hesitation and swallowed Reborn head-on.

"Gaaaah—!"

Reborn cut through the roaring fire in a single stroke and charged out of the curtain of flame. Burns covered his body; his eyes blazed with fury as he raised his shears and rushed them.
Black Dog swept past Subaru's group and met Reborn's body with a front kick. The impact spread without a sound, but Reborn stopped the man's heel with the handle of his weapon.

"You keep doing that over and—Guh?!"

In that instant, a quarterstaff swung up diagonally and struck Reborn across the face just as he was gloating.
Melty had leapt to her feet for a full-force counterattack.

"Gah—"

Nose and front teeth broken, Reborn arched backward and flipped away from the follow-up strike. His parting blow swept Melty's legs out from under her, and Black Dog caught her as she fell. He could not pursue.
But Reborn's brief retreat gave him no respite.

"—Duchess Karsten. Crusch Karsten."

"You little—"

The voice behind Reborn was the beautiful battlefield introduction of the "Valkyrie," who had tied back her green hair neatly with a ribbon.
Reborn whipped around and spread his field of "Silence," drawing her into his territory. But the resistance was futile.

"————"

The wind needed no sound. It swept through with a flourish, a flash of golden eyes tracing a line through the air.
The shears he raised to meet her were knocked aside. Her sword point danced with smooth, efficient motion and sped unerringly toward him—

"—Crusch-sama really is amazing."

As Ferris murmured with feeling, that single stroke decided the fight.
The blade cut across both Reborn's eyes. He clutched his face and screamed, the cry resounding through the cellar. No longer silent, it rang loud and far, as though announcing his defeat to the whole castle.

△▼△▼△▼△

"Kuhahahaha! They really put you through the wringer, didn't they? All of you beaten bloody, and one of you's even forgotten his hands! Forgetful only goes so far!"

"P-please don't talk like that, Black Dog! Subaru-san and Blue Snake-san and Verdant Beauty-san all fought so hard...!"

"It's okay, Melty-chan. I think he's just embarrassed. He slipped up and got taken out, so he arrived late when we really needed him."

Subaru shrugged at Melty's protest. Hearing him, Black Dog echoed, "Melty?" with suspicion.
He swapped his mask for an angry one and looked back and forth between Subaru and Melty.

"Well, well, you seem friendly. She even told you her name! Did you cozy up to them while this dog was away? Wicked woman!"

"W-wicked? I wouldn't—"

"I said cut it out! We just won with teamwork that didn't need words. Nice job picking up my signal, Black Dog."

"Kuhaha, pure luck! Your dear, adorable little one had already filled me in. Otherwise, how could I have known to walk through a door and let loose in a room I'd never seen?"

"...Yeah. I owe Beako big time."

While treating Black Dog, Beatrice must have kept an awareness of the path of her contract with Subaru and prepared to act as soon as he called on her.
Thanks to that, as soon as Ferris pulled Subaru back from the brink and "Detour" lifted from the castle, she had used their Cor Leonis link to send reinforcements through good old "Door Crossing."

"You really saved us. ...When we started fighting among ourselves and our forces fell apart, I didn't know what we'd do."

"—Hearing you say that only makes me more ashamed of my own failing."

Crusch lowered her eyes. Subaru stopped short with an "Ah..."
There was something subdued, almost demure, in her manner. Over the past two months she had presented herself like the old Crusch Karsten, sturdy and resolute; this wasn't that Crusch. Subaru had been nearly dead and hadn't followed every detail, but it seemed the change hadn't begun just now.
In other words, when he thought the Crusch from before her memories were stolen had returned, she had apparently only been acting the part.
And the reason was probably—

"—Crusch-sama, you're hurt. Please let me treat you."

Ferris offered to treat Crusch after her fierce fight. How she had behaved must have been her way of facing him.
Ferris seemed to understand that, but did not bring it up. Crusch frowned, then drew a sharp breath as she took in his condition.

"Your arms need attention more than I do! We must reattach them at once!"

"Don't panic, Crusch-sama. You're the one who cut them off, remember?"

"That's precisely why I'm saying it! The cuts should be clean, but can you reattach them?!"

Ferris had deliberately put his own arms last, so he must have had a way to heal them later. But he offered Crusch no reassurance, letting her worry.
Even if things were not quite back to normal, Subaru could feel some of the old warmth between them—or at least something like what they had shared in Priestella.
But then—

"...What the heeell are you all so chummy about?"

A voice rose like a curse from the bottom of a pit, spoiling their moment of victory.
Subaru and the others spun around. Reborn was staggering to his feet, leaning on his shears. Crusch had slashed both his eyes; he was blind and should have been unable to fight. Yet hatred twisted his face, and hostility and murderous intent poured off him.

"Enough. Stop it! We've figured out your 'Silence'! You can switch sound on and off within the area you can see, right? That's why you couldn't erase voices behind you. Now that your eyes are gone—"

"So whaaat?! I can't back down just because of thaaat! Beralis, kill my pain! Tornel, quit dawdling and put 'Detour' back up!"

Spitting the words, Reborn struck the floor hard with his shears as he called to his allies.
Beralis's power could probably use adrenaline to numb his pain. But she gave no answer. Blood loss and her injuries had left her motionless. Whether unconscious or dead, she had no strength left to help him.
Nor did the third ally, still unseen, show any sign of answering.

It was pitiful. Reborn could only wait for a finishing blow. But his standing as one of the Beloved Children, and his own nature, would never allow him to surrender.
Knowing no help was coming, he turned his face, streaked with bloody tears, toward them.

"Fine then. Doesn't matter... I'll kill every last one of you myse—"

"—No. That's enough. All of you have gone much too far."

The voice that cut in belonged to none of the people who had been in the cellar.

"————"

Before the stunned group, a figure dropped through the great hole in the ceiling.
He landed lightly between them and Reborn, shielding the bloodied man behind him as he faced Subaru's group.

Slim build, black hat, and a curved sword shaped like a crescent at his waist—Subaru knew that figure. Crusch and Ferris surely did too.
He had thought they might meet as enemies someday.
But he had never imagined the man would appear here, now, to protect the Beloved Children.

He was a pastor of the Divine Dragon Church, Filore's guard, that pleasant-looking young man—

"—Tiga."

"————"

At the sound of his name, the young man—Tiga Rauleon—closed one eye and furrowed his brow.
It was his name, without a doubt. That wasn't why he looked puzzled. It was the person who had called it.

Neither Subaru nor Crusch nor Ferris had spoken.

"—Melty-chan?"

Subaru caught his breath at the sight of Melty, the one who had called Tiga's name.
She held both hands over her mouth and stared at him with her large, round eyes. Shock filled them, and behind it a joy too great to hide.

"—?"

Tiga, though, seemed to have no idea why she felt so strongly. He looked at her with open confusion.
For a moment, his arrival and the strange tension between them cast the room into silence.
Then—

"—Tiga, do you know that girl, ya know?"

A small presence leaned out over Tiga's shoulder. It was a squirrel-like spirit with a curled tail and a face both cute and cheeky.
Tiga, who had brought along this unfamiliar spirit, shook his head at her question.

"I don't think I do."

"—!"

"Sure looks like she knows you, though. And... there's no mistake, I tell ya. That big one over there in the weird getup—he's *my* contractor."

"Huh? What the blazes is that tiny little spirit going on about?"

Melty's face clouded at Tiga's answer, while Black Dog bristled at the spirit's words.
The connections between them were a mystery, but the one who could stand the situation least was Reborn, the man being shielded.

"You, 'Executor'! What the hell d'you think you're doing? We were supposed to handle this—"

"—Time's up. Things have changed. I wasn't planning to rush in like this either. But after all that commotion..."

"Mind telling me what caused such a stir, my friend?"

Tiga raised an eyebrow as Subaru broke into his conversation with Reborn.
At the pointed "my friend," the corner of Tiga's mouth twisted. There was something almost self-mocking in the expression, and Subaru frowned.
Before he could voice his question, though, Tiga answered the one he had asked.
What had caused such a stir? The answer—

"This castle has burst out of its separate dimension into our world. —The whole kingdom's watching it now."

—News that, after four hundred years of silence, Kugelschreiber Castle had taken to the sky.
